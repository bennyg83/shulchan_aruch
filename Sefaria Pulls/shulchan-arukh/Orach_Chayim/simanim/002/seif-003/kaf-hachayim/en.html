3) One who places his garments at his head forgets his learning — Horayos end. One who dips his finger in salt and eats restores his learning. One who gazes at a dead face or reads writing on a grave forgets his learning — Likutei Pri Chadash, cited Peri Megadim O.C. letter 1; yet what he wrote about gazing at the dead or reading grave writing forgetting learning needs study — Horayos there says “hard for learning” and Rashi “hard to hear” — perhaps also through forgetting. Shaar HaMitzvos Vaetchanan: only raised lettering is a concern, not sunken. See Gemara there for more causes of forgetfulness and things good for learning. Eating from broken vessels causes forgetfulness because they hint at broken “shells” and forgetfulness comes from them as said below.
