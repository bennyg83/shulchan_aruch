If it is properly “made <i>tzitzis</i>,” one is permitted to go out in it to the public domain — whether a small <i>tallis</i> or <i>tallis gadol</i>, even nowadays when we have no <i>tekheles</i> — provided it is not resting [only] on his shoulders (words of Ran and Rosh Chullin 108b). We uphold the <i>tallis</i> with its presumption [<i>chazaka</i>] that it is properly made <i>tzitzis</i> and need not inspect before going out (Responsa Rosh klal 2; Responsa Ramban siman 215; <i>Kol Bo</i> citing Rambam Shabbat 19). .
