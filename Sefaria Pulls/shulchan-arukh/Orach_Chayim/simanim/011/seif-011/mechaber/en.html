If the <i>gdil</i> — called <i>orlayu</i> — is wide, one does not tie <i>tzitzis</i> on it; if one tied on it, it is invalid, for “on the corners of their garments” is written — and this is not considered part of the garment; but it counts toward the measure of a full thumb-knot and the three fingerbreadths [from the edge] since the hole is within the garment. Rema: Good to measure the thumb-knot without the <i>gdil</i>, and [the hole] within three fingerbreadths including the <i>gdil</i> (Beit Yosef citing Rashi and Rashba). .
