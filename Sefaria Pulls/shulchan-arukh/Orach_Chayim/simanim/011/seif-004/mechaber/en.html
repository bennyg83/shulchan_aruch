The length of the eight strands is not less than four thumbbreadths; some say twelve thumbbreadths — and such is the practice — and above [that] there is no upper limit. Rema: If one made them excessively long one may shorten — no issue of “you shall make” or “from what is already made” (Mordechai <i>Hilchos Ketanos</i>). One of the strands should be longer to wrap the <i>gdil</i> — the part of the <i>tzitzis</i> that is not woven.
