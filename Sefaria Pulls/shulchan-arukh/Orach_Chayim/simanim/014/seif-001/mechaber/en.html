<b>Laws of <i>tzitzis</i> made by gentiles and women, and a borrowed <i>tallis</i> — it contains five clauses.</b><br><i>Tzitzis</i> made by a gentile are invalid, for it says
“Speak to the children of Israel,” excluding gentiles; a woman is fit to make them. Rema: Some require men to make them — good to do so <i>lechatchila</i> (Mordechai <i>Hilchos Ketanos</i>; Hagahos Maimoniyos <i>Hilchos Tzitzis</i>; Tosafos 42a).
