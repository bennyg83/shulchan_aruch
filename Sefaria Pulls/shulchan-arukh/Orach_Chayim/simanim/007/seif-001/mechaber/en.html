דין לברך ברכת אשר יצר כל היום אחר הטלת מים. ובו ד סעיפים:
כל היום כשעושה צרכיו <b>The law of blessing <i>Asher Yatzar</i> all day after urination — it contains four clauses.</b><br>All day when one relieves oneself, whether minor or major matters, one blesses <i>Asher Yatzar</i> and not on hand-washing, even if one wishes to study or pray immediately. Rema: If his hands were soiled such that he rubbed them [clean], he does not bless on hand-washing (Semag siman 27, 49). .
