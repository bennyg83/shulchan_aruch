Meaning: one under constraint who cannot lengthen supplications and abbreviates them but says them with intention is reckoned before God like one at leisure who lengthens supplications with intention (Mishnah Berurah; likewise Yad Aharon, Taz, Shevus Yaakov II §44). See Tosafos Yom Tov on Avos 2:16. Mekor Chayim writes the same applies to Torah study; Soles Belulah likewise.
