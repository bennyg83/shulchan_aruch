Magen Avraham sk 12—as opposed to Eliyah Rabbah [is a] scribal error; the proof for these matters is in this Raavad chapter that he brings from Beit Yosef—that after the tamid they would offer the ketoret—therefore he writes unlike Eliyah Rabbah who wrote minchah before ketoret, namely the minchah belonging to the tamid shel shachar—likewise in Tosafot Shabbat.
