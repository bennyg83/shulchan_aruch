Early supplicant. Kabbalists elaborated Chatzos prayer; Rekanati Bereishis writes some say end night time of mercy—I decide Chatzos proper to plead for Israel… siman 131 seif 2; Gemara first explanation gave middle-middle sign—Chatzos auspicious—second explanation not—see Zohar Vayahel p. 382 always reckon night twelve hours summer/winter although prayer not so—siman 233 likewise Shaarei Tziyon.
