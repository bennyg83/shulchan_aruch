They come as voluntary offerings—unlike Chatas which comes only after transgression requiring Chatas; Asham—even though Asham Taluy exists and one can bring nazirus Asham, plain Asham does not come voluntarily. I saw Maharshal: may say after Parashas Chatas ve’Asham Yehi Ratzon in doubtful language—if I owe Chatas may this count—but in my opinion no—for Gemara often brings conditional Olah “if obligated this Olah, else nedavah”—but not Chatas since Chatas is not voluntary—how say “if not liable let it be nedavah”? Nedavah is unrelated to Chatas procedure—if liable and reading counts as Chatas like actual offering—how if not liable call it nedavah when reading followed full Chatas law? Therefore one does not say. I also saw crushing answer to those who say before Tamid and Mishnayos Eizehu Makoman “May our lips substitute”—in my view improper to protest—for it implies about what follows; Chatas laws appear there—no “peace-offering bulls” for nedavah—perhaps not liable—like chullin in courtyard—unless say “substitute for Tamid service” as above.
