If one wears a <i>tallis katan</i> while one’s hands are not clean, one puts it on without a blessing; when one washes one’s hands one handles the <i>tzitzis</i> and blesses on it — or when one wears another <i>tallis</i> one blesses on it and intends to exempt this one too; one need not handle the <i>tzitzis</i> of the first.
