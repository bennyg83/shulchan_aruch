If one slept in his <i>tallis</i> at night one must bless on it in the morning even if one did not remove it — and it is good to handle it at the time of blessing. So too one who wears his <i>tallis</i> before daybreak [<i>Pri Megadim</i> citing <i>Terumas HaDeshen</i>, Semag, Semak, Mordecai, laws of tefillin]. ]:
