“Measure of tallis,” etc. The beraisa words: a tallis in which a child covers head and most of body and an adult wears casually to market — obligated; if a child does not cover head and most even if adult wears casually — exempt. Rambam and Shulchan Aruch hold the main measure is the child’s covering — because stamma says “even if adult,” not the reverse — all depends on child’s covering; Tanna cited first “adult wears” for amplification — if adult wears regularly obviously obligated — then impossible measure only child — obvious even child covers if adult wears regularly — even only casual adult wear still “your garment” when child covers — Tur wrote whole beraisa but for adult wrote “adult not ashamed to go out” — appears he requires both. Stamma only stated if child does not cover — reverse learned a fortiori — obligation is on adult, estimate by child — stamma teaches: even if for adult only casual — exempt — surely if not garment for adult we disregard child cover — one could ask why two measures — truth one measure — answer: children differ — Tur cited nine-year-old — differences among nine-year-olds — shame differs — combined both — Tur nine years — from Bava Kamma mishnah ages nine and ten — each by cleverness — child here interpret broadly — commerce from nine — Beit Yosef Mahari Chaviv: Tur inexact — Darkhei Moshe cited — Rema: specifically when adult wears — not for measure but obligation of tzitzis — no obligation on child — obligation on adult who sometimes wears — main measure child — like Mahari Chaviv — my mind Tur’s intent: many protest masses making four corners from small cloth — Mordechai — “peddler calls” on those who make with or without tzitzis — lazy to leave four-cornered without tzitzis — duty of God-fearers warn not bless on small garment.
