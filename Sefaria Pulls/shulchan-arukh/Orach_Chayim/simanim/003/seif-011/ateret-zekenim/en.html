He shall not wipe with pottery because of sorcery. Or Zaru’a: on a weekday, even with shards of smooth vessels where there is no danger, he still does not wipe with them because of sorcery—rather with a pebble. On Shabbat, when both a pebble and shards exist, he wipes with the shards even though there is a sorcery concern, and not with the pebble, for it is not a moveable object [bar tiltul] (Bach).
