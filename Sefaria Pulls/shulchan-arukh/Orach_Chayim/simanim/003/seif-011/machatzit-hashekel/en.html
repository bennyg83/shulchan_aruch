(s.k. 11) “So they are accustomed [to wipe with flammable matter],” etc.—it appears to me the reason is not natural “virtue” causing hemorrhoids, but sorcery performed with something flammable one wiped with, or with pottery another used—sorcerers dominate it and bring hemorrhoids; nowadays privies are not in the field, so we are not concerned the matter will reach sorcerers.
