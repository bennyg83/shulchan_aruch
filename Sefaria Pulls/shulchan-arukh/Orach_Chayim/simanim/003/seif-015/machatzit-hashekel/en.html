(s.k. 15) Urinating—Niddah 13a and Magen Avraham as corrected; see Even HaEzer 182.

See Shakh s.k. 6: he rejects Bach there—the leniency was stated only for terumah: our mishnah, one eating terumah felt his organ tremble; if semen emerged it would become impure and he would have to spit out the terumah and lose it—so they permitted holding the organ so semen would not emerge (it does not impart impurity until it leaves), then he swallows the terumah. The Gemara asks: but holding the organ is forbidden; Abaye answers: with a thick cloth. Ra’avad held the leniency is only for terumah loss, not elsewhere.
