“He shall not speak” — Baer Hetev. See Rambam, Hilchot De’ot ch. 5: there is a prohibition not to speak while relieving oneself even for a great need. Mishnah Berurah writes this follows from the incident of Rav Safra cited in Berachot and Tamid—see there. Perhaps Rambam speaks only while one is actually relieving oneself, when even a great need is forbidden; whereas the Rema, who wrote “and he shall not speak there”—i.e., in the bathroom—perhaps while one is not yet relieving oneself it is permitted for a need.
