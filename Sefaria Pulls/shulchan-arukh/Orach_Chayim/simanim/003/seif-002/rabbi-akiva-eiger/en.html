Seif 5 — In the gloss about urinating: if so, clearly relieving himself to the front is permitted. As for not relieving oneself between east and west, and not [standing] toward the east with one’s face to the west—that is because east opposite west also has sanctity [relative to the Divine Presence]; therefore a woman is likewise forbidden to relieve herself eastward with her face westward. Beit Yosef.
