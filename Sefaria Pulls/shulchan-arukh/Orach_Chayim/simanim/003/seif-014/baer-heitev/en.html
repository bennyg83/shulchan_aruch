“And below [the corona].” Even if the organ is erect, he may hold from the corona downward—so from Tosafos, beginning of Kol HaYad, s.v. ocheiz, etc., since they did not answer that the Gemara there speaks of erection, for it says “his organ trembled.” Eliyahu Rabbah.

“The married man.” The Taz wrote that by strict law it is forbidden—see there; if his wife is not with him or she is a niddah, he is not called “married” for this leniency—see Taz, Magen Avraham, and Even HaEzer 23 s. 4 and what they cite.
