“From the lookout.” — Taz: Beit Yosef explains the Rambam text and as Shulchan Aruch states—one who urinates from the lookout inward sits and turns his face to the Holy or diverts the Holy to the side; “sits” means urine does not fully void standing, only sitting. “Or divert…” per Beit Yosef’s second explanation—only his face to the Sanctuary, not his back without exposure, is forbidden from the lookout… “or divert” and permitted in all cases—but Kesef Mishneh in Hilchot Beit Habechirah emended “shall not sit…” hence here it should read “shall not sit…” This gloss in Shulchan Aruch seems not from Rabbi Yochanan but an emendation per Taz and Bach; there Bach writes when there is no Temple it is permitted like Rabbi Yochanan Berachot 61b without lowering his head, and he thinks Rambam learned urination from there—I do not understand; better not emend Shulchan Aruch; on the contrary the text “sits with his face toward the Holy” is permitted (they state a stringency)—nowadays permitted in all cases, and Rambam spoke when the Temple stood. They derived from Yerushalmi as Kesef Mishneh wrote. Levush siman 67 likewise for urinating even from the lookout inward need not turn the face—per Beit Yosef’s first explanation—hence he omitted there “or divert the Holy to the sides.”
