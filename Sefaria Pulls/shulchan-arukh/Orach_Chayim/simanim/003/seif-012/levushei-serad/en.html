There: indicating Torah trop with the hand—Rashi there: even for the third reason [ta’am] it is permitted to show trop occasionally; we are not strict except about wiping with the right. But if one regularly shows trop and his gaze is fixed, since for the fourth reason a lefty must wipe with most people’s left, even if he regularly shows trop with that hand, we do not create a distant dispute—one agrees the third reason permits occasionally—and he concluded. Yet Beit Yosef wrote a lefty may not wipe with his own right but with most people’s right—even for the fourth reason—so there is no proof from showing trop; one could say for the third reason even occasionally it is forbidden.
