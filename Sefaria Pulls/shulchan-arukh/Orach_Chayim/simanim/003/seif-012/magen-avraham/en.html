“At night.” Meaning: he should not uncover more than by day—but he need not distance himself. If no one is there he may defecate in the street—but not before gentiles (Avodah Zarah 17a). All this concerns defecation; urinating is permitted even by day before many people if necessary because of danger. A woman should not stand exposed before a child in that way, but [relieving] off to the side—we have no issue (Bekhorot 44a).
