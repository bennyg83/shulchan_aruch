One does not fold the <i>tallis</i> and tie <i>tzitzis</i> on its corners as if it were folded — Rema: but one must tie on its four spread corners (Beit Yosef) — unless one sewed it entirely, even on one side. Rema: Some say one is obligated even without sewing — good to make <i>tzitzis</i> for it but not to bless on it (Rif, Rosh, Tur). If one tied <i>tzitzis</i> on <i>tzitzis</i>: if one intended to annul the first, one cuts the first and it is valid; if one intended to add, even if one cut one of the two it is invalid. Rema: Some validate in every case — and such is the principal opinion (Tur citing Rosh, Rabbeinu Yerucham).
