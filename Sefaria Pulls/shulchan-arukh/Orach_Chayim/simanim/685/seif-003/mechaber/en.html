On the third Shabbos, which is the fifteenth of Adar, we interrupt certain study. On the fourth Shabbos, which is the twenty-second of Adar, we take out two scrolls: from the first we read that week’s regular portion, and from the second we read Parashas Parah, and the haftarah is Uzerakti aleichem mayim tehorim [See siman 137 seif 4.']:
