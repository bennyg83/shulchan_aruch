The order of the Four Torah Portions; it contains
seven clauses. When Rosh Chodesh Adar that adjoins Nissan falls on Shabbos, we read the Torah portion of Shekalim—that is, Parashas Ki Tisa through the verse about making the copper laver—and the haftarah is Vayichres Yehoyada.
