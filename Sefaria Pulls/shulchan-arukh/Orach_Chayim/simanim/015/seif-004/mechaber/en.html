If the <i>tallis</i> tore within three fingerbreadths of the corner edge one may not sew it — Rashi explained we worry a sewing thread might remain and he would add seven strings for <i>tzitzis</i> on it; for this reason even any tear one does not sew. Accordingly a wool <i>tallis</i> torn within three [of the edge] may be sewn nowadays when people do not sew with wool thread. Rav Amram explained the reason: torn within three lacks garment status as if absent — though sewing makes it “cut off”; if one ties <i>tzitzis</i> on it it does not exempt the <i>tallis</i>. On this explanation any remnant after tearing may be sewn. Some say for Rav Amram only <i>tzitzis</i> tied at the time of sewing are invalid; if after sewing one tied <i>tzitzis</i> it is valid — a God-fearing person surpasses all where possible.
