One who sews a piece of cloth into the corners of the <i>tallis</i>, and similarly what people sew around the hole of the <i>tzitzis</i> — if the <i>tallis</i> is silk and sewn with white silk thread, there is concern per Rashi that there be no sewing below three [fingerbreadths from the edge] nor above a thumb-knot [from the hole]. Rema: Likewise wherever one sews with thread of the <i>tzitzis</i> species we worry one might take that thread for <i>tzitzis</i> strings (<i>Taz</i> 46 citing Hagahos Maimoniyos). .
