Seif 1. Mixture of chametz — one transgresses on it, etc. Note: behold in the law that permission joins to prohibition — such as when a minority of permission mixed in a majority of prohibition — if one is lashed for a kezayit of the permission and prohibition together; and see in my chiddushim to tractate Niddah daf 27b regarding perhaps two peridos would not be abundant — a revi'it on a perida of dust and its measure increases; and there I proved that also the minority permission joins the majority for impurity of the house; and the same for prohibition — on this the questioner and answerer there will disagree — see there and examine. And behold, see the language of the Beit Yosef in this siman who wrote in the name of Maggid Mishneh on our rabbi's words in this language: a contradiction appears in the language of the book from itself to itself, etc., with the end; and before I answer — behold this language is not understood, and also the body of his answer has no understanding. And it appears there is a textual error here, and first it must read: and before I answer I shall precede, for the reason the Mechaber distinguished, etc.; and his intent in this introduction settles the first difficulty: although he brought first that retaining is one degree lighter than eating, he afterward brings the Rambam's language that one transgresses bal yera'eh on mixtures — because first the Tur goes in the method of the commentators that Elu Overin means from the table; and for this explanation one may say retaining is lighter than eating on one side; but the Rambam who explains Elu Overin as bal yera'eh — therefore he holds the law of removal and eating are literally equal, and the first wonder is removed; and afterward it must read: and what is possible to explain in permission — contradiction two — namely he now comes to settle the second difficulty: namely what the Rambam ruled like the sages is for eating, but for bal yera'eh he ruled like the sages and for him removal is stricter than eating — and this is per the explanation of the Ramach; and therefore for transgressing bal yera'eh even without kezayit in kedei achilat peras, but for eating one is not lashed without kedei achilat peras — and with this he concludes; and thus the two contradictions I mentioned will be resolved: namely the first contradiction was resolved in the first introduction and the second in this introduction. So appears the intent of the Maggid Mishneh; without this his words have no understanding — examine; and see in Magen Avraham s.k. 1 what he wrote on mixtures of chametz, etc.; and Kessef Mishneh agreed to the second view. And behold the Beit Yosef explained the matter more: for bal yera'eh the Rambam ruled like R' Eliezer since the anonymous mishnah is like him — namely since in any case the chametz is in his house; but for eating the Rambam ruled like the sages. And I was asked by one man: if so, who pressed the Gemara to say the mishnah is like R' Eliezer — behold one may say it comes even like the rabbis, and they disagree only on eating but on removal they agree. And I answered: there is room, for the beginning of the mishnah one may say the intent is for bal yera'eh as Rashi; as a mishnah in any case the ending that concludes behold these are in a warning and have no karet — perforce deals with eating, as Rashi himself: if for bal yera'eh why conclude and have no karet — behold even chametz in its essence has no karet for bal yera'eh; and why does it teach us this in mixtures; also from the Torah it would be reasonable to say there should be karet in bal yera'eh and bal yimatzei — perforce it deals with eating. Therefore from the ending one must say it comes like R' Eliezer; and specifically the beginning is considered anonymous since he cited anonymous first it is anonymous; but the ending behold these in a warning is not anonymous — one may say this is the conclusion of R' Eliezer's words, for R' Eliezer spoke first in the mishnah: R' Eliezer says also women's ornaments — and everything after this is R' Eliezer's words; therefore not considered anonymous; therefore the Rambam ruled here like the sages; and so it is; and simple in my humble opinion — examine. And what Magen Avraham wrote there in the name of the Rashba that everything one intends to make so is not nullified — see what I wrote on it in my compositions Orach Chayyim year 5601 in chiddushim to Pesachim chapter 2 on the sugya one does not cook the Pesach offering in water — see there what he wrote to wonder on it greatly — examine there; and see what I further wrote on these words of Magen Avraham; and in Yoreh De'ah siman 134 he wrote in the name of the Rashba, etc., and we wrote to settle his difficulty in my compositions Orach Chayyim year 5627 in my answer to the community Stanislav — it bought its place in omissions to Sefer HaChaim to Kontras Minei Mitamim — see there what I wrote — examine. And behold see in Magen Avraham s.k. 1 what he wrote: the Maggid Mishneh wrote chapter 4: specifically when there is a kezayit of chametz within kedei achilat peras — this is the essence; and the second view: although one does not transgress by eating, one is obligated in removal; and although mixed in many olives one transgresses, for behold there is a kezayit of chametz there; and Kessef Mishneh agreed to the second view — end Magen Avraham. And behold in my opinion it appears to decide [like the view] of Kessef Mishneh from the Yerushalmi first chapter of Beitzah halachah 2: on the mishnah Beit Shammai say se'or in a kezayit, etc.; and thus: R' Avahu in the name of R' Yochanan — whether in its removal or in eating — a kezayit; R' Mana argued with R' Chizkiya; he said to him: yes; Rav heard this matter; he said: from R' Avahu; he said: and we too say R' Avahu in the name of R' Yochanan — whether for removal or eating a kezayit; if not so, thirty-seven karetot would be taught in the Torah — end. And behold this Yerushalmi is like the signed book; and the Kaf HaChayyim made a textual error and it must read: whether in removal whether in eating — in a kotevet. And behold besides the difficulty of corrupting the books, its explanation is also wondrous: one — that he himself wondered on the measures of offerings — what difficulty, perhaps it comes not like Beit Shammai; and I wonder more: even without his proof there that the mishnah would not be like all, even if we say it is like all — if it were a matter he could teach according to all it would be difficult why he put it in dispute — he should teach what is like all; but here behold if there would be thirty-seven karetot for inadvertence it would only be like Beit Shammai not Beit Hillel; if so in the end it would be like the tannaim — why abandon Beit Hillel's words and teach like Beit Shammai. And further wonder: behold there in Keritot they did not list measures but species — for what is one liable karet and chatat; if so behold one counts and eats chametz — what difference if one eats a kezayit chametz or two olives; if so he could teach that one ate several olives of chametz; and perforce he did not teach only the species; if so likewise what difference chametz or se'or, and what matter that this is kezayit and that in a kotevet. And strained to say since they disagree in oil and in judgment to Beit Shammai therefore he had to count them — this is strained to challenge thus — what difficulty. Therefore the text appears in its simplicity; and the answer is according to what I wrote in my chiddushim to laws of Pesach siman 431, and it is in my compositions Orach Chayyim year 5629 page 40 in explanation of Yerushalmi language in chapter 5 of Pesachim; and what emerges: that Beit Hillel hold both in a kezayit; and the Gemara explains the reason we learn removal from eating — the intent is not by way of mah matzinu, for certainly there is no proof, for eating is stricter; only the intent is by way of kal va'chomer: Beit Hillel hold therefore in all measures kezayit because the extra language is that the language of eating is kezayit; and automatically where eating is not written it should be kal va'chomer; and if so in removal, since eating is not written, it should be kal va'chomer; only we learn a leniency from eating, and a leniency is properly learned from eating by way of kal va'chomer; and since from kal va'chomer we learn removal from eating, we require also in removal a measure; automatically it is reasonable to say sufficient to come from the law to be like the case; therefore also in removal kezayit — see there where I extended on this. And behold the novelty here is in the dispute of Kessef Mishneh and Maggid Mishneh: we learn removal from eating in mah matzinu — if so reasonable to say argue from it and establish in its place: granted in eating if not in kedei achilat peras one does not eat together; but in removal, since in the end the chametz is in his house — all the more if by kal va'chomer — if so likewise sufficient from the law to be like the case; and since in eating one is liable only if mixed in kedei achilat peras — the same in removal. And this is the intent of the Yerushalmi: behold if we say we learn removal from eating in mah matzinu — difficult, we should also learn for karet to be liable on bal yera'eh; and one should not say the truth is so, for behold in mishnah Elu Overin it teaches behold these in a warning and have no karet; but from this alone is no proof, for one may say there it deals when there is no kezayit in kedei achilat peras — then one should not learn from eating, for in eating too one is exempt in such a case. And it would be that we say argue from it and establish in its place: one transgresses even if not in kedei achilat peras — from a place; if we say establish in its place — behold in it karet is not said, only a prohibition; but when it is within kedei achilat peras in a matter where in eating one is liable for karet — one may say also in removal one is liable. Therefore the Yerushalmi said this is impossible to say, for if so it should teach thirty-seven karetot also in removal when in kedei achilat peras; and perforce in removal in every case exempt from karet; and perforce we do not learn in mah matzinu removal from eating, only by kal va'chomer; and automatically sufficient from the law to be like the case; and one does not transgress bal yera'eh except in kedei achilat peras; and this is what the Yerushalmi said: whether in removal whether in eating kezayit — namely we require kezayit together in kedei achilat peras; and proof that perforce Beit Hillel who learn removal from eating is perforce by kal va'chomer, for he said in mah matzinu — if so to obligate karet also on removal; and one should not say the truth is so that one is liable if in kedei achilat peras; and the mishnah that teaches behold these in a warning deals with more than kedei achilat peras — if so it should teach thirty-seven karetot; and it fits well with R' Eliezer. And behold further proof to our words from what the Sma wrote in Choshen Mishpat beginning siman 72: according to the majority the latter bin is simpler than the first; and if we emend like the Kaf HaChayyim's text whether in removal whether in eating in a kotevet — then the first bin would be simpler; and strained to emend also this that in reverse it should teach whether in eating whether in removal in a kotevet — this too is strained to emend; and perforce as I wrote — examine well.
