**** ENGLISH ****
A firstborn of an impure animal—in what, in whom, and in what place it applies, and likewise its redemption and breaking of the neck. In it are 23 seifim. A firstborn of an impure animal applies in every place and at every time; and it applies only to donkeys. And this is its mitzvah: every Israelite who has a donkey that gave birth for the first time redeems it with a sheep—from sheep or goats, whether male or female, whether unblemished or blemished, whether large or small—and gives it to the kohen. And from when is he obligated to redeem it? From birth until death; but it is a mitzvah to redeem it immediately so as not to delay the mitzvah. And after he redeems it, it is entirely non-sacred and permitted in shearing and work like other non-sacred animals; and the kohen does with the sheep as with other non-sacred animals.
