**** ENGLISH ****And some say, etc. — because though exempt from giving to the kohen from doubt because of burden of proof, nevertheless separation is required to remove their prohibition; therefore with two males and two females he separates two lambs and they are his; and with male and two females he separates one lamb and it is his; and the reason of the first reasoning: in the first doubts, since in any event an act must be done to give something to the kohen, they also imposed separation to remove the doubt from prohibition; but in these latter ones where nothing need be given to the kohen they were lenient also that multiplicity of doubts removes from separation; and see Beit Yosef.
