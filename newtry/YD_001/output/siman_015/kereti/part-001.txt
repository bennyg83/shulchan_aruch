בגדיים וטלאים ואנן אין בקיאין בכלו חדשיו ולכן צריך תמיד שהיי'. ונתנו אחרונים סימן בשינים וקרנים:
**** ENGLISH ****
We do not rely—Taz wrote it is not a double doubt, doubt completed or not completed, and if you say not completed perhaps it waited, etc.; and there was an error in his writing—for certainly there is no double doubt here: if one can say "if you find to say not completed," you still cannot say "perhaps it waited"—what of it if not completed, behold it is full stillborn and no waiting helps at all. Only the reverse doubt: doubt whether it waited eight days, and if you say it did not wait, perhaps its months completed—and on this Taz explained it is all in the category of one name of coercion, whether stillborn or not. And he wrote well; and in vain Shach attacked him in Nekudat HaKesef. In kids and lambs we are not expert in completed months and therefore always need waiting. And later authorities gave a sign in teeth and horns.
