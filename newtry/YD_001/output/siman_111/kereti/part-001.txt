And the heter, etc. The Shach wrote: nevertheless, when each is recognizable which is issur and which heter, and at the time of falling it was not known, etc.; but if the heter and issur pieces mixed first, issur was established and we do not say "I say." The Peri Chadash challenged on this that what he wrote — even when recognizable we call it established forbidden — see Peleti where I brought proof for the Peri Chadash from a complete mishnah in Terumot, see there.
