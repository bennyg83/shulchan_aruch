<b>דין מליחת הלב והריאה. ובו ד' סעיפים:</b><br>הלב <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>מתקבץ הדם בתוכו בשעת שחיטה לפיכך צריך <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>לקרעו <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i>קודם מליחה ולהוציא דמו ולמלחו אחר כך: <small><i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i>ואז מותר אפילו <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>לבשלו (ארוך כלל ט"ו) <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="2"></i>(ויש מחמירין לבשלו רק צולין אותו) </small>ואחר כך מבשלו <small>(ש"ד):</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
The law of salting the heart and lung. And it has 4 seifim:

The heart — blood gathers inside it at the time of shechitah; therefore one must tear it before salting and extract its blood and salt it afterward; and then it is permitted even to cook it (Or Zarua general rule 15). {Rama: And some are stringent to cook it only by roasting.} And afterward one cooks it (Shaarei Dura).

**** ENGLISH ****
If one salted it and did not tear it — one tears it after salting and it is permitted even though it was salted with the blood inside, for as it absorbs so it releases. And the same law if one roasted it and did not tear — one tears it after roasting and it is permitted. But if one cooked it without tearing — it is forbidden until there is sixty against the heart, for we do not know how much exited from it. {Rama: And even when there is sixty, the heart itself is forbidden (Or Zarua and Shaarei Dura, Maharai, Rivash); and one peels a bit around the heart. And some forbid other meat that was salted with it, saying we do not say "as it absorbs so it releases" regarding heart blood enclosed inside, for it is actual blood and is not called emission blood (Hagahat Shaarei Dura in the name of R' Mattisyahu, and so in Mordechai in the name of R' Yitzchak). And the custom is to be lenient, and such is the main rule — that this too is called emission blood and "as it absorbs so it releases" applies, like a person in veins as explained above siman 22 (Or Zarua and Mahara Katz). And some peel a bit where the heart was stuck (Or Zarua and Mordechai there). And it is good to heed their words and peel a bit around the heart — then all is permitted. And there is no difference between meat with the heart or the heart itself. And there is no difference whether the heart is closed or open above (individual opinion, not like Or Zarua). And they practice l'chatchila to cut the foreskin of the heart and tendons inside (Or Zarua) — but this is only a stringency and mere caution.}

**** ENGLISH ****
There is no fowl in which there is not sixty against its heart, and it is permitted even if stuck to the fowl. {Rama: And every fowl has sixty even without head and lower legs (Piskei Maharai siman 91, R' Yitzchak Mintz, and Or Zarua) — as is customary to remove them, meaning until the lower knee; therefore if the fowl is whole, all is permitted. And if it is not whole and there is not sixty in it against the heart stuck to the fowl — some say the piece becomes nevelah and we require sixty in the pot against all the fowl (Tur in the name of Yesh Omrim, and so in poskim), and so they practice. And even when there is sixty in the pot, the fowl is forbidden since there is not sixty against the heart stuck to it. And if the heart is not stuck to the fowl, the entire pot joins to nullify the heart. And see below siman 92 regarding law of piece becomes nevelah. And you have no animal that is sixty against its heart.}
