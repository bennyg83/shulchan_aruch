**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Or one of the people, etc.</b> This implies that the blessing may be recited even by someone who is neither the mohel nor the father. The Bach writes that our custom is always for the mohel to recite it, but he objects because the mohel is sometimes unlearned, and not only fails to understand the blessing but skips and distorts its words. Therefore, one who fears Hashem should arrange for this blessing to be recited by a distinguished member of the congregation. Nevertheless, this custom is ancient, for the Kol Bo also writes in siman 73 that the present practice is for the mohel to recite the blessing over the wine and the blessing “Who sanctified the beloved,” and then to say, “Preserve this child,” etc.

Again I found in Rivash siman 384, he brought Ba'al HaItur's language: and where there are two infants to circumcise in synagogue it seems to us since it is impossible to circumcise them together, they are separate bodies, each needs a blessing, compared to slaughter where there is possible to slaughter both together as we learn "the slaughterer who tilted two heads as one, his slaughter is valid"; but here it is impossible, etc., end of quote; behold explicitly as I wrote; but on the Rav it is difficult, since he rules here one need not return to bless when his intent was on the second from the start, implies he holds like the poskim who disagree with Ba'al HaItur; and although it is impossible to circumcise them together, one blessing suffices; if so when his intent was not from the start also they are one species, and it requires investigation. And for practical law I concluded in siman 19 s.k. 9 that even for one species one must return to bless, so too here; however where there is no custom Bach wrote one should practice and bless on each one, and they should be careful not to bring two infants together to synagogue except one after the other, after the first is circumcised bring the second, end of quote; and so is correct to do, because mostly they interrupt in between with idle talk not needed for circumcision and it is an interruption according to all; and so Maharshal in chapter Kisui HaDam section 9, they refrained with two infants not of one person to exempt them with one blessing, for many times it leads to their being grown and distraction.
