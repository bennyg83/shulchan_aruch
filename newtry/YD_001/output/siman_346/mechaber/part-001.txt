**** ENGLISH ****
One whose relative is crucified in the city should not dwell in it. In it is one seif. One whose husband was with her crucified in the city, or his wife was with him crucified in the city, or his father and mother and the flesh had not yet entirely decomposed—he should not stay within it unless it was large like Antioch where these do not recognize those; and he should not stay on this side but on that side.
