**** ENGLISH ****
If a sheet was torn after it was sewn. In it are 2 seifim. A Sefer Torah in which a sheet was torn within two lines—he sews within three; he does not sew. When does this apply? To an old one whose patching is not noticeable; but if the gevil is noticeable that it is patched, he sews even if the tear came within three. And likewise between page and page, and between word and word, he sews. And all tears are sewn only with the sinews with which they sew the sheets to one another. {Rama: And some say they are accustomed to sew them with silk (Terumat HaDeshen siman 51, and Rosh general rule 3, and Sefer HaTerumah); and so they practice nowadays; but the essential law is to sew them with sinews if possible (Gemara and poskim and Rivash). And some say it is permitted to paste klaf on the outside as long as it is permitted to sew them; and to paste the tear thereby (there, siman 31). And likewise if something is missing, they write on the klaf that is pasted (Maharik, root 122); and so they practice. And see below siman 280.} And in all tears he should be careful that not one letter be missing or its form changed or divided. {Rama: And if any letter was divided because of the tear, it is invalid and what is pasted behind does not help (there). And likewise he should be careful that the needle not penetrate within the writing but outside the writing.}

<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="9"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="7"></i>ס"ת שבלו ממנו יריעות ובא להחליפם אפילו לא בלו ממנו אלא אחת או שתים <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="5"></i><i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="9"></i>צריך להחליף שלש לפי שאי אפשר שיהיו אותם שמחליף דומות לגמרי לאחרות <i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="7"></i>ולכך <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="7"></i>צריך שיהיו לפחות שלש דומות:
**** ENGLISH ****
A Sefer Torah from which sheets wore out and he comes to replace them—even if only one or two wore out, he must replace three, for it is impossible that those he replaces be completely similar to the others; therefore there must be at least three similar ones.
