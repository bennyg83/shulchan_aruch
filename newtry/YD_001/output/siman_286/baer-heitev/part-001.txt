**** ENGLISH ****
A portion. Even if most of them are Israelites, they are exempt due to danger; and for this reason one does not make a mezuzah at the gates of streets such as in the holy community of Kraków and the holy community of Prague, which established gates on the Jewish streets that close at night — and they never established any mezuzah there. And there is another reason not to make a mezuzah there: the non-Jews will surely take it and disgrace it, and thus they would cause disgrace to sacred writings. From this they derived a ruling for an Israelite who leases a concession from the lord to sell spirits or beer or mead — and non-Jews fill the house every day and customarily take the mezuzah and treat it with contempt — that one should not make a mezuzah at those doorways unless the doorway is in a place protected from non-Jews who would not take the mezuzah — end quote, Shach.

**** ENGLISH ****
Behind — Rashi explained: for example there are in the corner of the house fixed doorposts for two doorways cut in it, one to the north and one to the west; and they fixed the mezuzah in the doorposts in the northern doorway's opening; and when the door is open, the back of the door — if in the thickness of the doorposts there is a separation of a tefach between doorway and doorway, another mezuzah is needed for the western doorway since the mezuzah is not in its opening; and if not, no other mezuzah is needed since it is as though placed in its own opening — end; and it appears from here that those doorways that are divided with a pillar in the middle suffice with one mezuzah even if the pillar's thickness is a tefach — one is not obligated in two except when one is north and one west; but when both doorways are in one direction, even if the doorposts' thickness is a tefach, all is considered one doorway. And Taz explains Rashi's words: meaning both doorways are cut in the doorposts themselves — in the northwest corner stands one thick wide doorpost cut with a large cut for doorway measure from the north side into the house's opening, and there they enter and exit from north to house and house to north; and another doorway measure is cut from the west side to the house's opening, and there is entry and exit from house to west and west to house; and he made the mezuzah in the western doorway's opening — on the side that is right for one entering from west to house; and thereby the mezuzah of the western doorway is plainly as law; but for the northern doorway there is a distinction if in that part of doorposts separating west from north there is a tefach measure, etc.
