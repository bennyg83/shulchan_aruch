**** ENGLISH ****
And likewise, etc. — as written in Tanhuma, etc.; (Addition) And likewise, etc. — as in Shabbat 131b: since he can renounce them, meaning even to dwell in them and he dwells in them he is exempt, for a borrowed cloak is exempt, and so a borrowed house; and one need not force that he holds vessels in a box are obligated in tzitzit, for regarding his cloak and doorway he spoke unspecifiedly implying in all cases forbidden (see Rashba in Shabbat there and Tosafot Menachot 44a dh "cloak" — requires study); and that which we say in the first chapter of Rosh Hashanah "but your house" — why? It is to exclude one who dwells in a house that is not his, as written there regarding tzitzit "but your garment" — why? etc.; rather one of two reasons he cited (Rashba in Shabbat there and Tosafot Avodah Zarah 21a dh "Ha." And even though we expound, etc.; and in Menachot 44a dh "cloak," etc.) (end).
