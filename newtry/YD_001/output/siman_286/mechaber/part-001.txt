Places obligated in mezuzah. In it are 23 seifim. **** ENGLISH ****
A straw house, a wood house, and a cattle house are obligated; and if women bathe in them, since they stand there naked, it is not fitting for the Divine Presence to be there—no mezuzah. {Rama: And specifically these; but an actual room—even a place where a man and woman sleep and engage in relations—is obligated in mezuzah (in a responsum of Maharil, and so Beit Yosef). And it appears to me that where the door is from inside and when they lie there they close the door and the mezuzah is outside, it is obligated according to all (own reasoning in Darkei Moshe, and so in responsum of Maharil siman 98).}

**** ENGLISH ****
One who rents a house outside Eretz Yisrael and dwells in an inn in Eretz Yisrael is exempt from mezuzah for thirty days; and one who rents a house in Eretz Yisrael is obligated in mezuzah immediately because of settling Eretz Yisrael. (And the same law applies to one who borrows a house—like a renter) (Beit Yosef in the name of R' Rabbenu Menachem, and so in the Gemara).
