The laws of blisters and lung adhesions. In it are 7 seifim. A lung in which blisters are found — even if they are very large — if they were full of air, or clear water, or moisture stretched like honey and the like, or dry hard moisture even like stone — it is permitted. If putrid moisture or putrid water or murky water is found in them — it is treifah. {Rama: And many permit even murky and putrid (Rosh, Ran, Rashba, Tur, Maharil, and above siman 36 seif 7 on that basis).} And the custom is to permit blisters in every case if they are not adjacent.
