**** ENGLISH ****
And if, etc., and many, etc. — see above siman 36 seif 7. (Likkut) And if found, etc. — as the girsa of Rif and Rambam there 55b: there is kosher in this and in that with clear water; there is disqualification in this and in that with a flaw; there is kosher in lung and disqualification in kidney with mugla; there is disqualification in lung and kosher in kidney with puncture and clear water that are kosher here and here — we only said "clear" regarding murky, etc.; but there is another girsa: and clear water we did not say, and it returns to kidney. And even in the first girsa one may explain thus; and so agreed in Torat HaShelamim (end of his words).
