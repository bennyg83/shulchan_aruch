(סימן ל"ז סעיף ב') <b>אם סירכא תלויה יוצאה מן הבועה טריפה.</b> כתב הש"ך ומביא בלחה"פ והבאה"ט ס"ק ו' וז"ל אם עור הבועא מישתנית למראה הריאה כשר דכיון שיש בו מראה ריאה לא ה"ל אלא חיסרון מבפנים עכ"ל. ולפ"ז כ"ש סירכא תלויה או שאר ריעותא שיוצאה מן המורסא שקורין בלאט"ר דלא הוה תרתי לריעותא (שהרי קרום הריאה מכסה אותה) ואדרבה עדיפא מורסא מבועא דאינה אוסרת בסמיכי כיון שאינה גבוה מבשר הריאה. משא"כ בועא אף שנתכסית אוסרת בסמיכי כיון שגבוהה כמבואר בסעיף שאח"ז. וכל זה ברור ופשוט. ולא ירדתי לסוף דעתם של לחה"פ והאחרונים ס"ק י' שכתבו בשם מ"כ וז"ל מורסא שנמצאת בראשה סירכא תלויה אם יש בו מים זכים כשירה ואם מוגלא טריפה עכ"ל. וליתא אלא אפילו יש בו מוגלא כשירה דלא הוי תרתי לריעותא כיון שהיא אינה גבוהה. ות"ל שאחר כתבי ראיתי כן בס' פמ"ג והנאני. וע"ש שכתב דה"ה מורסא וטינרי העומדים על גבשושים או במקום דמשמעת קול כשירה דבועא דוקא אמרו וא"כ ה"ה מורסא על הקמט וכן שנוי מראה מהכשירות. וכן מע"ל אינו פוסל בה. סירכא תלויה מן האטום או מן מראה כשירה או מראה כשירה על הגבשושית מקרי ב' לריעותא. וב' לריעותא לא הוי אלא בשעומדין זא"ז ואין הפרש קש ביניהן ומעידין על שם א' ואיכא דעת א' להטריף בכה"ג מצטרפין. ולענין מיעוך ע"ל סי' ל"ט סעיף י"ג דל"מ רק בתלויה מבועא. ובשו"ת טור האבן סי' יו"ד מתיר ג"כ בסירכא לדופן במצר חזה מבועא כיון דכשר מדינא דגמרא מהני מיעוך אף בב' ריעותא:<br><b>ועיין</b> בנודע ביהודה מ"ת חלק יו"ד סי' כ"ג דמטריף סירכא תלויה מחסרון בפנים כמו יוצא מבועא שאין לסירכא במה להתחזק כיון שהעור ריק ואין תחתיו בשר. עוד מטריף שם סירכא בגומא דאין לך תרתי לריעותא גדול מזה כי איך לא נחשב החיסרון לריעותא כיון שהרמב"ם מטריפו לחלוטין. וזה דלא כס' ל"ש דמתיר משום דגומא וחסרון בריאה אין סופו לנקוב ואין ב' ריעותא על שם א' וחולק על התב"ש. ובתשובה הארכתי והוכחתי דהעיקר כהתב"ש דכל מילי מטעם שסוף לנקוב הוא:
**** ENGLISH ****
(Siman 37 seif 2) If an adhesion hangs out from the bubble — treifah. Shach wrote and brings in Lechem HaPanim and Avodat HaTaharah s.k. 6: if the skin of the bubble changes to lung appearance — kosher, for since it has lung appearance it is only internal deficiency — end. And accordingly all the more adhesion hanging or other flaw emerging from morsa called in Italian that it is not two flaws (for the lung membrane covers it); and on the contrary morsa is better than bubble, for it does not forbid in adjacency since it is not higher than lung flesh — unlike bubble which even if covered forbids in adjacency since it is high, as explained in the next seif. And all this is clear and simple. And I did not reach the end of the view of Lechem HaPanim and acharonim s.k. 10 who wrote in the name of Machatzit HaShekel: morsa in which at its head an adhesion hangs — if clear water in it — kosher; if mugla — treifah — end. And it is not so; rather even if mugla in it — kosher, for it is not two flaws since it is not high. And it is proper that after my writing I saw thus in Pri Megadim and it pleased me. And see there he wrote the same applies to morsa and tinnir standing on protrusions or where sound is heard — kosher; they only said regarding bubble; if so the same applies to morsa on the wrinkle; and so is the version of kosher appearances. And likewise across does not disqualify in it. Adhesion hanging from the sealed place or from kosher appearance or kosher appearance on protrusion is called two flaws. And two flaws is only when standing one next to the other with no hard gap between them and testify to one name; and there is one view to declare treifah — in such a case they combine. And regarding pressing see there siman 39 seif 13 that not all agree — only hanging from bubble. And in responsum Tur HaEven siman 10 he permits also adhesion to lining in narrow of chest from bubble, since kosher by Gemara law pressing helps even with two flaws.

Question and answer in laws of treifot siman 37. A practical ruling occurred: they slaughtered one ox and found its lung adhered to the fat of the heart, and the adhesion emerged from a depression that was on the lung. One posek ruled to peel the adhesion as is custom with all adhesions. When they peeled, the strands of the adhesion spread from the sides until near a wrinkle that was also on the lung. That posek ruled to peel everything and permitted; and I when I heard — my stomach churned, for in my short understanding he has no one to rely on, as will be explained with God's help; especially I heard he had already ruled thus before and says a depression is not considered a flaw. And he relied on Beit Lechem Yehudah who wrote at end of siman 39 to permit adhesion from within the depression. And how great is the leprosy plague that spread in our time — teachers from summary books who already warned and cried like street criers — all the great ones before us. And Rav Pri Megadim in siman 43 especially on that book found an error as summarizers do. And the great ones of our time need warn against innovating. I return to see from the posed question whether one can uphold Beit Lechem Yehudah to permit adhesion from the depression as that posek thought if we investigate from the source. Here it is simple in all poskim, examiners, and customs to declare treifah in every two flaws when they testify to one flaw-name; and every view to declare treifah counts as a flaw even if we do not hold thus. If so it is simple in books of great acharonim from whom we drink in these laws, headed by the illustrious Rav author of Tevuot Shor — depression or wrinkle on lung from outside, even any amount, joins another flaw, even though custom does not declare treifah in depression less than thumb-size; nevertheless it is a flaw, for from great rishonim who declare treifah even in any amount, brought in Shulchan Aruch siman 36 in the name of "there are those who forbid" — they are Rambam ch. 8, and Raavad since he did not challenge presumably holds thus; and this view was brought in Tosafot Perek Aleph Tav (end daf 47) in the name of some who wanted to forbid, and presumably they were not mere property owners as Turei Zahav siman 36 s.k. 50 wrote. And even though Tosafot there challenged that the mishnah should be established "or that it lacks" — all acharonim expanded to settle this in several ways; and since there is no resolution from the Gemara and great poskim declare treifah and Shulchan Aruch was concerned for their view — how can one be lenient in adhered adhesion emerging from it, especially like heart fat which the Mechaber in siman 39 declares treifah entirely; only we are lenient to press — and how can we press so much when emerging from a great flaw like depression. And behold even a flaw that no one would declare treifah, like tinnir — we hold one does not press adhesion emerging from it, as explained in all acharonim. And Beit Lechem Yehudah himself brought this earlier there in that siman. And now it stands: tinnir one does not press, and from depression one presses — a matter the mind cannot bear; and certainly if he erred in that book it is a scribal error, or it refers to hanging adhesion but not adhered as that posek thought. And it is not found in all acharonim that one is lenient in law of depression and wrinkle to join other flaws except one author of our time in Levushei Serad; and he too was not lenient except that it does not join flaws testifying to puncture, saying depression or wrinkle when beginning of health has no end to puncture and treifah only from deficiency and it is not two flaws from one name; and he is not lenient except when recognizable it is from beginning of health clearly, not in doubt. And he wrote it is a doubt of lack of knowledge as explained in his words siman 36 s.k. 142 and end of siman 37; and even in place of great loss as proven from his words siman 36 s.k. 60 and siman 30 s.k. 203. But he admits one does not press adhesion emerging from depression or wrinkle even when known from beginning of health, as explained in his book siman 39 s.k. 171. And so siman 35 regarding extra of ona — preferable even from tinnir, for in his view it does not testify to flaw-name at all; nevertheless pressing adhered adhesion emerging from it delays a great leniency. And a fortiori regarding adhesion from depression as above; and behold this matter is truly simple and I did not need write except to remove from the mind of that posek. However greater than this: even regarding other flaws on depression or wrinkle we judge to declare treifah like every law of two flaws, as Turei Zahav wrote explicitly siman 36 seif 21 s.k. 45 and siman 37 seif 5 s.k. 8; and Pri Megadim in opening to laws of examination and in siman 37 wrote that most poskim hold all lung treifot are from puncture; and so Darkei Yesharah; and so Turei Zahav from Tosafot Rif Perek Aleph Tav and from Shulchan Aruch siman 35; and so Pri Megadim from Shach's words there. And it appears to uphold their words upon which we live from their mouth. And I saw Levushei Serad who built a dike and poured a rampart on Turei Zahav's words with non-compelling matters — therefore I expand here: Turei Zahav wrote it is proven from Tosafot beginning Perek Aleph Tav (42b) "that he brought" that all lung treifot are because they will eventually puncture; they wrote for Rabbanan there are only 17 treifot and "lacking" for R' Shimon was needed — for Rabbanan all are included in punctured. And he wrote on this in Levushei Serad s.k. 3 there is no proof from Tosafot that no lung treifah is not because it will eventually puncture; even if we say treifot of deficiency, extra, exchange, etc. have no end to puncture but only from deficiency treifah — nevertheless they wrote well it is included in punctured for counting 18 treifot not counted separately since all are included in some puncture which is the lightest of flaws; and since treifah through puncture — no longer count removed and lacking. And why does the mishnah not teach removed and lacking and extra regarding heart and kidney as Tosafot wrote there. And the Rav held this view simply until besides rejecting Turei Zahav's proof he challenged in s.k. 11 on Tosafot themselves that it appears from their words for one who holds internal deficiency is called deficiency — it is counted in 18 treifot; how count separately since deficiency is more than puncture and included in lung punctured. Also challenged in s.k. 8 on Beit Yosef siman 36 — Rambam declares treifah depression and wrinkle on lung — he holds it completes count of 18 treifot; how complete since for him it is included in "lacking" which is more than puncture and already counted punctured — left in tzarich iyun. But in truth this view is not so — Tosafot only said regarding removed entire heart, lung, and kidney which are more than puncture since that organ's use is nullified; but in lung flaws where the whole is intact only a flaw in one place — certainly not more than puncture; especially flaws not found similarly in other organs declared treifah through puncture, such as missing ona, extra ona, exchange, and similar to a sponge, or adhesion and blister — certainly "lacking lung" was needed to say even deficiency and flaws like this are treifah; lest you say not included in lung punctured as no such flaw in punctured of other organs; or these flaws are not inferior to "created punctured" not treifah since it does not eliminate the channel as Taz wrote and he agreed in s.k. 10. And since "lacking" like this was needed — well one may count it in 18 treifot; as Rashba wrote brought in Beit Yosef siman 42 and Yesh Nohalin Perek Aleph Tav regarding that which they counted removed and lacking in two — Rambam learned from this whoever they said treifah through removal is not treifah through deficiency; and he disagrees and wrote therefore they counted in two to teach: one removed by hand and one deficient from birth — treifah, lest give loophole to disputant to say specifically deficient from birth but had it and removed — kosher or vice versa — end. And Tosafot in Kiddushin (daf 57) wrote deficient liver is treifah as Rashba's view; therefore he also holds they did not count removed and lacking in two except lest give loophole to disputant. If so one may say "lacking lung" — deficiency in flaws in whatever flaw in it, and "lung punctured" in two — lest give loophole. Rather necessarily from Tosafot that for Rabbanan there are only 17 treifot — we learn they hold all lung flaws will eventually puncture; therefore impossible to count them with punctured in two; for otherwise well possible to count in two. And now Beit Yosef's words are settled: though Rambam does not hold Rashba's view on removed and lacking to say counted in two lest loophole — because it is not reasonable there is room for disputant; if so for deficiency alone suffices and why need removed. But in treifot of deficiency on lung outside which Rambam declares treifah — there are truly those who permit — well one may say counted with punctured in two lest loophole. And so for one who holds internal deficiency is called deficiency — well count with punctured in two; for certainly there is room for disputant — they disagree on him; and well holds one should count with punctured in two; for all that is not more than puncture for all views — count in two. And unlike removed entire heart, lung, and kidney which is inconceivable to permit at all; if so what Levushei Serad thought to reject Turei Zahav's words — on the contrary there is proof for his words that they are compelling. (Except still tzarich iyun on Rambam's words that counted in two implies no end to puncture.) And further one may say another reason to count lung flaws included in "lacking" outside with punctured even if we say more than punctured — nevertheless mishnah was needed because difference for sealed: puncture is kosher through sealing lining in narrow of chest, and these deficiencies are not kosher through sealing as Pri Megadim wrote in opening to examination laws; and so in Chavat Daat siman 36 — see there; and since its law differs from punctured — well count in two. And accordingly Levushei Serad's difficulty from Tosafot and Beit Yosef and Turei Zahav is removed. Also what Levushei Serad wrote to reject Turei Zahav's proof from Shulchan Aruch that began "lung punctured treifah" and left and explained deficiency, exchange — and wrote therefore they began to write lung punctured treifah as introduction to all laws of deficiency and exchange, but we did not hear all flaws will puncture — behold he sought and as if he does not know Shulchan Aruch; for Turei Zahav's proof is not because they began puncture laws but because they began and left and explained deficiency laws in the middle and afterward returned and concluded puncture laws; and from this certainly one hears all are because they will eventually puncture; for if because punctured is beginning of all laws — they should have begun and finished all puncture laws first; why insert deficiency laws in middle of puncture laws — rather we learn as Turei Zahav wrote. Further Levushei Serad wrote in s.k. 7 to reject Turei Zahav from Taz siman 43 and Taz that even complete puncture created thus has no taste of puncture at all; if so all the more deficiency from beginning of health not puncture-form at all like missing and extra ona not in category of puncture; and so in s.k. 10 what they thought as more — one may say: granted puncture from beginning of health is kosher since it does not eliminate the channel; but missing and extra ona — one may say treifah because it will eventually puncture and eliminate the channel. Also what he wrote in s.k. 11 to reject Turei Zahav from Tosafot beginning Aleph Tav that it appears for one who holds internal deficiency is called deficiency — it completes 18 treifot; and according to Turei Zahav all lung treifot from will eventually puncture — internal deficiency too will puncture; how complete the count — rather necessarily not in category of puncture; if so other flaws on lung outside like missing ona are not in category of puncture; and no proof at all from internal deficiency to other flaws on lung; for this is inside lung flesh and no concern lest membranes puncture as Rashba wrote in his chiddushim — lung poured like a pitcher has no puncture concern, for inside flesh melted; and from puncture its skin did not suffer at all. And even though he wrote this for one who does not call it deficiency — nevertheless for one who calls it deficiency one may also say thus — only declares treifah from deficiency of flesh inside which he holds is called deficiency and did not return healthy; but he agrees no puncture concern at all from our reason; unlike other flaws outside on lung — concern they will eventually puncture it; and so in Chavat Daat. Behold explained there is no substance in Levushei Serad's rejection and proof; and we have only the famous acharonim — all from puncture, testify to one name, and combine to two flaws.
