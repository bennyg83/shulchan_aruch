Incident: lung adhered to pouch, adhesion full blood until around adhesion to pouch all blood appearance — I declared treifah, worse than two flaws — pressing does not help (large bubble like nut on middle ona of right on ribs, protruding outward, no lung adhesion except root that was adhered — rest outside; Shach siman 25 declared treifah and Pri Chadash agreed, but not for my reason).
