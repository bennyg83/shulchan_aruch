**** ENGLISH ****
On, etc., in one scroll, etc. — Yerushalmi chapter 1 of Megillah: Ribl asked — why do we not say there is in sefarim as for tefillin and mezuzot? R' Yochanan son of R' Chiyya objected: the written ruling of Rabbah is that sefarim are written on two hides while tefillin and mezuzot are written only on one hide. And see seif 2.
