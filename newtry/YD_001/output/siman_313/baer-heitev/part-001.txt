**** ENGLISH ****
<b>That it emerged.</b> It does not become sacred until it emerges from the womb, and in the time of the Beit HaMikdash it was forbidden to cause a blemish because that would destroy sacred property. Taz. Shach writes that the Gemara makes clear that even if part of a limb has emerged, as long as most of the fetus or its head has not emerged in the manner that gives it the status of being born, as explained above in siman 14 and below in siman 319, one may cause a blemish. For example, if a kid has long ears that protrude before the entire head has emerged, it is not yet considered born, and one may cut its ear before most of the head emerges.
