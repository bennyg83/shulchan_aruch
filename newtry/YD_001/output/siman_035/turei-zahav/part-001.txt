**** ENGLISH ****
The forms of the onot, etc. These laws in this hagahah Maharshal brought and wrote on them: and I am astonished what basis there is for these exchanges and to add treifos on what was not mentioned in the Talmud even by hint, for it only said five onot the lung has — three on the right and two on the left; but all these distinctions — who mentioned them that exchange should apply to them? This is only destruction of Jewish money — end. And my master may he live long wrote: and nevertheless, where there is no great financial loss, one should not rule permission in what they practiced as forbidden, even though by law there is no prohibition — end. And Maharshal wrote in the name of examinations: if the varda is larger than the ona next to it — meaning the ona adjacent to the ama — treifah; and he wrote well, for its name is ayinunita, and if it is so large that name does not apply — end his words. He also wrote: if the ama is above and the onot below — treifah; and it appears to me that where there is another suspicious matter, even though one does not declare treifah on its account, nevertheless it combines with the suspicious matter above and treifah.

**** ENGLISH ****
And the varda: if missing, etc. The reason: since in some animals there is and in some there is not, one should not declare treifah for any change of vardot. Changed its designation and form — Maharshal explained: when it is drawn out like an ona and not round — certainly for an ona there is no concern if it is not drawn and sharp, for only regarding ayinunita do we say changed designation is treifah, for treifos laws depend on the ways of lung growth, and Chazal established that an ona is sometimes round and sometimes sharp, but varda's law is round — end Igur. And he also wrote: if the ayinunita is entirely round without a groove — treifah, for its designation is known and according to its designation they called it by this name — end.

<b>שלמטה מן הורדא מקרי שלא בדרי דאונא. </b>לכאורה יש לאסור גם ההוא דסעיף ד' מן טעם זה אבל לא משמע כן לשון רמ"א שלא כתב שום חילוק ונראה הטעם דלעיל כשר ממ"נ אי הוה שם בדרי דאונא פשיטא דכשר ואי לא הוה בדרי דאונא מחמת שהורדא חולקת ומסיימת שיעור האונות מ"מ כשר מטעם דהוה כמו יתרת מקמא כיון שאינו על גבה כמ"ש ב"י משא"כ כשיש לפניך חסרון אונא בבירור ואתה צריך להשלימו בתשלומין ברור דשמא מיקרי שלא בדרי דאונא ואין כאן תשלומין של חסר וכן ראיתי לרש"ל שפסק בכאן לאיסור וההיא דסעיף ד' להיתר. כתב מו"ח ז"ל מעשה בא לפני כמה פעמים בריאה שלא היה לה שום ריעותא אלא שבצד שמאל יצאה בליטה מן האומה בחיתוך שבין אונא לאומא ממש במקום שדרך הורדא להיות עומד כנגדו בצד ימין ואותה בליטה היה לו כיס גמור ומונח בתוכו ולא ה"ל צורת ורד כלל וגם היתה שוה לאומא מצד קמא כאילו היתה חתוכה ממנו והכשרתיה משום דאותה בליטה לא היתה לא יתרת מקמא ולא ורדא יתירה ואין כאן ריעותא אלא מצד כיס ואין סברא לאוסרה מכח זה כו' עכ"ל ונ"ל אפילו היה לה צורת ורד אין כאן איסור דמקרי יתרת מקמא וכשר לדידן ואין איסור שני ורדות אלא כששניהם עומדים בימין במקומם הראוי ואין כעובי אצבע ביניה' כמ"ש לעיל והכיס ודאי אין אוסר דלא מצינו שבשביל כיס יתר יהי' איסור כנ"ל: כתב רש"ל אם היתרת של הריאה עומדת בדרי דאונא ונוטה לצד חוץ מעט על ידי הנפיחה טרפה אבל הב"י כ' דדוקא בליטה יתרה אבל נתעקמה והולכת למעלה כשר כיון שמושרשת במקום כשר:
**** ENGLISH ****
Below the varda is called not in the row of onot. At first glance one should forbid also that of seif 4 for this reason; but Rama's language does not imply any distinction; and the reason appears: there it is kosher either way — if it were in the row of onot it is obviously kosher; and if not in the row of onot, because the varda divides and completes the count of the onot, nevertheless kosher because it is not on its back, as Beit Yosef wrote; unlike when there is clearly a missing ona before you and you need to complete it with a clear replacement — perhaps it is called not in the row of onot and there is no valid replacement; and so I saw Maharshal ruled here to forbid and that of seif 4 to permit. My master may he live long wrote: a case came before him several times — a lung with no suspicious matter except on the left a protrusion from the ama at the cut between ona and ama exactly where the varda usually stands opposite on the right; that protrusion had a complete pouch, was placed in it, had no varda form at all, and was equal to the ama on the front as if cut from it; he permitted because that protrusion was neither extra from the front nor extra varda and there is no suspicious matter except from the pouch — end. And it appears to me even if it had varda form there is no prohibition, for it is called extra from the front and kosher for us; prohibition of two vardot is only when both stand on the right in their proper place and are not like finger-thickness between them, as written above; and the pouch certainly does not forbid, for we do not find that because of a pouch extra there is prohibition, as explained. Maharshal wrote: if the lung's extra stands in the row of onot and leans slightly outward through inflation — treifah; but Beit Yosef wrote: specifically protruding extra, but if bent and goes upward — kosher since rooted in a permitted place — end.
