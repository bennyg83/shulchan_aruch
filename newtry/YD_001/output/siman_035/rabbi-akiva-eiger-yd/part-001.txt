**** HEBREW ****
(שם סקי"ז) <b>אפי' עומרת היתרת בצד שמאל</b> היינו מקמא בצד שמאל אבל בדרא ממש בצד שמאל כשר. תב"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
It would be missing and treifah. Baer Heitev; and Me'ir Netivim siman 41 wrote: a lung that was divided in two, whether in length or width — one should not consider it a split, as long as the lung appears to the eye like the image of divided; and he again wrote there was extra pouch, and even though the varda does not touch this pouch — in this Rama permits; and even though it does not appear so from HaRav — nevertheless one may be lenient in great financial loss; except it may be said this part resembling divided is from the varda because somewhat it appears on that side the varda is not in the completeness of proper roundness, and there is room to doubt it was placed on the side of that extra pouch, for so it was when they inflated the lung those places adhere somewhat; and even though that part has no varda designation, nevertheless when there is a missing part of the varda and a separate extra pouch section, one should be stringent because of three suspicious matters, as explained in responsum siman 35 seif 36; nevertheless here, since even without combining the part there is a complete varda, there is no suspicious matter of missing, and also no suspicious matter of extra because that part has no varda designation at all — one may permit in great financial loss; and in that lung there was also permitted extra next to the split — there are not two suspicious matters adjacent, for healthy lung flesh separates them — this one alone and that one alone — end. And in my opinion it is not clear to say "resembles divided" if there is room to judge it as missing varda beneath — the wise one's eyes are in his head to distinguish between them; if it is this or that, there is no room to fall into doubt at all; and there where the piece in the place appears it has no connection to the name — if it is not relevant here, make it relevant to completing the varda; but when the eye sees it is a part from here, it does not appear to combine to the varda; and if there were no varda designation except the part — obviously we would not need to combine at all; and also in the substance of what he wrote: whatever appears to the eye as resembling divided is not considered a split — whence do we know this thing is divided and not split? And indeed regarding split versus divided, in my view it is explained well in my Beit Efrayim on treifos siman 35 and in my responsum Beit Efrayim Yoreh De'ah part. And see in responsum Pri Tevuah siman 40 regarding split from the front at the root — he wrote to forbid — and see there siman 70 and what I wrote on his words in my responsum Beit Efrayim; and there I clarified everything well with God's help.
