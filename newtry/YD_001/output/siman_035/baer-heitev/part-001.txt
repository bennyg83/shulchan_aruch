**** ENGLISH ****
Kosher. And Shach wrote: even for those who permit extra from the front, nevertheless for two vardot or missing or changed place — treifah. And the distinction by which we know which is extra varda and which is extra from the front: when it has a true varda designation, even if the extra stands on the left side — it is extra varda; but when it does not have a true varda designation in a matter that can be considered extra, one may permit since it does not stand in the place of the varda; and likewise even if it does not stand in its place and has no true varda designation but has a pouch by itself — it is recognizable that there are two vardot and treifah; but if it has no varda designation at all, even though it has a pouch — kosher. And Taz wrote: when two vardot are found — treifah; the reason is all extra is considered as removed; and accordingly it appears one should not declare treifah for two vardot except when both stand in the place where the varda should be and do not have a root like finger-thickness after inflation; nevertheless if you find an extra ona inside, even in the place where the varda should be, and it has no varda form — there is no disqualification here for two vardot, for the forms of the onot are not like a varda; and the same if two vardot are found, one on the right and one on the left — "as removed" does not apply since the extra does not stand in its proper place — see siman 55 seif 5. I found written in Knesset HaGedolah on koy that they call it boful and its way is to have two vardot, and so for most wild animals — and they are kosher; but for other laws they are like an animal regarding adhesion of vardot or varda designation and distance and inversion and all other treifos — only regarding extra of ayinunita we do not declare treifah in them. And Bach wrote: such a case came before him several times and he permitted; if it stands in the place of the varda itself I received from my master may he live long that they practice to declare treifah if it has some varda designation; but if it resembles an ona itself one may consider it extra from the front; and if it has one root — see above in this seif. And what Rama wrote regarding missing part of the varda that one should examine the ama of the left — there is a correction to right; and so in Torat Chaim, for if ayinunita is found on the left side — treifah; and Shach rejects his words — see there.

And the reason is in Shach: because it does not stand in the row of onot; even though we hold extra from the front is kosher; and accordingly even extra of an ona from the front does not complete; also one may declare treifah and say it does not complete, for if so it is missing varda and treifah — see Darkei Moshe daf 107. And Taz wrote s.k. 13: if extra is found in a bed above the ona adjacent to the spine inside the lung — it is kosher; and it appears to me treifah, for this is certainly included more in "on its back" mentioned in the Talmud (and he also wrote what Rama wrote regarding two vardot with one root that is like finger-thickness — even though there is no finger-thickness there; and a certain sage wrote: nevertheless we require the length of the root like a finger between the two vardot; and it appears to me the measure is the height of the root of both before they branch must be like a finger).

<b>נוהגים. </b> וכתב בש"ך ולא דמי למ"ש בסעיף ד' דאם נמצא בסוף שפולי אומא דכשירה דשאני התם דהאונות הם כתיקונן והוי כיתרת בדרי דאוני אבל בחסר אונא אחת אין בליטה זו משלמת החסרון וכתב הב"ח מעשה בא לידי כמה פעמים בריאה שלא היה לה שום ריעותא אלא בצד שמאל יצאה בליטה מן האומא בחיתוך שבין אונא לאומא ממש במקום שדרך הורדא להיות עומד כנגדה בצד ימין ואותה בליטה היה לו כיס גמור ומונח בתוכו ולא היה לו צורת ורד כלל <small>(וגם היה שוה לאומה בצד קמא)</small> כאילו היה חתוך ממנה והכשרתיה משום דאותה בליטה לא היה לו יתרת מקמא ולא ורדא יתירה ואין כאן ריעותא אלא מצד כיס ואין סברא לאוסרה מכח זה עכ"ל. וכתב עליו הט"ז שנ"ל אפי' היה לו צורת ורד אין כאן איסור דמקרי יתרת מקמא וכשר לדידן ואין איסור שני וורדות אלא כששניהם עומדים בימין במקומם הראוי ואין כעובי אצבע ביניהן והכיס וודאי אינו אוסר דלא מצינו שבשביל כיס יתר יהיה איסור <small>(ומיהו ק"ק שהרי כתב לעיל ואוסר בשני כיסין ואפשר דוקא אי קאי במקום הורדא ודו"ק)</small>. וכתב מהרש"ל אם היתרת של הריאה עומדת בדרי דאונה ונוטה לצד חוץ מעט ע"י הנפיחה טריפה אבל הב"י כתב דדוקא בליטה יתירה אבל נתעקמה והולכת למעלה כשר כיון שמושרשת במקום כשר עכ"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
Practice. And Shach wrote: it is not similar to what was written in seif 4 that if found at the end of the slopes of the ama it is kosher — it is different there, for the onot are as they should be and it is like extra in the row of onot; but for one missing ona this protrusion does not complete the deficiency; and Bach wrote: a case came before him several times — a lung with no suspicious matter except on the left a protrusion emerged from the ama at the cut between ona and ama exactly where the varda usually stands opposite on the right; and that protrusion had a complete pouch and was placed in it and had no varda form at all (and was equal to the ama on the front side) as if cut from it; and he permitted it because that protrusion was neither extra from the front nor extra varda and there is no suspicious matter except from the pouch, and there is no logic to forbid it for this — end. And Taz wrote on this: it appears to me even if it had varda form there is no prohibition here, for it is called extra from the front and kosher for us; and prohibition of two vardot is only when both stand on the right in their proper place and are not like finger-thickness between them; and the pouch certainly does not forbid, for we do not find that because of a pouch extra there is prohibition (however it is difficult, for he wrote above he forbids two pouches — and it is possible this refers specifically when it is in the place of the varda — examine). And Maharshal wrote: if the lung's extra stands in the row of onot and leans slightly outward through inflation — treifah; but Beit Yosef wrote: specifically protruding extra, but if bent and goes upward — kosher since rooted in a permitted place — end.
