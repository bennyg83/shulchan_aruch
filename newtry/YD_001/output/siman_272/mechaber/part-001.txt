**** ENGLISH ****
The law of the width of the pages. In it are 4 seifim. One does not make a Sefer Torah such that its length exceeds its circumference, nor its circumference exceeds its length (meaning: the string that surrounds all the sheets of the book, after they are sewn and rolled, should be long as its length) (Tur). And how much is its length? On gevil—six tefachim, which are twenty-four fingerbreadths in the width of the thumb of the hand; and on klaf, less or more, provided its length equals its circumference. And likewise if he made it on gevil less than six tefachim and made the writing smaller, or more than six and widened the writing until its length equals its circumference—this is as the mitzvah. (And the pole that is inside the book—some say it is counted toward the circumference) (Tur in the name of the Rosh).

**** ENGLISH ****
The length of each line is thirty letters, so that one can write "lemishpechoteichem" three times; and it should not be shorter than this so the page not appear like a letter, nor longer than this so one's eyes not wander in the writing (words of the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilchos Sefer Torah, halachah 4).

**** ENGLISH ****
One does not make a sheet of fewer than three pages, nor more than eight. If a sheet of nine pages came to him, he should not divide it so that there be three pages in one and six in the other, but rather four in one and five in the other. When does this apply? To all sheets in the book except the last one; but at the end of the book, even one verse—one makes it a page by itself and sews it with the other sheets.
