**** ENGLISH ****
Shusta — the view of many that it is geyl; and I wrote below siman 52 regarding one that fell to light that it is like kartei and tends to black — see there; and the acharonim elaborated on appearances: general rule — everything that tends to geyl, gold, etrog, and black — treifah; and appearance tending to kartei is a species of green like grass and the like — kosher.
