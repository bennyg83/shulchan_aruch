**** ENGLISH ****
The law of two doughs that do not have the measure. In it are 2 seifim. Two doughs, neither of which has the measure—if they touch one another until they adhere a little to one another, they combine if they are a species fit to combine as explained in siman 324. And if they do not adhere and they are in one basket—the basket combines them, even after they were baked and became bread. And if he placed them on a board that has no rim to spill—they do not combine. {Rama: Therefore when combining them in a vessel, he should be careful that nothing extend above the walls of the vessel—meaning each loaf or dough should be above the walls of the vessel (Tur).} And some say if he covers the bread with a cloth, it is considered like a vessel to combine them.
