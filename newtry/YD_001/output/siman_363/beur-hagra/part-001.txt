<b>(ליקוט) אין מלקטין כו'. </b>תה"א בשם א"ר ומסיים שם מקום שנהגו לעשות כן עושין מקום שנהגו שלא לעשות כן אין עושין וע"ז נאמר אל תסג גבול עולם ופי' שם דמ"ש מקום שנהגו כו' היינו כמ"ש בירושלמי לאחר עיכול הבשר וז"ש בס"ד מקום כו' (ע"כ):
**** ENGLISH ****
(Likut) We do not collect etc. Toras HaAdam in the name of Eliyah Rabbah, and he concludes there: a place where they were accustomed to do so, they do; a place where they were accustomed not to do so, they do not do. And regarding this it was said: "Do not move the ancient boundary." And he explained there that what is written "a place where they were accustomed" etc. is as it is written in the Yerushalmi: after the flesh has decomposed. And that is what he wrote in seif 4, "a place" etc. (end quote).
