**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(Shach seif katan 18) I challenged.</b> I challenged Derishah, but perhaps his intent is as follows. He means specifically sourdough taken from a dough, meaning from a large dough containing the minimum amount for challah, which had already become obligated in challah but challah had not yet been separated. By contrast, a small dough lacking the minimum amount, when mixed into something else, does not create tevel, since that dough itself was never obligated in challah. His wording is nevertheless very awkward, because he should not have distinguished between sourdough and dough. Sourdough from a small dough likewise does not create tevel, while ordinary dough from a large obligated dough does. He should instead have written: this applies specifically to material from a large dough, but material from a small dough that had never become obligated because it lacked the required amount does not create tevel. Perhaps there is a textual error in his words.
