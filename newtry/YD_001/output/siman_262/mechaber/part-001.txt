The time of circumcision for a healthy child, a sick one, and an androgynous. And it contains 7 seifim. **** ENGLISH ****
A sick child — we do not circumcise him until he recovers, and we wait for him from when he recovered from his illness seven days from time to time, and then we circumcise him. In what case is this stated? When his entire body was feverish and the like, which is an illness of the whole body; but if he was ill in one of his limbs, such as his eyes hurt him slightly — we do not wait seven days, but circumcise him immediately when he recovers from that limb's illness.

If immediately when the infant brought his head outside to the airlock three medium stars were seen, one may rely on them that it is night, even if it is the day after Shabbat; but if they waited afterward, if according to the delay it appears to them it was day when the head came out, they have only what their eyes see and circumcise on the ninth.
