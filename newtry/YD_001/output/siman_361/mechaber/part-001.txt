**** ENGLISH ****
Escorting the dead and cancellation of Torah study for escorting him. In it are 5 seifim. They cancel Torah study for taking out the dead. For one who teaches others — there is no limit; even if thousands are with him, they cancel for him. For one who read and reviewed — meaning he read and reviewed but has not yet taught students — if there are sixty myriads, he need not cancel for him. For one who neither read nor reviewed — once he has someone to occupy himself with him, he need not cancel for him; and this is when there are ten there {Rama: (And some say that nowadays they cancel by default, for there is no one in Israel now who is not in Scripture or Mishnah) (Beit Yosef in the name of Ritva ch. 2 of Ketubot, who so wrote in the name of Semag)}; and a woman — some say her law is like one who read and reviewed, and some say like one who did not read and reviewed; and so they practiced leniently for a woman and child. And they do not cancel Torah study for the dead when there is someone to occupy himself with all his needs — rather he occupies himself with Torah; and he need not go out to see whether all his needs are met, for once there is someone to act, his Torah study takes precedence; and schoolchildren never cancel at all.

**** ENGLISH ****
In what case? When they cancel Torah study to escort him at the time of taking him out; but all the time he lies awaiting burial they do not cancel Torah study for him — rather, if there are groups in the city so each occupies itself one day, that group whose day it is not is permitted both in study and in other work; and at the time of taking out they cancel from all other work and come to escort him, even if he neither read nor reviewed — for we do not distinguish except in canceling Torah study; but in other work, even if he neither read nor reviewed, all are idle and come to escort him. And if there are no groups in the city, all workers must cancel and occupy themselves with him until he is buried; but from Torah study they need not cancel except at the time of taking him out, as we said.
