**** ENGLISH ****To change. For since he said to the poor of the city immediately, those poor acquired it and it is as if the money came to their hand. Mahari"k wrote: two who agreed to give equally to charity and captives were redeemed; one wants to collect according to money and one claims they already agreed to give equally to charity, and redemption of captives is also included in charity — he ruled one should lean whether it is included in charity without specification; nevertheless in common speech it is not included, and in vows we follow custom, etc. The Taz wrote that in this he judged well because at the time of the agreement it did not enter their mind that redemption of captives would come to them, and it is like an uncommon coercion as stated in Choshen Mishpat siman 225. But from this he also derived another law that the Rama brought in siman 252 seif 1 regarding one who says "this coin for charity" that redemption of captives is not included, etc. — in this his words do not seem correct and he extends with many proofs to show redemption of captives is included in charity. Nachalat Kohen rejected his words and wrote it is not worthwhile to reject Mahari"k's words with mere arguments.
