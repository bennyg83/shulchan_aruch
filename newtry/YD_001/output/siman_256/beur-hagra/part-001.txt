**** ENGLISH ****And similarly regarding the gabbai, etc. His words are puzzling, for a gabbai is certainly forbidden to change it as stated in Bava Batra there from Marish who would do, etc., and in Arakhin there "once it came to the hand of the gabbai", etc. — I am not, etc., but rather it refers to when the community gave him authority, and as stated: since he listened to what Shmuel said to him, etc.

(Likkut) And similarly regarding the gabbai, etc. As stated in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Megillah halakhah 1: R. Chiya bar Abba went outside and they gave him perutot to distribute to orphans and widows; he went out and distributed to the rabbis — must he set aside others in their place? R. Bibi bar Mamal said he must set aside in their place; R. Zeira said he must set aside in their place; R. Ila said he need not set aside in their place; R. Yasa in the name of R. Elazar said he need not set aside in their place, etc. And similarly the Gaon explained what is stated in chapter 1 of Bava Batra "and to change it", etc. — that afterward he must give others in their place for that matter — and Avraham ben Shmuel included it among the 613 mitzvot; nevertheless the conclusion of Yerushalmi is that he need not set aside in their place (end quote).
