If the entire lung adheres to the wall — if there is no wound on the wall — treifah, and examination does not help for it, even if the whole thing adheres without any fenestration. And there is one who wrote that one who permits it is under the ban of excommunication and the vessels are forbidden (Rashbatz).

**** ENGLISH ****
If the uma and ona became sircha'd in one sircha to the wall — if the majority of the sircha is from the ona — kosher; and if the majority is from the uma — treifah, if there is no wound on the wall. And likewise an ona sircha'd in one sircha to the chest and to the wall — we follow the majority. And likewise an uma sircha'd in one sircha to the wall in which there is a wound, and the sircha is in the wound and outside it — if the majority is in the wound — kosher; and if not — treifah. And likewise if the uma became sircha'd in one sircha to the pericardium and to the wound on the wall — if the majority is in the wound on the wall — kosher; and if not — treifah. {Rama: A lung on which is found like a covering of milk and it is flesh in a mixture — they peel it off from above it; and if the lung beneath it is whole and beautiful — kosher; however one must examine the lung by inflation (Beit Yosef siman 38 in the name of Rokeach).}
