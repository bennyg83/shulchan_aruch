**** ENGLISH ****
The statement of Rava there on daf 47. (1) Rashi explains: because there is no sircha without a puncture, etc., and a little goes out little by little through the puncture and becomes a membrane; even though the puncture is sealed, the membrane that arose on account of a wound is not a [true] membrane, for in the end it will come apart; but when it is sircha-adherent in ksederan (aligned order), its fellow seals it and protects the puncture, and the membrane grows stronger. And Tosafos explained: that sircha is accustomed to come healthy without a puncture, because it draws all liquids, etc.; and that which is treifah when not in order — this is because in the end it will come apart and is considered like a puncture since in the end it will be punctured. And Beit Yosef wrote that thus appears the view of the Rambam there:

רמב"ם שם והרא"ש ורבינו ירוחם כתבו דאפילו לדעת האומרים שאין סירכה בלא נקב לפי שהסירכא הבאה מחמת נקב מחמת המשקה היוצא דרך הנקב הוא נעשה עב וחזק ונסרך אל המקום אשר יפגע בו אבל סירכא התלויה אינה אלא התפשטות לחות הריאה ואינה באה ממקום הנקב ולדעת התוספות לא חיישינן דלמא סרוכה היתה למקום אחר ונתפרקה דאם איתא באותה שעה היתה ניקבה:
**** ENGLISH ****
Rambam there, Rosh, and Rabbenu Yerucham wrote: even according to those who hold there is no sircha without a puncture — because sircha that comes on account of a puncture, on account of moisture going out through the puncture, becomes thick and strong and becomes sircha-adherent to whatever place it encounters; but dependent sircha is only spreading of moisture of the lung and does not come from a place of puncture. And according to Tosafos we are not concerned lest it was sircha-adherent to another place and detached — for if so, at that time it would have been punctured:
