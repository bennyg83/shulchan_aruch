(11) There: or apostate to idolatry, etc. — Maran in Beit Yosef wrote: Israelite apostate deals with apostate to the entire Torah or apostate to one mitzvah to anger; but apostate to one mitzvah out of appetite is not "removing the yoke." And informer is different, for it needed to be taught although only out of appetite, because it is very severe — see there. And so appears his view in Shulchan Aruch, who wrote "apostate to idolatry" — apostate to entire Torah — to teach us it deals with appetite; and similarly informer — although out of appetite, disqualifies — unlike what Ran z"l wrote in others' name that implies also for informer we require to anger — see there; this is not Maran's view. And "apostate to one mitzvah to anger" — Shulchan Aruch did not need to write it, since we hold generally that to anger is [like] a sect, as in Yoreh Deah siman 2 and siman 158, and is called apikoros; and we say nearby seif 4 that tefillin an apikoros wrote should be burned — if so, obviously disqualified as apostate to anger. Mateh Yehudah there. And see what acharonim wrote; no need to elaborate. And know: apostate to idolatry, or desecrating Shabbat in public, or apostate to entire Torah even aside from these two — his law is like a gentile, as in Yoreh Deah siman 2 seif 5 regarding slaughter; and the same regarding tefillin — from Shemen HaMor s.k. 3:

(24) Seif 4: Tefillin that an apikoros wrote, etc. — tefillin presumably are not written for idolatry, unlike a Torah scroll; see Yoreh Deah end of siman 281. Magen Avraham s.k. 8. And explanation of his words from Machazik Berachah: meaning here he brought two views — some say burn, some say bury; and in Torah scroll in Yoreh Deah stam burn.
