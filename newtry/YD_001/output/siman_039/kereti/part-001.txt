**** ENGLISH ****
Without relaxing the hands — many acharonim wrote warnings, but woe that they labored in vain: for in truth, if they press and it is [complete] sircha, all day it will not detach and cease; but they press and go until the sircha becomes thin, and then they push with a finger until it breaks, and afterward they detach the sircha from the lung — and on this basis they declare kosher. The sages of the generation sensed this in Prague and there was a great uproar against the slaughterers; but woe, flattery prevailed whereby some sages flattered and prevailed through authority without protesting a matter practiced as permitted from ancient days. My father-in-law the Gaon R' Yaakov Bek O'Brien was among those who supported the slaughterers' hands; afterward he printed this in his book, part 2 of Shevut Yaakov siman 60, and admitted the breach was improper, writing: "Did the sages of Israel close their eyes, Heaven forbid?" — only they lacked power to protest the destroyers, for many sages held their hands. Thus Torah departed and an unjust judgment emerged. At any rate, the God-fearing and soft-hearted in Prague did not eat from slaughter wherein there was concern for sircha; and so every God-fearing person trembling at His word should refrain from eating, for it is a matter of life — for false are those who say that when they press sircha it does not detach all day. Fortunate are you, inhabitants of the holy community Hamburg, whose meat is all from animals that were not pressed — and may it be sweet and not shameful for you:
