חוץ מן הריאה וכו' דהוי כמו מיעוט דשכיח ויש להבין הא דדייק הרא"ש בפ' אין צדין: מהא דאמר ר"ע מבית טביחת' בלי הפשט ונתוח דמותר לאכול מריאה בלי בדיקה וכן הוא בש"ע א"ח סי' תצ"ח ע"ש וקשה מנלן דלמא ודאי לידע כל י"ח טריפות צריך הפשט ונתוח לידע פרטי בהמה אבל לידע מומי הריאה א"צ הפשט כלל ומעשה בכל יום חותכין קצת מעור וחצי הכבד ומכניסין יד ובודקין ובהמה נשארה בלי הפשט כמה ימים בשלמא אם אמרינן אין חילוק בטריפות ניחא אבל אם יש לחלק בין שאר טריפות לטריפות הריאה לא ידעתי איך למדו מדברי ר"ע לשיטה זו וצ"ל דכך למד דהא דאמרינן דא"צ בדיקה משום דאזלינן בתר רובא אבל לר"מ דחיישינן למעוטי י"ל דצריך למיבדק וצ"ל דלא אזלינן בתר רובא וחיישינן לכך ור"ע ס"ל בבכורות דאזלינן בתר מיעוטי ועיין ספ"ק דמכות בתוס'. וא"כ מדר"ע בשאר ח"י טריפות יש לנו להחמיר בבדיק' כיון דאפשר ומ"מ ס"ל משום פסידא לא מחמירין לבדוק ומכ"ש לדידן בריאה דאזלינן בתר רובא דא"צ למיחש ואחד הוא לדידן בדיקת הריאה לר"ע דחייש למעוטי שאר ח"י טריפות וא"ש. אלא באמת איהו גופי' קשי' הא לר"מ אמרי' דלא אפשר וכ"כ רשב"א בחדושיו דלר"מ דוקא דלא אפשר אבל לרבנן דאזלי בתר רובא אפי' אפשר וא"כ לר"ע דס"ל כר"מ הא אפשר למבדק ודוחק לומר הואיל טריחא למבדק הוי לא אפשר וכן בדיקת הריאה ודוחק וצ"ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
Except for the lung, etc. — this is like a common minority. One must understand that which the Rosh was precise about in chapter Ein Tzadin: from that which R' Akiva said: from the slaughterhouse without flaying and cutting it is permitted to eat the lung without examination — and so in Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chaim siman 498 — see there. It is difficult: whence do we know? Perhaps certainly to know all 18 treifot one needs flaying and cutting to know the particulars of the animal, but to know flaws of the lung one does not need flaying at all — and it is an everyday occurrence that they cut a little of the hide and half the liver and insert a hand and examine, and the animal remains without flaying several days. Granted, if we say there is no distinction among treifot it is fine; but if one may distinguish between other treifot and lung treifot — I do not know how they learned from R' Akiva's words to this method. One must say they learned thus: that which we say examination is not required is because we follow the majority; but according to R' Meir, who is concerned for minorities — one may say examination is required. And one must say we do not follow the majority and are concerned for this; and R' Akiva holds in Bekhorot that we follow minorities. And see end of first chapter of Makhshirin in Tosafos. If so, from R' Akiva in the other 18 treifot we should be stringent in examination since it is possible; nevertheless he holds that on account of loss they are not stringent to examine — and all the more so for us regarding lung, where we follow the majority and need not be concerned — and it is one [law] for us: lung examination for R' Akiva who is concerned for minorities in the other 18 treifot. And it is difficult. But in truth it is difficult in itself: for according to R' Meir we say it is not possible; and likewise Rashba in his novellae: according to R' Meir specifically when it is not possible, but according to the Sages who follow the majority — even when possible. If so, for R' Akiva who holds like R' Meir — behold it is possible to examine; and it is strained to say that since it is burdensome to examine it is "not possible." And likewise lung examination — strained; requires study.

**** ENGLISH ****
The fence-breacher, etc. — one need not be precise from the words of Tosafos Beitzah daf 25b, s.v. orach, etc., who hold there is no prohibition once it was slaughtered it was permitted, only if afterward treifah is found we judge him as shogeg and not as ones who had no reason to hurry. And here they said "a snake may lie upon it" — and it is unnecessary: Tosafos deals with other treifot and not lung treifot, for we never heard of examining afterward, and there is no doubt until it is said from cutting the leg of one who hurries. Also difficult is what Tosafos challenged: perhaps "orach" refers to the other 18 treifot, and "cutting" refers to lung treifot — and one cannot say what does cutting do? A zav proves. Requires study. And behold, if the lung was lost and there is milk milked within three days — perhaps it is not like lost, for since you are concerned for the meat, what did the Sages accomplish in their enactment? Also it is safek sefeika: perhaps there was no ri'uta, and you will say it arose after milking. And one may side that Rashi challenged those stringent in lung examinations: "yesterday we ate from its milk" — to reconcile, it appears one may say in the Gemara Chullin he toiled to find proof that we follow the majority, and did not justify how we ate milk lest the animal was treifah — strained that we eat milk only after slaughtering the animal and examining, and it appears certainly all animals are included in safek, and it is clear the majority of animals are kosher — and behold this is like nine stores where treifot are unknown, and so one separates from the majority; one separates only because everything is in the house of safek it is fixed, but milk is separated as explained below siman 110 regarding an egg that is like separation; therefore it was difficult from milk. Also Rashi's difficulty is resolved: it is separation and from the majority one separates; therefore one may be lenient. Examine well.

שלא כסדרן טריפה הש"ך בס"ק י' הביא דעת רש"י וסייעתו דס"ל אין סרכה בלי נקב רק כסדרן הסרכה סותמת ודעת החולקים דיש סרכה בלי נקב רק סופו לפרוק ולנקוב וכסדרן אין סופו לפרוק והדברים מבוארים באריכות בגמ' דף מ"ו ובתוס' ד"ה היינו רביתא ע"ש וצריך לבאר מה נ"מ בין שני דיעות הללו סוף כל סוף כסדרן כשר שלא כסדרן טריפה ונראה דאם היה סירכא כסדרן וקפלוהו לסרכה ולגליד וקרום ואח"כ נמצאת ריאה נקובה לדעת רש"י כשרה דהא ידעינן דיש כאן נקב רק הסירכה סותם ובכסדרן הוי סתימה מעליא משא"כ לתו' אין סרכה סותם נקב כלל ולא הוי סתימה כי הוי קרום מחמת מכה רק סתם סרכה הוא בלי נקב ובכסדרן אין סופו להתפרק וא"כ בנמצא נקב הרי זה טריפה וכן לפי מה דס"ל לרוב פוסקים לקמן סי' פ"א דאין סרכה פחות מג' ימים והחלב תוך ג' ימים טריפה וקצת פוסקים חולקים תלי' בהנ"ל דאם הי' נקב רק העלה סרכה וקרום לסתום פשיטא דצריך לזה ג' ימים כדאמרי' הוגלד פי מכה דהיינו שעלה גליד על מכה ודאי בעי ג' ימים ואף בנקב שהוא המכה והעלתה גליד וקרום הסרכה לסתום פשיטא דצריך לזה ג' ימים אבל אי סרכה כבלי נקב ואין כאן מהכלל רק להתפשטות הליחות נסרך למקום אחר ושם יתפרק זה סגי ביום יומיים ואין מוחלט שצריך ג' ימים וא"כ נ"מ טובי ודו"ק:
**** ENGLISH ****
Not in order is treifah. Shach in s.k. 10 brought the view of Rashi and his supporters who hold there is no sircha without a puncture, only in order the sircha seals; and the view of those who disagree that there is sircha without a puncture, only in the end it will detach and be punctured, and in order it will not in the end detach. The matters are explained at length in the Gemara daf 46 and in Tosafos s.v. hainu revita — see there. One must explain the practical difference between these two views: after all, in order is kosher, not in order treifah. And it appears: if there was sircha in order and they folded it to sircha and scab and membrane, and afterward a punctured lung was found — according to Rashi it is kosher, for we know there is a puncture here, only the sircha seals; and in order there is superior sealing, not so according to Tosafos: sircha does not seal a puncture at all and it is not sealing, for it is membrane on account of a wound — only plain sircha is without puncture; and in order it has no end to detach — if so, when a puncture is found, behold it is treifah. And likewise according to what most poskim hold below siman 81: there is no sircha less than three days, and milk within three days is treifah — and some poskim disagree, depending on the above: if there was a puncture, only a scab and membrane arose to seal — obviously three days are required, as they say "the mouth of the wound scabbed over," meaning a scab arose on the wound and certainly requires three days; and even in a puncture that is the wound and it raised a scab and membrane and sircha to seal — obviously three days are required. But if sircha as without puncture and there is nothing in the general rule except spreading of moisture that became sircha-adherent elsewhere and there it will detach — two days suffice, and it is not absolute that three days are required. If so, there is a good practical difference. Examine well.

Since both came at once — see below siman 127 where Shach brought Ritva's view: when we say thus in matters of forbidden relations and similar that require two — if they believe the one who came first, he is like two; but in other prohibitions where we hold one witness is believed in prohibitions — he has no greater credibility than one witness, even if he came first; requires study. However, regarding one witness who says she became impure in Sotah and one witness says I saw the adulterer — the Rambam rules: specifically regarding valid testimony, when he comes first he is like two, but not when a woman comes first; and Lechem HaMishneh left it requires study. And one must say: since the verse is superfluous to validate one witness — the verse is unnecessary, for the general rule is one witness is believed in prohibitions. And one must say the Torah intentionally used a verse to teach that one witness in this matter is like two, since the Torah specified explicitly; otherwise it would be superfluous. But this is regarding a valid witness; women and similar — were it not that the verse expanded them, they would not be believed at all. And it is enough that the verse expanded them to be believed, but not to be believed like two. If so, one may say regarding two who say he died — the law is that nothing of forbidden relations is less than two, only the Sages equated them to two; if so, when a woman comes first she is believed like two. But regarding one witness and Sotah that require two — the Torah equated him to be like two since a verse was needed; therefore when he comes first his words stand — not so invalid testimony, where the verse taught us to be like a valid witness but not like two; he is only like any valid witness believed in a prohibition, and the law returns to Ritva's reasoning and we do not say he is like two. Examine well — and this is clear; therefore since the Rambam agrees to Ritva, certainly one should be stringent as he says, even if one witness came first and said "in order" and afterward the second contradicted him — nevertheless there is safek.
