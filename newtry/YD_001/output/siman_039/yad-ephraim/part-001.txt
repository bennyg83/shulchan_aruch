<b>סרוחה היתה.</b> עבה"ט ועי' בספרי שו"ת ב"א סימן ט"ז פרה שנשחטה ונמצא בה סרכא שאוסרים החלב שתוך ג' ימי' הנך ג' ימים חשבי' להו מעל"ע כיון שהענין טבעי שאין פי המכה נגלד אלא במשך זמן של ג' ימים וא"א לתת הדבר לשיעורין שבזו נגלד במשך נו"ן שעות ולזו במשך ע"ב שעות. וע"ש שכתבתי לחלק בין הך דהכא ליולדת לענין תענית יוה"כ שאין מונין מעל"ע כמ"ש בא"ח סי' תרי"ז והבאתי שם מכמה מקומות הדומים לזה ע"ש ועי' בספרי סימן כ"ג בחלב שנחלבה מבהמה שנטרפה מסרכא עם בועא וגם היה קמט בריאה והחלב שנחלבה יום א' לפני השחיטה נתערב בתוך חלב שנחלב מארבע בהמות כשרות ונשתמש מחלב התערובות הנ"ל בכלים שונים לכתחלה אין להורות היתר ובפרט שלפי הנראה אין בזה הפ"מ והחלב ימכור לעובד כוכבים והכלים שאפשר להגעילם יגעול ואם יש הפ"מ מחמת הכלים שא"א להגעילם ושימשו בהם בחמים של כ"ר עכ"פ ישהה אותם מעל"ע. ואם כבר יצא בהיתר יש לסמוך על מ"ש בפ"מ סי' פ"א להתיר בהפ"מ בלא נודע ע"ש:

It was sircha-adherent. Baer Heitev; and see in my responsa Beit Avraham siman 16: a cow that was slaughtered and sircha was found in it — they forbid milk within three days; those three days are counted retroactively, since the matter is natural that the mouth of the wound does not scab except over three days, and one cannot give the matter to measures that in this one it scabbed in 50 hours and in this one in 72 hours. And see there what I wrote to distinguish between this case and a woman who gave birth regarding Yom Kippur fast, where we do not count retroactively, as written in Orach Chaim siman 617 — I brought there several similar places — see there. And see in my book siman 23: milk that was milked from an animal that became treifah from sircha together with a boil, and there was also a wrinkle in the lung, and milk milked one day before slaughter was mixed into milk milked from four kosher animals and they used the aforementioned mixture milk in various utensils l'chatchila — one may not rule permissibility, and especially since it appears there is no great financial loss — sell the milk to a non-Jew and kasher utensils that can be kashered; and if there is great financial loss on account of utensils that cannot be kashered and were used with kli rishon hot water — at least let them stand retroactively. And if it already went out b'heter, one may rely on what Peri Megadim wrote siman 81 to permit with great financial loss without notification — see there:

And it is simple that in ksederan below half there is no distinction between animal and chayah, kids, lambs, and tender calves — in all of them one may permit in such a case. And see Har HaKarmel siman 1. **** ENGLISH ****
However, if the veredah, etc. And see in my book responsa Beit Avraham siman 3: that which they declare kosher when the veredah stuck at its root — specifically when one knows clearly that only the root stuck alone; but when it is not clear that it is the root alone — one should be stringent — see there. And see there further siman 9, where he wrote that Rama meant: if the whole [veredah] is stuck it is treifah; only if the adhesion is not over the entire base of the veredah but only at its root — the place where it begins to grow, at its connection to the ona — meaning if it sticks at the bottom of the pocket at its end, i.e., its lower part specified at the place of connection of the veredah between the cut of the ona and the uma. And the measure of the length of the adhesion — it appears to me to be lenient from the beginning of their connection until the place where it begins to round and take the form of a veredah; until that place the name "root of the veredah" applies, for there is its place of growth and increase, in that the root is stuck with the pocket — see there:

**** ENGLISH ****
Sircha. See Har HaKarmel siman 2, where he wrote: when the middle lobe is large, long and thick, and sircha stuck going out from it to the heart fat and passed by reduction — in a place where the custom is not to declare treifah, one may declare kosher with great financial loss. And it does not appear to me in this that reduction is a great leniency; if so, one may say it is treifah from two ri'utot, attributing that even the largeness of the middle lobe is not natural but illness caused it to swell and enlarge; and likewise if the sircha is long and thick, one may say illness caused it to shrink, and the stuck sircha indicates this — and all the more according to what Taz and Shach wrote in the name of Maharshal himself: 24 illnesses one must declare treifah for these alternatives. And what [they] say to call this illness lenient, that sircha by reduction is a different illness — for sircha is always common, etc. And in Machazik Berachah these words are muktzeh from the mind, as acharonim explain; and what he doubted on Bach's words to support the law of treifah from two ri'utot from the beginning of Niddah — he wrote to distinguish between things that exist, etc.:

**** ENGLISH ****
Rather mere saliva. Baer Heitev; and see in my book responsa Beit Avraham siman 24: regarding what some practice to inflate the lung before removing sircha to see if it rises in inflation — in my humble opinion there is no need, for if the sircha rises in inflation, after all the membrane is punctured and it is obvious it will bubble after removing the sircha; and one may say that after removal one no longer recognizes the exact place where the sircha stood, because in the reduction they practice they absorb much. That is: since in any case examination by reduction is required and they rely on reduction, examination for bubbling is only as a stringency, as Taz wrote — this suffices. And also the place of the sircha always remains recognizable and known to the examiner, and he can aim to examine that place well whether it bubbles there; therefore one need not [inflate first] except where they are accustomed — let them hold their custom — see there:

Baer Heitev; and see in my book responsa Beit Avraham siman 12: a slaughterer who inserted his hand inside to examine and felt sircha on the wall and detached it from its place of adhesion to the wall and left it there until he would examine it by reduction as is customary; afterward the arbitrator inserted his hand to remove the lung and they searched for the sircha and did not find it at all; they examined in lukewarm water the entire area of the ona in that place and it did not bubble at all. According to the questioner, the arbitrator is not suspected of transferring sircha — only that this one is a commoner and did not know with him when inserting his hand how weak the adhesion was; if so, it did not pass from the lung through inserting his hand even with force, since it was already cut from the wall and the sircha did not impede him — that which is said his insertion caused it is only if he intentionally sought to uproot, and he is not suspected of this; therefore through touching via passing his hand it became detached. <b>ע"י מעוך ומשמוש.</b> עבה"ט ועי' בספרי שהכל כמנהג המדינה. וכ' בפמ"א ח"ב סי' פ"ט שיש מקומות בין הספרדים שמנהגם להכשיר בסירכא לתוך הדופן לחתוך בסירכא ולבדוק בנפיחה הוא מנהג בטעות ומכ"ש בסירכא שלא כסדרן בריאה עצמו שלא עלה על לב שום פוסק להתיר יש למחות בידם ואם לא ישמעו אסור לאשכנזים לאכול משחיטתם ובדיקתם וצריך להרחיק אף מכליהם ע"ש ועי' בסי' קצ"ב שכתב בבילגראד שהיה שם קהל ספרדים משנים קדמוניות ונתישבו שם אשכנזים שלא היה להם דעתם לחזור יש להם לתפוס מנהגם אפילו לקולא אך עכשיו שקהל אשכנזים היו עם קדוש בפני עצמן ינהגו כמנהגם. ואם יזדמן סעודות מצוה צריך בעל הסעודה להודיע שאירע דבר שהם נוהגין לקולא כגון בענין ורדא וכה"ג וכל א' ממקהלות יחזיקו במנהגם בין לקולא או לחומרא והם ממש שני ב"ד בעיר אחת דלא חיישינן משום לא תתגודדו ע"ש וכ' בשבו"י ח"ב סי' סמ"ך בסירכא עוברת ע"י משמוש וקודם הנפיחה נתקרע או נאבדה היה נראה להכשיר ובפרט בהפ"מ וכ"כ חב"י ח"ב סי' מ"ב וכן נ"ל במ"ש הט"ז דהבדיקה אינה אלא לחומרא ומ"מ אין להקל אלא כשנתמעך הסירכא בין אצבעותיו ע"י משמוש בנחת כמ"ש הרא"ש ואז הבדיקה חומרא. אכן במקומות שמקילין וממעכין בחוזק קצת וגם בק"ק פרא"ג שהבודקים ממעכין בסירכא ובריאה עד שהסירכא נקלף מהריא' לגמרי כי סירכא גמורה נקלף מהריא' לגמרי כי סירכא גמורה אינה נקלפת כו' והעלימו חכמי הדור עיניהם וא"כ אם תמעך קצת ע"כ שמעשה הנפיחה עיקר. ואם ניקול בנקרעת או נאבדה באונס הו"ל תרי קולי דסתרי אהדדי וחלילה להקל בזה וכן בנמצא עצם על הריאה צריך נפיחה מדינא ואין להקל בלא נפיחה אליבא דכ"ע ע"ש ועי' בת"ש סי' ל"ו שכתב ג"כ להטריף בנאבדה בלא נפיחה. ובספרי בית אפרים העליתי שדבריו הם מחוורים כשמלה ע"ש וכתב עוד שם בעצם שנמצא על הריא' וראינו שהיא מצלעות. הנה אף שבט"ז אוסר בפירוש ושחיטת אחרוני' מתירים וכן הסברא נותנת כיון שמונח שם תמיד לא חיישינן לנקיבת שאר אברים גם הריאה אולי היתה עדיין תחובה בה שהבהמה בחייה נפוחה ודובקת עצמה בצלעות. ודוקא שהריאה תחתיה יפה בלי ריעותא ועלתה בנפיחה. ומ"מ אין להתיר כי אם בבהמ' גדולה של ישראל שהוא הפ"מ ועי' בספרי בית אפרים סי' ל"ט ס"ק ו':

By reduction and rubbing. Baer Heitev; and see in my book that everything follows the custom of the country. And Pri Megadim wrote, part 2 siman 89: there are places among Sephardim whose custom is to declare kosher sircha into the wall, cutting the sircha and checking by inflation — this is mistaken custom; and all the more sircha not in order on the lung itself — no posek ever thought to permit; one must protest against them, and if they do not listen it is forbidden for Ashkenazim to eat from their slaughter and examination, and one must distance even from their utensils — see there. And see siman 192 where he wrote: in Belgrade there was an ancient Sephardic community and Ashkenazim settled there who did not wish to return — they may adopt their custom even leniently; but now that Ashkenazim are a holy community unto themselves, they practice their custom. And if a seudat mitzvah occurs, the host must announce that something occurred whereby they practice leniently, such as regarding veredah and the like; and each community holds its custom, lenient or stringent — and they are literally two courts in one city, and we are not concerned for "do not cut yourselves." See Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 60: sircha that passes by rubbing and before inflation was torn or lost — it appeared to declare kosher, especially with great financial loss; and so Kaf HaChayim part 2 siman 42. And so it appears to me in what Taz wrote that examination is only as stringency; nevertheless one may not be lenient except when the sircha was pressed between his fingers by gentle rubbing, as the Rosh wrote — then examination is stringency. However, in places where they are lenient and press somewhat forcefully, and also in Prague where examiners press sircha and lung until the sircha peels completely from the lung — for complete sircha does not peel completely from the lung, etc. — the sages of the generation closed their eyes; if so, when it is pressed somewhat, the act of inflation is primary. And if it was peeled and torn or lost under duress — behold two leniencies that contradict each other; Heaven forbid to be lenient in this. And likewise when a bone is found on the lung, inflation is required by law and one may not be lenient without inflation according to all — see there. And see in Teshuvot VeHanhagot siman 36, who also wrote to declare treifah when lost without inflation. In my book Beit Avraham I raised that his words are broken as a word — see there. And he wrote further there regarding a bone found on the lung and we saw it is from the ribs: behold, even though Taz forbids explicitly and latter slaughterers permit, and reason supports: since it lies there always we are not concerned for puncture of other organs — perhaps the lung was still embedded in it while the animal lived, bloated and sticking to the ribs. And specifically when the lung beneath is fine without ri'uta and rose in inflation. Nevertheless one may not permit except in a large animal of Israel where there is great financial loss.
