**** ENGLISH ****
Salt absorbed from blood — such as from salting meat — and they placed it in a pot, or placed unsalted meat in a pot without rinsing — if there is sixty against the salt, it is permitted: {Rama: Even though the salt still imparts taste in the pot, since the salt is not forbidden from itself but because of blood in it — for wherever the forbidding agent cannot go, the forbidden does not forbid more than its forbidding agent (and see above siman 69 in Tur and Hagahot ShaDa and ShaDa):}
