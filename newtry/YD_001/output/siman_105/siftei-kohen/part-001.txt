["And some are lenient regarding what is outside the brine. From the rabbi's words in Turei Zahav general rule 22 section 4 it appears this is primary. And so Rosh and Rashba in chapter 105 regarding meat salted in an unperforated vessel; and even a permitted piece inside the brine that is fatty; and so Taz siman 69 section 18 and other poskim. And we forbid there meat salted in an unperforated vessel even what is outside the brine — but this was explained there s.k. 88: the reason is because when salted in an unperforated vessel the blood spread from place to place — see there. And perhaps for this the rabbi hinted in Bedek Malkhut; but certainly if the prohibition is fatty, even what is outside the brine is forbidden, as the poskim imply there; and so Tur at the end of this section; and Orach Chayyim general rule 30 section 10; and general rule 10. Therefore it would appear to forbid for us what is outside the pickling, since we do not distinguish between lean and fatty as below in section 5 and section 9 in hagahah — unless one might say the rabbi should not write a leniency here since for us all forbid; and all the more he should not rely on Turei Zahav's lenient reasoning as primary; and furthermore in section 70 he wrote that meat which fell into brine — what is outside is permitted; and Orach Chayyim also holds we do not distinguish lean from fatty, yet in pickling he permits what is outside; therefore it appears that even though we do not distinguish, this applies only in salting and roasting and similar cases, but we are not so stringent in all respects; and so I wrote in s.k. 17 — see there:"]
