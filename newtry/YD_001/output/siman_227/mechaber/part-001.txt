**** ENGLISH ****
The law of one who forbade himself from all Israel; it contains three se'ifim. One who forbade himself from all Israel — he is forbidden to buy from them and sell to them a middle sale, in which there is benefit for buyer and seller; rather he buys for more and sells for less. And if he forbade his benefit upon them — he buys from them for less and sells for more, if they heed him. And a sharp sale is the buyer's benefit and not the seller's benefit; therefore, if he forbade himself from them — he buys from them for more and sells to them equally; and if he forbade them upon him — he sells to them for more, if they heed him, and buys from them equally. And a sale thrown in his face is the seller's benefit and not the buyer's benefit; therefore, if he forbade himself from them — he sells to them for less and buys from them equally; and if he forbade them upon him — he buys from them for less, if they heed him, and sells to them equally:
