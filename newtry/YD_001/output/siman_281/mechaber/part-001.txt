**** ENGLISH ****
Who is fit to write a Sefer Torah, and if the scribe said he did not write the divine Names for their sake. In it are 8 seifim. A Sefer Torah that an apikorus wrote—one burns it. That a Canaanite wrote—one puts it in genizah. Found in the hand of an apikorus and it is not known who wrote it—genizah. Found in the hand of a Canaanite and it is not known who wrote it—some validate it; and some say genizah. And the public is obligated to buy it from him for its value, or a little more, and put it in genizah so he not treat it lightly. And if he wishes to raise its price much beyond its value, they leave it in his hand. And if Canaanites were presumed to plunder Israel's books, we presume those books were theirs and read from them.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="10"></i>ס"ת <i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="11"></i>שאמר הסופר לאחר שיצא מידו לא כתבתי האזכרות לשמן אינו נאמן לפסלו אבל נאמן הוא להפסיד כל שכרו ולמה אינו נאמן לפסלו שמא לא נתכוון אלא להפסיד על הלוקח או על זה ששכרו ודומה שאינו מפסיד באמירה זו אלא שכר האזכרות <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="11"></i>לפיכך אם אמר ספר תורה זה עורות שלו אינם מעובדים לשמן <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="9"></i><i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="12"></i>מתוך שנאמן להפסיד <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="5"></i>שכרו נאמן לפסלו שהכל יודעים שאם אין העורות מעובדין לשמן אין לו שכר כלל: <small>הגה <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="5"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="4"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="6"></i>מיהו כשכר חומש בעלמא חייב לשלם לו דהא מכל מקום לא גרע מחומש (מרדכי פ' הניזקין)</small>:
**** ENGLISH ****
A Sefer Torah regarding which the scribe said after it left his hand, "I did not write the divine Names for their sake"—he is not believed to invalidate it; but he is believed to forfeit all his wages. And why is he not believed to invalidate it? Perhaps he intended only to cause loss to the buyer or to the one who hired him; and it seems he does not forfeit by this statement except the wages of the divine Names. Therefore, if he said, "This Sefer Torah—its hides are not processed for their sake," since he is believed to forfeit his wages, he is believed to invalidate it, for everyone knows that if the hides are not processed for their sake he has no wages at all. {Rama: Nevertheless, as the wages of a chumash in general he is obligated to pay him, for in any case he did not fall short of a chumash (Mordechai, chapter HaNizakin).}
