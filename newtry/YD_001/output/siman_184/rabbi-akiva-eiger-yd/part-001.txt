(בא"ד) וסתה כל היום וודאי. לפענ"ד ערבך ערבא צריך דבפשוטו י"ל דגם בזה א"צ לפרוש בלילה שקודם דחכמים לא אסרו רק אותו היום שהיה בו הראיה. הן שהיה הראיה זמן מעט הן שהיה הראיה כל היום:
**** ENGLISH ****
(In the same passage) Her veset is certainly the entire day. In my humble opinion, this proof itself requires proof. According to the plain understanding, one may say that even in this case she need not separate during the preceding night, for the Sages prohibited only the day on which she saw, whether the sighting lasted a short time or the entire day.

(סעיף ג') שלא קבעתו ג' פעמים. בט"ז לקמן (סימן קפ"ט סקמ"ג) העלה דכל שראתה ג"פ אף דלא קבעתו חוששת. והש"ך שם בנקה"כ חלק דבעי' דוקא קבעתו:
**** ENGLISH ****
(Se'if 3) She did not establish it three times. Turei Zahav below, siman 189, subsection 43, concludes that whenever she saw three times, she must be concerned for the pattern even if she did not establish it as a fixed veset. Siftei Kohen there, in Nekudot HaKesef, disagrees and requires that she specifically establish it.

**** HEBREW ****
(ש"ך ס"ק ל"ד) שיעור שתוכל לספור ולטבול. לענ"ד י"ל באם היה הוסת המורכב לקפיצה מכ"ה לכ"ה והבעל שהה עד שעבר ל"ג דשריא מטעם ס"ס ספק לא ראתה כלל את"ל דראתה שמא קפצה ביום כ"ה וראתה והגיע עתה זמן טבילה:
**** ENGLISH ****
(Siftei Kohen, subsection 34) Enough time for her to count and immerse. In my humble opinion, one may say that if her compound veset involving jumping occurred every twenty-five days, and her husband remained away until thirty-three days had passed, she is permitted because of a double doubt. Perhaps she did not see at all. Even if she did see, perhaps she jumped and saw on the twenty-fifth day, and the time at which she could immerse has now arrived.
