**** ENGLISH ****
That one must separate from a woman one “onah” before her veset; it contains 12 se’ifim. Most women have vesatos (meaning: a fixed time, common among women) to see at a known time — for example, from 20 to 20 days, or from 30 to 30 days. And every woman who has a fixed veset: one may have relations with her not at the time of her veset, and she does not need a bedikah before tashmish (Rambam). {Rama: She also should not be stringent to check herself, neither before tashmish nor after tashmish, so that his heart not knock and he separate (Tur and Mordechai, beginning of Hilchos Niddah, and most poskim).} But not at the time of tashmish, whoever increases checking, behold this is praiseworthy.

<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="26"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="19"></i>היה לה וסת לימים <small>(טור בשם רשב"א) </small>ולוסת <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="32"></i><i data-commentator="Chiddushei Hilkhot Niddah" data-order="14"></i>מוסתות הגוף <small>(ב"י בשם הגהות מיי' פ"ד בשם רמב"ן) </small>כגון <i data-commentator="Torat HaShlamim" data-order="24"></i>קפיצה <i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="28"></i>וכיוצא בה כיון שהוסת תלוי במעשה <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="33"></i>אימור לא <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="17"></i>קפצה ולא ראתה <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="34"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="27"></i>אבל חוששת לעונה <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="18"></i>בינונית שהיא ל' יום <small>(ב"י בשם רשב"א וטור לקמן סי' קפ"ט):</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
If she had a veset dependent on days (Tur in the name of Rashba), and also a veset of body-vesatos (Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 4, in the name of Ramban) — for example, jumping and similar — since the veset depends on an act, we can say that perhaps she did not jump and did not see; but she is concerned for the “onah beinonis,” which is 30 days (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba; and the Tur below in siman 189).
