(Siman 184, Taz seif 2) It is surprising to be stringent, etc. — In Siftei Kohen I wrote this is also Ramban's view from Gemara Niddah 63 — Rava like R. Yehuda: if she sees at day's beginning or middle, separate entire day; end of night — entire night. R. Yoshiah: warn to separate near veset — how much? Rava: one onah. Not a different onah — Rava said one onah. If no fixed hour, entire day is veset time; separate one onah before — if day veset, entire preceding night. Rava's general rule: separate one onah near veset. Without fixed hour — "another onah"; with fixed hour — only that onah. Hagudah: nowadays no fixed hours — separate slightly before and after veset. Aviassaf's twelve hours for dam betulim differs — seasonal hours in Tevet/Tammuz. Gemara and poskim: if by day, separate entire day; if by night, entire night — halachic twelve-hour day/night, not seasonal borrowing. This is clear.
