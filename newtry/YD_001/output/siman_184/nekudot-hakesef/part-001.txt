**** ENGLISH ****(Siman 184, Turei Zahav seif 2) And it is astonishing to be stringent, etc. In Siftei Kohen small seif 7 I wrote that thus is also the opinion of the Ramban and it is proven in the Talmud; I abbreviated the matter, and in my books I explained the matters — for thus it is proven from what is said in the Talmud (Niddah 63b): why did Rava rule like Rabbi Yehudah, that if she sees at the beginning of the day or in the middle of the day she must separate the entire day, and at the end of night the entire night; and did Rava say thus? But Rabbi Yoshiyah said: a warning to Israelites that they separate from their wives near their veset; and how much did Rava say onah — what, not another onah (Rashi explained: if her veset is by day she is forbidden the entire day and the entire night before it)? No — that onah. It is difficult — from where would the thought come to say another onah, for behold Rava said onah; and if so it should have said two onot; rather certainly the Talmud holds that if she has no fixed hour in the day, the entire day is called her veset.
