**** ENGLISH ****He had relations with her not at her veset time. And the Mechaber who ruled anonymously here and in seif 11 appears he holds the main reasoning that the Talmud distinguishes in Niddah 12a between awake and asleep — because asleep she is not fit for taharot, unlike awake who is fit for taharot; therefore more bedikah is required. And so Rashi explained there for one explanation. And if so for us where there are no taharot — if so, the distinction between awake or asleep is no longer relevant — and it is simple.
