<b>אפילו עשו הקהל שבת. </b>כי ההיא תוספת לא שייך לענין נדה וצריך כ"ד שעות וכן איתא בפרק נערה (כתובות דף מ"ז ע"א) בתוספות ד"ה דמסר לה בשבתות ויום טוב וכן יש ראיה מספירת עומר דלא חשבינן ללילה אפילו בע"ש דתוספת שבת דאורייתא וכן מאכיל' מצה ופסח וסוכה עכ"ל האגור בשם מהרי"ל משמע מדבריו ומכל הלין ראיות דאע"פ שהתפללה היא ג"כ ועשתה שבת מותר (וכן דעת אחד מהגדולים בתשובה שהביא הת"ה שם דאפילו התפללה היא ערבית מותר עי"ש בת"ה) ולפי זה מה שכ' הרב דאפי' עשו הקהל שבת ה"ק אפילו עשו הקהל גם כן שבת וגם היא והגאון אמ"ו ז"ל לא כתב כן בתשובה אלא כתב דלהרב אם התפללה היא ערבית אין להקל אפי' דיעבד כדלקמן ס"ס שע"ה וע"ש (וכ"נ דעת מהרש"ל בתשובה סימן ע"ב שהבאתי לקמן ס"ק י"ט) ש"מ מבואר מהאגור שהבאתי דאף להמקילים היינו עד כ"ד שעות וא"כ כשהיום גדול כל כך חשבינן יום ולילה שוים וכמש"ל סי' קפ"ד ס"ק ז') אבל בתר הכי לכ"ע אסור:
**** ENGLISH ****Even if the congregation accepted Shabbat. For that addition does not relate to niddah matters and requires twenty-four hours; and so it states in chapter HaNa'arah (Ketubot page 47a) in Tosafot s.v. demasar lah on Shabbatot and Yom Tov; and there is proof from counting the omer that we do not consider night even on erev Shabbat, for tosefet Shabbat is from Torah; and likewise eating matzah on Pesach and sukkah end quote — the Agur in name of Maharil; from his words and from all these proofs it appears that even though she also prayed and accepted Shabbat it is permitted (and so is the view of one of the great ones in a responsum that Tashbetz brought there, that even if she prayed Maariv it is permitted — see there in Tashbetz); and per this, what the Rav wrote that even if the congregation accepted Shabbat, etc. — even if the congregation also accepted Shabbat and she too — the Gaon Ammo"z"l did not write so in his responsum, rather wrote for the Rav that if the prayer is Maariv one may not be lenient even bediavad, as below siman 375 end quote (and so is Maharshal's view in responsum siman 72 that I cited below s.k. 19) — it is clear from the Agur I cited that even for those who are lenient it is only until twenty-four hours; and if so, when the day is so long we consider day and night equal, as for example siman 184 s.k. 7); but after that, for all it is forbidden.

**** ENGLISH ****And some women, etc., within her counting days, etc. Bach wrote: from his words it appears specifically within her counting days; but at beginning of her count if she saw blood after Barchu, even though the day is still long, she does not stop in purity that day but on the morrow — and this is not so; rather even at beginning of her count she stops in purity even if she saw blood near evening, etc.; and per my humble opinion also per the Rav, even if she saw blood near evening she stops before Barchu at beginning of her count; and this means: even if she stopped before Barchu we do not say since she stopped before Barchu therefore after Barchu is considered night for her; rather if she was corrupted afterward she stops even after Barchu; and this is what the Rav wrote "and bediavad one need not be concerned"; and if she sees blood until near evening, it is obvious there is no greater bediavad than this and she begins to stop after Barchu; and what he wrote "and they practice lechatchila to be careful" — means where possible, such as when she does not see blood before Barchu — a fortiori.

<b>והכי נהוג דכבר נתבאר כו'. </b>לא ידענא מאי ענין זה לזה דהתם לא שייך בקיאות כלל וגזרינן לא שמשה אטו שמשה ואה"נ דבאשה לא מהני רחיצה וקינוח דלא עדיף מלא שמשה כלל והתם גזרינן במלתא דשכיח אבל מ"מ נ"מ ברחיצה וקינוח בדין שנזכר בסעיף הקודם בשכחה יום א' שצריכה להמתין ו' עונות דמהני רחיצה וקינוח דתמנה מיד אחר שתשלים היום ששכחה ודוק ונראה דגם הרב מודה בזה ומיירי בסתם אשה ומ"מ לענין דינא בלאו הכי כיון דהסמ"ק והג"מ כתבו דאין אנו בקיאין אין להקל לכתחלה וכ"פ הב"ח:
**** ENGLISH ****And so is the custom, as already explained, etc. I do not know what relevance this has to that, for there expertise is not relevant at all, and we decree lest she had relations as if she had relations; and likewise regarding a woman, bathing and drying do not help, for they are not better than having relations at all; and there we decree in a common matter; but nevertheless there is practical difference in bathing and drying in the law mentioned in the previous seif, forgetting one day that she must wait six onot — bathing and drying help, for she counts immediately after she completes the day she forgot — examine; and it appears the Rav also agrees to this and deals with an ordinary woman; and nevertheless for the law, in any case, since Semak and Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote we are not expert, one may not be lenient lechatchila; and so Bach.
