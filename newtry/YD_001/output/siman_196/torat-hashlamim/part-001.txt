See Taz s.k. 7 where his words make clear one should not be stringent at all for a bride, even a widow after her chuppah, as long as she has not yet come to full relations with him; law of bride after be'ilat mitzvah — as I wrote above siman 193 and Magen Avraham this siman s.k. 5.
