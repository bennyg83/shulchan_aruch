Not to treat the cemetery lightly. In it are 3 seifim. In cemeteries one does not treat them lightly {Rama: (such as relieving oneself there (Kol Bo), or eating and drinking there, and one does not read and study there (Semag), and one does not calculate accounts there) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham)}; and one does not graze animals in them nor lead a water channel through them; nor stroll through them as a shortcut (meaning: to pass from this side to that); nor gather grass from them; and if he gathered — or must gather for the cemetery's needs — they burn them in their place: {Rama: And likewise one should not take from eternal grave-land even though permitted in benefit (so implied from Rosh and Mordechai); and all this is only because of honor for the dead; therefore if needed for healing — permitted (same there); and likewise permitted to benefit from grasses on graves or fruits of trees on them for the cemetery's needs, such as when the ruler grazes animals on graves and they cannot protest except at great expense and the community cannot afford — they sell these things to rescue the graves from the gentile's hand, for this is honor for the dead (Terumat HaDeshen siman 284 and Mahariu siman 50); and if there is nothing on the graves to sell for the expense, if the community can afford and protest with small expense — they must protest unless there is concern the ruler will provoke them thereby; but otherwise they need not protest (same there).}
