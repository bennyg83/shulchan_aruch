**** ENGLISH ****
Hagahot Ashri and Kol Bo explain that people pull up grass together with a clump of earth and throw it over their heads as an expression of grief, following the verse, "They cast dust upon their heads." Afterward they wash their hands and say, "Our hands did not spill this blood," and, "Atone for Your people Israel," through the words, "among Your people Israel." Some explain that the earth symbolizes that man's end is dust, while the grass alludes to resurrection, as it says, "They shall blossom from the city like the grass of the earth." Tur.
