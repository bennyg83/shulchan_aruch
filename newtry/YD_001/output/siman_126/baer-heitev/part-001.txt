"A stream flowing into a stream." For example: a Jew poured from a jug into a utensil of stam yeinam, and what was in the jug became forbidden because of nitzok; and a Jew returned and poured from another utensil into the jug — what is in the other utensil is permitted:

<b>בשתיה. </b> שלא אמרו ניצוק חיבור אלא המערה לתוך יי"נ האסור בהנאה ונראה דאע"ג דכל מגעם וסתם יינם בזה"ז מותר בהנאה כדלעיל ר"ס קכ"ג מ"מ אמרינן בזה ניצוק חיבור כיון דמן הדין היה אסור בהנאה ופשוט הוא. ש"ך:
**** ENGLISH ****
"For drinking." For they said nitzok is connection only regarding one who pours into stam yeinam forbidden in benefit; and it appears that even though all their contact and stam yeinam in this time is permitted in benefit, as above siman 123 — nevertheless we say here nitzok is connection, since by law it was forbidden in benefit — and this is plain. Shach:

<b>להטפיח. </b> כ' רבינו ירוחם ואם הוא כלי שמכניסו לקיום אפי' בטופח לחוד הוי חיבור וע"ל סימן קכ"ג סי"ז וסי' קל"ז ס"א [יהודי ועובד כוכבים שישבו לשתות ושתה העובד כוכבים ולא הדיח הישראל את הכוס והיה בו טפח ע"מ להטפיח וחזר הישראל ועירב מהצלוחית לכוס הכל אסור אפילו מה שבצלוחית דקי"ל ניצוק הוי חיבור רדב"ז סי' ר"ץ ע"כ יש לזהר בשטיפת הכוס היטב אחר שתיית העובד כוכבים]:
**** ENGLISH ****
"Tofeach al menat l'hatfiach." Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote: and if it is a utensil that one keeps for ongoing use, even tofeach alone is connection; and see above siman 123 seif 17 and siman 137 seif 1 [a Jew and a non-Jew sat to drink and the non-Jew drank and the Jew did not rinse the cup, and there was in it tofeach with intent to tofeach, and the Jew returned and poured from the plate into the cup — everything is forbidden, even what is in the plate, for we hold nitzok is connection — Radbaz siman 200, until here: one must be careful to rinse the cup well after the non-Jew drinks]:

**** ENGLISH ****
"Greater." Shach wrote: here it deals with the halachah of the Gemara; but in this time one need not remove its own kind — rather all the wine and the grape pits and the skins and the grapes and the lees combine to be nullified in sixty, for we hold stam yeinam is nullified in sixty; and nevertheless, that if he touched the stream they nullify in sixty — requires study in the halachah from the words of Tosafot daf 72 side 2, for it does not appear so; however one who examines there in Tosafot will see for understanding, for they wrote there that in great loss one may nullify in sixty even if he touched the stream — see there; and the Mechaber holds that here is called great loss, as he wrote above seif 2 — investigate (*) — and see above siman 134 seif 2 in hagahah, further regarding the laws of nitzok:
