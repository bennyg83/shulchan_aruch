**** ENGLISH ****To mortar, etc. But on the ground — they too sprinkle on the ground before their idolatry — Rashba, and Beit Yosef and Perishah bring it; and in Taz he wrote: if one pours wine on the ground and a gentile touched that splash, the splash is forbidden from the touch and what is inside is forbidden through nitzok — end of his words; and he did not explain as plainly as what is written outside is forbidden because of his force — and he properly intended that if not so it would be forbidden only because of his force for drinking, as above siman 125, and what is inside would not be forbidden because of nitzok, for nitzok forbids only wine forbidden in benefit, as in the next seif; and even though from Rashba's words there it appears that even when pouring without touching it is forbidden because of nitzok — according to his reasoning he goes, who forbids his force in benefit — unlike for us.

<b>דאמרי' כו'. </b>תימה דזה אינו אלא לדעת הטור דס"ל לקמן סימן קל"ד דסתם יינם נמי מין במינו במשהו אבל להרב דס"ל התם דסתם יינם בטל בס' וכמ"ש בסימן זה סוף ס"ה א"כ א"צ לסלק את מינו אלא הכל היין והחרצנים והזגים וענבים ושמרים מצטרפים לבטל בששים וכן קשה על העט"ז ואולי ס"ל דדוקא סתם יינם בטל בס' אבל לא יין שנאסר במגע עובדי כוכבים אבל א"א לומר כן וכמש"ל סימן קכ"ג ס"ק מ' ויש ליישב דהכא מיירי לדינא דש"ס דדוקא בזמן הזה קאמר הרב בסימן קל"ד דבטל בס' ע"ש (אלא דצ"ע דמדברי הגהמ"י פט"ז מהמ"א ומביאו ב"י וד"מ לקמן ר"ס קל"ד משמע להדיא דאפילו למ"ד סתם יינם אינו בטל בס' היינו דוקא יין שנגע בו עובד כוכבים שנתערב אבל נצוק בטל בס' לכ"ע ע"ש וי"ל דס"ל להרב דלא כהגהמ"י אלא כהמחבר וסייעתו) ומ"מ הא דס"ל להרשב"א דאם נגע בקילוח מבטלין בס' ונמשכו לדעתו הט"ו צ"ע לדינא דהתו' פ' בתרא דעבודת כוכבים (דף ע"ב ע"ב) כתבו וז"ל אם משך ישראל מן החבית לכלי שיש בו יי"נ אם יש בחבית ס' כנגד היין שבכלי מותר אפילו למ"ד נצוק חבור דלא גרע מאלו היה כולו מעורב בחבית שהיה בטל בס' בסתם יינם כמו שפי' ר"ת וניצוק דמיתסר ה"ד כגון שהיה היין מקלח מן החבית ובא העובד כוכבים ונגע בקילוח שהוא הניצוק דהשתא הוי כאלו נוגע העובד כוכבים בחבית כי האי מיתסר עכ"ל וכ"כ המרדכי והאגודה שם וע"ל סי' קל"ד סק"ג וס"ב בהגה"ה עוד מדיני ניצוק:
**** ENGLISH ****For they say, etc. It is astonishing, for this is only according to Tur who holds below siman 134 that stam yeinam is also species in its species in any amount; but according to the rabbi who holds there that stam yeinam is nullified in sixty, as written in this siman end of seif 5 — if so one need not remove its species; rather all the wine, grape seeds, grape skins, grapes, and lees combine to nullify in sixty; and likewise Taz challenged; and perhaps he holds that specifically stam yeinam is nullified in sixty but not wine forbidden through touch of gentiles; but one cannot say thus, as written siman 123 s.k. 40; and one may resolve that here it deals with the law of the Gemara — specifically in our time the rabbi says in siman 134 it is nullified in sixty — see there (except requires study from the words of Hagahot Maimoni, Pri To'ar of Forbidden Foods, and Beit Yosef and Darkhei Moshe below siman 134: it appears explicitly that even according to the one who says stam yeinam is not nullified in sixty — that is specifically wine that a gentile touched and became mixed; but nitzok is nullified in sixty for everyone — see there; and one may say the rabbi holds not like Hagahot Maimoni but like the Mechaber and his camp); and nevertheless what Rashba holds that if he touched the splash they nullify in sixty — Taz was drawn to his view — requires study for halachah according to Tosafot last chapter of Avodah Zarah (daf 72 side 2) who wrote, and these are their words: if a Jew drew from the barrel into a vessel that has stam yeinam, if there is in the barrel sixty against the wine in the vessel — permitted, even according to the one who says nitzok is connection, for it is no worse than if all were mixed in the barrel and were nullified in sixty in stam yeinam, as Rabbenu Tam explained; and nitzok that became forbidden — for example the wine was spurting from the barrel and the gentile came and touched the splash which is the nitzok — now it is as if the gentile touched the barrel, for this became forbidden — end of his words; and likewise Mordechai and Agudah there; and see there siman 134 s.k. 3 and seif 2 in the hagahah, further laws of nitzok.
