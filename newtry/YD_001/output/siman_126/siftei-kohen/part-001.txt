**** ENGLISH ****
["For we say etc. It is surprising — for this is only according to Tur, who holds below siman 134 that stam yeinam is also min b'mino in any amount; but according to the Rav, who holds there that stam yeinam is nullified in sixty, and as he wrote in this siman end of seif 5 — if so one need not remove its own kind; rather all the wine and the grape pits and the skins and the grapes and the lees combine to be nullified in sixty; and likewise he challenged Pri To'ar; and perhaps he holds that specifically stam yeinam is nullified in sixty, but not wine forbidden through contact of gentiles; but one cannot say so, and as he wrote siman 123 s.k. 40; and one may resolve that here it deals with the halachah of the Gemara — specifically in this time the Rav says in siman 134 that it is nullified in sixty — see there (but requires study: from the words of Hagahot Maimoni, Pri To'ar from Mishneh Torah, and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe below siman 134, it appears explicitly that even according to those who say stam yeinam is not nullified in sixty — that is specifically wine that a non-Jew touched and became mixed — but nitzok is nullified in sixty according to all — see there; and one may say the Rav holds not like Hagahot Maimoni but like the Mechaber and his supporters); and nevertheless, that which Rashba holds that if he touched the stream they nullify in sixty, and Taz followed his view — requires study in the halachah from Tosafot last perek of Avodah Zarah (daf 72 side 2), who wrote: if a Jew drew from the barrel into a utensil that contains stam yeinam, if there is in the barrel sixty against the wine in the utensil it is permitted — even according to those who say nitzok is connection, for it is no worse than if all of it were mixed in the barrel, where it would be nullified in sixty in stam yeinam, as Rabbenu Tam explained; and nitzok that becomes forbidden — how so?
