<b>דין יין שנתערב ביין נסך ודין נצוק חבור. ובו ז' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>המערה יין לתוך כלי שיש בו יין עובד כוכבים האסור <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>בהנאה נאסר כל היין שבכלי העליון שהרי העמוד הנצוק מחבר בין היין שבכלי העליון ובין היין שבכלי התחתון ורואים כאילו יין של האיסור מעורב בשל היתר ואם <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>עירה וקיטף שקודם שנגע בשל האיסור הפסיק קלוח היורד מהיין של היתר מותר:
**** ENGLISH ****
The law of wine that became mixed with yayin nesech, and the law of nitzok as a connection. In it are 7 seifim. One who pours wine into a utensil that contains the wine of a non-Jew that is forbidden in benefit — all the wine in the upper utensil becomes forbidden, for the nitzok column connects the wine in the upper utensil with the wine in the lower utensil, and we view it as though the wine of issur is mixed with the wine of heter. But if he poured and then cut off the flow, such that before it touched the issur the stream descending from the heter stopped, it is permitted.
**** END BLOCK ****

**** ENGLISH ****
That nitzok is a connection is stated only in a small loss; but in a great loss — such as when he drew from the barrel into a utensil that contains yayin nesech — it is permitted. {Rama: And there is no difference whether there was much wine in the barrel or not; and even if he can sell it to a non-Jew. Anything that is in a jug or in a cup is called a small loss. And this is specifically regarding yayin nesech, but regarding other issurim, if he can sell it to a non-Jew and there is no great loss, it is not called a great loss (the conclusion of Terumat HaDeshen siman 204).}

**** ENGLISH ****
If a non-Jew pours wine from a utensil into mud, or into a garbage heap, or into a dirty place, it is not the manner of libation, and everything is permitted — both what went out outside and what remained inside. But if a Jew or a non-Jew was pouring into mud or into a garbage heap, and a non-Jew came and touched the stream, everything is forbidden.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who pours wine into a utensil of a non-Jew that was not rinsed, and there is not in it liquid that is “tofeach”: that which he poured into it is forbidden for drinking, but that which he poured from it is permitted even for drinking. {Rama: And if there is in it enough “tofeach al menat l'hatfiach”, it is forbidden because of nitzok (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham and Semag, and Tosafot and the Rosh, last perek of Avodah Zarah). However, it does not forbid more than if it were actually mixed into it; and if it is their stam yeinam, it is nullified in 60, as will be explained in siman 134.}
