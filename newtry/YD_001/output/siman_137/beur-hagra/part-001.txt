**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Likewise, it is permitted, etc.</b> Salt does not extract absorbed taste from a vessel; see above, siman 105, seif 12. Furthermore, it cannot impart flavor, just as one may place water and other beverages there for this reason; see above, siman 122, seif 5. In addition, while the substance is occupied with discharging, it does not absorb. It absorbs only something that adheres, such as fat or brine, but not blood, which slides off, and likewise not the wine discharged here. See above, siman 70, seif 3. Beit Yosef here and in siman 105, citing a responsum of the Rashba.

**** HEBREW ****
<b>אם נתן כו'. </b>ר"ל אע"ג דאמרינן בירושלמי פינהו ונתן לתוכו יין אחר היין אסור והקנקן מותר היינו בעירוי ג' ימים כמ"ש בא"ח וכמש"ל סי' קל"ה סי"א ואף בזה חולק הרמב"ן כמש"ש אבל בלא"ה פשוט דלא ואמרינן ל"ג א' ב' דדוקא ציר ומורייס אבל שאר משקין לא:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>If he placed, etc.</b> Meaning, although the Yerushalmi says that if he emptied it and placed other wine in it, the wine is forbidden but the jar is permitted, that refers to soaking for three days, as stated in Orach Chaim and above, siman 135, seif 11. Even there Ramban disputes the ruling, as stated there. Without such soaking, however, it is plainly not permitted. On 33a and 33b the Gemara says that only brine and fish brine have this effect, but other beverages do not.
