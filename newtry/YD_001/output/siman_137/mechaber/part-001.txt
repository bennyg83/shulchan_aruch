**** ENGLISH ****
A law of kosher wine that was placed into a vessel that was not made kosher; it contains 6 se’ifim. All vessels that are forbidden due to wine of a non-Jew, into which a Jew placed wine before he made them kosher — it is forbidden for drinking and permitted in benefit, even if it is dry, as long as 12 months have not passed over it (but if it is a vessel used for long-term storage, it is forbidden immediately) (Tur in the name of Rosh). And if it is a vessel that is not used for long-term storage (and it is dry), it is not forbidden unless the Jew’s wine stood in it 24 hours. And if in the wine there was mixed a pitcher of water: if there is in the water 6 times corresponding to the “peel” of the jug, it is permitted even for drinking. {Rama: And see above siman 134, that even if it has no water, it is batel in 60; and there is no difference between vessels forbidden due to yayin nesech and whether they are bnei yoman or not (Tur; and a responsum of Rosh; and R' Peretz at the end of Avodah Zarah).}

<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="8"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="4"></i>אם דרך ענבים בגת של עובד כוכבים שלא הכשירו מותר שהרי החרצנים והזגים יש בהם ס' כנגד קליפת הגת <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="4"></i>אבל אסור להוציא היין דרך נקב שהיין של עובד כוכבים יורד בו שהנקב אסור והיין היוצא משם צלול מבלי תערובות החרצנים: <small>הגה <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="9"></i>ואם עבר והוציאו דרך הנקב נראה לפי מאי דקי"ל דסתם יינם בטל בס' כדלעיל סי' קל"ד דכאן ג"כ כל היין <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="5"></i>שרי <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="11"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="10"></i>ולא אמרינן דנאסר ראשון ראשון <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="5"></i>וע"ל סימן צ"ט:</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
If one trod grapes in the winepress of a non-Jew that was not made kosher, it is permitted, for the pits and skins have 60 corresponding to the “peel” of the winepress. But it is forbidden to take the wine out through a hole through which the non-Jew’s wine runs, for the hole is forbidden, and the wine that exits from there is clear, without the mixture of the pits. {Rama: And if he transgressed and took it out through the hole, it appears that according to what we rule that stam yeinam is batel in 60, as above siman 134, here too all the wine is permitted, and we do not say that it becomes forbidden “first is forbidden and then the next,” and see below siman 99.}

**** ENGLISH ****
Wine that was placed into jugs of a non-Jew that were not made kosher, and afterwards he emptied that wine and mixed it with other, more abundant wine that he had in other kosher jugs: if there is in the second wine 60 corresponding to the first wine, everything is permitted even for drinking. And if there is in the second wine water in the measure of 6 parts corresponding to the forbidden “peels,” everything is also permitted for drinking, even though there was no water in the first wine.

**** ENGLISH ****
Vessels that are forbidden due to wine of a non-Jew: it is permitted to put into them either water, or beer, or other beverages, provided that he rinses first the moisture of wine that is on the surface of the vessels. And likewise, it is permitted to salt in them.
