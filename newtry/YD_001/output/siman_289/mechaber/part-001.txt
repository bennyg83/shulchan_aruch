**** ENGLISH ****
What is the place of its affixing? Within the hollow of the doorway, on the tefach adjacent to the outside (and if he changed, it does not invalidate, provided he place it in the doorpost itself) (Tur), at the beginning of the upper third of the height of the doorway; and if he affixed higher than this, it is valid—provided he distance it from the lintel a tefach. And he must affix it on the right of one entering. (And there is no difference whether he is left-handed or not) (in Mordechai, beginning of Hilchos Ketanim). And if he affixed it on the left, it is invalid.
