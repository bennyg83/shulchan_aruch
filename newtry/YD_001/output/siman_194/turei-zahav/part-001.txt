**** ENGLISH ****Until night 15, etc. In Tur written she must separate from husband night 81 and it is statement in Gemara; and there is dispute — in Behag explains reason because then she exits from days of purity to days of impurity — it is like her veset time; but other poskim Beit Yosef brought all hold reason because she was accustomed to have relations all days of purity and even if she sees we are concerned perhaps also now she will see unknowingly — therefore her husband informs her he separates from her this night because her days of purity completed — end quote; and per this reason of other poskim this prohibition is only where they have relations on blood of purity; but for us who do not have relations on blood of purity as mentioned nearby — no prohibition night 41 and 81; not so per Behag no this distinction and even for us who do not have relations on blood of purity must separate night 41; and since majority poskim hold reason because of decree we mentioned — need not separate night 81; and therefore they did not bring here in Shulchan Aruch; and no one is concerned.
