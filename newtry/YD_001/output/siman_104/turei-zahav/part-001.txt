granted dry in dry issur becomes heter through majority by Torah decree; not so here issur does not become heter, only taste in butter is permitted because cannot separate issur — that is the basis of heter; <b>ליין ושמן. </b>הרמב"ם נתן טעם לזה שאלו דברים צריכים להיות מבושמים וכ' רש"ל בפ' ג"ה סי' מ"ח דמשמע מטעם זה דשאר משקים אסורים ובדבש לכ"ע פוגם כמ"ש הסמ"ג מפי' התוס' לענין דברים שנתערבו בדבש והמרדכי פרק אין מעמידין ומסיק שם דיש להחמיר בכל המשקין חוץ מיין ושמן ודבש:
**** ENGLISH ****
To wine and oil. Rambam gave reason these must be spiced; Rashal chapter 3 siman 48 wrote: from this reason other liquids are forbidden; in honey all agree it spoils as Semag from Tosafot on things mixed in honey; Mordechai chapter Ein Ma'amidin concludes: one should be stringent in all liquids except wine, oil, and honey.

**** ENGLISH ****
Perhaps the fat was soft. But if we know certainly the fat was initially soft and there is safek it hardened during pickling — within twenty-four hours from falling — we are stringent, unlike Or Zarua general rule 30 who permits if it came before us hard: we follow time of finding; even if certainly soft at time of issur, perhaps it hardened within twenty-four hours of mouse falling; he brought proof from "all tumah follows time of finding"; Beit David disagrees: with one safek we are stringent.
