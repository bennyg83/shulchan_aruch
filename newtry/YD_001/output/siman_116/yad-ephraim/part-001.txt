<b>מיהו בדיעבד. </b>עבה"ט ועשו"ת בית יהודה סי' כ"ה חתיכת בשר שנפל ליורה של דגים ונשפכה היורה ויש ספק אם יש ס' אסור דהוי ספיקא דאורייתא ולחומרא וע"ש במקום שאוסרין מים מגולים לא מהני ס' רק שיעורם בכדי שתאבד המרה ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****However b'dieved. Baer Heitev and responsum Beit Yehudah siman 25 — a piece of meat fell into a pot of fish and the pot was spilled, and there is safek whether there are sixty — forbidden, for it is a safek deoraita to be stringent — see there; in a place where they forbid exposed water, sixty does not help, only their measure so the bitterness is lost — see there.

<b>רק כשמבשלם. </b>עבה"ט ועשו"ת שבו"י ח"ב סי' ק"ד דגים שנתבשלו עם שומן אווז הדגים אסורים משום סכנה כי גם בעוף עם דגים יש חשש סכנה אע"ג דעופות מן הרקק נבראו ויש להם קשקשת ברגליהם ועיין סימן ע' בנמלחו עופות עם דגים אין סכנה במליחה אבל בבישול אסור מ"מ אם יש ס' יש להקל וכ"כ הפר"ח וכ"כ בשו"ת חוות יאיר סי' ס"ד ע"ש ועיין בשבו"י ח"ג סי' ע' ושם כתב דהמג"א סי' קע"ג כתב דאפשר בזה"ז ליכא סכנה כ"כ ואע"ג דאין לסמוך עליו בזה בספק סכנה בכה"ג דאיכא ס' אף דט"ז מחמיר מ"מ כיון דדעת אחרונים להקל ובטל בס' אין להחמיר בזה כלל וע"ש דבפליטת כלים ליכא חשש סכנה כמ"ש במ"י כלל נ"ז וע"ש כלל ט"ו:
**** ENGLISH ****Only when cooking them. Baer Heitev and responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 104 — fish cooked with goose fat, the fish are forbidden because of danger, for also with poultry and fish there is concern of danger, even though poultry were created from moisture and have scales on their legs; and see siman 70 regarding when poultry and fish were salted together there is no danger in salting, but in cooking it is forbidden; nevertheless if there are sixty one may be lenient, and so Peri Chadash, and so in responsum Chavot Yair siman 64 — see there; and see in Shevut Yaakov part 3 siman 70, and there he wrote that Magen Avraham siman 173 wrote that perhaps in this time there is not so much danger; and although one should not rely on him in safek danger in such a case where there are sixty, even though Turei Zahav is stringent, nevertheless since later authorities are lenient and it is nullified in sixty, one should not be stringent at all — see there that in purging vessels there is no concern of danger as Mishnah Yomi wrote klal 57 — see there klal 15.

<b>ולא יתן משקים. </b>עבה"ט ובשו"י ח"ב סימן ק"ה אוכלין ומשקין הנתונים תחת המטה וכן שאר דברים המבוארים בסי' קט"ז שר"ר שורה עליהן אין לאסור בדיעבד כמ"ש הטור בשם בעל העיטור ביו"ד סי' ו' וכן מוכח בב"ב דף נ"ח בפירשב"ם שע"ה מניחים תחת מיטתם אוכלים וכלים ואפ"ה לא הזהירו לחבר לאכול עם ע"ה רק משום שמא יאכלנו דבר שאינו מתוקן אלא ע"כ בדיעבד אין קפידא:
**** ENGLISH ****And he should not put beverages. Baer Heitev and in Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 105 — foods and beverages placed under the bed, and likewise other matters explained in siman 116 on which an evil spirit rests — one does not forbid b'dieved, as Tur wrote in the name of Baal HaIttur in Yoreh De'ah siman 6; and so it is proven in Bava Batra daf 58 in Rashi, that on a bed they place foods and vessels underneath, and nevertheless they did not warn a colleague not to eat with a gentile except lest he eat something not properly prepared; but in any case b'dieved there is no concern.

לצער ב"ח ע"ש ואע"ג דבמריטת נוצות יש למנוע מ"מ זה צורך יותר משום הפסד ממון והשבו"י גופא הביא שם ראיה מע"ז דף י"ג שמותר לקטוע אצבעו של תרנגול לבן משום ריוח כל שהוא ועיין שם דמייתי משבת דף ע"ז שבלול בשם מ"ב ומ"ש הט"ז שם ובעה"ג סימן י"ג ובית יעקב סי' מ"ב דאפילו משנת חסידים אין כאן כמ"ש בספר חסידים סי' תרס"ז וכן סתם רמ"א באה"ע סי' ה' ומקור הדין מתוספות בע"א גבי עוקרין על המלכים ע"ש. וכתב יעב"ץ סי' **** ENGLISH ****Included in "Do not abhor." Baer Heitev and Yaavetz siman 76 in Hagahot HaKadmonim — he was cramped and wished to expand, but a fruit tree stood in that place — it is permitted to cut it as Rosh wrote in Bava Kamma daf 91 that if needed for its place it is permitted, and all the more so for the sake of a mitzvah (and Turei Zahav permits in siman 167 even for mundane need, only he should have explained specifically if the houses are superior to the trees, etc.); and for clarity of the matter he should have the gentile cut it, and all the more so since it belongs to a gentile — behold he is the agent of the gentile; without his permission he may not cut it, etc. — see there. And Shevut Yaakov part 3 siman 71 wrote that one need not be concerned for bal tashchit or tza'ar ba'alei chayim whenever one has need, and it is permitted to do even lechatchilah; even though Rama in Even HaEzer siman 5 wrote that in plucking feathers the world refrains because of cruelty — that is specifically plucking feathers which is done by hand and the bird feels pain with each plucking, not so in another matter such as one who wishes to make a drink for healing and it is not yet tested for him and he wishes to test it by giving to a cat or dog to see if they do not die from it — permitted, for there is no pain at the time of eating and drinking, only afterward it causes illness and pain, and it is for human healing — see there. And see in Peri Chadash siman 13 that he permits in a bird whose wing broke before us — if he leaves it so it will become treifah from concern the lung may be punctured — permitted to cut it while the bird is alive, and we are not concerned for tza'ar ba'alei chayim — see there; and although in plucking feathers one should refrain, nevertheless this is a greater need because of monetary loss; and Shevut Yaakov itself brought proof there from Avodah Zarah daf 13 that it is permitted to cut the finger of a white rooster because of any gain whatsoever; and see there that he cites from Shabbat daf 77 regarding crushing a snail, and daf 110 side b regarding this bar Kasha, and Avodah Zarah daf 44 that it is permitted to give exposed beverages to his cat even though they weaken it; and Rosh wrote in Bava Kamma daf 115 — see there what he wrote on what is written in Chullin daf 7 regarding the incident of Rabbi Pinchas ben Yair; see in Yoreh De'ah siman 114 in the name of Mishnah Berurah and what Turei Zahav wrote there and in Beit HaGadol siman 13 and Beit Yaakov siman 42 that even from Sefer Chassidim there is nothing here as written in Sefer Chassidim siman 467; and so Rama wrote plainly in Even HaEzer siman 5, and the source of the law is from Tosafot in Avodah Zarah regarding uprooting for kings — see there. And Yaavetz wrote siman 110: that tza'ar ba'alei chayim is forbidden applies to domestic animals; and it is possible that also dog and cat are included, for they too are cultured and domestic animals, and one should give them food first like an animal; but small creatures not fit for human labor, and all the more flies, fleas, and other abominations and creeping things — they have no Torah basis to be concerned for their pain; and killing them — only the Ari, of blessed memory, commanded his disciples not to kill even a louse from the attribute of piety — see there what he wrote regarding the workers in Rabbi's incident who said "And His mercy is over all His works" — see there. And Shevut Yaakov part 3 siman 70 wrote: in one house they fried salted fish with goose fat and did not know there is prohibition in the matter; they discarded the fish but the spoon with which they stirred the fish, while still not rinsed, they put into a pot of meat — if there are sixty in the piece it is permitted, not like those who disagree that one may not nullify in sixty in this; and because of purging vessels there is no concern of danger at all — see there. And Chinuch Beit Yehudah wrote that one should not be concerned at all to cook fish in a meat vessel or vice versa, and nullification in sixty also helps — see there; and see in Chut HaShani siman 67 that he also wrote nullification in sixty helps; and to cook in a rinsed meat vessel — and they are not forbidden in salting as implied in siman 70, and not in roasting as implied in siman 93 and siman 116 regarding odor being nothing — from a weak reason there is no prohibition in them — see there that there is no proof from Maharil who deals with where there were not sixty, etc. — see there; and what he wrote that also in roasting there is no concern is puzzling, for behold the entire source of the law is in roasting, as stated in Pesachim daf 77 — this fish roasted with meat, etc. — see there; and in truth so it is explicitly in siman 116 — see there. Beit Yehudah responsum siman 23 wrote: one washes hands between poultry meat and fish.
