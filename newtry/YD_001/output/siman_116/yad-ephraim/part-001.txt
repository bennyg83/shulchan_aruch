<b>מיהו בדיעבד. </b>עבה"ט ועשו"ת בית יהודה סי' כ"ה חתיכת בשר שנפל ליורה של דגים ונשפכה היורה ויש ספק אם יש ס' אסור דהוי ספיקא דאורייתא ולחומרא וע"ש במקום שאוסרין מים מגולים לא מהני ס' רק שיעורם בכדי שתאבד המרה ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
"However b'dieved." Avodat HaTaharah and responsum of Beit Yehudah siman 25 — a piece of meat that fell into a fish pot and the pot was spilled and there is doubt whether there is sixty — forbidden, for it is a Torah doubt and we rule stringently; see there — in a place where they forbid exposed water sixty does not help, only their measure is enough that the venom be lost — see there.

<b>רק כשמבשלם. </b>עבה"ט ועשו"ת שבו"י ח"ב סי' ק"ד דגים שנתבשלו עם שומן אווז הדגים אסורים משום סכנה כי גם בעוף עם דגים יש חשש סכנה אע"ג דעופות מן הרקק נבראו ויש להם קשקשת ברגליהם ועיין סימן ע' בנמלחו עופות עם דגים אין סכנה במליחה אבל בבישול אסור מ"מ אם יש ס' יש להקל וכ"כ הפר"ח וכ"כ בשו"ת חוות יאיר סי' ס"ד ע"ש ועיין בשבו"י ח"ג סי' ע' ושם כתב דהמג"א סי' קע"ג כתב דאפשר בזה"ז ליכא סכנה כ"כ ואע"ג דאין לסמוך עליו בזה בספק סכנה בכה"ג דאיכא ס' אף דט"ז מחמיר מ"מ כיון דדעת אחרונים להקל ובטל בס' אין להחמיר בזה כלל וע"ש דבפליטת כלים ליכא חשש סכנה כמ"ש במ"י כלל נ"ז וע"ש כלל ט"ו:
**** ENGLISH ****
"Only when cooking them." Avodat HaTaharah and responsum of Shevut Yaakov vol. 2 siman 104 — fish cooked with goose fat — the fish are forbidden because of danger, for also fowl with fish involves concern of danger although fowl were created from mucus and have scales on their legs; and see siman 70 — when salting fowl with fish there is no danger in salting, but in cooking forbidden; nevertheless if there is sixty one may be lenient; and so Peri Chadash and responsum of Chavot Yair siman 64 — see there; and see in Shevut Yaakov vol. 3 siman 70 where he wrote Magen Avraham siman 173 wrote perhaps nowadays there is not so much danger; and although one may not rely on this in doubt of danger in such a case where there is sixty — although Taz is stringent — nevertheless since later authorities are lenient and it is nullified in sixty one should not be stringent at all; see there that through purging vessels there is no concern of danger as Mateh Yehonatan wrote general rule 57 — see there general rule 15.

<b>ולא יתן משקים. </b>עבה"ט ובשו"י ח"ב סימן ק"ה אוכלין ומשקין הנתונים תחת המטה וכן שאר דברים המבוארים בסי' קט"ז שר"ר שורה עליהן אין לאסור בדיעבד כמ"ש הטור בשם בעל העיטור ביו"ד סי' ו' וכן מוכח בב"ב דף נ"ח בפירשב"ם שע"ה מניחים תחת מיטתם אוכלים וכלים ואפ"ה לא הזהירו לחבר לאכול עם ע"ה רק משום שמא יאכלנו דבר שאינו מתוקן אלא ע"כ בדיעבד אין קפידא:
**** ENGLISH ****
"And he should not place liquids." Avodat HaTaharah and in Shevut Yaakov vol. 2 siman 105 — foods and liquids placed under the bed and other matters explained in siman 116 upon which ruach ra'ah rests — one should not forbid b'dieved, as Tur wrote in the name of Ba'al HaItur in Yoreh De'ah siman 6; and so proven in Bava Batra daf 58 in Rashbam that they place under their beds foods and vessels and nevertheless they did not warn a colleague not to eat with a gentile except lest he eat something not properly prepared — thus b'dieved there is no concern.

"Included in Do not make yourselves abominable." Avodat HaTaharah and Yaavetz siman 76 in hagahah ketavah — one was narrow and they wished to widen but a fruit tree stood in that place — permitted to cut it as Rosh wrote in Bava Kamma daf 91 that if he needs its place it is permitted, and all the more for a mitzvah need (and Taz permits in siman 167 even for ordinary need only he should have explained specifically if the houses are superior to the trees, etc.); and for ease of the matter let a gentile cut it, and all the more since it belongs to a gentile — behold he is the gentile's agent; without his permission he may not cut it, etc. — see there; and Shevut Yaakov vol. 3 siman 71 wrote one need not be concerned for bal tashchit or tza'ar ba'alei chayim whenever he has need and permitted to do even l'chatchila; although Rama in Even HaEzer siman 5 wrote regarding plucking feathers people refrain because of cruelty — that is specifically plucking feathers done by hand and the bird feels pain with each plucking; unlike another matter such as one who wishes to make a potion for healing not yet tested and wishes to test it by giving a cat or dog to drink to see if they die from it — permitted, for there is no pain at time of eating and drinking, only afterward causes illness and pain, and it is for human healing needs — see there; and see Peri Chadash siman 13 that he permits in a bird whose wing broke before us and if left thus would become treifah from concern the lung might be punctured — permitted to cut it in the bird's lifetime and we are not concerned for tza'ar ba'alei chayim — see there; and although regarding plucking feathers one should refrain, nevertheless this need is greater because of monetary loss; and Shevut Yaakov himself brought proof from Avodah Zarah daf 13 that one may cut the toe of a white rooster for any profit; and see there he cites from Shabbat daf 77 regarding crushing a snail, etc., and daf 110 side 2 regarding bar kasha, etc., and Avodah Zarah daf 134 that one may give exposed liquids to his cat even though they weaken it; and Rosh wrote in Bava Kamma daf 115 — see there what he wrote on what is written in Chullin daf 7 regarding the case of R' Pinchas ben Ya'ir; see Yoreh De'ah siman 114 in the name of Mateh Yehudah and what Taz wrote there and in Avodat HaGolah siman 13 and Beit Yaakov siman 42 that even from Sefer Chasidim siman 467 there is nothing here as written in Sefer Chasidim siman 467; and Rama plainly in Even HaEzer siman 5; and the source of the law from Tosafot Avodah Zarah regarding uprooting for kings — see there. And Yaavetz siman 110 wrote: tza'ar ba'alei chayim forbidden applies to domestic animals; and perhaps also dog and cat are included since they too are cultured and working animals and one should give them food first like an animal; but small creatures unfit for human labor, and all the more flies, fleas, and other sheratzim and crawling things — have no Torah basis to be concerned for their pain and killing them; only the Ari, z"l, commanded his disciples not to kill even a louse from piety — see there what he wrote regarding workers in the case of Rabbenu who said "His mercy is upon all His works" — see there. And Shevut Yaakov vol. 3 siman 70 wrote: in one house they fried salted fish with goose fat not knowing there is prohibition; they discarded the fish but the spoon shaken with the fish while still not rinsed — if inserted into a meat pot, if there is sixty in the pot it is permitted, unlike those who say one may not nullify in sixty in this; and through purging vessels there is no concern of danger at all — see there. And Chinukh Beit Yehudah responsum wrote one need not be concerned at all to cook fish in a meat vessel or the reverse, and nullification in sixty also helps — see there; and see Chut HaShani siman 67 also wrote nullification in sixty helps and to cook in rinsed Jewish vessels; and also not forbidden in salting as implied siman 70, and also not in roasting as implied end of siman 93 and siman 116 regarding reicha — weak reason without prohibition in them — see there there is no proof from Maharil that there was not sixty, etc. — see there; and what he wrote that also in roasting there is no concern is astonishing, for the entire source of the law is stated in roasting in Pesachim daf 77 — this fish roasted with meat, etc. — see there; and in truth so it is explicitly in siman 116 — see there. Beit Yehudah responsum siman 23 wrote: one washes hands between poultry meat and fish.
