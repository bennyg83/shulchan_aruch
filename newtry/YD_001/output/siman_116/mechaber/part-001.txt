<b>דברים האסורים משום גלוי. ובו ז' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>משקים שנתגלו אסרום חכמים דחיישינן שמא שתה נחש מהם והטיל בהם ארס <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="1"></i> <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>ועכשיו שאין נחשים מצויים בינינו מותר:
**** ENGLISH ****
Things forbidden because of exposure [to poison]; it contains 7 se'ifim. Beverages that were exposed — the Sages forbade them, for we are concerned lest a snake drank from them and cast venom into them; and now that snakes are not found among us, it is permitted.

One must be careful not to put coins in his mouth lest there be on them dried saliva of one afflicted with boils; and he should not put the edge of his hand under his armpit lest his hand touched a metzora or harmful substance; and he should not put a loaf of bread under the armpit because of sweat; and he should not put a dish nor beverages under the bed because an evil spirit rests on them; and he should not stick a knife into an etrog or into a radish lest a person fall on its point and die. {Rama: And likewise he should be careful of all things that lead to danger, for danger is more severe than prohibition, and one should be more concerned for possible danger than for possible prohibition (Beit Yosef in the name of the Gemara). Therefore they forbade walking in any place of danger such as under a leaning wall or alone at night (there). And likewise they forbade drinking water from rivers at night or placing one's mouth on the water spout to drink, for these things have concern of danger (Rambam). And it is widespread custom not to drink water at the time of the tekufah, and so wrote the early authorities and one should not change (Avudraham and Mordechai chapter Kol Sha'ah Rokeach siman 475 and Maharil and customs). They also wrote that one should flee from the city when plague is in the city, and one should leave the city at the beginning of the matter and not at its end (responsum of Maharil siman 35). And all these matters are because of danger, and one who guards his soul will distance himself from them; and it is forbidden to rely on a miracle or endanger one's soul in anything similar; and see Choshen Mishpat siman 427.}

<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="19"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="12"></i>אסור לאכול מאכלים ומשקים שנפשו של אדם קצה בהם כגון משקים ואוכלים שנתערבו בהם קיא או צואה וליחה סרוחה וכיוצא בהם <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="13"></i>וכן אסור לאכול ולשתות בכלים הצואים שנפשו של אדם קצה בהם כגון כלים של בית הכסא וכלי זכוכית שמקיזים בהם וכיוצא בהם וכן לא יאכל בידים מזוהמות ועל גבי כלים מלוכלכים <i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="9"></i>שכל אלו <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="6"></i>בכלל אל <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="8"></i>תשקצו את נפשותיכם:
**** ENGLISH ****
It is forbidden to eat foods and beverages that a person's soul was repulsed by, such as beverages and foods into which vomit or excrement or rotten moisture were mixed and the like; and likewise it is forbidden to eat and drink in filthy vessels that a person's soul was repulsed by, such as vessels of the privy and glass vessels into which they let blood and the like; and likewise he should not eat with soiled hands and on dirty vessels — for all these are included in "You shall not make yourselves abominable."
