**** ENGLISH ****
Beit Yosef explained: a garment made of felts. And according to the view of the Rambam that felts are forbidden d'oraisa — and I noted this above in seif 1 — even if they are as hard as the namta gamda of Narash, it is forbidden to place them upon oneself.
