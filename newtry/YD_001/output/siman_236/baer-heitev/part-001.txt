**** ENGLISH ****
<b>In this manner.</b> Shach questions the scope of Rivash's ruling. If the woman refuses to marry him, he is released because his oath meant to marry her if she agreed. <b>Exempt.</b> Taz derives a general rule: when fulfillment of a condition is essential to the undertaking, failure of that condition prevents the obligation even if caused by circumstances beyond control. He applies this to commercial delivery dates and marriage agreements, and disputes Bach concerning whether a postponed wedding oath remains binding. Nekudot HaKesef supports Bach. Taz further discusses penalties and communal cherem in engagement agreements.
