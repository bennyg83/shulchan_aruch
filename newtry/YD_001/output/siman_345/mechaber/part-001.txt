<b>דין המאבד עצמו לדעת ומנודה והרוגי ב"ד והפורשין מן הצבור. ובו ח' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>המאבד עצמו לדעת <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i>אין מתעסקים עמו לכל דבר ואין מתאבלין עליו ואין מספידין אותו <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>ולא <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>קורעין ולא חולצין אבל עומדין עליו בשורה ואומרים עליו ברכת אבלים וכל דבר שהוא כבוד לחיים:
**** ENGLISH ****
The law of one who kills himself knowingly, a banished person, those executed by the court, and those who separate from the community. In it are 8 seifim. One who kills himself knowingly—one does not engage with him for anything; one does not mourn him; one does not eulogize him; one does not tear or remove shoes; but they stand in a row over him and say the mourners' blessing and anything that is honor for the living.

**** ENGLISH ****
A banished person who died—his law is like one who kills himself knowingly: they do not tear, remove shoes, or eulogize over him; and they place a stone on his coffin—and this is for contempt of court when he transgressed words of Torah; but for monetary matters, once he died he is exempt from their decree and they do not place a stone on his coffin and eulogize him properly.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="11"></i>ארון העובר ממקום למקום אם שלדו <small>(פירוש השדרה וצלעותיה) </small>קיימת עומדין עליו בשורה ואומרים עליו ברכת אבלים ותנחומי אבלים אם יש אבלים שמתאבלין עליו ואם אין שלדו קיימת אין עומדין עליו בשורה ואין אומרים עליו ברכת אבלים ולא תנחומי אבלים:
**** ENGLISH ****
A coffin passing from place to place—if his skeleton (meaning the spine and its ribs) remains, they stand in a row over it and say mourners' blessing and consolation of mourners if there are mourners mourning him; and if his skeleton does not remain, they do not stand in a row and do not say mourners' blessing or consolation of mourners.
