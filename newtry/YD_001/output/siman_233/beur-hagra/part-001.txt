<b>(Likut) Even though, etc.</b> This follows from what is stated there: "No, Rav Hamnuna derived it from the matter itself," etc. For if that were so, why would it be necessary to infer it indirectly, and to explain that the reason concerning the later age, etc.? **** ENGLISH ****
<b>(Likut) And from thirteen, etc.</b> Even though he did not produce two hairs. This is the Rambam's view, and it is likewise implied by the unqualified wording of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, which refers back to what was stated above, "even though," etc. However, the Lechem Mishneh and the Mishneh LaMelech question his ruling. Rav Ovadiah of Bartenura likewise writes this in the Mishnah there, as does the Tosafot Yom Tov; see there carefully. (End of Likut.)
