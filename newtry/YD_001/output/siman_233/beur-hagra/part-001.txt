**** ENGLISH ****(Likut) Even though, etc. — from what was written "and it is not so," Rav Hamnuna from the body of the matter, etc.; and if it were so, why need to infer from the precision, and the reason for after time, etc., to infer from the body, for the first part also comes from "I brought," and nevertheless it is not as after time. But what the Rambam wrote, that even after thirteen years likewise, even when he did not bring — his words are puzzling, for we explicitly say there specifically when he brought (end quote).
