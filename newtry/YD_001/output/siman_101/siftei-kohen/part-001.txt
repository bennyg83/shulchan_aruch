**** ENGLISH ****
["Every piece that was forbidden only on account of a peel, etc. It is written in Sefer Lev Chaim (daf 210b): and it requires study — from where was it forbidden on account of a peel? Only because it absorbed taste from another prohibition; and if so, even when the whole piece became forbidden, etc. end of his words. And to reconcile this: for we find it in basar b'chalav, etc. — investigate. In any case, what the Rav brought from Tosafot was not found; only that Tosafot at the beginning of the chapter of Gid HaNasheh (Chullin daf 96) wrote regarding a place of cutting, etc. — see there. And what the Shach wrote in the name of Lev Chaim is puzzling, for the peel itself has no prohibition upon it, etc. — investigate. And it appears that he holds that even if it is not rendered nevelah, nevertheless if it is possible to squeeze it out it is forbidden, as below siman 106, etc. — see there. And Tosafot in several places in Chullin and in the passage "a piece that absorbed," etc., hold that this depends on one another — it is impossible for the piece to become nevelah if it is possible to squeeze it out it is forbidden; we do not say thus; since the peel did not become nevelah, if it is possible to squeeze it out it is permitted and there is no prohibition upon it, and tomorrow it will return to being permitted — how can we say it is a piece worthy of honor? This is one point. Furthermore, since we hold like Rabbeinu Tam that a peel does not forbid its mixture in wet-with-wet, and one does not even need sixty, even though the taste is d'oraisa — how can we say that in dry-with-dry they will not be nullified on account of a peel, even in a thousand, on account of a piece worthy of honor, which is entirely a rabbinic decree? For regarding nosen taam d'oraisa we are not concerned, and for a rabbinic matter we are concerned — this matter has no relevance. And Rabeinu Yerucham and the other poskim who hold that a peel has the status of a piece worthy of honor hold thus because they hold like Riva that wet-with-wet forbids with nosen taam and requires sixty — and so too in dry. But we, who hold like Rabbeinu Tam that one does not need sixty against the peel and it does not forbid the mixture at all, it is obvious that we do not apply a piece worthy of honor to it. And the Rama ruled well:"]
