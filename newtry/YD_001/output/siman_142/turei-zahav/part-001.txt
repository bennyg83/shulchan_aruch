**** ENGLISH ****Forbidden to benefit from its coals. It appears to me even after the fact forbidden; and proof: so is stated end of chapter "they lower" — hekdesh coals one commits me'ilah with, flame one does not benefit from and does not commit me'ilah; idolatry coals forbidden — Rashi explained "nothing of the ban shall cleave to your hand"; and flame permitted; and we challenge: what is different regarding flame of idolatry that permitted completely as taught "permitted" and even rabbinically they did not decree; and what is different regarding hekdesh flame that forbidden as taught "one does not benefit"; and we answer: idolatry is repulsive and people avoid it — Rabbis did not decree; hekdesh not repulsive and people do not avoid — Rabbis decreed — end; and if coals were forbidden only ab initio — one must say rabbinically; if so we find regarding idolatry they decreed; and in Gemara it appears they did not decree except clear Torah prohibition in coals as Rashi explained; and if so even after the fact forbidden. And Beit Yosef wrote from the difficulty in this siman that specifically ab initio coals forbidden; and Maharach wrote so is simple; and per my humble opinion they were not precise at all; and Beit Yosef's difficulty will be explained in seif 6 with God's help — no difficulty.
