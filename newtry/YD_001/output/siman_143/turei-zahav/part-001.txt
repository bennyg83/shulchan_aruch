**** ENGLISH ****But he builds at first etc. So ruled Rashi and the Rambam in the Mishnah that teaches "they build with them bimosiot" — meaning something that is a utensil-of-a-utensil for idolatry. And Tosafot disagree and read in the Gemara "bimosiaot" — meaning bathhouses; and so the Arukh and the Tur brought in the conclusion. But a utensil-of-a-utensil for idolatry is forbidden; and therefore wonder on the Shulchan Aruch and Rama that they were not concerned to rule stringently, especially regarding idol prohibition, for we find many times they were more stringent than other prohibitions — behold at the end of siman 139 they were stringent to sell them even quills and ink and other stringencies; kal vachomer son of kal vachomer regarding the traklin belonging to the dome of an idol. And it appears further that even for Rashi and the Rambam there is no permission except through mere assistance alone, for it is stated in the Mishnah in this language "they build with them"; and the Rambam whom the mechaber copied here wrote at the beginning of the seif "it is forbidden to build with gentiles" etc., and above he says "but he builds" etc. — meaning with them specifically. And we find many times that assistance has no substance; but to build alone is forbidden for everyone. But in practice it is certainly fitting for us to rule like R. Yosi who saw Rashi's words and disagreed with them; and to him you shall listen, to distance from ugliness and the like. And the Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit wrote in the name of Or Zaru'a that for the Perishah — one who sells his house willingly to an idol, the money is forbidden and he leads it to the Dead Sea; but if under compulsion the money is permitted.
