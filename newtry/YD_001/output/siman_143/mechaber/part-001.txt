**** ENGLISH ****
A law that one should not build his house adjacent to a house of idolatry, and in what manner it is permitted to benefit from idolatry; it contains 6 se’ifim. One whose house was adjacent to a house of elilim, and it fell, is forbidden to rebuild it. How should he act? He draws inward into his own property and builds, and he fills that space with thorns or filth so that he not expand the house of elilim. If the wall was jointly his and of the elilim, it is judged half and half: his half is permitted in benefit, and the half of the elilim is forbidden (and if he cannot distinguish, all is forbidden in benefit) (Tur). Its stones, its wood, and its ashes are all forbidden in benefit. (Nevertheless, it is permitted to sell them his portion wherever it is) (Ran, perek Kol HaTzelamim).

**** ENGLISH ****
It is forbidden to build together with a non-Jew a domed structure in which they set up elilim. And if he transgressed and built (even the idolatry itself) (Kol Bo), his wages are permitted. But he may build l'chatchila the banquet hall or the courtyard in which that dome is.

**** ENGLISH ****
If elilim has a garden or a bathhouse, and the benefit that comes out of them goes to the priests, it is permitted to benefit from them not b’tovah, but it is forbidden to benefit from them b’tovah, even if others have a share in the benefit together with the priests. But if the benefit that comes out of them does not go to the priests but rather to their worshippers, it is permitted to benefit from them even b’tovah — even if the garden and bathhouse are for the elilim alone (so it appears in Tur according to Rashi). {Rama: And there are those who say that it is forbidden to benefit from it when the benefit goes to the priests only when they are standing in the courtyard of the idolatry itself; but when they are not standing before it, even though the benefit that comes out of them goes to the priests, it is permitted to benefit from it unless the benefit goes to the idolatry itself, and one may rely on this to be lenient (Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, and Tosafos daf 44, and Rosh and Mordechai in the name of Rabbeinu Tam).}
