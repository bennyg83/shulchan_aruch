**** ENGLISH ****
Regarding that it is permitted to lend to a non-Jew and to a meshumad with interest; it contains 3 se’ifim. By Torah law it is permitted to lend to a non-Jew with interest; but the Sages forbade it unless it is for his livelihood, or for a talmid chacham, or with interest that is by rabbinic law; and nowadays it is permitted (in any case) (the Tur).

<i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="5"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="6"></i>כותיים יש להם <i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="5"></i>דין <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="5"></i>מומר לעבודת כוכבים. <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="6"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="5"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="7"></i>הקראים אין להם דין מומרים ואסור להלוותם ברבית ואין צריך לומר <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="6"></i><i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="6"></i>שאסור ללוות מהם ברבית: <small>הגה וע"ל סימן קנ"ז וקכ"ד דין אנוסין. <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="6"></i>תינוק שנשבה לבין העובדי כוכבים ואינו יודע מתורת ישראל כלל דינו כקראים ואסור להלוות לו ברבית (כך משמע מב"י לדעת הרמב"ם) <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="7"></i>ולכן <i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="7"></i>מומרת לעבודת כוכבים שיש לה בן מן העובד כוכבים <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="8"></i>שהבן הרי הוא כמוה ונקרא מומר <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="7"></i>אסור להלוות לו ברבית (מרדכי פ' החולץ) דהוי כתינוק שנשבה לבין העובדי כוכבים:</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
The Kusim have the law of a meshumad to idolatry. The Kara’im do not have the law of meshumadim, and it is forbidden to lend to them with interest — and it need not be said that it is forbidden to borrow from them with interest. {Rama: And see above siman 157 and siman 124 regarding the law of those coerced. A captured child who was captured among non-Jews and does not know at all from the Torah of Israel — his law is like the Kara’im, and it is forbidden to lend to him with interest (so it is implied from the Beit Yosef according to the opinion of Rambam). And therefore, a female meshumedes to idolatry who has a son from a non-Jew — the son is like her and is called a meshumad — it is forbidden to lend to him with interest (Mordechai, perek HaCholeitz), for he is like a captured child among non-Jews.}
