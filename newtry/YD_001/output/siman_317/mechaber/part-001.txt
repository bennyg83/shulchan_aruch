**** HEBREW ****
<b>דין בכור שנתערב ומת אחד מהבהמות. ובו ג' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>שנים שהפקידו אצל אחד שני זכרים אחד בכור וא' פשוט ומת אחד מהם ואינו יודע איזה <small>(מת) </small>מניח הנשאר ביניהם <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i>והוא בין <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>שניהם ודינו כספק בכור:
**** ENGLISH ****
The law of a firstborn that became mixed and one of the animals died. In it are 3 seifim. Two people who deposited with one person two males—one a firstborn and one ordinary—and one of them died and it is unknown which died—they leave what remains between them, and it is between both of them and its law is like a doubtful firstborn.

**** HEBREW ****
<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>מי שהפקיד בכור אצל חבירו ולחבירו פשוט <small>(פי' שאינו בכור) </small>ומת אחד מהם המוציא מחבירו עליו הראיה <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="2"></i>וכיון שהנפקד מוחזק יחזיקנו ויאכלנו במומו:
**** ENGLISH ****
One who deposited a firstborn with his fellow and his fellow had an ordinary animal (meaning: not a firstborn), and one of them died—the burden of proof is on the one who extracts from his fellow; and since the depositor is in possession, he retains it and eats it in its blemished state.
**** END BLOCK ****
