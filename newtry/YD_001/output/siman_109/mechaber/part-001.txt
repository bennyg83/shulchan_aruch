The law of dry in dry that became mixed. In it are 2 seifim: A piece that is not worthy of honor that became mixed with others — same kind, dry in dry (meaning that it is not blended and the issur stands by itself but became mixed and one does not recognize it) (Beit Yosef in name of responsum of Rashba and Ramban and Sefer HaTerumah) — one in two is nullified and permitted to eat them, one person each one by itself; but he may not eat all three together; and there is one who forbids one person to eat them even one after another: {Rama: And so one should practice l'chatchila; and some are stringent to throw one out or give to a gentile (Hagahot ShaDa in name of Maharam and Rashi in Avodah Zarah daf 74a); and this is only a general stringency; and all this is when mixed in its kind; but when not in its kind and one does not recognize it — even dry in dry — need sixty (Tur in name of Baal HaTerumah and many other poskim); and there is no distinction in all this whether the issur is d'rabbanan or d'oraisa (Beit Yosef in name of poskim unlike Hagahot ShaDa); and see above siman 122 if vessels became mixed together.}

Dry in dry that was nullified one in two — if they cooked them all as one, and even to eat each one by itself — forbidden if there is not sixty, because the gravy is noten ta'am and is absorbed in the pieces; and if he wants to cook them together and there is not sixty — permitted to increase upon them so that there is a measure of sixty and to cook them, and there is no concern of nullifying issur: {Rama: And some say if the mixture was known before they were cooked together — all is permitted, for they do not return and become forbidden after they were nullified dry (Tur in name of Rosh and R' Yerucham in name of Tosafot, and he wrote that so is primary); and in a place of loss one may rely on the lenient and permit.}
