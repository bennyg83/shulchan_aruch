חד בתרי בטל. דכתיב אחרי רבים להטות ולא החמירו רבנן להצריך ס' כיון שאינה ראוייה להתכבד רק שהחמירו שלא יאכל שלשתן כא' דהא ממה נפשך אוכל האיסור באותה אכילה:
**** ENGLISH ****
One in two is nullified. For it is written "follow the majority," and the Sages were not stringent to require sixty since it is not worthy of honor — only that they were stringent that he not eat all three as one, for either way he eats the issur in that eating:

**** ENGLISH ****
"When it became mixed in its kind." For in its kind even wet in wet one does not need sixty only d'rabbanan, as he wrote siman 98 seif 8; in dry even d'rabbanan they were not stringent; but not in its kind, from the Torah one needs wet in wet sixty since there is giving of taste, end of his words — the Sages were stringent also in dry to require sixty lest one come to d'oraisa issur in cooking) and it is difficult to me: before cooking it was nullified in the majority, and if he wants he may eat it from the Torah and it becomes complete heter — why should it be forbidden afterward from the Torah in cooking; and what he wrote from that in siman 98 seif 2, when it became mixed in its kind and not in its kind we say its kind dominates over it and there is no issur in what the issur afterward imparts taste in not in its kind, since it has already become heter, and from the Torah one does not need sixty — let us say likewise here; and one may say that there even though the issur also gives taste in not in its kind, nevertheless the taste is not sensed by itself unlike in this law where the taste of the issur is sensed by itself:
