**** ENGLISH ****
And the issur, etc. — the piece by itself is called dry in dry; only the taste that it imparted to the piece at its side is called wet in wet. By way of parable: pieces of issur in same kind — according to R' Yehudah, same kind is not nullified, meaning wet in wet, but not dry in dry; the piece with its fellow is called dry in dry, but the taste it received from its fellow is called wet in wet and is not nullified:
