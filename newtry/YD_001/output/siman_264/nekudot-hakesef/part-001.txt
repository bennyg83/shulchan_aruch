המיקל ובזה ניחא מה שדחה הב"י דברי רבינו ירוחם שפסק כרשב"ג וכ"פ הסמ"ג עשין כ"ח כרשב"ג. מיהו קשה דה"ל להת"ה להביא פלוגתא דשמואל ורשב"ג ולהכריע להקל לכך נראה דס"ל דפלוגתא דרשב"ג ושמואל מיירי דהיינו שמצד בנער בן שנה כו' עד הפריעה למקומה עכ"ל משמע גם כן מזה כדעת התרומות הדשן דהיכא דקצת העטרה מגולה א"צ למול ש ית אלא יש לתקן כשיחזור העור לאחוריו: בש"ך סוף סי' רס"ד הקשתי על דברי החכם הספרדי ועכשיו מצאתי ת"ל ראיה ברורה לדברי מדברי הרמב"ם בפירוש המשנה ס"פ ר"א דמילה עיין שם:
**** ENGLISH ****(There Taz s.k. 9) He challenged Tur HaTov and wrote: there is no proof from this; it is not precise — Tur HaTov there did not infer anything from this, only wrote there it appears from reasoning thus, since he is already circumcised properly by Torah law it is circumcision and he did not infer from Rambam, only that in what appears circumcised one must repair the flesh from here and from here because of mar'it ha'ayin — that repair for appearance is to push the skin back and tie it there, and one need not cut the skin, for Rambam wrote one need not cut anything, etc.; and also from what he wrote at the end in what does not appear circumcised that one must cut the skin — it implies at the beginning in what appears circumcised one need not cut the skin — and not as appears from Rashi that for mar'it ha'ayin one must cut the skin. So are Maharai's words in Tur HaTov there — see there — explained explicitly thus. Also what he wrote "also this requires investigation" — for in Rosh, etc. — to me Tur HaTov's meaning is: in Rosh he did not write this repair for appearance circumcised, only wrote in general "and if it appears circumcised one need not circumcise him" — implies one need not cut skin at all; if so perforce Rosh must hold the mishnah refers to terumah — see there — so appears explicitly in Tur HaTov. Also Tur and Shulchan Aruch's words can be explained like Tur HaTov's view — this repair is to push the skin from behind him; and so Bach wrote.

However in my humble opinion it appears Rambam and Rashi do not disagree in law, only in explaining the baraita — Rambam explains the mishnah deals similar to Shmuel, and so appears explicitly from his words in commentary on the Mishnah, therefore he had to say one need not cut anything; but Rashi appears to explain the mishnah deals at the time of circumcision — if there was excess flesh, even though they circumcised properly, if it appears the flesh returns and covers the gid they repair it and scrape with a scalpel at that thickness — see there — so appears Rashi's view explicitly; but here if after time after circumcision he appears circumcised when aroused — Rashi concedes one need not cut from the flesh at all, and this is Shmuel's case; and so appears from Rabbeinu Yerucham Netiv 1 part 2: tzitzin that impede circumcision — one returns and repairs and cuts them; he circumcised and did not uncover as if he did not circumcise; and a fleshy child whose fat makes flesh appear to return and cover the gid — they repair and scrape with a scalpel at that thickness; and a child pendulous in thick fat flesh — and after circumcision appears as covering him — they look at him when aroused; if he appears circumcised one need not circumcise him — end quote; behold his view is like Rashi as I explained; so it seems clear to me, and not like Tur HaTov; also not like Bach who wrote Rashi disagrees with Rambam, and per this one may say also Rosh and Or Zaru'a and Semak agree with Rambam in law.

However it is difficult for me on Tur HaTov — why is it obvious to him from reasoning that in what appears circumcised like one-third of the atarah suffices — for this is the dispute of Shmuel and Rabbah; and Beit Yosef wrote that according to Rambam, Tur, and Ittur like Shmuel we require actual appearance, but "appears and does not appear" requires returning to circumcise; and one may say Tur HaTov ruled like Rabbah who is a tanna, and also since it is only rabbinic halachah follows the lenient view — and with this Beit Yosef's rejection of Rabbeinu Yerucham who ruled like Rabbah is satisfied; and so Semag positive mitzvot 28 like Rabbah. However it is difficult — Tur HaTov should have brought the dispute of Shmuel and Rabbah and ruled to be lenient; therefore it appears he holds the dispute of Rabbah and Shmuel deals where on one side it appears circumcised properly the entire atarah and on the other side around the gid the atarah does not appear at all — and this is "appears and does not appear" like one-third of the atarah — this is called "appears," and according to all one need not return to circumcise — so it seems to me — examine. And see in Maharil end of Laws of Circumcision he wrote: an incident with a youth one year old, etc., until the uncovering to its place — end quote — also implies thus like Tur HaTeshuvah, that where part of the atarah is exposed one need not circumcise, only repair when the skin returns behind it.
