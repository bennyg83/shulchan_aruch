אח"כ אבל אם היה נולד מהול קצת פשיטא לי שאחר כך אם נמשך העור שצריך לחתוך אותו כיון שלא נימול שום פעם כראוי עדיין:
**** ENGLISH ****One must repair excess flesh. Explanation: by drawing the skin, etc.; and as written after this; and that gloss explains Shulchan Aruch's words before it; and the reason is: by Torah law, since he was once circumcised properly, one need not circumcise him again even if afterward it became covered, even entirely, even at arousal — only rabbinically they required circumcising again; and this is specifically if now he appears completely uncircumcised — then one must cut the covering skin; but if now he appears partially circumcised even though the atarah is not seen except at its head like one-third, even when aroused one need not cut afterward anything — only repair by pushing the skin behind it; and not as Rashi implies that here too one must repair by cutting skin — so Pri Chadash wrote siman 264; and his main proof from Rambam: if at arousal he does not appear circumcised they return and cut from the pendulous flesh, etc. — meaning if he does not appear circumcised at all; and there is no proof that anything not circumcised properly is called "not circumcised." He further brings proof from that Or Zaru'a, Semak, and Rosh did not mention this matter of repairing for mar'it ha'ayin if he appears circumcised when aroused — and certainly that repair refers only to terumah; but for an infant's circumcision no repair is needed if he appears partially circumcised. Also this requires investigation — for Rosh, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch brought this — implies we require repair with a scalpel for mar'it ha'ayin; and also according to Tur HaTov there is concern that many times the mohel errs at circumcision and thinks he already circumcised and uncovered properly but in truth did not uncover properly, for sometimes very thin skin is found after cutting as Limel wrote, and it is like mere saliva, and he uncovers that Limel — and the main uncovering remains; and I have seen mohalim err in this — therefore at every circumcision there should be other mohalim who look well whether the uncovering is proper. Practical difference: if afterward it became covered again — if we say he already fulfilled circumcision properly by Torah as we said, it is uncertain for me: if he was born circumcised and they drew covenant blood from him, and afterward because of fat the skin stretched upward and the entire atarah is not seen even after arousal — who says one must cut the skin with a scalpel and repair properly? Here it is not applicable to say he was already circumcised properly by Torah, for at that hour he had no foreskin at all; it is comparable to covering blood — we say in chapter covering blood: wind covered it and it was revealed again — he is obligated to cover, for there is no deferral by mitzvot; so too here literally — we do not care that it was not fit to circumcise at its time, nevertheless the obligation comes upon him afterward even by Torah. Or one may say it differs here: by Torah obligation is only foreskin at birth and not what stretched afterward; but if he was born partially circumcised it is plain to me that afterward if skin stretched one must cut it, since he was never circumcised properly even once.
