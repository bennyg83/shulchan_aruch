**And brought out treifah.** See Terumat HaDeshen tinyana Choshen Mishpat siman 3 regarding a slaughterer who was tested and they asked the old slaughterer how it went and he answered “it is good, kosher”—and in truth treifah was found; it appears he did not check the lung properly. He wrote that what he slaughtered of birds afterward there is no concern to forbid, for one suspected of d'rabbanan issur is not suspected of Torah issur; and since he is suspected only that he does not check the lung properly, here there is only d'rabbanan issur—for from the Torah once slaughtered it is permitted also for animals; and the old man standing over him also no concern, since regarding knife examination he is not suspected, for that is Torah issur. Regarding lung examination—the old slaughterer did not become incompetent, for weakness of strength causes him not to feel knife flaws, not lung examination. Also on the slaughterer who failed one may be lenient not to remove him entirely, since he answered “it is good,” etc. It appears his intent: per his view the shechitah is kosher but he does not want to rely on his view—for otherwise he would have answered briefly; one does not remove him only for a month or two, then he confesses his sin and accepts to be very careful in lung examination; and in any case for a full year he should not rely on feeling by hand inside, only take out the lung until he is well accustomed—then his conduct like other slaughterers—examine. See Noda BiYehudah kama part 14 siman 1 s.v. veod. However if possible to compel them to come before court and testify what is proper—provided they are not unworthy people—examine.]
