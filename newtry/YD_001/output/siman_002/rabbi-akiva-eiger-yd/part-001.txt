**(siman 2 Shach seif 2) For even Israelite mumar to entire Torah except idol worship and Shabbat—his shechitah forbidden d'oraisa.** Per my view if this necessity from our sugya, it challenges Rashba that mumar eating nevelot to provoke—his shechitah kosher; and Ran regarding one not concerned to slaughter properly—still called fit to slaughter since commanded; if so mumar to entire Torah or idol worship—why forbidden d'oraisa? What of it that he is like non-Jew—non-Jew himself if fit to slaughter his shechitah would be kosher; mumar is called fit since commanded. What difference between mumar to entire Torah and mumar eating nevelot to provoke—rather certainly only d'rabbanan; and Shas that challenges Rav Anan from baraita—since we do not accept sacrifice from Torah from mumar to entire Torah; same mumar to idol worship—proven d'oraisa mumar to idol worship like mumar to entire Torah; since Rav Anan said only mumar to idol worship permitted to eat—implies but mumar to entire Torah forbidden in any case d'rabbanan; automatically mumar to idol worship forbidden too, since like mumar to entire Torah regarding sacrifice d'oraisa, same in shechitah d'rabbanan.

**(seif 2) And permitted to eat from his shechitah.** See Rashba and Ran who wrote we are not concerned perhaps majority simanim not severed—for majority who want to slaughter kosher slaughter majority simanim and immediately after shechitah check; if see majority not severed return and finish shechitah immediately—not called great effort—examine. We should judge for our times that we forbid delay—all the more concern perhaps majority not severed first and needed to check simanim and found majority not severed—although afterward finished majority, nevertheless there was some delay; must say specifically if we know he delayed we forbid, fear they cannot be expert in measure of delay; but since in truth per majority majority simanim severed immediately—only without checking simanim forbidden d'rabbanan as Tosafot (9a)—therefore not so concerned for delay. Still difficult: found meat where majority of city Jewish—should forbid concern slaughterer checked simanim and majority not severed; since we forbid any delay and slaughterer knows no remedy by finishing—he withdrew hand and did not return to finish majority since no benefit; double doubt: perhaps did not give another to slaughter; if he slaughtered, perhaps examined knife well first. Only one doubt Rambam stringent. Plainly with mumar one can say if sees majority not severed returns and finishes immediately—even though wants to eat biblically forbidden, does all possible in permitted way and finishes so as not to eat d'oraisa issur and at least permitted by law; no doubt we fear delay—so above. But found meat—great concern slaughterer due to delay forbidden and unwilling to eat does not finish; possible our times truly not permitted except found slaughtered and checks simanim see majority severed—then for law even where no concern meat hidden from eye like lost kids with presumption—even so requires found slaughtered seeing majority simanim severed. **I was asked** why Rif omitted Rava's statement mumar eating nevelot for appetite examines knife and gives him—we find no disputant except where Abaye said not like Rav Ashi does not hold that Rava; halachah like Rava; especially as challenge to Rav Anan resolved, support remains for Rava from baraita even Israelite mumar—we have no other establishment except to include mumar for that matter; all Rishonim wrote this proof. Add spice: Rif seems challenged—R. Eliezer b. R.
