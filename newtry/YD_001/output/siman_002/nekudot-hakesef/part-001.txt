**(seif 14)** He challenged Bach and wants to say slaughtering for idol worship requires specifically one siman and not any amount suffices. All his words are puzzling; Bach's words are clear—for explicitly proven in Shas 40a that even in any whatsoever of kaneh it is invalid when slaughtering for idol worship—for otherwise the challenge from Rav Huna is nothing: since Rav Huna compared his teaching to Ulla, and Ulla said any act whatsoever—for this “any whatsoever” is siman 1. If so all come together; and there is no need to emend there regarding when half kaneh was damaged and he added any amount and finished—for all come together; for even if kaneh not damaged at all, siman 1 does not obligate only when slaughtering for idol worship one siman of kaneh specifically—and if so all come together. Rather certainly since Ulla said “act” and meaning is act of any whatsoever, if so even in minority of kaneh suffices; and so proven explicitly from Rashi and Tosafot there—examine. Also what he brought from Rambam chapter 5 Hilchos Avodah Zarah and Tur below siman 145 that even slaughtering for idol worship is forbidden only when slaughtering one siman, etc.—certainly per his citation one can extract this meaning from “only when slaughtering one siman”—implies specifically one siman; but in truth not so in Rambam and Tur there: Rambam wrote there: when is this said? When the animal is not forbidden when he did no act; but if he did any act he forbade it, such as slaughtering one siman for idol worship—end. Apparently even any amount suffices; “such as slaughtering one siman for idol worship” is just an example, not only one siman. Tur siman 145 similarly: if he did an act that he slaughtered it for idol worship it is forbidden even in one siman—meaning he said; they came only to say even in act of any whatsoever suffices and we do not need great act of two simanim. Since in act of any whatsoever suffices, it is obvious even in minority of kaneh suffices; and Rambam and Tur's words are no better than Rav Huna in Shas who also said he slaughtered one siman—meaning so, as explained in Shas; how much more Rambam and Tur who wrote explicitly “any amount.” Also what Bach wrote he did not find resolution in the matter—it is not so: excellent resolution is found, for Rambba said not heretic attached to idol worship—examine; and necessarily proven to say so—for if heretic whose plain thought is idol worship, certainly forbidden even in act of any whatsoever, as I explained; all this is clear.
