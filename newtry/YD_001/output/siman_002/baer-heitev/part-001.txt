**in public.** That is, before ten Israelite men. He must also desecrate several times in public, but not for one matter. Implied from Tosafot Eruvin 69a: even transgressing a d'rabbanan issur is called desecrating Shabbat if before ten Israelites and he does so several times—Hagahot Beit Yosef—examine. Perek 8 of Kiddushin disagrees. A butcher from whom treifah came out once is fit to write a get even if he eats nevelot to provoke, even if he desecrates Shabbat in private—Radbaz beginning chapter 5. See Mishpetei Tzedek vol. 2 siman 75. Rashba wrote: a convert who returned to bad ways from fear, and an Israelite who sinned from fear lest they kill him—he and his shechitah are kosher. Chayei Adam siman 185 wrote: to sell nevelot to a mumar and all similar—one should be stringent l'chatchila (though one cannot say otherwise, as must be). Also one who pursues and procures a prostitute for him; unlike one who arranges with non-Jews, where there is no issur. Ramban made medicine for an idolatress to bear. See Rashba responsa simanim 109 and 164. Nevertheless he has kabbalah from elders dealing with these—he will not emerge clean from improper seed, as hinted in “do not intermarry with them… lest they turn,” etc.

<b>פסולה. </b> כתב בש"ך דרוב הפוסקים חולקין על דין זה ופסקו דכשר וסבירא להו דלא דמיא למה שכתב בסימן ה' בשנים שאחזו בסכין ואחד שוחט לשם דבר פסול שפסול דהתם מחשבת השוחט פסלה. אבל הכא דלא אסור אלא משום דלאו בני שחיטה נינהו וא"כ כשיש אחד בר שחיטה זולתו די וכשר ומ"מ הב"ח חשש להחמיר כדעת הש"ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
**invalid.** Shach wrote: most poskim disagree with this law and ruled kosher; it seems to them it is not comparable to siman 5 with two holding the knife and one slaughtering for an unfit matter—for there the slaughterer's intent disqualifies; here it is forbidden only because they are not “children of shechitah,” so when one fit to slaughter besides him exists, that suffices and it is kosher; nevertheless Bach was concerned to be stringent as Shulchan Aruch.
