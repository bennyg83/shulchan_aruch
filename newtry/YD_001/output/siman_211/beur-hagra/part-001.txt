<b>האומר כו' בד"א כו' וי"א כו'. </b>כ"ג א' וכיון דאמר כל כו' וכתב הרא"ש בפסקיו וא"ת לימא שביטל בלחש ופי' הרא"מ כו'. וסברא הראשונה היא לדעת הר"נ שם ד"ה כיון כו':
**** ENGLISH ****
One who says, etc. When is this said, etc. And some say, etc. Nedarim 23a; and since he said "all," etc.; and Rosh wrote in his piskei — you might say he cancelled in a whisper; Raam explains, etc.; and the first reasoning is Ran there s.v. keivan, etc.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who stipulated, etc. As Tosafot and Rosh wrote — Rava does not argue with Abaye, for the stam of the sugya says the opposite: when he remembers, etc.; and further Rava said the tanna completes, etc.; and if so it refers to Abaye, whose words are clear in the wording of the mishnah; and further he said lest they conduct, etc., which does not apply to Rava — see there.

<b>אין כו'. </b>דאזלינן בתר דעתו של המדיר וכמ"ש בסי' ר"י ס"א וראיה ממ"ש <small>(כ"ה א' ובשבועות) </small>שמשביעין ע"ד ב"ד ולא חיישינן שמא יבטל קודם וכן בנדרי אנסין יבטל קודם לסברת הר"נ בלחש ואף לסברת הרא"ש דהא כאן דברים שבלב מהני וכן בפ"ד דגטין <small>(ל"ד ל"ה) </small>דמדרינן לאשה כו' ואמרינן <small>(שם ל"ה ב') </small>ל"ש אלא כו' ואם איתא שמא תבטל:
**** ENGLISH ****
These matters are not said, etc. We follow the intent of the forbidding party, as written in siman 210 seif 1; and proof from what is written (25a and in Shevuot) that they make one swear before the court and we are not concerned lest he cancel beforehand; and likewise coerced vows he cancels beforehand per Ran's view of whisper, and even per Rosh's view that here heart-matters help; and likewise chapter 4 of Gitin (34–35) we forbid a woman, etc., and we say (there 35b) "lashon ela," etc. — and if it were so, perhaps she would cancel.
