<b>ואם נעקרו מהחוליא כו'. </b>ובה"ג דף קכ"ט ע"א מטריף בנעקרה צלע א' אע"פ שכל החוליא קיימת ויש לחוש לדבריו במקום שאין הפסד מרובה שכל דבריו דברי קבלה הם וכ"פ הב"ח אבל מ"ש הב"ח שגם בהפסד מרובה צ"ע הוא תימא דהרי כל הפוסקים הם להדיא נגד בה"ג וכן בש"ס ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
If they broke, etc. — Taz wrote: for that which we are stringent in siman 53 regarding a broken wing near the body because perhaps the lung was perforated — if so, all the more so regarding one broken rib in a bird in the place where the lung is hidden there one must be stringent for practical law of treifah; and so is implied from Rashba's words in Terumat HaDeshen that broken ribs are worse than a broken wing (and Beit Yosef brings it in siman 53), end of his words; and so wrote Bach — and it is implied from his words there that even in an animal it is treifah, only he permits when there is no spur on the broken rib; and he wrote: and it is certainly implied that even when the fragments rejoined, if one sees there was a spur there — treifah; but if it is doubtful whether there was a spur — kosher from double doubt (and Rokeach wrote: double doubt — if the rib broke and entered the body cavity one must check the lung [as] regarding a fallen animal, end of his words); and even though we declare treifah in a bird's wing even without a spur — nevertheless, since it is a stringency as written in siman 53, give it to them that they should not add upon it; and this is specifically in the wing itself of the bird, but not in ribs, since in any case it is implied that one may distinguish that a wing is worse than a rib, as Taz wrote; and nevertheless it appears that in a bird even Bach agrees it is treifah even without a spur, as is implied from Taz's words; and nevertheless requires further study in these laws, since the early and later poskim did not mention this) — and all wrote anonymously: its ribs broke — kosher:
