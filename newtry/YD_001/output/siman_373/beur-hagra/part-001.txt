**** ENGLISH ****
<b>It is forbidden.</b> Nazir 42b and the Yerushalmi in Nazir chapter 3. See Tosafot Niddah 57a. <b>(Likkut) It is forbidden.</b> Although Evel Rabbati records a dispute between R. Tarfon and R.
