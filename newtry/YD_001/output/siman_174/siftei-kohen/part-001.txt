<b>רבית קצוצה הוא. </b>ולדעת האומרים דמשכנתא בלא נכייתא הוי א"ר לעיל סימן קע"ב ה"ה הכא כדמוכח בתוס' והרא"ש וכ"כ ב"י והרב לעיל ס"ס קס"ד ומה שלא הגיה כאן כלום הוא מפני שסמך עצמו אמ"ש לעיל והעט"ז שסתם הדברים לא כוון יפה:
**** ENGLISH ****It is fixed ribbit. Per those who hold mortgage without deduction is avak ribbit — above siman 172 — likewise here as proven in Tosafot and Rosh; and so Beit Yosef and Rav above siman 164; and what he did not emend here is because he relied on what is stated above; and Eitan HaEzrachi who left matters unspecified did not aim well.

<b>אינו תנאי. </b>כיון שלא התנה המוכר וכ' הפרישה דהיינו דוקא באומר כן קודם גמר המקח אבל אם אמר כן בשעה שמתחיל המקח אפילו התנה הלוקח מהני כדאיתא בח"מ ס"ס ר"ז וכ"כ הב"ח וע"ש בסי' ר"ז איזה תנאי מבטל המקח ואיזה אינו מבטל ומשם תלמוד לכאן דכל היכא דהמקח קיים מותר ואם לאו אסור:
**** ENGLISH ****
It is not a condition. Since the seller did not stipulate; and the Derishab wrote that this is specifically where he says so before the conclusion of the transaction; but if he said so at the time when the transaction begins — even if the buyer stipulated it is effective, as in CM siman 207 end; and likewise Bach; and see there in siman 207 which condition voids the transaction and which does not; and from there apply here — wherever the transaction stands it is permitted, and if not, it is forbidden.
**** END BLOCK ****

**** ENGLISH ****And we extract from him, etc. Bach wrote this siman continuing after Beit Yosef — not so; and I say author follows his reasoning who ruled above siman 164 and this seif like Rif and Rambam and supporters — fixed ribbit; and Rav who did not emend here relied on above as he did not emend for this reason also this seif; also on essence of law it seems here even per those who hold avak ribbit — agrees we extract fruits he ate; as Rosh wrote: some say mortgage without deduction — here though not fixed ribbit — nevertheless forgiveness in error, for he entered field as sale and sale is not sale — like one who leaves fellow to take object from his domain, or gave him thinking it was his and it is not his — end quote; and so Tosafot and other poskim Beit Yosef brought CM siman 207 seif 13 — meaning; but in mortgage borrower knew it was his and forgives; only Derishah and Sema CM siman 182 and Bach strained themselves per Tur's words that fruits he ate are avak ribbit and do not come out in court; and he did not explain nevertheless because of forgiveness in error we extract — implies complete forgiveness; and per this they strained to reconcile Rosh's words agreeing with Tur; their words do not seem correct; also wonder on Derishah: difficulty Rosh raised initially Ravina on Ravina should remain per Rosh's view — see there; rather seems as Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe: Tur too agrees because of forgiveness in error we extract; and what he did not explain thus — seems automatically implied since he wrote it is avak ribbit and does not come out in court — evidently does not come out in court because avak ribbit; and ribbit applies only with borrower's knowledge, e.g. seller initially said he forgives eating fruits; and so Raavad in Hasagot: Ravina extracted fruits not because of ribbit but because of robbery; but if he forgave and again claimed — they do not return to him, etc.; and likewise he wrote there on him; and what he wrote: but if he forgave, etc. — even in fixed ribbit forgiveness helps, etc.; even in fixed ribbit forgiveness helps when he already took (and as above siman 165 seif 5, etc.) — this is not challenge, for we find even when he forgave before eating fruits that he eat afterward; and as above siman 165 seif 5 — understand; and so essence for law we extract fruits he ate, for all poskim ruled like Ravina who acted and extracted fruits — unlike Bach; only some hold because of fixed ribbit and some because of forgiveness in error — see CM siman 207.

<b>שניהם אסורים. </b>מוכר לא יאכלם שמא ישלים הלוקח המעות ונמצא שקנה השדה למפרע והרי שאכל המוכר הפירות בשביל המעות שנשארו לו ביד הלוקח ולוקח לא יאכלם שמא לא ישלים המעות ולא יצאה השדה מרשות המוכר ונמצא שאכלה בשביל המעות שהיו לו ביד המוכר. טור:
**** ENGLISH ****
Both are forbidden. The seller should not eat them, lest the buyer complete the payment and it will turn out that he acquired the field retroactively, and the seller will have eaten the produce in exchange for the money that remained in the buyer's hand; and the buyer should not eat them, lest he not complete the payment and the field will not leave the seller's possession, and it will turn out that he ate it in exchange for the money that was in the seller's hand — Tur.
**** END BLOCK ****
