**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of one who sells to his fellow on condition to return; it contains 8 se’ifim. If he sold a field to his fellow and said to him, “When I have money, return the land to me,” he does not acquire, and all the fruits that he ate are fixed interest, and we extract them in beis din. {Rama: And even if he did not stipulate so, but the custom in the city is that anyone who buys must return when the seller has money — for anyone who sells sells with intent of the custom — and it is forbidden for the buyer to eat the fruits (Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of Rashba).} But if the buyer said of his own accord, “When you have money I will return the land to you,” it is not a condition, and the sale takes effect immediately; therefore the buyer eats fruits. But if, when the buyer said so, the seller pressed it from him and strengthened it, it is a condition. But whenever the buyer did it — even within the sale — and the seller was silent and did not strengthen it, this is not a condition, but mere words, and the sale is a sale, since the seller did not do it, which he should have done. {Rama: And likewise, if he sold him land “from now,” and the buyer waited for him for the money, the buyer is permitted to eat the fruits and the seller is forbidden, for he is eating as payment for delaying his money (Tur).}

**** ENGLISH ****
He sold him a field for a maneh, and that same day he made for him a document that if he returns his money, he will return to him the sale document: if this condition was made before the sale or at the time of the sale, it does not have the law of a sale, but rather the law of a loan, and the buyer is obligated to return all that he ate. But if it was made after the transaction, even 1 hour [later], this is a complete sale, and even if he made him a document about this with acquisition and with all types of strengthening, it is an act of kindness that he wants to act kindly with him and return to him his purchase (a responsum of Rashba; Hagahos Ashiri in the name of R' Chananel; and Beit Yosef in Choshen Mishpat siman 207 seif 11 according to Rambam).

**** ENGLISH ****
If the seller said to the buyer, “When you bring the rest of the money, acquire from now,” both are forbidden to eat the fruits; rather, they place them in the hand of a third party: if he brings the rest of the money, he gives them to the buyer, and if he does not bring, he returns them to the seller and he returns the money that he received. But if the buyer says, “I will eat them, and if the transaction is not concluded, when the seller comes to return me my money I will deduct the value of the fruits,” it is permitted; and the same applies if the seller says so. {Rama: And this is specifically when he said, “I will deduct for you from the money,” but if he said, “I will pay you for the fruits that I ate,” it is forbidden, for in the end he is taking interest, only that he goes back and pays him (Tur).}
