**** HEBREW ****
דהיינו שנתן המעות ולא כתב השטר ובאתרא דקנו בשטרא אבל בתחלת המקח כשעדיין לא נתן המעות אפי' הלוקח אחר כך הוי תנאי דאדעתא דהכי מכר זה השדה. ב"ח בשם הרא"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
This means that he paid the money but did not write the deed, in a place where property is acquired by means of a deed. At the beginning of the sale, however, while he has not yet paid the money, even if the purchaser subsequently pays, the stipulation is effective, for the seller sold the field with that understanding. Thus writes Bach in the name of Rosh.
