<b>מתי חל על העיסה חיוב חלה. ובו ה' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>המפריש חלתו קמח אינה חלה <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>וגזל ביד כהן ושאר העיסה חייבת בחלה ואותו הקמח שהפריש לשם חלה <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="2"></i>אם יש בו עומר ועשהו עיסה ה"ז מפריש ממנה חלה <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>ודוקא באומר שיחול עליה שם חלה בעודו קמח אבל המפריש קמח ואומר כשתעשה עיסה יחול עליה שם חלה דבריו קיימים:
**** ENGLISH ****
When challah obligation takes effect on the dough. In it are 5 seifim. One who separates his challah while still flour—it is not challah, and it is robbery in the kohen's hand, and the rest of the dough is obligated in challah; and that flour he separated for challah—if there is an omer in it and he made it into dough, he separates challah from it; and specifically when he says that challah status shall take effect on it while still flour; but one who separates flour and says when it becomes dough challah status shall take effect on it—his words stand.
**** END BLOCK ****

**** ENGLISH ****
When do we separate challah? When he puts in water and the flour mixes with water—and this is when there does not remain in the mixing flour that did not mix with water in the amount of an omer; and if he said behold this is challah on the dough and on the leaven and on the flour that remains, and when all of it becomes one dough this in my hand shall be sanctified as challah—this is permitted; and likewise if he stipulates that challah shall take effect also on flour that will be mixed in later at the time of kneading; and it is good to teach women to stipulate thus (if there is concern that shiur challah will be mixed in later) (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag); and one must be careful not to burn the challah until after all the kneading.
**** END BLOCK ****
