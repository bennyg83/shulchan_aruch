<b>אינו מושבע מה"ס על ח"ש. </b>עיין בספר רב פנינים פ' נח שכתב דיש להסתפק אם שבועה חלה על ח"ש בבשר בחלב וכלאי הכרם דלא כתב בהו אכילה דבבו"ח כתיב לא תבשל ובכה"כ כתיב פן תקדש א"כ י"ל דבכ"ש יש לאו מה"ת אלא דלא לקי דשיעורין הלל"מ דפחות מכזית לא לקי א"כ אין שבועה חלה עליו או דילמא הלל"מ עקריה לקרא ממשמעותא וליכא כ"א איסור לחוד עכ"ד ע"ש ועיין בספרו תיבת גמא פרשה תולדות שכתב בפשיטות דהנשבע על חצי שיעור מבב"ח אין שבועה חלה דבכ"ש נמי יש לאו ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****He is not sworn from the Torah on half a measure. See in Sefer Rav Peninim chapter Noach, who wrote there is room to doubt whether an oath takes effect on half a measure in basar b'chalav and kilayim of the vineyard, for eating is not written regarding them; in basar b'chalav it is written "you shall not cook," and in kilayim "lest you be sanctified" — one can say all the more so there is a negative commandment from the Torah; only he is not lashed since these measures are halachah leMoshe miSinai that less than an olive's worth is not lashed — if so no oath takes effect upon him; or perhaps the halachah leMoshe miSinai uproots the verse from its implication and there is only prohibition alone — end quote; see there. And see in his book Teivat Gema, parashat Tolodot, who wrote plainly that one who swears on half a measure from basar b'chalav — no oath takes effect, for all the more so there is a negative commandment — see there.

**** ENGLISH ****He was asked about it and released. [And see in responsum Chatam Sofer siman 6 who brought that in responsum Shaar Efraim it proves Tosafos chapter HaSholeach in the sugya of one who vowed — they do not hold like Rosh here, since they challenged "she will be forbidden from bread and eat immediately before us" — learn there is no permission by eating it all at once, for there are no lashes; and he wrote it seems this is also Gemara's view end of chapter 3 of Shevuot that Rama brought above siman 230 regarding one who swore not to sell a certain object, etc.; and what Rama did not emend here — because what loss in releasing his oath, perhaps the law is like Rosh and release helps and he is saved from punishment — see there.] See in responsum Chavot Yair siman 129 and responsum Beit Yaakov siman 110; and see in Shaar HaMelech chapter 6 of Hilchot Shevuot halacha 18 what he wrote on their words.

**** ENGLISH ****And if he was asked. Beit HaTov in the name of Shach; and so in responsum Maharam Mintz siman 40 — see there.
