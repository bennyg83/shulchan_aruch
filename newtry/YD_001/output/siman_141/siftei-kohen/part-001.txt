**** ENGLISH ****All images etc. — presumably for idols etc. — certainly made for ornament etc. One can infer a precision from the first part against the end — in the first part he says presumably etc., behold in doubt permitted; in the end he says certainly etc., behold in doubt forbidden. And it appears in the first part he states the truth — presumably village ways are not to make for ornament but for idols; and likewise in doubt if made for idols or ornament they are forbidden, for we need to know specifically they were certainly made for ornament as he says in the end. And so is proven in the Tur — see there; and so the Perishah's opinion; and the Bach in the last pamphlet challenged him; and per the Magen Avraham's words his words are correct; and also wonder on the Bach — in the Gemara 41b regarding "rather say these images themselves are forbidden" — stam is like R. Meir etc. — the whole sugya is proven regarding images themselves found in the market we do not say "perhaps they nullified them" since they are complete — see there — investigate.

**** ENGLISH ****One who finds fragments of images. But in truth the Rambam's words appear primary in the Gemara — all idol fragments forbidden even not standing on base, for we are concerned gentiles did not nullify them; but image fragments permitted, for there is double doubt — perhaps worshipped or not; and if you say worshipped, perhaps gentiles nullified (and the law of images themselves see s.k. 1). And so Tosafot's and Ran's opinion; and wonder on the mechaber who himself copied in siman 146 seif 11 the Rambam's language — therefore one who finds idol fragments, behold they are forbidden for benefit lest gentiles did not nullify them — end; and here he wrote even finding idol fragments permitted; and so wonder on the Magen Avraham — investigate.
