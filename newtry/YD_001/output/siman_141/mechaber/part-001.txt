**** ENGLISH ****
A law of the images and forms of idolatry; it contains 8 se’ifim. All images of idolatry that are found in villages are forbidden, for presumably they were made for elilim; but those found in large cities are permitted, for certainly they were made for ornamentation (the language of the Mechaber) — unless they stand at the entrance of the province (the language of Rambam, Hilkhot Avodat Kochavim 7), and the form held in its hand a form of a staff, or a bird, or a ball, or a sword, or a crown and ring. {Rama: A form that they bow to has the law of an image and is forbidden without nullification; but those that they hang on the neck as a memorial are not called an image and are permitted (Mordechai, at the beginning of perek Kol HaTzelamim, in the name of Ravyah).}
