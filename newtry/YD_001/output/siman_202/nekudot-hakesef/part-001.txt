**** HEBREW ****
<b>(סימן ר"ב בט"ז ס"ק ו') ובזה צדקו כו'. </b>ז"א דהא כתב אם פיו צר קצת ואם איתא אפי' פיו רחב הרבה ועוד דגם זה בדוק שאינו דאפילו רחב הרבה אין המים עוברים לשוליו כשמניחו על פיו:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(Siman 202, Taz seif katan 6) In this they were correct, and so forth.</b> This is not so. The source says the opening is somewhat narrow. If Taz were correct, the same would apply even if the opening were very wide. Moreover, experience shows this is not so: even with a very wide opening, when the vessel is placed upside down, the water does not pass to its bottom.
