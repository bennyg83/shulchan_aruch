**** ENGLISH ****
To conduct oneself with honor toward a Sefer Torah, and the law of its accessories. In it are 19 seifim. A person is obligated to conduct himself with great honor toward a Sefer Torah; and it is a mitzvah to designate a place for it and honor that place and adorn it greatly.
