**** ENGLISH ****

And R' Menachem wrote: specifically not during the time of the study hall — but during the time of the study hall and when the space is cramped for the students, it is permitted to sit level with the books and commentaries; and so R' Meshullam of Béziers practiced; but when he was not in the place of the study hall, he was careful that the book be placed on top of something else, however small — end quote. And the author of Sefer Orchot Chayyim wrote: it appears one should not be lenient in this matter so as not to appear to belittle the honor of the books — end quote, Beit Yosef; and Darkei Moshe cites it.
