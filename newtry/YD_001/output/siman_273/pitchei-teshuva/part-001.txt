**** ENGLISH ****Four lines. See in Dagemar below siman 275 who wrote in name of Tosafot that these four lines must be in the middle of the page specifically; but to finish one book at the end of the page and begin the next book at the head of the page — even if he leaves four empty lines he is not permitted — see there. [And so ruled in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 255 concluding: for halachah I say — although initially we should not act against Rabbenu Tam in Tosafot, nevertheless when the scribe reaches the end of a chumash and the world presses him he should not reduce the writing so as to finish in the middle of the page — for that is like after the fact; it is not proper to condemn the Torah scroll now with such letters because of finishing a chumash at the end of the page which is a future disgrace and a general concern lest they cut it; and all the more now when there is no way to use chumashim made in a scroll at all — a person should never come to cut a Torah scroll into chumashim even if he finishes the chumash at its end — see there.] And see in Noda B'Yehuda tinyana part 4 siman 179 in responsum from the author's son regarding a Torah scroll where the scribe at end of Sefer Shemot "bekol maseihem" at end of the sheet did not leave even one line and above began Sefer Vayikra and also left no line; and he wrote that Torat Emet in mouth of Tashbetz above that only initially must leave four lines and after the fact not required; however if he did not leave space at all certainly disqualified even after the fact; and he raised: since there are thirteen words after the name Havayah baruch hu — if possible to engrave all thirteen words and write them in fine script so the bottom line remains completely empty — valid for all; and if impossible then leave at least in the bottom line space of half a row or at minimum enough to write gimel pei aleph which is open per Rambam's view — see there.

**** ENGLISH ****To write half inside the page. Shach — it appeared to him primary to be stringent like Rosh that three letters not remain even if the word is large; and he challenged that Mechaber's words contradict each other — Mechaber ruled here like Rambam that even many letters outside the line are permitted if half remains within the line; and in Orach Chayyim siman 32 seif 33 ruled like Rosh; and so Magen Avraham challenged there s.k. 46; and see in responsum Knesset Yechezkel siman 40 who wrote for practical difference that truly it appears to decide that a word with six or seven root letters like metusha'el shevatayim mahalalel pelishtim kaftorim and similar — certainly we depend on majority and if half is within the line it is permitted; however a word whose first letter is for action (meaning for use) like levhemtecha veha'avartem veshinantam letotafot — in this one rules like Rosh since the remaining half is half with the action; per this in a Torah scroll with a hundred words like metusha'el whose root is six or seven letters — valid if half remains; unlike in tefillin impossible to find this for we do not find a word in all four parshiyot with six letters without action except vehivosei and words hotzi'anu la'avoteinu — and these words impossible to find at end of line — see in Levush Orach Chayyim siman 35; therefore in Hilchot Tefillin he wrote plainly three letters unlike Torah scroll — end quote. And his language is somewhat garbled — what he said at beginning of his words like shevatayim pelishtim kaftorim — there are no six root letters and his intent is that the entire first half of the word is from the root; and what he said like levhemtecha veha'avartem veshinantam — even though there also the last letter is for action — nevertheless he holds the last letter is considered more the essence of the word since they already sanctified the word.
