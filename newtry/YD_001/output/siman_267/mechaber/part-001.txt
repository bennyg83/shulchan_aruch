One who acquires a slave — it is forbidden to maintain him uncircumcised, and his immersion, blessing, circumcision, and all laws of male and female slaves. And it contains 85 seifim. It is a positive mitzvah for the master to circumcise his slaves; if the master transgressed and did not circumcise them, beit din is commanded to circumcise him — whether a slave born in an Israelite's house or one bought from a gentile; he is obligated to circumcise him, and they are circumcised only by day. He bought a maidservant and her fetus with her and she gave birth — behold he is circumcised on the eighth; and even though he bought the fetus by itself, behold the fetus is acquisition with money since he bought his mother before birth — circumcised on the eighth. He bought a maidservant for her pregnancy, or bought a maidservant on condition not to immerse her for servitude — even though born in his domain: {Rama: (and she conceived in his house) (Tur)} — circumcised on the day he was born, for this newborn is as if acquisition with money alone and as if acquired today, for his mother is not in the category of Israelite maidservants so the son be house-born; and if his mother immersed after she gave birth, behold he is circumcised on the eighth.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who writes all his property to his slave — he goes out to freedom, for he is included in the property and acquires himself included in property; but if he reserved some property, land or movable, whether he specified the reservation or not, whether he had other property besides what he reserved or not — even if he wrote yourself and all my acquired property is yours except such-and-such item — he does not go out to freedom and does not acquire property.
