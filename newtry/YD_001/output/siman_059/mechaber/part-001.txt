**** ENGLISH ****
Laws regarding an animal flayed in its lifetime. In it are 2 seifim. A flayed animal (galudah) is forbidden — that is, one whose skin was stripped, whether by human hands or by illness that makes it treifah. If there remained of the skin the width of a selah coin on the face of the entire spine, and the width of a selah on the navel, and the width of a selah on the joints of its limbs — behold it is permitted (Rambam, Rashba, Ran, and most poskim). And if there was removed the width of a selah from above the entire back of the spine, or from above the navel, or from above the joints of the limbs, and all the rest of the skin remains — kosher. {Rama: But if it was removed from all three of them and all the rest of the skin remains — treifah (some say).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A bird whose feathers were plucked is kosher (Beit Yosef in the name of most poskim, and so Maharshal in Piskei Dinim siman 109). {Rama: And some forbid (R' Zera Halevi and Kol Bo), and it is good to be stringent if they all fell; however, if its feathers fell due to abundant fat, even though they all fell and it remains naked — nevertheless kosher, for since it occurred due to its fat it is kosher (Terumat HaDeshen siman 181 in the name of Or Zarua).}
