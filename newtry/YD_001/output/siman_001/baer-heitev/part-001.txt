**** ENGLISH ****
**Forbidden.** Reason: a person may cast his carcass there. Bach wrote: a refuse heap less than three tefachim is not considered a refuse heap; Taz and Shach wrote this is not necessary. Also in the name of the Mechaber “where most Israelites are found”—meaning even if most of the city and market are idolaters, only that in that place most Israelites are found it is permitted. Likewise if most idolaters are found there—even if most of the city and market are Israelite—it is forbidden; everything depends on who are found there. If idolaters and Israelites are found equally, we follow the majority of the market found there; if equal in the market, we follow majority of the city; if equal in the city—forbidden. Also: even where most idolaters are found, if most butchers there are Israelite it is permitted—specifically for animals, not for fowl and the like which they usually slaughter at home; rather we require specifically that most bird hunters who are Israelite slaughter immediately when they trap. What he wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 259 seif 6—a place where most are idolaters and most butchers Israelite, animal and bird found slaughtered there is permitted—deals with large fowl. Also what Rama wrote “and also most thieves of the city are Israelite”—refers to “were stolen”: for stolen we require specifically most thieves of the city are Israelite; we do not follow majority Israelite—if most thieves of the city are Israelite, even if most of it are idolaters, permitted; if most thieves of the city are idolaters, even if most are Israelite—forbidden.
