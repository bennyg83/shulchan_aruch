**** ENGLISH ****
One [case]: three [doughs], or two doughs, and none of them has the shiur, and they became mixed. In it are 5 seifim. Two doughs in which together there is the shiur obligating challah but neither alone has the shiur, and they touched one another and bit one another—if they belonged to two people, even if of one species, they are exempt from challah, for typically two people are particular; but if it is known they are not particular about mixing doughs, behold they combine (and students learning before a rabbi are typically not particular) (Mordechai, chapter Kol HaBasar). If both belonged to one person, if of one species as explained in siman 324 they combine and are obligated in challah; if of two species they do not combine, for typically one is not particular; but if he was particular that this dough not touch that and not mix, even if of one species they do not combine; and if one is kever bread and one is refined bread, even of one person, he is typically particular and it does not combine. {Rama: And likewise two doughs where one has saffron and the other does not—and all this when neither has the shiur alone; but if they have the shiur, he separates from one for the other if they belong to one person (Terumat HaDeshen siman 289).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A baker who made a dough to make it leaven to divide it is obligated in challah, for if he does not sell it he will make it bread; but (another person) who makes a dough to divide it while still dough is exempt.

**** ENGLISH ****
Two who gave flour to a baker to make leaven for them—if neither alone has the shiur, even though together they have the shiur, it is exempt. {Rama: And specifically if they mixed without their knowledge; but with their knowledge they are obligated, for behold they are not particular (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag).}

**** ENGLISH ****
Two who made a dough of shiur and divided it, and afterward each added to his portion until they completed the shiur—this is exempt, for they already had their time of obligation and were exempt at that time because they made it to divide. {Rama: And likewise one person who made two kavin and intended to divide them and divided them; but if he did not make them with intent to divide, division afterward does not help (Tur).}

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="11"></i>המפריש חלה מעיסה שאין בה שיעור אינה חלה לפיכך שתי עיסות שאין בשום <small>(אחת) </small>מהן כשיעור והפריש מכל אחת חלה ואח"כ עירב העיסות ועשאן עיסה אחת חייבת בחלה:
**** ENGLISH ****
One who separates challah from a dough that does not have the shiur—it is not challah; therefore two doughs in which neither has the shiur, and he separated challah from each and afterward mixed the doughs and made them one dough—it is obligated in challah.
