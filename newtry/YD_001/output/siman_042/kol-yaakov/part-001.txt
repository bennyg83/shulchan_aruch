**** ENGLISH ****
D) There. And they were new, etc. For here there is only designation; and designation is nothing — Levush Orach Tefillin ot 1; Magen Avraham s.k. 3; Mateh Yehonatan ot 1; Raz Siman ot 1; and straps even if tied shel Rosh and made ties of Shaddai — all this since not worn on him for mitzvah is only designation — Mateh Yehonatan there in Beit Yosef in laws of changing tefillin ot 1; Chayei Olam on Shulchan Aruch seif 1. And that which we say designation is nothing — meaning to lower from severe sanctity to light; but to profane — forbidden whenever designation is in body of sanctity — Peri Megadim Orach Chaim s.k. 3.

<b>ה) שם מותר לשנות וכו'</b> משמע אפי' שלא מדוחק מותר לשנות מש"ר לש"י אבל הדרישה סס"י ל"ב ובלבוש שם סעי' מ"ז וכאן כתבו דלא שרי אלא כגון שאין לו תפלין ש"י ויש לו ב' ש"ר הא ל"ה אסור פרמ"ג א"א אות א' ויש להחמיר היכא דאפשר והוי יודע שהדרישה שם כתב חידוש דין דוקא עשאן ד' בתים לש"ר שבדק נעשו ואין לו ש"י אז טולה עור הא עשאם תחילה ד' בתים לש"י בפיסול לא מהני טולה עור וכ"מ לשון הגמ' מנחות ל"ד שאם יש לו ב' ש"ר וכ"מ לשון הלבוש אבל לשון הש"ע בסי' ל"ב סעי' מ"ז משמע דבכל ענין כשר פרמ"ג א"א או"ג:
**** ENGLISH ****
E) There. Permitted to change, etc. Implies even not from distress permitted to change shel Rosh to shel Yad; but Derishah siman 32 and Levush there seif 47; and here they wrote permitted only such as he has no shel Yad tefillin and has two shel Rosh — if not so — forbidden — Peri Megadim Orach Chaim ot 1; and be stringent where possible and know Derishah there wrote novel law: only if made four compartments for shel Rosh that were checked — valid and he has no shel Yad — then take hide; if made four compartments for shel Yad initially — invalid — hide-taking does not help; and so gemara Menachot 34 and Levush language; but Shulchan Aruch siman 32 seif 47 implies kosher in every case — Peri Megadim Orach Chaim ot 3.

**** ENGLISH ****
F) If shel Yad strap broke near the knot — forbidden to turn other side up and make shel Yad knot; and piece that had knot tie below — lowers from its sanctity since it had knot and yud; and now make finger-wraps — Magen Avraham s.k. 3; Halachah Berurah ot 1; Beit Yosef in laws pertaining to changing tefillin ot 3; from Rashal general rule 21 ot 3; and even if discard broken strap piece and enough strap remains — do not turn it; must make knot specifically where it was initially near knot — Peri Megadim Orach Chaim ot 3; Emek Meir ot 1; from Rashal there; Kessef HaChaim there; and see Raz Siman ot 3; and some permit turning strap — Leket HaKemach chelek 2 siman 81; Mateh Yehonatan there; R' Akiva Eiger there in name of his elder; Emek Meir general rule 19 ot 3 in name of Tiferet Yisrael; and appears in hour of distress where end at break place is soft and weak and unfit to make tying on arm as required — close to breaking — and he has no other strap — there is to permit turning — see there ot 2 from Rashal.
