<b>א) [סעיף א'] מרה שניקבה טרפה.  </b>ואם ניקבה המרה ואפשר לתלות בסכין או ביד הטבח כשרה. ב"י בשם הרשב"א. ש"ך סק"א. כנה"ג בהגב"י או' א' פר"ח או' א' לה"פ או' א' בל"י או' א' כריתי או' א' שפ"ד או' א' שה"מ או' א' שו"ג מחו' או' א' וכתב דמרוב פשיטותו השמיטו הש"ע והרב בהגהה דין זה דנקב שנעשה אחר שחיטה משוך וניכר הוא לכל כדלעיל סי' ל"ו סעי' ה' בהגהה גבי ריאה שנמצאת נקובה יעו"ש. זר"א או' קי"ח. מחב"ר או' ב' ער"ה או' ב' חכ"א כלל י"ז או' כ"ט. זב"ת או' א' ענ"ה סי' כ"א או' ב' ובספרו שש"א או' ב' זב"ש או' א' תו"ז סי' ע' או' ג' ביא"ב בשפ"ז או' א' בי"צ בעמ"ז או' א' מנ"י בשרשים או' א' זב"צ או' א' ועיין לקמן סי' ן' סעי' א'.
**** ENGLISH ****
A) [Seif 1] A gall bladder punctured — treifah. And if gall bladder punctured and possible to attribute to knife or slaughterer's hand — kosher. Beit Yosef in name of Rashba. Shach s.k. 1. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 1. Peri Chadash ot 1. Lechem Panim ot 1. Beit Lechem Yehudah ot 1. Kereti ot 1. Sha'arei Perushim ot 1. She'erit Menachem ot 1. Zivchei Ra'anan ot 118. Machatzit HaShekel ot 2. Erech HaShulchan ot 2. Chayei Adam general rule 17 ot 29. Toldot Zevi ot 1. Ananah siman 21 ot 2; and in his book She'erit Adam ot 2. Zivchei Tzedek ot 1. Turei Zahav siman 70 ot 3. Beit Yitzchak in Sha'ar HaPerushim ot 1. Beit Yosef in Amud HaMa'arav ot 1. Minchat Yitzchak in roots ot 1. G) Gall bladder protruding between ribs outward between skin and flesh, and whole gall bladder found, inflated it and rose in inflation and no puncture found at all — kosher. Tzitz Eliezer siman 9; and disputed responsum Mishpetei Shmuel siman 130 who declared treifah — see there; and Peri Chadash ot 3 agreed; and so Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 5. Lechem Panim end ot 3. Beit Lechem Yehudah end ot 3. Mishnat Zekenim ot 1. Zivchei Ra'anan ot 119. She'erit Menachem end ot 3. Toldot Zevi ot 5. Ananah there ot 6. Zivchei Tzedek ot 2. Turei Zahav there ot 6. Beit Yitzchak in Taharat HaBayit ot 1. Kometz Bikkurim in laws of other treifot general rule 5 ot 8. And how so? If skin whole from outside; but if punctured from outside side — there is to attribute to thorn from illness and declare treifah. Peri Chadash there. Lechem Panim there. Mishnat Zekenim there. Zivchei Ra'anan there. She'erit Menachem there. Toldot Zevi there. Beit Yitzchak in Taharat HaBayit there. Kometz Bikkurim there. Zivchei Tzedek ot 8; and wrote: and not like Taz there. And in our opinion who permitted: Zivchei Ra'anan wrote: even if punctured from outside — does not emerge from doubt, lest via illness; and therefore wrote: in place of Peri Megadim there is to be lenient — see there. However from rest of aforementioned poskim who are many it is implied: if punctured from outside in every case — declare treifah. And see below siman 49 seif 4 and our words there ot 35.
