**(siman 4 Taz seif 2) And not like Beit Yosef, etc.** Per my view one may say Rabbenu Yerucham's intent: certainly proper to say we do not say “his end proved his beginning,” since there is presumption of righteousness—one may say his da'at now when he thought for idol worship he became wicked, not before; but reason it is doubtful is like in mikveh “wicked before you” and presumption of issur of animal; and even if we distinguish only deficient mikveh that came—nevertheless one may say “his end proved his beginning” is strong also to remove presumption of righteousness—for much was lost in that matter that this animal's kashrut is only with combination that against presumption of righteousness there is presumption of issur of animal; but for benefit which is even presumption of heter—not so. Afterward I saw Kessef Mishneh wrote so; nevertheless per my view this is only if we say even at that time he became mumar—if so if we judge “his end proved beginning” it is not slaughter at all and stands on presumption not slaughtered; but if we say in that slaughter he did not become mumar and it is slaughtered before us—only if his thought was so at shechitah forbidden because of idol-worship offering not because of nevelah—no place here Chullin 41 animal not presumed from issur to issur; and even Rashi who holds live animal in presumption of issur—that is issur oto v'et beno; we do not presume from issur to issur; per my view this is only for nevelah issur—for since every animal without corrective act goes from oto v'et beno issur to nevelah issur unless shechitah fixes—therefore in doubt whether he fixed properly we presume not fixed properly and it is nevelah; but for other issur that came from outside, since certainly corrective act done properly and oto v'et beno issur removed and did not go from oto v'et beno to nevelah—only doubt on other issur—here presumption of heter as must be for doubtful treifah; if so here too for idol-worship offering issur that caused it.

Yitzchak who say person does not forbid what is not his from libation; and they answered it deals with partnership. And afterward they said R. Nachman and R.
