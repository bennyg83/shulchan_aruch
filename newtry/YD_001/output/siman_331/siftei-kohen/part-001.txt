and if this one knew his fellow tithed he would not tithe and would rely on his fellow — even if he knew his fellow did not tithe per his generous, stingy, fine, or average eye he would not change — the second's terumah is not terumah — for his fellow whose way is to rely tithed and it is as his agent; and even if the first did not tithe that measure he would tithe — such as the first tithed stingy measure and he generous eye — nevertheless the first's terumah is terumah and the second's is not; and if not that it is not known whether their way is to rely one on the other — if not, behold they are partners without specification; even though we say partners without specification need not take permission one from the other and whoever tithes his terumah is terumah — this is when the second does not reveal his intent whether stingy, fine, or average eye — then we say if the first tithed the measure his fellow tithed the second's terumah is not terumah — for the first did his agency and aimed his intent; but if the first did not tithe the measure his fellow tithed but less — behold we see he was not the second's agent since he did not aim his intent — and both their terumot are terumah each for his share; so it seems to me to explain Rambam's view in ch. 4 of Terumot per what he understood there — all in Kesef Mishneh; and from now I wonder why he concluded his words in Shulchan Aruch here — and it seems in this time in ma'aser, etc.; it is fine in pure terumah to say stingy and generous eye and to say if the first tithed the measure his fellow tithed, etc.; but in ma'aser what is "the measure his fellow tithed," etc. — behold one must always separate ma'aser by measure not to diminish or add from its law; therefore it seems to me in this time whether in ma'aser or terumah the second's terumah is not terumah — for in ma'aser since the first separated ma'aser properly behold the produce is fixed and the second's terumah is nothing; and in terumah too in this time since its measure is whatever amount and the first separated behold the produce is fixed and the second's terumah is nothing. It seems to me.
