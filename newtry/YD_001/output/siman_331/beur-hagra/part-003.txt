<b>Produce.</b> The Yerushalmi in chapter 2 of Ma'asrot asks whether ma'aser rishon itself can fix produce. The case is neither a fully processed grain pile, which everyone agrees is prohibited, nor ma'aser rishon taken before processing, which everyone agrees is permitted. Rather, it concerns dates intended for pressing or dried figs intended for compacting. One separated terumah gedolah and later decided to leave them as they were, then separated ma'aser rishon. If liability applies retroactively, terumah is what fixed them; if only prospectively, ma'aser rishon fixes them. Rambam rules leniently, apparently because he understands a later passage as resolving the question. Yet that passage itself concerns purchasers of dates and dried figs and the dispute whether casual eating remains allowed, and does not directly resolve the original question.
