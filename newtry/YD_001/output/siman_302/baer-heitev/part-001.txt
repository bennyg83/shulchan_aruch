<b>By Torah law.</b> Taz writes that the dye test is effective even for a Torah-level prohibition, as is explicit in the Gemara. Tur's distinction between Torah and rabbinic law applies only to the second option he mentions, namely removing one thread and permitting the garment. In that case we distinguish because a Torah-level uncertainty is not resolved leniently by assumption. See his lengthy discussion.
