**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And likewise a maidservant, and so forth.</b> One should not object from seif 16, which says a mourner hired by others may not work during mourning. There it speaks of ordinary labor, which a mourner may not perform under any circumstances. Here the work consists of household needs, which the female mourner herself may perform as stated in the preceding seif. Therefore a maidservant may likewise do such work. She should not, however, perform profit-making work unrelated to the household, and certainly should not leave the house contrary to mourning practice. I saw someone permit a synagogue attendant in mourning to continue his duties, go to synagogue, and serve the community. That ruling is incorrect; it mistakes the basis of the maidservant leniency. Maharshal cites Agudah that a mourner may undergo bloodletting. Kol Bo writes that a mourner who works in violation of the law is placed under nidui.
