**** ENGLISH ****
The law of one who sets a price of wheat to obligate him at today’s rate; it contains 3 se’ifim. One who was owed money by his fellow, and said to him, “Give me my money, for I want to buy wheat with it,” and he said to him, “Go and make it for me at today’s rate, and you will have with me wheat as a loan”: if he has wheat corresponding to the amount of his money, it is permitted, even if the market rate was not set; but if not, it is forbidden, even if the market rate was set (Nimukei Yosef in the name of Ramban, and Maggid Mishneh there; and so wrote Ramban and Rashba). (And the seller is believed to say that he has [wheat], and no proof is required for his words) (Hagahos Mordechai, perek Eizehu Neshech), as above siman 162.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="4"></i>הרי שהיה לו חטים ועשה הלואתו עליו ובא אחר זמן ואמר ליה תן לי חטים שאני רוצה למכרם וליקח בדמיהם יין א"ל צא ועשה אותם עלי יין <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="6"></i>כשער שבשוק עתה <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="7"></i>אם יש לו יין הרי זה <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="3"></i>מותר <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="5"></i>ואם לאו אסור <small>(ר"ן ונ"י):</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
If he had wheat, and he made his loan upon it, and after some time he came and said to him, “Give me wheat, for I want to sell it and buy wine with their value,” and he said to him, “Go and make it for me as wine at the rate in the market now”: if he has wine, this is permitted; but if not, it is forbidden (Ran and Nimukei Yosef).

**** ENGLISH ****
He said to him, “Lend me a maneh.” He said to him, “I do not have a maneh; I have wheat for a maneh.” And he gave him wheat for a maneh at the market rate, and then he went back and bought it from him for 90 (and they are still worth 100) (Beit Yosef in the name of Tanna D’vei R' Yishmael). If in the end he repaid him the maneh with produce, it is permitted; but it is forbidden to repay with money, because of subterfuge of interest, since he initially said to him, “Lend me a maneh.” And if he transgressed and did so, behold he extracts from him 100 in beis din, for there is not even avak ribbis here. {Rama: And some say that it is avak ribbis (Tur in the name of Ramban and Rosh). And all this is only when they did not stipulate initially upon this; but if they stipulated initially to buy from him for less, it is full-fledged interest (Maggid Mishneh, perek 5 of Hilchos Malveh V’Loveh, in the name of Ramban and Rashba; and so it is in Nimukei Yosef), even if he repaid him produce it is forbidden (Nimukei Yosef, perek Eizehu Neshech).}
