**** ENGLISH ****
Prohibition of bathing and anointing for a mourner. And it contains 6 seifim. Bathing — how so? Forbidden to bathe his entire body even in cold water; but his face, hands, and feet in hot water are forbidden, in cold permitted; and if he was soiled with mud and excrement he bathes as usual and need not be concerned: {Rama: And all this is by law forbidden only seven days, but afterward bathing is permitted; but nowadays the custom is to forbid all bathing all thirty days (Maharam Laws of Joy and in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 7 Laws of Festivals and chapter 10 Laws of Mourning); and even to wash the head is forbidden (below siman 390 and in Avraham Zacuto); and one should not change the custom, for it is an ancient custom founded by elders (Avraham Zacuto in the name of Rashba).}
