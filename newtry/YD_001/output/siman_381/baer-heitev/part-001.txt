**** ENGLISH ****
<b>To forbid bathing for all thirty days.</b> This is the correct text, as in Levush, not the printed version "all bathing for thirty days." Certainly bathing in cold water is not prohibited at all. Shach. Or Zarua explains the custom as a safeguard lest one come to comb one's hair, which is prohibited for thirty days. According to our practice, where combing is permitted as stated in siman 390, this reason would no longer prohibit bathing. Maharshal gives a different reason: bathing was prohibited because a person entering a bathhouse commonly also shaves the hair of his head. Taz. See there.
