**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of avak ribbis, and what is called interest by Torah law; it contains 11 se’ifim. **** ENGLISH ****
A promissory note that contains interest — whether by Torah law or by rabbinic law — he collects only the principal, provided that it is recognizable that it is interest. {Rama: For example, where it is explicit in the promissory note — the principal by itself (Rosh, and Mordechai, and Hagahos Maimoniyos, and Nimukei Yosef, and Tosafos, and Hagahos Ashiri, and Tur-Choshen Mishpat siman 52, not like Ramban); or where only the principal was written in the promissory note and witnesses testify about the interest (Tur).
