<b>אבל בלא"ה אסור. </b>דקי"ל כמ"ד טבילה בזמנה לאו מצוה ועתוס' דנדה ל' א' ד"ה וש"מ כו' וביומא ח' א' ד"ה דכ"ע כו' אבל בסוף יומא משמע דאפילו להך מ"ד אפ"ה מותר אפי' ביה"כ ועתוס' דשבת קכ"א א' ד"ה ה"ג כו' וכ"ש בשבת כמ"ש בי"ט י"ח ב' תינח בשבת כו' אלא דתוס' שם ד"ה כל כו' כתבו דדוקא בימיהם לטהרות כו' ומ"מ לא כתבו אלא על יה"כ וכרב:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>But otherwise it is forbidden.</b> We rule according to the view that immersion at its appointed time is not a mitzvah. See Tosafot to Niddah 30a, s.v. 'and derive from there,' etc., and Yoma 8a, s.v. 'everyone agrees,' etc. At the end of Yoma, however, it appears that even according to this view immersion is nevertheless permitted even on Yom Kippur. See Tosafot to Shabbos 121a, s.v. 'this is the correct reading,' etc. It is certainly permitted on Shabbos, as stated in Beitzah 18b: This is understandable regarding Shabbos, etc. Tosafot there, s.v. 'every,' wrote that this applied specifically in their days, when immersion was needed for matters of purity, etc. Nevertheless, they stated this limitation only regarding Yom Kippur, in accordance with Rav.

<b>ואם היה כו'. </b>אבל בגמ' די"ט שם משמע אפי' אפשר מדמחלק שם במתני' בין אדם לכלים ובכלים בשא"א מבע"י טובלין בי"ט כמש"ש בגמ' (י"ח א') כלי שנטמא מעי"ט כו' דלא כמ"ש בד"מ וז"ש כאן אבל במקום כו':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And if it was, etc.</b> The Gemara in Beitzah there implies that it is permitted even when immersion could have been performed earlier, because the Mishnah distinguishes between a person and vessels. Concerning vessels, when immersion could not have been performed before Yom Tov, they may be immersed on Yom Tov, as stated in the Gemara, 18a: A vessel that became impure on the eve of Yom Tov, etc. This is unlike what is written in Darkei Moshe. This is why the ruling here continues: But in a place, etc.
