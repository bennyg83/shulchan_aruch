<b>שלא תטבול האשה ביום. ובו ה' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>אין הנדה והזבה והיולדת עולות מטומאתן בלא טבילה שאפילו אחר כמה שנים חייב כרת הבא על אחת מהן <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i>אלא אם כן <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>טבלו כראוי <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="3"></i>במקוה הראוי:
**** ENGLISH ****
A woman should not immerse in the day; it contains 5 se’ifim. A niddah, zavah, and yoledes do not ascend from their tumah without immersion; for even after many years, one who has relations with one of them is liable to kareis, unless they immersed properly in a valid mikveh.

**** ENGLISH ****
If her husband is in the city, it is a mitzvah to immerse at her time, so as not to prevent being fruitful and multiplying even for one night. {Rama: And it is permitted to immerse on Friday night (Rabbeinu Chananel; and Rabbi Eliyahu; and Or Zarua; and Ba’al Halachos Gedolos; and Sefer HaTerumah and Sefer Mitzvos Gadol in the name of Rabbi Shmuel who practiced so for his daughter) if she could not immerse beforehand (Beit Yosef and Mordechai in the name of several great ones, see there), and this is specifically when her husband is in the city; but otherwise it is forbidden (so it implies in Terumas HaDeshen, siman 295). And if it was possible for her to immerse beforehand — for example, if it was after childbirth, or if her husband was not in the city and he came on Friday evening — there are those who say that she is forbidden to immerse (there, and in the rulings of Mahari”v, siman 48, like the view of Beit Yosef), and so they practiced in some places. But in a place where there is no custom, one should not be stringent. And in a place where they practiced to be stringent, also on Motzaei Shabbos she should not immerse, for since it was possible for her to immerse beforehand, we do not distance the immersion from the chafifah (Agur and Mahari”l). And likewise, a widow who is forbidden to immerse the first immersion on Friday night because it is forbidden to come upon her with first intercourse on Shabbos, is also forbidden to immerse on Motzaei Shabbos (Mahari”l). And there are those who are lenient and permit her to immerse on Motzaei Shabbos, since she did not immerse on Shabbos due to concern of prohibition (Beit Yosef).}

**** ENGLISH ****
She is forbidden to immerse in the day of the 7th. And even if she delays immersing until day 8 or 9, she cannot immerse in the day, because of the “trace of her daughter” (meaning: the daughter’s attachment and closeness, to do like her mother’s practice — that she will immerse in the day like her — and she will not discern that her mother immersed after seven and not on the seventh itself). {Rama: And kallos who immerse before the chuppah may immerse in the day, for they do not come to the chassan until the night; but after the chuppah their law is like other women (Mahari”l).}

**** ENGLISH ****
Where there is an oness — for example, it appears [dangerous] to immerse at night because of cold, or fear of thieves, and the like, or that they close the city gates — she may immerse on the eighth day while it is still day. But on the seventh she should not immerse while it is still day, even though there is an oness.
