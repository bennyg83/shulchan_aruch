<b>איזה פת חייב בחלה. ובו י' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>אין חיוב חלה <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i>אלא <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>בלחם לפיכך <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>הסופגנין דהיינו רכים העשויים כספוג והדובשנין <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="(°)" data-order="2"></i>והאיסקריטים והם מטוגנים בדבש פטורים מן החלה:
**** ENGLISH ****
Which bread is obligated in challah. In it are 10 seifim. Challah obligation applies only to bread; therefore spongy cakes—that is, soft ones made like a sponge—and honey cakes and iskritin, which are fried in honey, are exempt from challah.

**** ENGLISH ****
A dough whose batter was thick and he rolled it with intent to cook it, or to fry it, or to make spongy cakes from it, or to dry it in the sun, and he did so—it is exempt; he rolled it to make bread from it and changed his mind to cook it, or fry it, or make spongy cakes from it, or dry it in the sun—it is obligated, for it was already obligated from the time of rolling; he rolled it with intent for spongy cakes and the like and changed his mind to make bread from it—it is obligated.
