**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(Likkut) When does this apply... and he does not.</b> The Yerushalmi there teaches that one who finds a met mitzvah must attend to it and bury it where found. This applies when it is outside the city boundary; if found within the boundary, he brings it to the cemetery and buries it there. R. The Yerushalmi also derives from "you shall surely bury him" that a corpse is not considered a met mitzvah unless the head and majority of the body are present.
