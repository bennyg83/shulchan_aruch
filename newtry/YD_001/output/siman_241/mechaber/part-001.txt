**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of one who strikes and one who curses his father and mother. And it contains 9 seifim. One who curses his father or mother — even after their death — is liable for stoning if there are witnesses and forewarning; both a man and a woman who cursed are liable. In what case is this stated? When they cursed them with the special Names; but if they cursed them with a substitute name, he is liable only for a negative commandment, like one who curses an ordinary Israelite: {Rama: And likewise one who strikes his father or mother during their lifetime — whether man or woman — is liable for strangulation; and specifically if they made a wound in them; but if they did not make a wound, it is only a negative commandment, like one who strikes an ordinary Israelite. (Tur)}

**** ENGLISH ****
Whoever disgraces his father and mother — even with words, even with a hint — behold, he is included in "cursed by the Almighty," as it says, "Cursed is one who disgraces his father and mother," and beit din may strike him for this with lashes of rebellion and punish as appropriate: {Rama: One who became liable to take an oath to his son in beit din — they do not impose upon him an oath that contains a curse, for this comes to curse his father; rather they impose an oath that contains no curse. (Tur in the name of Rambam)}
