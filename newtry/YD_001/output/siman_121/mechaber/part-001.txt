**** ENGLISH ****
The order of kashering utensils that are purchased from a non-Jew. In it are 7 seifim. One who purchases old utensils from a non-Jew — their kashering is in accordance with the way the non-Jew used them. Therefore, one who purchases old utensils that were used with cold, such as cups and plates and the like, rinses them, and he must scrub them well with water at the time of rinsing in order to remove and polish off the issur that is on their surface; and afterwards he rinses them with water and immerses them, and they are permitted. {Rama: There are places that practiced leniency to put wine into utensils whose staves are glued with fat, because the nature of wine is to flee from the fat, and the fat congeals and stands by itself and does not touch the wine at all (Rivash siman 149).}
