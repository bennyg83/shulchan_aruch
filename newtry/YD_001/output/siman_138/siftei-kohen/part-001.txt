**** ENGLISH ****[Without peeling, etc. Thus agreed the majority of poskim, and Beit Yosef brings them; and he wrote — not like the words of Rabbeinu Yerucham who wrote that if a gentile trod in it, milui v'iruy does not help while the pitch is still on it — end; and incidentally in his sharpness he did not examine well, for Rabbeinu Yerucham likewise wrote in Netiv 17 chapter 3 regarding earthenware; but regarding wood he wrote there explicitly that iruy without peeling helps — see there:, And all the kashering of these vessels that Chazal estimated according to their use and according to their nature — the reasons are explained in the poskim, see there:, And know that all the kashering in this seif is lekhatchilah; but if one transgressed and trod in a winepress or in a winepress vessel that was not kashered, it is permitted be-di'eved, for there is a siman, as above siman 137 seif 2. And Rashi and some poskim kasher the winepress and all its vessels through rinsing; and Bach wrote that lekhatchilah one needs kashering or peeling and wiping-dry and iruy according to what applies, as the view of the majority of poskim and like Rabbeinu Tam's explanation and Shulchan Aruch; however be-di'eved, if he did only rinsing in a gentile's winepress and trod wine in it — Rashi and his supporters are worthy to rely on them in a time of pressure — end; and I am astonished, for be-di'eved it is obvious it is permitted even without rinsing, for there is a siman in the pits and in the vats, as Taz wrote in siman 137 seif 2; and likewise wrote the poskim and Ran at the end of Avodah Zarah in the name of Rabbeinu Tam; and perhaps it deals with a very wide winepress that is not deep, in a case where there is no siman in what is inside opposite its lining:]

**** ENGLISH ****To age twelve months. And he did not mention permitting the knots — it implies he holds they need not be loosened, as he wrote in seif 9; and he follows his reasoning that he wrote in Kesef Mishnah end of chapter 11: the law of earthenware — for likewise is Rashba's view in Torah HaBayit like Rashi, that permitting the knots in the Gemara refers only to kashering through wiping-dry alone; and in Beit Yosef siman 135 he wrote that we rule like Rashi and Rashba; but in truth it is not so, for Rashba also explains that it refers to aging as well, and he concedes here and in seif 9 that one must loosen the knots, as he wrote explicitly in Torah HaBayit; and likewise is the view of Ran and the Rosh and the Tur; and so too is Rambam's view chapter 11; and likewise is Raavad's view.

**** ENGLISH ****[Rinsing suffices for them.
