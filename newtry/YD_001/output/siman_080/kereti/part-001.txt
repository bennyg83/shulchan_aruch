כל שיסתפק וכו' יש לדקדק אם באה בהמה לפנינו ואין מכירין אם חיה או בהמה מה טיבו דמין חיה מרובה מבהמה וא"כ נימא כל דפריש מרובא קפריש או י"ל מינים הן בחיה יותר אבל מנין ב"ח הן בבהמה יתר ד"מ מין שור מחזיק אלף צבי מחזיק ת"ק וא"כ אין לילך בתר רובא מיהו בגמ' אמרינן ספיקא דעזי כרכוז דהיה שינוי בקרנים א"כ י"ל מסתמא דיינינן בחיה רק ספק כה"ג דיש שנוי לפנינו דאל"כ כל חיות דאין בהם מסורה יהי' ספק ואדתני במשנה כוי שוה לחי' וכו' ולא קתני סתם כל ספק יש בו דרכים וכו' אלא סתמא הוא בחזקת חי':
**** ENGLISH ****
Anything about which you are uncertain, etc. — one should analyze: if a beheimah comes before us and we do not recognize whether chayah or beheimah — what is its nature; are chayah species more numerous than beheimah, and shall we say whatever separates from the majority separates; or shall we say species are more in chayah but how many species are there in beheimah — from where do we know; however in the Gemara we say a doubt regarding goats of Karkoz that there was a change in horns — thus one may say we judge chayah only in such a doubt where a change is before us; otherwise all chayot without tradition would be doubtful; and the mishnah taught koy is like chayah, etc., and did not teach that every doubt has ways, etc., but the default is chayah status.

**** ENGLISH ****
Koy — Shulchan Aruch abbreviated: if the mother is chayah we are concerned for the seed; if certainly its milk is permitted he relies on the law in its place. And it appears that even though koy has deer horns in their form, we are still concerned for beheimah seed, for we do not find that Chazal gave a sign in koy alone to check by horn signs; and do not wonder if a deer comes before us with its horns how we rely on signs — perhaps born from beheimah and chayah and its milk forbidden; and in horn signs there is no proof; and one must say either double doubt we are concerned for the father's seed too, or since it is uncommon they were not concerned — all this since we do not know it is from deer; investigate.
