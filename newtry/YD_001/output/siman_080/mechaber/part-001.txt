**** ENGLISH ****
The law of a pure chayah (wild animal). In it are 6 seifim: A pure chayah — its milk is permitted and its blood is forbidden and requires covering; and the Sages gave signs from tradition regarding its horns — if they are split (meaning many splits emerge from them) it is certainly a pure chayah; and if they are not split they need three signs in them: twisted, round, and grooved (meaning twisted are made like layers of an onion; round ones are round and not wide; grooved ones have grooves around them frequent and interlocking with each other) and that their grooves interlock; and if one of these three signs is missing its milk is forbidden.
