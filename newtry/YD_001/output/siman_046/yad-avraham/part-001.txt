(Siman 46 seif 1) Pure fat seals — kosher. Later authorities wrote: rosh ma'aya be'emtza — although we do not eat it, as siman 64 seif 16 — nevertheless it seals. And the same per geonim' explanation that it is end of intestine, as siman 64. And it is not clear, for explicitly in Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba (daf 38) tame fat does not seal — such as rosh ma'aya be'emtza does not seal; and we may not be lenient against Rashba without proof. And that which later authorities wrote "the same applies end of intestine per geonim" — I wonder greatly: even if so, that it is pure fat — nevertheless it does not seal, since it is made like a hat, as in Talmud and poskim; and Shulchan Aruch this seif: fat on kerkashta, although pure, does not seal. And I happened to have Mishneh LeMelech on Rambam chapter 13 of Hilchot Shegagot and found there the Rav elaborated and challenged: per geonim rosh ma'aya is end of intestine connected to kerkashta but not kerkashta itself; and it does not appear, for Rashba, Beit Yosef, Shulchan Aruch, and Semak do not imply so at all — for they wrote "remove a little from kerkashta"; and so Rambam chapter 7 Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot law 9: some geonim say head of intestine that must be scraped is intestine from which excrement exits, which is end of intestine. And that which compelled Mishneh LeMelech to say so — because he challenged geonim' explanation that rosh ma'aya is head of kerkashta — if so, why need Talmud and poskim to say fat of kerkashta does not seal because made like hat — behold without this it is tame fat? And therefore he wrote: per them rosh ma'aya is end of thin intestines connected to kerkashta, not kerkashta; and I did not get to bottom of his view — he contradicts what he wrote at beginning there to resolve this per hagahah siman 64 seif 16: rosh ma'aya is forbidden only the membrane on it, not fat under membrane stuck to ma'aya; if so, we are challenged: even if we say it is kerkashta as poskim imply; and without this also challenged: because of tame fat he need not say it does not seal but rosh ma'aya; rather because made like hat the whole does not seal; and so in Kiryat Sefer. Also what astonished Mishneh LeMelech about Rashba's view — I omitted Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba whom I brought. Again I happened upon Sefer Shev Yaakov siman 26, who wrote exactly as I wrote: rosh ma'aya — whether per commentators that it is beginning of exit from stomach, or per geonim that it is kerkashta — does not seal; and the reason is not equal: at beginning of exit from stomach the reason is tame fat, for even fat under membrane is forbidden; see there, where he elaborated to challenge hagahah siman 64; and at end of ma'aya which is kerkashta the reason is made like hat — even pure fat under membrane; and it is exactly as siman 64. See Noda BiYehudah Mahadura Tinyana siman 21 regarding puncture found in ma'aya near stomach going on slope, coming from liver simfonot, found so in several animals; butchers say most animals thus; he answered: coincidence would not persist and not recognize place and form; and since this puncture is one form on slope in one place near stomach in several animals — simple it is vein from thin intestines to liver; doctors say its function is liver throws bile via this vein to intestines and thence to stomach to digest; and although Rashba in responsum declared treifah and did not believe butchers in his days — goodness of vein not yet known; therefore nature of puncture at beginning of creation brings into thin intestines drop of bile, but taking out — no nature to expel excrement outward into cavity; therefore when we may hang vein tore at liver removal — kosher; but if certainly tore while alive — forbid; and simple no obligation to search this puncture.

Question and answer in laws of treifot siman 46. Incident: thin intestines were brought before me, length of cubit from stomach; in middle connected by wide vein to liver; vein cut near intestine; found vein's cavity open through into intestine; recognizable like puncture diagonally from vein's cavity into intestine; recognizable it was created thus from beginning of health; several people testified they know clearly this is way of all animals — thin intestines after exit from stomach some measure connected to liver by vein, not separated until cut; when cut, found hollow and punctured diagonally upward. And I saw several animals slaughtered afterward — found thus. Behold it appears exactly the case brought in Beit Yosef siman 47: incident in city Darom — extra found in intestine exiting stomach after pulled handbreadth or two, and they permitted; said all animals have one vein connected to liver, head in liver, connected to thin intestines after pulled from stomach handbreadth or two — hollow, enters intestine, etc.; only not mentioned whether vein enters open through to intestine cavity or not; nevertheless learn from his language "enters intestine" — implies into real cavity; and if vein not open through to intestine cavity but only attached outside — simple it has no law of extra intestine even if not so in all animals, for not called extra intestine unless serves entry and exit of excrement like intestine; otherwise mere protrusion, as Sefer Tur Beit Shmuel siman 47; but since he permitted only because all animals thus — implies enters intestine cavity really through and through. And that which he wrote everything there: sometimes entire vein remains in liver when liver cut from body and intestine exiting stomach partly with no mark of connection — meaning no mark it was connected; but nevertheless puncture may always be recognizable; or truly in that animal sometimes entire vein remained in liver and intestine part — without puncture; but certainly most times vein remains connected to intestine as clear from everyone's words there inside — open through into intestine cavity; otherwise no reason to declare treifah for extra intestine, as Peri Chadash when entangled in intestine — hollow to intestine only no excrement; if so, vein connected open through to intestine cavity — we have no concern; and this is not intestine puncture, for although organ with cavity created punctured is treifah — that is where not way of most animals; and in this Rashba in responsum Beit Yosef siman 46 was asked on puncture in thin intestines near stomach going on slope, etc., appears created thus; some say most animals thus; he answered treifah, do not heed those saying most animals thus, words have no substance — end. Not needed per Tzitz Eliezer in responsum brought in Tur Beit Shmuel siman 30: Rashba's reason for rejecting people — we do not hang it happened from animal's knocking; if so, certainly in our case puncture open through to vein cavity connected to liver — appears created thus, not from knocking; even per Tur Beit Shmuel: Rashba's reason rejecting people — "we never saw animals thus at all, all the more most" — end; then our case where we saw and heard vein and puncture in nearly all our animals, how forbid for thin intestines punctured — behold most animals are not treifot, for we hold majority animals are kosher; perforce this puncture is not included in thin intestines punctured; and so Tur Beit Shmuel per Rashba: if certainly most animals thus — we must say kosher. And Yeshuot Yaakov on everything there: requires great examination of butchers' assembly who understand matter thoroughly if exactly like their words — Rav said only regarding extra intestine if exactly like extra intestine — i.e., if excrement in it requires examination; but if not like intestine but like vein as way of all animals forever — we never saw nor heard examination needed; if so, would need examine and assemble butchers in all animals where vein found; and Yeshuot Yaakov perhaps said only l'chatchila; and so language "requires" as Taz Yoreh De'ah siman 87; but if already cut or lost and impossible to clarify if exactly like case there — permit in great financial loss at least; for we hold siman 50: anything common to permit more than forbid — even if certainly while alive we hang to permit; and siman 52 s.k. 6: discoloration found on organ and we do not know it fell in fire — we hang discoloration to something else like illness, since falling in fire uncommon; likewise puncture or extra in intestine uncommon and vein common — we hang puncture or extra in place intestine connected to liver to what is common in most animals; and he testified vein itself never one way: sometimes entirely in liver, implying need not cut from intestine; sometimes head in intestine; sometimes length three fingers or more; sometimes connection place finger or more; sometimes very thin — perforce puncture also varies — wider when vein larger, varies by animal size; certainly not defect that sometimes puncture wider, sometimes less. And even in Sefer Shekel: if treifot from birth would appear to permit if lived 12 months or gave birth — since most treifot do not live or bear, and this animal lived and bore — implies not treifah puncture but way of all animals; if treifah puncture — would be born thus and not live and bear; and although certainly treifah we do not permit from living and bearing — because we see it is from minority that live; but when we may hang not treifah — we hang well, and no worse than safek treifah permitted after 12 months; and so Tur Beit Shmuel permitted chickens with protrusions on skull, etc.

(Seif 5) Chalchulit, etc. Since I saw Beit Yosef challenged Ran improperly, I was compelled to copy Talmud and poskim in Sha'arei Dura. We learn in Talmud Piskei Avodah Zarah 50a: Ze'iri said: chalchulit punctured — kosher, since thighs hold it up. Rashi and Ran: they seal it, for attached to them. And we say in Talmud: Ze'iri was challenged — how much? Rabbi Ila said Rabbi Yochanan said: place of adhesion — in majority; not in place of adhesion — with any amount. Rav Nachman said: place of adhesion — even if all removed, kosher, provided kedei tefisah remains. Ran wrote: and how much place in chalchulit do thighs hold up to judge this law that it not be disqualified by puncture in place of adhesion in majority — where stuck to thighs not forbidden unless majority punctured; not in place of adhesion forbidden with any amount; and halakha is Rav Nachman: in place of adhesion, even if all removed, whatever remains of kedei tefisah — kosher; therefore Ze'iri's case above place of adhesion is resolved, kosher with any amount until there is majority — end Ran. Simple: "therefore" means Ze'iri deals above place of adhesion — when animal hangs it is called above (see siman 35); and he holds like Rabbi Yochanan: kosher with any amount until majority; for Rabbi Yochanan comes to explain Ze'iri. And more plausible: "from place of adhesion" Ran wrote is scribal error — should be "in place of adhesion" with bet. Even if we read "from place of adhesion" — means as I wrote; and this is simple in my eyes — only I saw Beit Yosef left Ran's words in doubt because he understood Ran's "therefore Ze'iri" means Ze'iri deals not in place of adhesion, per "halakha is Rav Nachman," and Rav Nachman argues Rabbi Yochanan also not in place of adhesion: Rabbi Yochanan — with any amount forbidden; Rav Nachman — with any amount kosher until majority; and Ze'iri deals not in place of adhesion like Rav Nachman; and therefore he wondered: it implies Rav Nachman does not argue Rabbi Yochanan not in place of adhesion — for all forbid with any amount; and he did not examine — Ran's words are simple and clear as I wrote; and wonder at yourself: how say Ze'iri deals not in place of adhesion — he said thighs hold it up; Rashi and Ran: they seal it, attached to thighs — behold deals where attached to thighs. Also Beit Yosef forced: Ran explains Rabbi Yochanan "not in place of adhesion with any amount" means Ze'iri said chalchulit punctured kosher not in place of adhesion — kosher in every puncture until majority — end Beit Yosef; I wonder — Ran explicitly not so; for he wrote on Rabbi Yochanan not in place of adhesion with any amount — meaning forbidden with any amount — end; finally Beit Yosef's words here need carpenter to fit them; all because he read Ran places Ze'iri not in place of adhesion — and I proved impossible; and so Rashba Mishmeret HaBayit and Ra'ah explain Ze'iri in place of adhesion; only they explain differently; and in my humble opinion Ran's explanation is correct among all explanations; and so Maharam of Tikotin in gloss on Ran as I explained — see and examine; and see Bach, Arukh Mesharim, responsum Mikdash Melech siman 11.
