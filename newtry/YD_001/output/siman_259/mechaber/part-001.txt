One who vows and does not know what he vowed, and the law of utterance to Heaven (and the law of whether one may change charity and the conduct of the collector with charity or the appointee over it). And it contains 6 seifim. One who says, "This coin is for charity," or who says, "Behold I am obligated a coin for charity," and separated it before it came to the collector's hand — he may change it until it reaches the collector's hand; and if it reached the collector's hand, he may not change it.
