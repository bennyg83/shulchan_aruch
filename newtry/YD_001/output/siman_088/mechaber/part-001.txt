**** ENGLISH ****
Not to place meat on the table where one eats cheese. In it are 2 seifim: Even meat of wild animal and fowl — it is forbidden to place it on a table where one eats cheese, lest they come to eat them together; but on a table where a cooked dish is set out, it is permitted to place this beside that.

**** ENGLISH ****
That which is forbidden to place on the table applies specifically when two people know each other — even if they are punctilious with each other; but guests who do not know each other — permitted. And even those who know each other, if they made some recognizable separation (heker) — such as each eats on his own cloth, or even they eat on one cloth and place bread between them as a marker — permitted: {Rama: Specifically that they do not eat from the bread placed between them as a marker; but if they eat from it, it is not a heker, for in any case the bread they eat from rests on the table (Arukh and Hagahot Ashiri). But if they placed between them a vessel from which they drink and it is not customary for it to be on the table — it is a heker even though they drink from the vessel (Beit Yosef in Orach Chaim siman 173). And all the more if they placed there a lamp or other things on the table — it is a heker. And they should be careful not to drink from one vessel because food sticks to the vessel (hagahah Ashiri and Orach Chaim). And all the more they should not eat from one bread; and so the custom is to set a salt vessel for each one by himself, because sometimes they dip in salt and food remnants remain in the salt.}
