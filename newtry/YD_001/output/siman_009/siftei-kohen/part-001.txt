["<b>שחיטתו פסולה. </b>דסכין כשנכנס לתוך החתך קודם שתשחט רוב הסימנים היא שורפת הסימנים ונמצא שהיא נשרפה קודם שחיטה שהרי ושט נקובתו במשהו ליטרף ושריפה כנקב היא רש\"י וע\"ל סימן כ\"ו ס\"ק א':"]
**** ENGLISH ****
**His shechitah is invalid.** For when knife enters the cut before majority of simanim are slaughtered it burns the simanim—and it is found it was burned before shechitah—for esophagus is punctured in something to become treifah, and burning is like puncturing—Rashi; and see above siman 26 seif kaf alef.

**** ENGLISH ****
**If he knew it was heated.** And then sharpened before gleaming—and one may say regarding flaw from the side that if he slaughtered with it treifah as below beginning siman 18; and we do not distinguish whether he knew initially or not—for there rust of knife goes out and spreads in sides of slaughter house and tears them; and even if he knew impossible not to touch sides—Ra'osh and Maharshal first chapter Chullin siman 10 and Bach and Ittur ruled like first view that even if he knew his shechitah is invalid.
