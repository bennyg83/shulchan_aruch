Taz wrote: even though in siman 65 he ruled that for roasting one need not cut or salt, for fire draws out — here is different, for the whole head is on the fire and it is as if the brain is in a pot since there is no place for it to exit; and Shach wrote that all the rest of the meat is permitted, and even peeling is not needed, for the membrane separates between the blood inside and the meat, and the blood is not expelled outside; and even if expelled — as it absorbs so it expels.

<b>שאוסר. </b> כתב הש"ך דיש לתמוה לאיזה צורך כתב הרב בהג"ה לעיל ס"י דלא מיקרי עירוי כשפסק הקילוח הא המחבר כתב כאן ב' דעות בזה ודוחק לומ' שמיירי כאן שהתרנגולת מונחת במחבת ועירה על התרנגולת והמחבת ביחד דפשט הל' לא משמע הכי כלל ועוד דבנדון זה כתב הרב בהג"ה סימן צ"ה ס"ג דפשוט דשרי מטעם דאין עירוי ככלי ראשון ממש שיעשה שיבלע מזה לזה לכן נראה דהדברים כפשטן והיש מי שמתיר הוא רשב"ם שהביאו הטור וב"י דס"ל דאם עירה על התרנגולת א"צ קליפה אפילו ראשה למטה ואע"ג דבסעיף י' סתם המחבר כדעת ר"ת שהביא הטור דצריך קליפה סמך עצמו אמה שיביא בסוף הסימן גם המתיר וע"ל סימן ק"ה:
**** ENGLISH ****
That forbids. Shach wrote: one should wonder for what purpose the Rav wrote in hagahah above seif 10 that it is not called irui when the stream stopped — behold the Mechaber wrote here two views on this; and it is forced to say that here it deals when the chicken is placed in a pan and he poured on the chicken and the pan together, for the plain law does not imply this at all; and further, in this case the Rav wrote in hagahah siman 95 seif 3 plainly that it is permitted because irui is not like a true kli rishon that would cause mutual absorption; therefore it appears the matters are as their plain sense, and 'there are those who permit' is Rashbam, whom Tur and Beit Yosef brought, who holds that if he poured on the chicken he need not peel even if its head is downward; and even though in seif 10 the Mechaber ruled anonymously like Rabbenu Tam whom Tur brought, that peeling is needed — he relied on what he would bring at the end of the siman also the permitting view; and see above siman 105.
