<b>ואם נדר כו'. </b>כוונתו לתרץ קושית המפרשים ממ"ש בנדרים כ"ה א' ואע"ג דקיי"ל כו' וכן אמרינן שם <small>(כ"ה א') </small>א"ל כי משתבע אדעתא כו' ואנן כו' אלא דשם אמרינן משמע מצות ציצית כו' ודוקא דבר שרחוק ממשמעות א"י לומר אבל באומר בפי' על דעתו אז אפי' כ"ד שבמשמעות א"י לומר ע"ש ובשבועות כ"ט א' ובכה"ג מיירי הרמב"ם וז"ל בפ"ב מה"ש מי שנשבע ואמר כו' ועל דעתכם אני נשבע אין זה י"ל כך וכך היה בלבי וכ"כ תוס' שם כ"ו ב' ד"ה גמר. ואי הוה אמרינן כו'. אבל בסתם י"ל תנאי וכמ"ש במסכתא כלה היה ר"ע נשבע בשפתיו ומבטל בלבו וכ"ש בנודר ע"י עצמו שי"ל תנאי היה בלבי כמ"ש בפ"ו דתוספתא דטהרות נאמן ע"י עצמו כיצד א"ל נדרת ואמר תנאי היה בלבי שומעין לו וכ"ה בת"כ <small>(פ' ויקרא) </small>א"ל נזיר אתה כו' <small>(וע"ל סי' רל"ב ס"ק ל"ה):</small>
**** ENGLISH ****And if he vowed, etc. His intention is to answer the commentators' difficulty from what is written in Nedarim 25a — "and even though we hold," etc.; and we likewise say there (25a) "he told him when one swears with intention," etc., "and we," etc.; but there we say it implies the mitzvah of tzitzit, etc.; and specifically a matter far from its meaning one cannot say; but one who says explicitly "with my intention" — then even all twenty-four that are in its meaning — see there. And in Shevuot 29a, and in such a case Rambam deals: "One who swore and said, etc., 'with your understanding I swear' — this is not yad," etc.; likewise Tosafot there 26b, "gamar." "And if we would say," etc. But unspecified — a condition applies, as in the tractate — Rabbi Akiva would swear with his lips and annul in his heart; and all the more one who vows by himself — a condition was in my heart, as in chapter 6 of Tosefta of Taharot — one is believed about himself: how? They said to him "you vowed" and he said "a condition was in my heart" — we listen to him; likewise in Torat Kohanim (Vayikra) — they said to him "you are a nazir," etc. (and see above siman 232 s.k. 35).

<b>וי"א כו'. </b>כמ"ש פ"ק דנדרים <small>(ח' א') </small>נידוהו בחלום כו' וכגירסת הרמב"ם אלא דל"ג רק עד י' דידעי למקרי. וסברא הראשונה ס"ל דלא דמי לשם דשם סי' קללה הוא משמים וצריך קירוב:
**** ENGLISH ****And some say, etc. As in chapter 1 of Nedarim (8a) — "they ostracized him in a dream," etc.; and Rambam's text, except he only counts up to ten who know to read. The first reasoning holds it is not like there, for there it is a curse from heaven and requires drawing near; but one who swore — even awake we require mouth and heart equal — all the more in a dream where there is neither mouth nor heart. "And some say" — this is Rashba, who wrote for mere concern one should be stringent even in an oath in a dream; it appears to my humble opinion — if a woman swore with self-affliction, annulment by her husband on the day he hears within twenty-four hours applies, lest this oath — which is a mere stringency — be preferable to an oath while awake; and even though for requiring ten initially they were more stringent than awake where three suffice — nevertheless one should not learn stringency except what Magen Avraham wrote — the husband cannot annul since they swore him from heaven; and I found Maharal of Prague who answered practically like Rosh — no annulment at all.
