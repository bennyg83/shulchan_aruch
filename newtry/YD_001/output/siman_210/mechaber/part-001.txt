<b>שצריך לפרוט הנדר כשפתיו. ובו ג' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>אין הנדר חל עד <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i>שיוציא בשפתיו ויהיו פיו ולבו שוים לפיכך היה בלבו לידור מפת חטים והוציא בשפתיו פת שעורים <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i>מותר <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="(°)" data-order="1"></i>בשניהם אבל אם היה בלבו פת חטים <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="2"></i><small>(או פת שעורים) </small><i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="3"></i>והוציא בשפתיו פת סתם <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="2"></i>אסור בשל חטים <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="1"></i>לבד <small>(או בשל שעורים לבד) (דכן משמע בסימן רי"ז ס"ט) </small><i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>ואם נדר על דעת <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="2"></i>אחרים אינו תלוי בלבו אלא כפי מה שיאמרו אחרים שהוא דעתם כך יחול הנדר:
**** ENGLISH ****
That one must specify the vow as his lips intend; it contains three se’ifim. A vow does not take effect until he articulates it with his lips, and his mouth and heart are aligned. Therefore, if it was in his heart to vow from wheat bread, and he articulated with his lips barley bread — he is permitted in both of them. But if it was in his heart wheat bread (or barley bread), and he articulated with his lips bread without specification — he is forbidden in wheat bread alone (or in barley bread alone) (for so it appears in siman 217, seif 9). And if he vowed according to the understanding of others, it is not dependent on his heart; rather, according to what others say is their understanding — so the vow takes effect.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who vows in a dream — it is nothing, and he does not need questioning. And there are those who say that they should release him with ten who know how to release (and they release him through regret, as if he vowed while awake) (responsum of the Geonim). And one should be concerned for their words. (However, if he does not treat vows lightly, ten who know how to release — they release him with three, like any other vow) (Rashba, siman 268).

**** ENGLISH ****
One who vows and retracts within the time of speech, or others persuade him within the time of speech and he accepts their words (within the time of speech of his vow) (the view of the Rav; and with this the objection of Raavad against Rambam is resolved, and not like Beit Yosef) — it is not a vow; and this is when he says so with his mouth. But if he said, “I retracted in my heart,” it is nothing.
