**** ENGLISH ****
**(seif 2) And even if severed.** See Be'er HaGolah and as written there in Tosafot “he stuck,” etc.—different is knife, etc.—thus the question is his regarding severed; and Tosafot haRosh wrote since sugya of Gemara was resolved for them what they said there “and from where do we derive,” etc.—we do not leave Gemara's resolution and seize Rava's question. (Likkut) **And even if, etc.** As written above in Tosafot “there was,” etc. “the owner in the wall,” etc.—thus the question is wall of a house; and as written regarding idol worship and plants; and there in Tosafot “he stuck,” etc. Tosafot haRosh wrote: even though for Rava there is a question, nevertheless sugya above is not difficult here regarding attached, etc.—it is resolved for them kosher in every case that only attached from outset disqualifies. And Ra'ah wrote Rava's question is only because it seemed to him in plants it is a tannaitic dispute; but per Rif the same here (end quote).
