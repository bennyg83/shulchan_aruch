<b>ואפילו אם בטלו. </b>ומהרש"ל שם פסק כהרא"ש דאם בטלו אפילו בדיעבד אסור וכ"כ ר' ירוחם שכן דעת רוב הפוסקים גם הב"ח נוטה להחמיר וכן דעת הרא"ה בספר ב"ה דף י"ד:
**** ENGLISH ****

<b>ואפי' אמר ברי לי כו'. </b>דחיישינן שמא ידרוס פעם אחרת כ"כ הפוסקים:
**** ENGLISH ****
Or in a detached thing. — One may question—is this not all the more, and for what purpose did the Rav emend it; and one may say because of the end he cited it—that animal neck above even if stuck in detached thing shechitah invalid because he pressed it—and so Rashba, brought by Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe; or also he came to teach that even when animal neck below that shechitah is kosher is specifically b'dieved and not l'chatchila even if stuck in detached thing. And it is proven in Gemara and poskim that with detached knife not stuck in anything—even animal neck above—permitted b'dieved, for whoever holds the knife in his hand presumably does not press; and it is implied in Gemara that l'chatchila nevertheless forbidden; and also with a bird one must be concerned l'chatchila with bird neck above even when knife detached in his hand.
