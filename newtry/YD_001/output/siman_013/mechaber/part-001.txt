**** ENGLISH ****
One who slaughters the animal and finds it kosher, and finds in it a fetus eight months old—whether alive or dead—or nine months dead: it is permitted in eating and does not require shechitah. And if he found in it a nine-month-old alive: if it spread its legs on the ground it requires shechitah; but other treifot do not forbid it (the mother). And if it did not spread on the ground—it does not require shechitah. And if its hooves are cloven (meaning: its hoof is entirely one and is not split) (or there was some other strange matter) (Hagahot Ashiri)—even though it spread on the ground it does not require shechitah. And some are uncertain in the matter.
