["But it is forbidden to draw with a vessel, etc. For perhaps they separated on the walls of the vessel — that specifically on the lip of the vessel they are not common to separate, but on the inner walls of the vessel it is common that they separate and they are forbidden, since from the beginning they were in the cistern; specifically when they separated with the liquid from vessel to vessel they are permitted, even if they afterward separated on the walls of the second vessel, as will be explained in seif 3 — meaning it is their place of growth to be with the liquid in the vessel; therefore the second vessel has the status of the first vessel, unlike here where they were initially in the cistern.
