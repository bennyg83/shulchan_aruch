**** ENGLISH ****
The law of birds filled with unsalted meat. In it is one seif: Birds or kids that one fills with meat that was not salted — if for roasting it is permitted even with their mouths upward, and even if the outer part was salted — for as it absorbs the blood of the filling so it releases it: {Rama: And all the more so if the inner part was salted and not the outer — it is permitted (it appears to me according to Arukh general rule 9) for fire draws and absorbs the blood from the outer part and it is not absorbed in the inner (it appears to me).} But for a pot it is forbidden until one salts the outer alone and the inner alone; but if after filling one salted the outer part it does not release the blood in the inner: {Rama: And all this is b'dieved; but l'chatchila one should not make any filling unless both were salted (Agur and Mordechai chapter 25 and Rashi). And all this applies when this filling has only meat or herbs; but if there are eggs that harden, its law is as if it was cooked in a pot (Hagahat ShaDa in the name of Maharit and Arukh and Bach). And even b'dieved one should forbid if both were not salted. Unsalted meat that was not rinsed that was roasted with salted meat that was rinsed is permitted b'dieved (there). But l'chatchila it is forbidden to roast salted meat with unsalted meat even if rinsed:}
