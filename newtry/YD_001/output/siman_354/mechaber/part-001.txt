**** ENGLISH ****
A city that has two dead — which takes precedence. In it is one seif. A city that has two dead — they take out the first and afterward take out the second; and if they were lodging overnight, the first — they take out the second. A sage and Torah scholar — they take out the sage; a Torah scholar and am ha'aretz — they take out the Torah scholar. A man and woman — they take out the woman because she is closer to disgrace. If they took out the first and buried him — they do not stand in a row for him nor say over him the mourners' blessing nor condolences until they take out the second. If they took out the second and buried him — they come and stand in a row, console, and dismiss the many; and they do not console two mourners as one unless their honor and praise were equal.
