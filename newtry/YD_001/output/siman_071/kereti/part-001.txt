**** ENGLISH ****
He returns and salts — and if he cut head and salted outer side and not inside: per hagahah above siman 69 seif 4, b'dieved forbidden; and even though if cut, salting head outer side all around would suffice — Peri Chadash gave reason of tannaitic enactment to salt two sides, and this is forced; but in truth we must say: when head is whole and salted all around, air does not cool and block heat of salt from emitting, for it is whole all around; but when cut and salted only one side, on the other side air rules and cools the piece and prevents salting heat on other side from emitting.
