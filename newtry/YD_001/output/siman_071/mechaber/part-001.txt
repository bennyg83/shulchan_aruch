**** ENGLISH ****
The law of salting the head, hooves, and brain. And it has 3 seifim:

One cuts the head in two and salts it well on the face side, and returns and salts on the hair — for hair does not block salting from emitting blood.

**** ENGLISH ****
The membrane on the brain has threads, and the brain itself has blood and does not leave its blood through salting the head, because the skull bone surrounds it and stands before it and there is no place for blood to flow; therefore when one comes to salt it he tears it and extracts the brain and tears the membrane and salts it. And if he wished to salt the head with the brain inside — he pierces the bone opposite the membrane and pierces the membrane as well and salts, and leaves the hole below, and it is permitted even for a pot. {Rama: And see above siman 68 how they practice l'chatchila (Shaarei Dura in the name of Maharam). And if the head was salted whole without piercing the bone — the membrane and brain are forbidden and the rest of the head is permitted, and certainly the rest of the flesh with it (Or Zarua general rule 17 and Shaarei Dura). The marrow bone and other bones that contain brain require salting, and salting the bone helps for the brain inside it and one need not pierce the bone (Tur in the name of Rashba). However l'chatchila one should not salt them with other meat, only alone; and b'dieved it is permitted (Or Zarua general rule 31).}
