["<b>הראש חותכו לשנים כו'. </b>אין לפרש הטעם משום דלכתחלה צריך למלוח מב' צדדין וכדלעיל סי' ס\"ט ס\"ד דאם כן אמאי שרי כאן בס\"ג ע\"י נקיבת העצם והקרום אלא ודאי כיון שמולח מבחוץ מכל צדדין סגי דלא יהא אלא חתיכה אחת עבה וה\"ק הראש חותכו לשנים כו' כלומר מי שרוצה לעשות מצוה מן המובחר למלוח הראש והמוח חותך הראש לשנים ומולחו יפה לצד פנים משום המוח כו' ובס\"ג מבואר דמי שרוצה למלוח הראש והמוח בתוכו ינקוב העצם והקרום:"]
**** ENGLISH ****
The head — one cuts it in two, etc. One should not interpret the reason as that l'chatchila one must salt from two sides, as above siman 69 seif 4 — for if so, why is it permitted here in seif 3 through piercing the bone and membrane? Rather certainly, since he salts from outside on all sides it suffices that it not be only one thick piece; and this is what it means: the head — one cuts it in two, etc. — meaning, one who wishes to perform mitzvah min hamuvchar to salt the head and brain cuts the head in two and salts it well on the face side because of the brain, etc.; and in seif 3 it is explained that one who wishes to salt the head and brain inside pierces the bone and membrane.

**** ENGLISH ****
The hooves — one cuts them slightly below, etc. Because otherwise it is like salting in a non-perforated vessel, for hooves block blood from exiting; and even when salted, blood does not exit through the hooves — unlike other bones whose salting helps for the brain inside, as the Mechaber wrote in seif 4, because blood is emitted through the bones by salting — and this is simple; and so it is proven explicitly in Rashba and Tur. And Pri To'ar wrote, and such is his language: hooves differ from other bones — for in other bones the brain inside fills the entire cavity and there is no place for blood to enter and remain; but hooves are hollow inside and flesh is not stuck to all of them, and if one does not open them there is concern lest emission-blood collect in one place and it be like salting in a non-perforated vessel — end of his words. And it is not clear, for one who salts in a non-perforated vessel is forbidden because the meat placed in it is in a cramped place and therefore does not emit well, and we are concerned it may have spread from place to place, as in siman 69 s.k. 68; rather the reason is simple as explained.

**** ENGLISH ****
And the rest of the head is permitted. Because the skull bone separates between blood inside it and flesh; and even if a little blood emitted and entered the flesh — as it absorbs so it releases — so wrote Rashba in Torat Chatas HaAroch daf 75b, and the Rosh chapter 5, and Or Zarua general rule 17 law 7; and it appears to me the first reason is primary, and for clarity of the matter they wrote the second reason; and also Ran in chapter Gid HaNasheh wrote only the first reason. And according to this, even other flesh that was salted with it and already emitted all its blood and gravy — in a matter where "as it absorbs so it releases" does not apply — nevertheless it is permitted (if the flesh was removed from the head outside on the skull). And the rest of the laws of salting, roasting, and cooking of head and brain are explained in siman 68 — see there.
