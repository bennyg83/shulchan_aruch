בתוך טבעת זהו לשון רמב"ם בפי' המשנה מזה משמע דחיטי' הם בתוך הטבעת ואם שחט בחיטי עדיין לא הגרים למעלה מטבעת ובגמ' משמע דחיטים הם למעלה מטבעת הגדולה ואם שחט בחיטי כבר עבר הטבעת הגדולה למעלה ועיין בככה"ג לי"ד שכ' שיש לעמוד בו. ומה שנראה לי אחר חקירה ועיון עם שוחטים הוא כך כי בהמה כאשר אוכלת והולכת שחוח החיטין הן בתוך הטבעת ונכפפי' לתוכו ובזה דבר הרב הרמב"ם אבל כשפושטת הצוואר בעת שחיטה כסדר הנשחטים אז מושכת החיטין למעלה לצד שיפוי כובע ולצד הראש ואז הוא למעלה מטבעת:
**** ENGLISH ****
Within the ring—this is the language of the Rambam in his explanation of the mishnah; from this it implies the wheat grains are within the ring; and if he slaughtered in the wheat grains he has not yet been derore above the ring. And in the Gemara it implies the wheat grains are above the large ring; and if he slaughtered in the wheat grains he has already passed the large ring upward. And see in Kol Bo siman 14 who wrote one should stand on this. And what appears to me after investigation and examination with slaughterers is thus: when the animal eats and walks bent over, the wheat grains are within the ring and bend into it—and with this the words of Rav Rambam fit; but when it stretches the neck at the time of shechitah as those being slaughtered do, then it pulls the wheat grains upward toward hat slope and toward the head—and then it is above the ring.

**** ENGLISH ****
His slaughter is invalid—and this means nevelah; and the latter authorities raised Shach and Peri Chadash that it deals with one who did not slaughter it after shechitah but left the animal or bird until it died—therefore nevelah, for there is no shechitah here and it died by itself is considered; but if he returned and slaughtered it properly, behold this is treifah and not nevelah, for shechitah purifies it; in the crop of the esophagus only treifah and not nevelah—and this is correct. And behold if the spinal column was severed in a place not fit for shechitah—the view of Peri Chadash and so too K'li Muor that its law is also like the crop and esophagus—not nevelah; and that which Rambam and Mechaber wrote in seif 1 "behold this is hagramah and invalid" means as they said: it deals with one who did not slaughter again but died from that shechitah; and see Peleti that tzarich iyun, for there is great proof in the spinal column that if majority was severed even not in the place of shechitah it is nevelah—for in any case majority exists and majority is like the whole, as if there is no spinal column at all it is considered; and therefore above where it teaches "leave in the wheat grains"—"invalid" is taught properly and it is nevelah—see there.
