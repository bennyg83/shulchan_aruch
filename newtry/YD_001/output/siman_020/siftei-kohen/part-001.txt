And that is at the end of the trachea, etc. — And the view of Mahariu appears not to validate above the large ring; and so Mahari"l; and so in Hagahot Mahariu; and so Agur on Ashkenazi shechitah; and so he saw practiced his teacher his father, may he rest in peace; and so Maharshal in his hagahot on shechitot—that we do not practice validating at all above the large ring because of concern for mishaps; and so in recent shechitot; even though Bach wondered—behold it is explained in chapter 1 of Chullin that it is kosher; and in my humble opinion they derived thus from the incident of hagramah in chapter HaGozel (Bava Kamma daf 99b)—it implies there that because of avodah zarah there is reason to be stringent not to validate above the large ring, as Tosafot there—see there; however Bach concludes in a place of great loss and the like one should rule as the Mechaber wrote; and so ruled Maharshal in shechitot.

<b>שחיטתו פסולה. </b>כל זה הוא לשון הרמב"ם וכתב עליו הכ"מ וז"ל משמע דנבילה הוי שהרי כתב בפרק ג' כל מקום שאמרנו בשחיטה פסולה ה"ז נבלה ואם אוכל ממנה כזית לוקה משום אוכל נבלה וקשה דתורבץ הושט וכרס לא הוי אלא טרפה ולא נבלה וי"ל דמש"ה לא אמר ה"ז נבלה ומטמא במשא אלא אם אכל ממנה כזית לוקה אבל לענין טומאה לא נחית ומשום דרוב הנזכרים באלו פרקים הוי נבלה נקט לה לשון נבלה עכ"ל ולא ידעתי מה היה לו שהרי כתב בהדיא ולוקה משום אוכל נבלה אלא ודאי אע"ג דנקיבת התורבץ (ועמ"ש בסי' ל"ג ס"ג) וכרס לא הוי אלא טרפה מכל מקום כיון שנתקלקל וניקב בשחיטה ה"ל שחיטה גורם לה לפסול והוי נבלה גמורה כדתנן בהדיא בפ' השוחט כל ששחיטה גורם לה לפסול נבלה דהא ודאי כל טרפה אם לא נשחטה ומתה כך נבלה היא והלכך כיון ששחט בתורבץ הוושט וכרס נטרפה ואין השחיטה מתירתה דזהו לאו מקום שחיטה ואין לך נבלה גדולה מזו וזה פשוט. ונ"מ לדידן במאי דהוי נבלה לענין או"ב וכמש"ל סימן ט"ז ס"ס:
**** ENGLISH ****
And in a bird below in the trachea, etc. — Thus wrote Darkei Moshe; and in Ran chapter Eizehu Mekoman daf 128a he wrote: and Ra'ah received from his brothers that the measure of the esophagus in a bird is opposite it in the trachea—end; and therefore it is recorded here in Shulchan Aruch "Ran in the name of Ra'ah"; and it is an error—for thus said Ran: measure of the esophagus of a bird is above opposite it in the trachea from hat slope downward; and so it is explicitly in his words, and Beit Yosef brings it; and so in Ra'ah in Beit HaBayit daf 25a; if so, tzarich iyun where Rav Berachot derived this "below in the trachea like the esophagus"; and it appears to me it came out to him from what Beit Yosef wrote that in a bird below is the esophagus until the roof of the zefek, and in the trachea until opposite the roof of the zefek; and below in s.k. 7 it is explained that the measure of the esophagus below until the zefek that Tur wrote means the roof of the zefek.

<b>בבהמה וחיה כו'. </b>גם זה הוא לשון הרמב"ם ומשמע דס"ל דכל בהמות וחיות שוות בשעור זה רק בעוף שיש במינו קטנים לפי גדלו וקטנו ולכן תמה ב"י על הטור שכתב אחר שעורו של הרמב"ם דהוא בשור הגדול ושאר בהמה וחיה ועוף לפי גדלו וקטנו דאין דבריו מכוונים שהתוס' והרא"ש לא כתבו כן אלא אחר שיעורו של רש"י שהוא ד' אצבעות עכ"ל. וגם הרשב"א כתב בת"ה אחר שיעורו של הרמב"ם וזו בבהמה וחיה אבל בעוף כו' והב"ח הבין שתלונות הב"י הוא על מ"ש הטור ובעוף לפי גדלו וקטנו ולכן תמה עליו שהרי בדברי הרמב"ם ורשב"א מבוארים כמ"ש הטור וז"א שהרי מיד אח"כ כתב הב"י והרמב"ם בפ"א כתב כפירוש בה"ג וכתב שזהו השעור בבהמה וחיה אבל בעוף הכל לפי גדלו וקטנו עכ"ל ב"י הרי בהדיא כוונתו כמ"ש אח"כ מצאתי בדרישה שפירש להדיא דעת הב"י כדברי ועיין ס"ק ד':
**** ENGLISH ****
His slaughter is invalid. — All this is the language of the Rambam; and K'li Muor wrote on it etc.: it implies it is nevelah, for wherever we said in shechitah "invalid" it is nevelah, and if one eats from it a kezayit he is lashed for eating nevelah; and it is difficult—for the crop of the esophagus and the stomach are only treifah and not nevelah; and one can say that therefore he did not say "it is nevelah" and "it imparts tumah in a burden," but only "if one ate from it a kezayit he is lashed"; but for tumah he did not descend; and because most of those mentioned in these chapters are nevelah, he used the language nevelah—end. And I do not know what troubled him, for he wrote explicitly "and lashed for eating nevelah"; rather certainly—even though perforation of the crop (and see siman 33 seif 3) and stomach are only treifah—nevertheless since it was disqualified and perforated in shechitah, shechitah caused it to become invalid and it is a complete nevelah, as we taught explicitly in chapter HaShochet: anything shechitah causes to become invalid is nevelah—for certainly every treifah, if it were not slaughtered and died, is nevelah; therefore since he slaughtered in the crop of the esophagus and stomach it became treifah and the shechitah does not permit it, for this is not a place of shechitah; and there is no greater nevelah than this—and this is simple. And there is a nafka mina for us in what is nevelah regarding oteh v'et beno, as written above siman 16 end.

<b>ולמטה עד הזפק. </b>פי' הפרישה והב"ח דהיינו עד גגו של זפק ולא גגו בכלל וכ"פ הרא"ש פא"ט ומהרש"ל שם סי' ו' וכן הוא במהרי"ו:
**** ENGLISH ****
In animal and beast, etc. — This too is the language of the Rambam; and it implies he holds that all animals and beasts are equal in this measure, only in a bird according to its value large and small; and therefore Bach wondered on Tur, who after the Rambam's measure—which is in a large ox and the rest of animal, beast, and bird according to large and small—his words are not aligned, for Tosafot and Ra'osh did not write thus but after Rashi's measure, which is four fingers—end. And Rashba too wrote in teshuvah ha'arokhah after the Rambam's measure—and this in animal and beast, but in a bird etc.; and Bach understood that Bachya's complaints are on what Tur wrote "and in a bird according to large and small," and therefore wondered on him, for behold in the words of Rambam and Rashba it is explained as Tur wrote; and this is that behold immediately after Bachya wrote "and Rambam in chapter 1 wrote as the explanation of BeHaG, and wrote that this is the measure in animal and beast, but in a bird everything according to large and small"—end; Beit Yosef—behold explicitly his intent as said after. I found in Derishah that he explained explicitly Bachya's view as my words; and see s.k. 4.
