Shach — it appears even if the lender already collected the ribbit he is not believed to say it was hekdesh and he gave it to hekdesh; and if it is fixed ribbit he must return it; and in Maharam's responsum brought by Mordechai it appears the guardian is believed with oath to say they are orphans' money; and if the borrower claims I do not believe you — it is a claim of perhaps and he does not even need oath; it seems to distinguish — specifically when claiming it was hekdesh money he needs proof lest everyone claim hekdesh or other mitzvah money; but regarding orphans' guardian who is known as guardian this does not apply; see CM siman 34 seif 12; and in Maharam responsum regarding Reuven who had charity money of individuals and lent via gentile with document without collateral and Shimon claims regarding ribbit he gave saying it was not charity but his — Reuven believed even without oath that it was charity money — end quote; it seems there different for it was known charity money was in his hand, therefore permitted lest he leave permitted and eat forbidden; nevertheless it seems he is believed without oath only because borrower does not claim certainly; but if borrower claims certainly even though charity money was in his hand he still needs oath; and although below siman 169 s.k. 205 lender exempt without oath — there different for he speaks permissively that he lent and permitted lest he leave permitted and eat forbidden; but here since it became clear he lent at ribbit only saying orphans' money — in such case he needs proof — end quote Shach; and Taz wrote that he is not believed except with proof — it seems plain that as long as he has presumption of orphans' money he is believed before orphans with oath that ribbit came to them and they lose what he returned of this ribbit.

<b>פקוח. </b> כתב הט"ז ומיירי שאין אדם כשר רוצה להלוות בחנם רק יש ישראל רשע א' שרוצה להלות ברבית מותר ליקח ממנו הלואה ברבית מפני פקוח נפש אבל המלוה פשיטא שעושה איסור דמה פקוח נפש יש לו שצריך ליקת רבית אדרבה מוטל עליו להלות בחנם וגם מ"ש רמ"א בהג"ה שנהגו להקל קצת לצורך קהל מיירי נמי בכה"ג דאין כאן רק אדם רשע שרוצה דוקא ברבית ולא כאותן שטועים בדבר ואומרים שגם למלוה יש היתר חלילה לומר כן עכ"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****Life-saving. Taz wrote — deals where no kosher person wishes to lend for free, only one wicked Israelite wishes to lend at ribbit — permitted to take loan at ribbit for pikuach nefesh; but the lender clearly violates — what pikuach nefesh that he must take ribbit? Rather obligation to lend for free; and what Rama wrote in Hagah that they were lenient somewhat for community need — also deals thus where only wicked person wishes specifically ribbit, not those who err and say lender also has permission — Heaven forbid to say so — end quote.

<b>מלאכה. </b> כתב הש"ך כלומר שהמלוה הוא בעל מלאכה והלוה אינו בעל מלאכה אלא שנותן לאיזה בעל מלאכה אם צריך לשום מלאכה אסור להלות לו ע"מ שכל מלאכה שתבא לידו שיתנה למלוה אבל אם הלוה הוא בעל מלאכה והוא מלוה לו ע"מ שיעשה לו מלאכה בזול פשיטא דהוי רבית קצוצה לכ"ע אפילו למ"ד לקמן סימן קס"ו ס"ב בחצר דהוי אבק רבית כיון שלא התנו בשעת מתן מעות מודה הכא דדוקא לדור בחצר דלאו מידי יהיב ליה אע"ג דעומד לאגרא הוי אבק רבית אבל אם קצבן לתת לו לא ומכ"ש לדידן דקי"ל אפילו בחצר דהוי ר"ק ועיין בא"ח סי' ק"ע ס"ג עכ"ל והט"ז כתב אילולי פה קדוש דהרא"ש הייתי אומר דהיתר גמור הוא וראיה ברורה מדברי הש"ס הובא בש"ע סימן קע"ב ס"ד דאם אמר המלוה ללוה אם תמכור קרקע שלך לא תמכור אלא לי בשויה וע"מ כן אני מלוה לך דמותר והיינו ממש הך דהכא <small>(ובנה"כ דחה ראייתו דיש לחלק ע"ש)</small>:
**** ENGLISH ****Craft/work. Shach wrote — meaning the lender is a craftsman and borrower is not a craftsman but gives to some craftsman; if he needs some work it is forbidden to lend on condition all work that comes to his hand he give the lender; but if borrower is craftsman and lends on condition he do work cheaply — plainly fixed ribbit for all, even for one who says below siman 166 seif 2 regarding courtyard it is dust of ribbit, since they did not stipulate at giving money — here agrees specifically for dwelling in courtyard he gives nothing though standing for rent is dust of ribbit; but if fixed to give him — no; and all the more for us who hold even courtyard is fixed ribbit; see Orach Chayyim siman 170 seif 3 — end quote; and Taz wrote were it not for the holy mouth of Rosh I would say complete permission, and clear proof from Talmud brought in Shulchan Aruch siman 172 seif 4 — if lender said to borrower if you sell your land do not sell except to me at its value and on that condition I lend you — permitted, and this is exactly this case (and Nekudot HaKesef rejected his proof that one can distinguish — see there).
