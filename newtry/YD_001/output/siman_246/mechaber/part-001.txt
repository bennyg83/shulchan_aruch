**** ENGLISH ****
Whoever abandons Torah from wealth — in the end abandons it from poverty; and whoever upholds Torah from poverty — in the end upholds it from wealth: {Rama: When one finishes a tractate it is a mitzvah to rejoice and make a feast, called a seudat mitzvah (Nimmukei Yosef, chapter Yesh Nochalin, and Abaye's statement, chapter Kol Kitvei); and it is forbidden to engage in words of Torah in filthy places; therefore they said a Torah scholar should not stand in filthy places lest he contemplate words of Torah (simple, chapter Mi sheMeto); nevertheless it is permitted to enter a bathhouse even from halacha that is not fixed (Terumat HaDeshen siman 61); and we are not concerned he will contemplate — in a bathhouse one guards himself from Torah thoughts (the rabbi's reasoning, not like Terumat HaDeshen's conclusion).}
