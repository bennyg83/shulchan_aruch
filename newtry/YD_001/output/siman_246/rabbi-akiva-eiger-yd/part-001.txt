(סימן רמ"ו סעיף א' בהג"ה) <b>תא"ו</b> נתיב ב' מש"ס דסוטה. וכתב שם ומסתברא שהעוסק אבד שכרו שכבר ביטל חלקו כ"כ המפרשים עכ"ל וע' בתשובת מהר"ם אלשאקר (סי' ק"א) בשם רבינו האי גאון וע' בס' אש דת להרב מו"ה חיים אלפאנדרי (פ' וילך):
**** ENGLISH ****
(A.C.A. in the Bible) is a route in the Qur'an. And he wrote there and wrote that the Musk had already lost his salary that he had already abolished his portion of the so-called “Gentlemen” and “the Lord of the Elders” in the name of his “Gentlemen” and Hashem’s people, in the name of “The Lord of Hashem.”

**** ENGLISH ****(Shach s.k. 27) That the world is accustomed to fast — in Orach Chayyim (siman 568) he deduced from this that because of the fast it is forbidden; and if so, on the night preceding the fast it is permitted to eat at a siyum feast; but not at a feast of circumcision — see there.
