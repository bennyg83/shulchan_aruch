Milk in meat — see Shach that milk and meat are not equal in taste, and so Peri Chadash; even regarding species in its species it is called meat and they are equal in name, nevertheless regarding sixty for taste they disagree. In our sugya Chullin 96 it appears from the Gemara and Tosafot the opposite — milk and meat are not equal in name, but the taste of meat fat and milk are equal; Tosafot in chapter 3 daf 96 s.v. she'ani challenged from Rabbah who forbade a kid roasted in its milk even from the tip of its ear, and established it is different because milk wafts; they challenged: gid fat also wafts — hence all hold they are equal in taste — otherwise what is the difficulty? One may say wafting — therefore according to Shulchan Aruch below 105, even with sixty removal is needed lest perhaps it does not waft throughout — see Chullin 100 where Tosafot had some doubt in milk wafting; we forbid only from doubt, no worse than nishpach, and this is in Torah issur for stringency; but in gid fat which is rabbinic and only holy Israel practiced issur — if nishpach, nevertheless permitted, and light issur doubt is for leniency — hence one must say they hold milk in meat is species in its species; if we doubt, behold species in its species even in Torah issur is for leniency as in nishpach — hence certainly it wafts and the challenge is valid; but at first glance there is contradiction — why does the Gemara challenge from Rav Huna who forbids a kid roasted in milk, and R"Y says peel and eat — lest that milk wafting is not certain; R"Y holds species in its species is nullified — rabbinic doubt that milk in meat is species in its species and permitted; but Rav Huna, disciple of Rav, holds like the sugya in Shabbat — species in its species is not nullified — Torah doubt and forbidden; the first is not difficult lest there was sixty and R"H forbids who holds species in its species is not nullified — the Gemara knows a stam kid not lean is not sixty against its milk; but the latter is difficult — there must be milk and meat unequal in name — this is called milk and this meat, but their taste in meat is equal as Shach wrote meat fat; if so Tosafot are correct and the Gemara's challenge is valid — R"H holds Torah issur, for species in its species we do not follow name — if so R"H forbids species in its species because it is called not its species; but above Tosafot hold for taste it is in its species — if we doubt whether it wafts it is permitted since it depends on taste and taste is species in its species — investigate. One may resolve: initially the Gemara challenges Shmuel's matter from R"H who forbids a roasted kid; afterward when they answered milk wafts, it challenges R"Y who permitted in such a case — why call it a challenge to Shmuel since R"Y agrees with me — the Gemara barely continues; accordingly the Gemara did not wish to challenge R"Y lest milk and meat are one species even in name per Raavad, Rashba, Ran — nafka mina as Peleti wrote: R"Y who holds species in its species permitted was lenient and R"H on species in its species was stringent; but now that they answered milk wafts and gid fat is lean as Tosafot hold, the Gemara holds since in any case it is milk only that does not waft so much as to spread throughout all noten taam, nevertheless something spreads and cannot escape — hence difficult to Shmuel who holds species in its species in the slightest; hence milk and fat for name are called not its species — hence R"H challenges R"Y — investigate. However per this reasoning Tosafot's challenge is difficult — gid fat also wafts — explain gid fat is rabbinic and all this is unnecessary; for us one must say truly what we say wafting does not waft in noten taam only in the slightest; only Tosafot's difficulty: something forbidden in kedei netilah returns and forbids — all in their place — no need Tosafot's explanation that forbidden etc., since something of issur goes with it and milk wafting something goes with it and therefore forbidden; but for Shmuel no difficulty since Shmuel does not hold piece-nevelah and peel-return-forbid does not apply — if so no difficulty — requires study; see above siman 92; one must say on daf 97 side 2 Ravina supports from that statement — they taught only if cooked, etc.; if so difficult to Ravina who certainly holds chein nafsho — hence l'ita; Tosafot are necessary that all hold gid fat does not waft — investigate.
