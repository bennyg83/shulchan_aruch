**** ENGLISH ****We have a non-Jew taste it. and in Bedek HaBayit Ra'ah challenged this that mesiach lefi tumo is believed only for testimony of a woman which is only revealing a matter in passing, etc.; and this difficulty Rashba also challenged in Terumat HaDeshen siman 79 on Rashba and resolved that Rashba holds taste is like substance is not Torah law and in rabbinic matters mesiach lefi tumo helps; and proof that an expert helps even when not mesiach lefi tumo because he does not ruin his presumption; but where testimony is required certainly such a thing does not help — until here in prohibitions one needs only proof — thus I resolve Rashba and Rosh's view; but for halachah Tur and Shulchan Aruch already ruled in siman 116 regarding firstborn that mesiach lefi tumo does not help, and if so it does not help anywhere there is Torah prohibition. In Tur it is written and likewise wrote "some say" Rosh that one needs, etc. — that he did not write stam; and likewise wrote "some say" Rosh — Beit Yosef explained meaning there is no decision in his words whether one needs specifically an expert. Requires study — behold Rosh wrote expert gentile baker implies we require specifically an expert, for otherwise why write this, for Rosh's way is not to write except what emerges for law; therefore it appears the opposite — certainly Rosh holds like "some say" brought before that one needs specifically an expert. <b>אם נתערב מין במינו. </b>המסקנא בפוסקים דמין שנתערב במינו מן התורה בטל ברוב בעלמא שנאמר אחרי רבים להטות אלא דרבנן אצרכו בה ששים על כן במקום שיש ספק כגון זה שנשפך אזלינן לקולא ואמרי' שהיה ששים אבל במין באינו מינו צריך ששים מן התורה כיון שמרגישין בהיתר טעם האיסור ע"כ במקום ספק אזלינן לחומרא:
**** ENGLISH ****If its kind became mixed. The conclusion of the poskim: its kind mixed in its kind is nullified in the majority by Torah law, as it says "follow the majority"; but the Sages required sixty in it; therefore where there is doubt such as when spilled we go to leniency and say there was sixty; but not its kind in its kind requires sixty by Torah law since they sense forbidden taste in permitted food — therefore in a case of doubt we go to stringency.
