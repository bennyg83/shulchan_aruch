**** ENGLISH ****
When one visits the sick, which sick people one visits, and how one prays for him. In it are 10 seifim. It is a mitzvah to visit the sick; relatives and friends enter immediately and distant ones after three days; and if the illness struck suddenly, both enter immediately. (Tur, abbreviated from Sefer HaTerumah for Ramban).

**** ENGLISH ****
One does not visit the sick in the first three hours of the day, because every sick person feels his illness lighten in the morning and he will not be concerned to ask mercy for him; nor in the last three hours of the day, for then his illness weighs on him and he will despair of asking mercy for him (and whoever visits and does not ask mercy for him has not fulfilled the mitzvah) (Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban).
