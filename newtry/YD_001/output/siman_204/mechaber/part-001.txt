**** ENGLISH ****
The law of one who associates in a vow; it contains 4 se’ifim. The essence of a vow stated in the Torah is that one associates with the vowed object — such as when one says, “This loaf is upon me like a korban,” or when he said, “This loaf is forbidden upon me,” or when he said, “This loaf is upon me as a prohibition.” And likewise, if he forbade upon himself one loaf, and hung it on the vowed object and said regarding another, “Let it be like this,” it is forbidden — and even up to 100, all are forbidden. Or if he vowed regarding one day to fast on it, or not to eat meat on it, and said regarding another day, “Let it be like this,” it is forbidden. (There are disputes regarding formulations in the matter of vows, and the Rav did not write them; see inside.)

(הגהות מרדכי דשבועות בשם מוהר"ם) <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="(°)" data-order="6"></i>מביאה ב"י בסי' רל"ז</small>:
**** ENGLISH ****
It is not specifically associating with a korban; rather, the same applies to anything holy with sanctity of the mouth — such as when one says, “This loaf is like a ewe,” “like rams,” “like the altar,” “like the Sanctuary,” “like Jerusalem,” or “like one of all the utensils of the altar” — this is a vow. {Rama: If he vowed “like the table in the Temple,” it is a vow; but if he said “like a table” without specification, and did not say “like the holy table,” this is nothing. Nevertheless, with an am ha’aretz one should release it so that he not be lax with vows (Hagahos Mordechai to Shevuos in the name of Maharam; brought in Beit Yosef, siman 237).}

<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="6"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="7"></i>שמע חבירו ואמר אני כמותך בתוך כדי דבור הרי זה אסור במה שנאסר בו חבירו שמע הג' זה שאמר ואני ואמר ואני אפי' היו ק' וכל אחד אומר ואני בתוך כדי דיבורו של חבירו הרי כולם אסורים:
**** ENGLISH ****
If one heard his fellow and said, “I am like you,” within the time of speech — he is forbidden in what his fellow forbade. If a third heard this one who said “and I,” and said “and I” — even if there were 100, and each one says “and I” within the time of speech of his fellow — all are forbidden.
