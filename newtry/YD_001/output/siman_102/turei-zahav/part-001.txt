<b>ואם נתערבה בשאינה מינה כו'. </b>הטעם כתבתי לעיל סי' ק"א סעיף ו' בשם הת"ח לפי שאין ההיתר אח"כ נקרא על האיסור אלא על התערובת שנאסר והוא אינו מינו של האיסור ואין טעם זה מספיק כל כך. ותמיהני למה לא כתב דברי הר"ן בזה פרק הנודר מן המבושל (נדרים דף נ"ב) וזה תורף תירוצו דחזינן לרבי יהודא דס"ל מין במינו באיסור והיתר לא בטיל ויליף לה מדכתיב ולקח מדם הפר ומדם השעיר שאע"פ שדם הפר מרובה משל שעיר קרי ליה דם השעיר אלמא לא בטיל משום דמין במינו הוא לפי שכל דבר שהוא דומה לחבירו אינו מחלישו אדרבה מחזקו ורבנן לא ילפי מזה לאיסור והיתר דבשלמא דם פר ושעיר שניהם כשרים לזריקה משא"כ באיסור והיתר אע"פ שהם מין א' מ"מ חלוקים הם שזה אסור וזה מותר ע"כ ה"ל כאינו מינו ומש"ה אמרינן ביש לו מתירין הוה דומיא דדם פר ושעיר דהא אין כאן חילוק בין איסור להיתר שהרי גם האיסור סופו להיות מותר וזהו במין במינו כההיא דדם פר ושעיר משא"כ במין באינו מינו אע"פ שמצד איסור יש להם שיווי בדבר שיל"מ מ"מ יש ביניהם חילוק גדול במה שזה מין אחר ע"כ הם בטלים זה בזה. זהו כוונתו שם אע"פ שאין זה לשונו והוא נכון מאד:
**** ENGLISH ****
And if it was mixed not in its kind, etc. The reason I wrote above siman 101 seif 6 in the name of Torat Chatat: because the permission afterward is not called on the prohibition but on the mixture that was forbidden, and it is not the kind of the prohibition — and this reason is not sufficient enough. And I wonder why he did not write Ran's words in this chapter of one who vows from cooked food (Nedarim daf 52) — and this is the essence of his answer: we see according to Rabbi Yehudah who holds in its kind regarding prohibition and permission it is not nullified, and he derives this from "and take from the blood of the bull and from the blood of the goat" — even though the bull's blood is more than the goat's blood, it is called the goat's blood — thus it is not nullified because it is in its kind, for everything similar to its fellow does not weaken it, on the contrary it strengthens it; and the Sages do not derive this for prohibition and permission — for certainly bull and goat blood are both fit for sprinkling, unlike prohibition and permission — although they are one kind, nevertheless they differ, for this is forbidden and this permitted — therefore it is as not its kind; and therefore we say regarding something that has a permitted aspect it is comparable to bull and goat blood, for here there is no distinction between prohibition and permission, for the prohibition too will ultimately be permitted — and this is in its kind like bull and goat blood; unlike in its kind versus not its kind — although from the side of prohibition they are equal in something that has a permitted aspect, nevertheless there is a great distinction in that this is another kind — therefore they nullify one another. This is his intent there, even though it is not his language — and it is very correct.

<b>מיהו אם לבנו כו'. </b>לא זו אף זו נקיט דלא זו לבנו בו מאכל דעביד לחזותא וטעמא אלא אפי' נתנו בקדירה לתקן כו' דמקרי ג"כ מין במינו דכל דבר שצריך זה לזה כגון מים ומלח בעיסה הוה מין במינו כ"כ התוס' סוף פרק משילין ומ"ש רמ"א שמלאוהו בתרנגולת הוא דלהוי ממשו של איסור לאפוקי ממ"ש לקמן דטעמא לא מקרי דשיל"מ. ומזה למד מהרא"י בכל דבר שנותנין לתקן הקדירה הוה כמינו עם הקדירה ואם אחד מהם דבר שיש לו מתירין אוסר התערובת עד אלף ואין לטעות מכח זה לענין מה דאמרינן בהרבה דוכתי חד בתרי בטיל מן התורה במינו ובאינו מינו צריך ס' מן התורה ולומר דגם שם הוה מין במינו במידי דמתקן הקדירה כמו כאן דשם תלוי בטעם כיון שנ"ט באינו מינו והאי נ"ט בקדירה ממילא יש לו שם חומרא דאינו מינו:
**** ENGLISH ****
However, if one whitened it, etc. He cited both: not only whitening food for appearance and taste, but even placing in a pot to fix — for that is also called its kind; everything one needs the other, like water and salt in dough, is its kind — so Tosafot end of chapter Meshilin; and what the Rama wrote that one filled it with chicken is because the substance of the prohibition is present — unlike what he wrote below that taste is not called something that has a permitted aspect. From this Maharai learned that everything placed to fix the pot is as its kind with the pot; and if one of them is something that has a permitted aspect it forbids the mixture up to a thousand; and one should not err from this regarding what we say in many places one against two is nullified from the Torah in its kind and in not its kind requires sixty from the Torah — to say there too it is its kind in what fixes the pot as here; for there the law depends on taste, since taste in not its kind, and this taste in the pot automatically has stringent name as not its kind.

**** ENGLISH ****
A vessel that became forbidden, etc. It is puzzling why Rashba needed this reason that outlay is required that the Rama mentioned afterward — for the forbidden item, which is the vessel, cannot forbid more than the forbidding agent; and the forbidding agent absorbed in it would not have the law of something that has a permitted aspect, for that prohibition has no permission — if so the forbidden should not be stricter than the forbidding agent; and we find this reasoning many times in Tosafot regarding salt absorbed from blood that the Tur brings end of siman 105; and one can say that since it has a permitted aspect we should not heed any reasoning for leniency, for we may not rely on any leniency since it has permission anyway; and I already wrote in siman 69 seif 14 to learn another law from this regarding meat not salted; and this is what the Rama hinted here in what he wrote "and similarly all such cases," and Rashal Yoreh Deah siman 88 wrote another reason on the vessel that it is not something that has a permitted aspect since permission does not come automatically through time; nevertheless it appears that even though permitted to use all these vessels that were mixed, as Rashba wrote and Beit Yosef brought end of siman 122 — nevertheless one should not leave them a long time unless he kasher all from doubt, since the prohibition was nullified in the majority — it has the law of dry nullified in dry majority explained in siman 110, forbidden to eat them all together; and there is concern that in much time the mixture will be forgotten and one will come to cook a dish cooked in all the mixture together and eat them and come to prohibition — therefore he should kasher all from doubt, in my humble opinion; and see what is written in siman 122 on this law.

Only on account of blood absorbed in it. This is written in Torat Chatat in the name of Terumat HaDeshen siman 170; Beit Yosef brought it in siman 69; and in truth he did not write this reason there to permit through absorption, but that it is not something that has a permitted aspect because permission is not for what was forbidden — namely cooking — and he concluded there that even a piece worthy of honor is nullified since here there is only absorbed prohibition — thus this reasoning of absorbed prohibition only serves to nullify a piece worthy of honor, not to nullify something that has a permitted aspect; for if so, why did Terumat HaDeshen write there for Rif it is something that has a permitted aspect since it has permission for roasting — even for Rif it is permitted because it was only absorbed prohibition? And further, why did the Rama need here a reason on vessels because of outlay and not say the reason was only absorbed prohibition? It appears the Rama wrote these matters according to Orach Chayim general rule 25 rule 19: "and a vessel forbidden through absorption of prohibition mixed with others is nullified in the majority, and even though one can kasher them it is not called something that has a permitted aspect to forbid all until kashered, since its prohibition is not from itself — no less than a whole beriah forbidden through absorption, and Beit Yosef explains in siman 122 in the name of Rashba because outlay is required," etc. — end of his words. Behold before you on the vessel law two reasons; therefore Rama first wrote on vessels Rashba's reason to learn a general rule that it is not called something that has a permitted aspect where outlay is required anywhere; afterward he wrote on an unsalted piece that this reason does not apply, and wrote another reason of Orach Chayim — on account of absorption; and although Torat Chatat wrote this in the name of Terumat HaDeshen siman 170 — this is because his Mishnah overpowered him and he erred in appearance of place, but in the substance of the law he wrote well according to Orach Chayim; and one may ask: what is different from a drop of wine forbidden by vow that fell in a barrel — that is something that has a permitted aspect, for there it was not absorption but mixture and the substance of the prohibition is there, and initially it was a separate entity — therefore the mixture does not permit it since it was something that has a permitted aspect; unlike this piece — but we are puzzled what reasoning exists that it should not be something that has a permitted aspect regarding absorbed prohibition: since in any event there is permission for this piece, they did not lighten to nullify it in the majority like everything that has a permitted aspect; granted regarding worthy of honor one can say they were stringent only if the prohibition is important, but not if the important piece is permitted and only has forbidden absorption — for nevertheless this piece has no prohibition from itself and prohibition does not apply on account of importance — thus prohibition alone and importance alone; but regarding something that has a permitted aspect it is primary: since it has permission without nullification we do not follow nullification — if so, everything that has permission for its prohibition from itself or for what is absorbed in it is equal; and one should not challenge: if so, it is also difficult on what he wrote that if there is no substance only taste it is not something that has a permitted aspect — for there it deals where one took the body of the prohibition that was not mixed in substance, only taste has no power to forbid, for taste was not sensed in its kind and it is as if the prohibition did not fall there at all — unlike here in an unsalted piece where prohibition is clear before us and even from the Torah requires nullification in majority — properly the Sages decreed nullification in majority does not help since it has permission anyway; and shortly we will mention more from this; and so Rashal on Torat Chatat's words above that regarding something that has a permitted aspect, that it is absorbed prohibition does not help. And further I am puzzled from the Mishnah in Bikkurim chapter 2 that said their growth is forbidden from eating in Jerusalem, etc. — meaning if bikkurim mixed in any amount, all is forbidden to eat in Jerusalem as ordinary produce, since it has permission to eat there as bikkurim; and if he planted the bikkurim in Jerusalem the growth is forbidden on account of something that has a permitted aspect — so Rambam explained this Mishnah, not as Rosh wrote to reject this explanation through Yerushalmi; and I wrote elsewhere it is not difficult on Rambam; and Bartenuro explained this planting refers to the mixture — even so the growth is forbidden on account of something that has a permitted aspect. If so we see even in this prohibition that is at least preferable to absorption in those growths — for even in growth of terumah that we learned chapter 9 of Terumot that is forbidden on account of a decree lest one keep impure terumah until planting time, and in bikkurim they said there on their growth that is ordinary — nevertheless we say growth of bikkurim mixture in Jerusalem, since there they have permission to eat them as bikkurim, we are stringent even in growth of bikkurim mixture — a fortiori we should be stringent on this unsalted piece that is forbidden by law to forbid the mixture on account of something that has a permitted aspect; and from this too the case we mentioned shortly is not difficult — if there is only taste of prohibition it is not something that has a permitted aspect, since one removed the body of the prohibition and only taste remains and it is in its kind — taste is not sensed at all initially and from the Torah no nullification is needed, for they did not say taste is like substance d'oraisa except in not its kind; and although from Tur Orach Chayim siman 513 where this law is in the name of Rabbeinu Tam it seems it deals with nosen taam — namely placing in food like whitening an egg in food — Beit Yosef already wrote this is not necessary; and from what I wrote the opposite is necessary — it deals only where kind mixed literally in kind and there is no taste at all; but here in bikkurim initially the body of bikkurim was among the mixture, and although nullified later in the ground when growth came from it — nevertheless permission is through nullification in this we say something that has a permitted aspect — no nullification helps; a fortiori in an unsalted piece that has full prohibition that you must nullify — this does not help for something that has a permitted aspect. From all this it appears clear to me: every prohibition in the world whose main permission is through nullification — permission of something that has a permitted aspect does not apply.
