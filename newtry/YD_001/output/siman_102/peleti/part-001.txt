And if it was mixed, etc. In Orach Chayim siman 513 seif 1-2 Shulchan Aruch wrote on an egg laid on Yom Tov: if mixed in a thousand all are forbidden; if doubt whether laid on Yom Tov or weekday — forbidden; Magen Avraham seif 5 wrote Shulchan Aruch reversed the order as the Tur wrote — there doubt whether laid, etc., and if mixed in a thousand that law also applies; but Mechaber switched to teach that regarding doubt, if mixed it is permitted as a double doubt — requires investigation; for in Yoreh Deah siman 102 Shulchan Aruch ruled to forbid even if the doubt was mixed; and possibly there it deals with Torah prohibition and Yom Tov after Shabbat, and in Orach Chayim with rabbinic prohibition and ordinary Yom Tov — thus one must understand: in Beitzah Gemara on that baraita "an egg laid on Yom Tov is forbidden," etc., "its doubt is forbidden; if mixed even in a thousand all are forbidden" — according to one who says egg laid on Yom Tov is forbidden rabbinically, its doubt is rabbinic and lenient; Gemara answers the doubt comes from doubtful tereifah — if so, why mention something that has a permitted aspect to nullify in majority? It answers: this tanna is of litra of pressed figs and holds everything customarily counted is not nullified; Rav Ashi said something that has a permitted aspect is not nullified even in rabbinic doubt — end. One should question Mechaber in contradiction: if Torah prohibition, double doubt forbidden because doubt in item and in mixture — if he deals with egg laid on Yom Tov per tanna of litra of figs, how teach "its doubt is forbidden; if mixed in a thousand forbidden" — in rabbinic we do not say double doubt? If we say as Rabbeinu Tam that "if mixed" refers to certainty not doubt — whence stringency in Torah doubt in item and mixture, for all R' Yehudah's stringencies are from that proof "if mixed in a thousand"? If "if mixed" refers to certainty per Rabbeinu Tam, there is no proof — difficult either way. One can say: if "its doubt is forbidden" deals with doubtful tereifah, why need "if mixed in a thousand" on certain tereifah — who mentions certain tereifah in the baraita teaching egg laid on Yom Tov forbidden, doubt meaning doubtful tereifah? Therefore above refers to doubtful tereifah forbidden if mixed in a thousand — but that is double doubt; one must say one doubt in item and one in mixture as R' Yehudah wrote; but for the answer that doubt means doubt of laid on Yom Tov — "if mixed in a thousand" refers to the beginning "egg laid on Yom Tov" which is certain, and that if mixed is forbidden; but in doubt it is not forbidden, for double doubt, and in rabbinic we do not forbid even one doubt in item and one in mixture. More reasonable: certainly per Rabbeinu Tam even one doubt in item and one in mixture is double doubt even for Torah, as Gemara regarding tereifah egg; R' Yehudah who forbids holds "if mixed" refers to doubt even in rabbinic — per his Gemara reasoning; only Shulchan Aruch was uncertain whether law is Rabbeinu Tam or R' Yehudah — therefore in Torah prohibition he was stringent like R' Yehudah forbidding double doubt; and so his words here; but in rabbinic prohibition he was lenient relying on Rabbeinu Tam who is lenient even for Torah — and so Orach Chayim; but Gemara passages explain whether "if mixed" refers to certainty for egg laid on Yom Tov or tereifah egg, or doubt applies to both — investigate. In Pilpul HaTalmidim I resolved Shulchan Aruch's words: he certainly holds like R' Yehudah that "if mixed" refers to doubt not certainty; but he was puzzled: since he did not know from "something that has a permitted aspect even in rabbinic not nullified" — if so, when challenging from "its doubt forbidden," challenge from "if mixed forbidden" — why not rabbinic doubt lenient? See Maggid Mishneh who wrote explicitly they depend on each other. Further: why was he puzzled why egg laid on Yom Tov forbidden — he holds doubtful prepared forbidden? Answer that muktzeh is like Torah is strained; doubtful fallen fruit is also doubtful prepared, same decree for egg — why didn't R' Nissim challenge? Reason muktzeh like Torah only per R' Nissim — otherwise challenge removed from Mishnah distinguishing chicken; therefore he knew something that has a permitted aspect: even in rabbinic we say doubt stringent, only we do not say two doubts; if this doubt mixed with others and law would nullify, it is not nullified because something that has a permitted aspect twice we do not say "until you eat in prohibition" on beginning of its doubt and again on its mixture — we do not say this; if so no challenge from doubtful prepared — he knew something that has a permitted aspect; likewise from "if mixed" one need not say something that has a permitted aspect; but difficult: it teaches "its doubt forbidden" — why, rabbinic doubt lenient? Cannot say because something that has a permitted aspect — if so why "if mixed forbidden" — we do not say two doubts; unlike if egg forbidden for preparation of Rav — then doubt is by law not because something that has a permitted aspect — one can say even in a thousand nullified because something that has a permitted aspect once we say; Gemara answers we come to doubtful tereifah, etc., concludes tanna of litra of figs holds egg is counted item not nullified; Rav Ashi: now that we came here, one can say Yom Tov doubt weekday doubt, something that has a permitted aspect, even rabbinic doubt forbidden; no challenge: if so nullify in a thousand — we do not say two doubts? No — not nullified is not from something that has a permitted aspect but from counted item — something that has a permitted aspect properly forbids its doubt. Possible this is Sefer HaTerumah's reason ruling like tanna of litra of figs — per Rav Ashi's ending: Orach Chayim permitted not because double doubt — he ruled Yoreh Deah siman 110 no double doubt — only they did not forbid from something that has a permitted aspect. Now Shulchan Aruch's words are correct in Yoreh Deah: Torah doubt something that has a permitted aspect like chadash — Magen Avraham ruled its doubt and mixed in a thousand forbidden; to forbid its doubt no need something that has a permitted aspect — Torah doubt stringent — thus something that has a permitted aspect helps forbid mixture even in a thousand. Unlike egg rabbinic; for us egg is not counted item — if you forbid its doubt because something that has a permitted aspect, cannot forbid mixture from two doubts. Bach and Peri Chadash brought Mordechai's challenge: how did Gemara Menachot say for one who holds chadash rabbinic, eating on 17 Nisan is rabbinic doubt — behold something that has a permitted aspect? Ri answered chadash returns to prohibition next year, not something that has a permitted aspect; Peri Chadash challenged: Yerushalmi and many places chadash is something that has a permitted aspect; grain permitted by omer never returns to this prohibition — unlike chametz on Pesach when Pesach returns. Well said until puzzling how hidden from Ri and Mordechai — egg laid on Yom Tov should be something that has a permitted aspect next Yom Tov when another egg laid. Therefore Ri seems to hold like Yerushalmi Tosafot Rosh brought: from entering Israel all grain even in ancient cisterns became chadash; for us chadash rabbinic, mitzvah ceased when Temple destroyed, chadash returns — all grain forbidden, day of waving in exile does not help like at entry; chadash we hold rabbinic — therefore reason all day forbidden until Temple rebuilt — returns to prohibition, not certainly something that has a permitted aspect; see Orach Chayim: some hold doubt is something that has a permitted aspect perhaps Eliyahu comes — they wrote spoiled food, all the more say so to be lenient — that was his intent.
