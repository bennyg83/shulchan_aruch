**** ENGLISH ****Laws of double safek. (a) It is not called double safek. Even if the first safek was not known until after the mixture — such as when several eggs became mixed together and afterward it became known there was a safek of tereifah in the hen, and it became known that one egg is from her that she laid after the safek of tereifah arose — and so in all similar cases; even though no safek of issur was known until after the mixture, and thus both safeks became known together as one — nevertheless, since the first safek is forbidden from the Torah, it is not double safek. So Darkei Moshe raised in siman 57 and Turei Chayim klal 43 din 7; and Beit Yosef challenged in siman 57 in the name of Masat Binyamin that one permits even when the first safek was not known before the mixture, on account of double safek — and that is what he concludes here in the hagahah: "but if there were two safeks whether there was issur here at all and both safeks became known together," etc. — thus at the beginning, even if both safeks became known together, it is not double safek; and so is implied from the poskim, unlike responsum Masat Binyamin siman 37 that challenged the Rav on this; and the foundation of his challenge is that from where the Rav brought this law from Issur VeHeter klal 26 it does not appear so — rather it is explained in Hagahot Issur VeHeter thus; and we should rely more on the Rav, author of Issur VeHeter, for he was a great established rabbi, than on the words of the hagahah — end of his words; and this is not so, for in Issur VeHeter itself it is implied many times that even if the issur was not known it is forbidden, for he wrote in his conclusion that the two safeks are not one matter, for the first safek was in the substance itself and is forbidden from the Torah — see there and investigate.
