And Mahariik wrote root 32: nevertheless there is no prohibition of touch of a non-Jew in grapes in a vat, even though it is forbidden to tread according to Rabbenu Tam — also in treading it is explained in Tosafot that the ancients did not heed this stringency of Rabbenu Tam — see there; and it appears the same in a closed vat; but in an open vat it is obvious that it is forbidden by touch even in grapes; and Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Semag: whenever there is a basket in the vat and clear wine enters there, it is called drawing and is forbidden even if a non-Jew touched the grapes.
