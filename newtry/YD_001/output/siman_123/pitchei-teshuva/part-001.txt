**** HEBREW ****
<b>שמרים של עובדי כוכבים. </b>עיין בספר לבושי שרד שכתב דה"ה חרצנים של עובדי כוכבים וע"ש היטב:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>That he drew it off.</b> Tiferet LeMoshe distinguishes grape skins: once thoroughly dried, no wine moisture remains, and after burning them to ash there is certainly none. Accordingly, charcoal made from grape skins should also be permitted. Dagul MeRevavah agrees. Chatam Sofer objects from Avodah Zarah and discusses oil produced from almonds grown among forbidden grape refuse, concluding that transformation from wine into oil may permit it, especially where no wine flavor is detectable.

Zikhron Yosef concludes that after the fact, in great loss or pressing circumstances, one may rely on them.

Because of the very great loss, he ultimately permits through several combined grounds.

<b>להטפיח. </b>עבה"ט והוא מדברי הש"ך ועי' בתשובת זכרון יוסף שם שהאריך לבאר מהיכן יצא לו דבר זה:
**** ENGLISH ****

Zikhron Yosef relied on this in a pressing situation involving non-Jews handling grapes on a sloped press. He nevertheless condemns the practice of sweeping the skins into one pile after the first pressing, because clear wine then collects and the subsequent handling may forbid it. He develops several additional grounds for leniency where only a small uncertain quantity of wine is involved.
