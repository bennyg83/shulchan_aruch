**** HEBREW ****
<b>דמזיק לבריה. </b>זה הטעם דחוק ולי נראה טעם אחר יותר מתקבל משום דשחיטה היא בידו ואינו מצווה לשחוט דהא יכול להיות לעולם בלא בשר או שישחוט אחר אבל בכסוי החיוב מוטל על כל אדם שרואה הדם לכסות כדלקמן סעיף ח':
**** ENGLISH ****
Recites shehecheyanu, etc. — Hagahot Minhagim that Darkei Moshe brought: covering is a mitzvah, as we say: in reward that Avraham said "and I am dust and ashes" his children merited two mitzvot — and one blesses shehecheyanu like tzitzit and sukkah, as explained in Hagahot Shechitot in the name of R' Yedidya of Shapiro — end of his words. And study is needed: regarding tzitzit we hold one does not bless shehecheyanu unless he acquired a new garment, because he acquired new utensils — as explained in Orach Chaim siman 223; but when making tzitzit on an old garment he does not bless shehecheyanu, as Ba'al HaItur, Mahari Abuhav, and Darkei Moshe in Orach Chaim siman 22 wrote; and so Shulchan Aruch and Levush there; and so appears in Tosafot, chapter Lulav and Aravah (Sukkah daf 46a), s.v. ha'oseh sukkah: the reason we do not bless shehecheyanu on tzitzit and tefillin is because shehecheyanu was enacted only for a mitzvah that has joy. And one can say regarding covering: since in reward for "I am dust and ashes" it is considered joy — on the contrary we say in chapter 25 (end of daf 88): Rava said in reward that Avraham said "and I am dust and ashes" his children merited two mitzvot — ashes of the red heifer and ashes of sotah; let us also count earth of kisuy hadam — there is no benefit in the mitzvah; and Rava said in reward "if from a shoelace to a sandal strap" they merited two mitzvot — tekhelet thread and tefillin strap — end; and so appears from Ran there: the reason we do not bless shehecheyanu on tefillin is because it does not come from time to time; and so Rashba in responsum 126. If so, all the more regarding covering. And it also appears: even for Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilchot Berachot, who blesses shehecheyanu on tzitzit and tefillin — it is because he wrote there: since they are mitzvot that are an acquisition for him — unlike covering. And I found in Rokeach siman 371: he wrote in the name of Rivash of Shapiro: any mitzvah incumbent on a person to do and he did not do it and is now beginning l'chatchila must bless shehecheyanu; and he brought proof from what we say in Menachot (daf 75): one standing and offering menachot in Jerusalem says shehecheyanu — I have become fit for service of the time — end; and perhaps on this they relied to bless on covering. But study is needed: if so, why do we not bless shehecheyanu on tzitzit, tefillin, and other mitzvot one begins? And likewise we do not bless shehecheyanu on kiddushin of a woman or her marriage, as Maharik wrote, shoresh 128; and likewise on mezuzah, as below siman 289. Rather, one must hold: even in a mitzvah one begins, one does not bless shehecheyanu unless its time is fixed — like Tosafot, Ran, and Rashba above; and our commentators explain that mishnah of offering menachot as Tosafot and Rashba in his chiddushim, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin: there were twenty-four watches and they did not renew more than twice a year; since they have a fixed time, one blesses shehecheyanu — end; if not so, not; and if so, the same regarding covering; and study is needed.

<b>השוחט כו'. </b>משמע דאתא לאשמועינן דאע"פ שלא נתקיים בהן מצות כסוי מותרים וכן משמע בתה"א שער ה' וכן משמע בעט"ז וב"ח אבל במשנה פרק השוחט (חולין דף ל"ג ע"א) שממנו מקור דין זה איתא השוחט בהמה חיה ועוף ולא יצא מהם דם מותרין ומדנקט בהמה משמע דלאו מכסוי מיירי וכ"כ הרמב"ם פ"ד מה"ש דין י"ג בדיני מסוכנת השוחט בהמה חיה ועוף ולא יצא מהן דם הרי אלו מותרין ואין אומרים שמא מתים היו עכ"ל וכן פירש בספר תורת חיים ובמשמרת הבית דף כ' פירש הרשב"א דאתא לאשמועינן דאע"ג דלא יצא מהן דם מותרין דדם האיברים שלא פירש מותר כו' וע"ל ס"ס כ"ב:
**** ENGLISH ****
The Sages enacted for him, etc. — Beit Yosef rejected these words: if so, the tanna should have taught this enactment before "burn his garment," for there is also bal tashchit; and further, since he does not cover with earth, how can he bless on it? Therefore it appears these words have no substance — end. And Rav in Darkei Moshe answered the first difficulty: the tanna deals when the bird equals the gold or garment he burns, and the Geonim deal when it does not equal; therefore it is impossible otherwise, and they made this enactment. And he did not answer the second: since when he absorbs blood in the garment it is the beginning of covering, and he can bless even though he does not complete covering until later — like checking chametz where we bless on bi'ur chametz although we do not burn until tomorrow (as explained in Orach Chaim siman 432); and the same here, etc.; and afterward I found in Or Zaru'a that he brought the Geonim's words and expanded on reasons and wrote one may rely on them — end Darkei Moshe; and so I found in Raavan who brought their words for halakhic ruling; and Maharshal, chapter 25, siman 17, followed Beit Yosef to reject the Geonim's words; and such rejection returns and appears wrong after it is explicit in great poskim mentioned; and their words are also settled — Maharshal himself brought the Geonim's words anonymously in his Hagahot to shechitot of Mahariu (indeed you will find several times in his book he retracts his first words). And so Bach. However what Darkei Moshe compared this law to checking chametz — I do not hold so: there the reason is checking is the beginning of bi'ur, and bi'ur is impossible without checking, and checking is tied to bi'ur — unlike here. It appears to me to add also the reason Bach wrote: the blessing was enacted only on covering blood, not on earth, as in Tosefta and Yerushalmi and poskim; and although we practice saying "with earth," it is for ease of speech; but here we establish the law, and therefore when he absorbs in his garment one does not say "with earth"; and also in the Geonim's language it says "he blesses on the covering" and no more; and Atzei Erez wrote: it appears one should not bless l'chatchila when absorbing in his garment, nor afterward when laundering the garment; and had he seen Rav's words in Darkei Moshe he would not have written so.]

<b>הנזרעים בהם מצמיחים כו'. </b>ולא מהני כשמשימים בהן צמחים והוא מגדלם אלא בעינן דוקא שהנזרעים בהן מצמיחים ב"י וכ"כ הכל בו בשם הרז"ה ובעל העיטור:
**** ENGLISH ****
The one who slaughters, etc. — It implies he comes to teach: although the mitzvah of covering was not fulfilled with them, they are permitted; and so Terumat HaDeshen, gate 5; and so Atzei Erez and Bach. But in the mishnah, chapter HaShochet (Chullin daf 33a), source of this law: "one who slaughters behemah, chayah, and bird and no blood came from them — permitted"; and since it mentions behemah, it implies it does not deal with covering; and so Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilchot Shechitah, law 13, in laws of dangerously ill: "one who slaughters behemah, chayah, and bird and no blood came from them — behold they are permitted, and we do not say perhaps they were dying"; and so explained in Sefer Torat Chaim and Mishmeret HaBayit, page 20: Rashba explained he comes to teach although no blood came from them they are permitted, for blood of limbs that did not separate is permitted, etc.; and see above siman 22.]

**** HEBREW ****
<b>בעפר לחה וכו'. </b>וכל שכן בטיט כדאיתא בש"ס שם:
**** ENGLISH ****
Therefore one does not cover, etc. — So in all books; and so Or Zaru'a translated; and it implies because moist earth does not grow — and it is not so, for in chapter Elu Overin (Pesachim daf 47) it is explicit it is fit for sowing and grows; and so Rashi there — although Beit Yosef translated Rashi's language "not fit for sowing" — plainly it is a scribal error; and Atzei Erez translated Beit Yosef's language and did not notice the error. But the reason is: we require fine earth fit to be counted, as written "if a man can count the dust of the earth," etc.; and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe brought it; and it appears "therefore" is a scribal error and must read "and so"; and it is easy to understand.
