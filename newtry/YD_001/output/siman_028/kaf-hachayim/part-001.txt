<b>א) [סעיף א'] השוחט חיה או עוף צריך לכסות דמו.  </b>והטעם כתוב במד"ר פ' בראשית לפי שקברו את הבל ונתן להם הקב"ה שכרן ב' ברכות שמברכין עליהם אחד לשחיטה וא' לכסוי הדם יעו"ש ור"ל כשם שהם קברוהו וכסו את דמו כך שכרם שדמם טעון כסוי מדה כנגד מדה.

<b>A) [Seif 1] One who slaughters chayah or bird must cover its blood.</b> The reason is written in Midrash Rabbah, parashat Bereishit: because they buried Abel and the Holy One blessed be He gave them reward — two blessings they bless on them: one for shechitah and one for covering blood — see there; meaning: as they buried him and covered his blood, so their reward that their blood requires covering — measure for measure.

<b>ב) שם. צריך לכסות דמו.  </b>יזדרז לכסות מיד דק"ל חביבה מצוה בשעתה מיהו אם לא כיסה מיד וכיסה אח"כ קיים מ"ע כהלכתה אלא דלא חיבב מצוה בשעתה. ואם לא כיסה מיד וכיסה אחר זמן מועט אכתי מיקרי מצוה בשעתה אלא דאינו בכלל זריזין מקדימים למצות. מחב"ר או' א' זב"צ או' א'.

<b>B) There. Must cover its blood.</b> He should hasten to cover immediately, for we hold a mitzvah at its time is dear; however if he did not cover immediately and covered afterward — he fulfilled the positive commandment as required, only he did not treat the mitzvah at its time dearly. And if he did not cover immediately and covered after a short time — still called mitzvah at its time, only not among those who hasten mitzvot. Machazik Berachah, Orach Chaim part 1, Zivchei Tzedek part 1.

<b>C) There. Must cover its blood.</b> Also with warm ashes permitted to cover — Taz Orach Chaim siman 498 s.k. 15. And Da'at Kedoshim at end of this siman wrote: do not cook blood or salt before covering; therefore proper the ashes not be yad soledes bo. And Gidulei HaKodesh wrote there part 6: on this some Torah students wondered — opposite Taz above; and wrote to settle: specifically lower earth one does not cover with warm ashes, for necessarily he waits a bit before covering above and at time of upper covering blood already cooked by lower ashes; but upper earth permitted with warm ashes — see there; and Beit Din Mani in Likutei Halachot part 157. Zivchei Tzedek part 2 wrote there Zivchei Tzedek: therefore warn slaughterers not to absorb shechitah blood in kiln that has fire and ashes that are yad soledes bo.

<b>J) There. On covering blood with earth.</b> Although we do not find anywhere mentioning the detail in the blessing except generally — it would suffice "on covering blood" alone; nevertheless here different, performing two mitzvot — below and above — learned from "with earth"; must say "with earth" for two mitzvot — Taz s.k. 3. But Rambam and other great ones brought formula "on covering blood"; and Rashbatz in Yavin Shemuah. And Kitzur Halachot wrote law is as Chayei Adam: custom when standing to say "on covering blood" and no more. But Shach s.k. 29 wrote their custom to say "on covering blood with earth"; in his long book brought so from Rokeach, Geonim, Tashbetz, Maharshal — see there; and custom of land of Tzvi and Egypt like Maran and his colleagues to say "on covering blood with earth." Neharah Neharah and Pestia.
