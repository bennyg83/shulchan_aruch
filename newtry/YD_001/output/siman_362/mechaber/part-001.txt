**** ENGLISH ****
To bury in the ground, and if they bury two dead together. In it are 6 seifim. One who places his dead in a coffin and does not bury him in the ground transgresses because of 'you shall surely bury him'; and if they placed him in a coffin and buried him in the ground, he does not transgress; nevertheless it is proper to bury him in the ground itself, even outside the Land.
