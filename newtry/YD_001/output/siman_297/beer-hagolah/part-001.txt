**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Raavad, Ran, and the Tur challenged Rambam on this ruling.</b> Rabbi Yosef Korkos answers that it refers to a non-Jew's field. Since both the sower and the land are non-Jewish, the Sages did not prohibit it, even if he acts as a Jew's agent. The Jew himself, however, may not sow for him, since the case is in Eretz Yisrael and a non-Jew does not acquire the land absolutely.
