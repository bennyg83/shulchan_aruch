**** ENGLISH ****
(297—9) Those born from kilayim—if their mothers were one species, it is permitted to mate them one with another; and if their mothers were two species, it is forbidden to mate them one with another, even though their fathers were another species. And it is forbidden to mate this offspring even on its mother's species. How so? A mule whose mother was a donkey—it is permitted to mate it on a mule whose mother was a donkey; and it is forbidden to mate it even on a donkey. But a mule whose mother was a horse—it is forbidden to mate it on a mule whose mother was a donkey, and likewise all similar cases. Therefore one who wishes to mate a mule on a mule or to drive with two mules examines the signs of ears and tail and voice—if they resemble one another, it is known their mothers were one species and they are permitted.
