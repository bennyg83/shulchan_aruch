**** ENGLISH ****
And some, etc. From what is stated 50b: the rabbis taught regarding a needle, etc.; and since it left the meses' mate — we learn that there too from one side it is treifah; and as stated in the second chapter of Shabbat for the conclusion regarding a needle, etc.; and so they said in chapter 3 of Sukkah; and that which the mishnah says punctured outward — Rashi explained: excluding punctured one into the other, as in the mishnah below, as above; and Tosafot there and there and there were precise about "outward"; and further that there is no treifah when not mafpolash — see there. (Collection) And some forbid, etc. — his words are not correct; Rashi does not disagree except regarding a needle, as stated second chapter of Shabbat and chapter 3 of Sukkah — we are concerned as in esophagus siman 33 seif 9; but without a needle it is not worse than esophagus, as written siman 64.
