On the yeter. Taz wrote: even though we rule in siman 64 that chelev on the yeter is forbidden and tame chelev does not seal — nevertheless since the people of Eretz Yisrael eat chelev on the yeter, it helps the people of Bavel to permit sealing the puncture (but chelev on the arch is forbidden to everyone — therefore it does not seal. It turns out: a peg or needle stuck at the end of the animal's kivah and the kivah was not punctured from side to side, only a hard growth called wart stands on the kivah opposite the place the needle is stuck from inside — he answered in Shulchan Aruch siman 62 that the wart is proof it was punctured and it is crust that rose from a wound, except one may permit because the wart was covered with chelev on the yeter; and although black stain was found in the chelev where it adhered to the wart — nevertheless there was so much thick chelev outside and they saw no black stain outside — therefore it is not korat dam from outside and did not puncture from side to side, end of his words. And so Peri Chadash — even if peg or needle is found stuck in the wall of the kivah, if a bit of skin remains that did not puncture from side to side — one may permit for everyone. And see Tzitz Eliezer siman 49 who leans to be stringent. The chelev. Taz challenged: we find proper sealing — if punctured where the spleen is attached. And one must say: since the membrane on the spleen is forbidden as full chelev as written siman 64 seif 10 — therefore there is no sealing for the kereis, for tame chelev does not seal (a gentile who found a peg in the kereis — if he is believed to him, forbidden; even though the gentile says it was not stuck but only partly in the wall of the kereis inside — the gentile may not know to aim; but if he says he found inside the folds of the meses called heib — one does not forbid. Taz end of siman 50. A chicken whose stomach was swollen — after slaughter when opened, clear liquid squirted from it — kosher; responsum of Bach siman 157; but in an animal such a case Peri Chadash siman 50 s.k. 1 wrote one may not permit except in great loss. And in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 61 he heard from doctors there is danger from eating this, lest one also come to an illness called Wassersucht. A puncture found in the wall opposite the kereis — we are not concerned lest the kereis was punctured — kosher; see there siman 130. And what the Mechaber wrote "for the chelev on it is forbidden" — this is for those who forbid chelev adhering to the kereis under the diaphragm; but the people of Rhineland who practice leniency to eat it as written siman 64 in the hagahah — all the more if the kereis was punctured this chelev seals it (Peri Chadash). And what Rama permitted regarding worms in the kereis — Maharam Tikotin in name of Taz wrote: specifically when found placed inside; but if they go outside and it is not known if they left before slaughter — it appears their law is like a healthy animal in siman 36 — see there seif 5, end of his words:

<b>עובי. </b> הש"ך מביא דעת רש"י והרשב"א ושאר כמה גדולי פוסקים דמתירים כל זמן שלא ניקב מפולש והט"ז כ' דמ"מ אין להקל כיון דקבעו בש"ע להלכה ועכ"פ ברור הוא דגם לענין מפולש אינו אוסר אלא בטפח. וכל זה כשניקב ע"י חולי אבל ע"י מחט או קוץ בכל גווני טריפה כבסי' נ"א ע"ש <small>(דשמא ניקב הבני מעיים)</small> <small>(ט"ז)</small> בהמה שנגחה חברתה בקרן במקום כרס החיצונה וניכר שנעשה המקום גבוה במקום הנגיחה עד שנראה שניקב הבשר שעל כרס החיצונה והעור שלם אם ידוע שניקב בשר החופה כשיעור שנטרפה בו דהיינו אורך טפח א' הוי טריפה מחיים וחלבה אסור כדין חלב טרפה אך אם אינו ידוע אם הנקב ארוך טפח נראה דאין לאסור החלב מחיים מספק. אך אם נשחטה ולא נמצא הקרע כשיעור טפח. מ"מ יש לבדוק באיברים הפנימים שנקיבתן במשהו <small>(בי"ע סי' י"ג ועיין כנה"ג קס"ח. ופר"ח ס"ק ו')</small>. <small>(כ' צ"צ סי' מ"ט אם נמצא מחט תחוב בכרס וכנגד אותו מקום נמצא סרכא בכרס תלינן לחומרא דודאי הסרכא באה מחמת המחט שניקבה הכרס מעל"ע. אבל אם נמצא קוץ או מחט שמונח כך בכרס. אע"פ שבית הכוסות נסרך לאיזה מקום אין להטריף עכ"ל)</small>:
**** ENGLISH ****
Thickness. Shach brings the view of Rashi, Rashba, and several great poskim that they permit as long as it was not punctured mafpolash; and Taz wrote: nevertheless one may not be lenient since they established in Shulchan Aruch for halacha; in any case it is clear that also regarding mafpolash he forbids only at a tefach. And all this when punctured through illness; but through needle or thorn — treifah in every manner as siman 91 — see there (lest the intestines were punctured) (Taz). An animal gored by its fellow in the horn in the place of the outer kereis and it is evident the place became raised at the goring until it appears the flesh covering the outer kereis was torn and the skin is whole — if it is known the covering flesh was torn at the measure for which it becomes treifah, namely length of one tefach — it is treifah from life and its milk is forbidden as milk of a treifah; but if it is not known whether the puncture is a tefach long — it appears one may not forbid the milk from life from safek. But if it was slaughtered and the tear of a tefach measure was not found — nevertheless one must check the inner organs punctured in any amount (Beit Yosef siman 13; and see Kenesset HaGedolah 168). (Tzitz Eliezer siman 49: if a needle is found stuck in the kereis and opposite that place a adhesion is found in the kereis — we suspend stringently, for certainly the adhesion came from the needle that punctured the kereis from side to side. But if a thorn or needle is placed thus in the kereis — even though beit hakosot adhered to some place — one does not declare treifah, end of his words):
