(Siman 296 seif 1) And likewise if he sowed two kinds of vegetables and a kernel, or one vegetable seed and one grain seed etc. — he receives lashes. This is Rambam Hilchot Kilayim chapter 5 halachah 2. Kesef Mishnah thought Tosafot Menachot and Ran Rishit haGez argue with Rambam on vegetables in a vineyard — that they are not kilayim from the Torah, for they use language "grain of five species are d'oraisa kilayim in a vineyard," but vegetables imply not d'oraisa; to me this is not compelled — perhaps they came only to exclude other garden seeds that are not eaten, similar to hemp and turnip discussed there. However from Tosafot Eruvin (daf 92) it appears as Kesef Mishnah wrote on dira gedolah bekatanah and ein dira ketanah begedolah etc. — vines in the small one, permitted to sow the large one; Rashi: even though dira gedolah bekatanah, seeds in the large do not forbid vines in the small, for Menachot says hemp and turnip the Torah forbade, other seeds rabbinically forbidden — this case did not make a prohibition so the rabbis did not penalize; so when he sowed the large in permission, no penalty applies. Tosafot challenged: how permit leaving the seeds when they grow — since dira gedolah bekatanah, even what grew spontaneously we learned in Kilayim: one passing through a vineyard saw a vegetable in his vineyard and said when I return I will harvest — if he added two hundred it is forbidden once he despaired of harvesting; that Menachot case is when the vineyard owner did not know seeds were in his vineyard — end; appears Tosafot' reason seeds are rabbinic is because Kesef Mishnah holds per Tosafot Menachot vegetables in vineyard are not d'oraisa kilayim — therefore Tosafot troubled Rashi from our mishnah vegetables in vineyard forbidden even what grew by itself — if only rabbinic why penalize; vegetables in vineyard are also only rabbinic yet forbidden even spontaneous growth; Rashi's view can be proved — he holds like Rambam vegetables in vineyard are d'oraisa kilayim; Mezarchi wrote plainly on Rashi in Chumash Teitzei and did not mention dissenters at all. Wonder of Tosafot on Rashi from vegetable case forbidden even spontaneous — if d'oraisa unlike seeds. However from Tosafot Yevamot (daf 81) one can prove the other side — Tosafot hold like Rambam vegetables in vineyard are d'oraisa kilayim — they wrote clover in vineyard is kilayim d'oraisa; Sukkah (daf 35) Tosafot hold clover is a vegetable species; so Tosafot Zevachim (daf 73) and Rosh Orlah chapter 3. Thus Tzlach's complaint on Maharam in Rosh Beitzah is removed — he wrote bundles of clover in vineyard are kilayim d'oraisa, but Rashi there clover is a legume species and not d'oraisa kilayim; even per Rambam vegetables in vineyard are d'oraisa but legumes all agree are not kilayim in vineyard, only rabbinically; per Tosafot Sukkah I brought, Tosafot hold clover is a vegetable species and like Rambam vegetables in vineyard are d'oraisa. I can bring proof clover is not a legume species per Tosafot from what is taught first chapter Eruvin (daf 28) — beans, barley, and clover sown for vegetable — his intent is annulled before everyone; therefore sowing them is liable and their vegetable is exempt — why teach these — are they not in the category of grain and legumes taught in Tosefta: grain and legumes sown for vegetable — intent annulled, vegetable exempt, seed liable for terumot and maasrot; Kesef Mishnah already challenged this in Terumot chapter 2 halachah 7 and answered: perhaps because of clover he teaches it; this is possible only if we say clover is not a legume species — then it is not in grain and legumes of Tosefta and therefore taught in the baraita in Eruvin; but if clover is a legume species, it is impossible to say because of clover he teaches this baraita — the difficulty stands: why teach it — he already taught legumes in Tosefta; therefore conclude clover is not a legume species. I have proof for Rambam and Shulchan Aruch that vegetables in vineyard are d'oraisa kilayim from Sotah (daf 43b) regarding grafting a tree into a vegetable — if forbidden it is d'oraisa; if only rabbinic for them it should return even when RHG repented as proven there; see Tzlach who proved Rambam's view from the sugya in Kiddushin (daf 38).

(סעיף ג') <b>אסור לזרוע ירקות או תבואה בצד הגפנים כו'.</b> דעת הש"ע דהא דקיי"ל כר' יאשיה דבעי' שיזרע חטה ושעורה וחרצן היינו לענין מלקות אבל איסורא מיהא איכא אפי' בחד מינא וכ"ה דעת הראב"ן בסי' נ"ג. והר"ן פ' ראשית הגז חולק על זה. ונראה לי לה"ר לדעת השו"ע ממנחות (דף ט"ו) תניא מעשה באחד שזרע כרמו של חבירו סמדר ובא מעשה לפני חכמים ואסרו את הזרעים וכתבו התוס' היינו דלא כר' יאשיה דאמר עד שיזרע חטה ושעורה וחרצן במפולת יד והיינו משום דהתוס' ס"ל דהא דר' יאשיה איירי נמי לענין איסורא דעד שיזרע חטה ושעורה וחרצן במפולת יד ליכא אפי' איסורא דאורייתא אבל לדעת הש"ע שהיא דעת הרמב"ם דאיסור דאורייתא איכא נמי בחד מינא לפ"ז שפיר י"ל דאתיא ברייתא דמנחות נמי אליבא דר' יאשיה דנהי דלא לקי איסורא מיהא איכא וגם הפירות נאסרין וכן יש לה"ר לדעת הרמב"ם והש"ע דבזה תתיישב קושיית התוס' בשבת (קל"ט) דלא פריך התם ולישלח להו כר' יאשיה גבי כשותא בכרמי מהו ולפ"ד הרמב"ם לק"מ דכיון דלענין איסור מודה ר' יאשיה דאסור בחד מינא אין נפקותא בהך דר' יאשיה שם בההיא האיבעיא דאיירי לענין איסורא עיי"ש אך לא משמע כן בבכורות (דף נ"ד):
**** ENGLISH ****
(Seif 3) Forbidden to sow vegetables or grain beside vines etc. Shulchan Aruch's view: that we hold like R' Yoshiah requiring wheat, barley, and kernel is for lashes; but there is prohibition even in one species; so Raavan siman 53; Ran Rishit haGez disagrees. It appears to me for Rambam and Shulchan Aruch from Menachot (daf 15) — taught: incident — one sowed his friend's vineyard semadar; the matter came before the Sages and they forbade the seeds; Tosafot wrote this is not like R' Yoshiah who said until he sows wheat, barley, and kernel in mafuleit yad — because Tosafot hold R' Yoshiah's words also concern prohibition — until three in mafuleit yad there is not even d'oraisa prohibition; per Shulchan Aruch, which is Rambam's view, that d'oraisa prohibition exists even in one species — accordingly the Menachot baraita can be even per R' Yoshiah — although he is not lashed, there is prohibition and the fruits are forbidden; so for Rambam and Shulchan Aruch Tosafot' difficulty in Shabbat (139) is resolved — why not ask according to R' Yoshiah about hops in vineyards; per Rambam's view, since for prohibition R' Yoshiah agrees forbidden in one species, the R' Yoshiah matter there in that inquiry about prohibition has no relevance — see there; but it does not appear so in Bekhorot (daf 54).
