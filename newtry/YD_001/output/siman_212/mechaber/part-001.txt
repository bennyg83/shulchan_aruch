**** ENGLISH ****
The law of one who vows by the Torah; it contains one se’if. One who vows by the Torah — for example, he said, “These fruits are upon me like the Torah” — he said nothing, and he does not need release if he is a Torah scholar. But if he is an am ha’aretz, he needs release, so that they not treat vows lightly. And if he vowed by something in which prohibition is written — for behold prohibition is written in it — he took it in his hand and vowed by it: it is like one who vows by something in which it is written. (But one who swears by the Torah in any matter needs release; and see below, siman 237.) (Beit Yosef regarding practical application.)
