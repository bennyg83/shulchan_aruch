**** ENGLISH ****Rather it is presumed to have given birth, etc. — not to say it is presumed to have given birth to this offspring; as Taz wrote, for in Gemara chapter 3 of Bekhorot it is doubtful whether we say one that gives birth becomes pregnant or does not become pregnant unless it gives birth to it; and the halachah is unresolved; therefore there it says like a pig is forbidden in eating, lest it becomes pregnant even though it did not give birth to it; and likewise poskim and Taz above siman 79 end seif 24 regarding impure species nursing and wrapped after the pure one — forbidden lest born from the impure one and wrapped after it; rather meaning even if it did not give birth to it, nevertheless it certainly gave birth to some offspring, and if so the one born after is exempt from firstborn status. Nevertheless requires study what Rambam and Taz wrote: even if this one nursing was like a pig, etc. — implying more reasonable for pig to obligate firstborn status; and in Gemara there we say on the contrary, rather more reasonable that even for eating it should be permitted, lest we say since not its species it does not become pregnant unless it gives birth to it; and it seems to me nevertheless they hold that for pig since not its species it does not become pregnant, and if so this one that became pregnant must be even though it did not give birth at all; and if so it would be reasonable to obligate firstborn status; he informs us that even so it is exempt because on the contrary more reasonable, since not its species if so more reasonable to say it gave birth to it, as the Gemara says — requires study.
