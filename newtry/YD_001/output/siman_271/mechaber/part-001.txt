**** ENGLISH ****
The hide on which it is written, its ruling, and the law of ink. In it are 7 seifim. One does not write a Sefer Torah on the hide of an impure domestic animal, wild animal, or bird; nor on the hide of a fish, even a pure one. But one writes on the hide of a pure domestic animal, wild animal, or bird—even the hide of their nevelah or treifah. And the hides must be processed by a Jew for the sake of a Sefer Torah—that he say at the beginning of the processing, when they place them into the lime: "These hides I am processing for the sake of a Sefer Torah." {Rama: And if a non-Jew processes them, a Jew should assist a little (Tur in the name of Rabbenu Yerucham) at the beginning when they give them to the lime, and he should say that he is doing so for the sake of the sanctity of a Sefer Torah (Beit Yosef). And he should mark those hides so the non-Jew will not exchange them; and we are not concerned afterward that perhaps the non-Jew exchanged them and forged his marks after him (Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 1 of Hilchos Sefer Torah); and see in Orach Chaim siman 32.}

**** ENGLISH ****
One writes a Sefer Torah on gevil, which is hide that was not split, and one writes on it on the side of the hair. And if one wrote a Sefer Torah on klaf, it is valid provided it is written on the inner side that faces the flesh. {Rama: And our klaf is more preferable than gevil, and one writes on them l'chatchila on the side of the flesh; and nowadays one does not write on gevil (R' Yitzchak Minz siman 66). And if one changed and wrote on our klaf on the side of hair, it is invalid (Or Zarua, Hilchos Tefillin). However, some are lenient in this (Tur and Rabbenu Yerucham).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A Sefer Torah must be written with ink made from the soot of oils soaked in gallnut water. (And l'chatchila it is good to be careful not to make the ink except from things that come from a tree—as appears in the Zohar, parashas Terumah, daf 72a.) And if one wrote it with gallnut water and gum, it is valid; but not with other kinds of dyes, such as red and green and the like—for if one wrote even a single letter with other kinds of dyes, or in gold, it is invalid. {Rama: And likewise it is forbidden to write any name from the twenty-four books except with ink (R' Yerucham in the name of Even Yerachi). And some say we require ink only for a Sefer Torah (Mordechai, beginning of Hilchos Ketanim).}
