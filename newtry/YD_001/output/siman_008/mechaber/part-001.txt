**** ENGLISH ****
**The measure of a shechitah knife. And it has one seif:** How long is the knife with which one slaughters—any size (only) that it not be a thin thing that punctures and does not slaughter, like the head of the small izmel and similar to it. And with a needle—even if it is somewhat wide like those of tailors with which they cut thread—one does not slaughter with it (Tur). And since we do not know the measure—one who slaughters with a small knife must be careful and estimate according to his judgment that when he performs holacha u'va with it he will not press; but with one overly small one does not slaughter: {Rama: and one who cannot estimate should take a knife as full as the length of two necks of that species he slaughters (Mordechai in name of R. Chiya); and some estimate fourteen finger-widths, and there is a hint for this in what is said “and you shall slaughter them”—“biZeh” (in this)—how many? fourteen (there and old shechitah texts).}
