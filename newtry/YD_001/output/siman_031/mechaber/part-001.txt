**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of piercing the brain and its membranes. In it are 4 seifim. The brain has two membranes, one upon the other. If the lower one was perforated — if the perforation is bore through completely — treifah; but if only the upper one was perforated alone — kosher. {Rama: And some declare treifah also when only the upper membrane was perforated (Bahag, brought by Hagahot Ashiri and Tur HaDeshen siman 166 and Hagahot ShaDaL siman 81) — and such is the custom unless it is not a place of great loss (his own opinion).}

**** ENGLISH ****
The brain itself — if a little of it decayed or was crushed, and the membrane remains intact — kosher. And if it spilled like water or like wax — treifah. And if water was found inside the membrane, and a corresponding amount is missing from the brain: if the brain surrounds the water on all sides so it was not exposed even to the membrane — kosher; and if not — treifah. And the same law applies if the water was placed inside a small pouch (meaning like a pouch-shape). If the brain surrounded it so it was not exposed even to the membrane — kosher; and if not — treifah. {Rama: And likewise if the pouch was placed between the membrane and the skull — treifah (Hagahot R' Yehuda on chapter Aleph Tav). If there was a doubt whether it was covered by brain or not — treifah. Therefore one must be careful when opening the head to open it in a manner whereby one can see whether the brain surrounds it if water is found there or not. And nevertheless one need not check afterward, for we rely on the majority and may throw away the head or sell it to a non-Jew without opening (his own opinion).}

**** ENGLISH ****
If a worm was found in the membrane of the brain and the brain remains intact, and there is neither a trace nor a drop of blood in the lower membrane — kosher. And some say we are not expert in examination and declare treifah (and such is the custom). But if it was found outside the cavity — such as in the nostril — kosher, for we presume it never reached the brain.
