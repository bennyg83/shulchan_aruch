(סימן ל"א סעיף א') <b>המוח יש לו ב' קרומים זע"ז ניקב התחתון כו' טריפה.</b> כך היא דעת הרי"ף ומשמע להר"ן שכן דעת הרמב"ם פ"ו מהל' שחיטה. והטור כתב בשם הרמב"ם דאינה טריפה עד שינקבו שניהם כדעת ראב"ן. והב"י מסופק באמת בדעת הרמב"ם. ונעלם ממנו מ"ש הרמב"ם בפי' המשנה בפא"ט בהדיא כמ"ש הר"ן לדעתו ומשם נלמוד פי' למ"ש בחיבורו ואם ניקב התחתון טריפה ר"ל גם התחתון לבד. וכ"ה בס' מ"ש ובתב"ש ובס' ארוך. וזה דעת רוב הפוסקים הרמב"ם והרי"ף והר"ן והגה' אשר"י ומרדכי ור"ח והרשב"א ור"ע מברטנורא בפי' המשנה ורא"ה בס' בד"ה. והכי משמע להדיא בש"ס והרי"ף והרא"ש והרשב"א והט"ו בס"ס זה גבי עד הפולין נקיבת קרומו במשהו ומשם ואילך ברובו. וכ"מ להדיא ברמב"ם פ"ו מה"ש ומביאו הב"י סי' ל"ב גבי התחלת חוט השדרה מן הפולין ולחוץ ושיעורו ברובו ואלו למעלה מזה במשהו ע"ש ודו"ק:<br><b>ויש</b> לתמוה על הטור שנראה מדבריו שאינה נטרפה בתתאה לחוד עד שינקבו שניהם. ואם כן האיך כתב בס"ס זה הא דאמר ר"י בן לוי בש"ס (דף מ"ה) עד הפולין ובהדיא איתא ברא"ש דריב"ל ס"ל בתתאה לחוד טריפה. ויש ליישב דנפקא מינה אם ניקב שם התחתון דטריפה אם ניקב גם העליון דאלו בעליון לחוד נמי אינה טריפה ודו"ק:
**** ENGLISH ****
(siman 31 seif 1) <b>The brain has two membranes one upon the other; lower perforated, etc. — treifah.</b> So is Rif's view; and it appears to Ran so is Rambam chapter 6 Hilchot Shechitah. Tur wrote in Rambam's name: not treifah until both perforated — Ra'avan's view. Beit Yosef is truly doubtful of Rambam's view; hidden from him what Rambam wrote in mishnah commentary chapter Aleph Tav explicitly as Ran per his view — from there we learn explanation of his composition; if lower perforated — treifah — meaning lower alone too. So in books, Turei Zahav, and Arukh. This is majority poskim: Rambam, Rif, Ran, Hagahot Ashiri, Mordechai, R' Chananel, Rashba, R' Ovadiah of Bartenura in mishnah commentary, Ra'ah in Bedek HaBayit. So explicit in Talmud; Rif, Ra'osh, Rashba, Tur in this matter until bulbs: membrane perforation in any amount; from there onward majority. So explicit Rambam chapter 6 Hilchot Shechitah; Beit Yosef brings siman 32 regarding spinal cord beginning from bulbs outward — majority; above that — any amount — see there — examine. One may wonder on Tur appearing not treifah in lower alone until both perforated; if so how wrote in this matter what R' Yehuda ben Levi said in Talmud (daf 45) until bulbs; and explicitly Ra'osh R' Yehuda ben Levi holds lower alone treifah. Resolve: practical difference if lower perforated there — treifah; if upper also perforated — if upper alone also not treifah — examine.

(there seif 3) <b>If worm found in brain membrane, etc.; and no trace or blood drop in lower membrane — kosher.</b> Mordechai and Maharshal brought proof from Titus — gnat entered his brain yet lived seven years. Per Tosafot chapter HaNizakin (daf 56) distinction treifot man and animal — no proof from there. Also no proof from Titus — miracle that God gave strength to receive judgment for fighting God — as we say chapter Aleph Tav (daf 43) regarding Iyov: tested differently — examine. In truth in my humble opinion proof from Talmud, Rashi, Ran end chapter Aleph Tav (daf 67) kukani — prisoners — Rashi: worms in liver and lung; see Rambam chapter 2 Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot: worms in brain in animal head — 49 kinds enter through nostril — Rashi: when animal sleeps they enter nostril to lung and liver — end; implies animal permitted like worms in liver siman 41 and Morana in lung siman 36 seif 5; worms certainly in skull when entering nostril — all the more Rambam found there; learn: kosher even if found in skull since entered nostril and did not perforate membrane — as Tosafot chapter HaNizakin s.v. veniker mochai. Do not challenge: per gloss to forbid worm in skull — how permit Morana in lung — was like Morana in skull since certainly there? Say: per Talmud conclusion chapter Aleph Tav — double safek: perhaps from outside, never reached brain; still forbidden as eiver min hachai as Tur siman 84 s.t. 16; if from outside — perhaps membrane not perforated — like worm in nostril kosher even if mouth outward — such double safek. Uncertain: per Taz here first answer — require membrane examination even if in nostril — require examination also Morana in lung and worms in liver — no posek said so; indeed not proper to be stringent on nostril worm where double safek — no need to clarify as Darkei Moshe siman 53 and gloss siman 110 seif 10; even per gloss siman 53 seif 4 to be stringent safek missing knowledge we are not expert — nevertheless main answer second of Taz: here like found in esophagus or belly siman 33 and 48 — we need not say it made a hole; unlike where one may say perforated — perhaps perforated further; see siman 36 s.k. 5 this reasoning in Ra'ah Bedek HaBayit. Know: I saw Rav Toviah in mishnah commentary these bird treifot wondrous — wrote bird treifah through upper membrane alone even per view in animal lower membrane; very wondrous — proven explicit Talmud, Rashi chapter Aleph Tav (daf 56), Ra'osh chapter Aleph Tav siman 11, Rashba Tur HaDeshen long (daf 30): per view in animal upper alone not treifah — same in bird; Levi taught general: all treifot listed for animal — corresponding in bird — needs study.
