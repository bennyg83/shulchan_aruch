We explain with a parable: one animal, neck four fingers abcd placed; beside it animal also four fingers hezach; knife eight fingers abcdefgh. If so, when he begins slaughter through drawing, knife letter aleph comes to animal one's daleth; when he draws, slaughter on both — behold aleph of knife on animal two's daleth. Beit Yosef learned: always the full eight-finger knife must pass over each animal's entire neck; therefore in the first manner of drawing, he draws the knife over animal one's entire neck from cheth until aleph passed; but on animal two at completion only aleph of knife on its daleth — only four fingers passed on the neck — from this Beit Yosef derived: if he wanted to pass the entire knife beyond until cheth of knife on animal two's hei — all eight passed — kosher; and reverse in bringing — only four fingers passed on first animal, rest remain on second — treifah; but if he drew and brought until cheth of knife on animal one's aleph and entire knife beyond animal also — kosher. only Tosafot and Ra'osh: with a long knife he does not darsah; but a short knife only according to the animal darsahes — it appears this knife must be free, not busy with another slaughter — then it is a long, spacious knife; only called as one because completion is as one — when bringing knife from one animal to the other, the first's majority was finished before reaching the second; but when coming to slaughter the second, the first's majority completes — called as one; and so Taz's view — see there; therefore one cannot say when beginning with drawing that both finish truly equally — only first head of knife cheth begins at first neck's start aleph and draws — at first neck's end daleth, hei of knife arrives and four letters remain outside animal — then he slaughters with spacious eight-finger knife — kosher; but at second animal's end, cheth of knife on its cheth — four fingers on first animal to complete majority onward, and four on second — no free knife, for on second cheth to hei, and aleph–daleth of knife on first to complete — no free knife, no spaciousness — therefore darsahed and second treifah;
