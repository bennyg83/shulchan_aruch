**** ENGLISH ****
That one may paste Torah, Prophets, and Writings together. In it are 5 seifim. It is permitted to paste Torah, Prophets, and Writings in one volume; and one leaves between each chumash and chumash four lines, and between each prophet and prophet three—and likewise between each prophet of the Twelve. And their sanctity is not like the sanctity of a Sefer Torah but like an ordinary chumash (and all this is in chumashim made on gevil like a Sefer Torah; but in ours, which are bound, there is no difference between chumash and Prophets) (Ran). And it is permitted to write the entire Torah chumash by chumash separately, and they do not have the sanctity of a complete Sefer Torah. And some say they are like a Sefer Torah in all their laws except that one does not read from them in public.
