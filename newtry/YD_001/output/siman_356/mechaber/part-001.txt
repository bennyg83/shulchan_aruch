<b>גבו לצורך המת והותירו. ובו סעיף אחד:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>מת שלא היה לו צרכי קבורה וגבו לו והותירו אם כשגבו <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>יחדו לצרכי זה המת <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i>ינתנו <i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="1"></i>ליורשיו ואם לאו יעשו מהם צרכי מתים אחרים:
**** ENGLISH ****
They collected for the needs of the dead and left over. In it is one seif. A dead person who had no burial needs and they collected for him and left over — if when they collected they intended together for the needs of this dead person, they give to his heirs; and if not, they use them for the needs of other dead.
