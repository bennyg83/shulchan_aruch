**** ENGLISH ****
That it depends on it. [Avnei Nezer; and see Peri Megadim in Sha'ar HaTzon s.k. 1 who wrote: accordingly three olives that fell into dish from three animals — one animal remained olive in place of omasum, one remained place of kidneys, one place of gall — need only 60 against one olive; and in drying all permitted, for perforce two kosher and one treifah and nullified in majority. And in opening to siman 98 I will explain whether in such case we say one in two nullifies, etc. — end. And in much — Rav's words are astonishing, for in such case on contrary perforce two treifot; and I thought printing error and where remained must say removed; but I saw in opening to laws of mixtures chapter 1 investigation 1 he mentioned both removed and remained: there one in two nullifies if specifically from permitted clarified initially, etc.; parable: three liver olives (not specifically) from three animals one removed place of vitality, one from gall, one from kidneys — if in each one olive remained, now perforce two of permitted, etc.; and likewise in flayed three animals one remained in navel, etc. — see his name; behold explicitly he equates remained to removed; and in truth this is not. Again I heard while genius author was alive a sage from community Slonim asked him this himself and he admitted to him a slip escaped his pen in this.]
