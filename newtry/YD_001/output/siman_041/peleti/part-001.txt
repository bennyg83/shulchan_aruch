Liver removed, etc. — meaning of all poskim: liver removed from treifot from itself and not on account of gall; and so is meaning of Gemara; however Tosafot HaRishonim daf 43b in his challenge on Rambam who holds liver removed specifically is treifah and liver not created lacking — behold all liver treifot are on account of gall in creation and certainly treifah if punctured; and this teaches he holds liver treifot are on account of gall; and this is wondrous in Gemara that says it counts treifot from it but not from what is not from it; and see Rashi on one explanation on that which Rabbi Shimon bar Rabbi would immerse in ten, for he held soul depends on liver and life — see his name — that it is from itself. And it appears certainly according to all life depends on liver and soul, only it is not specified specifically in place of gall — why is place of gall stronger than other places? It is strained to say in place of gall life depends — this is possible to say regarding place of vitality, but regarding place of gall there is no reason life should depend there and soul dwells. (And in derush I said wittily regarding this dispute that came to Pumpedita: Rabbah and Rav Yosef fled ten; Rabbi Zeira met them and said: "Flee, olive that they said in place of gall!" We learned — what is this matter to your fleeing from the king's tax? But the matter according to simple sense: therefore we require olive in place of gall because there life of the body depends; and even though you have nothing more than gall, and all illness depends on gall and Satan, as he wished to afflict Job — gall was poured on his body, as written "He pours my gall on the ground"; nevertheless such is in its place of judgment — there He acted for good in the bad place the good always; and so His wisdom in good creation; and Moses buried in Peor, and so all — measure for measure. Behold they wished to flee not on account of tax as Tosafot wrote, but they did not wish to learn Torah in place of king's decree and Israel's distress; it was hard for them to learn Torah there. Rabbi Zeira said one should not do so; such is the measure as they said: from day Temple was destroyed it was decreed to learn Torah amid sorrow, poverty, and pressure; and the proof is the olive they said life of liver depends in place of gall — and this is simple.) Therefore Rashba certainly holds remnant required in liver is treifot from itself; and if no remnant like olive — behold treifah; but that it must be specifically place of gall and not another place — for this there is no reason of body's life; what is written "this place from that place" — only to protect the gall; and treifot depend on gall — and so on. And he argued well nevertheless on Rambam: if created lacking liver without its remnant — kosher to Rambam; and he challenged well: after all there is no protection for gall and gall is forbidden treifah — and so on; and clear. Peri Chadash wrote per Tosafot he has no proof that they stood on this; Rabbi Pinchas said liver removed and nothing remained — why needed? Without this treifah from removal of gall treifah; and answer: perhaps membrane remained in sinews and simfonot — end; and one may say: certainly if liver removed from illness — common that it is removed and melts in illness and sinews and simfonot remain; not so if created lacking: if by nature liver not created from six days of creation — simfonot would exist and be created — this thing is uncommon; if this is not — this too is not; if so Rashba who said in lacking liver — fine, there is no place for gall since simfonot do not exist; but Tosafot deal with removal that is common — one may say flesh of liver melted and simfonot remain and in them gall depends; and that Rashba did not challenge briefly on Rambam if liver lacking — perforce sinews lacking; and Rambam ruled simfonot of liver punctured — treifah; if so the same in lacking — one may say Rashba did not wish to hang this law on that law, for if we do not hold like Rambam in that — there would be no difficulty here; therefore Rashba challenged his difficulty from before; and difficulty in every case even if we do not hold like Rambam in that. But in truth anyway, as stated, Tosafot and Rashba appear divided in essence of law: for Rashba simfonot of liver and sinews are included in remnant; and that which teaches remnant like olive — sinews combine to olive. But Tosafot hold like Rambam they do not combine; if so anyway Rashba has no dependence on sinews as Tosafot wrote, for we learned nothing remained and even sinews do not exist; and see what I wrote above. But in essence of law, if liver treifot are on account of gall — Tosafot also appear to hold so, for why need Tosafot this strain that gall depends on simfonot and not explain simply therefore needed to teach liver removed even though gall removed — teaches even in species of animal and fowl that have no gall such as deer and doves — nevertheless treifah from liver removed; but per my words it is fine that Tosafot hold that which requires olive in place of gall is on account of gall; but species that have no gall — no olive needed in place of gall; if so, if mishnah that teaches nothing remained — deals with species that have no gall; and mishnah that teaches nothing remained like olive and Rashi explains like olive in place of gall — means they have gall and olive needed to protect; what does Gemara challenge mishnayot against each other and establish Rabbi Chiya and Rashba and not establish simply: first clause deals with species that have no gall and need no protection for gall, only liver matter alone suffices with any remnant; but second clause species that have gall and require olive remnant to protect gall, as stated; and this resolves. Perforce first clause also deals with species that have gall; and Tosafot's words fit well; and this requires study.

Olive-volume. Behold Rashba and Tur hold simfonot and sinews are included in remnant; and from Tosafot above it appears they hold not included — one may say it is a distant dispute and we do not act on it. Behold Rabbi Chiya holds any remnant helps; and Rashba requires some remnant — how say distant dispute to Rabbi Chiya any remnant helps even combining sinews and simfonot; to Rashba even flesh does not help until olive exists; and vice versa to Rashba how say simfonot do not combine to olive — for Rabbi Chiya we do not require olive at all and any remnant suffices.
