And Maharshal's view in Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 42 does not appear so, for he wrote: that which is kosher when wormy in place of gall and place of its vitality — this is when between all of them there is only the punctures of the worms like an olive-volume of flesh in place of its vitality and in place of the gall; and if not, it is obvious one may say the Gemara teaches us that since it became so wormy, in the end it will become wormy and spoil entirely; and Ran leans to be stringent per this reasoning — and this is the essence — end. And if so, when all was removed and the two olive-volumes that remained became wormy — there are not here two olive-volumes alone but worm punctures — and treifah; and so is Ra'ah's view in Sefer Beit Hashem; and perhaps also Tosafot Chadashim and Bach do not deal except when it became wormy in a case where the two olive-volumes were not lacking from their measure; and this requires study.

This implies even if they are not adhered to one another in place of its vitality and in place of the gall — for any excess is considered as removed, meaning as though both were removed entirely; therefore wherever two livers are found — treifah; and so wrote Tosafot Chadashim general rule 89 law 11; and so Maharshal ruled Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 18. However Maharshal wrote there: if the two livers begin below that olive in place of gall and in place of its vitality — it is obvious it is kosher according to everyone, like excess of the leg that goes out below the knee (below siman 55 seif 4) — end; and one must say it is worse if excess of liver is found that is not adhered at all to the liver than if adhered below that olive, for since it begins below — from the place of its vitality and place of the gall it appears it is only liver tendrils; and this requires study; and see at end of siman 43 and siman 50 and 54 and 55 what I wrote from this.
