C) There. Unless olive in place of gall and olive in place of its vitality that depends on it. Namely place of its hanging when exposed and stuck under kidneys — Rashi Chullin 46a; and Rashba and Rambam chapter 8 Hilchot Shechitah; and Beit Yosef wrote and so is essence; therefore ruled so in Shulchan Aruch. Therefore Orach Chaim siman 51 ot 8 wrote: need three olives in these three places — meaning olive in place of gall, olive in place stuck under kidneys, olive in place of hanging in omasum. And Beit David Rama in his book Tosafot Chadashim general rule 89 ot 9 section ot 2; Taz s.k. 1; Shach s.k. 1; so is practice in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 3; Peri Chadash ot 2; for this Orach Chaim ot 2; Kereti ot 2; Turei Zahav ot 3; Sha'ar HaTziyon ot 3; Zivchei Tzedek ot 3. <b>ד) מיהו  </b>בהפ"מ או לכבוד שבת אם יש כזית במקום מרה וכזית במקום תלייתה בטרפש יש להכשיר. משאת בנימין בתשו' סי' ק"ג. כנה"ג שם. מנ"י על התו"ח שם או' כ"ה. וכ"כ בספרו שבו"י ח"א סי' נ"א. אבל המחב"ר שם כתב דבהפ"מ או לכבוד שבת אם יש כזית במקום מרה וכזית במקום הכליות יש להכשיר כפסק הש"ע וכתב דהגם דמור"ם ז"ל בתו"ח הביא דברי או"ה דבעי' ג' זיתים הכא בהגה חזר בו מדלא הגיה על דברי הש"ע. וכ"כ ענ"ה שם דבהפ"מ יש להקל אם נשאר כזית במ"מ וכזית במקום כליות לסמוך על פסק הש"ע. זב"צ או' ד' וכתב אבל אם לא נשאר אלא זית במ"מ וזית במקום טרפשא יש להטריף עכ"ל והכי מסתברא משום דכ"ה דעת רש"י והרמב"ם והרשב"א והש"ע והרמ"א שלא הגיה על דברי הש"ע.
**** ENGLISH ****
D) However in financial loss or for honor of Shabbat — if olive in place of gall and olive in place of hanging in omasum — one may declare kosher. Masat Binyamin responsum 103; so is practice there; Minchat Yitzchak on Tosafot Chadashim there ot 25; and so in his book Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 51. But Machatzit HaShekel there wrote: in financial loss or Shabbat honor if olive in place of gall and olive in place of kidneys — declare kosher per Shulchan Aruch ruling; and wrote: though Moharach in Tosafot Chadashim brought Orach Chaim's words requiring three olives — here in hagahah he retracted since he did not emend Shulchan Aruch's words. And so Anaf Yosef there: in financial loss be lenient if remained olive in place of gall and olive in place of kidneys to rely on Shulchan Aruch ruling. Zivchei Tzvi ot 4; and he wrote: but if only olive in place of gall and olive in place of omasum — make treifah — end; and so appears from view of Rashi, Rambam, Rashba, Shulchan Aruch, and Rama who did not emend Shulchan Aruch.

J) And it appears if third-of-egg measure exists from flesh alone and completion of half egg from sinews and simfonot — kosher, for double doubt in dispute of rabbis. And appears further: if fed in place of gall and olive in place hanging on kidneys — whole from flesh alone and half-egg measure for each; and third olive for omasum — its measure third of egg; and with sinews — kosher even without financial loss from reason I wrote; and likewise opposite. Anaf Yosef there. And his intent in "and likewise opposite": if olive in place of gall and olive in omasum whole in flesh alone and half egg for each; and third olive hanging on kidneys — third egg with sinews complete half egg — kosher even without financial loss; but if olive in place hanging on kidneys and olive of omasum whole from flesh alone and half egg each and third olive in place of gall — third egg and sinews do not permit; for as written "and likewise opposite" intent specifically regarding olive of omasum and olive of kidneys; but olive in place of gall — no permission; for so we find in law of flattened — need whole olive in place of gall and another with it; but if two olives of omasum and kidneys whole and olive in place of gall flattened — no permission as Machatzit HaShekel ot 7 — see his name. But it appears even olive in place of gall — third egg and sinews complete half egg — may declare kosher from above reason that flattened law is different, for Gemara asked even on one olive as Machatzit HaShekel wrote; but regarding half egg and sinews — dispute of rabbis — we do double doubt even on olive in place of gall. Zivchei Tzvi ot 12.
