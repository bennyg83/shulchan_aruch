**** ENGLISH ****
The law of a mourner who did not observe his mourning all seven days. And it contains 3 seifim. A mourner who did not observe his mourning within seven, whether inadvertently or intentionally — he completes it all thirty, except tearing: if he did not tear at moment of heat he does not tear except within seven, except for father and mother — he tears even after seven.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>במה דברים אמורים בשלא נהג אבלות כלל כל שבעה <i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="1"></i>אבל אם זלזל במקצת הימים ולא נהג בהם אבלות כגון מי שאמרו לו שמת לו מת ביום הא' והיה בעיר אחרת ולא נהג אבלות וביום השני בא לעירו ונהג אבלות אין צריך להשלים ומונה שבעה מיום הראשון:
**** ENGLISH ****
In what matters is this said? When he did not observe mourning at all all seven; but if he was lax on part of the days and did not observe mourning on them — such as they told him on day one that his relative died and he was in another city and did not observe mourning, and on day two he came to his city and observed mourning — he need not complete, and counts seven from the first day.
