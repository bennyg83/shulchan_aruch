**** ENGLISH ****
Who is considered like the dead even though still alive. In it is one seif. One whose neckbone was broken and the majority of flesh with it, and likewise one torn from his back like a fish — even if he is still alive he is considered like the dead and imparts impurity; but a goses and one in whom two signs were slaughtered or wounded with many wounds do not impart impurity until their soul departs; nevertheless it is forbidden to enter a house with a goses {Rama: (and some permit) (Ran end of Moed Katan and Mordechai parashat Elu Megalchin in the name of Geonim); (and it is good to be stringent)}.
