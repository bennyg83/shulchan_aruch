**** ENGLISH ****
Not to sell. He said, “The Lord’s Prayer is not a prophet, but he swore that he would not sell anything in his hands, and that he would be sold, and that he would not be able to do so, but if he swore that he would not sell it in my hands, and he would not sell it to him.”.
