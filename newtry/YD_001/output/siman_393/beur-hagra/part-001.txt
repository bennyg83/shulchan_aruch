<b>ומקצת כו'. </b>עתוס' שם ד"ה מכאן כו' וה"נ תניא כו' מיום ג' ואילך כו' ושם א' ד"ה בשלישי כו': <br><b>(ליקוט) ומקצת כו'. </b>שם <small>(בא"ר פ"ו) </small>מת באותה כו' בשלישי יוצא כו' וכן אמרו שם לענין שאילת שלום שאלו בשלומו כו' בשלישי כו' <small>(ועיין לעיל סי' ש"פ ס"ק ו' בשם הרמב"ן) </small>(ע"כ).
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And part of.</b> See Tosafot there, s.v. mikan, and the baraita that from the third day onward, and Tosafot on the following page. <b>(Likkut) And part of.</b> Evel Rabbati chapter 6 says that if death occurred in that period, on the third day he goes out. The same is stated regarding greeting: if others greet him, from the third day onward he may respond. See above siman 380, seif katan 6, in the name of Ramban.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And if he wishes... and now... all thirty days.</b> See Tosafot there, s.v. R. Yehudah. The custom follows the Yerushalmi ruling like R. Yehudah that during the fourth week he sits in his usual place but does not speak. Therefore the custom became not to sit in one's usual place, in order that speaking remain permitted. See Darkei Moshe.
