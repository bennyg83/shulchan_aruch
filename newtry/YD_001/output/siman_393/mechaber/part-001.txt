**** ENGLISH ****
When a mourner may leave his house. And it contains 4 seifim. A mourner — first three days he does not go out to house of mourning, nor to cemetery; from then on if another died in his neighborhood he goes out after the bier to cemetery; and he does not sit in comforters' place but in comforted's place; and if there is no bier and burial place there, he goes out even on first day even in another neighborhood: {Rama: And part of day three is like whole (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 7); and I have not seen this practiced now, for mourner does not go to cemetery or house of mourning all seven; possibly not obligation but permission; and since now they do not practice overturning bed, perhaps also this is not practiced (his reasoning).}
