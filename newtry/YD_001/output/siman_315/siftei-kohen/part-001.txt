**** ENGLISH ****Androgynous — even though generally we hold its status is unique and it is neither in the category of male nor female, and accordingly it is not a firstborn at all; nevertheless since poskim dispute it and some hold it is in the category of female, here regarding firstborn we are stringent and say it is in the category of doubtful firstborn, forbidden in shearing and labor, and not eaten except when another fixed blemish develops; and this androgynous condition is not a blemish; see above siman 194 seif 8.
