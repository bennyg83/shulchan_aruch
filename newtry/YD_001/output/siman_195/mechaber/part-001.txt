**** ENGLISH ****
The matters that are forbidden during the time of her niddah; it contains 17 se’ifim. A person is obligated to separate from his wife in the days of her tumah until she counts and immerses. (And even if a long time passed and she did not immerse, she is always in her niddah until she immerses (Beit Yosef in the name of poskim).) And he should not jest and he should not be lightheaded with her, even with words (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba, from Avos D’Rabbi Nosson), lest he come to be accustomed to sin. But it is permitted to be alone with her, for since he had relations with her one time, his yetzer will no longer overpower him (these are his words).

If a woman is ill and she is a niddah, it is forbidden for her husband to touch her in order to attend to her, for example, to lift her, to lay her down, or to support her. (And some say that if she has no one to attend to her, it is permitted in every case (Hagahos Sha”d and Hagahos Mordechai, perek 1 of Shabbos, in the name of R”m); and so is the practice if she needs it greatly.)
