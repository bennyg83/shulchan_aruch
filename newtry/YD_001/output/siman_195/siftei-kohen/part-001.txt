**** ENGLISH ****
And some say, etc. Darkei Moshe's language: whether during her niddah days or during her whiteness days — and the beginning of this siman has already been explained; however I found a gloss in Mordechai in the name of Ravyah — and his language: after the whiteness days there is no habit of transgression, and it is good to eat with her so that she will want to immerse when she is able — end quote; and regarding this I saw leniencies during whiteness days; and it does not seem to me one should rely on this; and Ravyah is alone in this — end of Darkei Moshe. Chiya — end quote; and this gloss is in Maharshal's glosses, brought by Derishah.
