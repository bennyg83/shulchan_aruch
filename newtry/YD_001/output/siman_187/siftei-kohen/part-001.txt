<b>מיד בכדי כו'. </b>כלומר דאם ראתה מיד בשיעור זה אף על גב די"ל דאחר תשמיש ראתה מ"מ כיון דהיתה חייבת אשם תלוי מקרי רואה מחמת תשמיש אבל אחר השיעור הזה טהורה אע"ג די"ל קודם לכן ראתה יצאה מכלל רואה מחמת תשמיש כן משמע בפוסקים אבל אם בדקה מיד אחר תשמיש ולא מצאה כלום ואח"כ ראתה אפילו תוך שיעור זה לא מיקרי רואה מחמת תשמיש וכל זה פשוט וכן ביאר הגאון אמ"ו ז"ל בתשובה בארוכה עיין שם ואני מוסיף דכן משמע להדיא מדברי הראב"ד בס' בעלי הנפש ומביאו ב"י וז"ל הרואה דם מחמת תשמיש כו' אסורה לשמש והוא שבדקה עצמה כדי שתושיט ידה לתחת הכר כלומר בכדי שיהא בעלה באשם תלוי אבל אחר כך הרי הוא כמעת לעת שבנדה שאינה מטמאה בועלה ואינה מוחזקת ברואה מחמת תשמיש כו' עכ"ל מבואר להדיא דאע"ג דלא בדקה עדיין כלל מ"מ אחר שיעור דכדי שתושיט ידה אינה מוחזקת ברואה מחמת תשמיש ועוד דהא קאמר הרי הוא כמעל"ע שבנדה כו' והתם מיירי שלא בדקה עדיין דאם בדקה אינה מטמאה למפרע מעל"ע אלא מפקידה לפקידה ולפי זה לדידן דקי"ל דכל סמוך לתשמיש מקרי רואה מחמת תשמיש וכמ"ש הרב היינו דוקא בידוע בודאי שראתה סמוך לתשמיש אבל אם במופלג אחר התשמיש בדקה עצמה ומצאה דם אע"ג די"ל דראתה בשעת תשמיש מותרת וכ"ש באשה שראתה ג"פ בבוקר בליל טבילתה כשקמה ממטתה מצאה על כתונת שלה ריבוי דם שהיא מותרת. שוב פסק הגאון אמ"ו ז"ל בתשובתו שם דאף אם תשמש אשה זו עוד פעם אחרת או ב' ותמצא מיד בשעת תשמיש מותרת ולא אמרינן אגלאי מילתא למפרע שהיה ג"כ בשעת תשמיש אלא ימים הראשונים יפלו ומכאן והלאה חושבנא וכל זה ברור לא הוצרכתי לכתבו אלא להוציא מלב הקורא בדברי הרב שחלק על תשובת הגאון אמ"ו ז"ל בענין זה והדבר פשוט דכל היכא דלא מיקרי רואה מחמת תשמיש אפילו ראתה כמה וכמה פעמים מותרת וכן הוא בדברי האחרונים וכ"כ בתשובת מהר"מ מלובלין סימן כ"ב דאין חילוק בין שמשה ג"פ או מ' ונ' פעמים ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****Immediately in the measure of, etc. Meaning if she saw immediately within this measure even though we say she saw after relations — nevertheless since she was liable for a guilt-offering dependent it is called seeing blood due to relations; but after this measure she is pure even though we say she saw earlier — she is removed from the category of seeing blood due to relations; so implies among poskim. But if she examined immediately after relations and found nothing, and afterward saw even within this measure — it is not called seeing blood due to relations; and all this is simple. And so the Gaon A.M.U. z"l explained at length in a responsum — see there. And I add that so implies explicitly from Raavad in Sefer Ba'al HaNefesh, brought by Beit Yosef: one who sees blood due to relations, etc. — forbidden to have relations; and this is when she examined herself in the measure that she extends her hand under the pillow — meaning so her husband would be liable for guilt-offering dependent; but afterward behold it is like from time to time in niddah who is not tamei retroactively to her husband and is not established as seeing blood due to relations, etc. — end quote; explicitly clear that even though she did not yet examine at all — nevertheless after the measure of extending her hand she is not established as seeing blood due to relations; and further for he says behold it is like from time to time in niddah, etc. — and there it deals where she did not yet examine, for if she examined she is not tamei retroactively from time to time but from deposit to deposit. And per this, for us who hold all near relations is called seeing blood due to relations, as the Rabbi wrote — specifically when known with certainty she saw near relations; but if long after relations she examined herself and found blood, even though we say she saw at time of relations — she is permitted; and all the more a woman who saw three times in the morning on the night of her immersion — when she rose from her bed she found on her garment an abundance of blood — she is permitted. Again the Gaon A.M.U. z"l ruled in his responsum there that even if this woman has relations again once or twice and finds immediately at time of relations — she is permitted; and we do not say the matter was revealed retroactively; rather the first days fall away and from here onward we calculate. And all this is clear — I did not need to write it except to remove from the reader's heart the Rabbi's words who disagreed with the Gaon A.M.U.'s responsum on this matter. And the matter is simple: wherever it is not called seeing blood due to relations, even if she saw many times — she is permitted; and so in words of the Acharonim; and so in responsum Maharam of Lublin siman 22 — no distinction whether she had relations three times or forty and fifty times — see there.

**** ENGLISH ****
And if it happened to her etc. Meaning: if it happened to her that she saw immediately after giving birth three consecutive times, and afterward she went ahead and had relations in the interim and did not see, and then returned and saw three consecutive times after a subsequent birth and did not again have relations — she is permitted; and likewise even if she saw three consecutive times after each birth, but afterward between each birth she had relations in the interim and did not see, and after the third or fourth birth she returned and saw three consecutive times and did not again have relations — she is permitted; but if she saw three consecutive times after a birth — she is not permitted to have relations afterward ab initio; for we permit only when afterward she went ahead and had relations and was established with permitted relations following the prohibited ones, and we attribute to weakness of childbirth — even though afterward she returned and saw three times after giving birth and did not afterward have relations; but if she had relations afterward and did not see — even if she originally saw three consecutive times not at the time of childbirth — she is pure; for this is superior to the tube examination. Such is the explanation of the Rabbi's words; and so appears in Eitan HaEzrachi as I explained — see there. And this is proven explicitly in the responsum of R. Yochanan there who wrote: at first glance it appears from his saying in the third birth, in this matter without change — implying that in the third birth as well, etc. — implying all births were exactly equal, and the questioner did not come to ask until after the third birth, and in the third birth necessarily they were consecutive — for otherwise it is obvious she is permitted to everyone even without birth, for we specifically require consecutive, as in the responsum there and as above seif 1; and there was no need for the respondent to find a reason for the childbirth permission; if so it implies the first three times as well were consecutive — see there and investigate; and Bach explained the Rabbi's view that she saw after the birth three times but they were not consecutive, etc., and wrote that so implies from R. Yochanan's responsum — but the opposite is true, as I explained; and apart from this, there is no logic to say so, for since she is permitted after the last birth even if they were consecutive — what does it matter whether the first relations were consecutive or not; and all this appears to me clear.
