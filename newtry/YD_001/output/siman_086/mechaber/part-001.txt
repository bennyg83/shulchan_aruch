The signs of eggs and their laws. In it are 10 seifim: Signs of eggs to recognize that they are from a pure fowl — if both ends are rounded or pointed, or if the white was outside and the yolk inside — it is known that it is an egg of an impure fowl; and even if the seller says they are from a pure fowl, we do not rely on him.
