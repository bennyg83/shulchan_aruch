<b>ואוסר ממנו כדי נטילה. </b>כתב רש"ל פכ"ה סי' ע' שזהו לפי סברת הטור דס"ל בחם דצלי דיו בכדי נטילה אבל בש"ד פסק לאסור בצונן לתוך חם בכולו וכן יש לפסוק ואפי' לסברת הטור יש להחמיר כיון דבשומן מפעפע בכולו וא"א בקיאין איזה מיקרי כחוש ואיזה מיקרי שומן ע"כ והך דם אחר שזכר המחבר פי' דם בעין לאפוקי דם הנפלט ע"י מליחה או צלייה דביה אמרי' מישרק שריק. ושיעור כדי נטילה הוא כעובי אצבע:
**** ENGLISH ****
And forbids amount of netilah. Rashal chapter 25 siman 70 wrote this is per Tur's reasoning that hot roast suffices with netilah; but in ShaDa ruled forbid cold into hot entirely; and so to rule; and even per Tur one may be stringent since in fat it spreads throughout and we are not expert what is called lean and what fat — end; and this other blood Mechaber explains blood in the eye — excluding blood exuded by salting or roasting regarding which we say it is drawn. Measure of netilah is like finger thickness.

**** ENGLISH ****
After meat stopped dripping, etc. Implies if removed from fire while meat still drips — no prohibition since it exudes and does not absorb; what absorbs it releases; therefore lenient ones are Rosh — Ran brings Beit Yosef: possible on spit too as it absorbs so it releases — do not be so stringent — cooked blood no violation; all the more permitted while it has what to exude and removed from fire; reason while fire heat on meat then meat exudes internal blood outside and when that heat cooled does not exude — blood of limbs that did not separate permitted; Maharach wrote Rosh agrees if not half roasted and removed from fire — spit knife and roast forbidden — wondrous — all permitted; daily practice when roasting unsalted meat do not remove spit with meat from fire to adjust fire.
