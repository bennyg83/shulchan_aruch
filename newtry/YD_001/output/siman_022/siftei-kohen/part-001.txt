also examination is required whether the membrane was perforated—end; if so, regarding veins that certainly separate—for they are hard—and furthermore because of this reason the blood in them is considered emitted blood regarding which we say as it absorbs so it expels as written in s.k. 6 (for regarding blood in the eye we do not say as it absorbs so it expels as below siman 69 and 70 and 72 and 76 and many places)—if so, the same if it was cooked, for they separate so that it does not hurry to absorb more than other pieces in the pot; and it is similar to what we say siman 72 s.k. 7 that even if there is 60 against the heart the heart itself is forbidden because sometimes the blood dries inside it and does not emerge outside, and it is not forbidden from the reason the piece becomes neveilah—for regarding this its separation helps (meaning its portions) that it does not hurry to absorb more than other flesh—and the same here; for although regarding heart adhered to the bird we require there 60 from the bird itself—that is because there is blood in the flesh of the heart itself too, which is not so in veins—and understand well.
