**** ENGLISH ****
The law of Grace after Meals in the house of mourning. And it contains 5 seifim. When they bless Grace after Meals in the house of mourning, they say the fourth blessing in this wording: Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, God, our Father, our King, our Creator, our Redeemer, our Holy One, holy Jacob, the King, the living and enduring God, the good and doer of good, the true God, the true Judge, Who judges in righteousness, etc.: {Rama: (And some omit the words "the good and doer of good" (Maharil).)}
**** END BLOCK ****

<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="5"></i>בשבת אם בירך ביחיד או בג' אבלים מברך כדרך שמברך בחול דצינעא הוא ואם אכלו אחרים עמו וברכו אין מזכירין מעין המאורע דכיון דאיכא אחרים <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="5"></i>לאו צינעה הוא:
**** ENGLISH ****
On Shabbat, if the mourner blessed alone or three mourners blessed — he blesses as he blesses on a weekday, for it is private; but if others ate with him and they blessed, they do not mention the matter of the event, for since there are others it is not private.
