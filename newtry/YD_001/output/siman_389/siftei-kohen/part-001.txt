**** ENGLISH ****And the world is accustomed to forbid it — that is, they are concerned for Riv'a and his supporters who forbade laundering all thirty; and it seems in Tosafot Mo'ed Katan 23a that he forbids only laundered with natron and sand, not in water alone; Bach wrote: accordingly for laundered in water alone one need not dress him afterward first; and obviously permitted immediately after seven.
