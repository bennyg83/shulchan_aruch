**** ENGLISH ****Until its fifth. Beit Yosef in name of Rosh: reason no appreciation regarding ingot — if returns to ingot fifth diminishes for smelting loss and smith's wage — end quote; and written in Derishah: he said to him — if it were from smelting his loan would also be less; one can answer he said I would not smelt my silver but take it in kind and your silver in kind has no superiority for spending — end quote. In my opinion his words have no explanation — why consider old coin as in kind and new as smelted; nevertheless he profits now making vessel from it; Levush answered we are not particular — rabbis established even old coin had some heavy ones exceeding fifth over others and can select from heavy ones — not precise; for then why need Rosh's reason about fifth diminishing in smelting; truly Beit Yosef has this reason there are heavy ones, etc., Rashba stated in responsum and he does not hold Rosh's reason we mentioned; Baal Levush opened with Rosh's reason and ended with Rashba's — not correct; rather thus explanation: what Gemara challenges "behold he praises regarding ingot" — not asking if old I would smelt and make small vessel and now large vessel — whatever you say new superior to old from aspect you spoil quantity and make other thing — I also say if you give those fruits I bought for old I can strive now for new coin — fruits did not depreciate because of them but everyone not concerned for this superiority unless coin in kind; rather Gemara's essence challenge: borrower tells lender behold I take new and smelt and make old coin like you gave and silver piece remains — on this challenges "behold praises regarding ingot" — meaning what remains small ingot; answers well if you come to smelt and thicken coin you need hire smith and also smelting loss — you profit nothing — so appears clear and correct.

<b>מנכה לו כל התוספת. </b>ולא סגי בנכוי עד החומש דכיון דחייב לנכות מנכה את כולו דוגמא לזה איתא בהמוכר פירות ובח"מ סי' רכ"ט אם בא לנכות מנכה את כולו:
**** ENGLISH ****
He deducts the entire addition. And it does not suffice to deduct only up to the fifth, for since he is obligated to deduct, he deducts all of it. Analogy for this is in "the seller of fruits" and in CM siman 229: if he comes to deduct, he deducts all of it.

<b>וה"ה למלוה כו'. </b>בטור סיים וכן הדין אם נושאים ונותנים כו' פי' דלאו דוקא דרך הלואה אלא הוא הדין דרך משא ומתן וכלל בזה שני דברים האחד אם הוסיפו או פחתו על המשקל דהיינו שהזקק ניתוסף או נפחת והשני שנעשה שינוי לענין עירוב עם נחושת בכל גווני אם לא נעשה שינוי לענין פירות או שאר דברים שנותנין עבורו לא ינכה עד חומשיתו:
**** ENGLISH ****
And likewise the lender, etc. The Tur concludes: and likewise is the law if they deal in commerce, etc. — meaning not specifically by way of loan but likewise by way of commerce. And he includes in this two matters: the one, if they added or diminished in weight, i.e., the denomination was added or reduced; and the second, that a change was made in the matter of mixing with copper. In all cases, if no change was made regarding fruits or other things given for it, he deducts only up to its fifth.
