**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of one who lends on a coin and they increased it; it contains 1 se’if. One who lends his fellow on a coin, and they added to its weight: if produce became cheaper because of the addition, he deducts for him the measure of the addition — even if they added to it anything at all. But if produce did not become cheaper because of the addition, he deducts for him only [as follows]: he gives him from the coin that is in circulation at that time. To what does this apply? When they added to it up to its fifth — for example, its weight was 4 and they made it 5. But if they added to it more than its fifth, he deducts for him the entire addition, even though produce did not become cheaper. And the same law applies to one who lends on a coin and they reduced from it. {Rama: And see in Choshen Mishpat siman 74, when one can return the coin that he lent him, even though it was disqualified. And if produce became cheaper because of the new coin and they did not add to it, he gives him the new coin (Gemara). And where they made a new coin and we do not know if they added or reduced a fifth, we rely on expert non-Jew craftsmen who are knowledgeable about this, speaking innocently, or on the officials appointed over this (Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer HaTerumos). And if the king decreed that anyone who repays must repay with the new coin, we follow the king’s decree, for dina d’malchusa dina, and in this there is neither משום interest nor a prohibition of theft (Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban; and see seif katan 8); and see Choshen Mishpat siman 74.}
