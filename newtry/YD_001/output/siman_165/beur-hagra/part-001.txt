<b>וה"ה למלוה כו'. </b>הרי"ף וש"פ דלא כהראב"ד שכתב דוקא והוסיפו משום רבית וכבר הקשה עליו בסה"ת וכתב וסברא רחוקה היא מאד שזה ישתכר וזה יפסיד הקרן שלו שיחזיר לו חמשים בעד מנה וכ"כ עליו הרא"ש שם:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The same applies to a loan, etc.</b> This is the view of the Rif and the other decisors, unlike the Raavad, who wrote that the ruling applies specifically in the stated case and that the authorities added the loan case because of interest. Sefer HaTerumos already challenged him and wrote: This is a very remote rationale, that one party should profit while the other loses his principal, returning fifty to him in place of one hundred. The Rosh there likewise challenges him.
