**** HEBREW ****
<b>מדעתו. </b> כתב הש"ך ואם עבר ומלו שלא מדעתו היכא שהאב הוא מוהל חייב ליתן לו י' זהובים כמ"ש בח"מ ס"ס שפ"ב:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>With his consent.</b> Shach writes that if someone circumcised the child without the father's consent, and the father himself is a mohel, the one who performed the circumcision must pay him ten gold coins, as stated in Choshen Mishpat at the end of siman 382.
