**** ENGLISH ****
Matters that are forbidden because of “a man shall not wear the garment of a woman”; it contains 6 se’ifim. One who removes the hair of the armpit and the pubic area, even with scissors like a razor, they would strike him “makkas mardus.” To what does this apply? In a place where only women remove it, so that he not adorn himself with women’s adornment; but in a place where men also remove it, if he removed it, they do not strike him. {Rama: And even l'chatchila it is permitted (Ran, perek 2 of Avodah Zarah), only that chaverim refrain everywhere (there; and in Beit Yosef in the name of Nimukei Yosef) (and see above siman 156).} And it is permitted to remove hair of other limbs with scissors in every place.

**** ENGLISH ****
A woman should not adorn herself with the adornments of a man — for example, that she place upon her head a turban or a hat, or wear armor and similar (from the garments of a man according to the custom of that place), or that she shave her head like a man. And a man should not adorn himself with the adornments of a woman — for example, that he wear dyed garments and gold jewelry — in a place where they do not wear those vessels and do not place that jewelry except women. {Rama: And even with one of the garments it is forbidden, even though by their other garments it is recognizable that he is a man or a woman (Beit Yosef). A tumtum and an androgynus are forbidden to wrap themselves like a woman.}

**** ENGLISH ****
It is forbidden (for a man) to pluck even one white hair from among the black ones because of “a man shall not wear the garment of a woman.” And likewise, it is forbidden for a man to dye (white hairs, that they should be) black — even one hair. And likewise, it is forbidden for a man to look in a mirror. (And see above siman 156.)
