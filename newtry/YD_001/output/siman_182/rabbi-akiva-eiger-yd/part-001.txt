(סמן קפ"ב סעיף א') גם האנשים. בפרישה כתוב נראה דכל אנשים גוים דכיון דנהוג נהג ולמדין ממנהג ההוא דעובדי כוכבים. גם י"ל דעל ישראל קאמר דהיכא דנהיגי כולם אין מוחין בהם עכ"ל ועי' בתשובת פרח שושן (כלל ו' ס"ב):
**** ENGLISH ****(Siman 182 seif 1) Also men. In Perishah it is written that it appears all men are gentiles, since a custom was practiced and they learn from that custom of idolaters; also one can say he speaks of Israel, that where they all practice it one does not protest against them — end of his words; and see in the responsum Perach Shoshan (kelal 6 seif 2).

**** ENGLISH ****(Seif 6) And likewise it is forbidden for a man to look in a mirror — but only where women alone look; but in a place where men also look there is no prohibition, only the pious abstain from this; and like that of seif 1. Responsum Ganat Veradim (kelal 5 siman 12).
