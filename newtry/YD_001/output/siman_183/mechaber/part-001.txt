**** ENGLISH ****
A woman who sees a drop of blood must sit 7 clean days; it contains 1 se’if. A woman from whom blood came out from her source — whether under duress or willingly — is tamei, and this is when she feels its emergence. However, from when she feels that it was uprooted from its place and came out, she is tamei, even though it did not come out externally; and even if she saw only a drop of blood like a mustard seed, she sits on it 7 clean days, as will be explained below in siman 196. {Rama: And there is no difference between an unmarried woman and a married woman regarding the prohibition of niddah (Rivash, siman 422, brought by Beit Yosef), for anyone who has relations with a niddah is liable for kareis.}
