**** ENGLISH ****Two gold coins, etc. — it is written in Hagahot Darkei Moshe that it is not less than one Reichsthaler and two-thirds Reichsthaler as in their time; the Reichsthaler was thirty groschen, etc., and now it is five Polish gold coins; and in Semag siman 88 he wrote that an eighth of a pfennig counts as money for kiddushin for judges; and one can say that in their days produce and acquisitions were cheap and for a perutah they bought much produce, therefore the perutah was significant as money; and accordingly nowadays when one cannot buy with a perutah but a tiny amount, kiddushin of a woman does not suffice with a perutah, nor pidyon haben with two gold coins — end quote; and I wonder: granted we could say kiddushin does not suffice with a perutah, nevertheless why should pidyon haben not suffice with two gold coins, for it is significant money? However in Sefer Maadan Ma'aseh page 268a he explained at length that we require three Reichsthaler, and wrote that even according to the Rav it is now not less than two R.T. — see there; and ab initio good to satisfy all.

<b>נתן כו' עד פדוי. </b>הוא לשון הרמב"ם ומ"ש והוא שישוה הוא לשון המחבר ע"פ מה שהביא בב"י דבריו אבל באמת דבריו דחוקים מאד גם מה שתי' הב"ק דחוק מאד גם דברי רבינו ירוחם בזה הם דחוקים אלא צ"ל דדעת המחבר כדעת הלכות גדולות דף קל"ט ע"ב דגריס אמר רבא לעולם דלא שוי ורישא דאמר אבי הבן וסיפא דקבליה כהן כי הא דרב כהנא פריק בסודרא אמר לדידי שוה ה' סלעים אמר רב אשי ולא אמרן אלא גברא רבא כרב כהנא דידע למחולי אבל כ"ע לא ש"מ כל היכא דקבליה כהן בנו פדוי וכן הלכה עכ"ל והשתא אתי שפיר הא דקתני בבריית' סתמא עגל זה בה' סלעים לפדיון בני דאלו לדעת הב"י וסייעתו ה"ל לפרושי דמיירי במה שדרכו להתייקר וזה ברור ודוק:
**** ENGLISH ****He gave, etc., until redeemed — this is Rambam's wording; and what he wrote "and that it is worth" is the Mechaber's wording according to what Beit Yosef brought his words; but in truth his words are very strained; also what he wrote Bava Kamma is very strained; also Rabbenu Yerucham's words here are strained; rather the Mechaber's view must be as Halachot Gedolot page 139b where the text reads: Rava said always it is not worth; and the first part that says the father of the son and the conclusion that the kohen accepted it — like Rav Kahana redeemed with a sudra, said to him it is worth five sela to me; Rav Ashi said we only said this regarding a great man like Rav Kahana who knows to waive, but for everyone it does not help — wherever the kohen accepted it his son is redeemed; and such is the halachah — end quote; and now it is resolved what the baraita teaches plainly "this calf at five sela for my son's redemption," for according to Beit Yosef and his supporters he should have explained it deals with what is accustomed to become expensive, and this is clear — requires study.

<b>אבל לא יתן הוא לכהן על מנת שיחזיר כו'. </b>כלומר שאינו מפרש דבריו שנותן לו ע"מ להחזיר דבכה"ג כיון דשעה א' גמר בלבו שיהא לשם מתנה יצא ידי נתינה וכמ"ש המחבר בסוף הסעיף אלא מיירי שאינו גומר בדעתו כלל ליתנו לו לשם מתנה אלא מיד דעתו שנותנו לו על מנת להחזיר בכה"ג לא יצא וכן מחלק המרדכי פ"ק דקדושין בשם ראבי"ה ומביאו ד"מ וכה"ג חלק בתשובת רשב"א סי' קצ"ח וכך היא דעת הרמב"ם פ' י"א מהל' בכורים דין ח' וגם העט"ז נראה שהבין כן ע"כ כתב במקום מ"ש הרב ודעת הכהן היתה מתחלה כו' אע"פ שדעת הכהן כו' וכן נראה מהדרישה שדעת הרב כן אבל אין נ"ל דעת הרב כן אלא נראה שהוא מפרש דעת המחבר דברישא כיון שלא פירש דבריו שנותן ע"מ להחזיר א"כ דעת הכהן היה לקבלם במתנה גמורה ולא ע"מ להחזיר והאב היה דעתו שיחזירם לו הלכך אינו פדוי אבל בסיפא כיון שפירש דבריו ואמר שנותן לו ע"מ להחזיר א"כ כיון שגם דעת הכהן כן יצא וכן פירש הב"ח דעת התוס' והרא"ש כן וגם דברי הרמב"ם יש לפרש כן וכן משמע ממה שנרשם בש"ע אמ"ש הרב ודעת הכהן כו' כן משמע בב"י ובב"י לא נזכר כלום רק הביא דברי התוס' והרא"ש הנ"ל מיהו אינו מוכרח דעת התוספות והרא"ש כן דמ"ש וי"ל דר' חנינא לא היה רוצה לזכות אלא במתנה גמורה ר"ל שהיה עושה כן כדי שיתנו לו הכל במתנה גמורה אבל בדיעבד פדוי בכל ענין וכן הבין ר' ירוחם נתיב א' סוף ח"ג דבריהם ומה דקאמר בש"ס הלכך אין בנך פדוי היינו שר' חנינא א"ל כן כדי שיתנו במתנה גמורה מ"מ דעת הרב נ"ל ברור כדפי' ודעת המחבר נ"ל ברור כדפי' דס"ל כראבי"ה וכתשובת הרשב"א ודוק:
**** ENGLISH ****But he should not give the kohen on condition that he return, etc. — meaning he does not clarify his words that he gives on condition to return; in such a case, since at one moment he decided in his heart it be for a gift, he fulfilled giving as the Mechaber wrote at the end of the seif; rather it deals where he does not decide at all to give as a gift but immediately his intent is to give on condition to return — in such a case it does not take effect; and Mordekhai first chapter of Kiddushin in the name of Ravyah divides thus and Darkei Moshe brings it; and similarly divided in Rashba's responsum siman 298; and such is Rambam chapter 11 of Laws of Bikkurim law 8; and Taz appears to have understood so — therefore wrote where the Rav wrote "and the kohen's intent was initially," etc., even though the kohen's intent, etc.; and likewise appears from Darkei Moshe that the Rav holds so; but it does not seem to me the Rav holds so; rather he explains the Mechaber's view in the first part: since he did not clarify he gives on condition to return, the kohen's intent was to accept as absolute gift and not on condition to return, and the father intended he return them — therefore not redeemed; but in the conclusion, since he clarified and said he gives on condition to return, since the kohen's intent is likewise it takes effect; and Bach explained Tosafot and Rosh hold so; and Rambam's words can be explained so; and likewise implied from what is recorded in Shulchan Aruch as the Rav wrote "and the kohen's intent," etc.; so implied in Beit Yosef; and Beit Yosef mentions nothing except Tosafot and Rosh's words above; however it is not necessary that Tosafot and Rosh hold so, for what they wrote one can say R. Chanina did not want to acquire except through absolute gift — meaning he did so so they give him everything as absolute gift; but after the fact redeemed in every case; and Rabbenu Yerucham netiv 1 end part 3 understood their words; and what the Gemara says "therefore your son is not redeemed" is that R. Chanina told him so in order they give as absolute gift; nevertheless the Rav's view seems clear as he explained, and the Mechaber's view clear as he explained, for he holds like Ravyah and Rashba's responsum — requires study.

**** ENGLISH ****And the father cannot redeem through an agent, etc. — this is surprising, for we hold throughout the Torah one's agent is as himself; and likewise explained in Ran chapter Kol She'ah that one can redeem through an agent; and in Sefer Maadan Ma'aseh end of page 266 he wondered on this, explaining at length one can redeem through an agent; and in Tzeda LaDerech that Mahar Ish Sakhar composed on Rashi in Chumash he explained there in one responsum that several gedolei ha'dor agreed one can redeem through an agent; and wrote even Rivash only said we find no Torah obligation on another person to make himself agent in the father's place to redeem if the father does not want or if the father died, and even beit din are not obligated from Torah to make themselves agents in the father's place; but certainly the father can redeem through an agent, and beit din can redeem without the father if the father transgressed and did not redeem or died before redemption, and all the more the father's father can redeem in such a case — end quote; and see there he explained at length; and likewise Maharshal in the name of Maharam Matz and brought in Darkei Moshe seif 14 that one can redeem through an agent. And in Sefer Maadan Ma'aseh he wrote there beit din cannot redeem when the father died while the son is a minor, for as long as minor he is not obligated, etc.; and his words are not compelling; and see above s.k. 20.
