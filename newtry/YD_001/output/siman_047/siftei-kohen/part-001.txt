<b>כענף מן הבד. </b>דוקא שיוצאים מן הקיבה או מן הקורקבן וניכר שהוא מעי יתר שדומה לשני מעיים יוצאין אבל יוצא מן הדקין כשר דכיון דמן הקיבה אינו יוצא רק מעי א' א"כ דק היוצא מן הדקין לא חשיב יתר כמ"ש בס"ק ז':
**** ENGLISH ****
Like a branch from the trunk. — Specifically when they emerge from the kivah or from the kurkavan, and it is recognizable that it is an extra intestine similar to two intestines emerging; but if it emerges from the dekin — kosher, for since from the kivah only one intestine emerges, therefore the thin one emerging from the dekin is not considered extra, as the Shach wrote in s.k. 7:

<b>כאורך אצבע כו'. </b>וה"ה יותר וכן מבואר בתשו' הרא"ש ומביאו ב"י וע' בב"י וד"מ מ"ש בשם הכל בו:
**** ENGLISH ****
The hadra dekaneta, etc. — Maharshal there challenged this law and wrote that it contradicts seif 1; and he wrote that Rosh in his rulings (who wrote as in seif 1) retracted from what he wrote in his responsa on this law; and also Bach and Derishah strained themselves to reconcile the matters. And in my humble opinion, for the Mechaber in seif 1 it is specifically when two intestines emerge — treifah; and likewise when they are not attached below for a finger-width; nevertheless it is recognizable that two intestines emerge as one — treifah. But here behold only one intestine emerges, only that afterward, far from the kivah, a small intestine emerges from that intestine; and so is proven in Ra'avan's responsa siman 21 (whence is the source of this law), that one may distinguish thus — and so it is there: and my teachers R' Kalonymus son of R' Yehudah and R' Elyakim son of R' Yosef permitted, and I after them and the rest of the sages of the community, for they said the Sages declared treifah only when two intestines emerge from two places like in fowl where two thin ones emerge from the kurkavan — one goes to the zefek and the other is the dekin; but here the thin one emerging from the dekin is it, and this is not comparable in fowl. And again once such an incident came before me; R' Shmuel his son-in-law ruled before me in halacha and told me he cannot learn from the sugya neither prohibition nor permission, for behold it does not emerge from one place and it ends until like a finger that it should return and become mixed at one head in the dekin; and I answered him: if two thin ones were emerging from one place, then they would need to return until like a finger and become mixed; but with one emerging — not needed, end of his words. Behold he distinguishes thus in his responsa, and this is Rosh's view and Tur and Mechaber; and so is implied in Orach Chaim kelal 55 din 7 that he distinguishes as I wrote. However Ra'avan wrote there afterward, and so it is: and he answered me: since it is explained that it emerges from two places, and from one place is the kivah — there the one is its way and the two that are not its way are extra — one may say that from the dekin even one extra is extra; and I said in my heart: perhaps it is forbidden; and I found an explanation corresponding to them in fowl, not like our rabbis who explained on the kurkavan, but two intestines emerging from the intestine emerge from the kurkavan, etc., until "and my teachers' words stood who permitted for one intestine like a finger emerging," end of his words. However his teachers who explained on the kurkavan — perforce he does not hold thus, but holds that in two dekin emerging the one is extra and is considered as removed; but the thin one emerging from the dekin is not extra but merely hangs; and likewise we say below siman 55 seif 4 regarding extra leg — and this is Rosh's, Tur's, and Orach Chaim's view; and so is explained in Mahari Weil's responsa there that we rule like this ruling of Rosh's responsa. Nevertheless, where there is no great loss, one should forbid the thin one emerging from the dekin, per R' Shmuel his son-in-law's view; and also in Mordechai he wrote that Rif and R' Chananel disagree on this ruling; nevertheless it is clear that even for Ra'avan it is not kosher except when one thin one emerges from the dekin, but with two dekin emerging from the intestine — treifah:
