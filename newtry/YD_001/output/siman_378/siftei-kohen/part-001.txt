**** ENGLISH ****Where, etc. — that is, even where they make two yom tov days and the second counts toward the seven, as explained in Orach Chayyim siman 548 seif 2; and below siman 399 (text reads 13): he needs habra'ah afterward — for it is not rejected — he should have habra'ah on day seven, since mourning applies to her since she counts toward seven; teaches that here too we say: since it was rejected, it was rejected and habra'ah is not needed.
