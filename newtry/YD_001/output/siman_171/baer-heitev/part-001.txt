**** ENGLISH ****And ribbit. Even ribbit that accrued from when he converted — from the moment he capitalized it on the loan, all of it is principal; but if he capitalized after he converted, even ribbit that accrued before he converted he also does not collect — Rosh and Tur; and Maggid Mishneh wrote that by law he could collect what accrued before he converted, but the Sages enacted to follow capitalization to be stringent; and Shach wrote he states it as a fortiori — all the more if he did not capitalize at all it is forbidden even for what did not convert; and so Bach and Levush, unlike Perishah — end quote.
