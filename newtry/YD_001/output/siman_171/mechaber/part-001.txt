**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of a non-Jew who lent with interest and converted; it contains 1 se’if. A Jew who borrowed money from a non-Jew with interest, and he combined it upon him as a loan, and [the non-Jew] converted: if before he converted he combined it upon him as a loan, he collects principal and interest; but if after he converted he combined it upon him as a loan, he collects the principal but not the interest. But a non-Jew who borrowed from a Jew with interest, and combined the interest upon him as a loan: even though he combined it upon him after he converted, he collects the principal and the interest, so that they not say, “This one converted for the sake of his money,” and the Jew collects from him after he converted all the money of interest that he became obligated in when he was a non-Jew.
