**** ENGLISH ****
A mourner is forbidden in Torah study. And it contains 5 seifim. A mourner all seven days is forbidden to read Torah, Prophets, Writings, Mishnah, Gemara, laws, and aggadot; and if the public needs him to teach — permitted, provided he not appoint a translator but say to another and the other to the translator and the translator announce to the public: {Rama: Or he may preach himself (Mordechai Laws of Mourning); and he may rule forbidden and permitted to an individual who asks him if there is no other and they need him (Avraham Zacuto and Rabbeinu Yerucham); but forbidden to teach halachah to his students, and so is the custom even though some are lenient (Tashbetz in the name of some say).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A mourner is permitted to read Job, lamentations, and the harsh matters in Jeremiah and laws of mourning; and this is between him and himself; but he does not study with others — rather they sit and discuss laws of mourning, and if they err he answers in a soft voice and he does not ask; and this when the public does not need him; but if the public needs him, even other laws permitted as we say.
