**** ENGLISH ****
The law of not sticking dough on unsalted meat. In it is one seif: One who coats with dough — a bird that was not salted — even though in the Gemara they distinguish between semolina and other flours and between thin dough (asmik) and dough that is not thin, we nowadays are not expert in the matter, and in every case one should forbid; but if it was salted and remained for the time of salting and afterward was rinsed, it is permitted in every manner: {Rama: And specifically coating with dough is forbidden, but one may smear with oil or meat juice on unsalted meat, for this does not impede exudation (Arukh general rule 9). And pashtida has every law of cooking in a pot, both for leniency and for stringency (Mordechai chapter 25 in the name of Rashi and Mahari and Arukh general rule 15):}
