**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of a kallah entering the chuppah; it contains 5 se’ifim. They asked her to marry and she agreed — she needs to sit 7 nekiyim, whether adult or minor, and even if she checked herself at the time of the request and found [herself] tehorah, lest because of desire she saw a drop of blood like a mustard seed and did not feel it. And she counts 7 from the day after the day of the request. And she does not need a hefsek taharah, for even though she did not check on the day of the request to stop in taharah, she counts from the next day 7 nekiyim. Nevertheless, she needs bedikah during the 7 (each day l’chatchila (Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh, and Bach that this is also the view of Ramban); nevertheless, b’dieved if she checked herself only one time during the 7, it suffices) (Beit Yosef and the later authorities).

**** ENGLISH ****
These 7 days are counted from the time she resolves in her mind and prepares herself for the chuppah, even though she was not yet betrothed. {Rama: And there is [an opinion] to rely on immersion close to the bi’ah of the mitzvah as much as possible (so it implies in Mordechai in the name of Rashbam and R”m and Rokeach in the name of his father and teachers, and in Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 11 of Hilchos Issurei Bi’ah), and the custom is to immerse the kallah on Wednesday night, even though she will not have intercourse until after Shabbos (Mordechai there). But one should not distance the immersion from the intercourse more than this; and if she will not have intercourse after Shabbos, she should check herself each day until the bi’ah of the mitzvah (there in the Hagahah, and in Tosafos, perek 1 of Yoma, daf 18). And this is specifically l’chatchila, but b’dieved we do not be stringent if she checked only one time during the 7 (Beit Yosef). And every chassan should ask the kallah, before he touches her, whether she kept 7 nekiyim (Hagahos Sha”d).}

If they postponed the marriage for some reason, even though she sat 7 nekiyim, she must again sit 7 nekiyim (and immerse) when they again agree to make the marriage. **** ENGLISH ****
If he transgressed and brought her in within this time, and likewise a chassan whose kallah became a niddah before he had intercourse with her: he should not be alone with her; rather, he sleeps among the men and she sleeps among the women. {Rama: Some say: if she was tehorah when he married her and he did not have intercourse with her, and afterward she became a niddah, she does not need further guarding (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 253, and Hagahos Alfasi), and one who is stringent will be blessed (Terumas HaDeshen there). And one should not distinguish in this between a bachur and a widower, or between a virgin and a widow (Rabbeinu Yerucham, nesiv 26). Some say that she is forbidden to be alone with him in the day as in the night, and there is no need for two guards; rather, either he among the men or she among the women (Tur in the name of Rosh). And some say that at night two guards are needed, and in the day it is permitted to be alone (Raavad). And the custom is to take a minor boy by the chassan and a minor girl by the kallah, and they are not alone in the day without a minor boy or minor girl.}
