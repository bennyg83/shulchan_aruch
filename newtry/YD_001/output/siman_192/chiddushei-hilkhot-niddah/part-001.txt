וקו' הגמ' לא ישא אדם אשה במדינה זו וכו' מ"מ קשה דלא פלוג עבדו רבנן בגזירות כנודע וא"כ קו' הגמ' שפיר דל"ל מי היא שזמנה ביום דהא בקטנה איירי וסתם קטנה אינו בחזקת רואה כנודע אך לפ"ז אין מקום לקו' התו' ביחודי מיחדי' לי' די"ל במסקנא אמרינן דגדולה נשאו דבאמת לא בעיל כלל ולא שייך בני גרושת הלב וא"כ בגדולה שפיר י"ל מאן הוי לי' ליומא כנ"ל מי הוא שז' לנדות' היום כנ"ל ולק"מ:
**** ENGLISH ****She was claimed for marriage, etc. Let us say from desire, etc. See Taz s.k. 1 that his view is if immediately when they reconciled she was with him in bed she is permitted to him — and the matter requires study; at first glance from where is this novelty, for there is concern immediately at time of claim she sees a drop of blood and it is lost — and who gave a measure how long she should stay? And Rav Baal Sha'ar Efrayim challenged Taz on this. A) And I found justification in Tosafot in chapter HaChalitz and Yoma that challenged on what we say "Rabbis — yichudei — he isolates her for him" — behold forbidden to isolate; and answered not "isolate for him" only general isolation so they have bread in his basket — if so there is no desire — see there. And this matter is difficult — from bread in his basket, behold it is impossible for them to take at all for the day, for immediately when he wished to take her there will be a new claim, for initially he did not take her only for isolation — if so afterward when she claims for relations, behold a new claim and she needs 7 clean days and is forbidden to him — if so there is no bread in his basket at all; and what did they accomplish by isolating them, for never will he be permitted to have relations with her until he waits 7 clean days and there is no bread in his basket; and it is clear like Taz's view that if she claimed and immediately he had relations he does not need 7 clean days; and if so one resolves that initially there was no claim for her husband and bread in his basket exists here — at night when he desires to have relations he will claim her and immediately have relations, and there is no concern of desire and she is permitted to him and it is properly bread in his basket. And perhaps Tosafot's view is like Ra'avyah who holds if she checked herself immediately at claim and did not find, she is permitted; and his words must be that why does the Gemara challenge on those who announce "who will be for the day" — behold Rav said "she was claimed for marriage", etc. — perhaps she was immediately checking; the Gemara's question would be they should announce thus and not simply "who will be for the day"; and perhaps women would not notice they answer to check immediately — they should announce who will be for the day they should check themselves; if so the Gemara answers properly that therefore they did not announce, for they only isolated and there is no heat and desire here; nevertheless there is bread in his basket, for if he wishes to have relations he will claim her and tell her to be careful to check immediately and she is permitted to him like Ra'avyah's view. And behold Taz challenged on Boaz and Tamar; and regarding Tamar plainly then this decree did not exist; and Boaz we do not find he had relations then at all — and I do not know what is difficult for him; on the contrary this is Boaz's strength. However regarding David it is difficult for him — possibly all delay that was on Bathsheba in divorce document and the like — nevertheless behold she was claimed for marriage; but also this requires study, for before R. Zeira's stringency on the day of her 7th of niddah she is permitted in the evening — what of it that she saw blood? Even if flowing all 7, only at evening she stops, immerses at night and is permitted; if so David when she was claimed for Boaz was on the 7th day as written "and she bathes from her impurity" — she had combing; if so even though then she had desire and saw, nevertheless at night when she immersed in water she is permitted to David — requires study. But one can challenge — why does the Gemara challenge "who will be for the day" — behold Rav said "she was claimed for marriage", etc. — perhaps the announcement was thus "who will be for the day" — namely the woman whose today is 7th of her niddah; if so she can marry him and at night be permitted to him in such case; and this is the precision in "who will be for the day" — the day happened ready she can become betrothed to him. And one can resolve — certainly they did not take an adult woman lest she become pregnant and be in category of sons of a divorcee in heart — for their intent was to divorce her, for although not in category of "do not devise evil against your neighbor", etc. when marrying with intent as known — nevertheless in category of sons of divorcee as Ran wrote, for although not hated; and it is clear they take minors not fit to bear; and the Gemara's question — a man should not marry a woman in this province, etc. — nevertheless difficult, for we do not distinguish in their decrees as known; if so the Gemara's question properly — who is she whose time is the day? For it deals with a minor; and a minor is not presumed to see as known; but per this there is no place for Tosafot's question on yichudei miyachadei leih — one can say in conclusion we say they married an adult, for truly he did not have relations at all and sons of divorcee in heart does not apply; if so with adult woman properly one can say "who will be for him for the day" as stated — who is whose 7th of niddot is today as stated; requires study.

אם דחו נשואין וכו' אע"פ שבדקה וכו' הרב הש"ך בסק"ז הביא בשם מש"ב דאם הוקבע הזמן לר"ח ניסן ובא אח"כ ציר דאתילד להחתן אונס וביום יומי' יבא וכן הי' דא"צ נקי' חדשים אפי' לא בדקה עצמה בימים שבנתיים כי לא נתייאשה מן ז' נקי' ראשונים וסמכה דעתה וכו' ודברים אלו יש בהן מסברא וכמ"ש הש"ך דאין להחמיר בדברים אלו איברא מה שהתנה המ"ב תנאי שלא יהי' עוברים ז' ימים בלי בדיקה וכן העתיקן הש"ך וכן הרב מ"ב בתשובה כפל הדברים ע"ש. ב) דבר זה אין מובן לי כלל הגבול ימים אלו מה טיבן ממנ"פ אם אתה עושה לזה הביאה של החתן תביעה חדשה אם לא עבר אפי' יום א' הרי כאן תביעה חדשה וחימוד חדש ואם אמרינן דלא נתייאש מן תביעה ראשונה א"כ אף דעברה ז' ימים ולא בדקה מ"מ הא כבר מנה ז' ימים בזמן ראשון ואם אתה מצריך לה בדיקה אח"כ א"כ אדרבה בנשאת בפחות מז' יותר ג) יש לחוש לאיסורא דאולי ראתה ולא עברו כלל אפי' ימי נדה דאוריית' כלל של דבר לאחר שספרה ז' נקי' לא ידעתי מה הבדל יש אם עברה ו' ימים ולא בדקה ונשאת תיכף דמותר' ואם עברו ז' ימים ולא בדקה שאסורה להנשא דמ"ש ששה משבעה אדרבה בעבר' ו' יש כאן חשש איסור דאוריית' משא"כ בשבעה דאין כאן רק חומרא דר"ז. ודאי אלו הי' דעת מ"ב שתצריך להמתין ז' ימים רק סגי בבדיקה בא' מימים הוה ניחא אבל כיון שמותרת להנשא וא"צ להמתין ז' ימים א"כ הך דעברה ז' ימים בלי בדיקה דאסורה לא ידעתי מה טעם יש בדבר ומאין אדני הדין הזה הוטבעו כי אין לו שרש ועיקר כלל בראשונים:
**** ENGLISH ****If marriage was postponed, etc. Although she checked, etc. Rav Shach in s.k. 7 brought in name of Mas'at Binyamin that if time was set for Rosh Chodesh Nisan and afterward a messenger came that an ones befell the groom on the road and in a day or two he will come — and so it was — she does not need new clean days even if she did not check herself in the days between, for she did not despair of the first 7 clean days and relied, etc.; and these words have reasoning; and as Shach wrote one need not be stringent in these matters. However what Mechaber conditioned that there not pass 7 days without bedikah, and Shach cited and Mechaber in responsum doubled the words — see there. B) This matter is not understandable to me at all — these boundary days, what is their case from a doubt? If you make for the groom's claim a new claim if not even one day passed, behold a new claim and new desire; and if we say he did not despair from the first claim — if so even though 7 days passed and she did not check, nevertheless behold he already counted 7 days at first time; and if you require bedikah afterward — if so on the contrary one who married in less than 7, more. C) There is concern for prohibition lest she saw and 7 days of Torah niddah did not pass at all — after she counted 7 clean I do not know what difference if she passed 6 days and did not check and immediately married she is permitted, and if 7 days passed and she did not check she is forbidden to marry — what is six of seven? On the contrary in passing 6 there is Torah prohibition concern, not so in seven where there is only R. Zeira's stringency. Certainly if Mechaber's view were that she must wait 7 days, bedikah on one of the days would be fine; but since she is permitted to marry and need not wait 7 days — if so this that she passed 7 days without bedikah forbidden — I do not know what reason in the matter; and from where is this law established, for it has no root and essence among the Rishonim.

עבר וכנסה וכו' א"צ שימור עוד זהו דעת הת"ה דס"ל הא דאמרינן בגמ' אבל בעל אין צריכה שימור היינו ראוי לבעול ועי' ט"ז ס"ק ז' במ"ש על זה ומדברי הרמב"ם דס"ל חופת נדה לא שמיה חופה וס"ל החופה היינו יחוד הראוי ומסור לביאה וא"כ הא דקאמרינן בגמ' וכן מי שפרסה אשה נדה הוא ישן וכו' ע"פ דהי' החופה קודם שפירסה נדה דלאח"כ לא שייך חופה וגם ע"כ דהי' מתיחד עמה דאל"כ לא שייך חופה וא"כ הרי הי' ראוי לבעילה ומ"מ אמרינן הוא ישן בין אנשים וכו' ש"מ דליתא לדינו של ת"ה ולכך יפה כתב הש"ע דהמחמיר תע"ב: 

וכתב הש"ך בס"ק י"א דעכשיו דמנהג רע בעו"ה נתפשט שאין מניחן לבעול רק אחר איזה ימים לאחר נשואין וא"כ אם פרסה נדה ודאי צריך שימור דהא דלא בעל משום מנהג וכו' עכ"ל ושגגה פלטה קולמס דא"כ אין יצרו תוקפו כ"כ עד שיוכל להעציר עצמו מבלי לעבור מנהג של שטות איך לא יעצר כח הגבורים לבל יבעול ח"ו נדה ואדרבה זהו מורה כי אין יצרו תוקפו וישמע לקול דברי חז"ל כי ראינו ששמע למנהג ואין ספק שתלמיד טועה הוסיף כן כי שפתי כהן ישמרו דעת:
**** ENGLISH ****He transgressed and entered, etc. Need not guarding — this is Torat HaShlamim's view who holds what we say in Gemara "but a husband need not guard" means fit for relations; and see Taz s.k. 7 on what he wrote; and from Rambam's words who holds chuppah of a niddah is not called chuppah and holds chuppah means isolation fit and handed over for relations — if so what we say in Gemara "and likewise one who spread a niddah woman — he sleeps", etc. — according to chuppah was before she became niddah, for afterward chuppah does not apply; and also then he was isolated with her, for otherwise chuppah does not apply; if so behold he was fit for relations; nevertheless we say he sleeps among people, etc. — learn our law of Torat HaShlamim does not exist; and therefore Shulchan Aruch wrote well — one who is stringent, praise to him.

והמנהג ליקח קטן וכו' כ' הש"ך בס"ק י"ד דצריך להיות יודעים טעם ביאה ואינו מוסרים עצמן לביאה והרב מ"י השיגו דכאן אשתו משמרתו ואפי' מוסרה עצמה לביאה ליכא כאן חשש יחוד ואפשר לומר דס"ל לב"ח כמבואר בא"ע סי' כ"ב דבלילה אפי' בעיר בעינן ג' וא"כ ודאי דבבעל ליכא חשש דאשתו משמרתו אבל הא מייחדין קטן עה הבעל ואותו קטן ג"כ איירי דיודע טעם ביאה כמ"ש הב"ח להדיא והיינו שהוא בן ט' שנים וי"א וא"כ אף הוא אסור להתייחד ובעל אינו רק שומר א' וא"כ הרי הקטן מתייחד עם הכלה ועוד בת א' גדולה ובלילה לא סגי בשומר א' ולכך מצריך שאותו קטנה לא תמסור עצמה לביאה וא"כ ליכא חשש פיתוי ולאונס ליכא חשש דא"כ תצעוק ויהי' השומרים נעורים וא"כ הרי כאן לגבי קטן נגד הכלה שני שומרי' הקטנה והבעל וא"ש משא"כ במוסרת עצמה לביאה לגבי קטן אין כאן שומר רק הבעל ובלילה בעינן ב' שומרין וזהו הטעם למנהג שלוקחין דוקא קטן וקטנה:
**** ENGLISH ****And the custom is to take a minor, etc. Shach wrote in s.k. 14 they must know the reason for relations and do not hand themselves over for relations; and Rav Mahari"y challenged him — here his wife guards him, and even if she hands herself for relations there is no concern of isolation; and one can say Bach holds as explained in Even HaEzer siman 22 that at night even in a city we require three; if so certainly with a husband there is no concern, for his wife guards him; but here they isolate a minor with the groom, and that minor also deals — he knows the reason for relations as Bach wrote explicitly — namely he is nine years and one day; if so he too is forbidden to isolate; and the husband is only one guardian; if so behold the minor isolates with the bride and another one-year-old great daughter; and at night one guardian does not suffice; therefore requires that that minor not hand herself for relations; if so no concern of enticement; and for ones there is no concern for she will cry out and the guardians will awaken; if so behold regarding minor against bride two guardians — the minor and the husband; and one resolves not so when she hands herself for relations regarding minor there is only guardian the husband; and at night we require two guardians — and this is the reason for custom that they take specifically minor and minor.
