**** ENGLISH ****
The cheeses — meaning milk was not mixed together but each made cheese separately then cheeses mixed; but if milk already mixed then cheeses made from all — nullified (and if doubt whether made from it alone — lenient, for piece fit to honor is rabbinic — Taz). And one may ask from chapter 6 seif 5 treifah egg needs sixty-one to nullify because eggs large and small — why here sixty suffices; Taz answered: in eggs Rabbanan established one suffices in measure; here they were lenient from presumption all equal. Shach answered further: in eggs because it is a living creature they added one in measure. Taz wrote: if sixty animals in herd even if we did not check others, still we say majority animals presumed kosher. See siman 101 where Taz brings Maharshal that cheese is not called piece fit to honor and he challenged — see there.

**** ENGLISH ****
In this — meaning in milk-water; but not in water of milk and urine. Milk-water is nesyova of milk; water of milk is after making cheese they cook the nesyova and floating food above and remaining water alone is water of milk; this view forbids because of eiver min hachai; and though with food permitted, nevertheless compared to blood of limbs that did not separate same law. Taz wrote: "some forbid" refers to milk-water; from siman 217 regarding vow from milk, kum is permitted — milk-water has no prohibition for benefit there, only for eating. Shach wrote: even though we rule siman 87 cooking meat in milk-water is exempt, thus not like milk — whence do we permit because of eiver min hachai, Torah did not reveal permission except milk; one may say specifically for meat in milk it is not considered milk as "in its mother's milk" — as from mother; but for other matters it is in category of milk.
