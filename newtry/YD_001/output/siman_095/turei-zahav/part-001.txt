<b>משום דהוה נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתירא. </b>פי' דתחילה אין ממשות של בשר רק טעם דהיינו הבשר נתן טעם בקערה והקערה בדגים ועדיין כולו היתר וטעם קלוש כזה אין בו איסור ובאיסור אפילו כמה פעמים נ"ט בר נ"ט אסור הכל:
**** ENGLISH ****
Because it is noten taam bar noten taam of heter. Explanation: at first there is no substance of meat, only taste — that is, the meat imparted taste to the bowl and the bowl to the fish and still all is heter; and such a weak taste has no prohibition; but in a prohibition, even many times noten taam bar noten taam, all is forbidden.

**** ENGLISH ****

The egg shell is clearly perforated, and when a person cooks it he finds the egg itself colored from that color; therefore the Sages forbade an egg of a chick and permitted a forbidden egg, for that one emits broth of a chick and this one emits only mere broth, end of his words Rashba, and Beit Yosef brings it; and requires study from what Rama wrote in siman 86 seif 5 that if the egg is pierced its law is like peeled, for every egg shell is perforated and it emits well; and even so it is permitted in a forbidden egg that emits only broth — why distinguish a hole that developed or did not develop; and if not for the holy mouth of Rashba, it appears to me there is no hole in an egg by nature, but rather it absorbs and emits through the shell from something that is actually in it — and requires study.

<b>ויש מחמירין בצלייה ובישול. </b>דבזה הוה כמו ממש של בשר בדגים ולא התירו נ"ט בר נ"ט אלא עלו לחוד דהיינו דגים רותחין מיד לאחר שסלקן מן האש ונתנן בקערה של בשר צוננת או איפכא שהדגים צוננים והקערה רותחת דמותר לאוכלן בחלב ובשניהם חמין הדגים והקערה אסור לפי זה דהוה כנתבשלו או נצלו וא"ל דמשמע כאן דדוקא עם חלב אסור הדג אבל בפני עצמו שרי הא יש בו איסור משום סכנה כמ"ש סי' קי"ו שיש סכנה אם נתבשל דג עם בשר דכאן לא נחית לדין זה אלא במה שנוגע לענין נ"ט בר נ"ט וכ"כ רש"ל בפרק ג"ה סי' ע"ו אלא שבאו"ה כלל ל"ט כ' וז"ל וכן כל דבר שהוא משום פליטת כלים אין אוסרים מבשר לדגים בדיעבד משום סכנת דבר אחר דהא מתיר בגמרא דגים שעלו בקערה רותחין של בשר וצ"ע לפי זה ממה שזכרתי כאן. אבל מה שאמרו בזה דגים שעלו בקערה כו' יש ללמוד מזה שאין סכנה באכילת דג שיש בו טעם בשר במה שנתבשל בכלי בן יומו של בשר דהא אמרי' בגמרא דהרבה אכלו דגים שעלו בקערה כו' ועיין סי' קי"ו מ"ש שם.
**** ENGLISH ****
And there are those who are stringent with roasting and cooking. For in this it is like actual meat in fish, and they did not permit noten taam bar noten taam except when it came up by itself — that is, fish boiling immediately after they removed them from the fire and placed them in a cold meat bowl, or the reverse where the fish are cold and the bowl is boiling — it is permitted to eat them with milk; but when both are hot, the fish and the bowl are forbidden, for according to this it is like when they were cooked or roasted. And some say that here it appears specifically with milk the fish is forbidden, but by itself it is permitted — behold there is still a prohibition because of danger, as he wrote in siman 116 that there is danger if fish was cooked with meat; here we did not descend to that law except regarding noten taam bar noten taam; and so too Rashal in chapter 35 siman 76; but in Issur VeHeter general rule 39 he wrote and these are his words: and so any matter that is because of emission of vessels — they do not forbid from meat to fish b'dieved because of danger of something else, for they permit in the Gemara fish that came up in a boiling meat bowl — and requires study according to this from what I mentioned here. But what they said in this — fish that came up in a bowl, etc. — one may learn from this that there is no danger in eating fish that has taste of meat from what was cooked in a ben yomo meat vessel, for they said in the Gemara that many ate fish that came up in a bowl, etc.; and see siman 116 what he wrote there.

**** ENGLISH ****
And the custom is to forbid l'chatchila. It appears that without cooking, but only came up by itself, it is permitted even l'chatchila; and so too Rama afterward explicitly; and this is like the words of Sefer HaTerumah that Beit Yosef brings — legumes cooked in a dairy pot are permitted l'chatchila to pour them into a ben yomo meat pot; and automatically for us, who are stringent in cooking, at least regarding came up it is permitted l'chatchila; except that Beit Yosef wrote that this disagrees with what Semak wrote — that l'chatchila it is forbidden to bring them up in a meat vessel in order to eat them afterward with kutach; and so I saw in Rashal chapter 25 siman 63, and he was not precise, for Semak also permits afterward l'chatchila with kutach, but l'chatchila he forbids bringing them up in a meat vessel for this reason, end of his words — therefore Rama is well lenient even without cooking even l'chatchila; but it is forbidden l'chatchila to bring them up in a meat vessel in order to eat afterward with kutach; and so too Issur VeHeter general rule 34 in name of Semag — that it is forbidden to cause noten taam bar noten taam l'chatchila even by bringing up without cooking; and in cooking it is forbidden l'chatchila even to pour afterward to milk. And Rashal wrote and these are his words: and since the world treats as forbidden when they were cooked, if so when they were cooked in a vessel that is not ben yomo it is also forbidden l'chatchila, for any taste for spoilage is forbidden l'chatchila — investigate; specifically the pot in which the food was cooked — they treat the food like the pot forever; but if a ben yomo meat spoon stuck into a pot that is not ben yomo, we permit to eat it with milk like the pot, for they were not stringent about sticking in the spoon which is accidental and a small amount, and the majority is that the spoon is clean, end of his words. In short, it appears from his language that also in a pot of milk it is forbidden to place the fish l'chatchila whenever it was first cooked in a meat vessel; and not like Rama who is lenient nearby even l'chatchila in their pots, also in a pot of meat that is not ben yomo Rama is lenient nearby; one who relies on Rama to be lenient in this has not lost.

**** ENGLISH ****
And there are those who forbid even if there is no fat, etc. Their reason is explained in Tosafot — for fish that came up in a bowl, this case is different here, since the bowls touch the pan and taste is emitted from one to the other and it becomes a second taste in prohibition; and further, when the second taste of meat and milk enters the water they are immediately forbidden and return and forbid the bowls and the pan, end of his words. And in Ran the second reason of Tosafot is explained further — this case is different in fish that came up in a bowl, etc., for kutach did not receive taste at all from the first taste, that is the bowl, but from the fish which is a second taste; but here the taste of milk that fell into the water receives taste from the first taste of meat in the bowls — for who will tell us that emission of meat and emission of milk will not mix in themselves without the intermediary of the water, end of his words. And nevertheless, if water or vegetables were cooked in a dairy ben yomo pot and one stuck into it a ben yomo meat spoon and there is not sixty against the ben yomo spoon, they forbid one another even if both were well scraped; and even though this is the actual law of bowls that were rinsed in a cauldron that Shulchan Aruch mentioned, nevertheless I wrote this because it is most common.
