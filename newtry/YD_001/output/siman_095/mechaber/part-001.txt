Fish and egg cooked in a meat pot — whether permitted to eat with cheese. In it are 7 seifim: Fish that were cooked or roasted in a meat pot that is well washed so that there is no clinging fat on it — permitted to eat them with kutach, because it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am of heter; and if it was not well washed — if on the surface of the pot there is more than one part in sixty in the fish, forbidden to eat them with kutach.
