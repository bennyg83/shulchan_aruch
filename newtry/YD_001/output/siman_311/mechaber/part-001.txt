**** ENGLISH ****
The law of one suspected of selling the firstborn. In it is one seif. One suspected of selling a firstborn as non-sacred—they do not buy from him the flesh of deer, which resembles calf flesh; and they do not buy from him hides that are not worked—even of a female, lest he cut its masculinity and say it is female; and they do not buy from him wool, even bleached. But they buy from him spun wool, and felt, and worked hides.
