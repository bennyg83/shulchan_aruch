**** ENGLISH ****
A law of a non-Jewish and Jewish midwife; it contains 2 se’ifim. A non-Jewish midwife should not deliver for a Jewess in private, even if she is an expert. And likewise, she should not nurse a Jewish child in her house, even if others are standing over her. But in the house of a Jew, it is permitted for her to deliver and to nurse if others stand over her or go out and come in — provided that he does not leave the child alone with her at night.

**** ENGLISH ****
A Jewess should not nurse a non-Jewish child, even for payment (unless she has much milk and it causes her pain — it is permitted for her to nurse him) (Mordechai and Agudah, at the beginning of perek Ein Ma’amidin). And she should not deliver for a non-Jewish woman unless she is known as a midwife, for then it is permitted; and this is specifically for payment, and on a weekday. (It is forbidden to teach a non-Jew a trade) (Hagahot Ashiri there, and Yerushalmi).
