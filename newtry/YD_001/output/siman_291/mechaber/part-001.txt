**** ENGLISH ****
One who rents a house from his fellow—the renter is obligated to affix a mezuzah in it and to repair the place of its affixing (and even if he rented the house with the presumption that it has a mezuzah, it is not a mistaken transaction) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rav Rabbenu Menachem). And when he leaves, he should not take it by force. (And if the owner was particular about its value, the second must pay him) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rav Menachem). And if he rented the house from a non-Jew, or rented it to a non-Jew—he takes it and leaves. {Rama: And a non-Jew who asked that they give him a mezuzah and wishes to affix it in his doorway—it is forbidden to give it to him (so Maharil responded). And it appears to me that nevertheless in a place where there is concern for enmity and lest he harm an Israelite because of this, it is permitted (as above).}
