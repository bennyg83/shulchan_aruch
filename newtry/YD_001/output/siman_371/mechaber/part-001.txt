The law of ohel — how it brings impurity. In it are 6 seifim. It is forbidden for a kohen to enter under an ohel with the dead beneath it, even if it is very large, even into another house or another upper story opened to that house, through an opening of a tefach by a tefach and a window to that house forever; and if he sealed the opening, it is permitted for him to enter another house, for the sealing interposes {Rama: even if not nullified to remain there forever; and all this when he sealed the entire opening; but if he did not seal the entire opening, only part less than a tefach — if they placed it to remain forever, it diminishes and interposes before the impurity that it not pass; and if not, it does not diminish; and anything that receives impurity does not interpose before impurity (Hagahot Alfasi ch. Bnei HaIr and laws of impurity for Rif, Ramban, Rosh, and Mordechai beginning of ch. Lo Yachpor); and sealing in a window is not called sealing unless it can stand without support (same there).}
