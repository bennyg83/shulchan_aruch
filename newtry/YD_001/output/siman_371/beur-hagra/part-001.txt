**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(Likkut) Or of.</b> This follows what is said there regarding a corpse. The phrase "when does this apply, in a courtyard" implies that otherwise it has the same law as a corpse. Rashi in Bava Batra 12a and Tosafot there write similarly. Their words require study, however, because a sealed grave conveys impurity around all its sides, yet one may stand in the grave's courtyard where the walls delimit the impurity, as explained below.
