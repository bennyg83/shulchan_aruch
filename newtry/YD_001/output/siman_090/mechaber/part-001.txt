**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of udder (kachal). In it are 4 seifim: Udder is forbidden by rabbinic decree, for there is no meat cooked in milk that is forbidden from the Torah; therefore if one tore it and rinsed the milk in it — it is permitted to roast and eat it; and if one tore it in two directions and smeared it on the wall until no moisture of milk remained — it is permitted to cook it with meat; and udder that was not torn — whether of a young animal that did not nurse or of an adult — is forbidden to cook; and if one transgressed and cooked it — if by itself (and all the more if roasted) it is permitted to eat it; and if he cooked it with other meat — one estimates it with sixty and the udder is counted in the number: How so? If everything with the udder was as sixty in the udder — the udder is forbidden and the rest permitted; and if there was less than sixty — all is forbidden; in any case if it fell into another pot it forbids that pot, and one estimates with sixty as at first, for the udder itself that was cooked becomes like the forbidden piece, and one estimates it only as it is at the time it was cooked, not as it was when it fell: {Rama: And some say if it fell first into a pot that does not have sixty and became forbidden — if it then fell into another pot the udder does not combine for sixty (Tur in name of Rashba and logical opinion for Ran unlike Beit Yosef for Ran); and so is primary.}
