**** ENGLISH ****

(And Peri Chadash wrote like Orach Chaim and wrote from what was written Chullin daf 154 side 2 that one may not eat udder after cheese without wiping, rinsing, and rinsing hands; and so too those who practice six hours between cheese and meat — afterward one must also wait if he comes to eat udder; and one must pick his teeth if he comes to eat meat after it, end of his words, see there.)
