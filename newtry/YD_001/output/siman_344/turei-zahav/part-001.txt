**** ENGLISH ****
<b>For the honor of the deceased.</b> Anything not genuinely for the honor of the deceased is certainly forbidden. This includes those who use a funeral discourse mainly to display their own scholarship rather than to honor the deceased. Such behavior is improper, and I heard that many great authorities were strict about it. Tur cites Rav Hai Gaon that Torah study is prohibited even beyond four cubits from the deceased. <br><b>In Torat HaAdam</b> it is written that custom and proper conduct are not to continue formally mentioning a deceased person after twelve months. If news of the death arrives only after twelve months, no eulogy is required. A leap month is not added, because the measure is twelve months, not a calendrical year.
