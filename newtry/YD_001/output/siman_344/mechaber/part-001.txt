<b>דין המאבד עצמו לדעת ומנודה והרוגי ב"ד והפורשין מן הצבור. ובו ח' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>המאבד עצמו לדעת <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i>אין מתעסקים עמו לכל דבר ואין מתאבלין עליו ואין מספידין אותו <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>ולא <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>קורעין ולא חולצין אבל עומדין עליו בשורה ואומרים עליו ברכת אבלים וכל דבר שהוא כבוד לחיים:
**** ENGLISH ****
The law of one who kills himself intentionally, a menudeh, those executed by beit din, and those who separate from the community. It contains 8 seifim. One who kills himself intentionally — they do not attend to him for anything, do not mourn him, do not eulogize him, do not tear, nor remove a shoe; but they stand in a row over him and say upon him the mourners' blessing and anything that is honor for the living.

**** ENGLISH ****
A menudeh who died — his law is like one who killed himself intentionally: they do not tear, remove a shoe, or eulogize him; and they place a stone on his coffin; and these matters apply to hefker when he transgressed words of Torah; but for monetary matters, once he died he is exempt from their decree, and they do not place a stone on his coffin and they eulogize him appropriately.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="11"></i>ארון העובר ממקום למקום אם שלדו <small>(פירוש השדרה וצלעותיה) </small>קיימת עומדין עליו בשורה ואומרים עליו ברכת אבלים ותנחומי אבלים אם יש אבלים שמתאבלין עליו ואם אין שלדו קיימת אין עומדין עליו בשורה ואין אומרים עליו ברכת אבלים ולא תנחומי אבלים:
**** ENGLISH ****
A coffin passing from place to place — if its skeleton (meaning the spine and its ribs) remains, they stand in a row over it and say upon it the mourners' blessing and mourners' consolation if there are mourners who mourn him; and if its skeleton does not remain, they do not stand in a row over it and do not say upon it the mourners' blessing nor mourners' consolation.
