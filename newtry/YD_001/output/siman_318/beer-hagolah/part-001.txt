שם במשנה די"ח ע"ב: <br>(°) כ' התי"ט דהא קמ"ל דלא אמרינן העמד ממון בחזקת בעלים למימר שהאחת טנפה וא' ילדה שני זכרים. א"נ ניחא לי' לפרש כל גווני ואע"ג דלא צריכי כדקתני זכר ונקבה נותן אחד לכהן דלא צריכי כלל תוס':
**** ENGLISH ****
This is stated there in the Mishnah, Bechorot 18b. Tosafot Yom Tov writes that the novelty is that we do not say, "Leave the money in the possession of its owner," by assuming that one animal discharged blood and the other gave birth to two males. Alternatively, the Mishnah simply wished to state every possible case even where the law was already obvious, just as it states that if there was a male and a female he gives one to the kohen, although that law requires no novelty. Tosafot.
