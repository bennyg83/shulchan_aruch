<b>כמה ספיקות בדיני בכור בהמה. ובו ו' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>רחל שלא בכרה וילדה שני זכרים אפילו יצאו שני ראשיהם כאחד <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>אי אפשר שלא קדם אחד והואיל ואין ידוע איזה יצא ראשון הכהן <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i>נוטל את <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="3"></i>הכחוש <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i>והשני <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>ספק בכור מת <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="4"></i>אחד מהם אין לכהן כלום שזה החי ספק הוא והמוציא מחבירו ע"ה <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="5"></i>וכן אם ילדה זכר ונקבה הרי הזכר ספק שמא הנקבה יצאה תחלה לפיכך <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="3"></i>אין לכהן כלום שהמוציא מחבירו עליו הראיה:
**** ENGLISH ****
How many doubts there are in the laws of a firstborn animal. In it are 6 seifim. A ewe that had not previously given birth and bore two males—even if both their heads emerged as one—it is impossible that one did not precede; and since it is unknown which emerged first, the kohen takes the weaker one and the second is a doubtful firstborn. If one of them died, there is nothing for the kohen, for this living one is doubtful and the burden of proof is on the one who extracts from his fellow. And likewise if she bore a male and a female—the male is doubtful, lest the female emerged first; therefore there is nothing for the kohen, for the burden of proof is on the one who extracts from his fellow.

Two ewes that had not previously given birth and bore two males—both are for the kohen. Male and female—the male is for the kohen. Two males and a female—the kohen takes the weaker one and the second is a doubtful firstborn. And if one of them died, there is nothing for the kohen, for this living one is a doubtful firstborn and the burden of proof is on the one who extracts from his fellow.
