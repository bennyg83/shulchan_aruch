**** HEBREW ****
(סי' פ"ב ט"ז סק"א) <b>ואינו אוכל העוף מחיים.</b> צ"ל אוכל העוף מחיים ואינו ממתין עד שתמות תחילה:
**** ENGLISH ****
(Siman 82 Taz s.k. 7) Taz elaborated to prove from chapter Ein Tzedadin tzippor deror is pure since forbidden to hunt on Yom Tov and place food before it; Nekudot HaKesef challenged — words unclear; clear to me there (daf 25) "and tzippor deror in its time during the day must tie its wings lest it be exchanged with its mother" — proves pure; also Peri Chadash preceded in Orach Chaim laws of Yom Tov.
