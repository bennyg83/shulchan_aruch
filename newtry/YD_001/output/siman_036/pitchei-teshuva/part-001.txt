They say birds do not feel — but lung shrivels from another slaughtered before it as Shulchan Aruch and BeHag in Tashbetz. Also one should not do this since it can lead to shriveling; even Rashba who wrote not concerned for momentary shriveling and permits immediate slaughter — concern per my books Rashba in Yesh Nochlin says so; also may say though not shriveling immediately, may shrivel later and immediate slaughter does not help — especially per Tashbetz from BeHag that in fowl can spoil immediately beyond repair. Though I cannot require examination in animals and especially fowl where examination is hard and b'dieved — nevertheless practice with fowl and animals when tying many oxen in slaughterhouse that one not see another's slaughter; especially heard they feel much when led to slaughter and fear falls in slaughterhouse — less concern for self-frightening, but still careful not see another's slaughter for tza'ar and shrivel concern though slaughter immediately — some leniency per Rashba; still careful for reasons written. But when slaughterer called to owner's feast and slaughter place set by full bird cage — slaughter as needed, leave birds in cage; and in lamb pen seems concern to require examination initially as Bayit Avraham on treifot siman 36 — be careful all ways — see there.
