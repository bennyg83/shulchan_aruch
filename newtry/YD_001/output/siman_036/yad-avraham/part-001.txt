(סימן ל"ו סעיף ה' בהגה"ה) <b>ולא תלינן ביד הטבח אלא אם הנקב משוך כו' אבל אם הוא עגול לא תלינן בטבח כי אין דרכו וכו'.</b> כאן דקדק לומר לא תלינן בטבח ולא כתב בשום דבר כדכתב בסיפא באם אדום סביב הנקב כו' לא תלינן בשום דבר דכאן אם הנקב עגול (עי' זבחים דף ע"ב) בידא דטבחא הוא דלא תלינן שקורע ביד בצפרניו כמ"ש רש"י ור"ן והגהת אשר"י וש"פ וסתם קריעה היינו לארכו. משא"כ תולעים תלינן אפילו אם הנקב עגול שדרכו להיות כן. או בראש האיזמל הקטן או במחט וקוץ (עי' זבחים שם) ודאי דרכו להיות עגול ותלינן שפיר משא"כ בסיפא ולשון הגה"ה מדוקדק הדק היטב:
**** ENGLISH ****
(Siman 36 seif 5 in hagahah) And we do not attribute to the slaughterer's hand unless the puncture is elongated, etc.; but if it is round we do not attribute to the slaughterer, for that is not his way, etc. Here he was precise to say we do not attribute to the slaughterer and did not write "for any matter" as he wrote at the end regarding if red around the puncture, etc. — we do not attribute for any matter; for here if the puncture is round (see Zevachim daf 72) it is the slaughterer's hand that we do not attribute, for he tears with his hand by fingernails as Rashi and Ran and Hagahot Ashiri and Shach wrote; and a simple tear means along its length. Unlike worms — we attribute even if the puncture is round, for that is their way; or at the head of a small awl or a thorn (see Zevachim there) — certainly their way is to be round, and we attribute properly; unlike at the end. And the language of the hagahah is very precise.

(ש"ע ס"ו) <b>ניקב אחד מהסמפונית לבשר מגין עליו וכשירה.</b> ונלע"ד דזה איירי שהבשר לא ניקב ג"כ כנגדו דאי היה ניקב כנגדו אזלא לה סתימה. וכדאמרינן בש"ס פא"ט (דף מ"ח) והביאו הש"ך ר"ס מ"ב גבי סתימת דופן לאונא וסתימת כבד למרה. וצע"ג דא"כ האיך מכשרי הט"ו סעיף ט"ז מחט שנמצא בבשר הריאה הלא כשיצאה המחט מהסימפון לבשר ניקב הבשר ג"כ כנגדו ואינו מגין ובפרט מחט שדרך לחסר הבשר. כדאיתא בש"ס שם (ע"ב) דמחט בריאה אינה כשירה אלא למ"ד חסרון מבפנים לא שמי' חסרון וע"ש בפרש"י. ומצאתי בס' פמ"ג בשם תב"ש שכתב דתלינן דמיד שנקבה המחט עברה בתוך הריאה ולא שהתה ולא נחסרה דהחסרון בא ע"י שהייתה כמ"ש רש"י ע"ש. ותמיהני דאכתי אף בלא חסרון יש כאן נקב וא"א להגין. דנקיבת הסותם ה"ל נקיבת הנסתם כדמוכח בש"ס שם דפריך ליתנו נקובת הדופן והכבד. וכבר ידעת שכל נקב במשהו ואפילו אין בו חסרון כמ"ש רש"י ור"ן ורע"ב וש"פ ריש פא"ט. וכן מוכח בש"ס ורש"י (דף מ"ז ע"ב) גבי הריאה שניקבה או שחסרה דשניקבה היינו שאין בו חסרון וזה ברור. ומ"ש בפמ"ג דבשר הריאה רך והדריא בריא צ"ע הא ה"ל קרום שעלה מחמת מכה שאינו קרום בכל הנקבים בין בסותם ובין בנסתם וכמ"ש הפמ"ג עצמו ר"ס מ"ב לענין סתימת כבד במרה דאם ניקב כנגדו ואח"כ עלה בו קרום לא מהני דקי"ל ר"פ המקשה (דף ס"ח) דטריפה אין לה היתר. וגם צ"ע במ"ש האחרונים דאם המחט מקצת תחובה בסימפון ומקצתה בבשר טריפה. שאין הבשר מגין שהמחט מונעת. ותימא דלפ"ז אפילו נמצאת בבשר הא בודאי מתחלה כשיצאה מהסימפון לתוך הבשר היתה מקצתה תחובה בבשר וא"א להיות מותר דטריפה אין לה היתר וצ"ע. ות"ל שאח"ז מצאתי קושיא זו בפמ"ג ולא אשכח לה פירוקא. ובאמת הני קושיות עצומות הן. ולפיכך אין להכשיר מחט בריאה כלל אפילו במקום הפס"מ ודלא כהג"ה וצ"ע. ומיהו בס' תורת יקותיאל סי' מ"ד הוכיח דכל הטריפות יש להן היתר וחוזרין להכשירן ע"י עצמן רק קרום שעלה מחמת מכה אינו קרום דסופו לסתור. וכ"מ בחה"ר פ' המקשה שכתב דטריפה אין לה היתר ע"י אדם כגון שיחתוך למעלה וכו' אבל פסול שחוזר ע"י עצמו או ע"י סמנין חוזר להכשירן:
**** ENGLISH ****
(Shulchan Aruch seif 6) If one bronchus was punctured into lung flesh it protects and is kosher. And it appears to me this deals when the flesh was not also punctured opposite it, for if it were punctured opposite it she would lose sealing. As we say in the Talmud Piskei Tosafot (daf 48) and Shach brought in siman 42 regarding sealing of the lining to the ona and sealing of liver to mara. And it is difficult: if so how does Taz permit in seif 16 a needle found in lung flesh — when the needle exits the bronchus to the flesh the flesh is also punctured opposite and is not protected, especially a needle whose way is to lack flesh. As in the Talmud there (72b) a needle in the lung is kosher only according to the view that internal lack is not called lack — see Rashi there. And I found in Peri Megadim in the name of Turei Zahav who wrote we attribute that as soon as the lung was punctured the needle passed inside the lung and did not remain and did not lack — for the lack comes because it remained as Rashi wrote there. And I wonder: even without lack there is a puncture here and it cannot be protected; puncture of the sealed is puncture of what was sealed as proven in the Talmud there, which asks why not teach puncture of lining and liver. And you already know every puncture in any amount even without lack as Rashi, Ran, Ra'avad, and Shach wrote at the beginning of Piskei Tosafot; and so is proven in the Talmud and Rashi (daf 47b) regarding lung punctured or lacking — punctured means without lack — this is clear. And what Peri Megadim wrote that lung flesh is soft — requires study; this would be a membrane that arose from a blow that is not a membrane for all punctures whether sealed or unsealed, as Peri Megadim himself wrote in siman 42 regarding sealing liver to mara that if punctured opposite and afterward a membrane arose — it does not help, for we hold in chapter HaMekasheh (daf 68) treifah has no remedy. And also requires study what the acharonim wrote that if the needle is partly in the bronchus and partly in flesh — treifah, for flesh does not protect because the needle prevents. And one could say: if so, even if found in flesh certainly at first when it exited the bronchus into flesh it was partly in flesh and cannot be permitted — treifah has no remedy — requires study. And say: afterward I found this difficulty in Peri Megadim and did not find a resolution. And truly these are great difficulties; therefore one should not permit a needle in the lung at all even in great financial loss, unlike the hagahah; requires study. However in Sefer Torat Yekutiel siman 44 he proved all treifot have remedy and return to kosher through themselves except a membrane from a blow is not a membrane for it will tear. And so in Chiddushei HaRashba chapter HaMekasheh who wrote treifah has no remedy by a person such as cutting above, etc.; but disqualification that returns through itself or through remedies returns to kosher.

(There seif 7) A lung poured like a pitcher, etc. — if the bronchi stand in their place, etc. — kosher; and the appearance of the water must be clear; but if murky or putrid — treifah. Such is the view of Ri"f and Rambam. And in truth Ri"f did not write regarding poured that they must not be putrid — only that they must not be murky; and regarding bubbles he said the opposite — putrid treifah and did not mention murky. And know all commentators did not reach the end of Ri"f's view here, therefore they multiplied difficulties on him; therefore I shall expand somewhat. Know Beit Yosef siman 37 wrote in the name of Sefer Orach Chaim in the name of R' Yonatan: wherever it did not putrefy outside we are not concerned it putrefies inside; and Beit Yosef wrote this does not appear so from the gemara and poskim. But in my humble opinion the words of Orach Chaim in the name of R' Yonatan are clear and necessary in the Talmud according to Ri"f's view in the version in Sukkah chapter Lulav HaGazul (daf 36): Rava asked regarding an etrog on which signs of treifah were born, etc., and concludes that he asks like Ulla said — R' Yochanan: lung poured like a pitcher is kosher; and Rava said: when the bronchus stands; here what — perhaps there it does not rule over it air and returns healthy; but here air rules and it putrefies. If not — etc. And from Rashi it is proven Rava's question deals with poured from inside, namely the seed chambers where seeds are inside and still did not putrefy. And so appears at first glance from Rava's words: but here air rules and it putrefies — meaning not yet putrefied at all. And it is astonishing: if so how does he resolve from the baraita that teaches putrefied inside when Rava's question deals with not yet putrefied. And in Kereti Peri Chodosh challenged Ri"f: if already putrefied even healthy is disqualified and all the more an etrog; and he was not precise, for even those who disagree with Ri"f and hold even putrid lung is kosher — how does he want to resolve here when this deals with already putrefied and his question is not yet putrefied. And one cannot say thus he resolves that etrog is stricter than lung in any case even putrid lung kosher — for if so what did Rava answer "not both from outside" when lung even in such case kosher and etrog treifah; therefore it is proven etrog is stricter with the reasoning air rules and putrefies. And one should not ask why Rava does not ask when already putrefied since lung even in such case kosher and etrog in May — say Rava knew from the baraita bringing etrog putrefied disqualified and only asks when not yet putrefied. Truly the first difficulty is huge how he resolves from the baraita. Also Ri"f omitted the question in Sukkah implying leniency — difficult, for it is a doubt in the Talmud and he should be stringent; and Ra'osh stood there and what he wrote it is a rabbinic doubt — strained that we know these disqualifications are rabbinic. But truth guides that Ri"f's view is even poured lung if it did not putrefy inside and outside only inside alone is not treifah. And Rava asks on etrog in such a case poured and putrefied inside alone from May: if we say since air rules it putrefies completely even outside — since outside air rules there it putrefies. Now he asks to resolve from the baraita etrog putrefied disqualified — what, not putrefied inside — thus even putrefied inside alone is treifah; and he rejects not both from outside, for there even lung is treifah. Since not resolved he rules stringently putrefied disqualified whether inside alone or outside. But poured without putrefaction at all he did not rule stringently, for he did not ask at all — this is clear. Thus proven from all the above for lung we require putrefaction outside as Orach Chaim wrote in the name of R' Yonatan, but not putrefied inside alone; and thus poured where the sign is inside even if putrefied we have no concern unless lung putrefied outside; and it is not treifah unless appearance murky as Ramban wrote in Milchamot Hashem the reason in murky one cannot examine bronchi for white threads. And this is the hagahah: one expert to examine bronchi did not melt may rely; and because of murky he wrote thus here expertise is needed. But for bubbles outside on the lung Ri"f wrote plainly if putrid treifah and did not mention murky since bronchus examination is not needed for bubbles as above siman 37 unlike Rambam. Thus all difficulties on Rashba, Ra'osh, Ran, and Magen Avraham are resolved — see Beit Yosef who brought them; examine well.
