**** ENGLISH ****
Times. Maharil's reason: initially sixty is needed against all the milk, for we are concerned lest a little milk remained; and when they removed it from the pot the entire spoon became nevelah from basar b'chalav, therefore the spoon must be nullified the second time. It seems therefore Mechaber specified "if inserted twice," etc., and did not write generally that many times require sixty each time — because he follows his view that we do not say noten ta'am only in basar b'chalav, for there each alone is permitted by itself; therefore when inserted twice there is no longer room to say noten ta'am, since the spoon was already forbidden, even if inserted many times two times of sixty suffice; specifically if not known in the interim; but if known in the interim the first was already nullified and the second time too was nullified in those sixty; and even though Beit Yosef's view seems below end siman 99 that if an olive of issur fell into sixty olives of heter and afterward another olive of issur fell — it returns and is stirred even min b'mino even if known in the interim — nevertheless here there is no reason at all, for there is sixty against the spoon and the spoon would be all milk, only from noten ta'am two times of sixty are needed (and even though min b'mino also does not impart taste, nevertheless since it can impart taste against it when not min b'mino it is forbidden, and this does not apply here — investigate, end); see end siman 92 what Taz challenges from here on what Maharil wrote in Pesach laws regarding hag'alah from kli rishon — see there.

<b>דהיתירא. </b> כתב הט"ז דבמרדכי כתוב הטעם מפני ששמנונית בדבש היא נטל"פ וסיים שם אבל אם היה לשבח היה אוסר והב"י ס"ל הטעם בזה משום דהוי נ"ט בר נ"ט ויש נ"מ בטעם המרדכי דאפילו היה בשר בעין במחבת שבשלו הדבש דאז הוי אסור ולא הוי נ"ט בר נ"ט מ"מ כאן שרי מטעם דהוי נטל"פ וא"כ לא היה לו לש"ע להשמיט טעמו של המרדכי בזה עכ"ל והש"ך כ' דמוכח שמיירי כאן שהריקוהו בעירוי ולא פסק הקילוח דאל"כ פשיטא דשרי כדלעיל סי' ס"ח ס"ו וא"כ ע"כ צ"ל דלא שרי אלא הדבש אבל הקערה אסורה לדעת הרב בהג"ה בסי' צ"ה ס"ג וליכא למימר דמ"מ שרי מטעם המרדכי דהוי נטל"פ דהא כתב הרב בסימן ק"ג ס"ד במקום שאין הפסד מרובה יש להחמיר לומר דשמנונית בדבש לא הוי נטל"פ ואפשר דמיירי הכא במשקה מע"ד העשוי מדבש דבכה"ג לכ"ע נטל"פ אכן מסתימת ל' המחבר משמע דאפילו הקערה מותרת ואולי כיון דיש כאן תרתי למעליותא דאפילו בנתבשלו הרבה פוסקים מתירים לאכלן בכותח וכן יש פוסקים דשמנונית בדבש הוי נטל"פ שרי לדעת הרב אפילו הקערה וע"ל סימן ק"ג ס"ב בהג"ה:
**** ENGLISH ****
Of the permitted. Taz wrote: in Mordechai the reason is because grease in honey is noten ta'am lifgam, and he concludes there: but if it were for benefit it would forbid; and Beit Yosef holds the reason here is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am, and there is a difference in Mordechai's reason: even if there were actual meat in the pan in which they cooked the honey, then it would be forbidden and not noten ta'am bar noten ta'am; nevertheless here it is permitted because it is noten ta'am lifgam; if so Shulchan Aruch should not have omitted Mordechai's reason — end; and Shach wrote it is clear the case is they poured it by pouring and the stream did not stop; otherwise obviously permitted as above siman 98 seif 6; if so one must say only the honey is permitted but the bowl is forbidden per Rama's gloss in siman 95 seif 3; and one cannot say it is nevertheless permitted per Mordechai's noten ta'am lifgam, for Rama wrote in siman 103 seif 4 that where there is no great loss one should be stringent that grease in honey is not noten ta'am lifgam; possibly the case is a beverage made from honey, where all agree noten ta'am lifgam; nevertheless from Mechaber's language it implies even the bowl is permitted; perhaps since here there is a double advantage — even if cooked much, many poskim permit eating them in a dip, and some hold grease in honey is noten ta'am lifgam — permitted per Rama even for the bowl; and see siman 103 seif 2 in the gloss.
