**** ENGLISH ****
If the spoon was inserted twice, etc. — the reason: Hagahot Maimoniyot on Chullin and Maharil responsum siman 161 and Terumat HaDeshen siman 283 wrote: initially sixty is needed against the milk, for we are concerned lest a little milk remained; and when they removed it from the pot the entire spoon became nevelah from basar b'chalav, therefore the spoon must be nullified the second time. It seems therefore Taz wrote if inserted twice, etc., requires two times of sixty, and they did not write generally; and if inserted many times, sixty is needed each time — because they follow their view that we do not say noten ta'am only in basar b'chalav, for there each alone is permitted by itself and through joining they become forbidden, unlike other issurim, as I wrote in siman 92 s.k. 10; therefore when inserted a second time there is no longer room to say noten ta'am, since the spoon was already forbidden; even if inserted many times, two times of sixty suffice; and Semak who holds noten ta'am in all issurim truly did not write in siman 213 this language that two times are required — see there and investigate.
