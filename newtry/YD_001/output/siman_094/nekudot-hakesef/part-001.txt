<b>(סימן צ"ד ט"ז סק"א) דגם ע"י עירוי כו'. </b>לא הו"ל לערבינהו ולתנינהו דהא אין דינם שוה דעירוי קי"ל דאינו אוסר רק כדי קליפה וא"כ א"צ אח"כ ס' אלא כנגד הקליפה ולכן כתב רמ"א דהיינו ששמשו בו בכ"ר על דברי המחבר שכתב משערינן בכל מה שנתחב בקדירה:
**** ENGLISH ****
(siman 94 Taz s.k. 1) Also by pouring, etc. It should not have combined them and taught them together, for their laws are not the same: by pouring we hold it forbids only the amount of a peel, and therefore one does not afterward need sixty but only against the peel; therefore Rama wrote that this means they used it in a kli rishon — on the Mechaber's words that we estimate for everything inserted into the pot:

**** ENGLISH ****
(there Taz s.k. 13) He wondered at Bach and lengthened in his proofs, and all his proofs are nothing, for one who examines Bach will see he did not write so for practical law; on the contrary, for the truth of the matter Bach holds one always needs sixty, only he wrote so to settle Tur's mind according to Rif, for it seems so at first glance in his words; if so, what proof does he bring from Rashba and other poskim — behold Ra'ah in Bedek HaBayit there disagrees and explains that passage "how do they roast" without sixty and with sixty no peel is needed, as in Mordechai perek Gid HaNasheh, and so it seems at first glance from Ra'ash perek 3; nevertheless for practical law his words are true as we rule below siman 105 seif 5, and I too in Shach s.k. 28 when I wrote that the grease on it does not forbid more than a peel since it is a slight matter, etc., I guarded myself from this and did not write that even without sixty and if it is a ben yomo one needs a peel as Beit Yosef wrote — investigate:
