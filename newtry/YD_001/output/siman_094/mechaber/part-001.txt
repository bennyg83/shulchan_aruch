The law of one who inserts a dairy spoon into a meat pot. In it are 9 seifim: One who inserts a dairy spoon into a meat pot, or the reverse — they estimate for everything that was inserted from it into the pot (if the spoon is a ben yomo, that is, they used it in a kli rishon within twenty-four hours) (Arukh general rule 37).
