**** ENGLISH ****
Which renting is permitted and which is forbidden; it contains 8 se’ifim. It is forbidden for a person to rent out his money; that he should say to him, “I will rent to you 10 dinarim for a dinar per month.” And this applies when he rents them to spend them. But if he rented them in order to learn with them or to display [them], and he returns them to him intact, it is permitted. {Rama: And this is specifically when he accepted upon himself only responsibility for theft and loss; but if he accepted upon himself all responsibility, it is forbidden (Beit Yosef in the name of Ba’al HaTerumos). And if the renter accepted upon himself all responsibility — both theft and loss and responsibility for accidents — some permit even when he rents to spend them (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 302), as below siman 177. However, if he accepted upon himself only theft and loss and not accidents, or accidents and not theft and loss, it is forbidden to spend them as their rent (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos; and Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 5; and Semag; and Rivash, simanim 305 and 308; and Terumas HaDeshen there; and according to Rosh). Nevertheless, it is only like avak ribbis; but if the renter accepted no responsibility at all, it is fixed interest (Rivash there).}

**** ENGLISH ****
If one rents out a field to his fellow for 10 kor per year, and said to him, “Give me 200 zuz, that I will support the field with them, and I will give you 12 kor each year,” this is permitted, because if he supports the field with these dinarim, its rent will be more. And likewise, if he rented him a shop or a ship for 10 dinarim per year, and said to him, “Give me 200 zuz that I will build with them and paint it and plaster it, or that I will repair with them this ship and its implements, and I will add for you 12 dinarim each year,” this is permitted. (Since he spends the money on the body of the ship or the shop) (Tur). But if he said to him, “Give me 200 zuz in order to do business with them in the shop, or to spend on merchandise of the ship, or to hire sailors with them, and I will add for you in the rent,” this is forbidden.

**** ENGLISH ****
It is permitted to increase the rent of the land. (Language of Rambam, perek 7 of Hilchos Malveh, din 8.) How so? He rented him the courtyard, and said to him — before he took possession of it — “If from now you give me [the rent], it is yours for 10 sela’im each year; and if you give the rent month by month, it is for a sela each month,” this is permitted. And similarly, regarding the wages of a person, it is also permitted. {Rama: But if he already took possession of it to rent it to him for 10 sela’im per year, it is forbidden to say to him, “Give each month 1 sela, and I will wait for you for the money” (Beit Yosef in the name of the students of Rashba). And all the more so, if he was already obligated to him for the rent, and he gives him something for delaying the money, for it is forbidden (Hagahos Ashiri; and Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek Eizehu Neshech; and in Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 8). It is permitted to increase the dowry of bridegrooms; for example, he vowed a dowry to his daughter and stipulated with his son-in-law that each year that he leaves the dowry with him he will give him such-and-such wage — it is permitted, for this is only like adding to him dowry; and specifically when they stipulated so before the marriage, but not afterward, for it is “ager natar” (Rashba, siman 429; and Rivash, siman 402). And see below siman 177.}

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="15"></i>השוכר את הפועל בימות החורף לעשות עמו בימות הקיץ בדינר ליום ומקדים לו שכרו מעתה ושכרו שוה בימות הקיץ סלע ליום אסור <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="16"></i>אבל אם אמר לו עשה עמי מלאכה מהיום ועד זמן פלוני בדינר ביום מותר דכיון שמתחיל לעשות עמו מעתה אינו נראה כנוטל שכר מעותיו שמקדים לו:
**** ENGLISH ****
One who hires a laborer in the winter days to work with him in the summer days for a dinar per day, and he advances his wages from now — while his wage is worth a sela per day in the summer days — it is forbidden. But if he said to him, “Do labor with me from today until such-and-such time for a dinar per day,” it is permitted, for since he begins to work with him from now, it does not appear like he is taking wages for his money that he advanced him.
