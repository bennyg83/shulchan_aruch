**** ENGLISH ****
General laws regarding perforation of organs. In it are 3 seifim. For all these perforations, if there is something to hang upon — such as the butcher or a wolf that took them — we hang upon them. If there is nothing to hang upon and there is doubt whether it was perforated while alive or not — we pierce a perforation next to it and compare this to that; and even if they are not similar, if they used by hand on the doubtful perforation they also use on the perforation they make to compare with it; if after use it is kosher — kosher in its place. And one compares only in the kereis and dekin, but not in the heart, spleen, and gizzard. {Rama: And see above siman 36 how we practice; every place where one cannot hang that it was done after slaughter — even though it is a treifah that has inspection, if they did not inspect it or cannot inspect it — behold it is like a safek treifah and we forbid it; and we do not say "she was slaughtered, she is permitted" except regarding a matter where we can say the defect was done after slaughter, that she did not leave her presumption of life.}

**** ENGLISH ****
Every organ regarding which we said that if it is perforated in any amount it is treifah — likewise if all of it was removed — treifah; whether removed through illness or by hand, or created missing, or created with two organs of that organ — treifah, for all excess is considered as removed. {Rama: (except the spleen, which if removed is kosher, and if perforated in its support is treifah) (Tur); and everything we said that if created missing it is treifah — all the more so if removed by hand; and everything we said that if removed it is treifah — the same law applies to created missing. And some permit in this (and in great loss one may rely on those who permit) (darkhei emet).}
