דמסתברא דספק דאורייתא היא ולחומרא וכדתניא וכו' וה"ה בכל שאר טריפות וכדאמרינן לקמן דף נ"א דההיא אימרתא דהוי משגרי כרעא בתרייתא א"ר יימא האי שגרונא נקטיה מתקיף לה רבינא ודלמא חוט השדרה איפסק א"ל שגרונא שכיח ש"מ כל היכא דאתיליד ריעותא אי לא דשכיחי הוא לחומרא תלינן רק היכ' דאיכא למתלי כגון הא דתלינן בזאב תלינן לקולא עכ"ל הרשב"א בחידושיו בשם רבו והמעיין בד"ת פרק א"ט דף מ"ג ד"ה קסבר עולא כו' וכן ביבמות **** ENGLISH ****
(Siman 50 in the hagahah seif 1) Every place where one can, etc. Shach wrote s.k. 3: an incident came before us in Krakow, etc. — before coming to explain his words we precede Rashba's words in his novellae first chapter of Chullin 4 s.v. velitei, etc.: and where the bird's wing slipped off, etc. — our Rabbi wrote it is reasonable that this is a Torah safek and stringently; and as is taught, etc.; and the same applies to all other treifot; and as we say below daf 51a regarding that statement of sending the leg after the other — R' Yema said: this is Shegruna; Ravina challenged him: perhaps the spinal cord was severed; he said to him: Shegruna is common — we learn: wherever a defect was born, if it is not because it is common — we hang stringently; only where there is something to hang, such as that which we hang on a wolf — we hang leniently, end of Rashba in his novellae in the name of his teacher. One who examines Darkei Torah chapter 8 daf 43 s.v. k'savar Ula, etc., and likewise Yevamot 30b s.v. deha isha zo, etc. — will see he holds: not only where one can say it was done after slaughter do we hang leniently, but even if the safek is from life — such as a thorn sat in the esophagus that certainly was inserted while alive, only the safek is whether it perforated outward or not — we also hang leniently, see there. And behold according to Tosafot's method it is difficult — in truth from Shegruna that is common, and likewise from that which he slaughtered the esophagus, etc. — and according to the flow of the matter one may reject that of Shegruna — for he wants to resolve the difficulty of the matter: Shegruna is common; and the same if it were not common we would hang leniently; and see above that if it were not common, inspection would in any case be needed — wherever inspection is possible we do not rely on presumption, unlike if Shegruna is common — inspection is not needed; and likewise that which he slaughtered the esophagus must be according to Tosafot's method: since the safek is in slaughter it is more severe and we do not establish presumption; and it appears Maharshal explains Tosafot's method according to Rama — only Taz himself adds and says there: where inspection is needed, if it cannot be inspected or was lost — treifah, see there; only Rama's language here does not bear this explanation that he wrote "even though it is treifah that has inspection" — it implies not only treifah that cannot be inspected do we not establish presumption. And according to Taz's view — specifically treifah that has inspection do we not establish presumption, but treifah that has no inspection we properly establish presumption. And what Shach wants to say that Tosafot wrote thus for Rabbah, etc. — wondrous, for from Tosafot's words it is proven that this reasoning of establishing presumption applies also to the conclusion; otherwise what does Tosafot challenge from Rabbah who said "she was slaughtered, she is permitted," etc. — lest Rav Huna holds like Abaye; and are you comparing man to man. Also in Tosafot chapter 8 daf 43 it implies explicitly that that which they challenge there — why not say we establish presumption — from the outset we hold thus; therefore it appears to me that one may say in Tosafot Yevamot there they resolve with drusah is common — if so it is difficult for Rav Huna to enumerate the eighteen treifot born from life and not common, such as water found in the head and we do not know if the brain surrounded it — we also say "she was slaughtered, she is permitted"; and likewise that which is stated "a thorn sat in the esophagus," etc. — wondrous why Tosafot did not mention this in their challenge and in their answer why they took what they did not challenge from the outset. And one may answer: behold Tosafot with Rabbenu Shimshon disagree regarding cheese that was made and afterward a treifah animal was found — Tosafot hold the cheeses are forbidden and we do not say establish presumption; and now that it became treifah — because presumption is not clear, for from the day it was born one may say now it became treifah; and Rabbenu Shimshon holds the cheeses are permitted — we say one hour before slaughter it became treifah, we establish presumption, and it is clear presumption because of majority; and see Tosafot Chullin 11a s.v. atya from a red cow, etc., and their dispute: Tosafot hold since now there is a defect, the animal is not from majority — thus its majority deteriorated also retroactively and it is not presumption from majority; and Rabbenu Shimshon holds: now it deteriorated for it; but retroactively there was no defect, and we say presumptively it was from majority — if so, according to Tosafot it is difficult that which Rabbah said "she was slaughtered, she is permitted" — we hang on a wolf because we establish presumption; behold this is presumption that is not clear. And it must be said: since there is room to hang on a wolf, there is no defect here at all and its majority did not deteriorate, and it is presumption from majority; and one cannot challenge: if so in any case why does Rabbah take the reason of presumption that is from majority — let him say from majority. One may say: because he came to inform us where a safek was born that has majority for prohibition against the majority that permitted — Rabbah teaches us that nevertheless it is kosher because of presumption. If so, according to the flow Tosafot challenge nothing — why did Rabbah not also say safek drusah; and likewise that which they challenge in Chullin to establish safek drusah on presumption from life — behold there is no clear presumption here. And it must be said they hold: granted, regarding other treifot there is a defect and it is not presumption from majority, only regarding drusah this certainly was not at the hour it was born, for all the while it did not see a lion enter — even though the flesh opposite the intestinal children reddened — nevertheless kosher; if so there is fine clear presumption from the outset. And Tosafot challenge well from safek drusah; according to this it is fine why they challenge: why did Rabbah not enumerate other eighteen treifot where safek was born from life — for regarding other treifot there is no clear presumption here. Only this is difficult for them: why did they not take safek drusah that has clear presumption; and they answered drusah is common. And that which they wrote "and likewise a thorn sat," etc. — not that Tosafot want to answer what Rabbah did not mention in his words that of the thorn — on that they would not challenge from the outset, for it is different there because there was no clear presumption; only this is difficult for them: according to what they want to answer that drusah is common — if so what does the Gemara challenge at the beginning of chapter 8, and what is different from safek drusah — drusah is common; and on this they wrote: nevertheless it challenges well, for also "a thorn sat in the esophagus" is common to perforate; and accordingly Rama wrote here the hagahah: every safek born from life — there is no establishing presumption, for it is presumption that is not clear; according to this Darkei Moshe's words are correct that so is implied in Tosafot chapter Four Brothers — because they are strained in answering that drusah is common, nevertheless it is difficult why they did not take other safek treifot born from life that are not common; and it must be said it is presumption that is not clear, as I wrote — requires further study. But it is difficult — certainly Tosafot hold thus, because according to their method they hold that every other safek treifah is presumption that is not clear; but according to Rama below siman 81 seif 2 regarding a blow in the hoof — one may not forbid the milk milked from it before three days; and if so, perforce they hold like Rabbenu Shimshon's method that it is presumption from majority — see there in Shach s.k. 4. And if so Rama's words contradict each other; and one may say: this case of cheese — even according to those who argue on Rabbenu Shimshon it is permitted, for the entire reason of Tosafot is where there is a defect the presumption deteriorated; but here, since the safek depends on time, we say now it deteriorated and regarding before that there was no defect at all — unlike here with water in the brain where the safek is whether the brain surrounded it — we would have to establish presumption that the brain surrounded it; we say well: since there is a defect, presumption deteriorated since now in any case there is a defect; therefore Rashba and Tosafot forbid even though they do not forbid for one reason alone — Rama did not want to be lenient against them; and see above. And behold it is difficult for Tosafot's method who establish presumption from the outset — difficult is that which they answer Shegruna is common, as Rashba brought his proof; and therefore it must be said one of two reasons, either because it is presumption that is not clear; and according to Rabbenu Shimshon who holds it is clear presumption — in truth it must be like Rashba's method; if so here in any case it is forbidden; but below he holds Rabbenu Shimshon's method is primary, and since retroactively there was no defect the cheeses are permitted.
