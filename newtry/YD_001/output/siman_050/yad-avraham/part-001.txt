(Siman 50 seif 1 in the hagahah) Every place where one cannot hang that it was done after slaughter — even though it has inspection, if they did not inspect it or cannot inspect it — behold it is like safek treifah and we forbid it. And he wrote in responsa Tur HaEven siman 41 that even the hagahah was stringent only if it was already slaughtered and the organ was lost so it cannot be inspected; but when it was not yet slaughtered he admits we go leniently — and therefore he permits slaughtering on Yom Tov a bird in which a defect was born. And therefore he permits milk from an animal that limps and it is not known if its nivin were eaten. And especially in this treifah that certainly was not born with it and it has presumption of its body — also Tur HaShalem is lenient. And moreover R' Dain: we do not establish from prohibition to prohibition in safek of reality from Yevamot (daf 119) — we do not learn presumption of a married woman from presumption of prohibition of a yevamah for the marketplace. And therefore only regarding throwing a get are we stringent because Tosafot wrote in Ketubot (daf 22) that we have a get before us — unlike a balanced treifot book where even though a defect was born from life that was in a presumption that it is not slaughtered — it is a different prohibition. And all the more there is a majority of kashrus and no other majority against it to remove it. And even in a book if the esophagus was perforated — nevertheless "she was slaughtered" before us. Only in a book where it sat or was gored we establish from life that it is one prohibition that does not leave, and also there is in it "I am certain" — organ 45 like intestinal children when it was not slaughtered properly — as one who places in a basket; from when it was slaughtered siman 1 and they did not permit unless it was slaughtered with slaughter that permits to Israel. And in siman 17 he agreed to Tzitzat Tzvi's view regarding a found needle — we hang that it fell; and the same regarding found broken — if it is not recognizable that it was done from life we establish the original presumption and now it deteriorated — and not like Tur HaShalem. And see in Levushei Serad who wrote plainly to forbid lost buka without inspection, and he saw this from Mordechai forbidding without inspection. And there is no proof — Mordechai is according to his view who holds like the poskim Shach brings that we do not say "she was slaughtered, she is permitted" where there is nothing to hang on for permissibility, as Tur HaShalem wrote siman 29. (Seif 2) And the meaning of what we said: if created missing — treifah, all the more if removed by hand; and everything we said: if removed — treifah, the same law applies to created missing; and some permit. This is Rambam. And in my humble opinion he deals with treifot that became treifah because of themselves — such as the liver was removed or flayed, which is in the category of removed, as Rashi wrote beginning of chapter 8 (daf 43) and Shach brings siman 29. But in treifah that is not because of itself — such as the upper cheek was removed, which Rambam declares treifah as written siman 33 seif 2 because of lung cooling. And if so, there is no distinction here between removed by hand or created missing — for if the cheek was created missing, this is lung deficiency that we taught in the mishnah in chapter 8, or that it was deficient — as above, clear. And with this the Rashba's challenge in Tur HaShalem (daf 43) on Rambam is removed. And see at length siman 41. And what Tur HaShalem wrote on Rambam at the end of siman 29 from the Gemara Niddah (daf 24) — he left in requires further study; also the Rav in Kiryat Sefer sensed this and they settled it — see there. Indeed, what he wrote that there is proof for Rambam from the above Gemara — in my humble opinion one may distinguish between person and animal, as written in novellae on Niddah and in Rambam's composition Hilchot Issurei Bi'ah.
