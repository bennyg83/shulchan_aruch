**** ENGLISH ****
And if he did not stir — if the piece is partly in the gravy and milk fell not opposite the gravy: for R' Yehuda as if in the gravy; for Rashi as if outside the gravy. Taz's view: that piece is forbidden and the rest of the pot does not join with it to nullify the milk in sixty; since it became nevelah it forbids the pot until sixty against the whole piece. Shach's view: it does not forbid the whole pot, only the piece, and all the more it does not join with the piece to nullify; but nevertheless need sixty in the pot against the milk that fell, for we say at falling it immediately spreads through the whole pot — see Peleti.
