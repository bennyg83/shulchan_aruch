**** ENGLISH ****
That which we permitted with puncture of the outer membrane alone — specifically when punctured on its own account through illness; but if on account of a thorn or needle — treifah, for we are concerned perhaps one of the inner organs whose puncture is in any amount was punctured. And if he did not recognize whether the puncture was on account of a thorn or whether it is on account of illness — we lean to leniency (private opinion and Ohel Mo'ed).
