<b>כנגד הערקום כשרה. </b>ובש"ד סי' פ"ו כ' דנשבר העצם במקום הערקום עצמו טריפה שיש לחוש לשמא נפסק אחד מצומת הגידין כי שם הוא צומת הגידין ואפי' לא נשברה העצם ויצא לחוץ טרפה דחיישינן לפסיקת אחד מהגידין עכ"ל וכתב הב"ח בריש סי' נ"ה ואע"ג דבסימן נ"ו יתבאר דהתחלת הגידין הם למעלה מהערקום ואם נפסקו כנגד הערקו' פשרה אפ"ה החמיר הרב משו' דא"א בקיאין להבחין שלא נפסק א' מהגידין למעלה ואין להקל לענין מעשה נגד הש"ד עכ"ל וגם מהרי"ל בהלכות איסור חלב נבילה כתב כדברי ש"ד וכן מוכח ברש"י פא"ט (דף נ"ז ע"א) גבי ההוא צני דאנקורי פירש"י עופות שנשתברו רגליהן בארכובה למטה או למעלה כו' ובדקו בצומת הגידין כו' אלמ' דבארכובה גופי' שהיא הערקום נמי צריך בדיקה בצומת הגידין ואם כן לדידן שא"א בקיאין טרפה והא דכתבו הפוסקי' והט"ו סתמא דכנגד הערקום כשרה אפשר דאינהו מיירי בענין דליכא חששא דצומת הגידין למעלה כגון דנחתך לפנינו רק כנגד הערקום אבל היכא דנשבר הערקום ואיכא חששא דצומת הגידין טרפה דלא כנראה קצת מדברי מהרש"ל פרק המקשה סי' י"א דמכשיר בכה"ג ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
Opposite the hock — kosher. And in Sha'ar HaDin siman 86 he wrote: if the bone broke in the place of the hock itself — treifah, for there is concern perhaps one of the junction sinews was severed, for there is the junction of sinews; and even if the bone did not break and protruded outward — treifah, for we are concerned for severing one of the sinews, end of his words. And Bach wrote at the beginning of siman 55: and even though in siman 56 it will be explained that the beginning of the sinews is above the hock, and if severed opposite the hock — permitted — nevertheless the Rav was stringent because we cannot distinguish that one of the sinews was not severed above, and one may not be lenient in practice against Sha'ar HaDin, end of his words. And also Maharil in laws of forbidden fat wrote like Sha'ar HaDin, and so is implied in Rashi Avodah Zarah (daf 57a) regarding those baskets that were perforated — Rashi explained: birds whose legs broke at the knee below or above, etc., and they examined junction of sinews, etc. — implying that at the hock itself, which is the arukum, examination of junction of sinews is also required; and if so, for us who cannot be expert — treifah. And that which poskim and Tur wrote plainly that opposite the hock is kosher — possibly they deal with a case where there is no concern for junction of sinews above, such as where it was cut before us only opposite the hock; but where the arukum broke and there is concern for junction of sinews — treifah; not as appears somewhat from Maharshal's words chapter HaBehemah HaMekasheh siman 11, who permits in such a case — see there.

**** ENGLISH ****
["<b>Even if it is only swollen, etc.</b> And Maharshal wrote in Or Zarua and in his book chapter HaBehemah HaMekasheh siman 15: indeed everything is according to the swelling, and the authority must before declaring treifah cut the swelling and examine it — if it appears from within the swelling that there is decay and dissolution, and there is concern that one of the strands was severed — then declare treifah, end of his words. And Bach wrote: possibly the Rav said only regarding swelling; but regarding change of appearance alone, such as bluish or greenish or yellowish — in such cases there is no defect at all, and without swelling we declare entirely kosher, and one need not cut and see if there is decay and dissolution; nevertheless it is good to be stringent and examine by cutting, etc., end of his words. And similarly Taz wrote: it appears to me specifically when the blood was greatly clotted until the sinews are almost unrecognizable because of the blood; but small blood clots — why declare treifah when no defect is seen in the sinews; if we come to declare treifah all that come before us swollen and with blood clots, even a little, we would need to declare treifah the majority and majority of majority — and this does not appear to me; rather inquiry and investigation of a God-fearing sage is required even in our time — so it appears to me, not like Sefer Maggid David who challenged him in lip-words but unnecessarily."]
