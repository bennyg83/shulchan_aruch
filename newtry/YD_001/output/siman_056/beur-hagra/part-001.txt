**** ENGLISH ****
And some say, etc. For so Ra'avad explained the dispute of Rabbah bar bar Chanah and Rabbah whether outward or inward; and this is what is written "outside and inside" — we rule like Rabbah bar Rav Huna whom Shmuel follows, who said the place where sinews form junction — this is inward; but outward the three are not all connected; and so Amemar said one is strong, etc.; outward you will not find one larger than two; and even when two — Rava son of Rabbah bar Rav Huna does not explain them but explains the place of their beginning.

**** ENGLISH ****
And so is primary. For every safek — stringently, as written above siman 55; and so all poskim ruled every unresolved treifah question stringently, and every amoraic dispute; and as written there in the name of Rav Pappa: therefore we require majority, etc.; and so there 46a in the name of Rav Pappa: therefore we require, etc. (At first glance there is no proof from this, for also in rabbinic matters we find thus in Shabbat 20a in the name of Rav Pappa; and the reason is as Ran wrote in chapter Eizehu Mekoman on what is stated there 108a: now that it was stated, etc.; and see our Rabbi in Orach Chaim siman 338 s.k. 3).
