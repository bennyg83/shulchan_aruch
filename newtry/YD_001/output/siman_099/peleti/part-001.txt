Peri Chadash expanded at length on this root and stood on investigation to clarify; and I will bring his words briefly to clarify the law on clear grounds with Heaven's help. And first Rambam ruled in the laws of Terumah: a se'ah of terumah that fell into a hundred of hullin and he did not manage to remove it before another fell after knowledge — it is not forbidden; and if not it forbids.
