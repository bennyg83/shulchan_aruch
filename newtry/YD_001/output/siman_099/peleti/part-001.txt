Issur bones, etc. — Ri'az in the name of a Gaon brought that this applies in its species — d'oraisa in the majority is nullified, but in not its species not; and the Rav author of Pri Chadash held to his view since they have no expulsion from themselves — like a pot, for we say "is heter absorbed and issur not?" — why should it combine to nullify, and why should the pottery body not combine? Therefore this is only in its species which is only shiyur d'rabbanan; and the Rav in several places wrote it does not appear so to Rashba and Rosh; and the correct view is that besides since they wrote stam and did not distinguish, they also challenged in what the Gemara says according to the one who says in sixty "meat with bones we estimate" — it appears bones do not combine to nullify issur; and according to Ri'az's view this is only asmakhta they learned from not its species, for in its species in the majority it is nullified and they only said in sixty because it applies to not its species; and in not its species indeed bones do not combine to nullify issur; and conversely according to Pri Chadash's words it appears even heter bones in not its species should not be combined, for it is like a pot; therefore it appears they wondered how the Gemara says according to the one who says in sixty "meat with bones we estimate" — behold bones combine to nullify issur; and see Rosh who forced that in the Gemara it is only asmakhta mere; and already the Ra'ah wondered in Beit HaYayin; therefore what appears in Pri Chadash's difficulties it appears Rosh etc. answered and said one may say there is moisture in bones, nevertheless it is not better than gid moisture, for we hold there is not noten ta'am in gid; therefore in truth there is weak taste in them, for the palate will testify if one cooks bones in water they change the water in taste and color, only as we must say in this that they disagree whether there is noten ta'am in gid or not — certainly they do not disagree about the reality of the palate tasting, as Rashba wrote — only weak taste exists and they disagree whether it is full taste; and if so even if we disagree whether there is noten ta'am in gid, certainly weak taste exists in bones like gid and not full taste like what the Torah forbade; but in any event since there is taste, even if extremely weak, at least it helps nullify issur taste like water and is not comparable to a pot that has no expulsion at all. And if so Pri Chadash's difficulty is resolved correctly; but accordingly according to the one who says there is noten ta'am in gid who holds even weak taste the Torah forbade — even in bones one may say so to be strict in the difficulty; one must understand that the Gemara seeks support from foreleg cooking to shiyur issurim — even Rashi wrote explicitly the conclusion is we estimate in meat all around, and this is in sixty as known, as the Gemara says; and one must say those amora'im of tradition hold there is gid as Rashi wrote and as appears from the Gemara; and this case is different — gid whose taste is weak for all, and if so they needed to doubt shiyur noten; but according to the one who says there is not noten ta'am in gid and they do not disagree about the conclusion that in this we estimate in meat all around — the dispute is difficult; and as Rambam wrote; and end here it was other issurim; and accordingly this foreleg cooking sugya is all according to the one who says there is noten ta'am in gid; and if so well according to the one who says in sixty that bones have weak expulsion and Pri Chadash's difficulty that it is no better than gid — there is a question, for in truth issur bones forbid and heter bones nullify; and the other view holds like the poskim that they have no expulsion at all and are not comparable to gid, therefore they are not included in issur and also not in gravy, as Pri Chadash wrote they have no expulsion at all. But still difficult is Yerushalmi that asked if heter bones combine to nullify and concludes heter shells raise the forbidden — how do we learn one from its counterpart regarding bones even in not its species, and shells one must say in dry and its species; and how do we learn one from its counterpart? But in truth in Yerushalmi chapter 3 it asked in this whether in a hundred or sixty and says according to the one who says in a hundred you remove issur bones — for that view one must say they have no expulsion as written above; and if so well it asked afterward even heter bones do not help, for it is like a pot; and it answers you cannot, for the Sages made it like its species so heter shells help to nullify; and even here in its species it is only stringency mere to help nullify and is only asmakhta that is only in its species. Yerushalmi's words are correct; but according to the one who says in sixty he holds bones have weak expulsion and therefore they forbid and heter ones help — investigate. But this is according to the one who says there is noten ta'am in gid — if so this weak expulsion for issur is no better than gid and permitted helps nullify — there is a question; and the Gemara's words are correct and the law is true law: and that which Yerushalmi says terumah in onion in egg and the vegetable and the water and shells combine — even though shells have no expulsion and are no better than a bowl and it is not its species — there is a question; one may also say it deals where there are also eggs; and d'oraisa in the majority is nullified; and even though vegetable and water are not its species, etc.; and this is proof according to Rashba in siman the blood that even on not its species they say etc. and egg without noten ta'am so much that they considered estimating in mashahu and fifty as explained in the Gemara and Yerushalmi only stringently in sixty because not to distinguish among other issurim; and in any event shells combine lest all shells exceed this measure, as Shach wrote explicitly in siman chapter 6 s.k. 15 — see there — investigate between in its species and not its species; and there is a question; and all Pri Chadash's and Rosh's difficulties are resolved:

"This applies in not its species, etc." — this is Rashba's view and the reason: it appears it deals where we knew how much issur there was when it fell and we estimate against all of it; otherwise even in its species one cannot estimate — are you saying heter absorbed and issur not absorbed? — why not in not its species; and one must say as if the pot absorbed from water to water why not enter that absorption into the calculation, for it also absorbs and expels; and one must say we are not clear what the pot absorbed in cooking whether it fell or receives from issur; and if so even in this lest a spoonful of known mixture gave issur taste to heter already absorbed in the pot and this does not enter the calculation as if absorbed from water — what Rashba wrote and afterward the issur in heter taste and there is not sixty here; therefore heter itself needs sixty and that is what appears Rashba's reason for stringency: and behold Tosafot separated in what we say regarding the pot itself — the explanation is not against the pot's earthenware, only if it absorbed two zaytim from water and afterward issur zayit — nevertheless if it expels also the two zaytim absorbed from water they combine to nullify; and if it does not expel, also issur does not expel; and it appears Tosafot hold we do not say in absorbed piece it becomes nevelah — for otherwise those two zaytim already became nevelah and forced to say it deals with absorbing meat, for still heter — this was not mentioned in Tosafot; and accordingly one may say the latter version that speaks in what the pot absorbed does not disagree on this halachah, only holds the explanation of pot is a different matter; but for halachah he agrees to this law; and from this Issur Veheter end general principle derives so too if two handfuls stuck absorbed — the second absorbed combines to nullify the first absorbed; and that is as appears if necessarily if it expels this too expels and if not this too does not expel — and these are Tosafot's words as they are; and in necessarily in pot or two handfuls when they are one species; therefore he wrote if the pot is metal and spoon metal this necessarily does not apply since one cannot expel more than its fellow — but these pot and spoon are also metal, both can necessarily expel, only afterward scrubbing to be strict in pot perhaps spoon touches spoon in pot and pot absorbs from it and becomes nevelah the absorbed and afterward expels; and if so how can absorbed in pot that is already chein nafsho combine — it is stringency mere; and in any event its law is founded on Tosafot's words as solid proof; and from kli sheni in its species that even if we say the latter version disagrees in a d'rabbanan matter we act leniently as Rosh and Beit Yosef explicitly; and if so clear law from Tosafot's view.
