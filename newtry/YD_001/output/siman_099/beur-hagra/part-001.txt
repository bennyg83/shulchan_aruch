**** ENGLISH ****The piece, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 5 of Terumot: waving of terumah does not combine with terumah to forbid hullin, but waving of hullin combines with hullin to raise terumah; and in chapter 1 of Orlah and chapter 6 of Nazir — R' Avahu in the name of R' Yochanan: all prohibitions we estimate as if they were onion and garlic. What is the case? These say noten taam one in a hundred, and these say noten taam one in sixty. One who says in sixty — you do not make the foreleg one part in a hundred of the ram; one who says in a hundred — you make the foreleg one part in a hundred of the ram. One who says in a hundred — you remove the bones from the foreleg; one who says in sixty — you do not remove the bones from the foreleg. And just as you remove the bones from the foreleg, so you remove from the ram — you cannot, for it teaches: waving of terumah does not combine with terumah to forbid hullin, but waving of hullin combines with hullin to raise terumah. Rav Beivai asked: does waving of terumah combine with hullin to raise terumah? From what R' Huna said — peels of prohibition combine with heter — this says waving of terumah combines with hullin to raise terumah. And in chapter 8 of Terumot — an egg in sixty: R' Chanina said the vegetable and the shell combine; R' Zeira said and its prohibition is from inside — meaning we estimate only the edible part of the egg. Rav Huna said peels of prohibition raise heter; and Yerushalmi above disagrees with that which there says according to one who says in sixty the bones of the foreleg combine with prohibition and all the more so they do not combine with heter; and the sugya in chapter 3 of Chullin 98b disagrees more — for according to one who says in a hundred even peels of heter do not combine with heter, for it says meat with meat, etc. And it appears the Yerushalmi there and "just as you remove," etc. — rather one may say it is not precise, and since it mentioned meat in the foreleg it also mentioned meat in the ram; nevertheless we learn that for all sugyot the bones of heter combine with heter, and all the more so according to one who says in sixty as written in the Gemara meat and bones with meat, etc.; but according to one who says in sixty, whether in the Gemara sugya or in the Yerushalmi there, the bones of prohibition combine with prohibition and all the more so they do not combine with heter; and at first glance this disagrees with that in chapter 8 of Terumot as above; and Ran wrote that bones in the foreleg are different — they combine with prohibition because of marrow in them and because of their softness moisture emerges; but dry bones are like peels in chapter 8 of Terumot that are no better than gidin where we establish there is no noten taam that forbids; and as written in chapter 1 of Terumot regarding bones of kodshim in the time, etc. — R' Avahu in the name of R' Yochanan at the wing-tips and tendons — that is the mishnah; and so in the Gemara in Pesachim chapter 3: if we say there is no marrow in them, etc. — and on this they disagree: according to one who says in a hundred, even those that have marrow do not combine with prohibition; and we establish like one who says in sixty; and this is what Yerushalmi there says "and just as you," etc. "you cannot" for it teaches "there is not," etc.; and at first glance that baraita is a refutation of one who says in sixty — but it is not, for even one who says in sixty concedes regarding waving and peels; only according to one who says in a hundred even those that have marrow are like waving; and this is what it says "you cannot," etc.; and this is what Shulchan Aruch says "but the marrow," etc.; and Shach: likewise bones that are soft, as above from Yerushalmi there wing-tips, etc.:
