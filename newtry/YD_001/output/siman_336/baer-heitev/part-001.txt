**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Their own.</b> Bach writes that the text should read, "his obligation." The meaning is that not only where the physician already received the fee he fixed is it not taken back from him, but even if he has received nothing and merely set a fee for which they obligated themselves, they must pay the full amount agreed. Taz writes that this differs from one fleeing prison who reached a ferry and said, "Take me across and I will give you a dinar," where he need pay only the normal fee, as stated in Choshen Mishpat siman 264. There the service has a standard market price. Medical expertise has no fixed measure, and the physician had no prior obligation to teach or provide this particular expertise. Therefore the condition is effective here.
