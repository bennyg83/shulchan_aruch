In the responsum of Maharam Prague printing siman 965: a scribe of Reuven gave a handshake to Shimon that when he finishes Reuven's work he will not write for anyone except Shimon; afterward the city burned and one of Reuven's pamphlets that the scribe had written was burned; now the scribe asks whether he may rewrite that pamphlet when he finishes his work before writing for Shimon. And when the pamphlet burned he had not yet finished the book for Reuven — he can certainly write, for Reuven's work is not finished. But certainly if the pamphlet burned after he had completed the entire book then his work would have been finished and he could not write. Evidence from tractate Kelim chapter 27: a garment in which he wove three by three, etc. Moshe Azriel son of Rabbi Eliezer Darshan — end. And the same applies to all similar work.
