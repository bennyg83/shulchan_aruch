**** ENGLISH ****
<b>It is forbidden, etc.; nevertheless, etc. </b>See Tosafot there, beginning with the words "And Ri said," etc. <br><b>(Supplement) It is forbidden, etc. </b>This follows Rabbi Yitzchak. See Tosafot there in the name of Halachot Gedolot. It appears to me that the reason is that the discussion at the end of the twelfth chapter of Yevamot accords with his opinion, for the Talmud says there: from the word "no" until "to take her." See Rosh in Yevamot, who expressed surprise at this and wrote that we hold that three words may be written. It is surprising that the cited Tosafot escaped his attention, especially since he himself wrote the same point in the second chapter of Megillah. <small>See Magen Avraham, siman 32, subsection 7; his words are strained.</small>
