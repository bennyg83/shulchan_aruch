**** ENGLISH ****
It is forbidden to write three words from a verse without sirtut if it is Assyrian script. (And there is no difference between writing and engraving on wood or gold) (Beit Yosef in the name of R' Yerucham, Netiv 2). Nevertheless, in a letter of greetings it is permitted to write even several words from a verse for a witty matter. {Rama: And if he ruled one upper line, he need not do so again (Tur in the name of Rabbenu Tam). And some say one should not write mundane matters in Assyrian script with which the Torah is written (there in the name of Rabbenu Yerucham).} (°)
