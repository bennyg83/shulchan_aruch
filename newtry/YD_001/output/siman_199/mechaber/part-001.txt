**** ENGLISH ****
A woman must check her concealed places and the laws of chafifah on Shabbos and on a weekday; it contains 13 se’ifim. She needs to rinse the armpit and her concealed places with water (and not with other liquids) (Mahari”k, shoresh 159). And she needs to comb the hair of her head well with a comb, so that her hairs not be stuck to one another. And a woman also needs to examine herself and her flesh, and checks her entire body close to her immersion, so that there not be on her anything repulsive that interposes. And she should wash her entire body, and rinse with hot water at the time she washes her body and her hair.

<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="28"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="30"></i>חפפה קודם טבילה ובין חפיפה לטבילה <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="29"></i>נתעסקה בדברים החוצצין <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="20"></i>או שנתנה לבנה <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="16"></i>תבשיל <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="30"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="31"></i>הראוי לידבק בה <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="31"></i>לא עלתה לה טבילה <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="32"></i>אפילו אם בדקה מיד אחר טבילה ולא מצאה עליה שום דבר חוצץ שאני אומר בעלייתה מהמים נפל ממנה וצריכה טבילה אחרת: <small>הגה מיהו אם בדקה עצמה קודם טבילה וראתה שלא נדבק בה שום דבר אינה צריכה טבילה אחרת (הגהות ש"ד ומרדכי וב"י בשם הרשב"א) <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="21"></i>אבל מותרת ללבוש <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="17"></i>בגדיה בין חפיפה לטבילה ולא תקח תינוק אצלה (ב"י בשם הרמב"ם וע"ל סימן קצ"ח):</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
If she washed before immersion, and between the washing and the immersion she dealt with interposing things, or gave her son a cooked dish that is fit to stick to her, the immersion did not count for her — even if she checked immediately after immersion and did not find on her anything interposing — for I say that when she ascended from the water it fell from her, and she needs another immersion. {Rama: Nevertheless, if she checked herself before immersion and saw that nothing stuck to her, she does not need another immersion (Hagahos Sha”d and Mordechai and Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba). But it is permitted for her to wear her garments between the washing and the immersion, but she should not take a child near her (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam; and see above siman 198).}
