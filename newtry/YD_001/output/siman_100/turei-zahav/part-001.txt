<b>בריה דהיינו כו'. </b>חשיבות דבריה שלא תהיה בטילה היא מדרבנן דכיון שמן התורה יש חשיבות דלוקין על אכילתה אפי' היא פחות מכזית דהא סתמא כתיב אצל מינים טמאים לא תאכל אותם אפי' הם קטנים ע"כ החמירו רבנן בתערובת שלהם אבל מן התורה היא בטילה כשאר איסורים ונ"מ לענין ספק אי הוה בריה או לא הוה ספיקא דרבנן ובטל וכן בכל הדברים דאמרינן לקמן שאינם בטלים כגון ראוי להתכבד או דבר שבמנין אבל אי הוה ודאי בריה או א' מהני שאין מתבטלים אלא שהאיסור ספק בזה אפילו באלף לא בטיל כגון ביצי ספק טריפה וכיוצא בו וכ"כ או"ה כלל כ"ה דין ז':
**** ENGLISH ****
Beriah, etc. Peri Chadash expanded here that it is agreed from all poskim and is clear in the Gemara in Gittin chapter Nezikin that all these that are not nullified — beriah and davar chashuv — are only d'rabbanan. However, Maharil in a responsum inferred from Tosafot in Bava Metzia who challenged from one who jumped into the counting, etc., that they should be nullified in the majority and he would be liable for tithe, and davar sheb'minyan would not apply for it is only d'rabbanan; and beriah likewise would not apply as I explained, and this is because it is not forbidden from the beginning of its creation, etc. — from the power of beriah it is d'oraisa; and likewise he inferred from Tosafot in Me'ilah — end of his words. And what I wrote regarding the assembly of elders for Bava Metzia bringing Tosafot's own view that one may say: even though only d'rabbanan it is not nullified, nevertheless since the Sages made it, they have power to nullify d'oraisa — see Yevamot 14; investigate. It appears that if the Sages established it certainly as an iron peg that will not move until even to nullify d'oraisa it is so — if so it is plain that even in d'rabbanan prohibition we say it is not nullified, for they established their words certainly until they nullified d'oraisa — how would it not be so in d'rabbanan; but if we see they were not stringent in d'rabbanan and there they said it is nullified, we learn they did not establish certainly, only they were concerned to be stringent; therefore in d'rabbanan they were not stringent — and behold this is similar to a doubt forbidden by Torah law and permitted d'rabbanan; if so it is only a stringency — how can we be lenient to nullify thereby an issur and mitzvah of Torah? This is clear in reason; if so we see regarding beriah we do not find a distinction between Torah law and their words; and eiver min hachai in which there is only a mitzvah of eating — is also called beriah; and likewise gid hanasheh even in a gid that is not forbidden by Torah as explained siman 65 is beriah as explained; but in davar sheb'minyan we find explicitly in the Gemara in Yevamot chapter HaAreil: I teach "round in its rounds" — it rises because of terumah d'rabbanan — behold this davar sheb'minyan is only in Torah prohibition and not in d'rabbanan; if so one can say Tosafot's words who challenged that it should be nullified in the majority — end of his words.

<b>וכן צריך שיהא שלם. </b>פירוש דל"ת דעיקר הקפידה שצריך שיהא שייך בו לומר אם יחלק אין שמו עליו ואז הוה בריה אפילו באמת אינו שלם קמ"ל דדוקא יהא ג"כ שלם. ואם נתרסקה הבריה עיין בסימן ק"א סעיף ו':
**** ENGLISH ****
And if it dripped beneath it, etc. — Shach wrote: if the fat is attached to one piece, there must be sixty in that piece against the fat — end of Taz.

<b>אם יש בו ס' נגד שומנו. </b>ואם השומן דבוק בחתיכה אחת צריך שיהיה באותה חתיכה ס' נגד השומן ואם לאו צריך ס' נגד כל החתיכה ההיא שהאיסור דבוק בה לדידן דאמרינן סי' צ"ב בכל האיסורים חתיכה עצמה נעשית נבילה:
**** ENGLISH ****
The fat. Taz and Shach wrote: if the fat is attached to one piece, sixty is required in that piece against the fat; and if not, sixty against the entire piece — for us who say in siman 92 regarding all prohibitions chad k'neged chad.
