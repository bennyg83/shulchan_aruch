Beriah — Peri Chadash expanded that it is agreed from all poskim and clear in Gittin chapter Nezikin that beriah and davar chashuv not being nullified is only d'rabbanan. Maharil inferred from Tosafot Bava Metzia regarding one who jumped into counting that it should nullify in majority for tithe, and davar sheb'minyan and beriah would not apply as d'rabbanan; he also inferred from Tosafot Me'ilah regarding one who declared a perutah in a purse of hekdesh — end of his words. What I wrote in the assembly of elders: Tosafot's view that even d'rabbanan is not nullified since Sages can nullify Torah — see Yevamot 14; if Sages fixed it certainly, even d'rabbanan is not nullified; if not fixed certainly, only stringency — cannot nullify Torah issur. We do not find distinction for beriah between Torah and rabbinic words. Eiver min hachai with only eating mitzvah is beriah; gid hanasheh even when not Torah-forbidden (siman 65) is beriah. In davar sheb'minyan, Yevamot HaAreil teaches explicitly it applies only to Torah terumah, not d'rabbanan — Tosafot's challenge is resolved: Sages did not fix certainly for Torah law. Maharshal on Mishnah Shekalim: priests derive from Scripture to exempt themselves from shekalim — how can their shekalim be nullified in majority of Israel's shekalim for omer — requires study; possibly dry-in-dry even according to Rabbi Yehudah; or Torah said omer is eaten though priests have a share — therefore no concern Sages decreed coin not nullified lest omer be eaten improperly — Torah shekalim nullify; Sages would not create stumbling block in offerings — end of his words. Objection from "Torah did not command to cause stumbling" in several places — answered: davar sheb'minyan for all is d'rabbanan per Tosafot; challenge for Rabbi who does not hold biryah chashuv — requires study. Rosh in Orlah: leper bird not nullified because Torah compared it to impure fowl — cannot say nullification where recognizable by signs of impurity — likewise bird by decree not to nullify — "sent to cause stumbling." Chullin 115 regarding sending the nest — challenge remains; possibly bird hunted and mixed with tereifah — strained. More likely: issur nullified in heter but issur still hidden — proof from shiluach where issur remains until eaten in issur — Yevamot 104 requires verses for blood and sheratzim to warn adults about minors — Tosafot: ant is beriah and forbids mixture unlike blood — end of his words.
