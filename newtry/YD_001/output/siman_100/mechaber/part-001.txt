**** ENGLISH ****
A beriah is not nullified even in a thousand. In it are 4 seifim: Beriah — meaning such as an ant or impure fowl, and gid hanasheh, and eiver min hachai, and an egg that has an embryo in it, and the like — even in a thousand is not nullified; and it does not have the status of beriah unless it is a thing that had life in it, to exclude one grain of forbidden wheat; and likewise it must be a thing forbidden from the beginning of its creation, to exclude pure fowl that became nevelah and an ox condemned to stoning; and likewise it must be a whole entity such that if divided its name no longer applies to it, to exclude milk; and likewise it must be whole: {Rama: And the essence of gid hanasheh is only on the spoon alone, and it is the width of four fingers; and if it is whole it is called beriah (Arukh general rule 28).}

A thing that is a beriah that was cooked with the permitted food — if one does not recognize it, everything is forbidden and the gravy is nosen taam; and if one recognizes it, one discards it and the others, and the gravy requires sixty against it, except gid hanasheh whose taste does not forbid, for there is no nosen taam in gidim; but its fat forbids and requires sixty against it to permit the gravy; therefore a thigh that was cooked with its gid — if one recognizes it, one discards it and all the rest is permitted if there is sixty against its fat; and if one does not recognize it, all pieces are forbidden; and if there is sixty in the pot against the fat, the gravy is permitted, and if not, forbidden; and if the gid melted and is not recognizable, one also needs sixty against it.
