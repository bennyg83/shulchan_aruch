One should be careful that two circumcisers do not circumcise one circumcision on Shabbat — that this one circumcises and that one uncovers; rather the circumciser himself uncovers: {Rama: And I did not find proof for his words; on the contrary it appears to me permitted, for circumcision overrides Shabbat like service in the Temple where several kohanim served and desecrated Shabbat — for once Shabbat was given to be overridden, behold it is like profane for everything; and so I found in Sefer HaTerumah the old, written on parchment at the end of Laws of Shabbat explicitly that it is permitted; however I found in a collection that one should forbid; therefore it is good to be stringent ab initio, even though by law it appears to me as I wrote (individual opinion, not like Beit Yosef).}
