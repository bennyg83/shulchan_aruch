<b>לא יעשה מלאכה כו'. </b>דוקא הני שהן דברים של פרהסיא אסור אבל להשאילו כליו או בהמתו וכה"ג מותר אם היה משאילו בלאו הכי כיון שעושה אותו מדעת הלוה כדלעיל סי' ק"ס ס"ז וכ"כ הפוסקים וכ' עוד הרא"ש שי"מ היכא דידוע שקודם ההלואה היו אוהבים זא"ז כ"כ שהיו משאילים חצר זה לזה אם היו צריכים מותר להשאיל גם לאחר הלואה ואינו אסור רק בסתם בני אדם וכ"כ המרדכי ושאר פוסקים ומדברי מהרש"ל פ' הגוזל עצים סי' ט' נראה דלא שרי בכה"ג אלא דברים שאינן של פרהסיא כגון השאלת בהמה וכלים ע"ש וכתב עוד מהרש"ל שם סי' י"א מי שלוה מחבירו אינו רשאי לכבדו במצוה כגון לקרותו לס"ת או לקנות לו גלילה אע"פ שהוא יודע בעצמו שבלאו הלואה נמי עביד ליה אפ"ה מאחר שאוושא מלתא דומה לבית ועבד דאסור אם לא שידוע לכל שלא מחמת הלואה קא עביד וע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
He shall not perform work etc. Specifically those matters that are public — forbidden; but lending him vessels or his animal and similar things — permitted if he had lent them even without the loan, since it is done with the borrower’s knowledge, as above siman 160 seif 7; and so the poskim write; and the Rosh further wrote that some explain: where it is known that before the loan they loved each other so much that they lent courtyard to each other when needed — it is permitted to lend even after the loan; and it is forbidden only with ordinary people; and so the Mordechai and other poskim; and from Maharshal’s words chapter HaGozel Etzim siman 9 it appears that only matters that are not public — such as lending animals and vessels — are permitted in such a case — see there; and Maharshal further wrote there siman 11: one who borrowed from his fellow may not honor him with a mitzvah — such as calling him up to Torah or buying him the hagbahah — even if he himself knows that without the loan he would do it for him too; nevertheless since the matter becomes publicized it resembles house and slave which is forbidden — unless it is known to all that he does it not on account of the loan — see there.

And if he already dwelt in it etc. Meaning: even though lechatchilah it is forbidden in every case — nevertheless if in practice he already dwelt in it, since the courtyard does not stand for rent and the case is such that if he had not lent to him he would not have been obligated to pay rent on account of his dwelling — in the manner explained in CM siman 363 seif 6, 7, 8 — even now that he lent to him it does not appear as ribbit.

מרויח לו זמן בפירוש רק שיכול לתבעו אימת שירצה א"כ כשא"ל דור בחצרי חנם בשכר הלואתך ע"ד שמרחיב לו זמן כך והוי ר"ק כשמרחיב לו זמן וגם הטור מודה לזה מיהו היכא שא"ל בשעה שמרחיב לו זמן דור בחצרי חנם אע"פ שלא א"ל בשכר הלוואתך ה"ל ר"ק למ"ד דלא בעינן קצץ בשעת מתן מעות ודו"ק. כתב עוד הרב המגיד שאם דר בה שלא מדעת בעליה ולכשידעו הניחו לו שכר מחמת ההלואה ה"ז א"ר ע"כ. ונ"ל שכבר הרחיב לו זמן וכמו שנתבאר ודלא **** ENGLISH ****It is avak ribbit, etc. Beit Yosef wrote in name of students of Rashba: even though avak ribbit and does not come out in court from ribbit — nevertheless he must pay rent, for since it stands for rent even unspecified he must pay rent, for he caused monetary loss; and because there is prohibition his power is not weakened; and even if courtyard owner knew he dwelt and was silent, it does not imply intent to dwell free; and so if he said when he lent him dwell in my courtyard — he must pay rent; however if after he lent him he said dwell in my courtyard free and did not mention rent of his loan — in such case certainly even though it stands for rent it is only avak ribbit since he did not fix with him; and if he dwelt exempt from paying rent — end quote; and likewise in name of Ramban and Rashba: where it stands for rent, if he dwelt without owners' knowledge — plainly he must give rent from his own and we extract from him, for it is no worse than if he had not lent him at all where there is no prohibition; and Beit Yosef brought — meaning: and some say other avak ribbit that is with borrower's knowledge; and it seems to me likewise where it does not stand for rent, if case where he would have been obligated to pay rent if he had not lent him — e.g. he said leave, or caused borrower small loss such as blackened or revealed intent to pay, as stated there CM seif 6, 7, 8; and all such we extract; and he further wrote likewise even if he paid and they did not claim, he can sue forever in court, unlike Raavad who wrote it is like delayed ribbit; Beit Yosef wrote reason: he did not forgive since he did not specify; or even if specified, forgiveness in error — requires study if explicit; and students of Rashba wrote; and so if he said when he lent him dwell in my courtyard — must pay rent — meaning even if implication of dwell in my courtyard is free, must pay rent since he said thus at time of loan — it is as lend me and dwell in my courtyard free, which is fixed ribbit; and what he wrote: however if after he lent, etc., and did not mention rent of his loan — implies if he said dwell for rent of your loan in my courtyard free, even after he lent — fixed ribbit; Tur's view implies after he lent him even if he said dwell in my courtyard free for your money — only avak ribbit, as below s.k. 9; however Tur deals where he already profited time; and it seems students of Rashba deal where he did not profit time — each day its time; therefore where he said for rent of your loan — fixed ribbit even if he does not explicitly profit time, only that he can claim whenever he wishes; so when he said dwell in my courtyard free for rent of your loan — per extending time — fixed ribbit when extending time; and Tur agrees; however where he said at time he extends time dwell in my courtyard free even though he did not say for rent of your loan — fixed ribbit per those who hold we do not require fixing at time of giving money — understand well. Maggid Mishneh further wrote: if he dwelt without owners' knowledge and when they knew left him rent because of this loan — avak ribbit — end quote; and it seems he already extended time, as explained, unlike Beit Yosef who wrote this is view of those who hold we require fixing at time of giving money.
