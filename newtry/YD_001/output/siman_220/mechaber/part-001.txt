**** ENGLISH ****
The distinction between "konam wine that I taste today" and "for a time," and the law of one who vows regarding an unfixed matter; it contains twenty-three se'ifim. "Konam wine that I taste for one day," or "konam wine that I taste for a day" — he is forbidden from hour to hour. And if he said, "Konam wine that I taste today" — he is forbidden only until it grows dark, whether he stood at the beginning of the day or at its end; and he needs questioning by a sage, as a decree lest another time he forbid upon himself "one day" and not know to distinguish between "day" and "one day," and come to drink at night. And there is one who says that if it was a vow of mitzvah, such as to learn or to fulfill some mitzvah, he does not need release. {Rama: However, regarding accepting a fast in general, he is permitted to eat immediately at night, and he does not need release by a sage (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran).}
