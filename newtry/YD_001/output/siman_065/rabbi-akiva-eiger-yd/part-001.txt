**** ENGLISH ****
(Siman 65 seif 4) Male eggs. In the Gemara it states "eggs of a kid"; and Rambam wrote kid or lamb; and Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote: I do not know if the Gemara mentioned kid to exclude coarse animals — that even within thirty days it is forbidden:
