**** ENGLISH ****
A child who is red or green, and one whose children die from circumcision. And it contains 4 seifim. A minor who is green — a sign that blood did not fall in him; we do not circumcise him until blood falls in him and his appearance returns like other minors' appearance. And likewise if he is found red — a sign that his blood was not absorbed in his limbs.
