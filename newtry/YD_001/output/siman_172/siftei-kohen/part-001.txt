**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And he writes, etc.</b> This implies that the basis for the permission is that he writes this formulation in the document. The Tur also implies this, and Tosafot likewise wrote so on page 67, at the beginning of the second side. Accordingly, even if the lender has the power to collect his debt whenever he wishes, it is permitted. This is also explicit in the Rosh. It is possible that the Maggid Mishneh agrees, and that when he wrote that the reason is that the lender lacks the power to collect his debt, he cited one of two reasons. A practical difference arises wherever the lender cannot compel the borrower, even though the borrower can remove him: even if the document does not state, “when he pays,” etc., it is permitted. The Maggid Mishneh also concludes there with this reason in the name of Ramban. Nevertheless, the plain meaning of his words there implies that we always require that the lender have no power to collect any part of his debt. This also appears to be the Mechaber’s view.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Some say there is no distinction, etc.</b> This wording is imprecise. According to these authorities, namely Rashba and those who follow him, there is certainly a distinction between a place where the borrower may remove the lender and a place where he may not, when there is no deduction. In a place where he may remove him, it is fixed ribbit; where he may not remove him, it is avak ribbit, as their words state explicitly. Since there is a prohibition in either case, the Rama did not trouble to distinguish between them. Their words also clearly imply that, according to both views, even if the lender can collect his debt, the arrangement is permitted when there is a deduction. This is also the prevailing custom, unlike the Levush, who inserted into the words of the “some say” that the lender may not compel the borrower, etc., and then wrote, “and this is the custom,” etc. See there, for his words are confused.

<b>אלא כשאחריות המשכונות כו'. </b>אין ר"ל כשיאבדו המשכנות לא יתחייב הלוה לשלם חובו דהא משמע מדברי ה"ה בשם הרמב"ן דאפילו גובה משאר נכסיו מותר אלא ר"ל לאפוקי כשכותב לו אחריות על שאר נכסיו דאז כשיתקלקל המשכון מיד יש לו משכון אחר אבל כשלא כתב לו אחריות על נכסיו אע"פ שאם יתקלקלו המשכנות גובה משאר נכסיו מ"מ כיון שאין לו מיד משכון אחר שרי וכן משמע בטור וכתב הב"ח סי"ב דאע"ג דגובה משאר נכסיו מותר (ולפמ"ש א"צ לדחוק ולפרש לשון מיד שכ' הטור כמ"ש הב"ח אלא ה"ק דאין לו לבקש על נכסים אחרים דמיד יש לו משכון אחר) ובעט"ז לא הבין כן וע"ש שדבריו מעורבבים:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Except when the responsibility for the collateral, etc.</b> This does not mean that if the collateral is lost the borrower is exempt from paying the debt, for the Maggid Mishneh’s words in the name of Ramban imply that it is permitted even when the lender may collect from the borrower’s other assets. Rather, it excludes a case in which the borrower writes that his other assets are responsible for the collateral, for then, as soon as the collateral is damaged, the lender immediately has other property serving as collateral. If the borrower did not write such responsibility over his assets, then even though the lender may collect from other assets if the collateral is damaged, it is permitted, since he does not immediately have replacement collateral. The Tur also implies this. The Bach wrote in seif 12 that it is permitted even though he may collect from other assets. According to what I have written, there is no need to force the Tur’s use of the word “immediately” into the explanation offered by the Bach. Rather, the meaning is that the lender need not seek other assets, because he immediately has replacement collateral. The Levush did not understand it this way. See there, for his words are confused.

<b>אפילו כו'. </b>משמע דר"ל אפי' הוא באתרא דלא מסלקי אלא עד זמן פלוני דמנכינן כל מה שאכל קודם זמן הסילוק וכדעת הרמב"ן שהביאו בה"ת והריב"ש וכתב דמ"מ לסלוקי לגמרי א"א דהא מקני' ליה אלא אכיל פירי בשומא ומנכינן ליה דמי כולי פירי כמה דשיימי ליה ב"ד עכ"ל. ובהא א"ש דלא תיקשי מה שכתב המחבר בח"מ סי' ע"ד ס"ג חוב דמשכונא דקאכיל מיניה פירות באתרא דלא מסלקי אם רצה אינו מקבל המעות קודם הזמן אא"כ מניח לו הפירות לאכול וסתם הרב כדבריו דהתם ה"ק דמחויב להניח לו לאכול בשומא מיהו דעת הראב"ד שהביא הב"י דקודם זמן הסילוק אינו יכול למחות ולא מנכינן ליה כיון דהוא באתרא דלא מסלקי וכן מוכח דעת הרשב"א שהביא הנ"י שם גבי אטרח ואייתי זוזי ונ"ל דספיקא דדינא הוא והמע"ה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Even, etc.</b> This implies that even in a place where the borrower may not remove the lender until a specified time, we deduct everything the lender consumed before the removal date. This follows Ramban’s view, cited by Beit HaTerumot and Rivash. Rivash wrote that the borrower nevertheless cannot remove the lender entirely, since the property had been transferred to him; rather, the lender consumes the produce at an assessed value, and the value of all the produce, as appraised by a beit din, is deducted from the debt. This resolves the apparent contradiction with the Mechaber’s ruling in Choshen Mishpat, siman 74, seif 3, that in a mortgage whose produce the lender consumes in a place where the borrower may not remove him, the lender need not accept repayment before the stipulated time unless the borrower leaves him the produce to consume. The Rama also rules there without qualification. The meaning there is that the borrower must allow him to consume the produce at its assessed value. Raavad, however, as cited by Beit Yosef, maintains that before the removal date the borrower cannot protest and no deduction is made, since it is a place where the borrower may not remove the lender. Rashba’s view, cited by Nimukei Yosef regarding the statement, “I will make the effort and bring the money,” also proves this. It appears to me that this is an unresolved question of law, and the burden of proof rests upon the claimant.
