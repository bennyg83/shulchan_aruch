**** ENGLISH ****
And slaughter until the majority, etc. — It appears to me that for this reason the Beit Yosef added the word "until," because Rambam wrote (chapter 3 of Hilchot Shechitah, law 4): if he paused the time to lift it, lay it down, and the time to slaughter a minority of simanim — not the time to complete shechitah — this is doubt of neveilah; and Beit Yosef explained, and so it appears, that this means he paused the time of shechitah by a hair's breadth more than he would have paused the measure of shechitah; and therefore, since the matter is estimated by judgment, it is possible he will think he only paused the time to slaughter a minority of simanim, and perhaps he paused the time to slaughter the majority of simanim — end of his words; therefore the Beit Yosef emended the word "until," meaning he paused the time of shechitah until he reached the majority of simanim by a hair's breadth — the majority (and after it the Taz continues). And even though in such a case it is doubt of neveilah, the Beit Yosef was not precise about this, and so in several places. However, from Tosafot there (s.v. im yesh bah), and Hagahot Ashiri in the name of R' Yechiel and Mordechai and Agudah there, it appears clearly that we say "the piece becomes neveilah" in such a case; and so too is the view of R' Yerucham there; and it appears from these poskim that even in salting we say "the piece becomes neveilah" — see there: the Mechaber and Beit Yosef and other poskim were not precise about this.

**** ENGLISH ****
And if after shechitah gomi is found, etc. — In Terumat HaDeshen he compared this law to seif 4, that when he paused on something that has no aspect of shechitah — it is shehiyah; and all the more so here. And afterward he wrote: and you may ask — who tells us that he paused when cutting the gomi? Perhaps he actually cut the esophagus and the gomi at once, equal in time, and if so the knife did not cease even a moment from the siman. And I did not know why Bach was lenient to permit long thin grass lying along the esophagus without reason and proof; and also from Beit Yosef's words, who wrote "gomi or the like," it implies as I said; and so it appears in the Taz — see there.

<b>ה"ז פסולה. </b>ה"נ ספק נבלה דבעיא בש"ס היא שהה במעוט סימנים מהו תיקו וקיימא לן לחומרא והטעם דכיון שנחתך רוב הסימן הוי כאלו נחתך כולו ומה שמוליך ומביא במעוט האחרון הוי כאלו מוליך ומביא בידה או ברגלה ולכך כשנתעכב שעור שהייה במעוט אחרון של סימן ראשון טרפה בבהמה משא"כ בעוף דהכשרו ברוב א' כ"כ הפוסקים ומדלא הגיה הרב נמי כאן דלפי המנהג בכל ענין טריפה אפילו לא נתעכב שעור שהייה משמע דס"ל דהכא דוקא בעינן שעור שהייה אפילו לדידן וכן משמע בעט"ז והיינו כיון דמתעסק הכא בשחיטת הסימנים ועיין בסי' י"ח ס"ק י"א י"ב מ"ש בזה:
**** ENGLISH ****
This is invalid. — Here too — doubt of neveilah that requires examination in the Gemara: he paused in a minority of simanim — what is the law? Teiku; and we stand stringently. The reason: since the majority of the siman was cut, it is as if the whole was cut; and what he does holacha u'va in the remaining minority is as if he does holacha u'va with its hand or foot; and therefore when he was delayed the measure of shehiyah in the last minority of the first siman — treifah in an animal; not so in fowl, whose fitness is in one majority. So too the poskim. And since the Rav did not emend here either "according to the custom in every case treifah" even if he was not delayed the measure of shehiyah — it implies he holds that here specifically we require the measure of shehiyah even for us; and so it appears in the Taz; and this is because he is occupied here with shechitah of the simanim. And see siman 18, seif kaf 11, what is written on this.

**** ENGLISH ****
And it is forbidden to sell it to a gentile, etc. — One should not challenge: for Beit Yosef wrote in seif 27 of treifot that are unknown, and some validate that treifah — even though we rule to forbid it, nevertheless it is permitted to sell it to a gentile because of safek safeka; and here too, even though we rule to declare treifah, nevertheless since it is only a concern that perhaps he will sell it — this is safek safeka: perhaps the esophagus was not perforated; and if we were expert in examination we would see it was not perforated; and if you say it was perforated — perhaps the gentile will not sell it to a Jew. These are two "perhaps"s here. They decreed on a Torah doubt, or even on something whose prohibition is only rabbinic from the root, such as bread of an Israelite that a gentile baked (below siman 112, seif 11) — the decree not to sell lest he sell it to an Israelite; but regarding something in which there is a dispute of amoraim or rabbis, we always say regarding selling to a gentile that it is rabbinic — follow the lenient side; for a doubt in a dispute is not like a doubt in the act itself — and it is simple. And it requires study in many places in the Gemara on this matter — end of Maharai in Terumat HaDeshen, seif 178. And in my humble opinion, in any case there is no difficulty: for here, since the doubt is because of our lack of knowledge — we are not expert in examination — it is not called a doubt at all, as the poskim wrote in several places; and as written in siman 53, seif kaf 14, and siman 98, seif kaf 9; and so Maharai himself in Hagahot Sha'arei Dura, siman 87, regarding a broken bone — see there; and even safek safeka does not help in such a case, as written in siman 55, seif kaf 4.

<b>אבל אם לא יצא דם כו'. </b>הא אם יצא דם אף שלא חתך כל העור. או שחתך כל העור אפילו לא יצא דם טרפה ומ"ש ברישא או חתך העור ויצא דם היינו מקצת העור וכ"פ בד"מ בהדיא אבל מהרש"ל שם הכשיר בין בחתך מקצת העור ויצא דם ובין בחתך כולו ולא יצא דם ואינו מטריף אלא בחתך כולו ויצא דם ובמה שמתיר נחתך כולו ולא יצא דם כבר כתבתי בס"ק ד' דלא קי"ל הכי אבל במה שמכשיר נחתך מקצתו אפילו יצא דם נראה לכאורה כן דמאי חששא איכא כיון שלא חתך כל העור וכן בעט"ז הביא ב' דעות בזה ע"כ נראה להקל בהפסד מרובה וכה"ג אבל בלא"ה אין להקל דלא יהא אלא תלישת הנוצה דנוהגים להטריף ביצא דם וכן הב"ח מטריף בחתך מקצת העור ויצא דם מיהו כל זה בשלא אמר השוחט ברי לי שלא נגעתי בוושט אבל כשאומר ברי לי אפילו חתך כל העור כשר כשלא יצא דם וכדמשמע בדברי הפוסקים והמחבר בס"א וכמ"ש בס"ק ד' והיכא שנתכוין מתחלה שלא ליגע בושט כגון שתפס הקנה לבדו בידו אפילו חתך כל העור וגם יצא דם כשר וכמ"ש בס"ק ט"ו ודו"ק:
**** ENGLISH ****
But if no blood came out, etc. — Behold: if blood came out, even though he did not cut all the skin; or he cut all the skin even though no blood came out — treifah. And that which it says at the beginning "or he cut the skin and blood came out" — this means part of the skin; and so Darkei Moshe wrote explicitly. But Maharshal there validated both where he cut part of the skin and blood came out, and where he cut it all and no blood came out; and he only declares treifah where he cut it all and blood came out. And in what he permits where he cut it all and no blood came out — I already wrote in seif kaf 4 that we do not rule thus. But in what he validates where he cut part of it even if blood came out — it appears so at first glance: for what concern is there, since he did not cut all the skin? And so the Taz brought two views on this; therefore it appears one should be lenient in great loss and the like; but in any case one should not be lenient, lest it be only plucking of a feather that they practice to declare treifah when blood comes out; and so Bach declares treifah when part of the skin was cut and blood came out. However, all this is when the slaughterer did not say "It is clear to me I did not touch the esophagus"; but when he says "It is clear to me," even if he cut all the skin it is valid when no blood came out, as implied by the poskim and the Mechaber in seif 1; and as written in seif kaf 4; and where he intended from the outset not to touch the esophagus, such as where he grasped the trachea alone in his hand — even if he cut all the skin and blood also came out, it is valid; and as written in seif kaf 15 — examine this.
