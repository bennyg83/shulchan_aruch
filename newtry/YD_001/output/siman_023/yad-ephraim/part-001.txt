<b>And it becomes shehiyah in any amount and treifah.</b> Ba'er Heitev. And he wrote in Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 54: hard thorn stuck between esophagus and trachea from outside — declare treifah; for that which Rama mentions regarding lying in esophagus speaks of what is normally found through eating — see there, where he wrote regarding a chicken with thread coming from its mouth, pulled, felt it goes outside — how slaughter? He answered: slaughter trachea alone; even though l'chatchila two simanim, here b'dieved: if he slaughters esophagus he comes to shehiyah when cutting the thread; after shechitah see from where it came and investigate. They did so; found slaughtered head pulled through esophagus to crop and gizzard, knot of threads the chicken ate; no defect — validated. And in Divrei Shalom, siman 236: slaughterer slaughtered fowl, soft thin grass leaf found cut in two — be stringent because of shehiyah, as Shach wrote — see there. And in Ba'er Heitev, what he wrote in name of Or Zarua, siman 86 — textual error, should read tzadi vav; and what he wrote that for kashering utensils he leaned lenient — not so: he wrote those utensils may be kashered, but if mixture increased in those utensils they again used other utensils — no kashering needed — see there. And see what he wrote in name of Beit Yosef on cut of holder's finger; and so in Avnei Nezer, part 2, siman 42: one slaughtered fowl, friend held fowl at shechitah and also cut finger — validate, as we say in Chullin, daf 32: slaughtered cow and cut pomegranate with it — kosher; even though one may say specifically for them who require shehiyah measure, not for us forbidden like gomi found along neck length, not stretched, and knife sharpness pauses to cut it more than cutting siman — not so human finger holding forcefully: one may say cut esophagus and finger at once, cutting both equally — therefore I permitted — see there. And Zichron Yosef, siman 1: slaughterer slaughtered calf on special slaughter chair called shraagin; felt at shechitah knife touched shraagin wood during shechitah, but said "It is clear I did not pause at all, nor startled at touch, nor cut wood, only touch"; with extra honor checked wood — no cut; from speed of shechitah did not know if before or after finishing — he answered: even though Or Zarua, siman 66, wrote we do not judge safek shehiyah like other shechitah doubts, etc.; and also per Chinuch, Beit Yosef, siman 24, law if knife head pushed to wall or hard ground — shechitah invalid — one might say do not add; specifically pushed at shechitah, not in safek; nevertheless custom among slaughterers to declare treifah unless clarified clearly he did not slaughter after push; they do not distinguish light touch; and since he did not intend to finish shechitah, it is habitual from speed not to interrupt shechitah only to complete his act — not called safek if slaughtered after push; nevertheless he raised it is kosher since safek whether he cut or only touched without cutting and shehiyah — safek for leniency. And all the more in our case there is safek safeka, and also permission because he says "It is clear"; and even though in gomi found they declare treifah because there he errs thinking he paused cutting siman but actually paused cutting gomi; and so cutting finger from panic — sense testifies he certainly raised knife somewhat; and we can say same for knife head touching wall or hard ground — not so in our case: we can say since he knows shechitah laws and says "It is clear I did not pause" — we say certainly he did not cut wood and shechitah kosher — see there.

<b>יכנו על ראשו להמיתו.</b> עבה"ט וע' בתו"מ בשם שמ"ח ומ"ש בבה"ט בשם דבר שמואל סי' ל"ז ט"ס וצ"ל רל"ז והביאו בלה"ק ועיין בבית יעקב סי' קל"ח וכ' בשבו"י ח"ב סימן נ"ד בשוחט ששחט תרנגול ושחט כל הקנה ונתן העוף מידו בחזקת כשר וכשהביאו לביתו היה חי עדיין ורץ כדרכו וצוה לאחד מבני ביתו לחתוך הוושט לשנים כדי שתמות וחתך בסכין פגום אם יש לאסור משום שהיי' ועיקור במיעוט בתרא או לא. והש"ך בסי' כ"ג ס"ק י"ג משיג על הר"ץ בזה ונ"ל להכריע דודאי לכתחלה אסור לעשות כן ובדיעבד אע"ג דמדברי הש"ך משמע לאסור בדיעבד לא נ"ל כלל דכל שסילק השוחט ידו מן השחיטה לגמרי אחר ששחט הרוב ונקרא עליו שם הכשר שחיטה אם אח"כ העוף שוהה למות אין לפסול בדיעבד אם גמר מיתתה בסכין פגום כיון שכבר יצאה מיד השוחט בהכשר שחיטה אין להטריף תו משום שהייה ובא מעשה כזה לידי בחולה אחד ששחטו לו עוף ושחטו הוושט והקנה הי' שלם והי' חי כבראשונה וצויתי שיניחה לילך עד שתמות מעצמה אולי תהיה כופר נפש של החולה וכל זמן שחיה התרנגולת גם החולה הי' חי עד שבא אחד וקצץ ראשו מיד נעשה החולה גוסס וכל זמן שפרכס התרנגולת לא יצאה נשמתו ע"ש. וכ' במאיר נתיבים סי' י"ז בענין עוף אינדיק שהרוצה להקל שאם ברור להשוחט שכבר נשחט סימן אחד לגמרי ולא התחיל השני מותר להתיז ראשו בקרדום ואין חשש שהייה מיעוט בתרא כ"א בנשחט רוב סימנים טעות הוא בידו וכמפורש להדיא בסמ"ק סימן קצ"ו ולכן חלילה להקל בכך:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Strike on its head to kill it.</b> Ba'er Heitev. And see Tosafot Yom Tov in the name of Sha'arei Tzedek; and what Ba'er Heitev wrote in the name of Divrei Shmuel, siman 37 — textual error, should read 237; and they brought it in Likutei HaKadmonim. And see Beit Yaakov, siman 138; and Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 54: slaughterer slaughtered chicken, slaughtered entire trachea, gave fowl from his hand while presumed kosher; when brought home still alive, running as usual; ordered household member to cut esophagus in two so it dies; cut with flawed knife — forbid because of shehiyah and ikkur in last minority or not? Shach in siman 23, seif kaf 13, challenges R' Tzvi on this; and it appears to me to decide: certainly l'chatchila forbidden to do so; b'dieved, even though from Shach's words it implies forbid b'dieved, I do not think so at all: once slaughterer removed his hand completely from shechitah after slaughtering majority and it is called valid shechitah name on him — if afterward fowl pauses to die, do not invalidate b'dieved if he completes its death with flawed knife, since it already left slaughterer's hand in valid shechitah name — do not declare treifah further because of shehiyah. Such incident came regarding a sick person: they slaughtered fowl for him, slaughtered esophagus and trachea whole, still alive as before; I ordered leave until dies by itself, perhaps atonement for the sick; as long as chicken lived, sick lived; until one came and chopped its head — patient became moribund; as long as chicken convulsed, his soul did not depart — see there. And Meir Netivim, siman 17, on indik fowl wanting to be lenient: if clear to slaughterer one siman already completely slaughtered and he did not begin the second — permitted to chop head with axe; no concern of shehiyah in last minority, only where majority of simanim slaughtered — error in his hand; and explicitly Semak, siman 196 — heaven forbid to be lenient thus.

<b>And I do not know if esophagus was perforated.</b> Ba'er Heitev. And Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 65: slaughterer began slaughtering animal, slaughtered part of skin, paused, slaughtered afterward elsewhere; place first cut was washed well, checked, saw skin not entirely cut — if no blood, lenient plainly; but even if blood — lenient as Peri Chadash, seif 23: not like plucking feathers, for fowl skin is thin unlike animal skin where examination can stand; nevertheless not fitting for every slaughterer to do so — only ask teacher or join other expert slaughterers.
