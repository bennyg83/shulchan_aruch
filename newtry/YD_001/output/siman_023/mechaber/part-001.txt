**** ENGLISH ****
Shehiyah — how so? (Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilchot Shechitah, law 2) Behold, he began to slaughter and raised his hand before finishing the shechitah — and paused, whether unintentionally, whether intentionally, whether under duress, whether willingly — whether he or another came and finished the shechitah (if he paused the time it takes to lift the animal, lay it down, and slaughter). The measure of shehiyah in fowl and a small animal: the time it takes to lift a small animal, lay it down, and slaughter (until) the majority of the simanim, which is the valid shechitah measure — his shechitah is invalid (see seif kaf gimmel). If it was a small animal, the measure of shehiyah was the time to lift a small animal, lay it down, and slaughter; and if it was large, the time to lift a large animal, lay it down, and slaughter; and in fowl, the time to lift a small animal, lay it down, and slaughter. And some say that the measure of shehiyah in fowl is the time to slaughter the majority of one siman in fowl without lifting and laying down. According to their words one must be careful when he began to slaughter fowl and cut a little until the blood comes out and raised his knife from the neck, lest he finish the shechitah — for there is concern perhaps he slaughtered something of the esophagus; and even if he only raised his knife slightly there is concern, because fowl shehiyah is very brief. And even if the slaughterer says, "It is clear to me that I only cut the skin" — we do not rely on him, since blood came out. And if he comes to ask after he raised his knife — how should he act? They tell him to slaughter the trachea alone in another place, and afterward invert the esophagus and check it. And regarding practical application, one should be stringent in this reasoning except if it is a time of distress or great loss — then one may rely on the first reasoning. {Rama: And the settled custom in these lands is to declare treifah any shehiyah, even any amount, whether in fowl or in animal (Mordechai and Mahari Weil in laws of shechitah); and one should not deviate. And if after shechitah there is found in it gomi or the like lying in the esophagus or trachea and it was slaughtered together with it — treifah; for certainly he had to pause somewhat when cutting that thing after he slaughtered the siman, and it becomes shehiyah in any amount, and it is treifah (Terumat HaDeshen, siman 185).}
