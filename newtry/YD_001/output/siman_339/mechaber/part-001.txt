**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of the goses (and saying tziduk ha'din), and what are the good signs [the signs were omitted in Shulchan Aruch and are found in Tur Yoreh De'ah at the end]. In it are 5 seifim. The goses is as alive for all his matters: one does not tie his cheeks, nor anoint him, nor rinse him, nor stop his orifices, nor remove the pillow from under him, nor place him on sand nor on pottery nor on the ground; nor place on his stomach a bowl nor a scraper nor a flask of water nor a grain of salt; nor announce towns for him; nor hire flutes and wailing women; nor close his eyes until the soul departs; and whoever closes with the departure of the soul, behold he sheds blood; and they do not tear nor remove shoes nor eulogize for him; nor bring a coffin into the house with him until he dies; nor open tziduk ha'din for him until the soul departs. {Rama: Some say one does not dig a grave for him even if not in the house with him until after he dies (Rivash siman 114); and it is forbidden to dig any grave to remain open until tomorrow so the dead not be buried in it that day, and there is danger in the matter (Rabbenu Yerucham in the name of R' Yehuda he-Chasid, may his memory be blessed); and likewise forbidden to cause the dead to die quickly, such as one who is goses a long time and cannot separate—forbidden to remove the pillow and sheet from under him because they say there are feathers of certain birds that cause this; and likewise not move him from his place; and forbidden to place synagogue keys under his head so he separate; but if there is something that delays departure of the soul, such as near that house a knocking sound like a woodchopper, or there is salt on his tongue—and these delay departure of the soul—it is permitted to remove it from there, for there is no action at all but removing the hindrance (all in Hagahot Alfasi, chapter Elu Megalchin).}
