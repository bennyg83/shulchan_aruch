**** ENGLISH ****
(Siman 339, seif 5) <b>The custom is to pour out all drawn water in the neighborhood of the deceased.</b> Birkei Yosef prohibits even water belonging to a gentile, and even if one already cooked with that water and there is sixty times as much permitted food against it.
