Bach wrote: although by law for Taz there is no distinction between one seal and two seals, nevertheless here it is permitted only with two seals as Tosafot imply; and from his words there it implies where the custom is to permit in such a case with one seal, that is the custom. And see responsum of Mahari Bei Rav part 2 siman 208.
