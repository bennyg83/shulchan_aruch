**** ENGLISH ****
The reward of the mitzvah of mezuzah. In it are 2 seifim. It is a positive commandment to write the parashah of Shema and "Ve'hayah im shamo'a" and affix them on the doorpost of the entrance; and one must be very careful with it. And whoever is careful with it—his days and his children's days are lengthened; and if he is not careful with it, they are shortened. {Rama: Nevertheless, if his hand cannot afford to buy tefillin and a mezuzah, he should buy tefillin and not a mezuzah (Yerushalmi end of Megillah and Orach Chaim siman 38, seif 12), for a mitzvah that is an obligation of the body takes precedence.}

Its placement is on the outer tefach. {Rama: Some say when a person leaves the house he should place his hand on the mezuzah (Maharil there, and it is evident in Avodah Zarah daf 11) and say, "Hashem will guard my going out," etc. (in the Midrash); and likewise when a person enters the house he places his hand on the mezuzah.}
