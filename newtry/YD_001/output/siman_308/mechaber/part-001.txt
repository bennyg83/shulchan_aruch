<b>איסור עבודת הבכור ואיסור גיזתו. ובו ה' סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i>הבכור <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i>אסור <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>בגיזה <i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="1"></i>ועבודה ואפי' אם עבר וגזזו או תלשו בידו או <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="2"></i>אפילו נשר הצמר מעצמו אסור בהנאה לעולם <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i>לא שנא מת מעצמו ל"ש שחטו אח"כ <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="3"></i>ואפי' על ידי מומחה <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="2"></i>אין השחיטה מתרת הצמר שנתלש מחיים <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="4"></i>אבל צמר המחובר השחיטה מתירתו כבשרו:
**** ENGLISH ****
The prohibition of working a firstborn and the prohibition of its shearing. In it are 5 seifim. The firstborn is forbidden in shearing and work; and even if he transgressed and sheared it, or plucked by hand, or even if the wool fell off by itself—it is forbidden in benefit forever. Whether it died by itself, or whether they slaughtered it afterward—even by an expert—slaughter does not permit wool that was plucked while alive; but attached wool, slaughter permits it like its flesh.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="5"></i>אם נתלש מחיים ועודנו מסובך בשאר הצמר את שנראה כשאר צמר ניתר בשחיטה ואת שאינו נראה כשאר הצמר <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="6"></i>והוא <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="3"></i>שעיקרו הפוך כלפי ראשו אין השחיטה מתירתו:
**** ENGLISH ****
If it was plucked while alive and is still entangled in the rest of the wool—that which appears like the rest of the wool is permitted by slaughter; and that which does not appear like the rest of the wool—and its root is turned toward its head—slaughter does not permit it.

**** ENGLISH ****
When he comes to slaughter it, or to see its blemish—it is permitted to pluck the wool of the place of slaughter in order to make a place for slaughter (and the wool he plucked is forbidden in benefit) (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rambam). And specifically by hand, but not with a utensil, so that it not appear like shearing.
