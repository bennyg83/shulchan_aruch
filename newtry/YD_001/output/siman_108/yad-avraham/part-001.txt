(siman 108 seif 1 in Hagahah) If they baked or roasted in Or Zarua under one uncovered pan — forbidden even b'dieved; and the same applies if they baked in such a case bread with meat — forbidden to eat it with milk; but one after the other there is no concern; and even l'chatchila permitted to eat it with milk one after the other; and even in something that has matirin; for according to Rif and Ran chapter 3 — bread has matirin, for it is possible to eat it by itself, and even so permitted one after the other to eat it with milk; and so it is proved explicitly in the Gemara first chapter of Beitzah (daf 4) that where mikalei kli issura — permitted even in something that has matirin; and see Tosafot Pesachim (27b) and in the Gaon's explanations; and in Or Zarua it is mikalei kli issura, as proved in Shach s.k. 11 who wrote that so it appears in the Gemara Avodah Zarah regarding that which he answers regarding reicha of cumin mikalei kli issura, see there and investigate; and see siman 102:

(there) Some say wherever we say reicha is significant — this is only when there is not sixty; but when there is sixty — even everything in the oven nullifies the issur; and for need of loss one may practice thus.
