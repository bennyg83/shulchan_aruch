And regarding bread — poskim wrote in name of Rabeinu Tam that reicha does not apply at all, therefore bread does not forbid chametz on Pesach baked with matzah — end of his words [Issur VeHeter wrote that our challah, which is rabbinic, is permitted l'chatchila to bake with bread]:
