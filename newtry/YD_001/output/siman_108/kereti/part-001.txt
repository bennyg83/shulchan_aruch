**** ENGLISH ****
That holds twelve esronim — that is, as in the time of the Talmud when they would stick the bread on the wall of the oven; and if so, on all four walls they would stick; and if so, if on all four walls one could stick together twelve esronim — behold this is a large oven; and so was the tradition from the ancients that a large oven its measure is twelve esronim; and therefore they did not give a measure in height, for this reduces the reicha, since all the walls combine; and if so, if the oven is tall the walls of the oven are large and much bread is tested on them, and if the oven is low the walls are diminished and the sticking of the bread; and if so, at the measure of twelve esronim everything is included, and there is no measure at all for the width and height of ovens.
