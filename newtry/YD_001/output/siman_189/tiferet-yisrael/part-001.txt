**** ENGLISH ****
Seif 2 Shulchan Aruch 63. If established hour veset — Raavad derives from leaving mikveh husband told her wait; Gemara reason — recognized friend imagined herself — permitted husband relations before city arrival (Rashi, Tosafot). Besides jumping veset possibility, Raavad may rely only on recognizing friend. Gaon wondered why Mechaber ruled uprooted in one hour without checking — Rif, Rambam, Raavad hold husband may come even if she sleeps since vesetot derabbanan; see siman 184 reason — this veset against most women not hour-intentional, not explicit in Gemara — Mechaber relied on above who need not check initially, presuming tehorah.

Tur like Ramban since month veset common, skipping uncommon. If never accustomed two-day seeing, 17th without continuation, 18th normal day — even if establish skipping veset we follow start so 15th uprooted; concern lest see 18th next month establish equal month veset. Truth: Rambam difficult though main missing — without Tosafot/Rambam against mishnah/Gemara if properly establish skipping no further month concern like month veset three skips — unresolved.
