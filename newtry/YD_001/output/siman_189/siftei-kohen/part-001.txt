Ra'ah wrote in Beit HaElohim that this does not refer to kiddush of the month and our shofar blast, but to the molad of the moon at the hour when it appears fit to fix the month by sight. But Rashba in Milchamot Hashem wrote that certainly the shofar blast causes it, and everything follows full and deficient months and leap years per the court's adjustments — for whatever the lower court does the upper court agrees, as it is written "which you shall proclaim" — "which you proclaim" at their appointed times; and so too regarding bodily renewal. As they expounded: "Lo alecha" — a minor girl of three years and one day who was betrothed, her virginity does not return; they counted a leap year in court and the year passed — her virginity returns; "Lo alecha" applies — end quote; and this is in Yerushalmi chapter HaNoder Min HaMevushal halachah 13 in name of R. Avin. And so Rosh in chapter HaIshah wrote that the matter depends on the shofar blast; and so we say at the end of Bnot Kutim (Niddah 38a) that the shofar blast causes it; likewise in Agudah there; and so in responsum of R. Yisrael Mintz siman 9 where he elaborated; and so appears the plain sense of Taz and the poskim. See in Asarah Ma'amarot ma'amar Im Kol Chai part 2 siman 17.

**** ENGLISH ****
Nevertheless if she had a veset, etc. Meaning: even if her veset was in a greatly spaced skip on the 12th or 13th of the month — but when she had no fixed veset, a greatly spaced skip does not suffice unless the skip is equal with four sightings — so Derishah siman 12; and so appears at first glance: if you say specifically where she had an equal veset for skipping — now that her veset was on the 14th — plainly there are four sightings; and even without a veset, since she saw four times in a skip — and so appears in Tur that Shmuel holds she did not establish her veset except with four sightings, etc.; and even for Shmuel if she had a veset, etc. — see there. But the difficulty is: whence this? For in the Gemara (64a) it only says when her veset was equal to her present skip; but when her veset was not equal — one may say: since she alternated she did not establish a veset, as below seif 12; and so is proven below seif 15 that in such a case she did not establish a veset for skips, as written there in s.k. 42 — see there; and so is proven from Rashba and Taz below seif 27 s.k. 68 regarding "but if she decreased or increased," etc. — see there; and see Tosafot first chapter of Niddah 9b s.v. pachatah, etc. — investigate well. Therefore it appears it deals where her veset was equal to her skip; and so Bach on Tur's words — but he informs us of a greater matter: even if she had a veset beforehand, three sightings afterward suffice — to exclude the other view that holds like Rav who establishes with three sightings; and for Rav, if she had a veset beforehand, two sightings afterward do not suffice — as stated explicitly in the Gemara: Rav says to the accustomed woman, I am different (Rashi: since her veset is fixed for the 15th we count the sighting of the 15th with the sightings that passed over her, for she saw at her time, and two sightings afterward do not suffice; but if I say: I am different where the sighting on the 15th was the beginning of her sighting, or she had no veset before, or she had a veset from the 20th — end quote; meaning: since she had a veset for the 20th which is later than the 15th, one cannot say we discard the 15th for her subsequent veset, for her veset has not yet arrived). The woman who saw, etc. Tur 30: Ramban — veset ha-chodesh she is concerned at its beginning once; but veset ha-hefsek cannot be concerned until she sees a second sighting, for she does not know on which day she intervals; one says the woman who sees on Rosh Chodesh Nisan is concerned for Rosh Chodesh Iyar — if she saw she is concerned for Rosh Chodesh Sivan; if she saw, her veset was fixed for Rosh Chodesh; if she did not see on Rosh Chodesh Sivan the Rosh Chodesh veset was uprooted and she is concerned for the 2nd of Sivan; perhaps she will see and establish a veset for interval from 31st to 31st, for the sightings of Rosh Chodesh Nisan and Iyar are equal in interval to seeing on the 2nd of Sivan — Nisan full and Iyar deficient — and forever she is concerned for these concerns for veset ha-chodesh and interval until she establishes one of them three times as required. How: she saw on the 1st of Nisan and 20th, etc. Beit Yosef wrote: what he wrote that we are concerned she will establish from 31st to 31st is not to say she need not be concerned for another day, for she has no fixed veset and whoever has no fixed veset is concerned for the 30th day which is onah beinonis; rather it means that even if Rosh Chodesh comes on the 30th day of the sighting it does not have the law of a fixed veset but has the law of an unfixed veset to be concerned — end quote; Darkei Moshe: if she saw on the 2nd of Sivan or another day she must be concerned for the 30th day from that sighting; and so Perishah s.k. 65; and Bach seif 16: if she saw on the 2nd of Sivan when Rosh Chodesh Tammuz arrives she is concerned for the 2nd of Rosh Chodesh Tammuz because of onah beinonis which is 30, and for the 2nd of Tammuz which is for interval — end quote; and Taz also wrote like Beit Yosef and added she must be concerned for onah beinonis even if she does not see that day, and the 30th day is not uprooted once in a woman without fixed veset as a fixed veset in a woman with veset — end quote (and this is not correct at all — investigate). But investigate this: if so Ramban and the Rav who came to explain what concerns she must have — why did they not write and explain she must also be concerned for onah beinonis? Rather from their plain words "the woman is concerned for veset ha-chodesh and interval" and "she is concerned for both until she establishes one" — it appears she need only be concerned for these two concerns and no more. Further wonder: certainly when we say onah beinonis is 30 days we mean she must be concerned for the 31st day of her sighting — onah beinonis is 30 days from beginning of sighting to beginning of sighting; and Rashba in Mishmeret HaBayit 167b; Ramban: "he found her within the days of her onah — how long is onah? 30 days from beginning of sighting to beginning of next sighting, for most women see thus from thirty to thirty," etc.; Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 4 Avot HaTumah 30 Ramban on this; Rashi Chullin 15a on "all women are in presumption of purity for their husbands when he comes and finds her within the days of her onah" — within 30 days of sighting, but after 30 she needs examination since women ordinarily see at the end of onah — end quote; plainly even on the 30th day itself she is permitted since onah beinonis is 30 days; if so she need only be concerned for the 30th day from the end of the first day she saw — the 31st from beginning of sighting; and Tur above siman 184: if 30 days passed it counts as her veset arriving — ordinarily onah is 30 days and forbidden at the end of 30 until she examines — plainly need only be concerned for the 31st; and so from poskim and Taz below seif 27 who wrote three onot beinonis is 90 days — if onah were only 28 it would be 84; rather onah is 30 days from beginning to beginning. If so wonder why Ramban needed to say concerned for Rosh Chodesh Iyar — seems because of Rosh Chodesh Iyar; but without that she would be concerned for onah beinonis anyway. Likewise why need to say concerned for the 2nd of Sivan — perhaps she will establish interval; let it suffice as onah beinonis. Many difficulties for Beit Yosef, Perishah, and Bach who hold every woman must be concerned for the day of the month and onah beinonis — how say she must be concerned for onah beinonis because veset is from thirty to thirty when she must be concerned for the month-day she saw? Rather it appears: when we say onah beinonis is 30 days we mean month to month, full or deficient — we find this often; 30 days is a month; regarding bechor we rule 305 that it is not redeemed until 30 days pass — plainly in Gemara in many places; meaning a month as Yere'im siman 253 wrote. But Raavad in Ba'alei Nefesh wrote a woman must first be concerned for veset ha-chodesh and veset ha-hefsek and did not mention onah beinonis at all; and at the end of Sha'ar HaVesetot he listed all vesetot fixed and unfixed and did not list onah beinonis; Rambam ch. 8 Avot HaTumah 6: every day she saw she is concerned for the next day and did not mention onah beinonis of thirty days; Rashba in Terumat HaDeshen Sha'ar HaVesetot wrote many times one without fixed veset must be concerned for onah beinonis of 30 days and did not mention veset ha-chodesh; below siman 196 (8) Ramban and Taz on a deaf woman without fixed veset: she is like all women concerned from thirty to thirty and did not write she must be concerned for the month-day — rather onah beinonis is month to month on that month-day she must be concerned for the coming month and no more. Clear proof: poskim and Taz below seif 27 wrote three onot is 90 days — from when we say onah beinonis is 30 days as in Beit Yosef; Rambam ch. 4 Metamei Mishkav uMoshav 20: three onot are three months, brought by Beit Yosef below end s.v. what they wrote she saw three sightings from three aligned onot — plainly what we call onah beinonis 30 days means a month; therefore Ramban and Rashi explained onah beinonis is 30 days from beginning to beginning — in an ordinary full month; and likewise if the month is deficient it is 29 days; they did not need this explanation since ordinarily month is full; and since she need only be concerned for that month-day forever, no need to distinguish full and deficient — see above s.k. 33 and 43 for more proofs.

Rather there is a difference, etc. Meaning: for an adult when she established a veset we say above we require uprooting three times — that her veset time arrives and she does not see three times; but a minor girl needs no uprooting — once three onot beinonis passed she returns to her former state. We find veset ha-chodesh in alternation where she saw from two months to two months: for an adult who established veset we require three times from two months to two months pass and she does not see; but for a minor girl 90 days suffice; and likewise veset ha-hefsek if her veset was from 35th to 35th — for an adult we require three times from 35th to 35th pass and she does not see, i.e. 105 days; for a minor 90 days suffice, as he concludes in the reason: since she is not a daughter of blood it was revealed the first bloods were happenstance, therefore her uprooting is in three onot beinonis like ordinary women — hence three onot beinonis. Derishah seif (52) [9] wondered why he listed three onot beinonis — should have said three onot of her first onot, whatever they are — see there where he elaborated; all his words do not appear correct here, for the matter is clear as explained; what he understood — the first part here without returning to see deals with that — correct as explained; Bach in last pamphlet challenged him; in my view nevertheless for interval it is not applicable to say she is not concerned for the first veset until she returns to see after 90, as below end of seif; what he wrote "and even if she returned to see in those onot," etc. — implies the first part without returning to see; we find veset ha-chodesh in alternation as explained; or veset ha-hefsek — and when she saw after 90 days that sighting is not considered a sighting at all, for any interval sighting cannot exist with fewer than two sightings, as above 192 and below 191; therefore what he wrote "and even if she returned to see in those onot" — if regarding interval, necessarily where she returned to see two sightings, that one counts as one sighting for interval (and perhaps Bach intended this in what he wrote in 237 — meaning for one who did not return to see but once, etc., "rather even if she returned to see times," etc. — see there); and this is clear. In Derishah and Perishah seif 9: what he wrote she is not concerned for the first veset, etc. — refers to what he wrote afterward "and even if she returned to see" — some say she did not see at all so need not be concerned for her first veset from Rosh Chodesh to Rosh Chodesh or her interval from twentieth to twentieth — if so why specify minor girl, all women likewise — end quote; and so Taz — but not so, rather as explained; and further difficulty for their words on elderly woman below seif 28 — there is no room to interpret thus, for from Rashba and Tur after "she returned and saw" and "she also ceased" which elderly woman, etc. — in between — and Mechaber made a separate seif on returning and seeing — from which in seif 28 without returning and seeing is plainly as I explained.

<b>ג' עונות בינוניות. </b>לאפוקי אם לא פסקה ג' עונות בינוניות אלא שלש עונות קטנות שהיתה רגילה לראות בהן אם היתה אח"כ חוזרת ורואה באותן עונות קטנות פעם א' היתה חוששת להן ואפילו לא ראתה באותן עונות אלא שראתה פ"א היתה חוששת לראייתה או לעונה בינונית כשאר נשים מה שאין כן כשפסקה שלש עונות בינוניות דראינו שפסקה ממנה הטבע דשאר נשים אז אמרינן דראיות הראשונות היו מקרים. פרישה:
**** ENGLISH ****
Three onot beinonis. To exclude where she did not cease three onot beinonis but rather three small onot that she was accustomed to see in — if she afterward returned and saw in those small onot once, she was concerned for them; and even if she did not see in those onot but saw once, she was concerned for her sighting or for the onah beinonis like other women; which is not the case when she ceased three onot beinonis, for we see that the nature of other women ceased from her — then we say the first sightings were happenstance — Perishah.
