נעקר וסת ראשון וכו' האחרונים תמהו דלרב דאמר בב' דילוגי' קבעה וסת וא"כ הרי כאן וסת הדילוג ודברים אלו הם מעותקי' מדברי רמב"ן דהוא פוסק כשמואל ולול' דמסתפינ' היתי אומר דהואיל דקיי"ל דעונ' בינוני' הוי כוסת קבוע כמ"ש הטור ואחרונים בסי' קפ"ד וסי' זה בכמה דוכת' א"כ כי היכי ביש לה וסת מודה רב דאין ראי' ראשונ' מהמנין דאין ראי' ראשונ' שייך לוסת הדילוג אף גם ראי' ראשונ' בעונה בינונית הוה כמו ראתה בוסת קבוע ואין מצטרף לוסת הדילוג כלל ורב מודה כמו וסת קבוע ומיירי שלשים היינו מיום ראי' בכלל והוא עונה בנוני' כנ"ל אלא שלא מצאתי דבר זה לא' מן המחברים כלל וצ"ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
The first veset has been uprooted, etc. The Acharonim objected that according to Rav, who holds that two skips establish a veset, she has already established a veset of skipping. These words are copied from Ramban, who rules like Shmuel. Were I not hesitant, I would say that since we rule that the average interval has the status of a fixed veset, as stated by the Tur and the Acharonim in siman 184 and in several places in this siman, Rav likewise agrees that when she already has a veset, the first sighting is not counted, because that first sighting belongs to the existing veset and cannot join the veset of skipping. The same applies when the first sighting occurred at the average interval, for it is treated like a sighting at a fixed veset and does not join the veset of skipping. The reference to thirty days means thirty days counted inclusively from the day of the prior sighting, which is the average interval. However, I have not found this stated by any of the authorities, and the matter requires study.

**** ENGLISH ****
Whose time to see has not yet arrived. According to Rambam, chapter 4, halachah 1 of Hilkhot Mishkav uMoshav, it appears that an elderly woman who saw twice after ninety days conveys impurity retroactively for twenty-four hours. See Kesef Mishneh there, who supports Rambam's ruling from the Mishnah. Accordingly, there is a substantial distinction between an elderly woman and a girl whose time to see has not yet arrived. I am surprised that the Mechaber departed from his usual practice and did not mention Rambam's opinion at all. Perhaps he holds that although she conveys impurity retroactively for twenty-four hours, this is still treated like an unfixed veset, for the original veset has nevertheless been uprooted, and an elderly woman need not be concerned for an unfixed veset. This requires study.

It is merely an incidental occurrence. Taz, seif katan 49, explains that she need not be concerned for this veset at all. This must refer to a case in which she did not see three times during the onot in which she had previously been accustomed to see. If she did see three times, she must be concerned for it as an unfixed veset, as stated above in seif 33. Rather, the case is one in which she saw only once or twice, and therefore we say that it was incidental. However, the words of Taz in seif katan 48 are difficult, for they imply that he understood the requirement of concern to apply only after the nursing period had ended. That is incorrect. The concern applies during the nursing period itself, and I am entirely unable to understand his reasoning here.
