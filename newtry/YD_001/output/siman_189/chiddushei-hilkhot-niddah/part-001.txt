**** ENGLISH ****
Four sightings are required, etc. One may challenge: veset ha-chodesh and veset ha-shavua are one in law, and we learn veset ha-chodesh from veset ha-shavua as explicit in the Gemara; if so plainly veset ha-dilug applies to both as to veset ha-chodesh. See Tosafot Bava Kamma 34 regarding the goring ox, likewise explicit. If so, for the view that we rule like Rav that two skips suffice and these are veset ha-chodesh, three sightings, likewise for veset ha-shavua. Example: a woman saw first on Sunday the 1st, at end of day 20 saw again on Friday the 6th, at end of twenty saw on Wednesday the 4th, behold three sightings and two skips in two days that are equal skips, behold veset ha-shavua for skip like veset ha-chodesh for skip. I wonder why the Acharonim did not notice this. My resolution: how can one say she established veset for skipping and veset ha-shaveh? We say it is more reasonable she established the equal veset since a woman rarely establishes veset for skip; therefore we say she is not concerned for skip until she establishes it three times. If so, truly when she saw three times with a thirty-day interval of two days she established veset ha-shavua in skip, only when she adds a fourth sighting at end of twenty. If so there are two types of veset here, either interval veset which is the equal veset, or veset ha-shavua in skip; Shulchan Aruch's intent is it is not established for interval until four times and then it is interval veset and not skip veset for the week. If difficulty: what difference, in either case she must forever be concerned for day twenty? Answer: if she saw in her youth or one onah passed and she did not see, if you judge this veset ha-shavua for skip, nevertheless at its time she is concerned for it is equal in law to veset ha-chodesh and there is no difference whether she sees in youth or one onah passed; but if we judge it for interval veset, if one onah she does not see at its time she is not concerned in youth, then interval is void and no concern as below. שעתה בלבד פי' כגון ראתה תמיד בשעה י"ב ביום אבל לא כוונה הימים כי פעם ראתה בכ' פעם תראה בכ"ב א"כ הרי כאן רק וסת לשעות וא"צ לפרוש רק השעה ויליף לי' מהך דחזית תמיד כד סלק' מטבילה ואמר הבעלי ע"ג הנהר והיינו כי היתה תמיד רואה באישון לילה בכניס' לעיר והתיר להקדים להתבעל טרם הכניס' לעיר כי קודם זמן ההוא מותרת וצ"ל הא דפסק הש"ע לעיל קפ"ז ס"י דאשה שראתה תמיד בליל ב' אחר טבילת' דצריכ' לפרוש ליל ב' אחר טבילת' הג' ולא אמרינן דקודם ראי' או אח"כ מותרת דשם לא כוונה השעה בלילה רק פ"א ראתה בחצי הלילה ופעם לפניו וא"כ כל הלילה בספק ועמש"ל עבק"א:
**** ENGLISH ****
Only its hour. This means, for example, that she always saw at the twelfth hour of the day, but the days were not fixed, for once she saw on the twentieth and another time on the twenty-second. Thus she has established only a veset tied to a particular hour, and she need separate only during that hour. This is derived from the case in which she would always see when returning from immersion, and the instruction was, 'Have relations with her on the riverbank.' The meaning is that she would always see in the middle of the night upon entering the city, and he permitted them to have relations before entering the city, because before that time she was permitted. One must distinguish this from the ruling above, siman 187, seif 10, regarding a woman who always saw on the second night after immersion, where she must separate on the second night after her third immersion. There, the specific hour of the night was not fixed, for once she saw at midnight and once before it, and therefore the entire night remained in doubt. See what I wrote above in the notes.

And even without examination. Beit Yosef in Ba'al HaNefesh searched and it is at end of Sha'ar HaVeset, the matter is not understood. Uprooted for one hour? One may say if this happened three times that she immersed and did not see at the beginning of night and the like, she is permitted, for she uprooted the veset since it always comes at the intended time; and afterward three times passed and she did not see. But why examination is not required I do not know; whence this Raavad? Since we equate for her a veset and every sighting from that which permitted her to have relations before time of entry and commanded relations on the riverbank, all before time. But if time passed that examination is not needed like veset without fixed time, whence this for Raavad? Further if so even the first time is uprooted since we equate for her a veset, it is not fixed, for Raavad holds veset of hours is not like fixed veset; veset of days we learned but not veset of hours. Must say this seeing due to immersion is like seeing due to jumping, established only with combination of days not hours; if so difficulty above siman 187 he rules if she saw twice on immersion night needs three times to uproot, all the more there days and hours were not intended needs three times, and here with intended hour once should suffice, requires study.
