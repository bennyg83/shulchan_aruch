**** ENGLISH ****
The law of the spleen. In it is one seif: The spleen — even though it has a reddish appearance and looks like an abundance of blood — its law is like other meat, and salting is required: {Rama: Some say the custom is not to cook kidneys or male eggs even after they have been scraped, because there is a majority of blood in them, and b'dieved one need not be concerned (Agur and Arukh general rule 1 in the name of Rokeach). And it is permitted to salt all of these with other meat even though there is a majority of blood in them, provided they remove the membranes and fat from them.}
