<b>ת"ח. </b> משמע אם לא היה בן הבן ת"ח גם בן הבן אסור דה"ל כאילו פירש שאם לא יהיה ת"ח שגם הוא יהא מודר מנכסיו כמו הבן וכ"פ הב"ח:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>A talmid chacham.</b> It follows that if the grandson is not a talmid chacham, the grandson too is prohibited. It is as though the person explicitly stated that if the grandson would not become a talmid chacham, he too should be barred from benefiting from his property just like the son. Bach rules likewise.

<b>Gave.</b> Bach writes that this is permitted only after the fact, if he already gave or repaid and the like; he does not remain in violation of the neder. Initially, however, it is forbidden.
