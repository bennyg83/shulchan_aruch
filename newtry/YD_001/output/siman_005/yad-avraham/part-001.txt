**** ENGLISH ****
**(Shulchan Aruch seif 3) Two who slaughter, etc., and one intended disqualifying matter—invalid; and if they slaughtered one after the other and one intended disqualifying matter—Rambam.** Shach wrote: appears we require invalid one begin with matter making nevelah—as above end siman 2—see there. Case not comparable to proof there regarding gentile slaughter—for there act of ape; here depends on thought for kodshim appearing like slaughter outside—even slaughtering only minority siman 1 appears like kodshim outside and they come to permit minority siman 1. We hold like R. Yochanan shechitah exists from beginning to end—as Rambam chapter 1 Hilchos Pesulei haMukdashim; if slaughtered minority simanim outside and finished inside—liable as Rambam there—and explained Shas chapter 2 Chullin (29); although minority simanim—even minority kaneh alone since shechitah from beginning to end—so Rashi chapter Merubah (72): shechitah exists, etc., and however much one considers counts as thought—end quote. Clear from “however much”—no distinction—even minority kaneh alone law is so; and afterward Teivat Shlomo and Peri Megadim explain so; and responsum Beit Yaakov siman 41; and Maharit on Tosafot Yoma (32b). That Rambam chapter 4 Hilchos Shegagot implies not liable for slaughter outside until slaughters all or majority—for where slaughter began for idol worship it was revealed retroactively cutting dust; but where finished in kosher manner certainly shechitah from beginning exists—as Rashi and Tosafot; think. **See** Pe'er Yitzchak who challenged: why is kohen believed to say it was pigul—for person does not make himself wicked; if we say rabbinic decree on Yom Kippur or verse “it shall be pigul to him”—then one who slaughters friend's animal and says he slaughtered for idol worship should not be believed; per Noda BiYehudah part Orach Chayyim siman 23 reason pigul believed is he intends for good—not to cause kohanim to stumble so they not eat from it—as such case Tosafot Bava Metzia (3). So too regarding friend's animal he is believed. But he himself refuted in Gizei Zahav siman 256 regarding disqualifying for others—not believed to say he committed transgression and now wants to save friend from issur; and Rosh Ketubot (18) anusim are because of money, etc.; and even in documents gittin—and one may say he intends now to save from issur; but from sugya end Nedarim regarding “I became impure willingly” implies we do not say person makes himself wicked—and because they intend to escape issur—even though causing stumbling to husband—tzarich iyun. Also he wrote there “person does not make himself wicked” applies only where Torah called wicked—death, lashes, false witness testimony disqualification—or false oath; but saying he caused others to stumble—not disqualified for testimony or oath or capital punishment—no place “person does not make himself wicked”; also on violating positive command called wicked as Yevamot (20) and Nedarim—and Shita Mekubetzet; and when friend commits issur through his thought it is like doing act when he does not separate him.
