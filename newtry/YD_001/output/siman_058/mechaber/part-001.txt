**** ENGLISH ****
Laws of a fallen animal. In it are 12 seifim. A fallen animal is forbidden. How so? An animal that fell — if it was standing on its legs when it fell: if there is a wall from its paunch until the place where it fell, ten tefachim — we are concerned perhaps its organs were crushed. And if it was lying and rolled and fell — the height of the place must be ten tefachim. And that we require height of ten tefachim is specifically when it fell on its own (some say in Beit Yosef), or others threw it and it knew they intend to throw it; but if others threw it without its knowledge, or even if it knew but they threw it at once — even in less than ten tefachim we are concerned for it. {Rama: And there is no difference between animal and bird, for in a bird too the law is thus (Tur and Beit Yosef, and so is the implication of the poskim).}
