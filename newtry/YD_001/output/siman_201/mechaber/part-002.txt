A vessel that was pierced in its bottom — even the slightest amount — is not considered a vessel to disqualify the mikveh; nevertheless, one should not be lenient to make a mikveh l’chatchila and bring water in such a perforated vessel. If the hole is in the sides, it is not nullified from the law of a vessel until the width of the hole is like the tube of a leather flask, which is: 2 first fingers of the 4 fingers of the palm turn over in the hollow of the hole with space, whether it is square or round. And it must be close to its bottom, such that it cannot hold any water from it and below; but if it holds any water below it, it is not nullified from the law of a vessel. If one mixed lime and pebbles and sealed the hole with them, it is not considered a sealing to return it to the law of a vessel; and if one set it upon the ground, even upon lime or gypsum, it is not considered a sealing. Therefore, one who wants to draw water from the mikveh to clean it, and he fears that from the vessel with which the water is removed 3 logim may return to the mikveh after it was reduced from 40 se’ah and disqualify it — he should pierce the vessel in its bottom even the slightest amount, and then the water in it is not considered drawn. Therefore, l’chatchila one should be stringent (see se’if katan 87) to perforate the vessel with which they draw, even with a spring. And if they did not do so and drew with a complete vessel, and 3 logim of drawn [water] fell there and the mikveh became invalid, and they want to clean it and make it kosher: if it is possible to plug the holes of the spring’s flow easily, it is good to be stringent and do so; but if there is great trouble in the matter, or b’dieved where they did not do so, one may rely on the lenient ones who hold that drawing does not disqualify a spring (Mahari”k, shoresh 55), for that is the main [halacha]. And even in a mikveh without a spring, if the vessel is not so large that certainly 3 logim of drawn [water] fell there, but there is merely concern that perhaps they fell one after another and the like, we go leniently, for a safek of 3 logim of drawn [water] is a safek d’rabbanan and one need not be concerned b’dieved (Mahari”k, shoresh 56; and Hagahos Semak).}

One [mikveh] has 40 se’ah and one does not have: I say that [the 3 logim] fell into the one of 40. One is drawn and one is not drawn: I say they fell into the drawn one.
