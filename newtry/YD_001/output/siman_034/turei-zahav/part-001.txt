<b>ברוב רוחב חלל הקנה. </b>הטעם בזה מדמצינו אפילו בעוף דחיותו מועט דמותר אם מקפלו ומניחו על רוחב חלל הקנה כמ"ש סעיף ג' כ"ש בבהמה דחיותו גדול כ"כ הרשב"א:
**** ENGLISH ****
By the majority of the width of the cavity of the kaneh. The reason for this is that we derive even in a bird, whose life-force is small, that it is permitted if one folds it and places it on the width of the cavity of the kaneh, as stated in seif 3 — all the more so in an animal whose life-force is so great — so Rashba.

**** ENGLISH ****
And according to what will be explained, etc. Meaning in seif 3: without deficiency it is lenient with a majority, and with deficiency it is stringent as an issur; and Rama's difficulty was that opposite the majority of the circumference the issur would be smaller than it, but opposite the majority of the width of the cavity of the kaneh the issur is larger than it. It is simple that this is not so — for a large measure covers whatever area of the width of the cavity, and fills it from what is in the line encircling the circumference of the kaneh; therefore Rama answered that here we deal with the cavity of a bird's kaneh. And Rashba's words do not imply this, for he did not mention a bird at all; and therefore Rama's difficulty was on the essential stringency below, that the intact portion combines with the punctures — for then it would be difficult for us to combine and estimate everything in the majority of the circumference as in punctures without deficiency; and why go here to leniency to estimate as an issur? I saw many force themselves to answer Rama's difficulty in other ways — not worthwhile to me; and it appears to my humble opinion there is no difficulty at all, for certainly we see that in a crack there is a difference between length and width: in length a crack does not harm even if very large, and in width it harms in the majority of the circumference of the kaneh — which is the severance of the gargeret, which is the halachic majority; but if there is deficiency in the puncture and in that deficiency there is part of the width and part of the length — the length side in it does not harm — what will you say? It harms at least from the side of the deficiency in it. Behold, in that deficiency the main damage is from the part of the width in it; and you wish to stretch it and make from it like a crack and measure it in the majority of the circumference of the kaneh — I say to you: stretch it along the length of the kaneh and it will not disqualify at all; therefore the Sages gave the measure of an issur — that if the entire deficiency is like an issur, which is more than the majority of the circumference, if you stretch it and make from it a crack in its width, nevertheless we require specifically this measure, since in truth the entire puncture is not in width but only with the part of the length it becomes so after you stretch it; therefore we declare treifah at this measure even though the majority of the circumference remains and a minority of it has a puncture with deficiency the size of an issur with the length; and therefore also at the end it works well for this reason — that there the punctures are on the circumference of the kaneh; therefore if they have no deficiency we estimate by its majority in its circumference, like other severance of gargeret that cracked one crack in its width in its majority — likewise these small cracks, and even though these small cracks are very long, nevertheless along the length of the gargeret we do not combine, only their width, for length does not harm at all; we consider only its width along whatever length; and if the majority of the circumference were intact — kosher; but if there is deficiency in these small punctures, then the deficiency causes you to estimate also all the length, since along all the length there is in it a part of the width and it harms in it — its law is like one large puncture was lacking, the measure of all these punctures with the intact portion between them, and its measure is like an issur, as above exactly; and the stringency here is that even though the majority of the circumference of the kaneh was not removed at all — treifah, since there is a cavity like an issur — behold this issur has no more stringency than the measure of the majority of the circumference, for both are one; except that the measure of majority is when there is no deficiency and you consider only the width of the cracks, not the length at all; and if there is deficiency you consider also the length, since there is deficiency in it; and nevertheless we do not say it is as though all of it is the measure of the width, but the Sages established that in a cavity like an issur there is disqualification from the side of its width as the measure of the majority of the circumference; but in less than an issur — kosher even if there is a measure of majority if you stretch it. And therefore they said well: one who is not expert in the measure of an issur should be stringent to estimate by the majority of the circumference; and the stringency here is that in a puncture with deficiency we consider the entire deficiency as though it were for its width — treifah is found even if the deficiency in the cavity is less than an issur — clear to my humble opinion after I descended to the depth of the matter with heavenly aid and proposed the matter before great ones and it was pleasing in their eyes.
