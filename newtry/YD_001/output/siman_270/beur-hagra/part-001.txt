<b>ואפי' אין לו כו'. </b>רמב"ם וטור וכמש"ש יתר ע"כ ארשב"ג כו'. והג"מ תמה על הרמב"ם דסי' ברכה כו' אבל איסורא ליכא דומיא דבתו ובירושלמי סוף ביכורים ר"י בשם ר' חייא בר ווא שאלו לרשב"ג מהו שימכור אדם ס"ת לישא אשה א"ל אין מפני חייו ולא אגיבון כו' ולמה לא אגיבון כי אתא ר"ח ר' פנחס ר' יוחנן בשם רשב"ג מוכר אדם ס"ת לישא אשה וללמוד תורה כ"ש מפני חייו ותי' בד"מ דאין לו מה יאכל דקאמר הרמב"ם רק ע"י הדחק:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Even if he has nothing, etc.</b> Rambam and Tur, based on the statement there, “More than this, Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel,” etc. Hagahot Maimoniyot questions Rambam: selling it does not carry a sign of blessing, etc., but there is no prohibition, just as regarding his daughter. At the end of Bikkurim, the Yerushalmi states: R' Yose said in the name of R' Chiyya bar Va, “They asked Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel whether a person may sell a Sefer Torah to marry a woman. He answered them, ‘No, because of his livelihood,’ and they did not respond,” etc. Why did they not respond? When R' Chiyya came, R' Pinchas said in the name of R' Yochanan, in the name of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, “A person may sell a Sefer Torah to marry a woman and to study Torah. All the more so may he sell it for his livelihood.” Darkei Moshe answers that Rambam's case, where he has nothing to eat, applies only in pressing circumstances.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Nowadays, etc.</b> The principal purpose of writing is to study from it, as the verse states, “Now write,” etc., “and teach it,” etc. In their time, only the Written Torah could be written, as stated in the chapter HaNizakin, 60b, and in Temurah concerning matters of the Oral Torah, etc. <b>(Collected note) Nowadays, etc.</b> See Beit Yosef. Maggid Mishneh writes likewise, and this is the primary view. Taz also writes so. This concludes the collected note.
