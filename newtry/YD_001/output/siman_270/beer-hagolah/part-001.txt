מימרא דרבא סנהדרין דף כ"א ע"ב (ורמב"ם רפ"ו מהל' ס"ת) ויליף לה שנאמר ועתה כתבו לכם את השירה הזאת ופי' כיון דקי"ל דאין כותבים התורה פרשיות דתורה תתומה ניתנה גטין דף ס' ע"א אלא היינו לומר שיכתבו כל התורה עד שירת האזינו שהוא גמר של תורה:
**** ENGLISH ****
A statement of Rava, Sanhedrin 21b, and Rambam at the beginning of chapter 7 of the Laws of a Torah Scroll. Rava derives this from the verse, "Now write this song for yourselves." Since we hold that the Torah may not be written as separate sections, because the Torah was given as a complete unit, Gittin 60a, the verse means that one must write the entire Torah through the song of Haazinu, which is the completion of the Torah.

**** ENGLISH ****
Tur in the name of his father, Rosh. Rabbeinu Yerucham likewise wrote in the name of the Geonim that this is a greater mitzvah than writing a Torah scroll and leaving it in the synagogue for public reading. It is obvious, however, that writing a Torah scroll from which he and his sons will read remains the primary form of the mitzvah even nowadays.
