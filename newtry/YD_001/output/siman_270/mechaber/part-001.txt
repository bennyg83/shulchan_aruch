**** ENGLISH ****
The reward of the mitzvah of writing a Torah scroll. And it contains 2 seifim. It is a positive mitzvah upon every man of Israel to write for himself a Torah scroll; and even if his ancestors left him a Torah scroll, it is a mitzvah to write one of his own; and he is not permitted to sell it even if he has many Torah scrolls (and even if he has nothing to eat — only under duress) (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 6); and even to sell an old one in order to buy a new one is forbidden; but to study Torah or to marry a woman, it is permitted to sell if he has nothing else to sell: {Rama: And likewise for the need of redeeming captives it is permitted to sell it (this too there, and Tosafot beginning of Bava Batra and Agudah there). And see these laws in Orach Chaim siman 153. If he hired a scribe to write a Torah scroll for him, or bought one and it was mistaken and he corrected it — behold, it is as though he wrote it. (Beit Yosef in the name of Nissim.) But if he bought it thus and did not correct anything in it, it is like one who snatches a mitzvah from the marketplace (Tur in the name of Rivash, chapter HaKometz) and he does not fulfill through this.}
