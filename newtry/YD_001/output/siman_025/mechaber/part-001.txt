**** ENGLISH ****
That one must check simanim after shechitah. In it are 3 seifim. One who slaughters must check the simanim after shechitah whether the majority were slaughtered, or see at the time of shechitah that they are slaughtered — the majority; and if he did not see that the majority were slaughtered — forbidden. {Rama: And they practiced checking in this way: one presses the thumb on the brim of a hat, and through the pressure the simanim protrude outward, and then one can see whether the majority were slaughtered; also if he made shemuta — for if the simanim do not go inward after he removes his finger, it would be shemuta and treifah (Hagahot Shechitot). However, one need not check after shemuta, for we rely on the majority as with other treifot.} And there is one who says one must see that they are slaughtered at the place of shechitah, without hagramah. {Rama: And one should check after this before throwing the bird from his hand (Maharik, shoresh 35, in the name of Ashkenazi shechitot); but if he did not check at first, he checks afterward, and we are not concerned lest through convulsion the cut widened (Maharik's reasoning there).}

**** ENGLISH ****
It was slaughtered properly and a wolf came and took the intestines and returned them when they were perforated — kosher; and we are not concerned lest at the place of the perforation it was perforated. {Rama: But before we know it was slaughtered properly it stands in presumption of prohibition (Tur according to the Gemara); and any doubt that occurred in shechitah — treifah (Mahariu, laws of shechitah, and it is in the Talmud Chullin daf 10 and 28, and agreed by all poskim in several places).}
