**** ENGLISH ****
(siman 93) A pot in which meat was cooked — one may not cook milk in it, etc. Tur wrote in name of Rashba that if one cooked vegetables in a pot of meat it is permitted to cook cheese in it afterward, for the power of what the meat absorbed was already diminished and the taste weakened until it is not fit to impart prohibitory status in basar b'chalav; in what case — when it absorbed heter, as we said — but if it absorbed forbidden matter it is always in its forbidden state until one hag'alahs what is fit for hag'alah and libuns what is fit for libun, etc.; and his proof is from the Talmud in Avodah Zarah and Zevachim, as explained in Beit Yosef that every day becomes purging for its fellow. And Beit Yosef wrote: however from Tosafot chapter 25 it implies they do not hold this of Rashba that they wrote in the sugya regarding fish that ascended in a bowl at the end of Avodah Zarah that every day becomes purging for its fellow, and the reason is because min b'mino is batel b'rov d'oraisa and in a Temple vessel they establish it on a Torah basis. And Shach wrote in his Netivot HaKesef that Beit Yosef's words have no substance at all, for even according to Tosafot Rashba's proof is compelled — that after the vessel became forbidden bitul b'rov does not apply at all, for otherwise why would hag'alah be needed at all, see there; or also Tosafot hold hag'alah helps in such a case when everything is expelled — and I might say you must conclude that certainly according to Tosafot's words one can say hag'alah does not help in such a case, and this is because it does not expel all the absorption, and even though it expels some, nevertheless the forbidden name can impart upon what remains; and that which helps regarding chatat is because certainly the majority of the absorption is expelled and only a little remains in it, and therefore when it absorbs afterward in the pot from heter like the measure of forbidden matter that the expelled absorption that remained inside brought out — it is batel b'rov, and properly one can say they established it on a Torah basis through this hag'alah; however this is specifically regarding chatat, but for other prohibitions where we hold min b'mino requires sixty, certainly hag'alah does not help in such a case. And here it appears proven like Tosafot and not like Rashba from Pesachim daf 30 per the view that one does not plaster the oven with dough in the attic, and if he plastered all the bread is forbidden until he fires the oven — and it challenges: why did Rav say pots on Pesach should be broken, etc., and answers, etc., see there. And Rashba's view: even though we hold that if heter absorbed hag'alah helps for a vessel requiring libun, nevertheless for a chametz vessel to use on Pesach hag'alah does not help even though heter absorbed, because perhaps chametz is upon it, and therefore when Pesach arrives it is called forbidden absorption — and if so per Rashba's view that where one hag'alahs until the taste weakened it helps that forbidden status cannot impart upon it — difficult: why does the Gemara challenge Rav's words that firing does not help for a pot from the oven that we say helps; perhaps it is different there because heter absorbed therefore firing helps, but chametz on Pesach that is named upon it — firing does not help, for even though its taste weakened through hag'alah, nevertheless regarding prohibition that forever stands in its prohibitions, therefore one must say per Tosafot's view that even though heter absorbed, weakening of taste entirely does not help, nevertheless forbidden taste can impart upon it. And there are further proofs against Rashba: behold the world challenges how Tosafot say in the Temple they establish on a Torah basis that min b'mino is batel b'rov — behold in the chapter on blood of chatat they establish this that every day becomes purging for its fellow like Rabbi, and Rabbi holds min b'mino is not batel like R' Yehudah; and one might say: Rambam holds the mitzvah of pouring out and rinsing is before it becomes notar, and Tosafot hold pouring out and rinsing is after it becomes notar; and here it appears from the sugya of the spit and grate whitened in fire at the end of tractate Avodah Zarah it is not like Rambam — for we read there the spit and grate are whitened in fire, and it was taught regarding kodashim the spit and grate are hag'alah in boiling; he said to him: here forbidden matter absorbed, here heter absorbed; Rava said: in the end when it expels, forbidden matter it expels; except according to Rava every day becomes purging for its fellow — if so hag'alah too should not be required — difficult. And per Tosafot's view that pouring out was after the time of prohibition, the sugya of our mishnah accords with the straightforward challenge that challenges when it expels, etc. — meaning since hag'alah is after the time of prohibition, now too forbidden matter is absorbed — what difference to me purging of idol worship, what difference to me purging in kodashim; and on this it answers every day becomes purging for its fellow — meaning properly heter absorbed expels for him before the time of prohibition, unlike per Rambam's view who holds pouring out and rinsing is before the time of prohibition — then necessarily that which challenges in the end when it expels is because it does not expel all the absorption and it becomes notar after time, and even though it is only weak taste it can impart upon it forbidden status, and it is not comparable to noten ta'am bar noten ta'am which is weak taste from weak taste; if so one should challenge why does it answer every day becomes purging for its fellow — still difficult, for behold the absorption remained inside and became notar after time; however per what I wrote in name of Beit Yosef per Tosafot's view that absorption is batel inside the vessel — then he properly challenges him in the end forbidden matter it expels — meaning absorption inside became notar after time; and one might say it was annulled inside the vessel — behold this hag'alah is through water and it is not its species with the absorption; and on this it answers every day becomes purging for its fellow — and if so it is its species with the absorption, and properly one can say it was annulled with absorption in the majority that they establish on a Torah basis; and the Gemara challenges if so hag'alah too should not be required — for even though taste is not weakened beforehand, nevertheless one could hag'alah in such a case that it was annulled b'rov in the entire vessel; therefore it also resolves what they challenge on Tosafot who wrote in the Temple they establish on a Torah basis — behold this mishnah is Rabbi's who holds min b'mino is not batel: behold Tosafot wrote in Yevamot chapter HaAreil that in dry with dry R' Yehudah concedes it is batel, and necessarily the reason in dry with dry there is no taste of prohibition at all — for granted moist with moist where there is taste of prohibition here, when the heter received some taste from the prohibition everything became prohibition and there is no longer majority heter to annul it; unlike in dry where there is no taste in the heter receiving from the prohibition — then the heter everywhere stands by itself and properly here is majority heter to annul it; if so likewise here with weak taste — even though it is prohibition, nevertheless it cannot come through imparting full taste in heter that heter taste turns to prohibition, and properly it is batel b'rov; therefore you must conclude that here where initially they hag'alah in boiling — then taste weakens, and afterward when they cook peace-offerings in it there is properly bitul b'rov even per Rabbi because it is like dry with dry as written — and you must conclude; and all this per Tosafot's view, unlike Rashba's view who holds that what we say every day becomes purging for its fellow does not come because of bitul b'rov — we derive it only because there is weak taste and it is not fit to impart upon it forbidden status; if so the entire sugya does not follow this that the Gemara challenges in the end, etc. — for it properly helps, for it is still heter and taste weakened and forbidden status no longer applies upon it — or it is proven like Tosafot, investigate.
