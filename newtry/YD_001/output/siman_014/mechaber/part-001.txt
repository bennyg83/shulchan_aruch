<b>דין עובר במעי בהמה. ובו ו' סעיפים:</b><br> <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Peleti" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Kereti" data-order="1"></i>המקשה לילד <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="1"></i>והוציא העובר <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i>את <i data-commentator="Kereti" data-order="2"></i>ראשו אעפ"י שהחזירו הרי הוא <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>כילוד ואינו ניתר בשחיטת אמו לפיכך אם הוא בן ח' חי או מת או בן ט' מת <i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="2"></i>אסור. ואם הוא בן ט' חי <i data-commentator="Kereti" data-order="3"></i>ניתר בשחיטת <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="2"></i>עצמו:
**** ENGLISH ****
The law of a fetus in an animal's womb. In it are 6 seifim. An animal straining to give birth; and the fetus brought out its head—even though it returned it—behold it is like one born (yelud) and is not permitted through its mother's shechitah. Therefore if it is eight months—alive or dead—or nine months dead, it is forbidden. And if it is nine months alive, it is permitted through its own shechitah.

**** ENGLISH ****
It brought out its hand and returned it (meaning: before shechitah)—what came out from the limb to the outside is forbidden. But what remained of it inside and the place of the cut is permitted. If it did not return it before shechitah—even the place of the cut is forbidden; but what is inside is permitted, even if it is its minority. {Rama: And all this applies only regarding that it be permitted through its mother's shechitah like other fetus found in her; but if the animal later gave birth to it—even the limb that came out and was returned is permitted, for its own shechitah permits everything (so Beit Yosef brought R' Yosef ibn Chaviv and Kol Bo in the name of some say, and so Maharshal proved in chapter Behemah HaMekishah seif 1, and so is the implication of the poskim—and it is straightforward).}

**** ENGLISH ****

And if it cut off each and every limb at the time it brought it out—even the minority that remained inside is forbidden.

**** ENGLISH ****
