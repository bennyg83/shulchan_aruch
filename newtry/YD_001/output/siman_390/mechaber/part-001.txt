**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of shaving and combing for a mourner. And it contains 7 seifim. A mourner is forbidden to shave his hair — head hair, beard hair, and all hair on him, even private areas, all thirty days; and lip hair and sideburns that impede eating — forbidden within seven, after seven permitted.
