(ש"ע סעיף ד') <b>עד</b> שיגערו בו חביריו ומהני אפילו לגלח ביום הל' אליה זוטא (סי' תקמ"ח) ואם גערו בו תוך ל' פשיטא דלא מהני דלא גרע משאר אבל ואף אם פגע רגל תוך ל' לא אמרי' דרגל מבטל ל' ויהיה מהני הגערה עי' (סי' שפ"ט ס"ה) וע' בא"ר א"ח (סי' תקל"א סק"י וסי' תקמ"ח סק"י) ובא"ר (סי' קל"ב סק"ד) בנושא אשה תוך ל' אפילו על אביו ואמו מותר לגלח ובמשבצות זהב שם הניח הדין בצ"ע ועי' תשו' שמש צדקה (א"ח סי' כ"ה):
**** ENGLISH ****
(Shulchan Aruch, seif 4) <b>Until his friends rebuke him.</b> Rebuke is effective even to permit haircutting on the thirtieth day. Eliyah Zuta, siman 548. If his friends rebuke him within thirty days, it plainly does not help, for he is no better than an ordinary mourner. Even if a festival occurs within thirty days, we do not say that the festival cancels the thirty days so that rebuke would help. See siman 389, seif 5, and Eliyah Rabbah, Orach Chayyim simanim 531 and 548. Eliyah Rabbah, siman 132, seif katan 4, writes that one who marries within thirty days, even for father or mother, may cut his hair. Mishbetzot Zahav there leaves this ruling unresolved. See Responsa Shemesh Tzedakah, Orach Chayyim siman 25.

**** ENGLISH ****

And this is what Hashem is doing, and if not, and then we do not feel that custom, but not what is going on between the ministers and others who have learned to wait for the time of the High Court to wait another lesson, but rather do so as to do it:
