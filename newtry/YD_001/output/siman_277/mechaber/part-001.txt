**** ENGLISH ****
Not to turn the sheet face down. In it is one seif. When he wrote the sheet and comes to set it to dry, he should not turn the writing downward even if he intends that dust not rise on the writing; for in any case it is a manner of disgrace. Rather the face of the writing should be upward, and he should spread a garment over it.
