Torat HaBayit writes likewise at the end of Shaar 4: Some explain this as involving two seals, but this does not appear correct, for if so, what novelty is taught? Sealed vessels are permitted because this is like a marauding troop in peacetime, etc. The Ran and Nimukei Yosef in Bava Metzia write likewise. See there.
