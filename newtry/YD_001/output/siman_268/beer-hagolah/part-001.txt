**** HEBREW ****
ברייתא יבמות דף מ"ו ע"א וכחכמים:
**** ENGLISH ****(Siman 268 seif 3) But bedieved, etc. Dagul Merevavah wrote that specifically for an adult convert circumcision and immersion suffice without three and at night, for with him acceptance of mitzvot is primary; but a minor convert to whom acceptance of mitzvot does not apply — immersion is primary that they immerse him on the court's intent — for all, it must be by day and with three — end quote. And Maharshal should know the poskim's opinion that bedieved it suffices without court immersion — from that which is in Yerushalmi chapter 4 of Eruvin halachah 4 — R' Chuna in the name of R' Nachman bar Yaakov: a convert immersed after the east lit — since if this were evening he would acquire residence, etc.; meaning likewise he acquires residence if he immersed from yesterday; and Ketzot HaChoshen challenged — you will not find that a convert immerses on Shabbat, for we rule a convert on Yom Tov. And it is strained to say the question was whether he transgressed and immersed, for there a court is required — and how would the court err in this — end quote. And according to the poskim's opinion that it suffices without court — no question.

מימרא דר' חייא א"ר יוחנן וכו' שם ומעובדא דההוא גר דא"ל שהי כאן עד למתר וכו' שם דף כ"ו ע"ב:
**** ENGLISH ****But he is believed, etc. Language of the Rosh; and that which he says he is believed to disqualify himself — not for this matter that if he had relations with a Jewish daughter he disqualifies her, for we rule a person does not make himself wicked; and furthermore an astonishing matter — he is disqualified and his sons are valid — therefore his testimony is nothing even regarding himself; and if he had relations with a Jewish daughter he does not disqualify her; but for this matter his testimony for himself helps — he makes himself a piece of forbidden matter and is forbidden with a Jewish daughter — end quote; and see Beit Chadash; and see above seif 3.
