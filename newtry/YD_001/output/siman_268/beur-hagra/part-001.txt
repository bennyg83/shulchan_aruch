Although the Gemara says there, “The halachah follows the words of,” etc., this refers only to the status after the fact, as stated below: even if it became known, etc., he has the status of a convert, etc. Rambam writes there that he is nevertheless not among the righteous converts. This is also the meaning of the ruling below: even if it became known, etc., we remain concerned about him, etc.
