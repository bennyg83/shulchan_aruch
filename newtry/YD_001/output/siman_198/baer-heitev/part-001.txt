Such as a dog or donkey or gentile or non-Jew or pig or horse or leper and the like — so too Shach; but in Rokeach and Kol Bo it states that if a horse encounters her she should ascend and have relations, that her sons stand handsome in speech, hear and understand, learn Torah and do not forget, and diminish sleep — and moreover that their awe rests upon creatures etc. — end; and we learn in Pesachim: one who encounters a woman at the time she ascends from obligatory immersion — if he preceded and had relations, a licentious spirit attaches to him; if she preceded and had relations, a licentious spirit attaches to her — what is her remedy? Let her say thus: "He poureth contempt upon nobles, and causeth them to wander in the waste" (Psalms 107), and thus Rokeach cites it; but in Toledot Aharon he did not cite the verse "He poureth contempt upon nobles" but rather "He looseth the bond of kings" (Job 12) — it appears he did not have the Gemara text "He poureth contempt" etc.; and in Yalkut he cites this sugya from Psalms and Job — therefore we reconcile both — end Shach:
