**** ENGLISH ****
A law of offerings of idolatry that became mixed; it contains 1 seif. Elilim, and their appurtenances, and their offerings forbid with any amount, such that if any one of them became mixed — even with 1,000 — all are forbidden. And if 1 of the first mixture became mixed with 2 others, and [then] 1 of the 3 fell into 2 others, these latter ones are permitted. And likewise, if 1 of the first mixtures fell into the sea or was burned, in such a manner that it was lost from the world, all that remain are permitted to benefit from, two at a time together, but not 1 alone — provided that 1 person does not benefit from all of them. And there is one who forbids unless 2 fell into the Great Sea or were burned (and see below siman 110).
