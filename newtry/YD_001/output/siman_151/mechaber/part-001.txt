**** ENGLISH ****
Things needed for idolatry — it is forbidden for a Jew to sell them; it contains 14 se’ifim. Items that are designated for a certain type among the types of elilim in that place are forbidden to sell to the worshippers of those elilim in that place. But if he buys much together, such that it is evident that he is buying them for merchandise, it is permitted. And likewise, if he says that he needs them for other matters, and the Jew knows that it is as he says, it is permitted. And items that are not designated for it — one sells them without specification; but if the non-Jew specified that he is buying them for elilim, it is forbidden to sell to him unless he disqualifies them from being offered to elilim. {Rama: It is forbidden to sell to a non-Jew water, when we know that he wants to make from it water for immersion (Tur, netiv 17).} If items that are designated are mixed with items that are not designated — for example, clear frankincense together with black frankincense — one sells all without specification, and we are not concerned that he will select the clear alone for elilim, and likewise anything similar. {Rama: And this is specifically for a priest of idolatry, or for a non-Jew whose presumption is that he will burn the frankincense for idolatry; but for an ordinary non-Jew it is permitted. Some say that that which is forbidden to sell them things that pertain to its worship is specifically when they have no others like it, or they cannot buy it in another place; but if they can buy in another place, it is permitted to sell them anything (Mordechai, first chapter of Avodah Zarah). And some are stringent, but the practice is to be lenient like the first reasoning, and every ba’al nefesh should be stringent for himself (Ran there; and in Tosafos and Ashiri and Hagahot Mordechai, first chapter of Shabbos, according to the Rav).}
