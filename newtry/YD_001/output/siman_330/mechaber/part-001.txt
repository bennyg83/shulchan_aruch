**** ENGLISH ****
The law of dough of a gentile, partners, hefker, and dogs. It contains 9 seifim. Dough of a gentile is exempt even if kneaded for an Israelite. Dough of an Israelite is obligated even if kneaded for him by a gentile. If given to an Israelite as a gift — if before it was kneaded, when given to him it is obligated; if after it was kneaded, when given to him it is exempt. (A gentile who separated challah — it is nothing and may be eaten by a zar) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam).
