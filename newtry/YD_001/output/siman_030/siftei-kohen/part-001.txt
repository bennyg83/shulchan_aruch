**** ENGLISH ****
Holes that have deficiency, etc. — thus wrote Beit Yosef: in the gullet we say in siman 34, etc., until as will be explained in siman 34, so too in the skull, end of his words; and according to his words, holes that do not have deficiency — even if they join together — do not constitute a majority to disqualify in the skull, and I do not know from where he is lenient so much, for it seems to me that here too the law of the skull in its measures is entirely equal to the law of the gullet. And the Tur — is he a merchant to calculate and go? He only wrote that holes that have deficiency join to a sela, and likewise those that do not have deficiency join to a majority. However, when you come to say that it is his opinion as Tosafot wrote in chapter Elu Treifot, daf 42b, that deficiency of the skull is not treifah on its own account but because the membrane will eventually tear — it is possible that since there is no deficiency in them, its end will not tear; and there is no comparison to the gullet; and see above in the beginning of siman 32; and examine this.

**** ENGLISH ****
That it is one-third of a tefach — thus wrote Beit Yosef, and in siman 48 it will be explained that the width of a sela is one-third of a tefach, end of his words; and this requires further study, for in siman 48, seif 4, the Mechaber wrote that the circumference of more than a sela is a tefach; if so, according to what we hold in several places in the Gemara and poskim that whatever has a width of a tefach has a circumference of an circle of three tefachim, then the width of the sela is less than one-third of a tefach (and all the more so according to what Rambam wrote in explanation of the Mishnah, first chapter of Eruvin, that the circumference of the width of a tefach is approximately one-seventh more than three tefachim according to the Tashbetz, and so too Tosafot in the first chapter of Eruvin, end of daf 14a, that the calculation is not precise according to the geometers — if so, since the circumference is only a tefach in more than a sela, all the more so that the width of a sela is not one-third of a tefach, that is, like a sela); and it is strained to say that he was not precise in calculation, for we say in several places in the Gemara: say that we say it is not precise stringently — do we say it leniently? And furthermore, he was precise in Beit Yosef explaining that the measure of a sela in the narrow sense is like here more than a sela; and it appears that Beit Yosef was drawn after what is written in siman 48, thus: and regarding that which we say that if you stretch it, it stands at a tefach — the Rosh wrote: the cord that encircles the pot — when stretched it will be a tefach; it is found that the width of the sela itself is one-third of a tefach, end of his words; but it is simple that the Rosh refers to the sela discussed there in the Gemara, that is, more than a sela, and as the Derishah wrote there, seif 11, and as the Mechaber himself wrote there that more than a sela — its circumference is a tefach; and even though Tosafot in chapter Elu Treifot, daf 50b, also wrote that when you wrap the sela with a cord and stretch the cord, its length will be a tefach, end of his words — that is according to what is his opinion that the Gemara there initially [holds that] a sela too is treifah: if you stretch it, it stands at a tefach; but according to what it concludes afterward that it is treifah only in more than a sela, therefore it must be that specifically the circumference of more than a sela is a tefach, or that we require in the circumference a little more than a tefach; if so, according to what the Mechaber wrote in siman 48 that the circumference of more than a sela is a tefach — it is difficult, for the width of the sela is less than one-third of a tefach; and this requires further study.
