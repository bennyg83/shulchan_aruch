**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of a blow to the skull bone. In it are 2 seifim. A behemah or chayah where most of the skull bone was crushed — whether in most of its height or in most of the circumference — is treifah, even though the membrane is intact.

**** ENGLISH ****
If it was dented and lacks from it the measure of a sela — treifah. And if holes were pierced in it that have deficiency — if between all of them together they equal a sela, which is one-third of a tefach — treifah. {Rama: But less than this may be declared kosher even nowadays, for we are not concerned in a behemah for puncturing of the brain membrane (thus it appears in the Tur and in the long Arukh, general rule 54).} And in waterfowl such as a goose (and bar oveza) (Rashba and responsum of the Rosh, general rule 20, siman 9) — even if the bone was not pierced but any deficiency whatsoever — treifah. And in land fowl that a weasel bit it in its teeth in its head, or it was struck by a tree or stone — one places his finger on the side of the hole and presses his finger there, or inserts his hand into its mouth and presses there; if the brain did not squirt and did not come out through the hole, it is known that the brain membrane was not pierced and it is kosher; and if it came out — treifah. {Rama: And we are not expert in examination of treifot; therefore one should declare treifah if the skull bone was pierced in any amount in a bird, even in a land bird (in the gates of Mahari — siman 31 — and in the long Arukh, general rule 94).}
