D) Scribe who wrote several parshiyot for pairs of tefillin shin-dalet in one context and was careful writing them in order so all four parshiyot of each head as scripture — but afterward all parshiyot mixed and not recognizable in each four which taken — if first parshiy he took written last of three joined with it and similar — require genizah. Machberet ot 3, Sha'arei Teshuvah ot 2, Zachor L'Chaim; and cannot permit based on safek sfeika, doubt perhaps all four parshiyot happened in his hand in order and if you say not all four parshiyot as scripture — perhaps halachah as those who say we do not require shin-dalet as scripture; since mixed parshiyot impossible four compartments together written in order and prohibition more established than permission — and such we do not do safek sfeika as Shach siman 110 laws of safek sfeika ot 33:
