**** ENGLISH ****
And until including. The question is stringent. And further, since he rejected "third parshah" alone, it implies even in the third parshah specifically — but the mouth of the parshah including I do not know. Turei Zahav: (Likut) And until including. From what is written — not "third parshah" specifically but parshah. And Rambam wrote the mouth of the third parshah is kosher; his reason: since we say, etc., if you say until and until including, etc., and if so the question should also be if you say until without until including, etc.; and so ruled Yeshu'ot Shelomo. But L'itas there they only asked whether it is definitely treifah or not as explained — if you say until and until including, etc., and if so also if you say until without until including should be asked. And Tosafot there on "if you say until and until" — likewise what he compared in Yeshu'ot Shelomo to what Tosafot say on "if you say until without" — but if until and until including he cannot say, etc. — the same here. And L'itas for the third there is no question until including (enough):
