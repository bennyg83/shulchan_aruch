**** ENGLISH ****
The dead, a bride, and circumcision — which takes precedence. In it is one seif. If a dead person and a bride encountered each other, they move the dead from before the bride; and likewise if the city lacks enough for both — they give precedence and bring the bride to the chuppah and afterward bury the dead; but after she entered the chuppah and he has before him to console a mourner and gladden a groom — the mourner's consolation comes first; and likewise the mourner's meal before the groom's feast — in what case? When he has enough for both; but if he lacks enough for both, the groom's feast comes first. {Rama: (When groom and mourner are in one house, the groom goes out with his attendants first and afterward the mourner and comforters; and likewise their invited guests eat with the groom when they wish and not with the mourner) (Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 4).} The dead and circumcision — circumcision comes first; and if it is a met mitzvah, met mitzvah comes first.
