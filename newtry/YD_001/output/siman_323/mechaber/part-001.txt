**** ENGLISH ****
Rabbinic challah—how it is given and eaten, and the law of its mixture with non-sacred dough. In it is one seif. Challah of the Diaspora that is permitted to a pure kohen is called "permitted to eat with a zar on one table," because it does not cause forbidden mixture (meaning: the matter of demua is terumah that fell into non-sacred food), and even if it became mixed equally. {Rama: And some dispute and hold that its mixture forbids up to one hundred and one if it is of its kind, and if not of its kind up to sixty (Tur in the name of R' Yitzchak, Sefer HaTerumah, Rosh, Rokeach siman 259, Mahariu 148, and R' Shimshon). And if it returned and became mixed in dough and there is not one hundred and one to nullify it—if he has not eaten the dough, he asks a sage about it and he permits it for him like a vow, for we ask about consecrations and he returns and takes another challah (responsum of Maimonides end of Laws of Seeds and Beit Yosef in the name of Avraham Chavivon).} And it is permitted to nullify it in the majority and eat it in the days of his impurity; and they may give it to a kohen who is an am ha'aretz, and there is no concern of a kohen assisting with his gifts—which is forbidden. And if he wished to eat first and afterward separate challah in the Diaspora, it is permitted, because its essence is only by their words. {Rama: And he must leave a little more than the measure of challah he separates so there be remainder when he separates (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rosh and Semag and Ran, Beitzah). And specifically for dough that became obligated with this challah he may release not from what is adjacent; but dough kneaded by itself—they do not separate challah from another dough not adjacent (Beit Yosef in the name of Semak and Kol Bo).} But challah of Eretz Yisrael—he may not eat until he separates; and it is taken only from what is adjacent (meaning: from what is close and near), like terumah; and it is not taken from pure upon impure l'chatchila. And everything regarding terumah—if he tithed, it is terumah; so too with challah. And everything regarding terumah—he may not tithe from this upon that; so too with challah. And whoever may not eat terumah may not eat challah; and whoever may eat terumah may eat challah.
