**** ENGLISH ****
Every letter is required. See Noda Biyehudah second series Choshen Mishpat siman 169 — he ruled regarding a sefer Torah where a hole was found next to the roof of the yud of the Divine Name, so that through this hole the yud is not surrounded by parchment at that spot; he wrote if this hole was made after it was already written properly — no correction needed; if doubtful when it was made — also valid because of double doubt perhaps made afterward; and if you say it was made at the beginning of writing — perhaps the halacha is that not surrounded by parchment only harms when touching another letter, but to fix by scraping part of the yud is not correct, for forbidden to erase even part of a letter when not for repair; and even if certainly from the beginning of writing — hard to permit, for perhaps halacha is that not surrounded does not harm, and then scraping is not needed repair — see there. And see Noda Biyehudah first series siman 75 — if all parchment between one letter and another was removed and no parchment remains between letters, or in that letter between right and left — it is like attached — see there.

<b>ליטול דיו. </b>עיין בספר בני יונה שכתב דרשאי לאבד אותו הדיו שנוטל מן האות אבל לכתוב בו דברי חול אסור והיכא דע"י שנוטל הדיו ממעט תואר שחרות האות פשיטא דאסור ליקח משום דזהו אכחושי מצוה אבל לכתוב בו אות בס"ת אפשר להתיר. ומן השמות שאינן נמחקים הכל אסור אם לא שהדיו רבה עליהן שיש לחוש לטשטוש ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
To take ink. See Sefer Bnei Yonah who wrote — permitted to discard that ink taken from the letter, but forbidden to write mundane matters with it; and where by taking ink one diminishes the blackness of the letter — plainly forbidden to take because of nullification of mitzvah; but to write a letter in a sefer Torah — possibly permitted. And from names that are not erased — all forbidden unless much ink remains on them that one must fear smudging — see there.

In correcting letters. If possible scrape chet with some letters or words before it until the Name, remove patch, rewrite chet per Rashi wide form — for in my opinion Rashi does not need measure of two zayinin and no contradiction. Also bet without heel — do not invalidate since not rounded behind like kaf but like dalet and yud without left protrusion above — do not invalidate, for some say no need for protrusion actually bulging outward and extending down, only corner above and below unlike round — see there (see Seder Gittin end of Beit Shmuel letter peh; Noda Biyehudah second series Even HaEzer siman 113 brought in Pischei Teshuvah Even HaEzer siman 125 note 16 s.v. ktzo). To fix the sefer — requires study because of yudin in holy Name, etc.; nevertheless if yud appears literally as lamed to all — may scrape so only dot body remains then add upper and lower thorns — see there. See Chinuch Beit Yehudah siman 74 asked regarding left yud thorns extended more than right — how fix and what regarding yudin of Names; he raised this depends on dispute of Hagahot Maimoniyot and R' Yitzchak of Alexandria brought in Beit Yosef Orach Chaim siman 36 — per Hagahot need only scrape all left thorn and rewrite left thorn properly; per R' Yitzchak must scrape also right leg and rewrite right leg and left thorn; likewise permitted to scrape and fix yudin of Names per each authority — see there (see below siman 276 note 21 in name of responsum Hashiv R' Eliezer).
