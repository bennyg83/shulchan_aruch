**** ENGLISH ****
Rosh in Hilchot Sefer Torah wrote there in the name of Rabbenu Baruch who was in doubt whether thought suffices — for we find pigul where it is written "it shall not be reckoned" and we require speech, and terumah is removed without speech only by thought, etc.; and he concluded there: nevertheless at the time of the Divine mentions one must think for sanctity of the Name, as we say in Gittin 54b — any sefer Torah in which all the mentions in it are not written for their sake is worth nothing; and it appears to me that after the fact thought alone suffices, for Rambam and Semag wrote that pigul is by thought without speech, and Raavad did not challenge this, and likewise Semag that one must write it for its sake from what we say in Gittin 45b — Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel: tanning requires writing for its sake, etc.
