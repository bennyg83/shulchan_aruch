**** ENGLISH ****
Each letter must be surrounded by hide on all four sides, so it not stick to its fellow; rather there should be between them like a hair's breadth. And they should not be separated greatly from one another so one word not appear as two. And there should be between word and word the full width of a small letter. And one should not bring them too close so two words not appear as one. And if he changed—distanced the letters until the word appears as two, or brought two words close until they appear as one—it is invalid.
