**** ENGLISH ****
<b>That they bud.</b> Fruit budding between Tishrei and the fifteenth of Shevat grew from moisture absorbed before Rosh HaShanah and is treated as though it budded before Tishrei. Therefore no distinction exists between planting forty-four days before Rosh HaShanah or fewer. Shach cites Rosh that nowadays no tree buds before the fifteenth of Shevat, so orlah is observed only three years. The Mechaber omits this because he follows Rambam, whose calculation never produces more than three years.
