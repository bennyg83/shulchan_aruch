["Since he was obligated, etc."] I did not grasp Rama's view here — for responsum to Ramban siman 259: one who swore to pay by a certain time or to betroth his daughter on a certain day and did not do so — one can judge and say he is obligated to do so after the time, and every day he transgresses the oath; for these are two matters — one that he swore to do a certain thing and did not tie it to time, and second that he accepted to do it by a certain time, and one is not dependent on the other; but if he swore to do a thing on a certain day — if the day passed and he did not do it, there is no longer oath liability upon him, and he is lashed for transgressing the oath and exempt from doing it; and he brings proof from one who vowed to fast and forgot and ate — if he said an unspecified day he lost his fast and must fast another day; if he said this day he did not lose his fast but completes that day — until here.
