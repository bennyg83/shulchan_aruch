**** ENGLISH ****
Several matters forbidden because of blood. In it are 6 seifim. Regarding blood of domestic animal and wild animal too, there is no kares prohibition except on lifeblood from which the soul departs; but blood of the limbs is only a negative commandment. And this applies when it separated from the limb and came outside, or coagulated in a piece, or separated from its place and was aroused to depart and was absorbed in another place; but if it did not separate and did not coagulate — permitted.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who breaks the neck-bone of a domestic animal — it is forbidden to eat from its flesh raw unless he salts it well, and through salting it is permitted even for a pot; for roasting it is permitted even without salting. {Rama: And the custom is to be stringent to cut it and salt it even for roasting; and the same applies if one cuts meat from the slaughter-house before the soul departs (Ran). Some say one should be careful l'chatchila to break the animal's neck-bone or to thrust a knife into its heart in order to hasten its death because it causes blood to be absorbed in the limbs (Binyamin Zeev and Beit Yosef in the name of Shibolei HaLeket).}

<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="10"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="8"></i>בשר שלא נמלח ונתנוהו בחומץ כדי <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="3"></i>להצמית דמו בתוכו אם נתאדם הבשר זהו סימן שנעקר הדם ממקומו והחומץ אסור וגם הבשר אסור <i data-commentator="Kereti" data-order="12"></i>לאכלו חי ואין לו היתר אלא בצלי ואם לקדרה צריך חתיכה ומליחה ואם לא נתאדם הבשר <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-order="9"></i>החומץ מותר <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="4"></i><i data-commentator="Siftei Kohen" data-order="12"></i>וגם הבשר מותר לאכלו <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="8"></i>חי:
**** ENGLISH ****
Meat that was not salted and they placed it in vinegar in order to constrict its blood within it — if the meat reddened, this is a sign that the blood was uprooted from its place and the vinegar is forbidden and also the meat is forbidden to eat raw and it has no remedy except by roasting; and if for a pot it requires cutting and salting. And if the meat did not redden — the vinegar is permitted and also the meat is permitted to eat raw.
