**** ENGLISH ****
The law of signs of fish and salted fish. In it are 10 seifim: The signs of fish are explicit in the Torah — any that has a fin and scales is pure. A fin is that with which it swims, and scales are the shells fixed on it. {Rama: And specifically when they can be peeled by hand or with a utensil; but if they cannot be peeled from the skin of the fish, it is not called scales (Maggid Mishneh chapter 1 of Forbidden Foods).} And even if it has only one fin and one scale it is permitted; and even if it does not have them now but will grow them after a time, or it had them while still in the water and shed them immediately upon coming up to dry land — it is permitted (Rambam end of chapter 1 of Forbidden Foods; Rif; Ran; and so too). {Rama: And some say one may not permit on a single scale except when it stands under its cheek, or its tail, or its fin (Tur and Rosh according to Beit Yosef and Ran and so too in the name of Ramban); and it is good to be stringent.}
