**** ENGLISH ****
Give up. He said, “Why can’t he give me a gift to his wife, he will not be denied, nor will he enjoy the gift of his wife, because he will receive a gift from his own wife, and he will not be able to receive any of his or her own sins, and he will not be able to make him a gift to his wife.”

<b>באומר. </b> כיון דאינו מפרש בהדיא לצורך מה נתנן לה הוי מתנה לכל דבר ותו לא מהני ע"מ שאין לבעליך וכו' ובא"ע סי' פ"ה סי"א סתם המחבר כסברא הראשונה ולא הביא דעת הי"א וכתב הב"ח דנראה בפלוגתא דרבוותא לענין נדרים הולכין לחומרא וגבי ממונא קולא לנתבע וחומרא לתובע ומי שמוחזק המע"ה עכ"ל הש"ך:
**** ENGLISH ****By saying. Since he does not specify explicitly for what purpose he gave it to her, it is a gift for any purpose; and further it does not help "on condition that your husband has no authority over them," etc. And in Even HaEzer siman 85 seif 1 the author wrote anonymously according to the first opinion and did not bring the opinion of the second. And Bach wrote that it appears from the dispute of the rabbis regarding vows we follow stringency, and regarding monetary matters leniency for the defendant and stringency for the plaintiff, and whoever is in possession has the upper hand — so said Shach.
