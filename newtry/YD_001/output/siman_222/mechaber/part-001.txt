**** ENGLISH ****
One who stipulated that one is forbidden benefit from him and wants to give a gift to his daughter; it contains one seif. One forbidden benefit from his son-in-law, and he wants to give money to his daughter so that she benefit from them and spend them on her needs — behold, he gives her and says to her: "Behold, these coins are given to you as a gift, on condition that your husband have no authority over them, but rather they are for what you put into your mouth or what you wear, and the like." And even if he said "on condition that your husband have no authority over them, but rather whatever you wish — do with them," the husband does not acquire them, and whatever she wishes she may do with them. But if he gave her a gift and said to her "on condition that your husband have no authority over them," and did not specify that this gift is for such-and-such or "whatever you wish, do with them" — behold, the husband acquires them to eat their fruits, and this matter is forbidden, for he is forbidden benefit from his father-in-law. And there are those who say that even if he said to her "on condition that your husband have no authority over them, and whatever you wish, do with them," the husband acquires them and it is forbidden.
