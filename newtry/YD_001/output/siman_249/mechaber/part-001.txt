How much one must give and how he should give it. And it contains 16 seifim. The measure of giving — if his hand reaches abundance, he gives according to the need of the poor; and if his hand does not reach so much, he gives up to a fifth of his assets — a mitzvah from the best; and one tenth is the intermediate measure; less than this — an evil eye.
