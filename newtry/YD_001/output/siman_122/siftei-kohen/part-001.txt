**** ENGLISH ****But if the prohibition was a sharp thing, etc. And in Darkei Moshe and in Torat Chayim general rule 61 law 12 he wrote in the name of Issur VeHeter: if he cooked a sharp thing in a meat pot that is not ben yomo and afterward cooked milk in it within twenty-four hours, the food is permitted; and we do not say the sharpness in the pot causes it to emit now for improvement — end of his words; and the same applies if he cooked a sharp thing in a forbidden pot that is not ben yomo and afterward it stood within twenty-four hours from when the sharp thing was cooked in it — permitted; and likewise if he cooked a sharp thing in a ben yomo meat pot, if he cooked in it afterward within twenty-four hours from when the meat was cooked in it, even though it is not me'et le'et from when the sharp thing was cooked in it and when the sharp thing was cooked in it it was ben yomo — permitted; but if forbidden matter was first cooked in it, even if afterward a non-sharp thing was cooked in it, if it is ben yomo — we count afterward me'et le'et from the time of the second dish, as above siman 94 s.k. 22.

<b>סתם כו'. </b>הטעם כתבו הפוסקים דהוי ספק ספיקא ספק נשתמשו בו היום או אתמול ואת"ל נשתמשו בו היום שמא נשתמשו בו בדבר שהוא פוגם בעין או שהוא אין נ"ט ומה"ט אסור לבשל לכתחלה בכלים של עובדי כוכבים דליכא אלא חדא ספיקא דקדרה שאינה ב"י אסור לכתחלה וכתב מהרש"ל בא"ו שלו סימן נ"ט דהא דסתם כלים של עובד כוכבים אינם ב"י היינו דא"צ לשאול אם הוא ב"י או לא ואם אחר ששאל נודע לו שהוא ב"י ישאל מה שבשלו בו היום ואם הוא דבר שאינו פוגם בודאי או שאינו יכול לידע אין ראוי להתיר מאחר שאין כאן ספק ספיקא ואע"פ שאין אנו חוששים לדבריו של עובד כוכבים לא לאסור ולא להתיר (וע"ל סי' קי"ח סוף ס"ק ל"ז) היינו לאותו דבר שהיה כבר בחזקת כשרות אבל הכא שכבר הורע חזקה שסתם כלי עובד כוכבים בלועי' באיסור רק מכח ספק ספיקא באת להתיר הלכך סמכי' אדבורו ועוד הא דלא סמכינן אדבריו של עובדי כוכבים היינו היכא שבא להשביח מקחו שאמר של ערלה הם כדאיתא בש"ס עכ"ל אבל מתשו' ר"ל ן' חביב ס"ס קכ"א נראה מבואר דאפי' אומר שהוא ב"י לא סמכי' אדיבורו וע"ל סי' קכ"ז ס"ק (י"ט) [כ]:
**** ENGLISH ****Stam, etc. The reason — the poskim wrote that it constitutes a double doubt: doubt whether they used it today or yesterday; and if you say they used it today, perhaps they used it in something that spoils visibly or that is not nosen ta'am; and therefore it is forbidden l'chatchila to cook in gentile vessels, for there is only one doubt; a pot that is not ben yomo is forbidden l'chatchila. Maharshal wrote in his Issur VeHeter siman 59: that stam vessels of gentiles are not ben yomo — that is, one need not ask whether it is ben yomo or not; and if after he asked it became known to him that it is ben yomo, he should ask what they cooked in it today; and if it is something that certainly does not spoil, or that he cannot know — it is not fit to permit, since there is no double doubt here; and even though we are not concerned for a gentile's words — neither to forbid nor to permit (and see above siman 118 end of s.k. 37) — that is regarding something that was already in presumption of kashrut; but here the presumption that stam gentile vessels are absorbed with prohibition has worsened, only through double doubt it comes to permit; therefore we rely on his word. And furthermore that which we do not rely on gentiles' words — this is where he comes to improve his merchandise, saying they are orlah, as stated in the Gemara — end of his words; but from the responsum of Rabbi Levi ibn Chaviv, double doubt 121, it appears clear that even if he says it is ben yomo we do not rely on his word; and see above siman 127 s.k. 20.

<b>ואין חוששין בדיעבד. </b>אין ר"ל שהמאכל שנשתמש בו מותר בדיעבד דזהו פשיטא ועוד דהא אכתי לא הזכיר כלום מהמאכל אלא ר"ל דכיון שכבר נתנו ביד עובד כוכבים ולקחו ממנו אין חוששין בדיעבד ומותר להשתמש בו אפי' כוכבים מחזקינן לה בהכי כדברי ר"ת והוה ליה ספק איסור דרבנן ולקולא עכ"ל ומעתה אין כאן מחלוקת בין הפוסקים כלל וזה ברור וכן במרדכי פא"ט הביא דברי ר"ת ודברי הר"ש מבונבורק בסתם זה אחר זה וכן באו"ה כלל ל"ג פסק כדברי שניהם בסתם וזהו דעת הרב בסימני ת"ח שם וכאן בהג"ה דאם הניחו ביד עובד כוכבים מעל"ע מותר להשתמש בו לכתחלה וכן עיקר. וכל זה בכלים שדרכן להשתמש בהן בחמין אבל כלים שדרכן להשתמש בהם צונן ודאי דסגי בהדחה וק"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****And we are not concerned b'dieved. It does not mean that the food used in it is permitted b'dieved — that is obvious, and furthermore he did not yet mention anything about the food; rather it means that since they already gave it to a gentile and took it from him, we are not concerned b'dieved and it is permitted to use even l'chatchila, as Ra'ah wrote and Torat Chayim brings in general rule 59 law 4, and so in the markers of Torat Chayim there — to exclude that l'chatchila it is forbidden to give it to a gentile with intent that he leave it afterward me'et le'et; and even though Ra'ah said only when the Jew left it overnight it is permitted to use l'chatchila, it implies to the Rav that all the more so whenever we can say it stood in a gentile's hand it is permitted, for it is not comparable to a gentile's vessel; and so it appears in the markers of Chokhmat Adam there; and even though in Torat Chayim there it appears that for Ra'ah likewise if he used it the same day without hagalah the food is permitted — that is according to the Rav's view; but Issur VeHeter, who holds the food is forbidden and like the plain meaning of Mordechai's words, one may say both apply: even though the food is forbidden because he used it the same day, but whatever stood overnight is permitted even l'chatchila; and for this the Rav wrote here in the hagahah at the beginning anonymously that we are not concerned — namely when it stood overnight it is permitted to use l'chatchila; and afterward he wrote the view of those who forbid the food; and even though in Torat Chayim there it appears that for Issur VeHeter, who forbids the food, he also forbids using the vessel even if it stood overnight — nevertheless for actual law it seems to the Rav to distinguish as he wrote. And all this according to the Rav; but Beit Chadash in this seif and in siman 137 disagrees and wrote: where it stood in a gentile's hand half a day and he returned it the same day — hagalah is required, and if earthenware — breaking; and standing overnight does not help; and if he used it the same day the food is forbidden; and this is Issur VeHeter's view who explained Mordechai's words in the name of Rabbenu Tam thus; and where it stood in a gentile's hand for an hour, standing helps to use l'chatchila; and with this Orach Chaim deals in the name of Ra'ah; and if he used it without standing the food is permitted — namely what Mordechai wrote in the name of Rabbenu Tam: but for an hour one need not be concerned — end of his words. However what Beit Chadash challenged below siman 137 from what the Tur and Rashi and poskim wrote, that one who leaves a wine vessel in a gentile's hand even one hour requires kashering, etc. — he forced greatly and his conclusion is that Rabbenu Tam in Mordechai does not hold like the Tur and his supporters, and for law there too it is permitted to leave them overnight and use l'chatchila; and where it stood in a gentile's hand a day or days it is forbidden to use l'chatchila in every regard like gentile vessels themselves — not like Rabbi Shalom of Bunburk in Mordechai, who is alone against the Tur and Rashi and poskim who require kashering for wine vessels; and Rabbenu Tam and Ra'ah in Yoreh De'ah agree that for vessels whose use is hot, hagalah is required — not as Beit Yosef wrote in this seif that Rabbenu Tam and Rabbi Shalom of Bunburk agree in this matter — end of his words. And I did not reach the depth of his view, for there with wine vessels standing overnight does not apply, as we establish below siman 137 that wine vessels cannot be distinguished between ben yomo and not ben yomo (and so Ran at the end of Avodah Zarah), and as the Rosh proved in responsum general rule 19 section 16: as long as twelve months have not passed on them there is taste for improvement; therefore kashering is required in wine vessels for Rashi and Tur and their supporters, for Ra'ah's reason does not apply there, that even if they used them it is spoiled after overnight; but in a pot everyone agrees with Rabbi Shalom of Bunburk that it is permitted to use l'chatchila immediately, from what Rabbi Shalom of Bunburk wrote there: establish the pot on its presumption (and therefore some say for gentile vessels forbidden to use l'chatchila — for a Jew's vessel we say establish it on its presumption); and say perhaps prohibition was not used in it; and furthermore even if he used in it, it is spoiled that is not ben yomo; and stam gentile vessels we establish thus, as Rabbenu Tam's words — end of his words. And now there is no dispute among the poskim at all, and this is clear; and so in Mordechai Pesachim he brought Rabbenu Tam's words and Rabbi Shalom of Bunburk's words one after another anonymously; and so Issur VeHeter general rule 33 ruled like both their words anonymously; and this is the Rav's view in the markers of Torat Chayim there; and here in the hagahah: if he left it in a gentile's hand overnight it is permitted to use l'chatchila — and so is essential. And all this in vessels whose way is to use them hot; but vessels whose way is to use them cold — certainly scouring suffices — investigate.
