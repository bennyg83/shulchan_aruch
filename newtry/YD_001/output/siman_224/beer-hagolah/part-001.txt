**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The Ran there, following Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov, whose view is accepted as halachah.</b> He expresses astonishment at the Rambam, who in the aforementioned chapter rules like Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov concerning partners who prohibited benefit from one another, but in the preceding law rules like the Sages. Ramban had already challenged him on this point. Rabbeinu Yerucham likewise cites a dissenting view. See the following siman and the sources cited there, and see the Taz's reconciliation of the Rambam.
