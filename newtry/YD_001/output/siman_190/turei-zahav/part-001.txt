Except stains of checked cloth, etc. This is Raavad's view — it implies specifically regarding checked cloth about which the Gemara says impure due to niddah — there we say it is like her seeing for all matters; but regarding unchecked cloth, although there is prohibition as seif 36 and Raavad's view that Tiferet HaYisrael brought — nevertheless there is no niddah matter, only safek impurity like a stain; therefore it is not a real seeing also regarding veset; and from this too proof for seif 38 that unchecked is not deoraita impurity even if she wiped herself.
