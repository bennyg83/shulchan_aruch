Seif 1 in Shulchan Aruch 61 — the sages decreed, etc. See Taz s.k. 1 who explained Kesef Mishneh's question on ketem sefeik sefeika — safek from elsewhere, safek from body perhaps from sides; Taz: since majority blood from source, not safek at all, as Tosafot Ketubot 9. Minchat Yitzchak: majority blood from source though most come with sensation — see there. One could reject: only rabbinic majority; then Kesef Mishneh's question strengthens with sefeik sefeika as Tosafot Ketubot 9.<br><br>Proof seems from Shach s.k. 87 like Minchat Yitzchak from Mordechai: blood from tashmish not at veset time — attribute to side blood due to presumption regular arrival; we do not rely on Mordechai to permit; weak presumption vs majority from source — majority and presumption: majority preferred; question on Mordechai who also holds vesetot derabbanan.<br><br>Further proof for Mordechai and Shach who fully permit from mishnah Niddah 14: afterward exempt from korban but impure out of doubt — impure applies to woman; she certainly saw blood; safek perhaps side blood after tashmish — Mordechai's law required; Mordechai may limit mishnah to near veset.<br><br>After analysis — no proof from mishnah; impure out of doubt refers to hour of tashmish; Rashi: both seven days safek impurity for attribution not burning; Rashi implies Mordechai — can attribute side blood; near fixed veset Mordechai permits; Gemara establishes mishnah as sensation of urine — no deoraita impurity.<br><br>Mordechai may disagree with Shmuel requiring sensation; Ra'if and Rif omitted Shmuel — if always impure anyway, sefeik sefeika remains.<br><br>Further proof for Taz against Minchat Yitzchak from Niddah 14: checked cloth pressed on thigh — impure niddah; sefeik sefeika from elsewhere on thigh, from her perhaps sides; where presumption of purity supports single safek as Ketubot 9 — Taz required; Mindei Yitzchak may concede regarding checking with safek sensation.<br><br>Summary: majority from source with side-minority and presumption of regular arrival — Mordechai permits; Shach who permits even seven clean days — mishnah plain; Mordechai limits to husband not full purity; Maharsha Chulin 11: minority and presumption against majority — derabbanan only; Mordechai tashmish derabbanan follows evident karov.<br><br>However — question on mishnah blood in prozdor impure: minority on karov with purity presumption should follow karov; Mordechai and Shach only permit to husband.<br><br>Taz proof from checked cloth — sefeik sefeika; chazakah supports; Taz words required, not Minchat Yitzchak.
