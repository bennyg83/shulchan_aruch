However it is difficult — the stain was found the same day after finding the stain on the borrowed garment; otherwise why need she be a stain-bearer, and because it is not from her body — for even if she saw immediately it would not help as below seif 51; how then can we say it deals with the moment she found the stain on the borrowed garment she knew her garment was not yet stained, and immediately found her garment stained too, to say she does not attribute because she does not know clearly the stain came from her body — it cannot be from elsewhere since it is known at that moment she had not passed — requires investigation.
