**** ENGLISH ****
The law of close and distant tidings. And it contains 12 seifim. One to whom tidings came that his relative died — if within thirty days tidings arrived, even day thirty itself — this is close tidings; obligated to observe seven mourning days from day tidings arrived; tears; counts thirty days (from day of tidings) (so Rambam and Nimmukei Yosef beginning Moed Katan and Mordechai) for haircut with other matters; rule: day of close tidings is like day of burial; if heard from day thirty onward — need observe only one hour, whether heard by day or night; if heard at night and observed part of mourning at night — one hour counts for him; and even for father and mother; and this for seven-day decree; but for thirty-day decree — for father and mother observe haircut until companions protest and pressing until festival comes and they protest; and greeting and entering banquet house; counts from day of death not day of tidings; therefore if tidings came for father and mother after twelve months — observes only one day even for thirty-day decree.
