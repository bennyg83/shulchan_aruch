In places observing second Yom Tov — if relative died on second Yom Tov last day of Pesach or Sukkot or second day Shavuot and buried that day — observes mourning since second Yom Tov is rabbinic and first-day mourning d'oraisa of death and burial pushes aside rabbinic positive command before Torah positive command; but if died second day Rosh HaShanah and buried that day — does not observe mourning, for both days are one long day; in what matter? Seven dead explicit in Torah that kohen becomes impure; those added siman 374 — mourning only rabbinic, therefore no mourning on any Yom Tov; and even those we said observe mourning second Yom Tov — do not tear; world practices not mourning second diaspora Yom Tov for any dead even death and burial day: {Rama: For they hold like those saying no mourning is d'oraisa; custom is widespread; do not change (Rabbenu Tam, R' Yehudah, Rosh).}
