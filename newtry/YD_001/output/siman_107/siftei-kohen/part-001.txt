and the opinion of R' Baruch, author of Sefer HaTerumah, and his supporters whom all the poskim brought to that place and agree with his opinion is that even though there is concern lest those twenty or thirty at the end of the pouring became forbidden, nevertheless they are nullified in the majority, since it is customary to have many small fish and there is majority against those that became forbidden, and those that became forbidden do not return and forbid the rest by their touch, since their prohibition is not on account of themselves they do not return and forbid without gravy, as explained in siman 105 seif 7; and he already poured all the gravy at the end to the vessel, and in the bowl there is no gravy, or the gravy in the bowl already became a second vessel and does not forbid at all, as written in siman 105 s.k. 4 — if so they only mixed in dry and are nullified in the majority; and so Bach explained, and so is explained in Issur VeHeter general rule 27 see there and investigate; and so is proven from words of Maharshal in his Issur VeHeter in the words "according to when he poured," see there, and so is proven explicitly in Hagahot Shach siman 50 that begins "there was a fish," etc., as he explained see there in all the places and investigate, for I abbreviated, and it is not like Beit Yosef and the Rav in Turei Chayim general rule 51 law 1, who understood the dispute of Ri Ba and R' Baruch when pouring little by little the fish with the gravy to the bowl — except that the Rav in Turei Chayim there wrote (in name of Hagahot Shach siman 51 the aforementioned, and its explanation is not so but as I explained shortly) that even R' Baruch and his supporters do not permit except when he poured the water in the meantime to another vessel after the first pouring before he afterward poured the fish in the pan;
