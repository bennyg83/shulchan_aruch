**** ENGLISH ****That we do not establish issur, etc. I already wrote opinion of Rashal that we do not establish so, and so is primary to be stringent in eggs; and even according to this opinion one should be stringent if he cooked many eggs in a pot and among them was one that is forbidden and he removed each one with one spoon — concern lest he removed the forbidden one first and the spoon became forbidden and consequently the others afterward; for that which we say we do not establish issur, etc. — that is because we say the issur is in the majority, and so Turei Chayim quoted in name of R"SH who is master of this opinion, unlike in this law.
