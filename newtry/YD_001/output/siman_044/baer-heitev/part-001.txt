**** ENGLISH ****
That they reach. Shach explained: namely on the loven. And in clear water — even from the side of the loven — kosher; however this is specifically per the Mechaber's view; but many poskim hold that if laketah occurred even in one kulya in murky water or its flesh became like flesh of the dead — even if they did not reach the place of the charitz — treifah; and it implies even in a small amount of putrefaction far from the place of the charitz — treifah {Rama: (for the Gemara says laketah in the kulya is treifah, and it is when the affliction reaches until the place of the charitz — implying only in laketah but not in putrefaction and murky water; and see Taz and Pri Chadash who disagree and rule like Shulchan Aruch that putrefaction must reach the loven in it. An incident in an animal: putrefaction was found in the kulya that was rotting and they did not manage to examine whether it reached the place of the charitz before a dog ate it — Maharil, Rashdam, Maharshach, and Shai declared treifah. Two testified they saw the kulya and there was no putrefaction, and two say there was putrefaction but they did not know if it reached the charitz — this is contradiction and two against two, and the animal stands in presumption of permission. And Maharshal wrote: although the Gemara says full of putrefaction is treifah — one may distinguish: if the blister was not from outside but from inside, the putrefaction must reach the place of the charitz; therefore it said "full"; but where the blister protrudes outside and there is putrefaction — treifah even if it did not reach the charitz — end of his words.
