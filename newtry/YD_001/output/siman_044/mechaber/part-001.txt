**** ENGLISH ****
A kulya that shrank — treifah; and how much may it shrink and become treifah? In a thin animal until a bean (ful) — and a bean is included in treifah; and in a large animal until a medium grape — and a grape is included in treifah; and specifically when it shrank on account of illness; but if it was small from its birth — kosher. And how do we know whether it is on account of illness or not? If we see that its membrane shriveled — it is known that it is on account of illness; and if it is not shriveled but made according to the measure of a kulya — the matter is proven that from the beginning of its creation it was thus — and kosher.
**** END BLOCK ****
