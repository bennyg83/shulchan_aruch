**** ENGLISH ****

However this is specifically per Tur, Mechaber, Rashba and their supporters; but Orach Chaim general rule 53 law 4 wrote: R' Shmuel ben Chafni the priest wrote in his Sha'arim: the animal was afflicted even only in one kulya in murky or rotting water, or its flesh became like flesh of the dead that reeks after days, that if one holds part of it, it dissolves and falls — treifah, even if it did not reach the place of the charitz, i.e. until the place of the loven — end; and Raavyah also wrote this: that requiring reaching the place of the charitz in the kulya is in sword-blow, but through putrefaction — even if it did not reach the place of the charitz — treifah; and he concludes: and likewise if split in two — treifah, from "He splits my kidneys" (Job 16:13) — end Orach Chaim (and it implies that even in a small amount of putrefaction far from the place of the charitz — treifah); and Beit Yosef brought this view of these poskim — see there Hagahot Ashiri; and so in Mordechai Piskei Avodah Zarah. And he wrote: it implies they explain what we say in the Talmud that we require the affliction reach the place of the charitz — only in sword-blow laketah; and so Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 86: in putrefaction even if it did not reach the loven — treifah; and so in Turei Chadashim general rule 9 law 1 he brings the forbidders at the end; but certainly in sword-blow all agree we require the affliction reach the place of the charitz (and what the rabbi wrote in the hagahah that sword-blow's law is like laketah of the kulya means it is treifah for all when it reached the place of the charitz — examine); and so Mordechai and Hagahot Ashiri in name of Rashi; and so Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 46, unlike Bach who forbids even in sword-blow if it did not reach the place of the charitz; and Bach is further stringent in several laws in this seif that are not essential in Talmud and poskim.
