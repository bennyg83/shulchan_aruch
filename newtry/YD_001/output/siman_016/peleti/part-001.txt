If that daughter of the doe, etc.—the great Shach in s.k. 15 wrote many challenges on Rambam: one challenge—how obligate lashes, for he ruled we doubt among Sages whether concerned for father's seed, as explained above seif 2 doubtful whether applies with males—if so if doubtful how lashes; and Shach rejects all poskim on this; and the main challenge: Gemara is arranged daf 80—Rav Pappa said in oto v'et beno: he-goat on doe—for prohibition Rabbis hold forbidden and R' Eliezer permits; deer on he-goat—for lashes; and how did Rambam write against Gemara; and further regarding gifts he ruled deer on he-goat liable half gifts from doubt, and he-goat on doe exempt from gifts from doubt; and here he establishes certainly liable for lashes; and also regarding kisuy hadam Rambam wrote unconditionally both he-goat on doe and deer on he-goat cover and do not bless and do not slaughter on Yom Tov—and this indicates doubt; if certainly liable for lashes, bless and cover on Yom Tov—see there he elaborated and left requiring study: truly all his challenges require study and especially against arranged Gemara words—no lashes—and requires study. And since we must toil to settle our great rabbi's words who did not err in Gemara words and his own words—I toiled and found with God's mercy slight settlement for his words.
