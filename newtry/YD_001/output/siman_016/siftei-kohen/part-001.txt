and they answered: there specifically they doubt whether oto v'et beno applies also with father because we expound "its son attached after it" to exclude the father whose son is not attached after it; but here it is plain we are concerned where the goat came upon the doe and she bore a female—for since this female has partial sheep it is considered as if entirely sheep; and if this female bore daughter or son and he slaughtered the female and her offspring—he receives lashes perforce; And know there is practical difference even for us in what they slaughter—for then he is disqualified for testimony from the Torah, as we hold in Choshen Mishpat siman 34; and likewise regarding kiddushin of a woman with a non-Jew siman 42—see there.
