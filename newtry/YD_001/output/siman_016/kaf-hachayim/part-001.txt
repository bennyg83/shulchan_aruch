<b>א) [סעיף א'] אסור לשחוט אותו ואת בנו ביום א' וכו'  </b>בין אדם א' שוחט שניהם בין שנים ששחטו א' האם וא' הבן או הבת או איפכא לעולם לוקה על שחיטת האחרון מדאו' שמ"ח או' א' קומץ או' א'.
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(1) [Seif 1] Forbidden to slaughter oto v'et beno in one day, etc.</b> Whether one person slaughters both or two who slaughtered one the mother and one the offspring, or the reverse—always lashes on slaughtering the last, from "or one day" "or one" handful "or one" (Vayikra 22).

<b>(4) There. <b>(12) [Seif 4] The one day stated in oto v'et beno, etc.</b> This R' Shimon ben Zoma expounded: said in Bereishit "one day" and said in oto v'et beno "one day"—as the one day in Bereishit, the day follows the night, so the one day in oto v'et beno, the day follows the night; but in kodashim the night follows the day, as written "on the day of his offering he shall eat, he shall not leave of it until morning"—apparently the night after it is day of his offering until morning. Chullin chapter 3 81a.
