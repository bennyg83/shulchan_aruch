**** ENGLISH ****
The signs of locusts. In it are 3 seifim: The signs of locusts — any that has four legs and four wings and its wings cover most of the length of its body and most of its circumference, and it has two jumping legs (meaning to leap and move from place to place) on them; and even if it does not have them now but will grow them after a time — and even though it has all these signs it is not permitted unless its name is chagav or they have a tradition that its name is chagav.
