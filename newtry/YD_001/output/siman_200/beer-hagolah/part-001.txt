**** ENGLISH ****Tur in the name of his father the Rosh and the Raavad, and like the opinion of the Rif in the name of the Geonim regarding that which is taught: all mitzvot one blesses upon them before doing them — Rabbi Chisda said except immersion — Pesachim page 7b. The Rif wrote in their name that Rabbi Chisda spoke only regarding immersion of a convert, for the person is not yet fit; and likewise the Rambam in chapter 11 of Hilchot Berachot.
