**** ENGLISH ****
(Seif 6) To reduce nidui from thirty days. And likewise to reduce in his prohibitions that are the particulars mentioned in this siman in law 2. Rekanati. And its source is in Beit Yosef, and these are his words: it is written in Kontresim in the name of Raavad that what they forbade in laws of a menudeh—that one may not sit with him, eat with him, speak with him, launder, or wear shoes—is only for an unspecified menudeh, or when they said "thus shall he be in nidui except for such-and-such a matter and such-and-such people," for what they excluded from nidui is permitted, and for all other matters he is a full menudeh; but if they specified his nidui, everything depends on their explanation—if they explained forbidden for this and that matter, for such-and-such people, and for such-and-such days—the menudeh has only what they specified and for the time they specified—end of his words; and even though the author of Kontresim wondered—from where do we have that there is authority to be lenient upon him—we have only Raavad's words z"l—end of Beit Yosef; and I wonder why the Mechaber omitted in our pure table in this seif this matter that they can be lenient upon him in his nidui law:

**** ENGLISH ****
(Shach s.k. 46) According to all there is no permission to release him. It seems to me Shach's intent: for Rosh there first brings the reason that they do not release him except in his presence, for it is fit to honor him to release in his presence; and according to this reason they released him in his presence—in advance it was released; and afterward he wrote: some say the reason is because of suspicion; and accordingly even b'dieved it is not permitted—see there; and Shach holds that for the first reason, because of his honor, notification does not help; and for this Shach wrote that according to all—that is, for the second reason because of suspicion as well—there is no permission to release through notification; and it seems so from that Rosh cited the second view regarding b'dieved and the second view stringently; and he did not cite a difference that the first reason is stringently that even through notification they do not release—necessarily that even for the second reason they do not release through notification; and this is correct with Hashem's help; and nevertheless regarding b'dieved there is no proof from Rosh; and one can say that through notification b'dieved helps; however one can say: since if they had not notified afterward it would not help b'dieved the release—one can say that since at the time of release not in his presence the release did not take effect, again the correction of notification afterward does not help; and similar to what Tosafot Gittin (daf 33b) s.v. "to scatter" etc.—examine:
