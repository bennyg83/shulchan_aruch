**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And some say that it does not.</b> This follows Rabbah. According to Rav Huna the passage must be forced into the formulation "when does this apply," and the flow of the Gemara does not support him, since it asks, "Until now?" Rosh. See Rashi there and Tosafot 69b, as well as Tosafot Bechorot 35a.
