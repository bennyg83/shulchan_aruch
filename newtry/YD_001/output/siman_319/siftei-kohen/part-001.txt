**** ENGLISH ****And the one born after, etc. — this is Rambam's wording and likewise in Tur; and Rambam follows the plain meaning perhaps it is not considered as born and deals where half emerged in majority of limb and therefore even if afterward all emerged it does not exempt the one born after since half emerged through cutting; and therefore even if afterward all of it emerged it does not exempt the second, as above seif 1; but Tur who holds even cutting limb by limb exempts the one born after, for nevertheless it is opener of the womb, and Rav likewise above seif 1 — if so it must deal here where not all was born afterward but its remaining half emerged through the wall, or e.g. it had twins and first half emerged in majority of limb and returned and afterward the second emerged and then the first emerged entirely — the second is not exempt by what the first initially emerged half in majority of limb; but if after half emerged in majority of limb all of it emerged and after time she gave birth, plainly exempt from firstborn status — what difference if initially half emerged in majority of limb, perhaps afterward all was born and this is simple.
