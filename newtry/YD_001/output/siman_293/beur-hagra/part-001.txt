**** ENGLISH ****
The prohibition, etc. — so wrote all the early compilations, and not like Semak; and Rosh already wondered at him and wrote that the anonymous mishnah at the end of Orlah is like R. Eliezer. And even though apparently the anonymous mishnah in chapter 49 of Menachot is like the first tanna, as written chapter 4, 1, like one who does not hold, etc., and he holds, etc. — the tanna of the mishnah does not hold so but explains his reason; but the tanna of the mishnah holds "when you come" depends on the land, and therefore Rambam ruled like the mishnah there, and so in Choshen Mishpat, see there. But all this is unnecessary, for we hold like the amoraim there 68b that all hold it applies outside the Land, and even R. Papa and R. Huna hold that nevertheless rabbinically one is obligated; and even in this we do not hold like him, for the sugya of halachah in all places is like the rabbis of R. Eliezer, and Ravina in the last [sugya] holds so and we hold like him, as written in 61. And the words of Behag here are a great error and need not be answered; and also what he wrote in the name of Bach — Bach holds it does not apply to gentiles' [grain], and all the later authorities have struck him on his head, and an error escaped from his hand; and it would have been better for Behag here to remain silent. And Maharshal (siman 489) wished to justify Semak's words, but his words are very forced, and he wrote himself that he wished to justify the custom; but a person of [refined] soul, etc. — but his words are not worthwhile even to justify the custom. And Taz wrote that in time of pressure it is worthwhile, etc.; but in the Gemara in Niddah there they said that if halachah was decided, tell him "time of pressure" — and it was already decided here in Menachot there (and so in Nachalat Kehuna, see there, and in Or Zaru'a, Laws of Orlah and Chadash siman 328). Rather, the leniency spread because they would sow before Pesach, as written in 63 in the gloss; and leave Israel — better, etc. (as written in Shabbat 148b and chapter 4 of Yevamot). (Likut) See in the gloss of Behag what he wrote as proof from Yerushalmi beginning Challah — but he erred, and it is in chapter 2, and thus Yerushalmi there [and Tosafot brought it in Rosh Hashanah 13a s.v. de'akrivu, etc.] — requires study.
