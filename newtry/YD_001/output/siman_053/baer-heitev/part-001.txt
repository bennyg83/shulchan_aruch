**** ENGLISH ****
Kosher. In an animal, from the thickness of the shoulder and breadth there is no concern for lung perforation as in a bird (Mordechai); and Bach is stringent in broken or slipped or cut even in an animal from concern perhaps its nivin were eaten; and Taz wrote: there are those who want to declare treifah if an extra wing is found in a bird like slipped wing; and Bach already obtained that declaring slipped limb is from concern perhaps lung was perforated — and this does not apply in missing or extra wing, for the lung was not perforated; if so, certainly no treifah in extra wing per his view:

**** ENGLISH ****

And so in Shach: he found in Tashbetz one should be stringent even if blood did not coagulate across ribs, from its nivin being eaten (and Maharshal is stringent in break like Shulchan De'ah and lenient in slipped; and Bach is stringent even in slipped); and the milk of that animal, and the same for an egg (Pri Toar) — question 53 permits; and see responsum Mabit question 10. Peri Chadash wrote: and it is good to be concerned for their words in a gentile's animal; but otherwise one should rule as Shulchan Aruch ruled, and even if blood coagulated in ribs there is no concern here. Levush is stringent in one broken rib in a bird opposite lung like broken wing; and Taz permits one broken rib and distinguishes rib from wing: in a wing, since the bird swings it, perhaps thereby it entered the lung; unlike a rib lying in one place — even inspection is not needed. The body. Perishah wrote: and if the spur entered within a thumb near the body, even though the break is more than a thumb from the body — treifah; and Shach wrote on this: he challenged — what of it the spur entered within a thumb; after all the place of breaking is far a thumb from the body; and Perishah must mean: even though the main break is more than a thumb, from the middle of the break there is far a thumb; nevertheless since the spur entered within a thumb — treifah; and so is implied in Taz. And if on the broken bone something remains connected to the body as thumb-measure, even if not from outer bone connecting a thumb — since the break was made far a thumb — kosher (and in responsum Mateh Yosef siman 5:6 he elaborated greatly to explain these Shach words in three ways, see there; and he raised for practical law: if broken near the body less than thumb-measure — treifah in every case even without spur and even if healed well, for it is not as at first — behold treifah; and if the break is far from the body without spur, even if the break is diagonal like a pole and a bit of the break is within a thumb — kosher; and if there is a round spur, if diagonal and part of outer bone is within thumb — treifah; and if the break is straight, even if outer bone entered within thumb — one may permit, end of his words, see there; and in responsum Noda BiYehudah question 61 he also elaborated. To be stringent. The reason is in Shach: this is not a complete double doubt, for the doubt whether the lung was not perforated is from lack of knowledge that we are not expert; therefore Rama was stringent in slipped (and Maharshal wrote: also in break one should be stringent in doubt when it occurred, for it is not a complete doubt).
