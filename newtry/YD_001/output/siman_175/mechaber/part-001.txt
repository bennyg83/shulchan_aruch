**** ENGLISH ****
The laws of setting a price on produce according to the market rate; it contains 8 se’ifim. We do not set a price for produce according to the rate of towns, because it is not fixed; but we do set a price according to the rate of the province, which is fixed. And once the provincial rate is set, it is permitted to advance money to his fellow and set with him that he will give him that produce all year, corresponding to his money, according to that rate — even if it becomes more expensive — even if the seller has no produce. {Rama: And some say that we set a price according to the rate of towns (Tosafos and Rosh and Tur and Hagahos Ashiri), and one may be lenient in a rabbinic prohibition (the author’s words).}

**** ENGLISH ****
Once the rate is fixed, it is permitted to set [a price] according to the high rate. How so? If wheat was selling 4 se’ah-measures for a sela, and he set with him that he will give him wheat at the low rate: if afterward they rose [so that it is] 10 se’ah-measures for a sela, he gives him 10 se’ah-measures for a sela.
