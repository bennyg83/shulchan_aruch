**** ENGLISH ****
Laws regarding an animal that ate deadly poison. In it are 3 seifim. One seized by blood, and one smoked (smoke entered its body), and one chilled (from cold illness), or one that ate deadly poison that kills the animal, or that drank bad water — behold it is permitted. {Rama: An animal that was fattened on forbidden things is permitted (Mordechai Yevamot chapter HaAlmanah leKohen Gadol); but if it was not fattened all its days only on forbidden things — forbidden (Tosafot Temurah chapter Kol HaAsurim).}

**** ENGLISH ****
An animal or wild beast whose legs were cut off — they may not eat from it except through examination, for there is concern perhaps a snake bit it; and this is its examination: they may not eat it except roasted; and if it was bitten by a snake, it will fall apart and drop in pieces. {Rama: And for us, who are not concerned for water exposure (gilui), since snakes are not common — therefore we are not concerned for a snake; and if it is on the road and did not become treifah, it is permitted without examination (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbenu Yerucham, and so Or Zarua HaAruch).}
