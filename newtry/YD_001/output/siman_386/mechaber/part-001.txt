**** ENGLISH ****
Covering the head in mourning. And it contains one seif. A mourner is obligated in covering the head — meaning he should cover his head with a tallit or scarf and turn some of it over his mouth and the tip of his nose; and this is when he needs covering all day, but when comforters come to him he uncovers his head for their honor: {Rama: Some say they do not practice covering in these lands (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 5); and so is the widespread custom, and one should not be strict to change what our fathers did not practice.}
