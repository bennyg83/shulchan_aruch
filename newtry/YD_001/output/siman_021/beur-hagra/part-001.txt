**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Even like a thread, etc.</b> In Torat Kohanim parashat Shemini "to distinguish," etc., certainly between cow and donkey they are explicit, but between tamei for you and tahor for you, between majority of kaneh slaughtered to half slaughtered—and how much between its majority to its half? Like a full hair. <br><b>(Likut) Even, etc.</b> Tosafot 123b s.v. olat, etc., and all the more in Chullin as is written 29a; and it is required, etc., and Ra'avad is as is written; but what is the end, etc., what is majority, etc. (end). <br><b>(Likut) Even like a thread, etc.</b> That which is said majority visible to the eyes means to exclude half—and even though half is like majority, nevertheless to the eyes, etc., but to the heart—and there is no question of "majority that is recognizable"; and in Torat Kohanim parashat Shemini they brought Rashi there, etc. Taz.
