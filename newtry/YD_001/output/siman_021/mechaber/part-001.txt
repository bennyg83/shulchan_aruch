**** ENGLISH ****
The measure of shechitah in quantity. In it are 5 seifim. How much is the measure of shechitah of the kaneh and veshet? The proper shechitah cuts both of them—whether in animal and beast or in bird; and to this the slaughterer should intend. And if he slaughtered a majority of one of them in a bird, and a majority of two in animal and beast—his shechitah is valid, provided that when they measure it they find that what was slaughtered is a majority; and once they find that what was slaughtered is more than half, even by a hair's-breadth—it suffices.

**** ENGLISH ****
[This is] not [a case where] he did not slaughter the majority of the siman in one place—such as that he began to slaughter and the siman turned over and he finished there, and between the two there is a majority: it is valid, whether in the kaneh or in the veshet. And not only if the two cuts are equal in one circumference, but even if one is toward the head and the second toward below—it is valid, whether one person slaughtered thus in two or three places, or whether two slaughtered with two knives. But if everything was on one side—such as that after he began to slaughter a little in this place, he left this place and slaughtered below or above it on that same side—he must have the majority in one place; and when there is a majority even in the second place, it is valid even though the shechitah is not spread out (meaning: revealed and recognizable).
