(There s.k. 24) And an incident came to us, etc. It appears to me he did not rule well, for certainly we do not say in such a case safek d'rabbanan lekula, for otherwise why did all the poskim here need speaking offhand or one entering and leaving — without this it would be permitted from safek d'rabbanan; and likewise above siman 68 seif 12 regarding meliga we only permit with one entering and leaving from fear of the gentile — without this it is forbidden even though it is safek d'rabbanan; and the old collection he found is certainly against all the poskim; rather certainly this case is different, since here an issur of blood was established, and wherever an issur was established we do not say safek d'rabbanan lekula, as written in Shulchan Gavoah siman 110 s.k. 63 din 20; or since it came to him from one side that it is rabbinic, as written there din 19; also one should not invent safek d'rabbanan to be lenient, as written there din 36, for we find many rabbinic doubts to be stringent — see there. (There s.k. 29) In Turei Zahav, etc. It is like that hunter who finds a large animal and takes small ones and takes — and I in my book challenged Turei Zahav on this, and thus I wrote: Mordechai wrote in the name of responsum of R' Shimon son of Abraham that even for one who permits in meat, in a whole chicken that was cooked one should be stringent even though there is sixty in the water, because of the liver and intestines, as one who places them in a basket of water and blood expelled from them is absorbed in the chicken and no longer exits, etc. — end of his words; and this responsum is in responsum of Maimonides; and Turei Zahav wrote that likewise if there are no intestines, it is forbidden in chicken according to all, for since it is hollowed blood spread from place to place and forbidden in such a case even in time of pressure and great loss — end of his words.
