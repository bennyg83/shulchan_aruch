And some, etc. Who hold it exits only from places adjacent to the flesh, and one must boil so it congeals inside and for adjacent places this does not help, that it exits before the flesh heats. His proof: Ran wrote from what is stated 112b: fish and poultry, etc., and melicha does not help — hence not all blood exits in melicha. And Rosh responsum 165 wrote explaining mulyata of Keitzad Tzolin (74) in meat that was salted [and so Rambam explicitly from there learned] and Rif wrote specifically for roasting, but for a pot we do not say as it absorbs so it expels — therefore he fixed it there at Passover. Rif. And the commentators explained in meat that was not salted — as stated siman 77: (Likkut) And some, etc. As stated in Pesachim 74b: this brine that is pickled, etc.; and in Chullin 111a: but Geonim wrote that pickling is to eat without melicha, and so all poskim; and Geonim wrote that nowadays they are not expert in pickling — therefore without melicha it is forbidden to cook even in boiling water. Tur Orach Chaim 451:1 (end of Likkut).
