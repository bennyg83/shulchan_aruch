**** ENGLISH ****In a place, etc. Pirkei de-Rabbi Eliezer chapter 2: why do we learn about purity — whoever eats with a gentile, etc., and whoever touches him is like touching a corpse, and whoever bathes with him is like bathing with a metzora. Mordechai in Avodah Zarah 44b wrote that we say there R. Gamliel bathed, etc., there the gentile preceded; Darkei Moshe wrote that what we are not careful nowadays is because they go in trousers, as the Agudah writes in chapter 4 of Pesachim and as written in Orach Chaim siman 23 s.v. 6 in the gloss; but it is not comparable there, and the Acharonim already wrote that in Pirkei de-Rabbi Eliezer he speaks because of purity, and it also appears there it deals with one bath — therefore there is no difficulty also in that R. Gamliel mentioned.
