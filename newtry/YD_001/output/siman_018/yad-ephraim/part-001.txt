<b>בתוך פגימת הצפורן.</b> עבה"ט ומ"ש בשם ד"ש סי' ד' הוא ט"ס וצ"ל סי' רי"ש וע"ש שכתב שאין פקפוק לבדוק סכין זו של שחיטה באצבע אגודל אע"פ שהוא העב והכבד שבאצבעות והרשות נתונה לבדוק באותו יד ואצבע שמרגיש בה ביותר כפי הכרתו בעצמו ובחוש משישו ומעשה בכל יום שכמה שוחטים בודקים באצבע אמה או קמיצה כ"א כפי הרגשתו אף שלא ראינו מי שבודק באגודל אפשר שימצא איש שמרגיש בו יותר משאר אצבעות והרופאי' במשישות הדופק יוכיחו יש מהם מי שאינו ממשש באגודל משום וריד הדופק בו ויש מי שממשש ומרגיש בו ביותר. ועיין מ"ש אא"ז בת"ש שמה שכתב לה"פ לבדוק באמה דוקא אין לחוש לזה ואין נוהגין כן וכ' בשו"ת אבקת רוכל בשוחט שלא הרגיש בפגימה אוגרת שאין להעבירו בשביל זה כיון שיש לתלות שמחמת ביעתותא שציוו אותו לבדוק בפני רבים וגדולים אבל אם נתברר שאין לו הרגש כלל וכן בכל דוכתי דמעבירין אותו מדינא מטריפין הכל למפרע אבל אם אומר ברי לי ששחטתי בלא פגימה ע"ש. ונראה שטריפה למפרע עד שעה שנבדק בה לפני חכם וראה שהוא בר הרגשה אז אומרים דאח"כ איתרע. וכ' במאיר נתיבים ס"ס ל"ה שאין לסמוך על בדיקת טופרא לבד כשנרגש בבשרא אף ששוחט הקנה וכן להיפך כשנרגש בטופרא אף שאינו מרגיש בבשרא אסור אף ששוחט הוושט לבד וחלילה לעשות כן ודלא כמ"ש בס' גן המלך וגם הוא אפשר שלא אמר כן אלא לפלפולא ע"ש:

<b>Within the flaw of the fingernail.</b> Avodat HaGolah; and what he wrote in the name of Darkei Shalom siman 4 is a printing error — it should be siman 200; and see there he wrote there is no question to check this shechitah knife on the thumb finger, even though it is the thickest and heaviest of fingers; permission is given to check with whichever hand and finger he feels most, according to his own knowledge and sense of touch; and it happens every day that several slaughterers check on index finger or middle finger, each according to his feeling, even though we did not see one who checks on thumb — perhaps one will be found who feels more on it than other fingers; and doctors in feeling pulse prove: some do not feel on thumb because of the pulse vein there; some feel and feel more on it. And see what Avodat Avodah wrote in Tiferet Shlomo: that which Lechem HaPanim wrote to check specifically on thumb — no concern for this, and we do not practice thus; and he wrote in responsum Avkat Rokhel on a slaughterer who did not feel a catching flaw — one should not remove him for this, since one can attribute that because of fear they commanded him to check before many great ones; but if it becomes clear he has no feeling at all — and so in all places they remove by law — they declare treifah everything retroactively; but if he says, "It is clear to me I slaughtered without flaw" — see there. And it seems treifah retroactively until the hour it was checked before a sage and he saw he has feeling — then they say afterward it deteriorated; and Meir Netivim siman 35 wrote one should not rely on atufra checking alone when felt on avshara, even if slaughtering trachea; and likewise reverse when felt on atufra even if not felt on flesh — forbidden even if slaughtering esophagus alone; Heaven forbid to do so, not like Sefer Gan HaMelech; and he too possibly did not say so except for pilpul — see there.

<b>Because it is soft.</b> Avodat HaGolah; and he wrote in Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 53: one who slaughtered with checked knife and flaw found, and certainly he did not touch joint-bone, for even simanim were not slaughtered and flesh of joint-bone intact on all sides; and he brought from Tur and Beit Yosef, Choshen Mishpat siman 306 in the name of Rashba: Rashba who exempts from paying deals with the animal-owner's knife — then if flawed in hide the butcher is not negligent if he did not check before shechitah; not so when the knife is the slaughterer's — then he was negligent for not having a good knife that will not flaw on animal's hide; and Tur deals with this, and there is no dispute — see there; and Shevut Yaakov siman 53: if we know the knife touched joint-bone — if there is still room to be lenient, such as flaw not as great as catching fingernail — there is a double doubt: perhaps law is like Rosh, etc.; perhaps on bone flawed; and flaw not so great — declare fit; also an incident: slaughterer heard knife sound on joint-bone like "krek" in Ashkenaz — recognizable thereby flaw came from there, and he declared fit; in such case all agree we say on bone flawed since legs to matter — see there; and he wrote in place of Shmuel siman 33: knife found flawed after shechitah — flaw that does not catch fingernail; they checked joint-bone and large cut found in joint-bone in depth of bone — declare fit, attribute to joint-bone, as appears from Tosafot Chullin daf 10 and Mordechai and Rosh and Beit Yosef in what he wrote to settle Tur's words in the name of Baal HaItur; and so other poskim; and so in Shechitot Mahara of Prague: large flaw found on knife and the three great men commanded to look after joint-bone and cut found — declared fit; and specifically cut in bone itself; but in white surrounding bone, which is soft — we do not attribute, for there the knife was flawed — end. And all the more in our case where it did not catch fingernail — see there.

<b>And he need not excommunicate him.</b> Avodat HaGolah; and see responsum Nachalat Shiva siman 15 on a slaughterer who ruled on an animal shaking its head and somewhat congealed matter found in the brain; the slaughterer showed it kosher, the membrane exists — since it required a sage's question and to see if brain surrounds the deficiency, concern lest laymen there, not Torah scholars, say in their opinion it surrounds; and possible if it came before the teacher he would declare treifah; and even if truly kosher — nevertheless do not forgive the excommunication; and see there he wrote: what sages forgave their honor in knife checking is toward slaughterers who trust them for shechitah and checking; but forbidden for slaughterer to give knife to check to one who is not their slaughterer — sages did not forgive honor except to appointees from their mouth on shechitah.

<b>כמו מן הכשרות.</b> עבה"ט וכ' בשו"ת עין משפט סי' א' בישראל שמושכר לעובד כוכבים למכור לו כשרות וטרפות והישראל נוטל שכרו הריאות של הבהמות למוכרה והדמים לעצמו בכה"ג אסור שהרי בשכר טרחו קונה הריאות מרויח בדמי גופן ולא דמי לנזדמנו לו אף שמוסר לו הבהמות מחיים בזה שמטפל בהם לשוחטן ולהפשיטן מ"מ אין לישראל קנין בהם וברשות העובד כוכבים נמצאת טריפה ולעובד כוכבים נזדמנו וכשישראל לוקחם הוי כלוקח להרויח אלא ימכור הבשר והריאות על כיס העובד כוכבים ויקח שכרו מכל דמי המכירה ואפי' מדמי הריאות גופא יכול ליקח כל שאין מייחד לו הריאות וכה"ג שרי דשכר סחורה של איסור אכילה מותר ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Like from the kosher.</b> Avodat HaGolah; and he wrote in responsum Ein Mishpat siman 1: an Israelite hired by a non-Jew to sell him kosher and treifot, and the Israelite takes wages of animals' lungs for selling them and the money for himself — in such a case forbidden, for through wages of his toil he buys lungs, profits in body money, and it is not like happenstance, even though he gives him animals alive — he tends them to slaughter and flay; nevertheless Israel has no acquisition in them and treifah is found in non-Jew's possession and happened to non-Jew; when Israel takes them it is like buying to profit; rather sell meat and lungs on non-Jew's purse and take wages from all sale money, even from lung money itself, provided he does not designate lungs for him; and such is permitted, for wages of prohibited food goods are permitted — see there.
