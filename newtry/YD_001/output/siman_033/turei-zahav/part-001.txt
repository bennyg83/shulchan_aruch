**** ENGLISH ****
And if reversed, etc. Reason in Tur: one is flawed and the other protects it — not like small puncture where friend protects; but here both entirely flawed — not sustained by second. Ran beginning siman 51 wrote: Rashi's "if reversed — treifah" means inner reddened through admonition and treifah, for we say it is derusah as below — found two drops of blood and treifah; Maharach z"l learned from this: even if only part of inner reddened — treifah, for we say derusah; even if predator not established before us. And he further wrote: even if redness found opposite intestines — treifah from this reason — end. Strange words — to cause loss of Jewish money wherever red point found in any flesh of animal saying derusah; if so, asmachta of blood congealed from blow in siman 57 — how find it; he says not where we saw strike, but from congealed blood we say from blow; and in siman 57 we forbid reddened flesh opposite intestines for derusah where doubt of derusah without seeing lion among animals; but without that, no; and siman 57 seif 14: even if neck torn — do not attribute to derusah if predator not seen between them; kal v'chomer for redness in esophagus. And in Teshuvot HaRishonim house 2 sha'ar 2 Rashi: if one reversed, e.g. both red — kosher, for only one corrupted — end; Rashi himself did not concern for derusah from redness; his words would contradict; rather Ran deals only where without that doubt of derusah we saw lion — relies on what we say below found two drops; there doubt of derusah; Rashba responsum 277: some concern doubt of derusah only with clear evidence — end. I had dispute with Maharach; presented to Maharam Yafe and rabbis — none concern for this at all; we permit daily when slight redness inside esophagus:

Darkei Moshe: only when both entirely red need entirety or majority; but actual exchange one red one white — treifah even slightly. And thus practiced to be lenient, etc. Explanation: in his city they were stringent on one side and lenient on other: per majority both membranes not punctured completely; sometimes punctured; if not punctured — flawed from fattening; all flaw as punctured — simple in places; common though not majority; minority frequent like lung adhesions — therefore stringent to examine; lenient on one side: if punctured from one wind — not concerned perhaps also second and healed — like "some permit" Rama mentioned; Rama on this: do not rely on "some permit" since d'oraisa; better not examine and rely on majority per all; than examine and be lenient where flaw. Because all places fatten — learn merit: permitted per all when flaw did not reach outer; when inner flawed, outer not — full permission when no flaw on outer. When examining geese we see all punctures on inner from fattening disease; since flaw did not reach outer — permitted to all who disagree only inner skin punctured without flaw before us; unlike when we see flaw ruled inner and not outer — why concern perhaps punctured and healed since inner did not heal — certainly full permission when no flaw on outer. Even if partial flaw on outer — no prohibition, for Tosafot Rosh "on outer no disease discernible" means no flaw at all on outer; if flaw ruled part of outer we follow reason: where ruled inner and not outer — ruled on inner; outer itself also not ruled — end of Tosafot intent. Therefore even if flaw ruled part of outer — no prohibition even if majority ruled; as gizzard siman 49 we permit even if little outer membrane remains. All the more esophagus: permit even if only minority outer remains if no flaw there per examiner — eyes of teacher.
