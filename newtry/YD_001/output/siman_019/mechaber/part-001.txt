The laws of the blessing on shechita. In it are 8 seifim. One who slaughters must bless before [reciting] "asher kidshanu bemitzvotav vetzivanu" on the shechita; and if he slaughtered and did not bless, it is kosher. {Rama: And if he slaughtered something in which a defect may develop and examination is required, he should slaughter it without a blessing; and when it is found kosher he blesses on the shechita, provided that he is close to the shechita, and he should not speak until after the shechita. (Or Zaru'a, laws of kisuy hadam, not like Rashi in a responsum.) And if he slaughtered in a kitchen, which is a foul place, he should bless at a distance of four amot before he enters there, and he should not speak until after the shechita (old Hagahot Shechitot in the name of the Agudah).}
