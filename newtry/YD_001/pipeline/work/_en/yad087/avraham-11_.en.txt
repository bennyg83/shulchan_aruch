(There, seif 10) Milk found in the stomach, etc., that stood in it one day — forbidden to curdle in it, for pickling is like cooking, as Shach wrote siman 105. And also the stomach skin is forbidden if there is not sixty against the clear milk in it, as stated in the Talmud in chapter Kol HaBasar (daf 109): a stomach cooked in its milk is forbidden. And in my humble opinion, if the milk congealed within twenty-four hours, it is not like pickling, as stated in siman 104 regarding fat that congealed. And likewise Acharonim siman 69 regarding ice; and thus it states in the mishnah at the beginning of chapter 2 of Taharot that milk that congealed is not considered a liquid but food. And likewise the Rambam chapter 1 of Hilchot Tum'at Ochlin halacha 17 — see there. And even if there is doubt whether it congealed within twenty-four hours or afterward — nevertheless one may be lenient, for he holds doubt of pickling in basar b'chalav, as the hagahah wrote siman 105 that it is permitted. And all this matters regarding the stomach skin, or regarding the milk without loss. For regarding the milk, when there is loss, in any case the view of the hagahah nearby is to permit clear and congealed — investigate. And all this is clear.
