Only through cooking. Baer Heitev — the law of smoked, cooked, and fried — see below seif 6 and what is written there. And see in Mishbetzot: milk that was in a pot that was pickled overnight and afterward they cooked animal meat in it — forbidden in benefit; and although the pot absorbed through pickling, nevertheless it discharged afterward through cooking to the meat. And if the meat was fowl meat, it is forbidden in eating and one may not be lenient. And he wrote in Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 61 regarding one who sent to ask a question of the decisor, and he forbade in benefit and the messenger discarded everything to loss; and afterward it became known that the decisor erred in a matter of mishnah — the decisor is exempt from paying since the decisor did not intend to harm, and even the messenger is exempt since he acted through an error in ruling. And he wrote in Noda BiYehuda siman 29: liver that was fried in forbidden butter is forbidden — not to speak of roasted liver that no longer discharges, it is nevertheless forbidden on account of the fat prohibition; but even living liver is forbidden even according to Rashi for this reason — see there.
