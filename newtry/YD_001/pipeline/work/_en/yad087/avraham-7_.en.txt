(There, in the hagahah) But milk of a dead animal and whey forbid the food, etc., and even in cooking one should forbid l'chatchila. It implies that regarding milk of a male, since it is not called milk at all and does not forbid the food, in cooking too it is not forbidden. And it requires investigation: who is worse than woman's milk in seif 4, where even though it does not forbid the food, nevertheless it is forbidden to cook, for in cooking marit ayin applies according to the view of the hagahah there, as Shach wrote there — and it requires investigation.
