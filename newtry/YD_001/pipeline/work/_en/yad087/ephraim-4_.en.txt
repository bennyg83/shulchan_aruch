And they are accustomed to make almond milk. Baer Heitev — see Kenesset HaGedolah that he inclined to permit what people practice in Kostel at a festive meal: after eating meat dishes they eat rice cooked in almond milk; they also practice eating vine leaves and cabbage stuffed with meat with almond milk — see there. And in Mishna Berurah he further wrote a leniency: since they do this only at a festive meal such as a wedding or circumcision, and they place them in fancy platters or at least in special beautiful platters in which one does not eat a regular dish, and they also place fruits and delicacies on the table — it is almost recognizable in its place and its time more than placing almonds, vine leaves, and cabbage; they have grounds to permit, since the filling is covered and is not recognizable in the platter — neither the meat nor the milk — only in his mouth does he sense it; there is no marit ayin here.
