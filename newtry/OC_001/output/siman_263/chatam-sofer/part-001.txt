**** ENGLISH ****
Regarding se’if 1, at the circled passage in the Rama: see the Rosh and Mordechai. This requires explanation in light of what is stated below in siman 591, se’if 4, where a proof is brought from the beginning of siman 282. However, this requires study from the Gemara in the chapter Arvei Pesachim, folio 104a, and from the beginning of the chapter HaTecheiles, folio 39a; one must explain the distinction. I later found this difficulty in the work written by the Shelah on the Mordechai. He labored greatly to resolve the passage in HaTecheiles, but he did not answer the difficulty from Havdalah; see there. It appears to me that there is a proof from the Mishnah in Parah, chapter 4, Mishnah 2: if he sprinkled the eighth time and then repeated and sprinkled an eighth time, it is valid. See Rabbi Ovadiah of Bartenura there. This is from the Gaon Kesav Sofer.
