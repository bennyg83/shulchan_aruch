**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 1, excrement, etc.</b> This is the view of the Rambam and the Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah, for the first version is the stringent one, and regarding a Torah-level matter we rule stringently. This requires study, however, because the Rambam's general approach is to rule in accordance with the latter version even concerning a Torah prohibition.

<b>ס"ב מי רגלים כו'. </b>דהא דאמר כתנאי לא קאי אמילתא דרבא דא"כ מאי קא' דכ"ע כו' דמ"מ הוא כתנאי וכ"ש לפי' הרמב"ם דרבא כר"י ומעיקרא נמי כר"י אלא אפלוגתא דרשומן ניכר ומטפיחין ומילתא דרבא כר"י וכלשונו:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 2, urine, etc.</b> The Gemara's statement that the matter is subject to a dispute among Tannaim does not refer to Rava's ruling. Were it so, what could the Gemara mean by saying, 'According to everyone, etc.,' since it would nevertheless remain subject to a Tannaitic dispute? This is certainly so according to the Rambam's explanation, under which Rava follows Rabbi Yehudah, and the initial position also follows Rabbi Yehudah. Rather, the Tannaitic dispute concerns whether a visible trace remains and whether it is moist enough to wet, while Rava's ruling follows Rabbi Yehudah and accords with his formulation.
