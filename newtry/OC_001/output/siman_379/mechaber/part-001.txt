<b>דין חצירו' ובתים ביניהם. ובו ב סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i>ב' חצירו' וג' בתים ביניהם <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="א"></i>פתוחים זה לזה ורוצים לערב יחד חצר זה בא דרך בית שאצלו ונותן עירובו <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>בבי' שבאמצע וכן עושה השניה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ב"></i>ומותרים בשלשתן שכל בית שאצל החצר <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ג"></i>חשוב לו כבית שער <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ד"></i>וא"צ ליתן פת והאמצעי הוא בית שמניחין בו עירוב וא"צ ליתן פת:
**** ENGLISH ****
Laws of courtyards and houses between them. Contains 2 sections Two courtyards with three houses between them opening into one another, wishing to make a joint eruv — one courtyard comes through the house adjacent to it and places its eruv in the middle house, and the second courtyard does likewise. They are then permitted in all three, since each house adjacent to a courtyard counts as a gatehouse for it and need not contribute bread, and the middle house is the house in which the eruv is placed and also need not contribute bread.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ב" data-order="2"></i>שני חצירו' ושני בתים ביניהם ולא עירבו יחד אלא כל אחת לעצמה ובא מן חצר זה דרך בית שאצלו והניח עירובו בבי' שאצל השני וכן עשה השני שגם הוא הניח עירובו בבית הסמוך לחצר האחר' לא קנו עירוב שכל א' הניח עירוב <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="2"></i>בבי' שער של חצר אחרת:
**** ENGLISH ****
Two courtyards with two houses between them, where they did not make a joint eruv but each made its own eruv separately — and one came from his courtyard through the house adjacent to it and placed his eruv in the house adjacent to the other courtyard, and the other did likewise, also placing his eruv in the house adjacent to the first courtyard — they have not acquired an eruv, since each one placed their eruv in the gatehouse of the other courtyard.
