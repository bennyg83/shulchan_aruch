**** ENGLISH ****
(ד) If there was a wedding on Rosh Chodesh — meaning the custom on Shabbat with a groom is to override the week's haftarah and read the haftarah of "Joy and gladness" which relates to the topic, as explained below at the end of siman 428; and it is explained there that all this is on ordinary Shabbatot whose haftarah is not mentioned in the Gemara and midrashim; but for haftarot mentioned in the Gemara and midrashim one does not override them for a wedding since that is only a mere custom; and even after reading them one does not read "Joy and gladness" because one may not skip from prophet to prophet — and therefore here in the gloss they inform us that at least where they are one prophet, as on Shabbat Rosh Chodesh when they read "The heavens are My throne" which is one prophet with "Joy and gladness," one does not override "Joy and gladness" entirely but reads part of it after finishing "The heavens are My throne":
