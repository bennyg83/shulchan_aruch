**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And if he received, etc., it is permitted.</b> It implies that this is so even if he himself received it, in accordance with the Rambam's view, which he wrote at the end of siman 443. So too is implied by the plain meaning of the Gemara in Avodah Zarah there. See Tosafos there, s.v. 'we learned'; 'it is astonishing,' etc., but with this it is resolved. See Ran at the beginning of chapter 5 of Avodah Zarah, who brought proof from the Yerushalmi and wrote there in the name of the Ramban that what we conclude there, that it is a fine they imposed on donkey-drivers and sellers of yayin nesech, applies specifically to yayin nesech and shevi'is, which transfer their prohibition to their money; and he relies on the first reasoning, that it transfers its prohibition to its money, etc.; see there. <b>Seif 5, there is.</b> There: he hired her to sit, etc. See the Gemara there, 65a: this is not difficult, this case, etc. The same applies here, where he takes nothing for the chametz, for even if he does not bake at all, he will pay him.

<b>וכן מותר. </b>נלמד מהנ"ל אבל להשכיר לו שיכניס חמץ לתוכו אסורכמ"ש בירושלמי פ"ב הלכה ב' לא ישכיר ישראל את ביתו לא"י ליתן בתוכו חמץ ואע"ג שאמרו בפ"ק דפסחים ייחד לו בית כו' כאן איירי בתוך הפסח או בע"פ שכבר נאסר בהנאה וה"ל משתכר באיסורי הנאה כנ"ל וכ"מ בירושלמי שם דבתוך הפסח מיירי בתוך הפסח מהו כו' והתניא לא ישכיר כו' ועט"ז ומ"א וע' ב"י בשם אגור. ול"נ דגם ההיא דפסחים פ"ק מיירי בתוך הפסח כמ"ש שם ת"ר א"י שנכנס כו' וע"כ בתוך הפסח ועלה קאמר הפקידו כו' ייחד כו' למימרא כו' אלא דשם מיירי שייחד לו בית שלא להכניס לו חמץ ומותר אף כשמכניס בתוכו כנ"ל וע"ז אמרו שם דא"י כי קאי כו' למימרא כו' והתניא כו' דשם ג"כ שוכרו לבית דירה ואעפ"כ אסור והגאונים מפרשים דלא כפרש"י אלא ייחד לו בית שמשכיר לו בית אחר שלא בביתו וז"ש א"י שנכנס כו' ר"ל כיון שהוא עם החמץ הפקידו כו' אפילו בלא אחריות ייחד לו כו' בלא אחריות כיון שהוא בבית אחר וכן מ' בירושלמי שם חמצו של א"י בתוך הפסח מהו ר' ירמיה אמר מותר ר' ייסא אמר אסור והתניא לא ישכור את תמורו לא"י להביא עליו חמץ פתר לה בבא עמו והתניא לא ישכיר את ביתו כו' אית לך מימר בדר עמו אלמא אף למאן דמתיר אסור עמו בבית וז"ש בתה"ד ולמעלה סי' תמ"ח ס"ג שמחוץ לבית:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And likewise it is permitted.</b> This is learned from the above. But to rent to him so that he will bring chametz into it is forbidden, as written in the Yerushalmi, chapter 2, halacha 2: a Jew may not rent his house to a non-Jew to place chametz in it. Although they said in the first chapter of Pesachim, 'he designated a house for him,' etc., here it speaks during Pesach or on erev Pesach after it has already become forbidden in benefit, and therefore he is profiting from items forbidden in benefit, as above. So too it appears in the Yerushalmi there that it speaks during Pesach: 'during Pesach, what is the law,' etc.; 'but was it not taught: he may not rent,' etc. See Taz and Magen Avraham, and see Beis Yosef in the name of Agur. It seems to me that the case in the first chapter of Pesachim also speaks during Pesach, as written there: the Sages taught, a non-Jew who entered, etc.; necessarily this is during Pesach. About that it says: if he deposited it, etc.; if he designated, etc.; does this mean, etc.? Rather, there it speaks where he designated a house for him not in order to bring chametz into it, and it is permitted even when he brings it into it, as above. On this they said there, 'when the non-Jew stands,' etc.; does this mean, etc.; but was it not taught, etc.? There too he rents it as a dwelling house, and nevertheless it is forbidden. The Geonim explain unlike Rashi, that 'he designated a house for him' means that he rents him another house, not in his own house. This is what it means by 'a non-Jew who entered,' etc.: since he is together with the chametz, if he deposited it, etc., even without responsibility; if he designated for him, etc., without responsibility, since it is in another house. So too it is implied in the Yerushalmi there: a non-Jew's chametz during Pesach, what is the law? Rabbi Yirmiyah said it is permitted; Rabbi Yissa said it is forbidden. But was it not taught: one may not rent his donkey to a non-Jew to bring chametz on it? It answers that this is where he comes together with it. But was it not taught: one may not rent his house, etc.? Can you say that this is where he dwells together with him? Thus, even according to the one who permits, it is forbidden when he is together with him in the house. This is what is written in Terumas HaDeshen and above, siman 448, seif 3, where it is outside the house.
