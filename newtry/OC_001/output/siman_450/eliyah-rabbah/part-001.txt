**** ENGLISH ****
[4] <b>Before Pesach, etc.</b> However, on Shabbos and on the four days of Yom Tov it is forbidden, as he wrote in siman 445 [Magen Avraham]. Chok Yaakov wondered about this and permits on Yom Tov, since kindling is permitted for a need; all the more so there is no concern for prohibition if a non-Jew does it as his agent, end quote. In my humble opinion, Magen Avraham holds that since the chametz is not fit for a Jew, such kindling is a complete prohibition even through a non-Jew.
