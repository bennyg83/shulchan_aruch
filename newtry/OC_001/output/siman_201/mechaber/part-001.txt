**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Who recites the blessing. It contains four se'ifim.</b><br>The eldest recites the blessing, even if he arrived last. And if he wishes to grant permission to a younger person to recite the blessing, he may. This applies when there is no guest present; but if there is a guest present, the guest recites the blessing, even if the host is greater than he, so that he may bless the host. And what blessing does he bless him? "May it be [Hashem's] will that this host not be shamed nor humiliated, neither in this world nor in the World to Come; and may he prosper in all his possessions; and may his possessions be successful and near to the city; and may Satan have no dominion over the work of his hands; and may no matter of sin nor sinful thought present itself before him, from now and forever." And if the host wishes to forgo {i.e., he does not wish to be particular about} his blessing and to recite Bircas Hamazon himself, he may. {Rama: And the same applies that he may give [permission] to recite the blessing to whomever he wishes. (Beis Yosef in the name of Ohel Mo'ed.)}
