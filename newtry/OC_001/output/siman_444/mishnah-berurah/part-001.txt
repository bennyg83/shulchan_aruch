(ז) He nullifies it in his heart — in a place where after nullification he need not burn it but rather from doubt; and in a place of mitzvah the Sages established it on d'oraisa. (ח) Even if there is time — to burn chametz and return and rescue; and the reason is because of saving lives they were lenient to rely on nullification l'chatchila, even where he can fulfill both.
