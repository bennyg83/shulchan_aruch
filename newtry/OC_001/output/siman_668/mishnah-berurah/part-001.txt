(ו) מפני שהוא ספק שביעי - ומטעם זה הרבה אחרונים מצדדין דצריך ג"כ לישן בסוכה וכן הוא ג"כ דעת הגר"א ויש אחרונים שסוברין שאין כדאי לישן בסוכה בשמ"ע לא ביום ולא בלילה דעושה אותו כחול דבשלמא גבי אכילה לא מנכר שכן דרך ב"א לפעמים לישב בצל סוכה אפילו שלא לשם מצוה משא"כ בשינה. ומנהג העולם להקל בשינה. ודע דלדעת הפוסקים שסוברין דאין ישנים בסוכה בשמ"ע י"א דה"ה דאין אוכלין אז פירות וה"ה פת פחות מכביצה בסוכה. כללו של דבר כל דבר שא"צ לברך עליו ברכת לישב בסוכה בשאר ימי סוכה אין אוכלין בסוכה בשמ"ע ויש מקילין בזה וכן מסתברא דבאכילת פירות וכה"ג דברים שאין חייבים מצד הדין יכול לעשות בשמ"ע כמה שירצה בבית או בסוכה:
**** ENGLISH ****
(6) Because it is a doubtful seventh day - For this reason many later authorities incline that one must also sleep in the sukkah, and this is also the view of the Gra. Other later authorities hold that it is not proper to sleep in the sukkah on Shemini Atzeres, neither by day nor by night, because he makes it like a weekday. Granted, with eating it is not recognizable as such, because people sometimes sit in the shade of a sukkah even not for the sake of the mitzvah; this is not so with sleeping. The common custom is to be lenient regarding sleeping. Know that according to the poskim who hold that one does not sleep in the sukkah on Shemini Atzeres, some say that the same applies that one does not then eat fruit or bread less than a kebeitzah in the sukkah. The rule is: anything over which one does not need to recite the blessing of leishev basukkah during the other days of Sukkos, one does not eat in the sukkah on Shemini Atzeres. Some are lenient about this, and this is reasonable, for eating fruit and similar things, which are not required by law, may be done on Shemini Atzeres as much as he wants, either at home or in the sukkah.

(ז) ואין מברכין וכו' - דכיון דיום שמ"ע הוא א"א לברוכי בסוכה דקשיין אהדדי אם יום סוכה הוא לאו שמ"ע הוא ואם שמ"ע הוא לאו יום סוכה הוא ומשו"ה עבדינן לחומרא. והסכימו האחרונים דאין לאכול בערב עד שחשיכה אכן בדיעבד אם נטל ידיו ובירך המוציא לא יברך לישב בסוכה דכבר קיבל שמ"ע בתפלתו ובקידוש:
**** ENGLISH ****
(7) And we do not recite a blessing, etc. - Since it is the day of Shemini Atzeres, it is impossible to recite a blessing in the sukkah, for the two contradict each other: if it is a day of sukkah, it is not Shemini Atzeres, and if it is Shemini Atzeres, it is not a day of sukkah. Therefore we act stringently. The later authorities agreed that one should not eat in the evening until it is dark. However, after the fact, if he washed his hands and recited Hamotzi, he should not recite leishev basukkah, for he has already accepted Shemini Atzeres in his prayer and Kiddush.

(16) And he announces Mashiv haruach, etc. - This is explained above in siman 114; see there. The practice in some places, after Minchah of the first day of Yom Tov, to sit down to drink until evening, sometimes continuing an hour or more into the night, is improper for several reasons. First, we hold in siman 99 that if one drank a revi'it of wine, he should not pray even if he can still speak before the king; the same applies if he drank other intoxicating beverages, as explained there in seif katan 1. Even if he drinks other beverages that do not intoxicate, once doubtful nightfall arrives the obligation of Kiddush rests on him and he may not taste until he recites Kiddush. Even after Kiddush, he is still prevented from eating because of the evening Shema and is permitted only a mere taste, such as fruit or bread up to a kebeitzah and no more. Yet one may search for merit for them, since many remind one another and they will not come to forget Maariv. In any event, one must be careful then not to drink an intoxicating beverage; all this is explained above in siman 99 seif katans 1 and 2.
