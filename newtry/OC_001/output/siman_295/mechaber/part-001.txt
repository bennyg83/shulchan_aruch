**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The Havdalah recited by the chazzan. It contains one se'if.</b><br>{Rama: And we say "Vihi No'am" and the Order of Kedushah at length, in order to delay the Order of Kedushah, for then the wicked return to Gehinnom (Tur). And at a time when we do not say "Vihi No'am" — such as when a Yom Tov falls during the [coming] week — we do not say the Order of Kedushah, but we say "Veyiten Lecha" (Kol Bo).}<br>The chazzan recites Havdalah in order to discharge the obligation of one who has no wine. {Rama: And the custom is to say and to make mention of Eliyahu HaNavi on Motza'ei Shabbos, to pray that he come and herald to us the redemption (Tur).}
