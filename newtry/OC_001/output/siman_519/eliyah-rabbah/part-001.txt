**** ENGLISH ****
<b>[Levush:] Who does not fear, etc.</b> It is implied that if he fears [theft], even in the house it is permitted (Taz); and this reason was written by all the poskim except for Rashi's explanation; and not like the Taz who ruled regarding both of them [cases] with a hinge in the middle, etc., that even putting it back completely as needed is permitted, provided that he does not wedge it in; and if he wedged it in he is liable because of makeh b'patish; and see siman 313.
