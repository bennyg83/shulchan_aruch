**** HEBREW ****
<b>(בס"ק א) גם בח'.</b> דאלו מתרמי סעודת' כו' בא ליישב אע"ג דקי"ל אמרינן מיגו דאתקצי ב"ר משום ספק יום שעבר וכמ"ש לעיל ר"ס תרס"ה ע"ז כתב דהכא גרע דגם ב"ה צריך לעשות בה מעשה דהיינו אם אתרמי ליה סעודה ב"ה צריך לאכול בה חמיר טפי כ"כ התוס' והרא"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(In s.k. 1) Also on the eighth.</b> For if his meal happens, etc. He comes to resolve that although we hold that we say miggo that it was set aside during bein hashmashos because of the doubtful previous day, as written above at the beginning of siman 665, about this he wrote that here it is worse, because even during bein hashmashos he must perform an act with it, meaning that if a meal happens for him during bein hashmashos, he must eat in it. This is stricter; so wrote Tosafos and the Rosh.
