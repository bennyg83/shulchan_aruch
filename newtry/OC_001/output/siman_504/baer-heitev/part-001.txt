<b>לגרוד. </b> ול"ד לחרדל שאסור לשוחקו דהתם רגילין לשחוק הרבה ביחד. ולפ"ז התמכ' שקורין קרי"ן אסור לגורדו בי"ט שדרך לגרוד הרבה לב' או לג' ימים מ"א ע"ש לפ"ז נ"ל דאסור לטחון בי"ט האקווי"א וכן נוהגין <small>(אבל בס' אליה רבה מתיר לגרוד תמכ' דלא הוי עובדא דחול אלא ברחיים שטוחן הרבה מאוד)</small>. גם נראה לי המגיה טעם להתיר דמפיג ודאי הטעם כשעושה מעי"ט. ולפ"ז גם בקאווי"א אפשר להקל אם שכח מעי"ט:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>To grate.</b> And it is unlike mustard, which is forbidden to grind, for there they are accustomed to grind a large amount together. And according to this, horseradish that they call kren is forbidden to grate on Yom Tov, for the practice is to grate a large amount for two or three days (Magen Avraham; see there). According to this it appears to me that it is forbidden to grind coffee on Yom Tov, and so is the custom (but in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he permits grating horseradish, for it is not weekday labor except with a mill with which one grinds a very large amount). Also it appears to me that the glossator [gave] a reason to permit, that it certainly dissipates the flavor when one prepares it from before Yom Tov. And according to this, regarding coffee as well one may be lenient if he forgot [to prepare it] before Yom Tov.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>A change.</b> And if it dissipates flavor, one does not need a change, for it is like spices (Rivash; Beis Yosef; Yam Shel Shlomo). However, there are those who are stringent if he knew from yesterday, as is written in s.k. 2 (Magen Avraham).
