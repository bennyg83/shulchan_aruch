**** ENGLISH ****
Laws of spices on Yom Tov. 4 sections: Spices may be pounded in the normal way, since if they were pounded the day before their flavor would dissipate. But salt may not be pounded on Yom Tov unless one tilts the mortar or pounds it in a bowl or similar vessel in order to make a change — since if salt were ground before Yom Tov its flavor would not dissipate. Pepper and mustard may not be ground in their special mills since that appears like a weekday activity; rather one pounds them in a mortar like all other spices. {Rama: Rema: Nevertheless the custom is to make a slight change when pounding spices. (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 3 in the name of Semag) And this is the proper ruling.}
