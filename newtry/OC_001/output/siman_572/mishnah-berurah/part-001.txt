(6) Congregation — once Maharil decreed not to eat meat every Monday until Rosh Hashana, and the 15th of Av fell on Monday and he did not wish to eat meat, but on erev Yom Kippur and at a seudas mitzvah such as pidyon haben it is permitted with meat and wine, and Magen Avraham concludes that specifically there, since he decreed and all relied on him, with his intent the matter depends — he did not intend to forbid on erev Yom Kippur nor at a seudas mitzvah; but an individual who accepted upon himself, it applies to him that he is not obligated to eat meat even on Shabbat and Yom Tov — see there.
