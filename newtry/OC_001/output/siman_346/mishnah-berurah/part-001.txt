(צ) And likewise our roofs etc. — Taz wrote here is explained the doubt many people are uncertain about regarding a house that has before it toward the street a roof projecting to the street called in foreign tongue "perliban" — what is their law whether one may carry there from the house; and many err to say the projecting ceiling to the street descends and closes and it is as if built down to the ground; and from here it implies explicitly the area under the roof is like the domain before them — see there; and so all the later authorities; and what is their correction — if the roof projection has pillars toward the street one should place aligned opposite them also poles toward the houses and the roof upon them will have the law of tzurat ha-petach and behold there is tzurat ha-petach from all three sides; and if they are of many houses standing one adjacent to another one need not make for each house but make this correction in two houses standing at the end from this side and from that side — but they need to join eruv chatzerot; and then permitted to insert and remove from the houses under the roofs and likewise the reverse [later authorities]; and in all cases forbidden to remove and insert from reshut ha-rabbim under the roofs and likewise the reverse [Chayei Adam]; and this is when the projection width was four tefachim — then there is upon the area under it karmelit; and see Chayei Adam general rule 53 seif 14 what he further wrote regarding gangways adjacent to inn houses and the roof rests on the pillars:
