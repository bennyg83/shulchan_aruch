<b>שלא לאכול אחר אכילת אפיקומן. ובו ב סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i>אחר אפיקומן <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Chok Yaakov" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="א"></i>אין לאכול <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ב"></i>שום דבר: <small>הגה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ג"></i>ולא יאכלנו <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Chok Yaakov" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ד"></i>בשני מקומות <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ה"></i>דלא עדיף מאלו הפסיק בשינה דאסור משום דהוי כשני מקומות (טור)</small>:
**** ENGLISH ****
Not to eat after eating Afikomen. Containing two sections. After Afikomen, one should not eat anything. {Rama: Rema: And he may not eat it in two places, because it is no better than if he interrupted sleep when it is forbidden to eat it because it is like two places (Tur).}

**** ENGLISH ****
One who sleeps during the meal and wakes, does not return to eat. Members of a group, some of which slept during the meal, may return and eat. If they all slumbered and awoke, the may not eat. If all of them napped, they may eat. {Rama: Rema: And all of this only applies where they slept after they began eating the Afikomen. But if they slept before that, it is not an interruption (Tur and Rabbeinu Yeruchem).}
