(2) (b) Utensils that have been sitting in it - and the the Master is permitted to take out of the yard to the house vessels that have been sitting in it, as well as from the house to the yard, and as above in siman 15, the Sages shall not rule over shared entrances, but to allow to take out to the house vessels that have been sitting in the house, but in the matter of yards and entrances themselves they will not buy, and one authority is right to move them from one to the other, and likewise they are also permitted in this, as well as in this way, they are the same as the right to shake all the yard. If they did not mix with the houses, then it is permissible to shake the entire building even if the members of the courtyards did not participate in the building and simply the 17th of Myri that the building itself was corrected in the cheek and legally done:
