**** HEBREW ****
<b>א) [סעיף א'] אם לא עירבו החצירות יחד  </b>ר"ל אף אם עירבו כל החצירות ביחד דאם עירבו כל החצירות דרך פתחים ביחד בלא"ה שרי דהו"ל כמו שיתוף כמ"ש בסי' הקודם:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1) [Se'if 1] If the courtyards did not establish an eruv together. </b>This means even if the residents of each courtyard established an eruv among themselves. If all the courtyards established a single eruv together through entrances between them, carrying is permitted in any event, since that eruv is equivalent to a shituf, as stated in the preceding siman.
