(א) בתוך עשרה טפחים - דהיינו שלצד ר"ה אינו גבוה י"ט ולצד החצר הוא גבוה י"ט דאלו לא היה גבוה י"ט אף לצד החצר לא היה מהני סולם כלל דהוי ר"ה גמור כשרבים מכתפים עליו וכשאין מכתפים עליו הוי עכ"פ כרמלית אבל השתא כיון דמצד החצר הוא גבוה י"ט יש על הגג תורת רה"י ומותר להשתמש עליו כשיש לו סולם קבוע לעלות בו מחצרו לגגו דבזה גלי דעתו דמסלק לגגו מן בני ר"ה ואין שייך רק לו ואם מצד ר"ה גבוה י"ט א"צ סולם. ואם הגג נמוך פחות מעשרה טפחים מכל צד ובתוכו עמוק י"ט כגון שחקק הקרקע בתוכו והשלימו לי"ט דעת המ"א דא"צ סולם דאין שם כרמלית עליו כלל דהוא רה"י גמור ורבים מאחרונים חולקין עליו ודעתם דמדרבנן שם כרמלית ע"ג מלמעלה כיון שהוא נמוך מי"ט מכל צד וצריך סולם להתירו ובפרט כשרבים מכתפין עליו אפשר דהוא ר"ה גמור עי"ז ואפילו סולם לא מהני להתירו:
**** ENGLISH ****
(א) Within ten tefachim — meaning that toward reshut ha-rabbim it is not ten tefachim high and toward the courtyard it is ten tefachim high; for if it were not ten high even toward the courtyard a ladder would not help at all, for it is a full public domain when the public leans on it, and when they do not lean on it it is in any case karmelit; but now since from the courtyard side it is ten high, the roof has the status of a private domain and it is permitted to make use of it when he has a fixed ladder to ascend from his courtyard to his roof, for by this he reveals his intent that he ascends to his roof from the people of reshut ha-rabbim and it applies only to him; and if from the side of reshut ha-rabbim it is ten high, a ladder is not needed. And if the roof is low less than ten tefachim on every side and its interior is sunken ten tefachim — such as if he dug the ground inside and completed it to ten — the view of Magen Avraham is that a ladder is not needed, for there is no karmelit upon it at all, for it is a full private domain; and many later authorities disagree with him, and their view is that d'rabbanan there is karmelit above from above since it is low less than ten on every side, and a ladder is needed to permit it; and especially when the public leans on it, it is possible that thereby it is a full public domain and even a ladder does not help to permit it:

(א) The place of the partition is judged like the sides of reshut ha-rabbim — the sides of reshut ha-rabbim ordinarily are called when they place large stones or insert stakes adjacent to the wall so wagons should not damage it; and the airspace between them is called the sides of reshut ha-rabbim and its law is like karmelit, and forbidden to carry from within it to reshut ha-rabbim; and it teaches us that even in our matter, even though there is no separation at all between the courtyard and reshut ha-rabbim and the public enters it directly and tramples it — nevertheless its law is only like the sides of reshut ha-rabbim and there is karmelit upon it, and forbidden to carry from this place outside which is reshut ha-rabbim; and if karmelit adjacent to a courtyard it is permitted, for from karmelit to karmelit one may carry; and in our reshut ha-rabbim one should be stringent:
