<b>(ס"ק א) וחוזר כו'</b> ובספר כו' אבל אם או' כו' הוי רמ"ט תיבות דהא בק"ש יש רמ"ה תיבות מלבד אמת וא"כ אי יאמר אמת ב' פעמים וגם ה' אלהיכם הוי רמ"ט תיבות והיחיד כיון דאפשר לו שיתכוין שלא לצרף תיבת אמת שאו' הש"ץ ויהיה רמ"ח עם תיבת אמת שאומר היחיד בלחש לכן עדיף שיאמר אמת בלחש כדי שלא להפסיק בין אלהיכם לתיבת אמת כמ"ש וה' אלהים אמת כמ"ש סי' ס"ו סעיף ה' וכ"ה בגמ':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(Seif katan 1) And repeats, etc.</b> And in the book, etc. But if he says, etc., there are 249 words, for Krias Shema has 245 words apart from ‘emes.’ If so, if he says ‘emes’ twice and also ‘Hashem Elokeichem,’ there are 249 words. Since an individual can have intent not to join the word ‘emes’ said by the shaliach tzibbur, and there will be 248 with the word ‘emes’ that the individual says quietly, therefore it is preferable that he say ‘emes’ quietly so as not to interrupt between ‘Elokeichem’ and the word ‘emes,’ as written, ‘and Hashem God is true,’ as stated in siman 66, seif 5, and so it is in the Gemara.

**** ENGLISH ****
Page 14b, regarding what Rabbi Yehudah holds: in the middle of a chapter one may greet because of fear and respond because of honor, and between chapters one may greet because of honor and respond to any person. Rabbi Yehudah said that between the word ‘Elokeichem’ and ‘emes’ it has the law of the middle of a chapter. If he needs to interrupt to greet because of honor or to answer any person, he should wait until he has also said the word ‘emes.’ The Amoraim dispute whether, if he interrupted between ‘emes’ and ‘veyatziv,’ he goes back and says it after he interrupted, and the conclusion is that he does not go back and say ‘emes.’ We say: the one who descended before Rava, Rava heard him say ‘emes’ twice. Rava said: this man has been seized by ‘emes, emes.’ Rashi explained: the momentum of saying ‘emes’ seized this man. The Beit Yosef asked: if so, why do we say ‘emes’ twice? He answered that we, since we interrupt between one ‘emes’ and the other with the words ‘Hashem Elokeichem,’ have no issue with it. But he asked: if so, the one who descended before Rava must have said ‘emes, emes’ adjacent to each other, without interrupting to say ‘Hashem Elokeichem emes.’ If so, Rava should have said to him: why did you not repeat ‘Hashem Elokeichem emes’? He answered: it may be said that in Rava’s days they did not repeat ‘Hashem Elokeichem emes,’ and to complete the 248 words in Krias Shema they would say ‘El Melech Ne’eman.’ He gave another answer as well; see there. The Magen Avraham wrote that according to the words of Asarah Maamaros, this is well understood: the one who descended before Rava did repeat ‘Hashem Elokeichem emes,’ but he also said ‘emes’ quietly, and regarding this Rava said ‘every emes, etc.,’ for this is not proper, since if so there are 249 words, as above.

<b>יכוין בוי"ו שבו אמונה.</b> כ' בספר של"ה שר"ל יצייר כאלו הוא"ו כתובה מלאה ואלף באמצע דהיינו וא"ו. א"כ ג' אותיות שבוא"ו יש בו להשלים ג' שמות. דהיינו ו' הראשונה מן א' עד וא"ו יעלה כ"א דהיינו א' עם ב' הם ג'. ויצרף גם ג' הם וי"ו. ויצרף גם ד' ואז יעלה יו"ד. ויצרף גם ה' ויעלה ט"ו. ויצרף גם וי"ו ויעלה כ"א. והוא מספר שם אהי"ה ואות אל"ף שבוי"ו שנכתב' כדין אל"ף יו"ד למעלה ויו"ד למטה וי"ו באמצע כדאי' בהרב"י סי' ל"ו בתמונת האותיו' והוא עולה כ"ו כמנין שם הוי"ה וא"ו האחרונ' יצרף כמו שמצרף בוא"ו ראשונ' יעלה שוב ג"כ כ"א כמנין שם אהי"ה הרי ג' שמות ב"פ אהי"ה. ופ"א הוי"ה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>He should have intent with the vav in ‘emunah.’</b> The Sefer Shelah wrote that this means he should picture it as though the vav were written fully, with an alef in the middle, namely vav-alef-vav. Thus the three letters in the written-out vav contain enough to complete the three Names: the first vav, by adding from alef until vav, totals 21, for alef with beis is three; add gimel and it is six; add dalet and it is ten; add hei and it is fifteen; add vav and it is 21, which is the number of the Name Ehyeh. The alef within the vav, written according to its proper form as an alef, with a yud above, a yud below, and a vav in the middle, as stated in the Beit Yosef, siman 36, regarding the forms of the letters, totals 26, corresponding to the Name Havayah. The final vav is joined as with the first vav and again totals 21, corresponding to the Name Ehyeh. Thus there are three Names: twice Ehyeh and once Havayah.
