(1) Another window on the south side—in it stand six lintels for ascent for those who recite Keriat Shema with will and kavanah every day. Rabbi Perachia said: The Divine Presence—as I gazed at that window that is near—the minister named Na'aniel, who is minister over the door of the south side, said to me: Stand in your place and see this window: no one has permission to ascend there or to look into it except those who recite Keriat Shema as fitting; and all who recite Keriat Shema as fitting—sixty ministers ascend before the Messiah and crown him with holy crowns and say to him, This is the crown of so-and-so who ascended in Keriat Shema as fitting. I asked him and said, My master, what is Keriat Shema as fitting? He said to me: If you are pious—the supernal secret is here in Keriat Shema, and it has four parshiyot. The first parshah is of unification—it is unifications in twelve words: six words in supernal unification and six words in another unification. The second parshah, "And these words shall be"—has 42 words in the secret of the name of 42 letters (and it is the name of Ana be-khoach, etc., as written in Pardes Keriat Shema ch. 24 in the name of the Zohar, see there, and Sha'ar HaKavanot in the derashot of the night, derashah 5 and derashah 9). The third parshah, "And it shall be if you heed"—is included right in left and left in right; and it is in the secret and concealment of the holy chariot until "and you shall bind them"—which is the parshah that rules over all; it is in the secret of 72 words within the secret of 72 names of the holy chariot (and it is the 72-letter name of Vayisa Vayavo Vayet, as written in Pardes there ch. 23 and Sha'ar HaKavanot there). The fourth parshah, "and you shall bind them"—has fifty words corresponding to the fifty gates of Binah. The parshah of tzitzit completes all of them, and the parshiyot ascend in number [of the stature] of man, etc. Come and see: whoever completes the unification as fitting and has kavanah in these parshiyot every day merits those lintels and windows to see; he rules here and none hinders his hand—provided he reads as fitting and has kavanah in these supernal secrets and unifies with my right hand. And he said to me, If you are pious—come see the honor of those who unify the holy Name as fitting and ascend in Keriat Shema in this manner. I entered one palace, and in that palace were 365 palaces by the count of the days of the year. Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob rule in that palace, and Adam ha-Rishon among them. I saw how many bundles of souls are clothed in one radiance of the Tree of Life, and how many modes of supernal invocation enter the palace—and Yofiel, the great minister over all, the great minister of Torah with them; they opened and said, Crown yourselves, holy ones of the world—behold these sons who unify the holy Name and have kavanah in the will of the parshiyot of Keriat Shema. And I saw Adam ha-Rishon striving on their behalf; and at the time one enters Keriat Shema that a person recites, I ascend first to that palace and Adam ha-Rishon receives me. If one letter is missing from Keriat Shema he does not receive her, for the structure of man is lacking—he is 248 in those words that the shaliach tzibbur repeats. And if he finds it as fitting he receives her, and Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob take her from him and kiss her. Whoever sees rejoicing—whoever sees confusion among all those righteous ones in the Garden of Eden who come in that rejoicing. And at the time one comes to this world—who will see his rejoicing? They seat him beside his fathers twice a day. And every day dew descends on him from the King's head, and in that dew they know and eat food that the supernal angels eat; and they know what was, what will be, and what is destined until the time HaKadosh Barukh Hu will resurrect the bodies that sinned in this world and raise them—HaKadosh Barukh Hu—in a repaired structure as fitting—end quote. Behold you know how great is the exaltedness of one who says Keriat Shema with kavanah; therefore a person must always be careful to remember these things whenever he recites Keriat Shema evening and morning—the statute was given and shall not pass; and at least he should give his heart to understand the meaning of the words and not lack a word or letter—and especially the kavanah of the names.
