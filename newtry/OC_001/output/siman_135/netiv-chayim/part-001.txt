**** HEBREW ****
<small>(בט"ז סק"ג)</small> ול"נ דר"ע ור"נ ס"ל כיון. נ"ב דברי תימא הן דאף שהם ממונים ות"ח וממנים במקומות אחרים משא"כ ת"ח בעירם. כמו כן יש ת"ח כר"ה שכל החכמים באותו דור כפופים ליה מצד חכמתו איכא למימר דסגי בחכמתו לחוד:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>(In Taz, seif katan 3)</small> “It appears to me that Rabbi Ami and Rabbi Assi hold that since they were appointed,” etc. Note: These words are astonishing. Even though they were appointed officials and Torah scholars who made appointments in other places, this differs from Torah scholars in their own city. Likewise, there may be a Torah scholar like Rav Huna, before whom all the sages of that generation were subordinate because of his wisdom. One may therefore say that his scholarship alone sufficed.
