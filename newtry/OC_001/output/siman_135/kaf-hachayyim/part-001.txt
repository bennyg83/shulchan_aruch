<b>א) [סעיף א'] בשני ובחמישי וכו'  </b>משה רבינו תיקן להם לישראל שיהיו קורין בתורה ברבים בשבת ובשני ובחמישי בשחרית כדי שלא ישהו ג' ימים בלא שמיעת תורה, ועזרא תיקן שיהו קורין כן במנחה בכל שבת משום יושבי קרנות, וגם הוא תקן שיהיו קורין בשני ובחמישי ג' בני אדם ולא יקראו פחות מעשרה פסוקים, הרמב"ם פי"ב עה"ת דין א' והוא מש"ס בבא קמא דף פ"ב ע"א יעו"ש, ועיין מ"א ריש הסי' ובשער הכוו' דמ"ח ע"ד כתב הטעם שמוציאין ס"ת בב' וה' לפי שבשני ובחמישי שולטת עולם היצירה שהיא בחי' התורה יעו"ש, וכ"כ פרע"ח פ"א משער קריאת ס"ת ופ"א משער השבת, וטעם לס"ת שמוציאין בשבת כי בבקר ביום שבת אנו קורין בס"ת כנגד מדת החסד המתעורר בתחילת היום, ובמנחה כנגד הגבורה ששולטת אז, והכונה הוא לכלול זה בזה פחד עם חסד מפני שהתורה מב' מידות אלו נתנה כמש"ה מימינו אש דת למו, שער הכוו' דע"ג מ"ד:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1) [Seif 1] On Monday and Thursday, etc.</b> Moshe Rabbeinu instituted for Israel that they should read from the Torah publicly on Shabbos and on Monday and Thursday at Shacharis, so that they should not tarry three days without hearing Torah. And Ezra instituted that they should read likewise at Minchah every Shabbos, on account of the idlers in the marketplace; and he also instituted that on Monday and Thursday three men should read, and that they should not read fewer than ten verses. Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilchos Tefillah, law 1; and it is from the Gemara Bava Kama, folio 82a; see there. And see the Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman. And in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 48d, he wrote the reason that they take out a Sefer Torah on Monday and Thursday: because on Monday and Thursday the World of Yetzirah, which is the aspect of the Torah, holds sway; see there. And so wrote Pri Etz Chaim, chapter 1 of Shaar Krias Sefer Torah, and chapter 1 of Shaar HaShabbos. And the reason for the Sefer Torah that they take out on Shabbos: for in the morning on Shabbos day we read from the Sefer Torah corresponding to the attribute of Chesed, which is aroused at the beginning of the day, and at Minchah corresponding to Gevurah, which holds sway then; and the intent is to include this within that, fear together with lovingkindness, because the Torah was given from these two attributes, as the verse says, 'from His right hand, a fiery law unto them.' Shaar HaKavanos, folio 73d.

<b>ב) שם ואין מוסיפין עליהם וכו'  </b>משום ביטול מלאכה לעם, וגם אין מפטירין בנביא מטעם זה לבוש, ט"ז סק"א, מ"א סק"א. ושבת במנחה דליכא ביטול מלאכה מ"מ כיון דדרשו עד סמוך לחשיכה לא רצו חכמים להטריח הציבור בהוספה, כן פי' רש"י שם במגילה מחה"ש סק"א ומיהו בשער הכוו' שם דמ"ח כתב הטעם בסוד משום דאין מתגלין עתה בב' ובה' רק הנה"י דאבא בלבד, כי נבקעים נה"י דאבא ויוצאת הארתם לחוץ ולכן קורין ג' עולין' כהן כנגד הנצח קו ימין, לוי כנגד ההוד קו שמאל, ישראל כנגד היסוד קו האמצעי, והנה הארה זו אינה נשארה כך רק לפי שעה, ואח"כ חוזרין ומתעלמין בפנים כמתחלה ולכן תיכף אחר הקריאה סוגרין הספר תורה ומחזירין אותו למקומו תוך ההיכל עכ"ד, וכן מנהג בית אל יכב"ץ שתיכף אחר הקריאה קודם אשרי ובא לציון מחזירין הס"ת למקומו בין בחול בין בר"ח בין חו"ה רק בשבת ויו"ט אחר אשרי וג' העולים במנחת שבת הם כנגד ג"ר דאבא המתגלים עתה א' כנגד חכמה וא' כנגד בינה וא' כנגד הדעת כמ"ש בשער הכוו' דף ע"ה ע"א וע"ב יעו"ש, ולכך אין פוחתין מהם ואין מוסיפין עליהם, ועיין לקמן או' י"ט:

<b>2) There: And one does not add to them, etc.</b> On account of interruption of labor for the people; and one also does not read a Haftarah from the Prophets for this reason. Levush; Taz, s.k. 1; Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. And [as for] Shabbos at Minchah, where there is no interruption of labor: nonetheless, since they would expound until close to nightfall, the Sages did not wish to burden the congregation with an addition. So Rashi explained there in Megillah. Machatzis HaShekel, s.k. 1. However, in Shaar HaKavanos there, folio 48, he wrote the reason according to the hidden teachings: because there are revealed now, on Monday and Thursday, only the Netzach-Hod-Yesod of Abba alone, for the Netzach-Hod-Yesod of Abba split open and their illumination goes forth outward; therefore three ascend: a Kohen corresponding to Netzach, the right line; a Levi corresponding to Hod, the left line; a Yisrael corresponding to Yesod, the middle line. And behold, this illumination does not remain thus, but only for a moment, and afterward they return and are concealed within as at first; therefore, immediately after the reading they close the Sefer Torah and return it to its place within the Heichal. End of his words. And such is the custom of Beis El, may it be built and established, that immediately after the reading, before 'Ashrei' and 'Uva LeTzion,' they return the Sefer Torah to its place, whether on a weekday, on Rosh Chodesh, or on Chol HaMoed; only on Shabbos and Yom Tov it is after 'Ashrei.' And the three who ascend at Shabbos Minchah correspond to the first three sefiros of Abba which are revealed now, one corresponding to Chochmah, one corresponding to Binah, and one corresponding to Daas, as stated in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 75a and 75b; see there. Therefore one does not reduce from them and one does not add to them. And see below, ot 19.

<b>3) There, gloss: And if there were two bridegrooms, etc., it is permitted to add, etc.</b> Maran, of blessed memory, wrote in the Bedek HaBayis that we did not have the custom to add on Monday and Thursday on account of bridegrooms, and we have also not heard of anyone who practiced so. End of his language. And the Knesses HaGedolah cited him in the Hagahos Beis Yosef, and wrote: and so it is written in the Tikkun Yissachar, folio 55a; and so the Pri Chadash concurred, ot 1. And in the Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, ot 2, he wrote that so he ruled in practice. And so wrote the Levush, that one does not practice so; and the Magen Avraham cited him, s.k. 2, and the Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1; and the Eliyah Rabbah wrote there on the words of the Levush that whether for two men of a bris or for two bridegrooms one does not practice to add. And so wrote the Eshel Avraham, ot 2; and so wrote the Maamar Mordechai, ot 1, that one does not practice to add on Monday and Thursday beyond three; see there. And so it appears from what was stated above in the previous ot in the name of the Arizal, of blessed memory, that according to the inner meaning of the matters there is no room for an addition on Monday and Thursday; see there, and examine it well.

<b>5) There, gloss: If they omitted one Shabbos, etc.</b> And the Maharam Mintz, siman 85, wrote that specifically two sidros they read if the reading of one Shabbos was omitted, since in any case we find that sometimes they read two sidros on one Shabbos in ordinary years; but if two Shabbosos were omitted, they do not read three sidros on the coming Shabbos, for we do not find any place where they read three sidros on one Shabbos. And likewise, if the parshiyos were joined on the previous Shabbos, there is no making them up, for there is no reasoning to divide them and read half the obligation on the coming Shabbos. And likewise, if they omitted parashas Vayechi, one should not read it with parashas Shemos, for when one reads two sidros, one man must read from the end of the first sidra to the beginning of the second sidra so that they be joined, and this is not proper to do with two separate scrolls; see there. And the Knesses HaGedolah cited him in the Hagahos HaTur, siman 282, and wrote that according to his words, if they omitted [reading] on a Shabbos whose parshiyos are not joined, and the coming Shabbos has joined parshiyos, one should not read the parashah of the previous week; see there. And so wrote the Olas Tamid there, ot 4; Ateres Zekeinim in this siman, ot 2; Magen Avraham, s.k. 4; Yad Aharon in the Hagahos HaTur; Soles Belulah, ot 1. However, in the Hagahos Minhagim he wrote concerning the aforementioned words of the Maharam Mintz that his words do not appear correct in his eyes; and the Eliyah Rabbah cited him, ot 2, and wrote that his reasoning is logical, that one should not skip any parashah, and all the more so when two are joined on this Shabbos, that they also read the sidra of the previous Shabbos; and the same applies when they omitted two or three Shabbosos, they read all the parshiyos that they omitted at one time; see there. And such is the view of the Bais Dovid in the laws of Shabbos, siman 106; and see there, where he refuted all the aforementioned words of the Maharam Mintz. And so wrote the Mateh Yehudah in siman 282, ot 14. And so he concluded in the responsa Devar Moshe, Orach Chaim, siman 50, and wrote that a shaliach tzibbur who erred on Shabbos Vayakhel-Pekudei, thinking that they were not joined, and read Vayakhel alone: he should read Pekudei on Shabbos Vayikra, along with parashas Vayikra, like the sefer of the Bais Dovid; and one should not say in this 'safek berachos lehakel,' since in every place where one reads from the Torah there is an obligation to bless; see there. And so wrote the Emes LeYaakov in the laws of error, siman 168, ot 2; Ledovid Emes, siman 9, ot 2. And one must read them in their order, on account of 'the Torah of Hashem is perfect': first the parashah that they omitted, and afterward the obligation of the day; and the majority of the seven who ascend should ascend for the obligation of the day. And it is better that one of those who ascend read the parashah that they omitted, and the rest for the obligation of the day, like the Rashal in the name of the sefer Leshon Chachamim; and see there. And in my humble opinion it appears that the one should read the entire omitted parashah, and also begin to read three verses in the parashah of the day's obligation, so that all of them read and bless for the day's obligation. And afterward I saw that so wrote the sefer HaShomer Emes, siman 15, ot 1; and the Pesach HaDvir cited him, ot 4, and concurred with his words, and wrote that it appears all agree in this; see there well. Only if the omitted parashah was the end of a sefer, such as Vayechi or Pekudei, then the one should not begin to read in the second sefer, which is Shemos or Vayikra, because it is not proper for one man to read in two separate scrolls, as mentioned above in the name of the Maharam Mintz; and see the Pesach HaDvir there. However, on Monday and Thursday they read the weekly parashah like the rest of the congregation, as stated below in ot 9; and further see below in siman 144, ot 2.
