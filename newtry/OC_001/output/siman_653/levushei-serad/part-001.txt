**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham</small> s.v. 'it appears to me.' In any event, the dispute whether to recite a berachah on the fragrance of the esrog during the festival is at the end of siman 216. The Magen Avraham wrote the reason there: it does not stand for fragrance but for the mitzvah. Consequently, on Shabbos, everyone agrees that he smells it and recites a berachah.
