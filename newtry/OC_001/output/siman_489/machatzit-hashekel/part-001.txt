**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(s.k. 4) And the weeks, etc.</b> For we hold, etc., that it is rabbinic. And that which the Rama wrote in se'if 4, "and if he began to eat he stops": and if it is rabbinic he need not stop, as above in siman 235, se'if 2. **** ENGLISH ****
<b>And if he counted weeks alone he has also fulfilled, as the Tur wrote in the name of Ravyah, that one should count thus l'chatchila.</b> That is, until seven days he should count days; and when the seventh day arrives he should say "today is one week"; and on the eighth day he should say "today is one week and one day"; and he need not say "today is eight days, which are one week and one day"; and similarly from there onward in this manner. Although we do not hold like them, nevertheless after the fact one may rely on him.
