(5) And in Eretz Yisrael, etc. — because there they need rain, as it is higher than all lands, unlike in exile; and we in all Diaspora follow the children of exile of Babylonia. A son of Eretz Yisrael in Diaspora, or the reverse — if he intends within a year to return, he requests as his place; and if after a year, he requests as the place where he is, even if he has wife and children at home — so Peri Chadash, and the Pri Megadim brought it; and in Brachi Yosef he agreed in the name of many great ones per Maharaz Gut and Mahari Molcho that each asks as the sons of the city where he is found, unlike Peri Chadash — see there. And apparently from the words of Ba'er Heitev it implies they speak specifically when he does not intend to return; and one needs to examine responsa Devar Shmuel and Yad Aharon, for there is their source.

(ט) בש"ת - ונראה דלפ"ז דכ"ש שיש לנו לשאול מטר בש"ת אחר ז' מרחשוון או בין פסח לעצרת במקומות הצריכין לכך דהא אפילו בתקופת תמוז דסימן קללה הם שואלין בש"ת. כתב ט"ז הא דצבור מותר להתפלל בש"ת היינו כשמתפללין בלחש אבל לא יאמר אותו הש"ץ בקול רם אפילו בש"ת ומנהגנו כהיום שאומר הש"ץ בש"ת אבל אינו אומר ותן טל ומטר לחוד כ"א בפסוקים ושאר לשונות וכבר נדפס בסידורים. ובשבת ור"ח ויו"ט מזכירין י"ג מדות ואומרים מזמורי תהלים של מטר כדאיתא בסידורים:
**** ENGLISH ****

<b>(9) In Shomea Tefillah.</b> It appears that, according to this, we should certainly request rain in Shomea Tefillah after the seventh of Marcheshvan, or between Pesach and Shavuos, in places that require it. After all, even during the summer season, when rain is considered a sign of curse, they request it in Shomea Tefillah. Taz writes that the congregation's permission to pray for it in Shomea Tefillah applies when they pray silently, but the chazzan should not say it aloud, even in Shomea Tefillah. Our present custom is that the chazzan does say it in Shomea Tefillah, but he does not say only, “Give dew and rain.” Rather, he uses verses and other formulations, as printed in the siddurim. On Shabbos, Rosh Chodesh, and Yom Tov, they mention the Thirteen Attributes and recite Tehillim concerning rain, as stated in the siddurim.

<b>(10) He repeats the prayer.</b> This refers to one who has completed his entire prayer. He should then repeat the prayer from the beginning as a voluntary prayer. If he remembered before completing it, he cannot return to the blessing of the years and continue as a voluntary prayer. Rather, he should first complete the entire prayer, and afterward repeat it as a voluntary prayer. Maamar Mordechai.
