**** ENGLISH ****
Chametz belonging to a Jew over which Passover has passed is forbidden in benefit, even if he left it [in his possession] by mistake or under duress. And if he sold it or gave it to a non-Jew who is outside the house before Passover—even though the Jew acknowledges to the non-Jew and knows that he will not touch it at all, but will guard it for him until after Passover and will then return and give it back to him—it is permitted, provided that he gives it to him as an absolute gift without any condition, or that he sells it to him in an absolute sale for a small amount. But a gift on condition to return it does not work.
