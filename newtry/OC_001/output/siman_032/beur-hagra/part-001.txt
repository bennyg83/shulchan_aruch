<b>Therefore, etc.</b> This is the language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 8: An open parsha has two forms. If he finished in the middle of the line and there remained a space of the required measure, he leaves it and begins at the start of the second line. If the required measure did not remain, or if he finished at the end of the line, he leaves one line without writing and begins at the start of the third line. A closed parsha has three forms. If he finished in the middle of the line and there remained a space of the required measure, and enough to write one word at the end of the line so that the space is in the middle, he leaves it. If the above measure did not remain, he leaves the entire rest of the line empty, leaves a small space at the beginning of the second line, and begins from the middle of the line. If he finished at the end of the line, he leaves a space of the required measure at the beginning of the second line and begins from the middle of the line.
