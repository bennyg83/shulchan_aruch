**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham, seif katan 10.</small> <b>And so it appears at the beginning of siman 42, where he wrote.</b> This is explicit in the sugya in Menachos 34. However, this is difficult for me, for if so, what is the Gemara’s question there, “and according to the one who says that designation is effective,” etc.? Perhaps that too is only to take it out for weekday use, but lowering it to a lighter sanctity is permitted, as it is for us regarding designation in the body of a sacred object. I found in a responsum of Avkas Rochel that he proved from here that there is no distinction between ordinary use and lowering in sanctity, and the Panim Meiros, part 3, siman 11, also agreed to this. Regarding the proof of the Magen Avraham from the answer, “with new ones,” according to the foundation of the Baal HaMaor, designation of the body of a sacred object is effective because we derive it from the eglah arufah. This follows our halacha, for we rule like Reish Lakish, that its descent to the strong-flowing valley forbids it. But there in Menachos, where the Gemara answers, “with new ones,” that is according to the master of that teaching there, Rabbi Yochanan, who says that one may not lower, etc. According to him, since Reish Lakish says in Kerisos that the breaking of the neck forbids it, in truth even regarding the body of a sacred object we say that designation is not effective, and the answer “with new ones” is proper. Then the Gemara asks, “and according to the one who says designation is effective,” meaning according to Abaye, and likewise according to the one who says that its descent to the strong-flowing valley forbids it, for according to him, regarding the body of a sacred object at least, designation is effective. Examine this carefully. In a responsum I discussed this at length.

<small>Seif 67.</small> <b>Provided that he covers it with a hide.</b> The Derishah disagrees with this.
