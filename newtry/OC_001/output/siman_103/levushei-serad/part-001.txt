**** HEBREW ****
<small>(מג"א סק"א)</small> לגמור פי' ששהה גם אחר שפסק הריח וע"י צירוף היה כשיעור לגמור וקמ"ל דלא תימא כיון שתחלת ההפסק היה באונס ה"ל כאלו היה הכל באונס:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>(Magen Avraham, seif katan 1)</small> “To complete” means that he also paused after the odor ceased, and the combined time equaled that required to complete the entire prayer. It teaches that one should not say that since the beginning of the interruption was caused by duress, the entire interruption is regarded as having been caused by duress.
