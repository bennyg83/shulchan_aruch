**** HEBREW ****
<b>י"א כו'. </b>עמ"א ס"ק ד' ה' וכ"מ בגמ' דברישא בס"א אין אומר רבון כו' ועפ"ג דברכות הנכנס לסעודת כו' אלמא גנאי הוא וכ"ש בכה"ג אבל העיקר כמ"ש בב"י דק' הדבר מאוד לבדות חילוקים מן הדעת כו' ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Some say, etc.</b> See Magen Avraham, seifim katanim 4 and 5. This also appears from the Gemara, for in the first case, in seif 1, he does not say, “Master, etc.” See chapter 3 of Berachos concerning one who enters a banquet, etc. It follows that this is disgraceful, and all the more so in a case such as this. Nevertheless, the primary ruling is as written in Beit Yosef, that it is very difficult to devise distinctions based on reasoning alone, etc.; see there.
