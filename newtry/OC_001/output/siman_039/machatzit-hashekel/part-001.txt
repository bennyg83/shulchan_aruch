**** ENGLISH ****
Until he is eighteen years old: therefore there is a scribal error here. Eliyah Rabbah also wondered at this, for there are three measures: if he brought two hairs after thirteen he is an adult; if he reached twenty and did not bring two hairs but signs of a saris appeared, he is an adult; and if twenty years passed without two hairs and without signs of a saris, he remains a minor until thirty-six years pass. All this is explained in Choshen Mishpat, but we have not heard of a measure of eighteen years, except regarding one opinion in the Gemara and regarding appointment as a judge.

<b>(Seif katan 7) Because of, etc.</b> This is what it means, etc. In Gittin 45b three baraisos are set against one another: one teaches that a Torah scroll written by a non-Jew is burned, another that it is placed in genizah, and another that it may be read. The Gemara resolves them by distinguishing between an assumed intent for idolatry, lack of intent for its required purpose, and a convert who returned because of fear. The Rosh holds that such a convert is still included in binding, since he refrains only because of fear.

<b>So it also appears from Tosafot, etc.</b> Tosafot asked why the baraisa of genizah was not established according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who requires writing for its required purpose.
