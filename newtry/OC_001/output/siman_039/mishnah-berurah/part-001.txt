**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Or a minor.</b> Since we derive this from a verse, we require an actual adult, namely one who brought two hairs after becoming thirteen years old. If it is doubtful whether he brought two hairs, he is invalid to write unless his beard filled in, most of his years passed, or signs of a saris appeared. Scribes who allow youths to write tefillin without verifying signs of adulthood should be rebuked; see above, siman 32, seif katan 103, and Biur Halacha.
