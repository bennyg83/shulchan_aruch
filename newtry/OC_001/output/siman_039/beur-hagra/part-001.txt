**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 3, a convert, etc.</b> See Magen Avraham. The Rashash raised a difficulty from what is stated there, and that which was taught, that one may read, etc., was established as referring to a convert, etc., who does it for its required purpose, while the three baraisos that disagree concerning a non-Jew speak of an actual non-Jew. In Mishnas Binyamin he answered that the teaching that one may buy books, etc., speaks of an actual non-Jew, and the incident of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel concerns a convert; see there.

<b>ס"ה נמצאו כו'. </b>רמב"ם וכלישנא בתרא וכ"פ הטור כאן. אבל הרי"ף ורא"ש גורסים להיפך ולפ"ז נקטי' דיגנזו וכ"פ הטור י"ד ברסי' רפ"א ורי"ו ובש"ע שם כ' ב' דיעות הנ"ל ועב"י כאן שדחק לתרץ דברי הטור וכנ"ל לחלק בין ס"ת לתפילין אבל דוחק הוא אלא תלוי בחילוף הגי' כנ"ל וכמ"ש הר"ן:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 5, they were found, etc.</b> Rambam, according to the latter version, and so the Tur ruled here. But Rif and Rosh have the opposite version, and according to that we rule that they are placed in genizah, as the Tur ruled in Yoreh Deah at the beginning of siman 281, and Rabbeinu Yerucham. In the Shulchan Aruch there he wrote both opinions; see Beit Yosef here, who pressed to reconcile the words of the Tur, as above, by distinguishing between a Torah scroll and tefillin, but this is forced. Rather, it depends on the change of version, as above, and as the Ran wrote.
