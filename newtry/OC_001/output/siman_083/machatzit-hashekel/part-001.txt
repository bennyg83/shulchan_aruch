And R' Yehoshua stands, etc. — meaning, how will Ravina answer the gemara's challenge from Rav Yosef? Therefore he said Ravina holds R' Yehoshua stands on the mishnah — meaning, in the mishnah it teaches the measure of distancing four amot, and Rav [Chisda] innovated specifically behind him, but before him one needs as far as his eyes can see; and since in the mishnah the measure of as far as his eyes can see was not mentioned, on this R' Yehoshua stands — that even when there is no feces in it, this measure of distancing is required, namely four amot; and regarding the measure of four amot it is equal whether there is feces in it or not; but regarding before him there is certainly a distinction between there being feces in it and not — when there is feces in it, distancing as far as his eyes can see is required, and when there is no feces in it, even before him four amot suffices.
