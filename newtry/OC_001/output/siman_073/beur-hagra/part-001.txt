**** HEBREW ****
<b>ס"ב בחזרת פנים כו'. </b>ר"ל אפילו הוא מחזיר לבד וכמ"ש בגמ' זה מחזיר פניו וקורא כו' וכ"מ מדברי רמב"ם דלא כמ"א וערש"י שם ד"ה ואפילו אשתו כו':
**** ENGLISH ****
Se'if 2: “By turning his face,” etc. This means that even if he alone turns away, it suffices, as the Gemara states: “This one turns his face away and recites,” etc. This is also implied by the Rambam's words, unlike Magen Avraham. See Rashi there, s.v. “And even his wife,” etc.

**** HEBREW ****
<b>ויש כו'. </b>כברייתא דפליגא על רב יוסף לחומרא ואף ע"ג דאמורא יכול לשבש הברייתא דוקא אי שמיע ליה וכמ"ש בשבת ס"א א' וז"ש שם אטו כולהו בחדא כו':
**** ENGLISH ****
“And there are those,” etc. This follows the baraita that disagrees stringently with Rav Yosef. Although an Amora may reject the text of a baraita, that is only when he has received a tradition to that effect, as stated in Shabbat 61a. This is what the Gemara means there by saying, “Were they all taught together?”

**** HEBREW ****
<b>ס"ד התינוק כו'. </b>מדקאמר תינוקת בת כו' ול"ק עד י"א כו' מ' דוקא בת י"א שנה אז בעינן שדים כו' ולא פחות ויותר א"צ:
**** ENGLISH ****
Se'if 4: “The child,” etc. Since it says, “A girl who is,” etc., and does not simply say, “until eleven,” etc., it implies that specifically when she is eleven years old do we require the development of breasts, etc. Below that age, and above it, this is not required.
