**** ENGLISH ****
Until what age are they considered young? Until the boy is eleven years old. Meaning, before the girl has completed eleven years and the boy has completed twelve years. Once each has completed eleven and twelve years respectively, and entered the next year, then if they have developed two pubic hairs it is forbidden without an intervening barrier. Likewise, regarding the boy's thirteenth year, this means even on the first day after completion of his twelfth year. This appears to me from the implication of the Tur. Others explain that 'in the eleventh and twelfth year' means after they have already reached eleven and twelve years respectively. This is the view of Maharshal.
