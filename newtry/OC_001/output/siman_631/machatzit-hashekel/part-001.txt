**** ENGLISH ****
See siman 630:8 and Magen Avraham there, small paragraph 12, for we rule like Rava that we do not say that the edge of the roof descends and seals where the roof was not made for the sake of the sukkah, but for the sake of the portico that is inside. Nevertheless, a complete partition, and the same applies through <i>lavud</i>, which is called a complete partition for this matter, is considered a partition even if it was not made for the sake of the sukkah, for it is superior to the edge of the roof.

<b>(ס"ק ה) ונתן כו'</b> ה"ל הכשר מרובה רש"י. באמת לשון רש"י כן הוא. אך כתב כן לפי שטתו דלא הוצרך הש"ס לאוקימתא זו כ"א למ"ד פרוץ כעומד אסור וא"כ אם הניח בין שפוד לשפוד כמלא שפוד ונתן שם סכך כשר אכתי ה"ל פרוץ כעומד ולכך הוצרך לומר דוקא אם נתן הפסול שתי. נותן הכשר ערב. דאז ע"כ הכשר הרוב דאל"כ יפול סכך הכשר לארץ. וא"כ הוי עומד מרובה על הפרוץ וכשר. אבל לדידן דקי"ל כר"ת דס"ל דאפי' פרוץ כעומד מותר אפ"ה הוצרך לאוקמי רהפסול מונח שתי והכשר מונח ערב מטעם שכתב בש"ע שא"א לצמצם שימלא כל האויר כו' וא"כ אפי' ע"י נתינת שתי וערב לא הוי כ"א מחצה על מחצה אפ"ה כשר לדידן דקי"ל פרוץ כעומד מותר אלא מ"א האמת נקט דע"כ הכשר הרוב דאל"כ יפול כנ"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(Small paragraph 5) And he placed, etc.</b> It becomes a majority of valid s'chach; Rashi. In truth, Rashi's language is indeed so. But he wrote this according to his approach, that the Talmud needed this explanation only according to the one who holds that breached space equal to standing material is forbidden. If so, where he placed between each spit and spit a space the size of a spit and placed valid s'chach there, it would still be breached space equal to standing material. Therefore he had to say specifically that if he placed the invalid material lengthwise, he places the valid material crosswise, for then the valid material must be the majority; otherwise the valid s'chach would fall to the ground. If so, the standing material is greater than the breached space and it is valid. But according to us, who rule like Rabbeinu Tam that even breached space equal to standing material is permitted, nevertheless the Talmud still had to establish that the invalid material was placed lengthwise and the valid material crosswise for the reason written in Shulchan Aruch: that it is impossible to be exact and fill all the open space, etc. If so, even through placing lengthwise and crosswise it is only half and half; nevertheless it is valid according to us, for we rule that breached space equal to standing material is permitted. Rather, the Magen Avraham stated the truth: perforce the valid material is the majority, for otherwise it would fall, as above.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(Small paragraph 6) Plaster, etc.</b> And this requires analysis in practice. Certainly mere loosening does not help, as the Ran wrote on this Mishnah, for plaster of earth is invalid s'chach in itself, as written in siman 629:1 in the gloss. But by removing the plaster and loosening the boards, it can be validated, for he performed a significant act, and it is better than where there was no plaster and he validates it by mere loosening, which is a small act.
