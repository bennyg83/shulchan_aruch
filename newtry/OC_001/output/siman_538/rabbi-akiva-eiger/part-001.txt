<small>סעיף ו' </small>לא יקנסו בנו אחריו. אפשר דוקא בלא עשה אביו המלאכה דהדין שלא יעשה בזה ל"ק לברי' דמותר לעשותו אבל אם כבר עשה אביו דהדין דמפקירים אית' לכל כיון דנעשה הפקר אפשר דאם מת לא זכה בנו ולא עדיף מאחר כיון דכבר נעשה הפקר ולפמ"ש בכוונת לישנא דהמחבר אם הוא דבר האבד מוכרח דגם אם עשה אביו ל"ק בנו אחריו:
**** ENGLISH ****
Se'if 6. They should not fine his son after him. It is possible that this is specifically when his father did not yet do the melacha, for regarding the law that he should not do it, in this they do not fine the son, since he is permitted to do it; but if his father already did it, for which the law is that they declare it ownerless for everyone, since it has become hefker, it is possible that if [the father] dies the son does not acquire it, and he is no better than another person, since it has already become hefker. But according to what I wrote regarding the intent of the Mechaber's language "if it is a davar haaved," it necessarily follows that even if his father did it, they do not fine his son after him.
