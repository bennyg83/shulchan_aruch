**** HEBREW ****
במג"א ס"ק ב' וה"ה לבנו. נ"ב היינו לפירש"י במס' כתובות דף ל"ד ע"א דלר"מ נמי אסור לו לעולם וא"כ מדנקט ר"י עולמית ש"מ להוסיף דגם לבנו אסור:
**** ENGLISH ****
In Magen Avraham s.k. 2, "and the same applies to his son." Note: that is according to Rashi's explanation in tractate Kesubos folio 34a, that according to Rabbi Meir as well it is forever forbidden to him; and if so, since Rabbeinu Yeruham employed the wording "forever," it teaches to add that it is also forbidden to his son.
