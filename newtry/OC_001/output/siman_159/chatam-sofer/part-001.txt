**** ENGLISH ****
<small>In the same passage: </small>“And a wooden vessel.” Note: If it has iron supports, such as hoops and bands, its law is that of a metal vessel, as Rambam writes at the end of chapter 4 of Hilchos Keilim, following the Sages who disagree with Rabbi Nechemiah. See Shabbos 60a, where its measure is determined according to the work for which it is used.

<small>בא"ד </small>חצי עגולה לא. נ"ב והעיקר בזה דבכיור לא היה הדד גבוהה מהכיור וכ"כ בא"ר אבל שפופרת לעולם פסול וכן משמע בהרמב"ם פי"ח מהלכות כלים הלכה א' וכן הסילונות ע"ש ולכן אין ליטול משום ברזא אם יוצאין המים ע"י שפופרת או צינור רק מכרזא כעין שיוצאין המים מחביות עצמו:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>In the same passage: </small>“A semicircular one is not.” Note: The main point is that in the laver, the spout was not higher than the laver itself, as Eliyah Rabbah also writes. A tube, however, is always invalid. This is likewise implied by Rambam, chapter 18 of Hilchos Keilim, halachah 1, and by the law of pipes there. Therefore, one should not wash from a faucet when the water exits through a tube or pipe, but only from a faucet arranged so that the water exits directly from the barrel itself.
