**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The law of the blessing for the search for chametz. It contains two se'ifim.</b><br>Before he begins to search, he recites the blessing "…Who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us concerning the removal of chametz" {and if he began to search without the blessing, he recites the blessing as long as he has not finished his search (Kol Bo)}. And he should be careful not to speak between the blessing and the beginning of the search; and it is good not to speak of other matters until he completes the entire search, so that he set his heart to search in all the places into which chametz is brought.

**** ENGLISH ****
With one blessing, one can check several houses. And if the head of the household wishes, a member of his household should arise at the time he makes the blessing, and he should then spread out to check, each person searching his designated place, upon the conclusion of the blessing said by the head of the household. {Rama: Rema: It is our custom to leave small pieces of chametz in a place where the searcher will find them in order that his blessing not be in vain (Mahar"i; Ber"in). However, if one did not so [place bread] it does not hold back the need for the blessing because everyone has in mind to burn if he finds [bread]. (Kol Bo).}
