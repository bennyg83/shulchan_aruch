(4) (d) 27 who did not finish his inspection - a time when he did not finish a case still passes to his doing. And if he has already finished the inspection, he does not bless now but instead blesses for tomorrow during the burning of a daha, who blesses him for burning chametz, and if he canceled it yesterday during the inspection, MM must burn the correction of the sages and another daha, then he said that every hamira without its fronts and the chametz that he saw was not canceled The 21st blessing was fixed during the inspection and per their view he blesses during cremation without a name and a kingdom. And behold by law he may rely in checking even on women and slaves [and not on our foreign maidservant] and minors; nevertheless l'chatchila it is proper not to rely except on adult men who reached the age of mitzvot — that is, from thirteen years and onward — because proper checking involves great effort, and one should fear perhaps they will be lazy and not check well [Acharonim]:
