**** ENGLISH ****
The Laws of Accepting a Fast, Contains 13 Sections. Every fast that does not last until sunset - that is, it is not completed until Tzeit HaKochavim, meaning that one can see three medium-sized stars or the moon shines strongly and illuminates the earth (Hagahot Ashiri, Chapter 1 of Taanit and Hagahot Maimonides, Chapter 1) - is not considered a fast. However, a prayer leader should not say 'Aneinu' unless the fast is completed, and this is the common practice.}
