**** ENGLISH ****
(7) <b>And so is the view of some of the authorities</b>: their reason is that they hold that what the Gemara said, "any fast upon which the sun did not set is not a fast," and that is even regarding Aneinu, is only if he accepted upon himself to fast a full day and did not complete, so that it is not a fast, then he does not pray Aneinu; but if he accepted upon himself only until Minchah, for it is mere affliction, it is a fast for this purpose that he prays Aneinu within his prayer even though he does not complete. But most poskim disagree with this; therefore the Rama decided that we should not rely on them except regarding an individual, that he say it in Shomea Tefillah and conclude "for You hear," etc., and not say "Blessed are You, Hashem, Who answers in time of trouble." And see the Taz, who leans toward the view that even in this he should skip the words "the fast of our affliction," so that he not appear as one who speaks lies since it is not a fast; and in Sefer Bigdei Yesha he rules that he need not skip, since he fasts until after midday, for then his eating is like throwing a stone into a sack and he does not appear as a liar; see there; and so leans Sefer Machatzit HaShekel; see there.
