**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Two blessings.</b> That is to say, to exclude [the view of] the Mahariv, who wrote that he does not recite Shehecheyanu, as the Beis Yosef wrote in the name of that source (see there). And it does not appear to me that it is relevant to the Rambam’s words; for here he recites the blessing on behalf of the hearer; and so wrote the Kesef Mishneh, chapter 11 of Hilchos Berachos, in the name of the Ramach, law 10; and the Rambam derives [it] from one who makes a sukkah for himself, etc. (Sukkah 46a); and see there in Tosafos and the Rosh.
