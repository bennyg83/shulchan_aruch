**** ENGLISH ****
<b>In Magen Avraham, seif katan 2.</b> "For presumably it was surrounded, etc." That is to say, the Ran asked that regarding walled cities outside the Land, we should be concerned lest their settlement preceded their wall. He answered that in most cases they surround first. This is what was difficult for him: according to Maharam, the Ran's difficulty remains. To this Maharam answered that it is not called settled and afterward surrounded unless it is known that it was settled with the intention not to surround it. But where nothing is known, we say that it was settled with intent to surround it, and it is properly called surrounded and afterward settled. Therefore he wrote that Rama's view requires analysis, since in siman 401 it is explained that he agreed with the Mechaber, which is the view of Maharam. If so, he should have written here that where nothing is known, it was settled with intent to surround it.
