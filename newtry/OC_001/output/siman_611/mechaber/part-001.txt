**** ENGLISH ****
<b>That the night of Yom Kippur — its law is like its day. It contains two se'ifim.</b><br>Yom Kippur — its night is like its day for every matter. And what are the things forbidden on it? Melachah, eating, drinking, washing, anointing, wearing the sandal, and marital relations. And there is no liability of kares except for melachah, eating, and drinking.

**** ENGLISH ****
Every melachah for which one is liable on Shabbos, one is liable for it on Yom Kippur; and whatever on Shabbos is exempt but forbidden is likewise [exempt but forbidden] on Yom Kippur — except that on Shabbos its deliberate [violation] is [punishable] by stoning, and on Yom Kippur its deliberate [violation] is by kares. And whatever is forbidden to handle on Shabbos is forbidden to handle on Yom Kippur. And they permitted to trim vegetables and to crack nuts from Minchah-time onward when it falls on a weekday; but nowadays the custom is to forbid. {Rama: If a fire broke out on Yom Kippur, it is permitted to save one meal for the need of the night, just as one saves on Shabbos for the Minchah meal (Ran, chapter "Kol Kisvei"). And it has already been explained in siman 334 how we act nowadays regarding a fire on Shabbos, and the same applies on Yom Kippur. And the custom is that children play with nuts (Agudah; Maharil), and one should not protest against them, even before Minchah; and the custom evolved from the law of cracking nuts mentioned (his own opinion).}
