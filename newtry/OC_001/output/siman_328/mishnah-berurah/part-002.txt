(ב) And forbidden to extract blood — this is a matter unto itself — forbidden to press with his hands on the wound to extract blood; or when he wraps something on the wound [even in a matter containing no dyeing concern] forbidden to tighten so blood exits, for he is choveil and it is a primary melacha; and some hold likewise if he places on the wound something that draws moisture and blood [such as zinc ointment and the like] — also melacha d'oraisa since he intended for this; and in any case certainly prohibition.
