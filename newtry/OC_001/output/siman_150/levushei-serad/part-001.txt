**** HEBREW ****
<small>מג"א סק"ד</small> חזקה בחלונות. יש מקום לתרץ ע"פ מ"ש רמ"א בח"מ ססי' קמ"ט דאין מחזיקין בשל קהל וע"ש ואין להאריך ודו"ק:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham, seif katan 4.</small> A prescriptive right regarding windows. There is room to resolve this based on what Rama writes at the end of Choshen Mishpat 149, that one does not acquire a prescriptive right against communal property. See there. There is no need to elaborate; examine carefully.
