(א) מותר וכו' - היינו אף שאין לאדם להלך בשבת ממקום שביתתו רק אלפים וכמבואר לעיל בסימן שצ"ז התירו לו להלך ארבעה אלפים לצד אחד ע"י שמניח מזון שתי סעודות בסוף אלפים ממקום שהוא עומד ביה"ש ועי"ז חשבינן לו כאלו היתה שביתתו שם ויש לו להלך אלפים לכל רוח ממקום ההוא ומשום דהמחבר כתב לשון דיעבד הוסיף הרמ"א דגם לכתחלה מותר לערב ולקמן בסימן תט"ו נתבאר דלכתחלה דוקא לדבר מצוה ובדיעבד אף אם עירב לדבר הרשות עירובו עירוב:
**** ENGLISH ****
(1) Permitted, etc. — that is, even though a person may not walk on Shabbat from his place of shevitah except two thousand cubits, as explained above siman 397, they permitted him to walk four thousand on one side by placing food for two meals at the end of two thousand from where he stands on erev Shabbat; thereby he is considered as if his shevitah were there and he may walk two thousand in every direction from that place. And because the Mechaber wrote in language of b'dieved, the Rama added that even l'chatchila one may make eruv; and this is specifically for a mitzvah matter l'chatchila, but for a discretionary matter only b'dieved, as explained below siman 415 — see there.

All this when he slept in that city; but if he slept at his shevitah place and his two thousand ended in the middle of the city, or slept in this city and his measure ended in half another city — he may walk only until where his measure ended, all agree. By throwing he may carry throughout the city if it were private domain, as above siman 403 — see there (acharonim).
