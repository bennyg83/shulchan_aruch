<b>דין מי שנשבע שלא לאכול מצה. ובו סעיף אחד:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i>אמר שבועה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="א"></i>שלא אוכל <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="1"></i>מצה <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i>סתם <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Sha'arei Teshuvah" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Chok Yaakov" data-order="1"></i>אסור לאכול מצה <i data-commentator="Chok Yaakov" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ב"></i>בליל פסח אמר שבועה שלא אוכל מצה בליל פסח <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Chok Yaakov" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ג"></i>לוקה ואוכל מצה בליל פסח:
**** ENGLISH ****
The Law Regarding One Who Swore Not to Eat Matzah (One Section): If a person swore generally, saying, "I swear not to eat matzah," he is forbidden to eat matzah on the night of Passover. However, if he specifically swore, "I swear not to eat matzah on the night of Passover," he receives lashes for taking an invalid oath and is still obligated to eat matzah on the night of Passover.
