**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And it is forbidden to take large steps, etc.</b> This is surprising, for the Gemara there says that taking a large step is forbidden even on a weekday; see there. He overlooked the aggadic novellae there, that on a weekday it is only a danger, because it removes one five-hundredth of a person’s eyesight, but on Shabbos there is also a prohibition. Also there, it seems to me possible that what the Gemara concludes there, that it is restored to him by the kiddush of Shabbos evening, was the answer of Rabbi Yishmael to Rabbi, or the Talmud itself said it, that on a weekday there is a remedy, whereas on Shabbos the prohibition remains. In Tosafot in Taanis 10b, Tosafot explained that it removes only with the first step specifically, but from then on, once he has trodden, he has trodden. Therefore there is a difficulty: what did Rabbi Yishmael answer Rabbi in Shabbos 113, and is it permitted on a weekday, etc.? Perhaps Rabbi’s question was where he had already trodden. <b>More than a cubit, etc.</b> Meaning, there should be half a cubit between one foot and the other, and the sole of the foot is also half a cubit. The Agudah wrote that he should not lift the second foot before placing down the first, and so wrote the Raavan, siman 360.

In truth, above as well regarding a thread or cord it requires study, for one may say that the reason is that it does not save her so much from pain; but a band of rags is permitted so that the garments should not scratch, and the like. What was said above regarding a band for the plaster was stated as a novelty, that even over the plaster it is permitted. Although the Bach, seif katan 40, did not explain this way, I have written what appears in my humble opinion. So too it implies in Shiltei Giborim, page 120a, and also in Maharil in the name of the Rash. See what I wrote at the end of this siman.
