<b>Se'if 2. One who makes, etc.</b> And the Raavad wrote: and that is when there is a ke-zayis within the time of eating a pras. And in the laws of challah the Raavad wrote that we require that there be grain in the [required] measure, like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, for he ruled the halacha like him; however, we do not require a majority of grain. And that is to say, as is written in the Tosefta, chapter 2 of Challah: one who makes a dough from wheat and from rice: Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says it is not liable for challah until there is grain in it to the [required] measure, and a person does not fulfill his obligation with it on Pesach until there is in it to the [required] measure. And in the Yerushalmi there, chapter 3 halacha 6: the Mishnah is not like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, for Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says, etc. R' Yose bar Idi in the name of Resh Lakish: the law is like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. Said R' Hila: whether like the Rabbis here or like the Rabbis there, we say until its majority is grain. And the reason of grain: Rav Huna said, the taste of grain, even though its majority is not grain, etc.; see there. And he ruled like Rav Huna, for so they said in the Gemara of Zevachim 78a: said Resh Lakish, pigul, etc., we learn from this that taste imparted in a majority is not according to the Torah. And Rava answered: "one who makes," etc., say the end, etc.; rather, a species with what is not its species, etc. Hence, even though the majority is rice, and specifically when there is a ke-zayis within the time of eating a pras, as is written in Avodah Zarah 67a: said R' Yochanan, whatever has its taste and its substance, etc. And Rashi explained, s.v.
