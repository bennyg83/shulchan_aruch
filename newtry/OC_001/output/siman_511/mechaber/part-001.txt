(Beit Yosef in the name of a German responsum, and the custom is to be lenient)}

It is permitted to heat water on Yom Tov to wash one's hands, but not one's whole body — even if one does not wash it all at once. {Rama: Rema: However, it is permitted to bathe a baby in water heated by a Jew on Yom Tov. One may not make fumigation — meaning scattering spices over coals, whether to smell them or to fumigate a house or vessels. But if one does so in order to give a pleasant scent to fruits to improve them for eating, it is permitted even if one scatters them over a wood coal. {Rama: Rema: It is forbidden to invert a perfumed cup over garments, since this creates a scent in them. (HaMagid ch. 1 and Hagahot Asheiri, end of ch. 2 of Beitzah)}
