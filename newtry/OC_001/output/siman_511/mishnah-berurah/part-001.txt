{א} מותר לעשות מדורה - דאף דכתיב בתורה אך אשר יאכל לכל נפש הוא לבדו יעשה לכם קי"ל דמתוך שהותרה הבערה לצורך אוכל נפש הותר אף שלא לצורך אוכל נפש ובלבד שיהא שוה לכל נפש וצורך יו"ט וי"א דכל מידי דהנאת הגוף בכלל אך אשר יאכל לכל נפש הוא ורק שיהא שוה לכל נפש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(1) It is permitted to make a fire.</b> Although the Torah states, “Only that which may be eaten by every person, that alone may be prepared for you,” we rule that since kindling was permitted for food preparation, it was also permitted for purposes other than food preparation, provided that the benefit is common to all people and is needed on Yom Tov. Some say that every form of bodily benefit is included in “that which may be eaten by every person,” provided that it is common to all people.

**** ENGLISH ****

<b>(4) For sometimes the cold is not severe.</b> Meaning, when the cold is not severe enough that ordinary people are generally troubled by it. If Yom Tov happens to be very cold, as sometimes occurs on Pesach or Sukkos, all agree that it is permitted. Even when it is not cold enough to require a fire for warmth, if fatty foods that were cooked on the stove will congeal because of the cold, all agree that one may heat the winter room and place them there so that they do not congeal, since this is an actual food preparation need.
