<b>[Levush] And even from erev Yom Tov etc.</b> He was precise to write that they are accustomed [to forbid], but as a matter of law it appears to me that the main ruling is like the decision of the Beis Yosef, that even the whole body at once is permitted; for those who permit are Geonim, and the Rif, and the Rambam, and the Semag, and the Semak, and the Maggid, and the Ran; and further, I saw that Rabbeinu Yerucham, the student of the Rosh, inclines to permit against his teacher; and likewise in Shibolei HaLeket, folio 34, he brought all the views and concluded to permit; and likewise the Riaz in Shiltei Giborim. I also wonder at what the Beis Yosef wrote, that the Agur wrote in the name of a responsum of the Rashba to forbid: for I saw in Avodas HaKodesh of the Rashba, folio 43, that he permits explicitly; and I looked in the Agur itself, siman 680: the Rashba wrote that they were accustomed to forbid, and he did not wish to permit; end quote. And it appears that his own view is to permit, only that the question was in a place where they were accustomed to forbid, so he did not wish to permit them to change their custom; and in any case it is clear that the Rashba permits; and likewise in the Agur itself, in his conclusion, he brought the words of Shibolei HaLeket mentioned above to permit. And I further say that it is implied in the Ran, chapter Kirah, s.v. it was stated etc., and s.v. and to examine etc., that to wash limb by limb, according to everyone, is permitted in hot water heated from erev Yom Tov; and only with difficulty can one explain his words otherwise; examine carefully. And likewise in the Kol Bo, folio 60, he wrote: Shibolei HaLeket [says] in the Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Beitza, one may wash the whole body limb by limb; end quote. And likewise I saw in the Yerushalmi itself there and in chapter Kirah to permit. And now I will explain: the view of those who permit is specifically outside the bathhouse, but in the bathhouse, [not]; and even outside the bathhouse, the Ran wrote specifically to rinse, but not to wash. However, if they were heated etc., it is implied that even according to those who forbid above, it is permitted for a baby; and so it is clear in the Agudah; and so it is implied in the Mordechai, not like the Magen Avraham; and all the more so is it permitted in hot water heated from erev Yom Tov; and with this the Beis Yosef establishes it in the name of the Mordechai. And in Sefer Olas Shabbos he wrote that the Beis Yosef did not understand the words of the Mordechai, therefore it is difficult for him; end quote. But he erred, for what the Beis Yosef wrote, and this requires further study etc., is the language of the Mordechai himself, and the Beis Yosef raises no difficulty at all. And what the Mordechai wrote in the name of the Yerushalmi appears to me to deal with an adult and not with a baby; and even aside from this, the words of the Mordechai require further study.
