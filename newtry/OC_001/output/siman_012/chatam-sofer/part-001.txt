**** HEBREW ****
<small>במחבר סעיף א' </small>שמא נפסק. נ"ב שמעתי מקשים נימא כל דפריש מרובא פריש, ונ"ל דהרי אין אנו דנים על הנתקין שאינן לפנינו אלא על החוטין התלוין והם קבועים וכמחצה על מחצה דמי.
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Regarding the Mechaber, se'if 1: Perhaps it was severed.</b> I heard people ask why we do not apply the rule that anything separated is presumed to have separated from the majority. It appears to me that we are not judging the severed pieces, which are not before us, but the hanging strings. They are fixed in place, and anything fixed is treated as an equal doubt.
