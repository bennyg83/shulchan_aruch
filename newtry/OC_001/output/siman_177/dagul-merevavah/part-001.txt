**** HEBREW ****
פירש המליח צריך ברכה לפניו ולאחריו והפת טפל למליח כמבואר בסי' רי"ב והרבות' דלא תימא כיון שאוכל פת עם המליח הוה כמו שלא משכו ידיהם מן הפת ולא יוחשב אחר סעודה קמ"ל שהפת הזה טפל הוא ולכן מחשב המליח דבר הבא בסעודה שלא מחמת סעודה:
**** ENGLISH ****
The meaning is that the salted food requires a blessing before and after it, while the bread is subordinate to the salted food, as explained in siman 212. The novelty is that one might have thought that because he eats bread with the salted food, it is as though he had not withdrawn from bread and the food would not be considered to have come after the meal. Therefore it teaches that this bread is subordinate, and the salted food is considered something brought during the meal but not because of the meal.
