<small>שם</small> בהג"ה מיהו אם חל וכו'. נ"ב וע' מ"ש בשכנה"ג סי' זה ומכל מקום לא הקלתי ואסרתי בערב שבועות שחל ביום א' לגלח עש"ק שלפניו אע"ג דיש לחלק וק"ל וכן מילה ביום א' לא התרתי ביום ו' שלפניו שאינו דומה להנ"ל כיון שאינו אלא יו"ט שלו ולא לרוב הציבור ול"ג בעש"ק טוב לעשות נישואין בלילה מביום עש"ק ולבעול בליל שבת כנ"ל כוונת אלי' רבה.
**** ENGLISH ****
There. In the gloss: however if it falls, etc. Note: and see what is written in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah on this siman; and nevertheless I was not lenient, and I forbade on erev Shavuos that falls on Sunday to shave on the erev Shabbos before it, even though there is room to distinguish, and it is straightforward. And likewise milah on Sunday: I did not permit on the Friday before it, for it is not comparable to the above, since it is only his festival day and not for most of the public. And Lag BaOmer on erev Shabbos: it is better to make the marriage at night rather than by day on erev Shabbos, and to consummate on Shabbos night; so it appears to me is the intent of Eliyah Rabbah.

**** ENGLISH ****
In the Mechaber, se'if 3, in the gloss: on Lag BaOmer day. Note: see Responsa Chatam Sofer, Orach Chaim, siman 142 [there he brought the wording of the proclamation that was announced in his community, the holy congregation of Pressburg, in the name of the Gaon Moreinu HaRav R' David Meshulam, of blessed memory, and there it was said: since they practiced here to make marriages on Rosh Chodesh Iyar and Lag BaOmer, and they also make marriages during the three days of restriction, and so one should not do, for that is two contradictory things; therefore from now on it shall be the fixed statute to forbid marriages during the three days of restriction. And he wrote on this that what appears from these words is that he was particular only over this, that they practiced to marry at both times, and it is two things that contradict one another in one place, as is understood, for in a large community like the aforementioned, every year two marriages will occur at the two times; but it is possible in a small place, where marriages are scarce, that if a wedding occurs on Rosh Chodesh Iyar none will occur during the days of restriction, and if so there are not here two contradictory things in one place; and it is possible that there is not so much particularity; and see there carefully in all the words of the responsum].
