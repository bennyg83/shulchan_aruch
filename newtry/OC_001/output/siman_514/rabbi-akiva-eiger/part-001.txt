Seif 5: It is forbidden to light it; and regarding a lamp lit for a deceased for seven days, see Mishbetzos Zahav above, siman 132, at the end of the siman, that one should permit lighting through a gentile, and the lamp should be on Yom Tov in a room where they do not eat.
