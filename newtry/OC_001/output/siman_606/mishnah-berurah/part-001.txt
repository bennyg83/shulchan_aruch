(א) צריך לפייסו - דגם בזה עבר על איסור דאונאת דברים והנה אע"פ שגם בשאר ימות השנה מחוייב לפייס למי שפשע כנגדו מ"מ אם אין לו פנאי הוא ממתין לפייסו על יום אחר אבל בעיוה"כ מחוייב לתקן הכל כדי שיטהר מכל עונותיו כדכתיב כי ביום הזה יכפר עליכם מכל חטאתיכם וגו' וכ"ש אם יש בידו מן הגזל ואונאה וכל דבר הנוגע בממון יראה לתקן. [דזהו המקטרג הגדול על האדם כמו שאחז"ל סאה מלא עונות מי מקטרג גזל מקטרג בראש] ואם יש לחבירו בידו ממון שיש לו תביעה עליהם יודיענו אע"פ שחבירו לא ידע מזה כלל ועכ"פ יסדר לפני הרב ומ"ץ הענין בשלימות ובאמת בלא שקר ולשאול האיך להתנהג. כללו של דבר כל דבר שבממון לא יסמוך על הוראתו כי היצה"ר יש לו התירים הרבה [ח"א]:
**** ENGLISH ****
(1) He must appease him - For even with this he transgressed the prohibition of verbal wronging. Although during the rest of the year one is also obligated to appease one against whom he sinned, nevertheless, if he has no time he can wait to appease him on another day. But on the eve of Yom Kippur he is obligated to repair everything so that he be purified from all his sins, as it is written, "for on this day He shall atone for you from all your sins, etc." All the more so if he has robbery, fraud, or anything involving money in his hand, he should see to repair it. This is the great accuser against a person, as the Sages said: a se'ah full of sins, who accuses? Robbery accuses first. If his fellow has money in his possession over which he has a claim, he should inform him, even though his fellow knows nothing of it. In any event he should set out the matter fully and truthfully before the rabbi and halachic decisor, without falsehood, and ask how to conduct himself. The general rule: in every monetary matter he should not rely on his own ruling, for the evil inclination has many permissions (Chayei Adam).

At the time of asking forgiveness, he must specify what he sinned against his fellow, unless he knows his fellow would be embarrassed if he specified the sin; then he should not specify it. One who asks forgiveness from many people generally has not fulfilled this if he knows he did something specific to an individual.

(21) Because of a seminal emission - Therefore once is sufficient, and he also needs no confession. A woman who had relations within three days must clean herself with hot water before immersion, so that she not emit semen and return to the impurity of a seminal emission, unless it is close to her immersion or close to her period, for then women are commonly impregnated, and if she cleans herself there is concern for destruction of seed. So wrote the Magen Avraham. In Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger he questions this and leans toward saying that she should never clean herself. Some say the reason for immersion is repentance; according to this, one should immerse three times, and so wrote the Rokeach. See the Magen Avraham, that even according to the one who holds the reason for immersion is a seminal emission, nevertheless one must always immerse on the eve of Yom Kippur, even if he already immersed on the eve of Rosh Hashanah and did not see a seminal emission afterward.
