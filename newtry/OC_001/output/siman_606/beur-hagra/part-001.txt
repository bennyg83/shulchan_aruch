**** ENGLISH ****
<b>However, he should say, etc.</b> Tanchuma: if he went to appease him and he did not accept him, what should he do? Rabbi Yosi ben Dormaskis said: let this sign be handed to you for all days: whenever you have mercy on your fellow, there are those who have mercy on you; and if you do not have mercy on your fellow, there are not those who have mercy on you. What should he do? Rabbi Shmuel said: he brings ten people, makes one row, and says to them, there was a quarrel between me and my fellow, and I went to appease him, but he did not accept me. The Holy One, blessed be He, sees that he humbled himself and has mercy on him, etc. See Magen Avraham subparagraph 2. And if, etc., this follows Rashi's explanation there, that Rav is different, and even the Rif agrees to the law.
