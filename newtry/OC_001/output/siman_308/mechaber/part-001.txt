**** ENGLISH ****
Objects that may or may not be carried on Shabbat All implements are carried on Shabbat except for implements that are muktze [=set aside] because of monetary loss. For example, a knife used for the ritual slaughter of an animal; for the performance of a ritual circumcision; hairdressers’ scissors; or the knife used by scribes to sharpen their quills. Since one shows great care not to use those for any other purpose, it is forbidden to move them on Shabbat, even for the "purpose of its place" (=its place is needed), or for the "purpose of the object itself". This too is the law regarding hammers used for herbs and spices, since one shows great care not to soil it. {Rama: RAMA: And even those [special knives] stuck in with a sheath with other knives, it is forbidden to move it (Maharil). RAMA: And this is the law regarding implements set aside for merchandise, where one is strict regarding their use (Beit Yosef in the name of Maimonides ch. 5).}
