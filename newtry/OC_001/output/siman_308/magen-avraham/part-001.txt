**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And a shofar, etc.</b> It implies that tefillin, even not at all for the need of their use, are permitted to move, unlike sacred writings that are permitted to move even not for need at all; but tefillin are permitted and are not like a vessel whose function is for prohibition, for on Shabbos the sign is not needed; but if he wishes to leave them there is no prohibition, as Tosafot beginning ch. 1 of Yom Tov [Beit Yosef] wrote. And one might ask: in siman 31 he ruled like the Zohar that one who branches tefillin on Chol HaMoed is liable for death, all the more on Shabbos; and also Tosafot Shabbos daf 60 imply they hold it is forbidden to leave tefillin except they hold a vessel whose function is for prohibition may be sent, as siman 376, see there; otherwise according to them why do they not leave tefillin on Shabbos? And what the Bach inferred from "one may not go out in tefillin", from that alone that in the house it is permitted; but in Tosafot Menachot daf 36 they wrote that per the view that Shabbos is not the time for tefillin, even in the house it is forbidden; and so Mordechai laws of tefillin. One may resolve: since it is not the time for tefillin he is not obligated to handle them, and we fear lest he go out to reshut ha-rabbim; see Sanhedrin beginning daf 68. Nevertheless it is like a vessel whose function is for prohibition and forbidden to move except for the need of its use or its place, as Tosafot Shabbos daf 46. However in Shulchan Aruch ch. 6 of Shabbos he wrote in the name of the poskim that moving is permitted, see Shulchan Aruch ch. 18 and what is written siman 33.
