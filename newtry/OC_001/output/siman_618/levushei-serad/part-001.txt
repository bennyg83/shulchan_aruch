See there in siman 328 in the Magen Avraham in the name of the Radbaz: if a sick person says he needs a certain remedy and the physician says that remedy will harm him, we listen to the physician. That case is different, because regarding remedies the physician is more expert than the patient. This is not so regarding whether he needs to eat, where the sick person knows better, for the heart knows the bitterness of its soul.
