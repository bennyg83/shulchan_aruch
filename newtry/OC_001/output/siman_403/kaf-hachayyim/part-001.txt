**** HEBREW ****
<b>א) [סעיף א'] שבת בבקעה.  </b>ר"ל שקנה שם שביתה ואז יש לו אלפים אמה לכל רוח ממקום ששבת שם בביה"ש.
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1) [Se'if 1] One who established Shabbos residence in an open plain. </b>This means that he acquired his Shabbos residence there. He consequently has two thousand amos in every direction from the place where he established residence during bein hashmashos.
