<b>And they drink.</b> And in a place where drunkenness is common, they say Hallel and afterward eat and drink. And the same law applies if most of the congregation wish to complete [the fast]: they should say Hallel HaGadol before eating, and they begin from Shir HaMaalos, "those who stand in the house of Hashem," until the twenty six [verses of] "for His kindness is forever"; see Magen Avraham. And they conclude "We thank You for every drop," as is written in siman 221; and afterward each individual need not recite a blessing; see Magen Avraham.
