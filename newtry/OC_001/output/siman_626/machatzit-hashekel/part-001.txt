<b>ובאמת התו' לשיטתם כו'</b> והוא דבדף כ"ב ע"ב רמי מתני' אהדדי דתנן בפ"ק שצלתה מרובה מחמתה כשרה. משמע אבל אי חמתה וצלתה שוין פסולה. ובפ"ב תנן שחמתה מרובה מצלתה פסולה. משמע הא שוין כשרה. ומשני כאן מלמעלה כאן מלמטה. ופירש"י אם מלמעלה הסכך והאויר שוין פסולה. אבל למטה אם הצל וחמה שוין כשרה לר"ת מהכרח קושייתו פי' אם עומד למעלה ורואה שהסכך והאויר שוין כשרה. אבל אם עומד למטה ומביט למעלה ונראה לו שלמעלה הסכך והאויר שוין פסולה. לפי שהאויר שהוא דבר שאין בו ממש אין חוש הראיה תופס בו כ"כ כשמביט מלמטה למעלה. ונראה לו האויר יותר קטן ממה שהוא. משא"כ הסכך שיש בו ממש אינו מתקטן כ"כ ע"י ההבטה ממטה למעלה ולכן כשמביט ממטה למעלה אע"ג שנראה לו שהאויר וסכך שוין יוכל להיות שהאויר יותר מן הסכך ולכן פסול ומזה יצא המחלוקת דלר"ת אם עומד למעלה ורואה שהאויר והסכך שוין כשרה. ולרש"י כה"ג פסולה. והרא"ש הסכים לפירש"י ע"ש. וע"ז כתב מ"א ובאמת התו' לשטתם אזלי. ר"ל דס"ל דהיכי דהאויר וסכך שוין כשר ולכן הוכיחו שפיר מהאי דהמקרה סכתו בשפודין אי יש ריוח ביניהם כמותן כשרה. ולא אמרי דמצטרף סכך פסול. משום דהסכך גרידא הוא שיעור הכשר סכה דהא יש ריוח ביניהן כמותן. וא"כ אפי' שקלת לשפודין יהיה הכשר סכה בנותר. דהא מכסה בסכך כשר חצי הסכה אבל הרא"ש דס"ל כפירש"י דאם האויר וסכך שוין פסולה. וא"כ אי שקלת לשפודין יהי' האויר והסכך שווין דפסולה לפירש"י דהא עי"ז לפירש"י מיקרי חמתה מרובה מצלתה. ואפ"ה מכשירים ע"י שפודין אע"ג דמצטרף סכך פסול. א"כ ה"ה דבאילן הדין כן:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And in truth Tosafot follow their own approach, etc.</b> On folio 22b the Gemara sets Mishnayos against one another. In the first chapter we learned that where its shade is greater than its sun, it is valid; this implies that if its sun and shade are equal, it is invalid. In the second chapter we learned that if its sun is greater than its shade, it is invalid; this implies that if they are equal, it is valid. The Gemara answers: here above, here below. Rashi explained that if above, the s'chach and airspace are equal, it is invalid, but below, if the shade and sun are equal, it is valid. Rabbeinu Tam, because of his question, explained that if one stands above and sees that the s'chach and airspace are equal, it is valid. But if one stands below and looks upward, and it appears to him that above the s'chach and airspace are equal, it is invalid, because air, which has no substance, is not grasped so strongly by eyesight when looking from below upward, and the air appears smaller than it is. By contrast, s'chach, which has substance, is not reduced so much by looking from below upward. Therefore when one looks from below upward, although it appears to him that the air and s'chach are equal, it may be that the air is more than the s'chach, and it is therefore invalid. From this came the dispute: according to Rabbeinu Tam, if one stands above and sees that the air and s'chach are equal, it is valid; according to Rashi, in such a case it is invalid. The Rosh agreed with Rashi's explanation; see there. On this the Magen Avraham wrote: and in truth Tosafot follow their own approach. Meaning, they hold that where the air and s'chach are equal, it is valid. Therefore they properly proved from the case of one who roofs his sukkah with spits, where if there is space between them equal to them it is valid, and we do not say that invalid s'chach joins, because the s'chach alone is the valid measure of the sukkah, since there is space between them equal to them. If so, even if you remove the spits, the remaining valid sukkah measure is present, for half the sukkah is covered with valid s'chach. But the Rosh holds like Rashi, that if the air and s'chach are equal it is invalid. If so, if you remove the spits, the air and s'chach will be equal, which is invalid according to Rashi, for through this, according to Rashi, it is called having more sun than shade. Nevertheless, they validate by means of the spits, even though invalid s'chach joins. If so, the same law should apply to a tree.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And perhaps when it is placed above, it is worse.</b> Meaning, worse than where the valid and invalid s'chach are placed next to one another. When it is placed above, one can somewhat use the reasoning of the some say opinion brought in the Shulchan Aruch, that the upper invalid s'chach nullifies the lower. The Rosh learns by degrees: since we find regarding spits, where valid and invalid s'chach are placed next to one another, that it is valid even when the valid s'chach alone does not have the full measure, then regarding invalid s'chach above, which is worse, we go down a level, and it is at least proper to validate where the valid s'chach alone has the measure of a valid sukkah.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Nevertheless, it requires examination to reconcile the Rosh's wording, etc.</b> That the valid s'chach is sufficient. But according to the Rosh it is not sufficient with the valid s'chach alone, for he holds like Rashi, and here the air and s'chach would be equal, as above. However, one must understand what the Magen Avraham wrote: and in truth Tosafot follow their own approach, which implies that Tosafot's words sit well with him, since they hold that the valid measure of a sukkah exists when the air and s'chach are equal. Nevertheless, Tosafot's own question there on folio 8b, s.v. ha kamitztaref, remains difficult. They challenged what is taught there: one who makes his sukkah under a tree is as though he made it inside a house, and it is invalid. Rava said: they taught this only regarding a tree whose shade is greater than its sun, but if its sun is greater than its shade, it is valid. The Gemara asks: if its sun is greater than its shade, what of it? Invalid s'chach joins with valid s'chach. Rav Pappa answers: when he beat them down and mixed them. On this Tosafot wrote that it must be where the sukkah's shade is greater than its sun apart from the tree, and we do not say that it joins, even if he did not beat them down. They bring proof from spits, where if there is space, etc., as above. According to this, Tosafot have a difficulty: what did the Gemara ask, but it joins, etc.? Perhaps it speaks where the sukkah has more shade without the tree. These are their words. Now according to their approach, that where the air and s'chach are equal this is a valid sukkah measure, it is proven from the case of spits that wherever there is a valid measure in the valid s'chach, even if the invalid s'chach alone also has a valid measure, nevertheless it is valid, and we do not say it joins. For there, regarding spits, the invalid s'chach has a valid sukkah measure, since half the sukkah is covered with spits, as it teaches: if there is space between them equal to them. If so, if you remove the valid s'chach, the spits and airspace would be equal, and nevertheless the Mishnah teaches that it is valid, because the valid s'chach alone also has a valid sukkah measure. If so, what is difficult to Tosafot? The Gemara properly asks, but it joins, etc., for it necessarily speaks where the valid s'chach does not have a valid sukkah measure. If it did have a valid sukkah measure, then even if the tree's shade were greater, it would still be valid, as is proven from the case of spits. Maharsha asks similarly on folio 10 on Tosafot s.v. poreis. What Maharsha answered there is that Tosafot hold that if the valid s'chach came first, then even if the invalid s'chach has a valid sukkah measure, nevertheless since the valid s'chach alone also has a valid sukkah measure, it is valid. But the Mishnah of one who makes his sukkah beneath a tree implies that the tree came first, and in such a case Tosafot hold that if the tree alone has a valid sukkah measure, then even if the valid s'chach alone also has a valid sukkah measure, it is nevertheless invalid. Therefore their question is proper: what did the Gemara ask, but it joins, etc.; perhaps it speaks where the valid s'chach alone has a valid sukkah measure? See there. This answer suffices to resolve his question there. But from the case of spits it is proven that if the valid s'chach has a valid measure, even if the invalid also has a valid measure, and even if the invalid came first, nevertheless it is valid. For we learned: one who roofs his sukkah with spits, etc., if there is space, etc. This wording implies that he first began covering with spits and left spaces between them, and afterwards filled the airspace with valid s'chach; so Rashi explains there. If so, the question returns: what was difficult to Tosafot in the Gemara's question, but it joins, etc., as above? It is forced to say that the Magen Avraham holds like Maharsha's other answer, that if the tree's shade is greater, it is invalid because of sukkah beneath a sukkah; however, Maharsha himself already rejected this answer there. More plausibly, in my humble opinion, for Tosafot the Magen Avraham is comfortable with the reasoning he wrote regarding the Rosh, that perhaps when it is placed above, it is worse. Tosafot too hold that we go down one level: if the law when they lie next to one another is that where the valid s'chach has a valid measure it is valid even if the invalid also has a valid measure, then when the invalid s'chach is above the valid s'chach, it is enough to validate only when the valid s'chach has the measure and the invalid does not itself have a valid sukkah measure. But if the invalid too has a valid sukkah measure, it does not help that the valid s'chach also has the measure. If so, their question is proper. But regarding the Rosh, the Magen Avraham was not satisfied with this reasoning, because the Rosh's wording still remains difficult, as the Magen Avraham concludes. But according to Tosafot, the wording works well, because according to their approach, if the air and s'chach are equal, it is a valid sukkah measure. Therefore what Tosafot wrote, since if you remove it the valid s'chach is sufficient, is understandable; see there.

<b>דמוקי לה במעדיף.</b> דשם על מתני' דשפודין מסיק הש"ס לימא תהוי תיובתא לר"ה בריה דר"י דאתמר פרוץ כעומד. ר"פ אמר מותר ור"ה בר"י אמר אסור. וסכך פסול דין אויר ופרוץ יש לך לענין זה. ומשני אמר ליה ר"ה ברי' דר"י בנכנס ויוצא. ופירש"י שסכך כשר נכנס ויוצא בריוח וע"כ שהאויר יותר מן השפודין. אם כן הסכך כשר הרוב. ופריך והא אפשר לצמצם. כ"ה גרסת רש"י ופי' דס"ד דהא דמשני בנכנס ויוצא פי' על כרחו נכנס ויוצא דאי אפשר לצמצם שיהיו האויר ושפודים שוין. ולזה פריך והא אפשר לצמצם. ומשני במעדיף. ר"ל לא משום דא"א לצמצם. אלא דמיירי שמעדיף ומניח סכך כשר יותר מן השפודים והתו' הקשו על פירש"י וא' מן הקושיות דא"כ צ"ל דכל השקלא וטריא דוקא לר"ה ברי' דר"י. והא אנן קי"ל כר"פ דפרוץ כעומד מותר. א"כ דוחק שיהי השקלא וטריא שלא אליבא דהלכתא. ולכך גרסו והא א"א לצמצם והקושיא היא בין לר"פ ובין לר"ה ברי' דר"י. כיון דא"א לצמצם ולמלא כל הריוח בסכך כשר. ונשאר מענו אויר. והאי אויר מצטרף עם השפודים. וא"כ לר"ה ברי' דר"י דמוקי בנכנס ויוצא שיהי' הכשר הרוב השתא יהיו שוין. ולר"פ דלא מוקי לה בנכנס ויוצא דס"ל פרוץ כעומד מותר. השתא יהי' הפרוץ מרובה ע"י צירוף האויר. וע"ז משני לכ"ע במעדיף. וא"כ מכסה כל האויר וגם יש קצת מן הכשר על הפסול עכת"ד:

<b>For it establishes it as where he adds extra.</b> There, on the Mishnah of spits, the Gemara concludes: let this be a refutation of Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua, for it was stated regarding breached equal to standing: Rav Pappa says it is permitted, and Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua says it is forbidden. Invalid s'chach has the law of airspace and breach regarding this. It answers: Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua said to him, it is where it enters and exits. Rashi explains that the valid s'chach enters and exits in the space, so necessarily the airspace is greater than the spits, and the valid s'chach is therefore the majority. The Gemara asks: but it is possible to be exact. This is Rashi's text, and he explains that the initial thought was that the answer, where it enters and exits, meant that this is necessarily so because it is impossible to be exact that the airspace and spits should be equal. On this the Gemara asks: but it is possible to be exact. It answers: where he adds extra, meaning not because it is impossible to be exact, but because it speaks where he adds and places more valid s'chach than the spits. Tosafot challenged Rashi's explanation. One of the questions is that if so, the entire discussion must be specifically according to Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua, whereas we rule like Rav Pappa, that breached equal to standing is permitted. If so, it is forced that the discussion would not be according to the halacha. Therefore they read: but it is impossible to be exact, and the question applies both according to Rav Pappa and according to Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua. Since it is impossible to be exact and fill the whole space with valid s'chach, some airspace remains, and that airspace joins with the spits. If so, according to Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua, who establishes it as where it enters and exits so that the valid s'chach is the majority, now they will be equal. And according to Rav Pappa, who does not establish it as where it enters and exits because he holds that breached equal to standing is permitted, now the breach will be greater through the joining of the airspace. On this the Gemara answers according to everyone that it is where he adds extra, so that he covers all the airspace and also has some of the valid s'chach over the invalid. These are their words.

<b>והרא"ש הסכים לפי' התו'.</b> וא"כ לפי המסקנא דהסכך כשר יותר מן המחצה דהא מונח קצת על סכך הפסול. ולכן א"ש דאי שקלית ליה לפסול אכתי צלתה מרובה מחמתה ע"י הכשר אלא דאכתי קשה על המקשן דלא אסיק דמיירי במעדיף. אדקשיא ליה והא א"א לצמצם ונשאר קצת אויר כנ"ל טפי ה"ל להקשות אפי' אפשר לצמצם וימלא כל האויר ע"י סכך כשר. מ"מ הוי מחצה על מחצה וא"כ אי שקלית לפסול לא יהי' בכשר לבד שיעור הכשר סכה לדעת הרא"ש דס"ל כרש"י כנ"ל. וא"כ ה"ל להקשות והא קמצטרף סכך פסול בהדי סכך כשר. ע"ז כתב מ"א שיעיין המעיין במ"מ פ"ה מהל' סכה. שתמה על הרמב"ם לפי גרסתו שהיה גור' כגרסת רש"י והא אפשר לצמצם והקושיא אינה כ"א לר"ה ברי' דר"י. וא"כ לר"פ דקי"ל כוותי' לא איצטרך לאוקימתא דמעדיף. לכן תמה על הרמב"ם שהעתיק אוקימתא דמעדיף. ותי' דגס לר"פ צ"ל דמיירי במעדיף. דאל"כ כיון דאי שקלת לפסול יהי' האויר וסכך שוין ובכה"ג פסול:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And the Rosh agreed with Tosafot's explanation.</b> If so, according to the conclusion, the valid s'chach is more than half, for some of it lies on top of the invalid s'chach. Therefore it is understandable that if you remove the invalid, its shade is still greater than its sun through the valid material. But it remains difficult regarding the questioner, who had not concluded that it speaks of adding extra. Instead of asking, but it is impossible to be exact and some airspace remains, as above, he should have asked a stronger question: even if it is possible to be exact and he fills the whole airspace with valid s'chach, nevertheless it is half and half. If so, if you remove the invalid, the valid s'chach alone will not have the measure of a valid sukkah according to the Rosh, who holds like Rashi, as above. Thus he should have asked: but invalid s'chach joins with valid s'chach. On this the Magen Avraham wrote that the reader should examine the Maggid Mishneh, chapter 5 of Hilchos Sukkah. He wondered about the Rambam according to his text, which was like Rashi's text, but it is possible to be exact, where the question is only against Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua. If so, according to Rav Pappa, whom we rule like, there should be no need for the establishment of adding extra. Therefore he wondered about the Rambam for copying the establishment of adding extra. He answered that even according to Rav Pappa one must say it speaks of adding extra, for otherwise, since if you remove the invalid, the airspace and s'chach will be equal, in such a case it is invalid.

<b>(ס"ק ג) ומבטלן כו'</b> והר"ן כ' כו' כונתו דגם הר"ן ס"ל כדעת י"א בש"ע וכמבואר שם וכוונות מ"א דלא תימא דגם לדעת י"א שכתב רמ"א מיהו אם השפילם כו' וערבם כו' והסכה כשרה. דגם קשיא הא אין מבטלים כו' ולא תימא דגם לי"א צ"ל כאחד מן התירוצים שכ' הב"ח. ע"ז כ' מ"א דהר"ן כ' כו' ומזה יש לשטתו תי' אחר והיינו דהר"ן מפרש מדקתני העושה סכתו תחת האילן כו' מדקראו בשם סכה משמע שיש בכשר לבד כשיעור והיינו כדעת י"א. וע"ז קאי רבא ואמר כשהאילן חמתו מרובה כשרה וע"ז קשיא ל"ל שהסכה הרוב סכך כשר. הא קי"ל כר"פ דאפי' פלגא ופלגא כשר וע"ז תי' תיפוק ליה דבלא"ה קשה מאי מהני חבטן וערבן. הא קי"ל היכי דיכול להסיר האיסור לא בטל. ואפי' מיירי כשאין האיסור ניכר ע"י התערובת. מ"מ הא יוכל לברר הפסול מתוך הכשר ומה"ט קי"ל בי"ד סי' צ"ח סעיף ד' דאם נפל חלב לתבשיל אע"ג שנתבטל צריך ליתן שם מים צוננים כדי שיקרש ויקפיא החלב ויסירנו כ"ש לפי מ"ש מ"א בסעיף קטן ד' דלהר"ן לא בעינן שלא יהי' ניכר דקשה טפי. וע"ז תי' הר"ן מ"מ הכא אגמרי' כו' שהן מעורבין בעי רובא כנ"ל ר"ל דאין דעתינו לבטל האיסור. אלא כיון דסכך כשר לבד יש בו שיעור הכשר אפי' בפלגא ופלגא. אלא ע"י שנתערב נתמעט חשיבות הכשר. אע"ג דגם חשיבות האיסור נתמעט מה בכך עכ"פ תו ליכא בכשר שיעור הכשר וע"י שהוא רוב אף שנתמעט חשיבותו ע"י התערובת. מ"מ האי רוב נחשב עכ"פ כאלו הוא פלגא ופלגא ובשר. ואין רצונינו לבטל האיסור. כי לא צריכים הפסול להכשר סוכה. כ"א למעט חשיבותו ע"י התערובות שלא יהי' בכחו לפסול הכשר. ולכך אע"ג שאפשר להסיר הפסול א"צ להסירו דהא א"צ כלל להפסול. וכן לא קשיא לפי זה הא אין מבטלים כו' דהא אין דעתינו לבטלו כנ"ל. וממילא תבין דכ"ז ניחא להר"ן דס"ל כהי"א ומיירי שיש בכשר לבד שיעור הכשר סכה וכמ"ש מ"א בס"ק ד' אין זה מבטל איסור. דהא אין מצטרפים את הפסול. אבל לדעה קמייתא דאפי' אין בכשר לבד כשיעור אלא ע"י צירוף הפסול אפ"ה כשר ע"י התערובות. ומיקרי מבטל איסור וצ"ל כתירוצו דהמרדכי הנ"ל. ולקושית הר"ן הא יוכל להסיר האיסור באמת צ"ב לדעה קמייתא:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(S.k. 3) And nullify them, etc.</b> And the Ran wrote, etc. His intent is that the Ran too holds like the some say view in the Shulchan Aruch, as is explained there. The Magen Avraham's intent is that you should not say that according to the some say view, where the Rema wrote, however if he lowered them, etc., and mixed them, etc., the sukkah is valid, there is also the difficulty that one may not nullify, etc.; and you should not say that according to the some say view too we must use one of the answers written by the Bach. On this the Magen Avraham wrote that the Ran wrote, etc., and from this, according to his approach, there is another answer. The Ran explains that since the Mishnah teaches, one who makes his sukkah beneath a tree, etc., and calls it a sukkah, it implies that the valid s'chach alone has the necessary measure, which is the view of the some say opinion. Regarding this Rava says that when the tree's sun is greater, it is valid. The difficulty is: why do we need the sukkah to have a majority of valid s'chach? We rule like Rav Pappa, that even half and half is valid. On this he answered: aside from that, there is another difficulty: what does beating down and mixing help, since we rule that where one can remove the prohibition, it is not nullified? Even if it speaks where the forbidden item is not recognizable because of the mixture, nevertheless he can sort the invalid from the valid. For this reason we rule in Yoreh De'ah 98:4 that if fat fell into a cooked dish, although it was nullified, he must place cold water there so the fat congeals and hardens, and remove it. This is all the more difficult according to what the Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 4, that according to the Ran we do not require that it be unrecognizable. On this the Ran answered: nevertheless, here this is what we were taught, etc., that since they are mixed, a majority is needed, as above. Meaning, our intent is not to nullify the prohibition. Rather, since the valid s'chach alone has the measure of a valid sukkah even with half and half, only because it became mixed its importance was diminished; although the importance of the prohibition was also diminished, what of it? In any event the valid s'chach no longer has a valid measure. Through its being a majority, even though its importance was diminished by the mixture, this majority is at least considered like half and half and is valid. We do not want to nullify the prohibition, for we do not need the invalid material to validate the sukkah, only to diminish its importance through the mixture so that it should not have the power to invalidate the valid s'chach. Therefore even though one can remove the invalid material, he need not remove it, for he does not need the invalid material at all. According to this, there is likewise no difficulty from the rule that one may not nullify, etc., for we do not intend to nullify it, as above. You will automatically understand that all this works well according to the Ran, who holds like the some say opinion and speaks where the valid s'chach alone has the measure of a valid sukkah, and as the Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 4, this is not nullifying a prohibition, for the invalid material is not combined. But according to the first view, where even if the valid alone does not have the measure, but only through joining the invalid, nevertheless it is valid through mixture, it is called nullifying a prohibition, and one must use the Mordechai's answer cited above. As for the Ran's difficulty that one can remove the prohibition, this indeed requires explanation according to the first view.
