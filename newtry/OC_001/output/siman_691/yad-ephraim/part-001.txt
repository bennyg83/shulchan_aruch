**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif katan 5.</b> It should read: in Responsa Maharam Mintz, and so on. See the Pri Chadash, who wrote that if he wrote with his left hand, it should be validated after the fact. See Sefer HaTerumah, brought by the Beis Yosef in siman 32, who wrote as follows: In this manner one must write a sefer Torah, tefillin, mezuzos, and Megillah, for the term “writing” is stated regarding all of them. See there. I saw that the Pri Megadim here wrote that the reason it is valid after the fact is that there are opinions whether it is equal to a sefer Torah in all respects. In my humble opinion, according to the reason that “writing” is stated regarding it, it should be invalid like a sefer Torah. See there, for he brings proof that it is not reasonable that one could write them on Shabbos and be exempt. See Beis Shmuel, Even HaEzer, siman 153, seif katan 4, and see what I wrote about this in Shaarei Teshuvah. See there regarding the law of a woman who wrote a Megillah, whether it is valid. I brought there from the Birkei Yosef and Machazik Berachah that his view is that it is valid. In Sefer Teshuvah MeAhavah, he brought that the famous Gaon, our teacher Rav David Oppenheim, of blessed memory, asked a question about a Megillah written by his famous daughter, Mrs. Sarah, wife of the famous Gaon Rav Chaim Yonah, of blessed memory, whether it is permitted to read from this Megillah. Rav Maharam Perles wrote that it is invalid, and afterward he mentioned the words of the Birkei Yosef and Machazik Berachah; see there. I have abbreviated.
