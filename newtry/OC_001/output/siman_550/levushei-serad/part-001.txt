<small>ט"ז סק"א</small> מה מקשי התוס' ר"ל כיון דהש"ס מביא מעשה זו ללמוד היתר רחיצה לק"מ קושיית התוס' דקמ"ל דרצה להתענות אפ"ה רחץ ש"מ דרחיצה מותר בשלמא אם רחיצה אסור באמת אם כן רבי שרחץ קמ"ל דלא רצה בתענית כלל אז שפיר הקשה התוס' מאי אריא לרחוץ וק"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Taz, s.k. 1.</b> What do Tosafos ask? That is to say: since the Gemara brings this incident to teach a permission of washing, the difficulty of Tosafos is not difficult at all, for it teaches us that he wished to fast and even so he washed; from this we infer that washing is permitted. Fair enough if washing were truly forbidden: then Rabbi, who washed, would teach us that he did not wish any fast at all; then Tosafos would rightly ask, why specifically mention washing? And this is easy.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>There.</b> In all these matters: since at that time washing and eating were equal in the days of Rabbi, and upon this they answered that it was Rabbi who innovated this law as we practice, that eating is forbidden and washing is permitted.
