(11) At Shacharis and Minchah, Vayechal: and likewise the sheliach tzibbur says Aneinu between Go'el and Rofei; but they do not say viduyim and nefilat apayim at Minchah, because it is erev Shabbos. And for this reason one should not make Yom Kippur Katan on erev Shabbos if Rosh Chodesh is on Shabbos, but rather on the Thursday before it, because one may not say Selichos and also viduyim on erev Shabbos at Minchah.

**** ENGLISH ****
(12) And afterward they make the chuppah: and then the groom is forbidden to drink from the cup of blessing, since it is still daytime; rather they give it to a child to drink. And when it is postponed, the groom may drink from the cup of blessing, and likewise eat and drink afterward; and see below in siman 562, se'if 2, that if the fast is only because of himself, that he fasted on this day, he need not complete it. And likewise when one of the fasts falls within the seven days of the wedding feast, he should complete his fast; but a yahrzeit need not be fasted within the seven days of the wedding feast if he married a virgin, or a bachelor married a widow.
