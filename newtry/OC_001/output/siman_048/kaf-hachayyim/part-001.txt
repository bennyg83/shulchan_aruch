<b>1) 'Our God</b> and God of our fathers, remember us with a favorable remembrance before You and recall us with a recollection of salvation,' etc. The dalet of 'ufekudas' should have a dagesh. Afterward, one should recite the passage of the Akeidah, as stated in Shaar HaKavanos, page 1b. 'Master of the universe, just as Avraham Avinu suppressed his compassion,' etc.; 'with the attribute of kindness and the attribute of mercy, and go beyond the strict letter of the law for us; in Your great goodness may Your burning anger subside,' etc.; 'Hashem your God will bring you,' etc.; 'He will benefit you and multiply you more than your fathers'; 'It is stated through Your prophet: Hashem, favor us; we have hoped to You,' etc., 'at a time of distress'; 'It is a time of distress for Yaakov,' etc.; and 'In all their distress, He was distressed,' etc. Rashash's siddur also includes the verses from 'Who is a God like You?' through 'which You swore to our fathers from days of old,' and the verse 'I shall bring them to My holy mountain,' etc. In 'These are the matters that a person performs and enjoys their fruits in this world,' one should say 'mipeiroseihem' with an added mem. The text is: 'Sanctify Your Name in Your world through the people who sanctify Your Name; through Your salvation, our King, raise and exalt our pride and save us soon for the sake of Your Name. Blessed is He Who sanctifies His Name publicly. Our Father in heaven, living and enduring, perform kindness with us,' etc.

<b>1) 'You are Hashem our God, before Whom our fathers burned incense,' etc.; 'The Rabbis taught: The compounding of the incense,' etc.</b> He would not add the verse 'The incense that you make according to its formula,' as some people customarily do. Say, 'He takes from them his full handfuls,' etc. 'Shiboles nerd' is said together, because the two words designate one substance, not two spices. Say, 'kelufah, three.' In 'yayin Kafrisin,' the first yod has a tzere, not a patach, because the word is in construct with 'Kafrisin.' Say, 'Sodomite salt, one quarter'; one need not add 'one quarter-kav,' because an unspecified quarter in Mishnah and Baraisa means one quarter of a kav. Say, 'kipas HaYarden, any amount,' with 'shehi,' not 'shehu.' One should say 'kipas,' whose letters form 'katef,' and not 'mikipas.' Mishnas Chassidim, Tefillas HaAsiyah 6:9; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 62c; and Rashash's siddur. Say: 'Why is Carshina lye brought? To rub the tziporen with it so that it will be beautiful. Why is Cyprus wine brought? To soak the tziporen in it so that it will be strong. Is urine not suitable for it?' etc.; 'It was taught: Rabbi Nasan says,' etc.; 'Bar Kappara taught,' etc.; 'because sound is beneficial for the spices.' The beis of 'labesamim' has a sheva, not a kamatz as some pronounce it. Our Sages explicitly explain in Kerisos 6b that the breath of sound issuing from a person's mouth while he pounds the spices in a mortar causes their fragrance to diffuse and become aromatic. Say, 'For no leaven and no honey may you burn as a fire-offering to Hashem.' Here one should recite the three verses: 'Hashem, Master of Legions, is with us,' etc.; 'Hashem, Master of Legions, fortunate is the person,' etc.; and 'Hashem, save; may the King answer us,' etc. Each time Pittum HaKetores is recited, these three verses must follow it. Afterward one should say 'Then the offering of Yehudah,' etc. No other verse or prayer should be recited at all, including the prayer printed in some siddurim after Aleinu: 'Master of the worlds, it is revealed and known before You that the attribute of judgment is extended,' etc. Even during an epidemic, may the Merciful One spare us, one should recite neither that prayer nor any other verse customarily said after Pittum HaKetores, such as 'Their father Yisrael said to them,' etc. See Shaar HaKavanos, end of Derushei Tefillas HaShachar, page 14. On Shabbos he did not customarily recite the verse 'And on the Shabbos day, two lambs,' etc., as Sephardim do, because these verses concern the Shabbos Mussaf offering and their time does not arrive until after Shacharis has ended. Say, 'Abayei mesader,' etc. Yoma 33 and Tur do not contain the word 'havah,' but read 'Abayei mesader,' and this is the proper wording, so that the yod of 'Abayei' does not join the word 'havah,' causing one to pronounce the Divine Name as written. Some carefully place 'havah' after 'mesader' for this reason, saying, 'Abayei mesader havah,' etc.; this too is correct. Moreover, the initials of 'mesader havah' have the numerical value 45, corresponding to the Divine Name of 45, while the initials of 'seder hama'arachah' have the value 65, corresponding to the Divine Name Adnus. Upon reaching 'and the incense precedes the limbs,' etc., from that point onward one need not repeat the word 'precedes.' One may say simply, 'the incense, the limbs; the limbs, the meal-offering,' etc., through the bowls, which are last. Then one must say, 'and the bowls precede the afternoon Tamid,' etc.; 'upon it complete all the offerings.' Do not say 'he completed.'

<b>1) Know one principle:</b> from the beginning of prayer through Baruch She'amar is the world of Asiyah. From Baruch She'amar through Yotzer Or is the world of Yetzirah. From Yotzer Or through the Amidah is the world of Beriah. The Amidah is in the world of Atzilus. From Ashrei through Tefillah LeDavid, it returns to the world of Beriah; from there through Ein Kadosh KaHashem, it returns to Yetzirah; and from there through Aleinu, it is the world of Asiyah. The intention is that one must first elevate the worlds from below to above, connecting each lower world with the world above it until, during the standing Amidah, all the worlds are joined together in Atzilus. The supernal union then occurs there. Afterward, the supernal influence drawn from that union must be received and brought downward into the lower worlds, from above to below. Therefore, when reciting from Ashrei through Tefillah LeDavid, we bring the influence down from Atzilus to Beriah; the intention is not to lower the world of Beriah itself. From Tefillah LeDavid through Ein Kadosh KaHashem, etc., we bring the influence down from Beriah to Yetzirah. From Ein Kadosh KaHashem, 'for there is none besides You,' through Aleinu, we bring the influence from Yetzirah to Asiyah. At Aleinu, the aforementioned worlds then descend, each to its place. Kaddish was therefore instituted after each transition from one world to another. Only between redemption and the Amidah, although they belong to two worlds (Beriah and Atzilus) was Kaddish not instituted, for the reason explained in Shaar HaKavanos, Derush HaKaddish, at the end of page 14d. All this is explained in Shaar HaKavanos, derush 1 of Tefillas HaShachar, pages 11d-12a, and in Derushei HaKaddish; Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaTefillah, chapter 6, and Shaar HaKaddish, chapter 1; and many other places. See also what was stated above in siman 25:27. There are further matters, but this is not the place to explain them. I have stated only the beginnings of these matters so that one may know the exalted greatness of prayer, which is among the matters that stand at the height of the world, as our Sages state in Berachos 6b. Although we do not possess this knowledge, a person should do what lies within his power. At the least, he should understand the meaning of the words, particularly when mentioning Divine Names, and omit no word or letter. Hashem, Who knows hidden things and examines hearts, will in His abundant mercy and kindness join good intention to deed.
