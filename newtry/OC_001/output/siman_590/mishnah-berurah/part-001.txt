(24) <b>Only for one tekiah.</b> That is, for the last one, because every tekiah must have a beginning and an end. If this tekiah is divided into two tekios, as the blower intended, then the last tekiah has no beginning and the first tekiah has no end. Therefore we do not follow his intention in this at all; rather, we consider this tekiah one long tekiah, and it counts for him as the last one.

**** ENGLISH ****
(26) <b>One tekiah between two orders.</b> That is to say, he blew tashrat, then shevarim-teruah, and stipulated regarding the final tekiah of tashrat: if this order is the correct one, let the tekiah count for it; and if the order of tashat is the correct one, let the tekiah count for it. For according to Torah law one order is sufficient, only because we do not know which order is correct we blow them all, as above at the beginning of the siman. Therefore after the fact he fulfills through his condition. Nevertheless, ab initio Chazal did not want to institute that he blow only tashrat, shevarim-teruah, teruah-tekiah, so that people not err and say that only shevarim-teruah or teruah-tekiah is needed.

(27) <b>Another shever.</b> This is sufficient, for he did not lose his order, since the teruah also now belongs to the shevarim, as both are the teruah in tashrat; therefore we consider it only like one who stumbled in the shevarim, which does not cause him to lose the shevarim thereby. There are poskim who hold that even if it was in one breath, he must also go back and blow three shevarim and complete the order, since in any event he interrupted in the middle of the shevarim. It is proper ab initio to be concerned and stringent like their words.

(כח) שגמר התרועה - פי' שעשה ג' כחות דבזה יצא ידי תרועה לדעה ראשונה הנ"ל בס"ג. וטעם החילוק בין גמר ללא גמר הוא דכ"ז שלא נגמר התרועה הוי קול בעלמא ונראה שעדיין עסוק בהשברים שגם התרועה לא גמר וחוזר להשלים השברים משא"כ בגמר התרועה שהוא קול שלם נראה שמתחיל אח"כ שברים אחר ולכך אינו מועיל בהשלמת שבר אחד אלא ג' שברים מחדש ומ"מ התקיעה הראשונה לא הפסיד גם בזה שלא הפסיק בקול אחר שאינו מענינא דשניהם הם מענין תרועה אלא שלא עשה כתיקונו:
**** ENGLISH ****
(28) <b>He completed the teruah.</b> Meaning, he made three forces, with which he fulfilled the teruah according to the first opinion cited above in seif 3. The reason for the distinction between completing and not completing is that as long as the teruah was not completed, it is a mere sound and it appears that he is still engaged in the shevarim, for he also did not finish the teruah, and he goes back to complete the shevarim. This is unlike where he completed the teruah, for it is a complete sound and it appears that afterward he is beginning other shevarim; therefore completing one shever does not help, but he must make three new shevarim. Nevertheless, he has not lost the first tekiah even in this case, for he did not interrupt with another sound that is not related to the matter, since both are part of teruah, only he did not make it properly.

(כט) אירע בתש"ת - היינו שהתחיל בשברים [שבר אחד או שתים] ואח"כ עשה תרועה לא אמרינן דהוא בכלל נתקל וכ"ש אם עשה כל השברים ואח"כ עשה תרועה כיון שהוא שלא מענינא קלקל בזה כל הסדר [וה"ה אם עשה התרועה קודם השברים ואין חילוק בכל זה בין שגמר התרועה או שלא גמר בכל ענין הפסיד התקיעה ראשונה] וכן מש"כ או תר"ת ר"ל שהתחיל בתרועה ובאמצע הפסיק והתחיל לעשות שבר אחד והכל כנ"ל לענין תש"ת וה"ה לענין תר"ת דכיון שאין זה שייך כלל לתרועה שבסדר זה הפסיד גם גם תקיעה ראשונה:
**** ENGLISH ****
(29) <b>It happened in tashat.</b> That is, he began with shevarim, one or two shevarim, and afterward made a teruah. We do not say that this is included in stumbling; all the more so if he made all the shevarim and afterward made a teruah, since it is not relevant to the matter, he spoiled the entire order. The same applies if he made the teruah before the shevarim; there is no distinction in all this whether he completed the teruah or did not complete it, for in every case he lost the first tekiah. Likewise, what he wrote, 'or tarat,' means that he began with a teruah and in the middle interrupted and began to make one shever; everything is as above regarding tashat, and the same applies regarding tarat, for since this does not belong at all to the teruah of this order, he also lost the first tekiah.

(לה) תקיעה ראשונה - ואם כבר תקע גם תקיעה אחרונה אותה תקיעה עולה לו במקום תקיעה ראשונה וגומר משם ואילך על הסדר. ואם לא נזכר עד שעומד בסימן אחר כגון שעומד בתש"ת ונזכר שטעה בתשר"ת גומר כל הסימן ואח"כ חוזר ותוקע תשר"ת פ"א ואע"ג שהפסיק באמצע הסימן דתשר"ת בקולות אחרות אין בכך כלום כיון שבכל בבא בפ"ע לא היה הפסק בקולות אחרות ודע עוד דבכל זה אפילו תקע בתקיעות דמיושב צריך לחזור ולא נאמר דנסמוך על תקיעות דמעומד דכיון שבירך על אלו צריך לעשותן כהוגן וגם בתקיעות דמעומד צריך לחזור דהם עיקר אבל אם טעה בתקיעות שאחר התפלה אין בכך כלום [מ"א וש"א]:
**** ENGLISH ****
(35) <b>The first tekiah.</b> If he already blew the final tekiah as well, that tekiah counts for him in place of the first tekiah, and he completes from there onward in order. If he did not remember until he was standing in another set, for example he was standing in tashat and remembered that he erred in tashrat, he completes the entire set and afterward goes back and blows tashrat once. Although he interrupted in the middle of the tashrat set with other sounds, this is nothing, since in each unit by itself there was no interruption with other sounds. Know further that in all this, even if he blew in the sitting blasts, he must repeat; we do not say that we should rely on the standing blasts, for since he recited the blessing over these, he must perform them properly. Also in the standing blasts he must repeat, for they are primary. But if he erred in the blasts after the prayer, it is nothing [Magen Avraham and other authorities].
