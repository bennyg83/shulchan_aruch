**** ENGLISH ****
<b>There.</b> 'And the groans would then differ between the first opinion and the latter opinion,' etc. Many have had difficulty with this, for it is explained in the Talmud that groaning is longer than wailing. If so, it is obvious that according to the latter opinion, where wailing is three forces, the measure of groaning is automatically greater than this. <b>It seems to me.</b> The Taz explains that certainly there is a distinction between the first opinion and the latter opinion regarding the sound of wailing and what its measure is: one says three forces, and one says one. Automatically this applies regarding groaning as well, for this is not reasonable; if so, the poskim should have explained that they also disagree regarding groans. Rather, the truth is that the essence of the dispute is this: the measure of the groaning sound is certainly known, for example, as long as two menitin, while the short sound, meaning one force, is as long as only one menut. They disagree only regarding wailing: one master holds that the way of one who wails is to wail with one short sound alone, while the latter opinion holds that the way of one who wails is to cry out three short forces close to one another. If so, the entire wail is three menitin long, but nevertheless it has breaks of sounds, each sound containing only one force. Therefore we indeed make in the teruah nine tromitin, meaning short sounds separated from one another. But regarding the measure of a groan, they agree that it is only the measure of two menitin, as according to the first opinion, except that it is one sound without a break in the middle, and it is longer than one short sound among the three sounds that the wailer makes. According to this, with the tashat blast, where we follow groaning, there is no distinction between the opinions, for the measure of the tekiah is like three groans, which is six menitin, and this does not depend on the dispute whether the yevavah is one short sound or three short sounds. See the Tur in the name of Baal HaTerumos and the words of the Beis Yosef there; examine it and you will find it.
