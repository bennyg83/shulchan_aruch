(ג) והאידנא וכו' - שהרי אנו רואין אותה תמיד וכתבו האחרונים דאפילו הולך לעיר אחרת וראה שם ע"ג אינו מברך כיון דראה את מין זה תוך למ"ד בעירו ואפשר עוד דאפילו קטן שהגדיל א"צ לברך כיון שראה אותה בקטנותו ובא"ר כתב עוד מילתא חדתי דאפילו הוא לא ראה אותה תוך למ"ד כיון שיש רוב העולם שהוא שוכן ביניהם שרואין אותה. כתב הב"ח בשם ספר האשכול השמר לך לראות קנגיאות של כותים וה"ה מחולתם או שום דבר שמחתם ואם תשמע קול כותים שמחים האנח ותצער על חורבן ירושלים ותתפלל להקב"ה עליה. ואיתא בגמרא דאפילו ההולך לקניגיאות ולטיטראות של ישראל ה"ז מושב לצים:
**** ENGLISH ****
(ג) And nowadays, etc. — for we see it constantly; and the later authorities wrote that even if one goes to another city and sees avodah zarah there he does not bless, since he saw this type within thirty days in his city; and it is further possible that even one who grew up from childhood need not bless, since he saw it in his youth. And in Avodah Zarah he wrote further something novel — that even if he himself did not see it within thirty days, since the majority of the world dwells among them and sees it. Bach wrote in the name of Sefer HaEshkol: Guard yourself from seeing the theaters of Kutim, and the same applies to their dances or any of their rejoicing; and if you hear the voice of Kutim rejoicing — sigh and grieve over the destruction of Jerusalem and pray to the Holy One, blessed be He, over it. And it is stated in the Gemara that even one who goes to the theaters and circuses of Israel — this is a seat of scorners.

**** ENGLISH ****
(14) Houses of Israel — meaning houses of wealthy Israelites settled with strength and might, such as in the settlement of the Second Temple [Rashi]; and it is possible that specifically in Eretz Yisrael and in the time of the Second Temple he speaks, but nowadays even in Eretz Yisrael not [Beit Yosef]; and some explain Rashi's intent that even if they are settled now as in the Second Temple one also blesses. And the Rif explains houses of Israel in their settlement — meaning synagogues in which they pray; and so people practice not to bless on other houses but only on the synagogue when one sees it in its beauty and proper state. And he wrote in Avodah Zarah that there is no distinction between Eretz Yisrael and outside the Land, even nowadays; and Peri Megadim leans that nowadays it is good to bless Baruch matsiv gevul almanah without Heaven's name and kingship.
