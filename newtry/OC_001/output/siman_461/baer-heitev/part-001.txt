**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Trail.</b> Even though they crumble when one moves them, even so one fulfills with them, so long as threads do not trail from them; Menachos folio 78. Magen Avraham. <b>נפוחה. </b> יש בזה שני פירושים פירוש א' שנחלקה עובי המצה ושהעליון עלה למעלה. ועי"ל נפוחה באמצעיתה שלא נחלקה כלל אלא שתפח גוף המצה כמו שהוא בלחם חמץ כ"כ הב"ח. ומהר"ם מלובלין כתב כפי' הראשון. והט"ז האריך להתיר כפי' הראשון ומסיק דאין איסור אלא כפי' השני ע"ש. ובמ"א כתב בשם הט"ז שהתיר לגמרי נפוחה וז"ל מעיד אני עלי שמים וארץ כי לא ראיתי פעם א' מן חותני הב"ח שאסר שום מצה שנפוחה וכו' ואדרבא שחק על האוסרין על כן אין להפסיד ממון ש"י בשביל זה עכ"ל הט"ז והפריז על מדותיו להקל ראש בדבר שאבותינו ואבות אבותינו נהגו בו איסור עכ"ל המ"א. הנה מ"ש בשם הט"ז לא נמצא כן בספר הט"ז שלפנינו אלא שעושה פשר דבר בין הדיעות כמ"ש. אכן בתשובת הט"ז כתב הלשון ממש כמו שהעתיק בשמו המ"א. וכתב הח"י בודאי אין להקל בדבר שאבותינו נהגו בו איסור. מ"מ די הוא לאסור אותה מצה דלא כמו שראיתי איזה מורים שאוסרין אף התערובות אפי' במשהו כאלו הוא חמץ גמור וכן אוסרין הרי"ב אייז"ן ושאר כלים ומה שנשתמשו בהן להמה מפסידין ממון ש"י ומונע משמחת י"ט כי די בחומרא זו לענין המצה עצמה. ועש"ת דב"ש סי' רי"ד ושפ"ד שמיקל ג"כ בזה ע"ש. ואם לאחר שנאפית טחו אותה בשומן והחזירה לתנור ונעשית נפוחה שרי לכ"ע. מ"א:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Puffed.</b> There are two interpretations in this: one interpretation, that the thickness of the matzah split and the upper part rose upward; and another interpretation: puffed in its middle, that it did not split at all, but rather the body of the matzah puffed as it is in chametz bread; so wrote Bach. And Maharam of Lublin wrote according to the first interpretation. And Taz lengthened to permit according to the first interpretation and concluded that there is no prohibition except according to the second interpretation; see there. And in Magen Avraham he wrote in the name of Taz that he permitted a puffed [matzah] entirely, and this is its language: I testify upon heaven and earth that I never once saw from my father-in-law the Bach that he forbade any matzah that puffed, etc., and on the contrary he laughed at those who forbid; therefore one should not cause loss of Jewish money for this; end quote of Taz; and he exaggerated his measures to take lightly a matter that our fathers and fathers' fathers practiced as forbidden; end quote of Magen Avraham. Behold, what he wrote in the name of Taz is not found thus in the Taz book before us; rather he makes a compromise between the opinions as I wrote. However in Responsa Taz he wrote the language exactly as Magen Avraham copied in his name. And Chok Yaakov wrote: certainly one should not be lenient in a matter that our fathers practiced as forbidden; nevertheless it is enough to forbid that matzah itself, not as I have seen some who rule and forbid even mixtures even in a morsel as if it were complete chametz, and likewise forbid the Riv Eisen and other vessels and what was used with them, causing loss of Jewish money and withholding Yom Tov joy; for this stringency suffices regarding the matzah itself. And see Shaarei Teshuvah, Devar Shmuel siman 214, and Shevus Yaakov who is also lenient in this; see there. And if after it was baked they smeared it with fat and returned it to the oven and it became puffed, it is permitted according to all opinions. Magen Avraham.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Forbidden.</b> The whole matzah, and even sixty does not avail, even before Pesach, for all of it became chametz; and so wrote Maharam of Lublin siman 105. And so is the agreement of the later authorities; and so wrote in Sefer Or Zarua; and he wrote there in the name of his grandfather the Gaon that one should not forbid unless it is recognizable that the hole rose upward, and the hole must have in it a cavity that holds like a hazelnut that they call a large Hazel-Nuss, or a middle thumb; and so wrote Magen Avraham; and Magen Avraham further wrote: and even with a hole that did not rise upward I have seen the great ones of our generation rule to forbid only the place of the hole, and they permit the rest of the matzah even during Pesach; and so it is written in the name of the Gaon Mahari Horowitz. And if there are two holes, a hole within a hole, they are concerned to forbid all of it. And I found written: if the hole is smooth it is permitted, and if there is belazlik inside it, it is forbidden; end quote.
