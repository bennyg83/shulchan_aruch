And there is no distinction between ovens whose mouth is on the side like ours and small ovens whose mouth is above.

(18) Soaked: in water; and whether it is permitted to soak it in other liquids and fruit juice or in broth, there are opinions among the poskim: some say it is forbidden because they blunt the taste of the matzah by giving it their taste; and some say specifically through cooking does it blunt the taste of matzah, and not through soaking; therefore an elderly person or a sick person for whom it is impossible to eat matzah soaked in water may soak it in wine or other liquids; but any other person who ate matzah soaked in other liquids besides water did not fulfill his obligation and must return and eat other matzah, whether the ke-zayit of the blessing of matzah or the ke-zayit of afikoman. And all this is specifically when he soaks the matzah in them; but to dip it in them Rabbeinu Manoach wrote simply that it is permitted, for in this the taste of matzah is not nullified.
