**** ENGLISH ****
Laws of baking matzah. 5 sections: An oven in which chametz was baked — when firing it to bake matzah one must take care that the coals spread across its entire surface, and it is not sufficient to use only a flame, since just as it absorbs through coals so it releases through coals, and sparks must fly from it. Therefore if it was fired several times before Pesach it is not sufficient unless one specifically intended to kasher it for Pesach, since perhaps the coals did not spread across the entire surface as long as one did not intend this. Some have the custom to apply new earth to it so that it does not require firing, and this is a fine custom. {Rama: Rema: When kashering through firing, it is good and proper to fire it again for the purpose of baking and not to do everything in one firing. (Responsum of Mahariv)}

**** ENGLISH ****
A new ceramic roof tile — whether it was fired from the inside or outside, it is permitted, since the heat acts beneath it; even though the flame does not rise above it, it becomes very hot and the bread bakes immediately and does not come to leavening. However one must first fire the oven, or a kubiyah, or a dry frying pan, or on the ground before baking. But first attaching the dough and then firing — some forbid it and it is good to be careful. Some say one should protest against making bread baked in hot ashes.
