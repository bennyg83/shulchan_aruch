**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The law of how to conduct oneself with tefillin when entering a bathroom, containing nine seifim:</b><br>It is forbidden to enter a fixed bathroom to urinate while the tefillin are on his head, as a decree lest he relieve himself with them. If he holds them in his hand, it is permitted to urinate with them in a fixed bathroom. <small>Gloss: This is specifically when he urinates while seated, where there is no concern for drops. But if he urinates while standing, it is certainly forbidden, for it is no better than a temporary bathroom. The same applies if he urinates seated, or into loose earth where there are no drops, in a temporary bathroom; that too is permitted. There is no distinction between fixed and temporary in this regard, except that in a fixed bathroom one ordinarily relieves himself seated, while in a temporary bathroom one ordinarily does so standing, since he does not relieve himself there for solid waste.</small> In a temporary bathroom it is permitted to urinate while they are on his head. But if he holds them in his hand it is forbidden to urinate with them while standing, even if he holds them in his garment, because he must wipe with his hand the drops on his legs. Rather, he removes them four amos away and gives them to his fellow. From the words of the Rambam it appears that it is forbidden to urinate while they are on his head, whether in a fixed bathroom or a temporary bathroom, and one should be concerned for his words.
