**** HEBREW ****
<b>מרבים בתפלות. </b> ויחיד א"א י"ג מדות. מטעם דאר"י מלמד שנתעטף הקב"ה כש"ץ כו' ולכך א"א כ"א בציבור ת' רשב"ם סי' קצ"א:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>They increase prayers.</b> An individual does not say the Thirteen Attributes, because Rabbi Yochanan said: This teaches that the Holy One, blessed is He, wrapped Himself like a shaliach tzibbur, etc. Therefore they are said only with a congregation. Teshuvas Rashbam, siman 191.
