Yehuda for dignity — dignity alone insufficient — not such embarrassment Beit Yosef — one could answer from end <i>Kometz</i> Mar brei deRav Ashi’s <i>tzitzis</i> tore Shabbos — if not dignity would remove cloak even market — Shabbos no prohibition — there carrying prohibition like load <i>karmelis</i> — needed dignity reason — here private domain.
