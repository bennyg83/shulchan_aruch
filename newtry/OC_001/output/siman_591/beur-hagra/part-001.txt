**** ENGLISH ****
<b>However, if.</b> Gemara 35a. See Tosafos 8b, s.v. 'if you say,' and Rabbeinu Tam explained, etc. The Shulchan Aruch omitted it, as written in Beis Yosef, because the Rambam holds like the explanation of Rashi. But the Rosh and the Tur, although they disagree with Rabbeinu Tam in what he wrote that it does not refer to the Mussaf verses, and see siman 268, seif 4 in the gloss, nevertheless agree with him in this, in that he holds like the explanation of Rashi's teachers; and this is the view of the Rav.
