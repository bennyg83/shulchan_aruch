<small>סק"ב</small> שנדחק שלא לומר מוספי. דכיון דפסוקי ר"ה פורט ופסוקי ר"ח אין פורט אין נכון לכוללם יחד בתיבת מוספי קמ"ל רמ"א דלא חיישינן לזה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>S.k. 2</small>, that he was pressed not to say 'Mussaf offerings.' Since the verses of Rosh Hashanah are specified and the verses of Rosh Chodesh are not specified, it would not be proper to include them together in the word 'Mussaf offerings.' Therefore the Rema teaches that we are not concerned for this.
