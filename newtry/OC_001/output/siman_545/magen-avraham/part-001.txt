**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Selections [of dayanim].</b> That they write: this one selects one for himself and that one selects one for himself; or documents of claims. And the reason all these are permitted is because they are communal needs to uphold justice; and even though each time it is for a particular person, nevertheless since it occurs regularly it is called communal needs (Beis Yosef; and see there). And if so, perhaps only layman's work is permitted, since it is not for the need of the moed, as is written in siman 544, se'if 2; but Tosafos and Beis Yosef wrote that it is something that would be lost, lest the giver or the beis din or the witnesses die; therefore even craftsman's work is permitted. One who fled with other people's money: it is permitted to write against him the judges' decrees and a power of attorney, and it is good to do so discreetly; but if it is certain that he will return to his city, it is forbidden (Maharashdam 27 and Maharshach 113); see what I wrote at the beginning of this [siman], and I do not know why this should be worse than judicial rulings.

And it appears to me that if after the moed he will not find money to borrow, it is permitted to write, for it is something that would be lost.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>It is permitted to copy it.</b> So that he not forget it. And the same law applies if he learned a novel point himself: it is permitted to write it; but reckoning the tekufos is forbidden to write, for this brings nothing out of his mind and he will not forget it. It is forbidden to prepare a booklet in order to write upon it after the moed (Rokeach; Beis Yosef; Darchei Moshe). It appears to me that whatever is permitted to write, it is permitted to make a quill and ink for that purpose, as is written at the beginning of siman 536 regarding riding; and see siman 547 in Beis Yosef, that Rashi and the Rambam permit digging in the launderers' pool to launder something permitted; and even though we do not hold so, nevertheless regarding preparing a quill there is not so much melacha, and the same applies to soaking ink.
