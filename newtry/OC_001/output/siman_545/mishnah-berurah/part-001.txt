Even where they were not previously written, he should at least avoid deliberately scheduling the task for the festival. Eliyah Rabbah.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>If he heard a novel Torah insight.</b> The same applies that he may initially listen to it in order to write it down. There is no distinction between an insight heard from another person and one he developed himself, for it is common to forget an insight and be unable to reconstruct it later. Even without concern for forgetting, a person is obligated at every moment to toil in Torah and develop insights according to his ability. One cannot say that he should wait until after Yom Tov to write the insight, since at that later time he will have a new obligation to develop other insights. There is no greater potential loss than this. It is reported that the Bach wrote his works on Chol HaMoed. Later authorities. Nevertheless, he should write his insights in cursive script, and if he must write a square letter he should make it broken, as stated above.
