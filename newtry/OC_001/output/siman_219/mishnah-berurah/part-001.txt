They also wrote that a minor is not obligated to give thanks [and even from the mitzvah of chinuch]; and also women — the custom of the world is that they do not bless this blessing; and the Achronim wrote the reason: because the order of this blessing is before ten as below, and it is not a woman's honor. And some wrote that nevertheless it is proper that she bless before ten at minimum, before women and one man.
