**** ENGLISH ****
I found in the writings of the Arizal that both explanations are true, and through each of the two pairs one effects, by putting them on, a distinct spiritual effect, as their secret is explained there. This is what the Gemara means when it says that there is room on the head to put on two pairs of tefillin.

The tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam are on a higher level than the tefillin of Rashi. Because of their lofty level, most people are unable to effect the required spiritual effect by putting them on, and therefore refrained from wearing them.

These are the words of Zohar Chadash, page 11b: “Since those of the later generations are not expert, they make two pairs of tefillin out of doubt, because they do not know this secret, that both are required.”

The Zohar in parashas Pinchas says similarly regarding the shofar blasts: “Therefore these Babylonians do not know the secret of sobbing and wailing, and do not know that both are required.”

Sefer Matzas Shmurim strongly warns that both pairs should be worn at the same time, and brings a profound mystical reason not to separate them.

In any event, one should conduct himself as written in the siddur arranged according to the kabbalistic intentions: “The Arizal did not put them on at the same time because of concern for arrogance, but rather after prayer, as mentioned in Shulchan Aruch.”
