**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(5) And he should put on, etc.</b> One who wears both pairs should place the Rashi tefillin lower, toward the face, and Rabbeinu Tam tefillin above them.

The Arizal writes that on the arm one should first insert the Rashi tefillin, positioning them toward the shoulder, and afterward the Rabbeinu Tam tefillin toward the hand, both upon the biceps. On the head, Rashi tefillin should be lower and Rabbeinu Tam tefillin somewhat higher, both within the valid area.

The Rabbeinu Tam straps should be beneath and covered by the Rashi straps, so that only the Rashi straps are visible. The Rabbeinu Tam tefillin should be smaller.

One who has arm tefillin according to Rabbeinu Tam and head tefillin according to Rashi and Rambam does not fulfill the complete obligation according to any view. In pressing circumstances he may wear them, since one may then fulfill the mitzvah with only one tefillin. He should recite a blessing only over the tefillin written according to our accepted Rashi and Rambam practice. If the head tefillin are according to Rashi, he should put them on first, and when removing them remove the arm tefillin first.
