<b>זמן הדלקת נר חנוכה. ובו ב סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i>אין מדליקין נר חנוכה קודם שתשקע החמה אלא <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="א"></i>עם <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>סוף שקיעתה <i data-commentator="Eshel Avraham" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i>לא מאחרים ולא מקדימים <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ב" data-order="2"></i>ויש מי שאומר <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ב"></i>שאם הוא טרוד יכול להדליק <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ג"></i>מפלג המנחה ולמעלה <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ג" data-order="3"></i>ובלבד <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ד"></i>שיתן בה שמן עד <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Sha'arei Teshuvah" data-order="2"></i>שתכלה רגל מן השוק:
**** ENGLISH ****
Chanukah candles are not lit before sunset, but rather with the end of sunset, neither later nor before. And there are those who say that if one is busy, it is permissible to light from Plag Hamincha and onward, as long one puts enough oil [so that the lamp will remain lit] until people stop passing through the marketplace.

**** ENGLISH ****
If one did not light with sunset, either due to forgetfulness or on purpose, he should go ahead and light until people stop passing through the marketplace, which is approximately half an hour, because then the people are passing and coming home and thus the miracle is publicized. Therefore one must place enough oil for that time, and if one put more oil in, he may extinguish the candle after that time has passed, and may use the light after that time. And this is the ideal case. But it this time has passed and s/he has not lit, go ahead an light all night, and if the entire night has passed and s/he has not lit, there is no making up for it later. {Rama: Re"ma: There are those who say that in this time, when people light indoors, there is no need to be careful to light before people stop passing through the marketplace (these are his own words, and the words of the Tur, quoting Tosafot), and regardless, it is good to be careful even now. Rema: And on other nights, one should light like all other people, even though s/he did not light at first. [He found this written in the name of the Maha"ril and also shared it as his own opinion.]}
