**** HEBREW ****
<b>מעובד לשמו</b>. ברצועות אף הרמב"ם מודה ונ"ל הטעם משום דהל"מ שיהיו שחורות א"כ צריך שיעבדם וישחירם לשמן משא"כ בבתים והרב"י כ' טעם אחר וסתרו בעצמו בבד"ה ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Processed for its sake.</b> Regarding straps, even Rambam agrees. It appears to me that the reason is that their blackness is a halachah transmitted to Moshe at Sinai. Therefore, both their processing and their blackening must be performed for the sake of the mitzvah, unlike the batim.

Beit Yosef gives another reason, but refutes it himself in Bedek HaBayis; see there.

<b>טוב שישחירם</b>. משמע דס"ל דבדיעבד כשר אפי' לא השחירן לשמן וא"כ רמ"א בהג"ה חולק עליו והל"ל בל' וי"א וכו' מיהו מצינו כמה פעמים כיוצא בזה כמ"ש בסמ"ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>It is proper to blacken them.</b> This implies that he holds they remain valid after the fact even if they were not blackened for the sake of the mitzvah. If so, Rama in his gloss disagrees with him and should have introduced the ruling with “some say.”

Nevertheless, we find several similar cases, as Sema writes.

**** HEBREW ****
<b>פסול</b>. נ"ל דאם חזר ישראל והשחירן לשמן כשר דהא אמרי' בגיטין דף כ' שאם כ' השם ולא קדשו מעביר עליו קולמוס ומקדשו ואפי' רבנן ל"פ אלא משום דמיחזי כמנומר הא לאו הכי שרי א"כ כאן שרי לכ"ע וע' בא"ע סי' קל"א:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Invalid.</b> It appears to me that if a Jew subsequently blackened them for the sake of the mitzvah, they are valid.

Gittin 20a states that if one wrote the Divine Name without sanctifying it, he may trace over it with a pen and thereby sanctify it. Even the Sages disagree only because it then appears mottled. Were it not for that concern, they would permit it.

Here, therefore, everyone would agree that tracing over the blackening is effective. See Even HaEzer, siman 131.

**** HEBREW ****
<b>יש מתירין לתפור</b>. מדסתם הרב"י כאן ש"מ דעתו כא"ח דאפילו בחוטין שרי אבל הת"ה ורי"ו כתבו דוקא בגידי' ולקשור לכ"ע פסול דבעינן קשירה תמה כדאי' בגמ':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Some permit sewing.</b> Since Beit Yosef rules without qualification here, this indicates that his view follows Orchos Chaim, that even ordinary thread is permitted.

Terumas HaDeshen and Rabbeinu Yerucham, however, write that only sinews may be used. Tying is invalid according to all opinions, because a complete binding is required, as stated in the Gemara.
