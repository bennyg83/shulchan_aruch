<b>Born before, etc.</b> So wrote Beis Yosef in the name of the Rambam, and it is in the poskim; therefore I am astonished at what Agudah wrote, as follows: the Maimonidean and most books ruled like the first version, specifically [only] one born on Yom Tov, end quote.
