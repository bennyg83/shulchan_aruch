<small>Ibid.</small> 'Or when it is raw.' From Tosafos, Shabbos 39b, s.v. 'From the incident,' one may learn that Rabbeinu Yonah maintains that it is permitted to leave raw water upon the fire. It is only with respect to insulating that it is forbidden, because one may not insulate a raw pot. According to Rabbeinu Shmuel, however, in Tosafos at the beginning of the chapter Bameh Tomnin, it is evident that water does not have the status of a raw pot. See below, siman 254 and siman 257, se'if 7.

**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham, s.k. 3.</small> 'And even if it later flared up.' See Tosafos and Rashba. In their opinion, only regarding food cooked completely does it have the status of a fire covered with ashes; if it is less cooked than this, it does not have that status. If the coals merely became glowing, which is less than having flared up, they have the status of coals covered with ashes for all purposes.

<small>מג"א ס"ק ה'</small> אסור לסמוך באינה גרופה בספר דגול מרבבה כ' היינו לסמוך תחלה בשבת אבל סמך מע"ש מותר להחזיר בשבת ולסמוך כדמוכח מההיא דב' כירות המתאימות וע"ש ובמחכ"ת אינו כן אלא כוונת המג"א דלהך דעה דלא קיי"ל כחנניה וזהו שטת ררי"ף והוכיח כן מדבעי לן מאי' לסמוך הרי דשהייה אסורה וביאר הר"ן דס"ל להרי"ף דאם איתא דהאי בעי' בלהחזיר איך פשטי' מהברייתא דב' כירות מתאימות הא התם מיירי בשהייה ודלמא חזרה חמיר ע"ש א"כ קיימא הדין דחזרה אסור בסמיכה ומזה מובן דמ"ש הגאון כדמוכח מב' כירות הוא שגיאה דהא התם מיירי בשהייה וע"ז כ' המג"א אבל לסברא השנייה שרי היינו לסברא השנייה דקיי"ל כחנניה והאיבעי' מהו לסמיך היינו בחזרה ופשיט לה מההיא דב' כירות הרי להדיא דגם בחזרה שרי ומ"מ דוקא דרך חזרה אבל לסמוך לכתחלה בשבת לא כדמוכח מהרמ"א ססי' זה במ"ש וכן נתבאר דנהגו להקל באופן דלכתחלה לסמוך אסור ובחזרה זהו תולה המגן אברהם בב' שטות ולא כדברי הגאון ז"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham, s.k. 5.</small> 'It is forbidden to place it adjacent to a stove that was not swept.' Dagul Merevavah writes that this means it is forbidden to place it there initially on Shabbos, but if it was placed there before Shabbos, one may return it on Shabbos and place it adjacent to the stove, as is evident from the case of two adjoining stoves; see there. With due respect, this is incorrect. The Magen Avraham means that according to the opinion that does not rule like Chananya, which is the position of the Rif, placing food there before Shabbos is also forbidden. He proves this from the Gemara's question whether one may place food adjacent to the stove, which indicates that leaving it there is forbidden. The Ran explains that the Rif maintains that if this question concerned returning food on Shabbos, how could the Gemara resolve it from the baraisa concerning two adjoining stoves? That baraisa concerns leaving food there before Shabbos, and perhaps returning it is more stringent; see there. It follows that according to this opinion, returning food adjacent to the stove is forbidden. It is therefore clear that the Gaon's statement that permission is evident from the case of two adjoining stoves is mistaken, since that case concerns leaving food there before Shabbos. Regarding this the Magen Avraham writes, 'But according to the second opinion it is permitted,' meaning the second opinion, which rules like Chananya. According to that view, the Gemara's question whether one may place food adjacent to the stove concerns returning it, and the Gemara resolves the question from the case of two adjoining stoves. It is thus explicit that returning food there is also permitted. Nevertheless, this applies only by way of returning it. One may not initially place food adjacent to the stove on Shabbos, as is evident from the Rama at the end of this siman, who writes, 'and it has likewise been explained that the custom is to be lenient.' Thus, initially placing it adjacent is forbidden, while returning it depends upon these two opinions, as the Magen Avraham explains, contrary to the words of the Gaon of blessed memory.
