**** ENGLISH ****
(ב) (sec. 1) in his sons who bring children to the church. We were little people who had not yet reached education and the taste because the children play and dance in the Church and the 'holy' in the Church and also confuse the minds of the worshipers, and also because our elders will not depart from the bad way they were brought up in their childhood to desecrate the Holy in the Church, but when they reached education they would rather bring him with him to the Church and teach him the ways of life to sit in the im' and bira' and not let him move from his place and rush him to answer Amen and Kaddish and Holy One for the end of matters of prayer and reading in the Shabbat:
