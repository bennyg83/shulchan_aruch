**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And in a place, etc.</b> From the fact that it says there, "Let him expound for them in accordance with Rabbi Akiva," it implies that we hold in accordance with Rabbi Akiva, but because of beautification of the mitzvah he did not want to permit it explicitly. And certainly, if he did not hold like Rabbi Tarfon and Rabbi Akiva, he would not have permitted it and caused them a loss, so that the students should not see. And Rabbi Yishmael also holds like him, as it says there: Rabbi Yishmael retracted. The explanation is that he retracted from what he said at first, that three hadasim are required, and instead one hadas that is not cut off suffices; and he mentioned two cut-off ones only for beautification of the mitzvah. This is unlike Rashi's explanation, according to which he retracted from the latter statement, and one would have to say that he did not say it and that it is not black. See the Ran.

<b>סט"ו לא יטול כו'. </b>כמש"ש נינקוט תרי או תלתא כו' פרי א' כו' וה"ה ללולב אבל בהדס אמרי' שם חזר בו ר' ישמעאל ומכשר אף בחד ולא אמר שלשה אלא להידור מצוה כנ"ל אלמא דיכול להוסיף וכ"ש בערבה דלא נתנה תורה קצבה ואמר ערבי אלא דמיעוט שתים ובהכי סגי אבל טפי ש"ד כמ"ש בב' עדים ומנין אפילו מאה ת"ל עדים וע' תוס' דר"ה כ"ח ב' ד"ה ומנא כו' ובסוכה ל"ז ב':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Se'if 15: he should not take, etc.</b> As it says there: let him take two or three, etc.; one fruit, etc. The same applies to the lulav. But regarding hadas we say there that Rabbi Yishmael retracted and validates even one, and he said three only for beautification of the mitzvah, as appears to me. It is therefore evident that one may add. And all the more so with aravah, for the Torah did not give a fixed measure, and it said "willows," only the minimum is two, and that is sufficient; but more is permitted. This is as we say regarding two witnesses: and from where do we know even one hundred? The verse states: "witnesses." See Tosafos in Rosh Hashanah 28b, s.v. "and from where," etc., and in Sukkah 37b.
