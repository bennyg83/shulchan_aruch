**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 1: If one was, etc.</b> There, the law of changing location, etc., applies not only within a house, etc., in accordance with Rav Sheshes, for Rav Chisda was refuted. Although Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak answered the objections, we do not rely on that resolution. Rif. Although Rosh questions him and writes that the Gemara contains no conclusive refutation, since Rabbi Yehudah holds this view and the first Tanna disagrees, Rabbi Yehudah is a lone opinion. Kesef Mishneh writes that Rabbi Yehudah and Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi disagree in Eruvin, chapter 7, and Sanhedrin, chapter 3, whether the phrase “when does this apply” comes to explain or to distinguish. We rule everywhere like Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, as Tosafos writes at the beginning of Megillah, chapter 4, and the other poskim likewise rule. Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak’s resolution is therefore rejected.
