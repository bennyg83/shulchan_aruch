**** ENGLISH ****
<b>“A hole.”</b> Beis Yosef in his gloss cites Bahag that some say to make two holes, put the tzitzis through, and bring them out to one side, etc.—and one who is stringent this way is not among the stringent but among those who cause wonder, for it looks pretentious—end Beis Yosef. Bach wrote that for a small tallis one may make two holes, for that does not look pretentious since it is not visible to all—and Kavvanos that the Arizal did so. I find it difficult: the Gemara learns the upper knot is deoraisa; if not deoraisa, permitted kilayim in tzitzis—how could it occur, for we hold one fold of stitching is not a connection? If we make two holes that is two folds as at the end of Kilayim and Rambam—and even Tur Yoreh Deah 10 holds two folds are not connected until one ties—how does the Gemara say one fold is not connection—should it not say not connected until tied? And Tur there explained if the needle passes only once, even tying both thread ends on the garment edge is not connection—end quote—so how can kilayim in tzitzis occur, for he rules one hole? One must say: when tying two knots, even inserting the needle once it counts as connection through two folds, and even one knot can be connection—see proof Rosh brings from Shabbos about stitching two folds: liable only if tying; Ran there says one knot—see there; Tosafos wrote the Gemara supports Bartenura that specifically two knots obligate—see there. Hence for all opinions with two holes only one knot is required, not two knots one atop the other—so one who makes two holes and two stacked knots contradicts himself; still one could say that although one knot suffices, if he wishes he may make two knots.

<b>לאורך</b>. אבל אין קפידא כ"כ לפוסלו בכך מ"מ עכ"פ יזהר מאוד שלא יהו הציצית תלוין באלכסון על קרן זוית [ב"י] ולבוש כתב הטעם דאסור מפני שנראה שצריך ח' ציצית מפני זה עושה באלכסון כדי לצאת בזה לב' צדדים ע"ש ול"נ דאסור מפני שהוא מנהג הקראים שעושין כן באלכסון וק"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>“Along the length.”</b> There is not so much stringency to invalidate for that; still one should be very careful that tzitzis not hang diagonally on a corner (Beis Yosef). Levush wrote the reason is forbidden: it looks as if he needs eight tzitzis, so he places diagonally to discharge two directions—see there. It appears to me it is forbidden because it is Karaite practice to do so diagonally—and this requires study.
