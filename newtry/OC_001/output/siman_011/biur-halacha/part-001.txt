<b> טוואן עכו"ם וכו'</b> -  ולעניין טויית חש"ו עיין בסימן ת"ס לענין עשיית המצה שיש פלוגתא בזה בין הפוסקים וה"נ בענינינו ועולה שם לדינא לפי הסכמת האחרונים [והוא הט"ז ומ"א וא"ר וח"י] דאם א"א בענין אחר מותר בעומד ע"ג אפילו ע"י עכו"ם וכ"ש ע"י חש"ו אבל לכתחלה יש להחמיר שלא לעשות ע"י עכו"ם וחש"ו אפילו עומד ע"ג [חרש מיקרי שאינו שומע ואינו מדבר ושוטה עיין יו"ד סימן א' וקטן הוא עד שנעשה בר מצוה וקטנה היא עד בת י"ב (א"ר וח"י ופמ"ג שם דלא כט"ז)] ובלא עומד על גבו פסול בכל הני אפילו בדיעבד ועיין בתשובת משכנות יעקב [סי' כ"א] שהוכיח מדברי התוספות דעומד ע"ג אינו נקרא באומר לו פ"א קודם העשייה רק צריך להזהירו בכל שעה שלא יסיח דעתו כלל אבל כל שאינו עומד על גבו ומזהירו תמיד לכו"ע אין לו כוונה כלל ומכל זה נמצינו למידין שאותן הנוהגין ליתן לנערות קטנות לטוות ציצית וסומכין ע"ז שאומרים להם לטוות לשמה לא הועילו כלום והציצית פסולות מן התורה לד"ה עי"ש וכן משמע מדברי ח"א ע"ש. ודע עוד דבטוויה לשמה כיון שהיא ד"ת מוכח לדברי הפמ"ג שם בסימן ת"ס דאין ליתן לטוות אפילו לנער בן י"ג וכן לנערה בת י"ב כ"ז שלא ידעינן שהביאו שתי שערות אך בדיעבד נ"ל שאפשר דאין להחמיר דסמכינן אחזקה דרבא [עיין בסימן ל"ט בש"ת שהקיל בשם הנוב"י ועי"ש בבה"ל] ובשגדול עומד עליהם ומלמדם לעשות לשמה נראה דיש להקל בזה לכתחלה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>A non-Jew spun them, etc.</b> Regarding spinning by a deaf-mute, a shoteh, or a minor, see siman 460 concerning the making of matzah, where there is a dispute about this among the poskim. The same applies in our matter. The practical ruling there, according to the agreement of the later authorities, namely the Taz, Magen Avraham, Eliyah Rabbah, and Chok Yaakov, is that if it is impossible in another manner, it is permitted when an adult Jew stands over him, even when done by a non-Jew, and all the more so when done by a deaf-mute, shoteh, or minor. But initially one should be stringent not to have it done by a non-Jew or by a deaf-mute, shoteh, or minor, even while standing over him. A deaf person means one who neither hears nor speaks; regarding a shoteh, see Yoreh Deah, siman 1; a minor is until he becomes bar mitzvah, and a minor girl is until age twelve, as written by Eliyah Rabbah, Chok Yaakov, and Peri Megadim there, unlike the Taz. Without standing over him, all these are invalid even after the fact. See the responsa Mishkenos Yaakov, siman 21, who proved from Tosafot that standing over him does not mean telling him once before the act, but that one must warn him constantly not to divert his attention at all. But whenever one does not stand over him and warn him constantly, according to everyone he has no intent at all. From all this we learn that those who are accustomed to give young girls tzitzis to spin, relying on the fact that they tell them to spin it for its required purpose, have accomplished nothing, and according to all opinions the tzitzis are invalid according to the Torah; see there. So too it is implied from Chayei Adam; see there. Know further that since spinning for its required purpose is according to the Torah, it is proven from the words of the Peri Megadim there in siman 460 that one should not give it to be spun even to a boy of thirteen or a girl of twelve as long as we do not know that they have produced two hairs. However, after the fact, it seems to me that perhaps one need not be stringent, for we rely on the presumption of Rava. See siman 39 in Shaarei Teshuvah, who was lenient in the name of Noda BiYehuda, and see there in Biur Halacha. When an adult stands over them and teaches them to do it for its required purpose, it appears that one may be lenient in this even initially.

<b> וישראל עומד על גבו וכו'</b> -  עיין בב"י שלמד זה מדין עיבוד שהכשיר הרא"ש בישראל עוע"ג ועיין בדגמ"ר ובחידושי רע"א ושארי אחרונים שתמהו על הב"י דהלא טעם הרא"ש שחילק בין גט למילה ועיבוד משום דבגט דבעינן שיכתוב כל תורף הגט לשמה וזה לא יעשה אבל בעיבוד לא בעינן אלא בתחלת העיבוד כשישים העורות לתוך הסיד שיאמר אז אני עושה כך לשם ס"ת וזה יעשה העכו"ם לשם ישראל כשיאמר לו עשה כך וכן במילה כיון דברגע הוא עושה ודאי יעשה אדעתא דישראל מה שיאמר לו וכן דעת בעל העיטור דסגי בעכו"ם שיעבדנו לשמן עכ"ל הרא"ש בהלכות ס"ת א"כ לפ"ז לא שייך הקולא גבי טויית ציצית דכל טווייה וטוייה מילתא באנפי נפשיה היא. וביותר יפלא בעיני על הב"י דהלא הרא"ש הביא ראיה לדבריו מדברי בעל העיטור שסובר כוותיה והעיטור גופיה פוסק לענין טוייה דלא מהני ע"י עכו"ם אף אם ישראל עומד ע"ג ולמד זה מגט עי"ש בדבריו בהלכות ציצית שכתב כן בפירוש הרי דלא כב"י שמשוה טוייה לעיבוד אמנם מדברי הגר"א בביאורו שהראה מקור להשו"ע שכתב דלהרא"ש כשר וז"ל בהעתקתו וכתב הרא"ש בהלכות ס"ת והטעם דל"ד לגט דשם בעינן כל התורף לשמה משא"כ במילה וס"ת וציצית וכיוצא שאין צריך אלא בתחלה כיון דרגע הוא עושה אדעתא דישראל עכ"ל ובאמת לא נזכר כלל בהרא"ש לענין ציצית משמע מיניה שהוא סובר דמה שכתב לענין מילה כיון דרגע הוא לאו דוקא אלא דבדבר שאין נמשך זמן הרבה כעין גט הוא עושה אדעתא דישראל ע"כ טויית ציצית לטלית נמי שאין נמשכת אלא רגעים אחדים מהני עוע"ג [ומה שכתב הגר"א שא"צ אלא בתחלה ר"ל כיון דאין נמשכת הטוייה אלא מעט מהני לזה המחשבה שחישב בתחלת הטוויה דלא אסח דעתיה עדיין מהמחשבה ההיא משא"כ בגט] וא"כ לפ"ז אם נמשכת הטוייה זמן הרבה נראה דלכו"ע לא מהני בעומד ע"ג וא"ל דיאמר לא"י עוד הפעם שיעשה לשמה ועוד הפעם א"כ אפילו בגט נתכשר באופן זה והמשנה סתמא קאמר וע"כ דלא ציית ליה באופן זה א"כ ה"נ בטוייה. היוצא מזה דהשו"ע לא הקיל להרא"ש בעומד ע"ג כ"א באינה נמשכת הטוייה זמן הרבה וכעין שכתב לעיל מיניה טווי לי ציצית לטלית אף דשם האי לישנא לאו דוקא דה"ה אם טותה עבורו כל היום אך ע"כ הרי חזינן דהשו"ע לא מיירי בטוייה הנמשכת כל היום כעין בזמנינו בהפאבריקי"ן ולפ"ז יש ליישב קצת הקושיא הגדולה הנ"ל מבעל העיטור דאפשר דהוא מיירי בנמשך זמן הרבה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And a Jew stands over him, etc.</b> See the Beis Yosef, who learned this from the law of tanning, where the Rosh validated it when a Jew stands over him. See Dagul MeRevavah, the novellae of Rabbi Akiva Eiger, and the other later authorities, who wondered at the Beis Yosef. For the reason of the Rosh, who distinguished between a get on the one hand and milah and tanning on the other, is that by a get we require the entire essential text of the get to be written for its required purpose, and this the non-Jew will not do; but by tanning, we require only that at the beginning of the tanning, when he places the hides into the lime, he say, I am doing this for the sake of a Torah scroll, and the non-Jew will do this for the sake of the Jew when he tells him, Do this. So too by milah, since he does it in a moment, he will certainly do according to the intent of the Jew what the Jew tells him. This is also the view of Baal HaItur, that it is enough for a non-Jew to tan it for its required purpose. End quote of the Rosh in the laws of Torah scrolls. If so, according to this, the leniency does not apply to spinning tzitzis, for each act of spinning is a matter unto itself. It is even more wondrous to me regarding the Beis Yosef, for the Rosh brought proof for his words from Baal HaItur, who agrees with him, yet Baal HaItur himself rules regarding spinning that it does not help when done by a non-Jew even if a Jew stands over him, and he learned this from a get; see his words in the laws of tzitzis, where he wrote this explicitly. This is unlike the Beis Yosef, who compares spinning to tanning. However, from the words of the Gra in his commentary, who showed the source for the Shulchan Aruch that wrote that according to the Rosh it is valid, it appears otherwise. These are his words in his quotation: the Rosh wrote in the laws of Torah scrolls that the reason is that it is not comparable to a get, for there the entire essential text must be for its required purpose, unlike milah, a Torah scroll, tzitzis, and the like, where only the beginning is needed, since he does it in a moment according to the intent of the Jew. End quote. In truth, tzitzis is not mentioned at all in the Rosh, which implies that the Gra understands that what he wrote regarding milah, that it is only a moment, was not literal, but that anything that does not continue for much time like a get is done according to the intent of the Jew. Therefore, spinning tzitzis for a tallis, which also lasts only a few moments, is effective when a Jew stands over him. What the Gra wrote, that only the beginning is needed, means that since the spinning continues only a little, the thought he had at the beginning of the spinning helps, for he has not yet diverted his attention from that thought, unlike by a get. If so, according to this, if the spinning continues a long time, it appears that according to everyone standing over him does not help. One should not say that the Jew should again tell the non-Jew to do it for its required purpose, and again, for if so even a get should be valid in this manner, while the Mishnah states the law without qualification, and it must be that he does not obey him in this way. If so, the same applies to spinning. What emerges from this is that the Shulchan Aruch was lenient according to the Rosh with standing over him only when the spinning does not continue for a long time, similar to what he wrote just above, Spin tzitzis for me for a tallis. Although there that language is not precise, for the same would apply if she spun for him the whole day, nevertheless we see that the Shulchan Aruch is not discussing spinning that continues the whole day, as in our times in factories. According to this, the great difficulty from Baal HaItur mentioned above can be somewhat resolved, for perhaps he is discussing a case that continues a long time.

<b> וצריכין שזירה</b> -  עיין במ"ב במש"כ שיכפלם לשנים הכי מוכח מסתימת לשון הר"ן ספ"ק דביצה וכן מוכח מלשון הגמרא עירובין צ"ו ע"ב ודילמא אדעתא דשיפתא דגלימא עייפינהו שפירושו כפלינהו כידוע. ועיין בט"ז בסק"ב שפירש על הא דספרי ת"ל פתיל תכלת טווי ושזור כעין פתילה [והוא מהגה"מ] ולכאורה יקשה ע"ז מיומא דף ע"ב ע"א בגמרא ת"ל פתיל פתילים הרי כאן ד' ועי"ש בפירש"י ותוספות שהוא ע"י קציצה ואם היה ע"י כפילה ושזירה לשנים א"כ היה שמונה חוטין אם לא דנימא דסובר דשזירה היטיב אפילו בלי הכפלת החוט ג"כ בכלל שזירה הוא וזהו דבר חדש אמנם לפי מה דמשמע בהראב"ד שם דהיה להם הגירסא בספרי דילפינן תכלת ממשכן דהיה שם שזירה ניחא הכל דסתם פתילה לאו שזור הוא ועכ"פ לכל הפוסקים די שיהיה שזור לשנים ועיין בארה"ח שהביא די"א דלמצוה מן המובחר טוב שיהיה כל חוט כפול לשמונה. ואולם באמת תמה אני על המהדרין לקנות דוקא כפול לשמונה שהוא עכ"פ רק למצוה מן המובחר לכו"ע הלא יותר ראוי להם לכתחילה להדר על הציצית לידע אם הם טוויים ושזורים לשמה כדין ע"י ישראל כי יש מקומות שסומכין לטוות ציצית ע"י גוי בציווי ישראל וכי האי גונא קולות ועיין בסימן זה במשנה ברורה ס"ק ט' וי'. נסתפקתי אם היו שזורים לשנים שלא לשמה ואח"כ שזרם לשמנה לשמה או להיפך מי אזלינן בתר שזירה בתרא או קמא וצ"ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And they require twisting.</b> See the Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote that he should double them into two. This is proven from the plain wording of the Ran at the end of the first chapter of Beitzah, and so it is proven from the language of the Gemara in Eiruvin 96b, perhaps he folded them for the edge of his cloak, whose meaning is, as is known, that he doubled them. See the Taz in s.k. 2, who explained the Sifrei statement, the verse teaches a strand of techeiles, spun and twisted like a wick, which is from the Hagahos Maimoniyos. At first glance this is difficult from Yoma, folio 72a, where the Gemara says, the verse teaches strand, strands, so there are four. See Rashi and Tosafot there, that this is through cutting; and if it were through doubling and twisting into two, there would be eight threads, unless we say that he holds that a thread is considered well twisted, and included in twisting, even without doubling the thread. This would be a new matter. However, according to what is implied in the Raavad there, that they had the version in the Sifrei that we derive techeiles from the Mishkan, where there was twisting, everything is settled, for an ordinary wick is not twisted. In any event, according to all the poskim it is enough that it be twisted into two. See Eretz HaChaim, who brought that some say that for the preferred mitzvah it is good for every thread to be doubled into eight. But in truth I am astonished at those who are meticulous to buy specifically doubled into eight, which is at any rate only a preferred mitzvah according to everyone. It would be more fitting for them initially to be meticulous about the tzitzis, to know whether they were spun and twisted for their required purpose, according to the law, by a Jew, for there are places that rely on having tzitzis spun by a non-Jew at the command of a Jew, and similar leniencies. See this siman in Mishnah Berurah, s.k. 9 and 10. I was uncertain if they were twisted into two not for their required purpose and afterward he twisted them into eight for their required purpose, or the reverse, whether we follow the latter twisting or the first. This requires study.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>For their required purpose.</b> See the Mishnah Berurah, and this is from the words of the Gra. If so, it implies that this is according to the Torah, and so the Peri Megadim inclines, that it is according to the Torah. The Bach, the Nezirus Shimshon, and the Eliyah Rabbah are lenient after the fact even without twisting, but one should not rely on this, for many disagree with it. From the Ran, the Nimukei Yosef, and Shulchan Aruch, seif 3, it is explicit that twisting prevents validity; so the Olas Tamid wrote explicitly in seif 3. So too it is proven from Chayei Adam, Derech HaChaim, Shulchan Shlomo, and Eretz HaChaim. What Eliyah Rabbah wrote to reject the proof of Olas Tamid, one who examines the Nimukei Yosef and the Beis Yosef will see explicitly that the law is with Olas Tamid; see there. The Peri Megadim has also already challenged him with several correct proofs. But what the Peri Megadim proved from the Levush, that he too agrees with the Nezirus Shimshon, one who examines the Beis Yosef and the Levush will see the opposite, for he wrote the accepted opinion plainly and the rejected opinion in the name of some say, as is known. If they were twisted but not for their required purpose, the matter requires analysis. Although from the words of the Olas Tamid, the Magen Avraham, and the Gra it implies that this invalidates, and Chayei Adam and Shulchan Shlomo quoted the words of the Magen Avraham as halacha, nevertheless perhaps in pressing circumstances one may rely in this matter on the above-mentioned lenient authorities regarding for its required purpose. So too Derech HaChaim and Eretz HaChaim quoted the above-mentioned words of Eliyah Rabbah regarding for its required purpose, and so Shaarei Teshuvah in the name of Mor U'Ketziah, and the Peri Megadim is also uncertain regarding for its required purpose; see there. Know further that it seems obvious to me that even according to those who are stringent regarding twisting for its required purpose, this is specifically when it was explicitly not for their required purpose; but if it was unspecified, and the one who spun them himself twisted them, one may say that whoever acts does so according to his first intent, even though these are two actions. There is proof for this from Tosafot in Zevachim, folio 2b, s.v. ha leshem, etc., where they explain that the Gemara is speaking of two services, slaughtering and receiving, and nevertheless the Gemara concludes there that even if we say that ordinary offerings do not stand for their required purpose, the offering is valid if he had intent during slaughtering for the sake of the Pesach offering and at receiving it was unspecified, because whoever acts does so according to his first intent. The same applies here. Although ordinary threads are not made for the sake of tzitzis, where he initially spun them explicitly for their required purpose, the unspecified twisting is also for their required purpose. Here and there are one matter, for there the Gemara derives that the four services must be for their required purpose from the verse, you shall perform the Pesach, meaning all its performances must be for the sake of Pesach; here too we derive from the verse, you shall make tassels for yourself, for the sake of your obligation, whether the spinning or the twisting, for all is included in making, as the Gra wrote. Even according to what Tosafot there rejected this explanation, because they asked there in Pesachim whether we learned it regarding one service, etc., in any case they did not reject it because the rule whoever acts according to his first intent is inapplicable, but for another reason. Although the Magen Avraham wrote that if they were not twisted for their required purpose it is invalid, and did not write if they were twisted not for their required purpose, implying that even unspecified is invalid, perhaps he did not state this precisely. Alternatively, this is where the twister was not the spinner himself, so the rule that whoever acts does so according to his first intent does not apply, as written in Mishnas Avraham, siman 19, seif katan 23, regarding writing sifrei Torah, tefillin, and mezuzos.

<b> יותר ארוך</b> -  ובדיעבד לכאורה אין להחמיר בזה אם לאחר כריכת החוליא ראשונה וקשירתו היה בו כשיעור דלא גרע זה משהיה נקטם אז לגמרי זה החוט דכשר לכו"ע דהוא גרדומי הציצית וכדלקמן בסימן י"ב. ולפ"ז מה שכתב הרמ"א דשיעור זה צריך להיות אחר שנקשר ור"ל כל הקשרים כדמשמע בב"י הוא רק לכתחילה דבדיעבד די אם רק לאחר קשירת החוליא הראשונה היה בהחוטין השיעור י"ב גודלין אף שנתקצרו אח"כ ע"י שיעור הקשירות לא גרע מגרדומין דקי"ל לקמן בסימן י"ב כסברא ראשונה. ועיין בפמ"ג שהבאתי את דבריו במ"ב בס"ק כ"ד ומשמע מסתימת דבריו היפוך דברי ואפשר דסברתו דכ"ז שהוא עוסק עדיין במצוה לעשות שליש גדיל אף דהוא רק לכתחילה מיקרי עדיין שלא נגמרה המצוה ולא נוכל להקרא עתה על הציצית שם גרדומין וגרע זה משאם היה עושה רק חוליא אחת ואח"כ נקטע חוט אחד. וכדמות ראיה לזה מהא דאמרינן שבת קל"ג ע"ב כ"ז שהוא עוסק במילה בשבת חוזר אפילו על ציצין שאין מעכבין פירש על ציצין המעכבין חוזר על ציצין שאין מעכבין אין חוזר ושם הלא הוא לענין להקל וכ"ש הכא לענין להחמיר וצ"ע למעשה. ודע עוד דאם הוא בדרך ואין לו ציצית אחרות כ"א קצרות מי"ב גודלין יוכל לסמוך ע"ד המחבר דמיקל בארבע גודלין להטילם בבגד וללבשו ובפרט לפי מש"כ הלבוש דמה"ת אין שיעור לאורך הציצית ממילא אין עובר על מ"ע דציצית בכל גווני וכ"ש להפוסקים דבמקום שאין נמצאים ציצית אין עובר על עשה דציצית אך לענין ברכה יש להחמיר לפי מה דנוהגין כדעה שנייה דצריך י"ב גודלין כן מוכח מהפמ"ג אות ג' במ"ז ובחידושי רע"א בסוף סי' י"ב עי"ש ועיין מש"כ לעיל בשם הגאון מהר"מ בנעט ז"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Longer.</b> After the fact, at first glance one should not be stringent in this if, after the wrapping of the first section and its tying, it had the required measure. This is no worse than if that thread had then been completely severed, which is valid according to everyone, for these are remnants of tzitzis, as below in siman 12. According to this, what the Rama wrote, that this measure must be after it is tied, meaning all the knots as implied in the Beis Yosef, is only initially. After the fact, it is enough if after tying the first section the threads had the measure of twelve thumbbreadths, even though they were later shortened by the amount taken by the knots, for it is no worse than remnants, regarding which we rule below in siman 12 like the first opinion. See the Peri Megadim whose words I brought in Mishnah Berurah, s.k. 24, and from the plain meaning of his words it implies the opposite of my words. Perhaps his reasoning is that as long as he is still occupied in the mitzvah to make one third tied section, even though this is only initial, the mitzvah is still called unfinished, and we cannot yet apply the name remnants to the tzitzis. This is worse than if he had made only one section and afterward one thread was cut. A semblance of proof to this is from what we say in Shabbos 133b, that as long as he is occupied with circumcision on Shabbos he may return even for shreds that do not prevent validity; the explanation is that for shreds that prevent validity he may return, but for shreds that do not prevent validity he may not return. There it is with respect to leniency, and all the more so here with respect to stringency. The practical law requires study. Know further that if one is on the road and has no other tzitzis except ones shorter than twelve thumbbreadths, he may rely on the view of the Mechaber, who is lenient with four thumbbreadths, to attach them to the garment and wear it. This is especially so according to what the Levush wrote, that according to the Torah there is no measure for the length of the tzitzis, so in any case he does not transgress the positive commandment of tzitzis. All the more so according to the poskim who hold that where tzitzis are not available, one does not transgress the positive commandment of tzitzis. But regarding the blessing one should be stringent, according to our practice like the second opinion, which requires twelve thumbbreadths. So it is proven from the Peri Megadim, letter 3 in Mishbetzos Zahav, and from the novellae of Rabbi Akiva Eiger at the end of siman 12; see there. See what I wrote above in the name of the Gaon Rabbi Meir Binet, of blessed memory.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>But, etc., the threads are valid.</b> See the Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote in the name of the Magen Avraham that he holds it is a change through an act. But from the language of the Rokeach brought in the Beis Yosef, s.v. the Rokeach wrote, it appears, etc., it seems at first glance that he holds that if the tzitzis were exactly at the completion of the thumb-joint measure, even though they are not above it, it is valid, for the tzitzis hole may be within the measure, provided it is not below the measure. What the Rokeach wrote, in the width, must mean the width and a little more. The words of the Bach also imply somewhat that he too holds this way as practical law; see his words carefully, s.v. he should make a hole in the length of the tallis, etc., and in the continuation. <b>And they were detached from the woof threads, etc.</b> See the Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote that this applies even if this happened immediately after the first section and the knot upon it, and this is learned from nearby, seif 13, for through this the tzitzis are rendered valid.
