(א) החוטין - לשון הרמב"ם המוצא חוטי ציצית בשוק אפילו פסוקים ושזורים פסולים דטרח איניש לעשות חוטין כעין ציצית וא"כ שמא לא נעשה לשם ציצית. והמ"מ חולק וגם בכ"מ כתב דהרמב"ם בתשובה חזר בו אח"כ עכ"ל המ"א והגר"א בביאוריו בסימן ש"א כתב דהדין הוא עם הרמב"ם כמו שכתב בראשונה עי"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The strings.</b> The wording of the Rambam is: one who finds tzitzis strings in the marketplace, even if they are cut and twisted, they are invalid, because a person troubles himself to make strings resembling tzitzis, and if so perhaps they were not made for the sake of tzitzis. The Maggid Mishneh disagrees, and the Kesef Mishneh also wrote that the Rambam later retracted this in a responsum. End quote from the Magen Avraham. The Vilna Gaon, in his commentary in siman 301, wrote that the law follows the Rambam as he wrote originally. See there.

(ז) שיאמר לאשה - ר"ל בתחלת הטוייה לבד. וה"ה אם האשה בעצמה אומרת שהיא טווה לשם ציצית דמהני וגם נאמנת על כך שאמרה משא"כ בעו"ג וחש"ו שאינם נאמנים. מומר לתיאבון שאין מטריח לעשות ציצית בבגדו מותר לטוות ומהימן כשאמר שטווה לשמה וכ"ש אם הוא עובר רק על שאר עבירות לתיאבון אבל מומר להכעיס אפיקורס הוא לכל התורה כולה ובודאי אדעתא דנפשיה עביד לכו"ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>That he should say to a woman.</b> This means only at the beginning of the spinning. The same applies if the woman herself says that she is spinning for the sake of tzitzis, for that is effective, and she is also believed that she said so. This is not so regarding a non-Jew, a deaf-mute, an insane person, or a minor, who are not believed. A mumar out of appetite, who does not trouble himself to put tzitzis in his garment, may spin, and he is believed when he says that he spun for their required purpose; all the more so if he violates only other transgressions out of appetite. But a mumar to provoke is an apikores regarding the entire Torah, and certainly acts with his own intention according to all views.

(יד) שזירה - היינו שיכפלם אחר הטווייה לשנים וישזרם ואם כפל כל חוט לשמונה ושזרו ג"כ לית לן בה. וטעם להשזירה דתנא בספרי ועשו להם ציצית שומע אני יעשה ציצית כמות שהוא ת"ל ונתנו על ציצית הכנף פתיל תכלת בטווי ושזור [פי' כעין משכן דהוי שזור א"נ דסתם פתילה היא שזורה] אין לי אלא תכלת לבן מנין אמרה תורה תן תכלת תן לבן מה תכלת טווי ושזור אף לבן טווי ושזור ועיין בביאור הלכה:

The reason for twisting is that it was taught in the Sifrei: and they shall make for themselves tzitzis. I might understand that he makes tzitzis as it is; therefore the verse states, and they shall place on the tzitzis of the corner a thread of techeiles, meaning spun and twisted, as in the Mishkan, which was twisted, or alternatively because an ordinary thread is twisted. I have this only for techeiles; from where do I know white? The Torah said, place techeiles and place white; just as techeiles is spun and twisted, so too white is spun and twisted. See Biur Halachah.

Magen Avraham wrote that even after the fact it is indispensable if they were not twisted for their required purpose, and this is also implied by the commentary of the Vilna Gaon. Some are lenient after the fact even without twisting. See Biur Halachah, where we explained that one should not rely on this. However, if the twisting was done without explicit specification, one may be lenient after the fact, since the spinning was for its required purpose and anyone who acts does so according to his original intention.

Likewise, if he made the hole higher than the measure of a thumb joint, and through the tying the garment folded until it is less than a thumb joint, it is valid, since in any event there is the required measure in the corner.

(66) <b>Only that they should be, etc.</b> As for what is stated in seif 13, that if he wound even one winding section, I will explain the root of the matter. Chazal received that we require in tzitzis a tied section, meaning that it be wound, as it is written, you shall make tied sections for yourself, and that it also have separate strings, as it is written, tzitzis, like the verse, by the locks of my head. But according to the Torah there is no measure for the length of the tied section. It is better then also to make the first knot before the winding section, as we practice, in order to satisfy the view of some poskim who hold that Chazal meant that this knot is according to the Torah.
