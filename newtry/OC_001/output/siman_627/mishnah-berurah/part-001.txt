**** ENGLISH ****
(15) <b>And one must be careful, etc.</b> - Meaning, although by law one need forbid only where there is a width of four tefachim, as above, nevertheless initially one should be careful even if it is not four tefachim wide, for there is concern that he may make many decorations until they have this measure and sit beneath them. According to this, one should not hang fruits, vessels, or similar items that are invalid for s'chach, even for decoration, except within four tefachim of the s'chach. Nevertheless, regarding hanging lamps in the sukkah opposite the table, one need not be stringent that they be within four tefachim; on the contrary, it is better and proper to distance them from the s'chach as much as possible.
