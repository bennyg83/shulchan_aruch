<b>אנשי אותה העיר</b>. ואם עירב א' מהעיר יכולין להוליכן עמו וה"ה אם נתנן לנכרי אבל אם מילאן נכרי אינו יכול להוליכן אלא למקום שכל אנשי העיר יכולין לילך (מ"מ ר"ן יש"ש):
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The residents of that city.</b> If one resident made an eruv techumin, the water may be taken along with him. The same applies if it was given to a non-Jew. If a non-Jew drew the water, however, it may be taken only to a place that all residents of the city may reach. See Maggid Mishneh, Ran, and Yam Shel Shlomo.

<b>קרן זויות</b>. בטור וברא"ש משמע הטעם דכשייחד לו קרן זויות לא קיבל שמירתן רק שא"ל הא ביתא קמך לכן הם כמוהו וכ"מ בגמ' דקאמר הכא הוא קבל נטירתא ועבח"מ סי' שצ"ח:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>A designated corner.</b> The Tur and Rosh imply that when he designated a corner for the owner's objects, he did not accept responsibility for guarding them, but merely told him, “The house is before you.” They are therefore considered as remaining under the owner's control. This also appears from the Gemara, which states that in the other case he accepted responsibility for guarding them. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 398.
