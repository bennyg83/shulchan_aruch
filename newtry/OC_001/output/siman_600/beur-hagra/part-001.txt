If it is because of doubt, it is like the other festival days of the Diaspora. Mi. Therefore it appears like the explanation of Rabbeinu Chananel, etc.; and one can say that this is the explanation, etc. See Rivash, siman 10, and see Tosafos, Menachos 100b, s.v.
