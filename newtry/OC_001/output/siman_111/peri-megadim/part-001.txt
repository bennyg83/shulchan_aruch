<b>לסמוך</b> עט״ז האריך ועב״ח ורש״ל יענך ה׳ הבטחה ול״ל קרא הא כל מצוה יש בו שכר ש״מ בשבת א״צ כו׳ וא״כ יש נ״מ בין פי׳ הטור בשבת סומך ולרש״ל לא (בש״ע בהג״ה נרשם טור אפשר לכוין זה ומ״מ ד״ע הוא בד״מ) ומ״ש הט״ז דמה ראיה מביא על סומך ולמ״ש בשם רש״ל א״ש נקיט סוף הקרא ונ״מ תרוייהו בשבת א״צ הא בחול צריך:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>To place immediately before.</b> See Taz, who discusses this at length, and see Bach and Maharshal. The verse “May Hashem answer you” is a promise. Why is a verse needed, since every mitzvah carries a reward? This indicates that on Shabbos there is no need to join redemption to prayer, etc. There is therefore a practical difference between the Tur’s explanation, according to which one does join them on Shabbos, and Maharshal’s explanation, according to which one does not. In the gloss to Shulchan Aruch, the Tur is cited, and perhaps this was the intended meaning. Nevertheless, it is Rama’s own ruling in Darkei Moshe. Regarding the Taz’s question as to what proof the verse provides for joining redemption to prayer, according to what was written in the name of Maharshal the matter is clear, since the end of the verse was cited. The practical difference between the two explanations is that on Shabbos it is unnecessary, whereas on weekdays it is required.

<b>והנה</b> בחידושינו ברכות מ״ב א׳ תיכף לגאולה כו׳ כתבנו כ״ב אמה ועתה ראיתי בפר״ח כ״ב ואמנם מסי׳ ס״ו ס״ח משמע דאין להפסיק ל״מ בדיבור א׳ כמו אמן אפי׳ בשתיקה להניח טלית אסור ועמ״ש שם ותיכף לנט״י כו׳ הא כדאיתא והא כדאיתא:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Now.</b> In our novellae to Berachos 42a, regarding “immediately after redemption,” we wrote twenty-two cubits, and I have now seen the same in Peri Chadash. Nevertheless, siman 66, seif 8, implies that one may not interrupt, not only with a single utterance such as Amen, but even silently to put on a tallis. See what I wrote there. As for the phrase “immediately after washing the hands,” each case follows its own law.

<b>חוץ</b> עט״ז בטור כתב כגאולה אריכתא ולפ״ז ש״ץ אין אומר בחזרה אבל המחבר בס״ב לשיטתיה כתפלה אריכתא. וכ״כ בכתבי מהר״א ק״י הביאו הב״י ומלת אפילו במנחה ומוסף א״י לכוין:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Except.</b> See Taz. The Tur writes that it is considered an extension of redemption. According to this, the chazzan should not say it during the repetition. The Mechaber in seif 2, however, follows his own view that it is considered an extension of prayer. This is also written in the responsa of Mahari, siman 110, cited by Beit Yosef. I do not understand the word “even” regarding Minchah and Mussaf.

<b>אבל</b> עט״ז ולפ״ז בשבת מניח טלית לכ״ע בין גאולה כו׳ עסי׳ ס״ו ס״ח ומצא ציבור מתפללין מתפלל עמהם דלא עדיף מערבית ומצאתי ב\א׳ר אות ד׳ בשם ל״ח כן וה״ה באיהש״ר וכדומה יע״ש אבל בי״ט צריך לסמוך כמו בחול כבסי׳ קי״ד ס״ב יע״ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>However.</b> See Taz. According to this, on Shabbos everyone agrees that one may put on a tallis between redemption and prayer. See siman 66, seif 8. If one finds the congregation praying, he should pray with them, for this is no worse than Maariv. I found the same in Eliyah Rabbah, os 4, in the name of Lechem Chamudos. The same applies to responding Yehei Shmei Rabba and the like. See there. On Yom Tov, however, one must join redemption to prayer as on a weekday, as explained in siman 114, seif 2. See there.
