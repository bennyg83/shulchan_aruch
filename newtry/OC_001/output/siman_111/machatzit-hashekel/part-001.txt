**** ENGLISH ****
(Section 3) in the Yot because this is a day. This is the reason for Hashem to trust salvation to prayer on Shabbat, because it is a day of trouble. This is the reason for trusting salvation to prayer because David finished Psalm 19, let it be to the will of the 20th and my redeemer, and next to it is Psalm 20, which begins, "The Hashem will reward you on the day of trouble, that is, to pray on the day of trouble."
