**** ENGLISH ****
And such is the custom's plain meaning — and this is in a place where there is no filth at all; but in a place of feces or filth it is forbidden to recite, whether Keriat Shema or other words of Torah. Nevertheless, one who wishes to conduct according to Ezra's enactment must immerse in forty se'ah of water — even drawn water is valid — or nine kav by pouring that is poured on him specifically, but not by immersion. And these nine kav combine even from three vessels — only that they must be poured consecutively one after another, that he pour from the second before the first has finished, and from four vessels in any case they do not combine (Beit Yosef). And he may immerse half and wash half, even though Ezra's enactment does not apply nowadays — it has practical bearing for one who accepts upon himself to conduct according to Ezra's enactment (Beit Yosef).
