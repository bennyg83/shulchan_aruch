(4) The custom — nevertheless there are men of deed who practice this enactment and immerse themselves for keri; and if immersion is difficult for them, they wash themselves with nine kav. Later authorities wrote that forty se'ah of drawn water are valid for this immersion, and likewise the nine kav is also in drawn water, even from three vessels. And in a manner that he does not interrupt pouring from one vessel until he begins the second; but more than three vessels do not combine for the measure of nine kav. And these nine kav must be poured on him, but not to immerse in them — whether in a vessel or on the ground, for whatever is the way of immersion we require specifically forty se'ah on the ground like other immersions. Nevertheless, if he entered half into a mikveh and did not bend his head to immerse the half of his body outside, only they poured on him from above nine kav — it helps to purify. Mateh Efrayim wrote: at the time of pouring the nine kav on the person, he should stand upright with his two hands placed against his heart, and he should not press them in a tight embrace — only with slight space so the water comes there too; and he should warn those pouring on him to pour directly on his body. And the measure of nine kav — see in Ba'er Heitev that it is the measure of fifteen Polish quarts; see in Mateh Efrayim cited that in our quart one needs twenty-four quarts for pouring nine kav; and in time of pressure when vessels do not hold so much, one may be lenient with eighteen quarts. And likewise one should be careful in all this for those who practice purification on erev Rosh Hashanah, and all the more by nine kav. Magen Avraham wrote: even one who practices immersion for keri specifically — healthy or sick who had marital relations; but a sick person who saw keri against his will is exempt from everything and need only wash that place so it is not soiled, on account of filth. And likewise a healthy person who saw separate drops is exempt from everything — for Ezra did not decree on this for words of Torah and prayer, only for terumah, which is impure on account of separate drops:
