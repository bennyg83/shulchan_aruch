**** ENGLISH ****
Magen Avraham, s.v. "He should eat." Beginning of siman 592. It should say 597; for he wrote there that the Maharshal did not eat fish on Rosh Hashanah because they were very dear to him; which is not so for one to whom they are not so dear: he should eat.
