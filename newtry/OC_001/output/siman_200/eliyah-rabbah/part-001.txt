**** ENGLISH ****
[2] <b>And he answers with them, etc.</b> Even one eating alone who is not reciting Bircas HaMazon with them answers, “Blessed is He of Whose food we have eaten.” Derashos Maharash. Sefer Chassidim, siman 900, records that one person was eating at a table where three others had eaten. They said to him, “Eat with us and hear Bircas HaMazon.” He replied, “You speak idle matters, while I eat quickly and immediately engage in Torah study.”
