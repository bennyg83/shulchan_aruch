If an error was found in a Torah scroll during the reading, they should take out another Torah scroll and begin [reading] from the place where the error was found, and complete the [number of] readers [i.e. aliyot] that are over [and above] those that were read [in the Torah] that had an error. This - that we take out another [sefer Torah] - is specifically when a complete error is found, but if was only because of missing or extra letters, we don't take out another [sefer Torah], since our Torah scrolls are not so precise so as to say that the other one would be more valid (Agur and Piskei Mahari"a - siman 80 and Ria"z). And Mahari"l ruled that another Torah scroll should not be brought, and Beit Yosef ruled that another Torah scroll must be brought (and therefore, we need the above compromise).
