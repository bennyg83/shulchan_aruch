<b>דין היאך מודדין אלפים אמה. ובו יא סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i>הבא למדוד אלפים אמה של תחום העיר אם היתה העיר <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="א"></i>אריכא וקטינא או שהיתה מרובעת <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ב"></i>לא לרבוע העולם הואיל ויש לה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ג"></i>ארבע זויות שוות מניחים אותה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ד"></i>כמות שהיא ומודדים לה אלפים אמה לכל רוח מארבע רוחותיה:
**** ENGLISH ****
When one comes to measure the 2000 amot teḥum of a city, if the city was long and narrow, or if it was square but not aligned with the square of the earth [i.e. the cardinal directions], but it has four square corners, we leave the size of the city as it is and measure 2000 amot in every direction from its four sides.

**** ENGLISH ****
If the city was round, we make corners for it and view it as if it was inscribed in a rectangle, and measure 2000 amot in every direction from the sides of the rectangle. It is thus found that the corners [of the circumscribed rectangle] increase the size of the city. So too, if the city was triangular or if it had many sides, we circumscribe a rectangle around it and afterwards extend 2000 amot in every direction from outside the rectangle.
