**** ENGLISH ****
(5) Downward toward the ground - Regarding those who raise their heads and eyes upward as though looking at the ceiling, the angels mock them. Sefer Chasidim, siman 18. Peri Megadim writes that one should not make an unusual movement. When praying alone he has discretion, but not in public, especially regarding raising his voice and the like. The later authorities write that anyone who does not close his eyes during the Amidah will not merit seeing the face of the Shechinah when his soul departs. However, if he prays from a siddur and keeps his eyes open in order to read from it, there is no concern. See above, siman 91, seif katan 6 in Mishnah Berurah.
