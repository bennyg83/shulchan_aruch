(א) Even etc. — behold, the essence of the mitzvah of enjoyment of Shabbat was explained to us through the prophets, as it is said "and you shall call Shabbat enjoyment"; and there are poskim who hold that its essence is from the Torah, for Shabbat is included in "holy convocations," as it is said "and on the seventh day — Shabbat, a complete rest, a holy convocation" etc., and "holy convocation" chazal explained in Sifra — that is, to sanctify it and honor it with clean clothing and enjoy it with enjoyment of eating and drinking; and chazal greatly emphasized this mitzvah [Shabbat 118] and said that whoever enjoys Shabbat is given inheritance without boundaries and saved from subjugation to kingdoms and merits wealth for this; and see there further several statements on this matter. And behold, within the boundary of this mitzvah there are three statements of chazal on this: (a) that which they said that one must enjoy it with large fish and heads of fat and a dish of beets — this was important food in their time, and so in every place according to its custom they should enjoy it with foods and drinks that are enjoyable to them [and because from the ordinary majority of people their main enjoyment is in meat and wine and delicacies, therefore it is in siman 250 seif 2 — he should increase in meat and wine and delicacies according to his ability]. (b) that which they said that even a small thing that he made for the honor of Shabbat fulfills the mitzvah of enjoyment of Shabbat, even a plate of harsana (meaning small fish fried in their oil). (c) that which R' Akiva said "make your Shabbat ordinary and do not need people." And the division of all these statements is in this manner — that is, for one who is able, he must honor it according to his ability; and one whose time is very pressed — that is, who has only food for two meals for Shabbat — on this R' Akiva said "make your Shabbat ordinary and do not need people," and he is not obligated neither for three meals nor for a plate of harsana; and one who has money enough to buy from this food for three meals and a little more is obligated to spend them on Shabbat in order that he fulfill three meals and a plate of harsana; and the same one who has nothing and is entirely dependent on charity — behold, the collectors are obligated to give him three meals and a plate of harsana at least; and for an honored person — everything according to his honor, as was explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 253. And we do not say on this "make your Shabbat ordinary and do not need people," for we do not say thus except for one who has not yet needed to take; but one who already reached this measure to stretch out his hand and take — they give him everything as mentioned [and in places where the collectors are accustomed to fix for the poor only two meals and not the third meal — they do not act properly; and at least in summer months they should certainly be careful about this]. And for this the Shulchan Aruch came to say that if he also has a little of his own, he must press himself to honor and enjoy Shabbat as is fitting, since it is possible for him and he has in hand to spend on this, and it does not suffice with a plate of harsana alone; and for this he concluded that he should restrict on other days from his expenses so that this remain for the honor of Shabbat. And see in Taz and Tosafos Shabbat and other later authorities who wrote that even one whose time is very pressed — who is exempt by law from a third meal and a plate of harsana — nevertheless it is very proper that he see to strive to be at least from the middle category — that is, in fulfillment of three meals and a plate of harsana:

(ב) לכבד את השבת - וטוב ליזהר שלא יפחות משני תבשילין גם טוב שיאכל בכל סעודה מג' סעודות דגים אם לא שאין נאותים לו לפי טבעו או ששונאן ושבת לעונג ניתן ולא לצער וכדלקמן בסי' רפ"ח אם מוכרי הדגים מייקרין השער נכון לתקן שלא יקנו דגים איזה שבתות עד שיעמוד השער על מקומו והנה בבה"ט הביא דלא יעשו תקנה רק אם הוסיפו המקח יתר על שליש מכמו שהיה מקדם אבל בא"ר ובפמ"ג כתבו דאף פחות משליש יוקר יש לעשות תקנה משום עניים. עוד כתב שם דאין בזה משום בטול מצות עונג דיש לענג השבת במאכלים אחרים. אם תקנו לאסור לאכול דגים כמה שבתות ואחד קנה מקודם מותר:
**** ENGLISH ****
(ב) To honor Shabbat — and it is good to be careful not to have less than two cooked dishes; also it is good that he eat at each meal of the three Shabbat meals fish, unless they are not suitable for him according to his nature or he hates them; and Shabbat is given for enjoyment and not for distress, as below in siman 288. If fish sellers raise the price, it is proper to enact that they not buy fish some Shabbats until the price returns to its place; and behold, in Be'er Heitev he brought that they should not make an enactment unless the purchase increased more than a third from what it was before; but in Eliyah Rabbah and Peri Megadim they wrote that even less than a third expense there is to make an enactment because of the poor. Further he wrote there that there is not in this nullification of the mitzvah of enjoyment, for one may enjoy Shabbat with other foods. If they enacted to forbid eating fish for several Shabbats and one bought beforehand — he is permitted:
