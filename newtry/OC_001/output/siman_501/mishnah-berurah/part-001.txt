{ב} אפי' אם היתה רעועה וכו' - דיכול להיות דמצפה מע"ש שתשבר ולא אקצה מ"מ אסור משום דעכ"פ בין השמשות לא היתה שבורה ותקועה בבנין ונאסרה ושוב אינה ניתרת ועוד דהויא נולד דמעיקרא קורה והויא ככלי והשתא עצים בעלמא ומטעם זה לא הגיה הרמ"א כאן כמו בסימן תצ"ה דיש מתירים מוקצה משום דסמך עצמו על מה שמסיים שם דאפי' המקילין לא רצו להקל במוקצה דנולד:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Even if it was weak, etc.</b> For it could be that from erev Shabbos he waits for it to break and did not set it aside; nevertheless it is forbidden, because in any case at twilight it was not broken and was fixed in the building and became forbidden, and it is not again permitted. And further, it is nolad, for originally it was a beam and was like a utensil, and now it is mere wood. And for this reason the Rama did not gloss here as in siman 495 that some permit muktzeh, because he relied on what he concludes there, that even the lenient ones did not wish to be lenient regarding muktzeh of nolad.

**** ENGLISH ****

And the meticulous are accustomed to be careful even on Chol HaMoed, preparing all the small wood needed for cooking fish before Yom Tov, for one can burn it without splitting. (And even to split large wood on Chol HaMoed itself, which one cannot cook with as it is, since it is possible to prepare before Yom Tov, one should prepare before Yom Tov unless he did not have it beforehand.) However, in a place where the price of wood is expensive and there is loss and something perishable if he burns them as they are, it is a mitzvah to cut them into fine pieces.
