**** ENGLISH ****
(ח) Unless the rim is, etc. — for then automatically the bucket with which he draws goes above ten where there is makom patur; if so he draws from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-yachid via makom patur. And specifically when the window is also ten high from the ground so the bucket never saw reshus harabbim air; and one standing in reshus harabbim lowering a bucket on a rope into a pit ten deep and four wide, filling water and taking out to reshus harabbim — liable; and even though on the bucket alone one need not make him liable since he did not take the rope from reshut ha-yachid when lowering to the pit — nevertheless regarding the water one should make him liable, for he uprooted the water from reshut ha-yachid to reshus harabbim; and even if around the pit there were not reshus harabbim but only karmelit, likewise one should be careful, for in any case there is prohibition. And a bucket hanging on a long tree as we make for our wells to draw, hanging in the pit or even above the pit opposite its air — even if water was in the bucket from before Shabbat without uprooting on Shabbat from the pit — nevertheless careful not to take it from there to lower on the ground around the pit, for he at least made resting on Shabbat; and even holding it in air around the pit Peri Megadim is uncertain if permitted:
