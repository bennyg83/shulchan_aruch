**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 8.</b> Regarding what Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 11, “and in Sotah 35 we say,” etc. Note: While studying this sugya, I found great difficulty with the Gemara’s question there: “But one may not perform mitzvos in bundles,” as we learned, “One may not give two sotahs to drink at once,” because one may not perform mitzvos in bundles. Why does the Gemara ask this? Let us say that the teaching there follows Rabbi Yehudah, and that this very point is the practical difference between the opinions. Rabbi Shimon explains that the reason is so that one woman should not become emboldened by the presence of the other. Accordingly, only there does this reason apply, while elsewhere, where this reason does not apply, one may perform mitzvos in bundles. Rabbi Yehudah says that this is not the reason, but rather because the verse states “her alone,” and not another with her, teaching that one may not perform mitzvos in bundles. We would therefore derive from that case to the entire Torah that mitzvos may not be performed in bundles, and the teaching that prohibits doing so would follow Rabbi Yehudah. This requires study. Examine it carefully.
