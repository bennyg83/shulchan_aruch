**** HEBREW ****
<small>(מ"א סק"ב.)</small> ועוד הנני יוסף להפליא. נ"ב ונראה דלק"מ דלא ס"ל הת"ה הך סברא ידוע כמו שהקשה המ"א גופי' דא"כ ה"ל לתקן שישיירו בר"ח ב' פסוקי' סמוך לפ' והוי ידוע לעולם אלא תי' ב' של תוס' וגם יש להם לחשוב וכו' וכ"ת הא צריכין לו' הסברא ידועה דאלת"ה ק' מפ' ויחל ומוהקרבתם י"ל דלדידן ל"ק דפ' ויחל הם ד' פסוקים אחר הפרשה והקרבתם ג' פסוקים כמ"ש המהרי"ל והמהרש"א:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham, seif katan 2.</small> “I further wonder.” Note: It appears that there is no difficulty. Terumas HaDeshen does not accept the rationale that the reading is universally known, as Magen Avraham himself objected that, if so, they should have established that on Rosh Chodesh two verses be left before the section break and the arrangement would become universally known. Rather, Terumas HaDeshen relies on Tosafos’ second answer, “They also should realize,” etc. One might ask that the rationale of being universally known is still needed, for otherwise there is a difficulty from Vayechal and Vehikravtem. This is no difficulty according to our texts, for Vayechal is four verses after the section break and Vehikravtem three verses after it, as Maharil and Maharsha write.
