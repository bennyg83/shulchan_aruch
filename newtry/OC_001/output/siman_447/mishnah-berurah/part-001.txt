Regarding chametz nuksheh during Pesach, some are stringent to forbid it in any amount, but the view of the Magen Avraham and many other later authorities is that since according to all opinions there is no kareis for it, and according to many poskim it is only a D’Rabbanan prohibition, it is nullified in sixty even during Pesach, and one may rely on them in pressing circumstances. Know that on the last day of Yom Tov, even though it too is D’Rabbanan, nevertheless since it was enacted because of doubt regarding the day, it is forbidden for eating through a mixture in any amount just like on Pesach, as is proven below in siman 467, seif 10.

Not nullified - since if moist became mixed its prohibition is in any amount, they were also stringent regarding it when dry. And therefore specifically when mixed during Pesach; but if mixed on erev Pesach until night, we hold above in seif 2 that its law is like other prohibitions - and therefore moist is nullified in noten ta'am like other prohibitions, and likewise dry is like other prohibitions and nullified by majority. Nevertheless, they must all be eaten before night, for otherwise we say it returns and revives on Pesach, as proven above in seif 4 in the gloss. Some wrote that Per the first view, dry in dry is not nullified specifically when the prohibition is from the chametz itself; but if the permitted item itself only absorbed a minute amount of chametz, such as matzah in which a leavened wheat kernel was baked and afterward became mixed with others - it is nullified one in two even during Pesach; but many Acharonim disagree.
