<b>The laws of a mixture of chametz during Pesach. It contains twelve se'ifim.</b><br>Chametz on Pesach forbids its mixture — whether of its own kind or not of its own kind — in any [minute] amount, even for benefit. {Rama: And one must burn it all; it does not suffice to redeem the value of the chametz and sell the rest (Mordechai, first chapter of Pesachim; Piskei Mahari, section 4; Responsa of Mahari Bruna). However, vessels in which [the mixture] was cooked are permitted after Pesach and do not require breaking or hag'alah (Tosafos, chapter "Kol Sha'ah").} And the law of its mixture is like the law of other mixtures, except that what forbids in other mixtures [when there is] less than sixty [times the volume] forbids in chametz in any amount; but if in other mixtures sixty were not required, but only peeling or removal of the [affected] place [sufficed], so too with chametz — for hot [touching] hot without sauce, peeling suffices, as with other prohibitions. And this is specifically in a place where "reicha" is relevant; for with other prohibitions it is of no consequence, as will be explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 108, with the help of Heaven.}
