<small>(במ"א ס"ק ב')</small> וכן משמע ברא"ש פ' כל הבשר סי' ט"ו דמקשה דמאי פריך הגמ' אחזותא מתני' היא ולא קא משני דקשאיל אי פסול כצ"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham, seif katan 2.</small> This is also implied by the Rosh, chapter Kol HaBasar, siman 15, who asks why the Gemara challenges the law of appearance when it is already taught in a Mishnah. He does not answer that the question was whether it is invalid. This is the correct reading.
