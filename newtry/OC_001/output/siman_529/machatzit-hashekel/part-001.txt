**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And it is impossible for Shabbos to fall on erev Sukkos, for if so the first day of Sukkos would be on Sunday.</b> And we hold "lo ADU Rosh." And also regarding erev Pesach (and see Chok Yaakov, siman 471), it likewise cannot be under discussion, for then it is impossible to fulfill the third meal with bread, as is written in siman 444. Necessarily, then, he is speaking specifically of erev Shavuos. And upon this the Rama wrote: and it is forbidden to eat, etc., as on erev Shabbos. Therefore Magen Avraham asked: regarding erev Shabbos it is explained in siman 249 that it is permitted until the time of Mincha Ketanah. And if so, one can eat the third meal at a permitted time, namely after the time of Mincha Gedolah and before the time of Mincha Ketanah, and then one can eat more than a little bread.

<b>עסי' רצ"א.</b> ושם כ' מ"א ס"ק ח' דאם חל י"ט במ"ש מ"מ אם יודע שיהיה יוכל לאכול קודם שעה עשירית לא יחלק סעודת שחרית לשנים כדי לצאת בזה ידי סעודה ג' כמ"ש שם. וכ"מ סי' קפ"ח. שכתב אם שכח יעלה ויבא בבהמ"ז אם הוא ראש חודש א"צ לחזור ולברך ברהמ"ז דהא אי בעי לא אכל פת אבל אם שכח יעלה ויבא בבהמ"ז ביו"ט צריך לחזור ולברך דבי"ט חייב לאכול פת. וסתם ולא חילק בין סעודת לילה לסעודת יום אלמא דבין ביום ובין בלילה צריך פת:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>See siman 291.</b> And there Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 8 that if Yom Tov falls on Motza'ei Shabbos, nevertheless if he knows that he will be able to eat before the tenth hour, he should not split the morning meal into two in order to fulfill thereby the third meal, as is written there. And so it appears from siman 188, which wrote that if he forgot Yaaleh VeYavo in Birkas HaMazon: if it is Rosh Chodesh he need not go back and recite Birkas HaMazon, for if he wished he need not have eaten bread; but if he forgot Yaaleh VeYavo in Birkas HaMazon on Yom Tov, he must go back and recite the blessing, for on Yom Tov one is obligated to eat bread. And he stated it without distinguishing between the night meal and the day meal; hence both by day and by night one needs bread.

<b>(ס"ק ג) על היין כו'</b> ע"ש בתוס' הטעם והטעם פסח לפי שהוא יום גאולה וחירות. ועצרת משום דאפי' לר"א בפסחים דף ס"ח ע"ב דפליג על ר"י וס"ל דברצון אדם תלי' אם ירצה יהיה כל היום לה' דהיינו ללמוד או להתפלל כל היום או כלו לכם. דהיינו לאכול כל היום. מסקי' שם דבעצרת כ"ע מודים דבעינן נמי לכם. דהיינו שצריך לאכול ולשמוח בר"ה לפי שהוא תחלת השנה עושים כן לסי' טוב. ושמיני עצרת לפי שהוא חשוב והוא רגל בפ"ע. אבל י"ט א' של סוכות לא משום דהעם טרודים במצות סוכה וד' מינים ואין להם פנאי להכין הרבה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(s.k. 3) Upon the wine, etc.</b> See there in Tosafos for the reason. And the reason for Pesach is that it is a day of redemption and freedom. And for Atzeres: because even according to Rabbi Elazar in Pesachim 68b, who disagrees with Rabbi Yehoshua and holds that it depends on a person's will: if he wishes, the whole day may be for Hashem, namely to learn or to pray the whole day, or entirely "for you," namely to eat the whole day, they conclude there that regarding Atzeres everyone agrees that we also require "for you," namely that one must eat and rejoice. And on Rosh Hashana, because it is the beginning of the year, they do so for a good omen. And Shemini Atzeres, because it is important and it is a festival unto itself. But the first day of Yom Tov of Sukkos is not [included], because the people are busy with the mitzvos of sukkah and the four species and do not have leisure to prepare much.
