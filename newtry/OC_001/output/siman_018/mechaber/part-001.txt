**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The time [for] <i>tzitzis</i> — it contains three clauses.</b><br>Night is not the time for <i>tzitzis</i>, for we exclude “and you shall see them.” According to the Rambam, whatever one wears at night is exempt even if designated for day; what one wears by day is obligated even if designated for night. According to the Rosh, a garment designated for night is exempt even if worn by day; a garment designated for day or for day and night is obligated even if worn at night. Rema: With doubt in blessings we are lenient — therefore one does not bless except when wearing by day a garment also designated for day (Hagahos Maimoniyos). After <i>Maariv</i> even while still daytime one does not bless (<i>Piskei Maharai</i> siman 221). On Yom Kippur night wrap while still day and bless (<i>Tashbetz</i>).

**** ENGLISH ****
When does one bless on <i>tzitzis</i> in the morning? From when one can distinguish its <i>tekheles</i> from its white. If one wears it from <i>alos hashachar</i> onward some say one blesses — and such is the practice (Mordechai Megillah ch. 2). If one wore earlier, e.g. at <i>Slichos</i>, one does not bless on it; when day breaks one handles it and blesses (<i>Tashbetz</i>).
