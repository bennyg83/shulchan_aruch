**** HEBREW ****
<small>מג"א סק"ג </small>הלכך חוששין שמא לא יועיל. עדיין ק' הא מלאכה גמורה אפי' בס' דבר האבד מותר כמ"ש המג"א רסי' תקל"ז וכ"ש טורח בעלמא ודוחק לחלק בין ספק אם בלא מלאכתו יהי' אבד וע"י מלאכתו יועיל בוודאי דזה מותר אבל בוודאי אבד והספק אם יועיל במלאכתו וצ"ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
Magen Avraham, s.k. 3. Therefore we are concerned that perhaps it will not help. Still it is difficult: for even a full melacha is permitted regarding a matter of impending loss, as the Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of siman 537, and all the more so mere exertion; and it is forced to distinguish between a doubt whether without his melacha it would be lost, while through his melacha it will certainly help (for that is permitted), versus a case where loss is certain and the doubt is whether his melacha will help; and this requires further study.
