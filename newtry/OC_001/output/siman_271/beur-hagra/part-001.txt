<b>That initially.</b> These are the words of the Rama. See Rashba, responsum 752, who explains that one who washed his hands should not recite kiddush, because it appears that he belittles kiddush; had he intended to recite kiddush over wine, he would not have washed both hands, for one who washes for fruit, etc. The statement that sometimes bread is dearer does not appear as belittling, because perhaps his intent was to recite kiddush over bread.
