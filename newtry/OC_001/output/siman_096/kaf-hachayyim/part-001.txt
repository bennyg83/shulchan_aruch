He said that he would not be able to do so with him, and that he would not be saved from his hands
His opinion is settled on him in his fall, and he makes a plea that he will fall and throw him in his leg and a full bowl that will not be scattered, and that if he falls, he will be fed up
It means that the other things that do not have a loss if they fall away from their hands are allowed to pray while in his hand, but the Lord Jonah wrote in the Book of My Name, Daesh, and the Cave of Hashem’s Word

This is the meaning of Hashem’s Word, and the Word of Hashem’s Word says, “And the Lord’s Word is revealed to him.”
A lawyer, and as such, is written in the words of the Lord, and he has agreed to the High Court, and as such, he has been given to the Lord, and he has agreed to the High Court, and as such, he is, and as such, he is, the Lord, and the Lord, and he is the same as the Lord’s

Nothing is in his hand, and as such is the case of Hashem’s promise, “He will not give him anything in his hand when he prays, except for him.”
