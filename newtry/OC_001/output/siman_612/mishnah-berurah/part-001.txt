(א) ככותבת הגסה - אע"ג דבכל איסורי התורה משערינן בכזית כל זה היכי דכתיבי אכילה משא"כ ביו"כ דלא כתיב אכילה אלא מנע הכתוב את האכילה בלשון עינוי כדכתיב הנפש אשר לא תעונה וקים להו לחכמים דבפחות מככותבת לא מייתבא דעתיה כלל והרי הוא רעב ומעונה כבתחלה ועיין בה"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
(1) Like a large date - Although with all prohibitions of the Torah we measure by an olive's bulk, all that is where the term eating is written. This is not so with Yom Kippur, where eating is not written; rather, Scripture withheld eating in the language of affliction, as it is written, "the soul that will not be afflicted." The Sages had a tradition that with less than a date's bulk his mind is not settled at all, and he remains hungry and afflicted as before. See Biur Halacha.

(3) And this measure is equal, etc. - For the Sages had a tradition that with this measure every person's mind is settled, whether a little or a lot, and with less than this no person's mind is settled. Now, the reason the Mechaber taught us the measure for liability, even though it is forbidden even in any amount, as below in se'if 5, is because this has practical relevance for the matter mentioned in siman 618 regarding a sick person, whom we feed less than the measure. Therefore we need to know the measure of liability.
