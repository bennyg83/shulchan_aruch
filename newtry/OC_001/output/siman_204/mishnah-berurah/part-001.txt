**** ENGLISH ****
(ט) The addition, etc. — for although it grows from the ground, nevertheless we do not bless on it even borei peri haAdamah but shehakol, for people do not plant the tree with intent to eat the korah (palm heart) when it is soft, for it diminishes the branches of the tree — only that it will harden and become wood and grow fruit. Taz wrote that wet legumes in garden [plots] that are sown with intent to eat them raw — one blesses borei peri haAdamah even on the shoots (sharvitin) alone, for with that intent they are sown; but field [legumes] whose custom is to leave them until they dry and eat them cooked — one blesses on the legumes when they are raw, even when they are wet, shehakol; and if one cooked them, borei peri haAdamah. And Peri Megadim is very doubtful of his words, and likewise is the view of many latter authorities that one should not distinguish between legumes and legumes — and one always blesses on them when they are wet borei peri haAdamah, and such is the custom of the world [Shelah]. However, regarding the shoots there are opinions among the latter authorities: when one eats them alone without the legumes — whether one blesses on them borei peri haAdamah or shehakol; therefore l'chatchila it is good to be careful to eat them with the legumes and bless on the legumes alone borei peri haAdamah and not bless again on the shoots; and if it happened that one ate the shoots alone — bless shehakol:
