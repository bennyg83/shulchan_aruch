**** ENGLISH ****
(42) And he drank water. This applies specifically to water, from which the palate derives pleasure only when one drinks because of thirst. If, however, he drinks another beverage or eats a piece of bread, from which the palate derives pleasure, then even if he drinks or eats it only to dislodge the obstruction, he must recite both a first and an after-blessing, as explained below in seif 8. If one drinks water in the morning for medicinal purposes, he should not recite a blessing. If he is also thirsty, he should recite a blessing. Yaavetz, siman 210.
