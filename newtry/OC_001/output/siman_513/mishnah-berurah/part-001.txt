Accordingly, if he brought it on Sunday before dawn, where there is no concern lest it was laid at night, and a non-Jew speaks casually that it was laid on erev Shabbos, it is permitted; for even if you say it was laid on Shabbos, its prohibition is only rabbinic, and in a rabbinic matter one who speaks casually is believed.
