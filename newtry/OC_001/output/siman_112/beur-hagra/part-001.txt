**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 1, specifically, etc.</b> Therefore Yaaleh VeYavo is recited in the blessing of Avodah, as stated in the fourth chapter of Berachos, the second chapter of Shabbos, and the second chapter of Beitzah. Likewise, Zochreinu is recited as stated in Maseches Soferim. See Tosafos there, beginning “Kel,” etc. Al HaNissim is likewise recited in the blessing of thanksgiving, as stated in the second chapter of Shabbos. According to Maseches Soferim it is said in the blessing of the Divine Name. See Maseches Soferim, chapter 19, halachah 7. In the fourth chapter of Rosh Hashanah, sanctification of the Divine Name is recited and Malchiyos is included with it, etc.
