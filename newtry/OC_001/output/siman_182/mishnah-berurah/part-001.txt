(32) At time of pressure—no other cup nothing to fix this cup; teaches not better bless without cup entirely; defective matter only l'chatchila if can be careful not for delay; Acharonim when must bless on defective cup if has small cup holding revi'it pour from large into it thereby somewhat fixes defect; some fix defect with bread crumb—Tur rejected custom ineffective; if not defective only not full and contents have revi'it measure all agree filling helps even via bread specifically one piece—for many there is repulsiveness bring it near etc.; on contrary must rinse cup from crumbs as below siman 183 s.1 (Acharonim).
