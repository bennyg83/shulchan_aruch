(1) (c) He will take care the whole year - lest they accept his fast and show him that the Omnipresent has no interest in his uses and look at the latter who wrote that it was precisely when it was not because of excessive eating and drinking or because of reflection and the like, but when he knows that it came because of reflection and the like, even if his punishment was great, he has no doubt that he will die because he himself caused it and also if it cost him a year, it is not guaranteed that he is a son of Abob. 6. In that he will be strengthened from here on and in the days to come in the study of the Torah and the right of the Torah will be rebuked for him and should be comforted in the midrash if a person commits a sin and commits his death to Heaven what will he do and live if he learns to change one chapter there are two chapters and if he learns to change one page there are two pages. [And if he does not know how to study, he will concern himself with the measure of charity and kindness and live as it is said that he who pursues charity and kindness will find a life of righteousness and honor in the Midrash] And the main thing of learning the Torah will be to keep the learner and it is not convenient for him that he was not created like Ahzal, nor will there be a blessing to show him HC:
