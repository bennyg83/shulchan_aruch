**** ENGLISH ****
(There, s.k. 2.) End quote; and this requires further study, for if so why does he say, etc. Marginal note: it appears to me that the Kesef Mishneh holds as Magen Avraham wrote at the end of his words in the name of the Maharit, that one does not create a double doubt by one's own hands; therefore he holds one should join to this the reasoning that a small amount is [only] what his household needs, and although that reasoning alone does not help (for even regarding one egg we are concerned), nevertheless this reasoning helps that one does create a double doubt by one's own hands; and there is no difficulty.
