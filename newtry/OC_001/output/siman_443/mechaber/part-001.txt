**** ENGLISH ****
Status of chametz on Pesach Eve after sixth. Contain 3 sections: Chametz from the sixth hour and on on the fourteenth is prohibited to benefit from and the Sages proscribed two hours previously, for from the start of the fifth hour and within it is permitted to benefit and permissable to sell it to gentiles even many at once, because we certainly do not eat it before Passover, though you can feed it to wild animals and birds, and only if you watch over them lest they be deterred from it and they eat what they have measured. And from the start of the sixth hour and on they proscribed it even in benefit. {Rama: (even chametz of gentiles is prohibited to benefit from) (Tur and Rivash) Rema And in a leap year when the day is long these are four hours according to the nature of the day, and it is permitted to eat chametz until a third of the way through the day. And some say until two hours before noon.}
