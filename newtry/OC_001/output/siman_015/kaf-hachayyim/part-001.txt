<b>א) [סעיף א'] מותר להתיר ציציות וכו'  </b>ואפי' מטלית חדש לטלית חדש, וכ"ש מטלית ישן לטלית חדש. ב"ח. ברכ"י או' א' ואו' ב' וכן מותר להתיר ציציות מטלית גדול ליתנם בטלית קטן, אה"ח בשם תשי' בית יהידה חיו"ד סי' כ"ט והביאו פתחי עולם אות א' אבל אין להתיר מבגד של גדול דחיובו מה"ת ולתתם בשל קטן דחיובו רק מדרבנן ה' מלבי"ם, והביאו עמודי השלחן על קיצור הש"ע סי' ט' אות ך' וכ"כ מ"ב בשם אה"ח והביאו פתחי עולם שם. ועיין לקמן אות י"ג מ"ש בשם העו"ת
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1) [Se'if 1] It is permitted to remove tzitziyos, etc.</b> This is permitted even from a new tallis for another new tallis, and certainly from an old tallis for a new one. Bach; Birkei Yosef, paragraphs 1 and 2. It is likewise permitted to remove tzitziyos from a tallis gadol and place them on a tallis katan. Archos Chaim, citing Responsa Beis Yehudah, Yoreh Deah, siman 29; Pischei Olam, paragraph 1. One may not, however, remove them from an adult's garment, whose obligation is D'Oraisa, and place them on a minor's garment, whose obligation is only rabbinic. The Malbim; Amudei HaShulchan on Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 9, paragraph 20. Mishnah Berurah likewise writes this in the name of Archos Chaim, and Pischei Olam cites it there. See below, paragraph 13, for what we wrote in the name of Olas Tamid.

<b>3) It is permitted to remove tzitzis, etc.</b> This applies specifically when one unties the tzitzis from this garment and then ties and winds them again on another garment. It is forbidden, however, to cut the garment and remove the tzitziyos intact, as they are tied and wound, and attach them to another garment by suspending them with a thread through the hole or the like. It is likewise forbidden to insert the ends at the place where the four strings are doubled into eight through the hole, then pass the loose strings and wound section through them and suspend them by means of a loop. This is unlike the first Rabbeinu Yeshayah, in Hilchos Tzitzis in Sefer Sam Chaim, folio 14b, who permits this, for he stands alone in this matter. Rather, the law follows Rashi, Menachos 41a, and those who agree with him, who hold that this constitutes ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy. This is also the consensus of the later rabbis. Lev Chaim, siman 96, writes this and expresses surprise that Birkei Yosef cites the first Rabbeinu Yeshayah without qualification in Shiyurei Berachah and does not mention that his view opposes all the poskim. The same follows from Tosafos, Menachos 42a, s.v. "ve'avik lehu meivak," unlike the first Rabbeinu Yeshayah. Tosafos writes: It appears to me that he would pass at most seven strings through the loop's opening, leave the eighth outside, and tie with it. Otherwise one could transfer completed tzitzis from a tallis through the loop, and shaatnez in tzitzis would never be possible. This implies that whenever completed tzitzis can be transferred from the tallis through the loop, their attachment is not considered a binding to the tallis, because the required binding must be one in which the prohibition of shaatnez is possible. This is unlike the first Rabbeinu Yeshayah. See Rav Pe'alim, volume 3, siman 1. It appears that he did not see this Tosafos. His comparison there of the tzitzis windings and knots to writing a mezuzah also differs from Birkei Yosef, siman 19, paragraph 2. Birkei Yosef compares spinning and twisting the tzitzis to writing the mezuzah, while fixing the tzitzis strings to the garment corresponds to affixing the mezuzah to the house. This law therefore remains subject to dispute, and one must be concerned about wearing a four-cornered garment without tzitzis. According to Tosafos, this is not considered a binding to the garment because it does not resemble shaatnez; one must further be concerned that it constitutes ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy, according to the plain meaning of Rashi and those who agree with him, as Lev Chaim writes. Even according to Taz and Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1, each of whom interprets Rashi differently, one can say that Rashi's words contain both their interpretations and the disqualification of ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy. We should not permit prohibitions on the basis of strained interpretations. Understand this.

<b>ד) שם,  </b>מותר להתיר ציציות וכו' היינו דווקא בשאין ציצית מצויין בעיר הא יש ציצית מצויין אין להתיר ציצית מבגד לבגד מאחר דאפשר לחנות בזול בקל ציציות אחרות, פרמ"ג במש"ז אות ב' לב חיים סי' צ"ה סידור בי"ע אות ה' פתחי עולם שם. ועוד כתב הפרמ"ג במש"ז סי' י"ט אות א' טעם אחר כדי לחוש נמי למ"ד כלי קופסא חייבין בציצית. יעו"ש ובדברנו לשם אות ב':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>4) There: It is permitted to remove tzitziyos, etc.</b> This applies specifically when tzitziyos are unavailable in the city. If tzitziyos are available, one should not remove them from one garment for another, since other tzitziyos can readily be purchased at little cost. Pri Megadim, Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 2; Lev Chaim, siman 95; Siddur Beis Oved, paragraph 5; Pischei Olam there. Pri Megadim in Mishbetzos Zahav to siman 19, paragraph 1, gives another reason: to take account of the opinion that garments stored in a chest are obligated in tzitzis. See there and our discussion there, paragraph 2.

<b>6) If one string was severed,</b> even if enough remains to tie a bow and it would have been valid had it remained on the corner, Maran writes in Beis Yosef that it is invalid for placement on another garment; see there. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 3, writes: Although Maran omitted this law from Shulchan Aruch, which somewhat implies that he was uncertain, the law is true and Maran's explanation is firmly correct. This is especially so now that Rabbi Betzalel cites it in the name of a marginal gloss from an early rabbi. The words of Rashi, the Rosh, and Rabbeinu Yerucham, who require the strings to be whole, likewise prove it, and Rif cites it. Birchas HaZevach also writes that the law is true, and Taz, Magen Avraham, and Eliyah Rabbah record it. It appears that Maran's statement that it is invalid for placement on another garment refers only to the initial practice, as implied by Rabbi Betzalel's wording cited by Birkei Yosef; Birkei Yosef equates Rabbi Betzalel's wording with Maran's view. The same follows from Taz to siman 12, paragraph 3. Magen Avraham to this siman, paragraph 1, explicitly writes that placing it on another garment is forbidden only initially. Olas Tamid, paragraph 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2; Pri Megadim, Mishbetzos Zahav to siman 12, paragraph 3; Shaliach Tzibbur, folio 109b. Regarding Shaliach Tzibbur's statement that Magen Avraham validates it after the fact if less than enough to tie a bow remains, see Zechor LeAvraham, volume 3, paragraph 90. Siddur Beis Oved, paragraph 2; Shesilei Zeitim to siman 12, paragraph 7. This is also implied by Chayei Adam, rule 11, paragraph 20, unlike Machatzis HaShekel.
