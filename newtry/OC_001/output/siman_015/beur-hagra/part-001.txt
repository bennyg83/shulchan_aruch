<b>וי"א דלר"ע כו'. </b>וס' ראשונה בשם ר"ע הוא הרא"ש וסובר הש"ע דפליגי וליתא דע"כ גם להרא"ש משום תולמ"ה כמש"ש מ' ב' ועוד דתפירה ודאי הוי חיבור כמש"ש מ"א א' ושוין שאם כפלה כו' ושם ל"ז ב' האי מאן דחייטיה כו' אם איתא כו' הלא"ה שפיר עביד וכ"כ ט"ז ע"ש ועל רש"י ל"ק דהוא פי' דחוט התפירה פסול משום תולמ"ה וכן פי' שם מ"ב ב' מן הגרדין כו' ג"כ משום תולמ"ה ור"ל שלא קצצן אלא הניחן בבגד וס"ל לרב דלא בעינן לשמה כלל ולהכי בסוכה ט' פריך משמואל ולא מרב ומפרש שגם רבא שם מ' ב' ס"ל כרב ואזיל לשיטתו דפי' פלוגתא דאביי ורבא בסנהדרין מ"ח דרבא כרבנן ואמרי' כאן מ"ב ב' כתנאי כו' אבל תוס' לא ס"ל הא דרש"י כמש"ש מ' ב' ד"ה שמא כו' אלא כר"ע ושם מ"ב ב' ד"ה הקוצים ואזלי לשיטתייהו דמפרש שם ד"ה עד דרבא כרשב"ג וקי"ל שם כפי' תוס' לכן פי' תוס' ור"ע עיקר וכ"כ ב"י שהעיקר כפי' ר"ע אלא שהתוס' מפרשים נקרע חוץ לשלש שנשתייר ג"ט באורך הבגד שלא נקרע ונ"י בשם ר"ע פי' שהקרע חוץ לג' ברוחב הבגד וכן י"ל דברי הרא"ש דלא כב"י ודבריו אין מובנים דהא כ' בב"י וש"ע בשם רי"ו דלפי' הרא"ש בשם ר"ע אם נשתייר כ"ש כשר ואח"כ כ' שדברי הרא"ש ורי"ו כפי' תוס' אבל לפי' ניחא:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>"And some say that according to Rabbi Akiva, etc."</b> The first opinion in the name of Rabbi Akiva is the Rosh, and the Shulchan Arukh understands that they disagree. But this is not so, for even according to the Rosh it must be because of the rule of "you shall make," and not from what is already made, as is stated there, 40b. Furthermore, sewing is certainly considered a connection, as is stated there, 41a: "And they agree that if he folded it, etc.," and there, 37b: "One who sewed it, etc.; if it were so, etc." Otherwise he acted properly. The Taz wrote likewise; see there.

There is no difficulty from Rashi, for he explained that the sewing thread is invalid because of "you shall make," and not from what is already made. He likewise explained there, 42b, "from the remnants, etc.," also because of that rule, meaning that he did not cut them off but left them in the garment. Rashi holds that according to Rav we do not require that the tzitzit be made for their own sake at all; therefore in Sukkah 9 the Gemara challenges from Shmuel and not from Rav. He also explains that Rava there on 40b holds like Rav, following his own view, for he explained the dispute of Abaye and Rava in Sanhedrin 48 as Rava following the Rabbis; and we say here, 42b, "It is a dispute of Tannaim, etc."

But Tosafot do not accept Rashi's explanation, as is clear there, 40b, in the comment beginning "Perhaps, etc." Rather, they explain like Rabbi Akiva, and likewise there, 42b, in the comment beginning "The thorns." They follow their own view, for they explain there in the comment beginning "Until" that Rava follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. We rule there according to Tosafot's explanation; therefore the explanation of Tosafot and Rabbi Akiva is primary. The Beit Yosef also wrote that the primary explanation is that of Rabbi Akiva. However, Tosafot explain "it tore outside three" to mean that three thumbbreadths remained along the length of the garment that did not tear, while the Nimukei Yosef in the name of Rabbi Akiva explains that the tear was outside three across the width of the garment. The words of the Rosh can also be explained this way, unlike the Beit Yosef. The Beit Yosef's words are not understandable, for in the Beit Yosef and Shulchan Arukh he wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham that according to the Rosh's explanation in the name of Rabbi Akiva, if even any amount remained, it is valid; and afterward he wrote that the words of the Rosh and Rabbeinu Yerucham are like the explanation of Tosafot. But according to this explanation it is settled.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>"There is room to doubt."</b> This applies according to all the explanations in the Beit Yosef. That is to say: according to the Rosh and Rashi, even though if any amount remained it is valid according to the Rosh, as above, and here the entire garment is intact, nevertheless the place where the tzitzit are inserted is considered as if it were cut. Likewise according to Rashi, perhaps because of the concern mentioned above it is invalid even after the fact. This is also explicit in the words of the Beit Yosef there, where he wrote: "And if it tore and some of it remained, etc., according to Rashi, etc.," because of the concern "lest he leave, etc."; otherwise not. See Magen Avraham, subsection 12. There is a scribal error in his words, and it should read: "This is according to Rashi and the Rosh; but according to the Nimukei Yosef, etc."
