**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(s.k. 9) Not, etc.</b> Therefore I was precise and wrote "and the people practiced," which implies that the people practiced thus of their own accord. And so it appears to me that the custom of the people, which they instituted of themselves, is as proper practice.
