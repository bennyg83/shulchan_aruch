**** ENGLISH ****
Order of Prayers for the Second Day and Chol HaMoed (Intermediate Days) of Passover (Nine Sections): On the second day of Yom Tov the Torah reading is from Parashat Emor, starting from "שור או כשב" (An ox or a sheep) until the end of the section and the Maftir reading is the same as on the first day. The Haftarah is from Melachim Bet (Kings II), from "וישלח המלך" (And the king sent) until "ואחריו לא קם כמוהו" (And after him, none arose like him), recounting the Passover of King Josiah.

**** ENGLISH ****
On the third day, which is Chol HaMoed, at Maariv, Shacharis, and Minchah he prays in the usual manner and says Yaaleh VeYavo in Retzei. If he omitted it, he must repeat the Amidah. He likewise mentions it in Birkas HaMazon, but if he omitted it, he does not repeat Birkas HaMazon. At Mussaf he prays as he does for Mussaf on Yom Tov. <small>Rama: When he reaches “through Moshe Your servant, as stated,” he says, “You shall offer an elevation offering,” through “and the two daily offerings according to their law.” Tur.</small>

**** ENGLISH ****
The mnemonic for the Torah readings of the eight days of Pesach is: Mashach, Tora, Kadesh, BeKaspa, Pesal, BeMidbara, Shelach, Bechora. This order changes only when Pesach begins on Thursday. The third day is then Shabbos, and the reading is “See, You say to me,” which is Pesal; on Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday the readings are Kadesh, BeKaspa, and BeMidbara.

When Shabbos falls on Chol HaMoed, at Maariv, Shacharis, and Minchah he prays the usual Shabbos Amidah and says Yaaleh VeYavo in Retzei.
