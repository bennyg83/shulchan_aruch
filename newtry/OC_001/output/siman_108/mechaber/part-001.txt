One Who Did Not Pray Due To A Mistake, Or An Extenuating Circumstance, Or On Purpose. Containing 12 S'ifim If one erred or was forced [by circumstance] and did not pray the morning prayer, one should pray the afternoon prayer twice: the first is the afternoon prayer, and the second as a make-up.
