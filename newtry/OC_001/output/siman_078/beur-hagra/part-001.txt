<b>ס"א אפי' כו' כיון כו'. </b>עתוס' ד"ה ממתין כו' וכיון שעומד כו' וראיה מדל"ק שם מרחיק כמש"ש בצואה הרשב"א ודעת הטור דה"ה לק"ש והג"מ כ' דאף בתפלה צריך לכסות בבגדים או להרחיק כשיעור והכריע כדעת התוס' בתפלה אבל בק"ש כדעת הג"מ כיון דיכול להלוך וז"ש כיון כו' ואם כו':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 1, even, etc., since, etc.</b> See Tosafos, s.v. "he waits," etc., and "since he is standing," etc. The proof is that it does not say there that he must distance himself, as it states there regarding excrement. This is the view of the Rashba. The Tur maintains that the same law applies to Krias Shema. Hagahos Maimoniyos writes that even during the Amidah he must cover it with garments or distance himself by the prescribed measure. The Mechaber rules like Tosafos regarding the Amidah, but regarding Krias Shema he rules like Hagahos Maimoniyos, since he is able to walk away. This is the meaning of "since," etc., and "if," etc.
