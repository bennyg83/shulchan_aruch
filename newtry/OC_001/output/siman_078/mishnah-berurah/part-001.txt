(ה) Amot — or waits until absorbed in the ground for the measure explained below siman 82 — as it should read. And Magen Avraham wrote that although it is possible that through waiting for absorption he will need to delay long enough to finish all of it and need to return to the beginning — nevertheless one need not be concerned for this in Keriat Shema, for it is like reading the Torah; but if it happened thus in the blessing of Keriat Shema, it is better he distance from them and not wait for absorption in the ground; and after the fact if he delayed in the blessing of Keriat Shema long enough to finish all of it, he does not return to the beginning, as above in siman 65 in Mishnah Berurah:
