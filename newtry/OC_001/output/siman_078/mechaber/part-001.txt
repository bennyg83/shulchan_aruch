**** ENGLISH ****
One who needed to urinate at the time of reading. Containing one seif: If he was reading and his urine began dripping onto his knees, he stops until the water finishes and returns to read; even if it fell on his garments and there is a moist spot intending to become dry — since they are covered by his garment. And if urine fell on the ground, he distances four amot from them: {Rama: Or for the measure that will be explained below siman 82; or waits until they are absorbed in the ground (R' Yehudah chapter 2 of Berakhot); and even if he delayed long enough to finish all of it he need not return except to the place he stopped.} N.B.: And some say if he delayed long enough to finish all of it he returns to the beginning; and so is the essence, as I wrote above siman 65; and they estimate per the reader:
