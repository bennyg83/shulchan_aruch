<b>They flog.</b> Meaning makkas mardus. And Mahara'i wrote in the annotations on Rashi at the end of Parashas Ki Seitzei that it is thirteen lashes; see there; and from the Rambam it does not appear so (Magen Avraham). And see Rashi in Chullin, folio 141b, who wrote that makkas mardus has no fixed measure; see there. And see Kenesses HaGedolah, and in Magen Avraham, and in Yad Aharon.
