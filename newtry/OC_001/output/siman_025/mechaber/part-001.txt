If one moved them from their place with the intent to replace them immediately, he still must recite the blessing. [If] one was putting on the arm tefillin and recited the blessing, and as he was tightening it, the knot severed and he has to form a new knot, however, he did not take his mind off [of the tefillin] - he does not have to recite the blessing again. If the arm tefillin became loosened before putting on the head tefillin, he should tighten it, and he does not need to recite the blessing again. But if he put on the head tefillin and then the arm tefilling became loosened, then he should tighten it and recite the blessing [again]. It is permitted to recite the blessing on borrowed tefillin, but not on stolen ones. {Rama: (Rem"a: If he returned [only] one of them [back to their proper place], he recites the blessing like one who one only puts on one tefilla - as seen below in siman 26 [this is the Rem"a's own opinion].) Rem"a: There are those who say you should not recite a blessing [Tur - siman 8], and that is the prevalent custom. This was already explained above in siman 8, se'if 14}

The widespread custom is to refrain from removing one's Tefillin until after the recital of "Kedushat U'va Letzion." On a day where there is the reading of the Sefer Torah, the custom is to not remove them until after the Sefer Torah is returned to the Heichal. On Rosh Chodesh, we remove the Tefillin before the Mussaf prayers. {Rama: [There is an opinion based on Kabbala to not remove one's Tefillin until one has recited the three Kedushot and the four Kaddishim, meaning after Kaddish Yatom and this is practice of those who are scrupulous [with mitzvos].] Rem"a: This refers to a place where the Sefer Torah is returned after U'va Letzion.
