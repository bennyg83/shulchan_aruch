**** HEBREW ****
<b>א) [סעיף א'] המערב לרבים משלו אומר וכו'  </b>וה"ה כשמערב משלהם צ"ל הרי עירוב זה וכו' ומברך ואח"כ אומר כמ"ש לקמן סי' תט"ו סעי' ד' יעו"ש
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1) [Se'if 1] One who establishes an eruv for many people using his own food says, etc. </b>The same applies when he establishes the eruv using their food: he must say, 'This eruv shall be,' etc. He first recites the blessing and then makes the declaration, as stated below in siman 415, se'if 4.
