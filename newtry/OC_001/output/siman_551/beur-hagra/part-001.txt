**** ENGLISH ****
<b>But without this, etc.</b> Certainly during the week in which Tisha B'Av falls it is forbidden according to the law, for even laundered garments are forbidden, all the more so new ones. During the thirty days of mourning laundering is permitted, but a mourner is forbidden laundering all seven days, and the thirty-day restriction applies only to pressing. New garments are forbidden according to the earlier opinions. All the more so where laundering itself is forbidden; and even from Rosh Chodesh, since the custom is to forbid laundering as written in seif 3 in the gloss.
