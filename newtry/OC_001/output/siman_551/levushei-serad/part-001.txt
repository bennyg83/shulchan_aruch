This reason Rashi, of blessed memory, wrote in Ta'anis folio 29 regarding that they forbid laundering to leave [garments] after Tisha B'Av: that he diverts his mind from the mourning and they occupy themselves with laundering garments; see there. And even if he gives to non-Jewish launderers to launder and to leave after Tisha B'Av, which is explained in the Rama in this se'if that it is forbidden, one must say that he likewise diverts his mind from the mourning and occupies himself to give garments to launder; and even if he told her to launder after Tisha B'Av, as is written in Magen Avraham, s.k. 9.
