**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And the baal bris.</b> Magen Avraham writes that this appears to apply specifically to the one who holds the infant on his knees during the circumcision. The person who brings the infant in and carries him out is not included. This is certainly so when Tishah B'Av falls on Shabbos, when wearing white garments is forbidden by strict law, and permission was granted only to the one who holds the infant. See Yoreh Deah 391, seif katan 3, and what is written there. Since he did not also write that they may shave, this implies that shaving is forbidden. Eliyah Zuta writes that his grandfather ruled that a mourner within thirty days should not trim his nails when another mohel is available, and likewise should not shave within thirty days for a circumcision. Rama, however, writes in Yoreh Deah 393:3 that shaving is permitted. This requires study. See also what Baer HaGolah writes there concerning this.

<b>שמחה. </b> קאי גם אמשא ומתן כגון צרכי חופה אבל שאר משא ומתן שרי כדרכו ב"י ב"ח וכתב מהרי"ל הסרסורים לא ישאו כלי כסף לסרסר בעיר דשמחה הוא לרואים. ובמקום שנהגו איסור בכל משא ומתן צריך התרה עיין בי"ד סי' רי"ד אבל במקום שנהגו למעט בכל משא ומתן לא מהני התר' כיון דיש פוסקים דס"ל הכי כבר קבלו אבותיהם כדע' ההוא. ובסי' תקנ"ד ססכ"ב משמע דממעטין בכל מו"מ ע"ש וכ' הב"י בשם הר"ן דכל בנין שא"צ לו רק להרווחה בעלמא אסור. אם קצץ עם העכו"ם בקבלנות לצייר ביתו מותר לצייר ואם יכול לפייסו בדבר מועט שימתין עד אחר ת"ב תע"ב מהרי"ל. נ"ל דבה"כ מותר דהוי מצוה דרבי'. מ"א:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Joy.</b> This also refers to commerce, such as matters needed for a wedding. Other commerce is permitted in the ordinary manner. Beit Yosef; Bach. Maharil writes that brokers should not carry silver utensils through the city for brokerage, because this brings joy to those who see them. In a place where the custom is to prohibit all commerce, the custom requires annulment; see Yoreh Deah 214. In a place where the custom is merely to reduce all commerce, annulment does not help, because some poskim rule this way and their ancestors accepted that opinion. Siman 554, at the end of seif 22, also implies that all commerce should be reduced; see there. Beit Yosef cites Ran that any construction not needed except for mere expansion is forbidden. If one contracted with a non-Jew to paint his house, the non-Jew may paint it; but if he can appease him with a small payment to wait until after Tishah B'Av, this is praiseworthy. Maharil. It appears to me that construction of a synagogue is permitted, because it is a communal mitzvah. Magen Avraham.

<b>From Rosh Chodesh.</b> Regarding wearing, the Mechaber in Beit Yosef is lenient. The reason for prohibiting their manufacture, as stated in seif 8, is the custom not to set up the warp. Spinning thread, however, is permitted, since it is not included in this. Magen Avraham writes that one may make items called kronin that are woven from wood, since they are not garments.
