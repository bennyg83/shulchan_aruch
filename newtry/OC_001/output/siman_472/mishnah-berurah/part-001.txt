**** ENGLISH ****

And he further added that according to this, even between the first and the second it is not worthwhile that he return and drink, for since he recites a blessing over it, it appears like adding another cup to the cups [as clarified in the Tur, siman 473, in the name of Avi HaEzri]; and therefore he should not return and drink, and he should rely on the view of Avi HaEzri as with the later cups. However, if he forgot and did not recline for the second cup, he should return and drink with heseiba without a blessing, for it is not like one who changed his mind, since also during the meal if he wishes to drink he need not recite a blessing and relies on the blessing of the second cup. And it is proper that before he recites a blessing over the first cup his intention be to return and drink between the first cups, and then even if he errs and drinks the first cup without heseiba he can drink another cup with heseiba and without a blessing, and it will not appear like adding.
