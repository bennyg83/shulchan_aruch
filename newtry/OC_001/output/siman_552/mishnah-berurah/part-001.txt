(22) To increase a bit, etc. And many of the later authorities are not at ease with this: to increase at first in several kinds of cooked dishes and to delight in them, and this is his main meal, and after Minchah when he is not hungry and not thirsty at all he eats as a remembrance of seudah hamafseket, and it is only like incidental eating; and therefore there are among them who say that before Minchah he should eat only one cooked dish, and after Minchah he should eat lentils or eggs as above; and even though this too is in the category of a cooked dish, they do not combine with each other. (23) He eats meat. And it is forbidden to withhold from him; even though there is no obligation to eat meat on Shabbos, nevertheless since he withholds because of mourning it is a sin. And behold even though the Mechaber wrote "like Solomon's feast," nevertheless he should sit in sorrow of soul that he not conduct himself with joy; and therefore he should not sit at a companions' meal [Magen Avraham]. And according to everyone it is permitted to eat with his household, and he may bless with zimmun since it is Shabbos [Eliyah Rabbah]; and if a circumcision falls on that Shabbos he should make the meal before Minchah.
