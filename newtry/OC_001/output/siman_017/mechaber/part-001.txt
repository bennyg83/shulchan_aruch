**** ENGLISH ****
Women and slaves are exempt because it is a positive time-bound mitzvah. Rema: Nevertheless if they wish to wrap and bless, they may like other time-bound positive mitzvos (Tosafos, Rosh, Ran Rosh Hashanah ch. 2, Kiddushin ch. 1); but it looks arrogant so they should not wear <i>tzitzis</i>, since it is not a man’s obligation [<i>Agur</i> 27] — meaning he is not obligated to acquire a <i>tallis</i> to become obligated in <i>tzitzis</i>; below siman 19 he says when one has a four-cornered garment and wears it. A <i>tumtum</i> and <i>androgynos</i> are obligated from doubt and wrap without a blessing. [<i>Tumtum</i>: gender unknown; <i>androgynos</i>: has male and female traits.] Rema: According to women’s practice to bless on time-bound mitzvos, they too bless (his own words).

**** HEBREW ****
<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ד" data-order="4"></i>קטן היודע להתעטף אביו צריך <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ט"></i>ליקח לו <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="4"></i><i data-commentator="Sha'arei Teshuvah" data-order="4"></i>ציצי' <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="י"></i>לחנכו <small>הגה ודוקא כשיודע לעטוף ב' ציצית לפניו ושנים לאחריו [הגהת מיימוני פ"ג] ויודע לאחוז הציצית בידו בשעת ק"ש [מרדכי סוף פרק לולב הגזול]:</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
A minor who knows how to wrap like his father — his father must obtain <i>tzitzis</i> for him to train him. Rema: Specifically when he knows to wrap two <i>tzitzis</i> before and two behind (Hagahos Maimoniyos ch. 3) and knows to hold <i>tzitzis</i> in hand during <i>Kerias Shema</i> (Mordechai end of chapter stolen <i>lulav</i>).
