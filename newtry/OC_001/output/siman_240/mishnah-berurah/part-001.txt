(ו) ת"ח - שבחול צריכים לעסוק בתורה בלילה שתורתן אומנותן ואל יאמר אדם אעשה עצמי כת"ח כי כתיב ועונתה לא יגרע [מ"א בשם הקנה] ור"ל להפקיע העונה שלא ברשותה אבל ברשותה תבוא עליו ברכה [פמ"ג] וע"ש שמסתפק בזמנינו אם נקרא ת"ח לזה ומ"מ מצדד דכיון דלפי הדור הוא ת"ח והוא נשאה ע"ד כן לית חיוב עונה כ"א בליל שבת ועיין בבה"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****

<b>(6) A talmid chacham.</b> During the weekdays he must engage in Torah study at night, because Torah is his occupation. A person should not say, “I will make myself like a talmid chacham,” for Scripture states, “He shall not diminish her onah.” Magen Avraham in the name of Sefer HaKanah. This means that he may not remove her onah without her permission. With her permission, however, a blessing will come upon him. Peri Megadim. See there, where he is uncertain whether in our times one is considered a talmid chacham for this purpose. Nevertheless, he inclines to say that since relative to the generation he is a talmid chacham and she married him with that understanding, his obligation of onah is only on Friday night. See Biur Halachah.

(ז) מליל שבת וכו' - יש שכתבו שה"ה בר"ח ויו"ט. ולכאורה לפי טעם המ"א הנ"ל בסק"ו שצריך לעסוק בתורה בלילה אין שייך לחייב אותו בר"ח ועיין בבה"ל. אין לשמש בליל א' של פסח וליל שבועות ושני ימים של ר"ה וליל שמיני עצרת אם לא בליל טבילה. וכ"ז אינו אלא לאדם שהוא מלא ביראה ולא יחטא ח"ו אבל אלו שיצרם מתגבר עליהם והם חושבים שהוא כעין איסור תורה ועי"ז באים ח"ו לידי כמה מכשולים מצוה לשמש אפילו בר"ה ויטבול למחר דמצד הדין אין איסור אלא ביוה"כ ובט"ב ובימי אבלות שלו או שלה [חכמ"א]:
**** ENGLISH ****

<b>(7) From Friday night, etc.</b> Some write that the same applies on Rosh Chodesh and Yom Tov. Seemingly, according to Magen Avraham's reason above in seif katan 6, that he must engage in Torah study at night, there is no basis to obligate him specifically on Rosh Chodesh. See Biur Halachah. One should not have relations on the first night of Pesach, the night of Shavuos, the two nights of Rosh Hashanah, or the night of Shemini Atzeres, unless it is the night of her immersion. All this applies only to a person who is filled with fear of Heaven and will not sin, God forbid. Those whose inclination overcomes them, and who mistakenly think this is comparable to a Torah prohibition and thereby come, God forbid, to various stumbling blocks, have a mitzvah to have relations even on Rosh Hashanah and immerse the following day. Strictly speaking, relations are forbidden only on Yom Kippur, Tisha B'Av, and during his or her period of mourning. Chochmas Adam.

**** ENGLISH ****
(54) While traveling: Magen Avraham writes that here the concern is medical, whereas in seif 1 the discussion is a matter of law. This is evident because the Mechaber also says not to have relations on the day one enters the bathhouse, nor before or after these events. Yet it is known that it is a mitzvah to bathe every Friday, while the conjugal interval of Torah scholars is from Shabbos to Shabbos. Necessarily, all these cautions are only medical. One who leaves a fixed privy, meaning one with a seat, as opposed to a temporary privy, should not have relations until at least the time needed to walk half a mil has passed, because the spirit of the privy clings to him. Gittin 70a. Seder HaYom writes similarly in the name of the Zohar, though he says not to have relations for that entire hour. Yad Efrayim explains that the Zohar does not mean a literal hour, but merely some period of time. Bedek HaBayis cites the Zohar, and the later authorities bring it, that a nursing woman should have relations only while the child sleeps, and after relations should not nurse until the time needed to walk two mil has passed. If the child cries, she should wait at least the time needed to walk one mil. A woman who delays immersion in order to distress her husband commits a grave sin and causes many evils. Zohar. Sefer Chassidim writes that one may not mention Hashem’s name or recite sacred words while semen remains on his body; he should first wash the soiled areas. The works of mussar write that one should always keep water near the bed and wash the hands before and after relations. If one awakens from a dream with an erection, he should be careful not to have relations then, for the children may be spiritually blemished, Heaven forbid. Even if she is pregnant, she may give birth to harmful spirits. Arizal. One should be careful not to place a sacred book on a bed upon which one sleeps, especially with one’s wife, since semen may be present there.
