**** ENGLISH ****
<b>There, seif 11, in the gloss.</b> A Torah scholar may darken the room with his garment, and it is permitted. Note: See Magen Avraham, who cites Rambam that one should resort to this only in a case of great need. In my humble opinion, there is a clear proof from Kesubos 65b. The Gemara challenges the view of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who says that she eats with him on Friday night and Shabbos. According to the opinion that this means actual relations, are not the Jewish people holy and do they not refrain from relations by day? It answers that Rava said that a Torah scholar may darken the room with his garment and it is permitted. This proves that it is permitted even without great need, for if it were allowed only in a case of great need, there could be no fixed initial obligation that she “eat with him” on Friday night and Shabbos, meaning relations. It must therefore be permitted even without great need. This appears to me a clear proof. Examine carefully. However, see my work Mei Niddah, novellae to page 17, where I brought a clear proof for Rambam’s view cited by Magen Avraham. See there and examine carefully.
