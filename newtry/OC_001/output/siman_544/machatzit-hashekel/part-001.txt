<b>(ס"ק א) לגוף כו'</b> דא"א שלא יצטרך אחד מהן למרחץ. ר"ל יצטרך במועד. וזה מיקרי צורכי רבי' אף שא"צ רק א' כמ"ש מ"א סי' תקמ"ה ס"ק י"ז:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(s.k. 1) For the body etc.</b> For it is impossible that one of them will not need the bathhouse. That is to say, he will need it on the moed. And this is called communal needs even though only one person needs it, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 545, s.k. 17.
