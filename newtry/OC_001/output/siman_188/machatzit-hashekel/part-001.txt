<b>(seif katan3) The formula, etc. Beit Yosef beRachemim. Kol Bo and Or Zaru'a wrote that one should say Boneh beRachemav Yerushalayim</b>: because it is not built except in judgment, as written in Yeshayahu chapter 1: Zion shall be redeemed through justice. But Mordechai in name of Maharam wrote one should say beRachemav, as written in Zekharyah: and I will return to Yerushalayim in mercy - my house shall be built in it (and one must say for him: that which is written Zion in justice is on account of doers of justice therein, as Rashi wrote there - but nevertheless it will be in mercy even though their merit is not sufficient to redeem); therefore Mahari Giinzburg wrote the formula of the reader mentioned - that the word beiti is between the word beRachemim and the word yivaneh; therefore one can say Boneh Yerushalayim beRachemim that the word Yerushalayim be between Boneh and beRachemim - even though in the reader beRachemim was mentioned first and at the end the word building - nevertheless here we have no concern, since it is impossible to fix the language of the blessing in this language.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Beit Yosef wrote one should say zonenenu, etc. His proof from Yerushalmi that Tur brought is brought in Magen Avraham seif 7 here: thus it is taught</b>: it is forbidden for a person to request his needs on Shabbos; Rav Zeira asked Rabbah: what is the law of saying Avinu ro'einu zonenenu on Shabbos? He said: the formula of blessings is so - end of his words. From this it is proven that zonenenu is said in language of request; therefore Rav Zeira was in doubt whether he may say it on Shabbos - for it is requesting his needs; but if it were thought it is said in language of praise and praise zonenenu with cholam - then in what was Rav Zeira in doubt whether he may say it on Shabbos? Therefore Rabbeinu Yonah wrote: since zonenenu is language of prayer, likewise ro'einu is language of prayer with missing vav and shin for resh. However Taz seif katan7 challenged: per this the language is doubled four times - ro'einu zonenenu parnasenu vechalkelenu; therefore Taz explained: two are the Name-title - that is ro'einu zonenenu; and two - that is parnasenu vechalkelenu - are in language of request, for we need two matters: one - things necessary for human life like bread and water; two - things added for human enjoyment like several kinds of fruits and delights of this world; therefore two titles are on these two matters, and likewise two requests - that is parnasenu vechalkelenu - that we request on these two matters; per this what Rav Zeira asked in Yerushalmi was not his question except because of parnasenu vechalkelenu which are request - even though Rav Zeira did not specify in his question except what is the law of saying ro'einu zonenenu - one can say he cited the beginning of the statement, but his intent is the end of the statement - that is parnasenu vechalkelenu.

And likewise in Niddah: we learned - one who miscarries on day 41 of her pregnancy (for before then it was mere water) sits [in uncleanness] for a male and for a female - meaning the stringency of both lest it was female and she would have fourteen days of tumah and days of taharah like a male until completion of forty days alone; and R' Yishmael holds she sits only for a male, for he holds formation of the female is at eighty days; and since she miscarried on day 41, perforce there is no room to suspect female; and the Sages say she sits for male and female, for they hold also formation of the female is at forty days like a male. And he challenges: the Sages are the first Tanna; and if you say the mishnah should be Stam like the first Tanna to inform us the law is like him - obvious that individual and many - the law is like the many; and if not we know the law is like the first Tanna; and he answers: it was thought the law is like R' Yishmael even though he is individual against many because his reason is sound - until here the content of the Gemara's words.
