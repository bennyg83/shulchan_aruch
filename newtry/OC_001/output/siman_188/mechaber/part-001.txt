One must mention the kingdom of the House of David in the third blessing, and one should not mention any other kingdom in it. One who says "and Your kingdom and the kingdom of David Your anointed" errs, for one may not equate an earthly kingdom with the heavenly kingdom. This is a sound reasoning, but I have not seen it practiced.}
