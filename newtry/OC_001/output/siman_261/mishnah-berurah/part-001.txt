(23) Three-quarters of a mil; and this is approximately a quarter of an hour. And behold Shulchan Aruch mentioned in this seif Rabbeinu Tam's view and his supporters; but many of the Rishonim hold, and also the Gra agreed to their method, that bein hashemashot begins immediately after the start of sunset, namely from when the sun was hidden from our eyes, and its duration lasts three-quarters of a mil; and afterward near it come out the three medium stars and it is night from the Torah for all matters. And therefore one must be very careful not to do melacha after the sun was hidden from our eyes, and even melacha of mitzvah such as kindling candles for the Shabbos meal; also be very careful to finish their kindling before the sun sets, for afterward it is included in bein hashemashot; and see above s.k. 6. And l'chatchila one should not wait until the last moment, rather advance their kindling from when the sun is at the tops of the trees as below; and one who is stringent upon himself and refrains from melacha half an hour or at least a third of an hour before sunset; fortunate is he, for he fulfills thereby the method of all the Rishonim. And regarding Motzei Shabbos see Biur Halacha where we explained in the name of poskim that for everyone the measure of three-quarters of a mil changes according to time and place, and this was not stated in the Gemara except for the horizon of Babylonia and in the time of Nisan and Tishrei when days and nights are equal; and in our places that lean toward the north of the world it lasts much longer; therefore one must be very careful not to do melacha on Motzei Shabbos even if much time passed after sunset until small stars are seen, as below siman 293 seif 2; see there in Mishna Berurah and Biur Halacha:
