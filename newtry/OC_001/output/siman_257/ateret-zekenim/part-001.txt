**** ENGLISH ****
<b>It is permitted according to all opinions, etc.</b> So too in the Rokeach, siman 90: One may not insulate within the coals. If he wishes, he may rake them out or place ash over the coals and set his pot upon the covered coals, even if the coals surround the pot. All the more so is what we do permitted, placing it in an oven in the house of a non-Jew, since he will not come to stoke the coals there. Maharar Shimshon, in the name of Maharar Leib of blessed memory, wrote concerning this that from here we have grounds to permit our practice of sealing the oven with clay and rags, for there is no concern that one will come to stoke the coals.
