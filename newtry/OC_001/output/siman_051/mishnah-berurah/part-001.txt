<b>(2) If he finished.</b> If he did not finish, he should not answer amen. This applies only once he has begun “Blessed are You, Hashem,” etc.; before that point the passage is merely praise.

This concerns amen after Baruch SheAmar or Yishtabach. Other amens may be answered even in the middle of these blessings, since they are not mentioned in the Gemara. However, after “Blessed are You, Hashem,” before completing “King praised through praises,” he may not answer, because he would disrupt the blessing.

<b>(3) The chazzan.</b> If he finishes together with the chazzan, he should not answer amen, because it appears that he is answering after his own blessing.
