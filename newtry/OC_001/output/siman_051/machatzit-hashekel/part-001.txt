**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Therefore he writes that it should not be said at Minchah.</b>

At Minchah there is no need to conclude with “But we shall bless,” since no Hallelujah psalms follow. Nevertheless, Maharshal said it after Ashrei at Minchah because Ashrei begins with language of praise, and he wished to conclude likewise.

At Shacharis before Lamnatzeach he did not say it, because neither reason applied. Our custom follows Kol Bo's reason, which applies whenever Tehillah LeDavid is recited. We also say “Fortunate are those who dwell” each time because no distinction was made.

<b>(ס"ק ח) אין לשחות כו'.</b> עיין סי' קי"ג ר"ל דהא הטעם דאין להוסיף על הכריעות שתקנו חז"ל כדאית' סי' קי"ג ושם כתב מ"א וז"ל ואפשר שישחה מעט כמ"ש ס"ס נ"א א"כ ר"ל דוק' מעומד סתמ' שוחה כדינו עד שיתפקקו כל חוליות שבשדרה משא"כ מיושב א"א לשחות כשיעור הזה וה"ל שחיה מועטת ושרי:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(Seif katan 8) One should not bow, etc.</b> See siman 113.

The reason is that one should not add bows beyond those instituted by Chazal, as explained in siman 113.

There Magen Avraham writes: “Perhaps one may bow slightly, as stated at the end of siman 51.”

His intention is that when standing, an ordinary bow is performed fully, until all the vertebrae of the spine protrude. When seated, however, one cannot bow to that extent. It is therefore only a slight bow and is permitted.
