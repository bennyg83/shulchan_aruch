<small>Seif 1. </small>He requests mercy for a sick person. Knesses HaGedolah, Yoreh Deah 240, writes in the name of Sefer Chassidim that when praying for one’s sick father, he should not say, “Heal my master, my father,” but should mention him by name, for Shlomo said, “David, my father.”
