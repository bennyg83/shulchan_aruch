(24) A person, etc. — meaning even though a vessel is forbidden to immerse and purify it from its impurity on Shabbat and Yom Tov because the vessel appears as fixed thereby; a person is different because he appears as cooling himself in water, and even if the time is cold and the water foul so it is not the way to wash in them — nevertheless sometimes when a person comes from the road soiled with mud and excrement he is accustomed to wash even in these; therefore permitted even if he could immerse before Shabbat.
