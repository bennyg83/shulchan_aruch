**** ENGLISH ****
Seif katan 4: Placing a hand. The Maharshal, responsum 72, permits this. He writes that although placing one’s hand over the heart, so that the heart not see the nakedness, is ineffective, as stated in siman 74, that is because people commonly place a hand over the heart, and it therefore does not appear to be a covering. Placing a hand upon the head, however, is not a person’s ordinary manner, and therefore it properly appears to be a covering.
