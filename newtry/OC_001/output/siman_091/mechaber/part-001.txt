<b>שיאזור מתניו ויכסה ראשו בשעת תפלה. ובו ו סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i>היתה טלית חגורה על מתניו לכסותו ממתניו ולמטה אסור להתפלל <i data-commentator="Ateret Zekenim" data-label="♦" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="א"></i>עד <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ב"></i>שיכסה לבו <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ב" data-order="2"></i>ואם לא כסה את לבו או שנאנס ואין לו במה יתכסה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ג"></i>הואיל וכסה ערותו והתפלל יצא:
**** ENGLISH ****
That One Should Gird One's Waist and Cover One's Head During the Time of Prayer: containing six s'ifim. If there was a garment (i.e. cloak, sheet) tied around one's waist, to cover from their waist down, then it is forbidden to pray until they cover their heart (ie. put on a shirt). If one did not cover their heart or is unable due to having nothing to cover themselves, since they covered their nakedness [genitalia] and prayed, they have fulfilled their obligation.

**** ENGLISH ****
One must gird a belt at the time of prayer — even if he has a sash such that his heart does not see the ervah — because of "Prepare [to meet your God]" [hikon]. But the other blessings, it is permitted to recite without a belt, since he has trousers. {Rama: And [it must be that] his heart does not see the ervah [Ran, first chapter of Shabbos; Rosh, chapter "HaRo'eh"; Hagahos Maimoni, chapter 5 of Hilchos Tefillah].}
