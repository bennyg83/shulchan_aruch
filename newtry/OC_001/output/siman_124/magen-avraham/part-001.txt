**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The chazzan should not wait.</b> The present practice is to wait for the head of the beis din. It appears that the reason is that most people pray hurriedly, and if individuals pray word by word they will be unable to recite Kedushah with the congregation; therefore, they wait. Accordingly, even if there is no head of the beis din in the city, one should wait for one who prays word by word. If he prolongs his prayer, however, one need not wait for him, as it is stated regarding Rabbi Akiva that when he prayed with the congregation he would shorten his prayer and rise. Sefer Chassidim 784 writes that if a person must prolong his prayer and fears others will mock him, he may step backward when the chazzan begins even though he has not yet finished, then return to his place and complete his Amidah.
