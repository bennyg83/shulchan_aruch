**** ENGLISH ****
One Should Not Belch or Yawn During the Prayers, containing 5 s'ifim One should not belch nor yawn . And if one needs to yawn due to a compulsion, one should place one's hand over one's mouth so that its opening will not be seen. {Rama: (i.e. one brings out from his body to his mouth, a blowing, as a result of one's satiation - "ritiur" in Old French) (i.e. the palate of one's jaws opens to bring forth a breath from the mouth) And one should be careful to not place one's hand on one's chin during prayer, for that is the way of haughtiness (Tur and the Ri"f).}
