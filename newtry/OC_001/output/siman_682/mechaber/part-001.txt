**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The law of Al Hanissim on Chanukah, containing three sections.</b><br>On all eight days of Chanukah one says Al Hanissim in Birkat Hamazon, in the blessing of the land, and in prayer, in the blessing of Modim. If he did not say it, we do not make him go back. See above, siman 294, sections 4 and 5. However, if he remembered within that blessing, as long as he has not mentioned the Name, even if he remembered between You and the Name, he goes back. Gloss: Some say that when he forgot Al Hanissim in Birkat Hamazon, when he reaches HaRachaman he should say: May the Merciful One perform miracles and wonders for us, just as You performed for our fathers in those days at this time, in the days of Mattisyahu, etc. Kol Bo. This was already explained in siman 187, section 4.
