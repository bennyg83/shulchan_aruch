**** ENGLISH ****
That one should not empty a storehouse on Shabbat. Containing 3 paragraphs: Even though it is permitted to take supplies from a storehouse of produce or wine barrels, it is forbidden to begin emptying it except for a mitzvah, like emptying it to host guests or to make a house of study inside. How should he empty it? If it is a big storehouse, he removes five kupas, . If the storehouse only has five kupas, he removes four kupas, but not all of them, lest he come to fill crevices in the floor. {Rama: (every kupa being three seahs) (Hagahot Mordechai and Ran in Perek HaMefanin) Rem"a: Every abstention from work that was allowed for the sake of a mitzvah is also allowed for the sake of guests. They are not referred to as "guests" unless they are staying in his house or he has invited guests staying at another house (Trumat Hadeshen Chapter 72), but if he just invites his friend to eat with him, he is not called a guest and the meal is not a commanded meal but just optional (Beit Yosef).}

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ג" data-order="3"></i>כשמפנה אלו ארבעה או חמשה קופות לא יחלקם בקופות קטנות להוליכם בהרבה פעמים כדי להקל המשאוי מפני שמרבה בהילוך ואוושא מילתא טפי:
**** ENGLISH ****
When he removes these four or five kupas, he should not divide them into small groups so that he makes multiple trips but the load is lessened because this increases his distance walked and becomes more noticeable and public.

**** ENGLISH ****
These four or five kupas that he removes are for one guest, but if multiple guests come, he removes this measurement for each one. One person should not clear it out for everyone because this is a lot of work. Rather, every individual should clear space for himself or someone else can do it for him. {Rama: Rema: It is forbidden to lower wine barrels brought by wagon, unless it is for honoring guests or for the sake of a mitzvah, in which case it is like clearing a storehouse (Or Zarua).}
