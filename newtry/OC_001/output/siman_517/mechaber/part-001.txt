**** ENGLISH ****
<b>In what manner it is permitted to take food items from the shopkeeper on Yom Tov. It contains four se'ifim.</b><br>To tell the shopkeeper to give him eggs, or nuts, or other items of food and drink — its law on Yom Tov is like [its law] on Shabbos {as explained above, siman 323, se'if 4}. And if he is a non-Jew, it is forbidden to take from him something whose kind is [still] attached [to the ground] or whose kind requires trapping, unless it is evident in them that they were not picked and not trapped that day; and likewise he may not take eggs from him, lest they were laid that day. But something that involves neither [the concern of being] attached, nor trapping, nor nolad, it is permitted to take from him — even if it was brought that day from outside the techum. And likewise, flour that was ground that day in a city that is mostly non-Jews, it is permitted to take from him, for they grind it with the non-Jews in mind; and the same applies to taking from him bread that was baked that day {and likewise if it was baked on the preceding Shabbos (Beis Yosef)}.
