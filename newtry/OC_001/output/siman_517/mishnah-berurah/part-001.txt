{ב} אפילו הובא היום מחוץ לתחום - ר"ל שהובא לעכו"ם החנוני ודוקא בעיר שרובה עכו"ם דמסתמא בשביל עכו"ם הובא לו כנ"ל בסימן תקט"ו ס"ו אבל בעיר שרובה ישראל או חציה אסור ליקח ממנו דמסתמא בשביל ישראל הובאו וצריך להמתין עד לערב יו"ט ראשון ובכדי שיעשו וכדלעיל בסימן תקט"ו ס"ה:
**** ENGLISH ****
{2} Even if it was brought today from outside the techum. That is to say: that it was brought to the non-Jewish shopkeeper; and specifically in a city whose majority are non-Jews, for presumably it was brought for non-Jews, as above in siman 515, se'if 6; but in a city whose majority are Jews, or half [and half], it is forbidden to take from him, for presumably they were brought for Jews, and one must wait until the evening after the first day of Yom Tov, and [wait] the time it would take to prepare them, as above in siman 515, se'if 5.

{ג} בעיר שרובה נכרים - היינו שרוב הלוקחים פת וקמח הם נכרים אבל אם רוב לוקחים פת וקמח הם ישראל ודאי אסור ליקח קמח דמסתמא אדעתם נטחנו אא"כ ידוע שנטחן מאתמול:

{3} In a city whose majority are non-Jews. That is, that most of those who buy bread and flour are non-Jews; but if most who buy bread and flour are Jews, it is certainly forbidden to take flour, for presumably it was ground with them in mind, unless it is known that it was ground yesterday.

{ה} ליקח ממנו פת וכו' - ר"ל אפילו ידוע שהקמח נטחן היום כיון שהוא בעיר שרוב הלוקחים הם עכו"ם מסתמא אדעתם היה הטחינה וההרקדה ואם היה ידוע לו שהטחינה וההרקדה היה מעיו"ט אז מותר אפילו בעיר שרובה ישראל דהאפיה הלא היא מלאכה המותרת ביו"ט. וכתבו האחרונים דכ"ז מיירי ששומת הלחם היה ידוע וא"צ עתה לישא וליתן עמו בעד דמי המקח דאל"ה גם גבי ישראל אסור כדלעיל בסימן שכ"ג וע"ש יתר הפרטים שצריך ליזהר גבי ישראל וה"ה גבי עכו"ם. עוד כתבו דאם אינו מאמינו העכו"ם בלי נתינת המעות מותר להניח לו משכון ע"ז אבל אסור לקרוא העכו"ם לביתו ולהראות לו המעות שיקח בעצמו דהוי עכ"פ עובדא דחול. ואפילו ע"י עכו"ם אסור לקנות:
**** ENGLISH ****
{5} To take bread from him, etc. That is to say: even if it is known that the flour was ground today, since he is in a city where most of the buyers are non-Jews, presumably the grinding and sifting were done with them in mind; and if it was known to him that the grinding and sifting were from erev Yom Tov, then it is permitted even in a city whose majority are Jews, for baking is a melacha that is permitted on Yom Tov. And the later authorities wrote that all this deals with a case where the appraisal of the bread was known and there is no need now to negotiate with him over the purchase money; for otherwise, even regarding a Jew it is forbidden, as above in siman 323; and see there the further details one must be careful about regarding a Jew, and the same applies regarding a non-Jew. They further wrote that if the non-Jew does not trust him without giving the money, it is permitted to leave him a security for this; but it is forbidden to call the non-Jew to his house and show him the money so that he take it himself, for that is in any case weekday-like activity. And even through a non-Jew it is forbidden to buy.

{ז} וכן אם נאפה בשבת שלפניו - ר"ל דלא אסרינן ליה משום הכנה שמכין משבת ליו"ט הואיל דמעיקר הדין אף בשבת גופא מותר לאכול (דאף דמשום חומרא דשבת מחמירין לעיל בסימן שכ"ה ס"ד שלא בשעת הדחק עכ"פ ביום טוב שלאחריו אין להחמיר):
**** ENGLISH ****
{7} And likewise if it was baked on the preceding Shabbos. That is to say: we do not forbid it because of preparation from Shabbos to Yom Tov, since according to the fundamental law it is permitted to eat even on Shabbos itself (for even though because of the stringency of Shabbos we are stringent above in siman 325, se'if 4, when it is not a pressing time of need, in any case on the Yom Tov that follows it one should not be stringent).

{ט} לא יפה עשה - פי' בין שנתן לו מעות לקנות לו למחר ובין שאמר לו קנה ממעות שלך ואני אחזיר לך בכל ענין אסור ואפילו אם הגוי קונה בלא מעות אפ"ה אסור כיון שהעכו"ם פוסק סכום המעות ביו"ט והטעם דכל מה שאסור לעשות על ידי ישראל בעצמו אסור לומר לעכו"ם לעשותו ביו"ט אפילו אם מצוהו מעיו"ט לזה:
**** ENGLISH ****
{9} He did not do well. Meaning: whether he gave him money to buy for him for tomorrow, or told him "buy with your own money and I will repay you," in every case it is forbidden; and even if the non-Jew buys without money, even so it is forbidden, since the non-Jew fixes the sum of money on Yom Tov; and the reason is that whatever is forbidden for a Jew to do himself is forbidden to tell a non-Jew to do on Yom Tov, even if he commanded him for this from erev Yom Tov.

**** ENGLISH ****
{10} Provided they do not fly. Meaning: that then there is no concern of trapping regarding them [for since they cannot fly, they are as already trapped and standing; but if they do fly, there is reason to fear that they were trapped today; and even though they were trapped for non-Jews, or even trapped of themselves, nevertheless it is known that anything that lacks trapping from before nightfall is forbidden because of muktzeh, as above in se'if 1]; and "invitation" like other Jewish pigeons they do not need, for that of a non-Jew needs no preparation, as above in siman 497, se'if 15. And all this is when they were brought from within the techum, or even from outside the techum and in a city whose majority are non-Jews, for they were brought with them in mind; but in a city whose majority are Jews and [brought] from outside the techum, it is forbidden to eat them until evening [after waiting] the time it would take to prepare them, as above in siman 515.
