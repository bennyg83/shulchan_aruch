<b>וה"ה ליקח כו'. </b>וא"ל דשמא הי' בה"ש קמח או עיסה דהוה מוקצה והוי כגרוגרות וצימוקים דאסור אף בשל א"י כמ"ש רש"י רפ"ג די"ט גבי מחובר וכמ"ש בפ"א דחולין בשוחט דאין חזי לכוס ואע"ג דבשוחט לא קי"ל כמ"ש הרא"ש שם היינו משום דלית בה תרתי דלא דחינהו בידי' משא"כ כאן דאית ביה תרתי ונמ"ש בפ"ג דשבת מ"מ מותר כיון שגמרו בידי אדם וכמ"ש בפ"ג די"ט:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And the same applies to taking, etc.</b> And one should not say that perhaps at twilight there was flour or dough that was muktzeh, which would be like dried figs and raisins that are forbidden even when belonging to a non-Jew, as Rashi wrote at the beginning of chapter 3 of Beitzah regarding attached [produce], and as written in chapter 1 of Chullin regarding one who slaughters, that it is not fit to chew; and although regarding the slaughterer we do not hold [that way], as the Rosh wrote there, that is because it lacks both [factors], for he did not push it aside with his own hands; which is not so here, where both are present. And as written in chapter 3 of Shabbos; nevertheless it is permitted since it was completed by human hands, as written in chapter 3 of Beitzah.

**** ENGLISH ****
