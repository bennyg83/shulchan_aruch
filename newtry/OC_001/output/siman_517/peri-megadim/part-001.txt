<b>וכן</b> עט״ז רוב הלוקחים בעינן וכ״מ כ״פ בש״ס אחר רוב אוכלי בשר וכדומה ומסל״ת בתקט״ו ס״ג ותקי״ג ס״ו אפי׳ נגד חזקה דעתה נתלשו נאמן בדרבנן עתה״ד פ״ג. מ״ש אין הכן בשל עכו״ם י״ל היינו דגרוגרת וצמוקים וכדומה דאין העכו״ם מקצה מדעתו וה״ה מלאכת טחינה גמרו ב״א עסי׳ שכ״ה רק מחובר וצידה לא חלקו בין עכו״ם לישראל עסי׳ תקט״ו ותיבת זה בב״י הקשה בעיר שרוב ישראל כל שהובא לשם ישראל הכל אסורים אם לא ה״פ דאם ידוע שהובא ביחוד לשם ישראל אחד מותר למי שלא הובא בשבילו. עא״ר אם קנה דגים או פירות מעי״ט והניח אצל עכו״ם כל שאין חשש שהחליפם בגרועים שרי (דלהכעיס לא חיישינן):
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And likewise.</b> On the Taz: we require a majority of the buyers, and so it is proven and so he ruled many times in the Shas according to the majority of those who eat meat and the like; and [regarding when a non-Jew] attests (mesales), in siman 515, se'if 3, and siman 513, se'if 6: even against a presumption that they were detached [today], he is believed regarding rabbinic [law]; see Terumat HaDeshen, [siman] 83. What he wrote, that there is no "preparation" requirement regarding that of a non-Jew: one can say that refers to dried figs and raisins and the like, where the non-Jew does not set them aside mentally; and the same applies to the melacha of grinding, which was completed by human hands; see siman 325. Only attached [produce] and trapping: they did not distinguish between a non-Jew and a Jew; see siman 515. And on the word "this" in Beis Yosef he raised a difficulty: in a city whose majority are Jews, whatever was brought for the Jews, all are forbidden; if not, this is the explanation: that if it is known that it was brought specifically for one particular Jew, it is permitted for one for whom it was not brought. See Eliyah Rabbah: if he bought fish or produce from erev Yom Tov and left it with a non-Jew, so long as there is no concern that he exchanged them for inferior ones, it is permitted (for we are not concerned that he acts to provoke).

<b>אם</b> נתערב דג הניצוד בי״ט עם הרבה דגים שניצודו מעי״ט וא״א להמתין למחר כי ימותו כולם י״ל דיל״מ בנתקלקל בטל ובריה לא הוי חיין דאין נוצר באיסורו. עי״ד ק״ב:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>If.</b> A fish caught on Yom Tov became mixed with many fish that were caught from erev Yom Tov, and it is impossible to wait until tomorrow because they will all die: one can say that when it spoils it is nullified, and a "creature" (beriyah) does not apply to live ones, for it was not formed in its prohibition. See Yoreh De'ah 102.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And it is.</b> On the Taz: that is, they lack trapping; and on the Taz regarding outside the techum, at first glance this requires further study; and it is a responsum of the Rosh in Beis Yosef. Again I saw that his words grow from the responsum of the Rosh, of blessed memory, and Olas Shabbos explained it to me; and see Yam Shel Shlomo, first chapter, [siman] 28, according to the Rosh: whatever cannot fly out of the domain, even though they flutter a bit, is not lacking trapping and needs no trap; see there. And the Mechaber stated it without qualification, and this requires further study; and see Levush.
