**** ENGLISH ****
Se'if 6. And if the kohen is not fasting. If in the synagogue there are a kohen and a Levi who did not fast, and they called the kohen, and afterward they remembered that he had not fasted: whether they should call after him a Levi who did not fast, or call after him a Yisrael who is fasting, I wrote in a responsum.
