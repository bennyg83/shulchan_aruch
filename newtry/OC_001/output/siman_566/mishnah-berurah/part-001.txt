(21) The kohen went out - so that they not say that he is blemished; nevertheless if he is in the synagogue and does not want to leave, there is no concern, for there is no blemish, since all know that there are people who are not accustomed to fast. However, this is regarding a BeHaB fast and the like; but on the fasts written [in Scripture], when all fast except one who is ill, there is a blemish if he does not leave, and therefore he should be careful to leave. And all this is when there is no other kohen in the synagogue; but if there is another kohen, it is obvious that he need not leave, and if he is concerned that they will call him to ascend, he should tell the chazzan not to call him. And know that after the fact, if at Minchah they called one who is not fasting, there are opinions among the poskim whether he should ascend (for some are stringent in this, that there is concern of a blessing in vain, for they enacted this reading only for those who are fasting; and the poskim who are lenient in this, their reason is that the blessing is for the honor of the congregation). And therefore one must be very careful not to be in the synagogue so that they not call him. And after the fact, if they called him and he is a Torah scholar, and due to some compulsion it happened that he did not fast on a communal fast, and it pains him to tell them that he did not fast so that there not be a desecration of Hashem's Name in the matter, it appears that in a time of pressing need he may rely on the lenient ones and ascend.
