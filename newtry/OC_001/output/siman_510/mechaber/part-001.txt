**** ENGLISH ****
One who sorts legumes on Yom Tov sorts in the normal way — in his lap or in a bowl (if he wishes to eat them that day) (HaMagid ch. 3) — but not on a board, sieve, or strainer. This applies when the edible portion is greater than the waste; but if the waste is greater than the edible portion, one removes the edible portion and leaves the waste. And if it would be more effort to remove the waste from the edible than to remove the edible from the waste, even if the edible is the greater portion, one removes the edible and leaves the waste. {Rama: Rema: Almonds and pistachios that are cracked and still in their shells — the shell is not considered waste since that is their normal state and they are still mixed together. (Tur)}

**** ENGLISH ****
Even though carrying is permitted on Yom Tov even for non-essential purposes, one should not carry large loads in the same manner as on a weekday — one must make a change. How so? One who brings jugs of wine from place to place should not bring them in a basket or crate holding four or five jugs, but should bring them on his shoulder or in front of him, one or two at a time, which shows clearly that he is bringing them for Yom Tov needs. {Rama: Rema: This applies specifically when carrying from place to place, but from corner to corner (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashi) or from house to house within the same courtyard (Or Zarua) it is permitted in any manner.}

**** ENGLISH ****
Similarly, loads that are normally carried on a pole should be carried on one's back; those normally carried on one's back should be carried on one's shoulder; those normally carried on the shoulder should be carried in front in one's hand, or a garment should be spread over them — and any similar change in how the load is carried.
