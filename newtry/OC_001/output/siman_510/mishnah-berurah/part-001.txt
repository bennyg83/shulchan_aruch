**** ENGLISH ****
<b>If he wants to eat it on that same day.</b> This does not come to exclude if his intent is to eat tomorrow, for in that case it is obvious that it is forbidden to make any preparation from Yom Tov to a weekday. Only with a sieve and a sifter the Sages forbade, because it appears like one acting for tomorrow's need, for the way of selecting with these vessels is to do so for many days, as we wrote above in seif 1 [Magen Avraham and other Acharonim].
