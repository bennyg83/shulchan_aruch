[ב] <b>שרוצה לאוכלם וכו'.</b> פירוש אף דבשבת אין היתר אלא לאלתר שאני יום טוב דדמי למבשל ומותר כל אותו יום והוא מדברי מגיד שחולק ארשב"א דאוסר גם ביום טוב, והט"ז הסכים לרשב"א משום דאוכל נפש שאפשר מאתמול אסור כמו שכתב בית יוסף סימן תק"ו גבי אין שונין קמח כו' ותמה על המגיד, ולעניות דעתי לא קשה מידי דמוכח בסימן תק"ו דרשב"א סבירא ליה דאף באפשר מאתמול מותר שהרי פסק דשונין קמח, והא דאין מרקידין משום שהוא לזמן מרובה, ומכל מקום בסימן תצ"ה מבואר דקיימא לן הכי רק על ידי שינוי מותר. עוד כתב הט"ז דמהרי"ל שכתב שלא יברר פירורי מצה בפסח גדולים מקטנים סבירא ליה כרשב"א ומרש"ל פרק קמא דביצה סימן מ"ב כשחולק על מהרי"ל נעלם ממנו דברי רשב"א, עד כאן, תימא הא רש"ל שם מביא דברי הרשב"א אלא שכתב שדברי מגיד נוטים, גם שאר דברי ט"ז יש לדחות, ומכל מקום יש להחמיר שכן משמע בספר תניא ומרבינו ירוחם דף ל"א וכן מגן אברהם ס"ק ט' בסימן תק"ד החמיר כמהרי"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
[2] <b>That he wants to eat them, etc.</b> Meaning: even though on Shabbos there is permission only immediately, Yom Tov is different, for it is like one who cooks and it is permitted the entire day; and this is from the words of the Maggid Mishneh, who disagrees with the Rashba, who forbids even on Yom Tov. And the Taz agreed with the Rashba because ochel nefesh that could be done from yesterday is forbidden, as Beis Yosef wrote in siman 506 regarding "one may not sift flour a second time," etc., and he wondered at the Maggid Mishneh. And to my humble opinion there is no difficulty at all, for it is proven in siman 506 that the Rashba holds that even when possible from yesterday it is permitted, for he ruled that one may sift flour; and that which one may not winnow is because it is for a long period. Nevertheless, in siman 495 it is explained that we hold thus: only through an alteration is it permitted. Furthermore the Taz wrote that the Maharil, who wrote that one should not select matzah crumbs on Pesach, large from small, holds like the Rashba; and from Maharshal, first chapter of Beitzah, siman 42, when he disagrees with the Maharil, the words of the Rashba were hidden from him; until here. This is puzzling, for Maharshal there brings the words of the Rashba, only he wrote that the Maggid Mishneh's words incline [to leniency]; also the Taz's other words can be dismissed. Nevertheless one should be stringent, for so it appears in Sefer Tanya and from Rabbeinu Yerucham, folio 31; and likewise Magen Avraham, s.k. 9 in siman 504, was stringent like the Maharil.

[ג] <b>[לבוש] אפילו לטלטלו וכו'.</b> צריך עיון דשולחן ערוך פסק דמותר לברור האוכל ועולת שבת דקדק כן ממה שכתב אף על פי שאוכל מרובה וכו', ולא דק דכתב השולחן ערוך כן להדיא קודם זה וגם קושייתו כאן ומה דשקול וטרי בסימן שי"ט הוא לחינם למעיין בבית יוסף. גם צריך עיון דבסימן שי"ט כתב הלבוש גופיה שני דעות בזה והמתירין רבים הם, ואפשר דסבירא ליה להחמיר ביום טוב יותר מבשבת לענין מוקצה כמו שכתב סימן תצ"ה, ולדינא העליתי לעיל להקל גם ביום טוב. והנה כשפסולת מרובה אלא שבאוכל שטירחא יותר מבואר בר"ן דבורר האוכל דוקא ובבית יוסף סימן שי"ט הביא דברי הר"ן בטעות עיין שם ודו"ק, וכן משמע מרבינו ירוחם שם שהרי כתב דהפסולת אסור בטילטולו אך מצאתי בתוספתא [ביצה א יד.] בית שמאי אומרים אם היו צרורות מרובין על האוכלין בורר את האוכלין ומניח הצרורות בית הלל אומרים בורר איזה מהן שירצה, עד כאן, ונראה לי דמיירי דצרורות מרובין בכמות אבל מועטין בטירחא דאי צרורות מרובין בטירחא ודאי לכל הפוסקים אין היתר לברור פסולות אם לא שנאמר דפליג אש"ס ודו"ק וצריך עיון. ומתוספתא הנזכר לעיל נמי ראיה לשיטת הרמב"ם דאף שפסולות מרובה בכמות מותר בטילטול:
**** ENGLISH ****
[3] <b>[Levush] Even to move it, etc.</b> This requires investigation, for the Shulchan Aruch ruled that it is permitted to select the food, and Olas Shabbos was precise about this from what he wrote "even though the food is the majority," etc.; and he was not precise, for the Shulchan Aruch wrote thus explicitly before this; and also his challenge here, and what he deliberates in siman 319, is for nothing for one who examines Beis Yosef. Also this requires investigation, for in siman 319 the Levush himself wrote two views on this, and those who permit are many; and it is possible he holds to be more stringent on Yom Tov than on Shabbos regarding muktzeh, as he wrote in siman 495; and for the practical ruling I concluded above to be lenient also on Yom Tov. And behold, when refuse is the majority, only that regarding the food there is more toil: it is explained in the Ran that one selects specifically the food; and in Beis Yosef siman 319 he brought the words of the Ran mistakenly; see there and consider carefully. And likewise it appears from Rabbeinu Yerucham there, for he wrote that the refuse is forbidden to move. But I found in the Tosefta [Beitzah 1:14]: Beis Shammai say, if there were many pebbles upon the foods, he selects the foods and leaves the pebbles; Beis Hillel say, he selects whichever of them he wishes; until here. And it appears to me that it deals with pebbles that are many in quantity but few in toil; for if pebbles are many in toil, certainly for all poskim there is no permission to select refuse, unless we say they disagree with the Gemara; consider carefully, and this requires investigation. And from the aforementioned Tosefta as well there is proof for the view of the Rambam, that even when refuse is great in quantity it is permitted to move.

[ד] <b>[לבוש] מותר לבררם וכו'.</b> אף לברר פסולת מאוכל כיון דמין אחד הוא ואין שם פסולת עליו תיקון אוכל בעלמא הוא ואין שם מלאכה עליו (רש"ל שם), וכן מוכח מרמ"א שכתב דין דלוזים וכו' אף השולחן ערוך סבירא ליה דבכל מיני פסולת מותר לבררן ועולת שבת וט"ז ומגן אברהם חלקו דמכל מקום איכא משום טירחא יתירא, ולעניות דעתי כיון שאינו אלא תיקון אוכל לא חיישינן לטירחא דדוקא היכא דשם פסולת עליו חיישינן לזה והיינו שכתב ואין שם מלאכה עליו, גם מצאתי ראיה מרבינו ירוחם שפסק נמי כשולחן ערוך וכתב נמי דין דלוזים ודו"ק, ועוד נראה לי דאפשר כוונת רש"ל ורמ"א ור"י היכא דפסולת מרובה בכמות ומועטין בטירחא דאז קאמר דמותר בלוזים לברר פסולת אף דבשאר פסולת צריך לברר האוכל כמו שכתבתי בשם הר"ן ור"י לעיל. והנה מגן אברהם הקשה מסוף סימן שכ"א דהקולף שומים ובצלים הוי בורר ונראה לי דלא קשה מידי דשם מיירי שעושה להניח דבזה גם הכא אסור אבל כשקולף לאכול לאלתר מותר אף לברר הפסולת כמו שקולף הקליפה. כתב ים של שלמה שם דאסור להניח קטניות או שאר זרעונים במים שיצוף הפסולת למעלה או שיפול העפר למטה וכן משמע מבית יוסף (מגן אברהם), וצריך עיון דאפשר דמודה הרמב"ם כשאין פסולת רק עפר שאז לוקח המאכל ממנו ומניח העפר למטה דעיקר ברירה אינו אלא בפסולת, גם צריך לעיין בירושלמי הא דתנן בביצה שם בורר כדרכו בחיקו אמר ר' חנינה דרבן גמליאל הוא דרבן גמליאל אומר אף מדיחו ושולה משמע דרבן גמליאל לא פליג על תנא קמא וזה סייעתא לרבינו ירוחם שהביא בית יוסף, וצריך עיון על הרמב"ם מיהו בסימן שי"ט סעיף ח' משמע לאיסור והוא משנה בשבת דף ק"מ ויש לחלק וצריך עיון:
**** ENGLISH ****
[4] <b>[Levush] It is permitted to select them, etc.</b> Even to select refuse from food, since it is one kind and there is no name of refuse upon it: it is mere food-preparation and there is no name of melacha upon it (Maharshal there). And so it is proven from the Rama, who wrote the law of almonds, etc.; also the Shulchan Aruch holds that in all kinds of refuse it is permitted to select them. And Olas Shabbos and the Taz and Magen Avraham disagreed, that nevertheless there is a concern of extra toil. And to my humble opinion, since it is only food-preparation we are not concerned about toil, for specifically where there is a name of refuse upon it do we concern ourselves for this; and that is what he wrote, "and there is no name of melacha upon it." I also found proof from Rabbeinu Yerucham, who likewise ruled like the Shulchan Aruch and also wrote the law of almonds; consider carefully. And further it appears to me that possibly the intent of Maharshal and the Rama and Rabbeinu Yerucham is where refuse is great in quantity and few in toil: then he says that with almonds it is permitted to select refuse, even though with other refuse one must select the food, as I wrote in the name of the Ran and Rabbeinu Yerucham above. And behold Magen Avraham challenged from the end of siman 321 that one who peels garlic and onions is selecting; and it appears to me there is no difficulty at all, for there it deals with one who does so in order to leave it; in that case here as well it is forbidden; but when one peels to eat immediately it is permitted even to select the refuse, as one peels the shell. Yam Shel Shlomo wrote there that it is forbidden to place legumes or other seeds in water so that the refuse will float to the top or the dirt will fall below; and so it appears from Beis Yosef (Magen Avraham). And this requires investigation: for possibly the Rambam agrees when there is no refuse but only dirt, that then one takes the food from it and leaves the dirt below, for the essence of selecting is only regarding refuse. Also one must examine in the Yerushalmi that which we taught in Beitzah there, "he selects in his usual manner in his lap": R' Chanina said this is Rabban Gamliel, for Rabban Gamliel says one may even rinse and scoop; it implies that Rabban Gamliel does not disagree with the first tanna; and this supports Rabbeinu Yerucham whom Beis Yosef brought. And this requires investigation regarding the Rambam; however in siman 319, seif 8, it implies to forbid, and it is a mishnah on Shabbos, folio 140; and one can distinguish; this requires investigation.

[6] <b>[Levush] Because he extinguishes it, etc.</b> On Shabbos, folio 134, they ask: what differs from meat roasted on coals? And they answer: there it is not possible, here it is possible. Rashi explained: it is not possible to roast from yesterday, for on the day itself it is better; but mustard is possible from yesterday; until here. And the Taz did not look into Rashi's explanation, and likewise at the end of the Ramban's words in Milchemes Hashem on this he did not descend; see there. Also what he wrote, that meat roasted on coals spoils the taste, and so it is explained in Beis Yosef siman 511; until here: he was not precise, for Beis Yosef deals explicitly with smoking fruits, but meat roasted on coals does not spoil the taste, as proven from Rashi's explanation there and all the poskim. This is the language of Magen Avraham: the Bach wrote, if he was occupied on erev Yom Tov and could not do it, it is permitted to do it on Yom Tov; however in siman 504, seif 1, it implies it is forbidden regarding salt, and it is possible that here too he means through an alteration; until here. And one may say salt is different, for all pots need it, and they did not distinguish. But it is puzzling regarding Magen Avraham: behold the Bach ruled the opposite, that even if it was not possible on erev Yom Tov it is forbidden, since there is no need except for the pampered; and it is possible he infers from the Bach that here, in places where all the people of the city eat mustard, it is permitted when impossible on erev Yom Tov; and likewise Yam Shel Shlomo wrote in chapter 2, siman 37; and on this Magen Avraham asked: what differs from salt? In any case it is astonishing, for the essence of the Bach's ruling is missing; and furthermore, according to the Rif and the Rosh it appears forbidden in every case, as explained in the Bach and Maharshal there. Moreover, in Avodas HaKodesh, folio 41, these are his words: meat on coals is permitted, and this is not like sweetening mustard, for mustard is fit without extinguishing a coal, except for the pampered; and moreover it is possible with a coal of metal, and it is more fit and available; until here. And this is that the Gemara mentioned above explained: here it is possible in this way; and what he wrote "and it is fit," etc., he explained that also with meat it is possible to roast near the coals; therefore he explained "and it is more fit and available," which is not so with meat, which is more fit on coals. And in any case we hear a second reason that in every place mustard is forbidden even when impossible from erev Yom Tov. And furthermore the Ramban wrote that the way is to sweeten a great amount, like grinding wheat; if so, certainly forbidden even when impossible from erev Yom Tov, like grinding in siman 495, seif 2; and likewise in Rashi's explanation one may intend thus; consider carefully. And furthermore the Ramban wrote that sweetening mustard is a preparation and is not included in baking and cooking, but in grinding wheat; and in the manner they said: everything needs preparation; mustard needs sweetening, wheat needs grinding, even a person needs preparation; and therefore they forbade it; until here. Behold it is truly comparable to grinding. And incidentally I mention what the Bach wondered on the Ramban mentioned above, that baking is more of a preparation than grinding wheat; until here; as it seems he did not see what the Ran wrote at the beginning of Ein Tzadin in the name of the Ramban; see there, and I shortened.

[8] <b>[Levush] That this is not similar to selecting, etc.</b> It is puzzling to me, for in siman 319, seif 9, he wrote that one who puts [liquid] into a hanging strainer on Shabbos is liable because of selecting, and likewise the Tur wrote, and it is a Gemara in Shabbos, folio 138. And I have no doubt that Rashi's explanation in the mishnah at the beginning of the chapter of R' Eliezer regarding milah misled him here, for he explained that this is not the manner of selecting; but plainly he explained thus according to R' Eliezer, who said one may put into a hanging strainer on Shabbos; but according to the Sages, with whom we hold, that one may not put, certainly this is the manner of selecting. And likewise it appears in Tosafos, folio 134, s.v. "here," etc., and likewise it is explained explicitly in the Ran. And that which is permitted on Yom Tov: it appears to me as Tosafos wrote there, that if he had done it from yesterday it would not have been so good and strong.
