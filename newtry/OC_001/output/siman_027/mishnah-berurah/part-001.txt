(א) שמאל - מדכתיב ידכה בה"א פירוש יד כהה דהיינו השמאל שהיא תשה וכהה ועוד דרשו מדכתיב וקשרתם וכתבתם מה כתיבה בימין שכן דרך בני אדם אף קשירה בימין וכיון דקשירה בימין ממילא הנחה בשמאל ואם הניחו בימין אף בדיעבד לא יצא [אחרונים]:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Left.</b> From the verse written as 'your hand' with an added letter, meaning the weak hand, namely the left which is weak. They further expounded from 'you shall bind' and 'you shall write': just as writing is with the right, as is the way of people, so binding is with the right; since binding is with the right, placement is on the left. If he placed it on the right, even after the fact he did not fulfill the obligation.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>At the head of the bone.</b> This does not mean the literal head of the bone, for there the flesh is still low and until it begins to be high it is not the place of tefillin. Rather, it means the height of the flesh on the bone. Rama comes only to exclude the half of the bone near the armpit. The Gra agrees in practice that the whole place of the kibores is valid; nevertheless below the place of the kibores is invalid according to all. Therefore it is proper to avoid very large tefillin, for often the end of the box rests below the kibores. If one has only large tefillin, it is better to tie them on the upper half where the raised flesh still is.
