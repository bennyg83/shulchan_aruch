<b>משילין</b>. בגמרא משמע דוקא כשהמטר יורד עליהם ומשום הפסד שרי וכ"כ הטור דאל"כ ה"ל כמפנה אוצר עסי' של"ג וכתב היש"ש אפי' טובא ואפי' לגמור האוצר שרי וכ"ד הרי"ף והרמב"ם מדסתמו דבריהם וכ"מ דעת הש"ע דלא כמ"ש בב"י:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>One may lower [produce].</b> From the Gemara it is implied specifically when rain is falling upon them, and because of the loss it is permitted; and so wrote the Tur, for if not so, it would be like one who clears a storehouse; see siman 333. And the Yam Shel Shlomo wrote that even a large amount, and even finishing the storehouse, is permitted; and such is the view of the Rif and the Rambam, since they left their words unqualified; and so appears to be the view of the Shulchan Aruch, not as written in the Beis Yosef.
