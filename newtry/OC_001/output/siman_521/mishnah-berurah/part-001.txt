**** ENGLISH ****
(ד) And one may not lower them through windows — meaning: if the roof was surrounded by partitions and in one of the partitions there was a window, it is forbidden to lower them through there with a rope, for there is extra exertion to raise them to the window and lower them; and even if he wishes only to raise them to the window and throw them through there to the ground — likewise forbidden, for in this in any case there is exertion to raise them; however, if the window was low level with the roof so there is no extra exertion, it is reasonable that it is permitted like through a skylight:
