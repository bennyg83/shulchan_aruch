**** HEBREW ****
<b>א) [סעיף א'] והוא שתהיה עשויה לצל.  </b>דאע"ג דסוכה לשם חג לא בעינן לשם סוכה בעינן ולצל הוא דמקריא סוכה שסוככת מן החורב. רש"י סוכה ח' ע"ב ויהא פי' דקרא לפ"ז חג הסוכות תעשם דקאי מלת תעשה אמלת סוכה כלימר תעשה לשם סכך בעלמא להגין תחת צלם אעפ"י שלא נעשית לשם חג. לבוש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1) [Seif 1] Provided that it was made for shade. </b>Although we do not require that a sukkah be made for the sake of the festival, we do require that it be made for the purpose of a sukkah. It is being made for shade that gives it the designation of a sukkah, because it shelters one from the heat. Rashi, Sukkah 8b. According to this, the meaning of the verse, "You shall make the festival of Sukkos," is that the word "make" refers to the word "sukkah," meaning that one should make it merely as sheltering s'chach, to protect those beneath its shade, even though it was not made for the sake of the festival. Levush.
