<b>אם מעט ואם הרבה.</b> ס"ל דמ"ש שם ואם באו לחשבון אסור קאי אף אם התנו בתחלה וכמ"ש בפ"י מהל' מ"א וש"ע י"ד סי' רצ"ד סי"ג ואם התנו כו' ובלבד שלא יבואו לחשבון כיצד כגון שיחשוב כו' ע"ש:

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And if they did not stipulate.</b> Meaning, we rule stringently. The difficulty there is that the Gemara should have resolved the question by concluding that it is prohibited, for otherwise why was Rava embarrassed? One must say that according to the latter version the matter remained doubtful. Since the first version is stated anonymously by the Gemara, the halachah follows it. Ran.
