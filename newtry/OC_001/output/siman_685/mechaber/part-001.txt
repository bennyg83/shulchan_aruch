**** ENGLISH ****
The order of the Four Torah Portions; it contains seven clauses. When Rosh Chodesh Adar that adjoins Nissan falls on Shabbos, we read the Torah portion of Shekalim—that is, Parashas Ki Tisa through the verse about making the copper laver—and the haftarah is Vayichres Yehoyada. We take out three Torah scrolls: from the first we read that week’s regular portion; from the second, that of Rosh Chodesh; and from the third the maftir reads from Parashas Shekalim:

חל ר"ח אדר הסמוך לניסן בתוך ימי השבוע <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ז" data-order="7"></i>ואפילו בע"ש מקדימין לקרות פרשת שקלים בשבת שלפניו ומפסיקין בשניה כדי שתהא פרשת זכור בשבת הסמוכה לפורים מלפניה <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ח" data-order="8"></i>ואם חל פורים בערב שבת מקדימים לקרות פרשת זכור בשבת שלפניו:
**** ENGLISH ****
Should Rosh Chodesh Adar adjoining Nissan occur on a weekday—even on erev Shabbos—they advance the reading of Parashas Shekalim to the preceding Shabbos, and they interrupt on the second Shabbos so that Parashas Zachor falls on the Shabbos immediately before Purim. If Purim falls on erev Shabbos, they advance Parashas Zachor to the Shabbos before that:

**** ENGLISH ****
The days on which Rosh Chodesh Adar II may properly be fixed: the mnemonic zavadlu matches weekdays to dates, and samech–lamed for the Sabbaths on which we interrupt study: zayin–tes–vav—when Rosh Chodesh is Shabbos, we interrupt on the fifteenth of that month; the sign is zayin–tes–vav. When it is Monday, we interrupt on the sixth; sign beis–vav. When Wednesday, on the fourth; sign daled–daled. When Friday, on two dates—the second and sixteenth; sign vav beis yud vav:
