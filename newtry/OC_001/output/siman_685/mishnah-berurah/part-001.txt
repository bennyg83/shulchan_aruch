13) <b>For you.</b> Until "you shall eat matzos." When Rosh Chodesh Nisan falls on Shabbos, which is then Parashas HaChodesh, they read the haftarah of Parashas HaChodesh, not "The heavens are My throne," see Taz in the name of Levush. They take out three scrolls. In one they read the weekly portion, then they place the second and do not say Kaddish; they lift the first, roll it, open the second, and read the Rosh Chodesh portion from "and on the Shabbos day" until "and its libation." They then place the third and say Kaddish, lift the second and roll it, open the third, and read the maftir in Parashas HaChodesh, Shaarei Ephraim.

16) <b>To a place where there is a minyan, etc.</b> This means even if he has a Torah scroll in his house but does not have a minyan in his house. Even if we say that a minyan for Parashas Zachor is only rabbinic, in any case the main reading of Zachor is Torah-level. Terumas HaDeshen wrote that hearing Parashas Zachor read with ten is more important than reading the megillah publicly, for according to most poskim, reading the megillah in its proper time suffices individually. Therefore, if he cannot fulfill both, he should seek to fulfill Parashas Zachor publicly. But Magen Avraham inclines that it is better to come to a place where they read the megillah publicly, and regarding Parashas Zachor he can fulfill, in pressing circumstances, through hearing the reading on Purim of Parashas Vayavo Amalek, for through this too he remembers the deed of Amalek and fulfills his obligation. That is to say, he fulfills Torah-level. Since we hold above in siman 60 that mitzvos require intent, at the time of hearing this section he would need to intend to fulfill the positive commandment of Zachor through it. But in my humble opinion, the main ruling of Magen Avraham requires analysis, for in the Torah it is written: "Remember what Amalek did to you, etc., how he met you on the way and cut off your rear, etc.; you shall erase, etc." The intent is not to forget what Amalek did to us, and that we tell this to our children and to our generations, saying to them, "such the wicked one did to us," and therefore we were commanded to erase his name, as the Ramban wrote in his explanation. This is not mentioned in Parashas Vayavo Amalek.

18) <b>With their melody and cantillation.</b> Know that some say one must read "zecher Amalek" with a tzeirei, and some say one must read "zecher Amalek" with a segol. Therefore it is proper for the reader to read both, to fulfill the obligation according to Heaven. Maharil wrote that on all four special sections, we do not mention souls and we say Tzidkascha Tzedek, Darkei Moshe. Regarding Av HaRachamim, in Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Hagahos Minhagim it says that one should say Av HaRachamim on them, but in Darkei Moshe in the name of Maharil it implies that we do not say Av HaRachamim on them; so copied Derech HaChaim. Regarding the fast of Shovavim Tat, see Baer Hetev and Shaarei Teshuvah.
