**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(s.k. 5) With, etc.</b> And nevertheless it is difficult: and what of it, etc.? For it is not correct to exempt dough from challah and to knead less than a shiur, as it is in Pesachim, which Taz brought; unless there is a necessity. And so wrote Derishah, etc.: this is difficulty 3, for even according to the Rosh who exempts, that is specifically fruit juice that is not of the seven liquids; and if so, why when kneading with wine is it permitted to make less than a shiur? For of that sort, according to all, it is obligated; rather [the ruling] is regarding egg liquid and the same for other fruit juices that are not of the seven liquids, since it is in the Yerushalmi that if he kneaded dough with them it is not a connection regarding tumah; and if a tevul yom touched part of the dough he invalidated only the place of his touch and not the whole dough. And the same applies that it is not a connection regarding challah, and it is like dough less than a shiur; therefore one must say that it refers to fruit juices that are not of the seven liquids. And with this difficulty 3 is resolved. But the first two difficulties are still not resolved.
