**** ENGLISH ****
{14} For example, upon the table. For on a weekday the way is to sift upon a chest or kneading-trough; and if this person's way is to sift even on a weekday upon a table, he should make a different change; and all the more so if he sifts upon the back of the sieve from the other side, it is permitted. And the later authorities wrote that all this is if the second sieve does not have holes smaller than the first sieve, and whatever came out in the first will come out in the second; but if the second sieve has fine holes, it is certainly forbidden, for the coarse flour will remain in the sieve and the fine will come out, and it is like a first sifting. And Machatzit HaShekel wrote that according to this, even in our times when flours are sifted at the mill house, nevertheless one should not sift them again at home even by means of a change, for we see that sifting at the mill house does not remove all the waste, and the home sieve removes [more], and it is like a first sifting.
