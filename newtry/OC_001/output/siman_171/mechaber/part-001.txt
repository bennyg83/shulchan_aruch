**** ENGLISH ****
A person can use bread as they need to, as long as they do not ruin it, but they may not ruin it. Therefore, one may not leave raw meat on bread or pass a full cup over it, not lean a bowl on it… and do not use wine even for a mitzvah like hand-washing. And do not throw bread because it is disgrace to food. And just as one may not throw bread, so too it is prohibited to throw any food that will become distasteful as a result.

**** ENGLISH ****
It is permitted to eat diysa (meaning: hulled wheat that is cooked) with bread in place of a spoon, provided that one eats the bread afterward. Those who are scrupulous eat a small amount of the bread each time they put it in their mouth together with the diysa. {Rama: Rema: And the bread that remains afterward, one eats it.}
