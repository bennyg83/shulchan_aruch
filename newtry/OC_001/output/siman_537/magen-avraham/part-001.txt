**** ENGLISH ****
<b>That he not give him wages.</b> This requires further study, for in siman 252 and 244 we are more lenient when he gives him wages, for a non-Jew acts for his own sake; and one must say that since it is melacha of something attached [to the ground], a fixed [wage] does not help, as written in siman 244; and the same applies that [doing it] for free is forbidden. Rather, since fertilizing is not a complete melacha, if the non-Jew did it on his own one need not protest; but when he gives him wages, they will know that he is acting with the Jew's knowledge, and they will conclude that he hired him to do melacha for days, as above.
