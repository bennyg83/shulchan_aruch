**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(In Shulchan Aruch se'if 5) They cry out over it alone, etc.</b> See the Tur, and see se'if 11 and se'if 12. And the questioner in Responsa Shevus Yaakov, volume 3, siman 40, raised a difficulty from the Gemara of Bava Kamma, folio 81, which proves that over wet [skin] chafing they sound the alarm even on Shabbos (and see Birkei Yosef in the name of that earlier authority, who sensed this difficulty regarding the Shulchan Aruch se'if 12); and see there that Shevus Yaakov replied that it is evident to one who examines there that Rashi and the Rambam do not have the word "on Shabbos" in their reading; see there.
