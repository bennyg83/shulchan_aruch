(5) <b>At a gathering of idolaters</b> - This means when they are reclining to eat at a meal; but if they were not reclining at all, it is permitted to bless over their spices. So Taz concludes as practical law in siman 298 seif katan 5. Nevertheless, he wrote at the end that initially it is good to be concerned for the view of Rabbeinu Yonah, who holds that ordinary spices of idolaters ultimately stand to be sold for idolatry and one should not bless over them unless it appears that they do not stand for idolatry, such as where he is a merchant whose way is to sell them to many people, or his way is to put the spices in his food; then there is no prohibition to bless over them even according to Rabbeinu Yonah.
