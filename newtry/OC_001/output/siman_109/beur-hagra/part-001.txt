**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 1, One who enters, etc. </b>This follows Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, since the halachah follows him even against Rabbi Yochanan, as stated at the beginning of chapter 4 of Megillah, and certainly against Rav, and all the more so against Rav Huna. See Tosafos, Berachos 40b, s.v. Rabbi Yochanan. The anonymous Mishnah in chapter 3 of Megillah likewise follows his view. This is certainly so regarding Modim, for Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi agrees with Rav Huna, as indicated by the Gemara's question, “What is their dispute?”
