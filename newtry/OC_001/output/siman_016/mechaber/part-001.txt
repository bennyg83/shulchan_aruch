**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The measure of a <i>tallis</i> — it contains one clause.</b><br>The measure of a <i>tallis</i> obligated in <i>tzitzis</i> is that with it one covers in length and width one’s head and the majority of a small child who walks alone in the market and needs no other to watch him. Rema: Then it is obligated in <i>tzitzis</i>, specifically when an adult sometimes wears it casually and goes out with it to the market (Beit Yosef citing Ben Chabib and Mahariya).
