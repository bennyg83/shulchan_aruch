**** ENGLISH ****
A non-Jew who lent to an Israelite on his Chametz and took it as a pledge in his hand, and said to him: "From now it shall be yours if I do not repay you by a certain time," and the time arrived and he did not repay him, it is permitted even if the time he set did not arrive until after Pesach. Since when the time arrived and he did not repay him, it is acquired by him retroactively, and thus it is considered to be the Chametz of the non-Jew.
