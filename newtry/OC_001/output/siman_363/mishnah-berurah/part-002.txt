(ו) Like a partition — and if around the city there is a ditch ten tefachim deep and four tefachim wide, which is like a partition, and the ditch was closed with earth and stones — if the closing does not rise more than ten amot, behold it is like an opening and does not forbid; but if it is more than ten amot, behold it is like a breach:
