**** HEBREW ****
<b>וטוב שלא כו'. </b>כמ"ש אראב"צ כו' והתענינו ולא השלמנו מפני שי"ט כו'. ואף ע"ג דאם התחילו קי"ל דמשלימין כמ"ש ספ"ב דתענית שם ת"צ אבל בתענית יחיד מפסיקין אפי' התחיל כמש"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And it is good not to, etc.</b> As is written by R' Avraham (Aravatz; uncertain), etc.: "and we fasted and we did not complete [the fast], because it is a festival day," etc. And even though if they began we hold that they complete, as is written at the end of chapter 2 of Taanis there regarding communal fasts; but for an individual's fast they interrupt even if he began, as written above.

**** HEBREW ****
<b>יש מי כו'. </b>דימים אלו הוחדו לתענית כמ"ש במתני' וגמ' וכן בתוספתא כמש"ל סי' תקס"ח ס"ב ע"ש ועתוס' דב"ק פ"ב א' ד"ה כדי כו' ורא"ש שם ובפ' בתרא דמ"ס הלכה ג' והתלמידים מתענים שני וחמישי מפני חילול השם ומפני כבוד ההיכל שנשרף ועבב"י בסי' תכ"ט:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>There are those who, etc.</b> For these days were designated for fasting, as is written in the Mishnah and Gemara, and likewise in the Tosefta, as written above in siman 568, se'if 2; see there; and see Tosafos on Bava Kamma folio 82a, s.v. "in order that," etc., and the Rosh there; and in the last chapter of Masseches Soferim, halacha 3: and the disciples fast on Monday and Thursday because of chilul Hashem and because of the honor of the Heichal that was burned; and see Beis Yosef in siman 429.

**** HEBREW ****
<b>ולעתיד לבא כו'. </b>כמ"ש צום הרביעי כו' יהיה לששון ולשמחה וכמ"ש קרי ליה צום וקרי ליה ששון כו' וכן אמרו באותן י"ט הכתובין במגילת תענית והני נמי כו' וקי"ל כותיה וכמ"ש והפכתי אבלם לששון:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And in the future to come, etc.</b> As is written, "the fast of the fourth," etc., "shall be for joy and for gladness"; and as is written, "He called it a fast and He called it joy," etc.; and so they said regarding those festivals written in Megillas Taanis, "and these too," etc.; and we hold like him; and as is written, "and I will turn their mourning into joy."
