And in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Shabbos, R' Yonasan bar Idi in the name of R' Yehoshua ben Levi: they may ask laws of the bathhouse in the bathhouse, etc. As this case: Rashba entered to bathe with R' Meir; he asked him whether rinsing is permitted; he told him forbidden, etc. And not so Shmuel, who asked Rav whether answering amen in a filthy place is permitted; he told him forbidden, and forbidden — for I told you forbidden. A tanna was found who taught: they may ask laws of the bathhouse in the bathhouse, and laws of the bathroom in the bathroom. And it would appear the halacha is like R' Yehoshua ben Levi; but in our Gemara it is not stated so, as written above. And in our version of the Yerushalmi a tanna was found who taught: they may not ask — and this is a scribal error.
