And even if the renouncers were many, they are also permitted to carry out from their houses to their fellow's houses. And it is not similar to one who renounces the domain of his courtyard, who has no permission to carry out from his house to the courtyard even if he also renounced the domain of his house, until they hold first; and even if they held, it only helps with individual who renounced to many, but not individual to individual, as above in siman 380; and all the more so many to individual — there, since he had a share in the courtyard but renounced to them, when he carries out from his house to the courtyard it appears he reverts from his renunciation and holds his share in the courtyard; which is not so when he carries out to his fellow's house, where he never had a share — it is not relevant to say he thereby holds his fellow's domain; and also regarding his house itself it is not relevant to say he reverts and acquires it through carrying his vessels out from it to his fellow's house, for on the contrary he withdraws from it.
