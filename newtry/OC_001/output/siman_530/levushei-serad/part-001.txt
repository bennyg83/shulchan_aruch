**** HEBREW ****
<small>מ"א סק"א</small> משמע דס"ל שמלאכת חוה"מ דאורייתא וכו'. מדכת' שהיה נראה לחכמים להתיר ואלו להאומרים דמן התורה מותרין כל המלאכות הל"ל שהי' נרא' לחכמים לאסור:
**** ENGLISH ****
Magen Avraham s.k. 1: It implies that he holds that melacha of Chol HaMoed is according to the Torah, etc. From his writing "that it would have seemed to the Sages to permit"; for according to those who say that according to the Torah all the melachos are permitted, he should have said "that it would have seemed to the Sages to forbid."
