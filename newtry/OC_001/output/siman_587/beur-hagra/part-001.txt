<b>ס"ג השומע. </b>מחלוקת בירושלמי וכפי' הרמב"ן הביאו הרא"ש פ"ד דר"ה ס"ח וכן הכריח הרשב"א כהאי פירושא דאי כפי' הראשון דאמתני' תקע בראשונה ומשך כו' קאי א"כ אפי' תקיעה ראשונה לא עלתה לו דהא הפסיק בינתיים וכמ"ש בסי' תק"ץ ס"ח וע' מ"א בשם הפוסקים דהלכה כסברא ראשונה וכמ"ש הרמב"ם וש"פ דקי"ל בכ"מ כמ"ד שהוקבע בגמ' באחרונה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Se'if 3, "the one who hears."</b> There is a dispute in the Yerushalmi; and according to the interpretation of the Ramban that the Rosh cited in chapter 4 of Rosh Hashanah, se'if 8; and likewise the Rashba compelled [the conclusion] according to this interpretation, for if [it were] according to the first interpretation, that [it] refers to our Mishnah "He blew the first [blast] and prolonged [it]," etc., then even the first teki'ah would not count for him, since he interrupted in between, as is written in siman 590, se'if 8. And see the Magen Avraham in the name of the poskim that the halachah follows the first view; and as the Rambam and other poskim wrote, for we hold in every place like the opinion that was established last in the Gemara.
