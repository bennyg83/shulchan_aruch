**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And one should not practice, etc.</b> As it says there: Rav Nachman was sitting, etc. And in Yerushalmi, Chalah chapter 1, halachah 1: There they were concerned for the Great Fast for two days. Rav Chisda said to them: Why do you put yourselves into possible danger? There is a presumption that the court does not delay. Bar Avuy, the son of Shmuel bar Rav Yitzchak, was concerned for himself and fasted two days; his intestines ruptured and he died, meaning that his bowels were severed. Also according to the law there is no need to be concerned, as stated at the beginning of Yom Tov, that we are expert in the fixing of the month; the practice is only because they sent from there, 'Be careful,' etc. This is not so regarding Yom Kippur, where there was never such a custom. See Rashi there, 5b, s.v. 'Mi lo modeh,' etc.
