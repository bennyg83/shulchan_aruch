**** ENGLISH ****
A Jew should not cut them. See above, siman 649, seif 1, and siman 666, seif 10. A sukkah made like an alleyway, and each of the two open walls was filled with a woven wall and a doorway-form in the middle, but they do not have the measure of the length of a sukkah, is valid (Maharav, part 2, siman 10). A sukkah that is not guarded from thieves is valid. Maharil, who brought the words of the Mordechai at the end of chapter HaYashen, and so too Hagahot Maimoni, did not say it is invalid except in a place where one is afraid to sleep in the sukkah because of thieves and robbers, as is proven by the language of the Mordechai. But an open sukkah from which they can steal was not discussed by the Mordechai, for by day he guards the sukkah, and at night he brings the money into his house. The sukkah does not need to be a place where his money is guarded, for the Merciful One was particular only about his dwelling and not about guarding his money. Specifically regarding him we require "you shall dwell" in the manner that you live, but not regarding his money (Responsa Rema, siman 29).
