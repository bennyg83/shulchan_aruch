**** HEBREW ****
<b>לא יצא. </b> אשה שאירע טבילתה בליל ט"ו אי מותרת לאכול בשר בסעוד' היום עיין ת' ד"מ דסיים ומ"מ אין לה לאכול כ"א תבשיל של בשר. ול"ד בשבת. ומסיק עוד דבי"ט אפשר להסתפק:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>He has not fulfilled.</b> Regarding a woman whose immersion occurred on the night of the fifteenth, whether she is permitted to eat meat at the daytime meal, see Responsa Divrei Menachem, who concludes that nevertheless she should eat only a meat dish. This is not comparable to Shabbos. He concludes further that regarding Yom Tov, perhaps there is room for uncertainty.
