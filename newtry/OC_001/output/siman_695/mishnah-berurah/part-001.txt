(20) <b>Kinds of foods.</b> But not garments or other things. The same applies to drink, which is acceptable, for drinking is included in eating. It is likewise sufficient to send one food and one drink. We require a type of food that is cooked, not raw meat, for mishloach manos implies portions fit to eat immediately. Some say that since it has been slaughtered and is ready to be cooked immediately, it is permitted.

(25) <b>And a woman is obligated, and so on.</b> For they were all included in that miracle, and she must rejoice and gladden the hearts of the poor. It is written, “the Jews established and accepted,” and so on, and women are included as well. The Magen Avraham wrote: I have not seen people being careful about this, and perhaps it applies specifically to a widow. But a married woman, whose husband sends on her behalf to several people, may be different. Nevertheless, one should be stringent.
