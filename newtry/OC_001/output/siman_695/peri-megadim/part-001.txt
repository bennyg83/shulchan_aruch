<b>חייב</b> עט״ז ועב״י מפרש בע״א ועא״ר אות ב׳ מפרש ג״כ וכתב יזהרו בנט״י ויהיה שמחה של מצוה וסעודת פורים ומתנות לאביונים ומשלוח מנות י״ל צריך ובסי׳ תע״ה ובמ״א י״ד דמצה שנהנה יצא אע״ג דלא איכוון י״ל סעודה עיקר לזכר הנס שנעשה ע״י סעודה ע״כ בעי כוונה וצ״ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Is obligated.</b> See the Taz, and see the Beis Yosef, who explains it differently. See also Eliyah Rabbah, letter 2, who likewise explains it and writes that they should be careful with washing hands, and that it should be joy of a mitzvah. Regarding the Purim meal, gifts to the poor, and mishloach manos, one can say that intent is required. In siman 475, and in Magen Avraham, seif katan 14, regarding matzah, since he derives benefit from it he has fulfilled the mitzvah even though he did not intend. One can say that the meal is primarily a remembrance of the miracle that occurred through a meal; therefore it requires intent. This requires analysis.
