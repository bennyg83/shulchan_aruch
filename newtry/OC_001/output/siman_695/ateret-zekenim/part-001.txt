First. See Shaarei Teshuvah and Rema, siman 132. If he forgot to say Al HaNissim in Birkas HaMazon, when he reaches HaRachaman he should say: May the Merciful One perform for us miracles and wonders as You performed for our forefathers, and so on, in the days of Mordechai and Esther, and so on, as the Kol Bo writes. This was already clarified in siman 187 and in siman 682. If he forgot Al HaNissim entirely, even though in davening we do not make him repeat, as above in siman 663, seif 2, regarding the Purim meal we do make him repeat, as the Maharshal writes, and Mateh Moshe, siman 1012. See above, siman 662, and see Terumas HaDeshen, siman 38.
