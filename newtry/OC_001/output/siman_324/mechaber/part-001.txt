But it is forbidden to rub them by hand as those who prepare animal food do to make them easier to eat — for making something edible from something inedible is permitted, but going to the trouble of preparing something that is already edible to make it more suitable, we do not do. {Rama: Rema: Some say one may only untie a knot that is not permanent.}
