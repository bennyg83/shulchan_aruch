<b>And behold.</b> What is written [that] a toldah of water is like water: and above in siman 160 se'if 10, a toldah of water if one crushed a fish eye for netilas yadayim; and in Yoreh De'ah beginning of siman 93; see there. And this requires further study regarding fish fat on Pesach, whether it is fruit juice like goose fat: for at first glance it is not an absolute general rule to say that a toldah of water causes chimutz, and you have only what Chazal wrote explicitly. And behold among the seven liquids some are machshir and receive tumah and do not cause chimutz; and see Tum'as Ochalin chapter 10 law 2: animal urine is not a toldah of water, and human urine is a toldah of water, and that is a Tosefta; so Kesef Mishnah there. And in Machshirin chapter 6 mishnah 5: human urine whether of adults or of minors is a toldah of water; and the Rash explained there: liquids that come from the orifice of the rectum: behold human feces do not cause chimutz; see there mishnah 7, feces, etc.; and in the Rambam, Tum'as Ochalin chapter 10; and see Chok Yaakov. See in Machshirin there mishnah 6: and his flux and spit convey tumah and are machshir; and in mishnah 7, the hot springs of Tiberias neither convey tumah nor are machshir; and if so this requires much further study, for one may not learn from those that are machshir and convey tumah that they cause chimutz, nor from what is not machshir that it does not cause chimutz; and the hot springs of Tiberias imply certainly that they cause chimutz; and see siman 425. And see Tiferes Yisrael there on mishnah 5 regarding the hot springs of Tiberias by way of the urethra; see there. Sour apples: see Magen Avraham 462 and Peri Chadash here regarding red vinegar; see there. Woman's milk: Eliyah Rabbah left in letter 12 as requiring further study; milk with a chirik is fruit juice. And blood and milk with a chirik that are fruit juice for stringency and are not nullified in sixty; or fish blood and human blood where there is no positive commandment. And animal urine: see Peri Chadash and Chok Yaakov; and God willing in letter 4 it will be explained further.

<b>עח״י</b> אות י״ז במ״ש אפילו פחות משיעור מיל דלא כנ״ץ יע״ש ובא״ר ט״ז יע״ש ובאמת לא הבנתי מתחלה דברי הח״י גם הנ״ץ לא כתב כ״א מ״ש בלי הפסק שלא יצטרפו השהיות כדלעיל תנ״ט ס״ב ואמנם ראיתי כי דברי ח״י בנוים על אדני השכל והאמת. כי התה״ד בסי׳ קכ״ג פלפל אי השהיות מצטרפות וכתב דגדול אחד הביא ראיה מדלף משמע שיש צירוף בין טפה לטפה כשיעור כו׳ וסיים התה״ד שיש לחלק בין עסק גמור אין שהיות מצטרפות אבל עסק מועט אין מבטל וראיה מפסחים ל״ט ב׳ א״ר פפא והוא דעביד טיף להדי טיף ואין שהות בנתיים והשתא אי בעלמא שהיות מצטרפות מה קמ״ל ר״פ כאן אלא בעסק גמור אין שהיות מצטרפות ודלף טורד דאין עסק גמור בעינן טיף להדי טיף דלא ליצטרפו השהיות ואפשר שזה דעת הגדול שהתיר שאין שהיות מצטרפות אלא שקצר בלשונו עכ״ל. הנך רואה ממ״ש התה״ד תחלה דגדול אחד הביא ראיה להתיר מטיף טיף משמ״ע שיש צירוף השהיות בין טפה לטפה כשיעור (מיל) מלת משמ״ע הם דברי תה״ד שרוצה לפרש דברי הגדול דהיה סובר טיף טיף זה אחר זה ויש קצת הפסק בין נפילת טפה א׳ לב׳ על הקמח וכ״מ קצת מפירש״י טפה אחר טפה סמוך ותיכף ואין שהות בנתיים וכ״ה לשון הר״ן ז״ל שם והתה״ד מפרש דאף משהו לית הפסק בין טפה לטפה וז״ש רש״י אין שהות בנתיים היינו כלל וכ״מ לשון הט״ז בלי הפסק דהיינו קודם גמר נפילת טפה א׳ בא טירוד הב׳ והוי כעסק תמידי אלא שעסק מועט הוא הלכך בעינן שלא יהיה הפסק כלל הא יש הפסק ובין כולם כשיעור מיל השהיות מצטרפין משא״כ בעסק גמור אין שהיות מצטרפות וזה ג״כ דעת הגדול שרוצה להתיר היינו בעסק גמור וראיה מדהוצרך ר״פ לאשמעינן בדלף ש״מ בעסק גמור אין שהיות מצטרפות (דלא כמו שהבין התה״ד בדבריו תחלה וז״ש אלא שקצר בלשונו יע״ש היטב) ועמ״א תנ״ט אות ד׳ והר״ב בתנ״ט בהג״ה ס״ד סתם משמע אף בעסק גמור שהיות מצטרפות הדרא קושיא לדוכתיה למה השמיענו ר״פ בדלף היל״ל אמשנה שלש נשים עוסקות בבצק ס״פ א״ע דשהיות מצטרפות כמ״ש התה״ד שם וש״מ לדידן טיף טיף בלי שום הפסק הא אם היה שום הפסק אעפ״י דבצירוף כל השהיות לא היה שיעור מיל מ״מ כיון דמתחלת טירוד הדלף עד סופו היה שיעור מיל ויותר ושהה קצת בנתיים החמיץ דאין עסק גמור וכאמור וז״ש הח״י דלא כנ״ץ וכאמור. ולפ״ז שלש פירושים בהא דטיף טיף ודברי הח״י אמיתיים והתלמידים לא ימהרו ללמוד כ״א במתון. והבן זה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>See Chok Yaakov</b> letter 17 regarding what he wrote, even less than the measure of a mil, and not like Natz; see there; and in Eliyah Rabbah 16; see there. And in truth at first I did not understand Chok Yaakov's words; also Natz wrote only what is written "without interruption," that the delays not combine, as above in siman 459 se'if 2. However I saw that Chok Yaakov's words are built on pillars of reason and truth. For Terumas HaDeshen in siman 123 debated whether the delays combine, and wrote that a certain great authority brought a proof from a drip, which implies there is combination between drop and drop for the measure, etc.; and Terumas HaDeshen concluded that one should distinguish: with complete work the delays do not combine, but with slight work it does not nullify; and a proof from Pesachim 39b: Rav Pappa said, and that is when it is done drop upon drop with no delay between them. And now, if in general the delays combine, what does Rav Pappa teach us here? Rather, with complete work the delays do not combine; and a continuous drip that is not complete work requires drop upon drop so that the delays not combine. And it is possible that this was the view of the great authority who permitted, that the delays do not combine, only that he abbreviated in his wording; end quote. You see from what Terumas HaDeshen first wrote, that a certain great authority brought a proof to permit from drop by drop, [and] "which implies" there is combination of the delays between drop and drop for the measure (of a mil): the word "which implies" are Terumas HaDeshen's words, wanting to explain the great authority, that he held drop by drop one after another with some interruption between the falling of drop one and drop two upon the flour. And so it is somewhat implied from Rashi's explanation: drop after drop, nearby and immediately, with no delay between them; and so is the Ran's wording of blessed memory there. And Terumas HaDeshen explains that even any amount [means] there is no interruption between drop and drop; and what Rashi wrote "no delay between them" means none at all; and so is implied by Taz's wording "without interruption," namely that before the completion of the falling of drop one the continuous striking of drop two comes, and it is like continuous work, only that it is slight work; therefore we require that there be no interruption at all; but if there is interruption, and among all of them for the measure of a mil the delays combine; which is not so with complete work, where the delays do not combine. And this too is the view of the great authority who wants to permit, namely with complete work; and a proof from the fact that Rav Pappa needed to teach us regarding a drip: hence with complete work the delays do not combine (not as Terumas HaDeshen at first understood his words; and that is what he wrote "only that he abbreviated in his wording"; see there carefully). And see Magen Avraham 459 letter 4; and the Rav in 459 in the gloss of se'if 4 wrote without qualification, which implies even with complete work the delays combine: the difficulty returns to its place, why did Rav Pappa teach us regarding a drip? He should have said on the mishnah "three women occupy themselves with dough" at the end of chapter Kol Sha'ah, that the delays combine, as Terumas HaDeshen wrote there. And hence for us [the law of] drop by drop is without any interruption; but if there was any interruption, even though in the combination of all the delays there was not the measure of a mil, nevertheless since from the beginning of the continuous drip until its end there was the measure of a mil and more, and he delayed a little between them, it caused chimutz, for it is not complete work, as said. And that is what Chok Yaakov wrote, not like Natz, as said. And according to this there are three explanations in this of drop by drop, and Chok Yaakov's words are true; and the students should not hurry to learn except with deliberation. And understand this.
