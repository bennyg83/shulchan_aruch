<small>(בט"ז סק"ד)</small> וק' לי מפ"ג שאכלו וכו' נ"ב לק"מ דהרא"ש דקדק מדבריו שצריך לאשמועינן טעמא והלכתא אבל חדא לא:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>In Taz, seif katan 4:</small> “This is difficult to me from the third chapter, ‘Three who ate,’ etc.” Note: This is no difficulty. The Rosh inferred from his words that both the reason and the practical halachah must be taught, but one without the other would not suffice.
