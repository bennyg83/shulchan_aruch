**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And to eat.</b> This follows Pesachim 99b, in accordance with Rabbi Yose, for the Mishnah there follows his view. It must refer to eating without formally establishing a meal, as explained below; this also follows from the wording, “he may eat,” and so forth. It must therefore concern the sort of meal he ordinarily eats, as explained above.
