**** ENGLISH ****
One who borrows from his fellow a <i>tallis</i> without <i>tzitzis</i> is exempt from tying <i>tzitzis</i> on it for thirty days, as it says “your garment,” not another’s; but after thirty days he is obligated rabbinically because it appears his own. Rema: If he returned it within thirty [days] and borrowed again it does not combine — we require thirty consecutive days (Nimukei Yosef <i>Hilchos Tzitzis</i>). A loan when it already has <i>tzitzis</i> — one blesses on it immediately.
