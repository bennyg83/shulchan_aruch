**** ENGLISH ****
It appears to me that this is why the Mechaber specifically writes “on Motzaei Shabbos.” Certainly Motzaei Shabbos is not meant exclusively. Rather, he intends to exclude Friday night, when the abridged blessing containing the essence of the seven blessings is recited, and through it one who forgot to mention Shabbos fulfills his obligation. Beit Yosef gives an additional reason, namely that the Maariv prayer is optional. Since Darkei Moshe writes that this custom is not practiced among us, I have been brief.
