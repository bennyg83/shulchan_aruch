And even so—see Taz: Mahari holds regarding vessels that on account of concern [of falling], less than three tefachim is also forbidden, and even to make himself heard to the congregation it is forbidden—only a high place of three tefachim on the ground and the like, where there is no concern, is permitted to make himself heard (see Netziv and what Maharshal wrote). And on Shabbos in Bikkur Cholim 12b—the reason regarding a chair is that even less than three tefachim is not nullified, and on account of height likewise here on account of height it is permitted to make himself heard on a chair as well; but it is impossible to say regarding a chair "all the more so" there would also be height—for perforce a measure is required, and that is three tefachim. And that which [the mishnah] only said "one shall not stand in a high place alone"—this means a vessel independently constitutes a separate domain and is permitted, as he teaches us. And see in our novellae Berachos 10b: Rabbah bar Chanah—"one shall not stand in a high place"; the Tosefta—"one shall not stand on a chair" etc., and not in a high place—and Rabbah bar Chanah holds "high place" alone, from which it appears that a chair too is on account of height, like the Taz, and so it appears to be correct.
