**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif katan 1, and even, etc.</b> If so, regarding an elderly or sick person whom he permits, etc. Meaning, if a bed or chair were forbidden only when three handbreadths high, the reason would be that one needs a low place, for there is no haughtiness before the Omnipresent, as it says, 'Out of the depths I called You, Hashem.' If so, it is understood that a sick or elderly person who lives in an upper story may pray there, since this is not haughtiness because he lives there. So too regarding a bed or chair for an elderly or sick person, since it is known that sitting on a bed or chair is not haughtiness for them. But according to the Rama, who forbids a bed or chair even when they are not three high because of fear that prevents concentration, what difference is there between a sick or elderly person and anyone else, since he cannot concentrate?
