**** ENGLISH ****
Those Matzot with which one does not fullfil his obligation. Containg 4 section One does not fulfill his obligation with husk bread, bran bread, rather, one may knead husks and bran into a dough and fulfill [the obligation] with it. One may fulfill [the obligation] with fine flour Matzah which is excessively clean and we don't say that this is not Bread of Affliction {Rama: Rema: and it's initially better to refrain from making the Matzah excessively wide to the extent that it is like a big [Ashisha] loaf (Ma"hariv).}
