[ד] <b>ויוציא וכו'.</b> אף שיש לו מעות בריוח דאסור בסעיף ד' מותר בזה דהא הרבה פוסקים מתירין בצינעה אפילו אינה מוציאה לשמחת יום טוב וכן נראה בסוף סימן זה במלוה בריבית אפילו בפרהסיא דעיקר דבפוסקים דהלוואה לא דמי לפרק משוא רק משום חומרא אמרינן דיוציא וכו' לכך מותר אפילו בשלו כן משמע בתשובת מהריב"ל ח"ס סימן ס"א:
**** ENGLISH ****
[4] <b>And he should spend, etc.</b> Even if he has money with ease, which is forbidden in se'if 4, it is permitted in this case; for many poskim permit in private even when he does not spend for the joy of Yom Tov. And so it appears at the end of this siman regarding a loan on interest, even publicly: that the main view among the poskim is that a loan is not comparable to merchandise dealing, only that out of stringency we say he should spend, etc.; therefore it is permitted even with his own [money]; so it is implied in Responsa Maharival, part 3, siman 61.

[ה] <b>[לבוש] בלא ריבית וכו'.</b> משמע דבריבית הוי כהלואה כעין פרקמטיא ואסור לפרוע בין מכותי כשיש לו משכון בין מישראל כשיש לו ריוח דרך היתר וליתא דמקור דין זה הוא מהרא"ש ושם קאי על הלוואה שיש בו ריבית, ואפילו להלות ברבית דגרע מפרעון מותר אלא דמצד חומרא אמרינן בסוף סימן זה דיוציא הריבית משבוע וכו' היינו כהלואה אבל בפרעון נראה לי דמותר לגמרי וכן משמע מרבינו ירוחם דף ל"ו וכן משמע קצת בשולחן ערוך:
**** ENGLISH ****
[5] <b>[Levush] Without interest, etc.</b> This implies that with interest it is like a loan that is a form of merchandise, and it is forbidden to collect repayment both from a gentile when he has a mashkon and from a Jew when he has profit by a permitted path. But this is not so; for the source of this law is the Rosh, and there it refers to a loan that has interest; and even lending on interest, which is worse than collecting repayment, is permitted, only that out of stringency we say at the end of this siman that he should spend the interest from that week, etc., meaning like a loan; but regarding repayment it appears to me that it is entirely permitted. And so it is implied from Rabbeinu Yeruham, folio 36; and so it is somewhat implied in the Shulchan Aruch.
**** END BLOCK ****

[ו] <b>[לבוש] ואפילו בחוב וכו'.</b> מסברא דנפשיה כתב כן אבל ריא"ז בשלטי גיבורים פרק קמא דמועד קטן דדוקא בערב פסח הוי דבר אבד מיהו דוקא בהלואה כעין פרקמטיא אבל בהלואה דמעות קיימא לן אף במשכון מותר והלבוש שכתב ואין צריך לומר שמותר היינו אף למאן דאוסר בהלואה:
**** ENGLISH ****
[6] <b>[Levush] And even for a debt, etc.</b> He wrote this from his own reasoning; but Riaz in Shiltei Giborim, chapter 1 of Moed Katan, [holds] that specifically on erev Pesach it is a davar ha'aved. However, this is specifically for a loan that is a form of merchandise; but for a money loan, we hold that even with a mashkon it is permitted. And what the Levush wrote, "and it goes without saying that it is permitted," means even according to the view that forbids regarding a loan.

**** ENGLISH ****
[13] <b>Market day, etc.</b> And in Responsa Maharshal, siman 79, he permitted even in a shop that is closed, and when the buyer comes to purchase he opens it somewhat; and from Responsa Maharival it is implied that one should not permit this except for clothing and vessels in se'if 10. However, se'if 10 deals with opening somewhat immediately, even before the buyer comes. However, Maharshal also concluded: and all this is in order to spend for the time of the joy of Yom Tov. And they practice leniency in sitting in a shop throughout Chol HaMoed; and it is possible they reckoned it a davar ha'aved, for if they do not sit so much time in the shop, business dealing from Jews will diminish. And in any case, certainly one should not learn melacha from this, for it is not comparable to merchandise dealing, which has many sides of leniency in the poskim.
