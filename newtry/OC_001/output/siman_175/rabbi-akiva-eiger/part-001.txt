**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham</small> “For he has already recited HaTov VeHaMeitiv in Bircas HaMazon.” This implies that otherwise it would have been appropriate to recite both Borei Peri HaGafen and HaTov VeHaMeitiv over the cup of Bircas HaMazon. This is unlike what I wrote in Responsa, siman 69, where I stated a general rule that whenever Borei Peri HaGafen must be recited, such as where he changed his mind or changed location, HaTov VeHaMeitiv is not recited. See there.
