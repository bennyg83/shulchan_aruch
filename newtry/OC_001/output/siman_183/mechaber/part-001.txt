One who blesses, how he should conduct himself with the cup of Birkat Hamazon. Containing 12 paragraphs. 1. The cup of blessing for the Birkat HaMazon requires washing on the inside and rinsing on the outside. And if it was clean without residue on the cups, the rinsing and washing are not necessary.

4. One receives the cup of blessing with two hands, and, when one begins blessing, one should lift it up with one's right hand without assisting it with one's left hand. And one should elevate it a fistbreadth from the ground if one was sitting on the ground. And if one was lounging at a table, one elevates it above the table a fistbreadth. And one sets one's eyes upon it, so as not to lose mindfulness about it. {Rama: Rema: And this [practice to not use one's left hand in elevating the cup of blessing] is specifically so that the left hand should not touch the cup; but, if one places the left hand beneath the right hand to help it, that is permissible. 9. One must sit at the time of blessing. Whether he was walking about his house when he ate, or standing, or reclining — when he reaches the point of blessing he must sit, so that he can concentrate better, and also so that he will not be reclining, which is a manner of haughtiness.
