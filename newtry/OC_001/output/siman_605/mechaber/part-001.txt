**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The custom of kapparos on Erev Yom Kippur. It contains one se'if.</b><br>That which they are accustomed to do — kapparah on Erev Yom Kippur, to slaughter a rooster for every male and to say verses over it — one should prevent the custom. {Rama: But some of the Geonim wrote [in favor of] this custom, and so wrote many of the later authorities, and so is the custom in all these lands, and one should not deviate, for it is an ancient custom [minhag vasikin]. And the custom is to take a male rooster for a male, and for a female one takes a hen (Beis Yosef in the name of Tashbetz); and for a pregnant woman one takes two chickens — perhaps she will bear a male. And one chooses white chickens, in the manner of the verse, "If your sins be like scarlet, they shall become white as snow." And the custom is to give the kapparos to the poor, or to redeem them for money that one gives to the poor (Maharil). And there are places where the custom is to go to the graves and to increase in charity, and it is all a fine custom. And one should juxtapose the slaughtering of the kapparah immediately after he has circled it over him and rested his hands upon it — resembling the [Temple] offering. And they throw its innards onto the rooftops, or in the courtyard, in a place from which the birds can take them (Tur).}
