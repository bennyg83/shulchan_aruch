<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ה" data-order="5"></i>שלשה שאכלו כאחד וא' נזהר בפת עכו"ם וא' אינו נזהר או אחד מהן כהן ואוכל חלות אע"פ שאותו שנזהר <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="1"></i>אינו יכול לאכול עם אותו שאינו נזהר ולא ישראל עם הכהן כיון שאותו שאינו נזהר יכול לאכול עם הנזהר וכהן עם הישראל <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Sha'arei Teshuvah" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ז"></i>מצטרפין <i data-commentator="Ateret Zekenim" data-label="♦" data-order="3"></i>אבל אם היו כהנים וזר אוכלים כאחד והכהנים אוכלים חלה ונזהרים מפת של עכו"ם <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="2"></i>והזר אוכל פת של עכו"ם <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ח"></i>אינם מצטרפין <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ו" data-order="6"></i>וה"ה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ט"></i>לשלשה שמודרים <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="2"></i>זה מזה שאינם מצטרפין לזימון: <small>הגה ודוקא כשכל אחד אוכל מככרו אבל <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="3"></i>אם אוכלים מככר בעל הבית מצטרפין דהא אוכלים מככר אחד (א"ז):</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
Three ate together: one of them is careful not to eat gentile made bread and the other is not careful [ie he eats gentile made bread], or one of them is a Kohane and he eats challah. Even though the one who is careful [not to eat gentile made bread] can not eat [gentile made bread] with the one who is not careful, and [even though] a Yisrael cannot [eat challah] with the Kohane, since the one who is not careful [ie he eats gentile made bread] can eat [Jewish made bread] with the one who is careful, and [since] a Kohane is permitted to eat bread with a Yisrael, [when these eat together] they combine [to form a zimun]. But when there are Kohanim and a Non-Kohen eating together and the Kohanim are eating Challah and they are careful not to eat the gentile made bread, while the Yisrael [ie Non-Kohen] is eating gentile made bread, the Kohanim and Non-Kohen may not say zimun together. The same law applies when three take vows [not to benefit] from each other, i.e. they may not say zimun together.
**** END BLOCK ****
