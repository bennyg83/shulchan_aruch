**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Prevent fulfillment, etc.</b> This implies that on the rest of the days of the year, if one knows one blessing he should say it, and they do not prevent one another; Magen Avraham. This requires analysis, for in siman 110 they instituted Havineinu and the short prayer, and no posek failed to mention this. In Rashi and Ran at the end of Rosh Hashanah, the blessings of fast days do not prevent fulfillment.
