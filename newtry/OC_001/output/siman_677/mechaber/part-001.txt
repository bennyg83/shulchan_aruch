**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The law of a lodger on Chanukah, containing four sections.</b><br>A lodger for whom they do not kindle in his home must give a perutah to the homeowner in order to participate with him in the oil of the Chanukah lamp. If he has an entrance open for himself, he must kindle at his entrance, even though that house is designated only for sleeping and he eats at the homeowner's table. The same applies to a son who eats at his father's. Gloss: Some say that in this time, when we kindle truly indoors, he should kindle in the place where he eats, and so is the practice. Responsa Rashba, siman 542.

**** ENGLISH ****
Some say that although they kindle for him inside his home, if he is in a place where there are no Jews, he kindles with the blessings. Gloss: For he is obligated to see the lamps. Mordechai. So is the practice. Even if he is among Jews and sees the lamps, if he wants to be stringent upon himself and kindle on his own, he kindles and blesses over them, and so is the practice.
