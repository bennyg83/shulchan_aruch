(א) את יום טוב וכו' - ומנהגנו לומר את יום חג המצות הזה זמן חירותינו מקרא קודש וכו' ואין אומרים באהבה מקרא קודש שהרי כבר אמרו ותתן לנו ה' אלהינו באהבה מועדים לשמחה אכן כשחל בשבת יש אומרים שאומרים שני פעמים באהבה [אחרונים]:
**** ENGLISH ****
(1) This Yom Tov etc.: And our custom is to say "this day of the Festival of Matzos, the time of our freedom, a holy convocation etc.," and we do not say "be'ahavah, a holy convocation," for they already said "and You gave us, Hashem our God, with love festivals for joy"; however, when it falls on Shabbos, some say they say "be'ahavah" twice [later authorities].

(ב) מקדש ישראל והזמנים - ואם אמר מקדש ישראל לבד לא יצא וכן בשבת אם לא אמר מקדש השבת ואמר מקדש ישראל ג"כ לא יצא אף שהזכיר באמצע התפלה של שבת כיון שלא חתם בשל שבת וכל זה בין בקידושא בין בצלותא:
**** ENGLISH ****
(2) Who sanctifies Yisrael and the seasons: And if he said only "Who sanctifies Yisrael," he has not fulfilled; and likewise on Shabbos, if he did not say "Who sanctifies the Shabbos" and said "Who sanctifies Yisrael," he likewise has not fulfilled, even though he mentioned [the formula] of Shabbos in the middle of the prayer, since he did not conclude with [the formula] of Shabbos; and all this applies both in Kiddush and in prayer.

(ה) אחרי שהוא יודע - ר"ל בעת שאמר ברוך אתה ה' היתה כהוגן ע"ד לסיים בשל יום טוב אלא שאח"כ נכשל בלשונו ומשמע מלשון זה דאם טעה בעת שאמר ברוך אתה ה' שהיה סבור שהוא שבת אפילו אם חזר בתוך כ"ד לאחר שסיים מקדש השבת וסיים כדין לא יצא אבל רוב הפוסקים ס"ל אע"פ שלא ידע שהוא יו"ט יצא כיון שנזכר בתוך כדי דבור וחתם כדין כן פסקו הרבה אחרונים ועיין בביאור הגר"א שגם הוא מכריע כן להלכה:
**** ENGLISH ****
(5) After he already knows: That is to say, at the time he said "Blessed are You, Hashem," it was proper, intending to finish with [the formula] of Yom Tov, except that afterward he stumbled in his speech; and it is implied from this language that if he erred at the time he said "Blessed are You, Hashem," thinking it was Shabbos, even if he retracted within toch kedei dibbur after finishing "Who sanctifies the Shabbos" and finished according to law, he has not fulfilled. But most poskim hold that even though he did not know it was Yom Tov, he has fulfilled, since he remembered within toch kedei dibbur and concluded according to law; so ruled many later authorities; and see in Beur HaGra that he too decides thus for the law.

(יא) ואמר יעלה ויבוא - דהרי מזכיר ענינו של יום ביעלה ויבוא ומיירי שחתם כל הברכה כדין דאם לא חתם אפילו אמר גם אתה בחרתנו לא יצא ואם נזכר אחר שהתחיל רצה חוזר לאתה בחרתנו ואם עקר רגליו חוזר לראש התפלה ועיין לעיל סימן קכ"ב ס"ה ואם לא אמר אתה בחרתנו וגם לא יעלה ויבוא רק והשיאנו אע"פ שאמר מועדי קדשיך אם לא הזכיר חג פלוני בפרט לא יצא [דה"ח אבל בפמ"ג מסתפק בזה]:

(11) And he said Ya'aleh Veyavo: For behold he mentions the matter of the day in Ya'aleh Veyavo; and it deals with a case where he concluded the entire blessing according to law, for if he did not conclude, even if he also said Ateh Bechartanu he has not fulfilled. And if he remembers after he began Retzeh, he returns to Ateh Bechartanu; and if he uprooted his feet, he returns to the beginning of the prayer; and see above, siman 122, se'if 5. And if he said neither Ateh Bechartanu nor Ya'aleh Veyavo, only Vehasienu, even though he said "the festivals of Your holiness," if he did not mention such-and-such festival in particular, he has not fulfilled [Derech HaChaim; but in Peri Megadim he is in doubt about this].

(13) And even if it was Shabbos: That is to say, since he did not say Ateh Bechartanu and "and You gave us," automatically he also did not mention [the formula] of Shabbos; nevertheless, since he went back and mentioned it in Ya'aleh Veyavo when he said "on this Shabbos day and on this day of such-and-such festival," he has fulfilled, for at any rate he mentioned [the formula] of Shabbos within the blessing. And it is implied from this language that if also in Ya'aleh Veyavo he did not mention [the formula] of Shabbos, he has not fulfilled. And one should study this, for behold he mentions Shabbos in Vehasienu when he says "and bequeath to us etc. Shabbos and the festivals of Your holiness"; and perhaps because he mentions it only adjacent to the conclusion alone, and we require that he also open with [the formula] of Shabbos, as is stated in the Gemara; and this requires further study.

**** ENGLISH ****
(15) He need not return: For there is a dispute of the authorities whether he must mention it, and therefore after the fact one need not return; and even without this, behold he already mentioned once in Ateh Bechartanu, and it is not indispensable after the fact according to everyone. And know that many later authorities agreed that even ideally one should not mention [the formula] of Shabbos in Ya'aleh Veyavo, and such is the custom; and see in the Levush who wrote a proper reason for this: that the essence of Ya'aleh Veyavo was founded on remembrance and visitation, and remembrance applies only on Yom Tov and on Rosh Chodesh, as it is written "and on your day of rejoicing and on your festivals and on your new moons etc. and you shall be remembered," and Shabbos is not mentioned there at all.
