<b>אחרי שהוא יודע שהוא י"ט</b>. רק שטעה בדבורו אבל אם טעה וסבר שהוא שבת ונזכר תוך כ"ד לא יצא וכמ"ש סי' ר"ט אבל רוב הפוסקי' ס"ל אף על פי שלא ידע שהוא י"ט יצא כיון  שנז' וחת' כדין עמש"ש ומ"מ משמע כאן דאם לא נזכ' וחת' בשל שבת לא יצא והיינו דוקא כשלא הזכיר של י"ט באמצע אבל הזכי' של י"ט באמצע יצא דהא כבר פסק בסי' רס"ח ס"ד אפי' התפלל תפלת חול יצא אם הזכיר של שבת באמצע וכ"ש כאן דחתם מקדש השבת דהא י"ט נמי מקרי שבת וכ"מ ס"ג אם אמר יעל' ויבוא יצא משמע אפי' לא חתם בשל י"ט יצא וא"כ אם חתם מקדש ישראל והזמנים אפי' לא הזכיר של יום טוב באמצע יצא וצ"ע דהא משנה שלימה שנינו מקום שאמרו לחתום אינו רשאי שלא לחתום, ותניא כללו של דבר הכל הולך אחר חיתום ברכות וע"ק כיון שקבעו חכמים לשבת ברכ' בפ"ע היאך יוצא אם התפלל של חול והזכיר של שבת ולא חתם בשל שבת וצ"ל כיון דבדין הוא לצלויי י"ח בשב' אלא מפני כבוד שבת לא אטרחוהו רבנן לכן אם התפלל של חול והזכיר של שבת יצא ומשמע בפסחים ד' קי"ז דאם חתם בי"ט או בשבת מקדש ישראל יצא ודוקא בתפלה אבל בקידוש לא יצא:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>After he already knows that it is Yom Tov.</b> Only that he erred in his speech; but if he erred and thought it was Shabbos, and remembered within toch kedei dibbur, he has not fulfilled his obligation, as is written in siman 209. But most poskim hold that even though he did not know it was Yom Tov, he has fulfilled, since he remembered and concluded according to law; see there. Nevertheless it is implied here that if he did not remember and concluded with the [formula] of Shabbos, he has not fulfilled; and that is specifically when he did not mention [the formula] of Yom Tov in the middle, but if he mentioned [the formula] of Yom Tov in the middle, he has fulfilled. For he already ruled in siman 268, se'if 4, that even if he prayed a weekday Amidah, he has fulfilled if he mentioned [the formula] of Shabbos in the middle; and all the more so here, where he concluded "Who sanctifies the Shabbos," for Yom Tov too is called Shabbos. And so it is somewhat implied in se'if 3: if he said Ya'aleh Veyavo, he has fulfilled, meaning even if he did not conclude with [the formula] of Yom Tov he has fulfilled; and if so, if he concluded "Who sanctifies Yisrael and the seasons," even if he did not mention [the formula] of Yom Tov in the middle, he has fulfilled. And this requires further study, for we have an explicit Mishnah: where they said to conclude, one is not permitted not to conclude; and a beraisa teaches: the general rule of the matter is that everything follows the conclusion of the blessings. And it is still difficult: since the Sages fixed for Shabbos a blessing of its own, how does he fulfill if he prayed weekday [Amidah] and mentioned [the formula] of Shabbos and did not conclude with [the formula] of Shabbos? And one must say: since by strict law one should pray eighteen [blessings] on Shabbos, only that out of honor of Shabbos the Sages did not impose the burden on him, therefore if he prayed weekday [Amidah] and mentioned [the formula] of Shabbos, he has fulfilled. And it is implied in Pesachim folio 117 that if on Yom Tov or on Shabbos he concluded "Who sanctifies Yisrael," he has fulfilled; and that is specifically in prayer, but in Kiddush he has not fulfilled.

<b>אין ש"צ מקדש</b>. שאין עני בישראל שאין לו יין מיהו נ"ל דאע"פ שאין יין בעיר אלא בב"ה לא יקדש בב"ה דהא בכל השנ' נמי רבים אומרים שלא לקדש בב"ה כמ"ש סי' רס"ט אלא שנוהגים מנהג ראשונים ובפסח מעולם לא היה מנהג לקדש בב"ה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The shatz does not recite Kiddush.</b> For there is no poor person in Yisrael who has no wine. However, it appears to me that even if there is no wine in the city except in the synagogue, one should not recite Kiddush in the synagogue; for throughout the year too many say not to recite Kiddush in the synagogue, as is written in siman 269, except that they follow the custom of the early ones, and on Pesach there was never a custom to recite Kiddush in the synagogue.
