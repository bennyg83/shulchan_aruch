**** HEBREW ****
דלפי זה בחתם ביו"ט בשל שבת אפי' הזכיר של יום טוב באמצע לא מהני דלא דמי להך דסי' רס"ח וכך הם דברי המג"א בסי' רס"ח ס"ק ל' דגרע טפי כשחתם בשל יום א' והיינו כמסקנתו כאן:
**** ENGLISH ****
According to this, if on Yom Tov he concluded with the formula of Shabbos, even if he mentioned Yom Tov in the middle, it does not help, for it is not comparable to that of siman 268; and so are the words of the Magen Avraham in siman 268, s.k. 30, that it is worse when he concluded with the formula of another day, and that is like his conclusion here.
