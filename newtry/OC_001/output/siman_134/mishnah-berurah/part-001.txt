(5) Quietly. Like the Amidah, which is recited quietly. The reason for the first opinion is to arouse concentration. Each locality should follow its established practice in this matter. Shaarei Teshuvah writes that nowadays most congregations do not wait for the chazzan, and each person performs nefilas apayim when he finishes. See there.

(ח) מראה וכו' - המחבר כתב דין זה קודם הלכות קה"ת שכן מנהג הספרדים להגביה קודם הקריאה ועיין לקמיה בהג"ה שמנהגנו לעשות ההגבה אחר הקריאה. המגביה הס"ת מעל התיבה להראות לעם גוללה עד ג' דפין ומגביה ואפשר דדוקא נקט ג' [מ"א] ונ"ל דתלוי לפי כח המגביה שיהיה ביכלתו להגביה כשהיא נגללת הרבה:
**** ENGLISH ****
(8) He shows, etc. The Mechaber placed this law before the laws of Torah reading because the Sephardic custom is to lift the Sefer Torah before the reading. See the gloss below, which states that our custom is to perform hagbah after the reading. The one who lifts the Sefer Torah from the bimah to show it to the people should open it to three columns and then lift it. It is possible that three is meant specifically. Magen Avraham. It appears to me that this depends on the strength of the person lifting it, namely how widely he is capable of lifting it while it is open.

The later authorities write that when the Sefer Torah is shown to the people while the person stands on the east side of the bimah, he should turn from east toward south, as written in siman 128 regarding the kohanim; see there. Before taking out the Sefer Torah, Kaddish is recited through “Le’eila.” On a day that has Mussaf, however, Tiskabel is recited before taking out the Sefer Torah.
