**** ENGLISH ****
The law of rising in the morning — it contains nine clauses.<br>One should rise like a lion in the morning to serve his Creator. He should awaken at dawn. Rema: In any case one should not delay until after the time of prayer when the congregation prays (Tur). Rema: “I have set the Lord before me always” (Psalms 16:8) is a great principle in the Torah and in the traits of the righteous who walk before God; for a person’s sitting, movements, and affairs when he is alone in his house are not like his sitting, movements, and affairs when he is before a great king; nor are his speech and opening his mouth at will when among the members of his household and relatives like his speech in the king’s presence — how much more so when a person takes to heart that the great King, the Holy One blessed be He, whose glory fills the earth, stands over him and sees his deeds, as it is said, “Can a man hide himself in secret places that I shall not see him?” (Jeremiah 23:24) — immediately awe and submission reach him in fear of Heaven and shame before Him constantly (Guide for the Perplexed III ch. 52). One should not be ashamed before people who mock him for divine service; also in modest private conduct, and when lying on his bed he should know before whom he lies; and as soon as he awakens from sleep he should rise promptly to serve his Creator blessed and exalted (Tur).
