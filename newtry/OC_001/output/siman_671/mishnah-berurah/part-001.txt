(44) And they light and bless - Although in the synagogue it is only a custom, nevertheless they bless over it, just as they bless over Hallel of Rosh Chodesh, which is only a custom. If a mourner leads prayers on the evening of Chanukkah, another person should light the lamps because Shehecheyanu is recited then, and it is recognizable as an expression of joy for the congregation, so it is not fitting that the mourner light them. On Chol HaMoed, which is like Yom Tov, I have sometimes prevented the mourner from leading Minchah and Maariv, as Peri Megadim writes.
