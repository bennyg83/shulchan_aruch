<b>א) [סעיף א'] תשעה באב אסור ברחיצה וכו'  </b>וכל מה שאסרו חכמים הכל אסור עד הלילה כמו אכילה ושתיה אלא דברים שאינם אלא מנהגא שלא מוזכר בתלמוד כמו ישיבה על הקרקע בזה יש נוהגין להקל אחר חצות. של"ה דף קצ"ט ע"ב. וכ"כ הב"ח רסי' תקנ"ט. ור"ח כתב יהא בנידוי מי שעושה א' מכל אלו כל היום. והב"ד התניא ושה"ל. מ"א סק"א. א"ר או' ב'. אך אותם ההולכים בין העכו"ם ועוסקים במשא ומתן אחר חצות אותם מותרים לנעול ומהם למדו כולם להקל ויש למחות בידם. מ"א שם. א"ר שם. מ"ב או' א' ועיין לקמן או ה':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(1) [Seif 1] Tisha B'Av is forbidden in washing, etc.</b> Everything that the Sages prohibited is forbidden until nightfall, like eating and drinking. Only things that are merely custom and not mentioned in the Talmud, such as sitting on the ground, in this some have the practice to be lenient after midday. Shelah p. 199b. So too wrote the Bach at the beginning of siman 559. R' Chananel wrote that anyone who does one of all these the entire day should be placed in niddui [excommunication]. The Tanya and the Shelah cited his words. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Eliyah Rabbah os 2. But those who walk among non-Jews and engage in commerce after midday, they are permitted to wear shoes, and from them everyone has learned to be lenient, and one should protest against them. Magen Avraham ibid. Eliyah Rabbah ibid. Mishna Berurah os 1. See below os 5.

<b>(3) Regarding</b> the matter of smoking tobacco on Tisha B'Av: the Knesset HaGedolah wrote in siman 551 in the Hagahos Beis Yosef os 21 that a case came before him of someone who smoked on Tisha B'Av and he excommunicated him. Likewise in siman 567 in the Hagahos Beis Yosef os 3 he discussed this at length and wrote that on Tisha B'Av it is forbidden according to all opinions, see there. So too wrote in the responsa Darkei No'am siman 9, and Beis David siman 291. The Zera Avraham siman 73 [wrote] to forbid on Tisha B'Av, see there. So too wrote Chayei Adam rule 135 os 27. The Birke Yosef also wrote in siman 567 that in our times the practice has spread to permit (i.e., on other fasts), except on Tisha B'Av where some are strict because of the mourning, see there. However, the Sha'arei Teshuvah siman 559 os 4 wrote that one may be lenient after midday and in private inside his home, see there. So too he wrote above in siman 511 os 35 that some are lenient after midday, see there. However, the Machberes above in siman 210 os 13 wrote that a great authority dreamed with a distinguished Rav and adjured him to tell him what they say in Heaven about smoking tobacco. He answered that one who smokes on Yom Tov is excommunicated, one who smokes on Tisha B'Av is cursed, and similarly one who smokes under the wedding canopy, see there. So too he wrote in his work Mo'ed L'Chol Chai os 212, see there, and I cited his words above in siman 210 os 35, see there. The Ruach Chayyim os 5 also wrote that it should not be permitted except after the Maariv prayer, i.e., after twelve hours, see there. So too he wrote in his work Milei D'Chasidusa siman 10 os 80, see there. See further in Sha'arei Shamayim by Rav Moed Katan p. 71b, who wrote: "Smoking tobacco, the Knesset HaGedolah forbade it and excommunicated over it, and the law is with him, for it is no less than pleasure, strolling, and diverting one's mind from mourning. So too my master, my father, my teacher, of blessed memory, was accustomed to protest and punish one who transgressed deliberately. Nevertheless, for a certain need, when one does not intend for pleasure but only to aid in bodily functions, as is known from its nature and beneficial effect in this regard, in such a case it is obvious to me that it is permitted. However, [it should be done] where no one can see, so that they do not learn to be lenient, and especially at night which is somewhat more lenient in any case regarding melacha, in my opinion. On this I relied and I myself did so once at night when I needed it, etc. Therefore during the day I am afraid to act leniently even for the aforementioned need, although there are grounds to be lenient for others in such a case, and it should not be more stringent than anointing and washing which are permitted when one does not intend to derive pleasure from them." It seems that if one needs it during the day for his bodily functions as mentioned, one may be lenient to smoke it specifically in the privy, in private, out of sight of others. See further below siman 567 os 18.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(5) Ibid.</b> And wearing shoes. It is forbidden to wear shoes the entire day. Magen Avraham s.k. 1 in the name of the Geonim. The Shelah wrote that some bring their shoes in their hands and when they pray Maariv they put them on. The Eliyah Rabbah os 2 cited his words and wrote that it is possible they prayed Maariv close to nightfall. Furthermore, he wrote above in the name of the Geonim that anyone who does one of all these the entire day on Tisha B'Av should be excommunicated, see there. This implies that his view is that even if they prayed Maariv, it is forbidden by strict law. However, see the Machberes os 2 where he discussed at length and questioned the words of the Eliyah Rabbah and wrote that it is merely an enactment, and if their custom all year round was to pray Maariv while it is still day, it is perhaps permitted. However, for practical purposes he wrote that one should wait until nightfall and it should not be permitted beforehand even if they prayed Maariv, as the later authorities wrote, see there.

<b>ח) שם.  </b>ותינוקות של בית רבן בטילים בו. אפי' בדברים הרעים שבירמיה וקינות ואיוב ודוקא בתינוקות שאינם מבינים מה שמוציאים בפיהם אבל התינוק שהגיע לידי מדה זו שמבין מה שהוא אומר מותר ללמוד עמו בדברים הרעים שבירמיה. ב"ח. שכנה"ג בהגה"ט או' א' עט"ז שו"ג או' א' והט"ז סק"א כתב דאיוב ושאר דברים המותרים לגדול מותר ללמוד גם עם התינוקות ולא אסרו אלא הלימוד שהקטנים לומדים בסדר שלהם דהיינו חומש וגמרות יעו"ש. וכ"ה דעת הדג"מ. אמנם המ"א סק"ב כתב דאסור ללמוד כלל עם אחרים רק מותר ללמוד לו החרבן דאינו אלא ספור דברים ומשבר הלב של תינוק יעו"ש. וכ"נ דעת הנה"ש או' א' והטעם כתב היא"פ דאסור ללמוד כלל עם אחרים. לפי שהגדול יש לו שמחה בזה שלומד עם אחרים וכ"כ המחה"ש. נמצא דג' מחלוקות בדבר דלדעת הב"ח דוקא תינוק המבין מותר ללמוד עמו בדברים הרעים ולדעת הט"ז גם באינו מבין בדברים הרעים שרי ולדעת המ"א בין מבין בין שאינו מבין ובדברים הרעים אסור ורק ללמד לו החרבן שרי ומשמע דלכ"ע אם התינוק לומד לעצמו ובדברים הרעים בין שמבין בין שאינו מבין מותר ולכן יש להניח לתינוקות ללמוד לעצמם בדברים הרעים כדי שלא ילכו לשוטט ברחובות ולהתעסק בדברים בטלים:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(8) Ibid.</b> And schoolchildren are idle [from Torah study] on it. Even regarding the sorrowful passages in Yirmiyahu, Kinos, and Iyov, this applies specifically to children who do not understand what they are saying, but a child who has reached the level of understanding what he says is permitted to study the sorrowful passages in Yirmiyahu. Bach. Knesset HaGedolah in the glosses os 1, Ateres Zahav, Shulchan Gavoah os 1. The Taz s.k. 1 wrote that Iyov and other subjects that are permitted for adults are also permitted to study with children, and they only forbade the studies that children learn in their regular curriculum, namely Chumash and Gemaras, see there. This is also the view of the Dagul Merevavah. However, the Magen Avraham s.k. 2 wrote that it is forbidden to study anything at all with others; one may only teach them about the destruction, which is merely a narrative account and breaks the child's heart, see there. This appears to be the view of the Nishmas HaShulchan os 1, and the reason as the Ya'aros P'ri wrote is that it is forbidden to study anything at all with others, because the adult derives joy from studying with others; so too wrote the Machatzis HaShekel. Thus there are three opinions in this matter: according to the Bach, only with a child who understands is it permitted to study the sorrowful passages; according to the Taz, even with one who does not understand, the sorrowful passages are permitted; and according to the Magen Avraham, whether the child understands or not, even the sorrowful passages are forbidden, and it is only permitted to teach him about the destruction. It seems that according to all opinions, if the child studies by himself the sorrowful passages, whether he understands or not, it is permitted. Therefore, one should allow children to study the sorrowful passages on their own so that they do not go roaming the streets and occupy themselves with idle matters.

<b>יב) שם.  </b>ואם יש ביניהם פסוקי דנחמה צריך לדלגם. מכאן מוכח דנחמות הכתובים בסוף כל קינה צריך לדלגם. וכ"כ הפר"ח בליקוטים סי' תקנ"ט סעי' ה' שהאומרים נחמה בסוף כל קינה הוא שיבוש דא"כ למה כתב כאן דאם יש פסוקי דנחמה צריך לדלגם יעו"ש. וכ"כ המחב"ר בסי' זה או' ג' כס"א או' א' מאמ"ר סי' תקנ"ט או' ד'. מיהו עיין במל"ח סי' יו"ד או' ס"ז דלחלק יצא בין פסוקי דנחמה לאמירת הנחמות שבסוף הקינות וע"כ כתב דאין לזוז ממנהגם כי בלילה אין אומרים סיום הקינות וביום אומרים סיום כל הקינות שיש בהם נחמות יעו"ש, אמנם אנן בדידן ק"ל כדעת הרוב וגם דשב וא"ת עדיף אכן אם יש נוהגין כן אין למחות בידם:

<b>(12) Ibid.</b> And if there are verses of consolation among them, one must skip them. From here it is proven that the consolations written at the end of each kinah must be skipped.
