<small>שם</small> וכ"כ בספר מ"ב וכ' דבד' וה' מותר כמ"ש סימן רמ"ז ורנ"ב כצ"ל ותימא הוא הא כיון דיום השוק בשבת ה"ל כאומר לו שיעשה בשבת וזה אסור בכל השבוע וכן הקשה הא"ר וכן עיקר שאסור וכן מוכח במג"א סימן רצ"ב סק"ח:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Ibid.</small> The same is written in Sefer Masas Binyamin, which states that on Wednesday and Thursday it is permitted, as written in simanim 247 and 252. This is the correct reading. This is puzzling, for since market day is on Shabbos, it is as though he told him to do it on Shabbos, which is forbidden throughout the entire week. Eliyah Rabbah raises the same difficulty. The principal ruling is that it is forbidden, as is also evident from Magen Avraham, siman 292, seif katan 8.
