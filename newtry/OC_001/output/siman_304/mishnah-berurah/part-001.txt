(א) A person etc. — to ease reader knowing reasons of laws in this siman I precede small introduction per Gemara and poskim. Two verses: one "that your slave and maidservant rest like you" — slave circumcised immersed for slavery accepted all slave mitzvot i.e. all woman obligated — Shabbat included — forbidden melacha Shabbat; verse teaches slave's rest also on master commanded prevent melacha even for slave's need; second "son of your maidservant and ger refresh" — Sages: gentile slave body-acquired to Israel not circumcised only seven Noahide mitzvot — master must prevent melacha for him Shabbat; forbidden all Israel command melacha for them Shabbat; for himself permitted — Shabbat not in seven mitzvot — law like ger toshav not warned Shabbat; nevertheless forbidden d'oraisa Israel command melacha for him; slave body-acquired not circumcised not accepted even seven — poskim dispute if commanded on rest — as will explain.
