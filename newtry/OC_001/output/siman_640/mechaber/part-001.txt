He whose candles in the Sukkah were extinguished, on the Sabbath, and has a candle in his house, it is permitted for him to leave the Sukkah to eat where the candle is, and he does not need to go to his friend's Sukkah which has a candle, if there is great effort involved (Terumat HaDeshen, Part I 5, Terumat HaDeshen, Part II 158).
