**** HEBREW ****
<small>סעיף ד' </small>אם יש טורח גדול בדבר. והוי בכלל מצטער. ובלילה הראשון דמצטער חייב באמת צריך לטרוח לילך לסוכת חבירו. ודלא כדמשמע במג"א רסי' תרל"ט:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Se'if 4.</small> <b>If there is great exertion in the matter.</b> This is included in the category of one who is distressed. But on the first night, when one who is distressed is in fact obligated, he must exert himself to go to his friend's sukkah, unlike what is implied by the Magen Avraham at the beginning of siman 639.
