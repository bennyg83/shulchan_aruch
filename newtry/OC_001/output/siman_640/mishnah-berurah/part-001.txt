(25) <b>But not under the sechach.</b> For then the sheet nullifies the sechach and invalidates the sukkah, as above in siman 629, se'if 19, according to the first view, which the Mechaber stated there without qualification. However, according to what we explained there, in a pressing circumstance, when spreading from the side does not help and he would have to eat outside the sukkah, one may rely on the second view, which permits hanging a sheet in order to protect. This should be forbidden only when he distances the sheet four tefachim from the sechach, for then the sheet is not nullified to the sechach. On Shabbos and Yom Tov it is proper to be careful not to distance it by an empty space of three tefachim from the sechach because of the concern of ohel. (26) <b>He is not called distressed.</b> For a person sometimes sleeps bent in this way. The proof is from what we rule, that the measure of a sukkah is seven by seven tefachim, where certainly a person cannot sleep unless he bends his body and limbs. According to this, even if he initially made it in a way fit for sleeping stretched out, and afterward some matter occurred that made it cramped and narrow to lie down, he is still obligated to sleep there. The Nahar Shalom wrote that nevertheless, one who is delicate and knows about himself that for him sleeping bent there is distress is indeed exempt from sleeping there. Even so, he fulfills the obligation of eating, since at the time it was made it was fit for him to eat and sleep there.
