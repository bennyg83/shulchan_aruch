(ה) חרש - היינו המדבר ואינו שומע כלל ואע"ג דבעלמא קי"ל דאם לא השמיע לאזנו יצא כדלעיל בסימן ס"ב וסימן קפ"ה הכא לענין מגילה דהוא משום פרסומי ניסא החמירו בו יותר דהשמיעה לאזנו הוא לעיכובא א"נ דחרש גרע טפי שאינו יכול להשמיע לאזנו ועיין בשע"ת דדוקא אם הוא חרש גמור שאינו שומע כלל לאפוקי אם הוא שומע כשמדברים לו בקול רם. וכ"ז הוא לדעת השו"ע אבל דעת כמה אחרונים דאפילו הוא חרש גמור יצא השומע ממנו ומ"מ לכתחלה כו"ע מודים שלא יעמידוהו להוציא רבים י"ח. ודע דמי שכבדו אזניו או שהוא רחוק מן הבימה ואינו יכול לשמוע היטיב מן הקורא יזהר לקרות לעצמו ממגילה כשרה או עכ"פ יאחוז חומש והתיבות שיחסר לו יאמר תיכף מן החומש:
**** ENGLISH ****
5) <b>A deaf person.</b> This means one who speaks but does not hear at all. Although generally we hold that if he did not make it heard to his own ear he has fulfilled, as above in siman 62 and siman 185, here regarding megillah, which is because of publicizing the miracle, they were more stringent, and hearing it with his own ear is indispensable. Alternatively, a deaf person is worse, because he cannot make it heard to his own ear. See Shaarei Teshuvah, that this is specifically if he is completely deaf and does not hear at all, excluding one who hears when they speak to him in a loud voice. All this is according to the opinion of Shulchan Aruch, but several later authorities hold that even if he is completely deaf, one who hears from him has fulfilled. Nevertheless, initially everyone agrees that they should not appoint him to discharge the public from their obligation. Know that one whose ears are heavy, or who is far from the bimah and cannot hear the reader well, should be careful to read for himself from a valid megillah, or at least to hold a Chumash and immediately say from the Chumash the words that he misses.

6) <b>Or a minor.</b> Although a minor is also obligated at least rabbinically, as above in section 1, nevertheless he cannot discharge an adult, for regarding a minor it is two rabbinic levels, meaning that the reading of the megillah itself is only from the words of the Scribes, and a minor's obligation in all mitzvos is also only rabbinic, whereas an adult has one rabbinic level. Two rabbinic levels do not come and discharge one rabbinic level. Above in siman 675, section 3, the Mechaber brought that there is one who validates a minor who has reached the age of education, that he can discharge others with his lighting, and the same applies regarding reading the megillah. See Ikrei Dinim, who wrote in the name of Sama DeChayei that in pressing circumstances, when there are no men expert in reading the megillah, a minor who has reached the age of education can discharge them.
