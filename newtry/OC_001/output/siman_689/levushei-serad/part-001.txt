<small>מ"א סק"ב</small> משמע בטור אפי' אין וכו'. בטור כ' סתם עבדים ופי' הב"י דהיינו משוחררים ע"ש ופליג עליו המ"א דסתם עבדים משמע אף שאין משוחררים:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham, seif katan 2.</small> "It implies in the Tur, even if they are not, etc." In the Tur he wrote simply "slaves," and Beis Yosef explained that this means freed slaves; see there. Magen Avraham disagrees with him, for the plain meaning of "slaves" implies even those who have not been freed.
