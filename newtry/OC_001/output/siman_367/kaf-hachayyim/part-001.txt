**** HEBREW ****
<b>א) [סעיף א'] אשתו של אדם מערבת וכו'  </b>מפני שהיא כגופו ואפי' אם מיחה בה שלא לערב דחשו חז"ל שמא יתכוין להקניט את הדרים לאסור עליהם ולכן הקילו שאשתו יכולה לערב שלא מדעתו ותו לא חשו שמא יתרצו שניהם להקניט דהוא מילתא דלא שכיחא. לבוש. תו"ש או' א':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1) [Se'if 1] A man's wife may establish an eruv, etc. </b>This is because she is regarded as part of him. She may do so even if he protested and told her not to establish the eruv. Our Sages were concerned that he might intend to spite the residents by prohibiting carrying for them, and they therefore ruled leniently that his wife may establish the eruv without his knowledge. They were no longer concerned that both spouses might agree to spite the residents, since this is uncommon. Levush; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 1.
