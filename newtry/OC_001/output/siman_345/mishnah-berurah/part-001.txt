(1) Four domains, etc. — I precede this with a brief introduction: hotza'ah from reshus to reshus is among the other melachot the Torah forbade on Shabbat, and like all bodies of Torah transmitted to Moses at Sinai; and our Sages brought proof from Scripture "And Moses commanded and they passed a proclamation in the camp: man and woman shall do no more melacha for the sacred contribution" — and the people ceased bringing — we learn bringing is called melacha (Rambam beginning chapter 12); and likewise forbidden to bring from reshut ha-rabbim to reshut ha-yachid; and there is a third domain called karmelit — not reshut ha-rabbim and not reshut ha-yachid without partitions as reshut ha-yachid requires, as explained in seif 14; from the Torah permitted to bring from it to reshut ha-yachid and reshut ha-rabbim, but the Sages forbade lest through this one come to permit from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim, for not all are expert in partition laws; and there is a fourth domain called makom patur as below at the end of the siman — permitted to bring from it l'chatchila to reshut ha-yachid and reshut ha-rabbim, as all explained in siman 346. Rak mel — meaning Carmel written in Torah — pounded by hand, not moist and not completely dry; when pounded by hand it softens completely (Korban HaEdah in the name of Arukh explaining Yerushalmi):
