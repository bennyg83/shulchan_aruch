**** ENGLISH ****
(3) <b>Whether, etc.</b> Meaning, to exclude the view that the reason for invalidating height in a sukkah above twenty is that, because of its height, its shade does not spread over the entire ground beneath it, or over most of it, and the shade is only from the walls, whereas we require the shade of a sukkah. According to this reasoning, if the sukkah was very wide, we should validate it even at a greater height, since when the sukkah is wide, the shade of the walls moves away from its middle and the shade of the s'chach spreads within it. It also excludes the view that the reason for invalidating the height is that the eye does not notice it because of its height, and one does not see that he is sitting under the s'chach, whereas the mitzvah of sukkah is that he see and remember that the Holy One, blessed be He, settled our ancestors in sukkos. According to that reasoning, one could say that if the walls also rose up to the s'chach, since he places his eyes on the walls that are in front of him, his gaze does not stop until he sees all of it, and when he looks at the top of the wall he also sees the s'chach resting on the wall. However, we do not rule like any of these reasonings. Rather, the reason for invalidating above twenty is that we require a sukkah to be a temporary dwelling, and the Sages assessed that up to twenty one need not pay so much attention to strengthening the partitions, and walls of reeds are sufficient; but higher than this, one must make the walls strong, like a house, so that they not fall because of their great height. According to this reason, if the walls reached the s'chach and it was very wide, all the more so it is invalid because it is a permanent dwelling. Nevertheless, below twenty, if he made the walls strong like a house, it is valid, for he could have made it temporary, and the Torah was particular only where he is forced to make it permanent, as in the case of above twenty.

(ל) יש ביניהם ג"ט פסולה - ולא מתכשר משום דופן עקומה [דהיינו כאלו הדופן נעקם למעלה עד המקום שכנגד החקק] שכל שאיננה גבוה י"ט איננה דופן ולא נאמרו בה הלכות דופן:
**** ENGLISH ****
(30) <b>If there are three tefachim between them, it is invalid.</b> It is not validated through a bent wall; that is, by treating the wall as though it bends above to the point opposite the hollow; because anything less than ten tefachim high is not a wall, and the laws of walls do not apply to it.
