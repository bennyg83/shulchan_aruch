**** ENGLISH ****
Some say one should not make a linen <i>tallis</i>; even though the halacha is not so, a God-fearing person surpasses all of them and makes a <i>tallis</i> of sheep’s wool, which is obligated in <i>tzitzis</i> from the Torah without doubt. Rema: However if only a linen <i>tallis</i> is possible it is better to make a linen <i>tallis</i> with linen <i>tzitzis</i> than to nullify the mitzvah of <i>tzitzis</i> (Responsa Rosh klal 2).
