**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The time of walking.</b> Meaning, the time it takes to walk four amos. But after he waited this measure, it is permitted to urinate even in the place where he prayed. Nevertheless, as a measure of piety one should not urinate within four amos of prayer. Sefer Chasidim, siman 828, wrote that one should not urinate or spit in a place where he covered the blood of a wild animal or bird with a blessing while the blood remains covered.
