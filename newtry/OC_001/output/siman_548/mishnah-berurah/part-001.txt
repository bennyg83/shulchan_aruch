(ג) ברגל - ומ"מ הנר שרגילים להדליק כל שבעה ידליק תיכף ואין זה תלוי במה שהרגל מבטל האבילות או שאינו חל עד אחר הרגל רק שלא ידליק במקום שאוכלים שם וכ"ש בהחדר שמת שם שמא מתוך כך יבוא להספידו אלא ידליק במקום שאין אוכלין שם. אכן כיון שאין נהנה לאור זה כלל לא ידליק בעצמו ביו"ט אלא ע"י עכו"מ [פמ"ג בסימן קל"ב והגרע"א בסימן תקי"ד]:
**** ENGLISH ****
(3) On the festival: nevertheless, the lamp that is customarily lit throughout the seven days should be lit immediately. This does not depend on whether the festival cancels the mourning or on whether it does not take effect until after the festival. However, he should not light it in the place where people eat, and certainly not in the room where the person died, lest this lead him to eulogize. Rather, he should light it in a place where people do not eat. Since he derives no benefit from this light at all, he should not light it himself on Yom Tov, but through a non-Jew, as stated by the Peri Megadim in siman 132 and Rabbi Akiva Eiger in siman 514.

(24) His house: regarding the partner of a mourner whose relative died on Yom Tov, the Gaon Rabbi Shimon of Prague ruled to forbid opening his shop, as in other mourning, meaning that he must wait three days after Yom Tov, as the practice is in other mourning regarding a partner, as stated there in Yoreh Deah, seif 21, in the later authorities. So the Baer Heitev wrote in the name of Eliyah Zuta. However, in Chochmas Adam, laws of mourning, klal 169, law 2, he cited the Eliyah Rabbah as lenient, namely that if the death occurred on the first day of Chol HaMoed, the partner may open his shop immediately after Yom Tov. See there in Chochmas Adam, where his view is that even if the death occurred on Erev the last Yom Tov, the partner may open his shop immediately after Yom Tov. See Biur Halacha, where we clarified that he went too far; it is enough if we are lenient like the Eliyah Rabbah.

(כז) וימי הרגל עולים וכו' - ומטעם שלשים אסור ללבוש בתוך הרגל כלים מגוהצים חדשים ולבנים וכן מה שהתירו לפעמים לספר ולכבס בתוך הרגל כגון הבא ממדינת הים וכל השנויים לעיל בסימן תקל"א ס"ב וסימן תקל"ד מחמת איסור שלשים אסור בכולם. וחתן שנשא אשה ערב הרגל ובתוך הרגל מת לו מת אין הרגל עולה לו למנין שלשים שהרי בתוך שבעת ימי המשתה היה מותר בגיהוץ ותספורת ואינו חושש לאבילות כלל וכדאיתא ביו"ד סימן שמ"ב ומה שהיה זהיר ברגל שלא לגהץ ולספר הוא מחמת הרגל לחוד נמצא שלא נהג אבילות ברגל כלל וע"כ צריך לנהוג לאחר שהשלים ימי משתה שלו שבעת ימי אבלות ואח"כ שלשים:
**** ENGLISH ****
(27) And the days of the festival count, etc.: because of the thirty days, it is forbidden during the festival to wear new, white, pressed garments. Likewise, cases where they sometimes permitted hair cutting or laundering during the festival, such as one who came from overseas and the other exceptions above in siman 531, seif 2, and siman 534, are forbidden because of the prohibition of the thirty days. If a groom married a woman on Erev Yom Tov and during the festival a relative died, the festival does not count toward his thirty days, for during the seven days of celebration he was permitted in pressing and hair cutting and was not concerned for mourning at all, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 342. The fact that he refrained during the festival from pressing and cutting hair was only because of the festival, so he did not practice mourning on the festival at all. Therefore, after completing his days of celebration, he must observe seven days of mourning and afterward thirty days.
