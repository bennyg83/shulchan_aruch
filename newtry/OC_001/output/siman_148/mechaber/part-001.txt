**** HEBREW ****
<b>שלא יפשיט ש"צ התיבה. ובו סעיף אחד:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i>אין ש"צ רשאי <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="א"></i>להפשיט התיבה בצבו' כל זמן שהם בב"ה <small> (פי' רש"י שהיו רגילים להביא ס"ת מבית אחר <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ב"></i>שהיא משתמרת בו וכו' עד שטורח צבור הוא <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="1"></i>להתעכב שם ועיין בפנים בא"ח):</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
The Prayer Leader May Not Fold Up [The Covering Of] The Table. Containing 1 Se'if: The prayer leader does not have permission to fold up the [covering of] the table [where the Torah was read] in the presence of the congregation as long as they remain in the synagogue. {Rama: (Rashi explains that they were accustomed to bring the Torah from another house where it was kept safe etc. and it was a burden on the congregation to have to wait there [while the prayer leader folded up the covering]. See further inside in Orach Chaim)}
