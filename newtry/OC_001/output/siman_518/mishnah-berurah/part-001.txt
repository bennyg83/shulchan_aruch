{א} מתוך שהותרה הוצאה וכו' - וכן הדין בהבערה ושחיטה ואפיה ובשול שהם מלאכות השייכות לאוכל נפש אמרינן בהו מתוך וכו'. דאף דכתיב אך אשר יאכל לכל נפש הוא לבדו יעשה לכם קיבלו חז"ל דהכונה הוא אותן מלאכות הרגילות להעשות לצורך אוכל נפש אפילו אם עתה אין כונתו לצורך אכילה התירה התורה ועיין לקמיה במה שסיים הרמ"א לענין הוצאה דאיירינן בה דבעינן שיהא עכ"פ צורך קצת וה"ה בכל הנ"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
{1} Since carrying out was permitted, etc. And the same law applies to kindling, slaughtering, baking, and cooking, which are melachos that pertain to ochel nefesh: we say regarding them "mitoch," etc. For even though it is written, "But that which every soul eats, that alone shall be done for you," the Sages received that the intent is those melachos that are ordinarily done for the need of ochel nefesh: even if now his intent is not for the need of eating, the Torah permitted it. And see below in what the Rama concluded regarding carrying out, with which we are dealing: that we require at least some need; and the same applies to all the above.

**** ENGLISH ****
{6} Lest they be stolen. If he leaves them in their place, even though they are not vessels fit for ochel nefesh; and for this reason it is also permitted to take out the key of the chest in which his money lies, if he is afraid to leave it there in his house, for since his heart is troubled and he suffers over this, taking out the key is considered a need of Yom Tov delight. And know that there are several poskim who disagree about this and hold that carrying out was permitted only for a real need of ochel nefesh or a mitzvah need and other matters belonging to that day, as above, but not for monetary loss; and it is proper to be stringent to conduct oneself like them [and especially where he can hand over the vessels or the key to someone trustworthy in his house, for then it is forbidden according to everyone]. Nevertheless, the machzorim that he brought on Yom Tov to the synagogue and he fears theft: even though he no longer needs them on Yom Tov, it is permitted to bring them to his house according to everyone, for they permitted their end for the sake of their beginning, for otherwise he would not bring them at first to the synagogue; but the machzorim that have long been lying in the synagogue, when he no longer needs them on Yom Tov, it is proper to be stringent not to return them to his house on Yom Tov, even if there is a concern of theft there.

**** ENGLISH ****
{10} And if he placed an eruv, etc. Meaning that he placed eruv chatzeros in addition to what he did as a repair of alleyways in the city; then it is permitted to move and to carry out from courtyard to courtyard even by way of the alleyway. And it implies that if he did not place eruv chatzeros, it is forbidden to carry out something that has no need of the day at all, even from courtyard to courtyard or from house to courtyard. And see above, siman 416 at its end, and below in siman 528, where the Mechaber was silent following the poskim that they did not institute eruv chatzeros at all on Yom Tov, and it is permitted to carry out from courtyard to courtyard or from house to courtyard even something that has no need of the day at all; and the Rama in our matter was silent following those who are stringent about this, and it is astonishing that he did not emend anything there. And Maharshal wrote that the custom is not to make eruv chatzeros on the eve of Yom Tov, as the Mechaber's view, and one should not change the custom. Nevertheless l'chatchila, when he makes eruv chatzeros on Erev Pesach for all the Shabbosos of the year, he should also include Yom Tov and say "and for all Yom Tov," in order to satisfy also the view of those who are stringent. And know that even vessels that pertain to ochel nefesh, such as knives and the like: if he already finished his meal and will no longer need them, it is forbidden to take them with him in a public domain or in a karmelit. And there are those who are lenient regarding knives, lest some fruit present itself to him to cut with it; however, if it is known to him for certain that he will not need a knife on this walk, such as when he is going to synagogue and the like, it is forbidden to take it with him according to everyone.

{מה} ונפחת - אפילו ביו"ט ולא אמרינן דכיון שהיה בין השמשות סתום ולא היה אפשר ליטול מהם מחמת איסור סתירת בנין איתקצאי לכולי יומא דאין זה בכלל מוקצה כיון שלא היו מוקצין מחמת איסור עצמן רק שהיו במקום שאין יכולין לילך ליטלן ולפי טעם זה אפילו היה הבית בריא מקודם שאין רגילות שיפחת מאליו ג"כ מותר ליטול הפירות וכן ה"ה במי שיש לו לפתות טמונים בבור ופתחן העכו"ם ביו"ט או בשבת דאין בהם משום מוקצה אכן אם עשה העכו"ם לצורך ישראל אפילו אם הפירות היו של עכו"ם אסור באכילה ובטלטול עד מוצאי שבת ויו"ט בכדי שיעשה [וצריך ליזהר בזה בימי הפסח כשעכו"ם מביא לפתות לישראל אם יודע שהגוי פתח הבור היום אוסר ליקח ממנו] ועיין בבה"ל דאין להקל בכל זה כ"א בשעת הדחק:
**** ENGLISH ****

<b>(45) And it was breached.</b> This applies even if the breach occurred on Yom Tov. We do not say that since the house was sealed at bein hashmashos and the fruit could not then be taken because dismantling a structure was forbidden, the fruit became set aside for the entire day. This is not included in muktzeh, since the fruit was not set aside because of an intrinsic prohibition, but merely stood in a place from which it could not be retrieved. According to this reason, even if the house had previously been sound and it was not expected to become breached on its own, it is still permitted to take the fruit. The same applies to a person who has turnips buried in a pit and a non-Jew opened the pit on Yom Tov or Shabbos. The turnips are not muktzeh. However, if the non-Jew opened it for the sake of a Jew, even when the fruit belongs to the non-Jew, it is forbidden to eat or move it until after Shabbos or Yom Tov and the passage of b'chedi she'ya'asu. One must be careful about this during Pesach when a non-Jew brings turnips to a Jew. If the Jew knows that the non-Jew opened the pit that day, he may not buy from him. See Biur Halacha, which explains that one should rely on this leniency only in pressing circumstances.

{מו} נוטל ממקום הפחת - אבל לא יפחות מן הבית יתר כדי ליטול יותר:

<b>(46) He may take from the place of the breach.</b> He may not enlarge the breach in the house in order to take more fruit.
