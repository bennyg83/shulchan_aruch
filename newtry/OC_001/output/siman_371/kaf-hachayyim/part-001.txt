<b>א) [סעיף א'] אחד מבני החצר שהניח ביתו וכו'  </b>דדירה בלא בעלים אינה אוסרת. טור. ומותר לטלטל ממנה לחצר שיש בה דיורים אחרים שעירבו ביניהם ואינו כמוציא מרשות לרשות. ר"ז או' א':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1) [Se'if 1] One of the courtyard's residents who left his house, etc. </b>A residence without its occupants does not prohibit carrying. Tur. It is permitted to carry from that house into a courtyard containing other residents who established an eruv among themselves, and this is not considered carrying from one domain to another. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1.

<b>ג) שם. שהניח ביתו והלך וכו'  </b>מבע"י. טור. ונ"ל דנקט כן לאשמעינן דאפי' חזר בשבת אינו אוסר. א"נ נקט מבע"י משום עכו"ם דאם חזר בשבת אוסר כמ"ש סי' שפ"ג אבל באמת אפי' היה כאן ולא עירב והלך בשבת אינו אוסר כמ"ש סעי' ד' גבי מת בשבת ולא עירב. מ"א סק"א. ולע"ד נראה דהטור מיירי בסתמא אזי בעינן מבע"י אבל כשישאלו ואמר שאין בדעתו לחזור אפי' הלך בשבת אינו אוסר ובהכי מיירי הש"ע ולבוש וכה"ג כתב בעה"ק דף כ"ג. א"ר אות א' אמנם מ"ש הע"א והא"ר דאפי' הלך בשבת אינו אוסר כסעי' ד' עיין בס' בית מאיר שכתב לחלק בין שם לכאן. וכ"פ בס' גאון יעקב על מס' עירובין דהטור דוקא קאמר מבע"י וגם כתב לחלק בין שם לכאן יעו"ש. וכ"כ רש"י (בדף מ"ז ע"א) דמיירי בהלך מבע"י. וכ"כ הרשב"א והריטב"א דמיירי דוקא מבע"י יעו"ש. וע"כ יש להחמיר:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>3) There. Who left his house and went, etc. </b>This means before the onset of Shabbos. Tur. It appears to me that this detail was stated to teach that even if he returns on Shabbos, he does not prohibit carrying. Alternatively, it states that he left before the onset of Shabbos because of the law concerning a non-Jew, who does prohibit carrying if he returns on Shabbos, as stated in siman 383. In truth, however, even if the resident was present and did not establish an eruv but left on Shabbos, he does not prohibit carrying, as stated in se'if 4 regarding one who died on Shabbos without having established an eruv. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. In my humble opinion, it appears that the Tur discusses an unspecified case, which requires that he leave before the onset of Shabbos. If, however, he is asked and states that he does not intend to return, then even if he leaves on Shabbos he does not prohibit carrying. The Shulchan Aruch and Levush discuss this latter case. Avodas HaKodesh, folio 23, writes similarly. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1. However, regarding the statement of Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah that even if he leaves on Shabbos he does not prohibit carrying, as in se'if 4, see Beis Meir, who distinguishes between the two cases. Gaon Yaakov on tractate Eruvin likewise rules that the Tur specifically means that he left before the onset of Shabbos, and also distinguishes between the two cases. See there. Rashi on 47a likewise writes that the discussion concerns one who left before the onset of Shabbos. Rashba and Ritva also write that it applies specifically before the onset of Shabbos. See there. One should therefore rule stringently.

<b>ד) שם. אם הסיח מלבו וכו'  </b>זהו מדברי הרמב"ם פ"ד. והגם דבסוגיא שם איתא ההולך לשבות אצל בתו אבל אצל בנו לא וכ"כ הטור. כתב המ"מ שם דס"ל להרמב"ם שלא אמרו כן אלא לדבר בהווה ומצוי שדרך בני אדם לשבות אצל בתו מאצל בנו ולעולם אם הסיח לבו אפי' הלך אצל בנו אינו אוסר ובשלא הסיח אפי' אצל בתו הרי זה אוסר יעו"ש ומביאו ב"י. וכ"כ הפו' כדברי הש"ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>4) There. If he put it out of his mind, etc. </b>This is from Rambam, chapter 4. Although the discussion there states that one who goes to spend Shabbos with his daughter does not prohibit carrying, but one who goes to his son does, and the Tur writes likewise, Maggid Mishneh explains that Rambam maintains this was stated only as a common example. People more commonly spend Shabbos with a daughter than with a son. In every case, if he put returning out of his mind, even if he went to his son he does not prohibit carrying; if he did not put it out of his mind, even if he went to his daughter he does prohibit carrying. See there. Beis Yosef cites his words. The halachic authorities likewise rule in accordance with the Shulchan Aruch.

A non-Jew, however, continues to prohibit carrying even if he went to another city, because as long as he can return, he is regarded as though he had already returned. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 3.
