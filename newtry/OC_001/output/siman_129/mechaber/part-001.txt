<b>באיזה תפלות נושאים כפים. ובו ב סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i>אין נשיאת כפים <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="א"></i>אלא בשחרי' ומוסף ובנעילה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ב"></i>ביום שיש בו נעילה כמו <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>ביום הכפורים אבל לא <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="2"></i>במנח' משום דשכיחא שכרות באותה שעה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ג"></i>שמא יהא הכהן שכור וגזרו במנחה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ד"></i>של תענית <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ה"></i>אטו מנחת שאר ימים אבל בתענית <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ו"></i>שאין בו נעילה הואיל <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="1"></i>ותפלת המנחה <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ז"></i>סמוך לשקיעת <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="3"></i>החמה היא דומה לתפלת נעילה ואינה מתחלפת במנח' של שאר ימים הילכך יש בה נשיא' כפים <small><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ח"></i>והמנהג שלנו כבר נתבאר לעיל בסי' קכ"ח:</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
During Which Services We Lift the Hands [Perform the Priestly Blessing], Containing 2 S'ifim: We only lift the hands [perform the Priestly Blessing] during Shacharit and Mussaf, as well as during N'ilah on a day that has N'ilah, such as Yom Kippur; but not during Mincha, since it is drinking [alcohol] is likely [by] that time, and perhaps the Kohen would be drunk. They decreed [similarly regarding] during Mincha on a fast day because of Mincha on other days (i.e., lest people come to think that Birkat Kohanim during a regular Mincha is permitted). But on a fast day that does not have N'ilah, since the Mincha prayers are said close to [the time of] the setting of the sun, it's similar to the N'ilah prayers and will not be confused with Mincha on other days, therefore they do perform Birkat Kohanim. {Rama: And our custom has already been explained above in Siman 128.}

**** ENGLISH ****
A Kohen who transgressed and went up to the platform [to perform Birkat Kohanim] on Yom Kippur during Mincha - since it's known that no one is drunk then, he may lift his hands [to perform Birkat Kohanim], and they [the congregation] may not bring him down because of any suspicion - in that people shouldn't say that he was unfit [to perform Birkat Kohanim] and that's why they brought him done. {Rama: Gloss: Therefore, during Mincha on Yom Kippur, they say "Our G-d, and the G-d of our Forefathers...", even though it's not a time that's fitting to perform the lifting of the hands; nevertheless, since if [a Kohen] did go up, he does not come down, it's considered to be somewhat of a fitting time (Hagahot Maimoni). And such is what we practice in these lands, even though there are some who disagree.}
