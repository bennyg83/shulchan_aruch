(32) The widow etc. first — the reason is that regarding a widow and likewise a divorcee, since in intercourse the essence of acquisition for her inheritance and for the work of her hands is the entry and not the chuppah — behold it is like acquiring acquisition on Shabbat; and see in Even HaEzer siman 64 seif 5 where the Mechaber explained that even regarding a widow we do not require actual intercourse and it suffices if there was seclusion fit for intercourse to acquire her through this complete acquisition to be like a married woman for everything; and so agreed many poskim; and accordingly if he married her on erev Shabbat and she was pure and he brought her after the chuppah to a room from while it was still day and secluded with her and there were no people there — he acquired her complete acquisition and permitted afterward to have intercourse with her on Shabbat; but if she was not pure from while it was still day or he did not seclude with her in a special room from while it was still day — forbidden to have intercourse with her on Shabbat; therefore one should be very careful when making chuppah of a widow on erev Shabbat that they make the chuppah from while it was still day great so there be fit to seclude with her after the chuppah. And know that many Acharonim wrote that even regarding a virgin one should be careful l'chatchila that they seclude after the chuppah on erev Shabbat from while it was still day, for some say our chuppah — what they stand under the canopy — is not yet called chuppah and she is like betrothed alone; and if so when he has intercourse with her at night the first intercourse is acquiring acquisition on Shabbat; however regarding a virgin seclusion alone from while it was still day suffices and we do not require specifically seclusion fit for intercourse; therefore even if she was a niddah or people enter and leave that room and it is not seclusion fit for intercourse — nevertheless it is a complete chuppah and he acquires complete acquisition in a virgin to be a married woman for everything; and he must seclude for the groom that room and it is like entering his house; and therefore if he married the virgin on erev Shabbat and brought her to a room from while it was still day even though she has not yet immersed and people enter and leave that room [for otherwise it is forbidden for her to seclude with him before first intercourse] — nevertheless he acquires her through bringing her to a special room for him and afterward when she immerses it is permitted for him to have first intercourse on Shabbat and there is no prohibition of acquiring acquisition on Shabbat since he already acquired her from while it was still day; and accordingly even in chuppah of a virgin one should be careful l'chatchila when making the chuppah on erev Shabbat that they make the chuppah from while it was still day great:
