**** ENGLISH ****
The Order of Circling the Bimah, 3 Seifim: The practice is to bring a Torah scroll to and to circle it once each day and seven times on the seventh day, in memory of the Temple [This is] because they would circle the altar. The circles go clockwise. {Rama: (the bimah) Rem"a: And we take seven Torah scrolls onto the bimah on the seventh day (Mahari"l). And on Shabbat we do not circle (Tur) nor do we take Torah scrolls on to the bimah.}

**** ENGLISH ****
It is the custom for even those without a lulav to circle. {Rama: Rem"a: And there are those who say that those without a lulav do not circle, and this is our practice (Tur in the name of Rashi and Ra"n ch. "Lulav"). On the seventh day we take the willows with the lulav while circling (Beit Yosef.). One who goes into mourning during the holiday does not circle. The same is the case for anybody within twelve months of a parent's death (Kol Bo). This is the practice.}
