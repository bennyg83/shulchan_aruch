<b>ס"ב וי"א שמי כו'. </b>עיין בגמ' שם ופסק הרמב"ם כמ"ד בלולב וכן מנהגינו כמ"ד בלולב דלמ"ד בערבה לא היינו מקיפין אלא ביום הז' כמ"ש מ"ד א' דערבה לא עבדינן שבעה זכר למקדש ולכן אין להקיף אלא בלולב. ר"ן. וסברא הראשונה ס"ל כמ"ד בערבה שהרי מ"ד בזקיפה איתותב שם צ"ל אף על גב דערבה לא עבדינן זכר למקדש כל ז' ההקפה עבדינן שאין ההקפה תלויה בערבה ולהרמב"ם והר"ן צ"ל דאף על גב דמסיק דבנטילה לא בהקפה רק חיבוט וניענוע כמ"ש הריטב"א ורב יוסף איתותב אבל למ"ד בלולב לא איתותב:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 2: And some say that one who, etc.</b> See the Gemara there. The Rambam ruled like the opinion that it is with the lulav, and our custom is like the opinion that it is with the lulav; for according to the opinion that it is with the aravah, we would circle only on the seventh day, as written: regarding aravah, we do not perform it all seven as a remembrance of the Mikdash. Therefore one should circle only with the lulav. Ran. The first reasoning holds like the opinion that it is with the aravah, for the opinion that it was by standing the aravah upright was refuted there. One must say that although we do not perform aravah as a remembrance of the Mikdash all seven days, we do perform the circling, for the circling does not depend on the aravah. According to the Rambam and the Ran, one must say that although the conclusion is that with taking it there is no circling, but only beating and shaking, as the Ritva wrote, and Rav Yosef was refuted, nevertheless the opinion that it is with the lulav was not refuted.
