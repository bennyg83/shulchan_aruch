Invalid. This means that it is round on every side like a ball, and so it is also implied from the language of the Maggid Mishneh. However, in my humble opinion it appears that for the mitzvah in the optimal manner one should be concerned for one version in Tosafot, daf 36, which reads 'like a ball,' so that even if it is not as fully round as an actual ball it is invalid.
