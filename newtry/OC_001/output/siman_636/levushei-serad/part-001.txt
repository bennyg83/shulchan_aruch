**** ENGLISH ****
<small>There</small> According to Beit Hillel; rather, it means that within thirty days it has no special advantage except that it does not need renewal, because presumably it was made for the sake of the festival. But before thirty days it needs renewal, because it was made only for shade. Automatically, regarding ganbach and rakbash sukkot, we require only that it be for shade; for this reason, even within thirty days its law is like before thirty days: according to Beit Shammai it is invalid, and according to Beit Hillel it requires renewal.
