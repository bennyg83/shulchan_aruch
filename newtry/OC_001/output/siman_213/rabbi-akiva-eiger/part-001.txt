<small>Magen Avraham, seif katan 7. </small>For there he acted unintentionally. The same applies if he acted deliberately: if, when he recited the blessing, he intended to eat, but afterward deliberately interrupted between the blessing and eating, then although he himself does not fulfill his obligation, the others do. Mishbetzos Zahav above, siman 167, seif 8.
