<b>ס"א ול"ש כו'. </b>טור ור"ל אפילו בכלי דאינו נבלע ומוכח ממש"ש ת"ר גרף כו' ומ"ר עצמן כו' מ' אף בכלי וערש"י ד"ה ומ"ר עצמן כו':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 1. And there is no distinction, etc.</b> Tur. Meaning, even when it is in a vessel that does not absorb. This is clearly established by the statement there: The Sages taught concerning a chamber pot, etc., and by the statement concerning the urine itself, etc., which implies that this applies even when it is in a vessel. See Rashi, s.v. 'and the urine itself,' etc.

<b>ס"ב רביעית כו'. </b>וצ"ע דאם השתין והפסיק באמצע צריך ב' רביעית ואילו היה משתין בפ"א א"צ רק אחת אבל העיקר נראה כהרשב"א שכ' דאפילו בתוך הרבה פעמים די ברביעית דומיא דכ"ש לת"ק דודאי אין חילוק וכ"ה בירושלמי א' כלי גדול וא' כלי קטן וכן הוא בה' שבת סי' ש"ב ס"י ועמ"א ודבריו דחוקים:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Seif 2. A revi'is, etc.</b> This requires study, for if one urinated, stopped in the middle, and then continued, two revi'iyos would be required, whereas had he urinated continuously, only one would be required. The primary ruling, however, appears to follow the Rashba, who wrote that even when one urinated on many occasions, one revi'is is sufficient. This is analogous to any quantity according to the first Tanna, where there is certainly no distinction. This is also stated in the Yerushalmi: whether it is a large vessel or a small vessel. The same appears in Hilchos Shabbos, siman 302, seif 10. See the Magen Avraham, whose words are difficult.
