**** ENGLISH ****
<b>He must complete.</b> See Yoreh De'ah, siman 220, se'if 15, for distinctions in this law; and the reason for the distinction between an individual and a congregation here, the Maggid wrote: that regarding the congregation, when they decreed a fast, the heart [of the beis din] stipulates for them thus; but for an individual, what is in his heart is not [effective] words.
