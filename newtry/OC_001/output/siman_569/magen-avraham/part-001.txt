<b>ציבור וכו'</b>. אין מטריחין עליהם וגם לב ב"ד מתנה עליהם וא"כ אפי' מת החולה א"צ להשלים כ"ה לדעת הר"ן אבל לדעת הרא"ש דוקא שנענו אז א"צ להשלים שצריכים להודות בנפש שבעה וכרס מלאה (כמ"ש ססי' תקע"ה) אבל כשמת צריכין להשלים ומלשון הש"ע שכתב ונענו משמע דס"ל כהרא"ש אבל בלבוש משמע כר"ן וכן נ"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>A congregation, etc.</b> We do not impose a burden upon them, and also the heart of the beis din stipulates for them; and if so, even if the sick person died, they need not complete [the fast]; so it is according to the view of the Ran. But according to the view of the Rosh, specifically when they were answered then they need not complete, for they need to give thanks with a satisfied soul and a full belly (as is written at the end of siman 575); but when he died, they must complete. And from the wording of the Shulchan Aruch, which wrote "and they were answered," it implies that he holds like the Rosh; but in the Levush it implies like the Ran; and so it appears to me.
