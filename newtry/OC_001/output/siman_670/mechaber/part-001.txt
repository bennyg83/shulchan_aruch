<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ה" data-order="5"></i>ריבוי הסעודות שמרבים בהם הם סעודות הרשות <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ו"></i>שלא קבעום למשתה ושמחה: <small>הגה <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="4"></i><i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="3"></i>וי"א שיש קצת מצוה בריבוי <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="3"></i>הסעודות משום <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ז"></i>דבאותן הימים היה חנוכת המזבח [מהר"א מפראג] ונוהגין לומר <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="4"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ח"></i>זמירות ושבחות בסעודות שמרבים בהם <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ט"></i>ואז הוי סעודת מצוה [מנהגים] י"א שיש לאכול גבינה בחנוכה לפי שהנס נעשה בחלב <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="י"></i>שהאכילה יהודית את האויב [כל בו ור"ן]</small>:
**** ENGLISH ****
The many meals which we add on [these days] are voluntary meals, since [the Sages] did not establish them as [days of] feasting and joy. Rem"a: But some say that there is somewhat of a mitzvah in adding meals, because during those days was the Dedication of the Altar (Abraham Kara of Prague). It is the custom to recite hymns and songs of praise during the feasts added on them, and then they are mitzvah meals (Book of Customs). Some say that cheese should be eaten during Hanukkah, because a miracle was done though milk which Yehudit fed the enemy. (Kol Bo and Nissim of Gerona).
