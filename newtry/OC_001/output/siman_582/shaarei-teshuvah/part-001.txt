<b>מסופק, </b> עבה"ט ובבר"י שאין מוכרח וי"ל דמהני אמירת צ' פעמים אף דאומר ברוך אתה השם וכ"כ הפר"ח ובש"ח כתב בדף ש"ו שנ"ל עיקר כהמג"א עיין בזקן אהרן סימן וא"ו ש"ץ שטעה בהמלך הקדוש שחוזר לראש חוזר ואומר קדושה ג"כ והביאו ש"ה ועיין במים רבים סימן נ"א אם חתם בעי"ת מלך אוהב צדקה ומשפט כמו בכל השנה צריך לחזור ולהתפלל ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Uncertain.</b> See Baer Heitev, and in Birkei Yosef that it is not compelling; and one may say that saying it ninety times helps even though he says "Blessed are You, Hashem"; and so wrote the Peri Chadash; and in Shaarei Chochmah he wrote on folio 306 that it appears to him that the main view is like Magen Avraham; see Zaken Aharon, siman 13: a chazzan who erred in "HaMelekh HaKadosh," who returns to the beginning, also repeats and says Kedushah; and they brought this as well; and see Mayim Rabbim, siman 51: if he concluded as throughout the year "Melekh ohev tzedakah u'mishpat," he must return and pray; see there.

<b>He says.</b> And see Peri Chadash: if he said on a weekday "zikkaron teruah," he need not return; and even on Shabbos if he said "yom teruah," he need not return, since a fixed and ordained beis din in Eretz Yisrael sounds the shofar, or another reason, etc.; and see below siman 591 regarding the blessing of "Who hears the sound of the teruah," etc. (•) <b>(In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 6) He says in the prayer, etc.</b> And if he erred and did not mention the character of Yom Tov, they make him return, as written in Beis Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah; and even though Maariv is optional, the prayer of Shabbos and Yom Tov is obligatory; so wrote Mor u'Ketziah; and Machatzis HaShekel wrote that this is not so, but rather that anyway nowadays every Maariv has been treated as obligatory; see there and in siman 265. And he further wrote regarding one who said Maariv properly and concluded "Who sanctifies Israel and the times": even though Rosh Hashana is included in "times," nevertheless he did not intend Rosh Hashana, and he was drawn by the habit of the festival and came thus; and as on Yom Tov if he concluded "Who sanctifies the Shabbos" he has not fulfilled, even though Yom Tov is called Shabbos; and further, that this one did not know from the outset that Rosh Hashana is included in "times," etc.; see there. And see Machatzis HaShekel, siman 268, who brought in the name of Maamar HaMordechai that if Yom Tov or Rosh Hashana falls on Shabbos and he forgot in Maariv and did not mention Yom Tov, he returns, and he cannot rely on hearing the blessing from the chazzan, since they do not mention Yom Tov in it; and Machatzis HaShekel wrote that he fulfills with the blessing me'ein sheva; and in Shaarei Chochmah, folio 306, he disputes Machatzis HaShekel, who compared it to eating fruits and grain foods, etc., which does not fit well; rather he does not fulfill with me'ein sheva; and so it appears to me; and see there in Shaarei Chochmah that if it falls on Motzaei Shabbos and he forgot to say "Vatodi'enu," he need not return, for he will yet make Havdalah over the cup. And see Dagul Merevavah: that the Mechaber omitted the word "be'ahava" is according to his view in siman 87, which is the wording of the Rambam; and according to our custom that we say it on every Yom Tov, also on Rosh Hashana one may say it, and not like Eliyah Rabbah; see there. And in Shaarei Chochmah he wrote that if he forgot to say "melokh al kol ha'aretz" in Shacharis he has fulfilled; and even if he did not say "Our God and God of our fathers, reign over all," but after Yaaleh VeYavo he concluded "Blessed are You, Hashem, Who sanctifies Israel and the Day of Remembrance," he has fulfilled; but in Musaf prayer, which is Malchuyos, it is possible that he must conclude "King over all the earth," analogous to Zichronos and Shofaros; but according to what Abudirham and Kol Bo wrote, that in the verses of Zichronos and Shofaros one says adjacent to the conclusion the last verse of Torah, because in Malchuyos he does not conclude regarding kingship but regarding the sanctity of the day, etc., it is implied from this that the word "melekh" was not fixed as a strict requirement, for otherwise he would need to say a verse of kingship adjacent to the conclusion so that what is adjacent to the conclusion be of like kind to the conclusion; see there. And if it falls on Shabbos and he erred in the conclusion and did not mention Shabbos, the view of Kenesses HaGedolah, siman 487, is that he has fulfilled since he mentioned it in the middle of the blessing, and the Peri Chadash disputes, and likewise several great authorities; see above siman 487 in Baer Heitev s.k. 5; and in Birkei Yosef, Machatzis HaShekel also agreed with the Peri Chadash, and wrote that so it appears from the Ritva, chapter 2 of Beitzah; see there. And see there where he elaborated that our wording "melokh al kol ha'olam kulo" is fine, as the Taz wrote; and Machatzis HaShekel brings proof from the words of the Gemara several times; and see Binyan Ariel on parashas Korach, "the entire congregation, all of them are holy," etc.; see there; and see Taz above, siman 380, and here in the Taz who wrote "and there is a proof for this above," where the printed text is missing the words "siman 380," as written in Yad Ephraim.

<b>רם, </b> ועיין במח"ב דלפי טעם הזוהר והאריז"ל שאסרו בחול כ"ש שצריך ליזהר בר"ה וי"כ דדינא יתיב ע"ש ועיין מה שכתבתי ביד אפרים לעיל סי' ק"א ושם כתבתי דאם אינו יכול לכוון בענין אחר שרי ועיין בש"ח שכתב בשם ספר בית דוד סי' שע"א ושע"ח דמה שנוהגין הציבור לו' כל החזרה של עמידה ומוסף עם הש"ץ אף על גב דנתבאר סי' קכ"ד שצריכים הציבור לשתוק כו' י"ל שאין איסור אלא בכל השנה אבל הכא כיון דכל יומא לא עבדי והשתא עבדי לית לן בה אבל מה שנוהגים להרים קול יותר מן הש"ץ זה ודאי אינו הגון וראוי לגעור בהם ע"ש ובמדינתינו לא ראיתי מנהג זה רק הציבור שותקים ושומעים לדברי הש"ץ אך בפסוקי מלכיות זכרונות ושופרות יש אומרים עם הש"ץ בלחש ואף שמזכיר ג"כ שמות הקדושים כיון שהם פסוקי תנ"ך הוה כקורא בתורה וכ"כ הט"ז סי' תרכ"א ס"ק ג' ועיין בש"ת לעיל סי' רט"ו וכ"כ הר"י לענין אמיר' אוחילה שאומרים הציבור עם הש"ץ מותר ודלא כקיצור של"ה ע"ש אך י"ל בלחש שלא יבטל הש"ץ מתפילתו וגם יזהר כשירצה לו' עם הש"ץ גם משאר נוסח התפלה כדי לכוון לבו בהתעוררת תפלה ותחנונים, מ"מ לא יזכיר שום שם וכשיתחיל הש"ץ לו' או"א מלוך כו' יתחיל הוא מתיבת מלוך וגם בשאר הנוסח ידלג כשיבא להזכרת שם ואפשר שגם זה יכול לו' דרך בקשה כמו שאומרים בשחרית לפני פ' עקידה דרך תפלה ותחנונים ומ"מ עכ"פ יזהר שלא לסיים הברכה וגם אין לעשות כן כ"א כשמרגיש בנפשו שיתעורר ע"ז לכוון לבו לאביו שבשמים:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Loud.</b> And see Machatzis HaShekel: according to the reason of the Zohar and the Ari, of blessed memory, who forbade it on a weekday, all the more so one must be careful on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur, when judgment sits; see there; and see what I wrote in Yad Ephraim above, siman 101, and there I wrote that if he cannot intend in any other manner it is permitted; and see Shaarei Chochmah who wrote in the name of Sefer Beis David, simanim 371 and 378, that the custom of the congregation to say the entire repetition of the Amidah and Musaf with the chazzan, even though it is explained in siman 124 that the congregation must be silent, etc., one may say that the prohibition is only throughout the year, but here, since they do not do so every day and now they do so, we have no concern; but as for those who raise their voice louder than the chazzan, that is certainly improper, and it is fitting to rebuke them; see there. And in our country I have not seen this custom; rather the congregation is silent and listens to the words of the chazzan; but in the verses of Malchuyos, Zichronos, and Shofaros some say them with the chazzan in a whisper, and even though he also mentions the holy Names, since they are verses of Tanach it is like one reading in the Torah; and so wrote the Taz, siman 621, s.k. 3; and see Shaarei Teshuvah above, siman 215; and so wrote R' Yosef regarding saying Ochilah that the congregation says with the chazzan: it is permitted, and not like the Kitzur Shelah; see there. But one should say it in a whisper so as not to distract the chazzan from his prayer; and he should also be careful if he wishes to say with the chazzan also from the rest of the prayer wording in order to focus his heart with arousal of prayer and supplications: nevertheless he should not mention any Divine Name; and when the chazzan begins to say "Our God and God of our fathers, reign," etc., he should begin from the word "reign," and also in the rest of the wording he should skip when he comes to mentioning a Name; and it is possible that even this he may say by way of request, as they say in Shacharis before the passage of the Binding by way of prayer and supplications; and nevertheless he should at least be careful not to conclude the blessing; and also one should not do so except when he senses in himself that thereby his heart will be aroused to intend toward his Father in heaven.
