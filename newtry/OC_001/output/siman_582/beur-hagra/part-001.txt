<b>ונוהגין כו' תכתב. </b>כי מש"ש שלשה ספרים כו' היינו לחיי ע"ה כמ"ש תוס' שם ודלא כהר"ן שכת' לעה"ז וצדיקים ורשעים בדינן דהא אמרינן ואדם נידון בר"ה וגזר דין כו' אלמא אין החתימה אלא ביה"כ ומתני' בכל אדם מיירי וכן מש"ש תבואה שאירע בה קרי כו' אדם כו'. החליט הגמ' בכל אדם אלא דחיי עה"ז שוין בכל אדם אלא דאע"פ שנכתב אם חוזר בעי"ת נקרע הגזר דין ונכתב ונחתם ביה"כ לטובה וזהו התועלת דאינו נחתם עד יה"כ וז"ש שב בנתיים כו' וזה שמבקשין בעי"ת וכתבנו כו' ליקרע גזר דין ויכתבנו לחיים ולכן אין לאומרו ותחתם אלא תכתב:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And they have the custom, etc., "may you be written."</b> For what is written there, "three books," etc., that refers to life of the World to Come, as Tosafos wrote there, and not like the Ran who wrote regarding this world, and righteous and wicked in their judgment; for we say "and a person is judged on Rosh Hashana and the decree," etc.; thus it is clear that the sealing is only on Yom Kippur, and the Mishnah speaks of every person; and likewise what is written there regarding produce concerning which an incident occurred, etc., a person, etc.: the Gemara decided it regarding every person; only that life of this world is equal for every person; only that even though it was written, if he returns during the Ten Days of Repentance the decree is torn, and he is written and sealed on Yom Kippur for good; and this is the benefit that he is not sealed until Yom Kippur; and that is what is written, "sit in the meantime," etc.; and this is what they request during the Ten Days of Repentance, "and write us," etc., that the decree be torn and He write us for life; and therefore one should not say "and may you be sealed," but rather "may you be written."
