**** ENGLISH ****
One who is under duress — such as one who needs to remain [outside the techum] until dark at the edge of the techum for the sake of a mitzvah — may pray the prayer of Motza'ei Shabbos from plag haMinchah and onward and make Havdalah [in the prayer] immediately; but he may not recite the blessing over fire [ner], and likewise he is forbidden to perform melachah until the emergence of the stars. {Rama: And the custom is to say "Vehu Rachum" and "Barchu" with an extended, pleasant [melody], in order to add from the weekday to the holy [Shabbos] (Or Zarua).}
