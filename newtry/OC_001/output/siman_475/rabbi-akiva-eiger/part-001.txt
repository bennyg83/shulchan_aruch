<small>מג"א סק"י </small>היאך נפטר הקרי"ן בזה דהא צריך לברך עליו שהכל. לענ"ד י"ל בזה אם בירך בפה"א יצא. כיון דהוא פרי האדמה. אלא כיון דמבושלים טובים אינו חשוב לברך על חי ברכה מבוררת ודי בברכת שהכל אבל מ"מ יש לומר דבדיעבד דיצא עמג"א לעיל סי' ר"ה ובהעוז"ר. גם מ"ש המג"א ועוד נ"ל שאין לברך כלל כמ"ש סי' ר"ב גבי פלפלים לא ידעתי דהתם אמרי' דמדינא סגי בשהכל אבל לא דלא יברך כלל זולת בפלפלים יבישים דליכא אוכל כלל ולזה וודאי לא דמי לקרי"ן:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Magen Avraham, s.k. 10.</b> How is horseradish (chrein) discharged thereby, for one must bless Shehakol on it? In my humble opinion one can say on this: if he blessed Borei peri ha'adamah, he fulfilled after the fact, since it is a fruit of the ground; only, because when cooked it is better, it is not considered significant enough to bless on the raw item with its distinctive blessing, and Shehakol suffices. Nevertheless one can say that after the fact he fulfilled; see Magen Avraham above, siman 205, and HaOzer. Also what the Magen Avraham wrote, "And further it appears to me that one should not bless at all, as written in siman 202 regarding peppers," I do not know [how that follows]: there we say that by law Shehakol suffices, but not that one does not bless at all, except for dry peppers where there is no food at all; and to that, horseradish (chrein) is certainly not comparable.

<b>Se'if 4.</b> For example, if gentiles or bandits forced him. In Mishbetzos Zahav, in the inclusive introductions, part 3, letter hei, he is in doubt whether, if they forced him to eat maror, one can say he did not fulfill, since it is bitter and harmful; see there.
