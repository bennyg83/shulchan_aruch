<b>א) [סעיף א'] יסדר שלחנו.  </b>והאר"י ז"ל היה מקפיד מאד וזהיר לאכול בשלחן של ד' רגלים כדוגמת שלחן של בהמ"ק כמ"ש בשאר הכוו' דף ע"ב ע"א. וכ"כ בס' התיקונים תיקון מ"ז וז"ל ושבת צריך לתקנא ביה פתורא בארבע רגלין כגוונא דפתורא דלעילא דאתמר ביה זה השלחן וכו' ובג"ד תיקנו מארי מתני' שלחן בצפון ונר דלוק לימינא כגוונא דלעילא דאתמר ביה מנורה בדרום. מטה באמצעיתא מסטרא דעמודא דאמצעיתא יעו"ש. ובההוא ביתא דאשתכח דירא דא מתקנא בסידורא דא מנרתא בדרום ושלחן בצפון ומטה בין צפון לדרום אמרין אלין מלאכין דקא נחתין עם שכינתא דאיהי נשמה יתירה לית דין אחר הדיוט דע"ה אלהין אתר דרעוא ביה מן קדם ה' תיקונים תיקון כ"ד:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1) [Seif 1] He should arrange his table.</b> The Arizal was exceedingly particular to eat at a four-legged table resembling the table in the Beis HaMikdash, as stated in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 72a. Sefer HaTikkunim, tikkun 47, similarly states: On Shabbos one must prepare a four-legged table corresponding to the supernal table, regarding which it is stated, 'This is the table,' etc. For this reason the masters of the Mishnah ordained that the table be in the north and a lit lamp to its right, corresponding to the arrangement above, regarding which it is stated, 'The menorah in the south.' The bed is in the middle, on the side of the middle pillar; see there. In a house in which this dwelling is found arranged in this order, with the menorah in the south, the table in the north, and the bed between north and south, the angels who descend with the Shechinah, which is the additional soul, say: This is not the dwelling of an ignorant commoner, but a place upon which favor from before Hashem rests. Tikkunim, tikkun 24.

<b>2) One must also</b> arrange twelve loaves upon the table at each of the three Shabbos meals, as stated in Sefer HaTikkunim there. Shaar HaKavanos there similarly writes that one must take care to place twelve loaves upon the table at every meal, corresponding to the twelve loaves of the lechem hapanim. Arrange them as follows: six loaves on the right side of the table and six on the left. The six on the right should be arranged three over three, in a segol-like formation, and likewise on the left. More specifically, place four loaves on the right, two over two, and similarly on the left. In the middle place four other loaves, two over two, but separate each pair from the other pair with a space between them, so that one pair is near the four on the right and the other pair is near the four on the left. The twelve loaves on Shabbos night correspond to the twelve strands; on Shabbos day, to the twelve white areas; and at Shabbos Minchah, to the twelve rectifications of the beard. Intend that the four loaves on the right, arranged two over two, correspond to the four letters of one Name Havayah. The two lower loaves are the two letters heh, which are feminine, and the two upper loaves are the letters yud and vav, which are masculine. Similarly, the four loaves on the left constitute a second Name Havayah in precisely the same manner. The two middle loaves near the right side embody the mystical meaning of the two letters yud and heh, with yud above heh. The two middle loaves near the left side are the letters vav and heh, with vav above heh. When you wish to recite the blessing of Hamotzi, take the two upper loaves among the four in the middle and join them in your two hands. The two bottoms of the loaves should touch one another, with the face of one on its right side and the face of the other on its left side. The two loaves will then appear like one loaf with two faces on its two sides, resembling the lechem hapanim in the Beis HaMikdash. Intend that the loaf in your right hand is the letter yud of Havayah and the loaf in your left hand is the letter vav, thereby drawing bounty from yud to the first heh beneath it and from vav to the final heh beneath it. Then recite Hamotzi. With the letter heh in the word Hamotzi, intend the supernal Binah; with the letter heh in the word haaretz, intend Malchus; and with the word lechem, intend Tiferes, meaning to draw illumination from Binah to Tiferes and Malchus. With the word lechem, also intend the aforementioned bread, which corresponds to thirteen strands. This means that the bread corresponds to three Names Havayah containing twelve letters, which together with their collective unit total thirteen. This applies at night; during the morning intend the thirteen white areas, and during Minchah the thirteen rectifications of the beard, as stated above. Afterward, break off an olive-sized portion from the right-hand loaf, corresponding to the letter yud of Havayah, and eat it yourself with that intention. Then break off a second portion the size of an egg and give it to your wife, intending that it corresponds to the final letter yud of Adnus. Intend that the two letters join to form the interwoven Name. Afterward, break off portions and distribute them to the other participants in the meal. All this is explained in Shaar HaKavanos there; see there. Even if a distinguished person reclines at the table, one should not give him a portion before giving one to his wife, so as not to create a separation between the two letters yud, which represent him and her, as is known to those familiar with esoteric wisdom. Aspaklaria HaMeirah, folio 107a; Kaf HaChaim, siman 36, paragraph 48. We already recorded the intention for Hamotzi above in siman 167, paragraphs 22 and 32, and the intention for eating in siman 157, paragraph 20; see there. Even when Yom Tov occurs on Shabbos, one must arrange twelve loaves and follow the same procedure as on other Shabbosos.

<b>ג) ומי  </b>שא"א לו בי"ב לחמים לא יפחות מד כמ"ש בזוה"ק פ' פנחס דף רמ"ה ע"א יעו"ש ויניחם ב' ע"ג ב' ויכוין שהם כנגד ד' אותיות הוי"ה י' ע"ג ה' ו' ע"ג ה' ויבצע ויכוין ככל הנז' ואם אין לו אלא שלשה לא יניח כ"א שנים. וכן אם יש לו חמשה או ששה לא יניח כ"א ארבעה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>3) One who</b> cannot obtain twelve loaves should use no fewer than four, as stated in the sacred Zohar, parashas Pinchas, folio 245a; see there. He should place them two over two and intend that they correspond to the four letters of Havayah: yud over heh and vav over heh. He should break the bread and have all the aforementioned intentions. If he has only three loaves, he should place only two upon the table. Similarly, if he has five or six, he should place only four.
