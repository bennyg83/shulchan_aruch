**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Taz, seif katan 1</small> “Occupied with the slice.” Accordingly, even the host should not give to the attendant while the cup is in the attendant's hand. Taz's concluding statement that it refers to the guests means that they are forbidden to give even when the cup is in the host's hand. This is clear.
