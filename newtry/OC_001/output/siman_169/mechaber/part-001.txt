**** ENGLISH ****
Anything that is brought before a person that has a fragrance and the person desires it, one must give from it to the meal attendant immediately. And it is a pious practice to give to him immediately from every type. And one should not give to him while the cup is in his hand or in the hand of the host. And it is forbidden to give him a piece of bread unless he knows that he has washed his hands. {Rama: [And this is specifically regarding the attendant, but regarding another person at the meal, it is permitted to give in this manner.] R' Yonah, chapter "Elu Devarim."}

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ה" data-order="5"></i>השמש מברך בורא פרי הגפן על (כל) כוס וכוס שיתנו לו לפי <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="7"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יב"></i>שהוא <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="5"></i>כנמלך וברכה אחרונה אינו מברך אלא לבסוף ואינו צריך לברך על כל פרוסה ופרוסה אם יש <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יג"></i>אדם חשוב בסעודה שיודע שיתנו לו <i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="4"></i>כל צרכו מפת ואם אין אדם חשוב בסעודה צריך לברך על כל פרוסה ופרוסה כמו על היין: <small>הגה <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יד"></i>שנים שהיו אוכלין ביחד השמש אוכל עמהם בלא נטילת רשות כדי שיצטרפו לזמון [וע"ל סי' ק"ע סכ"א]:</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
The servant recites the blessing "who creates the fruit of the vine" over each and every cup that is given to him, because he is like a king. He recites the final blessing only at the end. He does not need to recite a blessing over each piece of bread if there is an important person at the meal, since he knows they will give him all the bread he needs. But if there is no important person at the meal, he must recite a blessing over each piece of bread, just as he does over the wine. {Rama: Rema: When two people are eating together, the servant may eat with them without asking permission, so that they may join together for zimmun. [See below, section 170:21]}
