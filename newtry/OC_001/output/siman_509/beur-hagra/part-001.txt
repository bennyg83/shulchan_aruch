**** ENGLISH ****
<b>One may not sharpen, etc. And we do not rule, etc. When is this said, etc., lest, etc.</b> He ruled like the latter version there: R' Yehuda said in the name of Shmuel: that which you said regarding wood, it is permitted, etc.; according to his method of ruling in every place like the latter version. And there: who is the Tanna who holds that sharpening itself is forbidden, etc.? That is to say, to sharpen even on wood is forbidden, not like R' Yehuda; but on a stone whetstone R' Yehuda agrees that one is making a utensil, for making a utensil R' Yehuda agrees is forbidden, as written there, 32a: one may not hollow out, etc.; R' Yehuda says, etc.; and in the Gemara there regarding a knife, not like, etc. And the reason is that even regarding ochel nefesh itself, melacha done for a long duration is forbidden, as written above in siman 495, seif 2, and all the more so regarding makhshirim; and whoever makes a utensil is making it for many days. And that is what the Gemara said: one passes a knife over the mouth of a basket and over the waste of a mill to sharpen it, like R' Yehuda, for that is like a wooden whetstone; and it said "and we do not rule thus," so that one not do it on a stone whetstone; but regarding all preparations for ochel nefesh he holds that we do rule thus. And there, 1: a knife that became nicked, etc., meaning on a wooden whetstone; and there: a knife that stood, etc., likewise on wood, and like R' Yehuda; and these words are, etc., for otherwise it is entirely forbidden, lest one sharpen it on a whetstone. And so wrote the Rif, that a knife that stood is specifically on a basket and on a mill; and all this when it was nicked on Yom Tov; and see the Ran there. And so it is implied in the Yerushalmi, chapter 5, law 2: one may not sharpen a knife, but one may draw it over its fellow; R' Chisda said in the name of R' Yehuda: R' Yehuda said in the name of Shmuel that all agree it is to remove the greasiness upon it; and that is like the version that taught it upon the mishnah and like the latter version, and he establishes the mishnah like the Rabbis; and see Magen Avraham, s.k. 5: who is the Tanna, etc.; R' Chisda, etc.; and like their method in the Yerushalmi.
