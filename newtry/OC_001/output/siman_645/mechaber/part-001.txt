**** ENGLISH ****
If its leaves burst — that is, they became loose from the spine of the lulav like palm leaves, (meaning that they do not rise from the spine and instead hang down — it is invalid. {Rama: [the Maggid ch. 8] Rema: And obviously [it is invalid] if its leaves burst and were uprooted from under the spine, in which case it is invalid even if bound (Tur, Ra"n ch. 3. Lulav leaves grows like this: when they grow, they grow in pairs attached together from the back. The back of these sets is called the tyomet. If the tyomet is split it is invalid. **** ENGLISH ****
4. If the leaves were not one on top of the other like lulavs are but rather one below the other, if the head of one reaches the stem of the one above it such that the whole spine of the lulav is covered in leaves, it is kosher.
