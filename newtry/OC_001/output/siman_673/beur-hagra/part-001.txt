**** ENGLISH ****

At first glance this also seems implied by the Tur, except that he wrote, 'It does not appear so to my father, the Rosh, of blessed memory,' and the Rosh permits casual use. The Taz follows his own view, for in s.k. 3 he emended the Rosh. But it appears that the reason he permits is from what is written regarding coins, which is a weekday use.

<b>אם נתערב. </b>כתנא דליטרא קציעות. בפ"ק די"ט וכ"פ בי"ד סי' פ"ו ס"ג וכאן מדליקין אותו במנין אע"ג דאינו במנין רק משום שהיא קדושה ולא בעצמו דהכי אשכחן אפי' לקולא בפ' הערל פ"א ב' בחתיכות חטאות טמאה בשל טהורות דתעלה וכ' תוס' שם ד"ה דברי הכל כו' דלא שייך כו' ועט"ז שחולק ע"ז וכמ"ש בתוס' שם דאזלינן אחר מה שיהי' אחר הביטול וכ"כ בש"ע י"ד סי' ק"א ס"א ואפי' אם כו':
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>If it became mixed.</b> Like the tanna of a litra of pressed figs in the first chapter of Yom Tov, and so it is ruled in Yoreh Deah, siman 86, se'if 3. Here they are lit by count, even though it is counted only because it is sanctified and not intrinsically, for we find this even leniently in chapter HaArel, folio 81b, regarding pieces of impure sin-offerings among pure ones, that it becomes nullified. Tosafos wrote there, s.v. 'All agree,' etc., that it is not applicable, etc. See the Taz, who disagrees with this. And as Tosafos wrote there, we follow what it will be after the nullification. So too it is written in Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh Deah, siman 101, se'if 1: 'and even if,' etc.
