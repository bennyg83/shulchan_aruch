**** HEBREW ****
<small>(במ"א סק"ב)</small> לנר שרי (ב"י ר"מ ורש"ל בתשו') וב"ח פסק להחמי' וכ"כ הסמ"ג וסמ"ק דאפי' כצ"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>In Magen Avraham, paragraph 2.</small> For the lamp it is permitted. Beis Yosef, Rav Moshe, and Maharshal in a responsum. Bach ruled to be stringent, and so wrote Sefer Mitzvos Gadol and Sefer Mitzvos Katan; this is how one should read it.
