**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Se'if 5 in the gloss</small> With the waters of Tiberias he does hagalah in them. This is no novel ruling. Rather, in Orchos Chayim he wrote: and if they became forbidden through the hot waters of Tiberias, it appears that it is possible to kasher them by means of fire-heated water in a kli rishon, for hagalah by means of fire is valid for everything; end quote. It is clear that the novel point is that one may do hagalah even by means of fire-heated water, and we do not say that the hagalah water must be specifically Tiberias water, as in [the rule] "as it absorbed, so it expels." And in the Beis Yosef it is stated: he does hagalah in them or in fire-heated water.
