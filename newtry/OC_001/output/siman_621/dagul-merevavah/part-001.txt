**** ENGLISH ****
On the Mechaber: In my humble opinion, in the Shemoneh Esrei of Mussaf he should say 'and two goats to atone.' Although the inner goat is seemingly not part of the Mussaf offerings, nevertheless we see that the Torah mentioned it in Parashas Pinchas together with the Mussaf offerings, as it is written, 'besides the sin-offering of atonement.' Also the Rambam in chapter 10 of Temidin u'Musafin, when he mentioned the Mussaf of Yom Kippur, also brought the inner sin-offering, even though he did not mention there the other offerings of the day. This implies that the inner goat is connected to the Mussaf. According to this, if he says 'and two goats,' he should no longer say 'besides the sin-offering of atonement.' Even without this, it is not proper in my eyes to say the verse 'besides the sin-offering,' for there it says, 'and the continual burnt-offering,' etc.; how can he then say again, 'and two daily offerings according to their law'? Also in the Mussaf prayer of Rosh Hashanah, it is not proper to say the entire verse 'besides the burnt-offering of the month' as it is written, for this same reason: how can he mention again 'and two daily offerings,' as is written above in siman 551.
