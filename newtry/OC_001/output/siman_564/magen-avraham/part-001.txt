<b>אוכל ושותה</b>. נ"ל דבת"ח אסור דמשעה שחלם צריך להתענו' כמ"ש סי' רפ"ח ס"ד וכ"כ של"ה דמשעה שחלם לא ישמש מטתו:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Eats and drinks.</b> It appears to me that regarding a dream-fast it is forbidden, for from the moment he dreamed he must fast, as is written in siman 288, se'if 4; and so the Shalah wrote, that from the moment he dreamed he should not have marital relations.

**** HEBREW ****
<b>וי"א כו'</b>. ונוהגין להתנו' אפי' לשתיה ונכון הוא [לבו' וב"ח] ונ"ל דהכל לפי מה שהוא אדם אם רגיל לשתות בלילה מותר לשתות בלא תנאי ועסי' תקפ"א ס"ב בהג"ה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And some say, etc.</b> And the custom is to make a condition even for drinking, and this is proper [Levush and Bach]. And it appears to me that it all depends on what kind of person he is: if he is accustomed to drink at night, it is permitted to drink without a condition. And see siman 581, se'if 2, in the gloss of the Rama.
