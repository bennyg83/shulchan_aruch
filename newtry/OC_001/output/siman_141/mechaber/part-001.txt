**** ENGLISH ****
And word which is read [one way] and written [another way], it is a "Halakha l'Moshe Mi-Sinai" ["A law transmitted to Moses on Sinai"] that is should be written like it is in the Torah, but read in a different manner [i.e. read differently than it is written]. It happened once that someone read it the way it was written (and he was standing) before the great leaders of the generation, the Rabbis: R. Yitzchak Abuhav, R. Avraham Valenci, and R.
