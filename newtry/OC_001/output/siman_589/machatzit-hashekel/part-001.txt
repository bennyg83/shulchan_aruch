**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(s.k. 2) His own kind, etc.</b> And the Rif explained, etc. (I did not find this anywhere. And the matter itself is also astonishing: that for some time he should be male and for some time female) for does he not have both male and female organs? I also saw in the Shulchan Aruch Baer Heitev that he wrote: if so, why do we need a verse to exclude that circumcision of an androginos does not override Shabbos? Either way: if he was born at a time when he is female, it is obvious; and if then he is male, who knows that after some time he will be female? End quote.

<b>(ס"ק ג) יכולות לתקוע ולא כו'</b> דהב"ח דייק מדנקט הטור דוקא נשים וקטנים שכתב ואע"פ שנשים וקטנים פטורים יכולים לתקוע ולברך ואין מוחין בידם כו' ולכן כתב הב"ח וז"ל ונראה דוקא נשים כיון שיש להם דעת יכולים להכניס עצמן בחיוב דקי"ל גדול המצווה ועושה ממי שאינו מצווה ועושה דאלמא מי שאינו מצווה ועושה נמי שכר יש לו. וכן קטן אפי' הגיע לחינוך מאחר שבא לידי חיוב כשיגדל אין מוחין בידם עכ"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(s.k. 3) They may blow, and not, etc.</b> For the Bach inferred from the Tur's specifying specifically women and minors, that he wrote: and even though women and minors are exempt, they may blow and recite the blessing, and we do not protest against them, etc. Therefore the Bach wrote, and this is his language: and it appears specifically women, since they have understanding, may take the obligation upon themselves; for we hold "greater is one who is commanded and does than one who is not commanded and does" hence one who is not commanded and does also has reward. And likewise a minor, even if he has reached the age of training, since he will come to obligation when he grows up; we do not protest against them. End quote.

<b>ול"ד לציצית שב' מצות הן כו'</b> וכתב בספר ח"מ וז"ל צ"ע מ"ש מהא דכתב מ"א סי' תרל"ט ס"ק י"ג לענין סכה דההליכה לבה"כ הוי הפסק. והלא גם בסכה מצוה א' היא עכ"ל. ולא ידעתי מאי ק"ל דמאי הפרש יש בין ציצית לסכה דהא גם בציצית מ"ש שתי מצות הן הכוונה דאין ללבישה שניה שייכ' ללבישה ראשונה. וא"כ אחר לבישה א' נגמרה המצוה משא"כ כאן בתקיעות דכל התקיעות ואפי' דמעומד יש להן שייכות לתקיעות מיושב כי לפי תקנת חכמים צריך לתקוע בין דמיושב ובין דמעומד וא"כ לא נגמרה המצוה עד ששמע כל התקיעות וא"כ סכה דומה ממש לציצית דכשהלך לחוץ לסוכה כבר נגמרה מצות ישיבת סכה הראשונה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And it is unlike tzitzis, which are two mitzvos, etc.</b> And he wrote in Sefer Chokhmat Manoach, and this is his language: this requires further study; how is this different from what the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 639, s.k. 13, regarding sukkah, that walking to the synagogue is an interruption? For is sukkah not also one mitzvah? End quote. And I do not know what difficulty there is: what difference is there between tzitzis and sukkah? For regarding tzitzis as well, that which he said they are two mitzvos means that the second wearing has no connection to the first wearing; and so after one wearing the mitzvah is completed, which is not so here regarding the tekios, for all the tekios, even the standing ones, have a connection to the seated tekios; for according to the Sages' enactment one must blow both seated and standing, and so the mitzvah is not completed until he has heard all the tekios. And so sukkah is exactly like tzitzis: when he went outside the sukkah, the first mitzvah of sitting in the sukkah was already completed.

<b>דע דמ"ש בש"ע סעיף ז' המודר כו'</b> מותר כו' משום דמצות לאו ליהנות ניתנו מכל מקום אם א"ל כו' אסור דזהו מיקרי הנאה שעושה שליחותו ואם אמר קונם תקיעתו כו' אסור משום דנדרים חלין ע"ד מצוה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Know that what is written in the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 7, "one who vowed," etc.</b> is permitted, etc., because mitzvos were not given for the sake of benefit; nevertheless if he said to him, etc., it is forbidden, for this is called benefit; that he performs his agency. And if he said "konam" his blowing, etc., it is forbidden, because vows take effect upon a matter of mitzvah.
