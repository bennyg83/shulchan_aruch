**** ENGLISH ****
Even though women and exempt, they are permitted to blow. So too, even after a man fulfilled his obligation, he is still able to blow for their obligation. However, [in these cases,] they should not make the blessing, nor should he make the blessing for them. {Rama: Rema: And [our] custom is that women make blessings on positive time-bound mitzvot, so here too they would make the blessing for themselves. However, others should not bless for them if they have already fulfilled their obligation and are only blowing for the sake of women. However, if they are blowing [for women and] a man who is obligated, they make a blessing on his account, even though they already fulfilled their obligation, as has been explained in Chapter 585, subsection 2 in the first gloss.}

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="י" data-order="10"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יב"></i>המודר הנאה מחבירו מותר לתקוע לו תקיעה של מצוה <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="כ" data-order="11"></i>ודוקא שהוא תוקע <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יג"></i>מאליו להוציאו אבל אם אמר לו המודר תקע <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יד"></i>והוציאני אסור: <small>הגה ואם אמר קונם תקיעותיו עלי בכל ענין אסור: (ר"ן)</small>:
**** ENGLISH ****
If somebody swears off benefiting from his friend, that friend is permitted to sound a shofar for him to fulfill the obligation. This is only the case when he sounds the shofar unprompted to fulfill his obligation. But if the swearer says "Blow to fulfill my obligation," this is forbidden. {Rama: Rema: If he says "His soundings are konam to me," it is always forbidden (Ra"n).}
**** END BLOCK ****

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ל" data-order="12"></i><i data-commentator="Turei Zahav" data-order="3"></i>המתעסק בתקיעת שופר <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="טו"></i>להתלמד לא יצא ידי חובתו וכן השומע מן המתעסק לא יצא <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="מ" data-order="13"></i>וכן התוקע לשורר ולא נתכוון לתקיעת מצוה לא יצא <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="נ" data-order="14"></i>נתכוין שומע לצאת ידי חובתו ולא נתכוון התוקע להוציאו או שנתכוון התוקע להוציאו <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="טז"></i>ולא נתכוון השומע לצאת לא יצא ידי חובתו עד שיתכוון שומע ומשמיע:
**** ENGLISH ****
Someone who is engaged in blowing a shofar in order to practice doesn't fulfil his obligation. Similarly, someone who hears shofar from someone such a person doesn't fulfil his obligation. Similarly, someone who is blowing the shofar to make music and doesn't intend for the mitzva of blowing the shofar doesn't fulfil his obligation. If the listener intends to fulfil the mitzva but the blower doesn't have intent for him or if blower has intent for him but the listener does not; he doesn't fulfil his obligation until the two of them have intent, the listener and the blower.
**** END BLOCK ****

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ס" data-order="15"></i>מי שתקע ונתכוין להוציא כל השומע תקיעתו ושמע השומע ונתכוין לצאת ידי חובתו אע"פ שאין התוקע מתכוין לפלוני זה ששמע תקיעתו ואינו יודעו יצא שהרי נתכוין להוציא לכל מי <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יז"></i>שישמענו לפיכך <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יח"></i>מי שהיה <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="5"></i>מהלך <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Sha'arei Teshuvah" data-order="3"></i>בדרך או יושב בתוך ביתו <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יט"></i>ושמע תקיעו' משליח צבור יצא אם נתכוין לצאת שהרי ש"ץ מתכוין להוציא את הרבים ידי חובתן:
**** ENGLISH ****
If somebody blows the shofar with the intention of fulfilling the obligation of every listener, and somebody hears it and has the intention to fulfill their obligation, even if the blower is not intending for this specific person who hears his blasts and doesn't know them, they fulfill their obligation, given that he had the intention to fulfill the obligation of everybody who heard him. Therefore, if somebody was walking on the street or sitting at home and heard the communal blasts, they fulfill their obligation if they have the intention to fulfill their obligation because the communal blower has the intention to fulfill the members of the community's obligations.
