**** ENGLISH ****
(18) One who was walking on the way, etc., if he intended to fulfill, etc. It is stated in the Yerushalmi: this was taught only regarding one who is passing by; but regarding one who is standing, there is a chazakah that he intended. And see the Eliyah Rabbah, who wrote that the Yerushalmi's intent is regarding one who was passing and stood to hear the sound of the tekios, for then we say that even if he does not remember whether he then intended to fulfill, nevertheless there is a chazakah that he intended; but if he was standing in his house, we do not say there is a chazakah that he intended. A blind person is obligated in the tekios; and if he was established to blow every year and they removed him, if they do not find one as expert as he, they should restore him to his appointment. And l'chatchila one should not remove him because he is blind, even though another as expert as he is found [Magen Avraham in the name of the Radbaz].
