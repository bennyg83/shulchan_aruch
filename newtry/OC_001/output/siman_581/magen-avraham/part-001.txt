<b>מר"ח אלול ואילך</b>. אבל בר"ח א"א סליחו' ותחנונים: כתב הח"ש ולבוש ומ"ב ושל"ה דיש להתחיל לתקוע מיום ראשון דר"ח אלול דאז הוי מ' יום שלפני יה"כ כמו שעלה משה בהר כדאיתא בטור וכ"כ מט"מ בשם מהרי"ל וכתב דהנוהגים להתחיל ביום שני טעמייהו לתקוע ל' יום עם ב' ימים של ר"ה ול"נ דהטעם כמ"ש התוס' בב"ק דף פ"ב בשם התנחומא דמשה עלה ביום שני דר"ח אלול רק שעברו' לאלול דהאי שתא וא"כ אין לזוז מהמנהג להתחיל ביום שני:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>From Rosh Chodesh Elul onward.</b> But on Rosh Chodesh itself one does not say Selichos and Tachanunim. The Chochmas Shlomo and Levush and Maginei Eretz and Shelah wrote that one should begin to sound [the shofar] from the first day of Rosh Chodesh Elul, for then it is forty days before Yom Kippur, as Moshe ascended the mountain, as is stated in the Tur; and so wrote Mateh Moshe in the name of the Maharil; and he wrote that those who are accustomed to begin on the second day, their reason is to sound thirty days including the two days of Rosh Hashana. And it appears to me that the reason is as Tosafos wrote in Bava Kamma, folio 82, in the name of the Tanchuma, that Moshe ascended on the second day of Rosh Chodesh Elul, only that Elul was intercalated that year; and if so one should not budge from the custom to begin on the second day.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Erev Rosh Hashana.</b> Even young children fast (Maharil, siman 3). The Beis Yosef wrote: even though it is stated in the Midrash that on erev Rosh Hashana the great ones fast, they all became accustomed to fast, because regarding a matter of suffering, one who wishes to make himself great does so, as is stated in the third chapter of Taanis; end of his words. And this requires further study, for he himself did not rule so at the beginning of siman 575; see there. And it appears to me that there, where one fasts for the distress of the community, we say you are not important enough to seek mercy upon them, as is written at the beginning of siman 579; which is not so here, where each one fasts for his sins, for there are those who fast more than this; if so there is no arrogance. And that they fast specifically on this day is because it is a day of favor, as we find in the Midrash that the great ones fast, as above. And even though those [Ten] Days are days of favor even more, for even the middling ones fast, and if we consider ourselves great ones, all the more so for middling ones; nevertheless they cannot stand to have everyone fast, and they also at least fast on Tzom Gedaliah; if so, since they fasted on erev Rosh Hashana there is no need; see what I wrote in siman 576. It is written in the Shelah that the prayer leader and the one who sounds [the shofar] must separate themselves three days beforehand from anything that brings to impurity, and they should learn the intents of the prayers and the soundings; and this is from the Zohar.
