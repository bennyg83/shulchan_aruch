**** ENGLISH ****
The Laws Regarding An Interruption in the Torah Blessing. Containing 3 S'ifim: One who was reading from the Torah and became silent [i.e. became unable to continue], the one who stands as a replacement should start from the place where the first one began, and should bless before and after [reading]. And according to the Rambam, should not make the blessing before. {Rama: Gloss: And even nowadays where the prayer leader reads, the law is the same. (The R"i - chapter "Ein Omdin" [B'rakhot, Chapter 5)}
