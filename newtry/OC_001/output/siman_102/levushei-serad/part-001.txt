<small>(מ"א סק"א)</small> א"ש דגם כוונתו להתוס' אע"ג שפסקו דגם אחריו אסור מ"מ א"ש לפי שהם למדו מדכתיב עמכה מש"ה יכול להיות דגם מלאחריו מרחיק אבל הרא"ש דפסק ג"כ כהתו' ויליף מזה בגי' י"ב קשה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>(Magen Avraham, seif katan 1)</small> It is understandable that this is also his intent according to Tosafos. Although Tosafos rule that sitting behind him is also forbidden, their derivation from “with you” can indicate that one must distance himself behind him as well. The Rosh, however, rules like Tosafos yet derives the law from the numerical value twelve, and that is difficult.

**** HEBREW ****
<small>(שם)</small> ולהי"א לק"מ דס"ל דלפניו מרחיק כמלא עיניו ולא מיירי מלפניו מיהו הרא"ש לא ס"ל כך כמבואר מלשונו שהובא בט"ז סק"ב:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>(There)</small> According to the “there is one who says,” there is no difficulty, because he holds that before the person praying one must distance himself as far as the eye can see, and the numerical allusion does not discuss the area before him. The Rosh, however, does not hold this, as is clear from his wording cited by the Taz, seif katan 2.

**** HEBREW ****
<small>(ט"ז סק"ד)</small> זקנה שאני זה תמוה דהש"ס שם מיירי ביום שמת והיה בן צ"ח שנים כמבואר בפסוק דחנה היה ביום שנתמנה שופט והוא שפט את ישראל ארבעים שנה וא"כ לא היה אז רק בן נ"ח וכן הקשו המחברים וצ"ע:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>(Taz, seif katan 4)</small> “Old age is different.” This is astonishing, for the Gemara there discusses the day Eli died, when he was ninety-eight years old, as Scripture makes clear. The episode of Channah occurred on the day he was appointed judge, and he judged Israel for forty years. He was therefore only fifty-eight at that time. The later authorities likewise raise this difficulty, and it requires study.
