(Seif katan 2) When engaged in Torah, but contemplation does not suffice. The Beit Yosef infers this from the fact that Eli had to stand within four amos, although he was certainly contemplating words of Torah. Indeed, for this very reason the Sages say that a Torah scholar may not stand in a filthy place, since it is impossible for him to avoid at least contemplating Torah.
