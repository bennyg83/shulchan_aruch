<b>אפי' בכלי כו' ואפי'. </b>עתוס' שם כ"ד א' ד"ה והא כו' ואומר הרב כו' וכ"כ הר"י וע"כ דבאין אשתו עמו וכמו שמקשה תוס' ומ"מ כו' ומזה דייק נמי דבאין אשתו עמו ושלא כנגד ראשו א"צ כת"כ וז"ש אבל כו' ואם כו' ור"ל שהכר הוא כלי שני כמ"ש בתוס' שם ומ"מ כו' דלא כהרמב"ם:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Even within a vessel, etc.; and even.</b> See Tosafos there, 24a, s.v. “but was it not taught, etc.,” where the teacher says, etc. Rabbeinu Yonah writes likewise.

This must refer to a case where his wife is not with him, as Tosafos asks. Nevertheless, etc. From here he also infers that when his wife is not with him and the tefillin are not directly beneath his head, one vessel within another is unnecessary.

This is the meaning of “but, etc.; and if, etc.” The pillow is the second vessel, as Tosafos writes there. This is unlike Rambam.
