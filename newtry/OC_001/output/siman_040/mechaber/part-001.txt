**** ENGLISH ****
A room containing tefillin may not be used for marital relations until the tefillin are removed or placed inside one container within another, provided that the outer container is not designated for them. If it is designated for them, even one hundred such containers are considered like a single container.

<small>Rama: If neither container is designated for them, or if the inner container is not designated for them while the outer one is designated for them, it is permitted. See below, siman 240, seif 6.</small>

**** ENGLISH ****
Even when placed inside one container within another, it is forbidden to place tefillin beneath one's feet. It is likewise forbidden to place them beneath one's pillow directly opposite his head, even inside one container within another and even when his wife is not with him.

If they are not directly opposite his head, then when his wife is not with him it is permitted. If his wife is with him, one container within another is required.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ח" data-order="8"></i>שכח ושמש מטתו בתפילין לא יאחוז לא בבתים ולא ברצועה <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="5"></i><i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="יד"></i>עד שיטול <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="9"></i>ידיו מפני שהידים עסקניות הן ושמא נגעו במקום הטנופת:

**** ENGLISH ****
One who enters to eat a fixed meal should remove his tefillin and place them upon the table until the time for the blessing after the meal, and then put them on again.

For an incidental snack, he need not remove them.
