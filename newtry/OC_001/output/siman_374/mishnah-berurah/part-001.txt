(ה) And even if he sealed it intentionally — meaning intentionally. And know that Magen Avraham and many later authorities sided to forbid here when it reopened on Shabbat, for since he sealed the opening between the two courtyards intentionally it is like nullifying the eruv by hand — he revealed he does not want an eruv; if so, at bein hashemashot when it was still sealed it does not help when it opens later on Shabbat itself; we are not lenient when sealed intentionally except when it opened before Shabbat — then we say even if nullified initially, when it reopened they are still partners in the eruv bread and at bein hashemashot there is an opening fit for joining; see Biur Halacha where we explained practice should follow this. However if when sealing he intended only temporarily and afterward to reopen, it is reasonable to permit even when it reopened on Shabbat:
