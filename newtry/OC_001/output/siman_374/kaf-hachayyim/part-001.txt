<b>1) [Se'if 1] They established an eruv through a window, etc. </b>This refers to two houses or two courtyards whose residents established an eruv together by means of a window or entrance between them. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1.

<b>ג) שם. ונסתם בשבת וכו'  </b>אבל אם נסתם מבע"י אעפ"י שחזר ונפתח בשכת אסורים לפי שעדיין לא קנה עירוב מעולם דקניית העירוב הוא ביה"ש וכאן לא היה עירוב ביה"ש כיון שהפתח סתום ומשחשיכה לא קנה עירוב שאין מערבין משתחשך ואפי' עירבו כבר לפני שבת שמברך ואותו עירוב נשאר עדיין קיים אינם יכולין לסמוך עליו בשבת זו אחר שנפתח הפתח הואיל וכבר נתבטל מבע"י ואינו חוזר וניעור משתחשך ר"ז שם:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>3) There. And it became blocked on Shabbos, etc. </b>If it became blocked before the onset of Shabbos, however, they are forbidden to carry even if it reopened on Shabbos, because the eruv never took effect. An eruv takes effect during bein hashmashos, but here there was no eruv during bein hashmashos because the entrance was blocked. Once night fell, the eruv could not take effect, since an eruv may not be established after nightfall. Even if they had already established an eruv before Shabbos and that eruv remained intact, they cannot rely upon it on this Shabbos after the entrance reopened. Since it had already been nullified before the onset of Shabbos, it does not revive after nightfall. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, there.
