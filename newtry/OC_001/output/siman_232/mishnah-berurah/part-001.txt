**** ENGLISH ****
(5) A person should not sit etc. — because this seif has many details I must extend somewhat. This entire seif regarding eating, haircutting, entering bathhouse and other matters mentioned here is mishnah first chapter of Shabbat forbidding close to Mincha; and the mishnah did not clarify which Mincha — Mincha Gedolah [from six and a half hours and up, and close to it is half an hour before, meaning right after chatzot] or Mincha Ketanah [from nine and a half hours and up, and close to this is from beginning of tenth hour]. Also the mishnah did not clarify what it forbade regarding eating — only a large meal such as wedding feast, then forbidden lest Mincha time pass; or even a small ordinary meal forbidden lest they prolong [and similarly one must be uncertain regarding other matters in the mishnah — entering bathhouse: only the whole bathhouse matter of washing head, bathing hot and cold, sweating, or even casual sweating forbidden lest he faint from bath heat; and regarding tannery: only large tanning or even casual inspection; and similarly other matters as in this seif]. And there in gemara are two answers — according to both the mishnah deals with Mincha Gedolah. First answer: mishnah's intent forbidding close to Mincha Gedolah deals only with large meal [but small meal permitted until close to Mincha Ketanah; and some further lenient in this answer that even close to Mincha Ketanah small meal may begin]. Second answer: mishnah's intent to forbid even small meal close to Mincha Gedolah. Poskim disagreed — Rif and Rambam ruled like second answer, and Mechaber ruled like them; and Rama at beginning of his words ruled like first answer — this is the essence of this seif; and now we begin explaining the seif's words.
