**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(s.k. 2) But, etc.</b> Even, etc., because the dish, etc. Know that on folio 17a it was taught: A woman fills a pot with meat even though she needs only one piece; a baker fills casks of water, etc.; but regarding baking he bakes only what he needs. R' Shimon ben Elazar says: A woman fills an oven with bread even though she needs only one loaf, because the bread is baked well when the oven is full. Rava said: The law is like R' Shimon ben Elazar. And Rashi explained the reason for the first tanna who permits a woman to fill a pot, etc.: because one effort suffices. And likewise with a baker regarding filling; end quote. And Beis Yosef understood from Rashi's view that with a pot filling the pot is not considered an improvement; for specifically regarding an oven, where it is baked well, it is considered an improvement; and therefore he needed the reason of one effort. And the Rashba, and likewise the Tur, wrote the reason also for filling the pot as improvement of the dish. And Beis Yosef wrote that there is a practical difference between the two reasons: according to Rashi, if he already set the pot on the fire, it is forbidden to add, for there is extra effort.

And the wording appears that he did examine there, for he wrote, "and see in siman 681." And regarding that he forbade circumvention in slaughter, the reason is that the slaughter was after minchah and after the meal; certainly circumvention is forbidden, as the Shulchan Aruch also wrote.

<b>ומ"ש הג"מ דזה מיקרי הערמה.</b> היינו אפילו באמת צריך לפת א' ב' וג' מ"מ מיקרי הערמה. דהא יכול ליקח אותו פת שצריך לו הכל מתנור א'. וכ"כ הט"ז לדעת האוסרים הערמה אפילו צריך לקצת אסור לבשל שתי קדירות ממין א' ולאכול מכל א' קצת דהא יוכל לאכול הכל מקדירה א' וא"צ לשתי קדירות:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And what Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote, that this is called circumvention.</b> That is even if in truth he needs one loaf [for] two and three: nevertheless it is called circumvention, for he can take that bread that he needs entirely from one oven. And so wrote the Taz according to those who forbid circumvention: even if he needs a little, it is forbidden to cook two pots of one kind and eat a little from each, for he can eat everything from one pot and does not need two pots.
