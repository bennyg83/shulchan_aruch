**** ENGLISH ****
Regarding the Kaddish that orphans say: per the Arizal z"l, specifically the final Kaddish that they say before Aleinu l'shabe'ach benefits the soul of the deceased—as he wrote in Sha'ar HaKavanot in the discourse on Kaddish, and thus: "However, the final Kaddish is not among the Kaddishim, for all the worlds have already ascended; nevertheless this Kaddish is called Kaddish Yatom—that is, the Kaddish that orphans say. The matter is that this Kaddish is in the world of Asiyah, which is in the aspect of death, in order to raise all souls and spirits in the secret of resurrection of the dead; therefore orphans say it for the death of father and mother, etc." (And see above letter 1 in the name of Tikkunei Zohar.) I heard from my teacher z"l that it is good to say this Kaddish for the death of one's father and mother all year long, even on Shabbatot and festivals—for the reason is not as the multitude of people think, that it only helps to save the soul of the deceased from judgment of Gehinnom; for behold there is another benefit, namely to bring him into Gan Eden and to raise him from level to level; and accordingly one may say it also on Shabbatot and festivals. And I saw my teacher z"l, who would say the final Kaddish every leap year on the day his father died, in all three prayers, etc. It follows from the words of the rabbi z"l that the Kaddish that benefits the soul of the deceased is specifically the final Kaddish. And here in our holy city Jerusalem may it be built, some have the custom that orphans also say the Kaddish before Hodu and the Kaddish before Kaddish Yatom, and all other Kaddishim the chazzan alone says; and it seems to me the reason they practiced to say also these two Kaddishim is because they are in the aspect of ascent of the world of Asiyah, which is in the aspect of nefesh in the world of Yetzirah—and it is possible that they also benefit the soul of the deceased.
