Therefore, if there are nine [men] in the larger and one in the smaller, they join together because the smaller is "dragged" after the larger, and it is as if it is within the big one since the majority [of the men] are in the big one. But if there were nine [men] in the small and one in the large or five [men] in this one and five in that one, they do not join.
