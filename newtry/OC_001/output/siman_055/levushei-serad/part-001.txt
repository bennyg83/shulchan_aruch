Therefore, if they began the Shemoneh Esrei prayer on Motzaei Shabbos with ten and some left, he completes the order of Kedushah and the Kaddish, and so wrote the Peri Megadim, of blessed memory.

**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Rama, seif 10.</small> He does not become. Although the Mechaber already wrote this in seif 9, nevertheless one might have thought that this applies only when he was born in another month, for example on Rosh Chodesh Nisan, and the thirteenth year is a leap year, that he does not become bar mitzvah until Rosh Chodesh Nisan. But if he was born on Rosh Chodesh Adar, one might have thought that he becomes bar mitzvah on Rosh Chodesh of the first Adar. Therefore it teaches that the first Adar is not called Adar for this purpose, but rather Shevat. So the Peri Megadim, of blessed memory, explained.

<small>ש"ע סט"ז</small> אם תשעה בגדולה. הכלל דהגדולה א"א לה לימשך אחר הקטנה לפי שהוא כמופלגת. וגם הצבור דחשיבי א"א להם לימשך אחר היחיד ואפי' חמשה אחר חמשה א"א להם לימשך רק היחיד נמשך אחר הצבור מש"ה א"א שיצרפו אא"כ היחיד בקטנה והצבור בגדולה ובסעיף זה קמ"ל דאפי' ש"ץ לא עדיף להא מלתא מיחיד ואין הצבור נמשכין אחריו ודינו כיחיד ממש:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Shulchan Aruch, seif 16.</small> If nine are in the large one. The rule is that the large one cannot be drawn after the small one, because it is as though separate. Also, the congregation, being significant, cannot be drawn after the individual, and even five after five cannot be drawn; only the individual is drawn after the congregation. Therefore they cannot combine unless the individual is in the small one and the congregation is in the large one. In this seif it teaches that even a shaliach tzibbur is not preferable to an individual for this matter, and the congregation is not drawn after him; his law is exactly like that of an individual.

**** HEBREW ****
<small>ט"ז סק"ו</small> יצטרכו ר"ל דעיקר הדין שישוב שאין שם מנין כופין זא"ז להשכיר להשלים מנין הוא כדי שלא יצטרכו לצאת מבתיהם לעזוב ממונם ומצד דין זה נמשך הדין דכאן שאם הם י"א ורוצים שנים לצאת צריכים הם להשכיר להשלים כיון דעל ידיהם נחסר המנין. וא"כ סוף דינא כהתחלת דינא דיש סברא שהשכירות לתשלום מנין לפי הממון וק"ל:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Taz, seif katan 6.</small> They will need. Meaning that the essential law, that a settlement without a minyan may compel one another to hire someone to complete the minyan, is so that they will not need to leave their homes and abandon their money. From this law follows the law here, that if they are eleven and two wish to leave, they must hire someone to complete the minyan, since through them the minyan is lacking. If so, the end of the law is like its beginning, and there is reason to say that payment for completing a minyan is according to wealth. This is simple.
