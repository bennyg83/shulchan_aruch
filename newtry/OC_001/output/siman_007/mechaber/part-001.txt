**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The law of blessing <i>Asher Yatzar</i> all day after urination — it contains four clauses.</b><br>All day when one relieves oneself, whether minor or major matters, one blesses <i>Asher Yatzar</i> and not on hand-washing, even if one wishes to study or pray immediately. Rema: If his hands were soiled such that he rubbed them [clean], he does not bless on hand-washing (Semag siman 27, 49).

**** HEBREW ****
<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ו" data-order="6"></i>אין שיעור להשתין מים כי אפילו לטפה אחת חייב לברך שאם יסתם הנקב מלהוציא הטפה ההיא היה קשה לו וחייב להודות:
**** ENGLISH ****
There is no minimum measure for urinating: even for one drop one is obligated to bless, for if the opening were blocked from expelling that drop it would be difficult for him and he is obligated to give thanks.
