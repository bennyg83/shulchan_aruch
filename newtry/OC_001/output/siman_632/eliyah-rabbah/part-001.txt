**** ENGLISH ****
[2] <b>For we say bent wall, etc.</b> Even if the wall is only ten handbreadths high and the roof is much higher than it, we view the wall as though it rises upward, and we also say "bent wall" [Tur]. Although the Bach, whom the Magen Avraham brings, did not rule this way, nevertheless the Turei Zahav discussed it at length and agreed with the Tur. If one placed a board four handbreadths wide at the mouth of the sukkah near the fourth side, where there is no wall, even though he inserted only three handbreadths into the sukkah, it is invalid; and this is specifically in a small sukkah. See Magen Avraham.
