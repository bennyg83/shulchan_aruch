**** ENGLISH ****
<b>1. The custom concerning the lines, etc.</b> This follows the received teaching of the Arizal in Shaar HaKavanos, discourse 1 concerning tefillin. Regarding the division of the line-openings, see Tur and Beis Yosef. We shall record the division written by the Arizal there, for this is our custom. In the head tefillin: The four lines of Kadesh begin Vayedaber, Mizeh, Bachodesh, and Betzeisi. The four lines of Vehayah Ki Yeviacha begin Vehayah, Vechol Peter, Hashem MiMitzrayim, and Peter Rechem. The four lines of Shema begin Shema, Nafshecha, Levanecha, and Le'os. The four lines of Vehayah Im Shamoa begin Vehayah, Venasati, Yevulah, and Beshivtecha. In the arm tefillin: The seven lines of Kadesh begin Vayedaber, Es Hayom, Bachodesh, Zavas, Es Shivas, Hashem Li, and Hashem MiMitzrayim. The seven lines of Vehayah Ki Yeviacha are listed as Vehayah, Rechem, Ve'im Lo, Zos Leshalchenu, LaHashem Kol, and Einecha. The seven lines of Shema begin Shema, Es, Me'odecha, Hayom, Beveisecha, Le'os, and Al Mezuzos. The seven lines of Vehayah Im Shamoa begin Vehayah, Uvechol Nafshechem, Ve'achalta, Hashem Bachem, Asher Hashem, Bein Eineichem, and Beisecha.

<b>A person must be careful</b> when writing the Divine Name not to interrupt, whether in thought, speech, action, or deed. Such an interruption causes a blemish above and below, in accordance with the intentions mentioned in the laws of tefillin, separating and cutting the plantings, Heaven forbid, for everything is connected like a flame bound to a coal.

See Matzas Shmurim in the laws of writing, and also my sacred work Kaf HaChayim, siman 98, letter 3. Examine this carefully.

Open for Your servant, the son of Your maidservant, the gates of forgiveness from Your goodness. {Heh; Yod Vav Dalet; Heh Alef; Vav Alef Vav; Heh Alef; Alef Heh Yod Heh. Interweaving: Alef Yod Heh Heh Yod Vav Heh Heh.}

Before the passage Vehayah Ki Yeviacha, he should intend toward the first heh of the simple Name Havayah, the Name MaH, the Name 143, and the interweaving of 143 and MaH, as shown.

Afterward, in every line, he should intend toward the forty-two-letter expansion of SaG, the simple heh, six letters from the forty-two-letter expansion of SaG, and three letters from the interweaving of 143 and MaH. He should first join the first heh of the simple Name Havayah with them, as depicted before you:
