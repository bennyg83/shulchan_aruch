Biur HaGra, s.v. v'yesh omrim d'im, compares this to the present opinion concerning a Chanukah lamp, where use is permitted only if a valid lamp stands beside it, not in another room; he therefore understands like Eliyah Rabbah. Korban Nesanel is likewise stringent when it stands in another room.
