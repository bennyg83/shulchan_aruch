<b>From the place where the odor ceases, etc.</b> This is the dispute of Rav Huna and Rav Chisda. The Semag wrote that the halacha follows Rav Chisda, for a baraisa supports him. Let him derive it from the fact that it is the stringent view, as he himself ruled in the law of excrement on one's flesh above, siman 76, seif 4, which is also a dispute of Rav Huna and Rav Chisda.

[ב] <b>ארבע אמות וכו'.</b> עיין בלבוש טעמא דארבע אמות קונות לו בכל מקום והוי מחנהו וכתיב והיה מחניך קדוש, ובמעדני מלך ונחלת צבי הבינו דהלבוש כתב טעם זה מנפשיה עיין שם, ואני מצאתיו להדיא בחידושי רשב"א דף כ' דכל שהוא חוץ לארבע אמות אינה מחנהו:

<b>Four amos, etc.</b> See the Levush, who gives the reason that four amos acquire for a person in every place, and it is considered his camp, and it is written, Your camp shall be holy. In Ma'adanei Melech and Nachalas Tzvi they understood that the Levush wrote this reason on his own; see there. I found it explicitly in the Chiddushei Rashba, page 20, that anything outside four amos is not his camp.

[ג] <b>ומלפניו וכו'.</b> בכיסוי תלה רחמנא לבוש, קצת קשה דלענין זה תליא רחמנא בכיסוי דאם כיסהו אף שהוא תוך ארבע אמותיו מותר אבל שיהא אסור חוץ לארבע אמות לפניו אם לא כיסהו מנלן, שוב ראיתי בחידושים שם שכתב דמשום לא יראה בך ערות דבר אסור לפניו כמלא עיניו אף דקרא קאי על ערוה ממש, מכל מקום מדערבינהו רחמנא וכתבינהו קאי נמי לא יראה בך על צואה וכן אפרש דברי לבוש גופיה סעיף ב' ואפשר לדחוק ולפרש שגם כאן כוונתו כן:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And in front of him, etc.</b> The Levush writes that the Torah made it depend on covering. This is somewhat difficult: regarding this, the Torah made it depend on covering, so that if he covered it, even within four amos it is permitted. But from where do we know that outside four amos in front of him it is forbidden if he did not cover it? I then saw in the Chiddushim there that he wrote that because of There shall not be seen among you an indecent matter it is forbidden in front of him as far as his eyes can see. Although the verse refers to actual nakedness, nevertheless, since the Torah combined them and wrote them together, There shall not be seen also applies to excrement. I will explain the words of the Levush himself in seif 2 this way as well, and it is possible to force this explanation here too, that this was also his intent.

[ד] <b>בלילה או סומא וכו'.</b> כתב עולת תמיד זה לשונו, ואין להקשות דהא לעיל בסוף סימן ע"ה פסקו דאם עוצם עיניו דמותר לקרות, דהתם בראייה תלה רחמנא, אבל הכא במחנה קדוש תלה, והרי אין המחנה קדוש כיון שאין שם מחיצה, עד כאן. וכן משמע בבית יוסף סוף סימן ע"ה. ותמיהני דלפי דברי רשב"א שכתבתי מבואר דלפניו וחוץ לארבע אמות אין איסור משום מחנה קדוש כלל, אלא משום לא יראה בך. וזה סייעתא לפסק הב"ח שכתבתי שם דגם בערוה אסור בלילה ועוצם עיניו. כתב הט"ז אפילו שאר דברי תורה אסור אפילו במקום שספק אם יש שם צואה כגון לפני בתים וחצר. כתב הב"ח סימן פ"ו דהרחק מי רגלים דינו כמו צואה:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>At night or a blind person, etc.</b> The Olas Tamid wrote as follows: One should not ask from above, at the end of siman 75, where they ruled that if one closes his eyes it is permitted to read, for there the Torah made it depend on sight, but here it made it depend on a holy camp, and the camp is not holy since there is no partition there. Thus far. So it also appears from the Beit Yosef at the end of siman 75. I am astonished, for according to the words of the Rashba that I wrote, it is clear that in front of him and outside four amos there is no prohibition at all because of Your camp shall be holy, but because of There shall not be seen among you. This supports the ruling of the Bach that I cited there, that even regarding nakedness it is forbidden at night or when closing the eyes. The Taz wrote that even other words of Torah are forbidden, even in a place where there is doubt whether there is excrement, such as in front of houses and courtyards. The Bach wrote in siman 86 that the distancing from urine has the same law as excrement.

[ה] <b>דינו כמו מלאחריו.</b> כתב בכסף משנה בשם הרבינו מנוח דדוקא בדלא אפשר לאחריו, אבל בדאפשר כלפניו דמי, עד כאן. ומסתימת כל הפוסקים לא משמע הכי, גם מלשון כמלא עיניו משמע דדוקא לפניו דרואהו הוא בעינן כל כך. ונראה שמזה למדו הפוסקים, וכן משמע בפירוש המשנה לרמב"ם, ואף דלקמן סימן פ"א סעיף ב' קיימא לן דוקא בדאי אפשר ילך לצדדין, נראה דדוקא התם שעומד והצואה כנגדו, אבל כשעומד והצואה לצדדיו אין צריך להרחיק ארבע אמות אלא לצדדין, כן נראה לי. כתב מגן אברהם ובצדדין שלפניו כלפניו דמי כמו בנשיאת כפים סימן קכ"ח סעיף כ"ח, עד כאן. ולי נראה דלא דמי לנשיאת כפים דהתם טעמא דאחורי הכהנים אינן בכלל ברכה משום דמראין בעצמן כאילו אין הברכה חשובה בעיניהם כמו שכתבו תוס' סוטה דף ל"ח ד"ה מחיצה וכו', לכן צדדין הוי כלפניו דאכתי חשובה בעיניו כיון שאינו עומד אחריו, מה שאין כן הכא דטעמא משום לא יראה נראה דכל שאינו רואה בהשקפה בלא מכוין לא הוי כלפניו, ותדע דהא צידי הכהנים ממש הוי כלפניו והכא הוי כאחוריו אלא כדפירשתי:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Its law is like behind him.</b> The Kesef Mishneh wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach that this is specifically where it is impossible to go behind him, but where it is possible, it is like in front of him. Thus far. From the plain wording of all the poskim it does not appear so. Also, from the expression as far as his eyes can see, it implies that specifically in front of him, where he sees it, do we require that much. It appears that the poskim learned from this, and so it appears in the Rambam's commentary to the Mishnah. Although below, siman 81, seif 2, we rule that specifically where it is impossible he goes to the sides, it appears that this applies specifically there, where he is standing and the excrement is opposite him. But when he is standing and the excrement is to his sides, he need distance only four amos to the sides. So it appears to me. The Magen Avraham wrote: the sides that are in front of him are like in front of him, as with Nesias Kapayim in siman 128, seif 28. Thus far. To me it appears that this is not comparable to Nesias Kapayim. There the reason is that those behind the kohanim are not included in the blessing because they show themselves as though the blessing is not important to them, as Tosafot in Sotah 38, s.v. mechitzah, wrote. Therefore the sides are like in front of him, for the blessing is still important in his eyes since he is not standing behind him. This is not so here, where the reason is because of sight. It appears that anything he does not see by looking naturally, without deliberately turning, is not considered in front of him. Know this, for the actual sides of the kohanim are like in front of them, whereas here they are like behind him; rather, as I explained.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And he sees it, etc.</b> Berachos 25: if there was a place ten tefachim high or ten tefachim low, he sits at its side and reads Krias Shema, and if not, he distances himself as far as his eyes can see. The Rosh explained that it speaks even when he sees it. Nachalas Tzvi wrote that his reason is that otherwise, why did it need to teach, and if not, etc.? For if he sees it, even at a height of ten tefachim he distances himself as far as his eyes can see. Regarding the Rashba, it appears to my humble opinion that he holds that it needed to teach, and if not, etc., in order to inform us that where it is ten high and he does not see it, he need not distance four amos. Thus far. His words are astonishing and very forced to explain. How is this heard from, and if not, etc.? Furthermore, it already taught, he sits at its side and reads. Also, he overlooked the Rashba's words in his Chiddushim, where he explains the Gemara: here it says that if it is ten high, he sits at its side and reads, since it is not actually in his place and he does not see it, because the height or depth of the place causes him not to see it. But if it is opposite him, even though there is another cause preventing him from seeing it, whenever it is in a place where he could see it, he should not read until he distances from it as far as his eyes can see, since it is not another domain. This is explained even more clearly in Responsa Rashba, siman 473. Also the Beit Yosef, with all due respect, did not reach the depth of the Rashba's words in this, for he rejected his words from what is stated in seif 3, that it is permitted with excrement in the smaller room, which necessarily speaks where he sees it, for if not, even if it is in the same room it is permitted, and there is no proof, as explained.
