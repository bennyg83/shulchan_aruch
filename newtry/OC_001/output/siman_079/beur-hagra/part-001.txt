<b>להרא"ש. </b>ממ"ש היתה גבוה כו' ואם לאו מרחיק מלא עיניו כו' אלמא דברואה עסקינן ואפ"ה בגבוה כו' מותר ועוד דצואה לא תלי רחמנא בראיה כמש"ש והרשב"א ס"ל דמיירי שיש סיבה המונע מראות אותה ובשאינו גבוה מ"מ אסור וכמ"ש בס"א:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>According to the Rosh.</b> This follows from his statement, “If it was ten handbreadths high,” and so forth, “but otherwise he must distance himself as far as he can see,” and so forth. It is therefore evident that the case concerns one who sees it, yet when it is elevated, and so forth, it is permitted. Furthermore, the Torah did not make the law of excrement dependent upon sight, as stated there. The Rashba, however, maintains that the case concerns one in which there is some cause preventing him from seeing it, while if it is not elevated it is nevertheless forbidden, as stated in seif 1.

<b>ואם יש כו'. </b>ממש"ש ריח רע שיש לו עיקר כו' וברישא דברייתא חשיב צואה ודכוותיה שמרחיק ממנו ולכאורה היינו רישא ועוד דשם קאמר כמלא עיניו וכאן רק ד"א ועוד מאי ריח רע כו' מרחיק ממנו כו' משמע מן הריח ול"ק מן הדבר בעצמו עד שיכלה הריח אע"כ דמיירי בכה"ג שממנו עצמו א"צ להתרחק כגון שהוא בבית אחר או שהוא מכוסה וכמ"ש הרשב"א וא"צ להרחיק אלא מפני הריח וכן סתם בס"ג ובסי' פ"ג ס"א וס"ד:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And if there is, and so forth.</b> This is derived from the statement there concerning a foul odor that has a source, and so forth. Yet the first clause of the baraisa already lists excrement and things like it, from which one must distance himself. At first glance, this would be identical to the first clause. Moreover, there it says, “as far as he can see,” whereas here it requires only four amos. Also, the phrase, “a foul odor, and so forth, one must distance himself from it,” implies distance from the odor, and it does not say that one must distance himself from the substance itself until the odor ceases. It must therefore refer to a case in which he need not distance himself from the substance itself, such as when it is in another house or is covered, as the Rashba writes, and he must distance himself only because of the odor. The Mechaber likewise rules without qualification in seif 3 and in siman 83, seifim 1 and 4.
