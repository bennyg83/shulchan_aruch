**** HEBREW ****
<small>מ"א סק"א</small> עססי' ל"ח. דמבואר שם דתפילין קודמין למזוז' משום דהא חובת הגוף והרי דבלא"ה קודמין דלא להוי פושעי ישראל בגופו אע"כ דהנמנע מאיזה טעם אינו פושעי ישראל בגופו והתם נמי נמנע מחמת שאין לו על שניהם וה"א מזוזה קודמת אי לאו טעמא דחובת הגוף:
**** ENGLISH ****
<small>Magen Avraham, seif katan 1.</small> See the end of siman 38. It is explained there that tefillin take precedence over mezuzah because tefillin are an obligation upon the body.

This shows that without that reason tefillin would not necessarily take precedence merely to avoid becoming one of the sinners of Israel with their bodies. It must therefore be that one who refrains for some reason is not included among those sinners.

In that case as well, he refrains only because he lacks the means for both mitzvos. One might have thought mezuzah should take precedence were it not for the reason that tefillin are an obligation upon the body.
