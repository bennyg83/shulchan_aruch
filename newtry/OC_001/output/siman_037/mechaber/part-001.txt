<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ג" data-order="3"></i>מצותן <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ה"></i>להיותם עליו כל היום אבל מפני שצריכי' גוף נקי שלא יפיח בהם ושלא יסיח דעתו מהם ואין כל אדם יכול ליזהר בהם <i data-commentator="Magen Avraham" data-order="2"></i>נהגו <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ד" data-order="4"></i>שלא להניחם כל <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Sha'arei Teshuvah" data-order="3"></i>היום ומ"מ צריך כל אדם ליזהר בהם להיותם עליו <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ו"></i>בשעת ק"ש <i data-commentator="Mishnah Berurah" data-label="ז"></i>ותפלה:
**** ENGLISH ****
It is a Mitzvah to have them [tefillin] on all day, but because they [tefillin] need a clean body [meaning that] he does not pass gas, and [further require] that one not distract his mind from them, and not every person is able to be careful with them, the practice is not to wear them all day. Nevertheless every man needs to be careful with them that they should be on him during the reciting of Shema and Tefilah [aka Shemoneh Esreh].
