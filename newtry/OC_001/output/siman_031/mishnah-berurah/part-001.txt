**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(5) It would be a degradation.</b> He also violates the prohibition of bal tosif when he puts them on for the sake of the mitzvah. If he puts them on not for the sake of the mitzvah, he does not violate bal tosif, nor is there degradation, unless he puts them on publicly, in which case it is forbidden D'Rabbanan. Some are stringent in every case, unless the tefillin are being treated disrespectfully, such as where one finds tefillin in a field on Shabbos. In that case he may put them on and bring them into the city in the manner of clothing, as will be explained below in siman 301.
