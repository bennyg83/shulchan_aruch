<b>ס"ג אין מוציאין. </b>כמ"ש בספ"ג פרתו כו' ומקרדין כו' בי"ט מ' דבי"ט קאי אשניהם ועוד דכל הג' דברים דפליגי ראב"י וחכמים הכל בי"ט דלא כתי"ט ואמרו בירושלמי פ"ד די"ט והביאו הרי"ף ורא"ש שם אין רוכבין כו' בי"ט משום שביתת בהמתו אלמא דמצווה בי"ט ובבהמה לא אמרינן מתוך דלא שייך לצורך:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Se'if 3: One does not carry out.</b> As is written at the end of chapter 3: "his cow" etc., and "they card" etc. on Yom Tov: it appears that on Yom Tov it refers to both of them. And further, all three matters in which Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov and the Sages disagree are all regarding Yom Tov, not like Tosefos Yom Tov. And they said in the Yerushalmi, chapter 4 of Yom Tov [Beitza], and the Rif and Rosh brought it there: one may not ride etc. on Yom Tov because of resting one's animal; hence he is commanded [regarding his animal] on Yom Tov. And regarding an animal we do not say "mitoch" [since carrying was permitted for need], for [animal rest] does not relate to [personal] need.

**** ENGLISH ****
<b>And some permit, etc.</b> From what is written at the end of Shabbos: "And even Levi held" etc., like that of Levi when he would bring a [case of an animal found to be] tereifah; and he brings a proof from Yom Tov to Shabbos. And there: "And even R' Yehudah held" etc.; "And did R' Yehudah say so? But R' Yehudah said the law is like an anonymous Mishnah, and we learned: 'One may not'" etc. R' Yehudah [taught] anonymously etc., and he does not distinguish between Shabbos and Yom Tov; rather Rav Nachman alone distinguishes, to uphold his [own] resolution of the sugya; and we hold like the other resolutions there. And further, from the challenge in the chapter of the stove [Shabbos]: "And does Rabbi hold like R' Shimon" etc.; and if it were so, then Rabbi ruled anonymously on Shabbos like R' Shimon and on Yom Tov like R' Yehudah; rather we do not hold so.
