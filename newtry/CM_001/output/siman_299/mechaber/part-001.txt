**** ENGLISH ****
For years, one hand and one of them came to sue. One of them is a sign of Hashem’s people, and one of them shall not be given to him, until he is brought in the city, but if he is in the city and knows that he is in the city, and that he is not in the city of Israel, and that he is not in charge of his friends, and his mission is to be blessed with him, and that he will be given to him
