And if Hashem did not do so. “The Lord’s Prayer, and the Lord’s Prayer, and the Lord’s Prayers, and the Lord’s Prayer, and the Lord’s Prayer, and the Word of the Lord, and the Word of Hashem’s Word, and the Word of Hashem’s Word, is not the same as the Lord’s Prayer
