(א) She produced the get, etc. Ran wrote that this is specifically a get, but not get witnesses, because there is a weakness before us: the get is not in her hand, which indicates that she already produced the get and he paid her. Tosafot do not imply this. It appears that this law depends on the above dispute whether she collects without a ketubah in a place where ketubot are written. Tosafot follow their view, that she collects even in a place where they write ketubot and there is a weakness before us; nevertheless he is not believed to say, "I paid." So too here, he is not believed when the get is not in her hand. But according to Rif's approach, since there is a weakness, she is not believed. One should not ask against the Tur, who also holds that in a place where ketubot are written she does not collect without a ketubah, and the reason must be because there is a weakness that the ketubah is not in her hand. If so, why does he hold here that she collects with get witnesses?
