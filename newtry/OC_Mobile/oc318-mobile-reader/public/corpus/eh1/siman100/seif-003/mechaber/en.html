If unencumbered land is found — she collects from it. If not — she seizes from land the husband sold or gave, whether healthy gift or deathbed gift; and even if she consented to what he sold or gave — it does not help unless they acquired from her first. And if he sold land to one person and she did not consent to him, and afterward sold that land or other land to another and she consented and signed for him — she does not collect from him. And from the first — some say she collects; and some say she does not collect (and see above siman 90 seif 17).
