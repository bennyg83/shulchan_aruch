(ו) Even though her ketubah is in her hand and is not validated. In Maharik, shoresh 19, it is explained that although the poskim disagree whether she collects without a ketubah, they disagree only when the ketubah is not in her hand, for then there is a weakness. But if the ketubah is in her hand and merely is not validated, and from that time validation witnesses have not been found, all the poskim agree that she is believed, since there is no weakness. Where there is no clear custom, then even if she has the ketubah in her hand and it is not validated, she does not collect. So too Rama's words should be explained.
