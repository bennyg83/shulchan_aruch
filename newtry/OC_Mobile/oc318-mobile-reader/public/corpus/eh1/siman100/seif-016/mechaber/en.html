She produced a get and ketubah after the husband's death — if the get preceded the ketubah — she collects principal ketubah with this get if their custom is not to write ketubah. (And with the ketubah) she collects all that is in this ketubah, since she acquired it at his death. And if the ketubah preceded the get — she has only one ketubah that she returned based on her first ketubah.
