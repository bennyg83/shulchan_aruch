She produced two ketubot and one get — she collects only one ketubah. Which one? If both are equal — the later cancels the first and she seizes only from the time of the later. And if one of the two had tosefet beyond its counterpart and he did not write for her "and I add for you such-and-such over the first" — if she wishes to collect the first — she collects from first time, or second from second time. And if he wrote "and I added for you such-and-such over the first" — she collects the first from first time and the tosefet from second time. When does this apply? When both are from betrothal or both from marriage. But if one from betrothal and the second from marriage — in every case she collects only the one from marriage.
