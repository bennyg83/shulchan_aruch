She came and said "my husband died; permit me to remarry and give me my ketubah" — they permit her to remarry and give her ketubah. But if she came and said "my husband died — give me my ketubah and permit me to remarry" — they permit her and do not give ketubah. And if she seized — we do not remove from her hand. And there are those who say in both this and that — they do not permit her once she mentioned ketubah.
