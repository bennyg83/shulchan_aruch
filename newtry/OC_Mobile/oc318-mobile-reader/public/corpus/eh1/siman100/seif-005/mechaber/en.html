If he wrote in her ketubah for principal ketubah and tosefet "unspecified coins" — she collects only the least valuable of coins. How? He married a woman in one place and divorced her in another — (the woman was with him there; but if he sent her a get to the place of marriage — they follow the place of marriage (Beit Yosef in name of responsum Rashba)) If the coins of the place of marriage are better than coins of the place of divorce — he gives her from coins of the place of divorce. And if coins of the place of divorce are better — he gives from coins of the place of marriage. And this is specifically regarding coin equivalence when he collects for her two hundred and a maneh; but less than this — no. And if he specified in her ketubah a known coin — whether in principal or tosefet — she collects it as written in the ketubah. {Rama: And tzon barzel property always follows the place where it was liened, like an ordinary debt (so writes Maggid Mishneh ch. 16 and so Rashba). And in a place where they add a third to what is given to him as explained above siman 66 — it has the law of tzon barzel and not tosefet (Maharik root 81).}
