(ב) That she claims her ketubah. Meaning: although we hold that a woman who says to her husband, "You divorced me," is believed, because of the chazakah that she would not be brazen in his presence, nevertheless here, where she claims her ketubah, she is not believed. This is the view of the Tur according to Tosafot. The first view is Rambam's view: he holds that she collects at least the principal by force of the condition written in the ketubah, "when you marry another you will take your ketubah." But regarding the additional ketubah there is no condition of beit din, for it depends on the husband's will.
