A woman who produced a ketubah document and has no get with it and said to her husband "you divorced me and my get was lost — give me my ketubah" and he says "I did not divorce you" — he must give her principal ketubah but does not give tosefet until she brings proof he divorced her or that a get emerged with the ketubah from her hand. (Some say if she does not bring proof of divorce — even principal ketubah he does not give her, since once she claims ketubah she is not believed about divorce, as explained above siman 17 seif 2; and therefore she does not take ketubah) (Tur and this is primary).
