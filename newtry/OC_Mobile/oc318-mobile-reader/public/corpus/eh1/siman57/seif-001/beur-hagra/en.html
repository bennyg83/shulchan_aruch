The husband precedes, etc. From the Mishnah in Bava Batra 118a. And so is the halachah.
And some say, etc. Tosafot 47b; and he wrote that the baraita here means she, and as explained.
And likewise if she was an adult, etc. This is what the Mishnah says there: always she, etc.; and specifically when handed over, etc.
And some say, etc. Gemara there; and the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch wrote: seemingly the precise formulations contradict each other — at first he said specifically his, and at the end specifically hers. And he wrote: everything follows his — the matter proceeds in a courtyard not his; but all the more in a courtyard of both; only to exclude a courtyard belonging to neither. See Ritva.
