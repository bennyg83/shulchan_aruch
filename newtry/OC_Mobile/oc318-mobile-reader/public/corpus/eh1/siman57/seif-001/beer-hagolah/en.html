Language of the Rambam beginning of chapter 22 of Laws of Marriage; and explained in several places.
From Mishnah and Gemara Ketubot folio 49b.
Explanation: in her courtyard, or of both, or of others — for these courtyards of hers, seclusion alone is for marriage, per Rav Ashi's establishment there; and likewise this is explained in his words, etc., as the Rama wrote in his name.
The Hagahot wrote: this is clear — his betrothed wife, if she died, he does not inherit; and I cited this above siman 55.
