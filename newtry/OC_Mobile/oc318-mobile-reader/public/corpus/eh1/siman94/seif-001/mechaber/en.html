Obligation of a widow's dwelling and clothing, and if she said they should give her sustenance at her father's house. It contains 7 seifim. As the way his widow is sustained from his property — so they give her clothing, utensils, and dwelling; or she sits in the dwelling she was in during her husband's life. {Rama: And she does not use the entire house with the heirs; rather they give her a special dwelling according to her honor in the house (Ran chapter Na'arah in name of Rashba and Maggid Mishneh ch. 18). And some say they can give her a dwelling fitting for her in another house (so implied in Mordechai chapter HaNosei and chapter Na'arah SheNitpatah). And the widow cannot rent her dwelling to others (responsum of Rashba and in Ran chapter HaNosei).} And she uses pillows and blankets and servants she used during her husband's life when her husband was in a distant country (in Ran chapter Na'arah).
