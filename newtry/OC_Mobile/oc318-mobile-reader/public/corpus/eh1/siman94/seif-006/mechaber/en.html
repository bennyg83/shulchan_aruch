A widow who said I will not move from my father's house — they allotted sustenance for me, give me there — the heirs can say if you are with us you have sustenance, and if not we only give you according to household blessing. And if she claims because she gave birth and they are children — they give her sustenance sufficient for her alone and she in her father's house. {Rama: And some say this claim is only for a father's wife who is not their mother (Tur in name of Ramah).}
