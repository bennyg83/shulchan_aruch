When his months are completed and he is weaned — if the divorcée wished her son be with her they do not separate him from her until he is a full six years; rather they compel his father and he gives sustenance and the son is with his mother. After six years the father may say if he is not with me I will not give him sustenance. The daughter is with her mother forever. And even after six — if the father was assessed for charity they take from him against his will what is proper for her and sustain her and she is with her mother; and even if the mother remarried her daughter is with her and her father sustains her by reason of charity until the father dies and afterward she is sustained from his property per her ketubah condition and she is with her mother. {Rama: Specifically when it appears to beit din it is good for the daughter to be with her mother; but if it appears to them better to sit in her father's house the mother cannot compel her to be with her (R"M Padua siman 93). If the mother died the maternal grandmother cannot compel the children be with her (Beit Yosef in name of Rashbatz).}
