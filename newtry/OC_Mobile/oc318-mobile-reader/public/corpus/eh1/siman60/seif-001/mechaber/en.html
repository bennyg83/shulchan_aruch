Laws of wedding gifts (shoshbinut). It contains 1 seif. When a man marries a woman, his friends and acquaintances send him money so that he may be strengthened for the expense he spends on the feast — and these monies are called shoshbinut. This shoshbinut is not a complete gift, for the sender did not give it except that if he marries a woman he will return and send to him as he sent to him. Therefore if this one married a woman and did not return the shoshbinut to him — this one sues him in court and extracts it from him; and there are several disputes in this. Since in our time they do not practice suing this claim, I saw fit not to elaborate on it.
