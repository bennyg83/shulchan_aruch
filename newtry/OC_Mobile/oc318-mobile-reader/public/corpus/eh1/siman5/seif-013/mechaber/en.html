If one overpowered a person and a dog or other animals covered him until they made him kruat shafcha, or sat him in water or snow until his organs of intercourse ceased — he does not receive lashes until he castrates by his own hand; and it is fit to strike him with makat mardut. {Rama: But permitted to take a rooster's comb even though it becomes sterile thereby, and anything similar, for he does nothing to the seed organs (Hagahot Alfasi chapter Behemah).}
