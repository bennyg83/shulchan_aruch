Petzua. This means by sword or knife; dakah — crushed by anything; kerut — cut in two by anything; Beit Chadash; and in any of these presumption he does not produce; if he married and bore — known they are not his sons and presumption of mamzerim — so in Shulchan Gavoah in name of Riaz; and so Mordekhai, Semag, Tur in name of Raavam; regarding pierced — Ri MiAshkenaz Netiv 23 part 4 ruled pierced testicles are doubtful mamzerim; in Gemara pierced seems included in petzua — so Ri MiAshkenaz there and Semag wrote included in kerut — see Perush Maharaz Schtein; and according to many poskim no remedy for kruat shafcha; even for us who hold pierced disqualified and Gemara pierced and closed kosher — implies remedy exists; and so petzua dakah — nevertheless if he bore we do not presume healed unless known healed.
Surrounding the entire gid. So Rambam and Semag and Shulchan Gavoah; three views for Rambam: must surround entire gid, no concern if cut near body and thread surrounding entire gid not near body remains; Rashi: if majority of circumference remains and that majority is thread near body — kosher; and Tur's view; and Ri MiAshkenaz: must surround entire gid and be specifically near body.
