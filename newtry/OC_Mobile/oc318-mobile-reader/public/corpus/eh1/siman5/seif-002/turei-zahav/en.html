The atarah was cut — to settle Gemara and Tur's words we cite source Yevamot 75: if remainder from atarah even hair-thread's width — kosher; Rashi: atarah is high ring surrounding gid; Mishnah beginning chapter; atarah is flesh row surrounding circumcision place flowing to end; "full thread on majority facing head" — if cut toward body and remainder full thread on ring circumference toward ground — disqualified. Piercing below atarah — R' Chiya vs Rav Asi "atarah whatever blocks." Tosafot: cut like stylus or gutter. Tur: flesh at gid head high ring called atarah — see Rashi; section 4 explains above/below atarah (extensive anatomical analysis in Hebrew).
Surrounding entire gid — Rambam's view; Tur wrote majority of gid and depends (should read) on Gemara.
