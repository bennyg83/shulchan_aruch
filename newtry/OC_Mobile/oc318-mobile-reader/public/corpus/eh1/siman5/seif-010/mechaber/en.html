Every disqualification they stated in this matter applies when it was not by heavenly cause — such as if a person cut him or a thorn struck him and the like; but if he was born kruat shafcha or petzua dakah, or born without testicles, or became ill from his body and these organs ceased from him, or was born with boils that dissolved them or cut them — he is kosher to enter the congregation, for all these are by heavenly cause according to the Rambam; but according to Rashi and the Rosh, only thunder and hail or from his mother's womb is called heavenly cause, but through illness is considered human cause and disqualified; and the Rosh wrote that this is implied in the Yerushalmi.
