Every disqualification, etc. — Gemara stated on R' Yishmael's law: petzua dakah one testicle kosher — Rava said: we call petzua not ha-petzua. Mishnah taught: stated "a petzua dakah shall not enter" and "a mamzer shall not enter" — just as there one testicle so here one testicle. Per my view both needed: from comparison to mamzer already in Yerushalmi possible mamzer one testicle called — meaning formation; and from not calling ha-petzua we exclude only born from mother's womb as Rashi explains; but made afterward by thunder — whence; nevertheless hear cannot compare to mamzer in manner of formation — then even from mother's womb; must comparison from mamzer in manner coming by human cause — automatically excludes all one-testicle cases.
