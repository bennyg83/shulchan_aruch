If his testicles were cut, or one of them, or one was wounded, or one was crushed, or one is missing, or one was pierced (a through-hole) (Beit Yosef in name of Nissim in name of Tosafot chapter HaAreil) — he is disqualified. {Rama: And so is the primary view, unlike those who permit one testicle if the one taken was whole when removed and the right remained (this is R' Tam's view); nevertheless I have seen lenient rulings like the latter view, but it is good to be concerned for a Torah prohibition according to the stringent authorities, who are the majority in number and stature.}
