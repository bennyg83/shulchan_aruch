Sterilizing roots to person. Bar Ilan ot 7: drank cup sterilizing roots and became sterile — permitted in congregation. Mahari Cohen in Hagahot Zera Avraham Even HaEzer siman 11; agreed Rav Mahari Navon siman 144 part 2 siman 26 and Yad Aharon — end quote. These books not before me; apparently this matter disputed between Rashi and Tosafot — see Sotah 26a in Rashi implying thus; Tosafot there on "wife of saris"; see Kesef Mishneh chapter 2 Laws of Sotah law 6 and Maggid Mishneh there; see Teshuvot Kehilat Yaakov siman 2 on this.
