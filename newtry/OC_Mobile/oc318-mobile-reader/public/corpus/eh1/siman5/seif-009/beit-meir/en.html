And behold one testicle — therefore disqualified like one in heaven — meaning called one testicle because came through illness; per this view one who disqualifies one testicle certainly not like R' Yishmael but one who permits comes even like Rabbanan — dispute R' Yishmael and Rabbanan not literally one testicle but beginning through illness and end removed by human — sages disqualify and R' Yishmael permits; one who disqualifies one testicle discusses that case and calls one testicle since beginning in heaven; one who permits one testicle from beginning to end in heaven — kosher for all; if so conclusion meaning clear R' Yishmael discusses one testicle — end one testicle; if so question for R' Tam cut one testicle from beginning kosher for R' Yishmael; cannot push that without R' Yishmael holding cut one testicle produces he wouldn't permit because beginning through illness; rather learn from that reason he permits; automatically same cut from beginning not through illness — still produces; why force dispute in that manner; see Yeshuot Malko also almost left difficulty of understanding; know truly question if disqualified by heaven until ill and must remove one testicle lest worsen until because of this stands to amputate and removed one testicle — if not comparable to what we hold like R' Shimon all standing to shear considered sheared and kosher in heaven — see Bava Kamma 119b, Gittin 39, siman 124, Yeshuot Malko.
