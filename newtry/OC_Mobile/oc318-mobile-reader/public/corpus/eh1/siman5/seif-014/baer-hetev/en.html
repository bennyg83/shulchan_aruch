To castrate. For telling a gentile is forbidden in all prohibitions; and likewise forbidden to sell to him to castrate, as Rama brought (Beit Shmuel).
And some forbid. This refers also to selling to a Kutit, and all the more so forbidden to give for half-wages because Bnei Noach are warned about castration — see Chelkat Mechokek in Beit Yosef.
To castrate them. Then it is lifnei iveir lifnei deleifnei and permitted; and even if he gave to his agent — there is no agency for a gentile — see Beit Shmuel.
Feathers from geese. See what is written Yoreh Deah siman 24 s.k. 9 in name of Beit Yosef.
