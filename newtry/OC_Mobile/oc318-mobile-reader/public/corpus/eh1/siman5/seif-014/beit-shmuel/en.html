Forbidden to tell Kutit. In chapter HaPoalim questioned in Gemara if telling gentile forbidden like other prohibitions; seeks resolve from forbidden to tell Kutit to castrate — rejects there perhaps castration different — son of Noah commanded castration; Sanhedrin dispute if Bnei Noach commanded castration — Rambam, Rosh, Rashba, Gemara rule not commanded; Semag and Hagahot rule commanded. If so statement to gentile to castrate forbidden; nevertheless either because statement to gentile forbidden even what not commanded, or say gentile commanded castrate — practical difference even selling forbidden as Teshuvat HaRosh siman 294; concern here and there stringently; statement to Kutit forbidden in all prohibitions Yoreh De'ah 276; Teshuvat HaRosh 318; also forbidden sell to castrate.
They fine him. Although in kilayim behemah they don't fine as Yoreh De'ah 297 — here the prohibition is recognizable and different — see Derishah; another Israelite permitted to eat even though in "abomination to you" — nevertheless since forbidden to Heaven implies permitted to ordinary person — Mordekhai chapter Shechitah; Israelite who castrated or gentile castrated for him — seems forbidden to eat as Yoreh De'ah 99 — see there siman 16 regarding oto ve'et beno dispute if permitted to slaughter.
And some forbid. Refers to selling to Kutit; all the more forbidden half-wages because Bnei Noach warned about castration; I already wrote many disagree; nevertheless don't be lenient; and if can take from elsewhere no lifnei iveir; Gemara Nazir and Yoreh De'ah 131; here one can say forbidden because might castrate all; thing standing to be sacrificed to idol or food — then say certainly won't sacrifice all.
To another gentile. Then lifnei iveir lifnei deleifnei and permitted; even if gave to agent — no agency for gentile — Teshuvat HaRosh implies even gentile to gentile no agency, not as Magen Avraham siman 96 ruled simply gentile to gentile has agency; see Teshuvat Rashakh part 1 siman 114 also ruled like Teshuvat HaRosh.
