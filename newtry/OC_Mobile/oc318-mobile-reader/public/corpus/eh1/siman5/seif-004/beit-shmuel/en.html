Semen exits. Reason pierced hole disqualified: semen drips through hole and does not shoot like arrow and does not produce; therefore measure of hole is when semen can exit; and Gemara if closed from hairs — dispute if semen exits; but teaches even if closed nevertheless disqualified if when pierced semen exited.
If the hole closed. So Tur; apparently difficult why not write when closed we see if torn; however per Rashi and Nissim discussing small hole not visible — one can say large hole measure that semen exits, if closed we are not concerned it tore; but small hole even now semen won't exit — we are concerned it may widen and must test if torn; also one can say closed through healing we are not concerned it tore; also from Rashi above atarah no concern it tore because semen does not collect there but near hole at exit.
Returns to fitness. Implies all poskim even above atarah; and all holes if closed return to fitness — not like Ri MiAshkenaz who wrote in name of Rama only atarah closing helps not other holes; per him why establish Gemara only at atarah not above atarah; but Rashi and Nissim resolve above atarah we don't concern torn — but closing helps all holes.
