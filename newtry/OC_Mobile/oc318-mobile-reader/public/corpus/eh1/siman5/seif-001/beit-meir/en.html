Because he is not in his sanctity — Chelkat Mechokek challenges: since he is not in his sanctity and permitted with zona — implies also permitted to become impure to dead and forbidden in kodashim; and full verse "from your thigh and from your belly you shall eat kodshei kodashim" — and what Beit Shmuel wrote here per his view is not lengthy; especially the first view in Shulchan Aruch — examine; and per my view all that is said "not in his sanctity" is only regarding sanctity of marriage because forbidden in congregation; but for other matters he is a full kohen — know that petzua dakah Israel also not in his sanctity; shall we say considered Israelite for all matters? Your words that truly only in sanctity of convert to obligate in mitzvot and permitted with netinah like him — suffices for the sages; but R' Yehuda holds congregation of converts called congregation and forbidden with mamzeret without doubt per my text — same with netinah forbidden by "do not intermarry with them" — whence permit?
