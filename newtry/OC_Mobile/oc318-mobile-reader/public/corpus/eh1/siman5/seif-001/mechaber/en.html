The law of petzua dakah and kruat shafcha. It contains 14 seifim: Petzua dakah and kruat shafcha are forbidden to marry an Israelite woman, and permitted with a convert and a freed woman; and even a kohen who is petzua dakah is permitted to marry a convert and a freed woman, because he is not in his kehunah sanctity; and even a netinah or one of the doubtful cases is permitted to him, since petzua dakah is forbidden to enter the congregation — they did not decree on netinim or on doubtful cases; but forbidden with a definite mamzeret, for she is forbidden by Torah law. {Rama: Some permit even with a mamzeret (Tur, Raavad, Rashba).}
