Petzua dakah. There are three views here: according to the Rambam who holds prohibition of intermarriage with the seven nations and netinim — in their gentile state as written in the previous siman — therefore petzua dakah is permitted with netinim because they are converts deoraita and David decreed on them, and he did not decree on blemished persons; and forbidden with mamzeret because regarding mamzer prohibition it applies to their sanctity — so wrote the Maggid; and that a kohen petzua dakah is permitted with a convert and forbidden with mamzeret — because conversion is not explicitly forbidden in Torah, and it is a lav she'eino shavah bechol; but with mamzeret forbidden and we have nothing to permit except what Chazal enumerated — so the Maggid; and to marry divorcee and chalalah forbidden, for explicitly in Torah; even though a convert is forbidden as zona and zona is explicit in Torah — nevertheless the prohibition is not explicit but derived from Yechezkel that a convert is in the category of zona — see Maggid chapter 16 Avodah Zarah law 3; according to Raavad and Rashba we derive from Yechezkel "virgin of seed of house of Israel" and she is not in category of zona — not like Chelkat Mechokek who wrote permitted with divorcee; and according to poskim who hold like the latter language of Rava that intermarriage prohibition applies even with a convert — nevertheless petzua dakah permitted with netinim even though his lav is explicit and lav she'eino shavah bechol — from this we derive permitted also with mamzeret; and apparently difficult for this view: why not establish the mishnah chapter 11 "all forbidden to enter congregation permitted one with another" regarding petzua dakah forbidden to enter congregation permitted with mamzeret; and per this view a kohen petzua dakah seems permitted with divorcee; and according to Baal HaMaor who also holds like Rambam "you shall not intermarry" applies in their gentile state — therefore petzua dakah permitted with netinim after they converted; and he holds petzua dakah applies to sanctity and kohen petzua dakah forbidden with convert and mamzeret — and so ruled in Shulchan Gavoah in name of Riaz and brought in Darkei Moshe.
Or one of the doubts. Apparently wonder: why permitted with doubts — we hold definite mamzer forbidden with doubtful mamzeret; and why doubtful mamzeret permitted to petzua dakah even though stated in chapter 11 "certain congregation shall not enter, doubtful congregation may enter" — meaning deoraita; nevertheless we hold doubts certainly forbidden; and petzua dakah to marry maidservant — one can say for Rambam permitted, for maidservant prohibition is only rabbinic.
