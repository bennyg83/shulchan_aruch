It is forbidden to destroy the organs of seed — whether in a person, whether in an animal, beast, or bird — both impure and pure, both in Eretz Yisrael and abroad; and anyone who castrates receives lashes by Torah law everywhere; and even one who castrates after another castrated receives lashes. How so? If one came and cut the gid, and another came and cut the testicles or repaired, and another came and cut the testicular cords; or if one came and crushed the gid and another repaired and another cut — all receive lashes even though the last only castrated one already castrated — whether in a person or in animal, beast, and bird; and one who castrates the female — whether in a person or in other species — is exempt but forbidden.
