Forbidden, etc. Whether in person Shabbat 111b.
Whether, etc. One impure Chagigah 14b.
Beast, etc. Whether in Eretz Yisrael, etc.; Torat Kohanim chapter Emor "you shall not offer" — I have only not offer; whence not do? Teach "you shall not do"; say only unblemished; blemished whence? Teach "in your land"; say only land; abroad whence? Teach "you shall not do" wherever you are; whence also person? Teach "among you" — words of Beit Shamai.
How behold, etc. Goes his view explaining like R' Yehuda Bechorot 39b and Kiddushin 25b all even in eggs; although halachah like R' Yosi his colleague and all the more he decides because R' Yochanan who said bring nitak, etc. holds like R' Yehuda; and Rashi and Tosafot there; however Tosafot Bechorot there on "all" wrote even for R' Yosi end "you shall not do" even in eggs; however Rambam perforce doesn't hold thus — therefore explains like R' Yehuda as above.
Whether person whether, etc. As above.
And one who castrates, etc. Torat Kohanim there: whence also females in castration? Teach "for you destroyed them"; R' Yishmael in them; females not in castration; explains like Tannaim except no lashes since not included in "you shall not do" lav.
Whether, etc. As above all included in this verse.
