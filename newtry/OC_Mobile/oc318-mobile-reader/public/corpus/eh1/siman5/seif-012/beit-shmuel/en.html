Giving to drink. Even drinking for healing without intent to sterilize — since psik reisheih; although we hold psik reisheih deleina lei on Shabbat permitted — nevertheless in other prohibitions forbidden as Rosh and Ran; even if already has children or is saris — forbidden; see Perush Beit Shmuel.
And woman permitted, etc. Implies even without any pain permitted; previous seif "one who castrates female exempt but forbidden" — castration by action; Rambam and Semag hold even female castration by action applies; Tannaim in Torat Kohanim R' Yishmael "females not in castration" — no lashes on castrating females as Maggid; Rambam and Semag wrote female castration in law of castration by action then castration by liquids; see Tosafot and Beit Chadash what Maharshal wrote; female in animals and species not better than woman — so Chelkat Mechokek in Perush Maharaz Schtein on Semag — see there.
