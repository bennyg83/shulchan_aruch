Sterilizing roots. Even if he drinks for healing and does not intend to be sterilized — since it is psik reisheih it is forbidden; see what is written in Beit Yosef on this; and even if he already has children, and even if he is a saris — forbidden (Beit Shmuel).
Sterilizing roots. Even without birth pain — permitted; and what is written in the previous seif "one who castrates the female is exempt but forbidden" — this is castration by action, for even in a female castration by action applies; and gnawing in animals and other species is not better than a woman.
