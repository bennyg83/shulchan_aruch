Whole. Namely he lacks no limb or part of a limb; but if he was wounded in one of his limbs even lacking some flesh they testify about him — responsum Beit Yosef siman 6 on laws of gentile incidentally. Maharachash in klal 174 wavers on this. If a limb is missing even if the limb was found near him even if his severed head was found near him it is not called his body whole — responsum Beit Yosef there. Maharachash there wrote for me uncertain; Kenesset HaGedolah 27a agreed with Maharachash — Yoreh De'ah.
The later authorities. Choshen Mishpat infers from stam in the Gemara unlike R' Tarfon — implies his words were not entirely rejected and we combine R' Tarfon's view with other estimations and proofs. In responsum Rashdam siman 56 he sided with R' Tarfon to permit if the killed was found swollen and unknown if within three days as written below. Per Tur and Nimukei Yosef identifying mark helps per R' Tarfon without signs at all. But Tosafot and Ra'ash imply need signs with identifying mark — namely signs that are not prominent; see Darkei Moshe and Beit Chadash. The Mechaber wrote identifying mark without signs also helps per his reasoning following his responsum there is a correction in Ra'ash — it seems to me through identifying mark or through signs and a fortiori both together; and so Beit Shmuel's view. Darkei Moshe not so — see there.
