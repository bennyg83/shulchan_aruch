And the later authorities disagreed with him. Chelkat Mechokek infers that since he did not state plainly against Rabbeinu Tam, it somewhat implies that his words were not rejected entirely, and his view joins with other assessments and proofs. In responsum Maharshdam siman 56, he joined Rabbeinu Tam's view to permit where a killed man was found swollen and it was not known whether this was within three days, as written nearby. According to the Tur and Nimukei Yosef, visual recognition without any signs at all helps according to Rabbeinu Tam; but from Tosafot and the Rosh it implies that one must state signs together with visual recognition, meaning signs that are not distinctive. See Derishah and Bach. The Mechaber wrote that visual recognition without signs also helps, following his reasoning in his responsum, where he wrote that one should emend the Rosh to read that it must be by visual recognition or by signs, and one need not say both together. This is also the view of the Bach, but the Derishah did not write so.
