Whose presumption is that they hate. See responsum Knesset Yechezkel at the end of siman 83, where he asked: if so, even one valid witness should not be believed if he is an enemy, since one witness is not believed by Torah law and the Sages were merely lenient; with an enemy they did not act leniently. He answers that a valid witness is afraid lest the husband come alive, and more than he will ruin her, he will ruin himself by becoming disqualified for testimony and oaths; see there. See Machatzit HaShekel s.k. 16, who was not satisfied with his resolution, and brought in the name of Korban HaEdah, explaining the Yerushalmi, that only with women, whose minds are light, did they suspect perhaps they lie because of hatred, but not with men; see there. At first glance, from their words a new law emerges for us: a woman, even if she is not one of the five women, if we know that she hates her, is disqualified from testifying for her; specifically a man who is an enemy is valid, either because of the reason of Knesset Yechezkel, that a man will be concerned for himself not to become disqualified for testimony and oath, or because of the reason of Korban HaEdah and Machatzit HaShekel, that only with women, whose minds are light, did they suspect this. According to this, one must say that when they said five women, it means that with those women, even if we see no hatred between them, their presumption is that they hate one another; but if we see hatred, the same law applies even to an ordinary woman. This requires study, for we have never heard this.
