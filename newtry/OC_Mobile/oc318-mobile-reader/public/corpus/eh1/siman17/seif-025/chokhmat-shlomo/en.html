Seif 42 — Two witnesses say died, etc. See in Choshen Mishpat and Beit Shmuel that even if she married with permission she must leave; and this is from the Gemara Yevamot 88; and see in my edition 6 siman 1 second responsum in my responsum 130 to Laros in laws of Kiddushin siman 37 what I wrote to pilpul on this and that this is not compelled — see there and several matters belonging to the sugya of two say died — see there also regarding law of one witness says died and two say did not die — we wrote there and in resolving the sugya and the words of Peri Chadash 101 and Neta Sha'ashuim siman 75 and 77 — see there with God's help; examine. And behold what should be noted on this in the words of Rambam laws of inadvertent sins chapter 8 law 3 — see in my composition on Orach Chaim laws of Yom Kippur part the inscribed 102 page 66 what I wrote to resolve this there — see there and examine. And behold what should be noted in this sugya in what the Gemara challenges "who is so brazen," etc., and Rav Hamnuna said, etc. — and this contradicts the Gemara Nedarim; see in my responsum on laws of Gittin siman 141 incidentally what I wrote on this — and it is in my edition 7 siman 99 — see there and examine.
