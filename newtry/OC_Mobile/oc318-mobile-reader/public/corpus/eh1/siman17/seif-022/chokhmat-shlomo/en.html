In that seif in the gloss — However because of slander she should not marry. Behold if she married and was widowed or divorced — if she is permitted afterward to marry ab initio; see in my edition 6 siman 1 in responsum to Lemberg laws of Kiddushin siman 37 what I wrote there to prove she is forbidden to marry ab initio even then — see there and examine. And see in my composition Mishpat Tzedek on Choshen Mishpat edition 4 siman 48 laws of lost objects siman 262 what I proved from the Gemara Gittin that two inferior signs combine for an intermediate sign — and all the more per this that two intermediates combine for a prominent sign — see there and examine.
