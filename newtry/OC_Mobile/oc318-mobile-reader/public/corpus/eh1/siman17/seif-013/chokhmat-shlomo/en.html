Seif 14 in the gloss — And likewise if he said so-and-so died speaking offhandedly, etc., is called properly mesalfei lefi tumo, etc. Behold although there is no proof for the matter, there is remembrance of the matter — for mesalfei lefi tumo applies even without connecting words in Bekhorot (35a) in what is stated not to bring mesalfei lefi tumo; and Rashi explained they did not ask the butcher what its nature is but they themselves speak offhandedly and say "see, do not slaughter this ever except through a blemish" and they did not intend thereby to inflict a blemish on it — from this it is proven even without connecting words whatever is not through a question is mesalfei lefi tumo; and this is clear proof in my humble opinion; examine. And see in my composition Turei Zahav on Yoreh De'ah edition 6 siman 202 in my responsum laws of bekhor where I wrote proof to the contrary that mesalfei lefi tumo without connecting words does not help from the Gemara Bava Kamma 113b — see there with God's help; examine.
