Even one witness. The law whether one witness for woman's testimony is D'Oraisa or only D'Rabbanan (with practical differences throughout this siman) is disputed among early authorities: from Nekudot Yosef ch. Ha'ishah Rabah it appears in name of Ra'ah and Ritva ; D'Oraisa ; because matter done to be revealed and precision ; certainly truth testified ; considered full Torah testimony; what Talmud says "due to agunah they were lenient" ; Ritva explains rabbis lenient not stringent on Torah. But Tosafot there ; believed only D'Rabbanan (see Beit Shmuel below s.k. 109); likewise Rashi Yevamos 92b s.v. teda (and Shabbat 145b ; whoever betroths per rabbis, etc. ; however per Noda Biyhuda below Rashi there no proof ; speaks of witness from witness); likewise Rashba in his rulings 87. Rambam ; authors disputed: from Laws of Testimony ch. 5 appears D'Rabbanan; in ch. 13 Laws of Divorce words contradict ; "do not wonder Sages were lenient, Torah not strict," etc. ; Lechem Mishneh explains Rambam like Tosafot ; one witness D'Rabbanan ; Rivash siman 255 per Rambam; Maharik end shoresh 30 ; appears from Rambam D'Oraisa because matter done to be revealed; see responsa Noda Biyhuda simanim 27, 29, 33 ; decides D'Oraisa ; brings Sifrei "one witness shall not rise against a man but rises for woman to marry her," etc.; valid witness only ; not disqualified like woman, slave, witness from witness ; those only D'Rabbanan; explains Rambam in Laws of Divorce ; valid witness D'Oraisa, Sages added to validate disqualified; in ch. 5 ; for laws of testimony Torah did not believe one witness except sotah and capital cases; where belief is for revealing ; reason not testimony laws but matter speaks for itself due to hazakah they do not lie ; expanded there. See responsum Berit Avraham simanim 21, 39; Rashbatz vol. 1 siman 82-85 ; like Rambam as Noda Biyhuda explained; see responsum Shivat Tzion siman 81; Penei Yehoshua Kesubos 22; Peri Chadash on Gittin 2 ; asmakhta; responsa Imrei Binah, Shevut Yaakov vol. 2 siman 116, Beit Meir this seif; Chasam Sofer siman 94. See responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 123 ; man asked to adjudicate challengers disqualifying him and sister and children from congregation ; mother married father without court testimony that first husband died; now one witness testifies he heard from first husband's brother before second marriage that he mourns brother ; letter from Aland that brother R' Yozfa died. Apparently suffices to permit R' Yozfa's wife to remarry if witness testifies to permit woman ; Sages believed one witness and witness from witness with stringency and agunah as rabbis expanded ; not when witness testifies after her death ; testimony on children she bore ; perhaps believed here; but principally on children ; where hazakah married woman one witness not believed; especially woman who married without testimony then one witness first husband died ; perhaps ineffective: precision not only at marriage start but every moment under husband (Tosafot Bava Batra 32) ; yet perhaps hazakah weakened for her without precision at start; also no fear of stringency gain in leaving him ; if first husband comes full stringency of married woman for marrying without testimony; no precision at start or after ; one witness not believed. Not found explicit ruling testifying after death only to validate children ; like general testimony; if two witnesses brother said he heard brother died ; strong basis to permit though witness from witness; both only from one witness ; perhaps like those who testify testimony accepted in court ; here only one witness perhaps not believed; even with two ; perhaps not like accepted-in-court testimony (see below seif 39). Leniency: even testifying after death helps ; Sages believed one witness due to precision support for agunah ; here witness testimony plus precision ; woman does not marry without fear of stringency at end ; truth recognizable brother said so ; she precise and married ; with both reasons as lenient for agunah ; in pressing need and agunah to disqualify from congregation ; no greater agunah.
