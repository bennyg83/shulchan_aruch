That which we said that witness from witness is fit for a woman's testimony — in what case? When he heard from the mouth of one with understanding that so-and-so died, such as a slave or maidservant; but if he heard from the mouth of a fool or minor — he does not testify and one does not rely on their words. If he heard from children who say now "we just came from the eulogy of so-and-so" — "thus and thus we eulogized there, and so-and-so the sage and so-and-so went after his bier, thus and thus they did to his bier" — this one testifies from their mouths based on these words and the like, and they permit his wife. {Rama: And specifically immediately — such as when they say now "we just came from the eulogy of so-and-so," etc.; but if not immediately their testimony is not effective at all (Or Zaru'a and Mahariw siman 45 and Piskei Maharai siman 210) (and even if they grew up later they cannot testify what they saw in their childhood) (there in Or Zaru'a).}
