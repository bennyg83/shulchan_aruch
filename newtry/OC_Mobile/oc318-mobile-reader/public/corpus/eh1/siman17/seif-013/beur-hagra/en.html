That which we say, etc. Kesubos 22b. It appears from the Tur and Shulchan Aruch that this applies even if one witness supports her, as stated in HaMekabel. There, however, the husband also supports her, as written by the Rambam, Rosh, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch in siman 141, se'if 56. Yet from what is stated there, 'unless she says, it was before me,' etc., without a migo she would otherwise not be believed. See the Rosh there and the Tur and Shulchan Aruch there, se'if 55.
