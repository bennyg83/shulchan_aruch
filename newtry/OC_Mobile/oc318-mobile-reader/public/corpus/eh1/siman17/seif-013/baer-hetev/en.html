Or a minor. Meaning he said Ploni died and did not say other matters of his death such as such-and-such eulogizers were there — it does not help; and there is no difference whether they testify or a witness who hears from them — Rambam, Beit Yosef, Beit Chadash. And in Perishah he wrote if the minors said Ploni died it helps even if not incidentally; but if they said behold we come from his burial we do not rely on them until they say such-and-such eulogizers, etc. And Taz wrote specifically for gittin who reached the age of dexterity that his sale is valid; but less than this does not help even incidentally. And if a minor who reached dexterity tells that he was at a killing how and what was the cause of killing — similar to saying behold we come from his burial and such-and-such eulogizers, etc. and they permit on this — end quote Taz.
From the minors. In Tur it implies even if not incidentally; but in Pesakim of Maharai he wrote specifically when they are incidentally then they are believed.
The sage. Not both but one of them suffices — Ra'ash in pamphlet on agunot.
Immediately. Meaning immediately after seeing he testifies and need not see the deceased immediately after death but immediately refers to his seeing — he says now I come from burial — Pesakim Maharai; and Taz wrote this immediately does not refer to what he testifies before court that he must come immediately before court but immediately after coming among people he says this matter before them suffices and the hearer goes afterward to court even after time; see Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel.
