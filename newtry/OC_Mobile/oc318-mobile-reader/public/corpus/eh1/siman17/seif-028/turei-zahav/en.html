Does not testify; Tur wrote if one witness testifies saw immediately after raised and recognized through signs on body; believed (explanation signs not prominent but help with identifying mark combination); but if says only through identifying mark not believed; when deals witness saw him fall there not believed; but after did not see drowning believed even not immediately after raised even identifying mark without signs; end quote Perishah; that which above if not seen immediately changes; we know fell there and above doubt whether fell water at all; end quote; very wondrous certainly no distinction here and there; also there found in water essence said there relies what stated here; how much wrote siman 134.
