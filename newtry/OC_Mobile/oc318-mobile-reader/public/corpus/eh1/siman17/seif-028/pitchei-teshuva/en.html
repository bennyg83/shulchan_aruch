And the daughter of her mother-in-law means even if she is not the daughter of her father-in-law, as is also explicit in Mishneh LaMelekh there. In the Talmud, 117, the question was asked: what is the law regarding the daughter of her father-in-law who is not the daughter of her mother-in-law? Is the reason for the mother-in-law's daughter because there is a mother who hates her, etc.? The question was not resolved. All the decisors omitted it. Even the Rosh, Tur, and Rama, who brought the unresolved question of the mother of her yavam and ruled stringently, nevertheless did not mention this unresolved question of the daughter of her father-in-law, which is very surprising. In Kuntres Maharchash, p. 33, he wrote that although all the decisors omitted it, implying that their view is to be lenient, nevertheless it is proper to be stringent, like the view of Rabbenu Yerucham and the Maggid Mishneh. See Tosafot Yom Tov, Yevamot 15:4, who wrote that this is also the view of Rashi and Nimukei Yosef, since they used the wording "this one will inherit all the toil of my father and mother," carefully writing "the toil of my father" to say that it is not specifically her mother-in-law's daughter, but the same applies to her father-in-law's daughter who is not her mother-in-law's daughter. See Machatzit HaShekel, who wrote at length to give a good reason for the decisors who omitted it, but concluded that in practice it requires analysis to be lenient unless there is some additional ground. Maharchash further wrote there, and it is also cited in the addenda to Machatzit HaShekel, that the daughter of her rival wife, the daughter of her yevamah, and the others may testify for her. Although their mother hates her, we are not concerned for that. When the Talmud was uncertain whether the reason for the mother-in-law's daughter is that there is a mother who hates her, this means because there are two causes: her mother provokes her, and that mother herself hates her. But where there is only the fact that the mother hates her, we are not concerned.
