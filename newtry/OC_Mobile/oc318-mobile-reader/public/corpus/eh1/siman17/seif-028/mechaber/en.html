Some say in the name of Rabbenu Tam that that which they do not testify about him except until three days — this is when he is wounded in his face; but if he is not wounded in his face they testify about him even after many days by visual identification of his body and his form (and even if he fell into water). And it is not clear to the later authorities. {Rama: And some distinguish: whoever was with him when he drowned in water — even if he was whole — does not testify about him; but one who did not see his drowning and says he recognizes him by visual identification and he is whole — testifies about him (Tur and Terumat HaDeshen siman 205 in the name of the Ashiri).}
