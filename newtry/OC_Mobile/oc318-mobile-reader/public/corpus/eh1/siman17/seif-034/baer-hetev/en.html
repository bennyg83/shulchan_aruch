She leaves. If the second husband died or divorced her she is permitted to marry another — Radbaz ch. 1 siman 109. Not only in drowning in water without end but likewise anywhere that by majority he dies she does not leave if she married on a sage or by mistake. But those the Mishnah teaches "they do not testify" etc. — if she married she leaves. And likewise the three matters of R' Eliezer ben Parta — she leaves; and if he fell into a lion's den she leaves — Rivash siman 379; see Beit Shmuel.

Taz wrote on this witness from witness does not help unless he said explicitly how he drowned; although for many matters we are lenient on witness from witness it is specifically in matters where by the witness's language it indicates closer to death than life; but if he says drowned stam there is more reasoning he drowned in water without end for most drowned are in water without end.
