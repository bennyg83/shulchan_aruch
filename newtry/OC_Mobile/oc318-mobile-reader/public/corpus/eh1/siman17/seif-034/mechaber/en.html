And likewise a woman to whom one testified that her husband drowned in endless waters and did not come up and his memory was lost and his name forgotten — behold she may not marry on this testimony as explained (and even if the court permitted her and she did not marry — she may not marry) (Rivash siman 379). And if she married she need not leave. {Rama: And specifically if she married based on a sage or in error thinking she was permitted; but if she married wantonly she must leave (responsum of Maharam brought by Mordechai end of Yevamot). And all this is specifically regarding one about whom they testified he actually drowned in endless waters; but one about whom they testified he was on a ship that broke at sea or the like, or even that he drowned but they did not testify he remained in the water long enough for his soul to depart — she must leave. And even if he testified simply that he drowned — we are concerned lest he called such things "drowning" as is the way of the world to call these things drowning — and she must leave unless he testified explicitly that he actually drowned and remained long enough for his soul to depart (Beit Yosef in the name of the responsum of Ramban siman 128).} And even if it was a Samaritan who spoke offhandedly and said so-and-so drowned and she married on his word — behold she need not leave. And a sage who ruled to permit her ab initio (in endless waters) — they excommunicate him.
