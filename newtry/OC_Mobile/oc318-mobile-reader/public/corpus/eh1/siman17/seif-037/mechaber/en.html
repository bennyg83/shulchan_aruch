One witness came and testified that her husband died and they permitted her to marry on his word; and afterward one witness came and contradicted the first and said he did not die — behold she need not leave the permission and may marry; for one witness is believed for a woman's testimony like two witnesses for other testimonies, and the statement of one is not in place of two. However, because of slander she should not marry (Tur). But if the second came before they permitted her — behold she may not marry. And if she married she must leave because it is safek. And if she married before the witness testified for her and she says "it is clear to me he died" — behold she need not leave. And some say she must leave. And if two came and said he did not die — even though she married she must leave according to all. {Rama: And specifically when she is silent; but if she also says "died" it is like two against two if the witnesses are women; but the husband who married her is not believed regarding himself (Nimukei Yosef chapter HaIshah Kama).} In what case? When the one she married based on is like two who came and contradicted him — such as she married based on one man and two came and said he did not die; or she married based on a woman or on herself and two women or two disqualified persons of their words came and said he did not die. But a fit witness says "died" and many women say "did not die," or disqualified persons say "did not die" — this is like half and half. {Rama: And specifically when they came together before they permitted her based on the first fit witness; but if they permitted her based on the fit witness and afterward the disqualified ones came — she need not leave the first permission (Tur).} And if she married one of the witnesses and she says "it is clear to me he died" — behold she need not leave. {Rama: And Samaritans speaking offhandedly — their law is like other disqualified testimony (Mordechai chapter HaIshah Batra). And whoever does not speak offhandedly is not believed neither to forbid nor to permit.}
