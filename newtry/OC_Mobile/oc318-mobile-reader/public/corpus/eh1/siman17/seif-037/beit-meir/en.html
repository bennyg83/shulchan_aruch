Some say in the name of Rabbeinu Tam. Chelkat Mechokek wrote within the responsum, to answer the difficulty of the Rivash, that one can reject it, for it is still difficult: perhaps there were distinctive signs. In my humble opinion this is not difficult at all, for certainly this novel law was a received tradition in their hands; if it were by distinctive signs, what novelty would there be after five days? But according to Rabbeinu Tam, that they permitted through visual recognition, there is indeed novelty. In my humble opinion one can press further: it would certainly also have been possible to answer that there it was different because the whole body was intact, had that been received by them as the case; but because that was not received, and the reasoning that water constricts was obvious to them even without forcing the difficulty, just as the reasoning that the whole body is intact was obvious to Rabbeinu Tam, they answered with what was clear to them.
