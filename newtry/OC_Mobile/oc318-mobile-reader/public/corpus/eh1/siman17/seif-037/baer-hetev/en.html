And she may marry. And she may marry another man — as stated if her husband died since they permitted her on one witness there is no doubt at all — see Beit Shmuel. It is not called they permitted her to marry except in such a case they permitted her by law and truthfully; but they permitted her by mistake — no R' Yehudah Aderabi siman 244. An agunah where one witness said her husband died in Nisan and on that witness she married in Tammuz and afterward clear testimony came saying her husband was alive in Iyar certainly and before they removed her from her husband it was clarified by witnesses her husband had already died and they buried him but it was hidden from them when he died whether after her marriage or before the marriage — Shevut Ya'akov ch. 1 siman 38 ruled to be stringent she leaves with a get; nevertheless if possible to investigate the matter that her husband already died before the marriage then restore the first permission — see there. A woman presumed a widow who entered chuppah with one and was not betrothed saying she spread niddah; the next day after her chuppah a document came from a sage that she is in presumption of agunah prohibition — see responsum Chakham Tzvi questions 2–3 great debate with Maharar R' A Broda.
She may marry. See Tosafot Ketubot 22b and Yevamot 117b — it appears from their words in this-one-after-this they call it "they permitted her" for stam "they permitted her" is before the second witness comes; if the second witness came before they permitted her even if both came at once it is called and there is no distinction between one after another or at once — only regarding this is there distinction between permitted or not — see there. If so Beit Shmuel's words here are strained for it appears from his language there is distinction even without "they permitted him" between one after another and at once — see there; and so Rashba in explanation siman 348: the Gemara says here at once here one after another — not literally at once but whoever the second came before the court permitted her to marry is called at once — see there. See Beit Chadash and Kenesset HaGedolah 44b — it appears from their language too there is distinction even without permission between one after another and at once; and it was not hidden from me for stam Gemara and Tosafot imply the essence depends on permission and without permission even if both came at once it is called at once; examine.
She leaves. Even if the permitting witness came before the forbidding witness — since he came before they permitted her she leaves for it is as if they came at once as stated.
To me. Rashi explained Ketubot 26b: clear to me if he were alive he would have come. Ran disputes and holds such clarity is not clarity — only specifically she says she saw him dead.
She is silent. Namely if she married with permission on one woman or one disqualified witness and afterward two women or disqualified witnesses came who remove her — for all agree this is when she is silent; but if she too says died and one woman supports her saying died and she married and afterward two women say he did not die — if she married she does not leave for the first two are believed forever and two women do not remove her from presumption of married woman after she married with permission. Specifically if she already married or they permitted her — then the first two are like two valid witnesses.
She leaves. This is Tur's view; but Ramban, Rashba, and Nimukei Yosef explain Rambam's view that even though they permitted her on one witness if two women came at the end it is like two witnesses contradicting one another and she leaves unless she married the testifying witness and she says clearly.
Testify. See Beit Shmuel. Perhaps scribal error and must read to one of her witnesses — namely to the witness supporting her who says died.
Testimony. In all these laws woman and gentile incidentally and witness from witness are equal for leniency or stringency. Witness from gentile incidentally whether called valid or disqualified witness — see Maharival ch. 3 siman 8 and Rashdam siman 59. Where there are two witnesses testifying witness from gentile that Ploni died — per their testimony she is forbidden to marry; and there are two others contradicting them saying witness from the gentile himself that Ploni died — testimony suffices to permit her even though the two who come to forbid contradict one another — e.g. one says he was not incidentally only intended to permit and one says he was incidentally but death was not clear to him — nevertheless they combine since ultimately per both this woman was not permitted. Likewise if they contradict one another how he died — since per both he does not exist their testimony combines — Maharival ch. 3 siman 4. One witness testified died; and witness from witness or even two from two testified did not die — in practice be stringent and there is room to doubt if she married whether she leaves or not. Kenesset HaGedolah 31a wrote from Rashdam siman 59 it implies she leaves; see Maharival ch. 3 siman 5 and responsum Ramban siman 119; Maharachash klal 174 wrote specifically if they came at once; but if she married or they permitted her on the one who says died even though afterward these two came and said did not die she does not leave from the permission — see there. Witness from witness says died and the woman was permitted and afterward a valid witness from himself said did not die — depends on dispute of rabbis when the woman was permitted on a woman or disqualified witness and afterward a valid witness said did not die — Maharachash klal 174. They permitted or she married on witness from witness and afterward two witnesses from two witnesses said did not die — she leaves. Likewise if both came at once two from two said died and witness from witness said did not die — she may marry lekhatchilah — Maharachash klal 174. Both came at once witness from witness says died and witness from witness says did not die — she may not marry and if she married she leaves. Gentile incidentally says did not die and contradicts gentile incidentally who says died — she may not marry and if she married she leaves — see Kenesset HaGedolah 41a; Choshen Mishpat s.k. 74. Witness from gentile incidentally and she married and afterward witness from valid witness said did not die — she does not leave — Maharival ch. 3 siman 5. Witness testifying about a man who died not knowing his name only from himself — Maharival ch. 3 siman 8 considers him witness from witness. Rashdam siman 29 considers him the witness testifying himself. Maharachash klal 174 compelled like Rashdam.
