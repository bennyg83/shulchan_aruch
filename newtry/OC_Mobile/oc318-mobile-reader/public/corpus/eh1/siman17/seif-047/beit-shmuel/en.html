This one says, etc. The same applies because both came to permit and they contradict one another, so it is a contradiction. Granted, when she said he did not die and contradicts her rival who says he died, it is more reasonable that she is not believed because she contradicts her rival. See Tosafot there, folio 118. One should not ask: this law was already heard from what was written in seif 9, where one witness says he died and one says he was killed, that he is believed. There, at least there are two valid witnesses saying that he is not alive; but here one might have thought that since there are no witnesses except these, who say that he died and who contradict one another, it is not testimony. Therefore it teaches us otherwise.
