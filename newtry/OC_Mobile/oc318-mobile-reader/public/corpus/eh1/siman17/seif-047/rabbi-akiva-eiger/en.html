Beit Shmuel sign 107 — since she is forbidden to marry. Nb: Nevertheless supplementary kesubah and nedunya she collects — for even one witness that her husband died she collects her kesubah but not supplementary and nedunya; meaning for monetary matters where there is no derashah of kesubah one witness is not believed; and here the opposite — for monetary matters we consider finite water as certainly dead and the brothers inherit; but for kesubah since she is not permitted to marry she does not collect because of the derashah of kesubah — therefore supplementary and nedunya which are not included in "when you marry another you shall take" she collects; and the heirs descend to the inheritance — as appears plain to me, not like responsa Makor Baruch siman 11; and see responsa of Mahari of Lemberg siman 11.
