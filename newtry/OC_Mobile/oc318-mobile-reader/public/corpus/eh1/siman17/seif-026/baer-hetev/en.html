If he died in rainy season in cold days they testify about him even after many days for certainly he hardened and skin formed and does not let him swell — and better than finding in water — Ramban end of Yevamot. And so responsum Mahari Weil siman 104 and R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 15.
Extended. Namely as long as no change is seen in his body and form they testify about him — She'erit Yosef and Beit Chadash. If he was killed on land and they threw him into water and there is no wound it is as if he drowned in water — Rashdam siman 54 and R' Yehudah Aderabi siman 15. But Mahari Nachmi Ashkenazi — his words brought in responsum Beit Yosef siman 12 on laws of gentile incidentally — is uncertain on this — see there. Whatever is found in water — no concern perhaps they killed him before throwing him into water and he remained there three days — Rashdam siman 54, R' Yehudah Aderabi siman 15, responsum Beit Yosef siman 12 on laws of gentile incidentally. Likewise no concern perhaps he emerged from the water and swelled and was thrown back into water — R' Shmuel Levi siman 11; see Kenesset HaGedolah 28a.

That was cast. Meaning more than one hour; but within one hour they testify about him — Ran and Maggid. Maharar Betzalel siman 25 wrote even if he was raised from water to dry land if the sea passes back and forth over him still it constricts and does not let him swell and change. And so R' Shmuel Levi siman 11 and Mahari Weil ch. 2 siman 33. But Maharachash in klal 174 disputes this; see what Kenesset HaGedolah wrote 28a seif 67. Ram Alshkar siman 25 wrote that which we require he be seen immediately — namely to know that what the water expelled is Ploni husband of this woman and not another man whose form changed to her husband's form; accordingly where we know a number of men entered one ship and drowned and we know her husband was among the drowned and they testified all were found and buried — need not see him at the time — see there. But Maharar Betzalel siman 21 and Ra'ah Sasson siman 4 and Rashdam siman 26 dispute. If they saw him immediately and he swelled — there is room to doubt his law whether they testify or not. The reasoning inclines they do not testify. It seems to me to prove thus from Rivash who wrote in doubt of remaining she must leave. Difficult — why, behold there is double doubt doubt of remaining or not; and if you say he remained perhaps he did not swell — it seems it is double doubt of non-transformation: if you say he swelled we can no longer doubt perhaps within an hour or after an hour. If it were that they saw him within an hour and he swelled they testify — still there is room to doubt and if you say perhaps within an hour or after. But if we say Rivash holds where he remained he certainly swelled — it is not proven from his words that when he swelled immediately they do not testify; examine Kenesset HaGedolah 29a and 45a. Wound. In responsum Rashdam siman 54 he wrote water does not efface the wound unless the wound is on his body and not on his face. In responsum Rashak and responsum Mahari Weil they dispute this — see klal 174 siman 104. Darkei Moshe wrote the opposite — specifically when the wound is on the face of the face we say water effaces the wound; but when the wound is on the body and not on his face we do not say water effaces wound. On this agreed his words — see Beit Chadash. A wound made after his death — we do not say water effaces wound; and even if made in his lifetime if it healed we do not say water effaces wound — Choshen Mishpat. Likewise can prove from Rashi who wrote where there is a wound the place of the wound water makes heavy the pain and swells — end quote; and clear any wound that healed no longer pains even in water. In responsum Mahari Weil siman 63 not so — see there. They said water effaces wound only when cast in water alive; but when cast dead we do not say — Mahari Nachmi Ashkenazi brought in responsum Beit Yosef siman 12 on laws of gentile incidentally; and so Maharachash klal 174 and wrote all poskim agree on this.
No. Even for those poskim who hold if there was doubt whether he died within three days we follow leniency — they agree here we follow stringency because its measure is only one hour we follow stringency that it is more common he remained more than one hour — Ran. That we follow stringency in doubt of remaining is in water; but in snow and frost whenever there is doubt whether between killing and finding cold and freezing ceased a day or two we rely on leniency unless we know certainly cold and freezing ceased a day or two — Mahari Weil siman 104. Even witness from witness who saw him dead after they raised him from water we rely on leniency for the first saw him within the first hour — Maharival ch. 1 klal 1 siman 1 and 9 and R' Shmuel Levi and R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 8 and siman 12. See Kenesset HaGedolah 45 and 26a and 28a and 46b for he expanded on this. If it appears from his words the first witness did not see him at the time we do not rely for leniency — Mahari Weil siman 46.
