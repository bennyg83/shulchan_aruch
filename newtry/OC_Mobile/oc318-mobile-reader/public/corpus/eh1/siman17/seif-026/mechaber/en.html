They do not testify about him unless they found him within three days after his killing or after his death; but after three days they do not testify about him because the form of his face changes. In what case? At a time when he is on dry land; but if he drowned in water and the water cast him onto dry land — even after many days, if they recognized him they testify about him, for he does not change in water except after much time; and this is when they saw him immediately when they brought him up from the water; and also that there was no wound on him. But if he remained after being cast from the water — they do not testify about him (even within three) (Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban and Rashba). And likewise if there was a wound on him they do not testify about him, because water spoils the wound and it swells and changes. {Rama: Safek whether he delayed or not — we go stringently; and even if she married she must leave (Rivash siman 380). And all this is to testify about him by visual identification; but by prominent signs — even if he delayed they permit his wife (Tur and Beit Yosef).}
