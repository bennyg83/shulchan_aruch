The same applies to a gentile who said innocently: I killed him. We are not concerned that perhaps he said this only to intimidate, because it is not evident that his intent was to intimidate. This is unlike what is written in seif 14, where he said explicitly that it was in order to intimidate. Therefore, in every case of innocent speech, we are not concerned that he is lying or about other suspicions, so long as this is not evident from his words, neither beforehand nor afterward. Hagahot Maimoniyot, Kiddushin.
