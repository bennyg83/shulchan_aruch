There are those who say, etc.; but, etc.; or, etc. So wrote the Ra'avad in his glosses to chapter 4 of Hilchos Ishus. His reason is from Kesubos 23a: 'Let us say that they disagree concerning Rav Hamnuna.' This implies that even according to Rav Hamnuna, only where witnesses came after she married is she permitted, since she married permissibly because she had a migo. The Mishnah there teaches: 'But if there are witnesses,' etc.; even after the fact she must leave. The same follows according to the answer, 'No, all agree,' etc. All the more so regarding the kesubah, for she does not collect on her own statement except through the exposition of the kesubah, as written in chapter 15 of Yevamos.
