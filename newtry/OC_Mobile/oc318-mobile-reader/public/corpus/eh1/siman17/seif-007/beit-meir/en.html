And daughter of mother-in-law — Beit Shmuel: she says this inherits labor of my father and mother — this language also in Rashi and may Heaven protect in mishnah; Taz precisely from what we stated stringently — let us say daughter of mother-in-law — likewise daughter of father-in-law who is not daughter of mother-in-law — uncertain on Tur and Rama — should have mentioned mother-in-law coming; and this not in Tur; and what Beit Yosef brought from Rashba responsum siman 1253 — daughter of brother of husband not to her because concludes perhaps husband dies, etc. — end quote; and in my view forced per his words — if so all more forced per words of Orach Chaim above; and what he brought from Rosh responsum siman 51 — daughter of father-in-law not to her — also forced; and in my possession responsum of Gaon Rav Feivish who permitted and I was with him; and in Knesset HaGedolah 33b left for stringent mother-in-law coming law of daughter of mother-in-law and daughter of husband coming; and what Maharach Shach wrote siman 67 — learn from mother-in-law coming — likewise husband's daughter coming; and in my view all one reasoning; and what Orach Chaim wrote siman 67 — if we say mother-in-law coming included — daughter of husband coming all the more — end quote.
