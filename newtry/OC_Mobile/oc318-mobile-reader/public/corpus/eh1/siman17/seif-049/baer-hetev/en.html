War, etc. Explained in Gemara if unknown whether war is in the world need not ask her — Nimukei Yosef. If she presumed war and says I buried him she is believed even per Rambam where miggo if she wished she could say peace is in the world applies — Beit Shmuel — see there; see Beit Shmuel s.k. 156. If so even when war is in the world we should say if she says I buried him she is believed with miggo if she wished she could say died on his bed — this is not a good miggo since it is known war is in the world — Tosafot answered.
She leaves. See Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel. Kenesset HaGedolah 32a and 70a wrote scribal error in Tur that Ra'ash's words do not hold she leaves. Radbaz ch. 1 siman 219 wrote per Ra'ash if she married she leaves — and so Rif — see there.
