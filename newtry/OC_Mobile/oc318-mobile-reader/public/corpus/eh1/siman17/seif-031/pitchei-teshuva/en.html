And her husband's daughter. Kovetz Maharach Shach concludes that specifically a man's daughter hates her stepmother, because she commonly enters her father's house and constantly sees that this woman stands in her mother's place, uses her mother's clothing, and consumes all her mother's labor. But the daughter of his first wife from another man, who does not enter that house after her mother's death, is not suspected of such hatred and may testify. This is also cited in Chavas Yair to Machatzis HaShekel; see there.
