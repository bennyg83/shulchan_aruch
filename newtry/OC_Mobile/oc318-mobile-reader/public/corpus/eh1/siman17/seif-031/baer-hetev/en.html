Crucified. Beit Chadash wrote specifically if crucified by hands; but if crucified by his neck we say he was immediately strangled. Choshen Mishpat disputes for one can say the rope was cut and he fell — and so Taz disputes; see Mishpat Tzedek ch. 1 siman 50. One whose arms were broken and they threw him into the sea — his wife is forbidden — Maharival ch. 3 siman 51. A gentile incidentally says the ship sank and he and others with him drowned and one Jew was on that ship. A merchant saw one drew a knife and killed the Jew because he wished to ride on a plank the merchant was riding — this testimony has no substance even if there is double doubt — see there Tomer Yesharim siman 241, 249. But Ram Alshkar siman 111 and Mahari Bay Rav siman 16 dispute and hold on this testimony they marry his wife — see Kenesset HaGedolah 29b. They threw him into water while flayed — all testify — R' Ram Alshikh siman 122.
She leaves. Likewise if they stabbed him or shot arrows at him — if done in a place the soul would leave they testify; but if done where the soul would not leave even if he became treifah they do not testify even after twelve months — not like Beit Chadash; nevertheless Beit Shmuel. All this when he is not goses; but if goses Tosafot goses wrote if flayed they testify; but goses not flayed they do not testify; and if goses within three days they testify — so too Beit Yosef; and so Beit Yosef.
His heart. Likewise they testified they struck him with a staff on his skull until brain emerged — his wife is permitted — Rashak ch. 1 siman 74.
