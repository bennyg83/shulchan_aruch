And her yevamah. Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilchos Gerushin, states that her yevamah is disqualified even if she is her sister. The same appears in the Shulchan Aruch below, siman 141, seif 54. Based on this, Tevuos Shor writes that even where both yevamos have sons and there is no present concern of yibbum, she nevertheless may not testify, because the Sages did not distinguish and because she still fears that her sons may die and the other woman will become her rival wife. Mishneh LaMelech discusses this at length and ultimately concludes in the name of the Rosh that one should be stringent in practice. See Machatzis HaShekel, s.k. 23, for his discussion.
