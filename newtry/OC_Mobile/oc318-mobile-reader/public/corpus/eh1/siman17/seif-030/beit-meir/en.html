And some say that where he mentions only his name, etc. In the Rambam, chapter 13, he wrote: until the witness testifies that he is so-and-so and recognizes his name and the name of his town. Maharik also copied it in this language. But in the Amsterdam print before me, at the end of chapter 14 of Yevamot, the language is: we do not testify about him until they mention his name and his father's name, or his name and the name of his town. If so, this is exactly the view of these "some say." According to this, however, there is a slight difficulty: what was Abaye concerned about? Was Yitzchak Reish Galuta bar Amatya of Rav Bivi not considered like his name and his father's name as in the Tosefta? This requires study.
