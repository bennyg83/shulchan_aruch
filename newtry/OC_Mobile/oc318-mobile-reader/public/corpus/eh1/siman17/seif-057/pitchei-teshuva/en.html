A minor. See Baer Heitev, who wrote in the name of Perishah, etc. See Beit Yosef, for initially it occurred to him to explain the Tur's words this way, and afterward he rejected this explanation; the Perishah upholds this explanation. See Machatzit HaShekel s.k. 43, what he wrote about this, concluding that the words of Beit Yosef are primary. See there further, where he brought in the name of Yam shel Shlomo siman 14, who inferred from the Rambam's language in his commentary to the Mishnah that when a child says only, so-and-so died, even if he also says there were such-and-such eulogizers there, it does not help, since he did not say that he himself was involved in the matter of the eulogy, like the Mishnah's case, we are coming from eulogizing. The matter depends on an act of the minor himself. In Kuntres Acharon there he brought that R. Chaim Shabtai z"l also inferred this from the Rambam's words, and there is proof for this from Ketubot 28b: a child is believed to say, etc., regarding himself but not regarding another; see there. However, the conclusion of Yam shel Shlomo there is that it is not necessary for the matter to depend on an act of the minor himself, and he proved this from the language of Or Zarua in the name of Raavan and Raavyah. Rabbi Machatzit HaShekel discusses this and concludes that the law requires study; see there.
