Chelkat Mechokek sign 91 — that she is not believed to forbid. Nb: Nevertheless one may say only through contradiction to contradict the witness who says died; but without contradiction — such as witnesses testify these were his signs and it is fit to permit her — and these women say he had other signs and therefore this is not her husband — one may say she is believed to forbid her; responsa Noda B'Yehuda ch. 2 ch. EH siman 64.
