He is not a mamzer, and some say that he is a mamzer rabbinically. Chelkat Mechokek asked from what is written in the previous seif, that the child from the second man after the first died is a mamzer rabbinically. He overlooked the words of the Rosh in Perek HaIshah Rabbah, who wrote in the name of some that where the husband returns, since the sages penalized her that the child from him is a mamzer, they also penalized her regarding the second man, that the child from him is a mamzer even after the husband’s death or after he divorced her. But where the husband died overseas, since they did not penalize her regarding the husband, they also did not penalize her regarding the second man.
