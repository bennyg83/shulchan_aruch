This one says, etc., and her rival contradicts her and says, he did not die. This one may marry and the rival may not. Even if the rival wants to retract and say that he died, with an explanation that she said he did not die only to leave the rival an agunah, nevertheless she may not leave. Tosafot, s.v. Salka da’atach amina, wrote that this is not comparable to a woman who says, I am married, who is believed to retract and say, I am unmarried, with an explanation. Meaning, it is not comparable because there the explanation is needed only to uproot her first words, and she is automatically permitted; but here, even after uprooting her words, she is not permitted by her rival’s testimony, but only through her own later testimony, and this is a reversal from one extreme to the other, so she is not believed.
