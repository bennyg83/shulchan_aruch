At the time, etc., even though, etc. As stated in Sanhedrin 61b: It was stated concerning one who worships, etc.; we rule like Rava. See Tosafos there, s.v. 'and Rava, although,' etc., who answer, etc.; likewise in Shabbos 72b, s.v. 'Rava,' etc. The Rambam likewise ruled in chapter 20 of Hilchos Sanhedrin, halacha 2. He is also exempt from kares, as written in Kerisos: 'that person,' excluding one under compulsion. He is even exempt from lashes, as the Rambam wrote there, for it says, 'But to the maiden you shall do nothing,' as explained in chapter 3 of Nedarim: the Merciful One exempts one under compulsion, etc. This is what Rava means by entirely exempt. He is not even disqualified rabbinically, as stated in the chapter HaShole'ach, Gittin 45b, concerning a convert who returned to his former ways; see Yoreh De'ah, siman 119, se'if 9. Although afterward they could escape and would then become disqualified, since when they witnessed the matter they were under compulsion, their beginning and end were in valid status, like a son-in-law, a blind person, and similar cases in chapter 9 of Bava Basra.
