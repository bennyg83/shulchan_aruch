Seif 34 — gloss: they did not testify that he remained in water until his soul departed. Nb: This measure in responsa of Rivash siman 378 — 489 hours which is three hours; and so Maharaz Sashon siman 1; and Maharan Nachmeish in responsa Makor Baruch siman 12; and in responsa Pnei Yehoshua siman 7; and see responsa of Bach siman 79; and in responsa Perach Mateh Aharon siman 20; and in responsa Motza Me'esh siman 2.
