They found, etc. A dispute in Yerushalmi — Rif and Rosh brought it; and ch. like one who permits — for so the Gemara holds in ch. Mi she-Achzo; and as stated seif 12 — Ramban, Rashba, and Shulchan Aruch; and Beit Shmuel wrote that in Yerushalmi there is no resolution to rule like one who permits from that Gemara — they dispute in Yerushalmi regarding remembrance of words and in Gittin regarding actual testimony; truly one who permits permits even for remembrance of words — he brings proof from "from their mouths and not from their writing"; and there it is about remembrance of words and the like, as Tosafot Yevamos 31b s.v. de-chazu questioned; and likewise regarding testimony — for they collect with a document in beit din without testimony; but one who forbids forbids even with a complete document and is stricter than monetary law, as stated there; and the mishnah supports R' Bun bar Kahana — "the power of witnesses is greater than the power of a document and the power of a document is greater than the power of witnesses": witnesses who said "so-and-so died, so-and-so was killed" — they permit his wife; "they found written in a document so-and-so died, so-and-so was killed" — they do not permit his wife; and "the power of the document is greater than the power of witnesses" — one who lends to his fellow with witnesses collects from unsold property; with a document from liened property; therefore with a complete document; and this is what is stated initially regarding permitting; and no difficulty regarding monetary law that we do not collect from their writing — we say in ch. Mi she-Achzo that we are lenient in a woman's testimony; and we validate on the basis of writing; therefore not like him; and what Beit Shmuel questioned on Rambam — he follows his reason from Gittin that even a mute person helps; but the poskim dispute him, as stated siman 120 seif 5 — he did not analyze: the Gemara is arranged that for testimony of death writing helps, as stated; and per Tosafot there s.v. ketani — perhaps for one who does not hold Rav even for other testimony it helps from the writing even without us ruling like them — nevertheless here no one disputes the baraita.
