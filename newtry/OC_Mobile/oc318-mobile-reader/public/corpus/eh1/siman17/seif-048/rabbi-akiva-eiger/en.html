Seif 36 — gloss: she shall not marry. Nb: And regarding kesubah it appears she does not collect — for it is like one witness testifying that he drowned in water without end; see Chelkat Mechokek sign 65 and Beit Shmuel sign 107; and so in Shev Shmaiteta; but in my opinion it is not comparable — for there in truth we are concerned he is alive; but here we judge he certainly died and only because of levity of speech she shall not marry — one may say she collects kesubah.
