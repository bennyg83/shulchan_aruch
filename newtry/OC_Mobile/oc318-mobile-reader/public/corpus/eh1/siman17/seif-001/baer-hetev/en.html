Rashba siman 1164. The wife of Elijah the prophet or of R' Yehoshua ben Levi may marry; and there is a practical difference for generations whether one will merit like them — Darkei Moshe in name of Maharai. If one woman died under her husband — definite death — and was revived by a prophet like Elijah for the son of the Tzarfatite woman or Elisha for the son of the Shunammite woman, the husband's bond did not cease from her and she cannot marry another; and there is support from the incident of the wife of R' Chanina ben Chachinai — Ketubot 62b; examine Kenesset HaGedolah. The wife of a Sadducee — per Ra'am ch. 1 siman 94 — is a full married woman and one who lies with her is liable for married woman; likewise betrothal does not take if another betrothed her even an Israelite. And per Radbaz brought in Moharar Betzalel Ashkenazi siman 3 and Rashak ch. 3 siman 15 she is not a married woman and betrothal takes; see Kenesset HaGedolah.
