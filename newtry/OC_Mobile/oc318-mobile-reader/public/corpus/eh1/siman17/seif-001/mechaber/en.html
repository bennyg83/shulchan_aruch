Permitting a married woman whose husband died, and she says "he divorced me," and the laws of forbidden relations and the law of one who married in prohibited circumstances and in error. It contains 58 seifim. A married woman is among the forbidden relations and kiddushin do not take effect in her — in what case? In the case of a certain married woman; but if she is safek betrothed or safek divorced, kiddushin take effect in her from doubt and she requires a get from both of them; and likewise regarding rabbinically invalid gittin — if another came and betrothed her, she requires a get from both of them — from the first rabbinically and from the second from the Torah.
