Some disagree and hold that we require, etc. Their reason is that whenever she is not literally in his presence, she is not embarrassed. This resembles Nedarim 65: one who made a vow, etc., only in his presence. Tosafos explain that it means literally in his presence, and the Yerushalmi says that the reason is embarrassment. All the poskim, however, disputed this and wrote that it is enough that he know of it. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 258. Addendum.
