The woman herself is believed to say her husband died and she may marry or perform yibum on her word; and they give her the principal of her ketubah. If she performs yibum the yavam enters the inheritance on her word (and even a woman who is a fool is believed) (Teshuvot HaRosh niddah 23) (provided she knows the nature of marriage and widowhood) (there in the name of the Rama). In what case? When she came to court and said "my husband died, permit me" and did not mention the ketubah — they permit her to marry and make her swear and give her ketubah. But if she came and said "my husband died, give me my ketubah" — they do not even permit her to marry; for she came regarding ketubah matters and behold she is presumed not to have died, and her intention is not to marry but only to take her ketubah from him while alive (and some say even if she has a witness that he died it does not help in such a case) (Nimukei Yosef in the name of Ritva).
