One witness says, etc. The Mechaber's language; and he learned from responsa of Rosh kelal 51 siman 7 — two witnesses testified her husband died but one testified her son was occupied with his burial and buried him and one testified her son was not at his father's grave but wrote that his father died and replied: since they testify to his death we are not concerned with their contradiction in other matters, as stated above seif 17 in the mishnah "one says died and one says," etc.; and we rule like R' Yosi and R' Shimon; but in truth this is a great error — for from the mishnah the opposite is proved; the reason of R' Yosi and R' Shimon is because a rival wife is not believed regarding her fellow wife, as stated above seif 66; and in the Gemara "let R' Meir dispute," etc. — R' Elazar in a dispute, etc.; evidently their dispute is whether a rival wife testifies for her fellow; and which student erred — they wrote; and the listener heard and erred; Rosh permitted there for a different reason because they contradict in examinations, as stated above seif 21; but when rivals contradict in interrogations their testimony is void, as Chelkat Mechokek wrote siman 34 seif 62; and what Beit Shmuel wrote — it is like R' Yoel in Tosafot there s.v. de-kol, etc. — "but this one says," etc. — "since both," etc. — his words are astounding: he wrote only for R' Meir; but for R' Elazar R' Meir holds the rival wife contradicts them in all cases; and R' Yochanan holds specifically in this case; but for R' Yosi and R' Shimon in all cases their contradiction counts for nothing; and it is resolved in Yerushalmi explicitly there — R' Elazar concedes to R' Yosi and R' Shimon regarding witnesses; what difference between witnesses and a rival wife — they did nothing with the rival wife's words regarding her fellow; R' Yochanan said: who said R' Elazar? From when she heard and said; and see the sugya — R' Yochanan concedes to R' Meir at first; it teaches "she too is in dispute"; what difference between second and first — they did nothing with this regarding her fellow; R' Elazar concedes to R' Yosi and R' Shimon, etc.; as above — meaning for R' Meir only in this did they do nothing; and for R' Yosi and R' Shimon in all cases they did nothing; and there Rama and R' Yochanan both hold R' Meir concedes in the first case and both hold R' Yochanan and R' Shimon concede regarding witnesses — unlike Beit Shmuel.
