She may marry. Even if they did not permit her — Ra'ash.
Valid witnesses. If a hundred say died and two say did not die — she may not marry and she leaves unless she married one of her witnesses; if two say died and three say did not die — nevertheless if she married one of her witnesses she does not leave. But with disqualified witnesses if two say died and three say did not die even if she married one of her witnesses she leaves — Beit Yosef.
