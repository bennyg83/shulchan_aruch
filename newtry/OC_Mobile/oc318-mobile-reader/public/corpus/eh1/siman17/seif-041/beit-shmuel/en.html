If two testify that they heard from one. Meaning, each one heard from one person. So it is in responsum Ran, and so wrote responsum Maharam Lublin siman 133 and Chelkat Mechokek. But if many heard from one that he died, and one heard from one that he is alive, it is like one against one. It is explained there that we are concerned that the many heard from one person when they did not say that they heard from many. It is implied there that if one heard from many, even if the many are not here, nevertheless this is better than one who said he heard from one, provided that the one who heard from many says that the many themselves saw it, not that they heard it. This is unlike Chelkat Mechokek, who wrote that one witness from the mouth of many and one witness from the mouth of an individual are equal whenever the many are not before us.
