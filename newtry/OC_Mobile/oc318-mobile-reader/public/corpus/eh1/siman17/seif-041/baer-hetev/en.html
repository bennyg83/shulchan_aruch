That each heard. Namely each of them heard from one. So in responsum Ran — teaches just as in testimony itself we follow majority of opinions so too with witness from witness two who are witness from witness contradict witness from witness one. Specifically when the two come from two; but a hundred who heard from one count as one. Likewise witness testifying from many and one witness from one — they are equal since the many are not before us — Choshen Mishpat. Beit Shmuel wrote specifically if many heard from one they count as one; but if one heard from many preferable to one who said he heard from one — see there. What Choshen Mishpat wishes to prove otherwise let it teach novelty one from two and not two from two — see there. It seems therefore it stated two from two to teach; but two from one is like one and if it stated one from two we would not know two from one; simple.
