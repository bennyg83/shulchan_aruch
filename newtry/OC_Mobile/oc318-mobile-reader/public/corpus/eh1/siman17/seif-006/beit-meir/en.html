Gloss: even if not now mother-in-law — Rif and Rama omitted this; Beit Yosef wrote their view to be lenient; in my humble view their reason beyond what Beit Yosef explained — they hold: but in Tiberias it was required and they did not descend to analyze count of five women — not Talmud's method; therefore rejected plainness in "perhaps" and, etc. — since they descended to analyze as they analyzed earlier — cannot be eight — certainly great forced difficulty — mother-in-law coming is included in her mother-in-law — if so yevamah also should be in rival — therefore they relied on plainness of baraita; we find many times they rely on plain meaning as if question resolved; and so Knesset HaGedolah from Maharach Shach — this is view of Shulchan Aruch except R' Yosi and Hagahot Maimoniyot in question of daughter of father-in-law who is not daughter of mother-in-law. And in responsum Orach Chaim siman 67 wrote — learn from mother-in-law coming Rama's method likewise; and this is reason of husband's daughter coming — daughter of brother of husband not to her because concludes in her mind perhaps husband dies and she falls before yevam her father and eats mother's portion and hates her and intends ruin now; and as we need to say per stringent on mother-in-law coming she is in all mother-in-law so count five women intends — so say daughter of yevamah is in all daughter of husband because she is daughter of husband coming; and wrote: no distinction between mother-in-law coming and husband's daughter coming — mother-in-law hates her because of her own portion — therefore concludes more in mind perhaps husband dies because from then she is strict about her portion; unlike daughter of husband who hates because eats mother's portion — does not conclude perhaps husband dies — explanation we already wrote: if we say mother-in-law coming not to her included in mishnah's mother-in-law or not — mother-in-law of first meaning even mother-in-law coming; also daughter of mother-in-law meaning daughter of my mother-in-law even coming — forced difficulty mother-in-law of first and second meaning not equal; if so daughter of mother-in-law coming not to her — more so daughter of husband coming not to her — end quote (and see there brings in name of Gaon Rav Feivish who permitted and in my possession he was with him). And so Knesset HaGedolah from Maharach Shach 33b left for stringent on mother-in-law coming law of daughter of mother-in-law and daughter of husband coming; and wrote: and let us say revealed in name of Peri Chadash; and in responsum Tzitz Eliezer vol. 1 wrote — revealed from Tur siman 158 to daughter of husband coming — see there because I rejected well no revelation there; see siman 118 I brought Rosh's words in responsum and supported from mishnah of hands — we learned do not judge words of scribes one from another — therefore in my humble view to find novel law in daughter of mother-in-law and daughter of husband coming — we compare mother-in-law coming certainly difficult; especially Orach Chaim himself divides in reasoning and nothing remains except precision word mother-in-law should be equal — uncertain greatly to lean on this; one may say what is reasonable we include and expand word mother-in-law; and what not reasonable not; and so we find many times in derashah of verse; and in my view worth poskim's sealing to rely on them to be lenient in pressing need and nowadays when they do not perform levirate; see Choshen Mishpat and Orach Chaim there.
