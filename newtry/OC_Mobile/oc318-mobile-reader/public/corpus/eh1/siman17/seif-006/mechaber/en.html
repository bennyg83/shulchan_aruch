At the time of a decree they permit women based on penitents who testify what they saw at a time of distress even though they converted their religion under duress (responsum of the Rosh kelal 54). {Rama: Empty apostates and reckless ones who returned in repentance but not complete repentance — one should not rely on their testimony except when they speak offhandedly (Mahari in his writings siman 210) (or when it is known they testified without deceit and distortion) (there siman 224) — and all according to the nature of the witness and according to what the judge sees.}
