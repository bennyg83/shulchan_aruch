Who testify to what they saw at the time of the calamity. This means that when they saw the event they had become apostates under compulsion; afterward they could have escaped and nevertheless remained there, and at that point they were disqualified from testifying because they remained voluntarily. Later they repented, and thus their beginning and end are in fitness, even though in the interval they were disqualified. The Taz is lenient about this and writes that only regarding a complete wicked person do we require that his beginning and end be in fitness. But one who became an apostate under compulsion, even if he ate forbidden foods for appetite at the time he saw the event, is fit to testify once he repents. He brings proof from the writings of Mahari, siman 201. See Responsa Ben Sasson, siman 50.
