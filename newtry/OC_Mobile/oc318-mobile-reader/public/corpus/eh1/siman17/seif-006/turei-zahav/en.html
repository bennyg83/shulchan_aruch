Through penitents. In Rosh responsum: if known they were proper men before the decree and now returned properly; if they sinned under compulsion of souls and when they found to flee they returned immediately they were not disqualified; even if they found escape and did not act and ate nevelah for appetite and were disqualified to testify on what they saw at that hour; nevertheless what they testify about the hour of killing was during the decree while still among non-Jews and at that hour they were fit; even if afterward they ate nevelah and afterward returned; beginning and end in fitness as one who knew testimony before he became son-in-law and became son-in-law and his daughter died; we say fit for testimony; end quote; behold their testimony helps only what they saw at beginning of decree before eating nevelah; but what they saw after being among them; as disqualified at that hour disqualified forever even after repentance for beginning in disqualification and end in fitness; therefore the ruling authority must permit through penitents to investigate when they saw the killing of the one testified about; if at beginning of decree when this witness was not yet disqualified through eating forbidden foods then specifically he can testify; if so Rama abbreviated where he should explain; but it is wondrous if here for woman's testimony every testimony requires beginning and end in fitness; why are slave and maidservant fit for this testimony for they are disqualified for testimony; likewise regarding punishment from their mouths not from their writing; for woman fit as written seif 14; although because of agunah they were lenient whoever is not disqualified due to Torah sin; likewise for testimony of one compelled after repentance at least he is good in himself and in any event no worse than slave and maidservant since now fit for testimony if he sees any matter even Torah testimony; also Rama's language what they saw at time of decree implies even not at beginning of decree but even what they saw after becoming apostates; further in writings of Maharai siman 221: regarding intentional wicked like forced such we require beginning and end in fitness; this witness returns at time he was complete wicked, etc.; clear from this specifically complete wicked requires beginning also in fitness; not apostate due to compulsion who ate nevelah for appetite suffices for woman's testimony after repentance; see responsum R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 90; main law one may not permit through penitents unless known he was not complete wicked at time of compulsion to transgress intentionally great sins such as murder; then certainly his testimony what he saw at that time even if afterward complete repentance unless incidentally like non-Jew (see responsum Ben Lev ch. 1 siman 45 and Agunah of a Woman 5).
