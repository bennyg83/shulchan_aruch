In time of distress. Meaning when they saw they were forced apostates under compulsion and afterward could escape but remained there — then they were disqualified to testify since they remained willingly; and afterward repented — it is beginning and end in fitness even though they were disqualified in between. And Taz is lenient and wrote we require only a complete wicked person beginning and end in fitness; but a forced apostate even if he ate nevelah for appetite at the time he saw and repented and testifies — fit to testify — so too Kenesset HaGedolah in Beit Yosef's gloss seif 21 and above s.k. 15; see R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 50.
Tum'ah. Karaites in our time their law is like a gentile and incidentally in Beit Yosef.
