Even though he is in the city. See Chelkas Mechokek, s.k. 18, and Responsa Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Choshen Mishpat, siman 8. It is explained there that even according to Chelkas Mechokek, who holds that the court must send for the witness, this applies only where the woman tells the beis din that she heard the report from a particular witness. But if she merely says that she heard her husband died, the witness need not testify before the beis din. Responsa Shivas Tzion, at the end of siman 81, writes likewise; see there.
