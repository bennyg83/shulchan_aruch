And likewise she may not testify for them. See Machatzit HaShekel, s.k. 24, who wrote the total of women presumed hostile who do not testify: sixteen. They are: her mother-in-law; her mother-in-law's daughter; her rival wife; her yevamah; her husband's daughter; her daughter-in-law; her brother's wife; her father's wife; her father-in-law's daughter; her brother's wife from the father but not from the mother; the mother of her yavam; the daughter of the mother of her yavam; the daughter of her yavam; the wife of her son's paternal brother; the wife of the brother's brother, where they are brothers from the father but not from the mother, even if she is a sister from the mother and not from the father; and the wife of her father's brother. All this is explained according to the words of Rama above, that she too may not testify for them. If so, to the count of the five women mentioned above, another three are added: corresponding to her mother-in-law, her mother-in-law's daughter, and her husband's daughter, their inverses are added, namely her daughter-in-law, her brother's wife, and her father's wife. According to the view of Maharchash and Machatzit HaShekel above in s.k. 28, that one should be stringent regarding the unresolved question of the daughter of her father-in-law who is not the daughter of her mother-in-law, two more are added: her father-in-law's daughter and its inverse, her brother's wife from the father but not from the mother. According to the view of the Rosh, Tur, and Rama, who ruled stringently regarding the unresolved question of a future mother-in-law, two more are added: the mother of her yavam and its inverse, the wife of her son's paternal brother. According to Responsa Avodat HaGershuni, Tzemach Tzedek, and Beit Shmuel, whom I cited above in s.k. 27, the same applies to a future mother-in-law's daughter and a future husband's daughter, adding four more: the daughter of the mother of her yavam, the daughter of her yavam, and their inverses, the wife of the brother's brother, where they are brothers from the father but not from the mother even if she is a sister only from the mother, and the wife of her father's brother. In all, they are sixteen; the details of each law have already been mentioned above.
