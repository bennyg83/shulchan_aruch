They saw him fall into the sea — even if he drowned in the Great Sea — they do not testify about him that he died, lest he emerged from another place. But if he fell into enclosed waters such as a pit or cave where one stands and sees all around him and he remained long enough for his soul to depart and he did not come up — they testify about him that he died and they permit his wife. And likewise if they tied his legs and lowered him into the sea and they could not bring him up except his leg from the knee and above — they permit his wife after twelve months, for a fish does not live twelve months. But if he fell into the sea and they cast a net and brought up one leg from the knee and above and the like — they do not permit his wife; for I say it is another man's leg. However, if there was on it a most prominent sign on his leg — they rely on it to say it is the leg of the man who fell (and some say even a prominent sign on his garments helps here, since they saw him drown in these garments) (Maharam Padua siman 36).
