There sign 79 — and there is no wound. Nb: It appears that even if there is a wound it is so — for regarding a wound made after death we do not say the water erodes it, as Chelkat Mechokek wrote sign 6; and Beit Yosef's view in responsa siman 12 — if thrown into water after death there is no difference whether there is a wound or not; we do not say "his flesh was severed and the water does not erode a wound" and it depends on three days like initially.
