From another place. Taz wrote there was a case of one who went on the water on the ice and there was one open place and he fell into that open place and drowned and the water had no end and the sages of the generation permitted his wife; their reason since the water was frozen his law is like water that has an end. One bound in iron chains inside the ship. Likewise one whose feet were tied to the ship's plank and the ship sank — there is room for leniency — Binyamin Ze'ev siman 26 and 28, Ram Alshkar siman 159, Ram Metrani ch. 1 siman 188, Kenesset HaGedolah 44a.
His soul. The measure until the soul leaves is three hours — Rivash siman 375, R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 1. Maharachash was uncertain if until beginning of the third hour or its end and concluded until beginning of third hour; but Mahari Weil ch. 2 siman 26 wrote the measure until soul leaves is a light hour — see there. They testify about him if he remained until soul would leave and it did not even by moonlight — R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 11, Radbaz siman 34. If the witness is not before us or he is witness from witness and the first is not before us we rely he remained there until soul would leave — Kenesset HaGedolah in name of many poskim 48a — see there. See what Taz wrote there s.k. 108. Doubt of remaining in drowning if he remained upon him measure until soul leaves — Rivash siman 375 wrote there is room for leniency; and so Ram Alshkar siman 25, Maharar Betzalel siman 21, Kenesset HaGedolah 48a.
