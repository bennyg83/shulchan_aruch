If she married, etc. The Rashba proved the reverse from Kesubos 22b, 'Would she be so brazen?' See his responsa, siman 1,253. Addendum. If, etc.; and there is one who says, etc. This requires further study. Kesubos 23a apparently does not imply so, for it says, 'Let us say they disagree concerning Rav Hamnuna,' before concluding that she did not literally marry. Otherwise, it should have stated that answer earlier. See Tosafos there, 26b, s.v. 'we.' Nor is there a presumption that a woman does not brazenly lie, etc.; so Tosafos wrote in Bava Basra 32a, s.v. 'we,' etc.
