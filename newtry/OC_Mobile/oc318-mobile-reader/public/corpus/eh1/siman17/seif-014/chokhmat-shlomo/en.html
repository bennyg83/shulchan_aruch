Seif 15 — A gentile who spoke offhandedly, etc. See in Beit Shmuel s.k. 41 who brought opinions whether it requires in that same setting or not; and I in my edition 8 in my responsum on laws of Gittin in my composition siman 69 wrote it appears to me it requires specifically in that same setting. And behold now it appears to me proof for this from the Gemara Bava Kamma (114b) where Rav Ashi says mesalfei lefi tumo is fit only for woman's testimony alone; Ravina said to R' Elazar — but behold a swarm of bees, etc.; and afterward challenges there further from an incident with a certain man, etc.; and why does it challenge — let us say R' Elazar's intent that for woman's testimony it is fit means even if once he was mesalfei lefi tumo then he is fit even if after days he tells through a question; but for other prohibitions it does not help if he is not mesalfei lefi tumo in that same setting; but with a swarm of bees and with this one who left the school — since it is in that same setting he is fit even for Torah law; perforce if not in that same setting it is not reasonable to say he would be called mesalfei lefi tumo and be fit for woman's testimony; and this is clear proof in my humble opinion with God's help; examine well.
