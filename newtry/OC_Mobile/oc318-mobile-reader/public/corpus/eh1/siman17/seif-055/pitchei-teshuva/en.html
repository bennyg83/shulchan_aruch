One who became mute. See the addendum to Machatzis HaShekel on this seif, who writes in the name of the Rosh that a deaf-mute who neither hears nor speaks cannot testify concerning a woman's husband's death, neither through nodding nor through writing, just as in the laws of gittin. He further writes that one who speaks but cannot hear is valid for such testimony. Therefore, in the following seif the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch did not list a deaf person together with a mentally incompetent person and a minor, as they commonly do elsewhere; see there.
