Doubt. Namely even though she married with permission on the first two she leaves from permission on the later witnesses for we do not say she does not leave except with disqualified witnesses but when later ones are valid they remove her for two and two is doubt and a woman remains in presumption of married woman and one who lies with her is liable doubtful guilt offering — Choshen Mishpat. Not to permit on one witness there through permission the first witness becomes like two and it would be as if no doubt — see Kenesset HaGedolah 31a. Likewise even where we say she does not leave if they permitted her nevertheless she may not marry lekhatchilah because of wanton speech as written seif 37. However regarding captive woman permission helps even though captive witnesses came after permission she is permitted for their testimony does not contradict the permission for it can be though she was captive she did not commit adultery — therefore permission helps there if captive impurity witnesses did not come — end quote.
