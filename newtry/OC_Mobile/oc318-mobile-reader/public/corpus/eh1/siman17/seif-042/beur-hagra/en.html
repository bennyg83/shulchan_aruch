'that they saw,' etc. The Mechaber omitted the Gemara's requirement that they see a shadow of a shadow, following the Beit Yosef's report that the Maggid Mishneh questioned why the Rambam omitted all of this section. The Beit Yosef answered that he follows Rabbeinu Yeruham in the name of the Gaon, that nowadays we are not expert in identifying a shadow of a shadow and therefore do not act upon it. These words are astonishing, and the Beit Yosef later retracted them in the Kesef Mishneh, for the Rambam is more lenient and wrote in chapter 2 of Hilchos Gerushin that even a shadow of a shadow is unnecessary. The Kesef Mishneh explains that he follows the Nimukei Yosef in the name of the Ritva, that after the Tannaic teaching of Rabbi Yishmael was brought, we no longer suspect a demon, just as we do not suspect a rival wife. The Tur holds that Rabbi Yishmael's teaching refers only to a rival wife; this is also the view of the Ramban, Rashba, Maggid Mishneh, and many commentators. See below, siman 141, se'if 19.
