The witness is a slave. See Baer Heitev and Chelkas Mechokek, who writes that perhaps a slave is valid even without qualification, for a Jewish transgressor is worse than a slave even though the slave too is presumed to be a transgressor. See Machatzis HaShekel, s.k. 13, who writes that the words of Beis Shmuel are not convincing and concludes in accordance with Chelkas Mechokek; see there.
