Her ketubah. See Beit Shmuel who raised if two witnesses testified he drowned in water without end she collects ketubah even if she has not yet married. But if she has one witness she collects ketubah only if she already married; if not — see there. His words do not hold well per what Kenesset HaGedolah explained 88b seif 572 — one that Beit Shmuel decides Rashba deals only with one already married is not necessary as explained Kenesset HaGedolah there. Further Beit Shmuel wrote Ra'avad disputes this law — difficult to his words for if Rashba deals only with one married Ra'avad does not dispute; one can say Ra'avad deals with one not married as explained all this Kenesset HaGedolah there. On 49a Kenesset HaGedolah raised most poskim hold she does not collect ketubah and dispute these — see seif 43 this siman. A woman who cannot marry due to yavam's bond — all agree she does not collect ketubah — Rashak ch. 3 siman 176 and R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 29.
