See Beit Shmuel brought in the name of Maharival that Elijah's wife or R' Yehudah bar Liva'i is permitted, for she is not called a married woman but an angel's wife; and in my humble opinion this does not appear so — for if so there is difficulty on the plain sugya in chapter 4 of Eruvin that there are techumim above ten handbreadths from the baraita "I am a nazir on the day Ben David comes," etc.; and afterward it challenges there on one Shabbat to permit, and what proof from Elijah — behold among the dead he is free and is exempt from all mitzvot; also what did Rabbah challenge in chapter HaMekabel in Bava Metzia — "my master is not a kohen," behold he is an angel and is not obligated in fulfillment of Torah — perforce since he did not die he is judged as a person as if alive and is considered a person and not an angel, and automatically his wife is forbidden; and this is clear proof in my humble opinion. And see what I wrote in the sugya of Eruvin there in my composition on Orach Chaim laws of Eruvin part 95 — see there and examine.
