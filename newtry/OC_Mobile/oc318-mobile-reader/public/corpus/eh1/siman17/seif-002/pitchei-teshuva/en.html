And she accepted kiddushin. See responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 204 who wrote: also one may say to infer from sugya end of Gittin — if rumor emerged one betrothed her and another betrothed in his presence — if found first kiddushin were certain kiddushin — no get needed from second; and R' Huna disputes; per his words from where for Talmud to say they dispute — perhaps speaks when known she is his wife — then not believed to say "you divorced me" — perforce no distinction; see there.
