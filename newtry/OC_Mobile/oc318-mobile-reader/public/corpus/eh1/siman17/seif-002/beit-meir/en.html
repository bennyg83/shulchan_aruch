See Choshen Mishpat — what Beit Yosef wondered on R' Yonah: it seems if he contradicted her she is not believed — but this is certainly not so regarding hazakah, etc. In Hagahot Rama it states: some say further in this time when brazenness is common — or rather for stringency. It appears to me that when there is a convincing excuse proving she speaks truth — even in our time she is believed, as stated siman 154 seif 7 gloss 2 — one law and one reason; both stringencies of Maharam emerge. One may say R' Yonah also already adopted Maharam's stringency for nowadays — therefore he did not believe her except when an excuse joined because he did not contradict; and he has good support from the incident of Rabbenu Tam in Tosafot Gittin 92b s.v. halakhah — see there. In my view this is correct for law.
