Beit Yosef in name of Rabbeinu Yerucham: a married woman who extended her hand and accepted betrothal from another before her husband; betrothed and needs a get from the second if she remains with the first; if the first will not divorce she is betrothed to the second and permitted to him since it was before her husband and he did not protest; likewise as saying she was divorced from me; end quote. Wonder why he wrote and he did not protest; implies if he protested she is not believed; but certainly presumption a woman does not brazen applies even with protestor; end quote Beit Yosef; certainly plain as his words for even protestor this presumption helps; explicit proof from ch. 2 Ketubot 22: if her husband comes and says I did not divorce you she says you divorced me; they challenge is any woman so brazen; Rav Hamnuna said a woman who said to her husband you divorced me; presumption a woman does not brazen before her husband; they answer this is where there are no witnesses supporting her, etc.; behold explicitly where she is believed is even if he contradicts her. Moharar wrote to resolve Rabbeinu Yerucham when husband protests she is not believed but she made herself a piece of prohibition and needs get from second if she remains with first, etc.; I say when he sleeps and awakens he said this Talmudic matter as Beit Yosef as we recalled; further even per his words she made herself prohibition automatically forbidden to remain with first for he returns his divorcee after betrothal to another; prohibition applies; therefore no reliance on these words; incidentally in distraction of sugya he was not precise; plain to me scribal error in Rabbeinu Yerucham in what he wrote if she remains and must read also she may not remain with first; that copy of Rabbeinu Yerucham is scribal error in many places.
