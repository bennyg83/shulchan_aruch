Hazakah: a woman does not act brazenly. Rambam ch. 10 halakhah 3 wrote: when the husband says "I did not divorce you" and she says "you divorced me" — she is believed; and if the husband says "I divorced you" and she admits to him — she is not believed and we do not say in such a case "a woman does not act brazenly" since he supports her. And if she began to say "you divorced me" and afterward he supports her and says "I divorced you" — the law is not explicit; and Choshen Mishpat is uncertain. However per the reason Rambam wrote — and Tur siman 152 brought his wording — he wrote one reason: if he says "I divorced you" one may say perhaps he divorced with invalid get; therefore even if she began and says "you divorced me" and he says "I divorced you" — there is still concern perhaps it was invalid get and she is not divorced; only when he says "I did not divorce you" — therefore even if she accepted kiddushin from another she is not betrothed unless he says "I did not divorce you" — for otherwise still concern perhaps invalid get; see siman 152. Binyamin Ze'ev wrote: if she accepted kiddushin from another in his presence — she is permitted to her husband after the betrother divorces her; and apparently wonder — behold concern perhaps they say he returns a divorcee after betrothal; and so Darkei Moshe challenged and answered: if she accepted and did not say her husband divorced her — permitted to first husband after this one's divorce; and we do not find this distinction; also end of Gittin does not imply thus; also what Beit Shmuel and Bach wrote — not so; and Choshen Mishpat already challenged him.
In her husband's presence. And if she began to say not in her husband's presence and afterward her husband came and she says in his presence "you divorced me" — she is believed; see responsum Rashba siman 1213 and responsum Rivash siman 126 — it does not appear thus; and Darkei Moshe also brought dispute in this.
In our time. See Bach wrote bedieved if another betrothed her while betrothed to him we do not remove her; and Choshen Mishpat challenged him; and Be'er Heitev wrote in our time the custom is plain — the rabbi arranging the get stores the get after giving it — then she is not believed to say "you divorced me" for it is possible to clarify; and it appears what Ran and Maggid Mishneh wrote that forbidden to marry — does not speak of betrothal in husband's presence but if she says in husband's presence "you divorced me" — they wrote forbidden to marry; but if betrothed in husband's presence — permitted to betrother. Some say earlier that Rama's proof refers only if she said "you divorced me"; some say later add in our time even if betrothed forbidden to him; and all these laws imply from all poskim no distinction whether known through witnesses she is his wife or not known except hazakah she is his wife. In Orach Chaim he wrote if known through witnesses she is his wife we do not say hazakah a woman does not act brazenly — it does not appear from all poskim; also difficulty on him from sugya end of Gittin — if rumor emerged one betrothed her and another betrothed in his presence — and it says if found first kiddushin were certain kiddushin — no get needed from second; and R' Huna disputes who says woman saying "you divorced me" is believed — per his words from where for Talmud to say they dispute — perhaps speaks when known she is his wife — then not believed to say "you divorced me" — perforce no distinction in matter.
Kiddushin do not take effect. So is view of most poskim; but Ran wrote in name of Ramban to be concerned stringently even not in his presence.
Where someone supports her she acts brazenly. In Tur it is — even if one witness supports her she acts brazenly; and in truth we do not find explicit in Talmud if one witness supports that she is brazen; ch. 2 Kesubos there — where two support she is brazen; Gittin 64 — where husband and agent support she is brazen; Rosh wrote if agent alone supports her — cannot act brazen; and below siman 141 seif 51 he brought dispute; and here wrote if one witness supports her she is brazen; possible Mechaber ruled stringently here; however on Tur difficulty how he wrote plainly unlike Rosh — and where she can act brazen kiddushin of another do not take effect in her — so Tur below siman 152; nevertheless she is disqualified for kehunah and yibum as written below there; see what he wrote there.
Some say even if she did not marry. So Tur below siman 152; and likewise if she committed adultery not believed — apparently who disputes this; and Rosh in responsum disputes — from what Tur wrote reason in marriage not believed so as not to make herself brazen zona — implies in kiddushin believed; for in Beit Yosef he wrote incident that was so and compelled to say thus — for otherwise Tur disputes Rosh.
She collects her ketubah — meaning principal ketubah not additional gifts; for from derashah of ketubah she takes ketubah since he wrote her "when you marry another you take" — and derashah of ketubah we expound only on principal ketubah as Ran and Maggid wrote; and below this siman I brought their words; see Maharik there.
Some say she is not believed regarding money at all — but to marry another permitted; and this is a novelty here what he did not bring nearby — for there he brought dispute whether believed for everything or not at all; and some hold for money not believed and to marry believed — so Choshen Mishpat.
