A married woman who extended her hand and accepted kiddushin in her husband's presence — behold she is betrothed to the second. A woman who said to her husband in his presence "you divorced me" — she is believed; presumption: a woman does not act brazenly before her husband. {Rama: Some say this is specifically regarding whether kiddushin take effect in her — she is believed and requires a get; but she is not believed to marry another (Ran chapter 2 of Ketubot in the name of R' Yonah), or to collect her ketubah (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 2 of Gittin) (and some disagree and hold she is believed for everything) (Rambam) (and some say further that in our time, when brazenness and licentiousness are common, she is not believed except stringently — for the presumption that she does not act brazenly has weakened) (Beit Yosef in the name of Orach Chaim).} But if another betrothed her not in her husband's presence — kiddushin do not take effect in her until she brings proof that she was divorced before she accepted the kiddushin; for whoever acts not in his presence acts brazenly. And the Rosh wrote that whoever is in the city where she was betrothed to another is called "in his presence" (and some dispute and hold we require literally in her husband's presence) (Maharik shoresh 72 and Rivash siman 127). And that which we say a woman does not act brazenly before her husband to say "you divorced me" — this is where there is no one to support her; but where there is someone to support her — she acts brazenly and acts brazenly. And if she married another not in her husband's presence — even though when her husband comes she says to him in his presence "you divorced me," she is not believed; for since she already married she acts brazenly and acts brazenly so as not to make herself a zona. And some say that even if she has not yet married another but was only betrothed to him not in her husband's presence, and afterward her husband comes and she says to him in his presence "you divorced me" — she is not believed. And some say that if there was a quarrel between them or she is claiming her ketubah — she is not believed to say "you divorced me" even in his presence (and some say in a place where she is believed to marry she also collects her ketubah) (Maharik shoresh 72 and Rivash siman 127) (and some say she is not believed at all regarding money, and this was explained above).
