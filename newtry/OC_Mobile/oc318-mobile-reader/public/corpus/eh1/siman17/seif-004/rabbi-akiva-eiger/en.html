In Chelkat Mechokek sign 10 — it appears his son-in-law. Nb: In the plain sense it appears a scribal error in Yerushalmi and it should read "son of her mother-in-law" — from that wording the masculine form is discussed; as the mishnah teaches "daughter of her mother-in-law"; and he wrote that in such masculine forms "son of her mother-in-law" is valid.
