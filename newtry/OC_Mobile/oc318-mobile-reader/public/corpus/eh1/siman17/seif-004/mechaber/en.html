All are believed to testify for her this testimony except for five women whose presumption is that they hate one another and do not testify to the death of her husband lest they intend to forbid her to him while he still exists; and these are: her mother-in-law — even if she is not now her mother-in-law but only if she would levirate her (Tur); and her mother-in-law's daughter; and her co-wife; and her yevamah; and her husband's daughter (and likewise she may not testify for them) (Tur).
