And even if he did not testify explicitly that the first gentile was speaking innocently. However, if a Jew says that he heard it from a gentile, one must say that the first gentile was speaking innocently, because the way of a Jew is to ask about his fellow Jew. See responsum Mishnat Binyamin siman 63. In Turei Zahav he wrote that even with a Jew it helps. Chelkat Mechokek wrote: if a gentile testifies innocently from the mouth of a Jew who testified in response to a question, it requires analysis whether this helps, since the gentile says it from the mouth of a Jew whose intent was to testify.
