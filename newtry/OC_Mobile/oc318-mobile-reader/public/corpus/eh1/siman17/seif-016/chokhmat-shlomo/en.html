Seif 16 in the gloss — And some dispute this. Behold students of tractate Gittin 28 — on what it challenges there "what died and what was killed" — let us say died literally and killed literally as with gentile counter-missionaries; why do they not permit his wife; behold it is mesalfei lefi tumo; and what does it challenge — behold there is no connecting words there; afterward I saw in the commentators who brought this proof from there — Rivash himself; but after examination in my opinion it can be rejected. One may say the essence of the reason connecting words are required is to prove he is not lying and his intention is not to testify; if we knew he speaks truth it would help even if not mesalfei lefi tumo; and the essence of the concern is lest he lies; and if he says connecting words it is proven he is not lying; if so likewise if there is other proof he is not lying it is like connecting words. If so when saying "killed in courts" — why need to say this, for it is an uncommon expression; behold according to this opinion the Gemara did not know the reason avdei le'achzukei shakranei; if so why need to say an uncommon expression more than a common expression to say simply "died"; from this it is proven he speaks truth and it is like connecting words; perforce the Ran does not require connecting words except when saying simply "died" — there is concern for lying; but when saying "died" or "killed in courts" he does not require connecting words, for it is proven he is not lying and it is like a miggo — if he were lying he would say simply "died." And there is proof for this from the language of the baraita itself that taught he said "died" or "killed" — what difference to me "died" or "killed"; why did it teach "killed" — perforce as amplification for what is stated that even though he says "killed" which is uncommon, nevertheless at the beginning he is believed and at the end he is not believed. Granted in Yevamot where it taught there "died," "I killed him," "we killed him" — there it teaches even though he makes himself wicked and the dispute of R' Yehudah and the rabbis there; but here why need "killed" — perforce as amplification; if so one may say for this reason itself he does not require connecting words, since it is proven he is not lying he does not require connecting words. And similarly wrote Rama regarding disqualified witnesses if it is proven without deceit — and through rumor their testimony helps; so too one may say here. And with this one can resolve what Tosafot challenged there — why did it not challenge from the end that they do not permit his wife; and they answered one may say here mesalfei lefi tumo does not help — but it lacks reason; but in my opinion one may say what the Gemara states "rather what was killed — going out to be killed" — the intent is not that he says so explicitly, but the intent is thus: since he says simply "died" or "killed" and since the one who says so is among counter-missionaries — there is concern perhaps his intent is going out to be killed; therefore with an Israelite either way she is permitted; but with gentiles there is concern since he is among counter-missionaries perhaps he saw him going out to kill him — therefore he says in a manner of doubt "was killed"; if so it is a question: since in truth connecting words are required, only since he says "killed" in an uncommon expression it is proven he is not lying — if he were lying he would say simply "died" in a common expression; if so it works if he says already "died" or "killed"; but if his intent is to say he was going out to kill — again there is no proof from what he says "killed"; one may say he lies and fears perhaps afterward it will be revealed he lied; therefore he does not say simply "died" but says going out to be killed; if so he would not be established as a liar, for he can excuse himself that afterward they released him and found merit for him; if so if he excuses that his intent was he was going out to be killed — properly he mentions preferably "killed" in this which is more common among counter-missionaries than "died" to throw him to the furnace as Rashi explained; if so there is no proof from his language that he did not say simply "died" since his intent was not to be caught as a liar; and again since there is no proof from his words — again mesalfei lefi tumo does not help since there are no connecting words; and there would be proof from this on the contrary that connecting words are generally required — examine well. And further one can reject Rivash's proofs simply — one may say with two mesalfei lefi tumo connecting words are not required, for each one is like legs to the matter for the second; as I wrote in responsum in my composition edition 6 responsum 130 to Symiatish to distinguish between one mesalfei lefi tumo and two; so too one may say here, since here it teaches he heard from counter-missionaries plural language — from this that he heard from many, minority of many is two; and with this according to all connecting words are not required; examine and question.
