Explicitly, etc. However if the Israelite says he heard from a Samaritan — must be the first Samaritan was incidentally because it is an Israelite's way to ask about his fellow Israelite; see responsum Maharashbach siman 43. And Taz wrote even from an Israelite it helps. Choshen Mishpat wrote if a gentile testifies incidentally from an Israelite who testified through question — need resolve whether it helps since the Samaritan said from an Israelite his intent was to testify he too intends to testify; see there. If from the second gentile's speech it appears the first was incidentally — all agree his testimony helps — Ram Metrani ch. 2 siman 104. Even if the last gentile asked the first why did you do so and in answer he said I killed Jew Ploni — incidentally — Mishpat Tzedek ch. 2 siman 63.
