And there are those who say, etc. This resembles testimony of death in chapter 16 of Yevamos, 114b and 117a. There is also proof from Kesubos 89a: 'Here we are dealing with,' etc., 'because of a migo,' etc.; see Tosafos, Bava Metzia 17b. In Yevamos and Kesubos there, they wrote that this concerns one who demands her kesubah, but the same applies where there was quarrelling. The Rambam, however, holds that even when she demands the kesubah she is believed, as written in chapter 16 of Hilchos Ishus, halacha 26, and in the Shulchan Aruch below, siman 100, se'if 10. He follows his own position that through the exposition of the kesubah she collects only the principal kesubah, as written above in the small section; the questions of Tosafos cited above are therefore resolved.
