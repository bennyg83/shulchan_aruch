And gentiles fell upon us. Because in a small war we say that a woman has her weapons upon her, is not afraid, and stood and saw that he died; therefore she does not speak by mere conjecture. We are also not concerned that she lies except in a great war, according to the Rambam. Tosafot wrote that in a great war the reason is that they shoot arrows and ballista-stones and do not aim at the one who will fall; therefore she is afraid to stand there.
