Gentile. Because in small war we say a woman is a weapon upon her and she does not despair and stands and sees he died and does not speak in his imagination; also for lying we are concerned only in great war — see Beit Shmuel. Investigate an elderly woman unfit for intercourse whether one can say a woman her weapon is upon her. However this doubt falls only per Rashi there Avodah Zarah 25b who explained the gentile does not kill her and lies with her; but per Tosafot there because they are not warriors and fighters they pity her — if so all the more an elderly woman her weapon is upon her. Rav Idi there said even regarding an ugly woman — if so one can say likewise elderly. This is not so — a young ugly woman there is reason for intercourse unlike elderly — uncertain Kenesset HaGedolah 32b. Possibly even for elderly her weapon is upon her for otherwise let us say there is among them elderly woman — simple; see Beit Chadash and Beit Yosef.
