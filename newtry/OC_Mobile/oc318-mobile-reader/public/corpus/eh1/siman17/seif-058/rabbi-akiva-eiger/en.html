Beit Shmuel sign 142 — we are concerned she lies. Nb: See responsa of Ra'em ch. 1 siman 21 who wrote the reason — there are grounds she lies that a woman does not bury her husband while battle still rages; therefore when she says "I buried him" after battle ceased she is believed; also "I buried him" in battle — even though she says she recognizes him through signs we do not forbid lest she lies; however from Shulchan Aruch HaGadol ch. HaIshah in the name of Ri'az it appears the reason we are concerned she speaks falsely and she thought it was her husband — they say she knew he was struck in battle and thought he died; and if she recognizes him through a sign it helps; therefore also when she says "I buried him" after battle if she did not say she recognizes him through a sign it does not help; and see responsa Chayei Moshe ch. 1 siman 8.
