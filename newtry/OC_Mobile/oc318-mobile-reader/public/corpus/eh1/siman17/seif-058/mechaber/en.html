If she did not marry the second but was only betrothed and her husband came — she does not need a get from the second and is permitted to return to the first; and likewise she is permitted to the second if the first died or divorced her. {Rama: A woman who was betrothed and erred and thought she was not betrothed and married another — she must leave from this one and that one and all the above ways apply to her. But if they forced her to marry, or the court instructed her in error and she married on their word — it is as one compelled and she is permitted to her first husband (responsum of Rashba 1088 brought in Beit Yosef).}
