He went out with us. This implies that even if he does not mention his name at all, nevertheless when it is known that no one left except this person, she is permitted. It appears that everything depends on the circumstances: if the city is guarded so that one cannot leave without it being known, and it is known that no one left except this person, she is permitted. But if it is possible to leave without it being known, she is forbidden unless they testify that one of the residents of this city left; then we investigate, and if no one is missing except this person, she is permitted. See responsum Mahari Weil siman 40 and responsum Mishnat Binyamin siman 44.
