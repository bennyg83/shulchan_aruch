Seif 32 — But if he fell into the sea, etc., I say it is another man's leg, etc. Behold this is the opinion of the early authorities in Yevamot; but Rambam's opinion is the opposite — even when they brought up a leg simply she is permitted; and it is brought in Beit Yosef briefly their words; and see in my responsum in my edition 7 siman 207 and siman 209 where I wrote it is appropriate to rule like Rambam and the Rashba's words are wondrous in his proof; therefore in any case it is appropriate to be lenient here in a place of agunah — see there and examine.
