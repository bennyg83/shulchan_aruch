An Israelite who said "a Jewish man with us died in such-and-such place, thus and thus was his form and thus were his marks" — we do not say based on estimation "it is so-and-so" until the witness testifies that he is so-and-so and recognizes his name and the name of his city. But if one said "he went out with us from such-and-such city and died" — they search in that city whether only he left from there — his wife may marry. (And likewise regarding a Samaritan speaking offhandedly) (my humble opinion, and so it appears in the responsum of Rambam that Beit Yosef brought).
