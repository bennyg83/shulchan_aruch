Perhaps they were not hungry. Because a pit of lions is broad, whereas a pit of snakes is narrow. But we do not say perhaps a miracle was performed for him, like Daniel; and likewise with a fiery furnace, we do not say that a miracle was performed for him like Chananyah, Mishael, and Azaryah. See Tosafot there. Even if no person was found there, we say he left and went on his way. From the words of the Bach we learn that if bones were found there, we say they ate him; and likewise if he fell into fire but could leave from there, if bones were found there we say he was burned. However, his words are not compelled, and seif 32 also does not imply so.
