Nb: In my humble opinion — a birthmark in any case she leaves; Ritva in the name of Ramah — if she married she leaves since it is only a safek that perhaps signs are deoraita since the sugya in Bava Metzia supports that signs are deoraita; but for forbidden relations we are stringent lest signs are not deoraita; therefore bedieved she does not leave unlike a birthmark which is explained in the baraita "we do not testify" — therefore in any case the law is she leaves. And from this it appears to me in Rambam's language — even a birthmark which seems a greater matter; and therefore like other signs that change — the law is she leaves; not like Ramah.
