A woman says "died" or she said "my husband died" and afterward a fit witness came and said he did not die — behold she may not marry; and if she married she must leave. And some say that if another woman, or she herself, said "died" and they permitted her to marry on her word and afterward a fit witness came and said he did not die — she need not leave the permission (but if two women came first and permitted her to marry on their word and afterward one witness came — she need not leave the permission according to all) (so too Beit Yosef in the name of Maggid Mishneh).
