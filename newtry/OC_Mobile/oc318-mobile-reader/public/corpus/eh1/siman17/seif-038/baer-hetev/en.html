Valid. Namely even after they permitted her — see Kenesset HaGedolah 31a.
And they permitted her, etc. For law one does not rely for leniency on her herself — Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel. A woman who said witness Ploni told me my husband died and that Ploni contradicts her — she is not believed; for any witness from witness if the first came and denied the second the second who speaks in his name is not believed — Maharival ch. 3 siman 4 and Radbaz ch. 1 siman 242. Kenesset HaGedolah 31a expanded and wrote possibly to say specifically witness from witness if the first came and denied the second the second speaking in his name is not believed; but the woman herself from witness from witness possibly believed for she is precise and weighs, etc.; and concludes there nevertheless plain to me for all if she married and afterward the witness came and contradicted her she does not leave. If she went to another court and said my husband died and did not mention witness — she is believed — Radbaz ch. 1 siman 242. Even per those who say he is not believed it is specifically witness from witness; but with two witnesses from one witness even if the witness denies the two are believed — Ra'anach siman 37; even per those who say she is not believed it is specifically when they asked him and he denied; but we need not ask him if the matter is so as she said — Mahari Mintz siman 14 and Radbaz ch. 1 siman 242 wrote if she hung on a witness and he is not before us she is believed with miggo she said my husband died stam; and if he is before us we ask him — see there. Kenesset HaGedolah 31b wrote per those who say any witness from witness if the first came and denied the second the second speaking in his name is not believed — specifically Israel from Israel or gentile incidentally from gentile incidentally or from Israel; but Israel from gentile the gentile whose mouth speaks false to deny him — see there.
