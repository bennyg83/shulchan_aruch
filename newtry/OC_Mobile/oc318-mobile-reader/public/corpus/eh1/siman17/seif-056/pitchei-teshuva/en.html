See Noda BiYehudah, siman 41, who writes regarding his case: We must investigate whether those who received the testimony saw that this youth had grown an upper beard and that his beard was filled out, etc. In a Torah prohibition we do not rely upon the presumption that once he reached the required age he developed the signs of adulthood. Even if we examine him now and find them, it remains difficult to rely upon this. However, in my opinion, if the youth states with certainty that he had already grown two hairs before witnessing the event, he is believed. See there. Beit Meir writes that he is uncertain about this entire requirement, whether the witness must actually be an adult demonstrated by two hairs. It appears more likely to him that no such hairs are required at all; see there.
