Rambam; and as Maharai and Shulchan Aruch wrote — they presume, etc.; and Tur holds all courts of gentiles are like, as stated in the gloss of Tiferet Lemishpat — that is their custom; and it appears from what he said "was killed" — not by his hand; rather it should have said "he heard from gentiles so-and-so was killed in such-and-such place"; why mention commentary; therefore he said "and all this is specifically," etc.
