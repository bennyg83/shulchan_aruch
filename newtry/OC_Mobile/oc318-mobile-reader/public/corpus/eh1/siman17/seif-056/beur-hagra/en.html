We killed him. This is the Rambam's wording, as Terumas HaDeshen and the Shulchan Aruch write that they are presumed, etc. The Tur holds that all judicial officials are like this, as written in the gloss, because it glorifies their legal system. This is also implied by the phrase 'he was killed,' which suggests not by the speaker's own hand. Yet it is difficult, for it should have said that he heard from non-Jews that so-and-so was killed by officials. Why specify officials? Therefore it says: all this applies specifically, etc.
