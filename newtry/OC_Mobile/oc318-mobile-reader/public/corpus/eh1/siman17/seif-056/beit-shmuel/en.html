And he is not obligated to give the worn-out remnants. As a general rule, her law is like that of a woman who committed adultery, as written in siman 115; see there. Anything that belongs to her, even if she took it with witnesses present, is not removed from her. And anything she forfeited, seizure does not help, even without witnesses. See Nimukei Yosef. According to Rashi she does not even receive existing worn-out remnants, and so ruled Shiltei Gibborim, while the Mechaber rules like the majority of poskim, as written in Nimukei Yosef.

Regarding the five women who do not testify for one another..
If she gave birth. The same applies if she became pregnant. See siman 4, where I wrote that these laws divide into four categories. In Choshen Mishpat he brought responsum Ralbach: a woman who intentionally committed adultery, the child she bore from the adulterer after the first husband’s divorce, even though she is forbidden to him, nevertheless the child after the first husband’s divorce is not a mamzer. This is not comparable to one who married by mistake, for there it is a penalty; but with one who willingly committed adultery, the penalty does not apply.
