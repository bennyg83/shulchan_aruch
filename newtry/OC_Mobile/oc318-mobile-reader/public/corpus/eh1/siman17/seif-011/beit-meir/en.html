Gloss: witnesses who testify, etc. — Beit Shmuel wrote; and Taz is lenient; see Knesset HaGedolah siman 81 — empty reckless apostates who returned in repentance but not complete repentance — do not rely on their testimony; I saw in responsum unpublished of Gaon deceased Rav Chaim Rapoport zt"l av beit din of community Hamburg that he answered on this matter at length and decided to be lenient when they speak offhandedly and when known they testified without deceit — see there; and in my view correct per nature of witness and what judge sees.
