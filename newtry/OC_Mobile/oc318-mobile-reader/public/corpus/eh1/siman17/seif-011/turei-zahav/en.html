Because of rumor that emerged; even if found in writing one wrote to friend that Ploni died and did not mention drowning; nevertheless we say because of rumor he wrote so; in truth that rumor has no substance since water has no end; so Terumat HaDeshen there implies if rumor emerged on one that he died and afterward found Israelite writing he died even though that rumor was not perfectly clear we do not disqualify the writing and say because of rumor he wrote; for if rumor is true there is no inconsistency unlike drowning in endless water we mentioned.
