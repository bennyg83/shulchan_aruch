They found written in a document "so-and-so son of so-and-so died" or "was killed" — his wife may marry; even if it is not signed. And for Rambam they must know it is the writing of an Israelite. {Rama: And likewise one should be stringent — even if they know it is the writing of an Israelite — if it can be said he wrote thus because a rumor spread that he died, such as drowning in endless waters and the like — they do not permit based on the writing; for we are concerned he wrote thus because of the rumor that spread (Terumat HaDeshen siman 200).}
