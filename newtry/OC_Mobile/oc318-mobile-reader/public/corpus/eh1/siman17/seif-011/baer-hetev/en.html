They found written in a document he died. Even if written in gentile script it helps if it is known an Israelite wrote it — Terumat HaDeshen siman 200; R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 7 and Binyamin Ze'ev siman 28. And even if he wrote to the court he heard from another it helps except when writing for remembrance and not writing for action — then we are not lenient with witness from witness; but if writing for remembrance what he saw himself it helps; see responsum R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 7 and responsum Maharam Alshikh siman 44 and siman 118; see Kenesset HaGedolah and Beit Shmuel.
And even if not validated. We hold witnesses signed on a document are as if their testimony was examined in court and validation of documents is rabbinic; and for testimony of a woman they were lenient — therefore witnesses must be signed on the writing. And if one witness signed it helps — so agreed most poskim; see Kenesset HaGedolah in Beit Yosef's gloss seif 35 37. However some poskim hold validation is required; however validation by one witness helps because generally validation by one witness does not help because it is witness from witness — and here for testimony of a woman witness from witness helps. And so agreed Ram and author of Choshen Mishpat validation is required. However if a writing came from afar and is not validated Choshen Mishpat is lenient in place of agunah to permit writing found written in Israelite script — we rely on majority of fit Israelite witnesses for testimony. Also must be written Ploni son of Ploni from place Ploni; see Beit Shmuel and Kenesset HaGedolah in Tur's gloss seif 48 49.
That they knew. Meaning even if he wrote incidentally; and if he said orally it would help; nevertheless in writing it does not help — therefore must know it is Israelite writing — so Maggid Mishneh. And Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit wrote on him: he has no reason — if he wrote incidentally why should it be inferior; see there. But in Mekor Baruch siman 23 wrote a gentile incidentally in writing does not help; see responsum Choshen Mishpat.
That he wrote so. Not that he wrote likewise if he said orally we say perhaps he heard he drowned in water without end as written in Terumat HaDeshen siman 200. And Taz infers from Terumat HaDeshen if rumor went out on one that he died and afterward one wrote he died even if that rumor was not clear she is permitted; see Beit Shmuel nevertheless and Kenesset HaGedolah in Beit Yosef's gloss seif 39.
