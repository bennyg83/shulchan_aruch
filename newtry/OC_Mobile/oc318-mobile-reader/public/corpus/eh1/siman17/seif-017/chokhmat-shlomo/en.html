Seif 24 — They found him killed, etc., even though they have prominent signs, etc. Behold in several responsa I relied to be lenient where she left Torah prohibition and is forbidden only rabbinically — rabbinic signs suffice. And behold now I saw for one author in our time who proved from Hagahot Maimoniyot to the contrary from what is stated regarding falling in endless waters they were stringent until forehead and nose — and behold nose is required only rabbinically as stated in Bekhorot that for woman's testimony the rabbis were stringent, etc.; behold this is only blindness of the eye of the viewer — for this is the question in Bekhorot; but afterward the Gemara concludes and alternatively he recognizes alone and facial recognition alone; if so according to that version it is from Torah that nose is also required; and we hold in all versions both; and Hagahot Maimoniyot therefore holds both are required; and in any case this is correct that rabbinically rabbinic signs help; and I did not need to mention this except because it touches what I relied on several times — for this I raised in writing to remove from the reader's heart in his book; examine. And behold in law — dispute of the poskim whether prominent signs help for us on garments that are not worn on the person; see in my composition on Orach Chaim laws of Lulav part the inscribed 95 page 120 in responsum 130 Schwabach what I wrote as proof to permit also for us on garments not worn on the person; and I raised that even rabbinic signs — nevertheless signs that come from reasoning are preferable — see there and examine. And see further what I wrote on the law of rabbinic signs and whether two intermediate signs become prominent — see in my composition Chokhmat HaTorah on the Torah parashat Chayei Sarah year 5622 what I wrote proof for this from Torah incidentally regarding rabbinic signs and two intermediates becoming prominent — see there; may God merit me to aim according to law and enlighten my eyes in the light of Torah — see there and examine. And see in the novellae of Bach on Gittin what he wrote on matters of rabbinic signs — what to note, several with God's help; and what can be brought as proof for what the annotator wrote and I too intended this in several responsa where she left Torah prohibition and prohibition is only rabbinic — rabbinic signs help — see there and examine. And see Beit Shmuel s.k. 70 what he wrote in the name of Mahariw in the name of the Rama that with an intermediate sign if she married she need not leave; and see in my composition on Even HaEzer edition 5 siman 104 in my responsum 130 to Kashinov what I wrote to explain his intent — see there and examine. And see in my composition on Orach Chaim siman 248 (year 5626) what I wrote there in responsum to resolve the difficulty of Even HaEzer on the matter of profane signs for 909 — see there where I extended on this; examine. And it is known the opinion of the poskim who dispute whether two intermediate signs become a prominent sign; and we already spoke much on this with God's help; and now it appears to me proof it is not a prominent sign from what we say in chapter 2 of Bava Metzia that it was asked — are signs Torah signs or rabbinic signs; practical difference to return a woman's get through signs; and it is proven explicitly that if rabbinic signs we do not return a get with an intermediate sign; difficulty: why should it be — we are concerned for two Yishai sons or two Shevurai; at least they are not worse than an intermediate sign, for there is an opinion we are not concerned for two Yishai sons if they were not established, and likewise for two Shevurai — and so we hold; and with signs we hold rabbinic signs — behold an intermediate sign is worse than concern for two Yishai sons or two Shevurai; if so even for the opinion concerned for two Yishai sons and two Shevurai — at least not worse than intermediate sign; if so since there is another opinion they can combine his name and her name and the name of their city with an intermediate sign and it would be like a prominent sign; and even if rabbinic signs — why should he not return? However this could be rejected — one may say the intent of practical difference is where they know another such get was lost in such a name and afterward one get was found and we do not know whose it is — whether he returns it through an intermediate sign; however from tractate Gittin properly one can bring proof from what is stated Gittin 27b — R' Eliezer said such as he said there is a hole next to such-and-such a letter, but a hole in general — I am not precise whether Torah signs or rabbinic; and there the difficulty works well — behold the case is he does not know if there is another Yishai son or two Shevurai there — behold this is the main pilpul of the Gemara there whether we are concerned for two Shevurai; if so it discusses where it is unknown only to fear for him; if so why need prominent sign — even safek they can combine with the name and city name and it would be prominent; at least R' Eliezer should answer thus; perforce it is proven two intermediates do not combine for a prominent sign. And further from what is stated there afterward in Rabbah in Bach that he said I do not know whether because of sign or because of visual identification — specifically for a Torah scholar; and why did he not also say perhaps from both — perhaps visual identification alone does not help and there is no distinction between Torah scholar and other — only because of both we return not because of sign and visual identification together becoming prominent sign; perforce it is compelled that combination does not make a prominent sign; examine.
