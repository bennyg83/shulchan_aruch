From here, etc. Per Ran brought in gloss seif 14. Also here need say connection of matters — Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel, unlike Beit Chadash. Not only a Jew who went with us died; but even one gentile came and said a Jew who went with Ploni died — they permit his wife — responsum Beit Yosef siman 11 on laws of gentile incidentally. Even though from Rambam's words it appears we require he say a man who went with me from here died and if he did not say from here but said a man who went with me died we say perhaps that Jew who went with him from here went his way and another joined him and died — nevertheless Ra'ash and many rabbis wrote we are not concerned perhaps another joined him on the way and all we know a Jew went with him we permit his wife; see Kenesset HaGedolah in Tur's gloss seif 136 22a; and Taz did not rule so. No difference whether the gentile says a Jew from city Ploni or says went from city Ploni — Mishpetei Shmuel siman 21. Even per Ra'ash in two concerns we are not concerned — meaning this went and another joined; but in one concern that this went alone we are concerned — Ra'ash in responsum siman 134; and so.
He recognizes him. There are four views. Per Ritva — specifically if the Samaritan inserts himself to announce Ploni died then certainly dead and need not say I buried him. And per Ran, Maggid, and Rivash if he recognizes him we do not require I buried him. Per Mahari Migash — specifically when the Samaritan was there at his death or killing we are concerned he speaks in his imagination and must say I buried him. Per responsum Maharam ch. 1 siman 36 — distinguish if he went with him (or) [then] he thought dead since he parted from him and says in his imagination; see Beit Yosef; see responsum Maharam Lublin siman 6 and Rashak siman 7 and Kenesset HaGedolah in Tur's gloss 22b. And when the gentile says I killed Ploni — need not I buried him. Hiding them in garbage is like I buried him. Reuven writing to Shimon Levi wrote in his writing Ploni was buried — is like I buried him. Not only I buried him but even I moved him; see Kenesset HaGedolah 22 23 — many distinctions from this and brought many poskim who hold need not say I buried him unlike Rambam; see Kenesset HaGedolah 39.
In chains. So Rambam implies if they are not imprisoned in chains we are concerned perhaps those went to the world because he did not say went with me but they went themselves — therefore we are concerned perhaps they went to the world; and from this it is heard number is not a sign. And so Taz number and place are not a sign; and if he intends actual place it is a sign like a hole on the side of letter Ploni — Taz. And Choshen Mishpat infers from Rashi he holds number is a sign since he explained chains as group; see 174 siman 273. And wrote in responsum Maharashbach siman 44 if a Samaritan recognizes one of them and testifies the whole group was killed — we say presumably that group — Beit Shmuel.
