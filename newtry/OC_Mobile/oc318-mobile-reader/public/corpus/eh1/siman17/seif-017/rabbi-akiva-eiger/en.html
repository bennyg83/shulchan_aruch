Nb: And many later authorities wrote to distinguish that for a gentile mesalechet we attribute that he says so because of the force of rumor; and from this Maharai in his rulings siman 161 speaks. But an Israelite testifying — no distinction except for one drowned; as appears in Terumat HaDeshen siman 140. However if the gentile says explicitly mesalechet "I saw him die" one may say it helps where there was a rumor.
