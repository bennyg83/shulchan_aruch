Testimony from another witness. See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, siman 92, who writes that the unqualified language of the poskim implies that such testimony is effective even when the original witness had already died. That is, by the time the second witness testified, the first witness was already dead. This cannot be inferred from Baer Heitev, s.k. 13, who writes, 'whether the first witness died,' etc., for perhaps the first witness was still alive when the second testified and died only afterward. Rabbi Akiva Eiger later found that Responsa Ra'anach, part 1, siman 22, states this explicitly in the name of Responsa Mahariv, siman 88, and explains the reason; see there.
