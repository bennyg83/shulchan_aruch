Because of compulsion. See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, at the end of siman 87, who wrote to a rabbi: Your honor wished to disqualify the witnesses because they were taken for forced labor, on the concern that they might desecrate Shabbos even when not compelled. I am astonished. Why should we suspect that a person whom we saw transgress under compulsion also transgresses when not compelled? See what I wrote in Nachalas Tzvi, where I cited Gittin 47. As for the Rosh, klal 54, who writes concerning forced converts that their testimony must be examined carefully before extracting money, there the witnesses displayed a specific defect, unlike our case. Moreover, even there the Rosh writes that in an agunah case their testimony is accepted; see there.
