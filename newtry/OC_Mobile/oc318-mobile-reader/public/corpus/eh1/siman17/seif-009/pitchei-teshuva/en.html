In our time when brazenness is common. See Beit Meir who wrote that where there is a convincing excuse proving she speaks truth — even in our time she is believed, as stated siman 154 seif 7 gloss 2 — one law and one reason; see there he wrote to resolve Rabbeinu Yeruham's words in Beit Yosef — he did not contradict; Beit Yosef wondered it seems if he contradicted she is not believed — but this is certainly not so; per Beit Meir R' Yonah also adopts stringency of our time — therefore believed only when excuse joined because he did not contradict; see there. See responsum Galia Massechet siman 5: a woman lived with her husband in her city several years; they traveled to Russia; after about three years she returned with a gentile wagon-driver to an inn; her husband came — she screamed "why pursue me — you divorced me with invalid get three months ago"; he denied, snatched her box and fled; she says the box contained written proof from Rabbi R' Meir who arranged get at an inn near city P.; her husband wanted to leave Judaism; through intermediaries she obtained invalid get; afterward he regretted and sought to seize her and her daughter — she hid weeks. Inquiry to that city: rumor a wagon was sent after Rabbi R' Meir to village P. to arrange invalid get — unknown if divorce occurred; rabbi deceased; his wife knows he traveled for this but not village, woman, or man. Husband left Judaism among soldiers — whereabouts unknown; woman sits agunah seeking permission to marry — says he certainly divorced her and had written proof; presumed modest — end question. He answered at length: permitted to marry without doubt. Foundation: impossible to obtain get from husband — bedieved; convincing estimations support her words — as siman 154 seif 7 claimant "no male strength" — believed; poskim learn from Rav Hamnuna woman not brazen on what husband knows; Rama holds nowadays brazen — nevertheless with excuses and estimations — believed — likewise here (novel not citing Beit Meir). No concern from quarrel and apostasy intent — Beit Meir requires quarrel of seif 48 presumed liar (see Beit Meir — wrote opposite — below s.k. 12); Yevamos explains quarrel — lies vs. pain of soul — in divorce pain of soul no lie — not where presumed quarrelsome; see there. In my view permission not entirely clear — see Nekudot HaKesef where I expanded.
