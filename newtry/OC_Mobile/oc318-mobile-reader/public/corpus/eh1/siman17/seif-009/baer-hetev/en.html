He challenged Beit Yosef on Tur why he wrote this law in name of Ra'ash; see Choshen Mishpat and Beit Shmuel and responsa Tzemach Tzedek siman 59 and Kenesset HaGedolah in Beit Yosef's gloss seif 23. One says the murderers were Togarmians and one says they were Greeks — this is not contradiction — Mishpat Tzedek ch. 1 siman 7; see Mishpetei Shmuel siman 80; and Rashdam ch. 1 siman 44. One says they struck him on his arms and one says they plunged a sword in his belly — not contradiction — Mishpetei Shmuel siman 81. One says he died in place X and one says he died in place Y — not contradiction — Mahari Karo in his responsum siman 1 on laws of yibbum and chalitzah. One says he was killed and one says they drowned him in water — can resolve his words they killed him and afterward drowned him — Kenesset HaGedolah; see in Maharam Lublin siman 133. Two groups of witnesses — one says there was a mark on the killed under the eye and one says on the eye — whatever is not proven they testify about one man there is no contradiction here; possible both speak truth and there are two killed — these saw one killed and these saw another killed — Ma'aseh Binyamin siman 63.
