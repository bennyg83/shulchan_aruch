And some say further, etc. — that generations deteriorate, as stated in Yerushalmi ch. HaTekabel: Rabbi, etc. — "just as between kodshim kalim and ordinary chullin, so between our generation and the generation of R' Yosi"; R' Shmuel bar R' Yosi said: "just as between gold and dust, so between our generation and the generation of Abba"; and as stated in the Gemara ch. HaChalitz — "the generations have deteriorated"; and as they distinguished at the end of Nedarim regarding a tameh woman between the first mishnah that she is not brazen and the later mishnah; and as Rosh wrote in ch. 2 of Yevamos seif 8 and in Erech Shulchan ch. 5 of Kesubos siman 34.
