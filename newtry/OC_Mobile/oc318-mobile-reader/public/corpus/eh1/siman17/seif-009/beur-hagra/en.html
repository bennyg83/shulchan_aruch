And there are those who further say, etc. For the generations deteriorate, as stated in the Yerushalmi at the end of the chapter HaMekabel: Rabbi, etc., said, 'As great as the difference between sacrificial holy things and ordinary profane things, so is the difference between our generation and the generation of Rabbi Yose.' Rabbi Shmuel son of Rabbi Yose said, 'As great as the difference between gold and earth, so is the difference between our generation and my father's generation.' Likewise, the Gemara in the chapter HaCholetz says, 'Have the generations improved?' They also distinguished at the end of Nedarim regarding a woman's claim that she is defiled, between the first Mishnah, when she would not brazenly lie, and the later Mishnah. See the Rosh, chapter 2 of Yevamos, section 68, and the Rosh, chapter 5 of Kesubos, end of section 34.
