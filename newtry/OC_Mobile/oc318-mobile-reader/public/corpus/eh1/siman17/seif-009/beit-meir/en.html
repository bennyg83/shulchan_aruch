See Chiddushei Ramban beginning Ha'ishah sheNitarmelah — on Tosafot's challenge s.v. mi amrinan; and some say "perhaps" she needs to explain (meaning died on condition); but here where stam her permission — her explanation does not forbid her — we say what loss to lie — if she wanted she would be silent — clear at time of peace no need to ask; and at time of war need hazakah from war; and Ramban's words are clear; and what Beit Shmuel wrote — at time of peace if she says "died" without asking believed — per Rashi; at time of war even if asked not believed until hazakah — per Rama; and Beit Yosef agreed; and in my view all one reasoning.
