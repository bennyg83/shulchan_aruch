A woman who said "my husband died under a collapse" — she is not believed. And likewise if there was a sending of snakes and scorpions and she said a snake or scorpion bit him and he died — she is not believed, lest she rely on the majority of people who died thus from a bite (and therefore their law is like war and she must say "died on his bed" or say "I buried him") (Beit Yosef in the name of Maggid Mishneh).
