So-and-so died. See Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 71, concerning David son of Asher of Ginzburg, who traveled by ship across the sea with his father from Amsterdam to Portugal, after which all trace of them was lost for approximately ten years. Later, an authenticated document came before the beis din of Amsterdam, bearing a recognizable name and the testimony of one witness, with the wording, 'David son of Asher, of blessed memory, Ginzburg, may his resting place be honored.' Some wished to permit the wife of the aforementioned Anshil on this basis. Chavos Yair strongly protested and wrote: I cannot believe that such a thought could enter anyone's mind, for the Rambam permitted reliance upon a document only where it explicitly states, 'So-and-so died,' or, 'So-and-so was killed,' etc. See there.
