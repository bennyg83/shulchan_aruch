A woman who said: my husband died under the collapse. It appears that in such a case she also has a migo, for had she wished she could have said simply that he died. If she said, I buried him, this depends on the two reasons I wrote above: according to the first reason she is believed, and according to the second reason, I buried him does not help, for there is still concern that perhaps she is lying, since it is firmly fixed in her heart that he died and therefore she says, I buried him. See Maggid Mishneh, who wrote regarding this law that I buried him does not help. The Rama wrote plainly, according to the first reason there, that if she said, I buried him, she is believed.
