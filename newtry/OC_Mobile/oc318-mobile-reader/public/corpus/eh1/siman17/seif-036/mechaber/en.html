A city that was surrounded by troops and a ship broken at sea and one who goes out to judgment — behold they are presumed alive; therefore one does not testify about any of these that he died to permit his wife. And even one who is in a city conquered by troops and a ship that sank at sea and one who goes out to be killed — one does not testify that they are in doubt of life; rather they give upon them the stringencies of the dead and the stringencies of the living.
