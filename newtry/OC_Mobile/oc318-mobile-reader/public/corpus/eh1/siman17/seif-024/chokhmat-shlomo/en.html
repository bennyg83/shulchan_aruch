Seif 39 in the gloss — And the court must be three fit, etc. Behold for those who hold specifically permission of three is required — perforce it is not like other instruction of prohibition and permission where the court only informs the law whether to forbid or permit; for then one individual would suffice; perforce they hold speech causes the permission and so Chazal enacted she is permitted only on their word; therefore according to them it appears appropriate ab initio the permission should be before the woman — one may compare this to permission of vows that does not help through an agent, only we require the vow-maker himself come before those who permit; and this is because in permission of vows their speech causes the permission; therefore it does not help through an agent; so too here their speech causes the permission and with their mouths she is permitted — we require she be before the woman ab initio and not not in her presence — so is my humble opinion in this; examine.
