S.k. 38 — A woman says died, etc. See above in Beit Shmuel s.k. 38 what he brought as a difficulty in the name of the Gaon Rabbi Asher on Rivash from Gittin — why did it not challenge there, and perhaps he is a gentile; and see what I wrote on this in the words of Beit Shmuel in responsum 130 to Kamenitz in edition acquired place it is laws of Gittin siman 120. And behold the Gaon's difficulties appear to leave it in uncertainty — for granted regarding so-and-so a snake bit him and he died — here comes proof whether he is Israelite or gentile; therefore he should have challenged perhaps he is gentile; but regarding all who hear a voice write a get for my wife — it is proven he is expert in laws since he did not say simply "write a get for my wife" and specified those who hear his voice; and also said "let them write a get for my wife" and did not say "give a get to my wife" or "write and give" — proven he is expert in laws and it is not the way of a gentile to be expert in laws; therefore no concern perhaps he is gentile — examine and it is simple.
