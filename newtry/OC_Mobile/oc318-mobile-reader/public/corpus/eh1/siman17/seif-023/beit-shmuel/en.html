That he is so-and-so son of so-and-so from such-and-such place. This is the reading in the Tur, and it is also the reading of Maharik, root 175. Even according to the poskim who hold that if he mentions his father’s name he need not mention the city, here, since the form has changed, he must mention his father’s name and his city there. Here the Rambam holds that one need not see his shadow and the shadow of his shadow, and likewise below in the case of one cast into a pit; I already wrote about this above. However, the Mechaber is difficult: in siman 141 he brought an opinion that we require the shadow of his shadow for one cast into a pit, while here he did not bring that opinion.
