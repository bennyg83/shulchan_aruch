One witness came and said "I saw him die in war or in a collapse" — if he said "I buried him" she may marry on his word; and if he did not say "I buried him" she may not marry. And if she married she need not leave. {Rama: But two — even if they did not say "we buried him" — are believed (likewise Tur in the name of the Rosh). And some say even with one witness — even if he said "died" or "was killed" and "I saw him afterward and recognized him well by visual identification and saw him die" — he is believed, for it is as if he said "I buried him" (Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai chapter HaIshah). And a Samaritan speaking offhandedly who said he died in war must also say "I buried him" (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ran chapter HaIshah and so it appears in the Ashiri and Tosafot). And some are lenient in this (Mordechai in the name of R' Simchah and R' Natan and Rabeinu Yehudah).}
