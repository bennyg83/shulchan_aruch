Even per Rambam who holds if she says I buried him she is not believed because we are concerned she lies — nevertheless regarding a witness we are not concerned he lies; the concern is perhaps he spoke in his imagination and with I buried him in his imagination does not apply — see Beit Shmuel s.k. 143. No distinction whether he says died stam or says I saw him dead — Ra'anach ch. 1 siman 75. Many souls killed like sixty count as war — Rashak ch. 1 siman 26. See Ram Metrani ch. 2 siman 78 — sea war is like small war and no concern for in his imagination — Mishpat Tzedek ch. 1 siman 68. Not only witness who says I buried him but whoever was occupied moving from place to place in the manner of the dead suffices — Ra'ash siman 4 and siman 6. Specifically great war; but bandits on one caravan possibly must say I buried him — Maharival. But Maharam Lublin siman 51; siman 128 wrote bandits fell on us is like war and speaks in his imagination — see there. Kenesset HaGedolah 32a.
We buried him. For with two we do not say they speak in his imagination — so Ra'ash and Rashdam siman 55, Binyamin Ze'ev siman 19, responsum Beit Yosef siman 1 on laws of yibbum and chalitzah, R' Eliyahu Sasson ch. 1 siman 1, Maharam Lublin siman 128; to me Ra'ash's proofs are not proof for he wishes to prove from that which says a hundred women are like one witness, etc. — implies if like two witnesses they would not speak in his imagination — end quote. I see no proof here — one the Gemara wishes to prove even with one we do not say in his imagination; further per Rif who holds even with two they speak in his imagination — difficult why the Gemara does not ask with two witnesses as it asks with one witness; must say Rif holds here too that two are required as Beit Chadash wrote; if so the Gemara must say a hundred women are like one to resolve both that he did not speak in his imagination; see Beit Chadash; examine this sugya — difficult to me on the Gemara's proof a hundred women, etc. and it teaches difficulty — see there. Perhaps there it deals where they said died and I buried him. Also on the answer why not answer so; see what Nimukei Yosef wrote; examine. Even per Rif if the killers themselves testify — no concern for in his imagination — Rashak ch. 1 siman 27. Maharival ch. 1 klal 1 siman 2 ruled two are believed even without we buried him like Maharachash even if disqualified. Ra'ash siman 4 and siman 244 dispute and rule specifically two valid witnesses not disqualified. And so Rashak ch. 2 siman 4 and Rashdam siman 79 and Tzitz Eliezer siman 88 and Rama in his responsum siman 101 and Maharam Lublin siman 129. One testified in name of both — as if both were before us and we are not concerned for in his imagination — Maharam Metrani ch. 2 siman 59, Maharam Lublin siman 128, Tzitz Eliezer siman 88. Thus with two believed without we buried him — specifically when they said they saw he died; but if they said they heard he died even a hundred count as one — responsum Beit Yosef siman 1 on laws of yibbum and chalitzah.
