A woman whose husband went to a distant land and witnesses testified that he died — even one witness, even the witness was a slave or maidservant or woman or relative — she is permitted; and even one witness from the mouth of a witness, or a woman from the mouth of a woman, or a slave or maidservant or relative — they are fit for this testimony; and disqualified witnesses if they are disqualified rabbinically are fit for this testimony; but Torah-disqualified witnesses are disqualified; and if they speak offhandedly they are fit; and likewise a Samaritan, or an Israelite apostate regarding idolatry and regarding the entire Torah — if he speaks offhandedly he is believed. {Rama: If no witness came in the ways mentioned but a rumor spread in the city that he died — they do not permit his wife (Terumat HaDeshen siman 220 and in the responsum of the Ramban siman 80).}
