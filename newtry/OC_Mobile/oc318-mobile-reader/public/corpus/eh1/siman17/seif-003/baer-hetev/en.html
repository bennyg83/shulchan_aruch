Even one witness. See Maharival ch. 3 siman 89; Mishpat Tzedek siman 63. If initially when they asked him about the deceased he said he did not know and afterward testified he died — there is room to attribute why he did not wish to say initially — his testimony is valid; and if what he said that he does not know he did not say before a court of three even if there is no room to attribute why he said he does not know he returns and testifies — Rashdam ch. 1 siman 81. The witness testified his testimony and at the end of his words said a letter will come from the scholars — we do not say perhaps he did not complete testifying and did not want them to rely on his words or that he is judged as one who comes to be judged with presumption and document; rather they permit the woman on his testimony — Maharik ch. 2 ch. 1 siman 134.
Even the witness is a slave. Specifically where it is known he is fit — Beit Chadash, Beit Shmuel; see Choshen Mishpat.
Or a relative. Even his son or daughter or her maidservant from the woman herself — Rashdam ch. 1 siman 235.
Even witness from witness. Not witness from witness but even witness from witness from another witness even one hundred — Kenesset HaGedolah in name of responsum of Kenesset Yisrael. Whether the first witness died or was validated — need not send after him even as brought below — Mahari Weil siman 9 and Rama brought them in seif 8; and so Kenesset HaGedolah 19b seif 134. And I placed a spark there on s.k. 28. If witness from witness forgot who the witness is we are not concerned for trickery — She'erit Yosef siman 32. Witness from witness is valid both for leniency as here and for stringency when he says (who) [he] is — Rashdam siman 59; and Kenesset HaGedolah in name of Radbaz; and Ra'anach wrote specifically to permit her not to forbid — see there.
From the Torah. Meaning disqualified due to sin. One sent to another city to search if any testimony will be found to permit the woman — they give him one hundred gold coins; and if he does not bring testimony sufficient to permit the woman they give him only fifty gold coins. He came and said he heard from a gentile incidentally that a Jew was killed in the crusade — Maharival ch. 1 55b was uncertain whether to rely on his testimony since he has personal interest; see Mishpat Tzedek ch. 1 siman 7 and Mekor Baruch siman 22 and Ma'aseh Binyamin siman 98.
Or an apostate Israelite. One who says several times he will apostatize even if they appeased him and he did not act — possible to be stringent he is disqualified from the Torah — Maharival 63 siman 6; see Maharival ch. 2 siman 13; see Kenesset HaGedolah in Beit Yosef's gloss seif 21.
