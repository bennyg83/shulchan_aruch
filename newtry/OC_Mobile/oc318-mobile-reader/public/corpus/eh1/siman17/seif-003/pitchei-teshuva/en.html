That she said to her husband in his presence. See responsum Noda Biyhuda Tinyana siman 91 — what Maharashdam was uncertain — Rama wrote in siman 152 that if she married not in husband's presence even if afterward in his presence she says "you divorced me" — not believed; and Noda Biyhuda answered the reason is because she already married — she acts brazenly; see there at length.
