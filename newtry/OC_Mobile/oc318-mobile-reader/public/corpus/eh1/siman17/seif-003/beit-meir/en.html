See Beit Shmuel — "and if she began," etc. Choshen Mishpat is uncertain; however one may say invalid get to divorce — therefore if she began, etc., there is still concern, etc. See siman 152 where he repeated the matter: per Taz even if she began not in his presence — Rama wrote law as stated siman 152 in Tur in his name — "or he divorced her with invalid get and she does not know" — if so wonder: even when he says "I divorced you" and he contradicts her — also concern, for in any case we believe her only that he divorced with get; let us say both speak truth — she because she accepted get brazenly and he because he knows he always said "I did not divorce you." And per his words — even if she accepted kiddushin from another she is not betrothed, etc., as he wrote — unlike what is sealed in Shulchan Aruch. And what R' Yonah explained — from silence and no contradiction she is betrothed and permitted — Beit Yosef agrees. But in my humble view Rama never intended his reason for his ruling; rather in the Gemara regarding husband who said "I divorced" or not — early authorities questioned and answered: if so that he divorced her — a rumor would reach him; question: if so a woman who said "he divorced me" — also say if so, etc.; in Bava Batra we need whether we say migo in place of hazakah — at least it seems migo is not weaker than hazakah and they are balanced; if we nullify migo due to married woman we nullify hazakah that she is not brazen due to married woman — perforce strained that hazakot are not equal. But apparently Rama poses the question initially — there is no migo here; therefore he wrote in this language: even if she admitted to him that he divorced or perhaps he intends to ruin her or divorced with invalid get and she does not know, or perhaps she will act brazenly since he believes her — meaning perhaps entirely he said he divorced only to ruin her and there is no migo at all; he again explains modes of ruin: mode one — or he divorced with invalid get to ruin her and what she admits because she does not know; mode two — or perhaps he did not divorce at all and what she admits — perhaps she will act brazenly, etc.; all this we concern; because he intends ruin — thereby removes "since it is in his power to divorce her" — all this applies only to one who says "I divorced you" coming to permit her now in his lifetime; one may say his intention that they marry again his words will clarify for ruin; but if he has sons it does not help her except after death — no intention to ruin. But where he did not begin but she began to act brazenly — immediately comes to concern of invalid get — behold stam scribe of judge, etc.; also husband is not suspected to divorce with invalid get — therefore essence in my view: once she began brazenness before him without his support she is already permitted; what harm his admission until we concern perhaps invalid get (see siman 164 what I wrote). Again I saw Beit Yosef already brought clear proof from Rama himself unlike my view — "and if she married not in husband's presence although," etc. — if so there is support, etc., or he precisely wrote "or not" and not "kiddushin do not take effect in her" as above from what he wrote: but if another betrothed her not in husband's presence kiddushin do not take effect in her; and in Tur siman 152 he wrote also this — no get needed from second in my view — because Rashba responsum siman 1253 disputes this reasoning on the question: Reuven betrothed woman and another came and betrothed her not in his presence — reasonable no get needed from second, etc.; but in our case specifically when she does not now say he divorced her; but if she says he divorced — forbidden from Rav Hamnuna; and even though she began not in his presence we do not say initially she was not believed and now establish her as liar — as proven in chapter Ha'ishah sheNitarmelah — we learn: two say, etc., and we challenge: what difference — Rava said, etc., and we challenge: if brazen — but Rav Hamnuna said, etc. — meaning even though she began brazenness not in his presence she is not established liar now in his presence; it appears from his proof even if she married — similar to our case Rosh also is not established liar, etc.; nevertheless it appears her law is not like beginning in his presence per Shulchan Aruch above — rather if she married forbidden to both and leaves both with get; if only betrothed — first divorces and second marries — meaning stam "or not" is for leniency that she be permitted to second; but certainly needs get. And he said: therefore he wrote this language and requires get — because Beit Chadash precisely on Tur siman 152 — she made herself one-sided; if so all Rashba's question: why mention in our case when she now says in his presence forbidden to all without get — must answer from reason she made herself one-sided in what began not in his presence and accepted kiddushin; or we are compelled by Beit Chadash's words — at least in betrothal so she not remove herself from first we do not concern she made herself one-sided — compelled per his reason. But in marriage where without this forbidden to first — Tur's view uncertain; see Beit Shmuel s.k. 12 — they answered; but to require get one cannot say one-sided — his words are brief and unclear; rather Taz wrote there is only if prohibition at time of her speech; not so one who says "I am divorced" — at that time permitted to all per her words — only if another betroths her kiddushin take per his view and she becomes forbidden to others — this did not enter her mind to consider — end quote. In my view such reasoning — one who comes to betroth her on condition; and all responsa of Rosh in Beit Yosef this siman concluding "she leaves both" — no leaning to Tur's view; more explained like from first with get so from second — uncertain. Some say if there was quarrel between them — this is Rosh's view; Tur siman 152 proved thus in Ha'ishah sheNitarmelah from one variant — wonder: but in this sugya it states: quarrel — one who says "divorce me" — all women say thus; Rashi explained at time of anger; but one who says "you divorced me" — also stam from quarrel and anger; nevertheless they challenge: let us believe her from Rav Hamnuna. One should not force to establish sugya in Gittin in such quarrel where already contradicted by saying "you divorced me" and it turns out one side — great forced difficulty; and if so Shulchan Aruch law also in such quarrel; also need not write "must answer" that her brazenness hazakah weakened — cannot write in name of some in his presence.
