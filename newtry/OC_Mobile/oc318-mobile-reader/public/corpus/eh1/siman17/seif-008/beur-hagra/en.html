And there are those who say, etc. This follows from Yevamos there: 'Let her be believed because of Rav Hamnuna,' where she is believed even to marry. Likewise, in the chapter HaMekabel, folio 64: 'Let us say there is a presumption,' etc.; 'that applies only stringently,' etc.; 'let her be believed,' etc. Necessarily this applies even leniently. She likewise collects the kesubah through the exposition of the kesubah. Kesubos is no proof to the contrary, as Tosafos wrote in Gittin 89b, s.v. 'and the other,' etc.
