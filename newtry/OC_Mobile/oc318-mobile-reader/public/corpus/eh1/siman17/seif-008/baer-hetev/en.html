Choshen Mishpat is uncertain if she said in name of slave and maidservant or fit witness testifies in name of slave or maidservant and the slave and maidservant come and contradict or the fit witness — whether they can contradict. And in responsum She'erit Yosef siman 32 and brought below 174 siman 305 he ruled one cannot contradict the woman or the fit witness; see 174 siman 159 195 297. And if two witnesses say in name of one — that one cannot contradict two witnesses. And if the first witness comes and says he lied — Choshen Mishpat wrote he is believed since the first statement was outside court. Also wrote the woman must investigate well lest the first retract his testimony — Beit Shmuel wrote on this certainly his intent is good advice; but by law we are not concerned perhaps the first will retract and say he lied in testimony — for otherwise how permit witness from witness when the first witness is not before us. That he is in the city. This matter is not explicit in responsum Mahari Weil from whom the rabbi derives this law — there it deals where the first witness lived in another land; see there siman 9 he wrote explicitly: if you wish say send there to the land of Brindburg and ask them themselves whether the law is so, etc.; and what is written there "and strained to say" all deal where they are not present, etc. — meaning they are no longer in the other land and we do not know their place; see there for all his words deal where he was in another land. And in Maggid Mishneh ch. 13 Laws of Testimony he wrote on Rambam who wrote: we already informed that a witness who says I heard he died Ploni — even if he heard from a woman who heard from a slave he is fit for testimony of a woman and they permit on his word; but if the witness or woman or slave said Ploni died and I saw him — we ask him how he saw, etc.; and all this is brought in Shulchan Aruch siman 17 seif 5. And Maggid Mishneh wrote there: even when he says I heard he died we do not know how the first speaker knew he died — we do not judge impossible from possible for thus it is since it is possible we certainly ask him, etc. If so whenever the first witness is in the city too it is possible — why not ask him. And Rabbi Beit Shmuel copied responsum Mahari Weil who wrote: are all the mishnayot and all the poskim who permit on witness from witness dealing where the first witness is not in this city — he did not copy well and whoever adds detracts; for there it is written are all the mishnayot, etc. dealing where they are not present; and no more. And I already wrote per his words there it deals where they are no longer in the other land; see there. And what is written further one who examines the sugya will see Mahari Weil learned this law from this sugya; see there.
