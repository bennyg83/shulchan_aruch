In her husband's presence. See Baer Heitev, who writes, 'If she began saying it outside her husband's presence,' etc. Bnei Ahuvah, chapter 7 of Hilchos Gerushin, halacha 24, writes that the Rambam's words imply that she is believed. The Rivash, upon whom the Darkei Moshe and Beis Shmuel relied to disagree, does not state this conclusively, but merely expresses some uncertainty; see there. Regarding Baer Heitev's words, 'and so it appears to me,' etc., see what I wrote in Nachalas Tzvi.
