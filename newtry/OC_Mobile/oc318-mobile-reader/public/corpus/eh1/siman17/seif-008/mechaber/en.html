If one witness said to a woman privately "your husband died" — she need not again require his testimony; rather she comes to court and says "my husband died." But if the witness who said in her name that he died comes and says he did not say "died" — he is not believed. {Rama: And the court need not send after him even though he is in his city — rather they rely on witness from witness ab initio (responsum of Mahariw siman 9).}
