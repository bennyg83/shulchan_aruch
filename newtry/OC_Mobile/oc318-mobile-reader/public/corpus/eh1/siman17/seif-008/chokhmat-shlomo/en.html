See in my edition 3 in my responsum 130 to Bilyakamin what I wrote on these matters; and in explanation of the language of Yerushalmi in the mishnah "my husband died and afterward my son died" — many matters belonging to these laws; and in my composition in Mahadura Kama I also extended on the law of mother-in-law who comes afterward — examine. And behold if the daughter of her co-wife who has a husband from another man, or daughter of her husband's brother and the like — is she permitted to testify for her; or daughter of her mother-in-law who comes afterward — see in my edition 3 in my responsum 130 to Zinkowitz in this section seif 24 what I wrote that all are believed — see there in his reason; and see in Gevurat Pinchas who was uncertain in this; and in my humble opinion it is clear all are believed — see there what I extended with God's help; examine. And behold the later authorities brought the opinion of Shulchan Gavoah that males similar to these are believed; and in my opinion uncertain from the mishnah in Gittin 23 that taught there "even the women who are not believed to testify can bring her get — her mother-in-law," etc.; difficulty: why specify those; granted in Yevamot it teaches which women are not believed; but here that says "even the women who are not believed to testify on her husband's death can bring her get" — automatically we know what they are from the mishnah in Yevamot; therefore it would appear the opposite — one may say males all the more so they are not believed, since the reason one witness for death is believed is because of dayika; if so one may say with males testifying the woman should not fear damage and they will not be suspected in her eyes to lie; therefore do not infer all this; but with women who hate her — since women's minds are light and she herself will fear they intend her damage — therefore infer properly and she will be believed because of dayika uminsaba; therefore it teaches "even the women are not believed" and all the more the males. Per this one may say that although Chazal said the women who are not believed to say her husband died are believed to bring her get — one may say specifically females; and according to what Tosafot wrote in Ketubot 26b that also in divorce dayika applies lest witnesses be arranged — one may say here too writing alone does not help except combined with dayika that she fears lest they testify to damage her; therefore dayika and combined with the writing proves we rely on dayika uminsaba; but with males who are not suspected in her eyes to lie out of hatred — no dayika; therefore even for a get they are disqualified; therefore the tanna of the Gemara specifies women to exclude males who are not believed. However in my opinion it is correct — I have no power to innovate what is not mentioned in any posek; and particularly one must bring proof to the contrary for the opinion of Shulchan Gavoah from what is asked in the Gemara regarding mother-in-law who comes afterward; and the Gemara resolves from what the tanna taught five women — behold there are six; and the Gemara rejects — still the count, why need the count; and since it counts and continues — why need the count; and similarly challenges in the first chapter of Yevamot and several places; perforce it will be proven it excludes males; therefore the tanna taught the count; and it is proven males are believed; if so uncertain on the mishnah in Gittin why it specified the names of the women — uncertain now; examine. And see further in my composition Mahadura Zayin siman 181 in my responsum where I wrote proof to the contrary also from the words of Shulchan Gavoah; and I also raised that males too are not believed, and also in ashtamud'inanhu the five women are not believed nor males similar to them — see there with God's help; examine.
