One came and said to us "the court or people said: when you go to such-and-such place say to them that Yitzchak son of Michael died"; and the messenger came and said to us, and the messenger does not know who he is — since we know the known so-and-so by this name, behold his wife is permitted; and it cannot be that perhaps another Yitzchak son of Michael died — provided two Yitzchak sons of Michael were not established there, or when two were established and it is known one exists. {Rama: And some say all this is specifically when the witness mentioned the name of his city; but if he did not mention the name of his city — even though they know one was lost, it does not help even though he mentioned his name (responsum of the Rivash and responsum of the Ran and Maharik shoresh 184). And some are lenient; and some say in a place where he mentions only his name we require he mention his city; but if he mentions his name and his father's name we do not require he mention the name of his city (the Rosh kelal 51 and so too Maharik shoresh 176). And in a place where there are estimations and proofs that this is he — one may be lenient and rely on the one who says we do not require he mention the name of his city (Maharik shoresh 184 and responsum of the Rosh).}
