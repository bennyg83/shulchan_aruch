But those disqualified from testimony according to the Torah. Kehillas Yaakov writes: The language of the poskim seems to imply that all persons disqualified according to the Torah under Choshen Mishpat, siman 34, are disqualified from testimony concerning a woman's husband's death. However, Nimukei Yosef in the chapter Zeh Borer and in chapter 7 of Yevamos writes explicitly that one who is not wicked for monetary gain is disqualified only because of the Torah decree, 'Do not place a wicked person as a witness.' Therefore, in matters of prohibition that do not require formal testimony, where even a woman is valid, a wicked person whose transgression is not for monetary gain should likewise be valid. Beit Yosef, siman 28, also raises this possibility. It is astonishing that the authorities here did not mention the Nimukei Yosef, since no dissenting view is found. Nevertheless, one should be stringent. End quote.
