Beit Shmuel sign 36 — and whoever comes to beit din to testify. Nb: And if he came to the community there is room to question whether it is like coming to beit din — Maharival ch. 3 siman 20. And if he said "they told the wife of so-and-so her husband died" it is not called mesalechet — Noda B'Yehuda ch. 2 of Yevamos. And if he said "they told the household of so-and-so" it is mesalechet — Maharival. And see responsa of Rambam; and if he said "they told his wife and his sons" see responsa Or Ne'elam siman 35.
