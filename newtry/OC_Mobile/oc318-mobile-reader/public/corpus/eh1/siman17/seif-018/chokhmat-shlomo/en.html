Seif 27 — They found him killed, etc. Behold the poskim brought the opinion of Mabit and it is brought in Tikkun Soferim to be lenient when the killed one is found on the same road he was lost — based on this presumption like a lost object; and I already used this reasoning in several places. And behold now I wonder on this reasoning from tractate Bekhorot chapter 1 (7b) where it says the reason "I see it swallowed," etc. — if so we see it swallowed we say this one was eaten and this one spawned — difficulty: what does it challenge — let us say it is presumption that the lost one is the found one; perforce it is proven as I wrote in responsum already that we do not say "this is the one that was lost" except where they found him as he was before he was lost; but if he changed somewhat from before he was lost we do not say this presumption; therefore since the Gemara's intent is they did not find him whole — if so it is not as when he was lost; therefore this presumption is inapplicable; therefore it answers they found him whole; if so there is presumption this is the one lost. But from Rashi there s.v. that they found him whole — they found all his form and he is large; and if he were a fetus he would not remain in the womb so long, etc. — implies even when found whole we do not say this is the one lost, etc.; uncertain in this; examine well. And behold in law to say regarding agunah "this one lost is this one found" — see in my composition on Niddah in the omissions on Yoreh De'ah siman 197 page 120 in responsum 130 to Brigel where I raised one may say regarding a matter that has no permission for all we say this one lost even for Rashbag and they do not dispute this at all — see there with God's help; examine.
