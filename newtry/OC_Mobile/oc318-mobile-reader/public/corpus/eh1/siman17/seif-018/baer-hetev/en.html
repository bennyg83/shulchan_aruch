There, etc. Meaning in that city; see Beit Shmuel and in Ra'am siman 5; and Maharam Lublin siman 7 even in presumption if a gentile said to a Jew Ploni who was going with you to villages if he was not going with him only this one they permit his wife — Maharival ch. 1 klal 1 siman 6.
His city. See Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel. And likewise if the Jew said Reuven died it is as if he said Reuven from my city died since the Jew said — Maharshadam siman 53. A gentile asked a Jew where do you go and the Jew answered to the city meaning city Ploni as is the way of village people near a city to say to the city stam and the gentile said they killed the Jew who was selling in the city street, etc. — is like mentioning name of his city — Rashdam siman 53. And greater Ra'anach ch. 1 siman 20 wrote if the incidentally speaker asked the questioner are you from city Ploni and he answered yes and the gentile said they killed Ploni — behold it is like mentioning name of his city. Mentioning name of his city without mentioning his name and father's name is preferable to mentioning his name and father's name without mentioning name of his city — Mishpetei Shmuel siman 81. Reuven was killed and they sought him and a gentile incidentally came and said I found this killed one you seek — is like mentioning his name — Maharam Alshikh siman 405. Bridegroom Ploni is like mentioning his name and father's name; and likewise servant Ploni — Ra'ash in responsum siman 9 and siman 44. Ploni husband of Plonit or Ploni brother of Ploni is like mentioning his name and father's name. Even the witness testifies in the city itself from which the killed came need mention name of his city — Maharival ch. 1 siman 5; responsum She'erit Yosef siman 31 distinguishes first witness to second witness who testifies he heard — more lenient even if he did not mention the city; and so Maharam Alshikh siman 44 and 118 and Mahari Metrassi ch. 2 siman 134; but Maharival ch. 2 siman 15 and Ra'anach ch. 1 siman 27 are stringent; see Kenesset HaGedolah. Name of country does not help. Darkei Moshe wrote it helps poskim who hold must mention name of city — if he did not mention even if not common he left and was not presumed forbidden; accordingly seif 11 must have mentioned name of city; see Beit Shmuel.
His father. Even though the deceased was a kohen need not mention him as kohen — Mahari Levi siman 13. Choshen Mishpat wrote specifically when there are grounds for the matter such as knowing Ploni went to that place and this one died near this city. And Maharshadam siman 35 for law we rely if he said his name and father's name even if he did not mention name of city; see Kenesset HaGedolah who expanded.
