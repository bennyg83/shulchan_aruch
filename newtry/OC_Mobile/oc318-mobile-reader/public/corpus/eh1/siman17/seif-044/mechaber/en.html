She came and said "my husband died, permit me to marry and give me my ketubah" — they permit her to marry and give her ketubah, because the essence of her words is regarding marriage matters. But if she came and said "give me my ketubah and permit me to marry" — they permit her and do not give her ketubah; and if she seized it they do not remove it from her hand. And there is one who says that in both this and that they do not permit her since she mentioned ketubah.
