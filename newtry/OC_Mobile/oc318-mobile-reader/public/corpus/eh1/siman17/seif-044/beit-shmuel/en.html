And if she seized it, we do not remove it from her. This is the Rambam's view. This question remained unresolved. See these laws in Choshen Mishpat siman 390. Some say that even the first question, where she says "permit me and give me my ketubah," was not resolved. They understand that the question is whether we permit her to marry, lest because of her desire for money she marry in violation of the prohibition. The Beit Yosef and Chelkat Mechokek write that the Rosh did not have the reading "if you will say," and for that reason he holds that the question was not resolved. This implies that had he had the reading "if you will say," he would hold that the question was resolved like the view of the Geonim. In Tur Choshen Mishpat, however, the Beit Yosef did not write this. See siman 122.
