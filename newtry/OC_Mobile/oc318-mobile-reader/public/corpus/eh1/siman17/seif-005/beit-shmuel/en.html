Regarding the five women who do not testify for one another..
We ask him: how did you see? Although if he said that he heard it from another, we do not know from whom the first one heard it, we do not judge the possible from the impossible. Where it is possible to ask him, we ask him. Although derishah and chakirah are not required in testimony concerning a woman, nevertheless one must ask how he knows; so wrote the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 3. It appears that if he says he heard it, we also ask him from whom he heard it, as written nearby in the name of Shiltei Gibborim, for it is possible to clarify. What the gloss wrote, that if he said simply that he heard, it is valid, refers to where it is impossible to clarify. Although this is not implied by his wording, nevertheless one should be stringent in this, and so wrote the Taz. See Kuntres Agunot simanim 139, 148, and 233.
