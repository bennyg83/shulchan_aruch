There is a presumption that a woman does not act brazenly. See Chelkas Mechokek and Beis Shmuel, who cite the Darkei Moshe's question on the Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham. If she accepted kiddushin from another, etc., she is permitted to return to the first man, yet we say that if the second man divorced her she may not return to the first, lest people say, etc. See Bnei Ahuvah, chapter 4 of Hilchos Ishus, halacha 13, who resolves that Rabbeinu Yerucham is not discussing a woman who said she was divorced, but one who said she had never been betrothed to the first man at all. In that case it is obvious that she may return to him, and there is no concern. We should not add to the Sages' decree where no such extension is clearly found in their enactment; see there.
