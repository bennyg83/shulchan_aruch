If she was standing on her roof and he threw it to her onto the top of the roof, if it has a parapet, once it reaches the airspace within the partitions of the parapet, or even if it has no parapet and it comes within three tefachim of the roof floor, she is divorced, even if it burned before coming to rest, provided that the get preceded the fire, for it was fit to come to rest were it not for the fire that came after he threw it. But if the fire preceded it, she is not divorced, for it was not fit to come to rest in her domain.
