(ב) In her courtyard, and it is guarded with her knowledge. One must understand what "with her knowledge" means. In chapter HaZorek, Ulla says this applies only where she stands by her house or courtyard. R. Oshaya says that even if she is in Tiberias and her courtyard is in Tzippori, she is divorced. The Gemara asks from "she is in her house or courtyard" and answers that it means as though she were in her courtyard, since the courtyard is guarded with her knowledge. Rashi explains: as though she is in her house and courtyard; "she" means that the courtyard must be guarded by her will and command, excluding the later case where he placed the get in the hand of her awake slave, where she is not divorced, because it is a courtyard guarded not by her knowledge but by the slave's own mind. Taz explains that even Ulla agrees that guarding with her knowledge is required; the dispute is whether her physical presence is also required. The note further clarifies the difference between a courtyard, a slave, and other forms of guarded domain: acquisition by courtyard works for a get only when the guarding is legally attributed to her, not to an independent actor.
