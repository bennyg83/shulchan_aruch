Because presumably he had relations. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, siman 61, regarding a minor whose father married him off before twelve years of age, and he lived two years after the marriage. About a year before his death he traveled from his wife’s house to his father’s house, and there was doubt whether he had reached thirteen years and one day before leaving his wife. Even if investigation would show that he had reached the age before leaving, there was still doubt whether he had brought signs. The responsum discusses whether we assume that after majority he had relations for the sake of kiddushin, whether the presumption that a man does not make his relations promiscuous applies to a minor who grew up, and how to treat the absence of seclusion or proof of relations after majority. He distinguishes between cases where there was actual seclusion fit for relations after majority and cases where there was not, and analyzes whether a get is required because of possible rabbinic or biblical kiddushin. See there for the full discussion.
