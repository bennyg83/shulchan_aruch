Law: there is no kiddushin for a minor; and the laws of a minor boy and girl if they matured. It contains 2 seifim. A minor who betrothed or married — it is nothing, for the Sages did not institute marriage for a minor; and it is forbidden to marry him to a woman while he is still a minor. {Rama: However if he already married — need not divorce her and she may remain until he matures (Mordechai start He Comes Upon His Yevamah) (and so Ashiri chapter One Who Betroths).} And even if he sent gifts after maturing — it is nothing, for we are not concerned perhaps he sent for kiddushin. But if there are witnesses that he was secluded with her after maturing — she needs a get, for presumably he married for kiddushin. And if she fulfilled ketubah obligations after maturing — her ketubah stands and need not write another; specifically maneh and two hundred; but no additions. {Rama: If he did not write her a ketubah when he was a minor — when he matures he writes her only maneh, for at that time she is not a virgin (Hagahot Alfasi start One Who Was Married).}
