For the sages did not institute marriage. Since they will eventually enter the category of marriage, the sages did not institute marriage for them. Although they instituted marriage for a minor girl, there the reason is so that people not treat her lawlessly. Therefore, once a minor girl grows up, the rabbinic kiddushin grows with her and she requires a get by rabbinic law, as the Rambam wrote in chapter 11 halachah 3, and below in siman 155. But with a minor boy, even after he grows up the kiddushin does not grow at all, even by rabbinic law, unless he had intercourse or secluded himself with her after he became an adult.
