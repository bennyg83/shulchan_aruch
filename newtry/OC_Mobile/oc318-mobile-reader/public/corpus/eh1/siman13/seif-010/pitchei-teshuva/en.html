A woman who was divorced. See Sefer Yeshuot Yaakov s.k. 12 who brought in the name of responsum Chavatzelet Hasharon who was uncertain regarding an apostate whom her husband gave her a get (as written below siman 140 seif 5 in the hagahah) and afterward it was delivered to her hand and she returned afterward to Judaism — whether she must wait from when the get reached her hand or the agent's hand; and he was stringent in this — in his name. (And see Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel who challenged responsum of Maharam of Lublin siman 117 in the incident that the scribe erred and wrote Levi in the first get and he was not Levi and they required another get; and the second get was delayed between writing and giving a full year; and he wrote because of cancellation of shidduchim one should combine the Rif's view who holds we count from writing and Rabbenu Tam's view who counts from the first get; and there is room to be lenient in pressing circumstances; and on this they challenged: perforce Rabbenu Tam did not permit except if the first get was valid and was disqualified only because of foreign language — then he holds we count from the first get; but here that he wrote Levi and he was not Levi — it is void from the law (see siman 129 s.k. 21); also Rabbenu Tam agrees we count from the second — in his name. And see in responsum Chatam Sofer siman 27 who wrote to reconcile Maharam Lublin's words who holds such an error to write Levi where he is not Levi was not common like exchanging in kiddushin in the previous seif that the Sages decreed only per R' Yosi who was stringent in disqualifying the first get for mere foreign language — so too one should not be lenient, etc.; and accordingly he wrote in the case of his question that he arranged a get for so-and-so and four challenges were found whereby they required another get — in this Maharam Lublin also agrees one should not combine lenient views to permit even in pressing circumstances, for in this case it is close that the first get is biblically invalid since it is evident the arranger was not expert; and one cannot say it is not common, for unfortunately nowadays those who rush to arrange gittin are common who do not know the nature of gittin, etc.; therefore she must wait ninety days from the second get and we are not concerned for cancellation of her shidduch — in his name.)
