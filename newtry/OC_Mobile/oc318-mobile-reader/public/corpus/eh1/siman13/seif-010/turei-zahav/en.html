HaCholeitz in the name of She'iltot dispute on this; and possibly therefore they also are not lenient like the other matter of Or Zarua that if he married unintentionally to permit with mere separation; so he concludes — behold he only wrote as a possible way that one should not be lenient; and how do we abandon Or Zarua's plainness and rely on this possibility that has no proof at all; and further it is wondrous to me: per Terumat HaDeshen's conclusion there is no distinction between marriage and betrothal — why did Rama write this law specifically regarding betrothal; for in truth even regarding marriage permitted with a kohen's wife per Rama's decision who so divides to rule leniently with a kohen's wife — and this is Or Zarua's view — and this is even regarding marriage; and so seems primary to be lenient even regarding marriage with a kohen's wife; and even with an Israelite there is no reason to be stringent regarding unintentional — except Rama was stringent in this.
