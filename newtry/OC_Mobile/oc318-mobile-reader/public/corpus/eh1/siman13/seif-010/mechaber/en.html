One who betroths within ninety days — we place him under nidui: {Rama: And some say he must divorce; and if he is an Israelite he may return her after three months, and a kohen may not return (Tur). And specifically when he transgressed intentionally; but if he betrothed unintentionally — he need not divorce, only we separate them (Mahari Weil siman 73). And some are stringent even regarding unintentional (Terumat HaDeshen siman 216). And it seems regarding a kohen who is forbidden to return — one may rely on the words of those who are lenient and we do not require divorce; but regarding an Israelite one may not rely on one who is lenient regarding unintentional — so it seems to me. If he divorced her — she is forbidden to dwell in his alley (Rivash siman 396). If bet din did not coerce him until three months passed, even though he betrothed in transgression — we do not coerce him further, for once they passed they passed (Mordechai ch. HaCholeitz in the name of Maharam). And if he married and divorced — see above this siman seif 12 what his law is regarding ketubah.} If he betrothed and fled — we do not place him under nidui {and we teach him to flee (Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Yechiel).} Some say this fleeing must be to a distance such that the time of his return would be after three months {and it does not seem so from the words of the other poskim.}
