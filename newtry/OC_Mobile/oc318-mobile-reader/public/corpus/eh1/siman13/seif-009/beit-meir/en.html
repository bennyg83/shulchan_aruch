Af Al Pi — per his words it is not compelled, for he explains well Rabbenu Tam's proof that his inference is from the first reason of pregnant — even regarding a divorcee and the same nursing; and it was at least proven child regulations apply to nursing even though not obligated from the Torah for the month — for the conclusion "presumptively" is only regarding a widow; but in any case plainly possible for the Raavad pregnant refers also to a divorcee; and per the conclusion the reason changed the explanation and refers only to a widow; and if so one may say properly even though the Raavad deals also with dried breasts — per the conclusion not with dried breasts; however nevertheless the law is true that without proof it cannot be that pregnant with dried breasts is permitted; for regarding nursing they said dried breasts is not called nursing and she is not included in the decree; but pregnant plainly is called forbidden; and one may say properly this is the Rambam's reason; and he holds even though we conclude not from that reason — fitting to decree; nevertheless since they decreed from nursing they again did not distinguish and decreed also on dried breasts from the reason "lest she become pregnant"; and Chelkat Mechokek already wrote: possibly he did not read except; and in my humble opinion further: even at minimum those who do not mention the reason "lest she become pregnant" — nevertheless they agree as above; and they hold specifically nursing with dried breasts — "presumptively" is not decreed on account of presumptive nursing in general that has a voice as in the Rosh that certainly recognizable between she gave her son to a wet-nurse and did not give; and this does not apply to pregnant; and there is room to decree; and not like Beit Shmuel's implication that per the elder Rosh pregnant with dried breasts is permitted; and some are lenient regarding a zona — see in this responsum.
