Pilegesh. Chelkat Mechokek s.k. 6; and see Sefer Yeshuot Yaakov who wrote on him that his words are very wondrous in what he wrote: a pilegesh if she became pregnant from him and was betrothed to another and he divorced her and the first wishes to return her — she need not wait; and it is a wonder why it should differ from the law of seif 3; and is a pilegesh preferable to an ordinary woman — in his name; also in Sefer Beit Meir he wondered at him in this.
