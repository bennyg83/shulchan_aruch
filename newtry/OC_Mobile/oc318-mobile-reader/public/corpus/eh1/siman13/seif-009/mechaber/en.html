A woman who was divorced and a disqualification rumor emerged about the get and they required another get because of the foreign language — some say she must wait three months from the second get; and some say she need count only from the first get: {Rama: And one should be concerned for the first view (it is the view of Rabbeinu Yitzchak and the Rosh, who wrote so in the Tur in their name); and so if she was divorced because of a mere betrothal rumor — she must wait (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rashba).}
