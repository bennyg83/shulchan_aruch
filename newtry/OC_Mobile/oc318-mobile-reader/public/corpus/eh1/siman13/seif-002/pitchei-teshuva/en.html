With her in the house — Avodat HaGershuni; and see in responsum Noda BiYehudah siman 19 who was asked on this regarding one who betrothed another's wet-nurse: whether he must swear not to enter the betrothed woman's house per Chelkat Mechokek or not; and even if it suffices that he swear not to enter alone without two guardians and it would be permitted to enter her house with guardians; and he wrote at first he wondered at the essence of Beit Yosef's words regarding waiting and concludes Beit Yosef's words can be answered; but Chelkat Mechokek's words regarding a wet-nurse are wondrous; except an elder already ruled; but to forbid even through guardians — even Chelkat Mechokek possibly agrees she is permitted, even within the months of waiting — in his name; and see Sefer Beit Meir on him in this; and see Sefer Yeshuot Yaakov who was also asked on this matter and concludes regarding waiting one should not be lenient and an oath is required; but regarding a wet-nurse one should not be stringent and so is the custom; and where halakhah is loose — go out and see what the people practice — in his name.
