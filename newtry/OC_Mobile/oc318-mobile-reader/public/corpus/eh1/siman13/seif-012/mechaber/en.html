If he transgressed and married a pregnant or nursing woman within this time — we place him under nidui unless he fled; and he removes her with a get; even if he was a kohen; and he must give her ketubah if she claims it from him; and if he was an Israelite — he may return her after twenty-four months of nursing; and he writes her another ketubah. If he married and fled, and after some time came and dwelt with his wife — it is of no consequence. If he betrothed a pregnant or nursing woman — we do not coerce him to remove her, and he may not marry until after the nursing period or until the child dies: {Rama: And some say there is no distinction between betrothal and marriage (most authorities); and so is primary; and see above this siman seif 10 how we practice.}
