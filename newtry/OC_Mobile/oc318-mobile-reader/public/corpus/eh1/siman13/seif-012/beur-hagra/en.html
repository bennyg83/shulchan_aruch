He returns, etc. From what is written — Rava challenged: therefore, etc.; and if it were so — what is his challenge; behold in any case he holds a married convert generally must wait; and as written in Semag: and marriages in their gentilehood are nothing; and it is returning his divorcee; and so in Nedarim 91b: and I returned in my days; and the Ran wrote there: and I returned to her, etc.; but Ritva wrote he cited "I returned" because regarding another she is forbidden until ninety days; and mishnah there — R' Acha like R' Yehudah and R' Yosi in Yevamot.
