Rather, we separate them. See Responsa Mishkenot Yaakov, siman 6, who brought what Darkei Moshe wrote in the name of Mahari Weil, and so it appears in his responsum: at minimum he must distance himself from the city where she lives by a journey of two or three days, and swear not to enter the city until twenty-four months have passed. Mishkenot Yaakov wrote that Rama did not explain this in Shulchan Aruch because he relied on one who studies the responsum of Mahari Weil and Darkei Moshe; this leaves some omission for those who study only Shulchan Aruch. In my humble opinion, since Rama omitted it from his glosses, this shows the matter was not clear to him and he did not wish to be stringent in this. I wrote similarly below in siman 128 seif 7 in the name of Responsa Noda BiYehudah and R. Akiva Eiger. The reason appears to be that Mahari Weil held like the Rosh and Tur, that fleeing requires going far away, as implied by his language that when separating them one does not need as great a distance as with flight, but it suffices that he flee to another city. But Maran Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit and Shulchan Aruch below in this seif wrote regarding the Rosh that this does not appear from the other poskim, and Maharshal in Yam Shel Shlomo, brought by Korban Netanel in chapter HaCholetz ot 11, agrees. If so, where separation suffices, it works even if he is in the same city, provided he does not enter her house. Also, in Responsa Noda BiYehudah simanim 12 and 18, where he mentions separation, he did not write that he must distance himself from the city. At the end of siman 12 he wrote that they remove her from the house so that they not be together, and at the end of siman 18 he wrote that he should separate from her and not come to that neighborhood. See there, and see what I write below, seif 12 s.k. 28.
