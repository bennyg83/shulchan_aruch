And even Shulchan Gavoah's words in this — nevertheless correct: a gentile wet-nurse is permitted to marry even if he recognizes even though he himself ruled ch. Af Al Pi — his words were brought siman 82 in the hagahah that even gentile women who nursed until recognition we coerce them to nurse for wages — nevertheless properly she can marry since there is no shame for her to come to bet din; also no concern to curdle milk — then she will feed him eggs herself; but what he permitted from this even a divorcee even recognizing to marry — this is not in my humble opinion; and what was challenged to the Gaon author the aforementioned responsum "and further that a nursing widow even if she does not wish to marry should be forbidden to give her son to a married woman to nurse lest her milk curdle; and even for unmarried lest she retract and meanwhile her milk stops and the child dies"; and he wrote: and it should be said that if she does not marry even if she retracts or curdles her mother will feed him eggs or she will find another; and what he wrote "or she will find" is insufficient — perhaps she will retract after recognition; and per the above even the mother's feeding eggs remedy is insufficient after nursing from the wet-nurse until recognition and she is considered to him as mother and his longing for her; but in my humble opinion this is difficult from the outset: specifically if marriage was not forbidden to her there is concern she will give to a wet-nurse in order to marry; but when marriage is forbidden to her — presumptively mothers nurse their sons; and if she gives to a wet-nurse — mostly it is from necessity that she cannot nurse herself; and then certainly no concern she will retract — what could she do; and for the minority the Sages were not concerned; and if the wet-nurse is married — concern lest she become pregnant; per the above the opposite is satisfactory: even if it curdles after recognition the wet-nurse will feed them eggs and not the mother; and eggs suffice; and it is astounding.
