In responsum Emunat Shmuel siman 3 he ruled: in a widow too we find she is not obligated to nurse — such as when she is wealthy and brought him two maidservants, or where she has no sustenance; and Beit Shmuel disagrees — in his name; and so in responsum Kenesset Yechzekel question 59 he also disagrees with responsum Emunat Shmuel in a fine pilpul; and from his words there he raised that he permitted if she gave her son to a wet-nurse before her husband's death, etc. Meaning: she gave to a wet-nurse who is unmarried; but a married wet-nurse — what relevance; perhaps the wet-nurse will become pregnant and her milk will curdle and who will feed the child eggs — in his name.
Regarding a divorcee. Meaning: not like the Rashba of one view who wrote regarding a divorcee specifically when she nursed him before she was divorced until he recognized her; but before this time — no. Bach ruled: a divorcee is permitted where the child does not recognize her and she does not wish to nurse. And Taz is stringent in every matter — Beit Shmuel.
