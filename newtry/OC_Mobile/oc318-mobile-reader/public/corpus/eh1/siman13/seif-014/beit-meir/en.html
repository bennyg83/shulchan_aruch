What we said — see Beit Yosef; and see responsum of the Gaon siman 14 who wrote: accordingly it is implied the elder Rosh permits even when he recognizes; and I was astounded exceedingly — behold Tosafot 60b: Rabbenu Nissim bar Yosef said: these are Beit Shammai's words, these are Beit Hillel's words; and per the elder Rosh's words where do we find it; and behold per Beit Shammai no woman is obligated to nurse, etc.; and it is strained: the essence of Beit Shammai's dispute was not said regarding marriage but regarding the essence of nursing time; and even though the infant nurses and goes until four and five years — nevertheless there is a difference if Beit Shammai explained: permitted to return him if explained before twenty-four months; and Beit Hillel forbids after eighteen months; for R' Meir and R' Yosi dispute regarding extending marriage; and on this they said "these are these" — implies their dispute too is regarding marriage; and per Perach it would seem plainly Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel do not dispute regarding extension — if so R' Meir and R' Yosi would not dispute the ancients' dispute; and the language "these are these" does not teach except that this dispute is relevant to that; and not that this is literally the dispute; and so is Rashi's explanation's implication who notes on Beit Hillel's words eighteen months and explains: sufficient for the infant with this; and if for the reason of permitting marriage at eighteen months — he should have noted eighteen months on R' Yosi's words; however not for the relevance of separation — if so it would be leniency of Beit Shammai; but only as Rashi explained for the infant's sufficiency; and Chelkat Mechokek on the father until how much he is obligated to strive in his nursing or feeding eggs so he not be endangered; but in Tosefta Niddah ch. 2 I found the version in this language after the dispute of R' Meir and R' Yosi: R' Natan ben Yosef said: Beit Shammai say twenty-four months and Beit Hillel say eighteen months — this implies per the Gaon Neriah's explanation; and the question is uncertain — unless therefore the Talmud changed the version and it is primary per Rashi's explanation.
