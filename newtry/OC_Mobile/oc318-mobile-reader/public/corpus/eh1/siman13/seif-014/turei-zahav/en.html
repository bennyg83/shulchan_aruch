Af Al Pi 60: Rabbeinu Shimshon says a divorcee is permitted to marry for she is not obligated to nurse as taught above; and Rabbenu Tam says forbidden to marry for it is taught in ch. HaCholeitz another's pregnant woman and wet-nurse he may not marry; and he decides and the tanna taught "presumptively widow" — "presumptively divorcee"; so he concludes; and they also brought another proof for this; and so the Rosh ch. HaCholeitz in the name of She'iltot: "presumptively divorcee" — "presumptively widow"; and if so the Rashba must hold like Rabbeinu Shimshon; and if so Shulchan Aruch should not have brought this view in this seif since he ruled above seif 11 whether widow whether divorcee; and since in the Gemara's language that said he may not marry another's wet-nurse — widow and divorcee are included — certainly there is no distinction between them in any matter; and Maharach distinguishes between divorcee and widow regarding if the infant does not recognize her and she says she does not wish to nurse — regarding divorcee she may marry; and I do not recognize this distinction at all — for what do we care about her statement; certainly every divorcee would say so and nullify the Sages' enactment that they enacted also regarding a divorcee; and Beit Yosef wrote: and I saw authorities like the Rashba and I did not suppress them since they have whom to rely on; so he concludes; and certainly one should not rule leniency in this as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rivash; only if she married she need not give a get in this — in this one may rely on the Rashba.
