A divorced woman or widow must wait ninety days before marrying, and one must not marry another's nursing woman or pregnant woman. It contains 14 seifim. Every woman who was divorced or widowed — she may not marry or become betrothed to another until she waits ninety days, apart from the day she was divorced or her husband died on it, and apart from the day she was betrothed on it — so that it may be known whether she is pregnant or not pregnant, to distinguish between the seed of the first and the seed of the last. {But she is permitted to make shidduchim without betrothal (Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of the Rosh and Terumat HaDeshen siman 217). Only that he may not cohabit with her in the house (there).} And from the day of writing the get we count for a divorcee, even if it was conditional or did not reach her hand until several years from the day of writing — we count, for he does not seclude with her from when he wrote it for her: {Rama: And some say we count from the day of giving the get (Tur in the name of the Rosh, and so it is proper to be stringent; so it seems to me).} And it is a rabbinic decree that even a woman not fit to bear children — even if she was divorced or widowed from betrothal — must wait ninety days; even a minor, old woman, barren woman, or ailonit; even if her husband was overseas, or a saris, or sick, or imprisoned, and one who miscarries after her husband's death; even a virgin from betrothal — must wait ninety days.
