In the house. He wrote: Beit Yosef wrote that the matchmaker should swear not to enter the betrothed woman's house until the time arrives, so they not come to mishap. And so is the law regarding a wet-nurse — so too Chelkat Mechokek s.k. 1 and s.k. 9 in his name. And Shevut Yaakov vol. 1 question 96 disagrees with Chelkat Mechokek regarding a wet-nurse; and he wrote specifically regarding waiting he was stringent in Beit Yosef, not so regarding another's wet-nurse or pregnant woman where he was more lenient — in his name; also Be'er Heitev ruled not like Chelkat Mechokek in this — in his name; and Maharam of Trani vol. 5 siman 170; and Kenesset HaGedolah in hagahah on the Tur seif 16 wrote like Chelkat Mechokek — in his name; and see Radbaz siman 192.
