To distinguish between his seed. Avodat HaGershuni s.k. 1 wrote: and it seems halakhah is like Rava; and one may also say even for Shmuel there is no biblical prohibition and this derivation is only an asmakhta; and see Sefer Beit R' Yonatan who brought from the Radbaz vol. 2 (in Sedilkov edition it is vol. 1) siman 264 who wrote in the name of Tosafot that distinguishing seed is rabbinic; but the plain language of the Rambam seems regarding a woman fit to bear it is from the Torah and all other women are a rabbinic decree — in his name; and he elaborated greatly that from the Rambam's words there is no decision; and he brought many proofs that the entire essence of waiting is only rabbinic — end of discussion; and so in responsum Shevut Yaakov vol. 1 siman 97 and in Malbim ch. 8 of Melakhim — in his name.
