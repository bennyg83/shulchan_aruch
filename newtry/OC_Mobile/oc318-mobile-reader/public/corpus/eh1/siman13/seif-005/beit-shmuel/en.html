Who were married. In the Rambam the word "married" does not appear; the Maggid explains so, for the Rambam ruled regarding a zona she need not wait — if so regarding a convert it is obvious she need not wait; and Beit Yosef returned here from what he wrote in his Beit Yosef and challenged the Maggid there.
Who converted. From this they are permitted to dwell together and we are not concerned lest they return to their corruption, per the view of those who forbid — see Tosafot Yevamot 42 and Yoreh De'ah siman 269.
