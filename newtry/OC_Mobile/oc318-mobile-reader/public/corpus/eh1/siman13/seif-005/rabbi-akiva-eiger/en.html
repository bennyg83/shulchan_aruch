In Beit Shmuel sign 20: behold it is taught in Bava Batra. N.B.: in Tosafot and Rosh it is not mentioned thus to prove by way of comparison of wet-nurse to pregnant woman; but plainly since it is proven pregnant divorcee is forbidden; and since in truth the reason in pregnant that she is forbidden is "presumptively will nurse" — if so it cannot be more severe than wet-nurse; and if so automatically there is no question from dried breasts of wet-nurse — one may say specifically regarding divorcee it is relevant; since they are lenient regarding wet-nurse and did not forbid because "we do not distinguish" — widow; and perforce because there is a rumor she is a divorcee; if so pregnant too should be permitted regarding divorcee; but regarding dried breasts there is no proof — one may say it cannot be and it is famous that her breasts dried; only giving to a wet-nurse is a rumor; but in ordinary giving we are concerned she does so in order to marry and perhaps the wet-nurse will retract; but dried breasts — there is no such concern; not so regarding pregnant where there is no indication she gave to a wet-nurse; one may say dried breasts is also forbidden because of a rumor that cannot be that her breasts dried; however still difficult is what is written regarding a wet-nurse who swore — forbidden because she comes to exchange; and to permit also without swearing — so too in her drying and giving.
