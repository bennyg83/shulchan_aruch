Hagahah: but if he betrothed unintentionally — see Terumat HaDeshen's words in Beit Shmuel; and Terumat HaDeshen wrote this from his own reasoning and also brings proof from what he decided the law of a kohen who betrothed a chalutzah without chalitzah in siman 164 seif 7 — deals only with unintentional; and in my humble opinion it does not seem so per the poskim there; and in particular per what is explained in Mahari Weil that man and woman must be unintentional; and in siman 164 it is implied she is intentional from halakhah and halakhah and was betrothed — which is the language of intentional.
