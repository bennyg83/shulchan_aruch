And from the day of writing, etc. There; and halakhah is, etc.; and the elders of Spain and Maggid Mishneh testified that so is the version in all old books; and so they said in the Yerushalmi ch. HaCholeitz: halakhah — some teach in the name of R' Chiya in the name of R' Meir: the chalutzah counts from the time of death; the divorcée from the time of giving her get; R' Chanina said from the time of giving her get; R' Yosi says from the time of writing; Rav and Shmuel — Rav like R' Chiya and Shmuel like R' Yosi, etc.; and halakhah: for a get we count from the time of writing if he did not seclude with her; and so Rav Ashi holds in the Gemara.
