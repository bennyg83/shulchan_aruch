And she wishes to marry Reuven. For she is permitted to him, as Rashba wrote — even regarding a pilegesh: if she married another and he divorced her she is permitted to the first.
She must wait. The reason: because we do not distinguish regarding waiting; even where she is not pregnant; and even though we do not say she became pregnant from Reuven; and that she need not wait until the child is twenty-four months like another's pregnant woman — because regarding pregnancy we are not concerned lest she prostituted with another if both admit she became pregnant from him, as written in responsum of the Rosh that both admitted; and we already have in siman 4 s.k. 40 regarding rabbinic prohibition we are not concerned lest she prostituted with another; but regarding waiting she must wait, for nevertheless she is included as another's divorcee.
