Yevamah whose yavam died. See Sefer Yeshuot Yaakov who wrote: even nowadays when we do not practice yibum and a yevamah is not closer to relations — nevertheless since she was already included in the decree, even though the reason ceased it does not return to permission; see in the Talmud Beitzah 5 and Tosafot there; and see what is written below siman 145.
