Further Beit Shmuel s.k. 29 wrote: and if he fled he need not divorce — so Tur and Rivash; in Rivash in my humble opinion it is explained to the contrary — for he concludes first on betrothing a wet-nurse that fleeing should not help to save from a get except regarding betrothal within three months; but regarding a wet-nurse even regarding betrothal fleeing does not help because of danger to the child; and again he wrote he does not wish to rely on his reasoning in this; and he wrote: however in all there is no difference since he did not flee and did not reveal his intent he does not wish to marry — we coerce him to remove and place under nidui until he divorces, per Rashi's view — explicitly his explanation even regarding betrothal if he did not reveal his intent that we coerce to divorce; and all the more regarding marriage — except like the Tur states; and Bach already wondered he contradicts himself: in the law within three months he did not mention that fleeing helps except regarding betrothal; and Rashi's reason is explained because he revealed his intent — from this we learn if he married fleeing does not help to save from a get; and how is it possible that here where the get is explicit in the Gemara it should be preferable that even for marriage he is exempt from the get through fleeing; and what Beit Shmuel wrote at the end "however in the Tur it is implied" — he wishes to interpret this — the mind cannot bear that though Tosafot distinguish thus — to the contrary the divorce in the Gemara is only for marriage; and if because per Rashi divorce is required even regarding betrothal within three months — possibly this prohibition is light; but the lenient view of fleeing that is found only regarding betrothal — to invent it for marriage to a pregnant woman because its prohibition is light — this was never heard; and also in the Rosh itself fleeing that helps for a wet-nurse was not mentioned except regarding betrothal; therefore in my humble opinion possibly the Tur abbreviated incidentally and his intent is only regarding betrothal; and thus I do not find explicitly in any commentary regarding marriage to a pregnant woman that fleeing helps to save from a get except Chelkat Mechokek above; and I already wrote what is difficult per the Rambam and per Rashi's explanation I do not find at all except in a responsum: and some commentators hold the same regarding marriage, for since he fled so as not to divorce — fleeing helps — and the same for wet-nurse and pregnant woman; its implication: even marriage to a pregnant woman suffices with fleeing to exempt from a get; and one may wonder: if so what is asked on 37a whether we are lenient regarding a kohen because if he divorces she is forbidden to him — and it should be taught that he has a remedy through fleeing; and perforce he holds fleeing saves only if he fled of his own accord; but we do not invent this advice for him and it is also not effective; and so in Darkhei Moshe in the name of Or Zarua; and I need to ask regarding a shoteh who has no mind to flee; and examine; and with this there is a question on Rama in seif 10 in the hagahah who decided regarding a kohen forbidden to return to be lenient like the Rambam that regarding betrothal within three months he need not give a get; and at the end he wrote we teach him to flee — and if he has this remedy from the Torah to be more lenient regarding her than an Israelite — behold from the place this leniency flows, i.e., regarding betrothal of a chalutzah — there is no other remedy; and it is a great strain to say that since the get is not incumbent on him because he is a kohen — automatically he is also exempt from fleeing; and examine.
