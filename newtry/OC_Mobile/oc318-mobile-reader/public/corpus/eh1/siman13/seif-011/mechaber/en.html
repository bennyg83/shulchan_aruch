The Sages decreed that a man may not marry or betroth another's pregnant woman or another's nursing woman until the child is twenty-four months old {that is, as months are established — one full and one lacking (Hagahot Mordechai ch. HaCholeitz)} apart from the day it was born and the day she was betrothed on it; and the month of pregnancy counts toward the twenty-four months {and some say ab initio one should be concerned even for the month of pregnancy (Terumat HaDeshen siman 216)} whether she is a widow, whether she is a divorcee, whether she is a zona {and some are lenient regarding a zona (Hagahot Mordechai ch. HaCholeitz in the name of some; and one may be lenient regarding one abandoned to prostitution so her husband will guard her (responsum of R' Yitzchak Minz siman 5))} even if she gave her son to a wet-nurse or weaned him within twenty-four months — she may not marry; even if the wet-nurse swore or vowed before the public not to retract; {and some say if the wet-nurse swore and he married — he need not remove her (Hagahot Mordechai in Ketubot).} even if she swore to a great person such as those who walk in the king's court; but if her son died — she is permitted to marry and we are not concerned lest she killed him; and so if she weaned him in her husband's lifetime, or she never nurses — such as if she has dried breasts, or her milk stopped in her husband's lifetime, and he hired a wet-nurse for him in her husband's lifetime, or she gave her son to a wet-nurse three months before her husband's death and she did not nurse at all within the three months — she is permitted to marry.
