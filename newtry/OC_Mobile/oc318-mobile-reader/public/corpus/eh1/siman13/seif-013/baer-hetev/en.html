I do not nurse. See above siman 80 from Beit Shmuel.
She began. This hagahah is not in its place; rather it refers to a woman who gave birth at the time of her husband's death and does not wish to nurse at all — nevertheless she is included as a nursing woman and forbidden to marry until twenty-four months; and all the more if she was pregnant at her husband's death — forbidden until twenty-four months after birth; see responsum Rivash siman 503, Beit Shmuel; and see above s.k. 24, s.k. 18, and s.k. 13 from Beit Shmuel.
