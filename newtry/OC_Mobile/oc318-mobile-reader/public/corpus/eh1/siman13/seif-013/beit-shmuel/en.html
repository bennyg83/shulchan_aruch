She may say. See below siman 80 when a woman is obligated to nurse; and a widow is equal in this to an unmarried woman, as Tosafot ch. Af Al Pi wrote; and if she claims ketubah she has no sustenance and is not obligated to nurse.
And there is no distinction, etc. This hagahah is not in its place; rather it refers to a woman who gave birth at the time of her husband's death and does not wish to nurse at all — nevertheless she is included as a nursing woman and forbidden to marry twenty-four months; and all the more if she was pregnant at her husband's death — forbidden until twenty-four months after birth; and see responsum Rivash siman 503 and Shulchan Gavoah ch. Af Al Pi.
