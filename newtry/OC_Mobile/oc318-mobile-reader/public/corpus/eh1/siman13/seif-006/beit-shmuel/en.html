And some say all these, etc. But a me'unah need not wait; and so a minor regarding prostitution, and a convert even an adult need not wait, as written in the Talmud per the version after Shmuel; and regarding a captive the Tur ruled she must wait, and the RaMaH holds she need not wait; and his reason seems to me: because a captive is different, for it is only a safek of prostitution; but a convert is close to vadai that she prostituted; and so in Derishah; and not like Beit Yosef and Bach who wrote the halakhah is like R' Yosi — for the Tur omitted the view of the Rif and Rambam who ruled like R' Yosi; even though regarding Israelite daughters' prostitution we decree on account of marriage, and regarding a convert when she is married she must wait — nevertheless we do not decree so much prostitution on account of marriage regarding a convert — so too the Rosh and Tosafot in the last answer; and not like Rashi.
A woman who was raped. Meaning: for poskim who hold a zona must wait; therefore this woman who was raped, who is permitted to her Israelite husband — nevertheless requires waiting if she did not have relations with her husband within three months — and so in Chelkat Mechokek.
