A me'unah need not wait; they decreed only regarding a divorcee. So too a zona need not wait, because she turns herself over at the time of relations so as not to become pregnant. So too a woman who was raped or seduced need not wait; and the same applies to a captive, even if she is an adult: {Rama: And some say all these must wait if they are adults and fit to become pregnant (Tur).
