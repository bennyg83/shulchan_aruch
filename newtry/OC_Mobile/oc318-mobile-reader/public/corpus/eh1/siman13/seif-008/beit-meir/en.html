Hagahah: if he divorced her — forbidden to dwell, etc. (Rivash siman 396) — examine: behold in Rivash it is said only regarding marrying a pregnant woman; and he compares to a kohen who divorced even though this is Torah and this rabbinic — all that they enacted, etc.; and siman 119: even regarding a kohen who divorced from betrothal permitted to dwell with her in an alley if not "they examined each other"; and if so from where to be stringent here regarding plainly one who divorced from betrothal; and perhaps because he hurried to betroth in a significant prohibition it is considered to him as certainly brazen.
