Law of one who stipulates for his son or daughter and died. It contains 1 seif. One who commanded to give to his daughter such-and-such money for her sustenance to buy land with them — whether he was mortally ill or healthy and died and behold the money is in the third party's hand and the daughter said give them to my husband whatever he wishes to do with them: if she was an adult and married — the authority is in her hand; and if she is still only betrothed — the third party does what was deposited in his hand; and if she is still a minor — even if married we do not listen to her; rather the third party does as the father commanded. {Rama: And some say we require that it was deposited from the start for this purpose; and see Choshen Mishpat siman 292.}
