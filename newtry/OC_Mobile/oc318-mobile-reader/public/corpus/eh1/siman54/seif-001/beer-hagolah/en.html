Tosafot and likewise Rosh there Laws of Marriage.
According to the Talmud's resolution in the view of Rabbi Meir, for Rava ruled in the name of Rav Nachman that the halakhah follows him, and as the Rif and the Rosh wrote, that we rule like Rav Nachman in monetary law; and so the Rambam ruled there.
