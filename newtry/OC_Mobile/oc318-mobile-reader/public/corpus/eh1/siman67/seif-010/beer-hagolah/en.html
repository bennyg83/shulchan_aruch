(ד) There, folio 112b: a person does not dwell with a snake in one basket, and the rabbinic enactment would not endure; therefore the Sages did not enact marriage for her. The Maggid Mishneh wrote that it is obvious that if a competent man married a deranged woman and she recovered, he must betroth her again after she recovers and write her a ketubah, because a deranged woman has no marriage at all.
