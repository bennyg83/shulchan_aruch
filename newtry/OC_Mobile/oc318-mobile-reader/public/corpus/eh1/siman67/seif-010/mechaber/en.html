A deaf-mute or deranged man who married women — even though the deaf man later heard and the deranged man later became sane — their wives have no claim against them. If they wish to maintain them after they became healthy — they have ketubah, and their ketubah is a maneh. And if beit din married off the deaf man and wrote her a ketubah on his property — she takes all that beit din wrote for her. But a deranged man — beit din does not marry him off.
