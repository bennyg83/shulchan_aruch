(ב) Only that in this case her ketubah is only one maneh according to Rambam. Meaning that he distinguishes between a minor who grew up, where we follow the beginning, when she was married as a virgin, and a gentile whose wife converted, where we follow the end, since she converted when she was already a non-virgin. His reason appears to be that we say in Eizehu Neshekh, folio 71, that a minor has acquisition rabbinically, since after he grows up he will be fit for agency; but a gentile, who never comes into the category of agency, has no acquisition. So too here, we say that the minor's wife has a right at the time of intercourse, when she was a minor girl, since ultimately she will have that right, and it helps her already now. This is unlike gentile women, who have no right, for she does not stand destined to convert; they converted afterward only incidentally. Therefore she now has no status of virginity. And see seif 11 regarding a deaf-mute woman, whose ketubah is only one maneh according to Rambam, although she is not a virgin. Certainly that is because it is like a minor, since she likewise will have a right when she becomes competent. But the Tur and Tosafot treat her like a minor.
