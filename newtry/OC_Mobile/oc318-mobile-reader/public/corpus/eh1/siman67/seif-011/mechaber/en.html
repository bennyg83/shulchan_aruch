A minor — even from nine years and above whom his father married off — has no ketubah. And if he had intercourse with her after he matured — she has the principal ketubah: two hundred for a virgin, a maneh for a widow. But an addition he wrote for her in his minority — she does not have (even if beit din married her off). And even the principal he does not collect by force of the ketubah document but by beit din's conditions; therefore he collects only from unsold property and not from property sold in his minority. And the same law applies to a convert whose wife converted with him and they remain together — except that in this case her ketubah is only a maneh per Rambam's view.
