And see Beit Meir who disputes and holds other betrothal is required; he wrote: although bedieved if he had intercourse after maturity alone or through seclusion before witnesses, other betrothal is not required and we say a person does not act licentiously as explained siman 31 seif 9 — nevertheless when he comes to ask before intercourse, I have no doubt we instruct him to betroth with money kiddushin not "you are betrothed," as Rav Menagid, etc. See there and Noda B'Yehuda second edition siman 52 brought briefly above siman 43 s.k. 1.
