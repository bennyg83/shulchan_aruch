A woman who vowed enjoyment from her husband and he did not annul it for her, and she came before a sage to permit it and he forbade her because he did not find an opening to permit it — he may not marry her, lest they say he forbade her in order to marry her. And if he married her he need not divorce. And this is only regarding a single expert; but in court they are not suspected. And for this reason, if she refused or performed chalitzah before a court of three, one of them may marry her.
