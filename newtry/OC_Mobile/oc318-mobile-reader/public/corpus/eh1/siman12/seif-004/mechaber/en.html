And all of them who had wives at that time and afterward died or were divorced — and the women were those who accustomed their husbands to divorce them — behold these are permitted to marry them lekhatchilah. (If their wives were sick and died, they may not marry them) (Nimukei Yosef chapter 2 of Yevamot). And each one of them is permitted to marry the son of the witness who testified for her, or the son of the sage who forbade her upon her husband, or other relatives, for there is no concern that they will divorce them because of their relatives.
