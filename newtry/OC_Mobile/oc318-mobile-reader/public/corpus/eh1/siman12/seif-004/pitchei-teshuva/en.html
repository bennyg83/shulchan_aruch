That even with two. See in teshuvah Noda BiYehudah responsum 30 who wrote: apparently there is difficulty how Rama infers this from the words of Yerushalmi and Tosafot in Yevamot — they discuss refusal, witnesses to purchase, chalitzah, and witnesses to a get — in all these two are required and one does not help; if so even though the second will not testify falsely for him, nevertheless the second alone is not effective and his own testimony is somewhat ugly since she was permitted on his word; but two witnesses to a woman that her husband died — even one suffices and neither needs the other's testimony — why should she not marry on one witness's word to the second? And this is also difficult on the Mechaber who wrote the reason that two are not commonly found to sin for one — let it suffice that each is permitted to marry her since she is not married on his word; therefore it seems the intention of Shulchan Aruch is even where she is not married on one witness alone but on two — as in war or with a minor, or such as one witness initially that he is still alive and then two testified he already died; and on this Rama properly wrote a scrupulous person should distance; but where one suffices and two testified it is plain there is no concern at all that one of them marry her since his testimony is not needed at all — there is no ugliness at all; and also a scrupulous person need not be concerned at all — see there. And in my view although his words are correct from reasoning — there is difficulty on Taz and Beit Shmuel (Ba'er Hetev s.k. 3) who wrote likewise with three, etc. — for with three she is never permitted on his word since there are two besides him; and it requires examination.
