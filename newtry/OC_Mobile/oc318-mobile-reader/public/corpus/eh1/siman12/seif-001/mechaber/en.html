Witnesses to a get and likewise witnesses who testified to a woman that her husband died — whether they may marry that woman. It contains 4 seifim. One who brings a get that requires saying "before it was written and before it was signed," and likewise the single witness who testified to a woman that her husband died — he may not marry her because of suspicion. And if he married her he need not divorce. But one who brings a get that does not require "before it was written and before it was signed" is permitted to marry her, since she is not permitted on his account. And two witnesses who testified to a woman that her husband died — one of them is permitted to marry her, since two are not commonly found to sin for one. And see in the Tur itself siman 141. {Rama: In the teshuvah of the Rosh that the Tur brought in Even HaEzer — and some say that even with two witnesses, although permitted, nevertheless a scrupulous person should distance himself from this (Hagahot Maymoniyot chapter 10 of Gittin and Levush and Tosafot Yevamot).}
