See in teshuvah of Radbaz part 2 siman 389: they asked about one who brings a get from outside Eretz Yisrael and said "before it was written and before it was signed," and there are witnesses to validate the get — if she is permitted to the agent. And he answered: if they validated the get before giving it to her again he need not say "before it was written and before it was signed" and she would be permitted to the agent; but since the get was not validated initially and the woman was permitted on the agent's word even though there are witnesses to validate — she is forbidden to the agent, for she was permitted on his word; and even if he returns and takes the get from her hand and validates it and gives it to her — it does not help at all, for she was permitted on his word initially; and a divorced woman he divorces now, etc. — see there. And so explained in teshuvah of Rosh that the Tur brought later siman 141 — see there; and it was also mentioned here s.v. here.
