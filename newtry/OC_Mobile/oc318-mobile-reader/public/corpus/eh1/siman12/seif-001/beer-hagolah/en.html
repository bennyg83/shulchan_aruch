Mishnah Yevamot 25a.
Rambam chapter 10 of Laws of Divorce and likewise Tur; and as the Beit Yosef wrote there that he holds that what is said there in Yevamot 26a "if he married he need not divorce" applies to all of them; and likewise Rashi there 25b and Rav Acha of Shabcha Gaon; and also the Ramban ultimately agreed to this; and the Rashba according to Yerushalmi wrote that the precision is that if he had children from her he need not divorce.
There in the Gemara from the precision of the mishnah.
Yerushalmi — the Rif brought there, and likewise Rambam there, and likewise Tosafot and Ran there.
