(ג) And some say that the sons are forbidden to sell. So wrote Tosafot and Mordechai. This is according to the poskim who hold that if they sold small estates first, even after they stood in judgment, their sale stands. They ask: if so, what difference is there between large estates and small estates? Certainly, even with large estates they may not sell in order to remove sustenance. They answered that the practical difference is that with large estates they may sell for a great need, such as redeeming captives, but with small estates they may not sell ab initio. We learn that with large estates they may sell for redeeming captives even though this removes sustenance. However, one may say that only according to those poskim must one say so. But according to the poskim who hold that the sale is void if they sold after standing in judgment, there is another distinction between large and small estates, and one need not say this.
