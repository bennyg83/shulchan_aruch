Among the [automatic] conditions of the ketubah is that daughters shall be fed from the estate of the father after his death, until they are engaged (with kiddushin) or until they reach the age of majority. Note: [This rule holds] even if it was not explicitly written in the ketubah, or even if there is no ketubah, as in a place where ketubot are not written (Tur). Some say that even in a place where ketubot are written and they do not have the ketubah document, and even if the mother explicitly relinquished her financial rights in the ketubah, the daughters still receive food (Ra"avad to Hilchot Ishut Chapter 19). Similarly, if the mother was divorced, even so the father is required to feed the daughters.
