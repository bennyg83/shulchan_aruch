From which the sons and daughters are sustained. See Beit Meir, who was uncertain, for according to his view this measure applies only where the sons too are minors and need to be sustained from their father's estate. But if they are already adults and support themselves, one should not calculate anything for the sons' needs. This is reasonable, for if they also need support there is a reason for this measure, etc.; he left it requiring study. What Baer Heitev wrote in the name of Rashdam, that this measure is assessed by the assets themselves as they lie in the box, etc., see what Beit Meir wrote about this. What he further wrote, that the son was in one place, etc., see Mishneh LaMelekh, Laws of Ishut 19:17, who wrote in the name of Rashach, part 1, siman 177, that even if the sons are elsewhere they are obligated to set aside the daughters' share; see there. Apparently the word "not" is missing, and it should read that they are not obligated.
