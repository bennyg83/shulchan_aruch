A daughter was married, and either refused, or was divorced, or was widowed, even if she is a yivama. Since she returns to her fathers house and has not yet grown up [past the age of 12 1/2], she feeds from her father's possessions until she grows up or until she is engaged. Rem"a: And some say specifically [if she refused/was divorced/was widowed] from the engagement [does she feed from her father's possessions], but if [she refused/was divorced/was widowed] from marriage, she no longer has food [from her father] (Tur). And some say further that specifically in her father's life, since when her father died she was in his authority, but after her father dies, she loses the food immediately since she is engaged (Tur in the name of the Rosh). And this is the main [law].
