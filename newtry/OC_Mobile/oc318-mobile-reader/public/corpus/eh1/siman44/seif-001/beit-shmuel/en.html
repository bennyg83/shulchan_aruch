And she is permitted to her husband. Although if the deaf-mute divorced her and she married a competent man, then even with respect to the deaf-mute she is forbidden to her husband, as written in siman 10, nevertheless here he did not divorce her, and we are not concerned that people may say he divorced her. Even if she had been divorced, there is no issue of a married woman, for the kiddushin of a deaf-mute is only rabbinic. If a competent man came and married her, she is forbidden even to the deaf-mute husband; just as she is forbidden to the adulterer, so she is forbidden to the husband. In Sefer Beit Hillel he wrote that since there is no issue of a married woman, we do not penalize him.
