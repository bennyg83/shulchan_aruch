Or the first divorces. In the Tur it says, one divorces, implying that even if the second divorces her, she is permitted to the first, and we are not concerned that people may say the first divorced her and now is remarrying his divorcee, because everyone knows that his kiddushin was not full kiddushin. What is written here in Shulchan Aruch, that the first divorces, was written according to what he wrote in Bedek HaBayit, that we are concerned lest people say so; see there. If two brothers betrothed her, one before emancipation and one after emancipation, and they died, she is permitted to the third brother. It is not relevant to say here that she is the wife of two dead men, because either way she was betrothed only to one. Gemara.
