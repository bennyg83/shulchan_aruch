A mentally incompetent man or woman, etc. See Chiddushei Rabbi Akiva Eiger below, siman 121, s.k. 5, where he mentions the words of Beis Meir in that siman, who copied the responsum of the Noda BiYehudah printed in Or HaYashar, folio 59. He cites the Yerushalmi at the beginning of Terumos concerning a deaf-mute, mentally incompetent person, or minor who effected kiddushin, which distinguishes between one who acts by himself, whose kiddushin is not valid, and one who acts together with others, whose kiddushin is valid. See there. Yeshuos Yaakov there, s.k. 4, likewise cites this in the name of a certain sage who expressed great astonishment that this Yerushalmi had escaped the attention of all the poskim. Yeshuos Yaakov discusses the matter at length and demonstrates several passages in the Bavli that do not accord with that Yerushalmi. Responsa Minchas Ani, siman 69, also discusses this at length. Their words will be cited briefly below, siman 121, se'if 6, s.k. 5. Minchas Ani wondered how it is possible to say that the Yerushalmi is speaking of kiddushin of a woman. If so, how could there be kiddushin performed by oneself, when kiddushin without witnesses is ineffective even for a mentally competent person? Rather, the Yerushalmi must be discussing the sanctification of the ashes of the red heifer in the water of purification, etc. The lengthy discussion was therefore unnecessary. See there, and see what I wrote in siman 121 there.
