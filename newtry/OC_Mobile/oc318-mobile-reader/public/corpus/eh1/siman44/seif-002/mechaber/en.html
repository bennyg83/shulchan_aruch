An idiot man and idiot woman have no kiddushin — neither from Torah nor from rabbinic enactment — whether with their like or with hearing people. {Rama: And specifically a complete idiot; but if his mind is clear even though it is weak and greatly diminished (R' Yehuda in name of Ramah). And likewise one who is sometimes an idiot and sometimes clear and we do not know the time he stands in his right mind — we are concerned for kiddushin (likewise there).}
