A shoteh. Although with a deaf-mute woman the sages instituted marriage because of a deaf-mute man, and a deaf-mute woman stands within the rabbinic enactment and there is peace between them, this is not so with a shoteh. In the Tur the text reads shoteh and shotah, and the Derishah wrote in the name of Maharshal that it depends on pronunciation: shoteh, masculine, with segol; shotah, feminine, with kamatz.
