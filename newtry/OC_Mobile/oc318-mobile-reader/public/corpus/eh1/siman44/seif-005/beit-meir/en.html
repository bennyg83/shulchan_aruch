Beit Shmuel wrote in the name of Darkei Moshe: and all the more so, etc. One should not ask: do we not hold like Rava, who says in Temurah that whenever the Torah says not to do something, if he did it, it is ineffective? For these are the words of Rashba, Kiddushin 66b: and if you ask, do we not hold like Rava, etc., Tosafot answered that since the verse revealed regarding a High Priest, he shall not take, one might have thought to derive from it to the prohibition he shall not take concerning a married woman, that if he did it, it is effective. If so, one can say that since we see that even with those liable to karet, the Gemara had to derive from another place that kiddushin does not take effect with forbidden relations, by implication with all other prohibitions we derive from a High Priest with a widow, and Rava agrees that it is effective. All the more so, since it is included in the Gemara’s derivation from when there shall be, etc., and is there a hated one before Heaven, etc., and the Merciful One said, when there shall be. Therefore the verse he shall not profane is needed in Temurah only for R. Akiva, and this is clear.
