And likewise an aylonit. See Beit Shmuel below, siman 155 s.k. 19, and Lechem Mishneh, chapter 4 of Hilkhot Ishut. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah Tinyana, siman 78, who wrote that with an adult woman the Rambam ruled that she is certainly betrothed, because since he did not investigate the matter he surely accepted the risk. But with a minor girl, where the matter cannot be determined, the Rambam agrees that if he betrothed her without specification and afterward she was found to be an aylonit, it is mistaken kiddushin. This resolves the Lechem Mishneh's difficulty with the Rambam from the first Mishnah in Yevamot, and also the questioner's difficulty there from the sugya in Sanhedrin 69; see there.
