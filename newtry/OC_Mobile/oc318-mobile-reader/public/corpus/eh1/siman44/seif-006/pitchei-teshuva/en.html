And likewise an aylonis. See Beis Shmuel below, siman 155, s.k. 19, and Lechem Mishneh, chapter 4 of Hilchos Ishus. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah Tinyana, siman 78, who wrote that regarding an adult woman, the Rambam ruled that she is certainly betrothed. Since he did not investigate the matter, he certainly accepted the possibility. Regarding a minor girl, however, where the matter cannot be determined, the Rambam agrees that if he betrothed her without specification and she was afterward found to be an aylonis, the kiddushin was based on error. This resolves the Lechem Mishneh's difficulty with the Rambam from the first Mishnah of Yevamos, and also the questioner's difficulty there from the passage in Sanhedrin, folio 69. See there.
