Who betrothed. The same applies to a convert who returned to his waywardness and betrothed: the kiddushin is complete, as written in Yoreh De'ah 268, Beit Hillel. Likewise, if one betrothed an apostate woman, kiddushin takes effect; R. Meir Isserlan, siman 62. One who betrothed, and they thought he was Jewish, and afterward it was revealed that he had apostatized and then repented, but not with complete repentance: see the writings of Maharar Yisrael, siman 138. Regarding the coerced converts in France, we are not concerned that perhaps his mother was from gentiles, for all the coerced converts are careful not to intermarry with gentiles, and we do not take account of a minority, since anyone who separates is presumed to separate from the majority. Re'em, part 2, siman 31; R. Yaakov Beirav, siman 39.
