And even his seed. It appears that this refers to what was written, that his kiddushin is full kiddushin. This is also reasonable, since an apostate woman has the status of a Jewish woman; although she sinned, she remains Jewish. If so, the child from her is Jewish and has the status of an apostate. Even if she is extremely licentious, nevertheless this is like a gentile who has intercourse with a Jewish woman, where the child follows her. So it is written in Chelkat Mechokek. Likewise, a convert who returned to his former ways and betrothed a woman, the kiddushin is full kiddushin, as written in Yoreh De’ah siman 268.
