If a man died and left a widow and a daughter, the daughter does not take one tenth of the property (for her dowry) because of the sustenance support of the widow. Even if the daughter died after her marriage, her husband does not inherit the [dowry] income that would have been appropriate to give her, for the property is legally in the possession of the widow for purposes of her sustenance. Rama: In the same manner, dowry income of the daughters takes precedence over "male sons" ketubah inheritance (Ran chapter Metziat haIsha).
