(א) The husband died. This does not mean specifically that he died; even in his lifetime we collect from him where he made a kinyan, as Maggid Mishneh wrote in chapter 21. Likewise, if there is a creditor here, they collect from the heirs even without a kinyan, for an oral loan within its time is collected from heirs. Here too it is within its time, for he died within the five years; so it is there in Maggid Mishneh.
(ב) Specifically where they acquired from him by kinyan. See siman 112 according to the view of Ramah brought there in seif 8: here an unspecified kinyan helps, because they acquired from him regarding all the conditions; regarding his daughters it does not help because of the concern for deposited funds. The reasons stated in chapter HaNizakin, that one does not collect from encumbered property, were said only regarding his daughters; but for his wife's daughter an unspecified kinyan helps. And what we rule, that these matters were not given to be written, so that even if he wrote them one does not collect from encumbered property, refers to where he wrote the matters as they occurred, "so-and-so obligated himself for such and such," as written in siman 51. But a full document, even if they wrote a document about all the conditions, is effective. According to Tosafot there, a kinyan is stronger than a document; if so, even for his wife's daughter a document does not help, only a kinyan, and for his daughters even a kinyan does not help. However, one can say that according to Tosafot, although a document does not help, an unspecified kinyan on all the conditions does help for his wife's daughter. All this follows Rif, Rosh, Rashba, and many poskim who rule like the view that the reason is because they are not written. Rambam, however, rules like the view that the reason is because they are not fixed, as written in Maggid Mishneh chapter 11. He also holds that one who obligates himself to his fellow to feed someone for five years is obligating himself in something without a fixed amount; even if they acquired from him, he is not obligated to pay, even from free property. However, when he obligates himself to his wife at the time of kiddushin, it is effective regarding free property even without a kinyan. But at the time of marriage it is ineffective unless they acquired from him or he wrote a document; then they collect from purchasers, as written at the end of chapter 23 of Hilchot Ishut. The Talmudic sugya in chapter HaNizakin appears to hold that Rav Huna bar Manoach's question applies both according to the view that the reason is that they are not written and according to the view that they are not fixed.
