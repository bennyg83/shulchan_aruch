From here one may learn that if a father arranged a match for his daughter and afterward the father and the groom agreed to cancel the match, the father has no authority to cancel the match without his daughter's consent. If his daughter is under his authority, such as if she is a minor or a na'arah, he may cancel the match without her consent. Taz; Beit Shmuel.
