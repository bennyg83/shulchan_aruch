(א) And if he wrote to one, etc. In Responsa Rashba it is explained that certainly if he obligated himself to sustain her for a certain time and died within that time, he need not pay future sustenance. But in that case he obligated himself to give him a certain sum, and did not set a time for the sustenance; rather, he set a time for payment. They could have collected that sum even on the first day, and therefore he must pay even though the payment date had not yet arrived. As for what he wrote, "if he wanted to take the sum from him all at once," etc., Chelkat Mechokek already wondered about this. Perhaps his intent is that had he not fixed times for payment, the law would be that he must give him that sum immediately; therefore, even if he fixed times, nevertheless the obligation had already taken effect.
