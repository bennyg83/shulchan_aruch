(א) In the place where her mother will be. Why do we not say there is an assessment that he did not intend to give her daughter sustenance except as long as her mother is with him, just as we say an assessment regarding dowries of sons-in-law, as written in siman 53, that he did not intend to give the dowry unless his daughter would benefit? There it is different: the assessment is that his obligation was specifically to give to his daughter, and his daughter died, so he is exempt. But where a person obligates himself generally, we do not use an assessment to exempt him; what he obligated himself to do, he must fulfill. We do not say such an assessment, as written in siman 90 in the name of Mahariv. Taz, citing Terumat HaDeshen, distinguishes differently. There he ruled that one who obligates himself to provide sustenance for his son and daughter-in-law until a certain time, and his son dies within the time, must provide sustenance for his daughter-in-law until the time ends, just as here he must provide sustenance for his wife's daughter. If her mother must move from her place for some reason, Responsa Rashach, part 1, siman 177, is uncertain whether he gives her daughter sustenance in the place of her mother.
