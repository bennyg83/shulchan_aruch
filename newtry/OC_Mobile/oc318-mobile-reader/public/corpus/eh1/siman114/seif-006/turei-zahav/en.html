(ב) And some disagree, etc. In the Tur, Ramah wrote that these sustenances that he gives her are not given to her except like one who sustains his wife through a third party, etc. Even for a wealthy man he gives the lesser level of sustenance, for they said that he gives according to her honor only regarding his wife, who eats by condition of beit din; but regarding his wife's daughter, where he obligated himself, the holder of the document is at the lower hand. My father and master wrote: this does not appear correct, for one who gives money to the head of the household to support him presumably intends that he be supported like one of the members of his household, etc. Beit Yosef wrote: I do not know why the Rosh wrote "this does not appear correct," for Ramah does not speak of feeding her at his table but of supplying her sustenance, and his words are correct. It was already explained in siman 112 regarding his daughter's sustenance that they allot to her only the lesser level. Nevertheless, the Rosh's words can be understood where the undertaking was to support her in his house or in a manner of household support.
