(א) Movable property, etc. Only regarding leaving over is movable property considered like land after the enactments of the Geonim. But if he wrote movable property to her, even after the enactments of the Geonim it is not like land. Darkei Moshe; Bach; Rashdam, Even HaEzer siman 136. But Beit Yosef, Shiltei Gibborim, and R. Yehudah Adrabi, siman 132, hold that after the enactments of the Geonim, movable property is like land for all matters, and if he gave her movable property she lost. Keneset HaGedolah wrote that since there is a dispute of great authorities, if the woman is in possession she can say, "I hold like them"; see there. If he said to her, "Take this for your ketubah," then according to everyone it helps even with movable property, since she was silent. Likewise if she accepted explicitly. See Chelkat Mechokek; Beit Shmuel.
