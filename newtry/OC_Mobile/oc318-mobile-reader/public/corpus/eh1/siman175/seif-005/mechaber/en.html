If one of the [sisters] was prohibited to this one because of erva, and the second [sister] was prohibited to the other on because of erva, the one that was prohibited for this one is permitted to that one, for each [brother] is only attached to do yibbum with one. But if the prohibition on them is just a negative or positive prohibition, the attachment is not pushed off, so they must do chalitzah and not yibbum.
