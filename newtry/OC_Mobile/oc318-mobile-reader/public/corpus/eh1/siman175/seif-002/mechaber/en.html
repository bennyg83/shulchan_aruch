Two brothers of [many brothers] who marry two sisters, and both [brothers] die, and it is not known who died first, since one cannot perform yibbum on for both together, and the levirate association falls on both, they are performed upon chalitzah and not yibbum. Even if one of them is prohibited to her yibbum because of the second, or he is guilty of violating an positive-action sin or guilty of violating a negative-action sin, they do chalitzah and not yibbum. But if one of them [is prohibited] because of erva [prohibited sexual impropriety], her sister is indeed permitted to him, and so if he wants to do chalitzah [he can], if he wants to do yibbum [he can] for the levirate association did not fall on both together, for there is no levirate association in the case of erva.
