One who designated land for his wife at death that she be sustained from it — if he said "such-and-such land shall be for your sustenance" — he increased sustenance for her; and if its rent was less than sustenance fitting for her — she takes the rest from other property. And if its rent was more than fitting for her — she takes all. But if he said to her "such-and-such land is in sustenance" and she was silent — she has only the produce of that land alone.
