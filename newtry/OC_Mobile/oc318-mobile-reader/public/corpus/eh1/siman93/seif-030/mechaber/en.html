How much do they sell for sustenance? Enough to sustain from them six months and no more; and the money remains in the buyer's hand so they do not give her everything at once but enough sustenance for thirty days; and likewise from thirty days to thirty days; and she returns and sells again for six months; and she sells and continues always until nothing remains in the property except ketubah — she collects ketubah from the remainder and goes her way. {Rama: Some say that selling six months is specifically land; but nowadays that she is sustained from movables — one sells only one object (Tur in name of Baal HaIttur).}
