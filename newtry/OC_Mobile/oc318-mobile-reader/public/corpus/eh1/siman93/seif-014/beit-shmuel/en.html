That she delayed two years. This is the view of the Rambam and the Ramah. But the Rosh holds that she forfeits after two years only when she is licentious, because in the Gemara there are two answers: according to the first answer, a poor woman forfeits when she delayed two years; according to the second answer, she forfeits when she is licentious and delayed two years. The Rosh holds that she is the one in possession, and therefore she forfeits only with both disadvantages: poor and licentious. I have already written that in every dispute among the poskim she is considered the one in possession. The Rambam also holds this, but here he holds that the first answer is primary. Regarding these two or three years, the Taz wrote that if it is a leap year, the intercalated month must be included.
