A widow is sustained from the heirs' property all her widowhood — even if it was not written in the ketubah, and even if he commanded at death "do not sustain my widow from my property" — they do not listen to him. And the heirs cannot pay her ketubah and dismiss her from sustenance; rather she is sustained against their will as long as she does not claim her ketubah — unless they stipulated explicitly that his widow not be sustained from his property, or the custom of the place was so (and beit din can enact in their place that orphans may dismiss her when they wish (Beit Yosef and Rivash siman 107)).
