(א) A widow, etc. The Mishnah, 52b.
(ב) And even, etc. As they said there, 68b: one who says, "Do not," etc., that maintenance, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. "this is the version," etc.
(ג) And the heirs do not, etc. Or if it was, etc. See Tosafot 54a, s.v. "and Shmuel," etc. So ruled the Rif, the Rambam, and other decisors. So too in the Tosefta: all other lands write like the people of Jerusalem. In the Yerushalmi: Rabbi Avahu says, Caesarea is like Judea, and other lands are like Jerusalem.
(ד) Unless, etc. For every monetary condition is valid.
(ה) And they can, etc. For it depends on custom, as they said there in the Gemara, and as above.
