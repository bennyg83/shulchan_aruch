See Baer Heitev, who wrote: except for the first three months, etc. See Responsa of my grandfather Panim Me'irot II, siman 100, who wrote that the matter is clear: this applies specifically if the widow did not claim her ketubah in beit din. But if she claimed her ketubah in beit din, Rivash did not speak of that case, and everyone agrees that she has no sustenance. Specifically where the beit din wished to enact, etc.; but where she claimed her ketubah, the Sages initially did not enact sustenance for her, and certainly she has no sustenance even during the first three months. He concluded that this matter is simple, and he wrote it only to exclude the view of one judge who wished to rule that even where she claimed her ketubah one should give her sustenance during the first three months. It is clear that the law is not so. These are his words; see there. So too is written in the margin of the Shulchan Aruch of Rabbi Akiva Eiger zt"l. (ו) At the doors.
