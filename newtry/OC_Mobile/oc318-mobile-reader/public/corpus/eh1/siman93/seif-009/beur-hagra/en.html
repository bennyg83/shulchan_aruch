(א) But during his lifetime, etc. For this is not one of the conditions of the ketubah, but is in exchange for her labor. The Rosh and other decisors.
(ב) Although, etc. For in the Yerushalmi they say in the name of Rabbi Yochanan in the name of Shmuel: say that one who waives the ketubah to the orphans is maintained from their property. Our Gemara disagrees, for it is all the more so from one who waives to her husband. See Rashi there, 104b, s.v. "not maintenance," etc., and in the Mishnah there, 104a, s.v. "and the Sages say," etc.
