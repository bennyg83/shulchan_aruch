One who left a widow and a daughter from her or from another woman, and there is not enough in the property for both to be sustained — the widow is sustained and the daughter asks at doorways. And some say whether the property is abundant or scant — if there is a widow and son or widow and daughter, she is sustained with the son or with the daughter until the property is consumed; and even if the daughter marries and brings the property to her husband — she is sustained from them even after the daughter's death. But if there is a widow and son and daughter and the property is scant so there is not enough for daughter and son until the daughter matures — then son and daughter are pushed aside and the widow is sustained alone until she collects her ketubah. {Rama: And in a place where the widow pushes aside daughters — they give the widow sustenance until the time beit din estimates she can live, whether old or young, and give in the hand of a third party who sustains her one day in thirty (Beit Yosef in name of Maggid Mishneh who wrote so in name of Ramban).}
