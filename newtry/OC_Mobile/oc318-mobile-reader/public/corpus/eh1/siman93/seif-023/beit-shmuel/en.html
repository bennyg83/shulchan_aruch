(ב) Some say that it is not considered seizure. So wrote the Ran in the name of Rashi, and the Maggid Mishneh, Hagahot Ashri, and Agudah. A practical difference follows nowadays if she seized more than the proper amount. What the Ran wrote there, that nowadays there is no practical difference, refers to where she did not seize more than the proper amount. The proof brought by Chelkat Mechokek from Choshen Mishpat siman 107, that seizure helps even from the heirs' domain, is no proof. There he seizes what is fitting for him; although he has no witnesses to the debt, nevertheless he is believed because of a miggo. But seizure of more than the amount fitting for him may not help, in accordance with Rabbi Tarfon. In the Mesharim he wrote that we remove it from her hand and give it to a third party. Beit Yosef explained that he holds seizure from the heirs' domain does not help. This does not appear correct, for that reasoning is not mentioned there; also, if so, why not return it to the heirs? Perhaps he holds that although seizure helps, nevertheless we give it to a third party lest she die. It appears that even according to the poskim who hold that seizure does not help from the heirs' domain, nevertheless if she seized money it helps, as the Ran wrote regarding the Talmudic law according to those poskim who hold that seizure of movable property does not help for a ketubah, even during his lifetime; nevertheless, if she seized money we do not remove it from her hand.
