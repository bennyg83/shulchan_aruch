A widow who seized movables in order to be sustained from them — whether she seized during lifetime or after death, even if she seized a gold bar — they do not take from her hand; rather beit din writes what she seized and allots her sustenance and accounts against her, and she is sustained from what is in her hand until she dies or until she has no sustenance and the heirs take the remainder. {Rama: Some say it is not seizure unless seizure from public domain or from an alley; but not from heirs' domain (Ran chapter Almanah Nizonit in name of Rashi). And some dispute (so it appears from Rivash siman 364). And all agree we require complete seizure — meaning she seized through one of the modes of acquisition; but if she only seized the keys of the rooms — it is not called seizure and they give everything to heirs. And specifically when she seized herself; but through an agent her seizure is not effective (in Rivash siman 364 and siman 107).}
