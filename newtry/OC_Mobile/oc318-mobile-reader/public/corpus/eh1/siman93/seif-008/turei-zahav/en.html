(א) Whether before three trustworthy people. These are Rambam's words, which imply that in any event three are required. This is surprising, for at the end of chapter Na'arah the Gemara says that whether before beit din or not before beit din, it helps when she sold or mortgaged her ketubah; but when she claims her ketubah, before beit din yes, not before beit din no. If you explain "not before beit din" to mean not before experts, then regarding claiming, where beit din is required, it means experts; if so this law would not apply among us, for we have no experts, since every expert beit din derives from the verse "elohim," as stated at the beginning of Choshen Mishpat, meaning ordained judges, and this is not among us. Further, in the Rambam text in our hands this phrase "before three trustworthy people" is not written. Rather, this is his language: "whether before expert beit din or not before beit din," etc.; he did not write "before non-experts." This implies that not before beit din means before witnesses.
