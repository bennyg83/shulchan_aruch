(ב) In the dowry. Nevertheless, if the dowry is still in her hand or in her father's hand, she may hold it for sustenance; see Chelkat Mechokek siman 97 seif 26. Mahari HaLevi, siman 17, wrote: if we see that the husband's intent is to consume all her money and afterward get up and leave the city so that she will be forced to accept only a get from him, the ketubah was given to be collected during his lifetime; see there. If the husband stipulated that she should collect her ketubah during his lifetime, see Keneset HaGedolah, folio 112a, seif 4. Regarding the wife of an apostate, the custom is to collect her ketubah immediately, even without an oath. Iggeret HaRambam, siman 20, and Shiltei Gibborim, chapter HaMekabel. A woman who wants to give her daughter a certain sum and the husband does not want, and she claims that he should give her the sum and she will write in her ketubah document that she received that sum, the husband cannot prevent this.
