The law of a widow's sustenance. It contains 32 seifim. The ketubah — behold it is like a debt that has a time and is not collected except after the husband's death or if he divorced her. {Rama: And the same applies to what she received in dowry; and see above siman 66 seif 11. Gifts written for her are collected during lifetime; however if the custom of the place is to write thus for honor alone and not to collect during lifetime — they follow the custom (in Meisharim in name of Gaon, and not like Rashba). And the father cannot stipulate on his daughter in any matter after she married; but if he marries her off he can stipulate on her gifts to change from custom before she married as he wishes (and see Choshen Mishpat siman 97 seif 24).}
