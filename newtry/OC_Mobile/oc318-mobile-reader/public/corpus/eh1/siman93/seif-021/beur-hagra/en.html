(א) And if they sold, etc. Ketubot 69a, and as above, seif 20.
(ב) And she, etc. And there are, etc. See the Rosh at the beginning of chapter 9 of Bava Batra, that according to Talmudic law she certainly is not maintained from the money, because it is movable property. A proof is from what they said there: come and hear, for Rabbi Elazar said in the name of Rabbi Yochanan, etc. If it were so, do they still not need to be supported from the money? So too from what is written in Sotah 21b: who is a wicked person, etc. However, Rav Hai wrote that after the enactment of the Geonim, whereby she is maintained from movable property, she is likewise maintained from the money.
(ג) And there are, etc. For these coins were not liened, etc. To what is this comparable, etc. See there. So it is ruled in siman 112, seif 14, in the gloss.
(ד) He transferred ownership, etc. As written in Ketubot 102b: with what are we dealing? Where they made a kinyan, etc., and in Gittin 51a. Rashba. Although we say there and there: rather, daughters, etc.; say that there may be bundles of money set aside, etc. According to the Rashba one must say that this applies specifically to his daughter, but not to his wife, for we hold like Rav there, 107b, that one rules maintenance for a married woman. See Tosafot, s.v. "say there may be bundles of money," etc.
(ה) And specifically, etc. For the holder of the shtar has the lower hand.
