He left movables and she did not seize them — the heirs take them and increase sustenance for her, and she cannot delay them and say let the movables remain in beit din that I be sustained from them lest they be lost and I have no sustenance; and even if he stipulated explicitly that she be sustained from movables — she does not delay. But if he left land — she can delay them from selling; and if they sold — she does not extract from buyers and she is sustained from the land money in their hands. And some dispute and say she is not sustained from that money. {Rama: One who transferred to her with kinyan regarding sustenance — she extracts for sustenance from encumbered property; and specifically when he specified explicitly sustenance after death; but otherwise even if he wrote for her sustenance in her ketubah and they acquired from his hand — he did not intend sustenance only during lifetime (so it appears from Maggid Mishneh ch. 18 Ishut and so Beit Yosef in name of responsum of Rashba).}
