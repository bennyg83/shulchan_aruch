A widow who claimed her ketubah in beit din — she no longer has sustenance, even if they did not pay her (but if she claimed not in beit din she did not lose) (Tur). And some say even if she claimed in beit din she did not lose unless she claimed on her own; but if she claimed from duress because they did not give her sustenance, or they deceived her and said to her so-and-so wishes to marry you and on account of this she claimed her ketubah, or the like — she did not lose her sustenance. {Rama: One who swore on her ketubah before beit din did not lose sustenance, for her oath is not like a claim (Hagahot Alfasi end of chapter Na'arah).}
