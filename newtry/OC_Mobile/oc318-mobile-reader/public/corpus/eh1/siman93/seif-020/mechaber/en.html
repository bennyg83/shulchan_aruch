A widow is sustained only from free land and not from encumbered — not only if the husband sold or gave during lifetime, but even if the heirs sold or pledged or gave after their father's death — they do not extract for sustenance of the woman and daughters. And specifically when the father gave a healthy-person gift; but if he gave a deathbed gift — she is sustained from it (if there is no free land) (Rivash siman 107). If he consecrated his property — it is like a healthy-person gift (Ran beginning HaMedir) and she is not sustained from movables even if free; however if she seized movables even after death — we do not take from her. And now that the Geonim enacted she collects ketubah and ketubah conditions from movables — behold she is sustained from movables even if she did not seize (however if he gave movables to others even in a deathbed gift — some say she is not sustained from them) (Mordechai chapter Na'arah in name of R' Yitzchak).
