(ב) But some say it is proper to be stringent, etc. Taz wrote that if it is impossible without a receiving agent, she may appoint a receiving agent where there is clear testimony that they recognize the signatures of the three judges. Bach also permitted appointing a receiving agent. There was an actual case of one who was at times a shoteh and at times lucid, but his lucid periods were brief; he permitted the woman to appoint a receiving agent in the place where he would be during his lucid period, and she would return to her place, and during his lucid period he would give the get into the hand of her receiving agent.
