The entire agency is void. Beit Shmuel s.k. 35; and see Torat Gittin, what he wrote regarding its meaning. At the end he writes: what Sha'ar HaMelekh said — that it is effective when he brings another authorization — means that the husband reappoints him as agent before valid witnesses (see the following s.k.); alternatively, that he brings a court document (ma'aseh beit din) showing he was appointed agent before a court, which is also effective, since the court is not disqualified by the combination of a valid and invalid witness (kisher u'fasul), as I elaborated elsewhere — end quote; and this is found in his work Netivot HaMishpat, siman 36.
