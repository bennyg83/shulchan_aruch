And killed himself immediately. Torat Gittin wrote: it seems that the measure of "immediately" mentioned in the Yerushalmi means the time needed to write and give the get — for since his intention was that he would die instantly from the fall, and he wished to divorce her, his intention was therefore that they write and give [the get] before the fall, and he intended to delay the fall for the duration of writing and delivery. And even if he came down from the roof after saying "write" and then went back up to throw himself off, or killed himself by another means — the law is the same — end quote.
