(ב) I will uphold her. Taz wrote that this speaks where he sent an agent plainly to bring a get to his wife, and after making him an agent said to him, "if you wish," etc.; then it is a mere disclosure of intent. But if at the time he made him an agent he said to him, "when you wish," etc., or within the time of an utterance, then the agency was made on that condition. Then, if Reuven is in the city and cannot appease her, he may not give her the get. Beit Shmuel.
