If the divorce document came from the woman's hand and she says, "This agent gave it to me for the divorce process", and the agent says, "Yes, I have given it to her for the divorce process," and the husband says, "It was only given to her as a deposit," the agent is trustworthy and she is divorced. And if the divorce document was lost and there are no witnesses who saw the document in her hand, even though the husband says, "The agent gave it for the divorce process," and the agent says, "it was given to her," this is a divorce in doubt, since she held up as a married woman, and here there is only one witness and a husband. And even if the woman says, "In my midst it was given to him for the divorce process and the agent gave it to me," since the husband and the agent are assisting her it is possible that they will scorn her and perhaps she will not be divorced.
