So too it appears in the Yerushalmi there, that an agent for bringing kiddushin does not need witnesses, as stated there at the beginning of chapter 3, brought by the Ran there: the agent was not established through witnesses, etc. The same applies to divorce, as written on 40b, that even in Eretz Yisrael, "before me it was written" is effective, for if the husband comes, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. "if," etc., and Tosafot 2b, s.v. "who brought," etc., and the Rosh there, who explains in the name of Riba, etc. But when he says, etc., and so it appears to me, etc. See Tosafot 64a, s.v. "husband," etc. So too the Ran wrote as one answer, and he wrote that this is the view of Rashi there, s.v. "husband," etc. But in his second answer he wrote like the Rambam, distinguishing between witnesses of delivery-agency and witnesses of receiving-agency.
