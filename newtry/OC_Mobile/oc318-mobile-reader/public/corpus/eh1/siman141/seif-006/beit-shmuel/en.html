(ב) She is divorced through her father. The Talmud gives two reasons why a shoteh is not divorced. The first reason is that Scripture says, "and he sends her from his house," and a shoteh returns to his house. The second reason is concern for zenut. With a minor there is no concern for zenut, and there is also no concern that she will return to his house, since her father is alive.
