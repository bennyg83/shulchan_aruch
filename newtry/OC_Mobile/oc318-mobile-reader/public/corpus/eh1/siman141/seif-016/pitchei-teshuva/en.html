One signature suffices. Ba'er Hetev; Beit Shmuel wrote the reason: we rely on the principle that witnesses signed on a document are as if their testimony has been examined, etc. See Torat Gittin, who wrote: this is not the principal reason, for we do not rely on this regarding an agent who did not state "in my presence it was written and signed," and we disqualify him. Rather, the main reason is explained in the responsum of Rabbi Shlomo son of Rashbatz, cited by Beit Yosef in siman 35, that we rely on the authorities who hold that a court validation does not require [further] validation — end quote. Accordingly, if [the document] is not validated at all, it is possible that one should not be lenient if it is not a pressing situation.
