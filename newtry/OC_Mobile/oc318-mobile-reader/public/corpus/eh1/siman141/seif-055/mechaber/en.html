[If] she appointed a agent to accept [the Get ], and the Get is sent through him and the husband [later] says: It is forged! If the signatures or the sending agent are confirmed, she is divorced. And if he admits to writing it, but he says that he did not give it to her under the law of divorce but rather for safekeeping, and [her] agent says he received it for her to be divorced through it, the agent is believed, even if all three were in the same city. And there are those that say, if the three are in the same city, the husband is believed, because if he wanted it to be under the law of divorce, he would have given it to her. And this [last law] is only when he claims it given for safekeeping, but if he claims it was that he used a agent to bring it, but wanted to take it back before it reached her, he is not believed. And if they're not in the same city, the agent is believed and this is when witnesses who were appointed by the receiving agent come to us [ Beis Din ] and the agent said that he sent it in front of witnesses. Rem"a: There are those that say this is only when the Get is sent through him [the wife's agent], but if the Get is not in his hand, he is not believed, even if he has witnesses that saw it in his hand (Tur accord. to Rosh). Mechaber: And according to this logic, it is good to beware divorcing through a agent when the husband and the wife are in the same city because there is a possibility of destruction (translation is unclear...) because if he claims that he did not give it for divorce, he is believed. Rem"a: And there is no distinction between a agent for bringing and a agent for receiving (Rif). However, once there is [written] permission in his hand to be a agent of divorce, there is no distinction whether the husband is in the city or not (Maharam Padva Siman 3). And if the wife says: In front of me the agent accepted it for divorce, she is believed. And there are those that say this is only when the Get is [now] in the hand of the wife.
