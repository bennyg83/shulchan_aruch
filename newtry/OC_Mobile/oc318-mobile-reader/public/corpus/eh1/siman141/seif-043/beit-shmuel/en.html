(א) Because it is mere words. Since he does not hand him anything, it is considered mere words. But there, where he hands him the get, and likewise her delivery agent, it is not considered mere words, since the essential agency is completed when he gives the get into the agent's hand. In Bedek HaBayit he asked against the Tur: in this siman it implies that "tell others to write" is not mere words and can be done, while in siman 120 he invalidates "tell others to write." Darkei Moshe answered that when the Tur wrote that it is not mere words, he did not mean to validate "tell others to write"; rather, his intent was that there is no disqualification because of mere words, but "tell others to write" is forbidden for other reasons, as written in siman 100.
