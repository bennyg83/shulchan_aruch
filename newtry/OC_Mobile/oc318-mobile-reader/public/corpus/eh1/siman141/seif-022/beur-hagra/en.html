What, etc. And there are, etc. See the Rosh, chapter 4, seif 2, and the Yerushalmi, etc.: he made, etc., and one does not, etc. The Rosh explains there that the same applies where he is standing, etc. But Ba'al HaItur and the Tur disagree with him and write that since it says "in place of two," it does not count, but it does count for one. All of this applies equally to shlichut for receiving.
