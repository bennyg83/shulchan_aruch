If, etc. Rashi in Kiddushin 43b, s.v. "she," etc., and specifically from, etc., for the verse says "the betrothed na'arah," as written in Ketubot 46b and Kiddushin 18b. So too Tosafot in Kiddushin 10a, s.v. "and receives," etc. See there. So it is in the Yerushalmi in Perek HaTekabel regarding the above Mishnah: a na'arah, etc.; if she married, she and not her father; a minor, her father and not she; if she married, she and her father. One must say: not she and not her father. The Ran explains that it means they are equal: if she has understanding, she and not her father. The Ran brings this there.
