But a gravely ill person, etc. See Yad HaMelekh, ch. 2 of the Laws of Divorce, law 12, who wrote: from the implied language of Rambam and all the authorities it follows that they need not give her the get specifically while he is still ill. Rather, even if before it was given to her he recovered — provided he remained silent and did not void the agency — they may give her the get; for the decisive moment is the time of his statement, and if at that time he was gravely ill, we say that his intention was to have it written and given, and this is as if he explicitly appointed them as agents to write and give; so as long as he has not voided their agency, they write and give it to her — see there.
