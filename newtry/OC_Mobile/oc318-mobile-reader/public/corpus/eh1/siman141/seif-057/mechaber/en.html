A agent of receiving that receives a Get for a woman, and sends it to her in front of two witnesses, and it reaches her and she takes it, and therefore the Get has passed into her hand, but she doesn't know if her husband sent it to her or her receiving agent received it, or [if it was from] the agent of the husband, she is [nevertheless] divorced. And if the husband comes a protests that he didn't write it, or that it was an annulled Get , we confirm it through its signatures, because there are witnesses that the Get that was given to her came from her agent because his hand is like her hand (i.e. he can accept it for her), and even though she didn't know, the witnesses knew. And if it is not confirmed, she is not divorced. And there are those that say that if the three of them are in the same city, we are worried that maybe the husband will say: I gave it to her [only] for safekeeping. And even if they are not in the same city, there are those that say that a agent is only believed when the Get is in his hand (so it seems from the Tur in the name of the Rosh) because he is like a Shlish (???) which is explained in Choshen Mishpat Siman 56.
