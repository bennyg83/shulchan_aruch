But initially, etc. See the responsum of Bach, New Responsa (HaChadashot), siman 87, who wrote: regarding the validation of an authorization that was validated twice — because it was written close to the eve [of Shabbat] and due to the delivery date was written as "the twenty-third of the month," omitting the word "day" — even though there is no concern about invalidating it since there is no room for error about anything else, etc. — in any case it is not appropriate for us to release it from our hands with this deficiency, and we agreed to correct it and re-validate it a second time through the witnesses already signed on the authorization and through the court signed on the certification (hanhak), and we did not wish to write another authorization since the time had passed and we would have needed to change the standard text of authorizations — end quote — see there. His intention is that [otherwise] they would have had to write two dates: one for when the get was delivered to the agent, and one for when the authorization was signed — and it is not proper to change the format of the earlier authorities, as is noted in Darkei Moshe s.k. 15: there was an incident with witnesses who had signed the get who also signed the authorization, and Maharik required [them to] write another authorization with other witnesses, as he did not wish to change the wording of the authorization — see there. This is because the standard wording of all authorizations is "and its witnesses who signed it are so-and-so, in my presence and before so-and-so," whereas here it was written "and its witnesses who signed it are those signed below." Nevertheless, it is obvious that if there is no time to write it the same day, they may write it the next day and there is no impediment.
