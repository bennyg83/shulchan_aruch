One who betroths, and some say, etc. See Tosafot in Gittin there, s.v. "na'arah," etc., and in Kiddushin 43b, s.v. "we learned," etc. See Rashi there, s.v. "na'arah," etc., and s.v. "she," etc. The view of the Rif is like Rashi before his retraction. So it is in the above Yerushalmi: a minor, etc. Regarding the difficulty of Tosafot, the Ran wrote that here too one could have said: according to your reasoning. The Ramban answered that Rava, who initially thought that a na'arah can make an agent, initially thought that the hand of her father is an additional hand, and if so even a minor can receive. But according to Rav Nachman, that a na'arah does not act in place of her father except as an agent, a minor cannot, because she is not within the law of shlichut.
