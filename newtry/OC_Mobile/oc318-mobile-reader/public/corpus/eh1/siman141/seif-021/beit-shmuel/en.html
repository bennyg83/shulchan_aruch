And the son becomes an agent, etc. The Beit Yosef holds that both the son and his father became agents for delivery, except that father and son may not sign together; rather, either the father or the son signs together with another. Maharshal holds that even as agents for delivery, father and son were not both appointed together, for the husband did not intend to appoint relatives together. The law depends on whether the appointment was made to them jointly as one agency or whether each was independently included.
