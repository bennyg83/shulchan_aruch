One who sends a Get in the possession of a agent, he can go back [on sending the agent, and still] divorce her with it when he wants, because he did not annul the law of the Get , but rather the law of the agent. Therefore, if the Get was in the hand of the husband and he annulled it, for example he said: This Get is annulled, he cannot ever divorce with it. And so too, even he specified at the time of his annulment, and it was in the possession of the agent [at the time], and he said: The Get that I sent, behold, it is annulled from being a Get ! He cannot divorce with it, and if he did [try to] divorce, she is doubtfully divorced.
