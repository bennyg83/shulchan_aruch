And some say, etc. As written in the above Yerushalmi, which the Rosh brought in chapter 4, seif 20, and Sefer HaTerumah and other decisors. What the Rosh wrote from the first chapter, that witnesses are not needed, means that he is believed regarding this, that he appointed him before witnesses. This is what is written in the gloss: however, etc. But the Rambam explains according to its simple meaning, as above.
