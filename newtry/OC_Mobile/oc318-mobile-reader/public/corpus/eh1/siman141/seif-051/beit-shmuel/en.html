The Rambam. According to the Rambam, where the required agency is absent there is no get. Tosafot and Rashi may understand the sugya differently, and in some cases treat the matter as doubtful because perhaps the agency was effective. The Beit Shmuel notes the practical difference where another agent was appointed or where the get was delivered by someone whose status is uncertain.
