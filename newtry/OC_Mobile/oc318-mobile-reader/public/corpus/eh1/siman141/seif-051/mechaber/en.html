[If] the husband says to the agent: Take from her this item and give her this Get , he [the agent] cannot send it in another's hand. And if he sent it in another's hand, and the wife gave the [other] agent the item first and afterwards he gave the Get , she is divorced. But if he gave the Get first and afterwards she gave the item, even in the hands of the first agent it is not a Get . [If the husband] said to him: Give her the Get and take from her this item, he [the agent] cannot send it in another's hand. And if he sent it in another's hand, it is a Get whether she gave [the item] first or she only gave it afterwards. And there are those who say that when [the husband] says to him: Take from her this item and give her the Get , if he sent it in another's hand, even if she gave him the item first and afterwards he gave her the Get , because she did not give the item to the first agent before she received the Get from the second agent, she is not divorced. What should he do [to rectify the situation]? The first [agent] should take [the Get ] from her hand and return it under the law of divorce, after he has received the item.
