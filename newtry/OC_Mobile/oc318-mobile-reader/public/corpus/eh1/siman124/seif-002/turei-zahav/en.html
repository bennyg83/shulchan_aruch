(א) With delivery witnesses. The Tur wrote: provided that the delivery witnesses are before us; but with signing witnesses, even if they are before us, it is invalid. However, with something that cannot be forged, it is valid with signing witnesses. End quote. Below, siman 133, the Tur wrote a dispute where there were no delivery witnesses at all, only signing witnesses: the Rosh forbids and Rif permits. Here the Tur wrote according to everyone: according to Rif it is obvious that where it cannot be forged it is valid with signing witnesses alone; and even according to the Rosh, here, where there are signing witnesses alone, we say presumably it was done properly and there were delivery witnesses where it could not be forged. So too the Rosh wrote explicitly, as Beit Yosef brought. Where it can be forged, it is invalid with signing witnesses, even according to Rif, for Rif's reason is that we have found that witnesses sign on a get in order to create proof.
