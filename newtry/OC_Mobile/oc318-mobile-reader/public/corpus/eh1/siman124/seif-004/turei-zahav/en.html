(א) For we require "and he shall place it in her hand." This is difficult, for with shechitah we also require something detached, as we derive from the verse "and he took the knife," as stated in the first chapter of Chullin, folio 11. If so, why do we say there that one who slaughters with a tooth fixed in the jaw is forbidden, while here it is permitted to write the get on the slave's hand? It appears one can answer that with shechitah they derived it from the change in the verse's language, namely that it says "and he took the knife" and did not write "and he prepared"; therefore it comes for a derashah, as Tosafot wrote there in Chullin, and we therefore exclude everything attached. But with a get, we derive it from the implication of the verse, "and he shall place it in her hand," meaning that he must be able to place it in her hand. If so, something attached is included wherever giving can be fulfilled with it, such as where he gives the slave from his hand to her hand.
