(א) With tattoo writing. At first glance it is difficult: how can the witnesses sign with tattoo writing? They thereby become wicked; even if they wrote but did not fill it in with ink, nevertheless they become rabbinically wicked. Granted, if the scribe writes with tattoo writing there is no difficulty, for even if the scribe becomes wicked the Get is valid, as written in the preceding siman. But if the witnesses write with tattoo writing, they become wicked and the Get is invalid. See Rambam at the end of Hilkhot Avodah Zarah and Yoreh Deah siman 180. Perhaps the case is where they acted unwittingly; see Tosafot, Gittin 20. Chelkat Mechokek wrote that this applies specifically when the Get is written with tattoo writing and the witnesses also signed so. But if the witnesses signed with ordinary writing, it is as though the Get were written on an erased surface and the witnesses on paper, and it is invalid, because there is concern that one may come to forge it. One must say that although the Get is written with tattoo writing and cannot be forged, nevertheless there is concern that he will erase the signatures and forge and write something in the Get. However, this is difficult: why would we assume he will erase the signatures? If so, he could not authenticate the signatures. It is also difficult because the phrase according to the law of Moses and Israel is like firm and established, and forgery would be recognizable. I saw that his words are taken from Bach, and there Bach wrote the reason: if the Get is engraved with tattoo writing but the witnesses signed ordinarily, there is concern that from the outset the Get too was written ordinarily and he forged it. This is difficult: from where do we derive such a concern, since now there is no concern of forgery? It should be compared to what is written in Choshen Mishpat siman 45, that if a document ends in the middle of a line and the witnesses signed, we are not concerned for forgery. Therefore it appears that there is no concern in this. What the Tur wrote, that the witnesses were engraved, implies that the witnesses too must be engraved in tattoo writing. One may say this is because we decree signature because of writing, as Tosafot wrote at the beginning of Gittin, folio 4. But according to this, if the witnesses signed on an erased surface and the Get is on paper, it should be invalid for this reason without the reason that perhaps he will erase the Get; this does not imply so from the words of the poskim.
