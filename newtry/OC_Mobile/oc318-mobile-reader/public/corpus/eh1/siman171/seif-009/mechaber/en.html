One was a girl of sound senses and the other deaf, if the levir had relations with the girl of sound senses first, and then he or his brother had relations with the deaf girl, he has not disqualified the girl of sounds senses, but the deaf girl requires a get. If the levir had relations with the deaf girl first, and then he or his brother had relations with the girl of sound senses, he has disqualified the deaf girl. And the deaf girl goes out with a get, and the girl of sound senses [goes out] with a get and chalitzah.
