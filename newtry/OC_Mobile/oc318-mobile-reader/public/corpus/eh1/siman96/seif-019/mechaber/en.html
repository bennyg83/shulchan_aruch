When a court or heirs make the widow swear when she comes to collect her ketubah — they do not make her swear except outside beit din, because batei din were reluctant to administer oaths lest she not be precise with herself. And an oath outside beit din is not so severe — it is not with God's name nor holding a sacred object, but only with the curse "cursed." And if the orphans wish — they impose a vow on her for whatever they want, and they impose it in beit din, and afterward she takes her ketubah. And the vow must be on something involving affliction of the soul, and it must be forbidden all her days. And a vow does not help except as long as she has not remarried; but if she remarried — she does not collect by vow because the husband can annul it for her.
