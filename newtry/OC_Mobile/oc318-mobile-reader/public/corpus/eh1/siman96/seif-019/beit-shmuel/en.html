(ב) She vows. In the Shas it asks: perhaps she will go before a sage and he will release it for her. It answers that she must specify the vow, and then when the sage hears the matter of the vow he will not release it for her. So too it answers there regarding a kohen who married a woman in prohibition: he vows and passes on. It also asks there: let us be concerned that perhaps he will go before a sage and he will release it for him. It answers there too that he must specify the vow. The difficulty was left according to the one who says a vow must be specified; but according to the one who says it need not be specified, what can be said? It answers that he must vow according to the will of the public. In Yoreh Deah siman 228 it rules that one must specify the vow, and in Orach Chayim siman 128 regarding a kohen who marries a forbidden woman, he must vow according to the will of the public. This is surprising: why do I need "according to the will of the public," since he must specify the vow, and when the sage hears the vow he will not release it?
