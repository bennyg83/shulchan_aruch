The Rosh’s responsum discusses whether the husband or heirs can claim that the widow’s sale was invalid because she had not yet sworn. The practical question turns on whether the property was in her possession, whether she was selling to collect her ketubah, and whether the later oath is viewed as clarifying a preexisting right. Where the husband’s heirs remain in possession, they have greater strength; where the buyer or widow’s heirs are in possession, they can often maintain their hold under the disputed views.
