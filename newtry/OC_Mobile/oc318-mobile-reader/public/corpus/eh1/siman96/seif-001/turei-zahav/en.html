Only with an oath, for we say perhaps bundles were given to her. Tosafot asked in Perek HaSholeach, Gittin 34: a ketubah was not given to be collected during the husband’s lifetime, so it is within its time, and there is a presumption that a person does not pay within the time, even from orphans. They answered that this applies to an ordinary creditor, but a widow, because she has a beit din condition, may have been given bundles even within the time. End quote.
