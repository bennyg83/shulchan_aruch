(ה) Heirs, etc. There are three kinds of seizure. First, if the woman seized during her lifetime and her heirs are in possession of it; in this case we do not say that a person cannot bequeath an oath to his heirs. Second, if the woman seized but the heirs are not in possession, rather it is deposited in beit din, like the case of Terumat HaDeshen siman 330. Terumat HaDeshen there did not decide the matter in practice and concludes that at least it would appear that they divide. Third, if the heirs seized after death. It is explained in Mordechai that if there are no witnesses and seeing, according to everyone the seizure helps; but if there are witnesses and seeing, the poskim disagree, and this is what "some disagree" cited by Rama refers to. Ran and Raavyah hold that even so the seizure helps. See Choshen Mishpat siman 108. See R. Aharon Sasson siman 53, Maharit siman 44, and Keneset HaGedolah, Choshen Mishpat siman 108. If beit din already obligated her to swear and she died before swearing, her heirs do not collect the ketubah. So wrote Responsa Rashba. The husband is believed to say that the ketubah was paid; see Beit Shmuel.
