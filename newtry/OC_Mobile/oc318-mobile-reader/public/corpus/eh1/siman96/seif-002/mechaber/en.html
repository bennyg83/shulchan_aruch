What oath the widow swears: that she did not seize and that she did not seize from his anything. And there are those who say she must swear that she did not forgive him her ketubah and did not sell to him. {Rama: And if she swore vaguely that she did not benefit from my ketubah — that suffices (Rif ch. HaSholech). And it is also customary to make her swear that she did not squander anything without her husband's permission. And if her husband lacks competence — they say vaguely that she did not squander more than appropriate (Maharik root 19).}
