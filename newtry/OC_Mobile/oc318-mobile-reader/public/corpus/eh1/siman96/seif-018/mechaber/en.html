One married to four wives — this one's ketubah a hundred, this one's two hundred, this one's three hundred, this one's four hundred — and all were signed on one day and he died and he lacks enough for all the ketubot — how do they divide? See: if when dividing the money by the number of women they reach at least the smallest of them her ketubah amount or less — they divide equally. And if the money was more than this — they divide from it until the smallest reaches her ketubah proportion, and she returns and divides the remainder among the remaining wives by the first method.
