But with movables. Since the divorcee or widow has the force of collection, if she sold movables, the sale can stand more readily than with land, because movables are subject to possession. Nevertheless, where the sale preceded the required oath, the matter depends on the dispute whether her later oath validates the sale retroactively or whether at the time of sale she had no right to sell. The Tur, Beit Yosef, Rashba, and Rosh are discussed at length on this point, and the practical ruling depends on whether the property was known, whether she later swore, and whether the buyer is in possession.
