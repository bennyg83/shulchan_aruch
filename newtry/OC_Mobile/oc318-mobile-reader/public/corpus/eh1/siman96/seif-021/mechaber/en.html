There are those who say that nowadays they make a widow swear in beit din because in that matter where they are accustomed to make her swear — they impose cherem and Torah oath that she confess what she received of her ketubah — the penalty is not so great as an oath where at the time she swears a false oath comes from her mouth. {Rama: And she must swear before three who are fit; and if she swore before three who are relatives — she must repeat and swear (Beit Yosef in name of responsum Rashba and responsum Maharam end of Hafla'ah). And ab initio she must swear before the orphans; but if she swore not in their presence, or the orphans do not wish to be present at the oath — they make her swear not in their presence (likewise there in name of Rashba). A widow who swore on her ketubah — the orphans may afterward impose cherem on anyone who knows whether the widow deposited from their property (Rivash siman 325).}
