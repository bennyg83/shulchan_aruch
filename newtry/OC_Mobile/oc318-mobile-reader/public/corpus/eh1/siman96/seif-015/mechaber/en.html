She says "he married me as a virgin and my ketubah is two hundred" and the husband or heirs say "no — rather a widow" and the ketubah was lost, or in a place where they do not write a ketubah — if there are witnesses that they made for her like the custom for virgins — her ketubah is two hundred, even if one witness was a minor when he saw the testimony and now he is adult, and another is with him — it helps. And if there are no witnesses — she takes only a maneh and the husband must swear. There are those who say a Torah oath; and there are those who wrote a heses oath. {Rama: And some say: in a place where they do not write a ketubah — heses oath; where they write and it was lost — Torah oath, for he can deny everything and say "I paid" and therefore it is called partial admission (Maggid Mishneh ch. 16). And see below siman 100 seif 6.}
