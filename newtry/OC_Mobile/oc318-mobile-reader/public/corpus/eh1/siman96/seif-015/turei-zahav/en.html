The Tur, Rambam, and Choshen Mishpat are discussed regarding whether the sale of land or movables before the oath stands. The Rambam appears to validate even a sale of land, while other authorities distinguish. The Shulchan Aruch’s rulings in this siman and later in siman 103 require reconciliation, because in one place he implies the sale can stand after an oath, while elsewhere he speaks as though the sale already took effect and the oath follows.
