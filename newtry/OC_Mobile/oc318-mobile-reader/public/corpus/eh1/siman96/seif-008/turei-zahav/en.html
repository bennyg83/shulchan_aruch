Some say that the same applies even where she sold for support. See the Beit Yosef and the decisors cited there. The issue is whether we say that since her right to collect was not yet fully clarified by an oath, the sale was ineffective, or whether the later oath clarifies her right and the sale stands. Maharik and other responsa are cited regarding possession and the buyer’s ability to say kim li. The matter is treated more strictly with land than with movables.
