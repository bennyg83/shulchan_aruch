She produced her ketubah document for a thousand zuz and he says "all was paid" and she says "I was paid nothing, but I have only a claim of a thousand against you because there was trust between us" — she takes without oath. But if she says "in my ketubah document there is only a thousand" — she is not paid on this document that says a thousand at all. (And the husband swears a heses oath and is exempt) (Rambam ch. 16 and so writes Beit Yosef).
