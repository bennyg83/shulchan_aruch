(ד) But because of other defects, etc. He must provide the other obligations of food and clothing, but regarding conjugal relations we do not compel him to have children from a woman he hates. Even though he knew of her defects and waived them, nevertheless now she is repulsive to him and he cannot accept her. We also do not compel him to return the dowry to her, since she is the one delaying and does not wish to accept the Get; see there, shoresh 107. It appears that this speaks of defects that arose in her, or that he knew about. But if she had defects and he did not know, one may say that we compel her, since she acted improperly, as the Rosh wrote in his responsa, klal 35, that if he acted improperly and betrothed through deception, we compel him; likewise, we compel her.
