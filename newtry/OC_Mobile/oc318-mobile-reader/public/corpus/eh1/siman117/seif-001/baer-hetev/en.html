(ד) First. This refers to a widow. But if she is a virgin, we attribute it to blood of virginity. If she saw blood three times with every act of intercourse, and intercourse was not painful for her, then we do not attribute it to blood of virginity, since it was without pain. There is room to doubt: perhaps what she saw in the first acts of intercourse was also not blood of virginity, but rather she always saw at the time of intercourse; or perhaps what she saw at first was blood of virginity, and now his field became ruined and she saw because of intercourse. If so, she has a ketubah. See Chelkat Mechokek; Beit Shmuel.
