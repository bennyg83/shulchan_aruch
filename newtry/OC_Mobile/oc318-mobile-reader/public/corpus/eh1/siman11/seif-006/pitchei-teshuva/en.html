Leaving dark place. See responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 99 who wrote what witness testified in incident (his matter brought below s.k. 11) that at midnight she stood in city street with suspect — cannot compare to Rambam ch. 25 Hilchot Ishut and Shulchan Aruch siman 11 "or both leaving dark place"; nevertheless in responsum Maharshal siman 33 on such testimony that witness saw her standing in seclusion with suspect in dark at midnight — and geonim Rama and his signed assistants there compared this to ugly matter; and Maharshal wrote on this this is not ugly matter to forbid her to husband (instead) dark place Rambam wrote means hidden place unique for sin to hide matter — unlike our case at house door with passers and residents in entire great city and city guards always circulating — this is no hiding at all — see there; also in responsum Mabit siman 286 also wrote dark place means in that dark place no person's way who does not dwell there to enter — comparison to helping each other from pit and similar where no way to enter pit; and likewise entered sequentially and barred doors — seclusion and hiding place since house surrounded on all sides and no person can enter and see what they do — see there (and see book 108 who wrote what Beit Meir gaon told him no proof from Mabit — one may say it discusses seclusion by day — not so explicitly in Mabit responsum it was at night in thick darkness and gloom); also sage Maharam Naphtali Hertz brought in responsa Rama siman 17 wrote on Rama's ruling and his assistants above it is not similar: dark place means no human foot is found there and matters recognizable that sin was there — for otherwise why enter dark place; but place where human foot is found even in dead of night and gloom one cannot say certainly sin was there — for tremblers perhaps person will pass before them such as standing at midnight to study or one of city's sons awakens from sleep and goes out — see there; and it seems to support their words from precision of Rambam's language from Yerushalmi "both leaving dark place" and did not state "they saw them standing," etc.; and although Rama and assistants' view standing with suspect at midnight at her door is ugly matter — nevertheless since Maharshal and sage R' Naphtali Hertz saw their words and both prophesied in one style to reject their words in good taste and their colleague; and also in Mabit responsum cited — it seems we rule thus for halakhah — end opinion. And see siman 100 Beit Meir gaon agreed with him and wrote possibly Rama also admits in our case for there he was precise they stood in seclusion and considers this standing behind door as seclusion; but in street that is public domain and city guards surround — not enough called ugly matter but even seclusion possibly only transgressing religion certainly called — i.e. speaking and laughing with young men — Avnei Tzedek siman 115 s.k. 12; and see further simanim 104 and 105 on this.
