And he had children. Beit Shmuel wrote per Rif and Rambam meaning from defamer; and Ramban wrote: I did not clarify his reason, for even if she certainly committed adultery with him and married, the child is not disqualified even for kehunah; one may say nevertheless the child is tainted — so Rashba; and see Yevamot 49 Tosafot s.v. sotah. And if it were possible to say Rif's view is like Rabbenu Tam that from sotah would be rabbinic mamzer — possible likewise from paramour since both from prohibition "and she was defiled"; but per Rambam their words are certainly required; and see siman 4 — and understand.
