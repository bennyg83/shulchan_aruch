Wherever they warned him. Implies even defamer from maidservant mentioned in seif 5 has this law; and I already wrote in siman 10 there is a reversed version in Yerushalmi as Ramban and Rashba wrote; and it is in Beit Yosef there and Maggid ch. 10 law 3: for one version kiddushin are like marriage — per this warning is not after-the-fact and we remove; and for second version the reverse; and Rosh ruled if they warned it is not after-the-fact as stated siman 141; and Rivash siman 242 ruled leniently in both and we do not remove; and Bach s.k. 60 ruled in matter where there is no prohibition concern except slander we do not remove; and brought proof from Mishnah defamer on maidservant which teaches "and if he married we do not remove" and does not teach "and if he preceded and married" — implies he preceded before they warned as Rosh wrote in ch. Keitzad — learn: even if they warned we do not remove since there is only slander; and part 4 and Taz rejected his good proof and ruled not like him; nevertheless practically he ruled well, for Rivash who is later ruled leniently; and for what Mechaber wrote we remove after warning — if so after kiddushin it is like marriage; and see end siman 12.
