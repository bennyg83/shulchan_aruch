They entered one after another, etc. See responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger letter cited siman above — wrote certainly ordinary seclusion is not included in ugly matter — compelled from three proofs (he mentioned there); now must distinguish ordinary seclusion and they entered one after another and locked the doors — for apparently this too is only seclusion as Rashba wrote siman 1259; also Rama's language responsum siman 11 explains locked together is only seclusion ugly matter — language "locked" implies certainly locked with bolt, etc.; must say they entered one after another means both entered consecutively one after another — each entered on his fellow's word and appointed together to enter this place — recognizable counsel of lewdness between them; unlike if one was already there for his needs and afterward the second entered — only ordinary seclusion not ugly matter; possible to say further we also require locking doors immediately — but if locked after an hour or two can say also not ugly matter. And even though from responsum Maharam Lublin siman 80 implies Rambam holds all seclusion is ugly matter — per my view the proofs will testify and justify it is not so — end quote see there; in siman 100 there Beit Meir gaon answered him and agreed ordinary seclusion not included in ugly matter; but where he holds also locked with bolt is only seclusion — forced to explain they entered one after another means both entered consecutively, etc. — disputes him and wrote Rashba there is error (and truly seclusion exists even without bolt lock; if locked with bolt it is ugly matter — Rama's words responsum cited requires examination see there; there siman 101 in Rabbi Akiva Eiger gaon's second responsum wrote him what he wrote Rashba error — his words correct and compelled — certainly also without lock called seclusion; nevertheless regarding ugly matter possible to say as stated we also require entered consecutively — not to leave Rama's words responsum in difficulty, etc.; siman 102 there Beit Meir gaon again answered — wrote after Semag's words changed Rambam's language proves locking alone constitutes ugly matter — why stick against Rishonim to settle Acharonim; see there further simanim 104 and 105.
