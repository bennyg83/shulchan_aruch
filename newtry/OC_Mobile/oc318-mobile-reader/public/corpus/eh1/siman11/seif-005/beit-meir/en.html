Further he wrote in that principle folly of She'iltot per Maharam; and if there is non-decisive rumor we also remove from husband — see Rosh and Beit Yosef and Shiyurei Yisrael explained for this view in any case requires at least for removing from husband that husband himself saw ugly matter; and per Shiyurei Yisrael the rumor must be in his own presence — otherwise it is rumor after marriage and no distinction; and what Beit Shmuel challenges on Darkei Moshe's explanation for Rif is agreed from all responsa of Rif for law he holds beit din does not remove them from husband except witnesses of defilement. And what Maharshal in responsum wrote that Maharam disputes Rif in what he explains Rabbi's words on husband "and she leaves" — says it does not refer to beit din but commandment on husband and without ketubah — see Nissim Yisraeli; apparently he explains also Rif this way — clear in my humble opinion that per Ramban's view we are stringent on defamer more than husband and even Rabbi himself agrees from husband beit din does not remove with ugly matter and from defamer we remove — and this is also Rama's view in hagahah.
