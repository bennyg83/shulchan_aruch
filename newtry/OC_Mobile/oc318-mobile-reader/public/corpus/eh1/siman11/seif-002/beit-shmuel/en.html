One who removed her, etc. Per Mechaber's view we do not remove from suspect unless there are two aggravating factors — ugly matter and non-decisive rumor even if another later stopped — this law is no innovation but teaches initially she is forbidden to him even though another later stopped; but what he wrote "if she married she does not leave" — no innovation; and Rama did not emend anything here even though he ruled we remove from defamer with one aggravating factor — must say he holds since another later stopped the rumor is weaker and she does not leave. And Rivan wrote in name of majority poskim: wherever she leaves from husband another's stopping does not help and she leaves; however one may say where she does not leave from husband except from defamer another's stopping helps; and it seems it depends on the dispute I cited, for there he challenged: if there are witnesses and another came and stopped — what does stopping help; if so per poskim who hold ugly matter means explicit witnesses of defilement — if there is ugly matter another's stopping does not help and she leaves; but per other poskim one may say if there is only ugly matter another's stopping helps and no difficulty from what is written siman 4 seif 15 regarding a married woman, etc. — for there there is no ugly matter; but seemingly difficult on Rabbenu Tam's view who holds if there is non-decisive rumor she leaves from husband from sugya there; however Tosafot wrote it discusses rumor that is not a day and a half — and understand even per Rabbenu Tam's view.
