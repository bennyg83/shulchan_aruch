Further he wrote: and from suspect we remove when there is non-decisive rumor and witnesses of ugly matter — in my humble opinion he should not have included Rabbenu Tam, for ugly matter's intent is Rabbenu Tam in Tosafot before the return and there it is not mentioned that from suspect requires ugly matter together with non-decisive rumor; rather since he explains Rabbi refers to peddler, then everything goes per plain meaning of Gemara comparing non-decisive rumor to ugly matter; and so stated in part 4 s.k. 7 in his name; and also Rashi's meaning who wrote Rabbi said them — removes woman from husband with any rumor whatsoever — is through comparison of rumor to ugly matter.
