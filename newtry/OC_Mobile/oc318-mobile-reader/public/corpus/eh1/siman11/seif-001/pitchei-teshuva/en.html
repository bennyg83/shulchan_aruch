Avnei Tzedek s.k. 4: also to second paramour she is forbidden; and see in book Bnei Yehuda who was in doubt: second paramour who is inadvertent thinking she is his wife and behold a prostitute met him and again husband divorced through first paramour — in such case also forbidden per what Yerushalmi states beginning ch. Keshem Hayah as Tosafot brought there 27 — he inadvertent and she intentional — plainly forbidden to her house; he divorced — may he be permitted to her; perhaps she left from his hand and you say thus — see there; behold if he is inadvertent what forbids him is because through him she was forbidden to husband; per this in our case there is room to rule: since he is inadvertent and she was forbidden through the first and not through him — fitting she should be permitted to him. He elaborated on this and understanding Yerushalmi's words and raised we do not rule like Yerushalmi but like Shimon bar Ba per R' Yehuda there as our Talmud — the matter depends; if so also second paramour inadvertent and she intentional plainly forbidden and no difference first to second — see at length.
