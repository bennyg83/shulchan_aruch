A woman suspected regarding a married woman. It contains 8 seifim. A woman suspected regarding a married woman — if she had a warning and seclusion but did not drink the bitter waters, since she is forbidden to her husband on his account, behold she is forbidden forever to the one with whom she was secluded, just as she is forbidden to her husband; and if he transgressed and married her, they remove her from him with a get, even if she had many children from him. From oral tradition they learned: just as she is forbidden to her husband, so too she is forbidden to the paramour. {Rama: And likewise if she became forbidden to her husband on his account, she is forbidden to him. (his own reasoning.)} But if a warning did not precede and witnesses came upon her that she was secluded with this man, and they came and found an ugly matter — such as they entered after him and found her standing up from the bed and she was wearing undergarments, or fastening her belt, or they found spit above the canopy {Rama: or they saw the place of the shoes inverted (Tur)}, or they were leaving a dark place, or helping each other out of a pit and the like, or they saw him kissing on the neck of her garment, or they saw them kissing each other, or they entered one after the other and barred the doors {Rama: with a lock (Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of Rashba 1251)}, and similar matters {Rama: according to what the judges' eyes see} — if her husband divorces her for an ugly matter like this, behold she may not marry the defamer; and if he transgressed and married her, and he had children from her, she need not leave. When is this said? When the city gossiped about her and the defamer for a day and a half or more, and they said to him "So-and-so committed adultery with so-and-so," and the rumor did not stop, and neither she nor he {Rama: nor the husband (Ran ch. 2 of Yevamot)} had enemies who spread the rumor. But if there was no rumor in the city about this matter, or the rumor stopped not out of fear — if she married the defamer she need not leave, even if she has no children; even if one witness came that she committed adultery with him, she need not leave. {Rama: And some say that if there was here an actual rumor as explained, together with witnesses of ugly matter, they remove her even from her husband (Rashi and She'iltot and Mordekhai in the name of Maharam ch. 2 of Yevamot) if he has no children from her; but with only one of them they do not remove from the husband unless her husband divorced her and the suspect married her — then they remove from the suspect with only one of them if she has no children from him (thus is implied from Rif and Rambam). Two witnesses of ugly matter join together even though they saw sequentially, and the ugly matter this one saw the other did not see (Mordekhai ch. 2 of Yevamot in the name of a responsum of Maharam). One witness to an ugly matter is legally meaningless (responsum of Maharam end of Nashim).}
