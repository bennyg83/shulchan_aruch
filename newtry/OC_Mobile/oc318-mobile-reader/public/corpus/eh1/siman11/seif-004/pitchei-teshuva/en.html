To paramour. Avnei Tzedek wrote there is no distinction, etc.; and proof from Sanhedrin — behold this proof is not his but from responsum Shevut Yaakov there; and in responsum Sha'arei De'ah in Kuntras A he rejected this proof: intent of Talmud is witnesses should say thus — not full law and they have claim even if untrue; and see book Bnei Yehuda this rejection is not clear to him and he greatly elaborated on this matter; and see responsum Noda Biyhudah second siman 20 who also rejected this proof from another angle — see there; and see further in responsum Brit Avraham siman 94 letter 16 what is written on this (and see there s.k. afterward).
