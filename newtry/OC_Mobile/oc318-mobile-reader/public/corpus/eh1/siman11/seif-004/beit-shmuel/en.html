And not in her husband's presence. See Turei Zahav siman 21 where it is explained: if testimony was accepted not in litigants' presence — majority poskim hold it does not help; and so ruled in responsa Maharshal siman 11; and wrote: even one who holds there in monetary matters if accepted it is testimony — nevertheless here it does not help; and see responsum Rama siman 12; and see Mordekhai ch. HaMekabel: if they testified without derishah and chakirah they may retract; and wrote in part 4: if she already married the paramour acceptance of testimony must be in his presence; but if she did not marry him testimony need not be accepted before defamer even though through testimony she is forbidden to defamer.
