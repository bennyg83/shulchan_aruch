And one woman separated among men. For Rambam discusses ordinary or kosher men; for Rosh discusses licentious; Rashi who permits one man with many women must say here men whose occupation with women; Bach wrote when sitting in two rooms forbidden for concern one goes secretly to room where women sit — but when secludes and known he is among them they guard one another; this distinction for Rashi's view; but Rambam Tur and Mechaber wrote and one woman separated — implies even if known one separated among women still forbidden — must say as written; many licentious with many women Choshen Mishpat wrote possibly forbidden — therefore this seif not discussing licentious; that Rama did not hagah anything because adjacent to previous seif.
