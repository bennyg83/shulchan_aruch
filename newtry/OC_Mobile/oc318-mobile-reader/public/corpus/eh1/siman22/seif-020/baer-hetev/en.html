Woman. Mechaber ruled even if he had wife not dwelling with him forbidden to teach; however Maggid holds specifically bachelor who never had wife forbidden; and Beit Yosef wondered at custom permitting to learn in other communities without wife — and seems they rely on Maggid's view — Beit Shmuel — see Kenat HaGadol.
Children. With males or females; and likewise woman shall not teach with boys or girls — Perishah Beit Shmuel.
Shall not teach, etc. Even if she has husband because man's way is not always in house — see Beit Shmuel; one without wife shall not hire widow maidservant even if son with her — for son not there every hour — when he dwells alone in house — Mahari Weil siman 55 — see there.
