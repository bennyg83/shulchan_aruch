One without wife. Mechaber ruled even if had wife as wrote — teacher, etc. — for holds halakhah like R' Eliezer who said one who had wife not dwelling with him forbidden teach; Maggid rules like first tanna — specifically bachelor never had wife forbidden — so infers Rif; Beit Yosef wondered custom permitting learn other communities without wife — must say rely on Maggid.
Shall not teach child. With males or females; likewise woman shall not teach with boys or girls — so Perishah.
And found incites with women. Here wrote incites and regarding woman wrote becomes secluded — seems here even without seclusion forbidden because inciting women; regarding women incitement inapplicable and forbidden only if secluded — so Bach.
And likewise woman, etc. Even married — regarding man since wife with him she guards him because her way always in house; but man's way not always home — so Kesef Mishneh chapter 2 laws Torah study; meaning since his way travel elsewhere therefore forbidden; but if in city permitted as wrote nearby — not like Hagahot Tur Derishah — accordingly can say even if husband in same house forbidden since way travel elsewhere — unlike Taz Yoreh Deah siman 245; practical difference Kesef Mishneh seclusion permitted when wife with him in city; per reason I wrote previous seif helps when wife with him in city and no incitement concern — therefore no seclusion concern; but difficult — explained here even without wife permitted — then reason she guards him inapplicable; perhaps holds even if wife not dwelling in schoolhouse nevertheless anytime comes to him can say she guards him — if other city forbidden; Taz wrote in name Mahariik responsum siman 55 one without wife shall not hire widow maidservant even if son with her — son not every hour — when dwells alone in house.
