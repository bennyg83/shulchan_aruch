Androgyne, etc. Difficult to fit — opened with androgyne and concluded with tumtum and did not mention tumtum at start; and tumtum even with woman permitted; and taught in Tosafot there androgyne does not seclude neither with men nor women; and Kesef Mishneh resolved Rambam holds Tosafot like R' Yosi end Kiddushin — meaning androgyne considered male — words hard as vinegar to teeth — if so what Tosafot said there how equal to women, etc. and does not seclude with men like women — even with men likewise; and Rambam rules androgyne safek as wrote here — if secluded, etc. because, etc. — see above siman 44 s.k. 5 — rather scribal error — must read androgyne does not seclude with men nor women; if all safek — but man and woman seclude with tumtum — see Yevamot 82a his wives for tumtum from where.
