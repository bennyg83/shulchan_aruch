Permitted to be secluded with two yevamot or with two rival wives or with woman and her mother-in-law or with woman and her husband's daughter or with woman and her husband's mother-in-law — because they hate one another and do not cover for one another; and likewise with woman who has with her young girl who knows meaning of intercourse and does not surrender herself to intercourse — for she will not fornicate before her because she reveals her secret.
