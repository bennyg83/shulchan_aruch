And father with daughter. See Bach letter 1 wrote all descendants equal — daughter's daughter and daughter's daughter's daughter permitted like daughter — see above siman 51 in Avodat HaGershuni s.k. 14 what wrote s.k. 15; see Noda BiYehudah tinyana siman 18 brought below siman 178 s.k. 10; see Sefer Zechor LeAvraham letter 10 seclusion also Bach's words; wrote further apparently all specifically seclude with her alone — but other woman present even with daughter or daughter's daughter forbidden for two women forbidden seclude per all (see below seif 5); father and daughter traveling with other Israelite forbidden — only in city two men help not road lest father need bodily needs daughter alone with other Israelite; same reason woman traveling with son cannot go with wagon-driver unless other Israelite — three with wagon-driver; no distinction son or father etc. — always require three — only husband and wife said guard one another not others; see responsum Mahari Weil siman 55 (much in Avodat HaGershuni s.k. 10); see there also sage wrote even if son guards mother because son strict mother not fornicate unlike father daughter daughter not as strict if father fornicates — see there.
