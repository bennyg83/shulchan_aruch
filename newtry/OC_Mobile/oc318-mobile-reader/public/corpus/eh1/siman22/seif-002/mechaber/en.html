When the incident of Amnon and Tamar occurred David and his court decreed upon seclusion of unmarried woman — and though she is not forbidden relation she is within seclusion of forbidden relations; and Shammai and Hillel decreed upon seclusion with idolatress — found: whoever is secluded with woman with whom seclusion is forbidden — whether Israelite or idolatress — they strike both lashes of rebellion — man and woman — and proclaim upon them: {Rama: And specifically when known he was secluded with her — but she is not believed regarding him (Rivash siman 481); and even one witness he is not believed (Mahariik shoresh 289); and nevertheless where it appears to court there is concern of prohibition they must separate them (there in Rivash):} Except married woman — for though forbidden to be secluded with her — if secluded they do not lash him lest they cast aspersion upon her that she fornicated and thereby cast aspersion upon her children that they are mamzerim.
