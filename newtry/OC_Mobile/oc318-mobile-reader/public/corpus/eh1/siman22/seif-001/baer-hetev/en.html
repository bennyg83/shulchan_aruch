Forbidden relation. Tosafot and Tur wrote seclusion is from Torah; and Rambam wrote seclusion is from Kabbalah — only hint from Torah — therefore they do not lash him except lashes of rebellion; see in HaRaam part 2 siman 3 and in Sefer Maaseh Chiya siman 23; and within forbidden relations are included chiyvei lavin — Perishah; and in Talmud explained Rav Assi said permitted to be secluded with sister temporarily and dwell with mother permanently — and Rosh wrote halakhah is like Rav Assi — see Beit Shmuel.
