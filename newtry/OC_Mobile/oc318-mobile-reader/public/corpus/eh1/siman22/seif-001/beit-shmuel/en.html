Forbidden to be secluded. In Talmud learned from verse "if your brother son of your mother incites you" — and does son of mother incite and not son of father — rather hints son secludes with mother; and Tosafot first chapter Shabbat wrote seclusion from Torah forbidden — so Tur; and Rambam wrote seclusion prohibition from Kabbalah — only hint from Torah — therefore they lash only lashes of rebellion; and within forbidden relations included chiyvei lavin — so Perishah; and in Talmud explained Rav Assi said permitted seclude with sister temporarily and dwell with mother permanently — and Rosh wrote halakhah like Rav Assi; and I do not know why Rambam and Tur omitted — perhaps they hold though halakhah like Rav Assi where disputes Shmuel — what he added beyond mishnah halakhah not like him for mishnah only taught seclusion with mother.
