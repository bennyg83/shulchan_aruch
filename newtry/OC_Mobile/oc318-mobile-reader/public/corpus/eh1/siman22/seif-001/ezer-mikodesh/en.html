Seif 1 (except mother with son). Not explained regarding seclusion with one's mother whether she is like mother; incidentally while discussing I warned someone today about seclusion when his wife traveled and told him take a guard — afterward remembered perhaps if mother she is like mother in this — requires examination; apparently per Rashi's reason that Men of Great Assembly helped not incite through closeness — permitted seclusion with mother — can say if mother included in closeness like mother; per "if your brother son of your mother incites you" — need verse as written and not expound reason of verse — only mother Torah permitted — examine well. Maharsha in novellae to aggadot Yoma regarding not incite through closeness wrote — not add incitement but still needs guarding like others; as found regarding daughter — seems his intent per Shmuel end Kiddushin who is stringent on daughter and mother; Maharsha cited daughter because Shmuel said condition; brought proof from R' Meir "beware of me because of my daughter" — cited daughter but Shmuel also stringent on mother; "beware of me because of my daughter-in-law" — seems inapplicable to Shmuel since all agree; perhaps intent also son-in-law in city — but then no proof they dispute for certainly he was stringent on himself from piety; perhaps "without witnesses" not to exclude husband in city requiring two witnesses; no hint "because of daughter-in-law" means husband in city saying daughter-in-law like daughter since man does not refrain calling daughter-in-law daughter — not seen from all poskim who only permitted daughter; seems daughter-in-law forbidden; perhaps Shmuel for him daughter equal daughter-in-law — brought proof from daughter-in-law for mother and daughter; requires examination. Rashi's reason Men of Great Assembly — likewise Ran — requires examination for Talmud cited asmakhta "incites you"; must say since asmakhta only for mother and reason permitting daughter not explained — wrote this reason — son common with mother not daughter with father except with mother as Sages said; must say before Second Temple daughter seclusion forbidden (perhaps mother permitted from asmakhta even then without distinction in reason); enactment only for guarding depended on times; mother-in-law Sages cited in Talmud seems only because he mocked seclusion with mother-in-law; anyway even stringent on all forbidden relations hold Men of Great Assembly helped regarding kin — as Maharsha wrote they helped not add incitement — Maharsha's intent only per stringent; not per lenient who hold like them — intent no incitement or seduction whatsoever regarding kin; seems helped for every kin more distant progressively in removal — therefore seclusion permitted only mother and daughter; perhaps as son accustomed with mother so daughter with father — asmakhta applies from distinction in reason of habit; Rashi's reason Men of Great Assembly only regarding permitted dwell with mother and daughter as mishnah implies only flesh proximity when grown forbidden — no resolution from verse — permitted from Men of Great Assembly; needed this reason because permitted sister seclusion without asmakhta — do not learn beyond these; what Men of Great Assembly helped all kin — as Maharsha wrote at least not add; from R' Meir also daughter — heard rabbis likewise all except mother daughter sister; perhaps mother daughter no incitement before Second Temple; room to say abundant dispute not resolved — all agree mother seclusion forbidden; room to say regarding distance from seduction like R' Shimon — regarding kin opposite as known a fortiori daughter from daughter's daughter — examine well. Also I doubt if when mother or daughter with him in house — seclusion in that house with other woman inapplicable since no concern for mother she is like girl with guard; or deduce from "his wife guards him" — specifically wife; two yevamot or rivals do not cover — though no incitement for daughter and mother and no desire — nevertheless no guarding for they cover — examine; piety to distance from all kin even mother daughter; and from animal as all poskim wrote (piety not cited in poskim since piety style not equal every soul — amoraim stringent from piety per their locale — not learn absolutely for piety especially Shmuel prohibition only rabbinic — nevertheless piety path exists) amoraim stringent in piety and one explanation stringent also on others; perhaps any partition suffices; even opening and seclusion partition no benefit as poskim wrote; dividing house in two as wrote elsewhere — path only through room where woman — for piety path above can be lenient in reasoning; one version regarding animal partition from inclination helps — not partition like wall; door openable suddenly from outside can be lenient; lenient regarding women outside — good to be lenient.
