The prohibition of seclusion and with whom one may not be secluded. It contains 20 seifim. Forbidden to be secluded with forbidden relation among forbidden relations — whether old woman or young girl — for this matter causes uncovering of forbidden relation; except mother with her son, father with his daughter, and husband with his niddah wife; and groom whose wife became niddah before he had intercourse — forbidden to be secluded with her — rather she sleeps among the women and he sleeps among the men; and if he had first intercourse with her and afterward she became impure — permitted to be secluded with her (see Yoreh Deah siman 192).
