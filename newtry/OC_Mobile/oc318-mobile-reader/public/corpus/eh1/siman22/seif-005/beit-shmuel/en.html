Then this woman's husband guards her and she will not fornicate then other ashamed to fornicate — from this heard Rambam holds two women forbidden seclude with many men — for otherwise why need say even if one not wife of one permitted; accordingly seems what wrote afterward many women with many men permitted means three women permitted but two women forbidden — see Perishah what wrote in name Maharshal — see Bach; one woman with many men and one is husband permitted for husband guards her; one man with many women permitted when wife with him; also heard from Rambam holds ordinary men not established as kosher — for wrote with many men forbidden.
But in field or at night. Reason in field lest one need bodily needs leave and she remain with one; Darkei Moshe in name R' Yehudah of Orleans — specifically when go to guard her both not leave — if not going to guard permitted — if one needs bodily needs other goes with him and she alone; but no concern one remains with her for kosher discussed and will not transgress intentionally; at night forbidden seclusion for concern one sleeps and other remains with her — though can wake friend — nevertheless between them secludes with her; Raavad disagrees — wrote lest one sleeps and other transgresses and comes upon her — therefore on road forbidden even if not going to guard; but hard why concern intentional transgression for kosher discussed — can resolve somewhat; Perishah wrote from this spread custom women take infant traveling with wagon-driver — when she or infant needs bodily needs walks with infant; Bach in name Maharshal wrote forbidden — and implied from Shulchan Aruch not rely on R' Yehudah of Orleans since omitted his view here.
