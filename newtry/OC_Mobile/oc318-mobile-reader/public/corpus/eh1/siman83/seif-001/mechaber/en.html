Law of one who injures a married woman — to whom [does payment go]. It contains 2 seifim. One who injures a married woman — the cessation [of work] and healing belong to her husband, and her pain, embarrassment, and damage — if openly such as he injured her face and neck or her hand and arms — a third is hers and two thirds to the husband. If in private the damage — a third to the husband and two thirds to the woman. The husband's [portion] they give him immediately and the woman's — land is acquired with them and the husband eats the fruits and she cannot waive neither his portion nor hers; and if she waives she does not waive. Nevertheless he cannot claim her portion except with her authorization. When does this apply? When others injured her; but a husband who injures his wife — he is obligated to pay her immediately all the damage and all the embarrassment and pain — all hers and the husband has no fruits in them. And if she wished to give the money to another she gives and the husband heals her as he heals all his household. {Rama: If anyone insulted her with words — even though one who embarrasses with words is exempt — if they appeased with money (Daat Emet) — if they insulted openly two parts to him and one part to her; if in private two parts to her and one part to him (Tur).}
