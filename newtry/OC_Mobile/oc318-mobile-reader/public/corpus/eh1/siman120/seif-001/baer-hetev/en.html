(ב) The parchment and ink. The rule is this: when the parchment and ink belong to the scribe and the husband pays him, he must transfer ownership of the parchment and ink to the husband; even after the fact it is invalid if he did not transfer them to him. One should not ask from the case of one thrown into a pit who commanded that a get be written for his wife, where they write and give it without a transfer of ownership. From there Ramban brought proof that one need not transfer ownership to the husband. Ramah already rejected this: it is different where he commanded to write and give, for then the scribe is his agent to deliver the get to the woman's hand, and what he gives into her hand he gives through his agency; it is then as though he transferred it to the husband. This is unlike where the scribe writes and another person is the agent to deliver it to her hand; then it is invalid. According to Ramban, even in such a case it is valid, and he holds that it is automatically acquired by the husband through the money he pays. The practical difference in this dispute is with a get, etc.
