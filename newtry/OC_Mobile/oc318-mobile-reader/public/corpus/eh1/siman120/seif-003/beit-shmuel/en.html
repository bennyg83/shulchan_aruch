(א) That this is so-and-so. Meaning that this is his name and so too his wife's name. Even if he and his wife are before us and we know that this is his wife, they must know their names, as written in Beit Yosef; so too Beit Yosef wrote. It appears that even if they know their names, they must know that this is his wife, or know that his wife's name is such, for otherwise the difficulty of the Shas remains: perhaps this is another man's wife, just as it asks there that perhaps he will give the get to another man's wife. So too here one must be concerned that perhaps this is another man's wife. Rather, one must say that they know this is his wife and these are their names. If his wife is not with him, they must know that this is his name and that he has a wife whose name is as written in the get. It implies from all the poskim that even if no other person with this name has been established here, nevertheless they must know the wife's name.
