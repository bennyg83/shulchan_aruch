The name Binyamin, etc. In Mesoret HaGet 17 it is written full, but the rest are deficient. In Sotah 36b: "rather, in the whole Torah," etc. The questioner there, who asks from fifty, also plainly assumed that it is deficient; and so did the answerer, except because it is written, etc., and he said: only here. The Rambam ruled not like him, but like Rabbi Yitzchak.
