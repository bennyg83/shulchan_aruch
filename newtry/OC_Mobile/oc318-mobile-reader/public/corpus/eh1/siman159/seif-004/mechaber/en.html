If she consummated yibbum by accident, as when she heard that her husband had died, and then he appeared; If she was fully married (by nisuin) to her husband, she does not require a get (writ of divorce) from the yavam. Rama: And she is permitted to her husband (Tur). Mechaber: If she was only betrothed (by erusin) to her husband, she requires a get from the yavam. Rama: And she is forbidden to her husband.
