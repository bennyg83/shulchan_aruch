On condition I have with so-and-so. Avnei Miluim s.k. 27 wrote: meaning he said he will show maneh he has. See Birkei Yosef: doubt — one who said on condition I show you maneh I have and same day morning in nearby city inheritance fell to him reaching his share a maneh and afterward showed this maneh from inheritance — do we say since not in hand at kiddushin and he did not know she is not betrothed; or since truthfully at that time he had maneh from inheritance though he did not know betrothed? Hastily proof from one who betrothes on condition no vows on her and vows existed and sage permitted before husband knew — law she is betrothed as below siman 95 s.k. 2 — even if husband knew first not betrothed though permitted since at his knowledge doubt perhaps no permission as Tosafot Kesubos 74; we learn if permitted before his knowledge betrothed — likewise here since at kiddushin he had maneh from inheritance though unknown betrothed. Also wrote: doubt if at kiddushin maneh lacking perutah — say certainly she did not care about perutah deficient quantity; or any maneh lacking even perutah not betrothed — apparently since for theft vow charity debt obligation though people not particular he is obligated to pay maneh to end — likewise here not betrothed — needs resolution; see siman 31 s.k. 3.
