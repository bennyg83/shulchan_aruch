Every condition must contain four elements, and these are: that it be doubled; that the affirmative precede the negative; that the condition precede the act; and that the condition be something possible to fulfill. If the condition lacks any one of them — behold the condition is void as if there were no condition at all, rather she is betrothed immediately as if he had not stipulated at all. (If he mentioned the negative before the affirmative and then repeated the negative after the affirmative — it is a valid condition as if he had mentioned the affirmative before the negative (Ran ch. Mi She'achazo).)
