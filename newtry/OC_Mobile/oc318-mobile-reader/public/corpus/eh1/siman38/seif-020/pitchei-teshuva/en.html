The condition or not. Ba'er Heitev: if passive condition etc. until if both say not fulfilled but transgressed in practice also not believed etc. Beit Shmuel s.k. 69: if both say not fulfilled but transgressed condition and did act — unclear if believed; appears not believed — we are not concerned they transgressed active condition — proof from we rule when divorcing he stipulates passive condition permitted to remarry — we are not concerned she transgressed as siman 143; see s.k. 70; see Shaarei HaMelekh chapter 7 law 10 greatly wondered — from Ran chapter HaOmer (as Rav Tomer Yesharim emended) and Rashba chiddushim clearly distinction kiddushin vs divorce — in kiddushin she is believed to say voided by hand even contradicting him — all the more both admit; also clear Rama wrote passive condition each believed if no contradictor — implies if contradict in passive she can contradict; Beit Shmuel s.k. 70 wondered on him from Ran's words appears in all such cases she cannot contradict — we are not concerned transgressed in practice; truly this is not so in Ran — only in divorce; in kiddushin she is believed; what Beit Shmuel brought proof Choshen Mishpat siman 241 — receiver must bring proof in passive condition — no proof; only in kiddushin she is believed because miggo she could say he divorced me; in gift no miggo therefore not believed receiver brings proof. Per my opinion can defend Beit Shmuel: Shaarei HaMelekh reason in kiddushin she is believed to say voided by hand — not extract from fellow without proof therefore since he comes to forbid her to world she is believed; if so in divorce he should be believed to say voided by hand since she extracts from husband's domain — reason only in kiddushin miggo she could say he divorced me; see there at length; if so we rule also in gerashatani she is not believed stringently as Rama siman 17 hagahah Beit Shmuel s.k. 4 — no miggo; therefore kiddushin like divorce. All per Shaarei HaMelekh's reason distinguishing kiddushin and divorce; but truly cannot say this at all per Ran — therefore in kiddushin she is believed because miggo gerashatani; Ran's view Kesubos chapter 2 woman who says he divorced me not believed to remarry (and this is first view siman 17 hagahah) — no miggo gerashatani; Beit Shmuel siman 47 s.k. 9 — rather other reason to distinguish kiddushin and divorce — in gittin we are witnesses she did not transgress in practice due to stringency etc. as Rashba chiddushim Gittin 83 — see; therefore Beit Shmuel's words require study; see Beit Meir on this.
