One who betrothes on condition and retracts after several days and nullifies the condition — even though he nullified it between himself and herself not before witnesses, the condition is voided and behold she is betrothed plainly. Likewise if the condition was from the woman and she nullified it between herself and him the condition is voided. Therefore one who betrothes on condition and has relations plainly or marries plainly — behold she requires a get even though the condition was not fulfilled, lest he nullified the condition when he had relations or when he married. {Rama: And if another betrothed her she requires a get from both (his view).}
