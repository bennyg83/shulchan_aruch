And had relations plainly etc. Rambam wrote: therefore one who betrothes on condition and afterward brought her in plainly or had relations plainly needs a get. Beit Yosef challenged siman 39: Tur wrote if he betrothed and had relations plainly etc. and did not mention entry there; Derishah added challenge — if entry helps all the more relations for entry is the bringing in; if without entry relations are promiscuous relations and we stand on reason a person does not make his relations promiscuous — from this Beit Yosef greatly strained to reconcile. Per my opinion no challenge: in Beit Shmuel in name of Rif it is clear — where "brought her in" means after time; where "and had relations" means immediately. Reason: plain entry is not immediately but prepares between kiddushin and chuppah as mishnah chapter Na'arah — thirty days for virgin; Rif ruled even for relations immediately after kiddushin she needs get for presumably he waived condition since a person does not make promiscuous relations; if so for entry immediately we do not say he waived — no such reason applies except for entry after time. Tur properly wrote here in name of Rambam "brought her in afterward plainly" meaning after time as plain entry; "or had relations plainly" means immediately as siman 39; and relations alone without entry would be promiscuous — this is like chuppah entry; siman 39 relations alone because kiddushin adjacent to relations — nevertheless he waived condition; all the more entry after time even without relations as Ran wrote.
