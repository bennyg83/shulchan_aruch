One who betrothes etc. From what is written Kesubos 73a — do not say etc. he waived for her and this is in the heart; and sugya conclusion there because of the reason; and Tosafot there s.v. do not say and Rabbenu Yitzchak explained etc. and Tosafot there 73a part 1 s.v. words of all etc. we rule like Rav; and therefore etc. and even though there 73b Rabbah said dispute etc. and Rif explained this is after time but not immediately. Rif wrote we do not rule like him for we rule like Ula who said one who betrothes etc. and had relations — even not immediately; and as written Niddah 22a they counted and concluded etc. and Tosafot there. Summary: there 73a Abaye said etc. and Rashi explained what is written — since he brought her in plainly this is also chuppah and relations; but per the first view the reason is since he did not mention at time of entry the condition he waived entirely and she even has ketubah; but the reason is etc. he did not waive but the relations are for full kiddushin for a person does not etc. but ketubah she does not have — this is the distinction between first view because of waiver and conclusion because of "a person does not" etc. But Tosafot there s.v. no explained in name of R' Yitzchak — first view is because of waiver and if so even entry to chuppah alone; except because of "a person does not" and specifically in relations but ketubah even per first view she does not have for the mishnah is "he brought her in" etc. Tosafot there and likewise Rambam explained and as below; and "had relations plainly or brought in" etc. — conclusion is not like Abaye but because of waiver as Ran wrote and Tosafot 73a part 1 s.v. words etc. and Tosafot s.v. the above no. And if so how can we say etc. But Rambam did not want this and also holds chuppah does not acquire as explained siman 26; therefore waiver always helps. But Rashba disputes and Maggid Mishneh and Ran wrote waiver does not help except in monetary condition — one can say as if I received; and he explained Abaye's words per Rashi's explanation — even per first view it discusses relations; and do not challenge Rav's reason etc. since he did not mention waiver and relations for full kiddushin and she even has ketubah etc. and even per first view because of relations etc. for relations are other kiddushin and he disputes Rambam in three matters: (1) waiver does not help; (2) entry does not help; (3) even relations must be before witnesses since for kiddushin — likewise Rosh there specifically before witnesses but for Rambam not needed; and see Maggid Mishneh. Ran challenged what Rashi wrote s.v. except etc. but etc. — behold without this she has no ketubah since he has relations for kiddushin and we rule first chapter Kiddushin relations make betrothal and a betrothed woman has no ketubah; and even if he had no intent on the condition at all — and explain what is written "a person does not" — meaning he waived the condition and she is betrothed from the first moment but regarding money the condition applies etc. and from here Rambam derived he can waive — and one can say is not so as explained; and what is written conclusion is because of waiver — that disputes Abaye and as Tosafot s.v. words above; and likewise he himself explained the sugya there Abaye and Rava dispute thus and his challenge initially is not — for even though relations make betrothal nevertheless second relations after kiddushin make marriage and likewise he himself first chapter Kiddushin; and as written siman 33 s.k. 8 in hagahah and siman 55 s.k. 1; and further the entire sugya of the Talmud that a betrothed woman has ketubah as in several places — except Gemara challenges Bava Metzia 17b "likewise in what he wrote her" etc. and all the more here since she entered first for marriage; but Rambam's words are compelled as explained.
Between themselves. Not like Ra'avad who wrote before witnesses — for even plainly we say he waived in his heart.
And if he betrothed her etc. As written — this was the incident etc. and see siman 31 s.k. 9.
