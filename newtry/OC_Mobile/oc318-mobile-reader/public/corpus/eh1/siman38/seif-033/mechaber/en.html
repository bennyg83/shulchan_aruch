On condition that I am a town dweller and found both town and city dweller; on condition that I am a perfumer and found perfumer and tanner (meaning a craftsman who works hides); on condition that my name is Joseph and found his name Joseph and Simeon — behold she is betrothed. But if he said to her on condition that my name is only Joseph and found Joseph and Simeon; that I am only a town dweller and found town and city dweller; that I am only a perfumer and found perfumer and tanner — she is not betrothed.
