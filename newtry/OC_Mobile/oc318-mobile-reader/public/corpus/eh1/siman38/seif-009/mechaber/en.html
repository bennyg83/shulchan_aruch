On condition that my father not protest — if he heard and protested she is not betrothed. If he did not protest or died — behold she is betrothed (see nearby seif 10). If the son died and afterward the father heard — we instruct the father to say "I do not consent" so that there be no kiddushin and she not fall before the yavam. Some say one who says on condition that he consent — his law is as one who says on condition that he not protest. Some say his law is as one who says on condition that he be silent.
