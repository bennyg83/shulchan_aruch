Behold she is betrothed. For Rambam certainly betrothed — condition was if he not protest when he hears; for other poskim certainly betrothed when he died and did not protest — all his days doubt lest he still protest.
We instruct the father. For Rambam condition applies on condition he not protest at time of hearing — if he did not protest while engaged in that matter cannot protest — therefore properly say instruct him to protest even after hearing while engaged in that matter; if stipulated on condition he be silent — once silent cannot protest even if engaged in matter; for other poskim whenever he protests helps — therefore properly what is written instruct him to protest; but on condition he be silent cannot say instruct him for once heard and did not protest cannot protest; one can say instruct before he hears tell him if he hears he should protest — mishnah language does not imply thus; what is written son died and father afterward heard — likewise per Rambam if already heard cannot protest; for other poskim can protest — what is written seif 11 son died instruct father even if heard before death instruct father protest — per other poskim wrote thus.
And some say on condition etc. Beit HaEfod challenged from sugya chapter HaMedir Kesubos 73 — they dispute whether on condition he consent means on condition he be silent or say yes — we do not find one who holds on condition he not protest; possibly per poskim we do not hold like Rabbah mistaken woman needs no get — can say baraita there per questioner they dispute mistaken not like Rabbah who establishes they dispute on condition he consent silent or say yes — per conclusion no proof from there.
And some say. We rule stringently — one who says on condition he consent be stringent per all: on condition say yes, on condition not protest, on condition be silent — Ri'az.
