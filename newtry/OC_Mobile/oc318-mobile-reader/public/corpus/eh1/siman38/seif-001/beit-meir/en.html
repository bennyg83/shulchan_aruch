One who betrothes on condition — see Gittin 84: perhaps marry today divorce tomorrow and establish condition etc. — she stands to be divorced — Rashba wrote; question: perhaps betrothed on condition divorce at set time — now she stands to herself? Answer: even when he divorces still challenge "is she betrothed to this one" — difficult. Tosafot s.v. here wrote but halakhah does not appear from his words — we learn if betrothed on condition divorce on that day kiddushin fulfilled if condition fulfilled if not void. Per my opinion if worthy to consider his ruling: since we rule man divorces only willingly — like condition on condition you have no onah — for all considered stipulating against Torah condition void act stands; here since she accepts kiddushin she is his wife choice his to divorce as written "writes from his will" — stipulating on condition he divorce uproots stipulating against Torah etc. See Yoreh De'ah siman 61 seif 29; see Ramban Bava Batra 126 s.v. behold she is betrothed — per his reason no half marriage — likewise here and requires study.
