She is not betrothed. Even if he was compelled and did not fulfill the condition — it is not kiddushin (Ran in name of Yerushalmi ch. HaOmer). But at the end of ch. Kol HaGet Ran wrote that Ba'al HaItur asked before the head of the bride whether there is a claim of compulsion in kiddushin and it was not resolved; and likewise the Rav Maggid brought in ch. 9 Laws of Divorce. And Ralbach siman 120 raised that there is no claim of compulsion in kiddushin; and even for one who wishes to be stringent — if the groom stipulated at the time of kiddushin that he cannot claim any claim of compulsion in the world, and on this condition she accepted the kiddushin — there is no compulsion in kiddushin. See Knesset HaGedolah folio 69a and see Be'er Heitev.
