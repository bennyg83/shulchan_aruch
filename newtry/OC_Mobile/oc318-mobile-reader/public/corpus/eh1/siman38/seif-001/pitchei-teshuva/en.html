On condition. Ba'er Heitev on what Kenesset HaGedolah wondered. Also in Sefer Shaarei HaMelekh chapter 8 Laws of Divorce law 9 wondered at length — see there. See Beit Meir on condition he divorce her at known time — wrote from Rashba's chiddushim Gittin 84 it is clear this is like any condition: if fulfilled kiddushin stand, if not void. But if I were worthy to consider his ruling it would appear: since we rule a man divorces only willingly he is like condition on condition you have no onah upon me — for all this is stipulating against Torah, condition void act stands; likewise here since she accepts kiddushin she is his wife and choice is his to divorce or not as written "and he writes for her from his will" — if he stipulates on condition he divorce her he uproots and stipulates against Torah etc. — see there.
