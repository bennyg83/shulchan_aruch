Food and clothing etc. — this is not stipulating against Torah since it is monetary and given for waiver; unlike onah which is bodily pain not subject to waiver. Seif 4 on condition she have relations with one forbidden to her — Tur wrote on condition she have relations with my father etc.; Perishah wrote the reason is prohibition of incest; if so likewise with any other forbidden by incest; Tur cited father because in Gemara chapter HaMegaresh regarding get (it says there "to my father") and there prohibition is specifically to father but to another she is permitted after the get; therefore here too he cited father. Maharshal disputed and said here too specifically father — to another there is permission after death unlike father forbidden forever. Truth is as Perishah — in chapter HaMegaresh regarding one who gives a get and retains — we need to ask there besides husband's sister what if sister dies and he sees her — we do not resolve; perhaps per this reasoning death is required; if so how say plainly here it is possible through perhaps he dies — Perishah's words are essential.
