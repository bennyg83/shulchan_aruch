If he stipulated at the time of kiddushin that she will not have food and clothing — his condition stands and he is not obligated for them. But if he stipulated that he not be obligated in onah — his condition is void and he is obligated. {Rama: For anyone who stipulates against what is written in the Torah and it is not monetary — his condition is void (his own view). Some say that everything the Sages ordained is like what is written in the Torah (Hagahot Maimoniyot beginning of HaKotev).}
