Maneh. Meaning when we do not see a maneh in his hand; but if we see a maneh in his hand we are witnesses that he has it — for whatever is in his hand has hazakah it is his; likewise Ran. What Beit Yosef wrote that Tur disputes Ran — Bach was not precise see there. But in responsum Beit Yosef siman 11 he proved Tur disputes Ran even if the maneh is in his hand; see Be'er Heitev. If witnesses testify it was known to them he never had a maneh — she is not betrothed. If both admit he does not have it — it does not help (Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel).
In my hand. Even if he and she also say he has nothing with him — it is safek (R' Yehuda of Orleans in name of RaMAh). If there are no witnesses that he has with him and so-and-so said he has with him — it is only safek. If she also says he has with him — it is as if both say the condition was fulfilled (Beit Shmuel).
