Comes from them stringency — if so one should not obligate money due to the condition whenever the four elements are absent, for in monetary matters we follow leniency not to extract from the possessor.
Everything in one matter — except such as if this dinar is mine I betroth you with it, and if not mine you are not betrothed; this is unlike sons of Gad condition where the condition is they go to the land of Israel and the act is he gave them land of Gilad as inheritance — not one matter. "If not mine" means only when it is in his hand; otherwise it is theft and she is not betrothed.
That she eat pork — this is not stipulating against what is written in the Torah, for he did not tell her she suffices without doing so — if so she will not eat and not be betrothed; unlike nearby seif 5 where he certainly uproots since he gave kiddushin and she is his wife to be obligated — if he stipulates he not be obligated that is the essence.
