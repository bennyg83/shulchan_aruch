Some say these four elements are not required in a condition except where stringency results from them, but not where leniency results. {Rama: Some say further that even though all four elements are present, we also require that the condition refer to one matter and the act to another; but if everything is one matter it is not a condition (Rosh and Tur), and one should be concerned for his words stringently. If he stipulated with her that she eat pork or any other Torah prohibition — it is called possible to fulfill (Tur). But if he stipulated with her that she have relations with one forbidden to her — it is called impossible to fulfill, for another would not consent to her afterward (ibid.). But if he stipulated with him that so-and-so give him his straw or marry his daughter to his son — it is called possible to fulfill, for she can give so-and-so much money until he consents to the matter (also ibid.).
