Not required etc. Reason because we do not hold like R' Chiya who said doubling required except in gittin and kiddushin we are concerned stringently; but halakhah is like Sages; however what is written not required all four elements — we do not find thus for from Rambam chapter 3 law 7 appears doubling and affirmative before negative not required; though I wrote nearby must conclude with negative because at conclusion of words a person seizes — nevertheless in monetary matters we follow assessment; but condition before act and possible through agent required — so Maggid chapter 6 law 1 and Ran in Mi She'achazo and chapter HaOmer — reason even if we do not derive from sons of Gad what he considers we derive; likewise from Rosh chapter Yesh Nochalin and teshuvah kelal 1 all pilpul on doubling; other conditions required — proven there he challenged on condition you return etrog — condition and act one matter and answered Rabbah per his view condition and act one matter is condition; challenged did not double — answered in name of Rashba in monetary matters doubling not required — learn other conditions required; Tur, Mechaber, and Shach wrote four elements not required — whence?
Condition in one matter etc. Tosafot Kiddushin 6 and chapter Mi She'achazo 75 and Ran there and chapter Yesh Nochalin wrote not condition and act one matter except where condition contradicts act such as stipulated in get if you return paper — when will get take effect when paper returned then not a get; but in Rosh chapter Yesh Nochalin proven he does not hold thus — challenged on condition you return etrog; in on condition does not contradict — as Tosafot in those places; also chapter Mi She'achazo wrote in on condition requires condition and act different matters — Ri'az ruled like Rosh on condition return paper is condition and act one matter; therefore Tur in this siman in kiddushin condition when betrothing with dinar and stipulates on condition the dinar is mine — condition and act one matter and she is betrothed even if condition not fulfilled though not contradictory; why ruled siman 143 on condition return get paper condition and act one matter and condition must be fulfilled; also challenged Orach Chaim siman 258 on condition return etrog; also Rosh — all one matter; if even such case is condition and act one matter why said in condition return kiddushin like exchange siman 139 — why like exchange since need not return if all one matter; Derishah and Choshen Mishpat wrote Tur holds only when condition contradicts act; condition if dinar mine means betrothed when you return dinar — if dinar mine contradicts act; but if said on condition you return — not condition and act one matter for not contradictory — we hold on condition as from now; nevertheless not kiddushin because gift on condition to return; but if betrothing with document then on condition return paper helps as siman 32; when on condition is condition for not contradictory; Choshen Mishpat wrote nevertheless must explain why ruled here in kiddushin stringency if condition and act one matter — in document kiddushin; in money kiddushin already ruled even on condition does not help like exchange; if said if you return paper you shall be betrothed — he gave her nothing — why rule here stringently; per Derishah's explanation condition and act one matter is if condition; then need not fulfill condition and act stands — not like exchange since need not fulfill condition; hagahah words fine; even money kiddushin on condition if you return I betroth you — stringent per first view even such case condition stands and gift on condition to return not kiddushin; some say these are kiddushin; rule: if betrothing with document and said on condition return paper — condition does not contradict act as Tosafot — she is betrothed and condition fulfilled; per Rosh views need not fulfill since all one matter; if said return paper — condition contradicts act — condition and act one matter — condition void per these poskim — she is certainly betrothed; per other poskim and Rambam condition stands; if betrothing with money on condition return money — condition does not contradict — per Tosafot must fulfill condition and gift on condition to return kiddushin void; per Rosh condition and act one matter need not fulfill — kiddushin stand; if said behold betrothed if you return money — per Tosafot and Rosh all one matter condition void kiddushin stand; per Rambam condition stands gift on condition to return kiddushin void — be concerned stringently in all these disputes.
That she eat pork. This condition is in her power to eat — not stipulating against Torah for if she wishes she will not eat and kiddushin not kiddushin; if stipulated on condition she have relations with so-and-so — not in her power for so-and-so will not listen.
To forbid her. Likewise Tur; Derishah wrote likewise for permitted to her he will not listen for once condition fulfilled she becomes forbidden; Bach siman 101 wrote if stipulated on permitted to her condition can be fulfilled after death and kiddushin retroactive — see Tosafot Gittin 83; if stipulated on permitted to her cannot say nevertheless prohibition to have relations with unmarried woman — Tosafot 84; if stipulated on permitted to her can be fulfilled by not telling him condition — perhaps he will know.
