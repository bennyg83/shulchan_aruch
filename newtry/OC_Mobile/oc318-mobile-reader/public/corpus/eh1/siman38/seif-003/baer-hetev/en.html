From now. Every acquisition is as from now, and anything with a time limit is as from now — Rashba and Beit Yosef brought in Choshen Mishpat siman 56.
On condition. But one who says on condition such-and-such is not as one who says on condition — Beit Yosef in Choshen Mishpat siman 207.
Before the act. Likewise condition in one matter and act in another — also not required when he says from now or on condition; however affirmative before negative is always required (Beit Shmuel).
Complete. This law — since it is in Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 6 Laws of Marriage — there he did not decide the matter but wrote in language that it may help without doubling the condition — see there. Only in such-and-such a case he wrote explicitly, and in Choshen Mishpat siman 241 he wrote responsum of Rosh that in such a case it is an elevated condition — see there; and see Be'er Heitev and Knesset HaGedolah folio 69b.
