Need not double condition. Since from now the act takes effect it takes effect in the condition he stipulates; but condition "if" — since condition comes to void the act if it will not take effect from now — not a condition unless doubled — so Ra'avad; see Rosh and Ran — appears these poskim hold from now does not need doubling; likewise condition in one matter and act in another also not needed when said from now or on condition; however affirmative before negative — if required see nearby in name of Ran must always conclude with negative.
Only said plainly he stipulates like sons of Gad etc. Sema and Hagahot Maimoniyot did not require this law but wrote it may help; however in Rosh's teshuvah Tur Choshen Mishpat siman 241 ruled explicitly; therefore Rav Rama ruled thus and likewise Choshen Mishpat; if act preceded what he said like sons of Gad condition does not help — Choshen Mishpat.
