Anyone who says "from now" or "on condition" need not double his condition and need not place the condition before the act, but he must stipulate something possible to fulfill. Some dispute and say that even one who says "from now" or "on condition" must meet all four elements of a condition. {Rama: Some say that even if he did not express all the laws of conditions but simply said he makes a condition like the condition of the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben — behold it is a complete condition (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 6).}
