He said it is in my hand. Meaning he has at kiddushin time not at statement time — since said I have implies at that moment as Ri'az one who says on condition I show you I have — intent at kiddushin time; but if said on condition I show you and did not say I have — even if profited afterward helps unlike if said on condition I have; therefore all conditions I have require he had at kiddushin time; from this if said on condition I have in so-and-so's hand — so-and-so's admission does not help — though admission obligates from now cannot know if had at admission time.
