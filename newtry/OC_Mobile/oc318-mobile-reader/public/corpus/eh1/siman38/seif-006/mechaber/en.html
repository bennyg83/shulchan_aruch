If he gave her a perutah and said to her "Be betrothed to me with this on condition that I give you a maneh" — when the condition is fulfilled the kiddushin take effect retroactively even though he did not say "from now," for anyone who says "on condition" is as one who says "from now." If she accepted kiddushin from another before the condition was fulfilled and afterward the condition was fulfilled — the second kiddushin are nothing. If he said to her on condition that he give her a maneh within thirty days — if he gave within thirty days it is kiddushin retroactively; if he did not give within thirty days she is not betrothed. If she accepted kiddushin from another within thirty days — she is betrothed and not betrothed until thirty days. When thirty days are completed, if the first did not fulfill his condition the first kiddushin lapse and the latter kiddushin are completed, and she needs no get from the first; if the first fulfilled his condition she needs no get from the second.
