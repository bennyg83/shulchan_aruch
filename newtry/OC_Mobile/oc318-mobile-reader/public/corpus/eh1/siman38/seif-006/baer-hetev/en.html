Maneh. If he gave her objects more than a maneh as profit on the trouble of the sale she is doubtfully betrothed (Ralbach siman 134). And all the more if he said on condition I give you a maneh of such-and-such coin — he did not fulfill the condition if he gave objects worth the value (Maharam Alsheikh siman 32). But a groom who said to his bride on condition I give you such-and-such sum and there is estimation that he gave only to buy her jewelry and clothing and gave jewelry — his condition is fulfilled (Mahari Bei Rav siman 32). Maharam Alsheikh siman 32 and Ralbach siman 134 disputed this. If he stipulated to give her a vessel or objects and gave the monetary value of the vessel or objects — she is not betrothed (Ralbach there and Maharam Alsheikh there in name of Mahari Bei Rav; but he disputes and wrote she is betrothed). But if he gave the value of the vessel and a little more as profit on the trouble of acquisition — it is safek for all. If he gave her the maneh and she did not wish to accept and he gave by force — see Knesset HaGedolah folio 68b.
