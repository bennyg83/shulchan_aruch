One who says to a woman "Behold you are betrothed to me except for so-and-so" — meaning she is not forbidden to him but is a married woman to the whole world and to so-and-so as available — behold she is doubtfully betrothed. But if he said to her "Behold you are betrothed to me on condition that you be permitted to so-and-so" — she is betrothed and forbidden to him as to other people because he conditioned on something impossible to fulfill. {Rama: If they dispute whether the condition was fulfilled or not — some say that any condition of active fulfillment neither party is believed to say he fulfilled the condition that was upon him to fulfill; witnesses are required. But a passive condition — each is believed if the other does not contradict (Ran ch. HaOmer in name of the Acharonim). Some say that even in active cases each is believed as long as the other does not contradict (Ramban and R' Yitzchak).}
