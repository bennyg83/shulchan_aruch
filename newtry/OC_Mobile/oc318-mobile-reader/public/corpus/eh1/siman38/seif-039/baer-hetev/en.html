Or not. This law divides into four aspects: (1) if both say the condition was fulfilled; (2) if both say it was not fulfilled; (3) if they contradict each other; (4) if one says fulfilled and the other says he does not know. If the condition is active fulfillment: if both say fulfilled they are believed — lest they waive to each other; if both say not fulfilled — as kiddushin are void they are also believed; if he says fulfilled and she says she does not know — safek; if she contradicts him with certainty that it was not fulfilled — for all kiddushin are void. One who says the condition was fulfilled is as saying I betrothed you and she is forbidden to his relatives. If he said it was not fulfilled — even if she says with certainty it was fulfilled she is not believed without proof; nevertheless she is forbidden to his relatives. If the condition is passive: if both say fulfilled and did not transgress — likewise if he said fulfilled and did not transgress and she says she does not know — he is believed; if she contradicts him saying it was not fulfilled — it appears from Ramban too she cannot contradict without witnesses — we are not concerned he transgressed in practice; if both say not fulfilled but transgressed in practice — they are also not believed — we are not concerned they transgressed the condition in practice.
