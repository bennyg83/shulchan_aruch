If he said to her "Be betrothed to me with this perutah on condition that I give you a maneh" and afterward said he does not wish to fulfill his condition ever — we do not force him to fulfill it; rather his kiddushin are void and she needs no get. Nevertheless a scrupulous man will not marry her without a get lest afterward he say he wishes to fulfill the condition to harm the second. If he says he still wishes to fulfill it — they cannot force him for all twelve months per the law of time given to a virgin; from twelve months onward they write against him a rebellious wife document. If he died before fulfilling the condition — even chalitzah is not required. If she waived the condition during the betrother's lifetime — the kiddushin are completed immediately as if fulfilled. All this when he did not marry her; but if he married her she is betrothed immediately, for presumably at the time of her entering the chuppah she waived the condition and he is obligated to give her her ketubah.
