Even though they retracted within an utterance etc. — we rule in betrothal it is not within an utterance stringently as in chapter Yesh Nochalin 129 explained above siman 17; and voiding condition — the condition was for his benefit such as no blemish on her — suffices with his voiding alone; likewise if condition for her benefit suffices with her voiding alone; in both if the voider admits to voiding it suffices as explained plainly.
