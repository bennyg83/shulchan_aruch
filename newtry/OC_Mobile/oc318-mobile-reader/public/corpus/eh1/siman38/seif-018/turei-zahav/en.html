He did not have at time of kiddushin etc. So Tur in name of RaMAh: on condition I have means at time of kiddushin; on condition I show you means also for the future; even if he found an object or received a gift afterward and showed her — it is kiddushin; nevertheless we are not concerned lest he show her afterward to create doubt kiddushin. Question: why not be stringent lest he find an object? Due to this challenge Beit Yosef first strained to say this "nevertheless" is not RaMAh's words but Tur's, disputing RaMAh; and Yerucham in name of Rosh disputes RaMAh and holds even on condition I show you he must show at kiddushin time otherwise no limit — he can forever say he still has time; therefore Tur wrote this "nevertheless" — but "nevertheless" language is strained implying no dispute. I wonder: one cannot say this at all — if so why write we are not concerned lest he show — even if he certainly shows afterward there is no kiddushin since he did not have at kiddushin time. Beit Yosef wrote Tur holds like Yerucham in name of RaMAh: for show we are not concerned lest he showed — when concerned thus in condition on condition I have which is not lacking action, perhaps he has it and she does not know; but for show condition if he fulfills before witnesses he fulfills — if no witnesses we are not concerned. Tur intended this with "nevertheless"; per my opinion this explanation also fails — if so why say lest he show her afterward — rather should say perhaps he already showed; Rashal wrote: we hold certainly when he shows her it is kiddushin retroactively even from found object — only while she demands he fulfill his condition he must fulfill; if he does not have we are concerned lest he show her for then no limit except this limit while she was demanding as he promised. This explanation is also not essential — why say we are not concerned so much; even if he certainly shows afterward it does not help since he did not show at time of demand. Per my opinion: since we follow the woman's intent — they said she intended only to be shown from his own; additionally Tur in name of RaMAh: even if he showed before witnesses it does not help unless she intended specifically to be shown; all the more we say she certainly intended not to be bound all her days lest he still show her — rather clear that RaMAh's "show" language also means for the future; not that if he shows found object when he has nothing at that hour she is betrothed retroactively — rather she intended if he has at that hour only lacking showing, when he shows she is betrothed retroactively; this certainly depends on her demand he must show within time; we are not concerned lest he come and show while she can accept kiddushin from another. But if he has nothing at kiddushin time — if he finds afterward and shows, kiddushin from that hour onward not retroactively; even though on condition is as from now — since he has nothing there is no kiddushin; only if he has and lacks only showing, since in his power to show she is retroactively betrothed — before that was doubt; but one who has nothing if he finds and shows — like saying if I find I will show you then kiddushin take effect — not retroactively; on condition or from now language he said is contradicted by saying afterward if I find object; this is not in his power but if he finds and shows she is betrothed from that hour specifically. Example in Beit Yosef siman 40 in Rashba teshuvah: not only but even if he said from now and after I divorce you it must be so — impossible from now etc.; likewise impossible from now while within that time she is not bound and can betroth another; RaMAh said we are not concerned lest he show afterward since now he has nothing — this deception language is RaMAh's own; also answers Yerucham's challenges in name of Rosh — what limit does he have — no limit needed here she is not bound at all; if she demands him plainly exempt since he has nothing; then even if afterward he finds and shows it does not help; even if she does not demand no obligation to wait since he has nothing now — only if she does not demand he finds and shows kiddushin from that hour as explained — RaMAh's view; see seif 36.
