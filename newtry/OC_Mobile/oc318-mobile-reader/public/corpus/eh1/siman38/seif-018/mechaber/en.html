On condition that I show you a maneh — she is not betrothed until he shows her his own maneh; even if he showed her a maneh he has from a business venture with others she is not betrothed. {Rama: Some say that even if his share of the profit reached a maneh she is not betrothed since they have not yet divided (Ran ch. HaOmer). If he showed her his own maneh and it is collateral to a creditor she is betrothed (R' Yehuda in name of RaMAh). If he did not have it at the time of kiddushin and earned it afterward and showed her — some say she is betrothed (Tur). But if he said to her on condition that I have for everyone in such-and-such a case — she is not betrothed (Sema). We are not concerned lest he show her afterward to create doubt kiddushin (Tur). If he said to her on condition to bring her two hundred zuz — its law is like on condition that I show you a maneh, and it must be his own (Rashba in responsum brought in Beit Yosef).}
