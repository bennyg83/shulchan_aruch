apparently betrothal of woman is one; mishnah language Kiddushin 62 "one who betrothes and said I thought kohenet" — implies only if she did not reveal intent to be kohenet or rich — if deceived even if not said at kiddushin on condition she is such not betrothed — heart matters help; but from mishnah 48 on condition I am rich — infer opposite only if said at kiddushin but heart matters do not help — contradictory inferences; initially could distinguish man betrothing vs woman — woman pleased in all cases; but Rambam chapter 8 Laws of Marriage wrote likewise she said I thought kohen etc. — teaches excluding above — man and woman equal only if they did not deceive each other — if deceived though not said at kiddushin we follow assessment; therefore mishnah on condition I am kohen — likewise without on condition all who revealed intent beforehand and found opposite not betrothed; mishnah cites on condition since end mishnah "even if said in heart" on condition; only when says on condition at kiddushin but mere intent comes and voids other heart matters etc.; if only intent deceived in general not betrothed — likewise if he deceived as Rambam; in questioner's case presumably woman would not betroth distant land man unless deceived rich and scholar as revealed intent — since deceived kiddushin void;
