I say to explain Rambam's words: per Rashi's approach it is difficult why we do not establish the first and middle sections with one reason — on condition he be silent — and the last with one reason — on condition he not protest; and Ran challenged similarly. Since the first section is stated with one reason and the middle and last with one reason, Rambam learned the Gemara's simple meaning is the first with one reason — on condition he said no; and middle and last with one reason — on condition he not protest — examine. See Acharonim; and see Kesef Mishneh who wrote likewise regarding not protest — at time of hearing if he heard and did not protest she is betrothed and he cannot protest afterward — for Rambam see there, also brought in Beit Yosef see there.
