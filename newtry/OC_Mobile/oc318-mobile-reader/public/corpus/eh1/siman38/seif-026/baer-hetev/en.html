Mishnah. Its law is like on condition I know to read Scripture — he need not know all of Torah per the view Rama brings; if so here too he need not know all mishnayot (Beit Shmuel); and Choshen Mishpat is uncertain in this.
Betrothed. Meaning the homiletical expositions he expounds in the Talmud from Torah verses; and Bach emended that Torah expositions are not required.
