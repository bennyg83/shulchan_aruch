City dweller. See above siman 45 s.k. 6 and in Be'er Heitev.
Of honey. Even though honey is more valuable (Be'er Heitev), and it is clear in the Talmud — if he said be betrothed with a silver dinar and it is found gold there is concern (Bach in Be'er Heitev, Beit Shmuel). If he said be betrothed to me with one dinar and two dinars are found she is betrothed — for within two there is one and there is no change here (Shach in Bach, Be'er Heitev, Beit Shmuel, Knesset HaGedolah).
And found wine. So should read in Be'er Heitev.
Recognized it. For if she recognized it she thought and accepted; and likewise if he gave her a cup and said behold you are betrothed with a ring — nevertheless it is proper to betroth her anew so it not lead to ruin (Rashba, Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel).
Explicitly. Meaning she answered him even if it will not be as he said — "I am willing" — and he is silent and did not say to her "no, but on condition such-and-such."
Deceived him — not kiddushin unless he said explicitly. So should read in Choshen Mishpat.
