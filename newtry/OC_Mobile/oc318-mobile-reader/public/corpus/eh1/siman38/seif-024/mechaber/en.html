On condition that I am rich and he is found poor, or poor and found rich; on condition that I am a kohen and found a levi, or levi and found a kohen; natin and found mamzer, or mamzer and found natin; town dweller and found city dweller, or city dweller and found town dweller; on condition that my house is near the bathhouse and found far, or far and found near; on condition that I have a maidservant or grown daughter or baker and does not; on condition that I do not have and has; on condition that I have a wife and children and does not; on condition that I do not have and has — in all these, or if he said "Be betrothed to me with this cup of wine" and it is found honey, or honey and found water — for example it was covered and she did not recognize it until afterward — in all these and similar cases, even if she said in her heart she intended to be betrothed to him, even if the condition is not fulfilled she is not betrothed — whether she accepted the kiddushin herself, whether she told an agent to accept them conditionally and the agent changed it, and likewise if she deceived him — even if he said in his heart he intended to betroth her even though she deceived him she is not betrothed. {Rama: Unless she said so explicitly at the time of kiddushin and the husband heard and was silent (Tur); and likewise if she deceived him (and he said explicitly even if it will not be so) his kiddushin are valid kiddushin.} But if he married her plainly and said "I thought she was a kohenet and she is a levite," or levite and kohenet, rich and poor, poor and rich — behold she is betrothed.
