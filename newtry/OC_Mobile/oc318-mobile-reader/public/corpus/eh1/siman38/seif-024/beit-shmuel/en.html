Poor and found rich. Yerushalmi explains all conditions require stringency if changes; if no stringency if changes kiddushin not void; here stringency — when rich burden on her; when not kohen does not want priesthood trouble; when house near wants convenience going; likewise all; when has grown daughter if tall may not obey; per Rambam she grows hair and goes houses gossiping; town vs city — sometimes must live in city; but if stipulated plainly town dweller though town and city dweller — not change for he is town dweller; still requires name Joseph in condition — what stringency — see Rashi mishnah 49 and Ran chapter HaOmer and Ri'az 188.
And found honey. Though honey more valuable — she needs wine; clear in Talmud if said betrothed with silver dinar found gold stringency prefers silver even if not needed for jewelry; Tosafot if not needed for jewelry not change; one can say Tosafot specifically per Rashi but per Tanna Kamma even not needed say some prefer silver — Bach; if said betrothed with one dinar two found betrothed — Shach; likewise Maharshal hagahah Choshen Mishpat; even per Ran and Ri'az brought nearby no change.
Or honey and found wine. Should read so in Ba'er Heitev; compares to one dinar found two and name only Joseph found Joseph and Shimon.
And she did not recognize. If recognized she thought and accepted; if gave cup said betrothed with ring — nevertheless proper betroth anew lest ruin — Rashba teshuvah 1086.
Whether she said to agent. Do not say showing place — teaches stringency if said to agent accept silver dinar from so-and-so and accepted gold — likewise if told agent borrow silver dinar to betroth and borrowed gold and reverse — importance betrothing with gold — see Rashi.
She said so explicitly. She answered even if not as he said I am willing and he silent did not say no but on condition such-and-such.
