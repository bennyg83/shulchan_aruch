The condition. Likewise Tur in name of RaMAh; and one may say: per this why did the Talmud reject there that we cannot explain on condition father said no — from the middle section it would be preferable to reject from the first section itself that teaches if the father consented she is betrothed and if not she is not betrothed — behold even if he did not consent she is doubtfully betrothed lest he say yes afterward. One may say in such a case he says she is certainly not betrothed and forever she is in doubt until the father dies.
