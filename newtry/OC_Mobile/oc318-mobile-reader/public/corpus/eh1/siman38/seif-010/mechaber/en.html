If he said on condition that he say yes — even though he said at first "I do not consent," as long as he reconsidered and said yes the condition is fulfilled; therefore there is always doubt until the father dies. One who says on condition that he be silent — if he was silent at the time of hearing she is betrothed even if he later protested. {Rama: However, if he was not in his house when he heard and when he came home he protested — it is a valid protest, for his initial silence was because he was not in his house (so appears from Rivash simanim 14 and 153).} If he protested at the time of hearing, even if he later reconsidered she is not betrothed. If the father died before hearing — she is betrothed. One who says on condition that he not protest — as long as he protests the condition is cancelled even if he consented at the time of hearing. {Rama: Some say that if he said yes once he can never protest again (Tur).}
