One who went overseas and betrothed on condition "if I come from here within twelve months you shall be betrothed from now," and before he left they both agreed to extend the time, and twelve months passed and he did not come but came before the final deadline — there is kiddushin, for any act done on condition can cancel the condition (or add or subtract from it) (Rosh and Rabbeinu Yeruham), and even if the money was spent it is kiddushin retroactively when the second condition is fulfilled.
