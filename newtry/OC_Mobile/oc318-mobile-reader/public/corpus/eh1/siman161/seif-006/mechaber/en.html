If the older [brother] did not want to do yibbum preferring halitza, and the younger [brother] wants to do yibbum: sexual relations with the younger is preferable. But, if neither of them wishes to do yibbum, rather, halitza, the halitza of the elder is preferred. {Rama: see below paragraph 165 - that in our times halitza is given priority. If she is not desirous of yibbum, only halitza, then the halitza of the elder is preferred (Trumat HaDeshen paragraph 220).}
