(ה) But when they themselves, etc. For "your strength will be spent in vain" does not apply when they themselves brought in the dowry. Now the simple custom for orphans who have inherited money is to write explicitly that because of lack and quarrel it shall stand according to the enactments of Shum as though her father married her off during his lifetime. Chelkat Mechokek wrote that even if they forgot to write this, or the conditions are not before us, we follow the enactments of Shum, since they are accustomed to write it and it is as though they wrote it. The enactments of Shum mean that it returns in the second year, for the first year is the enactment of Rabbenu Tam. See Responsa Mishnat Binyamin, siman 13; Responsa Shevut Yaakov, siman 24; and Darkei Moshe siman 55.
