A widow who has to divide the estate with the heirs of her late husband according to the guidelines of the takana (ordinance) of Tulitula (probably Toledo, Spain. Part of the ordinance limits a ketubah to no more than half of the late husband's estate, see Tur and also Chelkat Mechokek) we assess the value of the Sabbath clothing she is wearing, but not her weekday clothing. Note: The conditions of the remainder ordinance (another part of the takana above) apply only if she dies in the lifetime of her husband ; but if she dies while awaiting levirate marriage, the yavam (living brother) is not restricted by the remainder ordinance (Tur).
