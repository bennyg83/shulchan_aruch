From these. Meaning: regarding these nations before they converted, the child is like her — as is the law for all nations that the child is like her.
For kehunah. As with every Kutit who cohabits with an Israelite daughter, as below siman 9; we derive in Gemara Yevamot 45 from a fortiori of widow with Kohen Gadol. But Rif is uncertain whether they are blemished for kehunah; Rambam ruled plainly they are fit for kehunah — therefore bedi'eved we do not remove if she married a kohen. Later authorities.
