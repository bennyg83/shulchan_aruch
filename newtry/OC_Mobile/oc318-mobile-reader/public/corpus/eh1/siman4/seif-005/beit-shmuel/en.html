Rather, the child is disqualified. The Rif is uncertain whether such children are disqualified for kehunah. The Rambam writes that they are valid, while the Rosh holds that they are disqualified for kehunah by an a fortiori argument: if the son of a Kohen Gadol who has relations with a widow is disqualified, although that prohibition does not apply equally to all, then certainly the child of one of these men who, before conversion, had relations with a Jewish woman is disqualified for kehunah. As practical halachah, it appears that b'dieved we do not require her to leave if she married a kohen, since according to the Rambam she is valid, and according to the Rif and Ramban as well we do not remove her.
