But only blemished. Rif is uncertain whether they are blemished for kehunah; Rambam wrote they are fit; Rosh wrote they are blemished for kehunah from a fortiori — Kohen Gadol who cohabited with a widow blemishes his son though the prohibition is not equal in every respect; if one of these before conversion cohabited with an Israelite daughter the child is blemished for kehunah. For practical halakhah it appears bedi'eved we do not remove if she married a kohen — for Rambam fit, and likewise for Rif and Ramban we do not remove. See responsum Maharshal siman 17; see siman 67.
