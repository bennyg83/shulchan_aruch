Mishnah Kiddushin 66b.
Conclusion of Gemara Yevamot 45b.
Tur, per his father the Rosh's rulings there, that there is no doubt he is disqualified for kehunah, for the unstated Talmud and all amoraim agree to this.
And explains that if she is a disqualified daughter forbidden to a kohen and married a kohen, they remove her from him and the child is a chalal.
