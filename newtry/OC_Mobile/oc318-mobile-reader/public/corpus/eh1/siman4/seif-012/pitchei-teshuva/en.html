For we say she waited — Baer Heitev; and see Birkei Yosef letter 7 who brought from Maharshal in Yesh Nohalin chapter HaAreil siman 29 extensive analysis to establish halakhah like Rabbah Tosefta, and also brought in name of Maharil — see there (see responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 99 who copied Maharil's language there and what Kenesset HaGedolah wondered on him — he z"l wrote to explain his words and remove Kenesset HaGedolah's wonder). Rabbi Birkei Yosef letter 8 further wrote what Gan HaMelekh siman 130 wrote on woman whose husband went abroad and she gave birth immediately after his departure and after several months (seven or nine) gave birth again — we attribute to say from the husband and validate him; for though woman does not conceive and conceive again, minority exists who conceive; second fetus one cannot rely on; for all views they do not return and conceive — fetus exists and no minority here; therefore child mamzer; also Yad Aharon wondered on this — see there. Letter 9 there wrote: however woman who immersed after Passover and afterward husband went abroad and she gave birth on Yom Kippur and again on Shabbat HaGadol — hastily it appears room to validate child — attribute that after Passover when she immersed she conceived twins and one born at seven months and second she waited until twelve months; proof we say she waited in such case from sugya Niddah 27; see Tosafot s.v. ella; see Rashba and Ran in Chiddushei Niddah 25 — matter needs resolution — see there (see responsum Chatam Sofer siman 5); see responsum Noda BiYehudah tinyana siman 80 on woman who bore twins at nine full months whose husband cohabited and traveled and at nine months bore twins — therefore husband suspects adultery because he found in a derush that twins cannot be conceived from one act; he wrote: this husband bears his sin and suspects the fit — the preacher's words are vanity and against many Gemara places; rather opposite — impossible twins from two acts that both are viable — see there.
