A yevamah who did not wait three months after her husband died and was taken in levirate marriage and gave birth — if the child might be a nine-month baby from the first husband or a seven-month baby from the second — the child is fit. But if afterward the yavam returned and had relations with her, she conceived, and gave birth — the child is a doubtful mamzer and forbidden to a mamzeret and to an Israelite daughter.
