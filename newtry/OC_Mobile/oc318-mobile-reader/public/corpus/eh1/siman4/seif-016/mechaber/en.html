A woman whose husband went abroad and remarried while her husband was still alive — the child from the second husband is a full mamzer and is permitted to a mamzeret. If the first returned and had relations with her before the second divorced her and she gave birth — he is a rabbinic mamzer and is forbidden to a full mamzeret but permitted to a rabbinic mamzeret. (But if she committed adultery and afterward her husband had relations with her — the child is not a mamzer (Mordekhai end of HaChalitz).)
