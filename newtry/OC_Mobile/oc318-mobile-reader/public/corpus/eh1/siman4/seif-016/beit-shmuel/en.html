Reason: fine — they fined her when actions still stand as Rashi chapter HaIsha 87 and Tosafot chapter HaChalitz and Rosh — therefore before second divorced actions still stand — child mamzer from first; after second's divorce actions nullified — not fined; if first divorced and she bore from second — all agree mamzer since fine still applies as Tosafot chapter HaIsha and Tur siman 17; Tosafot there in name R' Yehuda from second mamzer even after first's divorce like from first mamzer — meaning like from first mamzer before second's get because of fine; same with second even after first's divorce actions not nullified; if first and second divorced and zenut with second — plain no mamzer from second; perforce Tosafot's proof if born from first before get — else whence Tosafot plain more mamzer after get; Perishah and Choshen Mishpat 34 on this understood from Tosafot from first mamzer even after second's divorce; again saw in Chiddushei Lechem Avirim explains Tosafot as I do; so responsum Maharal Bach siman 42; Choshen Mishpat brought proof from Tosafot chapter HaChalitz 34 — Tosafot there aliba diR' Meir if zenut under husband and bore from husband child mamzer — per him plain if married one divorced and bore afterward mamzer; we do not hold like R' Meir and child not mamzer — all depends whether actions still stand; if second divorced and husband cohabited — child not mamzer; Darkei Moshe in name responsum Az: woman married then presumption divorcee with only voice and afterward bore and matter came out permissibly.
