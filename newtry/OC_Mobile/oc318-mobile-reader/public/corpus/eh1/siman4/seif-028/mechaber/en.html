If people gossiped about her that she fornicated with her fiancé and with other men — even though the fiancé had relations with her in her father-in-law's house — the child is a doubtful mamzer, for just as she made herself available to the fiancé, so she made herself available to others. If she was examined and said she had relations with the fiancé — the child is fit. (And likewise if it is known the fiancé had relations with her and no rumor emerged that she fornicated with others.) (And likewise is implied in chapter Almannah leKohen Gadol, as Ran brought at end of chapter 4 of Ketubot and Meiri in chapter 15 of 5 Megillot.)
