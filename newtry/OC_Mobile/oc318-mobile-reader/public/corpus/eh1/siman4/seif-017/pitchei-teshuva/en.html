The months cause. See Beit Meir here and responsum at end of his book siman 10 who poured battery of difficulties on Maharam Minz and Rama z"l; brought there also Mahari"t part 2 siman 15 decided not like Mahari Minz; also printed responsum gaon Rabbi David Tevel z"l of Lissa and gaon Maharam Barbi z"l who answered to strengthen Rama's ruling and he z"l returned and questioned their words and concludes this matter greatly doubtful in practice , see there; see responsum Noda BiYehudah siman 22 (brought below siman 13 s.k. 18) plain he rules like Mahari Minz and Rama z"l , see there. (Also responsum Chatam Sofer siman 6 discusses exactly such case , woman married 15 Sivan and cohabited again end Sivan and bore daughter start Kislev and people gossip perhaps cohabited before marriage but both admit they did not touch before and therefore cohabited after and husband claims mistake and wants to divorce , asked whether we say in this months cause per Rama's ruling; he expanded and concludes: emerges from all above that not only if cohabited again end Sivan and bore start Kislev we attribute to fitness but even only first cohabitation in Sivan , nevertheless well wrote in responsum Noda BiYehudah siman 22 brought siman 13 there that nowadays cannot rely on this reasoning (woman does not conceive from one act) and further minority exists and cannot say therefore two majorities to be stringent , majority women do not conceive from one act and majority women who bear at seven do not bear at five months , nevertheless presumption of fitness and righteousness from majority , majority Israelites are fit and presumption here found presumption of body that now she cohabited and worthy are these geonim to rely on them , woman and daughter both fit; nevertheless if such matter of Torah prohibition immediately before us I would require further waiting and moderation before final ruling , see there). There siman 7 regarding kohen who betrothed maiden from afar and she came to his place and after time married and after two months bore viable son who lived thirty days and both admit child from him for he said cohabited 28 Nisan and she bore thirtieth of Kislev which is first of Tevet and there are challengers saying they only came together 4 Iyar and per their words he is eight months every way even if months cause and bear truncated and cannot live thirty unless she waited and we do not say she waited except to uphold woman's fitness lest say she prostituted , see Rivash siman 246 and Maharik siman 103; but here we say she made herself available before betrothed and since she said from him and per my view she lies , as not examined and difficult to permit kohen for though if married not remove even majority promiscuous nevertheless needs say cohabited with fit and here said nothing in speech from him presumed , see that responsum siman 29 brought briefly below siman 6 s.k. 20. All this per challengers but they only challenge not full denial for he is trusted on himself to say cohabited end Nisan which is nine months per one who says months cause (for per one who says not months cause even bearing truncated nevertheless here need complete three missing months and then not enter ninth month even one day); though very difficult for me to enter this permission for kohen for Mahari"t part 2 siman 15 and Etz Avraham siman 4 and Beit Meir in his responsa siman 11 all question Mahari Minz's ruling , his teacher too disputants and lenient; there Beit Meir Tevel and Maharam Barbi disputed him and wrote he made practical halakhah and who will compel against these geonim and Rama's ruling , this language very difficult in my view though truly many geonim lenient say months cause even bearing at nine and as Beit Shmuel here in name Tosafot and Agudah and siman 156 s.k. 5 in name Ramban , nevertheless Rama's ruling certainly not so , only bearing at seven we say months cause but bearing at nine even truncated from changed natures nevertheless we do not say months cause as Rama z"l explicitly siman 156 s.k. 4 in hagahah; however months of pregnancy each thirty days, etc.; reason of distinction clear in Bach there letter 11 , doubtful); he expanded further and at end wrote general rule I conclude as Rivash above concluded: lenient has pillar to rely and stringent examine , see there; see responsum Chemdat Shlomo siman 79 what he wrote on this.
