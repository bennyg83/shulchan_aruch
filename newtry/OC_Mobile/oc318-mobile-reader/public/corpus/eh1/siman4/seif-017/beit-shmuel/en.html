Forbidden even with maidservant. Gemara: that the child has no remedy with Jewish woman; implies permitted with maidservant; the Rambam explained no remedy at all even maidservant since from slavery side valid; seemingly difficult why forbidden with maidservant and mamzer permitted with maidservant because enactment for the child; same should permit; for Ran's reason can say not applicable to say sacred prostitute standing; for the Rambam's reason difficult; see responsum Ramah siman 125.
And his sons like him. May not marry any woman except female convert; his daughter may marry only convert; his sons mamzerim as seif 22 explained with God's help.
