Forbidden even with maidservant. Gemara: that child has no remedy with Israelite daughter — implies permitted with maidservant; Rambam explained no remedy at all even maidservant since from slavery side fit; seemingly difficult why forbidden with maidservant and mamzer permitted with maidservant because enactment for child — same should permit; for Ran's reason can say not applicable to say sacred prostitute standing; for Rambam's reason difficult; see responsum Ramah siman 125.
And his sons like him. May not marry any woman except convertess; his daughter may marry only convert; his sons mamzerim as seif 22 explained with God's help.
