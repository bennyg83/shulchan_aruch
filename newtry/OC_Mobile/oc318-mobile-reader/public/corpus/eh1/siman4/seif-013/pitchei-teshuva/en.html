And specifically when they did not see him come, etc. This law is from responsum Rambam to laws of marriage siman 25 who wrote on his case: in our case if there were witnesses of seclusion it appears all sides equal she is forbidden to her husband; for since witnesses saw ugly matter and she gave birth likewise at end of twelve months in this , certainly Rabbah Tosefta would not validate; for since there are witnesses of seclusion we do not say she waited , see there. There the question was not about the child for the child was not in the world and he need not rule on him , only on the woman if permitted to her husband; but since he wrote in this certainly Rabbah would not validate, etc. , from this clear the law Rama wrote here regarding child we are concerned for him. So too from Rama's words here regarding child we are concerned , we do not say she waited in such case , from this clear Rambam' law regarding woman forbidden to husband (however one can reject this is not so clear regarding woman there is safek to permit lest she waited and conceived from husband; if from strange man still perhaps by force , unlike child no difference whether willingly or by force , see responsum Noda BiYehudah tinyana siman 19); see below nearby s.k. 14 and siman 11 s.k. 8 to 11.
