This person who comes, etc. Regarding a yevamah who married an outsider, see siman 159.
He is disqualified. Darkei Moshe wrote he is disqualified and family not esteemed and proper to distance from them but not disqualified from prohibition force and valid even for kehunah; so responsum Maharshal siman 6 and responsum the Rosh kelal 32 and responsum the Rashba siman 1002; in Gemara chapter HaAreil 60: Torah limited as written "these"; her daughter not chalalah; she did not become zona; her daughter not disqualified for kehunah; cannot derive from widow Kohen Gadol a fortiori; refutation exists; see Tosafot there. With this resolves Yoreh De'ah siman 296: if she served within seven clean days we do not say wait three months then immerse like convert and female convert; no concern of prohibition here requiring distinction; Taz there left unresolved and Chelkat Mechokek.
