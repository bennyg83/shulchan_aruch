He is blemished. Darkei Moshe wrote he is blemished and family not esteemed and proper to distance from them but not blemished from prohibition force and fit even for kehunah — so responsum Maharshal siman 6 and responsum Rosh kelal 32 and responsum Rashba siman 1002; in Gemara chapter HaAreil 60: Torah limited as written "these" — her daughter not chalalah — she did not become zona — her daughter not blemished for kehunah; cannot derive from widow-Kohen Gadol a fortiori — refutation exists; see Tosafot there. With this resolves Yoreh De'ah siman 296: if she served within seven clean days we do not say wait three months then immerse like convert and convertess — no concern of prohibition here requiring distinction; Taz there left unresolved and Chelkat Mechokek.
