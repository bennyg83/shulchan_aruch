In our day all nations have become intermingled; therefore an Ammonite, Moabite, and Edomite who converted are permitted to enter the congregation immediately, for whomever separates himself, we separate him from the majority, and we presume he is from the majority of nations that are permitted immediately. According to the Rambam, the same applies to an Egyptian; according to the Rosh, an Egyptian remains in his prohibited status.
