Mishnah 84 of tractate Yadayim, brought in Berakhot 28a.
There at the end of chapter 2.
Tur in name of his father the Rosh in his rulings there, who holds Egyptians returned to their place that Scripture allotted them, as written "at the end of forty years I will gather," etc.; so taught in his Tosefta, and so too here in name of Ramban; and in Beit Chadash ruled per Rambam.
