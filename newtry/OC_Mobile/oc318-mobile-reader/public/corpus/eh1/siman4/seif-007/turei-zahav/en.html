Egyptian convert who married Kutit Ammonit, etc. This assumption is difficult: if we follow as though Egyptian convert were Israelite who cohabited with gentile woman — even if she bore a daughter she would be permitted like Ammonit, as law of Israelite with gentile woman and maidservant — child like her for everything. If we follow both — even son would be Egyptian and must be daughter like her for everything. If we follow both, doubt whom to follow since one gentile and one convert — perforce follow stringency in both. Nevertheless difficult — no source for this; in some books Tosafot here "the daughter is permitted" — printing error; must read "Egyptian."
