An Ammonite who married an Egyptian woman — the child is Ammonite; and an Egyptian who married an Ammonite woman — the child is Egyptian. Among nations the child follows the male; but if they converted, the child follows the more defective of the two. Therefore, an Ammonite convert who married an Egyptian convert — if the child is male, his law is like an Ammonite to be forbidden forever; and if she is female, her law is like an Egyptian. {Rama: An Egyptian convert who married a gentile Ammonite woman — the son is Ammonite and the daughter is permitted. (Alternate text: Egyptian woman.) (Tur)}
