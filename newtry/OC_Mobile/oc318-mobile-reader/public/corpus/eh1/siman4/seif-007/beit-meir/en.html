The woman. etc., child mamzer, see Rema: behold in presumption of mamzer; and since Shulchan Aruch supports and since dispute, safek mamzer; implies per one view definite mamzer permitted with mamzeret; great question: Rema concludes if she says impregnated from gentile trusted to validate child and perforce even gentile also common and how definite mamzer, though lying in claiming impregnated from husband nevertheless perhaps from gentile; must explain presumption of mamzer from concern from husband we hold him definite mamzer and practical difference where entire city Israelites then per one view definite law applies. Chelkat Mechokek wrote: but if woman says husband came, etc., plain if husband admits appears not safek fit for Shmuel was fit, implies even if known all year in distant land no possibility he came except through name also fit since Shmuel fit; in my view doubtful in substance of Yerushalmi as Rosh said not heard for can say case was among prominent people that night he was with wife before witnesses and Behag brings only proof after we found once so, automatically also this concern included in what said regarding divorcee mamzeret not concerned; Beit Shmuel s.k. 69 in name Rabbi Duran: Rema discusses husband before us and admits did not come, wonder: if so why matter he was abroad even if with her and says did not approach more than twelve months still child mamzer for trusted say not his son and Beit Shmuel emended and wrote Rema discusses when before us and does not say came meaning silent, not in Rema's language; plain discusses when not before us; see siman 144 seif 7 Rema's view to rule even leniency because people do not come secretly; see siman 156 seif 4, from there no full proof here; nevertheless in my view without safek dispute between Rema and Behag.
