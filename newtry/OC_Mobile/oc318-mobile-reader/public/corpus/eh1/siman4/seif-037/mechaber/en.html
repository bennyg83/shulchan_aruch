Every country in which there is a maidservant or Samaritan woman fit to bear — since the foundling found there is a doubtful Samarite or slave: when he marries a convert, behold she is a doubtful married woman. And likewise a shetuki who married a woman who could be a forbidden relation to him — behold she is in doubt of being a married woman, for kiddushin do not take effect in forbidden relations. Which woman could be a forbidden relation to him? Any woman whose father or brother existed when his mother conceived, and every woman who was divorced or widowed — perhaps she is his father's wife or his father's brother's wife. {Rama: Karaites are forbidden to intermarry with; all of them are doubtful mamzerim, and we do not accept them if they wish to return (Beit Yosef citing responsum of R' Shimshon). But forced converts returning to the religion of Israel — it seems to me permitted to intermarry with them like other converts (ibid.).}
