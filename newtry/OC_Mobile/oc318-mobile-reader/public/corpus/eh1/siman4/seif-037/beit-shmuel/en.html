A doubtful forbidden relation. This is for the reason I wrote above: even where most people are Jews, his kiddushin is only doubtful, and no one is executed on the basis of his kiddushin, as the Rambam writes in chapter 15. See the Maggid Mishneh there.
And likewise shetuki, etc. Already wrote shetuki if no mamzer concern permitted marry woman and not concerned lest marry sister as the Rambam chapter 15; need reason why concerned here his wife forbidden to him even though discusses shetuki with mamzer; nevertheless regarding kiddushin why concerned lest marry forbidden relation; whence this law to the Rambam.
And we do not accept them. The concern is that they may intermingle with Jews, marry Jewish women, and thereby increase mamzerim, as stated in the name of Rav Natrunai. See the Beit Yosef. It appears that converts from the seven nations and Nesinim may be accepted, although intermarriage with them is forbidden. We are not concerned that they will come to intermarry, since there is no concern of mamzerus. See Darkei Moshe.
