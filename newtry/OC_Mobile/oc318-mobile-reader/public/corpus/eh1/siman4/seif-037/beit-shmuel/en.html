Safek forbidden relation. From reason I wrote above — even majority Israel only safek kiddushin and do not execute on his kiddushin as Rambam part 15; see Maggid there.
And likewise shetuki, etc. Already wrote shetuki if no mamzer concern permitted marry woman and not concerned lest marry sister as Rambam part 15; need reason why concerned here his wife forbidden to him even though discusses shetuki with mamzer — nevertheless regarding kiddushin why concerned lest marry forbidden relation — whence this law to Rambam.
And we do not accept them. Lest intermix with Israel and marry women and increase mamzerim — all the more Rabbi Neturai; see Beit Yosef — implies converts from seven nations and Netinim permitted to accept except to intermarry forbidden and not concerned lest come to intermarry since no mamzer concern; see Darkei Moshe.
