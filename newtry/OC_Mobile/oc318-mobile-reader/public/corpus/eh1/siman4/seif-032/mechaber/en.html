A child who was cast on the road — if one came and said he is my son and I cast him, he is trusted; and likewise his mother is trusted. If he was collected from the marketplace and his father and mother came and said he is our son — they are not trusted, since the name of foundling has been attached to him. In years of famine they are trusted, for because of famine they cast him and they want others to sustain him; therefore they were silent until he was collected.
