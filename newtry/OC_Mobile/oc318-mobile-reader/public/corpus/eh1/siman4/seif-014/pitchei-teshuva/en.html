Ugly matter. See responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 99 who wrote: since Rama stam and did not explain what is called seclusion and its boundary — implies he relied on seclusion in siman 11; so for examiner in Maimonides' responsum the matter is clear that any seclusion not sufficient for questioning is not sufficient even when joined with her giving birth at eight months. From Torah reason it appears clear too: in secluded path of prohibition and she gave birth at eight months — not forbidden; for for seclusion alone she is not forbidden for questioning, etc.; however from Beit Shmuel s.k. 18 language that reason in ugly matter she is forbidden when giving birth at eight months — we do not say she waited only when she has presumption of fitness — implies his intent: since she acted improperly her presumption of fitness was impaired; in my view cannot say so — see there. He further wrote: what implies from Rama's words here that all seclusion mentioned siman 11 forbids when giving birth at eight months — even seclusion of exiting from dark place — one can question; for Mara dehai shmaatsa is Maharam in Maimonides' responsum and he holds seclusion of dark place is not in category of seclusion (see Rosh chapter 2 Yevamot); if so we should be lenient in dark-place seclusion with birth at eight months. Though who will raise head to be lenient against Rama z"l — nevertheless the strap was permitted to judge where possible it is not in category of dark place — see there; siman 100 Beit Meir challenged him and siman 101 Ra'ak z"l answered and concludes: but since your honor questions in any seclusion in the world even in prohibited path with birth at eight months one can say forbidden — Heaven forbid for me to be lenient against him — see there.
