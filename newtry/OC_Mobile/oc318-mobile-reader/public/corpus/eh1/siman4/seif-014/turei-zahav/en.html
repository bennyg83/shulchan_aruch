And there is one who says he is not in presumption of mamzer — Hagahot Maimoniyot's view. Tur: because we attribute lest he came in private and cohabited. In that incident in Gemara Rabbah validated Tosefta regarding woman who sat twelve months without husband — because she waited in her mother's womb that time; could derive lest came in private; seems there discusses when she admits husband did not come within the time; nevertheless appears first view is Rambam — presumption of mamzer — even if she claims certainly came to her in private does not help; if it helped we would attribute also where she claims nothing — why make him definite mamzer per Rambam?
