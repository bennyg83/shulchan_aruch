A single woman who conceived and gave birth — if she is not before us to examine her, or she is a fool or a widow, and even if she says the child is from so-and-so and we know that so-and-so is a mamzer — this child is only doubtful; even if that so-and-so admits he had relations with her, for just as she fornicated with this one, so she fornicated with another. And if that so-and-so is fit — the child is fit; nevertheless we do not grasp him as his son certainly to inherit him if he does not admit he is his son. (And even if she was designated to him, she is not trusted about him (Ran end of chapter Almannah leKohen Gadol) (and even one witness is trusted about him when he denies him) (so too there).) But we are concerned for her words and he is forbidden to the relatives of that so-and-so. {Rama: And specifically when she is single; but if she fornicated under her husband, even though she says the child is from so-and-so and he is a mamzer — we are not concerned for her words, for we attribute the majority of her cohabitations to her husband; and the child is fit and permitted to the relatives of that so-and-so whom she names. (Piskei Maharai siman 37, and so too Beit Yisrael Zev.)}
