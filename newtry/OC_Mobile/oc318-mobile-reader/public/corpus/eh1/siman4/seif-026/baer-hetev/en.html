Even if she does not stand continuously before him, nevertheless concerned lest she fornicated with others; Chelkat Mechokek disagrees when she does not stand continuously and one admits he cohabited with her — he is definitely his son since he admits; all the more when designated to him and he cohabited many times — we attribute majority of cohabitations to him. Rabbi's words in hagahah here writing even if designated to him she is not trusted about him — contradicts siman 3 seif 3: kohen with single woman who admits son is kohen for everything and not concerned lest she made herself available; there unstated single woman and all the more designated; Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel agreed with Chelkat Mechokek for designated and agreed she makes him his son; wrote what Rama wrote "even if designated not trusted" — when he does not admit; but if not designated, even if admits cohabitation, even if not standing continuously — concerned she fornicated with another. On Chelkat Mechokek's difficulty Beit Shmuel answered above discusses if all kohanim where no Torah prohibition — see there. Responsum Knesset Yechezkel question 56 answered another way — see there. Beit Yosef answered here discusses when paramour is definite mamzer — rule leniently she fornicated with others even if he admits; proof: if so-and-so fit child fit; stringently not say she fornicated — if so above kohen paramour who is fit we do not say as she made herself available — see there. His son. If one woman claims against one that the child is from him and he should give her sustenance and he denies — exempt even from oath. Even if she claims he pay her marriage contract — liable for oath; nevertheless regarding child he is believed without oath — Rivash siman 41; see siman 177 seif 5 in hagahah and above siman 22 s.k. 4; so too in Beit Yosef; see responsum Chacham Tzvi questions 145–146.
Designated. See s.k. 29 in Beit Shmuel.
And fit. Meaning when husband was with her. Responsum Mahari Weil siman 74 ruled: woman who was divorced and bore — child fit because of double doubt — perhaps after divorce she fornicated; or say before divorce perhaps fornicated with gentile; if she says she fornicated before divorce — trusted to establish child as safek mamzer — end quote. Darkei Moshe on this wrote: discusses when husband was not with her — else attribute to husband; see Beit Shmuel.
