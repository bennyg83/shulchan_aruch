Congregation of converts is not called congregation; permitted means from mamzeret side there is no prohibition; nevertheless forbidden to increase mamzerim because his sons are mamzerim. Maidservant forbidden to convert because he is warned on all mitzvot. However Ammonite convert who wanted to marry maidservant — Chelkat Mechokek uncertain. Ammonite who married mamzeret — follow blemish in both and child is Ammonite mamzer — Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel.
