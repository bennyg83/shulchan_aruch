A convert and a freed slave are permitted to a mamzeret, and likewise a mamzer is permitted to a convert and a freed woman, because the congregation of converts is not called "congregation." And the child of a mamzer — even if his conception and birth were in holiness, such as his father was a convert who married a convert — nevertheless is permitted to a mamzeret only until ten generations; but from then on he is forbidden, for the name of conversion has already been forgotten from him and people will say an Israelite is marrying a mamzeret. According to the Rambam he is permitted to a mamzeret, as is his son, grandson, and great-grandson, until the name of his conversion has been forgotten from him and he does not know he is a convert; then he is forbidden to a mamzeret. Converts and freed slaves share one law.
