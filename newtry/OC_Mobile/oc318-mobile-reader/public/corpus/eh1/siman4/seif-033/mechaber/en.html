A foundling found in a city containing Samaritans and Israelites — whether the majority are Samaritans or the majority are Israelites — behold he is a doubtful Samaritan for purposes of lineage; if he betrothed a woman, she needs a get out of doubt. If the court immersed him for conversion, or he immersed when he grew up — behold, for purposes of lineage he is like other foundlings found in Israelite cities, for immersion is effective only to remove him from Samarite status.
