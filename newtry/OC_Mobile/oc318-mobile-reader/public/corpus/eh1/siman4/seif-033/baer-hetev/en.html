Kutit. Concern lest mamzer — forbidden with Israelite daughter and forbidden with mamzeret. Even in majority gentiles forbidden with mamzeret after conversion and forbidden with Israelite daughter — so Rambam's view; Ra'avad in majority gentiles permitted with mamzeret and Israelite daughter. Even half and half permitted with mamzeret and Israelite daughter. If two majorities gentiles — follow two majorities. If sign in foundling as previous seif not mamzer — permitted to marry woman and not concerned lest marry sister — see Beit Shmuel.
