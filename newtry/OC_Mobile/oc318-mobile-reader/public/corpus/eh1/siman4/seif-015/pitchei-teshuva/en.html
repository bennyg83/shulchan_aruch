We do not say she waited. See responsum Ra'ak Eiger there end siman 39 who wrote: also what your honor wrote that after husband left she became niddah — in such case there is room to forbid when giving birth at eight months, etc.; in my view plain in mishnah chapter 1 Niddah that pregnant woman only when fetus recognized is blood removed not before; likewise Kenesset HaGedolah in name Radbaz part 1 siman 12: if husband claims not his son that when he left she was in niddah and admits he cohabited before her period — if between last cohabitation husband admits and birth of child no more than eight months child fit — see there (likewise there siman 128 what she became niddah no evidence she is not pregnant; he added even though we see our women immediately when pregnant blood removed and natures changed — nevertheless see YD siman 189 and 190 where pregnant when fetus recognized mentioned and no hint in Acharonim that for us even without recognition — see there; see further responsum Noda BiYehudah siman 69 brought briefly below siman 156 s.k. 5; see Pitchei Teshuva YD siman 194 s.k. 3). He further wrote what responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 84: only when giving birth at eight months permitted but at nine or eleven months forbidden for she does not wait except nine or twelve months. In my view no need to heed his words for Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel plainly validate at eleven months and likewise precise Rambam's language and clear from Maharil brought in Kenesset HaGedolah — see there.
