A married woman about whom a rumor spread that she was prostituting under her husband, and everyone gossips about her — we are not concerned for her sons lest they are mamzerim, for we attribute the majority of cohabitations to the husband. But she herself we are concerned for as a zona, and a kohen is concerned for her by Torah law (and if her husband is a kohen, we are concerned for her sons that they are chalalim) (Mahariu siman 24). And an Israelite, if he wishes to distance himself from the ugliness; and if she is very promiscuous, we are concerned even for the sons. {Rama: And nevertheless she is trusted to say about her sons that they are fit (Maharam Padua siman 33). If she was promiscuous when she was single or betrothed and was not promiscuous after marriage — even though they saw her commit adultery once, her sons are fit. (So too there.)}
