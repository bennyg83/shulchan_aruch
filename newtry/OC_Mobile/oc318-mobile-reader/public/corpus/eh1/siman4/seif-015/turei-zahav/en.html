Concerned for her as zona. Mohakh challenged from end Gittin and brought siman 6: woman regarding whom voice of prostitute went out — not concerned to disqualify from kehunah — mere promiscuity suffices; answered: there discusses single woman — attribute to promiscuity of laughing in market or squeezing in alley as Rashi there — therefore not concerned and removed voice of zenut since she is single; but sitting under husband if only promiscuity would not gossip after her unless ugly matter seen — end quote. Logic is opposite — people are stricter regarding married woman playing with others in market and gossip after her more than single woman. Regarding above difficulty if difficulty — can challenge Gemara Sotah chapter Arusah (27): man may not marry similar woman — Rashi: accused and spoken of by all regarding all her adultery — same law mentioned here and end Gittin: voice she is prostitute — not concerned to disqualify from kehunah — same law siman 6; in Gemara no distinction married woman and single — Sotah also discusses single as explained nearby; no difficulty at all — "similar" means there is following gossip of adultery as Tur here; unlike end Gittin only general voice because saw playing as Rashi there; but no distinction at all between prohibition of married woman and single regarding kehunah in this.
Nevertheless she is trusted, etc. This ruling from responsum Maharam Padua siman 33 — very wondrous. First bring Gemara sugya and Tosafot: Sotah 26 — Shmuel said man may marry similar woman and not woman similar — Rashi: similar discussed on her adultery; woman similar — this from fit drop and that from unfit — gentile or mamzer; R' Yochanan said marry woman similar and not similar — this in presumption of fitness; unfit drop we are not concerned — majority relations from husband and from husband she conceived; this lacks presumption of fitness — she prostitutes under him, forbids herself to him, he does not see and does not divorce and cohabits biblically; halakhah: marry woman similar not similar — Rav Tachlifa taught: adulterous woman — sons fit — majority relations after husband; Rashi: therefore woman similar since she is fit better marry her under presumption of permission than similar who corrupts him to serve with her after forbidden to him. Rav Amram asked: if very promiscuous — are sons fit since he knows she is watched, or since very promiscuous cannot watch her she slips suddenly — teiku; Tosafot wrote majority after husband though Rav who said end Almannah leKohen Gadol one who cohabits with fiancée in father-in-law's house — child mamzer — when attributes also from elsewhere and we do not cast after him — only fiancée not available to him; but husband who marries her admits; for Shmuel no difference — always child shetuki; but if before us and she says to fit man I was impregnated — trusted — R' Yochanan aliba diR' Nachman who says she trusted even with majority unfit before her — see there. Plain Shmuel follows Tosafot who hold woman similar forbidden on this — specifically when not before us; in Maharam Padua responsum on these Tosafot: cannot escape two meanings or meaning Shmuel who said marry similar not woman similar — Shmuel lacks majority-relations reasoning — only when not before us. If intent on very promiscuous we follow majority and when promiscuous majority void — concerned for sons only when not before us — she is trusted; if intent on Shmuel who said do not marry daughter similar — discusses not very promiscuous since R' Yochanan disputes him — still no difference between very promiscuous for R' Yochanan who voids majority reason and concerned for sons if she says to fit man I was impregnated — trusted; what Tosafot wrote there trusted — their intent also on Shmuel.
