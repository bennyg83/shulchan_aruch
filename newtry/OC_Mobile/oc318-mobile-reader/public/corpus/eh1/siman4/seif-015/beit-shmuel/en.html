And all gossip. This is worse than voice of prostitute in city — we do not concerned as Gemara end Gittin and below siman 6; Bach wrote here discusses married woman which is worse therefore concerned; below discusses single woman; in sugya end Gittin "like tannaim" proves married woman and nevertheless not concerned; what written there not concerned means she shall not leave but forbidden to kohen — if so how say like tannaim and Rabbi holds like Rivan who only said shall not leave — see there; also Choshen Mishpat siman 6 wrote similarly.
Majority of cohabitations attributed to husband. Even if witnesses testified she committed zenut — nevertheless sons fit from this reason; for Rambam permitted lekhatchilah marry her daughter; Rashi explained there marry similar daughter — when cannot marry other except similar or daughter similar marry daughter similar.
Attribute to husband. Because husband available to her unlike fiancé — do not attribute to fiancé even if certainly cohabited because not available; see Tosafot there.
Concerned for her from Torah law. Meaning lekhatchilah; bedi'eved if she married do not remove; nearby if her husband kohen concerned for sons — lekhatchilah.
Concerned for her sons. For if she committed zenut under him once even raped becomes zona forbidden to kehunah; if husband cohabited afterward sons become chalalim; if husband Israel even intentional zenut sons afterward fit even for kehunah — we do not hold like R' Chiya as written nearby; likewise Rambam's view sugya discusses kohen and tanna marry daughter similar.
And if very promiscuous concerned, etc. Concern of mamzer since cannot say majority from husband; so Piskei Maharai siman 37 and Rashi implies; however Rosh chapter 18 wrote in name Hagahot Maimoniyot we say majority from husband even if very promiscuous — so Gemara part 15; disputants of Hagahot Maimoniyot do not dispute on this; so responsum Mahari Weil — sugya Sotah discusses kehunah prohibition but no mamzer concern; deduces from Rambam who wrote if promiscuous concerned even for sons — concerned for her and sons — her concern kehunah prohibition, sons concern mamzer; therefore sons lekhatchilah forbidden, bedi'eved unresolved if remove from Israel; per this fits what Maharam Padua ruled and hagahah nearby: if very promiscuous and says child from husband — trusted to validate; learned from Tosafot there aliba diShmuel who does not hold majority from husband — nevertheless helps when she says child from husband; likewise for us if very promiscuous trusted to validate child; Taz challenged and wrote cannot compare promiscuous to Shmuel's law — Rashi wrote very promiscuous there is concern majority from others — cannot compare to Shmuel who even though does not hold majority nevertheless no concern majority from others; but promiscuous has concern majority from others; if he saw these poskim's words would not have challenged — most poskim do not say mamzer concern even if very promiscuous.
