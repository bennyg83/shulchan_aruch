And all persistent rumor. This is worse than rumor of prostitute in city; we do not concerned as Gemara end Gittin and below siman 6; Bach wrote here discusses married woman which is worse therefore concerned; below discusses single woman; in sugya end Gittin "like tannaim" proves married woman and nevertheless not concerned; what written there not concerned means she shall not leave but forbidden to kohen; if so how say like tannaim and Rabbi holds like Rivan who only said shall not leave; see there; also Chelkas Mechokek siman 6 wrote similarly.
Majority of cohabitations attributed to husband. Even if witnesses testified she committed promiscuity; nevertheless sons valid from this reason; for the Rambam permitted l'chatchilah marry her daughter; Rashi explained there marry similar daughter; when cannot marry other except similar or daughter similar marry daughter similar.
Attribute to husband. Because husband available to her unlike betrothed man; do not attribute to betrothed man even if certainly cohabited because not available; see Tosafot there.
Concerned for her by Torah law. Meaning l'chatchilah; b'dieved if she married do not remove; nearby if her husband kohen concerned for sons; l'chatchilah.
Concerned for her sons. For if she committed promiscuity under him once even raped becomes zona forbidden to kehunah; if husband cohabited afterward sons become chalalim; if husband Israel even intentional promiscuity sons afterward valid even for kehunah; we do not hold like R' Chiya as written nearby; likewise the Rambam's view sugya discusses kohen and tanna marry daughter similar.
And if very promiscuous concerned, etc. Concern of mamzer since cannot say majority from husband; so Piskei Maharai siman 37 and Rashi implies; however the Rosh chapter 18 wrote in name Hagahot Maimoniyot we say majority from husband even if very promiscuous; so Gemara chapter 15; disputants of Hagahot Maimoniyot do not dispute on this; so responsum Mahari Weil; sugya Sotah discusses kehunah prohibition but no mamzer concern; deduces from the Rambam who wrote if promiscuous concerned even for sons; concerned for her and sons; her concern kehunah prohibition, sons concern mamzer; therefore sons l'chatchilah forbidden, b'dieved unresolved if remove from Israel; per this valids what Maharam Padua ruled and Rama's gloss nearby: if very promiscuous and says the child from husband; trusted to validate; learned from Tosafot there aliba diShmuel who does not hold majority from husband; nevertheless helps when she says the child from husband; likewise for us if very promiscuous trusted to validate the child; Taz challenged and wrote cannot compare promiscuous to Shmuel's law; Rashi wrote very promiscuous there is concern majority from others; cannot compare to Shmuel who even though does not hold majority nevertheless no concern majority from others; but promiscuous has concern majority from others; if he saw these poskim's words would not have challenged; most poskim do not say mamzer concern even if very promiscuous.
