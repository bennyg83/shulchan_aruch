Child Egyptian. Tur wrote like Ammonite convert who married Egyptian convertess, or Egyptian convert who married Ammonite convertess — if she bore son law like Ammoni forbidden forever; if daughter law like Egyptian forbidden two generations. Difficult — we say explicitly in Gemara regarding Egyptian convert and Ammonite convertess there is no following blemished party in both, for no blemish in her at all; only regarding Ammonite convert who married Egyptian convertess do we say when converted follow blemish in both — if male child follow him disqualified forever; if female follow her forbidden at least two generations. From this Beit Yosef emended in Bedek HaBayit: Egyptian convert and Ammonite convertess — child Egyptian; so too here. Some want to explain Tur's words "if she bore son" on that division of Ammonite convert and Egyptian convertess alone — no implication in language at all; seems opposite — must read like Ammonite convert who married Egyptian convertess — if son law like Ammoni forbidden forever; if daughter, or Egyptian convert who married Ammonite convertess — law like Egyptian forbidden two generations — as must be.
