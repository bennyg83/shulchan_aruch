The child follows the blemished party. We establish: wherever there is kiddushin and transgression the child follows the blemished party — mishnah end chapter HaOmer.
Even for kehunah. So R' Yochanan holds — though their cohabitation was in transgression nevertheless the daughter is fit; in chapter Almannah leKohen Gadol an Ammonite convert who cohabited with an Israelite daughter disqualified her — so Rif, Rambam chapter 8, and Rosh. Therefore difficult on Rosh — how he learned gentile/slave with Israelite daughter blemishes child for kehunah from widow-Kohen Gadol a fortiori when Ammonite convert proves he blemishes in cohabitation yet daughter fit for kehunah — Rambam's reason: gentile/slave child fit for kehunah because holds a fortiori refuted; Tosafot challenged on sugya in HaAreil; Bach wrote on Ammonite convert who married Israelite daughter — daughter fit for kehunah; but gentile/slave because kiddushin do not take effect; also could derive from widow a fortiori because unequal prohibition and did not give reason kiddushin take effect. Appears he omitted Tosafot's words there.
Second child. Maggid wrote on sugya "who are born to them" — Scripture tied them to birth, meaning tied even to mother; regarding father plainly tied. For poskim who follow mother — do not challenge "converts follow blemished party" — Rashi and Tosafot Kiddushin 64: only with two nations, not one nation.
