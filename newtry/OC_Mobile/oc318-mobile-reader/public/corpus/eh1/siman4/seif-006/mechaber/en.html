If one of these converts and marries an Israelite, or one of these converts and marries an Israelite woman — the child follows the defective one. Therefore, an Ammonite or second-generation Egyptian convert who married an Israelite daughter — the daughter is fit, even for kehunah, since after whichever of them she follows, she is fit. But a second-generation Egyptian who married a first-generation Egyptian woman — the child is a second-generation Egyptian; a first-generation Egyptian who married a second-generation Egyptian woman — some say the child is third-generation, and according to the Rambam the child is second-generation.
