Permitted to feed him carrion. All the more do not return lost object; for damages liable pay full damage — so Tosafot Yoma in Rashi's explanation Ketubot and Maggid; wrote reason because no presumption and he himself unaware if Israelite; Tosafot wrote other reason as nearby; Beit Yosef deduced Tur does not hold thus — not so; already challenged in Darkei Moshe.
Return to him. For damages if his ox gored our ox — Tosefta Yoma there pays full damage like mu'ad even though in money not follow majority — when came to his hand knowingly; Tur and Maggid wrote majority Israel does not pay because not follow majority in money; majority gentiles pays because no definite claim as s.k. before; returning lost object because finder lacks presumption — not his money.
Obligation to sustain him. Do not return lost object — tell him bring proof you are Israelite; even per Maggid's view cannot extract proof from him — here no majority against him can establish presumption.
And desecrate the ruin for him. Poskim already expanded to explain Rambam as Orach Chayim chapter 329 laws of Shabbos — will write briefly from Maggid and Magen Avraham chapter 2 laws of Shabbos; per Rambam's responsum to sages of Lunil per establishment in Yoma chapter 4 divides saving life between separated entirely vs partially — Rambam: separated entirely from fixed place and at uprooting one separated and ruin fell — follow majority; if one separated from fixed place do not follow majority in saving life even though for prohibition we follow majority in found; here found infant presumably city people uprooted and as if all uprooted and one separated — therefore follow majority; when majority Kutim do not desecrate ruin — Tosafot chapter 1 Ketubot wrote foundling in city not considered fixed since not found in house — therefore majority gentiles do not desecrate and do not sustain; half and half desecrate ruin and then obligated to sustain because holds obligation to sustain equals desecrating ruin — so Rashi Yoma there; Mechaber Orach Chayim siman 329 ruled per Rambam's explanation uprooted and one separated do not desecrate ruin; here brought in hagahah dissenters' view — even per Rambam city people considered uprooted and as one separated from fixed place; all discusses found in city; if found in house — fixed place and desecrate as Tosafot there.
And not commanded to sustain him. See Rashi and Tosafot Yoma there wrote all whom we desecrate ruin for — all the more commanded to sustain.
