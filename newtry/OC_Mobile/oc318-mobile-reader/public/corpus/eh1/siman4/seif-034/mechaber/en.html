If he did not immerse and the court did not immerse him — if the majority of the city were Samaritans, it is permitted to feed him nevelah; if the majority were Israelites, they return his lost object as to an Israelite; if half and half, there is a commandment to sustain him as an Israelite, and we oversee protecting the infant on Shabbat, and he is subject to the laws of damages; and in every monetary safek, the burden of proof is on the one who extracts from his fellow. {Rama: And some say that even with a Samaritan majority we oversee the infant on Shabbat, but the commandment to sustain him applies only with an Israelite majority.
