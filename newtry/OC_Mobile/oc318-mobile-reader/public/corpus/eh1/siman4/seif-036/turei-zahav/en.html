Child shetuki or foundling. Tur concluded mamzerim and Netinim certainly permitted with each other — Rambam part 15 forbidden relations words; there concluded child mamzer; Tur need not write child mamzer since wrote earlier safek mamzer or foundling and shetuki marry from converts and child follows blemished party — all the more mamzer and Netinah child mamzer; Mohakh labored to resolve why Tur did not mention child mamzer; Beit Yosef wrote reason child mamzer from mishnah wherever kiddushin and transgression child follows blemish in both — not that transgression in marriage; source: convert who married mamzeret — kiddushin and no transgression yet child follows blemish; same mamzer and Netinah; reason mamzer permitted with Netinah because congregation of Netinim not called congregation.
