Doubtful cases such as shetukim and foundlings are forbidden to marry one another; if they married, they do not maintain the marriage but separate with a get, and the child is doubtful like his parents. There is no remedy for these doubtful cases except that they marry from converts, and the child follows the defective one: how so? A shetuki or foundling who married a convert or freed woman, or a convert or freed man who married a shetukit or foundling — the child is a shetuki or foundling.
