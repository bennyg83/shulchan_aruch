The doubtful cases, etc. The Tur also lists a Samaritan as a doubtful mamzer. The Beit Yosef writes that although the Sages treated them as complete non-Jews, they did not do so where that would produce a leniency. The same appears below in siman 44.
The doubts, etc. Chapter 10 and chapter HaAreil explained certain mamzer and Netinim permitted with each other; per Rava's first version "do not intermarry" not in their conversion; Netinim who converted permitted from Torah except David decreed; with disqualified not decreed; per latter version Rava forbidden from Torah and "do not intermarry" in conversion; nevertheless mamzer permitted to intermarry because not in his holiness as chapter HaAreil like wounded or crushed; permitted with Netinim because not in my holiness; likewise mamzer; when Netinim converted prohibition from holiness and mamzer and foundling not in holiness; but to intermarry before conversion forbidden; specifically maidservant permitted because enactment for the child not with non Jewish woman; doubtful mamzer with Netinim depends on dispute; per poskim latter version seems prohibition lest perhaps valid; even per first version say also decreed; proven from Rashi chapter Asarah Yuchasin wrote doubts forbidden with Netinim even though holds first version as chapter Elu Na'arot; the Rambam rules first version and permits; mamzer with Netinah per the Rambam Netin is like convert; convert with mamzer and the child mamzer; even per other poskim Netin forbidden by Torah law even after conversion; still convert except liable for negative commandments; likewise Ammonite with mamzer and the child mamzer; Netin forbidden with maidservant; so Tosafot Gittin 41 and Pesachim; there wrote aliba diRabbeinu Tam who holds mamzer permitted with maidservant because sacred prostitute; say Netin forbidden; per the Rambam I brought above say permitted; Tosafot's difficulty there who challenged "and marry Netinah"; unresolved to settle.
