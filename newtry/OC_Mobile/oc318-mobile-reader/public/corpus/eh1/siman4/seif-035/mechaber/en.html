Women who gave birth at once — a kohen's wife, a Levi's wife, an Israelite, and a mamzer — the midwife is trusted to say this son is a kohen, or Levi, or mamzer, because he was not presumed of lineage and we do not know their pedigrees. In what case? When she was presumed of pedigree, she is trusted if no one challenged her; but if someone challenged her, even one person who said she testifies falsely — she is not trusted, and behold the son is in the presumption of fitness and has no pedigree.
