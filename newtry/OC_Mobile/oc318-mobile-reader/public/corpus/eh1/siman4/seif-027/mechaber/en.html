A betrothed woman who conceived while in her father's house — if she says she conceived from the fiancé: if he admits, or if he is not before us — the child is fit (and he is his son for inheritance, even if the fiancé says he does not remember but only that he does not deny her (there in Ran)). If he does not admit but denies her, saying he is not from him — the child is a definite mamzer. If she is not before us to ask her, or she says she does not know from whom she conceived — it is a doubtful mamzer. The woman is not presumed to be a prostitute, but is trusted to say she had relations with the fiancé, even if he denies her; and this applies universally — if she married a kohen, she is not removed and the child from him is fit. But to the fiancé himself she is forbidden, for she has made herself a piece of prohibition.
