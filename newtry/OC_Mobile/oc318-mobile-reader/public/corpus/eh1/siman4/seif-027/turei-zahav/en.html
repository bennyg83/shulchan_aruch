Gemara Beit Yosef brings explicit: even with majority unfit before her — bedi'eved not to forbid her to fiancé; but lekhatchilah she should not marry kohen; if fiancé died — neither she nor fetus until majority fit before her; but if he admits to her — then permitted lekhatchilah to kohen and fetus also fit lekhatchilah; even R' Yehoshua admits fit — Mohakh wrote this in name one sage — unnecessary, explained in Gemara as Beit Yosef in name Ran; see seif 14 regarding if very promiscuous.
