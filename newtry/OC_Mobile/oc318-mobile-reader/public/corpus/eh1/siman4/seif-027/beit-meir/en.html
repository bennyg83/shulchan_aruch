Hagahah: and he is his son to inherit him. Beit Shmuel wrote and appears even for yibbum we say he is his son; on the early ones I grieve and whence did Nissim Yisraeli learn he inherits from her words when he is not before us (see Piskei Maharai siman 252 wrote likewise and implies from Rosh even if examined mother and said from fiancé impregnated we silence him from properties), rather he rolls on us that she died without chalitzah and certainly question for we say do not act etc. and no more than this to exempt without chalitzah; appears he relies on what wrote and can say since we judge after him regarding not mamzer cannot divide etc., meaning fine single woman even if says from so-and-so though lying perhaps child is valid from another; but with fiancée once not believed regarding another concerned not from him automatically mamzer and there is disgrace, therefore all considered bedieved; likewise Chelkat Mechokek's reasoning, this built on Perishah's words that afterward when she before us and does not claim from gentile no reliance on gentile; but below even with fiancée can divide and say fit from gentile though not on fiancé; and this reasoning as appears in Chelkat Mechokek founded on Ran's explanation in Rashi and appears from his language discusses when method brought in name one who says Chelkat Mechokek chapter 17 wrote in single woman even child considered full lechatchilah and forbidden even through mother's examinations except with fiancée because of her bedieved lest heart be cut, but per what we rule like Tosafot or R' Nachman regarding child even lechatchilah for her considered bedieved, this reasoning not forced at all, doubtful.
