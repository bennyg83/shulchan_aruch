And he is his son to inherit him. Since she says clearly from fiancé — child also from fiancé permitted since we judge after fiancé and not concerned lest also fornicated with another; appears even for yibbum we say he is his son and she exempt without chalitzah; why judge after fiancé and he inherits — Gemara says judge after fiancé because bedi'eved — if so why inherit? Say since we judge after him regarding not mamzer cannot divide and say he is his son for everything; Beit Chadash wrote even though certainly his son nevertheless forbidden to kohen lekhatchilah unless he admits; Choshen Mishpat challenged and wrote since child marries Israelite and shares with brothers — for her too bedi'eved and fit for kehunah once permitted permitted; see nearby what I wrote; Perishah wrote if he before us and says child not from him and she not here — child safek mamzer lest conceived from Kutit; nearby if he contradicts her — definite mamzer when she here and does not say Kutit child; however nearby seems we do not say conceived from Kutit.
Definite mamzer. Because Torah gave trustworthiness to father to say child not from him; see Shulchan Gavoah chapter 10.
And the woman, etc. Above refers when she says conceived from fiancé and he contradicts her.
She need not leave. But initially she is certainly forbidden, for the first child is treated as a mamzer. If she married, she need not leave, even though the child is a mamzer. Although I wrote nearby that it is impossible to distinguish, nevertheless we distinguish between the child and her. This applies regarding her desire to marry a kohen, unlike Chelkat Mechokek, who wrote that since we cast the child after the betrothed man, she is valid for kehunah after the betrothed man dies. If she says that the child is not from the betrothed man and he is silent, he is a doubtful mamzer according to her word. If she says that he is not from the betrothed man and he says that the child is from him, see nearby in the Hagah.
