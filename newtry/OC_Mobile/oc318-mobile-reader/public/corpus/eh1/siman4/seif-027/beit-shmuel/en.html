And he is his son to inherit him. Since she says clearly from the betrothed man; the child also from the betrothed man permitted since we judge after betrothed man and not concerned lest also had relations with another; appears even for yibbum we say he is his son and she exempt without chalitzah; why judge after betrothed man and he inherits; Gemara says judge after betrothed man because b'dieved; if so why inherit? Say since we judge after him regarding not mamzer cannot divide and say he is his son for everything; Beit Chadash wrote even though certainly his son nevertheless forbidden to kohen l'chatchilah unless he admits; Chelkas Mechokek challenged and wrote since the child marries Israelite and shares with brothers; for her too b'dieved and valid for kehunah once permitted permitted; see nearby what I wrote; Perishah wrote if he before us and says the child not from him and she not here; the child doubtful mamzer lest conceived from non Jewish woman; nearby if he contradicts her; certain mamzer when she here and does not say non Jewish woman the child; however nearby seems we do not say conceived from non Jewish woman.
A certain mamzer. The Torah granted the father credibility to say that the child is not his. See Shiltei Giborim, chapter Asarah Yuchasin.
And the woman, etc. This refers back to the preceding case, where she says that she conceived from the betrothed man and he contradicts her.
She need not leave. But initially she is certainly forbidden, for the first the child is treated as a mamzer. If she married, she need not leave, even though the child is a mamzer. Although I wrote nearby that it is impossible to distinguish, nevertheless we distinguish between the child and her. This applies regarding her desire to marry a kohen, unlike Chelkat Mechokek, who wrote that since we cast the child after the betrothed man, she is valid for kehunah after the betrothed man dies. If she says that the child is not from the betrothed man and he is silent, he is a doubtful mamzer according to her word. If she says that he is not from the betrothed man and he says that the child is from him, see nearby in the Hagah.
