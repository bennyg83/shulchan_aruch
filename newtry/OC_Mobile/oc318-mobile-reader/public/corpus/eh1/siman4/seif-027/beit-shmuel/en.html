Since she says clearly from fiancé — child also from fiancé permitted since we judge after fiancé and not concerned lest also fornicated with another; appears even for yibbum we say he is his son and she exempt without chalitzah; why judge after fiancé and he inherits — Gemara says judge after fiancé because bedi'eved — if so why inherit? Say since we judge after him regarding not mamzer cannot divide and say he is his son for everything; Beit Chadash wrote even though certainly his son nevertheless forbidden to kohen lekhatchilah unless he admits; Choshen Mishpat challenged and wrote since child marries Israelite and shares with brothers — for her too bedi'eved and fit for kehunah once permitted permitted; see nearby what I wrote; Perishah wrote if he before us and says child not from him and she not here — child safek mamzer lest conceived from Kutit; nearby if he contradicts her — definite mamzer when she here and does not say Kutit child; however nearby seems we do not say conceived from Kutit.
Definite mamzer. Because Torah gave trustworthiness to father to say child not from him; see Shulchan Gavoah chapter 10.
