Lekhatchilah maidservant. Even though mamzer liable for all Torah prohibitions — how violate "there shall not be a sacred prostitute" regarding maidservant prohibition? Rabbeinu Tam answered: since not written "do not take maidservant" but "there shall not be a sacred prostitute" — mamzer automatically sacred prostitute whose creation was in transgression; specifically mamzer to maidservant but mamzeret to slave not — children like her; slave has no lineage (emendator: Beit Shmuel wrote per Rabbeinu Tam mamzeret permitted to slave — so I saw Tosafot Gittin — end quote; not so — prohibition exists rabbinically per Rabbeinu Tam; only no Torah prohibition in mamzer automatically sacred prostitute; because of enactment for child permitted to marry maidservant; but mamzeret where no enactment for child — forbidden; Tosafot Gittin discusses half-slave half-free who also has no remedy — therefore challenged properly opening mamzeret since no Torah prohibition — end quote).
