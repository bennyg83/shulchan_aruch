Maidservant. But mamzeret forbidden to slave — for still the children are mamzerim. However Tosafot Gittin 45 s.v. le'isha shifatah; and Chagigah 2 Tosafot s.v. le'isha shifchah — implies permitted for slave to marry mamzeret — only children mamzerim — see there. Rashal part 3 siman 15 ruled: only definite mamzer; but safek mamzer forbidden with maidservant. But Radbaz part 1 siman 288 ruled even safek mamzer permitted with maidservant — see there.
