A mamzer who had relations with a gentile woman — the child is a gentile; if he converted, behold he is like an Israelite. If he had relations with a maidservant — the child is a slave; when freed, behold he is a freeman. Therefore a mamzer may ab initio marry a maidservant who accepted upon herself the commandments, immerse for slavery, to permit his sons so they will be freed and permitted to Israelites.
