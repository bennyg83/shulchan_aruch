Mamzer may marry l'chatchilah. the Rambam's language chapter 15; they did not decree maidservant for mamzer because of enactment for the child; implies answers Ran's question how mamzer marries maidservant; liable for all mitzvot; answered because of enactment for the child they permitted; per his reason who holds no Torah prohibition with maidservant as siman 16; only rabbinically forbidden they said; because of enactment permitted; Rabbeinu Tam holds Torah prohibition; therefore must give reason permitted with maidservant because written "there shall not be sacred prostitute" and he is sacred prostitute standing.
