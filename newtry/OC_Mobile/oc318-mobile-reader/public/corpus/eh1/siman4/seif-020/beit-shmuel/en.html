Mamzer may marry lekhatchilah. Rambam's language part 15 — they did not decree maidservant for mamzer because of enactment for child — implies answers Ran's question how mamzer marries maidservant — liable for all mitzvot; answered because of enactment for child they permitted; per his reason who holds no Torah prohibition with maidservant as siman 16 — only rabbinically forbidden they said; because of enactment permitted; Rabbeinu Tam holds Torah prohibition — therefore must give reason permitted with maidservant because written "there shall not be sacred prostitute" and he is sacred prostitute standing.
