In nations follow male. Beit Shmuel wrote; and asked why follow damaged when converted; can say derive verse "to them", Yevamos 78 we expound two "to them" written regarding Egyptian: one counts and one follows blemish. Chaim Bachur's sons challenged: why we rule Ammonite not Ammonit, "to them" regarding Amon and Moav why needed, no exposition to follow blemish except for practical difference marrying woman follow him; heard from "forever", if not follow him how find her: if married maidservant and gentiles child like her; Ammonit is Israelite and child is valid; Egyptian child not blemished except three generations; Netinah per Rema Torah Israelite; likewise Ramban. Per Rashi if bore male no difference; likewise if married mamzerot; if bore female, conversely blemish from mother not his side; "forever" why needed, well challenged; perforce truly not said for exposition, incidentally other as written "from me men remain" Pesach as Yevamos 74; and resident and hired worker 71a.
