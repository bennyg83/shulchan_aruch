A convert who married, etc. — even for kehunah he is fit, as the mishnah there; and they say there 75b and in Yevamot ch. HaChalutz and ch. Mitzvat Chalitzah: "his mother from Israel from among your brethren," etc.; and so Tosefta there 102a; all the more his father from Israel, as Kiddushin 74a: Israel who married, etc. chalal, etc.
