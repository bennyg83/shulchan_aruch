See responsum Bashamayim Rosh siman 5 asked: married woman who left religion and married Ishmaelite and had sons and afterward returned to Judaism — what law of sons when one wanted to say we are concerned perhaps she prostituted with Israelite; he z"l answered: my opinion does not agree that if she was presumed all Israel not presumed; even if compare to that fiancé and fiancée case and even Israelites suspected or apostates go to her house — nevertheless husband guards her and majority cohabitations after husband — that majority R' Gamliel and R' Yehoshua dispute is where she prostituted; but man who returned with wife regularly — one gentile one Israelite — his sons attributed after him — see there. See responsum Chemdat Shlomo siman 1 similar case in practice (in Ishmaelite land) — woman left husband agunah more than forty years, mixed among Ishmaelites, did not practice Judaism though did not convert — no Jewish spark; afterward bore daughter and raised among Ishmaelites — daughter did not know what nation until before death mother told her Jewish and commanded stand in Judaism and she did; constant rumor this daughter known Arab; witnesses say before pregnancy mother prostituted in village several times with one Arab and no Jews regularly there only merchants; also one Jew sometimes — what judgment of daughter if concerned perhaps impregnated from Israelite; though majority fit before her for lineage need two majorities as clear in Gemara and posekim. He answered at length and initially sided per Noda BiYehudah siman 5 (brought below seif 31) — if mother died and need rule only on child — one majority suffices and only for kehunah lineage need two majorities; our case preferable to Noda BiYehudah's for even if mother alive no difference against mother whether prostituted with Arab or Israelite — ultimately forbidden to husband; from other side our case worse than there per what he raised that majority alone does not help except with examining presumption prostitutes lest disqualify — not applicable here; nevertheless his reasoning not forced; truly main words wondrous — from Rambam almost explicit even if mother not examined until death, etc.; even per above our case preferable since never place to judge mother — can say child called separated; nevertheless very difficult if so child from divorcee and chalalah where no judging mother — not in shetuki, etc. (above seif 31 wrote even woman already divorcee has safek forbidden to kohen also as zona — see there; therefore not proof for our case no difference regarding mother); he expanded and concludes per Tosafot Nazir 12 only minority mamzerim called fixed called name disqualified not near minority — room to permit if known born at least in majority Ishmaelites though one majority; per testimony no clear evidence — difficult to be lenient; even Beit Shmuel (siman 6 s.k. 31) where known prostituted with fit — majority helps; in hagahot Gemara wrote even one majority not needed — nevertheless not helping here known prostituted with Arab — can say abandoned worse since presumed to prostitute; can side since main connection only Arabs and changed dress and did not hold Jewish religion — all clear in testimony — Tur's view as Beit Shmuel s.k. 43 and Noda BiYehudah — with Israelite not concerned for Arab and reason change of religions not so close; our case behaved totally Arabite and certainly distant from Israel congregation as testimony did not gather to Israel house — not concerned for Israel; also supports presumption of fitness of Israel and majority Israel not promiscuous — can say even Rambam's view (concerned for Arab) not concerned for Israel; again brought responsum Bashamayim Rosh (as above this s.k.) — clear there this reasoning and wrote seemingly unnecessary his reasoning husband guards and majority cohabitations — only where known suspected and apostates enter her house but where unknown not concerned prostituted with Israelite — likewise our case though majority cohabitations reason not helping except marriage path — this reasoning stronger all Israel not presumed, etc. — concludes this daughter permitted enter congregation;
