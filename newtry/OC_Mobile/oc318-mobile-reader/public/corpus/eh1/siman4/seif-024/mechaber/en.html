A mamzer may marry a mamzeret — in what case? When both are certain; but if one is certain and the other doubtful, or even both are doubtful — they are forbidden to one another, lest one is a mamzer and the other is not. And what is a doubtful mamzer? For example, one born from a doubtful forbidden relation — his father had relations with a woman whose betrothal was doubtful, or whose divorce was doubtful.
