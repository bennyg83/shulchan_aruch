L'chatchilah maidservant. See Baer Heitev in name Shakh that definite mamzer; see Bnei Chayyim what Nissim Yisraeli wrote in name great sage who ruled safek mamzer can purify his seed thus: Jew frees his maidservant on condition she marry fit man , then this safek mamzer can marry that maidservant if he is fit his seed fit and if mamzer maidservant not freed, etc. , see there (likewise Ram"a Mipano in article Chikur Din brought below seif 22 s.k. 25). What Baer Heitev wrote but Radbaz ruled even safek mamzer, etc. , see Birkei Yosef letter 12 who brought responsum Zera Avraham part 4 siman 27 decided like Radbaz safek mamzer permitted with maidservant and hung on Rambam's words; wondered on responsum Pnei Moshe part 3 siman 1 disputing Radbaz; he z"l wondered on him and Radbaz , from Rambam Tur and Maran above seif 17 plain safek mamzer forbidden with maidservant for there half-slave who cohabited with forbidden relative child only safek mamzer (from ruling below siman 44 s.k. 1 if betrothed safek betrothed and Tur in this law child mamzer must mean safek mamzer) and nevertheless child has no remedy and forbidden with maidservant , see there. Also Shaarei Melekh part 15 end halakhah 4 wondered on Radbaz and Zera Avraham , omitted words of Ran Ritva and Rashba beginning chapter Ten Yuchasin , from their words plain shetuki and asufi forbidden with maidservant , see there; in margin Shulchan Aruch of Rabbi Akiva Eiger z"l wrote in name responsum Ramban siman 288 like Radbaz even safek mamzer permitted with maidservant , see there.
