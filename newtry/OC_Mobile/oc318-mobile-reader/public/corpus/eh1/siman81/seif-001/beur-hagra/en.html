(ב) And some say, etc. From what is written, "and this disagrees with Reish Lakish," etc., "since he could," etc., it implies that according to Rav Huna, whom we hold like, it does not help when he merely says it, for we have heard from R. Meir that a person cannot remove, etc., and it is like saying. Rav Nachman there, and so is the Tur's view. But at first glance this requires study: why did he not distinguish that where she is actually being sustained and he says, "your hands should be consecrated to their maker," it is consecrated? See Tosafot there, s.v. "what," etc. Although Tosafot wrote this according to Reish Lakish, nevertheless the same applies for us where he says, "your hands should be consecrated to their maker"; so too from Rashi there, s.v. "what, is it not," etc., and so Ran. But the Tur's view follows the explanation of Rabbenu Yonah, brought at the end of siman 84, that regarding surplus she cannot say, "I will not work and I will not take a silver ma'ah," because there the matter is because of animosity. If so, what the Gemara says regarding one who is sustained is literal: she is sustained.
