Time of marriage of a virgin and her rejoicing, and a widow, and if he does not wish to make a feast. It contains 6 seifim. One who marries a virgin must rejoice with her seven days — he should not do work and should not trade in the market but eats and drinks and rejoices with her — whether he is a bachelor or a widower. And a groom is forbidden in doing work and forbidden to go out alone in the market (Ran first chapter of Kesubos and Pirkei R' Eliezer).
