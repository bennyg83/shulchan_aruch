One does not bring a virgin to chuppah on Shabbat because through chuppah he acquires her found articles and earnings of her hands — and it would be as acquiring kinyan on Shabbat. And a widow — chuppah does not acquire her except through seclusion of relations; he acquires her found articles and earnings of her hands; therefore he must be secluded with her before Shabbat so that it not be as acquiring kinyan on Shabbat (and see Orach Chayyim siman 339).
