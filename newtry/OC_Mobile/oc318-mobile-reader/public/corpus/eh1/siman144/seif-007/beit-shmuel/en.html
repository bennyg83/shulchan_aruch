She is believed. According to Rashi, the sugya speaks where we do not know whether he was secluded with her. Nevertheless, if she says the condition was fulfilled and her claim is supported by the circumstances, she may be believed for certain purposes. Other decisors limit this credibility where the husband can contradict her.
A get. It is clear that if he said "from now on condition," the get can take effect retroactively when the condition is fulfilled. If the condition was not fulfilled, or if the wording was not proper, the get is void or doubtful. The Ramban and other authorities discuss whether the husband’s later conduct can show that he intended the get to work, but the primary law depends on the wording at delivery.
