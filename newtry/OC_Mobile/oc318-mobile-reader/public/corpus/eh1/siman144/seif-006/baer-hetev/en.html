The week. Meaning, they write during the entire year after the shemitah cycle, but not afterward. Likewise, after a year, they write during the entire month after that year, and so for all of them. If he said, "If I do not come after the week, write and give," they should not write until the week and the first year of the next shemitah cycle have passed; and so for all of them. If they delayed longer, there is no concern. According to Rabbeinu Chananel, if he said, "This is your get after the week," it is not a get until the first year of the eighth cycle has passed, and the first month of the second year, and so for all of them. See Beit Shmuel.
