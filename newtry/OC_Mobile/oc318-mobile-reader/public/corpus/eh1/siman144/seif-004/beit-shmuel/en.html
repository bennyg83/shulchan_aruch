Doubt. Meaning that there is doubt whether the condition created an immediate get or whether it failed. Rabbi Yonah and other decisors explain that if the language creates real uncertainty, she is treated as doubtfully divorced, not as certainly divorced. The practical difference is whether she requires another get and whether later kiddushin take effect.
