Unmarried. Unmarried means widow or divorcee or non-virgin; but virgin permitted — Bach Choshen Mishpat Beit Yosef Beit Shmuel; uncovering head in courtyard no prohibition except modesty; see Beit Shmuel; coerced woman must cover head; all not yet lain with even if betrothed permitted go uncovered like other virgins — Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 103 and responsum Chavat Yair.
