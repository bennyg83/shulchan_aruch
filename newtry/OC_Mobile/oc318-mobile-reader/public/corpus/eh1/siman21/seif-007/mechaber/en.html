One who hugs or kisses one of forbidden relations upon whom man's heart does not knock — such as his adult sister and father's sister and similar — even if he has no pleasure at all — behold this is most disgraceful and forbidden matter and act of fools; for one does not draw near forbidden relations at all — whether adult or minor — except father to daughter and mother to son. How permitted for father to hug daughter and kiss her and sleep with her in flesh proximity — and likewise mother with son all while they are minors; when they grew and son became adult and daughter adult until breasts are formed and her hair sprouted — this one sleeps in his garment and she in her garment; and if daughter was ashamed to stand before father naked, or she became betrothed — and likewise if mother ashamed to stand before son naked — even though they are minors once they reached maturity of either of them they do not sleep with them except in their garments.
