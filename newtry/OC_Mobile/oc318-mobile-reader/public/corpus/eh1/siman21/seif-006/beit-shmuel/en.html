But permitted to ask her husband. And likewise to ask another about her welfare permitted; and that poskim wrote through her husband — because we find angels asked through Avraham — so Bach; however in name chapter HaSocher answered through husband different — implies asking another about her welfare forbidden — and likewise Choshen Mishpat; from this custom to write at beginning of letter "regarding your wife," etc.; but at end of letter customary to write "and say shalom to your wife" forbidden — for this is asking shalom sent through husband — Bach; Taz wrote if not in line of health permitted to write "inform me of your wife's welfare."
