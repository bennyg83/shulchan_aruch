Forbidden to use a woman at all — whether adult or minor, whether maidservant or freedwoman — lest he come to lewd thought of transgression; in which uses they said: washing his face hands and feet — even to pour water to wash face hands and feet even if she does not touch him; and spreading the bed before him and pouring the cup. {Rama: And some say likewise eating with her in one bowl forbidden with any forbidden relation like his niddah wife (Binyamin Zev siman 143); and some are lenient in all these for they only forbade affectionate matters with his niddah wife (Rashba 1108); and some say all this forbidden only in place of seclusion but where most people are found such as bathhouse permitted to bathe maidservants (Mordekhai chapter Ayeip in name Rash bar Baruch); and some say whatever he does not do with affection — only his intent for heaven's sake permitted — therefore custom to be lenient in these matters (Tosafot end Kiddushin); and some say one should not conduct even with his wife affectionate matters such as examine in her head for lice before others (Nimmukei Yosef chapter Chazakat HaBatim).}
