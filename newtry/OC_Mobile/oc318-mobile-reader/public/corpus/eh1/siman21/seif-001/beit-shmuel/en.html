Very greatly. Since man's soul desires them therefore wrote very greatly; and likewise Yoreh Deah regarding interest and Choshen Mishpat regarding bribery; even though Sages said reduction in forbidden relations — nevertheless if accustomed to this sin harder to separate than other transgressions; see Perishah siman 25.
Or to look at her beauty. R' Yonah wrote forbidden from Torah as stated "you shall not explore after your eyes"; and Rambam holds rabbinically; and with unmarried woman per all from words of Kabbalah; and lewd thought even with unmarried woman forbidden from Torah.
And shall not pass through doorway of prostitute. And if he is suspected regarding one woman — we warn him not to pass her doorway; and if he passes they lash him; see Beit Yosef what he wrote in name R' Yehudah of Orleans.
Voice of forbidden relation. But voice of unmarried woman or his wife's voice permitted — except at time of prayer forbidden as written Orach Chaim; see Perishah what he wrote in name Maharshal; and distinguish voice of forbidden relation forbidden but voice of her speech permitted; and if so from Talmud Kiddushin 70 — there voice of forbidden relation even in asking shalom forbidden; Rashba answered voice of speech when she responds to shalom inquiry is worse per Beit Hillel.
