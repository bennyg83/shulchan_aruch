At her beauty. R' Yonah wrote forbidden from Torah; Rambam holds rabbinically; with unmarried woman per all from Kabbalah; lewd thought even with unmarried forbidden from Torah.
After her. Meaning within her four cubits per R' Yonah and Beit Yosef; and Kenat HaGadol in name Radbaz part 2 wrote not sufficient to distance four cubits but all who is not distant that he does not recognize and distinguish her in walking and movements forbidden; this prohibition all men all places even where women walk covered foot to head — Kenat HaGadol in name Radbaz part 2.
Prostitute. And if suspected regarding one woman — warn him not to pass her doorway; if passes lash him; see Beit Yosef in name R' Yehudah of Orleans.
Voice of forbidden relation. But voice of unmarried or his wife permitted except at prayer forbidden as Orach Chaim; but in Be'er Sheva 119 wrote whether virgin or widow forbidden — only his wife's voice permitted not at prayer; distinguish voice of forbidden relation forbidden but voice of her speech permitted; if so from Talmud Kiddushin 70a voice of forbidden relation even in shalom inquiry forbidden; Rashba answered voice of speech responding to shalom worse per Beit Hillel.
