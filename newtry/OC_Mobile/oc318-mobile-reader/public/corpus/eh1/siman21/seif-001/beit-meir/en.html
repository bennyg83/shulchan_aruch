Encountered woman in market in Berakhot 61 and Eruvin market not mentioned but road; and Rambam added market; known in market seclusion inapplicable — therefore reason lewd thought; if so why Rav Yehudah challenged Rav — but Rav said with kosher men fine in Kiddushin chapter 1 — though with kosher no seclusion concern nevertheless after her forbidden lewd thought.
