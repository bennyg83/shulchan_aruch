Where he did not acquire the dowry. See Ba'er Heitev, regarding the second year if she died, etc. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 3, siman 122, who also disagrees with the Chelkat Mechokek and agrees with the Beit Shmuel. He writes that Responsa Maharit Tzahalon, siman 61, explicitly shows that whenever the husband’s heirs or the yavam inherit part of the dowry, they are obligated in her burial. See Responsa Shev Yaakov, siman 28, who agrees with the Chelkat Mechokek that the burial expense falls on both and rejects the Beit Shmuel’s proofs. Sefer Tiv Kiddushin also concludes that the main law is like the Chelkat Mechokek, that burial expenses are split, and likewise what was spent during her illness. See there.
