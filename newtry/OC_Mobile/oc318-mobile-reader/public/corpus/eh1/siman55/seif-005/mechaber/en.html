A betrothed woman who died — if the betrothed was a kohen he does not become impure for her; and if he died she is not obligated to become impure for him and he does not inherit her and he is not obligated in her burial; rather her father inherits her and her father buries her. {Rama: And likewise if she married in a place where he did not acquire dowry (the Rav's view) as explained end of seif 53 and see above siman 69 seif 5.}
