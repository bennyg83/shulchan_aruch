With the dowry. That is, where the dowry is in her father's house, or where the dowry must be returned within the first year because of the enactments of Shum, he need not bury her from his own funds, but from the dowry. This is where he inherits nothing, even usufruct property; see Responsa Maharil, siman 80. If she died in the second year, Chelkat Mechokek ruled that the burial is incumbent upon both of them, since her father and her husband divide the dowry. Beit Shmuel ruled that the husband alone is obligated in the burial expense; see there, and see siman 118.
