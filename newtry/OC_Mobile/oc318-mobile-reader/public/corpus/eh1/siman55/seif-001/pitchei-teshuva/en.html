She is forbidden to her husband. See Responsa Shev Yaakov, siman 24, regarding Reuven, who betrothed a woman and had relations with her without chuppah and the seven blessings, and had three sons from her. He wrote that he is compelled with all possible force to separate from her until he performs chuppah and the seven blessings, and we are not concerned that this will cast aspersions on his earlier sons. See there for what he wondered about Teshuvot Maimoniyot’s explanation of "a bride without blessing," and also about Beit Shmuel, s.k. 1. See Sefer Beit Meir on this.
