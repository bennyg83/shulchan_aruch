Even after several days. See Beit Shmuel, s.k. 8, in the name of the Perishah: because this blessing does not mention anything about nisuin, he may recite it afterward, unlike the blessing of erusin. See Responsa Radbaz, part 5, siman 2326, who gives another answer: here it speaks where he did not have relations with her. Although she is fully married, he is still forbidden to have relations with her until they recite the seven blessings. Therefore, although her marriage was completed for other matters, the blessing is still before the act. This is unlike the blessing of erusin. However, Radbaz himself in part 1, siman 372, did not write this, but rather that one may have relations after the chuppah even without birkat chatanim, and this is precise in the Rambam’s language. According to that, one must rely on the Perishah’s answer.
