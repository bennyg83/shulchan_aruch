From unencumbered property. This is the Rambam’s view. But the Rosh in Perek Af Al Pi disagrees and holds that regarding the one hundred or two hundred, it is a complete shtar and is collected from encumbered property. Regarding the additional ketubah, even if he wrote it for her, she does not receive it from erusin, whether she was widowed or divorced. What the Derishah wrote in siman 66 in the name of Maharshal, that the Itur holds that if she was divorced she receives the additional ketubah if he wrote it for her, see what I wrote there.
But the custom is, etc. Meaning that an arusah has no ketubah unless he wrote one for her. With a betrothed match, even if he wrote one for her and they retracted before erusin, she does not receive even the one hundred or two hundred, because he wrote it only on condition that he marry her. So wrote the Rivash in a responsum.
