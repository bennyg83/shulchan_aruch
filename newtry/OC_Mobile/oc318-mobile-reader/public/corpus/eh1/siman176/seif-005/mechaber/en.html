If a woman had sons and whose daughter-in-law also had sons, and they each gave birth to a son in a [wartime] hideout, and the parentage of the two boys became mixed up, and the boys grew up and got married, and both later died, the surviving sons of the daughter-in-law should perform chalitzah on the two widows before those of her mother-in-law, but her sons should not perform yibbum. After the sons of the daughter-in-law perform chalitzah, the sons of the mother-in-law may perform either chalitzah or yibbum.
