Two men who married two sisters, and neither of them know which sister her married, and they die(the men), each of their brother performs the refusal ceremony for both of them and neither of them can do yibbum. If one of the men had one brother and the other, two, the one with one brother can do the refusal ceremony with both sisters and the one with two brothers, one of them can do the refusal ceremony with one sister first and then afterward, the second brother can do the yibbum. If the two brothers expedited the process and married the sisters after the one brother did the refusal ceremony, we don't divorce them from their hands, even if they are both priests. If both men had two brothers each, one of the brothers of one performs the refusal ceremony and the other brother performs the yibbum with aforementioned refused sister and the other second brother also performs the yibbum with the one his brother refused. If the two brothers expedited the process and performed the refusal service, the other two brothers cannot perform yibbum to the two women. Rather, one performs the refusal ceremony and one performs yibbum. If the two other brothers preceded and married the two women after the first two brothers performed the refusal ceremony, we do not remove them from their hands.
