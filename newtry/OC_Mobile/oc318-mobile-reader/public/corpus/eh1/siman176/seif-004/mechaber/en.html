One who's husband goes overseas, and she hears that he died [and upon receiving that information] she remarries and [after she remarried] her husband [who was thought dead comes afterwards] and then both of them [the ex-husband and current husband] die, for this brother [of the current husband] and for this brother [of the ex husband] brother of this one and this one preform chalitzah and levirite marriage is not preformed. this is the rule for the other wife see further on in Siman 183 Seif 11.
