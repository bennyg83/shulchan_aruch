If she was betrothed with her father's consent and married without his consent — whether her father is here or went to a distant land — they are not married. And if she dies he does not inherit her and he may not become impure for her. {Rama: And some say the same applies if the father died before she married and then she married (thus it appears in Mordekhai ch. 2 of Kiddushin in the name of some rabbis).}
