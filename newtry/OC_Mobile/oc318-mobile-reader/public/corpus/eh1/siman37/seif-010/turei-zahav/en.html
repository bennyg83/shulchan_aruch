Get from both — Maharshal wrote: this is if the first agent's kiddushin came first; but if the second came first she needs no get from the first — for perforce either the second canceled the first and the first's kiddushin are not kiddushin, or he did not cancel the first but did not rely on the first agent thinking the first will not find the woman — but if he finds, since the second came first the first is not found and the second's are kiddushin and the first's are not kiddushin — end quote; and it is not clear to me what he told us — in that case where he did not cancel the first, his intent is that if the first does not accept, perhaps we say the first agent is more pleasing to the sender for he will certainly do what is good in his eyes more than the second; rather if the first will not accept kiddushin at all then the second must accept for him — if both accept he prefers the first in any case; perforce in every manner she needs a get from both as seems plain to me. And Rashal wrote the husband should appoint an agent after an agent — such is the law.
