Or through herself etc. — ch. 40, even with a document; and per Ramban and Rashba who wrote from there ch. 9 halakhah 1, and Rav etc., "your daughter is betrothed" etc., whether through herself etc. — he wrote it discusses both na'arah and minor: na'arah because of agency, and minor not because of agency but because he said to her go out and accept etc., similar to saying give etc.; but Ran disputes them and wrote go out and accept does not help except with money and not with a document, for it is not from the Torah of benefit etc., see there; but per the view of Tosafot and Rosh one can say a hand to accept for herself works — except Ramban wrote because of give etc., as above, for Tosafot's reason is difficult at first glance, for the kiddushin revert to her father; and see Rashba there, two.
And if it is visible etc. — for likewise he acts with her father's consent, as written in Yevamot 114 regarding a minor who acts etc., for it is as feeding him by hand; and Rashi there s.v. one who acts etc.
And there is no distinction etc. — as written in Kiddushin 19a, even though he said to R' Yehuda bar Yehuda, likewise even per the rabbis in this seif — Rosh there explained.
But some dispute etc. — for they hold specifically per R' Yehuda bar Yehuda; and see in Mordekhai there who agreed with the first view and proved from what is written in ch. 2 that if the father consented, certainly the father's kiddushin are kiddushin, all the more in such a case — and all poskim hold so.
And in order etc. — for it is as etc., as above.
For some say etc. — as written above ch. 1 halakhah 1; and Maharam wrote that to accept kiddushin herself there is no prohibition.
And when betrothing etc. — likewise Ramban and Rashba explained; even though they say ch. 9 halakhah 1 "your daughter is betrothed to me" etc., whether through herself — they mentioned it for amplification; but "behold you" is also betrothed, as written 19b; and this is what he wrote and if he said etc. Rashba wrote: from there ch. 9 halakhah 1 "your daughter is betrothed to me" — do not say that all the more if he writes behold you, as written 19b etc.; rather they mentioned it for amplification. And further because of the beginning "whether through her father" — there if he wrote behold you she is not betrothed; and likewise in the conclusion behold you are betrothed etc., whether etc., specifically "you" but "your daughter," whether through herself or through her father she is not betrothed, like in a get: when writing through an agent one must write behold you are divorced. It appears from their words that for his minor or na'arah daughter, likewise in the statement of kiddushin if he said your daughter, even in her own acceptance; and likewise kiddushin with money — for their proof is from what is written 19b in kiddushin with money. But Ran disputes them and wrote like the conclusion regarding a bogeret, whether through herself or through her father one must write behold you; and in a statement through herself he must say behold you, and to her father in that [form] like with a divorce agent; likewise for minor and na'arah through herself in a document he writes your daughter, and in a statement he says behold you; and likewise kiddushin with money. The rule: Ran disputes Ramban and Rashba — all this when she is not of age, through herself; for per their view in all cases it helps, and per Ran in a document he must write your daughter and in a statement, and likewise with money he must say you.
And if he betrothes her with a document — not like Ran who disputes Ramban and said specifically with money one may say to his minor daughter go out etc., and as Raavad explained it is as saying give on a sela or to a dog, but not with a document; but Tosafot there and Rosh there wrote because of a hand — through another's consent she has a hand; and see Tosafot there s.v. he says etc.; and further Beit Yosef brought Rosh from what is written in Sukkah 46b "let him not acquire" etc.
He writes in the document etc. — Ran delved into this and disputes Ramban who holds even with a document he writes behold you are betrothed, and distinguished: just as one who gives a get to an agent writes in the get the woman's name and when he gives it he speaks with the agent — so too here he must write your daughter; and see there where he elaborated and the Rav explained bedieved like Ramban, for Ran too is only uncertain. And when they send etc., as above; and if it is visible etc. — it is no worse than if the father consented before kiddushin, for all agree there are kiddushin.
