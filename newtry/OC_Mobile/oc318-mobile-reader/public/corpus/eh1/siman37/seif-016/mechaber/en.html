One who has two daughters, even if both are minors, and he did matchmaking for one of them to one man, and after a time accepted kiddushin from him and said: Your daughter, unspecified, is betrothed to me — even if both were occupied with her, both are forbidden to him and need a get from him. {Rama: And even if he corrected himself within kedei dibbur and specified your daughter so-and-so it does not help (Mordekhai beginning Kiddushin in the name of Raavyah). And in all such cases where there are kiddushin that cannot lead to consummation we compel him to divorce (Rashba 236).} One who betrothes his daughter unspecified — the bogeret is not included.
