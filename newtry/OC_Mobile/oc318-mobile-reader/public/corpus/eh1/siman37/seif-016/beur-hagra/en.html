Who is this — see Tosafot 12a s.v. and the halakhah etc., and they brought proof from what is written in the Tosefta: two brothers who betrothed two sisters, this one does not know which he betrothed and that one does not know which he betrothed — both are forbidden from safek; if they were occupied with the elder for the elder and the younger for the younger, I say the elder was betrothed only to the elder and the younger only to the younger; and thus we rely on matchmaking here; and proof that we rely on assessment of intent — betrothed through robbery of her hand; and see in Maggid Mishneh where he elaborated proofs in the name of R' Simchah; but Rashba and nevertheless and Ran rejected the proof from the Tosefta, for it does not apply there, for there at the time of kiddushin they knew, unlike here where matters of the heart are not matters — and Hagahot Maimoniyot.
Even if they were occupied etc. — Mordekhai ch. 2, Hagahot Semag, not like Raavyah and Ran who permit in such a case, for we hold like R' Yosi that one need not specify; and with this Ran rejected the above proof from Tosafot — also there "occupied" etc.
And even if he corrected himself etc. — for we hold regarding kiddushin within kedei dibbur etc., as written at the end of Nedarim and ch. 8 of Bava Batra.
And all such cases etc. — as written in Yevamot 105: "or now you are disqualified from me, chalutz" etc., there.
