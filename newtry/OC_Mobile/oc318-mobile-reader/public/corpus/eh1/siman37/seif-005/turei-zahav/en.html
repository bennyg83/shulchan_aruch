That even if she does not contradict him — Tur brought two views: Rosh is one view and RaMAh is the other; and one must explain RaMAh's view — he holds specifically when she contradicts him we say her kiddushin are kiddushin, for we hold like Rav and conclude they dispute; and perforce Beit Yosef wrote it is established that Rav Yosef acted according to when she contradicts him. I do not know who inserted this, for the Gemara only seeks to prove there is a dispute between Rav and Shmuel — if Shmuel holds like Rav, why was Shmuel angry at R' Yosef who acted like Rav; rather certainly there is a dispute and Rav Yosef acted like Rav, and since we are not compelled to say they dispute except from the anger — we have before us one of two paths: either say they dispute when she contradicts and when she does not contradict Rav concedes there is safek — then we have no reason to be lenient like Rav except when she contradicts, but when she does not contradict per his words there is safek; or say they dispute when she does not contradict and when she contradicts all agree to be lenient — then for halakhah we are lenient in every matter, meaning when she contradicts all agree and when she does not contradict like Rav to be lenient; and one can also say they dispute in both cases — then we hold to be lenient in both like Rav; and since there are grounds to be stringent and lenient we listen only to be stringent — this is RaMAh's view. I wonder at Rosh who took the path of leniency; and I saw strained explanations in RaMAh's words without need and without precision.
