If her father betrothed her in the morning and she betrothed herself in the evening, and both on the day that completed the six months of na'aruth, and they examine her and find her to be a bogeret — they hold her with the presumption of bogeret and the father's kiddushin are void. And some say that even if she does not contradict him to say that the signs came in the morning. And some say that specifically if she contradicts him; but if she does not contradict him she needs a get from both.
