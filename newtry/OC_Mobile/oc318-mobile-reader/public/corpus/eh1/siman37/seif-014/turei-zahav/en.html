These are kiddushin — as if her father died, for the rabbis enacted kiddushin for a minor; and some hold they enacted only specifically when the father died.
His relations are not relations of promiscuity — even though siman 1 Tur wrote before age thirteen one should not marry them for it is like promiscuity — there it discusses when the father is with her he should not marry them in minority; but here where he is not with her they enacted marriage so she not be promiscuous as above; and Rama already wrote in seif 8 the custom of our time.
Unless the father betrothed her — meaning it is known the father betrothed her; but presumptively we are not concerned lest the father betrothed her, for we establish her in presumption of unmarried — otherwise every minor whose father died in a distant land would be forbidden to marry forever lest the father betrothed her to someone; rather perforce we are not concerned for this — Tosafot wrote ch. 2 in name of R' Tam who answered Rabbenu Menachem.
What she did is done — it seems plain that all this when she did nothing — he can object going forward that she not be betrothed in minority, for it is stated above that the sender can cancel the agent he sent to betroth — likewise here he cancels what he commanded her.
