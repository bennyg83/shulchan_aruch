And all in his authority to betroth them — Beit Yosef explained: meaning all are minors, or all are bogerot and gave him permission to betroth them; but bogeret in place of minor was already explained — even if he made her an agent she is not included — end quote; meaning because he does not abandon the mitzvah incumbent upon him as above.
From the eldest of the eldest — for a person does not put himself in doubt to mention the name eldest unless the eldest of all; lest the matter be forgotten and there be doubt.
