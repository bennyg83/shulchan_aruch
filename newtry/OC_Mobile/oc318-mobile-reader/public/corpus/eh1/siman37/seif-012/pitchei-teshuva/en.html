And some say that if the groom etc. — see Choshen Mishpat who wrote it discusses when two came etc.; and see Beit Shmuel; and see in Beit Meir who wrote there is further practical difference if he said after death of so-and-so who stood before him he is not believed and she is forbidden to everyone: (12) And I betrothed her — he is believed. In the margin of Shulchan Aruch of the gaon R' Akiva Eiger: in Yerushalmi — if he said "I betrothed one of my daughters" and one came and said "I betrothed so-and-so your daughter" — he is believed to marry her (there it says: not all the time is he believed). And in Ketzot HaChoshen he wrote to emend; and in Hagahot Maharit the main text is as this version — end quote.
