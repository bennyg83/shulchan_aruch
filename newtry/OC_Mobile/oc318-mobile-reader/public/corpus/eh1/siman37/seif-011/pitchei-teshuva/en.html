The father who said. See Maggid Mishneh ch. 9 of Laws of Marriage law 15 — the editor brought from there Rashdam siman 9 who wrote that the father of the daughter who says the young man was adult when he betrothed his daughter is believed to forbid his daughter even if witnesses say he was a minor at that time; and he brought proof from teshuvah of Rashba brought in Yoreh Deah siman 1 regarding a slaughterer who said he did not slaughter the animal etc. — see there. And see in Sha'arei Mitzvah there — he brought that Rav Meir Teomim part 1 siman 65 wondered on Rashdam, for the whole principle of shavya anachda d'am is from laws of vows of prohibition — if so, in that case of slaughter Rashba properly wrote per his view she is forbidden because whenever he said he did not slaughter, even though witnesses slaughtered, behold he forbade it upon himself as if he did not slaughter; but not so in Rashdam's case — after witnesses that he was a minor and the kiddushin are invalid, how can he make it kiddushin contrary to the witnesses; and here the law of vows of prohibition does not apply at all. And he wrote his words are astonishing and he omitted teshuvah of Rashba that Beit Yosef brought siman 48 (should be 58) regarding one who claims he betrothed the woman and at time of giving said to her "be betrothed to me with this" even though witnesses contradict him and say they gave it as deposit etc. — behold even though the reason of vows of prohibition does not apply, nevertheless we say shavya anachda d'am even against witnesses — uncertain. And I brought somewhat of these words also in Pithei Teshuva Yoreh Deah siman 1 s.k. 15 — see there. And see in Netziv — I elaborated on this matter and wrote there that Sha'arei Mitzvah wondered on Rav Meir Teomim from Rashba's words — this requires investigation; nevertheless without that Rav Meir Teomim's words are incorrect in holding the reason of shavya anachda d'am is from vows of prohibition — this is not true; the main reason is because a person is believed about himself more than one hundred witnesses, as also written in Noda BiYehudah second edition Choshen Even HaEzer siman 43 that I brought in Pithei Teshuva Yoreh Deah there; and in Rashdam's case, since Torah believed the father as witness for his daughter, behold he is as one who testifies about himself — and as I wrote siman 48 s.k. 2.
