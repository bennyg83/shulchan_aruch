A minor or na'arah who betrothed herself without her father's knowledge, or married herself — it is nothing; and even mi'un is not needed. And even if her father did matchmaking first. And even if the father explicitly consented after the kiddushin. And some say that if the father consented when he heard, there are kiddushin from the moment of hearing, even if he did not do matchmaking. And even if he did not consent immediately when he heard but was silent and did not object, and afterward consented — the end proves the beginning and there are kiddushin from the moment of hearing, even if the money was spent before hearing. {Rama: And some say that if the money was spent before, there are no kiddushin (Mordekhai chapter HaIsh Mekadesh in the name of Rashbam, Raavyah, and Raban).} This applies when neither she nor her father objected before he consented. But if she or her father objected before he consented, there are no kiddushin even if the father consents afterward. {Rama: And some say that if the father heard and was silent and did not object immediately it is as if he consented (there). And if the father did not hear or went to a distant land and the daughter grew and was silent and did not object — the kiddushin grew with her and she needs a get (Rivash siman 479). And some say we require that he have relations with her after she grows (Rashba siman 1219). One should be stringent like the first view.}
