And her brothers and mother married her off — see in teshuvah Radbaz part 1 siman 45: Reuven betrothed his minor daughter and went to a distant land, and her mother and brothers come to marry her off — may they recite the blessings? He answered: per view of She'elot uTeshuvot certainly you need not ask, for since there are kiddushin it is plain the blessings are not void; but even per those who say there are no kiddushin, nevertheless it seems to me permitted to bless lekhatchilah and the blessings are not void for several reasons — see there.
