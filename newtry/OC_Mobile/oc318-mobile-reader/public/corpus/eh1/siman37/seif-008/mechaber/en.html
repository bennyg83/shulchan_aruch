It is a mitzvah that one not betroth his daughter while she is a minor until she grows and can say: In so-and-so I want. {Rama: And some say that in our time the practice is to betroth our minor daughters because we are in exile and we do not always have sufficient means for dowry needs; also we are few and do not always find a worthy match (Tosafot beginning HaIsh Mekadesh), and such is the practice.}
