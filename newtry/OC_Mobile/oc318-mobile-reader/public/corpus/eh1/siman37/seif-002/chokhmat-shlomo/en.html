Seif 7, in the gloss "and if he said your daughter is betrothed to me it is kiddushin for she stands in place of her father" etc. — nb: see in Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel; and from Beit Shmuel's view it appears one may speak with the agent as with the principal, but per Chelkat Mechokek it is not so; and see in my work on the Torah, parashat Vayera year 1797 on the verse "and Sarah denied," on the midrash that teaches there that from here women are disqualified from testimony — there I elaborated on this, with God's help, and brought support from Yalkut on Yitro on the verse "and he said to Moses I am your father-in-law Jethro coming to you," that R' Eliezer and R' Yosi dispute whether he sent an agent or wrote "sent" — we wrote there they dispute the dispute of Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel whether one may speak with the agent as if he were the principal; see there with good reason, with God's help, and examine.
