And it appears to me specifically within kedei dibbur — as will be explained nearby seif 25: there "I divorced her" must be within kedei dibbur of "I betrothed her"; here regarding disqualified witnesses he is believed even after kedei dibbur. Beit Yosef explicitly disputes; Rama here says it helps from kedei dibbur while Shulchan Aruch ruled afterward betrothed her to another even after kedei dibbur. One can distinguish: in I betrothed and I divorced there is no excuse on the first statement, only conclusion of words — therefore only within kedei dibbur; but regarding disqualified witnesses the excuse is on the statement I betrothed her (they were disqualified) — the mouth that forbade permitted; proof from Ketubot "that woman." One can ask from end of Gittin that excuse at time of rumor is required — there the rumor came from elsewhere; here he produced the rumor himself, therefore excuse helps even afterward. Shulchan Aruch properly ruled in both; Rama's words here lack precision. Darkei Moshe only brought Beit Yosef that there is dispute between the two cases — truth as explained above; one must distinguish excuse from I betrothed and I divorced. Per my explanation: believed within kedei dibbur saying I divorced after I betrothed in migo he could say disqualified witnesses — but whoever was established betrothed, migo does not impair strong presumption (many places in Choshen Mishpat). Levush's migo reason is incorrect — if so what does Rashba challenge from end of Gittin where there is no migo; rather even saying first I betrothed her to so-and-so, excuse breaks first statement; proof Ketubot ch. 2 — woman with improper men, later betrothed herself, believed she first lied because of them — excuse, not migo; disqualified witnesses — excuse helps afterward, mouth that forbade permitted per Rashba.
