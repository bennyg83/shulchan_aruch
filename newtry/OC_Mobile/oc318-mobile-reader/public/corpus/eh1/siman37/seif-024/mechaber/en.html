A woman who said: I betrothed myself and I do not know to whom, and one man came and said: I betrothed her — he is believed to give her a get to permit her to the world, but not to marry her; but if he married her we do not take her from him. {Rama: And if there is one witness that he betrothed her, he is permitted to marry her ab initio (Ran chapter HaOmer).}
