The bogeret is not included — for a person does not abandon a mitzvah incumbent upon him, meaning to marry her off — as written "give your daughters to men"; and he performs a mitzvah not incumbent upon him, meaning the bogeret; and when she said "from so-and-so" there is somewhat reason he means her; and we hold kiddushin not consummated — meaning he forbade in each perhaps he betrothed her sister — even so they are kiddushin and all need a get.
And the money be his — all the more if the money is not his, for certainly he does not abandon betrothing the minor whose money is his and betroths the bogeret that is not his; and Tur concluded: the minor's kiddushin are certain — meaning if he has only one minor daughter certainly she is betrothed; but if he has two minors the law is as written in the next seif.
