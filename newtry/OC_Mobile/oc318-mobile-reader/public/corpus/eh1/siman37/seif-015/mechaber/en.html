One who betrothes his daughter unspecified, or says to him: One of your daughters is betrothed to me — the bogeret is not included, even if she gave him permission to accept her kiddushin and that the money be his; but only if she made him an agent to accept kiddushin unspecified and did not say from so-and-so. But if she said from so-and-so, she too is safek betrothed, and the rest of the bogerot are not included.
