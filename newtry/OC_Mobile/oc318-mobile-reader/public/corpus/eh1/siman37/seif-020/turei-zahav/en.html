And afterward said he recognizes him etc. — Beit Yosef in name of R' Yerucham in name of Tosafot on this: he said "I betrothed my daughter" and was silent, and after an hour said to this man — he is not believed, for since he did not say immediately within kedei dibbur it implies he certainly does not recognize his groom and therefore did not explain immediately — end quote; implies within kedei dibbur helps. Difficult from what Rama wrote seif 16 even if he returned within kedei dibbur and said "your daughter so-and-so" it does not help, for regarding kiddushin we do not say as speech that bled; and it seems above he concluded his speech first saying "your daughter is betrothed to me" but afterward came to explain; in this it does not help; and likewise if he comes to support his first words like that case in ch. 13 folio 123; but here he said "I betrothed my daughter" — there is no conclusion yet, rather the conclusion is in what he said "to this man" — perforce it helps if within kedei dibbur for it is all one speech; if so likewise above "betrothed to me your daughter" within kedei dibbur he concluded so-and-so — likewise it helps as seems plain to me; and see siman 49 what I wrote there.
