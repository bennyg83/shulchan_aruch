A father who said: I betrothed my daughter and I do not know to whom — she is forbidden to the whole world until he says: It became known to me to whom I betrothed her, even if he does not find out until she comes of age. {Rama: And some say that if the groom stood before him first and he did not recognize him, and afterward said he recognizes him — he is not believed (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot).}
