The father etc. — ch. 2 halakhah 1, and Rashi there s.v. when she is a na'arah etc., and as explained in the mishnah 64a.
Without her knowledge — ch. 1 and 67.
And her kiddushin etc. — there ch. 3 halakhah 2, and Ketubot 46b.
If she was widowed etc. — Ketubot 43b.
Until she comes of age — as written in seif 2.
From the day she is born — Yerushalmi ch. 3 on the mishnah "If she bore" etc.; a daughter one day old may be betrothed with money; and they brought Rosh, meaning to exclude bi'ah (Beit Yosef).
Some say there are no etc. — Niddah 23a, and not like Rashi there s.v. stringently, for nevertheless there is a practical difference regarding other relatives.
