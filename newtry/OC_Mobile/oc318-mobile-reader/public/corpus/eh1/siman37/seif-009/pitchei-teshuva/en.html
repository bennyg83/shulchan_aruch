She too is safek betrothed. See Maggid Mishneh ch. 7 of Laws of Marriage end of law 20; and what the editor brought from there Rashdam siman 9 — he brought somewhat above siman 35 seif 7: (9) "I betrothed to you my eldest daughter" etc. — in the margin of Shulchan Aruch of the gaon R' Akiva Eiger: in Turei Even he wrote that if he said "I betrothed to you one daughter of my daughters" and forgot which — the betrother is not believed to say "this is the one I betrothed," and it is not like below seif 22. (There in Turei Even he wrote the reason: in the law of seif 22 he does not remove her from her presumption, but here he removes the others from safek — one witness is not believed. And there in Tosafot R' Akiva letter 41 he brought proof for this from Ran in Gittin that even in such a case where clarification is needed, one witness is not believed — see there. And at first glance this law is explained in Yerushalmi that Rambam brought in seif 22 below; and one can say because it was possible to distinguish — for there the father too is uncertain to whom, and see in Perush HaMishnayot on Yerushalmi ch. 2 of Yevamot halakhah 11 — examine.)
