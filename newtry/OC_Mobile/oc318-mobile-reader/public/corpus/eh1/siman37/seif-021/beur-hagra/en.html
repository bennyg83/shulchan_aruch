Before the father — Rashi there s.v. even believed etc.
Since the father etc. — even though he has an interest and his testimony is nothing, here he is only clarifying his father's words; and as written in Yerushalmi: the mishnah disputes him — one who brings a get from a distant land and says in my presence and in my presence he may not marry his wife; and they answer there: there she was presumed a married woman, but here she was not presumed except before two — when two come and say so-and-so betrothed, meaning this is only when clarifying the father's statement etc. — Ran; and in Shulchan Aruch: but if the father etc.; and see there "he trembles" because the father is believed to contradict him, and as Rashi wrote there s.v. even etc. and s.v. he trembles etc.
