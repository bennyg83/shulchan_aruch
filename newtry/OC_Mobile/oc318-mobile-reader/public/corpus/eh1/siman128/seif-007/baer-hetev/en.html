(ב) His name. This means that most people call him only by one name; then only that one is written. This is specifically where the minor name was lost. But if the major name was lost, both names are mentioned. If some people call him by this name and some by that name, they write the major name alone and do not write both, because perhaps the major name is primary and writing both would be a change. In the reverse there is no concern, because the major name is not displaced. Levush. Regarding the name of a river or city pronounced with a soft kaf, although in these countries we pronounce chet like a soft kaf, one should nevertheless write a soft kaf, because in some countries chet is pronounced like heh. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 231. A river or city whose name is like two names, such as Regensburg and the like, is one name and should be written as one. See the relevant responsa and Darkei Moshe regarding local custom in get names.
