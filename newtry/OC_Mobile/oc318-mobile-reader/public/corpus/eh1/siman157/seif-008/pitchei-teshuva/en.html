To betroth and stipulate with a condition. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, end of siman 56, which gives the formula for conditional kiddushin in a case where a brother abandoned the religion, with all of its details; see there. See also Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, siman 93; Responsa Tzelot HaBayit at the end of Sefer Beit Meir, siman 6; Responsa Me'il Tzedakah, siman 1; Responsa Chatam Sofer, part 1, Even HaEzer, simanim 110 and 111, and in part 2, siman 68; and Responsa Torat Netanel, siman 36. I saw in Tashbetz, part 2, siman 17, that he questioned this remedy and wrote: this ruling is astonishing to me, etc., for this is a condition against what is written in the Torah, etc., and so it is stated in the Yerushalmi, Perek HaPo'alim, etc.; see there. Also Responsa Geonei Batra'ei, end of siman 32, questioned this very much; see there. However, as explained in the responsa of the great later authorities mentioned above, this remedy is clear and without any question. See further Responsa Shivat Tzion, simanim 70, 71, and 72, and Responsa Maharim of Brisk, siman 27, on this matter.
