Even a mamzer. Ba'er Heitev cites the Beit Shmuel and related authorities. The law is that even where the yavam is a mamzer or otherwise blemished, the zikkah can exist and chalitzah may be required. The discussion is whether the yevamah is exempt because of the yavam’s status, or whether the mitzvah of chalitzah remains. Where there is a doubt, she may require chalitzah because we do not dismiss the bond without proof.
