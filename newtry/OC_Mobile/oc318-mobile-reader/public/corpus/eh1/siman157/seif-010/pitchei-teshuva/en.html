Brothers. The Beit Shmuel cites Nimukei Yosef and other decisors regarding whether we presume the existence or nonexistence of brothers. Where a man was known to have no brothers, there is no zikkah; where the matter is uncertain, the presumption of prohibition or permission depends on the facts. The halacha distinguishes between a known family situation, a later rumor, and testimony that appears only after the husband’s death.
