A man on his deathbed who said, "Give two hundred zuz to my wife as is appropriate for her," she takes them in addition to her ketubah [monies]. If he said, "She should take two hundred zuz for her ketubah," she does not receive both (the 200 zuz plus her ketubah) amounts, but she has the upper hand: If two hundred zuz is more than her ketubah, she receives the two hundred. If the ketubah is more, she takes the ketubah monies.
