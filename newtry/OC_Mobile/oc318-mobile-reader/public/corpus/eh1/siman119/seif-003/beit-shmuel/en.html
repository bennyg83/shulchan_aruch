(א) He should not divorce, etc. The Tur wrote: "When a man takes a wife," etc. Beit Hillel hold that even if she burned his dish he may divorce her, for he expounds the verse this way: "he found in her ervat davar" means either ervah or a matter in which she sinned against him, etc. In what case is this said? With a second match. But with a first match one should not hasten to divorce her, for R. Eliezer said: whoever divorces his first wife, the altar sheds tears. This implies that Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel disagree regarding a second match; there Beit Hillel hold that it is permitted to divorce because of a "matter." But with a first match Beit Hillel agree that it is forbidden to divorce because of a "matter." The verse "ervat davar" speaks of a second match, and then it is permitted to divorce because of ervah or because of a matter. According to this, beit din should initially compel him not to divorce his first wife because of a mere matter.
