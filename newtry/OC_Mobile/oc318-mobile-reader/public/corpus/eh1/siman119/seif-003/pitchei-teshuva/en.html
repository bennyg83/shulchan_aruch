And it is not proper for him to hurry. See Chelkat Mechokek, s.k. 2, who writes that even with the woman’s consent there may be no prohibition, but nevertheless it is not proper to do so. Beit Meir writes that in his humble opinion this is specifically when he is the one demanding the divorce. In that case one can say that even though she agrees, her heart is pained but she cannot refuse. But when she is the one demanding it, it is obviously completely permitted.
