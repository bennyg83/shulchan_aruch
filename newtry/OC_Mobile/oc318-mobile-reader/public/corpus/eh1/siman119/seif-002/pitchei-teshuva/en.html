A matter of ervah. See Baer Heitev. Regarding what he wrote in the name of Rashdam, that this is specifically with a married woman, etc., see Mishneh LaMelekh, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Gerushin law 21, who wrote that according to Rashdam, with an arusah, even if he did not find another more beautiful than her, he may divorce her. However, Rashdam's words are difficult in his eyes, and in Responsa Re'em II, siman 1, it implies that his view is not so; see there further. This was already discussed in Sefer Get Pashut, seif katan 7; see what he wrote on this. See also Responsa Shivat Tziyon, simanim 88 and 89, who discussed this at great length. Regarding what Baer Heitev wrote in seif katan 4 in the name of Peri Chadash, that a first pairing means specifically when she is a virgin, see Sefer Beit Meir, who wrote that the language of the Gemara is also precise this way: first pairing means the first pairing from both sides of the match; see there.
