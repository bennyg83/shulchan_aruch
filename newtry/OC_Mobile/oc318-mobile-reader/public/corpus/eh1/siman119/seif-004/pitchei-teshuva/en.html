He may divorce her without her consent. See Responsa Brit Avraham, siman 109, who wrote that from the words of the Rosh, Gittin chapter 2 siman 5, regarding a postdated Get, one can derive a new law: a husband cannot divorce a woman against her will conditionally, such as, from today if I die within two years, and the like. For when he divorces conditionally, the law is that he may not seclude himself with her, as below in siman 148 seif 2, and if so she loses her conjugal rights. She does not consent to that; rather, he must either divorce her completely or remain with her. He cannot force his wife to accept a conditional divorce while she remains bound to him, all the more so from what the Rosh wrote there, that according to Raavad he cannot divorce her against her will with a postdated Get. See there at length.
