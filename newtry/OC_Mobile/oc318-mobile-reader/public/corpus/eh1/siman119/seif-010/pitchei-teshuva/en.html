In a place of mitzvah. See Tiferet Yisrael on the Mishnayot, end of Seder Nashim, in his hints to Even HaEzer for this siman. He writes that according to Responsa Maharshal, even a woman who cannot become tahor for her husband may not be divorced against her will. But Responsa Maharchash, siman 33, Mayim Amukim, siman 32, and Pleitat Beit Yosef write that whenever he cannot live with her, he may divorce her against her will, for in such a case Rabbenu Gershom did not decree. See Get Pashut, s.k. 19, on this. It appears that if with a man, when he has no company of men, we compel him to divorce as in siman 154, all the more so with a woman. So wrote my father in Responsa Or Yisrael regarding the case of "threshing inside and winnowing outside," where it is forbidden to have relations with her. Even if one says Rabbenu Gershom decreed also in a place of mitzvah, at least in a place of sin he did not decree. See there further.
