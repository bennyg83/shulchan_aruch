A married woman who claims that a man [not her husband] is pursuing her, and he denies it [the claim], she [the married woman] is not believed to the extent of punishing the man, but [the court] decrees with [the threat of] excommunication upon him [if he does not comply with the following:] that he should not speak with her at all, and that they should not live in the same neighborhood. And, if the man is presumed in their eyes to be suspected of major sexual violations, then it is appropriate to rubuke and reprimand him and to threaten him that if he doesn’t behave properly, they [the court] will totally separate him [from the community] for ill and push him away with both hands, as our Sages stated, “We flog people with [persistently] bad reputations.” And see what Rambam wrote in [Mishneh Torah] Laws of Sanhedrin, chapter 24.
