One who betrothed one of five women and each says "me." It contains 3 seifim. One who betrothed one of five women and wrote her a ketubah and does not know which she is, and each of them says "I am the one" — he is forbidden to relatives of all of them; and he gives a get to each one and leaves the ketubah among them and departs. And if he betrothed through intercourse — the Sages penalized him that he give ketubah to each one.
