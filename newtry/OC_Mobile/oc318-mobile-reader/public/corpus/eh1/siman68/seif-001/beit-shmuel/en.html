And anyone secluded with his arusah. Although we hold that one who claims "I found an open entrance" is believed because of the presumption that a man does not trouble himself with a wedding feast and then ruin it, nevertheless if he was secluded with her we say perhaps he had relations with her and forgot, or perhaps he had partial relations and does not know that he broke her virginity. So wrote Rashi. From Tosafot it appears that this is specifically where the custom is to seclude him with her; then we say she is secluded with him so that he should be familiar with her, and then a person certainly cannot restrain himself from relations. All this is regarding money, but regarding prohibition he is believed to forbid her to himself, as stated in the Talmud. Shiltei Gibborim rules like Rashi. If she claims she was a virgin but does not claim he had relations with her, it requires study whether she is believed with a migo that she could have said, "you had relations with me," or whether this is a migo to extract money.
