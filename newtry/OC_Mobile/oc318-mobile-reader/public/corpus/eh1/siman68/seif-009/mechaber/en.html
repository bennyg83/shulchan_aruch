When he claimed he did not find her a virgin — if she said "I am one afflicted by wood" — she is believed and her ketubah returns to a maneh, and he may impose a general cherem that no man had intercourse with her. And if she said "from when you betrothed me I was raped" — behold she is believed and her ketubah is one hundred as it was. And if he claimed: perhaps before I betrothed you you were raped, or perhaps after I betrothed you you had intercourse willingly — behold she imposes a general cherem upon whoever falsely claims in order to obligate him monetarily. {Rama: (Whoever claims virginity — his claim is a valid claim; if he wishes to maintain her afterward he writes her a ketubah of a maneh) (Tur).}
