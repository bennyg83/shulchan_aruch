If she was secluded. Tosafot and Mordechai write two explanations: either we assume she was secluded with him, or it speaks where there are witnesses that she was secluded with him. Even after she was secluded with him, if he says he did not have relations with her, he can make her forbidden to himself. Although ordinarily he would not restrain himself, sometimes a person does restrain himself. See siman 117 and siman 166, where regarding a yevamah we say he restrains himself.
