And the matter occurred. Although we hold that a person does not trouble himself with a wedding feast and ruin it, in such a case, where there is a weakness before us because he changed the custom, the Rosh and Tur are concerned. Rashi explains this inspection as done so that the blood not be lost. According to this, since there is a weakness before us, we are concerned that perhaps he caused the blood to be lost. According to those decisors who hold that even where he found blood he can claim an open entrance, and the baraita teaches that he cannot claim claims of virginity, it must mean that even an open-entrance claim he cannot make. Since we have seen deceit, for he changed the custom, certainly he is not believed regarding open entrance, where there is no proof to his words. Even regarding blood, where one might have thought he is believed because there is no blood, the baraita teaches that he is not believed, and we say perhaps he caused the blood to be lost.
