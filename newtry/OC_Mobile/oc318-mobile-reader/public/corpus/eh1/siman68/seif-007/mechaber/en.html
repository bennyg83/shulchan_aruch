When they said his claim regarding virginity is a valid claim — if he is a kohen, behold she is forbidden to him. And if she is an Israelite's wife — she is permitted to him, unless her father accepted betrothal for her when she was less than three years and one day old. {Rama: Some say this that she becomes forbidden to an Israelite's wife applies specifically when she does not justify herself; but if she said "I was raped" — she is believed (Tur). And some say further that this applies specifically when she is an adult before us; but if she is still a minor — she does not become forbidden (Beit Yosef in name of Rashi, and so from Ran's words). And see above siman 178 seif 3.}
