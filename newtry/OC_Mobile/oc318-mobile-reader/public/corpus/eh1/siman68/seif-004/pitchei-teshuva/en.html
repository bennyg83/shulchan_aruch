Behold she is forbidden to him. See responsum Maharival part 2 siman 6 — plain to him since the Gemara said open opening is like two witnesses, beit din compels divorce. See there. But responsum Mahari HaLevi brother of Taz siman 39 wrote the Gemara explanation "believed to forbid her" means we tell him she is forbidden when leaving Israel, but beit din does not compel him; and "open opening like two witnesses" means he must divorce to leave and regarding severity of prohibition they are like two witnesses, but not for beit din compulsion. See responsum Brit Avraham siman 86 letter 2; explained there agreeing with Maharival that beit din compels divorce. See what I wrote siman 115 seif 7 s.k. 37.
