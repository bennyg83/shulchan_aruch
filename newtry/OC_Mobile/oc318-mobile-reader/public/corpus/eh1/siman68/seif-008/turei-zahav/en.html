(א) But the principal ketubah. He is believed, for the Sages enacted ketubah for a woman, and they enacted that the husband is believed to cause her to lose it, because there is a chazakah that a person does not exert himself over a wedding feast and then ruin it. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Ran that this implies that if he did not exert himself over a feast, he is not believed. According to this, now that most grooms do not make the wedding themselves, but rather the bride's father does, the groom is not believed with a claim of virginity to cause her to lose her ketubah.
