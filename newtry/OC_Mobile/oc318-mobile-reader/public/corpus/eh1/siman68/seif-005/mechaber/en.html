If he claimed blood — if she was from a dorakti family (meaning a family of eunuchs through whom the generation is cut off from sons) — behold she is in her presumption. And if not — they examine her: perhaps she has a great illness that dried the moisture of her limbs, or she was fasting from hunger; and they bring her to a bathhouse and moisten her and feed and give her drink until she has intercourse again and we see whether she produces blood. And if there is no illness, hunger, or the like — behold this is a valid claim.
