If she was from a family of Dorakti, etc. So wrote the Rambam, and it implies that even if she did not make this claim, we claim it for her: perhaps she is from a Dorakti family. This is unlike the implication of the Tur.
Perhaps illness. Specifically regarding a claim of blood we attribute it to illness, but not regarding open entrance. From the Tur it follows that if she said, "I am from a Dorakti family," and that was found false, nevertheless if it is possible to attribute it to illness, we do so, even though her claim was already contradicted.
This is a claim. Even if her entrance is closed, this is a claim according to everyone, because since there is no blood we attribute the narrowness to some cause.
