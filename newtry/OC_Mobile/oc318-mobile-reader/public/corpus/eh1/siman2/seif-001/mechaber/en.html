Every man should strive to take a fitting wife. It contains eleven seifim. A man should not marry a woman who has any disqualification. {Rama: One who marries a disqualified woman because of money — his children will not be respectable; but without that — if she is not disqualified to him but he merely marries her for money — that is permitted (responsum of Rivash siman 15). Whoever wishes to marry a disqualified woman — members of his family may protest against him (Rosh, chapter HaMocher). If he does not wish to heed them, they should do something as a sign so his seed not mix with their seed. One who was promised much money for matches and they retracted — he should not torment his bride on account of this, nor quarrel over his wife's property. Whoever does so does not succeed and his match does not turn out well, for money a man takes with his wife is not honest money, and whoever does so is called one who marries a woman for money; rather whatever his father-in-law and mother-in-law give him he should take in a good spirit, and then he will succeed (Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim).}
