Because of money. So too one who marries without money also has punishment — but his intent is to be precise: if one marries a woman for money and there is no family disqualification in her, there is no prohibition. And what he writes nearby that it is not honest money is when he wishes to anchor her specifically for money, as in Choshen Mishpat.
