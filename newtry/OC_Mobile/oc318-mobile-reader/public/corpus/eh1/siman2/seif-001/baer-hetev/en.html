Disqualification — meaning disqualification forbidden to him, such as concern of mamzer or chalala for kohanim gedolim and the like.
Money. So too one who marries without money also has punishment — but his intent is to be precise: if one marries a woman for money and there is no family disqualification in her, there is no prohibition (Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel).
Money. Although above he wrote that whoever is not disqualified to him may marry for money — meaning if they give him money willingly — but to torment his bride on account of this, or for himself to wait to marry until he finds a woman who will give him money — forbidden; of them it is said "whoever marries a woman for money," etc. — meaning that delaying marriage for greed of money causes him to bear illegitimate children (Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel).
