In Tur "but a kohen," etc. — Beit Meir explains: need not distance — meaning one who knows from mixture need not reveal except for modesty; but one who knows from mixture is forbidden to marry from them, per his words siman 7. I question the language "but a kohen shall not marry" — implies one who wants to marry; why specify chalal became mixed when safek applies? If on one who marries — fine; see Beit Shmuel s.k. 16. If on the revealer — Ran and all commentators hold even definite mixture unknown who — also need not reveal, as Eliyahu will not reveal. Perhaps for all this Beit Shmuel expanded explaining Tur.
