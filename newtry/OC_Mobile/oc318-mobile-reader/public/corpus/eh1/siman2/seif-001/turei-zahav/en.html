All families, etc. Beit Shmuel seif 3 wrote that for Rambam a kohen is forbidden to marry a woman whose family is not known — even per Maggid Mishneh's second answer. From this he also wrote on what the Mechaber says in seif 11 regarding one called chalal who is silent — we do not marry from them unless they check — that this means four mothers, and that even without silence checking is needed per Rambam — end quote. But he did not examine well: per Rambam one need check only after a definite chalala, for all families are presumed kosher except regarding ascending to the platform, Sanhedrin, and service at the altar — that his son be fit for altar service as kohen and his daughter fit for a kohen — which implies for the altar; then checking is needed — so Beit Yosef and Rambam. Nevertheless others hold like our teacher that even for chalalot no checking is needed when no objection was raised — and so Rambam in his Mishnah commentary and chapter 2 of Issurei Bi'ah; but he holds, etc. — end quote. So Bach wrote there at length that Rambam holds one need not check after chalalot. In siman 3 he also brought Beit Yosef and Bach on Maggid Mishneh — it does not appear at all that checking is needed after a definite chalala per Rambam, for Maggid Mishneh's second answer.
