Or chalal. Per what Rashi wrote for Rambam — even if they did not call him and he is silent we are concerned he is chalal. It must mean when they called him and he is silent they check him four mothers; but for mere concern no four-mother checking needed.
We are concerned for him and his family. Even if they call him and he is silent it does not help to render his family fit — rather the family's concern remains in place; not as Chelkat Mechokek. So too in Perishah — does not help to render the family fit.
Became mixed in it. Difficult: if became mixed in it, why do I need this one's silence? It must mean checking is needed since one disqualification became mixed. So Bach and Chelkat Mechokek wished to answer: if became mixed and he is silent, we say this one is the disqualification and rest of family need not check. I already wrote this does not exist; also from Rama's words it is proven it does not exist — for he refers to Mechaber's words "we are concerned for him and his family" — hence entire family needs checking. Bach wrote: when witnesses testify became mixed, four mothers must be checked. Here it speaks when no witnesses testified became mixed — only mere concern. The law itself R' Yitzchak wrote thus. Some say R' Yitzchak wrote thus on the sugya where R' Yehoshua testified about almanot Issah to render fit all who have no silence of mamzer and chalal; but if she was silent we presume she is mamzeret or chalala. On this R' Yitzchak disqualifies when she was silent — specifically when one disqualification became mixed in the family; R' Yitzchak holds every widow from this family is fit, nevertheless if she was silent she is disqualified. And per what we hold like Rabban Gamliel — if disqualification became mixed every widow is disqualified — no difference except silence does not disqualify. Also difference: even per Rabban Gamliel if safek mamzer became mixed in family, Israel may marry woman from this family as written below; nevertheless if she was silent we presume she is mamzeret. Also difference: if safek chalal became mixed or widow from this family — if she married she does not leave; but if silent she leaves.
