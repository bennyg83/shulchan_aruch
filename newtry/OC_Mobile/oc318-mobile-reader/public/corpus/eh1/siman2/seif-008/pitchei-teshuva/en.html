Where it was established three times. See Ba'er Hetev, who wrote that this requires study, etc. See Birkei Yosef, ot 8, who brought the words of Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter HaBa Al Yevimto, siman 33, who wrote as follows: Rif and Rosh wrote, and it is reasonable that circumcision, etc. Although a family of epileptics and lepers is also a doubt of danger to life, nevertheless Rava ruled that three times are required. Even though Rava himself holds like Rabbi regarding marriage, here it is different, for even Rabbi agrees that one is not concerned for a family unless it has been established three times. So wrote Nimukei Yosef. Nevertheless, it appears that regarding sisters, even two sisters establish a presumption also for epilepsy and leprosy. End quote. He then brought that Yad Aharon also distinguishes by his own reasoning between sisters and a family, and we have gained the law that two leprous sisters establish a presumption. However, in Chiddushei Rashba to Yevamot there it is explained that Rava holds that even sisters establish a presumption only with three times; see there.
