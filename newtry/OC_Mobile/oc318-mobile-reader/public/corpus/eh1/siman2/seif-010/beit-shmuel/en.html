If he informed. Beit Yosef challenged Tur who wrote this law in name of Rambam — is this not explicit in Gemara: Rav Nachman said "who will be for the day"? Taz answered: per last answer there — Rabbanan have seclusion — they seclude but he did not lie with her; first answer rejected; for the day forbidden even if he informs. But Rambam rules even per conclusion this is not rejected and permitted if he informs.
