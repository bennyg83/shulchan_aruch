Uncertain — Yoreh De'ah siman 263 ch 3 ruled: if a woman circumcised her son and he died from circumcision, and her sister too circumcised her son and he died from circumcision — the rest of the sisters should not circumcise their sons, etc. — hence implies twice establishes presumption; explicitly in Gemara Yevamot 64a one law for them regarding circumcision and leprosy.
