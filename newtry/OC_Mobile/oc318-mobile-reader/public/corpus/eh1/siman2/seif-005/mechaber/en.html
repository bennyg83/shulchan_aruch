A family in which doubtful chalal became mixed — any fit woman who married one from that family and was widowed is forbidden to a kohen le-khatchilah; if she married she does not leave, because there are two doubts: perhaps she is the widow of that chalal, perhaps she is not his widow; and if we say she is his widow, perhaps he is not a chalal. {Rama: Some say specifically the widow who had presumption of fitness; but her daughter — even if she married — must leave. Some are lenient and say there is no difference between her and her daughter (two opinions in Beit Yosef siman 67).} But if definite chalal became mixed, every woman among them is forbidden until checked; if she married she leaves. The same if doubtful mamzer or definite mamzer became mixed. {Rama: All this is for one who knows the matter; but a family in which disqualified became mixed and it is not known to many — once absorbed, absorbed; one who knows she is disqualified may not reveal it but leaves her in presumption of fitness, for all families absorbed among Israel are fit for the future. Nevertheless it is proper to reveal to modest persons (so appears from Rosh, chapter Asarah Yuchasin). Specifically a family that was absorbed and mixed; but as long as it was not mixed they reveal the disqualified and proclaim on them so the fit separate from them (there in Hagahot Alfasi). See Choshen Mishpat siman 35 who is believed to testify about families.}
