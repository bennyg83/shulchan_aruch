Forbidden to kohen le-khatchilah. In the Gemara they establish it per Rabban Gamliel when she does not say clearly her husband was fit; but if she says clearly, Rabban Gamliel concedes she is fit for kehunah. However some say for us who do not hold like Rabban Gamliel regarding saying clearly "I was defiled by a fit man" — and we hold she is not believed even if majority are fit as in siman 6 — so too here her clarity does not help. So appears from Rambam's wording — and this is Mechaber's wording that she is silent, and he wrote forbidden to kohen — implies even if she says clearly. However question on Beit Yosef siman 66: Rabban Gamliel himself concedes an unmarried woman who became pregnant is forbidden to marry kohen unless there are two majorities of fit men. So what does Rabban Gamliel challenge in this sugya from almanot Issah — that safek chalal became mixed? For in safek chalal two majorities do not apply. It would seem to prove: in safek chalal when there is one majority similar to other prohibitions — when there are two majorities, her clarity helps / for two majorities her clarity also helps. From this sugya we hear Rabban Gamliel and R' Yehoshua dispute when she does not say clearly her husband was fit — as written. Also she is not silent when told she is mamzeret or chalala — per Rabban Gamliel she is forbidden to kohen le-khatchilah and if married does not leave; but if silent when told she is mamzeret or chalala — some say she leaves since there is mixture in this family as written nearby.
But her daughter etc. So Beit Yosef infers from Rambam and Tosafot. But not necessary; from Tosafot Ketubot 14 one may infer the opposite — they challenged there why daughter is more forbidden than widow — it is safek and generally we are lenient in safek. They answered: they made an elevation in lineage like two majorities; there we hold she does not leave if there are not two majorities as in siman 7. However some say she leaves for another reason — it is not full safek, for we hold safek must be in one body; but one doubt in body and one in mixtures is not safek. So not full safek. So too challenged the Torah scholar Ner Yisrael, especially Maharil Hilman of Prague. This question applies only to virgin and kohen coming to marry her; for Israel marrying from family where safek mamzer became mixed — properly safek due to biblical safek mamzer is permitted; and in rabbinic prohibition we hold even such safek is safek as in Yoreh De'ah there. Also if kohen comes to marry widow from family where safek chalal became mixed — some say since she has presumption of fitness [can reject: this presumption is not better than presumption of animal where due to majority we presume fitness — nevertheless we do not call such safek]. But if he married her daughter — some say she leaves because not full safek. However some say these laws are for issur heter laws. Per first explanation of Rashi it is not full safek — he explained almanat Issah e.g. first husband threw get to her mother (for her) — doubt close to him doubt close to her; he died; found this one doubt divorcee doubt widow; kohen married her and had son — called safek in Gemara since doubt was in his mother; this one came through her son — called safek though generally certainly not effective. On Tur's words seemingly difficult why he wrote Israel need not be concerned because all are pure for future — must mean it is safek as above.
And some are lenient. So Beit Yosef siman 67 in name of Rabbeinu Tam — this is what Tosafot there Ketubot 14 wrote: Rabbeinu Tam's text reads in baraita "which is Issah" and refers to the daughter.
The same if became mixed etc. So Rambam; these laws flow from the sugya where Rabban Gamliel and R' Yehoshua dispute almanot Issah — safek chalal became mixed in this family; there is widow from this family and safek. R' Yehoshua testified to render fit per Rabban Gamliel — what shall I do, kohanim listen to distance not approach — we hear: if there were concern for Israel e.g. safek mamzer became mixed, even Rabban Gamliel concedes she is fit — it is safek and he was stringent only for kehunah. Therefore Rambam's "the same if became mixed" must also speak whether she is permitted to kohen; he says regarding mamzer law is like chalal with distinction between safek and vadai — so Derishah. But Beit Yosef siman 67 explains Rambam's words: speaks of Israel forbidden le-khatchilah if safek mamzer became mixed in family. Must refer to marrying daughter from this family — for mamzer widow is permitted to Israel. Must mean marrying daughter; though Beit Yosef infers from Rambam if marries her daughter she leaves — must mean specifically kohen marrying daughter she leaves; but Israel marrying does not leave. All this per his explanation; but main law as written; so Maharshal and Turei Zahav — if safek mamzer became mixed in family Israel may marry from that family even le-khatchilah. What Tur wrote siman 67 "Israel where safek issur became mixed forbidden to marry from that family" is error — must read "issur became mixed." So Maharshal emended; Darkei Moshe's words explaining Tur are strained.
If became mixed. Meaning if witnesses testify definite mamzer became mixed — Israel forbidden to marry from this family. But if rumor went out definite mamzer became mixed — no prohibition; Israel permitted to marry from this family — so Bach. Also appears even kohen need not be concerned if rumor went out definite chalal became mixed in this family — for Rambam wrote specifically when witnesses testify chalal became mixed checking needed. How wrote here forbidden until checks — and did not write this checking is not like checking written before as in seif 3 — hence he holds need concern only when witnesses testify chalal became mixed in family; but if rumor — no concern. Thus Bach's words siman 7 explaining Tur — that Tur here speaks of rumor that disqualification became mixed — need not concern for safek chalal if rumor safek chalal; but if rumor definite chalal must concern — rejected.
Since absorbed. In Gemara refers to mamzer; Rashi — his disqualification was not known. Seemingly difficult — we hold unknown mamzer is not alive, not living twelve months as in Sefer Chassidim — not alive like treifah not alive. Possibly in his place it was known and he was alive; then went elsewhere and married woman — there unknown if mamzer.
Absorbed. Ran wrote proof for this law from Elijah not revealing future disqualified who were absorbed — though all revealed to him. Also in Gemara R' Yirmiyah: revelation is in our hands to reveal — but since absorbed, absorbed. Also another version there. From all this we hear: even if known to two that impurity occurred in some disqualified family — not proper to reveal; as we find in Gemara they did not reveal though known to sages. Also appears from that Gemara — specifically mamzerim and netinim not fit to enter congregation and absorbed — not revealed; but chalalim who have remedy to marry Israelites — revealed; Elijah too in future will reveal. Therefore R' Yossi said mamzerim and netinim pure for future — speaks of absorbed as Ran wrote. Nevertheless he took mamzerim and netinim — hence chalalim are revealed.
