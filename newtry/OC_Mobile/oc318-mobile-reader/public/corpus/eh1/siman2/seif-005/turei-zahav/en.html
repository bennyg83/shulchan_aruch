If he informed her first, etc. — Tur likewise in the name of Rambam. Beit Yosef wondered: is he not a regular student in chapter haChalitz? — meaning what we say on 37b Rav would say "who will be for the day"? Derishah answered: Rav did not wish to divorce her but to stay with her one day here and then go elsewhere and return periodically — and this is not settled, for Rambam also wrote if he informed her first that he marries her for known days, etc. One could explain this too the way you can explain Rav's Gemara — since there is room to explain thus, so too Rambam's words. But it seems better to answer: one cannot learn from Rav's Gemara since there is "or if you wish say mere seclusion" — they seclude as one who has bread in his basket; and perhaps this answer is primary — if so, no proof from there to permit intercourse, for there is prohibition at time of relations, like divorcee of the heart who is one of nine disqualifications. But from Rambam's words one may learn properly that he said he marries her, etc. — and stam marriage is for relations.
