"Cursed is one who lies with an animal" — Rashi: they are like animals who have no heart to understand "that your life and length of days," etc. It appears his explanation: women's nature is to neglect their husbands from Torah because they think that thereby they will engage in commerce and have benefit in this world; unlike if they engage in Torah they would have only the world to come alone. But in truth it is not so — rather "your life" in this world and "length of your days" in the world that is entirely long — the world to come. We learn from Rashi's words that if a daughter of am ha'aretz is found wise and understanding the matter we mentioned — there is no prohibition on account of her father being am ha'aretz, as seems to me.
