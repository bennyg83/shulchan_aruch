A family about which an objection was raised, and two testify that a mamzer or chalal became mixed in them, or that there is slavery among them — this is a doubt. If it is a priestly family, one should not marry a woman from them until he checks four mothers who are eight — mother and mother's mother, mother's father's mother and mother. So too he checks mother's mother, mother's father's mother and mother. If this family about which an objection was raised are Levites or Israelites, he adds to check them one more, and he ends up checking ten mothers. But a woman coming to marry need not check, for they were not warned regarding fitness to marry disqualified persons.
