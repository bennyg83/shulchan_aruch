Beit Yosef and Bach disagree explaining Tur. Therefore cannot explain what Maggid wrote letter 2 — Rambam holds even to marry kohen we are concerned lest chalal; not only not implied but opposite is explicit — some commentators wrote he is not trusted for lineage: every kohen who checks (per view — to serve on altar) does not marry his daughter — clear as he concludes; but if he does not want to check (plain kohen not wanting to check in order to serve) — he marries his daughter. So too checked attributed family — they do not check him for that reason: daughter not checked for attributed matters — implies; but plain kehunah prohibition concern — daughter kosher.
