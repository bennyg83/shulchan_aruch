Two. So Rambam. But Rashi explained they read to two an aspersion of disqualification — not that they testify full testimony.
Slavery. He did not write "or slave" — for a slave who became mixed in Israel, the child is fit; per Rambam even for kehunah not blemished. Therefore he took "slavery" — meaning the objection is that there are slaves and maidservants among them, and every child born in the family is safek slave lest born from the maidservant (Chelkat Mechokek).
To check. Per Rashi I brought nearby seif 6 — there is no full testimony mamzer became mixed — fine. But for Rambam who holds there is full testimony — difficult why she need not check; is she not warned not to marry mamzer? Therefore one should be stringent when there is testimony that she must check after him (Chelkat Mechokek, Beit Shmuel).
