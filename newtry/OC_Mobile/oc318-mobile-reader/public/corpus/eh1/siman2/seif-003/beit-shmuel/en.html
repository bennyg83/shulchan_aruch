That two testify. So the Rambam. But Rashi explained they read to two an aspersion of disqualification — not that they testify full testimony.
Levites or Israelites. Per R' Yehuda and Rambam — even if mamzer or natin became mixed, nevertheless there is distinction between families, for priestly families are more pedigreed and disqualifications are not common among them. However per other poskim, for concern of chalal — priestly disqualification — one need check only eight mothers; but concern he is disqualified for Israel — mamzer — requires checking ten mothers.
Ten mothers. And not fathers, because men have quarrels and disputes over lineage — they contend; if some disqualification were in the fathers it would be publicized. But women do not contend over lineage and the disqualification is not publicized — therefore we are more concerned with women. And if asked: why are we concerned for mother's father because of mamzeret and we do not say if mother's father was mamzeret then her father is mamzer — why is he not publicized? Some answer: since he is mamzer from the mother's side he is not publicized.
But a woman etc. Per Rashi's explanation I brought nearby — that there is no full testimony mamzer became mixed — fine. But for Rambam who holds there is full testimony — difficult why she need not check; is she not warned not to marry mamzer? We hold whoever is warned, she too is warned. Possibly because from one family he is safek mamzer and biblically kosher — therefore she is not warned regarding chalal and need not check for chalal. Therefore Chazal did not be stringent she needs checking for mamzer concern per Rashi's view that there is no full testimony. And Chazal did not be stringent on her to check since biblically no checking needed for chalala — so too per Rambam even if there is full testimony no checking needed. However Baal HaMaor and Milchamot Hashem dispute and hold for mamzer concern she must check after him; all the more when witnesses testify mamzer became mixed. So too per Rashi — therefore Rashi explained no full testimony because he holds when there is full testimony she must check after him. One should be stringent.
