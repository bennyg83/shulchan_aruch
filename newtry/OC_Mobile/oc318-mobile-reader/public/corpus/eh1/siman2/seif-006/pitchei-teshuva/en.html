And on their daughters. Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote Taz deduced from Rashi: if the am ha'aretz's daughter understands that one occupied in Torah benefits him in this world and the next — no prohibition from her father being am ha'aretz — see there. Accordingly if she is wealthy and sufficient for the groom — permitted; for the entire concern is lest she incite him to engage in commerce. Otherwise the world is not careful about this — see there.
