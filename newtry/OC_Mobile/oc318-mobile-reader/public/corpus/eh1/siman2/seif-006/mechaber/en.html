A man should always strive to marry a Torah scholar's daughter and give his daughter in marriage to a Torah scholar; if he did not find a Torah scholar's daughter he marries a daughter of the great of the generation; if not, daughter of synagogue heads; if not, daughter of charity collectors; if not, daughter of schoolteachers; and he should not give his daughter to an am ha-aretz. {Rama: Regarding their daughters he says "Cursed is one who lies with an animal" — all this regarding am ha-aretz who is not careful in mitzvot (Tur). It is a mitzvah for a man to marry his sister's daughter (Gemara Sanhedrin and Yevamot). Some say also his brother's daughter (Rambam chapter 2, laws of forbidden relations).}
