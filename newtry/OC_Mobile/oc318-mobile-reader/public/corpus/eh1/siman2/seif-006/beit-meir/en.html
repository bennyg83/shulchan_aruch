Forbidden to kohen — Beit Shmuel wrote in Gemara, etc.; but if she says "I am certain," etc.; and although Tosefta end Eduyot wrote so in this language — but if says "I am certain" even one sign Rabban Gamliel qualifies — nevertheless in my humble opinion for conclusion there is no R' Yehuda reason from presumption she checks and marries — we return entirely and hold claims of certainty not relevant in this law; if on divorce itself Rashi already explained as for us doubtful — for her too impossible to stand on the matter; if claims "I know he is not from mixture" — we tell her clarify your words to whom she married and whose son until clarified; therefore poskim sealed. Further he wrote: nevertheless question Beit Yosef's method; in my view say nevertheless challenges opposite reasonings: regarding ready-to-be-betrothed Rabban Gamliel lenient even without checking and even with clear disqualifications; regarding almanat Issah stringent from R' Yehuda ab initio even though checking relevant and precise in this he does not challenge Rabban Gamliel against Rabban Gamliel until answers Rome's words of R' Yehuda. Further wrote: if so what does he challenge, etc. — for in chalal there are not two majorities — meaning though majority of family kosher; can answer through safek and one majority considered like two majorities that do not exist since woman returned to comfort father's house and has fixed husband; though not recognizable and not Torah fixation — nevertheless not less than all importance of living husband not void and considered in place of fixed ones from Torah perspective; see Nazir 12 and Tosafot. Unlike what Perishah decided responsum Shai chapter 14 siman 48 regarding almanot Issah that was majority; see in Milchamot end first chapter what he challenges on Baal HaMaor — meaning for Rabban Gamliel in one majority also qualifies from almanat Issah needed to find majority in one place elsewhere and married there.
