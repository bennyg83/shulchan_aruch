One shall not marry a woman — Beit HaLevi wrote nowadays when we practice not marrying two wives there is no great practical difference in this law. Truth is Gemara and Rambam language "shall not marry woman in this country and woman in another country" implies specifically marrying in addition to his wife and not when she died or was divorced, even if he has children from her — reason: then usually he takes small sons with him and no concern; and because uncommon matter leaving with divorcee or mother's relatives — they did not decree.
