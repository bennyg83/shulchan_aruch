All families are presumed kosher and permitted to marry from them le-khatchilah; nevertheless, if you saw two families constantly quarreling with each other (or two people quarreling with each other) in chapter twenty of Yuchasin; or you saw a family always given to quarrels and strife; or you saw a man who frequently quarrels with everyone and is very brazen-faced — we are concerned about them and one should distance from them, for these are signs of disqualification. So too anyone who constantly disqualifies others, such as one who casts aspersion on families or individuals and says of them they are mamzerim — we are concerned he may be a mamzer. If he says of them they are slaves — we are concerned he may be a slave, for whoever disqualifies does so in his own blemish. So too one who has brazenness and cruelty and hates creatures and does not show them kindness — we are greatly concerned about him that he may be a Gibeonite.
