Upon his maidservant. Rosh wrote whether for marriage or promiscuity; and what he wrote above siman 4 if he married his maidservant and did not write even if for promiscuity — because there discusses he did not come upon her except marriage without betrothal/intercourse — Beit Chadash; and see Turei Zahav siman 279; and below siman 33 and siman 136.
