His sister is forbidden to him from the Torah — whether his sister from his father or from his mother, whether his sister from marriage or from promiscuity; even if his father came upon the forbidden relation and begot a daughter from her — she is his sister and he is liable for her; except his sister from the maidservant or from the gentile woman: {Rama: And it seems lekhatchilah forbidden to come upon her — for some are uncertain to say that specifically from the Torah the offspring follows the maidservant and the gentile woman, but rabbinically it is his seed — therefore one should be stringent lekhatchilah.} And some say this is only regarding an available maidservant; but if his father came upon his maidservant and begot a daughter from her — she is his sister (and even if he says he intended for promiscuity he is not believed) (Or Zarua).
