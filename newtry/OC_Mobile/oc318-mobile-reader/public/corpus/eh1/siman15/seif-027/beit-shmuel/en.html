And witnesses came. But single witness not believed his wife died to marry her sister as Tosafot and Nimukei Yosef chapter HaIshah Rabbah and Milchamot Hashem and Maggid; but Beit Yosef wrote in name of Rashi he holds single witness believed — and this is what is written there single witness came and said his wife died; and not compelled to say he means permitted to marry sister on single witness; rather Rashi explains because his brother-in-law's wife forbidden to brother-in-law — therefore discusses single witness; for with two witnesses she is not forbidden per mishnah as under duress — therefore Rashi explains entire mishnah discusses single witness; and Tosafot's explanation displeases him that here to marry sister with two witnesses; rather entire mishnah single witness; and practical difference if transgressed and married and single witness testifies first wife died and wants another woman — appears single witness not believed because of rumor as Yoreh De'ah siman 127; and in Talmud chapter HaIshah Rabbah all matters in presumption of prohibition — single witness not believed to remove from presumption.
And permitted with second relatives. Meaning permitted to take her daughter; and Derishah and Beit Chadash wrote specifically after second's death permitted to take daughter not in lifetime as seif 13; and Taz disputes — not comparable there; and here married by mistake and no promiscuity concern and we do not suspect her; however slight proof for their words — he is forbidden with rape-victim's relatives and certainly no concern he will come again by force upon her; rather since they were together in flesh even if initially by force or mistake — promiscuity concern now.
And permitted with second relatives. From this language implies even in lifetime — and it is not so for in lifetime forbidden as seif 13; and what he wrote compel to divorce — likewise Hagahot Alfasi; and this law requires examination for Nimukei Yosef wrote chapter 2 Yevamot Tosafot taught all those mishnah says do not marry — if consummated need not divorce since no issur concern in themselves only concern she will fornicate with him as in gloss seif 13; even though there writes this law regarding rape victim not specifically and even seduced likewise as Rambam chapter 2 law 1; and how ruled compel to divorce even though wrote here relative accustomed to come through wife — nevertheless only concern perhaps she will fornicate; also chapter 2 there reason explained women relative to women common; nevertheless post factum need not divorce; and whence distinguish common from accustomed; and perhaps Hagahot Alfasi holds what Tosafot taught all those need not divorce — regarding suspicion; but if certainly came upon her — compel to divorce; but Nimukei Yosef explains reason all that is not issur in itself but concern perhaps she will fornicate — even if certainly came need not compel divorce; and so Rambam and so essential; and likewise found in Taz.
