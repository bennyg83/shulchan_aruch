He betrothed a woman and she went to another country, and witnesses came and testified that she died, and he married her sister — and afterward it became known she did not die: he is forbidden with both and both need a get from him; and all the ways enumerated in chapter HaIshah Rabbah and chapter HaZorek apply to them; and if one of them died he is permitted with the other. But if his married wife went to another country and witnesses came and testified she died and he married her sister — and afterward it became known she did not die: her sister does not need a get from him and his wife is permitted to him; and he is permitted with the second one's relatives and the second is permitted with his relatives; and if the first died he is permitted with the second. And likewise other forbidden relations whom he married assuming permissibility and they were found forbidden — they do not need a get, for kiddushin do not take effect with forbidden relations (and likewise all promiscuity with relatives forbidden to him because of his wife — they do not forbid his wife to him) and permitted with the second one's relatives (Tur); however if the relative is accustomed to come to him through his wife — they compel him to divorce his wife (Hagahot Alfasi there). And why did they require his betrothed's sister a get — lest they say there was a condition in the betrothal and he married her sister properly; and since the sister left through a get — the sister who was his first betrothal is forbidden so they not say he married his divorcee's sister; but when his married wife went there is no concern for this that they say there was a condition in the marriage — for a man does not make his intercourse promiscuous intercourse; therefore all know the second marriage is nothing.
