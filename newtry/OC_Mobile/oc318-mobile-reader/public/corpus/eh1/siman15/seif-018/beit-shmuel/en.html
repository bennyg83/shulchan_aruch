Permitted. And likewise all poskim — even though his father's brother's wife from father forbidden from Torah — nevertheless this permitted; and Tur wrote in name of Rosh he holds forbidden — not compelled; also Ri'az wrote permitted; and see Beit Chadash proof from Rosh responsa — no implication there; and his mother's brother's wife from mother — Tosafot wrote later generations forbade; nevertheless appears from Rambam and Tur like other secondary relations for all matters and compel on this secondary like others; therefore listed this secondary among other secondary relations; and not like some who want to be lenient on this secondary not compelling divorce — and it is not so.
