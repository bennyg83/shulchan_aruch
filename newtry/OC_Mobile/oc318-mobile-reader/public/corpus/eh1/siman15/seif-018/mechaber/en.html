His father's brother's wife from the father — forbidden from the Torah; but his father's brother's wife from the mother and his mother's brother's wife — whether from the mother or from the father — they are forbidden only rabbinically; and from here onward such as his father's father's brother's wife from the mother (and his mother's mother's brother's wife whether from the father or from the mother) — permitted; and his father's father's brother's wife from the father and his father's father's sister whether from the mother or from the father and his mother's mother's sister — permitted; and some forbid these.
