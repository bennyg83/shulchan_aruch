These who are forbidden. Tur wrote "these who are not fit for kiddushin," etc.; and Beit Yosef wrote language imprecise for those forbidden rabbinically kiddushin take effect; and Darkei Moshe answered Tur teaches not full kiddushin — practical difference if another came and betrothed she needs get from him too, end quote; and incorrect for in siman 44 seif 7 wrote explicitly one who betroths one of secondary relations — behold full kiddushin; and it appears Tur's intent those who have prohibition in their kiddushin — meaning even if he does not consummate afterward nevertheless in betrothal per Tosafot there is also rabbinic prohibition revealing he does not heed sages and acts to marry her; and appears rabbinic prohibition heavier than Torah for with Torah no such prohibition in betrothal alone for his act and money given are gift as Bava Metzia — a man knows kiddushin do not take effect with his sister and gives for gift or deposit; unlike rabbinic prohibition where kiddushin take effect — his act effective — therefore prohibition showing he scorns sages' words as above (editor's words: Beit Shmuel s.k. 1 wrote for Beit Yosef if two betrothed rabbinic forbidden relation kiddushin of both take effect — otherwise Beit Yosef's words contradict siman 2 if one betrothed before disqualified testimony rabbinically and another before disqualified testimony rabbinically — kiddushin of both take effect — if so also rabbinic forbidden relation likewise, end quote; and in my opinion not comparable — for if betrothed before disqualified testimony rabbinically — like betrothing without witnesses though Torah-valid they are not fit to testify at all on his kiddushin — each disqualified rabbinically and this like betrothing before deaf-mutes — though fit to testify for themselves disqualified for testimony; likewise here though Torah-valid disqualified rabbinically cannot testify; and similarly Yoma chapter 18 regarding oath-offering sacrifice — disqualified testimony rabbinically exempt from oath-offering sacrifice — though Torah-valid not fit to testify since disqualified rabbinically — see there; if so perhaps kiddushin do not take effect from Torah before such testimony unable to testify — therefore second's kiddushin also take effect like first's; but betrothing rabbinic forbidden relation — from Torah take effect and they have no power to remove kiddushin only forbidden to consummate and needs get — if so at least if second betrothed second's kiddushin do not take effect at all for first's kiddushin are full kiddushin; and as Taz brought proof from siman 44; and as he wonders in Choshen Mishpat what he challenged Rama uncertain whether both kiddushin take effect — on contrary if Beit Yosef held both take effect he should challenge siman 44 there wrote full kiddushin from first and if second betrothed his kiddushin nothing, end quote).
