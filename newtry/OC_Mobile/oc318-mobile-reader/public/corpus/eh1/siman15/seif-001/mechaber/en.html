Prohibition of forbidden relations from the Torah and rabbinically. It contains 31 seifim. These who are forbidden because of forbidden relations — some from the Torah and some rabbinically. Those who are from the Torah — kiddushin do not take effect with them. And those who are rabbinically — kiddushin take effect with them and they require a get; and likewise one who betroths a safek forbidden relation — she requires a get.
