Second relatives. Ba'er Heitev wrote Beit Chadash and Derishah wrote specifically after second's death, etc., until Beit Shmuel agreed with Derishah and Beit Chadash; and see Maggid Mishneh chapter 2 Laws of Forbidden Relations law 11 who brought their words and wrote not upright in his eyes (for reasons he explained there); and clarification per his view: permitted even in second's lifetime; and not comparable to seif 13 for two reasons — one here both unintentional thinking first died therefore rabbis did not decree (and even if she intentional only he unintentional or under duress — also his view not clear) — also here no reason forbid second relatives for even if not marry second relatives second comes visit sister and promiscuity concern — forbidding second relatives accomplishes nothing; and wrote both reasons true for law; practical difference even if first died or divorced permitted second relatives even in second's lifetime per first reason; and also if fornicated with wife's sister willingly permitted second relatives while first still with him per second reason; but if first died or divorced forbidden second relatives — opposite, end quote.
