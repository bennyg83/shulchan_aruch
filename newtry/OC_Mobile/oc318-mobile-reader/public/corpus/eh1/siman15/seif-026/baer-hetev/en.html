His wife's sister is forbidden. And if he betrothed conditionally and the condition was not fulfilled — it is as if he did not betroth and she is permitted to his relatives — Beit Shmuel. Beit Yosef wrote: one who came upon a forbidden relation and she is his wife's sister and afterward his wife died; and also afterward the woman's husband died — if forbidden to him because she was forbidden to her husband since in any case she was forbidden to him — find in writings of Mahar Rabbi Israel siman 29. And Darkei Moshe printed alongside wrote there she is forbidden — for as forbidden to the husband so forbidden to the paramour even though when he was paramour she was in any case forbidden because of his wife's sister, end quote. And likewise Kenesset HaGedolah in name of Radbaz part 2; and one can infer from Tosafot chapter HaIshah Rabbah s.v. his brother-in-law's wife is forbidden that we fine him — see there; per this one can say this is what Rama explained siman 11 who wrote likewise if she became forbidden because of him to her husband she is forbidden to him — understand. And Kenesset HaGedolah copied another responsum of Radbaz part 2 who wrote likewise regarding those bound by negative commandments such as a divorcee who married and a kohen came upon her while she was still his wife's sister and afterward her husband died — forbidden to him, end quote. I did not understand his words — for a divorcee is always forbidden to a kohen.
