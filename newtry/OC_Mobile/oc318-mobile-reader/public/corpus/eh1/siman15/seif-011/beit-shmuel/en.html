And we are not concerned for marit ayin. In Tosafot and Maggid it appears stepdaughter and father-in-law's wife are equal; and Nimukei Yosef wrote regarding stepdaughter there is more marit ayin concern than father-in-law's wife; however Rosh decided father-in-law's wife forbidden per Rabbenu Tam; and two stepbrothers permitted for there is publicity; and likewise Maharil responsum 81; and see Beit Chadash.
