So Rosh ruled and distinguishes stepdaughter from father-in-law's wife; and likewise Maharil responsum; and Beit Chadash; and Hagahot Maimoniyot forbid father-in-law with son-in-law's wife because of marit ayin; and if already married father-in-law's wife — Tzemach Tzedek responsum siman 40: if married on renowned decisors' instruction she need not leave — not less than uncertain water — we hold she need not leave as siman 17; however appears specifically uncertain water for majority he died or father-in-law's wife where several poskim permit — one may be lenient if married she need not leave; but secondary relation if married she leaves and instruction does not help — like erring against established law; and post factum siman 157 regarding die rather than transgress — appears there for secondary one transgresses not dies; and Shach there; and if married secondary under duress and duress passed — apparently must divorce even though initially permitted; nevertheless after duress passed forbidden; and as in passage chapter 1 Kiddushin and Rambam chapter 8 Laws of Kings — beautiful captive permitted first intercourse, second forbidden; however one can reject for Rambam holds first intercourse before marriage therefore forbidden second; but if married permissibly one can say no need divorce; and what Tosafot in name of Rashi wrote first intercourse after marriage and nevertheless second forbidden — proof for secondary must divorce; however in Rashi not clear — Tosafot understood from Rashi and challenged; and one can say Rashi "do not pressure in war lest come in war" — not that first intercourse forbidden until after marriage as Tosafot understood; rather not come in war until brings to house as Rambam wrote forbidden in war until in house then permitted immediately before marriage; and with this resolves Tosafot difficulties.
