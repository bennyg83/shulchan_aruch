Relative accustomed. Beit Shmuel s.k. 22 wrote this law requires examination for Nimukei Yosef wrote, etc.; and see Maggid Mishneh there who wrote on him I found no reason for this difficulty — though they said if consummated need not divorce — nevertheless for accustomed they were stringent legs to matter for she is accustomed in his house; and what he wrote Hagahot Alfasi holds only said need not divorce regarding suspicion — I do not know how his mind cooled on this, etc.; and further wondered on Rama who ruled if relative accustomed, etc. — implies law in all forbidden relations; and Hagahot Alfasi only said if fornicated with mother-in-law; and perhaps specifically mother-in-law drafted because of her daughter and not other forbidden relations as we find Ra'avad chapter 2 law 12 distinguished thus for another matter — see there (and see Beit Meir and Etz Erezim and Chatam Sofer responsum siman 40 on this matter).
