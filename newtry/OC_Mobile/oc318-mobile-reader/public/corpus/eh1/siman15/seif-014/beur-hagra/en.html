Daughter's daughter, etc. Behold in third of his son and daughter four secondary relations counted; and fourth of father-in-law and mother-in-law; and here only two counted; and there twenty secondary relations counted; and in explanation chapter 2 Yevamot per text there Rabbina to R' Elazar what difference above, etc. — behold his son and daughter forbidden from his wife, etc.; and meaning above forbidden from his wife all above to fourth generation forbidden in every respect four; unlike below no prohibition from his wife each generation below only from his son — and order like father's wife all males; and likewise her daughter all females as mother's mother; and challenges behold his son and daughter likewise forbidden all below — no distinction and they are also four; and answers since, etc.
