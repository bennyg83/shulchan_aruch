His wife's daughter. See Sha'ar HaMelekh chapter 10 Laws of Marriage in Kuntrus Chupat Chatanim seif 5 who brought Tosafot beginning One Who Marries s.v. forbidden relations (who wrote: if prohibition of woman and daughter is from "nakedness of woman and daughter you shall not uncover" — say Scripture forbade only where he uncovered both; but she entered chuppah and was not lain with — permitted to uncover the other's; and R' Yehudah holds reasoning since "remainder" written in verse through marriage remainder comes, end quote); and wrote on this from their words evident specifically entering chuppah forbidden from Torah with her daughter but betrothal alone not; and evident from what they asked entering chuppah and not lain with; and if held even betrothal forbidden — better to challenge; and from what they answered since "remainder" written through marriage remainder comes — evident specifically chuppah where remainder applies but not betrothal for we hold betrothed wife does not inherit and he does not become impure for her — for "his remainder" written and she is not his remainder as Rashi Yevamot 29 and Ketubot 53; and evident from baraita language "married a woman forbidden to marry her daughter" and not "betrothed"; however Rambam chapter 2 Laws of Forbidden Relations law 7 wrote explicitly likewise if betrothed; and Maggid Mishneh wrote this evident in many places and I do not know how he wrote this evident in disputed matter — requires examination now, end quote (I again saw Beit Meir went this way to extract from Tosafot — see there). And per his words if he betrothed a woman and again betrothed her daughter — concern for kiddushin and needs get; and if her daughter fell for yibum before him — bound for chalitzah to fear Tosafot view; but in my opinion impossible to say thus per Tosafot at all for betrothal alone no Torah prohibition; and certainly Maggid Mishneh's words his lips spoke plainly this evident in many places — for behold Kiddushin 50b mishnah one who betroths woman and her daughter or woman and her sister at once, etc.; and there 51a stated kiddushin not given to intercourse, etc.; we taught one who betroths woman and her daughter, etc. — see there entire passage plainly evident also betrothal there is woman-and-daughter Torah prohibition; and further evident Nazir 12a R' Yochanan one who tells agent go betroth woman unconditionally forbidden all women, etc.; Rava said R' Yochanan concedes regarding woman who has neither daughter nor mother, etc. — see there for there concern only relative who is Torah forbidden relation as later authorities explain below siman 35 seif 11; and further evident beginning Derech Eretz Rashi cited in Nazir s.v. presumption agent — see there; and Rambam's words are literally Derech Eretz language there. Hence must say Tosafot's intent asked entering chuppah and not lain with as amplification — lest say Scripture particularized intercourse only but without intercourse even entered chuppah permitted to uncover other; all the more merely betrothed (and I do not understand Sha'ar HaMelekh if he held even betrothal forbidden better to challenge — in my opinion reversed — now that they asked entering chuppah their question is stronger; and what they answered since "remainder" in verse through marriage remainder came — must strain meaning since seen Scripture not particularized intercourse only that even entering chuppah without intercourse forbidden to uncover other — likewise betrothal alone like all forbidden relations; and your neighbor will tell Tosafot these words also written 3a beginning with slight change and there Tosafot conclude evidently betrothal is the matter — see there).
