His wife and his wife's sister's husband went to another country, and they came and told him his wife died and her sister's husband died, and he married his wife's sister — and afterward his wife and his brother-in-law came: his wife's sister needs a get from him — whether she married him on the testimony of two witnesses or on one witness; and she is forbidden to her brother-in-law; and his wife is forbidden to him — whether she was married to him or his betrothed. {Rama: And if he came promiscuously upon his wife's sister and afterward his wife and brother-in-law died — some say forbidden afterward to marry his wife's sister, for as she is forbidden to the husband so forbidden to the paramour even though she was forbidden to him in any case (Piskei Maharai siman 28) (and see below siman 178).}
