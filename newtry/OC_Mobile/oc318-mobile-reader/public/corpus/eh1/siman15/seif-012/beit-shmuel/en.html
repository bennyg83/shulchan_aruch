And for Rambam they have termination. Tosafot and other poskim challenged: since son's daughter-in-law has no termination all the more son's son's daughter; and Maggid answered per Rambam — forbidden relations prohibition is not by reasoning but decree of place; and son's daughter-in-law is in Talmud without termination; and son's son's daughter is questioned in Talmud — learn it is lighter. And Tosafot answered: son's daughter-in-law forbidden more as heirs; and Ramban and Rosh wrote proof from this difficulty — son's son's daughter has no termination; and Talmud question refers to son's son's daughter; and for their answer must resolve why his father's father's father's wife forbidden until Abraham; and his father's father's father permitted — if his father's father's father were female would be permitted and his wife forbidden; and perhaps these poskim hold truly his father's father's father forbidden; and Rosh who ruled his father's father's father permitted — here ruled son's son's daughter forbidden not from difficulty but learned from Yerushalmi teaching Abraham forbidden with all women; and for Rambam Yerushalmi forbids because of son's son's daughter-in-law and discusses widow; and Sarah forbidden with all men — and we hold his father's father's father has termination — must say forbidden because of his father's father's father's wife; but say forbidden because of mother's mother's mother's mother — for Sarah had no daughter even per view Abraham had daughter — nevertheless for Yitzchak no daughter — rather must say forbidden because of his father's father's father's wife; and apparently even per these views we do not compare one forbidden relation to another — all decree of place as Maggid wrote; except difficulty on son's son's daughter how say if male were female permitted and now male his wife forbidden; but other forbidden relations they agree no comparison; therefore even for poskim who hold Talmud question refers to son's son's daughter — do not challenge from mother's mother's father's mother versus son's son's daughter — in every generation one view and son's son's daughter permitted and mother's mother's father's mother forbidden; and per this no need for Derishah's strain what he challenged Rambam — as Maggid wrote. And for Rashi and Tosafot and Semag Talmud question refers to son's son's daughter; but most poskim did not resolve question and go stringently — therefore even for prohibition go stringently; nevertheless since rabbinic safek if Scriptural holding we do not remove; but per Ramban question does not refer to son's son's daughter but son's son's daughter of his wife — then son's son's daughter is certainly secondary and holding does not help; and see Turei Zahav siman 391.
