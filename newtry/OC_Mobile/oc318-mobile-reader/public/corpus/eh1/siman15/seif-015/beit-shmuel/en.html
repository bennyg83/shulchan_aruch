Apparently why from his side they did not decree so much and from mother-in-law's side they decreed mother's mother's father; and whence Tur and Mechaber this — not explicit in Talmud; and one can say Talmud question refers only to mother's mother's mother's mother-in-law and mother-in-law; and must say from his side we do not find in Torah except mother's mother-in-law — therefore not decreed so much; but from his wife's side mother's mother forbidden as in several Talmud places — therefore decreed more; and father equals mother.
And for Rambam they have termination. This too is Talmud question; and Ramban also holds like Rambam even though there is one view his wife and her father — we do not compare forbidden relations; and see Hagahot Derishah what he wrote in name of Maharshal.
