His mother's father's wife forbidden. Because of decree of his father's wife — both called great-grandfather; therefore one — Rashi; therefore even though his father's father's mother's father's wife permitted and we do not decree because of his father's father's father's wife — nevertheless such kinship great-grandfather is as one.
