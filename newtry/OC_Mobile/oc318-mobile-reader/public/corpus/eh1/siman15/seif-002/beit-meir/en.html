His father's father, etc., she alone — Yeshuat Shlomo wrote in siman 37: also in this I desire if it comes to my hand his mother's father's mother or his mother's mother's mother's mother and I would cancel my feast; but if his father raped a woman — permitted to him even in his father's lifetime; and it is not comparable to the law of seif 13 — women relative to women are common, men relative to men — no Gemara.
