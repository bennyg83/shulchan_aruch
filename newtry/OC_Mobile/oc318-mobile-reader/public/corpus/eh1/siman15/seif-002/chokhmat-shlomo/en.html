Seif 24 — a man is permitted with his father-in-law's wife, etc., and some forbid. Behold later authorities brought if she married on famous sage's instruction she need not leave; and it appears specifically if one sage did not first forbid him in this matter — judged as unintentional and like under duress therefore need not leave; but if he already asked one sage who forbade even if afterward asked another sage who permitted — certainly she leaves for this is not called unintentional but transgressor marrying in wickedness; and whoever marries in wickedness leaves; and like below siman 17 regarding uncertain water; and likewise above siman 11 all where they permitted him and he consummated not called he acted and she leaves — see there; and likewise here; and even if permitting sage greater and more famous than forbidding sage — nevertheless not called he acted and under duress for he saw one sage forbade and all the more a court of three — should not hurry to act and would have been to ascertain there are twists and resolutions — should not hurry to act; therefore not considered unintentional but transgressor and she leaves as it appears correct to me — correct and clear. I again saw Darkei Moshe siman 10 s.k. 4 in name of Rosh responsum there appears opposite and requires examination and understand.
