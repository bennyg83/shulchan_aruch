His wife's daughter — in Tosafot beginning chapter One Who Marries His Rape Victim s.v. forbidden relations: if the prohibition of woman and daughter is from "the nakedness of woman and daughter you shall not uncover" — say Scripture forbade only where he uncovered both nakednesses; but she entered the chuppah and was not lain with — permitted to uncover the other's nakedness; and R' Yehudah holds it is reasoning since "remainder" is written in the verse through marriage the remainder comes; and in beginning Yevamot within s.v. his daughter they wrote his wife's daughter — where she entered chuppah and was not lain with — it appears to R' Yehudah liable for her daughter even though no intercourse is written implying intercourse — he reasons intercourse is not dependent for he is not liable for his rape-victim's daughter — evidently dependent on betrothal; and requires examination — initially they wrote where, etc., implying specifically through chuppah and not betrothal alone; and at the end they conclude even through betrothal; and chapter One Who Marries also implies specifically chuppah since they derive from "remainder" and a betrothed woman is not called as we see for one who holds inheritance of husband from Torah excludes from "his remainder" and presumably betrothed he does not inherit; and likewise the reason is explained Yevamot 29b in Rashi regarding not becoming impure for him; and likewise in Tosafot rulings chapter 1: his wife's daughter who entered chuppah and was not lain with — liable for her daughter. However in Rambam chapter 2 Laws of Forbidden Relations law 7 it states: once a man betrothed a woman — forbidden to him, etc., and her daughter — behold his words are clear he betrothed alone is full forbidden relation; and there is proof from Yevamot 17b: R' Yochanan said in name of Rav — yevamot waiting for yibum who died — permitted with her mother — evidently he holds no zikah; and Rashi explained zikah is not strong enough to be like betrothed; and if only through chuppah forbidden — certainly zikah does not make marriage as proven 29b; if so even if there is zikah like betrothed what of it — rather learn like Rambam; and in my poverty requires examination whether to make a dispute from Tosafot words which are not entirely clear; and great practical difference if he betrothed a woman and his daughter fell before him for yibum — whether she exits without chalitzah — per Tosafot implication betrothed is not clear at least she is bound for chalitzah and requires examination. But his rape-victim's daughter, etc. — Maharshal already wondered why Tur and Shulchan Aruch omitted the great novelty that even when he merely came upon her forbidden with her relatives in her lifetime; but in my opinion also wonder he abbreviated; but his rape-victim's daughter — likewise all relatives as Rambam and Nimukei Yosef chapter How One Brings dispute; and at least her mother and sister like the baraita.
