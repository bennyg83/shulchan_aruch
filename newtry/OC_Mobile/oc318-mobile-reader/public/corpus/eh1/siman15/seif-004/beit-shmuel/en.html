His father's mother forbidden. Because of his mother's mother; and it implies even if he has no mother's mother — his father's mother is forbidden; and likewise in all: his son's daughter-in-law forbidden because of his son's daughter-in-law even if he has no son's daughter-in-law; and not like Shulchan Aruch who wrote specifically when he has son's daughter-in-law — Darkei Moshe.
Even his father's mother's mother's mother. Behold in Rashi 21a explained his father's father's father's mother forbidden forever; also 22a wrote his father's mother's mother's mother forbidden and his father's mother is only rabbinic decree of his mother's mother — must hold our passage disputes Yerushalmi Bar Kappara who taught only his father's father and no more; rather holds from mother's side forbidden whether mother's mother's mother upward and father's father's mother upward; but Rif, Rambam, Rosh ruled like Yerushalmi — prohibition only from mother's side all mother's mother; and from father's side specifically mother's mother upward forbidden. And do not challenge: since his father's father's father's wife forbidden how is his father's father's mother permitted — for she is his father's father's father's wife; one can say we find he came upon her promiscuously; and Beit Chadash so wrote.
