His daughter, etc.; and in seif 13 his wife's daughter — Rambam chapter 2 Laws of Forbidden Relations law 6: if he came upon his daughter from his wife he is liable for two — for his daughter and for his wife's daughter; and Ra'avad disputes — see there in Chelkat Mechokek; and the sharp young man Rabbi Yehudah Leib zt"l challenged Rambam from Mishnah Keritut chapter 3 mishnah 5: R' Shimon says one act of intercourse can incur six sin-offerings — and he calculated coming upon his daughter and sister and it is explicit there that is coming upon his mother and begot a daughter who is his daughter and sister from mother; and let him count seven sin-offerings — namely that he transgressed and betrothed and married and came upon his mother who is only secondary and kiddushin take effect with her and from her a daughter and came upon her and she is forbidden to him as his mother's sister and his wife's daughter and his daughter — all these unlike what he counted; but for Ra'avad why liable for two is fine. Nevertheless great difficulty — for let him count permissibly from the Torah; one can say we do not hold thus; however per Darkei Moshe in Tur beginning this siman regarding secondary kiddushin if another betrothed she needs a get — one can strain that if so the reason is proven "whoever betroths" etc. — they nullified these kiddushin and left them no room except stringently; and one can also say regarding sacrifice — namely his wife's daughter — must be through his wife with full kiddushin in secondary relations they did not take effect fully and there is desecration of mundane in the Temple — this is strained; and the difficulty requires great examination.
