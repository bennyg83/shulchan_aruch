And wife of mother's brother. Whether from mother, etc. — Beit Shmuel s.k. 16 wrote: wife of mother's brother from mother — Tosafot (Yevamot 21b s.v. they did not forbid) later generations forbade; nevertheless appears from Rambam and Tur like other secondary relations for all matters and compel on this secondary like others; therefore listed among secondary relations; and not like some wanting leniency on this secondary not compelling divorce — and it is not so, end quote; and see responsum Minchat Ani siman 54 regarding case one married mother's brother's wife from mother unintentionally saying permitted and afterward known — not compelled to divorce; he inclined to leniency not compelled since even secondary relation and moreh there is room to judge transgression married unintentionally thinking permitted whether compelled to divorce — for this law compelling divorce in secondary relations not found explicit only baraita Yevamot 85 discussing intentional transgression; but unintentional thinking permitted one can say post factum not compelled like uncertain water siman 17 s.l. 64 in gloss; and though Beit Shmuel siman 7 s.k. 18 wrote to distinguish uncertain water from secondary relations — in secondary erring against established law sage's instruction does not help except regarding sage's instruction; but regarding unintentional transgression saying permitted no reason to distinguish, etc.; however in full secondary relation not because we equate act done — especially from Rosh end chapter HaMedir Yerushalmi implies slightly even secondary no distinction unintentional and intentional; and Rosh's words incline from source of this law compelling divorce in secondary from Yerushalmi not Yevamot baraita; but in our case mother's brother's wife from mother they forbade only in later generations and already struck the gavel — there are lenient views Beit Shmuel brought to exclude from other secondary relations saying if transgressed and consummated do not remove; appears at least regarding unintentional transgression as our case thinking permitted we are not responsible to compel divorce — so it appears to me provided one or two great decisors agree, end quote — see there.
