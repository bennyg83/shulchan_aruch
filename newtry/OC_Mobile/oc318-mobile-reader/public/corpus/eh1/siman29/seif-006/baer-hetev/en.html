Pledge. But if he said be betrothed with pledge and pledge shall be yours — simple matter she is betrothed — Binyamin Ze'ev siman 38.
The dinar. Chelkat Mechokek disagrees and ruled giving document on dinar is safek kiddushin; also concern perhaps perutah in doubt — see there; and so Beit Shmuel — see there.
The pledge. Peri Megadim Tosafot Kiddushin 8 wrote in name of R. Cohen: if man said to fellow I will give you maneh etc. — appears same in kiddushin and need not say acquire body of pledge — end quote. Ba'al Atzamot Yosef 434a doubted whether kiddushin money like gift in this matter. Keneset Hagedolah 60b wrote: to my view simple in kiddushin she is not betrothed at all — see there reason. And so Beit Shmuel. To me also simple R. Cohen's view does not work in kiddushin — otherwise why does Rava challenge Rav Nachman "betrothed with pledge — betrothed" perhaps there deals per R. Cohen; and he answered now also difficult perhaps there he said do not acquire body of pledge — this is not so since plainly wrote betrothed with pledge — betrothed and did not write acquire body of pledge — understand; see Rosh there.
