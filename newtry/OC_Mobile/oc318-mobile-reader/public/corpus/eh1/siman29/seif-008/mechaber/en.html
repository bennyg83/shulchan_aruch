One who says to woman be betrothed to me with this dinar on condition I complete maneh for you and she says on condition you complete hundred for me — he went to his house and she to her house and they did not agree between them and afterward each claimed the other and betrothed and he gave her the dinar — if man claimed woman follow woman's words and he completes hundred for her; and if woman claimed man follow man's words and he completes only maneh.
