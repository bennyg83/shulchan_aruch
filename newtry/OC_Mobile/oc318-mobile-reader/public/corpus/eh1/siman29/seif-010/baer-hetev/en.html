And gave her etc. But if silent — does not work; silence only helps when he says second time at giving you are betrothed and she accepts in silence; but if asks whether I give you will you be betrothed and she silent and accepts — not admission for he only asked and she silent relying on first words give me without kiddushin — Chelkat Mechokek, Beit Yosef, Chelkat Mechokek, Beit Shmuel. Taz ruled if silent — safek. So Ba'al Atzamot Yosef 434, Mahari"t 2:38. See Keneset Hagedolah 61; responsum Mahari"t 1:139 wrote all who did not say yes and silent even in jest — not betrothed. Responsum Rav Betzalel 70 challenged from Kiddushin 13a case of man who snatched and said bring to me and said if I give you betrothed to me — she took and was silent; Rav Nachman said I do not take and mine takes — implies reason I do not take — in matter not hers betrothed even though silent. Therefore ruled there even if said in language of question and she silent — betrothed; Beit Shmuel answered Talmud abbreviates — truly at giving said be betrothed — see there. To me also difficult from what we learned Kiddushin 52b — woman marrying etc. man snatched money from fellows — none concerned for R. Shimon who said plain robbery owners despair etc. — implies if we say plain robbery owners despair betrothed even if silent and did not say yes; and he said question language. Ran wrote some say reason not betrothed because on first words she relied said bring not for kiddushin; but if he said initially if I give betrothed and she said bring or bring from bring as he said — betrothed; this Tosafot 9a "bring throw" view. See Hara'av Sasson 175. Rashba holds even if she said nothing initially — since he said question language if I give betrothed and she did not answer I do not betroth you but said bring or bring from bring — even if he returns and says you are betrothed — does not work; main reason not betrothed because said question language — see Beit Yosef. Per Rashba, Rav Betzalel's difficulty and my difficulty stand — not applicable to Beit Shmuel's answer that at giving said be betrothed; per Rashba only yes works. Per Ran my difficulty also resolved — there he said initially betrothed — then need not Beit Shmuel's answer. See Keneset Hagedolah 60, 61.
In silence only returned etc. Language garbled. Seems should delete words "in silence only" — meaning if she accepted when he returned and said you are betrothed she accepted and also returned and said etc.; word silence belongs before with and accepted betrothed — there deals she accepted in silence as explained Beit Yosef and Nachalat Tzvi; or should read if did not accept in silence only returned — so in Chelkat Mechokek; see Beit Yosef.
Jest. Even if she did not answer in jest language — safek; see Chelkat Mechokek, Beit Shmuel.
