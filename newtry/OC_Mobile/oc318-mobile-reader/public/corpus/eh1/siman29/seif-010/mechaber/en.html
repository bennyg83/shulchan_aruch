If he had types of utensils or types of food or any other thing and she said give me a little of these and he said if I give you you shall be betrothed to me — if she said yes and he gave her behold she is betrothed; but if she said give me of them or throw to me or words whose meaning is do not jest with me in these matters but give me only and he gave her — she is not betrothed. {Rama: And if he said to her at time he gives her you are betrothed to me and she accepted — betrothed (Tur in name of Rosh); and if she accepted in silence only returned and said give me or throw and similar — she is not betrothed (so implied from Rosh's words); and all this when she began saying give me a little; but if he began speaking if I give you you will be betrothed to me and she said to him in jesting language yes and accepted from him — it is doubt kiddushin (Ran's reasoning)}; and likewise if he was drinking wine and she said give me one cup and he said if I give you you are betrothed to me with it and she said give me drink (or give) and he gave her drink — she is not betrothed for matters appear only give me drink and not jest with me in other matter; and if he said at time he gave her you are betrothed to me and she accepted — betrothed.
