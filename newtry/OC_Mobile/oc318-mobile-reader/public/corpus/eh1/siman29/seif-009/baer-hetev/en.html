See siman 38 seif 24: if said cup of wine and found honey — not betrothed; we do not say intention only on cup — there said of wine certainly wine; but if said plainly in this cup I am — Beit Yosef, Chelkat Mechokek. If did not say in this cup but be betrothed with this — means whole not part; if part not his — not betrothed — Rosh Chayon 26. Division between this cup plainly and cup of wine — when not eating and drinking; but if eating and drinking she acquires only wine even if said in this cup plainly — Rashdam 5, Rosh Chayon 56. If eating at Reuven's house and betrothed daughter with it — certainly in wine not cup — Mishpat Tzedek 2:74; and above siman 21 s.k. 32 from Shach. Took chicken and placed in vessel or garment called nest and betrothed — all agree betrothed in it and contents; if one stolen or borrowed — safek — Keneset Hagedolah 60b.
