One who says to woman be betrothed to me with this cup — if it was full water behold she is betrothed in it and in what is inside for they combine to worth of perutah; if full wine behold she is betrothed in it and not in what is inside; if full oil behold she is betrothed in what is inside and not in it — therefore if oil not worth perutah behold she is betrothed in doubt; if oil worth perutah behold she is certainly betrothed; and some disagree and say in water in it and not inside; in wine inside and not in it; in oil or fish brine in it and inside.
