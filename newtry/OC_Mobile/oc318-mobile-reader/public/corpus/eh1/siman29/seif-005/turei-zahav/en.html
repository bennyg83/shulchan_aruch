Here is dinar etc. (should read seif 3 i.e. she said give maneh to so-and-so — here dinar not Rashba's difficulty Taz showed not important person accepted money betrothed; so Maggid Mishneh) Rashba asked here betrothed without important person — unlike here maneh and I betroth needs important person siman 27; Maggid Mishneh answered different there lacked money therefore if not important with what betroth; here lacks no money enjoys fulfilling mitzvah others' money; end his language; if says here maneh give so-and-so and I betroth likewise needs that so-and-so important person then betrothed when that so-and-so afterward says betrothed with benefit received your money as above.
