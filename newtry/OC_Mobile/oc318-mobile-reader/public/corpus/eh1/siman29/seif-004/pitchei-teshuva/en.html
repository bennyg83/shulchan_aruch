Keneset Hagedolah wrote: appears even if object not his only given to me also gift on condition — nevertheless kiddushin even if not given permission explicitly to lend — plain gift on condition has permission give others. However other conditions Rosh wrote certainly must give gift plainly to return then can return when wants as explained Choshen Mishpat 241 — if said on condition return immediately no kiddushin for use must be perutah's worth — with what does he betroth her. Sefer Hamakneh wrote if said with wage of ornament — not betrothed for in rental of utensils also applies changed to rental from beginning to end and is like loan. This law is true — wage of use means wage reaching him to pay after she uses and with wage she commits betrothed and wage is loan. However what Sefer Hamakneh required saying with this benefit what she needed to appease one and give perutah requesting give her this jewelry to adorn time — to my view appears when said with benefit of ornament also betrothed since acquires object through this pulling from betrother object enslaved to her to use and cannot retract — giving actual money; could say laborer has no acquisition in laborer; but here considered as giver gift of object for fruits for time — certainly complete money giving and betrothed — end quote see there.
