One who says to woman you are betrothed to me with hundred dinarim and gave her even one dinar — behold she is betrothed from when she took the dinar and he completes the rest for this is as if he said you are betrothed to me with this dinar on condition I give you hundred zuz — she is betrothed to him from now; {and likewise if one dinar lacking or was bad (Rosh and Ramban); and some disagree (Rashba)}; in what case when he said hundred dinarim plainly; but if he specified and said you are betrothed to me with these hundred dinarim and began counting into her hand {and some say in one of them such as he said these hundred dinarim or begins counting (Rosh and Tur)} — she is not betrothed until he completes for her and even in last dinar both can retract from each other unless he said to her lacking dinar shall be on me as loan and she consented; and likewise if maneh found lacking dinar or found among them copper dinar and she did not recognize initially — she is not betrothed {and they can retract in them (Rashi's view) (and some say even without retraction she is not betrothed (Ran first chapter Kiddushin) until he afterward says be betrothed with this and she is silent)}; and if she can extract through pressure — behold she is betrothed.
