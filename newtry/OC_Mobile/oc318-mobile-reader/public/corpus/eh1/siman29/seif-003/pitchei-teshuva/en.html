In order to betroth her within thirty. Sefer Hamakneh wrote: if gave ring gift on condition return and betroth with it — not betrothed. Keneset Hagedolah wrote on this: to my view plainly betrothed — similar to saying I give you this object gift on condition you acquire from me some other object with object I gave you — certainly works like get where woman gives paper gift that husband write get on it and return and give her complete — woman knows to transfer thus as explained Gittin 20b — see there.
