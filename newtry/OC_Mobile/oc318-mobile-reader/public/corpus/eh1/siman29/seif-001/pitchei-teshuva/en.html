One who says to woman. See Sha'arei Melekh chapter 5 Laws of Marriage law of within time of speech — wrote: in what we say regarding woman gift on condition doubtful to me if she gave to important person betrothed (as written siman 27 seif 9) if she gave gift on condition do we say betrothed — can say since per Tosafot and followers only rabbinic decree lest they say woman acquired through chalipin — here uncommon matter and no decree; or perhaps since reason betrothed with important person is because of benefit she wanted to give perutah to person to appease to accept from her as Rashi wrote — that perutah never returns and similar to what Rama wrote if said with this benefit etc. and concludes nothing emerges from doubt — see there.
