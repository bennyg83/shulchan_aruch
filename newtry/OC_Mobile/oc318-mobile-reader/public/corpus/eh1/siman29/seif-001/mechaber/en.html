One must give the money as a complete gift and law of one who betroths with pledge or maneh and it is found lacking or bad. It contains 10 seifim. One who says to a woman "You are betrothed to me with this dinar on condition you return it to me" — she is not betrothed. {Rama: But a woman who gave someone a gift on condition to return to her after thirty days in order to betroth her with it within thirty days and he gave her within thirty for kiddushin — she is betrothed (teshuvah Rosh kelal 35); some say if he gave her gift on condition to return and said to her with benefit you enjoy within the time you are betrothed to me — it is kiddushin (R' Yerucham).}
