On condition you return. Beit Yosef wrote deals with doubled condition otherwise condition void act stands; see Tosafot there in this sugya — wrote if gives etrog conditioned on condition return — if not returned did not leave even without doubled condition because of obvious assessment; in Beit Hashem I saw he challenged Beit Yosef from these Tosafot; cannot say Tosafot wrote only regarding etrog because obvious assessment he gave to return since he also needs etrog; per Tosafot views not dealing doubled condition — entire sugya must deal without doubled condition even in kiddushin yet condition stands; however for law Beit Yosef properly wrote Rosh end Yevamot also asked this difficulty and answered even etrog needs doubled condition — then in kiddushin plainly deals specifically doubled condition and Beit Yosef properly wrote if not doubled — proper kiddushin i.e. Beit Yosef so per Tur who holds plainly like Rosh; for us safek kiddushin since Tosafot and Rosh dispute; however can say even per Rosh we do not say if not doubled condition act stands i.e. kiddushin because she did not rely — she thinks must return — similar presence of three or betrothing with another's debt we say she did not rely; so too all conditions explained siman 38 we hold if not doubled condition — full kiddushin for there gives perutah with which betroths completely only conditions with it — then say condition void if not doubled and act kiddushin stands; however can say proper kiddushin per Rosh because even if she did not rely nevertheless like gift on condition return and per law betrothed — only because similar to chalipin not kiddushin; here truly not gift on condition return nor similar chalipin — only she thinks must return; per Rosh certainly betrothed. Regarding condition and act one matter explained siman 143; see siman 38 many poskim hold if said on condition need not double.
Not betrothed. Even though we hold generally gift on condition return is gift — in kiddushin sages enacted not kiddushin because similar chalipin when returns kiddushin and chalipin not kiddushin at all even rabbinically as Talmud beginning Kiddushin; Tosafot and Rosh wrote sages annulled kiddushin; so all poskim imply not kiddushin at all not like Shach beginning Kiddushin who wrote needs get; Chelkat Mechokek ruled not like Shach nor like Rashi.
