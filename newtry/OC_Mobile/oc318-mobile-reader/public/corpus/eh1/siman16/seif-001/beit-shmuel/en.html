This dispute depends on the dispute I wrote siman 4 regarding Rambam — we hold like Rava initially in their gentile state there is marriage and "you shall not intermarry" refers in their gentile state; also he ruled like R' Shimon that all nations are included in this negative command. And some say: those who hold like Rava to the conclusion — "you shall not intermarry" refers to the seven nations in their conversion, but in their gentile state marriage is inapplicable; also for Rambam it appears he is liable only through marriage in the manner of marriage and not mere betrothal alone nor intercourse alone; and for other poskim he is liable for the seven nations through intercourse without betrothal because of "you shall not take," and in their conversion liable because of "you shall not intermarry" immediately at betrothal even if he did not consummate.
