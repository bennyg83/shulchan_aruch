Prohibition of a non-Jewish woman and a maidservant. It contains 6 seifim. An Israelite who had relations with a non-Jewish woman in the manner of marriage, or an Israelite woman who was lain with by a non-Jew in the manner of marriage — behold these receive lashes from the Torah, as it is said: you shall not intermarry with them (and some dispute this) (Tur). But one who comes upon a non-Jewish woman in the manner of promiscuity incidentally is liable for her rabbinically because of non-Jewish woman and because of zona; and they strike him lashes of rebellion. And if he designated her for himself in promiscuity he is liable for her rabbinically because of niddah, maidservant, non-Jewish woman, zona. And if he was a kohen, even if he came upon her incidentally he receives lashes from the Torah because of zona.
