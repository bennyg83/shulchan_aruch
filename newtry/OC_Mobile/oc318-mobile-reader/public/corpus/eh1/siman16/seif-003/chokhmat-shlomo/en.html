There — one who comes upon the gentile woman if zealots did not strike him. NB: this law that zealots strike him is explicit in Gemara; and Rama in Choshen Mishpat siman 425 — specifically at the time of the act and not afterward. And it appears to me the reason of the matter: granted regarding court death where repentance does not help — therefore since he transgressed he is certainly liable death; but here since in court he is not liable except zealots strike him — and this is only like death by Heaven; and in death by Heaven repentance helps; and since we hold one who says on condition I am completely righteous even if he is wicked — betrothal is valid perhaps he contemplated repentance in his heart — if so here too perhaps he contemplated repentance in his heart; and granted at time of act repentance does not help — it is like immersing with vermin in hand; and per this it appears to me this is specifically if he was not established three times; but if established three times — then since we hold Choshen Mishpat siman 388 regarding informer when established three times permitted to kill even after the act — all the more here when established three times zealots strike him even afterward; and as they brought proof from one who was lashed and changed and lashed a third time — they put him in a cell as I see correct in this; examine.
