And this sin, etc. Yevamot 100b — there what does it warn, etc.
One who comes, etc. Meaning even though gentile who comes on Israelite daughter is not among capital crimes as stated Yoreh De'ah siman 157 seif 1 in gloss and as stated Ketubot 3b "and to expound for them," etc. and Sanhedrin 74b "but Esther publicly," etc. and does not challenge "but revelation of forbidden relations"; and as Rabbenu Tam wrote there because they are not liable death for intercourse with gentile unlike one who comes on non-Jewish woman in public as stated mishnah chapter 1:2 and Taz there; and specifically in public as stated.
