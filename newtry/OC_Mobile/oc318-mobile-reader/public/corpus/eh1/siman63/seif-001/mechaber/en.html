Mitzvah intercourse and its blessing. It contains 2 seifim. It is permitted to have relations with a virgin on Shabbat. One who has relations with a virgin — since he had mitzvah intercourse he withdraws immediately; even if she is a minor who has not reached the time to see and did not see. Nevertheless that intercourse he completes in the normal way with a living organ; and even if he examined and did not find blood she is impure — perhaps she saw a drop of blood like a mustard seed and the semen covered it — and she must stop in purity and examine every seven days and she should not begin to examine until the fifth day for her usage like any woman who had relations and afterward saw; and he treats her like any niddah regarding distancing — except a complete niddah he is forbidden to sleep on her bed even when she is not on the bed, and the bride he is permitted to sleep on that bed after she rose from beside him even on a sheet that has blood on it (and see Yoreh Deah siman 193).
