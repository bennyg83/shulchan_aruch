(ב) Or acquired for her. If he said "receive it for her," Baal HaItur holds that his intent is to make him a delivery agent, since he did not say it the way she said it, and she cannot certainly make him a receiving agent; therefore his intent is that he be a delivery agent. Rashba and Ran disagree and hold that even if he said "receive it," his intent is that he be a receiving agent, as the agent said that he was a receiving agent. It requires study to reconcile the sugya there according to Baal HaItur: what did it ask there, "is this not receiving upon receiving," meaning that he said "receive" in response to the agent's statement that he is a receiving agent? What is the question? For "receive" means "receive and bring." What is written that if he said "acquire for her" he becomes a delivery agent, Perishah wrote: do not ask from what is written that if she made him a receiving agent and the husband said "acquire for her," we do not say that he uprooted the receiving agency.
