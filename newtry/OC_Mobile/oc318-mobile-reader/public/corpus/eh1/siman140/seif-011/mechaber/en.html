An apostate who sends a get to his wife: besides having him swear, accept a cherem, and accept all the curses of the Torah that he will not nullify either the get or the agency, they should make another repair. When he gives the get to the agent, he should say to him: acquire this get on behalf of my wife, so-and-so daughter of so-and-so, and she is hereby divorced from me and permitted to every man. Afterward the sage should say to the apostate: your wife is already permitted to every man; but for extra assurance, take the get from the agent's hand, give it back to him, and say to him: take this get to my wife, so-and-so daughter of so-and-so; your hand shall be like my hand, your mouth like my mouth, and your action like my action, and she shall be divorced from me and permitted to every man. (See below, siman 141 seif 59.)
