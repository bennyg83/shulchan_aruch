But if he was wealthy, etc. But they do not compel him — even though clothing and dowry we derive from a verse: and your daughters you shall give — and it is stated in the Talmud: meaning clothe her and endow her and give her. Nevertheless they compel only to marry them off, as they do not compel regarding the amount of dowry gift — only to marry them off. And before he marries her off, garments are included in sustenance, as written in the aforementioned siman. Bach wrote they compel for clothing. See Hagahot Maimoniyot in Kiddushin.
