Safek chalalah. Avnei Heitev; and what he wrote "but he himself who returned and had relations with her receives lashes," etc. — these are the words of Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel. It is astonishing that they did not mention the Bach's words here — for it is explicit in his words that even he himself who came upon her afterward does not receive lashes. See responsa Brit Avraham vol. Even HaEzer siman 1 letter 11 and siman 8 letter 9; and see further in Chiddushei Yoreh De'ah siman 3 letter 11 from this.
