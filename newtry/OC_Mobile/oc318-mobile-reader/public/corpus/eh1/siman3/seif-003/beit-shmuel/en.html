Not concerned lest mother gentile. Apparently difficult: if testify he is kohen — perforce not gentile; why reason Rashbatz compelled witnesses do not marry gentiles? If discusses testifying father kohen not him — fear son of divorcee or chalutzah as nearby. Chelkat Mechokek answered: here discusses ascending Torah as kohen — even if only testified father not him — not concerned son of divorcee/chalutzah; rather because among gentiles accustomed marry gentiles — teaches not way to marry gentiles; hold him kohen to ascend for Torah reading.
