We are not concerned for reciprocity — Rambam here and chapter 12 of laws of tithes does not bring the establishment "with craftsman's tools in hand," etc. — Mishneh Torah for conclusion; and impossible to elevate from terumah to lineage without tools, etc. — Taz and Perishah wondered per Tosafot on "and impossible." Ran challenged: from where known entire sugya is dispute of R' Meir and Rabbanan — only derabbanan terumah; baraita implies even Torah terumah; in my view one can say. Tosafot on "and so two people" wrote: however per our establishment of dispute in elevating — fine, etc. It appears to me perforce here Tosafot follow and Rashi explains "with tools" — wrote that if per R' Chiya — question perforce from derabbanan dispute Rabbanan against Rabbanan; and Tosafot "with tools," etc. — meaning Rabbanan concerned for reciprocity only regarding kohen because tools in hand — no concern; if so why concern for captive? Answer: captives they were lenient — must teach kohen for terumah lenient from captive — as proofs Tosafot from casual speech; since for Rabbanan no elevation — perforce even this establishment must discuss Torah terumah and Rabbi discusses am ha'aretz; or here they hold Rashi's explanation; therefore law explains R' Chiya; per them not so; if so per R' Chiya's explanation what answers "with tools," etc. — no concern — we are concerned; question — must teach captive no concern; answer captives lenient — terumah lenient from captive; must say am ha'aretz lenient; truly for am ha'aretz no tools needed; difference between am ha'aretz and craftsmen: this primarily deoraita and that primarily derabbanan; only for Torah terumah tools needed; if so question from where to challenge Rabbanan against Rabbanan — perhaps believed to feed only derabbanan; however this is not so — establishment R' Yehuda's reason because of reciprocity questions from captive — must discuss deoraita per Tosafot's words; fine challenge unlike letter "impossible" — Rambam holds baraita only derabbanan; no question from craftsmen like no question from captive; no tools needed; resolved Ran's difficulty who holds Rambam's language closed — "impossible" implies suffices without "with tools," etc.; as stated. If said even deoraita no elevation — nevertheless discusses derabbanan; deoraita for other reason; or because reciprocity Gemara should say Rabbanan against Rabbanan — no question; per letter here in laws of tithes am ha'aretz; or seems to him plain to Shulchan Aruch that establishment of elevation — perforce only derabbanan; deoraita from Torah no elevation.
