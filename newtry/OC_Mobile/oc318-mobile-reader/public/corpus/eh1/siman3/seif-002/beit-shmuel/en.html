Even his father. Reason explained in Gemara — believed through migo it is in his power to feed terumah. Rambam holds even if known he is father — nevertheless believed; so Tosafot chapter 2 Ketubot. Ramban holds when known father — not believed; see Maggid. Father believed for terumah nowadays — same other matters; all one witness believes — father believed; for lineage not believed. When comes to marry woman — fear lest mamzer since no presumption of fitness; one witness also not believed as Tur siman 2 in Rama's name; even though we rule anything not presumption of fitness nor presumption of prohibition — one witness believed Yoreh De'ah 267; also safek mamzer Torah permitted enter congregation — must say because of lineage elevation not believed; accurate from Rama's language Tur brought. Kohen who married divorcee and says not kohen — not believed; compelled to divorce; see Levush.
So-and-so kohen borrowed. Per Rashba and Rama in such case when language of witnesses — witnesses write "so-and-so kohen borrowed" — helps even for lineage; perforce Gemara only when wrote "I so-and-so kohen borrowed" since borrower's language — say witnesses did not testify he is kohen only testified on loan itself. Rambam holds even if witnesses wrote "so-and-so kohen borrowed" — not helps lineage; Ran wrote reason: say witnesses wrote thus because presumed kohen ascends Torah as kohen but did not know he is kohen. Even per Rambam if borrower's language wrote "I so-and-so kohen borrowed" — helps derabbanan terumah and nazir; sugya also borrower's language — only lineage not terumah. Mechaber rules like Rambam not Rashba. Per Tosafot sounds if two Yosef ben Shimon — witnesses must specify if kohen to write sign who borrower; then say elevate even lineage even borrower's language. However per Rambam seems not helps lineage — say what they write he is kohen because presumed kohen and sign who borrower presumed kohen; second not presumed kohen; nevertheless did not testify this is kohen — not helps lineage since presumption not helps lineage. Per Rashba helps even lineage. Thereby resolves Choshen Mishpat difficulty from Beit HaYotzer siman 49 Rama in Rashba responsum two Yosef ben Shimon one father alive one dead — if witnesses wrote Yosef ben Shimon shlit"a to this — sign this whose father alive is borrower and extract money; borrower's language not sign. Challenged why extract money if witnesses' language; why not helps lineage if witnesses' language. Per Ketzot Hachoshen there sign to know borrower helps witnesses' language since wrote this one presumed father alive is borrower; here lineage not helps since even testify this presumed kohen — did not testify they knew he is kohen. Again saw Shach there wrote this reasoning. Per view say per Rambam even borrower's language wrote shlit"a — extract since anyway sign to know borrower; witnesses would not sign unless knew sign; for him witnesses sign testify all written in document. Per Rashba witnesses testify only loan body — therefore there borrower's language not extract. Rashba must say what helps two Yosef ben Shimon extract when writes kohen — specifically witnesses' language not borrower.
I so-and-so kohen, etc. Ran wrote reason because not less than casual speech he is kohen; holds even not telling other matters — casual speech; needs answer why Gemara said "I remember," etc. — implies if said plainly I am kohen not casual speech. Choshen Mishpat wrote what helps when wrote I so-and-so kohen — specifically Israel where terumah and not called Torah without proof — therefore helps if wrote. Abroad ascends Torah as kohen — say wrote because ascends as kohen; not relied in Israel.
