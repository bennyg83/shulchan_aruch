Regarding him. Even if known he is his father — believed. Other poskim hold when known he is his father — not believed — Avnei Shmuel. Incident: one served in kehunah fifteen years then betrothed a divorcee and said he is not kohen — not believed; compelled to divorce — R' Yehuda HaLevi siman 33, Chut HaMeshulash siman 110, Shevut Yaakov siman 93 part 1; see Levush. Law of firstborns definitely redeemed through him; those in doubt redeemed through him need not redeem again — see Chut HaMeshulash siman 110, Maharit siman 148, Mabit part 1 siman 219, Perach Mateh Aharon part 1 siman 51, Rashdam EH siman 235, Be'er Sheva 4:116.
Kohen. See Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel.
Himself. Specifically in Israel where terumah exists and he is not called to Torah without proof — therefore signing helps. But abroad where one ascends to Torah as kohen — say he wrote "I so-and-so am kohen" because he ascends as kohen; therefore not relied upon in Israel — Choshen Mishpat, Beit Shmuel. Regarding what is written above s.k. 1 in name of Beit HaYotzer — their words are hidden; see Yoreh De'ah 61 s.k. 13 from Shach in name of Perach.
