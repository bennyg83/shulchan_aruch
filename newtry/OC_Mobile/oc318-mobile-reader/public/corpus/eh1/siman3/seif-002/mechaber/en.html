If one witness testifies about him — he is believed to feed him terumah nowadays, to read first in the Torah, and to lift his hands; even his father is believed regarding him. We even elevate from documents to kehunah nowadays. How so? If it was written in a document: "So-and-so the kohen borrowed a mana from so-and-so," with witnesses signed on it — behold he has presumption of kohen like kohanim of this time. So too we elevate from nesiat kapayim and reading first in the Torah to be kohen like kohanim of this time. {Rama: Some say if he signed himself "I, so-and-so, am a kohen" — it suffices nowadays (Ran, chapter 2 of Ketubot).}
