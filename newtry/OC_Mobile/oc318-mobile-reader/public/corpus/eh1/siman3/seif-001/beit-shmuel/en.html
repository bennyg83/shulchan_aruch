And he may not read in the Torah. Even though there is no prohibition if he reads — nevertheless concern lest he eat terumah, for we rule we elevate from Torah reading to derabbanan terumah as stated below. This law is not per the dispute I brought in previous siman whether we fear lest chalal one whose family unknown — even per view not concerned he is disqualified, nevertheless fear lest he is not kohen but kosher Israelite. Bach's words on this are wondrous.
And lift his hands. Ran and Maggid did not write he lifts hands — only that he reads Torah since no prohibition; but lifting hands one can say forbidden — Torah positive prohibition "so shall you bless" — you bless, not strangers. Difficult on Rav Rama who permits lifting hands; so too challenged in Choshen Mishpat. Must say Rama compelled thus since Ran and Maggid wrote in Remakh's name our custom to believe him for Torah reading; not concerned lest eat terumah since terumah nowadays derabbanan; they answered since we do not practice eating terumah in our lands. Difficult — still concern lest ascend for nesiat kapayim. Rather certainly anything without benefit — not concerned he will do prohibition; no benefit from this — therefore not concerned except lest eat terumah; no concern for nazir or ascending Torah. In Israel where terumah practiced as Tur Yoreh De'ah 331 — not believed even nowadays. General principle: for lineage all require two kosher witnesses; previous siman I wrote meaning of lineage. Rambam holds we elevate from terumah to lineage — therefore need two witnesses he is kohen to eat Torah terumah; see Ran and Maggid on dissenters. For derabbanan terumah one witness suffices; mishnah when they testify about each other — believed — perforce derabbanan terumah; therefore one witness believed. If so what taught initially "if said I am kohen not believed" — even for derabbanan terumah not believed to testify about himself.
Lashes. Since he holds himself kohen — believed regarding himself to be stringent; definite forewarning on his word — Maggid.
Safek chalalah. Another kohen who had relations — not lashes for definite chalalah; seed safek chalalim. But he himself who returned — lashes for chalalah per his words as lashes for divorcee.
Took me from school. So in Gemara — otherwise fear lest slave; so Tosafot there and as Gemara end chapter 2 Ketubot; Tur omitted this and did not write "took from school." Choshen Mishpat wrote reason: since all families presumption of fitness — not concerned lest slave. Tur holds even unknown family — nevertheless not concerned lest slave as previous siman. Even per Tur there halachah follows mishnah one marrying woman needs checking — for marrying or Torah terumah; derabbanan terumah not concerned lest slave. Rambam wrote like Mechaber — must read "took from school" — proves Maggid's first answer who holds unknown family concerned lest slave or mamzer; therefore must say took from school. Mechaber stam ruled like him; Rama did not emend — from which unknown family concerned lest mamzer or slave. What Mechaber did not write seif 5 "took from school" — error; Beit HaYotzer siman 35 wrote explicitly took from school.
To kehunah. Established in chapter HaGozel — derabbanan terumah; from Rambam nesiat kapayim equivalent derabbanan terumah even though positive mitzvah of nazir — must say as "anything without benefit not concerned."
