Regarding them. Such as erroneous kiddushin exiting from first without get; so too discusses "they are onenim for him" — otherwise how find it when both alive? Perforce first kohen husband divorced her — then cannot find she married second kohen.
For kehunah. Chelkat Mechokek wrote apparently likewise reverse: committed zenut with kohen within three months of kohen husband's death — child also disqualified for kehunah. Beit Shmuel wrote on this not compelled — see there. Chelkat Mechokek challenged: why not follow majority — majority of relations attributed to husband; even if committed zenut under husband child kosher — see Yevamot 100b. Rather not zenut; Rashi there both came through zenut — implies specifically both zenut; but marriage after zenut attributed to marriage, end quote — meaning proved: since Rashi wrote both came through zenut — why not write plainly second married after first came through zenut? Rather certainly Rashi holds mishnah cannot discuss this — for then attributed certainly to second since all relations attributed to husband — he is son of second for everything; unlike Maharsha there. So appears Chelkat Mechokek's intent. Then Beit Shmuel's words on this void — see and examine. But at least true law: not attributed to husband except where committed zenut under husband — then since husband available certainly majority of relations from him; but if zenut before marriage — say already pregnant.
