Kohen. Meaning his father — but father's father too. If uncle or Levi's son presumed kohen — Maharam of Trani part 2 siman 40 doubted if he is kohen — see there.
Presumption. Meaning presumption that her sons are bound after her — relied upon even for lineage — Avodat HaGershuni siman 79; Tosafot Kiddushin 4a.
