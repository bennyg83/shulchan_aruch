One who comes and says "I am a kohen," and one witness testifies that he knows his father is a kohen — we do not elevate him to kehunah on his word, lest he be a chalal, until the witness testifies that this one is a kohen. But if his father was presumed a kohen, or two came and testified that this one's father is a kohen — behold he has his father's presumption. (In every matter we follow presumption — for we burn and stone on presumption.)
