He took me out of school. Beit Shmuel s.k. 5 — until "that one whose family is unknown to us we are concerned lest he be a mamzer or slave." See Shaarei Melekh ch. 20 of Hilchot Issurei Bi'ah din 5, who challenged him — for certainly Maran the Mechaber does not hold so, since at the head of the siman he wrote in the language of the Tur: one who comes and says "I am a kohen," etc. — he prohibits himself regarding a divorcee; if so, even regarding all women in the world he is forbidden, etc. What he compelled from what he quoted the Rambam's language "and he took me out of school" is no proof at all — for if so we do not say that even when he has no family presumption we are not concerned lest he be a slave or mamzer; rather specifically one who claims certainly, who knows in himself that he is a valid Israelite — therefore "he may not" and is believed through himself; unlike the case of school, where he himself does not know if he is valid — only that he came to testify in his adulthood what he saw in his youth. In this, certainly we are concerned lest he be a slave. This distinction is required from the words of the Tosafot, etc. — see there. And see what I wrote above siman 2 s.k. 1.
