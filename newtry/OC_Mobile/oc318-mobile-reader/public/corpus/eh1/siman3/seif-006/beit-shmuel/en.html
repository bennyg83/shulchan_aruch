And one witness testifies. Maggid wrote one witness not believed unless testifies he is kohen; if testifies on father — not believed — only what Chazal said; Gemara testifies he is kohen — implies father not helps. Per above distinction known family vs unknown — can give reason: father presumed or two testified father kohen — no longer fear chalal; if not presumed and only one witness — fear chalal.
After presumption. Meaning presumption sons bound after her — relied even for lineage though we rule bound after animal not helps Yoreh De'ah 79; see Tosafot Kiddushin 80a and Maggid chapter 2; Tur relies presumption bound after her even for lineage.
