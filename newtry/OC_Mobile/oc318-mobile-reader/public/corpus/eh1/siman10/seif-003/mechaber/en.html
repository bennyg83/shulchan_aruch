One who divorces his wife because of a bad reputation that spread about her, or because she is a niddah, or because she is an ailonit — he may not return her. And some say specifically when he said to her: "Because of this I am divorcing you," and doubled his words to say: "Were it not for this I would not be divorcing you." But if he did not double his words he may return her. And some say that if he said to her "Because of this I am divorcing you" — even if he did not double his words he may not return her. And there is one who says that even if he did not say to her "Because of this I am divorcing you" — he may not return her.
