Chelkat Mechokek wrote: so too an orphan — meaning this orphan who was divorced from husband one and again divorced from husband two in her minority is permitted to husband one from whom she was divorced through her father. In my humble opinion it is not comparable — for marriage of a deaf man is different, for the Sages did not enact marriage for him, as Tosafot explain siman 67. But a minor who exits by get has Tosafot; and one can say it appears more like a divorcee. Without this, in my humble opinion one cannot establish a foundation for the reason of the RaMaH z"l who gave to explain the division of the matter — for we see a minor who exits by get is forbidden to a kohen, as explained siman 6; and presumably in my humble opinion so too a deaf man who divorced is forbidden to a kohen, and we do not say they did not enact marriage for him to weaken great power. Therefore in my humble opinion these rabbinic laws are uprooted here and we have no authority to innovate beyond what is explicit. And were it not that the RaMaH z"l found the law of a deaf man in the Yerushalmi he would not have invented it from his own mind; but to invent our laws anew from reasons we have no authority.
