From this one learns for all rabbinic marriages: if the first divorced — whose marriage is rabbinic — and she married another, she is forbidden to the first. But if for the first marriage is biblical and he divorced and married one whose marriage is rabbinic, it does not forbid the first. All this when the first divorced; but if the first did not divorce — whose marriage is rabbinic — even if one whose kiddushin are biblical betrothed her, she is permitted to the first, and we are not concerned in such a case lest they say the first divorced and now he is returning his divorcee, since there is no biblical prohibition. Even though when he married a minor and she grew up with him and he did not have relations after she grew up — the Rambam wrote ch. 1 and below end of siman 155: if another betrothed her and the first did not divorce, she is forbidden to the first lest they say the first had relations with her — then there is a biblical prohibition.
