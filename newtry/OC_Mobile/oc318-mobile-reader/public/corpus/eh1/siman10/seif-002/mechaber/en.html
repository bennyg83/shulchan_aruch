A deaf man who divorced by hint and she went and was betrothed to another deaf man — and needless to say to a competent person — she is forbidden to return to her deaf husband. But the wife of a competent man who was divorced and went and married a deaf man and was divorced — she is permitted to return to her competent husband. (And see above siman 119 whether it is permitted to dwell with her in a courtyard after he divorced her.)
