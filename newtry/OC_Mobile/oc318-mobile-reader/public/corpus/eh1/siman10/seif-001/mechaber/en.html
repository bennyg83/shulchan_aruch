Which divorced woman is permitted to return to her husband. It contains 7 seifim. One who divorces his wife and afterward she committed adultery — she is permitted to return to her husband. {Rama: So too if she committed adultery with the first and was betrothed to a second and he divorced her — she is permitted to return to the first, even if she was designated to him initially (responsum of the Rosh klal 53 and Maharam of Padua siman 19).} But if she was betrothed to another and he divorced her or died — she is forbidden to return to the first; {and even if she was not actually divorced but only due to a general rumor (the Rosh klal 5) (and see above siman 6 and above siman 46 seif 5 and seif 6).}
