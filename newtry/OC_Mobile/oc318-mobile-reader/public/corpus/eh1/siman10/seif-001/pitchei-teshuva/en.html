One who divorces his wife. Rabbenu Yerucham wrote in note 1: there is room to question — a woman who was divorced with a valid get biblically and invalid rabbinically and she went and committed adultery — whether she is permitted to return to her husband ab initio; do we say we apply to her "and she became defiled" rabbinically since it was stated to him and the get is disqualified rabbinically? Or perhaps since the get is valid biblically, even though she committed adultery she is permitted to her husband? And he expanded on this and brought proofs that she is permitted to return to her husband — see there at length.
