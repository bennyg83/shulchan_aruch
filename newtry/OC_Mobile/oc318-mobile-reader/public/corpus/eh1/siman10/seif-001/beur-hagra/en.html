One who divorces, etc. Be'er HaGolah, and so too the Gemara and Tosafot 108b s.v. alma, etc., and Rif and Rosh in ch. 1 of Yevamot wrote: "and she became" and "to a man" are written — and he explained in Hagahot on ch. 1: since he does not establish even for the Sages except regarding a sotah who committed adultery under her husband — see there. Likkut: "One who divorces, etc." — the Rif wrote: because "and she became" and "to a man" are written in it. And it is seemingly wondrous — for according to R' Yehudah ben Kefar it was stated there. But I found in Hagahot 40 there: and that which he concludes even for adultery — "and she became" and "to a man" are written in it — this is from the words of R' Yehudah; but Rabbenu Tam explained in the name of the Gaon: a deaf man who had relations with a divorcee — even for adultery she is not forbidden to the husband who divorced her — "and she became" and "to a man" are written in it — end quote. And accordingly it comes out even for the Sages; and so we say at end of ch. 3 of Sotah: all agree that it is not, etc. — this is the rule, etc. That which in the first chapter of Megillah initially regarding coercion, etc. — see there 15a s.v. beshem, etc.; and see Tosafot Yevamot 108b s.v. alma, etc. — end quote.
