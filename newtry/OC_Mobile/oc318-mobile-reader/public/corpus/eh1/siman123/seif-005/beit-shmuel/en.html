One should not divorce at night. This means that the get is not delivered at night, but it may be written and signed at night, as written in siman 127. In Choshen Mishpat, siman 248, several decisors are cited who hold that even bedieved it is invalid. See Responsa Rama. If the congregation prayed Ma'ariv, even if he did not pray, it is considered night. Beit Yosef in siman 136 wrote that he did not know the reason for this law. Some say it is because arranging a get is like judgment and judgment is not done at night. He challenged this reason, because taking payment for arranging a get is permitted since it is not judgment. See Seder HaGet.
