One who stipulates money to his son-in-law and does not wish to give it to him. It contains 1 seif. One who stipulates money to his son-in-law and the father went to another country, or he has none — she may say to her husband I did not stipulate for myself, or marry me without dowry, or release me with a get. But if she stipulated for herself and her hand did not reach it — behold she sits until she finds what she stipulated or until she dies. When does this apply? To an adult; but to a minor who stipulated for herself — they compel him to give a get or he enters without dowry. {Rama: And all this when the woman's hand cannot reach to give what her father stipulated; but if her hand can reach she must give what her father stipulated and she cannot say marry or release (Hagahot Alfasi chapter Two Judges of Decrees and responsum of Maharam end Laws of Marriage). And all this discusses only before she married; but after she married — certainly he cannot betray his wife because they do not give him what was stipulated for him; rather he is obligated in everything a man is obligated to his wife (Hagahot Mordechai chapter Two Judges of Decrees). One who stipulated to give to his daughter and afterward says he gave to his daughter — it does not help; rather he must give to the couple (Maharil siman 137). And see above siman 2 that a person should not quarrel over dowry even though it was stipulated for him.}
