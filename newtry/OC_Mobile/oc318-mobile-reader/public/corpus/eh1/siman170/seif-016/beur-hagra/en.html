(א) Or upon her rival. Like a rival after maamar, as above.
(ב) Or upon, etc. This is learned from the previous case, and as written below: according to the law, etc.
(ג) There is room to say, etc. An explicit Gemara there: or a minor after a minor. All the more so regarding maamar, which is stronger, as Tosafot wrote there, s.v. "and likewise," etc. This is what he wrote: and a get, etc. See the Rosh there, who wrote that what we say, "they made the intercourse," etc., but did not say "like his maamar" also, is like the Gemara’s answer: these words apply with an adult after, etc.
(ד) And a get, etc. Not like Shmuel, for it is specifically according to Rabbi Meir that get is in place of chalitzah, as the above Tosafot wrote, and as written in the Gemara there and there, 3b. Since we do not hold like Rabbi Meir regarding chalitzah, as written on 105b, the same applies to get. See Tosafot there, s.v. "Rabbi Meir says," etc., and the Rosh there.
