So, too, statement (an explanation: a sister-in-law is acquired, by Torah [law], through intercourse, nothing else, but the sages amended that he must betroth her first, and this is called "statement", the explanation [of which is] "the sages' statement") does not acquire in her a complete acquisition: for if he wishes to divorce her after the statement she needs [both] a bill [of divorce] for the statement and chalitsah to release the connection.
