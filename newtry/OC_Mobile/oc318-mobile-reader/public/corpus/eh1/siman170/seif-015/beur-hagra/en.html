(א) He made maamar, etc. 52b: Rav Chananya asked, etc.; resolve, etc. We act according to Rabbah’s resolution. See Rashi there, s.v. "or perhaps," etc.
(ב) And she is not permitted, etc. The above Rashi.
(ג) And some say, etc. Tosafot there, s.v. "or perhaps," etc. They disagree regarding what is written "even she," whether it refers specifically to the Mishnah and to the brothers, or to the world at large. Since they established it there, it implies like Rashi. See Maggid Mishneh.
