(א) When he performs yibbum, etc. As written in the Tosefta and Yerushalmi: the second rival performs chalitzah or enters yibbum. The Rosh brought this at the beginning of chapter 3. So too in the Tosefta: all who are forbidden, etc., and he wrote above, seif 5.
(ב) He performs chalitzah to the second. This means even to the second. See Be'er HaGolah and Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 5.
(ג) And there are, etc. This requires study, for if he calls this an inferior chalitzah, then the same should apply that her intercourse is an inferior intercourse. We say in Perek Rabban Gamliel that after an inferior chalitzah nothing follows, and inferior intercourse, etc. Yet he ruled that her intercourse exempts her rival, as written at the beginning of siman 174. Furthermore, it is a kal vachomer: intercourse should be no less than chalitzah. See above, beginning of siman 174. But regarding what the Maggid Mishneh wrote from what is written on 20b: he had relations with her or, etc., one can say that that baraita follows the one who says there is no zikah. So too it appears in the Rashba. See Tosafot 26b, s.v. "from the fact that you said," etc., and according to Ri it appears, etc.
