Someone to whom two yevamot from one house (i.e. one husband) fell for consideration of yibbum, one is forbidden to the yavam as a secondary (i.e. Rabbinic) incestuous relation, and the other is permitted for yibbum, if he elects to perform yibbum, he does so with the permitted woman, and if he elects to perform chalitzah, he does so with the woman forbidden as a secondary incestuous relation. There is one sage who says that if performed chalitzah with the forbidden woman, her (permitted) co-wife is not thereby released (for general marriage), but if he performed chalitzah with the permitted woman, then the forbidden (to him) woman is released (for general marriage).
