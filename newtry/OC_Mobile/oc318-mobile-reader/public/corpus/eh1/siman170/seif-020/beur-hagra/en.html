(א) Two yevamin, etc. What is written on 24a: Shila taught, etc.; what is the reason, etc.? This is not so with a biblical case.
(ב) With a get. Such as where he came immediately after her kiddushin, so that even without kiddushin, whichever way one looks at it, a get suffices.
(ג) Until it is known, etc. This is not precise; the same applies if he is uncertain, as above. Rather, it excludes where it is known that his brother ultimately performed yibbum, or where it was never known at all that his brother performed yibbum, for this is a double safek: perhaps he did not perform yibbum; and if you say he did perform yibbum, perhaps he did so only at the end. This is what he wrote: until he knows, etc., at first. As written on 119a: she had, etc. See Rashi, s.v. "she is not," etc. But lechatchilah it is certainly forbidden, for there is only one safek here: perhaps he did not perform yibbum.
