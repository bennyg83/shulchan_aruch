(א) That which there is no, etc. The Mishnah: and the intercourse at the time, etc.; and the Gemara there: the intercourse of the disqualified ones has effect afterward, etc.
(ב) Such as, etc. For if he performs chalitzah, etc. 51a: and Rabban Gamliel agrees. See Rashi there. So too in the Yerushalmi there: and Rabban Gamliel agrees, etc. How is the case? There were three yevamot. He made maamar with this one, had relations with this one, and gave a get to the third, etc. He gave a get to this one, had relations with this one, and made maamar with the third, etc. And there at the end of the chapter it is taught: Three yevamot to one yavam. He made maamar with this one, had relations with this one, and gave a get to the third. Rabbi Natan says, etc., and the Rabbis say he is forbidden to the relatives of all three, and they require three gittin and chalitzah to one of them. There is a scribal error, and it should read "maamar with the third." So it is in the Tosefta, end of chapter 7.
(ג) And she too, etc. In the Mishnah: he gave a get and had relations, etc.; a get to this one and had relations with this one, etc.
(ד) But chalitzah, etc. In the Mishnah, and not like the Tanna of the baraita, 54b.
(ה) But if, etc. 53a: and here with an inferior chalitzah, etc. All the more so according to what I wrote in siman 13.
