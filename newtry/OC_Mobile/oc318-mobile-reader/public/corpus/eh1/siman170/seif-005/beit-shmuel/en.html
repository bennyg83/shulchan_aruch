And some say. The source of this law is the sugya at the beginning of Perek Arba'ah Achin, where a woman with a maamar and a woman with a get are considered parallel. Rashi explains that the chalitzah of one exempts the other because they are equal, since a woman with maamar also requires a get. Tosafot and the Rosh connect this to the dispute of Rav and Shmuel about defective chalitzah. According to Shmuel, where the brothers are equal in prohibition, one defective chalitzah can exempt; according to Rav, each may require chalitzah. The Tur inferred that Rashi may be more lenient in two yevamot, but Tosafot and the Rosh imply otherwise. The Beit Shmuel notes that the later seifim must be read with this dispute.
