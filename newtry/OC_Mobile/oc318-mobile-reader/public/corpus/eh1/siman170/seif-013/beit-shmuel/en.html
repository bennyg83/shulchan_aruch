There, yevamot. See the Rosh, Rambam, Ramban, and other decisors regarding a yevamah who had defective chalitzah or kiddushin-like acts. The question is whether the act removes the zikkah, whether it requires another chalitzah, and whether the rival wife is exempt. The answer depends on whether the defect is in the person, the act, or the order of the acts.
