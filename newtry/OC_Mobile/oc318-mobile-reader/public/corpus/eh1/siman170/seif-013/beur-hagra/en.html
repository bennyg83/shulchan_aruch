Whether for the sake of yevamot, etc. See Behag, and what is written there, that the Rabbis hold there is no zikah, an inferior chalitzah does not exempt, and there is a condition in chalitzah. Although we do not hold this way, it is not that the Rabbis hold this in every place. Rather, they hold that here it is effective, since there is kiddushin with a yevamah. Regarding this Rabbi and the Sages disagree, and we hold like the Sages. See the Rosh there.
