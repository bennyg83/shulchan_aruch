(ט) This is, etc. Sanhedrin 15a, as taught: "Do not profane," etc. Rambam's language is: I say that this which is stated, "Do not profane," etc., means that one should not say: since the Torah obligated an ones and a seducer only to give money to the father, I will hire my virgin daughter to this man, or for free. Therefore it says, "Do not profane your daughter"; what the Torah obligated is when it occurred incidentally without her father's knowledge, etc. But one who prepares his daughter for this is the same as if he consecrated her to prostitution, and the man and woman are lashed because of "there shall not be a kedeshah." Meaning, as written in Sifrei, "there shall not be a kedeshah" is a warning to one who fornicates, as it says, "there was no kedeshah here." Specifically with one made ownerless for promiscuity is one lashed, as written in siman 6 seif 8 and as written above at the beginning of the siman, etc.
