If had deposit in her hand or lent object and said be betrothed to me with deposit or loan in your hand and found stolen or lost — if remained worth perutah betrothed; if not — not betrothed; and specifically she does not know deposit amount and consents betroth whether much or little; but if knew deposit amount and found stolen or lost even if remained perutah — not betrothed {because not consent betroth except deposit amount (Tur)}.
