Likewise if he returned debt owed her and said take this coin I owe you and behold you are betrothed to me with it before you take it and she took and was silent — if shiddukhin between them behold she is betrothed; if not shiddukhin — not betrothed until she says yes; but if after gave in her hand said behold you are betrothed to me with it — even if said yes — not betrothed even if shiddukhin first. {Rama: All this when said take coin I owe you; but said take this coin plainly and returned said be betrothed to me with it even if owes her also and payment time arrived — betrothed and cannot say for repayment I accepted (Ran chapter 2 Kiddushin); said take this coin I owe you and returned said not for repayment I give only for kiddushin — some say betrothed even if not shiddukhin (there in name Beit Hagozer and Rambam); asked her lend money on pledge and she gave money and when gives pledge said be betrothed with it and she accepted and was silent — safek kiddushin (responsum Rashba 473)}.
