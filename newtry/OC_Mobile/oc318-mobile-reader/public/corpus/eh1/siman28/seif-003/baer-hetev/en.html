Go take. Whether document or witnesses or without witnesses — since he said I owe you — obligated and can say I do not take yours I take.
Shiddukhin etc. Specifically when engaged and consented betroth now; if explained not betroth until set time — shiddukhin does not help.
I accepted for repayment. See Beit Shmuel; Beit Yosef brought Rashba responsum debt payment time not arrived — even acceptance silence betrothed since not for repayment.
Betrothed. Even if not shiddukhin. Maggid wrote this law needs study; wonder rabbi wrote plainly Chelkat Mechokek.
Pledge. Chelkat Mechokek body of responsum deals saying lend dinar on this pledge automatically or say mine I took and it is mine.
