Even though shiddukhin first etc. — initially accepted as debt and now at kiddushin gives her nothing (if returned debt she can also say accepted for my debt). Words of the corrector — I do not know who corrected this and it is unnecessary; Taz cited in seif 3.
And payment time arrived — not mentioned except to include that even so we do not consider repayment; but if said first take this for debt — not dependent on payment time; even if time not arrived we say not betrothed for though we hold end that debtor pays on time — this is different.
Some say betrothed — Ran who brought this first wrote not betrothed since initially said for debt can say I hold to first language on my debt; and concludes for law not kiddushin certainly but safek.
Safek kiddushin if can say I took mine since she has nothing on pledge and not acquired to her; even per Beit Yosef pledge acquires — still did not pull yet and at pulling not given for pledge but for kiddushin — so responsum Rashba 127.
