Has acquisition etc. — Rambam view holds R. Yitzchak even pledged at loan time acquires pledge; Rambam wrote partial acquisition; therefore some say though not fully acquire from R. Yitzchak where pledged at loan — still acquired partially since has partial acquisition in body of pledge.
And if sold etc. — refers to gentile pledge; Mahari"v 138 wrote for borrower; his view Mar since robbery rabbinically not kiddushin like chametz hour six; here not robbery but like annulled his loan permitted; though in Choshen Mishpat 361 gentile pledge Israel acquires — here different.
