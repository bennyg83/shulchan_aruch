And betrothed. Ex post facto; initially money forbidden though does not acquire value — Rosh in name Rashi; Orach Chayim 443.
Buyer did not know etc. If buyer knew Rosh wrote then say gives money as gift; must resolve what recognized field bought from Reuven not his — we hold money deposit in gemara and Choshen Mishpat not gift except betrothing sister we hold money gift since sister presumably gift gave her; if gives other not gift; why Rosh here gave as gift.
Safek betrothed. See Rosh and Ran wrote even if not knew; can say like loan in his hand since way of sale came to hand; Beit Yosef words of reason.
