Regarding robbery. Source explained Choshen Mishpat there; Yeshuot Shmuel chapter HaGozel ruled plain theft Israel despair and gentile not despair and robbery even Israel not robbery.
Or with coercion. Prohibition of coercion violates do not covet — so Rambam and Shulchan Aruch 259; not like Semag 34 wrote plainly not violate.
Behold betrothed. Choshen Mishpat 356 there per Rambam if took from known thief even if despair and change domain taker must return to owner.
Betrothed. Reason immediately when came to her hand despair and change domain and she acquired object; with this acquires her — so Maggid; see Choshen Mishpat dispute Rambam and Rosh.
Theft of gentile. Per Rashi Sanhedrin 57 only rabbinic hillul Hashem; see Rashi chapter Lulav.
After despair alone. I.e. robbery of hers after despair and did not say yes and no shiddukhin — nevertheless betrothed rabbinically for despair acquires rabbinically — so Hagahot.
