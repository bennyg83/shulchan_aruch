And if they came — not betrothed, for plain robbery is not owners' despair.
Betrothed rabbinically — implies specifically after despair; for Shulchan Aruch already wrote despair only when known; if plain even rabbinically no kiddushin at all and no get needed. I wrote this to exclude from what Mahari Chayim wrote; I already wrote in chapter 4 Choshen Mishpat 361.
