One who betroths with robbery, deposit, loan, and biblical and rabbinic issur hanaah. It contains 23 seifim. One who betroths a woman with robbery or theft or coercion — if owners despaired and it is known he acquired the thing through despair — behold she is betrothed; if not — not betrothed. {Rama: If betrothed with robbery or theft of gentile — betrothed for she need only return from kiddushin sanctification (Mahari"v siman 138); if betrothed with robbery after despair alone — betrothed rabbinically (R' Yerucham).}
