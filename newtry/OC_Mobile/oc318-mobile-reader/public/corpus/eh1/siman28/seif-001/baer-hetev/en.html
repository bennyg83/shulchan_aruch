With robbery. If denial between man and woman — he says money his and she says hers — presumption all in person's hand is his; if she took and silent — not betrothed.
With theft. See Shach Choshen Mishpat 368 who elaborated plain theft among Israel — owners despair; gentile — no despair; robbery even among Israel no despair.
With coercion. Difficult for Shulchan Aruch Choshen Mishpat 34:13 — transgressor on rabbinic robbery disqualified rabbinically e.g. coercers etc.
Betrothed. Question per Rama Choshen Mishpat 356:6 custom now return all theft even after despair.
Gentile. Shach challenged Choshen Mishpat 348 — Rambam and Maharshal hold robbery of gentile must return and biblically forbidden.
Alone etc. I.e. robbery of hers after despair and did not say yes and no prior shiddukhin — nevertheless betrothed rabbinically for despair acquires.
