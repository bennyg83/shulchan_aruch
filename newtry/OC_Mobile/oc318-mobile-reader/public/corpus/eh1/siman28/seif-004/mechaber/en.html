If gave her deposit and said take this deposit and returned said behold you are betrothed to me with it — if said so before she took and she took even if silent — behold she is betrothed. {Rama: Some say specifically she took from his hand; but if threw kiddushin even into her lap silence thus nothing since did not initially consent betroth him (Tur Rambam); if after took as deposit said behold betrothed with it and silent — nothing; but if said yes — behold betrothed.}
