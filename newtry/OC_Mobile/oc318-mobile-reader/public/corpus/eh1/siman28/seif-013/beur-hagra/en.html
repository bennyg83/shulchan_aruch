Told take etc. Initially Rashba explained this braitot returned said not for repayment etc. from braitot and returned etc.; afterward wrote however Rambam words wrote take etc. behold betrothed implies explains without return etc. only with this repayment betrothed; braitot returned said not return from repayment only returned again said; not like return braitot above deposit; Beit Hagozer; Tosefta chapter 2 here coin I owe you at giving behold betrothed willing betrothed unwilling not; therefore returned said not repayment kiddushin silent betrothed even without shiddukhin; question; also wrote without return as above accepted also repayment; Rashi 52b regarding shiddukhin word of her; Jerusalem Talmud not so; snatching case also must pay; or there explains if I give betrothed implies by return alone betroths; from here also as above need not say not repayment; Rashi 52b end; Rashba briefly see there.
