If said to her be betrothed to me with this object and she said behold it is not worth perutah and he said be betrothed to me with four zuz in it — behold safek betrothed. {Rama: Same if did not return and say be betrothed only said behold inside worth perutah — betrothed from doubt (Hagahot Alfasi first chapter Kiddushin); woman who snatched money from man and he asked return and she refused and he said behold betrothed with them and silent and held money — not kiddushin for silence after giving money and nothing (responsum Maharam Padua 24)}.
