If he robbed the woman or stole or coerced her and returned and betrothed her with robbery or theft or coercion of hers and said to her behold you are betrothed to me with it — if they did not previously have shiddukhin and she took and was silent — not betrothed; but if he shiddukhin first {i.e. she consented to marry him even if not equal in dowry (Hagahot Alfasi first chapter Kiddushin)} or even if not shiddukhin first and when he said to her be betrothed to me with this and gave in her hand she said yes — behold she is betrothed and must pay her robbery (Ran chapter 2 Kiddushin); but if after it came to her hand he said be betrothed to me with it — even if she said yes — not betrothed even if shiddukhin first {and she is not believed to say she initially accepted for kiddushin (so Ran in name Rashba and Beit Yosef brought them)}.
