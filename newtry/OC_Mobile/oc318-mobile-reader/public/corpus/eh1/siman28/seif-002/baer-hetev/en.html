The woman. Woman stole from betrother and betrothed with that robbery — not betrothed even if engaged — Maharil 78, 101.
Betrothed. Can say mine I took; possible specifically if robbed object then say mine I took; but if robbed money from her — difficult.
Accepted. Refers beginning he betrothed with robbery of hers without shiddukhin and accepted silence — not believed said accepted for kiddushin.
