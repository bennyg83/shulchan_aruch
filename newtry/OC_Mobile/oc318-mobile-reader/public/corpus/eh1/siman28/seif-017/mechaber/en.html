One who enters friend's house and takes vessel or food etc. and betrothed woman with it and homeowner came even if said why not give her better than gave — not betrothed for did not say this except not shame him; since betrothed with friend's money without knowledge — robbery and not betrothed; if betrothed with thing homeowner not particular about e.g. date or nut — betrothed from doubt. {Rama: Guest sitting with homeowner takes portion and betrothed — betrothed (Hagahot Alfasi)}.
