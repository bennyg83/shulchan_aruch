Not betrothed. Holds craftsman not acquire improvement and holds rental from start to end — whole perutah becomes loan; last perutah loan before gave vessel; though we hold rental paid only at end — there rental applies from start.
And some say betrothed. Beit Yosef in name Tosafot R. Yehuda: when gives vessel must say behold betrothed with wage of this vessel; what said first with wage of work I will do and she said no — not kiddushin like get if said to wife behold divorced.
All betrothed. R. Yitzchak 385: my teacher R. Eliezer wrote all if already made ornaments and afterward betrothed with wage — betrothed since holds for wage like loan with pledge; if said make ornaments — not betrothed there.
Gave from his perutah. Seemingly specifically if gave specific thing; if gave money also loan like rental not kiddushin.
Did work for another. See Darkhei Moshe explains Mahari"k: if does work for one even not finished can betroth with wage; per below when accepted kinyan obligated himself Ba'al HaBayit pays — then can betroth with work.
