One who borrows object from friend and informed wants betroth with it — woman betrothed; if not — safek betrothed. {Rama: Same renter of vessel or object borrowed and betrothed (Terumat Hadeshen 210); borrowed vessel from married woman and betrothed — concern kiddushin even though all woman acquires husband acquires and did not borrow from husband (Rashba 1205 and Ramban 144)}.
