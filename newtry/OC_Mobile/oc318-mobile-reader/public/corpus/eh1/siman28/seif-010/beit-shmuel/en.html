Had loan with her. If payment time not arrived see below.
With benefit of waiving loan. But if said with waiving loan — not betrothed — Rashi Rosh Ran; not like Tosafot chapter Hamadir; acquiring with debt value when sold object explained Choshen Mishpat 194; Mahari"t wrote must waive then say be betrothed.
Because interest. I.e. circumventing interest — gemara; Rashi on questioner questioned full interest — why called circumventing interest; implies not questioned why betrothed but why called circumventing.
And some say not betrothed. Ran and Maggid; infer Rambam too from wrote loan not now etc. as previous seif — if owed before not kiddushin and time extension not help.
Not betrothed. Seems refers only if profits time; but if said betroths with benefit of waiving loan — betrothed all; Ran doubted not Levush.
Betrothed. And circumventing interest though permitted give perutah to lender say lend this one as Perek HaNichnasin; here returns and takes from her — so Tosafot; Rama did not write forbidden because interest.
