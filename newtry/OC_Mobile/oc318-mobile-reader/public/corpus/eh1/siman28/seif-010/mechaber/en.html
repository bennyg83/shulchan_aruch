If had loan with her and said behold betrothed with benefit of waiving loan; and same if said behold betrothed with what I profit you time — betrothed and forbidden because interest; and some say not betrothed. {Rama: Concern for both views and if betrothed another needs get from both (Kol Bo Rambam and Tur brought); if owed others and one came gave perutah to lender profit time and betrothed with it — betrothed (Tosafot, Mordechai, Ran beginning Kiddushin)}.
