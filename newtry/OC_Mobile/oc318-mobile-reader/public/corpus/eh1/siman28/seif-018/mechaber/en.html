If merchandise between him and friend and divided without friend's knowledge and betrothed with his portion — since needs court appraisal not betrothed for not take for self what wants leave what wants. {Rama: Even gave all merchandise in his portion more than perutah — not betrothed for no will betroth except vessel (Hagahot Maggid Mishneh chapter 2 Eirusin Ramban Rashba; Ran)}; if thing all equal value and needs no appraisal — behold betrothed.
