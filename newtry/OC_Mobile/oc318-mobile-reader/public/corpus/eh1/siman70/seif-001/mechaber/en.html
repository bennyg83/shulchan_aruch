Law of obligation to sustain one's wife and sons, and whether he must hire himself out. It contains 12 seifim. How is he obligated in her sustenance? She eats and drinks what he eats and drinks. And if all members of her family are accustomed to delicacies — he must provide for her accordingly. If they are not all accustomed to delicacies — he is not obligated to provide thus when she eats with him; but when she does not eat with him he must provide according to the way she was accustomed in her father's house (Tur).
