(א) Behold, if she did not, etc. This is what they said there: her labor went toward, etc.
(ב) And if she worked, etc. As written in Perek Af Al Pi, that whenever she is not maintained, her labor and surplus belong to her. See there in the dispute of Rav and Rabbi Elazar bar Ahavah, and there in the Mishnah, 64b: and if he does not give her a silver ma'ah, etc. See the Ran at the beginning of chapter 11.
