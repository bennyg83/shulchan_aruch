One who went overseas and deposited vessels with someone, or lent them to him — they take from the deposit for his wife's sustenance. {Rama: Even if the husband commanded not to give (so wrote R' Yehuda in a responsum brought in Mordechai ch. Dayanei Gezeirot). And if the depositor returned to the husband after the wife sued him in court — he is obligated to pay her (there in Mordechai). And when they take from the depositor — they take only for six months; even if the depositor wishes, they do not withhold the husband's money more (there). And they do not take from property in others' hands as long as there is unsold property here (Beit Yosef in name of responsum of Rashba). But from a borrower — they do not take until the end of the loan term.}
