(א) One who went, etc. As above in seif 5, that they descend to his property, etc.
(ב) Even if he commanded, etc. For it is not entirely in his power, as they said there, 48a: what is different when he says, "they should fall," etc.? It is specifically because they fall, etc. See above, siman 89, seif 2.
(ג) And if he returned, etc. See Chelkat Mechokek, siman 86, seif 5: and if another, etc.
(ד) And when they remove, as written, etc. 97a, regarding a widow.
(ה) And they do not remove, etc. See Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel.
(ו) But immediately, etc. For even he himself cannot take it from him, as written in Kiddushin 47, and learn from this. The same applies to a sale to him until the time. We say at the beginning of Perek HaNizakin: we do not remove, etc.
