(ג) And a little wine for drinking. This is according to Shmuel's interpretation, who said that if she was accustomed to much wine, nevertheless when he is not with her they give her a little wine; and if she was accustomed to drink a little wine in her husband's presence, they give her none at all when he is not with her. However, Rif, Rosh, and Tur have another approach to this, and hold that if she is accustomed to it, they give her, even not in her husband's presence, everything to which she is accustomed.
