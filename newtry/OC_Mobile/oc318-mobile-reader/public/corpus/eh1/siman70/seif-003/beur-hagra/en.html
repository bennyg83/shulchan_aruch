(א) Two meals. The Gemara there, that the measures in the Mishnah were in Eretz Yisrael and in those times. But in other times and other places, everything follows the matter. Rambam.
(ב) And meat or fish. The language of the Rambam is "according to the local custom." See chapter 15 of Shabbat.
(ג) And they give her, etc. Such as a lamp, wick, barrel, cup, and pot, which the Yerushalmi and Tosefta count there. In the Mishnah, however, only food itself is counted, not items that prepare food.
(ד) And if he was poor, etc. For Shmuel does not disagree with Rav in this kind of case.
(ה) And some say, etc. Since it says "one who says: I will not," etc., it implies specifically one who has means.
(ו) And some say further, etc. See Tosafot 63a, s.v. "one who says," etc. But the view of most decisors is like Rabbenu Tam.
(ז) The laws of land, etc. 107b: Come and hear: the woman, etc. And a ketubah is collected only from land.
