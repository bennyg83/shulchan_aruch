(א) Behold, she stood, etc. This is what they said there: they preceded, etc., that he cannot say "let her labor go," etc. The same applies that he cannot say "I allotted maintenance to her." As written in the Mishnah: she swears at the end. This shows that she is believed. This is what he wrote: she swears.
(ב) With holding a sacred object, for a Mishnah oath is with holding a sacred object. As written in Choshen Mishpat, siman 89, and learn from this. Specifically regarding land, where it is in his possession, and this is like one who swears and collects. This is unlike movables, where she was in possession, and it is like any one who denies everything. This is what he wrote: and if she sold the movables, etc.
(ג) And if she did not claim, etc. This is what they said there: and if he came and said, "I allotted," etc., as above in seif 5.
(ד) But the man is not, etc. As written in Bava Batra 5b, that within its time he is not believed. This requires study, for there they say we are concerned lest he handed over bundles of money.
(ה) He claimed, etc. According to the approach of the above Rosh.
(ו) And she is the holder, etc. The Gemara there.
