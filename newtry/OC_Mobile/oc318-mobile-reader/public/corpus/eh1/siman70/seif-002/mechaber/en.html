A husband who wished to give his wife her appropriate sustenance and she would eat and drink by herself — the choice is his, provided he eats with her on Shabbat night. {Rama: And some dispute and hold that he cannot say she should eat alone unless she accepted this willingly (Tur in name of Yerushalmi and Rosh, Beit Yosef in name of most poskim). And so it seems to me.}
