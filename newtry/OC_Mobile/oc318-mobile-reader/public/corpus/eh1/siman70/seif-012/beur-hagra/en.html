(א) But if not, etc. From what they said there, 96: a widow who delayed, etc. Specifically a widow, but a married woman, even one hour, loses it. A widow is different because it is possible for her to be maintained from her own property, such as from a found object, unlike a married woman, whose property all belongs to her husband. Even if she sold her nichsei melog at a discount and did not claim, she lost it, as above, end of seif 8 in the gloss. The Ramban, Ritva, and Ran there.
(ב) For the man is not obligated, etc. The Ritva wrote there that so it appears in Eleh HaDevarim Rabbah.
(ג) If she was, etc. See there, 63b.
(ד) And in a place, etc. As above.
