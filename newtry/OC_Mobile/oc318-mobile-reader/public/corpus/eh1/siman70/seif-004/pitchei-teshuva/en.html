Obligated to hire himself out. See responsum Radbaz part 3 siman 566 (see Chasam Sofer siman 132 about a rabbi who went to Israel leaving wife destitute; he commanded giving her chumash from the fund to send him; known insufficient in hard times; questioning Gaon wanted to withhold all fund money by known treasurer and give her — he answered very difficult since initially given only for his sustenance to take him to Israel — cannot change donors' intent; even he himself if wanted to eat it abroad would be thief changing donors' intent, etc. He further wrote nevertheless questioning Gaon's law since clear to us not for her clothing to go there to Israel per Maharai siman 88 and Ma'il Tzedakah siman 26 (brought below siman 75 s.k. 6), etc. — since nevertheless obligated to sustain her, evil and bitter this act to go there and not leave sustenance for his wife — therefore proper to compel in all compulsion ways not in his power to go Israel and starve wife; thus a fortiori that chumash he commanded give — we can give her and it is not changing donors' intent since all donors' intent to maintain him in Holy Land — if wife's sustenance not fixed here, lamentations written upon him forced to uproot and return abroad, etc.; even if seems insufficient for her sustenance we can take from treasurer to give properly, etc.; nevertheless good to warn him first and meanwhile she suffices with chumash he permitted and remainder money held by treasurer until rabbi's answer from there.
