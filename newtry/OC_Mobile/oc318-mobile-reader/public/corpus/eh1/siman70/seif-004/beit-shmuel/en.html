(א) And afterward she became deranged. But if she was deranged at the time of the marriage, Chazal did not enact marriage for a deranged woman. If she became deranged after the marriage, he is not exempt from his obligation except when he divorces her, and this woman cannot be divorced; therefore he is obligated to provide her sustenance, as written in Responsa Rashba. If he was deranged at the time of the marriage and wrote her a ketubah, it appears that he is obligated in all conditions of the ketubah. See siman 66.
