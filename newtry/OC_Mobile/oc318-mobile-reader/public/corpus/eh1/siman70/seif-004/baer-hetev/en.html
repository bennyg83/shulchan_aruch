(א) Became deranged. But if she was deranged at the time of the marriage, Chazal did not enact marriage for a deranged woman. If, however, she became deranged after the marriage, he is not exempt from his obligation except when he divorces her, and this woman cannot be divorced; therefore he is obligated to provide her sustenance.
