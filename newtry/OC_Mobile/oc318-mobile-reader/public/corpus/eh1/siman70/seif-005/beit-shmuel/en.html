(א) One who went overseas. If he went to a nearby place and intended to return home, but then settled there, they assess sustenance for her immediately; so wrote Ritva. In Darkei Moshe and Bach they wrote that Mordechai disagrees with this, for Mordechai wrote that "overseas" is not precise. They understood that Mordechai refers to this law and disagrees with Ritva, but this is not so. Rather, Mordechai refers to the second mishnah, "one who went overseas and another arose and supported his wife"; regarding that he wrote that "overseas" is not precise. I later found this in Gedolei Terumah, as written in kelal 65.
