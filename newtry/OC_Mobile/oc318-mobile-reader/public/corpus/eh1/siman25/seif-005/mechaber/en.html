Forbidden for a man to use his bed by candlelight — even through darkening with a tallit; behold if it was Shabbat and he had no other house and the candle was lit — he should not be intimate at all. And likewise forbidden to use his bed by day — it is brazenness for him. (And see Orach Chaim siman 240 seif 11 and siman 315 seif 1.)
