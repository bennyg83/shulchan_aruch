To erect a fence not to increase intimacy and how one should conduct at time of intimacy. It contains 10 seifim. It is fitting for a man to accustom himself to extra holiness and pure thought and correct mind in order to be saved from stumbling in forbidden matter; and he should beware of seclusion which is the great cause; and likewise he should conduct himself to distance from laughter and from drunkenness and from words of affection (meaning words of jest and desire); and he should not sit without a woman — this custom causes great purity. More than all this they said: he should turn himself and his thought to words of Torah and broaden his mind in wisdom — for lewd thought prevails only in a heart empty of wisdom.
