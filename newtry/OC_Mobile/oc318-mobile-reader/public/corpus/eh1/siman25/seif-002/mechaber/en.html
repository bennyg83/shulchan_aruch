And he should not treat his head lightly with his wife nor defile his mouth with idle words even between him and her — behold Scripture says: He tells a man what is his talk; Chazal said even light conversation between man and his wife — future judgment will be given on it; and he should not speak with her at time of intimacy nor before so he not set his mind on another woman; and if he spoke with her and was intimate immediately upon him is said "He tells a man what is his talk." But regarding matters of intimacy he may speak with her in order to increase his desire; or if he was angry with her and needs to appease her so she reconcile he may speak with her to appease her. {Rama: And he may do with his wife what he wishes — consummate any time he wishes and kiss any limb he wishes and be intimate whether in normal manner whether not in normal manner or via limbs — provided he does not waste seed (Tur); and some are lenient and say permitted not in normal manner even if he emitted seed if he does incidentally and is not accustomed to this (also Tur in name of R' Yitzchak); and although all these permitted — all who sanctifies himself in what is permitted to him — they call him holy (words of the Rav).} And he should not increase intimacy to be constantly with her — this matter is very blemished and act of boors; rather all who reduces intimacy is praiseworthy — provided he does not cancel onah except with his wife's knowledge. And even when he is intimate at time of onah he should not intend for his pleasure but as one paying a debt he owes in her season and to fulfill commandment of his Creator in procreation; and that he have children engaging in Torah and fulfilling commandments in Israel. And he should consummate only with her consent; and if she is not willing he should appease her until she consents. And he should be very modest at time of intimacy and not be intimate in presence of any kind of person even a minor unless an infant who does not know to speak.
