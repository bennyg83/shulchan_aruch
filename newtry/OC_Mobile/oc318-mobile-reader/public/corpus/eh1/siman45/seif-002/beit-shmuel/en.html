A new city. This means that they came from several places: in some of the places from which they came the custom was to betroth and afterward send gifts, and in some places they would send gifts and afterward betroth. This is like half and half, and we are concerned for kiddushin. See Choshen HaMishpat siman 331: regarding money we are not concerned unless the majority come from a place that has the custom. If it is known that they came from one province, from several places, we follow the custom in that province, unless the places differ in custom. If he is from a place where we are not concerned about gifts and he came to a place where we are concerned, we follow the custom of the place from which he came, unless he has been here thirty days, in which case his law is like the people of this city. See responsum Mahari ben Lev part 3 and responsum Roshakh siman 140.
