Nowadays the custom in all the kingdoms of the Land of Israel and Egypt and Togarma is not to be concerned for gifts except in Constantinople where they are concerned. {Rama: If there was a custom in a city and it was abolished — we follow the present practice (Rivash siman 5). If there was no custom in a city — such as a new city — it is judged half and half (Ran chapter 2 of Kiddushin). And some say one should be stringent even where all give gifts first and afterward betroth unless there is another lenient factor here (Terumat HaDeshen siman 207 and in his rulings siman 74). And it seems to me if the groom said he sent only as mere gifts — per all there is no concern in such a case; and so is practiced daily — sometimes they return in matchmaking and gifts return and they do not require a get since it is in places where now all give gifts first and afterward betroth and the groom does not say he sent for kiddushin.}
