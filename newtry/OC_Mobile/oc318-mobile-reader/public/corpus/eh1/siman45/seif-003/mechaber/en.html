We are concerned for ketubah: if he wrote her a ketubah even though it did not reach her — if the custom of some people of the city is to betroth and afterward write the ketubah — we are concerned perhaps she was betrothed even if no scribe was present; and we do not say perhaps a scribe happened upon him and wrote. And if the custom of all people of the city is to write the ketubah before kiddushin — we are not concerned for kiddushin.
