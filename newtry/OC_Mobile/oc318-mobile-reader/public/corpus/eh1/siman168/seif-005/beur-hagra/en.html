(ב) And some say, etc. Like the Sages; see Beer HaGolah and Rambam in his commentary there. Rambam did not write "whoever seizes first," etc., as written in the Mishnah, because seizure helps only during his lifetime, as Rashi wrote, and as written there on 54b; he did not need to explain this. But this is forced, for why did he omit it, since it is written in the Mishnah and is not so obvious? Rather, he explains that her seizure does not mean that he cannot use them, but that she can collect her ketubah after his death. Even if she seized after her husband's brother died, that stands in the husband's place, as Tosafot wrote in chapter 4b, s.v. "and according to R. Akiva," etc., in Rabbenu Tam's explanation; or he explains that she seized during her husband's lifetime like Rashi's explanation, and all of it concerns collection of the ketubah.
