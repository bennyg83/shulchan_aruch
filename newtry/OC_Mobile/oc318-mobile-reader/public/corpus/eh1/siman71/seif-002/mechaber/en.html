One who went overseas and left his children here — they sell from his property for sustenance until they are six years old; but from six and above they do not feed them from his property. {Rama: Some say this is specifically when he had not begun feeding them after six; but if he had begun feeding them they are sustained from his property, for certainly he prefers this (Mordechai chapter HaNa'arah in name of R' Yitzchak). Some say if he is assessed as wealthy they feed them in every matter by reason of charity (so implies in Tur).}
