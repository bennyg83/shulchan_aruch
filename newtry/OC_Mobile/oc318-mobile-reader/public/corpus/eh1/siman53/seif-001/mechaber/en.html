Law of one who stipulates for his son or daughter if they acquired; law if she fell before the yavam; law if the daughter died. It contains 3 seifim. A father who stipulated via his daughter — the daughter did not acquire that gift until her husband enters her; and likewise the son did not acquire until he enters, for every stipulator stipulates only on condition of entering. Therefore one who stipulates money to his son-in-law (or to his son) and died before he entered and she fell before his brother for yibbum — the father can say to the yavam: to your brother I wished to give, but to you I do not wish to give; and even if the first was an am ha'aretz and the second a scholar, and even though the daughter wants him.
