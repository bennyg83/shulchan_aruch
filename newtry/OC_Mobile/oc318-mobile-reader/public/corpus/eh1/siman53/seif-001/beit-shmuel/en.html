The father who pledged through his daughter. This is the Rambam’s language. One can say that he does not disagree with the "some say." What he wrote, "through his daughter," means that he pledged to his son-in-law to give him when he marries his daughter. So wrote the Chelkat Mechokek.
Or to his son. So wrote Maharik, shoresh 73. There it is explained that even if he had already married her and died during the first year after the marriage, and she fell before his second son for yibbum, the yavam does not acquire it, because we hold that the dowry is returned during the first year. In Darkei Moshe he wrote that Maharik speaks where he died before the marriage; I do not understand his intent.
