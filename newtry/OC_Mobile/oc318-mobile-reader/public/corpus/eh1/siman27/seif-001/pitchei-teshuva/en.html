Perutah — Ba'er Hetev wrote see Sema etc. and it is from Choshen Mishpat s.s. this that they brought; and see in Sefer Avnei Meluim s.k. 20 what he wrote on this. And what Ba'er Hetev wrote they do not betroth lekhatchilah with coin — from Beit Shmuel here and below siman 31 s.k. 5; and there he wrote this in name of Mordekhai; see in Sefer Avnei Meluim s.k. 2 who wrote in Gemara it implies they betroth with coin and in Mordekhai he did not write this except as investigation — requires examination how they betroth with coin; let us also say her mind is imprisoned and it is void; but already they wrote to distinguish between law of money and law of exchange (see first chapter beginning Kiddushin and in Beur HaGra siman 32); and in Sefer HaMakneh part 1 he produced reason for Beit Shmuel's words they do not betroth with coin — see there; nevertheless from Beit Shmuel's words it appears he intended this for Mordekhai wrote regarding her mind is imprisoned; and per what he wrote also Mordekhai holds they betroth with coin — end quote see there (and what Ba'er Hetev wrote s.k. 1 "and he betroths with his right hand and also bride's right hand and on finger" see in Sefer Kerem Shlomo in teshuvot at end of book siman 86 who wrote must be careful there be no interposition between ring and finger — therefore if bride wears gloves she must remove from her right hand — see there).
