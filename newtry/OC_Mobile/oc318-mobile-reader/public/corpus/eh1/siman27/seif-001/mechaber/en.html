How is the formula of kiddushin and what he should say and what he should give. It contains 10 seifim. With money — how? He gives her in the presence of two witnesses a perutah or something worth a perutah and says to her "You are betrothed to me with this." {Rama: And some say he should say to her "according to the law of Moses and Israel" (Kol Bo) and such is the initial practice; and such is the practice to betroth with a ring and they have reason in the tikkunim of the Zohar (opinion of the Rav).} And the same if he said to her "You are my betrothed" or "You are my wife" or any language whose meaning is certainly language of kiddushin in that place, provided she understands that it is language of kiddushin; and if he had spoken with her first about matters of kiddushin and gave to her even in silence — it is kiddushin, provided they are still engaged in that matter; and all the more if he said language of kiddushin certainly but she does not understand him — it is kiddushin. {Rama: Some say we do not require that they speak in that matter specifically but rather they speak from matter to matter in that matter — for one does not speak of kiddushin except in matters of their union (Mordekhai end of "The man betroths" and likewise Rashba); and some say we do not require that he speak with her but rather whenever they speak before her it suffices (Mordekhai beginning Kiddushin and likewise implied from Tur's language).}
