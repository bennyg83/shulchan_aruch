She gave etc. And it does not help even if they spoke about matters of kiddushin for it is written "when a man takes" and not "when she takes" — even though if they speak about matters of kiddushin it is as if he said; nevertheless when she gave and she said and they were engaged in matters of kiddushin it is as if she gave and he said — per this if he is important person and they were engaged in matters of kiddushin and she said and she gave — it is certainly kiddushin for what they were engaged in matters of kiddushin is as if he spoke and important person is as if he gave; nevertheless in Tosafot in this sugya it does not appear so; also in Perishah wrote she is not betrothed.
