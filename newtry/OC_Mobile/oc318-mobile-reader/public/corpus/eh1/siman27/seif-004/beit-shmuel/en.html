But some are stringent. Likewise in books Beit Yosef in name of Rashba; but Ribash in teshuvah siman 266 wrote in name of Ramban and Rashba who hold whoever did not say "to me" there is no concern; and likewise Maggid and in teshuvah Beit Yosef and Bedek HaBayit wrote these teshuvot in name of Rashba that it is kiddushin — they do not rely on them; and likewise Hagahot Maharit; and even per the stringent — nevertheless if there is additional closeness or where it is only doubt kiddushin even if he said "to me" they are lenient there in teshuvah folio 7; and if he did not say "to me" they agree their intent was for kiddushin — be stringent — teshuvah Rashdam siman 34; and likewise Beit Yosef explained teshuvah Rashba siman 574; see teshuvah Mahari of Lublin; and if there was matchmaking between them — ruled in teshuvah Ben Lev part 3 it is like presumptive evidence; and Hagahot Maharit disagrees.
And we are concerned for kiddushin. Meaning doubtful kiddushin — for one can say since he already betrothed one for himself he betrothed this one for his fellow — likewise Beit Yosef. Also one can say the doubt is per the girsa as Tosafot wrote there.
