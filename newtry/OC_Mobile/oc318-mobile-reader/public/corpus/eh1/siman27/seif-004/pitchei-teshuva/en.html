That she understand. See in teshuvah Noda BiYehudah siman 59 who wrote it appears to him what Shulchan Aruch wrote "provided she understand" etc. — meaning if she says she did not understand she is completely believed and there is no kiddushin at all; and as Choshen Mishpat wrote such is implication of all poskim and not like Beit Shmuel s.k. 5 who wrote it is doubt lest she lies — meaning why not believe her since there is no presumption of prohibition; Rava has presumption of permission supporting her certainly believed etc.; and perhaps since language is clear her claim to say she did not understand — see there; and on what is written below siman 42 s.k. 3.
