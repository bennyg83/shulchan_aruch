If he said to her "You are betrothed" and did not say "to me" — she is not betrothed; and such is the essential ruling (teshuvah of Rosh); but some are stringent and say it is kiddushin (find them in teshuvah of Rashba and Ramban and Rosh kelal 36). And if he had spoken with her about matters of kiddushin — behold she is betrothed. And if he betrothed one woman first with complete kiddushin and said to another in her presence "You shall be betrothed" and did not say "to me" — presumptive evidence (meaning as one who holds the handle of a vessel pulls the vessel itself — so these matters draw after them language "to me") — we are concerned for kiddushin.
