If he said to her "I am your husband," "Behold your master," "I am your betrothed" — it is nothing even if he had spoken with her first about matters of kiddushin, because it is written "when he takes (a woman)" and not "when he takes himself." {Hagahah: One who gave money to another and said to him "Behold you are my father-in-law" and did not say "your daughter is betrothed to me" — there is nothing in this (Hagahot Alfasi first chapter Kiddushin).}
