Kiddushin. Kenesset HaGedolah wrote it appears plain we require that witnesses testify he spoke with her about matters of her kiddushin. Maharival part 3 siman 61 raised to halakhah regarding one who said to a woman "Do you wish to be betrothed" and she was silent and did not answer yes or no and immediately he gave to her in silence and did not speak at all — it is doubt and we judge it as doubtful cases to be stringent. See Beit Shmuel.
