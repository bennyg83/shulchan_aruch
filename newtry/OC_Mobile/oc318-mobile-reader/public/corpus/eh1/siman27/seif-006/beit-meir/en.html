{Hagahah} As kiddushin — see Beit Shmuel and at end concludes and one can disagree saying "as kiddushin" for he did not say "I give to you" — meaning unlike when he said "I am giving to you" which is as saying I; and if so one can also disagree saying "behold it is yours for kiddushin" for also he did not say I; see end Hagahot Mordekhai Gittin and in Darkei Moshe here wrote he disagrees with Ribash; and per what I said it is not compelled; see siman 37 in Tur teshuvah Rosh regarding one who said "Take kiddushin for your daughter" and siman 32 seif 4.
