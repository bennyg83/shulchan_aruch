If she understood his words. See in Sefer Avnei Meluim s.k. 6 who wrote it implies even if witnesses do not know if she understood — whenever she says she understood it is definite kiddushin; difficult for behold it is like betrothing without witnesses; and possible since essence of kiddushin is from side of betrother as Ran wrote Nedarim folio 50 etc. therefore does not require two witnesses except for body of kiddushin they heard from betrother; but woman's will does not require witnesses and her admission suffices; and still requires examination — see there end quote; and see in Sefer Tiv Kiddushin s.k. 10 who also stood on this for behold it is like betrothing without witnesses; and he wrote thus: and possible here discusses she said in that same session when witnesses asked her she accepted for kiddushin — matter was revealed she knew at time of giving; but after time possible in truth since she can say she did not understand it is like betrothing without witnesses; nevertheless since there is doubt immediately at time of kiddushin lest she understood possible it is doubt etc.; however in her saying afterward she did not understand one can say since she was not established we believe her to clarify the doubt etc. end quote; and see in his words siman 58 s.k. 44 he also derives from this.
