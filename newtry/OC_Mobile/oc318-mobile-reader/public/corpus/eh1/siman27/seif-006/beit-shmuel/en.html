Behold you are my father-in-law etc. The reason because it is written "when he takes a woman" and not "when he takes himself" — even though the father is the one who transfers nevertheless he must transfer his daughter and not himself; and if he hints he betroths his daughter it does not help for language must be clear — there in the novellae; and even if they spoke about matters of kiddushin it is nothing; and Choshen Mishpat wrote great doubt practically even if he did not speak with him about matters of kiddushin — all the more if he spoke with him.
