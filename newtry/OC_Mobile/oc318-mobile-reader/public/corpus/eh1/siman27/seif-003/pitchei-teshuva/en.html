To me with this. Ba'er Hetev wrote and if he said to her "Take your kiddushin from atop land" — this is doubt kiddushin; see in Sefer HaMakneh part 1 his view it is certainly kiddushin and he brought in teshuvah Shach siman 252 and in teshuvah Maharival they also wrote this law is like attached to land for one reason — see there; and in Sefer Get Mekushar part 1 letter 10 who elaborated greatly on this law and his view there in kiddushin of money even if he said take from atop land she is properly betrothed; but in kiddushin of document there is room to doubt — see there (and see in teshuvah Chasam Sofer siman 82 who sided in his case to nullify kiddushin because ring was already in her hand as gift and it is take your kiddushin from atop land per Maharil in teshuvah siman 78 brought also in Ba'er Hetev siman 30 s.k. 8) — plainly in his eyes take from atop land she is not betrothed for acquisition is compared to release; and he wrote we do not abandon Maharil's plain meaning because of doubt of later geonim who did not see his words and if they had seen they would retract; and per my humble opinion without doubt betrothing with document we compare to divorce document invalid on it etc. and automatically invalid also in money for acquisitions are compared to each other as explained plainly in Ketubot 74 etc.; but what Maharil wrote there to distinguish betrothing with deposit in her hand as Beit Shmuel wrote siman 30 has no understanding; and per my humble opinion to explain per what Tosafot Bava Metzia 6a s.v. hekdishah etc. — if so here too initially when deposited in her hand her hand was hand of depositor and when he betroths her he removed his power from her and her hand became her own hand and it is as handing from his hand to her hand; and likewise proven in get itself end chapter HaMevi — even if we say depositor places get in wife's hand and afterward said be divorced — it is take your get from atop land; one can say this is depositing get which is document for it is diminished from law of guardians and her hand did not become like his hand except if she is agent her hand becomes hand of sender etc.; but in kiddushin of money his deposit helps for hand of guardian is like hand of owner and when he removes his hand she is betrothed etc.; and per this in the case where it was hers as gift she did not become guardian and even in place where they return in death and divorce nevertheless for others she is not obligated; and if so since her hand never became like his when he betroths her it is take from atop land; but requires examination from Rosh's words in the case he betrothed with gold plate Kiddushin 12 etc.; and possible to distinguish between money and object for with money waiver suffices etc.; nevertheless although we compare we do not act to permit on this heter alone; but in any case see to combine etc. end quote see there further siman 101 from this and on s.k. 12): and what Ba'er Hetev further wrote our custom now etc. see in Sefer HaMakneh part 1 below seif 5 who wrote it appears from language of Shmuel (in Gemara folio 5b) must say "betrothed to me with this money" and what we practice to say with this ring is for enhancement of the matter; and even with document of kiddushin it appears in sugya folio 9 need not write in it "You are betrothed with this document" — see there (also in teshuvah Chasam Sofer s.s. 107 wrote must say with this object; and view of Rav Nathan in his commentary end Gittin must say with this object — unique view etc. see there; and there s.s. 85 brought in teshuvah Rama wrote one who gives her utensil and says with this ring betrothed; and in teshuvah Shev Yaakov wrote on him meaning if both want kiddushin; but where they say their intent was jesting and he said ring on object — act proves matters of heart all was jest and play — see there;
