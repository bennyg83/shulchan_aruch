If he said to her "You are designated to me," "You are set apart for me," "You are my help," "Opposite me," "My rib," "My closed one," "Under me," "My seized one," "My grasped one" — she is betrothed in doubt. {Rama: And the same if he said "You are my betrothed (cherufati)"; but if he said to her "You are my married woman (nesuati)" some say it is nothing.} And this is when he had spoken with her first about matters of kiddushin; but if he does not speak with her first about matters of kiddushin — we are not concerned for these words. {Rama: One who said to a woman that he gives to her for love and affection — we are concerned for kiddushin for perhaps he said he gives to her that there be love and affection between them and it is as if he said to her "designated to me" or "set apart for me" (Teshuvot Mahariy end Ishut); some say that even though these languages do not help if he did not speak with her about matters of kiddushin, nevertheless if she says she accepted for kiddushin — it is doubt kiddushin (Ran); and even if he gave to her in silence and did not speak at all and she and he say they intended for kiddushin — it is kiddushin (Mordekhai chapter HaIsh Mekadesh and teshuvah Maharam); and where it is not kiddushin even if afterward he returns and says to her "You are betrothed to me" — he must return and take the money from her and give to her as kiddushin (Maggid Mishneh in name of Rashba).}
