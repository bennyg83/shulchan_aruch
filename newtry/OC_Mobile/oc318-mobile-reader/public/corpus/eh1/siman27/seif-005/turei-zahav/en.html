As kiddushin etc. Reason in Beit Yosef in name of Ribash — since it is mentioned he gives to her as kiddushin already appears from his language he is betrother and she betrothed — a person does not abandon his own mitzvah and throws on his fellow; and even if we knew he became agent for another nevertheless we say a person does not abandon what is incumbent upon him; and afterward Beit Yosef wrote in name of R' Yitzchak bar Rav in name of Rav Doran saying take this ring kiddushin for your daughter is nothing for he does not prove in these words the woman is betrother end quote; and apparently implies here there is dispute — for Ribash also here she is betrothed for certainly he performs mitzvah incumbent upon him; except one can distinguish between them — specifically in that Ribash case he said "I am giving to you" implying he is giver and betrother unlike in the second case of saying only take for you and he did not say from me; and if so here if he said behold it is yours as kiddushin it would not be as saying to me; and in hagahah Mordekhai end Gittin wrote incident with one young man speaking with one woman to lend money on one ketav etc. and afterward said to her do you wish to be betrothed with it if I give it to you; she began (to jest) and he returned and said to her behold it is yours for kiddushin literally with this law he said to her and did not say you are betrothed to me with this ketav; and behold the giver was inclined to permit her since he did not say to me; and it is wondrous who is worse than those who were silent and did not say for kiddushin that helps since he spoke first about matters of kiddushin etc.; and here too behold he spoke with her as he asked above end quote; and afterward wrote and further it appears to me even if he did not say at first his words do you wish to be betrothed with it — correct to be stringent etc. end quote; and this is from reason there are poskim presumptive evidence that does not prove are presumptive evidence; and this is like some are stringent Rama wrote above nearby even if he did not say to me; and here too even if he did not say I am giving to you but behold to you as kiddushin — be stringent in these kiddushin.
