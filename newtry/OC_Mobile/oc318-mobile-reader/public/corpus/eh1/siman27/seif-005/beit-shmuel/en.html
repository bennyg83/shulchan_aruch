As kiddushin. The reason explained in Beit Yosef in name of Ribam — since it is mentioned he gives to her as kiddushin it appears from his language he is the one who betroths and she is betrothed even if he is agent for another and she agent for another — we say a person does not abandon what is incumbent upon him; and if he said "I give to you for kiddushin" Hagahot Maharit wrote there is no concern of kiddushin — for one can say he gives for need of kiddushin and does not betroth with it. And one can disagree regarding saying "as kiddushin" — for there he reveals his intent in this giving that she is betrothed; nevertheless in teshuvah Rashba wrote if he said "I give to you for kiddushin" it is as saying "to me"; if he said "Take this ring kiddushin for your daughter" — it is not kiddushin; and one can disagree regarding saying "as kiddushin" since he did not say "I give to you."
It is as saying to me. It implies it is definite kiddushin — in teshuvah Rashba siman 574 wrote it is doubt; and likewise implied from teshuvah Ribash siman 266; see teshuvah Mahari mentioned.
They are kiddushin is nothing. And such we rule below siman 141 regarding nullification of get — must say language for the future; and if he says "this get is pottery" it is not a get; and presumably we compare kiddushin to divorce — per this must say what is written nearby for love and affection it is kiddushin — meaning he says language for the future; nevertheless Choshen Mishpat wrote kiddushin is language of sanctification and in sanctification it is sanctification — therefore be stringent in kiddushin; and if they spoke at first about matters of kiddushin it appears to be stringent.
