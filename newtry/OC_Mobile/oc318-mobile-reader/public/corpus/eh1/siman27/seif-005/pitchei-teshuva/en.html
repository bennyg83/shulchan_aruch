About matters of kiddushin. Maggid Mishneh wrote chapter 3 Laws of Marriage law 7 and thus: no difference whether they spoke about matters of kiddushin for himself or about matters of kiddushin for his fellow such as he appointed agent and gave her kiddushin and did not explain so — likewise Maharit in teshuvah Choshen Mishpat siman 12 — see there.
