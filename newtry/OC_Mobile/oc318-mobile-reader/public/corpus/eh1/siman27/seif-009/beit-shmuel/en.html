Who is called a person who is not important. It implies ordinary people are called important; and in teshuvah Rashba wrote ordinary people are called not important; see Choshen Mishpat siman 190 — if he said "Take a maneh and field sold to you" he acquires provided he is important person; one can say there the acquirer must know he is important person — likewise in Perishah.
And some are stringent. Majority of poskim hold so — likewise Rosh and Ran and R' Yehudah of Orleans and Maggid wrote in this sugya in "she gave and he said" it is kiddushin even if she did not say "and betroth yourself to me" for this sugya does not discuss she said; and likewise appears from Tosafot; see teshuvah Rashba there explained when she did not say "and betroth yourself to me" it is doubt even if he spoke with her about matters of kiddushin lest she does not want to be betrothed to important person; and per view of Hagahot Maimoniyot even person who is not important it is kiddushin; also Ran brought words of Hagahot Maimoniyot and Bach is stringent as view of Hagahot Maimoniyot and wrote practically needs settlement.
