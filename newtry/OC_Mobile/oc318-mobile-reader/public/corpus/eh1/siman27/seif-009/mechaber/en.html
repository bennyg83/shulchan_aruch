If she gave and said to him "Take this dinar as a gift and betroth yourself to me" and he took it and said to her "You are betrothed to me with the benefit I received from you as a gift" — if he is an important person behold she is betrothed, for benefit has force when one enjoys from her and with this benefit she acquired herself to him; and investigation of a sage is needed — who is called a person who is not important to permit her without a get. {And some are stringent even if she did not say first when she gave "and betroth yourself to me" but only gave simply and he said to her "be betrothed" etc. and she was silent (teshuvah Rashba siman 213).}
