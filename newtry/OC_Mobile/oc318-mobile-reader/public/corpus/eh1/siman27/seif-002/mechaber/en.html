If he said to her "You are my wife," "You are bound to me," "You are acquired to me," "You are my betrothed," "You are mine," "You are in my possession," "You are my purchase — betrothed" {Rama: if she understood his words or they spoke first about matters of kiddushin (Ran and Maggid Mishneh); but the man is not believed to say he did not intend for kiddushin (Beit Yosef in name of Rosh).}
