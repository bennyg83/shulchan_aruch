{Hagahah} But the man is not believed — Choshen Mishpat wrote from Rosh's words etc. Truth per Beit Yosef's explanation in Rosh one can infer thus: since he wrote in definite languages he is not believed — implies in doubtful ones he is believed; and if so compelled also in doubtful ones even if engaged and spoke about matters of kiddushin and afterward said doubtful language also believed to permit; and likewise Beit Shmuel s.k. 9 in his name for he concludes "and all these doubts when he spoke with her about matters of kiddushin; and if not there is no substance"; and implied also from him even if she says she accepted for kiddushin; and if so likewise if both say they intended for kiddushin there is no substance; and if so why would his credibility help in doubtful language or his intent in doubtful languages even if they spoke about matters of kiddushin he is believed; but I wonder if for this Choshen Mishpat intended — from where did he learn he is believed to explain his words he did not intend only for work; and just as she is believed on definite languages to say she did not understand — so he is believed to explain the doubts; and let us say conversely — behold we see Rama wrote seif 2 on definite languages discusses when they did not speak first — if she understood his words to teach that if she says she did not understand she is believed; and in seif 3 on doubtful language discusses when they spoke — we do not hear anyone say if she claims she did not understand and thought he retracted and spoke for work she exits from doubt and is better than giving to her in silence — we learn clearly here all agree he is not believed; and if so let us also say here he too is not believed since he spoke first about matters of kiddushin; and perhaps one can strain that specifically she who is presumed not to know at all what he said per Gemara's question — and it is found she is presumed not to understand neither for kiddushin nor for work — therefore regarding her there is substance like giving in silence; unlike him who knows what he says and we doubt — he has in his hand to explain; but requires examination for if so the matter depends on him whether to permit or forbid and witnesses cannot forbid her if she permits — it would be like betrothing without witnesses — see below.
