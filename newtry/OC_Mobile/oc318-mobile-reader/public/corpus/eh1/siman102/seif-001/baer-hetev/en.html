(ב) He collects. Baal Panim Chadashot wrote in the name of She'erit Yosef, siman 37: one who wrote his daughter a half-male-share document, and before his death made a large ketubah for his wife, and his sons-in-law claim that their document comes first for collection, they must first pay the ketubah, and afterward they take by the half-male-share document. In Responsa Rama, siman 109, he concluded: although one whose date is earlier collects first, if the creditor collected through gentile courts and the woman could not object and seize from the creditor, then if the creditor sold the land to another Jew, she may not seize from them.
