(א) She may not collect from the guarantor. Maggid Mishneh is uncertain whether this refers specifically only to ketubah and additional ketubah, where the guarantor performed a mitzvah and she lost nothing, and therefore it is proper to make an enactment for the guarantor; but regarding her dowry, which she brought in relying on the guarantor, she collects from the guarantor and he does not impose a vow on her. Or perhaps even in this case he imposes a vow on her, because ultimately there is concern for collusion. He left the matter requiring further study.
