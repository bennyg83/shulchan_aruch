Nichsei tzon ubarzel were written in the ketubah, and she claims that they were destroyed or the husband took them, her relationship to the nichsei tzon ubarzel is like any other lender, and she swears that she did not take them, nor did she give them away or forgive them, and she divides them with other lenders. Rem"a: If the lender collects the dowry of the woman that's substantial, see C"M siman 97 seif 26.
