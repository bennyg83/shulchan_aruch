(א) Agav karka. See Choshen Mishpat siman 113. Regarding an earlier oral loan and a later ketubah in which movables were written as collected agav karka, there is a dispute between Rashdam and Rashach. Rashdam, Choshen Mishpat siman 210, wrote that if the creditor of the oral loan seized on his own, he has acquired it. Rashach, part 1, siman 35, wrote that we remove it from his hand and give it to the widow. See Knesset HaGedolah, Choshen Mishpat siman 104, and the Shach there.
