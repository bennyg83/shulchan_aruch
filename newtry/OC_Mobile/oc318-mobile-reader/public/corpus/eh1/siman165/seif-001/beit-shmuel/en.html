Nevertheless, we do not compel, etc. The Rama writes that although the mitzvah may require chalitzah, we do not always compel the yavam immediately. Rabbenu Tam and the Rosh discuss whether coercion applies when the yavam refuses chalitzah, and whether he can say he wants yibbum. Where yibbum is not appropriate or where the woman is forbidden to him, the basis for compelling chalitzah is stronger.
