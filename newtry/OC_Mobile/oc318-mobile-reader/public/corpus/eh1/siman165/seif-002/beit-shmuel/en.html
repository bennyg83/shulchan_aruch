They compel, etc. Meaning that even though yibbum is a mitzvah, where the circumstances make yibbum improper or forbidden, we compel him to perform chalitzah. What is written in the earlier simanim must be read together with the rule that one may not maintain an impossible or prohibited zikkah when chalitzah can release her.
