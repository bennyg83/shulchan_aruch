A single woman whom they saw had relations with one man and the man who had relations with her went away, and they said to her "Who is this who had relations with you?" and she said "A fit man" — behold she is believed; and not only that — even if they saw her pregnant and said to her "From whom are you pregnant?" and she said "From a fit man" — behold she is believed, and she (and her daughter) (Tur) shall be permitted to a kohen. In what case? When the place where she had relations was a crossroads or corners in the fields where everyone passes, and most of those passing there were fit, and most of the city from which those passers came were fit — for the Sages made an elevation in lineage and required two majorities. But if most of those passing disqualify her — such as Kutim or mamzerim and the like — even though most of the place they came from were fit, or if most of the people of the place were unfit even though most passers there were fit — we are concerned for her and say to one who disqualifies her: "You had relations with her," and she shall not marry a kohen ab initio; but if she married — she need not leave. And some say that when she says "I had relations with a fit man," with one majority we permit ab initio; and bedi'eved, even with a majority of unfit.
