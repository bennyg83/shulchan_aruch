That the Sages made an elevation in lineage — this is the Rambam's view; and I wonder greatly: if so they were stringent even if she says "I had relations with a fit man" on account of elevation in lineage — and if so why do we challenge in the Gemara "do we require two majorities?" and it is taught "nine shops," etc. — meaning one majority suffices; and we answer they made an elevation in lineage — it should challenge more: behold if one majority we do not require, for we rule like R' Gamliel to permit even with a majority of unfit! And further: even per the answerers it seems elevation in lineage requires two majorities, but with one majority we require at least one majority like those nine shops — and this is not per what we rule like R' Gamliel even with majority unfit with her. Granted for the Ran and Tur who hold when she says "I had relations with a fit man" no majority is needed like R' Gamliel — and this sugya of "they made an elevation in lineage" deals when she does not say at all and then even two majorities are needed — it comes out well; but for the Rambam and per Beit Yosef's explanation it is very difficult — undecided.
