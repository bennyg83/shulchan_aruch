Majorities. Likewise two presumptions help — Mishpetei Shmuel siman 214. See Taz Ketubot chapter 1 mishnah 10; see Kenesset HaGedolah in hagahah Tur. Single woman who became pregnant, miscarried, known only from mother and sister-in-law per mother's report, demanded bride-price or marriage from one who admits almost he consorted and compromised for money — if permitted to kohen saying she had relations only with him; responsum Kenesset Yechezkel question 56 ruled forbidden to kohen. One who exchanged religion with Israelite prostitute willingly lived as husband and wife years, bore daughter, all returned to Judaism — he and wife testify daughter from them not another, intends permit her to kohen; Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 213 ruled disqualified for kehunah.
