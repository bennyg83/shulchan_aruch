A rumor went out that so-and-so is a kohen — Beit Shmuel wrote "kohen" is superfluous. In my humble opinion one may say: on the Rambam there is no difficulty, for he copied law 1 together with the law of seif 3. If on the Tur and Shulchan Aruch — since they distinguish whether they call "written" for a Netinah, and the Ran explains that if they call a Netinah "written" we are concerned even though for Netinah they call "written" — this rumor went out to forbid her to her husband, and "written" is nothing, so certainly on account of Netinah; only for a kohen. If the Prisha's words — why not copy? Perhaps even for Israelite — not in vain but to permit to everyone; Tosefta only for permission. Stam no rumor unless wonder of prohibition to husband. If they call — Beit Shmuel disputes Bach; law follows Bach. See YD 57.
