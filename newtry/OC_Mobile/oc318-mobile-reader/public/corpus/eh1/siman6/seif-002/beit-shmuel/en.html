There is one who says. The Rambam forbids to a kohen and the Tur permits; he wrote: even though we rule she is forbidden to her first husband—for there the reason is that she is cut off in his intimacies he goes and does as though she refused him and marries her—and one may bring proof for the Rambam's view from what is written in siman 155 that she is forbidden to his brother even though it is not applicable to say she is cut off in his intimacies. The Bach ruled bedi'eved we do not remove her.
