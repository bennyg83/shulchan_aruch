I was raped. See siman 115 and 178. If known she secluded with someone and says "I am defiled" — she is believed for legs to the matter; likewise if says defiled willingly — so from Tosafot Ketubot 63b s.v. aval amrah etc.; per their words Israelite wife who says she committed adultery willingly is believed; and appears they did not write thus except per view that "I am repulsed" we compel divorce; and we needed to distinguish between that case of three women and "I am repulsed"; but per our ruling we do not compel divorce — that case of three women even without taking ketubah she is not believed like Nimukei Yosef in hagahah Tur; so too Maharam of Padua siman 43 — see Beit Shmuel. Woman who admitted adultery and one witness says he committed adultery with her — forbidden to husband — responsum Chacham Tzvi siman 150.
In the world. Meaning ab initio; but if she married kohen she need not leave unless he believes her. If one witness said she committed adultery — if she denies, witness not believed; if she admits — same law as she said adultery; if silent — depends dispute whether silence like admission — Beit Shmuel; see siman 115.
Of prohibition. If she gives excuse for her words — trusted; so Raavad. Maggid disagrees since husband not obligated divorce by her speech she not believed saying so to divorce — see Rambam Perush HaMishnayot Avodah Zarah; Beit Shmuel wrote however for other excuses even Maggid agrees it helps. If she transgressed and married kohen — her sons kosher for she not believed to disqualify sons — Torah gave trustworthiness only to father not mother. If father says he believes her he can make sons chalalim as believed to say son of divorcee; however when he did not find her virginity and she says from betrothal I was raped — if married kohen sons safek chalalim since rivalry before us — Beit Shmuel.
