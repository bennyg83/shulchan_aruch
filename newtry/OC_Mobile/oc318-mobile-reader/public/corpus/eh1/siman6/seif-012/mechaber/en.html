A kohen's wife who says to her husband "I was raped," or "I erred and another had relations with me," or one witness came and testified to him about her that she committed adultery — whether by force or willingly — she is not forbidden to him, lest her eyes were given to another; but if she is trusted by him or the witness is trusted by him and he relied on his words — he divorces her in order to remove doubt (and see below siman 115 seif 7 and siman 177).
