All, etc. — mishnah chapter Baal Keri; and one who comes, etc.; plain mishnah — all those yevamah matters there; so too the Rambam explains there; Gemara 66b: what "and likewise" — what "not," etc.; although rejected there as mere rejection; since we rule like R' Akiva, the rejection does not apply; although dispute — two versions of Rava on a kohen's wife: whether lashed for zona; for other blemishes he admits; this is the mishnah, chapter 1 of Ketubot 14b: R' Yochanan, incident, etc.; even for a married woman we rule the first version of Rava — even by force she is a zona; Rav Amram perforce holds like him, for regarding an Israelite's wife force for impurity is inapplicable; we rule like R' Akiva, as siman 41; Rambam chapter 1, first version; see there chapter 1; Razah. But Ra'avad and Ramban wrote: all by force are not called zona — the latter version only: disqualified for kehunah because of "and daughter of a kohen," as above, but not lashed for zona. Therefore this mishnah fits; Rav Amram holds the latter version — therefore needed to teach us: for the first version it is obvious, since she became a zona; and likewise for all blemishes. With this, the sugya at the end of chapter 4 of brothers is resolved: Rava holds like R' Akiva; question on Rava's latter version.
