If a rumor went out about a woman that she was betrothed and divorced — we are concerned for the rumor to forbid her to a kohen; and specifically when the rumor went out without an excuse; but if it went out with an excuse — for example the rumor went out that he betrothed her, or divorced her on condition, or threw kiddushin or a get to her — safek close to him, safek close to her — we are not concerned for the rumor. And if there is an excuse in the divorce but not in the kiddushin — we are concerned for the rumor of kiddushin to forbid her generally, and we are not concerned for the rumor of divorce to forbid her to a kohen; and specifically when the excuse went out together with the rumor immediately; but if the rumor went out clearly and afterward the excuse went out — it does not nullify the rumor. And if afterward it became clear that the rumor was false — even if there was no excuse there, we nullify it. And any rumor that was not established in court is not a rumor to be concerned about (and see above siman 46: if a rumor of chalutzah went out about her — some say we are not concerned).
