That she was betrothed and divorced — in the Tur he wrote "and she was divorced immediately"; Beit Yosef wondered in chapter Beit HaBechirah why "immediately" is needed and therefore did not write it here in Shulchan Aruch. One can say what the Tur wrote means: the rumor of divorce also went out immediately at the hour the rumor of kiddushin went out, as afterward regarding excuse; but in truth the divorce itself was a long time after kiddushin; and so too the Ran in the name of the Raavad.
To forbid her to a kohen — meaning if the first died about whom the rumor went out.
And if afterward it became clear — this refers [to the rule above]; but rumors that went out even without excuse [are nullified when proven false].
