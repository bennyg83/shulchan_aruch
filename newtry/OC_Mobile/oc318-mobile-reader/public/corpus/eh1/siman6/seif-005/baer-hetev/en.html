And she was divorced. Specifically that rumor of both went out together; but if rumor of divorce went out after rumor of kiddushin we disregard the later rumor that she was divorced and she is permitted to the one about whom rumor of kiddushin went out — even if he is kohen and after his death forbidden to another kohen. And if two rumors at once — forbidden to marry him if he is kohen, for for him it is rumor before marriage; so is Raavad's view and not Rambam's — see Beit Shmuel.
We are concerned. Since chalitzah is rabbinic and safek chalutzah they did not decree — likewise for rumor they did not decree even for rumor established in court. Therefore ab initio one should be concerned for rumor like safek chalutzah; however language "we are not concerned" implies permitted ab initio and preferable to later authorities over safek chalutzah.
