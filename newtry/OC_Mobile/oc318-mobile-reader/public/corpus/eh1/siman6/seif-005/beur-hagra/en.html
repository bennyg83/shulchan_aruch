A rumor went out, etc. — although the Gemara establishes her to be permitted to marry, nevertheless the main point is to forbid her to a kohen, from what is written: provided there is no, etc.
And if there is, etc. — there in the mishnah: what is an excuse? He divorced, etc.; and only when there is no excuse in betrothal — otherwise there is no need for divorce.
And specifically — there 9:1: R' Bava bar Chana, R' Yosei, etc.
And if afterward, etc. — R' Yochanan holds in the Yerushalmi like those who nullify, who said the Name was examined and found from women and from unfit persons, the owner of the Name; and in scribal material we follow the lenient view; and Syria is the land of Rav Neharde'a, Samuel's land, and we rule like Rav in prohibitions; and Rav Sheshet, because his land is Neharde'a, as stated in Megillah chapter 4 — he sat in the synagogue, etc., and acted like Samuel; see siman 46 seif 4 in the hagahah.
A rumor went out upon her, etc. — Rambam; and the girsa of the Rif and Rosh: regarding an olah we are not concerned; regarding a chalutzah we are not concerned. The Ra'avad there appears to have had our girsa: married, etc.
