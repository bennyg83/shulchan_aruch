She shall not dwell with him in the alley. That is, as long as she has not married; if she married another see siman 119—the dispute of Tosafot and the Rosh. To the Rosh and Ran: every severe prohibition requires greater distance; and at first glance it is difficult from the categories at the beginning of Ketubot where the opposite is stated—that every light prohibition requires a greater fence. One may answer.
