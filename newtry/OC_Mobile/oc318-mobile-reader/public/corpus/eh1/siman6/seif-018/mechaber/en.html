If they saw her have relations or become pregnant in a city — even if only one Kutit or one chalal or slave and the like dwelt there — behold she shall not marry a kohen ab initio, for every fixed [unfit person] is like half and half; but if she married — she need not leave, since she says "I had relations with a fit man." If she was a mute or deaf woman, or she said "I do not know with whom I had relations," or she was a minor who cannot distinguish between fit and unfit — behold she is a safek zona; and if she married a kohen — she must leave, unless there were two majorities of fit available. And some say that even if she became pregnant in a city — if the man who had relations with her went to her, she may marry ab initio, since there is a majority of the city and a majority of caravans of fit; unless she went to him. And likewise stam — when it is not known who went to whom — she may marry ab initio.
