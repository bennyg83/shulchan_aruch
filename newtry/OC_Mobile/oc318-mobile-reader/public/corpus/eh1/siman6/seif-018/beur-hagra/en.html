They saw him, etc. — even with two majorities, not as in the horns, etc.: only at crossroads; in the city — no decree, perhaps she goes, etc.; girsa of Rashi and Tosafot: perhaps she goes, etc.
And if she married, etc. — since, etc.: learn even with a majority of unfit; only teaches: even in such a case she speaks definitely.
She was, etc. — likewise; Yerushalmi: R' Shmuel bar Rav Yitzchak asked — therefore no silence like R' Gamliel and R' Eliezer, only R' Yehoshua; he returned and said: there is silence like R' Gamliel and R' Eliezer in one who is silent; nevertheless, when she speaks she says "I do not know."
And there is one who says, etc. — thus the Tur; Beit Yosef: the Gemara girsa is like ours — must be "she goes," etc.; his words are wondrous, for even with a majority of a caravan we disqualify — decree because of the city, in the city itself, all the more at the conclusion of elevation in lineage. But the Tur holds: what is written regarding horns does not mean likewise in the city — wherever there are two majorities, no question; majority of the city — only majority of caravan in the city; but with two majorities, presumably fit; we do not decree so much from majority of the city, perhaps she goes; therefore "since," etc., and stam, etc.
