A rumor went out that a woman was betrothed and divorced — see Beit Shmuel and what he wrote: it seems this is the Tur's view. True, his language in siman 46 also implies together; but question: in siman 46, the main law of prohibition and permission to everyone — he should have explained immediately. What he wrote afterward to distinguish: if at once, considered before marriage for this husband if kohen — first rumor before marriage, forbidden to him; unlike at once permitted — rumor after betrothal. Derishah therefore did not need to say therefore wrote immediately per Ra'avad; always per Rambam as siman 46 without "immediately." Here since main law of kehunah prohibition he immediately taught she is forbidden also to her husband if kohen. Per Tur who is concerned for rumor to forbid kohen — seems only to another kohen afterward. Question on Beit Shmuel: whence for Gemara to explain mishnah "divorcee" — she is a divorcee — lenient, permitted to everyone; per Tur agrees Ra'avad what teaches divorcee — specifically immediately; or challenge and forbid to the man — say teaches divorcee only stringency, regarding one about whom rumor went out betrothed and divorced immediately and he is kohen — forbid to him as rumor before marriage; likewise to everyone. For Rashi one may say does not imply only at once; per his words question. See Chiddushei Ran similar challenge; answer this is no novelty but certainly novel — for the explanation itself is difficult: even at once — either betrothal rumor true, divorce rumor after betrothal; if false plainly permitted if betrothed with witnesses; even if immediately rumor — rumor after betrothal. Especially per Tur s.k. 11 — see there.
