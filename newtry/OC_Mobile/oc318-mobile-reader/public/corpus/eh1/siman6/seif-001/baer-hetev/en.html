Regarding divorcee. See Maharshal part 1 siman 255.
Rabbinically. See R' Eliyahu Sasson siman 211. One who married a chalutzah — even if he vowed nezirut from her from now, and even if he has monetary coercion — it is forbidden to keep her; Ra'ah part 1 siman 59. A kohen who was established as kohen for many years and married a chalutzah because he did not know she was chalutzah — we do not compel him to divorce; Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 93 rules. Per Rashdam in Choshen Mishpat siman 235 we do not compel kohanim nowadays since kohanim are safek; but responsum Mahari"t part 1 siman 149 and responsum Chut HaShani siman 17 lengthy refutation of Rashdam; and Shevut Yaakov there rejects them and upholds Rashdam. At any rate he raises that if he wishes to divorce — remember for good since his sons are in any case pushed from kehunah. But we do not compel the insane — see there. And responsum Kenesset Yechezkel question 56 at end disagrees with Shevut Yaakov and wrote Heaven forbid to cast aspersion on kehunah lineage nowadays that all firstborns be unredeemed — see there.
To divorce. But ab initio they decreed even on safek chalutzah — see Tosafot Yevamot 24a.
To divorce. And likewise safek chalalah and safek zona — all from Torah except safek mamzer is rabbinic. See Ranach part 1 siman 42. One who married safek shevuyah need not divorce her — R' Yehuda HaLevi siman 33.
Mere [rumor]. In responsum Rashal he wrote specifically regarding betrothed there is concern for such rumor, but generally not; see Maggid Mishneh chapter HaOmer and Beit Yosef siman 13 — explained there specifically if court required a get even though only on account of mere rumor we are concerned; but if he gave get himself and was not required by law — it is not a get; so too Beit HaLevi and Beit Shmuel.
For kehunah. Beit Yosef [Beit Chadash] wrote this is ab initio but she need not leave. Taz disagrees with Beit Shmuel.
