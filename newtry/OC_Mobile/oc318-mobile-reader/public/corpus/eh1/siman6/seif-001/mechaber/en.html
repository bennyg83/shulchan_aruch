Women forbidden to a kohen and the law of a kohen's wife. It contains 15 seifim: A kohen is forbidden by Torah law to [marry] a divorcee, zona, or chalalah; and forbidden to [marry] a chalutzah rabbinically. Therefore if he transgressed and married a safek chalutzah — he need not divorce her; but a divorcee, even if she is only a safek divorcee — he must divorce her, whether she was divorced from erusin or from nissuin; and even the smell of a get disqualifies for kehunah, and we compel him to divorce. What is meant by "smell of a get"? For example if he said to her, "Behold you are divorced from me and not permitted to any man" — even though she was not permitted by this get, she is disqualified for kehunah (and even if she was not actually divorced but only on account of a mere rumor of kiddushin, even though it is clear there is no substance to those kiddushin and they only give a get out of mere stringency — even so she is disqualified for kehunah) (Responsum of the Rashba siman 250). But if he gave her a get on condition and the condition was not fulfilled — it is nothing and does not disqualify; and a kohen is permitted ab initio to divorce on condition.
