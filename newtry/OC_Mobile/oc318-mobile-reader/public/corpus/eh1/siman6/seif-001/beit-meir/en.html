Hagahah: even if she was not divorced except on account of rumor — see siman 17 seif 58. Beit Shmuel brings in the name of responsum Maharshal: only when betrothed. See there, for he concludes: when an act occurred. In my humble opinion there is no difficulty regarding "kohen" — if on the Rambam, no difficulty, for he copied the law together with the law of seif 3. If on the Tur and Shulchan Aruch — since they distinguish whether they call a Netinah "written," and it is explained in the Ran that therefore if they call a Netinah "written," even though for a Netinah they also call "written," we are concerned — for this rumor did not go out in vain but to forbid her to her husband; and since "written" is nothing, certainly the rumor went out on account of Netinah — this applies only to a kohen. If these are the Prisha's words, which I do not have — why did he not copy them? Perhaps he holds that even for an Israelite it applies — not in vain but to permit her to everyone; but the Tosefta does not seem so, only for permission. Stam, no rumor goes out, etc., only per the wonder of prohibition to her husband — see there. If they call — see Beit Shmuel: disputes the Bach; in my humble opinion certainly the law follows the Bach, for reason two that he wrote; and all that depends on investigation — we do not rely, for we are not expert to investigate. See Yoreh De'ah siman 57.
