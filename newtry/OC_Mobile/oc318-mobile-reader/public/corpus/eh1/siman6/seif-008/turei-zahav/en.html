Therefore one who had relations with an animal, etc. — see responsum Maharam of Lublin regarding intercourse of wind called Tracht that does not forbid a woman to her husband.
And some say one who has relations with those liable to positive commandments, etc. — Beit Yosef challenged this from the baraita that teaches "disqualified for kehunah" — see siman 7 below; and likewise yevamah, etc. "he made her a zona" — he explained we establish like Shmuel who is doubtful whether there will be kiddushin efficacy for the deceased's wife going out — meaning there will not be efficacy of kiddushin — therefore she is a zona or not; therefore she needs a get out of doubt, as written in siman 18; and if so what is written that she is a zona — means from the side of stringency she is considered a zona; and with this Beit Yosef's difficulties are resolved.
