Who is [called] a zona? Anyone who is not a daughter of Israel, or a daughter of Israel who had relations with a man to whom she is forbidden to marry with a prohibition equal for all, or who had relations with a chalal — even though she is permitted to marry him. Therefore one who had relations with an animal — even though she is [liable to] stoning — does not become a zona and is not disqualified for kehunah, for behold she did not have relations with a man. One who has relations with a niddah — even though she is [liable to] karet — does not become a zona and is not disqualified for kehunah, for behold she is not forbidden to marry him. And likewise one who has relations with a single woman — even if she was a kedeshah who made herself available, who is [liable to] lashes — does not become a zona and is not disqualified from kehunah, for behold she is not forbidden to marry him. But one who had relations with someone [forbidden by] one of the negative commandments equal for all and not specific to kohanim, or [forbidden by] positive commandments — and needless to say one to whom she is forbidden on account of ervah, or a Kutit or slave — since she is forbidden to marry him, behold this is a zona. And likewise a convert and a freedwoman — even if she converted or was freed less than three years ago — since she is not a daughter of Israel, behold this is a zona and forbidden to a kohen. And likewise a yevamah with whom a stranger had relations — he made her a zona. And some say that one who has relations with those liable to positive commandments or negative commandments — even other negative commandments — does not make her a zona, except one who has relations with a yevamah.
