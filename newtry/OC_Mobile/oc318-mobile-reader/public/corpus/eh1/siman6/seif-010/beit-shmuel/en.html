Wife of a kohen, etc. He informs us hereby that she is forbidden to her husband and we do not hear from the law of an Israelite's wife—what is written in the next seif, for there it is different to permit lekhatchilah to a kohen. See in the Maor and Milchamot Hashem; see the sugya 56 that one is lashed for tumah and for zona; see Maggid chapter 1 and chapter 18 that the Rambam too holds she becomes a zona; hence also if she was raped by an Israelite or gentile she becomes a zona—for all lying disqualifies whether by force or willingly.
