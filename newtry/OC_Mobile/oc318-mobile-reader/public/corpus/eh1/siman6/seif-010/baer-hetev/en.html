Forbidden to him. See Maggid chapter 1 and Perush HaMishnayot Avodah Zarah whether kohen who has relations with her afterward receives lashes for zona and tumah or not. Tur wrote plainly he receives two sets; per Maggid who explained there Rambam holds she becomes zona — therefore even if raped by Israelite or gentile she becomes zona — Beit Shmuel.
