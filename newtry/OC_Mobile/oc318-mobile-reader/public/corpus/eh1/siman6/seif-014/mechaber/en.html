A kohen who betrothed an adult or minor woman, and after some time had relations with her and claimed he found her to be beset — she is forbidden to him out of doubt, lest she had relations before kiddushin or after kiddushin. But an Israelite who claimed this — she is not forbidden to him, for there are two doubts here: perhaps before kiddushin, perhaps after kiddushin; and even if we say after kiddushin — perhaps by force, perhaps willingly — for a raped woman is permitted to an Israelite. Therefore if her father betrothed her to an Israelite and she is less than three years and one day old, and he claimed he found her beset — she is forbidden to him out of doubt, for there is only one doubt here: perhaps by force, perhaps willingly; and a doubt in a Torah prohibition [is resolved] stringently.
