Forbidden to him. Unless he betrothed and had relations immediately, or there are witnesses she did not commit harlotry after betrothal; and on account of harlotry before betrothal she is permitted—for we rule an unmarried woman who committed harlotry, if she married a kohen she need not leave, as written shortly.
Lest before betrothal. We do not say establish her on her presumption that she was fit; one may say on the contrary establish her on her presumption that she was a virgin and now was lain with by harlotry. Tosafot resolve: if she were mukhat etz she would claim thus—for there is no disgrace for her to say "I am defiled." The Maggid resolves: defilement is not common; and it seems from Tosafot if she said "I am defiled" or if she said "before betrothal" she would be believed. See siman 68 where it is explained at length; see in the Rosh chapter 2 of Ketubot and Maggid chapter 18.
