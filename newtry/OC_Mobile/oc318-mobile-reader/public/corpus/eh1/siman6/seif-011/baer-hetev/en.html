Who was raped. Whoever beginning of intercourse was by force even if end willingly — is as raped. See Targum Yonatan parashat Ki Teitzei; see Beit Yosef. Woman who saved her husband and others by making herself available to prostitute — permitted to husband or not; Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 176 ruled if coercion is on the intercourse itself it is full rape and permitted to husband; not so if she acquiesces to intercourse and another coerces — forbidden to husband. If truly raped plainly permitted; nevertheless needs immersion lest she be repulsive to him — see there.
To her husband. And likewise to the one who had relations if he is not kohen — Beit Yosef; see siman 11.
