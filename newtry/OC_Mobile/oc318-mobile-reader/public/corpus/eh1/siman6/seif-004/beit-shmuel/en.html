A rumor went out that so-and-so is a kohen. It seems from what is written "kohen" is superfluous; even for an Israelite the law is likewise. And what the Prisha wrote on this—so it seems to me.
If they call her, etc. So it is in the Gemara "she must leave"; and the matter needs investigation whether they call a Netinah "written." So wrote the Tur; and the Rambam is silent and wrote "she must leave from the second" and did not distinguish whether they call a Netinah "written." The Ran wondered at him; therefore the Mechaber ruled like the Tur. See Bach what he wrote to resolve; also it may be resolved another way. However, that the Bach rules like the Rambam—so it seems to me, for to the Rif, Rashi, Rosh, Tur, and Ran she does not leave unless they call a Netinah "written"; it seems that is the essential view.
