She is not betrothed. Even if kiddushin in existence — nevertheless first kiddushin are not kiddushin and must betroth anew after conversion and say then he betroths her with this; specifically when in existence. However these kiddushin were not given to take effect since kiddushin void and she must return to him — we do not say he gave as gift like betrothing his sister. If one betrothed after thirty and second came and betrothed within thirty and said "after your husband dies" — she is betrothed for in his power to betroth immediately even if said from now and after thirty days — nevertheless in second's hand to betroth doubtful kiddushin; therefore now also doubtful kiddushin; see all this in Ketzot HaChoshen.
