Even though money was consumed. Some explain this money is a loan — loan given for expenditure and it is as though he gave her nothing; some explain deposit that was stolen — deposit stands in depositor's domain unlike this money in her domain standing — Gemara; some explain like one who says to his friend "draw this cow and do not acquire except after thirty days" — he does not acquire since drawing after thirty days the drawing expired; but here in any case money she must return and it is as though he gave money at end of thirty.
And if he retracted. Ran wrote he need not return kiddushin if he retracted — for if you say until hour kiddushin take effect money in his domain — if so when consumed why betrothed? In Rosh's responsum implies she must return even if he retracts; condition "behold you are betrothed after thirty days" means if he does not return; that she is betrothed if money consumed — because it came to her as kiddushin money; so wrote Chadashim of Maharit; if she retracted she must return kiddushin to all — there in Ran; and so Mordechai and Rosh responsum kelal 35 siman 8; when betrothed after thirty days or before thirty days — no concern of kiddushin; if she is kohen's daughter she may eat terumah — there in Ran.
If he betrothed with document, etc. Reason: granted if betrothing with money it is as money in existence since she must return; but with document if lost or torn nothing remains after thirty days; and likewise if document in existence not in her possession if placed in court; if paper of document worth perutah it is like kiddushin of money for his intent is also on paper — there in Ran; see siman 28.
Thirty days betrothed forever. Rosh Sifri applies if betrothing with money; but if betrothing with document Ran compares to divorcing man who said "today you are not my wife" as written siman 137.
