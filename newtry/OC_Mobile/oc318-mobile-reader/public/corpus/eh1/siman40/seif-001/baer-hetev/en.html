Betrothed. Must return kiddushin to all; if he retracted — per Ran need not return kiddushin; per Rosh's responsum and Maharit must return even if he retracts — Beit Shmuel.
With document. If betrothed with money — as money in existence since she must return; but with document if lost or torn nothing remains per those who say thirty days; likewise if document in existence not in her possession if in court — law is so; if paper of document worth perutah — like kiddushin of money for intent also on paper — Ran.
Per those who say thirty days. Rosh Sifri applies if betrothing with money; if betrothing with document Ran compares to divorcing man — doubtful divorce as siman 137; likewise here doubtful kiddushin; see below siman 38 s.k. 1.
