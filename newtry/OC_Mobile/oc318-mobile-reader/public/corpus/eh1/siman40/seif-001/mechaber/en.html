Law of one who betroths after time, such as after she gives birth and similar. It contains 8 seifim. One who says to a woman "behold you are betrothed to me with this perutah after thirty days" — even if the money was consumed within thirty days — she is betrothed after thirty days. If he retracted within the thirty days, or she retracted — she is not betrothed. {Rama: Some say if he betrothed with a document and it is not in existence after thirty days — rather torn or lost within thirty — she is not betrothed (Ran chapter HaOmer). One who betroths a woman for thirty days — betrothed forever (there).}
