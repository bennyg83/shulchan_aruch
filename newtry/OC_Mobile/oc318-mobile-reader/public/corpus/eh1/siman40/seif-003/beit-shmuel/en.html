From now and after thirty days. Rashi explained Rav is doubtful whether condition or not — can he retract within thirty or she retract from "from now" — therefore within thirty can retract in any case. But Ramban and Rashba in responsum siman 407 wrote: if condition — condition is if he lives after thirty days; therefore if he retracts within thirty needs get from him; in these conditions condition depends on him not her and he can retract unlike saying "behold you are betrothed after thirty days" — she too can retract; see Rashba responsum 407; if said from now if reaches until thirty days — all agree it is condition — Shach there; and if said from now and will not be betrothed except after thirty days — it is condition — Mordechai.
Both give get. Tur wrote forbidden to both unless one gives get — implies whichever gives get she is permitted to the other; even if second divorces permitted to first; so reasonable for here not relevant to say first divorced within thirty for whether retraction or condition in any case within thirty kiddushin still pending; not only per Rashi that condition if he does not return — why need get? He has power to retract; even per Ramban's explanation condition is if he lives after thirty and not yet finished — how divorce before kiddushin take effect? What Rambam wrote both give get — regarding if she wishes to marry to market; but if one wishes to marry her — one can say permitted; so Perishah and Taz; but Maharshal wrote stringently one should not marry the other; possibly concern they will say halachah like R' Yehudah who said leave one and all kiddushin take effect and first can divorce immediately as written nearby in name of Rashba; also per nearby what Rosh holds even per Rav can divorce immediately — fine.
