He says to woman, etc. — I wonder: Rashba wrote "and you will say: but every 'thus-and-thus' is like speech except idolatry and kiddushin" — one can say not actual retraction he speaks but similar, etc. — not correct at all; reasonable here one who speaks before giving money and giving not finished until he said such language — he can retract; even betrothed with deposit in her hand and said "behold you are betrothed to me from now and after thirty with deposit in my hand in your hand" — kiddushin not finished until after his words finish that he says with deposit, etc.; there when gave kiddushin and afterward retracted "thus-and-thus" — therefore since finished he cannot void — end quote; if so appropriate to distinguish: when he gave perutah before saying from now and after thirty — betrothed to first; perhaps for this Rambam hinted in law 10 wrote "behold you are betrothed to me with these coins after thirty"; in 5:12 wrote "one who says behold you are betrothed from now and after thirty with this dinar" — behold in law 10 wrote "with these coins" before condition for no difference; in 5:12 wrote "this dinar" after condition; but Shulchan Arukh who precedes "with this perutah" — difficult should have explained this only when not given until after his words finish.
