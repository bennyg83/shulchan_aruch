A second man came and betrothed her within thirty days — she is betrothed to the second forever; for at the time the second betrothed her she was not betrothed, and he acquired her with second's kiddushin and she became a married woman. After thirty days when first's kiddushin come — he finds her a married woman; the first is as one who betrothed a married woman — kiddushin do not take effect in her. {Rama: But if the second died or divorced her within thirty — first's kiddushin return (Tur in name of some). Therefore she needs a get from the first (Taz, Beur HaGra). And some dispute (Tur in name of Rosh).}
