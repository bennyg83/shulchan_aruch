But if, etc. Yerushalmi: therefore if second died within thirty or divorced — first's kiddushin take effect; after thirty died or inherited — first's kiddushin did not take effect on her, etc.; and so in mishnah that says betrothed to second and no question not betrothed to first — Ramban.
And some dispute. From what is written "she retracted," etc.; and as written in 61; all the more when she extended hand and received kiddushin from another — it is retraction; as written Yevamot 101a "her kiddushin are," etc. — Rashba, Rosh, and Rashba; Ran wrote this is not worth rejecting Yerushalmi's words.
