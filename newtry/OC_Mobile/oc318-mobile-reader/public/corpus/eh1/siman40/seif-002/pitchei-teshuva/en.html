Betrothed after thirty days. See Mishneh LaMelech chapter 6 Laws of Divorce law 3 who doubted if can divorce her within thirty days since lacking only time and automatically comes — or perhaps not subject to divorce. From Rambam chapter 7 Laws of Marriage (and Shulchan Arukh below seif 3) therefore both give get within thirty, etc. — no proof for different there for first betrothed from now and after thirty — in any case if condition — when time comes revealed at time of writing get she was betrothed and subject to divorce; if retraction — no kiddushin at all for another came and betrothed; second also divorces within thirty because betrothed without any time; if from first is retraction — behold betrothed to him from now. But forever if another did not come and betrothed — cannot divorce within thirty because possibly retraction; per this at time of writing get she was not subject to divorce; but per Rashba responsum 407 (also brought Beit Shmuel s.k. 8) who holds even there even if condition cannot divorce within time — learn all the more here no condition and kiddushin take effect only after time — cannot divorce within time. Again wrote no room for doubt at all since we hold both he and she can retract — if he did not retract explicitly and gave get — no greater retraction than this except doubt in general conditions such as saying to woman behold you are betrothed on condition you do something — whether can divorce within time, etc.; expanded there on this detail see there; see Sha'ar HaMelech chapter 7 law 12 who expanded all this.
