And per Rambam, etc. For he explains Abaye established them per method unnecessary for Rabbah and Rav Yosef who established in error; but for Rabbah and Rav Yosef — that of R' Aviyah even per Rabbanan not davar shelo ba le'olam; therefore strained and established in error in order to establish per Rabbanan because mishnah of R' Aviyah is clear; and R' Chanina states per Rabbanan; and this is meaning "when fetus recognized" s.v. But Rashba and Ran and Shulchan Arukh dispute him and wrote: if so Abaye should dispute them; further we say Bava Batra 141-142 that one who said, etc. to this pregnant — presumably fetus recognized — nevertheless we say there it is davar shelo ba le'olam; and from challenge there R' Nissim to R' Hoshaya "one who says," etc. — did not establish when fetus recognized; further challenged there "come learn child," etc. fetus no — apparently even when fetus recognized no; that which Rabbah and R' Yochanan established in error — language "fruits of this bed" forty that came to world partially — therefore they said he did not say, etc.; and R' Chanina like R' Aviyah; what Abaye said all although not similar to each other — because Rabbanan dispute on all; Tosafot Sukkah 7b s.v. kulhu, etc.; Rashbam wrote when said "all" he holds, etc. they are not equal; when said "they said" one matter they are equal; error in mishnah "if she was," etc.; but Rambam in explanation reads it and wrote: although we hold a person does not transfer davar shelo ba le'olam — they said here his words stand to be stringent, etc.; and this is meaning here and must return, etc.; difficult what they said "it is taught," etc.
