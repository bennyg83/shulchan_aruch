In this order. Implies if one betrothed from now and after first's time finished — not betrothed; but Ran wrote even in such case kiddushin take effect per R' Yochanan who said leave one for friend; also per Maggid Rif did not rule like Abaye — if so we are concerned perhaps one said condition language and one retraction language — if so perhaps this one who said from now and after thirty said retraction language and so all; and this is meaning "from now and after forty" said condition language. For many poskim halachah like R' Yochanan who said leave one — then certainly kiddushin; even though remainder kiddushin not kiddushin — nevertheless here no remainder for he left only until thirty days as Ran wrote; further difference: each can divorce immediately for kiddushin take effect immediately. For these poskim who ruled like Rav who said doubt whether condition or retraction as Rambam and author ruled — cannot divorce immediately lest condition and kiddushin not yet taken effect — so Rashba responsum 407; however Rosh appears not to hold thus for he understood Rambam rules like Rav who said we are doubtful condition or retraction; Rambam wrote both give get within or after thirty days; and so Maggid and Beit Yosef explained Rambam's words — if so must hold each can divorce immediately even if condition; reason: we say in any case if condition fulfilled — if so from now she is betrothed and get valid; if condition not fulfilled — kiddushin void and as divorcing another woman not his wife; only difficult: therefore require announcement for kehunah lest condition not fulfilled and get void; possibly similar to divorcing on condition — when condition not fulfilled permitted to kehunah and no announcement needed because known he divorced conditionally — likewise here. However appears should be stringent per Rashba's view; if betrothing with document and said from now and after thirty days see Ran end 247d and below siman 146 if must exist after thirty days.
