After you are freed — not betrothed. In sugya it states even if says to his maidservant "behold you are betrothed after I free you" — although in his power to free — nevertheless not betrothed from reason originally animal and now different matter; poskim omitted — we learn from law of seif 7 "he says to his wife behold you are betrothed after I divorce you" — not betrothed from reason granted in his power to divorce — not in his power to betroth; likewise here without aforementioned reason as Tosafot explained not betrothed — Beit Shmuel; and must return and we do not say money is gift like betrothing his sister; I think reason similar to seller when yovel practiced — Shmuel admits money returns; because no person knows from opinion of Rav who said sold; likewise here no person knows from opinion of R' Meir; see Gittin 45 Tosafot s.v. midi dehava.
