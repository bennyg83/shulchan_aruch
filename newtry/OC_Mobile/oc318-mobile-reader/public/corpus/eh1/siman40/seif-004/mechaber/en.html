If one man said to her, "Behold, you are betrothed to me from now, with completion after thirty days," and another came and said, "Your betrothal to me begins now and will be completed after twenty days," and a third came and said, "Behold, you are betrothed to me immediately, with completion after ten days," even if one hundred men spoke in this same descending order, the kiddushin of all of them take effect in her, and she needs a get from each one, because she is doubtfully betrothed to them all.
