He said to her, etc. Rambam and Rashba and Ran; nevertheless per R' Yochanan not because of doubt kiddushin take effect but for all certainly kiddushin as written in sugya and Rashi; further per R' Yochanan even if first said after thirty and second after forty — unlike Rav; Ramban explained like Rav; so Rif; their reason sugya in Ketzot HaChoshen like him; and Rosh and Rabbenu Chananel like R' Yochanan; Rav and R' Yochanan — halachah like R' Yochanan; Rambam ruled stringently like both; only difficult why Rambam wrote specifically in this order — therefore Ketzot HaChoshen wrote Rambam holds although in sugya forty per R' Yochanan certainly kiddushin take effect — not in sugya; rather R' Yochanan also holds like Rav it is doubtful; nevertheless all take effect; and strained Rambam thus because all sugya forty is doubtful; impossible all not like R' Yochanan; his words forced; Ketzot HaChoshen's words correct: Rambam is doubtful like whom halachah; and this is meaning "in this order" — not decisive; known he does not write except Gemara language; and Rashi s.v. deshavu nafshei, etc.
