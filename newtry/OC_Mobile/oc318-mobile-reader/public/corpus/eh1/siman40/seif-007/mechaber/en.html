One who gives two perutot to a woman and said to her "behold you are betrothed to me today with one and with one after I divorce you" — she is betrothed; and when he divorces her she will be doubtfully betrothed. But if he gave a perutah to a married woman and said "behold you are betrothed after you are divorced," or gave a perutah to his wife and said "behold you are betrothed to me after I divorce you" — it is nothing.
