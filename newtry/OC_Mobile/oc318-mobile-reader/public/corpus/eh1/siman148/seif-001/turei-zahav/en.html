He should not divorce her with that get. The reason is that people may say her get preceded her son. Rashi explained: perhaps he will keep the get for a long time between writing and delivery, and she will have children from him during that time, and afterward he will divorce her with it. Later, when the matter is forgotten, people will see that the date of the get precedes the birth of the son, and they will think that it was given to her from the time of writing, and they will say that the child was born from an unmarried woman after he divorced her. This is a blemish. End quote.
