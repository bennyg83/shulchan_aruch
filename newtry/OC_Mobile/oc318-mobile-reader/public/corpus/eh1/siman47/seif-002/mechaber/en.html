Two say she was betrothed and two say she was not betrothed — it is no different than if he threw kiddushin to her: two say closer to him and two say closer to her. It is no different than if two say there was never doubtful kiddushin in her — she may not marry; and if she married she must leave. And if she married one of her witnesses and says it is clear to me I was not betrothed — she need not leave.
