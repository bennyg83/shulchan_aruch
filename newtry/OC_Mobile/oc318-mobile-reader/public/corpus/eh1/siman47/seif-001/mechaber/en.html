Witnesses who contradict each other regarding kiddushin, or she says "I was betrothed" and retracted. It contains 4 seifim. Two say we saw she was betrothed and two say we did not see she was betrothed — behold she is betrothed; even if all of them dwell with her in one courtyard — "we did not see" is not evidence, for it is possible she was betrothed in private.
