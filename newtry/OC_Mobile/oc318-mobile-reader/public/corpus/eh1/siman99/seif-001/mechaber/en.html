They appraise for a widow her garments. It contains 2 seifim. A widow who comes to collect her ketubah — they appraise all her garments, whether weekday or Shabbat, and deduct them from her ketubah. (And a chalutzah has the law of a widow) (Beit Yosef in name of Rivash siman 302). But a divorcee whose husband divorced her willingly without a claim — they do not appraise weekday garments for her. But festival and Shabbat garments — they appraise for her. And they cannot remove her from her garments with money (Beit Yosef in name of Ritva). Rather she takes them at their value (responsum Rosh kelal 80).
