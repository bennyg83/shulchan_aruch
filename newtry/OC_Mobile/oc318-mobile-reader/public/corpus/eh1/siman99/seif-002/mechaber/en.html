One who gives a gift to his wife — even though he divorces her willingly — she acquired her gift. {Rama: It seems here there is an error; rather it should be: even though he divorces her unwillingly, such as if she was rebellious against him — nevertheless her gift is hers. And likewise a widow who takes a gift — all that are not items that are clothing or jewelry made to adorn oneself with; and specifically that the husband made them for her. But others who gave her jewelry at the time of marriage — they intended a complete gift; behold they are like her melog property (Beit Yosef in name of Rivash siman 301).}
