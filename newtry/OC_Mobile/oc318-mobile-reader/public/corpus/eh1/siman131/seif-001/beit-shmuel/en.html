(ב) How should the scribe, etc. The Mechaber omitted the law of whether the scribe may prepare blank forms of Gittin, and this has practical consequence. Rambam permits the scribe to prepare forms of Gittin, but most poskim disagree with him. Even according to the one who holds that if a deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor wrote the form it is valid, this is not comparable, because writing by a deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor is uncommon, whereas writing by the scribe is common, and there is concern that he will also write the essential part. In this, Ramban and Ramah disagree: if such a Get was given to her, according to Ramban she may not marry with it; according to the Rosh, if it was already written, he may divorce with it. Halakhot Gedolot and Rashba invalidate such a Get; it appears that they too hold like Ramban, that she may not marry with it, but if she married she need not leave, for it is no worse than other rabbinic invalidities, where we hold that she need not leave. What the Tur wrote in this siman, that she must leave, he wrote only according to Ramban's approach, for Ramban holds that with all rabbinic invalidities she must leave, as written in the Ran in Perek HaMegarresh. However, the Tur is difficult: in siman 120 it implies that he holds primarily like Rambam, who holds that with rabbinic invalidities she need not leave, while in this siman he wrote according to Ramban's approach. In siman 123 Rama cited Ri's opinion, which invalidates where a deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor writes the form. According to this, the same applies here: if the form was written not for her sake, it is invalid.
