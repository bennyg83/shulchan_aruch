The custom is to write it with good ink. The Beit Meir writes that it appears that if ink is not available, one should seek out the alternatives in the order in which they are listed, as we find in Orach Chaim, siman 473, se'if 5. This is certainly so regarding those materials that are included from the broad formulation that a get may be written with anything, such as that which Rabbi Chanina said: if one wrote it with teriya water and gallnuts, it is valid. Even though these materials are valid even ab initio, nevertheless there is a mitzvah to seek out the explicitly listed alternative first. See there.
