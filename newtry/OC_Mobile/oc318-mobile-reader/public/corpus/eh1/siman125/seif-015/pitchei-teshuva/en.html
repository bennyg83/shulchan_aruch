Chok tochot. See Ginat Veradim, se'if katan 18, who writes that Mahari ben Lev, part 3, siman 69, inferred from the words of the Rashba and Maharik, shoresh 71, that the fact that we invalidate chok tochot in a get applies specifically to the essential text, but not to the standard formula. See below, siman 16 in the gloss, and what is written there. He further writes in se'if katan 25 that Seder Get of Rabbi Yehuda Mintz, siman 85, writes that where chok tochot is invalid, passing a quill over it does not help. See there. In se'if katan 33, he brings that Sefer HaTerumah, siman 205, and Hagahot Ashri, chapter 2 of Gittin, write likewise. Magen Avraham, siman 32, se'if katan 26, also writes this. He himself writes about this that from the words of Tosafot in the second chapter of Gittin, page 19, under the words “ink on top of ink,” it is implied that where the second writing corrects something, it is considered writing. If so, one can discuss whether passing a quill over chok tochot makes it into chok yerechot. See below, siman 131, se'if 5, where passing a quill over the writing in order to make it for her sake does not make it a get, and some say that we are concerned for it. If so, it appears that the same would apply to passing a quill over it in order to make it into chok yerechot, that we should be concerned for it. These are his words. See Beit Meir, who writes: If I were worthy to disagree by reasoning alone, it would seem logical to me that even according to the opinion that passing a quill over the writing to make it for her sake does not help, nevertheless passing a quill over chok tochot does help, because this is like writing on blank paper. See there at length.
