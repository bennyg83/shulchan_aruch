And his hand continued and it became closed. From this wording it sounds that this applies specifically in such a case, where this mem never had a moment of validity, because before he removed his hand from it, it became closed. However, in Orach Chaim, siman 32, se'if 18, this language is not used. There it says: “An open mem whose opening became attached and it became closed,” and so on. From that wording it sounds that even though it had a moment of validity and only afterwards became closed, it is invalid retroactively, and one may not scrape away the attachment because it would be chok tochot. See Ginat Veradim, se'if katan 39, regarding what he writes about this. See also what I wrote above at the beginning of se'if katan 6.
