He concludes as follows: As to the halacha, it appears to me that if one wrote an open mem where a closed mem was needed, or a closed mem where an open mem was needed, and similarly with the other final-form letters מנצפ"ך, if this occurred in the essential text of the get, it is invalid and she may not marry with it. In the standard formula, after the fact it should be validated, as the Rama writes below in se'if 16 regarding where one wrote the alef and lamed of ישראל together. Even in the essential text, if there would be great igun, or after the fact if she already remarried, it is possible to be lenient. End quote. See there.
