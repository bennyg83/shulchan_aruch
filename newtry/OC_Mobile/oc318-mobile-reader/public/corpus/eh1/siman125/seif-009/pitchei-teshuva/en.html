That one should not separate them. See Ba'er Heitev. See the responsa of the holy gaon Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi, of blessed memory, who writes that it appears to him that even the Rama in his gloss speaks only where writing another get would be a pressing circumstance, unless one incurred great expense. For example, the scribe and witnesses are hurrying on their way, and the delay would cause them great financial loss, or a similar case where another get cannot be written except with difficulty and great loss. In such a case, it may be corrected. This is the implication of the language, that through difficulty another get could at least be written. However, where it is completely impossible to write another get, that is a case of igun, which the Rama explicitly permitted in Darkei Moshe, and the Levush writes similarly. This works out well, and the question of the Beit Shmuel from se'if 16 in the gloss is not difficult. The Rama relied on this and therefore did not need to mention it in se'if 6. These are his words.
