Not to accept deposits from women, slaves, and minors. It contains 2 seifim. One does not accept a deposit from a married woman; and if he accepted it he returns it to the woman. (And if she said they belong to so-and-so she is believed (Tur). If the woman went overseas and the husband came and claimed from the depositor and says his wife stole from him — he does not give them to him until the woman comes or dies — so it seems to me.) And if she said at her death that they belong to so-and-so — if she is believed by him he does as she said, and if not he gives to her heirs. (And likewise if she died and said nothing (Tur).) And some say that if she conducts business within the house she is not believed to say they belong to so-and-so. {Rama: However, as long as she is alive he returns to the woman (Tur). However, if he returned to the husband the depositor is exempt even if she does not conduct business within the house (Mordechai chapter Chazakat HaBatim).}
