(א) "Valid etc." — Proof from Ketubot and Niddah for the Rambam's view; the dissenting view. Valid etc. The Rambam, following his own method in seif 1. There is proof from what is written in two places [in Ketubot]: "Therefore" etc., or "she became a niddah" etc. — evidently she cannot marry. And in the last chapter of Niddah (66a): "He waited seven days" etc. Furthermore, according to his method, he explained that chuppah means seclusion, and a niddah is forbidden in seclusion with him unless he has already been intimate, as I wrote there, seif 4. The Ran at the beginning of Ketubot wrote: but according to those who disagree — as stated above — and since chuppah does not mean seclusion according to his view, as I wrote above in siman 54 — we do not object to this. Nevertheless it is proper etc., as written in the Shulchan Aruch here. Therefore Ravina waited etc. See also the Rosh, ch. 5, seif 6.
(ב) "Nevertheless etc." — Cross-reference to Beit HaGadol and Haggahot Asheri. Nevertheless etc. See Beit HaGadol and Haggahot Asheri there, s.v. "and one must say" etc.
