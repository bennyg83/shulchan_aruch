"Nevertheless it is good to inform him." Nevertheless it is good to inform him. Even though according to the Rambam's view her status is as betrothed and he acquires nothing from her — and even if she herself knows she is a niddah — nevertheless he does not know, and therefore he must be informed. Then the chuppah is performed with a view toward when she purifies, and he acquires her immediately upon her purification. But when he does not know, even if she knows, there is no acquisition.
