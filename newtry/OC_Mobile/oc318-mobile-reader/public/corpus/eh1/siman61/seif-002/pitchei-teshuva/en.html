"Behold she is as his wife." — Whether chuppah is Torah or rabbinic. Behold she is as his wife. See the Shitta Mekubetzet (Ketubot folio 7b) regarding whether the institution of chuppah is of Torah origin or rabbinic. See also the Sha'ar HaMelech there in the treatise "Chuppat Chathanim," seif 5, and the Hagahot Ta'am HaMelech there regarding what was written on this matter.
