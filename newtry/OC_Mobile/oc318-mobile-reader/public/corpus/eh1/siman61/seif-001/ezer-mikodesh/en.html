Gloss: "The custom that the groom and bride fast." — When to break the fast after chuppah. Gloss — (the custom that the groom and bride fast). When the chuppah takes place several hours before mincha, the groom and bride may eat immediately afterward. For according to the reason that it is for clarity of mind at the time of kiddushin, it is obvious that there is no point in fasting after the chuppah. And according to the reason that it is like Yom Kippur for them, it is as if after Ne'ilah — particularly since we hold that a partial-day fast is also considered a form of fasting. Many have so practiced.
