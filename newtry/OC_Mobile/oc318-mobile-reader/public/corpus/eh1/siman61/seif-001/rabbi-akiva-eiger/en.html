"Even though she was not intimate." — Challenge to the Raanach's safek dina claim. Siman 61, seif 1 — "Even though she was not intimate." Note: The Raanach (Part 1, siman 67) wrote that this is a doubtful legal matter (safek dina), since for those decisors who hold that the "if you say" (im timtza lomar) formulation is not equivalent to a resolution, it is an unresolved question. I am puzzled: even though he cited there the view of the Responsa Maimoniyot in the name of the Ritzba that it is an unresolved question, this does not prove that all decisors who hold the "if you say" formulation is not a resolution would agree with this — for the first question was resolved from what Rav Yosef taught.
