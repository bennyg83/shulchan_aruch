"It is proper that she not marry until she purifies etc." — Whether to betroth after a niddah's chuppah. Seif 2 — "It is proper that she not marry until she purifies etc." Note: Regarding a niddah's chuppah, whether it is proper to betroth her afterward when she has immersed — see my work on Choshen Mishpat, fifth edition, siman 27, "Kanah Mekomo," Laws of Surety — see there for what I wrote in passing. There is no difficulty regarding the matter of Niznav [a place name], for Heaven forbid that one do so — see there, with Hashem's help, and examine carefully.
