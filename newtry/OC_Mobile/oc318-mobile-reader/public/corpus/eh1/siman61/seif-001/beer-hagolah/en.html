(א) Source for the Rambam's ruling. The Rambam's language in ch. 10 of the Laws of Marriage derives from the Mishnah in Ketubot (folio 41b) and as the Gemara there explains; and from what the Sages said — "chuppah completes kiddushin" (folio 5a–5b) — and as Rashi explained: these are questions that were resolved.
(ב) Source for "if you say chuppah acquires." "If you say chuppah acquires" — Ketubot folio 56a.
(ג) The question remained unresolved; Maggid Mishneh's ruling. The question there was left unresolved. The Maggid Mishneh wrote — and so it is written in the Halachot — therefore it does not acquire. And Rabbenu's view is that the same applies to all matters: a niddah's chuppah does not acquire — not for inheritance, not for becoming impure for her. So too holds a portion of the Geonim.
(ד) The Rosh's view: question was only about the additional ketubah. The Rosh in his rulings, as cited by the Tur, holds that the question was only regarding the additional ketubah amount. So too wrote the Ran. The Maggid Mishneh there also recorded the dissenting view.
