The betrother and betrothed woman must be seen by the witnesses; but if two saw them from a window and they see but are not seen by him or her — she does not need a get from him. {Rama: And even if the witnesses heard her say she accepts kiddushin — she may say I knew there is no kiddushin without witnesses and I intended to jest with him (Rivash siman 266). And specifically when she says she intended to jest; but if she contradicts that she accepted while witnesses testify she accepted — she is no longer believed to say she intended to jest and it is kiddushin (Maharam Padua siman 32).}
