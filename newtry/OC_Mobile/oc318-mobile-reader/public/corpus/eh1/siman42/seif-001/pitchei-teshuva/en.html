Against her will she is not betrothed. See Ba'er Heitev, who discusses the case where two say she was betrothed under coercion, etc. See Sha'ar HaMelech, chapter 7 of Hilchot Ishut, law 2, who brings Responsa Maharadb, siman 46, regarding a woman who was betrothed and two witnesses say she was betrothed willingly, while two say she was betrothed under coercive fear. He wrote that she is certainly betrothed, and kiddushin from another man do not take effect, because there is a double doubt to be stringent: perhaps the truth is like those who say she was betrothed willingly; and even if she was betrothed under coercion, perhaps this is considered ones or perhaps not, meaning as in Choshen Mishpat, siman 205, seif 7 in the gloss. Wherever there is a double doubt to be stringent, she is considered certainly betrothed. See there. He, of blessed memory, questioned this. See there.
