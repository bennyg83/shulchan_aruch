Law: one betroths only with the woman's consent and before two witnesses. It contains 5 seifim. A woman is betrothed only with her consent; and one who betroths a woman against her will — she is not betrothed. But a man whom they compelled until he betrothed against his will — behold she is betrothed. And some say she is not betrothed; therefore there is doubt. {Rama: If she said first "betroth me" and he threw kiddushin into her lap and said "behold you are betrothed to me" and she shook her garments within the time of an utterance to cast the kiddushin from her and says she did not intend from the start except to jest — nevertheless she is betrothed (responsum Maharam end Laws of Women). And we do not follow presumptions and proofs in matters of kiddushin that indicate she did not intend for kiddushin (there). One who took the woman's hand by force without her consent and betrothed her and she did not cast away the kiddushin — she is betrothed even though initially it was coercion, and he gave to her plainly without saying anything to her, since he spoke with her initially about kiddushin (Hagahot Mordechai end Gittin). He owed her money and she said give me my money, and as he began to give he said behold you are betrothed to me and she cast away the money from her hand — they are not kiddushin (Tur in name of responsum of Rosh).}
