He betrothed before witnesses even if he did not say "you are witnesses" — she is betrothed. {Rama: And even if he designated witnesses, others who saw the act may testify (Rivash siman 479). And the witnesses must see the actual giving into her hand or her domain; but if they did not see the actual giving into her hand even though they heard him say "be betrothed to me with such-and-such object" and afterward it left her possession — they are not kiddushin until they see the actual giving (responsum Rashba siman 480). And we do not follow presumptions and proofs in this. One who betrothed through a hole in the wall — such as one stretched her hand through a hole and another came and gave her kiddushin and the hole is narrow and they cannot see her at the time of kiddushin and she says she did not hear that he betrothed her — she is believed with migo she could say it was another's hand (Rashba 1009), provided witnesses do not contradict her. Any testimony not examined in beit din is not called testimony for kiddushin (so Mordechai implies at start of One Laws of Monetary). Therefore even if witnesses outside beit din said she was betrothed they may retract in beit din to say she was not betrothed and likewise the reverse (responsum Rashba brought in Beit Yosef). Witnesses of kiddushin do not require cross-examination unless in a matter that appears to involve deceit (Rashba 100, Ran). If they found written in a document "so-and-so betrothed so-and-so" with witnesses signed on it — as long as the document is not validated there is no concern for kiddushin (Hagahot Mordechai Kiddushin). And not like one who was stringent (Maharik root 74).}
