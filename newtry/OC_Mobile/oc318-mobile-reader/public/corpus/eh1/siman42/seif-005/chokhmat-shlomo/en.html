There, in the gloss, "one who betroths, etc., or other wicked persons, etc." Note: See what I wrote in my edition, section 201, whose place is acquired in the laws of divorce, section 130, in my responsum to the holy community of Liska. I concluded there that although for a transgression without lashes we require a wicked person of monetary violence, nevertheless in betrothal and divorce one may say that according to everyone we do not require monetary violence; see there for the reason. I also discussed that even according to Tosafot Sanhedrin 9, who hold that for bodily pleasure one does not require monetary violence, this applies only to sexual transgressions, but for other bodily pleasures they agree; see there, for the reason is sound, with God's help, and examine it.
