One who betroths without witnesses, even with one witness — they are not kiddushin; even if both admit; even if he betrothed before one witness and afterward betrothed before another witness not in each other's presence — she is not betrothed. {Rama: Some are stringent if he betrothed before one witness (Tur in name of Semag). If both admit — but if one contradicts the witness there is no concern (so Rashbetz explains Semag). In a case of agunah and distress one may rely on the lenient authorities. And there is no distinction whether the witness says she was betrothed before him alone or says she was betrothed before two and he alone saw while the others say they did not see (Beit Yosef and so appears in responsum of Rashba). And likewise if he betrothed before two and one of them is a relative — it is as betrothing before one witness (and see above siman 47 seif 3).}
