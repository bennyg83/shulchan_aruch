Some say that if he did not press, etc. So wrote the Tur in the name of the Rif and Rambam, who hold that this is how the mitzvah is done, that he must press his foot down. Therefore it is not relevant here to say that since he is fit to press, it is valid, because he has still not performed the mitzvah, and it is as though he did not perform chalitzah at all. It is invalid even though he is fit for chalitzah. So wrote Ri Weil, and so in Shiltei Gibborim there he invalidates if he did not press his foot to the ground.
