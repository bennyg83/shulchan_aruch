(א) He should stand, etc. So that he can press well.
(ב) And she should untie, etc. There, 102a: whether she untied, etc.
(ג) Everything, etc. “And she should grasp and remove,” according to the above Yerushalmi and the reading of all the decisors: and she drags it with the right hand.
(ד) And all this without assistance, etc. From what is written in the Yerushalmi there, cited by Tosafot there: he ties it with a bow so that she can untie it with one of her hands. This is unlike Rabbenu Tam, who wrote that she assists with the left, according to the reading of Tosafot, “and she drags it with the left,” and he explained that she assists. See the end of chapter 7 of Berachot.
(ה) And in all this, etc. From the above baraita, “whether standing,” etc., it is bedieved, unlike the Rambam.
(ו) And she throws, etc. See what I wrote in Piskei Seder Chalitzah, seif 74.
(ז) If she removed, etc. From seif 31, and as written in the Gemara: Is it written “and she shall remove,” etc.?
