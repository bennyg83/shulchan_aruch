And some say it speaks where his foot was amputated. See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger of blessed memory, siman 98, where his son-in-law, the Gaon Rabbi Moshe Sofer of blessed memory, wrote that if it was cut off until the ankle and the ankle remains, there is room to discuss that this is a double doubt: perhaps an amputated foot is valid, and perhaps the ankle is considered the foot like Rav Pappa, Yevamot 103a. In pressing circumstances, one may rely on this. He, of blessed memory, answered that he too agrees to this. Although there is room to discuss that it is not a double doubt, because even according to Rav Pappa one can say it is not chalitzah according to what the Ramban wrote in Milchamot, etc., nevertheless, since many decisors, etc., one may rely on this in pressing circumstances.
