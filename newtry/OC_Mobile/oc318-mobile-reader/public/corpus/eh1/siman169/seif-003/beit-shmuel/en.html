Even if his father was a convert. Generally we hold that one whose conception and birth were in sanctity is a full Israelite. Nevertheless, here regarding chalitzah, the judges before whom she performs chalitzah must have fathers from Israel. It is not effective for the judges that their conception and birth were in sanctity, because we learn from the verse "in Israel" that the Torah was particular that their fathers be from Israel, and their conception and birth in sanctity is not effective for this. See Tosafot, Perek Mitzvat Chalitzah, page 101, and Ketubot, page 44.
