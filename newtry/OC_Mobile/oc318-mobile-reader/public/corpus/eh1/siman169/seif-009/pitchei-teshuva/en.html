See Responsa Noda BiYehudah Tinyana, end of siman 114, who wrote that one can say this is specifically with a married woman, because it is considered the beginning of a legal proceeding, since through the chalitzah she begins to claim the ketubah, and this reason is explained in Hagahot Mordechai, Perek Mitzvat Chalitzah. But with a betrothed woman who performed chalitzah at night, there is room to discuss leniency, except that he does not decide this absolutely. See there. See Yeshuot Yaakov, who wrote plainly that the rule that chalitzah at night is invalid applies even to a shomeret yavam from betrothal, because Chazal did not distinguish in their enactment.
