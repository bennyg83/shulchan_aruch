Large breasts. The Darkei Moshe wrote in the name of the Mordechai, who wrote in the name of Sefer HaTerumah, that this examination must be done by men. The examination whether she has many hairs is done by women. So too the Bach wrote in the name of the Semak: examination of breasts must be done by men, unless she has already been established as an adult by years, in which case women examine, because the presumption assists, since presumably after twelve years and one day she brought signs. If so, examination for hairs is even where she was not established by years. The Darkei Moshe asked: if so, why is women’s examination effective? He answered that since it is a matter that will be revealed, they do not lie. But women are not believed regarding two hairs where she was not established by years, and we do not say it is a matter that will be revealed, because two hairs are not so much a matter that will be revealed. For this reason Sefer HaTerumah wrote that we do not allow mi'un nowadays, because we are not expert to examine the whole body to see whether she has hairs. However, this is difficult, because breasts are also a matter that will be revealed. One cannot say that breasts are no better than two hairs, for breasts are effective because an upper sign cannot come without a lower sign. Therefore, when she was not established by years, women do not examine. If so, why is the examination of men effective? One must say that the Darkei Moshe answered this only according to the view of the Maggid, who truly holds that examination of breasts is done by women. But according to Sefer HaTerumah, who holds that it is not effective through women, it requires study why many hairs are effective through women.
