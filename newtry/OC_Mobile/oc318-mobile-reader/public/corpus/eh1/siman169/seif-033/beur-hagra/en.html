And some say, etc. As written in the Yerushalmi: R. Zeira showed R. Ba bar R. Yitzchak: How does he do it? He ties it so that he can walk in it by himself. This shows that it applies specifically to their sandals, in which they could not walk without tying. About this they said there: How does he do it, etc. They also said there: the essence of chalitzah is loosening the strap, because it becomes untied and falls by itself. But with ours, this is not necessary, for tying is not mentioned in the Torah.
