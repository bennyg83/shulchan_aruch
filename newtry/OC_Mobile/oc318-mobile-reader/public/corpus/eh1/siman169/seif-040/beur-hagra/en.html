(א) A yevamah, etc. As stated there: Is it written “and she shall spit,” etc.? This is unlike the case of the father of Shmuel there. The Ra'avad wrote that the case of the father of Shmuel concerns where it was not before a beit din, to disqualify her from the brothers, as written above in seif 45.
(ב) And some say, etc. Like the case of the father of Shmuel. He holds that the scholars did not dispute this, and like Rashi’s explanation there, s.v. mi, etc. Even if you say they disagree, in a Torah matter we follow the stringent view. But according to Rashi’s explanation that they do not disagree, it is not even invalid chalitzah. See above, seif 48. So too in the Yerushalmi, halacha 6, in the above case of Levi, it says there: if she spat blood, if it has moisture of saliva, it is valid. So too they said there in the name of many Amoraim, that it is specifically where there is moisture of saliva, and there is no distinction. Although the case of Levi there is in a different manner, where he did not ask the academy, see there, nevertheless in law they do not disagree.
