She must spit. In one version in the Talmud it says: he did nothing at all, and so wrote the Rambam. This implies that in this kind of case it does not disqualify at all. All the more so according to Shiltei Gibborim, where she spit because she ate garlic or other things that increase saliva, these too are considered "he did nothing at all" and do not disqualify. But according to what Ri Weil wrote, that if they dispersed and left it is valid, it appears that it does disqualify, even if they did not leave.
