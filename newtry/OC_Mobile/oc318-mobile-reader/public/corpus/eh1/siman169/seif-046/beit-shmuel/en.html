Therefore, specifically if she spit before beit din, she becomes disqualified from yibbum. If both intended, one can say she is disqualified according to the Rosh, and according to the other decisors even if she alone intended, one can say she is disqualified. So wrote the Darkei Moshe in Hagahot Perishah.
