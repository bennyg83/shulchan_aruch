Two in addition to the three. See Ba'er Heitev, who wrote that it is to publicize the matter. In the explanation of Seder Chalitzah, seif 9, he wrote Rashi’s explanation: to publicize that she is a chalutzah and may not marry a kohen; alternatively, so that people will come and seek to marry her. End quote. The Beit Shmuel here, s.k. 4, wrote that the practical difference between the two explanations is if she was already divorced: according to the first explanation, five are not needed, but according to the second, they are needed. See there. See Yeshuot Yaakov, s.k. 7, who brought a proof that the main explanation is like the second. See there. See Sefer Beit Meir, who wrote regarding the above words of the Beit Shmuel: if so, even according to the second explanation, if she is divorced and says, "I do not want this enactment of Chazal," since it was enacted for her benefit, it would be proper to listen to her and five would not be needed. But in my humble opinion we do not have the power to change the explained laws based on reasons stated to explain the matter, for perhaps there is another reason hidden from us, etc. See there.
