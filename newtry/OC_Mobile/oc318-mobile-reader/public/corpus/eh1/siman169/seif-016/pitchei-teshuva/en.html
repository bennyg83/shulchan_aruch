Many hairs. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, end of siman 93, who wrote that if many depressions were found without any hairs at all, it is not effective. They said above, siman 155, seif 16, that depressions are effective, and we say there is a presumption that hairs were there and fell out, only where it was clarified that he reached the required number of years, so that we have Rava’s presumption that he brought signs. But this presumption alone is not enough for chalitzah. When there are also depressions, and we have the presumption that there are no depressions without hairs, by combining these two presumptions we establish him as an adult. But where the number of his years is unknown, and Rava’s presumption is gone, we do not rely only on the presumption that there are no depressions without hairs in order to say that he is thirteen years old. End quote.
