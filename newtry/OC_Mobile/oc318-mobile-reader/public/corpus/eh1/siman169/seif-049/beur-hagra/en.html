(א) A minor, etc. As above, according to the reading “invalid,” and as written at the beginning of chapter 3 of Gittin.
(ב) And when she grows up, etc. Because the Yerushalmi’s reading is: if she did not perform chalitzah again, her chalitzah is valid. In the Tosefta: a minor who performed chalitzah should perform chalitzah again after she grows up; if she did not perform chalitzah again, her chalitzah is invalid, according to R. Elazar. But the Sages said: if she did not perform chalitzah again, her chalitzah is valid. According to this, what is written in the Gemara, “and the halacha,” etc., means lechatchilah, according to the Rashba. Even according to our reading, Tosafot 105b, s.v. ketanah, wrote that this is why it does not mix it in and teach the extra phrase “remove” and “after she grows up,” to teach that it is nothing, as with a minor. According to all the above, she does not need to repeat it; only the Rambam wrote that it is invalid chalitzah, and he is a lone view. Terumat HaDeshen, see there.
