(א) And he should wrap, etc. For this is why a cord is made, even though there are loops.
(ב) And he should tie, etc. See what I wrote. But in law, the main ruling follows the Yerushalmi: one knot and a bow over it. Although regarding Shabbat this is not considered tying, nevertheless for chalitzah we require only that it be tightened, as the Rosh wrote, and as written above in seif 33 in the gloss. A bow is tightened, as written in Shabbat 111b: Rav Achdevoy asked, etc. It is better than two knots that are not tightened, as written in Shabbat there: another version says, his reason, etc. This is what the above Yerushalmi says: he ties it with a bow so that, etc. There is no difficulty from a knot that is not tight. Even R. Zeira, who said he ties it, does not disagree with R. Chanina; rather, he means that he ties it with a bow. So is the version in the Yerushalmi: R. Zeira showed R. Ba bar Yitzchak: How does he do it so that he can walk in it by himself? How does he do it? R. Chanina said: He ties it with a bow so that, etc. Meaning, how does he make it tight enough that he can walk in it by himself, and also untie it with one hand? This cannot be done through knots. Even according to the version written by Tosafot, one can likewise say that it explains the words of R. Zeira. Even if you say they disagree, nevertheless the Yerushalmi concludes like R. Chanina, and so wrote the Mordechai, that R. Chanina’s ruling is primary.
(ג) Two, etc. For there is no complete knot with fewer than these, from chapter 4 of Shabbat, where it says that he tied them.
(ד) And he should not tighten, etc. “And a bow,” etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. af al gav, in the name of the Yerushalmi. He wrote, “however,” etc. So too the Rosh wrote, for we hold that a bow is not a knot. But Mordechai wrote: It appears that R. Chanina is primary; therefore he should not tighten the second knot. Sefer HaTerumah wrote that to satisfy Heaven, one should follow the first Tanna and R. Chanina. This is what he wrote: “and a bow,” etc. The Beit Yosef already asked: the bow is so that she can untie it, etc. One must say that he too holds that the second knot is not tightened, only that it cannot remain in place, and through the bow it remains. See Piskei Seder Chalitzah, siman 61.
(ה) And it is necessary, etc. As above, because of “above” and “above.”
(ו) And he should wrap, etc. As above, that it should be from Yehudah.
(ז) Around the shin. For there it is on the exposed shin, and this is what the Yerushalmi means: “between the Mishnahs,” that he ties from the knee and downward.
