And there is one who says. This applies where she spit saliva. At first glance this is astonishing, because it implies that the decisors disagree with this, but that is not so. Here the discussion is whether she is disqualified to the brothers. It is obvious according to the decisors, namely the "some say" in seif 40, who hold that even in the place of chalitzah, if she did not suck, it is not effective, that she certainly is not forbidden to the brothers when she spit blood and did not suck, even according to the first view there. It is explained according to the Rosh’s approach that the father of Shmuel speaks specifically regarding disqualification to the brothers; there saliva mixed in is required. This is unlike the place of chalitzah, where even if she spit blood she does not need to spit another time. If so, in this law there is no dispute at all.
