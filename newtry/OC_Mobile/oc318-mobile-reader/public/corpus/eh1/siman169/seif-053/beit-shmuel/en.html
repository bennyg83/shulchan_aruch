Although we hold that if one gives a writing to another, he cannot exempt himself with a claim that it was in jest, as written in Terumat HaDeshen, siman 81, here it is different, since we know the matter: this one is obligated to perform chalitzah for free. From where would it arise to say that he obligated himself for free? This is unlike there, where we say presumably it is true that he owes him, since he gave him his own writing regarding it.
