And if the yevamah has children. See siman 155, where I brought the sugya in the first chapter of Yevamot. According to Rav Safra, children are like signs, and even if she gave birth while still a minor, they are considered signs after twelve years and one day. This Hagahot Maimoniyot holds this way, unlike Responsa Mabit, siman 6. See there. The Rambam rules like Rav Safra, and according to this he ruled well here. However, from the Rif and the Rosh it implies that they hold like Rav Zevid, that there are no children without signs, meaning that children are not found without signs. According to this, it is not effective regarding chalitzah. It is astonishing that the Rav Rama rules plainly like Hagahot Maimoniyot. I already wrote in siman 155 that according to the Rambam, children are effective specifically after twelve years and one day.
