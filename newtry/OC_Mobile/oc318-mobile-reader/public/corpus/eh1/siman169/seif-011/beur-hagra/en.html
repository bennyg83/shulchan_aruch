(א) Only by, etc. Yevamot 39b: they all dispute this, but here everyone agrees.
(ב) And some say, etc. From what is written in Kiddushin chapter 1, “we administer lashes,” etc., “we administer stoning,” etc., even though the father is not believed for lashes and punishments, as stated there. This shows it is like testimony.
(ג) Or if a report emerged, etc. As stated there: “we administer lashes,” etc., “she was established as a niddah,” etc. He explains how she was established as a niddah: a report of niddah emerged about her among the neighbors. All this is from the words of Rabbenu Tam. But Rashi explained in Ketubot 72 that they saw her wearing, etc. So too above, siman 115, seif 2, and in Yoreh Deah, siman 185, seif 2. R. Yoel HaLevi already challenged him, as written in Mordechai, chapter Mi SheAchazo.
(ד) Or they examined her. The text should read: if they examined her. Examination works through any person, as written above, siman 155.
(ה) But not, etc. For perhaps they acted based on the father, since the father is believed regarding consecrations, etc., as stated there in Mordechai. Aside from this, we say that a minor counts toward the seven aliyot, except because of the sheliach tzibbur, as written in chapter 3 of Megillah, “the one who reads the haftarah from the prophets,” etc. Even according to what is written in Orach Chaim, siman 55, seif 5 in the gloss, and as written in the Yerushalmi, that we are not exacting regarding a minor, nevertheless two are required, as written there.
(ו) Or that they are long, etc. “And on this they rely,” etc. Sefer HaTerumah and Mordechai there.
(ז) And if there is, etc. According to the explanation of the Rif, Rashi, and the other decisors there, even before twelve years, unlike the Rambam and Rav Safra. But Rav Zevid was uncertain, and as Tosafot wrote there, s.v. Rav, “and in the hand,” etc., and answers, “we are concerned,” etc. So too the Rosh wrote there. But the Ramban and Rashba wrote that one can also explain according to Rav Zevid that she certainly has signs, and what the Gemara asks, “let us examine,” means: why rely on the chazakah in a place where examination is possible? See there.
