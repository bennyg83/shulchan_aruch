With a cloth sock, her chalitzah is invalid. See Perek Kol HaGet, where Rabbi Eliezer and Shmuel disagree. According to Shmuel, wherever it teaches that chalitzah is invalid, she is forbidden to the brothers. According to Rabbi Eliezer, with socks and a minor it is not chalitzah at all and she is permitted to the brothers. Although with spitting alone she is disqualified to the brothers, nevertheless with spitting and removing a sock it is worse, as written in the Maggid. But with socks, the chalitzah is void. The Rambam rules like Rabbi Eliezer. The author here omitted the view of the Rambam. In seif 3 regarding a minor he ruled that it is not chalitzah at all and she is not disqualified to the brothers, because according to the version of Tosafot in Perek Kol HaGet it is stated in Perek Mitzvat Chalitzah, page 105, that Shmuel said: the chalitzah of a minor according to the Rabbis is not chalitzah at all. Learn from this that there is a distinction between socks and a minor, because regarding socks Shmuel did not say that Rabbi Meir and the Rabbis disagree whether if invalid it is entirely void. This is the view of the author. So ruled the Rashba like Shmuel, and so it also implies in the responsum of the Rivash brought in Kesef Mishneh, chapter 4 of Hilchot Yibbum. With this the astonishment of the Bach is resolved. The Rambam rules like Rabbi Eliezer regarding both of these, that they do not disqualify at all. But the Maggid is difficult: why did he exert himself to find a reason for the Rambam’s words, and not write the reason that he rules like Rabbi Eliezer?
