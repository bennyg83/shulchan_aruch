A shoe of avodah zarah. See Tosafot there. If it is avodah zarah of a Jew, or of a non-Jew that was abandoned and came into the hand of a Jew, it is like avodah zarah of a Jew, which has no nullification and its measure is considered pulverized. Rather, it speaks of avodah zarah of a non-Jew who abandoned it, and his intent is not to acquire the shoe, or of avodah zarah of a non-Jew who did not abandon it, so the Jew did not acquire it. Bach. According to this, it is forbidden to perform chalitzah with it because the shoe is not his. According to Rabbenu Tam, it speaks of avodah zarah of a non-Jew that he nullified.
