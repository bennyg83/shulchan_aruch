(א) They should make in it, etc. According to the explanation of the Tur and R. Yerucham of what is stated there, “loops,” and according to the view of most decisors, both loops and a cord are required. Although the Rambam and Rashba hold that straps alone are enough, but loops alone are not; and the Rif and Ra'avyah hold that straps alone are enough. What he wrote, “ours,” means that it has no straps. The Rosh wrote that ours needs nothing, because ours is tight without this, as written in seif 53 in the gloss. See there. Nevertheless, one should do like the above great authorities.
(ב) And some say, etc. According to the reading “loops” in the plural; and “many” means three, as written at the end of chapter 4 of Gittin, or at least two.
(ג) And the knots should not be, etc. As written in Shabbat 112a regarding loops that are tied, etc.
