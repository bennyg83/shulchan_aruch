They should make on it a kind of knot called a chumarta on one side, so that it enters the hole on the other side and fastens well. Some say that they should make three knots in this manner, and at least two. Gloss: The custom is to make three, and the knots should not be from the shoe itself, but from another strap attached to the top of the shoe; likewise he should make the hooks on the other side. The custom is to sew these loops and hooks on the outside, not on the inside.
