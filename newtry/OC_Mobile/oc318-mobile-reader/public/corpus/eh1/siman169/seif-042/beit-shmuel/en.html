And saliva was flowing. So wrote the Rambam, and it implies specifically when it flows, but if it did not flow, even if she did not suck, it is effective. It does not imply this from the other decisors. Rather, since the saliva came because of another matter, it is not spitting even if it did not flow. However, when she sucks, one can say that it is effective according to all opinions. Shiltei Gibborim wrote that if she spit because of another matter, it is not considered spitting at all and she is not disqualified to the brothers.
