See Responsa Noda BiYehudah Tinyana, end of siman 155, who wrote to one Rav who asked whether he takes payment for chalitzah: Heaven forbid, it never happened. Even the two who join me, I do not give them permission to take even one perutah. But regarding the two additional ones, I do not protest. Sometimes it happens that those additional ones afterward waive something to the first two, and I turn a blind eye to them, but I do not take any perutah, not at the beginning and not at the end. End quote. Also in Sefer Yeshuot Yaakov, s.k. 2, he protested against those who relied on permitting taking payment from chalitzot, because since there is concern that the chalitzah may be entirely void, it is proper to fence the matter. He wrote that many practice that the two additional ones receive payment and afterward give from it to the three judges. Although even this is not proper, nevertheless regarding this it is appropriate to say, "better that they be inadvertent," but for the judges themselves to receive the payment, Heaven forbid to be lenient.
