To fix a place. See the explanation, seif 8, and see Bach. One does not fix the place on Shabbat, since a fixed appointment is required and it is like preparing from Shabbat for a weekday. See Darkei Moshe. The fixing of a place is for publicizing the matter; therefore it must be in a high place in the city. If there is publicity without this, see Responsa Ri Beit HaLevi, siman 10.
