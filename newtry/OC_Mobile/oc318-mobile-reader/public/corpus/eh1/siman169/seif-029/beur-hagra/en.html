(א) And there is one who says, etc. According to the Rif’s reading: Rava admits regarding “lo avah yevami,” and it is the conclusion of the matter, like R. Zeira, who said: anything fit, etc. Nimukei Yosef wrote: If she is fit to read it at once, it does not prevent validity; if not, it prevents validity. According to his words, the same applies to “lo chafatzti.” The reason it mentions “lo avah” is because ordinary men are fit for karet. See Piskei Seder Chalitzah, end of seif 6.
(ב) And they should read, etc. As above, seif 12.
(ג) And some say, etc. From what is written, “rather, lo avah yevami,” etc., and likewise regarding “lo chafatzti lekachtah.” But all the decisors wrote only “lo avah” and “lo chafatzti,” as written: “lo” by itself.
(ד) Lo avah yevami. Not precisely; rather, only two words, as written in Piskei Seder Chalitzah, seif 9, 96. The same applies to “lo chafatzti.”
(ה) Also lo chafatzti. According to the view of the Semak, that lechatchilah Rava agrees. See Piskei Seder Chalitzah there and what is written there.
