He does not perform chalitzah. The Beit Meir wrote: anyone who sees clearly will understand that there is no other meaning except that he does not perform chalitzah, but he does perform yibbum; and we do not say "whoever is not included in chalitzah," etc., unlike Responsa Shev Yaakov, etc. He discusses this at length and concludes that even nowadays, and even if he has a wife, he is obligated to perform yibbum in such a case.
