That she will give you two hundred zuz. See Yeshuot Yaakov, s.k. 27, who analyzes this matter and wishes to distinguish that this applies specifically where others said this to him. But if the woman herself promised him such an amount and said to him, "Perform chalitzah for me on condition that I give you two hundred zuz," the condition is effective, and if she did not fulfill her words, the chalitzah is void.
