She does not need to spit another time. So wrote the Ra'avad and the Rosh: in the place of chalitzah she does not need to spit another time. What the Talmud says, that specifically sucking is effective, means if she spit outside the place of chalitzah, regarding making her disqualified to the brothers, as written in seif 45. And some say specifically when she sucked. So wrote the Rambam. If she spit without sucking and without it flowing, it is not effective. So wrote the Rashba.
