On the day before. See Ba'er Heitev. See Yeshuot Yaakov, s.k. 11, who brought in the name of the Taz that if at the time of fixing the place they did not know on which day the chalitzah would be, and they fixed a place to perform chalitzah on this day or that day, it is not effective because there is no retroactive clarification. He, of blessed memory, disagrees: what connection does this have to retroactive clarification? The fixing of the place is for chalitzah, and what issue is there in saying that it was not clarified at the time of fixing on which day the chalitzah would be? What Mahari Bruna wrote, that if the day of chalitzah was canceled, the fixing was canceled, is because they fixed it for chalitzah on a specific day, and when that day was canceled, perhaps he will no longer perform chalitzah for her. But if from the beginning it was uncertain on which day he would perform chalitzah, why should the first fixing not be effective? See there.
