(א) Left-handed, etc. The Semag and Semak wrote that he should perform chalitzah with the right foot, for it is not comparable to tefillin, where the reason is the weaker hand. The proof is from the ear, where certainly the reason of greater strength in the right over the left does not apply; rather, it is a scriptural decree in the mitzvah of chalitzah.
(ב) Some say, etc. From what is written in Chullin 92b: R. Yirmeyah asked, etc. They asked: what was his question? Do we not say at the end of chapter 3 of Menachot that we follow his own body? They answered that tefillin are different, for the Torah did not write right or left, only the weaker hand, meaning whichever is weaker. But where the Torah was particular about right or left, or another matter, we are uncertain whether we follow his own body or the standard right.
(ג) And there is, etc. From the baraita in Bechorot 45b, that a left-handed person is disqualified, and he explains that this is because he has no right side at all. But the first view explains there that it is because of weakness, and it is a blemish, and specifically one who controls both hands, etc., for the baraita began with the left, as written there.
