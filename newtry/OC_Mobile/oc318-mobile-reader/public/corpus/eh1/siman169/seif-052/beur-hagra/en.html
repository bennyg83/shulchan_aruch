They said to him, etc. The Yerushalmi there says: A case came before Rabbi Chiyya bar Abba, and he said to him: My son, this woman does not wish to marry you through yibbum. Rather, perform chalitzah for her, remove your bond from her, and then she will marry you through regular marriage. After he performed chalitzah for her, he said to him: Even if Moshe and Shmuel would come, they would not permit her to you.
