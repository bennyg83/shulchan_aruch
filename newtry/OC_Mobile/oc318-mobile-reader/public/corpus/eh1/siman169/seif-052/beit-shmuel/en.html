But if there is a claim, etc. The Darkei Moshe wrote in the name of Or Zarua that this is specifically in a place where yibbum takes precedence and she misled him to perform chalitzah, in which case she must give his payment. But in a place where chalitzah takes precedence and he does not want to perform it, he does not even receive payment for his trouble. According to this, if he has a wife, then according to everyone the mitzvah of chalitzah takes precedence, and he does not receive even payment for his trouble, as written in siman 165. At first glance it appears that according to the dispute of the decisors whether the mitzvah of yibbum takes precedence or the mitzvah of chalitzah, as written there, because of the doubt the law is to perform chalitzah. If so, he should never receive anything, even his payment, because he always must perform chalitzah. But the words of the Tur and the author do not imply this, for they brought this dispute in siman 165 and nevertheless write here that it is specifically when she has a claim that he must perform chalitzah. This implies that because of the legal ruling that he must perform chalitzah, he can say "I hold like those decisors who say the mitzvah of yibbum takes precedence," and she must give his payment.
