And on this we rely not to examine, etc. See siman 155, where the Tur wrote in the name of Ba'al HaTerumot that we are not expert in examining depressions. According to this, at first glance it is difficult: every examination for hairs, even by men, should not be effective, for we hold that hairs without a depression are not effective, and we are not expert. So the Bach also asked. See there what he wrote. According to him, this is not settled as a halachic ruling: from where do we know to be lenient against the view of Ba'al HaTerumot? The Tur also brought his view plainly without any dispute. But at first glance it appears to me that this is not difficult. Certainly, regarding examination for depressions where there are no hairs on the entire body, we are not expert, and for this reason he was stringent there that she may not perform mi'un. But to examine whether there is a depression under the hairs, it is obvious that we are expert in this examination. So it also implies from the language of the author, who wrote: "on this we rely not to examine," implying that we are able to examine. Later I saw that the Taz in siman 155 also wrote this.
