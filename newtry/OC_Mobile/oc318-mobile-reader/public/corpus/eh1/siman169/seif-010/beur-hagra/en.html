(ב) Thirteen years old, etc. The Mishnah in Niddah 45b. See Tosafot there, s.v. eilu, etc.
(ג) And the yevamah is, etc. 106b: “and the halacha,” etc.
(ד) And that they brought, etc. As stated there: What is the case? If they did not bring, etc.
(ה) After, etc. There, 46a and 48b.
(ו) And if there is, etc. Tosafot 49a, end of s.v. ve'ibayit eima kerabbanan, from the beginning of chapter Ba Siman, that the upper sign cannot come without the lower one. Regarding breasts one cannot say it is a mole, for they do not come before their time.
(ז) And likewise, etc. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 35, seif 1.
