There is one who says that her chalitzah is valid. The Maggid wrote that the Rambam, who wrote "perform chalitzah for her, for this is a mitzvah," etc., as written in the previous seif, disagrees with this. The author holds that this case is stronger, because we tell him explicitly that he should uproot the zikah. This is unlike there, where he thinks that through this he can marry her. Also, what they said to him, "and if you want to perform yibbum, perform yibbum," leaves the zikah in place.
