(א) It is found, etc. The above Mishnah.
(ב) Or a convert. 101b: “And I am a convert,” which implies even one of them.
(ג) And some say, etc. Even regarding lineage, according to Ri, who disqualifies when his mother is from Israel, he validates when his father is a gentile. He wrote there: this requires study, for what proof is there from lineage? Here is different, for the Torah excluded him with “in Israel,” requiring him to be entirely from Israel. But according to R. Eliezer ben Yaakov, regarding lineage even if his mother is from Israel he is valid; and according to R. Yose, even his mother is not needed at all, and we hold like him in Kiddushin there. So wrote Nimukei Yosef, and so wrote in the name of She'iltot; see there.
(ד) However, etc. The Mishnah there says her chalitzah is invalid. We say at the beginning of chapter 3 of Gittin, 24b, “wherever,” etc. Even R. Elazar disagrees only regarding a minor and an anpilya, as the Rivash wrote in siman 482, and as the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 55.
(ה) Even, etc. See Ba'er HaGolah, and as written on 102b.
(ו) Some say, etc. “That the one who takes,” etc. This is the Mishnah in Bechorot, chapter 9.
(ז) For we require, etc. Mishnah 101a, and the Gemara there: “What is the reasoning of the first Tanna,” etc. And there, 101b: “among Israelites,” etc.; and 102a and 104a.
(ח) And see, etc. Ketubot 105a.
