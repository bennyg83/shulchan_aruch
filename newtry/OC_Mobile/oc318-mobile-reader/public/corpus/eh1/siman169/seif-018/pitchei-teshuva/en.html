Large in stature. See Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 2 of Hilchot Ishut, law 22, who wrote that specifically when the desired result is that he be an adult, then when we see that they are large in stature we say that they are also adults in years. But when the desired result is that she be a minor, even if they are small in stature, that is no proof that she has not reached her years, for perhaps she is a dwarf. This is unlike Responsa Pnei Moshe, part 1, siman 18. See there. See Magen Avraham, siman 571, end of s.k. 3, which implies like the words of the Pnei Moshe.
