A blind person should not, ab initio, perform chalitzah. But if he did perform chalitzah, his chalitzah was kosher. There is an opinion that if there isn't another brother [who could potentially perform the chalitzah], then the blind brother may perform the chalitzah ab initio, [Remah:] such that the widow not fall into the position of an agunah. Also note that one is not called "blind" unless [blind in] both eyes.
