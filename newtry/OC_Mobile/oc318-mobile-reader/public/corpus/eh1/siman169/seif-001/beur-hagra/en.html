(א) Israelites, etc. 101b: “among Israelites, in a beit din of Israel,” etc.
(ב) And not all of them. “And not all of them,” from the Mishnah 104b. We hold like the first Tanna, and the same applies between one another, as written in the Tosefta and Yerushalmi, Sanhedrin chapter 3, cited by the Rif and Rosh there.
(ג) And even, etc. As written in Choshen Mishpat, siman 33, seif 4 in the gloss, “however,” etc. The Mordechai wrote that this is because of a mistaken beit din, since it is not stated explicitly in the Gemara as permitted. With one husband, they are disqualified.
(ד) But two, etc. The Gemara there, and Bava Batra 128a.
(ה) And if she performed chalitzah before, etc. From what is stated in the Mishnah and baraita, “the mitzvah of chalitzah,” etc., this implies that it is specifically for the mitzvah. So too there, 106b: Rabbi Yehudah said, “the mitzvah of chalitzah,” etc.; this teaches us that the mitzvah is so, etc.
