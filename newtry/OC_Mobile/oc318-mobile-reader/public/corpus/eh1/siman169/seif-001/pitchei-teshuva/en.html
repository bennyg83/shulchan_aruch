The mitzvah of chalitzah. See Ba'er Heitev. See Sefer Zachor LeAvraham, Even HaEzer, letter chet, who wrote in the name of the Gaon Chida of blessed memory that it is permitted to perform two chalitzot on one day, and there is no concern of "we do not make mitzvot into bundles." After the first one is completed and the chalutzah and yavam have left, they should wait a little and then perform the second chalitzah. See there.
