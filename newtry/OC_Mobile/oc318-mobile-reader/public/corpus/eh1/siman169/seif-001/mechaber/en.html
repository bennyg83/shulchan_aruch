The mitzvah of chalitzah (release from yibbum) is to be performed in front of three Jewish judges, who are not related to each other or to the levir (yavam) or to the woman subject to yibbum (yevamah). And even two married to the daughters of two brothers shouldn't be on the same court, but a second generation with a third generation is valid (Terumat Hadeshen, siman 226). And even if they are ordinary judges, as long as they know how to read out to the yavam and yevamah. And if she performed chalitzah in front of ignoramuses who don't know how to read out, it is valid.
