And she does not need to return to all the brothers. The reason is explained in Terumat HaDeshen: since according to our version, her chalitzah is nothing and does not disqualify, it is not an invalid chalitzah. According to the Rif’s version, the chalitzah of a minor girl is valid even lechatchilah, only it is good to perform chalitzah again when she grows up. Therefore she does not need to return to all the brothers, since by law it is valid chalitzah. Only according to the Rambam is it invalid chalitzah. In rabbinic matters we follow the decisors.
