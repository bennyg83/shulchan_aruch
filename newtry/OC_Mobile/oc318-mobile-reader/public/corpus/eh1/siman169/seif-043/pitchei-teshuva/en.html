She deposited the money with a third party for him. In the glosses of the Shulchan Aruch of the Gaon Rabbi Akiva Eiger, of blessed memory, there is a note, and this is its language: In Responsa Rashba, "she deposited with a third party" is not mentioned, except in the Mordechai, chapter 4 of Yevamot. See there, where he distinguishes between where she gave it to a third party and said to give it to him after he performs chalitzah, in the presence of all three, in which case he has already acquired it, and where she simply gave it to a third party and the third party said he would give it to him, in which case he has not yet acquired it and she can say, "I was only jesting." See there. See Responsa Rama, siman 24, who wrote plainly that even where she deposited it with a third party, she can say, "I was only jesting," because the yavam has not yet acquired it. Rather, she trusted the third party, but not that this person should acquire it. End quote. See Choshen Mishpat, end of siman 264.
