(א) And a minor, etc. According to our reading: a minor, etc., is invalid.
(ב) And she is not like a shoteh or a minor, etc. “And some say that the same applies,” etc. All of this is from the words of the Rambam. A minor is explicit there, 105b, and at the beginning of chapter 3 of Gittin, and in the Tosefta, chapter 1 of Yevamot: a deaf man from whom chalitzah was done, a deaf woman who performed chalitzah, one who performs chalitzah from a shoteh, and likewise a shoteh woman who performed chalitzah, and those who perform chalitzah from a minor, must leave. There are thirteen matters in this according to R. Meir, who said it in the name of R. Akiva. The Sages say: there is no mamzer from a yevamah, as written in the Gemara 32a. Since R. Akiva holds that there is a mamzer, this shows that her chalitzah is nothing. Also, with invalid chalitzah, she does not leave, as written in seif 55. Therefore he mentioned these.
(ג) And some say that their chalitzah, etc. Since the Mishnah says “invalid,” and in the Gemara we say the reason is because he is not included in “saying,” and they said the same regarding a mute. So too we say there, 110a. See Maggid Mishneh there. What is written there, 105b, “a deaf man from whom chalitzah was done,” etc.: Reish Lakish said in the name of R. Yehudah, this is the view of R. Meir, but the Sages say there is no, etc. This shows that “invalid” in the Mishnah is precise. The words of the Rambam are very astonishing.
