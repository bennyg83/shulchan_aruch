The yavam and the yevamah. See Ba'er Heitev in the name of Maharit and the responsum of Chacham Tzvi. What he challenged against the Chacham Tzvi from the words of Tosafot, etc., was already preceded by She'eilat Ya'avetz, part 1, siman 27, who raised this difficulty against the words of his father, the Chacham Tzvi, of blessed memory. See there, and what he wrote on this. For practical halacha he ruled there that where they transgressed and performed chalitzah while they were onenim, and the matter cannot be redone, nothing further is required. See there. See Responsa Brit Avraham, end of siman 54, who wrote that this matter may be connected to Responsa Rashal, siman 70, and Responsa Noda BiYehudah, Orach Chaim, siman 27, brought in Pitchei Teshuva to Yoreh Deah, siman 341, s.k. 6, regarding counting the Omer. Since through this the mitzvah would be nullified even afterwards, the onen is not exempt from counting the Omer. The same applies here. See there. See further in the responsum of the Chacham Tzvi there, s.v. "and behold, if," etc., where he investigates whether the intent required for chalitzah is like intent for a mitzvah, or only like the intent required for transfers of ownership. If we say that it is like intent for a mitzvah, then chalitzah performed by a mumar is difficult, for he acts according to his own intent, and it should invalidate chalitzah just as it invalidates the writing of a get. See there. In Ketzot HaChoshen, end of siman 275, he wrote to prove that it is like intent for a mitzvah. See there. However, he did not resolve the difficulty from chalitzah performed by a mumar. See Beit Meir, who also wrote that it is in the manner of intent for a mitzvah, as the Rambam wrote in chapter 4, halacha 8: "and they shall perform these acts for their own sake," etc. He discusses at length how to resolve the difficulty from chalitzah performed by a mumar. See there. Also in Get Mekushar, in Seder Get Rishon, end of letter 7, he wrote to resolve this. See there. Also in Responsa Chatam Sofer, part 2, siman 90, regarding this.
