See Ba'er Heitev, who wrote: and we do not have the custom to write, etc.; and Rashal in Seder Chalitzah wrote, etc. See Korban Netanel, end of Perek Mitzvat Chalitzah, who wrote about him that according to his approach, brought below in Seder Chalitzah in brief, seif 29, s.k. 10, that with one who is married we do not say to him, "If you wish to perform yibbum," it is not correct to write this, for in the chalitzah shtar we write: "The rabbis, the judges, said to me: If you wish to perform yibbum, perform yibbum." See there. See Beit Meir, who wrote: Nevertheless, a woman who is established as being bound by zikah and is not publicly known to have performed chalitzah is forbidden to marry except through witnesses, and even the chalutz is not believed to testify about her, etc. See there. Therefore, if the chalutzah is from another city, one should give her a beit din record, as they give for inheritance.
