The judges sit as is it is necessary to sit in judgement, and the yavam and yevama stand before them, since the Mitzvah of chalitsah is ideally done standing. {Rama: But after the fact, if the chalitsah was done sitting, it is valid (Tur). So too an ill person who is unable to stand is able to do chalitsah sitting, since anything which has no fix is important after the fact (Piskei Mahari Siman 190). And see later Seif 64 for the specific order of the chalitsah.}
