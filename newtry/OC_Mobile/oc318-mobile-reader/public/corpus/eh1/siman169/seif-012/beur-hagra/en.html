(א) They sat, etc. See Tosafot 103a, s.v. bein, etc. Although we say that chalitzah is like the beginning of judgment, as stated there, 104a, that is only to be stringent.
(ב) And they shall stand, etc. There, 106a: “my daughter,” etc., according to one explanation in Rashi, and in the Sifrei, “and he shall stand,” etc., as written in the above Tosafot.
(ג) But bedieved, etc. The Gemara there: “whether standing,” etc., and in the third chapter of Moed Katan 21. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 17. But the Rashba holds that even lechatchilah it may be done seated, and proved it from what is stated there: R. Elazar said to him, “but was it not taught, whether,” etc. This follows the view of the Ra'avad and the Rosh, who hold that Ameimar spoke lechatchilah. But according to the Rif, who invalidates even bedieved, and explains that it belongs to the other statement of Ameimar, as Tosafot wrote there, s.v. man, in the name of Rabbenu Chananel, and as written above in seif 32, there is no proof from there. So too Piskei Seder Chalitzah, seif 31. The Rambam’s view is that the reading is standing from the Sifrei, and likewise spitting, but chalitzah itself may be seated from the above baraita. But from the sugya in Moed Katan one cannot say this. See Rashba, who pressed himself to answer the Rambam’s words, and wrote that his main proof is from R. Elazar’s challenge to Ameimar. See there. This cannot be said, for the Rambam holds like Rabbenu Chananel and the Rif, since he wrote, “or if he was walking on his toes,” etc., “for the one performing chalitzah,” etc., as written in Piskei Seder Chalitzah, seif 31. See there.
(ד) And likewise a sick person, etc. 106a according to the above explanation of Rashi.
(ה) For twenty-four, etc. As written in Ketubot 14a. See Tosafot, Pesachim 30a, s.v. lishehin. Ri explains, etc.
