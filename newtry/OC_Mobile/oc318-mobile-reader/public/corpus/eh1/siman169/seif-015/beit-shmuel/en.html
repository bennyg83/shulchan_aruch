Lechatchilah it must be hard leather. The reason for the dispute is that it is stated in the Talmud that one does not perform chalitzah with a shoe lechatchilah. Nowadays, when sandals are not common, by strict law it would be permitted to perform chalitzah with a shoe, but the decisors wrote that even nowadays, lechatchilah it is better to make a sandal. There are several explanations of the difference between a shoe and a sandal, and from this came all the views brought here. However, what he brought here in the name of "some say," that the stitching should be inside, I do not know who these "some say" are, for the Semag and Semak wrote explicitly that a sandal has its stitching on the outside.
