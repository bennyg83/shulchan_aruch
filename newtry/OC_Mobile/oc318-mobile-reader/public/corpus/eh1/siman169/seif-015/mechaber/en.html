The shoe must be entirely of leather. Therefore, [the shoemaker] must ideally sew all of its seams with leather. And that is our practice; and some [authorities] invalidate it even after the fact (Tur in the name of the RM"H), contrary to the opinion that since nowadays all shoes are sewn with flax, [the shoe for chalitzah] must also be sewn with flax ([this opinion is] in the writings of R"Y Mintz in the name of R' Shmarya) . But its straps do not need to be of leather. And some authorities say that the straps also need to be of leather (Nimukei Yosef), and that is our practice.
