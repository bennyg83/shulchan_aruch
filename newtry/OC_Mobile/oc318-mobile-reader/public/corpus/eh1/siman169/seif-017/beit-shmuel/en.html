So that it not be "from upon that which is upon." This is lechatchilah how the mitzvah is done, but bedieved there is no concern, since she removes both, as written in seif 37. So wrote the Beit Yosef. In the Talmud it implies that even lechatchilah there is no concern of "from upon that which is upon," for otherwise why did it not say that one does not perform chalitzah with a shoe lechatchilah because of "from upon that which is upon," as the Talmud says: perhaps the lining is upon the shoe, and the strap is upon that which is upon. See Bach and Perishah. Even lechatchilah there is no real concern here, only that the decisors wrote this as a mere stringency.
