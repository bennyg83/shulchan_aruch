(א) Or if they told him, “Perform chalitzah, for this,” etc. The Yerushalmi there says: What is a mistaken chalitzah? Reish Lakish said: any case where they told him, “Perform chalitzah, and she will become permitted to you after some time.” Rabbi Yochanan said: Last night I taught that he intends, etc.
(ב) But she became disqualified, etc. The Gemara there.
(ג) And we compel, etc. As stated regarding one who vows not to benefit, etc., until “we compel,” etc., where “her get goes out,” etc. This shows that since she is forbidden to him, we compel him. This is as stated above, siman 165, seif 2. In the Yerushalmi, a case came before Rav Huna and he acted like Reish Lakish. When Rabbi Yochanan heard, he disagreed and wrote, “He performed chalitzah for her a second time.”
