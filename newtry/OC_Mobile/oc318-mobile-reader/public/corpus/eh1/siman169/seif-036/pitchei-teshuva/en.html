Before beit din. See Ba'er Heitev, what he wrote regarding a yavam who wants to perform yibbum and she spat before him, etc. See Beit Meir, whose view in this is that it is exactly the law of a yevamah who vowed not to benefit after the death of her husband, as explained in siman 165, seif 3, that we do not compel, but only request. It is not comparable to "perform chalitzah for her, and through this you will marry her," below, seif 91, because there he misled himself, and she too did not do anything against his will; rather, others misled him, etc. See there.
