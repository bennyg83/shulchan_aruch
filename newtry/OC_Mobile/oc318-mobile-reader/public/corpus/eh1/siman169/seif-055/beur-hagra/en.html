If she transgressed and married, etc. Although without chalitzah we forbid her to her husband as a penalty, since she made herself forbidden to the yavam, as stated, “Did she gain?” And similarly regarding a married woman they said in HaIsha Rabbah that it is because of a penalty, as they wrote in the Yerushalmi there: they penalized her so that she should investigate carefully. This is not so here, because in any case she is forbidden to him. Rivash.
