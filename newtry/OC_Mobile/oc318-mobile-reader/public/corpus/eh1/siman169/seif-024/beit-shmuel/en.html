Some say that he performs chalitzah with both. Although he wrote nearby that if she is left-handed she performs chalitzah with her right, nevertheless regarding the yavam we are stringent because there is a gezeirah shavah that requires specifically the actual right. In the explanation of chalitzah he wrote that to remove from both of his feet at one time, she should remove from one foot with one hand and from the second foot with her other hand. If she removes one after the other, it is an invalid chalitzah. The Bach wrote that the custom is to remove one after the other, and the yavam and yevamah should intend to perform chalitzah with the one that, by strict law, is the one from which to remove.
