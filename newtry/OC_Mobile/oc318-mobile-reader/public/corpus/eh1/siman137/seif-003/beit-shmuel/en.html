(ב) Except for your promiscuous relations. Meaning: regarding promiscuous relations, you remain like a married woman. Although he permitted her to marry, since he reserved intercourse the Gemara asks whether this is considered a reservation, specifically in such a case, where he permitted her to marry and prohibited promiscuous relations. But the reverse, where he permitted promiscuous relations and prohibited marriage, such as saying "except that you may not marry so-and-so," is a reservation and it is not a get. So wrote Derishah. If he used the language "that you may not marry so-and-so," it is explained in the Gemara that he permitted promiscuous relations, unless he said that she may not have relations with so-and-so, in which case she is forbidden to him even in promiscuous relations. It appears that if that so-and-so was someone with whom kiddushin do not take effect, and he conditioned regarding promiscuous relations, it is not a reservation, just as regarding marriage, even though marriage does not apply to him and promiscuous relations do. This is also explicitly implied in the Gemara's initial thought: we taught that promiscuous relations are a reservation, and nevertheless regarding her father it is not a reservation; in the conclusion we find no contradiction to this.
