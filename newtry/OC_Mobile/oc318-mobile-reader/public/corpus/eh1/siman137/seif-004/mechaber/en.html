If he said to her, "You are permitted to every man except Reuven and Shimon," and then, at the time he delivered the bill of divorce to her, he said, "You are permitted to Reuven and Shimon," or he said, "You are permitted to Reuven," or he said, "You are permitted to Shimon," or he said, "also to Shimon," she is doubtfully divorced.
