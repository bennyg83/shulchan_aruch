A woman who has double levirate ties, either she or her co-wife undergo chalitzah, but not yibum. How is this (what is an example)? 3 brothers are married to 3 unrelated women. One brother dies and one of the remaining brothers gave the widow "ma'amar" (kiddushin-style money which creates a partial bond but does not effect full yibbum), but dies before they have relations. In this case, the woman is now a double yebama to the remaining living brother and he must do chalitzah- he can't marry her.
