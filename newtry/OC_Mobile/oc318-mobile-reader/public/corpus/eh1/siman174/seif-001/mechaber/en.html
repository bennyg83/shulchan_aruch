A yavama (a widow who must perform a yibbum with her late husband's brother) who is forbidden to the yavam (her late husband's brother), whether through a negative commandment [in the Torah], or a positive commandment [in the Torah], or is a secondary [relative of his] on a Rabbinic level, she performs chalitzah (action by which a yavama is permitted to marry a non-yavam) and does not perform yibum (a yibbum), and her co-wife performs either chalitzah or yibum. And her performance of chalitzah does not exempt her co-wife [from having to do chalitzah or yibum], but the chalitzah of her co-wife exempts her. And if he violated [this rule] and engaged in sexual relations with her, he [thereby] acquires her [as his wife] and this exempts her co-wife. And if he wishes to divorce her, he divorces her with a get (a divorce document).
