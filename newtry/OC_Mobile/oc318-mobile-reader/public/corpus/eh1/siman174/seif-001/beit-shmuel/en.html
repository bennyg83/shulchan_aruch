She performs chalitzah and is not taken in yibbum. See the sugya on daf 20. By biblical law she is fit for yibbum, because the positive commandment of yibbum overrides the negative prohibition. Therefore, if he had relations with her, she exempts her rival wife. Chazal, however, decreed against the first act of relations because of the second act, since the mitzvah of yibbum applies only to the first act. Tosafot write that this is not limited to a second act, but even completing the first act is forbidden once the yevamah is acquired at the beginning of relations. Tosafot also derive that we do not require her to be fit for establishing a name in the sense of pregnancy from the first act; it is enough that the couple are generally fit, excluding an aylonit and saris. If she is a widow from full marriage and a Kohen Gadol had relations with her, her rival wife is not exempt, because the positive commandment does not override both a negative and positive prohibition.
