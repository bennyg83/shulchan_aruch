If she had a verbal loan upon her and afterward married — it is not collected from the husband, for a verbal loan is not collected from buyers. {Rama: However, if the money the loan was for is still extant he is obligated to pay (R' Yitzchak and Mordechai). And if the lender came before she married and wishes to protest her marriage until she pays him and that he not lose his — the law is with him even though the time of payment has not yet arrived (Beit Yosef in name of Nimmukei Yosef).} But if she had a loan with a document — he collects from property she brought to her husband. A community that made regulations among themselves with fines and a woman transgressed the regulation — her husband is obligated to pay (Beit Yosef siman 70 in name of Terumat HaDeshen siman 282).
