A woman who brought in. It is explained in Choshen Mishpat, siman 66, that whether she brought it to him as nichsei tzon barzel or as nichsei melog, she cannot waive it. The language "brought in" also applies to nichsei melog, as I wrote nearby.
Therefore she is not believed, etc. Since she has no migo, because she cannot waive it, she is not believed to say it was paid. Likewise, with an oral loan, she cannot waive and cannot say it was paid when it is still within the time. If the borrower claims he paid the shtar, and the shtar contains no credibility clause, then she must swear; she can say, "I do not want to swear because it is paid." Regarding an oral loan within the time, whether she must swear depends on the dispute in Choshen Mishpat, siman 78.
