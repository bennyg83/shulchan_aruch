A woman who brought a loan to her husband and forgave it. It contains 5 seifim. A woman who brought a promissory note of debt to her husband and afterward forgave it — it is not forgiven, because his hand is like her hand. {Rama: Therefore she is likewise not believed to say it was paid (Nimmukei Yosef chapter HaKotev). And the same applies — she cannot forgive a verbal loan (Beit Yosef in name of R' Yitzchak).}
