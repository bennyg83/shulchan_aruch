One who sent gifts to a betrothed woman and she went and married another, and that one claims his gifts — their law is like a loan with a document. {Rama: If she took more than her ketubah and married — the husband must return the excess (Mahari"k shoresh 18). And likewise if she became liable for tax and married — it is like sending gifts, for all these have an outcry and there is no way to make a document for them, therefore it is like a loan with a document (Maharam Padua siman 55). But damages and purchases and other matters — he is not obligated to pay (Nimmukei Yosef chapter Yesh Nochalin). A woman who betrothed her daughter to a man and became liable for dowry and afterward married herself to the man betrothed to her daughter — he extracts everything she became liable for from her husband from property she brought him, for it is like a loan with a document; even though at the time she made the betrothal arrangement for her daughter she had not yet sworn on her ketubah — nevertheless since she swore at the end, the matter is revealed retroactively that what she did initially was lawful (Beit Yosef in name of responsum of Rashba). Some say that a loan with a document collects from the husband from property she brought him — this is when she brought him land or movable property and wrote for her husband the debt of the movables by way of the land; but if she did not write for him by way of land and did not bring him land, only movables — even a loan with a document she does not collect from her husband, for a creditor does not seize from encumbered movables (responsum of Ramban siman 56). And this is sound reasoning; nevertheless it appears to them that regarding a garment that is not hers, such as if she took more than her ketubah — he must return in every case (and see Choshen Mishpat end of siman 96) (explained above).}
