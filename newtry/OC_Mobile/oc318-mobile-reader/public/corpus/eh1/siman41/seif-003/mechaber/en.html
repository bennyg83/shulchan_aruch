One who betrothed many women at once among whom were two sisters or a woman and her daughter — if he said "the one among you fit for intercourse is betrothed to me" — behold all of them are betrothed to him except two sisters or a woman and her daughter and the like. And if he said "all of you are betrothed to me" — gentile women are betrothed and sisters are not betrothed. And some say even gentile women are not betrothed; therefore gentile women are doubtfully betrothed. And if he said "one of all of you is betrothed to me" — all of them, whether gentile women or sisters, are doubtfully betrothed.
