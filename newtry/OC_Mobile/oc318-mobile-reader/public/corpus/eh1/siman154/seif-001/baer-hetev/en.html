(ו) Prostitutes. Reuven had relations with a married woman and repented, and his wife demands from him a get and that he give her the ketubah. Chacham Tzvi ruled in his responsum, siman 133, that we do not compel him to divorce. This is not comparable to one who frequents prostitutes, for that person is established in this behavior and remains in rebellion; "one who frequents prostitutes will lose wealth," and in the end he will be unable to support her. Here, however, he repented. See there.
