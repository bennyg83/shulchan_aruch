(א) Supports. This does not refer only to one who says "I will not support"; where he lacks means, it is obvious that we compel. Maharam Mitrani, part 1 siman 76, wrote: there is no greater "does not want" than one who has been separated from her for several years, did not wish to send her sustenance, did not come near her, and goes fleeing from place to place; see there. See Keneset HaGedolah, folio 181b. If her father-in-law claims, "Let her come to my house and I will support her," we do not compel him with lashes, but we compel with words. If he does not wish to divorce, she may take all property found with her, whether from her dowry or from ornaments her husband gave her, and go to her father's house; he is obligated to give her sustenance as long as he does not divorce her by get. However, if sustenance in the city where he lives is cheaper than in her father's place of residence, we assess according to the cheaper rate. Maharit, part 1, siman 113.
