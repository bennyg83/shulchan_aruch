(ד) Her ketubah. This does not mean only where she explicitly demands her ketubah; even if she mentioned it by hint, she is not believed. Any matter that appears to beit din as though she is scheming to hide her ketubah claim is not believed. Keneset HaGedolah, folio 184a. If she seized from her husband's property the amount of her ketubah, so that she does not need to claim the ketubah, there is room to doubt that perhaps her claim is false. Ralnach, siman 29.
