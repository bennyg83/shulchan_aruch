When taking her from state to state and village to village within that land — he cannot take her from pleasant dwelling to unpleasant, nor from unpleasant to pleasant; and likewise he does not take her out from a place whose majority is Israel to a place whose majority is idolaters; and everywhere they take out from a place whose majority is Samaritans to a place whose majority is Israel. {Rama: And he cannot take her from a place where the ruler is good to a place where the ruler is bad (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 13 Laws of Marriage). Everywhere he can take her from her place — meaning after she entered the chuppah and married; but before marriage he cannot take her out — he must marry her in her place unless he explicitly stipulated with the woman; but if he stipulated with her mother it is nothing (Rivash siman 177); however if there are excuses and reasons for his words — the woman must follow him (responsum Maimoniyot to women siman 28).}
