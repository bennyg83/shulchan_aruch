Division of lands regarding marriage and laws of Eretz Yisrael. It contains 5 seifim. Three lands in Eretz Yisrael are divided from each other regarding marriage: Judea, Transjordan, and Galilee; and all the Diaspora are lands such as Land of Canaan, Land of Egypt, and Land of Yemen (which differ in their languages) (Maharam siman 217) — that one who is from one of the lands and married a woman in another land — they compel her and she goes out with him to his land or leaves without ketubah and without additional amount, for on this condition she married even though he did not specify; but he cannot take her out from city to regional capital or vice versa within one Galilee even if he stipulated with her to take her from Galilee to Judea (Ran ch. 2 of Sanhedrin on decrees, and so all similar cases). But one who married a woman in one of the lands and he is from the people of that land — he cannot take her to another land; but he takes her from state to state and from village to village within those lands; and he cannot take her from state to village nor from village to state. {Rama: Some say if both were from one land and she married in another land — she can compel him to go with her to her land even from village to state or vice versa (Tur in name of Rabbeinu Tam); but if both from one land and she married in that land — neither compels the other to take from state to village or vice versa; but from state to state or village to village the woman can compel him to go with her to her place since they are equal (likewise there). If he dwelled with her in his city and did not become accustomed there and traveled with her to her city by happenstance and did not become accustomed there — he can return and take her out (Rivash siman 48). Some say if he cannot sustain and support himself they compel his wife to go with him wherever he wishes (Terumat HaDeshen siman 215). Some disagree (Beit Yosef and so implies in Rivash siman 81). One who is from one state and has a wife there and married another in another state — she must go with him to his city, for certainly she married to return to his house (Beit Yosef in name of Rashbatz) (and see below siman 154 seif 9) more laws of this.}
