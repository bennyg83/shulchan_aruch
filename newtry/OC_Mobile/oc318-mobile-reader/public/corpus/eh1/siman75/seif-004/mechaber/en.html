If the man said to ascend to Eretz Yisrael and she does not wish — she leaves without ketubah. {Rama: But her melog property and ntz"b (guaranteed property) that still exists she takes; and if they no longer exist — if he caused their loss he need not pay ntz"b; n"m (property she brought in) must pay; and if stolen or lost — n"m has nothing and ntz"b must pay (in Mordechai end of Kesubos in name of Maharam); and that she has no ketubah is specifically when he remains in Eretz Yisrael; but if he returns after several years to settle abroad — he must pay her or her heirs also the ketubah (likewise there).} If she said to ascend and he does not wish — he divorces and gives ketubah; and the same for every place from Eretz Yisrael to Jerusalem — everyone ascends to Eretz Yisrael and not everyone is taken out from there; everyone ascends to Jerusalem and not everyone is taken out from there.
