When does this apply? From abroad to abroad or from Eretz Yisrael to Eretz Yisrael; but from abroad to Eretz Yisrael — they compel her to ascend even from pleasant dwelling to unpleasant dwelling, and even from a place whose majority is Israel to a place whose majority is Samaritans; and they do not take out from Eretz Yisrael to abroad even from unpleasant to pleasant dwelling, and even from a place whose majority is Samaritans to a place whose majority is Israel.
