If there are families accustomed to write in their ketubot more than the Sages' measure — one should not protest against them. And moreover, even one member of that family who did not write a ketubah for his wife — we collect for her under beit din's conditions according to what they are accustomed to write. Therefore a woman whose ketubah was lost — they should examine the ketubot of her relatives according to what the family members are accustomed to write, and they write for her; and even if he wrote for her "that which is fitting for you" — nevertheless she collects according to her family's custom (Beit Yosef in name of Ran who so wrote in name of Tosafot). And if the ketubot of the women of his family exceed those of the women of her family — she rises with him and does not descend.
