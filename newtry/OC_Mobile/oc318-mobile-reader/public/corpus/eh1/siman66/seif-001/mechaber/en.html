Laws of the ketubah. It contains 13 seifim. It is forbidden to be alone with the bride before he writes her a ketubah. {Rama: Some are lenient and permit being alone without a ketubah, provided he does not have intercourse (Ran beginning of Kesubos).} And the husband pays the scribe's fee. And even if he wishes to designate her ketubah in money or other movable property — we do not listen to him; rather he must write her a document upon himself for the ketubah amount and all his property shall be collateralized to her; or witnesses testify about him and acquire from him that he owes her a maneh or two hundred. {Rama: Some say one may rely on witnesses only in a time of duress, and as soon as he has leisure to write he must write — all the more so one may not rely on them ab initio (so implied from Tur's language, and likewise Maharik root 64 and Mordechai beginning of Kesubos); and so it is proper to rule. If he appointed a guarantor for her ketubah and stipulated that he would be exempt from it — it is forbidden until there is an obligation upon him so that divorcing her would not be light in his eyes (Beit Yosef in name of Rashba in a responsum). And see Choshen Mishpat siman 60 whether he can collateralize himself for her ketubah with property not yet in existence. Witnesses may not sign the ketubah until after the groom accepts kinyan before them (the master's words). And there are places that are lenient in this (Mordechai ch. 2 of Gittin). And likewise where witnesses sign on ketubot that were read under the chuppah even though they did not hear the reading — even though they do not act properly, one should not change their custom so as not to cast aspersion on earlier ketubot (Or Zarua).}
