(ב) To be alone. The reason is that she will not listen to him, because she knows that she will be light in his eyes to divorce her. Ran. Chelkat Mechokek wrote that therefore it is proper to inform her of this if he is secluded with her without a ketubah. Keneset HaGedolah, folio 92a, wrote: I saw a bad and bitter custom, that after the matchmaking the groom and bride seclude themselves in a room and close the room, and there is prohibition here from several sides. First, because of yichud, for it is like secluding himself with an ordinary woman, since there is no kiddushin here. Further, they stumble in the prohibition of niddah, and in most cases the bride goes out to her chuppah with her belly between her teeth. God knows that I labored greatly to abolish this custom and succeeded; I made enactments, bans, and excommunications about it. End quote.
