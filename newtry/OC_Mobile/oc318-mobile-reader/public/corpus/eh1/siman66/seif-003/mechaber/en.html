If he wrote her a ketubah and it was lost, or she waived it to him — specifically if she wrote him "I received my ketubah" — he must write another for the principal ketubah, for it is forbidden for a man to remain with his wife even one hour without a ketubah. {Rama: A city that was conquered or from which they were exiled and the women's ketubot were lost — they must renew their wives' ketubot, even though one might say perhaps their ketubot will later be found or returned to them; nevertheless, since the ketubot are presumed lost, other ketubot are required. And if the sum of the ketubot is unknown — if some will remain, we follow both of them; and if not, we judge according to the elders of the city and according to custom — the rich according to his wealth and the poor according to his poverty (Beit Yosef in name of responsa of the Geonim). And when the ketubah was lost and he comes to write her another ketubah — he must write her a ketubah as large as the first (Maharik root 116). And see below siman 177 that in a place where they divorce only with the woman's consent, a ketubah need not be written; if so, nowadays in these countries where a man does not divorce a woman against her will because of Rabbenu Gershom's ban, as will be explained below siman 119 — one could be lenient in writing the ketubah; but the custom is not so and one should not change (all this is the master's view).}
