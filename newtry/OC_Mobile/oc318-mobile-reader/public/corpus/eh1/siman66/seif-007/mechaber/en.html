If he wishes to add to this — he may add. And this addition that he adds — some matters have the same law as the principal ketubah, and some matters are distinguished from the principal ketubah, as below. {Rama: He need not specify the principal ketubah separately and the addition separately; rather he may include everything together if he wishes (Ran beginning of Even HaEzer and Maggid Mishneh ch. 10 and Rivash siman 68). And some dispute and hold that he must write the ketubah according to provincial custom separately and the addition separately — and so is the practice. And if there is a family where all practice with an addition — he need not write each one separately (Mordechai ch. 1 of Kesubos). A ketubah in which he wrote "and the nedunya that you brought him one hundred dinar and he added to her from his own such-and-such" — the principal ketubah is not included and he must pay her separately until it says "written and I give you your virgin bride-price, one thousand silver zuz" — then the principal ketubah is included (Rivash siman 65).}
