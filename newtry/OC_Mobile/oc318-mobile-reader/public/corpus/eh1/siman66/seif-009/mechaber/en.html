Anyone who reduces from the ketubah measure — his intercourse is licentious intercourse. This applies even if she afterward wrote him "I received from you the ketubah or part of it" — for then she has no ketubah per some poskim. Rather, even if he stipulated at betrothal that she would have no ketubah, or he reduced from its measure — even though his stipulation is void and she has a ketubah — he still pays; even so his intercourse is licentious intercourse, since she believes she has none and did not rely.
