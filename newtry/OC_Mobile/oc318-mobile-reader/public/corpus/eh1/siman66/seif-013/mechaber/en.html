In a place where they are not accustomed to sign on the ketubah except those fit to testify — one who does not know how to read may not sign. {Rama: Therefore an am ha'aretz who came to divorce and afterward said he did not understand what was written in the conditions or ketubah — he is not believed, for certainly the witnesses did not sign what they had not first testified orally before him (responsum of Rashba siman 629).}
