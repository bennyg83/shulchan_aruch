(ב) My wife shall take like one of the sons. Although if he made her an heir he has said nothing, because he does not have the power to give inheritance to one who is not fit to inherit, nevertheless if he said "she shall take," she acquires, because this is language of gift. Nimukei Yosef. We do not say in such a case that she waived the ketubah, for he is not dividing all the property; therefore she takes in addition to the ketubah. So wrote Nimukei Yosef.
