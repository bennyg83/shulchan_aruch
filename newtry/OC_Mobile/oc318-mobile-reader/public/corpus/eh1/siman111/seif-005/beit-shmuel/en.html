Even if they increased or decreased. We follow the time of death. If the assets were abundant at the time of death and later decreased, the son of the older wife has already acquired. If they decreased afterward and there no longer remains the measure of two ketubot, they divide the loss according to the amount of each ketubat benin dikhrin, each according to his share. If one ketubat benin dikhrin is double the other, it bears two shares of the loss.
