(א) They inherit her ketubah. In the Gemara, R. Yochanan said: Why did they enact ketubat benin dikhrin? So that a person would leap to give to his daughter as to his son. But say that what the father gives should be inherited, meaning that what the father gives should be inherited by the male children, while the husband's property, the maneh, two hundred, and additional ketubah, should not be inherited. It answers: if so, the father will refrain and not write for his daughter. But say: where the father gives a dowry to his daughter, ketubat benin dikhrin should be written, and where the father does not give, it should not be written. The Rabbis did not distinguish.
