A woman who sells her ketubah, whether to her husband or others, does not lose the "male sons" rights (an unwritten condition in the ketubah whereby her male sons inherit the value of her ketubah prior to any division of the husband's estate with sons from any other marriage; see Ketubot 53). However, if she relinquished rights in her ketubah to her husband, her sons also lose the "male sons" rights. Therefore, the sons do not inherit "male sons" properties unless their mother's original ketubah document is in their possession, but if they do not have the ketubah document, they have nothing, for perhaps their mother relinquished her rights. But if they are in a locale where the custom is not to write a ketubah, the sons do have "male sons" rights [even without a ketubah document].
