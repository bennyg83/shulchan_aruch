And afterward the sons of the first wife inherit, etc. Payment of the second wife's ketubah is considered surplus over the ketubat benin dikhrin of the first wife, because at the time of death the ketubah became owed to the second wife, and through this payment the Torah inheritance was fulfilled. This is unlike an ordinary debt owed to others, where we say that a creditor collects only from now onward; it is as if it came into being after death.
