Lest worth a perutah in Media — see Beit Shmuel's question and his resolution somewhat strained — for granted all works well if the stringent side is true witnesses already saw she is betrothed; but here even though worth a perutah in Media in their eyes it is not worth a perutah; nevertheless one can say the question is only for Rambam — for for Tosafot from outset per Kesef Mishneh they are only rabbinic from a decree; and possibly he holds like Tosafot chapter Merubah 70 — if witnesses saw what they are able to see it is called complete testimony; and if so if it is worth a perutah in Media without much appraisal witnesses will be found there to testify this object is worth a perutah there — and this is not called witnesses at all on the side of a blind alley; and here witnesses saw she was betrothed with this object and through joining she was betrothed by itself; and per this what Tosafot wrote "and it seems" it discusses witnesses know, etc. — is not necessary; and what Tosafot wrote "and it seems also," etc. — must say Ran wrote this reason for himself but not for Rambam — for otherwise no distinction between lasting things; and know per method of Maharam in siman 30 seif 5 — even though he holds in found object we establish presumption of unattached woman — nevertheless not necessary he disputes here Rambam; for one can say doubt standing to be clarified we do not establish presumption.
