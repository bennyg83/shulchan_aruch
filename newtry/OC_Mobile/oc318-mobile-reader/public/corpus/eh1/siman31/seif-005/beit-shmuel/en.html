Be betrothed to me with this date, etc. In Gemara they establish this sugya like R' Shimon who said in Shevuot that it is not a partial statement unless he says an oath on each one; and in Knesset HaGedolah wondered on Rambam who rules chapter 6 law 5 of Shevuot and chapter 4 of Nedarim not like R' Shimon; and in Shakh also rules even if he did not say "be betrothed" on each one it is partial — uncertain for law.
Those dates she ate. And some say for one who says "behold you are betrothed after thirty days" — she is betrothed even though the money was consumed before kiddushin took effect — for there nevertheless he finished his words and giving and afterward it came to her hand — Tosafot; and Ri'iv wrote where he interrupts between speech with this and this — even within time of speech we are concerned he retracted and each must be worth a perutah and then she is certainly betrothed. And regarding if he retracted before completion of kiddushin whether the money returns — see Rashba what is written.
