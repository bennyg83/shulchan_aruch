One who says to a woman "Be betrothed to me with this date; be betrothed to me with this one; be betrothed to me with this one" — if one of them has the value of a perutah she is betrothed; and if not she is only doubtfully betrothed, lest a single date is worth a perutah somewhere else. And if he said to her "Be betrothed to me with this and with this and with this" — they all join, so that if all of them together have the value of a perutah she is betrothed, and if not she is only doubtfully betrothed. If she was eating one at a time — if the last one had the value of a perutah she is betrothed, and if not she is only doubtfully betrothed, for those dates she ate are like a loan, and one who betroths with a loan — she is not betrothed; it turns out only the final date is a betrothal transaction.
