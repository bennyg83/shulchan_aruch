If he betrothed her with food or a vessel or the like that is worth less than a perutah — she is doubtfully betrothed, lest this thing is worth a perutah somewhere else. And some say that if he betrothed her with a cooked dish or a vegetable that cannot last or the like — if it was not worth a perutah in that place she is not betrothed at all, for this thing cannot go somewhere else before it loses its value and will not be worth a perutah.
