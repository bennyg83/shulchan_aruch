That is worth fifty zuz she is betrothed. Wrote in Kav HaYashar: since poskim did not distinguish — implies if it is not worth fifty zuz even if less than measure of ona'ah she is not betrothed since he said fifty zuz — we say she does not transfer herself for less than this sum; for in her acquisition waiver does not apply even for less than measure of ona'ah — as if he said with this maneh and a dinar was found lacking kiddushin are void. And if found the object is worth much more — such as betrother said worth fifty zuz and found worth one hundred — it seems the object is hers and need not return the money; for here too one can say as we say Choshen Mishpat siman 227 seif 20 in exchange — this one wants the needle more than the cloak; and explained there in Sema that even if they appraised it at first nevertheless one can say this one wants the woman more than the object — end quote; see Sha'arei HaMelekh chapter 4 Laws of Marriage law 19 regarding if afterward found not worth fifty zuz whether not betrothed at all or betrothed at least from doubt lest worth elsewhere — see there.
