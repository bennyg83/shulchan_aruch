He betrothed, etc. And in Beit HaGadol chapter 4 not like Shmuel from what is written; and not because he holds like Shmuel, etc.; and there 13a1 "you heard," etc.; and if it were otherwise it should have said because of Shmuel; but Rashba and Rosh and several poskim agreed to Rif's and Rambam's ruling to rule like Shmuel; Rav Yosef holds like him; and that which says "you heard," etc. — already explained Rashba and Rosh as above siman 28 seif 5; and that which says "not because," etc. — Rashba explained not because of Shmuel alone, etc.; and difference if he said explicitly worth a perutah or with a non-lasting thing for Rambam; and per what is written below "and some say if," etc. — otherwise it comes out well.
Some say if, etc. His view that with a lasting thing if it is known worth a perutah elsewhere she is certainly betrothed; but Rosh disagreed and his view is like Tosefta that from Torah we follow only its place and time as written Arakhin 24a1 "consecration has no," etc.; and proof from what is written Bava Kamma 105a1, etc.; and we are not concerned lest worth a perutah in Media; Tosafot Kiddushin 53b2 s.v. ihu, etc.; but Rashba agreed to Rambam's view that if per Tosefta it is only a decree in general he should not have said here doubtful kiddushin but rabbinic kiddushin.
