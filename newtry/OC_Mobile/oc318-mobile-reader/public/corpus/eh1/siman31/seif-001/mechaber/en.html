That one may not betroth with less than the value of a perutah, and if appraisal is required. It contains 9 seifim. One may not betroth with less than the value of a perutah; however if he betrothed her with an unspecified object he need not appraise it first — if it is worth a perutah she is betrothed. And even if he said to her "Be betrothed to me with this object that is worth fifty zuz" and they did not appraise it first, if it is afterward found to be worth fifty zuz she is betrothed.
