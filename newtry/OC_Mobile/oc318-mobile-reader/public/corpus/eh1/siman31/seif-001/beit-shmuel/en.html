Betrothed. Meaning retroactively from time of giving the kiddushin took effect; but if he betrothed with precious stones and the like — explained in Rashi and Tosafot — if there was no appraisal at time of kiddushin, even if they appraised afterward it does not help and she is not betrothed even from time of appraisal. And what Ran infers from Rambam that from time of appraisal she is betrothed — can be rejected; and what Rambam wrote "he need not appraisal and afterward she will be betrothed" — his intent is to say any matter that does not require appraisal — we do not say it requires appraisal and afterward betrothal; rather no appraisal at all; but a matter that requires appraisal does not help if they appraised afterward — so too Bach, Maharit. And if they did not appraise — he must betroth her anew; and Rashba wrote he need not take kiddushin from her and betroth anew — rather he says to her "behold you are betrothed to me with this"; and we do not say silence after giving money because it resembles sheidukh. At first glance difficult — contradicts what is written siman 28 that even with sheidukh silence after giving money does not help; and possibly here is stronger since she accepted for kiddushin. However difficult why he wrote siman 27 that he must take kiddushin from her — see what is written there. Also implied from Rashi and Tosafot in this sugya they disagree with Ritva I brought siman 28 who holds if he betrothed with robbery and afterward acquired from the robbed one — kiddushin take effect from that hour; and here it seems kiddushin do not take effect even after appraisal.
