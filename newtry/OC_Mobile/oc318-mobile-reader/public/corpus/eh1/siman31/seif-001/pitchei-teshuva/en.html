With less than a perutah. See Maggid Mishneh chapter 4 Laws of Marriage law 1 who copied responsum of Radbaz part 1 — they asked about one who threw kiddushin to a woman and they were not worth a perutah and did not reach her hand until they appreciated and stood at perutah — what is the law? Do we follow the giver — then there is no perutah and nothing is lacking — therefore he does not acquire; or perhaps we follow the receiver — then there is a perutah? He answered the matter is clear we follow her side — for she is betrothed only from hour money reached her hand; and when they reached they were worth a perutah. And even though when he gave it was not worth a perutah and nothing was lacking — nevertheless he acquires from law of Canaanite slave — even though nothing was lacking he acquires himself. And further — in his domain it appreciated and stood at perutah; and likewise one who makes an agent to betroth a woman for him with half a perutah and the agent did not manage to betroth her until it stood at perutah — she too is betrothed; and we do not say since there was no perutah here there is no agency — for there are several great ones (see below siman 35 seif 4) who said even if he did not make him agent at all; and others said if he desires before him and said his will to betroth a woman and this one went and betrothed her for him — she is betrothed; and all the more here where he made him agent in act with half a perutah — therefore since it stood at perutah and he betrothed her for him she is betrothed for we follow the receiver. And likewise implied from what Rava asked first chapter of Kiddushin "your two daughters for my two sons with a perutah," etc. (see above seif 7 and Beit Shmuel) — clear from here we follow receiver and giver we do not care; and in our case money reached her hand perutah — no question at all; for certainly we do not question there except because we follow their side and there is not for each one of them a perutah; and in the adult from this Ran was uncertain (Ba'er Heitev s.k. 4: and if he gives her worth half perutah and she says for me perutah — and perforce he was uncertain only because she did not accept perutah; but where she accepted perutah in our case plainly to him she is betrothed — end quote); see what I wrote above siman 28 s.k. 29. See in hagahah of Maggid Mishneh chapter 4 Laws of Marriage end law 19 who wrote: and know the author z"l was uncertain one who betrothed with an etrog not worth perutah and for its mitzvah it is worth — whether betrothed — his words brought in Bnei Chayei — end quote; see responsum Noda BiYehudah tinyana siman 67 and s.k. 120 what is written on this; see in Birkhei Yosef who wrote in name of one great authority she is not betrothed — see there; and per my view this depends somewhat on dispute between Maharam Mintz and responsum Chacham Tzvi and Shevut Yaakov brought in Ba'er Heitev Orach Chaim siman 656 regarding one who disqualified another's etrog; and if he must pay in full he has status of money; and per this per what Maggid himself raised in Petach HaDevir Laws of Offerings like words of Chacham Tzvi that he must pay all value of etrog — there is no room for doubt here — uncertain.
