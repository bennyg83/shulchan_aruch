Betrothed — see Beit Shmuel; but if he betrothed with precious stones — explained in Rashi and Tosafot; so understood Ran from Rashi; and what Tosafot wrote meaning besides what they copied Rashi's language — evident from their question from "they appraise the paper," etc.; and per my view question from Rashi 53b who explains the first version — if he betrothed with second tithe kiddushin do not take effect until it reaches Jerusalem where they are worth a perutah; but in the borderlands they are not worth a perutah and impossible they should take effect; and now — there certainly she did not rely at time of kiddushin for from this reason they do not take effect; from now per this explanation; and also lacking walking to place where they are worth; and if she reaches there nevertheless betrothed from hour they became worth a perutah; all the more here where in Media they were worth a perutah but she did not rely — why should she not be betrothed from time she relied. and per our view that she is betrothed retroactively — this applies to dispute of law; but in the actual case what was was; therefore he explained it discusses when she retracted; and R' Yehuda said there is a question from before; and if not cannot be since she retracted; and Tosafot's question too is not such strong proof for nevertheless they properly challenged from appraising the paper that proves we judge retroactively — for if from now and onward what is the dispute whether her mind is on the paper;
