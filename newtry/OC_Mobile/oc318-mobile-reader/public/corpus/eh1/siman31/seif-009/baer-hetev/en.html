Less than a perutah. Meaning in coin of silver; but worth silver we are concerned lest worth a perutah in Media — Chelkat Mechokek; see Beit Shmuel.
Kiddushin. It implies they are complete kiddushin and if another betrothed her she need not a get from the second; but Ran wrote it is only doubtful kiddushin; and likewise Kesef Mishneh chapter 7 Laws of Marriage. And if he betrothed with less than a perutah and afterward secluded with her — it is like intercourse — Rosh.
That he betrothed. Not in one blow do we strike them. Here with a minor who grew up — they are complete kiddushin. And difficult on Rav Rama who wrote "and likewise a minor," etc. — learn he holds with less than a perutah too they are complete kiddushin; and above siman 28 seif 16 Rama wrote likewise less than a perutah is doubtful kiddushin — see Chelkat Mechokek, Beit Shmuel.
Where there is room to err, etc. Meaning he did not know the betrothal ring was not worth a perutah and in the end it became known to him. See responsum Maimon siman 19; see Beit Shmuel.
