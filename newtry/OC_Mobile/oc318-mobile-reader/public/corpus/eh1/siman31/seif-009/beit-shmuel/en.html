We do not suspect. And in them there is not even doubtful kiddushin as Tosefta wrote; even in a place where they betroth and afterward send sivlonot — we rule we are concerned for sivlonot; here we are not concerned for these sivlonot were sent with intent of the first kiddushin; therefore even if they were engaged in that matter we are not concerned; see R' Yehuda siman 405. Even though if he had intercourse we rule he betroths her with the intercourse — Ran wrote every person knows kiddushin less than a perutah and does not know its meaning — therefore in intercourse he does not descend to doubt and betroths as where there will be no prohibition concern in intercourse; but if he sends sivlonot he does not betroth her.
We do not suspect, etc. Also no concern lest worth a perutah elsewhere because with money we are not concerned for this as Beit HaGadol wrote — measure of kiddushin is silver half a barley grain and any silver less than this measure even if worth much in other things — there is no measure of silver Torah requires for kiddushin; and not like Chelkat Mechokek who wrote it discusses coin of silver; and I already wrote we do not betroth with a coin.
He certainly had intercourse for kiddushin. Behold in Gemara chapter HaMedir there are three laws — betrothed conditionally; betrothed less than perutah; betrothed with a loan; and Maggid infers from Rambam chapter 7 law 1 who holds less than perutah and loan betroth her through intercourse certainly; and some say in all three it is doubtful kiddushin; and one should be concerned for their words — so Maggid and Ran; and wonder on Mechaber who ruled plainly like Rambam; and more difficult on Rav Rama who wrote "and likewise a minor," etc. "he certainly had intercourse," etc. — learn he holds with less than perutah like Mechaber he certainly had intercourse for kiddushin; and siman 28 seif 16 Rama wrote likewise less than perutah is doubtful kiddushin; but main view seems in all three she needs a get stringently and kiddushin of another take effect in her; and if he betrothed with less than perutah and afterward secluded with her — it is like intercourse and we say "they are witnesses of seclusion, they are witnesses of intercourse" — so Rosh; and Shakh chapter 2 of Kiddushin.
Where there is room to err. Meaning he did not know the betrothal ring was not worth a perutah and in the end it became known to him; and wrote in responsum Maimoni if after it became known to him it was not worth a perutah he lived with her as a man with his wife and there were no witnesses of seclusion — one can say it is like witnesses of seclusion; and in responsum Rivash siman 113 wrote in name of Ra'ah it is like witnesses of seclusion; and at end of responsum 6 disagrees; and wrote all early authorities wrote witnesses of seclusion are required; see siman 149.
