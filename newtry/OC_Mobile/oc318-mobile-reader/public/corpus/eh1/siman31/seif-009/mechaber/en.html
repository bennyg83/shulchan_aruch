If he betrothed her with money less than the value of a perutah — even if he afterward sent her sivlonot (meaning presents, for it is a term of burden and weight, and "weight" can be found as a term for present, as "and he carried presents") — we do not suspect that he sent those sivlonot for the purpose of betrothal. But if he betrothed her with less than the value of a perutah and afterward came upon her unspecified before witnesses — she needs a get, since he certainly had intercourse for the purpose of betrothal. {Rama: The same law applies with a child who betrothed and then grew up with her — she needs a get, because he certainly had intercourse when he became grown for the purpose of betrothal (Tur siman 46) (responsa of Rosh kelal 35). And only in this type of case because every person knows that kiddushin of a child or with less than a perutah is nothing, and so he had intercourse for the purpose of betrothal; but in a situation where there is room for mistake — new betrothal is needed (responsa Maimoni end of Nashim), as explained in this siman seif 4.}
