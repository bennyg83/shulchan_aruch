If he said to her "Be betrothed to me with these" and she ate them one at a time — those she ate also join to constitute the value of a perutah, since he finished all his words before he gave her anything; therefore as soon as he gave her the first one it reached her hand in the status of betrothal and she was eating her own. And the same rule if he said "with this and with this" and he finished all his words before she accepted them — they all join together even if she ate one at a time. And there is one who disagrees with this.
