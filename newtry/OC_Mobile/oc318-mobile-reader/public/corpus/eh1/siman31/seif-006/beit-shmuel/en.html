And there is one who disagrees. Meaning he finished his words and did not finish the giving — RaMHaH holds we depend everything on finishing his words; and likewise implied from Ran who resolves that some say for one who says "be betrothed to me after thirty days" — see there; and per Tosafot he must finish the giving before eating; and for all if he said "with these" — then speech and giving finished — they join since he did not divide and did not say "with this and with this"; see Shakh 425 who ruled also with "these" if she ate before finishing giving it does not join.
