And half of you with a perutah, etc. Tosafot wrote if he said in language of kiddushin it is certainly kiddushin that spread immediately also on the second half like consecration; and language of kiddushin is language of consecration as written at beginning of Kiddushin; rather the sugya discusses when he said in language of erusin; but Rambam and Tur wrote he said in language of kiddushin — they do not hold like Tosafot. Beit Yosef wrote the reason kiddushin do not spread to all of her because one can say at time he said "be betrothed half of you" he did not want to be betrothed entirely but afterward consented; per this if she said immediately she wants to be betrothed — behold she is betrothed; but in novellae of Maharit wrote the reason since per his speech kiddushin do not spread except through her and it is kiddushin through her and not through him; per this even if she said she wants to be betrothed it is not kiddushin. And know these questions — if he said half of you with half a perutah, etc. — it discusses when he gave the perutah immediately; but if he gave half a perutah and said half of you with half a perutah and then gave half a perutah and said — it is not kiddushin; and likewise half of you with a perutah and half of you with a perutah — also discusses when he gave immediately two perutot as Bach wrote in name of Ritva; also discusses when he said so at once; and Ran wrote he said so within time of speech — implies even if he pauses slightly it helps if it was within time of speech. And at first glance implied from words of Ri'iv I brought nearby even within time of speech does not help; and proof from Gemara that halakhah in Kiddushin — within time of speech is not like speech even though Gemara discusses regarding retraction when wanting to retract from kiddushin even within time of speech cannot retract as written siman 36 — for here it is like retraction that one can say he paused so his words are voided and afterward he returns.
Your two daughters. Tosafot wrote specifically when the daughters are minors and he wants to betroth — then the kiddushin money belongs to their father and it is one gives and one receives; but if they are adults even if their father accepted as their agent the agent is not better than the sender; and if they themselves would receive kiddushin it would not be kiddushin — therefore even if their father receives it is not kiddushin; but if the father is agent for his adult sons — it is kiddushin. In novellae of Maharit wrote specifically when father gives on their behalf the money — then it is one gives; but two who give one perutah — it is not kiddushin. In Gemara Abaye and Rava dispute whether kiddushin not handed over for intercourse are kiddushin; and they wrote there per Rava who holds it is not kiddushin — for the sugya discusses when he clarified his words which of them he betrothed; for otherwise it would be kiddushin not handed over for intercourse and not kiddushin per Rava; however we rule like Abaye that it is kiddushin — if so even if he did not clarify his words it is kiddushin.
With a perutah, etc. It implies if he said with two perutot it is kiddushin; and we say with one perutah he betroths her even though a cow is not acquired with money — nevertheless some say for one who says acquire you and the donkey — it does not help because from Torah money acquires.
