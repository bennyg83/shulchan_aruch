If he said to her "Let half of you be betrothed to me with half a perutah and half of you with half a perutah," or he said to her "half of you with a perutah and half of you with a perutah," or "half of you with a perutah today and half of you with a perutah tomorrow," or "your two halves with a perutah," or if he said to his friend "your two daughters for my two sons with a perutah," or "your daughter is betrothed to me and your cow is sold to me with a perutah," or "your daughter is betrothed to me and your land is sold to me with a perutah" — she is doubtfully betrothed.
