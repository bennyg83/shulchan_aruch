That which we said she is betrothed based on doubt that perhaps this thing is worth a perutah somewhere else — she requires a get to permit her to the world. And if he wants to keep her she needs other kiddushin. And if another betrothed her with complete kiddushin — the first should divorce and the second should marry; but the second should not divorce while the first marries. {Rama: And this is only when the second did not come upon her before the divorce; but if he came upon her she is thereby forbidden to the first (Mordechai first chapter of Kiddushin). And just as she is forbidden to the husband so she is forbidden to the one who had relations (and see below siman 46 seif 5).} And some say that if she bore a child from the second and the first had not divorced — the child is kosher.
