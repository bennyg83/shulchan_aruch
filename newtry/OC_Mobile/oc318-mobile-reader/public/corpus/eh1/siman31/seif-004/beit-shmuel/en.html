She needs other kiddushin. At first glance it seems even if it is known worth a perutah elsewhere she needs other kiddushin since per most poskim it is only rabbinic kiddushin; however one can say she need not other kiddushin like kiddushin of a minor that is only rabbinic and need not other kiddushin — rather through intercourse he should intend for kiddushin; so too Bach when known worth a perutah elsewhere need not other kiddushin; see Beit Yosef end siman 44.
Complete kiddushin. And if the second also betrothed with the same thing less than a perutah — she is permitted to the first and need not a get from the second in any case if worth a perutah in some place behold she is betrothed to the first; see siman 16. And if the second betrothed with another thing not worth a perutah — she needs a get even from the second; one can say of the first there is no perutah and of the second perutah in some place; and Chelkat Mechokek wrote she is permitted to the first after divorce of the second as written siman 46.
But the second should not divorce, etc. At first glance it seems to these poskim who hold it is only rabbinic kiddushin — the second divorces and the first marries as written siman 10 — see there. However one can say here there is concern lest they say in a place where it is worth a perutah he returns his divorcee — and then in that place it is kiddushin from Torah.
And just as she is forbidden to the husband, etc. There Maharik wrote even if she married per instruction of a sage she is forbidden to this one and that one; see Maharik shoresh 131 151 and in Knesset HaGedolah chapter 10 hagahah brought his words and wrote if one betrothed with doubtful kiddushin and married another — she need not leave and the child is kosher for she is in presumption of permissibility; unlike doubtful divorcee. And some say for two and two — we rule she leaves as written siman 47 that two and two differ from doubtful judgment. And must say this doubt lest worth a perutah also is not like doubtful kiddushin; and at first glance it seems he disagrees with Rashba who wrote whoever married per mistaken court ruling is permitted to her husband as written end siman 17; however Rashba's law in our case is not necessary as written there — Rashba also resolved there other resolutions.
The child is kosher. And the second should remove her — Tur in name of RaMHaH; specifically in such a case where it is only a general concern lest worth a perutah elsewhere; but if a rumor went out that it is worth a perutah — then the children are doubtful mamzerim as Shakh wrote; so too Bach and Derishah per RaMHaH that Tur brought; and what Chelkat Mechokek challenged from sugya on RaMHaH — not because he holds like Shmuel, etc.; see sugya and it seems RaMHaH derives his law from this sugya; and explanation "not because they hold like Shmuel" — to say there is concern on the child; rather he holds like Abaye and Rava and there is concern because of the rumor. And that he wrote view of RaMHaH in name of "some say" — because Beit Yosef wrote RaMHaH holds like R' Yehuda that this concern is only rabbinic; but per views of Rambam where there is Torah concern — one can say the children are invalid. And I already wrote most poskim hold it is only rabbinic; also per view of Maggid Rambam also holds only rabbinic. Also it seems even if there were Torah concern — that is where it is known worth a perutah elsewhere; but if unknown and it is only concern lest worth a perutah elsewhere — for all there is no Torah prohibition and the children are kosher; for even if known worth a perutah — nevertheless at time of kiddushin witnesses did not know. RaMHaH wrote if we do not know if it was worth a perutah at time of kiddushin — we follow the present; if now worth a perutah we say presumably it was worth a perutah at time of kiddushin; and likewise the reverse as Beit HaGadol wrote — see there.
