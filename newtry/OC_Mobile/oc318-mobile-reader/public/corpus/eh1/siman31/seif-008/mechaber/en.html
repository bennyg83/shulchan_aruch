One who says to a woman "Be betrothed to half of me" — she is betrothed. What is this similar to? To one who says to her "Be my wife — you and another," since it turns out she only has half a man. But if he said "Half of you is betrothed to me" — she is not betrothed, as one woman is not available for two. And similarly one who says "You are hereby betrothed to me and to this man" — she is not betrothed.
