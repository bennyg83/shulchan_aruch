(ב) And there is one who says, etc. This is the reading of Tosafot and the Rosh above. The difficulty of Ritva and Ran was already avoided by Tosafot there, who wrote in the above dibbur hamatchil that this was said only, etc. There is proof from the Yerushalmi: R. Yirmiyah asked before R. Zeira, if he wrote to her, "I have no claim or dealings with your property," and property then fell to her afterward, what is the law? Can a person stipulate about something not yet in his possession? Ramban answered that there it speaks where he wrote it to her after marriage; so too the Rosh wrote there.
