He stipulated with her that he not inherit her — he does not inherit her but eats produce during her lifetime. And likewise if he stipulated with her that if she dies without children the property returns to her father's house — all stands. When does this apply? When he stipulated with her before she marries while she is betrothed, or when writing for her in her ketubah at the time of entry. But if he stipulated with her after she married — his stipulation is void and he inherits her. {Rama: And see above siman 69. And that a stipulation after she married is not effective is specifically when he said he will not inherit her; nevertheless if he obligated himself to return to her heirs what he inherits from her — he must fulfill (Ran beginning of HaKotev in name of Ramban and Rivash simanim 64 and 102).}
