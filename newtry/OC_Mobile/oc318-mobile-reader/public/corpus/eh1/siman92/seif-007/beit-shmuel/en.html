In the Mishnah it is stated that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel holds the condition is void because he stipulated against what is written in the Torah, and Rav ruled like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, but not for his reason; rather, inheritance is rabbinic, and Chazal strengthened it like Torah law. The dispute speaks where he stipulated before marriage, as written in Maggid Mishneh, chapter 23, halachah 1. Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Ramban, Rashba, and Ran ruled that the condition stands, for we hold like R. Yehudah, who says that a monetary condition stands even regarding Torah law. From what was written, there is difficulty for Maggid Mishneh in chapter 12: regarding one who stipulated to reduce the ketubah, the condition is void. Maggid Mishneh wrote that the halachah follows R. Meir, who says the condition is void, though not for his reason, for he holds ketubah is biblical, but rather because Chazal strengthened it like Torah law. Yet even regarding Torah law a monetary condition stands.
