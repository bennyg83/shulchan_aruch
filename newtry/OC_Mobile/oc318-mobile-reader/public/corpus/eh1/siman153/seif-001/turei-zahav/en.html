(א) " behold you divorced me she is believed what he wrote Rosh lamp ' get if ' ' see ".
(ב) Even if she does not say: a hole, etc. Maharshal, z"l, wrote: this is very astonishing. What do simanim help? It was in her hand for several days and she recognized it; what proof is there that she did not return it to him? There is also a difficulty: above he wrote, for example, that she says there is a hole next to a certain letter, which is a definite siman, and this is against the Rosh's view in Bava Metzia chapter 2. Therefore it appears that what he wrote, "he returns it to her," means by force of the simanim, because we establish it on the chazakah that it fell from her, since it is not at all common for her to return the get, for she needs it as proof; and he should have written a receipt or written on the get, "this get we tore," as stated in Bava Metzia chapter 1, folio 18. What he wrote afterward, "even if she did not give definite simanim," refers back to what he wrote above, wherever the woman says she was divorced and he contradicts her.
(ג) Only with other, etc. Meaning "definite." In Ashri he wrote as follows: we do not require a definite siman, for there is no concern here that it was written for another woman, since it came into her hand before it fell, etc. And if the concern is that she will marry with this get, which is a Torah prohibition, even without a get a woman who says to her husband, "you divorced me," is believed, etc. If so, it is difficult why the Tur wrote "with other definite simanim." In truth, a definite siman is not needed. One must say that "definite" used by the Tur does not mean truly definite, but excludes something that is not definite at all. In some books the word "definite" is not mentioned in the Tur. Still, what the Rosh wrote, to be lenient because she is believed to say "you divorced me," is difficult: why is it written nearby that if she does not provide a siman, it should be given neither to her nor to him? Further, the reason for her credibility is that she is not brazen before her husband, and here there is no husband before her.
