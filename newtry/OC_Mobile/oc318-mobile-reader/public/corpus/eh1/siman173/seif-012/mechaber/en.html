The wife of a brother who did not live at the same time is considered as the wife of a brother in the absence of any mitzva (of yibbum), and she also relieves her co-wife from any levirate bond. How? Reuven passed away, and his wife fell to Shimon in levirate marraige, and Levi was subsequently born (after Reuven's death), whether before or after the yibbum, and then Shimon died, she (Reuven's wife) is forever forbidden to Levi. If Shimon had another wife, she is also released from chalitzah and from yibbum, and in turn frees her co-wife and her co-wife's co-wife and so on, as is the law with any other co-wife of a woman forbidden to undergo yibbum because of ervah. However, if Shimon died even before he gave her "maamar" (money showing intent to consummate yibbum), the wife of Shimon undergoes chalitzah but may not undergo yibbum to Levi, because we have establisehd that there is a levirate bond even when he did not give maamar, and therefore there is still an element of being the co-wife to the widow of a brother (Reuven) who was not in the same world (did not live at the same time).
