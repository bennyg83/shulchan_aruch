A female obligated in Yibum (yibbum) who is a forbidden sexual partner (lit. nakedness) to a male obligated in Yibum with a prohibition of excommunication, except for a menstruating woman who has not ritually purified herself, are not in need of performing Yibum, and are permitted to someone else (lit. stranger) without chalitzah (ceremony releasing woman from obligation to marry brother-in-law), and other wives in her widow's marriage are exempt from Yibum and chalitzah. And if he had another brother, and one of his other wives performed Yibum to the same brother, and this brother has other wives, and dies, all of them are exempt from chalitzah and Yibum. And if he had yet another brother, and one of these wives had performed Yibum to him, and this brother has other wives, and dies, they are all exempt from chalitzah and from Yibum. And so unto forever.
