Seif 1 — a woman may, etc. NB see Beit Shmuel s.k. 1 what he wrote: it appears to him if she revealed her mind she wishes to be betrothed and one came and betrothed her — she is betrothed — with God's help examine. It appears difficult from Gemara Makkot 16a which asks: if he betrothed to another, or made her agent — she cancels; if he did not make her agent — what did he do? And what is the question — say it discusses where she only revealed her mind she desires this and he himself went and betrothed her — then she did not cancel, for she did not make agent; only he cancelled from himself. However if we say he acquires because of agency — one can say since ultimately it only helps because we descend to her ultimate intent as though she explicitly made agent — then cancellation is from her. But if we say he acquires not because of agency — then the question is fine that he cancels for her; and it will be proven not per Beit Shmuel's view — examine and difficult.
