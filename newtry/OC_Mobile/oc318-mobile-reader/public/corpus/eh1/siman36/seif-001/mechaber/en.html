All laws of agency for a woman. It contains 12 seifim. A woman may appoint an agent to receive her kiddushin. It is a mitzvah that she be betrothed by herself if she can; but there is no prohibition, as with a man. And the man says to the agent: "Behold so-and-so whom you sent is betrothed to me," and the agent says "I betrothed her to you." If he said "yes" — sufficient; and even if he was silent, if they were engaged in that matter and he gave to the agent and did not explain and said nothing — sufficient. If he betroths her with a document he does not write it except with the agent's knowledge. Some say he does not write it except with her knowledge.
