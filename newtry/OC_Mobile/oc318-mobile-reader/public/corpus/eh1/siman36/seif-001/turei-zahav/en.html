As there is in a man. Meaning: in a man there is prohibition via agent because lest he see something unseemly and she become repugnant; unlike a woman who in any case is agreeable — "better to sit as two than alone."
And if he said yes. Meaning the agent did not answer the betrother "I betrothed her to you" but answered yes — sufficient; and even if agent was completely silent and they were engaged, etc., and betrother said no kiddushin words — sufficient.
