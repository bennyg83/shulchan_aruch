A woman who is an agent to receive kiddushin for her friend — when he gave the kiddushin he said to her "and you too," or "thus both of you are betrothed" — but if he did not say to her except "and you" — this one who received the kiddushin is doubtfully betrothed, lest he intended only to see what is in her heart, as though he said to her "and you — what do you say about this matter?" Therefore she received the kiddushin — for she is still being asked to see what is in her heart.
