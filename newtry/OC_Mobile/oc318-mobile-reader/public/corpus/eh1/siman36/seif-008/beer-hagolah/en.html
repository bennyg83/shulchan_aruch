Statement of Rav Shmuel bar Rav Yehudah from the mishnah, etc. there 52a; from incident of five women there mishnah 50b.
So explained the Rosh there with her — he challenged that this is no novelty since he made her an agent and she agreed to be agent, etc., even though she was made a rival to her; and Rashi explained we are taught even if she accepted without specification, etc.
