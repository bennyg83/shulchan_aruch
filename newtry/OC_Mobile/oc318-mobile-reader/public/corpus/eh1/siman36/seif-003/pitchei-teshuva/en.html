Who betroths both. See Beit Meir who wrote it appears a married woman cannot be shaliach kabbalah for her friend to receive kiddushin from her husband; even though here we hold she becomes agent even where made rival — meaning betrothing both — one can say since she acquires for herself, etc.; even if this one is already betrothed, nevertheless she can receive kiddushin from her husband for her friend since she is only betrothed and her hand is not like her husband's hand; unlike if already married — her hand is husband's hand — money does not leave his possession; as we hold Chelkat Mechokek siman 243 a person cannot acquire via his wife for another. Perhaps one can distinguish: specifically regarding benefiting without acquirer's knowledge he cannot; but regarding becoming agent to receive — preferable; this is difficult, not reasonable to distinguish thus — see there.
