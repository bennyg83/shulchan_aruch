And healed her. Even if healed before husband knew — nevertheless not betrothed; even in vows permission before husband knew helps because blemishes are repugnant in his eyes even after healed. If blemishes were before kiddushin and healed — betrothed for he did not stipulate on this — Chelkat Mechokek. If leper before kiddushin or epileptic from childhood — possibly blemish, concern lest return leper and epileptic under him — Chelkat Mechokek siman 116. Tosafot wrote: do not say healing does not help because condition language was on condition no blemishes and there were — if so when he stipulated on condition none in him why healing helps? Rather reason is repugnance; in man when healed not repugnant in her eyes. We hear from this: even condition she stipulated with him — if condition not fulfilled kiddushin void; only when healed we say condition fulfilled. If he stipulated on condition there will not be blemishes — even if healed it is as condition not fulfilled. If blemishes in him, known to her, afterward healed — doubtful in Chelkat Mechokek whether kiddushin void. It appears proof kiddushin void: sugya challenged and taught "go to sage to permit, go to doctor and heal — not betrothed"; and did not resolve it discusses already known to him — therefore must establish after known; if so difficult from end: taught "he went and vow permitted or healed — betrothed"; and after known to her does not help if healed. However one can say that which halachah not established discusses after known — regarding doctor and heal not betrothed even if not known — we learn regarding sage too discusses not known. If she stipulated on condition no vow in him and vows found — not betrothed even if sage permitted after known to her; for vows not from repugnance but reason: when he betrothed and stipulated on condition no vows and vows found and known — sage's permission does not help because he thinks perhaps no permission for vow as Tosafot and Rosh; if so even if she stipulated law is same — for I already wrote whenever condition not fulfilled even if she stipulated kiddushin void.
