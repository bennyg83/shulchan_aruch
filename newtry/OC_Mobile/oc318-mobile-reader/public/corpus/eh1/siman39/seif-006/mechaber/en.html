He betrothed her on condition there are no blemishes in her, blemishes were in her and she went to a doctor and he healed her — she is not betrothed. But if the woman stipulated (on condition) there are no blemishes in him, and blemishes were in him and he went to a doctor and healed him — she is betrothed, for there is no disgrace to a man in blemishes already healed, and a woman is not particular about this.
