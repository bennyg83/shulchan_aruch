So Tosafot, Tur, Ri'az; Yerushalmi does not dispute this as written nearby; and so Rosh's view — therefore Beit Yosef brought Yerushalmi and Tosafot adjacent, not like Beit Yosef. But Ran is stringent and holds permission helps even after husband knew; even husband's forgiveness does not help after he was particular in first chapter; nevertheless sage's permission is different — uproots vow from root as though she never vowed.
Important woman. Meaning when she says "I do not want a woman who vows" — for Rava's answer it is agreeable to him that kiddushin take effect at all with important woman, for where he need not divorce and his relatives not forbidden — this is meaning of important woman. For Rava's answer there even important woman is betrothed; Geonim doubt whether halachah like Rava against Rabbah who is later, or like Rabbah who is his teacher — therefore with important woman she is doubtfully betrothed. However it appears from Rosh, Tur, Ri'az they hold main view is like Rava, only stringently ruled like Rabbah; if so if he betrothed afterward she is betrothed to second biblically — as proven first chapter Bava Metzia where Rosh ruled like Abaye against Rabbah regarding signed witnesses benefit him; if so what is written here doubtfully betrothed — this is Ran as Ran wrote in name of Ramban; for Rosh it is not doubtful kiddushin. See Shitah Mekubetzet 174d: even non-important woman is doubtful kiddushin for halachah is doubtful; one cannot understand the doubt in non-important woman — perhaps it discusses permission after husband knew and doubtful in Ran's reasoning.
And husband unspecified. I already wrote previous siman these laws; there are two reasons: first reason — he betroths at time of bringing in — therefore requires witnesses of seclusion; second reason — he forgives the condition and first kiddushin take effect and other kiddushin not needed and witnesses of seclusion not needed. Rambam holds second reason; Tur holds first reason and requires witnesses of seclusion. Below explained: if he did not stipulate and betrothed and vows or blemishes found; likewise if betrothed and married without specification — she needs get from doubt and has no ketubah; if he stayed with her she has ketubah. If he said to her: since first ketubah is void how can she collect this ketubah? Say his intent: if he will not be particular afterward her ketubah stands as previous siman. Likewise if stayed with her afterward — certainly betrothed and certainly needs get — from reason when he stipulated nothing it is doubt whether he is particular; if stayed afterward it is clarified he is not particular — certainly betrothed.
