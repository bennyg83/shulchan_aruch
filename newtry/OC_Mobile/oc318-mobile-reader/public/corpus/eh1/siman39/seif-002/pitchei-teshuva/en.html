Disqualifying in kohanim. See responsum Noda BiYehudah siman 53: asked regarding matched woman who due to illness lost sense of hearing — can groom cancel match since all disqualifying in kohanim disqualify in women; in kohanim we said chapter 7 Bechorot deaf-mute — doubt if this deaf specifically does not hear and does not speak like ordinary deaf, or even does not hear alone considered blemish. Wrote from mishnah language implies means does not hear alone for we said both in person and animal; though can reject — all this in kohanim; in women no doubt this is great blemish. What they did not count among additional in women because there discusses she had before kiddushin — otherwise his field washed away; if at kiddushin she was deaf how betrothed unless needed hints — then he saw and was appeased; unless say betrothed via her agent or minor via father — not common; but in matchmaking where no field washed away — simple no greater blemish; can cancel match even if not actually deaf only needs shout loudly — considered blemish for when speaks on intimacy matters neighbors will hear. All per circumstance: if can hear raised quiet voice not heard room to outside — not blemish; if must speak very loud voice — certainly blemish in woman; can cancel without fine; even if seized does not help — no doubt see there; see Yad HaMelech chapter 7 Laws of Forbidden Relations law 10 what he wrote; see responsum Chut HaMeshulash siman 220: one wished to cancel match because bride has nose longer than proper — size of his small finger as explained chapter Eleh Mumim. Wrote: appears if elongation not extreme so majority do not mock — his words nothing, etc. Clear from his words: without this matchmaker explicitly stipulating no blemish — even if said explicitly no deficiency — no equal doubt and certainly obligated fine if wishes retract; if stipulated explicitly no blemish — doubtful and do not extract money from doubt; if groom has groom's money can seize fine money (wrote there also regarding kiddushin similarly doubtful if we say with stipulation not betrothed with slightly long nose not mocked) — clear there. Wrote further: likewise reverse incident — groom known bride has blemish — eyes very closed so to see opposite must force open eyes with effort; groom left court scot-free; though not found this blemish in kohanim for not blind nor small eyes like goose — nevertheless among people great blemish and great change, etc.; in omissions wrote likewise if found in woman tataranit (illness cannot smell) — though not blemish in kohanim — in woman blemish for must deal with dishes and taste, etc.; so proven Gemara Bava Batra 146; however per Rashi there no proof see there. Per Beit Shmuel below siman 90 s.k. 22 wrote we do not find tataranit is blemish, etc. see there.
