If she had vows and went to a sage and he permitted them — she is betrothed; even if she is an important woman. When do these words apply? When the sage permitted before the husband knew. But if they became known to the husband before he permitted — the kiddushin are void. Some say if she is an important woman, even if the sage permitted before the husband knew — she is doubtfully betrothed. (And if he betrothed on condition she has no vows and the husband is unspecified — see siman 38 seif 35.)
