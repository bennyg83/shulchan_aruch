One who betroths a woman on condition she has no vows or blemishes, and who must bring proof if they are found. It contains 7 seifim. One who betroths a woman on condition she has no vows, and one of these three vows is found on her — which people are accustomed to be particular about: that she not eat meat, or not drink wine, or not adorn herself with colored cosmetics — she is not betrothed (and does not need a get) (Tur). The same applies to vows between them and her, which are included in vows people are particular about; if a vow besides these is found on her, even if he says "I am particular even about this" — she is betrothed. If he said to her on condition you have no vow at all, even if it is found she vowed not to eat carobs — she is not betrothed. Some say we are concerned lest she go to a sage and he permit it for her (and therefore she is forbidden to marry without a get).
