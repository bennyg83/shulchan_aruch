One of three vows. Even though he can annul her vow under him — all that is a bit of affliction of soul — for it is not proper she vow under him; but in her vow before entering his domain he is particular only in things she fasts much and is degraded by them — Ran.
She is not betrothed. If he wishes to forgive — explained in previous siman kiddushin are void retroactively and other kiddushin are not needed. What is written siman 60 that he must betroth anew — discusses mistaken kiddushin of another type; or discusses particular when he heard — forgiveness does not help, as written there in name of Ran.
And the same for vows between them. Chelkat Mechokek wrote: not all vows between them explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 234, but great matters such as "I am removed from Jews" and similar that most people are particular about — and so Ran.
And some say, etc. In Yerushalmi there is a dispute; Rashba and Ran ruled she may marry and we are not concerned lest she go to permit, for she does not ruin herself to make mamzerim; Ri'az ruled stringently we are concerned lest she go to permit. Even though below siman 143 in condition "return and do not do" we do not say she will transgress the condition — for certainly she will not transgress to ruin herself; only here we are concerned lest she not want the vow on her all her days and go to permit — ruin comes even if his intent is only for mitzvah, not like Chelkat Mechokek who entered forcedly. However difficult: this concern before husband knew — afterward sage's permission does not help as written nearby; if so why need reason she will go to sage? Say lest husband forgive and not be particular — then kiddushin void retroactively as previous siman he can forgive even on condition without money. Perhaps from this Ran inferred Yerushalmi disputes Tosafot and Yerushalmi holds even after husband knew sage's permission helps; thus Yerushalmi discusses even when husband knew — nevertheless need reason lest she go to permit. However in Ri'az it implies he holds Yerushalmi does not dispute Tosafot; Yerushalmi discusses when husband did not know; nevertheless we do not say lest he not be particular — presumption is to be particular; and he explicitly stipulated — also not concerned lest he forgive intending to ruin them.
