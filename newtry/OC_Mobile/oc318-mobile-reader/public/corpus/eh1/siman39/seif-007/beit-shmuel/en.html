On condition there will not be blemishes, etc. Implies he is not particular about this blemish now but that he not leave a blemish in her afterward; even though Tosafot, Rosh, and other poskim wrote particularity is not in language — Tosafot's intent everything depends on fulfillment of condition as written nearby; when he stipulated on condition none in her and she healed — condition not fulfilled; in such case condition fulfilled — not like Beit Yosef who brought Tosafot dispute this law; see Bedek HaBayit what he wrote. All this per our text; but Beit Chadash emends Tur. However even per Beit Chadash's text this law seems true per what is written in such case condition fulfilled; and Chelkat Mechokek wrote it is doubtful kiddushin.
And doctor healed them. Possible even after known to him it helps; here if healed condition fulfilled that he not leave in her; here not relevant to say repugnant in his eyes for he is not particular except not to leave; however one can say when known he thought no cure and it is like vows when known permission does not help because he thinks no permission as written nearby.
