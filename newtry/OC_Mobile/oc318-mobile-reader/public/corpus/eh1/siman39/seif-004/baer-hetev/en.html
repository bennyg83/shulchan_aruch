Woman who always urinates in bed, or leper — Beit Yosef ruled blemish; in Bedek HaBayit left difficult; Taz wrote must give ketubah but can divorce against her will even nowadays cherem Rabbenu Gershom; see below siman 117 seif 5. Man urinating in bed wrote in Be'er Sheva 172b not blemish — see Hagahot Isserles. If she has pimples on head — if many considered blemishes; if few and small possibly women's way and not considered blemish — Ranach chapter 1 siman 102.

He brought: if he did not stipulate at all and blemish found after matchmaking — can cancel matchmaking exempt without fine; for if found after nissuin need not give ketubah — if so after matchmaking exempt without fine.
