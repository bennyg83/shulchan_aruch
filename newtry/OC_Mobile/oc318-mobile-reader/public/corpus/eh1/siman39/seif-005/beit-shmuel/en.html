Woman without specification. If she is ailonit — certainly no get needed as Tosafot wrote there; see siman 173.
Doubtfully betrothed. Chelkat Mechokek wrote: discusses immediately when he sees blemish he is particular and does not want her; but if silent when blemish known to him — even if screamed afterward — kiddushin are certain; likewise if betrothed without specification and married and had relations without specification — betrothed and no ketubah. In Gemara per Rabbenu Chananel she needs get from their words; per Rava it is doubtful kiddushin; Rambam and Tur ruled like Rava; not like Beit Chadash who wrote Tur's words doubtful kiddushin because he doubted whether halachah like Rabbenu Chananel or Rava — rejected.
