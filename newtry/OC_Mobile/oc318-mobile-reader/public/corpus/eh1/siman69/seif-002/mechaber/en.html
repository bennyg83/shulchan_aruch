These are the ten: her sustenance, her clothing, her marital duty, the principal ketubah, her medical care, to redeem her if captured, her burial, to be sustained from his property, to dwell in his house after his death for all her widowhood, and for his daughters to be sustained after his death until they are betrothed; and for his sons from him to inherit her ketubah in addition to their share in the inheritance with their brothers.
