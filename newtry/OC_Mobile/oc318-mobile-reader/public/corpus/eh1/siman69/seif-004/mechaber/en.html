Her handiwork is against her sustenance; therefore if she said "I will not be sustained and I will not work" — we listen to her. {Rama: And see above siman 80 seif 11 that any woman who says "I will not be sustained and I will not work" — the enactment is voided and she cannot retract and say "I will be sustained and I will work" (Beit Yosef in name of Ran who so wrote in name of Ra'ah). And some dispute (R' Yehuda of Corbeil). And whoever is not sustained has no clothing, for it is included in sustenance (there in Ran). But a husband who said "I will not sustain you and I will not take your handiwork" — we do not listen to him (but he may say "take your handiwork for your sustenance, and what is insufficient I will complete for you") (Ran chapter HaMedir).}
