(ב) And she cannot retract and say, etc. The reason is explained in the Ran: it is not proper that she be able to change each time she wishes, so that when she finds work she says, "I will not be sustained and I will not work," and when she does not find work she says, "I want sustenance and I am prepared to work." The dissenting view is that of Ri"v, who wrote that since we hold that sustenance is primary, it is proper that she have the upper hand, for there is a fixed weekly measure of how much she must produce. Therefore the choice is hers: she may say, "This week I will work for myself," and in another week say, "Give me sustenance and I am prepared to perform the fixed quota." Thus according to the Ran she cannot say for a fixed period that she will work for herself, while according to Ri"v she can say so. At first glance it appears that this dispute depends on the dispute whether clothing too is in exchange for her handiwork. The Ran follows his own view that clothing is also in exchange for her handiwork, and therefore he holds that she cannot say, "This week I will not be sustained, and another week I will be sustained." Ri"v follows the view of the Rosh that clothing is not included in sustenance. For if clothing were included in sustenance, the enactment of Chazal would have made all her handiwork for all her life in exchange for sustenance and clothing; clothing is not something divided week by week. If so, how could she say, "For a fixed period I will work for myself," and at another time, "I will work and eat"? What would be done about clothing? Similar to this, Tosafot in Perek Na'arah Shenitpatet wrote that the enactment of Chazal made the produce of all her life in exchange for her redemption; if she once says, "I will not be redeemed and I will not give produce," she can no longer say, "I will be redeemed and I will give the produce." Tosafot on 46 and the Rosh imply that they hold like Ri"v, for they wrote that if she says, "I will not be sustained today and I will not work," tomorrow he must sustain her and she works.
