Marital duty. Ba'er Hetev: but with kinyan nothing follows kinyan, Sma siman 22. See Beit Meir who wondered (Sma wrote this on another matter not here) — for marital duty there is no kinyan and it is less than kinyan; regarding ketubah licentious intercourse and inheritance Chelkat Mechokek s.k. 12 wrote even if they acquired from him like inheritance of his father. But see Rashba responsum in Beit Yosef end of siman 118: if they acquired from him to give heirs what she brought if she dies in his lifetime it helps even after marriage — this obligates himself to give, not dependent on inheritance law. Nevertheless Rashba per his view "I give" kinyan helps as explained Beit Yosef Choshen Mishpat siman 295; but for us kinyan must be language of obligation; but removing inheritance is clear even with kinyan ineffective after marriage.
