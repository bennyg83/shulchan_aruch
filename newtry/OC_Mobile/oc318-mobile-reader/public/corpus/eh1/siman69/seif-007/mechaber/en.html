What we said — that a condition is ineffective to remove her inheritance — applies to a gift with her after marriage or before betrothal. But a gift with her while she is still betrothed — is effective. {Rama: (And see above siman 92: the Sages said there is no virtuous woman except one who does her husband's will) (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 15 in name of Tanna deVei Eliyahu).}
