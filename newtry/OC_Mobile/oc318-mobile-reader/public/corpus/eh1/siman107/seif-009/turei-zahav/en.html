And after her, to his heirs. In the glosses of Mordekhai to Bava Batra, the reason is that the phrase "and after you" implies that the second recipient has only what the first leaves over. If so, on what does the remainder depend? If she wishes, she may leave nothing. Therefore he must have intended it as a gift; otherwise how could the principal become subordinate and the subordinate become principal? With the wording "after you," the first recipient is primary.
