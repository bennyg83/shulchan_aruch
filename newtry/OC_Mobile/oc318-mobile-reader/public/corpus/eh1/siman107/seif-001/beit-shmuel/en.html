(א) Whether healthy. In the Shas the question was not resolved regarding a healthy man who wrote all his property to his wife, whether his intent was to make her an apotropos, since he is still alive. The practical difference is whether seizure helps, as written in Choshen Mishpat in several places. However, one may say that before they give it to her, they tell her that her seizure is not a seizure, and they give it to her only in the capacity of apotropos. See Choshen Mishpat, end of siman 243.
