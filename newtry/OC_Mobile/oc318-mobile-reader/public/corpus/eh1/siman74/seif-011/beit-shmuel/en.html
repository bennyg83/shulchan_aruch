Konam, the benefit of your marital relations is forbidden to me; he must divorce immediately. This is because we say he hates her. See Yoreh Deah, siman 234, where it is ruled that the husband can annul a vow that she not go to a house of mourning. If so, even if she did not make it dependent on marital relations, he can annul it, and if he did not annul, she should leave immediately. The Taz discusses this. This question is difficult only according to the Taz, who holds that in every vow we say he hates her even without making it dependent on marital relations. According to the Perishah, however, there is no difficulty, because where she vowed forever she need not be divorced if after the get she remains forbidden, since we do not say he hates her where it was not dependent on marital relations. The Shach explains the Perishah and answers with difficulty; he should have resolved the Tur as above.
