One who forbids his wife from several matters. It contains 12 seifim. If he forbade her not to adorn herself and hung it on marital relations — saying konam the enjoyment of your marital relations is upon me if you adorn yourself — she adorns herself immediately and is forbidden in marital relations; and he waits seven days and then divorces and gives ketubah. Some say if he forbade her not to adorn herself — in poverty one year he upholds; more than that he annuls his vow or divorces and gives ketubah; and in wealth thirty days he upholds, more than that he annuls his vow or divorces and gives ketubah.
