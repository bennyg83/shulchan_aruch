He must divorce immediately. The Tur explains that this is specifically where she vowed without specification or for more than a month. But if she vowed for only one month, we do not say he hates her, because she does not suffer from not going to her father for one month, as written above. See Chelkat Mechokek, who wrote that the Mechaber ruled like the Rambam, that whenever she vowed, we do not force him to divorce; rather, it depends on his will. But this does not imply so in Yoreh Deah, siman 235.
