If she vowed not to go to her father's house and hung it on marital relations — saying konam your marital relations upon me if I go to my father's house — if she did not hang on marital relations but said konam my father's house upon me — he cannot annul; but once she hung on marital relations he can annul; and once he heard and did not annul — he divorces immediately and gives ketubah. If he says konam your marital relations upon me if you go to your father's house more than one month if he dwells with her in one city, or more than one festival if he dwells with her in another city — he upholds seven days and then divorces and gives ketubah.
