And likewise she who said: I do not wish that your father and mother and your brothers and sisters come to my house, and I do not dwell with them in one courtyard because they harass me and distress me — they listen to her. {Rama: And this is specifically when it appears in beit din that there is substance to her words that they harass her and cause quarrel between her and her husband; but without this they do not listen to her, for the dwelling is not hers but only her husband's (Maggid Mishneh ch. 13 Laws of Marriage); and it is customary to seat with them a trustworthy man or woman and she dwells with them until it becomes clear through whom the quarrel and dispute arose.}
