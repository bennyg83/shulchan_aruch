More than a month. If she made it dependent on marital relations, it is as though she vowed from marital relations. Tosafot at the beginning of Perek HaMadir imply that when support is made dependent on marital relations, one might wait thirty days and then seven days. The Tur, however, writes here that she waits only seven days. The Perishah distinguishes between a vow of benefit, which can apply to everyone, and a vow not to go to her father’s house, which has no independent vow without the marital-relations prohibition. The Taz gives another reason, but it is difficult. If he said, "marital relations are forbidden to me from now if you go to your father’s house," then even if it was only one month it is like a vow from marital relations, because she will not be careful with the condition. If she vowed, "marital benefit is forbidden to me from now if I go to my father’s house for one month," it requires study whether we say he hates her, since by law she is forbidden in marital relations even though she can avoid going.
