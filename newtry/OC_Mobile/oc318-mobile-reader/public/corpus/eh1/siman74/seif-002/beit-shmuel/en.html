More than this. This means that if he said, "Konam, the benefit of your marital relations is forbidden to me if you adorn yourself for more than a year," it is as though she vowed from marital relations, and she waits only seven days. We do not say she must wait a year and seven days. Although Tosafot imply that she waits the measure of adornment and then the measure of marital relations, the Tur and Mechaber did not rule so. For nearby he writes that if she vowed not to go to her father’s house for more than a month and made it dependent on marital relations, she waits only seven days, and we do not say she waits one month and then seven days. The Chelkat Mechokek wrote that she waits both measures, but he overlooked that the Tur and Mechaber did not rule like Tosafot here. There is no distinction between an undefined vow and an explicitly long vow, because an undefined prohibition is like specifying her whole life. If she vowed his marital benefit if she adorns herself without specifying time, it is as though she specified her whole life. Thus here it speaks of a poor woman where she specified one year; therefore he may maintain it. "More than this" means where she specified more than a year, or made an undefined vow, which is like more than a year; then she waits only the measure of the marital-relations vow.
