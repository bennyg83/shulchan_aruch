He must divorce immediately. If he made the vow, the reason is that it creates a bad reputation for her. If she made the vow and made it dependent on marital relations, he could annul it, and since he did not, we say he hates her; therefore he must divorce her immediately and give the ketubah. Since he was able to annul it, it is also clear that the Mechaber holds he is obligated to divorce her, for he grouped both clauses together. When he forbade it, he must divorce her; the same follows when she vowed, because the reason is that he hates her. If she forbade it and made it dependent on marital relations, it is like she vowed from marital relations.
