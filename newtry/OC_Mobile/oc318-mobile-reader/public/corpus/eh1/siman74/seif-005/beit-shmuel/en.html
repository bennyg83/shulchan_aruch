He must divorce. This means immediately, because she vowed without specification. If a poor woman vowed regarding adornment for one year, which she can tolerate according to the Rambam, and made it dependent on marital relations, then according to everyone we do not say he hates her. Whenever she can tolerate the vow, even if made dependent on marital relations and he maintained it, we do not say he hates her, just as where she vowed not to go to her father’s house for one month and made it dependent on marital relations. So too in all vows. If she said, "the benefit of marital relations is forbidden to me for one week if I adorn myself," and he maintained it, even though adornment itself cannot be tolerated, she can tolerate the vow. The reverse is also true where the vow is about fruit, which cannot be tolerated even one day, but the condition is marital relations for one week; the condition can be fulfilled.
