If the husband forbade his neighbor's utensils upon him or his utensils upon his neighbors so she not lend or borrow from them; or she said konam your marital relations upon me if I borrow utensils from my neighbors or lend mine — and he heard and upheld for her — he divorces immediately and gives ketubah. If he said konam your marital relations upon me if you borrow utensils from them or lend yours — it is impossible for her not to borrow from them and not lend hers; and behold she is forbidden immediately and he divorces after seven days and gives ketubah. If she forbade her neighbors' utensils upon herself so she not borrow from them; or forbade upon herself her husband's utensils so she cannot lend them so they not consider it a favor; or vowed not to weave nice garments for his sons — and he cannot annul for it is not matters between them — she is forbidden to weave and to borrow and lend utensils; she leaves without ketubah because she causes him a bad name among neighbors.
