Place it on the rock. Here per Rashi and Rambam sugya discusses she be betrothed through courtyard law if rock is hers; and likewise rock of both resolved in its place if betrothed through courtyard law even though generally we hold partners' courtyard does not acquire from one to another — nevertheless here in kiddushin she has share in courtyard and he wants she acquire — we say courtyard transfers to her; and per Tosafot' view not through courtyard law for partners' courtyard does not help even in kiddushin; rather entire sugya when money afterward came to her hand — then we say when she said place on rock and it is not hers she revealed she does not want kiddushin — even when came to hand like ba mavia; and in rock of both resolved if came to hand; nevertheless also per Tosafot if rock was hers and she stood at rock she is betrothed through courtyard law except sugya does not discuss this; and Rosh wrote entire sugya in give to pauper give to dog also discusses came to hand as written in hagahah nearby; nevertheless appears his intent if she said give to dog in general even if came to hand she is not betrothed; but if she said give to my dog she is betrothed through benefit he gave her dog even if did not come to hand; and likewise if dog ran after her resolved if she is betrothed through that benefit she saves herself — meaning even if did not come to hand she is doubt betrothed; and what is written in hagahah this too discusses he gave afterward into lap — meaning even if gave to lap nevertheless with general dog she is not betrothed and with dog running after her doubt kiddushin; and likewise Choshen Mishpat; and likewise per Rashi and Rambam beginning of sugya from courtyard law and give to my dog perforce not through courtyard but through benefit he gives her dog.
He gave afterward into her lap. In Rosh he did not write he gave to lap but wrote if he threw to lap and she was silent it is not kiddushin since she revealed she does not want kiddushin; nevertheless Rama wrote well even if he gave to her hand it is not kiddushin like saying ba mavia even if he gave to hand in silence it is not kiddushin since she revealed she does not want kiddushin; nevertheless appears there is another distinction if he said at time of giving when he gives to hand be betrothed to me behold she is betrothed like ba mavia as written previous seif; but if he threw to her it is not kiddushin since she revealed she does not want kiddushin and this does not apply to say if it does not please her to cast since she revealed she does not want kiddushin as written siman 28.
