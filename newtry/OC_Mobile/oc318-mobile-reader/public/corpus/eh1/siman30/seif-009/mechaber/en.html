"Be betrothed to me with a maneh" and she said to him "Place it on the rock" — she is not betrothed; and if the rock was hers she is betrothed {and some say specifically when she designated the rock to him such as she said "Give on rock so-and-so" or "on my rock"; but if she said simply "on the rock" and he gave on hers she is not betrothed (Tur in name of RaMAh)}; if rock of both behold she is betrothed in doubt. {Hagahah: And Rosh (Beit Yosef in his name) and Tosafot (Tur in their name) explain all this discusses when afterward he gave into her lap in silence; nevertheless if she said first "Give on rock" she is not betrothed; and on her rock etc.}
