The rock. Shach part 1 siman 252 wrote if rock is close to her four cubits there is concern for kiddushin — see Beit Shmuel s.k. 16. He gave money atop land and took from there — she is not betrothed at all. Teshuvah Maharil siman 74. But Rashdam Choshen Mishpat siman 24 and Raneach part 1 siman 84 and Rosh in his teshuvah siman 22 ruled she is fully betrothed. Maharival part 2 siman 36 wrote he has no power to decide if she is fully betrothed or doubt betrothed. Shach part 1 siman 252 wrote it is doubt betrothed. He threw kiddushin into her hem — it is not like take kiddushin from atop land and she is betrothed — Rashdam Choshen Mishpat siman 86 — see Kenesset HaGedolah folio 61b.
