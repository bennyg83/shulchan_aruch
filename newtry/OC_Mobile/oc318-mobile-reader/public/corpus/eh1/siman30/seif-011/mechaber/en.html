"Be betrothed to me with this ingot" and she said "Give it to this dog" — if the dog was hers behold she is betrothed; if not she is not betrothed; and if it was running after her to bite her behold she is doubt betrothed. {Hagahah: And this too discusses in such case he gave afterward into lap (Tur in name of Tosafot); she said first "Give ingot to dog" or "Give dinar on the rock and I will betroth myself to you" — some say its law is as if she said "Give dinar to so-and-so" (Tur in name of some) and as explained above siman 29 seif 2; and be stringent in the matter (Tur).}
