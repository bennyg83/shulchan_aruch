He said to her "Be betrothed to me with a maneh" — she said "Give it to father" or "to your father" or to so-and-so and they gave to them — even though he said to her "Be betrothed to me with the maneh I gave to them" she is not betrothed; but if she said "Let them receive for me" she is betrothed even if he did not return and say "Be betrothed to me with the maneh I gave to them."
