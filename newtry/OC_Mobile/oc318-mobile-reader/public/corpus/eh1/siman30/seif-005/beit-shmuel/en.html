Betrothed even if he comes afterward into her four cubits. Even though in get invalid in such case for there invalid only from stringency — and all such in kiddushin she is certainly betrothed as written nearby; and if both came as one it is like courtyard of both — see Choshen Mishpat siman 268 there dispute explained whether four cubits acquire while walking; and Choshen Mishpat wrote here in kiddushin be concerned stringently and it is only doubt.
Doubt close to him. Meaning two sets of witnesses disagreeing if close to him or close to her; but if witnesses doubted if close to her — no concern of kiddushin for it is as betrothing without witnesses — Hagahot Alfasi end Gittin; and if one wrote before us and one says close to him — it is rabbinic doubt — Ribash wrote in synagogue it is like alley regarding four cubits; and in Sefer Beit HaBechirah he brings proof it is like partners' courtyard as Gemara Nedarim and Yoreh Deah siman 224 one under vow of benefit law of synagogue as partners of that city; and likewise Maharil in laws of eruvin; and certainly this was not hidden from Ribash except gittin and kiddushin are different for they regularly do in synagogue as written there — therefore he holds it is like alley.
Both cannot guard. Tosafot chapter HaZorek wrote when both cannot guard it is not get at all; and likewise in kiddushin except explanation of what they wrote both cannot guard — meaning each cannot guard alone but both together; see siman 139.
