If they were standing in public domain or in domain that does not belong to both and he threw kiddushin to her — close to him she is not betrothed; close to her behold she is betrothed; half and half; or if they were doubt close to him doubt close to her and they were lost before reaching her hand — behold she is doubt betrothed. How is close to him and close to her? All that he can guard them and she cannot — this is close to him; she can guard them and he cannot — this is close to her; both can guard them or both cannot guard — this is half and half.
