Doubt close to her. Beit Shmuel s.k. 9 wrote meaning two sets of witnesses disagreeing if close to him or close to her; but if witnesses doubted if close to her no concern of kiddushin as betrothing without witnesses. Hagahot Alfasi end Gittin etc. end quote. And in Hagahot Alfasi this is not found only in Mordekhai and they are the matters Choshen Mishpat s.k. 5 brought in name of Mordekhai in name of Maharam but in different formulation. See in Sefer Pei Yesharim on tractate Kiddushin siman 9 who wrote matter needs study etc. see there; also in Sefer Sha'ar HaMelekh chapter 9 Laws of Marriage law 41 wrote even though Rav Beit Shmuel brought Maharam's words for halakhah without dissent nevertheless not decisive matter and many disagree etc. see there; see teshuvah Noda BiYehudah first siman 58 and second siman 77 what he wrote on this matter brought somewhat above siman 28 s.k. see there. See in Sefer Kehilat Yaakov who wrote thus: see in Sefer Nehora HaM'ar in kelalim of seizure what he wrote to settle that do not challenge from all doubt kiddushin she is betrothed from doubt — and one can distinguish between when doubt is for witnesses on matter they did not examine properly and could have seen entire matter but did not see and therefore doubted — in this certainly their testimony does not help for it is half testimony they saw only half of what they could see; unlike when doubt is in matter that even if they wanted to see they could not — then testimony helps for doubt; see Sema siman 30 s.k. 39; and per this it works here in kiddushin doubt close to her is like betrothing without witnesses since they could see entire matter and not proper — half testimony and no testimony at all — know even if other witnesses come who did not see throwing kiddushin only testify they measured kiddushin money testifying on throwing and was certainly close to her she would not be betrothed for half testimony since did not see all they could see; and if so doubt they doubted if close to her is not better than if here other witnesses who do not help since they did not try to see all they could see; unlike regarding other doubt kiddushin such as perhaps worth perutah in utensils since they could not see at time she is here what it is worth in utensils — not half dinar — therefore kiddushin proper; see there well.
