Need not place kiddushin into her hand. And if he said "Take your kiddushin from atop the land" — Shach wrote in teshuvah siman 252 it is doubt; and one can say for deposit already in her hand and now he betroths her with it it is kiddushin even though now he gave her nothing for there initially he gave her and in giving it came to her hand.
Once she wished to throw. It implies he holds like RaMAh that Tur brought siman 28; nevertheless consent is on kiddushin and even if there was no consent on throwing; and likewise Choshen Mishpat and likewise Taz siman 42 and not like Bach; nevertheless if she said "Give kiddushin in my hand" and he threw to her — it is not kiddushin — Taz.
Into her yard. Likewise Rambam; but Tur wrote yard guarded per her knowledge and Beit Yosef challenged why need guarded per her knowledge for in gift Rosh ruled like R' Papa who holds guarding of giver is like guarding of recipient — if so need not be guarded per her knowledge but if guarded per his knowledge suffices; and Mechaber ruled plainly and wrote Rambam's language; and Rambam relied on what is written in chapter 5 Acquisition and holds kiddushin resembles monetary law; and likewise Mechaber relied on what Beit Yosef/Choshen Mishpat wrote and rule is in kiddushin suffices yard guarded per her knowledge even if she does not stand at yard; unlike in gittin she must stand at yard even if yard guarded per her knowledge — I am different in gittin for it is debt to her as written siman 139; and if yard not guarded per her knowledge but per his — depends on dispute of poskim in gift: Rosh holds since another's mind transfers to her she acquires even if not guarded per her knowledge; and for Rif and Rambam even when another's mind transfers yard must be guarded per her knowledge or she stands there — therefore if she does not stand at yard and it is not guarded per her knowledge it is doubt kiddushin; even though Rav Rama ruled in Choshen Mishpat like Rosh — one can say for kiddushin resembling all matters of get it is doubt; and likewise Choshen Mishpat and Taz wrote also Rosh holds here must be guarded per her knowledge because we require her mind relies that kiddushin money be clearly in her hand; and likewise if he gave kiddushin to hand of her minor slave she is not betrothed because she does not rely and fears slave may lose kiddushin.
