And needless to say if she threw in his presence. Meaning if she threw to sea she is liable to pay — teaches she betrothed herself; and from Bach siman 42 it appears if she threw kiddushin and it was in her yard she is betrothed unless she explicitly said she does not want to be betrothed to him; and likewise if he threw into her four cubits if close to her; and likewise Choshen Mishpat; requires examination.
