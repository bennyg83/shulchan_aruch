A rumor that emerges after marriage, or even after erusin, saying she was betrothed to another first — we are not concerned for it. {Rama: Even if the rumor had already spread but was not upheld in beit din until after she was betrothed — we do not investigate the rumor to uphold it (so Beit Yosef wrote in name of Ramban). However, if she admits afterward that she was betrothed to the first — she is forbidden to her husband (so implied from responsum Rosh kelal 35), since the rumor had spread first (R. Nathan gave a praiseworthy reason to distinguish between our case and the case where she said "I am impure," etc.) (and see below end of siman 48).}
