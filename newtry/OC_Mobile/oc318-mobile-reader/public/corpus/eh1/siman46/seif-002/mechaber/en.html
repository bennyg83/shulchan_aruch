And likewise if two came and said, "We saw something like an engagement celebration," or "We heard a rumor," or "We heard from so-and-so who heard from so-and-so that so-and-so was betrothed before so-and-so and so-and-so," and the witnesses went to another country or died — behold this is a rumor that upholds her as betrothed. {Rama: Some say it is not a rumor unless two heard from two, but one from two is not (Tur in name of Rosh, Ran, and Maggid Mishneh in name of Rashba). We are not concerned for a rumor unless it was upheld in beit din — that beit din investigated the matter and heard there is substance to the rumor, as explained; but as long as it was not upheld in beit din we are not concerned, unless it is a place where there is a strong presumption she was betrothed — then we do not require upholding in beit din (Maharik root 87). And beit din may investigate the rumor to clarify whether there is substance (Beit Yosef in name of Tashbetz and Rashba).}
