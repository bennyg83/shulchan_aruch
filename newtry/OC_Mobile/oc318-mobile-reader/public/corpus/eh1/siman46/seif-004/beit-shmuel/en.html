(א) If the matters appear credible to beit din. In Maggid Mishneh it is explained that this refers to a case where a rumor emerged that he threw kiddushin to her, and afterward an explanation emerged that it was closer to him. If it appears to beit din that they may rely on this explanation, she is not established as betrothed. But if the rumor emerged without qualification, that so-and-so betrothed so-and-so, and only afterward an explanation emerged that it was by throwing and was closer to him, we do not rely on this explanation, since the explanation did not emerge immediately together with the rumor; see Chelkat Mechokek. Likewise, if they later said that it was doubtful whether it was closer to him, we do not permit her, since the first rumor was that he threw kiddushin to her without qualification. It also appears that if the first rumor was that he threw kiddushin to her, and afterward they said that he betrothed her conditionally, even if they said the condition was not fulfilled, we do not attribute the matter leniently, because conditional kiddushin was not included in the first rumor that he threw kiddushin to her. Ran wrote that if at first a rumor emerged that he threw kiddushin to her and afterward it became clear that it was closer to him, we permit her; if it was not clarified that it was closer to him, she is forbidden to marry, but if she married she need not leave. It seems that only in such a case she need not leave, because the rumor was that he threw kiddushin to her and one can say it was closer to him. But if the rumor emerged without qualification that she was betrothed, she must leave because of the rumor, as written in seif 5.
