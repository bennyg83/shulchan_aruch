This means she is forbidden to both of them, to the husband and to the paramour, and this is where there were at least witnesses of seclusion, that she secluded herself with the second man. But if there was only chuppah, she is permitted, since the first kiddushin was doubtful. Rashdam, Even HaEzer, siman 22. However, Mahara Sasson, siman 173, wrote that this is not sufficient, even if she was a niddah; rather, only where witnesses came and testified that their hands did not leave their hands and that they were not secluded. Most of the sages of Salonika agreed to this.
