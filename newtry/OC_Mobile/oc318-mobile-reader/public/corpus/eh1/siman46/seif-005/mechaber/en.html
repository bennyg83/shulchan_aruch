A rumor spread that she is betrothed to so-and-so, and another came and betrothed her before us — we examine the first kiddushin that are by rumor. If clear witnesses came with clear sight that she is betrothed to the first — the second kiddushin are nothing. If not — the first, whose kiddushin are by rumor, divorces her, and the second, whose kiddushin are certain, marries her. And if the second divorced her — the first may not marry her, lest people say he remarried his divorcee from betrothal after she was betrothed to another. And if the second married her before the first divorced her — behold she is forbidden to him forever, even after the first divorces her (and see above siman 30 seif 4).
