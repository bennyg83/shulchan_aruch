When does this apply? When there was no excuse (amtlaha). But if there was an excuse and they heard the excuse when they heard she was betrothed — she is not presumed betrothed. What is the excuse? Such as they say so-and-so was betrothed conditionally, or doubtful kiddushin — such as they say he threw kiddushin to her, doubtfully closer to him or closer to her; or if there is room to doubt whether the kiddushin were worth a perutah; or he was a minor — she is not presumed betrothed; rather we ask her and rely on her words, since there is no clear evidence and no strong rumor.
