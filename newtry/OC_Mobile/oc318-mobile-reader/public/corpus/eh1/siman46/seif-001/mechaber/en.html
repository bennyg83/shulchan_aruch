Law of rumor (kola) in kiddushin and whether the rumor is nullified. It contains 8 seifim. An unmarried woman about whom a rumor spread that she was betrothed today to so-and-so in this city — we are concerned for her and behold she is doubtfully betrothed, even though there is no clear evidence. And any rumor that was not upheld in beit din — we are not concerned for it. And what is the rumor that upholds her as betrothed? Such as if two witnesses came and testified that they saw candles burning and beds spread and people entering and leaving and women rejoicing for her and saying so-and-so was betrothed. But if they heard them saying so-and-so shall be betrothed today — we are not concerned for her, lest they happened to arrange betrothal and she was not betrothed. {Rama: And likewise if they said she was betrothed plainly without saying to so-and-so, or they said she was betrothed in another city — it is not called a rumor (Tur).}
