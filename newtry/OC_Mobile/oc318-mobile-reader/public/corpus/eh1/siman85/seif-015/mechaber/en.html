If fruits attached to the ground fell to her — behold they belong to the husband; even if their time to harvest arrived she detaches them from her land and they are sold and land is acquired and he eats fruits. {Rama: This is not so — for all fruits gathered under him are entirely his; and possible an error fell in the books and it is proper to be that detached from the land behold they are hers, and in the future she is (and so is Rambam ch. 22 Laws of Marriage).}
