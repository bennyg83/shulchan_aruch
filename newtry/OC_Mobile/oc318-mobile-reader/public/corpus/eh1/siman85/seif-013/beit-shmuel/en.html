(א) Whose produce is abundant. See the Talmud for several distinctions in this matter. The Rambam included everything in this: one buys something whose produce is abundant and whose expenses are few. Ready money is not left for him to profit with; rather, with it too one buys something whose produce is abundant. The produce from it he may sell and use the money for profit. Responsa Rashba. See Responsa Rashach, part 3, siman 47.
(ב) Only produce. He wrote plainly like the view of the Rif and Rambam, that even if only the milk is hers and not the goat, they do not sell the goat. We are not concerned for death, lest the goat die, even though if produce fell to her, we hold that he sells it. That case is different, because after he eats the produce everything is gone. But here there will be further milk, and likewise the palm tree will produce other fruit.
