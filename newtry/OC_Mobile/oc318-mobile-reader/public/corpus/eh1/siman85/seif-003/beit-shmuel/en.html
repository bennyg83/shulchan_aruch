There is one who says that nichsei tzon barzel, etc. This is also the view of the Rambam. See Taz. The choice is in his hand: if he wants, he does not accept liability for them. See Responsa Rivash, siman 76. If he already accepted liability, he cannot remove himself from it, for his law is like that of a guardian, who cannot retract within the time. Therefore they are called tzon barzel. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 253.
