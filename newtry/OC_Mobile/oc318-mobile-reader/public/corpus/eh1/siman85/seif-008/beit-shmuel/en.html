(א) While she was betrothed. Even if he specified it for her as a gift, whether before marriage or after marriage. Tur.
(ב) If she was widowed from marriage. But if she was widowed from betrothal, she does not receive it, because he wrote it for her only with the intention of marrying her. What he wrote, "not because of her rebellion," is because if it was because of her rebellion, she lost her ketubah and has no gifts. See siman 77.
