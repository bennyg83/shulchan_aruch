If one gave her a gift on condition that her husband has no authority over it — the husband acquired and behold it is like other melog property unless the giver stipulated in the body of the gift that it be for such and such, such as saying to her behold the money is given to you on condition you dress with them or on condition you do what you wish without the husband's permission. {Rama: If her father gave her a gift and stipulated in one of these ways and afterward her father died and she inherits him — the gift is voided and the husband acquired it like other melog property (responsum of Ramban siman 150).}
