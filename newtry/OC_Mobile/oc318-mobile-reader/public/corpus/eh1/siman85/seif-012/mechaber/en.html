If money or movable property was found in the woman's hand and she says they gave me as a gift and he says they are from your handiwork and are mine — she is believed and land is acquired with them and he eats fruits; and he may impose a cherem on whoever claims falsely. And if she said they gave me on this condition that my husband have no authority over them but that I do what I wish with them — she must bring proof. {Rama: And some say that which she is not believed — is specifically with hidden money; but with money that is not hidden she is believed to say they are mine (Rashi and Rosh siman 86). And specifically when she does not carry and give within the house (Beit Yosef in name of Rashbatz; see above siman 86). And some say if they did not see money in her hand and she has a miggo to say she has none or returned it to him — she is believed in every matter (responsum of Maharam siman 200; see Choshen Mishpat siman 62).} And if she said you gave me as a gift — she swears heset that the husband gave them to her and he does not eat their fruits.
