Whether the creditor collects. See Responsa Mahari Weil, siman 78, that he may seize dowry money, if it still exists, for the purpose of her maintenance. The Chelkat Mechokek wrote: if the creditor collects from the produce of nichsei melog, at first glance he does not collect, for he cannot even sell them for the second year from most of it because of household profit. I already wrote this in the name of Ba'al HaTerumot.
