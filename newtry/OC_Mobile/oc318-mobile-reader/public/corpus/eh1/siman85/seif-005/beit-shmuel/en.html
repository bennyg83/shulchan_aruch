The woman lent it. Hagahot Ashri wrote there in Perek HaShoel that she is permitted to lend nichsei melog without his knowledge. However, one can say that this speaks of where she borrows at a time when he has no loss. But he cannot lend without her knowledge. If the husband lent both the cow and her herself, it is not considered borrowing while the owner is with him. Beit Yosef.
