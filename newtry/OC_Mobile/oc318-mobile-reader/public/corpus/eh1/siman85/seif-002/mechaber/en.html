Tzon barzel property — these are the dowry of clothing or livestock and servants for which he accepted liability (Tur) — if they died they died to him and if they remained they remained to him; and likewise if they diminished or were stolen or lost — all is his. And nichsei melog are property that is not under his liability (likewise Tur) — if they died or remained or diminished or were stolen or lost — all is hers, for the husband has in them only usufruct of fruits; and if the husband was negligent with melog property and they were lost he is exempt because she is with him in his work and it is negligence by the owners. {Rama: See above siman 77 seif 3 I wrote the law of a woman's garments whether called tzon barzel or melog; the field the husband set for her in the ketubah — its law is like tzon barzel (responsum of Rosh) (and see above siman 90 seif 16).}
