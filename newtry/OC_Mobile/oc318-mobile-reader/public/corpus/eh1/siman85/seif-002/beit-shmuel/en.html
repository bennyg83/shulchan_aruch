(א) Nichsei tzon barzel. This means property that has ongoing existence, like iron sheep, because all liability rests upon him. Melog is so called because he eats in the manner of plucking a head, where one removes the hair. Hagahot Maimoniyot. A woman has three things: ketubah, nichsei tzon barzel, and nichsei melog. Regarding the difference between nichsei tzon barzel and the ketubah, see what I wrote in siman 53. See the Maggid Mishneh at the beginning of chapter 16, halacha 1. Even nichsei tzon barzel, which he is not permitted to sell because of the honor of her father’s house, and he has only the produce, nevertheless he has a greater advantage in them than in nichsei melog, for the produce of the produce all belongs to him. See Rivash, siman 150.
(ב) Clothing, animals, and slaves. Land too can be nichsei tzon barzel. Here it counted only items to which theft and loss are relevant. Clothing means what she brings in to him, but her own clothing is nichsei melog, as written in siman 77.
(ג) And if the husband was negligent, etc. It implies that for theft and loss he is exempt because he did not accept guarding upon himself. But from negligence, he is not exempt for that reason, since he uses them. Rather, he is exempt because it is guarding while the owner is with him. Regarding nichsei tzon barzel he is liable, even though it is guarding while the owner is with him, because since he explicitly accepts liability, he obligated himself. The Tur wrote this law in the name of Ri, that he is exempt because of guarding while the owner is with him. The Beit Yosef asks why the Tur made the law depend on Ri’s words, since this is explicit in the sugya in Perek HaShoel. According to what I wrote, it is understandable, for he came to teach us in the name of Ri that he is not exempt because he did not accept guarding upon himself. The practical difference is one who takes an animal for thirty days: he does not accept guarding, nevertheless Ri ruled that he is an unpaid guardian.
(ד) A woman’s clothing. This means what she wears. The Rosh wrote that they are nichsei melog. They are not truly nichsei melog, for she has no permission to sell or pledge them. Rather, they are nichsei melog regarding what is written in siman 90, and also regarding a rebellious wife, as written in siman 77.
