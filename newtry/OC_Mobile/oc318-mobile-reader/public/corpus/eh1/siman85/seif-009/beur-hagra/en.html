(א) One who sells, etc. According to the explanation of what is written there, "she did not acquire, and the husband," etc., meaning from the money. The version is: Rav said, the halacha, etc. The Gemara’s question is that Rav is difficult against Rav, for he said above that she acquired, etc.
(ב) And some say, etc. According to the explanation of the Rashbam there and the other commentators. However, Rabbeinu Yonah and the Rashba wrote that what is written there, "to reveal the money," applies specifically where he claims with certainty. See Maggid Mishneh and Tur.
