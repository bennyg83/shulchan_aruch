One who sells land to his wife — if the money with which she bought the land was from the husband, known and revealed to the husband, she acquired and the husband eats the fruits of that land; and if the money was hidden she did not acquire for the husband says I sold only to show the hidden money; and those monies that were shown — land is acquired with them and the husband eats fruits. {Rama: And some say if he claims plainly the money is his behold they are entirely his (and so Tur's language implies).}
