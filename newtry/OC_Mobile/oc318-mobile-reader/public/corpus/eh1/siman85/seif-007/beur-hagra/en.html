(א) They came, etc.; whether, etc. See Tosafot 65b, s.v. "and her inheritance," etc. See Be'er HaGolah.
(ב) Or, etc. As written in Kiddushin 23b, that according to everyone there is no, etc. See Tosafot 54a, s.v. "and Rabbi Eliezer says." Furthermore, know that they are not equal, etc.
(ג) Or they injured her, etc. In the above Mishnah in Ketubot, and as written above, siman 83.
(ד) And likewise if, etc. See Rashi 79a, s.v. "and if not," etc.
(ה) However, etc. Like the answer of Rabbenu Tam in Tosafot there, s.v. "as a gift," and in Gittin 77b, s.v. "what," etc. So too the Rosh wrote there, and so too the Rashba in the name of the Geonim.
(ו) And if she dies, he inherits her. The Maggid Mishneh in the name of the Rashba, that they have the whole law of nichsei melog except the eating of produce. Furthermore, he cannot remove himself from her inheritance even with respect to her own property, as written above, siman 69. Beit Yosef.
(ז) And the produce, etc. Like the final answer in Tosafot in Bava Batra there. So too the Rosh wrote there as well.
