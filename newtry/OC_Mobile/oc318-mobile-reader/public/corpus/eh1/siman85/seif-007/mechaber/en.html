Property that came to her after she married — whether it came by inheritance or was given to her as a gift, or they injured her and gave her her share in her embarrassment and injury — are called melog property and behold they are like those she brought in to him and he did not accept liability; but if she sold her ketubah and dowry those monies the husband does not eat their fruits. And likewise if she gave a gift to another before marrying to smuggle from her husband — even though the gift is not a valid gift the husband does not eat fruits. And likewise a husband who gave a gift to his wife whether land or movable property — she acquired and the husband does not eat fruits. However she cannot sell what he gave her nor give it to another but it remains in her hand; and if she dies he inherits her and the fruits that issue from it — their law is like other melog property and they are sold and land is acquired with them and he eats fruits.
