(א) And specifically, etc. The reason that he can litigate is that he is like a partner who can litigate, as written in Ketubot 94a. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 186, seif 26. Even regarding his share, he cannot compromise or waive. See the Ran in Gittin there.
(ב) He comes to litigate, etc. Since he can buy land with them, even regarding a shtar debt, and all the more so regarding ready money. See Tosafot in Sanhedrin 48a, s.v. "permitted," etc.; alternatively, etc. There.
