A husband who comes to litigate with someone about his wife's property needs authorization; and if there are fruits on land — since he may litigate about the fruits which are his he litigates about the principal and does not need authorization from his wife, for if she has no land he has no fruits. {Rama: Specifically to litigate; but to make a compromise he needs authorization (Rivash siman 496). If he comes to litigate about ready cash of his wife he does not need authorization for it is as if there were fruits on land (Terumat HaDeshen siman 312; see Choshen Mishpat siman 122).}
