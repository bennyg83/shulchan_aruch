(א) He is obligated in their maintenance. He cannot say to them: I will not feed you, and you work for me. Although regarding his own slaves he can say: work for me and I will not feed you. See Yoreh Deah, siman 266. However, regarding nichsei tzon barzel, which are under his liability, one can say that he may say: work for me and I will not feed you. So it also appears there.
(ב) The offspring of nichsei melog. We are not concerned for death, lest the maidservant die and the principal be exhausted. The offspring of the offspring, the Rif holds, belongs to the woman; Tosafot and the Rosh hold it belongs to the husband. Although Chazal did not enact produce of produce for the husband, here it is produce from produce of the body in which the husband has acquired rights, unlike produce of produce that comes from the outside. The Rosh further wrote that if the mother dies while the child is still alive, the child fills the place of its mother and again becomes principal.
(ג) The offspring of the maidservant. So wrote the Rambam. Rashi implies that with an animal of nichsei melog, the honor of her father’s house does not apply. So wrote the Chelkat Mechokek. The Tur at the end of siman 89 wrote that also with the offspring of animals, she gives money and takes it. However, Rivash, siman 21, wrote explicitly that she takes specifically the offspring of maidservants because of the honor of her father’s house, but not the offspring of animals. It is also learned from the Rif that if the husband sells the produce and buys land, then the land belongs to her and he has only the produce, because Chazal enacted the produce only for the profit of his household, not in order to buy land.
