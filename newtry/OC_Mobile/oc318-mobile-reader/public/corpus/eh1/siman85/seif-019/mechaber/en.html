The husband may compel some of his wife's slaves and maidservants that they serve him in another woman's house he married, whether they were melog slaves or tzon barzel slaves; but he cannot take them to another city without his wife's knowledge. {Rama: But he cannot compel them to serve his other wife in another house (Beit Yosef and so Rambam's language implies). And some say if they are ornaments not fit for her that he can give to another so she did not bring in so they be lost and no person use them (Beit Yosef in name of Rashbatz).}
