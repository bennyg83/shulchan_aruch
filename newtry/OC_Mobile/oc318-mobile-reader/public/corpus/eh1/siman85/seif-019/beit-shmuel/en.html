They serve him. In the Tur and the Maggid it implies that according to the Rambam he can designate the maidservant of one wife to serve another wife in another house. So too it implies from the Rif. According to this, the same applies that he can sell some of the produce. According to the Rosh he is not permitted. But for them to serve him, one can say that according to all opinions he is permitted. A cloak and similar items, where the use is his produce, he may use it even in the house of another wife. Tashbetz.
