(א) Some of the slaves, etc. According to the explanation of the Rif there, who wrote that there are others. This is what he wrote: "some," etc.
(ב) Whether, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. "two," etc., and all the more so nichsei tzon barzel.
(ג) But he cannot, etc. For it is like a sale to another, and we learned in the Mishnah, 79b: Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says he may not, etc., as above. All the more so in this kind of case, and unlike the Rosh, who wrote that according to the Rif he can sell.
(ד) But he cannot. According to Rashi’s explanation there, s.v. "but behold," etc., and the Rosh there.
