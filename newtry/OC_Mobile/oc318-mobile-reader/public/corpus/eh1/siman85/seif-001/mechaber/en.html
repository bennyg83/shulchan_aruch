Laws of melog property and tzon barzel. It contains 19 seifim. Her fruits — how did they enact that he eat the fruits of his wife's property in place of her redemption that they obligated him to redeem her; and if he said I do not desire her fruits and I will not redeem her — they do not listen to him. And likewise if she said she does not desire this enactment — they do not listen to her.
