(א) In exchange for her redemption. Because both are uncommon: a captive woman is uncommon, and nichsei melog for a woman are also uncommon. Therefore Chazal enacted this in exchange for that. Rashi, Perek Na'arah Shenitpatet.
(ב) We do not listen to her. So that she not mingle among the non-Jews. We are also concerned for her loss if she is not redeemed, and then he would not have a maidservant to serve him. Even if he stipulated with her before marriage, it is not effective. See Tosafot, Perek Chezkas HaBatim, page 49.
