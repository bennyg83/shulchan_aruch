How a woman is betrothed through a document. It contains 4 seifim. Through a document how? He writes for her on paper or on a pottery shard — even though it is not worth a perutah — "Behold you are betrothed to me" and he gives it to her before witnesses. And he must write it for the sake of the woman being betrothed, as with a get; and if he wrote it not for her name she is not betrothed. And he writes it only with her consent; and if he wrote it against her will — she is doubtfully betrothed.
