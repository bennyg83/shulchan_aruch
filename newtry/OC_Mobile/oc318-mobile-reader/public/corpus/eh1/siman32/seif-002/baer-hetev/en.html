Invalid. Meaning even if he wrote it not for her name and gave it to her as a document which is invalid — nevertheless they appraise the paper; they said invalid only when he says explicitly that he does not betroth her as money even if the paper is worth much. And Beit Yosef was uncertain whether it discusses even when he betrothed with it as a document — nevertheless they appraise, etc. — see there. And in truth it is explained explicitly in the Gemara 48 and Bach and Nachalat Tzvi.
