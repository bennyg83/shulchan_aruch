There are those who say that he must mention the names of the man and woman in the document of kiddushin as he must mention them in a get. {Rama: If he wrote it on something attached to the ground or on benefit prohibitions — some say it is invalid (Maggid Mishneh chapter 3 Laws of Marriage), and some permit (Rashba siman 703). He need not write the date in a document of kiddushin (Rabeinu Yerucham). If he wrote it in his own handwriting and there are no witnesses on it — she is doubtfully betrothed (Nimmukei Yosef chapter Four Brothers). If he gave her a document and said on condition that the paper remains mine — she is not betrothed (Ran first chapter of Kiddushin).}
