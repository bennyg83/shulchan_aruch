To the ground. But one who betroths with something attached to the ground as money — she is betrothed — Bach in name of Rashba; and likewise Hagahot Isserles; see Chelkat Mechokek.
Benefit. Rashba in responsum siman 703 concludes per Yerushalmi that with a Torah benefit prohibition she is not betrothed, and with a rabbinic benefit prohibition she is betrothed — brought above siman 28; and wonder on Rama that he did not mention this distinction.
