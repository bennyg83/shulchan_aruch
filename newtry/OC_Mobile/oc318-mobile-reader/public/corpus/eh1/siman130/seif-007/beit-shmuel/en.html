If he attached two hides, etc. The same applies if he sewed them. Darkei Moshe. This is invalid even if there are delivery witnesses, for even though it is not two gittin, it is invalid because Scripture says "a book," one book and not two books. Although "book" comes to teach the narration of matters, we also learn one book and not two books. So wrote Levush. This requires study: why should attachment not help? In Menachot 34, Rabbi Yehudah expounds that tefillin must be one sign outside and one inside, and therefore if tefillin were written on two parchments they are invalid; but if he attached them, they are valid. Rabbi Yosi validates two parchments because he does not expound this, but we still learn that attachment helps even where the Torah requires one sign. Tosafot and Agudah write that if tefillin or mezuzot were written on two parchments and sewn together, they are valid, even if sewn after writing, and all the more if sewn or attached before writing. Tosafot in Sotah invalidated only where sewn or attached after writing, but if sewn before writing it is valid, and so hold the Rambam and Tur in Yoreh Deah, siman 288. One cannot prove from an attached shofar, which is invalid because the Torah said one shofar, that attachment never helps, for Tosafot in Sotah write that shofar is different because the addition is recognizable. See Orach Chaim, siman 32, Bach. This requires study.
