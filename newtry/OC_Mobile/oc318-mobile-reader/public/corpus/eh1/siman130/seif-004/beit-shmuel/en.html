Or on its back. This means a get that ends on this page and the witnesses are signed on the back. If part of the get itself is on the back and the witnesses are signed there, that is the law stated in seif 7. The Taz invalidates in such a case, and validates only where part of the get is on the adjacent page and the witnesses are signed on it, because the witnesses are still signed within it. But if part of the get is on the back and the witnesses are signed there, it is invalid. See Rashi and Tosafot at the beginning of Perek Get Pashut.
