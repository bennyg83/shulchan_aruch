(ג) And if he erred or changed. Even though the error does not interrupt between his name and the word witness, nevertheless it is clear that it refers to witness, and it is valid. It appears that if he erred in his own name and not in his father's name, it is also valid, for it is not necessary to write his own name if he writes son of so-and-so, witness. Do not say that his own name comes first and is the essence of his signature; if so, son of so-and-so also comes before witness, and why is it valid? Rather, necessarily there is no distinction in this. Although Terumat HaDeshen wrote his law regarding one who erred in his father's name and did not write regarding his own name, one should not infer that he holds it invalid regarding his own name; for otherwise, why did he not write his law regarding his own name? In Terumat HaDeshen one cannot say that the event happened that way, since the questions in Terumat HaDeshen were not written about actual cases. Yet one may say that although the responsa were not arranged according to actual cases, this responsum also appears in his pesakim, and there it was arranged according to an actual case; we therefore say that this responsum in Terumat HaDeshen was also about an actual case. In Responsa Rashakh I, siman 142, he wrote that Terumat HaDeshen is lenient only regarding the father's name, not his own name. Perhaps since the main enactment of Rabbenu Gershom was that they should specify their names, he holds one should not be lenient with their names. However, Terumat HaDeshen did not write this reason; rather, his reason is from the Mishnah in Perek HaMegarresh: so-and-so, witness, is valid; son of so-and-so, witness, is valid. If so, his own name and his father's name are equivalent. I also wrote in the name of Tosafot that Rabbenu Gershom's enactment was only as sound advice. Nevertheless, in practice one should not be lenient against him. In all cases where an error does not invalidate, chak tokhot likewise does not invalidate it, and it is valid in a pressing case and where there is concern for an agunah. It appears that if an error or chak tokhot occurred in the word witness, it is valid even without pressing circumstances or agunah concern, since the signature has already been completed and the word witness can no longer invalidate.
