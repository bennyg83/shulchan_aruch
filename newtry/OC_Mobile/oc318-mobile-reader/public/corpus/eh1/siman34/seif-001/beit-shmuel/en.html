Via a messenger. As it is written "and they blessed Rebecca," and Eliezer was a messenger — Tosafot.
That another blesses. So as not to embarrass one who does not know how to bless; and if there is no other, the groom blesses — Perishah.
And permitted us the married women to us. So all the later authorities.
And after he finishes. Per several poskim he betroths before the blessing, lest she retract and not wish to accept the kiddushin; but we hold lechat'chila he blesses before kiddushin so it precedes the act. Bedi'eved he may bless after kiddushin.
