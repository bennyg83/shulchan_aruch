Messenger. As it is written "and they blessed Rebecca," and Eliezer was a messenger — Tosafot Ketubot 7b.
Blesses. So as not to embarrass one who does not know how to bless; and if there is no other, the groom blesses — Perishah.
And permitted us the married women. It should read "haneshu'ot lanu" — so all the later authorities.
Chuppah and kiddushin. See Chelkat Mechokek what he brings in name of the Ittur; and so in Hagahot Isserles there.
He betroths. Per several poskim he betroths before the blessing, lest she retract and not wish to accept the kiddushin; but we hold lechat'chila he blesses before kiddushin so it precedes the act. Bedi'eved he may bless after kiddushin — Beit Shmuel.
