Ramban chapter 3 Laws of Marriage from a baraita as Ravin explains, etc. Ketubot 7b.
From tractate Kallah: from "and they blessed Rebecca," and so too Tosafot there.
Rashi explains there: rabbinically they decreed seclusion with an unmarried woman, and even with a betrothed woman they said it is forbidden until she enters the chuppah; and in the blessing — a bride without blessing is forbidden to her husband like a niddah.
Ran wrote in name of Rabbenu Tam who emended the text of the blessing "vehitir lanu et haneshu'ot lanu," and so I saw practiced.
Per the Rif and Rambam there, and the Rosh in a responsum beginning of kelal 26.
