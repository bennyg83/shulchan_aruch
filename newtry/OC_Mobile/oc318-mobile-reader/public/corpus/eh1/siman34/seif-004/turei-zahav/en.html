Chuppah vekiddushin — in the Ittur he wrote the main text in kiddushin is with yud-tav; but the precise readers read that yud-tav as rafeh like vav adjacent to beged kefet; listeners erred and thought they said vekiddushin with vav; and he was forced to this since chuppah is mentioned before kiddushin; but truly kiddushin came first. If he says bekiddushin it works — meaning chuppah via kiddushin that came first; and automatically now that kiddushin is under chuppah, this is not needed.
