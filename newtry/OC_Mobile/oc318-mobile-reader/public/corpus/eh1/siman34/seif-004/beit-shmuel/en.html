Requires ten lechat'chila. If there are not ten there is no impediment; but the nissuin blessing is an impediment. See Beit Yosef siman 42/62 what he wrote in name of Rashba; see Piskei Mahari'a siman 101; and see Mahari'l whether we say at an erusin meal "sameach bema'ono." See Beit Chadash.
Ten. Meaning for testimony to kiddushin two are required; for the blessing, ten. See customs of Maharam Mintz.
