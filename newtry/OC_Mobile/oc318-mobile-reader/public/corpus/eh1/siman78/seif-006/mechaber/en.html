A kohen whose wife was captured — even though she became forbidden to him, since previously she was permitted to him — he redeems her and returns her to her father's house; even if in another city he cares for her until he returns her to her region, divorces her, and gives her full ketubah. And if her husband was an Israelite — the captive is permitted to him; he returns her to him as a wife; and if he wishes afterward he divorces her and gives her ketubah. {Rama: A captive who became forbidden even to her Israelite husband — we are concerned perhaps she was lain with willingly, as explained in siman 7 — her husband is not obligated to redeem her (Ran ch. HaNa'arah and R"Y 23).}
