If her ransom was more than her ketubah and he said: Behold I divorce her and this is her ketubah — go redeem yourself — they do not listen to him but compel him and redeem her; even if her ransom was up to ten times her ketubah; even if he has only enough for her redemption. This is said the first time; but if he redeemed her and she was captured a second time and he wishes to divorce her — he divorces her and gives her ketubah and she redeems herself. {Rama: But otherwise he is not obligated to redeem her a second time (Beit Yosef).}
