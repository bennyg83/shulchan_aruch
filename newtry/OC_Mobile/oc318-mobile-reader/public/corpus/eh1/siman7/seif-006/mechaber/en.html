One who says: I was taken captive and I am pure, and bet din permitted her to remarry — or she married before bet din and they did not protest against her — and afterwards two witnesses came that she was taken captive: behold she may marry ab initio and does not leave the permission. Even if the captor comes after her and she is now seen as a captive before us in the hands of her masters — she does not leave the permission that was granted her. They guard her from then on until she is redeemed. If two witnesses later come that she was defiled — even if she married and even if she had children — behold she must leave. If one witness comes — it is nothing.
