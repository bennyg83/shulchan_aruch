A city that came under siege and was conquered: if Kutim surrounded the city on all sides so that not even one woman could escape before they see her and she comes under their control — behold all the women within are disqualified like captives, lest they were lain with by Kutim; except one who was three years old or younger. If it was possible for a woman to escape without their knowing, or if there was one hiding place in the city — even if it holds only one woman — behold this saves all of them. How does it save? Any woman who says: I am pure — she is trusted even without a witness, since she could say: I escaped when the city was conquered, or I was in a hiding place and was saved; therefore she is trusted to say: I did not escape, I did not hide, and I was not defiled. To what does this refer? To a band of that kingdom that settles in the city and has no fear — therefore we are concerned lest they had intercourse. But a band of another kingdom that passed through and swept on — the women are not forbidden, for they have no leisure to have intercourse, being busy with booty and fleeing. If women were taken and came under their control — even if Israelites pursued them and saved them from their hands — behold they are forbidden. Some disagree and say that even with a band of another kingdom they are forbidden.
