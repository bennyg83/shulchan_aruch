Three views: Rashi and Ran other kingdom forbidden same kingdom permitted — when guarding no person can enter city; if one can enter included in conquered city like Kerakhum forbidden even though in hiding place lean lenient because with hiding place not included in Kerakhum. Per Tosafot Rambam other kingdom swept through permitted same kingdom more concern; if can flee from city lean lenient like hiding — specifically kehunah; yayin nesek no distinction wine permitted even that kingdom for libation no leisure therefore YD 129 ruled wine permitted. Per Rif Rosh Ramban Rashba for intercourse no distinction forbidden forever same or other kingdom wine always permitted; regarding fleeing Maggid Mishneh Ramban Rashba forbidden concerned perhaps from fear did not flee; however Tur and Ri plainly Rif Rosh permit when can flee included in hiding — Mechaber's view omitted Ramban Rashba because Tosafot Rambam Tur Ri permit and Rif Rosh also hold thus — be lenient.
