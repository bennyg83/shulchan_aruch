And she conceived from first intercourse — the child is not chalal — so early Ritva on this; and Tosafot wonder: behold at initial contact she is niddah; and when he completes intercourse she is kehunah disqualification and becomes chalalah — there is no difficulty, for when we say initial contact is called intercourse — when he withdrew and did not complete; but he completed his intention for completion of intercourse — therefore at completion she becomes zona; see siman 33; and his words are strained regarding what relevance to his view here; and the main point as stated in Maggid Mishneh chapter 19 of Hilchot Issurei Bi'ah halakhah 13 in the name of Rashba in the name of R' Chananel: one act of intercourse we do not divide that she becomes forbidden by two prohibitions.
