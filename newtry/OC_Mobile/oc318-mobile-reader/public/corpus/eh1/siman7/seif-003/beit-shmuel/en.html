He shall not marry her. But afterward his testimony helps; we do not say he testifies because he thinks he will marry her — so Choshen Mishpat and plain; all this when known she was in captivity; but if only he and knows pure — permitted to marry her; appears per Darkhei Moshe explanation except there Darkhei Moshe disagrees with what Ridbaz permitted even if Israelite that pure. If witnesses she was in captivity and he testifies but not known he is kohen and has miggo he would not say he is kohen — trusted; see responsum Sha'ar Efraim siman 107; if did not know she was captive and married without bet din permission see nearby.
