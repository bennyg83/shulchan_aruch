He may marry her. But afterward his testimony helps; we do not say he testifies because he thinks he will marry her. All this deals when it is known she was in captivity; but if only he and he knows she was pure — permitted to marry her. If there are witnesses she was in captivity and he testifies about her but it is not known he is a kohen and he has a miggo that he would not say he is a kohen — he is trusted; see responsum Sha'ar Efraim siman 106 — Beit Shmuel.
