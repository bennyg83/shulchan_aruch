We do not say let us wait, etc. — this too is because he concludes "regarding a captive they were lenient" — Semag siman 100 s.k. 4. Even if a rumor emerged — Beit Shmuel wrote even a rumor that was established in bet din — Beit Yosef in the name of Ritva. And although Beit Yosef wrote so, nevertheless in Shulchan Arukh he omitted it; if so what did he ask on Rama, as explained; also his words appear garbled: what connection does explaining witnesses of defilement have here, since Tosafot agree with Rambam and mainly Ritva's words I wonder at; although Ritva's book is on Ketubot — nevertheless from the old Ritva 12 it is written to the contrary that the sugya there certainly does not deal except with a rumor that was not established; and he concludes proof from the incident of Mar Shmuel that even when they were captives before us we are only doubtful whether they were lain with — we are not concerned for a rumor that they are witnesses of defilement since it was not established; behold not like him who explains specifically without establishment; and so in Mordechai Ketubot chapter 2 regarding the incident of a woman who was secluded and said she was raped — see there; for both camps explained the sugya here, but regarding a spreading rumor and not established — Shulchan Arukh did well to omit this novelty; and for practical law — uncertain; see siman 157 and in Rambam chapter 3 of Yibum per Ramban and Rashba there specifically a rumor that was established one may infer; behold regarding captives where they are lenient even when established. And per my view the sugya there decides like Tosafot that every rumor that was not established is nothing; and Ritva's words there are not relevant; and what he wrote from the rumor of kiddushin — consider.
