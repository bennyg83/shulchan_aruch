A chalal. See the Tur in this siman and in siman 2, where his words appear to contradict one another. In this siman he wrote that even an Israelite into whose lineage a doubtful prohibition became mixed is forbidden to an Israelite; while in siman 2 he wrote that this applies specifically to a kohen and not to an Israelite. See Maharshal, Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter 4, siman 15. Keneset HaGedolah answered that siman 2 speaks of a doubtful chalal, whereas here it speaks of a doubt among those disqualified from entering the congregation, such as a mamzer; see there. See also Perishah and Bach.
