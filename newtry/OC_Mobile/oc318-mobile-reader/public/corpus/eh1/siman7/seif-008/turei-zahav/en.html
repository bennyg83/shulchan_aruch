He is not trusted to forbid her. In the Tur he concluded that the Torah did not trust him to forbid her except regarding marriage, as it is written "I gave my daughter to this man" — and with this he forbids her to other men; but not to disqualify her for zona. Perishah challenged from siman 4 that the Torah trusts him to say of one of his sons he is not his son and he is a mamzer on his word; and he strained to answer — see there. There is a difference: different there, for the Torah trusted him as it is written "he shall acknowledge"; and in truth also there he is not trusted to disqualify the woman.
