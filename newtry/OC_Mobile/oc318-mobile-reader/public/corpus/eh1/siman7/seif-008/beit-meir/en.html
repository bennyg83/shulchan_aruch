To gloss 3: all this when imprisoned, etc.; but if she was secluded, etc. — on seclusion: behold the law of an unmarried woman who was secluded is not explained; and in Maharik siman 165 in the name of Shag in the name of R' Yosef Tirani wrote: an unmarried woman who was secluded with one of the disqualified, even with an Israelite — she is trusted to say she was not lain with and is permitted for kehunah, and they strike her with makat mardut for having secluded; and so in Tur siman 22; he derived from Rav's statement in Kiddushin chapter 1: they give lashes, etc.; and they explained regarding unmarried for kehunah similar to lashes that R' Elazar explained deals only with unmarried; and it implies to them anonymously even with an Israelite; and although in Ketubot the Gemara answers "R' Yochanan elevated" — if so Rav certainly does not deal with kehunah prohibition; it is possible they hold this is a mere pushback in the world that does not emerge from Rav who rules like Shmuel and R' Gamliel. And for this pushback — likewise necessarily lashes for seclusion apply; and R' Elazar who explains specifically unmarried — establish Rav like R' Gamliel; or they hold like the latter wording in Kiddushin; and one may say in the explicit Kiddushin: although certainly Rav deals with seclusion of unmarried with disqualified — nevertheless it is not similar to one who claims she was lain with, and they made an elevation between kosher and disqualified; but for seclusion in general even with disqualified they made no elevation and she is trusted to say she was not lain with. However per Maharik's first answer that Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel brought — all agree regarding seclusion of unmarried with Israelite that promiscuous ones — it is possible Rashi z"l relies on this and strained to explain Rav's words; and it is impossible regarding seclusion of unmarried with disqualified Israelite specifically; and he explains it does not deal at all with kehunah prohibition but with forbidden relatives; but I wonder on that answer: if it were required from R' Gamliel himself that unmarried who secluded with Israelite — ab initio forbidden to kohen; if so wonder on Ravina in Avodah Zarah on Bahag's challenge — for it is proven promiscuous unmarried if Israelite we are concerned even bedi'eved to forbid her to kohen; and what does he bring from mishnah of imprisoned that permitted only to her husband and buying animal for sacrifice — certainly similar to unmarried for kohen and not kohen's wife; and the opposite he should have inferred: from what is taught "to her husband" — behold unmarried is forbidden. Also per truth we had no proof to permit unmarried imprisoned even through money since already compelled there is a division between unmarried and not so; but from K.
