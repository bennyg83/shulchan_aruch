And one witness testifies she was defiled. Explained in Maggid Mishneh and Ran: even first witness is valid witness; afterward woman came and said she is pure — lenient regarding captive and permitted to kohen even valid witness saying defiled came first and woman at end; we do not say in such case wherever trusted one witness it is like two; also do not say establish one against one and captive witnesses remain; even though testimony husband died such testimony does not help — here captive lenient; even though regarding Kuti and witness from mouth of witness some more stringent than testimony husband died — appears as written nearby; see Shach.
If she says etc. So Shach and learned from baraita Ketubot 23 I am defiled and my friend pure and one witness says you are pure — trusted; and explained baraita even regarding married woman — when known she was taken captive then trusted to forbid herself to husband because once taken captive and simple — gentiles promiscuous in immorality nearly certain lain with and not less than presumptive evidence as siman 115; if gives excuse for her words why said I was defiled — trusted. What is written but not against two witnesses — specifically married woman cannot forbid herself to husband; but unmarried trusted to forbid herself for kehunah.
