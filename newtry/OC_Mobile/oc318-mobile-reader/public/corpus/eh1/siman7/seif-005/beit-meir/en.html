How does it save, etc. — Beit Shmuel brings the Ran's challenge on the Rambam; and in my view there is proof for the Rambam from Yoreh De'ah siman 129 seif 35: there too let us say the rock saves all of them, for it also applies what the Gemara states here — who says she became impure; let us say the rock should be like a hiding place and save both even if they came in one house, since all stains are only sixty — as the Rambam wrote chapter 9 of Hilchot Tum'at Okhel; and for the Rambam it works out that nevertheless we do not purify all of them except when she says I am pure; and per this wonder also on Rama z"l in his language here it implies he concludes like Shulchan Arukh, and in Yoreh De'ah 114 seif 10 in the gloss he learned from the law of hiding place to the law of one who saves all — not so, for there is no general statement; and for those who disagree with the Rambam — perforce strain as Chelkat Mechokek wrote that there should not be a double doubt, for whatever cannot be attributed to something else is like certainty from itself; so too here regarding this it is like certainly she became impure rabbinically. See there Taz and the words of the Rambam "and this impurity is rabbinic"; or one may say regarding a captive they were lenient — and it is possible it is similar to a captive since they forbade her rabbinically and it appears permitted.
