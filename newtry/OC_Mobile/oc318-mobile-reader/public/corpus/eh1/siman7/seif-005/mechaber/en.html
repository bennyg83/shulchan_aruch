If there were two witnesses that she was taken captive, and one witness testifies that she was defiled, and one contradicts him and testifies for her that she is pure and that no Kuti was secluded with her until she was redeemed — even if the one who testifies that she is pure is a slave or handmaid, she is permitted. {Rama: Some say that if she says: I was defiled, she is believed against one witness who says she is pure, but not against two witnesses (Hagahot Alfasi, Ha'ishah Shenitarmelah).}
