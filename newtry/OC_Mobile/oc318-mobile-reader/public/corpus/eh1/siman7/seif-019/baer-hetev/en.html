Chalutzah. Kohen who erred and married chalutzah and bore sons; after known forbidden to him — says after time wants to dwell with her and all forbidden women because now cannot divorce her due to her relatives who exaggerate to bring him to gentile courts to obligate money — these words have no substance; forbidden to use anything from Aharon's sanctity — responsum Ravan vol. 1 siman 59; above siman 7 s.k. 2 from Shevut Yaakov.
