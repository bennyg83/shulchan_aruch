Who is a chalalah? One born of kehunah prohibitions — such as an ordinary kohen who had relations with a zona or divorcee, or a Kohen Gadol who had relations with them or with a widow; or who married a non-virgin and had relations with her — behold these are desanctified forever. If he begot from her — whether he is the one who desanctified her or another after the birth, whether male or female — the child is a chalal. She herself is desanctified by his intercourse when he aroused her — whether he had relations with her unintentionally or intentionally, whether by coercion or willingly — provided he was a kohen from age nine years and one day and older, and the woman with whom relations occurred from age three years and one day and older. But a kohen who betrothed one of the kehunah prohibitions and she was widowed or divorced from betrothal — she was not desanctified; but from marriage — even if she was not lain with — she was desanctified, for every married woman is presumed to have had intercourse, even if she is found to be a virgin.
