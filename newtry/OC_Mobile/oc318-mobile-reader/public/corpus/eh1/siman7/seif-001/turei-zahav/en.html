A captive woman who was taken captive, etc. In the responsum of the Rosh klal 32 it states: not specifically that they saw her taken captive, but that it is known to witnesses that she dwelt among gentiles, etc.
And there are those who write, etc. So wrote the Ran and the Maggid Mishneh, as the Beit Yosef brought. They proved from what we say in chapter 2 of Ketubot: "this testimony — a man and woman, a boy and girl," etc., are trusted except his son and daughter, etc. — this refers to without mesich lefi tumo; if so, even his handmaid would be trusted, yet boy and girl are trusted (and it is simple). For it is stated in the last chapter of Bava Kamma (114): a minor is trusted to say "from here this swarm left," and we challenge: are woman and minor members of testimony? and answer: with mesich lefi tumo; and there we say regarding a swarm which is rabbinic — it is like a captive, for mesich lefi tumo helps; learn from this that in any case we require mesich lefi tumo for a swarm, even though he is an ordinary minor; if so, also for a captive — just as for an ordinary minor we require mesich lefi tumo, so too for an ordinary minor. It must be said: although for mesich lefi tumo helping, the reasoning of a rabbinic swarm applies to the reasoning that regarding a captive they were lenient — nevertheless regarding requiring mesich lefi tumo it is greater especially for a captive, for with an ordinary minor mesich lefi tumo is not required, but for a rabbinic swarm mesich lefi tumo is in any case required. It appears that because of this difficulty the author of the first view — the Rambam — ruled that even for a captive every minor specifically requires mesich lefi tumo; and the Tur also appears to hold so, for he did not mention anything regarding a minor but generalized as he wrote; but regarding his son and daughter, etc. — whether adults or minors require mesich lefi tumo. And from his generalizing (it appears) a minor here and not writing regarding an ordinary minor — learn that all minors are equal and require mesich lefi tumo and it helps for them. What is difficult on this from chapter 2 of Ketubot that boy and girl are trusted — and it refers to without mesich lefi tumo, for with mesich lefi tumo even a handmaid is trusted — it appears to answer: ordinary minor testimony has no connection to intending to testify, for he lacks understanding to testify; rather it certainly refers to mesich lefi tumo. But for adult male and female, "ordinary" means intending to testify, for they have understanding. It follows that what the baraita stated "girl and boy are trusted to testify" — the Tanna did not teach this except whether intending or mesich lefi tumo as above — the view of the Rambam and Tur.
