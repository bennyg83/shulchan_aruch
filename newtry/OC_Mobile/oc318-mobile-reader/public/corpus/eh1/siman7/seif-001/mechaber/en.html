The law of a woman taken captive and who is called a chalalah. It contains 23 seifim: A captive woman who was taken captive — if she is age three years and one day or more — is forbidden to a kohen, because she is a doubtful zona; perhaps she was lain with by a Kuti. If she has a witness that no Kuti was secluded with her — behold she is fit for kehunah. Even a slave or maidservant or relative is trusted for this testimony. {Rama: And even women who are not trusted in women's testimony, such as her mother-in-law, etc. — are trusted regarding a captive (Hagahot Alfasi chapter 2 of Ketubot).} Two captives who testify for each other — behold they are trusted. So too a minor who was speaking ingenuously (mesich lefi tumo) is trusted. An incident: one was captured — he, his mother, and his son were speaking ingenuously and said: We were captured among the Kutim, me and my mother; I went out to draw water — my mind was on my mother, to gather wood — my mind was on my mother; the sages married her to a kohen on his word. Some write that a minor is trusted even when he intends to testify.
