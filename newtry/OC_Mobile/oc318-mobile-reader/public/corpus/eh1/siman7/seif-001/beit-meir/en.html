The incident — Beit Shmuel wrote; and the law separately is, etc. In my view this explanation is not correct; the main point is as Maharshal and Chelkat Mechokek understood; likewise clear in the Mishnah commentary he wrote in the mishnah; likewise two men, etc., who are fit for woman's testimony — woman, relative, and minor from mesich lefi tumo; likewise mishnah R' Zechariah wrote; we already explained relative and woman are fit except husband and maidservant — clear not like my view. However this is doubtful, for the Rif does not mention mesich lefi tumo; apparently because the chapter opening "maidservant" as herself — it appears to him to be stringent with maidservant like herself even regarding mesich lefi tumo; the incident of Rabbi is explained through his adult son per baraita; since he ruled like stam mishnah even adult son needs no mesich lefi tumo — therefore he did not mention mesich lefi tumo; Rama appears though he follows his method that adult son needs no mesich lefi tumo — nevertheless appears to establish Rabbi's incident aliba dihilcheta and because the minor needed mesich lefi tumo ruled like my view; in mishnah commentary appears he agreed with Rif regarding maidservant that mesich lefi tumo does not help; in his composition he retracted — doubtful.
