A woman imprisoned by idolaters for monetary reasons is permitted even for kehunah. {Rama: Specifically when they owe them money and fear to touch her lest they lose from their purse; but if they seized her to ransom her for money so they not lose from their purse — she is forbidden to her kohen husband (Tur Netiv 23; implied Tosafot chapter Eimor).} Through danger to life she is forbidden for kehunah; therefore if her husband was a kohen she is forbidden to him. {Rama: Some say that through danger to life she is forbidden even to her Israelite husband — we are concerned perhaps she acquiesced lest they kill her (Tosafot, Rosh, Tur, Ran, and Piskei Maharai siman 92); specifically when imprisoned on her own account and they have concern for death (Maggid Mishneh chapter 18 in the name of some say); but women sitting imprisoned on account of their husbands are not forbidden unless their husbands' judgment was concluded to death (Piskei Maharai siman 92); some say wherever she expects to live through ransom and return — she is not forbidden to her Israelite husband, for she does not acquiesce even when imprisoned on her own account (there in Maharai); at a time of wrath and widespread killing our rabbis permitted her to her Israelite husband per the first view; if imprisoned by Kutim and Jews enter and exit with her — she is permitted to her husband (Hagahot Maimonides chapter 18); an Israelite who apostatized — he and his wife — and they repented: his wife is permitted to him and we are not concerned perhaps she prostituted under him, even if her husband is a kohen; if his wife alone apostatized she is forbidden to her husband even if an Israelite (responsum Rashba and Hagahot Mordechai Ketubot) unless she has one witness that she did not prostitute as with a captive; some are lenient and say we are not concerned perhaps she prostituted (there); at a time of decree when they apostatized under coercion and repented after the coercion passed — one may be lenient and permit them even to their kohen husbands (Rosh klal 32); if a woman vowed to apostatize and has not yet apostatized and retracted — she is permitted to her husband (Maharik shoresh 165).} However, when Israelite power is strong over the Kutim and they fear them; but when Kuti power is strong — even through money, since she came under Kuti control she is forbidden unless one testified for her as with a captive. {Rama: All this is when imprisoned by Kutim and delivered to them; but if she was secluded with Kutim even though she owes them — we do not forbid seclusion though done improperly; even if a prostitution rumor emerged — any rumor after marriage we are not concerned to forbid her to her husband (there in responsum of the Rosh); however if she secluded for prostitution — be stringent (Maharik shoresh 165).}
