The husband is not trusted to testify for his captive wife that she was not defiled, for a person does not testify for himself. So too her handmaid does not testify for her; but her husband's handmaid testifies for her. And a handmaid who was speaking ingenuously is trusted. {Rama: A Kuti is invalid for testimony regarding a captive, and even mesich lefi tumo he is not trusted (Rosh klal 32). Some are lenient regarding mesich lefi tumo (Riaz). The same applies to a witness testifying from the mouth of another witness. Mesich lefi tumo is only effective to be lenient, but not to be stringent (Rid Cohen 8).}
