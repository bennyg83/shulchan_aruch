Even if there was one witness there — one may say we hold miggo to contradict the witness is not applicable here, for this applies only in monetary matters where one witness stands for an oath and miggo to swear against the witness does not apply; not so in prohibition matters where there is no place for oath — we say miggo to contradict him. And all the more it fits well the words of the Ribam in Beit Shmuel Choshen Mishpat siman 75 s.k. 39.
