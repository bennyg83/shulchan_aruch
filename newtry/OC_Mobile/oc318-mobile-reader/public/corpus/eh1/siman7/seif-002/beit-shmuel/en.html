Testifies for himself. Even mesich lefi tumo though above Rashbag explained mesich lefi tumo trusted to eat terumah — nevertheless for lineage they elevated and were stringent — so Tosafot Ran Maggid Mishneh; specifically to testify he cannot; but if no man knew she was captive except him and was with her and known not defiled — permitted to him.
The same witness from mouth of witness. For Beit Yosef and Darkhei Moshe depends on above dispute whether Kuti trusted mesich lefi tumo; for Rosh who disqualifies Kuti — witness from mouth of witness also disqualified. But responsum Rashash vol. 1 siman 6 expands and wrote Rosh also permits witness from mouth of witness; nevertheless in practice his words not compelled — see there.
But not to be stringent. In Mo'ed Katan end Yevamot this dispute explained whether Kuti mesich lefi tumo her husband died and one Kuti mesich lefi tumo he lives — can he contradict; one may say Torah did not trust him except to be lenient; so Ridbaz there and responsum Rashba; see Beit Yosef siman 157 and there in Mo'ed Katan ruled can contradict; so responsum Ran and responsum Maharam Lublin siman 133; so too below siman 17 s.k. 37. One may say what is written here to be stringent does not help — same case Kuti testifies mesich lefi tumo she is pure and Kuti contradicts him trusted to be lenient; or per below we follow the lenient as written nearby if one witness says defiled and woman says pure; however language does not imply two gentiles — rather one Kuti testifying mesich lefi tumo she is defiled — testimony does not help to be stringent to hold certainly defiled — as if did not testify and law like any captive; so below siman 17 specifically when one contradicts the other; but one Kuti saying alive is as if said nothing and she is in doubt.
