A kohen who had relations with a doubtful zona or doubtful divorcee (there) (or doubtful chalutzah) (Tur) — behold this is a doubtful chalalah and her offspring a doubtful chalal; they impose upon him both stringencies of kohanim and stringencies of Israelites: he does not eat terumah, does not become impure for the dead, and marries a woman fit for a kohen; if he ate, became impure, or married a divorcee — they strike him with makat mardut. The same applies to a chalal of their words. But a definite Torah chalal — behold she is like a zar: she marries a divorcee and becomes impure for the dead, as it is said: "Say to the kohanim the sons of Aaron" — even though they are sons of Aaron, not until they are in their kehunah.
