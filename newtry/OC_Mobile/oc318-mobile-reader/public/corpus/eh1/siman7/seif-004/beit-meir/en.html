He is not trusted — see Beit Shmuel who wrote on the Maggid Mishneh; and I do not know from where he has this; and properly wonder, for it appears from the Ran himself chapter 11 to the contrary in the mishnah "one who says this is my son a mamzer" — he is not trusted; Rashi z"l wrote he is a relative; he wondered why this was needed, for since she is presumed kosher, challenge requires no fewer than two — behold he only wondered why it was needed; but he agrees that per Tanna Kamma who does not hold "he shall acknowledge" even one witness suffices; if so from being a relative the same applies to a daughter regarding a captive; and what Beit Shmuel strained in the Ran's words — and it is possible from this he derived Chelkat Mechokek's view as simple: for the Ran, since he gave the reason — if so it is a novelty with one witness; he needed to explain the novelty is that he is trusted like two; for with one it would not be a novelty that he is a relative — on the contrary, throughout the Torah a relative is disqualified, only also as stated in Choshen Mishpat siman 33 seif 10, and especially for his own obligation.
