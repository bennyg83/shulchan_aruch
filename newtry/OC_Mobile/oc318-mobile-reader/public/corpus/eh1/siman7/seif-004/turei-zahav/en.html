(In the Ran) he wrote: even before she came to bet din — nevertheless she is trusted to say I am pure, with a miggo that I was not taken captive to contradict the witness; and Perishah wrote: although in the version of R' Abba they needed to seize to pay and we do not say she is trusted even with an oath to contradict the witness, as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 90 — different here because regarding a captive they were lenient, etc. It appears to me there is a difference, for there is in any case power for the witness to obligate an oath, not so here. It must be said: even if there was one witness and there is a woman supporting the witness — we say the woman is as one who does not exist; for stringency we do not consider her at all.
Testifying that she is a captive — simple: behold even if a witness testifies she was defiled it is nothing, as Rashi explained in chapter 2 of Ketubot — so is the law that one witness in defilement is nothing except in sotah; and perhaps it was taught "captive" because here the main law is regarding a captive.
