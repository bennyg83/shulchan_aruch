For a person does not testify for himself. See responsum Noda BiYehudah Tinyana siman 29 who wrote that the mishnah needed to give this reason "a person does not testify for himself" — its explanation is as Maharshal wrote in siman 42: the husband is not believed with miggo, for if she was defiled she is forbidden to him and he loses nothing by testifying; and if she was not defiled he gains by testifying — therefore he is not believed; and so in siman 17; and see in responsum Shevut Yaakov siman 6 that he challenged this.
