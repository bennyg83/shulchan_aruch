A male convert who married a female convert and she bore a daughter — she may not ab initio marry a kohen, even her daughter's daughter for many generations, even though her conception and birth were in holiness. If she married a kohen — she does not leave. If she has one side of Israel — such as a convert who married an Israelite woman, or an Israelite who married a convert — the daughter is permitted to a kohen ab initio. The same for a freed slave who married an Israelite woman, or an Israelite who married a freed woman.
