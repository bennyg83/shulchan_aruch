(א) There is no, etc. As above, siman 2.
(ב) Unless, etc. There, 46a and 48b.
(ג) And some say, etc. There, like Ri, according to the explanation of Rabbenu Tam in s.v. ve'ibait eima, etc., 49a. See there.
(ד) However, etc. From what is written, 48b: it is understandable, etc., for if it is found, etc., it implies that otherwise we are not concerned. And there: and she is believed, etc.; how, etc.; she is a minor, etc. As Tosafot wrote there, s.v. ve'ibait eima, etc.: and if you will say, etc.; and one may answer that she is believed, etc. This also appears in Yevamot 105 and Bava Batra 155, as Tosafot wrote there, s.v. beshlama, etc.
(ה) And some say, etc. So the Beit Yosef learned from the above Tosafot, but this is not correct. Tosafot wrote this only regarding women examining, according to Ri, before the period. But regarding men examining, there is no one who says this. Even Ribam said there, in any case, only in the twelfth year, according to the Gemara’s explanation there, and we do not rule this way. Also regarding women examining, we do not rule like Ri, as written in seif 15.
(ו) Doubt, etc. Like every biblical safek. Even in the Talmud, the Rashba’s view is to be stringent. See Beit Shmuel.
