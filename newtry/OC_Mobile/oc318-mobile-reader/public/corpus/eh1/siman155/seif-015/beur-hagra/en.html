(א) When they examine, etc. This is the language of the Rambam, meaning both for stringency and for leniency, like the first Tanna there.
(ב) And even a woman, etc. There.
(ג) And even, etc. Since women are valid, the same applies to relatives, as written in Yevamot 39b.
(ד) And see below, siman 169, etc. The distinction between them is that here it is a matter that is likely to become revealed, etc., as written at the beginning of chapter 2 of Rosh HaShanah. This is also the reason in Yevamot 39b. All the more so here, where we do not need “likely to become revealed” in the same way as there. See Mordechai, siman 193 and siman 131.
