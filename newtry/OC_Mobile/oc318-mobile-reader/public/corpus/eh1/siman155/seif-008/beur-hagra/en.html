Her and her husband. Rambam. The Rashba and other decisors challenged him: why is recognition of her husband needed? Even if this is not her husband, it makes no difference, for she can perform mi'un even not in his presence. Why would she lie? She can perform mi'un against him wherever he is. It is understandable regarding the woman, for we are concerned that perhaps she will bring another woman and remove relatives through the mi'un, and they will inherit her. The Ran wrote that perhaps even the Rambam was stringent only lechatchilah. This is what is written in the gloss: “and specifically,” etc.
