(א) But there is, etc. Gemara there, 101a.
(ב) And all this, etc. There, and Yevamot 108a, like Rabbi Yehudah.
(ג) And he is obligated in her maintenance. There, 107b: how did they say, etc.
(ד) And her redemption. From what is written: she has no produce, learn from it that the husband consumes the produce. See Rashi there, chapter 1, s.v. avdu, etc. In Yevamot 107: he consumes the produce, and we say there, 47b: let him redeem her, etc.
(ה) But if, etc. There, 107b, as above.
(ו) Or for the need, etc. According to Tosafot’s explanation there, 101a, s.v. ilima miha ika, etc.
(ז) And even if they are, etc. According to Rashi’s explanation in Yevamot 84a, and as Tosafot wrote in the above s.v. The Mishnah taught that she performs mi'un, etc., and one should not explain, etc.
(ח) And property, etc.; and what, etc. The Mishnah says: but not worn garments. See Tosafot there, s.v. velo bela'ot, etc. In the Gemara there: if we say it refers to one who performed mi'un, etc. In the Tur: if they are extant, whether nichsei melog or nichsei tzon barzel, she takes them; and if they are not extant, he must pay. The Beit Yosef wrote that regarding nichsei melog this means where he lost them, for otherwise we learned: if they died, they died for her. This is what is written here: “and what,” etc. But his words are astonishing, for we say there: if they are not extant, etc., even regarding nichsei melog and even if he lost them, as Tosafot wrote there, s.v. havah leh, and in Yevamot 27a, s.v. de'i, etc. Ri said, etc. There is a scribal error in the Tur, and the word “not” is missing. It should read: he does not need to pay, even in all of them, because it was not in accordance with the law.
(ט) And he, etc. Yevamot there and Ketubot there.
(י) And she does not need, etc. There, and Yevamot at the end of chapter Arba'ah Achin.
(כ) And if she married, etc. The Mishnah in Yevamot there: one who performed mi'un against a man and married, etc.
(ל) And not only, etc. There: he gave her, etc.
(מ) For every, etc. There: this is the general rule, a get, etc.
(נ) But one who divorces, etc. Like Shmuel, for the sugya of the Gemara there follows him: if we say it refers to one who performed mi'un, etc., behold, etc. So too in the Yerushalmi, and the Rif brought it: an orphan has no ketubah. Resolve it as an orphan who performs mi'un, etc.
(ס) And it is forbidden, etc. Yevamot there.
(ע) And she requires, etc. Ketubot there, and Yevamot at the end of chapter Arba'ah Achin.
(פ) And if she married, etc. The Mishnah in Yevamot there: to another, and he divorced her, to another, etc.; this is the general rule, etc.
(צ) And it is unnecessary to say, etc. There: she performed mi'un against him and he took her back, etc.
(ק) And likewise she is forbidden, etc. There: and some say, Rabbi Yitzchak said, etc.
(ר) And if she performed mi'un, etc. The Mishnah, as above: or she performed mi'un, etc., as above. As written in the Gemara: rather, if it is difficult, etc., Rabbi Eliezer said, etc.; who is the Tanna, etc. It is written there: and some say, Rabbi Yitzchak said, etc. This is what he wrote: “and to his relatives,” as above.
(ש) For every, etc. The Mishnah there: this is the general rule, a get, etc.
