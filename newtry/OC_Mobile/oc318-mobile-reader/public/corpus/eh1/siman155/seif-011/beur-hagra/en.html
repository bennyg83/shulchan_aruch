(א) One who performs mi'un, etc. There, 107b and 3a.
(ב) But, etc. Like Shmuel there: she performed mi'un against this one, etc. Rabbi Yochanan also holds this way, and so does the sugya of the Gemara there, 13a, unlike Rabbi Oshaya. The Rambam holds that the same applies to other relatives. They disagreed with him and said that it applies specifically to brothers, because of yibbum, for she removes herself only from this one. This is what he wrote: “and some say,” etc.
(ג) And specifically, etc. As with one who performs mi'un against the husband, for it is no worse than if she had entered yibbum with him and then with the husband.
