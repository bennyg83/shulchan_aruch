(א) Until when, etc. Mahari, end of the first chapter of Chullin, and Niddah 22a, unlike Ri, for the sugya of the Gemara there, 46b, is not like him. So too they ruled in Bava Batra 156a: and the halacha is like Rav Nachman, etc.
(ב) After twelve years. Bava Batra there and Niddah 46a: the halacha is, within, etc., and like Rabbi, for the halacha follows Rabbi against his colleague, and the anonymous Mishnah follows him. See Tosafot there, 45b, s.v. elu, etc., and as they said there, 46a: Rabbi said, a minor girl, etc.
(ג) And from the time, etc. Since Rabbah bar Livai said: the boundary, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. harei, etc., and Ketubot 39a: can she become pregnant, etc. Although in Yevamot there we conclude that perhaps she will die, nevertheless since at the time of pregnancy she is like a na'arah, so too at birth she is like a bogeret, as they said there: and if you will say, etc. All this is even before twelve years, as Rashi, Tosafot there, and other decisors wrote, unlike the Rambam. There is no difficulty from “they had already become pregnant,” because their pregnancy is not proof until she gives birth to a viable child. In Yalkut, for otherwise it would be difficult: what does the Gemara ask in Ketubot 39a, “can she become pregnant,” etc., since we conclude here that perhaps she will die? Ramban and Rashba in the name of Tosafot. See there, where they forced themselves to explain the words of the Rambam, which were brought in Shulchan Aruch, that he holds specifically from the time of birth. See there at length.
