(א) Or until she gives birth. In the first chapter of Yevamot there is a dispute: according to Rav Safra, children are like signs of maturity; according to R. Zevid, there are no children without signs. Tosafot and the Rosh wrote that in most cases when she gives birth she has signs. The phrase "there are no children without signs" does not mean absolute impossibility, but that children are not commonly found without signs. Even if they examined her and she had no signs, we are concerned that perhaps they fell out. Even where there is no Torah doubt, namely where he did not have relations after she matured, nevertheless with children we are concerned that perhaps the signs fell out. According to Rif, Tosafot, and the Rosh, according to Rav Safra even if she gave birth while a minor, the children count as signs after she matures; pregnancy during minority likewise counts as signs after the time. Rama should not have needed to write this law in the name of Or Zarua, because it is already explicit in Tosafot and the Rosh. Beit Shmuel continues to compare the views of Rambam and other poskim on chalitzah, maturity, and whether signs are assumed or must be examined.
