(א) When does this apply, etc. As written in Bava Batra 156a: we examine for mi'un, etc.
(ב) But ordinarily, etc. Niddah 47a, according to the conclusion of the Gemara there. When Rava said the chazakah, etc. So too there, 48b. Although according to one answer Rabbi Shimon does not accept Rava’s chazakah, we do not rule like Rabbi Shimon, for between Rabbi Yehudah and Rabbi Shimon the halacha follows Rabbi Yehudah. Also, we hold like the first Tanna, as written above, seif 15.
