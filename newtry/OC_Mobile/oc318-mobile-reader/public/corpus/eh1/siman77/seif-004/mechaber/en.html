A man and his wife who came to beit din — he says: She rebels from marital relations; and she says: Not so, but as is the way of all the land I am with him. And likewise if she claimed and said that he rebels from marital relations and he says: Not so, but as is the way of all the land I am with her — they impose a cherem first upon whoever rebels and does not admit in beit din; and afterward if they did not admit they say to them: Be alone before witnesses. They were alone and still they claim — they seek from the one accused and make a compromise according to the judge's power.
