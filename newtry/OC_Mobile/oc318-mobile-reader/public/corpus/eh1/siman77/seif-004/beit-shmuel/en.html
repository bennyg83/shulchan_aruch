We first impose a ban. The Perishah asked from siman 154, where regarding a claim that he lacks male potency she is believed because he knows whether she lies and she would not be brazen. He answered that there she is believed not for money but to require divorce. Responsa Rashach answers that specifically with a claim of male potency she is believed because she would not be brazen to make such a claim, and that presumption assists her. In siman 85 I wrote in the name of the Maggid Mishneh that even regarding monetary claims we say a person is not brazen.
