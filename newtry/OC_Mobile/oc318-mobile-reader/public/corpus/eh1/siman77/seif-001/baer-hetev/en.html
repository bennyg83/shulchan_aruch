(ו) They add. Keneset HaGedolah wrote: it appears to me that this is specifically in a place where they did not accept the ban of Rabbenu Gershom upon themselves. But in a place where the ban of Rabbenu Gershom is practiced, we do not listen to him, for there is concern that perhaps he wishes to divorce her and knows that he cannot divorce her, so he rebels against his wife in order to divorce her; see there, folio 100a. So too Chelkat Mechokek wrote. But Beit Shmuel holds that even for us, where the ban of Rabbenu Gershom exists, when he wishes to divorce and give her the ketubah he is not considered rebellious. So too Bach wrote. See Responsa R. Eliyahu Mizrachi, siman 30, who ruled there that when he wishes to give her the ketubah and divorce her, he need not give her sustenance, since he is coerced by the enactments of the ban of Rabbenu Gershom. Chelkat Mechokek disagrees with him. In Responsa R. Aharon ben Sasson, siman 55, he ruled that the husband can say: I hold like R. Eliyahu Mizrachi. Bach also ruled like R. Eliyahu Mizrachi. However, from Responsa Rosh and Maharik it appears that he remains obligated in sustenance and clothing.
