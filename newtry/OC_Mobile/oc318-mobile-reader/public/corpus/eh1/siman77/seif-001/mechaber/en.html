Laws of a rebellious husband and rebellious wife, or one who says "he is repulsive to me." It contains 5 seifim. A rebellious husband against his wife who said: Behold I will sustain and provide for her but I will not cohabit with her because I hate her — they add to her ketubah the weight of thirty-six shekels of silver in barley every week; and he sits and does not have relations as long as she wishes; and even though her ketubah keeps increasing — behold he violates the negative commandment "he shall not diminish," as it is said "he shall not diminish her onah." And if she wishes — they compel him immediately to divorce and give ketubah. {Rama: Some say if he wishes to divorce immediately and give her the ketubah — they do not add to her ketubah (so appears from Rambam ch. 14 of Ishut); and it seems that even without the negative commandment of lo yigra he does not violate it.}
