Language of the Rambam chapter 6 of Laws of Acquisition and Gifts, from the statement of R' Chanina Bava Batra folio 144a. And in my humble view the word to a virgin was omitted from the citation — for so it is there in the Rambam: one who marries off his adult son to a virgin; and so in the Gemara and all the codifiers; and so in Beit Yosef. And the Rama already emended this in name of some authorities.
The Mechaber added the word male — and to explain what the Tur wrote: the explanation that even if he married off a daughter before him — as his father the Rosh z"l wrote.
Act of Rav Ashi there.
