The house. Immediately and not after death — responsum of the Geonim siman 161. And specifically the father who marries off — not the mother. Rambam part 1 siman 239. And specifically when the house is definitively his and the other has no right in the house; but if the other has some right in the house — he did not acquire it. Rashba part 2 siman 43. If the father died and the widow did not find from whom to collect her ketubah except from the house — she collects from it. And if she died, all her heirs acquire in the house. See Knesset HaGedolah folio 89a side 4. And he wrote in Be'er Heitev: and it seems this law applies even nowadays — if this comes before us nowadays, this law where the son claims from the father due to this house — we must rule as explained in this siman in all laws explained in this siman. See end. And so Beit Shmuel.
Anything. All the more if he built the house, or this house was a house in which the father did not dwell and did not leave even a small utensil there — Rambam part 2 siman 125; Maharam Alshikh siman 72. If there is a dispute between the father and son — the father says he did not completely clear the house, and the son says he completely cleared the house — the father must bring proof. And if he died, his heirs must bring proof. Rambam part 2 siman 125. And from the words of Maharam Alshikh in siman 72 it appears: specifically a new house — the father must bring proof; but in an old house — the son must bring proof. And Knesset HaGedolah in name of one sage brought: always the son must bring proof, even in a new house — only if the son has a twofold presumption he is believed to say he completely cleared the house.
