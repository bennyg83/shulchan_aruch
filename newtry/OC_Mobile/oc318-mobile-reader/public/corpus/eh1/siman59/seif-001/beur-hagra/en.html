And that the father have, etc. Rashi there s.v. he acquired, etc.; and in Gittin 14a s.v. in intimacy, etc.; and as written in Bava Batra there: he set aside for him a house, etc. And likewise from that which it says in the house and not in his house. And see Rashi in Bava Batra there s.v. his storehouse, etc. And it appears there is a textual corruption in the aforementioned Rashi — for behold he acquired; and let it be resolved.
