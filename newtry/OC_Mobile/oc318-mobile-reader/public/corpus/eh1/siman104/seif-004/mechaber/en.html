Similarly if a court sold items that do not require public notice [of sale to attract bidders] and erred by a sixth [or more, in evaluating the value] the sale is void. Less than a sixth, the sale is valid. These are the things which do not require public notice: Slaves, documents, and movable effects. Slaves lest they run away; documents and effects lest they be stolen. Therefore they are appraised in court and sold then and there, though if the marketplace is close to the city, the items are taken there to be sold. In a place where public notice is never given, all items are treated [in all respects] as things for which notice is not given. Tur.
