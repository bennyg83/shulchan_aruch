{Hagahah} Some say permitted and some say forbidden — per Perishah there is proof at least rabbinically forbidden — otherwise what place for decree one who marries pregnant or nursing woman of another that he divorce only with get if permitted to him afterward as before — here his will to designate her to be his wife forever — plainly no less than designated; and settled per R' Yehudah of Orleans in Tosafot Yevamot 36b who holds act of get is only to add rabbinic prohibition of pregnant woman — prohibition of unattached woman; therefore in kiddushin no get needed for otherwise forbidden rabbinically because of bride without blessing; but why Tosafot and Nimmukei Yosef wrote both rabbinic — implies through get there is act she is forbidden by Torah law. And this is not even per Rambam except unrestrained; and perforce their reason in get is not to fence but penalty that they not benefit from his act who preceded to marriage or betrothal in prohibition from fear another will precede him — if so no proof; and see siman 13 where I brought Rashba in name of Ra'avad that essence of get is penalty; and possible follows his view here that per this there is no fencing in get; and one can say also this permission of concubine is only when he designates in his house specifically — unlike pregnant woman whom even with get certainly they separate from his house — no longer matter of concubine even though their intent to remain man and wife; and forbidden to all because of prohibition of unattached woman and is fencing for all so he not come upon her incidentally and in secret — and see siman 1.
