A woman is betrothed in three ways — with money or with a document or through intercourse — from Torah; but the Sages forbade betrothal through intercourse because of licentiousness; and if he transgressed and betrothed through intercourse they strike him lashes of rebellion and she is betrothed. And even if he betrothed with money or with a document — if he did not arrange a match first, or if he betrothed in the marketplace, they strike him lashes of rebellion and she is betrothed. {Rama: And some say they do not strike if he betrothed with money or with a document even in the marketplace and without matchmaking (Tur in name of Rosh); and I have never seen in my days that they struck one who betrothed without matchmaking.}
