Seif 4 in hagahah (even in marketplace) — what Chazal said regarding kiddushin in marketplace — see beginning chapter 5 Kiddushin and Beit Yosef z"l — plain also per Nehardeans in any case prevent lekhatchilah; only they hold they do not excommunicate for we do not extend dispute; and likewise they prevent betrothal without definite matchmaking; and likewise betrothal through intercourse — also regarding excommunication we hold specifically father-in-law's friendship without matchmaking and through intercourse. And appears plain commerce and acquisitions man acquires for himself in marketplace — plainly there is licentiousness and frivolity; and how much more Hebrew slave forbidden to stand him on acquisition stone designated only for worldly matters — all the more time of finding when woman compared to holy Torah — not among merchants is place of her acquisition; unlike place not designated only for commerce — plainly better to increase publicity as Chazal said; and no opening of mouth objection whatsoever for all; and groom in mother-in-law's house — perhaps only when she is widow and sometimes his wife not with him; and nevertheless poskim z"l wrote permission in this.
