Not to betroth through intercourse and prohibition of intercourse with an unattached woman even if he designates her. It contains 4 seifim. A woman is not considered a married woman except through kiddushin whereby she was betrothed properly; but if he came upon her through prostitution not for kiddushin — it is nothing. And even if he came upon her for marriage between him and her — she is not considered his wife even if he designated her to himself; rather on the contrary they compel him to remove her from his house. {Rama: For certainly she is ashamed to immerse and he came upon her in her niddah (Tur); but if he designates a woman to himself and she immerses for him — some say permitted and she is the concubine mentioned in Torah (Ra'avad and some commentaries); and some say forbidden and they strike lashes for this because of "do not be kedeshah" (Rambam and Rosh and Tur); a Kutite who married a Kutit and likewise an apostate who married an apostate woman in their customs and afterward converted — there is no concern of kiddushin at all and she is permitted to leave him without a get even though he stayed with her many years — it is only ordinary prostitution (Ribash siman 6 and Teshuvot Maharil siman 200) (and see above siman 149 seif 6).}
