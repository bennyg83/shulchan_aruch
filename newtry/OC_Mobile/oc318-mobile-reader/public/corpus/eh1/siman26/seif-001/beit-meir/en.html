And even if he came upon her — see Beit Shmuel concludes per this if it was public that he marries her for marriage — kiddushin through intercourse, etc.; per Perishah contradicts himself in what he challenged there on Rama — for certainly what Tosafot and Ran spoke does not discuss nissuin as Torah law — examine well; and question — this is certainly intercourse; but one who takes unattached woman to his house without prior kiddushin before witnesses — from here to say kiddushin through intercourse as he himself wrote; and if he did not marry her as law of marriage — not from Torah to say he came for kiddushin; and if so even though in truth he came for kiddushin — betroths without witnesses and they have no substance; and requires examination his intent — for per my view this is concubine.
