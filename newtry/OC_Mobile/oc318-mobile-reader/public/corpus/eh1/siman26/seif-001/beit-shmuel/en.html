And even if he came upon her for marriage. See siman 149 — whoever marries and it is public to all is as if there were witnesses at time of intercourse; per this if it was public that he marries her for marriage — it is kiddushin through intercourse; only discusses when it was not public.
And some say forbidden and they strike lashes for this because of "do not be kedeshah." Even though one who comes upon a gentile woman does not receive lashes from Torah as written siman 16; and likewise one who comes upon a maidservant — they do not compare forbidden relations one to another for all is by decree as Maggid wrote chapter 1 law 1; nevertheless what is written they strike lashes for concubine is not compelling as I will write; and in Hagahot Maharit beginning Kiddushin wrote Rambam discusses when she is designated for prostitution then she is kedeshah; and likewise Kesef Mishneh end chapter 2 Laws of Na'arah Betulah; also Ramban in responsum wrote when she is concubine there is no prohibition per Rambam even for a commoner — per his version that did not read in Rambam Laws of Kings "commoner forbidden"; and even per our version where Rambam wrote commoner forbidden — nevertheless no proof they strike lashes for concubine; and from Rosh and Tur appears no prohibition at all; and what Darkei Moshe wrote they hold forbidden — this is what Tur wrote in name of Rosh's responsum that she is forbidden; but no proof from there for there forbidden because ashamed to immerse and he came upon her in niddah; but because of concubine prohibition one can say permitted; and proof from Rosh's words Ketubot that no prohibition in concubine — for he wrote there one can fulfill onah with concubine; also what Bach understood from Rosh's responsum that family members can protest one who designates concubine for himself — blemish to them that she be his concubine — shame is from prohibition — not so for shame is that she be his concubine and not fitting for him; and perforce must say if because of concubine prohibition — difficult why need family members' insistence — court should protest; and more appears even per Tur no prohibition in concubine except what he wrote they compel him to remove her from house — when there is concern she is ashamed to immerse; and from all these views appears no lashes for concubine even if prohibition not explicit; and one can say how could one think no prohibition in concubine — behold we hold bride without blessing forbidden like niddah and concubine is without blessing and kiddushin; also difficult how permitted to king — already answered in Ramban's responsum siman 284 specifically when he wants to marry woman who is acquired to him for everything and forbidden to every man — then Torah said he should betroth her and bring her under canopy and bless seven blessings; but concubine needs no blessing and kiddushin.
