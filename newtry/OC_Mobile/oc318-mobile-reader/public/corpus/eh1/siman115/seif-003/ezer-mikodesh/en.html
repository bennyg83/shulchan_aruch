I also found what I wrote elsewhere concerning a woman who had intercourse while a niddah: the cherem of Rabbeinu Gershom, z"l, does not apply here, neither regarding taking two wives nor regarding divorcing her against her will. She, and likewise her father and mother, are evading the matter, and now their answer is that she is pregnant. He wishes to assure her regarding the child's needs. It appears that this is no claim at all. Wherever the enactment of Rabbeinu Gershom does not apply, he may divorce her, and he need not wait any time because she says that afterward she will accept it willingly, even if it were known that there is no deception and that she has a proper claim. All the more so in the present case, where it is plainly deception. Since the enactment of Rabbeinu Gershom does not apply, one should not prevent him even for a moment when he wishes to send her away with a get of dismissal. The claim concerning the fetus also is not found as a ground to delay a get of dismissal. Only payment of her ketubah is incumbent upon him first, and nothing else. May the good God do what is good in His eyes.
