(א) Moshe, etc. The rule is that she does not leave without a ketubah unless she caused him to stumble in a prohibited matter, or where she transgresses a sin for which children die because of that sin, or if she did an act of licentiousness, for then there is concern lest she commit adultery under him. But because of another prohibition that she did, she does not leave without a ketubah. See Responsa Rosh, kelal 32, siman 3. See Beit Shmuel.
(ב) Her husband, etc. But if she herself ate a prohibited item, she has not lost her ketubah. If he feeds her a prohibited item, or vows and does not fulfill, they compel him to send her out and give a get. This is when she comes with the claim, "I need a staff for my hand," for when he vows and does not fulfill, the children die because of that sin. So too the Rav wrote below, siman 154 seif 1. If she vows and does not fulfill, and she is barren and has no child, Piskei Mahari, siman 68, is uncertain whether she leaves without a ketubah, since she has no children. Beit Shmuel; see there.
