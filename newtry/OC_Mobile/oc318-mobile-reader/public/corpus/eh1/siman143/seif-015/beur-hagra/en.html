(א) And if she married him, etc. As they said there: and if you will say it is possible, etc. It is forbidden only lechatchilah, as they said there.
(ב) If she married another, etc. There, according to Rava’s establishment, and so too.
(ג) And he may never take her back. This means even after the husband’s death, for she is like a sotah, who is forbidden to the adulterer even after the husband’s death. So too here, so long as the condition has not been fulfilled, she is a married woman.
(ד) And the child, etc. If the condition is not fulfilled. All this is according to the Ra'avad and the Tur in the name of Ramah. But the Rambam has another approach, and the Ra'avad challenged him, as written in the gloss.
(ה) If she married, etc. For we hold like Rav Huna, that it is a get retroactively, as above, seif 2.
(ו) If she became betrothed, etc. For if she is not like a sotah, since she was not violated, as written in Perek HaIsha Rabbah: he said to him, etc., and she became betrothed. What is written, "and she may not return to him," means even if she married that particular man, because it is like one who remarries his divorcee from betrothal. All this is in the Tur in the name of Ramah.
