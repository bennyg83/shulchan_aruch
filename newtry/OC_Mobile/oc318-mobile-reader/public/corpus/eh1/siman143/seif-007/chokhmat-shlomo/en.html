(Seif 15:) Even if he did not stipulate with her explicitly, etc. NB: It appears that this applies specifically where there had not previously been a quarrel between them. But if there had already been a quarrel between them, etc., there is no prohibition here. See proof for this above, siman 11, in Beit Shmuel, s.k. 8. Even according to those who disagree there, that is because there, even ordinarily, we suspect improper relations and require grounds for permitting it; regarding a quarrel, they hold that this is not sufficient grounds for permission. But in our case, where ordinarily we do not suspect improper relations, and it is prohibited only where it is evident that he divorces her for this reason, if they already had a prior quarrel it is not evident that he divorces her for this reason. Therefore it is permitted to marry her, as appears to me in my humble opinion; examine carefully.
