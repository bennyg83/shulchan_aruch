(Seif 1:) One who divorces on condition, etc. NB: In the case where someone wished to travel far away, and they asked him to give his wife a get, but he did not wish to divorce except on condition that the get take effect after three years, and he did not agree otherwise; afterward, when he prepared to leave, they told him that it was improper to leave his wife as an agunah and that he should not bear sin for this, and he agreed and gave an unconditional get. The question is whether his intent was that it take effect from today or according to the first condition. See Responsa Maharit, part 1, siman 35, who concluded that it follows the first condition. But in my third edition on Even HaEzer, siman 63, in my responsum to the holy community of Tarnopol, I properly rejected his words, with God's help, and concluded that the get takes effect immediately; see there. This is true in my humble opinion; examine carefully there.
