(Seif 10:) One who gives a get, etc. NB: See Choshen Mishpat siman 3 and the Shach there, s.k. 3. His words there contradict what is written here, for here it implies that the two conditions certainly do not take effect together, but only one or the other, whereas there the Shach implies that both take effect. This requires study. See also my responsum to the holy community of Tulchyn in this siman, where I wrote about this with God's help in my fourth edition; examine carefully.
