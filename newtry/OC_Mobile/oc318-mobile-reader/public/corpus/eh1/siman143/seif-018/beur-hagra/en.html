(א) Whether, etc. Rashi there, s.v. "that she have relations," etc. See Tosafot 83a, end of s.v. "and she stood." Alternatively, etc.
(ב) And lechatchilah, etc. Yerushalmi: Rabbi Shammai said: We find a woman who at first incurs liability for ervah with him, and at the end one does not incur liability for ervah with him. Rabbi Mana said: To what is this comparable? To one who says to his wife, "This is your get on condition that you have relations with so-and-so." At first it is forbidden for him to have relations; if he transgressed and had relations, the get is permitted retroactively, etc.
