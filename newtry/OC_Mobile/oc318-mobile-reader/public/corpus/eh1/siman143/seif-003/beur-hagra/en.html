(א) And since so, etc. There: she is not given as bound to the yavam.
(ב) And if he said to her, etc. See Be'er HaGolah, and as written on 76b: "from now" he said to her, etc. The Gemara asks there whether immediate all the more so, for he established, etc., and it was not resolved. So too there is a dispute in the Yerushalmi, and we follow the stringent view, as written above, siman 144, seif 3. This shows that even though it is impossible for the condition to become nullified, we do not hold the get in force until the condition has actually become void. And here it is impossible, etc.
