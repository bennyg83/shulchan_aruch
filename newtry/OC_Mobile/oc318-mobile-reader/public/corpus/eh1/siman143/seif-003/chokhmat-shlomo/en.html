(Seif 3:) She may not marry a stranger until she performs chalitzah. NB: See Beit Shmuel, s.k. 6, and what he wrote to resolve that this is not comparable to "the first coming Pesach." His distinction is not understandable. See what I wrote in explanation of his words in my work Mishpat Tzedek on Choshen Mishpat, third edition, siman 243, and in my work, siman 209, to the holy community of Rohatyn, where I explained his words and what I myself wrote to resolve this difficulty in another way; see there and examine carefully.
