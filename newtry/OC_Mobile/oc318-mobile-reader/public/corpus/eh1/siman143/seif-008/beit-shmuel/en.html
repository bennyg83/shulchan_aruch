(ו) Or the father said: I do not wish, etc. Rashba wrote: if he did not fix a time for serving the father, but said plainly that she should serve my father, one may say that in such a case even according to the Rabbis who disagree with Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel it is a get, since the delay is not from her, for he did not give a time for the service. This indicates that his intent was for his father's benefit. There is a dispute about this in the Tosefta. It implies from Rashba that even if she did not serve his father at all, and he said, "I do not wish that you serve me," it is a get. This follows his approach, for he holds like Tosafot that if the father died before she served him it is a get. One may say that here too, if he said "I do not want you to serve me," it is a get, even though the condition was not fulfilled at all. Although there he wrote plainly that it is a get, and here he wrote that it requires study, that is because here he is alive and she can still serve him.
(ז) And some say that whenever the delay, etc. This means both where he said, it is impossible for you to serve me, and where he died, as written in Tosafot and the Tur. But before she nursed at all, it is not a Get, because the condition was not fulfilled at all, and that is worse; so it is precise in the Tur, see there. These some say authorities do not refer to what the Mechaber wrote nearby, if the child died before she nursed him. This follows the approach of those poskim who hold that according to the Rabbis, if he stipulated without detail, it is a condition for his benefit; nevertheless, if the child died before she nursed, it is not a Get. So too according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who holds that even where he specified we say he stipulated for his benefit: if he died before she nursed, or before she served his father, it is not a Get. But according to the other poskim I cited, and Rashba nearby, who hold that according to the Rabbis, when he stipulated without detail, even if the child died before she nursed at all it is a Get, it appears that according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, if he died or said it is impossible for you to serve me before she nursed, it is a Get. Tosafot asked: regarding a condition if you give me, why does waiver or giving against his will not help, since the delay is not from her? They answered that here Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel holds he stipulated for his benefit, and since he died he no longer needed it, so it is as though the condition was fulfilled; but there, where he said that you give me, his intent was that he receive it, and ultimately when he does not wish to receive it, the condition was not fulfilled. According to this, one must say that the dispute between the Rabbis and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel on folio 73 regarding the condition if you give me is as follows: Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel holds that me means me or my heirs, so she can still fulfill it even though he no longer needs it, because the condition was to give to him or to his heirs; the first tanna holds that even though the delay is not from her, it is nevertheless not a Get. Tosafot's difficulty applies only according to their approach, that any condition for his benefit, such as where he stipulated without detail that she nurse, if he died before she nursed it is a Get; similarly, Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel holds even where he specified two years that he stipulated for his benefit, and if he died before she nursed it is a Get. Therefore waiver or giving against his will is difficult. But according to the other poskim, one may say that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel said it is a Get only where she began to fulfill it; there, in the case of I waive it to you, it was not fulfilled at all and is like a case where he died before she nursed. Nevertheless, it follows from this that if he stipulated that she give him one hundred zuz and she gave him part, while he does not want the rest, it is not a Get according to all opinions, even though if he died it would be a Get. It also follows from Tosafot that if he stipulated that she give specifically into his hand and not to his heirs, and he died, according to Tosafot's approach and according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, the condition is considered fulfilled, as where he died before she nursed. According to the other poskim, even according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, if he died before she nursed it is not a Get unless she began to nurse. So too in a condition that she give into his hand: if she gave some money and he died, it is a Get. According to the Rabbis it is not a Get, because presumably for the Rabbis the condition to give one hundred zuz is like the condition to serve his father two years, where we say he intended to distress her.
