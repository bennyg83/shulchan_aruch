(א) And if they died after, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. "died," etc. But the Rosh agreed with Rashi’s explanation and wrote that so it is in the Yerushalmi.
(ב) Died, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. "rather according to Rav Ashi," etc. If so, according to Rav Ashi’s words, even in this kind of case it is a get. So too the commentators wrote. But the Rambam explains that even according to Rav Ashi, the condition must be fulfilled through nursing. This is the explanation: rather, according to Rav Ashi, etc.; for necessarily even in the first clause, where she fulfilled the condition, why does the first clause differ, teaching "this is a get," while in the latter clause it is not a get? Where both are fulfilled, it is a get; where both are not fulfilled, it is not a get. See what I wrote above, that according to Tosafot, even according to Rava she does not need nursing at all.
(ג) Or if he said, etc. As the Rif wrote in Perek Get Pashut, that this rule, "wherever Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel taught," etc., is not precise.
