(א) He said to her, "on condition," etc. As written above at the beginning of siman 137.
(ב) Until a certain time. Specifically a time, according to the Rambam. See seif 22.
(ג) And there is one who, etc. See above, seif 2, what I wrote in the name of the Ran. He wrote there that others hold that with every condition, even "sit and do not act," we are concerned. What is written on 87a: if so, with all conditions, etc., means as follows: according to Rabbi Akiva, who says this is not severance because there is concern for its nullification, if so with all, etc. That is the "some say" here. He further wrote that others are lenient, that even with "arise and do," we are not concerned, except that anything dependent on others we are concerned for, but with a condition in her own control we are not concerned. He wrote that this is the view of the Rambam, etc. So too the Rosh wrote, chapter 8, seif 2. This is what he wrote: and there is one who, etc., since it is in her control, etc.
(ד) And there is no remedy, etc. The Rosh, klal 45, seif 56. See what I wrote above, seif 8. Although one could say that the Mishnah is specifically with "except," where it is not a get at all, or with "on condition" according to the initial view, nevertheless the Tosefta there records his refutation. See there, klal 46, seif 2, where he wrote that he can nullify the condition. The Tur brought this at the end of this siman, and he brought proof from what is written in Ketubot 73a: do not say that Rav’s reason, etc. He wrote that in divorce, even a condition that is for the woman’s benefit can be nullified. See above, siman 38, seif 38. See there in the responsum, where he discusses this at length. This requires study, and the later authorities already struggled with this. See there. Although in seif 2 he wrote that he cannot nullify the condition, that refers to the condition itself; but he can divorce her again without a condition, as written in the above Mishnah and the above Tosefta, etc., since she is not divorced without fulfillment of the condition.
(ה) And if she married, etc. The Gemara, 83a.
