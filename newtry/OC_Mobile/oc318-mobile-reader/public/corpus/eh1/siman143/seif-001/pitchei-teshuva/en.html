If, etc. See Ba'er Heitev and the decisors cited there regarding a get made dependent on a condition. The discussion is whether the condition is valid, whether the language creates a present divorce with a later condition or only a future divorce, and whether a later act or omission can retroactively affect the get. The cited responsa compare this to the laws of kiddushin and monetary conditions, and distinguish between conditions that are possible, impossible, doubled, and within the husband’s control.
