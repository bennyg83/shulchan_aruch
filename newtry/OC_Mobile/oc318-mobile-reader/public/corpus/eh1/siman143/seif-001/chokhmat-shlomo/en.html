(Seif 1:) One who divorces on condition, etc. NB: I have difficulty according to those who hold that with a condition framed as "on condition that," he cannot revoke it or add another condition to it, and all the more according to those who hold that where the date of the get proves its intent it is treated as from now, and he cannot add a condition to it. What, then, is the Gemara's question at the beginning of Perek HaSholeach regarding the rule that once the get reaches her hand he can no longer revoke it? Let the Gemara say that it speaks in such a case, where he gave her the get on a condition from now, or where its date proves its intent, and it teaches that even though she is not divorced immediately he still cannot revoke it. This requires study; examine carefully.
