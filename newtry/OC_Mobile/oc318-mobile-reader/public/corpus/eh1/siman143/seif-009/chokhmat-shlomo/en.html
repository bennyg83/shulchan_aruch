(Seif 20:) He must not impose a condition upon her, etc. NB: See my third edition on Choshen Mishpat, siman 181, where I concluded that only where he explicitly said "forever" do we require that result; but if he spoke without specification, it is not treated as forever. Although generally one might say that an unspecified term means forever, here it is different, since if his intent were forever the get would be void, and a person does not act for nothing. See there, where I wrote several distinctions on this matter with God's help, and where I explained at length the words of the Mechaber and the Rama so that they do not contradict one another; see there.
