Every woman who leaves her husband's authority through death, get, or refusal takes property. It contains 12 seifim. A woman who leaves her husband's hand whether through death or divorce takes all her property without oath — whether melog property or tzon barzel property — even though she does not take her ketubah (when she is a widow) except with oath; her property she takes without oath. And the practical difference: she takes them as they are even if they are heavily worn, and one should not restore them to their original craft; and if they were entirely lost he does not pay her; and if they appreciated they appreciated for her. (And see below siman 93 seif 1 whether she can claim during her husband's lifetime.)
