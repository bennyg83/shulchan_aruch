A husband who brought sharecroppers onto his wife's property and afterward divorced her — if the husband himself is the sharecropper, the husband is removed; they are removed because they descended only with the husband's intent, and they are assessed and their hand is on the lower portion. And if the husband is not the sharecropper they descended with the land's intent and they are assessed as sharecroppers. {Rama: And some say this is specifically when they knew the field belongs to a woman; but if the sharecroppers did not know it belongs to a woman they take their sharecropping in every matter (so it appears from Tur's language and so in Ashiri).}
