Tzon barzel property called nedunya — by law if they diminished he must pay the entire diminution even if they are fit for their original craft; but the custom is that as long as they are fit to do their original craft, even if they are heavily worn, she takes them as they are; and if they would not do their original craft he is obligated to pay their value that was assessed on him at the time of marriage. And if they appreciated she does not take them except at the original assessment of the time of marriage. When does this apply? In a place where they write in the ketubah: she brought him such-and-such vessel worth such-and-such and such-and-such vessel worth such-and-such; but in a place where they do not write any vessel in detail in the ketubah but include everything and write she brought him in garments and ornaments such-and-such sum of money — behold that sum of money is a debt upon him, and even if the property was new they return to assess them as they are worth now; and if they diminished they diminished for him, and if they increased they increased for him, and they are not concerned with the original assessment at all.
