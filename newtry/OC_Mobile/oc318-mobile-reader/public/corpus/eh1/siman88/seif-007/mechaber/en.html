If he spent expenses on his wife's melog property and divorced her — whether he spent little and ate much produce, or spent much and ate little produce, even one dried fig in the manner of honor as a man in his house, or he ate a dinar even not in the manner of honor, and even if he did not take from the produce what he spent but one bundle of vines — what he spent he spent and what he ate he ate. If he spent and did not eat, or ate less than the threshold — they assess how much they were enhanced and ask him how much he spent; if the enhancement is more than the expense he swears with holding a sacred object how much he spent and takes the expense; and if the expense exceeds the enhancement he has from the expense only up to the enhancement with oath. And likewise one to whom property fell in a distant place and he spent expenses to bring them and bought land with them and ate from the produce up to the threshold or less; and if he ate only from the principal he swears how much he spent and takes up to the enhancement. (And that he takes with oath is specifically when the woman does not contradict him; but if she contradicts him — not) (Mordechai chapter HaIshah Shenafelu).
