If she says I take the vessel and he says I give money — they listen to her, provided they do the original craft. And likewise offspring of a maidservant and animal of melog property. And not only that but even if she brought him two vessels or two maidservants as tzon barzel property and they assessed them at a thousand zuz and they appreciated and stood at two thousand — she takes one at her thousand and regarding the second if she wished to give its value and take it on account of enhancement from her father's house — they listen to her.
