(ב) We strike him, etc. In the Tur it says that we excommunicate him. Beit Yosef challenged this from what is stated in the first chapter of Kiddushin, that Rav would administer lashes to one who betrothed with cohabitation, but not excommunication, which is more severe than lashes; so too Rambam ruled. Therefore the Mechaber ruled that we strike him. They further challenged this, for the Rosh rules like Nehardei, that Rav would administer lashes only to one who betrothed with cohabitation; if so, one who betroths in the marketplace is not struck. This is also difficult on Rama in siman 26, who brought the Rosh's view, while here he did not bring it. It is also difficult on the Tur, why in Choshen Mishpat siman 8 he wrote that one who troubles an agent of beit din is struck, since the Rosh ruled like Nehardei. These difficulties can be answered well: I saw in Be'er Sheva, folio 99, that Nehardei disagree only regarding kiddushin, where one administers lashes only for cohabitation, but regarding other matters the Rosh agrees that he is struck.
