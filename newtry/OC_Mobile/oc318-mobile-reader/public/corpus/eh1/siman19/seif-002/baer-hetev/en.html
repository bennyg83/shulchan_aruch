Day. Bach wrote: specifically to obligate another man who had relations with her we require thirty days, because before thirty he can say he did not know she was his wife. But one who said of a certain woman that she is his sister and had relations with her is liable even within thirty days. And in Shulchan Gavoah chapter 10 it does not appear so; and Choshen Mishpat and Taz challenged Bach and wrote: before thirty days his excuse that he states is effective. And likewise a woman who said she is a niddah and had relations with her husband within thirty days — her excuse is effective; but if she said every day that she is a niddah and afterward had relations — her excuse is not effective. But if she wore her niddah garments — her excuse is not effective even within thirty days, since she acted. Chelkat Mechokek wrote: if she said "this is my husband" and she committed adultery with another — she is forbidden for this even within thirty days; for specifically regarding death we require thirty days, but to forbid her to her husband we do not require so much. And Beit Shmuel wrote: in this too his excuse is effective within thirty days if we did not see them conduct as man with wife.
