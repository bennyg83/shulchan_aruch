One who betroths his minor daughter and the husband demands marriage — whether she or her father can delay that she not marry until she grows and becomes a na'arah. If he wishes to enter her, he may enter; but it is not proper to do so. {Rama: And if she and her husband agree to enter and the father died — the relatives cannot protest, even if she is sickly and he is rushing to enter in order to inherit her (Mordechai chapter The Man Who Betroths).}
