He married her. Meaning: the time the Sages fixed arrived; but even if the time they agreed between themselves arrived — even if it was shorter than the times mentioned in the Mishnah — he is obligated in her sustenance if there is no duress. Mahari ben Lev responsum siman 12 in laws of marriage.
He. And after the duress passes he must enter her; and if he does not enter, he is obligated to give her sustenance if he delays. And nowadays when they do not betroth immediately but only make a match — even though the time arrived and he delays, they do not practice giving her sustenance. Beit Shmuel in Be'er Heitev. See Sema siman 227 s.k. 17.
