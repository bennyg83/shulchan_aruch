When the time they gave the man arrives and he did not marry her, he is obligated in her sustenance even though he did not enter the chuppah. If the delay was due to duress — such as he or she fell ill, or she became a niddah, or the time arrived on Shabbat when he cannot enter — he is not obligated to sustain her. {Rama: And specifically when the illness occurred during the allotted time; but if the time arrived and he did not marry her, and afterward she fell ill — he is obligated in her sustenance even though now it is duress (Mordechai beginning of Ketubot and chapter The One Who Performs Chalitzah).}
