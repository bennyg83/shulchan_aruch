Even though he did not enter. Nb: and if he died see Ran chapter Na'arah second side folio 83b. And in my humble view, from Tosafot Yevamot 53a s.v. the condition of the tanna is proven that they hold: when her time arrived and she became a widow — she is sustained from orphans. However, I still have a question: even if generally she eats — that is since she already ate; but in cases where she did not eat by Torah law — shall we say she may eat after death? And it is relevant to discuss when his time arrived where there is no time of prohibition for him. However, from Tosafot's language who wrote she is no worse than when his time arrived — and they challenged: nevertheless in the first chapter there are permitted women regarding whom we say she has no sustenance when standing and going out — they did not challenge simply that there is no when his time arrived here since it is forbidden for him — implying they hold nevertheless the law of when his time arrived applies here.
