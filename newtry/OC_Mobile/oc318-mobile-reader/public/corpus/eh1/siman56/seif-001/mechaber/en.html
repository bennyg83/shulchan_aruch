How long do they give a betrothed woman. It contains 4 seifim. One who betroths a woman and several years pass and she demands marriage — if she is a na'arah, they give her twelve months from the day of the demand to support herself and prepare what she needs, and afterward she marries. If she demanded after she matured, they give her twelve months from the day of maturity. And likewise if he betrothed her on the day of maturity, they give her twelve months from the day of betrothal, which is the day of maturity. If he betrothed her after she matured, if twelve months passed in her maturity and afterward she was betrothed — they do not give her more than thirty days from the day of the demand. And likewise one who betroths a non-virgin — they give her thirty days from the day of the demand.
