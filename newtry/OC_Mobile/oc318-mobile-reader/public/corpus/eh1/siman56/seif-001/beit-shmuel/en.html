And she is a na'arah, etc. So is the language of the Rambam, and so is the language of the Tur. However, Tosafot do not hold thus; rather if she was betrothed in her na'arut and afterward matured, she has twelve months after the demand — even though Tosafot likewise hold per R' Yehudah. Nevertheless it appears that even for the conclusion we say thus, as Hagahot Maimoniyot writes in name of R' Yehudah. And if she was betrothed to one and several months passed after the demand and he died and she was betrothed to another — she has twelve months from the second as from the first. See Yerushalmi that the Rosh brought. However, if the first demanded her during her na'arut days and the twelve months passed — then she is an adult for six months — she does not have from the second except six months; for if the first demanded after she matured, they count also from when she became an adult. And it appears that even for a minor the demand is effective, and they count twelve months from the day of the demand to perform the marriage in her adult years. See Ran.
From the day of the demand. So is the view of the Rambam, Rosh, and R' Meir HaKohen; but Rashi holds from the day of betrothal, and so ruled in Shulchan Gavoah.
