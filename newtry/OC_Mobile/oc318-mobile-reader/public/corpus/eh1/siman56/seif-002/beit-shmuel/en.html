As they give her. Meaning: if she is a widow and he is a bachelor, they do not give him twelve months but only thirty days, for they do not consider him but her. So is the view of the Rambam and Ran. However, in Yerushalmi it is stated: he is a bachelor and she is a widow — they give him twelve months, as the Rosh and Tur ruled. And what they give a man as time is to prepare the needs of the feast.
