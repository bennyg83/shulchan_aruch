It says in the Torah: "And a son is not to him" (Deuteronomy 25:5 is discussing when somebody does yibbum) one son and one daughter, or the seed of a son and the seed of a daughter, until the end of the generation, between this woman to another. Even if it was a seed for a bastard. Behold these exempt a wife from doing chalitzah and yibbum. But his sons with a slave or a non-jew don't exempt (yibbum and chalitzah), even if the son (of the slave or non-jew) is converted to freed (rambam perk 1 hilchot yibbum and chalitzah).
