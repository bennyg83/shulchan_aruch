Perhaps the brother or witnesses create a concern like a proper chalitzah. If witnesses saw an act of chalitzah, and the woman was otherwise permitted, we examine whether the act exempts her, whether she is permitted to a kohen, and whether the rival wife is permitted. The Taz discusses cases where a chalitzah was done by mistake, under invalid circumstances, or with a doubtful yavam, and concludes that one must distinguish between permitting the woman to the market and creating the prohibition of a chalutzah to a kohen. Where the act has the form of chalitzah but not full validity, it may create prohibitions without fully exempting, and the details depend on the status of the brother and the testimony.
