If he wrote for her "I have no vow nor oath upon you nor upon your heirs nor upon those coming by your authorization" — he cannot make her swear, neither her nor her heirs nor those coming by her authorization, any oath. But his heirs and those coming by his authorization — e.g., purchasers who bought from him — make her and her heirs and those by her authorization swear — e.g., if she was divorced and died. But if she was widowed and died before swearing — neither her heirs nor those by her authorization collect her ketubah.
