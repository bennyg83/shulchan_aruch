If he wrote for her "I have no vow nor oath — I nor my heirs nor those coming by my authorization — upon you nor upon your heirs nor upon those coming by your authorization" — he cannot make swear neither he nor his heirs nor those by his authorization — neither her nor her heirs nor those by her authorization — even if she impaired her ketubah or the heirs her ketubah or heirs of heirs. But if she became an administrator after the husband's death — this exemption does not help. But for an administrator appointed in the husband's lifetime — it helps, for even by gilgul he cannot make her swear. And there are those who say that even if she did not exempt — the heirs cannot make her swear regarding administration done in the husband's lifetime.
