(א) Even if she or the heirs impaired it. This is not comparable to an apotropos. Although he gave her exemptions, nevertheless if she became an apotropos after his death she must swear, for he exempted her only from his own money, and after his death the money left his domain. But with an oath for one who impaired the ketubah, where he exempted her from impairment of the ketubah, what difference is there whether she impaired it during his lifetime or after his death? Further, regarding becoming an apotropos after his death, this did not enter her mind. So wrote the Rosh.
