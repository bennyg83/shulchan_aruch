Seif 1 (if he exempted her). One witness testifies that there was a testamentary instruction from someone that his wife should be exempt from the ketubah oath against the adult heirs. Although one witness can serve as support to exempt from an oath, nevertheless this does not help, because according to the one witness it was not made with a kinyan; in any event, one cannot remove property from the possession of orphans on this basis. Also, according to his words this was not at the time of death, and he spoke in the form of a healthy person's gift. There is also reason to require her to swear, because the gift document states that he had such-and-such an amount of coins, while she says that this amount was not found. Although one might say, why believe his words more than hers, nevertheless since all of her credibility exists only because of his will, and since in this matter we live from his mouth, when there is a discrepancy on her side from what he testified, namely that there was such an amount, and she contradicts him by saying he erred, since we do not assume error in this matter, her credibility is at least weakened. Also, regarding the minors, an oath applies according to all. Therefore it is proper to make a compromise with the widow vis-a-vis the adults and the minors, as appropriate according to the assessment of knowledgeable people.
