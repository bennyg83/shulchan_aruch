If he exempted her from an oath or designated movables for her — she collects without an oath. It contains 7 seifim. If the husband exempted her from an oath — she collects her ketubah without an oath, according to the language of the exemption. How? If he wrote "I have no vow nor oath upon you" — he cannot make her swear any oath; but he makes her heirs swear. If he divorced her and she died — her heirs swear the heirs' oath. And those who come by her authorization — e.g., if she sold her ketubah to others and was divorced and died and the purchasers claim her ketubah — they swear "you did not command us." And if she is still alive — the purchasers do not collect unless she swears she was not paid. {Rama: And see above siman 105. If he exempted his wife at the time of his death from the oath — she takes her ketubah without oath. But if they afterward saw her squandering property — she must swear (Mordechai end of HaKotev). And if there are witnesses she squandered or gave to others, or she admits — she must pay and is not believed to say her husband commanded her thus (responsum Rosh kelal 50). But if he made her explicitly trusted on every matter like two witnesses — she is believed (Sma).}
