Members of the chuppah who divided into groups — even if they ate in houses that are not open to the place where the groom eats — all bless the blessing of bridegrooms; not only if the servant joins them but even if the servant does not join them — since they began to eat those in the other houses when those of the chuppah members began — all are considered as one to bless the blessing of bridegrooms since they eat from a meal prepared for the chuppah.
