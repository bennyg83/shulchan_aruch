If there are two grooms together they bless one blessing of bridegrooms for both of them. {Rama: And even if the grooms were not together but his intent was on both of them as explained in Yoreh Deah siman 265 regarding circumcision. And some say one should not bless for two grooms together because of ayin hara (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 10 Laws of Marriage). And so is the custom — to make for each one chuppah by itself and bless for each one; but after the meal they bless for many grooms together if they ate together.}
