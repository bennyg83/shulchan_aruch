When to bless the blessing of bridegrooms and if they eat in many houses. It contains 13 seifim. One must bless the blessing of bridegrooms in the groom's house before marriage; and they are six blessings. And if wine is there he brings a cup of wine and blesses on the wine first and arranges all of them over the cup — and behold he blesses seven blessings. And if wine is not available he blesses on beer.
