Some say one should not say seven blessings on the cup of Grace after Meals; rather he brings another cup and says on it seven blessings and returns and takes the cup of Grace after Meals and says on it the blessing of the land. And some say one needs only on the cup of Grace after Meals to bless seven blessings; and so is the widespread custom. {Rama: And in these lands they practice according to the first view. And some say even for Who created alone we require two cups (Minhagim Yeshanim). And the blessing of betrothal and blessing of bridegrooms they say over two cups even if he betroths at the time of chuppah (and the custom is to interrupt between them with reading the Torah scroll) (so Tosafot and Ashiri).}
