Some say they are not called new faces unless they are people for whom they increase; and some say Shabbat and Yom Tov are like new faces at the evening and morning meal but not at the third meal; and so is the widespread custom. {Rama: And now they practice in these lands to bless seven blessings at the third meal — possibly because it is customary for new faces to come. And some say because it is customary to preach and the sermon is like new faces.}
