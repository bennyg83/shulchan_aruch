This blessing they add in the groom's house is the final blessing of the seven blessings — which is the blessing Who created. {Rama: And some say even Who created one does not bless all seven except when inviting others; but if he eats with his household they do not bless (Ran in name of Ramban). And so is the custom. When does this apply? When those eating are the ones who stood at the marriage blessing and heard the blessings; but if those eating were others and did not hear the marriage blessings at the time of marriage — they bless for them after Grace after Meals seven blessings as they bless at the time of marriage — provided there are ten and the groom is among the number. And some say even if they were at the time of chuppah and heard the blessings — if they did not eat there until now it is called new faces and they bless for them seven blessings after Grace after Meals; and so is the widespread custom. And some say if there were new faces there even though they do not eat there he blesses for them night and day (Ran first chapter of Kesubos).}
