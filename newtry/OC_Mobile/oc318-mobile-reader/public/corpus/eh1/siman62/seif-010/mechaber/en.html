Some say if the groom leaves his chuppah even with his bride and they go to eat in another house — they do not say there the blessing of bridegrooms; and this applies when his intent is to return afterward to his chuppah; but if he goes entirely to another house and the whole company is with him and that house becomes the main one — there too is called chuppah and they bless the blessing of bridegrooms. And likewise sometimes when the groom and bride go to another city they must bless there the blessing of bridegrooms if it is within seven days. {Rama: And his intent is not to return (Beit Yosef).}
