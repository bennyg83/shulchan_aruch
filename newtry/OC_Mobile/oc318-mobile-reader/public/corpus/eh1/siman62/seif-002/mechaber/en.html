A man may marry many women at once on one day and bless the blessing of bridegrooms for all of them as one; but for rejoicing he must rejoice with each one the rejoicing fit for her — if a virgin seven days and if a widow three days — and one may not mix rejoicing with rejoicing. {Rama: And one should not make chuppah for two sisters together (Semak in name of Sefer Chasidim). And some say even for two non-Jewish women one should be careful not to do them as one because of hatred lest one honor one more than her companion (Mordekhai first chapter of Moed Katan). And they are not careful about this; on the contrary some intend to make poor chuppahs with rich chuppahs because of a mitzvah (Hagahot Maimoniyot in name of R' Yehuda of Paris).}
