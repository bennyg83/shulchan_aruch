A man may marry several women, even a hundred at once or one after another; and his wife cannot restrain him — provided he can give food, clothing, and onah appropriately to each one; and he cannot force them to dwell in one courtyard but each one separately. And what is their onah? According to number — how so? A worker who had two wives — this one has one onah a week and that one has one onah a week; if he had four wives — each one's onah is once in two weeks; and likewise if he was a sailor and has four wives — each one's onah is once in two years. Therefore the Sages enacted that a man not marry more than four wives even if he has much money — so that each receives onah once a month.
