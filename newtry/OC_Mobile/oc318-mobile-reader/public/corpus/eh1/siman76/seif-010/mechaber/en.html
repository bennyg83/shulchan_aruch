And how does he forbid her? If he said: the enjoyment of your marital relations is forbidden to me — but if he said: the enjoyment of my marital relations is upon you, or swore not to have relations with her — it does not take effect. {Rama: However, if he vowed with her permission — the vow takes effect; and he is permitted to be alone with her even though forbidden to have relations with her (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 4 of Ishut) (and see Yoreh Deah siman 234 seif 7).}
