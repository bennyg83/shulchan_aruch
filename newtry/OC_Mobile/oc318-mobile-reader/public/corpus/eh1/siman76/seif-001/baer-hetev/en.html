(ב) Night. This means an idle man who has no work, is healthy and refined, does not pay tax, and does not travel on the road. Tur. Rambam did not write this condition, that he does not pay tax; see Chelkat Mechokek. Beit Shmuel wrote: Rambam wrote specifically when he is exempt from tax, and the Tur omitted this; see there. On the contrary, the opposite is true. All this speaks where he is not a talmid chacham, but a talmid chacham, even if no work is imposed on him, he is healthy, and he does not pay tax, nevertheless Torah weakens his strength.
