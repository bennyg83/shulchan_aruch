According to Ran there is another distinction: if his practice is to work regularly in another place, his onah is once every seven days; and if his practice is not to work there regularly, his onah is once every eight days. He has permission to change his practice: if he had been accustomed to do his work in his house and his onah was twice each week, he may do work outside the city and his onah will be once each week.
