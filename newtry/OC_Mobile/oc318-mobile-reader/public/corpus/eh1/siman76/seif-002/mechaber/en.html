Workers — if their work is in the city, twice a week; and if their work is in another city, once a week. {Rama: And some say if they do not sleep every night in their homes — their onah is once in eight days (Tur and Tosafot in name of R' Yoel bar Baruch).} Donkey-drivers — once a week; camel-drivers — once in thirty days; sailors — once in six months. Torah scholars — their onah is once a week; and the way of Torah scholars is to use their bed from Friday night to Friday night.
