Seif 5 in the gloss: he may not delay more than a month. The measure of thirty days in Even HaEzer siman 76 appears not to distinguish whether there are no more than thirty days from one conjugal period to the next, or whether, because she is not tahor, there will be many days of delay before he leaves and, knowing the pattern of her veset, many further delays after he returns. Since Chazal stated the measure simply as thirty days, that is the rule. Nevertheless this still requires some further study, for perhaps the days of tumah are deducted from the thirty days, or at least one occurrence is deducted, like the ordinary veset in which there are always days of tumah within thirty days, while if there are more than that they are not deducted. Reason too suggests that one occurrence should be deducted. Even so, with a pregnant woman and the like the measure is thirty days. This still requires further study.
