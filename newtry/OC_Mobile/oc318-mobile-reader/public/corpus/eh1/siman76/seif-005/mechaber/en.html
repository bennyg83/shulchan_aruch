A wife may restrain her husband from going to trade except to a nearby place so he will not deprive her of onah; and he may not go without her permission. {Rama: And even if she gives him permission — he may not delay more than one month away and one month in his home (Tur and Rosh).} And likewise she may restrain him from work whose onah is close to work whose onah is far — such as a donkey-driver who wishes to become a camel-driver, or a camel-driver who wishes to become a sailor. Torah scholars go out to Torah study without their wives' permission for two or three years. {Rama: And likewise a traveler who became a Torah scholar — his wife cannot restrain him.} If she gives him permission — a Torah scholar may travel as far as she permits (Tur in name of Raavad).
