A woman who vowed an affliction vow or matters between them that he can annul — and he said to her: I annul for you on condition that you tell so-and-so what we spoke together in jesting words that a person speaks about intimacy matters; or on condition that she turn over after relations and not conceive; or on condition that she do a foolish act such as fill ten jugs of water and pour them in the garbage and the like — he divorces immediately and gives ketubah.
