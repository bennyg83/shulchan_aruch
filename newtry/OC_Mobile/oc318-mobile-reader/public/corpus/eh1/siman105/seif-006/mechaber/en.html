One who sells a debt document to his fellow, and then he goes back and forgives the debt, it is forgiven. And even the inheritor may forgive. Therefore, if she sells her ketubah and her husband dies and then she dies, her son can forgive the debt, and the sale will be invalid. And even if she has no inheritor except for this son, and it turns out that he has to pay her the ketubah, he can forgive it for himself in order to annul the sale and then he inherits the ketubah.
