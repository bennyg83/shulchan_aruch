One who seats his wife as a shopkeeper or appoints her as administrator cannot make her swear until she claims her ketubah. And some say he may make her swear whenever he wishes. (If he designated land for her in her ketubah — even though she comes to collect her ketubah — she is not called a claimant of ketubah and one cannot make her swear (Mordechai ch. HaKotev) according to the first reasoning.)
