Doubtfully betrothed. Here in Tosafot and other poskim I brought nearby clear kiddushin void for written "by mouth of two witnesses" and here not believed on their speech and oath upon them; and so implied from Ran she is not betrothed biblically for even when she admits kiddushin came to her hand — Ran compares to one who betrothes without witnesses and she is not betrothed. However in Maharit implies she is doubtfully betrothed and so R' Yosef Karo and Maharik and Rambam — even though oath upon them nevertheless oath is rabbinic hefsek oath and biblically no oath on them. And if one went with agent and testified he transmitted kiddushin — appears from Mahariq Beit Yosef brought it helps; not difficult Temimah Beit Yosef challenged; and likewise Taz and CM wrote does not help per Rashba; and reasonable for when monetary denial between them and witnesses want to swear — not believed; Tosafot and poskim wrote reason for "by two witnesses" means faithful witnesses on their speech; when not faithful on speech they are no longer witnesses; likewise here since not believed on his speech unless through assisting witness he has no witness status. Per this even two agents and one witness assisting them is not testimony.
