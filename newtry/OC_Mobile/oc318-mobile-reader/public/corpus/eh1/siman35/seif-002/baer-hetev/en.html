That she received. Bach and Chelkat Mechokek ruled stringently — even if she admits it is only doubtful kiddushin — see there. See innovations of Maharit. And if witnesses were exempted first from oath they are believed even if woman contradicts them and it is definite kiddushin biblically in name of Ran; and likewise Chelkat Mechokek. And Beit Shmuel wrote since in Shulchan Aruch CM 121 he brought only in name of "some say" it helps — therefore here it is not definite kiddushin. And if he went with the agents — also explained there in CM and there ruled in hagahah they are not believed to extract money; therefore here doubtful kiddushin.
Deposit. Specifically when money is in existence, or even not in existence and she admits she was negligent and wants to pay. But if money is not in existence and she says they gave for deposit and were lost — they are not believed for they are interested in their testimony as Kenesset HaGedolah — see there.
Betrothed. And if one went with the agent and testified he transmitted kiddushin — it appears from Mahariq that Beit Yosef brought it helps; and the difficulty the Temimah raised on him that Kenesset HaGedolah 64a wrote is not difficult for him. And CM wrote it does not help — see there; and Beit Shmuel wrote even two agents and one witness assisting them does not help — see there.
