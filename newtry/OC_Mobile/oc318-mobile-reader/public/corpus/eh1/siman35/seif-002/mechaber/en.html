The agent becomes a witness; therefore if he appointed two agents to betroth for him a woman no other witnesses are necessary. To what does this refer? When there is no denial of money — as for example he betrothed her with a document or even with money and she acknowledges she received it from him, but says she did not receive it for betrothal but for a deposit. However if there is denial of money and she says she did not receive money from them (or a document worth a perutah) (Maggid Mishnah ch. 3) — behold this is doubtful betrothal.
