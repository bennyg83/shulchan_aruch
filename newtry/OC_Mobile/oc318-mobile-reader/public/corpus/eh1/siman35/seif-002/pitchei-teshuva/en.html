Mitzvah he betroth in person. See Yad Malachi ch. 30 Laws of Shabbat law 6 who noted why we do not find this mitzvah applies more through agent only in two mitzvot — betrothal and Shabbat honor preparation — and not other mitzvot like Shabbat, theft, ma'akeh, hefker, mezuzah etc.; see there he wrote proper reason to distinguish; nevertheless for law his words are not correct and I omitted words of Magen Avraham OC 250 s.k. 2 who wrote plainly likewise in all mitzvot we say mitzvah applies more in person than through agent; and so implied in Gemara in several places — see there.
