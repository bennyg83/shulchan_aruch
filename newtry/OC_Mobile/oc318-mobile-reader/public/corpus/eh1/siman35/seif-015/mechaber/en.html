If the agent is not confirmed by witnesses and he says "I betrothed for myself" and she says "to the first" — and if the second said to the woman "You are betrothed to me" and she says "You did not betroth me" — he is forbidden to all her relatives but she is permitted to his relatives. If she says "I was betrothed to the first" and he says "I did not betroth you," or she says "I do not know" — presumption is for the second. If however the agent is confirmed by witnesses and he says "I betrothed for myself" and she says "to the first" — presumption is she is betrothed to the first {(even so the second is forbidden to her relatives) (Maggid Mishnah in name of R' Aharon)}. If she says "I do not know" — both give a get, and if they consent one may give a get and the other marries. {Hagahah: Some differ and say even if agency is confirmed, if the agent says he withdrew from his agency she is not betrothed to the first; and similarly if agency is not confirmed and the sender says he appointed the agent he is forbidden to her female relatives (Tur in name of Rosh). However if afterward the agent says "I thought I betrothed for myself but remembered I betrothed to my sender" — rely on the first opinion (Hagahot Mordekhai Kiddushin in name of responsum of Maharam). An agent who said "I betrothed you to the sender" and she says "You did not betroth me to the sender" — the sender is forbidden to her relatives on the agent's words.}
