Likewise from Rambam's words there ch. 9 from Yerushalmi (beginning of chapter); and Hagahot wrote and our Rabbi explains "confirmed" when he made him agent before witnesses and explained his words that this agent informed the woman he is agent of so-and-so; and if his appointment was before witnesses she relies on the agency and presumption is for the first etc.
Thus Ran explained Yerushalmi.
Hagahot Mordekhai in name of responsum of Maharam.
