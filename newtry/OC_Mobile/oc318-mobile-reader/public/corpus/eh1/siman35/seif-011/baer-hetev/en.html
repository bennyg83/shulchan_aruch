Investigate whether specifically when agent died; but if agent did not die and returned and said he betrothed him a woman who at time he was made agent was a married woman — is she betrothed? Or perhaps no; and what he cited "and died" is because he must say forbidden to all women in world. I found Maharam Mintz 39 wrote and from now even if agent afterward betrothed one of them when they were available it does not help etc.; his language proves even if agent returned and said he betrothed her she is not betrothed; and thus Ran — end Kenesset HaGedolah 63b. Per my view this is not in category of investigation for plain from Gemara Nazir 12a and R' Yochanan concedes etc. — see there Rashi and Tosafot; examine.
And married. Specifically they stood and married — certainly they did not do forbidden act; but otherwise we do not believe them they were not betrothed. Reason Tosafot wrote Gittin 64a and Tosafot Nazir 12a — penalty that he should not enter himself in doubt and should have commanded to betroth specific woman — see there; see Beit Shmuel.

Chelkat Mechokek, Taz, and Beit Shmuel disagree with Rama on this — see there. And if commanded to betroth specific woman and she says he did not betroth her — she is believed — CM Beit Shmuel.
