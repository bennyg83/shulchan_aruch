If one says to his agent "Go and betroth a woman for me" and the agent dies and it is not known whether he betrothed a woman for him or not — behold he is presumed betrothed, as presumption is that an agent completes his agency. Since he does not know which woman he betrothed him to, behold he is forbidden to all women who have relatives in forbidden degrees with her — for example a woman who has a daughter or mother or sister or the like; for if you say he may marry this one, perhaps her mother was betrothed to him by his agent, or her sister or daughter. But it is permitted with a woman who has no relatives; or for example if she has a female relative such as mother or sister etc. and that relative was a married woman when he appointed the agent — even though she was divorced before the agent died, behold he is permitted to her. And we do not say perhaps the agent betrothed him to her relative after divorce because she was not fit when the agent was appointed. And a man does not appoint an agent to betroth for him a woman unless she can be betrothed at time of agency. {Hagahah: And likewise if relatives came and said we were not betrothed and proceeded to marry — he is permitted to this woman for certainly the relatives were not betrothed (Ran ch. Hamekabel; Ribash 82). Some say similarly if he commanded to betroth a specific woman and the agent died he is immediately forbidden to her female relatives because presumption is agent completes his mission (Tosafot ch. Hamekabel); and some differ on this.}
