That presumption agent completes agency. We say this presumption stringently not leniently; and that we hold regarding eruv even leniently say presumption agent completes agency as OC siman 499 — because eruv is rabbinic as Eruvin 32; Taz wrote here resolves why not say double doubt perhaps did not betroth and if betrothed perhaps not this one he betrothed; and not so for this is double doubt one in substance one in mixture and such double doubt does not help in Torah matter.
That they are forbidden relation with her. This is biblical forbidden relation not secondary; likewise Rashi Nazir 14 forbidden to seven women as siman 16; likewise Perishah and CM and not like Taz.
And likewise if relatives came and said. Reason forbidden to all women even though no complete presumption here for agent lacks power to betroth her against will — Tosafot wrote reason is penalty since made agent to betroth any woman and did not specify which woman he would betroth; therefore minor whose father went to country — we do not fear perhaps father accepted kiddushin for her. Ramban wrote forbidden to all women per law due to presumption agent completes agency; minor whose father went permitted and we do not fear father betrothed her because she has presumption of availability; but this man forbidden to all women due to her relative perhaps betrothed mother or sister; presumption of relative does not help this one; if relatives said she not betrothed — this dispute of Ramban too — per Ramban helps; per first view their statement does not help due to penalty; practical difference in dispute if betrothed woman who has daughter or mother or sister — per Tosafot complete kiddushin; per Ramban we say presumption agent completes agency and double doubt perhaps betrothed sister daughter or mother.
And some differ. Rabbi Rama infers thus in Darkei Moshe from Tosafot who hold forbidden all women due to penalty — if so when commanded betroth specific woman not liable penalty permitted to her relative; and not so for Tosafot did not write this except to forbid all women — then forbidden only from penalty side; but if commanded specific woman and doubt perhaps agent betrothed her — forbidden to her relatives per law; CM and Taz preceded me; if commanded specific woman and she says he did not betroth her — believed even per Tosafot statement does not help due to penalty; but here where penalty does not apply her statement helps; thus CM's view.
