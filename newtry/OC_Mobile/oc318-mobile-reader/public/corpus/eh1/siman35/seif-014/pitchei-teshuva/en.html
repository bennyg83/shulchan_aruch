At time of agency. In responsum I wrote specifically regarding one who makes agent to betroth we say thus; but one who makes agent to matchmake any woman through matchmaker we do not say so. And all the more if one gives matchmaker to matchmake for his daughter and agent made match with one who had wife when he received authorization and he obligated himself with kinyan and penalty as customary — certainly sender obligated to fulfill matchmaking and cannot exempt himself with this claim that matter he cannot do now etc. — see there.
