And in all he changed. See Malbim ch. 10 Laws of Marriage law 20 who was uncertain if one tells friend go betroth me woman Ploni and he went and betrothed rabbinic kiddushin or doubtful kiddushin — is it change of agency; he expanded and concluded matter needs study. Clear from his words (in what he rejected proof from Nazir 12) if agent could not betroth her definite Torah kiddushin certainly no change; but per what he wrote afterward (to reject his proof) this distinction is not necessary. Further uncertain there: if we say he was particular — if sender says he is not particular but prefers even rabbinic kiddushin — what is law? Appears to him if particular we do not follow his words and even if he says not particular she is not betrothed; but he challenged from Kiddushin 51b and Rashi there implying wherever no explicit change only from assessment there is particularity — if he later reveals not particular it helps; concluded matter needs study (see Yad Malachi there what he wrote). Further Rabbi there was uncertain if one says go betroth me any woman and agent comes and says one of your daughters is betrothed to Ploni — for we hold all such are kiddushin not consummable and forbidden to all daughters and all need get; therefore no greater particularity than this plainly not in his mind he would betroth woman forbidden to him; he expanded and concluded afterward saw matter explicit ch. 2 Kiddushin 52 Tavyomei taught five daughters etc. (brought in Shulchan Aruch below siman 37 seif 20); clear from this even from appointed agent we say kiddushin not consummable are kiddushin; law is wondrous in my eyes and uncertain — end; editor there wrote perhaps somewhat distinguish between general agent and son or daughter where we equate agent to their father — see there.
