And gentile not son of covenant and slave etc. Thus read; and why ruled here slave invalid and in siman 145/141 brought poskim' view when sending agent doubtful get and likewise kiddushin as Beit Yosef wrote there; this law learned from comparison kiddushin to gittin as Maggid wrote; therefore likewise all invalid parties explained siman 141 invalid here.
That first agent. Here three views in Mordekhai ch. Hamekabel — can appoint agent when agent has money or document in hand; per Hagahot Mordekhai ch. 2 Kiddushin in name of Hagahot Mordekhai — cannot appoint agent since matter not finished for cannot betroth her against will perhaps she will not consent (and these are matters not transmitted to agent per Bach); and third view there appears distinguishes if betrothal document in hand — then cannot betroth her except with this document can appoint second agent; but when sends money without insistence if exchanges — cannot appoint agent; see Bach this siman.
Sender wrote through gentile etc. Implies does not transmit kiddushin in his hand but writes he will betroth from his money; but cannot say so for in get concern is get exits gentile's hand to agent's hand; but in such case all agree no concern.
Betrothed biblically. Implies per first view not betrothed biblically; and not so for even in get only ab initio forbidden; CM already noted this.
