All are fit for agency of betrothal except deaf-mute, insane, and minor because they lack mental capacity, and the Samaritan because he is not a son of the covenant, and the slave because he is not within laws of betrothal. {Hagahah: Some say the first agent can appoint a second agent (Mordekhai ch. Hamekabel in name of some) if he transmits to him the betrothal object the owner transmitted to him; but if the owner did not transmit betrothal money but only commanded him to betroth with any money he wants he cannot appoint an agent. And some say in all cases the first agent cannot appoint a second agent (Hagahot Mordekhai ch. Ha'ish Mekadesh). If the sender writes through a Samaritan to an Israelite friend elsewhere and appoints him agent to betroth him a woman — some say the law is as in gittin as explained below siman 141 (Rabenu Yerucham). And some say regarding kiddushin all agree she is betrothed biblically (thus Beit Yosef).}
