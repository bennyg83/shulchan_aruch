Laws of agency for betrothal. It contains 15 seifim. A man may appoint an agent to betroth for him a woman, whether any woman or a specific woman, and the agent says to her "Behold you are betrothed to Ploni." However if it is possible for him to betroth her in person it is forbidden to betroth her through an agent unless he knows her, lest afterward he find in her something reprehensible and she become reprehensible to him. Nevertheless even though he knows her, it is a mitzvah that he betroth her in person if possible.
