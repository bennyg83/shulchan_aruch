May make agent. See responsum Berit Avraham 119 who brought words of Noda Biyhuda 64 who debated how one can betroth through agent — behold it seizes another's property where he owes others; and resolves since she has free choice and consents to this automatically her will is not in another and it is not chalifin and not like found object — see there. And he wrote on this one can derive novel law regarding woman doubtfully betrothed — law that if she received kiddushin from two both kiddushin take effect and she needs get from second too; per above reasoning one can say specifically if second betrothed himself; but through agent even if commanded explicitly and change of agency does not apply — Maharshal debated this — Malbim ch. 7 Laws of Marriage law 20; nevertheless one can say in such case agency does not help since doubtful first kiddushin already fell on woman she no longer has choice to retract from first; and since bound to him she will consent because "better to sit" etc.; and through second kiddushin she is forbidden to first and second owes first and second agency helps nothing even to create doubtful kiddushin. He added further on law mentioned in Shulchan Aruch below siman 40 seif 3 "immediately and after thirty days" and second betrothed within thirty — one can say likewise specifically if betrothed himself but through agent does not help etc.; and further investigated per this since through agent does not help one can say also no condition applies and if second conditioned some condition the condition is void and act stands — see there at length.
