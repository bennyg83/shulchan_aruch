Agent. In Be'er Sheva 118 he wrote it is not proper conduct to equate a Torah scholar to an agent; and Kenesset HaGedolah wrote the custom now is to appoint an agent for a Torah scholar even if he is much greater in wisdom. And Beit Shmuel wrote ab initio one should not make an agent to betroth any woman — see there.
In person. Specifically regarding mitzvah they say thus; but regarding blessing on mitzvah it was explained in previous siman that ab initio another may bless. And likewise mitzvah that the father betroth his daughter in person and not through agent. And what we are not careful about this and make matchmaking and the groom does not see the bride — because it is only matchmaking and before kiddushin he sees her; for this they relied on the meal made at night before the chuppah so he sees her in it; however Tosafot Kiddushin 41 and 71 wrote permission afterward — see Derishah Beit Chadash.
