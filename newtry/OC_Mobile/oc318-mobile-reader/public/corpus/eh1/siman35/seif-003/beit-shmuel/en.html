As way of admission. Language of Ribash 193 — here is no admission but like recounting matters since he did not say "you are witnesses"; and when said before one witness even if said "you are witness" — no admission for he did not insist to admit before one witness; see this law in CM siman 81 and there ruled admission before one witness is admission; and what written here as way of admission and did not write must say "you are my witnesses" — because we hold if said in manner of admission even without saying "you are witnesses" it is admission as CM wrote. All this discusses if wants to deny afterward denial does not help after admission; and CM wrote Ribash discusses specifically reception agent but in sending agent no need complete admission but when not speaking jokingly it helps and not implied from Ribash; see there; see siman 141 if witness joins his appointment and there I wrote what is difficult from there to here.
And some say also in man's agency. This is Ra'avad's view; he wrote what Rambam compared betrothal agent to divorce agent — I differ for get proves on him; implies we discuss here specifically money kiddushin; but if agent with betrothal document witnesses not needed since document in his hand; even though below siman 141 ruled sending agent and get in hand needs witnesses — I differ get comes to permit; Rosh was uncertain if distinction between get and kiddushin for this reason; therefore was uncertain only in reception but not sending; there ruled sending needs witnesses; here implies rules like Rambam and his doubt only in woman's agency.
And some say even in woman's agency. Thus Rosh: we can distinguish get that comes to permit from kiddushin that come to forbid; and we equate man's and woman's agency in kiddushin; Ran too wrote matter not comparable at all — in get she removes herself from husband's domain impossible without witnesses; but in kiddushin she forbids herself — if husband's admission suffices for agency why not woman's admission for reception agent.
And if no witnesses. Meaning kiddushin were before witnesses but witnesses not before us and woman and agent say it was before witnesses — another's kiddushin take effect; even though we hold presumption agent completes agency — we hold this presumption stringently not leniently; even if man and woman come in presumption he is her husband — we do not apply another's kiddushin even though no attachment to us she is his wife but due to presumption we conduct as man with wife — we do not compare presumptions; there explained even agent confirmed before witnesses and she knows done before witnesses — nevertheless another's kiddushin apply and not as man with wife and not as CM.
Doubtfully betrothed. CM wrote appears plain agent lacks power to forbid woman's relatives on sender since he denies agency; and one witness in denial is nothing except regarding woman who cannot deny him — doubtful betrothal needs get from doubt and forbidden to his relatives therefore. Seemingly nevertheless forbidden to her relatives for she needs get from him since doubtfully betrothed as siman 49; perhaps here we compel him since agent betrothed her and said made him agent; not like mere person saying betrothed — denial testimony does not help there no get needed; here needs get; CM wrote permitted to her relatives since witness denied; perhaps intent when he did not give her get meaning she died.
And thus principal. And in Darkei Moshe concludes even father who received daughter's kiddushin and she denies made him agent — she is not betrothed; these words copied from responsum of Mahari Mintz; from this responsum decided like Ra'aviah she is not betrothed; examiner there sees discusses agent's denial and not relevant to this dispute; and decision to lenient side uncertain CM; see siman 141 there ruled doubtful get due to get in hand; but here discusses money kiddushin and cannot compare to simanim 17 and 115 witness from witness and first denies; Rosh holds need not lose his money; therefore also leniently cannot deny him; different here for testifies about himself that made him agent.
