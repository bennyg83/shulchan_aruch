Witnesses are not needed when the man appoints an agent to betroth for him a woman, only that the agent and sender acknowledge the matter (this is not considered admission unless he admits before witnesses as is the rule for admission) (Ribash 193). However when a woman appoints an agent to accept betrothal, witnesses are required. And some say also when a man appoints an agent witnesses are needed. {Hagahah: Some say even if a woman appoints an agent without witnesses we must be stringent (Tur in name of Rosh). And if there were no witnesses at the betrothal and the woman and agent say she was properly betrothed, if another comes and betrothes her she needs a get (Ribash 82). And if there are no witnesses to the matter and the sender denies he appointed him agent, some say it is doubtful betrothal (Mordekhai ch. Ha'ish Mekadesh) and some say she is not betrothed at all (Ra'aviah and R' Judah Mintz) and thus appears to be the principal view.}
