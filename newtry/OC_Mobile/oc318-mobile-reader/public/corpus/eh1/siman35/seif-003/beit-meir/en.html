Hagahah some say not betrothed at all — Ritva's language; we conclude person not brazen to make father agent; appears from here regarding ordinary person without that reason who betrothed woman for friend — we fear perhaps agent made her need get; even though agent did not claim; all the more if claims; specifically with matchmaker as here; one can say regarding ordinary person plain we fear perhaps acted on own; regarding father we fear unless that reason; this correct; nevertheless should be stringent as implied; but matchmaker need not be stringent; one can say specifically without agent's claim; with claim even without matchmaker should be stringent; see Ran 225b; see end siman 46 responsum of Rosh.
