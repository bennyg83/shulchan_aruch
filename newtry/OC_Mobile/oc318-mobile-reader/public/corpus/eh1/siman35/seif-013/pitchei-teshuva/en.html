And man does not make agent etc. See Malbim ch. 9 Laws of Marriage law 6 who brought words of Maharit CM 23 wondering on Tosafot Nazir there who taught one who makes agent to betroth woman after her husband divorces her — does not help perhaps Gemara's intent is presumption man's intent is not to make agent except in what he can do now; but if he specified explicitly he wondered on him for Gemara there proves clearly even explicit agent does not help (see Markevet HaMishnah ch. 6 Laws of Betrothal halakhah 3 who expanded to uphold Maharit's words and reject Malbim's attack; also Yad Malachi ch. 9 Laws of Betrothal wrote to resolve Malbim's difficulty and uphold Maharit — see there). He wrote further per this one should judge woman who was doubtfully betrothed — e.g. he threw kiddushin doubt close to him doubt close to her and another came and made agent to betroth this woman after first betrother divorces her — what is law? Do we say considered agent for if he betrothed her kiddushin would take effect and therefore considered matter he can do and properly can make agent; or perhaps since he could not betroth her complete kiddushin considered matter he cannot do and therefore not considered agent; he expanded — see there.
