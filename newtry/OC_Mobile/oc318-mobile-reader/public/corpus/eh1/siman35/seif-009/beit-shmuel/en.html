One who makes agent etc. In Gemara explained likewise if not his agent but one says to friend when you go there betroth me woman and he betrothed for himself — deceitful practice.
This is deceitful practice. Rambam wrote ch. 8 law 1 called wicked.
If not betrothed with money etc. Read: with money one does not betroth with coin; when betrothing with sender's money — when insistence if he exchanges then robbery in his hand; but matter without insistence and agent may exchange — not robbery.
And understood well. Meaning do not say although her intent to betroth agent it is matters of heart since said at first came to betroth sender; and what said "to me" — explain "to me" to make agent betroth you to whom I sent — teaches if she understood agent returns and says to me betrothed — agent betrothed; see Tosafot there; she believed to say if understood; see Agudah ch. 1 siman 13; if said did not understand and intent was for first — Perishah wrote perhaps needs get from both for betrothed to one; cannot say he said to me and exempt without get; cannot say if so why Tur did not write here too no concern as nearby; however no reason she needs get from both unless say we do not believe her saying did not understand or due to onlookers; therefore what wrote she not believed did not understand — not as Agudah; Taz wrote when she says did not understand needs no get from one or two; see nearby.
But if agent erred etc. Must say item without insistence to exchange then perhaps returned and betrothed himself unless says erred — believed; but if item with insistence — then robbery in his hand; from where stringency needs get from agent — either way not betrothed to agent for if returned from agency betrothed by robbery; therefore item without insisteness or sender asked he give from his own for kiddushin. Difficult what she says: if says intent for first — how wrote some stringent require get from agent; I already wrote she believed; if says intent for agent — how wrote no concern — implies definitely betrothed to first; per Perishah when says intent for first needs get from both due to onlookers — properly explains if says intent for first and stringent hold agent not believed saying erred — needs get from both; for Taz who does not hold need get due to onlookers — difficult here.
And agent believed. Implies without oath; Agudah wrote in name of Maharam responsum — believed with oath.
That he erred. Even though we hold not believed to say I erred except with migo as CM; here no migo — must say here there are logical grounds he erred — he is agent of Ploni and presumption agent completes agency.
And some stringent. See Maharam Metiktin margin — distinguishes responsa; wrote stringent discusses agency not confirmed in court; or say returned from agency; but if agency confirmed in court we say erred; appears this reasoning from Gemara Rama wrote in name of Yerushalmi.
