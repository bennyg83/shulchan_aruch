If one appoints an agent to betroth for him a specific woman and the agent goes and betrothes her for himself — behold this is deceitful practice, and what is done is done if he did not betroth her with the sender's money. Even if he said to her first "Ploni sent me to betroth you to him" {and at the hour of betrothal he said to her "Behold you are betrothed to me" — behold she is betrothed to the agent (Tur)} — provided the woman heard and fully understood before accepting betrothal that he said "Behold you are betrothed to me"; because otherwise she accepted betrothal according to what was said at first — to betroth her to Ploni. However if the agent erred and said "You are betrothed to me" there is no concern, as consecration by mistake is not consecration. {And the agent is believed to say he erred; and some are stringent to require a get.}
