What he did is done. See responsum Noda Biyhuda second vol. 71-72 that even if agent did not betroth himself only through his agent she is betrothed and we do not say here ein shaliach l'devar aveirah and agency void as explained Noda Biyhuda first 75 because essence of transgression here in not fulfilling his agency that friend relied on him; even if he did not betroth himself at all. And again what he betrothed himself does not add sin and ein shaliach l'devar aveirah does not apply to kiddushin — see there.
