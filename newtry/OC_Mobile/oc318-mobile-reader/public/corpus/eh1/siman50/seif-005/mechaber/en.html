Reuven matched his daughter to Shimon with kinyan and oath and deposited documents with a third party; afterward the engaged woman's sister became an apostate — Shimon may retract and is exempt from the kinyan, oath, and document obligation (meaning the penalties they made between them). And if the third party returned the document to the engaged woman he accomplished nothing. The same if the engaged woman became deranged — the engaged man may retract. And likewise if the engaged man ruined his conduct — the engaged woman may retract and is exempt from kinyan, oath, and document; and if the third party returned the document to the engaged man he accomplished nothing. {Rama: Some say all this is before erusin; but after erusin "his field was flooded" (Maharik root 171). And see Yoreh Deah siman 228 seif 43 and siman 232 seif 16.}
