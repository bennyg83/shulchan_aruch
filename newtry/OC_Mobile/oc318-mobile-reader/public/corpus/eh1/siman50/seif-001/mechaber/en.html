When kiddushin money and savlonot are returned; laws of penalties. It contains 7 seifim. One who betroths a woman — whether she retracted or he retracted, or he died or she died, or he divorced her — the kiddushin money is never returned; even if he betrothed her with a thousand dinar, behold they are an absolute gift without retraction. And if they were mistaken kiddushin — the money returns. And likewise if she refused (mi'un). And likewise if the kiddushin were conditional and the condition was not fulfilled. And likewise if they were doubtful kiddushin (and all this applies only from erusin; but from nisuin the kiddushin money returns (Maharil siman 61)).
