(א) Such as where he stipulated to her, etc. This means these conditions even after the essential text, or other conditions phrased "on condition" before the essential text where he then erased them. Rambam holds that it is a safek get; so wrote Maggid Mishneh. But if it is "except" and it was not erased, the get is void. A condition phrased "on condition" after the essential text is valid even if it was not erased, as written in the Shas; the reason is that at the time of writing it was written properly, and there is also no reservation, only a condition. But with "except," even after the essential text, there is a reservation. Although it was written properly, nevertheless there is a reservation; although he later erased it, nevertheless at the time of writing there was a reservation, and it is a safek if he erases it. All this is according to Rambam's approach. The approach of the other poskim I wrote nearby.
