Mitzvah to rejoice the groom and bride, and law of a corpse and bride. It contains 4 seifim. It is a mitzvah to rejoice the groom and bride and to dance before them and to say she is fair and gracious (meaning: from "and bear grace before Him") even if she is not fair. {Rama: And we find that R' Yehuda bar Ilai would dance before the bride and they suspend Torah study for bringing a bride to chuppah (words of the Rav) (first chapter of Megillah and chapter The Woman Who Was Widowed).}
