One must place ashes on the groom's head in the place of laying tefillin — a remembrance of mourning for Jerusalem, as it is written: to give to mourners of Zion beauty instead of ashes. {Rama: And there are places that practiced to break a cup after seven blessings — and this is the widespread custom in these lands that the groom breaks the vessel on which they bless the blessing of betrothal (Kol Bo). And every place according to its custom. Further they practice fasting (and see siman 61). And the law of throwing food before groom and bride see Orach Chayyim siman 171.}
