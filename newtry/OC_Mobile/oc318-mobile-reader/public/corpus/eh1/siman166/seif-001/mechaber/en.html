A yevama (widow subject to yibbum) goes [to court] according to the place of the yavam (living brother of her husband), and the court advises them appropriately, whether to consummate yibbum or to undergo chalitzah (ceremony to break levirate bond). Rama: This rule applies when the yavam is in his place of residence, and there is a court in town. But if he has traveled elsewhere, we do not force the widow to go after him (Nimukei Yosef, end of chapter Zeh Borer - Sanhedrin). Similarly if he lives among Kuthites (non-Jews) or non-educated [Jews] who do not know the rules of chalitzah, the judges are not required to go after him, but can force him to come to the place of assembly (court) and he frees his yevama (Hagahot Alfasi chapter Mitzvat chalitzah - Yevamot).
