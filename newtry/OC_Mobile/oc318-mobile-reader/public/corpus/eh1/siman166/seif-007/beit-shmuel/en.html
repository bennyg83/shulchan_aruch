(ב) Even with initial penetration. Although we hold that anyone who has relations intends full intercourse, nevertheless with a yevamah initial penetration suffices; even if he said explicitly that his intent is for full intercourse, he acquires her through initial penetration, since the Torah included initial penetration as acquiring. We do not require his intent, for even if both were coerced, he acquires. So wrote Ran, chapter 1 of Kiddushin, and Nimukei Yosef at the beginning of chapter HaBa Al Yevimto. Initial penetration acquires even with deficient intercourse, meaning unintentionally or for promiscuity; nevertheless with initial penetration he acquires. Likewise he acquires in an unnatural manner. Tosafot and the Tur. Although it is written "to establish his brother's name," Tosafot wrote that the verse comes to exclude where the yavam and yevamah are unfit to establish, excluding an aylonit and a saris. But where they are fit to establish, even if this act of intercourse cannot establish, he acquires.
