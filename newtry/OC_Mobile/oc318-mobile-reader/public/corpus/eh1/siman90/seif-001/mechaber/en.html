Law of the husband inheriting his wife and what he does not inherit, and law of a man's sale of property collateralized to his wife, and the claim "I did it for my husband's pleasure." It contains 20 seifim. The husband inherits his wife — whether melog property or tzon barzel property. When does this apply? In what is possessed; but not in what is due, such as an inheritance she was fit to inherit and died in her bequeather's lifetime — he does not stand in her place to inherit. {Rama: Document of half male share customary nowadays where the father writes for his daughter to take in his inheritance like a half-male share, and if the daughter goes without surviving offspring the obligation is void — if the daughter died in her father's lifetime and left surviving offspring and the offspring died and afterward her father died — the husband inherits the power of that document through his offspring who would inherit his mother, and he inherits his offspring (Mahariu siman 16). If her bequeathers wrote for her land from lifetime and produce after death — the husband inherits that land from her (Mordechai start of Yesh Nochalin). But enhancement he takes in what he inherits from her, for he enhanced it with his authority. And he does not inherit his wife's loans, such as if her bequeathers died and they had a loan in others' hands when she married and she died before collecting them. {Rama: But if her bequeathers left property even though the daughter did not collect in her lifetime, and even if they were collateralized for a widow's ketubah — they are called possessed for the daughter and the husband inherits them (Mordechai chapter Kol HaNishba'in). And likewise if she had a loan in others' hands when she married and the woman died before collecting it — he does not inherit it; but if she lent from melog property and died before collecting — in this the husband inherits it. {Rama: All property that belonged to the woman over which the husband has no authority and she lent them to others — the husband does not inherit them (Beit Yosef in name of responsum of Rashba).}
