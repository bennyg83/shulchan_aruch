A man may not sell movable property of tzon barzel nor pledge them; and if he sold or pledged them — what he did is done. {Rama: And some say his sale is void. And a woman's garments that she brought him do not have the law of tzon barzel but only melog as explained above siman 77; therefore if he sold or pledged them it is void for all — so it seems to me. And some say if he said he did what he did with her authority he is believed with oath; and likewise if he said he gave her the money (responsum of Rashba simanim 336 and 337). An Israelite who collected land from a Samaritan for his debt — if the Samaritan has authority to redeem it, it has the law of movable property and the woman has no power to protest that he not sell them; but if the Israelite took possession of it and the Samaritan cannot redeem it — it has the law of land and the lien of the woman's ketubah is upon it like other land (Terumat HaDeshen siman 339).}
