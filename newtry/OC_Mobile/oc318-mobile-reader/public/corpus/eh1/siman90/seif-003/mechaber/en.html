One who marries a woman subject to a negative prohibition — since kiddushin take effect in her, if she dies under him he inherits her. (And likewise a woman whom the husband made an apostate — he inherits her) (Beit Yosef ch. 20 in name of Avi HaEzri). And likewise one who marries a minor who needs refusal — even though her kiddushin are not full kiddushin, if she dies under him he inherits her. But a competent man who married a deaf woman — even if she became competent after he married her, if she died he does not inherit her; and a deaf man who married a competent woman and she died — he inherits her, for she is of sound mind and married him knowingly and granted him authority.
