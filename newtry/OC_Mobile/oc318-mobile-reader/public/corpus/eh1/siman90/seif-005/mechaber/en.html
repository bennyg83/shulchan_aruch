One whose divorce was doubtful and she died — the husband does not inherit her. {Rama: But as long as she was not divorced, even though he set his mind to divorce her — he inherits her (Tur in name of Rosh). One who claims on his wife that she had blemishes and his transaction was a mistaken transaction as explained above siman 39 — if she died he does not inherit her (responsum of Maimoniyot end of Laws of Marriage and in Mordechai chapter Mi SheMet). A woman who rebelled against her husband and died — the husband inherits her; so it appears from Rambam's language ch. 22. And see above siman 77. One rebellious against his wife or forbids her and does not conduct with her the conduct of marriage — some say if she died he does not inherit her (Hagahot Mordechai chapter Af Al Pi).}
