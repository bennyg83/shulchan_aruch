One who said my property to so-and-so and after him to my heirs — we have established the same law applies to heirs of heirs. And if he had a daughter and she died in that so-and-so's lifetime and the daughter had a son — the son stands in his mother's place to inherit that so-and-so; but if the daughter has no heir except a husband he does not inherit that so-and-so for it is due to him. And if he said my property to so-and-so and after him from now to my heirs — it is possessed and the daughter's husband inherits.
