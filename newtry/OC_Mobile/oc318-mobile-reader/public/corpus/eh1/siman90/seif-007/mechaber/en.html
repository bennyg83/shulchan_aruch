A woman who wrote all her property to another whether near or far before she married — even though if she became a widow or was divorced the gift would be void — the husband does not eat their produce; and if she died in his lifetime he does not inherit her, for she gave them before she married; and when she dies in her husband's lifetime the recipient acquires his gift with full acquisition. And not only that but even if she gave part of her property or all of them beforehand and wrote for the recipient "acquired from today and when I wish" — since he did not acquire full acquisition until she wishes — the husband does not eat produce of that gift; and if she died he does not inherit her.
