Property unknown to the husband — when they become known to him they immediately become melog property; and likewise produce found in her hand — from when they become known to him they are melog property. {Rama: A woman who said regarding an inheritance that fell to her that her father instructed to give from it to others — if the property is known to the husband she is not believed; however, if the husband died she must uphold her admission. And if the property is not known she is believed (Beit Yosef in name of responsum of Rashba).}
