And if the husband admits or there are witnesses that she sold with his authority — he does not extract from the buyers, for his agent is like him, and if the buyer wishes he can declare that she did not sell with his knowledge. {Rama: If he saw she sold and was silent he does not lose his right on account of this (responsum of Rosh kelal 40). If she instructed at illness to give such-and-such from her garments and the husband agreed — if she gave to poor people he cannot retract; and if to rich people he can retract (Mordechai end of HaZahav and responsum of Maharam). And see above siman 86 if she sold or pledged movable property.}
