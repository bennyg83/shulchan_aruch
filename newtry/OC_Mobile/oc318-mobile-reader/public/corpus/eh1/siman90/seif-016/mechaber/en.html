If both sold melog property — whether the buyer first took from the man and then took from the woman, or first from the woman and then from the man — their sale stands. And likewise a woman who sold or gave melog property to her husband — her sale and gift stand, and she cannot say regarding melog property "I did it for my husband's pleasure." But regarding other property she may say so. How so? A woman who sold or gave to her husband from nichsei tzon barzel — whether land or movable property, or a field he set aside for her in her ketubah, or a field he wrote for her in her ketubah, or a field she brought him from her own — her husband did not acquire; and even though they acquired from the woman's hand with her consent she retracts at any time she wishes, for she did not give or sell except for domestic peace; therefore the husband has no proof at all in his wife's property except melog property unless she accepted liability upon herself explicitly. {Rama: Some say specifically her sale and gift do not help; but if she waived to her husband all right she has in the property it helps as waiver helps regarding ketubah (Raavad and Rosh). And some dispute (Ramban and Rashba and Rambam per Maggid Mishneh's view).}
