A woman who sold or gave after marriage tzon barzel property — whether to her husband or to others — she did nothing. (And some say nevertheless when she becomes a widow or is divorced her sale stands) (Nimmukei Yosef chapter HaChovel). And likewise a husband who sold land from his wife's property — whether tzon barzel or melog — he did nothing. And some say that even he himself can protest the sale (and even if she died in his lifetime the husband inherits her power and can void it) (Tur).
