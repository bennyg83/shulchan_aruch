When do these matters apply — that if she sold after marriage the husband extracts from buyers regarding property known to the husband; but if property fell to her and the husband did not know initially — ab initio she should not sell; and if she sold them before he knew — her sale stands. And likewise an arusah ab initio should not sell property that fell to her from when she became betrothed; and if she sold before marriage — her sale stands (even property known to him).
