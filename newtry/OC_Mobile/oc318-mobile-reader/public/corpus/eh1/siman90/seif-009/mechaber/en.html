A woman who sold melog property after she married — even though those properties fell to her before she became betrothed — the husband extracts produce from the buyers all her days, but not the body of the land; and if she died in his lifetime he extracts the body from the buyers without payment. (And some say even in her lifetime he extracts the body of the land from the buyer without payment) (Tur and Rosh). And if the money she took from the buyers remains in itself (or money was found in her hand and we can attribute that these are they) (Tur in name of Rosh) — they return to the buyers and he cannot say perhaps they are found objects. {Rama: If she became a widow or was divorced — her sale stands (Tur and majority of poskim). And all her days if the husband wishes to build or demolish on the land the buyer can protest (Nimmukei Yosef chapter Chazakat HaBatim). And there is no difference whether she sold the property or consecrated them (Terumat HaDeshen siman 272).}
