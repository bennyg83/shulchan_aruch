Movable property he gave her from his own — their law equals tzon barzel property that he may not sell them ab initio; and likewise movable property he acquired for her even if they did not come to her hand, and even if he has nothing with which to sustain himself. And there is no difference between Shabbat and festival garments and weekday garments; but gold vessels and crystal he may sell if he needed to sustain himself from them. And if relatives gave her whether ornaments or garments — the husband may sell them to sustain himself.
