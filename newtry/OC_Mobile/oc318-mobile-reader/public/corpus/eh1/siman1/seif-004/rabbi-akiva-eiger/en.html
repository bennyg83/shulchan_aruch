Note: And in the responsum of Rashba siman 803 and siman 1181 it is explicitly implied that whenever [the Sages] did not enact marriage it is like promiscuity, and it is forbidden to marry a woman to one's minor son. And according to this approach, the difficulty of Tosafot Yevamot 113 in the passage "lest he feed her" is resolved — that a minor [marrying] an adult woman is uncommon since it is forbidden to marry them off until they are eighteen years old.
