He is obligated, etc. This is taught in the Mishnah in the chapter HaBa Al Yevimto, folio 61: a regular kohen, etc., as explained there in the Gemara. The Mishnah there also states, "he may not refrain, etc." As the Gemara there says, "I have only, etc.," and it states, "in order that, etc.," meaning that his intention should be for the sake of children. For at the end of Taanis our Rabbis taught: "The beautiful among them, etc., provided that you adorn me, etc." This means with ornaments, for a woman's beauty depends upon her ornaments, as stated in the first chapter of Megillah: "provided that she come naked," because a woman is for adornment. This is also what is stated in Kesubos 59b: R. Chiyya taught, "A woman is only, etc.," and R. Chiyya further taught, etc. These are the three matters stated here. The Tanna of the Mishnah, however, stated only, "Set your eyes upon the family," because a woman is for children. "Charm is false" refers to ornaments, for charm results from something external, as stated regarding Esther, who was greenish, etc., yet it says, "Esther found favor, etc." "And beauty is vain, etc."
