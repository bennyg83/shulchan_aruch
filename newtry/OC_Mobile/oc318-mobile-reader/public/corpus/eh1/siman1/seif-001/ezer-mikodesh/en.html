**Seif 1 (to marry a woman).** Upon the father there is a mitzvah to marry off his son — it implies even an exceedingly adult son who is not at his father's table; nevertheless it is incumbent upon him because he is ashamed. And in a second match one can say it is lenient because he is not ashamed as is customary. And to marry before twenty years — one can say the son should not rely on his father and will not be ashamed to send discreetly. "To marry" — one can say kibbud av does not apply; and if they say no — for grace of acquisition is on his portion. And it is written "a man shall leave his father," etc., "and cleave to his wife" — meaning desired for his portion well. And what is not from grace but only for money and the like — kibbud av is incumbent; and if they wish to marry. And after palm-slapping of conditions as is customary — kibbud av no longer applies. Only when money nullifies — permitted initially if practiced thus.
