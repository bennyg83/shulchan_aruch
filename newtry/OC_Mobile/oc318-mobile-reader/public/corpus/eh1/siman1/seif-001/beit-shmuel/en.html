Every man is obligated to marry a woman. He is also obligated to have relations at every appointed onah. Even if his wife permits him to withhold her onah, he remains obligated to have relations at every onah until he has children. So writes the Rambam in chapter 15 of Hilchos Ishus, and so it appears below in siman 76. This implies that after he has children her permission is effective even if he is still capable of fathering children. I do not know the Rambam's source for this distinction, that her waiver is effective after he has fulfilled peru u'revu but not before. For even after fulfilling peru u'revu he remains obligated to fulfill, "In the evening do not withhold your hand." Although I wrote nearby that this later mitzvah is only rabbinic, from where do we know that her waiver is effective? I saw that the Maggid Mishneh wrote there that the Rambam learned this from the sugya in the chapter Af Al Pi. The Mishnah teaches that students may depart without permission for thirty days, and the Gemara asks how long they may depart with permission. It answers that with permission they may depart as long as they wish. This shows that permission is effective. Yet if so, why is her permission not effective even before he fulfills peru u'revu? The sugya plainly does not discuss nullifying the mitzvah of peru u'revu. If so, from where do we know that permission is effective to nullify the mitzvah of "In the evening do not withhold your hand"? Perhaps the case is one in which he fulfills that mitzvah in the place to which he travels. Rather, the sugya addresses only her ability to waive her own onah.
