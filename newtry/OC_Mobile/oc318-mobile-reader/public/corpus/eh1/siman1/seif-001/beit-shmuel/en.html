Every man is obligated to marry a woman. He is also obligated to have relations at every appointed onah. Even if his wife permitted her husband to withhold her onah, he remains obligated to have relations at every onah until he has children. So Rambam writes in chapter 15 of Hilchot Ishut, and so it appears below, siman 76. This implies that after he has children her permission is effective, even if he can still beget. I do not know Rambam's source for this distinction, that her waiver is effective after fulfillment of peru u-revu but not before it. For even if he has fulfilled peru u-revu, he is still obligated to fulfill "in the evening do not withhold your hand." Although I wrote nearby that this later mitzvah is only rabbinic, still, from where do we know that her waiver is effective? I saw that Maggid Mishneh wrote there that Rambam learned it from the sugya in chapter Af Al Pi. The Mishnah teaches that students may leave without permission for thirty days, and the Gemara asks: with permission, how long? It answers: with permission, as long as he wishes. We learn from there that permission is effective. Yet this is difficult: if so, why should her permission not be effective even before he has fulfilled peru u-revu? Rather, the sugya certainly is not discussing the cancellation of the mitzvah of peru u-revu. If so, how do we know that it is effective to cancel the mitzvah of "in the evening do not withhold"? Perhaps it speaks where he fulfills that mitzvah in the place to which he goes. Rather, the matter is that she can waive her own onah.
