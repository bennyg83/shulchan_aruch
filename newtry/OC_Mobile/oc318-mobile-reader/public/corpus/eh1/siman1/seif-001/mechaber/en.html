The laws of periah u'rviah (being fruitful and multiplying) and not standing without a wife. It contains fourteen seifim. Every man is obligated to marry a woman in order to be fruitful and multiply; and anyone who does not engage in periah u'rviah is as though he sheds blood, diminishes the Divine image, and causes the Divine Presence to depart from Israel. {Rama: Whoever has no wife dwells without blessing, without Torah, and is not called a man; and since he married a woman his sins are wavering, as it is said "He who finds a wife finds good and obtains favor from Hashem" (Tur).}
