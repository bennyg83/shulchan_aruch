To marry a woman. See Baer Hetev in the name of Beit Shmuel, who raises the difficulty that even after fulfilling peru u-revu he is still obligated in "in the evening, etc." And see in Sefer Birkei Yosef by the Gaon Chida of blessed memory, who finds it satisfactory to distinguish: if he has fulfilled peru u-revu he is not obligated to fulfill the marital duty (onah) every period; for "in the evening do not withhold" it suffices to fulfill occasionally, as long as he does not neglect it entirely. And this is what Rambam and Maran write (below siman 76:6): if he has not fulfilled peru u-revu he is obligated every period; but if he has fulfilled it, he is not obligated every period — neither for peru u-revu (already fulfilled) nor for onah (since she waived it); but he is nonetheless obligated at times on account of "in the evening do not withhold" — see there. And what he wrote that Ra'anach was uncertain, etc. — these words of Ra'anach are cited at length in Pitchei Teshuva to YD siman 234 sub-section 9 — see there.
