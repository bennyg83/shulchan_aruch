A woman. He is obligated to fulfill the marital duty (onah) every period — even if she has granted her husband permission to forgo it, he is obligated every period until he has children (Rambam ch. 15, halacha 1), and likewise below siman 76:6. It follows that after he has children her permission is effective even if he could still produce more. But this requires examination: from where does the Rambam derive this distinction that her waiver is effective after fulfillment of peru u-revu, given that even if he has fulfilled it he is still obligated in "in the evening do not withhold your hand" — see Beit Shmuel there. Ra'anach part 2 siman 43 was uncertain whether a verbal waiver alone is effective with no ability to retract — see there.
