Beit Shmuel note 2; and see the responsum of Radbaz who wrote that a woman is commanded in shevet. Note: And from the words of the Ran in responsa siman 32 it is inferred that it is a mitzvah for a woman to marry — from the sugya at the beginning of chapter 2 of Kiddushin, where it is called "a matter of mitzvah" for the purpose of annulling her vow — see there. He does not derive this from the law of shevet, which implies the Ran holds a woman is not commanded in shevet.
