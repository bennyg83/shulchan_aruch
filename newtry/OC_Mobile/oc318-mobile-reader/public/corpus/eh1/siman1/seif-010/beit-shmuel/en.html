Rabbenu Gershom imposed the cherem, etc. The reason is explained in Maggid Mishneh on Ketubot — on account of quarreling was the enactment made, and not on account of a trace of prohibition. And Darkhei Moshe wrote: a practical difference from this is where there is a dispute in the enactment — the lenient party does not lose, since there is no concern of prohibition. He also wrote: now, after the year 5000, the time has already passed, and from then on it is only a custom that they practiced to be stringent; and you are not permitted to permit it in their presence; therefore in a case of doubt we establish the law of Torah.
