R"G imposed cherem on one who marries upon his wife; but regarding yevamah he did not impose cherem; and likewise regarding betrothed. {Rama: If he does not wish to consummate but only to release her (Maharik root 101); and similarly in every place where there is postponement of a mitzvah — such as if he stayed with his wife ten years and she did not bear (Mordechai, chapter haCholeitz, Rashba siman 280, and Maharam Padua siman 19). However, some disagree and hold that R"G's cherem applies even in a place of mitzvah, and even in a place of yibbum one must perform chalitzah (Hagahot Mordechai on Yevamot and Hagahot Mordechai on Ketubot, and so too Nimukei Yosef, chapter haCholeitz). And in a place where the first wife is not fit for divorce — such as if she went insane, or he is legally required to divorce her and she does not wish to take a get from him — one may be lenient to permit him to marry another (so is implied in Rashba's responsum); and all the more so if she is betrothed and does not wish to marry him or to be released from him.} And his enactment did not spread in all lands. {Rama: And specifically in a place where it is known his enactment did not spread; but stam it applies in every place (responsum of R' Yisrael Mintz siman 110). And see Yoreh Deah siman 290 if one went from a place where they practiced stringently to a place where they practiced leniently.} And he only imposed cherem until the end of the fifth millennium. {Rama: Nevertheless, in all these countries the enactment and custom stand in its place and one does not marry two wives; and they compel with cherem and nidui those who transgress and marry two wives to divorce one of them. And some say that in our time one does not compel one who transgressed R"G's cherem (Beit Yosef in siman 76) since the fifth millennium has already been completed (there in the name of Maharik). And they do not practice thus — some say one whose wife became an apostate — he gives her a get through another and marries another woman; and so they practice in some places (Piskei Maharai siman 256). And in a place where there is no custom one should not be stringent and it is permitted to marry another without divorcing the first (there, customs of Rinus).}
