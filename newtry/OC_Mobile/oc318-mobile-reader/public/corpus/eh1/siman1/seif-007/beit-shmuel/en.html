Children whom he had while he was a non Jew. Although we rule that a convert is like a newborn child, since he already fulfilled peru u'revu while he was a non Jew, when his offspring were genealogically related to him, he is exempt. So write Tosafos. See Terumas HaDeshen siman 277 regarding the firstborn, where this difficulty also arises, yet this answer does not apply there. This requires study. Tosafos imply that he fulfills peru u'revu even if his children did not convert with him. Responsa Maharil siman 223 states likewise. The Mechaber, however, rules like the Rambam and Tur, who hold that this applies specifically when the children also converted. Although the Shiltei Giborim rules like the Maharil, and his reason in the responsum is clear, for he compares a half slave to a non Jew because he holds that even if the children did not convert he fulfilled peru u'revu, the Mechaber's ruling concerning a half slave does not rest on this reason, but on the other reasons stated there in the responsum.
