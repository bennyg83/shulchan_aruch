Beit Shmuel note 20: her agreement is not effective. Note: In the responsum of Maharival at the end of siman 21 he transcribed the words of the Ran who wrote: regarding the cherem of Rabbenu Gershom not to take two wives — can the woman permit it and give her husband permission? If we say the Gaon of blessed memory was concerned that since women are subordinate to their husbands, if her permission suffices, the husband will provoke her until she agrees — then for the French scholars ("chachamei tzarfat") the right of redemption applies: they know how this enactment spread in their localities — end quote. And see Machaneh Ephraim, Laws of Oaths, siman 10.
