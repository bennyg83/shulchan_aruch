And he converted. In the responsum of Mahari Segal siman 223 he ruled that even if his sons did not convert with him he fulfills peru u-revu. And so it appears from the Tosefta Yevamot 62a in the passage beginning "Rabbi Yose says: he fulfills peru u-revu," etc. And Kenesset HaGedolah ruled that gentile children do not count toward fulfillment of peru u-revu — see there. And see what Beit Yosef wrote. And see what the Tosafot in Chagigah 2b wrote in the passage beginning "He did not create it to be desolate," etc., where they conclude "and furthermore, peru u-revu is written regarding all the children of Noah, even regarding Canaan" — see there. And this is against the sugya in chapter four of [Arba Meitot] where they say peru u-revu was stated for Israel and not for the children of Noah — see there. And so too Maharsha, who left it as undecided. And in the responsum of Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 134 he was asked about this and answered: the Tosafot in Chagigah wrote this as an answer for the one who holds that the children of Noah are commanded even regarding castration, according to Rabbi Chidka who said so in chapter four of Arba Meitot. And do not wonder at this, for Semag also ruled thus, and so too Tur HaShulchan siman 299, whose words are cited in Beit Yosef at the end of siman 5. And we must say: although it is straightforward in the Gemara that the mitzvah of peru u-revu was not stated for the children of Noah — meaning they are not compelled to fulfill the mitzvah of peru u-revu, and the choice is theirs not to engage in it at all — nevertheless, to castrate oneself in a manner that makes it impossible for them to engage in peru u-revu, this certainly remains forbidden according to Rabbi Chidka. And if so, regarding this half-slave half-freedman who is like literal castration — unable to marry a maidservant or a free woman — the Tosafot properly wrote that in this matter even the children of Noah are commanded — end quote. And I heard to answer based on Tosafot Yevamot 62a in the passage "sons of peru u-revu, etc.": "And if you ask — from chapter four of Arba Meitot it seems that gentiles are not commanded in peru u-revu" — one can answer that here he does not speak of those obligated in peru u-revu, but rather that his sons are called by his name — see there. Accordingly, one can explain what Tosafot wrote in Chagigah that peru u-revu "is written regarding all the children of Noah" — meaning: his sons are called by his name, and from this that they are relevant to peru u-revu even though they are not obligated. Thus I heard — but I did not understand it, for the Tosafot conclude "even regarding Canaan," and there they say that everyone agrees regarding a slave who has no lineage — see and think well.
