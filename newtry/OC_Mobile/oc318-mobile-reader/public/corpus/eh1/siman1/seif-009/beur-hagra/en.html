And in a place, etc. This is comparable to a donkey driver who became a camel driver in the chapter Af Al Pi. These are the words of the Ritva in the first chapter of Kiddushin 7a, where it states: "This is what he means: if I wish, etc." Although even without this stipulation we say that a man may marry several wives, etc., in a place where the custom is otherwise, she married him relying upon the custom, and it is as though they expressly stipulated. Therefore he must make such a stipulation with her, etc. See there.
