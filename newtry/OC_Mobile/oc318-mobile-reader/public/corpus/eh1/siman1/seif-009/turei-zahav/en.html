Provided that he is able to provide for them. Chelkas Mechokek writes that the same applies if she is willing to suffice with little and waives her claim. Beis Shmuel writes that in Yevamos, even when she waives it, beis din is obligated to give her suitable advice not to marry him, and that this is proven by the Gemara on folio 44. This is not found in the Gemara or the poskim. They say there that advice is given to him, not to her when she herself wishes to marry him. So too, the Tur in siman 165 writes that when she refuses on the claim that he is foolish and young and lacks the means to support her, they persuade him, etc. Beis din is not obligated to advise her against the marriage when she is willing.
And in a place where the custom is not to do so, etc. Darkei Moshe writes that her consent is effective to permit him to marry another woman, but in a place where the enactment of Rabbeinu Gershom is observed, her consent is not effective.
