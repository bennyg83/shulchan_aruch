A man may marry several women, when it is possible to support them. Nevertheless, the Sages gave good counsel that a man should not marry more than four women so that he reach each one's onah (marital relations) once a month. And in a place where they practiced to marry only one wife — he is not permitted to marry another woman upon his wife. (And see end of siman 2 that it is forbidden to marry two wives in two places.)
