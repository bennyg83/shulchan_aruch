And a female. See Baer Hetev, and what he wrote "and if the son is alive and has not yet fathered, etc." is according to Beit Shmuel; and see in the Glosses of Yad Ephraim, who wrote regarding this that according to Tosafot (at the beginning of this sub-section) — that three males coming from a male and female suffice — it goes without saying that this also works. And what he further wrote "there is a question: a woman who became pregnant in a bathhouse, etc." — see Sefer Bnei Ahuvah ch. 15 of Hilkhot Ishut halacha 6, what he wrote about this on Choshen Mishpat's words; and he also at length there refuted the Mishneh LaMelekh in that chapter — see there. And also in Sefer Birkei Yosef in this siman note 14 he wrote at length about this.
