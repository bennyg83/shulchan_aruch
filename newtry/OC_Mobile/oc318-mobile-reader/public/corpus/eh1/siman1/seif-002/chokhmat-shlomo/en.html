Seif 3 **However, if he is engaged in Torah, etc., he is permitted to delay, etc.** Note: See in Beit Shmuel who wrote there is no limit to this, and he is permitted to delay as much as he wishes; and accordingly it appears all the more so if he marries a woman who is not fit to bear children and he knows that through this he will not be distracted from his study; and if he marries a woman fit to bear children he will need to be distracted from his study — he is also permitted to do so. And all the more so she — what to sit without a woman at all, for there is further side of concern for sin; and it is double nullification of PUA and concern for sin. And he is permitted all the more so if he marries one not fit to bear children — for there is no concern for sin, only nullification of PUA; if through this he will not nullify his study, and through this he would nullify — he is permitted. And all the more so if he feels in his soul according to his strength that he is not fit to bear — he can marry a woman who is not fit to bear children. And in what Nekudot Yosef wrote, brought afterward in Rama in seif 8 — and even though there it speaks only regarding Shabbat, which is only rabbinic; nevertheless it appears there is no distinction in this, since assessment is a matter and we go in many places on rabbinic prohibitions according to assessment; if so, here too this helps for a Torah matter as well — clear in my humble opinion in this; examine well.
