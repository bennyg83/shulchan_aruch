**Seif 3 (they are not accustomed to compel).** What they turn the community's eyes from compelling — one who lived with his wife ten years and she did not bear a child that he divorce her; as written in responsum Rivash and in Shulchan Arukh. And according to the majority they do not even say gently without compulsion in this. There is merit on this from the side that there is also a double doubt. Doubt — the impediment is from her side only. And there is also doubt whether they will accomplish the matter and it will be heard. And there are more doubts: doubt from her side there will be prevention from receiving a get; doubt from his side there will be prevention from divorcing her — whether from love of her or from desire for the ketubah money and the like; doubt that both of them are not fit to bear. And this too is not a balanced doubt — for the majority of the world bear children; and here, since presumption of companionship was established, it suffices to hang the companionship on one of them and not on both. On all this perhaps "fixed" applies here. I also wrote elsewhere pilpul in the name of Maharach regarding one doubt balanced and the second not balanced; regarding what Pnei Yehoshua wrote on this in first chapter of Ketubot; and Magen Avraham siman 463. And in any case it is fit to combine several of the above doubts; and there is also a side that it is forbidden to divorce his first wife. And there is also svara regarding Eretz Yisrael, may it be rebuilt speedily in our days. For this there is complete merit for turning a blind eye — see this fully. But if the couple has already begun compromise dealings about divorce — it is right to enter himself to complete favorably the divorce; for presumably he knows in his soul he is fit to bear and there are no more doubts at all. And in any case it is right to inform him that from doubt he is exempt from she'er and kesus — perhaps by this they will compromise. And so I did and informed one who was already engaged before with his wife regarding a get because she did not bear him more than ten years; and they were already close to compromise — only from love of gain they did not compromise; and he is a craftsman and wealthy. And she too can easily earn a livelihood in comfort, or be in service with a household owner and have more than enough for her livelihood. And she can also marry an old man.
