One may not sell a Sefer Torah except in order to study Torah. This includes supporting students, and the allowance to sell it in order to marry includes marrying off orphans, as stated in Orach Chaim siman 153. It is likewise forbidden to sell other sacred books, as stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 270. The Chelkas Mechokek wrote that a Sefer Torah may be sold specifically for the mitzvah of peru u'revu, but not to marry off an orphan girl, since she is not commanded in peru u'revu. It may be sold only to marry off an orphan boy because of peru u'revu. However, according to what is stated below, that a Sefer Torah may be sold to fulfill "In the evening do not withhold your hand," one can say that it may also be sold to marry off an orphan girl in order to fulfill "He created it to be inhabited," for a woman is also commanded concerning inhabiting the world, as Tosafos wrote in Bava Basra folio 13 and Gittin folio 41. The phrase "in order to marry a woman" therefore also includes one who has already fulfilled peru u'revu. Thus, one may sell it to marry off a man who already fulfilled peru u'revu, or to marry off an orphan girl. The Magen Avraham likewise wrote this in siman 153. However, the Rama's statement at the end of seif 13 in the name of the Shiltei Giborim, that a woman should not remain without a husband because of suspicion, implies that she is not commanded concerning inhabiting the world. This is also implied by Tosafos at the beginning of Chagigah, and even the Tosafos cited above wrote that a woman is commanded concerning inhabiting the world only according to one answer. See Responsa Radach, who also wrote that a woman is commanded concerning inhabiting the world. See Orach Chaim regarding an individual who owns a Sefer Torah and wishes to sell it and use the money as he desires.
