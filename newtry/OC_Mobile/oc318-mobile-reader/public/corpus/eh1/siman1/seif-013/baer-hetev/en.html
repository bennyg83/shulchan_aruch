Suspicion. So too Rambam ch. 15 halacha 1. It is a wonder, for in ch. 21 of Hilkhot Ishut he writes "a woman has the right never to marry, or to marry a saris" — see there. And one can reconcile: what Rambam wrote in halacha 1, that she should not remain without a husband, is from the perspective of good counsel — so that she should not be suspected. Whereas there in Hilkhot Ishut — according to strict law — a woman has the right never to marry, for there is not even a rabbinic prohibition upon her, neither because of shevet yitzrah nor because of improper thoughts — unlike for a man. And so too Kenesset HaGedolah, and the Radbaz in Beit 17 Cheder 10.
