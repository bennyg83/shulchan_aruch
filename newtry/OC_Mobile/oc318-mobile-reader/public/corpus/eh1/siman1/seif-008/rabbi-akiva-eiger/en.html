Siman 13 in the gloss: because of suspicion. Note: Rambam ch. 15 halacha 16 of Hilkhot Ishut. And Radbaz Beit 6 Cheder 10 wonders at these words of Rambam in light of what he wrote in ch. 21 halacha 1 of Hilkhot Ishut: "a woman has the right never to marry." And Kenesset HaGedolah in his glosses on the Tur, note 19, wrote to resolve: that in halacha 12 [Rambam] speaks of a case where there is no suspicion, such as a city where all are women or eunuchs.
