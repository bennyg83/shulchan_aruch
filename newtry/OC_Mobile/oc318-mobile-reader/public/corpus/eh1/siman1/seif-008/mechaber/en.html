Even though he fulfilled periah u'rviah it is forbidden for him to stand without a wife; and he must marry a woman fit to bear children if there is doubt before him — even if he has several sons. And if there is no doubt before him to marry a woman fit to bear children unless he sell a Sefer Torah — if he has no children he sells in order to marry a woman fit to bear children; but if he has children he does not sell but rather marries a woman who is not fit to bear children and does not stand without a wife. And some say that even if he has children he sells a Sefer Torah in order to marry a woman fit to bear children. {Rama: However, if he recognizes that he is no longer fit to bear children and is no longer suitable to father, he marries a woman who is not fit to bear children (Nimukei Yosef, chapter haBa al Yevimto). And likewise if he has many children and fears that if he marries a woman fit to bear children quarrels and disputes will come between the children and his wife — he is permitted to marry a woman who is not fit to bear children; but it is forbidden to sit without a wife because of this concern (Terumat HaDeshen siman 263).}
