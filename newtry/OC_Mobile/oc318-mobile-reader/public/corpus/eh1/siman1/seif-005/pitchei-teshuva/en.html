They were not accustomed to compel. Birkei Yosef wrote that so too Maharshal in Yam Shel Shlomo chapter haBa al Yevimto siman 40; but in Sefer Kiryat Melekh Rav in his commentary on ch. 15 of Hilkhot Ishut he wrote that any court that does not compel in this is not acting well and the custom should not be heeded. And Birkei Yosef further wrote that Maharikash deduced from the Mechaber's language "and he does not wish to marry, etc." — implying that if he wishes but only delays until he finds a suitable match or for another reason, we do not compel him. Nevertheless, it appears that one should not delay for a reason external to himself beyond twenty-four [years] (see Kiddushin 30) — see there.
