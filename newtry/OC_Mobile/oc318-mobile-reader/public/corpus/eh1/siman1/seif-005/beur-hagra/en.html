That is, provided that, etc. In Kiddushin there it states: "If it is impossible without, etc." Shmuel and R. Yochanan disagree with the opening clause of the Baraisa, as stated there: "The halacha is that he marries, etc.," meaning unlike the Baraisa. R. Yochanan, however, upholds the Baraisa. It follows that the Baraisa concerns a case in which marriage would interrupt his Torah study, yet even so, if it is impossible for him to remain without a wife, etc., he must interrupt his study in such a case.
