Choshen Mishpat note 10: in a manner that will not dispute this. Note: We have merited to see the Ritva's novellae on Yevamot, where it is stated explicitly that one should not sell a Sefer Torah to marry a woman unfit to bear children. And it seems to me that the Ritva explains the teaching "there is a practical difference — to sell a Sefer Torah" as meaning there is an additional practical difference to sell a Sefer Torah, as Tosafot wrote in their first answer; and this is the view of "some say" — that what the mishnah teaches, "he should not desist from peru u-revu," means that even when he has children he must marry because of "it is not good," etc.; nevertheless, the practical difference is that for peru u-revu one must sell a Sefer Torah, but not for "it is not good." And this is the mishnah's ruling — hence "the mishnah disagrees with Rabbi Yochanan." But according to us who hold like Rabbi Yochanan, even if he has already fulfilled peru u-revu, he must marry because of "in the evening do not withhold." However this applies only to a woman fit to bear children. But for a woman unfit to bear children — since the mitzvah is only because of "it is not good" — in that case the ruling stands not to sell a Sefer Torah, just as the mishnah's tanna rules, and so too for us regarding a woman unfit to bear children. Such seems to me the intent of the Ritva.
