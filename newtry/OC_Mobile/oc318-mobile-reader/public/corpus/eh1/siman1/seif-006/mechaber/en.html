A son and daughter were born to him and they died and left him grandchildren — behold he has fulfilled the mitzvah of periah u'rviah. In what case? When the grandchildren were male and female and descended from male and female — even though the male is the son of his daughter and the female is the daughter of his son, since they come from his two children he has fulfilled the mitzvah of periah u'rviah. But if he had a son and daughter and they died and one of them left male and female — he has still not fulfilled this mitzvah. {Rama: If the son was a mamzer or deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor — he fulfilled the mitzvah (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba).}
