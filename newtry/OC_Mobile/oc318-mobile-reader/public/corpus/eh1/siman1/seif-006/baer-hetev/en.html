And a female. It implies [one needs] two males — and certainly two females are insufficient. But Tosafot in Yevamot 62b hold that with two males coming from a son and a daughter, one fulfills peru u-revu. And Shitah Gedushah added that even with two daughters coming from a son and a daughter one fulfills it. If he had two males and they left a male and a female — or if he had two males and one of them had a daughter — he has not fulfilled peru u-revu, since he himself must first father a male and a female. If the son is alive and the daughter died leaving a daughter, he has fulfilled it; but if the son is alive and has not yet fathered children and the daughter died leaving a son, he has not fulfilled it. There is a question whether a woman who became pregnant in a bathhouse [without intercourse] — does the father fulfill peru u-revu, and is the child considered his son for all purposes (Choshen Mishpat)? And Beit Shmuel brought proof that he is his son for all purposes.
Mamzer. Specifically a known mamzer who is alive. But an unknown mamzer, or one who is not alive — he has not fulfilled peru u-revu.
