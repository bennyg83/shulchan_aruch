The Tur explains this as close after their maturity, and not like Rashi and Tosafot, who explain it as close before their maturity. This is also what is meant in the first chapter of Kiddushin, "if I had married at fourteen, etc."; that is, in the fourteenth year, so he was thirteen years old. See Yoreh Deah siman 245 seif 8 and Hagahot there, for otherwise it should have said, "if I had married at thirteen," since the closer it is to maturity the better. It therefore appears as I wrote. But before thirteen he did not say so, which shows that it is forbidden, as written there: one who marries a woman to his minor son. One might have thought that even after thirteen it is also called such, as written in chapters 7, 10, and 14 of Yevamot, that the Sages did not enact marriage for a minor.
