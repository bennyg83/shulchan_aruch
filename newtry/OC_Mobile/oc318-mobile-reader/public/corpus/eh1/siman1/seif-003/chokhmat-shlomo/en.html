In that seif in the gloss **And even if he married a woman, etc.** Note: See in my responsum Likutei Kevod in Arshan, acquired place in Yoreh De'ah laws of mixtures siman 105, in my Toldot edition 7 — there I wrote that even those who permit cherem Rabbenu Gershom when he stayed ten years — that is only if the husband married his equal, e.g. a young man to a virgin and the like who are equal in years; but if a young man married an old woman, or at least one much older than him — according to everyone they do not permit cherem Rabbenu Gershom, since he acted negligently and married an old woman and did not fulfill Chazal's words "go to your kind" — see there and examine well.
