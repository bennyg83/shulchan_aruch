Beit Shmuel note 5: and the Rosh wrote there is a fixed time limit for that learning. Note: In the responsum of Rav Moshe Mintz siman 42 he wrote: but one can distinguish — this applies specifically in their days, when there was a limit to their learning and they did not need all their days to study with their teacher; therefore one learns first and then marries. But now, "I and one such as you, who wishes to be in the master's house all his days" — in that case he should not marry all his days, for "always," etc. — and at the end he concludes: nevertheless, if you can learn independently even when you have a wife and cannot come to the master's house, it requires examination whether we apply "a sword upon those who sit idle."
