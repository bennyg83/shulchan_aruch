**(I found further)** regarding turning a blind eye in matters of matches — regarding marrying an old woman; and one who stays ten years without a child and does not divorce her; as written in Shulchan Arukh in the name of Rivash — besides what Rivash wrote there reasons for turning a blind eye in this. Besides this, according to the majority there are doubts — doubt that he can no longer bear; and all the more so if it has not yet been established that he can bear. And even if he already bore — nevertheless there is doubt that perhaps from now he cannot bear anymore. And this is not against such a strong majority and presumption regarding one who stayed ten years and did not bear — for from her side too presumption applies because of the majority of women who conceive and bear. And all the more so if she already bore and miscarried or her children died — eighty from the time of the children's death as written in Beit Yosef in Beit HaEfodi in the name of Maggid Mishnah in the name of Rashba in Mordechai haRiv — see there; in this both are equal and why do you see to hang that the delay is from her — perhaps from him? And she is not commanded regarding peru u-revu, and we have no need to involve ourselves in this at all. And doubt that even if he divorces her — perhaps a proper match will not present itself to him, and some barren woman or old woman will present herself before him. And also that from an old woman he can guard himself, and the majority of women are not barren. On all this — from the side that the owner of a lost object must seek his loss — behold this is in a presumption of fixed, and majority does not apply; as Chazal said in Ketubot first chapter regarding "he goes to her." And also that now we judge stam what he will marry — whether from the majority or the minority — and there would be room to say that fixed does not apply here, like we establish that they go after the majority regarding refusal and chalitzah since the majority are not aylonit. Nevertheless there it is different — for we judge on what is before us, one soul, and decide by the world's majority; for even among those who move there is also majority in this. And in any case among those who move in the entire world there is decision through one alone; unlike here, where we do not judge on the soul before us — only we judge when he returns to marry a woman what will fall in his fate; and in this fixed applies as above — and see and consider. And perhaps this was the reason they do not compel when she did not bear, or bore and the children died and she aged — and according to what is known in women's custom she is no longer fit to bear; for also by law of the Gemara they do not compel in this; as written in Beit Yosef siman 154 in the name of responsum Rosh — see there. And only when she stayed ten years is there Chazal's enactment to divorce. Also when according to law it would be right to turn a blind eye from doubt or the above doubts — and there are further doubts: perhaps he himself will awaken tomorrow to divorce, and we need not involve ourselves in this; and all the more so for those who hold they do not compel fools, only in matters where there is further doubt that perhaps compulsion will not help at all in the matter (and all the more if they already involved themselves and urged once — as happened here, where I urged one who has no children with many harsh words; and there is also doubt perhaps he is not fit to bear; and there is here one who was established with several women that the impediment is from him; and they did not prevent a child from her since she is not commanded regarding peru u-revu); and all the more so in the length of the exile — that according to the majority it is impossible to establish religious judgments on their basis through compulsion; and all the more so regarding one whom they know knows obligation of peru u-revu — that from presumption of his fitness there is to remove him from presumption of majority who bear, and perhaps he cannot bear; and in any case perhaps a woman will not wish to marry him because she thinks perhaps he cannot bear and will say "I want a staff," etc.; and regarding what is only an enactment through custom — there is merit. Similar to the above in doubts regarding one who stayed ten years and did not bear — so too regarding bachelors who stay more than twenty years and do not marry; in this one can say perhaps he feels in himself he is not a son of sons, and perhaps he avoids this himself and a proper match does not present itself to him; and perhaps he will not heed even after warning; and perhaps in his haste an unsuitable match will present itself — not fit to bear or not his match — and he will not return to it; and all the more so in this, that it was not explained in Chazal's words to involve ourselves and compel — the above excuses help to merit the custom of turning a blind eye; and according to human nature sometimes there is cause for opposition to honor of the holy Torah if rabbis involve themselves in this regularly; and perhaps according to this one can explain what Chavot Yair and Beit Shmuel wrote to be precise whether staying ten years is more severe than marrying an old woman — see there in siman 1, that there are aspects for every side; for from the side that Chazal explained the enactment to divorce regarding one who stayed ten years — from this side staying ten years is more severe, for the enactment removes from the doubts; and similarly also inclines that when he sees the delay is from her she would wish to divorce from him — as with every marriage of a woman, the mitzvah on her side is through her becoming subsidiary to the mitzvah; and so too here, when she divorces and it will turn around through her that he bears — she too will have good reward from this; and there is a side that marrying an old woman is more severe now, since the enactment of compulsion to divorce through staying ten years was weakened through custom to turn a blind eye; and similarly; and they consider this a doubt that perhaps now he returns and "do not act" is preferable — since now there is doubt that compulsion is only in words or with fools; and if the intent is with fools — there is concern that compulsion in words is close to loss than gain, since better they be unwitting, etc. (The man I spoke with — regarding staying ten years and not bearing — and similarly we heard of him that he already knew well according to many that there is no cherem of Rabbenu Gershom and obligation to divorce; and even if he did not hear of the expropriation of cherem Rabbenu Gershom in this — nevertheless he already knew certainly that there is obligation to divorce, and wished to compromise with her to give her much — so I heard previously; and through this was what I urged him in this; and what I said before him that there is no cherem Rabbenu Gershom is close to gain, etc.) — so too sometimes on a side far from gain to loss; and from doubt it is impossible to compel with fools; and according to majority it is impossible to compel in the bitter exile; and similarly; and after the custom weakened compulsion — marrying an old woman is more severe, for it nullifies the mitzvah by hand. And she too will not so quickly agree to divorce; and he will not marry her since he inclines to gain; and he will marry an old woman; and through an old woman birth is more deferred than through one with whom he stayed ten years — for it is common in this that sometimes she bears also after staying ten years; and see and consider in this; and similarly that in these countries now it is not common for a child to marry an old woman; and so it is correct:
