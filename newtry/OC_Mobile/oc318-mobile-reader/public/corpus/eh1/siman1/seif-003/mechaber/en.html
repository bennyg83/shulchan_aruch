It is a mitzvah upon every man to marry a woman at eighteen years; and one who marries earlier at thirteen — it is a mitzvah of the finest quality. But before thirteen he should not marry, for it is like prostitution; and in any case he should not pass twenty years without a wife; and one upon whom twenty years have passed and he does not wish to marry — bet din compels him to marry in order to fulfill the mitzvah of periah u'rviah. However, if he is engaged in Torah and labors in it and fears to marry a woman so that he not be burdened with sustenance and be idle from Torah — he is permitted to delay. {Rama: And in our time they have practiced not to compel for this; and likewise one who did not fulfill periah u'rviah and comes to marry a woman who is not fit to bear children — such as a barren woman, an old woman, or a minor — because he desires her or because of her money — even though by law one should protest against him, they have not practiced for many generations to be exacting regarding matches; and even if he married a woman and stayed with her ten years — they have not practiced to compel him to divorce her even though he did not fulfill periah u'rviah; and similarly regarding other match matters (Rivash siman 15); provided only that she not be forbidden to him.}
