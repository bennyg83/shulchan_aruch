Without a yud. Because with a yud it implies a language of heresy. Levush and Beit Shmuel. In the responsum of Mahari ben Lev, part 3, siman 69, he wrote another reason: so that it not sound like the name of a man named Yannai, and that the shtar says that the divorce and release are from that man named Yannai and not from him, etc. See in my commentary to Seder Get Rishon, letter 103, what I wrote regarding this.
