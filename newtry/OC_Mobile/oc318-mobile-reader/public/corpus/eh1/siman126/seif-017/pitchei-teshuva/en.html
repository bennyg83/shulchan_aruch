He should not write vediyn. See Chelkat Mechokek. See Get Pashut, who writes that the practical difference between Rashi's explanation and the Rambam's explanation is where this woman is forbidden to him and he is obligated by law to divorce her. If he wrote vediyn full, according to Rashi the get is valid, because even if it is interpreted as "it is law that I divorce you," that is the truth of the matter. But according to the Rambam the get is invalid, because the meaning of the word vedan is nullified, and we require that he write vedan so that it be evident from within that he is divorcing her with this get, and we require clear indicators, etc. See there further.
