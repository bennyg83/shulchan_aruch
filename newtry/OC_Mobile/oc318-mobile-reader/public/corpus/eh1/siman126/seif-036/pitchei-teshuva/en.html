Yatichi. See Get Pashut, s.k. 86, who brings that Ralbach in responsum, siman 129, wrote that if he wrote yatichi as two words, the get is certainly invalid, for he changed the intent of the divorce, since he must dismiss and send away his wife, and with this wording he dismisses himself from her, and it is invalid. He also wrote there that if he wrote the word leichi as two words, the get is invalid. Mabit there disagreed with him, and Ralbach returned and challenged Mabit and upheld his own words. See there.
