One must be more careful. In Get Pashut, s.k. 51, he writes as follows: This "extra care" does not refer to the measure of spacing, but to the extra care that Maran the Beit Yosef wrote. However, in Terumat HaDeshen, siman 230, he explained that one should distance it more. The custom is to distance it, but one should not invalidate if he did not distance it excessively. I saw a get that came from overseas, and the scribe erred and wrote in the word lemehach a reish-shin instead of a final kaf, so that it read lemehar. They validated it, since there is no change at all in the meaning of the divorce. On the contrary, he told her that she should hurry to marry any man she wishes. In the responsum of Mahari Segal, siman 103, a get that came from a distant land had the final kaf of lemehach with a roof twice as long as its leg, so that it was read as a dalet, and he invalidated it. To me it appears that one should validate in a place of igun, since the word lemehach is in the tofes and there is no change at all in the meaning of the divorce. Even if the word lemehach was skipped, it should be validated bedieved. End quote.
