(א) it appears divorces and if there is no divorces it is get.
(ב) Yod. According to Rashi, it is language of a roof. According to this it is a change in the meaning of the word. According to Rambam it implies language of a zonah, meaning: if you committed adultery it shall be a get, and if you did not commit adultery it shall not be a get. If so, it is a change in the get and is invalid even in pressing circumstances. Tosafot wrote that one must write "ve-iggeret" with a vav, for without a vav it implies that he sends an ordinary letter, not for divorce. He should read the dalet of "vedein" with a tzere, and write "bedin" at the beginning of the line so that "sefer terukhin" through "piturin" is on one line and not on the last line. Seder HaGet of Maharam Padua; Beit Shmuel.
