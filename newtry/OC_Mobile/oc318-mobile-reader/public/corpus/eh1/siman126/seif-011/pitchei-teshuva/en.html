The thirtieth of the month of Nissan. In Get Pashut he writes that it appears one should write "of the month of Nissan," as above, seif 4. See there. It somewhat implies that what he concludes, that it is Rosh Chodesh Iyar, should not be changed to write "the head of the month," since that is what it is called in common language. See Chelkat Mechokek, s.k. 18.
