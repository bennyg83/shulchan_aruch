And he should write days. See Ba'er Heitev, that if he wrote "day," it is not invalid bedieved. See the responsa in Gevurat Anashim regarding where the word "days" is entirely missing and it merely says "on the third of such-and-such month." The view of the Gaon Morenu HaRav Yehoshua, of blessed memory, is that it is obviously valid, because in most places it is written "on the first of the month," "on the tenth of the month," "on the twentieth of the month," and similar expressions. However, from the words of my grandfather, the Gaon Maharam Katz, of blessed memory, in his responsum there, it appears that he was uncertain about this, since the get says "on the fourth day of the week, on the third of such-and-such month," and one may err and read it as three weeks of the month. See there. It implies that from the fifth onward, he too agrees. See what I wrote below, seif 21, s.k. 29.
