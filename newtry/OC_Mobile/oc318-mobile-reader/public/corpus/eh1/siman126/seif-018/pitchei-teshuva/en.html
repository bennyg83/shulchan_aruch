And iggeret. See Ba'er Heitev. Get Pashut writes in the name of Radbaz, whose words were brought in the responsum of Rabbi Betzalel, siman 23, concerning a get that came from another country and had written in it "ve'agirat shivukin." The language ve'agirat is a language of remembering. The Radbaz responded that it is valid in a case of igun, and said that the beit din should correct it, as the Tur wrote in the name of the Rosh at the end of this siman.
