In the final line there must be written neither "a document of divorce," nor "a bill of dismissal," nor "a letter of release," but only "according to the law of Moses and Israel." He should make those words in large letters so that he does not finish in the middle of the line, because there is concern that they may sign in the remaining half-line; and it is not proper to do so. If, however, he wrote in the final line more than "according to the law of Moses and Israel," it is not invalidated thereby.
