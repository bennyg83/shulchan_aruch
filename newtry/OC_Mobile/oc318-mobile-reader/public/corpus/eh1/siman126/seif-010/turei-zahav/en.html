(א) It should be written with three yods, for then the vav of "teheviyin" is read with a sheva and there is a chirik under the yod, so its meaning is the feminine singular addressed directly to her. But with only two yods it sounds like women in general. The words "ve-di teheviyin" are two words, for "ve-di" stands in place of "asher," and since the following word begins with a tav, it is proper to write a yod after the tav, "teheviyin," and it is language of direct address. But if it is all one word, then the tav is swallowed and is not felt, and it is not proper to write a yod after it; if so, it is not language of direct address.
