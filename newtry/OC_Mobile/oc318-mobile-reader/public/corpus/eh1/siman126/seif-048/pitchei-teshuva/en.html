One should validate. In Get Pashut he writes: This requires study. Why did Maran, of blessed memory, not write that one should correct them by attaching them, as Maran wrote nearby regarding attachment of the legs of the hehs to their roofs? Perhaps because this matter, that someone else can correct even though he was not commanded by the husband, was said by the Rosh, of blessed memory, in that responsum regarding not lengthening the vavs, as the Tur wrote at the end of this siman. We see that the Rivash, siman 56 and 315, holds that it is preferable to give it without correction, since the child reads it as a vav. If correction were required, how could another person correct by lengthening the vavs? Maran held that regarding writing, such as lengthening the vavs or attaching the yuds of shin and ayin, it is preferable to give it without correction by another, since a child reads it properly. But regarding scraping away the attachment of the legs of hehs and kufs, it is preferable to give it after correction, and another may scrape them even though he was not commanded by the husband. He further writes: I saw in a handwritten responsum of Radbaz regarding a get that came from a distant land, where the legs of the tavs were distant from their roofs, and he validated the get through correction. In my humble opinion, it appears that they should give it without correction, then the agent should take it back, correct it, and give it to her a second time. End quote. See above, s.k. 44.
