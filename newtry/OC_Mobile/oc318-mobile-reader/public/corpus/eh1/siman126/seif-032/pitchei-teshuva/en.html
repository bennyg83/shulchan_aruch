He did not lengthen the vavs. See Ba'er Heitev in the name of Beit Shmuel. See the responsum of my grandfather Panim Meirot, part 3, siman 47, who was asked about such a case in a get brought by an agent from afar, where the vavs of teruchin, peturin, and shevukin were longer than other vavs, but recognizable and known as the shape of ordinary vavs. He discussed this at length and also concluded that it is valid even not in a place of igun. See there. At the end he wrote that one should also not be concerned on this account that the beit din that arranged the get were laymen, etc. See what I wrote below, siman 141, seif 30, s.k. See Get Mekushar, Seder Get Rishon, letter 131, and what he wrote, and his words will be brought in my commentary to Seder Get Rishon there, along with what I wrote about him there. See there.
