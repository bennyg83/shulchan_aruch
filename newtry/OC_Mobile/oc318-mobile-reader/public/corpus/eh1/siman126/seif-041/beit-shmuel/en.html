One must write: "and you are hereby permitted to any man." Although we write "pashatit," "geirashit," and "patarit," nevertheless "and you are hereby permitted to any man" is the essential toref. The Ramban wrote that if he did not write it, it appears likely that if she remarried she must leave. See the beginning of the siman. This also appears from the Rambam, who holds it is the toref of the get. What Darkei Moshe wrote on the Rambam’s words is not conclusive. One must write "veharei" with a vav and "muteret" with a vav. If he wrote it without a vav, perhaps it is invalid even bedieved, because it implies that she is permitting another person. Levush.
It is good to be careful, etc. So that it is read all at once and he not pause; for if he pauses, one may say that the listener thinks he did not permit her to every man. Levush. This teaches that the reader should not pause in the reading.
