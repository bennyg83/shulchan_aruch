The masculine wording with a hei. The sign is "yamim yamimah": in place of "yamim" one writes with a hei. Levush and Taz. Regarding "shemoneh," there is no difference in writing but only in reading: for masculine the nun is read with kamatz, and for feminine with segol. So wrote Levush and Chelkat Mechokek. See Tosafot Yom Tov in Taharot, chapter 5, who writes that for feminine it is with kamatz, like "ro'ah hi." See Darkei Moshe, end of siman 129: "mechuneh" for a male is with segol, and for a female with kamatz.
