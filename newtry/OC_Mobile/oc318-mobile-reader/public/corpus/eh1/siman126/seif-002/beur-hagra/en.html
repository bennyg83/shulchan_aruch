On the third, etc. The rule in this matter is that we follow what is found in Scripture, as the decisors wrote; this is also the rule among grammarians. In Masoretic notes to Naso: every "first" is full in forty-one places; every "second" is defective; every "third" is full in forty-three; and so on: every "fourth" is full in forty-three, every "fifth" full in forty-three, every "sixth" defective, every "seventh" full in forty-eight, every "eighth" full, and every "ninth" full in forty-three.
