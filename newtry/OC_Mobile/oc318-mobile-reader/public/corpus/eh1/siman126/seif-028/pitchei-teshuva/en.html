Large letters. In Get Pashut he writes: I saw that they warn the scribe not to extend the kaf of kedat, nor the mem of Moshe, and similarly the shin and alef, for if he extends them he ruins their form. Rather, he should extend the dalet, tav, heh, reish, and lamed. With all these, if he extends their roofs, he does not ruin their form, and it is proper to be careful. However, it appears that by law it is not invalidated because of elongated letters except with a final kaf, where if he extends its roof it is invalid because it will resemble a dalet or reish, and likewise a chet, if he extends it until the form of the two zayins changes, it is invalid. End quote. See Seder Gittin, Seder Get Rishon, letter 92, and what is written there. See the responsum of Radbaz, part 1, siman 423.
