And he did not write "on Shabbat." The reason is explained in the responsum: "on the fourth" bears no other interpretation except the fourth day of the week, since the date of the month was written afterward. As for what he wrote, "on the twentieth," there is no concern that it is predating, because it is evident that it is a scribal error, for the fourth day could only have been the twenty-first. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 42, where he ruled plainly according to this view. Here, regarding concern for a get, he brought in the gloss the view of those who disagree, namely Rabbenu Tam and the Rivash, as written in the writings of Mahari, end of siman 11.
