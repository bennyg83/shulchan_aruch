In Get Pashut he writes as follows: It is written in Me'or Einei Chachamim, siman 24, concerning a get where the head of the vav of vechdei was somewhat wider than proper, and looked somewhat like a final kaf. Some children read it as a vav and some as a kaf. It appears that one should not invalidate it, because certainly, since the vav is longer than other vavs, its head must also be somewhat wider than other vavs, etc. Where it is clear to us that it was written properly, we do not follow children, etc. If it were possible to arrange for the husband to give another get, we certainly would not validate it lechatchilah, but since that is not possible, my view inclines to validate it. See there. In the responsa of Mahari ben Lev, part 3, siman 69, he was asked about a get in which vechdei was written with an overly long vav, so that it appeared like vechdan, and he validated the get bedieved after delivery, or lechatchilah in a place of igun, since it is in the tofes, etc. However, Mahari Segal, siman 103, concerning a get that came from a distant land where the dalet of lemehach had a roof twice the length of its leg and had a point and was read as a dalet, invalidated it and did not wish to give this get. See what I wrote below, s.k. 23. Also another get in which vechdan was written with a nun instead of vechdei, he invalidated. See there. Rashdam, siman 58, was asked about a get in which vavs were found that children read as nuns, and he invalidated even in a place of igun. See there. In my humble opinion, it appears that if the vavs that children read as nuns were in the tofes of the get, one should validate in a place of igun, but in the toref one should be stringent. End quote. See regarding this in the responsum of my grandfather Panim Meirot, part 2, siman 19, concerning this case.
