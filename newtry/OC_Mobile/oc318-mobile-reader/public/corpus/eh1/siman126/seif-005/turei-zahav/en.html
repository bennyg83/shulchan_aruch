In years, the larger number comes first; your mnemonic is "many years, other days." This means that with years one mentions the larger number first, whereas with days one mentions the smaller number first. This corresponds to a dispute among the tannaim in Yoma regarding the counting of the sprinklings, "one and one," and so on. Rabbi Meir holds that the larger count is stated first, while Rabbi Yehudah holds that the smaller count is stated first. Whoever follows one view has acted validly, and whoever follows the other has also acted validly; nevertheless, the great authorities decided to distinguish in this way between days and years.
