Nissan, etc. See Get Pashut, who writes that if they did not write in the get the names of the months that came up from Bavel, but wrote "the first month," "the second month," it is completely valid, since this count is written in the Torah and everyone knows that we count months from Nissan, etc. See there. He further writes that if the get was written "the month of Menachem" instead of "the month of Av," it appears to him that it is valid bedieved, since it is public and known that the month of Av is called the month of Menachem. The same law applies to MarCheshvan, that if he wrote Cheshvan, it is valid bedieved. See Ba'er Heitev, s.k. 22, that MarCheshvan is one word. Get Pashut writes there that if he split MarCheshvan there, "Mar" as one word and "Cheshvan" as another word, it requires study whether it may be validated. If you say it is valid, then if he wrote it on two lines there is room to be uncertain whether it is valid. See siman 129, seif 32 and 33.
