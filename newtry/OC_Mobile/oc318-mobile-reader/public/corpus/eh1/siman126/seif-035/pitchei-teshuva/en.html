Di yatva is valid. See Get Pashut, s.k. 77, that even if he wrote them on two lines it is valid. See further there in siman 128, s.k. 47, where he writes as follows: One may examine why regarding a river Maran, of blessed memory, wrote "that sits on the river so-and-so," placing the word "that sits" first, while regarding the sea he wrote, in siman 128, seif 4, "on the shore of the sea it is situated," and did not say "that sits on the shore of the sea." It appears in my humble opinion that also regarding a river, if he writes "that is situated on the river so-and-so" or "that sits on the river so-and-so," all is valid, as we say in Shnayim Ochazin, etc. So too regarding the sea, if he writes "that sits on the shore of the sea," all is valid, etc. He further writes that if he skipped the word yatva or motva, it does not invalidate.
