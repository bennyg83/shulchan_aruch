Valid. See Ba'er Heitev. See Get Pashut, s.k. 73, who also writes that logically, because of "from the good," we do not validate it to give it lechatchilah, and one should write another get, since the custom is not to write abbreviations and gematriot in gittin. However, if there is some pressure and they cannot write another get, they give it lechatchilah. He further writes there that it appears that just as regarding writing the date we validate if he wrote yud-bet instead of "twelve," the same applies regarding a condition at the time of delivery. If he said to her, "This is your get if I do not come from now until yud-bet months," it is a proper condition, despite his saying yud-bet instead of "twelve," unlike what appears from the responsum of Rabbi Shmuel HaLevi, Orach Chaim, siman 5, whose words are astonishing.
