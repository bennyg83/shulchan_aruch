He skipped the heh, etc. This is from the responsum of the Rashba. There it is explained that even if he omitted the entire word kol, it is not invalid, for the text of the get says "and behold, you are permitted to every man." See Get Pashut, s.k. 54 and s.k. 93, who brings the responsum of Mahari ben Lev, part 3, siman 69, who wrote that even according to the Rashba, nevertheless she should not be divorced with it lechatchilah. Rather, if she was already divorced, she may marry lechatchilah. However, the Ran in chapter HaMegareish holds that one must write lehitnasba lechol gevar, because otherwise it implies that she will be unmarried and permitted to every man, but not that she will marry one man and become forbidden to all others. According to the Ran, one should be stringent that she not marry lechatchilah. See there. At the end of s.k. 96 there he writes that this is not in a place of igun, but in a place of igun she may marry lechatchilah. See there.
