Antati. The Levush wrote that antati means "my wife," like iteti in Targum, but we write antati for completeness of the language, because the dagesh in the tav indicates the missing nun. Therefore we write the main form of the language, so that people not err and cast aspersions. End quote. In Get Pashut he writes that it appears to him that if he wrote iteti without a nun, the get is valid, for in the Talmud it says in many places "that iteta," and nowhere writes antata. This is surprising, since he did not mention that at the end of Nedarim antata appears four times. In the text of the get of the Rashba in chapter HaMegareish in the name of Rashi, it is written iteti instead of antati. End quote.
