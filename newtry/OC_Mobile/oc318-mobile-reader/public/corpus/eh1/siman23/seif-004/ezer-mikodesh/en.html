What Chazal said our holy teacher did not put his hand under his belt — appears this is only with his flesh exposed without any garment on him unlike over garment; also forbidden to rub through warming garment; nevertheless putting hand below belt from Rambam's language is only because of concern lewd thought not concern he will rub; and so from Taz Yoreh De'ah siman 182 and other acharonim; and in any case examine well.
