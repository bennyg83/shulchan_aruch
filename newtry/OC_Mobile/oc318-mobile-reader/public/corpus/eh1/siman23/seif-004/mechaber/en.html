It is forbidden for an unmarried man to put his hands in his garments lest he come to lewd thought; and even below his navel he should not put his hand lest he come to lewd thought. And if he urinated he should not hold his member and urinate. And if he was married it is permitted. And whether married or unmarried he should not extend his hand to his member at all except when he needs for his orifices. (And see Orach Chaim siman 3.)
