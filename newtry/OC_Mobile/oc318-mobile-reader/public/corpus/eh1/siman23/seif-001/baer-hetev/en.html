Seed. Chayei Adam siman 176 wrote that if one fears he will stumble in a married woman or his wife niddah God forbid — better to waste seed than stumble God forbid in prohibition of married woman or niddah; only fast forty days in summer or sit in ice in winter days. And Beit Shmuel wrote per this what is written here that sin of wasting seed more severe than all sins — not precise.
