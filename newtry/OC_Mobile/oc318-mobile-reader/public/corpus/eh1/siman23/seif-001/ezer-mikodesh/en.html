(Chayei Adam s.k. 1) It appears what he wrote in Sefer Chassidim siman 176 and was brought Even HaEzer siman 23: immersion in rainy season forty days equals forty days sitting in cold — his intention is certainly only a short hour each day; and cold water immersion equals sitting in cold; and instead of forty summer days fasting equals three consecutive fast days or weak two days as stated in Shulchan Aruch laws of fasting; and in name of Ari zt"l written intention on three days that equal forty days; and when consecutive are in warm season they equal forty days fasting in warm season; and it appears his intention is not consecutive; and so twenty days immersion and twenty days fasting in warm season combine; and it appears even for unintentional it helps thus and need not more — for he says permitted he is compelled; and this is like the case of compulsion there; and in name of Ari zt"l written more than forty days — perhaps his intention in rainy season; and his intention regarding intentional also what is not like compulsion or unintentional is light and he agrees it helps as stated; and especially in these generations when men are weak — one should be very lenient.
