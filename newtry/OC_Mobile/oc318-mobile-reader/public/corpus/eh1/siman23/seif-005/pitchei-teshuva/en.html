So too Yoreh De'ah siman 242 seif 17 in gloss; and per this nowadays when they do not cover there is no leniency in the matter; and I did not know on what the world relied to be lenient; and I found in Sefer Toldot Adam chapter 6 brought there that the righteous gaon Rabbi Zalman zt"l of Vilna once went to bathhouse; and when he came to entrance of bathhouse and found his father-in-law there he turned back and fled as fleeing from lion; and said: even though our Rabbi Rama introduced leniency for his time he spoke; and so Agudah that Rama brought in his time was custom among all to go in breeches in bathhouse; but now sense testifies opposite — and it is complete prohibition from Gemara law without any disputant — see there.
