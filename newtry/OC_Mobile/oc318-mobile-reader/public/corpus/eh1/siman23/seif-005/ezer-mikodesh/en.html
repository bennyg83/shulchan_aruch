Seif 6 (without saddlecloth) — regarding riding with saddlecloth plain in Shulchan Aruch for leniency; and in Gemara two versions here; and per what I wrote elsewhere there is no Torah prohibition "you shall not commit adultery" — only asmakhta; prohibition only from preventing procreation; and it works well that we are lenient rabbinically in two versions unlike if Torah prohibition one would fear perhaps latter version forbids even with saddlecloth when bouncing; but since not from Torah no concern at all; and custom is plain to permit mokh on saddlecloth so sitting not hard. And it also appears no opening here at all — animal warmth is different; and all the more when mokh is under skin though skin soft nevertheless not so much heating; and all the more it is also little only to ease sitting with difficulty.
