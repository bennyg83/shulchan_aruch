But all. A fortiori from challenger who is placed in excommunication. In excommunication, etc. Per Be'er HaGolah; and as stated Chullin 132b; but Ramban and Rashba explain as Tosafot there s.v. challenger, etc.; and also Rambam agrees they excommunicate him — only without this he sits in their excommunication; as stated Chullin there "these butchers," etc. "for what halachah" "strong," etc.; and so Rambam and Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 334; Semag 21 challenger, etc.; and per this proof of Tosafot is rejected.
