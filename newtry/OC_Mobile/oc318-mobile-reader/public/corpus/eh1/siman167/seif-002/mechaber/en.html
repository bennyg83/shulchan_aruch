A boy of 9 years and a day who has sexual intercourse for yibbum, cannot let her go through a divorce document, until he gets older [to 13] and then has sexual intercourse with her after that point. If he does not have sexual intercourse with her after that point, and he comes to let her go, she needs a divorce document and halitza. If he has sexual intercourse with her when he is a youth [younger than 3], and he dies before he gets older [to 13], or he dies after he gets older [to 13] but he did not have sexual intercourse with her after that point, she does halitza and not yibbum. And once he gets older [to 13], if he marries another [and he dies], and he has not [yet] has sexual intercourse for his yibbum after he got older [to 13], both [women] must do halitza, but not yibbum.
