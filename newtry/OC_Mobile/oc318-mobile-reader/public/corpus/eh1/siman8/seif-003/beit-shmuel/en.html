Behold the son is a Yisraeli. So too the Rambam and Tur hold like him, as stated in siman 4 — even though there is kiddushin and no transgression, it should follow the male and he would be a ger permitted to a mamzeret; we do not say so, as stated in siman 4 — see there. The Tur wrote that the child follows the male even though he holds the child is a Yisrael and forbidden to a mamzeret — nevertheless there is a difference that we follow the male and he is a ger, regarding whether he is valid to be a dayan for chalitzah, as stated in siman 169; so too Maharshal and Bach.
