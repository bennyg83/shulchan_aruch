One who says: Before I betrothed my wife I betrothed her sister before so-and-so and so-and-so, and they went overseas, and I never divorced her and behold she is married — he is not believed to forbid her to her husband as long as he does not bring witnesses; and even if she says it is true that he betrothed me — she is not believed to forbid herself to her husband; and even if he brings witnesses that he betrothed her — if his wife's sister was betrothed in his presence she is not forbidden to her husband. And he is forbidden to his wife, since he said he betrothed her sister first — he is forbidden to relatives of both (and see above end of siman 47).
