Wherever we said that the woman said to the man "you betrothed me" and he says "I did not betroth you" — we ask him to write her a get to release her. {Rama: Then he is forbidden to her relatives, since he gave her a get. But if he says "you are betrothed to me" and she says "you did not betroth me," and we compel him to give a get out of concern lest men refrain from marrying her — the woman is not forbidden to his relatives because of the get (Hagahot Mordechai on Kiddushin).}
