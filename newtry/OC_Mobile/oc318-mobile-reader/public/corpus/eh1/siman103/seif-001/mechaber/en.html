A widow, including one widowed while engaged as long as her fiance wrote her a ketubah, as well as a widow of a marriage, may sell her husband's posessions to raise the money for her ketubah without needing to go to a court or make an announcement, and it does not matter whether she is raising the full amount of the ketubah at one time or she is selling on three or four occasions to raise a small amount each time, she can sell without an expert court, but she does need a court of three regular trustworthy men who know how to appraise land. Rem"a: And this is specifically for the main part of the ketubah and any additions [written into the ketubah], but she cannot sell without a court in order to raise money for [reclaiming] her dowry, because the dowry money is just like a regular debt [without the special status of a ketubah] (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ritv"a chapter 11, "almanah nizoneit"). If she "sells" to herself by exchanging equal amounts [NEEDS BETTER TRANSLATION] the sale is valid, but some disagree.
