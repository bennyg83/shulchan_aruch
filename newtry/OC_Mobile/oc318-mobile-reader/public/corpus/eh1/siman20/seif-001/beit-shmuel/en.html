Or who hugs and kisses. Per Rambam he is liable lashes from Torah when he hugs or kisses those liable to karet or court execution; and for those liable to lavin there are no lashes; and Ramban holds even for chiyvei karet or court execution it is only rabbinic; and he wrote in responsum even if he does not act with affection Rambam holds from Torah forbidden and he holds rabbinically forbidden — and this is what Beit Yosef wrote Yoreh Deah siman 195 regarding Ramban's views; and Shach challenged him needlessly — examine the sugya Sanhedrin 74 and "do not stand with her behind the fence"; Rambam ruled chapter 5 foundations of Torah even with an unmarried woman "die and do not transgress" — and this is the resolution there because of fence the matter even with unmarried woman die; but "be killed and do not transgress" we do not say with unmarried woman as written Yoreh Deah siman 197; even though in other laws "be killed and do not transgress" and "die and do not transgress" are equivalent as written there in Rambam — nevertheless with unmarried woman which is only fence the matter and fence the matter applies only when standing with her; but not in cases of coercion therefore transgress and do not be killed; and if she is forbidden relation or chiyvei karet Ran wrote there is a lav if he stands with her behind fence because of "do not approach to uncover nakedness"; and he learned this from the questioner in Sanhedrin there.

Meaning he inserted the corona. But what he inserts until the corona is only flesh and not the tendon as written siman 5 and is not intercourse; and likewise in Derishah; but Taz wrote siman 5 on the upper side of the tendon there is corona and there is flesh before the corona begins; but on the lower side of the tendon all is corona for there it shortens until near the head of the tendon where there is a path between two ends that meet there — and each side of the path is itself body of corona and continues until the opening — found there all is corona; therefore in Talmud they only said kissing a limb in place of intercourse; but if he inserts immediately it is insertion of corona — and likewise in this siman; and it seems to me for there is in Talmud Yevamot beginning 56 — from here onward is only kissing and exempt; and Rashi explained from insertion of corona downward is only kissing and exempt; per his explanation there is nothing below corona; nevertheless appears even per his explanation that he explained on lower part of tendon all is called corona — nevertheless he is not liable unless he inserted corona of upper side of tendon; and if he inserts less than corona of upper side it is not intercourse — nevertheless he is liable lashes for chiyvei lavin or chiyvei karet.

Girl under three years. Here too practical difference regarding kohen — if she is under three years it is not intercourse and permitted even to kohen; but if more than three permitted to Israel and forbidden to kohen for seduction of minor is considered coercion — so hold majority of poskim and not like Rambam; and if she is coerced that one man come upon her and per law she should die and not transgress and she did not surrender her life — permitted to her Israel husband — such is Bach's intent as written Choshen Mishpat; and see Tosafot beginning chapter HaIsh Mekadesh and Rambam chapter 2 law 9 and Hagahot Maimoniyot hold one view; and see Yoreh Deah siman 157.
