In which manner of intercourse one who comes upon a forbidden relation is liable. It contains 2 seifim. One who comes upon one of the forbidden relations through limbs, or who hugs and kisses and enjoys flesh contact — behold he receives lashes and is suspected of forbidden relations. {Rama: And one who comes upon her whether in the normal manner or not in the normal manner — once he inserted the corona, meaning he inserted the glans, he is liable upon her death or karet; and they need not see like a brush in a tube, but once witnesses saw them attached to one another in the manner of adulterers they are executed for this (Tur); and the woman is forbidden to her husband (Nimmukei Yosef second chapter of Yevamot); an adult woman with whom a minor under nine years came is not liable death through him (Tur); and it seems to me likewise she is not forbidden to her husband; and therefore the Rav author of Tur wrote many laws when they are liable death or not — practical difference in our time in which manner of intercourse a woman is forbidden to her husband — see inside; a girl under three years — her intercourse is not intercourse (Tur) — for any girl under three years her intercourse is not intercourse; and her hymen returns (Hagahot Yevamot); even if years passed her hymen returns (words of the Rav from Yerushalmi).}
