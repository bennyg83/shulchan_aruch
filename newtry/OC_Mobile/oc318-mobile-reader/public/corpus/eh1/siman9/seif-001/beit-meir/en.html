He didn't recognize her, etc. Chelkat Mechokek wrote from Rambam's words it seems from "she has ketubah" — I don't see hint in his words, for he doesn't discuss without recognition and simple she has ketubah like all women. But without recognition — no ketubah per his words in Hilchot — she may not marry; implies forbidden — simple it's erroneous purchase. But even per his words in responsum — nevertheless from fear for weak bodies that might be harmed thereby — possibly no greater reason than spending his days in fear and confusion (tzarich iyun if not obvious similar blemishes — see siman 117). Beit Shmuel adds astonishment: per Rambam it seems even without recognition she has ketubah as implied from responsum Kesef Mishneh brought; also there no ketubah implied — what he wrote "and afterward beit din writes ketubah" is all when he recognized her; what he wrote "they would trick and betroth," etc. — trickery that they betrothed not publicly so beit din wouldn't protest initially. Therefore per my view what Mechaber wrote because we hold so — and implies difference for seizure — tzarich iyun.
