Seif 1: Woman who married, etc., two men and they died may not marry, etc. — NB: see my composition on Torah parashat Vayeshev verse "tzedakah mimmenni," etc. — there I raised new law: per poskim who hold mazal causes — distinguish between if she married herself to husband vs. father married her in childhood — say father's mazal caused, not hers; if she grew and married herself — no katlanit status. Conversely if grooms were minors and father married them — this woman: attribute to groom's father's mazal — no katlanit. But per ayin causes — no distinction; in every case she is katlanit — see my lengthy discussion; examine. Regarding katlanit law that we don't say sisters establish hazakah — if two sisters' husbands died, forbidden to take third sister like circumcision Yoreh De'ah 263 — see my Toldot Adam on Yoreh De'ah 47 siman 242 and Shulchan Aruch 263; see correct reason to distinguish; examine. This ~22 years since I wrote in my composition to be lenient on katlanit if divorce interrupted — now one told me Ba'er Heitev this siman brought they were uncertain; now I wrote in responsum it took place in Hilchot Sefer Torah siman 279 in omissions to my Sefer Stam — I wrote seems opposite to forbid; wrote proofs both ways; examine. I saw Beit Shmuel brought Hagahot Maimoniyot that old man doesn't become katlanit — per my view distinguish: until eighty per Gemara Gittin and below siman 141 — one bringing get from medinat hayam given to her presumed alive — she is katlanit; but from eighty onward — if he'd bring get from him, safek alive safek dead — no katlanit; so seems to me safek nefashot and proper to be stringent as much as possible; examine.
