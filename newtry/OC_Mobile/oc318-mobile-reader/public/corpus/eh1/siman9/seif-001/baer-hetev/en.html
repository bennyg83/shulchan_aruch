To a third. One should be uncertain whether he speaks specifically one after another or even intermittently — see Darkei Moshe. Woman whose husband died and married another who was married to another woman and died — Hilkhot Ketanot question 152 ruled deadly. Two rival wives married two men and died — possibly together should not marry one; but each alone attributes the mishap to her fellow — Hilkhot Ketanot 153.
