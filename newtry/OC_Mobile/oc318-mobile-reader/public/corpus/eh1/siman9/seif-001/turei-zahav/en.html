A woman who married, etc. The reason is that her bodily condition causes death to one who has relations with her. When she was only betrothed, the reason is that her mazal causes her husbands to die.
She has no kesubah. This accords with Tosafos, because his marriage was entered into in error. From the second husband, however, she receives a kesubah from his heirs, since she was not established as dangerous three times. Although with regard to the prohibition against marrying her, a presumption is established after two occurrences in accordance with Rabbi, nevertheless, regarding monetary matters Rabbi agrees that three occurrences are required, as with an ox that becomes forewarned. So wrote the Rosh in the chapter Naarah Shenispatsah. The Perishah rejected this reason and wrote: "The reason that a kesubah is monetary and we do not rule like Rabbi, who holds that a presumption is established after two occurrences, does not apply, for it is untenable to say that in one matter we rule like Rabbi in one half and not like him in the other half." End quote. The words of the Rosh cited above escaped his notice. His statement that we do not rule like Rabbi in one half is also incorrect, for rather we say that in this respect Rabbi himself agrees, as the Rosh wrote.
Many are lenient, etc. It appears that this may be compared to the discussion in the chapter Mefanin regarding bloodletting on erev Shabbos. Although it involves danger, nevertheless, since the public has become accustomed to it out of necessity, Rashi explains that the verse "Hashem protects the simple" applies. So too here, many have become accustomed to this because it is not possible for everyone to be cautious, since a suitable match may not be readily available. Therefore, they no longer treated it as a danger.
