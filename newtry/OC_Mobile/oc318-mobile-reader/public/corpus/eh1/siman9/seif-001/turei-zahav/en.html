A woman who married, etc. The reason in this case is that her source causes death to the one who has relations with her. In the case where she was only betrothed, the reason is that her mazal causes her husbands to die.
She has none — like Tosafot that his marriage was in error; but from the second she has ketubah from his heirs, since not established twice. Although for prohibition of marriage there is hazakah after two times like Rabbi — nevertheless for money Rabbi agrees we need three times like forewarned ox; so Rosh chapter Ne'arah sheNitpatetah; in Prisha he rejected this reason: "But reason of ketubah is money — we don't hold like Rabbi who holds hazakah after two times — not applicable to say in one matter half like Rabbi half not." I omitted Rosh's words cited; also not correct what he wrote we don't hold half like Rabbi — rather we say here Rabbi agrees as Rosh stated.
Many are lenient, etc. It appears that this matter may be compared to the passage in chapter Mefanin regarding bloodletting on the eve of Shabbat. Although it is dangerous, nevertheless since many have trodden in it, meaning, as Rashi explains, that they became accustomed to it out of necessity, "the Lord protects the simple." So too here: since many have become accustomed to this because not everyone can be careful about it, for a match is not readily available to him, they no longer took account of the danger.
