One who was struck dumb and they asked him if he wishes them to write a get for his wife and he nodded his head to say yes — they examine him in other matters intermittently: one yes and two no, one no and two yes; if he nodded on no-no and yes-yes — behold they may write and give. {Rama: Some say they examine him by means of fruits not found except in summer and ask him in winter days (Tur) if he wishes they gather for him from the tree or the opposite (thus Tosafot explained).}
