(ב) And if he did not reach it, this is a safek. This is Rambam's language, implying that even if he certainly did not reach Lot's drunkenness, there is a safek because in any case he is drunk. But the Tur wrote in Rambam's name: if there is doubt whether he reached it, this is a safek, implying that if he certainly did not reach it there is no safek. Beit Yosef wrote that this is also implied by Rambam's language regarding kiddushin, where he wrote: a drunk man who betrothed, his kiddushin is kiddushin, even if he became very drunk; and if he reached Lot's drunkenness, his kiddushin is not kiddushin, and they deliberate in the matter. This implies that if he certainly did not reach that level, it is certainly kiddushin. So too with a get. Therefore he concludes that what Rambam wrote regarding a get, "if he did not reach it," means that it is not clear to us that he reached it; that is, we are in doubt whether he reached it, and therefore it is a safek get.
