(ב) And if he did not reach it, this is a safek. This is the version of Maggid Mishneh and Beit Yosef. Maggid Mishneh explained that what it says, "it is a safek," means that if he did not say "give," it is uncertain whether his law is like a deathly ill person, who need not say "give." The Tur's version in Rambam is: if the matter was uncertain whether he reached Lot's drunkenness, this is a safek. Beit Yosef explained that even according to his version, his words accord with the Tur, and "if he did not reach it" means that it is not clear to us that he reached it; this is a safek. So it also implies in Darkei Moshe. From this law there is support that one should prevent them from drinking intoxicating things on the day of the get. So wrote Shiltei Gibborim in the name of Maharam. If when he commanded to write he was not drunk, and afterward became drunk, it appears at first glance that it is permitted to write the get while he is drunk.
