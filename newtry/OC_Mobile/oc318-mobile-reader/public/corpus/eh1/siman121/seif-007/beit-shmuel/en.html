A goses. Beit Yosef wrote that there is no dispute among the decisors: when there is time to examine him, he can divorce according to everyone, even if he cannot speak. According to this, what is written here that he is like a living person refers to where there is time to examine him. According to the Rama, even examination does not help. One must say the reason is not concern that his mind may become confused, for with one who fell from a roof there is also concern that his mind may become confused and examination helps. Rather, the Rama holds that since he had an illness and is now a goses and cannot speak, he is considered like dead and cannot divorce. One whose windpipe and esophagus were cut is considered more alive, because he was healthy until the cutting.
