One gasping (meaning: Targum on "on the side of their couch" means on their chest; gasping is one close to death who raises phlegm in his throat from chest distress) — behold he is like a living person and can divorce. {Rama: Some say specifically gasping who speaks; but one who does not speak — no (Semag and Semak and Mordechai); and one should be stringent.}
