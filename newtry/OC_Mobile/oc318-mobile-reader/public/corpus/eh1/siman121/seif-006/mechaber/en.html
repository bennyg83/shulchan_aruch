Specifically when struck dumb or a mute who hears but does not speak — they rely on his hint to divorce; but a deaf-mute who does not hear and does not speak does not divorce his wife by hint if he married when he was competent or a yevamah fell to him from a competent brother; but if he married a woman when he was deaf-mute, even if she is competent, he divorces by hint (and see Choshen Mishpat siman 235 seif 19).
