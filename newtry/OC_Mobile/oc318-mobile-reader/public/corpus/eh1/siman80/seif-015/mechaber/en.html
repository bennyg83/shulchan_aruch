Every woman who refrains from doing labors she is obligated to do — they compel her to do them. {Rama: And he does not give her sustenance until she does; and likewise beit din excommunicates her or sells her ketubah to hire a servant or maidservant (Rambam ch. 21 in name of Ramban and Rashba). And some say they compel her with rods (Tur in name of Rambam). All this when she says: I will not work and I will take sustenance; but if she says: I will not take sustenance and I will not work — the authority is in her hand (Beit Yosef according to Rambam, and so Ran) — as explained above siman 69. And some say even when she says I will not take sustenance and I will not work — she must do household needs; and for this they compel her even though she does not take sustenance (this too Beit Yosef according to Rambam) — one variant.}
