Her handiwork that she owes her husband, and laws of nursing and one who does not wish to work. It contains 18 seifim. Her handiwork to her husband — how so? Everything is according to the custom of the country: a place where their way is to weave — she weaves; to embroider — she embroiders; to spin wool or flax — she spins. {Rama: And if it was not the way of the women of the city to do all these labors — he compels her only to spin wool.} If she exerted herself and did more than is fit for her — the surplus is permitted to the husband. {Rama: And specifically when he gave her a ma'ah of silver every week as explained above siman 70; and a woman can say: I will not take a ma'ah of silver and I will not give the surplus; but the husband cannot say so (Tur in name of Rama, and so Ran ch. HaIsha, and so She'iltot parashat Mishpatim).}
