One who stipulated with his wife that he will not be obligated in her sustenance — and likewise she will not be obligated to him in handiwork — if afterward he wished and became obligated to give her sustenance, her handiwork is not his. {Rama: A woman who was silent and did not claim sustenance or a ma'ah of silver — from presumption her handiwork is with her surplus; and we do not say she waived handiwork (Ran ch. HaIsha). But what does not suffice for her — certainly the husband need not pay her; she certainly waived. A woman who went from her husband's house by force of quarrel and does not wish to return to his house until her husband calls her — she did not lose sustenance on this account; for she is ashamed before him to return since she left without his permission and he does not show her a welcoming face; and if he comes to her she does not prevent him anything. But if she does not wish to come to him until he pays what she is owed — she lost sustenance, for she is like a rebellious wife; for every rebellious wife has a claim and every woman has sustenance even though her ketubah is not in her hand; and he is not believed to say she waived except with clear proof; and even regarding past sustenance — if he did not claim we do not claim for him; and he must swear that she waived (all this in responsum of Rashba brought in Beit Yosef siman 77).}
