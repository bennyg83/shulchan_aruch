(א) One who stipulated, etc. Like one who undertakes to support his wife's daughter. See what Tosafot wrote, and the Rosh brought it at the beginning of chapter 11: if one wrote to support his wife's son or his wife's daughter, etc., he may not say to them, "Go out and work, and I will support you"; rather, they sit and he provides them sustenance. So too here, since she does not eat by condition of beit din, but only by his undertaking, there.
