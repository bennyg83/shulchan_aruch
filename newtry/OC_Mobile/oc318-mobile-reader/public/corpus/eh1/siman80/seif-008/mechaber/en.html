If she brought him two maidservants, or property fit to buy two maidservants from them, or he had two maidservants, or was fit to buy two maidservants — she does not cook and does not nurse her son. {Rama: If she brought him three maidservants or money, or he has money with which he can buy or hire — she does not spread the beds except his bed (Ran in name of Yere'im). And some say she does not even spread his bed except spreading sheets and arranging his bed — which is a way of affection; and every woman does this even if she has several maidservants (Tur).}
