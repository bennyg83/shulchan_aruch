And likewise every woman washes her husband's face, hands, and feet; pours his cup; and spreads his bed. {Rama: And some say she is obligated to spread all the beds of the house (Rambam ch. 21 and Ran ch. HaIsha).} And she stands and serves before her husband — such as giving him water or a vessel, or taking from before him and the like; but she does not stand and serve before his father or before his son. {Rama: And some say specifically when they are not dependent on her husband's table (Beit Yosef in name of Ran ch. HaIsha).}
