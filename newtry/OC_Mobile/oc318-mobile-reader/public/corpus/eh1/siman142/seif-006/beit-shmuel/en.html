Or near it. Meaning, whether he gave the get and said it near the giving, after the giving or before the giving. See Tosafot and the Ran, who were uncertain whether it is effective when he says "befanai nichtav uvefanai nechtam" before the giving. Perhaps there they speak after the time of toch kedei dibur. So too, what is written in seif 1, that he says "befanai nichtav uvefanai nechtam" and afterward gives it, means within toch kedei dibur.
