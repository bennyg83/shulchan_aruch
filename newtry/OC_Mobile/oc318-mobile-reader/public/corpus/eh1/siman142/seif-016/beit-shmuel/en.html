(א) And one witness signed. Although we hold that witnesses of the get sign only in each other’s presence, nevertheless that is not considered validation for the second, as written in Responsa Rashba.
(ב) Even if he is one of the witnesses of the get. Because we require either all of it through validation of the get, where each one says "this is my handwriting," or all of it through the enactment of Chazal, where the agent says both signed before me. Talmud. Although if he says "it was signed before me" it is effective, if so why is it not effective when he says "I am the second witness"? Tosafot answered that it is like one who is interested in the testimony, because he testifies about his fellow, since he is signed together with him. See there.
(ג) He and another. But he alone, if he testifies that he knows the second signature though it was not signed before him, is not effective. Specifically when it is signed before him, he is careful, and he does not damage himself by saying that it was done before him when he did not see it. But when he says "I know," he can excuse himself and say: I thought I recognized it. So wrote Tosafot, the Rosh, and the Ran, unlike Rashi.
