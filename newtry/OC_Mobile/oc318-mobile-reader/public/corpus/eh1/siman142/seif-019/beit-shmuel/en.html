(א) If there is only one agent. And the second one did not know that it was delivered to this one, for otherwise it is valid according to the "some say" brought in the previous seif.
(ב) Only one of them. Although he is like two, and two testify about the writing, nevertheless it is not effective, since he is one person and does not testify about the writing. It may be confused with other shtarot. This is unlike where two testify about the signatures, and even if one of those two who testify about the signatures is the agent alone, nevertheless it is valid, because in any case two testify about the signatures. The author wrote plainly and ruled like the Rambam, and omitted the view of the Tur. See Beit Yosef, Bach, and Responsa Ibn Lev, page 38.
(ג) For biblically, etc. Although even without validation it is also biblically valid, for we hold that witnesses signed on a shtar are considered as though their testimony was investigated in beit din, nevertheless he is lenient in this only regarding validation. In this, where several decisors hold that the validation of beit din does not need validation, he is lenient. See Beit Yosef, siman 41, where he brought the responsum of Rabbi Shimon ben Rashbetz.
