And some disagree and say that she should not be allowed to marry with a get that she has produced unless its signatures have been validated, even if there are no challengers to it, for no one has ever heard of anyone who employed such a leniency. (Rema: A husband who brings a get to his wife, and he himself transported it from where it was written to the place that {he} gives {it} must transfer it to her as an agent, and he must say: “It was written before me and it was signed before me” like the agent, so as not to come to err with another agent<unclear>, since at present people are unlearned [Beit Yosef in the name of A.Ch.).).
