(א) He takes it from her, etc. I already wrote in the name of the Maggid that even where one does not need to say "befanai nichtav," if he gave it between himself and her without witnesses of delivery, even according to the approaches of the Rif and Rambam that the witnesses of signing effect the get and not the witnesses of delivery, where the husband gives the get to the woman without witnesses of delivery he does not need to take it back from her. Nevertheless, when he gives it through an agent, he holds that the agent takes it from her. At first glance this contradicts what is written in seif 13 in the name of Chiddushei Rashba. See there.
(ב) Although he can write. Although regarding testimony of her husband’s death, testimony by writing is effective, as written in siman 17, there they were lenient because of the enactment for agunot. Although regarding women who hate one another they are more lenient with get, there the reason is credibility, and they are more lenient by get because the get assists her. But regarding the death of her husband, where there is no assistance, it is not effective. However, regarding testimony by writing, the issue is not credibility but that we should not distinguish between one testimony and another. Therefore, although they were lenient regarding testimony that her husband died for the above reason, nevertheless they were not lenient by get. So wrote the Beit Yosef. One should not ask, according to the Rambam, why when one became mute, if he writes to divorce his wife, we follow his writing. There it is not testimony, but only revealing his intent. According to what the Beit Yosef wrote, it appears that even if he can speak and he writes "befanai nichtav uvefanai nechtam," it is not effective, so as not to distinguish between one testimony and another. See Responsa Rivash, siman 413, who validates authentication through writing. So too it is written in the gloss of Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat, siman 46, seif 7. According to this, writing "befanai nichtav uvefanai nechtam" is also valid, since it is validation. What the Rivash wrote in siman 140, that it is not effective through writing, is specifically with a mute person who cannot speak; there writing is not effective. This is unlike where he can speak. So it is also precise there in the responsum. Although we hold regarding other testimony that one cannot testify in writing even if he can speak, that is specifically real testimony, where they need to say it with their mouths and writing is not effective at all, even if he can speak. But regarding validation they were lenient to testify through writing specifically when he can speak. In this way, what he ruled in the gloss there in seif 7 and seif 36 are both correct, unlike the Shach there. In Responsa Rivash, siman 240, he answered differently: in testimony of death they were always more lenient. As for the fact that women who hate one another testify regarding a get but not regarding death, that is because with a get she says "befanai nichtav uvefanai nechtam," and the husband can no longer challenge, so there is no suspicion against her, not because of leniency.
