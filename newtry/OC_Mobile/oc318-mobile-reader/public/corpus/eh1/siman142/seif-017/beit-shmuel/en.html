There is nothing to it. In the Yerushalmi this law is compared to the dispute of Rabbi Yochanan and Reish Lakish regarding one who says to ten people, "sign," whether all are for witnesses, or two are for witnesses and the rest are for a condition, as written in siman 120. According to Rabbi Yochanan, whom the author ruled like there, it is fine, because the third is for a condition, and there is no concern if he signs not in his presence. However, if he signed first, it is invalid, as above regarding a relative or disqualified witness. So wrote the Bach. But according to Reish Lakish, who holds they are all for witnesses, if the third signed not in his presence it is invalid. The Rama ruled there that one should be stringent like Reish Lakish, but here he did not gloss anything. This is difficult to resolve. One must say that he holds that although we are stringent there like Reish Lakish, nevertheless here, where there is no disqualification, for one can say that the third also signed properly, we are not stringent in this.
