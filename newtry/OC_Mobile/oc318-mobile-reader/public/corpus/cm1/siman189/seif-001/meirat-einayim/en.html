Rather, they must transfer it back to one another. For example, if Reuven acquired a field or house from Shimon through a complete kinyan and afterward they mutually waived the transaction, the original kinyan does not thereby disappear. Shimon must acquire the property back from Reuven through a new transfer. The Tur writes the same in siman 243:16, and the Mechaber already wrote it above in siman 66:13. The same applies to rental.
