And even if he waived, etc. A person cannot divest himself of property in his possession except through one of the recognized modes of transfer. This is like one who says to another, 'I have no claim or dealings,' etc.; a recipient of a gift who says, 'I do not want it,' etc.; and one who gave a gift and the recipient returned the deed, etc. The same applies to a sale. The Gemara states that the dispute concerns whether letters are acquired through delivery. Even regarding a mortgage in a place where the creditor ordinarily cannot be removed, if he says, 'I agree to be removed,' an act of acquisition is required.
