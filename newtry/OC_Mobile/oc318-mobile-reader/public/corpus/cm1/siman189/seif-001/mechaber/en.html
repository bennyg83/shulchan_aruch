The rule that a sale is not completed by words alone. It contains one seif.
A sale is not completed by words alone. If one asks another, 'For how much will you give me this item?' and the other states a price, and they both agree and fix the price, either may still withdraw. This is so even before witnesses, and even if they say, 'Be witnesses that this one sold and this one bought.' None of this is effective until each type of property is acquired in the legally appropriate manner: land by its mode of acquisition, animals by theirs, and movable property by theirs. Once the sale is completed through the proper acquisition, neither may withdraw, even if there were no witnesses. Rama: Even if they later mutually waive their rights, that does not restore the original ownership. They must transfer the property back to one another through a new acquisition.
