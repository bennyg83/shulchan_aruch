And transfer ownership. The Sema explains that if Reuven purchased a house or field from Shimon through a complete kinyan and they later mutually waive the transaction, the acquisition is not canceled merely by that waiver. Rather, Shimon must reacquire the property from Reuven. The Tur likewise writes this in siman 243, seif 16. The Mechaber records it in siman 66, seif 13. The same law applies to a lease. See the beginning of siman 315. See the Taz's explanation of the Tur and his resolution of the Beit Yosef's question there.
