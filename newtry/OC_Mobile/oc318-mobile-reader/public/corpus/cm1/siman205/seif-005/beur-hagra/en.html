There is an opinion, etc. As stated in Yevamos 106a. See Tosafos there, s.v. “and Rava,” where Ri explains, etc. We rule like Rava there. Likewise Bava Batra 138b. This requires study, for there the Gemara says “beis din after beis din, etc.; witnesses after witnesses, etc.” See below siman 255 seif 1.
Nevertheless, if, etc. A moda'ah is effective even orally, as stated there 49a. Only the same witnesses are restricted by the rule that once testimony has been given, etc. Other witnesses may testify orally. According to our ruling like Mar bar Rav Ashi, even the same witnesses may do so in the relevant context. Likewise at the end of Kesubos: “He should have delivered a moda'ah, etc.”
