The witnesses must know. Where one refuses to carry out a sale that he validly conveyed, and the other party adds something in order to induce performance, in a manner that is partly a sale, such as adding a movable item worth less than the additional amount, and the pressured party delivers a modaah, there is certainly great force to the absolute obligation to uphold Torah law without deviation or extortion. Nevertheless, because a person may be willing to pay merely to avoid litigation, where the pressured party will otherwise become the possessor, this may itself be comparable to a case in which the coercion is known. This requires further study, and compromise is preferable.
