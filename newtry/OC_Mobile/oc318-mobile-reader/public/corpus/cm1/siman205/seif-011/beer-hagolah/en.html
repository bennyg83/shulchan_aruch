Tosafot write so there in Arakhin. The Maggid Mishneh writes at the end of chapter 10 in the name of the Rashba that the proper method of nullification is to disqualify the witnesses to the protest and say, 'I hereby disqualify every witness who will say that I delivered a protest concerning this sale.' He thereby renders them disqualified witnesses against himself, like the law of contractual trust. Other authorities likewise write so.
Likewise, if he tells witnesses, 'Be witnesses that I owe so-and-so such-and-such,' when it is known that he does not actually owe him but intends to create a personal obligation through this acknowledgment, it has the law of a gift where the coercion is known, as the Sema explains.
