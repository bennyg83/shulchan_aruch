There is an authority who says, etc. The coerced sale is compared to the law of a sicarius, and Shmuel concedes where payment is given. Meaning, in the sicarius case Shmuel would concede if the purchaser paid the owner what he had paid the sicarius, even though this is one quarter below value, as explained in Gittin.
This applies only, etc. See Rashbam there, s.v. Shmuel concedes, who distinguishes a coerced sale from one who is forced to give a gift.
Nevertheless, etc. See Rashbam 40b, s.v. gilui milta, etc.
