Tet) The Tur, seif 1, in the name of Rabbenu Yonah.
Yod) The Tur writes that this appears to be the correct explanation of Rabbenu Yonah. The Beit Yosef writes that the reason is compelling. The Nimukei Yosef writes similarly in the name of the Ritva, and indicates that the Ran also inclines to this view. The Beit Yosef further writes that this appears to be the Rashbam's view, from his explanation of the Gemara's statement that here there are two factors, coercion and receipt of payment, and he loses nothing. This implies that he loses none of his own property.
Meaning, delivery of a moda'ah adds nothing once we know of the coercion; the sale is void.
