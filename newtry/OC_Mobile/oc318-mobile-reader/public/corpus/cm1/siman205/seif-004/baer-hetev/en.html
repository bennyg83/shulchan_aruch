Meaning, when it is independently known that he sold under coercion, then even if he did not deliver a protest the sale is void, just as though he had given him a gift under coercion and received no money at all. But if the beit din does not know that he was coerced, he must deliver a protest. Regarding this the Rama writes in his gloss that, for this purpose, it is treated like a sale. Meaning, even in the case of a gift, the witnesses to the protest need not know of the coercion, whereas here, since he at least gave him some money and the seller benefited, perhaps the seller conclusively resolved to transfer ownership. This is stated in the Rivash. Sema.
