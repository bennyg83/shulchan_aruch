But with movable property. The words of the poskim cited by Beit Yosef show that even if the seller knew of the ona'ah at the time of sale, and even if he then waited the amount of time ordinarily allowed to show the transaction to an expert, the forced sale does not stand. We say that because of coercion and money he fully resolved to transfer only where the price falls within a range in which a person could plausibly agree to sell. Where the price is beyond any reasonable range of consent, we do not say that he fully resolved to transfer. Mahara Chashon states this explicitly.
