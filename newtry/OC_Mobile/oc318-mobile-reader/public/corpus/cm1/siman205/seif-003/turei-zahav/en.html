(Seif 8: when does this apply, under compulsion.) The Sema distinguishes between this case and the rule at the end of siman 151, but Rambam's wording does not support such a distinction. In Hilchot Mechirah he states the present rule that with a robber no modaah is necessary, and concludes, "as we wrote in Hilchot Gezelah," meaning the very law our master cites at the end of siman 151. This shows that the two rules are identical. It appears to me that our master, and after him the Rama, relied here on what was written at the end of siman 151. There is no distinction between the two cases. This appears clear and correct.
