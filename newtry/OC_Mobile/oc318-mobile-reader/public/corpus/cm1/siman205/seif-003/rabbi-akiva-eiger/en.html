[Shulchan Aruch] Because of duress. Note: If he is a person who obeys a beit din and there is an available beit din in the city, then with respect to a gift it certainly constitutes a valid declaration of coercion. With respect to a sale, it may be said that such a declaration is likewise effective. Even where the beit din has the power to compel him to obey its ruling, see the Beit Yosef, who indicates that it nevertheless constitutes a declaration of coercion, and see Darkei Moshe.
