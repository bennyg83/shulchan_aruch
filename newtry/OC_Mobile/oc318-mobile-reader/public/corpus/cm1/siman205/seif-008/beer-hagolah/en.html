There, law 5. The Maggid Mishneh writes that a buyer who purchases under coercion is called a robber so long as the seller has not consented, even though he pays money, as stated at the end of HaKones in Bava Kamma, 62a.
The Kesef Mishneh writes there that Rambam appears to rule like Rav Bibi in the name of Rav Nachman, Bava Batra 47b, because Rav Nachman was a judge and the halachah follows him in monetary law. Rambam explains that even according to Rav Bibi the sale is valid unless the purchaser has an established status as a robber concerning this particular field. Although Rav Huna there does not accept Rav Bibi's qualification, Rav Bibi accepts Rav Huna's principle. This is unlike the Rashbam's explanation there.
The language of the Mechaber. The Sema writes that it appears more correct to read, 'as explained,' because the principal law is explained above in the Tur and Shulchan Aruch in siman 149, seif 12, and at the end of siman 151. See also the Rama's gloss there.
