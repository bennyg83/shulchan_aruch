In a gift or waiver. Netivot HaMishpat writes that coercion without a moda'ah also voids a gift. This is specifically where the person was expressly coerced to give that gift. If he was coerced generally and chose on his own to make a gift in order to escape the coercion, the matter depends upon the two answers given by Beit Yosef regarding the law of the sikarikon. Maharam Chaviv concludes according to the first answer that where one was coerced generally and voluntarily gave a gift to escape the coercion, the gift is valid. In my humble opinion, this is also implied by the Rama in Darkei Moshe, who discusses a case where the coercion is known but it is uncertain whether the moda'ah preceded the sale or gift. See there carefully. As for the Rama's later wording in seif 9, where he mentions only a sale and omits a gift, see below, seif 9, seif katan 8, and Pitchei Teshuva, Even HaEzer 134:11.
