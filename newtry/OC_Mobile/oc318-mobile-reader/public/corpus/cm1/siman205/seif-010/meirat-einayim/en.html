Likewise, if he acknowledged before them, etc. Until the phrase, "he is not liable," etc. Here, where he first delivered a modaah, the Rambam and the Mechaber hold specifically that we do not follow his later acknowledgment. This differs from seif 1, which speaks where he did not deliver a modaah. There the Mechaber writes that although he did not actually receive the money before witnesses, but merely acknowledged receiving it, we follow his acknowledgment to this extent, namely that the sale is upheld. See the Derishah, where I explained the Tur's language on this point.
