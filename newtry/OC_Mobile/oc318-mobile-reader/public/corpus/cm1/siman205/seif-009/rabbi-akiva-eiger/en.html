[Rama] And he holds. Note: See Responsa Maharit, part 2, Choshen Mishpat, simanim 20 and 54; Raanach, part 1, siman 63; Maharibal, part 1, siman 96; Panim Meirot, part 1, siman 48 and part 2, siman 125; Zera Avraham, siman 12; and Mahari Bezalel, siman 17, who writes: It follows that according to Rav Hai Gaon, Rabbeinu Chananel, the Rambam, the Ritva, the Rama, Baal HaIttur, the Ramban, the Ran, and the Maggid Mishneh, even with monetary coercion a threat alone is sufficient. This is also the view of Rabbi Yehudah HeChasid and the Rashba. Since all these great authorities, who are pillars of halachic ruling, hold this way, we heed them, and if an individual disagrees, as Maharik wrote, we do not heed him. See also Responsa Lechem Rav, siman 19; Responsa Rabbi Baruch Angol, siman 2; and Responsa Beit Shlomo of Rabbi Moshe, siman 7.
