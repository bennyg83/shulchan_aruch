What form of coercion, etc. Baal HaItur explains the Gemara's statement that Rava concedes where the person was actually coerced. It cannot mean that even without coercion the sale is invalid, for then why would he sell? It refers to a case like the orchard incident. This is the meaning of the Gemara's distinction between external coercion and self-imposed pressure.
Specifically, etc. See Beit Yosef in the name of Baal HaItur. In Even HaEzer, beginning of siman 42, I explained that all the authorities disagree with him.
