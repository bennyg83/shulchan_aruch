And some say, specifically when they do not contain four kav. The Sema asks that Rashbam, whose view this is, writes specifically more than four kav, whereas four kav are measured together with the field, and he therefore emends the text. It appears to me that the Rama here speaks in every circumstance, even where the area is not absorbed within five kav or within most of the field. Four kav, however, are included only when all the conditions stated above are present. According to Rashbam, the rule of a beit kor is superior only in that elevated areas are still measured with it, but in every other respect it follows the preceding rule. See the Tur, where this becomes clear.
