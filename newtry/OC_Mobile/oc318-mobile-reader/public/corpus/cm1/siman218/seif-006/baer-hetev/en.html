More. The Sema emends the text this way because of his question from Rashbam. See there. The Shach writes that it appears the Rama is speaking in every case, even where the excess is not absorbed within five kabin and within the greater part of the field. But with respect to four kabin, all the earlier conditions must specifically be present, because according to Rashbam the law of a beit-kor field is more lenient only insofar as elevated portions are included in the measurement. In all other respects it is like the case above. Examine the Tur and the matter will become clear. Shach.
Standing. Whenever the seller stands within the tract and it is known that this is the beit-kor of earth he is selling, even if he did not carefully say 'this beit-kor' at the time of purchase, it is regarded as though he said 'whether less or more.' Tosafot and the Rosh prove this from the Gemara. Sema.
And a half. This amount results when one sells an entire kor, which is thirty seah, because then a quarter-kav per seah totals that amount. The same proportional calculation applies according to the greater or smaller number of seah sold. Ibid.
