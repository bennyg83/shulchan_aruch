And there were found only one hundred and fifty, etc. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the actual incident happened to involve a deficiency of one quarter, but the amount is not essential. The same rule applies even if one half is missing: we say that this is what the purchaser received. I have found no authority who disputes this, and it is surprising that neither the Mechaber nor Rama noted it.
Meaning that it is worth, etc. The Tur writes that the Ramah requires the land truly to be superior to another field and worth the stated amount. Rashbam holds that it need not actually be better, because the seller may have spoken that way merely to praise his merchandise. The Tur agrees with Rashbam. It is surprising that the Mechaber and Rama leave this point unqualified.
