There, law 13, from the incident of Rav Pappa before Abaye there, 104b. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the incident involved a change from twenty to fifteen, which is one quarter. The authorities have already written that even if the change is only one half, the buyer accepts it. The Sema writes that he found no one who disagrees, and it is surprising that neither the Mechaber nor the Rama recorded their view.
The Tur writes in the name of the Rashbam that it need not actually be superior, because he said this only to enhance the attractiveness of his sale. The Tur agrees. The Sema wonders why the Mechaber and the Rama stated the matter without qualification.
