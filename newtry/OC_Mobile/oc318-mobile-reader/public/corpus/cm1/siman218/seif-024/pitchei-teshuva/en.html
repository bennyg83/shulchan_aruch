All lands. See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, end of siman 129. The word "all" is not inherently necessary to include houses within the term "land." It is needed here only because without it the grant would otherwise be limited to two parcels. In ordinary usage, houses are included in land even without the word "all." Although the Tur in siman 95 cites the Itur that something detached and later attached remains classified as movable, either houses are different, as the Shach writes there, or in common speech houses are included in the term land.
