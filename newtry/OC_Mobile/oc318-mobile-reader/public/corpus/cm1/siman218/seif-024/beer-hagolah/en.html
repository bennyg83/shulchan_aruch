So appears in the Tur, seif 22. I saw that the Sema writes that one may nevertheless say that if he said 'lands,' without the word 'all,' and he owns only one field and one orchard, the orchard is also included, because he used the plural 'lands' and owns only these two, and an orchard is included in the category of land. Perhaps for this reason the Rama did not write the word 'all.' He further writes that it is doubtful whether slaves are included in an unqualified reference to lands.
He wrote: "The author's version of the Caliphate did not even tell the property of Fredesa and Besie, and I did not have any assets even my daughter and slaves
The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is proven in chapter Mi SheMet, for even tefillin are called 'property,' there, 151a.
This implies that movable property is included only where he says 'all my property.' This is difficult in light of siman 248, seif 11, where the Mechaber writes that all movable property is included in the term 'my property' even if he did not say 'all.' The Shach distinguishes that there a donor gives generously. He discusses the matter further there; see there.
