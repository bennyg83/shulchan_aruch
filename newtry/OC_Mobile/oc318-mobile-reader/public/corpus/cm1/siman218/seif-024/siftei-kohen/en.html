And all movable property, etc. Although below, siman 248, seif 11, all movable property is included in the term 'my assets' even when he did not say 'all,' that case concerns a gift, and one who gives a gift gives generously. This distinction is mentioned by Tosafot, the Rosh, and the Ran, and in my opinion it is also the view of the Rambam and the Mechaber. The Bach likewise writes this, while the other answers mentioned by Tosafot and the other poskim are difficult to attribute to the Rambam and the Mechaber. The Maggid Mishneh writes concerning the Rambam that here, where one says 'all my assets,' movable property is included, as the Mechaber writes, and that this is proved in the chapter Mi SheMet, where the Gemara states that even tefillin are called assets. Although that chapter concerns a gift, his meaning is that the chapter proves that all movable property, even tefillin and the like, is called 'assets.' Therefore, although that case is a gift, once everything is included within the term 'assets,' here too, when he said 'all my assets,' all movable property is included. Although a seller is presumed to sell sparingly, that is only where he did not say 'all.' Without the proof from the chapter Mi SheMet, even saying 'all' would not necessarily include every movable item, since we would not know that such items are called assets at all. This appears correct to me. Examine carefully.
But if he said, 'The assets of so and so,' etc. This applies even if he said 'all.' So writes the Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah. His reason appears to be that Rabbeinu Yonah concludes, 'But one who says, I am selling all my assets, includes the movable property as well.' Why did he mention the word 'all'? It must be for the inference that regarding 'the assets of the house of Chiya,' even saying 'all' is ineffective. In my opinion, however, this is strained. It is more likely that the word 'all' is a scribal error. Alternatively, Rabbeinu Yonah may mean: if he said, 'I am selling you all the assets of the house of Chiya,' the movable property is also included. According to this, Rabbeinu Yonah may be reconciling the words of Rashbam. Examine carefully.
