My property. Even if he said 'all.' So writes the Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah. It appears that his reason is that Rabbeinu Yonah concludes: 'But one who says, All my property I sell, includes movable property as well.' Why did he need the word 'all'? It must be intended as a distinction, namely that regarding 'the property of the house of Chiya,' even saying 'all' does not help. In my humble opinion this is strained. It appears more likely that the word 'all' is a scribal error. Alternatively, one may explain that he means: 'But one who says, All the property of the house of Chiya I sell to you, includes movable property as well.' If so, Rabbeinu Yonah may be reconciling Rashbam's words. Ibid.
