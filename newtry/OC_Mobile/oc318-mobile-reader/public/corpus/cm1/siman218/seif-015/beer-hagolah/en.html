Mem) There, halachah 12. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this follows the Mishnah and Rav Huna's ruling that a discrepancy of one sixth is treated like a discrepancy of less than one sixth, Bava Batra 105a. The Rif likewise rules this way. The Rishonim write that if the seller said, "A beit kor that I own in a place whose boundaries and markers are such-and-such," or showed it to the buyer and said, "this beit kor," then even if the deficiency exceeds one sixth, the buyer must accept it.
Meaning, the seller deducts the entire deficiency, including the first one sixth, as in a deficiency greater than one quarter, as explained above in seif 8 and seif 11.
