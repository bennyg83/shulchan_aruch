Peh) There, halachah 21, based on the incident concerning the property of Bar Sisin before Rav Nachman, Bava Batra 30a. Rambam rules like Rav Nachman, as does the Rif. See Maggid Mishneh.
Tzadi) The seller must bring witnesses that he did not purchase the field from Reuven and that it merely happens to be called by Reuven's name. Until then the land remains with the seller, because we understand the phrase "Reuven's field" to mean "the field I bought from Reuven," not a field that he never bought from Reuven even if people happen to call it by that name. Rashbam there.
