In a garden, one-half kav has the same status as nine kav in a field. Thus, if the excess beyond the permitted quarter-kav units is less than one-half kav, the purchaser returns only money. If the excess reaches one-half kav, he returns all the quarter-kav units together with the excess, either in money or land, valued at the lower price prevailing at the time of return. Rama: The principal ruling is that whenever the excess is less than nine kav, the seller may choose land or money. If he takes money, it is valued at the lower price, whether the land was cheap and rose in value or expensive and fell. If the seller owns an adjoining field, the purchaser returns land. Likewise, when the excess is nine kav, all excess is calculated, including both the quarter-kav units and the remainder.
