[Shulchan Aruch] One who says to another, "fields." Note: See Beit Yosef, who writes in the name of the Ran, Rashba, and Nimukei Yosef that this applies specifically where he says "fields," "my field," or "the fields of so and so." But if he says "all my fields," all are included. If he says "the fields of so and so," it may likewise be that all are included, though some disagree. If he says "the fields that are mine" or "that belong to so and so," using the definite article, all are included. In Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Batra, Rabbeinu Yonah is cited as reconciling in this manner the sugya of one who sells a house with the sugya in the chapter Mi Shemet. Here the case is where he says "all the property of the house of Rabbi Chiya," and therefore the word "all" is required. In Mi Shemet, however, the case is where he says "my property to so and so," in which formulation all is included. See there.
