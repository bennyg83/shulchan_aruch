“This beit kor of earth I sell you,” etc. The Mechaber omits the word “this,” because he copies the Rambam, who copies the Gemara. The Gemara resolves that when one says simply “a beit kor,” without saying “measured by a rope” and without saying “more or less,” the law is as though he said “more or less”: up to a quarter kav per seah is waived. Tosafot and the Rosh question this and therefore explain the Gemara as speaking where the seller stands within the field, as the Tur and Rama write in seif 6. Ri Halevi explains that the Gemara speaks where he says, “This beit kor of earth I sell you.” Both are sound as practical law. When he expressly says “this,” even if he is not standing in it, that word was not used for nothing and makes the transaction comparable to “more or less.”
