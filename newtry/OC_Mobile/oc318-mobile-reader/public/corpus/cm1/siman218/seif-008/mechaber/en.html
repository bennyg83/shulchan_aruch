If the seller says, "I am selling you this beit kor of earth," it is as though he expressly said, "a beit kor of earth, whether somewhat less or somewhat more." If the deficiency is one twenty-fourth, namely one quarter-kav per se'ah, or the excess is one quarter-kav per se'ah, the purchaser must accept it. If the discrepancy is greater, the parties calculate all the quarter-kav units that are lacking or excessive. For every deficiency below a beit kor, the price is reduced proportionately. Rama: Some say this applies only while the remaining area can still be called a beit kor. If it is less than that, the rule does not apply, because he sold him a beit kor. This qualification applies where the seller owns no additional land. If he sold him a beit kor somewhere among his fields, he must deliver a precisely measured beit kor. Any land in excess of a beit kor must be returned to the seller.
