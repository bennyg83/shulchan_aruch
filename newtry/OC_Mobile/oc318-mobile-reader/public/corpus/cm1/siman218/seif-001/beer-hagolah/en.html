The language of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 28 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, from the Mishnah at the beginning of chapter Beit Kor, Bava Batra 102b. The Maggid Mishneh explains that this applies specifically where he said 'soil,' implying land fit for sowing. If he did not say 'soil,' even if it is entirely rocky ground it is included. So writes the Tur in the name of the Rashbam and Ramah. In my humble opinion, the Rama should have written this as an alternative opinion as well.
