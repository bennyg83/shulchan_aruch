Seif 1. Note: Concerning one who sells or gives as a gift 'tzon' and owns both sheep and goats, is only the sheep included or also the goats? If the parties speak in the vernacular and write in the vernacular, or conversely speak in Hebrew and write in Hebrew, we follow ordinary human usage, as in the laws of vows, and the same applies to documents. In ordinary usage goats are not included in the term 'tzon,' only sheep. But if they speak in the vernacular and the sale or gift document is written in Hebrew using the word 'tzon,' then goats are included as well. This is learned from the opening Mishnah of the chapter Ma'aser Behemah in Bechorot, where it is explicit that sheep and goats may be tithed together because they are one category of tzon. This is a novel ruling, with God's help.
