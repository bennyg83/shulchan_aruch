One who sells an area of one beit kor of earth containing rocks and depressions. It contains twenty-five seifim.
If one says, "I am selling you a beit kor of earth," Rama: and the same applies if he merely says, "a beit kor," and the field contains small depressions ten handbreadths deep, even without water, or rocks ten handbreadths high, Rama: four handbreadths wide, provided none contains a quarter-kav area, they are not counted toward the beit kor, because a person does not wish to pay for one tract that appears divided into two or three separate places. The purchaser nevertheless acquires those depressions and rocks along with the beit kor without additional payment. Rama: Some say they remain the seller's. Even so, the purchaser must take the rest of the field, provided a plow can pass between those features.
