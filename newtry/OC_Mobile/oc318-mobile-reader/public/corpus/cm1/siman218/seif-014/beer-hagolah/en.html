There is a judgment
(That is to say that two of the two languages of the Amadic Brigades in the region mean that they are lacking in grammar, and they are not allowed to leave without a single gram):
As Rav Yehudah said in the name of Shmuel: this is the view of Ben Nanas, but the Sages say, etc., there, 105a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rashi explains that the seller, who remains in possession of the land, has the stronger hand. Therefore, if the field is deficient, the phrase 'whether deficient or excess' governs; if it is excessive, the phrase 'measured by rope' governs. This is the Mechaber's view, as explained above. Even if the seller wishes to take money, we compel the buyer to pay when there is an excess, because land remains in the possession of its owner. A similar rule is stated regarding rental in the case of two inconsistent expressions, as will be explained in chapter 7 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, and below in siman 312, seif 15.
