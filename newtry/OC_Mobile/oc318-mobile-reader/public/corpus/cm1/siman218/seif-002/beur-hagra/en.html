Even if filled with water. The Gemara.
Within five, within most of the field. Meaning that these five kav are absorbed within the majority of the field, and the four kav are themselves absorbed within those five. For example, the five kav form a long narrow strip extending through most of the field. Ramah, following the Rif and Rosh’s reading of Rabbi Chiya bar Abba in the name of Rabbi Yochanan, holds that he does not dispute Rav Ukva but adds to his rule.
And they are three high. As stated in Shabbos chapter 11, because they cannot be smoothed with a plane and scraper.
Or they were, etc. Meaning more than five kav.
Or they are not, etc. Meaning less than the majority of the field.
