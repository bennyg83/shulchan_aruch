[Shulchan Aruch] Half a field. Note: Darkei Moshe, siman 248, subparagraph 11, writes in the name of the Ran, chapter 4 of Gittin, citing the Ramban, that one who gives half of his property to another does not receive half of each utensil or half of each field. Rather, the assets are appraised and he receives one half by value. See there. Ran there writes concerning the Ramban’s view: "I do not know his source. Why should one who receives half of another’s property not acquire one half of each and every item?" and so forth.
