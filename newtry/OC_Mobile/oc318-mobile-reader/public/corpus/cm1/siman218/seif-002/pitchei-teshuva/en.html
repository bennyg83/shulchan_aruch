All land. See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger at the end of siman 129, who writes that the word "all" is not intrinsically necessary to include houses in the term "land." Here "all" is necessary only because without it the giver would be transferring merely two parcels. In truth, even without the word "all," houses are included in "land." Although Tur, in siman 95, cites Baal HaItur as holding that an item detached and later attached is still considered detached, either houses are different, as Shach writes there, or in ordinary human speech houses are included in the word "land."
