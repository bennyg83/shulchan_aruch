The Maggid Mishneh writes that according to this, the Mishnah's rule that if he left over in the field nine kav, or in a garden, etc., must mean nine kav beyond the permitted quarters. It was already explained above, according to Rav Hai, that even if he exceeded the permitted quarters by the slightest amount, the entire excess is subject to return. The Mechaber nevertheless holds that if the excess beyond the permitted quarters is less than nine kav, the return is in money, even if the total excess reaches nine kav; but if there are nine kav beyond the permitted quarters, the return is in land. These words are surprising, for if the excess is land fit to be returned to the seller, why should he not receive land? His treatment of changes in price, restricting it to a nine-kav excess, is also unclear. Many explanations have been offered for that passage, and the matter requires study.
