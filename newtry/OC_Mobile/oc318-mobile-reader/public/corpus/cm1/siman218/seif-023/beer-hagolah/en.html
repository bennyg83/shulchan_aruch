Lamed) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 20, based on the incident before Rav Asi.
Mem) The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam derived this from the rule stated above: if one says, "I am selling you a house from among my houses" or "an ox from among my oxen," he may give the smallest one. This is a statement in Menachot 88b.
