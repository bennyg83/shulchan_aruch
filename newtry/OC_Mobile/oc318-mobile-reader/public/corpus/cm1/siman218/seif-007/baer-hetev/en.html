He deducts. This is unlike the ruling at the beginning of siman 232 that whenever something is sold by measure, a discrepancy voids the sale even if it is less than the ordinary measure of onaah. There, the seller explicitly misrepresented the quantity as a specific measure and it turned out otherwise. Here, however, he did not state with certainty that it contained that quantity. Rather, he said that it should be measured to determine whether it was a beit-kor as he believed, and if less were found the price would be reduced accordingly. So write Tosafot. Ibid.
