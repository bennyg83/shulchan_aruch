There, law 5, Mishnah there, 103b. The Maggid Mishneh writes that although he said 'a beit kor of soil' and the field is deficient, the sale remains valid and the price is reduced proportionately. The Rashba likewise rules so. He explains that in every purchase and sale, even one involving a single item, the parties do not necessarily insist on exactitude, but adjust according to weight and number. He brings proof for this, and this is the Mechaber's view in chapter 15, etc.
