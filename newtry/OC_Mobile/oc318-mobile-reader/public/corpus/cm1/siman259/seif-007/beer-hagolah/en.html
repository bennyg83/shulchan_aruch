Although the owners later despaired when they learned of the loss, when the finder first found it the object came into his possession unlawfully because the owners had not yet despaired. This is explained there and in seif 1.
In my humble opinion the text should read, 'an item that can be saved,' etc., and so appears in Tosafot and the Mordechai. They conclude that this is so even if it can later be saved only with difficulty, such as where at the moment of flooding it could have been saved without difficulty. The Sema emends the text differently. The reader may decide; this is not the place to elaborate.
