(Seif 6, a bird seized it.) The Sema, seif katan 7, writes that this applies even if the bird later drops it in the same city. This is difficult. Why did the Tur and Mechaber specify that the bird carried it elsewhere? Even if it dropped it in the same place, the owner seemingly despaired immediately when it was seized. One may answer that if the bird drops it again at that very place or nearby, the owner does not despair, for it is common that an object seized by a bird falls from its mouth because the bird did not grasp it securely. The owner therefore does not despair while the bird remains near the place of seizure. Rambam writes that the bird cast it into another courtyard.
