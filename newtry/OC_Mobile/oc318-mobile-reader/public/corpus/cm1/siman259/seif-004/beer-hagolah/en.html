The Maggid Mishneh writes there, law 8, in the name of the Rashba, who concludes that merely resealing the container suffices to permit drinking, because this is found property. If the gentile had touched it, why would he return and reseal it? It is comparable to the law of raiding troops, where sealed barrels are permitted, explained in Yoreh Deah siman 124.
