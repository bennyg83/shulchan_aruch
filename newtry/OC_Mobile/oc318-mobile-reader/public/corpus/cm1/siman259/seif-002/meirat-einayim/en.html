When does this apply? In a place where he is obligated, etc. Through the end of the siman this is the Tur's wording. The rules are explained in the following simanim. In the Perishah I identified the location of each one.
And the circumstances must prove, etc. Even where the circumstances prove abandonment, it must still be a place where it is reasonable to regard the object as lost. Otherwise, perhaps the owner despaired at the time it was lost, as explained in seif 3 and below in siman 262.
