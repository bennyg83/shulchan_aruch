And it must be in circumstances that demonstrate the matter. See Perishah, who explains that "in a place where there is reason for doubt" excludes a location behind a fence, and "in circumstances that demonstrate" means that the utensils are overturned, as explained in siman 261. According to this, there is a scribal error in Sema, seif katan 7. It should read: nevertheless, even where the circumstances demonstrate that it is an intentional placement, it must be in a place from which there is an obligation to return the item, excluding where it is found in a place from which there is no obligation to return it, such as an area with a gentile majority, as in seif 3.
