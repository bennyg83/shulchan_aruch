How so, etc. See Beer HaGolah. Tosafos there, s.v. "savar, etc.," and the Rosh also write this, and the entire sugya there follows this approach. Maggid Mishneh. The plain meaning of the Mishnah in Machshirin is likewise so.
(Collected note) How so, one who finds, etc. Unlike Ra'avad, who writes that this applies even where the majority are Jews, based on the incident there 24b where Rabbi Ami found young birds, etc. See Rosh there. His words require study, for if so Rav Asi contradicts himself, since there he says, "come and hear, for Rav Asi said: one who found, etc.," as above.
Or even. There: "come and hear, one who finds money in a synagogue, etc.; infer from this, etc."
Even if the majority are Jews, etc. See Beer HaGolah. There 24b, in the incident "he found a purse here," the word "here" implies that the majority of the city were Jews, but most passersby in the marketplace were non-Jews.
Even if, etc. See Tosafos there 24a, s.v. "ki, etc." This is the meaning of "for certainly, etc.," as stated there "because, etc."
(Collected note) Even if he knows, etc. This follows from there 24b, "and since Jews dug it, etc." See Rosh there.
And it has an identifying mark. There 24b: "come and hear, for Rabbi Elazar said, etc." See Beer HaGolah.
And such as, etc. For the ruling of Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar is established there 21b by Rabbi Yitzchak, etc., and we rule there like Abaye.
Others say, etc. See Tosafos to Bava Kamma 114b, s.v. "hamakir, etc." Nimukei Yosef, Mordechai, and Hagahos Mordechai bring proof from Bava Metzia there, where Jews dammed it and Jews, etc.
