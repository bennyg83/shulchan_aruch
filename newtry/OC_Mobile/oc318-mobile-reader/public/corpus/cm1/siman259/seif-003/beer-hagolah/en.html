Heh) There, seif 5, from the baraita of Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar on Bava Metzia 24a, as the Gemara resolves the matter at the beginning of 24b from Rav Ashi's statement concerning one who found a barrel, etc. The Rif writes that since Rav Ashi holds this way where the majority are gentiles but not where the majority are Israelites, the halachah follows him. The Rosh likewise rules so, and Rambam follows this in chapter 11 of Hilkhot Gezelah VaAveidah, halachah 7.
Vav) Mishnah 8 in the second chapter of Makhshirin, cited in Bava Metzia 24a.
Zayin) Rambam likewise rules this from the incident of one who found four zuz tied in a cloth, and the Gemara's answer there on 24b.
Chet) From the baraita concerning one who finds money in synagogues, etc., as explained there on 24a.
Tet) From the incident where Rava followed Rav Nachman, and Rav Nachman's response to him there on 24b.
