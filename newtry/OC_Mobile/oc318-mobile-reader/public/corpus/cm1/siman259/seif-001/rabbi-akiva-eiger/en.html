[Shulchan Aruch] One who sees a lost object. Note: The Ran, Rashba, and Rosh, in their rulings on Nedarim 41b, write that a physician has a mitzvah to heal another person's animal, for there is no greater form of returning a lost object than this. The Rosh, however, did not write this in his commentary there or in his responsum on that matter.
