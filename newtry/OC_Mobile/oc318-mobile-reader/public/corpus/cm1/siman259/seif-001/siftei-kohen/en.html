He has already violated 'You shall not hide yourself,' etc. The Rambam, Laws of Theft and Lost Property 11:6, and the Semag, negative commandment 159, write that even if he returned it after the owner's despair, the return is merely a gift and he has already violated the prohibitions. Ramban likewise concludes in Milchamot, Perek Elu Metziot, that even after returning it he has violated all of them. This also appears from the plain wording of Halachot Gedolot, the Rif, and our version of the Gemara; see there.
