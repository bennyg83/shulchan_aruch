The seer. Dr. Kramer, who sees only the eyebrows and walks near him, is not passing by the return of their return until the end of the hand, but cannot ignore the hour he sees and raises his eyes from the elite. A:
To ignore it. The Rambam, Hilchot Malveh 11:6, and Semag, negative commandment 159, write that even if the finder returns the lost object after the owner's despair, the return is considered only a gift and he has already violated the prohibitions. The Ramban reaches the same conclusion in Milchamot Hashem, chapter Elu Metziot: even after returning it he has violated all of the relevant prohibitions. This also appears from the plain wording of Halachot Gedolot, the Rif, and our text of the Gemara. See there. Shach.
