And he admitted. The Tur also writes, "and both swore," and so does the Rambam. The Mechaber omitted this because an oath is unnecessary for liability to repay the principal. The fact that both swore is relevant only to liability for the double payment and the additional fifth. The Mechaber did not state the laws of double payment and the fifth here, as explained in siman 348 and siman 350. Sema.
