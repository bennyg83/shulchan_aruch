This law is straightforward and the Mechaber did not need to write it. Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilkhot Geneivah, law 7, states that where two Yehudahs swore and afterward witnesses came against the second, both pay the principal; if the owner of the deposit seized the double payment, it is not removed from him, and the one who admitted pays the additional fifth. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the law of double payment is an unresolved question of Ravina in Bava Kamma chapter 9, 108a, and Rambam rules in his usual way in such cases. The Tur challenges him, saying the Gemara appears to make double payment certain and leaves only the additional fifth in doubt. Kesef Mishneh leaves the matter unresolved. In my humble opinion it is obvious that the word 'and they swore' is missing here; it should read, 'and they swore, and one of them admitted,' etc. The Mechaber rules leniently according to both views: they pay only the principal, neither double payment according to Rambam nor the fifth according to the Tur.
