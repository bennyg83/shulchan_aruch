(Supplement) See Be'er HaGolah. Rambam's wording is difficult. The entire discussion concerns the added fifth, whereas double payment certainly remains due, yet Rambam appears to reverse the matter. Perhaps he inferred from the wording, 'he first admitted,' though Tosafot already answers that inference. The Shulchan Aruch omits the issue because it has no practical application today and includes the siman only to preserve Tur's order. See 100. (End of supplement.)
