They claimed it was stolen and the first admitted, etc. The Tur and Rambam read: they claimed it was stolen, both swore, and one then admitted. The Mechaber omitted the fact that both swore because that detail matters only for liability for double payment and an added fifth. A custodian who falsely claims theft and is contradicted by witnesses does not incur double payment until he first swears. Since the Mechaber is not discussing penalties, he omitted the oath. For liability for the principal, no oath is necessary. I do not know why he needed to state even this rule, because that both must pay the principal is obvious. The Gemara and Rambam state it only alongside the rules of double payment and the additional fifth.
