The claimant shall swear. Ramban, cited by Tumim here and in siman 146, explains that even if the claimant admits that the possessor ate for three years but says that he never sold the property and that the possessor consumed it by robbery, the claimant is believed to say that the consumption was wrongful even though he admits the fact of possession. When he says, "Your possession was not lawful," or, "I protested," his admission is accompanied by its own qualification and therefore is not treated as an admission establishing chazakah. See what I wrote in siman 146.
