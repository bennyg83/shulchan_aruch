In all these cases he shall swear, etc. See Tumim. This requires explanation. If the protester claims, 'You consumed the produce by robbery for all three years,' he thereby admits that the possessor consumed for three years, which normally establishes chazakah. We must say that the protester is believed because he has a migo that he could have denied the consumption entirely. One cannot object that this is a migo used to extract property, because the protester possesses an ancestral chazakah to the land, and therefore this is not considered a migo to extract. See there.
