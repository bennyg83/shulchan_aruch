Whenever he admits the claimant's factual allegation but says, 'I consumed what was mine,' he is not believed on that claim even with an oath, because we leave the produce in the presumptive ownership of the original owner. Of such a case the Sages said that one who is obligated to swear but cannot do so must pay. Sema.
