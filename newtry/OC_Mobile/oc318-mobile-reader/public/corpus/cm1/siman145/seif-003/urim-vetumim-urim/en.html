For if we believe him, etc. The wording implies that the rule depends on this reason and not merely on migo. Accordingly, if he says, 'I possessed only two years and bought it from you,' with no witnesses at all, perhaps he would not be believed concerning the produce through a migo that he could have denied eating it, because the land itself leaves his possession. Hagahot Ashri supports this. Ramban, Nimukei Yosef, Rivash, and other decisors disagree. Their view is primary, as I wrote at the end of siman 140.
