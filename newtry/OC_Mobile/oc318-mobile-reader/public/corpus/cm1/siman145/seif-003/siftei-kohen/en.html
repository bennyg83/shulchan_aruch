For if we were to believe him, etc. This implies that he is believed only for the stated reason, but not by virtue of a migo. Accordingly, if he claims, "I ate for only two years, and I purchased it from you," and there are no witnesses at all, he would not be believed regarding the produce through a migo that he could have said he did not eat, because the land is removed from his possession. Hagahot Ashri says this explicitly, and Tosafot likewise implies it. Ramban, Nimukei Yosef, Rivash, and other authorities disagree, and their view appears primary, as I wrote above at the end of siman 140. See there.
