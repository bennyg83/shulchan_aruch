And the chazakah is established for him. Although testimony of three brothers cannot combine with another witness as ordinary testimony, it is effective for chazakah just like three ordinary sets of witnesses, each set testifying to one year. The Mechaber abbreviated these laws here and omitted the laws of hazamah because they are not adjudicated nowadays, just as he omitted them in siman 38. See the Tur, where these matters are explained, and the Perishah at the end of simanim 30 and 38. I also explained there in the Sema why the three sets combine for chazakah of land and we do not apply the rule of 'a whole matter and not half a matter.'
