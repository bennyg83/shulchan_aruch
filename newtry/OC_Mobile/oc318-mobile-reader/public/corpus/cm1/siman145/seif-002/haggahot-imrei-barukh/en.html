(Same Shach) And so it appears from Tosafot, etc. Note: His intent is to Tosafot, Bava Batra 133a, passage beginning "in the end he admitted to him," according to Rabbeinu Chananel's reading, which Ri considers primary. But Tosafot on 133b, passage beginning "and if he claims," ask that he should be believed by a migo that he could say, "I descended only for the fruits," and this appears to contradict the ruling of Hagahot HaRosh.
