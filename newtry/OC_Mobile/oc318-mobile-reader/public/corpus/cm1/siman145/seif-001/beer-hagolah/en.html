The statement of Rav Yehudah, Bava Batra 56b, cited by Tur in seif 4.
As Rav Yehudah said to Rav Nachman there. Tur therefore writes that if one witness says he grew wheat and another says legumes, they do not combine, because people do not ordinarily confuse such things. Tosafot write likewise at the beginning of 57a.
