Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah, halachah 15. Rambam rules like Rabbi Yose in the Mishnah in Bava Batra 136a, as the Gemara and Rif rule. The passage at the end of HaMegaresh, Gittin 85b, where Rav instituted the phrase "from this day and forever" to exclude Rabbi Yose's view that the date of the deed itself proves present transfer, is explained by the Rif as a stringency in matters of prohibition. Rashbam similarly explains it in Bava Batra, and the Rosh gives the reason. See Bach.
Bet) As Rav Huna said to Rav Nachman, "This is Rabbi Yose's reason," etc., there.

The Sema and Shach write that the recipient does not acquire after death. Although the Mechaber here copies Rambam's words, they must be understood carefully. In my humble opinion their reading is correct.
