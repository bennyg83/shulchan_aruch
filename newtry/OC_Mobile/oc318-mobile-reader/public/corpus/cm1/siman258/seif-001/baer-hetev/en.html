A gift. Meaning, a deed of gift made by a healthy person. When the Mechaber concludes, 'whether or not it contained a kinyan,' the case here is where the deed states, 'My field is given to you after my death,' and the like. A healthy person can transfer through such a deed, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write in simanim 191 and 144. The novelty is that even though the deed says, 'My field is given to you after my death,' without saying, 'from today and after my death,' and there is no kinyan, so that ordinarily a deed cannot take effect after death, nevertheless, because the deed bears a date, the recipient acquires the body of the property from today and the donor cannot retract, while the fruits pass only after death. Otherwise, why would the date have been written? From the unqualified language of the poskim it appears that there is no distinction whether the date is written at the beginning or end of the deed. So writes the Sema.
