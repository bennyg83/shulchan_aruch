But this, etc. See the end of chapter 7 of Gittin 76b. Bach writes that this resolves Rashbam's question there, s.v. 'proves,' etc. Rif and the other decisors understand likewise. The statement there, 'to exclude Rabbi Yose,' refers to a case after twelve months, where the halacha does not follow Rabbi Yose, so that Rav should not appear to contradict himself. See 81.
