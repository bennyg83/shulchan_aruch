But a guard who steals from his own authority, even though he stole something that does not have a sense of life in a thief in which a spirit of life does not belong to another way that he has returned to his place, and he knows that he will be saved from himself in a favor that he will not go in any other way, and that he will not be faithful to any other taste, and that he will not believe that he will return to him
And there are witnesses that he stole it. In the Perishah I explained why the Rambam and Tur carefully say that witnesses saw the theft. If there were no witnesses and the thief returned it on his own, we would see that he repented and would not necessarily say that the owners no longer wished to guard it. But where witnesses established the theft, his return may have been prompted merely by fear of the witnesses, and the owner's lack of renewed knowledge leaves the return deficient. This answers the question of the Shiltei HaGibborim.
