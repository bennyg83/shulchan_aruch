The wording of the Tur, seif 3, and Rambam there, law 10, ruling like Rabbi Akiva in the baraita and according to Rav Zevid in the name of Rava. The Halakhot likewise rule so.
Raavad objects there and writes that he may instead return the purse to its owner, who can count his money. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is correct, and Rambam also agrees; the ruling applies only while the purse remains in the custodian's domain.
