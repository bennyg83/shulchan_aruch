The wording of Rambam there, law 14, following the Halakhot which rule like Rav Chisda regarding living creatures, since Rabbah agrees with him and his reasoning is persuasive. The Maggid Mishneh cites an opinion that if the sheep is uniquely marked and there is none like it in the flock, then once the owners knew of the theft, returning it to the flock suffices even without recounting. This appears from the sugya but Rif and Rambam did not record it.
