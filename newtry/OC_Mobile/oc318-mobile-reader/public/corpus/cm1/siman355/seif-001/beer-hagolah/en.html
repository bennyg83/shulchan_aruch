The wording of the Tur, from the dispute of Amoraim on the Mishnah concerning one who stole a lamb, Bava Kamma chapter 10, 118a. Rif rules, regarding something not alive, like Rabbi Yochanan, that where the owner knows the count, returning it to the count suffices, etc.
The wording of Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilkhot Gezeilah, law 8, from the baraitot according to the final establishment there, 118b. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's explanation follows Rif and is primary. Rashba likewise agrees. According to them, even if the owner did not know the exact amount in the purse, since a person commonly checks his money, once it is returned he will find it among his funds. It requires study why the Mechaber first follows Rashi and Tur but later follows Rif and Rambam. Sema also notes this.
