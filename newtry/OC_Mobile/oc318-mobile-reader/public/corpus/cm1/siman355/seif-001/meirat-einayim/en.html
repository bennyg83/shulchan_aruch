The thief is not exempt, etc. The return was not a proper restoration. Once the owners knew it had been stolen, they stopped guarding it, and even though the thief later put it back, the owner did not know it was there and therefore did not resume guarding it.
“And they were given to him that he had no knowledge of how much he was, and that he had been given to him, and that he had no idea how much he was, and that he had been given to him The Rambam, however, said, “If you throw it into your box, you know that there is no one in it, or that you may not know what Hashem has done to him, and that it is not the same as that of Hashem’s mercy that we have not known him.”
