[Shulchan Aruch] Until he informs the owners. Note: See Milchamot at the end of Bava Kamma and the Rashba's novellae there, who writes that even if the thief returns the item to the owners' house, he retains the legal status of a thief until he actually informs them. Since the owners designated the guardian's house as the location of this item and do not want it in their own home, placing it there is not considered a return to the owners' domain. See Raavad, Hilchot Geneivah chapter 4, the Baal HaMaor there, and Shitah Mekubetzet, Perek HaMafkid. This requires study.
