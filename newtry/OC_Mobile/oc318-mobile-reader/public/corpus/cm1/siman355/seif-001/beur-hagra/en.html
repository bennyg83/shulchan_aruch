One who steals, etc. The ruling follows Rabbi Yochanan. Although Rav and Shmuel generally prevail jointly against Rabbi Yochanan, their views divide depending on whether the owner knew, and Rabbi Chisda's position is not decisive. Raza rules like Rav, but Ramban reconciles the sugyot by distinguishing return to the place from which it was stolen from return after the original guarding arrangement ended. The Shulchan Aruch combines Rashi's and Rif's explanations: ordinarily a person checks his purse and notices the return, but where he did not previously know the count, mere replacement is insufficient. The phrase, 'therefore,' follows the latter answer in the Gemara.
