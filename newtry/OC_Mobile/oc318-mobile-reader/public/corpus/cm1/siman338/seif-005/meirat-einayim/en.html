With the cow of a non-Jew, etc. Meaning, even if the threshing belongs to the non-Jew, because the verse simply says, "You shall not muzzle." Conversely, if a non-Jew threshes with a Jew's cow, even if the threshing belongs to the Jew, the Jew does not violate the prohibition. This applies even if the Jew knows what is happening, because he himself is not performing the muzzling act. The Tur's statement that this applies "when it is not in the Jew's presence" was written only with respect to a separate rabbinic prohibition, meaning that when it is not in his presence there is not even such a prohibition, as I explained there.
