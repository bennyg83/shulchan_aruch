There, law 4. The Maggid Mishneh cites the wording of the baraita at the end of 89b, 'cows that are treading through grain.' Raavad explains this as animals passing through the grain to shorten their route to another place. The primary explanation, however, is Rambam's, from the expression 'and the ridges into a valley.' Ramban supports him from Yerushalmi, chapter 15 of Terumot, which teaches: 'while threshing,' but not while merely walking.
This follows Rashi and Tosafot there, and Raavad likewise writes so in the name of the commentators.
This is the wording of the Tur and of the baraita there. In my humble opinion, however, that remedy was said only where the animal threshes terumah or maaser, whereas with ordinary produce one allows it to eat. Rambam likewise records it there only regarding terumah and maaser. Perhaps here the case is different because the damage from peeled kernels is substantial.
