Because he shortened the route for it. This is the Rambam's language. The Maggid Mishneh and Nimukei Yosef explain that the animal is merely passing over the produce on its way elsewhere in order to shorten the route. In this case there is no need to hang some of that species around its neck, as is required in other cases because of appearances, since everyone can see that the animal's purpose is not threshing.
