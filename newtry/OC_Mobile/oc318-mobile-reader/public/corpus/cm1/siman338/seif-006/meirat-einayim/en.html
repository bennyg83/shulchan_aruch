He said to him, “And he said to him, ‘No, no, no matter what he has done, and he has not been cursed, and he has not done so, and he has not done so, nor is he not allowed to do so, and he has not done anything to do with him.”
A thorn lodged in its mouth, a lion lay before it, etc. Tosafot explains that these questions arise specifically where the obstruction happened by itself. If the owner himself placed a thorn in its mouth or positioned a lion outside so the animal would fear it and not eat, he certainly violates the prohibition. By contrast, placing its young outside or spreading a hide over the threshing floor remains a doubtful case even when done intentionally, because those acts do not physically muzzle the animal in the same direct manner.
