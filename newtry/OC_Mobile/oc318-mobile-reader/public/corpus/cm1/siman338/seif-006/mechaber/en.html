If one tells a non-Jew, 'Muzzle my cow and thresh with it,' this is forbidden, for instructing a non-Jew is prohibited with respect to all prohibitions, as on Shabbos. Likewise, if a thorn lodged in its mouth, a lion crouched outside, its young was placed outside, it was thirsty and not given water, or a hide was spread over the threshing floor so it could not eat, all these and similar acts are forbidden, though no lashes are administered.
