Prohibitions. See Even HaEzer, end of siman 5.
He receives lashes. The Beit Yosef writes that although lashes are not administered nowadays, the Tur nevertheless followed the Rambam's wording. See Yoreh Deah, siman 27, in the additions to the Shach's work, where it is explained that there can still be a practical consequence nowadays where lashes apply. The Bach writes that the Tur intended to say that he does not receive lashes, for if he did receive lashes he would not also pay, because one is punished for only one act of wickedness. His words are surprising, for regarding muzzling it is explicit in the Gemara and poskim that although he receives lashes he must also pay, as stated in seif 4. Furthermore, it appears to me that nowadays, even where lashes and monetary payment would coincide, he is liable for the money. We rule that if he was not warned or acted unintentionally he must pay, as stated below in siman 350. This shows that with offenses punishable by lashes, exemption from payment applies specifically where lashes are actually administered. Nowadays, therefore, the case is like one in which no warning was given. This is also implied by Maharshal, chapter HaKones, siman 6. Shach.
