The same applies if he muzzled it beforehand. The phrase "you shall not muzzle" also implies that the animal must not be in a muzzled state while threshing, similar to the verse, "Wine and strong drink you shall not drink when you come, etc." So the Gemara explains, as I wrote in the Perishah.
Even if he muzzled it by voice, he receives lashes. We rule that movement of the lips is considered an act.
