The wording of the Tur, from the baraita there, 90a. The Mechaber's statement in siman 337, seif 19, that a worker should not starve and afflict himself, is different. There, the worker's principal sustenance, from which he derives strength, does not come from the vineyard, and therefore he may not starve himself. With an animal, however, its principal eating and satiation come from the grain in which it is threshing, and thereby it regains its strength. Sema.
