Bundles of straw. Meaning, one may give it bundles so that it will not eat so much of the crop. This resembles what the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write in siman 337, seif 15: a worker may dip his bread in brine, and the owner may give the worker wine so that he will not eat many grapes. I explained there that there is no prohibition in strengthening or satisfying the worker himself so that he naturally eats less. See there.
The owner of the cow may allow it to become hungry. See what I wrote at the end of siman 337, seif 19, to resolve why this does not contradict the rule there that a worker should not starve and afflict himself in order to eat excessively from the owner's produce. See there.
