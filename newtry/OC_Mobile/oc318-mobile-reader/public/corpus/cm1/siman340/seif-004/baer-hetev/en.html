Once he pulled it. He is likewise liable for unavoidable accidents from the moment he pulls the animal or utensil, even if he has not yet used it. This follows all the more so from the law of return below, siman 341, seif 5, as is evident from Kiddushin 47b and explicitly from Tosafot there. It is likewise evident from the Rosh and the other poskim. I further concluded in siman 72, seif 4, seif katan 31, that if one deposited an article with another, whether as an unpaid or paid bailee, and later gave him permission to use it, he becomes a borrower even though he has not yet actually used it. See there, and Responsa Mahara Sasson, siman 144. Shach.
Since the owners withdrew. Although the lender may retract so long as the borrower has not pulled the animal, nevertheless, since he did not retract and the owner withdrew from guarding the animal because the bailee accepted responsibility for it, the borrower becomes liable for unavoidable accidents. So writes the Sema. See Responsa Ibn Lev, part 1, klal 17, siman 97, that the party in possession may say, "I rely on the Rambam's view." See Responsa Rabbi Levi ibn Habib, siman 94. Shach.
