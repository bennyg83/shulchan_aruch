And some say, etc. Rosh derives this from Bava Metzia 99a, from the case, 'lend me your cow, etc.' See siman 291, seif 2, and siman 307, seif 2. Tosafot agrees regarding an unpaid or paid custodian, while Rosh extends the rule according to his own ruling in that passage. Rama's formulation here is therefore not exact.
And he must swear, etc. The Gemara says, 'go bring witnesses, etc.' Rosh explains that where witnesses were available, etc.; alternatively, etc.
