mem) The language of the Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilkhot She'eilah, law 4. The Maggid Mishneh cites Bava Metzia 51a: one who rents a cow becomes responsible for its food from the time of meshikhah, and the same applies to a borrower. This is explicit in the Rif and in the statement of Rav Pappa in Ketubot, chapter Elu Na'arot.
nun) The language of the Mechaber based on what was written above in seif 2.
samekh) Rava's statement in Bava Metzia 96b.
