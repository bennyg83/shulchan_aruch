Tur seif 3. Rosh writes so there in the name of the French sages, for we rule like Rava in Bava Kamma chapter 8, 87a, regarding one who struck another's hand and it will eventually heal, where he pays only the loss in value for each day and this is not treated as permanent damage. Here, since it is damage by a person to an ox, he pays only damage and not loss of labor.
