And because it will ultimately return, he is exempt. Even according to the opinion at the end of siman 307 that liability exists there, that case is different because the damage arose through negligence. Here the condition arose through unavoidable accident and according to everyone he is exempt. Sema. The Shach disagrees and holds that this too remains an unresolved legal doubt. See Beurim: this exemption applies specifically where the item will fully return to its original state without any residual deficiency. In that case it is not considered damage, as explained in siman 420. But if even a small part of the skin will remain deficient, such as where a scar remains, that is legally damage and he must pay the full depreciation caused by the injury, meaning the reduction in immediate sale value. Therefore, if one rents an animal from a gentile and another person injures it so that the renter cannot use it during the rental period, the tortfeasor must pay the renter the value of the lost rental use. Regarding medical expenses: whenever the animal cannot return to its prior state without treatment, the treatment is part of the damage and must be paid. But if it can return without treatment and treatment only accelerates recovery, the tortfeasor is exempt from treatment expense. Food during the period when the animal cannot work and support itself must be paid by the custodian. See Beurim for many further applications.
