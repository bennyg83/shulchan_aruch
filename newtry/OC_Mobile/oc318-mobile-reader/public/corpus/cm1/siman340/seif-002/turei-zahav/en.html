(Seif 2: The flesh became emaciated, etc.) I do not understand the Sema's distinction between this rule and siman 307. There is no relevant difference between a borrower's liability for unavoidable loss and a renter's liability for negligence: whatever deterioration creates liability in one framework creates it in the other according to its standard. The Tur's phrase “the flesh became emaciated by itself” is not intended to imply liability if negligence caused the emaciation. It contrasts natural emaciation with deterioration caused by the work. Even emaciation through negligence is exempt according to the French authorities, as the Beit Yosef and Rama explain in siman 307.
