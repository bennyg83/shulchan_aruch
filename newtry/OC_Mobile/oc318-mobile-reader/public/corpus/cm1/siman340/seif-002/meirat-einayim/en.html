If the condition will ultimately reverse, he is exempt. There is no law of compensation for temporary loss of use in an animal. Even according to the opinion cited at the end of siman 307 that can require payment where the animal became weakened through negligence, that is where the injury itself arose through negligence. Here it weakened by itself, and according to the view that might otherwise impose liability, the present case concerns unavoidable weakening. Although a borrower is liable for unavoidable accidents, if the condition is temporary and the animal will recover, this opinion exempts him.
Since it is no longer intact, etc. We say that the beginning of its wasting away is the beginning of its death, and liability therefore attaches from that point.
