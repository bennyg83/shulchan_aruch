Orphans. See what the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write in siman 122 concerning agency: one should not deliver the item even to the depositor's agent in the presence of witnesses without a power of attorney, for this reason, lest the depositor die. See also there, seif 7, that even with a power of attorney, if the claim was not transferred to the agent by means of land, the authorization lapses if the depositor dies. Shach.
