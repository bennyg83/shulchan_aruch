The same applies if, etc. Rashi there, s.v. “aval,” and this is compelled by the Gemara from its challenge concerning “you completed it.”
If he said to him, etc. Bava Kamma 104b; see also there 104a, Tosafos s.v. “shaliach.” Their words imply that if he said in his presence, “Send it,” this is effective even if the agent died. Tosafos in Bava Metzia 98b, s.v. “ve'amar lei,” however, imply that it is ineffective. This is the doubt recorded by the Mordechai and the Rama. See siman 121, seif 1, and siman 122, seif 1, where the ruling follows Tosafos here, and see what I wrote at the end of siman 121, that this is the view of all the poskim. As to the statement that if so he should not send it, see siman 122 at the end of seif 1 in the Rama, “However, if,” etc., and what I wrote there.
