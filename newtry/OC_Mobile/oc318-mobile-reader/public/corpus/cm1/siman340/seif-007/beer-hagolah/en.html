There, law 2. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam rules unlike Rav there, following what appears from the sugya that Shmuel disagrees even in this case, and that is primary. Kesef Mishneh further writes that because the Gemara brings a baraita as support for Rav and Rav Ashi compares the case, it somewhat implies that we do not rule like Rav.
