He struck it with a staff, etc. This is the Rambam's view. The Tur writes that the Rosh disagrees, but the Mechaber, Beit Yosef, and Sema rule like the Rambam, and this is correct, for Rabbenu Chananel, Tosafot, the Maggid Mishneh, Ran, and Nimukei Yosef agree. The Rosh appears to have ruled otherwise only because he follows his own view that once the owners withdraw, the custodian immediately becomes liable. According to those who rule that liability begins only when the custodian draws the object into his possession, the same principle applies here. It is therefore difficult that the Rama adopts the Rambam's ruling here while citing the Rosh's contrary view above. The Maggid Mishneh and Nimukei Yosef make the dependence of these two issues explicit.
