Mice ate it. If one borrowed weapons and the weapons were taken away because of the very use for which they were borrowed, this is considered death through use and the borrower is exempt. The Shach disagrees and rules that he is liable. See Beurim: if the weapons were borrowed for a life threatening rescue, the borrower is exempt, even if he borrowed without permission. The same applies to one who borrows utensils in order to rescue property from a fire.
