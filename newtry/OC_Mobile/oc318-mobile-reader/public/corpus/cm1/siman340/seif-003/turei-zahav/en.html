(Seif 8: See above, siman 231) There it is explained that a bailee who returns an item to the depositor's wife is exempt. A borrower is different because all benefit is his, and he must return it specifically to the lender. Regarding a pledged object, siman 72, seif 31, likewise requires return specifically to the borrower, and the Sema labors to compare a pledge to a loan. In siman 120 I cited Maharshal's view that the Rosh should be followed generally: in every case the item must be returned to the person who entrusted it.
