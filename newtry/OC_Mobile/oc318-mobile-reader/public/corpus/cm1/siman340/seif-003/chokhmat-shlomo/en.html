Seif 5. One who borrows a cow from another, and the lender sends it to him through his son or through his agent, etc. Note: The Machaneh Ephraim holds that if the owner says to his hired worker or retainer, "Go and make yourself available to the borrower together with my cow," this constitutes borrowing while the owner is in the borrower's service, because a worker has a stronger status than an ordinary agent in this respect. See my responsa on Even HaEzer, seventh edition, siman 49, in my responsum to the community of Simwatish, where I explained the view of the Machaneh Ephraim on this point. See there and understand well.
