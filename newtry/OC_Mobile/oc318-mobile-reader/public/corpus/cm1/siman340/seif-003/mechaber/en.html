If one borrowed an animal to travel a known route and robbers or wild beasts seized it on that route, this is considered death caused by the work. Rama: Others disagree because the same accident could have occurred without the journey. If one lent a cat to catch mice and the mice killed it, that is caused by the work. Likewise, an animal that became exhausted and overheated during its work and later died, or stumbled on the road and died, is considered to have died from the work, provided the problem was noticed during the work. If not noticed immediately, the borrower cannot swear that the work caused the death, because it may have died independently.
