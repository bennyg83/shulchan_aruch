(Collected note) Such as, etc. See there: “Granted, where it broke,” etc. Specifically, etc. This is also the reason that where it died because of the work for which it was borrowed, he is exempt; see Shach, seif katan 5. Therefore, etc. See Beer HaGolah, citing “it was caused from Heaven,” as stated at the end of Bava Kamma. The Mordechai gives another reason: he did not become liable for an unavoidable accident to the pledge because he never performed meshichah, as stated at the beginning of siman 291; see seif 4 and siman 72, seif 44. It need not be said, etc. The Mishnah there, and the Gemara there 95a: “Rather,” etc. (Collected note) It need not be said, etc. There 35a: “Theft and loss with respect to a borrower,” etc., “rather, like this,” etc. To perform work with it, etc. This is how the Rambam and Tur explain it; see siman 341, seif 7, and similarly in Taanit 23b. (Collected note) Even if it is easy, etc. See Sema and Beer HaGolah. Even for that same purpose, etc. This is how the Maggid Mishneh explains it, and this is the plain meaning of the Gemara.
