a) The language of the Tur, and so writes the Rambam at the beginning of Hilkhot She'eilah uPikadon.
b) Explicit in the Torah and in the Mishnah, Bava Metzia 93a and Shevuot 49a. The meaning is that the item is still pledged to the gentile on behalf of the lender.
c) He explains there that the lender cannot say, 'Give me the amount of debt from which you were released through my collateral,' because Heaven showed mercy to the borrower.
d) Rava concludes thus on Bava Metzia 96b, because the borrower may say, 'I did not borrow it merely to place it in a canopy,' meaning it was borrowed for use.
e) From the incident of the man who borrowed an axe before Rava and the incident of one who borrowed a bucket before Rav Pappa, Bava Metzia 96a.
f) The Maggid Mishneh writes this regarding the Rambam's ruling that if the animal died before he plowed with it, after he plowed with it, or after he rode it, but it died during the riding, he is liable.
g) The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes there, and states that the earlier authorities wrote so.
