In the Tur “He shall make no distinction between a small case,” etc. Sanhedrin derives from “You shall hear the small like the great” that a case of one peruta should be as dear as a case of one hundred maneh, and concludes that this concerns priority, not the obvious duty of equal deliberation. Why then do Rambam and Tur state the matter here regarding deliberation? Their statement follows the preceding rule that a judge may withdraw from a case of one peruta just as from a large case. The Gemara’s verse, however, concerns hearing both; it cannot refer to withdrawal. Therefore the Gemara asks that equal deliberation is obvious and concludes that the verse concerns priority.
