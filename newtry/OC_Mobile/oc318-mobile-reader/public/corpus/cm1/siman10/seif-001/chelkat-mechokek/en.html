Even if she was not divorced. Rather, the matter arose only because of a rumor, whether concerning the first man or the second. Even if the rumor was merely an unsubstantiated report that would not require a get as a matter of law, nevertheless, if she was divorced she may not return to the first husband, because people do not know that the get was given merely as an added precaution. If one asks that an announcement should be made, some may have heard about the get but not heard the announcement. So writes the Rosh in Responsa, klal 35, cited by the Beit Yosef at the beginning of this siman. See below siman 46 and above siman 6, seif katan 2.
