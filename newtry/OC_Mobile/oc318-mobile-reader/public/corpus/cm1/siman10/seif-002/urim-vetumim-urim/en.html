As clear as the sun. The Sages interpret, "House of David, judge justice in the morning." Do judges judge only in the morning? Judgment may occur throughout the day. Rather, if the matter is as clear to you as the sun, state it; otherwise do not. This gives some support to Tosafot's view above that one who has drunk wine may still adjudicate monetary law. Otherwise, why would the Gemara ask that judgment occurs all day? Morning extends until the sixth hour, after which a king ordinarily eats and likely drinks wine at his meal. If wine prohibited monetary judgment, the verse's emphasis on morning would have a straightforward meaning. The Gemara's question therefore indicates that moderate drinking does not disqualify monetary judgment.
