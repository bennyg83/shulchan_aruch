And he repeated his words, saying... See Gittin 46a, Tosafot, passage beginning 'for if he said to her,' where the case is one in which he repeated his statement, but not one in which he formulated a doubled legal condition. If he made a fully doubled condition and said, 'On this condition I give you the get, and if the matter proves false the get shall not take effect,' then if the condition is false the get is certainly void, and the rule that he may not take her back does not help. The same appears in the Rashba's novellae.
