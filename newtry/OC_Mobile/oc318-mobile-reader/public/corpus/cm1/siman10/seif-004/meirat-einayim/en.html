A case of one perutah, etc. The Gemara states this plainly and says that no verse is needed for the proposition that a small monetary claim is legally equal to a large one. The verse, "You shall hear the small as the great," is interpreted instead to require hearing the litigant who came first even if his case concerns only one perutah. This is unlike the Ir Shushan, who derives the equality of small and large cases directly from that verse.
