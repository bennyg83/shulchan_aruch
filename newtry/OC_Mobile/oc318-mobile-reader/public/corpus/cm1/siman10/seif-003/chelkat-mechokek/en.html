Some say this applies specifically where he said to her, etc. See Beit Yosef for the sugya from which this law is derived. The first opinion is that of the Rosh and Tur, who rule like the first tanna, the Sages, and Rava. The second is the view of the Rif and Ramah. The third is Rambam's view, likewise following the first tanna rather than the Sages, but ruling stringently in accordance with the latter version of the Gemara, that even if he did not make the statement to her he may not take her back, as a penalty. In my commentary to the Tur, Even HaEzer, I discussed at length the difficulties in the Beit Yosef's treatment, and there is no need to elaborate here.
