Any amount of land, however small, may serve as the basis for acquiring a large quantity of movable property together with it. Rama: The requirement of some land excludes a case where we know with certainty that the transferor owns none. If it is not known that he owns no land, his declaration that the property is transferred together with land is sufficient, and he need not identify the land. Therefore, people transfer property together with four cubits of land even when they do not know that he owns such land. See siman 113. One may also transfer property together with a seat in the synagogue. Although the seats belong to the public, each person's assigned place is regarded as lent to him and may serve as the land supporting the transfer.
