(Shach, seif katan 6) And he also paid the price of the slave, etc. Note: Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Kamma 11, s.v. 'rather, I know the Mishnah,' proves that where the slave is standing within the land, so that he is regarded as attached to that land, payment of the slave's price is not required. But in the passage beginning 'but he said,' the opposite is explicit.
