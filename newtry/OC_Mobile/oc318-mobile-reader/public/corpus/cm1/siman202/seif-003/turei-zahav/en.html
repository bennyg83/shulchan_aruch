(Seif 5: Even if he says, “Acquire,” etc.) The Beit Yosef asks why the word “even” is necessary. It appears to me that it was necessary to state this here because one who takes possession of land acquires the slaves upon it even without the expression “by means of,” as the Sema writes. One might therefore have mistakenly thought that when the slaves are not standing upon the land, the failure to acquire applies only where he did not say “by means of.”
