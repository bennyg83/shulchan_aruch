Even though he said to him, "Acquire the movable property." This implies that he said this to the person on whose behalf he was conferring the movable property. Rashi's wording in the Gemara is different. He writes that he said to the person to whom he transferred the field, "Acquire the field for yourself, and through it the movable property shall be acquired for so-and-so." See there. The Mechaber apparently holds that even if he said the formula to each of them separately, it is ineffective.
The second person does not acquire, etc. This is an unresolved question in the Gemara, and therefore the burden of proof rests on the claimant.
