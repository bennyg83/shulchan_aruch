[Shach, continuation] And this is also explicitly proven. Note: This is not a conclusive proof. One can say that the question of Tosafos and the Rosh is based on the statement in Bava Metzia that he transferred ownership through the recipient’s courtyard; therefore he should merely have lent them the place. Rabbi Shimshon, chapter 7 of Demai, similarly writes in response to Tosafos’ question: If you say that land is lent while movable property is gifted, it cannot be transferred by virtue of the land. This is satisfactory according to the view that movable property is acquired together with land, but according to Rabbi Eliezer, who says the acquisition occurs through the law of courtyard, that reasoning does not apply. End quote.
