[Rama] And some disagree. Note: The same formulation appears in siman 388, seifim 1 and 7; siman 390, seifim 2 and 7; at the end of siman 391; siman 410, seif 12; and siman 414, seif 8. In all of these, the Mechaber rules that seizure is effective and the Rama adds, "and some disagree." However, in siman 248, seif 11, the Rama rules without qualification like the Mechaber, and the Shach there in seif katan 7 expresses surprise at this. The same occurs in siman 362, seif 6; at the end of siman 363; and in Yoreh Deah, siman 267, seif 39, where the Rama again rules without qualification like the Mechaber. Yet there in Yoreh Deah, seif 80, the Rama writes, "and some say that seizure is effective in this case." From the Rama below, siman 390, seif 7, it is clear that even where a double doubt supports the one who seized, the seizure is ineffective, as I wrote in the margin there.
