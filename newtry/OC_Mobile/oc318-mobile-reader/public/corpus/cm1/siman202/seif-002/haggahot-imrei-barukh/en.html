(Shach, seif katan 3) Unlike here, where his courtyard and his gift, etc. Note: See Mordechai, Bava Metzia, ruling 241. This requires study. See below, siman 203, Shach seif katan 1, and also siman 198, seif katan 7. Responsa Maharit, siman 65, makes clear that even in such a case he acquires. See the Ritva's language in Kiddushin 26, passage beginning "the fifth," whose essential point is that where one wants to acquire land and movables simultaneously, both cannot be acquired together except through agav. But if the land is acquired first and afterward the movables through the law of courtyard, that is valid. Tosafot Rid there also implies this.
