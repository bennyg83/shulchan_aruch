There, law 9. The Maggid Mishneh writes that after the Gemara concluded that the movable property need not be piled upon the land, it asked whether the word 'with' must be stated. Rambam explains that question only where the movable goods are not piled on the land. Where they are piled on it, once the land is acquired, Rambam holds that the land is like the buyer's courtyard and a person's courtyard acquires for him. The wording of the Halakhot somewhat inclines this way. The Ran and Kesef Mishneh likewise write similarly.
