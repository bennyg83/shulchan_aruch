This rule applies when the movable property is piled upon that land. If it is elsewhere, the transferor must say, 'Acquire the movable property together with the land.' Even if the movable property is in another country, once he says, 'Acquire it together with such-and-such land,' then upon acquiring the land the recipient acquires the movable property, although it is not situated upon the land. If he did not say that it should be acquired together with the land, he does not acquire it when it is not piled there. Rama: Some say that even when the movable property is piled on the land, the transferor must expressly say that it is acquired together with the land. This appears to be the primary ruling. Some require specifically the expression 'together with,' and hold that saying 'with' is insufficient. Others disagree.
