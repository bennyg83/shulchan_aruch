The Shach writes that a courtyard acquires only when it had already become the buyer's courtyard, not where the courtyard and the gift enter his ownership simultaneously. See Beurim: this is difficult. Even where the courtyard and gift come simultaneously, courtyard acquisition works. The present case instead concerns where the seller said merely, "Go, make chazakah and acquire." If he also says acquisition together with land, that chazakah can acquire the movables even when performed outside his presence.
