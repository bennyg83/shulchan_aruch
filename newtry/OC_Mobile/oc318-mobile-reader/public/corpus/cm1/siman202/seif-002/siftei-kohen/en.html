Some say that even if they are piled upon it, etc. The Sema explains that this applies only when the land is not protected for the recipient, for otherwise it would acquire as a courtyard. This is incorrect. The Ran and Maggid Mishneh imply that even a protected courtyard does not help merely because the goods are piled there, and the Raavad's gloss states this explicitly. The Tur and Rama also imply it. The reason appears to be that a courtyard acquires only when it is already his courtyard; here the courtyard and the gift come to him simultaneously. This appears the primary ruling.
