The language of Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 8, from the Mishnah and Gemara in the first chapter of Kiddushin, 27a.
A question there, 27a, that was resolved. The Maggid Mishneh wonders why Rambam did not write that when movable property is sold one does not acquire all of it together with the land unless the price of all of it has been paid, since Rava expressly states this. The Kesef Mishneh writes that according to the Rif's version, the words of Rava apply only to the case of one who sold ten fields in ten countries, etc. There is no doubt that the Rif's version was also Rambam's version.
