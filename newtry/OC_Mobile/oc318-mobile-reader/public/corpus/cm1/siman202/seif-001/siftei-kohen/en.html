One who transfers land and movable property, etc. See Responsa Rashdam Kohen, part 3, siman 5. See also Responsa Mahar'i Beit Levi, siman 36, who questions Rashdam Kohen's ruling.
Some say that even if the land is leased, etc. This is also evident from Bava Metzia 11b and Kiddushin 27a. There, the law of land sold together with movable property given as a gift is derived from land leased together with movable property given as a gift. The Rama's presentation of this as merely 'some say' therefore requires study, for this case is actually the clearer one. Rabbeinu Yerucham further writes that some say borrowed land together with gifted movable property does not acquire, while Tosafot indicate that it does. I do not know why borrowing should be inferior to leasing. Tosafot and the Rosh in Bava Metzia, in the episode of Rabban Gamliel and the elders, expressly indicate that borrowed land acquires gifted movable property just as leased land does.
