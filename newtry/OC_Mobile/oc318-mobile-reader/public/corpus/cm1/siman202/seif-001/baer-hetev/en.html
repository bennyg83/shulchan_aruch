And some say. This is likewise demonstrated by the Gemara in the first chapter of Bava Metzia and the first chapter of Kiddushin. There it is clear that land by sale together with movables by gift is derived from land by rental together with movables by gift. It therefore requires study why the Rama presents this ruling as "some say," for it is in fact more straightforward. Rabbeinu Yerucham also writes that where land is lent and movables are given as a gift, some hold that the movables are not acquired, while Tosafot appear to hold that they are. The Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe cite this. I do not know why a loan of land should be inferior to rental. Tosafot and the Rosh in the first chapter of Bava Metzia, in the incident of Rabban Gamliel and the elders, explicitly imply that lent land can acquire gifted movables just like rented land. The Beit Yosef in siman 195, at the end of seif 12, likewise writes so. Ibid.
