One who transfers land and movable property together. This is the correct text, unlike printed editions that insert the word "or."
The movable property is acquired together with the land. The Mishnah and Gemara state: "Property without responsibility," meaning movable property, "is acquired together with property that bears responsibility," meaning land, "through money, a deed, or chazakah." In the first chapter of Kiddushin this is derived from the verse concerning the sons of Yehoshafat: "Their father gave them many gifts, silver, gold, precious objects, together with fortified cities in Yehudah." This means that he gave them the movable property through acquisition agav the cities he gave them. From there we infer that just as there the movable property was not self-moving, so too the rule specifically concerns movable property that does not move itself. This excludes slaves acquired agav land, regarding whom we require that they actually stand within the land and then be acquired together with it, as explained immediately below.
