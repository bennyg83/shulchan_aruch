And some say even. See Baer Heitev, who questions why borrowing should be weaker than renting and says Tosafot in the first chapter of Bava Metzia apparently proves otherwise. Dagul MeRevavah responds that in his novellae to Kiddushin he demonstrated that Tosafot supplies no such proof. If anything, Tosafot can be read to imply that land obtained by borrowing and movable property obtained as a gift are not acquired in that manner. The passage in Bava Metzia proves nothing, because there Rabban Gamliel's transfer operated through the law of courtyard. Examine the matter carefully.
