We will learn from the scriptures and give them many gifts with cities in shapes and bodies
Even where the land is rented. The Shach asks why this is a novelty, because the rule that rental of land can support acquisition is explicit in Bava Metzia 11. Indeed, acquisition of owned land is learned from rented land. The real dispute in Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe concerns rented land together with movables given as a gift. The Shach also asks why borrowing should be any weaker than renting.
