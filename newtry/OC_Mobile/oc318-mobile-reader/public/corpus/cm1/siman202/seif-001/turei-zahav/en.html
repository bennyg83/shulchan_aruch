(Seif 2, in the gloss: The word “with” is ineffective.) Regarding the Sema’s difficulty with the Ir Shushan, one may answer that this itself is the Gemara’s question: Is the word “with” in the verse merely the language of Scripture, so that the transferor need not use any particular expression, neither “with” nor “by means of,” or perhaps Scripture is stating the wording that the giver must use, namely that he must say “with,” and the same would apply to “by means of”? If so, according to the Gemara’s conclusion, “with” is effective. Nevertheless, since Rashi maintains that specifically the expression “by means of” is required, as the Beit Yosef writes, while the Ramah disagrees with him, we certainly do not follow the Ramah against Rashi, particularly where doing so would extract money from its present possessor.
