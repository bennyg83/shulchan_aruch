(Supplement) He said to him, 'Pull,' etc. The Gemara raises the question specifically regarding an animal because its modes of movement differ. Tosafot. Moreover, the chapter HaMatznia discusses one who steals a purse on Shabbos and acquires the coins before removing the entire purse. The chapter HaMokher Peirot teaches four measures concerning sellers, and states that acquisition requires lifting, even where the goods were in the seller's vessel. Rosh. (End of supplement.)
