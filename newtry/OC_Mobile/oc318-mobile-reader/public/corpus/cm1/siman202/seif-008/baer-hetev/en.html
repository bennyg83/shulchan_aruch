With slaves. See the Sema's explanation of the reason. All his words are taken from the Rosh at the beginning of Bava Kamma, and it appears that the Rambam also agrees. Shach.
He acquires. Meaning, unlike where the slaves are standing inside the land, in which case, according to the Rambam, he acquires them even if he did not say, 'Acquire them together with the land.' Even according to those who disagree with the Rambam, if he made a chazakah in the land and also paid the price of the slave, he acquires by the laws of acquiring land, as explained above. Thus far the Sema, and the Bach writes likewise. The Beit Yosef asks why the wording says 'even.' It appears to me that this wording was necessary because, since one who makes chazakah in the land can acquire the slaves even without agav, one might have mistakenly thought that where the slaves are not standing in the land the failure to acquire applies only without agav. Taz.
