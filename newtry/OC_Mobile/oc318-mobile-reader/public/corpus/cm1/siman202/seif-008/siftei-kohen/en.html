One who transfers slaves. See Sema, seif katan 15. All of his words are taken from the Rosh at the beginning of Bava Kamma, and it appears that Rambam also agrees. Examine carefully.
Even if he says to him, acquire. Meaning, unlike sheaves that are inside the land, which according to Rambam are acquired even if he did not say, "acquire them together with the land." Even those who disagree with Rambam concede where he took possession of the land and also paid the value of the slave, because the slave is then acquired under the laws governing acquisition of land, as explained above. This is the Sema's explanation, and the Bach writes likewise.
