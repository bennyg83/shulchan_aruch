[Sema, letter 20] An independent intent. Note: This is difficult for me. It is the Rosh who writes that he must also be sleeping, yet the Rosh in the first chapter of Bava Metzia rules like Rav Papa that when another person's intent is to transfer ownership, one acquires even through an unguarded courtyard. The rulings of the Mechaber are likewise difficult, for below, siman 268, seif 4, he rules without qualification that when another person's intent is to transfer ownership, acquisition occurs even if the recipient runs after the objects but cannot reach them. Why then was sleeping necessary here? According to what I wrote in the margin in the name of the Derishah, the matter is resolved. Analyze carefully.
