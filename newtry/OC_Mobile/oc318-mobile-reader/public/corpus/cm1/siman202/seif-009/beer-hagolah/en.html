Kaf) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 12, based on baraitot and the Gemara's explanations there.
Lamed) The Gemara explains there that a moving courtyard does not acquire.
Mem) The Tur, seif 8. Tosafot write there that the courtyard must be guarded with the owner's awareness. They bring proof from Gittin 78a, where the Gemara discusses placing a get in the hand of her sleeping slave while she guards him. Rashi explains likewise there, as does the Halakhot Gedolot. The Rosh also rules this way. Rambam, however, in the laws of get does not require the slave to be asleep. The Hagahot Maimoniyot write there that, in their view, sleep is required only for a get because receiving it is a disadvantage for her, etc.
