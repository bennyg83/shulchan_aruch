Some say, etc. See Baer HaGolah. Rambam holds that the Gemara's concluding phrase, 'And the halakha is, when he is bound,' retracts Rava's statement concerning a sleeping slave. The phrase 'and the halakha is' is precise, rather than merely saying 'when bound,' because the ruling does not follow Rava. Rambam therefore does not mention sleeping here.
