That he be sleeping. See Sema, seif katan 20, who says that the object must be guarded through the master's awareness. The language in Gittin 79 similarly states: if he is sleeping and she guards him, it is a valid get. This is difficult according to Rama above in siman 200, seif 1, who holds that in an unguarded courtyard the owner must actually stand inside it. According to Mechaber, who requires only standing beside it, there is less difficulty. One might read "sleeping and she guards him" to mean that it is enough for her to be able to guard him even from a substantial distance. But the Gemara necessarily assumes the ordinary requirement that she stand beside him. The difference is that a normally guarded courtyard requires only presence beside it, because it guards itself, whereas a sleeping person is not self-guarding and therefore also requires that she actually guard him, as Rashba explains. According to the view requiring presence inside the courtyard, however, the language remains difficult. The same question arises from acquisition through a bound animal; it is implausible that the buyer must literally stand on the animal. Gittin 79 also asks why roof airspace matters when both get and courtyard belong to the woman, a question that would not arise if she had to stand physically within that precise space. In my view, even if one requires actual presence inside an unguarded courtyard for found objects or gittin, in sale standing beside it can suffice. Tosafot in Eruvin require actual presence within the courtyard specifically regarding a get, where agency cannot operate because divorce can be adverse and the courtyard must function as hand. Perhaps a courtyard resembles hand only when she is literally within it. Maggid Mishneh similarly requires being within the courtyard principally for a found object, where ordinary agency is problematic. Ran asks why courtyard acquires a found object at all if courtyard is agency and lifting a found object for another may not work; he answers that a place in which one puts beneficial items is like an extended hand. In an unguarded courtyard, a person does not normally place property there merely because he is nearby, and therefore actual presence may be required to make it hand. Sale is different. Agency is fully effective in sale. If the courtyard is actually guarded through the buyer's presence beside it, it can act as his agent. What difference is there whether it guards itself or he actively guards it? Either way it is functioning as a guarded agent for him. This requires further study.
