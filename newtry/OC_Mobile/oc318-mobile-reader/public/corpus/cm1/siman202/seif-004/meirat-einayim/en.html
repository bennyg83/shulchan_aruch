And movables acquired for orphans are acquired, etc. A guardian's hand is treated as the orphans' own hand. The Beit Yosef adds that "orphans" is not exclusive. The same law applies to an adult who has a legally appointed guardian. Orphans are mentioned because ordinarily a guardian is appointed for minors, while we do not ordinarily appoint a guardian for an adult.
The same applies to charity collectors. The Gemara itself mentions charity collectors. Ramban holds that this rule applies specifically to a charity collector because responsibility for supporting the poor rests upon him, but not to a guardian for orphans. Others hold that the cases are equal. If so, Rama's phrase "the same applies" is actually weaker than the inference, because if anything the charity collector case is stronger.
