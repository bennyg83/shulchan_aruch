And movables for orphans. An administrator's hand is treated as the hand of the orphans. The same applies to an adult who has an administrator. Sema. See my work Mekor Chaim, siman 445: the reverse is likewise true. If an administrator sells his own land together with the orphans' movables to one purchaser, the purchaser acquires the movables through acquisition together with land. The same applies where an agent authorized to sell movables transfers them together with his own land. See there carefully.
The same applies to charity collectors. Ramban holds that this special rule applies only to charity collectors because the poor are placed upon them for support, but not to an ordinary administrator. Others hold that the two are equal. If so, Rama should not have written "the same applies," because charity collectors would be the stronger case. Sema.
