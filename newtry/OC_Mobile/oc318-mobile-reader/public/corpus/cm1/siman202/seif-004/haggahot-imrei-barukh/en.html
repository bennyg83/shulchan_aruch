(Sema, seif katan 22) Like movable property acquired together with land. Note: The Rashba's novellae to Kiddushin 26 make clear that with a flowerpot agav language is unnecessary. Regarding a document, once one took possession of the land and thereby acquired the document, the Rashba is uncertain whether agav language is required. Tosafot, Gittin 22, imply there is room for the opposite view: perhaps a document does not require agav while a flowerpot does. See Ran there and Derishah UPerishah here.
