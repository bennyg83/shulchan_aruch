[Shulchan Aruch] By agav with land, even if it was. Note: Regarding something detached and later attached to the ground, Rashi in Bava Kamma 104 writes that it is treated like land, and movable property may be acquired together with it by kinyan agav. See Rashi, Sukkah 31, s.v. "machloket," and see above, siman 95, seif 1, in the Rama.
