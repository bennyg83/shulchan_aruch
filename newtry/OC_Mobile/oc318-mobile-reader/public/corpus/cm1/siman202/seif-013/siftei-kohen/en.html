He did not acquire the utensils. Although he presently holds the utensils, possession does not resolve the doubt in his favor. The Rosh states this, as cited by the Tur. The Beit Yosef cites the Ran that possession does help here because he took possession with permission. This is unlike the case above in siman 3 in the gloss.
