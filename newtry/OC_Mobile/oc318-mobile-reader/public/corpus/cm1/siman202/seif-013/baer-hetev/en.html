The utensils. Although he is in possession of the utensils, possession does not help in a case of doubt. So write the Rosh and Tur. The Beit Yosef, however, writes in the name of the Ran that possession does help here because he took possession with permission, unlike the case above in seif 3 in the Rama. Ibid.
That walks. See above, siman 273, seif 15, and Responsa Shach at the end of siman 115.
For with a found object. The reason is that this is an unresolved question in the Gemara. In a sale, because of the doubt we leave the property in the possession of the original owner, and even if the buyer seized it by meshichah we remove it from him. With a found object, however, the first person who pulled it acquired it because of the doubt. If another then seeks to seize it from him, we remove it from the latter and give it to the first puller, who is regarded as the prior possessor. Sema.
