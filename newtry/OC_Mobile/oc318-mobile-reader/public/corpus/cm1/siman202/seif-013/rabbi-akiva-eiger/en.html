[Shulchan Aruch] He did not acquire. Note: This is the view of Tosafos and the Rosh in our sugya, because the two forms of meshichah are not comparable, as the Sema explains. Nimukei Yosef also writes so. In chapter HaSefinah, however, Nimukei Yosef writes that if the seller says, "Perform meshichah and acquire," and the buyer moves the goods by means of the animal, he certainly acquires, just as with a basket. Our sugya speaks where the seller said, "Acquire the utensils upon the animal," showing that he specifically intended acquisition through the law of courtyard. Ramban in his novellae to Bava Batra likewise writes so. See there.
