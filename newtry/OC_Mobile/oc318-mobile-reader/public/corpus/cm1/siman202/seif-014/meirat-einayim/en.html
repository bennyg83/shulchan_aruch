Even though he pulled it while it was bound, etc. He does not acquire the utensil through the animal as a courtyard because the animal belongs to the seller, not to the buyer. Nor does pulling the animal acquire the utensil, because their forms of meshichah differ, as explained above. Although the animal happens now to be bound, we follow its ordinary state, in which it is not bound. This differs from a basket: if the seller says, "Pull the basket," pulling it acquires the utensils inside, as explained in the following seif, because the movement of the basket and the utensils within it is always the same. See the Perishah.
