The first. And if they were caught, the second was made that “the first and the blades” and “J and D” were shown in the SS. Name:
Half. The Sema explains the reason: since we rule that the injured party and the owner become partners in the damaging ox, and any depreciation or appreciation belongs to both of them, neither becomes a guardian except for his own share, because each is obligated to guard his own portion. See the Shach, who cites many leading authorities who agree with the alternative opinion, and writes that this appears to be the principal view in the Gemara. See there also his challenge to the Sema's attempt to distinguish this case from the Tur's ruling in siman 177, seif 11, that partners are paid guardians for one another. This distinction has many practical ramifications. See there at length.
