Seif 1. 'An ox that gored,' etc. Note: Concerning whether one can acquire an object from which benefit is prohibited, see Ketzot HaChoshen. See what I wrote in my Orach Chayyim work entitled Sefer 102, in the addenda to Kuntres Lekuchah Tamah, where I rejected the Ketzot HaChoshen's proofs. See also Kuntres Hazmanah Milta, where I explained Rambam's position. One may distinguish between an object that can be redeemed and is therefore still called 'yours' and has not left his ownership, and an object that cannot be redeemed, which is no longer considered 'yours.' Examine it carefully.
