As partners, etc. According to this, if the injured party consecrated the animal before judgment, the consecration takes effect. The Gemara in Perek HaMeniach indicates this. Since Rabbi Akiva regards the injured party as having a proprietary share in the animal from the time of the damage, the parties are treated as partners to that extent. The consequences for sale, consecration, and subsequent damage follow the rules stated by the Gemara and the early authorities.
