According to the Mechaber's explanation of how an ox becomes forewarned after goring three times, one must say that this case concerns one where no warning was given to the owner, so the ox did not become forewarned. Alternatively, it is as Rashi explains at the beginning of the fourth chapter, for example, it saw an ox and gored it, then saw an ox and did not gore it, and so forth, in which case it is not even forewarned for intermittent goring.
