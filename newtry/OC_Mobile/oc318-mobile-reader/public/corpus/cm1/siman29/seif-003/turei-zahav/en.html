(Seif 2: they are believed regarding themselves.) The same is ruled in siman 46. This appears difficult in light of the Tur's ruling in siman 38 in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, concerning witnesses who say, "We testified falsely," that they do not pay money on their own admission. It appears that there the issue is not actual financial loss, for there may have been no loss. Rather, the discussion concerns the special liability of "as he plotted" imposed upon conspiring witnesses. They are exempt from that liability when they confess on their own, because the rule of confession does not apply to it. This is explicit in Tosafos at the beginning of Makkot, page 3. Here, however, the issue is ordinary liability for causative damage, because through their conduct they caused him a loss, and for that they must pay.
