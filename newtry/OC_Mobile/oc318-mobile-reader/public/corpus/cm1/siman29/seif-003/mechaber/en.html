Although the statement, 'We did not see it,' is ordinarily not proof, when two witnesses were equally situated and one says, 'I did not see it,' he is effectively saying to the other, 'You did not see it,' and their testimony is contradictory. {Rama: If one witness says that a particular person was with him and that person says he was not, this is not a contradiction, because a witness does not necessarily pay close attention to who was with him when the event occurred (Responsa of the Rosh, klal 59, siman 1).}
