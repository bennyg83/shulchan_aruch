We did not see. P.S. Witnesses:
Where both of them. This is implied in Ketubot 23a.
You did not see. Susing from A:
But. Weeks from B:
For a witness does not. Gittin 12a. Only exceptionally meticulous people are assumed to be exact, Sanhedrin 25a. Had the witness been precise, the statements would constitute a contradiction, as stated in Shevuot 42a regarding the market price, etc.
