The correct reading is: one said, "I did not see," which is as though he said to his fellow, "You did not see." Beit Yosef cites Hagahot Mordechai with wording similar to the present text and comments that this is the principle found in many places where witnesses say, "Our hands did not move from under his hand," meaning they were continuously present and would have seen any occurrence. Darkei Moshe, at the end of siman 30, rejects Beit Yosef's understanding. The Mechaber himself states the law here in accordance with Rama's reading.
But if the witness says that so-and-so was with him. The Mechaber states this rule as well in siman 30:4.
