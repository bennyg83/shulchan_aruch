And some say that he may add a condition. Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes that if, according to the original testimony, it had no legal effect because there was no kinyan, and the witnesses later add that a kinyan did occur, everyone agrees that the addition is ineffective, for it is equivalent to new testimony. This is stated by Rashba in Responsa, siman 563. Rabbi Akiva Eiger further cites Responsa Bnei Moshe, siman 10, that this ability to add applies only where there is still time to fulfill the condition, so that the original testimony has not been entirely uprooted. The wording 'to fulfill the testimony' appears to be a copyist's error and should read 'to fulfill the condition.' So wrote Rabbi Akiva Eiger the Elder in his glosses to the Sema; see also Tumim below, siman 46, seif katan 46.
