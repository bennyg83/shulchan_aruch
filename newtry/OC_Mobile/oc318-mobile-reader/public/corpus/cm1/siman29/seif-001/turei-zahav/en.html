(In the Shulchan Aruch, seif 1: he cannot add, etc.) Although in siman 46, seif 37, it is ruled regarding written testimony that the witnesses may say, "There was a condition attached to our words," that case is different. A written document may not have been intended to state every detail explicitly. With oral testimony, however, where beit din questioned them, they should have stated the additional qualification at the outset.
