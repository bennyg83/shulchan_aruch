After the time required for a brief statement. In monetary cases, which do not ordinarily require formal examination and interrogation, a witness may not retract after that brief period. In a matter requiring formal examination, he may retract within that brief period following the completion of the interrogations. Conversely, if his testimony was already invalidated by contradiction in the first interrogation and he then waited beyond that brief period, he cannot later restore the testimony even though the remaining interrogations were not completed. The Sema writes that the first witness may retract within the brief period following the second witness's testimony. If the second witness waited more than that period after the first witness testified, the first witness's time to retract has ended even if the second has not yet completed his testimony. Even if both first said, "We do not know," the first may retract within the brief period after the second's denial. If the litigants interrupted, then if the second witness had already completed his testimony before the interruption, he may afterward add a condition but may not completely contradict his testimony. If the interruption occurred before the second completed his testimony, he may retract even by contradicting it. Urim VeTumim.
