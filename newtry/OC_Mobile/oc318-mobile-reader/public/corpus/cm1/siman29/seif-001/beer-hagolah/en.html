From the statement, 'These witnesses came to uproot their testimony,' Ketubot 19b. They are believed only where their signatures are not independently established, because it is then as though their testimony was not yet completed. The Ran writes that Rashi explains so, as do some later authorities, and that the Ramban rules accordingly. The Rashba likewise writes in a responsum that those witnesses are not believed. But if other witnesses testify, he writes in a responsum that they are believed even where the document witnesses' handwriting is established from elsewhere.
So too the Ran in the name of Rav Hai and one group of early authorities.
