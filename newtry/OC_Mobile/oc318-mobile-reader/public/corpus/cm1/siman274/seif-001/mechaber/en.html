When a non-Jew sells land to a Jew, receives payment, but has not yet written the deed, the land is regarded as ownerless and the first to take possession acquires, as explained in siman 194. Rama: Yehoshua imposed ten conditions concerning the land; all apply even outside Eretz Yisrael. Rambam lists them in the laws of property damage and Tur in siman 274. I do not know why the author omitted them here.
