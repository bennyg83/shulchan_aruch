All these rules are explained in the Gemara in chapter 7 of Bava Kamma, 81a.
In his rulings in the last chapter of Bava Kamma, the Rosh discusses the Rif's statement that the halachah does not follow Rabbi Yishmael in the Mishnah there, 114a, nor the other ruling of Rabbi Yishmael in the baraita on 114b, that it is an enactment of beit din that one may spill his wine to save another's honey and receive payment for his wine, and unload his wood to load another person's flax when the other's donkey died, and receive the value of his wood from the flax. The Rif brought proofs, which the Rosh rejects, writing that perhaps these are indeed accepted laws. The Rama cites the honey law above in siman 274, seif 4. See there.
Rambam likewise rules so in chapter 2 of Hilkhot Gezeilah, law 14.
