Whether a defendant may appoint an authorized representative. It contains 1 seif.
A defendant cannot appoint an authorized representative to appear in beit din and answer the claimant while the defendant remains at home. For distinguished women whose dignity would be compromised by appearing in beit din, the court scribes are sent to them and hear their claims in their presence. The same procedure is followed for a talmid chacham whose Torah study is his occupation when it would demean him to go to beit din and dispute with an unlearned person.
