A husband is treated as a purchaser with respect to his wife's property. Since the Mechaber states this without qualification, it appears to apply even while she is alive and married to him. This is somewhat difficult in light of Rama's gloss above in siman 103, seif katan 10, where some say that the rule applies specifically after the woman has died. It may be answered that here the case concerns guaranteed dowry property, since the text says that she brought it into the marriage and it is immediately considered his property. There the case concerns usufruct property, similar to property appraised from her, in which the husband has only the produce during the marriage and the property remains identified with her. See Rashi's explanation there and what I wrote at the end of siman 103.
