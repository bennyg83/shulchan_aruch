He explains there that in this matter we do not say, 'omission of a warranty clause is a scribal error.' Ba'al HaTerumot writes this in Gate 43, and Rambam likewise writes in Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh chapter 11. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the view of Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash and Ba'al HaItur. The sugya in Chezkas HaBatim likewise proves it, for the Gemara says, 'perhaps he said to him: that which I will acquire,' indicating that property acquired later is not automatically encumbered. Nimukei Yosef likewise writes this there.
