[Shulchan Aruch] Then the lien also takes effect on property that he will acquire later. Note: If one granted a creditor a lien on after-acquired property, and afterward sold to others a field described as “this field that I will inherit from my father,” then according to the opinion cited by the Rama below, siman 211, seif 1, the creditor cannot collect it, because the sale and the lien take effect simultaneously. See Responsa HaRosh, principle 38, seif 5, at the end. This is likewise explained in the novellae of Raah, Ketubot 50a, s.v. “At Usha.”
