The reason is that he is transferring something not yet in existence, like one who sells the fruit of a palm tree before it comes into existence, discussed in siman 209. The dissenting view holds that a lien is different and may attach even to property not yet in existence. It appears that this is the accepted law, for siman 113, seif 2, regarding slaves likewise indicates that the law of liens is different.
