Why should you trouble yourself? See Sema, seif katan 2, who refers to the rule that if a second tenant is willing to pay the first tenant more than the original rent, the landlord must allow the first tenant to receive that greater amount. The only basis for preventing the first tenant from placing another person in his position is the principle "do not withhold good," so where he can benefit from the substitution he should be allowed to do so. The proof from siman 363 is straightforward.
