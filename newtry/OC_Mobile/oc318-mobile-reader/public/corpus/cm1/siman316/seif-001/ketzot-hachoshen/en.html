If the tenant wishes to leave the house. If the landlord cannot rent it to someone else, the tenant must pay the full rent. He cannot deduct an idle-worker amount merely because he voluntarily prefers to leave. Such deductions apply when performance became impossible through an unavoidable event, such as the tenant's death or comparable cases. If the tenant can continue and simply does not wish to, he must pay the full agreed rent. The same is true of workers where the employer can still provide the work. Even buying or building another house is not sufficient compulsion. The Shulchan Aruch's rule that one who leaves without notice must pay rent therefore means full rent.
