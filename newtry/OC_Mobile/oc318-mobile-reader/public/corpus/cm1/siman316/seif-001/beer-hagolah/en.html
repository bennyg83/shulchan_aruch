The wording of Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 5, 'from here I say,' etc. The Maggid Mishneh writes that its proof is beside it from the law of the ship in Bava Metzia 79b, as stated above in siman 311, seif 6. They said that there is cause for complaint only regarding a ship because the shipowner must live together with the renter and tolerate his character. With a house, there is not even a valid complaint. His reasoning is correct.
(b) And he shall be given to his servants
The question was there that the drunken gift with the renter who if he wanted a generation after the rental time until 10 years could not be removed if not for himself and his then apartment could be removed and so on, and he said that the power to remove him could not be recognized and so on
The Beit Yosef writes likewise in siman 315 and here in the name of a responsum of Rashba. They explain that although a borrower may not lend the borrowed object to another, that rule concerns movable property, while land may be transferred for use. In my view this concerns a house and courtyard given to him for his own separate residence. If he was to live together with the owner, the owner can certainly object to an additional occupant, as explained in the next seif. I saw that the Sema discusses this at length, contrary to a responsum of Rama cited by Maharshal in responsa siman 17.
