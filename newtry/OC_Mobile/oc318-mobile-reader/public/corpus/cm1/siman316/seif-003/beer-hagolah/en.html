He explains that because the document states 'to anyone he wishes,' this includes even a large group. One should not say the phrase was inserted merely to beautify the document, because wherever a legal inference can be drawn from a document, we draw it. Furthermore, he expressly allowed him and his company to live there, and that company could be large.
