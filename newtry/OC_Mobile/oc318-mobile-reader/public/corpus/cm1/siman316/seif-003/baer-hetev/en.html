Permitted. The Sema writes that this is learned from the Rashba's responsum: even if the mortgagor dies and leaves minor children, so that the mortgagee cannot sue them while they are minors, nevertheless the mortgagee's existing right is not removed. From the moment the property was mortgaged to him, he had the right to rent it out immediately, even during the term. It is therefore as though the mortgagor expressly promised that when the term ends he may rent it even to a household larger than the mortgagor's own. This right is regarded as vested in him during the mortgagor's lifetime and is not displaced by the latter's death. Sema.
