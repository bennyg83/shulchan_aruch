[Shulchan Aruch] If you do not wish to remain in it, leave. Note: This implies that the landlord has no right of preemption as an adjoining owner. See above, siman 173, or 175, seif 47 in the gloss. This can be rejected, since the practical difference may concern a case where he wishes to rent it to another person or to a minor, and also in light of the Nimukei Yosef, who writes that Rambam too agrees with Rabbeinu Tam that the law of an adjoining owner does not apply to a house.
