The wording of Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilkhot Gezeilah, law 15, explicit in the Mishnah in Bava Batra chapter 5, 87b, following the Sages, as Rava says: I and the lion of our group explained it, etc. The Maggid Mishneh writes that a slave has the same law as a minor until the item is returned to the owners. This is clear.
