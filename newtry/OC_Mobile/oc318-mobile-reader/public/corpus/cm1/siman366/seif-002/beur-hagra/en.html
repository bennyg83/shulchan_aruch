One who comes, etc. This is Rosh's second answer, although Tosafot rejects it. Bach explains that Rosh requires both conditions, which avoids Tosafot's objection, and this is the position of Tur and Shulchan Aruch.
(Supplement) Whose dealings, etc.; one who comes, etc. These are Rosh's two answers. Nimukei Yosef explains that both must apply together, for otherwise every robber would return the stolen property merely to prevent people from refusing to accept from him.
(Supplement) One who comes, etc. Rambam records only this condition and does not mention a notorious robber.
