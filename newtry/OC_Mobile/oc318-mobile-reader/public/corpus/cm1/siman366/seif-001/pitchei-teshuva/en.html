And his repentance is difficult. See Baer Heitev, who explains that he also does not know to whom he must return the money. His intent is Tosafos, Bava Batra 88b, s.v. hasam, who write that where one robbed the public and does not know to whom to return, although Bava Kamma 94 says that he should use the money for public needs, this is still not a complete form of repentance. Sema above, siman 231, seif katan 34, says likewise. Responsa Shivat Tzion, end of siman 112, writes that Sema's wording is imprecise, but he apparently overlooked these Tosafos and Sema in siman 231. Responsa Mayim Chaim, end of siman 26, already raised this objection against him.
