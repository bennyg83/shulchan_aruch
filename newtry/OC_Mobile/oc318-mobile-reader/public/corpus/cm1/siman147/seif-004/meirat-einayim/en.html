He was a judge and caused the property to be collected, etc. The Rivash writes that an act of beit din is not mere speech about which we could say that a person might happen to say something inaccurately, as the Gemara says concerning a witness. It is a substantial judicial act. Even if the judges did not themselves sign the collection document but merely instructed witnesses to sign it, their agents are treated as themselves.
