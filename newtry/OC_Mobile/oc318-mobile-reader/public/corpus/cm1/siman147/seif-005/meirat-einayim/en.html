Shimon came and consulted Levi, etc. The Mechaber follows here the Rambam, who places these laws together in chapter 15 of Hilchot To'en. Above, in siman 146:17, the Mechaber followed the Tur's wording and therefore stated the same rule there as well. For this reason here he writes, "There is one who says, specifically where he admitted," while in siman 146:17 he states the rule without qualification. Since this particular qualification is not explicit in the Rambam, he introduces it here as an individual opinion.
