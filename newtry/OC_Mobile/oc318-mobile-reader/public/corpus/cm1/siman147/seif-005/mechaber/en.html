If Shimon consults Levi and says, 'I intend to purchase a specified field from Reuven, and I will buy it on your advice,' and Levi replies, 'Go purchase it; it is good,' Levi may later protest ownership of the field and does not lose his right, because he performed no act. Some say this applies only when the protesting claimant admits that he gave this advice. If he denies it and says, 'This never occurred,' and the purchaser brings witnesses proving that it did, the claimant is bound by the advice.
