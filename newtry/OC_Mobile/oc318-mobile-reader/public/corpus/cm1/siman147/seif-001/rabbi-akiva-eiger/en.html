[Shulchan Aruch] And Levi was one of the witnesses to the deed. Note: This applies specifically where the challenger himself is one of the witnesses to the deed. But if other witnesses signed the deed by which Reuven sold his field to Levi, this does not help, because witnesses are not required to know that the land actually belonged to Reuven. So writes Rashba in Responsa, siman 961, cited briefly by Beit Yosef here.
