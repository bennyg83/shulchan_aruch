And we disregard proofs that he brings. Tumim asks that if he brings witnesses proving that the other party robbed him, why should they not help? An admission to a robber is ineffective. This is not difficult. Mechaber may mean only proofs that the property originally belonged to him. In that case, so long as he does not bring actual witnesses to the robbery, his signed conduct can be treated as an admission defeating his present claim of robbery. Alternatively, even actual witnesses to the robbery may not defeat the admission created by his signing as a witness, because when asked merely to sign as a witness he could have evaded the robber. His voluntary signature therefore remains a strong admission.
