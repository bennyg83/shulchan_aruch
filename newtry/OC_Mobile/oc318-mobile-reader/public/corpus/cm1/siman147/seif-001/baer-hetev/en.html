You testified. This applies specifically where the objector claims that the field was stolen from him. A creditor, however, who signed as a witness when the debtor sold the field to another may later collect it from the purchaser, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write in siman 111. Since the creditor has only a lien upon the field, there was no impropriety in his signing the sale originally. See there, and siman 129, seif 8, in the gloss. Sema.
