How can you testify and then come and challenge? This applies specifically where Levi, the challenger, performed an affirmative act, such as signing the document or giving testimony orally before beit din. It does not apply merely because he advised Shimon, the possessor, to purchase the property, as the Tur and Mechaber state in siman 146:17 and again at the end of this siman. It also applies specifically where the challenger claims that the field was stolen from him. A creditor who signed as a witness when the borrower sold the field may later collect it from the purchaser, as the Tur and Mechaber state in siman 111, because he had only a lien upon the borrower's field and his earlier signature was therefore proper. See also siman 129 in Rama's gloss, and see the Derishah and Perishah.
