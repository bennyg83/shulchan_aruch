And he made the field a boundary marker for another property, etc. This applies specifically when he described it as another person's boundary. If he used it as a boundary marker for property he himself was purchasing, he did not forfeit his rights, because he may say, 'Had I not done so, the seller would not have sold to me.' This is stated in the Gemara and ruled by the Rif and Rosh. It is also cited by the Beit Yosef and Sema. See Responsa Maharam Padua, siman 46. Citation references: 39.
