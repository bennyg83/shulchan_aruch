There, law 2. Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara on 32b records Rabbi Yochanan's statement, "If he claims," etc. Rambam holds that even though the entire field is in the possessor's hands, the witness may claim that he plowed only one furrow. This is the principal ruling, and Ramban agrees. These laws apply where the challenger has witnesses that the land belonged to him or his ancestors, while the possessor lacks witnesses establishing chazakah. This is straightforward and is the commentators' necessary explanation.
Maggid Mishneh writes likewise there in the name of Ramban. Tur writes in seif 4 in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah. See Responsa Rivash, siman 252.
