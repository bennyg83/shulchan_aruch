See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, siman 105, who questions Rama for adopting Nimukei Yosef broadly, as though in all cases of damages testimony cannot be accepted outside the presence of the opposing party even in circumstances of necessity. Rabbi Akiva Eiger writes that Nimukei Yosef was speaking specifically about formally warning an ox, where the Torah emphasizes that its owner be warned. But with respect to payment for damage the ox has already caused, the matter is like ordinary testimony. This is explicit in Rashba's novellae at the end of Bava Kamma. Rabbi Akiva Eiger therefore leaves Rama's general formulation requiring study. See there.
