Only through clear proof. This is the language of the Mishnah. Rambam, in chapter 8 of Hilchot Nizkei Mamon, concludes and the Tur cites him: Do not think that because usually only shepherds, slaves, and similar people are present in horse stables and sheepfolds, if they testify that this ox gored or caused damage to a person their testimony is accepted. Rather, the rule is that money is imposed only on the basis of valid witnesses, who testify before beit din, and beit din then obligates the damager to pay. See Derishah, where I cited Terumat HaDeshen concerning this. Rama cites it above in the gloss at the end of siman 35, that by communal enactment such people may be accepted as witnesses. The Tur further cites Rabbeinu Yitzchak that the Mishnah also teaches that even when a non-Jew's ox gores a Jew's ox, the matter is adjudicated only through valid witnesses.
