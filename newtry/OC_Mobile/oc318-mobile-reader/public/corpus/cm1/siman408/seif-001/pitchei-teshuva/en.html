See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, siman 105, who expresses surprise at the Rama for citing Nimukei Yosef without qualification as though in every case of damages testimony may not be accepted outside the litigant's presence even in a situation of unavoidable necessity. Rabbi Akiva Eiger writes that it appears clear that Nimukei Yosef was discussing only the law of establishing an ox as forewarned, because there the verse specifically requires that warning be given "to its owner." But with respect to payment for damage the ox has already caused, the matter is like any other testimony and may be treated under the ordinary rules.
