The Tur cites the Rambam: One might think that because only shepherds, servants, and similar people are commonly present in horse stables and sheepfolds, if they testify that a particular ox gored or injured a person we listen to them. Therefore the law teaches that this is not so. Monetary liability is imposed only through valid witnesses who testify before beit din, and beit din then obligates the tortfeasor to pay. See the end of siman 35 in the gloss, where such people may testify by force of communal enactment. The Tur further cites Rabbeinu Yitzchak that the law teaches that even where a non-Jew's ox gored a Jew's ox, the case is adjudicated only through valid witnesses. Sema.
