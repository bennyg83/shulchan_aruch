Seif 6: “Beit din rules in monetary cases” The Sema says capital cases require the defendant’s presence because otherwise the wrong cannot be repaired. Rashba states instead that this is a scriptural decree, “until he stands before the congregation.” In monetary cases, parties may send written claims to any beit din and receive a ruling. Siman 13, which says the first beit din should write rather than another court rule, concerns claims presented before that first court; the verse requires the court before whom the parties stood to decide. Perhaps this is also the Sema’s intent.
