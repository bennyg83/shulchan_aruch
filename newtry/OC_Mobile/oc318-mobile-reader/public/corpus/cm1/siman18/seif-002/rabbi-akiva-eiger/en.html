[Sema, letter 9] The two who constitute the majority. Note: Those who say, "I do not know," are regarded as though they are not present. But according to the Rambam, who holds that they are regarded as present, Maharshal writes, as the Sema cites in his name in seif katan 6, that in such a case additional judges must be added, because there is no majority opposed to those who say, "I do not know."
