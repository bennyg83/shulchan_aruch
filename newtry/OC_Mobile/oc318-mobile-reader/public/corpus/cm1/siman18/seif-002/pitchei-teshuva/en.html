It is proper to begin with the junior judge. See Sema, seif katan 2, and Tosafot Yom Tov to Sanhedrin chapter 4, mishna 2, as well as Birkei Yosef, letter 2. Tumim writes that Rama means the court should consistently begin with the junior judge. To do so only occasionally is forbidden, because it is demeaning to the senior judges present.
