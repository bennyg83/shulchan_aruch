If the judges are evenly divided and one says, 'I do not know,' two more judges are added. Likewise, whenever the matter remains uncertain, judges continue to be added up to a maximum of seventy-one. If the panel reaches seventy-one, with thirty-five ruling in favor, thirty-five ruling against, and one saying, 'I do not know,' they deliberate with that judge until he joins one side, producing thirty-six judges who either exonerate or impose liability. If neither he nor any other judge changes his view, the money remains in doubt and is left in the possession of its current owner. See also siman 140.
