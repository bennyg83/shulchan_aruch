(Collected note) A beit din, etc. The statement in chapter 2 of Sanhedrin, "let the owner of the ox come, etc.," was said only because that case resembles a capital case, since we compare the death of the ox, etc. In Bava Kamma 112b it states, "and if he says, take, etc.; if he does not come, etc." There too, regarding a minor who seized, etc., even where he has a chazakah, etc., the restriction is specifically because testimony is not accepted in his absence, etc. (End of collected note.)
