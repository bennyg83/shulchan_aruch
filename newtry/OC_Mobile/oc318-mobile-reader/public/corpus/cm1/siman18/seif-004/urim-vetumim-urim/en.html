And he removed himself from them. See Tumim, where I conclude that even if the judges already knew which way the law inclined before the withdrawal, the remaining panel may still require replacement or renewed consideration, contrary to what appears from Shach.
The same applies if one was originally selected and then withdrew. Although Rama above follows Rambam that if one judge merely says "I do not know" the decision can sometimes be rendered by the majority, withdrawal is different. Had the withdrawing judge remained, he might have offered a reason that persuaded all the others to reverse. A judge who says only "I do not know" offers no such reasoning and remains present to hear the discussion. Therefore the panel cannot simply treat withdrawal like an uncertain vote. Sema.
