[Sema, continuation] Who selected four or five. Note: I do not understand his intent. It is clear that the Rama rules in accordance with the law at the end of siman 13, that if one says, "I do not know," the remaining judges can decide. Nevertheless, that is specifically where they stipulated that the ruling should follow the majority. If, however, they simply selected ten people to arbitrate between them, all must agree to one view. Accordingly, here too, where a community selected judges, all must agree to one view, and we do not follow a majority. If they explicitly stipulated that the majority should govern, then if one says, "I do not know," the others can decide. The law is the same whether a community selected them or the litigants selected them, and this is clear.
