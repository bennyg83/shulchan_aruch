And one withdrew. Compare the end of siman 13, where the Mechaber treats one judge saying "I do not know" like one who completely withdrew and rules that in either case the remaining nine cannot decide. This is from Rashba. Rama, however, does not treat them alike. Earlier in this siman he ruled against Rashba regarding one who says "I do not know," but here he follows Rashba regarding actual withdrawal. Perhaps Rama holds that withdrawal is different because we can say that had the absent judge remained he might have offered a reason that persuaded the nine. One who says "I do not know," by contrast, was present with the nine and had no reason to offer. Therefore, once the others are more than the three required for a beit din and share one view, majority can govern.
