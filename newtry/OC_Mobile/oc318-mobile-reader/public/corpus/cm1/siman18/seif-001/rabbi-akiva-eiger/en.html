[Shulchan Aruch] They shall add two judges. Note: See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 1, siman 138, who writes that if a judge improperly joined two unqualified lay judges, and he sees that they wish to corrupt the judgment, he may formulate his response by saying, "I do not know," so that two judges will be added. In Responsa Chavot Yair, siman 147, p. 135b, he writes: "If the lone judge says, even if he had already stated his opinion to his colleagues who then outvoted him, that he reconsidered their words and is now uncertain about the law, he may compel them to add judges, just as when one judge originally says, 'I do not know.' Although the conduct is unsightly, and concerning this it is said, 'You shall fear your God,' nevertheless the other two cannot complete the judgment." See there.
