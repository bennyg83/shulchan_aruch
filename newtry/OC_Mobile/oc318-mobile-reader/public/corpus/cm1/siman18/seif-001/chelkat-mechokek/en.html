Kiddushin take effect with those forbidden by a negative commandment. Above, siman 15, it was explained that kiddushin do not take effect with Torah ervayot carrying karet. Here the Mechaber teaches that with those forbidden by a negative commandment or a positive commandment, kiddushin do take effect, for they are not included in the category of ervah. The law of a yevamah marrying an outsider will be explained below in siman 159, where kiddushin take effect in her only out of doubt, unlike Rav's view.
