They remove everyone outside. Bach explains that the litigants themselves are removed, just as the witnesses are, but other persons who are neither litigants nor witnesses need not be removed. On the contrary, a worthy student should be allowed to remain; only an ignorant student should not sit before the judges, as above in siman 9. Responsa Beit Yaakov, siman 67, agrees. Shevut Yaakov, volume 1, siman 141, however, understands Rambam, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch literally: everyone is removed, even those who are neither litigants nor witnesses, so that there be no concern of gossip. Those attached to the judges, such as one of their students, need not leave unless he is an ignorant student. See above, siman 7, seif 8, seif katan 19.
