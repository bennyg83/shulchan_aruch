Seif 1 “But four say,” etc. The Sema cites Maharshal, but once the two judges who say “I do not know” are removed, a majority remains, two of the three remaining judges. It is as if only three sat and two agreed. Rashba’s responsum cited by the Sema explicitly supports this. The correct practical ruling is to follow the majority. It is surprising that the Sema cites both Maharshal and Rashba although they conflict.
