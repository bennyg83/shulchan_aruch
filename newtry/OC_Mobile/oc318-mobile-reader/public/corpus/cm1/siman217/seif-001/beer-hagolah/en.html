Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 21 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 7, from the statement of Rav Yehudah in the name of Shmuel, according to our version of the Gemara, Bava Batra 99b.
Bet) He repairs the banks from that land and sows the remainder. If the two cubits are exhausted, he takes additional land from the field. Rashbam.
Gimel) Rambam has the reading "channel," and this version also appears in Rashbam.
