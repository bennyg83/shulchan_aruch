The grave. Because the grave in which the deceased is buried is forbidden for benefit, the purchaser must therefore return its value to him. This is unlike the route to the grave and the place where the funeral assembly and eulogy are held. If the purchaser consents and says, 'Eulogize him there, and afterward I will take it back for my own use,' he cannot be displaced from it permanently. For this reason the text says only that they bury him there against the purchaser's will. Ibid.
