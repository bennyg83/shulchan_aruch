He was slicked by his husband. They believe that the family will not be buryed in place as their custom:
And they give him the value of the grave. Since a grave in which a dead person is buried becomes prohibited for benefit, they must reimburse its value. This differs from a path or the place where people stand for the eulogy. If the purchaser agrees and says, “Use it for the eulogy and afterward I will reclaim it for my own use,” they cannot remove him from ownership of it. For this reason the rule says only that they bury the dead there against his will, not that the other areas are permanently taken from him.
