For he accepted it on that understanding, etc. The Gemara asks why the field owner cannot say, “The water in your channel caused the banks to collapse.” It answers that ordinarily one who sells a place within his field for digging a water channel agrees from the outset that if the banks collapse, earth may be taken from his field to repair them. Otherwise, where would the purchaser obtain repair earth? Must he transport soil from another place? This is the Mechaber's ruling.
