Thus between each grave and the graves at its sides there is one and a half cubits. The diagram follows the Rambam's interpretation. Rashbam and the Tur understand the layout differently. In the Perishah I gave their explanation and diagram as well. The Rambam and Mechaber abbreviate the baraita, which says that one makes a cave four by six, etc., and a courtyard at its entrance six by six, and makes another cave like it on the opposite side of the courtyard. The Maggid Mishneh and Beit Yosef question why the Rambam omitted parts of this arrangement. In the Derishah I offered some resolution.
