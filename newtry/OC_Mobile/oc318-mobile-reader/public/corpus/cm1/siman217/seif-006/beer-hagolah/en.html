There, law 6, Mishnah there, 100b. Rambam rules like the Sages.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Mechaber's words require study, for he mentions only one burial cave. According to the end of the Mishnah, even according to the Sages one makes a courtyard six by six cubits and opens two caves into it, as the Rashbam explains. It appears that Rambam understands the latter clause as a case where one expressly undertook to make a courtyard, but this requires study. The Beit Yosef writes that Rambam held the latter clause applies only according to Rabbi Shimon. I do not understand this either, for Rabbi Shimon disputes the latter clause as well.
