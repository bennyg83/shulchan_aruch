(Shach, seif katan 3) We do not say migo against a presumption, even against a presumption of monetary possession. Note: This wording is awkward. It should have said: we do not say migo against a presumption even though a presumption of monetary possession accompanies the migo. This is also proven from siman 79. But what he further cites from siman 99 is not understood, because there the migo stands against the presumption that whatever is in a person's possession is his.
