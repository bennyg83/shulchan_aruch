To him. A stringent oath cannot be reversed, just as a Torah oath cannot be reversed. The Tur writes this here. In siman 87 he gives another explanation and distinguishes between one who swears and collects and one who swears and is exempt, such as partners or sharecroppers who wish to reverse the oath and say, "Let the other party take the stringent oath that he suspects me and then pay me." Such a reversal may be allowed there. Sema.
