The Rosh at the beginning of chapter Kol HaNishba'in writes that in these two matters the Mishnah's oath has the characteristics of a Torah oath: it cannot be reversed and must be taken while holding a sacred object. So too writes the Tur here. The Rif and Rosh in chapter HaKosev write that an oath is reversed only from a defendant to a claimant. So too Rambam and the Tur in siman 87. See the Shulchan Aruch there, seif 11.
