An accommodation document. The Shach writes that it is obvious from the Gemara that an accommodation document is treated like a paid document for this purpose and that no authority disagrees. Yet Responsa Maimoniyot, Hilchot Mishpatim, responsum 9, rules that a borrower who says the document was an accommodation is not believed even where the signatures cannot otherwise be validated. His reason is the same used against the witnesses themselves: people do not normally sign an improper instrument. He applies Rav Ashi's principle not only to witnesses but also to the borrower. Thus there is indeed an authority who disagrees with the Shach's assertion that the law is uncontested. See what I wrote above in seif katan 1.
