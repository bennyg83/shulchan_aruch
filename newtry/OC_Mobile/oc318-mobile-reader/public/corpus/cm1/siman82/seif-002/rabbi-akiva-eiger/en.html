[Shach, letter 6] Rather, it concerns a case where he produced witnesses that he paid. Note: According to this, one may say that the witnesses are effective because a claim that the document is forged is not included in the rule that a claim of “I never borrowed” constitutes an admission that he did not repay. Nevertheless, it may still be that he is established as a denier with respect to that money, since he claimed that the document was forged and it was then authenticated. Accordingly, if there are no witnesses that he repaid, he would not be believed to say, “Swear to me that I did not repay you.” This resolves the Shach’s difficulty in seif 9, seif katan 11.
