I swear by oath. We did not command a father and we did not find his notes that this bill was wild:
I swear to him, and he will be sent out to him, and he will also be appointed as "the columnist of the C.C.," and the lawyer named the Rambam and the Ramban and the Ramban and the Rebbe," and he will not be able to take any oath, but that he will not lose this week
And this is the more reasonable view. The Tur also decides this way. See siman 92, where the Tur and Mechaber cite the dispute without an express ruling, but place this view last. I explained in Derishah how to reconcile their ruling here, that the claimant collects without an oath, with Even HaEzer siman 96:12, where regarding a widow who is disqualified from swearing, the opposing party swears and is exempt. The distinction is that a widow may already possess her husband's property, and there is concern that she took from his assets or that he gave her money in advance. That concern does not apply to ordinary documents. I also reconciled the Rif's seemingly conflicting rulings there.
