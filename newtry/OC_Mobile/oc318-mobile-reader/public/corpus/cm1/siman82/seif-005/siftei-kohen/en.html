If not, we do not introduce the claim. The Sema cites Nimukei Yosef that for heirs beit din raises the defense and that one may not collect from heirs on an unauthenticated instrument. This does not merely mean that beit din raises payment for heirs, which is an explicit Mishnah and is obvious from siman 108. Rather, although beit din ordinarily does not invent defenses, for heirs it raises forgery. Even where the instrument contains a credibility clause, collection may not be made until the instrument is authenticated. Nimukei Yosef and the other authorities agree, as I write below in siman 106.
