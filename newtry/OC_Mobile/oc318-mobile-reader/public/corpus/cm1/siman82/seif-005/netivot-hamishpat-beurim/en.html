This rule. This is explained by Shach. If the party has the presumption of original ownership supporting him, we do say a migo even to extract. Its source is Tosafot in Bava Batra 35, s.v. ve'i deleit lei, who ask regarding the rule that if one says, "I allowed him to descend to the property for its produce," he is believed, why the challenger should not be believed by a migo that he could have claimed, "I allowed him to descend for its produce." This would be so even though he thereby extracts the land. It follows that where the presumption of original ownership supports the migo, we say the migo even to extract land.
