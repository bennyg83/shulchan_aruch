No. Nimukei Yosef writes that for orphans beit din does advance the claim, and one may not collect from them except with an authenticated document. The Sema explains that this does not mean we claim simply that the document was paid, because the Mishnah already teaches that one may collect from orphans only with an oath, as stated throughout siman 108. Rather, although beit din does not generally open claims for an adult borrower, for orphans it does raise the possibility of forgery. Therefore, even if the document contains ne'emanut, the lender cannot collect until it is authenticated. This is the consensus of the authorities. See siman 106.
