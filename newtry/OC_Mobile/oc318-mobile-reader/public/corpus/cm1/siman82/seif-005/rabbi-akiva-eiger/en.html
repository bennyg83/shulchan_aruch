[Shach, letter 18] And he says, “Swear to me.” Note: The borrower cannot say, “I hold in accordance with Rav Sherira Gaon, who rules that in such a case the borrower takes an oath and is exempt.” In Responsa Ba’ei Chayei of Knesset HaGedolah, Choshen Mishpat, siman 10, he writes that from the words of Rashdam, Choshen Mishpat, siman 294, it appears that the borrower may say “I hold” in accordance with that view. See Knesset HaGedolah on the Tur, letter 24.
