Seif 11. In the gloss: 'Even though the document contains a clause of credibility, because that credibility applies only to payment,' etc. Note: This makes the Shach's words in siman 75 difficult. He writes that one who claims, 'You waived the debt to me,' is believed only through a migo that he could have claimed, 'I paid.' Yet even where the document contains a credibility clause and he could not impose an oath by claiming payment, the lender still must swear regarding the claim of waiver. This requires study.
