And if he says, 'Swear to me.' This too supports what I wrote above: the advantage of a Torah scholar lender is that the document is not removed from him and he may seize whenever he can. Therefore, if the borrower is also a Torah scholar, we hear his demand that the lender swear, so that the lender will not remain able to seize at any time. Otherwise, even where both are Torah scholars, we should simply withdraw from adjudicating and leave the property with its possessor until the matter is clarified.
