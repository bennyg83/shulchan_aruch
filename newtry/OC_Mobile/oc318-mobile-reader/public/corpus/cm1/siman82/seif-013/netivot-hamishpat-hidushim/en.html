He said to him. If he has claimed a note that he is wild and brings evidence that he has not written in his account of his property against the tax he has condemned and ruled that this is not true:
And witnesses testify. The case is where the witnesses did not testify until after he had already admitted half. If they had testified first, his later admission would be like returning a lost item and he would be exempt without an oath.
From unencumbered property. Purchasers can say, "We relied upon the witnesses." For the rationale of this seif, see siman 30. Also see Shach, seif katan 10, where there is a scribal error. The correct text is: if after the document was validated he says that he paid afterward, he is believed when he brings witnesses. In seif katan 9 the correct text is: the lender responds, "I do not know," and the borrower comes with a certain claim; the word "because" is a scribal error.
