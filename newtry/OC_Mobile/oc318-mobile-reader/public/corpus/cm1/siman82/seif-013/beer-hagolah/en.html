Send me R. Daddy and so on
So write the Rif and Rambam in chapter 14 of Hilkhot Malveh and the Tur. He is not treated as one returning a lost item, because fear of the document compels him. The Beit Yosef writes that they apparently explain this as the Gemara's statement, 'Where there are witnesses, he is afraid.' Nimukei Yosef writes that we judge it as though there were neither witnesses nor a document, so one who admits part of the claim must swear.
