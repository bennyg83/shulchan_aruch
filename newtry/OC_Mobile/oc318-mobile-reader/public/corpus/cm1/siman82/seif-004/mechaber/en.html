If the borrower has been established as a denier with respect to that document, such as where he said, 'This never occurred,' witnesses came, the document was authenticated, and beit din obligated him to pay, he can no longer say, 'Swear to me that I did not repay you,' even if the document contains no clause granting the lender credibility. He already admitted that he did not pay, because anyone who says, 'I did not borrow,' is regarded as saying, 'I did not repay.' If he imposes an unspecified ban upon anyone who took his money unlawfully, however, we do not prevent him.
