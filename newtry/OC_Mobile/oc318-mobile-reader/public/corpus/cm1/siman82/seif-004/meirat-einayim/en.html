He cannot say, 'Swear to me,' etc. In siman 79:1 the Tur and Mechaber explain that even if the witnesses who authenticate the document say that he borrowed and repaid, he must nevertheless pay, because one who says, 'I never borrowed,' is treated as having said, 'I never repaid.' Once he has been held liable by beit din, he is also no longer believed to say later that he repaid. They repeat the law here to teach that the same applies when the original claim was based on a document. We might otherwise have thought that when he said, 'It never happened,' he meant only that he never authorized witnesses to write a document against him, not that he denied the underlying loan. The ruling teaches otherwise.
