(Mechaber, seif 6) If the lender was a Torah scholar. Note: See Knesset Yechezkel, siman 95. He concludes: "May it be credited to me that in all my days I never ruled that a Torah scholar who denies the entire claim must take an oath; I required only acceptance of a ban. So is the practice throughout the lands of Poland, that with a rabbi or Torah scholar in a case of shevuat heset we rule that he accept the ban privately and respectfully before agents of beit din." He adds that especially today there are not many who show affection to Torah scholars and rabbis; people cast their eyes upon their money, seek to strip them of their garments, and know that Torah scholars will not degrade themselves and will therefore be forced into compromise. It is therefore incumbent upon every beit din to uphold the positive commandment of honoring Torah, "You shall fear Hashem your God." See there.
