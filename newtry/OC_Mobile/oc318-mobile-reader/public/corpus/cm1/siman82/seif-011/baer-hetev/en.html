A trust document. The Sema writes that the reason is that an ordinary promissory note is not presumed to have been waived, so the defendant's claim is weak. See siman 126, seif 11; Responsa Maharlbach, siman 109; Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 32; Responsa Maharanach, siman 21; Responsa Torat Emet, siman 76. The Taz writes that even an explicit trust clause concerning a claim of waiver is ineffective, unlike the Sema in the name of Maharshal; see there. See also Responsa Divrei Rivot, siman 211; Mahari ibn Lev, part 1, siman 100; R. Moshe Galante, siman 91; and R. Avraham ben Chaim, part 2, siman 3.
