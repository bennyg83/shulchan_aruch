(Seif 13) “The borrower claimed.” The Sema cites the Beit Yosef in siman 75 in the name of the Rashba regarding one who says, “Your note has been paid, and the proof is that this debt was not listed in the inventory of your assets when you swore to disclose all your property for taxation.” The Rashba ruled for the noteholder, because he may simply have forgotten to include it in the inventory. This is difficult, for in siman 81, seif 28, the Mechaber rules that one is not heard to say, “I forgot and therefore swore falsely,” because a person is presumed to be exacting when taking an oath. The answer is that where the particular matter was expressly raised and he swore about it, as in the earlier case, it certainly occupied his attention, and the severity of the oath would cause him to remember accurately. Here, however, this debt was not mentioned at all; it was merely one item among all his assets. Such an omission is common, and the oath does not overcome the possibility of forgetfulness.
