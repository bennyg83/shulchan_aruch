But if he seized... This is how the ruling is understood from the statement there, "we do not attend to him." Although the Rif in chapter HaKosev writes that the same applies to any person, that we do not remove property from him, nevertheless we place him under ban, as the Rosh writes in the incident of Rabbah bar Sharshom: we learn from this that where one took property without, etc., nevertheless, etc. See Ran in chapter HaDayanim 41a, who explains the Rif in this manner. This must be the interpretation, because the Rif in chapter HaKosev derives his rule from Shevuos 41a, where the difference concerns descending to his property, etc. The same applies here, and there the Gemara says that according to the Sages, etc., we place him under ban, etc.
