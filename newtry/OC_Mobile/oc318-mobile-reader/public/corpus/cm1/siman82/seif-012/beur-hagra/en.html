And some disagree, etc. Their proof is from Ketubot 12b and 16a, where Rabban Gamliel believes her because of a migo. Tosafot explain the Bava Batra passage accordingly. The first opinion holds that the migo in Ketubot is effective because the claimant is certain and the other party uncertain. One cannot explain it merely from her possession of the ketubah, for once the husband raises a valid virginity claim the document is treated as absent, just as with a condition. Ramban further proves the point from Bava Metzia 110a, where the lender is believed by migo although land remains in its owner's possession.
