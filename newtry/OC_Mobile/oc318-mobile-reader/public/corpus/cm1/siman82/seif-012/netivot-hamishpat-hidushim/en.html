Even if the lender admits that initially it was an accommodation document, but later he actually lent the money on the date stated in it, since he claims that the document is now valid, he is believed:
That it was made subject to a condition. This refers to a condition requiring the holder of the document to perform an affirmative act. In such a case the borrower is believed to say that the holder did not fulfill the condition.
