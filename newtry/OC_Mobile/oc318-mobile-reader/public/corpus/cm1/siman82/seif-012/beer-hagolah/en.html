In the passage s.v. 'Why did you rely,' etc., 32b, and similarly in many places. The Bach writes that the Rosh in Chezkas HaBatim, Tosafot in many places, Mordechai, and the glosses hold that where a chazakah or documentary proof supports the claimant and the opposing party comes to disqualify it, as in the incident 'Before me you sold it to him,' Bava Batra 36a, or where the claimant makes a definite claim and the defendant an uncertain one, we say migo even to extract money. This is the accepted ruling.
