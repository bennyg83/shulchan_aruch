A gift. In siman 303, the Beit Yosef writes regarding this that the matter requires consideration. The Rama there, seif 1, likewise introduces the ruling as "some say." It appears to me that receiving such a gift does not make him a paid guardian unless there is an indication that the gift was given because of the agency, for example, where the giver is not accustomed to giving him gifts, or even if he is accustomed, there is some indication that this gift was connected to the agency. Hagahot Mordechai derives the law also from interest, and there too this appears to be the rule. Taz.
