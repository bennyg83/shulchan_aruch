A broker who sells for less or more than the owner's instruction, and other claims between broker and owner. It contains ten seifim.
A broker is an agent who receives payment for his agency. Therefore, if he departs from the owner's instructions, he must pay the resulting loss. Thus, if Reuven gave an item to the broker Shimon and said, 'Sell this for me, but not for less than one hundred,' and Shimon sold it for fifty, Shimon must pay the missing fifty from his own funds. If he sold it for two hundred, the entire amount belongs to Reuven. Rama: If one sends an agent to deliver an item somewhere, and during the mission the recipient gives the agent a gift, the agent has the status of a paid custodian.
