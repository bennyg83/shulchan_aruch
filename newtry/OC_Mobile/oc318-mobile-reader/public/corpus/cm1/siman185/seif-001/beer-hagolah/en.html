The language of Rambam, Hilkhot Sheluchin chapter 2, law 6, cited by Tur. Kesef Mishneh writes that it appears to be learned from the Mishnah, Ketubot 58a, concerning a widow whose ketubah is two hundred and who sold property worth one hundred, etc. The same principle applies to a broker who receives compensation, since she too benefits from the sale by collecting her ketubah. The Gemara there on 58b says that where the extra amount has no fixed measure, it belongs entirely to the owner of the money.
