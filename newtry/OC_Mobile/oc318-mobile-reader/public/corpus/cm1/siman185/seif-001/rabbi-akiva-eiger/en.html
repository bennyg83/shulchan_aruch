[Shach, letter 1] Even if the recipient appointed him as an agent. Note: I have not merited to understand this. There, it is clear that since the recipient had previously appointed him as an agent for receipt, when the giver says, "Give," the transfer takes effect by virtue of the agency for receipt, as the Sema wrote there, seif katan 23. This is unlike a case where the giver first says, "Give," and only afterward tells the recipient, "You shall be my agent to acquire it from your own hand." There it is uncertain whether the agency must first be established with the giver or not. Examine this carefully.
