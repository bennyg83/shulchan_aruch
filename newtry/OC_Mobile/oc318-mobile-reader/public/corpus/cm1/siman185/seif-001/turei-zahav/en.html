Seif 1: He sent him a gift. The Beit Yosef wrote elsewhere that this requires consideration. It appears that receiving a gift makes the agent a paid guardian only where there is evidence that the gift was given because of the agency, such as where the sender does not ordinarily give him gifts, or where the circumstances now indicate that purpose. The Hagahot Mordechai derives this from the laws of interest, and the same inference appears there.
