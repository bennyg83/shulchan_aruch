On condition that he be appeased. Maharshal writes in Yam Shel Shlomo, siman 39, that an ordinary matchmaking arrangement is treated as though it were expressly conditioned upon the parties being appeased for the purpose of the kinyan by which the penalty is undertaken. Nevertheless, if one party breaches the agreement and the other cannot recover either the penalty or his losses, the matchmaker must return to him his share of the matchmaking fee. See there further.
