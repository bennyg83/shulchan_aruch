Rashba writes in a responsum that the broker has the law of a paid bailee and is liable for any loss that is not a complete unavoidable accident, based on the end of HaPo'alim, where even if he entered the city at the time people normally enter, etc., and in accordance with Rav Chisda and Rabbah bar Rav Huna there on Bava Metzia 93b, from section 6.
There in the responsum, from HaMadir: ordinarily, a person does not hand over the item until he receives the money, from section 6.
See further laws of brokerage at the end of siman 87, the end of siman 264, and Even HaEzer siman 13. If Reuven promised Shimon a fixed fee to speak on his behalf to a government minister and Reuven later claims that he promised only because he feared Shimon would otherwise harm his interests, he must nevertheless keep his word even though no kinyan was made.
