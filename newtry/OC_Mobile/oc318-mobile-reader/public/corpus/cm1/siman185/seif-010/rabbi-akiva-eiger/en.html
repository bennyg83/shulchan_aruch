[Rama] The matchmaker. Note: If a broker told the seller or the buyer, "I will take nothing from you as brokerage for this sale," and afterward said that he had said this only to improve the other party's disposition toward him, his later statement is of no effect. Maharikash, and similarly Responsa Chut HaShani, siman 5. Reuven promised Shimon that he would exert himself on his behalf in a certain matter without payment. When the matter was close to completion, Reuven retracted and said, "I will not complete it unless you give me such and such." Shimon gave it to him and appeased him. Later, money belonging to Reuven came into Shimon's possession, and Shimon said, "You took my money unlawfully, for I was under duress when I appeased you." The law is in Reuven's favor. Beit Yosef, siman 333, in the name of Maharik, siman 133, as copied by the Sema at the end of siman 14, seif katan 4.
