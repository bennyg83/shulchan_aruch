Sema. The Shach writes that an ordinary matchmaking engagement is understood as though the matchmaker stipulated that his fee is earned once the parties reach the stage at which the penalty is formalized. Nevertheless, if one side breaches and the other cannot recover the penalty, the matchmaker must return the matchmaking fee. If both sides mutually agree to dissolve the engagement, the matchmaker does not lose his fee in a place where it is collected after the conditions are written. Taz. The Sema writes that hiring a worker requires no kinyan. Therefore, if one says to another, "Speak on my behalf to the ruler and I will give you two gold coins," he must pay and cannot later say that he promised only out of fear that the other would harm his interests before the ruler, because unexpressed intentions have no legal effect.
