He acquires the surplus. See Shach, seif katan 5, who writes that his status is like that of a paid guardian. Although in siman 186:2 the law in a comparable case is that he is liable even for unavoidable accidents, the cases differ. There he took the object as a purchase, so that if sellers became available he himself would be the purchaser and the object would be his from that moment. If the seller wished to retract, he could not do so, because the buyer had already acquired it through meshichah. He therefore has the status of a purchaser and is liable even for unavoidable accidents. Here, however, he received the property only in the capacity of an agent. If the principal wished to revoke the agency, he could do so. He therefore never acquires the status of a purchaser so as to become liable for unavoidable accidents.
