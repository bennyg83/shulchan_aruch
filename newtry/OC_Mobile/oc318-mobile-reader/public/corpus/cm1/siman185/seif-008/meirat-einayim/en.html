And he must swear how much it was worth, etc. There in Mordechai it is written that Reuven need not swear that the stone had been well set, because one does not swear in response to an uncertain claim.
And he must pay its value. The wording deliberately says "its value" and no more, even if it was collateral belonging to a non-Jew and the non-Jew had assessed it at a higher amount, as explained above in siman 72 and below at the end of siman 378. See also Even HaEzer siman 50 and the Sema concerning lost rings.
