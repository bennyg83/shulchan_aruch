If Reuven says, 'I instructed you to sell it for one hundred,' while the broker says, 'You told me fifty, and that is what I sold it for,' the broker must take a Torah oath, because he admits part of the claim. If he already paid the fifty, or says, 'Here it is,' he takes a hesset oath that he followed the agency, and the purchaser acquires. If the purchaser knew that the item belonged to Reuven and that the seller was only a broker, he must return it to the owner. He may then impose a ban upon whoever either authorized the broker to sell for fifty or accepted fifty and later withdrew after the purchase. Rama: Some say the purchaser need not return it even if the broker admits the owner's version.
