One who gives his utensils to a broker to pledge. If a broker pledged an item without identifying its owner and then died, and another person brings witnesses that it had belonged to him, including reliable clarification through a woman where the object is women's clothing, there is concern that the broker may have purchased it. Nevertheless, since the claimant has a definite claim and the opposing side is uncertain, together with the prior-owner presumption, and there is no competing presumption that what is in the broker's hand belongs to him because his established role is that of a broker, the item is returned to the person shown to have owned it.
