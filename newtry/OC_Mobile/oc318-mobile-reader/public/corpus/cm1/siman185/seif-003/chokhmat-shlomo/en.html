Seif 10. 'A broker who gave an article to someone for inspection.' Note: There is disagreement whether his liability is based on his status as a purchaser or as a borrower. See the Ran in the first chapter of Avodah Zarah, on 15a, in the discussion of an item taken for trial, the Rosh in Bava Batra 88a, and Nedarim 38a with the Ran and Rosh there. Their words appear contradictory. In my responsum to the community of Botoshan I discussed at length how to reconcile their positions, with God's help.
