This too is there. The law is in the Mishnah, Bava Batra 87a: if the barrel broke, the broker bears the loss. Tosafot there in the name of Rivam explains that both parties were acting as ordinary paid brokers who measure for compensation, and that the breakage was due not to unavoidable accident but to insufficient care.
