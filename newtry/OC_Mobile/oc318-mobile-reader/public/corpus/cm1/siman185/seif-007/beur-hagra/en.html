Even while going. Meaning, although he has not yet benefited, since an opportunity may arise for him, etc., as the commentators explain regarding money changers, etc. Since an opportunity may arise, etc., see Bava Metzia 43a.
(Supplement) Even while, etc. It is not only after he sold, when he has benefited, but even in this case. The reason is that an opportunity may arise, etc., as stated in Bava Metzia 43a: he said to him, 'In this case,' etc. See Baer HaGolah, seif katan 60. (End of supplement.)
