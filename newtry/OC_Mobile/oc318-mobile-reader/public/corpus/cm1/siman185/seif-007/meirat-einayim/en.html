Even on the outward journey he is liable, etc. The Mordechai writes that even though the article has not yet been sold and perhaps will never be sold so that the broker will earn no fee, he is nevertheless a paid custodian. The expression "even on the outward journey" means that this is all the more so on the return journey, for example after he already sold the item and the money is lost from his hand. Then he is plainly a paid custodian because the moment he sold it his compensation for his effort became fixed against the seller. This differs from one who takes an article from a craftsman in order to sell it, discussed in siman 186:2, who is liable even for unavoidable loss and even on the return journey, because there he is not treated as an agent but as a purchaser who took the article from the craftsman at a fixed price in order to profit from resale.
