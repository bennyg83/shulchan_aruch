Ran and Nimukei Yosef write in HaMafkid concerning the incident where jewels were deposited, Bava Metzia 35a.
Ran there writes that even according to a responsum attributed to Rif stating that if he said, "I pledged them with so-and-so," and that person denied it, this is negligence because he should have pledged them in the presence of witnesses, the attribution is not convincing to Ran, and he is uncertain whether Rif actually wrote it.
Rashba writes that the same applies if he told the broker to pledge the item with a gentile and the gentile later sold the collateral: the broker is exempt even though he received compensation. Sema.
