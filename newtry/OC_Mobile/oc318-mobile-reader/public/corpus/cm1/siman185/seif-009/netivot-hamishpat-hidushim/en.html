It is negligence. In siman 121 the Mechaber adds that if the intermediary says he pledged the item with a particular person and that person denies receiving it, the intermediary is liable because he should have pledged it before witnesses. The Sema writes that this detail was accidentally omitted here. The Shach says the Mechaber intentionally omitted it because he does not accept that ruling. See siman 121.
The broker is exempt. Since the owner himself instructed him to pledge it with that particular person. The same applies if he instructed him to pledge it with a gentile and the gentile later sold the collateral. Sema.
