Negligence. The Rama records this law in siman 121, seif 11, and adds there that if the agent says that he pledged the item with a certain person and that person denies it, the agent must pay, because he should have taken witnesses when he gave the pledge. Nimukei Yosef writes similarly. It appears that the scribe accidentally omitted this clause here. See also siman 291, seif 7. Sema. The Shach writes that, in his view, the Mechaber intentionally omitted it, because the Ran expresses doubt about this law, and in our view as well the ruling is not correct, as I wrote in siman 121. See there.
