The agent cannot purchase it for himself, etc. The Tur explains that a person cannot transfer property to himself. A sale requires removing the object from one legal domain and placing it in another, while the agent stands in the place of the owner. Therefore the property has not left the owner's domain. This is why the Mechaber concludes that if the broker says, etc., "and the seller agreed," meaning that unless the seller expressly agrees to convey it to the broker for himself, the property has not left the seller's legal domain, because the agent's hand is like the owner's hand.
