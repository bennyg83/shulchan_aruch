For himself. See Responsa Maharit, siman 93; Responsa Mahari ben Lev, volume 1, page 140; and Responsa HaGeonim, siman 384, which implies that he may retain it for himself. Shach. See Bnei Chayei to the Beit Yosef, gloss 5, which cites a lengthy responsum in the name of two great authorities. At the end it cites a responsum of Maharashach, who writes that it is clear from all the poskim that even if the seller specified the price at which the agent was to sell the item, the agent nevertheless may not buy it for himself. If he did buy it for himself and later sold it to another for more, or the item subsequently appreciated, all the profit belongs to the owners. See there.
