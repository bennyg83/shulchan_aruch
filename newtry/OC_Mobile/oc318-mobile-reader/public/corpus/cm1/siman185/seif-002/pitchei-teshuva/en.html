And if the purchaser knew. Responsa Masat Binyamin, in the novellae appended to the book, siman 7, cites a responsum of the Geonim, siman 152: Reuven gave merchandise to Shimon to sell. Shimon sold it without receiving payment, after informing the buyer that the merchandise belonged to Reuven. Shimon later collected the money from the buyer, or the buyer returned the merchandise to Shimon. Reuven then demanded that the buyer pay him the price of the merchandise. The buyer answered that he had already paid Shimon, and Reuven replied that the buyer knew the merchandise was Reuven's and therefore should not have paid Shimon. The Geonic responsum ruled for Reuven. This requires study. I later found in Responsa VeShav HaKohen 17 a very similar case in which he did not rule this way. It will be cited below, siman 291:26, seif katan 16.
