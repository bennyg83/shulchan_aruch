Whenever a broker informed the purchaser that the item or land belonged to Reuven, and after the purchase the owner says, 'I do not wish to sell for this price,' the purchaser must return it, because the owner never fixed a price with him and merely told the broker to sell for such-and-such an amount. Rama: Some say this applies only where the owner gave no instruction when entrusting the item. If he said, 'Sell it for whatever you can obtain,' the owner cannot retract, and the broker's act stands. If Reuven had a house to sell, Shimon brokered a sale to Levi, and Reuven said Levi was his enemy and he would not sell to him, but later sold to Levi through another broker, Reuven must pay Shimon his brokerage fee.
