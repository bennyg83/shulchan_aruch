Reuven. Rabbeinu Meir explains that the reason is that a broker receives compensation even though he was not sent, but came on his own initiative, like one who descends into another’s field and plants it. In Bava Metzia 101a they say, “It was stated: one who descends, etc.” They dispute only there, etc. Likewise with a broker, since the item is meant to be sold. There, in the case of that person, etc., he said to him, “I do not want it, etc.” He replied, “You have revealed your intent, etc.”
