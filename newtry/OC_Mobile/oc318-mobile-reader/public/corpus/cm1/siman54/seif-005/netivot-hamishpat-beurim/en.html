Unspecified dinars. See Urim VeTumim, who cites Responsa Rosh concerning a borrower's signed writing acknowledging receipt of interest in a manner requiring deduction of fixed prohibited interest from the debt document, although the exact amount is unknown. Rosh rules that the lender swears as to the amount. Urim VeTumim explains that this differs from a receipt stating merely 'dinars,' which was written for the purpose of cancelling or reducing the document itself. Here the interest claim is treated as a separate counterclaim, analogous to two documents where each party collects on his own right.
