It cancels every deed that he has against him. The Ramban explains that the holder of the instrument bears the disadvantage of uncertain wording. Perhaps the witnesses did not know the amount and therefore wrote generally that any deed produced should be impaired. Since money cannot be extracted in doubt, every deed is canceled to the extent stated. If the lender seized the amount exceeding two dinars, the seizure is effective. The Rashba likewise rules that the deed remains with the lender and the money remains where it is until the facts become known, and that property already seized is not removed. I reached the same conclusion in Tekfo Kohen, siman 55.
