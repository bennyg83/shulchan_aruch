There, and likewise Baal HaTerumot, gate 53. Although with a loan document we say that an unspecified number of dinars means two, as explained above in siman 42, a receipt is different. There, since the witnesses know that the holder of the document has the weaker hand, if there were more than two they would have written the amount explicitly. But with a receipt, perhaps they did not know the amount of the original debt and wrote generally so that whatever document the lender produces is impaired. The Maggid Mishneh writes similarly at the end of chapter 9 of Hilkhot Malveh, citing the Ramban's answer, because the document comes to extract money and the burden of proof rests upon the claimant.
There in the name of Baal HaTerumot, who brings proof from chapter Arba'ah Achin 38b, 'Here it comes to her detriment,' as Rashi explains there.
