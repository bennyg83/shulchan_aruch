If a borrower comes to pay and the lender says, 'I lost my document,' the borrower cannot refuse payment until the document is returned. The lender writes a receipt and the borrower pays the entire debt. The borrower may impose an unspecified ban upon anyone who withholds his document while claiming it was lost. If the borrower makes a definite claim that the lender has the document and just placed it in his pouch, the lender takes a heses oath that it was lost; the borrower then pays and receives a receipt.
