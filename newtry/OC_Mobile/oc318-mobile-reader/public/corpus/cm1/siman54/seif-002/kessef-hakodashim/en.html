See Shach, seif katan 3. The Shach's question in siman 54 concerning the scribe's fee can be resolved by saying that the Shulchan Aruch discusses a case where only part of the debt has reached its due date. In such a case it is not reasonable to require either that the lender retain the entire document or that the borrower pay the entire balance. The parties should also have anticipated this situation when the loan was made.
