The lender must swear, etc. The burden imposed on the borrower by having to preserve his receipt while the lender continues to hold the original deed has monetary value of at least a perutah. Therefore, where the borrower makes a definite claim, the lender must swear. Where the borrower's claim is uncertain, a general ban is imposed. The Shach adds that this does not allow the borrower to withhold payment until the oath occurs. He must first pay what is due, and only afterward may he require the lender to swear.
