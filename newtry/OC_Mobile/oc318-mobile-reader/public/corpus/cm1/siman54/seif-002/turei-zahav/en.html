(Beit Yosef beginning, “If he wished to write him a receipt.”) See there. He writes that this is also the view of the Raavad and Tosafot at the beginning of Bava Metzia concerning the Mishnah of a woman who produces a bill of divorce without a ketubah. Even when the husband admits the ketubah debt and she holds the bill of divorce, a receipt is written, unlike the Raavad’s other position. For the difficulties among the authorities, see the Baal HaMaor and Milchamot Hashem there regarding one who pleads after an act of beit din.
