f) Tur in the name of the Rambam, chapter 23 of Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this ruling is obvious. Although the paper of the document itself may not be worth a perutah, because of the evidentiary value it contains, even after a receipt has been written upon it the borrower would pay more than a perutah for it. Therefore, when the borrower makes a definite claim, the lender must take a shevuat heset.
