And afterward. The wording is reversed. Its meaning is that the debtor first pays his debt and afterward the creditor takes the oath. We find similar reversed wording in siman 72, at the beginning of seif 18, 'the borrower swears and takes his pledge,' meaning that he first receives the pledge and afterward swears. Likewise, in siman 133, in the Tur and Sema, seif katan 12, the phrase 'he must return it after he swears' means that he returns it and afterward takes a hesset oath. At the beginning of siman 135, 'he takes a hesset oath and receives' likewise means that he receives and afterward swears. The same applies here. This appears correct to me. Thus concludes the Shach.
