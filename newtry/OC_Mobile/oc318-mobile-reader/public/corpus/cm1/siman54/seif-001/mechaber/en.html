The Law of an Asmachta, Whether a Receipt Is Written, How It Is Written, and Payment by Receipt. Five seifim. If a person paid part of his debt, then at the lender's option a beit din may write a document for the remaining balance and date it with the original date, but the original signatories may not do so. {Rama: This applies even with the borrower's consent. From the payment date onward, however, the witnesses may write another document with the borrower's consent.} Alternatively, the lender may write a receipt. {Rama: The lender pays the scribe's fee for the receipt. If the lender has no document and the borrower requests a receipt, the borrower pays the fee.}
