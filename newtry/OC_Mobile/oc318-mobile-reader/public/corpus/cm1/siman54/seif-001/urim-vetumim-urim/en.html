If it is with the borrower's consent. Without the borrower's consent, the witnesses may not write even from the later date. The Shach concludes this, unlike Bach. The novelty of saying that with the borrower's consent they may write from the later date is that one might have thought this creates an appearance of falsehood, because no loan occurred on that later date, similar to the concern about writing a postdated document initially. The law teaches that there is no such problem. Since writing the replacement is itself a proper legal act, its later date does not create a false appearance.
