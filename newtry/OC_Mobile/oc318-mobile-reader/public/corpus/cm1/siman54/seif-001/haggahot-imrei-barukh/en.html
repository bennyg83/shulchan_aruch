(Shach, seif katan 4) For otherwise, what we say there, etc. Note: One may answer that there the item has value only to the depositor, not to the bailee. With a debt document, however, once the lender has already given a receipt and later produces the document, he can still say to the borrower that he wants some amount for the document in his possession. This is precise in the Maggid Mishneh's wording that even after writing a receipt, the borrower would give the lender more than a perutah for the document. Thus it has value to the lender himself.
