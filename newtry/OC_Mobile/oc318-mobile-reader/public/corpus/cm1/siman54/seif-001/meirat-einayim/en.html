If the lender wishes, etc. At the time of partial payment the Sages gave the lender the choice either to obtain a new document or to give the borrower a receipt. Sometimes the lender prefers a receipt because the borrower will fear losing it and may hasten to pay the balance. Sometimes the lender prefers a new document so that the remaining debt is not represented by an impaired document. I explained these same considerations in the preceding siman regarding the borrower's preferences. This is unlike Ir Shushan's explanation that the lender seeks a new document merely because he fears a rumor that the original was fully paid. The essential explanation is as I have written.
But not the original document witnesses, even though, etc. In Perishah I wrote that even according to most Geonim and the Mechaber, who hold that if the witnesses remember the date of the kinyan they may write that original date even long afterward, as explained in simanim 39 and 43, here the original witnesses should not themselves write a replacement document dated from the original date after having already written the first one. It would appear like an antedated document. Beit din, or witnesses acting under beit din's authority, have stronger power to issue an enforceable replacement.
