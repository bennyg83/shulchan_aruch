(Shach, seif katan 5) Responsa Rashba there. Note: The Rashba writes that the borrower need not pay the money into beit din, because from the outset he did not intend to pay while leaving the document in the lender's possession. This requires study regarding the Shach's citation of the Rashba here, where the lender is willing to return the document after payment. In such a case perhaps the borrower can be compelled to deposit the money with beit din until the lender returns the document that same day.
