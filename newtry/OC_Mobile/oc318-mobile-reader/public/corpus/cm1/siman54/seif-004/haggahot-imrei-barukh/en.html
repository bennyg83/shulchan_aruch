(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 2) Since it is worth a perutah. Note: Since the document is not worth a perutah to the lender after he has given a receipt, assuming he does not intend to wrong the borrower by suing if the receipt is lost, the concern that he has set his eyes upon the paper does not apply. It is even possible that the cost of writing the receipt exceeds anything he could obtain from the borrower for the document after giving him the receipt.
