An incident: Reuven asked Shimon to sell him an article, but Shimon refused. Reuven placed the money before Shimon, and Shimon threw the money away, whereupon it was lost. We ruled that Shimon is exempt. A proof is found in Kiddushin 13a: “If she did not consent, she should have thrown it away,” which implies that if she did throw it away she is exempt. A further proof is found in the chapter HaParah, cited by the Tur in siman 378, where the court says: although you had permission to remove it, you had no permission to damage it. This implies that if the object was damaged through the act of removing it, he is exempt.
