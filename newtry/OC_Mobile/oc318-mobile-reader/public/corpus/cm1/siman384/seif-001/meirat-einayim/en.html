He is liable because it is his pit. A practical consequence is that he is exempt for utensils broken in the pit, for breaking is their equivalent of death. The verse concerning a pit says, "and an ox or donkey falls there." The Sages expound: an ox, but not a person, meaning that if a person dies there the owner of the pit is exempt, though he is liable for injury; and a donkey, but not utensils. This will be explained below in siman 410.
