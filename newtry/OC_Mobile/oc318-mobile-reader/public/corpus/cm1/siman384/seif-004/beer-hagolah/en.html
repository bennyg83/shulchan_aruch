The wording of the Tur, seif 4, from the baraita and Gemara at the end of Bava Kamma chapter 10, 118b.
P. To lead from the mountain that Seth had hidden there and moved to the hand of the Khamenei who was standing on the wall that was made of the gear of the artists and was given to the order that he had cracked in the stone’s land to the side of her on her seat in a return that would not be destroyed and would not be distinguished except for the amos
Although the Gemara establishes the case where all are partners by contract in the work, only the person to whom the material had been handed is liable, because the damage came through his force. A person is always forewarned, whether inadvertent or intentional, accidental or deliberate. Rashi.
