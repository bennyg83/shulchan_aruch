One who accepted by kinyan to litigate, etc. See Responsa Mahari Sasson, siman 62; Responsa Menachem Azariah, siman 51; Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 8, siman 65, concerning these laws; Responsa Maharam Alshich, simanim 16 and 86; and Terumat HaDeshen, siman 304.
Some say that beit din does not take it from his possession. The Sema writes that he did not find anyone who disagrees with this. See Bedek HaBayit, however, which does disagree. Regarding the Tur's statement that beit din does not extract from him what he accepted upon himself to give to charity if he would not go litigate with the other party before gentile courts, Bedek HaBayit writes that the Tur's interpretation of the Mishnah concerning the stringency of vows does not appear correct to him. See also the Bach.
