Likewise, an apostate man who married an apostate woman. One who studies the responsa themselves, especially the words of Terumat HaDeshen, will see that the reason given, that he has removed himself from the community through all the transgressions of the Torah, concerns a voluntary apostate, who certainly treats his intercourse as licentious and prefers a life of abandon. But regarding those compelled by the circumstances of the time, if it is known that they observed mitzvot privately and a compelled man married a compelled woman for the sake of marriage, even though outwardly he married her under the status of non-Jews, the matter requires careful consideration. This is especially so according to Terumat HaDeshen, unless we say that he certainly did not intend his intercourse to be licentious, since they secluded themselves before many other compelled Jews for the sake of marriage. None of the reasons given by Terumat HaDeshen apply here, especially if they were careful regarding niddah and immersion in a mikveh, which is one of the reasons given by Rivash. Therefore, in practice one must certainly not be lenient regarding the prohibition of a married woman. See Responsa Radach, bayit 24.
