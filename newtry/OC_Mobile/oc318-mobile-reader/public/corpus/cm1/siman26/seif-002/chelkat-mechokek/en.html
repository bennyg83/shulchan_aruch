Some say it is prohibited and one receives lashes for 'there shall not be a prostitute.' The words of the Rosh and Tur do not imply that maintaining a concubine is prohibited as kedeshah. They discuss only family degradation and lack of honor. If there were an actual negative Torah commandment, beit din would have to compel compliance. What difference would there be between the karet prohibition of relations with a niddah and the Torah prohibition of kedeshah? Rambam too does not clearly imply that there are lashes for a concubine designated to one man. He writes that anyone who has relations with a woman promiscuously without kiddushin receives lashes, implying that where she is designated to him in the manner of a marital relationship, even without kiddushin, at least this negative commandment does not apply. In Hilchot Melachim he merely writes that an ordinary person is forbidden to have a concubine; he does not say that he receives lashes.
