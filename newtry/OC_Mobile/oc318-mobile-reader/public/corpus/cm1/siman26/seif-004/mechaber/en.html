A document that states that the creditor may sue the debtor under non Jewish law does not permit him to sue there. If he gave the document to a non Jew to pursue the claim under their law, he must reimburse the debtor for every loss imposed beyond what the debtor owes under Jewish law. Rama: This applies where the creditor can compel him through Jewish law. If the debtor is violent and cannot otherwise be compelled, it is permitted to turn him over to non Jewish authorities. See the end of siman 369. See also the responsum of the Rosh, rule 18, siman 1, regarding a non Jew who sells to a Jew a debt instrument against another Jew and whether the matter is adjudicated according to non Jewish law.
