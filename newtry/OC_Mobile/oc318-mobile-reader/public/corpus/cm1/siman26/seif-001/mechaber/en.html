Not to litigate under non Jewish law. This siman contains 4 seifim.
It is forbidden to litigate before non Jewish judges or in their courts, meaning a fixed tribunal of government officials, even when their ruling in the particular matter would conform to Jewish law, and even if both litigants agreed to appear before them. Anyone who goes to litigate before them is wicked, and it is as though he blasphemed and raised his hand against the Torah of Moshe Rabbeinu. Rama: Beit din may place him under nidui and cherem until he removes the involvement of the non Jewish authorities from his fellow. Likewise, one who assists another in bringing a matter before non Jewish authorities is placed under cherem. Even if he does not actually litigate before them but uses them merely to compel the other party to appear with him before a Jewish beit din, he is fit to be punished. See below, siman 388. If one litigated in a non Jewish court and was held liable under their law, and afterwards returns and brings his claim before Jewish judges, some say the Jewish court does not hear his claim, while others say it does, unless he caused a loss to his opposing party before the non Jewish authorities. The first view appears to me primary.
