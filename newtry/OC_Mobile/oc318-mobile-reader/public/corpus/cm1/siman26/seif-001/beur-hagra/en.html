(Collected note) And anyone who comes, etc. Tanchuma, beginning of Parashat Mishpatim: from where do we know that Jewish litigants who have a dispute, even where they know a gentile court would decide the matter in accordance with Jewish law, are forbidden to litigate before them? Scripture says, "which you shall place before them," before Jews and not before gentiles. Anyone who leaves Jewish judges and goes before gentiles first denies the Holy One, blessed be He, and afterward denies the Torah, as it says, "for their rock is not like our Rock, yet our enemies are judges," etc., and "for the statutes of the peoples, etc." (End of collected note.)
And there is authority. Bava Kamma 117a, the case of that storehouse, etc.
