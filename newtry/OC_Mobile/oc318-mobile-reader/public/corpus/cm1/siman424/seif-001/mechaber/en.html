One who injures his father or mother, his children, or another person on Shabbos. It contains eleven seifim.
If one injures his father or mother without drawing blood, he is liable for all five categories of compensation. If he draws blood, he incurs the death penalty and is therefore exempt from monetary payment, even if he acted unintentionally. Accordingly, a son should not let blood for his father, remove a thorn from his flesh, or open an abscess, lest he draw blood. This is explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 241.
