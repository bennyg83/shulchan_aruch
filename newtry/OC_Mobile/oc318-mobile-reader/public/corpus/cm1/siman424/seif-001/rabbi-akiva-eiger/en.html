[Shulchan Aruch] One who injures another on Shabbat is exempt from payment, even if he acted unintentionally, since the act carries the death penalty imposed by beit din. Note: From the discussion in Ketubot 30b, according to Rav Ashi’s explanation of the case of a non-Kohen who ate terumah and tore silk garments, etc., it is clear that even where there are two separate acts, such as where he desecrated Shabbat with one hand and with the other hand caused damage to another person, we also apply the rule that one who incurs the more severe punishment is exempt from the monetary payment. Tosafot there, 38a, s.v. amar Rav Ashi, likewise write this. Regarding lashes and monetary payment in such a case, where he received a warning, see Tosafot 32a, s.v. ve’eino lokeh. It is evident from their words that even with two separate acts he is exempt from payment. See there.
