There, law 19. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the baraitot there are explained in the sugya. It follows that where a daughter is supported at her father's table and he injures her, he is exempt, for what difference is there between a daughter and a son? The Mishnah explicitly teaches that regarding sons and daughters he is exempt, and the Gemara establishes this as where they are supported by him.
