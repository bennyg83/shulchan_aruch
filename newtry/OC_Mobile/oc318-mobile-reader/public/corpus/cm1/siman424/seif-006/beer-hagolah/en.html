Beit Yosef writes that Tur understood Rambam's concluding reason, 'because her handiwork belongs to her father,' as necessarily referring to a daughter supported at her father's table, because otherwise her handiwork would not belong to him. I therefore do not understand why Rama writes 'some say'; this is simply Tur's explanation of Rambam, whose ruling the Mechaber copied.
A. There in his judgments as a fisherman there
The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes there, explaining this law from the following one. He asks why Rambam does not say the father pays her for loss of labor since she is not supported at his table. One can answer that the father feeds her throughout her illness, and only the amount by which the lost wages exceed her maintenance remains for the father. This is how the Gemara resolves it.
