And others injured him, they are exempt. The Tur writes that the slave cannot extract the payment for himself because his emancipation is not yet complete, and the master also cannot extract it. The Raavad objects that some say the master and slave can write authorizations to one another and thereby collect. The Beit Yosef writes that in his view everyone agrees that if the master gives authorization to the slave, or the slave to the master, collection is possible. Similar rules appear at the end of siman 43 and in siman 200. It is surprising that the Mechaber omitted the authorization rule here.
