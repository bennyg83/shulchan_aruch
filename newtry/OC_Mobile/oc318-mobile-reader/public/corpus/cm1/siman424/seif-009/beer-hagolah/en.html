The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara explains why she does not sell the value of her ketubah for its present discounted worth, as explained in Even HaEzer siman 105, in order to pay for the damage. The reason is that she could later forgive the ketubah to her husband, and the court does not burden itself with a futile proceeding.
