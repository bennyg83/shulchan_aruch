Present value. The Baal HaTerumot writes in Shaar 36 that if no one wishes to purchase the wife's melog property or her guaranteed dowry property for its present discounted value, the injured party himself may take it at that discounted value. If the discounted value exceeds the amount of the bodily injury claim, the injured party need not pay the excess to the woman until the time when she acquires independent ownership of herself. See what I wrote concerning this in siman 346, seif katan 19, and siman 388, seif katan 11.
