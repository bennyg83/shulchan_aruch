The same applies if he rented it to him. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, siman 50. The questioner asked that, if rental of land is sufficient for prozbul, it would seem nearly impossible to find anyone ineligible for a prozbul except one who literally sleeps in the town square and has no dwelling even by loan or rental. Chatam Sofer answers that one can find such a case in a person supported at another’s table who may be dismissed at any time, or one who lives in another’s courtyard without the owner’s permission. Although he need not pay rent retroactively, the owners may remove him at any moment. See there further.
