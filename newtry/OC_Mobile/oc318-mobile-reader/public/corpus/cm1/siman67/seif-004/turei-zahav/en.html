(In the name of Maharshal, Beit Yosef beginning, “Likewise, one who lends for a term,” through “according to the first version, Shemittah cancels.”) I do not understand the Rif. On the contrary, because he does not explain the Mishnah as a loan secured by collateral or as one whose documents were delivered to beit din, as the first version requires, it appears that he follows the second version, under which Shemittah does not cancel the debt and the court therefore estimates the loss. Had he held that Shemittah cancels, the entire amount would have to be paid, as the Gemara asks, unless the special collateral or judicial-delivery case applied. The Beit Yosef’s reading is therefore also difficult.
