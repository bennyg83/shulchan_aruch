Sema, seif katan 2, 'in the Sabbatical year and Jubilee.' Note: This requires great study, for Tosafot wrote this only concerning Jubilee. But the sabbatical restriction on the land, namely plowing during the Sabbatical year, certainly applies even nowadays in Eretz Yisrael and the nearby lands, as explained at the beginning of tractate Moed Katan; see there. There on 2a it also implies that the prohibition applies even on a Torah level, including at a time when there is no water libation. This appears to be the Rambam's view.
