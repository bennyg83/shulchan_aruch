Mishnah at the beginning of chapter 10 of Sheviit.
In accordance with Rav and Shmuel, and as Rabbi Yochanan acted in practice, Gittin 37a.
A statement of Rav Pappa and Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua, Bava Metzia 64b.
The Rambam there in the cited chapter, from a baraita in Gittin. The Kesef Mishneh writes there in the name of the Ran that the Rambam holds we reject only what the Gemara explicitly rejects, such as a document containing property warranty, but where specific property was designated and the Gemara did not reject it, we do not reject it.
Baal HaTerumot, gate 45, according to the Rambam.
