[Sema, letter 20] And a reason may be given. Note: Mishneh LaMelech writes concerning this that there is no doubt the Sema did not see the Rashba's responsum in its original place, because there the Rashba explains the reason: this case is different because it was within his power to write a prozbul. By contrast, the conditions discussed in siman 42 depend upon the circumstances existing at the time of the act.
