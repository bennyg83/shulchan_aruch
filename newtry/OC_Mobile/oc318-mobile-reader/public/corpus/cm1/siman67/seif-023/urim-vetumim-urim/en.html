The same applies if he rented it to him. One might have thought that only borrowed property suffices, because a borrower bears greater responsibility than a renter. Sema. Strictly speaking, land is not subject to liability for unavoidable accidents and ordinary custodial law does not apply to it. Rather, the comparison is conceptual. With movables, a borrower bears unavoidable-accident liability because all benefit is his, so his possessory relationship is stronger than a renter's. So too with land, even though those liability rules do not apply directly, borrowed land can be regarded as more fully within his use than rented land. Nevertheless rented land also suffices.
