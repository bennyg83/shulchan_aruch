Only at its end. This is Rambam's view. Ramban and Rosh disagree and hold that collection is already restricted throughout the seventh year. Accordingly, under their view a *prozbul* should be written before the Sabbatical year begins, not during the year itself. See Tumim, where I bring support for that view from the Gemara. Therefore one who is careful in halachah should preferably make a *prozbul* before the Sabbatical year. An exception may exist for a loan first made during the seventh year. Radbaz and Bach hold that even Rosh agrees a *prozbul* can be made later for such a loan. I wrote in Tumim that I do not understand their reason, but their view exists.
