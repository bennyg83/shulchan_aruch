To one. The reason is that we rule that all the borrowers become jointly responsible and guarantors for one another, as stated at the beginning of siman 77. Even if each borrowed separately, if one of them owns land, the prozbul is written for all of them, because that land is treated as though it were the lender's land, which he then conveys to the other borrowers. In that case, however, he must formally convey it to them. If they borrowed together in one document, no such conveyance is required. Sema.
