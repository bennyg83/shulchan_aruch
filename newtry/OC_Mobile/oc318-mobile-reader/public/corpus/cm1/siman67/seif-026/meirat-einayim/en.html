And if they borrowed under a single document, even if only one of them owns land, etc. The reason is that we rule that when five people borrow from one lender in a single instrument, each becomes responsible and a guarantor for the others, as the Tur writes at the beginning of siman 77. In the Perishah and Derishah there I wrote that even if each borrowed separately and only one owns land, a prozbul may be written for all of them, because the land belonging to that one is regarded as though it were the lender's, and the lender can then confer an interest in it upon his other borrowers, as the Tur and Mechaber write immediately above. In that case, however, the lender must actually confer such an interest. When all borrowed under one document, a prozbul may be written for all of them even without a separate conveyance, because they are guarantors for one another.
