Only one maneh. This is according to Rambam. Tosafot and the Rosh appear to imply that a convert and one who was a minor have the same law, as cited by the Beit Yosef in this siman. In my view there is no dispute. Tosafot and Rosh discuss a non-Jew who wrote his wife a ketubah of two hundred before they converted, as Rashi explains, and they converted together and he maintained her on that basis. Since the continuation of the marriage was on the original terms and he had written two hundred, she receives two hundred. Rambam discusses a case where, after conversion, he simply continued living with her without writing a ketubah. Then she receives only one maneh, because a non-Jewish woman is presumed to have had relations.
