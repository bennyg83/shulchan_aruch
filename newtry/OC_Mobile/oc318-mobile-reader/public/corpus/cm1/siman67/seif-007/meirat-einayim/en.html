And he remains in his denial, etc. The Sabbatical year does not cancel the debt. The reason is that at the time of the Sabbatical cancellation, since he had already sworn denying the debt, the lender then had no enforceable claim against him. When he admits the debt after the Sabbatical year, it is treated like a new loan. If he admitted before the Sabbatical year ended, however, the cancellation that follows the admission cancels that renewed debt.
