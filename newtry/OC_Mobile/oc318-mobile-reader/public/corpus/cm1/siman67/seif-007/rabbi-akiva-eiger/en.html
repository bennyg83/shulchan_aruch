[Shulchan Aruch] And a later-dated one is invalid. Note: See the final responsum of the Ran, who writes that it is invalid even from the date on which the prozbul was actually written. A document not written in accordance with law loses the status of a document, and the essential force of a prozbul derives from the written instrument. See there. In the Yerushalmi, Amoraim disagree on this point. See Tosafot Yom Tov to the final chapter of Sheviit.
