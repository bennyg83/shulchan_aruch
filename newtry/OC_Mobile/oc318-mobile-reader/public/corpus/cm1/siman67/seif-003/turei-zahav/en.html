(Seif 36: They remove the document from the creditor.) The Sema writes that the paper itself belongs to the creditor. This is astonishing, for the Tur expressly says that the paper belongs to the debtor, and apparently that is why it must be returned. Nor can the paper be treated as collateral, for then under the opinion in seif 12 even the portion of the debt exceeding the collateral would not be cancelled by Shemittah. The document was originally taken only as evidence, not as security, and therefore the paper is not collateral even to the extent of its value. Bava Metzia likewise distinguishes an item held merely as a memorandum. The law of betrothal by a document is different because at the moment of betrothal the parties intend the paper itself to be acquired, knowing that a loan cannot effect betrothal.
