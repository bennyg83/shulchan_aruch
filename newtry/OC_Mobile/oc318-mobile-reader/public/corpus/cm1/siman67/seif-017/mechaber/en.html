If a man divorces his wife before Shemittah, her kesubah is not canceled. If she received partial payment or converted it into a loan obligation against him, Shemittah cancels it. {Rama: One who derives title through a non-Jew has the non-Jew's status. Therefore, if a person buys from a non-Jew a document against another Jew, Shemittah does not cancel it (Responsa of the Rashba, siman 789). Likewise, if one guaranteed another's debt to a non-Jew, paid the non-Jew, took the document, and sues the borrower with it, Shemittah does not cancel. Without taking the document, the debt is canceled even though the guarantor paid the non-Jew for him.}
