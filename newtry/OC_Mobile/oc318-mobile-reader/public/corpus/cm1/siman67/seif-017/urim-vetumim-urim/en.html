A ketubah is not cancelled by shemittah. Even nichsei tzon barzel that she brought into the marriage, which constitute a complete debt obligation, are not cancelled. See Tumim for how I reconciled the Tur, and Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Maharshal likewise writes this.
