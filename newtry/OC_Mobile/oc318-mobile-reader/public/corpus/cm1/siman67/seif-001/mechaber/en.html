The Laws of Shemittah and the Prozbul. Thirty-eight seifim. Cancellation of debts by Shemittah applies by Torah law only when the Jubilee is observed. By rabbinic law, debt cancellation applies nowadays in every place. {Rama: This is the consensus of the poskim. Some say Shemittah does not apply nowadays, and communities in these lands that do not observe debt cancellation appear to rely on them. This practice already existed in the Rosh's time. He protested vigorously to abolish it, but people did not heed him. Later authorities also offered reasons for the practice, as explained by Maharick, shoresh 92, Terumat HaDeshen, siman 304, and Maharil, and one should not challenge them. There is disagreement in calculating the Shemittah year. The primary view is that the years 5320 and 5327 were Shemittah, making the coming year 5334 a Shemittah year. May it be Hashem's will that the Beit HaMikdash be rebuilt and that the counting of Shemittah and Jubilee resume when the year 5335 arrives.}
