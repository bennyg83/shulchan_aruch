This belongs to seif 1. Mechaber, seif 1, gloss, "the year 5334." Note: on Erev Rosh Hashanah at the end of 5544, while I attended my teacher, the gaon Rabbi Natan Adler Katz, of blessed memory, he delivered his declaration before us in place of a prozbul, because we were on the road. Shemittah will, with Hashem's help, occur in the coming year 5586, and again in 5593. May it be His will that the Beit HaMikdash be rebuilt in the coming year 5600.
