Money. They did not prohibit plowing and sowing during Shemitah and Yovel, because the majority of the community could not endure such a decree. Tosafot, chapter HaSholeach. The Sema writes that another reason appears to him: since the sanctity of these agricultural laws depends upon the Land and does not apply outside Eretz Yisrael, they did not institute them in Eretz Yisrael either when the biblical law ceased. Cancellation of monetary debts, however, is a personal obligation and therefore remains applicable by rabbinic enactment. See Responsa Mabit, part 1, siman 64, and Responsa Rashdam, simanim 30, 61, and 186.
