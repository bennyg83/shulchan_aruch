a) Rambam at the beginning of chapter 5 of Hilkhot Shemitah VeYovel, from what is stated in Gittin: when you release land, etc., 36a. Yovel is called "release of land," meaning that fields return to their owners in the Jubilee year. But the laws of shemittat karka in Eretz Yisrael apply even nowadays. Kesef Mishneh there. So too write Tosafot there in the name of Rabbeinu Tam.
b) There, and in the baraita, "a remembrance of the seventh year." The Rambam explains that the enactment was made so that the Torah of cancellation of debts through shemittah should not be forgotten from Israel.
c) Baal HaTerumot, gate 45, in the name of Baal HaIttur, in the name of Rabbi Yitzchak Albargeloni, and he writes that the Baal HaMaor likewise ruled in a responsum. So too writes the Ran in the name of some authorities.
d) Meaning, outside Eretz Yisrael, as is explicit there.
e) Responsa Rosh, rule 46, seif 4.
The Targum of "swift horse" is "shafanina kerukhi."
f) See there, where he writes that perhaps they relied on the customary clause written in promissory notes, "whether by Jewish law or by other legal means," etc. Although that explanation was not fully accepted because it does not explicitly state "on condition that the seventh year shall not cancel my debt," it did not contradict their established custom because they had some basis for it. In my humble opinion, what he writes in another responsum, that it is as though he stipulated that the seventh year not cancel the debt, refers to their practice of writing "whether under Jewish law or under other legal means."
g) See there, where he writes that ordinarily all places should be presumed to follow the law that one may plead shemittah and should be governed by Torah law, unless it is established that the locale follows a custom of collecting documents that passed through a shemittah year. He rules that a document containing a lien on property is not cancelled by shemittah, following Beit Shammai, as Rav Assi rules there 37a.
h) My relative, the author of Shnei Luchot HaBrit, writes there, 79b: I have come to warn you regarding cancellation of debts in shemittah, which applies even outside Eretz Yisrael, etc. The matter is easily remedied through a prozbul, etc. The Bach likewise writes that the contrary practice spread without any sound reason. One who refrains from that practice and acts according to the law is worthy of blessing. The shemittah year will be, with Hashem's help, the year 5425. May Hashem grant us life to see the redemption and merit once again to count the Jubilee cycles.
