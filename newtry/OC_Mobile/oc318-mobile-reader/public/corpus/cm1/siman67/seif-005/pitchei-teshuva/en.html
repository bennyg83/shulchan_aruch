As long as, etc., a prozbul may be written. See the Tur, Beit Yosef, and Bach. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, siman 50, concerning a student who wished to execute a prozbul at the end of the sixth year because he was concerned for the view of the Ittur, Rosh, and Tur that a prozbul may no longer be written once the seventh year has begun. Chatam Sofer replied that he knew a concrete precedent: his revered teacher, the pious sage of blessed memory, transferred his claims before him and another member of his court at the end of 5544 as 5545 began, meaning at the end of the Shemittah year. He knew that his teacher had not made a prozbul at the end of 5543, for he had remained constantly at his side and knew his practices. Chatam Sofer discusses at length the Bach's attempt to reconcile the Rambam with the Rosh, by saying that the Rambam speaks only of a loan actually made during the seventh year, which may be collected throughout that year, and that the Rosh agrees to this, while in other cases the Rambam agrees with the Rosh. Chatam Sofer writes that this reading is very strained in the Rambam. Moreover, the Raavad necessarily disagrees with the Rosh and the Ittur and holds that Shemittah cancels debts only at its end, not at its beginning at all, and therefore a prozbul may be written during the seventh year. This is the halachah. He concludes that there is no concern at all under the essential law. Nevertheless, if one wishes to be stringent and make a prozbul earlier, he is not to be criticized, nor is this arrogance of the sort feared by Maharshal and cited by Magen Avraham, siman 63, seif katan 2, because an early prozbul is valid and every time is an appropriate time for it. He is remembered favorably. See there. The Tumim likewise writes that one who is especially God fearing should be stringent and make a prozbul on the eve of the seventh year, except regarding a loan made during the seventh year, where in any event the Radbaz and Bach maintain that even the Rosh agrees that a prozbul may be made until the end of the seventh year. See there.
