A person is believed to say, etc. The reason is the presumption that a person does not abandon a permitted course and instead consume what is prohibited. We therefore presume that he certainly transferred his documents to beit din or had a prozbul written. This is unlike the rule in siman 66, seif 11, that a person is not believed to say, 'I had a deed of acquisition stating, Acquire it for yourself, it and all its liens, but I lost it.' There the matter did not depend on him alone, because perhaps the seller refused to write such a document, or perhaps the owner of the instrument lost it and this person merely found it. Here, however, the promissory note is certainly his and it depends solely on him to transfer his debts to beit din, so we presume he did so. For this reason the Tur and Mechaber write in the following seif that he is believed to say there was a condition, etc., only by virtue of a migo. Without a migo, the reason discussed here would not make him believed to assert such a condition, because that condition did not depend solely upon him.
