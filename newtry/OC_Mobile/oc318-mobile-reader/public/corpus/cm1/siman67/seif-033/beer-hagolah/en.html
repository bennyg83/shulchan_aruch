A statement of Rav Yehudah in the name of Rav Nachman, Gittin 37b, because a person would not abandon what is permitted and consume what is forbidden. Tosafot write there that he is believed even without an oath. The Tur writes in the name of Responsa Rosh that no one in Ashkenaz ever prevailed before him with this claim, because he would question and investigate the claimant until he was caught in a lie, in accordance with the Ramah cited by the Tur.
There, regarding those who came before Rav, etc. So rules the Rambam there, and likewise the Tur in the name of the Rosh, the Rosh in Responsa, and the Ran.
