Minor orphans. See Tumim. I conclude that according to all authorities, neither debts inherited from their father nor debts lent out by their guardian require a *prozbul*. I further conclude that even if the children later become adults and the Sabbatical year occurs only after they mature, the debts are treated as though a *prozbul* had already been made while they were minors, because the hand of beit din is considered their hand. Once that protection exists, the later Sabbatical year does not cancel the debt.
One who owes money to a charity fund. Even if the fund has a collector and therefore is money with identified claimants and might otherwise fall under the prohibition against pressing a debtor, the hand of beit din is considered the hand of the poor. It is therefore treated as though a *prozbul* exists.
