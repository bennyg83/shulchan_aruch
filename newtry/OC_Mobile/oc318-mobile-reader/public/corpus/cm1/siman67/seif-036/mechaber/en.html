When a borrower returns a debt that Shemittah canceled, the lender must say, 'I cancel it; you have already been released.' If the borrower says, 'Nevertheless, I want you to accept it,' the lender may accept. The borrower should not say, 'I give it as payment of my debt,' but, 'It is mine, and I give it to you as a gift.' If he returns the money without saying this, the lender should engage him in conversation until he says it is his and is given as a gift. If he does not say so, the lender may not accept it and the borrower takes his money and leaves.
