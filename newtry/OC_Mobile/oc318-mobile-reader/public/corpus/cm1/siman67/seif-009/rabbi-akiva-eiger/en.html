[Sema, subparagraph 57] But without a migo. Note: Responsa Ramban, siman 256, cited in Beit Yosef, writes that even nowadays, when people are not accustomed to writing a prozbul and are unfamiliar with it, the lender is believed to say that there was a condition between them, for the same reason that a person would not abandon a permitted avenue and choose a prohibited one. Mahari ben Lev, part 1, siman 68, likewise rules this plainly.
