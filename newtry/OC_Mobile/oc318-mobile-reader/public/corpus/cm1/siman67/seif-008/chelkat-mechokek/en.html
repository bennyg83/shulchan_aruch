And if a deaf-mute man married her, etc., she has a ketubah. Maggid Mishneh writes that this applies specifically where, after becoming competent, he wishes to maintain the marriage. We do not compel him to maintain it. In the case of one who was mentally incompetent and later recovered, since the Sages did not institute a valid marriage for him while incompetent, if he wishes to keep her after recovery he must perform kiddushin and write a ketubah. See Tur and Bach.
