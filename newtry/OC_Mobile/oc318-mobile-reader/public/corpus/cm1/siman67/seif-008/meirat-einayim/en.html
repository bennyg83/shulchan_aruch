And they wrote him a judgment, etc. Every formal act of beit din is regarded as though already collected. What was stated in the previous seif, that if the borrower denied the debt, swore, and then admitted before the end of the Sabbatical year, the debt is cancelled, refers to a case where beit din did not write a judgment for the lender. So writes the Beit Yosef. The Rosh writes there that even if beit din ruled in his favor, if they did not write the judgment, the debt is cancelled. See below, seif katan 30.
