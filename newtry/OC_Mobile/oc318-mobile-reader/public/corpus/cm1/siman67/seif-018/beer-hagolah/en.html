m) Mishnah 3, chapter 10 of Sheviit, 37b.
n) Rambam there, from what is stated in Gittin regarding Rav Ami and Rav Assi, that their courts were powerful, etc., as the Ran explains his words and the Kesef Mishneh cites. The Beit Yosef explains that Rav Ami and Rav Assi are not exclusive; the same applies to an important beit din of the generation. Tosafot there write that Rabbeinu Tam wrote a prozbul on this basis. The Beit Yosef agrees, and Baal HaTerumot likewise writes in gate 45.
