I transfer. The Sema explains that these words are the lender's formulation. He states them orally to beit din, and they write: 'We, three sitting together, were convened, and so-and-so the lender came and said before us: I transfer to you,' and so forth. All three judges sign below, each as a dayan, or they may sign in the language of witnesses, meaning that they testify that he stated this before them while they were sitting together as three. Since the Mechaber is not discussing actual delivery of the debt documents but an oral declaration, as above, the Rama should not have written, 'the same applies that he may,' for there would be no basis to distinguish the cases.
