An antedated prozbul is valid. Antedating weakens the lender who made the prozbul. If the prozbul was actually made in Iyar, all loans through Iyar would be protected. If it is dated earlier, in Nisan, loans made after Nisan will not appear protected. By contrast, if the prozbul was actually made in Nisan and is postdated to Iyar, loans made after Nisan, which under the true facts are not included, would improperly be brought within the prozbul. Therefore a postdated prozbul is invalid.
A postdated one is invalid. It is invalid even regarding loans that predated the true date on which the prozbul was actually made. For example, if it was made in Nisan but written as though made in Iyar, even a loan made before Nisan is not protected and is cancelled by shemittah. See Tumim, where I discussed at length whether this invalidity applies even according to our practice, where we rule that a prozbul is effective through an oral declaration even without a written instrument.
