And one dated later is invalid. The reason is that one cannot transfer to beit din a debt that does not yet exist at the time of the prozbul. If the prozbul is postdated, the lender may later produce it and collect even debts that arose after the prozbul was actually written. An antedated prozbul, by contrast, weakens the lender's rights. With an ordinary promissory note the rule is the reverse: an antedated note is invalid and a postdated one is valid, as the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 43. There, an earlier date improperly strengthens the creditor's rights by allowing collection from purchasers from that earlier date, whereas a later date weakens his rights.
