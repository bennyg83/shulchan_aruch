Before three witnesses, etc. One must explain that this opinion requires expert judges knowledgeable in the laws of shemittah, while these three witnesses are not qualified in that way. Otherwise, why not simply treat these three as the beit din? I did not find a clear reason why this opinion requires three witnesses rather than two. Perhaps it holds that because the borrower does not consent and the prozbul is made without his knowledge, later reliance on their written statement would raise the problem of testimony from writing. See Tosafos in Bava Batra, where they labor to explain how a written protest is effective. Therefore three are required so that they themselves constitute a beit din, eliminating the problem of written testimony.
