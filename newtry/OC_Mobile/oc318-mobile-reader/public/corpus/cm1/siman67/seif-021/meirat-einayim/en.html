And some say even before two, and he says to them, 'Be witnesses for me,' etc., until, 'and if judges signed it,' etc. It appears that the latter phrase does not refer back to the earlier declaration, 'Be witnesses for me.' Rather, the intent is that if the document began in the language of judges, stating, 'We, three judges sitting together as one court,' and judges also signed it, this is superior even to the first procedure mentioned, where witnesses merely provide him with a signed writing attesting that they know he transferred his prozbul before other judges.
