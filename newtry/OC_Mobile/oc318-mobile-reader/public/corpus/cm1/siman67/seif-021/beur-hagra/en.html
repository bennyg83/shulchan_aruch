Some say, even before two. Gittin 33a. Even according to Rav Sheshet, witnesses certainly number two. The first opinion holds that this applies where the delivery is made before beit din and the court itself is present, whereas where he merely makes the declaration before witnesses, three are required because they function like beit din.
Before three judges. This follows Rav Sheshet. Although regarding a get the halachah follows Rav Nachman there, the two issues are not necessarily dependent upon one another; the Ran merely brings proof that two may also be called a beit din. See the Ran there.
Who are so-and-so... As above in seif 19, "so-and-so..." and as Rabbi Shimon says there, "Must he enumerate them like a peddler?"
Who are in the place. From the Yerushalmi cited in the preceding seif.
And if they sign. Meaning the judges are in his locale and he delivers the declaration to them. The novelty is that witnesses are unnecessary, as explained later. See the Sema.
And he does not need. As above in seif 19, whether the judges or witnesses, etc.
