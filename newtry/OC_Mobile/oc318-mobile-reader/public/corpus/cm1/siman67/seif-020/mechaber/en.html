If Torah scholars lent to one another and the lender stated before students, 'I deliver to you every debt owed to me so that I may collect it whenever I wish,' he need not write a prozbul. They know that debt cancellation nowadays is rabbinic and may therefore be displaced by a verbal declaration alone. {Rama: Some say any person may make this declaration orally before a beit din, and it is effective without a written prozbul. It makes no difference whether the lender is in the judges' city; even outside their presence he may say, 'I deliver my documents to the specified beit din in the specified city.'}
