This is what Hashem’s promise is, and this is a thing that has forgotten even things and we were a oath:
And he swore to pay. The oath was made only in relation to an obligation that remained legally payable. If the lender himself waived the debt, would the borrower still be obligated to perform the oath? Certainly not. The same is true where Torah law itself cancels the obligation. The Sabbatical year operates as a sovereign legal cancellation. Sema writes that the same applies where the debt fell due on the eve of the Sabbatical year and the borrower swore to pay within two years. If the Sabbatical year intervened, he is exempt. See Tumim, where I leave this ruling requiring study.
