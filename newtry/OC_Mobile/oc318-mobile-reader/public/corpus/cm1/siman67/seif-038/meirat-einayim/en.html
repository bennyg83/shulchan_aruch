We advance the claim for the purchaser, etc. We rule that beit din advances for an heir or purchaser every claim the seller or decedent could himself have made. But if the purchase occurred before Shemittah, we do not advance a claim that a prozbul had already been written, because ordinarily the seller would not yet have had a prozbul when the time of release had not arrived.
