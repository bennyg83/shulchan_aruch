And for orphans, etc. Sema writes that if the orphans owe money to others, a *prozbul* may be written on the basis of land belonging to their guardian. According to Rashi, the same would be true if another person owes the orphans money they lent, because the guardian's land can count like land belonging to the lender. See Tumim, seif katan 25. I discuss this at length and conclude that even according to Rashi, loans belonging to minor orphans do not require a *prozbul* at all. Rashi himself can be read as speaking where the orphans are the borrowers. The "orphans" here are minors, because adults do not ordinarily have guardians in this sense.
