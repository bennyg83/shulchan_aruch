By means of a migo. Where a presumption supports the claim, a migo is accepted even to extract money. So writes Sefer HaTerumot, gate 45; see Ketubot 12b.
(Supplement) By means of a migo, etc. As stated there regarding a presumption. Hagahot Mordekhai at the beginning of Bava Metzia writes likewise. This requires study, since the Gemara also gives an alternative explanation, implying that according to the first explanation the migo is unnecessary. See siman 82, seif 12.
