The language of Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilkhot Sheluchin, law 8, from the baraita in Bava Metzia 81a, cited by Rif in HaSho'el.
The baraita's wording refers to "the benefit of gratitude contained in them," meaning that the recipients will be grateful to him for sending them gift items.
Rashi explains that since the price was fixed and he took the items for the purpose of purchase, they are considered purchased. Hagahot Maimoniyot likewise writes that Ri agreed with Rashi. The wording of the baraita supports them, because it speaks where the price was fixed, saying, "I will give you their value," implying the value had already been set. Rambam's own wording does not conclusively decide this point, as Kesef Mishneh writes.
This too appears in the baraita in the textual versions before us, and Rambam's wording supports that reading.
