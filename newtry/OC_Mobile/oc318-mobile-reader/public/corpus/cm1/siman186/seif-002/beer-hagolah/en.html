Hashem’s name is in His presence, and Hashem’s promise to Abraham, “I will not forget that I will not be born.”
Tur, seif 2, in the name of his father Rosh. These are Tosafot's words there, based on Nedarim 31a and 31b, that the purchaser is liable only in a rapidly marketable sale.
Beit Yosef likewise writes this from Nedarim and Tosafot.
See Maharik, root 155: if at the outset the agent said, 'I will improve the sale for you and sell the object for the amount you assess,' and asked for nothing, and the owner told him, 'sell it for such-and-such,' without saying the excess would belong to the agent, nevertheless the entire excess belongs to the agent because he initially said he would sell it for the amount assessed. Bach agrees, unlike the Mechaber in Bedek HaBayit.
