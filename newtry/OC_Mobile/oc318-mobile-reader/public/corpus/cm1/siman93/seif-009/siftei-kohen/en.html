He is obligated only in an oath. See the Sema, seif katan 17, who writes that in the Derishah he explained that even Rav Hai said this only where the plaintiff sues regarding an oath that had already become due, unlike the present case. This distinction is unnecessary. Rav Hai's reason is that we do not make an enactment upon an enactment. Accordingly, even the partners' oath, although modeled after a Torah oath, is still rabbinic, and we do not institute an additional enactment upon it. The Bach writes the same.
