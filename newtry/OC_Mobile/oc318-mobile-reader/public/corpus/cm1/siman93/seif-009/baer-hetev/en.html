Admitted. The Sema writes: In siman 87 the Tur concludes in the name of Rav Hai that regarding a Torah oath, if the obligated party says, "I already swore," he must take a shevuat heset on that assertion, whereas regarding a rabbinic oath they did not impose an additional oath, because the Sages do not enact one safeguard upon another. It appears to me that even Rav Hai said this only where one claims an oath that had already become due, unlike here, where the oath would arise now only by virtue of his admission. The Shach writes that this distinction is unnecessary, because Rav Hai's reason applies equally to the partners' oath. Although that oath resembles a Torah oath, it is nevertheless rabbinic, and we do not enact one safeguard upon another. The Bach writes likewise.
