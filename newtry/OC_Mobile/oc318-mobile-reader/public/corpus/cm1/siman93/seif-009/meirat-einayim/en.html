The Tur and Mechaber write the same in siman 87, seif 25.He is obligated only in an oath, etc. In siman 87, seif 42, the Tur concludes in the name of Rav Hai that where the original oath is a Torah oath and the person afterward says, 'I already swore,' he must take a hesset oath that he indeed did so.
Only regarding a rabbinic oath did they say that no further oath is imposed, because we do not make an enactment upon an enactment. The Mechaber and Rama omitted Rav Hai's distinction there. See the Derishah there and here, where I explained that even according to the other authorities the nature of the original oath can affect whether a later hesset oath is appropriate.
