(That Shimon knew with certainty) Beit Yosef explains that a definite claim is unnecessary for an oath by extension, as Sema writes in seif katan 15 or 26. It appears to me that the words should be understood literally. Siman 94 makes clear that an uncertain claim may not be added by extension unless there are grounds supporting it. What grounds are present here to believe that Shimon knows how much was lost? This also resolves the later wording, “but if Shimon claims that Reuven certainly knows,” followed by “or by extension.” Beit Yosef in siman 94 was forced to ask why certainty is needed for extension. According to what I have written, the answer is clear. See there. “He may give it to Levi” speaks of a case in which beit din heard no prior admission that partnership funds were in his possession until the moment he claimed that Levi held a share in them.
