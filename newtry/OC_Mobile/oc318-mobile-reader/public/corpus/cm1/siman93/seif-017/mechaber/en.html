If Reuven and Shimon are partners, and Reuven tells his brother, 'Work with us in the partnership, leave your money together with ours, and take profit in proportion to your money,' while Shimon knew about the arrangement, and Shimon now says, 'Since you did not notify me, I need not give your brother any of the profit from my share,' the law follows Reuven. His brother takes a share of the profit proportionate to his share of the money.
