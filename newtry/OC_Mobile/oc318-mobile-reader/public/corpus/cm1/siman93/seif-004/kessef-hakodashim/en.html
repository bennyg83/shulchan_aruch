(And a member of the household.) The oath imposed upon a member of the household appears to apply even where he receives wages. The concern of self-justification, illustrated by the ox eating from its threshing, can still exist. Accordingly, perhaps craftsmen known as wine measurers may be required to swear that they did not take more liquor than custom allows them. Perhaps tailors too may be required to swear that they did not take more cloth than was actually needed for the garment. This requires study.
