Even though he was not paid for this, etc. The phrase 'even though' indicates that all the more so the oath applies when he is paid for the work.
One might have thought that where he receives no payment, we should not impose an oath lest people refrain from doing kindness for one another without compensation. But where he is paid, that concern is weaker.
Moreover, because they receive compensation for their labor, such persons may rationalize taking additional amounts on the ground that the agreed compensation does not fully reflect their effort. This tendency to rationalize is one of the reasons Chazal imposed an oath on an uncertain claim.
