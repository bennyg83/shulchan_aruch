Wages. One might have thought that he should not be required to swear, lest people close the door before those seeking an act of kindness. The Taz explains differently: Since he acts without compensation, one might think that his intent is certainly for a mitzvah, and therefore we should not suspect that he rationalizes taking more for himself. Certainly, however, where they receive compensation they will not refrain because of the oath. Moreover, they may rationalize that they were not paid adequately for their effort. So writes the Sema. See Responsa Rashach 201, Responsa Mabit 53, and Responsa Maharashdam, end of siman 114 and siman 444.
In this. Meaning in the case of one who receives investment funds for half the profit. The same applies whenever he receives a fixed wage. Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 27, likewise rules that when compensation is fixed, there is no concern that he will rationalize taking for himself, and he need not swear while holding a sacred object. See there. Shach. If the parties stipulated that he be trusted without an oath, he need not swear either on an uncertain claim or a definite claim. Mahari Adarbi, siman 220. Knesset HaGedolah.
