Ibid., Sema, seif katan 24, "that he may swear." Note: meaning, in contrast to the Beit Yosef, who wrote that even if he wishes he may not swear because it appears false. There, however, the Kesef Mishneh's reason is that he would come to take a vain oath. See there, page 6d, in Tosafot and the Shitah Mekubetzet. The Sema nevertheless agrees that it is preferable not to swear concerning the entire amount, as Tosafot wrote in Bava Batra 70b, s.v. "and he swears." See there. Tosafot's citation there from Bava Metzia applies only to their statement that he does not undermine his own assertion, but there is no comparison to the opening portion of their discussion, as the Sema wrote.
