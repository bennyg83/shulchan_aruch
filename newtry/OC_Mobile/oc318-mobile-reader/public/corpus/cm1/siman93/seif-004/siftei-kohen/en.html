And some disagree with this. Meaning, they disagree regarding one who receives an iska for half the profits. The same applies wherever he receives a fixed compensation. So too in the responsum of Rabbeinu Tam cited by the Mordechai, and this follows from the Beit Yosef and Rama. Otherwise, from where would they know that according to the Rambam one who receives an iska for half the profits must swear? The Rambam wrote only language like that of the Mechaber here in seif 4. Rather, there is no distinction. Examine carefully. The Levush likewise appears to explain it this way, and the Sema in seif katan 12 appears to agree. Maharam Mintz, responsum 27, likewise writes that where the compensation is fixed there is no basis to say that the recipient rationalizes his conduct as permitted, and therefore he need not swear while holding a sacred object.
