An occasional agent does not swear unless he comes to collect, meaning that the principal instructed him to purchase with the agent's own money and the agent now says that he spent a certain amount and seeks reimbursement. In that case he must swear how much he paid, just as one who was instructed to improve another's field swears concerning his expenditures. See Shach, seif katan 8, where there is a textual error. The correct reference is siman 91 seif katan 30. Examine carefully his statement that if the workers are present they swear. His intent is to ask that there, where no price was fixed, the agent need not swear when no one contradicts him, whereas here the agent must swear how much he spent. This is also the question of the Taz there. See the Taz's resolution. Although two opinions are cited here concerning whether the recipient of an iska must take an oath, in practice the ruling follows the customary heter iska documents drafted on the basis of heter iska, because Rama's enactment concerning bills structured according to heter iska was that the recipient must swear when he claims, “I did not earn a profit.”
