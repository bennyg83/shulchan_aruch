He claimed from him... and he rolls onto him... As stated in the Mishnah, "He became obligated to me in an oath..." and in the Gemara, "For every oath we apply gilgul..."
And he does not roll onto him... for the reason we stated. The law of gilgul is derived in the first chapter of Kiddushin 28a from sotah. That derivation applies where the ancillary matter is one that can be made subject to an oath. Here, however, no oath is imposed through one witness, as explained above. This is the meaning of "for the reason we stated."
