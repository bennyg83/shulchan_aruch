The language of the Rambam there, law 4. The Kesef Mishneh writes that the Rosh at the beginning of Shevuat HaDayanim writes similarly in the name of Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash, and the Rambam follows his teacher. So too the Tur in his name. The Rosh writes that the words of some Geonim, the Rif, and Rav Hai incline somewhat toward Rabbi Yosef HaLevi's view.
