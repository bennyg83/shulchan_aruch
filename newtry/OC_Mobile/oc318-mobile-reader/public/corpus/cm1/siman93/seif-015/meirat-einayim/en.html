With witnesses that Levi did not pay. The Tur likewise uses this wording in the name of a responsum of the Rosh. It implies that if Reuven has no proof through witnesses that Levi failed to pay, then even if he has witnesses that Reuven himself was compelled to pay, Shimon need not reimburse him anything. Reuven undertook toward the gentile to pay only if Levi failed to pay. It is possible that Levi already paid and that the gentile is making a false claim against Reuven, and generally a person is not held liable merely because another person was seized on his account, as the Mechaber and Rama rule below in siman 128, and similarly as the Mechaber writes above in siman 42:1 and the Tur below in siman 378. It is possible to press the language of the Rosh and Tur differently, reading 'if Reuven establishes' as referring to what follows, namely that Reuven will be required to pay, and understanding the mention of Levi's nonpayment merely as the cause for which the gentile came against Reuven, not as a separate evidentiary requirement. This interpretation is somewhat plausible because Reuven undertook the obligation for the needs of the partnership, and perhaps the rule that one person is not held for another would not apply. Nevertheless, the first interpretation appears primary. See the Perishah.
