If Reuven and Shimon are partners, Reuven purchased garments from a non-Jew and sold them to Levi, and Levi departed to the non-Jew with the money, while Reuven gave the non-Jew a document obligating himself to pay if Levi did not pay by the fixed time, and Reuven and Shimon subsequently divided the partnership, Shimon must give Reuven a document undertaking the following: if Reuven proves through witnesses that Levi did not pay the non-Jew and Reuven consequently had to pay him, Shimon will reimburse his share. Shimon must also reimburse his share of every expense, bribe, and other outlay connected with the matter that Reuven proves through witnesses.
