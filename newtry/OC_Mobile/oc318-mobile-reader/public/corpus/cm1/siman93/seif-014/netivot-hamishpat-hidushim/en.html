He shall pay from his own funds and sue Levi. See Beurim: the case is where they are partners in all their business, or it is known that the debt arose from a specific partnership transaction. Therefore, when the note is in Reuven's possession and he has no migo, he certainly must pay. When the note is in Shimon's possession and Shimon has a migo that he could have destroyed it, the matter depends upon the dispute cited by the Shach in siman 62 whether one who conducts business is believed through a migo where debt instruments are written in his own name.
