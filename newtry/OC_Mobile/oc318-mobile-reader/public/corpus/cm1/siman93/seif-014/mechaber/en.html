Similarly, if a promissory note issued to Shimon for one hundred dinars of partnership money is produced against Levi, and Shimon says, 'I was repaid and returned the money to the partnership fund,' or says, 'I granted him a term of one or two years,' Shimon is not believed, lest he be colluding with Levi against Reuven's property. How is this case judged? Levi has already been released by Shimon's admission. If Shimon brings no proof, he must pay from his own property and may sue Levi at the end of the term he claimed to have granted. {Rama: If the document expressly states that the money belongs to the partnership and the document is held by Reuven, Shimon's admission is effective only with respect to his own half. Reuven may claim his half from Shimon, or from Levi if he cannot collect it from Shimon.} See note 259.
