Half of his share from Shimon. One must understand what damage Shimon caused Reuven that would entitle Reuven to collect from him. Shimon is not believed at all concerning Reuven's share, and Reuven seemingly can collect his share from Levi just as before Shimon's admission. Why then should Shimon become liable to Reuven? The answer is that if Reuven collects his half from Levi, Levi can then recover that same amount from Shimon and say, "I paid you the whole amount, and you caused me damage by failing to tear the document immediately, or by failing to tell your partner at once when you received payment, or by failing to establish witnesses to the payment." There is no greater indirect causation than this. It would therefore be a pointless circular collection: Reuven would collect from Levi and Levi would then collect from Shimon. Reuven may instead collect directly from Shimon. Alternatively, Levi may have no assets. Since, as explained, Shimon then owes Levi his half, Reuven can collect that amount from Shimon under Rabbi Natan's lien.
