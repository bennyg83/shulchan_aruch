As soon as she made a match she lost her support. The Bach objects and writes that a match should be no worse than where she was proposed to for marriage and consented, regarding which we say she may retract. His words are not clear. Mere consent is only speech, so it is reasonable to say she may retract. But where she bound herself by a penalty and a ban to marry a particular man, became known as that man's bride, a date was set, and written conditions were executed, this is the clearest possible indication that she intends to remarry. According to Rashi in chapter HaSholeach concerning one who demands her ketubah in beit din, once she reveals her intent to marry she loses support. Rambam speaks of actual erusin because in their times people would ordinarily effect erusin immediately after arranging the match.
