And it is collected only after the husband's death. Darkei Moshe writes that even if she is not yet permitted to remarry, she nevertheless receives her ketubah immediately. See there, and above siman 13, seif 13. See Responsa Rashba, siman 705. Hagahot Mordechai writes that in a case of water of unknown end, the ketubah is collected and the inheritance is divided among the heirs. Raavad, however, writes in his glosses to chapter 7 of Hilchot Nachalot that although the heirs take possession of the estate, she does not collect her ketubah, because one cannot apply to her the condition, 'when you marry another, you shall take what is written for you.' The Maggid Mishneh agrees there. Nevertheless, a pregnant or nursing widow, or a widow within three months of her husband's death, although she is temporarily prohibited from remarrying, collects her ketubah immediately, because the condition, 'when you marry another,' applies when the fixed waiting period arrives. See Maggid Mishneh, chapter 18 of Hilchot Ishut, and below siman 95, seif 2. See also Hagahot Alfasi, chapter HaIshah, which states that every woman is fit to collect her ketubah immediately upon widowhood or divorce unless she is bound by yibbum, and all the more so in a place where ketubot are written.
