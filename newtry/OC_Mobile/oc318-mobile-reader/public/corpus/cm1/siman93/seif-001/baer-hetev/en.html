It is evident that this is also the Rosh's view, and it appears to be the view of the Rif and Rambam as well. Sema. The Shach writes that the principal view follows the Beit Yosef, who holds that according to the Rif and Rosh no partial admission is required. The Ran likewise writes that this is the view of Ri HaLevi and of the Rambam in chapter 9 of Hilchot Sheluchin, and it is also evident from the Rambam's Commentary to the Mishnah.
