They swear on an uncertain claim.
The reason is explained in the Tur and shortly in the Mechaber: partners and similar persons may rationalize to themselves that because of the labor they invested they are permitted to take something from the money without the owner's knowledge.
Chazal therefore imposed an oath even though the owner cannot state a definite claim.Whom beit din appointed.
This excludes a guardian appointed by a father over his children after his death.
Such a guardian is generally not subjected to the same uncertain-claim oath, lest people refuse to serve as guardians.
But where beit din appoints him, the appointment itself is an honor and people are less likely to refuse because of the oath requirement.
The same policy concerns underlie the distinctions among partners, guardians, and others who manage property belonging partly or wholly to another.
