The law of an oath on a doubtful claim and the oath of partners. This siman contains 18 seifim.
The following people swear in response to a doubtful claim: partners, sharecroppers, guardians appointed by beit din over orphans, a woman who conducts business within the household or whom her husband appointed to manage a store, and a member of the household who conducts the affairs of the owner. Each of these takes a rabbinic oath while holding a sacred object in response to a doubtful claim, lest he have taken something improperly from the other party in the course of their dealings or failed to calculate their accounts precisely. None of them is required to swear on a doubtful claim unless the party demanding the oath suspects him of at least two silver ma'ah. Rama: This threshold may consist either of principal or profit. Any of these people may be required to swear whenever the other party wishes, and the other party need not wait until the relationship is terminated and administer the oath only at the end.
