[Sema, letter 2] And the Mechaber follows his own reasoning. Note: These words are astonishing, for on the contrary, the Sema himself writes in siman 107 that according to the Mechaber a partial admission is required, just as the Mechaber rules without qualification below in siman 290. Rather, one may perhaps explain the reverse: since where beit din appointed the guardian a partial admission is required, it follows that where the father of the orphans appointed him a partial admission is not required. This accords entirely with Rashi’s view. Examine this carefully.
