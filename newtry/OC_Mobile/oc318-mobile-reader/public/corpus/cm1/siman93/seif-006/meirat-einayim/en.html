Once the partners divided, etc., and the agent brought him, etc. The Mechaber lists all these cases to teach that the rule is not limited to partners and similar persons who have a share in the property that was divided. In such cases one could say that once they divided and remained silent without demanding an oath from one another, they certainly waived the oath. The same might not seem true of a member of the household or an agent, where the property belongs entirely to the owner and the owner did not permit them to take anything for themselves. One could therefore not infer from his allowing them to withdraw that he waived an oath. For this reason the Ramah requires such persons to take an oath even after their role ended, as the Tur cites in his name. The Mechaber teaches that they too are exempt. The Tur likewise cites the Rambam and Rosh as holding that all of them are exempt from the oath. Examine this carefully.
