[Shulchan Aruch] And he deposits the merchandise, or part of it. Note: Although this person does not engage in buying and selling and the rationale that he permits himself to take does not directly apply, nevertheless, in the case of partners, if the law were that one partner cannot impose an oath upon the other when he suspects that the other retained partnership funds for himself, then he too would permit himself to retain funds corresponding to what he suspects his partner of taking. For this reason the Sages instituted the partners’ oath, so that one partner’s heart should not harbor suspicion against the other. So writes Maharit, siman 105.
