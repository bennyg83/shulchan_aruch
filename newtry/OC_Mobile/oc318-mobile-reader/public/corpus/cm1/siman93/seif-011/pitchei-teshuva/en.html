And they impose oaths upon one another. See Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter 8 of Bava Kamma, siman 28, who writes that if a partner's wife also conducts business in the partnership, she too must swear. See there. His words are cited in Urim VeTumim and Ketzot HaChoshen. Urim VeTumim adds that perhaps even if one gave the partner an exemption from oaths, he may nevertheless make the partner's wife swear if she was not explicitly included in the exemption. See there.
