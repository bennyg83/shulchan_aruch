(Seif 15.) "If Reuven can establish," etc. One must say that the case is where the non-Jew does not hold a document against Levi. If, at the time Reuven paid the non-Jew, the non-Jew held a document against Levi and Reuven took that document from him, there is no clearer proof than this. The same is implied by siman 151 in our master's words and by the cited siman, where a document is treated as proof. Here additional proof is required, because perhaps afterward Levi repaid Reuven. One cannot say that if so Levi should have taken the document back from him, because this is not a valid argument, as stated in siman 130, seif 3. See what I wrote in siman 131, seif 6.
