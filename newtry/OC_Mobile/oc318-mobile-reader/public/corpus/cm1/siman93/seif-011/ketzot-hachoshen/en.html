The Sema explains that if one first denied ever having been partners and witnesses later establish the partnership, he has become an established false denier for purposes of the partnership oath. In my view the rule is broader. Even where his first plea did not itself amount to an admission against him, for example he first said, "We already divided," and witnesses then established that no division occurred, he is no longer believed to advance a new second plea such as, "We had stipulated that I was exempt from the partnership oath." Once he was established as a false denier in beit din concerning his first plea, he cannot substitute another plea unless he proves it with witnesses, as stated in siman 79, seif 13.
