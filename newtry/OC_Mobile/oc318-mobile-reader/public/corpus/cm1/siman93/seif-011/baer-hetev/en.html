A denier. One who says, 'We were never partners,' effectively says, 'We never divided the partnership.' Since witnesses establish that they were partners, it is as though they testify that the partnership still exists. Nevertheless, he is believed regarding his assertion that he already swore, because he has not been established as a definite monetary denier. Even if they were partners, it may be that nothing remained in his hands. Still, he has been established as a denier for this limited purpose: he cannot later claim that he swore unless he did so before witnesses, as the Tur and Mechaber rule in siman 87, seif 27, concerning one who was obligated to swear, claimed that he had sworn, and was contradicted by witnesses. Sema.
