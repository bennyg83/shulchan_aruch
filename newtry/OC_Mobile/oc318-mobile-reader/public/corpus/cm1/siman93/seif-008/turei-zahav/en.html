(See there, and Shimon shall pay from his own pocket) Sema explains that Raavad holds this even according to the view that partnership losses are divided equally. He apparently reads the phrase attributed to Raavad, “and it is not so,” as follows: first Raavad says that even if he agrees with Rambam that the loss is divided equally, it is nevertheless not legally proper for Shimon to pay anything from his own pocket; afterward he says concerning the primary rule, “and it is not so.” In Raavad’s actual glosses, however, the phrase “and it is not so” does not appear, and this reading seems primary. See what I wrote in siman 176, seif 6. Ramah likewise follows Rambam insofar as he agrees with Raavad that Shimon at least pays nothing from his own pocket. Although the Mechaber here cites only Rambam and not Ramah, that is because the principal issue, upon whom the partnership loss falls, belongs in siman 177. Here he comes only to teach that according to Ramban an oath can at least result in payment from one’s own pocket.
