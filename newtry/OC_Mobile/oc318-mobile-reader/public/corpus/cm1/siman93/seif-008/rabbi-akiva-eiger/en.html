[Shulchan Aruch] There is an authority who says that if he exempted him from an oath. Note: Nimukei Yosef in chapter HaKosev writes that specifically with a wife who claims her ketubah, even if he exempted her from an oath, he may still impose a vow upon her. This does not apply to another claimant. See there. The same is stated above in siman 75, seif 6, in the Rama.
