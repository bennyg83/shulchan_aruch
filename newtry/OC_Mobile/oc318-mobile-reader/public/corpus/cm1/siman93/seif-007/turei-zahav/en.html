(Seif 13, the oath of partners whose capital decreased) Sema, seif katan 13 or 24, cites Beit Yosef and disagrees with him. Sema’s position appears correct, although he misunderstood Beit Yosef’s reason. Beit Yosef does not mean that the person would literally swear falsely. Rather, the concern is that the public will cast aspersion upon beit din, saying, “He swore concerning the entire amount, yet they did not award it all to him.” Rashi explains this expressly at the beginning of Bava Metzia 5b, “Would we give him the entire amount?” It is therefore obvious that the same concern applies here.
