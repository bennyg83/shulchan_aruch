(Two silver ma’ot) Sema, seif katan 7, cites the Tur’s agreement that an admission of one perutah is also required. This remains questionable as a matter of practical law, because in siman 290 the Tur records a dispute between Raavad and Ramah on this point, while the Rambam mentions no admission at all, only a claim of two silver ma’ot. No decisive proof can therefore be drawn from the Tur. Ran writes that according to the Rambam no admission is required, precisely because the Rambam says nothing of it in chapter 9 of the Laws of Agency.
