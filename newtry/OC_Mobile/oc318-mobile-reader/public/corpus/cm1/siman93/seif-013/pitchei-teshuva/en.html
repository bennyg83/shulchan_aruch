We do not say that Reuven should swear. See what I wrote below, siman 176, seif 6, seif katan 8.
So that he may swear and collect. See Responsa Radbaz, part 4, siman 102. If one admits the loss and damage sustained by the partnership, or they are established by witnesses, but he claims that perhaps the partner earned profit elsewhere from which the partnership funds can be replenished, the partner swears that there was no profit elsewhere, and the established or admitted loss must be paid. Otherwise a loss could never be collected. See there. His words are cited in Urim VeTumim, Ketzot HaChoshen, and Netivot HaMishpat. See there.
