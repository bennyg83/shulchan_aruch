There is a lawyer and a column in his name
The Kesef Mishneh writes that even if he does not claim that Shimon certainly knows, he may impose gilgul shevuah on an uncertain claim, as the Maggid Mishneh writes in chapter 1 of Hilkhot To'en, law 12, where it is explicit in Kiddushin 27b and explained in siman 94, seif 2. The Rambam said, 'If he did not engage in the business, Shimon takes a shevuat hesset,' because if the claim were merely uncertain whether Shimon knew, we rule that one does not swear on an uncertain claim.
According to the Kesef Mishneh's explanation there, and likewise the Beit Yosef.
A lawyer and a lawyer named him
