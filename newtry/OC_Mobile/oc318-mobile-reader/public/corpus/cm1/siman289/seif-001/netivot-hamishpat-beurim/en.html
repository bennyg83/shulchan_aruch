In something where gud o agud applies. See Sema, seif katan 5, who says guardians may not sell a minor's share merely to facilitate division. According to this, if beit din sees a definite benefit to the orphans in using gud o agud, they can make the claim. Beit din may sell orphan property where there is a clear benefit, as Beit Yosef explains and as Rama rules in siman 290, seif 13. Therefore the rule that one may not assert gud o agud against minors applies specifically where there is no clear benefit to the minors from doing so.
