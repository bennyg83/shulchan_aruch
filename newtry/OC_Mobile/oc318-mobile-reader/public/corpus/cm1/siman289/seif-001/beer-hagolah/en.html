Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Nachalot, halachah 4. The Maggid Mishneh cites Rav Nachman in the name of Shmuel in the third chapter of Kiddushin 42a.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh explains that if all the heirs are minors, why divide the property at all, since even after division it remains in the guardian's hands? And if all are adults, they have no need for a guardian.
Gimel) If they prefer merely to remain partners, beit din does not intervene for them.
Dalet) There, Rav Nachman himself says, etc.
Heh) As the Gemara answers at the beginning of 42b, following Rashi.
Vav) The Maggid Mishneh writes that in his view Rambam follows Rabbenu Yaakov, who holds that a guardian may divide property only according to an appraisal of beit din. This appears compelled by Rambam's view. It is like an appraisal by judges, where an error of one sixth affects the division.
