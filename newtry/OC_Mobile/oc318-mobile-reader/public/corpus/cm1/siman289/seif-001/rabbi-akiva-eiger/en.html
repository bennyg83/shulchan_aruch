[Shulchan Aruch] And they wished to divide the property. Note: If a deceased left adult sons and a pregnant wife, and the adult sons seek to divide the estate among themselves, beit din appoints a guardian to take the unborn child’s share until it becomes known whether the child is male or female. We do not, however, take into account the possibility of twins. Beit Yosef, end of this siman, in the name of a responsum of the Rashba.
