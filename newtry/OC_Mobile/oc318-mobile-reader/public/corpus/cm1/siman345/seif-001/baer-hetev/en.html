The two. The reason is that he admitted part of the claim. The claimant demands two borrowed cows that died during the borrowing period, making the borrower liable even for unavoidable losses. The defendant admits liability for one, and regarding the second he is obligated to take a Torah oath as one who admits part of a claim. Since he cannot swear, he must pay. This is unlike where he borrowed one cow and rented another, one died, and the other is still alive. There the dispute concerns only the one that died, for regarding the live cow he can say, "Here it is, take it," so there is no partial admission. Sema.
