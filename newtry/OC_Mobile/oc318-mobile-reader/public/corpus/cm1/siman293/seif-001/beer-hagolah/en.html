The wording of the Tur, from the baraita in Bava Kamma 118b as Abaye explains it. Rambam likewise writes this in chapter 6 of Hilkhot She'elah U'Pikadon, law 11.
There. The Maggid Mishneh derives from the baraita's phrase 'they may be demanded only in their place' that if the guardian wishes to return the deposit elsewhere he may do so even against the depositor's will. The restriction against return applies only in the wilderness. This is simple.
