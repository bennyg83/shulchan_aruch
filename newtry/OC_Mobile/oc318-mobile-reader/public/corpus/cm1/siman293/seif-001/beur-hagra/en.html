But in any event, etc. See Beer HaGolah and what I wrote there in siman 74:1.
Even if he gave it, etc. Gittin 75a, according to Rava's latter version. See Tosafos there, s.v. "miklal, etc.," and what I wrote in siman 120:2.
And if he deposited it, etc. Ra'avad explains the statement in the first chapter of Kiddushin 13a, "she thinks, if I throw it, etc.," as referring to a deposit made for a fixed time. See Rashba there, who disagrees and writes: is the depositary better than a worker, who may quit in the middle of the day? He gives another interpretation. See Sefer HaTerumos, gate 50, part 1, in the name of Ramban, and what I wrote in siman 74:3 in the gloss and siman 120:2.
