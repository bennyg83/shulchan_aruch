[Shulchan Aruch] During the stipulated period. Note: The source of this ruling is the Raavad, who derived it from the sugya in the first chapter of Kiddushin, 13a. See the novellae of Rashba there, who writes that this ruling requires study, since a worker may withdraw even during the agreed period. See also Urim VeTumim, siman 74, seif katan 6; Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer, siman 85, seif katan 6; and Responsa Maharach Shash, part 1, siman 82.
