And if when he deposited it, etc. According to the Rosh's explanation of the statement there, "if I wish, etc.," these are the Gemara's words, as though he explicitly stated the condition, etc. Rambam, however, explains like Rashi, and so does the Rif. They therefore omitted it because it is obvious. The reading of Halachos Gedolos is "and so he said to him, if, etc." See Baal HaMaor.
