At your responsibility. The Tur writes that if the custodian accepts responsibility, the depositor must accept the item even in the wilderness. Certainly the Rambam agrees, because in that case the wilderness is treated as a settled place. This is also evident from the Beit Yosef. This is the Sema's language. The Shach writes that Tosefta, chapter 10 of Bava Kamma, states that a lost object may be returned to its owner in any place. It seems to me the reason is that it was not knowingly deposited with the finder. However, this applies only when he returns it directly to the owner's hand. Otherwise, it must be placed in a guarded location within the owner's domain, as I wrote at the beginning of siman 267.
To go. Maharshal, chapter 10 of Bava Kamma, siman 54, rules that he must also say, "If you wish, I will return it to you." See there. Shach.
