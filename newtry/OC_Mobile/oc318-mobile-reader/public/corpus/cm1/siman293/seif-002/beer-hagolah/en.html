Tur seif 3, from the Gemara's qualification, 'It is obvious, it was needed only,' etc. The Rosh's version omits the phrase found in our Gemara, 'if I wish I can return them.' The Rif and Rambam omit the whole discussion, apparently because it is obvious that when the guardian says he too intends to travel into the wilderness, he may return it there. In my view they likely had our version, where the guardian had stipulated, 'if I wish,' etc.; this is obvious and therefore omitted.
