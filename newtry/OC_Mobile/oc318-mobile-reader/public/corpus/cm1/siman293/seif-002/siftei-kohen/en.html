He does not accept it from him, etc. The Tosefta, chapter 10 of Bava Kamma, states that he should not return it to him in the wilderness, etc., while a lost object is returned to its owner in any place. It appears to me that the reason is that the owner did not knowingly entrust it to him. Nevertheless, this applies specifically when he returns it directly into the owner's hand. Otherwise, he requires a guarded place within the owner's domain, as explained above at the beginning of siman 267.
I too, etc. Maharshal, chapter 10, siman 54, rules that he must also say, "If I wish, I will return it to you." See there.
