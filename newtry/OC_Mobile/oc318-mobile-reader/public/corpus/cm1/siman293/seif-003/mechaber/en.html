If the owner of a deposit goes overseas and the bailee wishes to sail or join a caravan, he may bring the deposit to beit din and thereby be released from further custodial responsibility. One person is not confined to a country because another left a deposit with him, and he cannot safely take it lest an accident occur for which he would be liable. Beit din deposits it with a trustworthy person to preserve the owner's property. Rama: If he could not leave it in an inhabited place and had to carry it through the wilderness because of danger, or transferred it to another bailee and an unavoidable accident occurred, some exempt the first bailee.
