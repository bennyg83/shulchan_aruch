The correct text is: and it is impossible for him to carry it, etc. One should not read, "and they do not tell him," because those who would instruct him are ordinarily beit din. If beit din told him to take the deposit with him into the wilderness, the risk would not remain upon the custodian, since he would be acting with authorization. The Rambam at the end of chapter 7 of Hilchot She'eilah and the Tur write simply: "and it is impossible for him to take it with him, lest an accident occur." Meaning, he may not do so on his own initiative, and beit din certainly would not authorize him to place the depositor's property at unnecessary risk. Better that beit din instruct him to leave it with them. The printed phrase "and they do not tell him" is therefore a scribal error. The Ir Shushan copied it without correction.
