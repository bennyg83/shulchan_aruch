Rambam reads, 'and it is impossible for him to transport it,' etc., and the Tur copies this. The Sema writes that one should not read 'and we do not say,' and discusses it at length. In my view the three words 'and he will be liable for its responsibility' are a scribal insertion, as they do not appear in the Tur manuscripts. This is preferable to reading 'it is impossible,' since it is possible if he wishes to expose his own property to danger.
