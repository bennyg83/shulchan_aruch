Unless he was accustomed, etc. Since the owner was accustomed to deposit with that person, he presumably wishes the property to remain with him now as well. At first glance, this applies only where the original custodian was not less trustworthy than the person with whom the owner customarily deposited. Otherwise, the owner may say that he entrusted it only to the first because of his superior reliability. Examine the authorities cited in the Sema.
