If the bailee sends the deposit through another person, whether saying 'Take it to him' or 'Give him his deposit,' he remains liable until it reaches the owner, because the owner did not appoint that messenger. He may therefore retract and recover it from the messenger. This applies when the bailee was not established as a denier. If he was established as a denier, the messenger immediately acquires possession on the depositor's behalf, and the bailee cannot recover it, though he remains liable until delivery. Some say the same where the depositor regularly entrusted property to this messenger, or sent word that the messenger is trustworthy, even without expressly releasing the bailee.
