Reuven hired a youth from Shimon. The printed wording obscures Rosh's responsum. The actual case was that Reuven hired the youth from Shimon, and Shimon undertook to pay whatever loss the youth caused because Shimon held property of the youth. Rosh ruled that this undertaking was part of the hiring terms, and conditions of employment require no separate kinyan. The correct text is therefore that Reuven hired a youth from Shimon, not merely that he hired a youth to serve him.
