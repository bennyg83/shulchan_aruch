The Mechaber already wrote this in siman 195, seif 9, as explained there. The Mordechai in Kiddushin, HaOmer, cites Raavyah regarding a woman who rented a house from Reuven for residence from Pesach until Shavuot, but moved in before Purim as an act of kindness or for another reason. Reuven then withdrew before Pesach. He ruled that Reuven could withdraw, because possession is effective only after the transaction is complete and the right of possession is immediately exercisable. If one acquired through one mode of kinyan and later waived it, the waiver is ineffective until it is returned through a new kinyan. Rivash, siman 510, writes so, cited by the Sema.
The Mechaber likewise writes this in siman 127, seif 32. See there.
