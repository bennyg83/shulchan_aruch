Rental, etc. Bava Kamma 79b and Bava Metzia 99b.
And there is no, etc. The law of ona'ah in rental derives from the law of sale, as stated there 56b. See siman 227:32. Nimukei Yosef writes there: Therefore we conclude from here that rental is like a sale regarding ona'ah. Accordingly, with movable property, etc. But with land, slaves, and documents, since their sale is not subject to ona'ah, neither is their rental, etc. There, seif 34, "he hired him, etc.," likewise proves that rental is subject to ona'ah only in the same categories as sale.
