[Shulchan Aruch] One suspected with respect to an oath. Note: The Bach above, siman 34, seif 12, writes in the name of the long Mordechai that one who intentionally damages another’s property, even if he intends to pay, is considered a wicked person guilty of monetary wrongdoing. See there. Knesset HaGedolah cites this here. However, Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter 6 of Bava Kamma, siman 30, writes: “You should know that we do not find that one who directly damages another’s property is disqualified from testimony.” End quote.
