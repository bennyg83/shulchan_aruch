We do not listen to him. This is derived from the rule concerning hesset where the person obligated to swear is suspected: he is exempt, and the opposing party does not simply get to insist that the suspected person swear. If the claimant could compel him to swear anyway, why would the claimant prefer total exemption to an oath? The reason is that we do not permit a person we expect to swear falsely to utter Hashem's name in vain. The entire world trembled when false oaths were introduced. Sema. This is straightforward.
