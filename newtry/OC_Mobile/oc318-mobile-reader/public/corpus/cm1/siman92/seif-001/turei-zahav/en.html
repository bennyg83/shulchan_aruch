(Shulchan Aruch, seif 3) “Perhaps he has an old loan.” Sema emends this to “perhaps there is a doubtful old loan.” It appears to me that no emendation is required. The Gemara first asks why an oath administered against the opposing party is effective if the person is suspected of monetary robbery. It answers that perhaps he has an old loan against the claimant, so he is not truly a robber. The Gemara then asks why an oath is needed at all and answers that perhaps the old loan is itself doubtful. The element of doubt is necessary only to explain why an oath is imposed. The Tur here is not explaining why an oath is needed; he is explaining only why the person is not legally suspect and why an oath remains effective. For that purpose, it is enough to say that perhaps he has an old loan. This appears plainly correct.
