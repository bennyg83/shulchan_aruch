One who is suspected regarding oaths is not administered an oath, etc. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is learned from the beginning of chapter Shevuat HaDayanim, where it appears that a person suspected regarding oaths is not even administered an oath of hesset. It also appears there that even if the claimant wishes him to swear, he is not administered the oath, for with hesset the opposing party does not take an oath and collect, and ordinarily the claimant would certainly prefer that the defendant swear. This is clear. The reason appears to be that since this person is suspected of uttering Hashem's name falsely, administering an oath to him involves desecration of Hashem's name. Therefore, even though by not swearing he may be exempt without any oath, as the Tur and Mechaber state below in seif 11, we nevertheless do not heed the claimant who wants him sworn. The Ir Shushan writes that his disqualification is derived from the verse, 'Do not place your hand with a wicked person,' but he presses the verse very far. I do not know what forced him to do so, and his discussion there is not clear.
