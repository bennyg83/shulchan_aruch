The wording of the Rambam there, law 4, cited by the Tur. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this law is not explicitly stated in the Gemara, but Rabbeinu derived it from the rule that the opposing party swears and collects. The opposing party never collects without an oath unless both parties are suspected of false swearing, etc. Therefore, whenever the oath is based on an uncertain claim and the claimant does not assert certainty, he does not collect.
