He cannot swear and collect, etc. According to Rambam, whose view the Maggid Mishneh and Mechaber follow, the custodian is exempt in such a case.
This excludes Rabbi Yeshayah and Ramah, who hold that because he is obligated to swear but cannot swear, he must pay, as the Tur records.Therefore, if he claimed, etc. The word 'therefore' is not precise. It would have been enough to write simply, 'and if he claimed.' In the Perishah I explained that Rambam's wording links it to the preceding principle, but the second ruling is not logically derived from the first in every respect.
