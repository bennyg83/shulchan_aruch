A document against orphans. See Baer Heitev, and see Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, siman 6, regarding a non Jew who presents a document against orphans. He discusses the matter at length and concludes, among other things: "As for the practical ruling, I have already written that it appears from the Gemara, and it is likewise explicit in the responsa attributed to the Ramban, siman 22, that we do not apply the law of one disqualified from swearing to a non Jew who comes to collect by means of a document in his possession. It appears that the reason is that one certainly would not repay a non Jew, nor place money with him as security, until receiving the document back, since he knows that the non Jew can later circumvent him and collect money without any oath at all." See there what he writes concerning the words of Tumim, siman 110, seif katan 2, on this matter.
