“The little one in the way that he is called a man in the sign of a small claim:
The opposing party swears. This refers to a Torah oath. For a rabbinic oath we do not make an additional enactment upon an enactment. This is straightforward, and the Shulchan Aruch relies on what it wrote earlier. The same is true regarding a claimant who swears and collects, each authority according to his view.
