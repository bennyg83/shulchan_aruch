And he produces a promissory note against the orphans, etc. The law is lenient with orphans, so that money is not removed from their possession, even from orphans who inherited from other orphans, where they cannot take the oath that would have been incumbent upon their father had he sought to collect from them, and the oath that they were not instructed by their father is insufficient. All the more so, where the lender himself comes to collect from orphans and he is suspected and therefore cannot take the oath, he cannot collect from them.
