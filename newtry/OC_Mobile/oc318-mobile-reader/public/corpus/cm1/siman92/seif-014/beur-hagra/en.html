He always remains in that status, etc. Makkot 23a says that once he has received lashes he is again 'your brother.' As explained in siman 34, seif 29, this applies only where the offense was not monetary. Here too, it applies to a vain oath, an uttered oath, and the like; one who denied money must manifest repentance. Thus witnesses must establish both that he received lashes and that he repented properly. See citation 34.
