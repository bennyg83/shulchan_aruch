Until he receives lashes before beit din. Meaning, he must also repent, as the Mechaber concludes: 'If there are witnesses that he received lashes and repented,' etc. At times, however, no separate act of repentance is required. For example, where he ate a prohibited food, when he receives lashes and suffers physically in place of the pleasure he derived from the prohibition, that itself constitutes his repentance, as explained above at the end of siman 34. See the Perishah.
