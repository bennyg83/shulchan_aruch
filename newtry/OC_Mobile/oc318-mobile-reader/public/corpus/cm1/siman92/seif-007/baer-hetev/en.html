And collects. Regarding why we do not apply here the rule that one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear must pay, see the Sema and Taz. The Shach writes that there is no distinction between one who admits part of the claim and one contradicted by a single witness. If the obligated party is disqualified from taking an oath, the opposing party swears and collects. One cannot say that the witness should exempt him as a supporting witness, because once the witness has created the oath obligation he can no longer exempt the litigant from that oath, as explained above in siman 86, seif 11.
To adjudicate. The Shach writes that this appears to be the primary ruling from the Gemara, where Rav Nachman actually ruled that they divide, and he cites many authorities who rule accordingly.
They divide. The Shach writes that the first opinion is the primary ruling. See there for his proof that in his view there is actually no dispute. See also the Sema and Taz on this matter.
