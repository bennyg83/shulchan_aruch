Ibid., Shach, seif katan 14, "where she became obligated to swear concerning her ketubah." Note: above, siman 82, seif katan 19, the Shach wrote that according to the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, who hold that a ketubah is d'rabbanan, a widow who is suspect loses. See there. Sanhedrin 108a appears to indicate that Rashi too holds a ketubah to be d'rabbanan. See Tosafot there. One must say, however, that Rashi goes further than the Tur, for Rashi holds that even with a validated document of rabbinic force a suspect party does not lose. The Tur and Shulchan Aruch adopted an intermediate position, distinguishing between a Torah-level document and a rabbinic ketubah.
