And some say specifically, etc. See Shevuot 46b and Rashi there. The first opinion understands the omission from the mishnah as excluding only the formal category, as in Bava Kamma, but one who fails to fulfill his oath is nevertheless disqualified like one who eats forbidden food. Ran so explains. Tosafot cite Ketubot 85a, where a woman suspected of swearing falsely was subjected to a reversed oath, and Behag explains that she had uttered a vain oath and failed to fulfill it.
