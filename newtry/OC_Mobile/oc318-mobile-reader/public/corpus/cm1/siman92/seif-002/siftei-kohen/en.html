One form is the oath concerning testimony. This means that beit din adjured a person who knew testimony for another to testify, and he answered under oath that he knew nothing. The case is one in which the oath took effect in the manner required by law. See the laws of the oath concerning testimony. Cited cross-references in the source include: 30.
