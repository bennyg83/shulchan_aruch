She collects without an oath. See Baer Heitev and Shach, seif katan 14, who writes that this is also implied by Rashi in Ketubot 85. At first glance this requires explanation, because with a ketubah, which is rabbinic, all agree that if the woman is disqualified from swearing she loses her claim, as the Shach himself states in siman 82. See Teshuvot Chatam Sofer, siman 75, for discussion.
