For he has a validated document in his possession. See Shach, seif katan 14, in his discussion of the principal law governing a shopkeeper on his ledger. As I wrote above in siman 91, only where the employer pays a single payment is the shopkeeper's payment due by strict law. Where he must make two payments, all agree that the shopkeeper's payment is only by enactment, and therefore payment is not made to the shopkeeper's heirs. Accordingly, here too, when two payments are being made, the shopkeeper is not paid if he is disqualified from taking an oath.
