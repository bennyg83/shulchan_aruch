Maggid Mishneh in Hilchot Ishut chapter 23 holds that a formal kinyan made by a married woman is effective even to remove the husband's right to fruits, because the husband's power in his wife's property should not be greater than that of an ordinary partner in his share. If one partner tells another, "I have no claim or dealings regarding this field," and a kinyan is made, the other partner acquires. The Tur, however, holds that with ordinary partners there is no distinction between the body and the fruits, whereas regarding a wife the kinyan suffices only so that her sale and gift are valid. See Kesef Mishneh and Bach.
