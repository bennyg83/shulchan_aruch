There, Sema, seif katan 21, s.v. 'his opponent.' Note: Tosafot, Bava Kamma 108a, s.v. 'and two,' sought to prove from this that one suspected of swearing falsely is not disqualified by Torah law. This proof is not decisive, as is implied by the two answers of Tosafot there, and likewise by Tosafot in Bava Metzia, s.v. 'without payment.' Only a violent taker is disqualified rabbinically. This is also explicit from the wording of Tosafot, Ketubot 11b, end of s.v. 'and in all'; see there. As for the case in Bava Kamma 108a cited above, it can be explained that he repented between the two oaths.
