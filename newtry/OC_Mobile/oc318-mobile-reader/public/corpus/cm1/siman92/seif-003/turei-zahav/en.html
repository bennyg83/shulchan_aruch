(Seif 6) “But if he says it with certainty.” The Tur cites the first clause in the name of the Rosh and the latter clause in the name of Rashba, and writes that they disagree. The Shulchan Aruch here attempts a compromise by saying that Rashba speaks where the claimant asserts certainty. This is insufficient, because anyone could simply claim certain knowledge and publicly shame the other person. It appears to me that Rashba speaks where there is some indication supporting the claim, namely that the witnesses whom he says know the matter evade giving testimony. One who examines Rashba’s wording carefully will see this. The Mechaber’s phrase “if they know with certainty” may likewise be understood in this way.
