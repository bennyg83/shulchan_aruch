The Bach writes that this applies specifically where the claimant brings witnesses before beit din and the witnesses themselves tell the court that they refuse to testify until a cherem is imposed. This is explicit in Responsa Rashba cited by the Beit Yosef in siman 28, seif 4, and this is the accepted ruling. The Ri similarly explains in chapter 2 of Ketubot 27b in the incident of Mari bar Isak. So too the Tur, siman 28 seif 6, and the Rama there, seif 5.
