One who became obligated to take an oath, etc. This follows Kiddushin 28a concerning the extent of an oath obligation and the incident of the person placed under a ban.
But if, etc.; and those people, etc. Since witnesses are obligated to testify, even a single witness bears that duty, as stated at the beginning of HaKones, although it is uncertain whether the claimant will ultimately swear. Here too, although the claimant does not know who the witnesses are, a general ban may be pronounced against them, as in every uncertain claim.
