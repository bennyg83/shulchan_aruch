If a person became obligated to take an oath to another and asks that an unspecified ban be proclaimed in the synagogue against anyone who knows that the other party violated an oath, the person making this request deserves a reprimand. But if he says with certainty that witnesses know the other party violated the oath and refuse to testify until a ban is proclaimed in the synagogue, he may have the ban proclaimed there. {Rama: Those who know must testify, as with all other testimony, for Scripture states, 'If he does not tell.'}
