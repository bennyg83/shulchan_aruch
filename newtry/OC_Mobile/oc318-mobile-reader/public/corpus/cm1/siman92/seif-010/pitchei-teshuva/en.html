This also appears to me from Rashi's commentary in the chapter HaKotev, Ketubot 85a, where he explains that one should not say that the case concerns a woman who had become obligated to swear regarding her ketubah, etc. At first glance this is difficult to understand, because with respect to a ketubah, which is rabbinic, everyone agrees that if she is disqualified from swearing she loses her claim, as the Shach himself writes above in siman 82, seif katan 19. See there, and see Responsa Chatam Sofer, siman 75, concerning this matter.
