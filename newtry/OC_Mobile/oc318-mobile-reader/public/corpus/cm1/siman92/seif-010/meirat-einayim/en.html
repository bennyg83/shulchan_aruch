Among those who swear on an uncertain claim. Meaning partners, sharecroppers, and the other categories listed at the beginning of the following siman.Because this person did not become obligated in an oath, etc. Here the Mechaber uses his own formulation rather than the exact words of Rambam or the Tur.
Rambam writes that the defendant did not become obligated in a Torah oath because the claimant did not assert a definite claim against him upon which such an oath could be imposed. The point is that an oath arising from a special rabbinic enactment on an uncertain claim has a different structure from an oath imposed in response to a definite Torah-law claim. That difference affects what happens when the person is suspected of false oaths and cannot take the oath himself.
