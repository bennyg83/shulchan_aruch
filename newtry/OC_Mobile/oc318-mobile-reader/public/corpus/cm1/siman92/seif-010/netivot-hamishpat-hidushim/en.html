Where there is a validated document. In the case of an injured or robbed claimant, the opposing party takes a hesset oath and is exempt. Regarding the shopkeeper, the Shach holds that he collects without an oath. See Beurim: this applies specifically where the householder pays only one payment, because the shopkeeper's payment is then required by strict law. Therefore, if the shopkeeper is suspected, he nevertheless collects without an oath. But where the householder must pay two payments, he is exempt from paying the shopkeeper.
