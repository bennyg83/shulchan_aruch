They contradicted them or made them plotting witnesses. Contradiction means that two witnesses say, "So-and-so borrowed from so-and-so on a certain day," and two others say that throughout that entire day the borrower or lender was with them and no loan took place. Hazamah means that two witnesses testify that Reuven borrowed one maneh from Shimon on a certain day in a certain place, and two others say to those witnesses, "But you yourselves were with us that day in another place." The law of contradiction was explained above at the beginning of siman 31: the testimony concerning this loan is void and the alleged borrower is exempt, but for other matters each of the two pairs may testify independently. With hazamah, however, the first witnesses are disqualified and the second pair remains fully valid. The reason is that in hazamah the later pair testifies about the persons of the first witnesses themselves. The first witnesses are no more believed to deny what is testified about their own presence than persons accused by two witnesses of desecrating Shabbat or killing someone are believed merely to say, "We did not do it." The Tur explains this fully. The Mechaber shortened the matter and wrote no separate law of hazamah here. His opening phrase, "they contradicted them or made them plotting witnesses, he is exempt," equates the two only insofar as the person against whom they testified is exempt.
