(Shulchan Aruch: but they are believed regarding themselves, etc.) The Tur writes similarly in siman 46, seif 37. Why, then, does he cite Rabbenu Hananel here as ruling that witnesses who say, “We testified falsely,” are exempt from payment? Rabbenu Hananel means they are exempt from the statutory liability of “as he conspired,” which does not apply through self-admission, as Tosafot explain in the first chapter of Makkot. He does not address compensation for loss already caused by their false testimony, which may not yet have occurred. The Tur in siman 46 and the Mechaber here address liability as indirect tortfeasors for loss actually caused, which they must pay.
