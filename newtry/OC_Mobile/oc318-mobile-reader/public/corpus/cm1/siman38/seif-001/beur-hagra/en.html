(Collected note) Therefore the Tur wrote, etc. His words are puzzling, for this is no reason to write the amount of the payment for hazamah. Everything written there, and see Beer HaGolah, follows the Rosh at the end of chapter 1 of Bava Kamma and the Tur at the end of siman 349. The Beit Yosef, however, omitted laws 4 and 5 in siman 350 because he follows the Ramah's view, and therefore he omitted them here as well. Nor is there a practical difference concerning placing him under ban, because according to all opinions he is not placed under ban in this case, as the Sema writes in siman 1, seif katan 18, and the Shach there. Knesset HaGedolah writes similarly. See Sema there. Therefore the Mechaber omitted the rule both here and there. (End of collected note.)
