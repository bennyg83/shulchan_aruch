That Reuven. See the Ran's responsum there and above siman 72; consider carefully. The Sema writes that if Reuven acted this way on his own initiative and did not make one matter conditional upon the other, even the Ramah, who disagrees above, agrees here. The plain language of the Rama does not imply this, and Maharik there likewise indicates that there is no distinction. Even if he made the one conditional on the other, since the pledge is not in his hands he is not its custodian. Shach.
