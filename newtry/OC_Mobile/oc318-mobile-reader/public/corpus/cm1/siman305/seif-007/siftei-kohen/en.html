For example, where Reuven left collateral. See Responsa Ran, siman 19, and above, siman 72, and examine the matter carefully. The Sema writes that this applies if he acted on his own initiative. The plain language of the Rama does not indicate this, and Maharik likewise implies that there is no such distinction. Even if he made one matter dependent upon the other, since the collateral is not in his possession he is not its custodian, and the Ramah also agrees.
