If one says, 'Lend me your light cloak and take my heavy one,' and robbers come and take one of them, the remaining cloak belongs to its original owner. Rama: This is treated as reciprocal borrowing and therefore as borrowing while the owner is in the borrower's service. However, if the borrowed items are not in the borrowers' hands but with third parties, such as where Reuven left collateral with Levi for Shimon and Shimon lent an item to Reuven, this is not borrowing while the owner is in his service, because Shimon is not guarding for Reuven. They are instead paid bailees for one another.
