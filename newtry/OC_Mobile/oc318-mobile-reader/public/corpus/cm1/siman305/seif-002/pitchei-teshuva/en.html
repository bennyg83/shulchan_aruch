To become liable even for unavoidable accidents. See Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, siman 21, cited above in siman 129, seif 1, under the words "for each day." He indicates that where a paid custodian was required to provide collateral, his stipulation to accept liability even for unavoidable accidents is not effective without a kinyan. The ordinary rule that such enhanced liability can be accepted by words alone rests on the benefit the custodian receives from the reputation of being trusted. Here that rationale does not apply, for the very requirement of collateral shows that the owner did not rely on his trustworthiness. In my humble opinion, this point is also evident from Hagahot Ashri at the end of the chapter HaPoalim. See there.
