Seif 6. One says to another, 'Guard for me and I will guard for you.' Note: See my work on Orach Chaim, year 610, Hilchot Shabbat, siman 307, where I discussed this law with respect to Shabbat in the case of one who says, 'Guard for me the produce that is within your Shabbat boundary.' I concluded there that on Shabbat, in such a case, each does not acquire the status of a paid custodian toward the other. I also discuss there whether a finder guarding lost property on Shabbat has the status of a paid custodian.
