He must pay the owner, etc., and then he pays the owner. The Mechaber copies the Tur's language here. Above at the end of siman 294 he copied the Rambam's language from the end of Hilchot She'eilah. There the Tur did not write that the custodian must first pay the owner, because the Rambam himself did not say so. I explained there that according to Rashi and the Tur, once the thief is identified the custodian must pay the depositor and then recover from the thief. The Maggid Mishneh understands the Rambam differently, that the custodian's obligation is only to trouble himself to pursue the thief in judgment. The Beit Yosef suggests that perhaps the Rambam too agrees that the custodian must ultimately pay the owner, and presumably for this reason the Mechaber writes the Tur's wording here. But the Rambam did not actually say this. According to what I proved in siman 294, the Rambam follows the Maggid Mishneh rather than Rashi. The Mechaber's formulations therefore do not sit perfectly together.
