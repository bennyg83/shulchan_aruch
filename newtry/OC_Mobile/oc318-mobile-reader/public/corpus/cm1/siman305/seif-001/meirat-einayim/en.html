If he claims it was stolen, or even says, "I was negligent and I will pay," etc. The phrase "I will pay" applies also to the claim that it was stolen, because a paid custodian is liable for theft and loss. The wording "or even says, I was negligent" is awkward in the Mechaber because why should negligence be the greater novelty? He copied the Tur in abbreviated form. The Tur concludes that if the thief is later found, the double payment belongs to the custodian. As explained above in siman 295, the Sages assessed ordinary intent and understood that owners are pleased when a custodian voluntarily pays the principal after theft without forcing them to litigate. In return, they transfer to him the potential double payment if the thief is later found. The case where he says, "I was negligent and will pay," is the greater novelty because his own conduct was less trustworthy, yet since he is willing to pay without litigation he still acquires the double payment.
