[Shulchan Aruch] And the thief was identified. Note: If the thief returned the item to the guardian's house and it was afterward lost through negligence, this is an unresolved question in Bava Kamma 108: do we say his guardianship had ended and the guardian is exempt, or since the item returned it returned to his guardianship? Rambam records the question at the end of Hilchot Sheelah U’Pikadon and concludes that the guardian is exempt, but if the owners seized payment it is not removed from them. Yam Shel Shlomo likewise cites the question, but rules that if the owners seized payment it is removed from them. From the omission of the Tur and Rosh, however, it appears to me that they agree with the Ramah cited in Shitah Mekubetzet there, that according to Abaye and Rava, who hold that a paid guardian may litigate with the thief, and similarly an unpaid guardian may do so if he wishes, his guardianship certainly did not terminate. We rule in accordance with them. See there.
