In his possession. Although he already swore that an unavoidable event occurred or that the animal died, he must nevertheless swear that it is not in his possession, because if he made unauthorized use of it, from then on it is deemed in his domain even regarding unavoidable losses. Thus the phrase means that he must include in his oath that he did not make unauthorized use of it, in which case it is not in his domain. See below, siman 295, and what I wrote there, seif katan 5.
