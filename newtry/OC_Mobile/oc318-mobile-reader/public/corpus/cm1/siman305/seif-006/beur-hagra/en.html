And some say, etc. Tur cites Ramah, who explains that the Gemara's question, 'Why, safekeeping, etc.,' applies to all the clauses. Rambam also holds this view, as the Shulchan Aruch states below regarding one who says, 'Guard for me, etc.' Rosh likewise rules this way in a responsum, as stated in seif 7. This also appears from the incident of the two men in the Gemara, where the Sages said to him, etc.
And some say that 'lend me, etc.' This is Rashi's view there, s.v. 'safekeeping,' and Tur agrees. In the incident there, Rashi carefully writes, s.v. 'a loan,' that he carried, etc. See Beit Yosef.
