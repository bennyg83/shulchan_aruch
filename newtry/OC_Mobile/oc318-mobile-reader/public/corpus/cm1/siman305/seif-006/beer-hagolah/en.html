The wording of Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Sekhirut. The Maggid Mishneh cites the baraita, 'Guard for me and I will guard for you; lend me and I will lend you,' etc. Rambam appears to understand Rav Pappa's qualification, 'Guard for me today and I will guard for you tomorrow,' as applying to the entire baraita. Therefore he writes specifically, 'lend me today and I will lend you tomorrow.' But if he says, 'and I will lend you now,' it is shemirah beba'alim.
