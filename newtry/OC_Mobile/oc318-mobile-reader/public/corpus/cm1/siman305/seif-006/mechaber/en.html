If one says to another, 'Guard for me and I will guard for you,' and some say the same of, 'Lend to me and I will lend to you,' this is guarding while the owner is in the borrower's service. If he says, 'Guard for me today and I will guard for you tomorrow,' or, 'Lend to me today and I will lend to you tomorrow,' or combines guarding and lending on different days, each is a paid bailee for the other. Rama: Some say that 'Lend to me and I will lend to you,' or one guards while the other lends, even on the same day, makes each a paid bailee and is not considered guarding while the owner is in his service.
