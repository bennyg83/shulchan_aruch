He stated it without qualification, etc. So rule Responsa Maharam, Hagahot Maimoniyot, and the Agudah. The Sema says that if a paid custodian or borrower declared at the outset, “I accept no responsibility,” he is exempt from every level of liability and is not even a gratuitous custodian; but if he first accepted custody, he must later use the language “on condition.” I do not understand this distinction. The Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot explicitly state that a declaration made at the time of acceptance, “I accept no responsibility,” suffices even without the words “on condition,” and the Mechaber rules so above. If the parties have already separated after full acceptance, even saying “on condition” cannot retroactively remove liability. The general rule that one who says “on condition” means “from now” is irrelevant here.
