He said simply, "on condition," etc. Responsa Maimoniyot, Sefer Mishpatim siman 11, writes: If a paid custodian or borrower says at the outset, "I do not accept responsibility upon myself," he is exempt from all liability and does not even become an unpaid custodian. This must apply specifically when he says so at the beginning. Then it works even without the formal phrase "on condition." If he first accepts the custody and only afterward seeks to limit his liability, he must use an effective condition, namely "on condition."
