He said without qualification, etc. As Menachot 109a states: 'He has fulfilled his obligation; it is as though, etc.' Although one who says, 'I undertake an olah,' is liable even for accidents, this formulation exempts him even for negligence. One might have said that it is as though he stipulated that he would offer this particular animal, yet the Sages' formulation, 'it is as though he said, etc.,' shows that the same principle applies here.
