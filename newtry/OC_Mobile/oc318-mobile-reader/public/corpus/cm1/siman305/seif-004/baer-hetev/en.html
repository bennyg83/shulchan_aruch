Without qualification. Responsa Maimoniyot states: A paid custodian or borrower who said initially, "I do not accept responsibility upon myself," is exempt from all liability and is not even an unpaid custodian. The Sema writes that this must apply specifically where he said so initially; then it is effective even without saying "on condition." But if he initially accepted the guarding, he must later say "on condition." I do not understand his words. If he means after they separated following acceptance of the guarding, it is obvious that even "on condition" is ineffective. The Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot imply no distinction between saying "on condition" and saying simply, "I do not accept responsibility." If he means at the moment he accepts the guarding, that is incorrect, for it is explicit there that if, at the moment of acceptance, he says, "I do not accept," he is not even an unpaid custodian. The Mechaber likewise rules in siman 72, seif 7. The general rule that one who says "on condition" is regarded as saying "from now" is irrelevant here. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 141. Ibid.
