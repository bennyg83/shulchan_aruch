(Seif 7) It is not a loan together with the owner. This means that if the article lent to Reuven was lost while in Reuven’s possession, Reuven must pay for it. He cannot say to Shimon: You are considered lent to me because you are guarding for me everything that I lent to you and delivered to Levi on your behalf. Shimon himself is not guarding that particular utensil; Levi is.
