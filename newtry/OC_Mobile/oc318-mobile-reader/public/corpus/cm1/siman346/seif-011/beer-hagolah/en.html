kuf) The language of the Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilkhot She'eilah, law 8, regarding Rami bar Chama's unresolved question.
reish) Ibid., in the Rambam. The Maggid Mishneh writes that although negligence while the owner is present is exempt, here it is uncertain whether this case is considered one in which the owner is present. The Rambam's view is that a negligent guardian is like one who directly causes damage, as he wrote in chapter 2 of Hilkhot Sekhirut. Since he is treated as a damager and there is no clear proof to exempt him, he must pay. The Maggid Mishneh adds that others disagree. This resolves the Tur's objection to the Rambam.
shin) The Tur and Maggid Mishneh there cite those who disagree.
