He borrowed from two partners, etc. This too is an unresolved question in the Gemara. The Gemara asks: do we require all of the owners to be with him, which is not the case, or perhaps he is exempt at least regarding the share of the owner who is with him? Likewise, where partners borrowed, do we require the entire borrower to be working for the owner, which is not the case, or perhaps the borrower is exempt at least with respect to the half belonging to the one who is with him? End of the Gemara's words.
If he was negligent, some obligate him. Their reason is that negligence is treated like actively causing damage, and unless we have clear proof that the negligence occurred while the owner was with him, he must pay. The Tur holds that no distinction should be made between negligence while the owner is with him and theft or loss while the owner is with him. The Tur and Mechaber record the same dispute above at the end of siman 66. See there.
