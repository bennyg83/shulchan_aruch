If one tells an agent, 'Go and place yourself in the borrower's service together with my cow,' this is not borrowing while the owner is in his service, because the verse requires the owner himself, not an agent. Some disagree and apply the rule that a person's agent is like himself. If he tells his Canaanite slave to do so, it is considered borrowing with the owner present, because the slave's hand is like his master's. Rama: If the slave entered service without the master's knowledge, it is not considered so.
