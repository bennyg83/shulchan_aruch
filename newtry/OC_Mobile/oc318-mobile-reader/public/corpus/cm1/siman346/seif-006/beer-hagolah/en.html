The wording of Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilkhot She'elah, law 5, from Ravina's question to Rav Ashi. Rav Ashi resolved that it is the dispute of Rav Oshaya. Rambam rules like Rabbenu Chananel and Rif, who rule like Rabbi Yoshiyah, from Rava's statement, 'It is reasonable that the hand of a slave is like the hand of his master,' there, 96a.
It's called "Arise, shine" and so on
There in the cited law. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is obvious, for the slave has no power to create a liability for his master. This is what is meant by saying to his slave, 'Go out and be borrowed together with my cow.'
