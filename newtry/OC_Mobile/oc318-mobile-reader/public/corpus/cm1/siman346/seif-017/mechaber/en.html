A woman who borrowed an item and then married is followed by a husband who is treated as a purchaser, not a bailee. Therefore, if the item is an animal and dies, he is exempt even though he uses it throughout the loan period, even if negligent. Rama: He is not exempt because the owner is in his service, because the item is only borrowed by the wife and not hers. Similarly, if a robber deposits or lends the robbed item, the borrower remains liable for unavoidable accidents even if the robber served him, because the rule applies only to the true owner. The wife must pay when she later has assets. If she informed her husband that the item was borrowed, he assumes her liability, provided he used it after being informed or explicitly accepted responsibility as borrower.
