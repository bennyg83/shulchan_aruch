To mate it or to display himself with it, etc., this is doubtful. These are unresolved questions in the Gemara. If he borrows an animal merely for mating, is that considered a normal beneficial borrowing because he has use from it, or not? If he borrows property merely to appear wealthy so people will extend him credit, does that count as use of the borrowed item itself? If the value borrowed is less than one perutah, is that legally money for purposes of borrowing? If two animals together are worth a perutah but each alone is worth less, do we look at the borrower and lender as a whole or at each animal separately? The Gemara leaves these unresolved. Rashi and the Tur understand the questions primarily as whether the borrower has the law of a borrower and is liable for unavoidable accidents. The Rambam also understands them regarding whether the exemption of borrowing with the owner present applies. The Mechaber states the cases without resolving which interpretive framework one adopts.
