Doubt. The Sema writes that all these are unresolved questions in the Gemara and concludes that they end with teiku. This is not correct. They are questions that were not resolved, and the practical difference is that if the lender seized payment, it is not removed from his possession. If the Gemara had concluded with teiku, it would be removed from him, as stated in siman 25, seif 2 according to the Rosh and Tur. I wrote there likewise in the name of Maharshal, and the Tur explicitly writes here that regarding unavoidable accidents, if the lender seized payment we do not remove it from him. I later discussed this matter at length in my work Tekfo Kohen. Shach.
