To mate with it, etc. The Sema cites the Gemara's unresolved questions and says that they conclude with teiku. This is imprecise. The Gemara and the authorities describe an unresolved question, but the text does not formally conclude with teiku. The practical difference is that if the lender seized property, it is not removed from him. Had the Gemara concluded with teiku, the property would be removed according to the Rosh and Tur, as explained in siman 25. The Tur here expressly writes that even regarding accidental loss, if the lender seized property it is not removed from him. I discuss this at length in Tekfo Kohen.
This remains uncertain, etc. The uncertainty concerns liability for accidents. He is nevertheless liable for theft and loss, because the borrower receives a peruta's worth of benefit and is therefore at least a paid custodian. The Rosh writes this in the name of the Ramah. It follows that a paid custodian is not liable for theft and loss unless the compensation is worth at least a peruta.
