(Collected note) Some say that during the time, etc. This follows the statement there 96a, “Rava said: according to the sharpness,” etc. Although she is not constantly engaged in his work, she is constantly obligated to him. For this reason the Raavad writes in chapter 21 of Hilchos Ishus that a wife who breaks her husband's utensil is exempt because she is considered hired to him at all times. The Rambam, however, gives the reason of the Yerushalmi in the chapter HaKosev, cited by Tosafos there 96b, s.v. “Rabbi Eliezer,” etc. The Maggid Mishneh writes that specifically she is hired to her husband, whereas the husband is not hired to her except while occupied with her needs. So write the Rashba and the other poskim; see Nimukei Yosef in Bava Metzia there.
