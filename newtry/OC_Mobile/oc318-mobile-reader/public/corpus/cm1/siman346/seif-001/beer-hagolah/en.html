b) The dispute of Rav Acha and Ravina on 95a, with the ruling following the view lenient toward the borrower. Tosafot write likewise there in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel, because whenever Rav Acha and Ravina disagree, the halakhah follows the lenient view. Although this results in a stringency for the depositor, it is regarded as a leniency because it exempts the party in possession.
