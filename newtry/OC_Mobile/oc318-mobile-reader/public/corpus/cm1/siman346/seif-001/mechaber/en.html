The exemption of borrowing while the owner is in the borrower's service, all its laws, and a husband's status regarding his wife's property. It contains nineteen seifim.
If the lender was working for the borrower when the borrowed item was drawn into the borrower's possession, the borrower is exempt even for negligent theft or loss, as the verse states, 'If its owner is with him, he shall not pay.' It makes no difference whether the owner was serving through a loan of his labor or as a paid worker, whether in the same task as the borrowed object or another task. Even if he said, 'Give me water,' and the lender did so while lending the cow, this is borrowing while the owner is in his service. If the cow was drawn first and only afterward he gave water, it is not.
