Seif 1. But if he first performed meshichah and only afterward the lender gave him something to drink, this is not considered borrowing while the owner is in his service, etc. Note: It appears to me that this is so only according to the Mechaber's consistent view that a guardian does not become liable for unavoidable loss until the time of meshichah. Accordingly, only where he first performed meshichah and the lender afterward gave him drink is it not borrowing while the owner is in his service; before meshichah it would be so considered. According to the Rama, however, who holds that from the moment the owners withdraw from the property the guardian becomes liable for unavoidable loss even before meshichah, then even if meshichah had not yet been performed, once the owners had withdrawn and afterward the lender gave him drink, it would not be considered borrowing while the owner is in his service. The Rama's silence here therefore requires study. Understand this well.
