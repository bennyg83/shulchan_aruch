Even if he was negligent, etc. Rambam and Tur write this. Rambam understands the Gemara to exempt a purchaser even from negligence; Rashba and most authorities agree. Tosafot and Rosh hold that negligence remains liable, except that ordinary spousal possession is exempt because it is negligence while the owner is in service. The distinction matters for a purchaser. Tur elsewhere follows Rosh, although here he quotes Rambam's language. Darkei Moshe inferred otherwise, but that inference is not compelling. The principal ruling supports Rambam. See simanim 8 and 32.
