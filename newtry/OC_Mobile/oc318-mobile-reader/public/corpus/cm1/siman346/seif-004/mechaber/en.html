If one lends or rents his animal for carrying a load and accompanies the borrower in order to assist with supporting and loading it, this is considered borrowing "with the owner," even if the owner volunteered his assistance. This is contrary to those who disagree. Mordechai, beginning of chapter HaShoel. If he accompanies it only to ensure that the borrower does not overload it, this is not considered borrowing with the owner. Likewise, if the owner holds the stirrup for him, he does so for the benefit of his animal and it is not considered borrowing with the owner. Mordechai there in the name of Ravyah.
