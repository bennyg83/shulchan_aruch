If one borrowed while the owner was in his service and later rented the item without the owner present, he is exempt because the rental follows the borrowing. But if he rented while the owner was present and then borrowed without him, or borrowed with him, then rented and borrowed again without him, or rented with him and then borrowed and rented again without him, these are unresolved cases regarding guarding while the owner is in service. Rama: Some say that renting with the owner present and then borrowing without him remains guarding while the owner is in service.
