Doubt. The Tur writes that if the lender seized payment, we remove it from him. The Beit Yosef and Bach write that this is a scribal error and should read, "we do not remove it," as above in seif 11. In my view, however, one can say that the Tur wrote this intentionally here because these questions conclude with teiku. The Rosh already wrote in chapter Keitzad HaRegel that where a question concludes with teiku, seizure is effective, as the Tur writes in his name in siman 388, seif 1 and seif 4, and similarly in siman 390 at the end of seif 1 in the name of Ri. This differs from seif 11, where the question does not conclude with teiku, as I wrote there. I later found an old parchment manuscript of the Tur that reads, "we do not remove it." I likewise concluded in Tekfo Kohen that according to the Rosh there is no distinction between teiku and an unresolved question, and I discussed the matter there at length. Shach.
