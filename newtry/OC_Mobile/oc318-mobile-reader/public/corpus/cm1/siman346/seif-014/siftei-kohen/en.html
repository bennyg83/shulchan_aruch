All of these are doubtful cases of guarding with the owner. The Tur writes that if the owner seized property, it is removed from him. The Beit Yosef and Bach say this is a scribal error and should read that it is not removed, and later editions altered the Tur accordingly. In my opinion the original wording can be deliberate. These questions conclude in the Gemara with teiku, and the Rosh writes that seizure is ineffective in a matter concluding with teiku, as the Tur himself records in simanim 388 and 390. This differs from the earlier unresolved question that did not conclude with teiku. I later found an old parchment manuscript of the Tur reading, 'it is not removed from him.' In Tekfo Kohen, however, I conclude at length that according to the Rosh there is no distinction between a formal teiku and any unresolved question.
