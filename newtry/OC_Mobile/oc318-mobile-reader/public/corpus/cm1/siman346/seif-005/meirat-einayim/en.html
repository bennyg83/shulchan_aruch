This was explained in siman 305. There the Tur and Mechaber write that 'guard for me and I will guard for you' immediately creates custody with the owner present and exempts from all liability. 'Guard for me today and I will guard for you tomorrow' does not, and the first custodian has the law of a paid custodian because his compensation is the reciprocal guarding promised for tomorrow. There is also a dispute regarding 'lend to me and I will lend to you,' so the present siman mentions only the clearer rules.
