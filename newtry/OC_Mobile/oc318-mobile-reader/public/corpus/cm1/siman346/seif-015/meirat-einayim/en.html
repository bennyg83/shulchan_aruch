The first does not drag the second after it. Meaning, do not say that because we require the beginning of the rental or borrowing to be with the owner present, if it was absent at the very first beginning then even a later renewal while the owner is present remains liable because the whole period is one extended rental. The ruling teaches that when the first term ends and the second begins, the second is a new custodial period. Therefore if the first was without the owner and the second begins with him, the second is exempt. Even though in the reverse case some hold that an earlier period with the owner present can extend into a later one without him, in doubtful custodial law we follow the lenient side. So writes the Tur.
