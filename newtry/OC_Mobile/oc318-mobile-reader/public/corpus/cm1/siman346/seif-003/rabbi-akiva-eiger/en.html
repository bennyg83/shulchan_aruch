[Shulchan Aruch] The Canaanite slave. Note: This means that the master himself was borrowed together with the cow and sent his slave in his place. But if one borrowed both his slave and his cow, it is like borrowing two cows. Tosafot, Bava Metzia 59a. If Reuven borrowed Shimon's slave and afterward Reuven lent his cow to Levi and told that slave, Go and lend it, this is not considered borrowing with the owner, because here the rule that a slave's hand is like his master's hand does not apply, since Reuven is not his master. Even according to the Tur and those who agree with him that an agent is treated like the principal regarding borrowing with the owner, a slave is inferior in this regard because he is not himself subject to mitzvot in the same manner. See the sugya and the early authorities. See Lechem Mishneh, Hilchot Sheelah BeBealim chapter 2, who infers from Rambam, whose words are cited in the gloss in seif 7, that if done with the owner's knowledge it is considered borrowing with the owner, unlike Tosafot cited above.
