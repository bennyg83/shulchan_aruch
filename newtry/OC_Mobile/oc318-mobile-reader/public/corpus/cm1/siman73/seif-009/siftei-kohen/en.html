He must pay on the first day of Rosh Chodesh. Both days are called Rosh Chodesh, and a doubt concerning a Torah oath is resolved stringently. I wrote in Yoreh Deah that even without this principle the same result may follow because ordinary people call the first day Rosh Chodesh.
