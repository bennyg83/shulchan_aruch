Although the principal day of Rosh Chodesh is the second day, in vows we follow ordinary human speech, and people call the first day Rosh Chodesh as well. One might ask: since the second day is also called Rosh Chodesh, perhaps he intended that day rather than the first. Rashba answers that this is indeed a doubt, and in matters of vows a doubt is treated stringently. This also answers the Shach's question in Yoreh Deah 228.
