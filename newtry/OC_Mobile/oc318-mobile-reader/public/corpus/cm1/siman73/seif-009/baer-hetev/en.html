The first. The Taz was asked about one who fixed the due date as Chanukah and swore to it. Must he pay on the first day of Chanukah, as in a case involving the two days of Rosh Chodesh? The Taz distinguishes them. The two days of Rosh Chodesh are not one unit: the first belongs to the completion of the previous month and the second begins the next month. Thus a doubt whether he swore to pay on the first or second day resembles a doubt whether he swore to pay in Adar or Nissan, and stringency requires payment on the earlier date. Chanukah, however, is one continuous observance. It is like a single-day date whose obligation is not necessarily at the beginning of the day, so he does not violate his oath so long as he pays by the final day.
