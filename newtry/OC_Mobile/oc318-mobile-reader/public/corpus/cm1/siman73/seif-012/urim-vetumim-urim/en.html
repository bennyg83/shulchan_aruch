Shach disagrees and says he cannot compel redemption before one year. See Tumim, where I wrote that the Shach is correct according to most authorities, except Rashba. However, if the lender consumes no produce at all by deduction, perhaps everyone agrees that after thirty days he may compel redemption, just as with movables, because he derives no benefit from the land. If it is a Sura type mortgage, most authorities hold that the lender cannot compel redemption until the agreed mortgage years end.
