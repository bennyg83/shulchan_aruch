(Seif 1) “Whether an oral loan.” The principal novelty is an oral loan. One might have thought that beit din grants a thirty-day period only for a written loan, since people do not trouble to write a document for less than thirty days, but not for an oral loan. The Gemara in Makkot states this expressly. The Sema says the novelty is the written loan, and the Bach offers another novelty for an oral loan. They evidently did not consult the sugya.
