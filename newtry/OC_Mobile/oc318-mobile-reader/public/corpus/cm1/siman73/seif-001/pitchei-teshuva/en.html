And he did not establish a term. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger it is written: If the document states that he must pay 'during a period of thirty days,' the intent is that he may pay little by little during that period. Nevertheless, if he has no cash available, beit din does not compel him and does not descend to his assets until the period has ended. Maharikash.
