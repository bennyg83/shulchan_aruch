In the rainy season and in the summer. The mishnah, Ketubot 64b, teaches that he does not give her new clothing in summer and worn clothing in winter. Rashi explains that new clothes are uncomfortable in summer because they are warm, while they are beneficial in winter. The Mechaber therefore mentions winter first, to indicate that he buys her new clothing at the beginning of every winter. The Tur adds that the worn garments remaining afterward belong to her to wear during her days of niddah. This is an explicit Gemara in Ketubot 65b: surplus worn clothing belongs to the woman. I do not know why this was omitted here.
