In a place... The Tosefta at the beginning of chapter 10 of Bava Metzia says: one who lends without specifying a term lends for not less than thirty days; in a locale where the custom is shorter or longer, local custom governs.
(Collected note) He cannot demand payment beforehand. Meaning only for this purpose, as stated at the end of siman 78. See the Sema and Shach. (End of note.)
Whether... Makkos there.
Whether with a pledge or... Shabbos 148a says that a person may borrow, etc., and the passage speaks both with and without a pledge, as the concluding clause says, "and if he does not trust him..." The Gemara distinguishes the language of borrowing and lending. See Rashi there, s.v. halveni.
But borrowing an object... See Beer HaGolah. The Rosh further proves this from Maseches Semachos: one who lends his cloak to another to attend a house of mourning may not demand it until the days of mourning have ended; on a festival, until the festival ends; at a feast, until the feast ends. This implies that otherwise it can be demanded immediately. See siman 341, seifim 1 and 2.
Nevertheless... Bava Kamma 103a, "and money for nothing..." The particular novelty stated there does not apply here.
(Collected note) Nevertheless, if... See the Shach and Taz. (End of note.)
