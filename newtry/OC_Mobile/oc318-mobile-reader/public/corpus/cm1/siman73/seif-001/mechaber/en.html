The Standard Term of a Loan and Various Detailed Loan Laws. Twenty seifim. In a place without a contrary custom, an unspecified loan has a thirty-day term during which the lender cannot demand payment, whether it is documented or oral, secured or unsecured. If they stipulated that the lender may demand whenever he wishes, he may demand even that same day. An unspecified loan of an object, however, is immediately due and may be demanded at once. {Rama: If the borrower protests against giving the lender a document but the lender demands one, the borrower must immediately return the money, because the loan was presumably made on the understanding that he would write a document; since he refuses, he must repay.}
