One who comes, etc., because, etc. As stated in Kiddushin 16a: Rava said, 'This teaches,' etc.; 'and the master,' etc. Tosafot in the first chapter of Sanhedrin infer that waiver requires no act of acquisition. But where the body of the object belongs to him, waiver is ineffective, as above. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'and the master.'
