He may sell it, etc. Baal HaTerumot cites Sefer HaMekach that, although the loss results from the borrower's own conscious neglect, the diminution is still his loss. It appears that even if the pledge initially was worth no more than the debt and the lender now has no other source of payment, he may nevertheless sell it, because continued deterioration would still create a loss to the borrower by leaving an outstanding balance owed to the lender.
