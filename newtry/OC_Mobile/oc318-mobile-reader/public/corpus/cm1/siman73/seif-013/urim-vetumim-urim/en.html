He may sell it through beit din. This applies even if the pledge is worth no more than the debt and the borrower has no other assets from which to cover any shortfall. Thus, strictly speaking, continuing depreciation of the pledge is not presently the borrower's loss. Nevertheless selling it is part of returning a lost object to the borrower, because the borrower remains obligated to make good the loss when he later acquires assets. Therefore the lender may sell it even without the borrower's knowledge. Baal HaTerumot and Shach.
