The Tur. Sema in the name of Responsa Rosh, rule 8, siman 2.
He swore and stood there
(b) And he brings to C. C. C. S. C. C. C.C., if he does not swear to make him and so on to fulfill a debtor
See Yoreh Deah siman 238 seif 17, where some doubt whether he is exempt from a Torah oath, namely the Rashba. According to that view, if he found grounds for release from his vow, he certainly is not exempt from disciplinary lashes. This removes the major difficulty raised by the Bach as to what benefit there is in finding grounds for release. See there, and as the Sema writes. The Bach adds that beit din appears to have authority to descend to his property and collect for the creditor; he need not sell it himself.
