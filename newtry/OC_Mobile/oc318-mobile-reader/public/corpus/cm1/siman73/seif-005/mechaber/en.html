Reuven swore not to sell any of his property to repay any debt, and afterward swore to repay Shimon by a specified time. When the time arrived, he claimed, 'I have no money, and I already swore not to sell my property to repay any debt.' If Shimon's debt preceded the oath not to sell his property, that oath did not take effect, and he is beaten until his soul departs if he refuses to pay. If the oath preceded Shimon's debt, the second oath was an oath taken in vain, and he receives lashes for rebellious conduct on its account. If fear of the lashes causes him to regret the first oath and he finds grounds for its annulment, they annul it for him; he then sells his property and fulfills the second oath.
