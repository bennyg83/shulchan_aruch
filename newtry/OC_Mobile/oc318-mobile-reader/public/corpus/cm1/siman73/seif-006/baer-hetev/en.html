Remains. The obligation remains to pay him when he demands payment, just as with a fixed-term loan whose due date has passed, where the debtor is not discharged merely by the passage of time. So writes the Tur. The Sema learns from this that even if a debtor swore simply to pay on a certain date, the severity of the oath does not obligate him to seek out the lender and pay without demand; he must pay when the lender demands it. See responsum R. Aharon Sasson, siman 230. The Taz challenges the Sema and rejects his proofs; see there and Yoreh Deah siman 238, seif 18. See also the Shach in Yoreh Deah siman 252, seif katan 28; responsum Rama, siman 108; and responsum Mishpetei Shmuel, siman 129.
