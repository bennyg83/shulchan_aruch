One who swears, etc. Meaning, the time passed. See Sema. Rosh likewise writes in that responsum, based on Bava Batra 5b concerning payment. He writes that this is unlike one who swore to eat this loaf today and the day passed without his eating it, who is no longer obligated to eat it. There the principal oath concerns the time, whereas here the principal oath concerns discharge of the money, and the time was included only to urge prompt payment. See what I wrote in seif 7. Responsa attributed to Ramban, siman 256, disagree and compare it to one who swore to eat a loaf that day.
