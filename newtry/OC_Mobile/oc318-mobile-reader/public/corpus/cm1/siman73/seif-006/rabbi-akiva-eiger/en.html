[Sema, in the continuation] And it appears that if he wrote. Note: See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 142. The Beit Yosef, end of siman 29, writes: If a deed states, “So-and-so swore,” but it is not written whether he swore by mentioning the Divine Name or by holding a Torah scroll, Rabbeinu Yerucham, Netiv 2, part 2, writes that the witnesses are asked. End quote.
