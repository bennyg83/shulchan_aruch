One who sells... As stated in Kesubos there. See Tosafos there, s.v. "de'amri lah..." and Bava Metzia 38a: "and he sells them through beit din," and when he sells them, he sells them, etc.
And if he sells it. The Rivash proves from Kesubos there, "Who appraised it for you like this...," which implies that if an expert beit din appraised it for her and for him, it is valid. See Tosafos there, s.v. cited above: "This is difficult for Rebbe..." and "perhaps there, in the Yerushalmi..." As the Rivash writes, the case of HaMafkid likewise concerns an ordinary beit din, and he writes that the commentators explained it this way. See there.
