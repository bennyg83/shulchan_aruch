There is an authority who says, etc. The Rivash follows his own approach, but Tosafot in several passages indicates that even with authorization of an expert beit din the creditor may not take the pledge for himself. The Maggid Mishneh, citing Sefer HaMekach, likewise states without qualification that he may not acquire it himself. Sefer HaMekach sets three conditions: he must notify the owner before sale if the owner is locally available; he may not take the pledge for himself even at its full price; and he may sell only through beit din. Baal HaTerumot indicates the same. Tosafot further implies that a self-sale is ineffective even after the fact. This is the principal ruling. The ordinary phrase "he sells it through beit din" means a qualified beit din, and nevertheless the Mishna teaches that he may not sell it to himself.
