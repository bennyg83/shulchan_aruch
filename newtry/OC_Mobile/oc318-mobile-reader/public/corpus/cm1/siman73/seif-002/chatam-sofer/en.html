Mechaber, seif 4, 'the lender shall take a hesset oath.' Note: See Makkot 3b. Concerning a loan secured by collateral, one can say that the collateral is not worth the full amount of the money, and therefore he must take a more stringent oath. But with respect to a written document there is a difficulty, since at the time of the Mishnah the hesset oath had not yet been instituted. One must therefore say that for this reason Rashi explains the case as involving a prozbul, unlike Tosafot; see there, and this is straightforward.
