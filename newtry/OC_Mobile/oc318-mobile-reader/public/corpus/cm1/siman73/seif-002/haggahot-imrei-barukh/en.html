(Shach, seif katan 11) Since he swore by inclusion. Note: This requires study in light of Responsa Ran, end of siman 74, whose intent, cited by the Rama in Yoreh Deah 238:6, appears to be that a second oath does not take effect by inclusion where doing so would divest another person's right. See also Taz, Yoreh Deah 234, seif katan 59; Rashba's novellae to Nazir 16, passage beginning "Rabbi said"; and Responsa Rivash, siman 159.
