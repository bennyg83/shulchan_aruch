If a lender fixed a payment date, he cannot demand payment before it even without a formal act of acquisition, whether the loan is oral or documented, secured or unsecured, and whether the lender or borrower dies, provided the borrower's heirs are as financially sound as he was. If the lender says, 'Today is the due date you fixed,' and the borrower says ten days remain, the borrower takes a heses oath. If one witness says today is the due date, the borrower must take a Torah oath. See also siman 40.
