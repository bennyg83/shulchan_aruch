He shall not sell it. As stated in HaMekabel: he sells it through beit din. The mishna refers even to collateral seized without authorization of beit din, as explained in the first chapter of Arakhin: Scripture's command to return the pledge, etc. Tosafot explain this there and in HaMekabel, s.v. 'he returns.' All the more so does this apply to collateral taken at the time of the loan. Collateral seized after the loan is acquired under Rabbi Yitzchak's rule, unlike collateral taken at the time of the loan, as stated there and in Shevuot. See also Bava Metzia 69a, 'Who divided it for you?' All the more so when the item is not his, as stated there, 31a and 32a.
