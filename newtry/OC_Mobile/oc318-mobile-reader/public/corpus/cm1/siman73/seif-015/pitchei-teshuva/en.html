See Baer Heitev, through his statement that although the authorities have already agreed to the underlying novelty, we do not extend a novel ruling beyond its precise case. For this reason, Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part III, siman 158, rules that if a loan was made with a guarantor and the lender argues, 'Nevertheless, I lent only on the understanding that both the borrower and guarantor would remain responsible, and now that the borrower is dissipating his assets I do not wish to rely upon the guarantor alone,' we do not listen to him and do not compel payment before the due date.
