Everything belongs to the borrower, etc. In my humble opinion this applies even to an item with a fixed market price, such as where it is worth twenty gold coins here but the agent brought it to Media, where it is worth thirty. The Bach’s statement that the ruling concerns only an item without a fixed price, based on siman 183, seif 6, refers there to an item sold here. Examine the matter carefully. Regarding compensation for the agent’s labor, see Responsa Maharshadam, siman 444.
