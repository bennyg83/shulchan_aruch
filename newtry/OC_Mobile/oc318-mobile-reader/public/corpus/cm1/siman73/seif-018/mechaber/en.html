If the lender says to the borrower, "They will give only twenty gold coins for the collateral," and the borrower tells him, "Sell it," and the lender happens to have business in Media and takes the collateral with him and sells it there for thirty, the entire thirty belongs to the borrower. The expenses incurred in bringing it there are charged to the borrower, but the lender is not paid for his labor, since he was traveling there in any event.
