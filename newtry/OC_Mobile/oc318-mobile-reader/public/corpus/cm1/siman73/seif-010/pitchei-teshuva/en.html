He is dissipating his assets. See Baer Heitev. Although the authorities accepted the rule permitting acceleration where the debtor is wasting his assets, one may not extend that novelty beyond its case. Therefore Responsa Shevut Yaakov, volume 3, siman 158, rules that if the loan was made with a guarantor and the creditor argues that he relied upon both borrower and guarantor and does not now wish to rely on the guarantor alone because the borrower is dissipating his assets, he is not heard and the borrower cannot be compelled to pay before the due date.
