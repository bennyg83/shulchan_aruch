For fixed times. Knesset HaGedolah cites Rabbi Shlomo Yonah, responsum 34: where one sets different due dates for another person concerning different amounts or goods, the creditor cannot at the end of all those dates demand the entire balance at once. If all the dates passed without demand, the creditor still cannot force payment of the entire loan in one lump sum. Instead, beit din sets successive payment periods according to what appears proper.
