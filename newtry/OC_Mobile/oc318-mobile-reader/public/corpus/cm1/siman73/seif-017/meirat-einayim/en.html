If he said to him, 'Acquire it from now,' etc. Beit Yosef at the end of siman 72 and Darkei Moshe here present this law as though it disputes the preceding ruling, where one stipulated that if the collateral was not redeemed by a certain time the lender would collect his debt from it and the excess would be a gift, which is treated as an asmachta. I explained in Hagahot Darkei Moshe that there is no dispute.
Here he says that the lender should acquire the entire collateral from now, and 'from now' removes the concern of asmachta, as explained in siman 207. In the earlier case he did not say that the lender should acquire his debt portion from now, but only that he should collect the debt from the collateral and acquire the excess from now.
Since the clause permitting self-collection is itself an asmachta, the clause concerning the excess is also an asmachta. This appears to be the Mechaber's intent in presenting the rulings without language of dispute.He may retract. At first he merely appointed him as an agent to sell, and a principal can revoke an agent's authority.Likewise, if the lender pressed him, etc. The Mechaber already stated this law in siman 72, seif 26.
