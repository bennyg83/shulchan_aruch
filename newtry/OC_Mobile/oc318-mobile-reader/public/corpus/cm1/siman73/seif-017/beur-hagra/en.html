One who has... Agency does not require a kinyan. See Kesubos 98a to 99a, and the end of chapter 9 of Pesachim: "He said to him, go and slaughter..." and "if I delayed..." The same applies to kiddushin, gittin, terumah, me'ilah, and elsewhere. At the end of Bava Metzia, "the borrower said to him, send it by..." Even an indication of intent suffices, as stated in Kiddushin 52b. Even for kiddushin, "go to the better produce" would have sufficed were it not that we say it was because of embarrassment, etc.
