(Seif 20) “This wording does not imply it.” The implication is that if he stated expressly that the gentile relied solely on the pledge, there would be no interest prohibition. The Shulchan Aruch rules similarly in Yoreh Deah 169, seif 6. The Beit Yosef’s discussion beginning “He claimed, ‘I lent you one hundred,’” and continuing to the Rambam in chapter 4 of Laws of Claims, was copied in the wrong place; it belongs under seif 12. See the Sema.
