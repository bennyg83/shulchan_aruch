This language does not imply, etc. Matters merely intended in the heart have no legal effect. But if he expressly said that the second Jew is lending on his behalf, then the first borrower must pay the interest, because the second Jew became his agent, the gentile primarily relies on the pledge, and the pledge belongs to the first Jew. See the main laws of this subject in Yoreh Deah 169 and what I wrote there.
