(Seif 9) I was also asked about one who fixed repayment “on Chanukah” and swore to it, whether he must pay on the first day, like the rule that one who swears to pay “on Rosh Chodesh Adar” must pay on the first of the two Rosh Chodesh days. The cases differ. The Rashba explains that the two days of Rosh Chodesh Adar are distinct: the first completes the preceding month, while the second begins Adar. Faced with doubt between two distinct dates, the oath is treated stringently and payment is required on the first. The days of Chanukah, however, form one continuous festival period, comparable to different hours of a single day. Payment on the last day therefore fulfills the oath. All the more so, if no oath was taken and the document merely fixes payment “on Chanukah,” collection cannot be compelled until the last day.
