(Sema, seif katan 19) At the end of the passage: until the lender demands payment. Note: See Shiltei HaGibborim in the name of Ri'az, chapter HaGozel U'Ma'achil, Rif Vienna edition, page 48, ruling 1; Responsa Rashba, siman 670 and siman 1018. Those responsa imply that the Rashba holds that no demand is required. Moreover, in Responsa Rosh, rule 8, siman 7, the question states that the borrower swore to pay him or whoever produces the document. One may say that only in such a case is a demand required, because the obligation is specifically to pay whoever produces the document. This makes the Rosh's wording precise: "and although he did not violate the oath so long as no person came and demanded payment." The intent is that until the holder of the document made a demand, he did not have to pay money in exchange for the document, because his undertaking was only to pay whoever would produce it.
