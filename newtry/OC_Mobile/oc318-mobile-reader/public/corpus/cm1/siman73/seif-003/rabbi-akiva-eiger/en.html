[Shulchan Aruch] He cannot demand payment before the due date. Note: If the creditor seized property, it may be that the seizure is effective; see Tumim, siman 67, seif katan 16. Maharikash writes that if the deed states that payment shall be made “during thirty days,” the intent is that he pay little by little. Nevertheless, if he does not have available cash, beit din does not descend to his property during the term. See Knesset HaGedolah, letter 5. The compiler writes: It appears to me that regarding an ordinary loan, Rav Hai Gaon and the Ramban would agree that beit din attends to the creditor’s claim even during the first thirty days, as stated in Tur, part 5, siman 91, page 124b, line 27, in the Beit Yosef gloss.
