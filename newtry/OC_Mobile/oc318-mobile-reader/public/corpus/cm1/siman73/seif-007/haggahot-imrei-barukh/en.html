(Shach, seif katan 39) For we say dina demalchuta dina in every matter. Note: See the language of the Rashba cited by the Beit Yosef at the end of siman 56, who distinguishes between a rule adjudicated because of the law of the non-Jewish courts and a rule enacted by the sovereign specifically concerning that matter. See also Maggid Mishneh, Hilchot Malveh VeLoveh, chapter 26; Yam Shel Shlomo to Bava Kamma, chapter 7, ruling 14 and chapter 10, rulings 18 and 21; and Sema at the end of siman 349, who cites the above responsum of the Rashba.
