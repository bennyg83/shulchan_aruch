Responsa attributed to the Ramban, siman 243. The Beit Yosef writes that the Rosh agrees. The responsum concludes that permission was required because this is a matter of mitzvah and is no worse than arranging a match or teaching a trade, which may be discussed on Shabbat, 150a. If the item had been given as actual repayment, it would follow the explicit Mishnah there. The Sema writes that from this one can learn that he permitted even others to engage in the appraisal, analogous to arranging a child's match. The Bach prohibits this and allows only transferring an ownership share in the collateral itself. See there.
