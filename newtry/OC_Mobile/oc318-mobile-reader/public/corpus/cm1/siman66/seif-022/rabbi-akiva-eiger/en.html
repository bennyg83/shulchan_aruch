[Shach, there, s.v. And after we rejected] For merely because they should not treat it lightly. Note: It follows that the Shach's view is clear: the enactment applied only to negligence, not to theft or loss. With respect to theft and loss, where the guardian is exempt even from payment, the Sages made no enactment at all. See Responsa Maharan Sasson, siman 73, who infers from the Rambam that the enactment was that all the laws of guardians should apply to consecrated property. See there and examine carefully.
