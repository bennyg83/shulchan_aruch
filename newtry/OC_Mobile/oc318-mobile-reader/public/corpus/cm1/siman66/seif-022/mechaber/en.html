If Reuven holds a document against Shimon and gives it to Levi through writing and delivery, with Shimon and another person serving as witnesses to the gift, and Reuven later seeks to retract by arguing that the transfer is invalid because Shimon is interested in testifying so that he will face a more lenient creditor, Reuven prevails. Shimon is considered an interested witness. See also siman 205.
