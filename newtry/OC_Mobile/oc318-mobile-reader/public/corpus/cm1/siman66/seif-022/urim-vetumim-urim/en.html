And Shimon waived, etc. Strictly speaking, the language of waiver is not normally appropriate to a mere deposit, as explained at the end of siman 73, and all the more not to debt instruments that require written assignment and delivery. Sema therefore explains that the documents were written in Reuven's name but the underlying money actually belonged to Shimon. In that sense Shimon had some monetary interest that might be described as waived. Even so, waiver is ineffective because the documents' bodies are not Shimon's and transfer requires written assignment and delivery. Shach says the Shulchan Aruch merely used ordinary loose language and really means that Shimon gave them away. Even under Sema's case, “waiver” is not truly the precise term.
