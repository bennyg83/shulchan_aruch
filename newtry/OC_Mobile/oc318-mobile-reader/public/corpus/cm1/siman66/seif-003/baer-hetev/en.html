twisted. Lkman S. D. D. D.N. wrote that his estate had been given to him as a result of a G-d, and that he had finished no estate. A:
Wrote. According to the Mechaber's ruling in seif 10 that a document can be acquired incidentally with land, with which the Rama agrees there, the phrase here 'he did not write' cannot be literal. Rather, it means that he did not even state orally the formula of acquisition. In siman 248 the Tur likewise writes regarding a gift by a healthy person that it is ineffective because he did not write, 'Acquire it,' but there too the wording is not literal, since the Mechaber rules here in seif 10 that an oral declaration suffices.
