Nevertheless, he may seize, etc. This gloss belongs later, on the Shulchan Aruch's statement that even the paper itself was not acquired. Rama there teaches that the transferee may nevertheless hold it. Shach limits this to where the borrower has not yet paid, or has paid but does not insist that his deed be returned. If the borrower demands return of his instrument, Rama's ruling below in seif 16 governs. See there and my comments.
