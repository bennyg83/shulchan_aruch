One who gives. Bava Batra 151a, according to the explanation in Shittah Mekubetzes, and as explained above in seif 42.
(Collected note) He did not acquire them... Nimukei Yosef and many poskim disagree and hold that here one need not say "Acquire..." because the debt lien itself is included in the term "my property." This also gives a straightforward explanation to Bava Batra 150, where a document is called property even in the case of a healthy person, as Nimukei Yosef writes there. (End of note.)
Since. Even in acquisition together with land one must say, "Acquire..." as explained above in seif 10, unlike the rule for a shechiv mera. See seif 42.
(Collected note) Since he did not write... This follows his own view in seif 10 that documents may be acquired together with land. It is not specifically writing; the same applies if he said it, as explained there. (End of note.)
