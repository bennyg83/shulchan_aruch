He did not acquire it, etc. Ritva disagrees and holds that debt documents and all their liens are included in the term ‘my property.’ Thus, even in a lifetime gift, saying ‘my property to so-and-so’ includes the documents. If they are transferred together with land, the statement is equivalent to expressly granting the documents and all their liens, and the land-based acquisition substitutes for delivery. Under the view explained below that writing is still required, the written gift of all property itself supplies the necessary language, while acquisition together with land supplies delivery. The same applies to bearer instruments that require no writing. Ritva states that this is the view of Raah, Ramban, and Rashba, contrary to the Mechaber’s ruling.
