Movable property by means of land. Meaning, he transferred his assets under the law of movable property acquired through agav of land. If he merely said "movable property," debt documents would not be included at all, because documents are not categorized as ordinary movable property for this purpose, but they are included in the broader term "assets":
Since he did not write. According to the Mechaber, who holds that transfer by agav does not require an additional written assignment, the word "write" is not literal. The intent is that he did not expressly say that the debt document was included, because according to this approach a verbal specification is required. The Shach cites the Ritva, who holds that a debt document together with all its liens is included in the term "assets" without any express specification. The primary ruling follows the Mechaber, as the Tumim also writes.
