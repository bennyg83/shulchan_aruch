Corresponding to her ketubah. Meaning, according to the amount of her Torah-level ketubah, as will be explained nearby in seif 6. The Ran asks: it is prohibited to contract nissuin with a widow on Shabbos lest it appear that one is making an acquisition on Shabbos. If so, how could he instruct the husband to designate movable property to her on Shabbos, which also appears to be an acquisition on Shabbos? One can distinguish between nissuin, which is a public act, and designating movable property, which is not public. Alternatively, the case concerns a time before nightfall on Friday, when there was enough time to designate movable property to her but not enough time to write a ketubah. End of the Ran's words.
