(In the Tur: Even according to the authority who requires writing, it is unnecessary, etc.) See the Ran in HaKotev, who derives from the rule that two men named Yosef ben Shimon in one city may produce debt instruments against others. Since documentary rights are not acquired by delivery alone, the present holder must be believed to say that he once had a written transfer and lost it, without bringing proof. This shows that even against the debtor no additional proof is required, contrary to the Rambam. The Beit Yosef’s attribution of the Rosh to Rabbi Yosef Migash is therefore incorrect.
