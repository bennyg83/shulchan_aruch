One who forgives is a damager. There, according to the authority who applies dina d'garmi.
Even if he accepted. Forgiveness is necessarily not included in an ordinary warranty unless expressly stated. Otherwise, even according to the authority who does not apply dina d'garmi, he should be liable by virtue of the warranty, since omission of warranty is a scribal error, as explained in the preceding seif.
