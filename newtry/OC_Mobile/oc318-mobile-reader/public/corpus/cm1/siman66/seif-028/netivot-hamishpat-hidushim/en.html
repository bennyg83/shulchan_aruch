And even if they were nichsei melog. With property over which the husband has no control and where the woman's sale or gift is valid, her waiver is likewise valid. If she waived a debt included in nichsei melog and later became widowed, then according to the Rama in Even HaEzer, siman 68, who holds that a lifetime sale is valid, the same applies to a waiver. With nichsei tzon barzel, the result depends on the two opinions there regarding a lifetime sale. These rules concern property the wife herself brought into the marriage. But if the bride's father transferred a debt document to his son in law, then although the rule of obligations acquired by verbal undertaking at marriage also applies, the son in law still requires writing and delivery from his father in law, and the father in law retains power to waive the debt.
