It lapses and he is discharged. The source states that since, had the convert waived the debt, it would have been waived, because the borrower's personal obligation remained with him, when the convert dies that obligation lapses and becomes ownerless. The borrower then acquires his own personal obligation. This is comparable to a pledge belonging to a Jew that is in the hands of a convert, where the convert dies and the person who takes possession does not acquire the pledge, etc., as the Tur explains in siman 72, seif 39.
