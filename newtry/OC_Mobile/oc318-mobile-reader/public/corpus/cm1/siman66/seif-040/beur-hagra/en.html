Even for negligence. Certainly this applies to a paid custodian. A negligent custodian is not treated as a direct tortfeasor, as explained above, but is liable only under the special law of custodians. Bava Kamma 93a discusses a charity purse over which the custodian was negligent. Abaye answered from the verse, to guard, showing that wherever the Torah exempted a custodian, the exemption applies even to negligence. See Tosafot, Shevuot 42b, s.v. Shomer chinam. There the custodian was a paid custodian according to Rav Yosef. See also Tosafot, Bava Metzia 57b, s.v. Shomer chinam.
