y) Tur in the name of a responsum of the Rif, and he writes that his father the Rosh likewise rules in his rulings there.
k) The Rosh there.
l) Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilkhot Sechirut, from the Mishnah that a gratuitous bailee does not swear, which implies that if it becomes known that he was negligent he pays. This also appears from Rashi. Maggid Mishneh.
m) Responsa Rosh, rule 39, siman 2. He explains that since the Torah excluded these items from the laws of bailees, one can obligate him only as a person who directly causes damage. Mere negligence followed by loss is not considered direct damage, for even negligence where the owner is present is exempt. The Raavad writes likewise there in his gloss, and so too in the name of the Ramban and Rashba.
n) Beit Yosef, from seif 27, and Rambam there, from the interpretations of Rav Sheshet and Rabbi Yochanan, Bava Metzia 58a.
