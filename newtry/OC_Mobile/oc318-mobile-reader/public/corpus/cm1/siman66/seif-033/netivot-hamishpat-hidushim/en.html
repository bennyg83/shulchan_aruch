Forged. Even the purchase price need not be returned by the seller, because it has not been established that the note was forged. The purchaser caused his own loss by buying an unvalidated note. Under responsibility, however, the seller must pay the full value of the note according to everyone. Sema. From encumbered property he may recover only the amount of the purchase price. Any additional amount reflecting the note's higher value has the law of appreciation and may be collected only from free property. If the purchaser knew at the time of purchase that the note was not actually the seller's, he receives only the money he paid.
