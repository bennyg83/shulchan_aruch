[Shulchan Aruch] He did not acquire it. Note: Maharikash was uncertain about this. Since the author of the underlying teaching is the Rosh, perhaps he follows his own view that our documents are merely evidentiary documents. According to what we rule below, siman 291, that such a document is an instrument of acquisition, perhaps here the purchaser does acquire it. Alternatively, because in truth he did not acquire by means of this document but had already acquired through another kinyan, he may not acquire the document itself. See there. It appears that this is what the Shach intended in seif katan 46.
