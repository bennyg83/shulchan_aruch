It is not acquired through agav. See Sema and Shach regarding whether a promissory note can become subject to a lien through agav. The issue is where the holder of the note pledged the note to his own creditor through agav. The creditor thereby has a proprietary right in the note until he is paid, and he can in turn encumber that right to others. If the debtor on the note later sells the note to someone else, the creditor can seize the note from the purchaser, because the substance of the note had already become subject to his lien, and the later sale is ineffective against him. This resolves Shach's difficulty in seif katan 26. Examine this carefully.
