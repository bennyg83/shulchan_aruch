Without. Anything transferred together with land has the law of land, which is acquired by money, a document, or chazakah. Nevertheless, an oral declaration of transfer is required, as the ruling concludes. Sema.
Together with land. The Rosh writes in a responsum that an obligation can nevertheless be created through transfer together with land. Ibid.
The first. The Shach writes that the primary ruling is to distinguish as follows: transfer together with land is equivalent only to delivery, and a written transfer is still required. See there at length.
That has not yet. This differs from other matters, where one can transfer and say, 'Acquire together with it a cow or garment when I later acquire it,' as stated in siman 37 and siman 81, because one can obligate movable property that he will later acquire. Thus writes the Sema. The Shach objects that the Sema was imprecise, because it is obvious that a person cannot transfer property that he will acquire only later, as explained above in siman 209. The cases in simanim 37 and 81 concern an obligation, which is different: a person can obligate property that he will later acquire, but cannot presently transfer ownership of it, as this distinction is explicit in the Gemara and all the poskim. The Taz likewise objects to the Sema on this point. See there.
