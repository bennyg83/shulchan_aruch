He acquires the document wherever it is. Meaning, even if the document is not resting on the land transferred together with it, as the Tur explains below in siman 202 regarding acquisition through agav.
Without writing and without delivery. Whenever a promissory note is transferred together with land, its law follows the land, and land is acquired by money, document, chazakah, or kinyan, as the Tur writes at the beginning of siman 190. Nevertheless, the Rosh and Tur write that an oral statement is required. This is why the Mechaber says, 'provided that he says,' etc.
For a promissory note is not acquired through agav, etc. The Rosh writes in Responsa, klal 76, siman 3, that nevertheless a promissory note can be encumbered through agav. So cites Darkei Moshe, seif 11.
A promissory note that has not yet been written. This differs from other property, where one can transfer land and say, 'Acquire together with it a cow or garment when I acquire it,' as explained in siman 37 and siman 61, where movable property may be encumbered through an after-acquired-property clause.
