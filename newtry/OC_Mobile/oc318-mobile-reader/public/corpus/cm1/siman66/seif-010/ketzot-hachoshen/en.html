A document that has not yet been written. Tosafot in Bava Batra discusses a dying person's instruction, "Give a maneh to so and so," which is effective after death because a dying person's words are treated as written and delivered. Yet where he instructs that a get or emancipation document be given after death, it is ineffective. The novelty is that the special power of a dying person's words can transfer existing money but does not create and deliver a document that does not yet exist. This fits the rule that a dying person's special acquisition power applies only to matters that a healthy person could transfer in comparable form. An as yet unwritten instrument has no present existence and therefore cannot be transferred merely through deathbed instructions.
