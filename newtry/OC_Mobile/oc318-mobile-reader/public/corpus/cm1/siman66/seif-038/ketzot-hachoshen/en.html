To collect it. The exclusion from the laws of overreaching applies to the sale of the debt itself. The physical paper, however, has ordinary monetary value and is subject to the law of overreaching. This is also proof for what I wrote above in seif katan 28, that the paper is held by the creditor as an acquired object rather than merely as collateral. If it were only collateral, the creditor would not have the right to sell it for ordinary paper use.
