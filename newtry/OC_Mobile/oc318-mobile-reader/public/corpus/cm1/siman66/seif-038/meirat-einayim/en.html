To collect it, there is no, etc. The Mechaber carefully writes 'to collect it,' because in that use the document itself is not monetary property but only evidence. Therefore the laws of overreaching do not apply, as documents are excluded from the verse concerning overreaching, 'When you sell a sale,' which requires an item whose body has monetary value. If, however, one sells documents to use as paper, such as to stopper the mouth of a flask, or sells them to a spice merchant for wrapping his spices, their bodies have monetary value for that purpose and the laws of overreaching do apply. So writes the Beit Yosef.
And there are those who disagree and hold that only when it exceeds one half, etc. The Tur quotes Rabbeinu Tam as saying that at exactly one half there is no law of cancellation of the sale, and the wording implies that one half itself is not included. Yet elsewhere it is clear that he holds that if the discrepancy is one half, the sale is cancelled. Likewise, later in siman 175, seif 14, the Tur writes, 'he purchased it for slightly less than two hundred,' and similarly in siman 227 it appears that a discrepancy of one half constitutes cancellation of the sale and one need not exceed one half, contrary to the wording here. See what I wrote in the Derishah to simanim 175 and 227.
