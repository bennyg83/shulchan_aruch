Dao, who is dressed in the mouth of his sword, has an ear named after him
The Rif there, in accordance with Rav Nachman, Bava Metzia 108a, and from the Mishnah's rule concerning money's worth, as Rabbah bar Ulla explains in the Gemara, Bava Kamma 14b. So too the Rambam, chapter 13 of Hilkhot Mekhirah.
So write Tosafot, beginning of Bava Metzia 57a, regarding Rav Nachman's statement there in the name of Chasa, implying that Rav Nachman does not disagree with Chasa his teacher. This also follows from the Mishnah concerning one who sells animals and pearls and the Gemara there 58b.
