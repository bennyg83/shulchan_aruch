They write "divorcee" in the ketubah. Piskei Mahari, siman 96, indicates that even if she was divorced from her first husband and widowed from the second, the ketubah should still identify her as a divorcee, because of the concern for Kohanim. What purpose would there be in writing only "widow"? Likewise, if she had been taken captive, and all the more so if she had previously had relations, this status should be recorded because of Kohanim. If a man remarries his own divorcee, I saw in Yam Shel Shlomo that the ketubah should write, "my divorcee who returned to me." He gives the reason in the name of Raavyah, so that she not collect two ketubot, since he restored her on the basis of the first ketubah obligation.
