Or a document. Bava Batra 169a-b: Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says, "even documents of sale..." and the discussion of documents acquired by delivery.
But if. So writes Ri Migash. This resolves Tosafos' question there, s.v. aval b'shtarei: according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, the seller could still, etc. Tosafos offers a strained answer. According to Ri Migash, one may write for the seller without the purchaser present only an evidentiary document, as proved from Bava Metzia 13a and as explained in siman 238, seif 1. Such a document is not acquired by delivery.
