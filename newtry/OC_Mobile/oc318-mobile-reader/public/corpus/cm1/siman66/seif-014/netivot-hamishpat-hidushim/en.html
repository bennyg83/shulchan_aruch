Which is the principal acquisition. A debt note is fundamentally created through writing. An oral loan has no lien, and even where the loan was made with a kinyan, the principal effect of the kinyan is that it is intended to be reduced to writing. Thus the document is the core instrument of acquisition, and even sale of the note itself requires written transfer and delivery. Land is different. It can be acquired through other means and its lien can be established by witnesses. Therefore written transfer and delivery of the evidentiary deed does not itself transfer the land, because the deed is only evidence.
He did not acquire. Maharit writes that an investment note cannot transfer the deposit half through written transfer and delivery. See Beurim, seif katan 39. Through acquisition together with land, however, even the loan half can be transferred. When transferred through acquisition with land, the original lender cannot waive it. When the loan portion is transferred only through written transfer and delivery, he can waive it.
