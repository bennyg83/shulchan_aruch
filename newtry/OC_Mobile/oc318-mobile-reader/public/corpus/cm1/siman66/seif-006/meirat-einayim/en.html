A document made in a non-Jewish court, etc. The Tur writes that we do not say it is like an oral loan that can be transferred only through ma'amad sheloshtan. A document properly made in a non-Jewish court may be collected even from encumbered property. A debtor's handwriting, although it cannot be collected from encumbered property, can at least be collected from unencumbered property. See Perishah.
Where it is valid under our law, etc. But if it is invalid under our law, even though the non-Jewish courts would collect with it, it has only the status of an oral loan. So writes Beit Yosef, seif 5, in the name of Baal HaTerumot.
