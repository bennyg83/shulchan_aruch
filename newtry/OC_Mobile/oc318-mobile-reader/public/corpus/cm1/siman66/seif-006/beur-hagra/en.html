A document made. Meaning even if, according to Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch in siman 68, it cannot be collected from encumbered property. This is the meaning of, "and likewise a document..." which also cannot be collected from encumbered property. Proof is from a deed of gift below in seif 13, which is acquired by writing and delivery even though an ordinary gift cannot be collected from encumbered property, as stated in Bava Batra 178a.
(Collected note) A document... and likewise... Meaning even though it cannot be collected from encumbered property, as in Bava Batra 169: "When does this apply? To loan documents... but..." Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says, etc., and similarly he says, etc. There 169b, "Rav says, regarding documents..." (End of note.)
In a manner whereby it is. Otherwise it is merely an oral debt, as explained in siman 65, and an oral debt is not transferred by writing and delivery as above.
