She brings into the marriage. A married woman has no independent power of acquisition against her husband, which is why he cannot forgive the debt, as explained below in seif 28. The reason is that the underlying loan obligation itself becomes subject to him. For this reason no writing and delivery are required.
(Collected note) She brings into the marriage... The underlying loan itself is acquired by him. It is comparable to a gift of a shechiv mera, as in Bava Batra 151a, "but he did not pull it," and as the Gemara compares them regarding forgiveness, as explained below in seifim 27 and 28. (End of note.)
