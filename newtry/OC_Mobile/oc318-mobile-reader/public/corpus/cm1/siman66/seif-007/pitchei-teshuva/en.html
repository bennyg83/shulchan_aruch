He did not acquire it. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger: Maharikash is uncertain whether, since the source of this ruling is the Rosh, it may follow only according to the Rosh’s own view that our documents are merely evidentiary documents. According to what we rule below in siman 191, that such a document is an instrument of acquisition, perhaps here he does acquire. Alternatively, since in truth he did not acquire through this document but had already acquired through another act of acquisition, the document itself does not effect anything. See there. It appears that this is what the Shach intended in seif katan 45.
