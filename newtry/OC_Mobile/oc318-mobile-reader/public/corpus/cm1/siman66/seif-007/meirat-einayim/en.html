A deed of transfer, etc. It appears this means a document drawn up by a non-Jewish court for one person to transfer a promissory note to another. If the non-Jewish document uses language whose meaning corresponds to our formulation, 'Acquire this note and its lien,' it is as though it were written in Hebrew with that transfer language and acquisition is effective. See below seif 25 regarding a non-Jew who sells his own promissory note to a Jew.
