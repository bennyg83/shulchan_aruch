A deed of conveyance. It is like every deed of sale, as explained above in siman 68, and as stated in Gittin that all documents are governed alike. This requires study, because the Shulchan Aruch rules in siman 68 that a document whose principal acquisition is effected through it is invalid when executed in a gentile court. The same difficulty arises in the preceding seif, which necessarily concerns a deed of acquisition, as explained above in seif 14. These rulings are tenable only according to the opinion that even a deed of acquisition is valid there, as stated in the Rama there.
