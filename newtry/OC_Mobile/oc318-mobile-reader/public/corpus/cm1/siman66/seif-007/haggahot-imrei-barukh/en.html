(Shach, seif katan 36) In my opinion this answers the difficulty he raised, etc. Note: This requires study. Why do we return the receipt? If the husband has the receipt and the purchasers later come to collect from her, the woman can defend herself with the receipt in her husband's possession by claiming that she had already been paid before she sold them the ketubah. One must say that the Shach holds that the existence of the receipt, and the fact that it was returned based on the woman's admission, becomes publicly known. See Bava Metzia 19a, 'bring proof,' etc.
