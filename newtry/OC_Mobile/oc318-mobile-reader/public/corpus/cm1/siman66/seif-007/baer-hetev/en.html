Under their laws. Another reading is 'in their own instruments.' Meaning, the transfer document was written in a non-Jewish court. If the non-Jewish scribes use in their language wording whose meaning is 'Acquire it and all its liens,' it is as though it had been written in Hebrew and the transfer is effective. See seif 25 regarding a non-Jew who sells his promissory note to a Jew. Thus the Sema. See Responsa Mahari Bezalel, siman 36, and Responsa Maharshal, part 2, siman 146.
