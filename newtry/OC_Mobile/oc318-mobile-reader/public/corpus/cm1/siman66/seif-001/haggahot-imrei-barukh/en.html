(Shach, seif katan 1) The words of Tosafot, the Ran, and Nimukei Yosef. Note: The Ran and Nimukei Yosef clearly support the Shach. But in the three Tosafot passages cited by the Shach there is no decisive proof for the Levush and Shach. One may say that since a deathbed gift is rabbinic, the recipient is not literally an heir but only like an heir, as the Sema writes below in siman 253, seif katan 44. The Sages strengthened the deathbed gift only to prevent the donor's mind from becoming disturbed, so that a Torah heir would not have the power to waive it.
