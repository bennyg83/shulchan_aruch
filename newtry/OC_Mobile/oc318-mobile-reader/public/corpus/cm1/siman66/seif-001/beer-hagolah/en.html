A statement of Ameimar, 'The halachah is,' etc., beginning of Bava Batra 77a, according to the Rif's text. So too the Rambam in chapter 10 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, and this is the view of Tosafot and the Rosh there. The Sema writes that the same applies if he wrote on the document, 'I, so-and-so son of so-and-so, transfer to you, so-and-so son of so-and-so, this document against so-and-so, together with every lien contained in it.'
