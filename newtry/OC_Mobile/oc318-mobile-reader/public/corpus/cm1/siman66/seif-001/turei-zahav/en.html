(Seif 1.) "Nevertheless, he may retain the paper," etc. His words imply, unlike the Beit Yosef here, who understood the Tur in siman 15, where it says that the purchaser may use the paper, as disagreeing with what is written here in Ramban's name that he did not acquire even the paper. Rather, Ramban too agrees that he may use the paper until the seller repays him its value. Once the value of the paper is returned, he must return the paper because he never acquired it through a valid sale.
