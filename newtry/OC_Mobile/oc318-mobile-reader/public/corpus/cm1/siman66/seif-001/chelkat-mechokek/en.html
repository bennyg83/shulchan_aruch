It is prohibited to be secluded with the bride. The Bach writes that Rambam chose his wording precisely. Rambam did not say explicitly that seclusion itself is prohibited; he wrote only that one must write a ketubah before she enters chuppah, after which she is permitted to her husband. This implies a preferred initial procedure, so that one not come to an actual prohibition, but does not establish a direct prohibition of mere seclusion.
