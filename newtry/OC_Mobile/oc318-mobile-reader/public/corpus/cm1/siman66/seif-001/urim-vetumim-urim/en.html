They are not acquired, etc. Since debt documents are not themselves money and embody only a lien, they are not transferred by mere delivery together with the words, "Acquire it and all its liens." Writing is required as the Shulchan Aruch explains. Whether sale of debt documents is effective by Torah law or only rabbinically is disputed among the poskim. Shach discusses at length and rules that their sale is effective by Torah law. See Tumim, where I concluded like Rambam and Rosh that ordinary sale of debt documents is rabbinic, except for a document expressly payable to any bearer. Transfer of such a document is effective by Torah law. Sema writes that a practical difference concerns selling debt documents to a gentile. See what I wrote above on this matter.
