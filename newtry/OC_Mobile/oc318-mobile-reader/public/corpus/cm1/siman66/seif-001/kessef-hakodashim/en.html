Only through writing and delivery. One person gave, on account of payment, vouchers issued by an official, valuing them at an agreed amount corresponding to the price of liquor and honey, and paid the balance in cash. Later the official honored the vouchers for less than the amount at which they had been accepted. The giver claimed that the recipients had accepted upon themselves the stated valuation. Although there was no writing and delivery sufficient to transfer the claim formally, since waiver does not require a kinyan, if they accepted the vouchers absolutely at the agreed valuation the loss would appear to fall upon them. Nevertheless, I arranged a compromise.
