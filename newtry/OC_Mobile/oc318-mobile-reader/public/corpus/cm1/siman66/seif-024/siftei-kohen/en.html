There is one who says, etc., that he can make a receipt, etc. One may ask what novelty this teaches, since the Mechaber already wrote the same in seifim 11 and 15. Perhaps the novelty is that the seller may give the debtor a written receipt stating that the debt was paid. But that too is obvious and is compelled by the Gemara at the end of the first chapter of Bava Metzia, folio 19, and by all the poskim. The Mechaber therefore should not have stated it in the form, "there is one who says." Perhaps he used that formulation because of the additional possibility of extending the payment date, which is not expressly stated in the Gemara and poskim. Nevertheless, that too appears correct. Forgiving the debt until a later date is no different in principle from forgiving it completely. It also follows that the seller can forgive half of the debt document or any portion of it.
