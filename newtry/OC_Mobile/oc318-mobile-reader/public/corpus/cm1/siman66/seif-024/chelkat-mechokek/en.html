They calculate according to Torah zuz. Rabbeinu Tam holds that the ketubah is Torah law, and therefore she receives two hundred Tyrian dinar. By way of example, if the twenty dinar of redemption of the firstborn equal approximately five lot of pure silver, then two hundred dinar equal fifty lot of pure silver, about twenty Rhine gold coins in their time. The Rosh holds that although the ketubah is rabbinic, the amount is nevertheless two hundred Tyrian silver dinar. Ramban holds the reverse: even if the ketubah itself is Torah law, the amount is not fixed by the Torah. The Torah merely required that a mohar be given without specifying its value. Therefore the amount follows provincial money, namely twenty-five Tyrian silver dinar, which equal two hundred provincial zuz.
