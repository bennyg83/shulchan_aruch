Give them to Levi in the presence of all three, etc. This law is not comparable to the preceding one. There, the borrower and Shimon, the recipient, were both present when the maneh was transferred to him, except that the body of the document was not conveyed to him. Here, by contrast, the borrower was not present. Only the depositary was present together with the depositor and the recipient. By right, Levi would have acquired the body of the promissory note that was conveyed to him through Shimon, since both of them were present, but because he did not acquire the lien, as the borrower was not present, he likewise did not acquire the body of the promissory note.
Whether by delivery or by exchange. The Mechaber already explained in seif 4, and the Tur likewise explains according to Ramban, that with respect to acquisition by exchange, writing 'Acquire it for yourself, it and all its liens,' is ineffective. Rather, the meaning is that wherever he did not acquire the lien, each case according to its own form, he does not acquire the body of the document. In an exchange transaction, this means that he wrote, 'Acquire it for yourself,' etc., but did not deliver the promissory note to him. In a transaction by delivery, this means that he delivered it to him but did not write, 'Acquire it,' etc.
