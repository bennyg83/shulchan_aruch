Delivery. The Shach writes that the Tur and Mechaber require study for ruling this so simply. The Ramban disagrees and holds that maamad sheloshtan and barter have the same law. Since the Tur and Mechaber rule in seif 4 that barter does not count as delivery for documents, why do they rule here that assignment by maamad sheloshtan counts as delivery? The Gidulei Terumah raises the same difficulty. Perhaps they distinguish between the cases, but the matter requires study. In practice, money may not be extracted contrary to the Ramban's view, and this also appears to be the Ritva's position in chapter HaIsh Mekadesh. Thus the Shach.
