"And if he wrote him," And the tongue turns out that the tongue is becoming and that the tongue is exalted, and another is written to him as the first verse of the book. Yes, yes."
This assignment is equivalent to delivery, etc. The Tur and Mechaber state this categorically, but Ramban disputes it. He treats maamad sheloshtan and barter alike, and since debt documents are not acquired through barter, the assignment should likewise not substitute for delivery. Ritva also states that maamad sheloshtan applies to money or ordinary deposits, not to a document that requires writing and delivery. Therefore one should not extract money against Ramban’s position. The assignment may transfer the underlying debt, but not the document’s full written lien and rights against purchasers.
