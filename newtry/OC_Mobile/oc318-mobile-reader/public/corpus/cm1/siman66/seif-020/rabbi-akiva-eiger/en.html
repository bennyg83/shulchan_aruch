[Shulchan Aruch] That he swear that he guarded it properly. Note: This means that he guarded it properly such that it was neither stolen nor lost, but rather was lost through an unavoidable accident. Such conduct is considered proper guarding for a paid guardian. See below, siman 305, Shach, seif katan 5. This is unlike the strained explanation in Responsa Bnei Aharon, siman 5.
