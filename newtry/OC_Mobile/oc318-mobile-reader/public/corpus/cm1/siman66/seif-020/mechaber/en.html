If Reuven deposited debt documents with Shimon and, in the presence of all three parties, told him, 'Give the documents I deposited with you to Levi,' Shimon should give them to Levi so long as Reuven has not retracted. If Reuven retracts, Shimon must return them, because until Levi acquires the encumbrances represented by the documents he does not acquire the physical papers, whether through delivery, exchange, or the presence of all three parties. If Reuven writes a transfer granting Levi both the documents and all their encumbrances and directs him to the custodian holding them, this direction functions as delivery, so Levi acquires through writing and delivery. See also siman 205.
