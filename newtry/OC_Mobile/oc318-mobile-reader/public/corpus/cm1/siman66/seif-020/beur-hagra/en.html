As long as he has not retracted. Although the recipient has not yet acquired, this is like saying, "Take a maneh," as above, and like saying, "Take a get to my wife," where the sender may retract. An agent for a gift has the same status as an agent for a get, as stated in Gittin.
Whether by delivery. As above in seif 1.
Whether by chalipin. As above in seif 4.
Whether by ma'amad sheloshtan. Kiddushin 47 and 48 distinguish between a documented loan and an oral loan, which shows that ma'amad sheloshtan is not the mode of transferring a document itself. Likewise Gittin 13a, "It must be speaking of a healthy person..." If the rule applied to the document, a deed of emancipation could also be acquired that way. See the Ran. This also explains the precision of the Kiddushin sugya, which says, "Become betrothed to me with a debt document, or if he had..." rather than simply "with a documented loan," because the latter could refer to the underlying debt, which can be transferred through ma'amad sheloshtan without writing and delivery.
(Collected note) And if he wrote... and assigned it... Meaning that he assigned the very writing. The Shach's statement that the words should be reversed is difficult. See Beer HaGolah in his name. He understood "and assigned it" as referring to the debt document, but that is incorrect. Even according to his interpretation, the point is difficult, since in ma'amad sheloshtan all three parties are present together, so what difference does it make whether the document came into the custodian's possession? (End of note.)
