Give him a gift to his friend. P. A.A., who did not do any other property for the gift, but in this section, wrote to him that he was given to you, and that he had not returned to the gift when he was exalted and moral in his hand, until he was written to him, and all that was written, but when he wrote to him that he had been given to him, and he had bought the same as he had written in his first book
