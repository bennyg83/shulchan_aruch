And he gave it to Levi, etc. The Shach explains that Levi is the one who makes this claim and Reuven denies it; if Reuven admits it, the rule is different. This is logical, for what difference does it make that Shimon is an interested party? See there. Rabbi Akiva Eiger writes: I am astonished, for since Shimon is an interested party, the written instrument that serves as the instrument of acquisition is itself invalid and the note was not transferred. What difference does Reuven’s admission make? The debt was not acquired by means of this invalid written assignment. This is clear. See Ketzot HaChoshen.
