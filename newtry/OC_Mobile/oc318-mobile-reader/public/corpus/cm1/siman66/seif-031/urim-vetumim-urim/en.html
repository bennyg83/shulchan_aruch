Provided he says to him, etc. Maggid Mishneh adds that he must also say, “Acquire it incidentally with the land.” See Tumim. Rashba holds that even if the debtor instructed witnesses to write a loan document for the lender and deliver it to him, or to hold it that day until he later consents and he later does consent, a separate written assignment and delivery to the lender is still required unless it is a deed of kinyan. The Shulchan Aruch, Rama, and the other authorities did not cite this, and several places imply that they disagree. See Tumim for the reconciliation.
