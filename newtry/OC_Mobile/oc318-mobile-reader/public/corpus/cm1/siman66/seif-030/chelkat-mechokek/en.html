And some disagree and hold that the ketubah must be written, etc. This is Mordechai's view. He asks that one mishnah implies that specifically Kohanim and distinguished families write four hundred zuz, while another teaches that any person who wishes may add even one hundred maneh. He answers that an ordinary person must write the principal one hundred or two hundred separately and the addition separately, whereas such distinguished families write directly, "four hundred zuz that are fitting for you." Tosafot, however, explain the difference differently: those families write the wording "that are fitting for you," and even if no ketubah document was written, she collects four hundred by the beit din condition.
