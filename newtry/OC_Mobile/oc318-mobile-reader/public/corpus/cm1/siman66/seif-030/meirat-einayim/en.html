And there is an authority who disagrees, etc. This refers to one who sells a promissory note secured by mortgaged property in his possession. Even though the purchaser took possession of the mortgaged property, since it is not entirely his and the borrower can redeem it with money whenever he wishes, it is treated like an ordinary debt.
