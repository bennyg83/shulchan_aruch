There in the name of Baal HaTerumot, gate 51, citing a responsum of the Raavad from the Rif in Ketubot: the Sages treated a deathbed gift like a Torah gift, as though it had already reached the recipient's hand. This shows that where the collateral was delivered to the purchaser, the original creditor cannot waive the debt.
There and there in the name of a responsum of the Raavad citing Rabbi Yosef ben Maran HaLevi. So too the Rashba in a responsum and Nimukei Yosef in the name of the Ritva.
The Raavad there in the name of Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash, because the creditor can be removed with money. So too the Tur in the name of a responsum of his father the Rosh, and the Beit Yosef in a responsum at the end of Sefer Chazeh HaTenufah.
