A non-Jew. Bava Batra 35b, even where this is advantageous, as stated in Bava Metzia 73b, "now, according to their laws..."
And a Jew. Bava Kamma 96a, "Whatever the first sold to the second..." and regarding improvement by a non-Jew, which was subject to a special enactment. Without that enactment the law would be the same as a sale to a Jew, and certainly so where the consequence is detrimental.
