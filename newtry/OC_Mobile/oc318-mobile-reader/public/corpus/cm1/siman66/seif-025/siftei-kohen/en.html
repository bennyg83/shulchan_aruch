And likewise whenever he gave, etc. A Jew who acquires from a non-Jew receives all enforceable rights that the non-Jew possessed, as the Rosh and Rama state elsewhere. The precise extent of those rights depends on the form of the obligation and the governing law of the transaction.
A Jew who sold to a non-Jew is treated as though he sold to a Jew. Since the sale of debt documents is legally effective, a sale to a non-Jew is likewise effective. The non-Jew purchaser receives the rights conveyed by the document, subject to the same halachic rules governing forgiveness and transfer.
