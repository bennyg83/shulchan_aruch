(Collected note) Or... Meaning that it was not his property. (End of note.)
(Collected note) The purchaser recovers... According to all opinions he recovers the full value of the document, as with any warranty. Even in a gift, if the donor expressly accepted warranty he is liable. He likewise recovers any appreciation if the debt document increased in value because the borrower became wealthy or similarly, just as with ordinary warranty, as stated in Bava Metzia 14a and 15b. This is the meaning of, "and if he produced..." and as stated there 35a that an appraisal made in error is void. (End of note.)
