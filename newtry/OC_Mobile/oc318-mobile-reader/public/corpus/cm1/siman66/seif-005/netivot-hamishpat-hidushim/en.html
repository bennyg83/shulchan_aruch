And he gave him authority. The underlying notes still belong to Reuven, so written transfer and delivery are required. But if the arrangement was that Shimon had the right to return the money to Reuven, then when Reuven later gives the notes to him as a gift, that gift is effectively a gift of the money itself and the notes automatically become his. Written transfer and delivery are then unnecessary. Shach. The same applies to partners who divided debt instruments: they do not require written transfer and delivery after division, and neither can later waive the other's portion. See Beurim.
