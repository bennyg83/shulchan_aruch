See Shach, seif katan 18, who is inclined to say that when partners divide debt instruments among themselves they still require writing and delivery so that one partner cannot later retract. According to Rama's responsum, siman 92, however, even forgiveness of the debt is no longer possible after the division, and Tzemach Tzedek siman 90 rules the same. It follows that no separate written assignment is required.
