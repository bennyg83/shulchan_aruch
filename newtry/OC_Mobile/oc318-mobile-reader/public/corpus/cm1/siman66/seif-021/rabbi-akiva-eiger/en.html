[Shach, letter 126, s.v. I also saw] And furthermore, why not say that it is discussing. Note: This is the text in Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Metzia in the name of the Ritva. See Responsa Maharan Sasson, siman 73, who already noted this difficulty against Tosafot and explained that Tosafot's words are to be understood as follows: regarding an unpaid guardian it was obvious to them that he is exempt even for negligence. Tosafot then added that the same is proved regarding a paid guardian, for otherwise, if a distinction could be drawn, the Gemara's question would be difficult. See there, and his words are most insightful. Nevertheless, the Shach's words remain correct against the Rambam, who rules that even an unpaid guardian is liable for negligence; against that position the Shach's rejection stands in full force.
