If Reuven holds a document against Shimon, Levi is one of its signatories, and Reuven sells it to Levi, Levi is not considered interested in the testimony. We do not suspect that he originally signed falsely so he could later purchase the document, because the other witness would not have agreed to false testimony for Levi's benefit. {Rama: This applies when Levi's signature appears on the document; he may not give oral testimony. If the document is sold to both signatories, this concern does arise. If the borrower does not admit the debt, they cannot collect.}
