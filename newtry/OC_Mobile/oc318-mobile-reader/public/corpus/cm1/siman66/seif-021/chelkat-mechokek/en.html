And both are calculated in provincial silver. This is Rambam's language in Hilchot Ishut 10:8. The Sages did not establish these amounts in pure silver, but in provincial coinage consisting of seven parts copper and one part silver. Thus two hundred zuz contain only twenty-five zuz of pure silver. Each zuz is one and one-half dirham by weight and equals ninety-six barleycorns. Therefore the pure-silver weight of the ketubah is twenty-five times ninety-six barleycorns, namely 2,400 barleycorns.
