To testify orally. When a person signed the note, his testimony is treated as though it was examined in beit din at the time of signing, when he was not yet interested in the matter. By contrast, when he testifies orally now, he is presently interested. We must therefore say that the case where his signature helps is one where witnesses saw the note already signed by Levi before Levi purchased it. Otherwise, perhaps he signed only now after becoming interested. Maharit.
