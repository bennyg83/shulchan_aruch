Orally. The reason is that once he has signed the document as a witness, his testimony is treated as though it had already been examined in beit din, and at that time he was not an interested party. When he testifies orally, however, at the time of his testimony he already has an interest in the matter. Thus the Sema. See the Taz for the distinction by which this does not contradict the rule in seif 22.
