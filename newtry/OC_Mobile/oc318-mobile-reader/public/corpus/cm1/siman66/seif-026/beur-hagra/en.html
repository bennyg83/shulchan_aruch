From here there is proof for Terumas HaDeshen's ruling in seif 1 that in such a case writing and delivery are unnecessary, as stated in seif 12 that whenever the debt cannot be forgiven, writing and delivery are unnecessary. This is contrary to the Shach there. The Shach follows his own approach here in adopting the second opinion, and he also rejects the Sema's view in seif katan 64, as he states in seif katan 88. He further writes there that even according to the first opinion, delivery alone does not acquire. His words require study: once the case is compared to ma'amad sheloshtan, even an oral debt is acquired, and writing and delivery are unnecessary, as shown by the Kiddushin sugya. (End of note.)
