r) Tur in siman 25 in the name of the Raavad, and he writes that Baal HaIttur likewise rules. The Rashba agrees in a responsum and supports the Raavad's view from Ameimar's statement that transfer in the presence of all three parties is as though the debtor said, "I obligate myself to you," etc., Gittin 13b. If so, its law is like a three-party transfer, as explained below in seif 29. The Ran likewise writes in Responsa, siman 69, that reason dictates this and that the leading later authorities have already agreed.
