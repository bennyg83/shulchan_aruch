If he did owe him, this document would already be subject to the purchaser by virtue of Rabbi Natan's rule even without its sale, and the seller, who is himself indebted to him, would have no power to waive it, as explained in siman 86. What Rama writes at the beginning of this siman, 'Likewise, if the seller has no means to pay,' means that he has no means to repay the purchaser the money he received from him for the purchase of the document.
