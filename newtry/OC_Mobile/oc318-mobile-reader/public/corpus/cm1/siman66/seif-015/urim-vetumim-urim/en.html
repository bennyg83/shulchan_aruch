Some say it must come into his possession. It is not enough that the witnesses merely write the assignment. Shach says he does not know the source of this rule, though it is reasonable. It appears to come from Baal HaTerumot, who writes that the seller must tell the witnesses to prepare the sale document stating that the buyer acquires the debt and all its liens, and afterward, if he wishes to complete the acquisition, he must cause the written assignment to reach the buyer together with delivery of the underlying instrument. The meaning is that the written assignment itself must be actually transferred to the buyer. See Derishah.
