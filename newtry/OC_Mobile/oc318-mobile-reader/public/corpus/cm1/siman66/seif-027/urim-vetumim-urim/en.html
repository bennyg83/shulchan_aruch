I wrote that according to Rama in Even HaEzer siman 28, only one who betroths a woman with collateral taken after the loan creates valid kiddushin, but not with collateral taken at the time of the loan. If so, the same distinction applies here according to these opinions: writing and delivery are required, and the debt remains subject to waiver. Likewise, according to Rama there, one who betroths with collateral received from a gentile does not create valid kiddushin. Here too, writing and delivery are required and the debt may be waived.
