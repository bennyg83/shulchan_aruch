He must return the document to the borrower. Rama above records an opinion that after the creditor waives the debt, the purchaser need not return the paper. Here the law of actual payment is different. Baal HaTerumot and Gidulei Terumah explain that Rashba's position allowing the purchaser to retain the paper concerns waiver, not payment. Once actual payment has occurred, the purchaser has no remaining claim at all and must return the borrower's document. The Shach rejects a distinction between payment and waiver, but in my view the distinction is sound. See what I wrote in siman 86 and above in this siman.
