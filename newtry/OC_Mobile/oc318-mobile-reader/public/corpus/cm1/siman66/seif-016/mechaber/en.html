If a creditor validly sells a debt document and both seller and buyer demand payment from the borrower, the beit din instructs the borrower to pay the buyer. If he pays the seller, the buyer may collect the money from the seller. If the borrower paid the seller before the buyer's demand, the borrower is exempt and the buyer has only a grievance against him. {Rama: Some say the buyer must return the document to the borrower.} The beit din requires the creditor to give the buyer what he collected. If the creditor spent it and cannot repay, the borrower remains exempt. Others say payment to the original creditor does not exempt the borrower from the buyer.
