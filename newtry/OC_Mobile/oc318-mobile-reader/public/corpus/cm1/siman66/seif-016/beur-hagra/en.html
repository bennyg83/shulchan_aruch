Beit din says. Bava Batra 173a: "Rather, that which was taught..." concerning acquisition of documents, etc.
And if he preceded. Because he can forgive the debt, as Nimukei Yosef writes in Bava Metzia 20a concerning the rule of Shmuel. Nimukei Yosef explains that this is not merely a migo, since if she had to assess and reimburse the entire value of the document she would prefer a receipt; rather, once she admits that it is paid, the payment itself functions as her forgiveness. See there.
