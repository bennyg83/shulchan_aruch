Through ma'amad sheloshtan. This phrase also modifies the case of giving. The reason is that by this transfer, in which the lender places the recipient before the borrower and directs that what the borrower holds be given to the recipient, it is as though the borrower newly established the debt in favor of the purchaser. This is effective so that the original lender cannot later waive it, as explained in seif 23. This differs from seif 36 below, which does not involve ma'amad sheloshtan but a transfer by writing and delivery. There the original lender can waive the debt. Understand this carefully.
