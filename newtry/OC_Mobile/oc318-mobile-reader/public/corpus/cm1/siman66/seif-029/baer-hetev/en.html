All three. This is because it is as though the borrower converted the obligation into a direct loan owed to the purchaser, which is effective so that the original lender cannot forgive it, as stated in seif 23. This differs from seif 36, which does not concern a transfer in the presence of all three parties but rather a transfer by writing and delivery, and therefore the lender can forgive it. Thus writes the Sema. See the Shach, who discusses the matter at length and seeks to prove from the Gemara and poskim that even a debt transferred in the presence of all three parties can be forgiven.
