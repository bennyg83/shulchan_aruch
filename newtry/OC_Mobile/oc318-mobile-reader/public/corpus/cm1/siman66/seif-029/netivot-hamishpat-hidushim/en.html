Through assignment in the presence of all three parties. The Shach rules that although the debt itself is acquired through acquisition together with land, the physical note is not acquired except through written transfer and delivery. Without written transfer and delivery, the purchaser has only an oral loan claim until the seller formally transfers the note. If the note was formally transferred and the seller then waived the debt, the Shach holds that the debt becomes only an oral loan. See Beurim: here I agree with Tumim that it remains a documented loan.
