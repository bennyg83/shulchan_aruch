A person on his deathbed. The Rambam, Hilchot Zechiyah chapter 10, and the Tur and Shulchan Aruch in siman 253, seif 20, write that if a person on his deathbed says, 'Give this debt document to so-and-so,' the recipient acquires the document as though the giver had written and delivered it, even though the recipient did not perform meshichah. The Maggid Mishneh and Ran explain that even though he did not say, 'and all its liens,' it is as though he said so, and the Maggid Mishneh agrees with this as practical law. This also appears to be the Ran's view and is the view of Baal HaTerumot. The Rashba, however, holds otherwise. He writes that only when the dying person includes all his property will the transfer of documents take effect even if he did not say, 'and all the liens contained in them.' But if he specified the documents, he must say, 'and all the liens contained in them,' because whatever would not be effective for a healthy person is not effective for a person on his deathbed. The practical law requires study. See Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 4. Shach.
Who relinquished. The Shach writes that this applies specifically at the time of the wedding. Maharik also addresses such a case, and so write the Rivash in simanim 64 and 102 and the Maggid Mishneh cited by Beit Yosef in Even HaEzer at the end of siman 69. His wording, 'one who writes for her at the time she enters,' means while she is still betrothed, excluding our practice, where betrothal is performed at the time of the wedding, in which case a relinquishment is ineffective. See Even HaEzer at the beginning of siman 94.
Inheritance. The same law applies to a debt document that one gives to his daughter together with her offspring. Sema.
Included. In ordinary speech, documents are not included in the term movable property. Sema. The Shach writes in the name of Nimukei Yosef that even if one says, 'my property, land and movables,' documents are not included, because the general category includes only what is specified in the particulars. He must say, 'land and my other property.' See there.
