Some say that delivery of the document must precede the writing of the assignment. If the document to be transferred is not physically present with the transferor, even if he transfers it through a kinyan chalipin, this is not considered delivery. Rama: If a royal document granted Reuven and Shimon jointly the right to collect the revenues of a village, and one claims that the other waived his share to him while the document is in his possession, that claim is not valid. A document of this kind is acquired only through writing and delivery, since it contains no debt obligation but only a royal grant of authority. Nevertheless, if one partner has already collected and says that the other waived to him what he collected, what he collected remains his. See below, seif 17. If Reuven has documents against Shimon deposited with him and Shimon waived them to Reuven, Reuven does not acquire them, because writing and delivery are required.
