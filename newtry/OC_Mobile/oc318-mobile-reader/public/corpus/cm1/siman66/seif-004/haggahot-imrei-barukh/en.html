(Shach, seif katan 2) If so, it is obvious that there is, etc. Note: This requires study, for where the disparity is of a kind in which no reasonable person could err, the laws of ona'ah do not apply. Ritva explains this explicitly in his novellae to Kiddushin 47a: 'The law of ona'ah does not apply, for it exceeded the amount in which a person's judgment could err.'
