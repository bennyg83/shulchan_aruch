A deed of transfer, etc. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: This is difficult to me. This document is itself a deed of acquisition, for through it he acquires the debt document, as stated above in seif 2. Yet according to the Mechaber below in siman 68, a deed of acquisition executed in a non Jewish court is invalid. This requires further study. End of quote.
