He explains there that such a kinyan is merely a kinyan of words, and words are not acquired by words. So writes Rav Hai Gaon. Nimukei Yosef in chapter HaSefinah writes in the name of the Ri and Rabbenu Yonah that a person cannot transfer to his fellow land currently subject to another's lien, because it is not presently his. We rule like Rava that a creditor acquires only from the time of collection, Pesachim 31a. So too the Ran in chapter 1 of Bava Metzia in the name of the Rashba. Mahari Weil, siman 162, writes that since the sale does not stand, the seller is only a paid bailee over the money and is believed to say, 'I repaid,' through a migo that he could have said, 'It was lost through accident.'
