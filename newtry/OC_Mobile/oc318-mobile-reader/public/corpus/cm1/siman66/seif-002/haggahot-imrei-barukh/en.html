(Shach, there) They treated it like a Torah-law gift. Note: According to the Shach, the Ran should have said that they treated him like a Torah-law heir and as though the money had already reached his hand. Rather, Rif's intent is that in truth he is not literally an heir. They treated it like a Torah-law gift only so that the dying person's mind not become distraught. Lest one object that there is no circumstance in which a Torah-law gift can take effect for a dying person, Rif explains that they treated the matter as though the money had already reached the recipient's hand.
