Rather, he must say, etc. And the transfer must be effected through that writing. Meaning, the witnesses must specify that the transferor told them to write in his name, "Acquire it, etc." This is the meaning of "he must write," namely that they should write this formula in the transferor's name. The statement, "and some say that this writing must come into his possession," teaches that one should not think that since the transfer was made to him through the witnesses, the written instrument need not actually reach him. See the Perishah.
And he must acquire through that writing, etc. The Baal HaTerumot concludes this passage: "And afterward it is necessary to make an acquisition from him, by which he transfers to him the document that he delivered to him." See the Derishah for my explanation of his meaning.
