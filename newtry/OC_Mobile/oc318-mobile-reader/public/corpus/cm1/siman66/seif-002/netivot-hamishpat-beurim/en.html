That it come into his possession. Ketzot HaChoshen questions this from siman 235, where a minor can sell a promissory note. Since such a transfer requires a document, and a minor has no legal capacity for a document, how can the sale be effective? See there. This is no difficulty. There, the statement that it is ineffective applies specifically to a kinyan sudar performed before witnesses, because that kinyan is made for the purpose of having the witnesses write a document, and such a document cannot be created for a minor. Here, however, the minor himself can write in his own handwriting, "Acquire it and all of its liens," and this is effective even for a minor.
