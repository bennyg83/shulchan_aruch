The ruling is nevertheless reasonable: because written obligations are acquired only through a written instrument of transfer, the transfer deed resembles a deed conveying land and must reach the transferee’s possession. Witnesses are not essential, for witnesses were created only against false denial; a written deed stating that the field is sold or given effects acquisition even without witnesses.
