And if he did not write this, he did not acquire it. See Baer Heitev, seif katan 3, citing Mahari Weil. The Shach challenges this and writes that the Gemara and all the poskim clearly indicate that the money has the status of a loan. Dagul Merevavah comments that Tosafot at the end of Eruvin 81b, beginning with the words ‘Perhaps they will say,’ clearly implies that in any event he was no more than a paid custodian. See Tumim, Ketzot HaChoshen, and Netivot HaMishpat on this matter, and Responsa Mishkenot Yaakov, siman 30.
