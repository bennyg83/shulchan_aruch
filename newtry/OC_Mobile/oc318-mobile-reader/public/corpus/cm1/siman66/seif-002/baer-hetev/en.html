That it come. The Shach writes that he found no authority who expressly states this, but it is logical. Since documents are acquired only through writing and delivery, the deed of assignment is comparable to a deed for land, which must reach the recipient's hand, as stated at the beginning of siman 191. According to this, it appears that the transfer is effective even without witnesses, because witnesses were created only to prevent false denial, as stated below regarding one who writes on paper, 'My field is sold to you.' Thus the Shach.
