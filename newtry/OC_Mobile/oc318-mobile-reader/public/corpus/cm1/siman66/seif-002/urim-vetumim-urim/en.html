Only by writing and delivery. Shach cites Ritva that if a person says, “This debt document is for charity to the poor,” the poor acquire it because a declaration to sacred purposes is like delivery to an ordinary person. That principle means delivery in a legally effective form, which here would still include the required written assignment. See Tumim. I discuss this at length and note that Yoreh Deah 258 rules the opposite, that the poor do not acquire a debt document without written assignment and delivery. Since acquisition of debt instruments is itself rabbinic, a declaration to charity does not dispense with the rabbinic mode of acquisition. Written assignment and delivery are still required.
