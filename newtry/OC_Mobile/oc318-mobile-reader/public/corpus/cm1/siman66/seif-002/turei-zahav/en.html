The Sema asks why the Rama did not add that the bearer must derive his right from the first holder, as in siman 50, and therefore explains that witnesses must testify that the purchaser transferred it to him. This is very surprising. A bearer document acquired by exchange is like other movable property, and the person possessing it is believed to say that he acquired it. This case too concerns one deriving title from the first holder. The Rama did not repeat what he had already stated in its primary place, siman 50.
