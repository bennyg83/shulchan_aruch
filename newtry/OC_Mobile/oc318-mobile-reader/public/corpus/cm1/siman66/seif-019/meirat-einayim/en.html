'Give it to Shimon' in the presence of all three. Meaning, Reuven told Levi to give it to Shimon while Reuven, Shimon, and Levi were all present together. This transfers an oral debt through ma'amad sheloshtan, as will be explained at the beginning of siman 126. The Tur and Mechaber teach here that although Shimon thereby acquires the loan and its lien against the borrower and purchasers, he still cannot compel Reuven to hand him the physical document. A promissory note contains two distinct acquisitions: ownership of the physical document and ownership of the debt lien. One is not automatically acquired through transfer of the other. So writes the Tur.
We compel the lender to produce, etc. The Tur's wording is that the beneficiary's evidence is in the lender's possession, and one may not suppress another person's testimony or evidence. The Tur and Mechaber write similarly at the end of siman 16.
