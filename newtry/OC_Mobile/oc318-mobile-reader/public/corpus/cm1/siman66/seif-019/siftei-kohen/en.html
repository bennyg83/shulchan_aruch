Shimon acquired, etc. In my view, maamad sheloshtan transfers only the debt itself, not the status of a written loan. Therefore purchasers of the debtor’s property are not bound to Shimon, and Levi may claim that he paid Shimon unless Shimon possesses the document through proper writing and delivery. The original creditor need not surrender the document merely because he redirected payment, for it remains security until Shimon is actually paid. If the redirection discharged an existing debt owed by Reuven to Shimon, then if Levi denies payment or collection from encumbered property becomes necessary, Reuven must execute a written transfer of the document to Shimon or pay him directly. If the redirection was merely a gift, Reuven need not make any further transfer; Shimon receives only what was given by maamad sheloshtan and cannot collect from encumbered property without a proper transfer. Reuven may nevertheless later choose to transfer the document properly, after which Shimon may collect and Levi may no longer plead payment.
