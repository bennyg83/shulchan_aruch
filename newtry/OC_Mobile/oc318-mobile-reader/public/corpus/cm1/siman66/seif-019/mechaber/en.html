If Reuven holds a debt document against Levi and says to him in the presence of all three parties, 'The one hundred zuz you owe me by document, give to Shimon,' Shimon acquires the debt even though the document was not delivered. Purchasers become liable to Shimon just as they were to Reuven. Shimon cannot, however, compel the creditor to hand him the physical document. Shimon claims directly from the borrower; if the borrower denies the debt or collection must be made from encumbered property, the beit din compels the creditor to produce the document and adjudicates upon it. If the borrower pays Shimon, the physical document is removed from the creditor and returned to the borrower.
