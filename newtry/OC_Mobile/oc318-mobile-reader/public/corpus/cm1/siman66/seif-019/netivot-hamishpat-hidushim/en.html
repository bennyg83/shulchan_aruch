Beit din compels the lender to produce the note. The Shach writes that according to what he later rules in seif 29, the body of the note is not acquired through assignment in the presence of all three parties, only the debt itself. Therefore purchasers of the borrower's property are not subject to Shimon through the note and can say, "You are not our litigant." Levi too is believed to say, "I paid." Accordingly, beit din does not compel the lender to produce the note. However, the lender may afterward transfer the note formally through written transfer and delivery.
