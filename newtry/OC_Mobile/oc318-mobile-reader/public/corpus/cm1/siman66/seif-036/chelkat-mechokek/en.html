Even if he wrote for her, 'that which is fitting for you.' One might have thought that she does not collect, since legally this amount is not fitting for her but only the standard amount due to other women, and the language of an added undertaking was not written. The ruling teaches that since this is the accepted custom, it has the status of an enactment for them. Although the Gemara said this concerning distinguished families, that was not exclusive but reflected the usual case, because in ordinary families such a claim would not be accepted.
