To pay. The Sema writes that it appears that even according to those who disagree with the gloss in seif 32, here he must pay the entire value of the document, because the recipient can say, 'I provided benefits to the giver corresponding to the entire value of the document.' The Shach disagrees and holds that according to those who disagree there, he is exempt here as well. See there, where he brings proof from the authorities.
