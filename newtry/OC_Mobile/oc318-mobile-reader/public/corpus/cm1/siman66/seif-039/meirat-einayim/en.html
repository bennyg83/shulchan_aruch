No oath is imposed concerning them, etc. They too are excluded by the verse, 'If a man gives to his fellow money or vessels.' See the beginning of siman 95. Even if the claimant says he saw the custodian intentionally damage them and the custodian denies it, no Torah oath is imposed, only a rabbinic hesset oath. If the claimant says he saw that the custodian was negligent in guarding them and they were stolen or broken, then even if the unpaid custodian or paid custodian admits that negligence, he is exempt, as the Mechaber writes in the following seif.
