According to Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash, etc. Baal HaTerumot explains that, because the holder can be removed by payment, Rabbi Yosef Halevi ibn Migash treats his right as an ordinary loan; even after taking possession, it is acquired only through writing and delivery. This appears tied to his view that the creditor can forgive the debt and may not collect from heirs without an oath. Since the primary ruling there is that he cannot forgive, the practical law here follows the Geonim.
