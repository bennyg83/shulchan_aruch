He did not acquire. The purchase money in the seller's hands has the status of property for which he is a paid custodian. He is believed with an oath to say it was lost through unavoidable circumstances. Therefore even if he acknowledged receipt of the money through a document, he can later say, “I repaid,” through a migo that he could have claimed unavoidable loss. Mahari Weil, Darkei Moshe, and Sema. Shach objects, but his objection is not clear, as I explain in Tumim.
