And the Geonim. According to the Mechaber's ruling that one cannot waive a debt secured by a land mortgage, the law follows the Geonim, Shach. See Beurim. The assignee may retain the mortgaged property until he is paid, but he cannot sue the borrower and collect from the mortgage after the original lender waived the debt where the pledge was not acquired under Rabbi Yitzchak's rule, such as a pledge given at the time of the loan or a pledge belonging to a non Jew.
