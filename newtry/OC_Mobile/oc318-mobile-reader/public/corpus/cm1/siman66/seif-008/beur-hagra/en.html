(Supplement) And the Geonim. As stated in Bava Batra 77a regarding three types of documents: once he takes possession, and as explained in seif 10. Even the authority who disagrees there concedes regarding the deed to the land itself, because it is the land's reins. Here, however, he acquires only the document. Therefore a written assignment is needed to acquire the underlying debt, though no acquisition together with land is required, because the document concerns the land itself. Although the creditor has only a lien upon the land, he can transfer that lien, as indicated there, 44b. Ri Migash holds that only the debtor, who owns the substance of the property, can encumber movables together with land. A creditor cannot do so, since even consecrating the property has no effect, as stated in Pesachim, because the creditor acquires only from the time of collection. For this reason Nimukei Yosef writes in Bava Batra that even a kinyan and delivery are ineffective. See below, siman 103, seif 6. Sefer HaTerumot cites Rabbeinu Chananel that Pesachim's rule applies even though the creditor ultimately collects automatically only in a possessory mortgage. Ramban holds that since Rava's reason depends upon the debtor's ability to redeem it, the same applies even to a possessory mortgage. This is the meaning of the phrase here, since he can redeem it.
