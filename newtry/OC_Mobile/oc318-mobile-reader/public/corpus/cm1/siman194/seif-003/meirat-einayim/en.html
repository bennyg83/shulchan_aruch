With movable property, however, he acquires, etc. The reason is that only with acquisition of land is there room to say that the purchaser does not rely upon the transaction. With movable property, which the purchaser takes immediately into his home, we do not have the same concern that the non-Jew will forcibly remove it from his possession. Whether a non-Jew acquires movable property from a Jew through payment was already discussed at the beginning of this siman, where I explained that Rambam and the other authorities disagree.
