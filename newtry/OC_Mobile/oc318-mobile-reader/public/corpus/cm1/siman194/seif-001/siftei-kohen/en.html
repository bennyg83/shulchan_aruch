A non-Jew does not acquire through chazakah. See the Sema, seif katan 1. His words appear to imply that all agree that a Jew purchasing movable property from a non-Jew acquires through payment, and this is also explicit at the end of this siman. That is incorrect. The beginning of the second chapter of Bekhorot proves explicitly that a Jew purchasing from a non-Jew and a non-Jew purchasing from a Jew are governed by one law. The Maggid Mishneh likewise implies that according to the Ramban and the other Geonim both cases have one rule. The Rosh explicitly rules there that in either direction acquisition is effected only by meshichah, and the same is implied by the other poskim and explained by the later authorities, as I wrote in Siftei Kohen on Yoreh Deah, siman 320, seif katan 8. See Responsa Maharshadam, siman 171.
