A non-Jew who sells land to a Jew, receives the money, but does not write a deed. It contains three seifim:
A non-Jew does not acquire land through chazakah nor through payment of money alone; he acquires only through a deed together with payment. A Jew who purchases from a non-Jew is treated like the non-Jew for this purpose and acquires only through a deed. Rama: A lease from a non-Jew, however, is acquired by payment alone. Beit Yosef, siman 192. Likewise, if the purchaser expressly stipulated that payment alone should effect acquisition, he acquires immediately. Beit Yosef to this siman.
