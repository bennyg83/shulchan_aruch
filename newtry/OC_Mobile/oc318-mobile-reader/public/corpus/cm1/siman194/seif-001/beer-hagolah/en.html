Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilkhot Zechiyah, law 14. Maggid Mishneh writes that it is explained in Chezkat HaBatim that a gentile transfers ownership only by deed, based on Rav Yehudah in the name of Shmuel, Bava Batra 54b. The same applies that a gentile does not acquire without the required form. Kesef Mishneh writes that this appears to be Rav Yehudah's statement, "A Jew who comes by virtue of a gentile," at the end of 35a, which Rambam understands as concerning acquisition, as Maggid Mishneh writes in chapter 1 of Hilkhot Mekhirah.
Meaning, together with payment of the money.
