By taking possession. The Sema discusses this law at length and it appears from his words that when a Jew buys movable property from a non-Jew, all agree that money effects acquisition. He likewise states this explicitly at the end of this siman. This is incorrect, for the beginning of the second chapter of Bekhorot explicitly indicates that a Jew buying from a non-Jew and a non-Jew buying from a Jew have the same law. The Maggid Mishneh likewise indicates that according to Ramban and the other Geonim both cases are the same. The Rosh explicitly rules in Bekhorot that in either direction acquisition is effected only by meshichah, and this is also evident from the other poskim and the later authorities, as I wrote in Yoreh Deah siman 320, seif katan 8. See Responsa Maharashdam, siman 171. Shach. The Taz writes that here the discussion is not about a three-year chazakah but about an act of acquisition itself, such as locking. There is no dispute of fact; both parties admit the sale, but the seller wishes to retract. Therefore the text says, "just as a Jew..." See there.
