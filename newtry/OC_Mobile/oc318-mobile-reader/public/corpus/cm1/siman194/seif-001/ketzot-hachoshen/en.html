Nevertheless, rental. Taz disagrees with Rama and says Beit Yosef allowed a rental from a gentile to be acquired by money only according to Rashbam, who does not distinguish between acquisition from a Jew and from a gentile. According to the authorities who hold that money does not acquire a gentile's property even where it would acquire from a Jew, Taz says rental should likewise not be acquired by money. I find support for Rama in Tosafot. Bava Metzia discusses the case of one who gives consecrated money to a bathhouse attendant and says that misuse occurs immediately because no meshichah remains to be done. Tosafot asks that rental of land normally requires hazakah and answers that a land rental can be acquired with money. When challenged that this appears to refute Rabbi Yochanan, Tosafot says Rabbi Yochanan can establish the bathhouse attendant as a gentile. Rosh gives the same answer. Since the case is specifically a gentile, and yet the rental is treated as acquired by money, Tosafot and Rosh clearly support Rama that rental from a gentile can be acquired by money.
