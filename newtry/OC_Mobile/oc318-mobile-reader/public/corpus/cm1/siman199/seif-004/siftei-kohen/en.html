If a treasurer gave money to another person for the needs of hekdesh, the treasurer may not retract, for in an ordinary transaction the party would be subject to the curse of Mi Shepara. The case concerning supplying flour to the Temple presents no difficulty, as the Ran explains in the first chapter of Kiddushin.
