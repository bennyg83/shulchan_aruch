[Rama] According to Torah law. Note: See Magen Avraham, siman 369, and Tosefet Shabbat there. According to this, the view of the Rambam, who writes so in chapter 6 of Hilchot Avadim, and the Maggid Mishneh, who writes that this is the primary ruling, proves contrary to Mahari Weil. If so, one can say "kim li" and retract. See Responsa Mutzal MeEsh, siman 42.
