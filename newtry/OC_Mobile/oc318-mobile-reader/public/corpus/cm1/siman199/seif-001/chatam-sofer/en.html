Mechaber, seif 3, 'the ox died.' Note: The same applies on Purim, according to what the Magen Avraham writes in siman 696, seif katan 15, that one is obligated to eat meat. If so, it is no less necessary than wine for Kiddush. The reason it is not counted among the four periods is that they were accustomed specifically to calves rather than cows; see Bava Metzia 78b. But regarding insisting upon the strict Torah-law rule, it is no less necessary than wine for Kiddush.
