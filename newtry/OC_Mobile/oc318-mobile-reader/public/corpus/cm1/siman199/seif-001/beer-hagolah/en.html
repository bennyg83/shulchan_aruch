This was inferred from Rashi's explanation there that he did not care to ask how many there were. The same is implied by the students of the Rashba. Regarding Rambam's statement in chapter 5 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 3, that one scooped money without weight or count, the Kesef Mishneh writes that perhaps Rambam merely stated the ordinary case and the same law applies where the buyer knew. The Bach likewise rules so.
