Other movable property to sell. See Responsa Shav Yaakov, part 3, siman 170, who writes that the same rule applies, and all the more so, to those very same movable items. It is exceedingly uncommon for one to sell property and afterward repurchase those very same utensils. The authorities merely spoke in the more common case. Moreover, the wording of the Gemara, "there are payments that function like barter," cannot be explained as referring to the same object, because that formulation applies only to a different item. The present rule is therefore derived by an a fortiori argument. He writes that one who disputes this is astonishing. See there. At first glance, however, this does not appear consistent with the words of Maggid Mishneh cited by Sema, who explains the matter on the basis of what people normally do.
