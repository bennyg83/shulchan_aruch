Many commentators write that now that the practice is to state that an obligation is neither an asmachta nor a mere standard form, every ambiguous expression is construed in favor of the document's holder. {Rama: Any wording broad enough to enhance the holder's rights, and ordinarily understood that way by listeners, is construed accordingly, even to the holder's benefit (Beit Yosef, section 5, in the name of Responsa of the Rashba). See also siman 523.}
