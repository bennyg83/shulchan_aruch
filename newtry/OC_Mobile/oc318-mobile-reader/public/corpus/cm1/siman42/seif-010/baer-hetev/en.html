Those who hear it. The Rivash likewise writes that documents are interpreted according to ordinary human speech. The Beit Yosef cites the Rashba regarding one who mortgaged a house to another, with upper stories built one above another, and wrote that he mortgaged "from the lower levels of my house until the end of the uppermost." He later claimed that he had mortgaged only the single upper story directly over the house, while the scribe said that his intent had been to include all the upper stories. The ruling favors the lender. Sema. See Responsa Maharanach, siman 21; Maharit, siman 122; Responsa Maharshach, simanim 150 and 195; and Responsa Maharshdam, siman 366.
