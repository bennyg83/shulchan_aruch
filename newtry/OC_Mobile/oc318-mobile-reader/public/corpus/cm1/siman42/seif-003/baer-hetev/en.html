No. The Tur writes that even if the defendant does not raise the claim, the judge must raise it on his behalf. The Shach writes that the two statements do not conflict. The Mechaber discusses a defendant who does not raise this defect at all. The Tur discusses one who claims that he owes nothing under the document or makes a similar general denial. Although he does not specifically allege that this alteration was made, beit din raises that point for him. This appears to be the primary practical ruling. See Responsa Mabit, part 2, siman 53.
