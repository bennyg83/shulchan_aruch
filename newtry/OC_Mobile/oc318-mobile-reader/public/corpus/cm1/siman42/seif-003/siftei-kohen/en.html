The Tur writes that even when the defendant does not state it, the judge must raise it in order to bring the law to its truth. It appears that the authorities do not disagree. The Ramah discusses a defendant who never asserted any defect in the document. The Tur discusses one who claims that he owes nothing under this document. Once he makes that general denial, even though he did not identify the precise alteration, the court may argue for him that the specific forgery occurred. This is also the correct practical ruling. See Responsa Mabit, part II, siman 53.
