And when he begins to give it to her. The same applies if he gave all the money at once and said, "Behold, you are betrothed to me," and she immediately threw it away. She is not betrothed, as ruled above in siman 30:6, where she took it and threw it into the sea or before him, which demonstrates that she did not desire the kiddushin. This differs from a case where she first said, "Betroth me," and he then gave her the money; there, throwing it away after acceptance does not undo the kiddushin because she had initially revealed her consent.
