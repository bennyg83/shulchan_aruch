(See there: my master the Rosh wrote so, etc., through “in the name of Rabbenu Saadiah, etc.”) The Tur below, siman 113, seif 4, does not explain the matter this way. The Beit Yosef there cites Baal HaTerumot and Ramban similarly. Rabbenu Yeruham himself writes that standard document forms are not conclusive, for otherwise we would fear the scribe wrote them on his own without instruction from the obligor. The Rambam likewise rules this way in chapter 18 of Hilchot Malveh. There is therefore no proof that such language is effective absent the rule that the document-holder’s hand is on the lower side.
