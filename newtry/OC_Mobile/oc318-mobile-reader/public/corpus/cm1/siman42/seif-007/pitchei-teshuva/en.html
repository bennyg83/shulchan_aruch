Where it would cause the document to be void, no. See Responsa Rabbi Moshe Rothenburg, Choshen Mishpat, siman 8, and the gloss there. He explains that this principle is stated specifically concerning a written document. With respect to a matter acquired orally by a verbal undertaking, if there is uncertainty concerning the meaning, we always say that the burden is on the party seeking to extract, even where this causes the entire oral undertaking to become void. A written document is different because the party performed an affirmative act by writing and signing it. See there, and see what I write below in siman 245, seif 1, seif katan 1.
