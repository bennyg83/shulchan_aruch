(Anthology) That kefel, etc. Ri Migash writes that in either event we follow the lower expression, whether the discrepancy resulted from retraction or from removal by a fly. Rambam, however, explains that we must also suspect that a fly lengthened a letter, turning samekh into kuf. Therefore both concerns are necessary.
(Anthology) That kefel is less than sefel. These are Rambam's words, and he is also concerned that a fly lengthened the letter. Rashbam and all the other commentators do not explain it this way, for they do not suspect lengthening. A practical difference is that according to Rambam, if one seized property it is not removed from him, as stated in seif 8, even though here there is a compound doubt. See siman 390. Another difference concerns a receipt, as stated in the gloss to seif 8. A further difference concerns the statement in seif 5, 'and it is possible to reconcile them,' etc.; according to Rambam this applies only to the rule that the holder of the document has the weaker position.
