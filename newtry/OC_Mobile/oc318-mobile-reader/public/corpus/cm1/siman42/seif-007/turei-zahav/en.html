(Seif 11: that sum was erased.) If a document states “selas” but leaves a blank beside the word without any amount, and the borrower trusted the lender to insert whatever sum he wished, the lender may not collect even two selas. The document is capable of alteration, for he could insert more than was lent. As stated at the beginning of the siman, the fact that the defect could have been repaired before presentation to the beit din does not help, because once the witnesses originally signed an invalid document it cannot later be repaired, as stated in siman 45, seif 16.
