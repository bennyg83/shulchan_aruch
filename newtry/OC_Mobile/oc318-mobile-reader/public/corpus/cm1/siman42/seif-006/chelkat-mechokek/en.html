And one of them is a relative. The second witness is not disqualified through joining with the relative, as explained in Choshen Mishpat siman 36. The case is one where he did not join with the relative at the time of seeing the event, according to the Rosh, who requires both elements, namely intention at the time of seeing to testify and subsequently coming to beit din and testifying. Alternatively, according to Rif, he is believed to say that he did not know of the relationship. Because of the stringency of kiddushin, we take this into account initially. See Choshen Mishpat siman 36 and Responsa Maharam Padua, siman 37, regarding this matter.
