If one letter was erased. Torat Chaim objects to the reading “erased,” because the example uses the names Chanan and Chanani. Since Chanani is written with a bent final letter and Chanan with a final letter, it would appear obvious that the money cannot be given to Chanan. This is no difficulty. Although it is not paid to Chanan, it might also have been withheld from Chanani because erasure of one letter could have been treated like erasure of two letters, which invalidates the document. The ruling teaches otherwise. Torat Chaim's additional objections are likewise not compelling.
