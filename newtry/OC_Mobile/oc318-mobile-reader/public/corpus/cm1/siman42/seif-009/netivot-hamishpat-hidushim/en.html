A formulation that is ineffective. For example, a dying person used a language of leaving property where there is a dispute whether that wording works. In such a case we may say that he erred and thought the wording effective. If he used wording that all agree is ineffective, such as "I will give," the Sema holds that the document is also void. The Shach writes that where no authority considers the wording effective, we do not attribute the matter to a mistake.
