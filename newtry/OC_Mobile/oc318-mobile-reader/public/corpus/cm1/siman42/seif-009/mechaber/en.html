The rule that a document is construed against its holder applies only to cases like those above, where the document is not thereby entirely nullified. It does not apply where that interpretation would void the document. Thus, if a document states that a person obligated himself to pay another one hundred zuz on Pesach, he must pay on the first coming Pesach and cannot say that he will pay only on a Pesach several years later. The same applies in similar cases. {Rama: Some say this is so only when the wording cannot be attributed to an error. If the grantor may have erred, as when he used wording that is ineffective for a gift, the document is indeed void (Responsa of the Rashba and Maharick, shoresh 94).}
