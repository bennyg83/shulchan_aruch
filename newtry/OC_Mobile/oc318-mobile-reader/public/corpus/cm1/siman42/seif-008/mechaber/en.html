In all the cases discussed above in which an ambiguous document is construed against its holder, if the holder has already seized movable property, it is not removed from him. {Rama: All the more so, in construing a receipt, it is interpreted in favor of the holder because he is in possession (Nimukei Yosef, chapter Get Pashut, in the name of the Ra'ah), contrary to those who say that even a receipt is construed against its holder (ibid., in the name of his student, the Ritva).}
