The Tur, and likewise the Rambam at the end of Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh, and the Rif in chapter HaSho'el. The Maggid Mishneh writes there from the rule, 'Set two against two and leave the money in its owner's possession. If one seized it, we do not remove it from him,' Ketubot 22a. This implies even if he seized it before witnesses, and the same applies to any doubtful document.
