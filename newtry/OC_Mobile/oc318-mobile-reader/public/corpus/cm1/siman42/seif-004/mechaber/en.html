Care must be taken not to write at the end of a line a number from three through ten, lest ten be altered to twenty or three to thirty. {Rama: If he wrote the full words 'three' or 'ten,' it is acceptable, because there is no concern that they can be falsified (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and Hagahos Maimoniyos).} If such a word would fall at the end of a line, he should repeat the matter as many times as necessary until it appears within a line. He should likewise avoid writing a sum with single Hebrew letters, such as beis or dalet, lest they be altered into kaf or reish. {Rama: If he failed to do so, the document can be falsified and is invalid. If he used numerical notation or abbreviations that cannot be altered, it is valid (Hagahos Mordechai, Gittin).}
