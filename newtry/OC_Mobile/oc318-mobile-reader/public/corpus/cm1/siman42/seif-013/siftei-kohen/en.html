If it states “silver dinars” or “silver, dinars,” and so forth. The Tur reads “dinarin” with a final nun. The Sema proposes changing both phrases to “dinarei kesef” and “kesef dinarei,” without the nun. In my humble opinion the Tur's text should not be altered. Tosafot in Bava Batra state that only the forms with “dinarin” clearly determine the meaning. The forms without the nun remain doubtful, and if one seized property it is not removed from him. The Rosh's abridgment and the Levush likewise preserve the reading with the nun. See the Bach.
