Therefore, if. Meaning, where its witnesses are inside it. The Gemara there 165 says, "in a place where the custom is, etc." Nimukei Yosef explains this regarding a folded document whose witnesses were written inside, where the form itself is treated as significant. In Kiddushin 33, "and he did not say to him," implies that even without an explicit instruction the form can be significant. See Tosafot there. Nowadays, however, where one does not contemplate a folded document, there is no reason to say the unusual form is significant, and the document should be valid.
