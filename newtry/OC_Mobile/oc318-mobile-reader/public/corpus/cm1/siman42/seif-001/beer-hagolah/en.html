The language of the Tur, in accordance with Rabbi Elazar, who said that Rabbi Eliezer validated such writing only for gittin, Gittin 23b. For other documents he did not, because the verse says, 'Place them in an earthen vessel so that they may endure many days.' So rule the Rif and Rosh. The Rosh writes that although Rabbi Elazar was a student of Rabbi Yochanan, the halachah follows him here because the anonymous Gemara challenges from this premise on Gittin 11a, asking that writing incapable of forgery should be required. So too the Rambam, chapter 27 of Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh.
