Immediately. This means that the document is invalid even though there is no concern for falsification because the witnesses remember what was written in it. The reason is that an instrument must be fit to endure for many days. Thus writes the Sema. The Shach writes that, as a matter of law, since the Rosh, Rabbeinu Chananel, and Rabbeinu Yerucham appear to hold that if collection is sought immediately the document is valid, and the Rashba also rules so, the matter appears to be a doubt in law. It requires study even where collection is sought later. See his lengthy discussion.
