(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 1) For a document is a complete instrument of acquisition. Note: This requires study. What difference does it make that the witnesses later testify orally? The writing itself still lacks the status of a valid document, because if it can be forged it is merely ordinary handwriting and never acquired the legal status of a document. It should therefore follow that delivery without witnesses does not help, as the Bach asked against the Sema.
