One suspected of sexual immorality, etc. There is a major distinction between one who actually committed a prohibited sexual act and one merely suspected because he secludes himself with forbidden women. A person merely suspected appears to be disqualified only rabbinically, because there is no explicit Torah prohibition making him a wicked witness; he is only suspect in that area. But if he actually had relations with a forbidden woman and there are witnesses, according to those who rule like Rav Sheshet in Sanhedrin he is disqualified by Torah law. According to some authorities who rule like Rav Nachman there, even one who actually sinned may remain qualified for ordinary testimony because his inclination overcame him, although for testimony concerning a woman he is treated as suspect rabbinically.
