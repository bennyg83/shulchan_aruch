(Collected note) We say that he should give, etc. Rambam and Tur. Rashbam also explains this in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel. Rashbam and Ri Migash, however, do not explain so; rather, a zuz is the smaller amount. This also appears from the language there, "what do you say, etc.," and so Rashi explains in Sukkah 22b, as stated there at the beginning of siman 631. The Rambam rules the same there; see his commentary and code, unlike what he writes here. This also requires study: why not convert six hundred istira to zuz, or else he should explain the zuz in terms of istira as well? (End of collected note.)
