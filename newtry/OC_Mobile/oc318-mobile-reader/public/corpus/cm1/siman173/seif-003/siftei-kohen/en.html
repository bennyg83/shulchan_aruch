And they have no right of passage against one another, etc. This applies even where there is no other route, as Rashbam explains in the Gemara. See Responsa Rabbi Shlomo Kohen, volume 3, simanim 90 and 109, and Responsa Maharashdam, siman 298. See Sema, seif katan 7, who writes that in the Derishah he proved that this case concerns a division not made by appraisal and equalization. See what I wrote above in siman 171 in the name of the Rosh. The Sema must therefore be understood as commenting not on the Gemara's phrase "brothers who divided," but on the language of the Tur and Mechaber. This appears correct. Concerning the statement that the owner of the vineyard pays the owner of the field, the reverse is equally true, as I wrote above in siman 172, seif katan 5.
