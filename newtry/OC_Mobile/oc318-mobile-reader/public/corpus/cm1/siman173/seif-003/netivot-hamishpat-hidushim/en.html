Like purchasers. Meaning, it is as though each acquired his portion from the original owner. Since a seller is presumed to sell generously, one may build opposite the other's window. Each had a share in the whole and is therefore treated as having purchased from the other. This is unlike the rule at the end of siman 154 concerning one who sold the house and retained the courtyard. Sema.
