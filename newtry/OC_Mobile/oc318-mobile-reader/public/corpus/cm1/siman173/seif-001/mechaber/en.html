Partners or brothers who wish to divide property that is not subject to compelled division, division of sacred books, and matters in which they do not acquire a chazakah against one another. It contains four seifim:
Partners who wish to divide an item that is not legally subject to compelled division may divide it even though by doing so they destroy its original designation. With sacred writings, meaning the twenty-four books of Tanakh written on a scroll like our Torah scroll, if they are in a single volume they may not divide them. It is degrading to the sacred books to separate them from one bound volume. Tur. Even though if one person owned them all he would be permitted to cut them apart. Hagahot Ashiri. If they are in two separate volumes, and they concern two different subjects, the rule of "buy or I will buy" does not apply; if they concern the same subject, that rule does apply. See above, siman 171, seif 13.
