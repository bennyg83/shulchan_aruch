He merely determines the portions, and if one then takes possession, etc. Just as when the parties explicitly select their respective sides, saying, "I want this side and you take that side," and afterward possession by one of them prevents either from retracting from the portions they selected, as explained at the beginning of siman 157, so too, once they agreed to divide by lot and the lot assigns a portion to one of them, it is as though both explicitly selected their portions. Possession by even one party in his allotted share then prevents the other from retracting. See the Tur here and siman 157.
