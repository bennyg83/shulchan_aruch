Maggid Mishneh explains: for example, they agreed to divide according to birth order, Shimon receiving the parcel next to Reuven and Levi next to Shimon. Once the lot for Reuven was drawn, Shimon thereby acquired the adjacent parcel and Levi the parcel adjacent to Shimon. Maggid Mishneh cites other explanations but writes that this one appears principal.
