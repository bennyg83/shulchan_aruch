The language of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 22 of Hilkhot Malveh, cited by the Tur. Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam follows Rif's responsum: if the debtor has no money or movable property and the court must collect from land, the court gives him a standard thirty-day period. The reason is that even without granting him additional time, the court cannot collect land immediately, but only after announcement and appraisal.
Baal HaTerumot likewise writes in Shaar 3. They write that he is not made to swear at the outset. If he in fact possesses funds and refuses to pay, he violates the verse, "Do not say to your fellow: Go and return."
Maggid Mishneh writes that in many places in this work he has noted that a general cherem is an enactment of the Geonim that became so customary that it acquired the force of established law.
Tur, seif 1, who writes at the end: If no one can be found who wishes to serve as guarantor for him, it appears to me that he is made to swear or accept excommunication that he will not conceal assets.
