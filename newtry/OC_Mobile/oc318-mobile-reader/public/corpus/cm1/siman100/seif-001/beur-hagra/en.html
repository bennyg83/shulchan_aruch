A borrower... so that I may borrow... they set him a time... Bava Kamma 112b distinguishes the first thirty days. A writ of contempt is issued only if he fails to appear before beit din and request time. As explained in siman 11, one who cannot appear may be granted time. Even against a validated document time may be granted, as stated there.
