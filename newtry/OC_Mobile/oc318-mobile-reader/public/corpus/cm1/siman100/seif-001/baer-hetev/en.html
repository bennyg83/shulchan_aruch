For the remainder. The same applies to a borrowed object or a deposit: beit din does not grant a period of delay. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 325. The Bach rules likewise, as does Maharam Mintz in responsum 92. Maharam Mintz adds that if he cannot return the item immediately because it is not presently with him, he is granted the amount of time reasonably required to bring it. Knesset HaGedolah.
