They do not grant him another period. Since he fixed a date for payment, it is as though he stipulated that he would not delay beyond it, and he should have arranged the money before the date arrived.
Some say that even in such a case, etc. This is also implied by the Tur at the beginning of siman 24: even where the repayment date was fixed in the document, they still give him thirty days unless the document expressly said "without delay." In that case an express monetary condition is binding, as Rama explains in siman 16.
