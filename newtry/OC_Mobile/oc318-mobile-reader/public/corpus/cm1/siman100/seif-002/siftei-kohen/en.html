Even if he came, etc. The Darkei Moshe derives this from the Mordechai in the chapter HaBayit VeHaAliyah. At first glance the Mordechai appears to say the opposite, but careful examination shows that the Rama understood it correctly. Rabbenu Tam's reason for denying additional time is that one who already fixed a payment date does not receive another extension. Therefore, according to the later opinion cited by the Mechaber, even if the creditor sued thirty days before the due date, the debtor receives thirty days beginning from the due date. This applies only if the debtor requests the extension after the date arrives. Beit din should not presently announce, before maturity, that he will automatically have thirty days afterward.
