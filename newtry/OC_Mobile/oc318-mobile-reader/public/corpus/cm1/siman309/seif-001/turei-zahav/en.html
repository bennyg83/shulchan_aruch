(Seif 1) Although he acted contrary to the owner’s instructions, etc. The Tur likewise writes this. The reason is that Rabbi Meir and the Sages dispute in chapter HaOmanin concerning one who deviates from the owner’s instructions. Rabbi Meir regards him as a robber, whereas Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel is lenient. Rabbi Chiyya bar Abba explains the Mishnah that imposes liability where he changes the route specified by the owner in accordance with Rabbi Meir. The halachic authorities rule that the law does not follow Rabbi Meir. Therefore, whenever the damage was not caused by the deviation, he is not liable merely because he violated the owner’s instructions. The Tur nevertheless requires study, for in Orach Chaim, siman 694, regarding Purim charity collections, he writes that a poor person may not alter the use intended by the donor, which accords with Rabbi Meir. The Gemara in chapter HaOmanin explicitly compares the two matters. This requires study.
