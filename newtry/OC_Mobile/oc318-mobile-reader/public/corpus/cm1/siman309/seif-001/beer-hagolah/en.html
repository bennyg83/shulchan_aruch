The wording of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 4 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, from the Mishnah in Bava Metzia 78a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the first clause, where one rents a donkey and changes the route even by ten mil and it dies, makes him liable without distinguishing whether the animal overheated or slipped. Rabbi Yochanan establishes that first clause like Rabbi Meir, who says anyone who departs from the owner's instructions is called a robber and is liable for accidents. We do not rule like him. It is possible that Rabbi Yochanan disputes the other resolutions there and holds that according to the Sages, so long as the negligence is not evident, the renter is exempt. Otherwise he would not have established the Mishnah contrary to halachah.
