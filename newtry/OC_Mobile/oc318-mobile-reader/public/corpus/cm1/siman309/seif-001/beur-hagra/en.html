(Collected note) Although, etc. For we do not rule like Rabbi Meir. We rule like Rava there 36b, who says that the first bailee can say, "you trusted me, etc.," and not like Abaye, who reasons, "I do not want, etc." As stated there, "according to you, etc." The Rif and Rosh write this there, and Shitah Mekubetzes likewise indicates that they do not hold like Rabbi Meir.
