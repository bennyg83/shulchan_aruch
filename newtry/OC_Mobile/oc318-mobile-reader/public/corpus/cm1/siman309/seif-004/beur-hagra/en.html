And some say, etc. Their reasoning is that since the workers were hired by the owner and he specified a valley, they accepted responsibility that the implement not break in the valley, but not on a mountain. Therefore the Gemara asks only where the renter did not deviate. Rashi's explanation of the Mishna, s.v. 'one who rents,' and his mention of the renter's servants, addresses why the Gemara raised its question only where there was no deviation, as Tosafot and Rosh note. Rambam appears to explain that the Gemara did not ask about a case of deviation because the plain meaning of the Mishna might suggest that the renter himself plowed. When the Gemara says, 'where he did not deviate, etc., that you say he is exempt,' it necessarily assumes that another person was plowing.
