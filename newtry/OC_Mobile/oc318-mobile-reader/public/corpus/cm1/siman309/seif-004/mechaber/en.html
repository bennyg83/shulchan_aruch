If one rented a cow to plow on a mountain and instead plowed in a valley, and the plowshare broke, the renter is exempt and the owner of the cow proceeds against the workers who plowed. The same applies if he did not deviate from the owner's instructions. If he rented it to plow in a valley and plowed on a mountain, and the plowshare broke, the renter is liable and proceeds against the workers. Rama: Some say that if the workers were hired by the owner, they are exempt from the renter because he deviated, and the owner must pay their wages. Regarding workers who break the implement while plowing, the one holding the implement pays. If the field had terraces, both the driver with the goad and the one holding the implement are liable for the plowshare.
