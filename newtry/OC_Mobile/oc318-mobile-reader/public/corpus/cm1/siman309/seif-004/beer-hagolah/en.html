The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara explicitly asks there, where the renter did not deviate, who pays, and it is certainly the owner of the cow who is paid because the animal belongs to him.

The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara makes clear that whenever the renter did not deviate from the owner's instructions, the craftsmen pay the owner of the cow even though it was rented to plow on a mountain. It follows likewise that even if the renter did deviate, his dispute is with the craftsmen and they pay. Rambam speaks where the craftsmen are hired workers, and Rashi likewise explains so.
