Rabbi Akiva Eiger, in his novellae to Bava Metzia 80, asks that since both workers undertook the plowing they should be guarantors for one another, as in siman 76, so that one could collect half from either in any event, either as the negligent party or as guarantor. He answers by comparison to the case of two people with the same name, where a creditor cannot collect from the purchaser of one of them on a double doubt. Here too, although each is guarantor for the other, the principal debtor himself can say that perhaps he was not negligent, and is legally exempt because of monetary doubt. Therefore the guarantor also does not admit that his fellow is liable. This differs from an ordinary guarantor who admits that the borrower truly owes the debt. Thus one cannot collect from either on a claim of 'in any event.' This appears correct in law. See also Ketzot HaChoshen, siman 386, and siman 388.
