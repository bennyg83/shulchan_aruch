The same applies to the laws of testimony. Jerusalem Talmud, beginning of chapter 1 of Sotah.
(Likkut) The same, etc. The Jerusalem Talmud at the beginning of Sotah states: she secluded herself with one witness in the morning and with another witness in the evening. Such an incident occurred, and R' Eliezer ben Tadai asked the Sages whether this constitutes seclusion. They said: testimony is not accepted unless both witnesses saw the event together. R' Yehoshua ben Korcha says that it is accepted even if the witnesses saw the events one after another, and we rule like R' Yehoshua ben Korcha, as explained above. End.
(Likkut) And likewise the laws of testimony, etc. See what I wrote in Even HaEzer, siman 11, at the end of seif 1 in the Rama's gloss. End.
