Together. Meaning, each witness saw the act or heard the admission that the other did not see or hear. Nevertheless, since according to each witness the defendant owes one hundred, their testimony combines. If they testify about one event but were not physically together when they saw it, for example each saw from his own house, this is called "separate testimony" in the language of the Talmud, and certainly such testimony combines.
