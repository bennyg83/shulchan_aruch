In monetary law, even if the two witnesses did not see the act together. Meaning, each one saw the event or heard an admission from the defendant that the other did not see or hear. Nevertheless, since each one's testimony establishes that the defendant owes one hundred, they combine. If they testify about the same act but were not together when they saw it, such as one seeing it from his house and the other from his own house, the Gemara likewise says that they combine. This is called 'special testimony' in the language of the Gemara. The Tur and Mechaber did not state that case because it is all the more obvious from the present rule, where each witness testifies to a separate act.
