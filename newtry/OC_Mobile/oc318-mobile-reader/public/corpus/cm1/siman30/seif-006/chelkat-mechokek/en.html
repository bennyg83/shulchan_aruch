Since he entered the four cubits before she did, he acquired them. If both entered the four cubits simultaneously, since we rule regarding a found object that both acquire it, with respect to a get the area is like a courtyard belonging to both of them, and the kiddushin are therefore doubtful. See Choshen Mishpat siman 268, where the Tur cites the Raavad's view that the Sages instituted acquisition through four cubits only when one is standing still, but not while walking. Although the Rosh disagrees, perhaps because of the stringency of kiddushin one should take the Raavad's view into account, so that while she is walking there would be only doubtful kiddushin. According to what I wrote above, all acquisition through four cubits effects only rabbinic kiddushin according to Rabbeinu Yerucham.
