In monetary cases, the two witnesses' testimony combines even if they did not witness the same event together. If one says, 'He lent him money in my presence on a certain day,' or, 'He admitted the debt in my presence,' and the other testifies to a loan or admission on another day, their testimony combines. Likewise, if one testifies to a loan and the other to an admission, whether the admission preceded or followed the loan, their testimony combines. {Rama: The same principle applies to testimony concerning a matter of prohibition (Mordechai, chapter 2 of Yevamos). See Even HaEzer, simanim 11 and 143, seif 10.}
