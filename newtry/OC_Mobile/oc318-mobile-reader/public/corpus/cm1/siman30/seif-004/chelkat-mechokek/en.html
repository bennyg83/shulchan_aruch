Or at the sides of the public domain. But not in the public domain itself, for the Sages did not institute acquisition within four cubits there, even where another person's intent is to transfer ownership. Bach writes that according to Rambam and other authorities, where another person's intent is to transfer, four cubits can acquire even in the public domain. Yet Rambam himself, Hilchot Zechiyah 4, writes regarding a gift that four cubits were not instituted in the public domain even where another intends to transfer. In truth, Rambam did not write that kiddushin within four cubits in the public domain acquire. He wrote only that where the kiddushin are nearer to her and she can guard them, she is betrothed. See Rambam, Hilchot Gerushin 5 and the commentators there. This will be explained, with Hashem's help, below in siman 139.
