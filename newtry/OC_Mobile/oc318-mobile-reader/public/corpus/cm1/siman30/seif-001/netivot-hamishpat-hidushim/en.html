The positions of the months. This means whether one witness says the event was in Nisan and the other says Iyar while the claimant asserts two separate maneh obligations, or even where he claims only one and one witness says, "I do not know." According to the Mechaber the testimony is valid. In a suspicious case the Shach holds that even if one says, "I do not know," the testimony is invalid, unlike Rama in siman 15, though the Shach concludes that the matter requires study. The Shach also writes that where two groups of witnesses contradict one another, the fact that one group was formally designated for the kinyan while the other happened to be present does not make the designated group more credible.
