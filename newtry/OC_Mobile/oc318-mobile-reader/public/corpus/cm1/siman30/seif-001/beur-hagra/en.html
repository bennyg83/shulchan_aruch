(Collected note) Except witnesses to bodily injuries. See the beginning of Sanhedrin 2b: "by strict law, etc." It implies that robbery and bodily injury, which require expert judges, also require formal examination and inquiry. Tosafot there, s.v. "Shelo," also write this. The Rosh answers there that we rely on the conclusion in HaChovel that a common matter, etc. If so, the same should apply to robbery, leaving only bodily injuries; see there. So too above, siman 1, seif 3. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) Except witnesses to, etc. But not robbery, following the conclusion of Bava Kamma, HaChovel, which mentions only bodily injuries; see Rosh. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) Except bodily injuries, and likewise robbery. Following the conclusion at the beginning of chapter 8 of Bava Kamma. Rosh at the beginning of Sanhedrin. Rashi 13b, s.v. "LeModan," writes similarly. The same applies regarding examination and inquiry, as Tosafot 3a, s.v. "Shelo Yo'il," write. (End of collected note.)
