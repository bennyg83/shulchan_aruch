Examination. Even if the judge wishes to be stringent and conduct formal examination and interrogation, he should not do so. If, after the fact, beit din did interrogate the witnesses and they answered "I do not know" to the formal questions, their testimony is void. See Responsa Mahari ben Lev, part 3, siman 25. Shach.
Corresponded. Meaning that if one witness says the loan occurred in Nisan and the second says in Iyar, while the claimant claims two separate maneh, their testimony combines. Likewise, if one says Nisan and the second says "I do not know," they combine even though the claimant claimed only one maneh. It appears that the Mechaber's unspecified wording refers particularly to the latter case, where one witness says "I do not know," because the claimant's allegation is left unspecified and because it parallels the later examples involving the coinage and place, which also concern such a case.
