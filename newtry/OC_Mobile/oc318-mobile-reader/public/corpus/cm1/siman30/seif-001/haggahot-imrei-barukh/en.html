(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 1) If they were rendered zomemim, they are disqualified retroactively. Note: This requires study. Tosafot, Bava Batra 71a, in the passage beginning "who testified simultaneously," indicate that even witnesses who are rendered zomemim are not disqualified retroactively to the instant of their testimony, but only from the end of the period of toch kedei dibbur. During that interval they could retract, and therefore are not yet disqualified. All the more so, they should not be disqualified until after completion of examination and interrogation, since before that time they can still retract their testimony, as stated in the Tosefta: before their testimony has been fully examined. It seems to me that the very foundation of his doubt conflicts with Responsa Ran, siman 34. See there.
