Witnesses in Monetary Cases Do Not Require Formal Examination, but the Testimony Must Be Complete. Thirteen seifim. Witnesses in monetary cases, other than witnesses concerning personal injury, do not require formal examination and inquiry. If they testify, 'In our presence this person lent the other one hundred zuz in a certain year,' their testimony is valid even if they did not specify the month, the place of the loan, or the type of currency. If the case appears fraudulent to the judge, he must examine and investigate so the true judgment emerges. {Rama: See above, siman 15. Regarding witnesses to a matter of prohibition, see Even HaEzer, siman 17, seif 21; siman 42, seif 4; and siman 11, seif 4, as to whether formal examination and inquiry are required.}
