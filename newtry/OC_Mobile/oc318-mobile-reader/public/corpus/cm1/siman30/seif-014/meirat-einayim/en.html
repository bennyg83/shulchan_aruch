Testimony based on knowledge. This will be explained in siman 90. There, at the end of the siman, the Tur and Mechaber write that if an object was stolen and witnesses know that he had the item in his house, and there was no other person there besides the defendant, we adjudicate as though they saw him steal it. Likewise, if witnesses see a person enter another's house healthy and emerge injured, and no one else was there, we attribute the injury to the person found inside.
Whether a matter likely to become revealed, etc. This means that one witness is not ordinarily believed in monetary law merely because the truth is likely to become known. Only in testimony concerning a woman's husband's death, to permit her to remarry, is one witness believed because the matter will likely be revealed. See Even HaEzer siman 17.
