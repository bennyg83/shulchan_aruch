And he threw them into her four cubits. Acquisition by four cubits is a rabbinic enactment. According to Rabbeinu Yerucham, cited by Beit Yosef above in siman 28 regarding ma'amad sheloshtan, such kiddushin would therefore be only rabbinic. Ran in chapter HaZorek, however, holds that they are Torah kiddushin because property declared ownerless by beit din is ownerless, just as the Sages instituted regarding a get that if she can guard it even from one hundred cubits away she is divorced. See Gittin 78b.
