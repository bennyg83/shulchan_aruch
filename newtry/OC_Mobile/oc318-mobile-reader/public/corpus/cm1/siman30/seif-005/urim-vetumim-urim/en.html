He became established as a denier, etc. See what I wrote below in siman 75 at length in reconciling Rosh.
And he pays two hundred. Here, joined testimony obligates him in an oath under the first rule of Rabbi Chiya. Since he is established as a false denier, he cannot swear and therefore pays. Although some say joined testimony does not create Rabbi Chiya’s oath, that was where each witness testified to a distinct maneh. Here both witnesses speak to the same maneh through repeated admissions, and such joining does obligate an oath. Sema. According to what I wrote above, whenever the plaintiff makes a definite claim, joined testimony can obligate the oath and no special distinction is needed.
