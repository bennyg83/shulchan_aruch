[Shulchan Aruch] One thousand five hundred gold coins. Note: In Baal HaTerumot the text reads "one thousand," and he brings five witnesses. It is evident that his view follows the Ramah cited by the Tur, that where there was one loan after another, it is unnecessary for the plaintiff to claim both loans. Therefore he states only one thousand. The Tur, who holds that he must claim both, changed the text and wrote one thousand five hundred.
