He is established as a denier. Meaning, he is suspect under the law of one who falsely denies a deposit, Bava Metzia 5 and 8. Here we do not require the condition stated there on 5b, "such as where witnesses came, etc.," because here there is no specific identified object, only monetary value. Responsa of Rav Chaim Shabbetai.
For that money. Not literally limited to that exact money.
And he pays. Through the claimant's oath.
(Collected note) He is established as a denier, etc., and pays. He pays the principal through the oath of the opposing party, as stated at the beginning of Bava Metzia regarding that shepherd. (End of collected note.)
