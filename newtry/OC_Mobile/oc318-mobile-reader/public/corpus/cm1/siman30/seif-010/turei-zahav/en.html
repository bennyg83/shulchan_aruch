In the name of Mahari of blessed memory, in the Tur: “The witness to the one hundred does not combine and requires a Torah oath” One might ask why the oath is not required even without that witness, since witnesses establish payment of six hundred, as the Rosh writes regarding a deposit. The suggested distinction is that evasion is not presumed regarding a deposit but may be presumed regarding a loan, so no oath arises without one witness. The appended note rejects this: the rule of Rabbi Chiya applies primarily to loans, as the Gemara derives it from one’s own admission and one witness. The Rosh needed additional reasoning only to establish that Rabbi Chiya’s rule also applies to deposits.
