[Sema, subparagraph 29] For it is not the way of a lender. Note: It is clear that this applies only where the defendant claims that he repaid one hundred. According to his account, one of the witnesses is contradicted, since one would not ordinarily admit a debt before repayment. But if he claims that he repaid two hundred, one can say that he first admitted one hundred that he had already repaid, and afterward repaid another one hundred. The witnesses therefore combine. This appears clear to me.
