One from each beit din should come. See Tumim. Three different batei din are needed rather than two, because otherwise a witness would be becoming a judge and would have to testify before a new beit din. By joining one member from each of three batei din, the same three function collectively as both witnesses to the prior judicial reception and as the beit din.
He does not join the witness, as the witness of the Dayan, until he has heard the testimony, and it is testified that he has not seen any of me in combination, and that even before the “other shall not testify as a result, and does not testify to the return of the Sages:
He said, “If the witness does not testify at all, he does not join him.”
