They do not combine. Although the rule against testimony from hearsay does not apply to a judge, because a judge may testify to what he heard in court, this judge testifies that he heard testimony while the other witness testifies to what he personally saw. Their testimony is not of the same kind and therefore does not combine. For the same reason they should not testify even before another beit din. Sema.
Before him. The word "before him" is not literal. The same rule applies if the witness never testified at all. The reason itself governs. Sema.
