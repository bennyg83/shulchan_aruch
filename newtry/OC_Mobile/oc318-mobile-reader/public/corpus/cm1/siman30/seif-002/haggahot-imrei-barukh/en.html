(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 3) In my humble opinion the reason is, etc., as explained in the novellae of the Ran. Note: See Nimukei Yosef. But the Ran's novellae indicate that in the case of testimony of one hundred and two hundred, we do not say that one witness erred. Rather, the claimant is awarded one hundred because one hundred is included in two hundred, so there is no contradiction concerning the first hundred. The contradiction concerning the second hundred does not nullify the testimony concerning the first hundred, regarding which there is no contradiction at all. See there at length for his distinction between contradiction among the witnesses concerning the second hundred, which does not nullify liability for the first hundred, and contradiction between the litigant and the witness, where the claimant admits that he lent only one hundred and therefore cannot collect even one hundred.
