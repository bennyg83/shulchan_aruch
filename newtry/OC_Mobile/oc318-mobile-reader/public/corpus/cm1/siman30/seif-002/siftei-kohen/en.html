Their testimony is void. The Ramban writes in responsum 43, addressed to the rabbis of Regensburg, that even a contradiction in the formal interrogations does not invalidate testimony, because we do not rule like Rabbi Yehudah. He derives this from the opening chapter of Sanhedrin, where the Gemara says that monetary cases do not require formal examination and interrogation. Below, siman 33, seif 15, I cite Maimonides, the Semag, the Rivash, and the Nimukei Yosef, who rule like the Tur, and I explain there that the principle that monetary cases do not require examination and interrogation does not support the Ramban’s conclusion.
