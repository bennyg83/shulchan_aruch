That he repaid one hundred in Nisan. Sema says the borrower must claim that he paid two separate hundreds. Urim VeTumim suggests that if he claims only one hundred he may still be believed through a migo that he could have claimed two. In my view the Shulchan Aruch follows Maharshal's position that a migo does not help where the party's own claim necessarily treats one of his witnesses as false. Here, if he claims only the Nisan payment while one witness says there was also a Tishrei payment, he is effectively contradicting and disqualifying that witness as to himself. This differs from the ordinary siman 82 case, where the discrepancy is merely between one hundred and two hundred and does not necessarily brand a witness false in the same manner.
