[Shulchan Aruch] With respect to that money. Note: This is an abbreviated expression. In the case of Rabbi Chiya, he is not considered one who has been established as a denier with respect to that money, because he is believed upon taking an oath. Rather, it is as Tosafot write in Shevuot: he has been established as a denier only concerning the fifty, while the other fifty are considered different money, regarding which he has not been established as a denier. Here, however, we judge him under the law of one suspected regarding an oath, and the concept of being a denier concerning that money does not apply.
