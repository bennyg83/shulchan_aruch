Seif 8: “And the second says in Tishrei” The Sema writes that if the borrower did not claim to have paid both debts, the lender takes a Torah oath. This is imprecise. Where one witness says a written debt was paid, the lender owes only a rabbinic oath, as stated in the chapter HaKotev and in siman 87, seif 5, where the Sema himself so rules.
