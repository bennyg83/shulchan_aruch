Successive loans combine where the claimant demands two hundred zuz, one hundred lent on Sunday and another hundred on Monday. If the lender admits that he lent only one hundred, we examine whether one witness might have mistaken the date, as where one says the second of the month and the other the third, so both may be testifying about the same day but differ because one erred about the month's intercalation. They then combine, for they may be testifying about the same hundred. If it is established that they describe two separate hundreds, one witness is certainly false; the defendant therefore takes a Torah oath and is exempt. {Rama: If one witness testifies that Reuven conveyed land to Shimon through a formal act of acquisition and another testifies that Shimon properly took possession of the land, their testimony combines, like an admission following a loan (Responsa of the Rosh, klal 59, siman 1).}
