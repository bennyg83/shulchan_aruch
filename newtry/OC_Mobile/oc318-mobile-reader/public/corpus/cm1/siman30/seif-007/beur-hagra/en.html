(Anthology) This, etc. As explained above from, 'Perhaps Rabbi Yehoshua ben Korcha said,' etc. All the more so where the litigant contradicts the witness, for a litigant's admission is equivalent to one hundred witnesses. See Bava Batra 128b.
(Anthology) The rule concerning one loan, etc. This is the Rosh's language in the name of the Ramah there, and the Nimukei Yosef likewise writes that the distinction applies specifically to one loan after another and one admission after another, unlike the Tur, who did not distinguish.
(Anthology) Certainly one of them, etc. Based on the third chapter of Bava Batra 32b and Tosafot, s.v. avid, etc.
(Anthology) It is like an admission, etc. Because he claims only one sum from the defendant. See Sema, seif katan 21, and the Gemara's statement according to Rav concerning one admission, etc.
