And if the matter has been clarified. See Baer Heitev, which gives the example of testimony after half the month has passed. Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes that if, after the middle of the month, witnesses testify about an event at the beginning of the month, one saying it occurred on the second and the other on the third, we attribute the discrepancy to an error concerning the intercalation of the month, because by then each has already fixed in his memory the date as he understood it. See Responsa Shav Yaakov, Even HaEzer, end of siman 15.
