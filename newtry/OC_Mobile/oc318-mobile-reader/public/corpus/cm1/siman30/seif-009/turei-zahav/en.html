Seif 11: “One beit din joins another beit din” Here the entire first tribunal is required because it heard only one witness. In seif 12, once two witnesses testified before a tribunal, two judges or even one may join another, because complete testimony already exists. Thus one of two conditions is needed: a complete beit din that heard one witness, or complete testimony heard by a beit din. In the second case, even two outsiders who heard the tribunal receive the two witnesses should be at least equivalent to one judge. Their later testimony concerns an act of beit din, not hearsay testimony. Mordechai’s wording and Rama’s formulation support this. The Sema’s contrary assertion that two who heard the completed judicial examination cannot transmit it has no basis in Darkei Moshe or Nimukei Yosef.
