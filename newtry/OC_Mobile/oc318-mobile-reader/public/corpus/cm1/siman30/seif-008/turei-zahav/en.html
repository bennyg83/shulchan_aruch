(There: before him he admitted that he had repaid.) The Sema writes in seif katan 29 that where the admission came first and the repayment afterward, the testimonies do not combine. I am astonished that such a statement could have issued from so holy a mouth. We find many times that a lender trusts the borrower, as stated in siman 71. Here too, one may say that the lender feared that the borrower might eventually repay him, so he trusted him in advance before witnesses and admitted that payment had been made. Moreover, how is this different from one witness saying that in Nisan he repaid one maneh and another saying that in Tishrei he repaid one maneh? They are certainly not testifying about the same maneh, yet their testimony combines. Here too, the admission may refer to another maneh and can combine to exempt him from one maneh, since the borrower claims that he repaid everything. The Tur did not list here the case of admission before repayment because Baal HaTerumot does not even separately list admission after repayment, and the Tur relied on what he wrote above. The same applies to the Tur's wording here.
