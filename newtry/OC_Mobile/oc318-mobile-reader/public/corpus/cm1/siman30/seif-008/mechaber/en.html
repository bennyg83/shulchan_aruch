If a creditor claims 200 by virtue of a document and the debtor says, 'I paid you,' bringing one witness that he paid 100 in Nisan and another that he paid 100 in Tishrei, their testimony combines. If the document predates Nisan, it is treated as paid to the extent of 100. Likewise, if he admitted before one witness that the debtor paid him 100 in Nisan and before another that the debtor paid him 100 in Tishrei, or one witness testifies to payment of 100 and the other to the creditor's admission of payment of 100, their testimony combines. The creditor cannot say that those payments were applied to another debt.
