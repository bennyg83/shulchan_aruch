[Shach, subparagraph 11] That the claimant must swear. Note: It appears to me that if he wishes to collect only one hundred and fifty, he may collect without an oath. With respect to the one hundred, the witness who says one hundred and fifty is a supporting witness according to the Shach in subparagraph 10. Even according to the Sema in subparagraph 15, one can say that his limitation applies specifically to imposing an oath, because a witness who already produced a monetary consequence cannot then be contradicted as to part of his testimony by an oath. But with respect to supporting a party, that concern does not apply.
