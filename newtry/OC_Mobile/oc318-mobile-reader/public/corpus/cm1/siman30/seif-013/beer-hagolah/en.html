ch) So Rabbeinu explains the statement there concerning one witness to one hair, etc., and so rules the Rambam there.
t) This is clear there, because half of a matter, meaning one hair, is also half of the testimony, since there is only one witness concerning that hair.

k) The Beit Yosef writes that this applies specifically to testimony in monetary law or buying and selling. But regarding gittin and kiddushin, the laws of capital cases apply, and this constitutes separate testimony that is invalid in capital cases, as stated in Makkot 6b.
