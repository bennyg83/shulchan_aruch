Further in the Beit Yosef, s.v. “Likewise, one says a barrel of wine,” through “because each barrel has only one witness” This is not convincing. One barrel has two witnesses, but they contradict each other: the barrel one calls oil the other calls wine. Since one hundred is included in two hundred and we follow Beit Hillel, the testimony must be void because the contradiction concerns an essential inquiry, the identity of the object itself, as Rambam and the explanations of the Rosh in Semag indicate.
