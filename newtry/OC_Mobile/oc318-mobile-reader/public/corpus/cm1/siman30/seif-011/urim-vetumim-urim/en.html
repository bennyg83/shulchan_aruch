One beit din should come to another beit din, etc. A complete beit din is required. One member from each court does not suffice, because each member heard only one witness, and testimony through him would be testimony from a witness who heard another witness. In the next seif, however, where two witnesses testified before the original beit din, one member of that beit din can suffice to report the judicially received testimony. Sema.
But others who heard, etc. In Tumim I agree with Taz that this applies specifically where they heard the examination of one witness. If, however, they heard two witnesses testify before beit din and the testimony was properly received, they may testify before another beit din that such testimony was accepted. From this I derive that if a document bearing only one witness was burned, people who saw what was written cannot later testify to its contents in beit din in order to collect.
