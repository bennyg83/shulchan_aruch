In the hands of beit din. One member from each beit din does not suffice where each court heard only one witness, because that would be testimony from one witness through another. This differs from the following seif, where the court heard two witnesses, in which case one member of that beit din is sufficient, Sema. Others who heard. This applies specifically where they heard the interrogation of one witness. But where they heard two witnesses testify before beit din and the testimony was formally examined, they may later testify in another beit din and say, "Such testimony was accepted in beit din," Taz.
