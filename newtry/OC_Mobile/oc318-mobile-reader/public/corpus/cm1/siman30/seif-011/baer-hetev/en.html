Beit din. Specifically the entire beit din may combine. Some judges from one beit din and some from another cannot combine to form a new beit din and adjudicate on the basis of what they heard, because only one witness testified before each original beit din; this would amount to testimony from the mouth of another witness. Sema.
Who heard. Meaning, even if they heard two witnesses testify before one beit din and the testimony was formally accepted there, they may not themselves testify before another beit din so that it may decide the case on the basis of their report. By contrast, a beit din that formally received the testimony of two witnesses may itself testify before another beit din regarding the testimony it received. Thus concludes the Sema.
