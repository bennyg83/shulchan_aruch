And if the matter was clarified, etc. See Baer Heitev, who writes, for example, etc., or where the testimony is given after the middle of the month, etc. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: If, after the middle of the month, witnesses testify concerning an event that occurred at the beginning of the month, and one says it occurred on the second and the other says on the third, we say that they erred regarding the intercalation of the month. This is because by then each has already fixed in his mind the date he believes to have been the third of the month. See Responsa Shav Yaakov, Even HaEzer, end of siman 15. End of quote.
