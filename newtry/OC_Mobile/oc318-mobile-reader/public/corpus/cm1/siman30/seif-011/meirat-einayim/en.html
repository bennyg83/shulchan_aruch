One beit din shall come, etc. Specifically the full two courts may combine. Part of one court and part of the other cannot join to create a new beit din and rule from what they heard, because in each original beit din only one witness testified. Their new combination would effectively rest on testimony heard secondhand. This differs from the next seif, which concerns a case where both witnesses testified before each beit din.
But others who heard, etc. Meaning that even if they heard that two witnesses testified before the first beit din and that the testimony was properly examined there, they may not themselves testify before another beit din regarding what they heard so that the latter court may adjudicate on that basis. But a beit din that itself received testimony from two witnesses may transmit the testimony to another beit din. See Darkei Moshe.
