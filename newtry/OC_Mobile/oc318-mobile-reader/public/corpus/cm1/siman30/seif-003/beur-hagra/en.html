(Anthology) Likewise, if, etc. One might object that the gloss in seif 1 states, 'wherever we can,' etc., and seif 7 states, 'we assess,' etc. The Taz likewise raises this difficulty. It appears to me that we reconcile testimony only by attributing the discrepancy to a common occurrence. Thus in seif 1, where the witnesses contradict one another regarding time, the word 'No' is required for an actual contradiction; without it, we assume they are not contradicting. But with a barrel of wine and oil, even without an express contradiction we judge them contradictory, as is evident from the Gemara's question, 'Perhaps,' etc. The reason is that with two different times it is common for there to have been two loans. With a barrel of wine and oil, however, the possibilities are evenly balanced, so we rule leniently for the defendant. This is the meaning of the Gemara's question: how does one loan differ from another loan, and one admission from another admission, etc.? See Rashi there, s.v. hodaah achar hodaah, who writes, 'perhaps,' etc. Similarly, Pesachim 12a states: 'Do we investigate them in these matters?' etc.; rather, etc. Nevertheless, we attribute the discrepancy to error and do not say that they are precise but contradictory, because such error is common. Where the witnesses explain that one meant the beginning of the hour and the other its end, however, the probabilities are balanced. The rule cited by the authority, that we attribute a discrepancy to error where that is the majority possibility but not where the possibilities are balanced, is an explicit Gemara in Sanhedrin 69a: 'He said to him, and in capital cases do we not follow,' etc.; perhaps these are specifically, etc., and the majority of people, etc. This implies that an even possibility does not suffice. This also answers Tosafot's question on 41b, s.v. shezeh, etc. Otherwise their words remain difficult, for what would they say where one witness says the second hour and another the third? One must say that Tosafot regard error regarding the intercalation of the month as uncommon unless they expressly identify which day it was. See there.
