Reuven claims 1,500 gold coins from Shimon and brings five witnesses: one testifies that he saw Shimon borrow 100, another testifies to 200, another to 300, another to 400, and another to 500. If each describes a separate event, such as the first placing his event on the first of Nisan in the first year and each of the others giving another time, Shimon pays 700, takes a Torah oath concerning 100, and through a rolled oath also swears concerning the remaining 700. If all testify about one day in mutually contradictory amounts, he pays only 200. If they give no dates, their testimony is treated as contradictory in order to rule leniently, because the burden of proof rests upon the claimant.
