l) Tur. So too writes Baal HaTerumot in gate 10 in the name of a responsum of the Ramban.
Meaning, we combine the witness who testified to four hundred with the witness who testified to one hundred, obligating four hundred through them. Then we combine the remaining one hundred from the first witness's testimony with the one hundred testified to by the witness who said three hundred, obligating another one hundred, for a total of five hundred. Then we combine two hundred from the second witness with the two hundred remaining from the witness who said three hundred, making a total of seven hundred. The witness who testified to one hundred has no further testimony with which to combine, but his testimony requires a Torah oath, etc.
