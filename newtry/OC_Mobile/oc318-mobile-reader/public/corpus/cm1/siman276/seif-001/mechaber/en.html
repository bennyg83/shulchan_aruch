The order of inheritance. It contains seven seifim:
The order of inheritance is as follows. When a person dies, his son inherits him. If there is no living son, we look to the son's descendants, whether male or female, through all generations; they stand in the son's place and inherit everything. If the son has no descendants, the deceased's daughter inherits him. If the daughter is not alive but has descendants, male or female, through all generations, they inherit everything in her place. If she has no descendants, the inheritance returns to the deceased's father. If the father is not alive, it passes to his descendants, who are the deceased's brothers. If the deceased has a brother, that brother inherits everything. If the brother is not alive but left descendants, they stand in his place through all generations. If there is no brother and no descendants of a brother, the inheritance passes to the deceased's sister, or to her descendants through all generations. If there is no sister and no descendants of a sister, the inheritance passes to the deceased's paternal grandfather. If he is not alive, it passes to his descendants, who are the brothers of the deceased's father, or to their descendants through all generations. If the deceased's father has no brothers and there are no descendants of such brothers, the inheritance passes to the deceased's paternal aunt or to her descendants through all generations. If there is no paternal aunt and no descendants of hers, it passes to the father of the deceased's paternal grandfather. If he is not alive, it passes to his descendants, who are the brothers of the deceased's paternal grandfather, or to their descendants through all generations. If none of those brothers or their descendants exist, it passes to the sister of the deceased's paternal grandfather or to her descendants through all generations. In this manner the inheritance continues upward through the paternal line as far as Reuven.
