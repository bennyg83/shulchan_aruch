We examine whether the son has descendants, etc. This is derived from the wording of the inheritance passage, "and he has no son, then you shall transfer his inheritance to his daughter." The word for "no" is written in a form that the Sages expound to mean: investigate whether there is any son, meaning even a descendant of the son. Although the Gemara uses the phrase "investigate him," this does not require an actual letter substitution. The point is that Scripture's formulation teaches absence only after checking through all descendants. The same exposition applies in every stage of the inheritance order. Even where the verse says, "if he has no daughter, give the inheritance to his brothers," the meaning is only if the daughter has no descendant. Likewise with brothers and paternal uncles, one follows their descendants through all generations.
