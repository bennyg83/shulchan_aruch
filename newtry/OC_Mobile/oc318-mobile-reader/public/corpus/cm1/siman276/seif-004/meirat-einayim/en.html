Maternal brothers who do not share a father do not inherit one another. This depends upon the rule stated immediately before, that a mother does not inherit her son. If she did inherit him, she could afterward transmit that inheritance from the grave to her other sons, just as a father transmits through his descendants. Since she does not inherit her son, maternal brothers alone do not inherit each other through her.
