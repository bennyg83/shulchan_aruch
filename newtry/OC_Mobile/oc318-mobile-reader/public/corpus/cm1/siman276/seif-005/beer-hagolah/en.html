Lamed) There, seif 4, and Rambam, halachah 13, based on the Gemara on Bava Batra 114b. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the same applies where the child does not inherit in order to transmit the inheritance to his father, for example where his mother was divorced. This is straightforward, and the Rashba likewise writes it in a responsum.
Mem) The Mishnah in the fifth chapter of Niddah 43b, "a one-day-old infant," etc., as Rav Sheshet explains on 44a and in Bava Batra 142a.
Nun) Rambam writes at the end of chapter 1 of Hilkhot Nachalot that this applies even if the infant did not complete its months. The Mechaber omitted that phrase. In my humble opinion he follows the Raavad, who holds that this is only where the infant completed its months. The Maggid Mishneh discusses the matter at length and leaves Rambam's words requiring study. I also do not understand why the Mechaber stated the law without explaining this point. The Bach discusses the Tur's similarly unqualified wording at length and concludes that in practice the property remains in its present possession and cannot be extracted from the holder. See the Maggid Mishneh's discussion.
Samekh) There, in the baraita of Rav Sheshet.
