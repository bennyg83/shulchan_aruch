First. The Sema writes that the Rama's words can be reconciled by understanding the Mechaber to discuss a case where the owner already removed the goods and only afterward informed beit din. The dissenting opinion requires him to notify beit din before removing them. See there, and Responsa Rashdam, siman 296. The Bach concludes that if the person who brought in the goods immediately left the city and cannot be notified, the householder is certainly legally obligated to notify beit din, not merely as an act of piety. Otherwise, if he throws the goods into the marketplace they may be lost, and he would be actively causing damage.
