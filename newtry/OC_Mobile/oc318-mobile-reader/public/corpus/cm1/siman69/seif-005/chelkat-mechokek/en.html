And she cannot retract. The Ran, cited by the Beit Yosef at the end of siman 80 in the name of the Ramah, explains that justice does not permit her to change the arrangement whenever convenient. Otherwise, whenever she cannot find work she would demand support, and whenever work is available she would say, "I will not be supported." That would distort the arrangement. Therefore, once she says that she does not wish the Sages' enactment in her favor, she loses the right to support thereafter. The same applies to clothing, because clothing is included in support.
