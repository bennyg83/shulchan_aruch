Her support, clothing, and conjugal rights. According to Rambam all three obligations are Torah law. See Maggid Mishneh, who distinguishes support from clothing. The Rosh in chapter Shnei Dayanei Gezeirot writes that support is rabbinic. The Ran in chapter Na'arah, at the end of page 505b, holds that even clothing is rabbinic, in accordance with Ramban, who interprets the verse as referring only to conjugal rights. See there.
