The matter. Even though it is not written in formal document language but merely, 'I, the undersigned, acknowledge,' whenever there are witnesses to the matter, one does not leave such a writing in the lender's possession after payment. Sema. The Shach writes that it may be collected from encumbered property, as is explicit in the Beit Yosef and in the Rambam and Mechaber above, siman 40, seif 2.
