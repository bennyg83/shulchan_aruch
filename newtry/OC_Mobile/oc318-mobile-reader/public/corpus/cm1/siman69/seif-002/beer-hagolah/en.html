Meaning, regarding an acknowledgment of a loan. The Beit Yosef writes, from siman 65, in the name of the Rashba: Reuven claims against Shimon, "Here is your handwriting stating for how much you sold my merchandise and for how much you purchased for me, and the amount exceeds what you admitted to me." Shimon says, "I wrote it only as a working note and it fell from me or was stolen." The Rashba answers that we do not suspect loss or theft, as stated at the end of Get Pashut, Bava Batra 172b. His claim that he wrote it merely for working purposes is also ineffective, because a person does not casually write something that causes himself financial loss.
