(Shach, seif katan 14) And what the Rashba asked. Note: It appears simple to me that the Rashba holds that so long as a deposited object exists, it does not create a lien on the guardian's property. This is also implied by Responsa Rashba, siman 211, where he discusses an admission concerning movable property held as a deposit. One can perhaps add that a deposit document creates no property lien even from the time the deposit is lost through the guardian's negligence, because witnesses to a deposit do not generate publicity concerning delivery of the deposit, and perhaps the depositor himself does not want his property being held by another to become publicly known.
