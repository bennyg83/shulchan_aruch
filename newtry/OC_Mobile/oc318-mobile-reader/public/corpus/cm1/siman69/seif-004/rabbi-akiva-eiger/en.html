[Rama] Only what his eyes see. Note: See Responsa Mahari Adrabi, siman 105. According to all views, where Reuven gave Shimon a written instruction to go to Levi, who was to pay a specified sum on Reuven’s behalf, and Shimon later claims that sum from Reuven on the basis of that writing, Reuven is believed to say that he already paid. Raanach, part 1, siman 18.
