Note: Responsa Rivash, siman 478, proves that one must distinguish between a handwritten acknowledgment and a complete document signed by witnesses. Even if the latter cannot be collected from encumbered property, because it retains the status of a formal document the debtor may be told, "why is your document in my hand?" Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot Malveh VeLoveh 11, writes that according to the Rivash the Rashba's proof from a deposit document is refuted. Yet the Rivash himself, in siman 454, cites that very proof in the name of the Rashba and his associates.
