If she says, "I will not be supported and I will not work," we listen to her. Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of the commentators that this means she may exempt herself from productive labor through which she earns money, such as spinning wool and other work not needed for the household. But grinding, baking, cooking, and the other household services remain her duty as though she were receiving support. The Rama rules likewise below, siman 80:15.
