They are exempt even without an oath, etc. The Sema writes that even if it is known that the heirs did not swear, they remain exempt. Bedek HaBayit writes likewise, and this is also implied below in siman 87, seif 32. Baal HaTerumot further explains that the general ban is directed against heirs who heard from their father that he owed the money and had not paid it. See there, Responsa Mahari ben Lev, and Responsa Maharit. Regarding the rule that witnesses to delivery effect the transaction, see the end of simanim 40 and 51 and what I wrote there.
