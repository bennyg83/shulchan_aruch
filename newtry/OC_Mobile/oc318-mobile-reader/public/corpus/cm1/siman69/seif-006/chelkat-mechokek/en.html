And some disagree. Their reason is that we rule that support is the primary enactment. Therefore she should have the superior choice. There is a fixed weekly amount of labor, namely the weight of five sela. In any week she may choose to work for herself, and in another week she may say, "Give me support and I am prepared to perform the fixed quota required of me." See the Rosh, chapter Na'arah, seif katan 10, who writes that if she says, "Today I will not be supported and will not work," tomorrow she is again supported and works. The Rosh there is uncertain whether she can permanently remove the husband's right to her earnings by saying, "I will never accept support.
