Even if he has already paid. Shaar Mishpat raises a question. Suppose one person asserted a doubtful claim against another based on witnesses, the defendant answered, 'I do not know,' and beit din required payment on the strength of those witnesses. Later the defendant produced witnesses establishing that the original witnesses were zomemim. Or suppose heirs collected from other heirs on a document in beit din, and later witnesses testified that their father had already paid the document. Shaar Mishpat suggests that perhaps in such cases the recipients need not return the money, because once paid they had acquired it as ownerless property, and he discusses the issue at length without deciding it. Beit Yehudah on this siman disputes him and maintains that such money is certainly not ownerless. See also Nechmad LaMar'eh on this issue.
