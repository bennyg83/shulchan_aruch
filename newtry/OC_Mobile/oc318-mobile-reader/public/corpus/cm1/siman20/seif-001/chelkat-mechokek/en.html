Maggid Mishneh explains that these lashes apply specifically to ervayot, not merely to relationships forbidden by a negative commandment. Ramban disagrees with Rambam and holds that Torah lashes apply only to actual sexual contact at the threshold level, while other intimate acts are rabbinically prohibited. Tashbetz in Zohar HaRakia, negative commandment 10, agrees with Ramban and proves it from the Gemara in Sotah, which calls intercourse by way of limbs merely licentious conduct and asks, 'did the Torah prohibit licentiousness?'
