But if he did not say, “I have no evidence,” etc. This is not limited to testimony and evidence. The same applies if he advanced one argument and beit din did not ask whether he had further arguments or legal grounds. He may later advance additional arguments that do not contradict the first and thereby overturn the judgment. The Rosh writes this in responsa, rule 13, siman 19, as cited in Darkei Moshe. Thus writes the Sema. Likewise, if they asked whether he had evidence and he remained silent, he may later bring evidence and overturn the judgment, or advance another argument. See there.
