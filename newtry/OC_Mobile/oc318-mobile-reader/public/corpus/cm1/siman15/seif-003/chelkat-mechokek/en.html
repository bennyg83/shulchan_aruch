Some authorities are uncertain. That is, they are uncertain whether rabbinically such a child is treated as offspring. Rama did not cite this opinion in Darkei Moshe. It is in truth a surprising view. If a man has a son from a gentile woman, would that son count as his offspring rabbinically, so that the man's wife would not enter yibum and would instead require chalitzah? Yet the mishnah in Yevamot 22a implies that a son from a maidservant or gentile woman is not offspring at all. See Rabbeinu Yerucham cited by Beit Yosef at the end of siman 16. I later found that Darkei Moshe in siman 156 cites this opinion in the name of Or Zarua at the beginning of Yevamot.
