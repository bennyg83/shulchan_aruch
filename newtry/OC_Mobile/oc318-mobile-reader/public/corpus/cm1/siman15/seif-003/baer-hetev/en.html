He writes. It appears that Rambam disagrees and holds that the judge should not give the defendant a written statement that no judge should involve himself in this case. According to Rambam, the investigation is not intended to discover that the claimant is deceitful, for the judge already knows from the outset that he is deceitful; rather, its purpose is to defeat the deceit. Therefore Rambam's text reads, "and let one whose heart is complete in the matter adjudicate it," meaning that even if this judge cannot ascertain the truth, perhaps other judges will investigate more deeply and discover it. The Tur, however, follows the Rosh, who holds that the investigation is intended to determine whether the claimant is deceitful. Once this is established, the judge must withdraw from the case; therefore the Tur writes, "and let the King of the universe judge him." The Mechaber writes, "and let one whose heart is complete in the matter judge him," yet then states that the practice follows the Rosh. These two statements appear contradictory. Thus concludes the Sema.
