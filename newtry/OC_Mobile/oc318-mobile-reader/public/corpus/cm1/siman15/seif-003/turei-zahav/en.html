In the name of Maharshal, in the Beit Yosef, s.v. “The judge must give priority,” through “and he was not concerned that the argument would be stopped up” According to Rambam there is no difficulty. That was an orphan’s case, which precedes a scholar’s; therefore the other litigant’s argument was chilled. Against an ordinary person, however, a scholar has priority and the dispute may properly be opened for him first. Lechem Mishneh writes similarly.
