But when the batei din became numerous, etc. Gloss: Maharik, shoresh 108, writes that even in the present era a judge is obligated to rule according to the truth as it has become established to him, rather than according to the litigants' claims when those claims contradict the truth. The Rashba likewise writes this in a responsum. Thus writes the Sema. See Responsa Rabbi Moshe Alshich, siman 8, and Responsa Maharam Alashkar, siman 119.
