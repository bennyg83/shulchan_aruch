The Sema writes in the name of Maharik, shoresh 108, that even nowadays the judge must rule according to the truth as established to him, not merely according to the pleadings when they contradict the truth. The Beit Yosef cites this, and likewise the Rashba in Responsa 1147.

If one litigant exaggerates to the other and says, 'The law will obligate you to pay me such and such,' when the law is not so, the judge must tell him, 'You speak falsely.' This is from Rav Menyumi's statement, etc., as Tosafot write there in the name of Rabbenu Tam, Ketubot 69a.
