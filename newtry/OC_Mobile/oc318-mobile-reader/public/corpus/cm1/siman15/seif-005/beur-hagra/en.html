That they should not overturn. Ketubot there.
And they should not impair. There, "I would weaken it, etc." According to the Rif's explanation there, this means requiring him to take an oath. He writes there that one must say the witness was a relative. This is the meaning of "on the testimony of a woman," there in the incident of Rava, "or a relative," as above.
Although his mind is. There, "for it is established for me, etc."
Likewise, he may not. There, 2, the case of a man with whom they deposited, etc.; see the end of siman 297.
Not according to the judge's personal judgment. There, 1. So too the Rif writes in the second explanation, "or for shuda d'dayanei, etc."
(Collected note) And not according to the judge's personal judgment. This is what is written in siman 240, seif 3 in the gloss, following his approach that shuda d'dayanei is interpreted like Rashi, as written in siman 219, siman 240, siman 253, and elsewhere. Therefore it says "according to the judge's judgment," not "according to the judge's desire." (End of collected note.)
And not by estimation. There, 2, because he was not a person of such means.

He delays judgment, etc. As above in seif 3 concerning a fraudulent case.
And he investigates and examines. As above from the Sanhedrin passage.
Or they should make a compromise. As written above in siman 12, seif 5, that he may not issue the money, etc. This follows Tosafot's view in Bava Metzia 42b. According to Rashi, however, the judge himself may make a compromise-like disposition, as above.
Or he should withdraw. From the Shevuot passage as above, and as written above, "if it appears to him, etc.".
