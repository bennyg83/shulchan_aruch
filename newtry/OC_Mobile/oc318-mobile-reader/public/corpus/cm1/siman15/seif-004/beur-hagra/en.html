(Collected note) And if it appears, etc. As stated in the chapter HaIsha Shenaflu: "I am a decisor of halacha, etc." According to Tosafot there, even if she said only a mere disclosure of a fact, from the passage in Bava Batra; see there. Likewise from the incident of the two women before Shlomo; see Beer HaGolah. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) And if it appears, etc. As stated, "she is his mother," and Tamar, Makkot 23b; likewise Shlomo, etc. The law of one who transfers property to evade a claim at the beginning of HaIsha Shenaflu and in Mi SheMet, and many other laws, all depend on assessment of the circumstances. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) And in this manner, etc., and he can, etc. As Tosafot write in Shabbos 10a concerning "a judgment of absolute truth," excluding a fraudulent case, which he should not adjudicate as truth. Likewise in seif 3, "and when it was, etc.," except that there it concerns a claimant and here a defendant. (End of collected note.)
