The foregoing applies when the claimant is deceitful. When the defendant is deceitful, the judge may not withdraw, lest the deceiver profit from his deceit. Instead, the judge must examine and investigate thoroughly to defeat the deception. If a compelling assessment indicates that the defendant is liable, an expert judge who is preeminent in his generation may hold him liable. {Rama: In this manner, the judge may also deny the defendant additional time unless he first provides security, for in these matters the judge has only what his eyes perceive (Maharick, shoresh 14). He may even impose an oath where strict law does not require one, if he does so to clarify the truth (ibid., shoresh 187).}
