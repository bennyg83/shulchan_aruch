To seek grounds in his favor, etc. It appears that a Torah scholar in our times does not have this status. So writes the Sema in seif katan 5 [4], though the Bach does not write this. Gloss: See above, at the end of siman 1, what I wrote in the name of the responsum of Rabbi Yosef of Trani. According to his words there, a Torah scholar in our times does have this status for this purpose, by an a fortiori inference from the law of a litra of gold. Indeed, this follows from the Sema himself, for if the status of a Torah scholar applies in our times regarding a litra of gold, it certainly applies regarding seeking grounds in his favor. This is readily understood.
[A.C.] He has already opened his tongue and has not been given to him
