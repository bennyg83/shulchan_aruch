Seif 1. Who came before him first. The priority normally given to the case that came first is displaced by a case involving restoration of peace between husband and wife, analogous to the verse in which Moshe instructed, "Build cities for your children," before "pens for your sheep." Even where the two matters are legally separate, if the urgency is otherwise equal and both cases are already before the judges, the marital dispute should be heard first. If both arrive simultaneously, it is obvious that the dispute between husband and wife should receive priority. This applies also to monetary matters between spouses, because resolving them is likewise part of seeking peace between them.
