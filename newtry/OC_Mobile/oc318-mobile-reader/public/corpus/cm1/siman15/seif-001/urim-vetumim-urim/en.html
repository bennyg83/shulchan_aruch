Sema says that just as the fixed litra-of-gold humiliation fine for a Torah scholar is not applied to scholars today, so too this special duty of the judge would not apply today. Even if they already opened the case. The rule not to advance a later case ahead of an earlier one is learned from "You shall hear the small as well as the great": once the first case has already been heard, it may not be displaced by a later case. Before the court has actually opened the first case, the priority is only a rabbinic ordering rule. Therefore the Sages themselves allowed even a relative of a Torah scholar to receive priority at that stage out of respect for Torah. But once the first case has begun, the Torah priority is in force. It can be displaced only by the positive commandment of honoring the Torah scholar himself, not by honor due merely to his relative.
