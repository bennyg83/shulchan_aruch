g) All the more so, the orphan's case takes precedence. The Kesef Mishneh asks from the incident of Rav Anan, etc., where Rav Nachman removed an orphans' case from before him and advanced another case, Ketubot 106a, and leaves the matter requiring study. In my humble opinion, it appears that the Rambam did not rule in accordance with that incident because the Gemara concludes there that the litigant's arguments became sealed, meaning his ability to argue was impaired.
