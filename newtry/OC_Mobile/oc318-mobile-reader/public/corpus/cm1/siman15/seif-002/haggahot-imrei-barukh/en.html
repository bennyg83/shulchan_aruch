(Rama, seif katan 3) As much as possible, Rivash siman 266. Note: The Ran, Sanhedrin 32, writes that the intent is not to require all the formal laws of examination and interrogation. Rather, if the judge suspects a particular aspect of fraud, he must investigate and interrogate that specific point. Thus his view differs from Rabbi Betzalel Ashkenazi, who in responsum 5 disputes the Rivash. See also the Ran there on page 40a, where he explains the practical difference in monetary cases when the case appears fraudulent. This requires study.
