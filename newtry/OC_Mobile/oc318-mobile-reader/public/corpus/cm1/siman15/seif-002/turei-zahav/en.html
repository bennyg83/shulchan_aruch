In the name of Mahari of blessed memory, in the Tur “The orphan’s case is heard first,” etc. This appears contrary to Rav Nachman, who advanced a scholar’s case before an orphan’s. Rambam carefully speaks of many cases before the court together. When an orphan, widow, scholar, and ordinary person all arrive together, one can fulfill both the duty to protect the orphan and the duty to honor Torah by hearing the orphan first and the scholar before the ordinary person. If only an orphan and scholar are present, the scholar’s case is heard first because the positive duty of honoring Torah is stronger.
