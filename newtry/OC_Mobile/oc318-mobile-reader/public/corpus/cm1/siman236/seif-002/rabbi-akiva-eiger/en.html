[Shulchan Aruch] If he is a robber. Note: This implies that we infer from the circumstances that the property was not stolen from him. But if it is established that the gentile possessed it through robbery, the acquisition is ineffective, because the Rambam follows his view that ye’ush does not operate on land, as we rule in siman 371. If so, it is difficult how the Mechaber in seif 9 cites the Rosh’s ruling, since the Rosh follows his own view that ye’ush does operate on land. This requires study. Urim Gedolim, study 219.
