(Collected note) Even within, etc. This too is the Tur’s wording. The Rambam omitted it because he holds that this rule does not relate to the later Mishnah. The Mishnah’s language, “How so? He purchased, etc.; the later beis din, etc.,” supports the Rambam. (End of note.)
Or he admitted, or, etc. Bava Batra 48a: according to Shmuel, etc. As explained above in siman 150 seif 3, the Rambam did not read the phrase “and according to Rav Bibi,” which concludes, etc., and therefore distinguishes a robber from a sikarikon. In the case of a sikarikon, the owner’s coercion is not treated like that of an ordinary robber. According to this, Rav Bibi’s statement “When did they say, etc.” also applies specifically to a robber, and regarding the land an admission is treated like receipt of money.
