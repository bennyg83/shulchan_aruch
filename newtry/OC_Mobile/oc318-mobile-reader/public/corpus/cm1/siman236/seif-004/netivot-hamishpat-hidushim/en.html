Or he admitted. Meaning, he now admits that he received the money, but says that he accepted it at the time only because of fear. We do not listen to him, because he should have issued a protest declaration. But if he had previously admitted receiving the money and now says that in truth he never received it, and that his earlier admission itself was made because of fear, the Sema says that we do listen to him, as in siman 205. See Beurim: the primary law is that even the earlier admission is ineffective, and this case is not comparable to siman 205.
