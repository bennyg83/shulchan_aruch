The language of Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Gezeilah. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this entire clause is found in chapter 5 of Gittin, 55b, in the later Mishnah, and in the baraita there, 58b, following Shmuel and Rav Ashi's explanation of the baraita according to Shmuel. It implies that the owners have the stronger hand and may choose either the land or the money. So too in the Rif.
