The language of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 10 of Hilkhot Gezeilah. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it comes from the baraita concerning property taken because of a debt or because of anafra'ot. He explains anafra'ot as a claim that the Jew caused the gentile damage or loss; the word means injury or loss.
He said, “If you don’t have any judgment, then every rapist will tell me about it, but certainly when the owner admits or has witnesses
The statement of Rav Yosef there, as the Gemara concludes: what is the reason? Since there is a governmental court, etc. The Maggid Mishneh writes that because there is a fixed gentile court and the Jew did not go before it, it appears that the gentile's claim is true. Since a true anafra'ot case presupposes that the gentile has witnesses, as stated above, the Gemara says here that this is not treated as anafra'ot.
