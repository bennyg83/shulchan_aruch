When does this apply? When they admitted, etc. The Gemara explains the baraita's phrase concerning confiscation itself as a case where the non-Jew took the property for a debt without proof. Rabbi Yochanan's rule means that in Babylonia a non-Jew ordinarily does not seize without witnesses. The Gemara asks from observed cases and answers that there is no fixed law there. This is the meaning of the continuation: and likewise if, etc. See Baer HaGolah.
