[Sema, letter 6] He said to him, "I purchased it." Note: This is not mentioned in the words of the Rosh, even in the very same volume, as a basis to exempt him with respect to his father. Rather, he writes to exempt him where the claimant did not demand it from him. It may be that the case is specifically where he says, "one of you," as in the case of partners, except that he does not know from which one he received the deposit. This can also be inferred from the Tur, siman 76, who concludes that with a document, if he claims from him and so forth, and likewise one who says "one of you lent me one hundred" is exempt even from an obligation to satisfy Heaven. This implies that the two cases have the same rule. Analyze carefully.
