And the money shall be, etc. As stated in the Mishnah, Bava Metzia 37a.
If he is suspected, etc. Bava Kamma 103b relates the incident of a pious man, etc., who came before Rabbi Akiva. See Tosafos there 104a, s.v. “since, etc.,” who raises a difficulty and gives an alternative answer. We rule like Rabbi Akiva, unlike the Kesef Mishneh’s interpretation cited by Beer HaGolah.
