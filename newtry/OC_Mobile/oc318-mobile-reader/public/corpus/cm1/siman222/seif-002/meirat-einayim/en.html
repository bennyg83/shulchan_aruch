He deposits the purchase money between them. Meaning, he deposits it with beit din, as the Mechaber concludes.
If he is pious, he gives, etc. This applies specifically when each claimant demands payment from him. But if neither is claiming against him and he approaches them on his own and says, "I purchased from the father of one of you and do not know from which," then even to satisfy his obligation toward Heaven he need not pay each one. See above siman 75, or 76 according to the correct reference, and below siman 365.
