(Sema, seif katan 10) Perhaps here it is different because this person comes to extract. Note: This requires study, for the Gemara explicitly gives a different reason for believing him: since the seller admits that the object is not his, the claimant can say to the seller, "Do you not admit that it is not yours? Go away. If another person comes and contradicts me with witnesses, let him do so." See there. See also Korban HaEdah on Yerushalmi Yevamot, chapter 2, halachah 10, passage beginning "we do not derive."
