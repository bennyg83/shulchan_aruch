He must pay each and every one because he committed a transgression. The Sema explains that he does not fulfill the requirements of repentance until the stolen property is returned to the victim, and the Ir Shushan writes similarly. This formulation is not precise. The same penalty applies even if he persists in rebellion and does not wish to repent, provided beit din learns that he swore falsely, such as where he admitted this before others. In such a case we penalize him and obligate him to pay each claimant, as is evident from the Gemara and poskim. See Even HaEzer 40:1. When Rambam and the Mechaber begin by saying that he repented, they merely describe the usual case, since otherwise it is uncommon for the false oath to become known. This is why they do not repeat that condition at the end.
