This requires study, because one does not impose an oath upon an uncertain claim, as the Tur and Mechaber write in simanim 75 and 87. Perhaps this case is different because the claimant seeks to remove the field from the purchaser's possession. We find an even stronger rule in the following siman, regarding one who exchanges a cow for a donkey and the owner of the cow pulls the donkey, after which the cow gives birth and it is unknown whether the birth preceded or followed the meshichah. Even if the owner of the donkey claims only uncertainly that the birth was afterward, while the owner of the cow claims definitely that it was before, the owner of the cow does not retain the offspring except upon oath, as I explained there in the Perishah and Sema.
