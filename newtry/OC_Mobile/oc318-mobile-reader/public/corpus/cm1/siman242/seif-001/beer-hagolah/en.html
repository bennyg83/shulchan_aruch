Alef) This is the language of the Tur, and is straightforward from the third chapter of Bava Batra 40b.
Bet) There in the name of Rabbenu Yonah.
Gimel) This is clear from the rule that even a concealed gift is void. We see that even a minimal disclosure of contrary intent can invalidate a gift.
Dalet) This is explicit there in the Gemara: with respect to a get or a gift, a mere disclosure of the matter is sufficient.
Heh) The Tur, and Rambam likewise in chapter 6 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 3. Rambam explains that if the giver does not truly intend to transfer ownership wholeheartedly, the recipient does not acquire.
