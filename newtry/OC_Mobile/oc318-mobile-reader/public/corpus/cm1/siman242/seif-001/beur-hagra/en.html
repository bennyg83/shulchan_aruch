One who gives, etc. Bava Batra 48a, Rav Hamnuna raised an objection, etc.; "Shmuel concedes, etc." See Rashbam there at the end of s.v. "modeh," and Tosafos there, s.v. "amar," and see Beer HaGolah.
Even if he accepted, etc. This follows from the Gemara's challenge, "but according to Shmuel, etc.," where it does not answer that he accepted responsibility, as it does according to Rav regarding a deed. Likewise, at 47b, "to exclude the view of Rav, etc." We must say that because the baraisa states generally that a robber who brought proof, etc., it implies every circumstance, even a deed. If so, what does the Gemara mean that it teaches Shmuel's rule? Here too it implies even where he accepted responsibility.
And even if, etc. See Beer HaGolah. These are the words of the Rosh there 40b, and all the more so according to the latter explanation of Ri Migash, who explains "no, there, etc." to mean even without the original gift. See below seif 9.
For indeed, etc. Tosafos 48a, s.v. cited above, and the Rosh, unlike Rashbam 40b, s.v. "gilui, etc."
