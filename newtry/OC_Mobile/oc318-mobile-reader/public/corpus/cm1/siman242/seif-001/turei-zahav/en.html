(Seif 3: conceal yourselves, and so forth.) The Rashbam explains that the reason is that the donor may already have given the property to someone else. Although Tosafot in chapter HaSefina, 77b, writes that a person does not want a rumor to circulate that he gave his lands to others, lest people refuse to lend him money, and if so why should we suspect a prior gift here, the Beit Yosef already cites the Ran at the end of this siman that whenever there is a plausible reason why the donor made the gift secretly, it is not considered a concealed gift. This applies where it is reasonable that such a reason existed and the donor himself stated it spontaneously, without evasion.
