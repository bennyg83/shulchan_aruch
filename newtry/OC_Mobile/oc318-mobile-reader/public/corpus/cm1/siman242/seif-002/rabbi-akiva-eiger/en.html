[Shulchan Aruch] He said to the witnesses, "Hide yourselves." Note: If he gave a reason for this, saying, "Hide yourselves so that my son, so and so, will not hear," it may be said that this is not a concealed gift. This differs from the case at the end of this siman, for there one may say that since he did not give it of his own free will, his intention was not to give it to either one. See Beit Yosef at the end of this siman and Responsa Mahari ben Lev, part 1, siman 58, at the end.
