(See there: write it in the marketplace and sign it publicly.) The reason the ruling did not combine the two into a single clause, “write and sign it in the marketplace,” is to clarify that the writing too should be public. Although shortly afterward we say that the writing itself need not be public, it is nevertheless preferable that it be public. Moreover, we follow the rule that witnesses to delivery effect the transaction, so publicity at the writing stage alone would not suffice.
