We are not concerned with an unspecified instruction, etc. Meaning, if the donor did not explicitly tell the witnesses, "Write it in the marketplace," but merely told them, "Write," it is still valid according to this view. The Beit Yosef likewise explains the words of the Rosh and Tosafot this way. In the Derishah, however, I wrote and proved that this is not necessarily their intent. See there.
