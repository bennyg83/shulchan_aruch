Maharikash implies that even if he said, "Do not reveal it," we interpret his words as meaning only until the time of death. See Responsa Kiryat Melech Rav, siman 2. From Responsa Mahari ben Lev, part 1, siman 8, it is evident that he holds that the law of a concealed gift also applies to consecrated property. This is likewise evident from Responsa Rashdam, Yoreh Deah, siman 173, and Responsa Mahari Adrabi, siman 119. See Knesset HaGedolah, glosses to the Tur, letter 12, and glosses to Beit Yosef, letter 17, concerning consecrated property and gifts to the poor.
