A shechiv mera who instructed. Ketzot HaChoshen asks what difference there is between a complete deathbed gift and one who expressly commands because of impending death. There is no difficulty. Where one expressly commands because of death, it is natural to say that he is not flattering anyone. He is arranging affairs because he expects to die. In an ordinary gift by a shechiv mera of all his property, however, we infer the death-related intent only from the presumption that a person does not ordinarily give all his property away. In the present case there is another explanation: perhaps he gives it all merely to flatter the recipient. Once that explanation exists, the usual presumption that the gift was made because of death no longer necessarily applies.
