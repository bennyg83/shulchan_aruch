Even if he seized, etc. Although the Gemara says that we are concerned, the Gaon explains that the gift is entirely invalid, as Rav Yehuda says that collection is not made, following the second version. Maggid Mishneh. Even if the matter were doubtful, seizure would not help. This is obvious for land, which remains in its owner's possession, and even for movable property we rule that property seized in doubt is recovered from the seizer. Rosh and Rama rule accordingly in many places. Rambam, however, rules that it is not removed from the recipient, and therefore speaks here specifically of a gift of land. Shulchan Aruch follows Rambam. See Lechem Mishneh.
