Zayin) The Tur, seif 4.
Meaning, this excludes one who gives instructions because of impending death, as explained below in seif 6.
Chet) A statement of Rav Yehudah there.
Tet) According to Rav Ashi and the Gemara's conclusion there at the beginning of 41a, that we are concerned for the concealment. The law of one who gives instructions because of impending death is explained at the end of seif 6; see there.
Yod) According to the second version in the name of Rav Yosef there on 40a.
Kaf) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah U'Matanah, halachah 2, based on the statements above. The Maggid Mishneh explains that Rambam's reference there to land is only because a deed alone is ineffective for movable property without a kinyan. The same concealment rule certainly applies to a kinyan of movable property when the giver instructed that it be hidden.
Lamed) The Tur, seif 6, in the name of Rav Yosef according to the final view, to which the Rosh agrees. The Ramban cites a responsum of a Gaon and Maharam similarly. The Maggid Mishneh also rules this way, as do the Ran in the name of Rabbenu Tam and Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 15, part 2.
