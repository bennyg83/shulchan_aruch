[Shulchan Aruch] But if Shimon. Note: The Ramban, in his novellae, holds that it is not reasonable that one should acquire land merely by assisting the other party. Rather, the Gemara concerns a case where both parties were obligated to build the partition on both parcels because of visual damage, and Reuven built the entire wall on Shimon’s portion. If Shimon assists him while knowing that the entire wall stands on his own land, that constitutes a waiver of any claim he might otherwise have against Reuven for the portion of land used to support the wall.
