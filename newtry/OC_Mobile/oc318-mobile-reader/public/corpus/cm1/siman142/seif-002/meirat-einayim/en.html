Since the assistance was given in error. The Mechaber states 'since it was, etc.' as a settled matter because once we know that Reuven himself brought him into possession by mistake, we also believe Shimon's claim that his assistance was likewise given under that mistake. See the Perishah.
But if Shimon, who assisted him, admits, etc., it constitutes a chazakah. One must say that here all agree that three years and a supporting claim are unnecessary, because we ourselves testify, as it were, that he waived his right. See below siman 149:79 and siman 153:16.
