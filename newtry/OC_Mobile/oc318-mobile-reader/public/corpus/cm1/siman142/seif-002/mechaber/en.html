If the wall between Reuven and Shimon fell, Reuven rebuilt it but mistakenly placed it within Shimon's boundary because he did not recognize the wall's original location, then even though Shimon assisted in the construction, Reuven does not establish ownership of the portion taken from Shimon's property, because the assistance was given in error. But if Shimon admits that he knew Reuven placed the wall within Shimon's property, Reuven establishes ownership even if Reuven himself did not know; he immediately acquires the portion placed inside Shimon's boundary.
