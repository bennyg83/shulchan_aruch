(Seif 1, gloss) The same applies if he rented it. According to Sema, even a lifetime lease is not treated as purchasing one's lawsuit. I do not understand the reason, since the lessee will no longer need to litigate with the other party. Moreover, Bava Metzia 56 asks whether rental is considered sale for the law of overreaching and resolves that the Torah does not say “a permanent sale,” but simply “a sale,” and a rental for a day is itself a sale. This implies that had the verse said “a permanent sale,” the basic distinction would be that sale is permanent and rental temporary. A permanent lease should therefore plainly be treated as a sale. The matter requires study.
