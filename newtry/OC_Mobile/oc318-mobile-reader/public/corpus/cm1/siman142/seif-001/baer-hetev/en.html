He lifted. This is how the Rashbam explains it. The Rambam and Tur, however, write that he assisted in gathering the fruits one by one, lifting them from the ground and placing them into a vessel. It appears that they do not disagree, but rather each states one valid form of assistance. The Mechaber mentions one of them, and the same law applies to the other. Sema. See Responsa Maharshdam, simanim 116, 206, 226, and 234, and Responsa Maharil, siman 81.
He rented it. The Taz challenges the Sema's explanation that, in a rental, even a permanent rental does not invoke the principle that a person may purchase his adversary's claim. He writes that this requires study, for there is proof from the chapter HaZahav, Bava Metzia 56, that a permanent rental is treated like a sale. See there.
