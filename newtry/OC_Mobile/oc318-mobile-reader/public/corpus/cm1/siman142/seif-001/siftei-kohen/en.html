Although, etc. See the responsa of Maharashdam, simanim 206, 226, 116, and 234.
But if he makes the claim after three years, etc. We do not listen to him, meaning even where there is no adverse indication from the fact that he raised the aforementioned produce for the owner. This is the Sema's language. This also resolves the apparently repetitive language of the Tur, first saying until after three years and then saying that this applies only within three years. Examine carefully.
