[Shulchan Aruch] The challenger lifted a basket. Note: Rambam's wording is, "For had he not sold or given it," and the Tur uses the same wording. This implies that a claim is required. Nevertheless, one may say that with respect to this basket of produce, it constitutes a gift even without a claim, analogous to the next seif: if the possessor gave a basket of produce to another person and the challenger assisted the recipient in receiving it from the possessor, one may say that this does not establish a chazakah. Urim Gedolim, study 143.
