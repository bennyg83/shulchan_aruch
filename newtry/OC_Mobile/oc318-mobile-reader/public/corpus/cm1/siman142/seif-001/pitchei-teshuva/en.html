If he rented it from him. See Baer Heitev and Responsa Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, end of siman 53. He writes that the words of the Taz are difficult. Even if a lease is regarded as a sale for its term, the principle that a person may buy peace rather than litigate does not apply here. That principle was stated only concerning a relatively small payment, and perhaps even the full value of the property, but not where a person would have to continue paying rent year after year without limit, so that the aggregate might exceed the value of the land without end. People do not ordinarily act that way.
