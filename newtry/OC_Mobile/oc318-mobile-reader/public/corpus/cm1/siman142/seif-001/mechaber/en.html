The law when the protesting claimant assists the possessor in establishing possession, and when the assistance was given in error. It contains 3 se'ifim.
Although presumptive ownership is not established in fewer than three years, if the possessor brings witnesses that the protesting claimant lifted a basket of produce from this field onto the possessor's shoulder so that the possessor could carry it to his own house, presumptive ownership is established immediately. {Rama: Some say the same applies if the possessor sent the protesting claimant a gift from the produce he was consuming and the claimant accepted it. Presumptive ownership is established immediately, because had the land belonged to the claimant, he should not have accepted its produce as a gift but should have taken all of it. From this we learn that if Reuven and Shimon dispute a right of residence in a community, and Reuven purchases that right from Shimon for one year, the right belongs to Shimon. Had it belonged to Reuven, he would not have purchased one year from Shimon. The same applies in every similar case, and also when he rented it from him, as explained below in siman 147.} If the protesting claimant says, 'I placed him on the land only to consume its produce, and the produce was his, but I did not sell him the land itself,' he is believed, and the possessor establishes no ownership. This applies within three years. If the claimant makes this claim after three years, he is not heard. Therefore, if he placed the person there only to consume produce, he must protest within three years and make known that the grant was only for produce. See note 76.
