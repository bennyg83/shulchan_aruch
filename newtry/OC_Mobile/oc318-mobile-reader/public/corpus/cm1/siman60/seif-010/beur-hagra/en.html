(Supplement) Shimon. Meaning, as stated in seif 9, that the document was written in Levi's name, but the right was not transferred to Levi. If it was written for Levi, however, Reuven cannot waive the debt, as explained above in seif 8. See siman 66, seif 23, in the Rama, and above, siman 60, seif 8. (End of supplement.)
