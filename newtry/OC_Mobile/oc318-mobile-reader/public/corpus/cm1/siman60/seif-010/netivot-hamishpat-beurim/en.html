The law is with Shimon. See Sema. His intent is that where the money itself was never transferred to Levi and only the document was written in Levi's name and then delivered to the original lender, Levi does not acquire. But if the lender instructed, 'Write it in Levi's name and give it to Levi,' Levi can acquire, as Tur and the rules of transfer indicate. Sema's phrase that 'the document later came into Levi's hands' refers to its coming there only afterward, not as part of the original act of acquisition. There is therefore no disagreement between Sema and Shach.
