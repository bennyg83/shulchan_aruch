Shimon owed Reuven one hundred, and they compromised that Shimon would execute a debt document to Levi for twenty dinars. Afterwards Reuven gave Shimon a release from all monetary claims. Shimon now argues that since Levi did not lend him anything and the underlying creditor was Reuven, Reuven's release of all monetary claims includes this obligation as well. The law is with Shimon.
