The law favors Shimon. This refers to a case in which the debt still belongs to Reuven, and he merely had the document written in Levi's name for some reason. This is explicit in the cited responsum of the Rashba. It is obvious that if Reuven had actually transferred the money to Levi, Reuven could no longer waive the debt. The Levush and Sema, seif katan 30, explain that the document had not yet reached Levi's possession. That qualification is unnecessary. Even if it had reached Levi, so long as Reuven did not transfer the money to him and merely had the document written in his name, Levi acquires nothing, as explained above in seif 1. See the responsa of Maharashdam, siman 50.
