He is obligated, etc. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: This applies specifically where he obligates himself personally to give the fruits of a palm tree and accepts responsibility if they are destroyed before he gives them, so that he must pay their value. If he obligates himself to give the fruits of a palm tree but expressly stipulates that he assumes no responsibility for them, no obligation takes effect at all. See Maharit, Responsa Choshen Mishpat, siman 84; Ketzos HaChoshen, siman 203; and Responsa Raanach, part I, siman 66. End of quote. See what I wrote above, seif katan 12, and examine carefully.
