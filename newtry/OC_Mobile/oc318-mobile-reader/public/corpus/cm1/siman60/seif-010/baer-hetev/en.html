Shimon. The Sema writes that here too the Mechaber abbreviated the source. The responsum states: afterward Levi wrote Reuven a power of attorney to claim those twenty gold coins from Shimon. Reuven came and demanded them, and Shimon argued as recorded there. The response ruled that although Reuven had written the original document in Levi's name, he had not thereby transferred the money to Levi. This implies, says the Sema, that the ruling applies specifically where the document never came into Levi's possession. Had it reached Levi, one could say that by writing it in Levi's name and delivering it to him Reuven transferred the money to him, and even an explicit waiver would then be ineffective, as stated in siman 66, seif 23. This is clear. The Shach writes, however, that even if the document reached Levi, if the circumstances show that Reuven did not transfer the money to him and merely wrote the document in his name, Levi did not acquire it. See Responsa Maharashdam, siman 90.
