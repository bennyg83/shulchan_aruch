To support his fellow without specifying a term. See Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, siman 75, concerning a document stating that Shimon undertook to support Reuven for 'several years.' He writes that Shimon must at minimum provide three years. The phrase 'several years' is intended to add and naturally implies more than two. However, because the holder of the document has the lower hand, he cannot extract more than three years. At minimum, though, three years are required. See there.
