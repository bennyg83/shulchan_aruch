(Seif 8: If he does not prejudice others.) The wording implies that if Levi owes others and cannot pay them, he is not believed to say that the principal debt belongs to Reuven. This is very difficult. Even if Levi says the debt is his own, his creditors cannot collect from this document because it is held by Reuven. Reuven cannot collect without writing and delivery, but Levi also cannot collect while the instrument is outside his possession, and the debtor need not pay Levi even against a receipt when another holds the document. The Tur’s reading is therefore preferable: “since he does not prejudice others,” stating as a fact that no creditor can collect from it. The Rosh’s responsum has the same language. The Mechaber’s conditional wording is inaccurate.
