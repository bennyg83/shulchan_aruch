The woman. The Sema writes that this means a wife who does not conduct business within the household, as explained in siman 62, seif 1, where Rama repeats this rule. The Shach writes that the source, a responsum of the Rosh, implies that the rule applies even where she conducts business in the household if the husband himself wrote the documents. The Sema himself writes similarly in siman 62 in explaining the Tur's position in Even HaEzer, siman 86. For practical halacha, however, the Shach writes there that the Rashba's opposing view is primary.
