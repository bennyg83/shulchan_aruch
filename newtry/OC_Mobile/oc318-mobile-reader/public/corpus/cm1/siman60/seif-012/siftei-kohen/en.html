The law favors the woman. The Sema explains that this means she does not conduct business within the household, as stated in siman 62, seif 1, where the Rama repeats this ruling. However, the source of this law, Responsa of the Rosh, rule 96, siman 4, implies that it applies even when she does conduct business in the household, where the husband himself had the documents written in her name. I explain this below in siman 62, seif katan 7. The Sema himself, there in seif katan 6, likewise understands the Tur in Even HaEzer, end of siman 86, in this manner. Necessarily, the Tur derived his ruling from this responsum of the Rosh, as I explain there. As to the practical ruling, however, I wrote there that the principal view is that of the Rashba in responsum 957, who disagrees. See there.
