m) The law of one who purchased land or lent money in the names of his minor sons, had the documents written in their names, deposited them with a trusted person, and said that if he died they should belong to his sons after him; he later married and died. His sons acquire the land and those documents. Responsa Rosh, rule 57, siman 3. See also Rivash, siman 250, who discusses this at length. Sema. The law of one who produces another's handwritten statement that he swore a Torah oath to give him a certain item, while the other says he did not swear but only wrote that wording, and the claimant admits this, is discussed in Yoreh Deah siman 232, seif 12 in the gloss.
