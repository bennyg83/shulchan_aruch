(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 19, in the novellae) Nevertheless he is believed by a migo. Note: This requires study. Had he claimed that the money was lost through unavoidable circumstances he would have been required to swear. When he claims that he did not have the money at that time, if the holder of the document does not contradict him with certainty, he need not swear and only a general ban is imposed. There is therefore no full migo.
