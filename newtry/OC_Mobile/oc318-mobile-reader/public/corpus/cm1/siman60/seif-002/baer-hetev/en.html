Five. The Shach cites several poskim who hold that such an obligation is considered fixed because the number of years is fixed. He therefore writes that the practical law requires study. He further writes that Rama's phrase, "or he did not fix a number of years," also requires study. What is Rama's source? The Rashba explicitly writes the opposite in a responsum: one who undertakes by kinyan to give another one hundred zuz for as long as he serves as a scribe or cantor has undertaken a fixed obligation even though the number of years is not fixed. Rabbeinu Yerucham likewise rules so. See there.
We adopt. The Shach writes that Responsa Mahari ben Lev rules that the party in possession may say, "I rely on Rambam," and Rashdam and Maharashach rule similarly. The Shach disagrees. It is well established that one cannot say "I rely on the lone opinion" where all other Jewish authorities disagree with it. He rejects the proofs brought for Rambam's position. He adds, however, that this concerns collection from unencumbered property. Whether such an obligation may be collected from encumbered property requires study.
