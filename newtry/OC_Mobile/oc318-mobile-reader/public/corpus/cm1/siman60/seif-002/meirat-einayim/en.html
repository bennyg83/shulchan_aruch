Or to clothe him for five years. Although the number of years is fixed, the clothing obligation is not fixed in amount, because prices may increase. See Derishah.
Or he did not fix a number of years. According to Rambam this is all the more so. Rama writes it because of those who disagree, to teach that even if the number of years is not fixed they hold that the acquisition is effective. Alternatively, it means that he transferred a fixed quantity whose value is not subject to price fluctuation, but did not fix the number of years. Rashba, cited in Darkei Moshe, writes that this is considered a fixed obligation even according to Rambam.
