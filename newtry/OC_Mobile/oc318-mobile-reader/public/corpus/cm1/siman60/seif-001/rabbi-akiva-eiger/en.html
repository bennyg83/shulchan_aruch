[Shulchan Aruch] Movable property together with land that I have acquired. Note: If the borrower owned land received from others as a gift on condition that it be returned, and he encumbered movable property to the lender together with that land, the lien is ineffective. Since if he had encumbered the land itself the creditor could not collect from it, the ancillary acquisition of the movable property through that land likewise fails. Shitah Mekubetzet, Bava Batra 44, s.v. De'iknu, in the name of Aliyot deRabbeinu Yonah.
