The borrower had a debt instrument. The Mechaber appears to hold that debt instruments can be subjected to a lien through acquisition together with land even though the creditor did not transfer them by writing and delivery and did not write the formula, "Acquire it and all its liens." This is because writing and delivery are required for sale, but lien is stronger than acquisition. Rosh writes similarly that after acquired property can become encumbered even though it cannot yet be sold. See my discussions in simanim 64, 66, and 59.
