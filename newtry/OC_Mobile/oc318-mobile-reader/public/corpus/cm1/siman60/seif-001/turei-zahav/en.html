(Shulchan Aruch, seif 3: Or for as long as he needs, meaning.) This requires study. Why is it different from one who undertakes to support his wife’s daughter, where the Gemara says that even if she later has food from elsewhere, he must pay her the value of support? The gloss answers that there the obligation was fixed for five years, whereas here the undertaking is open-ended. An obligation stated generally lasts only while the recipient actually needs support. One could also distinguish that the marriage undertaking depends on the wife’s intent as well, but the distinction between a fixed term and an indefinite undertaking is sufficient.
