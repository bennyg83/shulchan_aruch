They compel Shimon. The Shach disagrees, holding that since the document is written in Shimon's name, Reuven is not believed. In the end, however, he concludes that the primary ruling follows the Shulchan Aruch and Baal HaTerumot. See Beurim, that this applies even where it is known that the admission was false. If the admission harms other creditors and is therefore not believed, the person must return even the physical debt document itself. If the document cannot be recovered from him, the borrower must nevertheless pay. See Beurim.
