Or he accepted responsibility. See Baer Heitev in the name of the Shach. See Responsa Noda B'Yehuda, from siman 25 through siman 28, where he debates this matter with his son in law, the Gaon Rabbi Yosef, regarding the proper explanation of the Ramban's words. The Shach's interpretation of the Ramban is difficult at first glance, for according to it, what relevance does acceptance of responsibility have here? It neither adds nor detracts, because the legal effect comes from the personal obligation itself. See there at length.
