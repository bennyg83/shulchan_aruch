By a kinyan. The Sema writes that this does not mean that a separate kinyan was made for him, for then it would be obvious that he acquired, and indeed he would acquire even the body of the field. Rather, it means that a deed of acquisition was written for him, as opposed to a mere evidentiary document, which does not itself effect acquisition until the purchase money is paid, as explained by the Tur and Shulchan Aruch in simanim 190 and 191. The ruling teaches that even so, this person acquires the mortgage right. See Responsa Maharshal, volume 2, siman 49.
