With a kinyan. This does not mean that a separate kinyan was made, because in such a case even an outright sale of the body of the field would take effect. Rather, it refers to a deed that itself effects acquisition, meaning the document says, "This field is mortgaged to you." This excludes a mere evidentiary document. In an ordinary sale of this kind, the purchaser does not acquire until he also pays the purchase price, unless the seller sells the field because it is undesirable, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch explain in siman 190. With a mortgage, however, the mortgagee acquires the lien through the document, Sema.
