The Tur, seif 16. So too Baal HaTerumot there.
Baal HaTerumot explains there that since there was a kinyan, and land is acquired by exchange, this follows from the statement, 'All are acquired by kinyan except a woman,' Kiddushin 6b. We do not say that a loan is given for expenditure and therefore cannot be acquired, because just as the loan itself cannot be acquired, so too its lien does not take effect, as we say, 'There is no maneh here, therefore there is no collateral here,' Kiddushin 8b.
