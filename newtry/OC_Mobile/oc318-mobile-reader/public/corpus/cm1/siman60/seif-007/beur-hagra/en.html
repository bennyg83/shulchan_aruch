Reuven acquired the pledged property. Bava Metzia 65b discusses one who lent money on the security of his field. The Geonim explain that after the loan he pledged the field to him. Likewise, the Gemara there, 66a, states in the name of Rav Nachman that even if this occurred afterward, although he may retract because it is an asmachta, if he said that it should take effect from now, the acquisition is valid.
A document accompanied by a kinyan. Without this, the property is not encumbered merely because of the loan, as indicated by Kiddushin 47a. Encumbrance is comparable to hire, and Bava Kamma 79 and Bava Metzia 99 state that just as land is acquired by money, so too hire is created by money. With a document, however, we say that the money was given on the strength of the document, as stated in Bava Metzia 51a. [Citation references: 81.]
