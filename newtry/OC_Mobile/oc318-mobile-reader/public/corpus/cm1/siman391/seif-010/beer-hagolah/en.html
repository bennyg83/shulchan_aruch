tet) The Tur there in the name of his father the Rosh. In his rulings the Rosh follows Shmuel's first version, exempting where the animal turns its head, and all the more so where the owner set aside a place. This follows from the Gemara's later statement, without qualification, that according to all opinions the case is one where it turns its head, whether according to Rav or Shmuel. It also appears from the Rif and Rambam, who do not bring the law of setting aside a place.
