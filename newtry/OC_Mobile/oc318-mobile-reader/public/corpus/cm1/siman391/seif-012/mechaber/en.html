If an animal stood in the injured party's domain, pulled produce from the public domain, and ate it in the injured party's domain, the law is unresolved. Rama: The same applies if the animal stood in the public domain, took food from the injured party's domain, and ate it in the public domain. This uncertainty applies only where the food began in one domain and the animal rolled or drew it into another. If the food is long, with part inside and part outside, and pulling one end causes the other end to follow, it is treated as having been eaten in the place where the animal began pulling it, whether that place was private or public. Accordingly, the owner pays only the benefit received. If the injured party seized the amount of full damages, it is not removed from him. Rama: It was already explained in siman 390 that some authorities hold seizure ineffective in an unresolved question of law.
