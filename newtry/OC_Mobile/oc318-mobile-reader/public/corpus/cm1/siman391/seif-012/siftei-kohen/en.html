For some disagree, etc. Sema explains that because the claimant initially seized lawfully up to the amount of benefit, the seizure is effective for the remainder as well. This is strained. Rather, this case differs because it is an unresolved question that did not conclude with the formal term teiku. In such a case even the Rosh and Tur permit seizure, as explained above, though Sema later retracted and in Tekfo Kohen held that there is no distinction between teiku and another unresolved question.
