A wild animal that tore apart an animal and ate it, etc. See siman 389, where the Tur and Rama write that this applies specifically to a wolf and the like, whose normal manner is to tear and eat. According to Ri and the Rosh, a lion tearing prey for eating is an unusual act, because its normal manner is to crush and then eat. See the Perishah.
But the value of the carcass. Meaning, the value of what it ate after killing the animal.
