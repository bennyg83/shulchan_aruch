“Son qua.” B:
And it consumed. This word does not appear in Tur. It means that tooth is also derived from 'and it consumed,' just as stated on 3a. Bach and Sema likewise add the word. The law is correct, for tooth is certainly derived from that verse. The Gemara's answer is only a formal resolution. This also avoids Tosafot's forced explanation.
(Supplement) And it consumed, etc. The statement that an additional verse was needed lest one have thought otherwise is merely a formal answer. The Tanna cites the verse 'as another consumes,' and the beginning of HaKones indicates likewise. So too on 4a the Tanna says, 'ox, etc.': foot cannot be derived from tooth, nor tooth from foot.
