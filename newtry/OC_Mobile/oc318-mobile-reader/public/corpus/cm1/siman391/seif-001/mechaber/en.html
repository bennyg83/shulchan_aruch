Where the category of tooth applies, and when the owner pays the full damage or only the benefit received. It contains twelve seifim.
Tooth is a primary category derived from the verse, 'He shall send forth his livestock and it shall consume.' It includes an animal that eats another person's produce, where the food is fit for it and it derives benefit from eating it. The owner pays full damages whether the animal consumed the produce or merely spoiled it without fully consuming it. Every similar act in which the animal causes damage while deriving benefit is a derivative of tooth, such as rubbing against a wall for pleasure and damaging it, or soiling produce or utensils for pleasure by rolling upon them.
