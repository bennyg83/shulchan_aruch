Rather, etc. One may rule like Rabbah because he was Rava's teacher, or like Rava because he was later. Rif and Rambam therefore cite both views, and Mechaber rules leniently.
(Supplement) Meaning, etc. Some maintain that the law follows Rabbah because he was Rava's teacher. Rif and Rosh discuss this principle, and the leading authorities distinguish between Rava expressly responding to his teacher, where the teacher's ruling prevails, and an ordinary dispute between two authorities, where the law follows Rava.
