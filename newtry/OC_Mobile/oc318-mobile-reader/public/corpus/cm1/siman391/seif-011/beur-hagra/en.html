If it was, etc. This follows Rif's view that Rava's statement explains Ilfa rather than rejecting him. Unlike Rashi, Tosafot, and Razah, the animal pays full damages rather than half damages. Raavad, Rashba, and Rosh likewise state that this is not an unusual act, since eating in this manner is its ordinary behavior. The Yerushalmi and Tosefta explicitly say that it pays full damages. The rationale is that such a place is treated like the injured party's domain, for travelers are entitled to carry their produce there.
And if it is written, and if it is called, and it is called. According to Dr. Danch, he pays, and as “the groceries in which he has changed and eaten a container that he has changed and that he will pay a visa to his back, and that he has stripped her mouth and eaten her from paying a visa.”
And some say, etc. Rosh explains Rif and the Gemara differently. The phrase 'it is not its normal manner' means that it could reach the food only by jumping. The reason is that where the animal cannot reach in its ordinary manner, the owner of the produce may place it there.
