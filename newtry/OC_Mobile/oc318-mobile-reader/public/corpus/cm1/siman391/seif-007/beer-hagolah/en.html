a) The Sema struggled to explain how this is a reason for the opinion that imposes liability, and also raised a difficulty. In my humble opinion, the text is clearly corrupt: the word 'because' should be deleted, and instead of 'mouth of pages' it should read 'the mouth of the cow.' It is an independent law, as in the Tur, and the Beit Yosef corrected it there. The source is the Gemara on 23a, which asks whether the mouth of the cow is considered the injured party's courtyard. Tosafot imply that this is indeed the conclusion, and the Rashba and Rosh likewise write so.
