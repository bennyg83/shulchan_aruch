When is this said? With a deposit whose owner went overseas, but if he is with him in that country, etc. This is also the Rambam's language. The Tur writes here, "when the depositor is not with him in the province, but if he is with him, etc." Ordinarily in Talmudic and halachic language "province" can mean city. The Tur says explicitly in siman 267: "As they said regarding a lost object, so they said regarding a deposit whose owner went overseas, but if he is in the city, he need not tend to it." Since the passage begins with "overseas" and ends with "in the city," opposite extremes, "in the city" there should not mean literally in the same city as the custodian. Rather, the distinction is between an owner gone to a distant place and hidden from view, whose return time is unknown, and one who remains in an inhabited area within the same region, so that the custodian can reach him. According to this, the Rambam, Mechaber, and Tur all agree.
