If a barrel is deposited and the bailee moves it for his own use, he is liable if it breaks, whether before or after returning it to its designated place. If he moves it for the barrel's own benefit, he is exempt whether it breaks in his hand or after being placed elsewhere. Rama: If moved because he needed its location, he is liable for negligence but exempt from unavoidable accidents. If the accident resulted from the changed place, he remains liable after setting it down when the owner had designated a place, but is exempt after setting it down when no place was designated.
