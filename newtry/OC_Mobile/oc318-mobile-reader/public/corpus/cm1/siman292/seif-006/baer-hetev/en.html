From this language, Derri Epi means that she did not take away from her place to exaggerate or send her hand to her deficiency, but that she was moved to her instead of a ladder to take chicks from your back and return her later on to the place of the Affia owes her rape as she swayed to the siege, soaked in the pocket. A:
Its place. The Sema explains: for example, it was lying in an exposed place and he moved it to a concealed place so that it would not break. The Shach writes that in his view this is a scribal error and should read "for its benefit," and Rashi likewise explains it that way.
