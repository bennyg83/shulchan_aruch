[Shulchan Aruch] Or through his agent. Note: If he instructed a non-Jew to misappropriate the deposit, one may say that he is likewise liable. The Torah imposed liability for this very act, namely, that he commands and allows another person to misappropriate the deposit. The liability is not based upon the ordinary law of agency. See Maharit, Chiddushei Poskim, at the beginning of chapter 2 of Kiddushin.
