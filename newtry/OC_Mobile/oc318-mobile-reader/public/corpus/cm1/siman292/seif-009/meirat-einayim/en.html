If he did not have money available there, etc. The word "there" is precise. Even if he has money at home or elsewhere, so long as he has no money at the place where the extortionists confronted him, he may use the deposited money to redeem himself. He need not later reimburse the deposit from his own funds when he returns home. Nor do we say that the extortionists came against him merely because he had a reputation as a wealthy person. The Tur writes this in the Ramah's name. A similar rule appears in Yoreh Deah siman 253 regarding one traveling who has nothing to eat. He may receive charity and need not later repay it.
