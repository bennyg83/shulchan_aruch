A bailee of produce may not mix it with his own. If he does, he calculates the amount deposited, determines the total shrinkage, attributes the proportional shrinkage to the deposit, and returns the balance after taking an oath. Rama: If money entrusted for transport at his responsibility is mixed with his own and some is lost, loss is apportioned proportionally. This applies to fungible money. If distinct gold coins or similar identified objects are mixed and one is lost, the possessor may tell the claimant that perhaps the claimant's item was lost. If two people deposited with one bailee and one deposit was lost or stolen, follow the majority; an item separated is presumed to come from the majority. If fixed in place, it is treated as half and half.
