This applies, etc., because, etc. As above in seif 7 regarding money, where the law distinguishes a moneychanger from a householder. This applies only when the owner is not particular, as the Gemara distinguishes tied from loose money. Thus the wording, 'because certainly, etc.' If the owner is known to object, use is forbidden. For preventing interruption of Torah study, however, copying may be permitted in every case, as stated below. Mordekhai cites the teaching that one should not shame a person who 'steals' words of Torah to satisfy his hunger for learning.
