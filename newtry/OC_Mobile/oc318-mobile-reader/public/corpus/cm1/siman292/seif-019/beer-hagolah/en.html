Heh) There, chapter 7 of Hilkhot She'eilah U'Pikadon, halachah 5, from the baraita in the third chapter of Bava Metzia 38a.
Vav) There, halachah 5. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this was not explained explicitly in the Gemara, but Rambam compares it to money tied up with a shopkeeper, as explained in seif 7. This is not comparable to money received from the sale of a lost object, where the finder is liable even for unavoidable accidents although he never used the money, as explained above in the relevant siman. There, however, the finder is already liable for theft and loss even regarding the lost object itself, although he may not use it. Since he is permitted to use the money received for it, his liability is raised one additional level to liability for accidents. Here, by contrast, without permission to use the money he would be only an unpaid custodian. It is therefore enough to raise him to the status of a paid custodian because he may use the principal.
