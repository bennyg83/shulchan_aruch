[Shulchan Aruch] Rather, from the time he uses it. Note: Nimukei Yosef writes explicitly that he becomes liable from the time he lifts it. In Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Gezelah, siman 18, it is possible to say that even a custodian who lifted the object in order to use it does not become a borrower until he actually uses it. But one who takes an object from the owner's possession by way of borrowing without the owner's knowledge becomes a robber immediately.
