Like a robber. A borrower who borrows with the owner's consent becomes liable for unavoidable accidents from the moment he draws the item out of the owner's domain, even if he has not yet used it or lifted it for use. Tosafot explain that where the borrowing is consensual, since he intends to use it tomorrow or on another day, once he borrows it and draws it from the owner's domain it is considered as though he has already used it. This differs from one with whom an item was deposited merely to remain there unused. So long as he has not used it, it remains in the owner's domain, since it was not placed there for use. Sema.
