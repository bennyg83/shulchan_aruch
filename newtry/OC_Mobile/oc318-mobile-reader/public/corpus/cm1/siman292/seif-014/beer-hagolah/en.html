Law 6 there, following the ruling of Rambam's teachers, Rabbi Yosef HaLevi and his own teacher. The Maggid Mishneh writes that their ruling rests on the principle that one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear must pay, without an oath by the opposing party. This has already been explained in the Gemara and above in siman 75, seif 9. The Tur likewise cites the Rosh. Rambam adds that the guardian may impose a cherem on anyone who took more from him than was proper.
