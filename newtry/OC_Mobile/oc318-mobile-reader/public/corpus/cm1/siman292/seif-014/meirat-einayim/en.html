He is negligent. Since he is negligent, if after mixing the deposits the depositor says, "Return my deposit, which contained such and such an amount," and the custodian says, "I do not know," the Rambam in chapter 5 of Hilchot She'eilah begins with this rule and writes that when a custodian is liable to pay and says, "I do not know how much," while the depositor makes a definite claim, the depositor takes without an oath. The Tur and Mechaber cite this in the beginning of siman 298. Although the Rosh distinguishes that rule in some situations, as the Tur records there, he agrees in this case. Here the custodian mixed the property with his own hands, which is a greater negligence. I explained an additional distinction at the beginning of siman 298.
