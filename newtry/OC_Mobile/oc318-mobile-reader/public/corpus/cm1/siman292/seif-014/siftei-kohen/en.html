He deposited produce with him, etc. This is the Rambam's language in chapter 5 of the Laws of Borrowing. The Rosh cites it in Perek Shevuat HaDayanim and concludes that even less than a perutah may be called produce. His language implies that he understood the Rambam to mean that the defendant expressly admits, 'I know the produce was worth at least one perutah more, but I do not know how much.' The Tur cites this at the end of seif 7. In my opinion, that is not the Rambam's intent. Rather, even a general admission concerning produce is not construed as an admission of less than a perutah. I discussed this at length above, siman 72, seif 12, seif katan 50, and challenged the Rosh's reading. See Responsa Rabbi Shlomo Kohen, part II, siman 17, and Responsa Rashdam, siman 69.
