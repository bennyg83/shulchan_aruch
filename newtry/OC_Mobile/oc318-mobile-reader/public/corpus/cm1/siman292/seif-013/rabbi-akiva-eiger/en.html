[Shulchan Aruch] If it was not readily available. Note: This refers to a case where it was deposited with him for him to return it to its owner. But if it was given to him to distribute to captives, he is exempt in any event, for the verse is expounded as referring to property given "to guard," and not to property given "to distribute." So write Nimukei Yosef and Lechem Mishneh. See below, siman 301, seif 6 in the gloss.
