[Sema, subparagraph 12] Meaning, for example. Note: In my humble understanding, "because he needs its place" means that the custodian needs the area where the deposited object lies for his own use and moves the deposit elsewhere. In such a case, this is not considered use of the object for his own benefit that would make him liable for unavoidable loss as one who misappropriates a deposit, because his need concerns the place, not the object. At most, it is negligence. A similar concept appears in Hilchos Shabbos regarding moving an object because one needs its place. I later found that this is precisely the Taz’s explanation.
