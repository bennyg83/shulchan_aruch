(Supplement) He did not mix them, etc.; in what case, etc. This follows Rif, who rules like Rava in the name of Rav Nahman, like the Sages, and like Rabbi Yochanan's limitation. Rosh questions why Rif did not follow Rav Nahman bar Yitzchak, the later authority. Yet according to Tosafot's approach, even Rav Nahman bar Yitzchak accepts Rav Kahana's reason where the ordinary deficiency allowance is involved, so there may be no practical difference. Rosh also questions preferring Rav Nahman over Rabbi Yochanan, but Rashba, Raah, and Ran explain that a later student is followed where the master heard the student's reasoning. Rif's ruling is therefore defensible. (End of supplement.) See 413.
