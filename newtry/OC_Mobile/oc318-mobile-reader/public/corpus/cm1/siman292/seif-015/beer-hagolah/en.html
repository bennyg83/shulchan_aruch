Reish) There, at the beginning of chapter 7, from the Mishnah on Bava Metzia 38a, following the first Tanna and Rava in the name of Rav Nachman on 38b.
Shin) Rabbah bar bar Chanah's explanation in the name of Rabbi Yochanan there on 38a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam may agree with the Ramban, who explains that the produce diminished within a short period by the amount ordinarily expected over an entire year. The Tur writes similarly in the name of the Ramban and Rif: since the year has not yet ended, the custodian should not sell it.
Tav) The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes this and says that the same is written in Sefer HaMekach.
