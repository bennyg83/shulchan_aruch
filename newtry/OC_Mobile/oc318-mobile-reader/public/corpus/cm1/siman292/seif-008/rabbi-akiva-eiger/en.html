[Shulchan Aruch] He is permitted to use it. Note: Beit Yosef, Yoreh Deah, siman 169, s.v. "And from the words of the Ramah," writes that when one sends unrestricted money to another person, even if the agent is a moneychanger, the agent may not use it merely for that reason. Rather, this applies where the agent accepted responsibility for the money. See there. See also Shach, siman 126, seif katan 35.
