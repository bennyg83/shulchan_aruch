Who came only because they heard of the deposit. This rule applies specifically to a deposit. It does not apply to one whom extortionists forced to hand over his fellow's money, as explained by the Tur and Mechaber below in siman 388:4. The Beit Yosef explains that a deposit is different because the wealth attributed to him resulted from the deposit in his possession, and because of that perceived wealth the extortionist came against him. See my comments there, where I cite the Ran's alternative formulation of the distinction.
