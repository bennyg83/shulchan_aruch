Rambam, chapter 5, law 2, from the incident in Bava Kamma 116b, according to Rav Ashi's conclusion. The Maggid Mishneh explains why the guardian is not liable for directly handing over the money, as in siman 388. Since the money was in his custody and the robbers came because of this deposit, it is not just to require him to pay from his own property. The Beit Yosef writes that Tosafot and the Rosh likewise distinguish a guardian, who accepted custody with this risk in mind.
