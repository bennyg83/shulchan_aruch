But if, etc. The Rif and Rosh omitted this because Rabbi Elazar's question was according to the opinion that misappropriation requires diminution, whereas we rule that it does not. Hagahos Maimoniyos and Maggid Mishneh. Ra'avad likewise writes so there. One may answer, however, that even according to the view that actual diminution is unnecessary, since his intent was not to misappropriate the entire object he does not become liable for the rest. Yet it is difficult: why does the Gemara say, "shall we say Shmuel holds, etc.?" According to the above, that would not help. It may be that this was the initial assumption, and the answerer rejects it: "no, etc." Even according to the view that actual diminution is unnecessary, he is liable only because of this special reason. Maggid Mishneh.
(Collected note) That they are not one body, etc. Tosafos 41a, s.v. "shenatalah al menas, etc.," implies that this distinction applies specifically to wine, but not oil, even though oil is physically one body.
