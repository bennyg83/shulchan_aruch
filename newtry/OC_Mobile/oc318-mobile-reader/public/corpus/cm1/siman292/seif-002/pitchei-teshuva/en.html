He may use them. See Netivot HaMishpat above, siman 121, seif 5, seif katan 10. This applies specifically to a bailee. A messenger for delivery has no right to use the money, even if he is a money changer and the coins were delivered loose. Thus, if the lender appoints him to bring money to the borrower, or the borrower appoints him to deliver money to the lender, he may not use it. Beit Yosef writes similarly in Yoreh Deah, siman 169, because the money was not given to remain with him for any period but only to be transmitted. All the more so where the borrower gave him the money with language of immediate transfer, making it the lender's money, while the lender never deposited it with him as loose money. But if the lender appointed him as an agent to receive payment from the borrower, the agent may use the money, because this is as though the lender deposited loose money with him. See there, and also siman 125, seif katan 3.
