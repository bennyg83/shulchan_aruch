Doubt. This was Rav Ashi's unresolved question in the Gemara. However, the Gemara implies that the question was asked only according to the opinion that unauthorized use requires actual diminution. According to our ruling that diminution is not required, there is no distinction between a purse and a barrel. For this reason, the Rif, Rosh, and Tur omit this question and its uncertainty. The Rambam, in chapter 3 of Hilkhot Gezelah, records it, and the Raavad objects for this same reason. The Maggid Mishneh reconciles the Rambam. This is the Mechaber's view in citing the Rambam, relying on the Maggid Mishneh's explanation. Ibid.
