To take one dinar from it, etc., this is a doubt. This is Rav Ashi's unresolved question in the Gemara, chapter HaMafkid. Regarding a barrel of wine or honey, which is one unified body, when one lifts it intending to take a little, it is reasonable that the whole barrel serves as the base protecting what he intends to take. Wine keeps properly only in a full vessel, so he becomes a borrower regarding the entire contents because the remainder protects what he takes. But if the deposit is a purse of coins, one dinar does not protect another. Or perhaps a full purse of coins is more presentable and better guarded than a single dinar, and the case therefore somewhat resembles wine, making him liable for all. The Gemara leaves the question unresolved. It appears there, however, that the question arises only according to the opinion that unauthorized use requires actual diminution. According to our ruling that no diminution is required, there should be no distinction between a purse and a barrel. For this reason the Rif, Rosh, and Tur omit the question. The Rambam records it in chapter 3 of Hilchot Gezeilah. The Raavad objects for this reason, while the Maggid Mishneh defends him. The Mechaber follows the Rambam's language and relies upon the Maggid Mishneh's resolution.
