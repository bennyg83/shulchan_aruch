(He is a robber.) The Sema writes that otherwise the agent would be an agent for a transgression. This is an inadvertent error from a great authority. The Gemara explicitly states in the second chapter of Kiddushin, page 42, that misuse of consecrated property and unauthorized use of deposited property are two verses that teach the same rule and therefore cannot establish a general rule. Only in those two cases do we say that agency applies to a transgression, but not elsewhere. Accordingly, the present ruling applies in every circumstance and not only according to the distinction drawn by the Sema.
