Seif 10, gloss. Whatever separates is presumed to separate from the majority, etc. Note: See the Shach, and see Rashi to Bava Metzia 22b, who writes that here we follow the majority. His words require consideration because in monetary law we ordinarily do not follow a majority to extract money. Perhaps lost property is different. See also Rashbam to Bava Batra 93, beginning 'according to the presumption,' where it appears that when the majority is one that exists before us, it can be followed even in monetary law. This supports the Rama's gloss here.
