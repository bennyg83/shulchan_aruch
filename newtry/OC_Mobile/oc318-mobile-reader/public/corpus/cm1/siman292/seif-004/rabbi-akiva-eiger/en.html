[Shulchan Aruch] He does not become liable. Note: This implies that liability arises specifically through lifting or meshichah. Without one of those acts, even if the item is in his courtyard and he intends to diminish the owner’s rights and hold it for himself, he does not become one who misappropriated the deposit. See Responsa Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, siman 89.
