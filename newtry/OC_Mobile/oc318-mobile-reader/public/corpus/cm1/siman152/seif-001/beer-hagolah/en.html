The language of Rambam, chapter 14 of Hilkhot To'en, law 3. Maggid Mishneh follows the textual version, also found in the Halachot and many commentators, that the witnesses say, "He admitted before us that the property belonged to him."
Maggid Mishneh cites those who challenge this text: if witnesses testify to such an admission, why is the rule limited to the sons? Even the original possessor himself, if he brings such witnesses, would retain the property, as stated in the preceding siman. Because of this difficulty, they emend the text to read, "It is necessary where they say: He admitted before us," as Rama cites in the name of Tur. Rashba agreed with this version.
