My learned son-in-law, Rabbi Moshe, asked: Rashi ruled this way because he held that regarding the property of an informer the halachah was not decided according to either side of the dispute. His words clearly imply that if we rule that it is forbidden actively to destroy an informer's property, as the Rif, Rambam, Rosh, and all the poskim rule in siman 388, seif 13, then even according to the dissenting opinion in Rama's gloss there that one may take such property for oneself, the person here would acquire it. Nevertheless, Rama should not have written this ruling here without qualification, because some authorities disagree with that dissenting opinion. Rama himself writes there that Maharshal disagrees with it and that his view is primary. Rashi's own words here also clearly imply that he disagrees. Shach.
