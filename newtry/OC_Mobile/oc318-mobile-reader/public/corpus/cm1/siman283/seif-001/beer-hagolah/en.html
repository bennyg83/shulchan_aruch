The wording of Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilkhot Nachalot, law 9, from Rava's statement in Kiddushin 17b, derived from Scripture.
B) There is a name in Miyamer, and I will tell her that she is a widower
He said, “This is true in our hearts.”
There in the baraita in Kiddushin.
Rambam writes likewise in chapter 9 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah, law 7. The Maggid Mishneh derives it from the incident of Isur the convert. It is explained in siman 275, and the law regarding his loan is explained in siman 127.
It is explained there that if the gentiles know of it and he does not return it, he bears sin. It appears to me that Rambam holds that the Torah permitted gentile property only in a case resembling a lost object, as stated above in siman 266. I later found this explicitly in Rambam, chapter 11 of Hilkhot Gezelah. See siman 359, seifim 1 and 8, and what I wrote there.
