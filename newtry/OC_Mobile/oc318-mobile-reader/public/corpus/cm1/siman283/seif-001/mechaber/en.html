The inheritance of a non-Jew, convert, and Jewish apostate. It contains three seifim.
A non-Jew inherits his father by Torah law. A convert does not inherit his non-Jewish father by Torah law, but the Sages enacted that he inherit what he would have inherited before conversion, lest he return to his former ways. Conditions are effective regarding this inheritance because the non-Jew is not bound by rabbinic inheritance law.
