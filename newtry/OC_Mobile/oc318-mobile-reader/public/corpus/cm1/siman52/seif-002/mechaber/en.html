If a document was erased or blurred but its writing remains discernible, it is valid; otherwise it is invalid. If it decayed and became perforated like a sieve, it is valid. {Rama: If moths or mice ate its beginning, it remains valid so long as the lender, borrower, and essential matter can be recognized from its end (Responsa of the Rosh, klal 68, siman 29, and Responsa Maimoniyos, Mishpatim, siman 62).}
