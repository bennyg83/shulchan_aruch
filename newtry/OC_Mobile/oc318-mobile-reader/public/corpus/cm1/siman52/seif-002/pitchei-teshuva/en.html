See Baer Heitev, that the disqualification applies only when the crosswise tear runs through the entire writing of the document, for otherwise the rule would have no fixed measure. See Tumim, who writes that this does not appear from the Rivash. Nevertheless, if only one loan entry was torn, all agree that the entire document is not disqualified. See there. Get Pashut, Even HaEzer, siman 125, seif katan 92, also writes regarding the Shach's position that his words are not compelling, and that the implication of the authorities is that a crosswise tear even through a single line disqualifies the document. See there.
