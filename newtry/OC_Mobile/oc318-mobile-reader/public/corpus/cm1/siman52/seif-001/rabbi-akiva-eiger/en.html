[Shach, letter 1] For Tosafot did not enact their decree, etc. Note: At first glance this does not appear to be Tosafot's view. When they answered that the case concerns rabbinic interest, they added a new rule that for a rabbinic transgression one must be a wicked person motivated by monetary gain in order to be disqualified. According to the reasoning of Gidulei Terumah, they would not have needed this novelty, for if the case involved rabbinic interest, it could have been one in which the interest was incorporated together with the principal and the witnesses did not know they were disqualified. Necessarily, Tosafot hold that even in such a case we decree concerning the principal because of the interest.
