An animal, etc. Meaning that although Scripture mentions only an animal, the rule applies to utensils as well. See Tur at the beginning of siman 291. Proof comes from the Mishna at the beginning of the third chapter of HaMafkid: 'an animal, etc.'
(Supplement) An animal or utensils. This is the wording of Tosefta Bava Metzia, chapter 8: Who is a renter? One who rented an animal or utensils. Who is a paid guardian? One hired to guard an animal or utensils. (End of supplement.)
