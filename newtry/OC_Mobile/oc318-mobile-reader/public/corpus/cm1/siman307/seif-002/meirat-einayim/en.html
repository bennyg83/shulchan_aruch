Until he performs meshichah. The Gemara says: Just as the Sages instituted meshichah for purchasers, they instituted it for custodians. The Rambam understands this to refer both to the right of the owner or custodian to retract and to the custodial liability itself. A renter, and likewise other custodians, do not become obligated in their respective forms of liability until they make meshichah. Tosafot and those with them disagree. They hold that the statement concerns only retraction, while custodial liability begins as soon as the owner withdraws from guarding in reliance upon the custodian's undertaking. This explains the two opinions cited by the Mechaber. See the Derishah.
