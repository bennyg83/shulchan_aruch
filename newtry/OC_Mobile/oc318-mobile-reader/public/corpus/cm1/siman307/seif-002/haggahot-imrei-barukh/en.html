(Shach, seif katan 4) What if the renter lent it to the owner. Note: See Shitah Mekubetzet, passage beginning "let the owner say to the renter," citing the Rashba and Ran. Their words imply that according to us, who rule like Rabbi Yose, the entire discussion of Rabbi Zeira, Rav Acha, and Rav Ashi has no practical consequence.
