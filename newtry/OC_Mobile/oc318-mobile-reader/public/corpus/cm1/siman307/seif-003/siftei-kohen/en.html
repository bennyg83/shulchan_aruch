He is negligent and must pay the entire damage. This is not merely ordinary negligence. The damage is directly caused by him and is treated as though he damaged with his own hands. Otherwise, one who is negligent regarding land or a house would be exempt. The example of renting a house from Shimon is therefore not exclusive; the same applies to any person. It teaches that even a renter who ordinarily may use the property as he wishes is liable for directly causing this damage. This also explains why the Sema distinguishes the case from one who merely breaches a fence. See Responsa Maharshadam, simanim 266, 371, and 372.
