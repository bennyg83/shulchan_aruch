The first. Even if the lessor knows what happened, this remains the law. So rule Nimukei Yosef, the students of the Rashba, and Maggid Mishneh at the end of chapter 1 of Hilchot Sechirut. This also appears from the Rif and Rambam, who omit the law that sometimes the owners must pay several cows to the renter. Ordinarily, when the animal died in his possession the renter knows that it died naturally. The Rosh likewise wrote that there is a practical difference only where the renter told the borrower, "Your legal claim shall be with the owner," and the like; see there. Mahari ibn Lev, vol. 3, rules similarly. This is the primary ruling, unlike Tosafot, and the Bach also rules so. Shach.
