[Shulchan Aruch] There is no payment for lost time in the case of an animal. Note: Nevertheless, what the animal eats during its illness is included in damages, because beforehand it ate and worked, whereas now it must be fed although it does no work. Only that which it used to produce beyond the cost of its upkeep is considered lost profit. Responsa Shevut Yaakov, volume 3, siman 175.
