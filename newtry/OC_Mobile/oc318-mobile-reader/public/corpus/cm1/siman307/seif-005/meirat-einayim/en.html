And he lent it to another. Meaning, if he violated the Sages' instruction and lent it to another person.
She returns to the owners, etc. Although the renter is exempt from accidents where the animal died naturally, nevertheless, why should the renter conduct business and profit with the owner's cow? The Tur concludes this law by writing that this applies even if the owner knows that the animal died naturally. See the Perishah.
And my legal dispute will be with you, etc. This teaches that we do not interpret his words to mean: whatever your law is with the borrower, my law will be with you, such that you would have to turn over to me whatever you recover from him.
