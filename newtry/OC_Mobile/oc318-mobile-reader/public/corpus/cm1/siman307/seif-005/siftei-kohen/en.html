It returns to the original owners. The Tur adds that this is so even if the owner knows that the animal died naturally. The Nimukei Yosef, Tosafot Rid, and Maggid Mishneh rule likewise, and this is indicated by the Rif and Rambam, who omit the statement that an owner sometimes pays several cows to a renter. Ordinarily, when an animal dies in the renter's domain the owner knows that it died naturally. The Rosh recognizes a difference only where the owner told the renter, “Conduct your litigation with the borrower.” This is also the ruling of Rabbeinu Yerucham and the Bach, contrary to Tosafot.
