Kuf) There, from Rava's statement. The Maggid Mishneh cites some commentators who explain that even though half the money has the status of a loan and half of a deposit, since he received it as an investment to take to a particular place, if the merchandise depreciates before he reaches that place he may return it, because he did not accept the arrangement on that understanding.
Reish) There in the Gemara, where two versions dispute the law. The Rif rules according to the latter version.
