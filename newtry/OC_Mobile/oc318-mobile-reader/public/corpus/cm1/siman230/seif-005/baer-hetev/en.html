Even if the buyer's jugs are new and sound, and even if the buyer never carried the jug on his shoulder, nevertheless we attribute the spoilage to the pouring from the seller's jug into the buyer's jug. This is the meaning of the expression, "Wine leaps upon the shoulders of its master." It does not mean only where he literally carried it on his shoulder, but where he handled it by means of shoulders, meaning workers who moved it and poured it from one barrel into another. The Ran explains it this way, and this must also be the meaning according to the Rif, Rambam, Rosh, and Tur. Ibid.
