Until Shavuot, etc. The Rama distinguishes the case specifically where the wine remains in the seller's cask. In my opinion the ruling follows the Ri cited by the Beit Yosef in seif 2, that even when the wine was transferred into the purchaser's casks, the seller must provide wine that will last until Shavuot. This is also the view of the Ran cited by the Beit Yosef in seif 1. The Ran writes that where the seller said, 'I am selling you fragrant wine,' this is the case taught in the Mishna, that he must provide wine lasting until Shavuot, even though it was poured into the purchaser's casks, for the latter clause is taught in a manner parallel to the first. I found this among writings from my youth.
