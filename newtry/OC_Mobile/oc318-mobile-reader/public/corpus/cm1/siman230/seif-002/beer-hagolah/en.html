Heh) There, halachah 8. The Maggid Mishneh explains that the words "he did not say to him, this" reflect the conclusion of the Gemara there.
Vav) The Maggid Mishneh explains that the case is where the seller did not say, "this for cooking." The reason is explained in the Gemara there, 95a: since a barrel of wine is a single unit, it is not reasonable that some of it should be wine and some not.
