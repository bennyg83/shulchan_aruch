A healthy person, etc. The Yerushalmi cited by Rashbam 143b, s.v. "lekalteih."
Who is fit, etc. As stated in the Gemara there, and certainly in such a case.
If they were fit, etc. See Beer HaGolah. Specifically regarding his daughter-in-law do we say there that he would not leave, etc., and send to his daughter-in-law. Since the Gemara did not say generally, "if his daughters, etc., he sends to his daughters," it implies that this is specifically concerning a daughter-in-law, because he would not leave, etc.
One who had, etc. See Tosafos 131a at the end of s.v. "u'shema minah": "and in the commentary, etc.; this does not appear correct, etc." Even according to Rashi's commentary, a daughter among sons is different, for even Rabbi Yochanan ben Berokah concedes, as stated there 130a, even if she exists in the world. Moreover, a daughter is not included in the term "sons." See Beer HaGolah.
