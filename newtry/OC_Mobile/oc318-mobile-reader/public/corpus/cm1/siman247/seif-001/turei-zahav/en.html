(Seif 3: To my sons and my daughters, etc.) In such a case we must interpret the deathly ill person's words, "My property to my sons and my daughters," as language of gift, because language of inheritance is ineffective for a daughter. We learn from this that whenever one uses regarding a daughter an expression that is doubtful whether it denotes gift or inheritance, we interpret his intent as a gift, even when he is deathly ill.
