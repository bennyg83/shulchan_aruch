Vav) An unresolved question in the Gemara, resolved there by the Amoraim from verses showing that even one son can be referred to in plural language.
Zayin) The incident there, following Mar bar Rav Ashi, who held that people do not ordinarily call a son's son simply "son." A baraita supports him.
Chet) The Tur, seif 4, in the name of the Ramah.
Tet) He explains that the reason is that they are fit to inherit him and stand in the place of sons. See the Sema, who infers from this wording that where the sons are alive the rule is different. He further writes that if one says, "to my relatives," whether masculine or feminine, this includes all relatives who are disqualified from testifying for him.
