Kaf) This is the language of the Rambam there, chapter 11 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah, halachah 6, from the incident of one who said to his wife, "My property is for you and my sons," and Rav Yosef's ruling, Bava Batra 143a.
Lamed) The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's view is that the collective group always takes one half.
Mem) The Tur, seif 7, following the words of his father the Rosh in his rulings there.
Nun) The Beit Yosef cites Responsa Rashba, siman 993: Reuven instructed that five hundred dinars from his estate be given to Rachel "and they shall be for her marriage." The money is given immediately and is not held until she marries. The same rule applies if he said, "Give five hundred for her marriage," without adding "and they shall be." This resembles one who says, "Give one hundred to so-and-so for his food," where the heirs are not required to provide food in addition to the hundred. The Beit Yosef also cites the Mordekhai in Mi SheMet: if one says, "Write my property to my sons and daughters," and he is deathly ill or gives instructions because of impending death, and his property includes estates and women's ornaments, the property fit for sons goes to the sons and the ornaments fit for daughters go to the daughters.
