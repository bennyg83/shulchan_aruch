General. The Sema explains this as referring to matters that it is reasonable to assume were intended because without them the transaction expressed in the document could not be completed. The Shach explains it as referring only to matters already included in the underlying transaction, not to an entirely new obligation. As a matter of law, both explanations are correct, Urim VeTumim:
