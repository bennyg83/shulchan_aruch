Everything according to the established custom. So Tosafos write in Bava Batra 42b, s.v. ika.
And one who... Ramban writes so there in the name of some authorities, even though the document does not expressly state it, because once it contains, etc.
(Collected note) This applies to all general clauses... Meaning clauses without which the document's legal effect is not completed, such as writing it publicly and similar matters. This is why the passage concludes, "but conditions..." So explains the Sema, contrary to the Shach. (End of note.)
But conditions. From Bava Batra 44b, "If he transferred to him movables..." and "provided that he wrote..." It follows that even though the document stated that the transaction was not an asmachta, one must still write that the movables are transferred together with land.
A document in which it is written. Bava Metzia 104b, the incident involving a certain man, etc.; and there 48b, "If I retract, I will double..."
(Collected note) Regarding the double amount. This applies specifically where the amount is exaggerated. Ordinary expenses are not an asmachta, as stated in the ninth chapter of Bava Metzia 104b. See the Sema. (End of note.)
Even though it is written therein. Such wording is effective only regarding movables transferred together with land, where the asmachta is not absolute. Otherwise, why would acquisition together with land be necessary? It could be acquired ordinarily merely by writing that it is not an asmachta. See Terumas HaDeshen.
And everything according to the custom. There, 104a; see Tosafos there regarding, "it was..."
(Collected note) And everything according to the established custom... This is only where the borrower knew of the custom, as Rambam writes in chapter 16 of Hilchos Ishus concerning the enactment of the Geonim for collection from movables. See the Shach here, at the end of siman 42, and siman 71, seif 14. Accordingly, there is no need for the strained explanation of Beit Yosef and the Shulchan Aruch there concerning "if the witnesses...," since he is not, etc. See there. (End of note.)
