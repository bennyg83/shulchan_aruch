g) Meaning, the document says that whoever produces it may collect with it, which implies even without authorization. But if it explicitly says "even without authorization," it may be collected everywhere without authorization. This is explicit in the responsum cited by the Tur and Beit Yosef. So too writes the Rashba in Responsa, siman 925.
h) In another responsum in the cited rule. Meaning, we do not say: since this provision is ineffective because a gentile cannot collect through it, the entire provision is ineffective. Rather, a Jew who produces it may collect without authorization.
