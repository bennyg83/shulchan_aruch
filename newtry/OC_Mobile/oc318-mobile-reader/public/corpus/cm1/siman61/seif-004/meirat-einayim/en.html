So that anyone who produces it may collect with it without authorization, etc. The Rosh's responsum, cited by the Tur, implies clearly that if the document expressly says 'without authorization,' collection by any holder is unquestionably effective in all places, because monetary conditions are binding. The responsum's discussion concerns a document that merely says anyone who produces it may collect. In that case, by default the holder may collect without separate authorization unless local custom requires the document to say expressly 'without authorization.' Mahari in Terumat HaDeshen, siman 331, writes the same, and Rama cites him at the beginning of siman 66. Accordingly, the Mechaber's language here should perhaps be understood not as saying the document expressly contains the words 'without authorization,' but that a clause allowing any holder to collect is intended to permit collection without authorization. The Mechaber then concludes that where the local custom is otherwise, the custom controls. Still, because the wording is unqualified, the matter requires study.
