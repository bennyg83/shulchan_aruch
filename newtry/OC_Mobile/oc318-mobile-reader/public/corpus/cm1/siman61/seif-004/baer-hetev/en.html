Custom. The Sema writes that if the document expressly states that collection may be made without formal authorization, it is obvious that the holder may collect in every locale. The Shach cites the Bach's objection: if a particular place has an established custom not to compel payment even when the document expressly says "without authorization," local custom governs. This also appears to be the Mechaber's view. The Taz writes similarly.
Born. The Taz writes: from this I infer that if a person issues a transferable note obligating himself in a matter where he was not otherwise liable, and a person who had not yet been born when the note was written later produces it, that later holder cannot collect. The obligor never originally subjected himself to that person, just as the present rule states regarding a document payable to whoever produces it.
