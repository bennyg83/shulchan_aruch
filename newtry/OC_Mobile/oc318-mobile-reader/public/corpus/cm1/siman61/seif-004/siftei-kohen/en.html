That he may, etc. The Sema writes that this refers to a document stating generally that whoever produces it may collect. If it expressly states that the holder may collect without authorization, however, local custom is plainly irrelevant, and it may be collected everywhere without authorization. The Bach challenges him, maintaining that if a particular place follows a custom not to compel payment even where the document expressly says, 'without authorization,' that local custom is followed. This also appears to be the Mechaber's view. See below, siman 103, seif 7.
