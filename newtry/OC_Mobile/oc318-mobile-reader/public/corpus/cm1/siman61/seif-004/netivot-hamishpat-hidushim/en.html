Custom. The Sema holds that if the document expressly states that collection may be made without authorization, it may be collected everywhere. The Shach writes that if local custom requires formal authorization even where the document expressly says otherwise, then authorization is still required. The Taz rules likewise:
Born. Accordingly, the same applies to negotiable instruments: a person who had not yet been born when the document was written cannot collect it. But one who was already alive as a minor, or was then a non Jew and later converted, may collect it. This applies even if the borrower retracted before the document reached the minor. See Beurim.
