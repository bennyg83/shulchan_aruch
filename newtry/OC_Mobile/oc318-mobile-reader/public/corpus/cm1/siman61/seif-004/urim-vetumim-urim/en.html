If there is no local custom, etc. Where there is no local custom, even if the instrument does not explicitly say that collection may be made without authorization but merely says that whoever produces it may collect, the holder may collect without authorization. If, however, the deed explicitly says that no authorization is needed but the local custom nevertheless requires authorization, Sema says the custom does not override the express clause. Bach disagrees and holds that the custom governs even against the express wording. The language of the Shulchan Aruch supports Bach, and Shach and Knesset HaGedolah agree. This is the correct ruling.
