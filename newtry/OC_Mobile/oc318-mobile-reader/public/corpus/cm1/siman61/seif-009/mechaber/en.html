A document is not invalidated merely because its holder did not produce it during his father's lifetime, or because the creditor reached extreme poverty yet did not produce it. Nevertheless, the judge must examine and investigate to arrive at the truth. If compelling circumstances indicate that the case is fraudulent and false, the judge writes and signs a statement that no Jewish judge should involve himself in the case and gives it to the defendant. {Rama: See above, siman 15, seif 3. Every old document should be carefully investigated by the beit din (Tur, siman 98, in the name of the Rosh, klal 81, end of siman 9, and Maharick, shoresh 191). Even if the document states that the defendant may raise no defense against it and he accepted this under ban and oath, the beit din must investigate so that the true judgment emerges.}
