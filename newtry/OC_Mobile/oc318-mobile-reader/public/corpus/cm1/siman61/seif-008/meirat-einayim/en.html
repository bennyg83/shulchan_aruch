Levi has given me that he has no need for a hundred golds and that he will be given to him, and that he will be given to him, and that he will not be able to do so, and that he will not come to him, and that he will be given to him, and that he will be done to him.
Shimon is believed with an oath. The Tur explains that he has a migo. Shimon could have said to Reuven, 'I repaid you,' and would have been believed with an oath because the debt document is not in Reuven's possession. Therefore he is also believed to say, 'You never lent me anything.' His present explanation does not contradict the earlier words he spoke before the witnesses, but interprets them consistently.
