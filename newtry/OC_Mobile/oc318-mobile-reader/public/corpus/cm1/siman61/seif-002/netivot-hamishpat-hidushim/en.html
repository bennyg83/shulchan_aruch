A memorandum form. Meaning, they wrote a memorandum stating that on a particular day a kinyan was made concerning such and such matters and their details, and when necessary they later write a full document and deliver it to the litigant. If there was a kinyan, the final document is dated from the first date. If not, it is dated from the second.
To be written. Meaning, the witnesses wrote it on their own initiative without the borrower's knowledge.
It is written, but it is not possible to write a wise man and a book that is:
Some disagree. The Shach writes that there is no actual dispute. The Rosh, whose view appears here as the dissent, speaks of one who obligated himself before witnesses to write a fully effective document, then instructed them to write a document recording that very obligation, and they delivered it to him. Therefore that document too has the status of a formal debt instrument.
