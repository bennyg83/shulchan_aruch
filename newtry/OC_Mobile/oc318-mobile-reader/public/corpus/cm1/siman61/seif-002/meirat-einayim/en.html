For it is testimony from their writing. Scripture requires testimony 'by the mouth of two witnesses,' not merely from their writing. But a document legally intended to be written and held by a purchaser or lender was accepted by the Sages for the sake of social order, as explained in siman 28:12. Rama's phrase 'perhaps they erred' means that perhaps the witnesses mistakenly wrote and delivered to him a type of document that was not legally authorized to be written. We do not say that although ordinarily it was not fit to be written, perhaps this borrower changed his mind and instructed them to write it.
