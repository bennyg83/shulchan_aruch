And he did not admit before us, etc. This is also the text in the Rosh's responsum and in manuscript copies of the Tur. According to this version, the word 'clarification' in the document weakens the later phrase 'he admitted before us,' because 'clarification' sounds like a mere accounting statement rather than a complete judicial admission. This is somewhat strained. Printed editions of the Tur read instead, 'but he admitted before us.' In Perishah I explained that version as well.
