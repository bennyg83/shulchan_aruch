And he must swear regarding his expenses. This oath is imposed when the opposing party claims that he spent less, or when the court itself is uncertain whether the claimed expense was necessary and reasonable. It is not enough for him to state the amount without verification. Where the expense is established by witnesses or by clear accounts, no oath is required. The ruling concerns reimbursement of actual and reasonable expenditures, not speculative profit or an inflated contractual penalty. See the related discussion in siman 14 and siman 207.
