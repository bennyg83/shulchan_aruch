In accordance with the enactment of the Geonim, who ordained concerning a borrower that if she has movable property, etc. This implies that even if she wishes to pay with land, she must swear, under the enactment of the Geonim, whether she possesses movable property. This is explicit in the Tur and in Responsa of the Rosh, rule 39, siman 5, contrary to the Sema, seif katan 7. The Bach's words here are likewise unconvincing.
