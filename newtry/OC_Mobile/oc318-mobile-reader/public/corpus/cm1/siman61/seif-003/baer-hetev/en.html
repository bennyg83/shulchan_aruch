Movable property. The Sema writes that this does not mean the debtor wishes to pay with land while the creditor asserts that the debtor has movables from which payment should instead be made. The Geonic enactment of an oath was not made for such a case, but only for one who claims that he has nothing at all with which to pay, as the Tur and Mechaber write in simanim 99 and 101. The Shach disagrees and holds that the Geonim instituted an oath even where the debtor offers land while the creditor claims he possesses movables. He writes that this is explicit in the Tur and a responsum of the Rosh. The passages in siman 101 concern only the law of the Gemara before the Geonic enactment. Shach.
