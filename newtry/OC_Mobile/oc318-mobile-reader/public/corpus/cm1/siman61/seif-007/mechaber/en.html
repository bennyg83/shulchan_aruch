On the first of Nisan, Shimon wrote Reuven a document obligating himself for any sum, up to one thousand gold coins, that Shimon would later acknowledge in a signed handwritten admission as owed to Reuven. Reuven then produced Shimon's handwriting, dated the twenty-seventh of Nisan, acknowledging one hundred gold coins, and the signature was authenticated on the first of Iyar. Reuven collects with priority dating from the first document, because on that date public notice arose that Shimon's document could impose liability up to one thousand gold coins.
