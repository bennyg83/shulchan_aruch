(Shulchan Aruch, seif 4: Whoever presents it.) The Sema asks that if the document expressly says “without authorization,” it is obvious that collection may occur anywhere. The meaning is straightforward. The Mechaber holds that although a bearer clause is legally effective, a local custom not to collect upon it overrides the default rule. The same applies to an express waiver of authorization: if local custom nevertheless requires authorization, the custom governs.
