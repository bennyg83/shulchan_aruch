Because the paper itself belongs to him. The borrower pays the scribe's fee, as stated above at the end of siman 39. The Sema writes that if the lender paid the scribe, the amount is considered a loan in the borrower's hands and the borrower must reimburse him. Nevertheless, the lender may not withhold the loan document on that account, as the Mechaber wrote above in siman 57. It appears from his words that even if the lender intends to write on the document that it has been paid, or to tear it, he may not retain it. In truth, this indeed appears from the words of the Mechaber in siman 57, but I already challenged him there and proved that where the lender will write upon it that it is paid or will tear it, he may retain the paper until the borrower reimburses him for the scribe's fee. See there in seif katan 4 at length.
