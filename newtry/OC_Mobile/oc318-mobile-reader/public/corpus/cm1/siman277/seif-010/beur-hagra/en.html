(Supplement) If the firstborn, etc., as it is stated, etc. Sifrei on Ki Tetzei explains that the verse includes even forbidden unions, including a widow to a Kohen Gadol and a divorcee or chalutzah to an ordinary Kohen, and then includes relationships punishable by karet. A maidservant and a non-Jewish woman are excluded by the words 'and they bear him sons,' meaning children attributed to him. This is the basis of seif 9. Rambam explains that these children are excluded from firstborn inheritance, because they are not genealogically attributed to the father. Even regarding a non-Jew, absent paternal attribution, the child would not be his firstborn for inheritance, and the same applies to a slave. (End of supplement.)
