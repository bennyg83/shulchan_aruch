Who is hated in her marriage. The cited language explains: even though he committed a prohibition by marrying her and should not have married her because of the prohibition. The more compelling explanation is that "hated in her marriage" means a woman with whom kiddushin cannot take effect. This is why the passage concludes, "all the more so a son of a divorcee or chalutzah," meaning a woman with whom kiddushin does take effect.
