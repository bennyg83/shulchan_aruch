Who became mute. See Even HaEzer siman 121, where the manner in which he is tested is explained. See also below, siman 250, seif 6. I found written in the glosses of Maharikash: if he did not become mute, but there is found a document in handwriting recognized by beit din stating, 'So-and-so is my firstborn son,' it requires study whether this is effective, or whether we say that he wrote it in error since no one had questioned him about the matter. Bnei Chayai.
