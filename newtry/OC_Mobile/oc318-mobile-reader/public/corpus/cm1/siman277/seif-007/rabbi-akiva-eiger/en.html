[Shulchan Aruch] The firstborn was. Note: Rabbeinu Yerucham writes concerning this, unlike the Shiltei Gibborim, who holds that he is not considered a firstborn. Sefer Panim Yafos on the Torah, by the author of Sefer Kesubah, Parashas Ki Seitzei, writes that perhaps there is no dispute. The Rambam speaks where the father married a mamzeres, in which case kiddushin takes effect, whereas Shiltei Gibborim speaks of a mamzer whose status resulted from intercourse with a forbidden relative.
