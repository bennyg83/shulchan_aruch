His forehead. See Baer Heitev, who notes that the Tur reads "most of his forehead." See Bach, who deletes the word "most" and writes that a majority does not suffice because there is no recognizable distinction. See Shev Shemaatsa, discourse 7, chapter 15, who agrees with Bach. See also Chatam Sofer, Even HaEzer, volume 2, end of siman 144.
