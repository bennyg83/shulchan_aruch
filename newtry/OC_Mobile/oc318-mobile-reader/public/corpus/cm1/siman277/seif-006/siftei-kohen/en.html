Even though the head of the stillborn child emerged. The Sema, seif katan 13, writes that even if the stillborn child was fully delivered, it appears to him that a son born afterward, whether during the same labor or at a later time, is the firstborn for inheritance. This is contrary to Responsa She'erit Yosef. The Bach rules likewise, and Rashi states this explicitly in Perek HaMekasheh, 68a. See there.
