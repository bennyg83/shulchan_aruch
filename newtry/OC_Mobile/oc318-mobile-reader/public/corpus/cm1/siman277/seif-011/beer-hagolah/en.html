There, law 6, from the statement of Rabbi Yannai in chapter 8 of Bava Batra, 127a.
The Beit Yosef writes in the name of Responsa HaGeonim that one does not rely on a remembered date of birth together with the date of the ketubah, because the ketubah is not a testamentary declaration. It is possible that he erred, or wished to write what he wrote even though it was not factually true. The Sema cites this.
