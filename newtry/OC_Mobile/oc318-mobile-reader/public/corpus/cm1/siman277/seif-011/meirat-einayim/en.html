One whose firstborn status is doubtful. The Beit Yosef cites a responsum of the Geonim. One brother said, "I am the firstborn," and the other denied it. The claimant produced a written note stating that so and so was born on the fourth of Sivan in year fourteen, while the mother's ketubah was dated the second of Kislev in year thirteen. The Geonim ruled that one does not rely upon the written date of birth together with the date of the ketubah. The note is not a formal testament or evidence, and perhaps its writer erred or intentionally wrote an inaccurate date.
