An authorization may be written. Tosafot asks according to Rav Pappi, who holds that a firstborn son has no proprietary right in the double portion before division, how can he grant an authorization to litigate regarding that share? Tosafot answers that he appoints the authorized person as his agent. See what I wrote in siman 123, seif katan 1, in Ramban's name: agency alone is not always enough for litigation. Even though an agent is like the principal, the defendant can say, "You are not my litigant." The root of the special institution of authorization is discussed there.
