His forehead. See Baer Heitev, which notes that the Tur reads "most of his forehead." Bach deletes the word "most" and rules that a majority is insufficient because the infant cannot yet be recognized by appearance. Shev Shema'teta, discourse 7, chapter 15, writes that Bach's view appears correct. See also Chatam Sofer, Even HaEzer II, end of siman 144.
