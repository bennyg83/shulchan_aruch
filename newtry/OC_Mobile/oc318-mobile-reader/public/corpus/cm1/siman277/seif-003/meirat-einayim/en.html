Therefore, when the Gemara says that once most of the forehead emerges the child is regarded as born, it cannot mean that the forehead emerged while the father was already dying and the child was not fully born until after the father's death. In that case the child would not count as firstborn for this law. Rather, either most of the forehead emerged while the father was healthy, the child then moved back, and the father became ill and died before the child was fully born; or the forehead emerged while the father was healthy and then the father died suddenly before the rest of the child emerged.
