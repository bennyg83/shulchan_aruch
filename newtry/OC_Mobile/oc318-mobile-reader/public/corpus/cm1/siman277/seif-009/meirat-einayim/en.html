He had sons while he was a non-Jew. This is a greater novelty: even if those sons were both conceived and born before his conversion, sons conceived and born afterward in Jewish status are not firstborns for inheritance. See the beginning of siman 275, where even a son born after conversion but conceived before conversion does not have the status of an heir; all the more so he does not have the status of firstborn.
He has no firstborn for inheritance. The reason is that sons he had while a non-Jew have genealogical relationship to him, as Scripture says "Baladan son of Baladan." This differs from a Jew who has a child from a maidservant or non-Jewish woman, as explained at the end of the preceding siman.
