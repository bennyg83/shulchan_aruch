Samekh) There, halachah 12, from the beginning of Bekhorot 47a, the dispute between Rabbi Yochanan and Reish Lakish. We rule like Rabbi Yochanan.
Ayin) There. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the plain meaning of the Mishnah in Bekhorot 46a. The Gemara does not establish the case according to Rabbi Yochanan where an Israelite had relations with a maidservant or gentile woman because it preferred not to establish the Mishnah in a case involving prohibition. Anyone who studies the sugya will see this clearly.
