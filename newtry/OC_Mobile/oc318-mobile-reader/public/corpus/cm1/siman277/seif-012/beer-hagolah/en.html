Kuf) There, halachah 14, from Rav Nachman's statement in the fourth chapter of Kiddushin 74a.
Reish) The dispute between Rabbi Yehudah and the Sages in the baraita on Kiddushin 78b and Bava Batra 127b. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is explained there that the halachah follows Rabbi Yehudah, and Rabbi Yehudah speaks even where the person had not previously been established as his son, as is evident there and elsewhere.
Shin) From the question sent by the people of Akra DeAgma to Shmuel, there on 127a, and the Gemara's conclusion on 127b that if he follows Rabbi Yehudah, etc. We rule like Rabbi Yehudah. The baraita there and the report sent by Rabbi Abba are explained in accordance with Rashbam on Bava Batra 128b.
