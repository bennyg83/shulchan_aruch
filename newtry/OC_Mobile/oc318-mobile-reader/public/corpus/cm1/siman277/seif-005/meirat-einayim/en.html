A minor one day old reduces, etc. Meaning that even if he lived only one day and then died, for calculation of the firstborn share he is treated as though still alive. If there are also one ordinary son and one firstborn, the estate is initially divided into four portions. The firstborn receives two, the ordinary son one, and the one portion belonging to the child who died is afterward divided between the surviving firstborn and ordinary son as that child's heirs. We do not say that the estate should initially be divided into only three parts, the firstborn first taking one part as his extra share, and then the remaining two thirds being divided among the firstborn, ordinary son, and the share of the deceased child.
