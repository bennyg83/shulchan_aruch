The midwife immediately. This means where she did not leave and then return. If she left and returned, she is not believed, as stated in the Gemara, Kiddushin 73b, and in the Tur. Korban Netanel there, letter 6, writes likewise. See Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer, siman 4, seif katan 40, and Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 31.
