[Shulchan Aruch] Therefore, a nine month child. Note: Responsa Mahari ben Lev, part 3, siman 44, writes that there is room to doubt whether this means that we know with certainty that the pregnancy reached full term, or whether "a nine month child" simply means an ordinary newborn, excluding an eight month child. End quote. Responsa She’eris Yosef, siman 25, discusses an ordinary newborn who lived for several hours and died, and concludes that the next child is not a firstborn for redemption of the firstborn, because once a nonviable infant has emerged, the later child is not a firstborn for inheritance. In that point the Sema and Bach disagree. Nevertheless, in my opinion, we rule that most newborns are viable. With respect to a woman marrying a kohen, we rely on that majority so that she need not perform chalitzah. Even if the first child were only doubtfully viable, the later child would be only a doubtful firstborn and therefore would not receive a double portion, as stated below in seif 11.
