Seif 9: One who purchases land, etc., and leaves collateral, etc. Note: See my responsum in the laws of the firstborn, where I developed several distinctions. One should distinguish whether the purchaser is an Israelite or a non-Jew; where the purchaser is a non-Jew, acquisition may perhaps be effective even through collateral. I also distinguished between a sale and a gift, and there is room to say that in a gift even collateral may suffice. See there for the reasoning.
