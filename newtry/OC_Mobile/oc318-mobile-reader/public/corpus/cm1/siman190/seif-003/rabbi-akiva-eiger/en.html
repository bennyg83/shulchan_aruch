[Shulchan Aruch] To so and so. Note: This applies even if that person is a gentile. It is not necessary that the seller be acting as the agent of that person, which would raise the rule that there is no agency for a gentile. Rather, through receipt of the money he transfers ownership to the gentile. Likewise with a sudar, in such a case, if Reuven gives a sudar to Shimon so that Shimon should transfer his field to a gentile, see Taz at the end of siman 123, seif 14, who rules that this is effective. Sefer HaMakneh, Kiddushin 80a, questioned this, for it may be that with a kinyan sudar we do not say that one acquires by the law of the Canaanite slave. See there, and see Chavot Daat, siman 169, seif katan 8.
