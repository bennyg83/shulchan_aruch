(Supplement) See what I wrote above, that Rif omitted the statement, 'And likewise concerning monetary law,' because he understood it as stated by an important person. Although Bava Metzia presents it specifically according to Levi, and even regarding a person who is not important, Ran already explained in Kiddushin that the benefit is significant to Levi even when the recipient is not important, although it is worth less than a peruta. Barter does not require the value of a peruta. According to Rav, however, even an important person provides no legally significant benefit when the gift is given on condition that it be returned, because the giver does not regard that as a benefit. (End of supplement.)
(Supplement) Seif 3, some say, etc. Rif omitted Rava's statement entirely. It appears that he did not have the reading, 'And it was necessary,' because that reading has no coherence. Earlier the Gemara similarly stated concerning a sale that he did not acquire, and concerning a woman, etc.; likewise at 5b and regarding divorce, yet it did not say, 'And it was necessary.' There are many similar cases. Rather, the statement, 'It was also said in his name concerning monetary law,' refers to Rava's ruling in Bava Metzia 47a concerning Rav Huna of Diskarta, etc., and relates to Mar Zutra's resolution. This is correct. (End of supplement.)
