Regarding Rambam's omission of the cases mentioned in seifim 4-6, Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh suggest that Rambam's text placed Rava's statement, "and likewise regarding monetary law," immediately after the case, "Give him money and I will become betrothed to you," which works by the law of a guarantor. Rava may not have invoked the law of a Canaanite slave for monetary matters for the reason Ran gives, namely that the analogy to a Canaanite slave is limited to kiddushin.
