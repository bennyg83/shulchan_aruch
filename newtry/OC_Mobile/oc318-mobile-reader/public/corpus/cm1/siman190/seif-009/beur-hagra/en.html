If he said to him, 'Acquire,' etc. Bava Metzia 78a discusses a case where the bundle came into his possession. Tosafot there, s.v. kegon, imply that he takes it as collateral. See Tosafot, Kiddushin, s.v. maneh, who raise difficulties, etc., and discuss the usual practice, etc.
(Supplement) But if, etc. This follows Tosafot and Rosh there, unlike Rabbi Chaim Kohen. See what I wrote in Even HaEzer, siman 29, seif 6, in the gloss. (End of supplement.)
