Some say the same applies where one says to another, 'Take one hundred, and let my field be sold to you by virtue of the benefit I receive from your accepting the money.' Once the recipient acquires the one hundred, he acquires the field, provided he is a distinguished person whose acceptance of the gift gives the seller benefit. Rama: If Reuven owed Shimon one hundred and the debt became due, and Shimon said, 'I will sell you land for one hundred,' Reuven agreed and paid him one hundred, Shimon cannot say that he is applying this money to the old debt. The land belongs to Reuven, and Shimon must afterward collect his debt separately.
