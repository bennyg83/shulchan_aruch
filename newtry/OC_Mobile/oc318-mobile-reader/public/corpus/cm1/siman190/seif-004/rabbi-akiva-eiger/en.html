[Shulchan Aruch] An important person. Note: The Bach writes that here giving a gift on condition that it be returned is ineffective, because the giver does not thereby gain importance. This accords with the Ran, who writes that for this reason a kinyan sudar requires the utensil of the acquirer and not that of the transferor. There is no distinction even when the transferor is an important person, because an ordinary sudar is understood to return to its owner. See there.
