(Rama, seif 4) Provided that the particular person desires this. Note: This requires study, for why not rely on the principle that one may act beneficially for another in his absence? Kiddushin is different because it is not necessarily a benefit, as the Rashba explains in Kiddushin 7, passage beginning "here is a maneh." See siman 195. In a sale obligating the purchaser to pay the price, one can say that perhaps he does not wish to acquire. The source of this law is Responsa Mahari, siman 4, whose rationale is that the seller does not rely upon the transaction. See Tur below, siman 238, concerning writing a document for a seller.
