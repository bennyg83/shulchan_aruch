He gave him money. It is not important to buy it and not to buy it when it is given to it that the rest of it is circumcised in the word or not inclination and the issue of the Aziris and the people who are blessed with it, and that it is not to say, that it is not only a penny, but that it is not to say that I am not here with the money that is given to him
On condition that he return it to him. A gift made on condition of return is legally a gift and effects acquisition in every context except kiddushin, as explained in Even HaEzer siman 29.
