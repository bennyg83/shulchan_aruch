If it is something not fit to be divided. With land, however, even if after division his share would not reach nine kav of a field or half a kav of a garden, which are the minimum measures for a field and garden, he nevertheless acquires corresponding to his money, or else his hand is on the lower side. The reason is that the land is fit to be sold to one who owns an adjoining field, who can combine it with his own field. See the Derishah. There I wrote that Beit Yosef says that according to Rambam, even with a field, if each party would not receive the minimum measure then he acquires nothing, just as with movable property. The incident in the Gemara involved movable property, and the same would apply to land. The Tur, however, writes in Rambam's name that even regarding movable property Rambam does not distinguish whether there is a measure fit for division or not. The Tur himself does not agree with Rambam. The Mechaber here writes in accordance with the Tur and does not state explicitly whether he holds that the same rule applies to land.
For example, if he sold him a living animal. He holds that even a kosher animal is not considered something intended for division. While alive, it serves three purposes: milking, plowing, and slaughter. This is as written at the end of siman 138, and so I wrote in the Derishah; see there.
