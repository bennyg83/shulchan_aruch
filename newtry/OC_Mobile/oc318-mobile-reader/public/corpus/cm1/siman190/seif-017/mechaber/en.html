For this rule, movable property is generally like land: even if the purchaser performed meshichah and brought the goods into his possession, if the seller repeatedly demands the unpaid balance, the purchase is not completed. There is, however, a distinction. If the movable item is indivisible, such as an animal, and the buyer paid only part while the seller presses for the remainder, the entire transaction is void, because the item cannot be divided and the seller does not wish to become a partner with the buyer. Rama: If the seller expressly said, 'Let my earnest money acquire,' the buyer acquires a proportional share corresponding to the money paid. If the item is divisible, the sale is void only to the extent of the unpaid amount.
