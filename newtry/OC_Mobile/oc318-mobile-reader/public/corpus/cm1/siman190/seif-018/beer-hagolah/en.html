Rav Huna establishes it in a case where one hundred was promised and one dinar was missing, yet the sale is effective. It is therefore clear that this is not treated like one who paid only part of the price; rather it is as though he paid all of it and the remaining amount is returned. This is the Mechaber's view.
A similar rule appears in Even HaEzer siman 90 concerning a woman who transfers assets to prevent her husband from acquiring rights in them.
