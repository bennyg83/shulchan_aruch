And he was forced to sell in the hands of the quaint until the taste of Davidi Eile and the remaining Azazis issue to buy them another land that was stored because he did not want to sell all his land:
And he did not trouble himself, etc., this is doubtful. Meaning that it is doubtful whether this is treated like one who sells his field because of its poor quality. Here too it appears that the field must have been undesirable to him, for otherwise he would not have sold all of it merely to avoid a small amount of trouble. See the Perishah.
