[Shulchan Aruch] And none of them. Note: If he sold him ten fields and the buyer gave part of the money for one particular field with the intent that through that payment he acquire all of them, he acquires that field even though he did not pay its full price, but he does not acquire the other fields. See below, siman 192, seif 12. If, however, he gave part of the money without designating it for any particular place, he acquires all of them. Ritva in his novellae. See also Sema, siman 195, seif katan 7.
