Even if he fixed a time for payment, etc. The Tur cites the Rosh: Everything depends upon the judge's assessment. Sometimes a person owes money and sells property before the debt is actually due so that people will not see that he is pressed for cash and thereby depress the value of his assets. If he does not wish to receive the purchase money until the time he must pay his debt, lest he spend the money before then, there is no greater example of a seller pressing for his money. Therefore, whenever the judge sees that he is under financial pressure and is pressing for payment, the sale is void.
