Seif 16: And the rule we stated. The word “likewise” is a scribal error and should read “and all that we stated.” It refers back to seif 10, where the Mechaber says the purchaser did not acquire all of it, implying that he did acquire in proportion to the money paid. The Ran also holds this, as the Sema himself cites. I am therefore surprised that the Sema interprets the Ran here as discussing earnest money acquiring the whole. That is unnecessary, since the Ran holds that even where the seller persistently demanded payment, the purchaser acquires proportionately and may choose his remedy when the seller retracts.
