[Shulchan Aruch] For even if he mortgaged and brought them in. Note: Nimukei Yosef writes in the name of the Rashba that this applies specifically where the buyer performed meshichah, or took possession of the land, in the seller's presence. But if the seller merely told him, "Go, take possession and acquire," or "Perform meshichah and acquire," the sale is acquired, the unpaid money becomes a loan owed by the buyer, and the seller cannot retract.
