A document does not effect acquisition partially. See chapter HaKotev, Ketubot 88a, concerning a woman who reduces her ketubah claim and collects without an oath. The Gemara asks how she can collect with that document, since if her assertion is true the document is merely a shard, and concludes that she says there was an amanah arrangement between them. The sugya implies that the document remains a valid document, for we do not use a migo to extract unless there is a valid document. Yet according to her account only five hundred of the debt written in it is genuine, while the remainder was merely amanah. How then can the document operate partially? See what I wrote in siman 82, seif katan 8. Perhaps that case concerns a document of evidence, which can function even partially. Tosafos, Gittin 20, write that a loan document differs from a conveyance document because it is only evidence, principally to create publicity. If so, one must say that she acquired her ketubah obligation through a separate kinyan and the written instrument merely evidences it. But if her only obligation arose through this ketubah document itself, and it states one thousand although the valid obligation is only one hundred, then the instrument cannot effect acquisition in part. This requires study.
