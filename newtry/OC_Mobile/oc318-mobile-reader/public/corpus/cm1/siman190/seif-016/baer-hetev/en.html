Dakumo. Hashem’s promise to Abraham is that he is given to him, and that day he is given to him, that he will be given to him, and that he will be given to him, and that he will be given to him for the time he has set for him, and he will be given to him
Likewise. The Sema writes that the Rama's word "likewise" requires study, because its connection to the Mechaber's immediately preceding ruling is unclear. See there, where he resolves the matter with difficulty. The Taz writes that it appears to be a scribal error and should read, "And all that we have said..." This refers back to the Mechaber's ruling in seif 10 that the buyer did not acquire the entire property. See there.
