See Responsa Shav Yaakov, part 2, siman 178, who writes that every sale of wine is considered a sale because of the merchandise's deterioration. He brings proof from the chapter Arvei Pesachim, Pesachim 113a, where Rav told his son Ayvu: Sell everything and regret it, except wine, and so forth.
