[Shulchan Aruch] If I wish. Note: Even if he did not say this at the time of the sale, but afterward said, "I wish to acquire by money alone," it may be said that the acquisition takes effect retroactively. At the very least, it certainly takes effect afterward from the time he said this to him, even if the money has already been consumed. This also appears from Responsa Maharachash, part 1, siman 10.
