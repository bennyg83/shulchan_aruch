The language of Rambam in chapter 8 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 7, from the incident before Rav Yosef and Abaye's response, at the end of Avodah Zarah, beginning of 72a.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rav Hai Gaon explained it this way, and so do the Halakhot in the case of a beit kor. We hold that throughout this entire discussion the case is where he said 'from now' and made a kinyan with him. The Rashba writes in the name of Rav Hai that the land remains in the buyer's possession. The Ittur likewise writes so in the name of the early authorities.
This is the very incident cited above, in accordance with Abaye's statement there. The Maggid Mishneh writes so and adds that the Halakhot in the chapter Beit Kor likewise state that whenever no price was fixed, the parties' intent is not settled.
