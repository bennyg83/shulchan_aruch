[Shulchan Aruch] The first purchaser acquires. Note: Property appraised and transferred to a creditor is not considered a sale, and certainly not where it is transferred to a widow. See Rashdam, siman 414, cited by Maharikash. See also below, siman 103, seif 2, and Sema there. If he said, "It is acquired to you at the price at which such property is sold in the city," the parties divide the valuation difference between them. Tosafos, cited in Maggid Mishneh, chapter 9, halachah 2 of Hilchos Sechirus. See Mishneh LaMelech there.
