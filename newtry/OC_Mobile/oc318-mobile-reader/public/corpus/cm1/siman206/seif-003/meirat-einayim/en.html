As a beit din of four shall appraise, etc. Since he specified a number greater than the ordinary composition of beit din, we say that he was precise and intended that all of them agree. The novelty is that even though he used the term "beit din of four," we do not follow a majority. The Maggid Mishneh writes this as well. The Tur concludes, "and it goes without saying if he said, according to what the four shall say." The Mechaber, who follows the Rambam, omits this because according to the Rambam there is no distinction between the language of appraisal and the language of saying, as explained immediately above.
