A beit din of three. Rambam specifically requires that he use the expression "beit din"; if he says that, two of the three are sufficient. According to Rambam there is no distinction between saying language of appraisal and saying, "as they say." The distinction is whether he said "beit din" or merely "three people." The Tur holds that the principal distinction is different. If he uses language of appraisal, then even if he merely says "three," two of the three suffice. If he says, "as they say," all three must agree. Rambam and Tur write these laws regarding, "When I sell, I will sell to you," but the same rules apply to an immediate sale where one uses similar language. Sema.
