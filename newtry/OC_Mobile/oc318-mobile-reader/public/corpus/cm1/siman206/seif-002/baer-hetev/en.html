If one stipulated, "When I wish to move my residence from here, my field is sold to you from now," and then secretly sold it to another, only the first buyer acquires it. Mahari Ta'itzak, manuscript responsum, cited in Maharik, shoresh 20. If the buyer died, his heir is not obligated to sell it to the second person. Maharit Tzahalon, simanim 163 and 164; Mabit, vol. 2, siman 20. Ri Chaviv writes that with the customary rights now in use, he is obligated to return it, whether he or his heirs, and whether to the original party or to his heirs. If the condition was written and a time was stated in it, it is as though he said, "from now." Rashdam, Choshen Mishpat, siman 223. See there further, and Maharibal, vol. 4, siman 26. If he sold it to another and there is an oath involved, whether the second buyer acquires is discussed in Responsa R. Shlomo Yonah, siman 31. Bnei Chayai.
