And his grandson is like an unrelated person. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov I, Even HaEzer, siman 111. He writes that Terumat HaDeshen, siman 350, ruled plainly to the contrary. The fact that the Rama states the rule without qualification against that opinion may be explained because property stands in the presumptive possession of the heirs. Therefore, even if the matter is only a doubt in law, one does not remove the property from the heirs' possession, and the Rama accordingly stated the position of the Rosh as the operative rule.
