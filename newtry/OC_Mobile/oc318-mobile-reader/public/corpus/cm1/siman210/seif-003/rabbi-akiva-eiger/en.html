[Shulchan Aruch] And if it was his son. Note: The same applies where one transfers to his son something that has not yet come into existence. Responsa Mabit, part 2, siman 137, holds that this exception applies specifically to a father. But if a mother transfers a right to her son while he is still a fetus, he does not acquire. From the words of Rashach in Responsa, part 3, siman 34, however, it is clear that he does not agree. See Responsa Pnei Moshe, part 3, siman 30 and part 1, siman 31, and Responsa Devar Moshe, siman 69.
