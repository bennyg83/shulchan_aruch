She was already pregnant. Nimukei Yosef writes that this applies only after forty days, because before then the fetus is considered mere fluid. The Tur and Mechaber state the law without distinction, and the Beit Yosef accordingly says they disagree. The Sema follows this. In my opinion, however, the Ritva and Nimukei Yosef are correct. The Gemara at the end of Almanah LeKohen Gadol states regarding terumah and automatic inheritance that within forty days the fetus is mere fluid, and the Rambam rules likewise at the beginning of chapter 1 of Hilchot Terumot. The same applies here. See the Rosh there. The Tur provides no proof, because before forty days it is not legally called a fetus at all.
