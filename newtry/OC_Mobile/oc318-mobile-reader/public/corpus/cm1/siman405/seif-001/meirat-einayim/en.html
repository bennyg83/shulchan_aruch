Even if it intended to strike an animal and instead injured a person. See Perishah, where I explained that this contains a greater novelty than where an ox intended to gore one animal and gored another. There both are animals of the same general type. A human being, however, has a mazal, as I wrote in siman 1, and it is not the ordinary way of an ox to direct its attack toward a person. One might therefore have thought that this is more nearly an unavoidable accident.
And it is exempt from pain, etc. For the verse says, "if a man injures his fellow," excluding an ox that injures his fellow.
