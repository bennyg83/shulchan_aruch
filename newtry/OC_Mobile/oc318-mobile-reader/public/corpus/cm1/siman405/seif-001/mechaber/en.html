An ox that injures a person, or gores a woman or a maidservant. It contains three seifim:
If an ox injures a person, even if it intended to strike an animal but instead injured a person, its law is like that of an ox that damages another ox: if it is tam, its owner pays half damages, and if it is mu'ad, full damages. The owner is exempt, however, from payment for pain, medical expenses, loss of work, and humiliation, because the Torah imposed these four additional payments only when the one who caused the injury was a person.
