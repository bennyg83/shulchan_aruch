(Collected note) Specifically, etc. Bava Kamma 34a: "he can say to him, the horn," etc. The Rashba asks why this differs from depreciation of a carcass. He answers that a carcass is ordinarily sold immediately, so the injured party should not wait and allow it to deteriorate, whereas injured property is ordinarily left until it heals and appreciates. This refers to a case where it did not die. Hence "some say the same applies," for the same reason. The Ramah explains instead that the reason is that one should have anticipated decay, unlike ordinary depreciation, and the same would apply to a decline in market price. This is the basis of "some disagree." See Sema. (End of note.)
