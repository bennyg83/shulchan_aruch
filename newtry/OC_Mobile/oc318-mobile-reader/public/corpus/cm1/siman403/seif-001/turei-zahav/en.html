(Seif 2: we do not say that the tortfeasor takes, etc.) The Sema distinguishes this rule from the beginning of siman 404. His distinction is difficult. Once the Torah gave the tortfeasor a share in the appreciation of the carcass, from where can one distinguish among kinds of appreciation? It appears to me that we follow the meaning of the verse, "and they shall divide the dead animal." The tortfeasor shares specifically in appreciation of the carcass. In siman 404, however, there is no dead animal. Therefore the tortfeasor has no share in the appreciation of the injured animal. I found Maharshal in Perek HaMeniach, siman 29, making this same distinction: sharing in appreciation applies only where the injured animal died, because the tortfeasor has a partnership interest in the carcass under the verse, "and they shall divide the dead animal." Where it did not die, the tortfeasor has no proprietary right in it.
