Rather, we assess how much they decreased in value. The Tur and Mechaber state the same rule concerning a borrower in siman 344, and the Tur states it regarding damages in siman 387. Theft and robbery differ. There the wrongdoer must return the stolen item as it was when taken, or pay its original value, as explained in simanim 354 and 362. In ordinary damages, Scripture says "he shall return," from which we learn that when payment is made with movable property he may pay with anything capable of being returned, even bran, as explained in siman 419. Therefore the damaged object itself remains with the injured party, and the tortfeasor pays only the diminution in value.
