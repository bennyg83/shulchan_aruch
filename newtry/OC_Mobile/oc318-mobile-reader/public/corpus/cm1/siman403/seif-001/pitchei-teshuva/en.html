The tortfeasor must smear it. See Sema, who writes that he already explained above, seif katan 2, that the injured party must raise the animal from the pit and then recovers the expense of raising it from the tortfeasor. Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, siman 35, writes that Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, siman 31, and Shitah Mekubetzet, Bava Metzia 56, in the name of Ramban, indicate otherwise. In their view, the physical effort itself rests on the tortfeasor. Nevertheless, responsibility for avoiding further loss rests on the injured owner once he learns of the situation. He must promptly demand that the tortfeasor raise the animal. If he demands it and the tortfeasor neglects to act, the tortfeasor is liable for the resulting loss. But if the owner knew and did not demand removal, his silence shows that he did not object to leaving it there, so the subsequent risk is his and the loss falls on him. See there further.
