The damager must make the effort to recover the carcass and deliver it to the injured party. Thus, if the ox fell into a pit and died, the damager must raise the carcass from the pit and give it to the injured party. Beit din then assesses the reduction from the live animal's value to the carcass's value, as Scripture states, 'He shall return money to its owner, and the dead animal shall be his.' This teaches that the damager must return the carcass and pay the reduction in value. If the damaging ox was innocuous, he pays half of that reduction.
