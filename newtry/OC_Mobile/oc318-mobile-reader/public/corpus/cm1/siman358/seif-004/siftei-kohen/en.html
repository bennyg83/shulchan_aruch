It is permitted to purchase from the sharecropper, etc. Tosafot, Hagahot Ashri, and Hagahot Mordechai indicate that this applies only to produce and similar items that the sharecropper is authorized to sell or take as his share. One may not purchase household utensils or other property that ordinarily belongs exclusively to the owner unless circumstances establish permission.
Some say specifically after they divided. See the Sema. In my opinion, the Rama too means that before division it is ordinarily prohibited, while after division each may sell his own portion. His language does not establish a dispute.
