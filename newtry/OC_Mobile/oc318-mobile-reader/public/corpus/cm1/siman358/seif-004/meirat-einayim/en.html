Specifically after they divided, etc. The Tur's language is not this. He says: When is this said? In an ordinary case we assume they already divided, and that he sold from his own share. But if it is known that they did not divide, it is forbidden. Rama's wording appears to imply that without evidence of division it is prohibited, and we do not presume they already divided. That would be a third opinion, and I have not found such a view stated elsewhere.
