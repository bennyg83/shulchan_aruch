The wording of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 6 of Hilkhot Geneivah, from the Mishnah in Bava Kamma chapter 10, 118b.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam appears to rule like Rav, who says it is prohibited until the majority is from one's own property. Some texts read that Rav Yehudah ruled for Adda Dala'ah like the opinion that even a minority from one's own property suffices. Rambam rules like Rav, for the halachah follows him in matters of prohibition. The Raamah likewise writes this in the name of his teachers and the Geonim. There is some difficulty in Rambam's wording, and the matter is explained below in Hilkhot Gezeilah chapter 5. See also siman 369.
