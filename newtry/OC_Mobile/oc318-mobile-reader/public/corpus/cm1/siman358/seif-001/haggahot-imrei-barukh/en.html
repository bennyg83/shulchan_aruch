(Shach, seif katan 1) And if he knew that it was with me, etc. Note: This requires study. According to Rava, ye'ush without knowledge is treated as effective from the moment of loss, before the object reaches the finder. The Shach's essential ruling has a basis according to the Ran cited in Shitah Mekubetzet in the sugya of Eilu Metziot, Bava Metzia 22, passage beginning "Ameimar and Rav Ashi ate," where Ramban raises a difficulty and Rashba answers that this resembles the rule in Tosafot, final chapter of Bava Kamma: a son may eat from his father's property, and every slave eats from his master's property; one may give a piece of food to his child or to his friend's slave without concern for theft from the owner, because that is the accepted practice. For the same reason the Gemara in HaGozel Batra allows charity collectors to accept small donations from women.
