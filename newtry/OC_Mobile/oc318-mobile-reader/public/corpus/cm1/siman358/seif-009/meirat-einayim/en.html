From the thread, enough for the pull of a needle. The Tur writes: If a tailor leaves over thread sufficient to fill the needle and an additional needle length beyond it, it belongs to the householder. Likewise, if after trimming a piece of cloth to straighten it there remains a strip three fingerbreadths wide, it belongs to the householder; less than this belongs to the artisan. The Mechaber copied the Rambam, who used the Yerushalmi's wording. The Maggid Mishneh explains that the measures are equivalent. If enough remains to fill the needle and still extend another needle length, it is fit for a small amount of sewing and the owner is particular about it. See the Raavad's objection to the Rambam.
