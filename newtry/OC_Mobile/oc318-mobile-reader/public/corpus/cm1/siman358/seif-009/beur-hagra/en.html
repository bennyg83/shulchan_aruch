(Supplement) The amount of a needle's pull. This requires study. The Gemara's wording is clearer, and according to the Yerushalmi the measure is twice a needle's pull. Rif follows bar Kappara's later formulation. Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch are difficult to reconcile with either the Bavli or Yerushalmi, while Tur follows the Bavli and Rabbi Chiya. (End of supplement.)
