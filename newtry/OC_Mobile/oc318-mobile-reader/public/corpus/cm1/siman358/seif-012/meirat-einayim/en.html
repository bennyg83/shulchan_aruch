It is like a knife that sells the scanner. This is the case before this:
For it changed in the thief's hand. The stuffing is no longer called loose stuffing but a cushion or pillow. This explains the conclusion that even though the change is reversible, since one can remove the stuffing and restore its original name, here a minimal change is enough because there is only a doubt whether the stuffing in the carder's hand was stolen at all. In such a doubtful situation the Sages permitted purchase after even this slight change. This is from the Maggid Mishneh. See the Derishah.
