"The husband of his wife," he said, "that he should be given to him, and that he should be given to him."
For this is the present practice, etc. The Rama grounds this in the law of the kingdom. Although I proved above in siman 73 that the law of the kingdom does not override Torah law, the ruling is still correct because the wording is that this is now the accepted practice, meaning that Jews too have adopted it. Terumat HaDeshen's wording, that courts presently rule this way, is even clearer. A defective custom contrary to Torah law is not followed, but where the practice was established by agreement and accepted commercial conduct, it is binding.
