Regarding a woman who sold or pledged her husband's movable property, see the Beit Yosef, siman 72, new matter 20; Darkei Moshe, siman 348, seif 3, citing Rabbeinu Yerucham; these words are also brought in Even HaEzer at the end of siman 86 in the gloss, and see the Beit Shmuel there.
Law of the kingdom. Although I proved above, siman 73, seif 14, at length that we do not apply dina d'malchuta where it contradicts Torah law (see also the end of siman 369), nevertheless here the wording is exact: "this is the present custom," meaning Jews too follow this practice. Terumat HaDeshen's wording, "this is how they now judge," is even clearer. Although a bad custom contrary to Torah law is not followed, as stated in siman 72, seif 5, in the gloss and in many places, one may say that this custom was enacted by communal ordinance, and a generation certainly has authority to enact ordinances. Moreover, this is also the law of the kingdom. Shach.
