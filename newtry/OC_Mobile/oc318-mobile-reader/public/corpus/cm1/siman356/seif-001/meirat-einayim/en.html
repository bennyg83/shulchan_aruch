Likewise, it is forbidden to assist, etc. This alludes to Rivash responsum 108. A locksmith secretly made two keys for Reuven's wife without her husband's knowledge and she paid him an unusually high price. Rivash ruled that he deserved punishment for failing to inform the husband. He should have understood that she was paying so much only because she intended to steal from her husband's property, and by making the keys he assisted her theft.
