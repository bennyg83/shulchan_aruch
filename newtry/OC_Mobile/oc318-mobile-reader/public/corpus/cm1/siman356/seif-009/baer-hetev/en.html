Without qualification. The Mechaber wrote this based on his words in Bedek HaBayit, where he comments on the Maggid Mishneh: 'I do not know why this oath is required.' I do not know why he does not know. When the claimant comes to take the object he must swear even against an uncertain claim, as stated in seif 4. I likewise wrote above, siman 72, seif katan 67, in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham and many authorities, that whenever one claims the utensil up to its full value, he must swear even if the claimant's assertion is uncertain. See there. Shach.
