[Shach, subparagraph 6] And Maharshal likewise ruled. Note: See Responsa Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, siman 12, who treats this as an unresolved monetary question. He discusses whether, because in any event the purchaser must return the object, the purchaser is considered the party seeking to extract money with respect to reimbursement, similar to the doubt whether the tree preceded the city, or whether, since movable property is in his possession, he is fully established as possessor and that principle does not apply.
