Beit Yosef cites Rashba that the market enactment applies even to items commonly lent or rented, for otherwise no one would buy such items.

He said, “And thou shalt go out there, and thou shalt not make the market alone, and so on, and so on, that it is necessary for him to take a break in his hands, and I shall see him as if he had not done anything before him.”
He explains there that according to the view that if the purchaser knew the item was not the seller's and nevertheless bought it, he does not even recover his money, as discussed in Bava Metzia 15b. In my humble opinion, this is only according to that view. We rule like Rav, as do the poskim, that the purchaser recovers the money. So too below in siman 373, seif 1.
