[Shulchan Aruch] Rather, he gives them the money. Note: It appears to me that even if the purchaser already consumed the item, since at the moment he purchased it from the thief he acquired it and became obligated for its value, he is not exempt merely because he later consumed it. This is unlike the rule below in siman 361, seif 7, where there was no change of possession and he never became obligated for it.
