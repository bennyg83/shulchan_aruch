[Shulchan Aruch] He took it as collateral. Note: Nimukei Yosef in Perek Eizehu Neshech writes that if one gave another money for an iska arrangement and the latter pledged the stolen object to him, the recipient does not rely exclusively on the collateral, since the managing partner is not permitted simply to spend the investment funds for personal consumption. Therefore the market regulation was not enacted in this case. See there, Hagahot Ashri, and the commentaries there.
