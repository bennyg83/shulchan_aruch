If I return each one's document to him, etc. If all four died, he must provide the claimant with another route, as stated above in siman 119, seif 5. See there and examine carefully.
And if he established possession of the road by the law of chazakah. The Maggid Mishneh explains Rambam this way and understands it as applying to all the cases in this siman: once he has established possession, he is not removed. The Sema rejects an explanation that "established possession" means that he forcibly took hold of the road against the landowner, which would imply that the rule applies only to the immediately preceding case. In truth, that limited reading appears explicitly in the Kesef Mishneh and Bedek HaBayit. In my view, however, the rule applies to the entire siman, yet it still does not require three years of chazakah. Since this person is presently in possession and makes a definite claim, the others are the ones seeking to remove him. As their claims are uncertain, they cannot remove him without clear proof. This appears certain to me. According to the Sema's approach, the ruling would be obvious.
