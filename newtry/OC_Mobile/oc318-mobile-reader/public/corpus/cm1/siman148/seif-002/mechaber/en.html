Similarly, if the four surrounding fields belong to one person who purchased them from four separate owners, the owner of the enclosed field has no right of passage through them. The surrounding owner may say, 'If I now return each deed to its seller, you cannot pass through any of their fields, and I purchased from each seller every right he held.' But if all four surrounding fields belonged to one person who was their boundary owner from beginning to end, the enclosed owner may say, 'In any event, my path crosses your property.' He takes the shortest route through whichever field the surrounding owner chooses. If he established presumptive use of a particular route and says, 'This is my path,' he is not removed from it without clear proof.
