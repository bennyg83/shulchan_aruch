He established chazakah in the path. This refers only to the immediately preceding case, one person purchasing from one seller. There, if he seized the path and asserts certainty, he cannot be displaced to another path. Sema. See Beurim for the rationale. The Shach disagrees and holds that even where there are four adjoining owners, seizure helps when he claims certainty and they claim uncertainty. See Beurim: the primary law follows the Sema.
