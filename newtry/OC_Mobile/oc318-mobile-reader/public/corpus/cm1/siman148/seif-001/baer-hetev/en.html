That they were. Meaning, they were always so, and even now the four fields are held by four people. This includes a case where they originally belonged to one person who sold them to four people. In seif 2 the Mechaber then writes, 'and similarly, if the four fields were,' meaning that at the time of the present objection all four belong to one person who purchased them from four people. Sema. See Responsa Rama, siman 109.
