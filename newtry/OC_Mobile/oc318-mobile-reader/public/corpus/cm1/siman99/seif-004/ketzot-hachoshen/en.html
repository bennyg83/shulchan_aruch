It is known that he has an iska. It appears that if the investor himself cannot identify this property as certainly belonging to his iska, he is not believed merely through a migo. Under the enactment concerning one who absconds, property received within a quarter year must be returned, and therefore there is no useful migo that he could simply have said he received the property as a gift or in some other manner. This resembles Tosafot's rule that a possible alternative plea is not a migo where the other party may refuse to accept the arrangement.
