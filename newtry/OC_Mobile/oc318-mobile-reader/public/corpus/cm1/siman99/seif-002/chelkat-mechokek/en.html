Whom her husband divorced willingly and without a claim. The plain language implies that if he has a claim for divorce, such as that she offended against him, he never intended to give her the clothes on that assumption. But Maggid Mishneh, Hilchot Ishut chapter 16, writes that if she leaves against her will, as in cases where he is compelled to divorce, we appraise the clothing. This implies that if he is not compelled, even if he has a reason to divorce, since he could choose not to divorce, the clothes are not appraised against her. This is also the plain implication of Rif and Rosh. Ordinary divorce is not necessarily without any reason, as explained below in siman 119, because the halachah does not follow Rabbi Akiva but Beit Hillel.
