Reuven required Shimon to take this oath under the enactment of the Geonim, but did not make him swear concerning future earnings. Levi, whose document was later in date, subsequently sued Shimon and made him swear that he would give Levi whatever he earned beyond his expenses. Had Shimon not sworn, Reuven and Levi would divide those earnings. Since he swore to give them to Levi, he must fulfill his oath. This is the ruling the Tur cites in the name of the Rosh. It appears to me, however, that beit din compels Levi to give half to Reuven. See above, siman 104, seif 1, where I recorded the Rosh's view.
