Afterward Levi sued him, etc., unless he swore. The Tur describes a case where Levi made him swear concerning future-acquired property, and afterward Reuven, whose document was earlier, also wished to make him swear regarding future-acquired property, arguing that his earlier debt entitled him to collect first. The Rosh answered that priority does not apply to property that did not exist and will arise only later. Therefore, unless he had already sworn, etc. I explained why the reason is not merely that there is no priority in movable property and how this relates to the rulings in Even HaEzer.
It does not appear so to me, for beit din should compel him. In my responsum I proved that the law follows the Rosh and the Tur.
