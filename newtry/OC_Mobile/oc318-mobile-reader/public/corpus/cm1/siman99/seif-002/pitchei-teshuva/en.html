He need not swear this oath to each and every one. Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes in his glosses to Shulchan Aruch that since this oath is an enactment of the Geonim, we do not scrutinize it in order to impose stringencies, but only to be lenient. This is the view of the Rambam and Tur. See Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 58, and what is cited in his name below in siman 176:24, seif katan 25.
