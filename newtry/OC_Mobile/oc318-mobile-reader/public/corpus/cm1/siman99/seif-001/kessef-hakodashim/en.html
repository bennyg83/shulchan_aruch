Seif 1. (To give to his creditor.) If a debtor stipulated that he would pay a particular creditor and retain for himself only a specified sum, and later claims that he also owes other creditors, the meaning of the condition must be examined carefully. If he does not prove those additional debts, the ordinary understanding is that the condition was intended to prevent him from withholding funds on such a claim. Even if he proves the other debts, however, the wording may indicate that the condition addressed only what the named creditor would receive, rather than eliminating the rights of all other creditors. Everything depends upon the exact language of the stipulation.
