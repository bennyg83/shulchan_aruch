And he runs to take the oath, etc. The Tur, citing the Rambam, concludes this discussion by saying: In general, any such measure that a judge takes with the intention of pursuing justice, he is authorized to take, and he receives reward for doing so. The Maggid Mishneh, however, comments that with respect to judges who are not expert authorities, there is concern that each person may break through accepted limits and make a path for himself according to his own wishes. The Tur and Mechaber already recorded that warning in siman 15:5. Therefore the Mechaber abbreviated the matter here.
