Pursues. The Tur concludes in the name of the Rambam: The general rule is that whatever measures a judge takes in these matters, if his intent is to pursue justice, he is authorized to do so and receives reward for it. The Maggid Mishneh, however, writes that since judges who are not expert have become numerous, one must be concerned lest each person break through proper boundaries and fashion his own path according to his wishes. The Tur and Shulchan Aruch already record this in siman 15, seif 5. Sema. See Responsa Maharashdam 164.
