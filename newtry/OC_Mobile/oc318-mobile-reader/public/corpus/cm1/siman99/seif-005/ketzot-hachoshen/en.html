Two witnesses. One witness does not suffice. See the Sema's reason that this is not a case of one obligated to swear who cannot swear, and the Shach's objection. It appears that the rule of compelled payment does not apply here for a more basic reason. Just as heirs are excluded from 'one who must swear and cannot swear must pay' by the verse, 'the oath of Hashem shall be between the two of them,' and not between heirs, so too other successors in title are excluded. The verse specifically says 'between the two of them.' See what I wrote in siman 153.
