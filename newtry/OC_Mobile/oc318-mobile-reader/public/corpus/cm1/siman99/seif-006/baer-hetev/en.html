And gave. See the Bach regarding whether a gift of all the donor's property, or a gift of part while leaving something for himself, is legally effective. The matter requires study. Shach.
And he shall swear. By strict law, the recipient of the gift should not be obligated to swear, because the claim against him is only uncertain. The Rosh nevertheless imposed a stricter rule upon him because he acted improperly. So it appears to the Shach.
Land. I do not know what connection this has to the other case. There the gift was valid, whereas here there was never a valid gift at all and it is void retroactively. Therefore, if the circumstances are such that the profit or interest would legally have belonged to the donor, the recipient must give it to the creditor, and it may be extracted from him under the rule of Rabbi Natan. The matter requires study. Shach.
