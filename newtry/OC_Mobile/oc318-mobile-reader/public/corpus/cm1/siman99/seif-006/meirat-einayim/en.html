And the prosecutor gets a gift. But the giveer is not faithful in his oath to take away from the recipient’s acceptance of what he does not admit:

However, if it appears, etc. The recipient of the gift is not required to swear that there was no attempt at evasion. Rather, a general ban is pronounced. So writes the Beit Yosef in siman 111 in the name of the Rashba, and Darkei Moshe there. See Yoreh Deah 292:14, where a person may swear under compulsion that an item is his. That ruling too is from a responsum of the Rashba, cited by the Beit Yosef here and in Darkei Moshe.
