(In the Tur, seif 4, that this oath is an enactment of the Geonim) This is difficult. Siman 176, seif 24, states that when one has already sworn to one creditor, that oath is effective for the others. Why, then, is the present reason necessary? The same principle applies even to a Talmudic oath. The answer is that there the person already swore. Had the other creditors first told him, “Do not swear except in our presence,” he would certainly have had to swear before them because the oath is imposed by the Gemara. Here, by contrast, even initially he need not swear in their presence, because the oath is only an enactment of the Geonim.
