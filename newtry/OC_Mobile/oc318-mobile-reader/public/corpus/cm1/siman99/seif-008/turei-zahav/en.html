(In the Tur, seif 19) “We derive it from the standard wording of a ketubah.” One might object that the Tur writes in siman 96, seif 31, that even if the creditor stipulated that the debtor would bind his body, etc. It appears that there the creditor made an improper stipulation contrary to the Sages’ will. Here it is different, because the Sages themselves instituted that such language be written. Therefore the Tur says that it is derived from the wording of the ketubah, meaning that this is the established formula.
