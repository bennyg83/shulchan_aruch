That he admitted before them. In siman 81 the Mechaber follows the Rambam that an admission made in a formal manner is like saying, "You are witnesses." Therefore he writes that false witnesses should not say, "He admitted," because beit din would thereby obligate the person based on a false admission and unlawfully extract money. According to the dissenting authorities, the wording should instead be that they may not say, "He owes such and such person," because that implies they know with certainty that he is liable. Sema.
