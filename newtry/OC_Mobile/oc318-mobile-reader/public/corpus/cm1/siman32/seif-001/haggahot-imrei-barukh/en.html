(Shach, seif katan 3) Gloss, etc.; at first glance there appears to be proof. Note: One may distinguish the case of the serpent. The serpent did not have the status of an agent such that we could say, with respect to Heavenly liability, that a person's agent is like himself. The serpent's enticement was not performed as an agent commissioned to cause Chavah to eat for the sender's benefit. Rather, the serpent acted as an adviser. A distinction of this sort follows from Magen Avraham, Orach Chaim 307, seif katan 29.
