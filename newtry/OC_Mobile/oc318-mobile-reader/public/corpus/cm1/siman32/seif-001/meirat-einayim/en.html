They should not testify in beit din merely that he admitted before them that he owes, etc. The Mechaber altered the Tur's language. The Tur says they should not simply testify, "He owes him one maneh." According to the Tur and Rosh, an admission before witnesses does not become fully effective unless the debtor says, "You are my witnesses," as explained in this siman and siman 81. Therefore, according to them the witnesses may state simply that he admitted owing money, but they may not turn that into the legal conclusion, "He owes him." The Mechaber, however, rules below in siman 81 like Rambam and Ramah that a complete admission before witnesses can be effective even without the words "you are my witnesses." He therefore writes that the witnesses should not simply say, "He admitted before us," because that could imply a legally complete admission. Rather, they should recount the exact words and circumstances, and beit din will determine whether the statement was a full admission or otherwise legally binding.
