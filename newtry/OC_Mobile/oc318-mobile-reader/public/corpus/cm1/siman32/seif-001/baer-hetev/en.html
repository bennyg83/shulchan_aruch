Without qualification. The Sema writes that the word "without qualification" does not appear in the Tur. The Mechaber added it consistently with his ruling in siman 81 that where a person makes a complete admission before witnesses, he need not expressly tell them, "You are my witnesses." Therefore the witnesses should not testify merely, "He admitted before us," without explaining the circumstances, because that wording implies a complete legal admission. Rather, they should recount exactly what occurred, and beit din will determine whether the words create liability.
