That he admitted before them, etc. The Mechaber below in siman 81 follows Rambam that an admission stated in formal terms is equivalent to saying, "You are witnesses." He therefore writes that witnesses should not testify simply, "He admitted," because such testimony would cause beit din to impose liability on the person who supposedly admitted, and if the statement is false they would remove money unjustly. According to the authorities who disagree with Rambam, the proper warning is that the witnesses should not say, "He owes so and so," because that formulation means that they know with certainty that he is liable.
