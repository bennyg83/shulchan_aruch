Witnesses to an Admission Must State Exactly What Occurred, and the Claim, 'I Was Jesting with You.' Two seifim. If Reuven said before witnesses that he owes Shimon one hundred zuz, they should not testify in beit din merely that he admitted owing Shimon one hundred. They must recount exactly what occurred, because he may be exempt through the claim, 'I was jesting with him,' or, 'I intended only that people should not think me wealthy,' as will be explained in siman 81. The beit din then determines whether the circumstances support either defense.
