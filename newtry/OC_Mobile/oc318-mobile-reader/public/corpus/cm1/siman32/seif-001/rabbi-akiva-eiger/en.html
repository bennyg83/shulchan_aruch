[Rama] He is exempt under the laws of Heaven. Note: The same is true if he hired false witnesses for his own benefit; this is explicit in Shitah Mekubetzes. If he hired them for himself and admits that the witnesses lied, but claims that the debt itself is nevertheless genuine, whether he is believed by virtue of a migo requires examination. See Penei Yehoshua on the Tosafos passage beginning "Mamona," and Shach below, siman 77, subparagraph 15.
