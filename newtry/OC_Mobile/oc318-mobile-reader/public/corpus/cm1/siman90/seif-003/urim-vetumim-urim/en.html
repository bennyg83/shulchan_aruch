Since the witness does not know, etc. If the witness knows what was taken, the taker must return all of it. So long as he does not contradict the witness, one witness is treated like two. Just as two witnesses who saw him take the objects require their return, so too one witness requires their return without an oath.
He therefore swears. Meaning, he swears concerning the remaining objects. One item, namely the least valuable item encompassed by the testimony, he certainly must return in all circumstances, as I wrote in Tumim, although Perishah does not appear to understand it this way. See there.
He swears. Tur holds that he takes a hesset oath, whereas Rambam requires an oath while holding a sacred object. See Tumim for my attempted reconciliation of Tur's words. I concluded that those answers are strained and Rambam's view is correct.
