The wording of the Rambam is that he swears while holding a sacred object. So writes the Semag, and so rules the Bach. We do not say here, "since he cannot swear, he must pay," as in the incident of the silver ingot of Rabbi Abba, which the Mechaber records in siman 75, seif 13. The distinction is that there, if two witnesses had been present, the defendant would have had to pay without any oath by the claimant. Here, by contrast, the defendant is liable only if the homeowner swears, as the Tur writes in siman 72, seif 17, in the name of the Ramah.
