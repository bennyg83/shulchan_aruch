[Shulchan Aruch] So too, the homeowner’s custodian. Note: Even where the custodian is not liable to pay for the utensils and is therefore not an interested witness, he must nevertheless take an oath. See Responsa Chut HaShani, siman 7. If the homeowner’s hired worker or household retainer is not his relative, it appears that since the witnesses know that the defendant took some of the utensils, rendering him suspected according to Rabbeinu Yeshayah, then when the homeowner claims on the basis of his worker or retainer that the defendant took more, this is a claim based on one witness. The defendant must therefore swear to contradict that witness and deny taking more. Since he is suspected and thus one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear, he must pay. Accordingly, under the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch, siman 32, seif 8, where a custodian is suspected and the opposing party cannot swear because his claim is not definite, the custodian is exempt, the same should apply where the claim is based only on a witness.
