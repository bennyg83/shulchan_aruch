[Shulchan Aruch] Or that he deposited them with him. Note: The injured party did not claim that the utensils were his own. According to Sema, seif katan 6, here he is believed by a migo that he could have said, “They were deposited with me,” because the tortfeasor makes only an uncertain claim, and where one party has a definite claim and the other an uncertain claim we do apply a migo even to extract money.
