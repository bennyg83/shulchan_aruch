The language of the Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilkhot To'en VeNitan, law 4, from Rav Yehudah's statement, Shevuot 46a and 46b. His text follows that of the Geonim and is primary. Tosafot likewise write at the beginning of 46b that Rabbenu Tam preferred Rabbenu Chananel's text and the Sha'arim of Rav Hai. The Tur writes similarly in seif 20 in their names, and says the Rosh agreed.
Tosafot there in the name of Rabbenu Tam, from Reish Lakish's statement, 'Livestock have no chazakah,' Bava Batra 36a. Even where this person says, 'They were stolen,' etc., if the possessor says, 'I purchased them,' there is no question that he is believed. This is also the Raavad's view in his gloss at the end of chapter 5 of Hilkhot To'en VeNitan.
