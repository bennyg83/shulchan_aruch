The rule concerning items ordinarily lent or rented, etc. The same applies to items that are not ordinarily lent or rented, provided that the other defects in his claim are present.
Through a migo that I returned it. Even if the owner claims that the articles were stolen from him, and the possessor has been established as a thief, he is nevertheless believed to say that he returned them. See Tumim. This does not prove that one who robbed in the presence of witnesses must return in the presence of witnesses, for this ruling is accepted according to all opinions. As a matter of practical law, however, the later authorities concluded that the halachah follows Ramban and Ramah, that a robber need not return in the presence of witnesses. See Tumim for what I wrote there.
