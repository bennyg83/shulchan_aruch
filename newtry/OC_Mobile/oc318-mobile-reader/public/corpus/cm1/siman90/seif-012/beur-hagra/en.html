The rule concerning articles, etc. An artisan holding an article upon which he works is treated like one holding an article that would ordinarily have been in the owner's possession. Nevertheless, witnesses and prior observation are required, as stated in Bava Batra 45a and 46a. See Tosafot there, beginning, 'If there are,' and their conclusion. The case also involves witnesses, as stated in the Tosefta. See siman 134.
