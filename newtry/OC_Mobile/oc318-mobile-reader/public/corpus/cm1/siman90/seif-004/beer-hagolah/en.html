A statement of Rabbah there, 46b.
A question of Rav Tavi there that remains unresolved. It is therefore treated stringently for the claimant and leniently for the defendant. The Rambam likewise writes this there, law 7. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is where the claimant did not seize. If he seized, it is not removed from him, because the entire law is rabbinic and where the Sages did not explicitly enact removal after seizure, we do not remove it.
