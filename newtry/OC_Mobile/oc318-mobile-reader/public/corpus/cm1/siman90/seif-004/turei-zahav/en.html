(Seif 3.) "One witness testified against him." According to the Sema's explanation in seif katan 13, since with two witnesses he would have been obligated to swear, one must say that the parties dispute how much he took and the witness does not know the amount. If there were two witnesses in that situation, the employer would need to swear regarding the amount. But if they do not dispute the amount, there would be no oath with two witnesses at all, only payment. This is the case discussed in siman 364, seif 3. Therefore, with one witness there, he is one who is obligated to swear, etc.
