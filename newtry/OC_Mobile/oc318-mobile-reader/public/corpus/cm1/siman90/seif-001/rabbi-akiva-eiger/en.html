[Shach, subparagraph 1] For the Sages likewise treated this person as one disqualified from taking an oath. Note: In my humble opinion, this cannot be said. It is only with respect to the robbed person’s claim that the Sages treated the robber as disqualified. Yet below, where the claim concerns an amount that the defendant is not presumed to possess, according to Rabbeinu Yeshayah he is exempt even from hesset. Since that is not a benefit granted to the robbed person, it implies that by strict law he is considered disqualified.
