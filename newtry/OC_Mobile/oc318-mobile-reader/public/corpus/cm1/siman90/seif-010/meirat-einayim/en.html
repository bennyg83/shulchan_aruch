And the depositor was negligent regarding it.
The Tur cites the Yerushalmi about a person who deposited a tied sack with another and an accident occurred. The depositor claimed it contained silver filings, etc.
In the Perishah I explained that 'an accident occurred' does not mean that the custodian was blameless from beginning to end, for a custodian is exempt from true accidents.
Rather, he was negligent initially, and because of that negligence an unavoidable event later occurred. Where the beginning was negligence and the end an accident that came about through that negligence, he is liable, as explained by the Tur and Mechaber.
The depositor's own negligence may nevertheless affect the amount or nature of what can be recovered, depending on what he can establish was inside the package.
