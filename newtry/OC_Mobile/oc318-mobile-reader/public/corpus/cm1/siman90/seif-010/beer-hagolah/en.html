This refers to the beginning of the seif, where the bailee says he does not know, perhaps the contents were dross, etc. The Raavad likewise challenges the Rambam there from the Gemara's statement that where the injured party makes a definite claim and the damager is uncertain, it is still the rule of Rabbah bar Natan, etc., there 35b. The Maggid Mishneh discusses this at length and concludes that the primary view is that of the Raavad, and that Baal HaIttur likewise wrote so.
