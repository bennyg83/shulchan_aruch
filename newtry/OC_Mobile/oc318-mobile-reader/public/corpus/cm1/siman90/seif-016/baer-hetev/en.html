Clear. The Tur concludes in the name of Ramah that the rule allowing him to take it with an oath applies when two parties are actively disputing over it. If they are not disputing, he does not swear and take unless the matter is clear. This means that when the matter is clear he takes without an oath. Sema. The Shach agrees, unlike the Bach, who has a different textual version. See Responsa Rashach, siman 13.
