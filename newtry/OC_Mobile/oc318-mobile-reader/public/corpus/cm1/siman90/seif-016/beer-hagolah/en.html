Tur in the name of the Ramah. The Beit Yosef writes that the Ramah's words are clear in their reasoning. Piskei Mahari, siman 28, discusses one who injured his fellow where there were no witnesses, but he admitted the injury himself and claimed that the injured party started the fight.
