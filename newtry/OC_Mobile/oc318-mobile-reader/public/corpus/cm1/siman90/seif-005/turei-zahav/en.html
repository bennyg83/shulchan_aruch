(Seif 8) In siman 384, seif 7, it is ruled that if an informer does not know how much loss he caused, the victim swears and collects. This requires study. The wording implies that the informer admits that the other party suffered loss, but does not know how much, just as the language here says that he does not know how much loss he caused. If so, this is a case of “I know fifty and do not know fifty,” and the victim should collect without an oath, as in the law of a bailee.
