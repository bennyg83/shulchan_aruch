Meaning, we do not say that because he admits part of the claim he should swear, and since he is a robber he cannot swear. The reason is that the homeowner does not make a definite claim; he merely says that a certain amount is missing from his house and that presumably this person stole it. One is not obligated to swear on an uncertain claim. Beit Yosef.
