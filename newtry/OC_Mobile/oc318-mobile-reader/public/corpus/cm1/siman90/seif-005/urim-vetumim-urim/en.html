We do not administer an oath to the robber, etc. See Tumim. It appears that this refers back to the preceding case where the homeowner's employee or hired hand was present, so that the homeowner has a definite claim. Otherwise, no oath is administered on an uncertain claim in any event, particularly according to the Mechaber, who holds that an open box does not suffice to generate an oath. See Tumim, where I offered another explanation, but this one is more correct.
For the homeowner does not make a definite claim against him. Even if he has a witness, such as his employee, a supporting witness cannot cause money to be collected from one who is suspect concerning an oath. See below in siman 92 and what I wrote above in siman 87.
