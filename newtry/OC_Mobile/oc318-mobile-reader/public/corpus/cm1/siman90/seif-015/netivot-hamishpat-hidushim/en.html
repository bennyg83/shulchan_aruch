If he snatched it. Since he is believed to say, "I took it in payment of my debt," only through a migo that he could have denied taking it, and that denial would have created a Torah oath, he is one obligated to swear but unable to swear. Accordingly, if he snatched a debt instrument, where there is no Torah oath on its body, he is not in that category and is believed. This seif concerns a case where there are witnesses who saw the item in his possession. Otherwise he would be believed through a migo that he could have said, "I returned it."
