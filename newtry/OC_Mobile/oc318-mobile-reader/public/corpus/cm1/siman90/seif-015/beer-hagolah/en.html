From Rabbi Abahu's statement: we cannot tell him, "Go pay," because there are not two witnesses.
From Rabbi Abahu's statement: we tell him, "Go swear," etc.
This is precisely the incident of the silver ingot. Baal HaTerumot writes that the law of the silver ingot applies specifically where witnesses saw the item in his possession so he cannot claim, "I returned it." If he could have claimed that he returned it, he would be believed through a migo. The Raavad and Ramah answered likewise. So writes the Rivash in siman 392, and he states that this is the view of the Ramban and Raavad.
The Beit Yosef likewise explains the Tur this way. This applies even though the claimant's claim is uncertain and is based upon the witness, according to the Rif, Rosh, Ran, and Rambam, as stated in siman 75.
