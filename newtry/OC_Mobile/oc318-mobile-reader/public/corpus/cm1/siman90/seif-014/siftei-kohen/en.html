He is not believed to say, “I purchased them,” etc. Even if he says that he bought them from the owner earlier, and only afterward entered in the owner’s absence and took them, he is not believed. In every case where he entered in the owner’s absence, his claim is rejected. This is explicit in the Rif in Kol HaNishba’in from the proof he brings concerning the person who said, “I cut down the date palm of so-and-so,” and it likewise follows from the Rambam in chapter 4 of Hilchot Gezelah. The Mechaber cites the Rambam’s ruling below in siman 364.
