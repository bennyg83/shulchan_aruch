Purchased. Even if he says, "I purchased them from him previously, and afterward I entered in his absence and took them," he is not believed under any circumstances. This is proven from the Rif in the chapter Kol HaNishba'in and is also implied below in siman 364. Shach.
Saw. The Sema writes that it is not necessary that the witnesses actually saw him at the moment he removed the utensils from the house. Rather, since the homeowner was absent, witnesses saw him enter empty handed and leave carrying utensils, and he admits that these are the utensils he removed, it is as certain as witness testimony that he stole them, and they are taken from him without an oath. See Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 91.
