388. In seif 7 there it is ruled that if the informer does not know how much loss he caused, the person informed upon swears and collects. This requires study. The wording implies that the informer admits that the other suffered a loss but does not know how much. If so, why is this different from one who says, "I know fifty and do not know the rest," where the claimant should collect without an oath? So writes the Taz. See the Shach in siman 388.
