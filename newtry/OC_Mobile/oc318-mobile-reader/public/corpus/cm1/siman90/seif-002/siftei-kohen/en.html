He is exempt, etc. Rabbeinu Yerucham and Semag rule this way, and it is correct. A matter a person was not required to mention is not ordinarily retained in mind. Therefore, even if his later statement contradicts that incidental detail, it is not a legal contradiction so long as he was not contradicted concerning the monetary liability itself. This resembles one who says, “I lent you a maneh beside a certain pillar,” and the defendant answers, “I never passed that pillar,” as above, siman 79, seif 11. The Bach agrees. See Responsa Maharshadam, siman 174, and Responsa Rashach, part 2, siman 29.
