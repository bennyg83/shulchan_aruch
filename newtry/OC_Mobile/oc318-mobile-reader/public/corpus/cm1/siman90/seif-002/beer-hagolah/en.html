The Beit Yosef writes that the reason is a migo: he could have said, "I entered but took nothing."
The Beit Yosef writes that according to the Rambam this is not considered a contradiction, because a person does not necessarily remember a matter that was not incumbent upon him to note precisely. Therefore the migo is not considered to stand against witnesses.
