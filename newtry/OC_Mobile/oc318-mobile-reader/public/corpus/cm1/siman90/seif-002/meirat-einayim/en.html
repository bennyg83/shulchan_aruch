And nothing was visible beneath his garments. This is also the Rambam's wording in chapter 4 of Hilchot Gezeilah. I wrote above that this suggests that he follows Rashi's approach. If he follows Rabbeinu Tam and the Rosh, the word "anything" would be a textual error and should read "utensils." The same emendation should be made in the Rosh at the beginning of this discussion, as I wrote in the Perishah. The Tur, when citing the Rambam in seif 6, writes that the witnesses did not see him carry out utensils. It is possible that the Tur deliberately altered the Rambam's wording according to his own view that the law follows Rabbeinu Tam and the Rosh, in order to teach the result under their approach where the witnesses did not see actual utensils and the entrant contradicts their testimony. Examine this carefully.
