Note: Maharikash writes as follows: All this has no bearing on one who purchases merchandise suitable for a partnership and charges it to himself as a loan, where he has authority to do so. There the deed certainly belongs to the partnership. If he has authority to purchase as he sees fit, the loss too is shared, and he obligates his partner according to all opinions, even if the partner was not present. The authorities disagree only regarding a simple loan. Mahari Ben Lev mentioned merchandise in his responsum, but that is not found in the words of the earlier authorities. End quote.
