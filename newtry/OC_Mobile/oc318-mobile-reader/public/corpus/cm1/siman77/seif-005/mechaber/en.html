Reuven sued two people who had borrowed from him together. One denied the debt and the other admitted it. The admission of one does not obligate the other to take an oath. If there were three defendants and two admitted the debt as to themselves and as to the third, the third is not obligated to pay money on their testimony. But if the claimant produced a document against three people stating that they owe him a specified sum, and all three claimed, 'We repaid him in one another's presence,' then, if a separate definite amount was written against each of them and they are not partners, they may testify for one another, and we do not suspect reciprocal testimony. See above, siman 37, se'ifim 4, 5, and 6.
