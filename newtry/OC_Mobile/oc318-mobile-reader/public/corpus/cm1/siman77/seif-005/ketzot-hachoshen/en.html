If the creditor himself no longer remembers the debt, and one of the two comes and says, "My fellow and I owe your father a maneh," that person may be able to testify against the fellow. He is not necessarily financially interested, because the creditor is not asserting any claim against him. This resembles the law where tenants are not interested witnesses when the challenger did not know they occupied the property and they could have denied ever living there.
