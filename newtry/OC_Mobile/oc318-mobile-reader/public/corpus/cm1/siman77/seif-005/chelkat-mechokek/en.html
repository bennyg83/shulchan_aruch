If the husband wishes to divorce her. This is unlike Rashbam and Rambam, who hold that when a woman claims, "he is repulsive to me," the husband is compelled to divorce. See Maggid Mishneh, Hilchot Ishut 14:8, who writes that the practice is not according to Rambam and that even if she remarried on the basis of a coerced get of that kind she must leave the second husband.
