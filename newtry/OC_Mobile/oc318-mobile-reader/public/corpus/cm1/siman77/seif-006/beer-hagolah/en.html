The Beit Yosef according to the Rambam, Ittur, Avi HaEzri, and other poskim, who hold that each one becomes liable for the other's half only under the law of guaranty. Nevertheless, one can learn from Responsa Rosh cited by the Tur that where both are kablan guarantors for one another, the creditor may collect the entire amount from either one. If he waived one of them, he waived the whole claim and both are exempt according to all opinions, as the Rama writes.
