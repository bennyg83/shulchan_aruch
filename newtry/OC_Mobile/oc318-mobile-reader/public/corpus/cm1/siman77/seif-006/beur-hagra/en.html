And some disagree. Rosh at the end of Bava Kamma and Mordechai explain that where one partner asked the other to compromise, the latter acts as his agent, while where they compromised on their own there is no agency. Here, because the entire debt was released, even without solicitation we infer that he released it for all. See Responsa Rosh, rule 73, and Sema and Shach. Ketubot 93a appears to support Rosh, although that passage does not remain in the conclusion because there the waiver was not complete.
