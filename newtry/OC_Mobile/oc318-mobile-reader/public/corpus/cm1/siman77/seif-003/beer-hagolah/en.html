The language of the Rambam, chapter 25 of Hilkhot Malveh, law 10. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this law is supported by the rule in chapter Beit Kor, Bava Batra 107a, concerning three brothers who divided an estate and a creditor took one brother's share, etc. So too the Tur in the name of the Rosh.
They hold that this applies specifically where collection cannot be made from the second obligor. These are the Baal HaIttur and Avi HaEzri. Baal HaTerumot, gate 44, writes that the Ramban answered him that the Rambam's words appear so. So too Mordechai in a responsum of Maharam, the Maggid Mishneh there in the name of the Rashba, Nimukei Yosef in chapter HaMafkid in the name of the Rashba, in chapter HaSho'el in the name of the Raavad, Maharik in the name of Sefer Agudah, and the Beit Yosef and his colleagues. The Bach agrees.
