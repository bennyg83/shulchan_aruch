Reuven owed Shimon one hundred. Reuven and his wife subsequently borrowed one hundred from Levi. Reuven died, and after his wife's earlier ketubah was collected, nothing remained for the creditors. Levi then collected the one hundred from the widow. Shimon cannot proceed against Levi and collect from him, because Levi collected nothing from Reuven's property, but only from the property of Reuven's wife. See notes 259 and 707.
