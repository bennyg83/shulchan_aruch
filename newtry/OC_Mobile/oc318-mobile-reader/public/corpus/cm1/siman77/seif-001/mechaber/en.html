The law of two people who borrowed from one person, and the law of partners when one borrowed alone. It contains 11 se'ifim.
If two people borrowed together, purchased one item together, or jointly accepted a deposit, whether by a written document or orally, each is a guarantor for the other even though they did not expressly stipulate this. If one has no property, the lender may collect the entire amount from the other. If the first has property, however, the lender may not initially demand payment from the guarantor, because he has the law of a guarantor in every respect. He collects from each one the half for which he is primarily responsible, as stated in Responsa of the Rosh, rule 73, responsum 10. {Rama: This is unless they expressly stipulated that each is an unconditional guarantor for the other, in which case the lender may demand payment from whichever one he chooses, as stated in Responsa of the Rosh, rule 73, responsum 12. See also source 40. If one of them paid the entire debt, he may recover the other's share from him. Therefore, when two people borrowed and one of them paid, we presume that he paid on behalf of the other as well, as stated in Maharik, responsum 120.}
