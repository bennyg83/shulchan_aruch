(See Shach, seif katan 1.) In Shach, Choshen Mishpat, at the beginning of siman 77 there is a scribal error. It should read either "one who borrowed from two" or "one who lent to two." What is written there, that a partner who was negligent, does not follow the exact language of the Gemara. The meaning of the Gemara is that he was negligent in leaving the guarding to his fellow, as Rashi explains there. The Shach's phrase "who was negligent" refers to the partner into whose hand the item had been left to guard and who failed to guard it, for the animal then gored. It appears that the Sages used the case of entrusting it to five rather than to two so that the case can be illustrated even with a forewarned ox, including according to the view that guarding by a person is required repeatedly, with payment afterward, where the animal would be in one person's hand at each stage. This requires careful study.
