Two who became guarantors. Ketzot HaChoshen seeks to prove from the law of two purchasers that if one guarantor paid, he cannot collect reimbursement from the other. This is incorrect, for seif 4 expressly states the opposite, that reimbursement may be collected. Indeed, the very source from which Ketzot sought his proof instead supports this ruling. The matter will be explained in siman 132.
