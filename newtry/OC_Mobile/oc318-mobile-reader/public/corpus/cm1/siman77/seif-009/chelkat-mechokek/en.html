And if she did not retract, the court consults her again and she loses her ketubah. The Ran explains that it is not enough for beit din to warn her through an agent. The judges themselves must again consult her before issuing the final ruling that forfeits her ketubah. This implies that if she retracts at that point she loses nothing. The Ran asks whether she could manipulate the rule by rebelling for three weeks and retracting in the fourth, repeatedly. He answers that perhaps once four weeks of public announcements have accumulated across the episodes, she loses the ketubah. Others say that during the four weeks the ketubah is reduced progressively by seven dinar each week. On that view the system cannot be manipulated, yet the entire ketubah is not forfeited at once unless there were four consecutive weeks.
