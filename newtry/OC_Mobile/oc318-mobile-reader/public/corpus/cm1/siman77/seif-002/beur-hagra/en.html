Partners, one of whom borrowed... As above, because he acts as his partner's agent, as learned from the law concerning a field not ordinarily planted, etc.
And specifically. He is not believed so as to impose liability upon his partner, as stated in Bava Basra there: they are interested parties with respect to one another's testimony.
And some say... Since his partner can deny his assertion, the lender did not subject himself to relying on that partner's statement, as Rabbi Yosef ibn Lev writes. His reasoning requires study. See the Shach.
