Even if both of them are named in the document. See Baer Heitev, who cites the Shach as saying that this rule is not compelled. Tumim rejects the Shach and concludes that the principal ruling follows Rama: even the partner in whose name the document is written cannot waive the other partner’s share. This is true not only where the parties have no independent partnership and their joint interest exists only because of this document, but even where they are full partners in an ongoing business. Netivot HaMishpat agrees. Beit Meir, Even HaEzer at the end of siman 86, likewise disagrees with the Shach.
