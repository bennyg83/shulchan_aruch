And he must pay half, etc. The Sema writes that the same applies where three guarantors guaranteed a borrower, the borrower became insolvent, and the creditor collected the entire debt from one guarantor. If another guarantor also became insolvent or lives far away, the third guarantor must reimburse one half of the amount paid. But if all three are present and able to pay, though collection from one is more difficult, the paying guarantor cannot recover one half solely from the easier guarantor. He must pursue the third for the one-third share attributable to him, unless they expressly became guarantors for payment for one another.
