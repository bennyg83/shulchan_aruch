Some say. The phrase is used because there is an opinion that even without any waiver the creditor can recover only half the debt from the other guarantor, as stated in siman 132. Still, all agree that at least half may be collected from the other guarantor. This differs from two co-borrowers who are kablan guarantors, discussed in seif 6, because a guarantor is secondary to the principal borrower. Even after one guarantor is released, the principal debt remains on the borrower. Accordingly, if the released guarantor has no assets, the creditor may collect the entire amount from the second guarantor, a point on which all agree, as stated in siman 132, seif 3. Shach.
