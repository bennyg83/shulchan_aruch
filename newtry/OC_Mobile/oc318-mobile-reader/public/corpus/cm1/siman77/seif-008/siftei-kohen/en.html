Some say that he can, etc. The Mechaber uses the phrase some say because there is an opinion that even without a waiver a creditor can collect only half the debt from the other guarantor, as explained below in siman 132. Nevertheless, everyone agrees that he may collect at least half from the other guarantor even after waiving one guarantor. This differs from two co borrowers who are each full guarantors, because with an ordinary guarantor the principal debt remains upon the borrower even after the guarantor is waived. Accordingly, if the waived guarantor has no means to pay, the creditor may collect the entire debt from the second guarantor according to all opinions, as explained in siman 132, seif 3.
