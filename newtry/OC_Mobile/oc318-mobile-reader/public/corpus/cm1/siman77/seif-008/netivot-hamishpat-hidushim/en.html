Because he can collect. This differs from two borrowers who are mutually guarantors, where waiver to one of them can waive the debt against both when they are kablan guarantors. There the body of the debt itself is extinguished. Here, where the lender waives only one guarantor, the principal debt against the borrower remains, and therefore the other guarantor remains liable. One should not ask why the lender needed to waive one guarantor at all if he could collect from the other. The rationale of extending a waiver to the partnership applies only where there is already a separate reason to say the waiver was intended for both, as with two borrowers. Shach.
