Land, etc. Sukkah 30b and 31a. This follows the Sages who dispute Rav Huna, and many sugyos follow them, as explained below. Even, etc.; and they despaired, etc. Bava Batra 44a; see Rashbam there, unlike Tosafos. Rashi and Tosafos in Sukkah and other poskim support this approach. Without, etc.; and anyone who, etc. The first chapter of Bava Metzia, as explained in siman 373 and the beginning of chapter 6 of Gittin, and elsewhere. And the seller returns, etc. Bava Batra there, following Ravin's explanation. Ramban explains that the claimant says he purchased the land from the robber, as stated above in siman 37, seif 16. The later passage concerning whose warranty applies does not answer by saying that he bought from a robber, which supports this reading.
