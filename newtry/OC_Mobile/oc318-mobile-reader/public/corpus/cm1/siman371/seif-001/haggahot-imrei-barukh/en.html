(Shach, seif katan 5) Or he lived in the rented house and took the rent. Note: In such a case even regarding movable property he must return it to the victim, as explained above in siman 363:5 in the name of the Ramah. The Rama's words are cited at length in Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Kamma 97 in the passage beginning "and regarding practical law."
