[Ps 119:105; 119:105; 119:105; 119:105; 119:130]
Even if it was sold, etc., and they despaired, etc. Although Tosafot and the Rosh hold in Bava Batra that when a robber sells or bequeaths the property the purchaser or heir acquires the enhancement, that applies where the owner had already despaired before the transfer, so that despair together with change of possession operates. Here the sale occurred before despair. The later despair does not combine retroactively with the earlier transfer, and the original owner therefore recovers the land and its enhancement. This is also the implication of the Tur and the authorities cited by the Beit Yosef. Where the purchaser acted in good faith, he may recover the principal and appropriate enhancement from the robber according to the rules stated below, but the owner's title to the land is not displaced.
