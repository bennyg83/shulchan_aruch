Ibid. The Tur cites the Rosh's responsum that Shimon shall swear. The Beit Yosef asks that Shimon should be disqualified from taking an oath because he robbed the vineyard. It appears to me that until he actually worked the vineyard he did not violate the prohibition against robbery, as stated nearby. The essential robbery lay in consuming the produce. The case may concern one who took it in the owner's absence and permitted it to himself, reasoning that since he spent his own money cultivating the vineyard, the owner would not object if he ate the produce. He is therefore not deemed a robber, similar to the Tosafot's explanation in the first chapter of Bava Metzia that some people understand “You shall not covet” as applying only when one takes without payment.
