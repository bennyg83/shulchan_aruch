It is still in his possession, etc. Bava Kamma 97a, where the Gemara rejects the possibility concerning a slave, etc.
Although, etc. This is evident there, and likewise from the incident on 96b.
Rather, etc. As explained above, and like one who dwells in another's courtyard, as discussed in siman 363. However, according to the gloss in seif 5 there concerning one who rented it, and the gloss in siman 375 seif 7, no proof can be brought from that case. The preceding source, however, is a clear proof.
And he consumed its produce, etc. See the beginning of chapter 6 of Gittin concerning rights to consume produce, and Bava Batra 33b, where the Gemara says the land returns, etc. Although Rashi in Gittin does not explain it that way, the sugya in Bava Metzia supports this. See Beer HaGolah to siman 373 seif katan 5 in the name of the Maggid Mishneh, who also proves it from the verse requiring that all be restored.
