An imbecile, etc. Rambam explains the disqualification on the ground that such a person is not included in the commandments. Lechem Mishneh asks why this reason is needed, since such persons are not mentally competent. I do not understand his question. We rule that a deaf mute, an imbecile, or a minor who writes a get while an adult stands over him can have sufficient understanding to write it for the proper purpose. This demonstrates that such a person may possess a degree of understanding and can learn from instruction. If, for example, he testifies that a woman was betrothed in his presence and a competent adult was there with him, why should he not be qualified? If he can possess sufficient understanding for writing a get, why should he necessarily be incapable of testimony concerning betrothal? Rambam therefore needed the reason that he is not included in the commandments. This is straightforward.
