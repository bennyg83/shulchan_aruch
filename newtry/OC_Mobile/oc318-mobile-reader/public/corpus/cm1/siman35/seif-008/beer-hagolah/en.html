tz) The wording of the Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilkhot Edut, from the baraita, "Who is a mentally incompetent person?" etc., following Rabbi Yochanan, who says even one of these signs is sufficient, Chagigah 3b. The Kesef Mishneh writes there that Rabbeinu understands that the examples listed in the baraita are not exclusive; the same applies to any behavior characteristic of mentally incompetent people. He discusses this further at length in Beit Yosef, Even HaEzer siman 121.
