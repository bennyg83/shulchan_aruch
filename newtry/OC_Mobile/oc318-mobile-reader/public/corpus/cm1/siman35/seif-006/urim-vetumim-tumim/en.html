Even by himself. Sema in seif katan 11 and 13 understands that according to Mechaber, following Rambam, even testimony concerning the bridal procession can be given by the person alone, without another witness. Shach objects that in Even HaEzer siman 96 Mechaber explicitly requires another person with him. I do not understand either formulation if it means that one witness alone can establish the bridal procession and extract money. No authority holds that. How could money be extracted through one witness? The Mishnah itself says, "if there are witnesses," and Rivash likewise speaks of witnesses in the plural. Moreover, the husband can contradict a single witness. Even if the witness were fully adult from beginning to end, one witness cannot by himself extract the higher ketubah amount. At most he could create an oath where the relevant monetary framework permits. Thus everyone agrees two witnesses are needed. The Torah rule remains, "by the mouth of two witnesses shall a matter be established." The dispute is different. Can both witnesses be persons who were minors when they observed the bridal procession and are now adults, or must at least one witness have been adult from beginning to end? According to Tosafot and Rosh, one witness must have been adult from the outset, because their requirement that "an adult be with him" applies also to bridal-procession testimony. According to Rambam, that requirement does not apply to the bridal procession. Therefore both witnesses may have been minors when they saw it, provided they are now adults. This leniency rests on the supporting presumption that most women were married as virgins. But two witnesses are still required. Accordingly, Mechaber in Even HaEzer says "provided that another is with him" without saying that the other must have been adult from the beginning. Likewise Rama's statement concerning Shabbat boundaries that "even by himself" is meant to contrast that rule with contexts requiring two witnesses. It is not a statement about whether the companion in bridal-procession testimony must have been adult from beginning to end. This is the correct reading.
