And some say that the man's agency also requires witnesses. This is the Ran's language: What difference is there between her agent for acceptance and his agent for delivery, and what comparison is there between kiddushin and gittin? With a get, since the get emerges from the agent's hand, it is reasonable to believe him because the husband entrusted him. But with an agency for kiddushin, why should he be believed where there is no external indication of the agency?
