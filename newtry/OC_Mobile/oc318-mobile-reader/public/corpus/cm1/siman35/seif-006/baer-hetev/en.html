Rabbinic. The Sema explains that this means the entire underlying matter is only rabbinic. Validation of documents is also rabbinic, but because it results in extracting money there is a Torah-level aspect to the matter.
The school. We do not suspect that he is a kohen's slave who eats terumah by virtue of his master, because a slave may not be taught Torah. Gemara, Ketubot. Sema.
Receives a share. In that case there is no concern that he is a slave, because a slave does not receive a share at the granary unless his master is with him. Gemara, ibid.
Himself. Meaning that the child testifies that he himself acted as the messenger. If he merely says that the matter was sent through someone else, he is not believed, because perhaps he did not pay sufficient attention. So writes the Kesef Mishnah. Sema.
