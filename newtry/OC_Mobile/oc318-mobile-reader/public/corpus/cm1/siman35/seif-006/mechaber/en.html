A person is believed to say, even by himself, 'On Shabbos we would come only this far,' because the law of Shabbos boundaries is rabbinic. The same applies to other rabbinic prohibitions. He is believed to say that a particular person would leave school to immerse so that he could eat terumah that evening; that the person shared terumah with us; that we personally delivered challah and priestly gifts to a particular kohen; or that his father told him, 'This family is of valid lineage, and this family is of disqualified lineage.' He is also believed concerning a sign made to indicate that one member of a family married a woman of disqualified lineage, as explained in Even HaEzer, siman 2. See also simanim 82 and 125.
