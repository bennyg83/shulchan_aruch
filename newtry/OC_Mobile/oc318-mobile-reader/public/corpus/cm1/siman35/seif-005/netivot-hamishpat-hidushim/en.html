The custom of virgins. This means the bridal canopy procession or similar signs. The reason is that most women marry as virgins. According to the Mechaber, no adult witness need accompany the person who remembers these signs from childhood. According to the Rama, an adult witness is required, Sema. The Tumim writes that all agree an adult must join him. The dispute is only whether that adult needed to have been an adult at the time of the event, or whether it suffices that both witnesses testify now about what they remember from childhood:
