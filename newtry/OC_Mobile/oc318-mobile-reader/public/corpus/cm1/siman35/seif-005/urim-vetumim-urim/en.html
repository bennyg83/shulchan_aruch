According to the custom for virgins, etc. This means the distribution of parched grain or the bridal canopy customarily used for virgins. The law was relaxed for this testimony because most women marry as virgins. Sema writes that according to the Mechaber an adult need not testify together with him, whereas according to the Rosh, whose view the Rama follows, an adult is required here as well. For this reason the Rama writes regarding Shabbat boundaries in seif 6 that the witness is believed even by himself. See Tumim, where I wrote that this is not correct. According to all opinions an adult must be with him. The dispute is only whether that adult had to have been an adult already at the time of the event, or whether he may have been a minor then and reached adulthood afterward. See there. The words of the Sema and Shach are difficult.
Beit haperas. This refers to a grave that was lost in a valley containing many fields, and he testifies that this field is impure while the other fields are pure. Ran.
