To twenty. This means nineteen years and thirty days old, contrary to what is written in Even HaEzer, siman 155, seif 12. Although Rambam there also writes otherwise, the Tur has already stated that this is a scribal error. See there. The signs of a saris are explained in Even HaEzer at the beginning of siman 172. Even if such signs appeared before he reached twenty, they do not make him an adult at that time. Nevertheless, once he later reaches twenty, those signs make him an adult from then onward, as is evident in Even HaEzer there and at the end of siman 155. Thus writes the Sema.
