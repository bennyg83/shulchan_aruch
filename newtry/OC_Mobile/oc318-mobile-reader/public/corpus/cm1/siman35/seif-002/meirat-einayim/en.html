He reached twenty years. In Perishah I demonstrated that one who is nineteen years and thirty days into his twentieth year is already called one who reached twenty, because thirty days in a year can count as a year in this context. This differs from the Mechaber's wording in Even HaEzer 155:12 citing a view that she must be twenty years minus thirty days. Although Rambam wrote so, the Tur there calls it a scribal error. See also Even HaEzer 172. Likewise, when the Gemara says thirteen years and one day, the "one day" does not mean a complete additional day. Immediately after completion of thirteen years he is an adult. The phrase only teaches that the thirteen years must be complete.
And he developed one of the signs of a saris, etc. The signs of a saris are listed in Even HaEzer at the beginning of siman 172, such as that his flesh does not produce steam when he bathes, his hair is abnormal, and his flesh is smooth. I wrote there that even if signs of a saris appeared before he reached twenty, they do not make him an adult immediately. When he later reaches twenty, however, those earlier signs then cause him to be treated as an adult going forward.
