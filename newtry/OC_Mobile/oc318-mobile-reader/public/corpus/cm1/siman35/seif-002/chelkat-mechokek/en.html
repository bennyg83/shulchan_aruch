And she admits that she received it. This is the view of Rambam and those who follow him. Other authorities disagree and hold that even if she admits receiving it, there are only doubtful kiddushin, because the witnesses are believed only because of her admission, making it analogous to kiddushin without witnesses. Rashba also remained uncertain about the matter. Therefore, it is proper to be stringent and regard the kiddushin as doubtful even where she admits, as the Bach rules, unlike the ruling here that they are definite kiddushin. If from the outset the witnesses were exempted from an oath, they are not interested parties, as explained in Choshen Mishpat siman 121, and the kiddushin are therefore definite.
