[Sema, letter 6] He becomes an adult only prospectively. Note: This is astonishing, for it is clear in Even HaEzer there that he becomes an adult retroactively. My master and father, the great Rabbi Wolf Eiger, of blessed memory, already noted this, as did Pri Megadim, Orach Chaim, Eshel Avraham, siman 52, seif katan 6.
