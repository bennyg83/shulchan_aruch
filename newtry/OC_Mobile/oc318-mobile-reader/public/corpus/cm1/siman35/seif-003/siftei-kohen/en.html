His testimony is not testimony. Tosafot and the Mordechai explain that this applies only to testimony that the property was worth that amount. Otherwise, why should land differ from movable property? It appears that the Rambam and Tur agree, contrary to the Beit Yosef and Bach's understanding. The Beit Yosef's argument that this law should then have been placed among appraisal laws rather than testimony laws is not persuasive, because the witness is testifying to the property's value. The Gemara itself also frames the matter as testimony. This is the correct ruling, and the Agudah rules likewise.
