(In the name of Mahari in the Tur: even if he reached the required age, his testimony is not accepted.) The question is raised from the Mishnah’s rule that anyone valid to judge is valid to testify, whereas above, siman 7, the Tur writes that one thirteen years old or older may judge even without two pubic hairs. The answer is that “anyone valid to judge” means that anyone who possesses some form of validity for testimony possesses corresponding validity for judging, and anyone who possesses some form of disqualification for testimony possesses a corresponding form of disqualification for judging. Minority is a disqualifying status for both, but the age thresholds are not identical. For testimony one remains a minor until producing two hairs; for judging, until age thirteen. The Sema writes similarly in siman 7.
