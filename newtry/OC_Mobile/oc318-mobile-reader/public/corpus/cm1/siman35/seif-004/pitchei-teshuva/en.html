And there are matters, etc. See Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Edut, siman 15, who writes: If one became obligated to take a heset oath and brought a witness to exempt himself from the oath, and it was discovered that the witness had formerly been a relative but subsequently ceased to be related, since a heset oath is rabbinic we do not require the witness to have been qualified both at the beginning and at the end. This is analogous to the cases where adults are believed to testify about matters they saw while minors. End of his words. This requires study. See what I write below in siman 87, seif 6.
