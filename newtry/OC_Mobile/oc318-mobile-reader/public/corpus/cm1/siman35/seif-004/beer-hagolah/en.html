t) Mishnah, Ketubot 28a.
y) The Gemara there explains that since validation of documents is d'rabbanan, the Sages trusted him concerning a rabbinic matter, provided that an adult is with him, as Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua states there. So too writes the Rambam in chapter 7. The Maggid Mishneh writes in chapter 17 of Hilkhot Ishut that according to the Rambam this applies only to identifying his own handwriting.
k) Tur, and so writes the Rosh there in his rulings.
