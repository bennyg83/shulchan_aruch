(Anthology) And some say. This is because the Mishnah lists only those cases. The Jerusalem Talmud there likewise teaches: only matters with which women are ordinarily familiar.
(Anthology) And some say, etc. The Raah disagrees, based on the Gemara's statement, 'It was necessary,' etc., 'it teaches us that they believed her,' which implies even a wholly uncommon matter.
