Darkei Moshe concludes that even where a father accepted kiddushin for his daughter and says, "You appointed me as agent," but they deny it, she is not betrothed. These words derive from Responsa Mahari Mintz, and on the basis of that responsum Darkei Moshe decided in favor of the view of the Raavyah. But one who studies the responsum will see that it concerns denial by the alleged agent, and is therefore not relevant to this dispute between Rabbi Yaavetz and the Raavyah. This lenient determination accordingly requires study.
