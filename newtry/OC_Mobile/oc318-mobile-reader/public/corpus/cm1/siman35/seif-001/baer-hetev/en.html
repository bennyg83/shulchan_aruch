Complete. Meaning full years. One should not think that once he is thirty days into his thirteenth year he is considered an adult. Regarding judging, the Mechaber writes in siman 7, seif 3, that some say a thirteen-year-old is qualified even if he has not produced two hairs. The reason is that judging depends upon understanding. For the same reason, some say he may not judge until age eighteen. Thus writes the Sema. The Shach writes that here we do not require the years to be complete from day to day. See Orach Chaim, siman 53, seif 1.
