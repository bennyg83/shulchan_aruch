A minor, etc. The verse states, "two men," and this refers to witnesses, for if it referred to the litigants, are minors never brought for adjudication? Rather, the Torah excludes minors from testimony. Gemara and Sema.
Two hairs, etc. Although above, in siman 7, one is qualified to serve as a judge even if he has not produced two hairs, there qualification depends upon understanding, comprehension, and sound judgment. Testimony, by contrast, is governed by a Scriptural decree requiring an adult who is called a "man." Sema.
Thirteen full years. Lest one say that part of the thirteenth year counts as the whole year, this teaches that thirteen complete years are required. Thus, "thirteen years and one day" means thirteen full years. Sema. See what I wrote in Tumim, where I conclude that exact hours are not required. If one was born at the end of Rosh Hashanah, he becomes an adult at the beginning of the corresponding night entering his fourteenth year. See there.
And if they delayed, etc. The same applies to judges. If they delayed examining him and he afterward produced two hairs, we presume retroactively that he had reached majority. Sema.
His beard filled out, etc. The hairs need not have reached a particular length. Therefore the Tur says, "his beard grew," while the Mechaber says, "filled out," meaning only that two hairs are insufficient and a substantial growth of hair is required. Sema. See Tumim, where I write that if he has fathered a child, he is presumed an adult and no further sign is required.
