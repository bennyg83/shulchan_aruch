(In a place where there are no men.) This requires study in light of the Tur’s ruling in siman 408 concerning sheepfolds where only slaves are present. One might have thought their testimony is accepted, yet the Tur teaches that valid witnesses are always required, and the Mechaber rules likewise there. Why is the present case different? The Beit Yosef also disagrees with the ruling cited here by the Rama. It appears that here the Sages acted as a protective measure. The gloss notes that this is stated explicitly in the source.
