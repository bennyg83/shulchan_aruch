And if the principal denies that he appointed him as agent, some say the matter is doubtful. It appears clear that the agent has no power to prohibit the woman's relatives to the principal when the principal denies the agency. Scripture states that one witness cannot establish a matter against a person, and one witness who is contradicted is of no effect, as likewise explained in siman 42. With respect to the woman, however, since she cannot conclusively contradict the agent and the agent may be telling the truth, she is doubtfully betrothed. She therefore requires a get out of doubt and is prohibited to the agent's relatives. I will write further on this below.
