A woman is disqualified from testimony. A tumtum and an androgynos are disqualified because of doubt, and anyone whose qualification itself is doubtful is disqualified. Rama: All of these disqualifications apply even where qualified men are not commonly available to testify. This is the strict law. Some, however, say that there is an enactment of the early authorities that in places where men are not ordinarily present, such as the women's section of a synagogue, or regarding an incidental matter about which women ordinarily know and men do not, such as identifying which garments a particular woman wore and that they belong to her, women are believed. Therefore some write that even one woman, a relative, or a minor is believed regarding assault, disgrace of a Torah scholar, other quarrels, or acts of informing, because such events do not normally occur after qualified witnesses have been arranged and there is no opportunity to summon them. This applies where the claimant makes a definite claim. See also siman 28, seif 15, in the Rama.
