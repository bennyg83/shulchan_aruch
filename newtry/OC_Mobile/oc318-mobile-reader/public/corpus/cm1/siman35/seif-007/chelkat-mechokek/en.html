Regarding the woman's agency without witnesses, one should be stringent. The Rosh writes that we may distinguish between a get, which comes to permit, and kiddushin, which comes to prohibit, and therefore equate the agency of the man and woman for kiddushin. The Ran likewise writes that the case is not comparable at all to the purported proof: with a get, where she is leaving the husband's domain, witnesses are indispensable; but with kiddushin, where she is prohibiting herself to others, if the man's admission suffices to establish his agency, why should the woman's admission not suffice to establish an agency to receive her kiddushin?
