(Sema, seif katan 11) They dispute the Maggid Mishneh's understanding, etc. Note: His intent is that they dispute whether Rambam requires one witness to be a fully qualified witness who was already an adult when she entered the chuppah, or whether he holds that even where two testify regarding what they saw while minors, it is effective. In Even HaEzer, siman 96, seif 15, from the wording 'even if one was a minor,' it is clear that the halacha was decided as explained by the Rosh and Ran. Tumim therefore objects to the Sema here without cause. The difficulty with the Bach, however, remains, because he explicitly writes that according to Rambam it is sufficient for only one witness to testify that he saw, while still a minor, that she went out with the bridal veil.
