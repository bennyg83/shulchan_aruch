(Collected note) The rider acquires. Unlike Shmuel, against whom many objections were raised there. Although the Gemara answers them, the answers are strained. The sugya there likewise supports this from the case of two people riding, etc., and "perhaps, etc." The Rosh, however, rules that riding alone does not acquire. See there. His words do not appear correct. Shulchan Aruch likewise rules so in Yoreh Deah 292:12. See the Rosh, laws of kilayim, at the end.
