(Collected note) When does this apply, etc. This is Rambam's explanation. The Rosh, however, follows Rashi that the latter version does not disagree at all with the first. Regarding the dispute about a camel and donkey, in a sale and gift he acquires neither of them, but in a found object or ownerless property he acquires both, because one does not remove property from him in a case of doubt.
