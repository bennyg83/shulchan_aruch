Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 17 of Hilkhot Geneivah VaAveidah, halachah 5. The Maggid Mishneh explains that even with a camel, if two people lead it they both acquire. But where one leads and one pulls, only the one pulling acquires and not the one leading, following the latter version in the first chapter of Bava Metzia 9a: some say that leading does not acquire a camel when done instead of pulling. A baraita there supports this, and the Maggid Mishneh gives a sound explanation.
