But he acquires the reins. The reason he acquires all the reins is that he can pull the entire reins toward himself from the head of the animal. This differs from the following seif, where one person is riding. Since the rider acquires the animal, the part of the reins fixed to the animal's head is subordinate to the animal. Once the other person does not acquire the whole reins, he also does not acquire the middle portion, because he lifted only one end while the other end is legally regarded as resting upon the ground. See siman 197 and the beginning of siman 269.
