You sold it. The Sema writes that this concerns a case with witnesses who presently saw the object. If the purchaser himself knows that it belonged to the claimant, then even without other witnesses it is removed from him, because his knowledge is equivalent to one witness, and the craftsman is one who cannot swear and therefore must pay, as I wrote above in siman 72, seif katan 97. I proved this from Tosafot in Bava Batra 30a in the name of the Rashba. See there. I also wrote there that according to Responsa Rashba, even without witnesses who saw the object, the purchaser stands in their place. The Tur's words here can accordingly be read literally. Nevertheless, as a matter of law I concluded there not in accordance with that Rashba.
