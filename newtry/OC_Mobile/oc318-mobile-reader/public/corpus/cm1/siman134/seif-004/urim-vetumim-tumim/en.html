If the garment emerges from another person's hands, etc. See what the Shach writes, and what I wrote above in siman 72 supporting the Rashba's view that when the object is in a third party's possession, this itself has the evidentiary force of having been seen, because the third party will not falsely deny what is actually in his possession. There is therefore no need to say, as the Sema does, that there are separate witnesses who saw it. See there.
