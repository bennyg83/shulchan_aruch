The language of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 9 of Hilkhot To'en VeNit'an. Maggid Mishneh cites the Mishnah in Chezkat HaBatim, "An artisan has no chazakah," Bava Batra 42b. The Gemara on 45-46 discusses the matter at length and concludes that even if the owner did not deliver the item before witnesses, so long as there are witnesses who now saw the item, the artisan is not believed to say, "I purchased it." Rambam follows all the Geonim and his teacher Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash, who hold that wherever the artisan has a migo he is believed, etc., except for Rif. Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam and the Geonim have the principal view. Tur and Rosh write likewise, as do Tosafot on 45a, s.v. "If so."
