Note: At first glance, it appears that the Rosh, Responsa, principle 56, siman 2, who writes that if one displayed the object outside beit din and said, "It is pledged to me," he is not believed because a migo does not apply outside beit din, and who brings proof from the sugya of an object that was seen, does not accept the law stated here, which is Tosafot's explanation of that sugya. For if he did, there would be no proof for the Rosh's ruling. This requires study.
