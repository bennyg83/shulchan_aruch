Even though he delivered it before witnesses. The Sema writes that the Mechaber merely followed the wording of the Tur. In truth, in the Tur the phrase "even though he delivered it before witnesses" refers to an earlier case, not to this clause. Here the case is where he delivered it without witnesses. This also explains the statement later in this seif that if he claims, "You never gave it to me," for if it had been delivered before witnesses he could not make that claim.
