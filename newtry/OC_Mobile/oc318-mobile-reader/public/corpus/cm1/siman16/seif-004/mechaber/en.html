If a person says to another, 'A document in your possession contains a right belonging to me,' and the other admits that it does, he must produce it in beit din. The beit din copies the portion that records that person's right. If the other says that he has no document containing any right of that person, he is not required to show a document to anyone. The claimant may, however, impose an unspecified ban upon anyone possessing a document that contains a right belonging to him who fails to show it. If the claimant asserts with certainty that he knows the relevant document is in the other's possession, the other takes a heses oath that it is not in his possession.
