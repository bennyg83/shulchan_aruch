Below, siman 28, seif 2, in the gloss, when a ban is imposed after testimony is sought, the Rama writes that the ban does not apply to relatives, and that this is the primary ruling. Here the Mechaber states without qualification that the opposing party must disclose the information, and the Rama says nothing. It appears that here the intent is that the opposing party possesses a document containing the claimant's right, or knows who the witnesses are who can establish that right. The ban applies to require him to produce the document or identify the witnesses. By contrast, when a general ban is imposed upon anyone who knows oral testimony to come and testify, relatives are not included, since they are not legally fit to testify. Sema.
