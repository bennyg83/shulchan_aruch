Because of Nashgaz. Tosafot in Avodah Zarah 36b, s.v. 'because of Nashgaz,' write that the category of zonah refers to a Kohen. Rambam, however, holds that the Sages decreed even for an Israelite, while for a Kohen she is prohibited by Torah law as a zonah. Tosafot hold that she is not a zonah by Torah law unless she certainly had prohibited relations.
