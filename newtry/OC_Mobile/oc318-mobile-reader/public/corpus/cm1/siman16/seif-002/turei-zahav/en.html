See there: “But if he initially claimed, ‘I have evidence’” The meaning is that he must now answer orally, affecting when the beit din’s allotted period begins. He is not heard because one who answers an oral claim must nullify any document he possesses, as the Geonim rule at the end of siman 75, and this party refuses to nullify it. This also explains the opinion that after the opponent swore and was discharged, the claimant is no longer believed to produce proof: by accepting the court’s direction and administering the oath, he nullified his proofs. Were the rule learned only from siman 20, one should distinguish whether the proof is presently within the country.
