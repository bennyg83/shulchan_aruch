How Much Time Is Granted to Produce Evidence, and the Law of Imposing a Ban. Five seifim. If one litigant says, 'I have witnesses to bring,' he is granted thirty days. After thirty days, the judgment is issued and he must pay. If he later brings the witnesses, the judgment is overturned. If it is evident and known to the beit din that specified people are witnesses to the matter but have traveled far away, he is granted enough time to bring them. {Rama: Similarly, if the beit din knows that he can prove his claim within thirty days, it does not grant him needless additional time (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ritva). See below, siman 98, seif 4.}
