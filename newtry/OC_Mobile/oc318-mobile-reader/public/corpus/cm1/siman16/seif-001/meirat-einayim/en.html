One of the litigants says, etc. This means either defendant or claimant. If the defendant is ready to swear and the claimant says, 'I do not want him to swear and be exempt now, because I have witnesses,' he receives thirty days and no more. The defendant can say that an indefinite unresolved lawsuit depreciates his property. The gloss below in siman 24 saying that a claimant may delay the defendant's oath until he chooses is likewise limited where the delay does not depreciate the defendant's assets. Here, because the claimant asserts that he has witnesses who will defeat the oath, we still allow thirty days.
They give him time, etc. This is for ordinary people. The law regarding a violent person is explained below in siman 98. See Darkei Moshe citing Rashba.
And he must pay. The same applies that he must swear at that point even against the claimant's preference. The Mechaber mentions payment as one example.
They do not give him time for purposeless delay. Even where there is no direct financial loss. Rashba adds that even if it is not known that his intent is merely to evade, where the other party will suffer loss he must produce his proof within thirty days.
