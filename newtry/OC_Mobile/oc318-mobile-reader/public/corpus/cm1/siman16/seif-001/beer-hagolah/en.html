a) The wording of the Tur, from Rava's statement, etc.: "If he says, set me a time," etc., Bava Kamma 112b. As the Rosh writes, since the Talmud did not specify the duration of this period, it is presumably thirty days.
b) From the Mishnah, Sanhedrin 31a, following Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel.
c) So too writes the Rosh, responsa, rule 81, siman 2.
d) He further writes there that if the opposing party will suffer a loss through the delay, he must produce his evidence within thirty days. Sema in the name of Darkei Moshe, who cites this.
