Because regarding a matter that is not incumbent upon a person, people say things imprecisely. The meaning is: any matter that a person is not obligated to do and from which he receives no benefit in the particular manner in which he did it is not something he places carefully upon his memory.
Since it was not incumbent upon him to do it in that manner, he does not focus on remembering exactly how he did it, and therefore he may later vary the details and say the opposite of what occurred.It was explained in siman 70, seif 2. See what I wrote there in the Sema.
