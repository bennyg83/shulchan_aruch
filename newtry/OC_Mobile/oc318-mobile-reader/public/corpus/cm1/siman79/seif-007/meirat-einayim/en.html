The same applies where the claimant first sued orally, the defendant said that there had never been such a transaction, and the claimant then produced a handwritten acknowledgment of the debt. If the handwriting is authenticated, the defendant becomes established as a false denier, just as in the preceding case where the claimant produces a promissory note signed by witnesses. This is also implied by the Tur's wording. The Mechaber followed the Rambam's formulation in chapter 7 of Hilchot To'en. He already stated this law at the beginning of this siman, seif 1, and repeats it here to teach that even with the defendant's own handwriting he is not believed to say, after the signature is authenticated, "I repaid."
