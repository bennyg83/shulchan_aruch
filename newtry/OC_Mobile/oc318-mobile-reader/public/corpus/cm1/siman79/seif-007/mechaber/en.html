Similarly, if the claimant produces the defendant's handwritten acknowledgment that he owes him, and the defendant says, 'This never occurred; this is not my handwriting,' then if the handwriting is authenticated in beit din or witnesses saw that it is his handwriting, he is established as a denier and must pay. He is not believed to say, 'I paid,' unless the opposing party admits it or he produces witnesses before whom he paid. {Rama: If a person admitted in beit din that he owes another and afterward said, 'I remembered that I paid him, and here are witnesses that I paid him,' he is believed.}
