Witnesses that he repaid before them. This means payment made after he had already been held liable by beit din. Before that, such witnesses do not help, because by saying "I never borrowed" he already contradicted the existence of the debt and thereby effectively admitted that he had not repaid it. Sema, and this is clear.
One who admitted, etc. This rule already appeared above in seif 3 and is repeated here concerning a handwritten acknowledgment. One might have thought that because the rule allowing him to say "I later remembered" is itself disputed, Ran and Rashba disagreeing, and because many authorities independently hold that one cannot claim payment against a handwritten acknowledgment, the combination of those positions would defeat him here. The ruling therefore teaches that he is nevertheless believed, because the matter remains doubtful and the burden of proof rests upon the claimant. There is no difficulty and no need for the Shach's forced explanation.
