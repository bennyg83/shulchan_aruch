That he admitted. The Sema asks what the Rama adds here, since the Mechaber already wrote the rule in seif 3. The Shach answers that here the admission concerns a written document, where one might have thought the law is worse for the defendant. Maggid Mishneh explicitly distinguishes the cases, and the Rama teaches, following the Tur, that there is no difference.
