But one who claims... As stated in the Yerushalmi, chapter HaDayanim, cited by Sefer HaTerumos at the end of section 41: one who first comes with a document and then relies on a chazakah, Rebbe says he is judged by the document, while Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says he is judged by the chazakah. Rabbi Zeira said in the name of Rabbi Yirmiyah that an incident came before Rebbe and he ruled like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. Rabbi Yosi asked Rabbi Zeira: has Rebbe reversed his position? There he said he is judged by the document, while here he says he is judged by the chazakah. The Yerushalmi answers that the first ruling was before he retracted his claim. Meaning, the rule that he is judged by the document applies only while he still claims that he has a document but has not produced it, or he produced it but it was not validated. If he retracts and says, "I have no document," Rebbe agrees that he is judged by the chazakah. If he retracted and said... Bava Basra 6a: "No. What does 'you have nothing of mine' mean..." See Tosafos there, s.v. "whoever..." who ask, etc. Sefer HaTerumos answers that the Mishnah means as follows: if, before saying "I gave it to you," he said, "you have nothing of mine," he is liable. For this the Gemara needed to invoke the principle, etc. The case is outside beit din. If it were in beit din, he would already be established as a denier without this principle. Outside beit din, he is not established as a denier; the only issue is the rule that whoever says, etc. The practical difference is that if he says, "I paid afterward," he is believed, since he was not established as a denier, and the earlier effect arose only from the rule that whoever says, etc.
