[Shulchan Aruch] That he can correct his words. Note: The Tur cites Sefer HaTerumos, gate 12, part 1, that if one was sued in beit din and said, "You have nothing in my possession," and witnesses later prove that he borrowed, he may explain that by saying, "I meant that you have nothing in my possession because I repaid." But if he says, "I repaid after I said that you have nothing in my possession," then, since he admits that his original words meant that there had never been a loan, he is established as a false denier and is not believed. This is difficult to me, for why should he not be believed that he later paid, by virtue of a migo that he could have explained his original words as meaning that he had already paid? I later found, with God’s help, that Gedolei Terumah asks this and gives a very forced answer. See there.
