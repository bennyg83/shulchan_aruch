And two witnesses came, etc. If the defendant says, 'I never borrowed,' and one witness testifies that he did borrow, then according to Rambam and most authorities, with Rashba nearly alone against them, he cannot later say, 'I repaid.' The reason is that he is one who was obligated to swear against the witness and cannot take that oath, so he pays, except in matters that do not generate a Torah oath, such as certain documents or rents of houses. But if he says, 'I repaid afterward,' he is believed, because one witness does not establish him as a false denier. The Gemara in Bava Metzia states explicitly that with one witness he is not established as a false denier. See Tumim.
