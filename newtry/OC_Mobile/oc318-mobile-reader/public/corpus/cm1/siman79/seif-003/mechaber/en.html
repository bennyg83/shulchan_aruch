If a person admitted in beit din that he owes the claimant one hundred and afterward said, 'I remembered that I paid him the debt I admitted,' and produces witnesses, their testimony is effective and we act upon it, because he did not contradict their testimony and is not like one who says, 'I never borrowed.' {Rama: This applies only if he claimed that he paid before the witnesses came. If the witnesses came first, he is not believed.}
