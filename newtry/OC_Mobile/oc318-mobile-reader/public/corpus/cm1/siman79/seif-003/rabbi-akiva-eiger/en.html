[Shulchan Aruch] Or by way of a gift, he is established as a false denier. Note: If one claimed, "You lent me one hundred," and the defendant said, "I have never borrowed from anyone," and witnesses then testify that he did borrow, but they do not know whether from this claimant or from another, he is considered established as a false denier and must pay this claimant. This appears to me from the Yerushalmi, chapter 4 of Shevuos, mishnah 5.
