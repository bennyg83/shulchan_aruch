Ever. If he says, 'I never borrowed,' and witnesses come that he did borrow, then even if he later says, 'I now remember that I paid,' and witnesses come that he paid, he is not believed. So writes Nimukei Yosef, and this also appears from the Rivash, siman 392. See below, siman 81, seif 20. Shach.
First. From the words of the Maggid Mishneh cited by the Beit Yosef at the end of siman 82, it is explicitly clear that even if the witnesses came first, he is believed. Shach.
