Or, "I hereby divorce you," etc. The distinction between redemption and medical treatment is clear. With redemption, his obligation already arose at the moment she was taken captive. Medical treatment, however, is an obligation that renews from day to day. He may therefore divorce her during her illness. Maharshal, cited by the Bach, already writes that nowadays, when one may not divorce a wife against her will, he remains obligated to pay for her medical treatment. It does not help that he is prepared to give her ketubah and divorce her. See what I wrote above in siman 77 concerning the Bach's citation of Rabbi Eliyahu Mizrachi, who held that even nowadays, if the husband wishes to divorce her and pay her ketubah, she has no further claim upon him.
