One who admitted, etc. Although in the earlier incident a person is believed against himself more than witnesses, here he claims that he later remembered and that his admission was mistaken. He is believed only where he brings witnesses proving the mistake, as in the case in Gittin and as stated in siman 126:13.
Specifically when he claimed, etc. Otherwise, why does Bava Batra say that one who produces a deed and is contradicted by witnesses swears and collects half? He should be able to retract and say that he remembered payment of the entire amount. Nimukei Yosef explains the Gemara accordingly and adduces the same proof.
(Likkut) Specifically. This follows from Bava Metzia, daf 4a: his own admission cannot be contradicted, etc. They ask that there the reason is that he gives it to him as a gift, and answer that the case is of this kind. Nimukei Yosef there. He further brings proof from Bava Basra, daf 128b, concerning one who produces, etc., and the witnesses, etc. He explains that ordinarily, after the witnesses came, he retracted his admission. See there at length. End.
