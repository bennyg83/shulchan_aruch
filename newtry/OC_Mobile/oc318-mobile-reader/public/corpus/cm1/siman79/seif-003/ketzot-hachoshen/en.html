Witnesses came first. This is from Nimukei Yosef at the beginning of Bava Metzia. Nimukei Yosef writes that perhaps one who denied before beit din and said, 'I never borrowed,' and afterward says, 'I remembered that I borrowed and repaid,' and even brings witnesses to repayment, is nevertheless liable. Although 'I never borrowed' implies 'I did not repay,' and thus resembles one who admits liability, it is worse than merely saying, 'I owe,' because his later words contradict his original statement. He first says he never borrowed and then says he borrowed and repaid. Therefore he cannot retract. I am astonished. If he retracts only after witnesses have come, then even one who had simply admitted liability cannot retract according to Nimukei Yosef's own position. If he retracts before witnesses come, all authorities agree he may retract because he is moving from one exempting claim to another exempting claim, even without witnesses that he paid, as all the poskim rule and as explained in Tur and Shulchan Aruch siman 80. This requires study.
