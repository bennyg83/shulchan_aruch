[Shulchan Aruch] And witnesses testified that he admitted to him. Note: See Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, siman 36, who writes that in such a case he is not believed only where he subsequently says, “I had already paid you beforehand.” But if he says, “I paid you afterward,” he is believed, since he was not contradicted by witnesses but only by his own admission. Although we do not believe him that his admission was made jokingly or by mistake, he is nevertheless not considered established as a denier. See there.
