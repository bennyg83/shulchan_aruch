If the defendant said, "It never happened," witnesses testify that he borrowed, and he then says, "I already paid," he cannot impose a hesset oath on the claimant even after payment. But if he claims that he paid only after he had already been found liable in beit din, although that claim is also not believed because he became a false denier, after paying he may impose hesset upon the claimant that he did not receive payment twice.
