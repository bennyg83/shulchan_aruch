“And he came back and said, ‘You are like him.’ That is to say that since he is forced to pay and out of the High Court he has returned and said to another groceries that have been held in the possession of the same money, he is no longer loyal to ever claiming him my fault until he brings witnesses
But not regarding other money. Even if he denied one hundred and was established as a denier regarding fifty, he must still swear regarding the other fifty, as ruled above in siman 75, seif 4, and siman 87, seif 5. The Sema cites a responsum of Rashba concerning a person shown to have stolen some documents from Reuven’s house. Rashba held him an established denier regarding every document he later produced against Reuven. This requires study. Why should theft of some documents establish denial regarding all? Perhaps the point is that one who stole some is suspected of stealing all, and Rashba follows the first view below in siman 92, seif 9, that where a suspect produces an instrument, the opposing party swears heset and is exempt. In Rashba’s case Reuven had died and his widow proved theft of some documents, so she was exempt. According to our ruling like the final view in siman 92, he would not be established as a denier regarding the other documents. Rashba himself concludes that the matter requires further consideration. Rivash writes that once Reuven was established as a denier regarding certain objects, Shimon is believed to say that they remain in Reuven’s possession and is also believed, without an oath, as to their value within an amount Reuven could reasonably possess. This too requires study, for Reuven was not established as a denier regarding their value. See above, simanim 75, 87, and 90.
And he is believed, etc., like any other person. Although he was established as a denier, he did not thereby become a generally disqualified or suspect person. Regarding other money he has the same status as anyone else. See Responsa Rabbi Shlomo Cohen, book 3, simanim 77 and 79, and Responsa Mabit, part 2, simanim 281, 286, and 297.
