And he is believed, etc. Responsa Rivash 354 rules that if one says, 'I lent you objects,' and the defendant says, 'It never happened,' while witnesses establish the loan, the defendant is established as a false denier. Rivash further says that even regarding the value of the objects, the lender is believed without an oath to state their value, provided he was a person known to own such objects. Shach correctly objects that the defendant was not established as false concerning valuation and therefore the lender should take an oath. If the enactment made for one whose property was robbed does not apply here, then even with an oath the claimant should recover only the lowest reasonable value of such objects.
