An admission in beit din, etc. From here to the end of the siman I already discussed the entire subject at length above in siman 39, so I will only summarize the principal points. Sema understands Rambam, Tur, and Rosh alike: the debtor may say, 'I paid,' yet the admission may still support collection from purchasers. Above I wrote that this is indeed the view of Rosh and Tur, against the Shach. Rambam and Rif, however, do not hold that combination; for them the two issues depend on one another. I also agreed above with the Shach that most authorities dispute Rambam and hold that even a beit din admission, so long as it has not been written into a formal record, does not allow collection from encumbered property and the debtor may claim payment.
