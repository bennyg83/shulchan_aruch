The language of the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot To'en VeNitan, laws 3 and 5.
As Rav Zevid said in the name of Rav Nachman, Bava Metzia 17a. The Rif writes similarly: although an admission in beit din is like a written loan, that is where the debtor does not comply with the judgment. Here he complied, and it became clear that he did not pay only where the facts show so. But where he complied and left beit din intending to accept the ruling, and later says, 'I paid,' he is believed. The Maggid Mishneh writes likewise. So rules the Rosh, explaining that ordinary people do not distinguish between 'Go give him' and 'You are liable to give him'; both appear to them as a final judgment, and people often pay accordingly.
