And I do not owe you.
The phrase 'and what you say you owe me likewise' means: an amount equal to this amount.It is as though he said, 'I did not repay.' The practical result is that if witnesses later come and say that he borrowed and repaid, he must pay, because he already admitted that he had not repaid, as explained in seif 1.Whether he can say, 'I made a mistake,' etc. This gloss does not belong to the Mechaber's words in this seif.
It should have been placed in the following seif.
