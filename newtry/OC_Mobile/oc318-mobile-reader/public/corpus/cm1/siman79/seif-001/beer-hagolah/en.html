The Mordechai at the end of chapter HaKotev writes that one who says in beit din, "I did not borrow," cannot later amend his statement by saying that he meant only, "I did not remain indebted because I repaid." Rabbeinu Shemariah proves this there. This is also implied in gate 5 of the Rif, and Rabbeinu Chananel rules likewise in the chapter HaShutafin.
