[Shulchan Aruch] And he said to him, “It never happened,” and this is his handwriting. Note: Where one sued him orally and he claimed, “I never borrowed,” and afterward the claimant produced a writing in the defendant’s handwriting, while the defendant claims, “It was written in trust,” or, “I signed my name at the bottom of a blank sheet, and perhaps he found it and wrote above it whatever he wished,” Maharit writes in a responsum in Choshen Mishpat that he is believed. The exception is where he does not claim that it was written in trust and persists in his statement that he never borrowed, for we do not advance that claim on his behalf. See there, and see Responsa Mizrachi.
