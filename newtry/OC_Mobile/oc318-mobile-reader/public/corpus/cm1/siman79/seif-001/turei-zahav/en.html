(Seif 1: More than one hundred witnesses.) The phrase “more than” is deliberate. If an admission were merely equal to two witnesses, one might set the admission against the two witnesses and preserve the person’s presumption of honesty, so he would not become established as a denier. Since an admission is stronger than witnesses, the admission controls and his contradiction makes him a proven denier. The point is straightforward; the Sema’s lengthy discussion is unnecessary.
