In beit din. This excludes a denial made outside beit din. Even if witnesses testified in another beit din, and not in the beit din where he denied the obligation, he is liable. Sema:
He must pay. Even if, after witnesses arrive and establish that he borrowed, he says that he paid earlier, he is no longer believed. By saying, "I never borrowed," when witnesses establish that he did, he has already become established as a denier. Shach, unlike the Mabit:
More than one hundred witnesses. Therefore, the claimant cannot afterward impose a hesset oath upon him. If his admission were only equivalent to witnesses, one could at least impose hesset, for the admission and the witnesses would cancel one another and the matter would become an ordinary monetary claim requiring hesset. But since his own admission is stronger, no oath can be imposed. Sema:
He is established as a denier. If he subsequently claims that he paid, he is likewise not believed, because he is already established as a denier regarding this matter. Shach:
We do not administer a hesset oath. For he admitted that he did not pay, while witnesses testify that he borrowed. Sema:
