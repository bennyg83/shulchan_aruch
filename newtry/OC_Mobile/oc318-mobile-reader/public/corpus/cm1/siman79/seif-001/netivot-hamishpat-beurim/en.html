For he was contradicted by witnesses. One should not argue that the witnesses are themselves disqualified because they testify that he paid while he personally admits that he did not pay. That is not so. The matter can be understood as the Ran writes at the beginning of Perek Shevuat HaDayanim, namely that the witnesses may remember the event more accurately than the litigant himself.
