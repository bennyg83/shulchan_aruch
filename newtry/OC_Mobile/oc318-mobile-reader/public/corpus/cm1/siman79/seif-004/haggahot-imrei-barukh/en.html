(Sema, seif katan 15) And he produced his handwriting against him, etc. Note: This requires study according to the view in siman 69 that a debtor is believed to claim payment against his own handwriting. If so, he should likewise be able to claim that the writing was given in trust by a migo that he could say "paid." Why is he established as a false denier by his statement "it never happened"? Perhaps in truth he never borrowed, and the handwriting was given only in trust, a claim he had no reason to fear because he is believed to assert it. See Urim VeTumim, siman 46, seif katan 1; below, siman 126, seif 12 in the Rama, where one is believed against handwriting to say "I erred"; Shach, siman 81, seif katan 43; above, Shach seif katan 11; and siman 82, Sema seif katan 53.
