He was established as a denier. This is not precise. Rather, he is merely not believed unless his denial was in beit din. Here, however, that is unnecessary.
If he said to him merely, "you have nothing of mine"... Although the Mishnah in Shevuos cited above states, "you have nothing of mine," and Bava Metzia 17a records Rabbi Yochanan's case where he said, "you have nothing of mine," witnesses came, etc., and then he retracted, etc., those formulations do not mean that he spoke without qualification. Rather, he explained his words, as stated in Bava Basra 6a: "What does 'you have nothing of mine' mean? I never owed you..." Likewise there on 31a: he later said to him, "Yes, it belonged to your fathers..."
