But if he said to him, "You never had anything in my possession." This means that after his earlier acknowledgment he did not later explain, "I gave it to you," but instead said, "There was never any such transaction," and witnesses then testified that he had acknowledged the debt, meaning that he had previously answered, "Yes." In that case he becomes established as a false denier. If, however, after first saying, "Yes," he did not later say, "There was never any such transaction," but merely told him the next day, "You have nothing in my possession," he may reconcile his statements by saying: "It is true that you had money in my possession, and therefore I initially said yes. Afterward I repaid you, and that is why I later told you that you have nothing in my possession." This is clear.
