When does the rule that he is believed to say, "I paid," apply? When he obeyed the judgment of beit din. If he left beit din in defiance and they placed him under nidui, he is not believed to say that he paid, and the creditor may collect even from encumbered property. Therefore a written judgment may be issued to the creditor even without the borrower's consent. Rama: If one accepted before beit din that he would pay his fellow and later comes and says, "He already owed me money," he is not believed, because if that were so he certainly would not have accepted before beit din an obligation to pay. Although he has a migo that he could say, "I paid," a migo is not accepted against a presumption as strong as this one.
