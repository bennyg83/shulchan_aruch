And they placed him under ban. This is not literal. Rather, whenever he refused to comply with a judicial ruling, the same rule applies. Bach writes this, and the Shach agrees, unlike the Sema. See Shach, seif katan 35. There is a scribal error there. The text should read that where he did not obey the court, even if it was not established that he failed to pay, and so forth. There are two explanations of the case of one who stood in judgment: either he did not comply with the ruling, or it was established that he did not pay.
