An heir who sold, etc.; there is one view, etc.; and some say, etc. The Gemara rules that heirs who sold movable property before collection defeat the creditor's claim to the property and its proceeds. One view nevertheless imposes liability for damaging another's lien, by analogy to releasing a slave expressly designated as an apotiki and forgiving a debt instrument. The opposing view rejects that analogy. See the beginning of siman 104.
