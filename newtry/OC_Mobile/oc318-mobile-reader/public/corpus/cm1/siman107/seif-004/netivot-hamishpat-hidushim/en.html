And some say that he does not collect. See the Beurim. According to this view, even if the sale proceeds are still in the purchaser's possession, where the heir transferred the land to the purchaser by a kinyan sudar, the creditor does not collect those proceeds. If the heirs sold movable property, this likewise depends on the same dispute whether the creditor can collect the proceeds.
