For the same reason, if each heir inherited two fields, the creditor must take one field from each heir, because the father too could have paid him from two separate locations where each contained the minimum measure of a field, as stated by the Tur and Shulchan Aruch at the beginning of siman 171. Whichever he wishes. The Sema writes that the heirs are nevertheless entitled to make an arrangement among themselves and give the creditor an inferior field, since a creditor collects from heirs only from inferior land, as stated at the end of siman 108. The Shach cites Maharshal that after the enactment of the Geonim allowing creditors to collect movable property, if one heir received land and the other movable property, the heir holding the land may say to the creditor, "Take half the land from me and half the movable property from my brother." Obviously, this assumes that the half-field offered by the first heir contains the minimum legal measure of a field.
