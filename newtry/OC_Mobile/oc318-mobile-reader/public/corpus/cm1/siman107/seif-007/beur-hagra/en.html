They inherited... if not... See Sema. Bava Kamma 95b speaks of three categories and shows that even property due to heirs need not always be divided with them in land; all the more so here.
But if they inherited... As stated in Bava Basra 124a, where a debt document comes against them.
Unless... Tosafos answers the brothers-who-divided passage in Bava Kamma this way.
Even if he did not... Otherwise Rav Asi would not have said that he receives a quarter in money. Tosafos discusses this according to Rashi's first explanation, though they prefer Rashi's second explanation. See also Bava Basra 107a.
