Later. The Sema writes that the same rule applies where the deceased left movables and the heirs expressly state that they used their own money in exchange for those movables; the later creditor cannot collect on that account. The Ran and Maggid Mishneh say so. The Shach objects that those authorities wrote only according to Talmudic law, whereas the Mechaber is speaking after the Geonic enactment making movables collectible. This follows from the Mechaber's words in Bedek HaBayit. See there.
