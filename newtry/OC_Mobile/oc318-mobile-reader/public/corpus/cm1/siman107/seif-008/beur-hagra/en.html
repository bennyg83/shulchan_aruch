He left... if they are... As explained above and as stated in Bava Basra 138a: if a debt document later appears, the creditor collects from all of them.
And if there is no... The Raavad explains the statements in Bava Kamma and Bava Basra about brothers who divided and a creditor who then appeared in this manner. Just as one collecting in the debtor's absence must swear because of the concern that credit otherwise be restricted, so too with heirs. Sema, citing the Tur, explains that the heirs stand in their father's place for this purpose.
