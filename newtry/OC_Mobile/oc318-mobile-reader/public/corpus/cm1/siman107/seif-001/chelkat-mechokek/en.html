Even though they made a kinyan from him. With a dying person, whose words have the force of a written and delivered act, the phrase "even though" is understandable. One might think that the kinyan strengthens the gift and shows that he intended a complete gift, as where the document uses language of added force. Otherwise, a deathbed gift written with a kinyan can even be weakened. But with a healthy person, "even though" is not natural, because without a kinyan he obviously acquires nothing. See Choshen Mishpat 246:4 and 7, where some authorities hold that when a kinyan is made, we do not say he intended only to appoint the person as guardian, because a kinyan is not made merely for guardianship.
