Compelling heirs to pay their father's debt. It contains 12 se'ifim.
It is a mitzvah for heirs to pay their father's debt, and they are compelled to do so just as their father would have been compelled. When does this apply? When he left land. If he left only movable property, under the original law they are not compelled to use it to pay their father's debt, although it is a mitzvah for them to do so. The Geonim, however, instituted that a creditor may collect from movable property the father left to his heirs. Therefore, today they are compelled to pay their father's debts, even an oral loan, from the movable property he left, including property he acquired after the loan without writing an after-acquired property clause. See below, siman 111. An heir stands in his father's place. They must likewise pay their father's debt from money owed to their father by others, whether they collected land or money. If they inherited both land and movable property, and the document does not expressly provide for collection from movable property during the debtor's life or after his death, and the heirs wish to pay the creditor with land while he wishes to take movable property, the law follows the heirs. If they inherited nothing from their father, they have no obligation to pay his debt, not even as a mitzvah. {Rama: If an heir inherited some property, he need pay only the amount he inherited, as stated in Rivash 478. If he says, 'I do not inherit and I will not pay,' see below at the end of siman 278 whether he is heard.} See note 40.
