The heir claimed... See Sema. These rabbinic oaths were modeled on Torah oaths. Even heses was instituted only where the analogous partial-admission case would require a Torah oath. The oath that one has no property was similarly modeled on the oath that an entrusted item is not in one's possession. Heirs take a Torah oath on a definite claim, not a doubtful one, as stated in Shevuos 47a.
Reuven who was... and if Shimon... Bava Basra 154b supports this, following the second formulation in Tosafos. See Hagahos Ashri and Sema.
