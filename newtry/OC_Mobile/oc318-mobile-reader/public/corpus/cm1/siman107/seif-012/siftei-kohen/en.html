It was found written in his ledger. The Sema, seif katan 27, distinguishes this from siman 91:5, where the Tur and Mechaber permit judgment based on a person's ledger, because there are corroborating circumstances there. This formulation is imprecise. Even when there are grounds to believe the ledger truthful, there may still be concern that the debt was subsequently paid, as in the present case. His answer therefore does not resolve the difficulty. Rather, here too there are corroborating circumstances, but payment remains possible, as the Rashba states explicitly. Siman 91 concerns a case in which payment need not be suspected, such as before the due date, or as the Tur explains there in seif 7. The Sema's words can perhaps be understood this way, but his language is not exact.
