Rashba, in his responsa, part 7, siman 7. He explains there that the fact that the debtor did not instruct at the time of death that the debt had been paid, or did not erase the written notation, proves nothing, for perhaps the Angel of Death prevented him. An even stronger principle is stated in Bava Batra 70b: one Master holds that the Angel of Death prevented him. There this is said even though there was a formal business document, all the more so concerning a notation written in a ledger.
