Or two-thirds. It appears the same applies where the stated sum exceeds the value of the estate. For example, he writes that he gives her assets worth ten thousand, while his entire estate is worth only five thousand. Even so, she did not waive her ketubah, because the essential waiver depends on the honor he showed her by writing to her all his property, even if technically he left a large remainder. This is the Rosh's view. I have already written that Rambam's plain language does not appear to agree.
