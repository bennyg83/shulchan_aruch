There, seif 22, in the name of a responsum of his father Rosh, rule 80, siman 13. He explains there that with an heir or purchaser who encumbered property, we do not apply the rule that one does not collect from encumbered property where unencumbered property is available.
He writes there, siman 8: Two brothers compromised with a claimant who said that their father owed him money. A third brother then came and demanded that they return his share. He may collect his share from whichever party he wishes, either from the creditor or from his two brothers. Sema in the name of Darkei Moshe.
