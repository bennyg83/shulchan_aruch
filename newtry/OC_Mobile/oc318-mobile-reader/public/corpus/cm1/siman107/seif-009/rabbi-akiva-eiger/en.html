[Shach, letter 10] And see Maadanei Melech on Bava Kamma. Note: See Ramban in Milchamot, first chapter of Bava Kamma, who writes explicitly that if the creditor comes to collect movable property, he cannot collect it from one heir while land is in the possession of the other heir, because where land is available the Geonim did not enact collection from movable property.
