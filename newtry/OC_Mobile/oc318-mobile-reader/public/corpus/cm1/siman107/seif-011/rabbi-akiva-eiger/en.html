[Shulchan Aruch] The heirs are exempt. Note: Maharach Shashon, siman 32, raises a difficulty from Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilchot Maaser Sheni, who rules in accordance with the Rabbis, who maintain the law of second tithe in the case of one who found a utensil and an inscription upon it. He writes that this is difficult. The Yerushalmi in the second chapter of Maaser Sheni provides no proof, because there Rabbi Yose follows his own position, as is evident from their dispute concerning one who found a utensil with an inscription upon it.
