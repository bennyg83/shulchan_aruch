An heir who sold... As stated in Kesubos 69a, whether they sold or mortgaged the property. See Rashi there.
Even if... Bava Kamma 8b establishes the case with orphans. Tosafos there shows that the heir cannot simply remove the creditor by tendering other property.
Even property of his father. See Beer HaGolah.
But if... unless... Heirs are treated like purchasers in many respects, as Tosafos in Bava Kamma explains. Just as a purchaser can ordinarily remove a creditor by payment unless the property was made an explicit apotiki, so too an heir. See Bava Kamma, Bava Metzia 14b and 110b.
