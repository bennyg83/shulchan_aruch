If Shimon tells heirs, 'You have one hundred of mine in your possession,' and they admit it before witnesses, but when he later sues them they say, 'We did not admit that we personally owe you; we admitted only a debt arising from our father, who left no property from which to pay,' while Shimon says, 'No, you owe me personally,' they must pay because their admission was unqualified. See above, siman 81. If they admitted before one witness and now deny that they made the admission, they swear to contradict the witness. If they acknowledge that they made an unqualified admission before the witness but say that they intended only to admit a debt arising from their father and not a personal debt, they must pay. See above, siman 75, seif 13.
