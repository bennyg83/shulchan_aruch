“I sell you such and such field.” The Tur and Rama already stated this law in siman 218:22 and repeat it here in order to arrange together the laws of a sale described by boundaries, which are discussed together in the chapter HaMokher Et HaBayit. They also teach a further point here. Although he did not explicitly state the boundaries at the time of sale, there must at least be one surrounding boundary recognizable around the parcel, as explained there.
