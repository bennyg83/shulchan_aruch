Separated from it. Meaning, he wrote: 'I sell such-and-such field to so-and-so; its western boundary is Reuven, its northern boundary Shimon, its southern boundary Levi, and its eastern boundary is the land that was separated from it.' This means that the field which I divided off from the field I sold him forms its eastern boundary. The same applies if he stated only the western boundary, while regarding the eastern boundary he wrote 'that was separated from it,' for this alone suffices, as explained above. Alternatively, instead of 'separated' he wrote 'cut off,' meaning the field that I retained for myself and cut off from this field that I sold him. He did not specify how much of it he sold, nor is it known how much he retained for himself. Sema.
