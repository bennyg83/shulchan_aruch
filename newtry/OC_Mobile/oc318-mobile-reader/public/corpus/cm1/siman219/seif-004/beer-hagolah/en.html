Chet) There, halachah 16. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam explains this as the case of "he marked the corners" mentioned in the Gemara. Many explanations were offered. Rambam understands it in this manner, while the related cases remain unresolved questions in the Gemara. Rambam rules by shuda dedayanei. The Kesef Mishneh asks that since these questions remained unresolved, Rambam should have ruled in all of them that the buyer did not acquire, because the seller is in possession of the land and the buyer bears the burden of proof. Possibly Rambam's text read that the law is shuda dedayanei.
Tet) In my humble opinion, Rambam understands this question according to the explanation Tosafot cite from Rabbenu Yitzchak: east and south means two adjacent directions forming an angle. The Rama inserted into Rambam's words the Tur's explanation, which follows Rashbam there. This may be because Tosafot challenge the alternative and conclude that Rashbam's explanation is primary.
