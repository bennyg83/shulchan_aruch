As they see fit. The Maggid Mishneh writes that all these cases were unresolved questions in the Gemara and remained in teiku, and that the Rambam therefore ruled according to the judges' discretion. Bedek HaBayit questions this, correctly. The Bach mistakenly understood the question as directed at the law in seif 2, but that is not so, because there the Gemara explicitly states judicial discretion. Here it does not. It therefore appears that the Rambam's phrase here means only according to the judges' assessment of the quantity he sold, unlike seif 2, where he writes that the judges should act according to whichever direction they believe the seller intended. So it appears to me. Shach.
