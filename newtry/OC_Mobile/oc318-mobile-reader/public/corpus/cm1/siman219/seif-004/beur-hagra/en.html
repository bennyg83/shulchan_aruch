(Supplement) Some say, etc. This resembles the discussions in the first chapter of Bava Kamma, in the first chapter of Yom Tov, and in chapter 5 of Bava Metzia, where Rav's silence before Rav Kahana and Rav Asi cannot be dismissed as indifference, since they were scholars who required his received tradition though not his reasoning. The first opinion maintains that the Gemara's phrase 'Rav concedes' shows that he did not retract. His silence indicates that the objection was difficult, but he still relied upon his received teaching. In Bava Kamma, however, the Gemara was compelled to say that Rav retracted because the wording 'my father concedes to me' implies a prior retraction. This also explains why the Gemara there needed an express halakhic ruling. Ramban struggled with the passage. See also the first chapter of Sukkah. (End of supplement.) See cited source 100.
