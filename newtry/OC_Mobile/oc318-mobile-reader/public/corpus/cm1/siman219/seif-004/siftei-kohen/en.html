The Maggid Mishneh writes: 'All of these were raised as unresolved questions in the Gemara, and our master ruled that the matter is subject to judicial discretion.' The Bedek HaBayit questions this, and properly so. This is unlike the Bach, who mistakenly understood the objection as directed to the law above in seif 2. That is incorrect, because the Gemara explicitly applies judicial discretion to the earlier law, unlike the present case. Nevertheless, it appears to me that the Rambam's intent here is not judicial discretion. Rather, the judges estimate the quantity that he sold him. This differs from seif 2 above, where he writes that they act according to whichever interpretation the judges believe his intention favored. This appears correct to me.
