Nichsei tzon barzel are the dowry, etc. Three types of property are specified here in which death or depreciation can occur. The same applies if she brought him land, which is also called nichsei tzon barzel, and he may not sell it because of the honor of her paternal family, as will be explained below in siman 90. But if she brought money or merchandise as a dowry, he may spend it and conduct business with it, and the profit or loss is his. For this reason, under Talmudic law he adds one third to its value. When she brings items that he may not sell, however, one deducts one fifth. See Maggid Mishneh at the beginning of chapter 16 regarding the distinction between dowry and ketubah. See above siman 65 and Responsa Rivash, siman 150.
