Even in a place where they give the money, etc. The Tur writes: not only in a place where they first write the document and then give the money, where one can say to the creditor, 'You should have withheld the money as payment of your debt,' but even where they first give the money and then write the document, the same applies.
You should have collected your debt so that the debtor would not take the money, spend it, and leave you without a source of repayment.I feared that you would not sell to me. Meaning, I feared that you would hide the property or sell it first to someone else, so that I would no longer be able to recover it from you. Therefore my participation in the transaction does not necessarily prove that no prior debt existed.
