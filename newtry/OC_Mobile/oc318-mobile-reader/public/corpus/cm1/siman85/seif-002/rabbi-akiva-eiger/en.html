[Shach, subparagraph 4] It is a migo against witnesses. Note: In my humble opinion, one can say that the case concerns authentication by comparison with another document. Such authentication is effective only because by Torah law a document does not require authentication, as the Tur writes in siman 49. Here, however, because of the proof that "if I owed you," and so forth, the defendant is believed by Torah law to say that the document is forged, so that even by Torah law authentication is required. Consequently, authentication through such comparison is ineffective.
