After the due date arrived... Rashi there, and as stated in the Gemara: if the due date had not arrived, etc., what is Admon's reason? The same applies here.
And even... See Beer HaGolah. Certainly according to Rashi, who holds that he is believed even to say the document is forged, there is no distinction whether the document contains a grant of credibility or not.
And some say... Perhaps he forged it even though it was validated, as stated in Bava Basra 52b and above at the end of siman 83. Likewise in chapter Get Pashut, where one says, "It is indeed my handwriting, however..." The first opinion holds that since forgery of this kind is uncommon, the document does not lose its force merely because of such an allegation.
