Seif 2. (He may say, "Had I...".) It appears that nowadays, when Torah monetary law lacks independent coercive force because non-Jewish courts exercise jurisdiction under legal systems that do not recognize these rules, the claim "Had I actually owed you, I would have acted differently" may not apply in the same manner. Only in a locale where the practice is to pay money first and afterward execute the deed, in a case where the lender sells property to the borrower, does this argument remain consistently available. This requires further study.
