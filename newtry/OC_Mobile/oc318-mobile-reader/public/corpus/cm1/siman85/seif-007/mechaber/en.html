Reuven became obligated to pay Shimon two hundred, while Shimon owed Reuven one hundred. Shimon had already litigated with Reuven over the one hundred and was held liable by beit din. Shimon said, 'You owe me two hundred; deduct this one hundred from it.' Reuven replied, 'I have not litigated with you over the two hundred, and I think I have evidence that exempts me from it.' If Shimon's document is authenticated, the law follows him.
