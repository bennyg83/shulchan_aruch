The Sema explains that the primary reason beit din generally does not collect from the property of minor orphans is concern that the father may have set aside funds for payment in advance. That concern applies when someone seeks to extract property from the orphans, but not where the claimant merely retains property already in his possession up to the amount of a debt that the father personally acknowledged, as indicated by the document in the claimant's hands. See Responsa Rivash 399 and the cited Responsa Raavach 113.
