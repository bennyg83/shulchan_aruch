The Tur, seif 3, writes: “If he says, ‘I intended to sell it and to repay myself from the proceeds’” This is difficult, because the Tur has already rejected this argument. Moreover, why must he add, “and to repay myself”? It appears that at first the Tur says that a person would not ordinarily make a loan and only afterward sell his property; he should sell immediately rather than first transferring money by way of a loan. The debtor may answer that he never intended a loan at all. From the outset he intended an immediate sale, taking the money as payment, but misled the creditor by presenting the transaction as a loan.
