Even if the heirs are minors... See Beer HaGolah. This is also clear from the incident of Rabbah bar Sharshom in Bava Basra 33a: "when I say that I will hold the money..." as Ramban explains there in his novellae in the name of Ri Migash, and as stated above in siman 110, seif 2. There the Shulchan Aruch writes that this applies even after their father's death, because he had taken possession without witnesses and therefore could have claimed, etc. Here, however, his possession is established by witnesses, and it is known that the heirs hold a document against him. Therefore the Shulchan Aruch writes here that this applies because he took possession during the father's lifetime, etc.
