It is not only where the heirs possess inferior land while the creditor possesses superior or intermediate land, despite the rule that one does not collect from heirs, etc. Even where they possess nothing, they may say, 'Our father left movable property of ours in your hands.' Under the geonic enactment this claim is available even regarding money. If the court had transferred land, the same result would follow even under Talmudic law.
