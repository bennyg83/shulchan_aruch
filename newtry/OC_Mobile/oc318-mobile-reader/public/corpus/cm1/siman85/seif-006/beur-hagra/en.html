Two... the purchaser... As stated at the beginning of chapter HaNizakin: one does not collect from encumbered property where unencumbered property is available. This is contrary to an opinion that treats the creditor's document as though it had already been paid from that earlier time. See Sefer HaTerumos. If that were correct, why, according to Rav Nachman, would each creditor collect from the other?
And if he collected from him... and said to him... As stated in Kesubos 92a: Rabbi Chiya bar Abba said, Reuven who sold, etc.; Rav said, if he is astute, etc. The same applies here, as stated in Bava Basra 44b. See there.
