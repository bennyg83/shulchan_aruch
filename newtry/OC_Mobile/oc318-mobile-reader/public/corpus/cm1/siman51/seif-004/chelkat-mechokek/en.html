That which they stipulated is presently in their possession. This limitation applies where he gives her a specific item by way of gift. An oral undertaking at the time of kiddushin cannot be stronger than a formal kinyan at another time. Just as a kinyan does not transfer an item not yet in one's possession, neither does the special efficacy of speech at the time of kiddushin. But if he personally obligates himself, or encumbers all his property for a specified sum, in a manner where a kinyan would be effective even though the money is not presently in his possession, the verbal undertaking at the time of kiddushin is likewise effective. So rules Rivash, siman 345.
