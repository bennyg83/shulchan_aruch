It is void. Because testimony partly void is entirely void, as explained in siman 36.
For it is possible that they signed, etc. This gives a reason even if the disqualified witnesses signed before the valid ones. All the more so if the disqualified witnesses appear at the end, the document is valid because it was already completed through valid witnesses, as explained in siman 45:12. Rama there cites those who disagree and invalidate where the disqualified witnesses are signed last.
