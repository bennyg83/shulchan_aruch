If there is clear testimony that they all sat together to sign, etc. It must also be known that the qualified witnesses were aware of the disqualification, such as where they were regularly together. Otherwise the deed is not invalidated merely from doubt, for perhaps they did not know. This is how the Beit Yosef understands the Rambam in light of the Rif’s responsum cited by the Baal HaIttur. The Rosh, however, holds that the testimony is void even if the qualified witnesses did not know of the disqualification. Both views are cited above at the end of siman 36.
