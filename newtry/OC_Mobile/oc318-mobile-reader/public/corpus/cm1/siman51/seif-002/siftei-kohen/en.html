He is believed with an oath. Meaning, with a heset oath, as is implied by the poskim and stated explicitly in Responsa Rashba cited by Beit Yosef at the end of siman 61. Rashba explains the reason: since there is only one witness, the debtor was not concerned to leave the document in the creditor’s possession, and the ordinary argument, ‘Why is your document in his hand if you paid?’ does not apply as it does with a full document. This is likewise stated by Maggid Mishneh, cited by Beit Yosef to this siman, seif 4. This also appears to be the view of the Tur and Rivash, siman 392, and is evident from Rashbam in Get Pashut and from Hagahot Ashri there in the name of Or Zarua. The other poskim also appear to agree. Bach rules likewise, and this is the primary ruling.
