If a debt document signed by one witness is produced and the borrower claims, 'I paid,' he is obligated to swear but cannot take the required oath, and therefore pays. If he demands that the creditor swear he was not paid, the creditor swears. If the borrower claims, 'I never borrowed,' he takes an oath contradicting the witness and is exempt. Some say that even when he claims payment, he is believed upon taking an oath.
