A deed that was delivered, etc. This ruling requires practical study. The Beit Yosef cites only the view that delivery witnesses validate such a deed, but the Rif, Ramban, Ran, Rashba, Raavad, Baal HaTerumot, Tosafot in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel, Rosh, Rashi, Mordechai in the name of Raavan, Baal HaIttur, Rav Hai Gaon, and Ritva hold that Rabbi Eliezer’s rule that delivery witnesses effect the document does not govern ordinary evidentiary deeds. Some distinguish a deed of gift, but several of these authorities reject even that distinction. Since many leading authorities hold that a deed with defective signatures cannot collect from encumbered property merely through delivery witnesses, the possessor may certainly plead that he follows those authorities. The matter therefore requires study in practice.
