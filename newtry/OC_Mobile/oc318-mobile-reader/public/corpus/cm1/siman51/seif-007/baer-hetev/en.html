That they. The Sema writes that the word 'they' is not literal. If other valid witnesses had signed the document, delivery witnesses would not be necessary. The Shach cites many authorities who disagree with the underlying ruling and hold that the principle that delivery witnesses establish an instrument applies only to gittin, whereas evidentiary monetary documents require signing witnesses. At the very least, therefore, the party in possession can certainly plead that he relies on those authorities. This requires study.
