A document that was delivered, etc. Regarding whether the names of the seller and buyer, or of the lender and borrower, are required in a document that is validated through witnesses to delivery, it appears from Perishah, siman 66, that they are required. Tosafot likewise write at the beginning of Perek HaNosei in Ketubot, s.v. aliba deBen Nanas, see there. However, Ran in the first chapter of Kiddushin implies that the names of the man and woman are required specifically for bills of divorce and kiddushin, because a bill of divorce requires a narrative identifying the parties, and kiddushin is compared to divorce. This implies that for other documents the names are not required.
