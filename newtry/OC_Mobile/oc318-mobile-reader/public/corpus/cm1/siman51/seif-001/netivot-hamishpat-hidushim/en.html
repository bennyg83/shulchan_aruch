He cannot say, "I paid." But he cannot collect from encumbered property. Shach. The Sema writes that he may even collect from encumbered property. See Beurim: where the borrower made separate kinyanim before each witness outside the other's presence, the creditor cannot seize encumbered property unless the borrower informed each witness that he was making the kinyan in the presence of both. If neither witness knew of the other's kinyan, encumbered property cannot be taken. Likewise, with a promissory note, if the borrower instructed each witness to sign separately outside the other's presence, he cannot collect from encumbered property unless he told each one that he was also instructing the second witness to sign. Nevertheless, the borrower is never believed to say, "I paid," because he himself knows there are at least two witnesses to the kinyan or document.
