A Document Containing Only One Witness, or One Disqualified Witness. Seven seifim. One written witness and one oral witness combine, where no formal act of acquisition occurred. If the oral witness says, 'I made a formal act of acquisition from him regarding this matter, but the lender never came and asked me to write,' the two combine to give the loan the status of a documented loan, and the borrower cannot say, 'I paid.' If the oral witness says the document was delivered in his presence, it is a complete document from which encumbered property may be collected. {Rama: This applies only where it is possible that delivery also occurred before the signatory, yielding two witnesses to delivery. If it was delivered before only one witness, then even two separate documents, each signed by one witness and delivered before one witness, do not combine to permit collection from encumbered property. See also siman 205.}
