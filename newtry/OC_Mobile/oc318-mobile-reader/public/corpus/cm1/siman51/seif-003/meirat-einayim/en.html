Only two witnesses are signed, etc. It is treated like a shard. The Mechaber carefully says that only two witnesses are signed. Then we presume they sat together to testify, so if one is disqualified the whole testimony is void and no oath is even required against the valid witness. If many witnesses are signed, however, the next seif presumes that only the valid witnesses sat together and the disqualified person signed without their knowledge. Beit Yosef writes this. See Hagahot Darkei Moshe.
Only one witness is signed. Meaning that the rule depends upon the dispute cited in the preceding seif.
This applies specifically in the manner, etc. Rama's gloss refers to the nearby dissenting view. It disagrees only where it is not certain that the witnesses sat together to sign. If it is certain that they did sit together, everyone agrees the valid testimony is void, as stated in the following seif.
