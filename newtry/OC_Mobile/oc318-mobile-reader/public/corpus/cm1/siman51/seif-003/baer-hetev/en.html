Like a shard. The Shach writes that the Mechaber appears to hold that the document does not even have the status of a document supported by one witness. In the Shach's view this is not reasonable. When Rambam says it is "like a shard," he means only that witnesses to delivery cannot validate a document whose text is internally defective through forgery. The document itself is ineffective as a formal instrument, but the valid witness's signature should still have the force of one witness for purposes of imposing an oath. The principle that a witness may have left space before his signature for legitimate reasons can also apply. The Shach discusses this at length and brings proofs from many poskim. He concludes that the Mechaber's phrase "and some say" does not represent a real dispute on this point; all agree to the stated law. The Mechaber himself reaches this conclusion in Bedek HaBayit. This is primary, contrary to the Ir Shushan and Sema.
Was voided. This means a case where the witnesses certainly did not sit together to sign. Then according to all opinions the valid witness's testimony is also voided, as explained in the following seif. Sema. See the Taz's discussion.
