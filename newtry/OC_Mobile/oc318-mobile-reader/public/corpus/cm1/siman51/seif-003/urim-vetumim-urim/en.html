Signed by two witnesses, etc. Specifically with only two witnesses we say that they certainly signed to testify and not merely to fill space. But where many witnesses are signed, we may say that relatives signed merely to fill the document and for honor, Sema. In Tumim I gave another reason, namely that here everyone agrees we are concerned that people might later validate the document from the signatures of those relatives, as with a tied document. See there. Shach challenges this and holds that even when Rambam says the document is like a shard and lacks the status of two witnesses, it still retains the force of one witness. In Tumim I established from Rosh that this is incorrect.
