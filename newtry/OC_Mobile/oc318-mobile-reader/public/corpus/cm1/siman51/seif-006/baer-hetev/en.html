Removed his interest. The Shach disputes this ruling. He writes that no posek says removal of an interest can cure this problem. Such removal is effective regarding oral testimony, where a witness can cease being interested before testifying. Here, however, the document has already become invalid under the rule that where part of a body of testimony is void, the entire testimony is void. Once the document itself has been invalidated, what can later removal of the relative's interest accomplish? The Mechaber's ruling is therefore difficult and requires study. See the Shach.
