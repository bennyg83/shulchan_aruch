Kol Bo writes concerning the enactment of Rabbeinu Gershom's ban: when one person summons another and the latter refuses, the claimant may interrupt communal prayer only in the synagogue where the recalcitrant party himself attends and prays. If he has interrupted three consecutive prayer services there, he may thereafter interrupt prayer in all synagogues. He may not interrupt the Shabbat or Yom Tov service unless he has already interrupted three times. In a matter concerning the community, however, he may interrupt even then. Wherever it appears that a great authority or beit din once existed, we presume that this ban was adopted there and adjudicate accordingly. Thus concludes the Sema.
