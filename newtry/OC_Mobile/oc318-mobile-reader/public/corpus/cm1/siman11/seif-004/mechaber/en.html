If a writ of excommunication was issued against a person for failing to appear and he says, 'I will come,' the writ is not torn up until he actually appears. If a writ was issued because he refused to obey an order of the beit din, as soon as he says, 'I accept upon myself to obey the order of the beit din,' the writ is torn up, and he pays the scribe's fee. {Rama: If a document declaring him recalcitrant was issued and he later claims that he did not hear of the summons, he is not believed, because an act of beit din becomes publicly known (Mordechai, beginning of the second chapter of Bava Kamma).}
