If she married the man suspected with her, she need not leave, even if she has no children. This wording implies that initially she may not marry him even if there had been no public rumor in the city. Where there are witnesses to seclusion and an indecent circumstance of the kind Rambam describes in chapter 2 of Hilchot Sotah, it is obvious that she may not marry him. Yet perhaps even a mere rumor is enough that, wherever there is scandal, one should distance himself from anything that gives rise to slander, similar to one suspected concerning a maidservant or gentile woman.
