One who did not, etc.; and if he did not, etc. As stated in Bava Kamma 112b, the court writes a writ of excommunication after the first thirty days. If he requested additional time for payment, the court grants it, but he is nevertheless placed under ban for failing to appear before beit din to request that time.
(Anthology) One who is unable, etc. This follows from Bava Kamma 113a, which distinguishes a person who refuses to appear for judgment. Such a person is rebellious, even if he is unable to pay. This is unlike one who obeys the court, as the Rashba explains there. Likewise, Bava Kamma 112b states that if he does not appear, the court writes a writ of excommunication, although it does not issue an enforcement order until ninety days have passed. The Gemara limits this to one who says, 'I will come,' etc. See the Rashba there.
