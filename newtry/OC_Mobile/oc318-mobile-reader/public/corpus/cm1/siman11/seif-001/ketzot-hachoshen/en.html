How are litigants summoned? The Shach cites Be'er Sheva concerning one who wishes to litigate with another person, while the other says, "Tell me your claims first. Perhaps I will admit them and pay." Be'er Sheva rules that the claimant need not reveal his legal arguments outside beit din. The Shach disagrees and holds that he must tell the other party what he is claiming before they descend to litigation.
