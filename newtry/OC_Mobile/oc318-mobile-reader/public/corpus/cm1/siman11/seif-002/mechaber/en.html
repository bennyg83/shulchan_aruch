If an officer says, 'So-and-so sent me in the name of one of the judges,' and the litigant refuses to appear, a writ of excommunication is not issued against him until the officer summons him in the name of all three judges. {Rama: If one of them is an expert judge, they accord him honor by mentioning only the expert's name (Nimukei Yosef, chapter Eilu Megalchin).} This applies when the officer went on a day not known as a regular court day. On a day known to be one on which the judges sit in judgment, everyone knows that all the judges have assembled; even if the officer comes in the name of only one, it is as though he came in the name of all three.
