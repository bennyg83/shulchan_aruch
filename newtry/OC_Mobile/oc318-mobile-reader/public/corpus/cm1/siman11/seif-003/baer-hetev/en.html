In the evening. The Sema writes that the Mechaber changed his wording here from seif 1. There he writes that the recalcitrant party is placed under excommunication "the next day," whereas here he writes "in the evening." In both places the intent is merely to exclude issuing the writ on that same day. "In the evening" means at night, when the following day, which was set as his deadline, begins. The wording was changed because each formulation teaches an additional point. See there.
