If a non Jew has a claim against a Jew and a Jew knows testimony in favor of the non Jew against the Jew, but he is the sole witness, and under the non Jewish law money would be extracted on the testimony of one witness, he may not testify for the non Jew. If he did testify, he is placed under nidui. Rama: He is not, however, required to reimburse the Jew, because he may say, "I testified truthfully." If it can be established that he testified falsely, he must pay. If the litigant admits that the testimony was true, the witness is not placed under nidui. If from the outset the non Jew specifically designated the Jew to serve as a witness, failure to testify would create a chillul Hashem, and he should testify for him.
