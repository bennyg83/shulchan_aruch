Seif 12: “Monetary judgments are decided,” etc. Beit Yosef challenges Rambam’s view that, by Torah law, collection is not made through a written document, since document validation is rabbinic and signed testimony is treated as already examined in beit din. The explicit statement in Gittin 3 is an even stronger question: by Torah law signed witnesses require no validation. Rambam can be understood as follows. Jeremiah’s verse, “Write in a document and seal,” proves documentary effectiveness in sale, while “by the mouth of two witnesses” excludes written testimony generally. Therefore a document is effective by Torah law only where Scripture specifically revealed it, such as sale and divorce. In those cases validation is only rabbinic. Other documentary uses are themselves rabbinic and accordingly require only rabbinic validation.
