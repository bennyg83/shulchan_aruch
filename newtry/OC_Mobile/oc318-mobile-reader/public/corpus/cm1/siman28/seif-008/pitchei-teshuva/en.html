When circumstances require administering an oath to them. For example, the court sees that people treat false testimony lightly but do not treat a false oath lightly. Sema. Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, siman 207, writes that this applies only where beit din itself has doubts about the witnesses. If the judges have no doubt, the opposing litigant cannot say, 'They are not credible to me until they swear.' See siman 92, Sema, seif katan 19.
