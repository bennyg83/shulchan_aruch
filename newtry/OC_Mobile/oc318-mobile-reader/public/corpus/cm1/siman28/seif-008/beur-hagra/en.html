(Collected note) He admitted in my presence. And he must say, "you are my witnesses." Tur. The Rambam and Shulchan Aruch, however, do not write this, following their view that a complete admission does not require it, as stated in siman 81. This is the meaning of "this supports, etc.": since it says "admitted," it implies a full admission; otherwise it is ineffective until he says, "you are my witnesses." (End of collected note.)
