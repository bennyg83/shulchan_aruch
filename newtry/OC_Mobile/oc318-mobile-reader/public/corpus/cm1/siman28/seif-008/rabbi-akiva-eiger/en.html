[Sema, subparagraph 23] One witness who supports him exempts him. Note: See Beit Yosef, who writes that if a gentile admits part of the claim, even though under our law the gentile would be obligated to swear and is unable to take the oath, it is nevertheless permitted for a Jew to testify for him, because under our law a supporting witness exempts from an oath. The Tur objects that where one is obligated to swear but cannot, a supporting witness does not exempt him, because the witness thereby directly determines a monetary result. The Shach below, siman 72, subparagraph 61, also alludes to this difficulty. See what I wrote in the margin below in siman 87 on Shach subparagraph 16.
