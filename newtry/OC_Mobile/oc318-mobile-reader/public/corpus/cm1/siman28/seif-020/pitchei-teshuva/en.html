From their mouths and not from their writing. Birkei Yosef, letter 16, writes that if a witness gives the beit din his testimony in writing and states, 'What is written here is what I testify,' this is considered testimony from his mouth. So writes Ritva at the end of the first chapter of Makkot, cited by Maharikash in this siman. See also Ginat Veradim, Choshen Mishpat, klal 5, siman 12.
