Seif 16: “Lest the property fall before orphans” The Sema’s citation of Maharshal is difficult, for the Rosh in Bava Kamma shows that beit din may attach funds even before the claim is fully clarified. Tur’s phrase that testimony is not received outside a person’s presence “to impose an unlawful loss on orphans” does not imply that others may be harmed unlawfully. Rather, the attachment in siman 73 is a rabbinic protective ordinance that departs from ordinary procedure to prevent loss. Rivah here follows the view that it is an ordinance, not strict law, and says that it is not applied where it would prejudice orphans. For the same reason, the demand that beit din take the funds into custody is rejected.
