In the name of Maharshal, in the Beit Yosef, s.v. “He further wrote there” Regarding the final chapter of Bava Kamma, through “if one witness supports him,” this requires study in light of the Rosh at the beginning of Bava Metzia, who rules that a single supporting witness does not exempt one already obligated to swear and unable to do so. See Tosafot Yom Tov in the chapter HaMafkid on the Mishna “One who rents a cow,” etc.
