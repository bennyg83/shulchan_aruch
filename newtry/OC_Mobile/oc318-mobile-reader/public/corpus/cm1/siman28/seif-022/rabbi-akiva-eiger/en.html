[Rama] And some say that whenever they sent. Note: Nevertheless, if one of the judges was a friend of one litigant, then although if they judged the case their judgment is valid, as above, siman 7, seif 4, still, in such a case, the litigant acted properly by refusing to go before them. If they then received testimony in his absence, it may be argued that the testimony is ineffective. See Responsa Maharam Alashkar, part 2, siman 24.
