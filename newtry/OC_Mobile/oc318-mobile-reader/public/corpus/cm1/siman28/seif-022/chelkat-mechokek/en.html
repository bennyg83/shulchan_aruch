Some say she is not betrothed. Although in Choshen Mishpat 66:29 the Shulchan Aruch rules that a debt transferred by ma'amad sheloshtan cannot be waived, here, because of the stringency of kiddushin, we are concerned for the view that even such a transferred debt can be waived. The woman therefore does not rely fully upon it, and the kiddushin do not take effect according to that view. Beit Yosef already explains that according to Rabbeinu Yerucham, even the authority who regards her as betrothed considers the kiddushin only rabbinic, because ma'amad sheloshtan does not acquire by Torah law.
