A person may have a ban proclaimed in the synagogue against anyone who knows testimony for him and fails to come and testify. {Rama: He cannot impose an oath upon them; rather, the verse states, 'If he does not testify, he shall bear his sin' (Responsa of the Rashba, siman 658). If the beit din considers it necessary for the circumstances to require them by oath to tell the truth, it may do so (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rosh). See above, siman 17, seif 3, and below, siman 71, seifim 7, 8, and 11. Some say that when a ban is imposed concerning testimony, even relatives must testify (Mordechai, beginning of chapter Shevuas HaEdus), and even a litigant himself must testify (Tur, siman 16). Others disagree (Responsa of the Rashba, siman 627, and Responsa of the Rosh, siman 20), and this is the primary view. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 232, regarding a king who ordered that a ban be imposed concerning testimony.}
