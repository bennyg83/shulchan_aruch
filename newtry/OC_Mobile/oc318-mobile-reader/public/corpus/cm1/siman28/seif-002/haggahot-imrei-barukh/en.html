(Sema, seif katan 9) For one witness can bring about a Torah oath. Note: See his words in seif katan 5. His wording implies that even where a single witness can only obligate an oath, if he suppresses his testimony he violates 'if he does not tell.' Kesef Mishneh, at the beginning of Hilchot Edut, disagrees and holds that in such a case 'if he does not tell' does not apply unless there is another witness to the matter. See below, siman 92, seif 6, in the Rama, and Tumim there, seif katan 7.
