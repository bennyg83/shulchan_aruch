To make them swear. Not only can he not compel the witnesses to verbally accept a ban or oath upon themselves, but the witnesses may explicitly say, "We do not accept the oath upon ourselves." A general ban imposed by beit din, however, takes effect even if they say that they do not accept it. See Beurim, seif katan 3, that beit din may impose such a ban only upon the public generally and not upon particular individuals.
