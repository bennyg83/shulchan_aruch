Urim asks that according to Rambam, who holds that for the oath concerning testimony it is unnecessary for the witnesses to answer "Amen," and so forth, why is this required here? This is no difficulty. Here they may state explicitly, "We do not accept the oath upon ourselves," in which case they are certainly exempt from the oath concerning testimony. According to Ramban, liability in the absence of an express acceptance requires silence.
