So too writes the Nimukei Yosef in the name of Tosafot, and the Rashba writes in a responsum that for one who is sick or not present in the city, testimony may be accepted even though they did not summon him, as the Rama writes in the gloss. This is unlike the Rosh's view according to Tosafot's approach, which the Tur cites, and as is written in Bedek HaBayit.
