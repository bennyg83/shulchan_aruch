If the litigant or the witnesses are ill, testimony may be received outside his presence. If the witnesses seek to travel overseas and the litigant was summoned but did not appear, or is not present in the city, their testimony may be received outside his presence. {Rama: It may not be received if he was not notified (Tur), provided there is no concern that the witnesses will depart before he can be notified, as explained immediately below. If the defendant is ill, testimony is not received outside his presence merely because his property may fall before minor heirs with whom the claimant will be unable to litigate, as explained in siman 110. Some say that whenever he was summoned and did not appear, testimony may be received outside his presence if proceedings were opened against him, or even if they were not, where he is required to litigate before those judges as explained in siman 14. Likewise, where the matter is urgent and testimony cannot be received later, as when the witnesses or claimant are ill and may die, or when the witnesses are traveling overseas and cannot wait for notification, testimony may be received even though he was not summoned. This accords with the ruling below in siman 140, seif 10. See also siman 40.}
