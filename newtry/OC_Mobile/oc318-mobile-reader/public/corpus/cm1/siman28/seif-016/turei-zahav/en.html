In the Beit Yosef, s.v. “Testimony is not received” through “even if it was Rabbi Yirmiyah,” etc. Tosafot in the chapter Shum HaYetomim do not write this way, as the Sema cites in siman 110, seif katan 13. Yet one who reads their words carefully in the chapter HaGozel will explain them consistently with what they wrote in Shum HaYetomim. This is unlike Maharshal in Yam Shel Shlomo; the Beit Yosef’s view appears correct.
