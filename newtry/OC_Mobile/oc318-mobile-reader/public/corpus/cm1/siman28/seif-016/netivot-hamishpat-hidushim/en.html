To adjudicate before those judges. The Rama's statement that "they began before him" means that the parties already began presenting their claims, at which point one can no longer say, "I will not litigate before you, only before another beit din in this city." The Rama follows his view above in siman 14, seif 1, that in the present time one cannot insist on going to a greater beit din.
