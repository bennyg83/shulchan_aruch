[Shach, continuation] And so wrote the Rivash. Note: They proved this from the law of rabbinic interest in Yoreh Deah, siman 161, seifim 2 and 3. See also Nimukei Yosef, chapter Eizehu Neshech, in the discussion of a mortgage in a place where the borrower may redeem it, where he cites the Rashba and Ran. It is clear there that where one is obligated to satisfy the laws of Heaven, seizure is effective. Thus this law depends upon a major dispute among the Rishonim. As a practical matter, one must consider whether the party in possession can say kim li in accordance with those who hold that seizure is effective. See Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 4 of Hilchos Malveh, at the end of his discussion, and Tumim in the laws of seizure.
