Seif 5, in the gloss “They send to him,” etc. Rabbeinu Yerucham says that a distinguished scholar need not come before an inferior beit din; they send to him, and he testifies before a beit din or its agent. The Sema interprets this as sending three men. But the phrase “or before their agent” shows that one emissary suffices, because testimony given to one acting as beit din’s agent is not treated as testimony from hearsay. Rabbeinu Yerucham’s wording is admittedly awkward and his book contains many scribal errors, but the legal conclusion is that three need not be sent.
