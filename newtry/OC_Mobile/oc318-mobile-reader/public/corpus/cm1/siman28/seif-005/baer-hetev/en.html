A side. The Sema writes that it is obvious that if beit din wishes to permit a woman to remarry because they believe her husband has died, while this witness knows he is alive, he must testify. Even the reverse is true, for there is also an aspect of preventing prohibition: if another man later betroths her, people must not disregard his kiddushin. Otherwise a stringency may lead to a leniency. This is contrary to Beit Yosef, who writes that in such a case he need not testify.
