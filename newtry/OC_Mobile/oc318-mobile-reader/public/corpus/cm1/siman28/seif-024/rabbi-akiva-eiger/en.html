[Shulchan Aruch] Three expert judges are required. Note: Three must sit together as a tribunal. If one received testimony separately and another separately, it is ineffective, even according to the second opinion below, siman 46, seif 25, because there they were lenient only regarding authentication of documents. Maharit, Responsa Orach Chaim, siman 43.
Three expert judges are required. Note: Responsa attributed to the Ramban, siman 112, writes that a single expert judge who is authorized to adjudicate alone may also receive testimony alone. See there. If one judge is learned and two are laymen and they received testimony, see Tumim, siman 4, and Responsa Maharchash, siman 18, at length.
