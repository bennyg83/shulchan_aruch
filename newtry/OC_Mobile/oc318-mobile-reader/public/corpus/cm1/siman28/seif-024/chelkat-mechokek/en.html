And he transferred the document to her by writing and delivery. Even by delivery without writing there is reason to say she is at least doubtfully betrothed, because perhaps the paper itself is worth a perutah in Media, as explained below, siman 31:3. Although the paper technically belongs to the borrower and, if the lender waives the debt, the woman should have to return the document to the borrower, as Ran writes and Rama cites in Choshen Mishpat 66:23, other authorities disagree and hold that the purchaser need not return the document to the borrower.
