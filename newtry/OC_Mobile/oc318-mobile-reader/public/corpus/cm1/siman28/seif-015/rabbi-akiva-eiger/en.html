[Shulchan Aruch] And if the witness is a Torah scholar. Note: Knesset HaGedolah, glosses to the Tur, subparagraph 63, writes that even nowadays we apply the law of a Torah scholar in this respect, contrary to Responsa Rama. Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 147, agrees. He also concludes there that the rule that if the litigant reminds the witness and he then remembers, he may not testify, applies specifically when the litigant himself reminds him. If a relative of the litigant reminds him, the testimony is valid. A wife and children are treated like the litigant himself.
