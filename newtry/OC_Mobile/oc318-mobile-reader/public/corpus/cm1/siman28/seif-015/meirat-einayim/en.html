We do not receive testimony outside the presence of the litigant. This is derived from the verse, "and warning was given to its owner," which the Sages expound to require that the owner come and stand regarding his ox. See below, siman 110:9 in the gloss, where clear matters may be received outside his presence, and siman 140:10 for additional rules concerning receiving testimony outside the litigant's presence.
