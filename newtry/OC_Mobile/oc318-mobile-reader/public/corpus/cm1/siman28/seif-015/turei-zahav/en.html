In the Beit Yosef, s.v. “Rambam wrote” “If the litigant is a Torah scholar,” etc., through “he said, ‘I make the calculation for myself,’” etc. According to this, it is difficult how the Gemara proves from Rav Asi that a Torah scholar may testify from memory. Perhaps that case was different because he expressly said before us, “I make the calculation for myself.” See Bach and Benei Shmuel.
