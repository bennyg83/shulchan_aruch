The Tur, as Mordechai writes in the name of Riva from the rule that hazamah outside the witnesses' presence is not valid hazamah, Ketubot 20a. So too the Rosh in his rulings and in Responsa, end of rule 41, and the Rivash, who writes that this is also in the Yerushalmi and that the Rashba and Ramah likewise rule so.
Maharshal writes similarly in Responsa, siman 33.
