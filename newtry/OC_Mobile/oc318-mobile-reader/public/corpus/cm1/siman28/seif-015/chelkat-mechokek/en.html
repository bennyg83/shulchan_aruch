This applies specifically where she does not know the amount of the deposit. Bach cites Ritva that even if she knows the amount, if he says merely, 'Be betrothed to me with the deposit I have in your possession,' this means with whatever remains in her hand, whether much or little. But if he says, 'with everything I deposited with you,' and the deposit is found deficient by even one perutah, she is not betrothed. Bach writes that in practice one should be stringent like Ritva and treat her as doubtfully betrothed.
