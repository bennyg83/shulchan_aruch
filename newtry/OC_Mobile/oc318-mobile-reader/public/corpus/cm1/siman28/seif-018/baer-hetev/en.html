To exempt. Although in siman 108, seif 16, the Mechaber writes that where a minor heir possesses his father's promissory note and a receipt against it dated after the father's death is produced, the note is not torn up even if witnesses remember the payment, because testimony is not accepted outside the litigant's presence, that case is different. Although the testimony would ostensibly exempt the debtor, it actually operates to extract property from the minor by removing and destroying his note. See Responsa Ri Trani, part 3, siman 32. Shach.
