To exempt. In siman 108, where a minor heir possesses his father's promissory note and a receipt dated after the father's death appears, we do not tear up the note even if witnesses support the receipt because testimony may not be accepted outside the litigant's presence. The Shach answers that there the opposing party seeks to take the note from the heir and is therefore the claimant. Maharit answers that a defendant's testimony may be accepted outside the claimant's presence only after the claimant has already sued him. If no claim was yet brought, testimony is not accepted outside his presence. Regarding minor orphans, even if beit din or an administrator brings a claim on their behalf, it is treated as though no claim had yet been brought, and testimony for the defendant is not accepted outside their presence.
