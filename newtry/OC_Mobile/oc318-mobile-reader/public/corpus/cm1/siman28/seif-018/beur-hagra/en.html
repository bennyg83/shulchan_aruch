No. Bava Metzia 32a, explaining that laypersons require it because of witnesses.
(Collected note) No, etc. See siman 110, seif 7, and likewise if he robbed, etc.; see Beer HaGolah there. The reason is that the property remains in the presumptive possession of the person robbed, like the Gemara's distinction there between one who has ancestral chazakah and one who does not. From this there is some proof for what was written that the Rambam holds one who robbed his fellow in the presence of witnesses must return it in the presence of witnesses, as written at the end of siman 361, and all the more so in such a case. (End of collected note.)
