In the Beit Yosef, s.v. “If they received the testimony” outside the litigant’s presence, etc., through “in the name of the later great authorities,” etc. This does not appear correct. The source explicitly says that if the witnesses died or went overseas, judgment may be rendered on testimony received outside the litigant’s presence. It follows that if the witnesses are still available, the earlier reception is not accepted even after the fact, as a penalty for having received it improperly. See, however, Radakh, bayit 26, chamber 2, who is uncertain whether they may return and testify; see also Mahari ben Lev at the end of volume 3.
