And even if he returned the document to her. Tur, in the name of the Ramah, explains the reason: because at the moment of kiddushin he did not mention the document as the object with which he was betrothing her, but simply betrothed her generally, it is like one who betroths with the debt alone. If he expressly mentioned the document, however, the Ramah distinguishes and holds she is betrothed, as explained below in seif 8.
