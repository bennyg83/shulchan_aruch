Seif 8: “They send everyone outside” Rif’s better reading is: “They send everyone outside and examine each witness separately.” On that reading, there is no special insistence that unrelated bystanders leave during reception of testimony, and daily practice permits them to remain. Tur and Mechaber in siman 18, who say that after receiving testimony everyone is sent out, derive this from the baraita that the witnesses are removed before the judges deliberate, so they do not hear who favors liability or acquittal. Naturally other people are then absent as well; the baraita mentions witnesses because they were certainly present. The Rosh reads “they send everyone outside.”
