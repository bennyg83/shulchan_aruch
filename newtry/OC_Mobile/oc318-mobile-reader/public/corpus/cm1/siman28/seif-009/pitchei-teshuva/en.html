We place him under a ban. See Birkei Yosef above, siman 18, entry 3, who asks why the witness is placed under a ban. Let him say: I truthfully know that this Jew owes the non Jew. Either the Jew would have come to a Jewish beit din and refused to swear, in which case he would have paid the non Jew and my testimony caused him no loss; or he would have sworn falsely, in which case I benefited him by causing him to pay rather than swear falsely. He cites Maharitz, siman 102, who explains that since the Torah did not grant credibility to this witness to impose payment, and the defendant is liable only to swear, the witness must allow him even to swear falsely and may not seek to outsmart his Creator.
