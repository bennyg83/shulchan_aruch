Seif 15: “They may return and testify,” etc. Responsa Maharshal 11 and Responsa Mahari ben Lev say that once witnesses testified outside the litigant’s presence, later repetition before him is ineffective. This has no foundation in Gemara or early authorities. On the contrary, Rif and Baal HaItur, cited by Rama in seif 21, imply that the witnesses themselves may later be relied upon, and Beit Yosef rules so in Rivash’s name. Maharam Alshakar likewise rules this way. This is the primary law, as Rama states here based on Rabbi Menachem HaArukh.
