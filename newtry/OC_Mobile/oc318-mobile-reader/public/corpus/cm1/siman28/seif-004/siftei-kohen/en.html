If they sue him. See the responsa of the Rama, siman 52, folio 116c.
Where money is not extracted on the testimony of one witness, they may testify. The Rosh rules that even regarding an eminent person, from whom money is extracted on his statement alone because his stature makes evasion difficult, he may testify. These are the words of the Sema. Yam Shel Shlomo writes that this is a scribal error in the Rosh and that the text should read: “He should not testify, but should pay from his own pocket.” Nevertheless, if he did testify, he is not placed under a ban.
