If a Jew has a claim against a non Jew who denies it, and the non Jew has one Jewish witness, that witness may testify for him if the non Jew demands his testimony. Rama: Certainly two witnesses may testify, since even under Jewish law the defendant would be liable on their testimony. Likewise, in a place where money is not extracted on the testimony of one witness, a single witness may testify. Two relatives are treated like one witness, since under our law money is not extracted on the testimony of one witness.
