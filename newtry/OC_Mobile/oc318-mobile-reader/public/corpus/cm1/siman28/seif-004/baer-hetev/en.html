To testify. The Rosh rules that even an important person whose testimony alone would cause money to be extracted, where his status makes it difficult for him to evade the request, is permitted to testify. Thus concludes the Sema. Yam Shel Shlomo writes that this is a scribal error in the Rosh and the text should read that he should not testify but should pay from his own pocket. Nevertheless, if he did testify he is not placed under a ban. Shach.
