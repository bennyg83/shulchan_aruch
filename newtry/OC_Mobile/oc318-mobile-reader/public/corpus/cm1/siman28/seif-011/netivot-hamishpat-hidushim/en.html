Unlike those who validate it. Regarding a Torah scholar, the practice is that he may send written testimony to beit din, following the validating opinion. Sema. Even where testimony ordinarily may not be accepted outside the litigant's presence, he may send the written statement to beit din and beit din reads it before the litigant. Before the document reaches beit din, the witnesses may retract. See Netivot, seif katan 6.
