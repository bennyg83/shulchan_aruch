Contrary to those who validate it. The Sema writes that since many poskim accept the validating view, which is Rabbeinu Tam's opinion, the practice is to rely on it for a Torah scholar. We do not compel him to appear personally before beit din, but allow him to send written testimony. See Tumim, where I wrote that this applies only where testimony need not be received in the presence of the litigant. Where testimony must be received in the litigant's presence, a written statement is ineffective. See there. In a matter of issur resembling a capital case, all agree that writing is ineffective and the witness must testify orally. See Tumim.
