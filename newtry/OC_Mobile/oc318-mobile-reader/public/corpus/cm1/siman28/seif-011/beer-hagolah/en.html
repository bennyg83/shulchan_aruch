e) There in Tosafot, which Rabbeinu Yitzchak heard from Rabbeinu Tam. The Sema writes that he saw halachic authorities follow the practice of not troubling a Torah scholar to appear, but rather sending to him that he should write the testimony in his own handwriting and send it to beit din. This appears correct to him, since according to Rabbeinu Tam and those who agree with him, this is permitted even for one who is not a Torah scholar.
