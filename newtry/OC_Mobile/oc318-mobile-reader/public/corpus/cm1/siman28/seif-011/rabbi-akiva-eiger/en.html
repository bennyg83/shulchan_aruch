[Shulchan Aruch] To go and testify. Note: Responsa Masat Moshe, Choshen Mishpat, siman 63, writes that this applies specifically where no oath is involved, such as when one claims from orphans on the basis of witnesses. But where an oath is involved, it is considered a case of separating someone from a prohibition, so that he not swear falsely. This requires study as a matter of practical halachah.
