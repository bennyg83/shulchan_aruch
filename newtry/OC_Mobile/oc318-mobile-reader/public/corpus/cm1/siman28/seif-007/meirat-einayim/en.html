The power of false testimony. A false witness comes from hunger and a word in the world is better at the time, and will add to it a presidential dictate and a rain does not (for) a man praises a false gift and dictates a shattered sword and a witty arrow (for) a man answers his cry to a lie:
And the shame of one who testifies falsely in this world and the next, and that he is despised in the eyes of the one who hired him. This exact wording appears neither in Rambam nor Tur. Rambam writes that they inform him of the disgrace of false testimony in this world and the World to Come, without separately adding that he is despised by the person who hired him. Nor does Rambam separately explain what the disgrace in this world consists of. In truth, that disgrace is precisely that he is contemptible even in the eyes of the person who hired him. This is implied in the Gemara at the beginning of Zeh Borer from the verse describing the hired false witnesses against Navot as "worthless men." Even those who hired them called them worthless.
