For anything not done in his presence is like testimony outside beit din. See Responsa Avodat HaGershuni, siman 3, citing Responsa Raanach, siman 3, that this applies only when the absent litigant does not accept the earlier testimony. If he says, 'Although I was not present, I accept that testimony,' then in his opinion the original testimony is valid. The rule that testimony is not received outside a party's presence was stated for the protection of the party against whom the testimony operates. The party who benefits from the testimony may accept it even though he was absent. Avodat HaGershuni agrees; see there.
