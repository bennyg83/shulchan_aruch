Before a beit din inferior to him. See Responsa of Rabbi Moshe Rothenburg, Choshen Mishpat, siman 2, who discusses whether, when one of the judges is greater than the scholar but the others are lesser than he, the scholar is obligated to go and testify. He concludes at length that even if only one judge is greater than he, or equal to him, although the other two are lesser, he must go. Where the litigant himself is a great person, according to the Rambam, who rules like Rav Yosef in Sanhedrin 19b, he must likewise go to testify. From the words of the other authorities it appears that they disagree with this; see there.
