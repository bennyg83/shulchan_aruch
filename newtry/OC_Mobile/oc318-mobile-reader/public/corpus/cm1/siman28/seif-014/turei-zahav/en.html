In the Beit Yosef, s.v. “Although one witness, where he does not contradict him,” through “whether it rests upon him or does not rest upon him” Benei Shmuel, citing Baal HaItur and Ritva, writes that even according to Rav Chisda, where the matter was not one the witness was expected to remember, such as testimony about possession, Rabbi Meir’s rule remains accepted: testimony may be recalled even after sixty years. Rav Chisda’s position was rejected only where the matter naturally rested upon the witness’s attention. Where it did not, we follow Rabbi Meir as Rav Chisda himself explains.
