The Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilkhot Edut. The Kesef Mishneh writes that from the Rambam's words one can derive a fine answer to Tosafot's question in Yevamot from the statement, 'Let him write another document,' Bava Kamma 98b. He nevertheless writes that this requires study from the rule that validation of documents is rabbinic, Ketubot 21a, which implies that by Torah law signed testimony is treated as though examined in beit din.
