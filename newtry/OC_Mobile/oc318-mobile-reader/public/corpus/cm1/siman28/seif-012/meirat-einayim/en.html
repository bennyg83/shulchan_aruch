In testimony contained in a document. This leniency applies specifically to a complete legal document, permitted for the sake of social order so that people will not stop lending out of fear that the witnesses may die or travel overseas. If witnesses merely wrote their statements in notebooks, that does not help, even according to Rabbeinu Tam and those who allow written testimony, when the witnesses are unavailable. They are then like mute persons incapable of giving the required oral testimony. See Hagahot Darkei Moshe and simanim 46 and 146. Hagahot Mordechai to Kiddushin writes that in monetary law there is a received tradition validating written testimony like other testimony.
