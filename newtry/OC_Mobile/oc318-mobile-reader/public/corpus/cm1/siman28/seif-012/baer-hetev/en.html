In a document. The Sema writes that only a formal document was permitted for the sake of social order. If witnesses merely wrote their statements in their private notebooks, that does not suffice even according to Rabbeinu Tam. The Shach discusses the matter at length and proves from several Talmudic sources that written testimony in a document has Torah force, since capital cases, fines, and gittin may be adjudicated on the basis of documentary testimony. Hagahot Mordechai likewise writes that in monetary law it is a halachah transmitted to Moshe at Sinai that written testimony is valid like other testimony.
