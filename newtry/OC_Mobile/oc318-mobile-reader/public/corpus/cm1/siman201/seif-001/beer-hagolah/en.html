Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 6, from the statement of Rav Pappi in the name of Rava concerning situmta, Bava Metzia 74a.
Bet) Before the price is fixed he certainly does not acquire, as explained in the preceding siman.
Gimel) This is the conclusion of the Gemara there. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Rashba: We learn from here that accepted commercial custom can override the ordinary halachic mechanism in comparable monetary matters, because acquisition and transfer in monetary matters can be governed by established custom. This accords with what is written in the following seif.
