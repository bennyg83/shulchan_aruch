(b) A column called his father the Rashi there:
There, seif 2, in the name of Rabbenu Chananel. The Rosh likewise writes so in his name. It does not specifically require striking palms; rather, they may strike hands or arms against one another. The Bach writes that nowadays there is a custom of handing over a key, and most commonly of giving a handshake. Nevertheless, he says that such custom applies only to movable property, not to acquiring land. This is implied by the Tur's wording that there is another method by which movable property is acquired. For land there is no such custom, and therefore even if they shook hands it has no effect.
