The key. The Sema writes that siman 192 explains that land is not acquired merely by delivery of a key. Thus the meaning here cannot be that he acquires the room by receiving the key and then the room acquires the merchandise for him. Rather, merchants customarily transfer ownership of the merchandise itself by delivering the key of the room in which it is stored. The Beit Yosef says likewise. One may also answer that siman 192 discusses the strict law, whereas here the discussion is commercial custom. See Responsa Rama, siman 19.
