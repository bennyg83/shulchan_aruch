[Shulchan Aruch] The merchants. Note: See Responsa Chut HaShani, siman 4, and Sema above, siman 197, seif katan 16. See Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 8, siman 1, who writes that situmta applies specifically to a matter that is already in existence. With respect to something not yet in existence, where no valid kinyan applies, we do not find that commercial custom can cause it to be regarded as a valid acquisition. See there. In my opinion, Responsa HaRosh, rule 12, siman 2, makes clear that situmta is effective even for something not yet in existence. See also Responsa Maharshal, siman 38. This requires study.
