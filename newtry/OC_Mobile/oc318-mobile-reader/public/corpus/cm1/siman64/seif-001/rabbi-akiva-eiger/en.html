[Shach, subparagraph 3] He is believed to say, "I seized them as collateral." Note: According to the Mechaber, who always holds that he is believed only where there are witnesses to the deposit and witnesses who saw the item in his possession, there is likewise no migo regarding our bearer notes. The practical difference lies in the basis of the seizure. Even where witnesses establish that the owner demanded the item from him and he refused to return it, so that this is a proper seizure, ordinary seizure helps only to retain possession, whereas with a bearer note it can even enable collection.
