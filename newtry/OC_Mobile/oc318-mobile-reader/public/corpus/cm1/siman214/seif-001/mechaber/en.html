What is included when a house is sold without specification. It contains thirteen seifim.
One who sells an item that has appurtenant uses does not sell those appurtenances unless he expressly includes them. Thus, one who sells a house does not sell the gallery or annex surrounding the house, even if it opens into the house. Rama: Some say this remains so even if he sold the property by its outer boundaries. This applies when the gallery is four cubits wide or more. If it is narrower, it is included in the house. Rama: This is so even if it does not open into the house. An upper story above the house that opens into it through an aperture in the ceiling, meaning an opening in the roof of the house that forms the floor of the upper story, is included in the house. Rama: These rules concern the actual time of a sale or gift. If a person merely acknowledges that he sold or gave his house to another, everything is included, because an acknowledgment need state only the general transaction and not every detail.
