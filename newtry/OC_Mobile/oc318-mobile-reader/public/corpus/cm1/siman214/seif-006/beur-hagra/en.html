(Supplement) But one who purchases, etc. This is unlike Rashbam, whose explanation Ramban strongly challenges. Ramban reads the sugya like Rabbenu Tam. Rambam, however, follows Rashbam in part. The apparent contradiction is resolved by distinguishing construction directly on the existing roof from construction upon separate columns. An express condition allows rebuilding the original upper structure if it falls, but not imposing a new burden. Rambam's wording remains somewhat difficult. (End of supplement.)
