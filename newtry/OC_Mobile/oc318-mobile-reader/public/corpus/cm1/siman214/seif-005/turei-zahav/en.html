(Seif 12.) He gives him the smaller one, etc. This means that he is in any event obligated to give him a house rather than an upper story. Although the halacha follows the Sages who disagree with Rabbi Meir and hold that an upper story is included in the term “house,” when one says, “a house in my house,” his wording indicates an item from the superior category. Within that superior category, however, the holder of the document has the weaker hand, and the grantor may give him the least valuable house among them. This also appears to be the Rama's view, unlike the Beit Yosef, who treats this as a dispute between the Rambam and Rabbeinu.
