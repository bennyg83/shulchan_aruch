Attachment by pegs is not considered attachment. See Baer HaGolah, letter 100, through the statement that because the issue was unresolved, the buyer did not acquire. See Responsa Shivat Tzion, siman 110, concerning mezuzot fixed in doorways, which I cited in Pitchei Teshuva to Yoreh De'ah, siman 291, seif katan 7. That responsum concludes that the foregoing technical analysis is necessary in other countries, but in his country the civil law provides that when a house is sold, everything attached to it by mortar or pegs belongs to the house and is conveyed with it even if the deed does not say so expressly. Therefore the seller has no claim against the purchaser regarding the mezuzot, because the law of the kingdom is law. See the Shach and above, siman 73, seif katan 39.
