(Seif 11, the legs of the bed.) Rabbeinu wrote concerning the Rambam's words, “I do not know what the bed-frame blocks have to do with the sale of a house.” The Sema appears to understand that Rabbeinu's objection is directed at the Rambam, namely that he should not have recorded them here. In my humble opinion, however, Rabbeinu's objection is directed only at the Gemara. Rabbi Yirmeyah asked what the law is concerning the blocks beneath the legs of a bed, and the Rambam explained that the question concerns the sale of a house, whether these blocks are sold with it like the window frames mentioned immediately above. Rabbeinu therefore asked what connection this has to the sale of a house. Rabbeinu agreed instead with the Rashbam that the question concerns the sale of the bed itself. This also appears from the Darkei Moshe, which identifies this as the principal difference between the Rashbam and the Rambam.
