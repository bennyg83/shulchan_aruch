Items attached with mortar. See Responsa Pnei Yehoshua II, siman 98, concerning a kettle fixed into a stove, a large brewing cauldron, and a still used for distilling spirits, each attached with mortar over a hollow fire chamber. The question was whether such fixtures are included in the sale of a house. He answers that they plainly are not. First, established local practice is controlling in matters of this kind, as the Rambam rules and the Tur cites in siman 215. Second, even under the default legal rules, he concludes that they are not included. See there.
