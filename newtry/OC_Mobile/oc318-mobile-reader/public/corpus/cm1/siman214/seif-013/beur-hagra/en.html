Or he accepted, etc. This phrase should precede “and likewise, etc.” The Rambam merely stated the ordinary sequence of events. Taz.
A large house, etc. He omitted the rule of a small house, following Rabbeinu Meir’s explanation that the alternative version treats even that statement as Rabbi Yehudah’s view, referring to a bridal chamber according to Rabbi Akiva. Rabbi Akiva does not disagree with Rabbi Yehudah regarding a cattle stall according to this latter version, and the Rambam regularly follows the latter version.
(Collected note) Except, etc. Following Rabbi Shimon in the Mishnah, modeled on the structure of the Heichal, as stated at the end of Middos chapter 4. (End of note.)
