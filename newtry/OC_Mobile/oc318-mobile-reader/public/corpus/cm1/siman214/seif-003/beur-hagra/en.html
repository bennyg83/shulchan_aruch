One who sells, etc. This entire seif is Rambam's language. He understands 'tunnels' as spaces above, which explains the phrase 'to the height of the heavens.' Tosafot therefore had to explain the passage concerning a roof. The reference to ceilings and plaster layers likewise belongs to the upper story. See simanim 155 and 164.
And also the roof, etc. Without express inclusion he does not acquire the roof, even if the deed says 'depth and height,' because the Mishna's statement that these words are insufficient also applies to the roof. This is the view of Ri, Ramban, and Rosh, unlike Rashbam.
(Supplement) And also the roof, etc. Rashbam was pressed by the Gemara's challenge from the roof and by the omission of any explanation of the phrase 'to the height of the heavens.' Rambam's interpretation through upper tunnels resolves the difficulty. (End of supplement.)
But anything that is not, etc., and even, etc. The Gemara says, 'Come and hear: not the roof, etc., because it is ten handbreadths high.' It follows that if it is not ten handbreadths high, it is included even without express wording.
