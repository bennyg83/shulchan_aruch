So writes the Maggid Mishneh there in the name of Rav Yosef ibn Migash, and so writes the Tur there.
He explains there that we rule like Rabbi Akiva, there in the Mishnah, 64a, that he retains a right of way.
He explains there that since we rule like Rabbi Akiva that one who reserves a pit or cistern must acquire a right of way, the recipient of a gift acquires only the rights that belonged to the owner.
