But, etc. Rosh reconciles the passages. Once he says 'a house from among my houses,' he certainly means a house. Menahot's statement that even an inferior upper chamber may be given means the least valuable unit among the houses, not literally any upper chamber. This differs from Rambam and Shulchan Aruch, who allow an upper chamber. Nevertheless the passage follows the Sages, because 'a house from among my houses' is stated as a novelty.
