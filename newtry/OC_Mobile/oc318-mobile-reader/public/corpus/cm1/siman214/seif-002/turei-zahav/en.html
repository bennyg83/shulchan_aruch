(Seif 4, in the gloss: So that he would lose his house.) This implies that the rule applies specifically where the loss of his house as a whole is at stake and there is no other possible arrangement. If he has another place in which to construct a privy, even though this will entail additional expense, we are not concerned. It is comparable to the access route that the seller must purchase for the buyer to reach a pit or cistern, as stated in seif 2. This appears correct.
