[Shulchan Aruch] The depth and the height. Note: If a deed states that he gave him the upper story "from the depth of the earth to the height of the heavens," and the giver claims that the courtyard below was not included in the gift, the words "from the depth of the earth" may be a scribal error. If the giver held possession for three years in a manner that gives him a migo, it is possible that this is not considered such a strong migo against witnesses, because perhaps the scribe erred and called the floor of the upper story "the depth of the earth." Had the courtyard been included, the deed should have expressly mentioned the courtyard. In every similar case, the beit din must investigate carefully and ascertain the truth of the matter. Rashba, siman 880.
