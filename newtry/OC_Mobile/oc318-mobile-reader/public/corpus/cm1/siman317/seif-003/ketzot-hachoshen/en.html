Even on the last day. The Gemara compares a tenant's payment dispute on the due date to a worker's claim for wages. Ritva asks that an employer has an extra reason to pay promptly because of the prohibition against delaying wages. According to what I wrote in siman 339, rental of a house can also fall under that prohibition because a house has the law of detached property later attached, like rental of utensils. The comparison is therefore sound.
