Or by witnesses. Whether the rental was merely witnessed without a kinyan, or there was a kinyan but the witnesses are unavailable, the tenant's later plea of payment is treated according to the laws of rental. The absence of the rental document does not weaken him as in an ordinary loan. A rental document is needed to prove the tenant's possessory right, not primarily for collection, and therefore even after rent is paid the tenant still needs it. Its absence does not itself imply payment.
