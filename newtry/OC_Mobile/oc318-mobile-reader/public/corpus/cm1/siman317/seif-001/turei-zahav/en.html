(Seif 1: an independent claim.) Meaning, after he now pays this debt. The Sema, seif katan 3, distinguishes between a loan and rental. This is unfounded. On the contrary, the poskim equate a loan and rental in all these matters. Furthermore, if his concern is some possible payment issue, why should the tenant not be believed to say, "I paid all the past rent for the entire year"? And the landlord cannot presently remove him, so what exactly does the tenant fear? The Sema's principal difficulty is addressed by what I wrote in siman 78, seif 2, explaining our master's statement in the Rosh's name. The Rama's words there, however, require study, as I wrote there.
