Whether it was without witnesses. Although in siman 78 we require witnesses to the fixing of a due date, because otherwise the debtor is believed through a migo, here there is no such migo. The tenant does not wish to claim that the term has already expired, because he wants to continue living in the house and does not wish to diminish his right of residence. Nor does he have a migo to say that they fixed a lower rent, because a claim of payment exempts him from the entire amount. If, however, he claims that he paid part, it would at first seem that where there are no witnesses he should be believed through a migo that they fixed the rent only at the amount he admits still owing. Perhaps, though, because the claim arises within the fixed term, if he were to say that they agreed only to that lower rent the landlord would be believed sufficiently to remove him from the house, as explained immediately below.
