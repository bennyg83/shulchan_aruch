Whether, etc. Meaning that even when the rental was recorded in a document, once its term has passed the tenant is believed to say that he paid. Conversely, even when the rental was oral, he is not believed within the term, as stated above in siman 78, seif 1. Rambam's wording is: whether it was by document or by witnesses. The Mechaber added: whether it was without witnesses. His words are difficult, since siman 78, seif 1 states that this applies only when witnesses are present, as Tosafot write there. Rambam therefore speaks here specifically of witnesses. Tur's wording caused the error, for he writes: whether the rental was oral, by witnesses, or by document. The first 'or' is plainly a scribal error, since the phrase 'oral or by witnesses' is itself difficult, for testimony is also oral. Rambam's reference to a document follows Rabbeinu Chananel. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'holding.' Rabbeinu Chananel's wording is cited there, and Baal HaIttur writes that the Gemara's reading is: 'For this reason I wrote you a document, so that you should not establish a presumption against me.' See Rivash 313. If he claimed from him within thirty days, etc.; and likewise if he rented from him, etc. All this is Rambam's language. He holds that even without an express term, the tenant is not believed within thirty days, following Ramban in the name of some Geonim, as Ran explains in the second chapter of Ketubot. Ramban himself, however, follows Tosafot, as stated in Bava Batra and cited in siman 39, seif 3: there are those who say, etc. The same applies to rental, whose ordinary term is thirty days, as stated in Rosh Hashanah 7b. Maggid Mishneh writes that the Halakhot and some versions of the Gemara read: When is this? If within, etc., we learned: within thirty days he is presumed not to have paid until he brings proof that he paid; after thirty days he is presumed to have paid until the other party brings proof that he did not pay. Rambam apparently understood this as a baraita stated concerning this very law. Rashi altered the reading and wrote, 'This is the correct reading: as we learned,' which shows that the text before him had been the former version. This explains the phrase 'within thirty days.' The continuation, 'and likewise if he rented, etc.,' is the question there, resolved from the rule that a landlord who claims at the proper time, etc. This is the approach of Ramban in the name of those Geonim. Rama, however, emended the text to read 'within the rental term,' following Tosafot, as stated at the end of siman 78, and Rashi's reading here. Tur likewise follows this view. Rambam's position, however, is as explained. Maggid Mishneh's reference to thirty days of notice is difficult; rather, the reason is that an unspecified rental lasts thirty days. Rambam then adds a separate case in which the property was rented for a fixed term. According to Rama this addition is redundant.
