The same applies to other claims, etc. Mordechai writes that if the landlord says, "You rented it for six," and the tenant says, "I rented it for five," or the owner says, "I never rented it to you," the land remains in the presumption of its owner and the tenant must bring proof. The Tur and Mechaber write a similar rule in siman 312:16. This is another application of the principle that land remains in the presumption of its owner.
