He demands payment within thirty days. Rambam treats the thirty-day notice period of an unspecified tenancy as its term. The Shach asks why an ordinary unspecified thirty-day loan permits a plea of early payment. The answer is that the presumption against early payment follows commercial practice. In some relationships people do pay early; in others they do not. An unspecified rental can follow a custom of payment at the end. If rent is stated annually, each completed year is its own payment period even though failure to give notice creates another year. The same is true monthly. Tosafot likewise says that a ten-year lease at ten dinars per year creates a separate due date each year.
