If a renter or mortgagee possessed the produce for three years, concealed the document, and claimed that he had rights to the produce for five years, while the landowner claimed three, and when told to produce the document he said it was lost, the renter is believed. Had he wished, he could have claimed ownership, since he possessed it for three years. This applies only where there are no witnesses that it was rented or mortgaged to him and the landowner did not protest. Otherwise, he is not believed.
