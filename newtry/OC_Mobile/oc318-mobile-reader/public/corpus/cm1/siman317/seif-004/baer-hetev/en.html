This is also the Tur's conclusion above in the name of the Rif and Rosh, that even where the mortgage was documented and he claims the document was lost, we do not remove the land from him because of this migo, unlike the dissenting opinion regarding a written document. The Tur cites Rabbeinu Yonah: this is not considered a migo to extract, because according to his claim he already acquired a right in the land from the time he entered and held it for five years by the acts of locking, fencing, and breaching. This is the Sema's language. The Bach writes that the Tur here follows the Rif and Rosh, unlike Rabbeinu Yonah. I wrote at the end of siman 150 that Rabbeinu Yonah also agrees here. Ibid.

This is difficult, because here the ruling is stated without qualification, while in siman 150, seif 3, the Mechaber cites an opinion that if he later consumed it for three years he is believed. One could answer that here there are witnesses that the property is held as a mortgage but they do not know for how many years; in that case all agree he is not believed, as stated there. Yet this law comes from the Maggid Mishneh, while the Beit Yosef in siman 150 writes that the Rashba disagrees with the Maggid Mishneh. According to the proposed answer the Rashba would not disagree. Consider this carefully. This requires study. Ibid.
