Two. Meaning, even if he found many of them, when they are lying separately, one by one, this is considered the manner of falling, there is no identifying sign, and they belong to the finder. But if he found them arranged in pairs, two by two, their number constitutes an identifying sign, for the claimant can say, 'You found four, and they were arranged in pairs.' The same applies if he found three lying together and it is evident that they were placed there intentionally; he must announce them because their number is an identifying sign. Ibid.
