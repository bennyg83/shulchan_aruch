Or more, he must announce it. See the Bach, whose words are strained. His distinction from two coins on the ground, because here the finder announces the object, still does not resolve the difficulty, for the place itself remains an identifying mark, as the Rosh writes regarding coins. One must therefore say that such an arrangement can sometimes occur through an accidental fall, but if so the same could be said of two bolts of cloth. Rather, the meaning is that when several pairs fell together, such a configuration is not ordinarily produced by a fall. This is clear in the Bach's wording, and the Sema writes similarly.
