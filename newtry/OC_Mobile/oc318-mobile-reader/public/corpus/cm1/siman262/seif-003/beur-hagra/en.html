For example, where it is recognizable, etc. On 23a the halacha states: in a private domain, if, etc.
(Supplement) Because of its weight or, etc. In the Mishna there, money and produce are retained because of their importance, as the Gemara explains through Rav Yitzchak's teaching. The same applies to strands of purple wool, as stated there, and to cakes of pressed figs and baker's loaves. Rosh's text likewise reads that they are retained because they are important, explaining that edible items are significant. He gives the same reason for strings of fish and cuts of meat. Bundles of wool and flax are retained because they are heavy. His Gemara text must therefore have read: come and hear, bundles, etc. According to this reading, the entire Mishna rests on importance except for wool and flax bundles. Bundles in the public domain have a different rationale, as explained on 22a and 23a in Rabbah and Rava's dispute: bundles are ordinarily placed intentionally. See Tosafot 21a, s.v. 'these,' etc. (End of supplement.)
