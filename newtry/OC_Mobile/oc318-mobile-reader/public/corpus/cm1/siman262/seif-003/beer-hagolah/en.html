Vav) There, seif 4. This is explained in the Mishnah and Gemara on Bava Metzia 21a and 21b: what benefit would announcement provide when the object has no identifying sign by which it could be returned?
Zayin) Following Rava there at the end of 22b.
Chet) This is straightforward in the Gemara at the beginning of 23b.
Tet) Rav Sheshet was asked whether weight constitutes an identifying sign. The question was resolved from a baraita that it does. The Gemara concludes that if weight is a sign, measure and number are likewise identifying signs.
Yod) As the Gemara answers regarding the Mishnah's rule that cakes of pressed figs belong to the finder, 21b.
Kaf) As the Gemara answers regarding scattered coins and similarly strips of purple wool.
Lamed) Following Abaye there, whose position is halachah in the mnemonic YA'AL KAGAM, 22b. This is a case of ye'ush shelo mida'at according to one of the views.
