Some would remain inside it. The Gemara distinguishes between a basket with fruit and a jug with flax. The Tur records the differing explanations of Rashi and Ri, and the Rosh rules stringently. The Mechaber follows the Rambam, who holds that Rav Pappa disagrees with Rav Zevid and therefore does not preserve that distinction. It is surprising that Rama omitted the Rosh and Tur's stringent view in a matter involving the Torah commandment of returning lost property.
