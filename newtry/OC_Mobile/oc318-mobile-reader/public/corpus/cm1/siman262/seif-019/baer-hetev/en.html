Remains. The Gemara distinguishes between certain kinds of produce and other goods. The Tur records the differing explanations of Rashi and Ri, and the Rosh rules stringently. The Mechaber follows the Rambam, who holds that Rav Pappa disagrees with Rav Zevid and therefore does not make that distinction. It is surprising that the Rama omitted the view of the Tur and Rosh as a 'some say,' since their position is a stringency requiring return of lost property, which is a Torah commandment. Sema.
