Ayin) This is the language of the Rambam there, chapter 15, halachah 11. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam rules like Rabbi Yehudah in the Mishnah on 21a and not like Rabbi Meir, according to the plain reading of the Mishnah and the version in our texts.
Peh) The Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh write that the Rosh disagrees because his version did not read "the words of Rabbi Meir"; rather, he treated it as an anonymous Mishnah and therefore ruled accordingly.
Tzadi) A baraita on 23b, and like the incident where Rabbah bar Rav Huna cut it, etc.
Kuf) From the implication of the Gemara there.
Reish) There in the Gemara concerning the threshing floor, following Rashi's first explanation.
