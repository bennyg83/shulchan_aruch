Alef) This is the language of the Rambam there, chapter 15, halachah 7, based on the Mishnah. The Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh cite the scholar who annotated the text that the Gemara's explanation on 21b involving the threshing floor was given before the Gemara knew the reason that the items are heavy. Once that reason is known, the Mishnah can be established in all circumstances where they were found in a manner indicating accidental loss. The Ramban writes this according to the Rif, and the Nimukei Yosef likewise rules so. But where they are found in a manner indicating deliberate placement, one should not touch them, because the location itself is an identifying sign, following Rava at the end of 22b.
