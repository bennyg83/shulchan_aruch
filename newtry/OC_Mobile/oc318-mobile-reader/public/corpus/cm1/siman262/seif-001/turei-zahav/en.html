(Seif 1: even if its value decreased after the finding, etc.) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is Ramban's view: since the item was worth a perutah at the time it was found, the finder becomes obligated to return it, and this is implied by Perek Eilu Metziot. The Beit Yosef writes that the matter requires study, and the Kesef Mishneh similarly expresses surprise at the Maggid Mishneh's statement. I am astonished at these great luminaries. An explicit Gemara in Perek HaZahav 55 states that if a lost object was worth a perutah when found, one must announce it even though its value later fell. Rashi explains that it fell in value between the time of finding and the announcement. Why then did the Maggid Mishneh need to seek an implication from Perek Eilu Metziot? In truth, there is no such indication there because the law is explicit elsewhere.
