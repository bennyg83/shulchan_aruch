At the time it was lost and at the time it was found, etc. This is derived from the verse, "And so shall you do for every lost thing of your brother that is lost from him and you find it." It must have the status of a lost object when it is lost and the status of an object of monetary significance when found. Less than a perutah has no legal significance.
It decreased in value after it was found. Meaning, do not say that because at the time of announcement and return it is no longer worth a perutah, he need not announce it. Once the obligation took effect at the time of finding, it does not lapse merely because the object later decreased in value.
