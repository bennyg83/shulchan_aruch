The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes there in the name of the Ramban. The Kesef Mishneh writes that from Rambam's plain wording it appears that even if the object was found in a place where Torah scholars are not commonly present, if a Torah scholar comes and says that he recognizes the lost utensil by visual recognition, one must show it to him. When a scholar notices that an item is lost he does not despair even though it bears no identifying mark, because he knows it will be returned to him by visual recognition.
