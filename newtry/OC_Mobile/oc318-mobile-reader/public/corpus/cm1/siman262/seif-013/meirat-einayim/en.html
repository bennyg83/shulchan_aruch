Some say that a crack in a coin is an identifying sign. Nimukei Yosef writes this and explains that for this purpose a coin is like any other utensil. Ramban similarly writes that although the ordinary reason a coin is not returned by an identifying sign is that money is meant to circulate and perhaps the claimant spent his coin and this one fell from another person, that concern exists only when many identical coins were minted and the claimed sign does not uniquely distinguish one from another. If the coin has a distinctive crack or other unique mark, we do not assume sale or transfer any more than we would with another found object.
