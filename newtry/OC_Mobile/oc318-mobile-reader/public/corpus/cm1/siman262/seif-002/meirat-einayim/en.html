Perhaps they are partners in it. If we knew that they were not partners, then even if the amount were large the loser might despair. He would say: I told my companions several times that I lost this object and they did not return it. They evidently intend to steal it, and I cannot sue them because each one can say, "I did not find it, perhaps the third traveler did." If they are partners, however, the loser does not suspect that his partners intend to steal from him. He assumes they are merely delaying or teasing him, and therefore does not despair. Likewise, when there are only two travelers, he does not despair. Even if the companion denies finding it and swears, the loser thinks: Today or tomorrow I will seize some of his property and hold it against what he is withholding from me. So write the Rosh and Tur.
