They failed to do so, and the beit din then performed everything required of it through appraisal and public announcement. Therefore its sale remains valid. A purchaser, by contrast, bears no personal duty to pay the lender's debt. The beit din therefore acts as his agent. Just as where an agent errs in a manner that harms the purchaser the purchaser may void the transaction under the law governing an agent who deviates from his instructions, so too, if the beit din errs in a manner that benefits the purchaser, the lender may void the transaction.
