And some say... As the Gemara says: just as a widow acts as an individual, so too an ordinary agent, excluding beit din.
And if someone comes... The public announcement is made so that even though the purchaser must in any event buy at the court's valuation, the process benefits the orphans and creditor by inviting better offers. The Yerushalmi explains that thirty days is chosen to strengthen the orphans' position, while excessive delay can weaken it. Consecrated property differs because each successive bid can be accepted separately, as explained in Erchin 27a.
