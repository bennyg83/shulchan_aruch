We do not heed the purchaser. The Rosh in Mi Shehaya Nasu'i explains that the reason is for the borrower's benefit, so that he will be released from his debt. This is also evident from the Tur and Mechaber at the beginning of siman 109, seif 1, regarding heirs, where we do heed them. However, according to what I rule below in siman 115 at the end of seif 1, here even if the borrower died and left no other property at all, we do not heed the purchaser. This is the principal ruling.
