If the purchaser wishes to remove the creditor, etc., he may do so. I explained that this rule divides into three cases. If the purchaser seeks to remove the creditor before beit din has appraised and announced the field for the creditor, he may do so by paying the field's actual value even if that is less than the creditor's full debt. If beit din has already appraised and announced the field for the creditor, even if the creditor has not yet actually received it, the purchaser can remove him only by paying the entire debt. If appraisal and announcement were completed and the creditor has already received the field, Rambam holds that the purchaser may still redeem it because judicial appraisal can be reversed even for a purchaser, while the Rosh disagrees and limits that equitable return to the borrower and his heirs, to whom the principle of "good and upright" applies. The Tur discusses this in siman 103, and the Mechaber there rules like Rambam while Rama cites the Rosh.
