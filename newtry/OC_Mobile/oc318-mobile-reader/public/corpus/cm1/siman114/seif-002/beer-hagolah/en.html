There, seif 5. The Rosh writes so there.
The Rosh concludes that every period granted by beit din is not required by strict law but is a measure of mercy practiced by the Sages toward litigants so as not to press them excessively. This is from Ramban's glosses. Maggid Mishneh likewise writes in chapter 23 of Hilkhot Malveh in the name of Rashba.
