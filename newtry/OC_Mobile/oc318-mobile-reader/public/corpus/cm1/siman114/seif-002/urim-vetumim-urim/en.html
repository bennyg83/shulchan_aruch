And not to come through evasive tactics. The time granted by beit din is not strict law but a merciful practice established by the Sages. One should not therefore frustrate it through evasions. Rosh. Knesset HaGedolah writes that after the Geonim instituted the debtor's oath that he has no assets, one cannot collect from a purchaser until the debtor has first sworn, because perhaps he does have available property and one does not collect from encumbered property where unencumbered property exists. If the debtor is not present, however, we do not wait for his oath and may collect from the purchaser.
