If the purchaser wished to remove the lender. The Sema distinguishes as follows: before completion of the appraisal and public announcement, the purchaser can remove the creditor by paying the appraised value of the field. After the announcement, he can remove him only by paying the entire debt. After the land has been finally awarded to the creditor, the matter depends on the dispute in siman 103 whether an appraisal may be reversed for a purchaser. The principal ruling, however, is that a purchaser can never remove the creditor except by paying the entire debt. Shach. See the Beurim:
