The law governing a creditor and a purchaser, and whether the purchaser can direct him back to the borrower. It contains 7 se'ifim.
If a lender knows of purchasers who bought property from the borrower after the loan, he goes and seizes from them through beit din. If he seized without beit din, his act has no effect. Beit din tears the writ of authorization and writes him a writ of seizure, stating in the writ of seizure that the writ of authorization was torn.
