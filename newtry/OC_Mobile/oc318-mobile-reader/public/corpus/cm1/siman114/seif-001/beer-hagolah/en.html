The language of Rambam, Hilkhot Malveh chapter 22, law 7. The Maggid Mishneh writes that in truth an adrakhta is issued against unencumbered property, while a tirpa is issued against encumbered property, and Raavad writes likewise. This is Rambam's view. From here it follows that the correct text in Get Pashut, Bava Batra 169a, is: any adrakhta that does not state that the loan document was torn is not a valid adrakhta; any tirpa that does not state that the adrakhta was torn is not a valid tirpa; and any appraisal that does not state that the tirpa was torn is not a valid appraisal. This is the correct sequence.
