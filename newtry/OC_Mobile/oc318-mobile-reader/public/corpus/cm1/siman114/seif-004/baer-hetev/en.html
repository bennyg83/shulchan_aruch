In the province. Meaning in the city or within one day's travel. If the debtor is farther away, there is no need to wait for him, because the oath that he has no other property is only an enactment of the Geonim. The purchaser may later seek reimbursement from the debtor. Sema.
Sold it. If the creditor had already sold it, the purchaser could say to him, "You are not my litigant. My claim is against the person who bought it from you." Sema.
And includes. This wording is imprecise, because the statements that the creditor did not waive the debt and did not sell the document are also included by way of gilgul. Therefore, during the term of the debt he need not swear at all. The Tur's wording is more accurate: "He includes in his oath that this is not a trust document, that he did not sell it to another, and that he did not waive it." Shach. The Sema writes that only when he must take the primary oath that the debt was not paid did the Sages require him to include the other matters in that oath. If it is established that the debt was not paid, he need not swear at all. Therefore the Mechaber concludes that during the term he may seize from the purchaser without an oath. See siman 78, seif 2.
