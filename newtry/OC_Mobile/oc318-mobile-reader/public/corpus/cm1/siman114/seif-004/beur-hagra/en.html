Three. As stated in Bava Basra concerning three who descend to appraise. Fewer than three do not constitute a proper appraisal, as Avodah Zarah says regarding valuation by three.
Experts. As stated in Sanhedrin 14b concerning three purchasers or evaluators.
And half the improvement. Bava Basra 157b.
And they announce... Orphans and purchasers are generally treated alike for these procedural purposes. See above, siman 98 seif 9.
And afterward they administer an oath... As explained above, siman 99 seif 1.
And that he did not waive... Rambam writes this. See above, siman 82 seif 11, that any claim which does not nullify the document itself can still require an oath. Thus the oath here includes that he did not sell the claim to another. Beit din itself raises such claims for a purchaser.
And he includes in his oath... Through gilgul one swears even on matters that would not independently warrant an oath, as the Yerushalmi derives from sotah. The rule applies even if the claimant did not expressly demand the ancillary oath, as explained above in siman 94 seif 1.
And if... As with orphans, Bava Basra 5b.
