(Seif 1: The river ceased, etc.) This cannot refer to the main river, for then it would be a calamity affecting the region and the entire loss would fall upon the employer, as I proved in siman 321 from the Mordechai's ruling concerning a ruler's decree. The Rama's later words, “if it was a regional calamity,” likewise show this. The Sema disagrees, but I already demonstrated in siman 328, from Hagahot Ashri, that his view is incorrect.
