Seif 1, gloss. Therefore, if he received the entire rent, he need not return the utensils. Note: See the Shach, and see my third edition on Choshen Mishpat, siman 34, where I explained that this applies only if the tenant already lived there at least briefly. If he never lived there at all, the payment must certainly be returned. Nevertheless, because the underlying law is disputed, he may be able to retain it on the basis of doubt rather than as a matter of definite right. Even the Shach concedes this distinction. Understand this well.
