a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 6, from Rava's statement in Bava Metzia 77a regarding one who hired workers to irrigate.
b) The language of the Tur, seif 2, following his explanation of the Gemara concerning an interruption of the river where the workers are local townsmen. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes that the principal explanation is that if the workers are residents of that town, they know the river's customary behavior, unlike the Rambam's explanation.
c) The Rosh writes that where both sides should have anticipated the possibility, the loss falls upon the workers, because the burden of proof is upon the claimant and they have the lower hand for failing to stipulate.
d) In the name of the Raavad. He explains that once Shimon rented the house, it is like a complete sale and he became obligated for the rent whether he lives there or not. Although we rule that rental liability accrues continuously from beginning to end, the renter nevertheless may not simply leave the premises and terminate the obligation.
