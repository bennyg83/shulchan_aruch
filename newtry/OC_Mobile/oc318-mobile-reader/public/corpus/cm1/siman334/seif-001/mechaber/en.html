One who hires a worker to irrigate a field, or hires a teacher and the child becomes ill. It contains four seifim.
If one hires a worker to irrigate from a river and the river stops midday, then if the river does not normally stop, or it normally stops and the worker knew this, the worker bears the loss and receives nothing, even if the employer also knew. If the worker did not know and the employer did, the employer pays him as an idle worker. Rama: The same applies to any unavoidable event: if both knew or neither knew it might occur, the worker bears the loss; if only the employer knew, the employer bears it. For a regional calamity, see siman 321. One who rents a house and dies during the term need pay only for the period used, because the landlord is like a worker and should have stipulated. Others disagree, and therefore if the landlord already received all the rent he need not return it. Flight because of an epidemic is treated like another unavoidable event, and the worker or teacher bears the loss.
