Zayin) There, halachah 5, from the Mishnah on Bava Metzia 31a.
Chet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this applies specifically outside the Sabbath boundary; within the boundary one need not return it. This is explicit in the Gemara's second version there.
Tet) The Maggid Mishneh writes concerning the Mishnah on 31b that it appears that even outside the boundary one need not return it where the animal is merely grazing.
Yod) There in the Mishnah on 32a, as Rava explains it, following Rashi. The Maggid Mishneh writes that a stable likewise creates an obligation even outside the boundary, according to the second version of Rabbi Yitzchak there. See the Rama's gloss.
Kaf) In his rulings, the Rosh cites only Rabbi Yitzchak's first version.
