Standing in the public domain. Our text of Rambam reads, 'standing outside the inhabited boundary.' This follows his general method of ruling according to the latter version. Maggid Mishneh explains this. The reading 'standing in the public domain' has no coherent meaning, and it is also unclear why Rambam would omit Rabbi Yochanan's ruling.
Grazing among the grasses. This is the baraita on 31a, according to the text of Rif, Rosh, and the other decisors. Our Gemara text is erroneous, for according to it the later discussion has no point.
