By day this is not lost property, etc. The Sema writes, not like the Rashba cited by the Maggid Mishna, who says that after three consecutive days even these are lost property. This is mistaken. The Rashba spoke of a cloak beside a fence, where finding it on three successive days renders it lost property, as with a donkey. A donkey in the middle of the day is not lost property even after many days. The Rambam does not distinguish regarding a cloak beside a fence and holds that it is never treated as lost property merely because of the passage of days. This is explicit in the Maggid Mishna.
