During the day. The Tur writes: 'Even for several consecutive days, because it is evident that this is not a lost item, but rather that people deliberately leave it there in this manner.' This also appears to be the Mechaber's view, following the Rambam in chapter 15 of Hilchot Gezelah, and not the Rashba's view cited by the Maggid Mishneh, according to which even these circumstances constitute a lost item after three consecutive days. Thus writes the Sema. At first glance, he erred here, for the Rashba said this regarding a garment beside a fence, the case of the baraita, that when one finds it there for three consecutive days it is deemed a lost item, as with a donkey. But a donkey seen in the middle of the day is not deemed lost even after many days. The Rambam did not distinguish regarding a garment beside a fence, holding that it is never deemed lost even after many days. This is clear in the Maggid Mishneh there. Shach.
