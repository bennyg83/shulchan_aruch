Alef) The Mishnah in the second chapter of Bava Metzia 30b.
Bet) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 15 of Hilkhot Gezelah VaAveidah. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is explicit in the sugya, where the Gemara says that if the animal is seen at night, even for a single hour, it has the status of a lost object and one must attend to it.
Gimel) A statement of Rav Yehudah in the name of Rav, as the Gemara explains it there.
Meaning, either at twilight near nightfall.
