Although, etc. According to this view, the Torah exempted him only from the commandment, 'You may not hide yourself,' but he remains liable under the law of theft because the owner did not abandon the property. The proof is from Bava Batra there: 'This is understandable, etc.' Although an issar and oil are also knowingly abandoned, once they came into his hand he must pay. See Rashi and Tosafot there.
(Supplement) Although it is prohibited, etc. As stated in Nedarim 45a and above in siman 273, seif 7, an abandonment is ineffective until made before one person, and rabbinically before three.
