(Supplement) Nevertheless, an item, etc.; and see below, etc. There it is explained that a renter may sublet, and similarly a borrower may lend the borrowed property. Proof comes from Arakhin, chapter HaMokher: if he sold it to the first for one hundred and the first sold it to the second for two hundred, etc. Every sale of land while the Jubilee applies is only a lease. See Mordechai, Bava Metzia, section 497, at length. He also proves it from the case in HaUmanin where he unloaded the burden, etc. (End of supplement.) See 497.
