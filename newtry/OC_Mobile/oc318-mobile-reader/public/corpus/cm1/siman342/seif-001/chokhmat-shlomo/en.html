Seif 1. The borrower is not permitted to lend the borrowed item to another. Note: See the Shach, who exempts the borrower where the owner is accustomed to lend the item to that second person. See also my responsa on Even HaEzer, sixth edition, siman 94, addressed to the community of Dzikov, where I wrote to prove that the Shach does not merely exempt him after the fact, but that it is permitted even initially to act in this manner. I cited there the words of Tosafot in Gittin 29, which indicate the opposite, and, with the help of Heaven, I distinguished between the cases. See there and understand well.
