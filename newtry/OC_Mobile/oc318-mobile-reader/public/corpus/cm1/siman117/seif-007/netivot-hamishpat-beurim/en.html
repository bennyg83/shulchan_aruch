According to what the Mishneh LaMelech writes in chapter 4 of Hilchot Meilah, halachah 9, that according to the Ran, when one states a prohibition without attaching it to another prohibited object there is no law of meilah, the case can be established where he prohibited it to the entire world without such attachment. It can also have practical application to an oral loan, where even property with monetary sanctity cannot be seized by the creditor, or to movable property according to the many poskim who hold similarly. In any event, the prohibition takes effect against the creditor, although redemption is required because of the enactment of Rabbi Abahu, just as the Gemara states in Bava Kamma, chapter HaManiach, that consecration is effective because of Rabbi Abahu.
