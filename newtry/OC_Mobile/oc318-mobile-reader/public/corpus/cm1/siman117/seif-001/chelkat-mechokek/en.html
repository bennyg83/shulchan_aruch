Neither the basic ketubah nor the additional amount. Although it was explained in the preceding siman that with women prohibited by a negative commandment, or an aylonit whom he did not recognize as such, she receives the additional ketubah, the Maggid Mishneh explains that there is no greater defect than this. Since we rule that a woman who violates Jewish religious practice and similar cases, including one found to have vows or bodily defects, does not receive the additional ketubah, the same applies to this defect. Ramban and Baal HaItur, however, hold that this is unlike other defects. There she knows of her own defects and it is incumbent upon her to disclose them, whereas here she too did not know of this defect, and she therefore receives the additional ketubah just as an aylonit does. Rambam's view is that this case is unlike an aylonit. There, he could have examined her before intercourse for the physical signs of an aylonit, and she can remain married to him if he wishes to marry another wife and thereby fulfill procreation. He therefore fully committed the additional ketubah to her. Here, by contrast, she is prohibited from marital relations with him and he could not have examined her beforehand. She is therefore included among the women of whom the Sages said that they leave without a ketubah and do not receive the additional amount. This appears to be Rambam's view and is the principal interpretation. End of the Maggid Mishneh's words.
