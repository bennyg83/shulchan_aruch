The statement of Rabbah, Bava Batra 44b. Rashba writes in Responsa siman 618 that the same applies if he gave the property as a gift: the creditor does not collect from the recipient.
Tur, seif 3. Nimukei Yosef writes likewise, and Baal HaTerumot in Shaar 43. The reason is that movable property is not ordinarily the subject of recorded documents and has no publicity. Otherwise, the Gemara in Chezkat HaBatim should have asked whether perhaps he had made the movables an apotiki by written document.
See what was written at the beginning of siman 113. It is explained there that even if the creditor warned the purchasers, etc., the creditor still does not collect from the movables.
