Likewise regarding priority... Even in a case where a creditor can collect from purchasers, such as land, the first Tanna and Ben Nanas dispute whether an earlier creditor who collected retains what he collected. Bava Kamma likewise shows that a later creditor may have a claim even though an attempted sale would not stand. Although we rule that an improper prior collection does not stand, an apotiki is not stronger than an outright sale for all priority purposes.
