He made his slave a specifically designated lien. The designation may be oral, provided the loan itself was in writing, according to the Maggid Mishneh. Bach and Torat Chaim question this and maintain that even an oral loan suffices because a slave creates publicity. On their view, there must still be proof that the debt was not paid, such as witnesses who remained continuously present or a debt still within its term. Shiltei Giborim cites the Maggid Mishneh and understands the Rosh to require that the designation itself be written.
