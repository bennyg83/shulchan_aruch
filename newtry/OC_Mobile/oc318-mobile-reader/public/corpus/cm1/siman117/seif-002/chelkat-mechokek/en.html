Nor any of the conditions of the ketubah. The law is not explained regarding property from the principal of nichsei tzon barzel or nichsei melog that was worn out or destroyed. Where a woman leaves because of her own conduct, such as one who violates religious practice, and all the more so one who leaves because of a bad reputation, the law is that she receives only property still in existence. But this woman bears no blame for this defect, and according to Ramban she even receives the additional ketubah. Why then should the husband not reimburse the principal of nichsei tzon barzel that was lost or stolen? This is unlike the case where he did not know that she was prohibited to him by a negative commandment. There she misled him, there was no valid marital framework, and he did not acquire rights in the nichsei melog. He also need not reimburse nichsei tzon barzel, because she gave him permission to hold it and he accepted responsibility to repay only if she left by his choice, whereas she knew that she would ultimately be compelled to leave. Here, however, logic dictates that he should repay all the nichsei tzon barzel as though he had borrowed it from her, and all the more so the principal of nichsei melog that he consumed without authorization. I found the same in Responsa Maharam printed among Responsa Rashba, siman 840, concerning a woman who developed the defect while married: she receives her ketubah and certainly the dowry she brought him, because she is blameless and we do not penalize her by depriving her of it. It appears that even if she already had the defect at the beginning of the marriage and therefore does not receive a ketubah, she nevertheless does not lose the dowry she brought him, because she too was blameless.
