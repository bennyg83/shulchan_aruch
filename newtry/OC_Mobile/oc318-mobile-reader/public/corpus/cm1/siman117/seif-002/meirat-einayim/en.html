A mortgage. Meaning that he mortgaged his field to the creditor so that the creditor would consume its produce under an arrangement of deduction from the debt.
Its law is like an express apotiki. In the Perishah I explained that this teaches us not to say that when the parties state concerning a mortgage, 'You shall have payment only from this,' their intention is merely that the debt be paid through consumption of the produce year by year under the deduction arrangement, or that the mortgage is stronger than an express apotiki so that the debtor could not remove the creditor by giving movable property or even cash. Rather, the mortgage is no stronger than an express apotiki, and that is the legal category intended.
