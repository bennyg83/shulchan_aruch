If one conceals thorns or glass in another person's wall, and the wall owner later demolishes it so that the objects fall into the public domain and cause damage, then if the wall was unstable, the one who concealed them is liable; if the wall was sound, the wall owner is liable. Rama: Some say that if the damage occurred during the actual collapse, the wall owner is liable in every case. If the wall fell on its own, then if it had been sound both are exempt, while if it was unstable the one who concealed the objects is liable. If an unrelated person demolished the wall, the demolisher is liable and the person who concealed them is exempt.
