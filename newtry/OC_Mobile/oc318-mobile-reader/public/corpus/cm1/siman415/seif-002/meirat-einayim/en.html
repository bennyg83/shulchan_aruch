The owner of the wall is liable in every case. Meaning, even if the wall was unstable. Since he actively demolished it, he is like a person who takes his fellow's glass and uses it to damage another person. The person who performs the damaging act is liable. For this same reason the ruling concludes that if an unrelated person demolished it, that person is liable. This applies specifically where the wall was demolished by hand. If it collapsed by itself and had been unstable, the one who concealed the dangerous object can be liable, as the ruling concludes.
