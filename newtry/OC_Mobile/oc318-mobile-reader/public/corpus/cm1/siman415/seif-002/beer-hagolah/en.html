Beit Yosef did not explain the source of this law. Bach writes that it was learned from Rosh there. In my humble opinion, one who examines Rosh will see clearly that he says the opposite: the person who concealed the object in an unstable wall is liable, not the owner who later demolished the wall. I did, however, see Nimukei Yosef also write that with an unstable wall the wall owner who demolished it is liable, bringing proof from the law of a stone that was in one's lap even though he never knew of it, explained in siman 421, seif 10. The Mechaber's omission appears to me because the cases are not analogous. There, there is no other person from whom payment can be collected; here there is the person who concealed it, and payment may be collected from him. Rabbi Natan's rule is explained in siman 410, seif 52, and this accords with the plain Gemara.
