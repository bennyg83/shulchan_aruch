For lending. The Sema understands the Rambam to hold that regarding utensils normally lent or rented out, even if there was no public report of a theft in the city, the owner swears that they are his and that he did not sell them, and may remove them from the purchaser. See the Shach, who discusses this at length and rejects the Sema's position. See also Shiltei Giborim, chapter Kol HaNishba'in, concerning these laws, and the end of siman 133.
