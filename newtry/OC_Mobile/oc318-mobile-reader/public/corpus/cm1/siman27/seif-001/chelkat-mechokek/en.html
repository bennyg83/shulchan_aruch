Provided that she understands that it is language of kiddushin. If she says that she did not understand, there are no kiddushin at all. But if he used language universally recognized among women as language of kiddushin, such as 'behold, you are betrothed to me,' which is the standard formula and every intelligent woman knows its meaning, she is not believed to say she did not understand. Nevertheless, she is believed stringently in this sense: if a second man later betrothed her, she requires a get from him as well, because according to her own words she was still unmarried with respect to the first. With expressions not generally understood, however, she is believed to say that she did not understand them. This is the implication of the authorities. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that even there it is doubtful kiddushin. In practice, this requires study whether his view must be taken into account.
