And "accursed" is language of a curse. Meaning that if he says, "May he be accursed by Hashem," or by another Divine name, he incurs lashes. Without a Divine name or appellation he is not liable even when using the word "accursed." The phrase means only that "accursed" can function as a curse. Depending on context, however, the word can also function as an oath or as excommunication. See Tur and Perishah.
