Not to curse a judge or any Jew. This siman contains 2 seifim.
One who curses a Jew, even himself, using one of the Divine Names, a descriptive Divine Name, or one of the names by which non Jews refer to the Holy One, blessed is He, receives lashes for violating "You shall not curse a deaf person," if the act was committed in the presence of witnesses after due warning. If the person cursed was a judge, he receives an additional set of lashes for violating "You shall not curse judges." The expression "cursed" is itself a form of cursing.
