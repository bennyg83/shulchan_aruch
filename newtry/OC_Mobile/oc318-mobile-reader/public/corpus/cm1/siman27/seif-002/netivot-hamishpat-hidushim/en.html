Even if the person insulted forgave him. See Sema. Rama's term "the one insulted" is not precise. The rule applies to one who curses a sage or an ordinary person, whether the curse is one carrying Torah lashes or merely a prohibited expression such as "May such and such strike you." Forgiveness does not erase the prohibition itself. But if he merely degraded or insulted him verbally and the only sanction is disciplinary for the victim's honor, the victim may certainly waive his honor, just as beit din may waive its honor. This is correct.
