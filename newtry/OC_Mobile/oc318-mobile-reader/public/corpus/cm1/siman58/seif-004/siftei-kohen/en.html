And not only this, etc. The Beit Yosef cites a responsum of the Rashba: Reuven owed Shimon two debts, one secured by an oath and one not secured by an oath. Reuven paid a sum and claimed that he paid it toward the debt bearing the oath, while Shimon said, “No, I accepted it for the other debt, and the debt secured by the oath remains.” The law follows Shimon, because a creditor may allocate an undesignated payment to the debt he chooses, absent a binding designation by the debtor accepted by the creditor.
