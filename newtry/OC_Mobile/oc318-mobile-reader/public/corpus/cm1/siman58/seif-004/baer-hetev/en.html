Debts. Beit Yosef writes in the name of a responsum of the Rashba: Reuven owed Shimon two debts, one secured by an oath and one without an oath. Reuven paid him a sum of money and claims that he paid it toward the debt secured by the oath. Shimon says, 'No, I received it toward the other debt, and the debt secured by the oath remains due.' The law follows Shimon.
