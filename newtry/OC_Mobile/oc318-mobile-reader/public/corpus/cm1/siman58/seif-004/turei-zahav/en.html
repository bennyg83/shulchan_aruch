(In the name of Maharshal, Beit Yosef beginning, “The Ramban wrote.”) Tosafot in Bava Batra, the Ran in HaKotev and Kol HaNishba’in, and the Ramban in his novellae disagree and hold that even one witness impairs the document. They explain the episode of Avimi as occurring after the enactment of the oath of hesset, for before that the claimant would not have been believed. Accordingly, when the Ramban later answered the Baal HaTerumot, he must have retracted his earlier view. This requires study; see Maharam of Lublin, responsum 114. The Beit Yosef was imprecise.
