Seif 1. An agent. If an agent failed to plead that payment received was for another debt, there is room to say that the court should advance that plea for him, since the borrower is subordinate to the lender and especially where the principal himself knows that such a plea is available. If the agent returned the stronger document even though he could have claimed that the money was for another debt and surrendered only the weaker document, and this causes a loss, perhaps he is liable as one who burns a document. Although ordinary custodians are not liable for negligent loss of documents according to some authorities, physically handing over the stronger document is direct damage. If, however, the document was not placed in his hand and he merely failed to guard the stronger instrument, he is exempt according to those who hold that negligent custodianship of documents does not create liability.
