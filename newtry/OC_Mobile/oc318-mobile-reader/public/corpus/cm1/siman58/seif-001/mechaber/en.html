One Who Produces a Debt Document Against Another, and the Claim That Payment Was Applied to Another Debt. Four seifim. If a creditor produces an authenticated document and the borrower says, 'I paid you,' while the creditor answers, 'It is true that you paid me, but I accepted the money toward a different oral loan; this document remains outstanding,' the creditor is believed if the borrower did not pay before witnesses. The document remains effective. If it grants the creditor credibility, he collects without an oath; if the borrower later claims the creditor wrongfully took the money, the creditor takes a heses oath and is exempt. If the document grants no credibility, he collects only after taking an oath. If the borrower did not personally pay but sent money through an agent, whether he told the agent, 'Take the document and give him the money,' or, 'Give the money and take the document,' the agent must reimburse the sender. See siman 130, seif 5. {Rama: Some say that once the agent is liable, the creditor may not retain the money by claiming another debt from the borrower, unless he claims that the agent also delivered it toward another debt. Others hold that he may always claim another debt. If property was deposited with a custodian who claims it belongs to another person who owes him money, then if he says the depositor delivered it as that owner's agent, he may assert a claim against it up to its value through the defense that he could have returned it, even though the depositor will have to reimburse the owner. If the depositor never mentioned that owner and merely deposited it, while the custodian independently claims it belongs to the debtor, he cannot retain it without proving the owner's debt. Some say this restriction applies only when the item came to him as a deposit. If he seized it from the depositor and has an alternative defense, he may claim that its owner owes him its value, while the negligent depositor must reimburse the owner.} If the borrower did not tell the agent to take the document, the agent is exempt, because the borrower did not instruct him to deliver the money before witnesses. See also siman 393.
