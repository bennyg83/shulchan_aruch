That which is allotted to the wife of a poor Jew. Below, siman 73, the clothing a husband must provide his wife is explained. It is not clarified here whether a father must give his daughter clothing sufficient for twelve months, or whether temporary clothing sufficient only for summer or winter is enough. The mishnah in Ketubot 67, however, indicates that he must provide clothing for a full year: one who marries off his daughter without specifying an amount may not give her less than fifty zuz, and the mishnah in chapter Af Al Pi, Ketubot 64, speaks of clothing worth fifty zuz from year to year.
