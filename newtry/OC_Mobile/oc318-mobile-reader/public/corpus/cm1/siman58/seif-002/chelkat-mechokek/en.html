It is proper to give her according to his wealth. But he is not compelled to do so. What the Tur writes, that he 'must give according to his honor,' likewise means that it is proper, but beit din does not compel him. They do compel him to marry her off and to provide the minimal support needed for the marriage, but they do not compel a particular dowry amount, which depends upon his judgment. See Hagahot Mordechai to Kiddushin. The Bach does not write this way; see there.
