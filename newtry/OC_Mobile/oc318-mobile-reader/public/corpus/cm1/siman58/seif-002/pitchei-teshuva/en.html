And he said, 'Did I not repay you?' See Shach, seif katan 11, through his statement that he found in the Rosh that this applies even with disqualified witnesses. See Tumim, in the rules of migo, entry 18, who asks that according to the Rosh, relatives should then become liable for a korban shevuas ha'edus. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, Even HaEzer, part II, siman 96, who writes that there is no difficulty. See also Tumim there regarding his distinction in this matter, and see what I write in Pitchei Teshuva to Even HaEzer, siman 178, end of seif katan 12.
