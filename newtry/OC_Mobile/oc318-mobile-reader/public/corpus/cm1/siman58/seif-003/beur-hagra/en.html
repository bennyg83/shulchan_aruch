(Collected note) Since, etc.; if, etc.; that he paid him, etc. This is the correct reading. The witnesses do not know that the creditor received the money as payment; they know only that he received money from the purchasers. His own admission is therefore needed to identify it as the proceeds of the ox. Consequently, the rule applies specifically where they paid him the value of the ox. Although his admission supplies the final link, it is effective against the document because it concerns money he personally received.
(Collected note) The document is void. According to the opinion in seif 2 that the document itself is not void, the same should apply here, as the Rosh and Ran write. The Tur and Rama may nevertheless hold that the Rif's ruling that the document is entirely void in this case is controlling, even though the Rosh and Ran dispute the broader principle.
