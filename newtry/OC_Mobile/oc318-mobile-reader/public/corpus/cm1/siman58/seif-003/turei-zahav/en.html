(Seif 3: If there are witnesses that he paid.) The Sema explains that payment from the ox proceeds is weaker for the creditor than ordinary “other-account” payment, because according to the Rambam the document is entirely cancelled here. That may be said according to the Rosh, but not according to the Rambam, who uses the same language of cancellation in both cases. The phrase “that he paid” is not exact. It means that witnesses saw the creditor receive the ox proceeds, even without an express statement of repayment. This is the added leniency according to Rabbi Yosef HaLevi. The following opinion is the Rambam, who goes further and cancels the document even when there are no witnesses to receipt, because once the creditor admits receiving the ox proceeds, the circumstances function like witnesses and defeat any migo.
