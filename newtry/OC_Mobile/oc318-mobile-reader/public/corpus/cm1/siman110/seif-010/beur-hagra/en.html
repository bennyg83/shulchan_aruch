For a woman’s ketubah, etc. This is derived from the Mishna in Ketubot 97a, which permits her to sell without a beit din. Although Bava Metzia 32a requires a panel of ordinary judges, that means persons competent in valuation, even unspecialized local judges, as Tosafot explain in Ketubot 98a, s.v. ‘for they say.’ It must therefore be without a guardian, since the sale is not conducted with the court’s knowledge.
