Tosafot prove that leaving the property with the minor and refusing to receive the adult claimant's testimony applies only where two conditions are present: the minor had ancestral possession, and the property is presently in the minor's possession. Sema. Tosafot in the chapter 'Shum HaYetomim' imply this, but Tosafot in the final chapter of Bava Kamma appear to say the opposite, that ancestral possession is effective even if Rabbi Yirmiyah had already been in possession. The Yam Shel Shlomo rules like the Tosafot cited by the Sema and writes that a textual error appears in the Bava Kamma Tosafot. The Shach responds that careful reading permits those Tosafot to be understood consistently with the Tosafot in 'Shum HaYetomim.' See Yam Shel Shlomo, final chapter of Bava Kamma, siman 7; above siman 149, seifim 20 and 23; and Responsa Maharashdam, siman 374.
