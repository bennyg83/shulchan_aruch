See Responsa Panim Meirot of my grandfather, part 3, siman 10, who writes that this does not mean a guardian is permitted to make a false claim. Rather, the novelty is that even if he advances an unusual argument that presently appears to have little substance and could later be disproved, we nevertheless appoint him, because perhaps he will reach a compromise beneficial to the orphans. But where even the guardian knows that the claim is false, he certainly may not plead it, as the Sema writes below, siman 290, seif katan 36. See there further.
