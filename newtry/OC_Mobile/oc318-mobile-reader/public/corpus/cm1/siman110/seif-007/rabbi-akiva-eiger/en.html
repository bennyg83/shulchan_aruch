[Shulchan Aruch] By delivering his statement to beit din. Note: If he delivers his statement before three witnesses, this is included in delivering his statement to beit din. Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, siman 7, writes that nevertheless these authorities agree that where a creditor holding a document claims and the father admitted that he was liable, the witnesses to the admission may be accepted against minor orphans. The principal collection rests on the document, and the witnesses are accepted only with respect to the concern that the father may have left money with which to pay the debt. See there, and see Responsa Lechem Rav, siman 228.
