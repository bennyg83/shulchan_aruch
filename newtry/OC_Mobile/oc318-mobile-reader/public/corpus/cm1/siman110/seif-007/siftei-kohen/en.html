Likewise, if their predecessor robbed it. This means that the predecessor also admitted in his handwriting before his death, and it refers back to the preceding clause. Maharshal writes this in siman 6 and gives additional distinctions there. Yet Baal HaTerumot, Gate 14, part 1, cited by the Beit Yosef in seif 15, appears to hold that testimony is accepted against minors in such a case. This is also the plain meaning of the Tur and is stated explicitly by Ir Shushan. The matter requires study. See the responsa of Rabbi Eliyahu ibn Chaim, simanim 15 and 18, and Maharshadam, siman 196.
