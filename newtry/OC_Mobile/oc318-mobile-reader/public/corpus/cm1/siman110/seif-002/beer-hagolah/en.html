Rabbeinu Yerucham, Netiv 26, cited by Beit Yosef in siman 108, seif 23, writes: If a creditor seized money that the father left to the orphans without witnesses, we do not remove it from him and he need not swear. If he seized it in the presence of witnesses and he has a document, we do not remove it from him, but we require him to swear. If he seized movable property and it still exists, it is left with a third party until the orphans mature; he then swears and collects. This applies specifically where he seized it now because of a debt owed to him. But if he claims that their father had given it to him as collateral, we do not remove it from him at all; he swears and collects.
