[Rama] Beit din has authority to collect it for him. Note: This is also stated in Responsa Rashba, siman 1015. See Responsa Ralbach, siman 35, which questions this. Chacham Tzvi, in his responsa printed in Beit Aharon, siman 3, writes that where orphans seek to collect money, beit din may compromise on their behalf if there is concern that otherwise the debt will later be lost or impaired.
