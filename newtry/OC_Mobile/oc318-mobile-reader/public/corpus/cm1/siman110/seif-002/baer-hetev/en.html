Seized. The Shach explains that the Tur's wording reflects concern that the father may have set aside other assets for payment. Therefore the creditor is permitted to seize property after death. Nevertheless, if he comes before beit din and admits that he seized after the father's death for a debt owed by the father, he must return it, because beit din rules that property belonging to minor orphans cannot be collected in this manner. The Tur means only that the seizure itself is not theft. The creditor should therefore not come before beit din at all, or should assert that he seized during the father's lifetime. Although it is permissible to act this way, if he admits in beit din that the seizure occurred after death, the seizure is ineffective while the heirs are minors, even after the Geonic enactment.
