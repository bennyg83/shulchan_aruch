There, in the name of Responsa Rosh, rule 85, siman 7. The Mechaber omitted the responsum's qualification that this applies if the document is authenticated, whereas if it is not authenticated we do not authenticate it. This is because he follows Rambam, chapter 12, law 6, that a document may be authenticated even while the orphans are minors. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam derived this from the ruling that a document may be authenticated outside the presence of the opposing litigant, Bava Kamma 112b. Rabbi Yirmiyah there on 112a did not say, 'I have a document,' but rather, 'I have witnesses.' Ba'al HaTerumot likewise writes this in Gate 14, as do the other poskim.
