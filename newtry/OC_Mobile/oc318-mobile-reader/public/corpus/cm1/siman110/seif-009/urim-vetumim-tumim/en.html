The Sema wondered at this, for we rule that a document may be authenticated in the absence of the opposing litigant. He sought to explain that this follows the Rosh, who holds that with minor orphans authentication is not performed. This is strained. It appears that Rabbenu Yerucham follows the reason that a document may be authenticated in the absence of the opposing litigant because authentication of documents is rabbinic, as most authorities hold. We have already clarified above in siman 46 that authentication of handwriting itself is of Torah force. If so, it is obvious that such authentication may not be performed in the absence of the opposing litigant, because the leniency applicable to rabbinic authentication does not apply. The Mechaber followed that view. This is precise.
