Note: See Responsa Torat Chesed of Maharach Shashon, part 1, simanim 32 and 40, where he resolves Rashach’s objection to Mahari Ben Lev, part 3, siman 35, who ruled that beit din may proceed against the property of minor orphans with respect to a debt within its fixed term. See there. Maharshadam, siman 197, writes that one cannot say, “I hold in accordance with some authorities who rule that witnesses to a will are not accepted.” When a defendant can say “I hold” because according to that view money will never be extracted from him, the argument is understandable. Here, however, the claim is merely that perhaps when the orphans grow up they will discover a contention that invalidates the will. That is certainly a remote concern, and one cannot invoke “I hold” on that basis. See Shach in this siman, seif katan 10.
