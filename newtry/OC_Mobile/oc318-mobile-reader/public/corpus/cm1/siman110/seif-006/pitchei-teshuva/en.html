Recognizable to beit din. See Baer Heitev, who writes that according to the Rambam the beit din need not recognize it, etc. Dagul MeRevavah writes: "See the Shach, siman 69, seif katan 13, and you will see that there is a distinction between a handwritten note and a document signed by witnesses. Examine carefully." See Ketzot HaChoshen and the gloss of the Gaon Rabbi Ozer on Baer Heitev. What Baer Heitev writes, "and it must be said that this refers to what precedes it," is a printing error. It should read: "and it appears to me that it refers also to what precedes it."
