["Unless there is an interest bearing loan upon them, etc. See the Sema, seif katan 2, through his statement that in this case they sell by appraisal even without announcing it for thirty days, etc. This is unless the creditor accepts upon himself not to take interest throughout the days of announcement, in which case they announce for thirty days, as stated in Rashi and Tosafos there."]
["Who accepted upon himself, etc. See the Sema, seif katan 3, through his statement that it appears the Gemara and the authorities establish the case this way as a greater novelty, etc. This is not correct, because the Gemara is compelled to establish the case where he accepted this upon himself. Otherwise, why would they announce for thirty days, as stated in Rashi and Tosafos there and in the Ran in Almana Nizonet? The authorities also intend this. Perhaps the Sema refers specifically to the Tur, and what he wrote about the Gemara is according to the Tur's view. Examine this carefully. See Responsa Maharshal, part 2, siman 113."]
["The beit din has authority to collect it for him. The same applies where a penalty is imposed upon repayment. So writes Responsa Rabbi Eliyahu ibn Chaim, siman 94. See Responsa Mabit, part 1, second collection, siman 214, and part 2, siman 229, and Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 127."]
