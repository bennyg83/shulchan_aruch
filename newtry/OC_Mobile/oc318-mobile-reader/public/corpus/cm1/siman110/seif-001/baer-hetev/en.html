With interest. In order that their principal not be consumed by continuing interest, it is in the minors' interest for beit din to attend to the matter and repay the debt from their assets while they are still minors. In such a case their property is sold according to appraisal even without waiting thirty days for public announcement, because any increase above the appraisal is uncertain, whereas the continuing interest is a definite loss. We do not set aside a certainty because of a possibility. This is the rule at the beginning of the chapter Shum HaYetomim. Accordingly, this differs from a sale made to pay a woman's ketubah, where one waits thirty days, unless the sale is made to prevent the estate from having to continue supporting her, in which case the property is likewise sold without waiting thirty days. See Rashi and Tosafot there. Sema.
The government. The same applies if a penalty accrues because of delayed payment. Responsa Raanach 94. See also Mabit I, second collection of questions, siman 214, and II, 229, and Responsa Maharam Alshich 127. Shach.
