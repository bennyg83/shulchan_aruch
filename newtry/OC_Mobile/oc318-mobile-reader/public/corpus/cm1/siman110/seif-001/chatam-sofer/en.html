Beginning of seif 1. Note: I copied from the enactments of the Four Lands printed at the end of Ateret Tzvi, siman 9: it was agreed that one may collect on a promissory note that contains no defect even from minor orphans. Thus, after the due date, collection is made with an oath; before the due date, the creditor collects without an oath. Their law is like that of adults. End quote. This appears to apply even to receiving testimony and validating the document. So too wrote the gaon, author of Chavat Daat, in a responsum.
