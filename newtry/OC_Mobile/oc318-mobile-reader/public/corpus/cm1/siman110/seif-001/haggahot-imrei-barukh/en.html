(Shach, seif katan 15) But in Tosafot in chapter HaGozel, etc. Note: See Ramban's novellae to Bava Batra 33a, who explains the case of Rava bar Sharshum, "you cannot say it is purchased in my hand," by this rule: since the orphans have the presumption of their father's ownership through the public report, beit din will not entertain the claimant's evidence of possession. See also Hagahot HaRosh to Bava Kamma in the sugya of closing the doors, and Tosafot to Bava Batra 32 in the case where a report had gone out against him.
