If they borrowed from the beit din — in this case the concern of tzerari does not apply, for they were the borrowers, and also "they are not people of mitzvah" does not apply, and also so as not to lock the door before borrowers — their law is that one collects from them, and also medium-quality [property] like other creditors, and all of this is so that people will be available to lend to them; and therefore they are preferred over the borrower himself, for regarding a borrower one needs at least a proclamation, and regarding them no proclamation is needed.
One does not attend to their property — because one does not accept testimony, etc.; but if it is known or they admitted that they borrowed, and they have reached the age of pe'utot, one must pay immediately — Meirat Einayim:
