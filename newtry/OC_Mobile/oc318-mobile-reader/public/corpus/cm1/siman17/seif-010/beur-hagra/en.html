(Collected note) A judge may not, etc. Torat Kohanim, Parashat Kedoshim, on "you shall not favor a poor person": lest you say, "this man is poor, and since the other is wealthy and obligated to support him, I will find in the poor man's favor so that he will be supported honorably"; therefore Scripture says, "you shall not favor a poor person." "You shall not honor the face of a great person": lest you say, "this man is wealthy and from a distinguished family; I will see his humiliation and how far I can disgrace him"; therefore Scripture says, "you shall not honor the face of a great person." This is likewise in the Sifrei on "the small as the great, etc."; see there. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) And if they came, etc. Mekhilta, Parashat Mishpatim, on "you shall not pervert the judgment of your poor person in his dispute": if a wicked person and a fit person stand before you in judgment, do not say, "since he is wicked, I will incline the judgment against him." Therefore it says, "you shall not pervert the judgment of your poor person in his dispute," meaning one who is poor in mitzvot. (End of collected note.)
