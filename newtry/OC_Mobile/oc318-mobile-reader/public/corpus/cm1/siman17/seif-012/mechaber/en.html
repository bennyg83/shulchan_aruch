When one litigant exaggerates before the judge and says to his fellow, "The judgment will obligate you to pay me such and such," if the law is not so the judge must tell him, "You are speaking falsely." Rama: If a litigant claims a small amount from his fellow and the judge sees that according to law the defendant owes him more than he claimed, the judge may not award more than was claimed. If he did so, it is an error of law and the ruling is reversed.
