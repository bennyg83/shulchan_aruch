The Sema challenges the Rama's ruling here and writes that the proof the Rama brought from the first chapter of Bava Batra concerning Ronya is not a proof, for the reason he explains at length. See there. The Shach defends the Rama's position and discusses the matter extensively. He further writes that when we say the judgment is reopened, this applies only so long as the judge has not yet transferred the money. If he has already collected everything due under the judgment and given it to the claimant, it appears that the judgment is not reopened, since the money has already been transferred and we apply the principle that money remains where it presently stands.
