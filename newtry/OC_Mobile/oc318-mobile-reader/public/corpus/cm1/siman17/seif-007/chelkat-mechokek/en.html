Some say that even if she has not yet married another man. This view is not cited here by Beit Yosef, but it is the Tur's own position, as he writes in siman 152: if she makes the claim outside her husband's presence she is brazen and is not believed even if she later marries or becomes betrothed, and when her husband arrives she maintains her claim. Beit Yosef cites this in Bedek HaBayit. The case is where the kiddushin occurred outside the city, for within the city Rosh already ruled that this is treated as being in his presence.
