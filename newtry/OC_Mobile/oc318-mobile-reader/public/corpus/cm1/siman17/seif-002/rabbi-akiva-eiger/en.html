[Shulchan Aruch] So-and-so, you are liable. Note: See Bach, who agrees with the view of Tosafot in Sanhedrin, s.v. "the litigants must stand," that at the time testimony is received the litigants must stand, because receiving testimony is comparable to the final judgment. He cites the Ran, who writes that this is explicit in the Yerushalmi. See Knesset HaGedolah.
