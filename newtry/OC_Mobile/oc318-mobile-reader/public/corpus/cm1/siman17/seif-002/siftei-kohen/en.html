A Torah scholar who comes to litigation, etc. See Imrei Binah, folio 75a, who did not know the source from which the author derived this ruling. Its source is the Ramban and the Ran in the chapter Shevuat HaEdut; see there. Their words also resolve the Imrei Binah's question as to why Rav Nachman had to cause a goose to fly up, for she was the wife of a scholar only after her husband's death. See there.
