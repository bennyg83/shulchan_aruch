And there is degradation. Where it is possible to avoid it, the court certainly takes degradation into account.
(Anthology) Also because of, etc. The term 'degradation' is not exact, since in the second chapter of Ketubot and the third chapter of Bava Batra we rule that degradation is not taken into account after the fact. Rather, initially one should be concerned lest degradation result. There the issue arose after the fact. This is explicit in Bava Batra 114a: 'If you should think,' etc.; see Rashbam, 113b, at the end of s.v. ratzu kotvin. Tosafot, 114a, s.v. neichush, disagree and explain that the concern is needlessly burdening the beit din, which likewise reflects degradation of the judges.
