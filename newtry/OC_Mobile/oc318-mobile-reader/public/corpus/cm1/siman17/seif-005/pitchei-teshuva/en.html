Standing by leaning. See Taz, Magen Avraham 141:2 and 422:11, Urim VeTumim here, Ateret Tzvi, and Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, siman 1. Birkei Yosef discusses this at length and concludes that the correct explanation is that of Rav Tzvi Ashkenazi in his glosses and of the Taz: Rama relied on the end of Rivash's discussion, which is lenient because the requirement is rabbinic and the verse is only an asmachta. According to this, when Rama is stringent concerning chalitzah in Even HaEzer 169, it is because the Sages were stringent there in several respects. This also explains why Rama did not cite in Orach Chaim 585 the ruling brought in Darkei Moshe in the name of Maharil that the shofar blower should not lean. Rama held that one need be stringent only for chalitzah, whereas regarding shofar we are lenient just as we are regarding witnesses and litigants. This is precisely the force of Rama's wording here that leaning is considered proper standing 'for this matter and the like,' meaning matters whose standing requirement is rabbinic.
