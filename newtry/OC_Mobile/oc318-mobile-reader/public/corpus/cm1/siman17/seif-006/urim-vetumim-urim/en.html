The judge should not hear through interpreters. Our practice is nevertheless to adjudicate cases involving foreign speakers whose language beit din does not understand, using interpreters. Sema explains that because the city has an established beit din and the parties appear before it, their participation is equivalent to acceptance of the procedure, and acceptance cures the defect. Bach explains more broadly that where there is no practical alternative, the use of interpreters is permitted, for otherwise society would become lawless. See Tumim.
