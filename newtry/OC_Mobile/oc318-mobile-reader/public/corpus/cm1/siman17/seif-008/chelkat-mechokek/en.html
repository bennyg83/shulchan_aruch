Some say she is not believed at all regarding money. Meaning that even though she is believed to remarry, she is not believed to extract monetary rights. This was not actually explained above by Rama's statement that the matter had been addressed previously. The immediately preceding 'some say,' namely Raavad, holds that she is believed neither to remarry nor to collect a ketubah, while the other authorities believe her for both. Maharik, however, shoresh 72, infers from Tosafot that although she is believed to remarry, she is not believed to collect her ketubah. See his lengthy discussion.
