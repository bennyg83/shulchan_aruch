In the name of Maharshal, in the Beit Yosef, s.v. “He shall not teach one litigant,” through “since,” etc. Tur does not dispute the rule concerning one witness. He too holds that the judge should not tell the litigant that the witness is not believed, for otherwise Rav Huna’s Yerushalmi statements would contradict. This is unlike the Sema. Lechem Mishneh writes likewise.
