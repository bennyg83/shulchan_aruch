According to this, the rule does not depend upon the litigants' wishes at all, but beit din itself must arrange matters this way. Therefore the correct text in Shulchan Aruch is "therefore." Sema. Bach rules that it depends only upon the litigants' wishes, and Urim VeTumim considers that view primary.
