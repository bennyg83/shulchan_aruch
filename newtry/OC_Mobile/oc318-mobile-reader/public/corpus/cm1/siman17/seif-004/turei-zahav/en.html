Seif 5, in the gloss: “Only where the judge knows,” etc. The Sema does not explain this clearly. Responsa Maharil 222 teaches that if one heard a litigant’s words without knowing he would later serve as judge, and afterward willingly became judge, he did not violate “You shall not bear” when he heard them. Rama likewise teaches that although the hearing was permitted, later appointment does not retroactively create a prohibition. Where no prohibition existed at the time of hearing, none arises merely because he later judges.
