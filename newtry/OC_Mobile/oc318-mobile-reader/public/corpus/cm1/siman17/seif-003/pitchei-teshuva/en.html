One sits and one stands. The Bach writes that this means it is forbidden to tell one litigant to stand while allowing the other to remain seated, or to tell one to sit while allowing the other to remain standing. This is explicit in the Tosefta: one may not cause one to stand while seating the other. However, if they came before us and one sat on his own while the other stood on his own, the Bach holds that beit din need not intervene. Knesset HaGedolah, letter 2, disputes this and forbids even that situation; see there.
