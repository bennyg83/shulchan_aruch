In the presence of her husband. Rashba, Responsa siman 1253, cited by the Beit Yosef here, seif katan 4, brings proof that even if she began to make her statement not in her husband's presence, one cannot say that initially she was not believed and now she merely persists in maintaining her falsehood. Rather, she is always believed whenever she makes the statement in her husband's presence and has the audacity to say it before him.
