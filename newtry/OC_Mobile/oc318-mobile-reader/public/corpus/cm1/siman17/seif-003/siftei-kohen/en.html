That the litigants are seated, etc. See the Bach, who writes: Nevertheless, the witnesses and the litigants themselves, every person whose heart is touched by fear of Heaven and whose ancestors stood at Mount Sinai, should be mindful of the honor of Torah and of the mitzvah expressed in the verse, "The two men shall stand."
