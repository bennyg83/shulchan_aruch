Seif 1 “One shall not speak,” etc. Even if the judge said nothing to the first litigant, there can be a prohibition in telling the second to shorten his words, namely if he says so before the second begins. Rambam addresses that case. The Tur addresses a litigant who has begun and is speaking at length; the judge may tell him to be concise, as is common practice. Nevertheless, if the judge expressly allowed the first to state everything, he may not tell the second to shorten under any circumstances.
