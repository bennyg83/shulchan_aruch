Projections and balconies may not extend into the public domain unless they are higher than a camel with its rider and do not darken or obstruct the public way. A person may recess his wall into his own property and then extend a projection over the recessed area. If he recessed the wall but did not build the projection, he may build it whenever he wishes. He may never restore the wall to its former line, because a boundary that the public has acquired through use may not be impaired. Rama: This applies where the public actually walks upon the recessed strip. If they do not walk there, such as where a platform was built and people merely pass loads across it, he may restore the wall to its former place.
