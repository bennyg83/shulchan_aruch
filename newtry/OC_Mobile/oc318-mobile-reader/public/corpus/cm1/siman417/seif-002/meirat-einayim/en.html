One may not project beams, etc., into the public domain. The Tur writes that in an alleyway one may do so with the consent of the residents of the alleyway, unlike the public domain, which has no particular owners who can consent. Ri explains that this refers to a closed alleyway. If it is open from end to end and the public passes through it, it has the law of the public domain. The Beit Yosef cites this, and it is surprising that the Mechaber omitted it.
