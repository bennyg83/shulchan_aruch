Because it is necessary to appoint a guardian for the minor's half. The reason the text says 'half,' although it begins by stating that only one third belongs to the minor, appears to be that it follows the wording and sequence of the Gemara. In the Gemara itself, the reason the first clause says 'one third' is, as the Nimukei Yosef writes, that this suffices to establish that the daughter is not placed in possession of the estate. The Gemara therefore did not concern itself with saying that perhaps both captive women had died. In the latter clause, however, Abaye was compelled to say that one half is given to the sister and a guardian is appointed for the other half on behalf of the child. Rava therefore likewise states, 'Since we appoint a guardian for half,' although he could have said, 'Since we appoint a guardian for one third.' He used that formulation because he was responding to Abaye and speaking apart from the sister's share. The Rambam adopted the Gemara's wording, as explained. This is clear.
