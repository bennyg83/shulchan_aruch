One does not place a relative, etc. One might ask that above in siman 250 the Tur and Mechaber already ruled that women are not appointed as guardians. The answer is that the reason women are ordinarily not appointed is that it is not their usual practice to go out, conduct business, and exert themselves over property. Here she must in any event exert herself over her own share or appoint a sharecropper and supervise him. Her own need would incidentally protect the minor's share as well, were it not for the separate rule that one does not place a relative over a minor's property.

The minor shall descend to the one third that is his share, etc. Nimukei Yosef explains that the case concerns a minor who knows how to supervise and manage property. Therefore the portion that is truly his is given to him, but he is not fit to serve as guardian over the shares of others. The Rosh writes regarding the Gemara's statement that one third is given to the child: if he knows how to manage his property and exert himself over it, he may do so. If his father appointed a guardian for him or placed him under the supervision of a responsible householder, they will manage it.
