In the name of the Raavad, from the Mishnah in Bava Batra 143b, where one left adult and minor sons and the adults improved the estate. It is evident there that this applies even with authorization of beit din, as they say, 'See what our father left us,' and it also implies they all eat at one table. So writes the Maggid Mishneh. He cites some commentators and adds that Rambam's view is also so, as explained below at the end of siman 287, seif 1.
