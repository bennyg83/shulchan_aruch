Money. The Sema explains that with money and movable property, even when they are in another person's hands, there is concern that he may sell or conceal them and later claim, 'There never was such a thing; they were never delivered to me.' We therefore must rely upon beit din to deliver them into his hands and to write and sign a record as testimony and proof. If so, the same procedure can be used with a relative. See Responsa Rashdam, simanim 308, 312, and 456, and Responsa Maharam of Lublin, siman 12.
