He takes all the fruits. It appears that he takes even fruits that grew on their own, and so is stated in Hagahot Ashri; see there. This resolves Tosafot's question against Rashi, s.v. vekhulan shamin lahen ke'aris. Rashi's statement that "the remaining fruits shall be left" can refer to fruits of the tree for which he exerted no effort. Fruits produced through his labor, however, he consumes entirely. The baraita's statement that "we do not allow them to descend" implies that they are not allowed to take possession at all.
