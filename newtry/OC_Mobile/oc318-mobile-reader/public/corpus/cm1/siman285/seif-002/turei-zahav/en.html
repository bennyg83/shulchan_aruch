(But one who departed knowingly.) Raavad asks: “Regarding one who departed knowingly and concerning whom they heard that he died, what source does Rambam have that we remove the heir?” At first glance Rambam’s own rulings also contradict each other. In seif 5, quoted by the Mechaber, he writes that where one departed knowingly and they heard that he died, a relative is placed over his property. According to Maggid Mishneh’s explanation immediately above, however, the present case concerns an heir who seeks to take permanent possession of the inheritance and sell it, rather than serve as an administrator. This resolves Rambam’s internal contradiction: we remove him from ownership and place him only as an administrator. Nevertheless, Raavad’s question about the source for removing him remains.
