According to his intention. Meaning, had he wanted others to take possession of it, he would have instructed them to do so before his departure. See Responsa Rashakh, part II, siman 65; Responsa Maharam of Lublin, siman 12; and Responsa Rabbi Moshe Alshich, siman 58.
A captive. We do not say that because he gave no instructions concerning the property he left behind, thereby revealing that he did not want others to take possession of it, the same applies to property that came to him after his departure. Rather, what he revealed, he revealed; regarding what he did not reveal, we do not say so. Sema.
