If they heard, etc. Rosh likewise rules this way. According to his approach, completely abandoned property means no report of death was heard. Rambam, however, explains the baraita as involving a report of death but a claim to divide the inheritance. He derives from the Gemara that an adult relative may otherwise be installed. Rashba disputes Rambam and maintains that one who did not know he had property is treated like a captive.
(Supplement) If they heard, etc. Although the Gemara could have established the case where death was reported, it was discussing one who entered as an heir. Rambam derives his rule from the later passage concerning the minor. (End of supplement.) See 40.
