A captive, etc. See Tosafot there. Rambam avoids the difficulties by explaining that the baraita concerns one who descends to divide the property. Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel explicitly treats abandoned property like the property of captives, while the first Tanna disagrees. The same dispute governs whether a relative is initially installed to manage the estate. Property whose owner is completely absent is treated stringently even when a report of death was heard.
