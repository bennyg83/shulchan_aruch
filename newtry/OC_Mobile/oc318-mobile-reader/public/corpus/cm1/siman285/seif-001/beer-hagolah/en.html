The wording of Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot Nachalot, law 4, from the baraita in Bava Metzia 38b concerning one who descends into the property of a captive. The Maggid Mishneh explains that Rambam interprets this as one who descended intending to possess and inherit, hence the wording 'one who descended' rather than 'we appoint,' as explained in the next seif. Therefore Rambam appears to hold that when a person was captured, even without a report of death we appoint a relative over his property to manage it as an aris. If death was reported, the relative is raised one level, so that if he descended as an heir he is not removed. But one who departed knowingly is different. Without a report of death, no relative is appointed even as an aris. If death was reported, he is not raised so far that one who descended as heir remains, but he is raised enough that a relative may be appointed over the land as an aris. This removes the Raavad's objection.
