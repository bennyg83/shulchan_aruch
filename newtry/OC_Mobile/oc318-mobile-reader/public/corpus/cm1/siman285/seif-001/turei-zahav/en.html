(Seif 1, one who was taken captive.) Rambam’s words require substantial analysis. Raavad objects: “Our teachers said that even without hearing that he died, a relative is placed over his property. Rambam says that even if they heard he died, if heirs entered the estate we do not remove them.” Maggid Mishneh explains that Rambam discusses heirs who entered in order to sell the inheritance. Only if they entered by force do we refrain from removing them, but we do not authorize such entry initially. To work the land and receive an administrator’s share, however, a relative is appointed even initially. This requires study.
