The rule that a relative is not placed in possession of a minor's property applies, according to the Bach, where the captive is a woman, because in that case we are concerned that she may have died, but not where the captive is a man. The Shach rejects this as astonishing and unsupported. From where could one derive such a distinction? The Beit Yosef, citing Rabbeinu Yerucham at the end of section 13, states explicitly that there is no distinction. Whenever there are both an adult and a minor, we must be concerned that the captive may have died, and a relative is therefore not placed in possession. This appears to the Shach to be the principal ruling.
