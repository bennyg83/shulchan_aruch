He may collect nothing with it except from unencumbered property. It appears this means only the remaining balance that has not yet been paid. As to the portion already paid, the Rosh writes in the fourth chapter of Nedarim, and I cited his language in Derishah, that the lender cannot again collect it unless there was a new writing and delivery like the transfer of a debt document. This differs from Mordechai, cited by Rama in siman 48, that if the borrower again delivered the document before witnesses it may be used for collection, because there the borrower borrowed again that same day. As to encumbered property, even the unpaid balance cannot be collected, because we fear the lender may also collect the half that was already paid, like the law of an antedated document. But from the borrower himself the Sages did not make that decree. Thus Rama's phrase 'he collects nothing except from unencumbered property' means he cannot collect anything from encumbered property, but from unencumbered property he may collect only the amount not yet paid. Darkei Moshe, however, says he collects the entire amount from unencumbered property. If so, it should have added that this requires writing and delivery. The matter requires study.
