Only from unencumbered property. Sema says that for the half not repaid he collects from unencumbered property, while for the half already repaid he cannot collect unless he acquired the claim by written assignment and delivery. The Shach objects that even written assignment and delivery cannot help because the lien has already been waived. See Tumim at length. I conclude that he may collect the entire amount from unencumbered property and requires no written assignment and delivery. This is certainly so here, where a kinyan exists. Even if there were no kinyan and the arrangement had an asmachta element, if the borrower voluntarily instructed the escrow holder to give the entire document to the lender, or personally gave it himself, the lender can collect the whole amount from unencumbered property without a formal assignment. This follows the rule above in siman 48.
From unencumbered property. According to the opinion above in siman 43 that an antedated document is totally invalid, the present rule must mean only that if the debtor admits the debt, the document is not treated as mere pottery and he cannot claim total denial. But if he claims payment, he is believed. See Tumim. I also conclude that the Shach is correct that the half never repaid may be collected from encumbered property. There is no penalty as to that half, because no wrongdoing occurred when the deed was written and when the lien originally took effect.
