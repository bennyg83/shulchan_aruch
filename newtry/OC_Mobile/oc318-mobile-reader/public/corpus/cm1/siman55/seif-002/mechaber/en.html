This rule, that the document is given to the opposing party, applies where the debtor said, "If I do not pay by such and such a day, the money I have already paid shall be considered a gift and the document shall remain fully in force as before." Otherwise, the document is one whose lien has already been discharged to the extent of the money paid. See seif katan 4. Such a document may collect nothing from encumbered property, but only from unencumbered property.
