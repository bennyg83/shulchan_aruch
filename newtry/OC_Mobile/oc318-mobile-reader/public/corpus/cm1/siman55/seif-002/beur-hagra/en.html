And he does not collect. This is like a predated loan document. See the Rosh and Shach there.
(Collected note) And he does not, etc. Darkei Moshe writes that the entire debt may be collected from unencumbered property. The Sema objects that the Rosh's second answer requires a written assignment and delivery, while the Shach says even those do not help unless the document says, 'Acquire it and all its liens,' which is absent here. The Rama apparently holds that written assignment and delivery are unnecessary in this case, as in siman 66, seif 1. As to the basic law, the language of Tur and Shulchan Aruch at the ends of simanim 57 and 117 implies that nothing is collected even from unencumbered property. According to the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch in siman 43, seif 7, however, Bava Metzia 17a implies otherwise regarding a predated document. Rivash assumes that here even unencumbered property cannot be collected, and on that basis disputes the Rambam concerning predated documents. See page 382.
