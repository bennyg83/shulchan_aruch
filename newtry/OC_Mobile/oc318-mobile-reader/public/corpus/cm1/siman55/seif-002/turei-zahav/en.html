(In the name of Maharshal, in the Tur.) Through “the obligor deposited it with this person, and secondly, perhaps he never borrowed,” etc. This might seem to apply even to an inter vivos gift document involving a kinyan, because perhaps the donor instructed that the document be returned only to him. The Baal HaTerumot indeed explains the Mishnah of a found deed of gift this way. Yet at the end of siman 250 the Tur cites the Rosh that an inter vivos gift is acquired by the kinyan alone even without the document, and the document should be returned to the recipient; the Mishnah concerns only a deathbed gift. The Tur therefore deliberately frames the present case as lender and borrower, not donor and recipient. He holds that a kinyan alone transfers an ordinary gift even if the donor said to return the document to him, in accordance with the authorities cited in siman 39 and siman 65.
