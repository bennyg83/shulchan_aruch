(Rama, seif 1) The third party custodian is exempt, etc. Note: In Responsa Rashba there is a case in which part of a debt was paid, the document was deposited with a third party, and the custodian returned the document to the lender. See there. One can say that specifically in such a case the custodian is exempt, because when he returned it to the lender he did not intend that the lender should claim and collect more than was actually owed under the document. If so, this does not conflict with the law of witnesses who signed falsely.
