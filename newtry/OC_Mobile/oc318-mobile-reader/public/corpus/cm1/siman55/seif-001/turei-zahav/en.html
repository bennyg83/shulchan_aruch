(Gloss to seif 2: Only from unencumbered property.) This implies that the entire debt may be collected, because the creditor acquired the document through delivery, in accordance with the Rosh’s first answer. The Rama apparently follows that answer because the Mordechai agrees that delivery is effective, as the Rama states in siman 48, even against encumbered property when the document was returned the same day. Although we generally require both writing and delivery to transfer a debt instrument, this case may differ because the document was originally written in this creditor’s name and only its lien was forgiven; renewed delivery alone may suffice. Alternatively, even the Rosh’s second answer may concede that delivery before witnesses is effective. Here the document was delivered to an escrow holder without witnesses, as explained in siman 48. This is the Rama’s view; the Sema’s explanation is strained.
