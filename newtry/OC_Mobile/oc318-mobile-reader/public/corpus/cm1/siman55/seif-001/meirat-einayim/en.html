The third-party custodian is exempt. This is only indirect causation, not actionable causative damage. It differs from the laws above in siman 46:37, where witnesses who admit falsely signing must pay under dina degarmi, and similarly in simanim 29 and 38. Tosafot and Rosh distinguish between actionable causative damage and mere indirect causation, depending on whether the loss occurs immediately and is certain. The Tur and Mechaber explain this below in siman 386. According to what I wrote in Derishah and Sema there, Rashba and those with him distinguish between loss occurring before the person undertook to pay and loss occurring after he accepted liability under excommunication. On that basis one might have said that above in siman 46 the Mechaber copied the Rosh, while here Rama copied Rashba. But if so Rama should have stated that the matter is disputed. Therefore the first explanation is primary, and the Rosh too agrees here.
