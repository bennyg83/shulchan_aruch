The law of one who steals a purse on Shabbos. It contains one seif.
There is a thief exempt from payment when liability to death and payment arise simultaneously. Thus, one who steals a purse on Shabbos without lifting it in the owner's domain, but drags it into the public domain and it is lost there, is exempt. Rama: If it was lost only afterward he is liable, and certainly if still intact he must return it. Others say that whenever it is no longer intact he is exempt. Since the Shabbos violation, theft, and damage occurred together, he is exempt from payment. But if he lifted it in the private domain and only afterward carried it into the public domain and threw it into a river, he must pay, because liability for theft arose before liability for the capital Shabbos offense. The same applies in similar cases.
