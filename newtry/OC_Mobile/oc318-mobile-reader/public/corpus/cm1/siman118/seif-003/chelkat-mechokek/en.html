The condition of retaining a portion, etc. This is the language of Ran, responsum 54, copied by Beit Yosef below in siman 160, seif katan 9. The questioner reasoned as follows. The husband had obligated himself that if his wife died during his lifetime, she would leave one-third of the dowry, or a fixed amount, to her heirs. She died as a shomeret yavam. Since the yavam inherits her property as the husband would, perhaps he must also pay the retained portion, just as the husband's heirs are obligated to bury her in exchange for the dowry they inherit. Ran answered that burial is a rabbinic enactment: whoever inherits her ketubah must bury her. The retained-share clause, however, is a monetary condition. We have only what was actually stipulated, namely, if she dies during her husband's lifetime. No stipulation was made regarding death during the yavam's lifetime. There is therefore no retained share in such a case; the property follows ordinary law. Ran ruled similarly in responsum 46. See below in siman 160.
