But not weekday garments. The responsum explains that the communal enactment said, "whatever is found belonging to him, the widow shall divide with the heirs." Her weekday garments were not property "found belonging to him," because he had no ownership in them. Moreover, the division replaces collection of her ketubah, and even now those garments are not charged against her. This implies that the authority held that weekday garments are not appraised against an widow for her ketubah. I do not understand this, for Ketubot 54a expressly says regarding an orphan and an widow, "strip and leave," meaning remove her garments. That was ruled above in siman 99:1. The first reason also requires study because even her Shabbat garments should not be included in "whatever is found belonging to him" according to the ruling above in siman 90:15.
