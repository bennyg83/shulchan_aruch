And in the first hand, we were the debt against two fields and so that it was already prey of the first field for a second round, and not the first one to say, I gave you a place that was connected to both of them:
He forecloses. Why can the first purchaser not say, 'If you remain silent, fine; if not, I will return my foreclosure deed to the second purchaser,' thereby changing the collection route? See Tumim for my discussion according to Sema and Shach.
And they continue in a circle, etc. This does not mean that the parties literally continue collecting back and forth forever. Rather, we view the situation as though such circular collection would continue, which would ultimately force a compromise among them. Therefore we avoid pointless circling and make the compromise immediately. Sema. Ritva writes the same, and this is also implied by Tosafot at the conclusion of the sugya.
