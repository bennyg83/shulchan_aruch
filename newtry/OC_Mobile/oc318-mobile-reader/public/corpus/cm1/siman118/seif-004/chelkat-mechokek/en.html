Even though the heirs have the power, etc. Meaning that ordinarily the enactment applies only if the widow demands her entire ketubah. Then the heirs can discharge her by giving her half the property. But if she never demands the ketubah, they must continue supporting her against their will, as explained above in siman 93. Even if there is a local enactment allowing the heirs to discharge her whenever they wish, until they notify her that they are ready to do so they remain obligated to provide support.
