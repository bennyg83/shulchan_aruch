According to the custom of sailors. Responsa Rabbi Moshe Rothenburg, end of siman 20, writes that custom is effective only if it requires the owner of gold to contribute more, because there is some basis in Torah law for treating more valuable cargo differently. But if the custom requires the owner of gold to contribute less than the owner of iron, he argues that the custom is ineffective. His words are not persuasive, and the editor in the addenda at the end of the volume already questions them. See Nachalat Tzvi.
