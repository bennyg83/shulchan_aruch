As in Abiddida, there is a saying that it is possible to take a fee and how much it is to be paid and how much it is written here in the diaspora:
But for loading, etc. The Gemara proves this. Otherwise the Torah would not have needed to write the law of unloading, because it could be derived a fortiori from loading. Loading involves neither animal suffering nor monetary loss, yet the Torah obligates assistance. Certainly unloading, which involves both loss and animal suffering, should be obligatory. The verse therefore teaches unloading separately so that we should not say, "It is enough for what is derived by an a fortiori argument to be like its source," and conclude that unloading too may be done only for pay. Rather, unloading must be done without charge.
