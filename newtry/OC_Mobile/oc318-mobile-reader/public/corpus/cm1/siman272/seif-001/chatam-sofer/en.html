Sema seif katan 18, "a prohibited hatred." Note: He did not quote the wording accurately. His intent is only as Tosafot write there on 32b, and as Shitah Mekubetzet cites in the name of the Ritva, who writes in the name of the Ramban that the case concerns an enemy toward whom he acts with prohibited hatred because he hates him. Meaning, the hatred itself is already prohibited by Torah law.
