Bet) In the second chapter of Bava Metzia 32a, Rabbi Yose HaGelili says in the Mishnah that if the animal was loaded beyond its normal burden, etc., implying that the Sages disagree. This is also stated in the Gemara on 32b, and Rambam rules like them.
A baraita at the beginning of 33a. The Beit Yosef writes that the Rif and Rambam omitted this because even where one is exempt from the mitzvah of unloading for the ordinary reason, there remains an obligation because of animal suffering, as explained below in seifim 8 and 9.
