The Kesef Mishneh asks that if so, the Gemara's question in Bava Metzia returns: if relieving animal suffering is d'oraisa, what difference is there whether the owner is an Israelite enemy or a gentile enemy? One can answer that the Gemara could have said that for an Israelite one must help without payment, while for a gentile one may charge, as Tosafot write there and as the Rama writes above in the gloss.
This is Rambam's wording in chapter 13 of Hilkhot Rotzeach U'Shemirat HaNefesh, halachah 14.
Gimel) The Kesef Mishneh asks that the verse "when you see the donkey of your enemy" refers to unloading, as indicated by "crouching under its burden," whereas the verse concerning loading does not mention an enemy. He answers that since the Torah equated an enemy with a friend regarding unloading, we infer the same equality regarding loading.
