As it is stated, "You shall surely help." This is not derived from the repetition in "help, you shall help" and "raise, you shall raise." The simple words "help" and "raise" themselves imply even one hundred times. The additional words are expounded in the Gemara to teach that even if the owner is not with him he remains obligated, as the Mechaber writes below in seif 7. See the Perishah.
Up to a parsah. This is the Gemara's and authorities' version. The Tur writes "up to a mil." In the Perishah I wrote that the Tur apparently had a version reading "mil," and I gave a substantial explanation for his text.
