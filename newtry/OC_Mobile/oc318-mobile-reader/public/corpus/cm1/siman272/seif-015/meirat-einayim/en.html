And they should not depart from the custom of donkey drivers. Rashi explains that if the local custom is to collect in every situation according to the number of people, or conversely according to the amount of property, they follow that custom. It appears that the same applies in our time when people travel together from city to city to a fair and soldiers arrive to rob them. If they settle with them for a larger or smaller sum, the amount is collected according to the value of the property. Likewise, if they must send messengers ahead to investigate a safe route because of robbers, that expense is assessed according to property value. If there is also actual danger to life, half the expense should be assessed according to the number of people and half according to the value of the property, unless the group has an established custom, in which case they follow it.
