A caravan. See Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 10, siman 42, and see what the Bach writes in the name of the Mordechai.
The road. So that they not be endangered by wild animals and bandits. This is the language of the Tur. As for Rama's second gloss in siman 163, seif 3, that if a group traveling in the desert loses its way and there is danger to life, the assessment is collected according to the number of persons, perhaps he means that it is collected also according to the number of persons, in accordance with the Mechaber there, upon whose ruling he comments 'and likewise.' Alternatively, that case concerns a situation in which there are no plunderers who come because of the travelers' money.
