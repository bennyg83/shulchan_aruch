If the custom is to assess payment for the guide according to property or according to persons, they follow the custom. The Sema writes that nowadays, when a group travels together from town to town to a fair and soldiers come to rob them without danger to life, all expenses are assessed according to property. If there is an established custom among traveling groups, it controls. The Bach writes that if a troop attacked them and took property only from some members, all participants must reimburse those victims proportionately. He concludes that this is the accepted law and brings proof. This requires study.
