If one finds another's animal lying under its burden, he must unload and reload it even when the owner is absent, as Scripture repeats the commands unconditionally. Why then does it say 'with him'? If the owner sits aside and says, 'The commandment is yours; unload alone if you wish,' the passerby is exempt, though if the owner is old or ill he remains obligated. Rama: The owner must pay the loader's wage, but unloading is free unless local custom provides otherwise.
