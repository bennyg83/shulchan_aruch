Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 8. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is disputed by the Geonim. Some require a claim of two silver ma'ah because the phrase 'money or utensils,' from which the minimum of two silver is derived, appears in the section of guardians. Rambam's teacher Rabbi Yosef Ibn Migash holds that guardians do not require it, because the passages are intermingled and the requirement applies only to an oath on partial admission.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Geonim agree to this. It is explained by the dispute of Amoraim in Bava Kamma 106b and 107a, and they rule like Rav Chiya bar Yosef that the passages are intermingled. This is explained in the Halakhot. It is also evident at the end of chapter 4 of Bava Batra, 70b, as Tosafot explain, that even one who claims the entire deposit was lost by unavoidable accident must take a Torah oath.
Some Geonim hold so, and Ramban and Rashba agreed. The Tur cites this in Ramban's name and in the name of his father the Rosh. This is also the view of Baal HaMaor, and the Ran agreed with them. The Beit Yosef writes that their position should govern because they are the majority and the later authorities.
