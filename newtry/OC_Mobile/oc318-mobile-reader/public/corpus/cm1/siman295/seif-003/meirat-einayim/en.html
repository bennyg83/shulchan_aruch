Even if the deposited item was worth only one perutah, he swears, etc. Meaning, even if it is worth only one perutah, the oath of custodians applies. Although we derive from the words "money or utensils" in the custodians' passage that the claim ordinarily must involve at least two silver ma'ot, some authorities hold that this minimum does not apply to every law of custodians. There is also an intermingling of scriptural sections, as explained at the beginning of siman 88. The Tur there records the dispute whether two silver ma'ot are required. It is surprising that the Mechaber there rules without qualification that they are not required, while here he cites last the authorities who require two silver ma'ot. The Beit Yosef here writes that their view should be followed because they are the majority and later authorities. We must therefore say that he relied there upon what he writes here, which is the primary place for this law.
