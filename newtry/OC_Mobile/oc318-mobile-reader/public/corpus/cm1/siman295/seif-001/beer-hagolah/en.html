Alef) This is the language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 6 of Hilkhot She'eilah U'Pikadon.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that although this distinction is not explicit in the Gemara, it is reasonable: so long as the item remains intact and is fungible, why suspect that the custodian would pay its value and take the object without the owner's permission? The Gemara speaks only of the concern that he set his eyes upon the object, etc.
Gimel) This is Rambam's wording and the Tur copies it. The Mishnah in the third chapter of Bava Metzia 33b explicitly speaks of an animal and utensils.
Dalet) A statement of Rav Huna there on 32b.
Heh) The Beit Yosef writes that this is implied by the Gemara's challenge to Rav Huna from the Mishnah concerning one who lends against collateral.
Vav) This is explained in the Gemara there on 32a.
Zayin) It is explicit in the sugya there on 32b.
