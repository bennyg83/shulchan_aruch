He concludes that according to Rif and Rosh, a borrower acquires the right to the double payment only if he actually paid, even if he stipulated that he would have the liability of an unpaid custodian. For other custodians, even if they undertook the liability of a borrower, a declaration "I will pay" is generally sufficient. According to Rashi, however, the first version of the Gemara may rest on both rationales stated there, and it would then follow that even another custodian who undertook the liability of a borrower must actually pay. VeShav HaKohen adds that the Rambam may also hold this way. Accordingly it remains doubtful whether a custodian other than a borrower who stipulated for borrower's liability acquires the relevant rights merely by saying, "I will pay," or only after actual payment.
