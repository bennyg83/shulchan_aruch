The man, etc. Some disagree and limit the rule to hidden money, as in the law of sale. The Gemara's challenge from a wife who borrowed must concern hidden money. Rambam, however, holds that once the Gemara answers that a loan is different, the distinction applies even to money that was not hidden, because even according to the view that one does not assume she merely wished to reveal her funds, the lender has no claim against the husband.
