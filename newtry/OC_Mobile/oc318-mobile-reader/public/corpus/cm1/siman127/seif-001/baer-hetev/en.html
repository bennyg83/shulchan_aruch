The Sema writes that according to this reason and this opinion, even if the husband or master does not make a definite claim that the money belongs to him, and even if a promissory note was written for the money and delivered to them, they nevertheless have no claim against him. The Maggid Mishneh writes likewise. See Responsa Mabit I, 89, and Responsa Mahara Sasson 198.
They divide. This view holds that the rule applies specifically where the money was hidden when the loan was made, because then we say that his purpose was merely to induce disclosure of the money. If the money was not hidden, this does not apply. Even where it was hidden, they must make a definite claim. The source of these laws is explained in Even HaEzer 85:6.
