When does this apply, etc. This follows Rashi's interpretation, which Beit Yosef correctly attributes to Tur. Darkei Moshe's reading is not correct. Earlier editions of Rif had a different text concerning conception and birth in holiness, but Rashba rejected it, for in that case repayment is required. Tur generally follows Rosh, and Rosh cites only Rashi's interpretation. Tosafot, Rash, and Rosh offer additional resolutions. Rosh's answer that Rav Mari followed his mother and was not treated as a convert is primary. Tur's language, 'a loan or deposit,' follows that view. Rashba and Ran cite both Rashi and Ri without deciding.
