Although the replacement deed states that it was written not for collection and thus itself indicates a defect, namely that he previously held another deed, lost it, and came to beit din for a replacement based on the original kinyan, one might think no one would believe him later if he said this document reflects a second purchase. Yet we fear that he may say: I never bought it previously. I bought it only from this later date. I merely feared that my deed might be lost, so I asked the witnesses to sign two deeds for me, one ordinary complete deed and this second deed stating that it was not for collection. The ordinary deed was lost and this one remained. The date on this document will then appear to prove that I purchased from that date. Sema.
