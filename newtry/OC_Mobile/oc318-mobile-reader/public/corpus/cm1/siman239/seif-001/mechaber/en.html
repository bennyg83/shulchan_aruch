One who claims that his deed of purchase was lost, and how another deed is written for him. It contains two seifim.
If a person says, "The deed by which I purchased a certain field from so-and-so was lost," his request to write another deed is accepted. Rama: Likewise, when he initially purchases the field, two or three such deeds may be written for him. The replacement deed must state: "This deed may not be used to collect from encumbered property or from unencumbered property. It was written only to establish title to this field, so that the seller or his heirs cannot remove it from the purchaser." Some say the replacement must bear the date of the original deed rather than a later date, lest the purchaser later resell the field to the original seller and then produce the replacement deed, dated after that resale, claiming that he subsequently bought it back.
