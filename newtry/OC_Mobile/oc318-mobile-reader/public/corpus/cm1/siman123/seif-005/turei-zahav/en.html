(Seif 14) He should take care not to write. According to Sema, seif katan 21, the matter depends on whose kerchief is used for the formal acquisition. If so, the authorities should have discussed the kerchief, not the wording of the document. One cannot answer that the two are necessarily linked, so that “we acquired” is written only when the witnesses' kerchief was used. In the era of the Talmud the acquirer customarily supplied the kerchief, yet documents still used the formula “and we acquired,” as stated in Yesh Nohalin. It therefore appears that the witnesses may give their own kerchief to a gentile so that he acquires, but they must not write “and we acquired from him,” since that wording implies that they acted as agents to acquire on his behalf, and there is no agency for a gentile. They should instead write, “the gentile acquired,” meaning that through the witnesses' kerchief he acquired the specified debt. This appears clear.
