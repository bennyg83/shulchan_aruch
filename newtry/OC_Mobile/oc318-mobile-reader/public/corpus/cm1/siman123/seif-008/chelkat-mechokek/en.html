It is rabbinically invalid. The Sages decreed concerning the standard text because of the essential operative text. Regarding the operative text itself, the get may be void by Torah law because writing requires agency, and having an adult stand over the writer does not suffice. Although with a get written while attached to the ground the Sages did not decree concerning the standard text because of the operative text, that is because writing a get while attached is uncommon, and the Sages therefore did not make such a decree.
