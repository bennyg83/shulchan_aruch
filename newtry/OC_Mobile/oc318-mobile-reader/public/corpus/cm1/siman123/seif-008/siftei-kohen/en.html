And it appears to me, etc. The reason is that perhaps the other party is asserting a false claim and will never swear, with the result that this person’s money would remain held by beit din indefinitely, forcing him against his will to compromise and surrender part of it. See what I wrote in siman 75, seif katan 83: one who is uncertain of the facts may authorize a relative, who can require the defendant to take an oath of heset; similarly, if he is presently in possession, he may authorize a relative, and the relative may swear so that he remains exempt.
