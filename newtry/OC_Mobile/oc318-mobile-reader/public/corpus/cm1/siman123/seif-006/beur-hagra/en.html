If he had, etc. As stated at the end of the chapter HaChovel: if one says, "Tear my garment" or "break my vessel," the other is liable unless he explicitly said, "on condition that you be exempt." All the more so here, where he empowered the representative over the debt for his own benefit and never told him to forgive it.
What it was worth, etc. As stated at the beginning of Makkot regarding witnesses who testify about a man who divorced, etc., and about a man who owes, etc., "until thirty days," etc. See also Bava Kamma 89a, "We do not say," etc.
