The defendant need answer only the second representative. The Tur uses the same language. The Beit Yosef writes that if the defendant litigated with the first representative and the claimant lost, the claimant may reopen and overturn that judgment. In the Perishah I cited the Baal HaTerumot, from whose language it appears that even if the defendant paid the money to the first representative and it was then lost in that representative's hands, the defendant must pay again to the second representative or to the principal.
