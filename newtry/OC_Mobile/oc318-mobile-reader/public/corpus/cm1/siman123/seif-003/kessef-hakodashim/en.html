Seif 13. (He may authorize his wife.) If the woman's husband is present in the city, the opposing litigant may perhaps object that he does not wish to engage in litigation with a woman where her husband could bring the claim himself before beit din. If, however, the husband is summoned and explains that there is a genuine reason why he needs to authorize her, beit din should weigh the claimed inconvenience, the dignity concerns, and the credibility of the explanation according to the circumstances.
