Terumat HaDeshen goes further and writes that even if the principal says, “Your compensation depends entirely upon me; I may pay you or not,” the agent is not considered interested and may testify, because he has no assured compensation for this agency. He concludes that the matter depends upon the judge's assessment. Beit Yosef rejects this, correctly in my view. Terumat HaDeshen relies on the rule that a witness is not disqualified merely because he may later become wealthy. There the witness presently has no possible gain at all. Here, however, he may profit immediately, and there is real concern that he will testify favorably because the principal is more likely to reward him after success.
