From the statement of the scholars of Nehardea that authorization is not written concerning movable property that the defendant has denied, implying that concerning land it is written even if the defendant denied the claim.
Tur. Baal HaTerumot writes likewise in Shaar 50, because denial of a debt secured by land is treated as denial of a land lien.
There and there. Rambam likewise writes in chapter 3, law 7, and Beit Yosef writes that it is straightforward.
The cited statement of the scholars of Nehardea.
Kesef Mishneh writes in that chapter, law 14, that according to Rambam this applies specifically where the denial preceded the authorization. But if the authorization was already written and only afterward it became known that the defendant had denied the claim, the authorization is not void, since it was validly written at the time. Although Raavad tentatively suggests a distinction, this is the correct explanation.
