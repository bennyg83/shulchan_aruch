(Seif 1) But the Geonim enacted. This is the Tur's wording, yet it is difficult because in seif 7 the Tur states that the same rule is the law of the Talmud. One must say that “the Geonim ruled” is Rambam's formulation, with which the Tur disagrees. The Tur holds that the Geonic enactment concerned an oral loan, while collection of a documented loan is already Talmudic law. Rambam, however, holds that even collection of a documented loan from such property rests on the Geonic ruling, and therefore an oral loan is not included even by enactment.
