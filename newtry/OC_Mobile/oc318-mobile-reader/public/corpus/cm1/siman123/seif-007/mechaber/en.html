If Reuven comes under a power of attorney from Shimon and sues Levi, and Levi says, 'This never occurred, but let Shimon swear and collect,' the money is removed from Levi and held by beit din until Shimon comes, swears, and collects. The same applies to every matter Levi makes dependent upon Shimon: Levi litigates it with Reuven, and the money is held by beit din until Shimon comes. Reuven may impose an unspecified ban upon anyone who makes a false claim in order to restrain and delay the money.
