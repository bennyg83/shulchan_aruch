The language of Rambam there at the end of chapter 3. Kesef Mishneh explains that when the authorization was written we did not yet know that the defendant had denied the claim; even if he denied it afterward, the authorization is not void, as explained at the end of seif 1.
The law is with the claimant, as we say in HaKotev, Ketubot 85a, that he must swear concerning two hundred, because perhaps shame will cause him to admit the truth.
The reason is that Levi may deliberately make this claim because he estimates that Shimon will not leave his place and come here to swear and litigate with him. The Sages therefore acted wisely and required the money to be removed into the possession of beit din. Shimon then has the option to come, swear, and take his money from beit din, and perhaps Levi will instead compromise with the agent so that Shimon will not need to come. Kesef Mishneh.
