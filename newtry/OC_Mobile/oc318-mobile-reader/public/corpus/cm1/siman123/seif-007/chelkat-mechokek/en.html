He wrote only the standard text and left the essential portions blank. The reason is that where the get is invalid because it was written while attached to the ground, the standard text remains valid, as will be explained in the following siman, seif 5. Likewise, regarding the invalidity of not having been written lishmah, Rambam holds that the standard text is valid, as will be explained below in siman 131. The same therefore applies where the standard text was written by a person who was not qualified to write it: it is valid.
