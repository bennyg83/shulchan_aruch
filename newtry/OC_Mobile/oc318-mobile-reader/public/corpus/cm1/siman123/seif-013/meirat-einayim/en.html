A slave has no independent acquisition. Therefore an authorization that does not transfer the subject of the claim to the representative and state, "Litigate and recover for yourself," is not a valid authorization, as explained in siman 122.
The defendant can say, "You are not my litigant." Meaning he cannot compel the defendant to pay him, because there remains a concern that the agency may have been revoked or the principal died, in which case the money could be taken from the agent by force and the defendant might have to pay again. Since this authorization lacks the legal status of a complete authorization, the concern remains. Nevertheless, it is no worse than an ordinary agent appointed before witnesses, as explained earlier.
The husband does not consume the fruits. If the husband were entitled to the fruits, perhaps the wife could not issue an authorization even though the principal ownership remains hers. This is unlike the rule that a husband can litigate regarding property held by another when he has rights to the fruits, even without authorization, because there his own present benefit gives him standing. Here, if the husband consumes the fruits, the wife's practical benefit from recovery is not apparent; she may die first and even the principal of the field will ultimately remain with the husband. The source of this law is explained in Even HaEzer siman 85.
