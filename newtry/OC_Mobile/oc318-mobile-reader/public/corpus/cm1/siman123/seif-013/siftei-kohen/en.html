But not his slave. Maharshal rules likewise. Although authorization may generally be given even concerning something that cannot be transferred, that is because the agent will acquire the money when it reaches him. A slave, however, acquires everything for his master, so even when the money reaches him it remains the master's. The formula “go litigate and recover for yourself” therefore cannot apply. Sema adds that if the defendant nevertheless pays the slave, the defendant is exempt, since the slave is no worse than an agent appointed before witnesses.
