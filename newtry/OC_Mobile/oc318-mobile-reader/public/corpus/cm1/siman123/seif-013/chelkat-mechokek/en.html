And one should not divorce at night. This does not refer to writing or signing the get, for it is certainly permitted to write and sign a get at night, as will be explained in siman 127. Rather, it refers to delivery of the get. Beit Yosef writes below in siman 136 that he heard the reason to be that delivery of a get resembles adjudication, and courts do not adjudicate at night. Beit Yosef rejects this reason, as I will cite shortly.
