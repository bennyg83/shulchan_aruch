To delay. Some say that the local beit din should send a written communication to the beit din where the principal is located, impose a ban there in his presence, and then the defendant here swears. This is the Tur's language, and Maharshal rules accordingly. The first opinion appears to apply even to a shevuat heset, contrary to Responsa Mabit, part I, second collection, siman 23, which limits the rule to a Torah oath. So writes the Shach. See there also what he writes in the name of Maharshal concerning a representative who owns a share in the underlying claim, and see the Taz.
