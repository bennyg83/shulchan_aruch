Authorization to another. See Baer Heitev in the name of the Shach. The Shach further cites Responsa Mabit concerning an authorization empowering two named persons to sell the principal's house, and providing that if one was prevented by an unavoidable circumstance from participating in the sale, the other could sell alone. No external accident occurred; rather, one simply said that he did not wish to involve himself in the sale. Mabit ruled that the other could sell alone. The condition requiring both to act, with an exception for unavoidable circumstances, was intended only to exclude a case where one sold alone without any impediment and without informing the other. It was not intended to cover a case where the other refuses to participate. The Shach writes that this conclusion is not compelled. Shaar Mishpat, however, maintains that the primary ruling follows Mabit. Once the principal revealed his intent that if one cannot act the other may sell alone, refusal by one to participate is itself no less an impediment. The principal did not expressly mention such refusal only because he did not anticipate it, but the condition demonstrates his intent. Shaar Mishpat brings a proof from the Tur and Shulchan Aruch at the end of siman 225. He adds that where an authorization simply gives permission to "Ploni and Ploni" to sell a house, the law depends on a dispute in Yoreh Deah, siman 216, seif 7. According to the Rama there, and the Mechaber in siman 228, seif 40, a father who makes his son swear not to lend money except with the permission of Reuven and Shimon may lend with the permission of either one, because the phrase "Ploni and Ploni" can mean either of them unless both are expressly required. If so, one of the two agents could sell alone. According to the Reem, however, whose view is cited by the Shach, common speech may indicate that both together are intended. The same rule applies to documents, as explained at the end of siman 61. See Taz to siman 228, Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 4, and Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, siman 46.
