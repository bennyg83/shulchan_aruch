(Mechaber, seif 13) But not his slave, because a slave cannot acquire, etc. Note: Tosafot Yom Tov to Shevuot, chapter 4, mishnah 12, proves that one cannot grant authorization to slaves from the mishnah's language, "he sent through his slave." This proof can be rejected. The mishnah may be teaching a novelty that even a slave is not considered the owner's equivalent for this purpose. A slave is superior to an ordinary agent in some areas, as in the law of borrowing with the owner present, where an agent is not treated as the owner himself but a slave is, as stated in Bava Metzia 96a. The mishnah may be discussing a case without formal authorization; with authorization, perhaps a slave is no different from anyone else.
