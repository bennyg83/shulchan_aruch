Seif 7. (They extract the money.) If a seller sends merchandise together with an agent who also carries the buyer's promissory note, the agent does not require a separate power of attorney when he is instructed not to release the merchandise until payment is deposited with him. The merchandise itself remains in his control as security for payment. If the buyer disputes the amount, and the note contains a credibility clause in favor of the seller, local practice may nevertheless require the seller to swear after the money has first been deposited, while the buyer accepts a formal ban before the agent departs. The buyer cannot refuse to deposit the money merely because the seller's oath will occur only after the agent travels away. The money must first be deposited so that the agent can then travel to obtain the seller's oath, or bring authenticated testimony that the oath was taken there. Even if the agent cannot travel immediately, and even if the goods have already reached the buyer or representatives from whom they cannot practically be recovered, the deposit should still be made immediately where the seller's oath exists only by custom rather than by strict law. A customary oath should not be expanded beyond its established innovation. The buyer's formal acceptance of the ban should likewise occur before the agent departs. As a matter of reason, where the oath is imposed only by custom, the seller should not be burdened with substantial travel expenses merely to take the oath in the buyer's presence. If travel is easy and inexpensive, it may be proper, subject to beit din's assessment, that the oath be taken before the debtor. Where the oath is required by strict law and there is no documentary credibility clause, however, the creditor may have to appear even at significant expense so that the oath is taken in the debtor's presence. The rule that an oath taken outside the debtor's presence can sometimes discharge the obligation may refer only to an oath of exemption administered under beit din's supervision and in the proper formula. An oath required in order to collect may be different, because without the oath the claimant has not yet established his right to take. Where the disputed funds are already deposited, however, the position may resemble one who swears merely to be discharged. This requires further study.
