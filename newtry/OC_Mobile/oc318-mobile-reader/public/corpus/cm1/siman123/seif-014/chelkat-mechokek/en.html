After the congregation has prayed Ma'ariv. In the get procedure of Mahari Mintz, siman 70, he writes: "I saw Maharam act stringently. Even after the congregation had prayed, although he himself had not yet prayed Ma'ariv in his synagogue and it was still broad daylight, he was stringent not to deliver a get." This is from Responsa Maharil. Beit Yosef, however, writes below in siman 136: "I do not know what connection adjudication has to a get, and none of the authorities made such a distinction." Beit Yosef also rules below in siman 154 that in pressing circumstances one may even write and deliver a get at actual nighttime.
