And they divide the remainder equally and take oaths, etc. This is the language of Maimonides in chapter 9 of Hilchot To'en. He holds that they swear only regarding the disputed remainder, while each party need not swear concerning the portion already in his own hand unless the opposing party chooses to extend the oath to it. The Maggid Mishneh writes similarly in the name of Rav Hai Gaon. The Raavad, Ramban, and Rashba disagree and hold that each party swears even concerning the portion already in his possession. The Tur also states the law without qualification according to their view. It is surprising that neither the Mechaber nor Rama mentions this dissent, although those who hold it are both numerous and later authorities.
