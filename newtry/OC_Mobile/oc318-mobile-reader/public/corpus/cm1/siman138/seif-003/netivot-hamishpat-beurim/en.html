And the remainder they divide equally. See Shach, seif katan 8, in his question on Torat Chaim. His words imply that he understood Torat Chaim's question to be that the Gemara should establish the case where each one is holding half. It can instead be explained that the question is why the case is not established where each party is holding an equal portion of the actual object, such as a handbreadth or more.
