Where nothing is in either person's hand... The Rosh derives this from the Gemara's statement concerning a kerchief.
The remainder they divide... and there is... Otherwise he swears only heses. The Gemara's statement concerning the kerchief indicates that what he actually grasps is treated as though it were in his possession. See also Bava Metzia 6a concerning two holding the object. Through gilgul shevuah, however, the oath is taken while holding a sacred object.
Collected note: The remainder... and there is... The Rosh writes that he swears regarding all of it, for otherwise the ruling would merely repeat the Mishnah. Tosafos rules the same way.
