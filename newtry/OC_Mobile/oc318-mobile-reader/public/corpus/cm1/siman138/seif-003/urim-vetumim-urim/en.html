Three, etc. Meaning, because a piece smaller than three by three is not significant, one would not ordinarily say that the person holding it should first take that piece before division. Certainly, however, if he wishes to take it first, he may do so even if it is smaller than three by three. This is implied by the Rosh. See Shach; this is correct.
And they divide the remainder. The Shach inclines to the majority view that even regarding the portion each one physically holds, an oath is required. He attempts to prove this from a difficulty in the Gemara. See Tumim, where I wrote that his proof is not decisive and that there is no difficulty. Initially I thought to prove the opposite, like the Rambam, from the Gemara in Bava Metzia, but I rejected that proof as well. Therefore, although there is no proof for the Shach, as practical law his conclusion appears correct. See Tumim.
