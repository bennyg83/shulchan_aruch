[Sema, subparagraph 17] For this would be a migo against a chazakah. Note: Nimukei Yosef writes that it is a migo against witnesses, because we are as certain as witnesses that he did not rent it. Alternatively, it concerns an unresolved question in monetary law from the first chapter of Bava Batra. See Chiddushei Anshei Shem. The practical difference is that if it is merely a migo against a chazakah, and he claimed that he rented it and seized from the possessor, he is believed, because seizure is effective in an unresolved monetary question. But if it is a migo against witnesses, seizure does not help. Maggid Mishneh also writes that it is a migo against witnesses.
