He swears. Tosafot at the beginning of Bava Metzia ask why the claimant is not believed through a migo that he could have claimed the whole item. They answer that a migo used to extract is ineffective. See above, end of siman 82. Although some disagree with that general rule, I wrote in Tumim that even they agree here for the reasons Tosafot give. In addition, I concluded that the supposed migo moves from one monetary claim to another and therefore is not used. I discussed the Shach's objection to that principle above in the general rules of migo.
