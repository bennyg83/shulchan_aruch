And that he has no less than three quarters in it. If he were to swear merely that he does not own less than half of what he says belongs to him, meaning no less than one quarter of the whole, we would have to fear deceit. He might intend his oath to refer only to the half that he already concedes to the other party. Therefore the Maggid Mishneh writes that this exact formulation is needed specifically for an item that cannot practically be divided into identifiable portions. Where the item itself is divisible, the rule is different, as Rama states in his gloss.
“And my half-hud is to say, ‘My wholeness is mine, and I will be willing to take away my words, and I will not be faithful to say that my half is in my body, and I will like them, and I will want them to do so, and I will be able to do so, and I will be able to do so, but I will share it, and I will bring them to the rest of it
