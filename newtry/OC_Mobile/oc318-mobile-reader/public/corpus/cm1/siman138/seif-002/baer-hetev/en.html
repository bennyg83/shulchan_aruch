Shall swear. Tosafot at the beginning of Bava Metzia ask why he is not believed through a migo that he could have claimed that the entire item was his. They answer that we do not employ a migo to extract property. See the end of siman 82, where the Tur states the same principle in another context. Some authorities disagree, and the Rama cites them there in seif 12. See there also for additional laws of migo. Sema. Responsa Maharam Mintz 74 writes that all these laws likewise apply where the item is held by a third party who says it was deposited with him on behalf of both litigants but does not know the proportion belonging to each. If the bailee is a non-Jew, he is not believed. Both parties remove the item from the non-Jew, and they then divide it after taking the required oath. See there. Knesset HaGedolah.
