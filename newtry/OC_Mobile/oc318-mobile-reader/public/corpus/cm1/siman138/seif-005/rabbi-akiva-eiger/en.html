[Shach, letter 8] And the Bach disagrees. Note: Urim VeTumim likewise rules this as the primary view. The same applies if the matter occurred before witnesses, according to the authorities who say that here too we assume that he admitted to him. Witnesses produced afterward are ineffective, because we certainly say that he admitted, and there is no alternative explanation to which the matter can be attributed. This is explicit in Ritva.
