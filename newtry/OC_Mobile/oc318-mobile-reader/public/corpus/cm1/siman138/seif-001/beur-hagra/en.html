Collected note: See below siman 271... Meaning the Tur there, who records the Rosh's view that riding acquires only when the rider moves the animal with his feet, in accordance with Shmuel. The Shulchan Aruch there, however, records only the Rif and Rambam's view that riding itself acquires, as stated here. See what is written there.
Or they are sitting beside it. Bava Metzia 2a and 10b concerning seeing a found object. Since the Sages instituted acquisition by four cubits, the same applies to one who says, "I found it," meaning that he saw it.
Or in a courtyard. This is learned from the rule of four cubits in an alley, since a courtyard and four cubits operate similarly.
However, if... Meaning where he did not rent it. See siman 260, seif 4. It is then like a garment protruding from beneath one person's hand, Bava Metzia 40a.
While holding a sacred object. This is the rule for an oath imposed by the Mishnah, as the Geonim write at the beginning of Kol HaNishba'in and as the Rosh records. See above siman 87.
If one said to the other... As stated above siman 87, seif 12, that a litigant can say he does not wish to use the rabbinic enactment and can shift a heses oath to his opponent. Here too he can shift the oath even regarding his own half, except that through gilgul shevuah the other swears regarding the entire matter while holding a sacred object.
If the second also... Meaning that the first shifted the entire heses oath to him and he then shifted it back, as stated there.
Two who were... Tosafos in Bava Metzia there, s.v. "and they divide," explains that where a roof lies between two houses and rests upon both, the same division rule applies.
