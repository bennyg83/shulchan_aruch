Or he overpowered him. If this claim were believed, he would likewise be believed by a migo to say, "I rented it."
Some say. They hold that this is a migo against witnesses, as Tosafos explains. The Gemara itself shows that he is otherwise believed, since it must add the reason, "Until now you suspected him..." According to the Rambam, Ritva explains that the case concerns articles ordinarily lent or rented, where the possessor could have claimed rental absent that special reason. A claim of forcible seizure, however, is not believed even regarding such articles, because we do not presume people to be thieves.
