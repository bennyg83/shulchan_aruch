In the Derishah I proved that even if the item will not be entirely ruined by division, but division will diminish its value from what it was before, such as a large tallit that after division can only be made into two small talleitim, it is nevertheless sold intact and the proceeds are divided. Sema. Knesset HaGedolah cites Terumat HaDeshen 336 that this applies specifically where the loss caused by division exceeds one fifth. Maharashach II, 122, challenges this. See there.
