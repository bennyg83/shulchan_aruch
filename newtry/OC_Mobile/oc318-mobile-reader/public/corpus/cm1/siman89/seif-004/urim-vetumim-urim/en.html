This oath for a hired worker was instituted by the Sages for the worker's livelihood, and also because an employer is occupied with his affairs and workers and can easily forget whether he paid. The Sages applied the rule uniformly even to an employer who is not especially preoccupied, and even where the employer denies everything. If the employer has one supporting witness, see Tumim, seif katan 16, where it appears that the worker does not swear and collect. The matter requires study.
