And if he did not, etc. The academies sent this question, and the response was that in this case he swears. The meaning is an oath taken while holding a sacred object, because the hesset oath had not yet been instituted, as Tosafot explain and as stated above in siman 87. The Gemara resolves the baraita by explaining that its clauses address different cases.

If the claimant failed to bring proof and his claim simply lapsed, one would never have thought that a partial admission could exempt the defendant from an oath.
