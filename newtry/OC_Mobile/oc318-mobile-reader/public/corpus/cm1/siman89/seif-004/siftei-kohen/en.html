Even though he already, etc. The same applies where the employer says, “Here is what I admit.” Rambam, Rosh, Ri Migash, Ran, Rashba, and Nimukei Yosef require the employer to take the Mishnah-level oath even where he is not a partial admitter. In my opinion, however, the Gemara is better understood like Baal HaMaor, Ramban, and the interpretation attributed to Rif: where the employer does not partially admit, he need take only hesset. The Gemara's initial difficulty, why a partial admitter should perhaps be exempt, is resolved because one might have thought that even wage rates are forgotten through the employer's preoccupation, so no oath should be imposed upon him, while the worker would not swear and collect because the dispute over the rate does not ordinarily implicate his basic livelihood. Ramban's later explanation of the passage is therefore the principal one.
