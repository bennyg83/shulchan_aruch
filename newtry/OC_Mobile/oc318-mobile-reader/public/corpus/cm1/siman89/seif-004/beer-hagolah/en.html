The Maggid Mishneh writes: the Gemara's wording is that the employer must swear. Rashi explains that this means an oath patterned after a Torah oath, because shevuat heset had not yet been instituted; had it already existed, the Gemara would not have needed to state that he swears. The Beit Yosef writes similarly in the name of the Ran in the name of Rabbi Yosef HaLevi.
There. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash likewise ruled so, because the law regarding the fixed wage should not be more stringent than the law of "I paid and he did not receive," where in these circumstances the employer takes a shevuat heset and is exempt.
