The employer swears. The Shach rules that he needs only a hesset oath.
He swears while holding a sacred object. If the dispute concerns payment, or where he is an artisan and the garment is in his possession, the case remains within the time. A worker within the time who has seized and possesses a migo can, according to the Shach, be believed without an oath where he could have claimed purchase. Likewise, among all those who ordinarily swear and collect, if they have seized property and possess a valid migo, they may be believed without an oath. See Beurim, seif katan 14: if witnesses establish that the item was given to him as an artisan, the artisan is believed while he holds the garment even without a migo. If the employer's own claim is uncertain, the artisan is likewise believed even without a migo.
