He swears while holding a sacred object and collects. See Shach, seif katan 10, in the passage beginning "his words do not appear correct to me," where he writes that what was said, namely that a worker is different, is surprising to him. See there. In Rabbi Akiva Eiger's glosses to the Shulchan Aruch he writes: "With due respect, that baraita follows Rabbi Yehudah, who holds that even regarding the amount of wages we say that a person does not remember precisely." See Responsa Noda B'Yehudah, second edition, siman 19, and also what Netivot HaMishpat writes on this matter.
