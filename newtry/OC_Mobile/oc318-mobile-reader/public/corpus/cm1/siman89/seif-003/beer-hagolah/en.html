Statement of Rav Nachman in the name of Shmuel there, 45b.
According to Rashi's explanation there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that if the worker has no witnesses that he actually performed the work, witnesses to the hiring itself accomplish nothing, because perhaps he was hired but never performed any labor.
So writes the Maggid Mishneh there in the Rambam's name. He explains that the worker swears by rabbinic enactment with an oath patterned after a Torah oath. The Beit Yosef cites this.
Mishnah, Bava Metzia 111a.
