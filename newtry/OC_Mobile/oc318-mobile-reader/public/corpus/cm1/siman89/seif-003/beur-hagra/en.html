And some say that even... Since the worker's credibility rests only on a migo that he could have said, "You never hired me." Had he said that, he would have been required to take a Torah oath. Therefore, even now he takes a Torah oath, as explained in Bava Basra 70a.
And how long is his period... We follow Rav there, because the halachah follows Rav in matters of prohibition. Even according to those who generally rule like Shmuel in monetary matters, here the rationale is the presumption that an employer does not transgress the prohibition of withholding wages. The additional presumption that a worker does not delay collecting his wages is not tied to the specific time period; during the statutory period, the decisive factor is the employer's presumption.
(Collected notes) A night worker... We rule like Rav because the central dispute concerns the prohibition, even though it also has monetary consequences. The reason the worker swears and collects during the proper time is the presumption that the employer does not violate the prohibition of withholding wages. (End of collected notes.)
