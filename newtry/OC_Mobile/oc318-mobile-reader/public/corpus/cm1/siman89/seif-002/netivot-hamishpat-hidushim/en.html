His wage was one perutah. See Beurim: the Shach concludes that there must ordinarily be a denial worth two silver maah. It is enough, however, that the total denial, including disagreement over the wage rate and disagreement over payment, reaches two maah, even though the worker is not believed merely on the wage dispute and the payment denial itself is only one perutah. But if the entire denial is only one perutah, he does not swear. The primary law is not like the Shach. If the worker's entire wage is only one perutah, he does swear, so that the worker should not leave empty handed. But if the wage is greater and the denial concerns only one perutah, he does not swear.
