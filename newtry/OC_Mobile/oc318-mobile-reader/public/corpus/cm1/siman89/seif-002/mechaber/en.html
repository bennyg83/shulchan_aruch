What is the law of a hired worker? If a worker performs labor for an employer and demands his wages, while the employer says, 'I paid all or part of your wages,' then even if the wage was only one perutah and the employer says he paid it, the worker takes an oath resembling a Torah oath while holding a sacred object and collects. Even if the worker demands payment from a minor, he swears and collects. {Rama: If the worker was a minor, a slave, or one suspected of swearing falsely, the employer swears and is exempt. A worker swears and collects from the employer's heirs if he demanded payment within the prescribed time, but the worker's heirs do not swear and collect.}
