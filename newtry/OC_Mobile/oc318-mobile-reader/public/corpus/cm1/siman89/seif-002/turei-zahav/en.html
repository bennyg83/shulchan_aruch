(Seif 3) “And some say, nevertheless.” The reason is that, since he hired him without witnesses, the employer would ordinarily be believed by virtue of a migo that he could have said, “I never hired you.” Therefore, where there is one witness, so that had he claimed “I never hired you” he would have been required to swear against the witness, when he instead says, “I paid your wages,” he must swear under the enactment of the Sages with an oath modeled after a Torah oath, as though he were swearing to contradict the witness. This is stated by Beit Yosef in the name of Maggid Mishneh.
