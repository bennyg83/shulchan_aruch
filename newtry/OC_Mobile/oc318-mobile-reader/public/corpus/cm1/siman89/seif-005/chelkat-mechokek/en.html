And they did not agree with him. This is Raavad's language in his gloss: "I see weak reasoning here. Because she did not take an oath, does he call the husband's heirs the heirs of her ketubah? They themselves say that she had already collected her ketubah and taken collateral, and that she bequeathed it to her own heirs. Rather, certainly, the heirs of her dowry and nichsei melog bury her even though they inherited nothing." It is possible to explain his words as follows. Even if she did not seize collateral and her heirs inherited nothing from the ketubah, she requires no oath regarding her dowry and nichsei melog. Her heirs at least inherit those assets. Why then should they not bury her? According to this, if she was poor and possessed neither dowry nor nichsei melog, they would not be obligated to bury her and she would be buried from charity. But Responsa Maharil, siman 64, does not sound so, as I wrote in the previous seif.
