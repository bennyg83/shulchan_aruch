The language of Rambam, Hilkhot Chovel U'Mazik chapter 8, law 6. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the source is the Yerushalmi in the final chapter of HaGozel and the final chapter of Ketubot, following Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi even though Rav disputes him. Beit Yosef explains that the Gemara concludes there that Rabbi Yochanan agrees with Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, and the halachah does not follow Rav against both of them.
In seif 22 concerning the law where a gentile coerced him, etc.
This too is there, from Rava's statement in HaGozel, Bava Kamma 113b, as Rashi explains there, based on the rule dina d'malkhuta dina.
Kesef Mishneh writes that Rambam apparently alludes to the Gemara's statement there: this applies to land tax, which Rashi explains as a tax on land from which one eats produce, and to head tax, which Rashi explains as a poll tax, for that current year. But for a past year, once the king accepted settlement, it has passed, as Rashi explains there. Rama's gloss in the name of some authorities requires study.
The Maggid Mishneh writes there that the Yerushalmi's 'arnon' is the arnona mentioned in the Gemara. Rashi explains it as provisions, hospitality, and tribute.
