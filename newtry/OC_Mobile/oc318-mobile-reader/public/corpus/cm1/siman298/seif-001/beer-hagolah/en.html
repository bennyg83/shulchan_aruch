Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilkhot She'eilah U'Pikadon, halachah 6. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam and his teachers base this upon the principle that one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear must pay without requiring an oath by the opposing party, as explained in siman 75.
Bet) This is explained in the Gemara in HaKones, Bava Kamma 62a, from Rav Ashi's statement to Rav Yeimar.
