[Shach, letter 7, s.v. And furthermore] As below, siman 356. Note: In my humble opinion, the cases are not comparable. There, the owners seek to take the object from the purchaser, and the Sages enacted that they may not take it except upon payment. Here, however, the purchaser himself seeks to extract property on the strength of a mere shard of pottery, meaning an instrument with no substantive evidentiary force.
