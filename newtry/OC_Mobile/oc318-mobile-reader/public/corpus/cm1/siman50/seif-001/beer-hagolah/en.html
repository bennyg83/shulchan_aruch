a) Incident in Rav Huna's beit din, as Rava said: the phrase "from you" means from whichever person the document is produced against. Bava Batra 172b. The Bach writes that the same applies if the holder claims, "I purchased it expressly from the person from whom you borrowed"; the person producing the document may collect even without authorization.
b) Tur, and so writes Baal HaTerumot, gate 13, in the name of the Ramban.
c) There and there. The Bach writes that he cannot claim, "There never was such a debt," as the Ramban writes regarding notes in siman 69.
d) This refers to the opening case, where no lender is named in the document. The Nimukei Yosef writes: nevertheless, once another person produced this document, collected payment, and issued a receipt for it, its lien is extinguished with respect to everyone.
e) There. The Mechaber himself writes this, and writes there that it applies provided the person was already born at the time the document was written.
