Tur writes in the name of Rosh, who writes in his rulings that the owner can say, 'I prefer an open grain field,' and that the Ramah likewise writes. Furthermore, the owner of the trees must hire workers to uproot them from the other's land. Beit Yosef writes that since Rambam's words in chapter 10 of Hilkhot Gezeilah, laws 8 and 9, plainly imply this and there is no proof otherwise in the Halakhot, we rule accordingly. In my humble opinion there is also proof from the incident before Rav there.
