He said to him, etc. Ramban, Rosh, Nimukei Yosef, and the other authorities derive this from the Gemara's sequence. When the owner said, 'I do not want it,' Rav was silent until the end, indicating that the improver may remove his plantings. Ramban, Rashba, Nimukei Yosef, and most authorities limit this to land not ordinarily planted. Rosh disagrees and infers from the repeated instruction to appraise that initially the improver had the upper hand. Nimukei Yosef answers that Rav did not know whether the field was ordinarily planted, and therefore initially gave an unqualified instruction. When the owner objected, Rav considered that perhaps it was not ordinarily planted and allowed removal.
