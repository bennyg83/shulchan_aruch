And he said to him, “And he shall be blessed with thee, and he shall be blessed with thee, and he shall be given to him, and he shall not be given to him.” His hand on the top of an epic field in a field that is not made of a mockery and the author of just the things here, and I wrote in the words of the column proven by the island of Daraa:
If the expense exceeded the improvement, he receives the expense, and if the improvement exceeded the expense, he receives the improvement, etc. This is the correct reading, excluding texts that reverse the two clauses. The meaning is that he receives from the appreciation like the ordinary planters of the city, as explained above under "his hand is on the upper side." This is also the text in Maimonides, chapter 10 of Hilchot Gezeilah, in accurate editions and an old manuscript, and so it was corrected in newer Krakow printings of Shulchan Aruch. One may ask from Rama at the end of siman 178 concerning a partner, who is treated like one who descended with permission, yet if expense exceeds improvement he receives only expenses up to the amount of the improvement. I explained there, following Nimukei Yosef in the names of the Rambam and Raah, that a partner resembles one who entered with permission only where the improvement exceeds the expense, in which case he may receive the customary share of a sharecropper or planter. But where expense exceeds improvement, a partner does not have the full status of one who entered with permission and receives only up to the value of the improvement. That is why Rama says "like one who entered with permission," not "he is fully one who entered with permission." Here, by contrast, the Tur and Mechaber speak of one who truly entered with permission, who therefore receives all his expense even where it exceeds the improvement. See there.
