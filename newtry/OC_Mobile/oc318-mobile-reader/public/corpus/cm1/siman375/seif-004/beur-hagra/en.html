One who descends into another's property, etc. See Be'er HaGolah and siman 178, seif 3.
(Supplement) One who descends, etc., for if, etc. See there, seif 6, concerning one who enters another's property. The Tosefta, Bava Kamma chapter 10, states: one who enters another's ruin and builds it without permission is appraised, but has the lower hand; if he entered with permission, he is appraised and has the upper hand. How is his upper hand determined? If the improvement exceeds the expenditure, he receives the improvement; if the expenditure exceeds the improvement, he receives the expenditure.
