The expense. Regarding what the Rama wrote at the end of siman 178, in the gloss concerning a partner, that he is treated like one who entered with permission, yet if his expenditure exceeded the improvement he receives only the amount of the expenditure up to the value of the improvement: I already wrote there, in the name of the Nimukei Yosef, that a partner is treated as one who entered with permission only where the improvement exceeds the expenditure, in which case he receives a share of the improvement like a tenant farmer or planter. He is not regarded as one who entered with permission in every respect where the expenditure exceeds the improvement. See there. Sema.
