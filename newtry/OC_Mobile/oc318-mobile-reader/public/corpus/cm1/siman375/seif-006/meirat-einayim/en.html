He hears that he is a dovron by a land that does not belong to a Daraa like a doll of evasion before it in the U.S.:
This applies specifically if the ruin, etc., but if it is intended for building, etc. The Tur, citing the Rosh, writes: It seems reasonable that we do not listen to the owner who wishes to remove the construction where he was not using the ruin and has sufficient means of his own to build a structure like this without impairing his livelihood. In that case he is clearly seeking a pretext merely to cause a loss to the builder. Otherwise, however, we do listen to him.
