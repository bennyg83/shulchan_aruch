yud) The conclusion of the Gemara there on 101b. The Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of the Rashba that if the owner of the ruin initially said, 'I will pay their value,' showing that he is pleased with what was done, his courtyard acquires the improvements for him, as explained in the Rashba's Chiddushim. The Beit Yosef likewise writes in the name of the Nimukei Yosef citing the Rashba, and that this also appears to be the Raavad's view.
