One who enters another's field or ruin and plants or builds there. It contains nine seifim.
If one enters another's field without permission and plants it, then if the field is ordinarily planted, the owner pays the amount a person would willingly pay to have such a field planted. If it is not ordinarily planted, the improver receives the lesser measure. Rama: An ordinary vineyard is presumed intended for planting. For one who benefits another without permission, see siman 264; for one who teaches another's child without the father's knowledge, see siman 335, seif 1.
