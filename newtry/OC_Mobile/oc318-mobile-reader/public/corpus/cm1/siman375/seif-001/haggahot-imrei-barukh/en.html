(Rama, seif 9) And they damaged one another in the course of rejoicing. Note: See Orach Chaim 695, Magen Avraham seif katan 6. From Mahari's rulings it appears there is no distinction between damage to property and bodily injury. So is it explained in his name in Responsa Bach, siman 62. Bach distinguishes between minor bodily or monetary damage and major bodily or monetary damage.
