We assess how much a person would wish to pay for this field in order to plant it. Meaning according to the customary compensation of other planters in that city. Rashi and the Rosh say this, and the Beit Yosef cites them. He also writes that Rashi's students disagree, because this person entered without permission and also stands to be removed. See the Perishah and Derishah.
