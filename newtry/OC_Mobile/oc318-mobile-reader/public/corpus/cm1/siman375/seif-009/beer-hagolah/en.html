The Maggid Mishneh writes that this appears straightforward: anyone obligated to pay in such a case and who knows how much he owes is believed to say he paid; if he does not know, he is not believed. This is similar to the Mishnah in Bava Batra chapter 1, where one is presumed not to have paid until he brings proof. The Gemara gives the reason, 5b: who says the Rabbis will obligate me? Rambam derives this law from there according to his view.
