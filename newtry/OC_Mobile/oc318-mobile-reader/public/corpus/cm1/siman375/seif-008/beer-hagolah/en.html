There, law 16. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is learned from the Mishnah in Ketubot chapter 8, 79b: if a husband spent money but consumed no produce, he swears how much he spent and collects, even though a husband is treated as one who enters with permission.
Meaning where it is known that the expenses exceeded the improvement. Since his law is to collect the expenses after swearing while holding a sacred object, he can instead say: give me the smaller amount, namely the appraised improvement, without an oath. Sema explains likewise.
