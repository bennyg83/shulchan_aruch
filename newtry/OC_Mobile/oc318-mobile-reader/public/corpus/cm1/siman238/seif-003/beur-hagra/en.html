There is an opinion that says, etc. Perhaps the prospective buyer does not actually wish to buy, and this document might obligate him to do so. This is the meaning of the continuation, “and if not, etc.”
And there is, etc., if it indicates, etc. Meaning that the document itself states the matter in a way that evidences intent. A person does not ordinarily make himself appear wealthier or obligated without reason, as stated in Sanhedrin chapter 3, and certainly not regarding property that is not his.
There is an opinion, etc. As the continuation explains, “for, etc.” Ramban responds to Ri Migash’s concern that perhaps the buyer will be forced to pay based on the document, since we rule that one may write a deed for a seller without the buyer present. The buyer can answer that the document was written without his knowledge. See above siman 39 seif 17.
