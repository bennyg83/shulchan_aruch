Provided that he immediately transferred title, etc. This is unlike the Rambam in chapter 24 of the Laws of Creditor and Debtor, who distinguishes between a promissory note and a deed of sale. Rather, the law is as the Tur writes, that even regarding a deed of sale one may write it only where it is a deed that itself effects acquisition, according to Rav Asi. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes there in the name of Rashi, Ramban, Rashba, and other commentators that there is no clear reason to distinguish between sale and loan in this law. The Bedek HaBayit cites this. See there.
