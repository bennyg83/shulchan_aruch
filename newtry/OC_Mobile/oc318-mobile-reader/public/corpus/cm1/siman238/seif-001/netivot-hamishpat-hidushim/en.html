With a kinyan. Otherwise there is concern that perhaps the seller instructed them in Nisan to write the deed for delivery to the purchaser, but did not actually deliver it until Tishrei, and in the interim sold the property to another. The first purchaser could then produce a deed dated Nisan and wrongfully remove the property from the later purchaser. Therefore a kinyan is required, because then he would lawfully have priority. This is the Rif's view. Rabbeinu Yonah rules that witnesses who effect acquisition on behalf of the recipient may write the deed even without an additional kinyan. See Beurim: this applies specifically where he told the witnesses, "Acquire on his behalf." See also above, siman 235, seif 23.
