And some say... As explained above.
Unless he made it an apotiki... Meaning, as stated in seif 6, where an apotiki lets the creditor take improvement beyond expenditure. Therefore with spontaneous appreciation he may take all of it. Gift recipients are treated similarly, as above. With purchasers too, if the debt equals only the land, spontaneous appreciation may still be fully collectible under that approach. Rambam goes further and allows full collection of spontaneous appreciation even without an apotiki. This is what the Maggid Mishneh explains when he says the Geonim disagree with Rambam's extension except where the property was specifically made an apotiki.
