Apotiki. This implies that even if the creditor is owed an amount equal to both the land and its improvement, and the land was designated as an apotiki, he must reimburse the orphans for their expenditures. So rule all the authorities, and it is compelled by the Gemara, contrary to the Beit Yosef. Shach.
With money. The Shach writes that according to what he explained in seif 1, where the primary view follows the Raavad and his supporters that a creditor may say, "To me this land is worth the full amount of my debt," the orphans cannot remove him from the land merely by paying its market value. They must pay his entire debt without deducting anything. Perhaps the Mechaber means that they may remove him when they pay the entire debt. One who stated that his daughter should receive half the share of a son does not thereby give her a share in improvements produced by the heirs. Maharam of Padua, siman 51. Knesset HaGedolah.
