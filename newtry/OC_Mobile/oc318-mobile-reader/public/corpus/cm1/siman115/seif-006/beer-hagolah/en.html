Seif 14. This requires study, because there it explicitly says that he gives them the expenses, and Sema writes likewise. Sema also cites those who reconcile the matter by saying that a printing error occurred and that this gloss belongs to Rama's statement at the end of seif 2 concerning collection from a purchaser, teaching that this applies specifically when the debt did not exist, etc., as Tur writes there in seif 6. Although Sema rejects this because Rama presents it as 'some say' even though no one disputes the rule there, in my humble opinion this is nevertheless a reasonable explanation, since the law itself is true and is mentioned neither by the Mechaber in Beit Yosef nor by Rama in his gloss there. This also appears from Rama's wording, for he writes 'and he does not give him,' rather than 'and he does not give them.'
The Maggid Mishneh writes that it was already explained above that with appreciation resulting from expenditures, the creditor takes nothing at all from the orphans unless the property was designated as an apotiki.
