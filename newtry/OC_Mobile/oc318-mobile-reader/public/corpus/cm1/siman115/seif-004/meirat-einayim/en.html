He does not collect from heirs just as he does not from a gift recipient. Rambam and the Mechaber likewise equate heirs with recipients of gifts according to their own view that a creditor may collect passive improvement from a gift recipient. Rama writes this according to the Rosh, who holds in seif 3 that the creditor does not collect even passive improvement from a gift recipient. The novelty here is that the same is true of heirs, even though they are regarded as continuing their father's legal position. Conversely, in seif 3, where the field was made an apotiki and the creditor does collect from a gift recipient, the ruling says the same as with heirs, for with heirs collection in an apotiki case is even more straightforward.
