He does not collect from heirs. See Shach, who introduces a new rule that an after-acquired property clause does not become encumbered for a ketubah. Tumim has already rejected this from Bava Batra 157, and the contrary is also demonstrated in several other places. Therefore this ruling certainly should not be accepted. In the Shach there is also a textual error in seif katan 32. It should read: "Is he worse than one who descended without permission? Rather, it must be because he will go back and collect his own from the seller..." See also Shach seif katan 33, where the text should read: "the creditor is considered the muchzak, and the purchaser and the heirs must bring proof." The word "from the heirs" should read "and the heirs."
