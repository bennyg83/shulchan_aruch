And some disagree. See Shach, seif katan 32, who concludes from his discussion that after acquired property does not become subject to a ketubah. The Tumim and Netivot HaMishpat challenge him. Yeshuot Yaakov, Even HaEzer, siman 100, seif katan 3, likewise disagrees. Responsa Brit Avraham, siman 50, ruled in practice that collection for a ketubah may be made from after acquired property, contrary to the Shach. See there, and Pitchei Teshuva to Even HaEzer, siman 100, seif katan 4.
