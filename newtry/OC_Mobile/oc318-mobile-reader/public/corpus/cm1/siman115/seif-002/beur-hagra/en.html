Even all the improvement, etc. This means two things: first, that he collects the improvement even though the debt does not equal the value of the land and the improvement; second, that he does not divide the improvement with the purchaser. The gloss printed at the end of the siman, 'Some say that this applies only, etc.,' belongs here. It should read: 'This applies only where he did not, etc.' The meaning is that designating the field as an apotiki benefits the creditor by relieving him of the need to divide improvement with the purchaser, while the fact that the debt equals land and improvement benefits him by relieving him of the need to reimburse the expenditure. This differs from Rambam's understanding of the Geonic position, under which the creditor must reimburse the purchaser for the expenditure attributable to the purchaser's half. See Raavad's glosses.
