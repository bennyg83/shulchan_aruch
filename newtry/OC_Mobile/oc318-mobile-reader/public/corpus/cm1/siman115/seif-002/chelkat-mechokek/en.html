Or one of all the prohibitions. Maggid Mishneh writes that tithing nowadays is only rabbinic. Even if it were Torah law, there would all the more be grounds to apply the rule to other prohibitions. With untithed food, the husband could himself separate the tithe and need not rely upon her. All the more so if she feeds him prohibited food and he does not know, so that he sins unknowingly. It is not clarified whether the same applies if she causes him to violate other prohibitions indirectly, for example, he instructs her to establish an eruv, relies upon her and carries on Shabbat, and afterward discovers that she did not establish it; or he instructed her to destroy the chametz and she failed to do so, leaving chametz in his house. Does she likewise forfeit the ketubah in such cases?
