Seif 5. (The recipient is poor.) Although the word "give" can sometimes operate like "acquire for him," if a person sends charity to a visiting poor person and the visitor leaves town before the agent reaches him, it appears that no automatic acquisition arose. The sender's ordinary intent was presumably that the agent should give the charity if he found the visitor at his lodging in that place, not that the agent must pursue him outside the city. Nevertheless, the matter requires further study.
