To take it back. See the Shach, who writes that this implies that even if the agent accepts full responsibility, the sender may say, "Perhaps it was not actually lost by accident," and reclaim it. Dagul MeRevavah explains that even though the agent would be obligated to pay, the sender may still suspect that the agent has set his eyes on the object and falsely claims a loss.
