The rule regarding a deposit. A loan is given to be spent, and the original loan to Reuven was made only to satisfy his request. It is therefore a benefit to Shimon for the money to leave Reuven's control and come intact into Shimon's possession. A deposit is different. It remains intact even in Reuven's hands, and Shimon entrusted it to Reuven for his own convenience. So long as Reuven has not later become an established denier, Shimon gains no special benefit merely from its return, and the depositary may therefore retract.
