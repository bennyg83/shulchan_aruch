The rule that, with a deposit, the sender cannot retract and take it back from Levi applies specifically when the custodian has been established as a denier, because it benefits the depositor to remove the deposit from his possession. If the custodian has not been established as a denier, he may retract and take it back. If the owner of the deposit appointed a person as his agent to bring it to him, then because he revealed that he wants the custodian to give it to that person, the custodian cannot retract. This applies even when the owner merely made an agent available, which does not exempt the custodian from responsibility, or did not make him available at all but the agent is trusted by the depositor and the depositor regularly entrusts property to him. In each case, the custodian cannot retract.
