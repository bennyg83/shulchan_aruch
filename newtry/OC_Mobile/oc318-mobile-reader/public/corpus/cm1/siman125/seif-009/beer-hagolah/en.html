There, in the name of Ramah: it is unlike an ordinary gift of a deathly ill person, which is not transferred by delivery and does not leave the giver's possession, and therefore is acquired only after death. Here, however, the giver physically removed the money from his possession, clearly showing that he intends the recipient to acquire it immediately.
