If the giver was on his deathbed, then if the intended recipient had already died when the giver delivered the one hundred to the agent, it is returned to the giver's heirs. But if the recipient was alive when the giver delivered it to the agent, it is given to the recipient's heirs, even if the recipient died during the giver's lifetime and even if the recipient's heirs were born only after the giver's death. The words of a person on his deathbed are treated as written and delivered, and the giver cannot retract as long as he does not recover. If he recovers, he may retract even if the money has already reached the recipient.
