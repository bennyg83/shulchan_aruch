He said, “We were able to return to him all the time he was alive, but here he died, when he was taken out of his hand, we were able to return to him all the time he was alive, but here he died, and he was given to him, and he was given to him.”
He shall return it to the sender’s heirs. “Take it” in the context of a gift is not equivalent to “Acquire it.” Although the donor later died, the principle that it is a mitzvah to fulfill the words of the deceased does not apply, because the recipient died during the donor’s lifetime and the donor’s instruction was thereby nullified while he was still alive. See Responsa Maharshadam, siman 384.
