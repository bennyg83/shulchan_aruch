He shall give it to the recipient's heirs, etc. See what I wrote in the preceding seif katan. The case must be one where the giver physically handed the property to the agent. Only then is "bring" treated as "acquire" for a person on his deathbed. But if he did not hand it to him, even though it was in the recipient's domain, "bring" is not equivalent to "acquire," even for a person on his deathbed.
