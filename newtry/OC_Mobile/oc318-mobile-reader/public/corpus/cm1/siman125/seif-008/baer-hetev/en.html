To his heirs. Although regarding a gift the word "bring" is not equivalent to immediate acquisition according to all views, that means only that the giver may retract while alive. Here the giver died. Once he removed the money from his own possession, the mitzvah to fulfill the words of the deceased applies, as stated in siman 250, seif 23, and siman 252, seif 2. The recipient therefore acquired it during his lifetime and transmitted it to his heirs. Shach.
The sender. With a gift, "bring" is not equivalent to immediate acquisition. Even though the giver later died, the mitzvah to fulfill the deceased's words does not apply because the recipient had already died during the giver's lifetime, so the giver's instruction became void while he was alive. See Responsa Maharashdam 384. Shach.
