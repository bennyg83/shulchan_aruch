If the sender died and left no property, the dispute remains between the agent and the lender to whom the money was sent. Even if the agent received it from the sender before witnesses, because he could say to the lender, 'I paid you,' and be believed upon taking a heses oath, he is likewise believed upon taking a heses oath to say, 'I am retaining it for a debt you owe me.' The sender and his heirs no longer claim the money from him. Some say that if the agent seeks to retain it for a debt the lender or depositor owes him, he cannot do so. See note 40.
