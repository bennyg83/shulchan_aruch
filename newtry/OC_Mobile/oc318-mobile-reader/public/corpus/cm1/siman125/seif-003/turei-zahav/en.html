In my view the Tur imposes liability on the agent specifically where the borrower became poor. The agent is then considered negligent, because while the money remained with him it was intact, whereas upon returning it to the borrower the borrower would spend it, since loan money is given for expenditure. The same applies to a depositary who has denied the deposit, as explained above, because he will certainly consume it. For other losses, such as accidental loss, the agent is exempt. This appears from Raavad's language, which calls him specifically negligent. Rambam, however, holds that in every case both the sender and the agent bear responsibility.
