Knesset HaGedolah, seif 22, cites Mabit, part 1, siman 195, that the same rule applies when the recipient is a Torah scholar and the sender transfers the money for the mitzvah of supporting Torah, as Scripture calls Torah "a tree of life to those who support it"; the sender cannot retract. Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 159, objects that Mabit did not actually rule so broadly. His ruling concerned a poor Torah scholar, and he mentioned support of Torah only as an additional consideration. If the scholar is not poor, but has ample livelihood for his household, the donor may retract. Shevut Yaakov brings proofs for this. I already mentioned the point in Pitchei Teshuva to Yoreh Deah, siman 258, seif katan 13.
