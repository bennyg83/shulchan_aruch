And some say that 'give' is, etc. Rabbenu Tam reads the Gemara according to its plain sense, that 'give' is equivalent to 'acquire.' He therefore limits the contrary mishna to property already deposited with the messenger, or to a transfer in the presence of all three parties. This view disputes the Jerusalem Talmud's general equation of 'give' with 'send.'
And even if, etc. The Tosefta's case, 'Give this maneh to so-and-so,' implies that the money is already in the messenger's possession. Nevertheless, with a gift the sender may retract unless he said, 'Acquire it for him.'
Or even, etc. Bava Metzia 10a expressly states that saying, 'Acquire it for me,' effects acquisition.
But if, etc. Bava Batra teaches that property merely deposited in another's domain is not acquired without an act or declaration of acquisition.
