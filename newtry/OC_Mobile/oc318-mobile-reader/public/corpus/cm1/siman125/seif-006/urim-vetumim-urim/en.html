The Shach holds that even with a debt or deposit, where the principal says, 'Acquire it for him,' the property must already have entered the agent's possession before the principal retracts. He cites Ramban, Tosafot, and Rosh. See Tumim, seif katan 9. I agreed with him as practical law based on the underlying reasoning, although his proofs from Ramban, Tosafot, and Rosh are not decisive. See there at length.
