As a gift. Sema explains that a deposit or debt already belongs to the person to whom it is being sent, and therefore another can readily acquire it on his behalf. A gift, by contrast, is property that never previously belonged to the recipient, and therefore a stronger and clearer expression is required. See Responsa Mahari ben Lev III, siman 11.
Bring. By saying bring, the donor cancels the agency that the recipient had given the messenger to acquire and receive it from the donor. Sema.
