The rule that saying "take this" is equivalent to acquisition on the recipient's behalf applies to a loan or a deposit, but with a gift it is not equivalent to acquisition. Even if the recipient appointed him as an agent to receive it, and the giver said, "Take it to him," the recipient has not yet acquired it. Therefore, if one says, "Take one hundred zuz to so-and-so as a gift from me," he may retract until it reaches the recipient. If the recipient is poor and does not possess two hundred zuz, however, the commitment becomes a vow and the giver may not retract.
