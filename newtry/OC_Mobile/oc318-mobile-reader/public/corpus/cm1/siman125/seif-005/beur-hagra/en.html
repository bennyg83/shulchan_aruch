When does this apply, etc. Gittin 14b and 32b.
Even if he appointed him, etc. There, 63a: the Sages taught, "Receive," etc. Rabbi Natan says, "Take and give," etc. We rule like Rabbi Natan there, as the Rif writes: "And the halachah follows Rabbi Natan," etc., for Abaye said, "We have a received tradition: an agent for a gift," etc. This also answers Tosafot's discussion on 32b, s.v. KeHolach, etc. But in such a case the expression "acquire for her" is effective regarding a get, as stated there: "Acquire it for her and receive it for her; if he wished," etc.
Therefore. Rashi there, and this is likewise implied there on 14a.
And if he was, etc. The Yerushalmi is cited by the Rif and Rosh in the chapter HaZahav, Bava Metzia 49. See also below, siman 243, seif 2.
