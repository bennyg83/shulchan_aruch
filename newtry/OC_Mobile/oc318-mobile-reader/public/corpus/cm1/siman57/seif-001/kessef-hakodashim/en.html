A paid promissory note. 'Do not let injustice dwell in your tent.' If the receipt is placed inside the paid document, perhaps the concern is less severe, all the more so if the receipt is sewn or attached to it. Perhaps the same applies when the receipt and document are kept in one container in a way that they are not ordinarily separated. If the lender is uncertain whether the document was paid, it appears he should mark that uncertainty on the document. In light of the foregoing, placing a written note of the doubt inside it should suffice.
