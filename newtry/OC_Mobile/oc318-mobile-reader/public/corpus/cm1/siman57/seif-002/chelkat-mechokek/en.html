For the sake of marriage. This entire seif is the language of Rambam, Hilchot Ishut chapter 22. Maggid Mishneh writes that the same applies if she entered his courtyard, even without an express statement. Rambam did not mention that case separately because it is already included in his explanation that once she has entered her husband's domain he inherits her. Accordingly, there is no dispute between Rambam and the later authorities cited by the Rama in the following gloss.
