[" He thereby admits, etc. The creditor cannot collect with that document even from the borrower himself, as I proved above in siman 48, contrary to Sema there. Why is the creditor not believed to say, ‘I returned the money to you,’ by a migo that he could have said, ‘You never paid me’? Because this would be a migo used to extract money. According to the poskim who allow a migo to extract, one must explain that here the document is not authenticated. The rule then teaches that although it might later be authenticated, once it is presently unauthenticated and the creditor admits it was paid, it is like a shard and he is no longer believed even after later authentication. I explain this at the end of siman 83 regarding a document alleged to be forged or an amanah. The Gemara in Chezkat HaBatim compares the cases. See there further, where I conclude that in all circumstances this remains a doubt in law, and if the creditor seized, even in the presence of witnesses, we do not remove the property from him. The same applies here:"]
