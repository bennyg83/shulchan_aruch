There and there in Baal HaTerumot, from the Mishnah, "He said to two people, the father of one of you deposited a maneh with me," etc., Bava Metzia 37a. This differs from the baraita's conclusion that all agree where one purchased from five people and does not know from which one, he may place the purchase money among them and withdraw, Yevamot 118b. The poskim likewise explain that an ordinary purchase immediately has claimants, so the buyer had no reason to be especially precise. A deposit, by contrast, may have been entrusted to him for a long period, and likewise an ordinary loan lasts thirty days; therefore he should have been precise. Nimukei Yosef there in HaMafkid.
