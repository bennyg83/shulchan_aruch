To fulfill his obligation toward Heaven. See Baer Heitev, who refers to siman 75 seif katan 19. There the Shach writes that whenever a defendant is obligated only in order to fulfill his obligation toward Heaven, seizure by the claimant in the presence of witnesses does not help, while seizure without witnesses may help. That applies where the claimant himself makes a definite claim. Here, however, where the claimant does not make a definite claim, seizure is ineffective even when made without witnesses. The wording of Siftei Da’at here, seif katan 12, is abbreviated. Mesgeret and Maamar Kiddushin copied the Shach here incorrectly and overlooked the Shach’s explicit statement in siman 75 that in this case seizure is ineffective even without witnesses.
