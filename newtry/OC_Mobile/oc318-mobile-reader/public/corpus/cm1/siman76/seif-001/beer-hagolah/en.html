Tur. So writes Baal HaTerumot, gate 39, from the law of two who deposited money with one person, Mishnah Bava Metzia 37a, as the Gemara explains there.
Because they informed him from the outset, he is considered negligent for failing to distinguish between their deposits, and he caused his own loss. It is therefore like one who says, "I borrowed two hundred from one of you and I do not know which one," where each claimant demands two hundred and he must give two hundred to each.
Tur in the name of his father the Rosh in the chapter HaMafkid, in his rulings there.
