Even from his duty toward Heaven. When two people knowingly lent him money, the borrower is not negligent merely because he remembers that he borrowed but did not make sure to remember every detail of which lender gave which amount. Where, however, he does not know at all from whom he borrowed, his failure to keep track is negligence, and that is why the stricter rule applies there. See Tumim, seif katan 4.
By the law of Heaven. If he says, "The father of one of you lent me a maneh, but I do not know whose father," all agree that he must pay in order to fulfill his duty toward Heaven. Shach, and so it appears from the Gemara.
