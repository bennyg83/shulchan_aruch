He is exempt even from an obligation before Heaven. The reason is that since the lender does not demand payment, this shows that the lender himself did not pay careful attention, and the borrower likewise does not pay close attention when he sees that the lender himself does not. This differs from the case where both lent to him and neither demands payment, concerning which the Mechaber wrote above that one who wishes to satisfy his obligation before Heaven must pay. There each lender can say, "We heard from the borrower that he knew how much each of us lent, and therefore we assumed that he would return to each one what he had lent." But where only one of them lent and the truth is that the borrower does not know which one, they cannot say that they heard him state that he knew. See the Perishah.
