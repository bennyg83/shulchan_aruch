Even from fulfilling his obligation before Heaven. The Sema and Shach explain why this does not contradict the earlier seif, where one who remembers borrowing from each of several people but not how much can have a duty before Heaven even without a claim. The distinction follows siman 75. One who says, "I do not know whether I ever became obligated," and is not being claimed against, is exempt even before Heaven. One who remembers that he certainly borrowed but is uncertain how much has a stronger duty to resolve the matter. Accordingly, in the earlier case he remembers that he borrowed from each person and only the amounts are uncertain. That creates a duty before Heaven toward each. Here he does not remember any loan from this particular person at all. As to each claimant his position is, "I do not know whether you ever lent me anything." If that person is not actively claiming from him, he is exempt even before Heaven.
