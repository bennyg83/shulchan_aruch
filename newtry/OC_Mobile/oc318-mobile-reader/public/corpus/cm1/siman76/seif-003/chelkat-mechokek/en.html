The conjugal schedule of Torah scholars is once each week. The Ran, Ketubot 495b, asks why the conjugal schedule of Torah scholars is not mentioned in the mishnah together with the other occupations, and is asked only in the Gemara. He answers that Torah scholars do not have an absolutely fixed schedule. Their obligation changes according to the degree to which they must deprive themselves of sleep and exert themselves in study. The Gemara's statement that their schedule is once a week refers to a time when nothing new has arisen in their learning that requires additional exertion. Because it is uncommon for no new matter to arise in their studies, their schedule was not listed as a fixed category in the mishnah.
