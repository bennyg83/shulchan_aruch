loyalty. Demallow is faithful in his body, and it is not his duty to carry out his hand. A:
Admits. He is not believed through a migo because that would be a migo used to extract money. Tosafot and the Rosh write this. According to the Ramban, Rashba, and other later authorities whom I cited at the end of siman 83, however, the lender is believed here. According to what I wrote there, that the matter is an unresolved question of law and that if the lender seized property it is not removed from him, the same applies here. See there. Shach.
