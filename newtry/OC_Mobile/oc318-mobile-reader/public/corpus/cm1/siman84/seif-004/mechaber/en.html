This law was stated above in siman 47, seif 2. One who reduces the amount stated in his document collects without an oath. How so? His document is for one thousand zuz and the borrower claims, "I paid it all," while the lender says, "I received nothing, but you owe me only five hundred. The amount of one thousand written in the document was merely a trust arrangement between us." Even if the document contains no trustworthiness clause, he collects without an oath unless the borrower says, "Swear to me." If the lender says, "I received nothing, but the debt was only five hundred and the witnesses erred and wrote one thousand," he thereby admits that the witnesses gave false testimony. The borrower takes a heset oath and is exempt.
