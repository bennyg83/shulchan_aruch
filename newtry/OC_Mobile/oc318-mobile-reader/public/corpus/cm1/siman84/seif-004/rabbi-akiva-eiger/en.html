[Shulchan Aruch] When does this apply? When the due date has passed. Note: The same applies on the very day that the due date arrives, as the Perishah writes. See Bach. The Perishah further writes that once the due date has passed, even if the borrower claims that he paid the entire debt within the fixed time, he must nevertheless take an oath, by virtue of a migo that he could have said, “I paid him after the due date.” See there. However, Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer, siman 96, seif katan 8, infers from Responsa Rif cited in Baal HaTerumot, Shaar 16, part 2, that we do not employ a migo in order to impose an oath. Gedulei Terumah writes likewise there.
