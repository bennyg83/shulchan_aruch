In the Tur, seif 3: Rav Hai wrote that even within the due date he may collect only with an oath It is difficult: the Gemara asks what difference there is between this case and one who impairs his note, as the Beit Yosef cites in siman 82. Why not answer that an impaired note requires an oath even within the due date? The answer is that the law of an impaired note is derived from the Mishna in HaKotev, which lists those who swear despite holding a note. Had the only distinction concerned whether the debt was within or after its term, the Mishna should also have listed an unimpaired note, which at least after maturity requires an oath, just as it lists many cases applicable after maturity. According to the Gemara’s answer, however, the matter is clear: the Mishna lists only oaths imposed by the court on its own initiative, whereas an unimpaired note requires an oath only upon the debtor’s demand.
