Ramah holds that just as a woman may say, 'I will not be supported and I will not work,' she may also say, 'I will not be supported and I will not give you my found property.' The Rosh disagrees; see the Tur and Bach. I am not worthy to decide between such great authorities. It appears, however, that when she says she wishes to receive support, Ramah's position is correct, whereas when he says to her, 'Support yourself from your earnings,' the Rosh's position is correct. It is possible that they do not disagree. Examine this carefully.
