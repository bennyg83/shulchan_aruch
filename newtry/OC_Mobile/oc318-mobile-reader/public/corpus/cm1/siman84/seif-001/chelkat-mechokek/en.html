A wife's found property belongs to her husband. In Ketubot 64, the first Tanna and Rabbi Akiva disagree, and Rabbi Akiva holds that her found property belongs to her. The Gemara further states that Rabbi Akiva follows his view that surplus earnings produced through exertion also belong to her. Any found object that she lifts while engaged in work is comparable to surplus earnings produced through exertion. Above, at the beginning of siman 80, we already ruled that if she exerted herself and produced more than the amount required of her, the surplus belongs to the husband. Accordingly, all of her found property likewise belongs to the husband. But according to Rabbeinu Chananel and Rav Hai, who hold that surplus earnings sometimes belong to her, found property too may sometimes belong to her. So rules the Bach.
