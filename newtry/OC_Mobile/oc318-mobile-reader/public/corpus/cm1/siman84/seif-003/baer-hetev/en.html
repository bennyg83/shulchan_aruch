The Shach writes that this is a novel ruling found in no authority other than the Tur quoting a responsum of the Rosh. Had the authorities accepted such a distinction, they would not have remained silent. It also appears that even the Rosh, Tur, and Mechaber speak only where the borrower admits that the notation of partial payment was written between the lines with his knowledge. But if the borrower claims that the lender himself wrote the partial payment between the lines, this is properly considered an impaired note. The Shach brings proof for this distinction. See Responsa Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, siman 123.
