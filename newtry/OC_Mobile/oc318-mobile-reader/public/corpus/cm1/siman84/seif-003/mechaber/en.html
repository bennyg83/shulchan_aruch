A payment notation written between the lines does not make the lender one who impaired his document and thereby require him to swear. See above, siman 44, seif 6, and the end of siman 45. If a document contains a payment notation between the lines stating four hundred gold coins, written over an erasure, and the borrower claims that he paid more and that the lender erased the place of the original notation and wrote whatever he wished, then if the payment notation is in the borrower's handwriting, the law follows the lender.
