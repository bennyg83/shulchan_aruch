It is not considered an impairment of his document so as to require an oath. Meaning, unless the borrower himself demands an oath, as with an ordinary unimpaired document.
The reason is that where the payment is written between the lines, it is as though the document had originally been written only for the remaining balance.The law is with the lender. Meaning, the case is treated as though the payment had been written between the lines without an erasure, so there is no reason to suspect that the lender forged the notation. This is especially so where the notation is in the borrower's own handwriting.
