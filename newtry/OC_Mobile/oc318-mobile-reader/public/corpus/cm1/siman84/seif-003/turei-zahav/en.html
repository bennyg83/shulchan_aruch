(In the name of Maharshal, in the Beit Yosef, s.v. “Baal HaTerumot wrote in gate 21 that his proof,” through seif 5, “he administers a heset oath”) This is no proof at all. There, had the witness testified that the possessor purchased the land from him and consumed its produce for three years, no heset oath would be required. The witness there testifies only that he consumed the produce for three years, which is not direct support for the asserted purchase. This is simple.
