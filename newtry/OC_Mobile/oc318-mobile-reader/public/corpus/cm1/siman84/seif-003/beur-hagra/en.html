It is written in it... As stated there in the Gemara: if it were true that he had repaid him, etc. Alternatively, perhaps it happened, etc. Meaning, with the first payment it may have happened by chance, but it would not repeatedly happen with the second. That differs from a case of this kind, where he can arrange matters in that way at any time. See the end of the first chapter of Bava Metzia 22a: "not only... but even..." meaning that there he cannot say, "it happened by chance," etc.
