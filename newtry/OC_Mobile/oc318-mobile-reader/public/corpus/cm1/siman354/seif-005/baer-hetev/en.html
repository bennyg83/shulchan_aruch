The broken pieces. Although regarding damages we say this, as the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 387, there the reason is the verse, 'the carcass shall be his,' and we rule according to the opinion that it belongs to the injured party. Regarding a thief, however, Scripture says, 'he shall pay double, alive.' See also the end of siman 362. Sema.
