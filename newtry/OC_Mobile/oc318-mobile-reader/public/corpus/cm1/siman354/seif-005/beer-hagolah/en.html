The wording of the Tur, seif 5, and Rambam there, law 15, from the Gemara's conclusion in chapter 1, 12a: the halachah is that we do not appraise for a thief or robber, etc.
There, and similarly chapter 2 of Hilkhot Gezeilah, law 15. Rambam explains that this is an enactment for the benefit of the owners; if they do not wish it, they may refuse. The Maggid Mishneh understands that the broken pieces are taken from the robber even against his will. It requires study why the robber does not acquire them through physical change; perhaps their original name still applies.
