Although in ordinary damages the tortfeasor may sometimes give the carcass or broken pieces to the injured party and pay the difference, theft differs. With damage Scripture says, "the carcass shall be his," which is interpreted as belonging to the injured party. In theft Scripture requires restoration of the stolen object as it was when stolen. The same rule will be explained regarding robbery in siman 362 from the verse, "he shall return the robbery that he robbed."
