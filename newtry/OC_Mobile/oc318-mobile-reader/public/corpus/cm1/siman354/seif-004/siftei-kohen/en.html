It is treated as though it appreciated in value. See the Sema, seif katan 9, who disputes Ir Shushan on the basis of the Rosh. Maharshal, however, rules against the Rosh and holds that even fourfold and fivefold payment is always calculated according to the value at the time of trial. The Rambam and Tur calculate double, fourfold, and fivefold payment according to the time of theft, but Maharshal follows Rabbeinu Yitzchak in Tosafot that double payment is calculated at the time of trial even where the object broke by itself.
