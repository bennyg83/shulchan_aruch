When stolen property appreciates in the thief's possession. It contains six seifim.
If stolen property appreciated on its own, such as a ewe pregnant when stolen that gave birth, or bearing wool when stolen that was shorn, the thief returns the animal together with the wool and offspring. If the birth or shearing occurred after the owner's despair, he pays according to the value at the time of theft. If he invested in and improved it, such as fattening it, the appreciation belongs to the thief even before despair, and when returning the stolen property he receives the value of the improvement from the owner. Rama: Some say even spontaneous appreciation belongs to the thief before despair, especially where the ewe became pregnant only in his possession, because he stole it empty.
