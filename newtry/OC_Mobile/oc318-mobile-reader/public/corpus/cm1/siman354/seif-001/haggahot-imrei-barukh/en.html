Sema, seif katan 7: where it is known that it was lost without negligence. Note: See the later authorities and Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Kamma 45, s.v. 'these are the words of Raavad,' and s.v. 'Riva explained,' where Riva's words appear to imply that even a robber is liable because he should have saved the property. In the Rosh, chapter Merubah, regarding 'it broke by itself,' the opposite of the Sema's words is explicit. See Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 4, siman 30, and Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Metzia 43, s.v. 'that which was said, it broke,' etc.
