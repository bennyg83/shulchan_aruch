A deposit made before witnesses where the bailee claims theft or denies the transaction. It contains eight seifim.
Whether one deposits, lends, or rents property before witnesses or without them, the law is the same. Once the recipient admits that the property came into his hands as a deposit, loan, or rental, he takes the bailee's oath. A potential alternative claim does not exempt him from the oath, though it can exempt him from payment. Some say a credible alternative claim can exempt from an oath, but this particular claim is not sufficiently strong.
