(To exempt him from an oath.) According to the Sema's reason, this is difficult for the authority who holds in siman 82 that a migo may be used to extract property. It appears to me that the reason is as follows. Although the migo provides a strong basis to accept his claim as true, whenever the matter can be clarified we require clarification. Since it is possible that through the oath he will withdraw his claim, we impose the oath. A parallel is the rule that we follow the majority in all places, yet where the matter can be clarified we do not rely upon the majority, as Mordechai writes in the name of Rabbi Yosef HaLevi.
