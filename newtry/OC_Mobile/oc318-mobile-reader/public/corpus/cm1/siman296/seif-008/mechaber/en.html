If the bailee admits negligence but claims they stipulated that he need not guard according to ordinary bailee standards, and the depositor denies any condition, the bailee is believed even when witnesses saw the deposit but do not know the terms. Since he could have claimed that he guarded properly and an unavoidable accident occurred, he may claim the condition. He must swear that he did not use the deposit, that it is not in his possession, and that the condition existed. If the depositor brings witnesses to the negligence, the bailee is liable and cannot claim the condition because he lacks that alternative claim. Rama: This applies when witnesses know he accepted custody. Otherwise, even if negligent, he is believed through the claim that he never became a bailee and merely said, 'The house is before you.'
