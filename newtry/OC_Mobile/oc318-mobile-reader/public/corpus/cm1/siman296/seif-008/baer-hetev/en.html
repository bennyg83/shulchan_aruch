He shall swear. See the Shach, who discusses at length how to reconcile the Maggid Mishneh, unlike the Bach.
Witnesses. Meaning, even if the witnesses before whom the deposit was made saw that he came to deposit it but do not know whether there was a condition, the custodian is nevertheless not believed by oath through a migo that he could have claimed an unavoidable loss. The witnesses testify that he was negligent and that there was no unavoidable event, so he is not believed to say there was a condition between them exempting him from guarding it. Sema.
