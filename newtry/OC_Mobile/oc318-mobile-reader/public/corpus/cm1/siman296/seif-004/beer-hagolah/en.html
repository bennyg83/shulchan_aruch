The Maggid Mishneh writes that this too is explained there in a baraita. Ramban, Rashba, and some commentators hold that this applies only when the produce he admits to holding is no longer intact, having been lost or destroyed through negligence. If it remains intact as it was, then even if the guardian does not explicitly say 'here it is,' every deposit remains in its owner's domain and it is as though he said 'here it is.' Rambam and Raavad disagree. Tosafot, Rosh, and Ran also follow the former view, as stated in siman 88, seif 24. It requires study that the Mechaber rules here simply like Rambam while above he ruled like Ramban, Rashba, and the other commentators. It is difficult to say that he relied upon what he wrote there.
