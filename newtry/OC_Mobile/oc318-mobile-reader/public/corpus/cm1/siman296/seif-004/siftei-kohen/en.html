The custodian takes a heset oath. The Sema explains that because the custodian says, 'This is all you deposited, and you may take what you gave,' his admission is treated as immediate tender. In my opinion, the exemption is not based on immediate tender, but because he did not admit to a quantified amount, as explained above, siman 88, seif 23. The Beit Yosef states this expressly. Accordingly, the case may involve produce that decayed through his negligence, where immediate tender would not apply. See the Mechaber above, siman 88, seif 24, and Responsa Rashdam, siman 48.
