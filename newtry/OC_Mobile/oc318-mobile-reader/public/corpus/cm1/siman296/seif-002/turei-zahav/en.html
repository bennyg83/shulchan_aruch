The Tur's statement that this concerns a matter in which it is impossible that others would not know comes to exclude something that does not ordinarily attract observation, in accordance with the Mechaber's ruling in siman 294, seif 3. It was unnecessary to repeat that qualification here. The Sema's words that one ordinarily must bring proof are astonishing, for it appears that these are one and the same standard. Whenever the event is readily observable, it is considered impossible that many people would not know of it.
