Chapter 2 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 9, from the Mishnah at the end of Bava Metzia chapter 7, beginning of 94a.
"Bry" there
Rambam rules like Rabbi Yochanan there, because precisely in that case a reputation goes forth that he is trustworthy, and on that basis he obligates himself. The Halakhot likewise rule so.
