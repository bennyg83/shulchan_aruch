The Gemara's explanation there.
The language of the Tur. Beit Yosef does not state the source of this rule. In my humble opinion Tur learned it from Tosafot there, s.v. "if so," who write that the reason the debtor is exempt when the money is nearer to him is that he explicitly instructed the creditor to throw it to a place where the creditor could guard it. This implies that if he did not specify that condition, even if he threw it far from the creditor, the debtor is exempt. I found Bach likewise, and he further writes that in his view this is Rambam's opinion at the beginning of chapter 16 of Hilkhot Malveh, because Rambam states without qualification that he is exempt, implying even where he threw it far from the creditor, unlike Maggid Mishneh's citation of Rashba there.
The Mishnah there on 78a and the Gemara's explanation on 78b. Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of Rashba that the debtor said precisely this wording and no more.
The language of Rambam there. Maggid Mishneh writes that the law regarding a get is explained in chapter 5 of Hilkhot Gerushin, laws 13-14, and is cited by the Mechaber in Even HaEzer siman 139, seif 13. See what was written there. Maggid Mishneh explains Rambam as ruling in accordance with Shmuel's instruction to Rav Yehudah, "sharp one," etc.
This follows the Tur, who rules like Rav there regarding within four cubits and, outside four cubits, like Rabbi Yochanan. He holds that Rav and Rabbi Yochanan disagree only outside four cubits, and he rules like Rabbi Yochanan, as Beit Yosef and Bach write in the name of the Rosh. In my humble opinion the Tur necessarily speaks only of a side street or the sides of the public domain, as written in Even HaEzer siman 30, seif 4. The Mechaber's ruling from Rambam concerns the public domain or a courtyard belonging to neither of them, as Rambam writes in chapter 5 and the Mechaber in Even HaEzer siman 139, seif 13. Rama's citation of the Tur as though it explains Rambam's view requires study in my humble opinion; Sema did not comment on this.
Tosafot write likewise there.
