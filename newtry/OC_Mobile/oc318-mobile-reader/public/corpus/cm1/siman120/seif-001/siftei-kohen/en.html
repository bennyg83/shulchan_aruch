The money remains at his risk, etc. See Responsa of Rabbi Shlomo Kohen, book 2, beginning of siman 11 and siman 101.
And it was lost before it reached his hand. The Rambam rules likewise. The reason is that he follows the approach of Tosafot in Gittin, in the passage beginning, “If so, what is there to say,” see there, and not the approach of the Rashba and Maggid Mishneh. This is what the Sema intended in seif katan 3. The Bach also writes this, see there, and it is correct.
