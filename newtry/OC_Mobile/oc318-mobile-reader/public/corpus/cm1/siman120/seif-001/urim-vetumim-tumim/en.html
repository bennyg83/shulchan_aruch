His cubits, etc. The Sema wrote in seif katan 4 that the Sages instituted for every person four cubits in an alleyway or at the sides of the public domain, like his courtyard and his hand. From his words we learn that the four cubits mentioned here mean in an alleyway, in a place where they effect acquisition, as discussed below in siman 268; see there. Yet the plain wording of the Shulchan Aruch does not indicate this, for it should then have explained so. In truth, according to what is written in Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer siman 139, where the primary ruling follows the Rashba that regarding gittin, because of the concern of agunah, the Sages instituted four cubits even in the public domain, and see Even HaEzer siman 30 and the Beit Shmuel there in these simanim, this is what is meant by his statement, "Throw me my debt according to the law of gittin." He accepted upon himself all the laws of gittin, and whatever is effective regarding a get is likewise effective regarding this exemption. In monetary matters a condition is binding, especially with respect to waiver. Therefore the Shulchan Aruch states the ruling without qualification, that within any four cubits he is exempt. This is precise.
