If the lender can guard it. Regarding gittin, the Tur and Mechaber write in Even HaEzer siman 139 that we do not rely upon her ability to guard the get when it lies outside her four cubits. In monetary law, however, this ability is sufficient, as in the present ruling, and even with respect to kiddushin we rely upon it and regard her as betrothed, as stated in Even HaEzer siman 30. Although kiddushin too is a matter of personal status and may later produce a leniency if another man betroths her, with respect to the present kiddushin the ruling is a stringency.
