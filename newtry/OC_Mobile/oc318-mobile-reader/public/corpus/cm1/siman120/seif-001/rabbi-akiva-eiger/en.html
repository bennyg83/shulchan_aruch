[Shulchan Aruch] But if he said to him, “Throw my debt to me.” Note: This is effective even without a kinyan, because forgiveness does not require a kinyan. So write the Rosh and Tosafot in the chapter HaMekabel. Mishneh LaMelech writes that according to this, in the opinion of Rabbi Yeshayah cited by the Tur above, siman 12, and the Sema there, seif katan 21, where the loan is evidenced by a document it is ineffective merely for the creditor to say, “Throw my debt to me and be exempt.” See there, and see Maharit, part 2, Choshen Mishpat, siman 118.
