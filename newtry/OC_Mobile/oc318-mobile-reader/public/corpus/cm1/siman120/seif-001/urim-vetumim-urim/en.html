Even if he said, etc. His instruction means, 'Throw it and guard it,' and this is unlike where he says, 'Send it through a deaf mute or a gentile,' for there he certainly did not instruct him to accompany the messenger. See Sema.
For he authorized him to do so. Unless he told him to throw it into a protected place. Ordinarily, however, the statement 'throw it' applies in every manner. See Tosafot, Bach, and Derishah.
According to the law of gittin. Since he used this wording, even if he did not add the words 'and you shall be discharged,' the law is as stated here. See Sema, who writes this contrary to the Beit Yosef. I did not find any contradiction to this in the Beit Yosef.
Into four amot. See Tumim, where I concluded that this applies even to four amot in a public domain.
Both can guard it. Either each one can guard it independently, or the two together can guard it. See Sema.
As though he threw it into a place of loss. See Tumim. This must refer to a deposit. Regarding a loan, in any event, since she is not divorced, his debt is not discharged either.
