In his possession. At first glance, the words of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch imply that even according to R. Yehudah Barceloni, cited in seif 18, here money is not considered properly guarded until it is actually placed in the bailee's possession. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 47, who writes that even here, if he placed it in the place where he keeps his own money, he is exempt. This also appears somewhat from the words of the Sema. See Responsa Mahara Sasson, siman 150, and Responsa Mabit, part II, siman 342. Shach.
