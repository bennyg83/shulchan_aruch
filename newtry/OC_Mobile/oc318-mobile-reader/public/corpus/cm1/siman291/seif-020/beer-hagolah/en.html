The wording of Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilkhot She'elah U'Pikadon, law 6, from the Mishnah in Bava Metzia 42a. The Gemara derives the reason from the verse 'and bind up the money,' etc. The Hagahot Maimoniyot explains that Rambam describes the ordinary manner of conduct, and the verse too speaks in the ordinary manner.
