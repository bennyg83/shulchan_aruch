And they are resting in his hand or tied, etc. The wording of the Tur and Mechaber initially implies that even according to Rabbi Yehuda of Barcelona, cited in seif 18, money is not considered guarded here unless it is actually in his hand or tied to him. Responsa Rabbi Shlomo Kohen, siman 47, however, writes that even here, if he placed it where he ordinarily keeps his own money, he is exempt. This is also somewhat implied by the Sema, seif katan 30. See Responsa Maharan Sasson there and Responsa Mabit, part II, siman 342.
