[Shach, subparagraph 40] And this is not compelling. Note: This means that one can say the homeowner was not negligent at all in this circumstance. Even though he sensed that the agent took from the deposit, it is not conclusive that he thereby revealed consent. Such consent can be inferred only together with the fact that he did not explicitly tell the agent not to take from it, as the Sema writes in subparagraph 43. Shitah Mekubetzes likewise cites Ramban this way. Therefore, with a deposit from a relative, which is as though he explicitly said not to take from it, the homeowner never imagined that the agent would violate his instruction, and therefore the agent is liable. See Toras Chaim, which writes the opposite.
