On your way to his home, he is. Hashem’s promise is not to save money, but rather to the ground where we are not, such as entrusted with the hands of a business that should be taken from them that I will take them to the ground as before:
And they are placed in his hand. The Gemara derives this from the verse, "You shall bind up the money in your hand." Even though the coins are tied, they should remain in your hand. The same rationale applies if they are kept directly before his eyes.
