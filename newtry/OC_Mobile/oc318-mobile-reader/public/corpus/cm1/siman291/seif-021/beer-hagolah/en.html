The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam holds this person is not included in the rule that every depositor deposits with the understanding that the guardian may entrust the item to his household. The reason is that a person does not ordinarily entrust his property to another's slave.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is as explained in the following incident regarding the guardian's mother. Rambam holds that she was supported at her son's table or lived with him in the same house. Otherwise the depositor is not presumed to have deposited with her in mind. This is simple.
