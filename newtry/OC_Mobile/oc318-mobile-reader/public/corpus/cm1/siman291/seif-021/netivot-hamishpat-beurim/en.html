With the knowledge of his wife. See Sema, seif katan 31, concerning money lost while being transported. Sema follows the position Shach records in seif katan 26, that if the wife placed the money where she ordinarily keeps her own money she is exempt. Shach likewise understands Sema this way. Sema's cross reference to siman 292, seif 10, concerns a case where some money was lost and it is unknown whose money it was. The matter is explained there in Rama and in Shach, seif katan 28. At the end of Shach's discussion there is a textual error. The correct reference is siman 72, seif katan 136. As I explained there, with a deposit everyone agrees the custodian can be exempt when he transfers it to the depositor's wife. She is no worse than a person to whom the depositor regularly entrusts property.
