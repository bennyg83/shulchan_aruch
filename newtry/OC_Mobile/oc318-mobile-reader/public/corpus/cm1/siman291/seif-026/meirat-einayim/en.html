To deposit such an item with him regularly. The word "regularly" cannot mean to exclude a person with whom he deposited only once, even if most of the time he deposits with this person. That would be unreasonable. Rather, it excludes a case where most of the time he deposits with other people. Proof appears below in siman 65, where the Mechaber states this law regarding a paid custodian but does not use the word "regularly." Certainly that is so here regarding an unpaid custodian.
Regularly, an item of this kind. "This kind" is not literal. It means an item of comparable importance and value. It excludes a person with whom he was accustomed to deposit only things worth less than this. So writes the Beit Yosef. See also the Derishah.
