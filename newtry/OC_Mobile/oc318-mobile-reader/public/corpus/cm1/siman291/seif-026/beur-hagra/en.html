(Collected note) Provided he does not diminish, etc. From Rava's statement, "it was unnecessary, etc.," it appears that in such a case we do not need the reason "you trusted me, etc." Although Abaye also says this there, it may be that Rabbi Yochanan indeed does not accept the rationale that once it was entrusted to a competent person the first bailee is exempt; perhaps only Rav holds that view.
But if, etc. For its beginning was through negligence. Tosafos do not agree; see there 93b, s.v. "if so, etc."
And if there is, etc. See Rif and Rosh there, and Tosafos there, s.v. "Rav, etc."
And even, etc. Tosafos to Bava Metzia there, s.v. "at, etc.," because he himself will swear.
(Collected note) Even if he did not, etc. The Gemara there 36b: "he said to them, according to you, etc." See Rashi there, s.v. "according to you, etc.," and Tosafos there, s.v. "at, etc."
