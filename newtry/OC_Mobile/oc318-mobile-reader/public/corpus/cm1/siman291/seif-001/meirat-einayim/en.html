He is exempt by oath from theft and loss. In the Torah section concerning custodians in Mishpatim, the first passage concerns an unpaid custodian. It says, "and it was stolen from the man's house," meaning that the custodian makes that claim, and afterward, "the householder shall approach the judges." The Sages interpret this as requiring an oath on his claim.
