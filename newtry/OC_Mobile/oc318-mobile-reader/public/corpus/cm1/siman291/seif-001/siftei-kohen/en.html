And he is not required to pay, etc. At the beginning of Bava Kamma the Gemara explicitly implies that all four custodians must pay from superior-quality land, like tortfeasors. The Rif, Rosh, and Nimukei Yosef rule accordingly there, as does Maharshal, siman 6. See there. This is likewise the law below in simanim 385 and 388, seif 2, and see what I write below, siman 420, seif 3. The gloss states: In an investment arrangement consisting half of a loan and half of a deposit, where collection must be made from the recipient's property, one collects half from superior-quality land and half from intermediate-quality land. Shiltei HaGibborim writes this in the name of Ri'az in the first chapter of Bava Kamma.
