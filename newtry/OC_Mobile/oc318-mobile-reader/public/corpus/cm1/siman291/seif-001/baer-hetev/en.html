Unpaid. See Responsa Mabit, part II, siman 241; Responsa Mahara Sasson, simanim 77, 78, and 79; and Maharit, siman 105. The beginning of Bava Kamma clearly implies that the four types of bailees who are liable to pay must pay from superior land, like one who causes damage. The Rif, Rosh, Nimukei Yosef, and Maharshal, in the first chapter of Bava Kamma, siman 6, all rule accordingly. The same law applies below in simanim 385 and 388, seif 2; see also what the Shach writes in siman 420, seif 3. In an iska arrangement, half loan and half deposit, where collection becomes necessary, half is collected from superior land and half from intermediate land. So writes the Shiltei Giborim in the name of Riaz in the first chapter of Bava Kamma. Shach.
