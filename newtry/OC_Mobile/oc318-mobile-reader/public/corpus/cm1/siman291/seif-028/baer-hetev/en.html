As it is stated. This verse refers to "if a man borrows," implying that the owner must be with him at the time of the borrowing, even if he is not with him afterward. The verse states this with respect to a borrower, and all the more so with respect to the other custodians. Sema. Above, at the beginning of siman 346, the Sema himself challenges the Ir Shushan for writing this. See the Gemara, chapter HaShoel. Meisharim, netiv 30, part 1, states that when an item suffers an unavoidable accident for which the custodian must pay, his property does not become encumbered from the time the item was borrowed or deposited with him, but only from the time of the accident. Darkei Moshe, siman 28. See also siman 341, seif 4, and Responsa Ri MiBeit Levi, siman 28, p. 151. Regarding a custodian who transfers the deposit to another custodian, see Responsa Shev Yaakov, Choshen Mishpat, siman 11.
