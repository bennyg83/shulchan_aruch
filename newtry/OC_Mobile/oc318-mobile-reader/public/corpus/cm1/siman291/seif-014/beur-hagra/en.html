And although, etc. This is from the Yerushalmi, cited by Rif and Rosh.
(Supplement) And although he placed it, etc., among his own, etc. The Yerushalmi states this, and Raavyah explains that it applies even where the deposit was placed in a chest in his room; otherwise the ruling would add nothing. See Mordekhai, siman 360. (End of supplement.)
