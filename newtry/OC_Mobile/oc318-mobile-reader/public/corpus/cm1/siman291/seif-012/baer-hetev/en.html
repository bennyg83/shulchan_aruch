The Raavad writes that this is considered an accident resulting from prior negligence, because we say that had the bailee been there, the verse 'the fear and dread of you' would have been fulfilled in him, or as David said, 'both the lion and the bear...' The Tur agrees with him. This also appears to be the view of Tosafot in chapter HaMafkid, Bava Metzia 42a, at the end of the page, and Tosafot state it explicitly in chapter HaUmanin, 78a. Although the Mechaber rules like the Rif and Rambam, the Ma'adanei Melech and Bach agree with the Tur. It therefore appears to the Shach that, in practice, the burden of proof rests upon the party seeking to extract money. This also seems to follow from the Rama in siman 303, seif 10. It requires study why the Rama states the law here without qualification. Perhaps he distinguishes between an unpaid bailee and a paid bailee, but in truth there is no basis for such a distinction. More likely, when the Rama writes there, 'and see above siman 291, seif 12,' he means that the dissenting opinion there disputes this case as well. Examine carefully. Shach.
