At a time when people ordinarily enter, etc. For he guarded it in the manner people ordinarily guard.
Otherwise he is exempt. Although the beginning involved negligence with respect to theft or loss, the eventual accident did not result from that negligence. Even had he been there, he could not have saved it from the lion. Ra'avad writes that this is nevertheless considered an accident resulting from negligence, because we say that had he been there the verse, "the fear and dread of you shall be upon every beast of the earth," might have been fulfilled through him, or as David said, "Your servant struck both the lion and the bear." The Tur agrees with Ra'avad. Nevertheless, the Beit Yosef and the Mechaber rule like Rif and Rambam. See there and in the Derishah.
