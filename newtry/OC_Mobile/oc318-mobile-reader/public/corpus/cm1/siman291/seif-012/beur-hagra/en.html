If he entered, etc. As implied there: 'What, is it not, etc.?' Regarding an unpaid custodian, everyone agrees with Rabbah, as explained above; the dispute concerns only a paid custodian, because he is paid precisely for this additional vigilance.
And if he entered, etc. Although the Gemara asks, 'If so, why is he exempt?' and the same reasoning could apply to an unpaid custodian, Rif explains that this statement follows Abaye's view, whereas we rule like Rava. Tosafot likewise write this in Bava Kamma 52b in the name of Rivam. Raavad and Rosh disagree, reasoning that had he been present the animals might have fled from fear of him, as indicated by the verse, 'the fear of you,' and by Bava Kamma 107a. See siman 303:10.
