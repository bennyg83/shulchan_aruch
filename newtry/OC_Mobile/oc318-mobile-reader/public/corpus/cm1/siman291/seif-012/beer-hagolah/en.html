Dalet) There, seif 14. The baraita on Bava Metzia 93b, which obligates a custodian even when the loss occurred at the time people ordinarily enter the city, concerns a paid custodian, as the Rif explains and Rambam rules in halachah 8. An unpaid custodian, however, is exempt where the loss occurred at the ordinary time people enter the city. The Maggid Mishneh writes this in the name of the Ramban and Rashba.
Heh) There in the name of the Rif, because the accident did not result from the negligence.
