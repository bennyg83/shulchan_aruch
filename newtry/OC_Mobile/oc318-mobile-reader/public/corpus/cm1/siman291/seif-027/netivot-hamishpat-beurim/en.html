He is liable even by words. See Ketzot HaChoshen, who proves from Shach that if a renter was already hired for a fixed term in a manner from which the owner could not retract, and the parties later agreed that he should have the liability of a borrower, a new kinyan is required. The Gemara's reason that verbal acceptance can suffice is the benefit the custodian receives because the owner trusted him. Once that benefit already passed at the beginning of the earlier hiring, it cannot serve as consideration for the later increase of liability. Ketzot's conclusion is correct.
