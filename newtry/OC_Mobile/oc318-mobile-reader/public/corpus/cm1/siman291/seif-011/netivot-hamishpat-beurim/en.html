It ascended by itself. This is difficult. Mechaber treats an animal that went up by itself as a case comparable only to theft, not negligence. Yet in siman 303, seif 14, where it went up by itself, overpowered him, and fell, he rules that the custodian is liable. If the beginning was merely comparable to theft and the end was an accident, why is he liable? In such a case one whose beginning was theft and end was accident should be exempt, as Sema there, seif katan 13, explains. I will address the matter there, with Hashem's help.
