Even if he did not, etc. Rif writes that the rule applies where the animal overpowered him, as stated on Bava Metzia 36b. Raavad objects that the Gemara needed this only according to Abaye, whereas we rule like Rava. Ramban answers that the passage follows Rabbah, but our ruling follows Rav Chisda and Rabbah bar Rav Huna, who explain that the additional payment given to a paid custodian is precisely for this greater responsibility. This therefore applies specifically to a paid custodian. See Rosh and Tosafot there.
