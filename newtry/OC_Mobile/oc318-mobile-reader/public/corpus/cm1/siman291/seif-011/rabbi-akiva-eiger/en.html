[Shulchan Aruch] This is not negligence. Note: Maharsha, in chapter HaMafkid, infers from Tosafot there, s.v. Alatah, that Tosafot considered it obvious as a matter of reasoning that if the animal went up by itself it constitutes negligence. In my humble opinion this is not compelled. One may say that Tosafot considered it obvious that it is comparable to theft or loss, and nevertheless they required their entire discussion because of how the Gemara understood the matter even without this according to Abaye, who said, "He guarded it in the manner that people guard." Examine this carefully.
