"Wait for me until I look for it," etc. Every custodian must take thought where he put the entrusted item and keep it in a guarded location. Terumat HaDeshen writes that a custodian who does not know whether the theft occurred through negligence or without negligence must pay because he cannot take the required oath. This applies specifically where he admits that it was stolen. Below, at the end of siman 298, the Rashba's view is that if he swears that he does not know that anything was stolen, he is exempt. There it is possible that nothing was stolen from him at all, and the Rashba explicitly gives that reason. Darkei Moshe cites it.
