Where did I place it? See Beurim: he is liable on the basis that he is a damager, and he is liable even where the owner was with him at the time:
To pay immediately. He need not wait until the owner requests payment. A custodian who does not know whether the item was stolen through negligence or through unavoidable circumstances is liable because he is one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear. The same applies where he claims that he understood from the wording of the deed that he had permission to act as he did, in circumstances where changing the terms was not itself negligence.
