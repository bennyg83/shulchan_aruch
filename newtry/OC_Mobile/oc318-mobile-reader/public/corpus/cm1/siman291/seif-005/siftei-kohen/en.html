And the owners withdrew, etc. See Sema, seif katan 8, through the words, 'as the Tur and Mechaber write at the end of this siman.' I do not know the source for this. It is not comparable to the case at the end of this siman. Examine carefully. The ruling below, siman 346, seif 4, also does not appear so. See there.
And there is an authority who says, etc. Rashi writes likewise in Perek Merubah, Bava Kamma 79a, as do the Rambam in his commentary to the Mishna there, the Maggid Mishna in chapter 2 of the Laws of Hiring, and the Nimukei Yosef in Perek HaOmanin. This also appears to be the primary implication of the Gemara in Perek Merubah. See there and examine carefully. See Responsa Mabit, part II, siman 341.
