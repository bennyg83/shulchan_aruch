Sema can be explained to mean that after the custodian accepted responsibility, the owner then entered the custodian's service to guard together with him. This is shemirah b'baalim because the owner's service began at the commencement of the guarding task. Even though the custodian had already become obligated to guard, the owner's service at the start of the actual work can still create the exemption, as Tosafot explain at the beginning of HaShoel in Bava Metzia.
Until he draws it. If a paid custodian receives his fee in advance, it appears clear that he becomes a paid custodian even without meshichah. A kinyan can create the employment relationship. In siman 333 I explained that when a worker is hired through payment, the money acquires his labor to the extent that he cannot retract in the ordinary manner, because we follow R. Yochanan that money acquires d'oraisa. Bava Metzia 48 and Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilchot Me'ilah, support this distinction. With ordinary movable property, rabbinic law suspends money-acquisition because of the concern that goods may burn in the seller's attic. With hired labor that concern is irrelevant. There is no difference whether one hired him to perform active labor or to guard. True, a worker hired for a period may ultimately retract, but until he retracts he is legally hired to the employer; the later retraction is a separate event. Thus payment is an acquisition for the employment relationship and makes him a paid custodian. Ketzot HaChoshen asks why Scripture needs to exclude debt instruments from custodial liability when transfer of a debt instrument ordinarily requires writing and delivery. According to what I wrote in siman 66 in the name of Tosafot in Ketubot 85, transfer of a document's physical holding right, such as the right to retain it for delivery or sale to the borrower or lender, can itself be a d'oraisa sale accomplished by meshichah. All the more so, drawing the document can be sufficient to undertake custodial responsibility for its market value. A full transfer of the underlying debt is not required merely to create the custodial obligation.
