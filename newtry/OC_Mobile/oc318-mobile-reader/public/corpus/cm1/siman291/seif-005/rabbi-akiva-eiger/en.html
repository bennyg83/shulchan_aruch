[Shulchan Aruch] And the shoes were lost. Note: It is also possible to say that the Rosh wrote this according to his own view that an act of acquisition is not required in order to create custodial liability. But according to Rambam and Nimukei Yosef, which is the view of the first opinion in seif 5 that he is not liable until he performs meshichah, one may say that here he never became liable. As a matter of meshichah, it is doubtful whether drawing the animal constitutes meshichah for the utensils upon it. See above, siman 202, seif 14. Acquisition by courtyard is unavailable because an animal is a moving courtyard. Consequently, there was no act of acquisition. I later saw Machaneh Ephraim, siman 7, where I found these same points.
