Kaf) There, seif 7, in the name of Rabbenu Yitzchak, and the Rosh likewise writes this, based on the statement that if one says "Place it before me," he becomes an unpaid custodian, as explained above in seif 1 and more fully in siman 307.
Lamed) There in the name of Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, halachah 8. The Maggid Mishneh cites Rabbi Elazar's statement in the eighth chapter of Bava Metzia 99a and explains that if the owner is a lender, he can no longer retract once the borrower's acquisition is effective, and that the custodian's liability begins from meshichah and not before. Regarding the rule that saying "Place it before me" creates liability, the Maggid Mishneh cites the Rashba, who suggests that the depositor placed the item before him in a side alley within his four cubits, or that in the case of an animal the custodian said, "Strike it with a stick and it will come," as explained in siman 340, seif 7, in the name of the Raavad. All the more so, if the item was placed within the custodian's domain, he becomes liable.
