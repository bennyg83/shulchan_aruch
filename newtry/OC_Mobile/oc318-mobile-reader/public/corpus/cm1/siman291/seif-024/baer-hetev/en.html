Liable. The Rosh writes that otherwise every deposit entrusted to a person could simply be consumed by his wife and children and he would be exempt. The Shach writes that if it is known that the bailee does not guard the property himself but regularly gives it to his wife and children, he is exempt. See Mordechai, Agudah, and Bach. See sefer A.A., 103b.
He is exempt. Since every depositor deposits with the understanding that the bailee's wife and children may handle the property, it is as though the depositor himself placed it in their hands. If the first bailee was a paid bailee and transferred it to an unpaid bailee, see Responsa Divrei Rivot, simanim 217 and 218, at length; Responsa Rashdam, simanim 40 and 50; and Responsa Rama, end of siman 27.
