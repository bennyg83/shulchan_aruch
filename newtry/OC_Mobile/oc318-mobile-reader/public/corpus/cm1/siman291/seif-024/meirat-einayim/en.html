The householder is liable to pay. The Rosh writes that otherwise every deposit entrusted to a person could be consumed by his wife and children, and he would thereby be exempt.
Some say he is exempt. Since anyone who deposits property does so with the understanding that the custodian's wife and children may have access to it, it is as though the depositor himself entrusted it to them.
