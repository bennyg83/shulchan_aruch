[Shach, subparagraph 32] And in Bach. Note: Mishneh LaMelech likewise concludes that the primary ruling is that where it is known that the first custodian would not personally guard the item, the first custodian is exempt. Otherwise, according to all views, the first remains liable, except where he gave it to his wife. In that latter case the authorities dispute, as cited in the Rama, subparagraph 4, whether this is comparable to a case where it is known that he would not guard it personally. The ruling above in siman 72, according to the Rama, is agreed upon by all. See there.
