[Shulchan Aruch] And if he delayed. Note: If after Shabbat he discovered that it had been stolen, and he does not know whether it was stolen before the conclusion of Shabbat, in which case he is exempt, or after the conclusion of Shabbat, when there had already been sufficient time to bury it, one may say that he is liable to pay. This is exactly comparable to the ruling of Terumat HaDeshen, siman 333. See also Responsa Maharam of Prague, siman 935. There, Reuven gave an object to Shimon to bring to a specified place and deliver it there to another person. Shimon later said that he had forgotten about it and that it was lost from him, but he did not know when it was lost. He argued on the basis of the possibility that it was lost earlier and that he should pay according to its value at that earlier location. Maharam ruled that he must pay according to its value at the specified destination. See there. This requires study. See also Responsa Mutzal MeEsh, siman 26.
