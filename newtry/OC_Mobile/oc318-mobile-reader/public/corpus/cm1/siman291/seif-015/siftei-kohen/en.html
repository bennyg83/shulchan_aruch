He must hide them in the wall within a handbreadth, etc. The Beit Yosef questions why the Rambam omitted those who probe walls, and the Bach further asks why he omitted the money changers mentioned in the Gemara, forcing the text into strained explanations. It appears to me that the Rif and Rambam understand the Gemara as follows: Nowadays wall-tappers are common, but wall-probers and money changers are not. Therefore, the Rif and Rambam mention only wall-tappers. Examine carefully, for this is clear. See the work Imrei Binah, folio 102d.
