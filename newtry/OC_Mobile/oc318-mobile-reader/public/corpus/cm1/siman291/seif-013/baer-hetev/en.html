And a vessel. Meaning garments made from silver and gold threads, comparable to the silk garments mentioned there. Actual silver and gold vessels are no less like money, regarding which the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write at the end of seif 15 that proper safeguarding requires burial in the ground. Alternatively, silver and gold utensils whose workmanship is delicate would be ruined or broken if buried in the ground. The first explanation appears primary. Ibid.
