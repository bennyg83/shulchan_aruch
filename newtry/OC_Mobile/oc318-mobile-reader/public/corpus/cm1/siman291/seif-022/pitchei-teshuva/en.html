See Baer Heitev, who cites Responsa Maharam Sasson concerning the doubt whether an unpaid custodian who stipulates to be liable like a paid custodian, and then claims unavoidable accident, becomes liable without a kinyan. See Ketzot HaChoshen below, siman 301. Tosafos, Bava Metzia 58a, s.v. amar Rabbi Yochanan, write in one answer that where a person obligates himself as a borrower, people assume the owner lent him the item and, in exchange for that benefit, he obligates himself. According to that answer one could apparently say that an unpaid custodian does not become liable as a paid custodian without a kinyan, and that only a borrower can undertake enhanced liability in this manner. However, the decisors cite only Tosafos's first answer, as Sema writes below in siman 301, seif 4, and above in siman 66, seif katan 90. According to that approach there is no distinction.
