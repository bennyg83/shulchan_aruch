The attendant is exempt, etc. The Beit Yosef cites Tosafot as limiting this to a single house, and Hagahot Ashri likewise rules so. It appears to me, however, that no distinction should be made. On that approach it is also strained to distinguish between whether the agent delayed or did not delay. Tosafot and Hagahot Ashri were compelled to this conclusion by the law of misuse of sacred property by an agent. One may answer that misuse is different, because even if the agent says that he acted inadvertently, misuse still applies. Alternatively, that case differs because the sender expressly mentioned the window. This answer appears in Tosafot, Gittin, Perek HaTekabel, 65a. See the Ran and Rashba there; Responsa Rabbi Shlomo Kohen, part II, siman 200; and Responsa Rabbi Moshe Alshech, siman 23.
