Because when he saw that he was delaying, etc. The Tur adds that he hung the matter upon the sender because he had not told him, "Take from this," and had not said that it should not be taken from the deposit. In the Perishah I wrote that if he had expressly told the messenger, "Take from this and not from the deposit," then the messenger's mere delay in returning would not by itself make the custodian liable. The custodian could say that something happened along the way that forced the messenger to delay briefly.
