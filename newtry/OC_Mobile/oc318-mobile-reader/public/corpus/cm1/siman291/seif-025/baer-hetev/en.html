A place. The Beit Yosef cites Tosafot that this applies specifically where both places are in one house, and Hagahot Ashri rules similarly. The Shach writes that he sees no reason to distinguish in this way, and according to that approach it is also difficult to distinguish between whether the agent delayed or not. Tosafot and Hagahot Ashri were forced to this explanation because of the law of me'ilah regarding an agent. One may answer that me'ilah is different, because even if the agent says he acted inadvertently, me'ilah still applies. Alternatively, that case is different because the principal specifically mentioned a window. This second answer appears in Tosafot, Gittin 65. See the Ran and Rashba there, Responsa Rashach, book II, siman 200, and Responsa R. Moshe Alshich, siman 23. Shach.
