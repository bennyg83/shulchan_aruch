One who receives money for safekeeping shortly before twilight on Friday need not trouble to bury it until after Shabbos. If enough time passes after Shabbos to bury it and he fails to do so, and it is stolen or lost through accident, he is liable. If the depositor is a Torah scholar, the bailee need not act until enough time after Havdalah has elapsed to bury it. Some say the same when the bailee is a Torah scholar.
