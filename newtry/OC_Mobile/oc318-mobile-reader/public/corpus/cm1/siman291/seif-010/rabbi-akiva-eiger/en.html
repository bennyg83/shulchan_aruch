[Shulchan Aruch] For payment, he is exempt. Note: If he rescued it for payment, he swears and collects what he expended. See siman 350, seif 9. Responsa Mahari ben Lev, siman 104, writes that even if he rescued his own property he collects. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 125. This is the language of Maharikash. Darkei Moshe likewise writes this in the name of Responsa Maharik, and Shach writes the same in siman 303, seif katan 8.
