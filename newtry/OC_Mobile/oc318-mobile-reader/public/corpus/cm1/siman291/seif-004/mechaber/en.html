Even after accepting custody, a bailee is liable only according to the value he accepted. If given a gold dinar and told it is silver, and he negligently loses it, he pays only a silver dinar, since that is the custody he accepted. If he directly destroys it, he pays its actual gold value. Rama: If Shimon accepts books from Reuven to transport and customs officials seize them because Reuven falsely said books incur no duty, Shimon's claim that he never accepted the burden of customs is valid.
