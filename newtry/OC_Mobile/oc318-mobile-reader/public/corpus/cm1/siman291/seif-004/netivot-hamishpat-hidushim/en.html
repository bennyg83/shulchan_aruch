See Beurim: even if the borrower knows that the object is actually gold and uses it, nevertheless he is liable only according to the value of silver. Likewise, a finder guarding a lost gold object that is plated to look like silver is liable only according to silver value. But one who steals gold plated to look like silver is liable for its actual gold value.
