[Rama] Behold, the house is before you. Note: Beit Yosef, siman 195, writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham, Netiv 9, part 4, concerning one who purchased in a manner that neither party can retract, but the item remained in the seller's possession. Some leading authorities write that the seller has the status of an unpaid custodian, while others write that he does not even have the status of an unpaid custodian, because it is comparable to saying, "Behold, the house is before you." This latter view appears primary. See Sema, siman 66.
