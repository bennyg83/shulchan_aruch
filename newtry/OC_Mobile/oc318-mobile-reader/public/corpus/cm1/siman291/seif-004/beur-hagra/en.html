Even an acquisition made in error is reversed, as stated at the end of the first chapter of Gittin; all the more so an acceptance of custody made under error. He therefore never became a custodian. In Bava Kamma 41a the Gemara says that he may answer, 'Your ox, etc.,' and does not resolve it by saying that he accepted it under a mistaken assumption.
