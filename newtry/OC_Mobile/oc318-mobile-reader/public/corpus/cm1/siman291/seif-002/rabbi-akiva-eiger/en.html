[Shulchan Aruch] He is an unpaid custodian, for he deposited it. Note: Reuven transferred ownership of an object as a gift to Shimon through a third person, but the object remained in the possession of Reuven, the donor. See the language of the Raavad cited by the Tur in siman 60, and Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Shomrim, siman 15.
