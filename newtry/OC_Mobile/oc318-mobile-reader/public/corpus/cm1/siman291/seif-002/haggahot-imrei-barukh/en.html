(Shach, seif katan 35) And in such a case, even if the money belonged to the son, etc. Note: This requires study. Why is this not a case of the owner being with him in his work, since a son is obligated to act according to the wishes of his father and mother? See siman 346:12 and 13. What Tosafot write on page 12, passage beginning "everyone who deposits," is correct only before the Gemara there concludes that the mother did not know the property was not the depositor's.
