The present time. The Beit Yosef quotes Rabbeinu Tam as follows: The requirement to guard money by burying it in the ground applied specifically in their times, when their houses were fragile. Nowadays, however, there is no need to guard money by burying it in the ground. See Terumat HaDeshen, siman 333, regarding a roofed room that is like a vaulted chamber. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 47, and this requires study. See there further, simanim 49, 76, 117, 125, 134, 141, 314, and 444; Responsa Ibn Lev, end of book IV, ruling 18; Responsa Mahara Sasson, siman 150; Responsa Rashach, part II, siman 169; Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 59; and Responsa Maharit, siman 105 and beginning of siman 123.
