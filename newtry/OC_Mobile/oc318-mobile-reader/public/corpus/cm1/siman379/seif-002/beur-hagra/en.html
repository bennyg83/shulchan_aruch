(Collected note) And if he stopped, etc. The Rif and other poskim omit the Gemara's qualification that he stood like a beam. Their reason is that Rava disagrees with Reish Lakish even in that case. A lying animal is acting without permission while a walking animal acts with permission, and Bava Kamma 48b teaches that one acting without permission is liable even when the other merely suffers injury. The Rosh forces the passage unnecessarily; his explanation is difficult. (End of note.)
See above at the beginning of siman 413. There the Rosh's view is cited, ruling like Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak, and the Tur likewise cites it there, whereas here the ruling is stated without qualification in accordance with the Rif and Rambam.
