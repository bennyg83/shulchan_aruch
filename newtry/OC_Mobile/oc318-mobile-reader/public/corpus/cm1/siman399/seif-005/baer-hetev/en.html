He seized. The Tur writes this in the name of the Rosh, unlike the Ramah, who holds that seizure does not help for a penalty even without witnesses. Nimukei Yosef at the end of HaManiach follows the Ramah. The Bach asks from another statement of the Ramah cited by the Rosh and Tur, which indicates that seizure does help for half damages, a penalty. The Shach answers that there the claimant had witnesses to the damage and therefore there was no exemption based on admission to a penalty. He further writes that even according to the Rosh and Tur, seizure accompanied by a migo helps only if it occurred before the defendant admitted. In our times, however, when there are no ordained judges and the defendant's statement is not treated as a formal admission before such judges, as Ramban, Rosh, and Tur explain at the end of siman 1, seizure of a penalty is effective whenever the claimant has a migo. This appears correct. Shach.
