However, if he seized. See Baer Heitev, who writes that even according to Rosh and Tur, seizure together with a migo helps only if the seizure occurred before the wrongdoer's admission. Dagul MeRevavah questions this. According to Shach, possession by seizure functions like witnesses. If so, even where the wrongdoer admitted first, the case is one of admission to a penalty followed by witnesses. Since the admission itself created no liability, the later equivalent of witnesses should establish the penalty. See Rambam, Hilchot Geneivah, chapter 3, law 9, and Mishneh LaMelech there, end of law 8. Dagul MeRevavah then notes that Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter Merubah, siman 26, holds that with every penalty, even where the admission creates no payment obligation at all, later witnesses do not revive liability, except in the special case of admission regarding slaughter. See Ketzot HaChoshen above at the end of siman 1.
