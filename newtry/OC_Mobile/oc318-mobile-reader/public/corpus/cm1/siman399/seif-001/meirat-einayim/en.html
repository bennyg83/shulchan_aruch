From her and from her offspring. Meaning, even though the ox was a tam and a tam pays only from its own body. Tur.
Even if the cow is no longer present. One might have thought that there is no basis for liability against the offspring. But if the cow itself is present, what difference does it make to the damager whether payment comes from the cow alone or also from the offspring, since he need pay only half the injured party's damage? The novelty is that the entire half-damage may be collected from the offspring even where the cow is absent, and we do not say that only one quarter can be collected from the offspring. Still, there is also a novelty where the cow is present, such as where the cow is not worth enough to cover the injured party's half-damage.
