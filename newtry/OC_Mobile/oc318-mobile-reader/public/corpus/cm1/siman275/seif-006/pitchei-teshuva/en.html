And he thought they were his, he did not acquire them. See Ketzot HaChoshen, who writes that this applies specifically to a found object and to the property of a convert who died without heirs. In a gift, however, he acquires even in such a case, because another person's intent transfers the property to him. Even if the recipient did not know, once he takes possession he acquires. This is learned from Raavad, cited by Nimukei Yosef in the chapter Chezkas HaBatim regarding Rav Anan. With this he resolves the difficulty raised by Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot Mechirah, chapter 4, concerning the Tur. See there. See also Chatam Sofer, volume 6, siman 32, and what I wrote in Pitchei Teshuva to Yoreh Deah, siman 320, seif katan 5.
