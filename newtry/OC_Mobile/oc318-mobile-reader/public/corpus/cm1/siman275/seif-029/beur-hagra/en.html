A convert, etc. Rambam explains the passage as follows: first they heard that the convert had died and took possession of the property; afterward they heard that he had not died, which weakened their claim; then they again heard that the first report had been true. According to Rava, the first possessor did not acquire because his possession was uncertain, while according to Abaye he did. Rambam's text differs from ours and resolves the discussion without assuming that Rava disputes Abaye concerning an inheritance that comes automatically. See 26, 40, and 41.
