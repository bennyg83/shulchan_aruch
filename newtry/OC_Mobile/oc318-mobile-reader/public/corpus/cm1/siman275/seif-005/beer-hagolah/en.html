The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam learned this from the Gemara's conclusion there regarding Rav Asi's statement: but concerning pe'ah and ritual impurity, no; Ravin said, etc., even concerning pe'ah, etc., but not Shabbat. If so, anything that interrupts for pe'ah, impurity, and Shabbat certainly interrupts acquisition of a convert's property. Indeed, a boundary marker and a stonecut boundary, according to some, do not interrupt for pe'ah, impurity, or Shabbat, yet they do interrupt acquisition of a convert's property. This is straightforward.
