Because he can say, "I am taking my wood and stones." This will be explained below in siman 375. One who enters another's ruin and builds it without permission may afterward say, "I am taking my wood and stones." Therefore, if the first builder says that he is removing his wood and stones, the second person has accomplished nothing by merely setting up doors in that structure.
Therefore the land remains ownerless, etc. The Tur, in the name of the Ramah, writes just before this, and the Rosh agrees with him: If the first person removes the second person's doors and installs his own doors, the first acquires the land. If not, the law is that the first takes his building materials, the second takes his doors, and the land remains ownerless as before. Whoever then first takes possession of the land acquires it.
