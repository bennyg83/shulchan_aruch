There, law 8, from the statement of Reish Lakish in the name of Rabbah bar Avuha.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that this follows Rambam's reading and the Rif's reading there: if he took possession of the inner area intending to acquire it and the outer area, he acquires the inner but not the outer. The Beit Yosef writes in the name of the Ran that this is the reading of the Geonim. He further writes that in matters of textual tradition the early authorities were more expert, and we therefore follow them.
He said, “And so it is written in his name, and it is a bit seen in this verse that he said in the fields and so on.”
