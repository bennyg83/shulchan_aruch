The Maggid Mishneh writes that in the Mechaber's view, the rule stated above that acquisition extends only as far as the ox's plowing line and returns, explained above in seif 3, applies where acquisition was made by a single blow of a hoe. Here, however, where he performed another form of chazakah among those explained in Bava Batra and above in siman 192, the law differs. The Rif apparently holds likewise, for it records both statements without distinguishing them.
The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes in the name of the Rashba that some reconcile the statements this way and hold that they do not disagree. Rabbenu Chananel likewise explains so.
