The language of Rambam there, law 10. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam rules like Rav Huna in the name of Rav in the third chapter of Bava Batra, 54a, and Rav Nachman likewise agrees there at the beginning of 55a. The Rif likewise rules so. This applies specifically to a field intended for plowing, but not to a house. The case concerns one who intended to acquire the entire field, as written in seif 24. The Tur likewise writes so.
