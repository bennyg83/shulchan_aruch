And he benefited from the body of the land. But beautifying the land by spreading bedding upon it does not count as an act in the body of the land because it is not lasting. The reason eating the produce does not acquire, as explained above, is that the person does not benefit from the body of the land itself. It resembles one who seeks to acquire a slave through the slave cooking food for him. That service does not acquire the slave. Rather, the slave must lift the master, so that the slave acts upon the master's body. Likewise here, either the land itself must directly provide bodily benefit to the acquirer, or the acquirer must make an improvement in the body of the land. See the Perishah.
