The substance. He does not acquire land by eating its produce, because this is not considered deriving benefit from the substance of the land. It is comparable to acquiring a slave: one does not acquire the slave because the slave cooked food for him. Rather, we require the slave to lift something for his master, for then the master is making use of the slave's body, as written in siman 196. The same applies here. Plowing the field, however, is an act performed upon the substance of the field, and therefore he acquires it through that act. Ibid.
