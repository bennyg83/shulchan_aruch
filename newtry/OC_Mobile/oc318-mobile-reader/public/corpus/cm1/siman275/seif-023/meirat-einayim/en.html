Even if the lower partition was swallowed up. This differs from the law in Orach Chaim siman 358 concerning an enclosure not originally surrounded for residence, where one built another partition above it and the lower one was later swallowed into the ground, and the upper partition remains effective. Acquisition from a convert's ownerless property is different. We require that the benefit and improvement to the land created by the possessor be apparent and recognizable.
