There, law 18, from the baraita there in chapter 9 of Bava Batra, 142a. The Maggid Mishneh explains the baraita according to his reading: 'and afterward they heard that his son had died,' meaning that he had already died. The words of Ibn Migash incline this way. The Tur likewise writes so. His reading is that the practical difference is where they heard that she miscarried but she had not yet miscarried, and afterward she miscarried, etc. The Maggid Mishneh discusses the interpretation at length; this is not the place to elaborate.
