Second. And he said to him, “When his father died, and he had a bit of his clothes, and then he was told that he had not died, and that his father had returned to him, and that his father had been brought back to him, and the days that he had come to him, and that I was brought to him, and that he had been brought to him. A:
And in the first. See the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 2 of Hilchot Zechiyah, the Gemara in Bava Batra 142, and the Rosh in the chapter Almanah LeKohen Gadol. Examine carefully; the matter requires study. Shach.
