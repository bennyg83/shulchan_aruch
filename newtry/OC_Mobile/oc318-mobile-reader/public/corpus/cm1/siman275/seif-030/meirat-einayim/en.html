First of all, he died while holding the first one, he was already dead
Everyone holding the other bought. I wrote in a statement that he held after he had already heard that he had died:
In the first instance he did not acquire. Since his possession was based only on hearing that the owner had died, and afterward he heard that the report was false and withdrew from the property, his original possession was completely uprooted. This differs from the rule in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 340. If one heard that his father died, tore his garments and observed part of shivah, then was told that the report was false and stopped mourning, and later it was confirmed that the father had indeed died originally, he fulfilled the obligation of tearing and the days of mourning he observed count. There, in any event he tore while emotionally aroused and actually practiced mourning. Nothing later undid those acts.
