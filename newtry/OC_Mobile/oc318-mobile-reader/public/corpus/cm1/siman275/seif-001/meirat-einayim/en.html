A convert who died, etc. See Hagahot Mordechai concerning a Canaanite slave who died without making any deathbed instructions and had deposited all his property with someone who did not wish to acquire it for himself. Others then came claiming rights in the money. The ruling was that their claims are heard and they may recover on oath, while the remainder goes to consecrated purposes. See the lengthy discussion there and Darkei Moshe to this siman.
