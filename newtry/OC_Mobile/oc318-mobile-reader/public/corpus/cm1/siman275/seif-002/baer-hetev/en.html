For his burial. The Tur explains the reason: immediately upon the death of a convert without heirs, his property becomes ownerless. We do not find that the obligation of his burial is imposed as a lien upon his property, because during his lifetime such a lien had not yet taken effect. If a creditor came and took the deceased's property after his death, the creditor would not be obligated to bury him. The Shach writes that the Bach did not rule this way, but the Bach's words are not compelling.
