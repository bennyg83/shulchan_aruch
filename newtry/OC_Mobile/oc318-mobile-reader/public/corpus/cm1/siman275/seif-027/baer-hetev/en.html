Pledges. The Tur states: if the convert has a mortgage on land held by a Jew and the convert dies, the land returns to its owner. The Bach emends the text to read: if he has a document concerning a mortgage on land that is held, and explains that the document is held by another person, but if the pledge itself was deposited with another, that other person acquires it. See there. This does not appear correct to me. Since the convert's lien terminates upon his death, what right would the other person have in the pledge? It appears more correct to read: if he has a mortgage on land held by him from a Jew, as the Tur writes above in siman 72, seif 30. This is also implied by Baal HaTerumot at the end of Gate 49, which is the source of this law. See above in siman 72, from seif 36 through the end of seif 40, for laws relevant here. Shach.
