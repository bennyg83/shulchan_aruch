In the convert's possession, etc. The Nimukei Yosef in Perek HaParah writes in the name of the Rosh that this applies even to collateral taken after the loan was made. Tosafot in Perek HaParah state the same, as does the Maggid Mishna in chapter 2 of the Laws of Acquisition and Ownerless Property. According to what I wrote above, siman 72, seif 2, however, this is not so. See there and examine carefully.
If the convert has, etc. The Tur's wording is: 'If he has mortgaged land held by a Jew and he dies, the land returns to its owner.' The Bach emends the text to read: 'If he has a document concerning mortgaged land that is held,' and explains that the document is held by another person, whereas if the collateral itself was deposited with another person, that person acquires it. This does not appear correct to me. Since the lien lapses upon the convert's death, what right could the other person have in the collateral? It appears more likely that the text should read: 'If he has mortgaged land that he holds from a Jew,' as the Tur writes above, siman 72, seif 30. This is also implied by Baal HaTerumot at the end of Shaar 49, which is the source of this law. See there.
Concerning mortgaged land, etc. See above, siman 72, from seif 36 through the end of seif 40, for the laws relevant here.
