Seif 3, in the gloss: a testamentary document. The Sema asks from siman 255, where a testamentary document is effective even to collect from encumbered property. It appears to me that the Rama can be reconciled by distinguishing between one who writes a will in general terms and one who writes it in formal document language. This distinction is mentioned in siman 250, seif 21. As a matter of practical law, however, one should not extract money contrary to Rashba's opinion at the end of siman 253. A will does not gain the enhanced force of an ordinary document, as I demonstrated in siman 43, seif 9. See what I wrote there.
