If a person's document was lost and he asks the beit din to write another, it may not do so, even if he brings the original signatory witnesses and they testify that they wrote and delivered it. Therefore, a beit din should not copy any document unless it sees a need, as in the case of an erased debt document. If a person presents a copy, the beit din does not collect upon it until he produces the original, unless the copy states why another document was made, as with an erased document or a similar case, such as where witnesses know it was lost. If they do not know, they may not write a replacement. Even if witnesses saw it become lost, they do not write another, because it may be found, unless witnesses testify that it was burned. {Rama: This applies to a loan document. If a sale or gift document without a guarantee was lost, another may be written. No document should be copied without need (Responsa of the Rosh, klal 68, simanim 16, 21, 23, and 27). If a will grants gifts to many people and one beneficiary asks the beit din to write only his portion, it may do so, because the will is not a collection instrument and the heirs can claim payment (Responsa of the Rashba, siman 978, and Responsa of the Ramban, siman 83).} If the document was held by another person and was lost from that person's possession, a replacement may be written, because there is no concern that the owner will find it, since it was not in his possession. {Rama: Responsa of the Rashba, siman 978; this is repeated below at the end of siman 253, and see there, seif 2.} If the borrower is overseas and the lender fears taking the document on the journey lest it be lost, he may come to beit din for a replacement and leave the original with the beit din in his city. The beit din where the borrower is located notifies the lender's local beit din when the debt is paid, and the latter tears up the original. If the replacement is lost on the journey and witnesses know this, the lender testifies to that fact before a beit din and the original is returned to him. {Rama: Every copied document must be reproduced letter for letter from the original and marked as a copy. A beit din is not required; even two witnesses may copy it, and some add even if they are related to one another (Rivash, siman 413). See also simanim 16 and 50.}
