One whose debt document was erased, etc. Sema says this repeats the rule from the beginning of the siman. It appears that the opening case concerned a document that was beginning to be erased, in accordance with Rabbi Yehudah, while some trace was still visible and the creditor merely feared total loss. Here, however, the document has already been completely erased. The novelty is that even then a replacement can be written. Also, the requirement to bring witnesses applies only when it was completely erased. If its writing is still visible, there is already a valid document before us and no additional proof is needed; the creditor merely seeks protection against future destruction. This explains why Mechaber repeated the matter and here specifically requires evidence. See Tumim.
