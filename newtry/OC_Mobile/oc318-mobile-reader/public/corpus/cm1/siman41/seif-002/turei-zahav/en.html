(Seif 3: they do not write another document for him even if he brings witnesses, etc.) The Tur gives the reason that perhaps it was paid. Rashbam in Gittin Batra 161 says perhaps he later found the original and will collect twice. The Tur abbreviates Rashbam’s reason to “perhaps it was paid,” because Rashbam’s formulation may refer even to a document found within its term, where payment is not suspected but improper collection from purchasers remains possible. The Tur, however, holds that even within the term a fallen document raises concern for payment, as stated below, and therefore states the shorter reason.
