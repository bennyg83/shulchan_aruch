One whose promissory note was erased. At the beginning of the siman the Mechaber copied Rambam's wording, and here he begins to copy the Tur. Along the way he repeats, 'one whose promissory note was erased,' even though he already wrote it. Meaning, what we said above, that when a document is erased and witnesses were appointed over it a replacement certification is written, applies specifically where there are witnesses, etc. See also what I wrote at the beginning of this seif, Sema seif katan 3.
But if the lender erased it knowingly, etc. Meaning, whenever he has no witnesses that it was erased without his knowledge, the law treats it as though he erased it knowingly. He should have safeguarded his document, and since he did not, we say that it had already been paid.
