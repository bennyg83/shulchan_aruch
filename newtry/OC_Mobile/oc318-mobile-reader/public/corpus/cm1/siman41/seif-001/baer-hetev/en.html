The Responsa of the Rashba imply the opposite. He ruled permissively only regarding a deed of sale, because the purchaser is already in possession of the land, or for purposes of exemption, where testimony may be accepted outside the litigant's presence as stated in siman 28, seif 18. But to testify concerning a debt document so as to impose liability and create a complete instrument permitting collection from him should not be allowed. The Rama's words require study.
