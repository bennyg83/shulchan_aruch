A worker's find belongs to himself. See Responsa Chut HaShani, siman 38, regarding a servant who bought a horse with his own money and sold it, while the employer remained silent until the sale yielded a large profit, and then demanded the profit. He rules that the employer's claim has no substance. A worker's find belongs to himself, and a servant has the status of a worker, as is evident from Terumat HaDeshen. Merchandise bought cheaply has the law of a found object, as is evident from Mordechai, Bava Metzia, chapter 9. See there.
