The found property of a deaf-mute, minor, or mentally incompetent person, etc. See Responsa Mabit, part I, siman 122, who writes that if one steals from a minor an item that the minor acquired only by rabbinic law, such as through a deathly ill person's gift, it is not recoverable through beit din. His words require study. See my work Tekfo Kohen, siman 30; Hagahot Mordechai at the end of Sanhedrin; and Responsa Mahari Bezalel, simanim 26, 27, and 38.
