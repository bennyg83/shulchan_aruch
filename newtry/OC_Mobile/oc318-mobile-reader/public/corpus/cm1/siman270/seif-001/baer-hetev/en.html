Robbery. See what I wrote in siman 243, seif 15. Responsa Mabit, part I, siman 122, writes that if one robs a minor of something the minor acquired only by rabbinic law, such as through the gift of a dangerously ill person, the property cannot be recovered through beit din. His words require study. See my work Tekfo Kohen, siman 30; Hagahot Maimoniyot at the end of Sanhedrin; and Responsa of Maharam Ashkenazi, simanim 26, 27, and 38. Shach.
Wages. The Rashba writes, as cited by the Darchei Moshe: 'But if another person conveys it to him, or it was already his, or he earned it, it is completely his.' Meaning, it may be recovered through beit din from one who comes to take it from him. Sema.
