But the wages of a minor and the like are recovered from them. Rashba writes, as cited by Darkei Moshe: When another person intentionally transfers property to a minor, or when the property was already the minor's, or when he earned it, it is completely his. This means that beit din removes it from anyone who seeks to take it from him. At the end of seif 2 it will be explained that when a minor eats at his father's table, the earnings belong to his father. The Ir Shushan writes: "But the wages of a minor or the like, which another person is obligated to transfer to him, are recovered through beit din." His phrase "obligated to transfer to him" is not literal. He means only that the other person is obligated to give it to him.
