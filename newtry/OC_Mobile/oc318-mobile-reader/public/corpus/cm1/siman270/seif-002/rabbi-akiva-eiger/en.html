[Rama] If they profited. Note: Ramban writes similarly in a responsum in Toldos Adam, siman 105. This is also clear from Tosafos, Bava Kamma 87b, s.v. "VeKatani," and from Shach, Yoreh Deah, siman 380, seif 4. However, the Itur, letter 20, laws of writing, page 73, holds that the earnings of sons supported at their father’s table, and likewise of a daughter after majority, do not belong to the father. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 1, siman 105; Responsa Shitah Mekubetzes, siman 38; and Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchos Zechiyah UMatannah, siman 3.
