A worker's find belongs to himself. Responsa Chut HaShani 38 discusses a servant who bought a horse with his own money and sold it at a large profit. His employer remained silent until after the sale and then demanded the profit. Chut HaShani rules that the employer has no claim. A worker's found property belongs to the worker, and a household servant has the legal status of a worker, as is evident from Terumat HaDeshen. Merchandise purchased at an unusually low price is treated like a found object, as appears from Mordechai, Bava Metzia, chapter 9.
