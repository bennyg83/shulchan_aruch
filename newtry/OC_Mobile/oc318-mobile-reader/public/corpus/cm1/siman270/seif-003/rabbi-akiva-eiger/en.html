[Rama] And if they gave him a gift. Note: See Nimukei Yosef, who states an even greater novelty, that this applies even if his father himself gave him the gift. See Responsa Rabbi Betzalel Ashkenazi, siman 38, and consider carefully. Regarding whether others can acquire on behalf of third parties through him, see Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chaim, siman 366.
