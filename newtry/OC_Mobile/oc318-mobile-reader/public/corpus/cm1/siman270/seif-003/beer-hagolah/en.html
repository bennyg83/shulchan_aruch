The language of Rambam at the end of chapter 9 of Hilkhot Sekhirut. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is a baraita cited in the first chapter of Bava Metzia, and Rambam rules like Rav Pappa that when a worker's found property belongs to his employer, that is specifically where he hired him to collect finds. Rabbenu Chananel likewise explains so.
For a worker or Hebrew slave, according to the Tur in the name of the Ramah. Since afterward he took him along or told him to collect found property, this is included in the work he initially assigned him. Beit Yosef. See the end of siman 181.
