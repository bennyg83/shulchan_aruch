Or one who became unable to speak. Meaning, he had been able to speak and later lost that ability. This differs from one born mute, who was never able to speak from birth.
In the manner he is examined regarding gittin. The Tur in Even HaEzer and the Mechaber there in siman 121 explain the examination. The Tur writes: if one became unable to speak and they ask whether he wants them to write a get for his wife, and he nods yes, they test him with unrelated questions whose answers are known. If they see that his mind is settled and he indicates "no" in the normal manner when the correct answer is no, and nods "yes" when the correct answer is yes, three times for each, they write and give the get. They should not ask three consecutive yes questions followed by three consecutive no questions. Rather, they should vary them, such as one yes and two no, or one no and two yes. They should not test him with matters currently present, but in summer ask whether he wants winter fruit, and in winter whether he wants summer fruit. If he answers yes, he is clearly incompetent; if he answers no, he is mentally competent. See there for further details of the examination.
