[Rama] He sold land. Note: This applies specifically where he inherited it from his ancestors, and likewise where it was given to him as a gift through another person who acquired it on his behalf, as below, siman 243, seif 16. But if he purchased it and then sold it, we say that when he sold it he was of sound mind. The Ran holds that if he purchased by deed, we know that he bought it while mentally competent, because witnesses do not sign and so forth. We then say that he was the original owner of record, and his sale is void because of the uncertainty created by two sets of contradictory witnesses. The Ritva, however, holds that there is no such presumption, because witnesses scrutinize only the seller, whereas they do not scrutinize the purchaser, for acquisition may be made for his benefit. See Responsa Maharit, part 1, siman 127.
