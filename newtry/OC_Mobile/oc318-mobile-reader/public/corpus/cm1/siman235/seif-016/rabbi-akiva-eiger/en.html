[Rama] The seller. Note: In Beit Yosef, siman 29, he cites a responsum of the Rashba that this applies specifically in a case where there are two sets of contradictory witnesses regarding the sale itself. But if two witnesses say that he sold on condition that if he does such and such the sale is void, and two witnesses say there was no condition, then according to everyone he lawfully entered the land initially, and they dispute only whether he must later leave because of the condition. We therefore leave the land in the purchaser's possession. See Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 15 of Hilchot To'en, and Tekfo Kohen, siman 58.
