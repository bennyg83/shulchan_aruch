Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 29 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 7. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is straightforward, because the child has not yet reached the age of the pe'utot, as discussed in the Mishnah in Gittin 59a. Rava states in Gittin 65a that there are three stages with respect to a minor, etc.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh explains that Rambam refers to the Gemara on 59a: the purchases and sales of pe'utot are valid. How old are they? Rabbi Yehudah indicated to Rav Yitzchak his son that this can be about age six or seven. The statements do not disagree; each child is judged according to his sharpness.
The Rama inserted into Rambam's words the view of the Rosh cited by the Tur. Rambam himself does not hold this way, as explained below in seif 8. It therefore should have been presented as a "some say" opinion. The Sema also notes this.
Gimel) The Gemara's conclusion there.
Dalet) A statement of Rabbi Yochanan there, based on the minor's need for livelihood.
Heh) There in the Mishnah.
Vav) This is explained in the ninth chapter of Bava Batra 155b.
Zayin) The Tur, seif 3. The Rosh writes in responsa at the end of rule 85 and the end of rule 1 that even so, the minor's gift is not valid. The Rashba writes in a responsum concerning a six-year-old who received a gift through an adult, where the giver did not specify when it should be delivered and the adult handed it to the child immediately: if the child is not sharp and knowledgeable, the adult acted negligently in giving it to him. See Responsa Ran, siman 49, and Sema in the name of Darkei Moshe.
Chet) In the Rosh's responsa there.
Tet) The Tur, seif 4, in the name of the Rosh's responsum at the end of rule 85.
