[Shulchan Aruch] The seller does not. Note: It appears to me that this can be derived from the Rosh, chapter 9 of Bava Metzia, end of siman 23. However, this is difficult from Chullin 83a, where regarding a transfer to the purchaser through a third party on ordinary days of the year, the Gemara says that it is a detriment to him, and so forth. This shows that the purchaser cannot compel the seller to uphold the sale. Long afterward, Sefer Machaneh Ephraim came into my hands, and in Hilchos Zechiyah, siman 6, I found that he raises this issue and distinguishes that an item intended for consumption may be considered a detriment to the purchaser, because people do not ordinarily wish to increase their expenses.
