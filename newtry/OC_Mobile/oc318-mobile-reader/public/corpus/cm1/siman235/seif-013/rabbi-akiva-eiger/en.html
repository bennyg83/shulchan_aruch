[Shulchan Aruch] He can no longer protest. Note: See Responsa Rivash, siman 71, who agreed with the Rambam's teachers that he may protest. Maharachash, Responsa part 1, siman 63, writes that land remains in the possession of its owner, and he may say, "I follow the view of the Rambam's teachers." Even if he held possession for three years after reaching age twenty, and claimed, "I later repurchased it from you after I turned twenty," the matter depends upon the dispute among the authorities concerning a mortgage, discussed above in siman 103, seif 3, and he may likewise say, "I follow the authorities who rule in my favor." See there. If there is uncertainty whether he was twenty when he sold, one may say that with witnesses in our times the rule is not that a person under twenty cannot sell, and we do not apply to such witnesses the presumption that witnesses do not sign unless they know the facts. See Responsa Rashach, part 1, siman 149, and a similar discussion in Beit Yosef, siman 375, in the name of Rabbi Yehudah son of the Rosh.
