Reish) This is the language of the Rambam, halachah 16. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is explicit in the Gemara, Bava Batra 154a, that the heirs are not heeded when their request would disgrace the deceased's body. Moreover, physical signs are liable to change. Some explain that the relevant signs can fall out.
Shin) Rambam likewise rules this there, based on the Gemara on 155a.
