There is a judgment:
(a) And then, there was a lot of money
There according to Rambam's view. The Maggid Mishneh writes that perhaps Rambam learned this law from the law of a girl betrothed while a minor who has the right of refusal: once she has matured for even one moment she can no longer protest, as explained in Even HaEzer siman 155. Although that case concerns prohibition and this one monetary law, both depend upon the legal effect of silence. Likewise concerning support for a daughter, they said that if she failed to protest she forfeited her right, as explained there in siman 93.
