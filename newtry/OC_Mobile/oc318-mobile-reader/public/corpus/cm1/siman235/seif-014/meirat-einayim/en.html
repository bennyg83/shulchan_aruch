He can no longer protest, etc. The Rambam, as quoted by the Tur, writes that once he received the money and the purchaser used the land in his presence after he reached twenty and he did not protest, the purchaser's title is confirmed because the seller showed that he accepted his own sale. This implies that both elements are required: he received the full value and, after reaching the requisite age, permitted the purchaser to use the land in his presence without protest.
