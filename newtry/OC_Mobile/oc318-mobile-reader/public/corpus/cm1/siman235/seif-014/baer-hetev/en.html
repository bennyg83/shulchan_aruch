The Tur writes in the name of the Rambam: Since the minor accepted the money and the purchaser used this land in his presence after he reached age twenty and he did not protest, the property becomes established in the purchaser's hands because the former minor demonstrated consent to his sale. This implies that both elements are required: he received payment equal to the property's value, and the purchaser used the property in his presence after he became an adult while he remained silent. Even in such a case some disagree and hold that he may still remove the purchaser, as the Rambam cites in the name of his teachers and the Tur records. Sema.
