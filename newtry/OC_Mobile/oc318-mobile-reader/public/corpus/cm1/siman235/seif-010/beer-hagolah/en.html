The language of Rambam, law 14.
. .
The Maggid Mishneh writes that although the Gemara, Gittin 59a, says only generally that his gift is valid, the same law is learned for the gifts of pe'utot with movable property, as explained above in seif 1, because the gift of a healthy person and that of a dying person are equivalent in this respect. Ibn Migash explains that this applies even if the minor does not understand business dealings. He further explains that whether the property came from his father or is his own property, his gift is valid.
