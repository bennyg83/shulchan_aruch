Rama rules according to the intermediate view, neither as stringent as they are nor as lenient as those who say that once a thirteen year old boy with signs of maturity is sharp he may sell even inherited land. According to the Rosh, property he acquired himself may be sold after thirteen and signs of maturity even if he is not especially sharp, provided he is not mentally incompetent.
