Kaf) There, halachah 4, from the statement of Rav Yonah in the name of Rabbi Zeira: up to one sixth, a minor is treated like an adult, Gittin 59a.
Lamed) There. The Raavad objects that a minor cannot waive rights, as we say that orphans are not legally capable of waiver. The Maggid Mishneh responds that the Gemara does not imply this distinction; had it been so, the Gemara should have stated it. Although orphans ordinarily are not capable of waiver, the Sages who enacted that a minor's sales are valid for his livelihood likewise enacted that his transactional error is treated like that of an adult.
