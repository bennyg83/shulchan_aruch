[Shulchan Aruch] But with land he may not sell. Note: If a minor sold or gave land and movable property together, his legal status requires analysis. Perhaps since the land is not acquired, the movable property is likewise not acquired. See Mordechai, chapter Mi Shemet, responsum of Rabbeinu Gershom Meor HaGolah; Maharam Alshich, siman 101; and Kerem Shlomo, Choshen Mishpat, simanim 65 and 66. Mishneh LaMelech.
