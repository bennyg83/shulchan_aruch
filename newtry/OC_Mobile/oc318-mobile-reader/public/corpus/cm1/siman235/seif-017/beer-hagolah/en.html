The language of Rambam, chapter 29 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 2, from the Mishnah in chapter HaNizakin, Gittin 59a: a deaf-mute communicates by gestures and others communicate to him by gestures, etc., following the first Tanna and Rashi's explanation there.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that he is no worse than one who became mute with respect to gittin, as stated in Mi SheAchazo, Gittin 67b. Yet it is surprising why Rambam did not equate one who speaks but does not hear with one who hears but does not speak. This requires study.
