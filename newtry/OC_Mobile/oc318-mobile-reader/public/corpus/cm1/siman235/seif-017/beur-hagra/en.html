Or one who speaks, etc. The Rambam understands the Mishnah’s term cheresh broadly enough to include one who speaks but does not hear, while the cheresh grouped with a shoteh is specifically one who neither hears nor speaks, as stated at the beginning of Chagigah. See Rashi there. This explains the Gemara’s question in Chagigah and Gittin asking how we know that “one who speaks, etc.” differs. The practical distinction concerns a deaf person not grouped with a shoteh. The Gemara does not simply say that all deaf persons have the same law, because only one who neither hears nor speaks is fully comparable to a shoteh.
