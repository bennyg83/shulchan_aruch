[Shulchan Aruch] With movable property it is valid. Note: The Rosh and the Tur, in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel, hold that even if he has a guardian, his gift is valid. See Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 101, who writes that with our gifts, since they take effect only after death, and at the moment the gift takes effect he no longer has a guardian, according to all views the recipient acquires. Mishneh LaMelech writes that Rabbi Yosef HaLevi likewise ruled so. See Maharitatz, siman 8, who disagrees, and Toras Chaim, siman 99, which follows Maharitatz. See also Responsa Tashbetz, part 4, second section, siman 3.
