Kuf) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is an explicit statement in the first chapter of Bava Metzia 10b.
Reish) The ruling and its reason are likewise explained there: a minor girl's courtyard functions as her hand.
This also appears in Rambam. The Sema explains that since the Torah expanded a minor girl's ability to acquire through her courtyard like an adult, the Sages likewise allowed her acquisition through kinyan sudar. In the versions of the Tur before us, however, the text reads that a male minor who acquired movable property and a kinyan was made from them, etc., while regarding a minor girl, etc., and a kinyan was made from them, etc. In my humble opinion this requires study, as does the Bach's discussion there. In any event, he writes that Rambam agrees with the Raavad where others transfer real property to the minor through a sale, etc. This is not the place to elaborate.
