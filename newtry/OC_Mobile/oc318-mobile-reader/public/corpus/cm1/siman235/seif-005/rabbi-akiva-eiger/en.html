[Sema, letter 17] For it contains a lien upon land. Note: See the Ran, Ketubot chapter 4, who writes in the name of the Rashba that a lien upon land is treated as land in this respect. Thus, if a minor holds a promissory note against orphans, before the Sages enacted collection from the movable property of orphans he cannot sell or give the note to another, since collection is available only from land and the note is therefore treated as land. Hagahot Ashri, chapter 7 of Ketubot, and Mordechai there, however, hold that for this purpose it has the law of movable property. Tosafot, Ketubot 86a, s.v. "she should go," plainly follow Hagahot Ashri.
