If he said, "This house." Ritva says the landlord must initially provide the specified house in usable condition, but if it later falls he need not replace it. This appears inconsistent with the Shulchan Aruch in siman 312, following Rosh, that if the house still stands but becomes dangerous the landlord must repair it. A broken lock or bolt certainly leaves the house existing and repairable. Ritva may disagree with Rosh, but since the Shulchan Aruch follows Rosh and Rama does not dispute that ruling, Rama's citation here is difficult and requires study.
