The wording of Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 3. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Mishnah and baraita in Bava Metzia 101a.
He wrote, "And P. N. P. N. P. P. P. P. P.N. is a kind of orphan who is bombarding the door and understanding it as a ecclesiastic man."
This is the correct text, and so appears in the Nimukei Yosef. He explains that since the landlord told him it was for residence and it is unfit for residence without these things, it is as though he expressly stipulated them.
