And he raises, etc., or. The Gemara's objection, 'But he can look,' and its failure to prescribe a vertical measure show that no vertical distance is required at all.
(Supplement) He built, etc. The Gemara did not answer that the wall is angled because that answer was needed only under the initial assumption that the mishna did not discuss a side placement. Since no vertical measure was stated, no such measure is required. Once the Gemara establishes that the mishna does discuss a side placement, as Tosafot explain, no angled construction need be assumed. (End of supplement.)
