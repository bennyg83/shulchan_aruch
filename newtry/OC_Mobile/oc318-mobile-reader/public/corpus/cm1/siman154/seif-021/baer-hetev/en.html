Courtyard. The Sema writes that this is not literal. It also applies where one opened a window toward the courtyard of his fellow, whose courtyard predated the window, and he acquired a chazakah in it, as explained above. See there. (The Taz explains the matter differently. See there.)
And to raise it. The Maggid Mishneh writes that some say that four amot suffice only where the wall was not distanced beyond the required measure except by a small amount. But if it was placed at a great distance, then certainly one could see from there when the wall is raised only four amot above the other person's window. Accordingly, the tanna of the baraita means that it must be raised sufficiently so that one cannot peer over and see. Sema.
