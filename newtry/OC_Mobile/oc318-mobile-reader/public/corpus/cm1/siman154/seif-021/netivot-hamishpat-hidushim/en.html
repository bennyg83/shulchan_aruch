A window in his wall. The case requires explanation. If the adjacent area had been ownerless, the closing statement that he had acquired chazakah in this damage would be difficult, because at the outset there had been no one to damage. One also cannot say it was his fellow's ruin or the public domain for the same reason. Nor can it mean that the courtyard already existed, because the Mechaber says, "and he made a courtyard beside it," implying that previously there was no courtyard. Therefore "made a courtyard" is not literal. The case includes even where a courtyard already existed and the window holder established chazakah. The Mechaber's words mean: one who had a window lawfully in his wall, however that lawful state arose, and later the other came to challenge it because he made use of the adjacent courtyard, and so forth.
