With him. See the Sema, who writes that here too the case applies even if the window has already been held for several years, and that the Rashba likewise writes so. This is difficult, for the Mechaber already ruled in seif 18 that even a purchaser may not build in his courtyard in front of Reuven's window once Reuven has established a right to that window. All the more so he should not be able to compel him to seal it. See there for the distinction the Sema makes. See also Even HaEzer regarding this matter, namely that seif 18 concerns a Jew who lawfully opened a window onto his roof. The Bach concludes that the Rashba disagrees with what is written in seif 18. Shach.
