However. The Sema writes that it requires study what the Rama adds here, for the Mechaber already wrote the same at the beginning of seif 3 and in seif 6, which likewise deal with a window that causes visual intrusion. Perhaps the Rama teaches that the owner of the window himself must seal it, and one should not think that only the owner of the courtyard must build opposite it. See Responsa Mabit, part 1, second collection of questions, siman 23; part 2, siman 96; Responsa Maharashdam, siman 254; Responsa Maharit, simanim 107 and 114; Responsa Rashach, part 2, simanim 98 and 208; and Responsa Mahari LeBeit Levi, siman 2, pages 9 and 13.
