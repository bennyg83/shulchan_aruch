The Maggid Mishneh writes that he already explained Rambam and his teacher's view that visual damage can be established by chazakah when the injured party sees the act done against him and remains silent. According to this approach, the sugya and Mishnah in Chezkas HaBatim, 58b, are read literally as concerning a window that causes visual damage. Rabbi Zeira and Rabbi Ila'a dispute a small Egyptian window into which a person's head cannot fit. But a larger Tyrian window, into which a person's head can fit, establishes chazakah even when higher than four cubits. This is the approach of Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash.
Meaning, each authority according to his own view: according to Rashbam, immediately. Tur likewise writes in the name of Ramban and Ramah, and this is the view of the Geonim. According to Rabbenu Tam and Rosh, only after three years of chazakah.
