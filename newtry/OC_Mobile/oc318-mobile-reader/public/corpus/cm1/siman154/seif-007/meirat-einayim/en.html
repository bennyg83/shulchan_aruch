And the courtyard owner waived it, etc. Rambam and the Mechaber state the cases progressively. Not only does an express waiver establish chazakah; even assisting him does so, and even without assistance, if it is known that he knew of the matter and remained silent, chazakah arises. Rosh and those with him, who require three years, apply that requirement where he merely knew and remained silent, and likewise where he assisted, as the Tur and Mechaber write in his name at the end of siman 142. But even Rosh agrees that an express waiver establishes chazakah immediately, as explained in siman 155:36.
Since he has already established chazakah in this window. This is according to each view: immediately according to Rambam, and only after three years according to Rosh, as Rama concludes at the end of this gloss. The point here is only that chazakah can help with visual damage.
It is prohibited to stand at the window, etc. See what I wrote just above in Sema, seif katan 14, so that these statements are not misunderstood as contradictory.
But as long as he has no chazakah, etc. This refers back to the beginning of the gloss, where Rama says this is the proper ruling once chazakah has been established. Yet it requires study what novelty Rama adds here, since the Mechaber already wrote in seifim 3 and 6 that one cannot open such a window. Perhaps he teaches that the window owner himself must close it, and we do not say that the courtyard owner should merely build opposite it.
