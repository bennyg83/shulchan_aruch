(Seif 10, gloss: What kind of window, and so forth). The Tur's wording here is confused. In siman 14 it appears that Rashbam and Rabbeinu Chananel disagree, but in truth there is no dispute. Rashbam himself explains the Mishnah as speaking where there was no other source of light, and on the following page the Gemara defines “a window made for illumination” as one made in a dark place. These are the same criterion. Rashba likewise writes in the responsum cited by Beit Yosef, new section 13, that Rabbeinu Chananel and Rashbam agree. It therefore appears that there is a scribal error, and the text should read: “Rabbeinu Chananel and Rashbam explain that it was made to bring light into a dark place, meaning that there is no other source of light. Ramah writes that even without this, and so forth.” Meaning, Ramah disagrees. Some versions read, “and so too Ramah wrote,” referring to windows that do not provide primary illumination, concerning which possession does not establish a right merely for added light.
