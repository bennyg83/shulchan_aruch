Nimukei Yosef writes that the later authorities agreed. Beit Yosef writes that they learned it from the Tosefta, where the statement is attributed to Rabbi Meir, while the anonymous Tanna of the Mishnah is Rabbi Meir. He explains 'frame' as a lintel above and below, right and left, and 'form of an entrance' as a lintel above with sideposts. From seif katan 5.
