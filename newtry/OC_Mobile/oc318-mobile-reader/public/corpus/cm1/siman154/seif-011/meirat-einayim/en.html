The form of an entrance or a frame. Beit Yosef explains that a frame means a lintel above and below and jambs on the right and left. A 'form of an entrance' means a lintel above with side posts, as we say elsewhere, one post here, one post there, and a beam across the top, but no threshold below. This is from Nimukei Yosef in chapter Chazakat HaBatim. See also the end of siman 214.
