A:
The windows. In the text of Rabbeinu it read 'the windows,' with the definite article, and this appears correct. The practical difference is where these were the principal windows opening into this house, so that closing them entirely deprives the house of light. Even so, he may do so, as appears from Rabbeinu's failure to distinguish. Although below, in seif 26, a distinction is made regarding additional light, there the owner of the house paid the owner of the courtyard an increased price, and that benefit is presumably included in the increase. Here, however, perhaps he sold him the house without a right to light. This appears from the words of the Rosh in the first chapter of Bava Batra. Taz; see there.
