According to Rabbeinu Yonah. Bava Basra 6b requires each side to construct a protective barrier even where one opened first.
According to the Rosh... See Tosafos 17b. Their reasoning is that one may initially act within his own domain even if the other will later be restricted, as with a shop and cattle shed. Even according to Rashi and the Rif, here the public domain is ownerless and the first opener may act. Thus the second party must provide the full required distance. The earlier case of two houses is therefore explained as one in which both openings were made simultaneously.
