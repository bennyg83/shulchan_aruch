According to Ramban, the absence of a prescriptive right means only that when the window eventually causes actual injury it must be sealed. Until then, however, the existing window does confer a right preventing the neighbor from darkening it so long as it causes no injury. Rabbi Akiva Eiger further argues that most poskim disagree with Ramban and hold that a complete prescriptive right is acquired, so that even when injury later arises the window need not be sealed. Ramban's own treatment of the incident of Yonah Pappa also indicates uncertainty on the question, as Beit Yosef notes from Responsa Rivash, siman 322. A doubt in Ramban's position cannot displace the definite view of the majority of poskim. See also below, seif katan 16.
