The public domain is ownerless, etc. One should not ask why Rama below in seif 16 writes generally that where one opens toward the public domain or similar place where no one can protest, no chazakah arises. There he speaks of the neighbor's right to build on his own property even though his construction will darken the existing window. Here the point is different: the person who opened toward the public domain acquired the ability to look through his window into the ownerless public space, such that the person opposite cannot then open a window facing him and look directly into his premises. Rashba's responsum expressly distinguishes these matters. I cite his wording below in seif 16. See also the Derishah, seif 8, where I prove this distinction from other places.
