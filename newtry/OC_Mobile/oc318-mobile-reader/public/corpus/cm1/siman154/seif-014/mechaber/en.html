According to Rabbeinu Yonah, if one person is first to open a window in an upper story opposite his neighbor's upper story, he may open only along half the width of his own upper story, leaving the remaining half for the person opposite. According to the Rosh and Rashba, the neighbor cannot prevent him by saying, 'Today or tomorrow I will open a window, and you will prevent me,' because the public domain is ownerless and the first person to open a window is like one who acquires ownerless property.
