Ownerless property. One should not challenge this from the Rama in seif 16, who writes that one who opens toward the public domain does not acquire a chazakah according to anyone. There the issue is that his fellow may build on his own property even though his building will thereby darken the window that was already opened. Here, however, it says that he acquired from ownerless property the right of viewing through his window, in the sense that the person opposite him may not likewise open a window and look through it. This distinction is stated explicitly in Responsa Rashba. Ibid.
