Rashba writes in a responsum and brings proof from the last chapter of HaGozel, Bava Kamma 112a, concerning the son-in-law of Rabbi Yirmiyah who locked the gates, etc., until the Gemara distinguishes that there the property had not been established in his father's possession. The same reasoning applies here, because he is presently causing damage to the other party. From section 27.
This is analogous to one who sells a courtyard to a gentile adjacent to his fellow's courtyard, as in siman 175, seif 40.
