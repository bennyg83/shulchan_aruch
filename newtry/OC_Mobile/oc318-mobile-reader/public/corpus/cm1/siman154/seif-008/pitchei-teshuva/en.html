Nevertheless, etc., because this is a constant, permanent injury. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot Tur, letter 29, discusses neighbors Reuven and Shimon along a public domain. Shimon projected a new structure into the public domain in front of Reuven's window. Reuven objected that the barrier would prevent him from seeing down the length of the marketplace from his house. The ruling was for Shimon. Reuven's claim is legally irrelevant: the Sages recognized injury from being observed, not a right to preserve one's view. See Responsa Ralnach, siman 44, and Tosafot to Bava Batra 7a.
