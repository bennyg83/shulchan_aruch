[Shulchan Aruch] Since he does not yet cause him damage. Note: It appears to me that this applies specifically insofar as, if the other later builds in his courtyard and suffers damage, the first person must then close the opening. This is also precise in the language of the Rivash, siman 322: a chazakah established while no one was being harmed does not help after the injured party comes to be present, as appears to be Ramban's view in the chapter Lo Yachpor. However, the chazakah established while he was not causing damage is effective to this extent, that the other may not build and block his light. This is the subject of Responsa Rivash, end of siman 471, whose words there are taken from Ramban's novellae on the sugya of the Tyrian window. This resolves the objection of Rabbi Aharon Sasson. Examine this carefully.
