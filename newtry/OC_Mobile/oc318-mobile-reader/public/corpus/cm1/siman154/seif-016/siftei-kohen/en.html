The view of the Rosh is that one who opens a window, etc. See the responsa of Maharit, end of siman 132; Rabbi Shlomo Kohen, book II, simanim 62 and 98, folio 66b, and siman 132; and Maharashdam, simanim 242 and 257.
Nevertheless, the Ramban wrote, etc. See the responsa of Maharan Sasson, who challenges this from a responsum of the Rivash that does not cite the Ramban in this manner. See simanim 87 and 88, and further siman 192.
