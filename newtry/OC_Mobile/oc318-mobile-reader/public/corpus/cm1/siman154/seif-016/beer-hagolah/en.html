According to Rava he may place it, even though we rule that coercion applies against the trait of Sodom, because if the neighbor later builds, he immediately suffers direct damage from the window owner's arrows until he brings the other party to judgment, etc. Beit Yosef cites this in seif 16.
In a responsum he brings proof from the Yerushalmi's rule that ordinary ruins are expected to be rebuilt. There is also support from the beginning of Lo Yachpor, Bava Batra 17b, where the Gemara challenges Rava's ruling concerning one who comes to place something near the boundary.
