Who wished to raise it, etc., at his own expense. This is the correct text. See below siman 157:9, where the Tur and Mechaber write that if one wishes at his own expense to build the shared wall higher than four amot, his fellow may object. The reason, as explained there, is that the other can say, 'I use my half of the wall's thickness.' Here, by contrast, the case concerns one who builds and raises only upon his own half of the wall's thickness, which is permitted, as Rama also writes there.
