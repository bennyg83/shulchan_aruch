To demolish. Meaning, he may demolish it for his own need and say to his fellow: After I demolish my wall, we will build another wall at the expense of both of us in order to remove the damage caused by overlooking. One should not challenge this from what the Rama writes at the end of seif 16, concerning Reuven who had windows in his house, that he must seal his windows even though he made them permissibly. You might say that if there, where the wall fell, he must seal his windows and remove his damage, then certainly here he should not be permitted to demolish his wall initially. The distinction is that there, once the wall has fallen, if he looks from his window he becomes the one causing damage to the owner of the garden, while the owner of the garden causes him no damage. Here, however, both are equally subject to the damage. Ibid.
