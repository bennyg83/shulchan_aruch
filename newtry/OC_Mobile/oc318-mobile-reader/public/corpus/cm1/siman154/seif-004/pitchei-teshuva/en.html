Although in a courtyard one need not be particular, etc. See Sema, who explains that the Gemara cited there is different because the additional opening would create regular passage and fixed use of the courtyard from a permanent house. Responsa Avodat HaGershuni, siman 26, initially considered distinguishing an animal shed, whose use is less constant than that of a house or upper story, and allowing it to be divided with a new opening. He ultimately rejects that distinction: since the shed itself is a fixed structure, one may not create a new opening into the courtyard even if its use is not as frequent as that of a residence.
