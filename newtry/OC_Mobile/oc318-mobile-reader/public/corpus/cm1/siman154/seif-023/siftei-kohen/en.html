And the window is within the four cubits. “Within” does not mean precisely centered. It means that there must be four cubits between the two walls, together with the width of the window, which is included in the required four-cubit distance. The Sema explains that “within” means included within the four cubits. The Rivash, siman 225, writes likewise in the names of the Ritva and Ramah. This is the correct ruling.
