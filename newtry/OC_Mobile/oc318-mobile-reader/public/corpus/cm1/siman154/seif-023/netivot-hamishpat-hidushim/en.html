And in the middle of the d’ah, that is to say, ‘I will be with the width of the window, and I will die.’
Unless he distances the roofing. Therefore with our tiled roofs, where the lower edge slopes outward and projects a cubit or more beyond the wall, if Reuven wishes to build opposite Shimon's window he must leave four cubits from the end of Reuven's roof projection to the window. The roofing itself must also be distanced from the window. But he need not distance from the end of Shimon's own roof, because Reuven has no obligation merely because Shimon roofed over the area above his own window.
