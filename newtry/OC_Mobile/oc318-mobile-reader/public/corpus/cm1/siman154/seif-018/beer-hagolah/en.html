As above, siman 149, seif 9. Although regarding gentile documents we say that one deriving title from a gentile has the gentile's legal status, as explained above in siman 45, seif 7, and siman 66, seif 25, and there in Rama's gloss, in my humble opinion the Geonim and Rashba distinguish because those cases concern monetary law, where dina d'malkhuta dina applies. Visual damage also involves prohibition, as explained in seif 7 in the gloss, and dina d'malkhuta dina does not override a matter of prohibition.
