Reuven... Meaning he opened and acquired the right lawfully.
For we do not find... This refers to the case of chazakah and follows Baal HaItur, cited in Teshuvos HaRamban, who explains that one who comes through a non-Jew has the non-Jew's legal status, as stated in Bava Kama 113a.
Collected note: Reuven... for we do not... Hagahos Ashri, chapter 3, siman 24.
Collected note: For we do not find... Mordechai and Hagahos Ashri likewise rule so. See seif 19.
Some say. This follows the plain meaning of the Gemara, and there are also instances where one who comes through a non-Jew receives an advantage, as in Bechoros 11b. The same approach is followed in simanim 45, 56, and 57. See Sema, seif katan 44.
All the more so in a place... Bava Basra 55a.
He must... Bava Basra 54-56.
And he acquired. Meaning he acquired the right to prevent the neighbor from building opposite the window, because he acquired the airspace.
All this concerns... and no chazakah... This gloss properly belongs to seif 19, where the purchaser from the non-Jew seeks to close the Jew's windows. In the present case the purchaser merely seeks to build opposite them, and even if the windows predated the acquisition, one who acquired ownerless property may build opposite them, as stated in seifim 16 and 31.
