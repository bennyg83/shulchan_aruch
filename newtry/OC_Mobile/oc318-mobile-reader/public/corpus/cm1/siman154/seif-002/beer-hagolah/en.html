The Maggid Mishneh writes this there to explain Rambam and resolve Raavad's objection: in such a case one may leave his own house wide open, and blessing will come upon him.
He explains there that although we rule like Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov, who says one may tell another, 'I enter my friend's property,' Nedarim 47a, that applies to a courtyard, because people do not object to ordinary passage through a courtyard. They do object to passage through a house.
