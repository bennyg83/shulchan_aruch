The Mechaber learned this law from Tur there, seif 1, in the name of Responsa Rosh, rule 99, siman 7. From the question it appears that the custom of the city was that everyone extended projections and balconies and others could not protest. In such a case the open air is not ownerless, as explained in seif 16. See Responsa Rashba simanim 1085, 1132, 903, and Rivash siman 313.
