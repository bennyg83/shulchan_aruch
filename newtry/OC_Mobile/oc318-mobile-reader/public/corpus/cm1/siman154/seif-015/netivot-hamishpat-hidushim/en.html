Where a person may not extend projections. The Mechaber's language is compressed. In seif 14 he discusses windows, while here he discusses projections. There the issue is opening opposite the other side, while here his conclusion speaks of one who opened a window facing the space in front of an upper story, implying that the projection and window concern the neighbor living beside him. It therefore appears that the end of seif 14, which states according to the Rosh that one is like a person acquiring from ownerless property, applies both to opening opposite an upper story across the way and to a projection extended by the adjacent neighbor. The Mechaber then qualifies this here: that applies only where people are not permitted to extend projections. If the first person opened unlawfully, the second may likewise extend his projection and darken the first window, or may open there too, until they reach a compromise. Sema.
