Even if he built an upper story. Meaning, even if the upper story was already built above his house and opened into the house, he may not later open a doorway from it into the shared courtyard. The Mechaber then writes that he may even build the upper story anew, provided he does not open it into the courtyard. The first part follows the Rambam's wording. The clause beginning, "but he may build," is not the Rambam's own language, but the Beit Yosef inferred it from the Rif and wrote that the Rambam agrees. Ramban states the same in his novellae.
