The language of Rambam, Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 5, law 8, from Mishnah 2, 59b, as the Gemara explains on 60a.
Kesef Mishneh writes that Rambam explains like Rif, who wrote in a responsum that he may open the entrances into his own house but not into the partners' courtyard, as the Mechaber writes at the end of the seif. Ramban likewise writes this.
The Maggid Mishneh and Kesef Mishneh write that Rambam's interpretation of the sugya follows Rif: he may build the upper story so that it opens into his own house. The reason is that when the upper story opens only into his house, it is not generally intended for separate residents, since access passes through his own dwelling. Ramban likewise writes this, as does Nimukei Yosef in their names. Ritva writes that reason supports it.
This is explained above in seif katan 2. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Ramban and Rashba already explained the sugya according to Rif, and the Tosefta supports him. Nimukei Yosef likewise writes this in the name of Ritva.
Kesef Mishneh writes this there in the name of a responsum of Rif and states that this is Rambam's view. Although a person does not ordinarily bring separate residents through his own house, as stated regarding an upper story, it is possible that where an opening is made into the courtyard, those residents will use it as a shortcut through the partners' courtyard. Tosafot likewise explain that it is forbidden so that people should not enter from it into the partners' courtyard.
