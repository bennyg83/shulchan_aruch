To make. The reason is that through one large opening the view has greater command than through two openings, even if the separation between them is small. Alternatively, use of one large opening is more frequent, since large loads can be carried through it, requiring the neighbor to preserve greater privacy. So writes the Sema. The Taz rejects his explanation and writes that this ruling is surprising to him and is not stated in the Gemara or Rif. They state only that one may not replace two openings of four cubits each with one opening of eight cubits, and there another reason applies; see there.
