A person may not open a window toward his fellow's courtyard. See Beit Yosef at the end of column 14 concerning one who sets his building back within his own property, whether a distance of four cubits suffices. See the lengthy discussion in Mordechai at the beginning of Bava Batra. Netivot HaMishpat, at the beginning of siman 153, likewise states plainly that one may not make a window even in a wall recessed within his own property facing his fellow's courtyard unless there is a barrier in front of it. See there, and below in gloss 3 and siman 155:21 in the gloss.
