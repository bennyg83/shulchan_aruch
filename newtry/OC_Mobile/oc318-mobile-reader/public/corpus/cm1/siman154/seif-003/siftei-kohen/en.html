Some authorities say, etc. See the responsa of the Rama, siman 32.
An opening toward the public domain, etc. This means that he causes an injury of a type not caused by the public, such as where the opening is higher than four cubits or a similar case. This is explicit in the Darkei Moshe. On this basis he resolves the Beit Yosef’s objection to the Nimukei Yosef in the name of the Baal HaIttur. The Bach gives the same answer. The Baal HaIttur, folio 90, column 4, likewise states this explicitly.
