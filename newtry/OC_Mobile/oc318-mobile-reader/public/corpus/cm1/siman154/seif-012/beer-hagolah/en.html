The language of Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot Shekhenim, law 7. Maggid Mishneh cites Rav Nachman's statement at the beginning of Bava Batra 60a that immediate closure creates chazakah, etc. Rambam understands "closure" to include building opposite the opening; Rashbam likewise explains it this way.
This is the text in Tur, seif 15.
