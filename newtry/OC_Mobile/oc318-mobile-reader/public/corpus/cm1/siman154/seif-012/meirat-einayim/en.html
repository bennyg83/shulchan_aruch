And it is immediately considered a waiver. Even the Rosh and those who follow his general view agree here. The Mechaber gives the reason: a person is not likely to allow his light to be blocked before him and remain silent unless he has waived the right. Although the Rambam uses the same rationale elsewhere and generally holds that other nuisance easements may also arise immediately, there this is only where we know the affected person became aware of the interference and remained silent, as the Mechaber states in seif 7. In the case of blocking a window, the novelty is that awareness is presumed. There is a strong presumption that a person notices when his light is being blocked. His silence therefore proves waiver.
