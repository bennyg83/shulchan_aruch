In contrast, if they merely appraised an increase in the value of the house because of the courtyard, the increase is likewise attributable to the air and light, contrary to the dissenting view in the Tur. See Beit Yosef. See also below, siman 172, seif 2, and siman 173, seif 3, that the same applies if the owner of the courtyard paid money to the homeowner on account of some increase in value. In that case too he has rights with respect to the windows, as the Beit Yosef writes there.
