Tur, seif 23, in the name of Responsa Rosh. The Gemara likewise says in Chezkas HaBatim, 60b, in Rav Huna's statement that if it fell, he may rebuild it. Responsa attributed to Ramban, siman 17, likewise writes this and distinguishes it from one who had established a right to place beams in his neighbor's wall. If that wall fell and was rebuilt, he has no chazakah in the new wall. The responsum gives a sound reason for the distinction.
