heh) The language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 5, and so writes the Rif, and so appears in the Gemara. However, insofar as the Rambam writes there that the same law applies to all animals, we do not rule like him. Rashi explains that the ruling applies even on a day that is not a market day.
