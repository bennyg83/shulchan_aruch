The person who caused bodily injury must appease the victim and obtain forgiveness. It contains two seifim.
Even after paying all five categories of compensation, one who injures another is not forgiven until he asks the victim to forgive him. The victim may not be cruel and refuse forgiveness, for that is not the way of the descendants of Israel. Once the tortfeasor asks once and again, and it is clear that he has repented and regrets his wrongdoing, the victim should forgive him. One who forgives promptly is praiseworthy and earns the approval of the Sages. Rama: See Orach Chaim, siman 606. It is forbidden to ask Heaven to punish another who wronged him where an earthly court is available, and one who cries out against another may be punished first. Some say that even where no earthly court is available, he may not cry out to Heaven unless he first warns the wrongdoer.
