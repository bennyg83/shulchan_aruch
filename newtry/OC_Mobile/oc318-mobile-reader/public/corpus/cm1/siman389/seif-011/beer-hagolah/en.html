gimel) The language of the Rambam there.
dalet) The Maggid Mishneh explains that the Rambam means that if an animal belonging to an outsider, not one of the partners, enters and causes damage there by tooth or foot, the courtyard is treated entirely as the injured party's domain with respect to that outsider. This is explained at the beginning of chapter HaParah, at the end of 46a. The Rama writes it in seif 16.
