yud) Ibid., law 5, from the Mishnah there and as explained in the Gemara on 9b. The meaning is that an animal becoming forewarned for one form of damage does not make it forewarned for another, for if it became forewarned to gore it did not thereby become forewarned to bite. So too the Gemara says regarding five forms of forewarning on 16b.
kaf) Ibid., in the Mishnah.
lamed) Ibid., chapter 1 of Hilkhot Nizkei Mamon, law 5. The distinction concerning large utensils is explained in seif 7 there.
mem) Mishnah there on 19b.
nun) Mishnah there on 17a.
