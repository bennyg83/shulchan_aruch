chet) Tur, seif 6.
tet) This is learned from Ravina's case, concerning which the Rosh writes that although the damaging ox entered with permission, the area is still called the injured party's courtyard because it is designated for only one party's produce.
yud) This is straightforward, since it is designated for his oxen and not for the other's.
kaf) This is explained in what was written above.
