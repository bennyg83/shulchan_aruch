And foot. Whenever an animal damages produce, the category is called tooth and foot based on the verse, "He shall send forth his animal." Whenever an animal damages another animal, it is called horn based on the verse, "If a man's ox gores his fellow's ox." The Mechaber's wording is therefore precise. "It damaged by tooth and foot" means that it damaged produce. "By horn he is exempt" means that the owner's animal damaged the produce-owner's animal that had been brought in without permission. At the end, "by tooth and foot he is exempt" refers to a case where both parties deviated: the produce owner brought in an animal, the ox owner brought in produce, and the animal damaged the produce.
