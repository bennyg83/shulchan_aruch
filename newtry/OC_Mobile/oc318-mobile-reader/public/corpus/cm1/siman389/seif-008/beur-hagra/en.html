A.D. if they are. Despite this:
(Likkut) By goring or, etc. This is the meaning of the statement concerning the wolf, etc., 'these are, etc.' See Rashi there, s.v. these are.
And even, etc. In this case even Rabbi Eliezer agrees, as stated there.
Oh, and I'm like that. A.D. (S.A.A.) and A. “Ah, O Lord, and the serpent is.”
(Likkut) And some say, etc. See Tosafos, daf 16a, s.v. "and the snake," etc. End.
To maul. Meaning to eat, whereas 'to seize' means to kill and leave the prey. See the Gemara there and Rosh.
And a wolf, etc. As stated in Bava Metzia 93b: if a wolf came and seized, or a lion came and mauled. See Tosafot there, s.v. and the snake. Rashba rules likewise. Rosh writes that every wild animal has the law of a wolf; only a lion, which is king among animals, differs. Rambam rules against Shmuel because the Gemara raises numerous objections against him and all the answers are strained. Rif also omits his view. This is why the text says, 'five, etc., by goring, etc., and the like.'
