peh) The language of the Rambam, law 6. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the Mishnah on 15b, following the first Tanna; the snake is likewise explicitly mentioned in the Mishnah. If any of these animals causes damage in the manner of tooth or foot, its law is that of tooth or foot. If it causes damage in the manner of horn, namely one of the five acts listed above, its law is that of horn regarding the distinction between domains, as explained below in simanim 390 and 391, except that these animals are deemed forewarned from the outset.
The foreign-language term means a polecat.
