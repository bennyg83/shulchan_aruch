If Reuven brought his produce into Shimon's courtyard without permission, and Levi's ox entered and ate it, Levi is exempt. Rama: If Reuven brought the produce in with permission and Levi's ox entered without permission, Levi is liable, because the courtyard is then comparable to a courtyard jointly used by the permitted occupant and the owner, into which an outsider's ox entered and caused damage.
