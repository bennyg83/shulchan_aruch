Rambam there. The Maggid Mishneh cites Rabbi Elazar's statement in chapter 1, 16a. There are differing versions. Rambam rules according to the latter version, which is primary. The Maggid Mishneh wonders why it is absent from the Halakhot. Kesef Mishneh writes that Rambam and the Maggid Mishneh may have called what our books label the first version the latter version. The Maggid Mishneh's statement that it is absent from the Halakhot may reflect a text in which the latter version did not distinguish between small and large utensils. In our books the anonymous Gemara follows the latter version.
