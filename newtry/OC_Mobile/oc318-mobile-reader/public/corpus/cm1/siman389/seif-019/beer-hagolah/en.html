(b) There is a page named B
Explained there in the Mishnah, 15b, and Gemara, 16a, following Rosh, who does not distinguish between small and large utensils. He understands the Mishnah and baraita in their plain form and follows the latter version of Rabbi Elazar. For this reason Rif omitted the distinction. See seif 7 and what I wrote there.
