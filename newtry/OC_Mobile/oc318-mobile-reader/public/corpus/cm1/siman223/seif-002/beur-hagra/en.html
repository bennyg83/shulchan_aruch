(Supplement) Some say they divide. Maggid Mishneh and Bach explain that Rambam understands the Gemara's attribution to Sumchus as retracting the first limitation to an animal in a marsh, so the rule applies even where one party is in possession. This avoids Tosafot's questions. On this view the Sages disagree only where one party possesses the property, but in a marsh they agree with Sumchus. The Gemara's question about maintaining the prior possession belonged only to the initial assumption that both parties claimed certainty. Its later attribution to Sumchus was intended to establish the Mishna even in a case of possession, in accordance with its plain wording. (End of supplement.)
