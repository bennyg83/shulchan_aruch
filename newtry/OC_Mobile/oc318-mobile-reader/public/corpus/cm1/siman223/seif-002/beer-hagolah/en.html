A columnist and wise men named Damer, and if he did not bring Okinawan in the possession of Maria:
Rambam there, law 11. The Maggid Mishneh writes that perhaps Rambam holds that the Gemara establishes the case according to Sumchus only when the animal stands in the seller's domain, but if it stands in a marsh even the Sages agree. This is not the plain implication of the sugya and requires study. The Bach writes that this was also the Rif's reasoning and that this is the primary view.
