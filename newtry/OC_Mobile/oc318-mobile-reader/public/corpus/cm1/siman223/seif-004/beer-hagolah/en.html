There, law 11. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Mishnah there states: if he had two slaves, etc., the seller swears that he sold the smaller one. The Gemara establishes this where the buyer claimed the large slave with his clothing, and the seller admitted the small slave with its clothing; through gilgul shevuah he must therefore swear. But the Mechaber's law concerns a case where the claim is only for the slave and not clothing. Since no Torah oath is taken on slaves, only a shevuat heset applies. Therefore the Maggid Mishneh writes as he does, and this law is compelled by the sugya.
