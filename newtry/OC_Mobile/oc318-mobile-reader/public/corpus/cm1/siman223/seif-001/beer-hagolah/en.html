The language of the Tur, from the Mishnah Bava Metzia 100a. The Gemara establishes the Mishnah, which says that they divide, according to Sumchus, while the Tur rules like the Sages that the burden of proof rests upon the claimant. The Rif and Rosh likewise rule so at the beginning of chapter HaParah, and Rambam writes likewise in chapter 20 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 10.

The language of Rambam, cited by the Tur there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this applies only where the seller makes a definite claim. If the seller himself is uncertain, they divide according to Rambam. The distinction between the offspring of a cow and the offspring of a maidservant is stated in the baraita there, Bava Metzia 100b.
