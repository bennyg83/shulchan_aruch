(Seif 1.) One who exchanges, etc. Rashi explains why the Mishna specifically used the case of exchange here, as the Sema notes. In truth, the same law can also arise in a sale of a cow or donkey, namely where buyer and seller contradict one another, each asserting with certainty that the birth occurred before or after the sale. The present passage, however, does not concern contradictory certain claims, but an uncertainty shared by both parties. Such a case ordinarily arises only in an exchange.
