[Rama] And some say that even the claimant. Note: The Ramban writes similarly in his novellae to Bava Batra 34a. But in Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Metzia, in the sugya of one who exchanges, the Raavad is cited as holding the opposite, that in a definite versus uncertain claim we do not place the property in the possession of the original owner.
