To build beneath the drainpipe. In the Derishah I proved that the same applies beneath a gutter, whether made of wood or stone. The Rif, Rambam, and Rosh permit building beneath it. This differs from the Tur, who distinguishes between wood and stone and questions why the Rosh omitted the Gemara's distinction. In the Derishah I explained the sugya differently, thereby resolving several difficulties that the Beit Yosef left unresolved.
