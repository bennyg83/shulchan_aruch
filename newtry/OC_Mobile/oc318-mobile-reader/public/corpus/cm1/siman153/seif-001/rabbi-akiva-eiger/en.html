[Shach, letter 6] And in the responsum of Maharan. Note: There he wrote regarding a case in which the wall fell and remained that way for many years, that he may return and rebuild it together with the gutter. This applies specifically against the heir to the courtyard. But if the courtyard was sold to another person while the gutter was no longer there, he may not return and install a gutter. See there. In my opinion, this ruling requires study.
