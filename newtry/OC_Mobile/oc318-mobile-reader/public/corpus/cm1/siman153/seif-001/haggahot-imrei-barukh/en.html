(Mechaber, seif 11) And all similar cases, and some permit, etc. Note: See the Bach. In the midst of his discussion he writes that this is only where there is no actual damage preventing use entirely, but merely a loss of convenience so that the place cannot be used as before. If there is real damage making use impossible, even the Ramah and Rashbam agree that the neighbor may prevent him from raising the structure. This is why the Tur carefully speaks of drops falling so frequently that use underneath becomes inconvenient. If use were entirely impossible, it would be obvious that he could prevent it.
