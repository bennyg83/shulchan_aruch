That one hangs. He cannot say, 'I will hang there facing backward and will not look into my neighbor's courtyard,' because we are concerned that he may turn his face and deliberately look. He could then excuse himself by saying that he turned around because he feared falling while standing at the edge of the upper story, and he would not be caught as a thief for looking. Gemara and Rashbam. Sema. See Responsa Maharit, simanim 68 and 108, and Responsa Rashach, part 2, siman 98.
