[Sema, letter 36] The Rambam and those who follow him do not require three years. Note: In my humble opinion, I do not know from where he derived this. Anyone who examines all the Rishonim will see that, with respect to being believed concerning ownership of the wall itself, all opinions require three years and a claim. See Ramban, Maggid Mishneh, and Nimukei Yosef. All of them wrote only of three years together with a claim. The Rama did not state this explicitly because it follows automatically from his formulation that the person says the wall is his, meaning that a claim is required. Once a claim is required, three years are likewise required. This is clear, yet requires study.
