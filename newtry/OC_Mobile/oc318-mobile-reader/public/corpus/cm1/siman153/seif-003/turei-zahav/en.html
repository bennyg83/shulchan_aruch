(Seif 5) He shall remove them entirely. The Sema's question in seif katan 10 from siman 154 appears to have been answered by Rama's wording here. His intent is that in the present case the owner of the courtyard need not trouble himself to sue the other party. Since the damaging object is situated in his courtyard, he may remove it, and the owner of the projection will then be compelled to sue him to prevent its removal. By contrast, in the case of visual intrusion discussed in siman 154, the owner of the courtyard would have to sue the owner of the window and demand that he seal it so that he not cause visual intrusion. The courtyard owner does not wish to build a wall at his own expense opposite the window merely to eliminate the injury. He would therefore be compelled to initiate litigation and trouble himself in beit din.
