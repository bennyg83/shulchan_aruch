Against that new wall. See Sema, seif katan 37, in the gloss, who says that the right is lost unless he previously established a chazakah through a ceiling and plaster. Nimukei Yosef indeed writes this, but Nimukei Yosef follows the opinion of Rabbeinu Yonah that a completed ceiling creates chazakah even in the place occupied by the wall itself. The land remains even if the stones fall. According to that view, Sema's statement is correct. But according to the other opinion cited by Rama in seif 18, that one never acquires the site itself and acquires only rights in the wall stones, then when the stones fall the servitude disappears. Under that opinion there is no distinction between a completed ceiling and an incomplete one, and the matter is straightforward.
