He may not insert into that new wall, etc. Nimukei Yosef writes the same, except that he concludes that this applies unless he previously established chazakah with a ceiling and plaster. The reason is that the original place upon which his right rested has disappeared. This differs from the case of a wall containing a window that fell, discussed in siman 154:9. See what I wrote there in Sema, seif katan 25.
He has only the foundation, etc. One can say that Reuven acquired from Shimon only the amount he needed to widen the foundation, not the area directly above it.
