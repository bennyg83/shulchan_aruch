If Reuven established a right to insert beams into a wall known to belong to another, and the wall fell and its owner rebuilt it, Reuven may not insert beams into the new wall. Accordingly, if the original wall became unstable and Reuven seeks to reinforce and repair it, the owner may prevent him. {Rama: If Reuven built the foundation of his wall two handbreadths into Shimon's courtyard beyond the wall above, and established his claim and possession, he establishes ownership of the space directly above the foundation up to the heavens. Although the upper wall is not as wide, one may say that Reuven recessed it within his own property and now wishes to return the wall to its full boundary. He must swear that he did not encroach upon his neighbor's boundary. It appears to me this applies only where there is no established custom. In a place where foundations are customarily made wider, as in these countries, Reuven owns only the foundation he actually possessed.}
