The Maggid Mishneh writes that according to Rambam and his teacher, waiver of servitude rights takes effect immediately, not through a claim of sale or gift but by tolerance and waiver. He further writes in Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 11, law 4, that according to Rambam and the Geonim, once one sees the damage done against him, knows of it, and remains silent, chazakah is established immediately. The three-year chazakah with a claim of sale or waiver was stated only regarding ownership of the land itself. Ramban appears to agree.
There, seif 26. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes in Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 11, law 4, that this is the view of the French authorities, Rabbenu Yonah, and Rashba, and possibly also Raavad in his glosses.
