And the courtyard owner did not protest immediately. It appears that 'immediately' is not literal. It means immediately from the time the damage became clear to him and he remained silent. Rambam writes this in his Commentary to the Mishnah. The Tur likewise writes in siman 154:16 in Ramban's name that three years are unnecessary; rather, once the damage becomes clear and the injured party remains silent, chazakah arises. Only with blocking another's window did they say that immediate silence creates chazakah, because the harm is ordinarily apparent at once. See Rivash, siman 471. See also below in siman 155:35, where the Mechaber and Rama give the final ruling on these disputes, and siman 154:12 and many other places where Rama records the differing opinions. I set out the general principles in the Sema here, seif katan 16; see there carefully.
The owner of the ledge has thereby established chazakah. Nimukei Yosef at the end of chapter Chazakat HaBatim writes that even according to Rif and those with him, who hold there is no chazakah for visual damage, that is specifically a fixed use from a window into a courtyard. From a roof into a courtyard, and certainly for use of a ledge, chazakah is effective.
And he may use it, etc. The Tur writes this concerning the law of chazakah of a ledge. Accordingly, under Rambam's view the same rule applies immediately when there was no protest.
