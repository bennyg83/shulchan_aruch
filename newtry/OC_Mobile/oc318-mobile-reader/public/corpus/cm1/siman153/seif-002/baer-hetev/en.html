And he left it. It appears that where the projecting beam is less than a handbreadth, the owner of the beam cannot remove it so long as the courtyard owner wants it to remain. By contrast, with a beam of a handbreadth, once the courtyard owner protested, the owner of the beam may also remove it whenever he wishes. This is indicated by the Tur's language in two places, contrary to the Bach's explanation. The Bach's words also do not adequately resolve Tosafot's question. Shach.
