(The Tur writes). In seif 12 the Tur writes, “It follows that I acquired a right in your courtyard to discharge my roof water into it,” and so forth. This is difficult, since in siman 155, seif 28, he rules that when Reuven sold the house to one person and the courtyard to another, the owner of the house must seal the windows. One must say that here the seller retained the relevant property for himself. The Tur wrote this according to the opinion that a seller is not presumed to sell generously.
