And if he did not protest, etc. It appears that where the projecting beam is less than a handbreadth, the owner of the courtyard may remove it whenever he wishes, but the owner of the beam may not remove it so long as the owner of the courtyard desires it to remain. By contrast, where the beam is a handbreadth wide and the courtyard owner protested, the beam owner may also remove it when he wishes. The wording of the Tur in both seif 6 and seif 11, and the wording of the Rosh, indicate this, contrary to the Bach. Examine the matter carefully. The Bach’s own words are also difficult according to the first explanation of Tosafot.
