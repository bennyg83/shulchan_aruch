To prevent. The Sema writes that this applies specifically when the courtyard owner does not claim that he suffers damage from using the place where the water descends, but merely protests because he prefers to receive the water at the present location. In that case we do not listen to the courtyard owner. Underground water flowing from one spring to another can be subject to chazakah. Maharam Galanti, siman 85. See Responsa Mabit, part 2, siman 96.
