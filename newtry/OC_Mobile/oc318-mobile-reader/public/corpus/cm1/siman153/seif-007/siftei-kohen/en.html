To move the drainpipe from this side, etc. The Sema writes that in Derishah he discussed at length whether the Rambam derived this law from his interpretation of the Mishna and the Gemara, namely that the courtyard owner acquires no prescriptive right to prevent the roof owner from shortening the gutter or moving it from one side to another, or whether the Rambam wrote it independently. This explanation has no basis. If so, what did Rav Yehudah in the name of Shmuel add? The matter would already have been explicit in the Mishna. It would also follow that the possessor’s right is weaker regarding a fixed drainage channel than regarding a gutter, so that the courtyard owner could shorten the fixed channel but not the gutter, which is inconceivable. Moreover, Rav Yirmeyah’s answer concerning building would then not correspond to the other answers and the underlying principles would be reversed. The Rambam certainly derived this ruling from reason, not from that reading of the Mishna.
