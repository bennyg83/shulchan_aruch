And concerning a prescriptive right to maintain a ladder, etc. See the Sema, seif katan 31, who concludes that the Rama remained uncertain and did not decide these cases. Accordingly, the rule would be that the stronger party prevails, as explained at the end of siman 139. The Sema further suggests that one may challenge the Rashbam’s view because he does not require three years for placing a ladder or inserting beams. This is not conclusive, since the Rashbam need not hold that position concerning inserted beams. See the Bach. The statement that a window involves visual injury means that reason favors treating a window as a distinct domain. See the responsa of Maharashdam, simanim 261 and 267.
