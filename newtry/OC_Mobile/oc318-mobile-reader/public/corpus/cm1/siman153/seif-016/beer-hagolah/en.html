The Maggid Mishneh cites the statement there on 6a, according to Rav Yosef or the latter version of Rav Nachman, that one who established a right to place small beams established a right to place large beams. He explains this and the related laws according to the Geonim: these are waivers of servitude rights, for which mere silence suffices and neither a claim nor three years of chazakah are needed. The French authorities require a claim of sale or gift and three years of chazakah, and some later authorities agree. Rama writes accordingly in his gloss.
