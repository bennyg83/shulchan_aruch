He claimed. See the Rivash, where you will find that the case concerns two houses standing on opposite sides of the public domain, one belonging to Reuven and the other to Shimon. Reuven rested a beam from his own wall upon Shimon's wall, so that it was evident to everyone that the wall upon which he rested it was not his. This is likewise the Mechaber's intent in writing, 'When is this so? When he admitted,' meaning that he must admit it because we are witnesses to the fact. This is the Sema's language. The Taz writes that the Sema's words are strained and explains the matter differently; see there.
