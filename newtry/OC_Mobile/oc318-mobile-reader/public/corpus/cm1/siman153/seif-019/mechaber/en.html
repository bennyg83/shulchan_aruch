If Reuven's beams are inserted into the wall, while on Shimon's side there are recesses prepared to receive beam ends, Shimon has not thereby established the right to insert beams in those recesses and cannot claim partnership in the wall, because he has never used it. Reuven may say, 'I made these recesses on your side so they would be ready if you later purchased a right from me or asked me and I waived my right. You could then insert the beams without cutting the wall and thereby weakening it.'
