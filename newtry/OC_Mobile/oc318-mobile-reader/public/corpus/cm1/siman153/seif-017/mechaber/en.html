If the beam supported a temporary shelter, Shimon establishes no right during the first thirty days without further proof. Reuven may say, 'I did not waive my right; I allowed you to place it only because it was temporary.' After thirty days, Shimon establishes a right, because it is no longer temporary. If it was a sukkah for the festival, he establishes no right during all seven days of the festival, but does establish one afterward. If he fastened the end of the beam into the wall with mortar, he establishes the right immediately, provided he proves that Reuven assisted him or saw the work and did not protest. See note 80.
