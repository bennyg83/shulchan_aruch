After thirty days he has established the right, etc. This is the Rambam's view and that of those who follow him, namely that three years and a claim are required only to establish title to land itself. Rabbeinu Tam and those who follow him hold that even here three years and a claim are required, and they interpret the Gemara's source for this rule as referring to another matter. In the Derishah I also wrote that the seven days of Sukkot mentioned in the gloss are in addition to the ordinary thirty days required for a sukkah during the rest of the year.
