One who receives, etc.; and likewise the son, etc. This appears in Tosefta chapter 9, whose first clause is cited by Rosh, section 37.
(Supplement) One who receives, etc. The same appears in Bava Metzia 109a: 'That is not a valid claim.' See Rosh.
(Supplement) One who receives, etc. Rosh explains the phrase 'without appreciation,' thereby resolving Rivan's explanation and Tosafot's objection. Tosafot declined this reading because the owner sought only to remove them, but the statement may have been made to forestall resentment, just as the preceding statement was not strictly necessary.
