The reason gamblers are disqualified from testimony is that they do not engage in productive settlement of the world, specifically where gambling is their sole occupation, whether the other party is a gentile or a Jew. All the more so because the Rambam himself rules in chapter 10 of Hilkhot Edut, as explained above in siman 34, seif 16, that a gambler is disqualified only when he has no other occupation. There appears to be support for the Rambam from Shabbat 141b, which says that even on a weekday it is forbidden to stake a large portion against a small one because of gambling. The Kesef Mishneh in chapter 10 of Hilkhot Edut, law 4, discusses at length how to reconcile the Rambam's words, and this is not the place to elaborate.
