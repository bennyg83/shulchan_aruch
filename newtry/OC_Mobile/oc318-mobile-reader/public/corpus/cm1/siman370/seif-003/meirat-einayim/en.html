One who gambles with a gentile does not violate the prohibition of robbery. Although robbery of a gentile is prohibited, that refers to Torah robbery. Here the gentile does not regard the game as theft at all, but intends to transfer the wager to the winner under the agreed conditions. The Sages imposed the additional concern only in transactions between Jews, for the reason explained above.
