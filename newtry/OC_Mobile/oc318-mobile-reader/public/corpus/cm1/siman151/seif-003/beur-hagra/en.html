If he wrote. The Gemara says this is Shmuel's novelty. See Beer HaGolah. Some authorities are cited because the Rambam omitted the rule and apparently held that an assumption of responsibility does not help in a case of robbery. Yet the Rambam omits the law of responsibility in other places as well, prompting Maggid Mishneh's question. Nimukei Yosef distinguishes among types of responsibility, hence the ruling is introduced as one view.
If they testified. The Rambam does not have the textual passage attributed to Rav Bibi. The Rif's language likewise suggests that this was a later addition of a Gaon, as Tosafos questions there. See Bach, siman 205, seif 13.
