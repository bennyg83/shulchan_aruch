Who produced proof by means of a document. Likewise, regarding a husband in his wife's property, or a wife in her husband's property, a document does not suffice. The Tur and Mechaber mentioned this specifically regarding a robber only because there is a dispute in the Gemara concerning a robber, for Rav holds that a document does suffice; they therefore teach us that we do not rule that way. The Sema writes in the name of the Tur: A robber who does not kill people over monetary matters, but is merely established as a robber with respect to this particular field, certainly, just as we say that if he is established as a robber regarding this field he can establish chazakah over another field, because people are not afraid to protest against him except regarding the field for which he is willing to endanger himself, so too they are not afraid to protest, etc. This is unlike one who is a robber in all matters, where even a document containing a warranty clause does not help, etc. See Tumim, where I brought a proof from the Gemara for this opinion, and wrote that Tosafot must also hold this way. See there at length. Similarly, I raised a difficulty against the Mechaber, who follows the Rambam's view and omitted the rule that a warranty clause is effective. There is no difficulty on the Rambam himself, but there is a difficulty on the Mechaber, because below, siman 236, seif 4, he cites a ruling contrary to the Rambam, namely that there is a distinction when a warranty clause was written, etc. See there.
