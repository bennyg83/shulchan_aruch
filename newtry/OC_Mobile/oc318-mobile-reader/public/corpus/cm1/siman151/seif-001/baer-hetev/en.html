The rule that a husband has no chazakah in his wife's property applies where she did not receive payment. If she did receive payment, or if she wrote him a warranty undertaking for the sale, the sale is valid. Nimukei Yosef, chapter Chezkas HaBatim. The Bach writes that this is obvious.
