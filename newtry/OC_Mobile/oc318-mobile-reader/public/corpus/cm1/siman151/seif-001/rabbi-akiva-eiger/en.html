[Shulchan Aruch] Who was established as a robber. Note: See what I wrote in my gloss above, siman 149, seif 13, in the name of the Tur and Maggid Mishneh. Nevertheless, regarding whether we say that an admission is ineffective, one may distinguish that this is specifically when the field remains in the robber's possession. In such a case we say that the owner is afraid of him and might endanger himself over it because it is still in his hands. But if it left the robber's possession and only afterward the owner admitted his title, one may say that the admission is effective. So writes Responsa Raanach, part 1, siman 63. See there, where he cites Ritva as maintaining that with respect to this type of robber, one who was established as a robber of this particular field, proof based on the owner's admission is effective. The Gemara's case concerns only one who kills people. See there.
