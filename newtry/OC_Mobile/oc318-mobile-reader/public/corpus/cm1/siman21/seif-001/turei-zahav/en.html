“He is exempt from the act of acquisition” The Sema’s statement that this remains so even after the coercion passes is unclear. The issue resembles the dispute of Rosh and Rashba in Yoreh De'ah 221 concerning an oath to act on a specified day when coercion prevented performance. Rashba says the oath lapses with the day; Rosh says the obligation remains afterward. Since the law is unresolved, where the stipulation would make the other party collect, the obligor may plead Rashba; where it would exempt the other, that party may plead Rosh. Under the Tur’s wording, “on a specified day,” all agree that after the coercion ends the acquisition remains binding.
