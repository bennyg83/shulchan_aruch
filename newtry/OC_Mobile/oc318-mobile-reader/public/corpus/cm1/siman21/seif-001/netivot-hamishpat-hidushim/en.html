He is exempt from the kinyan. Even if the unavoidable circumstance later passed and he did not come, he may still come whenever he wishes, as though no kinyan had ever been made. Sema. This law applies both to a "waive for you" commitment and an "I will give you" commitment. Shach. If one obligates himself to pay his fellow one hundred if the fellow goes to a certain place, and the fellow was prevented by unavoidable circumstances from going, the obligor is nevertheless exempt. We say, "The Merciful One exempts one prevented by unavoidable circumstances," but we do not say, "The Merciful One imposes liability because of unavoidable circumstances." Shach. If the time is not part of the essence of the transaction, such as where parties agreed to a match and fixed a wedding date, an unavoidable delay does not cancel the match. But if they made the wedding date an express condition upon the match itself, and the condition failed even because of unavoidable circumstances, the match too is void. Shach.
