He should not pass by the entrance of a prostitute. If a man is suspected concerning a particular woman who is forbidden to him as an ervah, beit din warns him not to pass by the entrance of her house, and if he does so he is flogged. This is as in Yevamot 52a regarding one who passed the entrance of his mother-in-law's house when she had been suspected with him. Beit Yosef cites Rabbeinu Yerucham to this effect here.
