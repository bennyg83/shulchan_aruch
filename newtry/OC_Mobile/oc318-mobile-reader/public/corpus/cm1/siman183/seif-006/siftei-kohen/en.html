However, if he says, etc. It is surprising that Rama rules this way without qualification, for according to Rif, Rosh, and Baal HaIttur, the principal and agent divide the benefit. This also appears to be Ran's view. The Jerusalem Talmud likewise follows Rif's reasoning, that because the agent received the benefit through the owner, they divide it.
