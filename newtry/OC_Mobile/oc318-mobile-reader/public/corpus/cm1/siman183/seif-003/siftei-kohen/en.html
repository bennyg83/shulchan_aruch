He converted them into a loan owed by him, etc. This means there are witnesses to that fact. This appears to be Rambam's and the Mechaber's view, and the Maggid Mishneh likewise implies that this is Rambam's position. The Rama's language is somewhat awkward. See Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 175 and 177, and Responsa Rabbi Shlomo Kohen, volume 2, simanim 103 and 174.
Some say that he acquires it for himself. Maharshal also rules this way in Bava Kamma, chapter 9, siman 31, in a case involving a change. It is possible that the authorities of this view agree even in that case, as the Sema writes, although his reasoning there is not compelling. See Responsa Maharam Alshikh, at the end of the cited siman, and Responsa Mabit, volume 1, siman 108.
