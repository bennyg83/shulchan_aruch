Rambam likewise writes this according to his view in Hilkhot Mekhirah chapter 7, law 12.
He writes that the authorities who rule this way derive it from the sugya in HaGozel Etzim, 102b, concerning one who gives money to an agent to buy wheat, etc. This applies specifically where witnesses saw that he took the money for himself or he declared before them that he was withdrawing from the agency and purchasing for himself. Rashba likewise writes this.
