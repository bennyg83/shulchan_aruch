[Shach, continuation] But I have a difficulty with the Rosh. Note: I am astonished. There, in truth, the Exilarch did not acquire it. The main discussion there is that from the teaching, "We do not compel the Exilarch to sell," it follows that in any event the purchaser acquired it. The Gemara then asks whether this means they disagree with the Sages of the West, according to whom the purchaser would not acquire it because the seller thought he was purchasing on behalf of the Exilarch. But according to us, since we do not rule like the Sages of the West, the purchaser indeed acquires it, because he is the owner of the money. Afterward I saw that Responsa Rosh, klal 96, siman 3, likewise appears to explain that the Exilarch acquired it. See there. See also Responsa Shav HaKohen, siman 46, and Urim Gedolim, study 192.
