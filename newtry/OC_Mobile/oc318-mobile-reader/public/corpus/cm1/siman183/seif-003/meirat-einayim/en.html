He treated them as a loan against himself. Meaning, he thought: 'These funds the principal gave me I will use to purchase for myself, and I will regard the principal's money as a loan in my hands that I will repay today or tomorrow.'
He retracts from his agency, etc. There is no greater unauthorized use of entrusted money than this. The dissenting authorities hold that even such unauthorized use does not make the purchased item his unless he also deviated from the instructed transaction itself. For example, the principal told him to buy wheat and he instead bought barley for himself; then he acquires through the deviation. See the Derishah and Darkei Moshe.
