It belongs to the sender. See Baer Heitev through the words, "nevertheless, in my humble opinion this is not decisive." Dagul MeRevavah also challenges the Shach. He writes that the cases are not comparable. Since the seller knowingly gave an additional amount and intended to benefit the agent, how can the sender acquire that addition? The agent himself acquires even without prior knowledge, because another person's intention transfers it to him. Netivot HaMishpat explains that this is also the Shach's own position. When the Shach writes that the same rule applies in seif 6 to an item with a fixed price, he is referring to the case of an error in the sale cited by the Sema, seif katan 21. See there.
