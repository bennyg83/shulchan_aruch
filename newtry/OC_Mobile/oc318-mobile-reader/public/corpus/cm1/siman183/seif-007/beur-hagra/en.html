And Durk. “And thou shalt be thrust down to hell and shalt be thrust down to hell.”
And one who had. This follows the explanation of Rif and Baal HaMaor there concerning an item with a fixed price, based on the Midrash: 'You with the perutot and I with the feet,' etc. See there and Beit Yosef.
(Supplement) And one who had, etc. According to Rif and Baal HaMaor, they divide because the profit resulted from the combined fortune of both parties, through the principal's money and the agent's agency together. Tosafot and Rosh write likewise. Mordechai and Hagahot Asheri prove this from the comparison to the mishna there. The Yerushalmi expressly explains that the mishna concerns an increase in the market price. Tosafot cite it there at 98a, s.v. almanah. See the Yerushalmi, chapter 6 of Demai, and Hagahot Asheri. (End of supplement.)
