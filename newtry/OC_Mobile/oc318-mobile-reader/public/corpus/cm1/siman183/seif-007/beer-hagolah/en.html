Tur, seif 10, in the name of Rabbenu Yonah, with whom Rosh agreed. Tosafot there, s.v. 'Rav Pappa said,' likewise writes at the end of the passage that according to Rabbi the entire amount belongs to the agent, because it differs from an ordinary mistaken valuation. There the seller gives everything because he thinks the money is worth the amount given, whereas here it is a separate computational mistake. Bach likewise writes that an accounting error is a separate matter and is like theft from the other party.
He explains there that without this person's money the agent could not have deceived the gentile, and without the agent's deception there would have been no profit.
