And one who was conducting business, etc. See Darkei Moshe, who writes that R. Yerucham and the Rosh also agree here because the business concerns both parties. His words require study. See Mordechai and Responsa Maimoniyot concerning one who sends merchandise to another place where customs duties were due, and the agent, through ingenuity and skill, evaded the customs. This is not merely removing a lion from another's property, because the customs loss was certain. The principal must therefore pay the agent the value of the benefit he provided.
