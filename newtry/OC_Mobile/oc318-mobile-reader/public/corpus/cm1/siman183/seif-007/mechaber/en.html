Rama: See Darkei Moshe, who explains that this is also Rashi's view. If one sends an agent to collect money from a non-Jew, and the non-Jew mistakenly pays extra, the entire excess belongs to the agent. Rama: This applies only if the agent recognized the mistake before the money reached the principal. If he did not recognize it and delivered the money to the principal, the entire excess belongs to the principal. If one conducts business with non-Jews and another person assists him and deceives the non-Jew in measure, weight, or count, they divide the gain, whether the assistant worked for pay or without pay.
