(Seif 8: specified merchandise.) It appears to me that the agent is called deceitful only where the principal designated a specific known item of merchandise. This is like the beginning of Perek 3 of Kiddushin, where Rabbah bar bar Chanah told Rav, "Go purchase that field for me," and the Ran writes that regarding a specific field, because he acted deceitfully, etc. The entire sugya there likewise concerns a woman where a specific woman was designated. Concerning merely having a grievance, however, the principal can still say, "You caused me a loss, because I would have given the money to someone else to purchase for me." Our master says "specified merchandise" because that is the case where the agent is called deceitful, and the Mechaber follows our master's wording.
