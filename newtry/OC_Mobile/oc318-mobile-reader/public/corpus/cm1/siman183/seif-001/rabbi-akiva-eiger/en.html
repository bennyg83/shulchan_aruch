[Shulchan Aruch] Specified merchandise. Note: Responsa of the Geonim, siman 152: Reuven gave merchandise to Shimon to sell. Shimon sold it to Levi but did not receive payment, and he informed Levi that the merchandise belonged to Reuven. Afterward Shimon took the money, or took back the merchandise, from Levi. Reuven can say to Levi, "Once you knew that the merchandise was mine, you should not have given the money or the merchandise to Shimon." This was copied in Responsa Maharam of Brisk at the end of the book among the novellae of laws. This requires study.
