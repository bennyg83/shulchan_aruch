To his agent. See Responsa Mahari ben Lev, volume 3, siman 112. For an extensive treatment of the laws of agency, see Responsa Maharashach, volume 3, simanim 65 and 66, and Responsa Mabit, volume 2, siman 83. Shach.
Specified. This does not mean specifically that he told him, Buy this particular garment from this particular person. Even if he merely said, Buy me a garment, the rule applies. The proof is that the Tosefta says simply, Buy me produce, and the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write the same in Yoreh Deah, end of siman 177. Even in such a case the principal has grounds for resentment and the agent is called deceitful if he buys it for himself. Nevertheless, even if the principal explicitly said, Buy this particular garment from this particular person, he has no legal claim beyond resentment. Sema. The Taz writes that, in his view, with respect to being called deceitful, this applies only where he designated a specific known item, as in the beginning of chapter 3 of Kiddushin concerning Rabbah bar bar Chanah. But with respect to resentment, the principal may still say, You caused me a loss, because I would have given the assignment to someone else to purchase it.
