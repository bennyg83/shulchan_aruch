And if there was profit, etc. Baal HaMetivot and Baal HaIttur hold that the principal and agent divide the profit even where it has no fixed amount, because the agent deviated from instructions. Nimukei Yosef, in HaGozel, writes likewise in the name of Raah. This appears to me the primary interpretation of the Gemara. Although the law follows R. Yehudah that a deviation does not transfer ownership, nevertheless, because the profit resulted from the agent's deviation, they divide it. Many authorities, including Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Rashi, Tosafot, and others, rule like the Tur and Mechaber. Still, it appears to me that the party in possession may plead that the law follows Baal HaMetivot, Baal HaIttur, and Raah. R. Yosef ibn Ezra likewise writes in Responsa R. Shlomo Kohen, part II, siman 116, p. 80, beginning of column 3, that the possessor may say, 'I follow these authorities.' See there. See also Responsa Mabit, second series, siman 226, and part II, simanim 90, 103, and 179.
