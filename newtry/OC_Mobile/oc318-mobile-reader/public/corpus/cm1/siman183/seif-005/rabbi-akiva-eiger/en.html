[Shulchan Aruch] And he bought barley for him. Note: If the agent bought it for himself, the profit belongs to him, as the Sema wrote in seif katan 6. See Hagahot Ashri, chapter HaGozel, that he is believed to say, "I purchased it for myself." See also below, siman 292, seif 7, in the Rama, and Sema there, seif katan 20. Examine this carefully.
