The Shach writes that even with a full agent, and even concerning an item that has no fixed market price, if the agent deviated and bought barley instead of the instructed wheat he may say, "I rely upon the many authorities who hold that where the profit was caused by the agent's initiative, the profit is divided." See Beurim: this applies specifically where at the time of purchase the agent intended the acquisition for both himself and the principal, and he must immediately give the principal half the purchase money. But if he conducted the business entirely on behalf of the principal, everyone agrees all the profit belongs to the principal.
