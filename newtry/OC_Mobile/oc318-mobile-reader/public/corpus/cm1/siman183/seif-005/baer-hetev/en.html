Profit. The authors of Metivot and the Itur maintain that the parties divide the profit even in an item that has no fixed price, because the agent deviated. Nimukei Yosef in the chapter HaGozel cites the Raah similarly. This appears to me to be the primary reading of the Gemara. Although we rule like Rabbi Yehudah that a change does not acquire the property for the one who made the change, nevertheless, since the profit resulted from the agent's deviation, they divide it. Although many poskim, including the Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Rashi, Tosafot, and others, rule as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, it nevertheless appears to me that the party in possession may say, I rely upon the authorities who rule otherwise. Mahari ibn Ezra likewise writes in Responsa Maharashach, volume 2, siman 116, page 80, beginning of column 3, that the possessor may plead that he relies upon those authorities. See there.
