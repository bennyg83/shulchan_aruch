It belongs to the principal. See Baer Heitev, who writes that it appears this is the accepted ruling, and Responsa Mishkenot Yaakov, siman 52, likewise concludes this in practice. However, regarding the words of Atzei Levonah, who explains that presumably we say that the agent certainly did not retract, Mishkenot Yaakov writes that this requires study. That is not the reasoning of Rambam and those who agree with him. Even if the agent explicitly states before witnesses that he charges the amount to himself as a loan, the item nevertheless belongs to the principal, because the agent has no power to acquire it for himself with the principal's money.
