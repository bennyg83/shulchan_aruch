If one gives an agent money to buy wheat, whether for trade or consumption, and he buys barley instead, or vice versa, any loss is borne by the agent and any profit belongs to the principal. Rama: The same applies where the money was given as an investment with equal division of profit. Likewise, if one sent an agent with merchandise to take it to a particular place and the agent took it elsewhere, the agent alone bears any loss, while any profit is divided between them.
