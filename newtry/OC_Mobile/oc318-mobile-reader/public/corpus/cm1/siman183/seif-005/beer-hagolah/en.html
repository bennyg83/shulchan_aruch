There, seif 6. Rambam likewise writes in chapter 1 of Hilkhot Sheluchin, law 5, from the baraita, "One who gives money to his agent," etc., Bava Kamma 102b, according to Rabbi Elazar's explanation. The Rosh writes that since we rule like Rabbi Yehudah, there is no distinction between purchase for consumption and purchase for commerce: it is always treated as agency. If the goods decline in value, the loss is the principal's; if they increase, the gain belongs to the owner of the money. Rif likewise concludes.
