The same applies if Reuven owed money to a non-Jew and gave it to another person to pay him, but the non-Jew forgot the debt: the agent must return the money to Reuven. Rama: If one gave another fifty gold coins to compromise with his creditors and he settled for twenty-five, the balance belongs to the principal. Likewise, if he gave him two hundred to pay a non-Jew, and the agent deceived the non-Jew and paid only one hundred while the non-Jew believed he had received two hundred, the entire saving belongs to the principal. It is as though the non-Jew waived half the debt, and such a waiver belongs to the debtor.
