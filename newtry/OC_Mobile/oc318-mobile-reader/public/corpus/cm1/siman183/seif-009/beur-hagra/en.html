Likewise, one who gave. Ketubot 91b: and at what valuation was it seized, etc.
Likewise, if he gave. The money remains in the principal's possession until it is delivered to the non-Jew, because the rule that 'give' is equivalent to 'acquire' applies only with a Jewish recipient. It is therefore like one who says, 'Bring a maneh to so-and-so,' and the recipient was not found, in which case it returns to the sender, as stated at the end of the first chapter of Gittin.
