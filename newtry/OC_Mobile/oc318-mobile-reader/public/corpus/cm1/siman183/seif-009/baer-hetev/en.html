To compromise. The same applies where Reuven sent his pledges with an agent to borrow money upon them at interest from a non-Jew for as much as he could obtain. The agent says that he borrowed a certain amount, but Reuven later learns that the agent borrowed for less interest than he had claimed. Reuven need pay only the amount that the agent actually pays the non-Jew. This is written in Responsa Dvar Reuven, siman 331, where the matter is discussed at length. It is obvious and correct in my view. See Responsa Maharashdam, siman 184. Ibid. See also Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 147.
