As though the gentile waived half the debt. This is because the money belongs to the principal, so whatever remains is presumed to remain under its owner's authority. This differs from the previous case where an agent deceived a gentile while receiving money from another person, in which case the agent acquired the excess. Sema. The same applies where Reuven sent his own collateral through an agent to borrow money on interest, and later learns that the agent obtained the loan at a lower rate than the amount the agent represented. Reuven need pay only the amount the agent actually pays the gentile. Shach.
