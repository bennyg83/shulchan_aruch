For it belongs to the principal, etc. The same applies where Reuven sent his pledges with an agent to borrow money upon them at interest from non-Jews at whatever rate the agent could obtain. The agent claimed that he obtained a certain amount, but Reuven later learned that the agent borrowed at a lower rate than he reported. Reuven need pay only what the agent actually pays the non-Jew. This is stated in Responsa Divrei Rivot, siman 331, which discusses the matter at length. The ruling is clear and correct in my eyes.
