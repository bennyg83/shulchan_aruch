The Tur explains: since Shimon originally trusted Reuven and became guarantor for him without collateral, he cannot now retain Reuven's funds merely because of a speculative concern. Nor may he say, "I want to sanctify Hashem's Name by paying the gentile," because he must sanctify Hashem's Name with his own property, not another's. This is unlike a case where an agent deceived a gentile and received extra money. There, if he wishes, he may return the excess to the gentile because the principal never acquired that excess. Here the money is already Reuven's property. Sema.
