Reuven sent Shimon to buy a garment for him on credit. When payment became due, Reuven gave Shimon the money, but the seller forgot the debt. Shimon must return the money to Reuven. He cannot retain it on the ground that the non-Jew may later remember, nor may he say that he wishes to sanctify the Name by returning it to the non-Jew. Rama: A similar rule appears in the Tur and in siman 72, seif 31.
