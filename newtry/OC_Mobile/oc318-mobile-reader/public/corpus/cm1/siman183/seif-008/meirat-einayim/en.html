Lest the gentile remember them later, etc. The Tur explains that since Shimon originally trusted Reuven and became guarantor for him without collateral, he must trust him now as well and may not retain these funds merely because of the possibility that the gentile will later remember them. Nor may he say, "I wish to sanctify Hashem's name by returning them." He should sanctify Hashem's name with his own property, not with another person's. This differs from the preceding case where the agent deceived the gentile and may return the excess if he wishes. There, the principal never acquired that excess. Here, however, the funds already belong to Reuven. This distinction also resolves the Tur's rulings in the following seifim.
