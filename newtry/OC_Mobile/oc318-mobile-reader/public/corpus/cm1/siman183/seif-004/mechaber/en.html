If Reuven told Shimon, 'Buy this item for me,' and Shimon bought it without specifying for whom, Reuven acquires it from the moment Shimon performs meshichah. Even if Shimon afterward says that he intended to buy it for himself, he is not believed. Even where Shimon paid with his own money, Reuven acquires it and must reimburse Shimon. If Shimon withdrew from the agency before meshichah and declared that he was buying for himself, even if he did so after payment but before meshichah, he acquires it for himself, provided he paid the seller with his own money. If Reuven gave money to Shimon, Shimon paid the seller in Reuven's name, and before meshichah Shimon said, 'I am buying for myself,' Shimon does not acquire unless he informs the seller and the seller intends to transfer ownership to him. This applies when Reuven said, 'Buy for me.' If he merely said, 'Bring it to me so that I may buy it,' and Shimon went and bought it for himself, Shimon acquires it. Rama: If Reuven knew of inexpensive merchandise and told Shimon to buy it jointly for them, and Shimon remained silent, bought it, and later claimed he bought for himself, then if he used his own money Shimon acquires it. Even if he initially agreed expressly, he may withdraw, though he is deceitful. If he used Reuven's money, he must divide it with him. The principal cannot withdraw from the agency, even if Shimon used his own money.
