See the Sema's resolution of the apparent contradiction with the conclusion in seif 9, that according to all opinions, if he initially agreed to purchase for both of them, he acquires for both and cannot retract. The Shach cites the Bach that here they had not made a complete arrangement between them, but merely discussed purchasing together, without specifying how they would purchase or in what amount.
