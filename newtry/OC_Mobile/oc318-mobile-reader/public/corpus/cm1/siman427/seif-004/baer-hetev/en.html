For a parapet. Meaning, one should not think the homeowner must build a parapet from the public domain upward along the roof until it reaches ten handbreadths above the public domain. Since the public domain belongs collectively to all, it is obvious that those using it are not obligated to build a parapet for it. Ibid.
From it. The Sages expound "from it," but not within it. This is somewhat difficult: why is an exclusion needed? Even without it the homeowner could say that absent my house, the person would all the more have been injured or killed by falling from the height of the public domain to the ground. One may answer with difficulty that otherwise we might have thought the homeowner must fill the area occupied by his house with earth until it is level with the public domain. Ibid.
