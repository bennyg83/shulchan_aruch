A parapet for his roof. See above, siman 314, seif 2, that one who rents a house from another is obligated, as the tenant, to construct a parapet; see there. See also the work Ara DeRabbanan, entry 403, which cites Knesset HaGedolah as ruling that a tenant is obligated in a parapet only by rabbinic law, while the author himself writes that the tenant is obligated even by Torah law; see there.
