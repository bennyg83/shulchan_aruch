Rambam holds these prohibitions are rabbinic and Scripture is only an asmachta, as above in seif 9. Nevertheless, lashes of rebellion may be administered for them like for other rabbinic mitzvot. Alternatively, the Sages had a tradition that they are Torah prohibitions, but lashes are not administered, just as half a minimum measure is prohibited by Torah law yet carries no lashes. The author to finish a good thing
In my humble opinion, the reason the Torah warned us to guard life is that the Holy One, blessed be He, created the world in His kindness to benefit His creatures, so that they recognize His greatness and serve Him by observing His mitzvot and Torah, as Scripture says: 'Everyone called by My Name, whom I created for My glory,' etc., and so that He may grant them good reward for their labor. One who endangers himself is as though he rejects the will of his Creator and desires neither His service nor His reward. There is no greater contempt or lawlessness than this. May this be pleasant to those who hear it, and may the pleasantness of Hashem our God be upon us; establish the work of our hands for us.
