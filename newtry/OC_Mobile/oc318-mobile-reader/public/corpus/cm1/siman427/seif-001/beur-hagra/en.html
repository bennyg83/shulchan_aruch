(Collected note) And it is, etc. Sifrei states: I know only a dwelling house. From where do we include a barn, cattle shed, woodshed, or storehouse? The verse says, "do not place blood in your house." Could this include a gatehouse, portico, or veranda? The verse says "house," meaning a structure designated for dwelling, excluding those not used as dwellings. Semag likewise rules so. The Rambam, however, holds that this Sifrei follows the opinion that barns and similar structures require mezuzah, while he himself rules like the opinion exempting them, as stated in chapter 6 of Hilchos Mezuzah. His reason is that he understands the Gemara's conclusion that way, despite the challenge to Rav Yehuda. See Yoreh Deah siman 286 seif 2, where the Mechaber rules with those who disagree with the Rambam and require mezuzah in the ordinary case. It therefore requires study why the Mechaber here follows the Rambam. (End of note.)
