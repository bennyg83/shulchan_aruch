[Shulchan Aruch] A parapet for his roof. Note: He recites the blessing, “Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to make a parapet.” If another person makes the parapet, the one who performs the work recites, “concerning the making of a parapet.” Rambam, chapter 11 of Hilchot Berachot. If he has the parapet made by a non-Jewish worker, whether he may recite a blessing requires consideration. See Machaneh Ephraim, Laws of Agency, siman 11. End.
