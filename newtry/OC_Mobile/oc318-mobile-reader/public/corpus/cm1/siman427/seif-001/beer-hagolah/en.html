The wording of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 11 of Hilkhot Rotzeach U'Shemirat HaNefesh.
Kesef Mishneh writes that although Sifrei obligates a barn and cattle shed in this law, and Semag likewise writes so, Rambam may reason from Yoma chapter 1, 11a, concerning mezuzah, where this is a dispute of Tannaim. The Tanna of Sifrei follows the opinion that obligates them, while Rambam in Hilkhot Mezuzah rules like the opinion that exempts them. In my humble opinion the reason is that in Yoma the exempting opinion is the anonymous first Tanna, while the baraita then says 'some obligate,' implying that those who obligate are the minority.
