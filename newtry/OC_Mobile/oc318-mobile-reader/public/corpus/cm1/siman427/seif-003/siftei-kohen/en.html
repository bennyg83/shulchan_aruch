Belonging to two partners, etc. Regarding a partnership with a non-Jew, Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter 11 of Chullin, rules that there is an obligation. See there. However, according to the Rama's ruling in Yoreh Deah, siman 286, seif 1, in the gloss concerning mezuzah, that such a partnership is exempt, it is possible that the same applies to a parapet. According to the Mordechai in Avodah Zarah, the matter remains doubtful.
