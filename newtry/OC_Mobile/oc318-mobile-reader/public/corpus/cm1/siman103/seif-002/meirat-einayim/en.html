It is valued at one hundred and ten. In the Derishah I proved that this is not the view of the Rif, Rosh, and Tur. Rather, they agree with the Rashbam that in such a case it is valued at one hundred, not one hundred and ten, because two of the appraisers say that it is worth no more than one hundred. The third, who says one hundred and thirty, also admits that it is worth at least one hundred and merely adds that it is worth more. We therefore follow the two out of three whose valuations coincide at one hundred. This is the Rashbam’s position, and in my view the halachah follows them because the reasoning is sound and they are also the majority of authorities.
