It is valued at one hundred ten. This is the middle amount among the valuations. Sema writes that most authorities disagree and say the property is valued only at one hundred. The appraiser who says ninety and the one who says one hundred both testify that it is worth no more than one hundred, while the one who says one hundred and the one who says one hundred thirty both testify that it is worth at least one hundred. Chiddushei Gershuni agrees. See Tumim. I brought proof from the Gemara and concluded that this is correct.
