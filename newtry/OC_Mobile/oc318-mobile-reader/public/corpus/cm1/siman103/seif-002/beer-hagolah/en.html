The language of Rambam, chapter 22 of Hilkhot Malveh, law 14, based on the baraita in Bava Batra 107a.
In accordance with the first Tanna there, as ruled by the poskim, and in accordance with Rav Ashi on 107b, that since the reasons of the other opinions are unknown, we do not act according to them. The Gemara explains the reason of the first Tanna on 107a as that he states the intermediate position.
Rambam explains the Gemara's phrase "it is an intermediate matter" in this manner, and Raavad does not dispute him there. Nimukei Yosef likewise explains Rif in this way. The Rosh in his rulings there copies Rif's language. Sema writes that Rif, Rosh, and Tur follow Rashbam, but in my humble opinion there is no proof of this in their words. I did not merit to see what Sema wrote in Derishah concerning this. In my humble opinion, although I am unworthy to decide, one should not depart from Beit Yosef's conclusion, especially since Nimukei Yosef also explains Rif this way.
