Experts in appraising land. It appears this is not limited to land. If she sells movable property after the enactment of the Geonim, she likewise requires three trustworthy people expert in appraisal. Rif and Rosh write that there must be trustworthy people skilled in valuation. Maggid Mishneh explains that even if none of them is a learned judge fit to adjudicate monetary cases, this suffices, for otherwise their authority would be no stronger than ordinary judges. In any event, she must swear before selling, as the Gemara concludes: one who sells outside beit din must take an oath but need not make a public announcement. The law of the oath was already explained in siman 96. The issue of a sale before the oath is discussed there.
