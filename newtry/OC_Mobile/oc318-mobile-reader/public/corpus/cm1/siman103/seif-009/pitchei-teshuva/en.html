And afterward it was found, etc. The Sema explains that the Mechaber states a progression from the lesser to the greater novelty, following Nimukei Yosef: certainly where the property is later found to have been in the custodian's possession and the custodian himself knew this from the outset, and even beyond that. Responsa Chavot Yair, end of siman 30, challenges the Sema, writing that one who studies the Gemara sees that it does not discuss a case where the custodian knew; see there.
