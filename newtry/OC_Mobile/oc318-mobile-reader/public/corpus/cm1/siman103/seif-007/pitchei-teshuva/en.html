If land was appraised for a woman, see Baer Heitev. Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes in his glosses to Shulchan Aruch that Tiferet Shmuel, by the author of Beit Shmuel, likewise rules in accordance with Maharshal. Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part II, siman 161, however, writes that there appears to be clear proof from Bava Metzia 16b that even where land was appraised in payment of a woman's ketubah the appraisal may later be reversed, from Rava's question there; see there.
