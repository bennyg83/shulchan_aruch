(Seif 8.) "They deduct from him." Our master states this concerning orphans because the creditor must swear. The Bach writes that orphans are not specifically intended. The Sema, seif katan 12, writes that there is nevertheless a greater novelty with orphans. It appears to me that the same applies to an ordinary creditor who must swear. The novelty with orphans is that the oath arises only from uncertainty. It seems that the case is where appraisal and announcement had already been completed during the father's lifetime, but the creditor had not yet been placed into possession before the father died. The only remaining requirement is the oath against the orphans, lest their father have paid afterward. Otherwise, the rule immediately nearby states that before appraisal and announcement the creditor has no proprietary right in the property at all.
