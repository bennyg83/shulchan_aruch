Rambam writes so in chapter 22 of Hilkhot Malveh, law 15.
Not as Raavad explained his words. Maggid Mishneh likewise gives the reason: the purchaser is not personally responsible to repay this debt and does not inherit from the debtor as the orphans do, who stand in their father's place. Therefore the court acts as agents, etc. Consequently, the law requires that if the appraisal was deficient by any amount, the sale is void. See siman 14 and siman 165, where Rama comments in the gloss, as explained there.
