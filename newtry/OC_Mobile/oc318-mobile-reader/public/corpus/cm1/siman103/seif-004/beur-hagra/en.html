Beit din. Baal HaTerumos, citing the Rif, writes that the rule that an appraisal differing by one sixth remains effective applies particularly to orphans, whose property is under the authority of beit din. A purchaser is treated like an ordinary principal acting through agents. Kesubos 100a, which compares an agent to a widow, supports this; Tosafos' difficulty there is thereby resolved.
But if... So writes the Maggid Mishneh. See below, siman 109 seif 3, and the dissenting gloss there. The Gemara's general wording concerning matters for which appraisal is invalid supports the Maggid Mishneh's interpretation.
