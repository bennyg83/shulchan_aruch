[Shulchan Aruch] Land was appraised away from a woman and she then married. Note: If property was appraised away from her after she married, for a debt she owed before the marriage, the matter depends upon the dispute between the Rosh and Rambam above, seif 9, whether property appraised away from a purchaser is returned to him. See the Beit Yosef and Shach, seif katan 19.
