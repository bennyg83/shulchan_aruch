The three appraisers. It appears that all three must sit together. The Gemara calls them a beit din of laymen, and just as judges must sit together so that each hears the reasoning of the others, so too the three appraisers must be together. If one says that he does not know, another appraiser must be called, because perhaps if the first two hear the reasoning of the third they will agree with him. The proof is straightforward. Otherwise, once two appraisers agreed there would be no reason even to ask the third, because whatever he said would be nullified by the majority. It must therefore be as stated.
