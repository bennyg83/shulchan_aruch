He has priority. Yerushalmi Kesubos. Even though we do not follow the Yerushalmi in dispensing with announcement, its priority rule stands. The reason is that an appraisal from the debtor can later be reversed, unlike an appraisal involving a purchaser who bought with intent to own the land. Baal HaTerumos explains this.
