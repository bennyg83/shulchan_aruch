Tur, siman 10. So writes Ba'al HaTerumot in Gate 3, from the Mishnah in Beit Kor, Bava Batra 103b: 'What does he return to him, money,' etc. The Gemara concludes that this is in order to strengthen the seller's position, etc. Thus the seller is not required to accept another parcel of land when what remains for him is not of a usable size. All the more so, a creditor need not accept land in payment when it is not of a sufficient useful measure. This also follows from Rif at the beginning of Bava Metzia, who writes, 'this applies where it is fit for him,' etc. Rambam likewise agreed in a responsum.
The Mechaber likewise ruled this in Bedek HaBayit, following Rashba in a responsum and as Rabbenu Yerucham writes in his name, Netiv 7, part 3. His reasoning is sound, especially since it appears that the sages of the generation did not dispute him.
