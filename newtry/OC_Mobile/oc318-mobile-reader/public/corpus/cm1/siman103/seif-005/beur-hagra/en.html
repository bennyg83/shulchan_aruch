And if there is no... As above, from the question whether he returns the money.
And we compel the creditor. Gittin 36a says that if one lends another a place, a prozbul may be written on the basis of that land, meaning it is considered property from which the debt can be collected. Therefore the owner can be required to rent it or mortgage it so the creditor can realize his debt. Responsum of Rashba.
And we do not show mercy... Bava Kamma asks why a woman cannot sell the beneficial value of her ketubah, showing that she can be compelled to sell even at a reduced value.
If the matter was... Bava Basra 104a establishes that when a term is ambiguous we interpret it to strengthen the seller's position, and this remains the conclusion, particularly where value rose or fell. All the more so in favor of a borrower where appropriate.
If he was collecting... The Rif writes this in the first chapter of Bava Metzia and brings proof from the rule in the first chapter of Bava Basra that where property can be divided while retaining its name, division is required.
