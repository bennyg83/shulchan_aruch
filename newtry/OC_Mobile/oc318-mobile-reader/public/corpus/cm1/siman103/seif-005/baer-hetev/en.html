To compel. The Sema writes that this refers to a case where the purchaser insists specifically on cash. If he instead gives the creditor a choice and says, "Either take this for cash, or give me land equal to this amount and leave the remainder with you toward your debt; I will sell the portion you give me and pay you the sale proceeds," the Tur rules that he is heard. The Mechaber concludes that where the creditor collected from a purchaser, the purchaser is heard even in such a case where he does not give the creditor this choice. According to the Sema, one need not say that the Tur and Mechaber disagree. The Shach writes that had the Sema seen Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit, he would not have written this, for it is explicit there that they do disagree. See the Shach's practical conclusion.
