I agreed with Sema that this applies to every person: before the days of public announcement have been completed, the creditor may not consume the produce of the foreclosed land, and if he did, its value is deducted from the debt. Even if the announcement period ended, if the borrower died before final foreclosure was delivered to the creditor and the creditor then entered and consumed produce, or if custom or condition allowed him to consume produce without announcement and the borrower died, in all these cases the property has fallen before the heirs. Since collection from heirs requires an oath, until the creditor swears the assets remain presumptively the heirs' property, and the produce is deducted. This is the distinction between heirs and a living borrower. See there.
