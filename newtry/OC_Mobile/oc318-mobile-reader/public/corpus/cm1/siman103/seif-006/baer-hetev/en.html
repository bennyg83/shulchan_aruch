To sell it. Although with a pledge or other movable property the Tur writes above, in siman 73, seif 19, that the creditor may sell it only through beit din, he concludes there that literal beit din is not required, but rather three appraisers. Here too the case is that he sells the land based on three appraisers, but without first obtaining authorization from beit din. The disagreement here exists because land is more significant. Thus writes the Sema. The Taz writes that the Sema's words are difficult, for the Mechaber certainly would not have omitted the important qualification that three appraisers were involved. Rather, there is no difficulty. Movable property is different because at the time of sale the creditor lacks secure control over it, for the purchaser may remove it and afterward it may be impossible to establish whether it was worth more. Land, however, remains before us, and it can later be established whether the sale was proper and whether the property was worth more.
