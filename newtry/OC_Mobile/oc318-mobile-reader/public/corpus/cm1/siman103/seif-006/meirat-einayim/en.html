He may sell it. Although regarding a pledge of movable property the Tur writes above, siman 73:19, that he may sell it only through beit din, he concludes there in seif 23 in the name of the Rosh that "beit din" is not literal, but means three appraisers. He explicitly writes there that there is no need to bring the pledgor before judges. Rather, the lender warns him privately, then takes three appraisers who assess what the pledge is worth for sale, and he sells it at that valuation. The Mechaber likewise states this explicitly there in seif 15. Here too the case is where he retains the land or sells it based on the valuation of three appraisers, but without obtaining authorization from beit din. The dispute here exists because land is more significant than movable property.
