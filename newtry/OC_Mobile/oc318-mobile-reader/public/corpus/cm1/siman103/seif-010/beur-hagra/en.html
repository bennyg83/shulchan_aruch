For if they appraised it... It is not right and good that the creditor suffer a loss, as the Gemara says where he bought for two hundred and the property was worth one hundred.
Or if they appraised it... The reason the appraisal returns is that the recipient should not be better situated than the original owner.
Or if they appraised it... Rambam explains the Gemara's phrase "he himself assigned it..." in this way.
And some say... They explain "he is like a purchaser and does not return it" as referring to property the owner had written to one of his heirs during his lifetime. Rambam instead follows Tosafos' approach.
Unless... As stated in Kiddushin 42b concerning brothers who divided an estate.
And some say specifically... As stated there in the teaching of Rabbi Yochanan, in the case where one of them later died.
