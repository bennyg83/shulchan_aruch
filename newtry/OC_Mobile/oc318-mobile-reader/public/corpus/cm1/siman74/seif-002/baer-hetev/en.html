With him. If the lender is absent, payment is effective when the borrower gives the money to beit din or to a trusted officer of beit din even without any prior communal enactment authorizing this. Responsa Maharshach II, 99 states this, and the Shach notes that this was the practice in times of emergency in his region. He disagrees, however, with Maharshach's further statement that it suffices to confer ownership of the money upon the lender through another person, for the reasons explained at the beginning of siman 125.

But where the danger already existed when the loan was made, fixing a term may have been more for the lender's benefit than the borrower's, and the borrower cannot force early repayment.
