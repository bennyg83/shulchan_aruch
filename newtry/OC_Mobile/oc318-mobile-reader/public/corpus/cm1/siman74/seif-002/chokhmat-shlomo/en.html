Seif 6. A borrower says when the time for payment arrives, “I have no money,” etc. See the Shach regarding sale of the collateral. In my responsum in Yoreh Deah, Hilchot Bekhor, siman 5, I discussed many laws concerning sale of collateral and concluded that with movable collateral there is no genuine doubt: the creditor may sell it in order to collect his debt in the manner provided by law.
