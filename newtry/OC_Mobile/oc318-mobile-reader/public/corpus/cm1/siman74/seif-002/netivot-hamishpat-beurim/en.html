The law is with him. See Shach, seif katan 9, who writes that if the lender is not present, the borrower may deposit the payment with beit din by virtue of an enactment. Tumim seeks to prove from Gittin that payment of a debt in the creditor's absence constitutes payment by strict law. This is certainly incorrect, as is evident from Rashba cited by the Sema in siman 73, seif katan 24. The Rashba cited by Beit Yosef states explicitly that such payment is ineffective. Tumim's proof can also be refuted, but I did not wish to lengthen the discussion here.
