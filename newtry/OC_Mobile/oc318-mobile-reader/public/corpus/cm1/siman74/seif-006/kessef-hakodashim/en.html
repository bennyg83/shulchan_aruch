Where a person seizes collateral on his own after the original loan, the rule that the debtor may say, "retain collateral only up to the amount actually due to you," may apply more readily than with collateral voluntarily delivered at the inception of the loan. Moreover, one who seizes property specifically for immediate collection and sale through appraisal should not be able to hold more than the amount that the appraisal shows is due. This is especially so with items such as daytime clothing, where seizure is permitted only because it is part of the lawful collection process. Once the lawful collection amount is covered, excess property should not remain held.
