For a poor woman, he may maintain the vow for one year. Whether he expressly stipulated one year or imposed the vow without a stated period, he must wait one year according to Shmuel, Ketubot 71b, whose view we follow. The Mishnah at the beginning of the chapter, Ketubot 70a, states that regarding a poor woman where no duration was fixed, meaning an unstated vow, he must divorce her immediately. According to Shmuel, that case is where she herself vowed and he upheld her vow. If he imposed the vow upon her, he waits until he can find grounds to annul it. See Tosafot 71a, s.v. 'for a poor woman where no duration was fixed.'
