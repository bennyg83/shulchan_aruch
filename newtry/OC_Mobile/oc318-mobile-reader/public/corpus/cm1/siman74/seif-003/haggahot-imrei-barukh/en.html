(Taz, seif 1) For it is possible to wait. Note: One may say that this itself is considered a loss, because he will have to incur expenses by remaining here. Therefore he is not obligated to accept payment before the due date. In siman 100, however, the reason is that since the law requires granting the borrower time, the lender cannot prevent that period merely because he needs to continue on his journey. See Responsa Rosh, rule 20, ruling 3. The Rosh did not write there at all that they tell him to appoint an agent; the Tur added those words. The Rosh's principal reasoning is as I have stated.
