A debt secured by a pledge... Bava Metzia 67b: "rather, in a place where the borrower cannot remove the mortgage early, and he said..." The entire sugya there concerns this.
A borrower... but with a deposit. As stated in the incident involving sesame in Bava Metzia 49a: "He said to him, since they told you..." Although we do not rule according to the first version, that is because he must accept upon himself the curse of mi shepara. See the Rif and Rosh there, and likewise there in chapter 2, "all those concerning whom they said, take..." This differs from a loan, which remains under his responsibility, as the Rif and Rosh write regarding the sesame incident: even with a deposit, once he took it out, it became his responsibility and had the status of a loan in his hands. See there. So too Bava Metzia 43a, "in consideration of that benefit..." and there, 81a, "it is reasonable that he is exempt... since..."
And this is when. See the Shach, especially seifim ketanim 14, 15, and 16. See the end of siman 120, the Shach there, and Mordechai, chapter Mi She'achazo.
