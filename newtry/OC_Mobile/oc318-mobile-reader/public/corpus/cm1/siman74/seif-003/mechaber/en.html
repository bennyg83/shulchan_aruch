A mortgage debt from which the lender consumes the produce, in a place where the borrower may not remove him until the term arrives, is like the purchase of the produce until that time. If the lender wishes, he need not accept the money before the due date unless the borrower leaves him the produce to consume. {Rama: If a borrower tells the lender, 'Take your money,' while the loan is still within its term, but the lender refuses to accept it, and the money remains tied up and deposited with the borrower and is stolen or lost through unavoidable accident, the borrower must pay. In the corresponding case of a deposit, however, he is exempt.}
