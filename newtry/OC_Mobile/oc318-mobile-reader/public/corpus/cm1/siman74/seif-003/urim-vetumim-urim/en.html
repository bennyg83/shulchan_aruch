He takes a hesset oath. Shach explains that this requires a definite claim, not a merely uncertain one. Ramban's language at the end of Baal HaTerumot, gate 30, does not sound that way. It appears that he requires an oath even on uncertainty, as part of the Geonic enactment that a debtor swear, 'I have nothing,' derived from the rules of arranging a debtor's assets.
