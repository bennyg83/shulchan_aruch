(Mechaber, seif 1) Who has nothing, etc. Note: See Baal HaTerumot, gate 30, end of part 1, who explains that the reason is that if the debtor were in an inhabited place we would arrange for him the property necessary for his subsistence. According to this, study is required where the claim is made in the wilderness for a debt arising from wages, or from hire of the debtor's animal, since according to Baal HaTerumot the rule of arranging subsistence does not apply to such obligations. See below, siman 97, seif 29, and Shach here, seif katan 3.
