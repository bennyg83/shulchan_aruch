Seif 3. A debt secured by a mortgage, etc. See my responsum in Yoreh Deah, Hilchot Bekhor, fifth edition, siman 199, where I distinguished between an ordinary open-ended mortgage and one with a fixed term. With a fixed term, the debtor cannot satisfy the lender merely by paying the monetary value of the fruits; the lender can insist upon receiving the fruits themselves during the agreed term. With an ordinary mortgage, although there may be a customary annual period, payment of the value of the fruits can suffice. I was compelled to this distinction by the Gemara, as explained there.
