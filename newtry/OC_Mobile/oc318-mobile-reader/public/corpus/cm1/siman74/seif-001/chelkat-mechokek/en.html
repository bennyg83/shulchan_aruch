He prohibited her by vow from adorning herself. The Tur in siman 72 presents the laws of one who prohibits his wife by vow from benefiting from him or from tasting certain food among the laws relating to support, which were explained in siman 70. Here, in this siman, he arranges the laws concerning clothing and utensils and going to her father's house, matters explained in the previous siman. Rambam likewise places each matter in its appropriate chapter; see chapters 12 and 13 of Hilchot Ishut. The law of one who prohibits marital relations by vow will be explained in siman 77 under the laws of conjugal obligations, and one who prohibits her from work is discussed below in siman 80 under the laws of her earnings.
