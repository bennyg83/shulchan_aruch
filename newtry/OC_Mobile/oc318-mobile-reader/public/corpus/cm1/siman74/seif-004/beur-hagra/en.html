'Why,' beginning 'This does not appear,' etc. Rashba and Ran in Kiddushin reconcile Rashbam: when the Gemara says he redeems in installments, it does not mean the Temple treasurer must release half the field. Rather, the owner may compel him to accept half the money, with the result that half the field will not become absolutely vested in the priests at the Jubilee. The same applies to collateral: when he pays the other half, the creditor must return it, but he need not return half the object now. This follows all the more from consecrated property, where Scripture expressly revealed the rule. Ramban also rules this way.
