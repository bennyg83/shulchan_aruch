The same applies if the due date arrived. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger: Where a debtor fixed repayment in installments at different times, but those dates passed and the creditor did not demand payment, the creditor cannot thereafter compel payment of the entire sum at once. Payment should instead be spread over intervals as beit din sees fit. See Knesset HaGedolah, siman 73, Tur section 6, in the name of Rabbi Shimon Yonah.
