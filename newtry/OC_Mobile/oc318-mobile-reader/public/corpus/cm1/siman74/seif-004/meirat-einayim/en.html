He has a grievance against him. He can say: you caused me a loss by paying me little by little, because as I receive small amounts I spend them, whereas had you paid me the entire amount at its proper time I could have invested it profitably.The same applies if the time arrived, etc. Rama's phrase 'the same applies' is imprecise, because this case is even stronger.
Once the due date arrived, the lender has both options: not only need he not return the collateral, but he can also demand immediate payment.
