(Sema, seif katan 7) And for this reason he likewise wrote, etc. Note: See below, siman 176, seif 23, and Tosafot to Bava Metzia 105a, passage beginning "these." I later saw that the Tumim raises this point and notes that the words of the Shiltei HaGibborim appear contrary to that rule. Ketzot HaChoshen answers that one who has an obligation of guarding cannot withdraw. But from the Shiltei HaGibborim's statement that the loss is visibly caused by him, it appears that his intent is that one cannot withdraw from the investment arrangement. See Even HaEzer 58, Beit Shmuel seif katan 34. In my humble opinion, the Shiltei HaGibborim's wording is exact. He writes that if the funds are returned before the agreed term they will remain idle and earn nothing, and the owner will lose the profit that would have accrued until the end of the term. This is a case of a matter that will be lost, in which even a worker cannot withdraw. See below, siman 333, seif 5.
