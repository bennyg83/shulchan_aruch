ch) Like Rava's statement, "One who," etc., "has only a grievance against him," Bava Metzia 77b. The Rosh writes in his rulings, and Baal HaIttur likewise writes, that if the lender could prevent partial redemption, the Gemara should have said that he may refuse to accept it, not merely that he has a grievance. A further proof is from the implication of the Gemara, which needed to derive that a house in a walled city may be redeemed in installments from the law of an ancestral field, Kiddushin 20b. Baal HaTerumot likewise writes in gate 69 in the name of the Ramban, and so writes the Tur.
t) There, seif 8, in the name of the Ramban. Baal HaTerumot there likewise writes in his name and says there is proof from the sugya concerning division being void, Bava Batra 106b.
y) What is stated that a borrower may redeem in installments, Kiddushin there, means that against the lender's will he may pay the partial amount. Nevertheless, the lender continues to consume the produce until the remaining amount is paid. He writes that this also appears from the Rashba, and so writes Rabbeinu Yerucham at the end of Netiv 12 in the name of the Rashba.
