Disagree. The Darkei Moshe writes this in the name of Responsa Rosh and the Rivash. I am surprised, for the Rivash explicitly writes the opposite in siman 196, and there is no conclusive proof from Responsa Rosh either. I have already discussed this at length in the Shach to Yoreh Deah, siman 166, and ruled that the law of the kingdom is law, unlike Maharshal and the Bach. I later found the same in Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 79, where he rules simply that the law of the kingdom applies in such a case. It also appears from there that no authority disputes this and that the Rosh in his responsum agrees. Shiltei Giborim rules likewise. See further on these laws in Shiltei Giborim, chapter Get Pashut, folio 240, Responsa Maharam Alshich simanim 115, 116, and 129, Mahari ben Lev part 2 siman 110, Mabit part 1, second collection of questions, siman 171, Maharashdam simanim 75 and 103, Mahara Sasson siman 123, and Maharit simanim 10 and 74.
