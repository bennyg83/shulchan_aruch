[Shulchan Aruch] And he did not have money. Note: So too in the Tur. I do not understand the precision of this wording, which implies that if he has no money, even though he possesses property worth money, he does not transgress. The baraita says: one might think even if he has nothing, therefore the verse says with you, implying specifically where he has nothing at all. Sefer HaChinuch of the Raah, mitzvah 588, writes: or he has nothing at all from which to pay. There the Chinuch also writes that if he cannot pay that day unless he would suffer a substantial loss of his own property, it appears that the Torah did not obligate him in that circumstance. See Responsa Mutzal MeEsh, siman 45, and what I wrote in the gloss to Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh Deah, siman 109, seif 1, in the gloss.
