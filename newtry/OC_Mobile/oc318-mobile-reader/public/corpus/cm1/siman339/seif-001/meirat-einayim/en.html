It is a mitzvah to pay a worker's wage on time. This is learned from the verse 'the sun shall not set upon him.' Although withholding wages can involve several prohibitions, the Rambam and poskim emphasize here the prohibition that applies immediately when the payment deadline passes even if the employer still intends to pay. The other prohibitions generally relate to actual withholding. The Ir Shushan's formulation, deriving the immediate violation from 'you shall not oppress,' is therefore imprecise.
Whether he hired a person, etc. This is derived from the verse, 'You shall not oppress a hired worker, poor and needy, from your brothers or from your convert who is in your land within your gates.' The words 'who is in your land' extend the wage laws to hiring a person, animal, or utensil. Regarding rental of land itself, the Tur cites the Ramah, which is the view the Mechaber brings as 'some say,' that the prohibition does not apply, because Scripture says 'in your land,' excluding the land itself.
