Rental of land. Tur cites Rabbi Meir HaLevi Abulafia that the prohibition against delaying wages does not apply to land rental. The verse includes the wage of people, animals, and utensils through the phrase "all that is in your land." Land itself is not something "in your land"; it is the land. However, according to the authorities who treat a house as detached property that was later attached, a house can retain the halachic status of detached property for prohibitory law even if monetary law treats it as land. Rama in siman 95 gives a house the law of land for monetary rules, but prohibitory law can differ. Therefore, regarding the Torah prohibition of delaying payment, there is reason to be stringent and treat rent of a house as rent of detached property according to those authorities. See the Shach in siman 95 and what I wrote there and in siman 317.
