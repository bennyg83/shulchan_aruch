But with respect to wages for land. Ketzot HaChoshen writes that according to the authorities who regard a house as movable property because it was detached and only afterward attached, even though Rama in siman 95 gives a house the legal status of land for monetary law, one may still distinguish between prohibitory law and monetary law. Therefore, with respect to the Torah prohibition of delaying wages, one should be stringent for the view that a house has the status of detached property. Shaar Mishpat is even more stringent and applies the prohibition to actual land. He notes that Semag, negative commandment 181, and Sefer Yereim, mitzvah 263, cite Torat Kohanim that one violates the prohibition of delayed wages even for rental of land, and that this is also the text of Torat Kohanim before us in Parashat Kedoshim. He concludes that one should not be lenient regarding a Torah prohibition. See there, and see Beur HaGra.
