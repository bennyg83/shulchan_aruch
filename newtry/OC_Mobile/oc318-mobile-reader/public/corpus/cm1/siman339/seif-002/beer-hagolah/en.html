(b) The name of section J.B.B.B.B.B.B.A
The baraita there, 111a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara objects that the prohibitions applying by day do not all apply by night and those applying by night do not all apply by day. Rav Chisda answers there that it is one hiring. Rashi explains that one cannot find all of them together; rather, some apply to a day laborer and some to a night laborer. The Maggid Mishneh comments that if this were Rambam's intent, he should have explained it. Kesef Mishneh writes that where he withheld wages by both day and night he violates all of them.
