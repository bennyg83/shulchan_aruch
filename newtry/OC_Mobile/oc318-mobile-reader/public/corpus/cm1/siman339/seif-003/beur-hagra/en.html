And workers, etc. As stated at the end of the preceding discussion.
Although he did not hire him with this in mind. This is the correct reading, as in Sema. The reason is as stated in the Gemara: wages become due only upon completion, and here the workers did not complete their work.
(Collected note) Although he did not hire him with this in mind. Nevertheless, wages become due only at completion even in such a case, as stated there 73a, where Rava said, etc., concerning wages, etc., although they were not hired on that understanding.
