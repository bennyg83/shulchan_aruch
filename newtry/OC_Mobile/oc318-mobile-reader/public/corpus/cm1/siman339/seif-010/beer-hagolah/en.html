Beit Yosef cites Rambam's wording, 'and he accepted, he is exempt,' and the Tur explains that this means he accepted upon himself there to pay him.
The Tur, and Rashi rules so in the Mishnah there. This is also clear from Rif, Tosafot, and Rosh there.
The dispute of Rav Sheshet and Rabbah there, 112a. Rosh writes that the halachah follows Rabbah, because the Mishnah does not treat it as waiver and is precise like him. From here there is proof that waiver does not require a kinyan, for if it did, it would be obvious that he could retract. Nevertheless Rav Sheshet says he cannot retract because he waived, whereas Rabbah says he can retract because it is not considered waiver. Tosafot write likewise there, and Rif also rules like Rabbah.
