Even if there is no, etc. Otherwise Rabbah's ruling that he may retract would be difficult, since a transfer in the presence of all three parties effects acquisition. Rif and the other authorities rule so, as stated in siman 126, seif 2. The Yerushalmi that states, 'this was said, etc.,' follows Rav Sheshet, but we rule like Rabbah, because the Mishna's wording supports him. This is the meaning of, 'nevertheless, etc.' See siman 126.
