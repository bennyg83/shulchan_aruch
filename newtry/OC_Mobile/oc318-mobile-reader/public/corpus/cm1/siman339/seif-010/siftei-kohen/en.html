Or he directed him to collect from a moneychanger, etc. Sefer Chasidim, siman 1171, writes: If you hire a scribe to write for you, stipulate with him so that you will not violate the prohibition of delaying a worker's wages, because perhaps when he demands payment you will not have the money available. Even if you stipulate that you will pay him when he demands it if you are able, this is not a stipulation contrary to Torah law, for the Sages said that a person may stipulate with a worker to feed him meager bread and limited water even though he would otherwise be obligated to feed him like the meal of King Shlomo.
