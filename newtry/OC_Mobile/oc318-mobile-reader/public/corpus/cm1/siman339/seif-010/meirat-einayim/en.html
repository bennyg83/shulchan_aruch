Unless he demanded payment, etc. This is derived from the phrase "it shall not remain with you," meaning that the delay is against the worker's will. If the worker did not demand payment, the delay is with his consent.
Or he demanded it, etc., or he directed him, etc. We expound "with you" to mean where the money is available with you. "With you" excludes a case where the employer directed him to collect from a shopkeeper.
Nevertheless, if the worker wishes. Meaning, even if the shopkeeper has not retracted and remains willing to pay, nevertheless, since the shopkeeper does not actually hold the worker's money and did not become directly obligated to him through ma'amad sheloshtan, the shopkeeper can legally retract if he wishes. Therefore the worker can likewise retract and demand payment from the employer.
