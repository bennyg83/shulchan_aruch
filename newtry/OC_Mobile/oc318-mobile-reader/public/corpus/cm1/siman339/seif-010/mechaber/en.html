An employer violates the prohibition against delaying wages only if the worker demanded payment. If he did not demand it, or demanded it and the employer lacked money, or the employer assigned him to a moneychanger who accepted responsibility to pay, the employer does not violate the prohibition, even if he has no funds with the moneychanger. Nevertheless, the worker may retract and demand payment from the employer, unless a formal acquisition was made, in which case he may not retract.
