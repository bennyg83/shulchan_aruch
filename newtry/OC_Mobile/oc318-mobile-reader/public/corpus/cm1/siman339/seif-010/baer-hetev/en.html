Demanded. Sefer Chassidim, siman 1071, states: If you hire a scribe to write for you, stipulate with him that you will not violate the prohibition of delaying a worker's wages, lest he demand payment when you do not have the money. Even if you make such a condition, pay him when he demands it if you are able. This is not a condition against Torah law, for the Sages said that a person may stipulate with a worker to feed him coarse bread and limited water even though otherwise he would be obligated to feed him like a banquet of Shlomo. To retract. Meaning, even if the storekeeper has not retracted from paying him, nevertheless, since the worker has nothing presently in the storekeeper's hands and no obligation arose through ma'amad sheloshtan, the storekeeper may retract if he wishes. Therefore the worker too may retract. See above, siman 126, seif 2. Sema.
