And if he did not, etc. Rif uses this formulation. Nimukei Yosef infers that if he hired them without qualification, it is as though he said, 'the obligation is upon me.' Tur writes likewise, as is stated below, 'even if he did not, etc.' See the beginning of siman 336. This is the meaning of Tur and Rama's qualification, 'however, etc.,' contrary to Ramah.
