One who says to his agent. Maamar Kiddushin writes that because the Mishnah says "one who says to his agent" rather than "a person may say to his agent," it implies that only after the fact does the employer avoid the formal prohibition, while initially he still violates the prohibition against delay. Tiferet Yisrael likewise implies this and cites Tosafos. Pitchei Teshuva notes that there is no practical novelty here, because Tur and Shulchan Aruch state explicitly in this seif that the employer nevertheless violates "Do not say to your fellow, go and return tomorrow," which is derived from the same Tosafos. Tiferet Yisrael's point was only explanatory, that the Mishnah chose the wording "one who says" in order to allude to that Tosafos.
