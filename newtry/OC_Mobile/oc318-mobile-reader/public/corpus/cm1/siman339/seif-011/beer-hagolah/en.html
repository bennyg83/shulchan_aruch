Rosh writes at the end of chapter 4 of Ketubot that if a worker hired by an unlearned person for a fixed term received clothing from him and leaves before the term, as in the rule that a worker may withdraw even in the middle of the day, the employer gave it only on the assumption that he would complete the term. But if he leaves at the end of the term, since it is known that workers commonly leave when their term ends, the employer should have stipulated; by giving the clothing without qualification he fully transfers it to him. From the margin of the Shulchan Aruch of my master and father.
