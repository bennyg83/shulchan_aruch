Because it is contract work, etc. The Sema explains that a craftsman does not acquire title through the improvement added to an object. In Even HaEzer, siman 28, two opinions are recorded, and because of the possible validity of kiddushin we are stringent for the view that the craftsman does acquire an interest. In monetary law, however, the accepted ruling rejects that view. The Tur's citation of the Rosh is difficult and appears internally inconsistent. In any event, as I explained above in siman 306, the present ruling can apply even according to the opinion that a craftsman acquires through his improvement.
