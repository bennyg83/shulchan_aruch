The language of Rambam in chapter 4 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 40. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the Mishnah there, 87a, established as involving a broker's measuring vessel and located in an alleyway or the buyer's domain, 85b, as stated in the Halakhot. The reason is that the broker lends the measure to them: until it is filled it is lent to the seller, and once filled it is lent to the buyer.
There, law 9. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is a baraita there, 87a.
