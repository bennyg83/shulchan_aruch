If one sells wine or oil in an alley, a joint courtyard, or the purchaser's domain, and the measuring vessel belongs to a broker, the contents remain the seller's until the measure is full. Once it is full, they enter the purchaser's possession and neither party may withdraw. The same applies to produce piled in an alley or joint courtyard when the measuring vessel belongs to neither party: while the seller measures, it remains his until the vessel is full, and once full it belongs to the purchaser.
