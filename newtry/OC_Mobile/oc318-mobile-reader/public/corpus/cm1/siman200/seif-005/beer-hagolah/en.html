The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is explicit in that sugya, 85b: consider the latter clause, in the buyer's domain, etc.; the latter clause concerns the buyer's utensils, etc. Perhaps once we see that the Gemara established it this way, we say that this is the truth according to all views. Rabbi Ibn Migash likewise writes that since the buyer seeks to remove property from the seller, if the domain or utensils belong to the seller, the buyer does not acquire.
