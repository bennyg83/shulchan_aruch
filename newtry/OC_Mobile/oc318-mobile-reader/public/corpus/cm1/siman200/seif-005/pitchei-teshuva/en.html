And some say that he did not acquire, and therefore if he did not pay the money, and so forth. See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, siman 37, who writes that this wording implies that if the purchaser did pay the money, he cannot retract, because the seller is in possession and may say, "I rely upon the view that the latter expression governs." Yet in Bechor, from the wording alone it appears that even where the money was advanced, the purchaser may retract. This accords with the Tur and not with Rashba cited by Beit Yosef, as Rama writes in Darkei Moshe, letter 4. However, this proof can be rejected, because perhaps Rama himself was uncertain and merely stated the case in which the result is unquestionably so.
