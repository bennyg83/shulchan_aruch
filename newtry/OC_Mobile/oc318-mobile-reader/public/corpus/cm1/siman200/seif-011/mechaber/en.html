If a person takes an article from an artisan's shop to inspect it, and its price is fixed, he is liable if it is lost through an unavoidable accident. Since the price is fixed, the article enters his responsibility when he lifts it, and the seller can no longer withdraw. This applies when he lifts it with intent to acquire the entire item and the item is desirable to him. If it is an item the seller is eager to dispose of and is actively seeking to sell, it remains in the seller's responsibility until the price is fixed and the purchaser lifts it after the price was fixed. If the purchaser indicates that he does not want it, and it is stolen or lost before he returns it, some say he is a paid custodian, while others say he is an unpaid custodian.
