Provided he lifts it, etc. As stated there, “he selected and placed, etc.” He did not acquire even though he lifted all of them and intended to buy from among them, because he had not yet chosen to acquire all of them, only one particular item. This is why the Gemara asks from the fact that he completed it in his mind, etc.; at the time of lifting he had not yet directed his intent to this particular one.
