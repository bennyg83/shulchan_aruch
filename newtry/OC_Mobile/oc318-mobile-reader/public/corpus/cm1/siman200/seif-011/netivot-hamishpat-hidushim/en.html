That he lift it. This is not literal; the same applies to other valid methods of acquisition. Sema:
In order to acquire. This follows the Rambam. Tosafot and the Rosh hold that even if he did not lift it with intent to acquire, he is liable even for unavoidable accidents so long as he did not reveal that he did not want the purchase. Sema:
Like a paid custodian. This is because he benefits from having the option either to keep the goods or return them. It requires study why the Mechaber, Tur, and Rama did not cite the Rashba and Rosh, who hold that he is liable even for unavoidable accidents. Sema.
