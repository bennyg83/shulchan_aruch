There are those who disagree and hold that if he measured. The Tur cites Raavad that even if the purchaser himself measured goods while they were on animals, he does not acquire merely through that measurement, because his intention was only to measure and not to perform meshichah or acquisition. The Tur says this is not persuasive. Mishneh LaMelech notes that in Yoreh Deah the Tur rules that when a gentile measures wine, merely lifting it in the course of measurement does not acquire because the lifting was done only for measurement. That appears to follow Raavad's reasoning. The distinction and fuller analysis are discussed in siman 275, seif katan 4.
