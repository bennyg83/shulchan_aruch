This too is there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam apparently derived it from the explanation that the husband was a seller of baskets, etc. Rambam explains that this husband sold baskets and sold this basket to his wife, so he does not object to its place. The same applies to every comparable case. Although that explanation is not maintained regarding a bill of divorce, since we rule like Rabbi Yochanan that in every case a husband does not object to the place of his wife's basket, it remains relevant to another case. The Maggid Mishneh adds that he later found this explanation in the words of Rabbi Ibn Migash.
