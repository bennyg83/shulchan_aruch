[Rama] If he did not give. Note: See the Rashba cited in the Beit Yosef, that if he gave money, then even with a firstborn, each one acquires successively. This is also the view of the Ritva cited in Shittah Mekubetzet. Darkei Moshe writes that the Tur disagrees. See Rabbeinu Yerucham, who cites a proof for the Rashba's position.
