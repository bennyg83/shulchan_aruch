And likewise with other methods of acquisition. Darkei Moshe writes at the beginning of this siman that the rules stated in siman 190, where the seller gives money to the purchaser and says that through receiving the money the purchaser should acquire the field, apply likewise to movables where relevant. The Beit Yosef writes there that an important person can acquire in this manner, and the same applies to movables. In my view all the distinctions of that law apply equally to land and movables, so the Tur did not need to repeat them here and relied upon what he had written there. The Beit Yosef similarly writes at the beginning of siman 204 that the laws of acquisition of land teach the corresponding rules for movables.
