And likewise with other modes of acquisition. See Sema, seif katan 32, citing Darkei Moshe. I am puzzled because Beit Yosef at the beginning of siman 190 cites Ran that in the case of a distinguished person, receipt of the benefit is comparable to chalipin and therefore acquires movable property completely. By contrast, the other modes listed at the beginning of siman 190, when used with movable property, do no more than money and create only mi shepara. Sema's broad statement therefore requires qualification.
