One person, etc. This is like acquisition through another person in the transfer of money in siman 190, a formal kinyan in siman 195, one who lifts a found object for another, property deposited in another's possession, an instruction to deliver a deed of emancipation, 'bring one hundred to so-and-so,' and the many passages stating that one may confer rights upon others, because one may acquire a benefit for a person in his absence.
