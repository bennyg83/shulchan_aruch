Seif 2: Until he draws them. This is surprising, because meshichah does not acquire in this setting, as all authorities state. The Rambam himself writes that the buyer must lift the produce or remove it from the seller's premises by renting the place. With difficulty, one could interpret “until he draws them” to mean until he removes them from the seller's domain. It appears, however, that the text contains a scribal error and should follow the Rambam's wording.
