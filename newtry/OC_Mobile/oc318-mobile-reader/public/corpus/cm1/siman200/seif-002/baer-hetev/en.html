Until he pulls them. This is difficult, because meshichah does not effect acquisition in this case, as all the poskim state. Rambam himself writes: until he lifts the produce or removes it from the seller's domain by renting the place. With difficulty one might say that "until he pulls them" means until he removes them from the seller's domain. In my view there is a scribal error and the text should read as Rambam states. Taz.
