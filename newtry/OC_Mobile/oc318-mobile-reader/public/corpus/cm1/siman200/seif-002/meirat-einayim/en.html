The Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe explain that according to the Ramban, if the custodian accepts that his domain should now be regarded as the buyer's domain, this does not help unless he accepts that status in the seller's presence or by the seller's instruction. If the buyer merely tells the custodian, "So and so sold me the produce in your possession," and the custodian agrees to give it to him, the buyer does not acquire until the seller instructs the custodian and the custodian accepts on the basis of that instruction.
