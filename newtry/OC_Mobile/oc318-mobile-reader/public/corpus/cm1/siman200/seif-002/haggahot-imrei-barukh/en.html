(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 8) s.v. 'According to this it is difficult,' etc. Note: There is no difficulty at all, for Rashi there states explicitly: 'He placed his vessel in the air and received the disks into it.' Thus it is specifically where the vessel is suspended in the air and there is no possibility of acquisition except through the airspace of his courtyard that the airspace is effective.
