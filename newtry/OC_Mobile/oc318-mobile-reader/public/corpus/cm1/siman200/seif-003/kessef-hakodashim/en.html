The seller cannot retract. One had an article in his possession that was about to be sold to another. They fixed the price, and the seller told the buyer to give the money to a certain person. Before the money was delivered, the seller retracted. They do not remember whether the seller expressly said, “Acquire it and give the money,” or whether no language of acquisition was used. Nevertheless this appears to be the case discussed in siman 200:11. See there. This requires further study.
