Domain. Meaning, for example, a simta or a courtyard belonging to both of them. Ibid.
Measured. Meaning, the seller measured the goods and placed them into the purchaser's utensils standing in a simta. If the purchaser himself measured them, he acquires even without using his utensils, because he pulled them while measuring, as stated in seif 7 in the gloss. See there. Ibid.
The seller. Meaning, this excludes the public domain, where the seller's statement, 'Go and acquire,' is ineffective. So writes the Sema. The Shach writes that the same applies if the seller gave him permission to place the utensil there, in which case he acquires. The Tur writes this in the name of Rabbeinu Yitzchak Migash, contrary to the Ramah. The Bach rules accordingly, and his words are correct, unlike what is written in Bedek HaBayit. See Imrei Binah, folio 94b. Shach.
