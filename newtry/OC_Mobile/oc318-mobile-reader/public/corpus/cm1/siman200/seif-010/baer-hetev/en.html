One. The Taz writes that this cannot mean either the seller's vessel or the buyer's vessel, because then the seller's vessel would be superior to a third party's vessel. With a third party's vessel, acquisition requires that the vessel be filled, whereas with the seller's vessel merely marking off the measure would suffice. Rather, it appears that the Rambam did not wish to depart from the Gemara's wording, which says, "If the measuring vessel belonged to one of them," and all the commentators explain that it refers to the buyer. The Raavad likewise comments on the Rambam's words "one of them": "It means the buyer." This shows that he came to explain, not to disagree. Accordingly, when the Rama writes, "And some say that if the measure...," there is in fact no dispute, because the Rambam also agrees. Taz.
