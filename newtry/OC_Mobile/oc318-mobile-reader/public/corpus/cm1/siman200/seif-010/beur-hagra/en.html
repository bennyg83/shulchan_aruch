If it was, etc. Even if the measure belonged to the seller, as above; and if it belonged to the buyer, acquisition occurs only at the marked divisions.
Some say, etc. According to Rashbam’s explanation, and they do not require marked divisions. This is also the Raavad’s view and that of the other poskim. Hence the Raavad writes: “The first portion is acquired successively, even in a side alley,” meaning each portion is acquired even before reaching a marked division, following the Rashbam’s reading that the seller said “a hin at a time, etc.”
