The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is how the Gemara explains the matter regarding Rav and Shmuel above. He explains that the seller sold each marked unit separately. Otherwise it would resemble a kor for thirty, where each first unit is not acquired separately. The Maggid Mishneh wonders how the buyer can acquire in the seller's measuring vessel, since a complete Mishnah teaches that if the seller measured but the buyer did not perform meshichah, he does not acquire. Perhaps Rambam understands that Mishnah as applying specifically to a kor for thirty, where the measurement process had not been completed.
