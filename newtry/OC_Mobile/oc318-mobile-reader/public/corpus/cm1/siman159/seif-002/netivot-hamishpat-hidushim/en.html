But between roof and roof. This is the Rosh's approach. Abaye's rule that two roofs on opposite sides of the public domain require a parapet is because each owner does not sense the other's arrival and is therefore exposed to visual intrusion. Rav Nachman's statement that between one roof and another no parapet is required refers to adjacent roofs, where each can guard himself from private use when he sees the other owner on the roof. There is no distinction based upon whether the roof is ordinarily used for dwelling.
