VA. There's a name in the second:
There is no distinction, etc. This is stated in the Gemara. The ruling emphasizes the opposite of the wording in seif 1, 'even if they were,' etc. According to that interpretation it applies specifically where they are far apart. Therefore it says 'there is no distinction,' meaning that the novelty concerns even airspace of a private domain, as the Gemara explains.
