This differs from ordinary members of the public domain. So writes the Sema. The Taz writes that this is difficult in light of siman 154, seif 3, where a person may open an entrance toward the public domain because he may say, "I am no different from any member of the public domain." Why can the neighbor not answer that the public do not see him at night? One must say that the primary reason here is that the public do not see him when he is sitting, while the other reasons were stated only for added support. See Responsa Re'em, siman 8.
