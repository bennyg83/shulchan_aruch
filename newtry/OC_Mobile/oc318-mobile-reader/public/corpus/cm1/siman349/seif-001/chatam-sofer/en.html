Mechaber, seif 3, 'to another.' Note: See Rashi's explanation of the incident in which Rav Pappa compelled Rafram, in chapter HaChovel. See also what the Rosh writes regarding the rule that one does not impose an oath on the claims of a deaf person, an incompetent person, or a minor, in chapter HaDayanim. See Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chayyim, siman 343, at the end. This also appears in the rulings of Mahara, siman 62, who cites the Rosh in chapter HaChovel, siman 9; see there. It appears that monetary law is different, for in Bava Metzia 71a we say that he should go after him so that they will leave him, even though he is not careful regarding the prohibition of charging interest.
