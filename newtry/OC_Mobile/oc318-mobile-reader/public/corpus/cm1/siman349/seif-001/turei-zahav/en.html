[Seif 3] Even after he reaches adulthood. The Gemara in Bava Kamma 98b relates that Rafram compelled Rav Ashi and collected from him “like a beam fit for carving.” Rashi explains that Rav Ashi had burned a document in his youth, and Rafram collected from him the entire amount written in the document, with full-quality land, like a beam selected from other beams for carving images. One would then have to say that Rav Ashi was not literally a minor but merely young, and that Rashi meant to defend him by saying that he committed such an act in his youth, before becoming a Torah scholar. The primary explanation, however, is like Rashi in Ketubot 86a on a similar expression: the case came before Rav Ashi, and Rafram surrounded him with proofs and arguments until Rav Ashi ruled that the claimant should collect in full. The same may be understood here concerning the law of one who burns another's documents.
