He must swear. See Baer Heitev, which states that he must swear that he was not negligent even though he never accepted custodial responsibility. Apparently Baer Heitev did not see Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit, who expresses surprise: if the person is not a custodian, why should negligence matter at all? See Bach, Ketzot HaChoshen seif katan 2, Shaar Mishpat at the end of siman 96, and what I wrote above in siman 291 seif 2, seif katan 1.
