The wording of the Tur in the name of Rambam there, law 10. The Maggid Mishneh writes that he did not find this explicitly stated, but it appears obvious. Even according to the view that when a minor eats forbidden food beit din need not separate him, that applies to transgressions between man and Hashem. With transgressions between one person and another, they certainly separate him so that harm not result through him.
