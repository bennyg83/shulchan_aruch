(Supplement) A minor, etc., if the object remains intact, etc. This follows Bava Metzia 102a. The objection there is only that the minor's taking is not full-fledged robbery. Therefore, where the father did not send him, liability is limited, but if he sent him, even a minor must return the object because it is robbery. See Bava Kamma 112a-b. For this reason the Mishna speaks specifically of bodily injury, and the same applies to a woman or slave.
(Supplement) They return it, etc. Even where the original taking is prohibited only for the sake of peaceful relations, the Gemara still requires return, as shown in Bava Metzia 102a, the end of Chullin, and the opening of the final chapter of Bava Kamma. (End of supplement.)
