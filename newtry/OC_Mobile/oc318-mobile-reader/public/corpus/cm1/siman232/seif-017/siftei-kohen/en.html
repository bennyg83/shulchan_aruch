See the Sema, seif katan 36, through the words, 'Nevertheless, it is not comparable to that case, because there the doubt arose while the item was in his possession,' etc. This is straightforward and is not a matter of unresolved law. This appears to me to be the principal ruling. Examine carefully. See Responsa Maharashdam, siman 80, concerning these laws, and see further there, siman 379. See also the Sema, seif katan 37, through the words, 'when the purchaser comes to take an oath,' etc. Responsa Maharashdam, siman 85, labored to explain this wording.
