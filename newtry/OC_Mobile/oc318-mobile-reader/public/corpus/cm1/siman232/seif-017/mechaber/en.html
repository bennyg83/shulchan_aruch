If Reuven sold Shimon a skin container of oil, and Shimon did not open it but relied on the seller's statement that it was good, and upon opening found it cloudy, the seller swears that he delivered good oil as agreed. If the seller refuses to swear, the purchaser swears that the agreement was for good, clear oil and that this is the oil delivered. He may then return the oil, or the diminished value is appraised and, if he wishes to keep it, the seller refunds the difference.
