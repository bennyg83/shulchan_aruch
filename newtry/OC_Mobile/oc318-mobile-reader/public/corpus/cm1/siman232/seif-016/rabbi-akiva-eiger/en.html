[Rama] Some say that the purchaser may say. Note: Since both the majority and the presumption support him. So writes the Ramah, and the Bach likewise writes that this is not precise, for the same rule applies where they are evenly balanced, since possession of the money is stronger. See siman 23. The Derishah, however, distinguishes that here, because he does not return the seed itself, both a majority and a presumption are required.
