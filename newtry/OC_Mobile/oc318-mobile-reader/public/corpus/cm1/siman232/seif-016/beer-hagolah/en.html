Kaf) The Tur, seif 14, in the name of a responsum of the Rosh. It is learned from the law of one who sells a cow to another, the buyer slaughters it, and it is found to be a tereifah, explained in seif 51.
Lamed) This is the correct reading, as appears in the Tur, Beit Yosef, and the responsa.
Mem) It is explained at the end of seif 11 that he must return the money if it is still in his possession, as stated there in the name of Tosafot. This is also the view of the Beit Yosef. The Sema explains the matter differently; the reader should evaluate the approaches.
