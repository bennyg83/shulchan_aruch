Provided that the money is in excess, etc. The Darchei Moshe writes that if the giver later comes and says, "I erred in counting the money I gave you," Rabbeinu Yerucham rules that the recipient must return the excess, and the Rosh writes similarly. The Hagahot Mordechai in Perek Eizehu Neshech writes that he need not return it. The Sema also cites this dispute. The Shiltei Gibborim at the end of Perek HaGozel Batra rules that it must be returned. He further writes that where one lends money to another but does not demand repayment, the borrower is not obligated to pay on his own initiative, for since the lender does not demand it, he evidently intended to give it as a gift. This requires study.
