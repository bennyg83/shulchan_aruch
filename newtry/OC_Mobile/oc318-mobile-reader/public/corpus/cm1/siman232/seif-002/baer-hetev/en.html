All of them. Meaning, if six or eight extra coins were found, we do not say that five of them should be returned as a likely counting error in fives while the remainder may be retained. Rather, just as the extra amount beyond five is treated as a gift, so the five are also treated as a gift. Sema.
A gift. Shiltei HaGibborim at the end of the chapter HaGozel Batra writes that if a lender does not demand repayment, the borrower is not obligated to pay on his own, because by not demanding it the lender intended to give it to him as a gift. This requires study. Shach.
Counts. Meaning, people who count money commonly place one dinar aside each time they complete a group of ten or five, as a marker of how many tens or fives they have counted. Therefore, if the number of extra dinarim corresponds to the number of tens or fives counted, they are certainly those marker coins that were accidentally mixed back in, and they must be returned. Sema.
