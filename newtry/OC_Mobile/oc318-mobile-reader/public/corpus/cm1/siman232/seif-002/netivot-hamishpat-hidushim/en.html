Rather, we say that all of it was given as a gift. If the giver later comes and says, I erred in the calculation, the poskim dispute whether it must be returned (Sema and Shach). He further writes that where one lent money to another and does not demand repayment, the borrower is not obligated to pay him, for we say that he gave it to him as a gift.
