Dalet) A statement of Rav Nachman at the end of the fifth chapter of Bava Metzia 63b.
Heh) This is the language of the Tur, and appears there in Rav Nachman's statement.
Vav) The Mechaber copies Rashi's explanation there.
Meaning, there were fifty-five extra. Since he counted five at a time, saying once five, twice five, and so on, he erred by ten groups between forty times five and fifty times five, an error of fifty, and then made another error when he counted four groups of five but thought he had counted only three, creating an additional error of five.
Zayin) Rambam likewise writes in chapter 4 of Hilkhot Malveh, halachah 10. The Bach cites the Rosh in Eizehu Neshekh that specifically if the one who overpaid does not come to claim it, the recipient need not return the money because he may rely on the possibility that it was intentionally included. But if the payer comes and says, "I made a counting error," the recipient must return it. The Bach infers this from the Tur, as does the Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbenu Yerucham, and rules that this is primary.
Chet) Tosafot write at the beginning of 67a, and the Maggid Mishneh cites an authority who writes similarly. Hagahot Ashri cites Rabbenu Yitzchak that nowadays one must always return such an overpayment, even if the payer did not demand it. The Bach cites this view.
