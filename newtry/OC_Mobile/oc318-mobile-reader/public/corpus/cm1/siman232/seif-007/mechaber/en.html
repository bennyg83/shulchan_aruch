A purchaser without qualification is presumed to buy an item complete and free of defects. Even if the seller says, "On condition that you shall have no claim against me for a defect," the purchaser may rescind unless the seller specifies the particular defect and the purchaser waives it, or says, "I accept every defect that may be found in this item that reduces its value by up to such-and-such an amount." A waiver is effective only when the person knows what he is waiving, just as with a specified waiver of overcharge. Rama: If one sells a wooden utensil while calling it gold, but the purchaser can see it, the seller may say, "I meant that it is as good as gold."
