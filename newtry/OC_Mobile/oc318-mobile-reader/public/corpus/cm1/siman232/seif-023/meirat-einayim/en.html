If the price provides evidence, etc. The Tur writes that although we rule like the Sages that price ordinarily does not prove the subject of the sale, that is specifically regarding a yoke and oxen, where the price would contradict the primary meaning of the words, because in common speech a yoke without oxen is itself called a yoke. Here, however, whether the ox was intended for plowing or slaughter, it is called an ox by everyone. Therefore the price may show what was intended. See the Perishah.
