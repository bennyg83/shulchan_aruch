(Mechaber, seif 5) And he will uphold the sale, the law is with Reuven. Note: See above, siman 230; Sema there, seif katan 14; Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot Mechirah 15:3; and Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer 117, seif katan 14. It appears simple that this rule applies only where the buyer was able to inspect the item at the time of sale. Since he did not care to inspect it, he revealed that the defect was not significant to him. The wording "he could inspect it immediately" is exact. This answers the Mishneh LaMelech. It also requires study because seif 7 states that even when the seller says explicitly, "on condition that...," the buyer may retract unless the particular defect is specified.
