He may retract forever. Meaning, the seller must make up the deficiency. This applies specifically where the deficiency can be supplied. But if he sold him land and said it was twenty cubits long, and it is deficient by half a cubit in circumstances where he cannot add land from the adjoining area, the entire sale is void, as in siman 218, seifim 7 and 8. This is unlike one who says, "a kor of earth measured by the rope," where the deficiency is deducted from the price, because there he expressly stated that the sale was according to measurement. Sema.
