[Sema, letter 2] And he has nothing with which to complete it. Note: But the Rashba and Ri Migash hold that even where completion is impossible, he acquires and the erroneous amount is returned, because an acquisition can take effect partially. The same applies where one sells a field and half is discovered to have been stolen. See the Rashba cited by the Beit Yosef, siman 200, which I copied in my gloss there at seif 7 in the Rama. See above, siman 182, seif 8; Mishneh LaMelech at the end of Hilchot Zechiyah; and Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Zechiyah, siman 1.
