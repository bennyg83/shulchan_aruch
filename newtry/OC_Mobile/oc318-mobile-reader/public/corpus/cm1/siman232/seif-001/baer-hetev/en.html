Forever. Meaning, even after a long time. This is unlike ona'ah, where the buyer is limited to the time needed to show the item to a merchant or relative, while the seller's period lasts only until a comparable sale comes his way, as explained in siman 227. Here the error is objectively known, and the case resembles the return of stolen property, which has no time limit. So writes the Sema. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 385.
