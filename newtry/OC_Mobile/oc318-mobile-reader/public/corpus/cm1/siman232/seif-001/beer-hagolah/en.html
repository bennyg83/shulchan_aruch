Alef) This is the language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 15 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, from Rabbah's statement at the beginning of the second chapter of Kiddushin 42b: any error in measure, weight, or count must be corrected, even if less than the amount of ona'ah. The Maggid Mishneh cites Rav Yosef ibn Migash that "must be corrected" means the transaction remains valid and the deficiency is returned. Such an error never causes cancellation of the sale, even if greater than one sixth, because the mistake is not in the agreed value itself.
Bet) This is explicit in Rabbah's statement above.
Gimel) It is explained at the end of the first chapter of Gittin 14a, in the incident involving the gardeners, that any kinyan made in error is reversed.
