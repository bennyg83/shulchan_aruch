Some disagree, etc. See Baer HaGolah. It appears to me that Rambam also explains the passage this way. The Gemara's incident involved a cattle keeper, whereas Rambam and Shulchan Aruch discuss the purchaser himself placing the animal among his herd. Rama's words therefore require study, as Bach also writes. Perhaps Tur and Rama distinguish a hired keeper, who must exercise enhanced care, from the purchaser himself, who is not a paid guardian because the sale was mistaken. See seif 21. This still requires study.
