There is no, etc. As stated in the first chapter of Yom Tov 7a: what practical difference, etc. The same applies where one sold an ox and it proved to be a goring ox, at the beginning of chapter 5 of Bava Kamma and chapter 6 of Bava Batra, according to Rav, who rules that it is a mistaken sale, even if the price of meat rose. Likewise in Bava Batra 83b, the purchaser may retract even if the item rose in value, as the Gemara explains, and the same applies to the concluding clause. See Baer HaGolah. Reference 81.
Likewise, if, etc. See Tosafot there, 84a, s.v. If, and Riva's explanation. In such a case they said in Bava Metzia 50b that where the discrepancy exceeds one-sixth either party may retract. The same applies to every mistaken sale.
