"I want the object intact." This is not comparable to ordinary restitution of ona'ah. There, the object purchased is itself complete and free of defect; the problem is only that he paid more than its value. Here, the object itself is deficient.
The seller has the option, etc. According to what the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 227 in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, that so long as the injured party has not revealed that he wishes to maintain the sale and we therefore presume he may retract, the party who caused the ona'ah likewise has a right to retract, it appears to me that the same rule applies here where the purchased object contains a defect. See the Perishah.
