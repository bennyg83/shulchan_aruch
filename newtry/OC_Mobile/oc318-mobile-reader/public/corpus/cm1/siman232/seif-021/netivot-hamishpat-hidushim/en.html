And if he did not notify him. See Beurim: if the purchaser is from another place, or if this type of merchandise is ordinarily transported to another place, it is considered as though he notified him. The same applies where one seized an item from another in order to take it to another place, if he did not intend to steal it for himself but only to damage the other person. Likewise, one who relocated to another place must return what he seized, or a borrower must bring repayment, at his own expense, to the other person's location.
