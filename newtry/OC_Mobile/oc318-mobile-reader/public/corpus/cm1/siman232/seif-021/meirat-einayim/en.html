Even if it was lost or stolen, it is, etc. The Rambam, and the Tur quoting him, write: "If it was lost or stolen after he notified him, it is, etc." The Mechaber intentionally omits those words because in the Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh he explains them to mean after the buyer informed the seller that he intended to transport the item to another country. On that reading the context itself already makes the point clear. In the Perishah I wrote that the main interpretation is different: "after he notified him" means after the buyer told the seller that he was rescinding the purchase because of the defect. Otherwise the seller can say, "Had you told me you were rescinding, I would immediately have sent for my merchandise and it would not have been stolen." Accordingly, before notice, loss or theft is ordinarily upon the buyer. If it was stolen before the buyer had any reasonable opportunity to notify the seller, perhaps the Rambam holds that it remains at the seller's risk. The Rambam is not discussing that precise situation here. The Beit Yosef rejects this explanation, but in the Derishah I showed that it is the Rambam's view.
He is liable only for the expenses of returning it. The Tur, in the name of the Ramah, adds that the actual burden of arranging the return falls upon the purchaser. The Mechaber omits that addition because the Rambam disagrees, as appears from the preceding ruling that the seller cannot say, "Return my merchandise here." Rather, the seller must arrange to retrieve it or sell it where it is. According to that view, the Mechaber should not have written that the seller is liable "only for the expenses of return," since the effort itself also falls upon him. He merely followed the Ramah's language in the part with which he agreed. It is surprising that the Ir Shushan writes two contradictory rules in the same discussion. He first follows the Rambam and says that the seller must arrange to bring back defective merchandise taken to another country, but then follows the Ramah and says that the seller pays only the expenses while the buyer must perform the work of returning it.
