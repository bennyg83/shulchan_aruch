[Shulchan Aruch] But if he is found to be a thief. Note: See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 1, siman 174, that this applies specifically to a Canaanite slave, in whom all such bad traits are presumed. But regarding a Jewish servant it does not apply; rather, it is considered a defect and the sale is rescinded, and the purchaser need not pay the value of an idle laborer.
