Because he did not mention it to him, etc. See Sema, seif katan 19. His inference that this is certainly true where he did not find all of them is correct, for since he found only some of them, the inference of waiver is even stronger. The novelty is that even if he found all of them, one might have thought that he would not waive so substantial a matter; therefore the ruling teaches otherwise. This is contrary to the Mechaber's understanding, and it is clear.
