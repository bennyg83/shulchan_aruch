If he used the item. Where the defect consists of price fraud or another defect and the buyer continued using the item after learning of it, a migo does not help him. The Shach's rule in siman 227, that after the time needed to show the item to a merchant one may have a migo of "I paid," does not overcome the fact that the buyer may prefer his own measure of produce and should have returned the item. This is certainly so where he could have notified beit din and obtained permission regarding property that was no longer rightfully his.
