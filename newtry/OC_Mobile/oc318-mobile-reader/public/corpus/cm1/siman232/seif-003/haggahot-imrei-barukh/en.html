Shach, seif katan 15: but if he claims, 'I do not know whether they are mine,' etc. Note: See Responsa Panim Meirot, part 1, siman 60, which rejects the reasoning that this is like one who says, 'I do not know whether I repaid you.' One of his proofs is from the law of a ring sold on the assumption that it was gold, cited here in the Rama, where if he does not believe him he swears and is exempt. See what I wrote in the margin on the cited Shach and how I rejected that proof.
