Likewise if he was found to be an armed robber, etc. See Beer HaGolah and Bava Batra 92b. The Tosefta there, chapter 4, states explicitly that if a slave is an armed robber or is registered for seizure by the government, the sale is a mistaken sale. See Tosafos there, Kiddushin, and Kesubos 58.
