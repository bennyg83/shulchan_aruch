Kuf) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is straightforward. The earlier baraita in the sixth chapter of Bava Metzia 80a states that insanity, epilepsy, and mental disturbance are defects. In the ninth chapter of Gittin 86a the Gemara likewise says, by way of additional precaution, that a deed should state that the slave is free of boils and tzaraat. The rule is straightforward.
Reish) A baraita cited at the end of Bava Batra 92b. From the sugya at the beginning of Kiddushin 11b concerning an armed robber or a person against whom the government has issued a warrant, the commentators explain that if such a condition existed, it would have become publicly known. Therefore there is no reason to suspect it absent evidence. There the discussion concerns terumah with respect to a slave purchased by a Kohen from an Israelite. This resolves the doubt raised in the Raavad's gloss. The Maggid Mishneh writes that he later found the same rule in the Tosefta. Tosafot in Kiddushin likewise write this in the name of Rabbenu Tam.
