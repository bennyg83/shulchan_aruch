[Shulchan Aruch] He caused himself the loss. Note: This is because the purchaser is retroactively deemed an unpaid bailee in every case of a mistaken transaction. If so, he was negligent in failing to inspect it. Terumat HaDeshen writes likewise. See Derishah, who writes that he is considered a paid bailee, following the Rosh's view that in every mistaken transaction the possessor is retroactively a paid bailee. See Tumim, siman 72, seif katan 22.
