There in the name of the Ramban. The Ran's words also appear to follow this view. The same is evident from Rambam, chapter 16, law 6, where he writes that if it is known with certainty that the animal was already a tereifah when purchased, the buyer returns it. Since he writes without qualification that it was found to be a tereifah, this implies that the same rule applies to every form of tereifah.
