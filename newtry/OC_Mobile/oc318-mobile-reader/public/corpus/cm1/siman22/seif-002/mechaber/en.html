One who accepts the testimony of a non-Jew is like one who accepts the testimony of any otherwise disqualified witness. If he accepts a non-Jew as judge, however, even a formal act of acquisition has no effect, and it is forbidden to litigate before him. {Rama: If the case was already adjudicated before him, the party may not retract his acceptance (Mordechai, beginning of chapter Zeh Borer).}
