There: “It was not known that they erred” If one accepted an ignorant judge who knows no Torah law, he cannot retract even when that judge erred, because he cannot claim he accepted him only to render true law. Mordechai rules similarly regarding one who accepted a gentile judge, even after noticing distortion and bribery. Since an ordinary gentile is not presumed to know Torah law, the acceptance was unconditional. The same applies to one known to be an ignoramus.
