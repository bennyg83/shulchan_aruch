If he already adjudicated. Shach disagrees. Since the kinyan itself is invalid, even completion of the judgment cannot validate it. He distinguishes between accepting the substantive laws of gentiles, which is prohibited, and accepting adjudication before a particular gentile, which in his view may be different because the parties merely trust that individual. He concludes that the matter requires study. It nevertheless appears that even adjudication before a particular gentile is prohibited even with both parties' consent, because this gives honor to idolatrous judicial authority.
