They give both of them disciplinary lashes. The Tur writes that he is lashed, but she does not become prohibited merely because she secluded herself with one whose intercourse would prohibit her. This is what Kiddushin 81a means when it states that a woman is not prohibited because of seclusion, according to Tosafot there, s.v. 'they lash him,' as cited by the Beit Yosef here.
