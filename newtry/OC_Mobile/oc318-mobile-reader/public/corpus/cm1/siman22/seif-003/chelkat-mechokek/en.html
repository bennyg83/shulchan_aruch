And they publicly announce concerning them. Tur does not mention this announcement. Rashi in Kiddushin explains that the announcement concerns a married woman, and is made for her benefit, to clarify that she did not commit adultery and that the lashes are only for seclusion. The entire language of this seif in the Mechaber is copied from Rambam, chapter 22 of Hilchot Issurei Biah, halachah 3, and the order of his words implies that he did not explain like Rashi. Rather, the announcement refers only to an unmarried woman and is likewise for her benefit.
