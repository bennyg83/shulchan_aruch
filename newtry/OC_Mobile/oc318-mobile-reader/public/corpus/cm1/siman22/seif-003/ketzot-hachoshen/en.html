He cannot retract. See Beit Yosef, who writes that if the defendant denies having accepted the other person as credible, he must swear that he never said so. The Shach objects: since the claimant cannot collect except through an oath, we do not impose an oath regarding the alleged acceptance itself, because this is not a denial of money. He brings proof from Baal HaItur concerning a single witness when the parties agreed to reverse the oath. See what I wrote in siman 91, seif 6, resolving the Shach's difficulty.
