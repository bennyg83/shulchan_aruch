Seif 1. “And if he denies it.” Note: See the Sema, who writes that an oath has no relevance here, and the Shach who challenges him. According to one of the Shach’s own explanations earlier, however, the Sema can be defended. The reason retraction is ineffective is that it is not known with certainty that the judges erred. If they did not err, there is no monetary consequence to the litigant’s acceptance and therefore no subject for an oath. If they did err, their judgment ought to be void independently, so again the litigant’s prior acceptance creates no relevant monetary issue to be established by oath. Thus there is no place for an oath here. Examine this carefully.
