a) The wording of the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot Sanhedrin, from the Mishnah, Sanhedrin 24a.
b) There, according to Rashi's explanation that Rav Dimi son of Rav Nachman refers to Rabbi Meir's view.
c) Tur, based on Tosafot there, who explain that Rav Dimi, etc., refers to the statement of the Sages. So too write the Rosh and the other poskim, and the Beit Yosef writes that this also appears to be the view of the Rif.
d) According to Rav Ashi's formulation, who said, "This is what he sent him," etc.
e) As they sent from the academy of Rav to Shmuel there, 24b.
f) Like the Sages in the Mishnah there, and as Rav Nachman bar Rav Chisda sent, etc. It appears there that regarding "I forgive you" before the judgment is concluded, the Sages do not disagree, as the Rosh writes there. Although the Rambam concludes there, "and money was extracted by this invalid judgment," the Kesef Mishneh has already demonstrated that the meaning is that once judgment was concluded it is regarded as though money had been extracted. The Rosh writes in Responsa, rule concerning judges, siman 10, that this applies even if both litigants consented. The Beit Yosef explains that the reason is the honor of the judge. From siman 66.
g) Responsa Maimoniyot relating to Hilkhot Sanhedrin, and the other poskim, from siman 61.
Meaning, if they accepted them for judgment and they made a compromise or some other error, as is evident from the responsum there. Sema.
h) Tur, and so writes the Rosh in Responsa, rule 26, siman 1.
