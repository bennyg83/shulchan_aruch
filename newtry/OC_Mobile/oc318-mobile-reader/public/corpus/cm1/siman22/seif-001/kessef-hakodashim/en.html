Seif 3. And he left the beit din. We rule that one who says, "Swear and I will pay," or makes a similar procedural commitment, cannot retract after leaving beit din. It appears that the same is true even if he did not leave on his own but the judges instructed him to step outside, as is customary while they deliberate. He could have said before leaving that his exit should not be treated as final acceptance of what he had previously stated. Since he left without such a reservation, the rule of having left beit din may apply. This point nevertheless requires further study.
