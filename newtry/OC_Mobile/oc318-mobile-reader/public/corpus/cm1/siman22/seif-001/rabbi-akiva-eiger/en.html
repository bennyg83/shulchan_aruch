[Rama] If they accepted him as two. Note: The Perishah implies that this is specifically where he expressly accepted the disqualified judge as equivalent to two judges. But if he accepted him without qualification, and certainly if he expressly accepted the qualified judge as equivalent to two, it can be said that this is not considered two disadvantages. Although he accepted the disqualified judge as a judge and also accepted the qualified judge as equivalent to two, nevertheless, since the two disadvantages do not attach to the same matter, the acceptance is effective.
