And one. The Shach writes that Baal HaMaor, in chapter HaGozel Etzim and chapter HaMafkid, rules like Rabbi Tarfon that the robber places the stolen item between them and withdraws. Although the Ramban in Milchamot objects, careful examination shows no clear proof against Baal HaMaor. The Shach discusses the Gemara and authorities at length and concludes that although most authorities rule like Rabbi Akiva, the robber in possession may say kim li like Baal HaMaor, Rav Hai Gaon, Halakhot Gedolot, and the Raavad.
