But if two people claimed against one person, etc. This is neither the Tur's language nor the Rambam's in chapter 4 of Hilchot Gezeilah, where the preceding rules are also stated. It is repetitive, because the Mechaber already wrote the same law in seif 1.
Likewise, if he robbed two people, etc. This applies also to what the Mechaber wrote in seif 1 and is the same rule. The Tur arranges the cases together. Each person claims two hundred, etc. If neither claimed and the robber himself volunteered the information, the Mechaber already wrote that when he says he robbed one of them he must pay each to satisfy Heaven. All the more so where he knows he robbed both, as explained above.
