a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 10. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Tosefta: if a worker leaves and there is produce ready to harvest, his work is appraised; if he was a hired laborer he is paid his wages, and if a contractor he is paid his contract amount. The Ramban writes that even though the produce still needs the land, its value is appraised according to what it is worth. This is the Rambam's view when he writes that it has not yet reached the point of sale.
b) A baraita in Bava Metzia 109a, as explained in the Gemara there.
