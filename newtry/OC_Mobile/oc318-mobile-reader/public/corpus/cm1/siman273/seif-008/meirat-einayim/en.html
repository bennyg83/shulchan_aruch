The second item is doubtful ownerless property. This is called an incomplete designation of abandonment. In charity and consecrated property, if one says, "This shall be consecrated or charity, and this," although it is unclear whether "and this" means that the second should also have the same status or should remain his as before, we rule that it becomes consecrated or charity. With ownerless property the corresponding question was left unresolved in the Gemara. Therefore we rule leniently in monetary law and do not remove the second item from the owner's possession.
