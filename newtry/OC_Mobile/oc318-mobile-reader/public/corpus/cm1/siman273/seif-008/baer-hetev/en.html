Doubt. This is called an incomplete expression of ownerlessness. In a comparable case of charity or consecrated property, if one says, 'This is charity,' or 'This is consecrated,' and then says 'and this,' although it is uncertain whether he means that the second item should likewise be like the first or that the second should remain his as before, we rule that it becomes consecrated or charity, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write in Yoreh Deah at the beginning of siman 258. With ownerless property, however, this question remained unresolved in the Gemara. Therefore we rule leniently and do not remove the item from the owner's possession. Ibid.
