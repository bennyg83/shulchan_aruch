Because it is not a vow. The Gemara's language is that a vow operates only for the poor, whereas hefker applies equally to poor and wealthy people, as the Mechaber writes below in seif 5.
He is forbidden to retract. Meaning, by mere speech he cannot retract and protest against another who comes to acquire it by saying, "It is still mine." But if he himself acts first and reacquires it through pulling or possession, he is like anyone else, as explained in seif 4.
