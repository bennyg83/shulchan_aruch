[Shulchan Aruch] One who declares it ownerless for the poor. Note: See Yerushalmi, beginning of chapter 6 of Peah, cited by the Rash there. If one declares property ownerless for Jews but not for non-Jews, or for the people of his city but not for others, according to Reish Lakish it is not hefker, while according to Rabbi Yochanan it is hefker. This is also cited in Tosafot, Gittin 47a, s.v. Adaata.
