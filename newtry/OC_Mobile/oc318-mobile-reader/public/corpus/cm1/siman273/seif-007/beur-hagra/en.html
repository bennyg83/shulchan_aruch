(Collected note) Others say, etc. This view disagrees in two respects: even before one person the declaration is ownerless property, and even rabbinically one person suffices. The requirement there of three is specifically to exempt the produce from maaser. See what I wrote there. That even rabbinically one person suffices follows from Bava Metzia 32b, in the incident where Rabbi Yishmael son of Rabbi Yose declared it ownerless. No other person was there with him, for otherwise that person could have lifted it for him. Some answer that Rabbi Yishmael son of Rabbi Yose was concerned only to avoid others violating the d'oraisa prohibition of theft. This is the first opinion of Rambam and Shulchan Aruch. See Nimukei Yosef in Bava Metzia there and above in siman 261, where he agrees with the Rama's opinion.
