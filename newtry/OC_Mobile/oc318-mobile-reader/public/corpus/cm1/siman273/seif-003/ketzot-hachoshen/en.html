What constitutes ownerless designation. Bach infers from Rambam's formulation, "He should say, These properties are ownerless for everyone," that throwing one's purse into the public domain without words is not enough. Rambam elsewhere says similarly that knowingly losing one's property does not automatically make it ownerless. Bach found this difficult from the Gemara's scattered produce in a threshing area, which is treated as knowingly abandoned and belongs to the finder. He therefore distinguishes between cases where conduct clearly demonstrates abandonment and ordinary negligent exposure. I discussed this at length in siman 261.
