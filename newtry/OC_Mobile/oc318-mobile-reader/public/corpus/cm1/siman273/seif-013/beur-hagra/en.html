One who traps, etc. As above in the preceding siman and as stated below, that even with his own utensil he acquires. "And if it was, etc."
Provided, etc. Only to save his swarm did the Sages permit this in Bava Kamma 114a.
And if he trapped, etc. As stated in seif 14 regarding one who spreads, etc., and as stated in Bava Metzia 11a, specifically if he stands alongside his field and can run after it and reach it.
And if they were, etc., and likewise, etc. As stated there, in a guarded courtyard he need not stand in his field and need not be able to run after it and reach it, because he is not alongside the field. This applies even to a deer running normally. See Tosafos there, s.v. "hayah tzvi, etc."
