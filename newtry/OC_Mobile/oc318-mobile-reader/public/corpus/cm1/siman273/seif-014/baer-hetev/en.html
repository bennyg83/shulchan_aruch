The field. The Sema writes that the Tur states: 'It appears that this applies specifically where he can take it while it is still within the field, as I explained above, and then his field acquires it for him even though he said nothing.' His intent is that this law resembles the law of a found item in siman 268, where it is written that if a deer was crippled, or was standing close enough that he could reach it while it remained within his field, and so forth, his field acquires it for him.
