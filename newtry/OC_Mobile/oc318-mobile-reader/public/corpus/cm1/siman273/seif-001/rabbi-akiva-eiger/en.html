[Shulchan Aruch] That he is prohibited from retracting. Note: See Responsa Bach, siman 124, who writes that one can declare ownerless something that has not yet come into existence. See Ketzot HaChoshen, siman 211, and Responsa Maharit, Even HaEzer, siman 40, who write that one cannot declare ownerless something that has not yet come into existence or is not presently in his possession.
