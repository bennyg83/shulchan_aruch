(Seif 18, they render it ownerless.) Beit Yosef asks the Tur from the statement that on the fifteenth of Adar they would render such property ownerless, which implies that a beit din must formally declare it ownerless. The difficulty is easily resolved. The meaning is that on the fifteenth of Adar the beit din investigated who had acted improperly, so that the matter would become public and the public would treat his property as ownerless.
