It is not ownerless. Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Yoreh Deah 154, writes that although we rule like Beit Hillel that property is not legally ownerless until it is abandoned to everyone as in the Sabbatical year, this refers to one who declares it ownerless only for all poor people while excluding the wealthy, so that no particular poor person acquires a personal right. But if one waives his right directly against one or two identified persons, the waiver is effective. See also what I wrote above, siman 20, under "so long as he brings proof."
