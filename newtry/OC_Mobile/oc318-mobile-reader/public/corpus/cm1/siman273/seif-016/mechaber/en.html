If a poor person cuts fruit from the top of a tree and causes it to fall, he acquires what first came into his hand before falling. If another takes those fruits after they fall, they are removed from him. But fruits that never came into the poor person's hand are not recoverable from another as theft, although taking them is prohibited by rabbinic law. Rama: If the poor person gathered them into his hand and then dropped them, all agree another who takes them is a thief.
