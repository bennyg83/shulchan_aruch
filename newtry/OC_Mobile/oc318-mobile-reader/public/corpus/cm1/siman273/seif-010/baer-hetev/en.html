For a week. Meaning, until the Sabbatical year. Even so, since he did not declare it ownerless permanently, the law is that even after three days he may retract, and it remains obligated in tithes even before he retracts, so long as neither he nor another person has made a complete acquisition in it as ownerless property. The Rosh explains the reason: 'Since people do not ordinarily declare property ownerless for a limited period, and this person deviated from normal practice, the Sages said that it should not be considered ownerless so as to exempt it from tithes until someone actually comes and acquires it.' Nevertheless, once someone acquires it, the declaration is effective as ownerless property even though it was only for a limited period, for we derive this from the Sabbatical year, which likewise renders produce ownerless for only one year.
