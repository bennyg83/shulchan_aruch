They declare ownerless. The Mechaber follows his own view in Beit Yosef that the field does not become ownerless until beit din announces that it is ownerless. The Tur, however, implies that it becomes ownerless automatically. The reason appears to me to be that, once transgressors became numerous and were not careful regarding the prohibition of mixed species in a field, the Sages enacted that any field in which one part in twenty-four of another species is found should become entirely ownerless.
