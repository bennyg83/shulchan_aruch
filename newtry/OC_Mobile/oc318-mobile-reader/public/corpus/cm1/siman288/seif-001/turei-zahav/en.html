The Sema's difficulty from siman 175 may likewise be raised from the Tur at the end of siman 227, which states: "Furthermore, since at the time of the division the judges erred..." In that case the Sema's answer cannot resolve the matter. It appears to me that here the case is different, because the obstacle depends upon them, for the matter rests upon the brothers' consent. Whichever one wishes to give the palm tree, the recipient is obligated to accept it from him. From the outset they relied on the fact that one of them would later remove it from him. This differs from siman 175, where the obstacle depends upon the will of the brother who has now arrived, and likewise from the end of siman 227, where it depends upon the consent of the residents of the alley. Therefore the division is void. This appears correct to me.
