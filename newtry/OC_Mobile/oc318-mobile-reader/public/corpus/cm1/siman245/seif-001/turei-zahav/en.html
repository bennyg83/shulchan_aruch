(Seif 1: one who writes in a deed, “I gave.”) See the Ran in chapter HaSholeach, in his explanation of one who emancipates two slaves together. One who gives half of his property to a single person need not give him one half of every individual article. Rather, the estate is appraised and divided. I copied this ruling in Yoreh Deah, siman 267, seif 59.
