One who writes or says, "I gave a certain field to so-and-so," while the alleged recipient says, "You did not give it to me." It contains eleven seifim.
If one writes in a deed, "I gave a certain field to so-and-so," "I have given it to him," or, "It is his," the recipient acquires when the deed reaches him. Rama: Mere speech does not transfer it unless a kinyan was made. This applies when the donor presently seeks to make the gift using these expressions. If he says, "I gave," as an acknowledgment of a past transfer, some say the recipient acquires. If the deed says, "I will give it to him," the recipient does not acquire even if witnesses attest to the statement. Rama: This remains so even if a kinyan was made, because it is merely a kinyan upon words.
