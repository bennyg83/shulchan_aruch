And some authorities say, etc. The proof is from Avodah Zarah 72a, concerning that man, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. the first one acquired, etc. Rif, however, at the end of chapter 7 of Bava Batra, explains the case as one where he said, 'from now,' and they performed an act of acquisition from him.
