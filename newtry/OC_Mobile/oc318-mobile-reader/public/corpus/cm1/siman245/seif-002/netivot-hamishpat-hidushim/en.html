New conditions. This means phrasing them as an immediate personal obligation and acceptance of liability, which creates a present lien even concerning something not yet in existence, as explained in siman 60, or making a kinyan sudar effective from now, which works even for a commitment to give because he transfers the body of the land itself. The reason this is not done in the original engagement conditions is that each party fears that the other may withdraw from the engagement. They therefore obligate themselves only in a penalty for the embarrassment, and do not wish to obligate themselves in the actual matters stipulated. Consequently, if the marriage was performed without writing new conditions, they are not obligated in the substantive matters stated in the original conditions. There is also no penalty, because there is no embarrassment. For the same reason, the cherem written in the conditions does not take effect upon one who withdraws.
