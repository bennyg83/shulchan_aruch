It shall be given to you. This is not language promising to give at a later time. Rather, it transfers the property presently. The cited alternative view adds that even if he says "I will give," if he also says "from now," the recipient acquires, because the words "from now" convert the formulation from a mere future promise into a present transfer. These two opinions belong to one seif. The next matter, beginning "One who says, I gave," is a separate subject.
