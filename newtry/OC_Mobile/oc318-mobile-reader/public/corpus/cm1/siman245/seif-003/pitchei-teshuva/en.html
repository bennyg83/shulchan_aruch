Shall be given. See Baer Heitev and Responsa Chavot Yair, siman 80, who writes that there may be a distinction between the Hebrew forms meaning "shall be" in the immediate sense and those that more naturally indicate a future event. Thus, one form may imply effect at once, while the other implies future effect. According to what Shach writes in siman 253, seif katan 2, in the name of Mabit, the quoted wording uses the immediate form. Yet in siman 281, seif katan 5, Shach quotes Mabit using the future form.
