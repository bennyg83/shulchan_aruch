The same applies to ownerless property. Rambam disagrees and holds that only with the language, "I do not want it," does it become ownerless, while with other formulations it remains in the recipient's possession. Sema. See Beurim, where this ruling is difficult.
A creditor may collect from it. Even an oral creditor may collect from ownerless property. Taz. If the creditor himself acquires it as ownerless property, that acquisition does not discharge the debtor's obligation. But once another person acquires it and the creditor later obtains the money through ordinary debt collection, the recipient is discharged from his obligation. See Beurim.
