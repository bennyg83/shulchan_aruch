The recipient of the gift, etc. See Responsa Rabbi Shmuel Kohen, part 2, siman 122; Responsa Menahem Azariah, siman 72; and Responsa Rabbi Yosef of the House of Levi, siman 54. See also the Sema, seif katan 15, who writes in the name of the Tur, Rif, and Rosh that regarding a dangerously ill person, if the recipient was silent initially when the donor instructed that the gift be given to him, it is as though he remained silent when it later came into his possession. The Mechaber copied the words of the Rambam, but the Rambam does not state this distinction. In chapter 9 of the Laws of Acquisition and Gift, laws 13 and 14, the Rambam writes the matter explicitly.
