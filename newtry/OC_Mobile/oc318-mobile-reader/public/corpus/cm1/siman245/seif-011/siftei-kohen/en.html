And some say that if it was the gift of a dangerously ill person, etc. This formulation follows what the Mechaber wrote in Bedek HaBayit, but he overlooked the Rambam's words in chapter 9 of the Laws of Acquisition and Gift, laws 13 and 14, which are virtually identical to the language of the Rif and the Rosh. There is no dispute here except whether the law follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel or the first tanna. The Sema's attempted reconciliation is incorrect. See Rabbeinu Yerucham, path 24, part 1, and examine carefully, and see Imrei Emet, page 98b. The meaning is that the Mechaber's words, 'some say that if it was the gift of a dangerously ill person,' refer to the view of the Rif and the Rosh cited by the Tur and copied by the Sema, seif katan 15. They refer back to seif 10: if the donor was dangerously ill, the gift need not actually reach the recipient's hand. The Mechaber used the phrase 'some say' because he thought the Rambam did not distinguish this case, as he states explicitly in Bedek HaBayit. In truth, however, he overlooked the Rambam's words in chapter 9 of the Laws of Acquisition and Gift. The Rama did not grasp the Mechaber's intent here, nor did the Sema or the Bach. Examine carefully. The Beit Yosef writes that one may infer from the Rif and Rashbam, who rule like the Sages, that the recipient acquires in every circumstance. Rabbeinu Yerucham, however, writes in the name of the Rif that his view accords with the Rambam. Raavan, page 108b, rules likewise, as does Maharshal in the chapter HaShochet, siman 20. See the Sema, seif katan 20, who writes that the rule that the law follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel applies only when his words appear in a Mishna, not in a baraita. My father-in-law wrote above, siman 171, seif 10, in the name of Tosafot that this is incorrect and that the law follows him even in a baraita. See also the Sema, seif katan 21, regarding his statement that this is unlike other monetary doubts, and see Sefer Tekfo Kohen of my father-in-law, where he challenges the Sema on this point.
