I have not seen, etc. Rif and Rosh made this distinction in seif 10, and Tur did likewise. Regarding this seif Tur writes: 'From the words of my father Rosh it appears that the recipient acquired it.' Meaning, Rosh states the ruling without qualification, indicating that the halacha follows the first tanna. Rashbam and Nimukei Yosef rule so explicitly. This is the meaning of 'and some authorities say, etc.' The opposing view assumed that here too there is a distinction between a deathly ill person and a healthy person, but that is incorrect, as the gloss continues. This is also the meaning of 'and this is the view of Rosh, etc.,' namely that he ruled like the Sages.
