If it was the gift of a shechiv mera. The primary ruling follows the Shach and Taz, who explain that this refers to seif 10, where silence is required when the gift reaches the recipient. The gloss teaches that in the case of a shechiv mera, if the recipient was silent when he heard of the gift, this is equivalent to silence when it reached him. Nimukei Yosef, Bava Batra 218a at the end of the page, writes that this is only where he remained silent even after the giver's death. If he was silent only during the giver's lifetime, the recipient may retract, because he acquires only after death and may retract before then. Ketzot HaChoshen did not cite these words of Nimukei Yosef and wrote that according to Tosafot and the Rosh, who hold that after death the recipient acquires retroactively, the recipient cannot retract. The primary ruling is that of Nimukei Yosef.
