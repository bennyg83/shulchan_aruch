Heh) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 3, from Bava Batra 138a, where Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak says that the case enters the dispute between Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel and the Sages, as Rava, or some say Rabbi Yochanan, explains there.
This follows the view of the Sages in the baraita.
Vav) This follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam treats the matter as doubtful because of the sugya in Chullin 39b, where the Gemara says that the item was not permitted merely out of respect for Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, nor forbidden merely out of respect for the first Tanna. Since the Amoraim themselves did not decide between them, who can decide? The Baal HaIttur likewise writes this in the name of the Geonim.
Zayin) The Tur, seif 13, following the Rif and Rosh in their explanation of Rav Yehudah in the name of Shmuel and Rabbi Abba bar Mamel's statement at the end of Bava Batra 136b. This "some say" view does not refer to a case where the gift was acquired for him through another person.
Chet) The words of a deathly ill person are treated as written and delivered. But with a gift of a healthy person, acquisition does not occur until the deed reaches the recipient or another person acquires on his behalf, as explained above.
Tet) If he says, "I do not want it," the property becomes ownerless. Tur.
