Admission of a litigant. See Baer Heitev, letter 20, who cites the Maggid Mishneh in the name of Tosafos, and similarly the Sema, seif katan 8. Netivot HaMishpat further writes that some authorities explain the rule because the donor is the party in possession. It appears that there is a practical difference between these explanations where witnesses come and testify that the donor's account is true. Consider this carefully, and see what I wrote in Nachalat Tzvi above, siman 75, seif 1, beginning with the words, "Clarify your words."
