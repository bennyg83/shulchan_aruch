We follow the donor's words. This concerns a recipient who wants the gift but truthfully says that the donor had not previously given it to him. No new kinyan is needed, because the donor's acknowledgment itself establishes the recipient's title and the donor cannot retract. This differs from the next seif, where the donor says that he delivered the gift into the recipient's hand. We follow the donor here rather than the recipient because both statements can be true. The donor may have transferred the property to the recipient through a third person without the recipient knowing until now. If the recipient does not wish to accept the gift, however, he cannot be forced to acquire it. If he protests immediately upon learning of it, he is treated as one who protested from the outset.
