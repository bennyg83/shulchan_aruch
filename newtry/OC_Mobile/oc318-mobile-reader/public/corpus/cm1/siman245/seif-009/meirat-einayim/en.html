The gift is not thereby void. Once Reuven acquired it through one of the valid methods of acquisition, it does not leave his ownership until he reconveys it to the donor through another valid kinyan. Returning the gift deed does not suffice, nor does simple forgiveness. Forgiveness substitutes for repayment only in a debt, where Reuven can say to Shimon, "I forgive the money you owe me." But if one merely forgives a tangible object or pledge presently held by another, nothing happens unless he says, "I give it to you." The Tur cites this from the Rosh in siman 73, and the Mechaber states it there. Rama likewise explained it above in siman 241.
