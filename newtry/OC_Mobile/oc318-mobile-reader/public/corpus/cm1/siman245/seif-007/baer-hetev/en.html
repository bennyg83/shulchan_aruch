Heset oath. The Sema writes that this applies where the creditor claims with certainty, "I know that the property in your hands is a gift." But if the creditor is uncertain, the holder is subject only to a general cherem. The Beit Yosef writes this according to the Maggid Mishneh. See what the Taz writes in explanation of the Tur's words on this matter. See Responsa Rashdam, simanim 64 and 278, and Responsa Mabit, part II, siman 314.
