Tet) This is the language of the Rambam there, based on the baraita there.
Yod) Rav Chisda says that the giver consumes the fruits, while Rabbah says the fruits are held in escrow. They do not disagree: one case concerns a father, the other a son.
Kaf) The Maggid Mishneh cites Tosafot: why do we not say regarding the giver that an admission of a litigant is like one hundred witnesses, causing him to lose the fruits? They answer that the recipient is more likely to remember than the giver. The giver first had the thought to give, and sometimes later believes that he actually gave merely because he had intended to do so, whereas the recipient has no such prior thought.
Lamed) Rashi explains that perhaps witnesses will later come and testify that the giver delivered the deed to the recipient's father, while the recipient himself did not know this.
