The admission of a litigant. Here one cannot explain his words as in seif 4, because he said explicitly, "I gave it into his hand." We do not simply follow the giver's own admission because it is possible that, being occupied with the process of giving, he erred and thought he had already delivered it. Some authorities explain instead that the giver is the one presently in possession.
