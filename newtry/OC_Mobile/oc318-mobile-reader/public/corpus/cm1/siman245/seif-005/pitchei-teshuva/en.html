A litigant's admission. See Baer HaGolah, letter 20, who cites Maggid Mishneh in the name of Tosafot, and Sema seif katan 8, who writes similarly. Netivot HaMishpat adds that some authorities explain the ruling on the ground that the donor is the party in possession. A practical difference between these explanations would arise where witnesses testify that the truth accords with the donor's account. Consider this carefully, and see what I wrote in Nachalat Tzvi above, siman 75 seif 1, s.v. “Clarify your words.”
