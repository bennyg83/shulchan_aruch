Yet we do not say that the donor's admission has the force of one hundred witnesses, because it is possible that while engaged in arranging the gift he mistakenly believed that he had actually delivered it. The recipient, by contrast, would not ordinarily say that it never reached his hand if it actually had. The Maggid Mishneh gives this explanation. See the Perishah for the Rosh's approach.
