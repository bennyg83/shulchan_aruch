Some say this applies specifically when the first person is lying diagonally. The reason is that the first person should have been careful when he came and saw that the second person had tripped over him. But if the first person is lying diagonally, when the second trips over the first person's head, the next person who comes and trips over the first person's feet may not have seen the second person lying before him. Likewise, if another person then trips over the first person's torso, one can say he did not see before him either the one who had tripped over the first person's head or the one who had tripped over his feet.
