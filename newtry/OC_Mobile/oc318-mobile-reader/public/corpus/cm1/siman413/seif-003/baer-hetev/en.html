Diagonally. The reason is that if the first person is lying straight, the third person should have taken care when he came and saw that the second had tripped over the first. The gloss notes that this appears to be the correct text and that the printed editions of the Sema contain an error. But when the first lies diagonally, and the second trips over his head, one who subsequently comes and trips over the first person's feet can say that he did not see the second person lying before him. Similarly, if another person then trips over the first person's abdomen, he can say that he saw neither the one who tripped over the first person's head nor the one who tripped over his feet. Ibid.
