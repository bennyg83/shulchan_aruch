The wording of Rambam, chapter 13 of Hilkhot Nizkei Mamon, law 8, from the Mishnah in Bava Kamma chapter 3, 31a.
He rules like Rabbi Yochanan there. Rif rules likewise. I also saw in the glosses there in the name of Or Zarua that Mahari Chaviv likewise ruled so.
Following Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak there. Beit Yosef writes that Rosh's questions against Rif for ruling like Rabbi Yochanan are not decisive and can easily be answered. In my humble opinion, Rosh himself did not definitively rule like Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak; he only writes that it would appear so. Since Or Zarua and Mahari Chaviv also agree with Rif and Rambam, and Rosh did not definitively rule otherwise, we follow them. I later saw Bach write likewise.
