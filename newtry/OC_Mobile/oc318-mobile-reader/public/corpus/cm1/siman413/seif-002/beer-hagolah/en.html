The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is straightforward that where Rambam makes the first carrier liable, he means only when the load was not abandoned. If it was abandoned, the preceding law applies. This is explained in the previous siman, seif 4. Raavad likewise writes in his glosses: I saw Rif write so, and I said he could not have erred; the entire matter concerns where the load was not abandoned. Accordingly, there is no basis for Tur's claim that Rosh disagrees with Rif and Rambam, nor for Rama's statement that some disagree.
Raavad explains there that even though the first did not abandon his load, at that moment it is nevertheless the first person's pit, while the first person himself is exempt because it was not originally his pit, and one who abandons damage created through an unavoidable accident is exempt.
