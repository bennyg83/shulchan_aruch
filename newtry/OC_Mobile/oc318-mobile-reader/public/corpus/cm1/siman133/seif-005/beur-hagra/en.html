Some say. See the Rosh there, siman 7. Raavad's view accords with Tosafos, except that here, because the claimant is taking property, he swears while holding a sacred object, even on a doubtful claim, as in other cases of one who swears and collects, such as one who recognizes his stolen utensils or one who spent money on his wife's property.
