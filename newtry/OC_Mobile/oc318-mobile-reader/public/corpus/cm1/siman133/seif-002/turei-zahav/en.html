(Seif 3: we properly read it as, etc.) I do not understand the Sema's statement in seif katan 8 that we accept what he says to his own detriment. If a person says outside beit din, "I borrowed and repaid," do we accept the statement "I borrowed" while rejecting "I repaid"? Certainly we do not split his statement any more than if he had said the same thing in beit din. Rather, the essential point here is that he must possess the migo at the time he comes before beit din and they impose the oath, not at the earlier time when he spoke outside beit din. Otherwise this would be a retroactive migo.
