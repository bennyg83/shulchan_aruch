If it was merely seen in his possession in beit din, he does not thereby lose his claim, because his proof and its rebuttal are together. By contrast, if witnesses saw it in his possession before the litigation, then even if he told them it had been purchased by him or was held as collateral, he is not believed when the claimant has witnesses that the item belongs to him. The reason is that a statement made outside beit din does not establish his claim, and the witnesses saw it in his possession immediately before suit.
