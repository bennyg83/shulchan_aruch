The Shach in seif katan 13 discusses at length the view of Tosafot, Rosh, and Mordechai that where the claimant merely says the objects were stolen, without identifying a thief, he is believed concerning objects ordinarily lent or rented because he has a migo, and the rule that we do not presume people to be thieves does not apply when he does not accuse a particular person. See what I wrote above in siman 90 at length. In any event, according to all opinions, if no report of theft arose he must reimburse the current possessor for what he paid because of the market enactment, as the Shach writes. This is simple.
