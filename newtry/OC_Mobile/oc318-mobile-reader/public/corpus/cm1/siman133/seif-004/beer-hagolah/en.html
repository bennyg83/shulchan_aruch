Following Tur's view in seif 7, where he questions Rabbenu Yonah's ruling in favor of the challenger. Tur explains that the possessor can say that the depositary returned it to him in the presence of witnesses and that he afterward bought it from him. Had the depositary been before us, he too would have been believed to say, 'I returned it to you in the presence of so-and-so and so-and-so, who have gone overseas.' Bach writes that Rabbenu Yonah's law is correct where the depositor designated specific witnesses and they testify that the item was not returned.
