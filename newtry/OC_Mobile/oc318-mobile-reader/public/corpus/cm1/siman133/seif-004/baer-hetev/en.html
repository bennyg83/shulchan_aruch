See Responsa Mabit II, 205, which writes that the Rosh's responsum applies only where witnesses saw the item, not where the possessor admitted having it. The Shach rejects this distinction: what difference is there between witnesses who saw it and an admission? See Responsa Mahara Sasson at the end of siman 107. See also the Taz on this matter.
