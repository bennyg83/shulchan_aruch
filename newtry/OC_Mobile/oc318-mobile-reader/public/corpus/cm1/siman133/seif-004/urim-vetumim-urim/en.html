You sold it. The same applies if the possessor says, 'You instructed so-and-so to sell it to me.' He is believed through a migo that he could have claimed, 'You sold it to me yourself,' as explained below in siman 134. Mabit writes that Rosh stated the rule only where witnesses saw the item, not where the possessor himself admitted having it. The Shach rejects this distinction. There is no material difference between sight witnesses and the possessor's own admission. See Responsa Mahara Sasson 107.
