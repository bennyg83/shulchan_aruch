And I said to him, "Do not return it," etc. Even if the claimant has witnesses that he said this, the present possessor nevertheless takes an oath. Since he is in possession, we advance on his behalf the argument that perhaps, if the original custodian were before us, he would say, "I returned it to you in the presence of witnesses," and would establish that claim according to the Rif, or would say, "I returned it before so and so and so and so, who have gone overseas," according to the Rosh.
