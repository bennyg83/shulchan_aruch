The law of movable property... Bava Basra 46a states regarding an artisan that in ordinary movable property possession creates a presumption of ownership. See Rashbam on the Mishnah, s.v. "they have no chazakah," who explains that with movable property, once it is in another's possession... Thus the ruling here applies even where... The same principle appears in Kesubos 17b, for where there is a valid chazakah even witnesses do not displace it. With movable property the presumption operates immediately, as Rashbam writes, and as appears in Bava Basra 36a and from the incident of the goats there.
