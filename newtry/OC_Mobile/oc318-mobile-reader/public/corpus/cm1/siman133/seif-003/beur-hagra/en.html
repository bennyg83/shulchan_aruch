If they saw... Bava Basra 46a, "Come and I will show you..." Tosafos explains that this means before they came to beit din. Thus the Gemara says, "He speaks correctly," and, "If he is wise..." See Hagahos Ashri at the end of HaMekabel.
Know that... The Gemara's phrase "if he is wise..." is not because...
He warned... so that he cannot... Although he is not believed to say afterward that he had already returned it, similar to one who says "I never borrowed." See Shevuos 6a, where one who initially says "You have nothing of mine" before witnesses and then claims he had already paid is not believed. The Gemara elsewhere requires the witnesses actually to contradict him only when the original denial was outside beit din. See above siman 79, seif 9. Here, his warning preserves the evidentiary status and prevents a later claim of prior return.
