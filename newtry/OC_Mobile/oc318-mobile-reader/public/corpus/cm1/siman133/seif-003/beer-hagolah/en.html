There, seif 6, in the name of a responsum of his father Rosh, rule 106, siman 2. Rama also cites it in his gloss in siman 72, seif 18.
He explains there that everything depends upon the oath that beit din would require him to take, namely that the other party owes him the item's value, through the migo that he could have sworn, 'I returned it.' Once witnesses saw the item in his possession, that migo is unavailable and he must return it.
