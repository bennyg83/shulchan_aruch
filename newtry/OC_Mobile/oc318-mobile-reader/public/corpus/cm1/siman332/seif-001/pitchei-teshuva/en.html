Therefore we assess. See Baer Heitev, who cites a ruling that the amount of compensation itself reveals the parties' intent concerning a worker's expenses. Terumat HaDeshen indicates that this is so even where the city custom is that employers ordinarily pay expenses separately. Ketzot HaChoshen, siman 331, questions this from the rule that where local custom is to provide food, the employer must provide it even if the workers' cash wages are higher, and we do not say that the amount of wages proves otherwise. Netivot HaMishpat resolves that Terumat HaDeshen involved a case where, even according to the worker's version, there was already another deviation from local custom, because all employers ordinarily advanced travel expenses before the mission.
