If the employer authorized three and the agent offered four, and the workers replied, 'We accept whatever the employer said,' their intent is that the employer may give more than four. The value of their work is assessed. If worth four, they receive four from the employer; if worth less or uncertain, they receive only three. Rama: If the agent said, 'Your wages are upon me,' he must pay four in every case. The same applies if workers were available only at four.
