We estimate. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 323, discusses Reuven, who hired Shimon to travel on an errand for ten dinar. Before leaving, Shimon told others that he also expected travel expenses in addition to the ten dinar, but Reuven was absent and could not be informed. Reuven later refused to pay the expenses, arguing that although other workers would be hired for four, he had offered Shimon ten precisely in order to include expenses. Terumat HaDeshen rules for Reuven, because the amount of the wage itself indicates the parties' intent in a labor contract, even though we do not apply the same inference to house rentals.
