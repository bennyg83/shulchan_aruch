Terumat HaDeshen ruled that in a dispute over a worker's wage, the amount paid can indicate what was included in the employment even where local workers ordinarily receive expenses separately. Ketzot HaChoshen questions this from the rule that where the custom is to provide food, food must be provided even if the wage is high. Netivot HaMishpat answers that Terumat HaDeshen's case itself deviated from ordinary custom, such as where workers normally receive expenses before departure, so the price can clarify what this unusual agreement included.
