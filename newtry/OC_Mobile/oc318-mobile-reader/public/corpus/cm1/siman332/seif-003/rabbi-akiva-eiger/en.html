[Shach, letter 16] And see the Nimukei Yosef there and you will see. Note: On the contrary, one who studies the Nimukei Yosef there will see that even where the employer misled the workers, they have only a grievance because even without the stipulation he must pay the reduced rate. This implies that were it not for that reason, such as where even the lower rate is five or six, he would have to pay it, contrary to the Shach. So too in Shitah Mekubetzet in the name of the Rashba.
