[Gloss] An article in payment of his wages. Note: See Responsa Maharit, volume 2, Choshen Mishpat, siman 23. His words are puzzling, for his question and answer are the same as the Rosh's question and answer. Maharit merely adds afterward, Perhaps, and so forth, that perhaps there is not even a monetary obligation because the article is no longer extant and therefore no lien exists. This contradicts the explicit words of the Rosh, who writes that he may give him the monetary value of the article. See what I wrote in the gloss at the beginning of siman 198.
