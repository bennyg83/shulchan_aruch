If the employer authorized four, the agent offered three, and the workers said, 'As the employer said,' they receive only three even if the work is worth four, because they heard and accepted three. Rama: If the employer said, 'Work for me for four as your colleagues did,' and they agreed to the same terms as their colleagues, and it is found that the colleagues received more, he must pay these workers equally. If either side misrepresented the prevailing wage, it is as though no wage was fixed, and the workers receive the lowest prevailing wage. If an employer promised an object as wages, he may later pay its value because the worker did not acquire the object without meshichah.
