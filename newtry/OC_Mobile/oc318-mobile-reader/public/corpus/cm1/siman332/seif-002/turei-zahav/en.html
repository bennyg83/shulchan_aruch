(Or the workers misled, etc.) The Sema, seif katan 8, labors to explain why Rabbeinu Yerucham says "most workers" in the latter clause but not the former. His words are very unclear and almost unintelligible. The effort is unnecessary. The Yerushalmi itself, which is the source of this law at the beginning of Perek HaOmanin, also says "most workers" in the first clause. Necessarily the phrase is not exact in Rabbeinu Yerucham's wording. The Ir Shushan therefore correctly states the two clauses in parallel form.
