Although their work, etc. Meaning, although it is evident before us that their work is worth four.
They receive only three from the employer. It appears that the employer need not pay the fourth dinar even to the agent.
They have a grievance against the agent. This is because of the verse, ‘Do not withhold good from its owner.’ Since the employer authorized him to hire them for four, the agent should not have withheld that benefit from them. The Beit Yosef cites the students of the Rashba that if the agent hired them for three, or hired them without specifying a rate, and they performed work worth four, they receive only three. The statement concerning an unspecified hiring means that the employer had instructed the agent to offer three. But if the employer told him to offer four, or merely said, ‘Hire workers for me,’ and the agent hired them without specifying a rate, then workers who performed work worth four receive four. Nevertheless, if the agent expressly hired them for three, then even if the employer authorized four they receive only three, despite the superior quality of their work, as the Rambam and Mechaber rule.
