Even though their work is worth, etc. This is Rambam's wording, and it is as though the text says, "even though it is evident before us that their work is worth four, nevertheless, etc."
They have a grievance against the agent. The Gemara says that they tell him, "Do you not know the verse, 'Do not withhold good from its owner'?" Meaning, since the employer told you to hire at four, you should not have withheld that benefit from us.
