(Above, seif 3: Their intention, etc.) The Sema correctly states that the Tur's text requires correction. The Rosh explains that where the employer said three, the agent said four, and the workers answered, “As the employer said,” while some workers are hired for three and some for four, their words are understood as seeking an increase, not as accepting less than the agent stated. This does not itself entitle them to four. Rather, it prevents their answer from harming them; their case is treated as though they had said nothing. They receive four only if their work is actually worth four, as stated earlier. Indeed, even if all workers are usually hired for three, superior work worth four earns four. The Tur should therefore read: “and some are hired for four, if their work is worth four.” An interpretation that awards four merely because some workers receive four, despite work worth only three, is untenable, particularly where the agent expressly said that payment is upon the employer. When they claim against the employer, who authorized only three, and their labor was worth no more than three, there is no basis to charge him more. Their answer to the agent cannot create such a liability.
