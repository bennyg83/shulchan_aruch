And he cannot say, etc. The Gemara explains that the workers may say: Initially we agreed to less only because we saw that you were unfamiliar with prevailing wages and assumed that in your view other workers would also accept that amount. We were in need of money and therefore settled for less than what was properly due us. Now that you see all workers are paid this amount, you cannot rely on our earlier concession. The corresponding rationale applies in the reverse case where the employer says this to the workers. See the Perishah.
