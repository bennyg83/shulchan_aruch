"I will pay" is exempt. Baer Heitev suggests that "exempt" may mean only exempt from the entire loss, while Levush distinguishes the case by saying that the partners had not yet divided. Meil Tzedakah, responsum 76, rejects both explanations and argues that the ruling here actually conflicts with the Rama's rule in siman 176, seif 12. In his view the straightforward meaning here is the correct one. See the discussion cited above in siman 176 regarding a partner who authorizes a prohibited act.
