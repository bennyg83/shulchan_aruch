One of the brothers who are partners, etc. The Tur's text says one of the partners. The Beit Yosef writes that this law concerns specifically brothers who are partners, because otherwise, if ordinary partners share their labor and business, who gave one of them permission to leave the enterprise and travel to study Torah? In the Derishah I demonstrated that the rule can also apply to ordinary partners, for example where they agreed that each would work during a separate designated period and one wishes to travel to study Torah during the period in which his fellow is conducting the partnership business.
