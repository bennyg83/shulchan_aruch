Seif 1: But not another trade. Service for the king is a common source of income, and the partners presumably relied upon it from the beginning. A difficulty arises from the Rama's ruling in siman 176 that even stolen gains are shared, all the more so income from a trade obtained because of the partnership, even if it did not come from the king. The answer is that the present authority follows the Ittur, cited by me in siman 176, who holds that stolen gains are not shared. The opposing view cited by the Rama would not distinguish here between royal service and another trade. This supports ruling like the Ittur.
