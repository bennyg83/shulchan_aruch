The language of Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilkhot Nachalot, law 10, from the Mishnah and baraita in Bava Batra 144b.
The language of Maggid Mishneh there in the name of the Yerushalmi; Tur likewise writes so.
Beit Yosef likewise writes in the name of Ramban: in Responsa Ramban, siman 2, the correct text is "brothers who are partners," not "brothers and partners," because this law applies specifically to brothers who remain joint heirs. As long as they have not divided the family estate, we view the brothers as one unit, almost as though their father were still alive with respect to the inherited property, and they and their children are supported from the common estate.
