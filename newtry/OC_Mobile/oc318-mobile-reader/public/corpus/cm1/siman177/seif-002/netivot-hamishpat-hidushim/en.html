And some say the reverse. The Sema concludes like the Tur, while the Shach rules like the Mechaber. Regarding a widow, the Shach writes that according to everyone, even if she became ill through her own negligence, she is entitled to medical expenses where the treatment has no fixed amount. Bach writes that according to the Tur here, a widow is likewise denied such medical care when the illness was caused by her negligence.
