Seif 3, in the gloss: Specifically when they are not. The Beit Yosef proves this from Rabbeinu in siman 176. The Sema's discussion is difficult. In the case there, Reuven was alive when Shimon died, and the early responsa imply that both had actively conducted the partnership. Had the rule depended on whether each personally worked, the Rosh should have made the medical expenses depend on that factor. Since he did not, the Beit Yosef and Rama's ruling appears correct.
