In winter. The Sema writes that there is no distinction in these times between one who travels on foot and one who rides in a wagon, because the Gemara derives this from the statement, 'Everything is in the hands of Heaven except cold and heat,' and no distinction is made there. See Be'er Sheva, folio 104c.
