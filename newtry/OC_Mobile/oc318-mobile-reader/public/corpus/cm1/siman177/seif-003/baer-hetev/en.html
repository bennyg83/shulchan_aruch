Partners. See Responsa Mabit, part 1, end of siman 125, and Responsa Maharashdam, siman 161. This requires study. Shach.
And specifically. The Sema writes that it appears there is no such distinction. Rather, even if they are supported from the partnership, whenever they conduct the business together each partner pays his own medical expenses. If they do not conduct the partnership business together, but take turns, then if one becomes ill through an unavoidable circumstance while it is his turn alone to conduct the partnership business, he is treated from the common funds because he was then engaged on behalf of both. See the Taz, who challenges this from siman 176, seif 45, and concludes that the views of the Beit Yosef and Rama appear correct. See also Responsa Rashach, part 1, simanim 116 and 162, and Lechem Rav, siman 109.
