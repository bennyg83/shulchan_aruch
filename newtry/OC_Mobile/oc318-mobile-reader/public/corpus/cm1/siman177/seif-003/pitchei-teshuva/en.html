Specifically when they are not supported from the partnership. Baer Heitev and Netivot HaMishpat agree with the Taz, who follows the Mechaber and Rama rather than the Sema. Mishkenot Yaakov, Choshen Mishpat, responsum 10, likewise appears to rule this way. He adds that even if Ri HaLevi's ruling were understood to apply where the partners are maintained from partnership funds, the Rosh's responsum would then necessarily disagree with his basic distinction and hold that there is no difference between one partner alone conducting the business and both conducting it. Tosafot and Ramban also appear to support that approach. Thus, even according to the Sema's framing, the weight of the later authorities is against Ri HaLevi.
