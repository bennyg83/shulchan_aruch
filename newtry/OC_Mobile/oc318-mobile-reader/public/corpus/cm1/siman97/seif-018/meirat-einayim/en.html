A sub tongue is at the recipient’s desk, whereby one vessel of one day and one vessel of one night is set against both of them, and when we take a second, a night vessel returns to him at the same time as the day of the evening and the world that does not return to our neighbor and the like we will be there
From things that he does not need, and they do not leave, etc. These two clauses depend upon each other. It is as though the text said: If the item is something he does not need, so that it is not left with the borrower, nevertheless the lender does not sell it immediately. Rather, he waits thirty days before selling it, just as in the case of collateral delivered to him at the time of the loan, as explained in siman 73.
