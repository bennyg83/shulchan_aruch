Money in the dowry. Money placed with a couple may be collected by the husband's creditor, and this is not a case of the money having been given in vain, because it benefits the household. If a promissory note was made in the father's name and contained no language of acquisition for the son, perhaps the son's creditor cannot collect it. Language such as 'it belongs to him' may not itself create an acquisition, and even 'it shall belong to him' may not be language of present transfer.
