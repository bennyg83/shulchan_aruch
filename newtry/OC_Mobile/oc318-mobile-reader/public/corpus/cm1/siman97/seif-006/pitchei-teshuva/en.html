He shall not take a pledge from him. Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, siman 76, writes that violation of the prohibition occurs when one forcibly seizes a pledge from another's hand, but taking it by stratagem is permitted. Otherwise it would be difficult to explain the law of one who seizes property for a creditor where this harms other creditors, and the opinions that seizure through an agent may be more effective; one might otherwise invoke the principle that there is no agency for a transgression. See there, Ketzot HaChoshen above in siman 4 and here seif katan 2, and Netivot HaMishpat on this law.
