He swore to pay. Above, siman 73, seif 7, regarding shemittah, we find that when the debt is extinguished he is also released from his oath to pay. The reason is that there he swore only to pay a debt, not to give a gift. Once shemittah cancels the debt, there is nothing left for the oath to require. Here, however, poverty does not cancel the debt. If he later becomes wealthy he must pay. Since the debt still exists, he must fulfill the oath he took to pay it.
