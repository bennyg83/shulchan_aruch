(Seif 12, gloss) “And he need not return it.” Although we rule that when the Torah says “do not do” and one nevertheless acts, the act is generally ineffective, this case is different because the Torah says, “You shall surely return it,” thereby multiplying obligations of return. That multiplication can be fulfilled only by returning a daytime utensil each day and a nighttime utensil each night. From this verse we derive that the creditor acquires the pledge. This is stated in Temurah 6.
