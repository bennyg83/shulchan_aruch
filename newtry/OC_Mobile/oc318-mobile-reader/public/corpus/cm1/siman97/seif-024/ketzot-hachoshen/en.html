The loan for support. The reason given is that the support obligation is rabbinic while the creditor's lien is Torah law. The Shach asks from authorities who hold that liens are rabbinic, as discussed above in siman 39. There is no difficulty. Even according to the view that property liens against purchasers and heirs are rabbinic, the debtor's personal obligation is Torah law and he must pay from his own assets by Torah law. The support obligation here is rabbinic even against the person himself.
