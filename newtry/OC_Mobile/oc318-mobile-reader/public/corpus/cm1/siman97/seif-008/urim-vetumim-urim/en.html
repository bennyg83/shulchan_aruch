Utensils, etc. See Tumim concerning whether one may seize food-preparation utensils when they are not presently needed for work, or millstones when no grinding is currently taking place. The authorities dispute this. Maggid Mishneh and many others, and the implication of Mechaber and Tur in the preceding seif that the item is returned at the time of work, support permissibility. Tosafot and Baal HaTerumot appear to prohibit it. Since this is a Torah prohibition, one should be stringent, although the Jerusalem Talmud appears to permit it.
