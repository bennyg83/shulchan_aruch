We do not compel him to hire himself out. See Baer Heitev and Shaar Mishpat regarding whether he himself is nevertheless obligated to hire himself out. Shaar Mishpat discusses this at length and concludes: "The rule emerging from our discussion is that although according to everyone beit din does not compel him to hire himself out in order to repay his debt, whether he personally is obligated to do so depends on a dispute among the authorities. According to Tosafot, he has no obligation at all to hire himself out in order to repay his debt. According to the Mordechai in the name of Maharam, which is also the position of the Rama in siman 333, although beit din does not compel him, he himself is obligated to hire himself out in order to repay his debt."
