To return. Meaning during the time when the utensil used for preparing food is needed for its work, as the Rama explains in his gloss. Sema.
Items. Meaning items that the borrower does not need at all. Sema.
Effective. Although we generally rule that when the Torah says not to perform an act, if one performs it the act is ineffective, here the verse says, "you shall surely return," thereby requiring repeated returns. This rule could not operate unless the creditor acquired the collateral and then had to return a daytime utensil during the day and a nighttime utensil at night. From here we learn that he acquires it. So the Gemara states in Temurah 6. Taz.
