In the Tur: it is a positive commandment to lend, and it is greater than charity. Meaning, if two mitzvot are before him, one to give a gift to a poor person and the other to lend to a poor person, he should first engage in lending. The person who needs to receive a gift has already become accustomed to doing so and can seek assistance more easily. The one who does not ordinarily accept gifts, but only loans, is more embarrassed and has greater difficulty obtaining help.
