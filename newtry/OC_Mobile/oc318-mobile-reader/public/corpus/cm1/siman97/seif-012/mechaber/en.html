If the creditor himself took collateral from the debtor, he must return it, unless the one who seized it was a treasurer of consecrated property or a charity treasurer. {Rama: This applies specifically to implements needed for preparing food, because regarding them the lender is treated like an agent of beit din. If the lender seized other objects, although he violated a prohibition, his act is effective, and he need not return them until the debtor pays him.}
