[Rama] A beit din acting on its own initiative is not obligated to compel him. Note: Although the matter also entails the prohibition, “You shall not lie down with his pledge,” nevertheless, since the prohibition is rectified by a positive commandment, beit din does not compel compliance with it. See Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 7 of Hilchot Matanot Aniyim, halachah 10, and Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 3, halachah 14, of Hilchot Avadim.
