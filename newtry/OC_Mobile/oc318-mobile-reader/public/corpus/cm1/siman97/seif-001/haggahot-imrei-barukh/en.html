(Shach, seif katan 5) If taken as a pledge other than at the time of the loan, he is liable even for unavoidable accidents. Note: See Ritva's novellae to Makkot 16, passage beginning "since he wishes," where he seeks to prove according to the Ramah that one who seizes a pledge without authorization pays according to its value at the time of seizure if it later breaks by itself.
