[Sema, subparagraph 18] Or utensils from which one eats, and from them always. Note: The Sema plainly appears to hold that utensils from which food is eaten are included in the prohibition concerning millstones and the upper millstone, and this also appears from the Tur, who includes bowls. However, the words of Terumas HaDeshen, ruling 5, s.v. "Ha," indicate that plates and cups are not considered utensils used in the preparation of food.
