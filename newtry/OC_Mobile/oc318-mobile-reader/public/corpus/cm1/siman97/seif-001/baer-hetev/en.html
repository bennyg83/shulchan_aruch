More. So write the Ramban and Tur. The person who already receives charity has already been forced to ask for it. Meaning, even if one does not lend him money, he can ask the charity administrators to support him. By contrast, the person seeking a loan has not yet reached that condition. Sema. The Taz explains that if two mitzvot are before him, one to give a poor person a gift and the other to lend to a poor person, he should first occupy himself with the loan. The one who needs a gift is already accustomed to seeking such support and can obtain it more readily, whereas the other is not accustomed to receiving gifts and seeks only a loan.
