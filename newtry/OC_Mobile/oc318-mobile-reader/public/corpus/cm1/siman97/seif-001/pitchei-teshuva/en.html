He shall not take a pledge from him. See Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, siman 76, who writes that the prohibition is violated when one forcibly seizes the pledge from his fellow. Taking it through stratagem, however, may be permitted. Otherwise it would be difficult to understand the law of one who seizes for a creditor where this causes loss to others, especially according to those who hold that action through an agent is preferable. One might have wished to invoke the rule that there is no agency for a transgression. See there.
