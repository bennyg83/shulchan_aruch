It is true that this is the wording of Baal HaTerumot, but it appears entirely incorrect to me, notwithstanding the strained explanations offered by the Sema and the Bach. In my view, whenever the creditor lacks a migo, the burden of proof rests upon the claimant, as stated above at the beginning of siman 71. See there. It appears that this is why the Tur omitted the concluding words of Baal HaTerumot on this point.
