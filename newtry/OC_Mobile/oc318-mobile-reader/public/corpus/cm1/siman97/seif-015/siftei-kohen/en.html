“There is someone who says yes. “And then, in the book, I will see it as a sign.”
This applies specifically when he has no means with which to pay. See the Ateret Aharon, folio 84b, who emends the text to read, “Some say that this applies specifically when he has no means,” but this is incorrect. See the Darchei Moshe and my own work. See also Responsa Rabbi Eliyahu ibn Chaim, siman 58, and Responsa Maharashdam, siman 390. Cited cross-references in the source: 50, 300.
