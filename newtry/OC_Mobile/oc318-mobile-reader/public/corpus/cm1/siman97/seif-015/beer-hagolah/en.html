The Rosh writes similarly regarding Shmuel's statement that an agent of beit din may seize an item outside the house, etc. He writes: This is difficult, for if he does not find the item in his hand, how can he compel him to pay his debt? Payment of a creditor is a mitzvah, etc. According to Rabbenu Tam's explanation, however, the matter is resolved, etc.

There, in the name of a responsum of his father, the Rosh, rule 68, siman 7. Rashba likewise writes in Responsa siman 1069, and so writes Rivash. As for what is cited in the name of Sefer Agudah, that one may imprison him, with proof from the incident in which Abba bar Marta owed money to the house of Rabban Gamliel, etc., Shabbat 121b, one may answer that those were servants of Rabban Gamliel, who were forceful men, as stated in Gittin 67b and elsewhere.
