In her possession. Responsa Rosh discusses Shimon, who owed Reuven money, married a woman, and received from his brother in law a deed on his house. When Shimon was about to die, he instructed that his wife's deed be returned. It was ruled that Reuven could not collect from that security even though his debt was earlier. Either way, if the deed is treated as dowry, a creditor does not collect from it; if it is treated as a pledge, Shimon expressly instructed that the deed be returned. See the lengthy discussion there. Darkei Moshe, siman 104, seif katan 31. Sema.
