When does this apply... Yerushalmi, first chapter of Kiddushin, concerning brothers who divided an estate.
(Collected notes) When does this apply... Nimukei Yosef explains that garments worn by the sons are not appraised because the father could not have intended to reserve a right to seize them at division when he himself had no recoverable right in them. The Rif distinguishes ordinary weekday clothing. Rashi and the Rosh instead explain that the reason is embarrassment at bringing the clothing into beit din, which limits the rule differently. This is the basis for the dissenting view. (End of collected notes.)
And some disagree. They distinguish the case of brothers. There even their own garments may be appraised, and clothing on their children is excluded only because of the indignity of bringing them to court. That rationale may not apply to Shabbos and festival clothing. Here, however, the reason is that the husband is treated as having transferred ownership to his wife from the outset, so no distinction exists. The Gemara at the end of Perek Na'arah supports this understanding. See Even HaEzer siman 90 seif 15 and Choshen Mishpat simanim 287 and 288.
