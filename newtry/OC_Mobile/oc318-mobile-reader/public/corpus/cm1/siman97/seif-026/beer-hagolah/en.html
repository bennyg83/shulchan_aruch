There, in the name of Rambam. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it appears Rambam derived this from the statement in the first chapter of Bava Kamma concerning brothers who divided an estate, etc., and from the Yerushalmi cited by the Rif.
So writes the Maggid Mishneh there in the name of some commentators, because a husband transfers ownership to his wife of both weekday and Shabbat clothing. Therefore, if he consecrates his property or vows his value, he has no rights in his wife's clothing, even her Shabbat garments. The Maggid Mishneh brings support for this and writes that it appears correct to him. Mordekhai writes there that according to all views, if he expressly transferred her jewelry to her, the creditor cannot collect it from her.
Tur, seif 46, and Ba'al HaTerumot, Gate 1. Rabbenu Yerucham likewise writes this in the name of Ramah.
