Name of Judgment and
Beit Yosef. The Maggid Mishneh explains that the borrower brings out the pledge, or the agent seizes it outside. Rambam holds that even tools of one's trade may be seized as a pledge, although they are among the items left for the debtor when arranging collection, as explained in chapter 1 and in Tur siman 98. That arrangement applies at the time of collection, but not when taking a pledge.
The Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of Ramban: This applies where he was not accustomed to sleeping on a pillow and cushion. But if he had a pillow and cushion and was accustomed to sleeping on them, they are left for him. The Rosh likewise writes this in the name of Rabbenu Tam.
