Articles without which one cannot manage. This is Rambam's view. Although an agent of beit din does not generally arrange the debtor's necessities before taking collateral, there are articles without which it is almost impossible for the debtor to live, such as the couch upon which people in those times and places sat while eating. An agent of beit din may not take such an article at all. A pillow, however, although it is among the objects that must later be returned for the debtor's use, can be dispensed with, and therefore the agent may take it as collateral. See there.
