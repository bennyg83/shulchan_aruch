(Supplement) An officer of the beit din who comes, etc. This is Rambam's language. He understands that only the items enumerated in the baraita must be left with the debtor when collateral is taken. Maggid Mishneh, citing Ramban and Rashba, maintains that everything exempted at final collection must also be left at the time of taking collateral. The baraita and the passage in Arakhin support their view. According to them, the beit din never takes the debtor's essential property, but only the excess. Rosh and Tur, however, also hold that the full arrangement of exempt property applies only at collection, and they interpret the opening of the baraita accordingly. The mishnah concerning return of a pillow and plow is then explained either as a case where the creditor acted unlawfully or where the debtor surrendered the item voluntarily. Ramban instead holds that an item needed continuously is always left with the debtor, while one needed only by day or night may be taken when not needed and returned at the proper time, as the Yerushalmi states.
