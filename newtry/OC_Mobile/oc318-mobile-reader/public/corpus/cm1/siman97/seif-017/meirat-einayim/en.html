Items without which he cannot manage, etc. Rambam holds that although one does not formally arrange the debtor's necessities when merely taking collateral, but only when collecting the debt, nevertheless items without which he cannot live are left with him even at the collateral stage. Therefore he leaves him a bed and mat or bedding, but not necessarily a pillow, because he can manage without it. The pillow may be taken and returned at night, as explained above. The Mechaber added "a bed to sit on" because he asked in Beit Yosef why Rambam omitted it, but I explained that Rambam intentionally omitted it because one can manage without it.
A bed to sit on, etc. In their time people regularly sat on beds, especially while eating. For health, one should not sleep immediately after eating in the same place, but should first walk four cubits. In order not to forget, they commonly had a separate bed for sleeping at least four cubits from the bed on which they sat and ate. Therefore two beds are left for him.
A bed and bedding suitable for him. Meaning suitable for a person who is not poor and does not ordinarily sleep without bedding. A poor person can manage with a mat, which is harder than bedding.
