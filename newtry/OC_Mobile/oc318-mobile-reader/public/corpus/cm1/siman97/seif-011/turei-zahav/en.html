(In the name of Maharshal, in siman 96, Beit Yosef beginning, “And what he wrote, if they claim against a minor,” etc.) This applies only after the minor reaches majority, contrary to what appears from Rashi in HaGozel 98 concerning Rafram’s coercion of Rav Ashi. In Ketubot, Rashi himself explains otherwise, and that explanation is primary. The same is proved in HaGozel 112 regarding the question whether minors can become liable. See also the end of the responsa of Maharam of Padua.
