Food itself may be taken as collateral, etc. The Torah’s particular concern is with implements used to prepare food, because through them a person continually labors to obtain sustenance or prepares food from them. Actual food, such as bread, meat, or drink already in his possession, is different. After beit din has arranged for his support and left him food for thirty days, as stated below in seif 23, the excess may be taken. During those thirty days he can work and obtain food for the period afterward.
