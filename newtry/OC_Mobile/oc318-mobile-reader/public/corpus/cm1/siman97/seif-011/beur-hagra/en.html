Food-preparation implements. Tosafos there. Their proof is from Erchin, where the debtor is left food for thirty days, but the remainder may be seized. Thus one must leave him what the court's arrangement requires. This view holds that the same items which beit din leaves with him may not be taken as a pledge. The baraisa mentions some examples, but the same applies to all. The Maggid Mishneh writes similarly in the name of Ramban and Rashba. See below, seif 17. They interpret the passage as discussing seizure of collateral.
