It is like a documented loan. Sema says this means the written rental obligation can collect from encumbered property. I find that difficult. A rental deed is not ordinarily a collection document. It is written to establish the rental and prevent adverse possession claims, and the tenant can still plead payment. If payment can be pleaded, the deed should not have ordinary power against encumbered property. The better meaning of Tur is that once the obligation is written, the parties have created a formal written self-obligation from which they cannot retract. It is like a documented loan in that sense, not for collection from encumbered assets or elimination of the payment plea. The precise source of Tur's phrase remains unclear.
