Law 3 there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is simple in HaMekabel, Bava Metzia chapter 9, 103a. Both a chokher and a mekabel pay in produce, except that a mekabel gives the owner one third or one quarter of the crop according to his agreement, while a chokher gives a fixed quantity of produce.
