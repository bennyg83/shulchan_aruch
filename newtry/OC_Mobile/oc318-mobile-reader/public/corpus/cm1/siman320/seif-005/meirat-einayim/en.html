And he cannot say, "I added for you, etc." This is specifically where he did not explain at the time of accepting the field that he was adding to his payment for this reason. If he expressly did so, he must fulfill the condition. The Tur and Mechaber did not need to state that point because any condition made at the time of the rental or sharecropping arrangement is binding. In the preceding seif, by contrast, the stipulation was not made at the beginning but only later at the time of weeding, so it was necessary to teach that his explanation is effective against the other party's silence.
