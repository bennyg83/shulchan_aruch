Excavating the places where water gathers. Tur, following Rashi, explains the relevant work as making channels that bring water over the field. Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch speak of digging places in which water collects. Shitah Mekubetzet cites Raavad interpreting the term as a reservoir from which water is supplied to the planting beds. Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch follow that interpretation rather than Rashi's.
