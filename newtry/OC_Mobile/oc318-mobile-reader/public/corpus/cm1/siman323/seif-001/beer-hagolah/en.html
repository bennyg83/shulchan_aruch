a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 7. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Mishnah in Bava Metzia 106b and explains that even though he received the field for a fixed rental amount, whether the field produces little or much, the owner has not completely removed himself from the produce of the land, and the fixed amount is paid from that produce.
b) Ibid., as explained in the Gemara there on 106b, where Rav Ashi concedes.
c) The ruling follows Rav Ashi there, because he is the later authority.
d) Since the grapes were not damaged until after they had become wine in the tenant's barrels and his own house was afflicted, we say that his fortune caused the loss, Rashi.
