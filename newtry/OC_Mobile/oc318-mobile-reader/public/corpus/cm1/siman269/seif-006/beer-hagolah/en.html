Kaf) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 2, from Rabbi Chiyya bar Abba in the name of Rabbi Yochanan, Bava Metzia 10a.
Lamed) The Maggid Mishneh explains that once he said "acquire it" and the other remained silent, the finder is lifting the found object for his fellow and the fellow acquires it.
Mem) The Mishnah there on 9b. The Maggid Mishneh explains that the person was not appointed as an agent to acquire it through lifting, but only to hand it over. Rashi similarly explains on 10a.
Nun) There in the Mishnah, as Rashi explains the reason.
Samekh) Rambam explains that then it is unlike an ordinary found object, because in the ordinary case the claimant could have dismounted from the donkey and acquired it himself.
Ayin) See siman 183, seif 4, where it is written that even if the agent expressly agreed initially, he may retract, although he is considered deceitful. One can distinguish that there he retracts before witnesses before acquiring it. But if after acquiring he claims that he acquired it for himself, he is not believed, like the law of one who lifts a found object for another stated by the Rama here in the name of the Tur. I saw a similar distinction in the Sema there.
