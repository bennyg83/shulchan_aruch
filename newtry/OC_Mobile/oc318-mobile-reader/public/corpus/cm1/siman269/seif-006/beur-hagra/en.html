And the lifter does not, etc. The distinction is that when the owner says 'acquire it for me,' the person who silently lifts it is presumed to have fulfilled the agency and cannot later claim that he first acquired it for himself. If he says only 'give it to me,' the agency begins at delivery, so before delivery the lifter may retract and claim it. See 183.

Some say, etc. The first view is primary. With a found object, one who silently accepts the instruction is presumed to have acted as agent until he expressly retracts. Raavyah did not clearly dispute this, but only wrote that the matter was uncertain to him. See 183.
