The back. He said to him, “I am not in the back of his back, or before, to myself, and in his own direction, I am in the back of his back, and I am not sure who has come back to him, and I am not sure who has been called to him, and I am not sure who has been told by his father, and I am not sure who has been told by his father.” “Third:
Enjoy. And if he said to me, “I will be able to do it, but now I want to win it, and I will give you a chance to make it harder for you to do it, and I will not say that the words of the column are clear to you, and that it is difficult for you to do so.” My life and my soul:
Money. The Mordechai writes in the name of Raavyah: this is unlike a found object, which Reuven himself could have picked up from the ground. In a sale, however, which depends on purchase, if Reuven could have obtained money and bought it before the seller sold it to another, Reuven acquires the benefit; if not, he does not, because this is a case where one benefits and the other suffers no loss. Darkei Moshe cites this. See what is written on this in siman 183, seif katan 14.
