If a rider sees a found object and tells another, 'Acquire it for me,' the rider acquires when the other lifts it, even before it reaches him, and the lifter cannot claim that he lifted it for himself, even while holding it. If the rider said, 'Give it to me,' and the other picked it up and claimed it for himself, the picker acquires. If he first hands it to the rider and only afterward claims he had acquired it for himself, his claim is ineffective. Rama: If Reuven tells Shimon to buy certain merchandise jointly, Shimon buys it and later claims he bought only for himself, some require division as analogous to lifting a find for another. Others say that if Reuven had no money, Shimon acquires for himself; if Shimon agreed to buy for both, he acquired for both.
