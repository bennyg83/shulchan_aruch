And they rose through his action. See the Bach. Yam Shel Shlomo at the end of Chullin questions this ruling of the Rosh. His words are astonishing. The Rosh clearly proved the rule from the questioner's words, 'If it is in order to acquire them,' as explained by Rashi. The Rosh's question is therefore how one can acquire them by lifting through striking the nest, since he has not taken hold of them. Examine this carefully, for the matter is clear.
