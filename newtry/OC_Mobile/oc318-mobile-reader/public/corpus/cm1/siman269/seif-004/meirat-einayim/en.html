Since the deaf-mute did not acquire. By strict law the deaf-mute's lifting has no effect because he lacks legal understanding, as explained. The Sages instituted that a deaf-mute acquires when he lifts the entire object, or when two deaf-mutes lift it together, for the sake of peaceful relations. Here, however, he lifts only together with a competent adult, and his lifting cannot help the competent adult acquire the other half. No special enactment for peaceful relations is needed for the competent adult, who has many effective means of acquiring for himself. Since the competent adult does not acquire the half through the deaf-mute, the deaf-mute likewise does not acquire the other half through the competent adult, because there is no concern for quarrelling when the deaf-mute sees that the competent adult also did not acquire.
