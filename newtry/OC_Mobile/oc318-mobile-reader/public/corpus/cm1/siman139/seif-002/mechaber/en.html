If a third person comes and seizes the object while claiming that it belongs to him, the other two have no claim to it, since the person possessing it claims ownership. But if he seized it without making a claim, it is removed from his possession, and he has no remedy until he returns it to each of them, because he does not know to whom it should be returned. If the disputed property is land and he took possession and consumed its produce, the land itself cannot be stolen, but he must pay the produce to each claimant.
