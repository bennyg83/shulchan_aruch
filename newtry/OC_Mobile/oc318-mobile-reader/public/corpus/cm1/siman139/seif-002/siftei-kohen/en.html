Until he returns it to each one, etc. Tosafos support the Ramban, and their words also imply that Rashbam agrees that the third party need not pay each claimant separately. They must have had a different text of Rashbam, as Maharsha notes. The Rosh's language can also be understood consistently with Tosafos and Ramban. Nimukei Yosef likewise rules like the Ramban. In my view the Rambam, Hilchot To'en chapter 15, also supports this. Regarding land he writes that if a third party comes and seizes it from the two claimants, we remove it from him. If the law with a ship were that the interloper must repay each claimant separately, why would the Rambam omit that central rule and instead state only that with land the third party is removed? It appears that he sees no distinction between a ship and land: in every case we remove the third party, and he need not pay each claimant individually. Rabbenu Yerucham, netiv 11 part 2, states explicitly that if an outsider comes to steal it, we remove it from him. Had he followed the Tur, he would have written that the outsider must return it to each claimant. See Beit Hillel and examine carefully.
