(Seif 4, gloss) A matter about which the authorities disagree. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 352, writes that even the view holding that in every unresolved Talmudic question the burden of proof rests upon the claimant, namely the Rosh cited by the Tur in siman 30, agrees here that the parties divide the property, because neither is in possession. The same should apply to an unresolved question, since Terumat HaDeshen equates the two. See siman 167.
