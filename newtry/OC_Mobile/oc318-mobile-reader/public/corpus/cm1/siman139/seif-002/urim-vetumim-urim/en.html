That he return to each one, etc. Meaning that he has no atonement until he restores the item to each claimant in accordance with the law of theft, which requires return to the owner. Merely removing it from his hands and placing it again in a state of uncertainty is not enough. Sema. The Shach argues at length that Tosafot follow Ramban, who does not require return to each one, and he proves Rambam likewise agrees. Although parts of his discussion can be questioned, his practical conclusion appears correct.
