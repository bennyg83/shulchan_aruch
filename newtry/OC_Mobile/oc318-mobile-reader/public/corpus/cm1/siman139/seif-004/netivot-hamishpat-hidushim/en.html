And if it is not applicable. See the Shach. This applies specifically to property that cannot be divided at all, such as the case at the end of siman 170. But where division is possible, division is preferable. We say "whoever is stronger prevails" only where both make definite claims, or where the matter may later be clarified, because perhaps witnesses will come and testify, which could make a prior division by beit din appear mistaken and contradictory. But where the matter cannot be clarified and neither party makes a definite claim, division is preferable. All this concerns the initial ruling.
