And if division is not possible, etc. The Shach discusses this at length, explaining that the Rama's ruling, whose source is Terumat HaDeshen, concerns a case where division is impossible. See Tumim. There is no need for lengthy proof, because this is explicit in Terumat HaDeshen. The Shach nevertheless believes there is clear proof that in a dispute among authorities the Rosh's rationale cannot apply, namely that the person who knows the law is on his side will exert himself more strongly. See there. In Tumim I rejected his proof. Nevertheless, the law itself is correct: where the matter cannot ultimately be clarified, we do not apply 'whoever is stronger prevails.' This is explicit in the Gemara and stated by Terumat HaDeshen. Although I initially thought the Rambam might hold that 'whoever is stronger prevails' applies even where clarification is impossible, I retracted. The rule is clearly as stated above. See there.
