The law of two people disputing an object, and the law if a third person seized it. It contains 4 se'ifim.
If two people dispute an object that neither possesses, like the case of a ship over which two people quarreled and each said, 'It is mine,' the stronger party prevails. It belongs to the one who first gains control until the other brings proof. The opposing party may nevertheless require him to swear, and he must swear that it is his. Until the other brings proof, even if that other later becomes stronger, he is not permitted to take it from the person who first gained control.
