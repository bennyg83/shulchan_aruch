The tongue of the column:
A strong chapter (in the wake of a page) and a ship that is built:
Tur. Rosh writes this there in his rulings: the Sages ruled that the stronger party prevails in this instance, relying upon several considerations. First, the party whose claim is true is more likely to bring proof. Second, the true owner will be more willing to risk himself to retain his own property than the other party will be to steal. Third, the wrongful claimant will reason: what do I gain by risking myself today if tomorrow the other party may bring proof and remove it from me?
There and there, by Dalai, it turns out, that they will be wise to all their days in the quarrel and controversy
