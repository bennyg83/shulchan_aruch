(Seif 1) Whoever is stronger prevails. The Rosh explains this rule by saying that the true owner will rely on the evidence he possesses. This is difficult in light of siman 170 concerning a water channel, where the law is also “whoever is stronger prevails,” although there the law was not decided between Rav and Shmuel and no party can bring evidence that the property is his. The rest of the Rosh's explanation cited by Beit Yosef likewise does not apply there. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 352, writes that perhaps the Sages enacted the same procedural rule there even though its ordinary rationale is absent.
