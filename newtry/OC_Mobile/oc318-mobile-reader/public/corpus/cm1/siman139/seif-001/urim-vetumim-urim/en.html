And every single man said, “If every Hashem has brought witnesses that he is his or his fathers, or that he eats two of them as strong as he is, and he is the one who is the one who is called, and he is the one who is the most powerful.”
It is necessary to swear, and so on, that it is a simple and simple one:
Even if he later overpowers him, etc. The Shach notes that Tosafot in Bava Metzia appear to hold that if the second party later retakes the item, he acquires it, and Hagahot Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot say the same. See Tumim. I showed through the sugya that Rosh can still be reconciled with Tosafot: Tosafot's statement belongs to an earlier stage of the Gemara, while under the final conclusion they agree with Rosh. Therefore Mechaber should not lightly be rejected. Nevertheless, according to Mechaber's broader view that seizure after a doubt arises can be effective, the practical result still requires study.
