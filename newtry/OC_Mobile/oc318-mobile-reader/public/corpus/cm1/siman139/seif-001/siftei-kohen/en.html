"And I have to swear to him."
Even if his hand becomes stronger, we do not permit it. See below, siman 245, seif 11. Tosafot to Bava Metzia 6a, Hagahot Mordechai, Hagahot Ashiri, and Agudah there in the name of Ri clearly indicate that if the other party seizes it back, he acquires it. See there. The same appears in Hagahot Maimoniyot at the end of chapter 10 of Hilchot To'en, cited in Responsa Mabit, volume 1, second set, siman 31. In my opinion, all these authorities escaped the attention of the Beit Yosef, for he did not cite them at all. Therefore, as a matter of law, if the other party seizes it back, it is not removed from him, since this is a dispute among leading authorities and they also constitute the majority. See Responsa Maharashdam, siman 328.
