Although. This is Rambam's language, as above. He holds there is no distinction between whether he was sued or not. In all cases the defendant must himself make the defense. See Beer HaGolah. The Raavad likewise writes this, and his proof is from the Tosefta cited above: "When... he may retract." Since it says "he may retract," this shows that an actual retraction or defense is necessary. The case there concerns the defense that he admitted so as not to appear wealthy.
And some say that even... This follows from Rav Nachman's statement, "a person is likely..." where he did not require the defendant to make the defense himself, and from chapter HaNoseh there, "if he did not say..." The first opinion holds that it is possible that the defendant did in fact raise the defense. See Sefer HaTerumos, section 42.
