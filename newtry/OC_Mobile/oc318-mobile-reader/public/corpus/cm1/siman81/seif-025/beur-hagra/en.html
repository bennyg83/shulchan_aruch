Reuven, etc.; but, etc. This is as stated above in seif 6: the rule is effective only where, etc.; but if, etc.
Even, etc. A person is not believed to impose liability on others even where he thereby also causes himself a loss. Ketubot states this concerning a lender, and Rosh and Sefer HaTerumot write explicitly that even if he also harms himself, we still suspect collusion. See siman 47.
