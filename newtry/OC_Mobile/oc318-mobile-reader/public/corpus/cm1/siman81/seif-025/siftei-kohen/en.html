It constitutes an admission with respect to Shimon, etc. The Sema writes that the novelty is that Shimon is liable for the entire amount, as the Tur rules above in siman 77, seif 5. The Bach writes that if they were not partners, Shimon need pay only half. If they were partners, Shimon admitted that they jointly received the money for the partnership, and partners become responsible and guarantors for one another, as explained above in siman 77, seif 1. It is possible that the Sema intended the same. See below, seif 31, and my comments there in seif katan 77.
