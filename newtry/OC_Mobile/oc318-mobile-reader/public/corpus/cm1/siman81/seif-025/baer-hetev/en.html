Shimon. The Sema writes that the novelty is that Shimon is liable for the entire amount, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write in siman 77, seif 5, and the same applies here. The Bach writes that if they are not partners, Shimon is liable to pay only half. If they are partners, he admitted that they received the money together for the needs of the partnership, and partners become mutually responsible and guarantors for one another, as written in siman 77, seif 1. See there. It is possible that this is also the Sema's intent. See below, seif 31. Shach.
