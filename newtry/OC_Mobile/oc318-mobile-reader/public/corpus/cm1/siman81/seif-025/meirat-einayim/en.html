If Reuven said to the witnesses, 'You are my witnesses,' etc. The Mechaber copies the Tur, who states this in the name of a responsum of the Rosh. According to the Rosh, the phrase 'you are my witnesses' is specifically required.
According to Rambam, a complete admission suffices even without those words.It is an admission with respect to Shimon. This is itself obvious. The novelty appears to be that Shimon is liable for the entire amount once he admitted the entire debt, as the Tur and Mechaber explain in siman 77, seif 5, concerning Reuven who sues two people who borrowed together and one denies while the other admits.
