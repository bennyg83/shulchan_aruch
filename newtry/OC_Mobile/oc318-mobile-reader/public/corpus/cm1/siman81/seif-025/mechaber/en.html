If Reuven says to Shimon and Levi, 'You have one hundred of mine in your possession,' Shimon answers, 'Yes,' and Levi remains silent, then if Reuven tells the witnesses, 'You are my witnesses,' and Shimon remains silent, this is an admission with respect to Shimon but not with respect to Levi. Even if Shimon and Levi are partners, one does not become liable through the admission of the other.
