(Shach, seif katan 9) And specifically a woman, etc. Note: This requires study. The Mordechai in the name of the Raavyah writes that because she borrowed in his presence and he remained silent, it is as though he admitted that the seven zekukim in her possession belonged to her and that he had no share in them, whether they came to her through a find or through savings from her household allowance. The words of the Taz in Even HaEzer, end of siman 106, require even greater study. He cites this Mordechai and explains that the protection is effective because the obligation is certain, as in a ketubah, which will ultimately have to be paid after she takes the appropriate oath.
