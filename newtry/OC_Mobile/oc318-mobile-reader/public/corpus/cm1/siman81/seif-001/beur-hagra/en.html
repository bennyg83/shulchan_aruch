And he must swear... Since the claimant asserts a monetary claim against him, as stated in Shevuos 42a: there is a chazakah that a person does not, etc. See the Shach.
And not... for one does not... We do not say that a person was joking in every case. It applies only where people commonly speak jokingly, as stated in Shitah Mekubetzes on the second chapter of Bava Basra 175a.
One who said to his son in law... Since in any event there is a mitzvah to teach one's son, as stated in the first chapter of Kiddushin, he can say that he was merely joking, as stated in Yevamos 106a: "He said to him, I was joking with you..." See Rashi there, s.v. "he said to him, since..."
