Even when the witnesses were concealed, they may testify to the loan. Concealed witnesses are ineffective specifically regarding an admission, because the defendant can say, “I was jesting with you.” Regarding an actual loan, however, their testimony is effective, because a borrower is liable even if no witnesses were present at all. Witnesses were created only to prevent falsehood. These are the words of the Rivash cited by the Beit Yosef above, at the end of siman 32. See what I wrote above, siman 79, seif 4, seif katan 13.
