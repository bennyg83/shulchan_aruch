(Seif 24) “Moreover.” The opening clause is not merely a lesser included case, because if saying “You are my witnesses” were wholly unnecessary there would be no reason to state it. The first clause teaches that the declaration is effective even when made outside the claimant’s presence and even where Reuven did not come with Levi’s authorization. The “moreover” clause, however, must specifically concern a case where Reuven did not act under Levi’s authorization. Its reason is that Levi never demanded payment, so Shimon cannot say that Levi mocked him and that he responded in kind. If Reuven acted as Levi’s authorized agent, Shimon could say, “I mocked you through your agent just as you mocked me through him.” Thus the two clauses address different points.
