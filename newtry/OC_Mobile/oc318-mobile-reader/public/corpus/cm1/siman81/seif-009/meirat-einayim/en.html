When witnesses to the event come and testify, etc. The Tur writes that he is liable because an admission is equivalent to the loan itself. Just as with a loan we do not require the borrower to accept the witnesses upon himself before they can testify, but once they see the loan they are believed to testify to it, so too with an admission. Even though he did not formally accept the later witnesses, they are believed, because the obligation arose from the moment of the admission and their testimony merely reveals what occurred.
