If Reuven says to Shimon, "You owe Levi one hundred," and Shimon says, "Yes," and Reuven says, "You are witnesses," this constitutes an admission. When Levi later sues Shimon, Shimon cannot claim that he was jesting or that he made the admission merely so as not to appear wealthy. Moreover, even if Reuven did not say, "You are witnesses," Shimon cannot say to Levi, "I was merely jesting with you."
