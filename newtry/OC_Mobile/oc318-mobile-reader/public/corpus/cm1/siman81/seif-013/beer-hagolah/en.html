He is called to be jealous of him
Rashi explains: 'Do you want everyone listening to serve as witnesses, awake or asleep?' He knew this person would not admit before witnesses, so he thought perhaps the person would assume everyone was asleep and say yes. The same applies if he hid witnesses in a cemetery and said, 'The living and the dead shall be witnesses against you,' as in the incident there. The Sema likewise cites it.
