And he answered, etc. If he answered yes, Rabbi Meir HaLevi Abulafia and Rosh dispute the matter. According to Rabbi Meir HaLevi Abulafia it is an admission, because saying, 'I will admit to you,' is like an admission before witnesses. According to Rosh it is ineffective. The Shach writes that the practical law requires study, though the arguments incline toward Rosh.
