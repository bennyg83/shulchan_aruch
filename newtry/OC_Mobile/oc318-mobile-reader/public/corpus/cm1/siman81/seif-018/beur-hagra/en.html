Admitted... See the Shach there, who writes, "and I found further..." It appears to me that there is proof for his view from Sanhedrin there, concerning a written acknowledgment that did not, etc. If the Shach were incorrect, even if the acknowledgment was written, there would still be a concern that perhaps he never said "you are my witnesses," so long as one does not accept Reish Lakish's chazakah, etc. In Kesubos there, the Gemara concludes, "Rather..." and establishes the case where he said, "I owe you by document." Rambam explains that he said those exact words, and then there is no need for "you are my witnesses." So too Sefer HaTerumos, section 42. Certainly the same applies in a case of this sort.
