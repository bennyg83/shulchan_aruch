(Seif 8: “He pays on their testimony.”) The Sema struggles to explain why the defendant cannot say that he admitted merely so that people would not think him wealthy, as in seif 14. The Mechaber can be understood as dealing with a defendant who expressly says, “I was joking with you.” He thereby abandons the alternative explanation that he wished not to appear wealthy. The joking defense fails because the admission was formal. The Rambam, source of this view, actually distinguishes whether the admission was made in the creditor’s presence, as the Mechaber records in seif 17. The Tur’s wording in siman 32, “this person has such-and-such with me,” uses “this person” to indicate that the admission was made before him; therefore the “not to appear wealthy” defense does not apply.
