The claim of jesting does not help. Hagahot Maimoniyot to chapter 6 of Hilchot To'en VeNit'an and the Mordechai's glosses to chapter Zeh Borer write that with respect to any condition that people stipulate and then carry out between themselves, one cannot later say, "I was merely jesting with you." This is cited by Darkei Moshe in siman 32:19.
