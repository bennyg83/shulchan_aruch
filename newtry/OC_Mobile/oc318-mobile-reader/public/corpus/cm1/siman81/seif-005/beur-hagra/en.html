It does not help... Rava's question in the chapter Get Pashut whether we say that a person jokes at the time of death proves this. If such a plea were made automatically for the heirs, the Gemara could have resolved that we do not say so from Sanhedrin, where a person said while dying, "So and so..." and Rabbi Yishmael and Rabbi Chiya dispute the matter because of the claim that he spoke in order not to appear wealthy. Without that, the heirs would already be exempt because we would plead for them that the deceased had merely been joking, as stated above in seif 3.
