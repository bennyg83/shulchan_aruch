A plea of joking does not help, etc. Conditions that people make with one another are generally not subject to a plea of joking. Darkei Moshe and Sema. This means a condition concerning something the other person was not already obligated to do. But where the matter was already obligatory, such as an undertaking to perform chalitzah or the like, the plea of joking can certainly apply. See seif 1 concerning one who promised something to his son-in-law. Likewise, a completed act is not subject to a plea of joking. Therefore one who betroths a woman cannot later say, 'I was merely joking with you.' See what I wrote in my Chiddushim to Even HaEzer.
