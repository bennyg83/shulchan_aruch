If he gave... Meaning, he told the witnesses that he gave it to him for the purpose of earning a profit. See Sefer HaTerumos.
It is not within his power. As stated above in seif 26: what he gave is given. It is as though he said, "you are my witnesses," and is analogous to one who says, "You have a maneh of mine," and the other answers, "I never owed you," who is liable at the beginning of chapter HaDayanim. He is not believed, after making his own admission, through a migo, because that would be a migo against witnesses, since a litigant's admission, etc. So too writes the Shach.
Nevertheless... See Beer HaGolah.
He swears. As stated in chapter Kol HaNishba'in concerning those who swear even without a definite claim, etc.
And there is an opinion... Since the remainder is still in his possession, he did not have reason to be precise. It is like the rule stated above in seif 26 that he can say, "I was joking..." regarding, etc. See the Shach.
