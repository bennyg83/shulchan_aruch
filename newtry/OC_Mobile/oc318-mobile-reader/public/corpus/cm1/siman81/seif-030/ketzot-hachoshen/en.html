Before witnesses. Baal HaTerumot writes in the name of Raavad that the reason is that the person can otherwise say, "I was joking with you." The Shach infers from this that Raavad allows a joking defense even against a volunteered admission. According to what I wrote above, however, the reason a volunteered admission ordinarily cannot be dismissed as joking is that there must be a recognizable explanation for why the person would joke. Merely saying that he spoke in jest is not enough, even in prohibitory law. When the other person first makes a claim against him, the explanation is clear: "Just as you joked with me, I joked with you." Here there is likewise an evident explanation. He made the statement in order to induce the other person to leave his money with him. The Shach notices this point but considers it strained. In my view it is the natural explanation and fully resolves Raavad's ruling.
