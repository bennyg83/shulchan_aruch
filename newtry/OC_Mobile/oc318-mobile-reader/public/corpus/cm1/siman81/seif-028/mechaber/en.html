If a person said, 'I have one hundred belonging to so-and-so,' without saying, 'You are my witnesses,' and immediately swore to his admission, then when the other person comes and sues him, he is not heard if he answers, 'I intended only not to make myself appear wealthy, and the oath was false.' Similarly, he is not heard if he claims, 'I forgot, swore because I thought I owed him, and now remember that I owe him nothing.' {Rama: Giving one's hand as a pledge has the law of an oath. This applies only when he admits the matter or there are witnesses. If he denies it, or says that he pledged his hand only upon such-and-such condition, he is believed.}
