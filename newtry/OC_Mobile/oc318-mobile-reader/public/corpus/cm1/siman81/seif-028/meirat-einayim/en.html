Baal HaTerumot concludes that the defendant claims, 'God will punish me for my false oath, but beit din cannot obligate me to pay because of it.'We do not listen to him.
The Tur's formulation is that there is a presumption that a person swears only truthfully and with clarity.I forgot and swore. Meaning, when he swore he had forgotten that he had already paid and thought otherwise, so the oath was mistaken rather than intentionally false.And a handshake, etc.
Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that where a person obligated himself by a handshake, it has a status comparable to a solemn undertaking and is not lightly treated as forgotten or mistaken.
The legal effect therefore depends on whether his later explanation is compatible with the original undertaking or directly contradicts it.
