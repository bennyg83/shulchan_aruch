The Rambam at the beginning of chapter 7 of Hilkhot To'en, cited by the Tur. The Kesef Mishneh writes that the Rambam learned this from the Mishnah's phrase, 'until they say: he admitted before us,' meaning an actual admission. The Gemara's statement supporting Rav Yehudah means that if he did not explicitly mention admission, it is insufficient. The Yerushalmi likewise says in Rabbi Yose's name citing Rabbi Yochanan that if he intended to transmit testimony to him, etc.
