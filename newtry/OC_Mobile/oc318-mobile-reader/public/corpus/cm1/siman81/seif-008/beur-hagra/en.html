He said before, etc. Bava Batra 175a and Rif distinguish a mere statement from one made by way of formal admission. In the latter case it resembles one who delivers his words for legal record, as in Sanhedrin. See Baer HaGolah.
Although. As stated concerning one who says, 'A maneh to so-and-so.' When the admission is made before the claimant, Rambam holds that he cannot explain it as having been said merely to avoid appearing wealthy. See seif 14.
