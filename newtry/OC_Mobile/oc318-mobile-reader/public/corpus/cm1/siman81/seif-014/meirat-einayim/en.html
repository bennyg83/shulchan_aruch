I admitted only so that I would not appear wealthy.
Meaning, I did not want to appear to be a wealthy and fully satisfied person.He is believed.
Even though his admission was made in a full form, Rambam also holds that he may explain that he admitted only so that people would not think him wealthy, as explained above in seif 8 in his name.
What Rambam writes below in siman 255, seif 2, that if a person spoke by way of admission and there was no concern of trickery, the money is given accordingly, is not difficult.
The phrase 'there was no concern of trickery' means that the circumstances did not allow the explanation that he admitted merely in order not to appear wealthy.
Where that explanation is plausible, it remains a valid defense even against a formal admission.
