Reuven gave one hundred to Shimon and Levi for investment. They executed a document obligating themselves and swore to give him half the profit. Reuven demanded his money, and they gave him one hundred. When he demanded the profit, they told him, 'We earned such-and-such, and no more.' Reuven said to Shimon, 'You told me that you earned fifty,' and Shimon replied, 'I told you nothing.' Because Reuven's document against them was still in force and bore no notation of payment, they were compelled to give him the amount he claimed. Levi now sues Reuven, claiming that he wrongfully took profit from them because they had earned none. The law follows Reuven.
