[Sema, letter 54] For he knows the truth, that he does not owe him. Note: According to this, where one says, “I hold one maneh belonging to the father of these orphans,” the rationale that he admitted only so as not to make himself appear wealthy should not apply. Yet from the Beit Yosef’s objection above, at the beginning of siman 75, section 5, to Responsa HaRosh there, it is clear that he holds that even in such a case we apply that rationale. See Responsa Bnei Aharon, siman 60, who indeed wished to resolve the Beit Yosef’s difficulties in this manner but rejected the resolution. See there.
