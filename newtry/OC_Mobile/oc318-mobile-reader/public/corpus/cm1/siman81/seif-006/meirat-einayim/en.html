And they designated them as witnesses, etc.Whether he said, etc. All this concerns an admission made before witnesses.
An admission made in beit din requires none of these formulations. Later in this siman, seif 22, the Mechaber repeats the law and states explicitly that an admission in beit din requires neither 'you are my witnesses' nor another formal expression of admission. Although that seif speaks of a beit din of three, that is according to the Geonim and Rambam.
According to the Rosh and Tur, the same applies before a single expert judge, as explained in siman 3 and again later in this siman.But he may claim, 'I repaid.' This admission is no stronger than a loan made before witnesses, and we rule that one who borrowed before witnesses need not repay before witnesses. Therefore, after an effective admission outside beit din, he may still claim repayment unless a separate rule prevents that claim.
