And he designated them as witnesses. This requirement applies to ordinary witnesses. A formal beit din need not be expressly designated. Sema.
And he remained silent, etc. The same rule applies to an oral loan where the lender says to witnesses in the borrower's presence, "Write a document," and the borrower remains silent. They may write it. Bach and Shach. This follows the opinion above in siman 39:2 that the witnesses need not later consult the borrower again. According to the view that they must consult him before writing, the issue has little practical effect, because they must ask him in any event, and if he then agrees the result is obvious. See what I wrote above in siman 39.
But he may claim that he paid. We rule that one who borrows, and certainly one who merely admits a debt, in the presence of witnesses is not required to repay specifically before witnesses.
