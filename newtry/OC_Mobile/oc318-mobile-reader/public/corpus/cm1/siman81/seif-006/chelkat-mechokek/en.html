He must annul her vow, lest he divorce her. Although the vow does not take effect until after the divorce, he can nevertheless annul it, as the Ran writes. See Tosafot and Ran as to why the Gemara specifically says 'lest he divorce her,' since even without divorce she can release herself from her obligation to work by saying, 'I will not be supported and I will not work.' If so, a wife would resemble a field regarding which one says, 'when I redeem it, it shall be consecrated,' where the consecration takes effect immediately. The same should apply here, so that the konam should take effect immediately. See there.
