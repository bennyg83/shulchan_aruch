Of three. Maharit writes in Choshen Mishpat, siman 46, that regarding an admission everyone agrees that even if none of the three judges is learned, since they need not decide a legal question, they have the status of beit din just as though one learned judge were among them. See there.
Whether another person claimed from him, etc. See Shach, who holds that if a person spontaneously admits on his own initiative, he can retract even if the admission was made before beit din. See Tumim, where I wrote that one should not depart from the ruling of Tur and Mechaber.
