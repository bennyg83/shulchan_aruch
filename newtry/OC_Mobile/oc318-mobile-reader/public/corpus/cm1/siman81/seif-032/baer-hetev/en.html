To retract. See siman 292, seif 7 in the gloss, where if Reuven demands his money so that he can profit with it and the holder keeps it because Reuven owes him money, the holder is liable for the profits from that point onward. One may distinguish that there the money was a deposit, whereas here it was a loan. See what I wrote there. See also Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 8, siman 70; Yoreh Deah 177, seifim 14 and 18; and Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 94 and 95. Shach.
