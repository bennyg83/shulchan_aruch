For even according to Reuven's own words this was not a condition, etc. See below, siman 292:7 in Rama's gloss, that if Reuven demands his money so that he can earn with it and the other person retains it, he is liable for the profit from that point onward. Here we must therefore be dealing with a case where the time for repayment had not yet arrived, or alternatively where Reuven had not demanded his money.
