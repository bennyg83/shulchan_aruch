For even according to Reuven’s words, etc. See below in siman 292, seif 7, in the Rama’s gloss, that where Reuven demands the return of his money so that he may profit from it and the custodian wrongfully retains it, the custodian owes the profits from that point onward. The Sema therefore says the present case must concern a debt not yet due, or a case in which Reuven did not demand his money. One may also distinguish that the rule there concerns a deposit, whereas this case concerns a loan. Rosh’s responsum here expressly deals with dowry money. See my comments below in siman 292, seif 7; Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 8, siman 70; Yoreh Deah 177, Siftei Kohen seif katan 41; and Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 94 and 95.
