If Reuven demands from Shimon the profit on a debt that has been outstanding for several years, claiming that Shimon stipulated that he would pay him profit, while Shimon says there was no such stipulation, Shimon is exempt even from an oath. Even according to Reuven, the condition was not made when Shimon first became obligated. Even if Shimon later said, 'I will give you profit,' these were mere words without a kinyan, and he may retract.
