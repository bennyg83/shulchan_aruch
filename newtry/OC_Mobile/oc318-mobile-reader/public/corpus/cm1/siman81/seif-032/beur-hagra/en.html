Reuven claimed, etc. As stated in Bava Metzia 65b concerning one who sold, etc. Since the purchaser, etc. See Tosafot there. The same principle appears in Kiddushin concerning a stipulation that one is a kohen, even though the promisor himself stated it, and in many similar cases. This is the meaning of 'even though,' etc. Wherever the defendant's admission would exempt him, he need not swear. Alternatively, he has no oath obligation at all, since his admission exempts him. See Rambam, chapter 1 of To'en, halachah 15.
