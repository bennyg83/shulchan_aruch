He may say, “I was jesting,” etc. The Sema explains that the admission was made in response to a demand. This must indeed be said according to the Mechaber’s ruling above in seif 5. The language, however, is taken from Baal HaTerumot, gate 42, part 6, and there it bears its simple meaning. As I explained above in seif 5, seif katan 12, Baal HaTerumot holds that the defense of jesting applies even to a volunteered admission, unlike Gidulei Terumah, page 183b. See there.
But what he already gave remains given. Beit Yosef writes above in siman 32, novel ruling 5, that Raavad’s words as quoted by Baal HaTerumot appear to disagree and to hold that even what was already given must be returned. In my view, Raavad does not disagree. He merely compares the case to the remaining fifty that has not yet been paid, as I explain below in seif katan 78.
