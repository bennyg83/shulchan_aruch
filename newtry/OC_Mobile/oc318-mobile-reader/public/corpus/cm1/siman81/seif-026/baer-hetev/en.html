Jesting. The Sema explains that the admission followed a demand. The case applies even if the defendant says he intended to jest regarding the entire maneh, and therefore the text must say that whatever he already gave remains given. This reading is necessary according to the Mechaber in seif 5. The wording, however, comes from Baal HaTerumot, who holds that a jest defense can apply even to a volunteered admission, contrary to Gidulei Terumah. Shach.
