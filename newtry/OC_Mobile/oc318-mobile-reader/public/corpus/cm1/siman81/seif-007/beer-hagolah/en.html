There and there in the cave
He's in a column
Rashi rules so, and likewise the Rashbam, from the statement in Kiddushin 43a, 'If so, one who betroths a woman,' etc. This shows that regarding any condition people make and fulfill between themselves, one cannot say, 'I was merely jesting with you.' Mordechai and Hagahot Ashri in Zeh Borer, Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 9 of Hilkhot To'en VeNitan, and Beit Yosef in siman 32.
