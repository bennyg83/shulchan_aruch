There is an opinion that the defense, etc. The Tur writes this in the name of the Rosh both here and in siman 32 without citing any dissenting view, and neither the Beit Yosef nor Darkei Moshe explicitly mentions anyone who disagrees. As I have written several times, the Mechaber's formula "There is an opinion" does not necessarily imply a dispute. Here, however, it may be that he uses it because according to the Rambam, who holds that whenever one admits in the claimant's presence he cannot claim that he did so merely not to appear wealthy, as the Mechaber states in seif 11, the same would apply even if the claimant had not first demanded payment. Once the admission was made in his presence, the defense is unavailable. This is clear.
