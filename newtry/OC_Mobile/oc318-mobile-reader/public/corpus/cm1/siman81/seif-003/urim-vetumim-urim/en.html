But if he does not make the claim, we do not make it for him. See Tumim, where I reconciled Mordechai on this point. This applies where he admits that he made the earlier statement. But if he denies and says, 'It never happened,' and witnesses later come, we already explained that we make the claim for him that he forgot because the earlier words were merely in jest, and he is not established as a false denier.
But for his heirs, etc. This refers to an admission made while he was healthy and he later died. If the admission was made while he was deathly ill, there is no plea of joking at the time of death at all.
