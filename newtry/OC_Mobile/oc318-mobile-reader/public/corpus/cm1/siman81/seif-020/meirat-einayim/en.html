Where he was already presumed to owe him. Meaning, if he was not already presumed to owe him, then even if it was later established that he had not owed him previously, he would nevertheless have to pay after making a fully binding admission, according to the Rambam, or after saying, "You are my witnesses," according to the Rosh. We would say that he deliberately obligated himself as a complete gift through the admission or delivery of the document, as explained at the beginning of siman 40. See there.
