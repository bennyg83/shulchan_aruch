Presumed. The Sema explains that otherwise, even if it later became clear that no debt truly existed, the defendant could still be liable if he made a fully binding admission according to Rambam, or said "you are my witnesses" according to Rosh. In that setting we say he voluntarily subjected himself to an obligation through the admission or delivery of a document, as at the beginning of siman 40. The Shach notes that many authorities in siman 40 disagree and hold that "you are my witnesses" cannot create a new obligation where none existed. Even according to Rambam, who does recognize voluntary assumption, perhaps only the explicit formula "you are my witnesses" works, not every formal admission. The Sema gives no basis for equating them. Rambam's rule that a formal admission is like "you are my witnesses" may concern only the inability to later claim jest, not the power to create a debt that never existed. See Responsa Maharam Lublin 135.
