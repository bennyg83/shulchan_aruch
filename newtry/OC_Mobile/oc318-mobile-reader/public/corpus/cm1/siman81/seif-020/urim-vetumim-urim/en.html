If he knows that he does not actually owe the other person but nevertheless obligates himself to pay him, the obligation is valid, as explained above in siman 40. If he did not say, 'You are my witnesses,' but spoke in a formal manner of admission in the claimant's presence, in circumstances where neither joking nor the plea of not appearing wealthy is available, Sema holds that he can thereby obligate himself. The Shach holds that even according to Rambam he must still say, 'You are my witnesses.' See Tumim, where I established that Sema is correct.
