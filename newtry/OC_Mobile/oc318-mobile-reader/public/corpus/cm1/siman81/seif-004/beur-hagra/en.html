There is an opinion... This is analogous to one who was sued for wheat and admitted barley, as stated in Bava Kamma 35b. Sefer HaTerumos adopts the view of Ramah, cited by the Rosh there, that in the case of wheat and barley the admission of barley can be dismissed as joking. So too Sefer HaTerumos, section 7, part 2, writes in the name of the Raavad. He does not reject the Raavad's words, but merely writes, "and it appears reasonable..." The same conclusion can also be reconciled with the other explanations.
At the time of his admission. See siman 88, seif 12 in the Rama: "And some say, specifically..." This is the view of Sefer HaTerumos, section 7, part 2. This is why Sefer HaTerumos writes here, "at the time of his admission." There he writes that even witnesses who saw the matter are effective, and this is the meaning here of "although there are witnesses..." See also section 42.
