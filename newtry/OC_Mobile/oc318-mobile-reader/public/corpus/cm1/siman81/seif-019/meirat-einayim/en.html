Even though he did not say, 'You are my witnesses,' etc. According to what was explained in seif 17, where he physically delivered the handwritten acknowledgment into the claimant's possession, no oral admission at all is required. Therefore the wording here, 'even though he did not say, "You are my witnesses,"' is not exact. Even if he made no oral admission before witnesses, the delivery of the writing itself is enough. Since this case concerns a document not signed by witnesses, however, it must at least have been delivered to him before witnesses. The point is that at the time of delivery he need not say, 'You are my witnesses.'
