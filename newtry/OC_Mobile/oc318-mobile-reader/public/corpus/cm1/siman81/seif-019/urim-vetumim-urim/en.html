One who delivers, etc. The Shach discusses Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam at length concerning the opening of the chapter *HaNosei*. There is no need for this according to what I wrote: we rule that delivery witnesses effectuate a document. Therefore any writing, provided it is on a medium not capable of forgery, can have the status of a full deed through proper delivery. The novelty is that one might have thought delivery witnesses work only where the parties specifically designate them as witnesses. See what I wrote above in siman 40. This also answers Sema's question about what new point is taught here.
