[Rama] For there was some degree of claim against him. Note: This implies that where there was no claim at all and he admitted that he holds property belonging to orphans, we say that he made the admission only so as not to make himself appear wealthy. We do not say that since the orphans will suspect that he truly has property of their father, he should not have made such an admission. This also appears from the Beit Yosef in siman 75, from his objection there to Responsa HaRosh in the case involving the claim against Rabbi Uziah. This is contrary to Responsa Pnei Moshe, part 1, siman 8, which writes that in such a case we do not apply the rationale of avoiding the appearance of wealth. See what I wrote in the marginal note in this siman on Sema, seif katan 54.
