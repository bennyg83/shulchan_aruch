An admission before one witness. See Shach seif katan 10, where he says that he does not understand the Ramban’s statement that a certain interpretation had already been rejected, and criticizes the Ramban as though he had not understood Baal HaMaor. In the selections appended to Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 27, the Chacham Tzvi sharply objects to the Shach. He writes that the Shach should be forgiven for suspecting the Ramban of an error obvious even to schoolchildren, and that in truth it is the Shach who failed to understand both the beginning of the Ramban’s reasoning and the position of Baal HaMaor.
