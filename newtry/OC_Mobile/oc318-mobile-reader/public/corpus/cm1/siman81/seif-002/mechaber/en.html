This rule applies to a healthy person. But if a person on his deathbed is sued by another and admits the claim, he cannot later say, "I was merely jesting with you," because a person does not jest at the time of death. Rama: The same applies when others admit a claim to the person on his deathbed or remain silent in response to his instructions. Their conduct is treated as an admission, and they cannot say that they acted only so as not to anger the dying person.
