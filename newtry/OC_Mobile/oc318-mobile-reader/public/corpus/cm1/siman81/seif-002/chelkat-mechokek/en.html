She works and eats. The same applies that she herself may benefit from her earnings, since the consecration does not take effect. Likewise, if one consecrates his own future earnings, he works and eats, because the consecration does not take effect upon something that has not yet come into existence. Tosafot write this in Ketubot 59a, s.v. 'he need not annul.' The case of a husband's consecrating his wife's earnings is mentioned because in a case where he says, 'Your hands are consecrated,' even his wife's earnings can become consecrated.
