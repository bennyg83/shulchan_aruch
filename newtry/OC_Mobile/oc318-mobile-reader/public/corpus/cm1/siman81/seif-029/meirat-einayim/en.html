Nor the defense that he intended not to make himself appear wealthy. The Tur concludes: To what is this comparable? To one who repays his debt in the presence of witnesses. The act itself is completed through what they saw, and he need not say, "You are my witnesses." Here too, the release is completed through the witness's testimony and there is no need to designate the witness formally. The same applies when one waives a claim against his fellow, for the same reason. Just as he need not say, "You are my witnesses," he likewise need not say, "Write it," because the instruction to write is required only when one creates a new obligation by admission and wishes it to have the force of a documented loan without expressly authorizing writing. But for a waiver or release no such instruction is required. See the Perishah, where I wrote another reason as well.
