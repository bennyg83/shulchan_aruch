An admission before one witness, etc. This rejects Baal HaMaor, who holds that even saying, 'You are my witness,' before one person is ineffective because a person does not take an admission before a single witness seriously. The Shach writes that the possessor can claim certainty like Baal HaMaor. In Tumim I wrote that he cannot, because the principal halachah follows the Shulchan Aruch.
To pay if he admitted. Even though he says, 'The witness testified truthfully, but I was joking,' we do not let him rely on a migo that he could have denied the witness and sworn. Whenever he does not contradict the witness, that witness is like two witnesses for this matter, and he cannot say, 'I was joking.' Shach, correctly.
