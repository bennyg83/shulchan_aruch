And he cleared them away, etc. This means even if he removed them in the other's absence. In the Derishah there, I explained the basis of the ruling, for this is the opinion of all the authorities. This is the language of the Sema. The Nimukei Yosef in the chapter HaBayit VeHaAliyah, however, writes in the name of all the commentators that he must specifically have removed them in the other's presence; see there. This appears to me to be the primary ruling. The Bach rules like the Sema and not like the Nimukei Yosef, but the matter requires study.
