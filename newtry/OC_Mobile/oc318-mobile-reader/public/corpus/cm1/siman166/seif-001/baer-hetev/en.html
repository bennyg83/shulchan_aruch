The gardener. It is written to the garden and not to be separated from a court in which the two of them are inscribed to the garden at the center of the garden, and do not require it to be built at the same time as the High Court, nor does it take place in the garden, but to return and build from them as soon as possible
And he removed them. The Sema writes: Meaning, even if he removed them in the other's absence. In Derishah I explained the reason, for this is the view of all the authorities, and I demonstrated it from their words, contrary to the Beit Yosef, who questioned the Tur and wrote that according to Rif and Rosh he must specifically remove them in the other's presence. The Shach writes that Nimukei Yosef, in the chapter HaBayit VehaAliyah, cites all the commentators as requiring that he remove them specifically in the other's presence. This appears to me to be the principal ruling. The Bach agrees with the Sema and not with Nimukei Yosef, but the matter requires study. See what the Taz writes on this issue.
