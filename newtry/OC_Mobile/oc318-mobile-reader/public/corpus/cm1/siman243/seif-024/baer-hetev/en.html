Original owner. This is difficult, for that is true only according to the Rosh and Tur, who hold that possession does not help in an unresolved question of law. But the Mechaber rules like the Rambam that possession is effective in a case of legal uncertainty, as stated in siman 202, seif 3, siman 248, seif 11, and in simanim 346, 388, and 390, among many other places. If so, here too the present owner should acquire. It is strained to say that the case is one where the present owner did not take possession, for the expression "we leave it in the possession of the original owner" does not imply this. Moreover, in Bedek HaBayit the Mechaber writes explicitly that even if it is in the present owner's possession, we remove it from him and give it to the giver, because we leave it in the possession of the original owner, and he mentions no dissent. This requires study. Shach. The Taz also questions this from siman 275, seif 9, where if another person came and took possession, the latter acquires. All the more so should that apply here, where the subject is movable property and the second person acquired it through possession, yet here it is removed from him because the first has a claim arising from an unresolved question. The matter there concerns land, where possession in the same sense does not apply, yet the later person acquires. The Chacham Tzvi writes in his glosses that it appears the Shach overlooked the Nimukei Yosef cited by the Beit Yosef at the end of this siman, who writes that a gift never leaves the giver's possession until it enters the recipient's possession. Thus this case is unlike ordinary uncertainties in the Gemara. The Taz's question may also be answered in the opposite direction: precisely because possession in land does not operate in this way, and the land remains where it always was, as the Rosh writes in the chapter Chezkas HaBatim, the later claimant may acquire there. With movable property, however, where physical possession is legally significant, a doubtful possession can matter because there is no definite original owner before us. Examine this carefully.
