Nevertheless, taking it from him constitutes robbery because of the ways of peace (Sema), even when he is younger than this age but has reached the stage of distinguishing a pebble and throwing it away. The Shach writes that once he has reached the stage of distinguishing a pebble and throwing it away, he acquires even a found object by Torah law. The Mishnah's statement that the findings of a deaf mute, minor, or mentally incompetent person are acquired only because of the ways of peace refers to one below the stage of distinguishing a pebble and throwing it away. See Beurim: the primary rule is that once he has reached the stage of distinguishing a pebble and throwing it away, taking his finding constitutes robbery because of the ways of peace. Below that stage, he has no rights even because of the ways of peace, for the item is regarded as though it were lying in refuse.
