A gentile does not acquire for another. This is the correct text, as in the Rambam. One who acquires for another does so because he is treated as the recipient's agent and stands in his place to receive the property from the transferor. A gentile is not legally eligible for agency. For the same reason a Jew cannot acquire property through agency on behalf of a gentile, because a Jew does not become the agent of a gentile for this purpose.
