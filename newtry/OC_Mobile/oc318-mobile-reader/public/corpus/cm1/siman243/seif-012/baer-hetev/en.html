Acquired. The case is where the recipient admits that the document remained in the giver's possession until after the giver's death and only afterward came into the recipient's possession, with no witnesses to the transfer. If he were claiming that it had been in a third party's possession, we could ask that person how it came into his hands. If that person forgot, or died, it would be forbidden to deliver the document to the recipient, as explained at the beginning of siman 65 regarding forgetfulness and in the following seif regarding death. Even if someone improperly delivered it to him, the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write in siman 65, seif 16, that he cannot collect by virtue of the document. If the case were that he received it from another person without witnesses, so that he would be believed through a migo, we could equally say that it came to him directly from Reuven the giver without witnesses, and he would be believed through that migo. The Mechaber was too concise in a place where he should have explained the circumstances at length. Sema. See what the Taz writes on this matter.
