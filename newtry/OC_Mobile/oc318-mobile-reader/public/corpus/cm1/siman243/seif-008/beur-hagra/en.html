Nevertheless he is liable, etc. This gloss belongs here and refers to what was stated that he may retract when he tells others. Nevertheless, he is liable under a warranty if he said that he would assume responsibility once the recipient acquires the gift. This applies only against unencumbered property, since the undertaking was oral. In a sale, however, recovery may even be made from encumbered property, as stated in Bava Batra 42b. So write Tosafos, s.v. "chozer," and the Rosh and other poskim. This is also the Tur's text in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah. This is the reading in the books, placing the gloss here. The Sema emended it into seif 8, with great difficulty, and afterward challenged the Rama. The Rama placed the gloss here in accordance with the arrangement of Rabbeinu Yonah's words in the Tur. See there.
