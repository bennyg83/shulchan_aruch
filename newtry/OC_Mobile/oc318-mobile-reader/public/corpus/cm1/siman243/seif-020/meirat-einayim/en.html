A person's courtyard, etc. In the first chapter of Bava Metzia 10b this is derived from the verse concerning theft, "If it shall indeed be found in his hand." The Sages expound: I know only his hand; from where do we know his roof, courtyard, and enclosure? The verse says "if it shall indeed be found," in every place. We thus learn that his courtyard is like his hand, specifically for an adult and not for a minor.
