(Collected note) The deaf mute, etc. This follows from Gittin 59a, where a deaf mute has the law of a minor whose transactions are valid, and he is stronger than a minor who acquires for himself, as stated there 65a: "there are three stages in a minor, etc." This is unlike a mentally incompetent person, whose purchase and sale have no validity, as stated above in seif 16.
And he cannot, etc. Gittin 23a, "all are fit, etc.; granted, etc." Acquisition on another's behalf operates by agency, as stated in Bava Metzia 71b. See Tosafos to Gittin 64b, s.v. "shani," "but is it not, etc.?" Nevertheless, one may confer acquisition upon a mentally incompetent person or deaf mute, as above in seif 16 and as stated in Bava Metzia 8a, "granted, a deaf mute, etc."
