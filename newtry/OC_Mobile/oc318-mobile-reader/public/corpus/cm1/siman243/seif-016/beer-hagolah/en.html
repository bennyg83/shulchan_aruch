Rabbi Meir writes in chapter 14 of Yevamot, 113a, from the baraita concerning a competent man who marries a deaf-mute woman or mentally incompetent woman: even if he wrote her a ketubah of one hundred maneh, it is valid because he willingly undertook it, etc. The authorities infer that the Rabbis did not institute a ketubah for her, and from here that a mentally incompetent person can receive a benefit through a competent person.
