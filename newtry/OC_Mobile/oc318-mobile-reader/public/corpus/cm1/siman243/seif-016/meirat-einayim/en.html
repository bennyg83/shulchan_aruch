An incompetent person does not acquire even for himself. Meaning, even when others transfer property to him. Only a minor and a deaf-mute, whose transactions in movable property have legal effect as explained by the Tur and Mechaber in siman 235, acquire for themselves when others transfer property to them. An incompetent person does not. This is unlike the Ir Shushan, who writes that even an incompetent person acquires for himself when others transfer property to him. Rambam, the Tur, the Maggid Mishneh, and the Beit Yosef explicitly do not say so.
But one who confers a right for him, etc. In this respect he is no worse than a one-day-old infant, as the Mechaber concludes in seif 18, because one may confer a benefit upon a person in his absence.
