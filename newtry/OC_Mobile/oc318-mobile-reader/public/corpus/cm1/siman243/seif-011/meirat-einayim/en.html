Even if they wrote and acquired, etc., and some say that once it was written they acquired for him and he cannot retract. The correct text is "and he cannot retract." The meaning is that since the donor explicitly said, "Acquire for him through the deed," once the witnesses actually write the deed they acquire it for the recipient and the donor can no longer retract or prevent delivery. Before the deed was written, however, he certainly may retract even though he said "acquire for him through the deed." The Tur writes this explicitly. The Ramah disagrees and holds that even after writing he may retract, because at the earlier time when he said "acquire through the deed," the deed did not yet exist and a person cannot transfer something not yet in existence. This is unlike the implication of the Ir Shushan, who suggests that merely saying "acquire" prevents retraction even before the deed is written.
