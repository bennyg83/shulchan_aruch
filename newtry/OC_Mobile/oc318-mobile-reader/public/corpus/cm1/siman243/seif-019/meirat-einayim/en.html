One who obligates himself to a minor, etc., until the property comes into the minor's hand. The Ran proves from apparently conflicting Talmudic passages that we say a minor can acquire for himself only when the object actually comes into his hand and he acquires it by meshichah or lifting. Through acquisition agav land or exchange, where the purchased item or gift does not physically enter his hand, he does not acquire even for himself. The Tur states a related rule in siman 235 in the Rambam's name, and the Mechaber writes it there as well.
