One who obligates himself to a minor, whether by kinyan or by document. Tosafot and Ran say that a male minor ordinarily acquires only something that comes into his own hand, such as through meshichah, and does not acquire a new debt merely through sudar or a document. A female minor is different. Since she has courtyard acquisition where applicable, she can also acquire by barter. Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule accordingly. It therefore appears that she can likewise acquire a debt through a written instrument delivered to her. The limitation concerning one who obligates himself by kinyan or document applies to a male minor, not necessarily to a female minor who possesses these acquisition mechanisms.
