Some authorities say, etc. See Tosafot to Gittin 65a, s.v. A pebble, etc., who answer, etc. Ran writes likewise in the name of other authorities, and there gives an alternative explanation. In his first answer Ran explains that acquisition on behalf of a minor applies only to movable property that actually reaches his possession. It does not apply through another form of acquisition, such as acquisition together with land or barter. Likewise, when the Sages said that the transactions of young children are effective, that applies specifically when the recipient physically holds the object; through another form of acquisition the transaction is entirely ineffective. Therefore one who undertakes an obligation toward a minor, etc. This is the basis of the opinion introduced here as 'some authorities say,' since the other authorities answer differently, as explained above.
