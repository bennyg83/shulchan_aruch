Zayin) There, halachah 9. The Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of Rambam that the Rif's words indicate that a gift is equated with a found object, and therefore the recipient must be standing beside the courtyard. The sugya in Kiddushin 44b likewise proves this. This is the Mechaber's view.
