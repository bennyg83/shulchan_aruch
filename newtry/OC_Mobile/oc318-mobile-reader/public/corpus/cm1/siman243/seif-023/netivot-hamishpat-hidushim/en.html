A small number of verses above:
A minor girl who has no father. The Shach concludes that the same rule applies even if she has a father. See Beurim: with a found object or gift, if she is supported at her father's table, the gift belongs to the father and her courtyard cannot acquire it for her. But with a gift, when she is not supported at his table, the gift belongs to her and the courtyard given to her can acquire for her.
