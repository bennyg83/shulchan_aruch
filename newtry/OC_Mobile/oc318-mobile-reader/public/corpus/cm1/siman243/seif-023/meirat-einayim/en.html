A minor girl acquires through her courtyard. This too is derived in the first chapter of Bava Metzia from the law of a get. Scripture says, "He shall write for her a bill of severance and place it in her hand." The apparently superfluous wording teaches that delivery to her roof, courtyard, or enclosure can have the legal status of delivery to her hand. An adult woman would not require this particular derivation, because acquisition through a courtyard is learned elsewhere, and her ability to appoint an agent is learned from "and he shall send her." The additional verse therefore includes a minor girl. Since the verse is written regarding a woman, it includes a minor girl but not a minor boy. This is the explanation of Rashi and Tosafot, unlike the independent rationale offered by the Ir Shushan.
