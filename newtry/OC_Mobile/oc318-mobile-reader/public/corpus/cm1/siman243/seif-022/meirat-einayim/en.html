Likewise, his four cubits, etc. The Sages enacted that a person's four cubits acquire for him like his courtyard, so that people not come to quarrel when someone else takes ownerless property that came next to him. This concern for quarrels applies only in places open to all where people are not so numerous. It does not apply when Reuven stands in Shimon's courtyard. Even if he entered with Shimon's permission, others have no right to enter there, so there is no basis for the Sages to give Reuven the four cubits around him the legal status of his own courtyard. The same applies when he stands in the public domain, where many people are present, press together, and one person enters within another's four cubits, or their four-cubit areas overlap. The Sages therefore did not enact acquisition by four cubits in such places.
