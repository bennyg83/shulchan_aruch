He may retract so that they do not write it. The reason is that a deed requires agency. Once he revokes the agency, the witnesses cannot write something that creates a liability for him. Consequently, the underlying act is void, since he made the two dependent upon one another. Regarding a condition of two hundred zuz, although fulfillment of the condition is not itself a liability upon the giver, we do not say that the condition may be fulfilled against his will as with ordinary conditions. One might argue that since this is not a true condition, the act should remain effective even without the condition. This is incorrect. Only the portion of the condition that benefits the purchaser is treated as though it does not exist. The portion that benefits the seller remains in force, and once he revokes that condition, the act is consequently void:
