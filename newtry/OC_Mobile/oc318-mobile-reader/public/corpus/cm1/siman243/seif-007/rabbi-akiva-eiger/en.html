[Shulchan Aruch] But in the public domain. Note: I am uncertain about the following case. A person stands in the public domain one cubit from an alleyway, and a lost object falls from the alleyway into the three cubits adjacent to the public domain, so that it lies within four cubits of where he stands. Does he acquire the lost object lying in the alleyway by virtue of his four cubits, or, since the place where he himself stands is the public domain, does he have no four-cubit acquisition at all?
