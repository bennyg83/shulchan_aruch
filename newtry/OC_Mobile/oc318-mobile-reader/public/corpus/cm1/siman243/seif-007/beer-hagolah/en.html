There in the name of his father the Rosh, in his rulings. He explains that since the condition benefits the buyer, it is merely words of inducement, as stated in Bava Metzia 67a. But where the condition benefits the seller, it is certainly a true condition, and if he retracts from the condition the transaction itself is void. He also writes this there in the name of the Ramban, and that in this case we do not require a condition formulated like that of the tribes of Gad and Reuven.
