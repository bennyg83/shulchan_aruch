Meaning, even with a substantial gift. The Tur likewise writes in seif 2 regarding an ordinary gift. Concerning the statement in the fourth chapter of Bava Metzia 49a, where Rav Pappa says that Rabbi Yochanan agrees regarding a small gift, the Yerushalmi says that this applies to a wealthy person, but for a poor person the promise becomes a vow under the verse 'that which left your lips,' meaning charity. The Mechaber writes this above in siman 125, seif 5. See Yoreh Deah siman 253 regarding how much a person may possess nowadays without being considered poor, and see the end of siman 212 regarding one who vows or consecrates something not presently in his possession.
