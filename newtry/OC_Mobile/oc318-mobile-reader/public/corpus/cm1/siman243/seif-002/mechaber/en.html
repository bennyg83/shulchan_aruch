The rule that the sender cannot retract applies when he says, "Acquire this gift for so-and-so." If he says, "Take this maneh to so-and-so," he may retract until it reaches the recipient. If the recipient is poor, he may not retract. Rama: Even where the recipient is wealthy, retracting from a small gift constitutes lack of trustworthiness. The relevant rules are explained elsewhere. Rama: If Shimon's wife made a gift while ill, her husband consented, and later seeks to retract, he may not do so when the gift was to a poor person.
