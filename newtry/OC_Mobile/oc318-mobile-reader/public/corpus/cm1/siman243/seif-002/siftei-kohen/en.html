"And if he is poor," he said, "and if he is poor, he will not be able to do so with him."
And even regarding a wealthy person, etc. This is an addition by the Rama based upon the Mechaber's words, although the Mechaber himself intended the same, as the Sema writes in seif katan 5. This is also indicated by the Mechaber's wording, 'as explained.' He plainly means as explained above, siman 204, seif 8, which does not concern a poor person but any person regarding one who lacks trustworthiness. See Responsa Rabbi Avraham ibn Chaim, simanim 30, 103, and 108; Responsa Menahem Azariah, siman 104; Responsa Rama, simanim 47 and 48; the gloss to the Sema, seif katan 7; the Rif at the beginning of the chapter Shor Shenagach Arbaah VaChamishah; and Yoreh Deah, siman 258.
