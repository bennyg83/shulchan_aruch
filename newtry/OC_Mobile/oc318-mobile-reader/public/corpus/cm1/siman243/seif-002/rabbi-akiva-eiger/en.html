[Shulchan Aruch] He does not acquire. Note: Lechem Mishneh writes that this is why the language "and they caused him to take possession" is used. In a place where deeds are ordinarily written, acquisition does not take effect until the deed is written, and he may retract even from the field. It must therefore be speaking of an act of possession, because once they caused him to take possession of the field, he conclusively transferred it even without a deed, as above at the end of siman 194. See what Nimukei Yosef writes on our sugya.
