The donor may retract regarding the document until it reaches, etc. This is so even though possession of the field was already made on behalf of the recipient, so that the donor can no longer retract from the gift of the field itself. The Tur concludes that this is true even where the field was transferred by a kinyan. Although an ordinary kinyan is presumed to be intended for writing, as explained in siman 39, that means only so long as the transferor does not object. During that period the witnesses may write a deed without obtaining separate permission. If the transferor later protests, then even if the witnesses already wrote the deed, they may not deliver it to the recipient. He may say that he does not want many deeds circulating against him and thereby diminishing the value of his property. Responsum of the Rosh, klal 34, ruling 3, gives an additional reason: even if the kinyan included the writing of the deed, that aspect is merely a kinyan on words.
