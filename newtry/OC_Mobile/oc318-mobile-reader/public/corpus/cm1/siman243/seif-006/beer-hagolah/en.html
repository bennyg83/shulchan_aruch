Heh) There, seif 7, and Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 14, from the statement of Rabbah bar Yitzchak in the name of Rav, Bava Batra 77a. Rashbam explains there that one does not write a deed against the will of the giver, for he may not wish the gift to be documented in a deed. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes that Rambam's words indicate that no kinyan was made from the giver; rather, he appointed them as agents to acquire on behalf of that person.
