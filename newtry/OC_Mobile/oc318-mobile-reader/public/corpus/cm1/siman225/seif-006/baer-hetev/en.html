Libation. It appears to me that if, before the wine is used for idolatrous libation, the purchaser knows that this is about to occur, as in the case in the Rosh's responsum cited by the Tur, where a violent official came to plunder the villages in which the wine was located and the seller warned the purchaser to remove the wine from there or cancel the sale so that the seller would take it back, then certainly the seller is heard. The purchaser cannot cause him a direct loss. The Rosh's responsum does not address such a case. Nevertheless, even according to what I have written, if the purchaser says, 'Go save your wine and place it for me in a safe location,' the purchaser is correct. He simply cannot compel the seller to leave the wine in a dangerous place while making the seller bear the risk. Taz.
