Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 19 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 3. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the conclusion of the sugya at the end of Bava Metzia 15a: omission of a warranty clause is treated as a scribal error, even in a deed of sale.
Bet) The Nimukei Yosef writes that there are three levels of warranty, each broader than the previous, and all are included in a general warranty even if not specified. But if the seller explicitly limited the warranty to claims arising from himself, this excludes other claims that are broader. See Sema in the name of the Darkei Moshe.
