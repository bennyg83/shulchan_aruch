The money. A creditor also collects half of the appreciation from the buyer, and the buyer then recovers that amount from the seller, as stated at the beginning of siman 115. This passage does not discuss that issue. Moreover, ordinary movable property, which is also discussed here, does not generally appreciate. Sema.
With a deed. The words of the Rambam and Mechaber imply that if one sells his field before witnesses without a deed, then all the more so the seller bears responsibility even if responsibility was not expressly stated. This is even clearer from the Semag, and the Rashba, Ran, and Nimukei Yosef in the first chapter of Bava Metzia write likewise. The Bach also rules this way. The Tur and Mechaber state it explicitly in siman 116. The Rosh in chapter Chezkas HaBatim likewise writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah that even according to the opinion that omission of responsibility from a deed is not automatically treated as a scribal error, that applies only when the parties chose to execute a deed and omitted responsibility, for perhaps that was their condition before witnesses. But in a sale before witnesses without a deed, even if responsibility was not stated, the seller is responsible. Gidulei Terumah writes that the Ramban also appears to hold this way. See Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 274 and 413. Shach.
