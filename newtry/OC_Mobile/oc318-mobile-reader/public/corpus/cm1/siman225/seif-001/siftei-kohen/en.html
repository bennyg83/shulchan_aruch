Even if he sold the land by deed, etc. The Rambam and Mechaber imply that where one sells land before witnesses without a deed, responsibility certainly rests upon him even if it was not expressly stipulated. This is even clearer from the Semag, negative commandment 170, page 61, beginning of column 2. The Rashba, Ran, and Nimukei Yosef at the beginning of Bava Metzia write likewise, and the Bach rules accordingly. The Bach says that this is implied by the Tur here, but in truth it is not strongly implied here. He should instead have cited the Tur above in siman 116, where this is stated explicitly, and the Mechaber rules likewise there. The Rosh in chapter Chezkas HaBatim writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah that even according to the view that omission of responsibility is not treated as a scribal error, this is only where the parties resorted to a deed and responsibility was not written in it, for perhaps that was their express condition before witnesses. Where one sells before witnesses without a deed, however, he bears responsibility even if it was not stipulated. The Gedolei Terumah, sha'ar 47, writes that the Rambam's general statement in chapter 1 of Hilchot Malveh, that one who sells land before witnesses allows collection from encumbered property, also indicates this position. This proof is not decisive, because the Rambam merely follows the language of the Gemara. It would have been stronger to prove it from chapter 19 of Hilchot Mechirah, which the Mechaber copies here. The Gedolei Terumah further proves it from the Ramban cited by Baal HaTerumot, who writes that a deed may be written for the seller without the purchaser's presence, because even concerning responsibility, a sale of land before witnesses permits collection from encumbered property. The Ramban writes this briefly in his novellae to chapter Get Pashut, and the Tur cites it in siman 238. This is a sound proof, for otherwise there would be concern that the seller did not undertake responsibility. Nevertheless, the Gedolei Terumah writes that he searched and found no authority supporting Baal HaTerumot, such that the possessor could say, 'I rely upon their view.' I already wrote above in siman 116 that in my opinion Rashbam also agrees with Baal HaTerumot that a seller before witnesses without a deed does not bear responsibility unless he stipulated it. See there. See Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 274 and 413.
