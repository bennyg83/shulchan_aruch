[Sema, letter 12] For they did not contemplate it. Note: Even an uncommon accident is not included in ordinary responsibility, as stated in seifim 3 and 4. Here the case is different, because the purchaser expressly stipulated that he accepts only liability for spoilage. The seller is therefore liable for every other type of responsibility. One might object that the seller also stipulated specifically only responsibility for breakage and spillage. We answer that since the seller accepted ordinary responsibility upon himself, presumably had he contemplated the accident of libation he would have accepted that as well, because the purchaser would not have wished to buy until the seller accepted all ordinary responsibility. Derishah. See Responsa Mekor Baruch, siman 51.
