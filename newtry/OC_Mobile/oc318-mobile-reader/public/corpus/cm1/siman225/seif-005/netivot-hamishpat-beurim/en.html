And he brought gentile witnesses. This means witnesses to the robbery. If he brings Jewish witnesses who saw the item in the gentile's possession earlier, and there are also witnesses who later saw it in the present possessor's hands in circumstances where, had the prior possessor been Jewish, we would remove it from the present possessor, since violent possessors do not obtain chazakah, it appears that the buyer may recover from the seller under the seller's warranty. See what I wrote at the end of siman 356.
