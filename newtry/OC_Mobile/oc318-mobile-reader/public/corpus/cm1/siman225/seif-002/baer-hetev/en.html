The king. Although the law of the kingdom is law, that applies to what the king does on his own authority pursuant to the laws of the kingdom, as Shulchan Aruch and Tur write in siman 369. This case is different, because the king renders judgment on the basis of gentile witnesses, and there is concern that they may be lying before him. The phrase 'even in their courts' teaches that the rule applies although such courts are more careful in their proceedings. Sema.
