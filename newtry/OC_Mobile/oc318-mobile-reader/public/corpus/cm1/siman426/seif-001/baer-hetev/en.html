That he hire. The Shach writes that the Sema's question against the Mechaber and Rama, for not recording that if the person who was saved has money he must reimburse the rescuer, is answered because the Rama already records this in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 252 in the name of the Mordechai. See there and the Beit Yosef, seif 22, who cites Mahari Weil. Shach.
