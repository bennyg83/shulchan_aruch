A person is obligated to save another, whether through his own person or his money. It contains one seif:
One who sees another drowning in the sea, or being attacked by bandits, or threatened by a dangerous animal, and is able to save him personally, or by hiring others to save him, but does not do so, violates "You shall not stand idly by the blood of your fellow." The same applies if he hears non-Jews or informers plotting harm against another or laying a trap for him and does not warn him, or if he knows of a non-Jew or violent person who is coming against another and he is able to appease that person on the other's behalf and remove the hostility but does not do so, and similarly in comparable cases.
