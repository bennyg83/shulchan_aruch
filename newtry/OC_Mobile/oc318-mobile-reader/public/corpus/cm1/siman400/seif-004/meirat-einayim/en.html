If the injured party seized, etc. Since he claims regarding both oxen that both gored his animals, and the damager likewise admits that much, even though he has no witnesses identifying which ox gored which animal, seizure is effective according to everyone. So it is in the Gemara. See Derishah.
He pays for the smaller animal from the larger ox, etc. This applies specifically where the injured party seized in the presence of witnesses, as the Mechaber concludes, for then the injured party has no migo. But if he seized without witnesses, he may collect for the larger animal from the larger ox in accordance with his own claim, as Rama writes in siman 399, seif 3, following Rosh and Tur. The Tur likewise writes here, "but he does not collect according to his own words because he seized in the presence of witnesses," implying that if he seized without witnesses he does collect according to his own words. Thus the sequence of the Tur and Mechaber is as follows: if the injured party seized, his seizure is effective to collect, and this is not subject to the rule of one who claims wheat and is admitted barley. Do not think that this applies only where he seized without witnesses. Even if he seized before witnesses, the seizure remains effective. They then conclude that where he seized before witnesses, however, he does not collect according to the full extent of his claim.
