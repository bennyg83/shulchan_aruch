The one standing. If the surviving ox is tam and the forewarned ox died or was lost, the claimant may collect half damages from the surviving tam regardless of which ox actually gored. If the surviving ox gored, half damages are collected from the body of the damager. If the dead ox gored, it was forewarned and its owner owes full damages from his general assets, of which the surviving ox is part. The Mechaber must therefore be discussing a case where the surviving ox is the forewarned one, or where it is not known which ox was forewarned and which was tam. Sema.
