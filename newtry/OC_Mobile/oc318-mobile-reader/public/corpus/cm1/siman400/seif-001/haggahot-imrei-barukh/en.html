(Shach, seif katan 6) Since there are witnesses, he is liable for a Torah oath. Note: Rashba's novellae to Bava Kamma 41b cite the Raavad as holding that even regarding a penalty we can impose an oath, including an oath based on one witness, and that the rule of one obligated to swear but unable to do so must pay likewise applies. Rashba disagrees and follows Tosafot there, who hold that an oath does not apply to a penalty because an admission would exempt the defendant. It is clear that even according to the Raavad there would be only a Torah oath regarding a penalty, not a shevuat heset. There is therefore no contradiction to what is explained at the beginning of this siman. The Rashba's novellae cited above appear also in Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Kamma.
