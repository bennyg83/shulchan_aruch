If he claimed, etc. See Tosafot 46a, s.v. even. Tosafot limits the rule where the defendant could not have known. Rambam, however, applies the rule even where he was not expected to know, as stated in siman 90, seif 10, provided the claimant alleges that the defendant knows. This differs from heirs and from the present case. Shach writes similarly in siman 72. See 2, 72, and 90.
