Certain. The wording implies that this is true even with a forewarned animal. Maharshal writes that when a forewarned ox pursued another animal, the owner is liable if the injured party makes a definite claim and the damager is uncertain, relying on Rav Huna and Rav Yehudah, who award money in a definite-versus-uncertain claim. His ruling is incorrect. Since we rule like the Sages that money remains with its current owner, the burden of proof rests on the claimant in every case. Shach.
