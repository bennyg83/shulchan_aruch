Tur, seif 22, in the name of a responsum of his father the Rosh, rule 59, siman 5, as explained above in siman 63. The Bach writes that the Beit Yosef here cites Baal HaTerumot: if witnesses testify that the actual loan money, or property obtained directly from it, still exists, then there is certainly no doubt that the lender may collect from those assets. This is also the view of the early authorities. Accordingly, the same applies to a borrower who lacks enough assets to pay all creditors: if merchandise he purchased on credit remains intact, or property directly derived from it remains, the other creditors have no share in those assets. So writes the Rif in the chapter HaMekabel, siman 550, cited by Rabbeinu in siman 108, seif 6. The same is written in siman 280, seif 1, in the name of the Ramah, and in the Rif in the chapter Af Al Pi concerning a rebellious wife. This is the practical ruling.
