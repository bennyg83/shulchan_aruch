A married woman... This is unlike the view that one does not administer an oath to her. The statement in Gittin 46a that a person does not want his wife to be disgraced applies specifically in relation to her husband, not to other parties, as shown in Bava Basra 154b. Moreover, even to her husband she may have to swear, as in Shevuos 45a regarding a wife who conducts business, and Kesubos concerning a wife made a shopkeeper or administrator. See also the final chapter of Kesubos and Tosafos there.
And if she refuses... they summon... As stated in Bava Kamma 15a and elsewhere, where men and women are equated for legal obligations.
Or they send... As in Yevamos 100a. See siman 124.
And if there is no... Bava Kamma 87a.
But there is no... As the Gemara says there, dealings with them can cause loss. This implies that the husband is not personally obligated because of her acts, and the same applies to her debts.
