If a married woman becomes obligated to take an oath, it is administered to her. If she refuses to swear, she is compelled in the same manner as a man. {Rama: She is summoned to judgment, or messengers are sent to her, in the manner explained below in siman 124. If she has no means to pay, a judgment is written requiring her to pay when she is widowed or divorced. Her husband is not obligated to pay for her, even if she conducts the household's business. See Even HaEzer, siman 91. If the husband admits that the utensils claimed from her entered his domain, he must swear that they are not in his possession, that he did not make unauthorized use of them, and that he was not negligent regarding their theft. Some say an oath is not administered to a pregnant woman, since she is removed from the synagogue when an unspecified ban is pronounced; see responsum 385.}
