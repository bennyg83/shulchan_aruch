And she gestured before her death. Rashba writes that if she is adjured by others through an imprecatory oath, then when she is unable to swear and answer amen, they may administer that form of oath and she can collect. It is therefore possible that an imprecatory oath suffices where no other method is available. Accordingly, the statement that she gestured before her death that she had taken nothing should be understood to mean that they administered an imprecatory oath to her and she responded by gesture, and her gesture is treated as though she answered amen.
