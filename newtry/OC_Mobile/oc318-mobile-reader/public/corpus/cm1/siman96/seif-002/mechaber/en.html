Although a Torah oath is not taken in response to a minor's claim, a heses oath is taken. This applies even when the minor lacks commercial acumen. Thus, whether a minor claims against an adult and the adult admits part, denies everything, or is supported or contradicted by a witness, the adult takes a heses oath. He cannot reverse it to the minor, because no oath at all is administered to a minor. The minor does not even accept an unspecified ban, because he does not understand the punishment for an oath. {Rama: Some say an oath is not taken in response to his claim unless he has reached the age at which a child's commercial transactions are valid.}
