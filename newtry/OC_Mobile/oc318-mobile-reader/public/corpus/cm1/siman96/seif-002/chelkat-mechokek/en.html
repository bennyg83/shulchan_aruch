Or property specifically designated for her ketubah, still extant. See below in the Tur, siman 98, where the dispute between Rashi and Rif is cited. According to Rashi, even if the movables designated for her ketubah are no longer extant because they were stolen or lost, she collects without an oath. Since those particular items were lost, we are no longer concerned that the husband set aside payment for her a second time. The Rosh agrees with Rashi. Here, however, the ruling follows Rif and Rambam.
