Rashba in a responsum. He explains that the oath concerning documents is only a rabbinic enactment to appease the claimant, as stated at the end of Ketubot 87b. Likewise, when one administers an oath to a minor, that too is only by rabbinic enactment. We do not enact one rabbinic safeguard upon another, as stated at the beginning of Bava Metzia 5b. So writes the Maggid Mishneh there: it is explicit in the Gemara, chapter HaNizakin, Gittin 59a, that the purchase of minors of limited maturity is valid, etc., regarding movable property. It is likewise explained in the chapter HaChovel, Bava Kamma 87a, that a minor who injures others is forever exempt from payment. So too writes the Tur from seif 12 onward.
