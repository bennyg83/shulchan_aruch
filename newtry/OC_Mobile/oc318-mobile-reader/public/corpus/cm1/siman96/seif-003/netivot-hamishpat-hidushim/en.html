Concerning the documents. For example, the claimant says, "I gave you three debt instruments," and the defendant partially admits, namely one instrument. Even so, no hesset oath is required. Sema. The Shach writes that if a minor holds a note inherited from his father, the debtor dies during the child's minority, and the minor impairs the note or one witness testifies that it was paid, the minor collects without an oath. Urim VeTumim disagrees, and the primary law follows the Sema.
