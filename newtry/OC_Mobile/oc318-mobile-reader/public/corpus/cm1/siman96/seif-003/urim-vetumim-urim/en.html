Concerning documents, etc. Sema explains that if a minor claims he delivered two documents and the custodian admits only one, there is still no hesset. Shach wishes instead to explain the clause as a creditor who holds the father's document against the minor, the father having died within the term, or where the creditor impairs the document or one witness testifies that it was paid, and says he collects without any oath. See Tumim. I wrote that this is not correct as practical law. The Shach also suggests that if the minor says, "Swear to me," perhaps the creditor must swear. I do not accept that. The principal explanation is Sema's, although Mechaber above, siman 95:4, holds that for documents and a total denial there is no oath at all. The distinction must therefore be a partial admission, as appears at the beginning of siman 95.
