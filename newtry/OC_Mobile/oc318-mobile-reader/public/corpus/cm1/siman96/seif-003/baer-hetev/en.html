The documents. The Sema explains that this means the minor claims documents and the defendant admits some of them. The Shach objects that this cannot be the law. Since for purposes of a shevuat heset there is no distinction between documents and other property, and since we do not impose a shevuat heset in response to a minor's claim lest anyone be able to take a minor's property, the same should apply to documents. There is no basis here to distinguish documents from other property, nor is this a case of one enactment built upon another. Rather, the phrase means an oath concerning documents, such as where one impairs his promissory note by admitting partial payment or one witness testifies that it was paid. This is clear in the Shach's view. See there.
Benefit. If beit din would refuse to entertain the claimant's case, people would avoid doing business with the minor, and the minor would thereby lose the benefit available to him. Sema. See also siman 235, seif 15, siman 349, seif 3, and Responsa Mabit I, 30.
