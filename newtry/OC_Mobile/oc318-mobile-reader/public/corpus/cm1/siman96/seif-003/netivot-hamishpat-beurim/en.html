On documents. See Shach, seif katan 5. The primary interpretation is that of the Sema, as Tumim also writes, that every category of one who swears and collects applies even against a minor.
From which he has benefit. See Maharik, shoresh 81, who writes that a minor's admission has no effect and the rule that a litigant's admission is equivalent to one hundred witnesses does not apply to him. Ketzot HaChoshen challenges this from the end of the chapter Zeh Borer, Sanhedrin 31. There is no difficulty. When Maharik speaks of a matter from which the minor has benefit, he means that where the matter arises from commercial dealings or a loan, the minor's admission is an effective admission even when it is to his detriment. Maharik's case concerns a matter from which the minor derives no benefit. In such a matter, his admission has no binding force.
