A deaf mute and an incompetent person, etc., not even for a minor oath, etc. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 1, siman 156, concerning a mute person who is otherwise legally competent in all respects, who conducted business with others and became obligated to take an oath. How is he made to swear? He writes that the oath may be administered through a nod of the head: the full formula of the oath is recited before him and he nods his head, which is equivalent to answering amen. Alternatively, he may take the oath in writing by writing, "I hereby swear," etc. See there at length, and see what Tumim and Ketzot HaChoshen write concerning this.
