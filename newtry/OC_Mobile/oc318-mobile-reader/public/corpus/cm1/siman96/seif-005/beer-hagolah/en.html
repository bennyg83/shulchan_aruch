The wording of the Rambam at the end of chapter 8 of Hilkhot To'en. The Maggid Mishneh explains that this means a deaf-mute who neither speaks nor hears. Although he may conduct transactions in movable property after examination, as discussed in chapter 29 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, beit din does not entertain his claims, neither through gestures nor through examination.
A blind person was excluded only from serving as a judge or witness. Maggid Mishneh.
