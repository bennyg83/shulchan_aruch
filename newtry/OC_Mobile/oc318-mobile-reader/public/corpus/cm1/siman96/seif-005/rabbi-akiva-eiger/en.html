Note: Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchos Shomrim, siman 10, writes: "A minor who lends an item to an adult creates, according to all views, a full borrower’s liability, because the law of borrowing does not use the word ‘man.’ This likewise appears from the lengthy responsa of Maharam." It further appears from his words there that if the minor was working for the borrower at the time, it is considered custodianship while the owner is in his service.
