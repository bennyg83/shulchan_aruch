The deaf-mute and the mentally incompetent... The rule that collection may be made from a minor's property is learned from the Mishnah's statement that one does not administer an oath to..., as explained above in seif 3. Since it does not say that an oath is not administered to a deaf-mute, mentally incompetent person, or minor, and in the later clause speaks only of collecting from a minor's property, it follows that the other categories differ.
But a blind person. As stated at the beginning of the second chapter of Niddah and at the end of the second chapter of Gittin 23a.
And there is an authority... Baal HaTerumos, in accordance with Gittin 59a concerning a deaf-mute.
But the mentally incompetent... In Gittin there only a deaf-mute and minor are mentioned because their commercial transactions can have validity, unlike those of a mentally incompetent person. See siman 235 seif 22.
