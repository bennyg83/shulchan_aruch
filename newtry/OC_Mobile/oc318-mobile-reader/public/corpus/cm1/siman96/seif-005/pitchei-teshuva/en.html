The deaf-mute and the mentally incompetent, etc., not even for a light oath, etc. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part I, siman 156, concerning a mute person who is mentally competent in every respect, conducted business with others, and became obligated to swear. He writes that the oath may be administered by nodding: the entire formula of the oath is recited before him and he nods his head, which is equivalent to answering Amen. Alternatively, he may take the oath in writing by writing, 'I hereby swear,' etc. See there at length, and see Tummim and Ketzot HaChoshen on this matter.
