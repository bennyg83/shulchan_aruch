[Shulchan Aruch] And slaves and documents. Note: Regarding a Hebrew slave, whether he too is classified as land, see the Shach in this siman, seif katan 18, and below, siman 227, seif 33, and the Sema there, seif katan 60. Examine this carefully. Regarding wheat planted in the ground, the Gemara in the chapter HaZahav raises an unresolved question. See below, siman 227, seif 34.
