Regarding rent, Tosafot at the end of Perek HaMokher et HaBayit says that a daughter's one tenth share can be collected from rents arising from houses. Tosafot asks that rent should be movables and answers that because rent is payable only at the end, accrued rent before completion resembles produce still attached to and dependent upon the land. Mordechai says the same. Although we rule that rent accrues from beginning to end, each increment becomes a loan only retroactively once the hiring is completed. Before completion, the accrued rent remains dependent upon the land. The Shach derives that if a lessor says, "You rented from me for two months," while the tenant says only one month, and the payment date is at the end, there is no Torah partial admission because payment is not yet due. In my view this confuses the lessor's right against the tenant with a creditor seeking to collect from the rent itself. As against the lessor, the tenant has already undertaken a monetary obligation from the beginning conditioned on completion. This is like a claim of a maneh payable in ten days. A present oath can arise even though payment is deferred. The characterization of accrued rent as still attached to land applies primarily when a creditor seeks collection from the lessor's rent stream, not when the lessor sues the tenant for the contractual rent. Once the condition is fulfilled the debt is treated retroactively as having arisen from the hiring, and the ordinary monetary rules apply.
