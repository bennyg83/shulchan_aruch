Even if he was negligent. The Shach concludes that if he claims, "It never happened," he must swear, because he is asserting that the document is still in his possession. Likewise, if he claims that it was lost, he must swear that it is not in his possession. See the Beurim. This applies specifically to a document written payable to whoever produces it. But where the note names the lender, there is no reason to suspect that the possessor is keeping it to collect for himself, since he cannot collect with it. Nor need we suspect that he burned or damaged it, as the Sema writes.
