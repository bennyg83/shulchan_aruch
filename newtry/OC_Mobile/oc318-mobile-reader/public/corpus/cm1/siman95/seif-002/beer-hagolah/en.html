The wording of the Rambam from Hilkhot To'en, law 4, from the Mishnah there in Shevuot, according to Rabbi Yosi bar Chanina's interpretation there 42a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Rambam rules like Rabbi Meir in the Mishnah based on the principle stated in the chapter Na'arah SheNitpatetah, at the beginning of Ketubot 51a, that anything standing ready to be harvested is considered as though already harvested. Tosafot there likewise write in the name of Rabbeinu Tam that this follows from the chapter HaKones, Bava Kamma 59b, where we rule like Rabbi Shimon that one who consumes fully ripened produce pays the value of fully ripened produce. So too is written in several places, in the Semag, and by other poskim.
The same applies regarding acquisition, as explained below in siman 193, but not regarding bailees, as explained below in siman 301, seif 5. See there.
He explains there that since the principal deposit consisted of produce attached to the ground, even though the custodian later harvested it, the claimant is still regarded as claiming land, according to the Sages.
According to the Sages, who hold that produce ready to be harvested is not considered harvested, denial concerning that produce is denial concerning land. According to Rabbeinu Tam, who holds that it is considered harvested, it is denial concerning part of movable property.
