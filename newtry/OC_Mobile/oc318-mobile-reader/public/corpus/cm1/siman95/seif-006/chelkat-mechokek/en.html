I have already written above in siman 70, and it is explicit in the Responsa Rashba cited by the Beit Yosef, that whenever she works we say that her earnings belong to the husband, or alternatively that he can compel her to work. But if she remained idle and did not work, she is not required to reimburse him, as is evident from the relevant sugya. See above siman 70. Regarding a woman who borrowed money and consumed it, the Rosh holds that the husband can argue that she should have supported herself from her earnings.
