The wording of the Rambam there, law 3, cited by the Tur. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Rambam derived this law from the Gemara's answer at the beginning of Bava Metzia 5a: the verse was needed for a case where he dug pits in it, etc. Rabbeinu explains that their dispute concerns the digging, where one party claims that he dug and damaged more than the other admits.
