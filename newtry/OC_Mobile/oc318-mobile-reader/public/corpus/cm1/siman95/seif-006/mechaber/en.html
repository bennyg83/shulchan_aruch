The buffer in the field drew thick holes and caves. Long and narrow discourse. And he must pay for the land that he exalted from the side not to the bottom of the hole and the grove, and he must pay for his shofar, and he says that I did not dig or claim that he had dug two caves, and he says that he did not exalt it with his or her father, and he said, “I am not exalted to him.”
