That he claims that he should refill the pits. See Tumim. I wrote that Maggid Mishneh's position appears clearly correct, that the owner cannot force him to refill the pits if he prefers to pay money. Nevertheless, because he could choose to refill them and thereby avoid payment, Raavad holds that the claim retains the status of a land claim. If refilling is impossible and the claimant necessarily seeks money, it is plainly a monetary claim. Shach rules like this view. In Tumim I wrote that the Mechaber's position appears primary. Whether the status of a person himself resembles movable property or land is discussed at length in Tumim together with the various laws that depend upon it.
