The language of the Tur, seif 12, from the statement of Rabbi Acha son of Rav Ika and the baraita there. Although the Gemara states this regarding mixing hard wine with soft wine, Rabbenu applies it to mixing water as well because the same law and reason apply.
Meaning, specifically a large quantity of water, such that anyone who tastes the wine will know it contains water, as in Rava's incident there. That case was certainly in a place where the custom was to taste, and they permitted only a ratio of one to three. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes in law 6 that Rambam did not record a greater admixture because it is uncommon for so much water to be mixed into wine. So I saw in the Bach.
