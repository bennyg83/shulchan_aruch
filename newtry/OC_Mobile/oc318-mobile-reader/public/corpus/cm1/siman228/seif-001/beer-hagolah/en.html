Alef) This is the language of the Tur, based on the Mishnah in the fourth chapter of Bava Metzia 58b. Rambam likewise rules this in chapter 14, halachah 12.
Bet) A statement of Rabbi Shmuel bar Nachmani there.
Gimel) A statement of Rav Chisda there on 59a.
Dalet) From the statement of Rabbi Chanina bar Idi: "You shall not wrong one another" means that one who is with you in Torah and mitzvot is included in the prohibition of verbal wronging. The Bach, citing the Mordekhai, infers from the Mishnah's wording, "If he was a penitent, one should not say to him," etc., that if he is not a penitent, this prohibition of verbal wronging does not apply in the same way. He cites Sefer HaChokhmah.
Heh) See there, where the wording is that if he wronged you. In my humble opinion the meaning is simple: if he wronged you, you are likewise permitted to respond in kind.
