It is forbidden to cause verbal distress, deceive people, or misrepresent merchandise. It contains twenty seifim.
Just as there is overreaching in commerce, there is verbal wronging. Verbal wronging is more severe than monetary wronging, because money can be restored whereas words cannot be taken back; one affects the person, the other only his property. One who cries out because of verbal wronging is answered immediately. Rama: Some say the prohibition of verbal wronging applies only toward those who fear Hashem. One who wrongs himself may be answered in kind.
