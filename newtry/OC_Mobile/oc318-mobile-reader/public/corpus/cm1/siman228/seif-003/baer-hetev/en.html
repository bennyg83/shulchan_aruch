Common. This is from the Gemara in the chapter HaZahav. Meaning, because a woman is by nature more sensitive, and even a small pain may cause her to cry, Hashem is particularly exacting concerning her tears. The Gemara there cites the teaching that although the gates of prayer may be closed, the gates of tears are not closed, as it is written, "Hear my prayer... and do not be silent at my tears." Rashi explains that although the beginning of the verse is phrased as a request, "do not be silent" refers to the future, meaning: In this I place my trust, that it is not Your way to remain silent in the face of tears.
