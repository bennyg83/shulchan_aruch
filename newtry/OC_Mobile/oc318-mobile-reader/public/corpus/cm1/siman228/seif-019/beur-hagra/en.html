(Supplement) It is forbidden, etc., whether, etc. Concerning wine, this is the Mishna on 60a. Concerning oil, it follows the Sages and Abaye, as Rif rules. Rosh rules like Rav Pappa but explains, following Rashi, that one may mix the sediment remaining in that same barrel, as with wine. This is the basis of Rama's gloss. (End of supplement.)
