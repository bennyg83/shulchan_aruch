It is forbidden to mix lees. Meaning, into other wine. Into this very wine, however, it is permitted, as explained in seif 15. Therefore, if he gave him clarified wine, the amount of lees is later deducted. The dissenting opinion Rama cites afterward holds the reverse: because he was permitted to mix in the lees, if he nevertheless supplied clarified wine we do not deduct afterward, for his failure to mix them indicates that he waived the matter. Sema.
