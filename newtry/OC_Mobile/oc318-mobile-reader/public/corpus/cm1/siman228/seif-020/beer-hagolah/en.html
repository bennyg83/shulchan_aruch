The Maggid Mishneh cites the wording of the Gemara and Rif: where he gives him, etc., in Nisan according to the measure of Tishrei, etc. From the Mechaber's wording it appears that he holds the seasons are not equivalent in measure. Rashi explains 'measure' as the market price rather than literal volume. Rambam in chapter 16 deducts the sediment. Tosafot likewise write there, s.v. 'they said only,' that when one sells cloudy oil he gives one hundred full log of cloudy oil and loses the sediment because he sold it while cloudy, whereas when he sells clarified oil the buyer's loss of sediment is not considered a loss because he prefers clear oil.
