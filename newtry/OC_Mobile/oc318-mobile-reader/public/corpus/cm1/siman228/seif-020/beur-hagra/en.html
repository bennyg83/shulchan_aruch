(Supplement) One who sells. Rambam rules unlike Rabbi Yehuda and understands Rabbi Yehuda as discussing one who sold expressly as refined oil. Rashi understands him even of an unspecified sale, which is the basis of the dissenting gloss. Shulchan Aruch and Rama differ in several points: the meaning of mixing sediment, whether the halakha follows Abaye or Rav Pappa, the scope of Rabbi Yehuda's ruling, the meaning of dregs, the measure of permitted sediment, and whether ordinary refined oil includes the natural waste. Rambam and Shulchan Aruch distinguish oil from wine, and Bach agrees. (End of supplement.)
