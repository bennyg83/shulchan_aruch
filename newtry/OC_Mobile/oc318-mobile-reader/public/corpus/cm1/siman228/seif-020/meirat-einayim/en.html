Rather, it is permitted to mix, etc. Rama's word 'rather' is somewhat awkward, because their reason for not deducting the lees is precisely that it is permitted to mix them in, and since he did not mix them we say that he waived the right to deduct. But because Rama states the ruling briefly without setting out the reason, he uses the word 'rather' to contrast this opinion with the preceding one.
