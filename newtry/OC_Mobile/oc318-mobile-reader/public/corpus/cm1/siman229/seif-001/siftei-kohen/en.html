A quarter kav of legumes, etc. See the Sema, seif katan 1, where he writes that according to one view, with wheat and barley the purchaser need not accept any dirt even if it is less than a quarter kav. I do not understand his words, because everyone agrees that he accepts less than a quarter kav, as the Gemara states concerning one who removes a pebble from another's threshing floor. See the Gemara. The Beit Yosef also writes this explicitly. Examine carefully.
