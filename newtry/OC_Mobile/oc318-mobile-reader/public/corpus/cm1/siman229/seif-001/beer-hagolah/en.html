Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 18 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 11, from the baraita in the sixth chapter of Bava Batra. All of these quarter measures mean one quarter of a kav, which is one twenty-fourth of a seah.
The Maggid Mishneh explains that this refers to dust and straw that become mixed in through winnowing.
Bet) The Mishnah there, Bava Batra 93b.
Gimel) A statement of Rav there on 94a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that its explanation is as Rambam states, and this is the Rif's ruling.
Whenever it is known that the seller intentionally mixed impurities into the produce, even the smallest amount is unacceptable and the buyer need not accept any of it. The Tur states this; it requires study why the Mechaber omitted it.
Dalet) This applies where produce of that type is sold in the market for eating. So rules the Tur in the name of the Ramah.
