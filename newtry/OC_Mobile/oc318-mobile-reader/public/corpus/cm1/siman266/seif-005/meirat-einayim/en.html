If his father told him, "Do not return it," etc. Rambam explains that even if his father says, "Do not return the lost object; instead go perform this immediate need of mine," he must not listen to his father. Honoring father and mother is a positive commandment, whereas returning lost property involves both a negative and a positive commandment. This is also the Gemara's reason. Lest one still say that honoring parents is superior because their honor is compared to the honor of God, the baraita gives an additional reason from the verse, "Every man shall fear his mother and father, and you shall observe My Sabbaths, I am God." All of you are obligated in My honor. See the Perishah.
