The lost object. The Rambam explains that even if one's father tells him, 'Do not return the lost object; instead go and attend to this immediate need of mine,' he may not obey his father. Honoring father and mother is a positive commandment, while returning lost property involves both a negative and a positive commandment. This is also the reason given in the Gemara. Lest one still say that honoring a parent should take precedence because parental honor is compared to the honor of the Omnipresent, the baraita gives another reason from the verse, 'Every man shall fear his mother and father, and you shall keep My Sabbaths; I am Hashem,' teaching that all of you are obligated in My honor.
