A gentile and a Jew who publicly desecrates Shabbat. In the Perishah I wrote that the word "publicly" applies also to the gentile in the clause concerning idolatry: if he served idolatry only once, it must have been done publicly to create this legal status. Tosafot and Mordechai in the first chapter of Chullin write similarly.
