Whoever finds it may kill it. The Gemara also states that it is forbidden to keep it alive. The Beit Yosef writes that the Tur omitted this because this is not its proper place. The Sema writes that it is included in the Tur's statement that whoever finds it may kill it. The Gemara, however, finds it necessary to state the prohibition as well, even after stating that killing it is permitted. See the Bach's discussion.
