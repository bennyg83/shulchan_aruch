The law of lost property belonging to transgressors. It contains five seifim:
Lost property belonging to a non-Jew is permitted to be retained, as the verse says, "the lost property of your brother." One who returns it without another justification commits an offense because he thereby strengthens those who transgress. If he returns it in order to sanctify the Divine Name, so that they will praise the Jewish people and recognize that they are trustworthy, this is praiseworthy. In a situation where failure to return it would cause a desecration of the Divine Name, one is forbidden to keep it and must return it. In all places, one protects their utensils as one would Jewish utensils, because of the ways of peace.
