His fellow. If that person has no money, and the coercer forces this person to reveal him so that he can be detained until the community or relatives redeem him, while the coerced person could instead free himself from torture by paying the coercer a comparable sum, it appears that he may not cause the community or the relatives that loss. So writes the Sema. See Responsa Rashdam, simanim 55, 135, and 207.
