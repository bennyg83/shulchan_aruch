If litigants dispute ownership of land or movable property, each claiming, 'It is mine,' and one of them delivers the disputed property into the hands of a violent authority, he is placed under a ban until he restores the situation to its former state, removes the authority's control, and submits the dispute to Jewish adjudication. Rama: Nevertheless, some say he is not treated as an informer even if he caused the other party a great loss, because the status of informer applies only to one who intends to harm, not to one who seeks to recover what he believes is his own. Others disagree and hold that he is an informer and must pay all the damage, unless the other party was recalcitrant. This is certainly so where he was warned not to litigate before non-Jewish courts and nevertheless did so.
