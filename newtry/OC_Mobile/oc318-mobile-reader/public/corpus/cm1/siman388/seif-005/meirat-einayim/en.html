And handed it into the oppressor's possession, we place him under ban, etc. Rivash, siman 80, writes concerning one who insulted a Torah scholar. The scholar arose and handed over his punishment to the city's ruler. Rivash writes that this conduct approaches the category of informing. Darkei Moshe cites this.
The psalmist says, “And he must pay all the humiliation and acquor in the return of the book of harmonies, and he must pay for one who wanted to take his seat and force him to trial, and that he did not want to assume himself to be beaten and killed by the judge, and he quickly condemned the prosecutor with a murderer and the IDF in response from his army.”
