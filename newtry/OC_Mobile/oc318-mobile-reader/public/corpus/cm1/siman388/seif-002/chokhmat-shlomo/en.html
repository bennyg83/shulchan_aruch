Seif 7. Note: See the Sema. His meaning is that since the informer is believed to say, "I did not hand over the property," he is also believed to say that he handed over only such and such an amount. Accordingly he is believed as to the amount. Similarly, what the Shach writes in seif katan 1 concerns a case where there are no witnesses that the claimant actually suffered the asserted loss. Therefore, if the alleged informer denies it, he is exempt. The Shach did not need to bring a proof from siman 418, because the rule is explicit here in this very seif. Examine carefully.
