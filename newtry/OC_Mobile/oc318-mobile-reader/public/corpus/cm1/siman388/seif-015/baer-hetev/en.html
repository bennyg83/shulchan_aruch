To kill him. The Tur indeed cites a responsum of the Rosh in the name of Rabbenu Chananel to this effect. It is surprising that the Rama states it without qualification, because the Mechaber in siman 11 implies that such a person may be killed. That is the view of the Rambam, Rashba, and other authorities, as I wrote there with proofs. The Sema says that Baal HaIttur clearly agrees with Rabbenu Chananel, but in my view Baal HaIttur was speaking only about property. See there. Shach.
And he became established. The Shach objects and writes that all the authorities, in many places, state without qualification that there is no agency for a transgression and do not distinguish between an agent who is habitually involved in that transgression and one who is not. See there.
Liable. Darkei Moshe writes concerning this to see below siman 386, law 18. It appears he means the Rashba responsum cited there. If so, the Raah's unqualified statement here is difficult. As a matter of practical law, the Rashba's view appears primary, as I wrote there. (See what I wrote in siman 386, seif katan 14.) Shach.
