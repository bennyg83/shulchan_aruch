So it appears there in Responsa Rosh, and he writes that Rabbenu Chananel also says so. Tur and Beit Yosef cite it. The responsum states: I heard from my teacher that after an informer has already informed, he may not be killed directly by Jewish hands, but only through the ruler, since he is established as an informer. Similarly in siman 1 he writes in the name of Rabbenu Chananel that such persons are not killed directly, but are lowered into a pit from which they cannot climb so that they die on their own. Telling the ruler to kill him is comparable to lowering him into a pit, because the Jew himself performs no direct act. So too in the cited responsum.
