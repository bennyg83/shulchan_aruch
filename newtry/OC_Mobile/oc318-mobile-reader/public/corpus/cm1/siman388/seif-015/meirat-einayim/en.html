The Rosh cites Rabbeinu Chananel at the end of this siman that outside the immediate act of informing, even where he has become established as an informer, he may be killed only through indirect means. For example, he may be lowered into a pit and left to die on his own. If he already descended into a pit using a ladder, the ladder may be removed. Similarly, one may seek his death through gentiles, which resembles lowering him into a pit. In the Perishah I wrote that according to the Rambam and Rabbi Asher of Lunel, once he has been established as an informer three times he may be killed even directly, and even when he is not presently engaged in an act of informing. The Baal HaIttur appears to follow Rabbeinu Chananel.
