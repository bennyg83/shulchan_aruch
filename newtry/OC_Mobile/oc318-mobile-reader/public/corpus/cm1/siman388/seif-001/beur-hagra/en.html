(Collected note) But if it is not their way, etc. The Rambam's text does not contain the Gemara's question "are pearls normally placed there?" Rather, the question is whether a claimant who alleges a pearl may recover without witnesses. The doubt is not based on disbelief that a pearl could have been present. It asks whether the special enactment allowing a robbed party to swear and collect extends to an object that people do not ordinarily place there. Since such conduct is outside normal practice, the claimant was negligent. (End of note.)
