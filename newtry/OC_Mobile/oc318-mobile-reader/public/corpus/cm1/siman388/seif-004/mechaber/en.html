If one physically takes another person's property and gives it to a violent person, he must pay in every case, even though the king compelled him to bring it. Rama: If two partners jointly hold a debt and a ruler coerces one partner to release the debt, he is exempt, because this is not considered physically handing over the property. The rule that one who was compelled to bring property and brought it is liable applies only before the property came under the violent person's control. But if the violent person compelled a Jew to point out the property, then stood beside it so that it came under his control, and afterward compelled the Jew to carry it elsewhere, the Jew is exempt even if he himself transported it. Once the violent person stood beside the storehouse and could control and seize it, everything there was already considered lost, as though it had been burned.
