Every informer, etc. This is a scribal error and should read "every person who oppresses," etc. The continuation, "but because of," proves it. An actual informer may be stopped even to protect a single person, as stated in the gloss to seif 9.
It is permitted, etc. Such a person is comparable to an informer, as stated in the first chapter of Rosh Hashana concerning sectarians, informers, and those who terrorize the community.
But because of, etc. As stated in seif 9.
One who engages in, etc. He too is treated as a pursuer, even if he does not specifically intend the resulting danger. Comparable cases appear in Bava Kamma and Sanhedrin, and even indirect causation or a serious likelihood of harm can suffice in the informer context. See siman 425 seifim 1 and 2.
They warn him, etc. As stated in seif 10.
One who wishes, etc. See siman 28 seif 3 in the gloss regarding litigants who seek recourse elsewhere.
Nevertheless, with an evildoer, etc. Bava Kamma 113b permits withholding repayment of a debt to a gentile in circumstances where no chillul Hashem results, treating it like retaining a lost item. Sanhedrin 76b discusses the improper return of lost property to such a person. See siman 348 seif 2 in the gloss.
And if, etc. See Beer HaGolah and the gloss to siman 28, which distinguishes a case where the matter can be established through proper evidence.
