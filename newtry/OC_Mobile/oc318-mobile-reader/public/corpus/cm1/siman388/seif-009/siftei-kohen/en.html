Every informer, etc., has no share in the World to Come. Rosh Hashanah 17a also teaches that informers descend to Gehinnom and are judged for generations; their bodies and souls are consumed. Below I cite Tosafot and Hagahot Ashri that an informer is worse than an apostate. Yoreh Deah, siman 2, records authorities who invalidate the slaughter of an informer like that of an apostate, and siman 281 rules that a Torah scroll written by an informer, gentile, or apostate Jew is invalid.
