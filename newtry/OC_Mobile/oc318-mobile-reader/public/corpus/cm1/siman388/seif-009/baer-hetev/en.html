The World to Come. Rosh Hashanah, chapter 1, likewise states that informers descend to Gehinnom and are judged for generations, and their bodies and souls are consumed. Yoreh Deah, siman 2, seif 9 in the Rama, records authorities who invalidate the slaughter of an informer, and siman 281, seif 3, states that a Torah scroll written by an informer is invalid. Shach.
