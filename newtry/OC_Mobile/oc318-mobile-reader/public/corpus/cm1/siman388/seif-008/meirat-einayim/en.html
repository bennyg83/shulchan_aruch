Who were compelled to show, etc. Meaning, even if after being compelled to show the property they later carried and delivered it into the oppressor's hand, in which case they are liable to pay as the ruling concludes, they are nevertheless not called wicked. Alternatively, the meaning is: not only where they were compelled merely to identify the property, and did so without physically handing it over, in which case there may be no monetary liability at all, are they not called wicked. Even where they were compelled to bring the property and therefore had to carry and hand it over under duress, although they may be liable to pay according to the rule above, they are still not called wicked.
