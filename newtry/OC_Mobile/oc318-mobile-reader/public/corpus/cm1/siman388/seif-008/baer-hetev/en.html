Although dina d'garmi is a penalty, we do not apply the rule that one who admits a penalty is exempt except to a Torah penalty, regarding which the verse "whom the judges condemn" excludes one who condemns himself. A rabbinic penalty, however, is owed even through one's own admission. So writes Maharil, end of siman 90, and this is implied by the authorities, unlike Maharshal at the end of siman 50. Ibid.
