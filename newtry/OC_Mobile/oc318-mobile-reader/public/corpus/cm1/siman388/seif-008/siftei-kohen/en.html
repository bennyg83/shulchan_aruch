Nevertheless, he must pay. Maharshal writes that if the informer admits on his own, he must pay because dina de-garmi is not a fine. This reasoning is unnecessary. Even if it is a rabbinic fine, the rule that one who admits a fine is exempt applies only to a biblical fine, not to a rabbinic fine. The authorities indicate this, and Mahari Weil expressly rules that although the liability is rabbinic, an informer who admits must pay. Mordechai states the reason explicitly: the verse excluding self-conviction applies to a biblical fine, but a rabbinic fine is payable upon one’s own admission. This is straightforward.
