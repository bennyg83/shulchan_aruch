[Rama] That he has vouchers. Note: The Beit Yosef writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham, citing the Rashba, that "claims against the property" means specifically challengers to title. But if debts were discovered against him, even before he took possession he cannot retract. He further writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham that if a person purchased a house by kinyan sudar, had not yet taken full possession, and had not paid the money, and the house collapsed, some say that he may retract and others say that he may not. See Tosafot to Bava Kamma 9a, s.v. "and claims came against it." It likewise appears at first glance that the rule that before taking possession he may retract might apply even to accidents, and thus also where the house collapsed. This may be rejected, however, because Tosafot's proof is only that the term "claims" must mean challengers to title. Once that is established independently, it follows that in cases of accident he cannot retract even before taking possession. Analyze carefully. See Responsa Rama, siman 19.
