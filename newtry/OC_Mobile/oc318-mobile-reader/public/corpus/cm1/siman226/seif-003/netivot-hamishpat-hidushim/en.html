At the time of the sale. This means responsibility arising from his own right, such as the case in the preceding seif where that right already existed at the time of sale. But if Shimon owed money to Jacob, Reuven's father, and afterward Reuven sold a field to Shimon, and only later inherited Jacob's debt, Reuven may collect from the very field he sold to Shimon even though he is the seller and now seeks to remove the property. It is as though Reuven himself lent Shimon the money after the sale, in which case it is obvious that he may collect his debt from him. Sema.
