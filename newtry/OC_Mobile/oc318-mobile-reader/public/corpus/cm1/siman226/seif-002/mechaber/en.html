Reuven sold a field to Shimon without warranty, then bought it back from Shimon with warranty. If Reuven's creditor later seizes it from him, Reuven cannot recover from Shimon. Although Reuven did not accept warranty toward Shimon in the first sale, he accepted responsibility for claims arising from himself, for he cannot be both the original seller and the person who removes the field from his own purchaser. If, however, the creditor of Reuven's father Yaakov seizes it from Reuven, Reuven may recover the full purchase price from Shimon.
