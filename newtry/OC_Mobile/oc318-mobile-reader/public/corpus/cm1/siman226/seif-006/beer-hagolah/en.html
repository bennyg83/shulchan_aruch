There, law 1. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's ruling is straightforward in reason, because this should be no less prohibited than other forms of ona'ah. In chapter Shevuat HaEdut the Gemara applies the verse 'and one who did not do good among his people' to one who buys a field with pending claims against it. Since the buyer acts improperly, it follows that a seller likewise may not sell such property without informing him. The term asikin means challengers or claimants, from the expression 'they contended with him,' there, 31a, in the name of Shmuel.
