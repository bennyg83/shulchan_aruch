That they sold. One might have thought: How can they be both the sellers and the parties extracting the property for themselves on account of a debt owed to the orphans? The ruling teaches that we do not say this, because they did not sell anything for themselves. It is comparable to a beit din that sold Shimon's property to Reuven in satisfaction of Reuven's debt. It is obvious that if the court itself later has an earlier-dated debt instrument against Shimon, it may collect from him. See above siman 147:2 and 4, Rama's gloss and what is written there in explanation. Sema.
