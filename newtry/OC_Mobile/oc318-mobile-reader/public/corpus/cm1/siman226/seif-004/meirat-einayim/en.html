Guardians who sold, etc. The warranty of a sale made by guardians does not fall upon the guardians' own property but upon the orphans' estate, as the Tur and Mechaber write above in simanim 108 and 109. One might still ask how a guardian can be both the seller and the person now seeking to collect for his own debt against the orphans. The ruling teaches that this is not comparable to a seller seeking to remove property for himself. The guardians sold nothing of their own. They are like beit din selling Shimon's assets to Reuven in satisfaction of a debt. If beit din themselves held an earlier promissory note against Shimon, they could certainly later collect by virtue of it. See above siman 147, seifim 2 and 4, Rama's gloss, and my comments in the Sema resolving that matter.
