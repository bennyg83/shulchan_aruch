Zayin) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 19 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, from Abaye's statement at the beginning of Bava Kamma 9a. The Maggid Mishneh explains Rambam to mean that Shimon already acquired the property through one of the valid modes of acquisition before he made use of it. Rambam holds that use itself can constitute chazakah for acquisition, as stated in chapter 1 and above in siman 141. Here, even after the acquisition, the buyer may still retract before using the property, even though he already paid. The Kesef Mishneh cites the Baal HaIttur in the name of the Rif that this applies only where the seller has property from which the buyer can later collect. If not, the buyer may retract, because he can say, "By the time I seek collection, you may have consumed the money and I will have nothing from which to collect."
Meaning, this is before he has walked along the boundary, as explained above.
