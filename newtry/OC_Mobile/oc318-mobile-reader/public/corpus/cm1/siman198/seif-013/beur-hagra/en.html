Some say, etc. See Rashbam, Bava Batra 76b. Because pulling is at issue, the Gemara says that in the public domain it does not acquire by pulling but by delivery. Rashbam therefore explains that the ship is in water. Baal HaItur agrees, unlike Sema. The reason is that a ship is not ordinarily pulled without water, and a commercial acquisition must be performed in the customary manner, as stated in the first chapter of Bava Metzia. See siman 197, seif 5, and Sema there, seif katan 12.
