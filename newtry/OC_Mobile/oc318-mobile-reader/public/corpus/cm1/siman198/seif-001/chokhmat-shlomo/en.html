Seif 1: Rather, by lifting. Note: If the price was fixed but the buyer lifted the item not for the purpose of acquisition, but only to mark it as his, inspect it, or determine whether it was suitable, such lifting may not acquire. This appears from the Shach in Yoreh De'ah citing Rashba, who distinguishes lifting intended for acquisition from lifting performed while measuring. At first glance the same should apply here. However, the Shach's presentation is not entirely simple. The issue seems to depend on the dispute cited later in siman 210. Rashba and Raavad hold that an act performed merely to measure and not to acquire does not effect acquisition. The Tur, however, holds that measuring can acquire even where the person intended only to measure, and the same would apply to lifting performed for another purpose. The dispute may concern whether we accept the circumstantial inference that the act was not intended as a kinyan, or whether such an inference cannot defeat the objective acquisition act. Practically, this remains a legal uncertainty. See Machaneh Ephraim and my responsum where I reconciled Tosafot's apparently conflicting passages.
