By Torah law money effects acquisition. Rashi derives this from the verse, "He shall give the money and it shall be established for him." The Ran writes that we do not derive ordinary transactions from consecrated property, but rather the rule is based upon logic, since most acquisitions are ordinarily made through money. In the Perishah I explained, and brought proof, that by Torah law money effects acquisition of movables, while meshichah and mesirah do not.
