Seif 5: Since it is readily available at the house. Although the Rambam writes, “because it is in his possession,” his intent is that money acquires in this case because the seller will certainly rescue the goods if danger arises. The Gemara explicitly gives the reason that otherwise one fears the goods may burn and the seller will not trouble himself to save them. The Rambam's following phrase, “and likewise one who rents,” refers back to the principal rule, including the Gemara's case of transferring property by renting its location. This resolves the Sema's question.
