Alef) A statement of Rabbi Yochanan at the beginning of HaZahav, Bava Metzia 47b, with the reason explained there.
Bet) The statement as taught in Sura, Bava Batra 86a.
Gimel) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 2. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it derives from the baraita and the Gemara's explanation there on 86b: flax is different because it can be pulled out, and the usual manner is to untie it and lift it bundle by bundle. Most commentators explain it this way and establish this interpretation. The Kesef Mishneh writes that Rambam appears to explain that flax is different because it can be pulled out little by little and lifted; therefore even large bundles are acquired only through hagbahah. The same applies to anything that does not involve substantial effort to pull out little by little and lift. But produce such as nuts, almonds, and pepper, which cannot readily be pulled out because there is considerable effort in untying and lifting them little by little, is acquired through meshichah.
