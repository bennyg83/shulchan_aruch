This mesirah, etc. Meaning that although Bava Metzia chapter 1 asks, “What is the meaning of moserah? Like one who hands over, etc.,” and there, “Who handed it to him so that he should acquire?” it does not literally require hand to hand delivery. Rather, it is as explained below. Proof comes from Bava Batra there: if he said, “Go take possession, etc.,” and afterward, “Here he said, etc.” If the seller physically handed it to him, how could the Gemara suggest that the seller was particular about the manner of acquisition? By handing it over himself he nullified any such insistence. Likewise the phrase “he grasped it by its hooves, etc.” implies that the buyer acts on his own.
In the presence of, etc., or. As stated in Bava Batra chapter 3 and Bava Kamma chapter 5 regarding chazakah: in the seller’s presence there is no need for an express instruction, etc. Here, however, without the seller’s authorization he does not acquire even ownerless property, as stated by “Who handed it to him?” meaning who told and instructed him to acquire. See Tosafos in Kiddushin there.
