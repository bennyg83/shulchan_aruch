[Shulchan Aruch] For rental of movable property. Note: From the words of the Tur below, siman 310, who writes in the name of the Raavad that when one rents an unspecified donkey there is no acquisition, but only the sanction of Mi ShePara, it necessarily follows that this is where the renter prepaid the rental fee, because otherwise even Mi ShePara would not apply. See Sema, siman 310, seif katan 6. Nimukei Yosef, in the relevant discussion there, indeed cites the Raavad and likewise writes that rental of movable property is acquired by money. See there carefully. However, Nimukei Yosef in the chapter HaShoel, in the discussion of the enactment of meshichah for bailees, writes in the name of the Rashba that rental of movable property is not acquired by money, and concludes that this is also the view of the later authorities. This is likewise implied by Rashi in Bava Kamma 79b. In my opinion, it is difficult to understand how the concern that the property might burn can apply to rental of movable property, since if it burns the renter will not have to pay the rental fee, as we say regarding one who rented a donkey to ride and it died halfway along the journey, that he pays only the rental fee for half the journey. If so, when the owner says, "It burned," the renter suffers no loss.
