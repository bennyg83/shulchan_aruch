To lift. Tosafot in the first chapter of Bava Metzia 9b and the glosses to Mordechai there imply that it counts as hagbahah when the object is raised through his action by way of ascent even if he did not take it in his hand. This is also the view of the Rosh there and Rabbeinu Yerucham, Netiv 10, part 1. Tosafot in Bava Kamma 98a, however, imply some doubt, and Hagahot Ashri there records Rabbeinu Yitzchak's doubt whether this is so only when he physically lifts it. Regarding living creatures, however, Tosafot there also write that it counts as hagbahah when birds rise because he beats upon the nest. If they descend because of his beating, that is not hagbahah; only their ascent is. Maadanei Melech in Bava Kamma writes that according to the Rosh it is obvious that he does not acquire merely because the object was raised, and that Tosafot in Bava Kamma and Bava Metzia conclude so. Anyone who examines Tosafot and the Rosh will see that this is not so, but rather as I have written. See Yam Shel Shlomo at the end of Chullin. Its words require study. See also what I wrote in siman 269, seif 5. Shach.
