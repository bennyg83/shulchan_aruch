Seif 2: Some say that three handbreadths are required, etc. Note: The Mechaber does not explain that even according to this opinion, one handbreadth should suffice for ownerless property. The Rosh in Bava Kamma explains that because some authorities hold that even looking at ownerless property can acquire it, actual lifting of one handbreadth is certainly sufficient. What is stated elsewhere, that three handbreadths are required, concerns a case where the object is not lifted directly by hand but only through one's force. Actual lifting by hand of an ownerless object requires only one handbreadth.
