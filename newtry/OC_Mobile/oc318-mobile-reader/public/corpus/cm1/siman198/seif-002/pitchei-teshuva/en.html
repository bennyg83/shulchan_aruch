Or by meshichah of an item whose ordinary manner of acquisition is not hagbahah. Shaar Mishpat writes that the unqualified wording of the authorities implies that even if the purchaser has already paid all of the money, meshichah does not acquire an object whose normal method of acquisition is hagbahah.
