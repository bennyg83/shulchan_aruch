Hagahot Maimoniyot there, chapter 2, in the name of Rashi, because it has left the law of lavud.
It is written in the name of R. R. A.D., and it is written in his book, “If he showed food to the elephant, and he jumps and feeds himself from the land and eats evidence of it from the mouth of the elephant, and thus brings evidence to it from the end of the earth.”
