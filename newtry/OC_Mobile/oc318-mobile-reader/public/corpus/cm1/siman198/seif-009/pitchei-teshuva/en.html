He has already revealed his intent. See Responsa Mishkenot Yaakov at the end of siman 51, who questions Rama. Here Rama states the rule without qualification, whereas in siman 268, seif 1, concerning acquisition of a found object, he cites the opposing view that even where one revealed that he wished to acquire by falling upon the object and not by his four cubits, he nevertheless acquires through the four cubits. That view disagrees with Rashba. Mishkenot Yaakov leaves the matter requiring very great study. It is surprising that he did not mention Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot Zechiyah, chapter 2, halachah 9, who resolves this well. There is a distinction: in the case of a found object, at the very time when the four cubits were available to acquire for him, he intended to acquire. Although he did not intend that the acquisition operate through the four cubits, he nevertheless intended to acquire at that moment. Here, by contrast, at the time the item was delivered into his hand, he did not intend to acquire through that delivery, but only through meshichah, and he had not yet performed a proper meshichah from the public domain into a side alley.
