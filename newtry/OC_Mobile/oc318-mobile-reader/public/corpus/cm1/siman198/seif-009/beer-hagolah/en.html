P. Rambam is named in the D.C. and as the “This Is Memerji and Rava” (P.D.)
So writes the Maggid Mishneh there in the name of the Rashba. Since he had no permission to place the goods there, that location is like the public domain for them. Had he had permission, the goods would be a deposit in the courtyard owner's possession, and the Mishnah teaches that in the domain of the custodian he does not acquire, etc., 85a.
