The language of Rambam there, law 4.
So the Maggid Mishneh explains Rambam's reasoning there. This is what the Gemara says in the discussion of a ship, Bava Batra 76b: does meshichah in the public domain effect acquisition? It answers that 'until he pulls it' means from the public domain into an alleyway, etc. Rambam holds that pulling even part of it into the alleyway is sufficient, even according to Shmuel. The Maggid Mishneh writes that he found the Ramban saying so in chapter 3 of Ketubot in the sugya of one who dragged something out, 31b.
