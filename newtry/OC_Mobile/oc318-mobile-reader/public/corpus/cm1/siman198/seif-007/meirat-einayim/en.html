But we will buy in the boat of P.M. when it stands in the yard that is not theirs:
If he said to him, "Pull it and acquire," etc., until he pulls all of it out of the place. As I wrote in seif 3, this language implies that he must pull the object through its full length. Since the present discussion concerns an object standing in the public domain or a courtyard belonging to neither party, moving it merely its own length within the public domain is insufficient. He must pull it from the public domain into a side alley or a courtyard belonging to both, and once it enters there he need not again pull it its full length. The Mechaber abbreviates here and relies upon what he writes below in seif 14. His purpose here is only to teach that when the seller insists on meshichah, mere mesirah does not help; a complete act of meshichah is required.
