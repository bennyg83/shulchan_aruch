See Netivot HaMishpat, who cites Bach as explaining that this case is different because the Sages did not uproot monetary acquisition except for the purchaser's benefit. He may therefore stipulate, "I do not desire the rabbinic enactment that was instituted for my benefit." See there. It appears that this is also the view of Netivot HaMishpat, and Shaar Mishpat likewise agrees. Ketzot HaChoshen also writes that the view of Beit Yosef and Rama appears correct from the beginning of the second chapter of Bechorot, where the Gemara challenges from the case of one who purchases scrap metal and answers that the case concerns one who accepted upon himself to be judged according to Jewish law.
