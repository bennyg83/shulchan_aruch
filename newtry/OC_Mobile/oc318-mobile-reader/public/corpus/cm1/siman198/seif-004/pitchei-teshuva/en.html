See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, siman 133, who questions Shach because his words contradict what Shach himself writes below in siman 313. According to Shach's explanation there of Rambam, in every case the courtyard remains that of the lessor. If so, Shach's present explanation of Rambam is difficult, because the purchaser should acquire the movable property in any event by virtue of his courtyard.
