[Sema, letter 21] Like an unpaid bailee. Note: The Beit Yosef writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham that nevertheless the buyer has acquired in a manner that neither party may retract, yet while the item remains in the seller's domain the seller is not even an unpaid bailee, for it is as though he said, "Your property is before you." See there. Tosafot in Eruvin 81b, s.v. "shema yomru," do not appear to agree. Analyze carefully.
