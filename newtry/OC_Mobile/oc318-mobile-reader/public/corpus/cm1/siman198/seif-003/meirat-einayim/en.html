He must pull the entire object, etc. This differs from an animal, where uprooting one foreleg and one hind leg is enough even though the body has not yet moved from its original place, as the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 197:3. The reason is that with a living creature, once it begins moving its legs it will naturally move its whole body. Other movable objects do not move further by themselves.
And we will get out of all the place in which he is in, even when he is in the midst of a place where he continues to receive the length of the same thing that he has hoped and will end up in the place that his head will be replaced, and if he is a knee and must be brought to the end of his life, and he will bring him to the end of his life
