[Rama] Likewise, if he swore. Note: If he sold through an agent, it may be that the sale is void because there is no agency for the commission of a transgression. Although the agent himself did not swear, the matter nevertheless depends on the dispute among the authorities whether we follow the formulation that the agent must himself be subject to liability for the transgression, or the formulation that the reason is that the agent could choose whether or not to act. It may also be said that even according to the formulation requiring the agent to be personally subject to the prohibition, the rule that agency fails applies specifically to a kohen who tells an Israelite, "Go and betroth a divorced woman to me," because the agent has no personal connection to the prohibition concerning a divorced woman and is not warned against that prohibition at all. Here, however, the agent is also personally subject to the prohibition and is warned regarding the prohibition of violating an oath. Analyze carefully. If we conclude that the sale is void, there is a further question where one swore not to sell but transgressed and sold subject to a condition: perhaps the condition is void while the act remains valid, because the act cannot be performed through an agent. See Sefer HaMakneh at the beginning of siman 38, and see below at the beginning of siman 235 in my marginal note. See Responsa Panim Meirot, part 1, siman 34.
