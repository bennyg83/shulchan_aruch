A transaction, etc. From Rava’s three statements there. See Bava Kamma 70b, the end of siman 195, and what was written there.
(Collected note) A transaction, etc. This is also ruled in Yoreh Deah siman 161 seif 8 and at the end of siman 175, unlike what is written in siman 230. (End of note.)
Likewise if, etc. This is derived from the preceding rule. See Tosafos, Temurah 6a, s.v. “and now,” Sema and Shach, the end of siman 195, and the end of siman 235. Hagahos Mordechai disagrees, based on the principle at the beginning of Temurah that whenever the Torah says not to do something, etc., and Kesubos 81b, “since the Sages said not to sell, etc.” Certainly the same should apply here. See Yoreh Deah siman 230.
(Collected note) And it was confirmed by a kinyan, etc. This is the Rosh’s wording there in the name of the Gaon. See Beer HaGolah seif katan 3. Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 175 seif 8, likewise rules so. (End of note.)
Nevertheless, etc. As stated there: why not say to him “when you hired, etc.,” because, etc. That reasoning does not apply here.
