Melog. Regarding nichsei tzon barzel, the husband does not need authorization. Even if his wife objects, he may litigate concerning them, as stated in Even HaEzer 75, seif 4. See Beit Shmuel there. Shach.
Fruits. The Shach holds that there must presently be fruits on the land. See his lengthy discussion. See Even HaEzer 83 and Beit Shmuel there, seif katan 5.
Needs. The Shach writes that the wording implies that if the wife objects, the husband cannot litigate. Responsa Mabit II, 164, states this explicitly. Although the Beit Yosef in siman 176 cites the Ritva that a husband does not need authorization regarding his wife's property because his hand is like hers, and may sue orally even if she objects, that case apparently concerns fruits that legally belong to him. Regarding such fruits she certainly cannot prevent him. See the Shach.
