And if the land has produce, etc. In the Perishah I proved that even if it presently has no fruit because the fruit has already been detached, nevertheless, so long as the field is ordinarily productive in future years, the husband may litigate regarding the body of the land as well. According to Rav Hai, only land that produces no fruit at all is excluded. According to the Rosh, even in such a case he may litigate regarding the land itself. The only excluded case is where husband and wife admit that the produce was granted to the defendant for several years and that title to the land remains with the wife, while the defendant claims title to the land itself. Mahari likewise proves this in Terumat HaDeshen siman 312, contrary to the Rivash in responsum 496. I discussed this at length in my responsa relating to Choshen Mishpat siman 25.
