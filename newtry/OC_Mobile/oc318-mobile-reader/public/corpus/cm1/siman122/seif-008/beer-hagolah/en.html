The statement of Abaye, Gittin 48b. Sema writes that in Perishah he proved that even if no produce is presently upon the land because it has already been detached, since the field is intended to produce fruit in future years the husband may litigate even concerning the land itself. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 312, writes that even if the property itself produces no fruit but must be sold to buy land whose produce the husband will consume, he may litigate without authorization, because there is presently some practical consequence concerning produce. When the Gemara rules that authorization is required where he does not descend to litigate concerning produce, it means, as Rashi explains, that he has no claim with the woman regarding produce at all. Terumat HaDeshen explains this as where the produce had already been sold for three or four years to the possessor of the land by the one who bequeathed the land to the woman, so that she presently has no practical interest in the produce.
