This is based on Rav Huna's statement regarding two brothers, Ketubot 34a, and on the baraita in Shevuat HaPikadon, Shevuot 37a, where the verse "and denies his fellow" is explained in a case where he said to both partners, "You are false claimants," etc. Raavad writes that from here we learn that one partner may sue for the entire claim.
This distinction is explicit in the Gemara in Ketubot. It is also explained there that if the defendant is obligated to swear and swears to one partner, an oath to one is deemed an oath to a hundred.
