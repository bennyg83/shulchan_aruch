The defendant is exempt. He can say, 'I relied on the credibility of your authorization and agency, assuming you would not revoke it. If you did revoke it, you caused your own loss.' Sema holds that even if the agent himself knew of the revocation, so long as the defendant did not know, the defendant is exempt. The Shach disagrees because the agency was nevertheless revoked. See Tumim. His objection is not compelling. The correct view is Sema's, although I wrote that the matter may depend on the dispute between Rosh and other authorities. See Tumim.
