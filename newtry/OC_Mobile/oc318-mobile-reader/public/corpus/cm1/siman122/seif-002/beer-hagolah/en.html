According to his textual version there. Bach writes that it accords with what is implied by his rulings in the first chapter HaGozel.
Tur in the name of Ba'al HaItur, because the borrower can say to the lender: I relied upon you that the agent remained acting on your behalf. Just as there is a presumption that an agent performs his agency, there is a presumption that the principal maintains his authorization, etc. I should not suffer loss because you later retracted. Ba'al HaTerumot writes this in Gate 2 in his name and agrees. Rambam likewise holds this in chapter 3, law 10, cited by the Mechaber in the following seif, and Raavad did not challenge him there.
