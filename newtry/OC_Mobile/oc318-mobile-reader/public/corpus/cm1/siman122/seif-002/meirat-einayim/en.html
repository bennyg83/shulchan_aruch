The defendant is exempt. The Tur cites the Baal HaItur's reason: the lender may say, 'I relied upon you to stand by your agency. Just as there is a presumption that an agent carries out his mission, there is also a presumption that a person stands by his word. You should not have retracted, and I should not suffer a loss because of your retraction.' The Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe write that even if the agent knew that the authorization had been revoked, if the defendant did not know, the defendant is exempt from responsibility according to the Rif and Rambam. Only where the authorized representative actually litigated and lost does the effect of revocation depend upon whether the agent knew of it, as Rama writes in seif 3, because requiring the defendant to litigate again does not impose a comparable loss upon him.
