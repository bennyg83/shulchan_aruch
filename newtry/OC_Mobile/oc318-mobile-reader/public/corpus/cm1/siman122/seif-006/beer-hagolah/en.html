There, siman 3. Rambam writes likewise and derives it from Rav Ashi's statement there: since the principal wrote, "Whatever claim may arise," etc., he made him an agent.
He writes there that although, if the money were still in the depositary's possession, Reuven could certainly retract even after making a kinyan with Shimon, because money is not acquired by exchange, nevertheless once Shimon has already received it, Reuven has no claim against him and Shimon may give him its equivalent in Reuven's town. He brings proof from one who sells produce that has not yet come into existence: the produce is not acquired, but if the purchaser went first and took it, it is not removed from his possession.
