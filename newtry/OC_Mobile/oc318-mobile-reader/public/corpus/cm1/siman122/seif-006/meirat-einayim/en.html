For this is what is written in an authorization. In the Derishah I explained that if the authorization does not state, 'Whatever legal consequence you incur, it returns upon me,' then all the more so the principal must reimburse every expense, because the authorized representative could otherwise have retained the entire recovery. The practical difference is that if the representative already handed over the recovered money before being reimbursed, he need not be repaid his expenses where this clause was not written. But once the clause is written, it appears that even if his expenses exceed the amount recovered, the principal must reimburse all of them, not merely up to the amount collected, as I wrote in the Sema to siman 375:7.
