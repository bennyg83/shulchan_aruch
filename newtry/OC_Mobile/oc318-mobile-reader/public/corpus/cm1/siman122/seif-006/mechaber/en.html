If one grants another a power of attorney, the principal must bear all expenses the authorized agent incurs in this litigation. See below, siman 375, seif 4. This is why the power of attorney states, 'Whatever you incur through this litigation is chargeable to me,' meaning, 'I undertake to repay every expense you incur in this litigation.' {Rama: The authorized person is only an agent. If he wishes to retain the money, he cannot do so and must return it. Therefore, if the principal demands a deposit from its custodian, who claims that he gave it to the authorized agent and then lent money to that agent against it, the custodian must return the deposit to its owner, because the authorized agent was only an agent. If Reuven had money in another city, Shimon traveled there, and Reuven authorized Shimon to receive the money there and give him its equivalent in Reuven's city, then after Shimon received it, Reuven came to that city and demanded that Shimon give him the money there, the law follows Shimon. See Terumat HaDeshen 313.}
