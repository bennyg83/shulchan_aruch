Tur, seif 10, in the name of Responsa Rosh, klal 15. In the actual responsum the case involved a gift to Shimon rather than to the donor's son. The Mechaber changed the case and wrote 'to his son' because he follows the Rif and Rambam, as written in the preceding seif, that specifically a gift of a dying person to his son is invalid in this setting, whereas a gift to others is valid.
