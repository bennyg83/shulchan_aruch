If a deathly ill person acknowledges, "I hold such-and-such for so-and-so; give it to him," his acknowledgment is effective. Even a convert's acknowledgment in favor of a son conceived before conversion, or in favor of a non-Jew, is honored. But if a deathly ill person merely directs that a gift be given to a non-Jew, the instruction is not obeyed, because it is like directing that a transgression be committed with his property.
