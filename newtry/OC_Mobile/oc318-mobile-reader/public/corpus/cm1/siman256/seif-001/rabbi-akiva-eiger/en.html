[Shulchan Aruch] For the language of inheritance and gift. Note: Why is it ineffective for one to uproot the inheritance by saying, "I do not give this by way of inheritance but by way of gift," when the Rambam rules, as codified above in siman 248, that in such a case it is a gift and he may interrupt the succession? The answer is that there he has only one son. In such a case, even with language intended to uproot inheritance, the recipient retains the status of heir. Indeed, in that case even an "after you" disposition cannot interrupt his inheritance. But if a convert had several sons and gave his property to one of them, saying, "not by way of inheritance" and so forth, according to the Rambam it is effective. See Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, simanim 6 and 74.
