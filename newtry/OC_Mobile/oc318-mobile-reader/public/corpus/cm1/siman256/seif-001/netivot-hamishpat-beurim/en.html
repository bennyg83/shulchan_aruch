And a shetuki. See Ketzot HaChoshen, who asks why this differs from a person who came from overseas and whose heirs are unknown. The distinction is clear. Here it is impossible ever to establish lawful heirs. Even if witnesses testify that the mother had relations with a particular man, that does not establish paternal inheritance. The estate is therefore like property abandoned through complete despair, and ultimately like absolute ownerless property. Since it can never pass by ordinary inheritance, it is legally treated as property not subject to inheritance.
