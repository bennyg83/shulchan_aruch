A deathbed gift takes effect after death, and a gift cannot take effect after death. The Sages therefore established it through the law of inheritance, and every, etc. Rashba. Ramban defends the first view. Rif and the Geonim's statement that wherever inheritance is inapplicable, etc., is difficult, for how can any person unfit to inherit acquire by a deathbed gift? Rather, their meaning is that where the beneficiary is fit to inherit, the Sages did not establish acquisition through gift but only through inheritance. By strict law he would not acquire, as explained, and the enactment was made only so that the property should not be seized, etc. An heir, however, acquires independently through inheritance.
