The road. It appears that this still concerns a case where he could have gone around from the side or raised his feet and passed over the objects. Therefore he is exempt specifically if he tripped and broke them. But if the owner filled the road so completely that it was impossible to pass, then even if the passerby deliberately broke them with his hands he is exempt, as the Mechaber states immediately afterward. This is also the implication of the Gemara at the beginning of HaManiach and of Rashi and Tosafot there. See above, end of siman 379. Sema.
