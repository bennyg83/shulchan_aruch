If it was dark, or he filled the entire road with barrels, he is exempt, etc. This is the Rambam's language in chapter 13 of Hilchot Nizkei Mamon. It appears that even when the road was filled with barrels, the case still assumes that it was possible to go around them, walk along the side, or step carefully over them. Therefore the Mechaber says only that if he stumbled and broke a barrel he is exempt. But where the road was filled so completely that passage was impossible, the Mechaber immediately writes that even if he deliberately breaks the barrels in order to pass, he is exempt. This is also the implication of the Gemara at the beginning of HaManiach, Rashi and Tosafot there, and see the end of siman 379.
