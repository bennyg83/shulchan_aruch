One who leaves a jug in the public domain, one carrying a jug who stumbles and breaks it, and one who pours water into the public domain and causes damage. It contains five seifim.
If one leaves a jug in the public domain and another person stumbles over it and breaks it, the passerby is exempt, because people do not ordinarily inspect the road closely while walking. If the passerby is injured by the jug, the jug's owner is liable. This applies even if he renounced ownership of it, because one who abandons a hazard that he had no right to create remains liable.
