Stumbling is not negligence. The Rif and Rambam rule this way, and the Tur likewise writes without distinction in this siman. At the beginning of siman 304, however, the Tur distinguishes between stumbling on a slope and stumbling on level ground and writes that on level ground it is negligence by strict law. The abridged rulings of the Rosh at the end of HaOmanin state the same. The Rosh himself, however, does not explicitly mention such a distinction there. In the Derishah to siman 304 I discussed this at length.
