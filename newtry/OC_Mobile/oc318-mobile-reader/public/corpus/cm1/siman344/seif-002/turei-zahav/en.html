(Seif 4: He rented one and one of them died, etc.) The text should read: “when the lender makes a definite claim,” etc. The arrangement of the Tur and Rambam implies that even when the lender says he does not know, the renter must swear that he does not know. This is difficult, for even a rabbinic oath is not imposed upon a claimant's doubtful allegation. The Mechaber's statement that the lender's doubtful claim may be included through a rolling oath, even without grounds for suspicion, is likewise astonishing. The Sema answers that the ruling follows the Rambam's Commentary on the Mishnah, but siman 94 explicitly follows the Rosh that a doubtful claim cannot be added by rolling oath without some basis. This requires study.
