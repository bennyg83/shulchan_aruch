And he is not called one obligated to swear. In the Derishah I explained that the lender himself is not making a definite claim about the exact value of the loss. He says only, 'Perhaps it was worth such and such and you lost it,' and it may be that the loss was not even worth a perutah. Alternatively, the borrower admits a definite small amount and offers it immediately, saying that beyond that he does not know whether he caused further loss, while the lender does not make a definite claim for the excess. In that case this is not a person who is obligated to swear but cannot swear, so he does not pay on that basis. The Ir Shushan's comparison to the rule of an uncertain rolling oath is not exact.
