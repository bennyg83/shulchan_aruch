And he is not considered one obligated to swear but unable to swear. The case is where he says, "Here it is," or where the lender himself is uncertain. The Shach asks that if so, how can the lender swear and collect? He therefore explains that the borrower claims the item deteriorated through the ordinary work for which it was borrowed, concerning which he cannot be required to swear, or that he is willing to pay that amount. Therefore he is not treated as one who partially admits concerning the remaining claim.
