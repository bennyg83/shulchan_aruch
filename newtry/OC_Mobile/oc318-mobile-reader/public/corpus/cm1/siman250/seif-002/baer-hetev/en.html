Stipulated. The Sages enacted that the words of a dangerously ill person are treated as though written and delivered only with respect to a gift that is to take effect if he dies, so that concern that his wishes will not be carried out will not agitate him. That enactment does not apply here, where he stipulated that even if he recovers he will not be able to retract. Such a condition is effective only if he made an actual complete kinyan expressly for that purpose, in which case the gift has the same status as an ordinary gift made by a healthy person. Sema.
