Established as possessing. This is the language of the Gemara. The Tur writes that the Rambam explains that it must be established by witnesses that the donor owns no more property, whereas Rabbeinu Yitzchak writes that witnesses are unnecessary and an ordinary presumption is sufficient. From the unqualified language of the Mechaber it appears that he follows Rabbeinu Yitzchak. Moreover, if witnesses were required, the previous case, where the donor says, 'All my property, which consists of these items,' would already suffice and this ruling would be superfluous. Ibid.
