One who goes to sea. Many authorities disagree and hold that such a person was equated with one in danger only regarding a get, where saying, "Write," is effective without also saying, "Give," because he is anxious and preoccupied. For these monetary laws, however, he is not treated like one giving instructions because of impending death, since he expects to return. Nimukei Yosef writes that ordinarily one who goes overseas is presumed to intend to settle there, and therefore if he later does not settle there he may retract from his gift. See Beurim for the respects in which such persons differ from one giving instructions because of death.
