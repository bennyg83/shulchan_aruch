[Shulchan Aruch] If he recovered, he may retract. Note: Responsa Rashba, part 3, siman 122, writes that this is specifically if he expressly retracts. Merely recovering does not automatically void the sale. See there. This applies specifically where the purchaser acquired by a kinyan. But if there was only an oral sale, then even if he sold all his property and died, the sale does not take effect, because the Sages enacted that a deathly ill person's words are effective without a kinyan only for a gift, not for a sale.
