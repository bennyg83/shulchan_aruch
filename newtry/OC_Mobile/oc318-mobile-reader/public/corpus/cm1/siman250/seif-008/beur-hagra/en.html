One who sets out to sea, etc. This follows the statement in Gittin there, 'and just as his bill of divorce, etc.' Likewise on 65b: 'Originally, etc.' The ruling at the end of chapter 4 of Chullin follows Rabbi Shimon Shezuri, unlike Rosh, who limits the rule to one in danger or being taken to execution. For if their view were correct, what did Rav Huna add? Bava Batra 147a already asks, 'From where, etc.?' and derives the rule from Ahitophel, who was like one being led to execution and in danger.
