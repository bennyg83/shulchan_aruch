And no authorization is required. See Baer Heitev, who writes that we likewise do not require that it came, etc. See Netivot HaMishpat, who writes that the words "and to give it to him" in Sema, seif katan 60, are a scribal error. The principal distinction is between a case where he instructed only that it be written and a case where he also instructed that it be delivered. See there.
