Even, etc., and he delivered, etc. This follows Rashbam. According to Tosafos there, s.v. "vekhasav, etc.," and the Rosh, however, even if he also conferred the gift upon him through another, he does not acquire until all four elements are present: he transferred possession, wrote, delivered, and conferred acquisition. See Tur seif 21 and seif 24.
And likewise if, etc. See Tosafos to Kesubos 55b at the end of s.v. "mattanah": "it may be said that every, etc." See below siman 61:5.
There is an authority who says, etc. Since it was unnecessary and nevertheless he made a kinyan, just as with a deathly ill person.
Others say that if, etc. Since he comes to strengthen the recipient's position. A similar distinction is made in Kiddushin 59b: "it was necessary, etc."
He transferred, etc. Meaning, since acquisition through land is unnecessary for a deathly ill person, and he said it with intent to convey immediately by means of the land, it does not take effect immediately because the land is not currently his.
And if, etc. The same as with a kinyan.
A deathly ill person who instructed, etc. In this case there is no concern that he did not fully intend to transfer, since no kinyan is necessary.
And likewise if, etc. As stated above in seif 9, "and likewise if, etc."
