Even. The reason is that where the donor wished to give only by means of a kinyan sudar, a deed, or another formal mode of acquisition, he revealed that he did not rely on the rabbinic enactment that the words of a shechiv mera are treated as written and delivered, but intended the gift to operate like other acquisitions. It therefore does not take effect unless he wrote or stated that the kinyan or deed accompanying the will was not intended to detract from the deathbed disposition but only to strengthen it. So writes the Sema. See Responsa Rama, siman 48, and Responsa Maharashdam, siman 317.
