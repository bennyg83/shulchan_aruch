[Shulchan Aruch] And if they made a kinyan from him. Note: If the gift included a waiver of a debt, and no kinyan was made, it may likewise be said that we are concerned he may own property overseas, in which case this would be a gift of a healthy person and the waiver of the debt would take effect without a kinyan. This is according to the Rosh's reason that a gift is not voided on the basis of an uncertain presumption. But according to the Rashba's reasoning, which I cited in my gloss to Sema, seif katan 10, that rationale does not apply here. It may therefore be that since the waiver itself is doubtful, the recipient does not acquire even though he is in possession, as I write below, siman 251, seif katan 6. Analyze carefully.
