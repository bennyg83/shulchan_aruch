The rule that delivery of the deed does not suffice without strengthening applies when the deed is drafted as a deed of gift, such as, "My field is given to you," and delivered during life. Delivery then functions like a kinyan and requires strengthening. If the document is written in testamentary language, as a person ordering his household, it serves only as evidence; if delivered during life, the recipient acquires. Rama: Likewise, if he writes in his own hand, "My field is given to so-and-so," it is treated as an ordinary will and is valid without strengthening language.
