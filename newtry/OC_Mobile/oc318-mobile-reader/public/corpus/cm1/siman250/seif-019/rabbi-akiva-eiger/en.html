[Shulchan Aruch] If he dies, even without a kinyan. Note: All the more so if there was a kinyan together with language strengthening the recipient's rights. But a kinyan alone is worse, for even after death the recipient does not acquire. Even according to the Ramah's view below in seif 20, that is specifically where he retracts from what he gave to the first recipient and gives that same property to the second. Here, however, he is not retracting, but merely gives the remainder afterward to the second recipient. So writes the Perishah.
