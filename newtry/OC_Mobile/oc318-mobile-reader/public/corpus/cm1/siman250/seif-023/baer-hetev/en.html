A mitzvah. According to this opinion, which is Rabbeinu Yitzchak's view, Tosafot and the Rosh write that even a partial gift of a shechiv mera made without a kinyan is effective because of the mitzvah to fulfill the words of the deceased. They said that the recipient does not acquire only in the particular case where the donor tells an agent, 'Give one hundred zuz to so and so, which I am giving him,' the recipient dies during the donor's lifetime, and the donor then dies. In that case the mitzvah to fulfill the words of the deceased does not apply, because the recipient died first. The law of a deathbed gift, however, would apply if the gift comprised the donor's entire estate, since he was a shechiv mera at the time of the instruction. Ibid.
