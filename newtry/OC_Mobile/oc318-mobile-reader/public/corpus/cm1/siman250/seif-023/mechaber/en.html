If a healthy person says, "Write and give one maneh to so-and-so," and dies, nothing is given even if he strengthened the instruction. Some say this is because he said both "write" and "give." If he said only, "Give," the money is delivered because it is a mitzvah to fulfill the wishes of the deceased. Others say that principle applies only where he placed the property in the hands of a third party for that purpose. Rama: See siman 252.
