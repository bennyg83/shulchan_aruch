Even with enhancement of his power, etc. One might ask why, if he said “write and give” with enhancement, we do not treat the enhancement as showing that the writing is merely additional and then apply the rule that it is a mitzvah to fulfill the words of the deceased, as some authorities do even regarding a healthy person. The answer is that here the donor is healthy and does not know that he will die. He therefore clearly intends the recipient to acquire specifically through the deed. A deed cannot effect acquisition after death. When he gives no instruction to write, however, he shows that he does not require a deed at all.
