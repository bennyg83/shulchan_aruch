Likewise, a shechiv mera who said, "Give," etc. This ruling is accepted by all opinions. It teaches that even though he afterward said "write and give," we do not infer that he intended the recipient to acquire specifically through the document. The Tur says this explicitly.
Not to retract from the gift, etc. Meaning, not to have the recipient acquire through speech alone, but only together with receipt of the document, as explained above.
The witnesses write, etc. Since there is a legal distinction depending on the formulation, the witnesses must be careful not to write merely that he told them, "write and give," etc.
