If he retracted part, without specification he retracted all. The rule in siman 250 that a partial retraction cancels the entire disposition appears to apply also to one who expressly gives because of impending death, since even if the original gift encompassed the whole estate, the entire deathbed disposition is subject to the special law of retraction. Even if one were to distinguish an express deathbed instruction, the Rama's rule in seif 5 that after the first three days of illness a gift is treated as a command due to death would not necessarily suffice unless the wording itself shows that character. Perhaps the rule in seif 14 that no additional express formula is required bears on this. In any event, the simple implication is that a partial retraction cancels the whole disposition. This requires further study.
