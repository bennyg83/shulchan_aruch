These rules apply only when he gives all his property and retains nothing. If he retains anything, the transfer has the law of a healthy person's gift and is acquired only through an effective kinyan. Rama: Not only a formal kinyan, but meshichah, delivery, lifting, or any other appropriate mode of acquisition suffices for land or movables. Possession helps only in such a gift; with a revocable deathly ill person's gift, seizure does not help. Even if the donor dies, a kinyan was required. Therefore, if he recovers, he cannot retract. Any retained amount, land or movables, is sufficient. Rama: Some say he must retain enough for his support.
