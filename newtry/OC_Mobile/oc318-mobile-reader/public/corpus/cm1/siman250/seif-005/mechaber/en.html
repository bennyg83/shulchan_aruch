A blind person, lame person, amputee, or one suffering in his head, eye, hand, foot, or similar localized ailment is treated as healthy for all sales and gifts. A person whose entire strength has been weakened by illness so that he cannot walk in the marketplace and lies confined to bed is called a deathly ill person. Rama: This is so even if he does not say that he gives because of the illness. Some say that during the first three days he has the ordinary status of a deathly ill person, but after three days, or if the illness suddenly becomes severe even within three days, he has the status of one commanding because of impending death, as explained below.
