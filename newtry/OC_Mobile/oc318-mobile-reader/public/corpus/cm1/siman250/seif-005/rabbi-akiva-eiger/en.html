[Shulchan Aruch] If he recovered, it is void. Note: Nimukei Yosef, folio 225a, writes: "The rule that every gift from which he could retract if he recovered has the law of a deathbed gift is not an absolute rule," and he rules regarding one who consecrated all his property or sold all his property and the like. It is evident that he holds that even consecrated property can be retracted if he recovers, but only if he actually recovers; if he does not recover, it stands. Responsa Rashba, part 3, siman 122, rejects this and concludes otherwise. This also appears to be the view of Tosafot at the end of Bava Batra, s.v. "a deathly ill person." See Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Tzedakah, siman 3.
