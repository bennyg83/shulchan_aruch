Note: If the document expressly states that he was giving because of impending death, and it also states "from now," see Responsa Mahari ben Lev, part 1, siman 61. See Kiryat Melech Rav, responsa simanim 2, 3, and 4, who writes that although the document first states that he is giving because of impending death and that the gift is to take effect after death, this does not override the definite effect of the phrase "from now" written at the end. We combine the two expressions, and the gift is treated as one taking effect from today and after death, in accordance with the Ritva. See Responsa Mahari ben Lev, part 4, siman 26, and Responsa Bnei Moshe, siman 4.
