And if he wrote, etc. These are the words of the Ran there: One may ask why he cannot retract regarding a slave, since it is an assessment of intent that a deathly ill person who recovers retracts. The answer is that because he knows that it is prohibited to create a reputation of freedom concerning slaves, we presume that he resolved fully to give him the gift. Nevertheless, if he wrote explicitly, etc., then since he expressly stipulated otherwise, we cannot say that he fully resolved to give it.
