Because a public report emerged about him. In the Perishah I cited the Rashba, who explains that the master himself knows that once the report of freedom goes out, the slave is immediately regarded publicly as free and the master cannot later reduce a free person to slavery. Therefore he fully resolves to vest freedom in him immediately. This is why the master himself must know of the report. Otherwise the Sages would have had to impose the stringencies of slavery upon the person and prohibit him from marrying a free Jewish woman.
