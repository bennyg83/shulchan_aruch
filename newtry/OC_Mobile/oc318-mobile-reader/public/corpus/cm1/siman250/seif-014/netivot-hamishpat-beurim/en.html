That he gives as a healthy-person gift. This applies specifically where he states explicitly that he is giving as a healthy person. A kinyan and the words "from now" alone do not prevent retraction if he recovers. During the illness, however, he cannot ordinarily retract, as explained above in the name of Nimukei Yosef.
And there is an opinion that this applies only when he stated it explicitly. This is the view of Ramah cited by Tur. Nimukei Yosef, however, writes explicitly that even if he merely says, "Woe, for I am dying," that is insufficient.
