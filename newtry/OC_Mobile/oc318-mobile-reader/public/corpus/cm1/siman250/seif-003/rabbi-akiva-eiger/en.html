[Rama] If he recovered, he may retract. Note: This implies that only if he recovered may he retract, but if he died the gift remains effective. This is also explicit in the source of the law, Responsa Rashba, end of siman 975, and Responsa Ramban, siman 6. See Responsa Rivash, who writes concerning a will that states that even if the testator recovers the will shall remain valid, that these are mere words, because there is then no status of shechiv mera. Whenever the testator removes the disposition from the ordinary rules of a shechiv mera gift, we do not carry out his words, just as a partial shechiv mera gift is not upheld even if he dies. This requires study.
