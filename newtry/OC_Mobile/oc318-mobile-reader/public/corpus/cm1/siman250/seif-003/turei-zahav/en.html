(Seif 3: Even if the poor took possession, etc.) This requires study. Here the Rama decides, in a doubt involving a prohibition concerning consecrated property, that nothing is removed from the heirs. He rules similarly in Yoreh Deah, siman 259, where a person's wording leaves his intent doubtful, which is the very issue here. Yet in Yoreh Deah, siman 258, seif 2, regarding one who said, "This sela is for charity," and then used a corresponding expression regarding another object, which likewise concerns charity, the Gemara in Nedarim leaves unresolved whether the law of an incomplete designation applies to charity as it does to offerings. There the Rama follows the Rosh and Rashba, ruling stringently because a doubt in a prohibition is treated stringently, as the Beit Yosef explains. This requires study.
