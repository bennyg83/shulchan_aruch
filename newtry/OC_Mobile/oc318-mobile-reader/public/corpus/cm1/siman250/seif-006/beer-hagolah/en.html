Samekh) The Tur, seif 9, from the baraita in the seventh chapter of Gittin 70a: just as one tests a person's mental competence for a get, so one tests him for commercial transactions and gifts.
Ayin) There, seif 10, in the name of Rabbenu Yitzchak and Rashbam, and this is the Rosh's conclusion. Tosafot write likewise in Kiddushin 78b, as does the Rosh there. The Rashba and Ran in Mi SheAchazo agree, as do the Sefer Mitzvot Gadol and Sefer Mitzvot Katan. See Tur, Even HaEzer siman 121.
