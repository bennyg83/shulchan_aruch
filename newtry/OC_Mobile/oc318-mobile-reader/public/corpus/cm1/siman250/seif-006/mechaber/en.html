If he becomes mute and cannot speak but signals that he wishes to give a gift, he is examined as one is examined regarding a get. If he consistently answers no to negative questions and yes to affirmative questions, his gift is valid; otherwise it is invalid. Rama: A deathly ill person's will need not state that he was of settled mind. The custom to write this is merely an enhancement of the document. A person in the throes of death may make a valid gift.
