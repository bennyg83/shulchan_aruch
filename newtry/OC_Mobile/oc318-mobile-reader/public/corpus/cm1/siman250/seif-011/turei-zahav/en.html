(The Tur implies that it is effective.) Even according to Rabbenu's ruling at the end of siman 125 that, if the donor recovers, he may retract although the property already reached the recipient, the present case is different because it has the additional advantage of a written instrument. This appears correct to me. The Rama writes that the matter requires study as to Rabbenu's source. It appears to me that Rabbenu derives it from the Gemara's statement that when he wrote and transferred possession, he may retract and the second recipient acquires. Had delivery to the recipient himself also been ineffective, the Gemara could have taught a greater novelty: instead of saying that he transferred possession through another person, it should have said that the property reached the recipient himself. We must therefore conclude that reaching the recipient is equivalent to an act of acquisition, just as writing together with transfer of possession is effective according to the Gemara. All this applies only when there is the additional advantage of a written instrument. Without that, delivery alone is ineffective. According to the Mordechai cited above in seif 3 in the name of Maharam's responsum, however, even delivery to the recipient is ineffective, as I explained there. This appears correct to me.
