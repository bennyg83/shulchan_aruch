And the last one is a complete shechiv mera gift. This applies where the gifts to the first recipients were made with kinyan. If the earlier gifts were made without kinyan, those earlier gifts are void. The property designated to them therefore remains part of the estate and constitutes a retained portion with respect to the last gift. The final gift would then no longer be a complete gift of the entire estate, and the special rule would not apply in the same manner. See the gloss to Sema citing Rosh's responsum.
