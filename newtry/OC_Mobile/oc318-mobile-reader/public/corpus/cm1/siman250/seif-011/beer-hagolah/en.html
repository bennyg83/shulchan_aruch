Vav) This is the language of the Tur, seif 17, from the statement of Rav Yosef bar Minyumi in the name of Rav Nachman there. The Gemara explains that an ordinary deathly ill person carefully considers matters and only then gives.
Zayin) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 21, and appears likewise in the Gemara.
Chet) This is the language of the Tur.
Tet) The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes this there.
