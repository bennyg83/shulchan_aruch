(Seif 16: Their words have no substance.) In the Derishah it is written that he did not confer ownership upon them during his lifetime, as the Sema wrote in seif katan 36. The reason is explained in the Derishah: even though a kinyan was made at the time of the stipulation, it was ineffective for the brothers. One should not be misled by our master's wording, "he did not confer ownership upon them," as though, had he explicitly conferred ownership upon the brothers so that they would inherit it after his death, it would be effective. That would be a gift by a healthy person and would have to take effect from today and after death. Here, however, he merely said that they would inherit, and no kinyan can take effect after death. Rather, his meaning is that he did not confer ownership upon them in the manner required for a gift by a healthy person. His further statement that he stipulated only with the woman was written lest one say that even the woman's stipulation is ineffective. He therefore explains that, regarding the woman, the stipulation is effective because she removes herself from two thirds of her ketubah entitlement. We have already established at the beginning of HaKotev that a person may remove himself from a matter not yet in existence by saying, "I have no claim or dealings in your property."
