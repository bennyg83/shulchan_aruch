Perhaps he did not fully resolve to transfer except through the deed. Therefore either the recipient's power must be enhanced or the deed must reach him during the donor's lifetime. Where it reaches him during life, enhancement is unnecessary. This seif explains that these requirements apply specifically where the donor's wording suggests that the deed itself is meant to be the acquisition. If instead his words show that he is merely arranging the details of his will and instructing that it be written and delivered as documentation, neither enhancement nor delivery during life is required. The Mechaber's concluding phrase “and he need not enhance his power” is abbreviated. He should also have said that the will need not reach the recipient during the donor's lifetime.
