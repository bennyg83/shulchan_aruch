The Beit Yosef writes in siman 7 in the name of the Rashba: Yaakov gave a parcel of land to his son Reuven as a gift of a healthy person, effective from today and after death. Years later Yaakov, while dying, distributed his estate and gave that same land to his son Shimon, and ordered the will to conclude that anyone who challenged the will would receive nothing from his estate. The Rashba ruled that Shimon acquired nothing. Although Reuven cannot take possession of the land during his father's lifetime because of the condition, Reuven's heirs will ultimately acquire it. The Sema cites this in the name of the Darkhei Moshe and discusses it at length in seif 17.
