The dispute of Rabbi Elazar, the Sages, and Rabbi Yehoshua in chapter 9 of Bava Batra, 156b. It is well known that the halachah follows the Sages and Rabbi Yehoshua. Rav Nachman likewise ruled so in the incident involving a bundle of documents, there 151a. Rambam writes likewise in chapter 8 of Hilkhot Zechiyah, law 2.
