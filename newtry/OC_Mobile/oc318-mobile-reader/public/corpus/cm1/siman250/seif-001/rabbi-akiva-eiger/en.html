[Shulchan Aruch] A deathbed gift. Note: This applies specifically when he gives his own property. But if another person appointed him an agent to transfer property to others, and this agent then purported to give it as a deathbed gift, it is ineffective, because the Sages did not enact regarding this that the words of a deathly ill person should have the force of written and delivered instruments. So it appears from Sefer Ketivah, Kuntres Acharon, siman 90, seif katan 24. Beit Meir there, seif katan 10, writes that a minor who gives movable property as a deathbed gift can effect the gift, but not land, as stated above in siman 235.
