The law of a deathly ill person's gift, of part or all of the estate, with or without a kinyan. It contains twenty-six seifim.
A deathly ill person's gift requires none of the ordinary modes of acquisition, because his words are regarded as written and delivered. Rama: There is no distinction between directing that the gift be delivered immediately after death and directing the heirs to deliver it later. If he does not designate any particular gift but merely appoints administrators and authorizes them to divide his property as they wish, with their acts to have the force of his own, some say his words are ineffective because upon death the estate vested immediately in the heirs and the gift lapsed. Others say his directions are valid.
