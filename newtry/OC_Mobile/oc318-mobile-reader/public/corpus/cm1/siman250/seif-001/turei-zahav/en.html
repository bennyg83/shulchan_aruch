(Seif 1: As though written and delivered.) The earlier editions state that the recipient acquires retroactively upon the donor's death. The Beit Yosef objects that a deathly ill person's gift does not take effect until after death. It appears to me that the practical difference of retroactive acquisition arises where the recipient dies during the donor's lifetime and the donor then dies. The recipient's heirs acquire, even if they were born only after the donor's death, as Rabbenu and the Mechaber rule at the end of siman 125, because the recipient is deemed to have acquired retroactively.
