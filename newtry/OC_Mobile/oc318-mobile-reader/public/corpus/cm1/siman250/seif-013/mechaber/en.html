If a deathly ill person writes a gift to one person and then writes another to someone else, the latter acquires, because he may retract until death. Rama: This applies where he specified that the transfer should retain the law of a deathly ill person's gift. The rule applies whether he gave all or part, whether retracting for himself or another. Even if he wrote and caused the first recipient to acquire, and then did the same for the second, the second acquires, because the first remains a deathly ill person's gift. Rama: Seizure ordinarily does not help. The Tur implies that if the written gift actually reached the recipient, it may help, but this requires study. If, however, he wrote and transferred possession and a formal kinyan was made, nothing can undo that kinyan, and he cannot retract for another or for himself, whether he gave all or part.
