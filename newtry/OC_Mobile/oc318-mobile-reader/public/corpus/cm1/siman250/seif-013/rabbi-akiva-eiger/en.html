[Shulchan Aruch] And if he was saved and recovered. Note: In the case of one who sets out to sea or departs with a caravan, he must return to his city and country, or establish residence in a known place, before the gift is voided, according to Maharikash. See Responsa Masat Moshe, siman 8, who cites Responsa Rashach that regarding one who departs with a caravan, the rule applies specifically if he dies on the road. But if he arrives at the city for which he set out and only afterward dies, the gift is void. He also writes that this applies specifically where he gave the gift at the time of his departure with the caravan, not where he gave it several days beforehand.
