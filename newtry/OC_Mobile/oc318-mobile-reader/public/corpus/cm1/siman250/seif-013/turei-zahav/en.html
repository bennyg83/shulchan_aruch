(Seif 17: he did not fully intend to transfer ownership.) In the rulings of Terumat HaDeshen, siman 230, it is written that one should not distinguish and say that an unlearned person, who does not know the law and habitually performs every matter with a kinyan, should not be subject to the rule that an unnecessary kinyan weakens the gift. If that were so, the entire Talmudic discussion would apply only to Torah scholars. Nevertheless, it appears to me that if we clearly see that the person believed a kinyan was actually required, then the kinyan certainly does not weaken the transfer.
