The language of Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilkhot Zechiyah, law 15. It is explicit on 151b and 152b that a later testamentary document cancels an earlier one.
The dispute of Rav and Shmuel there. Rambam rules like Shmuel. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Geonim explain 'he wrote and conferred possession' to mean that he said, 'Go, take possession, and acquire.' Tosafot likewise write so, unlike the Rashbam's explanation. Tosafot further write that if the property is movable, the donor writes in the document that he conferred possession through another person. The Tur likewise writes in the name of Ri and the Rosh in seif 22.

The Tur objects that Rambam requires both a kinyan and an act of acquisition for a partial gift. The Maggid Mishneh writes in chapter 8, law 10, that the case is where the donor revealed that he was giving because of impending death. The Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh struggle to reconcile this, suggesting that at the time of the gift he said that he was giving it specifically as a gift of a dying person.
