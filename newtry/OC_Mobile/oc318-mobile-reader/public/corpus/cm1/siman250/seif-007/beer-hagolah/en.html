Peh) There, seif 11, from the Gemara's conclusion at the end of Bava Batra 151a: Rava said, "and the halachah is," etc.
Tzadi) From the incident involving the sister of Rav Dimi bar Yosef at the beginning of 151b. The Rosh writes that although she did not mention death at the time of the gift, a mere disclosure of her circumstances was sufficient. Rambam likewise rules this in chapter 8, halachah 3.
