The rule that a deathbed gift with a retained portion requires a kinyan, and that if the giver recovers he cannot retract, applies when he gave it without qualification. But if he explicitly said that the gift was because of impending death, or even did not say so explicitly but his words show that he was giving because of death, such as where he lamented his impending death and the like, then even if he retained some property, the gift takes effect by verbal instruction alone, without a kinyan, upon his death. If he recovers, he may retract even if a kinyan was made for the partial gift.
