Even if there is, etc. The reason is that whenever he did not expressly say he was giving because of death, we assume that he did not give because of death, for if that had been his intent he would have given all his property. But when he expressly states that he gives because of death, or speaks mournfully about his death, then even a partial gift has the law of a full shechiv mera gift. This is straightforward, unlike the Ir Shushan, who says that when he expressly gives because of death there is greater concern that his mind may become distraught. There is no basis for that explanation.
