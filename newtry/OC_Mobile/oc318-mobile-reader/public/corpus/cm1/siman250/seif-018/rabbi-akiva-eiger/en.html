[Shulchan Aruch] And the last is a deathbed gift. Note: This is where the earlier recipients received by a kinyan. But without a kinyan, since the earlier recipients did not acquire, the gift to the last recipient is considered a gift of only part of the estate. So writes the Perishah, and likewise Responsa Rosh Yosef of Mahari Ashkafah, folio 9a.
