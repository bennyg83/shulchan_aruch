Zayin) There, seif 34. Rambam likewise rules there, halachot 12 and 13, from statements of Rabbi Yochanan and Rabbi Elazar on Bava Batra 135b.
Chet) There in the Gemara, from the report of Rav Dimi and the Gemara's explanation.
Tet) There in the name of the Raavad. The Maggid Mishneh likewise states plainly that according to the Raavad, even if the deed was delivered during the giver's lifetime the gift is void. The Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh write, however, that even the Raavad spoke only where the deed was written during life but not delivered until after death. If it was delivered during life, the agents already completed their mission. This is also the Tur's view.
