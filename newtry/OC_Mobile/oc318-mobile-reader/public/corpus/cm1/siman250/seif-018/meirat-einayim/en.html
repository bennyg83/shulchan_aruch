They do not write and give it. Meaning, after death they do not give him the document, even if it had already been written during the giver's lifetime, as the Mechaber concludes. But if it was delivered to him during the giver's lifetime, he acquires even without an enhancement clause, as stated at the end of seif 19, for the reason explained there. Rambam and the Maggid Mishneh write this. The Beit Yosef writes that the Raavad, cited by the Tur at the end of seif 34, agrees. He infers this from the Raavad's words, "even if the document was written during his lifetime they do not give it to him," meaning they do not give him the gift, rather than saying the stronger statement, "even if the document was written and delivered to the recipient during his lifetime, they do not give him the gift." This indicates that if it was delivered during his lifetime, the gift is effective.
