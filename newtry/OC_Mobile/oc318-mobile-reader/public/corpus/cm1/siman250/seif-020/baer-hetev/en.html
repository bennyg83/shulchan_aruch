Nevertheless, even according to this reasoning, he writes that if a shechiv mera divided his property between two people, made a kinyan for the first and no kinyan for the second, the disposition has the law of a gift of his entire estate: the first does not acquire even if the donor dies, while the second acquires if he dies. This is straightforward; see there. The Sema distinguishes between a case where the first gift was written with language strengthening the recipient's power and one where it was not. The Bach does not accept this distinction. Shach.
