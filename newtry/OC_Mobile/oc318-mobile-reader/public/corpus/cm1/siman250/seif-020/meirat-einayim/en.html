The second acquires even without enhancement of his power. See what I wrote in seif 13. It appears from the Tur that this is specifically where the first recipient's power was not enhanced. If the first recipient's power was enhanced while the second's was not, the second does not acquire. In such a case we cannot say that the second kinyan was surely only an additional strengthening, because he was in fact given less strength than the first. This, in my view, is the case behind the Tur's later statement that the second does not acquire unless his power was enhanced.
