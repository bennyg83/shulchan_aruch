Nevertheless, even according to this view, if a person divided his estate between two recipients, making a kinyan for the first but none for the second, the law is that of a deathbed gift of the entire estate, as stated in seif 31. The first does not acquire even if the donor dies, while the second does acquire upon the donor's death. This is straightforward. See Sema, seif katan 59. The Bach did not accept the distinction and rules contrary to the Rama.
