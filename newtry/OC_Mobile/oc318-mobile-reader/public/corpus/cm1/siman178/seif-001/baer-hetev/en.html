The Sema writes that even if the tax collector expressly says that he is waiving the tax for this particular partner, the benefit is nevertheless shared, so long as the collector did not say so on his own initiative without any request by the person for whom it was waived. Otherwise the text should have made that distinction directly. An official who, by evasion, saves property from an oppressive customs tax keeps the profit for himself, and the risk likewise rests upon him. See Derashot haRashba, siman 111; Bnei Aharon, siman 43; Responsa Maharashdam, siman 21; Responsa Mahara Sasson, siman 21; Responsa Mahari Halevi, siman 23; Responsa Raanach, volume 1, siman 122; Responsa Mabit, volume 1, siman 73, second collection siman 90, and volume 1, siman 149.
