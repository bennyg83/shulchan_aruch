The language of Tur, from the Tosefta in Ketubot, cited by Rif and Rosh in the final chapter of HaGozel, according to Rosh's explanation there in the name of Rabbenu Simcha. Rosh writes that Rabbenu Kalonymus, his ancestor, acted accordingly. Mordekhai in the final chapter of HaGozel and Hagahot Maimoniyot in Hilkhot Shutafin chapter 5 cite Rabbenu Simcha and explain that in the first case, where they waived, the waiver benefits all partners because one partner does not divide without the other's knowledge and partners ordinarily exert themselves for one another.
