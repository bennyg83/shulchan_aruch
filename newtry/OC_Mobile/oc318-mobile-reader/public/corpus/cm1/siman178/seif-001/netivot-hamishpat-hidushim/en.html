It belongs to the partnership. This is so even if the tax collector expressly says, "I waive it for so and so." Sema. See Beurim: this applies specifically because the body of the merchandise upon which the tax falls belongs jointly to the partners, and responsibility for conducting that merchandise rests upon both. Therefore when one partner requests relief, he is effectively acting as agent for all, and the waiver belongs to the partnership. With an ordinary debt that both partners owe, however, if the creditor waives one partner's share even as a result of that partner's efforts, it belongs to that partner alone, because after separation they have no remaining joint property in that debt.
