On the matter of judges, through “and he concludes with proof from document authentication,” etc. According to the Tur’s ruling in siman 62 that a claim requires proof through testimony, the authentication itself makes no practical difference; see there. The Tur further writes that judges who do not recognize the signatures cannot proceed because receiving testimony requires a proper sitting. This is also implied by the Ran in the name of the Ramah concerning three who sat and one died. The Ran says that if all three had signed, we would not suspect that they had not sat together. This implies that if we know they did not sit together, the authentication is invalid.
