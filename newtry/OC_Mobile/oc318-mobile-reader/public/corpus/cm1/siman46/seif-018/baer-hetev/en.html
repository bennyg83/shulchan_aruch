To the judges. The Sema explains that testimony must be capable of being refuted by witnesses of alibi, and these judges cannot accept such refutation concerning their own relatives. The Shach writes that the Ran rejects this reason. Rather, it is a Scriptural decree derived from 'Fathers shall not be put to death because of children,' for whenever the matter is determined through relatives, whether by two witnesses or by a judge together with one witness, the cases are equivalent and they are disqualified. He objects to the Rashba, whose view is the lenient opinion cited by the Mechaber. See there.
