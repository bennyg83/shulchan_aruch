(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 5) And the witnesses were not known at all. Note: The Beit Shmuel there brings an additional proof from Rambam, Hilchot Gerushin 12:3, and from Tur and Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer 152:10, yet there is no indication in those sources that the witnesses were entirely unknown.
