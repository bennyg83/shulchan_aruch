Two witnesses to each signature. Rabbi Akiva Eiger records in his glosses, in the name of Maharikash: A document bears the signatures of three witnesses. One person testifies to the signatures of two of them, and another person testifies to the signature of the third witness and also to the signature of one of the first two. Although according to each testifier the document contains two authenticated witnesses, this authentication is ineffective. See Radbaz, responsa 50 and 55.
