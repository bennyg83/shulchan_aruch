He cannot testify. Meaning that he cannot authenticate by writing, "This is my handwriting," or by nodding his head. The case assumes his present handwriting has changed so that comparison cannot establish his signature. If his present handwriting can still be compared with the original signature, the deed may certainly be authenticated through comparison. He is no worse than a robber or apostate whose signature can be identified. Sema and Shach intended this.
