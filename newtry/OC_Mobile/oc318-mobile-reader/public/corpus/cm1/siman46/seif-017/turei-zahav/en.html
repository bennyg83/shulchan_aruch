(Further there: Three who sat to authenticate a document, etc.) Through “after they signed they may no longer testify,” etc. See the Ran for the reason, as I wrote in siman 39; that explanation is primary. The Shiltei Gibborim, citing Riaz, writes that according to the ruling rejecting Rav Pappi in the approach of Rabbenu Chananel and the Rif, they may testify even after signing, because we are not concerned that the record appear false. The chapter HaKotev implies that even with signatures there is no such concern. According to that approach, Tosafot’s contradiction between Rav’s statement here and the absence of concern in Kol HaGet must be answered by saying that the Gemara’s conclusion there rejects Rav, though Rav himself distinguished writing from signing. The Ritva likewise records such a distinction, consistent with the Rambam.
