(Anthology) Although, etc. This follows from the rule that one witness and one judge may combine, as Tosafot write there. The Jerusalem Talmud at the end of the first chapter of Sanhedrin and elsewhere likewise asks whether the certification of judges requires a full beit din. Rav Hoshaya said in the name of Shmuel that it may be authenticated either through the handwriting of the witnesses or through the handwriting of the judges, etc.
