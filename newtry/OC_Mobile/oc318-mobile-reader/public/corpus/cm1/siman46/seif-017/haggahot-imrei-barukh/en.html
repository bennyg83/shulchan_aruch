(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 26) In the passage beginning "according to this, regarding a condition," etc. Note: One may say that a condition is different. Since the condition can be written within the loan document itself, and this is proper so that the lender not hold an unconditional document when the obligation is actually conditional, the witnesses are not authorized to write the condition in a separate instrument. Such a writing was not given for that purpose.
