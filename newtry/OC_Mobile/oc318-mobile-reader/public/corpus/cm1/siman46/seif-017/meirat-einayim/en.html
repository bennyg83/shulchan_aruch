To join with another. Meaning, with one who saw the signatures after reaching adulthood, even if that person too is related to the witnesses on the document. This is proven by the Mechaber's opening statement that the authentication witnesses may be relatives of the document witnesses, which implies that both may be related. He requires joining with a 'valid' person only where one of them saw the signatures while a minor. Ran writes likewise. The two authentication witnesses must, however, be valid relative to each other. Perhaps this is why the Mechaber wrote, 'with another valid person,' but if so he should have stated this also in the opening clause. Moreover, Rama writes this law explicitly nearby in seif 18, that they may not be related to each other. Why state it twice? See Perishah.
Specifically those whom he was accustomed, etc. Meaning, this is why he is believed to say that he recognizes their signatures from his childhood. He is not believed regarding other signatures first seen while a minor. But if he saw the signatures after reaching adulthood, he may testify regarding anyone's signatures.
