Witnesses who authenticate signatures may be related to the signatories. Moreover, if a signatory father died while his son was a minor, the son may, after reaching adulthood, identify his father's signature together with another fit witness. A person may likewise testify to the handwriting of his brother or teacher that he saw while a minor. Some say this applies only to those with whom he was regularly familiar; he is not believed as an adult to identify another person's handwriting solely from recognition acquired in childhood.
