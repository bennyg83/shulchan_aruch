Urim VeTumim asks that according to this, the Gemara's case of one who says a document is an accommodation should have been established where the borrower speaks and the deed contains credibility, rather than resorting to another explanation. But where the Gemara speaks of the lender himself saying it is an accommodation and thereby harming others, the case need not contain any credibility clause if the borrower has not said, 'Swear to me.' As Shach explains in siman 47, the lender can collect without an oath unless that demand is made. Therefore no proof follows from the Gemara's chosen case.
