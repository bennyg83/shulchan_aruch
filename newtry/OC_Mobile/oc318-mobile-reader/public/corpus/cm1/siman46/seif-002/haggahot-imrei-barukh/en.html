(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 1) At the end of the passage: no presumption applies, etc. Note: See Shitah Mekubetzet to Ketubot 19, citing the Ramban on Tosafot's question that he should be believed by a migo, since he could have waived the debt. The Ramban answers that this is a migo against the presumption that whatever is in a person's possession belongs to him. The same appears in a responsum of the Rashba cited by the Shach in siman 47 and siman 99, seif katan 6. The Shach questions this reasoning there because the document itself is not money, and he similarly rejects it in siman 47, seif katan 6. See also Tosafot, Yevamot 116a, passage beginning "letters are acquired."
