To retract. The Shach writes that this requires study, for we rule in siman 51 that one witness in writing obligates the defendant to take an oath. If so, it is comparable to oral testimony, and in oral testimony a witness cannot retract and testify differently, as stated at the beginning of siman 29. See further in the Shach what he cites from a responsum of the Rama on this matter, which he disputes and leaves requiring study.
