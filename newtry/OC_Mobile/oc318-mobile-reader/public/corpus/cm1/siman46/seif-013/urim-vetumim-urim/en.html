Even on a shard, etc. Ordinarily one should not write on the back of a legal deed because it is an erasable or forgeable surface. Nevertheless, for the limited purpose of handwriting comparison, a signature written there may be used. All the more may the witness sign on a blank sheet, but he should be careful to sign at the very top, lest someone later find the blank sheet and write a fraudulent obligation above the signature.
