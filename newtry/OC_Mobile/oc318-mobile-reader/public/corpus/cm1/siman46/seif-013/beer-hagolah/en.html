There, from Rav Yehudah in the name of Rav in Ketubot.
Miyamara Dhabi there
The Kesef Mishneh writes that the Rambam means in the presence of witnesses, and his reasoning is correct. Otherwise, how would they recognize that this is his signature so as to validate a document from it?
The Sema writes that all the more so this applies on the top of a scroll, meaning the beginning of a sheet, where there is no concern that an unscrupulous person will write a promissory note above the signature. Anything written after his signature is ineffective because it was written afterward, in accordance with Abaye's statement, Bava Batra 167a.
