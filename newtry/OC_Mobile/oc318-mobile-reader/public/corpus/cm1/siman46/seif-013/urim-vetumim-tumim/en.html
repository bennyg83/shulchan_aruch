Even on a shard, etc. Shach, seif katan 37, notes that Tosafot say Abaye's advice to sign on a shard follows R. Meir, who holds signing witnesses create the document. Since we rule like R. Elazar that delivery witnesses create the document, Shach asks what protection a shard provides. According to what I explained above in siman 42, there is no difficulty. With gittin, Abaye can agree that a document on alterable material can be valid through delivery witnesses. But regarding loan documents he follows the stricter rule associated with R. Meir that the writing itself must be on material that cannot be altered. The concern in Abaye's advice is precisely that someone will find the signature and write a loan document above it. Since a loan written on a shard or other alterable material is invalid under our law, the shard prevents that fraud. Thus the advice remains fully effective.
