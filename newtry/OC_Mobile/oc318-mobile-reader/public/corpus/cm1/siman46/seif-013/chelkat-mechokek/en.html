The first man should not marry her, lest people say, etc. If the first man did marry her, the wording at first glance implies that he need not divorce her, and that the restriction is only initial because of what people may say. It is possible, however, that even after the fact he must divorce her because of the same concern. See above, siman 31, where the Ramah rules that even though we remove her from the second man in a case of kiddushin worth less than a perutah, the child is nevertheless not a mamzer.
