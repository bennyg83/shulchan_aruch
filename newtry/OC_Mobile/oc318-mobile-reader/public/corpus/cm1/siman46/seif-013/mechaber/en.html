If two witnesses signed a document and one died, two independent witnesses must identify the deceased witness's signature. The surviving signatory cannot join one other witness to identify it, because then three quarters of the money would be established through him. If only one other witness is available, the surviving signatory writes his signature before witnesses, even on a potsherd, and casts it before the beit din until his handwriting is established there. {Rama: This certainly applies if witnesses are available to authenticate the living witness's signature.} Since he no longer needs to identify his own signature, he may then join the other witness in identifying the deceased witness's signature.
