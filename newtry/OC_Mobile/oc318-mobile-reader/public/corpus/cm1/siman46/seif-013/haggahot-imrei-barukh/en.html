(Shach, seif katan 102) And according to the Rosh we must say. Note: Be aware that nowhere in the Rosh's discussion here does he say that the witnesses were disqualified for robbery. It is possible that the Rosh, like Tosafot in Bava Kamma, interpreted the case as disqualification through kinship. If so, Tosafot's statement in Ketubot that the case does not concern witnesses who say they are still disqualified refers to disqualification through kinship. There is a distinction between saying they were relatives but are now qualified and saying they remain relatives. On that point the Rosh disagrees.
