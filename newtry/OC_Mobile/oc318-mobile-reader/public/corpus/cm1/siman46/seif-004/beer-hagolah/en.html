The Rambam is there
Explained by what was written above, siman 5 seif 2, that if the borrower comes together with the lender to validate the document, it may be validated.
Hagahot Mordechai to Kiddushin writes that if the witnesses say, 'We signed this document,' or if the witnesses are no longer alive and a litigant says the document is invalid, it requires validation even by Torah law. Sema.
