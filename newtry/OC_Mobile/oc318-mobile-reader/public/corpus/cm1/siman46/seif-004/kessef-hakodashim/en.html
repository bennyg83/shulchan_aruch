Seif 37. Within the time needed for speech. Tosafot at the beginning of Nazir, 20b, writes that the measure of toch kedei dibbur is the time needed to say the three words of a student's greeting to his teacher, together with an additional interval sufficient for the teacher to respond to the student. I have not presently seen this formulation cited by the halachic authorities. Perhaps it applies only in the law of nazir for the reason stated by Tosafot there, whereas elsewhere the Sages did not define the measure in this manner. This may explain why the authorities omit it. The matter requires careful study.
