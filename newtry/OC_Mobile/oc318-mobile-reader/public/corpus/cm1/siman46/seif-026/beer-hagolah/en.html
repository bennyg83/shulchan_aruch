According to the explanation of Rabbenu Tam and the Rif, and likewise Tosafot there in the name of Rabbenu Chananel: since the later witnesses admit to the earlier ones that he had been a robber, their challenge is a valid challenge and his prior standing is nullified. Since it is true that he had been disqualified, he remained presumed disqualified until we learned of his rehabilitation. Otherwise, we would say that there are two against two and leave the person in his original chazakah, as Tosafot write there in the name of Rabbenu Chananel.
