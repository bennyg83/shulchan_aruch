(Collected note) And some say that even from a book, etc. This follows from what is stated there: one should write his signature on a shard, etc. If so, he could instead write alef, bet, gimel. Mordechai, siman 200; see there.
(Collected note) And some say that even from a book, etc. Otherwise, the Gemara there concerning a shard should have said that he should write alef, bet, or the like. This also resolves what was asked against Rashi: if the witness is a scribe and has a book of his own, would it be impossible to authenticate his signature? See the Rosh and Mordechai.
