I do not accept this reasoning in that broad form. If the judges did not personally recognize the deed witnesses' handwriting and relied on oral testimony, then according to authorities who say the repentant person is not retroactively restored until his repentance is established before beit din, the original reception of testimony itself took place before an invalid panel and must be repeated. This depends on the dispute above, seif 25, whether valid joinder is required at the stage of receiving authentication testimony. According to Rosh, fresh reception is indeed required. But according to those who say the repentant person was actually fit retroactively and only the formal joinder is defective, then under Rosh's view that the testimony-receiving stage itself need not have full joinder, renewed testimony is unnecessary.
