The Sema writes that this applies only where the document was newly authenticated by three valid judges, or where witnesses are presently available through whom it can be authenticated. The novelty is that the document itself is not invalidated by the signature of a disqualified person on the authentication. The Shach adds that the document is not invalid even if the defective authentication was not cut away. Nevertheless, initially one should correct the matter and cut it off. If that cannot be done, such as where the authentication is written on the reverse side of the document, ink lines should be drawn through the authentication, for wherever the matter can be corrected, we correct it.
