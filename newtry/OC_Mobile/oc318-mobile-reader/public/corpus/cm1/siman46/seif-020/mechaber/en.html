Ran, there. Judges who sign an authentication of a document may sign even if they have not read the document. Specifically, they need not read the substantive terms of the document. They must nevertheless see who the lender and borrower are, so that the judges will not be relatives of them or of the witnesses testifying before them. Some say they need not read the document at all. Provided that they record who the witnesses to the authentication were, so that if those witnesses are later found disqualified by relationship to the lender or borrower this can be determined.
