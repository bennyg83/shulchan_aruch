We do not suspect that the beit din authenticated the scratches. This concern applies only to ordinary witnesses, as the Tur and Mechaber explain in siman 45:6. With judges who authenticate the document, we do not suspect that the holder had some adverse matter written there and relied upon the judges' signatures, because people do not ordinarily write a detriment to the document holder after the document witnesses' signatures. See Perishah.
