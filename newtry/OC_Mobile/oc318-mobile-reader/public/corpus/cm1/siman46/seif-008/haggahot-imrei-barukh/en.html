(Shach, seif katan 10) For the reason written by the Rashba in a responsum, etc. Note: See also his novellae to Bava Batra 112b. Shitah Mekubetzet there cites the Ramah that where the witnesses themselves testify to their handwriting, and we say that they are testifying to the money stated in the document, this must be done before a beit din.
