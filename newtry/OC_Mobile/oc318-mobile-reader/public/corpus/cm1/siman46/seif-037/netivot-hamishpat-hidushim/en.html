Although siman 29 states that a witness may not later add a condition to oral testimony, the Sema distinguishes between oral testimony, where such an addition is barred, and a written document, where they may explain that there was a condition. See Beurim. With a condition relating to the past, even the Sema agrees that they are not believed. The Shach concludes that even regarding a document they are not believed to add a condition. This restriction applies to monetary documents, but regarding a get they are believed to say there was a condition. It further appears that if the borrower instructed them to write the condition, whether in a separate document or in the document itself, they are believed to state the condition even according to the Shach, including a condition concerning the past. Likewise, if the condition was made at delivery after the signing, all agree that they are believed to report it. Other witnesses are believed according to all opinions to say that there was a condition even before signing, provided they say that the condition was made outside the presence of the document witnesses and those witnesses do not contradict them. If they say the condition was made before the document witnesses, the result is two against two even when the document witnesses are not presently before us.
