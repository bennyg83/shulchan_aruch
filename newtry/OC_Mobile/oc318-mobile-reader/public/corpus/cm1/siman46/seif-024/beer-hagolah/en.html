The language of the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot Edut, from Rav Huna in the name of Rav, etc., Ketubot 21b.
The Kesef Mishneh writes that although reception of testimony ordinarily requires three judges, since document validation is rabbinic, even while they testify they may become judges. The Rashba writes in siman 969 that a witness signed on a document may serve as a judge for validation of the second witness who signed with him.

There in Tosafot in the name of some commentators, that regarding other documents this concern does not apply, but with an ashrata, which is an act of beit din, all agree we are concerned. So write the Rosh and Tur in seif 21.
