They testify before him. In authentication of documents, a witness may also serve as a judge. Thus the testimony given before the third member is treated as testimony before a beit din even though that person is sitting alone, because the other two witnesses themselves can join as judges. The same applies where a witness signed on the document serves as a judge to receive authentication testimony concerning the second witness.
