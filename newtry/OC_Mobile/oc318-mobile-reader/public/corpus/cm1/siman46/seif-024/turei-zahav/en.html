(See there: The Rambam wrote.) Witnesses who said, etc., through “the same applies if another witness joins.” This is incorrect. If joining another witness helped, then one witness who testifies to his own signature and his colleague’s signature would also suffice, contrary to seif 14. Under the Rambam’s view the document is like a shard and cannot be restored through the addition of one witness. Maharshal writes likewise in responsum 80.
