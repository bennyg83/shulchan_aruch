y) Meaning, in the manner that the Rambam continues to explain at the end of the seif. The Kesef Mishneh there, at the beginning of chapter 8, writes: There is no doubt that when Rabbeinu says "they are like deaf-mutes," this applies only so long as there are no other witnesses. If there is one other witness who testifies regarding both signatures, that certainly suffices, as is implied by the Yerushalmi. This resolves the Raavad's objection. Accordingly, there is no dispute here; all agree that where there is one additional witness who testifies to the handwriting of both, it is effective.
