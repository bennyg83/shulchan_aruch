They are believed. See Baer Heitev, through his statement that they are believed even if their explanation of their earlier words is strained. See Responsa Rabbi Moshe Rothenburg, siman 8, who writes that this applies specifically to witnesses. The Torah believed witnesses so long as they do not retract what they first testified. Therefore, if they can explain their first words in a way that does not contradict their original testimony, their explanation has the status of full testimony. Litigants themselves, however, certainly cannot explain an admission they made through remote and strained interpretations. See there.
