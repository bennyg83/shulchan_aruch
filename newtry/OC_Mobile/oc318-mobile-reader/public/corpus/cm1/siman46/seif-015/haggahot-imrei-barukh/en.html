(Sema, seif katan 104) This requires study, for the Mechaber ruled, etc. Note: Here the witnesses say that the condition was that the lender perform a certain act, and if he failed to do so no liability ever began for the borrower. In siman 82, by contrast, the debt was unquestionably created, but there was a condition that if the borrower did what he undertook to do, fulfillment of that condition would count as payment. There is no difference between witnesses testifying directly that payment occurred and their testifying to a condition that functions as payment. This is therefore not considered retracting and retestifying. Here, however, the liability recorded in the document was suspended until the lender fulfilled his condition, and without that fulfillment there was no debt. I later found that the Tumim makes a similar distinction, though he does not explain its basis as clearly as stated here.
