Authenticated. The Tur writes: We do not say that they are testifying about their own handwriting, which would require each one also to recognize his fellow's signature. Rather, each one testifies to the authentication of the document as a valid document. Regarding the Mechaber's wording in the preceding seif, 'or a judge and another with him,' the Sema explains that this is stated as an even greater case: even though another person joins him in testimony, it does not suffice. The Taz explains the Mechaber differently; see there.
