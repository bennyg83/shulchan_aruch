Even when none of the three judges died, the practice is to write, 'One is no longer present,' so that only two judges need sign the authentication. {Rama: If the authentication states, 'We sat as a panel of three,' and three judges signed, but one is found related or disqualified, some say it remains valid. We assume three other judges sat and only two of them signed, after which the related or disqualified person added his signature (Rivash, siman 413), as explained above in siman 45, seif 12, regarding other documents. Others say it is invalid (Rosh, klal 60, siman 4). An authentication written in the form, 'We, the witnesses,' is valid when three signed it, because their three signatures demonstrate that they acted as a beit din (Rivash, siman 382).}
