Some say it is invalid. If four judges signed a validation and one is a relative, the validation need not be void automatically. Even according to the view that a disqualified judge can nullify the entire court, there is strong reason to preserve this validation. If the certification says, "We were sitting as three together," the fourth signature can plainly be treated as added for honor. Even without such wording, where four signatures appear and we do not know that all four actually sat as the court, we presume a valid arrangement rather than assume that the disqualified fourth signer participated as a judge. This follows the general rule that where more than the required number sign, an extra signature can be attributed to honor unless the circumstances show otherwise.
