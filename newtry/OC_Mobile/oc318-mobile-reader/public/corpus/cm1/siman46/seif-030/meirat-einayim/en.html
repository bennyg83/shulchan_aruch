In order to lighten their burden, etc. Meaning, if the three judges were initially together and received the testimony as three, and afterward one left and only two signed, they need not seek out a third before writing the certification and obtaining his signature. Rather, they write the certification and state, 'and one is no longer present.' The same applies if they write, 'two out of three,' according to Beit Yosef. The signatures of the two suffice because the testimony was received before three.
If they wrote in the henpek. Meaning, in the beit din certification. They customarily wrote, 'this document was produced before us,' and because of that wording the certification is called a henpek. See below siman 65, seif 7.
And some say it is invalid. Meaning, the authentication is invalid, but the original document itself remains valid.
If they wrote, 'we are witnesses,' it is valid. Rivash, siman 382, explains that we can say that they themselves recognized the signatures and are therefore functioning as witnesses and judges. This too is included in the rule that hearing is no greater than seeing, as I wrote above in Sema seif katan 13, unlike Eir Shushan there and here.
