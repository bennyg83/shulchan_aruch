(Seif 16: Two outsiders testify concerning the judge, etc.) The Beit Yosef’s text reads “concerning the judge and the witness.” The Sema correctly says this is a scribal error. If they testify only concerning one witness, their testimony is no stronger than if the judge himself testified to his signature, which would still be only one witness. Those who identify the judge cannot be stronger than the judge himself. Rather, the clause continues the opening case, where one witness testifies concerning both document signatures. There are then two witnesses regarding each signature. Some explain the invalidity where two testify concerning the judge and the witness on the ground that the testimonies do not combine, just as the original witness’s own testimony to his signature does not combine with a judge. I do not agree. The original witness is different because he is deemed to testify to the debt in the document and therefore cannot combine with the judge. The correct reason is that there are not two testimonies concerning both original witnesses.
