(Shach, seif katan 6) The Sema wrote in seif katan 6, etc. Note: Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Batra 40, in the discussion beginning "authentication of documents requires three," cites the Aliyot of Rabbeinu Yonah that even when the judges themselves recognize the witnesses' signatures, three judges are nevertheless required. One reason is that the Sages made no distinction. A second reason is that testimony must be from their mouths and not from their writing. See there. The second reason does not accord with the Rivash's approach.
