Who heard from so-and-so that a certain woman was betrothed. It is not clarified whether even for this type of kol the report must specify that she was betrothed to a particular man and in this city, or whether, since the report ultimately rests upon two persons who are no longer present, just as the precise day need not be stated according to the Ran at the end of Gittin, perhaps the man's identity and the city are likewise unnecessary.
