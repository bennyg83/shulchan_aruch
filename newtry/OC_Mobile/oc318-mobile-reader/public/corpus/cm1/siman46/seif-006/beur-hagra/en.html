(Anthology) And some say, etc. His statement is not equivalent to witnesses. A document of trust is like payment accompanied by a clause of credibility, regarding which even witnesses are not believed. This applies even when their handwriting is not independently authenticated and they would otherwise be believed, as Sefer HaTerumot writes in seif 1. See siman 82.
