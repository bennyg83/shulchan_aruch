(Shach, seif katan 9) And so the Rashba wrote in his novellae to the first chapter of Gittin, etc. Note: This is also evident from the Rashba's novellae to Kiddushin 65a, passage beginning "Rav said." See Lechem Mishneh, Hilchot Gerushin 7:2, who raises the same difficulty as the Shach against the Rambam, whose words there accord with Rabbeinu Avigdor. See also Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer 141, seif katan 70, who proves from that Rambam and from Hilchot Gerushin 12:3, as well as from Tur and Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer 152:14, that they follow the Rosh cited in Hagahot Mordechai.
