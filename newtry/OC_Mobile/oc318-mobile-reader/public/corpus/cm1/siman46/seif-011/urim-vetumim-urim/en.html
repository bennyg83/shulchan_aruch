It has not been validated, etc. Even if the signing witness says, "I will not testify regarding the money in the document, only regarding my handwriting," and another witness joins him, so that only half the money would ultimately be established through his testimony, nevertheless according to the Sages he necessarily testifies regarding the money in the document, and this device does not help. So write Tosafos and Rosh. See Tumim. See also Tumim regarding a signing witness who no longer remembers the substance of the document. According to the dissenting opinion cited by the Mechaber, which requires another witness to join him, he may together with one additional witness testify regarding the handwriting of the second signing witness, because only half the money is then established through his own testimony. This is implied by Bach and Perishah. See there.
