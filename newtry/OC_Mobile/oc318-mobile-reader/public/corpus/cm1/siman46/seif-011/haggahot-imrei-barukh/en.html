(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 7) It follows that something dependent upon visual recognition, etc. Note: From there one can infer only that where formal testimony is unnecessary because the matter is visible and known to everyone, contradiction by two witnesses does not apply. Here, however, authentication requires the taking of testimony. What difference is there between two and four? Once they have the status of witnesses, they are no stronger than two witnesses who contradict them, for two witnesses are like one hundred. This is also indicated by the language of Maharik, shoresh 171, cited by the Shach later in this siman, seif katan 67. His essential reasoning is that where testimony is unnecessary, the matter is merely a disclosure of fact known to all, and two contradicting witnesses do not displace that.
