Who validates. The Taz writes that it appears we do not rule this way, for in seif 20 the Rama rules that such a case is invalid, aside from an additional difficulty that may be raised against this opinion.
And the judges. The Shach writes that the Rama's words imply that even one who validates relatives as witnesses to authentication would disqualify relatives as judges of authentication. The Ir Shushan says this explicitly. Their words are astonishing, for it is obvious that one who validates relatives as witnesses to authentication would likewise validate them as judges of authentication; there is no reason to distinguish between them. The words of the Rama and Ir Shushan require study.
