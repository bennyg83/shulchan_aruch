e) There in the Mordechai, from the Mishnah, "A person is believed to say, this is my father's handwriting." Rabbeinu Shimshon rejects the proof because there the person is related neither to this party nor to the other. So too writes the Rashba in a responsum as halachic analysis but not for practical ruling, based on the statement that judges may sign a validation even though they did not read the document. If the rule were otherwise, we should be concerned that the witnesses to the validation are relatives of the lender or borrower. Those who invalidate reject this proof, as explained in the following seif, from siman 17.
