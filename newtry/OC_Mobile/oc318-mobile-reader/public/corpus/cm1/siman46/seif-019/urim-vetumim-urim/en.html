The witnesses to the validation, etc. Their validation creates liability for both lender and borrower. The opinion that validates is Rashba, who holds that the Sages were lenient in document validation and that there is no true creation of liability against their relative, because the document already has a presumptive status of validity and the validation is merely clarification of an existing fact.
