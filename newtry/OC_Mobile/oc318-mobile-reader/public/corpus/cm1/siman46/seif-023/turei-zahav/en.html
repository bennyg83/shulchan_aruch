(See there: The Rashba was asked, etc.) Through “he entered his vineyard lawfully.” This is not comparable to Bava Metzia and Tur, siman 317, where absent disclosure the land remains in its owner’s possession even though the tenant entered lawfully and consumed produce. There the tenant is not considered the possessor because he must ultimately return the land, and the landlord is therefore treated as established in possession, in accordance with Rabbenu Yonah and unlike Tosafot, the Rosh, and Ramban concerning an orchard. The Baal Divrei Rivot distinguishes that there the landlord never admits the tenant’s asserted term, whereas here the seller admits at least that he sold the field.
