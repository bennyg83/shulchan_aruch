And likewise if they merely said, "She was betrothed." According to my explanation, this does not refer to the women emerging from a place where candles were burning, but to those who spread the report. If they merely say, "So-and-so was betrothed," without further particulars, this is not a sufficiently definite kol. Likewise, if the report says she was betrothed in another city, it is not a valid kol. The women themselves, however, need not mention either the city or the man's identity. So too writes the Bach.
