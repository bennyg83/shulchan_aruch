[Shulchan Aruch] Because two witnesses are required. Note: If a document bears the signatures of three witnesses, and one person testifies to the signatures of two of them, while another person testifies to the signature of the third and also to the signature of one of the first two, we say that although according to each witness there are two authenticated witnesses on this document, this is ineffective. Radbaz, simanim 50 and 55, cited by Maharikash.
