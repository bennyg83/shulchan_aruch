Two witnesses regarding each signature. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: If three witnesses are signed on a document, and one person testifies to the signatures of two of them, while another testifies to the signature of the third witness and also to the signature of one of the first two, then although according to each testifier there are two authenticated witnesses on the document, this is of no effect. Radbaz, simanim 50 and 55. End of the words of Maharikash.
