The witness testifies regarding the money in the document. Ordinarily he is regarded as testifying regarding the money stated in the document. Therefore, even if one additional witness testifies regarding the handwriting of this signing witness, it still does not help, as explained above. See what I wrote in Tumim in seif 16.
And the judge, etc. See Tumim, where I established that this does not even have the status of an oral loan, unlike what appears from Responsa Maharit. See there.
Likewise if it was validated, etc. Sema writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yeruham and Darchei Moshe that nevertheless it may be collected from unencumbered property. Shach objects to this ruling and explains that the case is one in which the scribe testifies orally to the loan itself, so that there is one witness in the document and one oral witness. Rama himself already preceded this explanation in Responsa, siman 110, where he states it explicitly.
