If neither the signatures of the witnesses nor the signatures of the judges who authenticated the document are recognized, and one of the witnesses to the document testifies to his own handwriting while two witnesses testify to the signature of one of the judges, or that judge himself and one other testify to his handwriting, this does not suffice. What one testifies to is not what the other testifies to. The witness testifies to the one hundred stated in the document, whereas the judge testifies to the witnesses' signatures. Likewise, if the scribe's handwriting and that of one witness are authenticated, this is not an authentication of the document.
