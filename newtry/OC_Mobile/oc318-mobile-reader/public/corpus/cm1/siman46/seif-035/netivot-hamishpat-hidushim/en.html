If it emerges from the witnesses' possession. This limitation follows the Rif, who holds that a document still in the witnesses' possession is not treated as testimony already examined in beit din, Sema. See Beurim. Even the Rif agrees that in a deed of acquisition the witnesses need not remember the event, even if the deed is still in their possession. In a document without a kinyan that remains in the witnesses' possession and where they do not remember the event, meaning they do not remember whether the borrower instructed them to deliver it, all agree that they may not give the document to the lender. Likewise, where a witness signed the document and then became the borrower's son in law, there is a distinction. In a deed of acquisition the document remains valid even according to the Rif.
