[Taz, s.v. They validated both, etc.] That is, if the members of the beit din themselves recognize, etc. Note: Nevertheless, where the document is in the borrower's possession and witnesses testify that the borrower acknowledged before them that this is his signature, which the Sema and Taz write is effective, in my humble opinion there is much room to question it. The Rama writes in seif 7 that testimony from writing is accepted because, as the Rivash explains, validation of documents is rabbinic. But regarding a handwritten document, the Sema writes above, siman 35, and the Shach writes here, seif katan 10, that by Torah law validation is required. Accordingly, testimony from their writing should not be effective.
