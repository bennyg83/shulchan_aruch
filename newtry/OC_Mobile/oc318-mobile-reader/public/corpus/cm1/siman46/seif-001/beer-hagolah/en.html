A. A. The column is revealed to him that he is with him, and that he is called, and so on, he is written
The language of the Rambam, chapter 14 of Hilkhot Malveh. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is a dispute among Tannaim and Amoraim in many places, including Get Pashut 154a, and that most commentators explain like the Rambam. He writes that this is also the view of the Ramban, Rashba, Rif, and Maggid Mishneh.
