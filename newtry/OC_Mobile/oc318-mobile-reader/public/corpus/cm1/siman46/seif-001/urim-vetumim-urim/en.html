Or, "I wrote it intending to borrow." An accommodation document means that he never truly intended to borrow; he gave the lender the deed for some external reason but trusted him not to use it. By contrast, "I wrote it intending to borrow" means he genuinely intended to borrow and delivered the deed on that understanding, but the loan was never completed. Another possible plea is: "I wrote it intending to borrow, but the deed fell from me and you found it." See Tumim. The case must involve a deed without kinyan. If a kinyan was made, he cannot claim, "I wrote it to borrow but never borrowed," for the reasons explained there.
