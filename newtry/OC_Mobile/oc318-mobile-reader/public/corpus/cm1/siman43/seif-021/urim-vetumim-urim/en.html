They should write only the name of the place. Even if they write only the date of writing, the deed is not invalid in this arrangement, because they state the place of the kinyan while intentionally using a later date. See Tumim for my resolution of Shach's question. Sema cites Ran that the same is true even if the two locations lie within one another's Shabbat boundary. They are nevertheless treated as distinct places, like Hini and Shili in Beitzah, even though each lies within the other's boundary and their pitchers were carried between them on Yom Tov.
