(Anthology) That the currency, etc. As stated in Ketubot 110b, we assume that he lent in the currency of that place, as stated in siman 42, seif 14. This is certainly so where the document records a self-imposed obligation, for he became obligated in the currency of that place, as the Gemara says there regarding one who married a woman. This differs from the special leniencies of a marriage contract.
