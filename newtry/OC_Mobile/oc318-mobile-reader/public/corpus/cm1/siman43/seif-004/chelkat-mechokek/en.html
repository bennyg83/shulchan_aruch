He did not write her a ketubah while he was a minor. The plain language of Rambam and Tur does not support the gloss's limitation. Rambam simply writes that she has the statutory principal of a ketubah, which implies that he is not speaking of a ketubah written while he was a minor. By the ordinary beit din condition she receives two hundred once he keeps her after majority, because he maintains her as though the marriage begins anew at that point. Certainly, before he has secluded himself with her after attaining majority, he can say, "I will keep you only on condition that you receive one hundred, as though I were marrying you today." But after ordinary marital relations he must pay her the amount due to an ordinary virgin. This is likewise clear from the Tur below in siman 67, who writes that even the principal is not collected by virtue of the written ketubah but by the beit din condition. Thus, in my opinion, whether a ketubah was written while he was a minor should not matter, and the Hagahot HaRif ruling that without such a document she receives only one hundred requires study.
