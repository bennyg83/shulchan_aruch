If he wrote on Wednesday, the twentieth day. Responsa Rosh teaches a novelty although a document with no weekday at all could be valid. The case is where we know the act actually occurred on the twenty-first, but that Wednesday was the twenty-first. The error is evident from the document itself because the weekday and date conflict. One might have thought that omission of the weekday would have avoided the problem, but once both are written the contradiction could invalidate it. The ruling teaches that where the error is evident as a simple dating mistake, the instrument can remain valid.
