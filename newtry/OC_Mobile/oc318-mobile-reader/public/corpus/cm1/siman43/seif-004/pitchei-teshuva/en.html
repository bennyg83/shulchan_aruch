The postponement explicitly. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: With a deed of acquisition in this form, according to Tosafos in the second chapter of Gittin, which is the first opinion in Shulchan Aruch Even HaEzer, siman 127, seif 9, the buyer does not acquire the land until the date written in the document. Even if the grantor wanted the acquisition to take effect immediately, this document does not effect an immediate acquisition. The Ran in Gittin there writes this explicitly. According to the Raavad and those who agree with him, however, the acquisition takes effect immediately. End of quote.
