A handwritten obligation does not require, and so forth. The reason is that it cannot be collected from purchasers, as the Sema states. Likewise, if the borrower has other notes against him, this handwritten obligation has no priority, because those creditors are comparable to purchasers. Otherwise, a date would be required, as explained above. We therefore fear collusion, namely that the borrower will insert an earlier date. This is also stated in the Rashba's responsum cited by the Beit Yosef at the end of siman 47. See what I wrote in siman 48.
