His handwriting. Gittin 86a. The Mishnah says, 'he wrote it in his own handwriting,' etc. Since it states regarding a document with witnesses that it has no date, but does not state this regarding one written in his own handwriting, the distinction is implied.
(Anthology) His handwriting, etc. See Tosafot, Yevamot 32b, s.v. katav, where Ri explains that he wrote it in his own handwriting, etc. The Rosh writes likewise there. This is the reason the Jerusalem Talmud states that these three bills of divorce collect from unencumbered property, all because of the date.
