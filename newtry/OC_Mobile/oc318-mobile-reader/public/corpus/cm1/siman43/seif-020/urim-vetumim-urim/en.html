Sema cites many authorities who say the deed should record both facts: “The matter was delivered to us in such and such place, and we wrote in such and such place.” It appears that everyone agrees the deed is valid after the fact even if only one place is written, because the baraita in the first chapter of capital cases clearly indicates that the discrepancy does not invalidate after the fact.
