(Anthology) They are lying, and it is invalid. Meaning, although a document that merely appears false is valid after the fact, as stated in the gloss to seif 12, here there is actual falsehood and it is invalid, as stated in the first chapter of Gittin. Tosafot there and Rashi in Sanhedrin 32a, however, hold that such a document is valid, based on the passage in Sanhedrin. Those who invalidate it explain the passage as Tosafot do there. See Tosafot, Ketubot 21b, s.v. ha'amar, and Ri, etc.
