The late ones are kosher. And he does not bother only from the late time, as if he is sickened by the same day:
Even the later dated documents are invalid. The reason is that perhaps the seller did not know that they postdated it, and he may later buy the property back from the purchaser. Then the holder of the later dated document could produce it and say, "I subsequently repurchased it from you." Accordingly, if they informed the seller that they postdated it, it is valid. Shach.
