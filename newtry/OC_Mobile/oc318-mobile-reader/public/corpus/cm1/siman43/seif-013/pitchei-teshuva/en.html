The delay is stated explicitly. Rabbi Akiva Eiger writes in his glosses to Shulchan Aruch: In a deed of acquisition of this type, according to Tosafot in the second chapter of Gittin, which is the first opinion in Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer, siman 127, seif 9, the recipient does not acquire the land until the date written in the deed. Even if the grantor intended to transfer it immediately, this deed does not effect acquisition immediately. The Ran in Gittin states this explicitly. According to the Raavad and those who follow him, however, the recipient acquires immediately.
