(Anthology) They write in a document, etc. The Mishnah in the third chapter of Gittin requires the place and date. The Tosefta at the end of Bava Batra states: the body of a document should state, on such-and-such day of the week, such-and-such day of the month, such-and-such year, in the reign of such-and-such sovereign. If the day is not written, the holder has the weaker position throughout that week. If the day of the week is not written, the holder has the weaker position throughout that month. If the month is not written, the holder has the weaker position throughout that year. If the year is not written, the holder has the weaker position throughout that reign.
