(Shach, seif katan 2) From what we say in the first chapter of Bava Metzia, etc. Note: He overlooked the Rashba's novellae there, in the passage beginning "how is this different from a debt document," cited in Shitah Mekubetzet. It also requires study according to the Shach's position that she collects only from the time the witnesses later saw the get in her possession. If so, there is no need to disqualify it entirely as an antedated document. Before the get was lost, when no defect had arisen, had she sought to collect she could have collected from the date written in it, and purchasers could not have required her to bring further proof, according to Tosafot and the authorities who disagree with Rashi. According to Rashi, the written date provides no such advantage because even without any defect she must bring proof. See Tumim.
