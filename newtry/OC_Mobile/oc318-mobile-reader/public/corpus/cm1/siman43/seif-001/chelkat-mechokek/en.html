It is nothing, for they did not institute, etc. Meaning that she does not require a get from him, and the laws of yibbum and chalitzah do not arise through this union. The Bach writes above in siman 1 that if a father marries off his twelve-year-old son, the kiddushin have rabbinic force. I know of no textual basis or proof for this novel rule. One who innovates such a law must bring clear evidence.
