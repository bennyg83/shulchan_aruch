Seif 29. Until after Pesach. If one simply says "Rosh Chodesh," he cannot demand payment until the second day of Rosh Chodesh. If the first day falls on Shabbat, where the borrower did not know this circumstance there is no proof that he intended the first day. The same applies where the borrower did not know. Even if both knew, it may still be understood as referring to the second day, and payment can therefore wait until after Shabbat.
