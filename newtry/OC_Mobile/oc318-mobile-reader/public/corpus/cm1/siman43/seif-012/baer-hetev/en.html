After acquired property. The Sema writes that the source of the law concerning inclusion of an after acquired property clause is explained later in simanim 104 and 112. There it is explained that we rule that even such a clause is presumed to have been omitted by scribal error, unless the document expressly contains other liability provisions but omits liability for after acquired property.
