But if it does not contain an after-acquired-property clause, the witnesses are disqualified, etc. The Tur explains the reason. Perhaps the loan occurred in Nisan, the borrower acquired land in Iyar, and the witnesses postdated the loan document to Sivan. Had they written the true date in Nisan without an after-acquired-property clause, the creditor could not have collected the land purchased in Iyar. Because they wrote the later Sivan date, the document would make it appear that the land already existed before the written loan date and could therefore be collected. The laws of an after-acquired-property clause are explained below in simanim 104 and 112. There we rule that such responsibility can itself be treated as a scribal omission unless the document expressly contains ordinary responsibility but omits after-acquired-property responsibility.
