Postdated deeds are valid. The Beit Yosef cites the Baal HaTerumot for the proposition that one who presents a postdated deed cannot collect until its date arrives. This is explicit Gemara at the beginning of Avodah Zarah: the Sages assumed that the deed was postdated and that collection could not be made until its date. The Beit Yosef himself cites that passage at the beginning of this siman.
But if it does not state that after acquired property is encumbered, they are invalid. According to the opinion cited at the beginning of siman 112 that omission of an after acquired property clause is supplied by scribal presumption, such deeds are valid here even without an express clause, unless the deed expressly excludes after acquired property. See my discussion there, where I defend that opinion against the objections of the Maggid Mishneh concerning Rambam and Rashba.
