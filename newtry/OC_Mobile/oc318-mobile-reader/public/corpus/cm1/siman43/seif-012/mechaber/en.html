Postdated debt documents are valid because the holder weakened his own rights and may seize property only from the document's date onward. They are valid even if they do not state that they were postdated. This applies when the document includes an after-acquired-property clause. If it does not, the document is invalid unless it expressly states, 'We postdated it.' {Rama: The same is written in siman 258. No document should initially be postdated, because doing so appears false (Rabbeinu Yeruham, netiv 4, part 4).}
