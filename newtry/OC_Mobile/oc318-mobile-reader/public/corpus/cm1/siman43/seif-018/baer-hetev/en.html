The writing. The Sema writes that the text should be emended to read, "we associate the kinyan with that day," meaning that the document explains that the kinyan itself occurred on that particular day, not that the writing occurred then. When the document is dated with the day on which the writers are presently standing, the writing is associated with that day, not the kinyan. The Shach likewise writes that this is the primary reading. See Responsa Maharanach, siman 43.
