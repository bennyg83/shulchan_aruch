A document whose date is written as Shabbos or the tenth of Tishrei is presumed to be postdated and is valid. We do not suspect that it was actually written earlier and merely dated for Shabbos or Yom Kippur, because it is well known that documents are not written on those days. We therefore presume the date was deliberately postponed. Some say that if the borrower says, "Swear to me," the creditor must swear. This applies where the document has been authenticated. If it has not been authenticated, the burden of proof rests upon the holder of the document.
