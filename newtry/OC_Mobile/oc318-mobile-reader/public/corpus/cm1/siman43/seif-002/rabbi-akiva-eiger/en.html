[Shulchan Aruch] Only from unencumbered property. Note: The Derishah writes that the same applies if he later borrowed from another creditor. Even property in the borrower’s hands is then considered encumbered for this purpose, because the concern likewise exists that the earlier dated creditor may collect property that became subject to the later lender, who actually lent first, and then force him aside. It is possible, however, that this concern is less applicable. See there.
