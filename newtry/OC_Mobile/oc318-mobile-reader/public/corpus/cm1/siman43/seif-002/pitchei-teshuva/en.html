See Responsa Chut HaShani, siman 39, concerning a document dated the fifteenth of Av in the year 5347, with a due date before Rosh Hashana of the year 5360, which would be approximately thirteen years later, while it was evident that the scribe had erred and should have written 5357 rather than 5347.
