(Seif 16: even if they signed only much later.) This implies that in any event they must sign. Yet at the beginning of siman 39, witnesses to a kinyan alone allow collection from encumbered property even if no document was signed. According to what I wrote there, the cases differ. Siman 39 concerns collection within the original term. Here the creditor demanded payment after the due date, while the witnesses signed during the term. The Sema’s lengthy discussion in seif katan 29 is therefore unnecessary.
