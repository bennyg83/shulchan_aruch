Rabbeinu Tam disagrees, as cited by the Mordechai and Terumat HaDeshen. The Rama also records dissenters in Even HaEzer, following the Ribash. It is surprising that he rules here without qualification like the Mechaber. Perhaps he was stringent only for bills of divorce, or perhaps this case differs because the date is the twenty-second of Tishrei, Shemini Atzeret, making it clear that the document was postdated, as discussed below. Nevertheless, Rabbeinu Tam expressly disagrees, and Terumat HaDeshen concludes that the Or Zarua cites Rabbeinu Tam together with two other Geonim who invalidate such a document. Their reason may be that one does not attribute an error of two days, or an error late in the month, to confusion about the new month. The practical law requires study.
