Valid. The Sema writes that one should not object from the rule that after most of a month has passed we do not attribute a date discrepancy to error concerning whether the preceding month was full or deficient. That applies where witnesses testify orally. When the date is written in a document, the error can be demonstrated from the adjacent text. If there is a discrepancy of two days, one can say that the witnesses erred regarding two successive month lengths. The Shach writes that the Rama here rules without qualification like the Mechaber, whereas in Even HaEzer, siman 126, seif 21, he records dissenting opinions. Perhaps the Rama was stringent only in gittin, or perhaps this case differs because the date is the twenty second of Tishrei, which is Shemini Atzeret, making it necessarily clear that the document was postdated. Nevertheless, Rabbeinu Tam expressly disagrees, and the rulings of Terumat HaDeshen likewise conclude otherwise. The practical law requires study. See Responsa Maharanach, siman 110.
