When may a predated document be used to collect from unencumbered property? When the witnesses did not intentionally predate it. {Rama: Some say the witnesses are believed to say that they erred even when their signatures can be authenticated independently. The same applies to any error that witnesses commonly make: they are believed to say they erred. Others disagree.} If they intentionally predated the document, it certainly cannot be used even against unencumbered property, because witnesses who signed a falsehood are disqualified.
