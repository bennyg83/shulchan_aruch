[Shulchan Aruch] And because of doubt we do not extract money. Note: If Reuven granted Shimon a complete waiver of every matter and every lien, and afterward Reuven produced against Shimon a deed of sale containing a warranty, the warranty is not included in the waiver. The waiver includes only matters concerning which they had been dealing, or monetary matters of loan, deposit, or partnership. Responsa Rashba in Toldot Adam, siman 217. Likewise, language waiving "every claim" does not include a debt that clearly belongs to him by a loan established through witnesses or by a deed. Responsa Mabit, part 1, siman 314.
