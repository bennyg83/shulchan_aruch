Until he brings proof. Sema explains that the possibility of later proof does not create concern that the deed was repaid because the lender himself found it. In Tumim I reject this. If the lender himself found it, then even a deed written in anticipation of a loan that never occurred would not create the same concern, so why would proof be required? Taz asks similarly. I therefore explain that the rule concerns sale and gift deeds, where repayment is irrelevant. This also resolves the question why Tur cites this law even though he holds that witnesses' signatures effectuate the transaction. See Tumim and my resolution of Bach.
