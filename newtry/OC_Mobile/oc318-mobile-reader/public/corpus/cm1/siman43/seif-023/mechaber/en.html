If Reuven and Shimon each hold a document against Levi, Reuven's is dated the twenty-fifth, or according to another reading the fifth, of Nisan, while Shimon's is dated simply 'Nisan,' and Levi owns only one field insufficient for both debts, the field is awarded to Reuven, because Shimon's document may have been written at the end of Nisan. Shimon also cannot seize from purchasers who bought from Levi from Iyar onward, because they may say: 'Your document may date from the first of Nisan. We left property from which you could collect, namely the field that Reuven took, because he collected it improperly since you preceded him.' Therefore, if Reuven and Shimon grant one another authority to act, they may collect from purchasers who bought from Iyar onward.
