The correct reading is: in Reuven's document it is written the twenty-eighth of Nisan. The Tur has the fifth of Nisan, but in Perishah I wrote that the correct text should also be the twenty-eighth. This teaches the greater novelty that even then the property is given to Reuven rather than Shimon, because Shimon's loan may have occurred only on the twenty-ninth of Nisan. This is why the continuation says perhaps Shimon's document was at the end of Nisan.
If they write authorizations to one another, they may collect. Then one of them comes against the debtor both by his own authority and by the authority of the second person, and he will collect from him in either event.
