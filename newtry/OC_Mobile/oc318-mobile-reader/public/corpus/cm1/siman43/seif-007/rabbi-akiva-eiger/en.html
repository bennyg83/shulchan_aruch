[Shulchan Aruch] The holder of the document has the inferior hand. Note: The source of this law is Responsa HaRosh, where he writes that although an ordinary loan is for thirty days, we do not apply the principle that a person does not ordinarily repay within the term unless a fixed term was expressly established. According to this, it appears that the case here is specifically one in which no fixed term is mentioned in the deed. But this is difficult to me, for the Rosh previously wrote that a receipt is a stronger defect than a fallen document with respect to the presumption that a person does not repay on the day the loan is made. Since a fallen document is impaired even within its term, a receipt should certainly be so. This requires very great study.
