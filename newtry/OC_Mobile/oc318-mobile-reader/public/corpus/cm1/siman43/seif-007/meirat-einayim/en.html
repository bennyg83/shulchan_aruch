In Derishah I wrote that even if beit din takes possession of the document and prevents collection from the earlier date, or writes on the document that it may be collected only from the present time, the creditor still cannot collect from purchasers. This is a penalty because when the document was originally written it was legally capable of collecting from the improper earlier date.
It is completely invalid. In Perishah and Hagahot Darkei Moshe I wrote in the name of Rama that this does not mean the document is utterly worthless for every purpose. If the debtor claims, "This never happened," the document can still establish him as a false denier. But if he claims, "I paid," he is believed. See there.
