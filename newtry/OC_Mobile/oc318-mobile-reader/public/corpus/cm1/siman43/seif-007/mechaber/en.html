Predated debt documents are invalid because their holder could unlawfully seize property from purchasers. The Sages therefore penalized the creditor: he may collect with a predated document only from the debtor's unencumbered property, lest he seize property based on the false earlier date. If the borrower claims, 'I paid,' his claim is governed like the same claim against other documents. If he claims that the debt never existed, he is established as a denier. {Rama: Some say the document is entirely invalid (Tur in the name of the Ri and the Rosh, and Rivash, siman 382).}
