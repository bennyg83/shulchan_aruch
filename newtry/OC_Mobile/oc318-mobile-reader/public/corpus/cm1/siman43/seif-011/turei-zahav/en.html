(There, seif 15: perhaps they lied and said, “We wrote it at its proper time,” etc.) This is very difficult. Why invalidate the document? Perhaps it is postdated, and the witnesses now lie in saying it was written at its proper time. We should not destroy another person’s document because of uncertainty. The case must be that the witnesses were questioned before they knew other witnesses would render them zomemim. Otherwise, by saying they wrote it at its proper time they would be making themselves wicked, and a person cannot make himself wicked. Their statement was therefore made independently and later caused their disqualification through other testimony. We cannot say they lied in stating that they did not postdate it, because that very statement is what produced their disqualification. Yet their statement that they wrote it at the proper time is not accepted, because at that moment they became disqualified and their words have no evidentiary force. By contrast, regarding “we did not postdate it,” their disqualification arises only because of that statement, so they are not disqualified until the matter is established in beit din.
