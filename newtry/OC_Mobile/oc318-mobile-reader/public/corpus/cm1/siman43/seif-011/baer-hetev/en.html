According to the poskim who rule like Abaye that witnesses acquire rights for the beneficiary through their signatures even without a kinyan, once the document is ultimately delivered to the lender he acquires retroactively from the day the loan was made. See Responsa Mabit, vol. 2, siman 99. Shach. The Taz writes that although the Rosh expressly ruled like Abaye, with respect to practical halacha his view is effectively overridden by his rulings elsewhere. On that basis, the Tur's statements in many places are correct where they imply that we do not rule that the witnesses' signatures themselves acquire the right for the lender.
