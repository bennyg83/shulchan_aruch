They write in the document the day on which it was delivered. Baal HaTerumot explains the reason: a document is acquired only either when it reaches the creditor or from the time of a kinyan in a document that records an acquisition. According to Tosafot, Rosh, and Tur, however, who rule like Abaye that the witnesses' signatures acquire for the creditor retroactively, even if the document was dated on the day the borrower ordered it written and signed but was not delivered to the creditor until several days later, collection goes back to the written date. See the end of siman 39.
