With a migo that if he wished, etc. In Urim I cited Rashba that if other witnesses also saw the kinyan, those other witnesses may write the replacement document. One should not object that if the original signatories' handwriting is independently authenticated and they are therefore disqualified for signing a false date, then the whole witnessing group should be invalid under the rule that if one witness in a group is found related or disqualified, the entire testimony is void. The answer is simple. At the time of the kinyan, the original signatories were still valid witnesses. Their disqualification arose only afterward, when they signed the false antedated document. They are not disqualified retroactively. Therefore any other witnesses who joined them in witnessing the kinyan were part of a valid witnessing group at that time. The later misconduct in writing the false document does not invalidate the earlier kinyan testimony or the other witnesses who participated in it. Accordingly, those other witnesses may still testify to the kinyan and write the appropriate replacement.
