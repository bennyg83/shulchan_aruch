Obligation. The source concludes with the reason: what could obligate him during the daytime, etc.? It appears to me that just as the case of an obligation created by a written document means that the document itself was not written until nighttime, so too the case of an obligation through kinyan means that the kinyan was not made until nighttime. During the day they merely discussed and agreed upon the obligation verbally, and the binding obligation was completed at night through the document or kinyan. But if a kinyan sudar was actually made during the day concerning the obligation, then he certainly became bound from that moment. Thus the Sema.
