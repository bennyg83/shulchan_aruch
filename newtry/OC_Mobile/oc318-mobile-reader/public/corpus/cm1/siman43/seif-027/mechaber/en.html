If Reuven produces a debt document against Shimon dated the fifth of Nisan, and Shimon produces a receipt written on the fifth of Nisan stating that Reuven waived all claims he had against Shimon until that day, Reuven may collect the document. In ordinary usage, 'until' excludes the endpoint. If the receipt says that he waived all claims he had 'until now,' the matter is doubtful, and money is not removed from its possessor on account of doubt.
