For in ordinary speech, 'until' excludes the endpoint. This requires study. In Darkei Moshe, Rama rejected this responsum and held that in a receipt we do not say that 'until' excludes the endpoint. Yet here the Mechaber rules according to it and Rama raises no objection. See Hagahot Darkei Moshe, where I reconciled the two responsa of the Rosh that Rama thought contradictory. It appears the Mechaber likewise understood that they do not contradict each other, which is why Beit Yosef cites both and the Shulchan Aruch rules accordingly here.
