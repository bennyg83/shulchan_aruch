Until that day. The Rosh records a case where Reuven produced a document dated the fifth of Nisan stating that Shimon owed him one hundred. Shimon produced a receipt also dated the fifth of Nisan stating that Reuven waived all monetary claims he had against him until that day. The Rosh ruled that although it is uncommon for a person to repay a debt on the very day it is incurred, it sometimes happens, and the receipt itself testifies to payment.
