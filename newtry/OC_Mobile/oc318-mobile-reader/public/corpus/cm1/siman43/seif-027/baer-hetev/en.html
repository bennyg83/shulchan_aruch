In the Darchei Moshe the Rama rejects this law and holds that with a receipt we do not interpret "until" as excluding the endpoint, yet here the Mechaber rules this way and the Rama does not object. Sema. The Shach writes that many later authorities noted an apparent contradiction between two responsa of the Rosh on this law. In truth there is no difficulty. In the latter responsum the Rosh himself writes that where a person wrote "until now," the matter is doubtful. Therefore, in the first responsum, where the receipt states that the creditor waived all claims he had against the debtor "until this very day," the phrase is comparable to "until now," like the many Scriptural expressions "until this day." In the later responsum, however, the wording was that he waived all claims "until the fifth of Nisan," which is like saying "until Pesach," meaning until the beginning of the fifth of Nisan. This appears clear.
