Written by day and signed at night, etc. Regarding a ketubah the authorities dispute whether it is treated like a judicial record, in which case writing by day and signing at night is valid. The Rosh, Ran, and Maggid Mishneh record the dispute, and Rashba rules that a ketubah is comparable to an act of beit din.

The same is true even if they never signed, because a kinyan becomes publicly known and permits collection from encumbered property even without any document, as explained at the beginning of siman 39. This is the view of most authorities, unlike the Rosh and those who follow him. The Sema's distinction between whether the witnesses are presently available is unpersuasive; the Rosh and Tur indeed require writing, but the Mechaber follows the majority.
