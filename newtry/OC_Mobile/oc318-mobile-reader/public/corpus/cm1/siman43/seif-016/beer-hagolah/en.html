The Rosh writes that the document is not considered antedated, because once the document is written the witnesses publicize the lien from the time of the kinyan. Nimukei Yosef writes similarly, concluding in the name of the Ri that if the kinyan was not written, one cannot collect from encumbered property because the publicity was nullified. This requires study, because above at the beginning of siman 59 the Mechaber writes that even if it was not written, he collects from encumbered property. The Sema reconciles this with difficulty. In my humble opinion, above concerns loan documents, where the kinyan is stronger because the money itself creates the lien, as written in the following seif in the name of the Ran. Therefore, even if the kinyan was not written, he collects from encumbered property. Here, however, the discussion concerns other obligations created by kinyan.
