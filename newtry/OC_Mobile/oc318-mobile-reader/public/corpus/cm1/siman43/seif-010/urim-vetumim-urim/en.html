For it is written in it, etc. The relevant clause must appear before the final line, because we do not derive rights from the last line. Although a statement adverse to the document holder can be learned from the final line, perhaps the witnesses never wrote it and the holder later inserted it to validate his deed. See what I wrote earlier in Tumim. If the clause appeared earlier, there would be no reason for the holder to erase it, because an erasure itself would invalidate the document. See siman 45, where this is discussed.
