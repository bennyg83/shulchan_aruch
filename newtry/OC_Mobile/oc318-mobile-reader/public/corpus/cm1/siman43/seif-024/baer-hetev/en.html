The Sema writes that the two documents need not match in every respect, as is evident from siman 71, seif 9, concerning a document containing a credibility clause, etc. The present rule comes only to exclude a case where the amount stated in the receipt does not appear at all in the debt document held by the lender. In such a case the receipt certainly does not refer to that document. See below, siman 54, seif 3, the principal source for the rules governing how receipts are written for the protection of both parties.
