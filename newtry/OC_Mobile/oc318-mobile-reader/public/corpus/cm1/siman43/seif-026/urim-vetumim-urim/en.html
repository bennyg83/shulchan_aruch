Written on that very day. Rosh's responsum involved Reuven producing a deed dated the fifth of Nisan saying Shimon owed him one hundred, while Shimon produced a receipt also dated the fifth stating that Reuven had waived all monetary claims he had against him through that day. Rosh ruled that although people do not commonly pay a debt on the same day it is created, sometimes they do, and the receipt itself testifies that repayment occurred. See Rosh and Sema.
