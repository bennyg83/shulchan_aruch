We ordered the time of the funeral:
It was written on that very day. Although there is a presumption that a person does not ordinarily repay on the day he borrows, sometimes one does repay that same day. Since a receipt dated that same day lies before us, it is as though we saw repayment, and the burden of proof rests upon the one who seeks to extract. So writes the Tur in the name of the Rosh in siman 54:7.
