Until most of the days have passed. Shach cites Rabbenu Chananel that fifteen days after the festival can suffice, and he struggles to explain the measure. In my view Rabbenu Chananel spoke specifically after Pesach. Thirty days before Shavuot is already considered the period related to Shavuot, just as one studies the laws of Pesach thirty days before Pesach and an old sukkah is defined by the thirty-day period. From Pesach until thirty days before Shavuot is exactly fifteen days. Thus after those fifteen days one has already entered the recognized preparatory period for Shavuot, and the previous festival designation no longer governs. Rabbenu Chananel need not have said anything comparable regarding Sukkot. The rule should therefore not be generalized automatically beyond the Pesach-Shavuot calendar context.
