Shach says one should also consider Rabbeinu Chananel's interpretation, cited by Tur in Even HaEzer 147, that this can mean fifteen days after the festival. Regarding oaths one should be stringent and account for that view. Sema writes that once exactly half the period passes, the term is triggered. Shach says a true majority is required, except where one swore to pay within the period, in which case once half has passed he may already be obligated.
