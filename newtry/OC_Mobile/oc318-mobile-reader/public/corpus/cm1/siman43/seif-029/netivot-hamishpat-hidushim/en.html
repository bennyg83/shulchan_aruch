Until most of the days have passed. Likewise, regarding a festival, until three months have passed. The Shach writes that one should take into account the view of Rabbeinu Chananel, namely fifteen days after the festival. Concerning an oath, one should be stringent for Rabbeinu Chananel's position. The Sema writes that once half the period has passed, the obligation applies immediately. The Shach writes that specifically a majority is required, except where he swore to pay, in which case the obligation arises immediately once half the time has passed.
