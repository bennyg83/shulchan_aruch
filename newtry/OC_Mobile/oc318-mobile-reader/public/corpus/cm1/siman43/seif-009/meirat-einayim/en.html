Witnesses to a gift need not have been specifically designated in advance. Alternatively, they may testify before beit din and the court may prepare a judicial record of their testimony. The reason designation is unnecessary for witnesses to a gift is that unlike a loan, there is no concern that a first set of designated witnesses already wrote and delivered a document which the debtor later paid and recovered, such that an undesignated second set might allow double collection. A specific gift is not collected twice.
