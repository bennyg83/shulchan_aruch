This follows the dissenting opinion cited by Rama shortly below, that where the witnesses' handwriting can be authenticated from another source, they are not believed to say that the document was antedated. Rama did not add a gloss here because he relied on what he wrote in seif 8. The Ir Shushan writes that if their handwriting can be authenticated elsewhere and they have no migo, they may not sign another document. The Sema also appears to understand the matter this way. The Shach writes that it is not convincing to distinguish, regarding writing a replacement document, between a case where their handwriting can be authenticated elsewhere and one where it cannot.
