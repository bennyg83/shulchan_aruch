The Tur. Baal HaTerumot likewise writes in gate 56 from Rava bar Sheilat's statement cited above. The Beit Yosef writes that this is the necessary explanation of Rava's statement, for if it merely meant not to write a date they do not remember at all, the document would already be invalid because perhaps it is antedated.
Beit Yosef in the name of his teacher Rabbi Yaakov Bei Rav.
