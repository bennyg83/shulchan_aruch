(Ibid.: Perhaps they concluded the document.) It is difficult, for the document should be invalid because perhaps an obligation of the document holder was written there and he cut it away. One may answer that the case is where some blank space remains before the first line, showing that no complete line had been written there in the ordinary uninterrupted manner, since the existing line begins in the middle. The gloss argues that this is unnecessary. We never suspect that matter adverse to the document holder was written before the beginning of the document, since that portion could simply be cut away. The concern over erasure applies only between lines or on the reverse directly opposite the writing, according to the disqualifying opinion in siman 44, seif 6. An erasure before the beginning of the document, and certainly a cut portion, leaves it plainly valid.
