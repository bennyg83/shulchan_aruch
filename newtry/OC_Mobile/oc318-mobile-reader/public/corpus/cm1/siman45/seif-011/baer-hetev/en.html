To his detriment. The Sema writes that although no inference is drawn from the last line, that rule applies only to a matter benefiting the holder of the document, as stated in siman 44, seif 1. The Shach writes that this is unconvincing. If so, how could the Tur and Shulchan Aruch subsequently write that if “firm and established” appears above at the end of the document it is valid? We should suspect that a matter adverse to the holder was written after “firm and established.” Rather, it appears that the Tur and Shulchan Aruch speak according to the contemporary practice of drawing inferences from the last line because “firm and established” is written. Accordingly, for us even a matter adverse to the holder is not inferred if written after “firm and established.” According to what we rule in siman 42, seif 5, and at the beginning of siman 44 in the gloss, that a matter adverse to the holder may be inferred, the Rama's failure to comment here is difficult and requires study. In any event, it appears clear to me that this rule does not apply according to our practice.
Blank. The Sema writes that from here one should learn not to make the text of a document and its witnesses occupy a single line when there is blank space above it. Rather, the blank area should be filled with ink or cut away. Nevertheless, since a defect is presently before us, we suspect that such an occurrence took place. The Shach writes that the Gemara and the authorities imply that a document whose text and witnesses occupy one line is valid in every circumstance. It is also unconvincing to suspect the witnesses of having acted improperly. Rather, such a document is valid, and we do not fear that the portion above will be cut away meaning, cut from the side before the signatures upward, because ordinarily every document has a margin along its sides. If he cuts it away, there will be no side margin alongside the witnesses' signatures, and the forgery will be recognizable. Accordingly, when witnesses write a document and sign on the same line and there is blank space above, they must be careful to leave no space at all between the text and their signatures. This appears clear; examine it carefully.
