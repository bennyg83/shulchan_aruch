And not like the one who was stringent. The stringent authority was Maharar Shachna. His responsum shows that in the case before him the groom claimed with certainty that he had given the gifts for kiddushin. The Rama himself rules nearby that only where the groom does not say that he sent them for kiddushin are we unconcerned. Accordingly, in Maharar Shachna's case there is good reason for stringency. Moreover, he brought proof that in our lands there are places where people sometimes betroth first and send gifts afterward. He records an incident in Poznan in which the local rabbi, Maharar Mendel Frank, required a get. See the responsum at length.
