We do not say that they signed on the ink blot. The reason is that the lender has no need for their testimony about the blot. If he wished, he could simply cut off the margin containing the blot and there would no longer be any suspicion concerning it. By contrast, when the blot lies in the line immediately before their signatures, one can say they signed in reference to the blot as well. According to this, the Mechaber's later rule that if they began, etc., and cut off the blank portion, etc., would apply equally if they filled the space before the signatures with ink.
