z) The wording of the Rambam in chapter 24 of Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh, from the incident of other documents before Rav Kahana, where he flogged the scribe, Gittin 19b. The Gemara establishes the case as concerning a signature made by cutting marks in the paper and then signing, meaning that the witnesses fill the cut marks with ink. This is the correct explanation. Maggid Mishneh.
ch) Since the Gemara does not say that he flogged him and disqualified the document, etc. Since with regard to a get this method is permitted initially, with other documents it is at least not invalid after the fact, etc.
