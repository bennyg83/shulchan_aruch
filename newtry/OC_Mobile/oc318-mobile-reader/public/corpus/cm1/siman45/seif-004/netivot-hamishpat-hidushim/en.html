And they cut it for them. Meaning, they cut letter shaped openings into another sheet, place that sheet over the document, and the witnesses fill the openings with ink. But if one merely traces letters on the document and the witnesses then write over the tracing, it is writing over writing and is invalid. The Shach validates it.
