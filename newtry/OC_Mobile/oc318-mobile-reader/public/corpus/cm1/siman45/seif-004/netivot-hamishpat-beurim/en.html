And they cut blank paper for them. See Sema and Tosafot in Gittin, who cite the Yerushalmi asking that the first writing is not really theirs and answering that the cuts were widened for them. This indicates a real anti-fraud requirement. Otherwise a person could take the paper bearing those shaped cuts, lay it over a larger document, fill the openings with ink, and manufacture a forged signature. The fact that later decisors do not spell out every technical detail does not remove the obvious need to prevent such fabrication.
