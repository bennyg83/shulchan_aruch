Because anyone who forges, etc. Whether the fraud consists of adding text in the blank line or cutting away the original deed and writing a new one in the two blank lines, a forger does not normally go back to the original scribe. He forges privately. Darkei Moshe was uncertain whether the available space should be measured according to the handwriting of the witnesses actually signed on this deed or according to ordinary witnesses. Ramban cites the Jerusalem Talmud at the end of *Get Pashut* that if the scribe's handwriting is also present with the witnesses, we follow the scribe's handwriting. In general we measure leniently. Sema and Darkei Moshe.
