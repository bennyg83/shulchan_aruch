If the witnesses wrote, 'We the witnesses,' etc. The same would apply even if they did not write this where there is space between one witness and the other, because there is no concern of forgery by erasing the document: in that event the document would be invalid, since people could say something had originally been written between the witnesses stating that the document was on the paper. Perhaps, however, the case concerns witnesses signed on an erased area while the parchment between the witnesses is not erased. More likely, this wording is required initially in order to clarify the matter expressly. See Perishah.
