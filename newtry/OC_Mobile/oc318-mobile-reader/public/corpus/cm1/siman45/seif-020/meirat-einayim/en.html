Lest he erased it. Meaning, after the lender received a document whose text and witnesses were written over an erasure, he forged it by erasing that document again and writing whatever he wanted over it.
And the place of the document once, etc. The witnesses will not notice this when they come to sign, because the difference is not evident until the writing over the two erased places has dried, and the witnesses will have left before the writing dries. Tosafot also write that even without this, the witnesses do not scrutinize the matter as carefully as the beit din does. See Perishah.
And when he erases it a second time. Meaning, the lender does so after it comes into his possession, as explained above.
