The borrower's handwriting. Although Mechaber in siman 44 validates certain erasures, where the writing itself represents repayment or another defense and contains an erasure or scraping, the ambiguity is construed to the detriment of the holder of the document, similar to a receipt containing an erasure. The earlier clause warning one to be careful is therefore primarily an initial drafting rule; after the fact the document can still be valid where the erasure does not undermine its meaning. Urim VeTumim writes the same.
