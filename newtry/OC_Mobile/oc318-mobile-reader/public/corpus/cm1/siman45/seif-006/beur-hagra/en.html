(Collected note) Even if he wrote, etc. Tosafot there, s.v. "because," states that although we derive from the last line, etc. The Rosh and Ramban there disagree with the Rashbam, for the reason stated, lest he cut away part of the document. See Beer HaGolah and the Gemara, 163b.
Even if it is known. Rashbam there. This is also implied by the Gemara there, since it states that if he filled the space with relatives the document is valid, implying that otherwise it is invalid.
