Even if they marked it with ink. This applies even where there is no full erasure, but only dots or scratches with white space visible between them. The Beit Yosef, Nimukei Yosef, Sema, Tosafot, Maggid Mishneh, Rambam, Tur, and Mechaber all indicate this. The explanation that the concern is merely that people might say the witnesses signed upon an ink blot is unnecessary, since the rule applies even to scratched markings. See below, siman 46, seif 32. I note there, however, that scratches may perhaps be valid while a surface fully covered with ink is the case addressed by the Gemara.
