And even if he later sent her gifts again. This ruling is not derived from Maharik's responsum. On the contrary, one who studies the responsum carefully will see the opposite. Maharik writes that he had only clearly permitted gifts sent after the formal penalty arrangement, and he concludes that if, unexpectedly and outside the fixed customary time, the groom sends bridal gifts to the bride, there is greater reason for stringency. The Rama's ruling here derives instead from Responsa Rashba, siman 1180. In practice, Maharik's stringency should be taken into account.
