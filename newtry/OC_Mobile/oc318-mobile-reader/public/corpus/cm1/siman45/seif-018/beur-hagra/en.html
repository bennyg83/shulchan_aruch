(Anthology) And likewise if, etc. Mordechai follows his own view that if two clauses of 'valid and established' were written, the document is invalid even if a blank line was left, or if it appeared on the line before the last, based on the Gemara's statement that two such clauses are not written. Therefore there is concern for this possibility. According to our ruling, however, two such clauses do not invalidate the document, as stated in siman 44, seif 9. If they acted in that manner, the document would be invalid for the reason stated above in seif 10, and we do not suspect that they acted unlawfully, as stated in the gloss there.
