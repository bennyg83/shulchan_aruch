Where the first line does not begin at the edge of the parchment. Meaning, the edge of the parchment at the level of the following line. The first line begins in the middle relative to the second line, and the parchment before it has been cut away. This is the case in Mordechai.
For we are concerned that perhaps the document originally ended in the middle of the line, etc. Although it was already explained at the end of seif 10 that we do not suspect witnesses of signing a document that is susceptible to forgery, and this too could be forged by adding words to the first half-line and then repeating the substance of the document in the final line without the forgery being evident, nevertheless, since there is a defect presently before us in that the document begins in the middle of a line, we take that concern into account.
