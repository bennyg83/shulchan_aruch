The witnesses. The Sema writes that although in seif 10 it was explained that we do not suspect witnesses of signing a document that can be forged, here the holder could add material in the first half-line and then repeat the substance of the document in the last line, leaving the forgery undetectable. Nevertheless, because there is an irregularity before us, namely that the document begins in the middle of a line, we entertain this concern. The Shach disputes this: Should we suspect witnesses of having signed an invalid document merely because of such an irregularity, something found neither in the Gemara nor in any authority? Rather, this document is invalid only according to the opinion that two occurrences of “firm and established” are not written, because we suspect that the original text ended with “firm and established” in the middle of a line, after which there would have been no further forgery concern. Otherwise, despite the irregularity that the document begins in the middle of a line, it would be valid. Accordingly, under our ruling that two occurrences of “firm and established” may be written in the penultimate line, this document is valid. It was certainly made this way from the outset, for otherwise the witnesses would not have left such a large blank area, since it would have been possible to forge the document by inserting “firm and established” twice. The Rama's ruling invalidating such a document therefore requires study. Shach; see there.
