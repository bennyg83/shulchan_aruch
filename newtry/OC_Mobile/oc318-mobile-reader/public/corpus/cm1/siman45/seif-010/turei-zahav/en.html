(Seif 21: If the witnesses wrote, etc.) The Sema writes that the same law applies where there is an erasure between the text and the witnesses, the document itself being on clean paper and the witnesses on the erasure. He validates it because the holder would fear forging the upper portion and writing the document over an erasure, since beit din would say that the witnesses had originally written on clean paper, “we witnesses,” and he erased it. In my opinion this is not valid. Perhaps the holder has witnesses that nothing had been written between the document and the signatures and that the erasure arose independently. He would then have no fear of forging the upper portion and placing the document itself on an erasure.
