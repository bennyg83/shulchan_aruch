But if another person receives them, we are not concerned. One who studies the responsum, siman 170, will see that this was written only as an additional consideration supporting the principal reason that we do not follow the minority possibility of prior kiddushin, even in a place where the bride herself may refrain from receiving gifts out of embarrassment. Terumat HaDeshen cites Or Zarua that there may be concern even where another woman receives the gifts, because perhaps the bride appointed her as agent after already accepting the penalty arrangement; all the more so if her father received them, where Rashi's explanation gives reason for concern. According to Rabbeinu Chananel, even if a minor girl incapable of agency received them, there can still be concern that the bride had already been betrothed and refrained from receiving the gifts herself only from embarrassment.
