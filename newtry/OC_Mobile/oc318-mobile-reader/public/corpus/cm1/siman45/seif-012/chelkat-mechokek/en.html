Where he committed a prohibition, etc., by sending them on Shabbat, or there was a communal ban, etc., perhaps they are kiddushin. Meaning that according to Rashi there is no concern based on the gifts. According to Rabbeinu Chananel, one might think we should nevertheless fear that he had already betrothed her. But one who studies the responsum will see that even according to Rabbeinu Chananel there is generally no concern, because had he betrothed her before ten people the matter would have become public. In the first case, however, where he actually sent the gifts on Shabbat, there is concern according to Rabbeinu Chananel that he betrothed her before Shabbat.
