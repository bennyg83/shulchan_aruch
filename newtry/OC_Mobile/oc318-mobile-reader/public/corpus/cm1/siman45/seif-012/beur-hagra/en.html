(Anthology) And some say, etc. This follows the Tosefta cited above, which says specifically that we presume the relatives signed merely to fill the blank. Proof is also brought from Gittin 81b: only one witness was validated there, etc. Here, since the document is authenticated through two of the signatories, how can relatives be accepted? The answer is that authentication is made only from the lower witnesses, as stated in Bava Batra 163b, 'in every such case,' etc. This implies that ordinarily authentication may be made from the lower witnesses, and relatives there are disqualified. We are not concerned about them, as stated in Gittin 82a. It also follows that document authentication requires only two signatures, not all the signatures, from the Gemara's question, 'let us be concerned,' and its answer, 'he holds that in every such case,' etc. See Tosafot, Bava Batra 162b, s.v. nimtza, and Tosafot, Gittin 18b, s.v. amri, etc.
