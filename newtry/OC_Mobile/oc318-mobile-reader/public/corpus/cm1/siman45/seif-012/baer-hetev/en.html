They are believed to say that all of them sat together to sign, even if their handwriting is authenticated from another source, and this is not considered retracting after testimony. If they say that they did not all sit together to sign, while other witnesses contradict them, there are two witnesses against two witnesses, and the money remains in the possession of its current owner. Thus writes the Sema in the name of the Beit Yosef. The Shach writes that according to the first view in siman 36, seif 2, this case concerns one in which we know that the valid witness was aware of his fellow witness's disqualification. According to the latter view, there is no distinction, and even if they were unaware, the document is void.
