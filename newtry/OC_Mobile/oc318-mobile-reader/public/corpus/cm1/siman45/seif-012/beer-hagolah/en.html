d) The wording of the Rambam in chapter 5 of Hilkhot Edut.
e) As Rava explains: "If you came to observe," etc., Makkot 6a, as stated above at the beginning of siman 36.
f) According to the latter version, "for example, one was found," etc., Gittin 18b. As stated there, if a related or disqualified witness signed at the beginning, etc. Rabbeinu holds that this distinction was said only regarding gittin, but with other documents, even if the disqualified witness signed in the middle or at the end, the document is invalid, as the Kesef Mishneh explains there. So too writes the Tur in the name of the Geonim.
g) From a baraita, Bava Batra 163b. Rabbeinu explains it where there are no witnesses that all of them sat together in order to sign. Although witnesses signed on a document are regarded as though their testimony was examined in beit din, if they were still present they would be believed to say that they all sat together to sign, because the document has been weakened by the presence of a related or disqualified signatory.
h) So too write Tosafot there and elsewhere in the name of Rabbeinu Tam. All this applies when they signed one beneath another. But if they all signed on one line, they certainly intended to serve as witnesses, and the document is invalid. Nimukei Yosef, chapter Get Pashut, from siman 70.
