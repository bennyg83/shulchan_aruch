Signs. This is not considered testimony from one witness through another, because the case concerns one who personally saw the testimony. The reader is only informing him that this is what is written in the document. Since this is a matter destined to become known, regarding which people do not lie, he was believed, especially because the witness stands in awe of the matter. Rashi; Sema.
