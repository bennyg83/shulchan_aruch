Since the deed could have been made valid by filling the blank with relatives, Tur in the name of Rabbi Yonah rules that the debtor cannot say, "Nothing ever occurred." We must therefore assume the witnesses erred by leaving space for additional signatories. Otherwise the witnesses themselves would have signed an invalid deed and total denial would obviously remain available. See Tumim: even if delivery witnesses testify that the deed originally had two blank lines, it remains invalid as a document capable of forgery. For a get, and similarly for acquisition deeds that resemble a get, Rama at the beginning of siman 42 cites Rambam as validating through delivery witnesses. See Even HaEzer 130. If delivery witnesses testify to what was originally written in the final line, see Tumim, where I prove from Ran that we may derive from it.
