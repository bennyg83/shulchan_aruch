If the witnesses' signatures are more than two lines below the text, but the space between the text and the signatures is filled with signatures of related or disqualified people, the document is valid because it cannot be falsified. If the document was seen by a beit din before that space was filled, it can no longer be repaired. If it was made valid by filling the space with related signatures before reaching beit din, it is valid even if the lender did so on his own initiative without the borrower's knowledge. Some say it is valid only if the space was filled with the borrower's consent; otherwise it is invalid.
