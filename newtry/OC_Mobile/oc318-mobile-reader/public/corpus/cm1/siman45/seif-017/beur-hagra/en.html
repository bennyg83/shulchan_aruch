One who comes by virtue of another. Bava Batra 35b, according to the explanation of the Geonim, even where it benefits him.
(Anthology) One who comes, etc. See siman 154, seifim 18 and 19. Some say that this applies only to his detriment, some say even to his benefit, and some say that even to his detriment it applies only regarding chazakah. Rama there follows the view stated here, that it applies even to his benefit. Shulchan Aruch rules this way in several places. See Baer HaGra.
