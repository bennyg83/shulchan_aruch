And beit din. It appears to me that specifically beit din may remedy it, but the witnesses themselves may not, because they were appointed as agents to sign the document only together with the disqualified witness, and they have already discharged their agency. Shach. One should not object from the Mechaber's ruling in siman 49, seif 6, cited by the Sema and Shach in siman 41, that if the document would otherwise become entirely void we do not say that the witnesses have already discharged their agency. There the witnesses were appointed to produce a valid document. Here, by contrast, they were appointed to produce a defective document by signing together with the disqualified witness. We therefore say that their agency has already been discharged.
And it is considered. The Sema writes that although in siman 36 the Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule that when valid and disqualified witnesses testify together the testimony of the valid witnesses is also invalidated, there the testimony was delivered orally together before beit din, after which there is no way to repair it by having the valid witnesses testify again alone. Here, however, they did not give oral testimony together before beit din. The Shach answers that here the case is where the disqualified witnesses were not present with the valid ones when they observed the event. The authorities did not need to state this explicitly, because if they were together at the time of observation, the testimony is plainly void according to all opinions, since both the observation and the testimony were joined. Witnesses signed on a document are regarded as though their testimony had been examined in beit din, and for this reason we rule everywhere that witnesses signed on a document cannot later retract and testify differently, since once a witness has testified he cannot testify again. Testimony in a document is like testimony before beit din. According to what I wrote in siman 36, that joining together merely for observation invalidates the testimony even if they did not subsequently testify together, if the disqualified witnesses were present with them when the event was observed, the testimony was already void even before they signed. If they were not present together then, but they all signed the document, it is likewise invalid. Although I wrote there that merely giving testimony before beit din does not necessarily constitute joinder, testimony in a document is worse in this respect because all the witnesses are signed together on one document, rendering it intrinsically defective.
