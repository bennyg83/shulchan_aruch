If the valid witnesses remember the testimony. The Tur and Mechaber write similarly below in siman 51. Above, at the end of siman 36, they cite the Rosh that if valid and invalid witnesses testified orally together, the valid testimony is also disqualified. That case differs because they testified together orally in beit din. Since they originally saw the event together and then testified together, the disqualification attached at the time of their oral testimony and cannot later be cured by having the valid witnesses testify again alone. Here they did not give oral testimony together before beit din. In Derishah on siman 36 I explained that this ruling derives from the Rosh, rule 60, siman 3, concerning a will. Yet the Rosh in simanim 4 and 5 there appears to contradict it, implying that even where witnesses signed a document the valid ones cannot return and testify unless the disqualified witnesses were not present with them when the loan was witnessed but joined only later to sign. If the disqualified witnesses were present and intended to testify already at the time of the loan, the valid witnesses could not later testify alone. I suggested with difficulty that the latter statements were written according to Rambam, who holds that mere joint witnessing can invalidate the testimony, while the Rosh himself requires both joint witnessing and joint testimony. This remains difficult and requires study.
