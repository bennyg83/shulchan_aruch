Every document. Sanhedrin 29b and Ketubot 18b to 19a.
And if they are present. See seif 2 and what is explained there, and see the Shach.
(Collected note) And if the witnesses are present, etc. This contradicts what was written in seif 4, that the document is invalid; the same applies where it was read to them.
(Collected note) And if they did not say, etc. This view holds that although the witnesses did not have someone else read the document to them before they signed, since they knew the substance of the document, then although initially they should not have signed it, once they did sign and there is no forgery in the document, it is valid after the fact. This is unlike the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch in seif 4. See the Shach. The reason is that all of this is because of concern for forgery, and since there is no forgery, it is valid after the fact, as written in siman 44, seif 1.
