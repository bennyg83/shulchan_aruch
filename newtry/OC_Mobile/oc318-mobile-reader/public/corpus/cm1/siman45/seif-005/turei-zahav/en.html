(Seif 15: That which we validate.) It appears to me that even the authority who validates the case in seif 14, where the insertion was made without the borrower’s knowledge, disqualifies it here. Purchasers can say that when they bought the property from the borrower, the document was invalid, as though it had not then been written at all. Above, however, the borrower’s lack of knowledge is immaterial, because the correction was made that same day and his knowledge is unnecessary. Thus the ruling of this seif accords with all opinions, and this is the law. The Sema’s emendation, which makes this depend on the earlier dispute, is astonishing.
