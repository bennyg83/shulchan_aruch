If they say, "It was read before us." From this the Shach proves that although the Mechaber wrote above that having someone else read it before them does not help, that restriction is only initially. After the fact, the document is valid:
They are careful with their testimony. Meaning, they must always have had the document read by someone who knew how to read. The difference is as follows. If the witnesses now know the document's contents by heart, beit din does not automatically invalidate it, but asks whether it was read before them by someone qualified to read. If they do not know the contents by heart, beit din presumptively invalidates the document until the witnesses themselves say that they had someone read it for them, Sema.
