Masks. In Mordecai, Dham signed a Flemish man until he saw the C.C.C. A:
Above. The Sema writes that in such a case he can forge the document by adding below whatever he wishes. The Shach disputes this and writes that Tosafot at the beginning of the chapter Get Pashut prove that even where he cannot forge it, such as where “firm and established” is written, where there is no blank space below, or where one witness signed below and the second at the side, it is nevertheless invalid because it was not made in accordance with the enactment of the Sages; see there. It appears that even those authorities who rule at the beginning of siman 44 that where the substance of the document was not repeated the entire document is not invalid, agree in this case.
