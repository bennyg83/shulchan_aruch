But. The Sema writes that even if the document is formally invalid because it was susceptible to forgery, the witnesses still establish that the underlying transaction actually occurred. The case is therefore like a loan or sale proved by witnesses without a valid written document, and the defendant cannot claim that the event never occurred. The Shach adds that even those poskim who hold that an antedated loan document is completely invalid and allows the debtor to deny the transaction entirely agree here. The reason is that this testimony was potentially capable of producing a valid document if the blank area had been filled with the signatures of relatives or with repeated wording, so the defect does not undermine the witnesses' testimony to the transaction itself.
