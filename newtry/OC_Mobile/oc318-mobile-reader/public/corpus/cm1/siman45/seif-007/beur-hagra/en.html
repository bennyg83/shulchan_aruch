That which we say. See Rashbam, s.v. hirchik.
(Anthology) That which we say, etc. Even if the document was lost and witnesses testify to its contents, the debtor cannot say, 'This never occurred,' and he is established as one who denied falsely, as stated in Bava Metzia 17a in the incident of Shabbatai. The same applies here, because there is testimony of witnesses. One cannot object that this is written testimony, for the signatures themselves were not invalidated, as shown by the rule that if the blank was filled with relatives the document is valid.
