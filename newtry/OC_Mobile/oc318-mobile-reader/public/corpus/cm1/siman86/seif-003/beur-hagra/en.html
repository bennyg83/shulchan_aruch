Even at a time... This follows from what is stated in the first chapter of Bava Kamma, the first chapter of Bava Metzia, and chapter ten of Kesubos: "Reuven who sold..." and he cannot say to him.... This shows that the creditor has no direct claim against him; rather, he himself collects from the purchaser. Here too, the property is treated as encumbered to him, as explained above from Bava Kamma. See Beer HaGolah.
