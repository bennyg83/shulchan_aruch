There is an authority who says... Although one does not collect a loan itself, because money lent is in the borrower's domain, since a loan is given to be spent, this differs from a deposit. This is as stated in Kiddushin 47a-b regarding one who betroths with a loan, and in the ensuing discussion. See Rashi there, s.v. "which does not return in its original form." So too, the Rif and Rosh write in the first chapter of Bava Metzia regarding the bathhouse case that movable property likewise..., but where one has a deposit..., because we rule that a deposit remains in its owner's domain. The Rosh brings proof from Bava Basra 88a.
For whenever there is... See Kesubos 86a regarding one who hides his money, and Bava Kamma 46 and Bava Basra 32. See Tosafos there.
