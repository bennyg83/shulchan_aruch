The bailee is exempt. Even where she is not one who conducts the household, Mordechai was uncertain whether a return to the husband is effective after the fact. He concludes, "the matter is weak in my hands, but I incline more to exempt the bailee." In any event, it appears to me that the property retains the law of nichsei melog, even if the money had been hidden. This is no worse than a case where a wife purchases property from her husband using hidden money. Rambam rules that the money then has the status of nichsei melog, even where the husband claims with certainty. Where the money was not hidden, everyone agrees with Rambam, as explained in the preceding siman, seif 9.
