If they have property. Meaning property inherited from their father. He cannot go back against property belonging personally to the heirs, because what connection do their own assets have to their father's debt? If one asks: if the heirs possess other inherited property, then Rabbi Natan's lien would not apply in any event because there are other assets, the answer is that the other property may be overseas or otherwise unavailable. Shach holds that he may even go against the heirs' own property. I already established above in siman 66 that Sema is correct. See there.
