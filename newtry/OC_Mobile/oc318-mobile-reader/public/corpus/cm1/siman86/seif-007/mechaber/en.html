If Shimon died and left heirs, and Levi claims that his debt to Shimon was paid, Shimon's heirs take the heirs' oath and collect from Levi. If Reuven's debt was recorded in a document, Reuven then collects from them upon taking an oath. If they refuse to swear, Reuven proceeds against them if they have other property. If they have no other property and claim that they are unable to take this oath, they take a heses oath that they have no means with which to pay their ancestor's debt. See note 40.
