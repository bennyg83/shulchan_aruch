If this condition is not explicit, etc. The Tur cites the Rosh: If the document expressly states that Shimon cannot compel his brother Levi to pay unless Shimon wishes to do so, then Reuven has no greater lien upon Levi than Shimon himself had. Shimon could give his movable property to whomever he wished, and all the more so could give away or lend the money owed to him without pressing Levi. But if the condition is not written explicitly, even if everyone knows Shimon ordinarily would not pressure his brother, he still has the legal power to do so whenever he chooses. Reuven therefore acquires that same power against Levi after beit din establishes that Shimon has no other means with which to pay.
