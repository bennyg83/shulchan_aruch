(Collected notes) When does this apply? See Beer HaGolah, which explains that it is like encumbered property. This requires study, for if so, with an oral loan he should not collect, and even with a written loan he should not collect, since this is movable property, as stated in Pesachim 31a, "We left movable property..." Moreover, his proof is not conclusive. (End of collected notes.)
When they arranged his assets... and administered an oath to him. This follows the law of encumbered property, as explained from the Gemara cited above. See below, siman 98 seif 9, that afterward they administer an oath to the borrower.
Even though there is... This is likewise the rule regarding encumbered property. See siman 111 seif 11, and as stated in Kesubos 88a, where Rava says that this applies even to a creditor who did not..., although one does not collect from property unless collection cannot be made from the person himself, because his assets are guarantors for him, as stated at the end of Bava Basra.
