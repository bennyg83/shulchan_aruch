(In the Tur, at the end of the siman) “Levi did not become obligated to Reuven until after the loan,” and so forth. The explanation is that Shimon certainly has authority to give a gift. Therefore, so long as Shimon granted Levi additional time at the time of the loan, he acted lawfully. After the loan, however, Levi became obligated to Reuven by force of that loan, and the loan itself then belonged to Reuven. Shimon can no longer extend Levi’s time, because he has no authority to give away another person’s property. At the time of the loan, by contrast, it was still Shimon’s.
