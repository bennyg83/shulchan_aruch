(Shach, there) And if that were so, he should have said that this applies only, etc. Note: This requires study. Rashba's words there address the Gemara's question, 'Where he has no property, what can be said?' and the answer, 'We tell him: just as I am indebted to you,' etc. Rashba proves from this that even without first being sued in beit din, the debtor may pay directly under Rabbi Natan's rule.
