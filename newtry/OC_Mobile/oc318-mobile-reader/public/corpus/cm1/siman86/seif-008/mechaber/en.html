If Reuven lent Shimon money orally, Shimon lent Levi money under a promissory note, and Levi sold all his property, Reuven may use Shimon's note against Levi to seize property from Levi's purchasers. If Shimon admits that his debt to Reuven has not been paid, the purchasers cannot repel Reuven by saying, 'Shimon paid you, and his admission is not credible against us.' Since Shimon admits the debt, Reuven may seize from them. See note 6.
