Reuven, etc., since Shimon, etc. The halachah follows the view that a lien is Scriptural, as stated in Kiddushin 13b and by Rif at the end of Bava Batra. An oral creditor does not collect from purchasers only because the debt lacks publicity. Here the purchaser should have guarded himself because of Shimon's known interest. Nor do we suspect collusion, as indicated in Bava Batra 128b. Nevertheless, Reuven remains liable to Shimon.
