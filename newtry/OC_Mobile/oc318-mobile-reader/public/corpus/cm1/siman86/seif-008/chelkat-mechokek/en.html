A husband who borrowed from his wife. I already wrote in the preceding siman, seif katan 23, that according to Rambam, once she gives the money to her husband and does not acquire land with it, it leaves her possession and she loses her rights in it, even where the money was not hidden. It certainly appears that according to Rambam, forcible seizure by the husband does not help him even with hidden money. Even where she purchased property from him with hidden money, so that he can say, "I only wanted to reveal where your money was," the money nevertheless does not lose its status as nichsei melog. All the more so where he seized it against her will. The "some say" cited by Rama afterward, distinguishing between hidden and non-hidden money, is not an explanation of Rambam but a separate opinion that disagrees with him and makes this distinction even regarding a loan.
