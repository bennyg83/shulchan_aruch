However, if beit din has already assigned the promissory note to the creditor by appraisal in payment of his debt, it is like an ordinary sale and the original creditor can no longer waive it. Shach. See the Beurim: even if part of the debt remains unpaid, once he pays the remainder, a waiver concerning the portion already transferred is effective as to any amount beyond the actual debt. For example, if his debt was one hundred and beit din assigned him a note worth two hundred in payment of that hundred, he cannot waive the hundred actually owed to him, but his waiver is effective regarding the excess hundred:
