To waive it for Levi, etc. Only one who sells a promissory note to another can later waive the borrower's personal obligation, because the lien of the body remains with the seller, as explained in siman 66.
Here, however, Shimon has entirely removed himself from Levi, so there is no remaining personal obligation in Shimon's hands to waive.Likewise if he says, 'It was a document of trust,' etc. Meaning that even in such a case, where he admits and says he has no money in the hands of his own debtor, he is not believed.
This applies specifically where the document contains a credibility clause. Otherwise, since the second creditor is not stronger than the first lender, the debtor's ordinary defenses may still have force.
