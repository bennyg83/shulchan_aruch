Shimon cannot forgive Levi's debt or extend his time for payment, because Shimon's lien upon Levi has left his control and Levi has become obligated to Reuven. Similarly, if Shimon says that the document was delivered in trust or has been paid, he is not believed to cause Reuven a loss. If, after Levi was held liable to Reuven in beit din, Levi went and paid Shimon, he must pay Reuven a second time. If a recalcitrant person refuses to appear for judgment, or appears, is held liable, and refuses to comply with beit din's order, and beit din restrains his money held by another, then if that custodian violates their order and returns it to the recalcitrant person, the custodian must give the money to the creditor if no other assets can be found from which to collect.
