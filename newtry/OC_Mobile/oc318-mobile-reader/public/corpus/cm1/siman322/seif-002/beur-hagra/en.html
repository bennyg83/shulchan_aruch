And until when, etc. See the Gemara there and Be'er HaGolah. Rambam and Rif omitted the dispute between Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel and Rabbi.
(Supplement) They omitted the baraita, one taught, etc. They understood it not as a binding legal rule, because possession is irrelevant where the event is a regional calamity, but merely as prudent advice concerning one's own field, similar to the dispute between Rabbi and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. Rashba's suggestion that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel disputes the baraitot requires study. (End of supplement.)
And specifically, etc. See Sema and Rashi on 104a, s.v. 'we assess it,' that the rule applies specifically where the superior wheat was affected. See also 106b and 105a, where the owner may say, 'The wheat of my land,' but if the tenant left the field uncultivated he has no such claim.
