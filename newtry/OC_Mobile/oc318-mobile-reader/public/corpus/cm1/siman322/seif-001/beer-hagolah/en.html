a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 4. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the Mishnah in Bava Metzia 105b. Where the Mishnah says 'one who receives,' the Mechaber here writes 'one who rents,' as the Maggid Mishneh explains in the name of Rashi, because the Mishnah cannot refer to a sharecropper, for what deduction would there be when whatever produce is found is divided between them? Perhaps the Rambam used the Mishnah's wording because it says 'one who receives,' but its meaning is one who receives the field under a fixed-rent tenancy or rental.
b) Rav Yehudah's interpretation there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Rambam's reading is 'the majority of the valleys,' meaning all the valleys belonging to the town, and that he found this reading in some versions of the Rif.
c) Ibid., Gemara 106a: it is reasonable for the tenant to say, 'If this occurred because of my misfortune...'
d) Ibid.: it is reasonable for the owner to say, 'It occurred because of your misfortune.' The reason is explicit in the Gemara there.
