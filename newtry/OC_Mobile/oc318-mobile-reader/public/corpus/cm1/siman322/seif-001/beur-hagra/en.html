(Supplement) Or the renter. Meaning, not in accordance with Rabbi Yehuda, as the Gemara states on 106b. (End of supplement.)
(Supplement) And locusts consumed it, etc. The codifiers omitted the baraita distinguishing a first occurrence from repeated occurrences. It explains Shmuel's law where the tenant did not sow: if the crop had been consumed only once he must sow again, but after the same event occurred in two or three successive years he need not, because the calamity has become established and the owner cannot claim that the verse 'they shall not be ashamed in an evil time' would have been fulfilled through him. Because such repetition is extremely uncommon, Rif, Rambam, and the Shulchan Aruch omitted it. (End of supplement.)
