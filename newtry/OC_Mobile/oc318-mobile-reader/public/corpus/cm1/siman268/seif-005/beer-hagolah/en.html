Peh) A statement of Rabbi Yochanan in the name of Rabbi Yannai and a baraita on Bava Metzia 10b. According to both explanations at the beginning of 11a, Reish Lakish likewise agrees. The Rif rules this way. The Rosh writes that this follows either the first version according to Rabbi Yochanan or the latter version, where each Amora states a different point and they do not disagree.
