Since, etc. If one declares an item ownerless and throws it into another person's courtyard, its law is like a gift, and the courtyard acquires it for the owner even if he runs after it and cannot reach it. So writes the Darkei Moshe in the name of the Nimukei Yosef, and the Sema cites it in his gloss. The Rosh writes likewise and reads the Gemara's question as including the words 'and he declared it ownerless,' contrary to Tosafot's version there. See the Tur at the end of siman 243 and Imrei Binah, folio 101d.
