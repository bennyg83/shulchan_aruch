(Likkut) And if it was a deer, etc. See Be'er HaGolah, seif katan 60. At first glance this is also implied by the Gemara, since the question was asked on R' Yochanan's statement rather than on the Mishnah concerning a deer running in its ordinary manner. But this does not appear correct to me, for if so, what does the Gemara mean when Rav Pappa said to Rava, etc., 'Is this not our Mishnah?' On the contrary, from there the opposite is proven, because throwing a purse resembles a deer running in its ordinary manner. End.
