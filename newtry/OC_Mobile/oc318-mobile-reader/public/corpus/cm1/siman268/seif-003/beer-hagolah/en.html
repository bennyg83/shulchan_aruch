Kaf) There in the Mishnah. The Maggid Mishneh writes in chapter 17 of Hilkhot Aveidah, halachah 8, that two conditions are required for an unguarded courtyard: the owner must be standing beside the field and must say, "My field acquires for me." This is the plain implication of the Mishnah and Gemara, is explicit in the Yerushalmi, and is how most commentators explain it. This differs from the rabbinic acquisition of four cubits, which operates even without a statement, because there one can readily lean over and take the object, making that acquisition stronger.
