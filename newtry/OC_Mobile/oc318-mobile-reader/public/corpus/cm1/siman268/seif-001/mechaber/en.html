The law of acquisition through one's courtyard and four cubits. It contains five seifim.
A found object that belongs to its finder is not acquired until it reaches his hand or domain. Merely seeing it, even falling upon it, does not acquire it; if another takes possession, that person acquires it. Rama: This applies in a public domain. In a place where one's four cubits acquire for him, he acquires it, and falling upon it does not diminish that acquisition.
