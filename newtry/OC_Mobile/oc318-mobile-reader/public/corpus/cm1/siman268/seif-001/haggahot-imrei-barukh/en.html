(Shach, seif katan 2) For regarding one's courtyard as well, ye'ush without knowledge is relevant. Note: See Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Metzia 23, passage beginning "because of the owner's ye'ush," where the Ritva asks why the owner of the press should not acquire the item by means of his courtyard. This shows that in the Ritva's view the owner of the press could have acquired it if the courtyard were guarded. We must say that he holds that acquisition through the courtyard begins only when the owner despairs, so that it comes into his possession permissibly.
