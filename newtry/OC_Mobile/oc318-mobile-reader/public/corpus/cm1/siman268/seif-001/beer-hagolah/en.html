The language of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 17 of Hilkhot Gezeilah VaAveidah.
The Mishnah there, 6a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara gives two explanations. Rambam states the law without qualification, apparently relying on the first explanation: since he fell upon it, he revealed that he wanted acquisition through falling, not through the four cubits. This implies that even in a place where four cubits would acquire, his fall prevents acquisition. That is the Gemara's explanation, etc.
