With a heses oath. Rashbam explains that this is the ordinary rule for property presently in his possession. Although Shevuos 42b gives the reason of possible temporary evasion, that reason alone cannot suffice, for otherwise he should be believed even regarding a future claim. Rather, his present possession is also decisive.
Even if there are witnesses. Ritzba gives this answer in Hagahos Maimoniyos to Tosafos' question, s.v. "and if he claims." When witnesses saw that he gathered the produce, he is not believed if he gathered it outside the owner's presence. This accords with the Rif in Kol HaNishba'in, in his tenth gate.
