Rashbam and Tosafos explain so, and the Gemara implies this by stating, "If he claims and says, 'I entered for the produce,'" in connection with Rav Nachman's statement that the produce is returned, rather than attaching it to all the earlier cases where the land itself is returned. Rashbam's statement that Rav Zevid's ruling is an independent matter means only that it does not refer back to the earlier claim, "I bought it from you and consumed the produce."
