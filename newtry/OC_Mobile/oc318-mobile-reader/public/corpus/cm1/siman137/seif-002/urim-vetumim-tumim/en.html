And even if he already gathered them, etc. He is not believed even concerning produce he already ate. Rashbam holds that if there are no witnesses that he ate the produce and he himself says, I ate the produce for three years and the land is mine by purchase, we do not extract the produce from him because he has a migo that he could have denied eating it. Ramban asks that his purpose is to establish ownership of the land, so how can he rely on the alternative plea that he did not eat at all, which would destroy his own chazakah? We do not recognize a migo that weakens the claimant's present case. Ramban answers that because there are no witnesses to the alleged eating, this is not considered a migo that weakens his claim, since he knows the court will not rely solely on his statement about the eating. Rashbam alternatively explains that he could have claimed only a minimal amount of eating sufficient to establish a chazakah. See Ramban's novellae at length.
