We protest. The reason is that a tree is not ordinarily destined to be cut down. We therefore presume that he entered without permission and intended to damage the owner, and we protest his conduct. It follows that where a tree is intended for cutting, we do not protest. The Mordechai in the chapter Kol HaNishba'in writes likewise. See Darkei Moshe. Sema. The Shach writes that the Raavad holds that we do not protest even in the former case, and this also appears from Chiddushei Ramban.
