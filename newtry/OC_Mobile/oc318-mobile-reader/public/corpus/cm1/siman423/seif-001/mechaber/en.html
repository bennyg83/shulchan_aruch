One who strikes a pregnant woman and causes miscarriage, and how fetal damages are assessed. It contains four seifim.
One who strikes a woman and causes her to miscarry, even unintentionally, must pay the value of the fetuses to her husband and bodily damage and pain to the woman. The value of the fetuses is assessed by comparing the woman's market value before miscarriage with her value afterward, and the difference is paid to the husband. Pain and bodily damage are assessed according to the additional weakening and suffering caused by miscarriage through a blow as compared with ordinary childbirth. Lost time and medical expenses are also paid where applicable. If the husband died, the fetal payment goes to his heirs. If the blow occurred after the husband's death, some say even the fetal payment goes to the woman, while others say it belongs to his heirs.
