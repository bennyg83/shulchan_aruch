To the woman. In Even HaEzer siman 83 it is written that the compensation for injury and depreciation is divided between the husband and wife. There is no contradiction. There the subject is permanent injury, such as severing her hand or another limb, by which she becomes unable to perform labor, so the husband has a share in the compensation. Here the discussion is of bodily weakening that does not permanently diminish her labor. The husband's loss is only the compensation for unemployment while she is ill, and that payment belongs to him. Sema.
