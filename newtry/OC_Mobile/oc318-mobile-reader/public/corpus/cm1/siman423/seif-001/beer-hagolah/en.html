The wording of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 4 of Hilkhot Chovel, from the Mishnah in Bava Kamma chapter 4, end of 48b.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is explicit there, 49a, in the baraita concerning one who strikes a woman. 'Damage' means the reduction in her bodily value caused by the injury. Rashi explains similarly that the damage here is ordinary bodily injury, whose rules are fixed in Ketubot, as explained in Even HaEzer siman 83. Rambam follows the plain meaning of the baraita.
