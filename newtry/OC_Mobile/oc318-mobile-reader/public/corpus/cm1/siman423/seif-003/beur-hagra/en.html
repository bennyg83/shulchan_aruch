If she was, etc. See Beer HaGolah and Tosafos 43a, s.v. "even," etc.
It belongs to her. This follows his view in seifim 1 and 2 and the Gemara's question, "a divorced woman also," etc. Here the husband has no share.
And some say, etc. The Tur writes that according to the Rosh, since the woman herself does not acquire the value of the fetuses, following Rabbeinu Chananel as above, the rule differs. His words are difficult, for even while the husband is alive the damager may be exempt in the relevant circumstance. So too according to Riva's explanation of the Mishna, "if she was," etc. See Tosafos 42b, s.v. "if she was." Tosafos rejected that interpretation only because the sugya does not appear to follow it. I later found that Yam Shel Shlomo also questions the Tur and writes that Riva's explanation can stand because Rabbah follows his own view that in such a case the woman acquires. Rabbah therefore had to explain the Mishna like Rashi, whereas according to Rabbeinu Chananel the Mishna may be explained like Riva.
