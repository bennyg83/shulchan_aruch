“And he shall not pour half above and in the place where he is exalted, he shall be seeded down.”
They compel him for his olive press. This applies even to partners. Even according to the opinion at the beginning of siman 164 that where a house ceiling collapses the upper owner must repair it, this case is different. There the lower owner does not need the ceiling because the roof protects him from rain. Here there is no separate roof, only the vault of the olive press itself. Sema. See Beurim.
