He must tend to it so that it not deteriorate. The Rambam at the beginning of chapter 14 writes that this is learned from the verse "and you shall return it to him," meaning, see to it how you return it to him intact. This is unlike the Ir Shushan, who derives it from the word "ox," written to include even the shearings of its tail although they are not presently worth a perutah, so that they may grow to that value. That interpretation of "ox" appears in Tosafot and the Rosh, but it addresses a different rule in the Gemara. The Ir Shushan mixes the Rambam's source for the duty of care with Tosafot's exposition concerning future value. See the Tur and Mechaber immediately below.
