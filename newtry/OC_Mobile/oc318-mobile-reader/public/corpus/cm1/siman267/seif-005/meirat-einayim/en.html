And the fraudulent person, even though, etc., until he brings witnesses that it is his. In the following seif Rama writes that a fully distinct sign is sufficient even nowadays. There I cite the Rosh in language showing that even a person definitely known as fraudulent receives the object upon a fully distinctive sign. Rama's phrase "nowadays" therefore should not be taken narrowly as referring only to people presumed possibly fraudulent. The same rule applies even to a definite fraud. The present requirement that a known fraud bring proof is the Rambam's position alone.
