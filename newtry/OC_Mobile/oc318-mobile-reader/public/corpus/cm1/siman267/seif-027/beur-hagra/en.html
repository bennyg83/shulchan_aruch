The conclusion concerns one who claims a larger animal, and the Yerushalmi distinguishes between denying that two oxen were found and admitting that two were found but claiming that both were returned. In the former case the Torah excludes the claim because the objects are separate; in the latter the Sages instituted an oath. The halacha follows Rabbi Yochanan. See 75.
