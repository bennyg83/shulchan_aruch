There, chapter 13 of Hilkhot Gezeilah VaAveidah, law 2, from the Mishnah in chapter 5 of Gittin, 45b.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam rules according to the plain Mishnah, unlike Rabbi Yitzchak in the Gemara there, 51a, who follows a baraita. This is also the Rif's view, since he cites only the Mishnah. Although he rules like Rabbi Elazar there, 51b, he does not cite Rabbi Yitzchak's statement or the other explanation there. This is primary.
