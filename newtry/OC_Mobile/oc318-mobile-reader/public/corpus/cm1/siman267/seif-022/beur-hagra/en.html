And if their hire, etc. As explained above, this applies even to the shearing of its tail and similar minor produce.
(Supplement) He appraises their value, etc. Rashi explains that in every case he sells through beit din. Tosafot explain that in some cases he may appraise the item for himself, as with tefillin. Rif and Rambam distinguish cases where self-appraisal is impossible because the care would exceed the value, and therefore require sale through beit din. (End of supplement.)
