The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam rules like Rav Yosef, who says that one guarding lost property has the status of a paid bailee, in chapter HaKones, 56b, and in chapter Elu Metziot, Bava Metzia 29a. The Rif likewise rules so. Some disagree and say he is like an unpaid bailee, but Rambam's view is primary.
See the Beit Yosef, who writes that in his view the Rosh rules like Rav Yosef, as also appears from a responsum of the Rosh.
