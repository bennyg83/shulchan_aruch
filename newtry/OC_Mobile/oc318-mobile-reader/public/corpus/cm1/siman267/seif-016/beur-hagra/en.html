All this, etc. The discussion in Nedarim follows Rav Yosef, as does the exposition in Bava Metzia. The Mishna concerning one who lends on collateral also treats him as a paid custodian, and the Gemara there assumes Rav Yosef's rule. Since we rule like Rabbi Akiva, this supports Rav Yosef.
Some say, etc. Others rule like Rabbah against Rav Yosef. They explain that Rabbah agrees that the finder occasionally gains Rav Yosef's peruta, but since that benefit is uncommon it does not make him a paid custodian. The collateral cases can be explained as negligence, and several passages indicate that the halacha follows Rabbah. See 72.
