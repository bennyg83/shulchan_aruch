Although with a charity collector who changes coins into dinars the Sages said that he must exchange them with others and not with himself because of suspicion, and likewise regarding one who rents out a pledge for the benefit of the debtor, where it produces substantial rent while suffering little depreciation, the Tur and Mechaber write at the beginning of siman 72 that he rents it to others and not to himself, this case is different. A finder returning lost property is presumed trustworthy and is not suspected. According to the Mechaber, in seifim 21 and 22 as well a beit din appraisal should be required. It requires study why this was not stated there. Sema.
