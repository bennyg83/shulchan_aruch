Shin) This is the language of the Tur. The Maggid Mishneh likewise explains Rambam's phrase "he should not study from them initially." He further cites the Rashba in the name of the Ramban that this law was stated only regarding a Sefer Torah, Nevi'im, and Ketuvim, where one familiar with them need not handle them repeatedly. But now that people commonly write Gemara texts, one who has reviewed his chapter one hundred times and one who has never seen it are alike in needing to handle and examine the volume, because it requires greater concentration; therefore one may study from it initially.
