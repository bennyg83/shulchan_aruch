In Rambam, chapter 13 of Hilkhot Aveidah, law 10, the wording is 'he announced or informed,' and so in the Tur, as written above in seif 3 concerning a time when coercive authorities are present, etc.
He said, “This is a reality.”
“If you are not close to your brother, and you don’t know him or her brother.”
From here it appears that he has not acquired it, which is difficult because at the beginning of siman 260, seif 9, the Mechaber explicitly writes that he acquires it and need not return it. One must distinguish that there the case concerns an item without an identifying sign, whereas here it concerns an item with a sign, where we must be concerned that its owner may arrive at any moment. Therefore it remains deposited until Eliyahu comes. The Shach likewise rules so.
