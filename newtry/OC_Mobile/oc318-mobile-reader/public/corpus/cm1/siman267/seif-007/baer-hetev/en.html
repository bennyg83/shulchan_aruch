They are. Meaning, we rely on them even according to the Rambam and Mechaber for one who brings witnesses that he is not fraudulent, or who is a Torah scholar, for a Torah scholar is presumably not fraudulent. So writes Rabbeinu Yerucham. Thus writes the Sema. The Shach writes that he did not find this in Rabbeinu Yerucham, nor did he find anywhere that a Torah scholar need not bring witnesses that he is not fraudulent. Nevertheless, the conclusion is independently compelling. Shach.
