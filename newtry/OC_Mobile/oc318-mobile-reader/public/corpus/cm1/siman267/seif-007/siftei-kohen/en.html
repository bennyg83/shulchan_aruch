Distinctive identifying marks, etc. See Sema, seif katan 10, who writes that these are relied upon even according to the Rambam and Mechaber when the claimant brings witnesses that he is not fraudulent or when he is a Torah scholar, and that Rabbeinu Yerucham writes likewise. I did not find this in Rabbeinu Yerucham, nor did I find anywhere that a Torah scholar need not bring witnesses that he is not fraudulent. Nevertheless, the conclusion itself is necessarily correct.
