And for his own need. The reason is that he may forget the garment on the bed and it will be stolen. Although regarding books the finder is permitted to read from them because this also serves the books' own preservation, that case is different. Since he is actively using them, we are not concerned that he will deliberately transgress and use them in a manner the Sages did not permit, as explained below in seif 20. Spreading a garment, however, involves no continuing act, and he may simply forget it there. Shach.
