But he should not place the vessel upon the fire. This is Rashi's language in the Gemara. The Tur quotes Rashi as explaining that he should not leave the hot water upon the fire. At first glance this implies that prolonged leaving is prohibited, but a brief placement might be permitted. In the Perishah, however, I explained that the word "leave" is not literal and that even placement itself is prohibited. The Rambam too states the law without qualification, in the same manner as the Mechaber.
