He announces it in synagogues, etc. This supports the custom of announcing thefts and other public matters in synagogues while the community is gathered. It appears, however, that such announcements should be made between Minchah and Maariv or after the congregation leaves the formal prayer, so as not to interrupt public prayer. The practice of some who announce between Ashrei and Lamnatzeach is improper. Although the order of Kedushah is a distinct unit, immediately after the Kaddish following Tachanun the congregation begins that unit by saying Ashrei. It is better to announce before Ashrei begins, and best after the entire prayer. Maharik writes that if one publicly announces in the synagogue that a lost object should be returned under the ban of Rabbeinu Gershom, and some people say the matter was entrusted to them in confidence, one should place under ban anyone who seeks to defeat Rabbeinu Gershom's enactment. Even an oath not to disclose does not help. See my comments at the beginning of siman 28 regarding a similar rule in testimony.
