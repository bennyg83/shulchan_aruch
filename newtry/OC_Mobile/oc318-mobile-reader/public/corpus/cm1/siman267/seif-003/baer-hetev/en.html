He announces. See Orach Chaim, siman 306, seif 12. Maharik, shoresh 110, writes that if an announcement was made in the synagogue requiring the return of a lost item under the enactment of Rabbeinu Gershom, and some people say that the matter was entrusted to them in confidence, one should place under ban anyone who attempts to nullify the enactment of Rabbeinu Gershom. Even if he swore not to reveal the information, that oath does not help him. See what I wrote at the beginning of siman 28 concerning a comparable case regarding testimony. Sema.
