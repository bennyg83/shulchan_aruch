[Sema, letter 38] And it is possible that he relied. Note: See what the Shach wrote in siman 72, seif katan 31, that only with money received for a lost object are there those who hold that he is a paid custodian. But if one deposited a utensil with another and later gave him permission to use it, since he expressly granted him permission, his position is worse and he is liable even for unavoidable accidents.
