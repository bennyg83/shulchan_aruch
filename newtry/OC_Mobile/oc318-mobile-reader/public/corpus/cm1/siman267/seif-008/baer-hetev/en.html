Remain. The Darkei Moshe writes this in the name of the Hagahot Maimoniyot. In my humble opinion, however, the Hagahot Maimoniyot discusses only the law stated in the Gemara and codified by the Tur and Mechaber in the following seif; the present law is not mentioned there at all. Perhaps, since this claimant does not know the object's identifying marks, it is evident that the witnesses are false, unless the circumstances are such that he would not have been expected to know them. This requires study. Shach.
