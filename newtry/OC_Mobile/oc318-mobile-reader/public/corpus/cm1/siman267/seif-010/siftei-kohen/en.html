And he should leave it, etc. This applies even if the witness testifies that he saw it fall, as stated by the Rosh and Tur. See Responsa Maimoniyot, Sefer Mishpatim, siman 6. See Sema, seif katan 12, who writes that one witness is regarded as though he were absent, meaning that the claimant need not swear against the witness, because even after he swears the object is not given to the one who swore. This is also the view of the Nimukei Yosef, and the Bach rules likewise. The Hagahot Mordechai at the beginning of Shnayim Ochazin also implies this, and Maharam of Tiktin likewise wrote in the margin of the Rif, contrary to the explanation of the Rosh. This is the primary ruling.
