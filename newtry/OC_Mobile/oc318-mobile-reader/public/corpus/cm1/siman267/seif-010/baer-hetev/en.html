That he does not. This applies even if the witness testifies that he saw the object fall from him. So write the Rosh and Tur. See Responsa Maimoniyot, Sefer Mishpatim, siman 6. The Sema writes that the claimant need not swear against the witness, because even after such an oath the object would not be given to the one who swore. The Nimukei Yosef agrees, the Bach rules likewise, and this also appears from the Hagahot Maimoniyot at the beginning of Bava Metzia. This is the primary ruling. Shach.
