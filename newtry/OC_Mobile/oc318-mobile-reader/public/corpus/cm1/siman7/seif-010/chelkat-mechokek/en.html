Rather, we permit her immediately. Ketubot 23a concludes in the baraita: they permitted her, and afterward witnesses came and said, 'We do not know,' she does not leave. Rif and Rosh, however, read: and afterward witnesses came that she had been taken captive. Rashi's text also appears to have been this way, for he explains that now the prohibition no longer rests on her own mouth, yet nevertheless she does not leave.
