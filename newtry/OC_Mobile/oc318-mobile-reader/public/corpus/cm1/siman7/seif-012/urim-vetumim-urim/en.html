Unless they have another Torah scroll. If another Torah scroll is available in the same synagogue, no formal withdrawal is needed, because there is no meaningful personal benefit in reading from this one rather than that one. If the other Torah scroll is only in another synagogue, the judge must remove his personal interest either by arranging that another scroll be brought and used in this synagogue or by committing himself to pray and read regularly in the other synagogue. Similarly, if the litigation concerns a synagogue and there is another synagogue in town, the judge must withdraw by committing to use the other synagogue. If he continues attending the disputed synagogue, the availability of another one elsewhere does not eliminate his benefit. Sema, against Ir Shushan.
