In the name of Maharshal of blessed memory, in the Tur Through “and a convert is disqualified from judging an Israelite,” etc. Yet according to the Rosh in the chapter Mitzvat Chalitzah, a convert may adjudicate monetary law even for an Israelite who accepted him, or without coercion and compulsion, and if he is an expert he may judge so long as his authority does not derive from gentile rule. The Tur likewise writes this in Yoreh De'ah, siman 269. It is therefore difficult why this was not mentioned here, since the Mishna’s statement that all are qualified to judge is expressly explained as including a convert, even one whose mother is not Jewish, and even to judge an Israelite, contrary to the Rif and Rambam.
