A woman is disqualified from judging. See Responsa Pri Tevuah, siman 46. Birkei Yosef, letter 12, writes that although a woman is disqualified from serving as a judge, a learned woman may issue halachic rulings. This is also evident from Tosafot according to the explanation that Devorah taught the laws to the people. So too Sefer HaChinuch, although in mitzvah 83 he agrees that a woman is disqualified from judging, writes in mitzvah 158 concerning intoxication and the prohibition against issuing rulings, “and likewise a learned woman who is fit to issue rulings.” See Shaarei Teshuva to Orach Chaim, siman 461, seif katan 17.
