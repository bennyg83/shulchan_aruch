And even though he had drunk from foreigners and so on, and in the ways of Moses, he wrote to Moses that he was an artist from Israel when he began to discuss Israelis:
Each one of them is sued in one of his eyes as a minister, and so on, even with a judgment. And in retirement, I wrote the taste of the name of the Rashi that the whole world ends at night and in one of his eyes, a fiery vision per day, because of the most part, Dan Affith in a judgment:
Blind in both eyes is disqualified. Since everyone agrees that a court may not adjudicate at night, for such a person daytime is like night. The Ir Shushan derives the disqualification from the analogy between monetary law and the laws of tzaraat. That is imprecise. Sanhedrin 34b says the mishnah using that analogy is an individual opinion and is not accepted. One who derives from that analogy holds that even blindness in one eye disqualifies. Ir Shushan also strained to explain why blindness in one eye is valid and tied one source to another improperly. I wrote my objections elsewhere.
