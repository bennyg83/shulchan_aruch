A beit din of. The Midrash at the beginning of Devarim states that judges require certain qualities and lists them. The Rambam counts wisdom and discernment as one quality, and humility as the seventh, based on the phrase 'with you,' meaning judges resembling Moshe Rabbeinu, who was more humble than any person.
