Hatred of money and love of truth, etc. Rambam and Tur explain more fully: "Hatred of money" means that even concerning their own money they are not panicked by loss and do not chase after wealth. "Love of truth" means that they naturally pursue justice, love truth, hate robbery, and flee every form of wrongdoing. "Beloved by people" means that people are comfortable with them, which comes from having a generous eye, humble spirit, pleasant companionship, honest business dealings, and gentle speech. "People of good reputation" means that they are strong in mitzvot, exacting with themselves, and conquer their inclination so that no disgrace or bad reputation attaches to them, their past conduct is honorable, and they have courage to save a poor person from one who robs him. See the Perishah for the verses from which these qualities are derived.
