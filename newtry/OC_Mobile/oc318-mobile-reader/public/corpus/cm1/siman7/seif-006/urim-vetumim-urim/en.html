And they deliberate among themselves. Bach explains that the assembled scholars deliberate only whether the Torah scholar is entitled not to stand before that particular beit din or judge. Sema's first explanation is more plausible: the scholars gather, examine, and decide the actual case. Once the entire body of scholars is assembled, there is no humiliation in the Torah scholar's appearing before them. Sema's second explanation, that they arrange a compromise, is less satisfactory. It would be a desecration of Heaven's name if a leading scholar received distorted justice merely to preserve his dignity. Therefore the first explanation is primary, and Knesset HaGedolah likewise reads "deliberate" as actual judgment.
