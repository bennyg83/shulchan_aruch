Further in the Beit Yosef, s.v. “One who is blind” “In one eye,” etc., through “Rabbi Yochanan said, to include,” etc. Why did he not answer, according to the Rif and Rambam, that the inclusion is a convert whose mother is not Jewish, who may testify, as stated in the chapter HaChovel, but may not judge? Because such a convert may judge another convert. The text therefore chose an unqualified case. Ramban and Rashba answer differently, as does Ritva in Kiddushin 14. My explanation appears correct.
