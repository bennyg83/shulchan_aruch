[Rama] Therefore, it is permitted for him to serve as a judge when this party selects him. Note: Even if he is actually his friend, so that by law he would be disqualified, nevertheless, if Reuven selects a friend of Shimon, Shimon cannot disqualify him, and this is not comparable to a relative. If Reuven selects one who is his own enemy, there is doubt whether Shimon can disqualify him. Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 2.
