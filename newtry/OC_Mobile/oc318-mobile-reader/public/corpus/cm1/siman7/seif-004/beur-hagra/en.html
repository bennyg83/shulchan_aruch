A woman. Niddah 49b, "anyone who is qualified, etc.," and a woman is disqualified from testimony, etc., at the beginning of Shevuot HaEdut. The Yerushalmi, chapter 3 of Sanhedrin and chapter HaDayanim, states: "Since a woman may not testify, a woman may not judge." See Tosafot, Shevuot 29b, s.v. "Shevuot, etc." The Yerushalmi at the beginning of Shevuot HaEdut states: "We have learned that a woman may not judge; consequently a woman may not testify, etc." See also chapter 3 of Sanhedrin, halacha 9, and Tosafot in Shevuot 29b and Niddah 51, s.v. "Kol," regarding the Yerushalmi, etc. (End of collected note.)
