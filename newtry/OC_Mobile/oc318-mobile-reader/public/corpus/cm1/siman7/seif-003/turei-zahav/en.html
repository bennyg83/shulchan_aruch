Seif 7: One who placed his fellow under a ban, etc. The Sema, in seif katan 24, writes that this is derived from the tenth chapter of Kiddushin. He apparently alludes to the incident of the man who disparaged Rabbi Yehuda. Rabbi Yehuda placed him under a ban, and when the man responded that Rabbi Yehuda was a slave, Rabbi Yehuda publicly declared him a slave. This would imply that after imposing the ban, Rabbi Yehuda adjudicated the man's status as a slave. In truth, there is no proof, because the rule there was explicit, as Shmuel said: Whoever stigmatizes others does so with his own defect. It appears that the Darkei Moshe left the matter requiring study for this reason.
