He is a man in Guberin and he has the power to save the poor immediately, and he is a Hashem of the learners who should be the same as the column in this section:
From age thirteen and above he is fit. This applies even if he has not produced two pubic hairs. Although witnesses require two hairs, there Scripture insists upon the status of "men," as it says, "the two men shall stand." A judge depends rather upon sharpness and legal knowledge. Therefore the Tur writes in the name of an opinion that if he is incisive and expert in the chambers of Torah, he may judge. Below age thirteen he is disqualified because he has no halachic status of majority at all, and the Torah does not impose commandments or authority upon minors. Although we say that anyone disqualified as a witness is disqualified as a judge and vice versa, apart from one who loves or hates a litigant, the disqualification of minority exists in both fields according to the requirements of each.
