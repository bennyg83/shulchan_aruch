Blind in both eyes is disqualified. Birkei Yosef writes that if he is not actually blind but merely has weakened vision in both eyes, meaning that his eyesight is impaired but he is still able to see, all opinions agree that he may serve as a judge. He further writes that according to the majority of authorities who disqualify one who is blind in both eyes, one may explain accordingly Rav Nachman's statement to Rav Sheshet in Ketubot 94b, “For one thing, I am a judge and the master is not a judge.” See there.
