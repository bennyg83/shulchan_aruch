Some say. Shabbat 56b.
And he brought in a hair. A lot of women:
And some say. This is from the Yerushalmi.
(Anthology) Some say, from age, etc. Yerushalmi, chapter 4, halakhah 9: 'What is the difference between monetary cases and capital cases?' Rabbi Abahu said in the name of Rabbi Yochanan that even one under twenty who has not developed two pubic hairs is fit for monetary cases but not for capital cases.
(Anthology) And some say, etc. They understand the passage concerning Yoshiyahu in accordance with Rashi's interpretation in Shabbat there.
