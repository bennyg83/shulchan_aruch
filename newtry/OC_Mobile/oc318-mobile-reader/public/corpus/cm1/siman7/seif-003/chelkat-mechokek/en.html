And likewise a minor who spoke innocently. This is the Rambam's language in chapter 18 of Hilchot Issurei Biah. It implies that any minor, even one who is not her son, is believed only if speaking innocently. The Maggid Mishneh has already observed that the Gemara does not appear to say this. Responsa Rosh, rule 32, cites the Yerushalmi that even a minor and a relative, even her son, is believed, without mentioning any requirement that the minor speak innocently. As I wrote above, seif katan 1, the Rif's language likewise implies that the first baraita is not accepted and that everyone is believed except her maidservant.
