In the same passage, through “and to contradict” “Even a witness signed on the document,” etc. Rashba certainly does not dispute Baal HaItur. Baal HaItur discusses judging matters concerning the substance of the document, which would be as though the witness retracts or supplements his testimony after signing. Rashba, however, discusses a witness signed on a document serving as a judge for validation of that document, for the reason Rashba gives in responsum 969.
