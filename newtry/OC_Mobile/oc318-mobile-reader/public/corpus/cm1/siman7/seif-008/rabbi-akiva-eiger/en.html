[Shulchan Aruch] They are disqualified from judging. Note: If one of them is found to be a relative or otherwise disqualified, all of them are disqualified. See Knesset HaGedolah, glosses to the Tur, letter 33. See also Knesset HaGedolah, siman 36, glosses to the Tur, letter 6; Tumim, siman 46, seif katan 28; Responsa Maharam Alashkar, siman 15; and Responsa Ginat Veradim, Even HaEzer, klal 1, siman 2.
