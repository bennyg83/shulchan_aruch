Even if he has not reached twenty years. Urim VeTumim in siman 7 writes, based on the verse 'gather yourselves and gather,' to distinguish regarding judges between land and movable property. It may be said that this principle need not apply specifically to the very subject being adjudicated. Since the principle applies to movable property, perhaps such judges are valid also for land. See Ketzot HaChoshen. Alternatively, because adjudicating land requires greater knowledge of commercial matters, and judges must be skilled in reasoning, a minor is not fit. Yet regarding reasoning it is expressly written that two hairs before the usual age have the same status as ordinary signs. The requirement that one be twenty regarding his father's estate is a separate rule and not based on reasoning ability.
