A convert is disqualified. In Ketzot HaChoshen he questions how a convert could ever judge his fellow under Torah law, since by Torah law ordained judges are required. This is no difficulty, for even appointment as an official over the water channel is prohibited by Torah law. Accordingly, since his judicial authority is rabbinic, it is considered an appointment. Such an appointment over a Jew is prohibited by Torah law, whereas appointing a convert over another convert is permitted, because the prohibition against appointing a convert applies only to authority over Jews, not over his fellow convert.
