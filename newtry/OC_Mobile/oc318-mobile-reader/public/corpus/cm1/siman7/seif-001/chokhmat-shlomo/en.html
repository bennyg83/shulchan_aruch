Seif 7. In the gloss: 'And some say that an actual enemy is one who has not spoken with him for three days.' Note: See my responsum to Yoreh Deah, sixth edition, and my responsum to the community of Stanislav, printed in Yoreh Deah, siman 1, where I wrote that nowadays, when people commonly speak one way with their mouths and another in their hearts, false speech has become widespread in this regard. Therefore, whenever it is evident that he is truly his enemy, even if he speaks with him, he is considered an actual enemy and is disqualified from judging his case. See there.
