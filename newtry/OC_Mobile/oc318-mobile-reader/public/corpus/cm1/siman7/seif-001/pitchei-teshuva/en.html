His fellow convert. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written that this means an actual convert. If, however, both his conception and birth were in holiness, he is a full Jew, and for the judge it is necessary that his mother be Jewish. See Tosafos, Yevamos 102, s.v. le'inyan. With respect to a judge, conception and birth in holiness are not sufficient. See there in the Maharshal, and see Responsa Mahara Sasson, end of siman 66. End of quote. See Birkei Yosef, who discusses this matter at length.
