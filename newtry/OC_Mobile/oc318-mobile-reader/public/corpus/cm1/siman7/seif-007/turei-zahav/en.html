(Further in the Beit Yosef, s.v.) "A witness does not become a judge," etc., until "but if he did not testify," etc. This implies that even if they originally intended to testify or were designated as witnesses, the rule applies. This is unlike what the Kesef Mishneh writes in chapter 3 of Hilchot Sanhedrin, that Rambam's view follows Rashba. That cannot be correct, for if so Rambam's rulings would contradict one another.
