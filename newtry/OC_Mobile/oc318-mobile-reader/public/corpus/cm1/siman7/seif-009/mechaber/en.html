All who are disqualified from testifying because of kinship or transgression are disqualified from serving as judges. {Rama: See simanim 33 and 34 for the details of disqualification from testimony; the same applies to judging. The judges may not be related to one another or to the witnesses (Ran, chapter 2 of Kesubos; Responsa of the Rashba, siman 790). See also siman 33, seif 17.}
