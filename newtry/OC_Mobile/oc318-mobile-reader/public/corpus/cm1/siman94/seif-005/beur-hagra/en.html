When he rolls matters onto him... if he wishes... As stated in the Yerushalmi, Rabbi Zeira told him, "Either give him all that he claims from you, or swear to him regarding everything he rolls onto you." This is so even though he had already admitted the first liability. One cannot explain that Rabbi Zeira meant he should swear only on the gilgul, because then there would be no need to say "either pay." Rather, the meaning is that paying the two dinars alone does not discharge him. He must either satisfy the entire demand or swear concerning all of it.
Even after... Otherwise he cannot retract and choose to swear once he has refused the oath, as stated at the beginning of Perek HaDayanim, where if he says, "I will not swear," they immediately proceed against him. Tosafos cites Rashi to this effect and the Rosh agrees. See above, siman 22 seif 3 in the gloss.
That he did not... This too is learned from the Yerushalmi, where he admitted the debt. There is no distinction there between whether he already left beit din or not.
