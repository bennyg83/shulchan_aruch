The Tur and Mechaber write the same immediately below in seif 8 regarding a Torah oath. We do not say that this rule applies only to a Torah oath because such an oath cannot be reversed. The reason in both cases is that, in any event, all the claims that the claimant wished to attach by gilgul will be adjudicated. Ibid.
