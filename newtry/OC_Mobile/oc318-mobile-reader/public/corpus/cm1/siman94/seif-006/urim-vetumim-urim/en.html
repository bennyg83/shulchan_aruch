Swear concerning the rolled claims. The reverse is likewise true: he may swear concerning the principal matter and reverse the rolled claims onto the other party. Sema. This must be understood according to what I wrote above in seif katan 11: absent the rolled oath, although both parties make definite claims, there is no independent hesset because he was exempted from oath or the like. Only through rolled oath is there an oath here. Otherwise, since this seif speaks where both parties make definite claims and reversal is possible, even without rolling the matter he could already impose hesset upon the other party. Taz struggles with this unnecessarily; the same answer given above applies here as well.
