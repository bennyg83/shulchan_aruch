If when he became obligated... Meaning, although the Yerushalmi at the end of Shevuos recounts a case before Rabbi Zeira where he obligated a litigant to swear about two dinars, and the litigant replied that he did not owe two dinars because they had already been paid, whereupon the other party added additional matters and Rabbi Zeira told him either to pay everything claimed or swear to every matter rolled onto him, that case involved claims initially raised by the plaintiff. The wording there indicates that the plaintiff had asserted the additional matters before the defendant's admission.
