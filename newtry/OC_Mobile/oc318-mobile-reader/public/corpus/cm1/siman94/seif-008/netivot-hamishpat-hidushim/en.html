And concerning the matters added by extension she shall swear. The Shach disagrees. Since an oath imposed through extension is also Torah law, it cannot be reversed. See Beurim: if he says his conscience troubles him and he cannot swear, then where the underlying claim is a loan he may reverse the oath. But where the issue is repayment, such that if he were to say, "I do not know," he would have to pay, he cannot reverse it.
