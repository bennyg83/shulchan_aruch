The wording of the Rambam in chapter 1 of Hilkhot To'en, law 13. The Maggid Mishneh writes that in the Halakhot, chapter Kol HaNishba'in, the Yerushalmi records the case of a certain person, etc., and rules likewise according to the opinion in the Yerushalmi, chapter HaMafkid, that when they see litigants rolling additional matters into the oath, they take account of them, meaning the defendant must swear concerning everything or pay. The Tur likewise writes this in seif 9 in the name of the Rif.
The Maggid Mishneh infers this from Rabbeinu's use of the word "admissions." He writes that some halachic authorities likewise ruled this way, and the Ramban and Rashba agree.
