One should not object that if the additional claims are definite, the claimant could independently impose a hesset oath even without extension. The case can be one where the defendant had been exempted from oaths, as above in siman 71:5; one who claims, “I had a prozbul and lost it,” as in siman 67:33; one who says that interest was taken permissibly, as discussed above in siman 82, Sema seif katan 33, and Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 169; or similar cases.
