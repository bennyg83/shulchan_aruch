He makes a definite claim. Meaning that the matter added by gilgul shevuah is based on a definite claim. When the Mechaber says that he "became obligated in an oath," it means that he became obligated to swear to the claimant from another source. Likewise, "an uncertain claim" means that the matter added by gilgul is uncertain. See what I wrote in siman 75.
