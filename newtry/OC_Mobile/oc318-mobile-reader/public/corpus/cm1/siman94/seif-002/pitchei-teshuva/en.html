From definite claims. See Baer Heitev, who writes that one can find such a case where he exempted him from an oath, as stated in siman 78, seif 5. This means according to Nimukei Yosef and the Ramban cited by the Shach there in seif katan 14. The view of the Shulchan Aruch, however, is that if he exempted him from an oath he cannot make him swear even by gilgul, as the Shach explains there. See what I wrote above, siman 93, seif katan 9, and see what is written in Tumim concerning this.
