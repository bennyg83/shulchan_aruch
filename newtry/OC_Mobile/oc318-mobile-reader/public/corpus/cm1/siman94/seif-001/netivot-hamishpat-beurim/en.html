And there is one who wrote. See Shach, seif katan 2. There is a textual error. It should read: “And certainly according to Rabbeinu Yitzchak, whose view we follow, that gilgul applies to all those who swear and collect.” Later in that same seif katan it should read: “According to all the poskim who hold that gilgul may be imposed,” and similarly Nimukei Yosef writes.
