And he is exempt from swearing. This is because an uncertain claim is not imposed by extension, as stated above in seif 2. It is difficult, however, because above in siman 93:13 the Tur and Mechaber quote Maimonides that one may impose by extension an oath that the defendant does not know. One must distinguish that case because the principal oath there is rabbinic, as I explained in siman 72, seif katan 47, according to the Tur. See also siman 298.
