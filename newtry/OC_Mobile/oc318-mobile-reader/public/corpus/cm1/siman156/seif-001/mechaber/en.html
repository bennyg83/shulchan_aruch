The law governing one who enters another person's trade, and one who brings merchandise to another city. It contains seven seifim:
A resident of a closed alleyway who wishes to practice as a physician, craftsman, bloodletter, weaver, document scribe, or teacher of children in secular studies may be prevented by the other residents of the alleyway, because he increases the traffic of people entering and leaving. Even if all consent except one, that one may prevent him. Likewise, one who owns a house in a jointly owned courtyard may not rent it to any of these practitioners. Rama: Some say he may sell it, after which the residents may litigate with the purchaser. He may not sell it to a non-Jew who will not obey the judgment of beit din.
