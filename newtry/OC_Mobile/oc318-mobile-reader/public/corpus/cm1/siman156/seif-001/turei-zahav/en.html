[Seif 1, a physician or a craftsman]. It appears that even in the Rambam the phrase “a physician, a craftsman” means “a physician or a craftsman,” for he concludes, “he may not rent it to a physician or to a craftsman.” The Sema asks that if the Tur intended a mohel, he later concludes, “and certainly for a mitzvah,” and so forth. I am astonished what his interpretation of “a physician of persons” resolves, for is that not also a mitzvah? It appears that the Tur therefore specified mitzvah activities such as distributing charity or praying with a quorum, to teach that the rule applies specifically to a mitzvah dependent upon assembling people, such as those examples. Where he can go to the homes of those who need his service, however, the courtyard residents may prevent him from practicing as a mohel when the arrangement would cause many people to come to his house. This appears correct in my humble opinion.
