The language of Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilkhot Shekhenim, law 11. Maggid Mishneh cites the baraita on Bava Batra 21b. Rambam's version reads "one of the residents of the alley," consistent with his earlier position that residents may object even when none of them practices the same occupation.
Maggid Mishneh also cites other commentators whose version reads "one of the residents of the courtyard." According to them, in an alley, if none of the residents practices the same occupation, they cannot object. Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash appears to explain according to the first version. Tosafot likewise write in the name of Rashba.
