A ban establishing a right of residence. Meaning that the townspeople place a ban among themselves that no one will house a newcomer, and that no one will transact with a person who comes to reside without communal permission. The ban itself does not directly bind the newcomer, since under strict law the community cannot exclude him, unless it is a teacher's ban upon his students. Sema:
And they claim an established right. Such a claim may be adjudicated even on the basis of hearsay testimony and testimony from persons ordinarily disqualified, because in any event all local residents are financially interested in the matter. Sema:

But Reuven is forbidden to lobby the ruler for such a privilege.
