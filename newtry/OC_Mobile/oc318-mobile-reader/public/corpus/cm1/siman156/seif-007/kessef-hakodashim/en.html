Rama. (To close the door.) Rama writes that local residents may petition the ruler to prevent additional residents from coming. Beit Yosef cites this from Maharik, and Rama copied that ruling. Rama, however, apparently did not see Beit Yosef's later work Bedek HaBayit, where Beit Yosef disagrees with Maharik and writes that this should not be done without authorization of beit din. It is possible that had Rama seen Bedek HaBayit, he would not have copied in the glosses an opinion from which Beit Yosef himself later retracted, especially because the later ruling ordinarily carries greater weight. The fact that Shach gives no indication of this Bedek HaBayit does not prove otherwise. It appears that Shach did not complete the second portion of Choshen Mishpat with the same settled, comprehensive treatment as the first portion, but only began notes there with the intention of later producing a fuller work. Had he completed it as planned, he might well have cited Bedek HaBayit here as the halachic ruling.
