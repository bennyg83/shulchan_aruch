And the law concerning a chazakah of residence is judged like the law of chazakah in land. Darkei Moshe writes that Mordechai, chapter Chazakat HaBatim, cites a case in which one maintained a right of residence alongside another for three years. Maharam ruled that this did not constitute a chazakah because the owners themselves were also living there, while others disagree. Their dispute depends upon the case in which the claimant said, 'I was living in the inner rooms,' whose law was explained above in siman 140. See what I wrote there in seif katan 4.
