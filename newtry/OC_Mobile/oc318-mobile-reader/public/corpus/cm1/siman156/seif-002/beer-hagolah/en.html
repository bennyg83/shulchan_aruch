This too is there, law 12. Maggid Mishneh writes that it is the Mishnah on Bava Batra 20b.
Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam apparently holds that this applies specifically after the use has been established. But if a neighbor objects at the outset, he may prevent it. He derives this because hammer noise interferes with sleep more than people entering and leaving, and because the Mishnah says "a shop in the courtyard," implying that the shop already exists. As explained above in siman 155, seif 35, according to Rambam chazakah for damages does not require three years; once the neighbor saw or knew of the activity and did not protest, the right is established.
