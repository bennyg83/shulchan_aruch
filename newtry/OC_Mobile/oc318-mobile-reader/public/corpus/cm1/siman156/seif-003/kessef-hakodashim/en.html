He cannot prevent him. Just as an artisan cannot prevent another artisan from competing where the newcomer bears the local tax burden, so too regarding the customary liquor trade or similar licensed business: one who bears the local tax is not considered as unlawfully entering another's trade. The same applies to renting or selling one's house to another liquor seller next to an existing one. If he cannot find a Jewish purchaser and sells it to a non-Jew, we have not found a prohibition.
