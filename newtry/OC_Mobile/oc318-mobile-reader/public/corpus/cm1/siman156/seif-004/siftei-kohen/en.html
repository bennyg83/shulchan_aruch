Many authorities say that the residents of a courtyard, etc. The Rama follows the Beit Yosef at the end of seif 1, who writes that this law does not accord with the other authorities. He means that in a closed alley, even if they did not object to the first person, they may object to the second. In my humble opinion, this requires study, for it is possible that the other authorities agree with the Rambam on this point and simply did not discuss it. It also requires study why the Rama compares a courtyard to an alley in this respect. See the responsa of the Rama, siman 9, first principle, and simanim 73 and 75, and the responsa of Maharam Padua, siman 41.
