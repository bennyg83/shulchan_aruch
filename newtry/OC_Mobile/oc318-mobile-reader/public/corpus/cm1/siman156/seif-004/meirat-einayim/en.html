My son is crying. R. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. M. A.D. is a man who is not afraid to protest him because many of them are in and out as a “S.A.” and here we have even learned whether he has no benefit to it:
They come and go to purchase. The correct text is: "They come and go to purchase, and they remained silent; he has not thereby acquired a right regarding this, and they retain the right, etc." This is explicitly the Rambam's wording in chapter 11. The meaning is that even though they remained silent and he acquired a prescriptive right to continue practicing his trade in his own house or shop, they may nevertheless object to the incoming and outgoing customers. Prescriptive use does not help regarding that separate harm. The Mechaber already wrote this in seif 2.
