If they wish to reverse their oaths. The Sema explains that each party may ask the other to take both the oath originally imposed upon him and the reversed oath. Thus Reuven may tell Shimon: “Swear that the article was sold to me and be exempt, and also take the oath I could have reversed upon you, that it was not pledged to me, and I will pay the three dinars you claim.” Or Shimon may make the converse demand. The Sema says this prevents evasion through a single ambiguous oath. In my view this is unnecessary, because every oath must in any event state the claim clearly. Rather, the parties may agree to reverse the respective oaths as a procedural arrangement, but neither can compel the other to accept a compound formulation that changes the substance of the original burdens.
