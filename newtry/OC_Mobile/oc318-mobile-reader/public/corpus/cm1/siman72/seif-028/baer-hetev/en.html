Reverse. The Sema explains that Reuven says to Shimon: 'It is upon you to swear that it was sold to me and thereby be exempt, and additionally take my oath in reverse, swearing that it was not pledged to me, and I will give you three dinarim'; or Shimon says to Reuven similarly. This procedure is used to prevent evasive manipulation through one oath. The Shach explains differently: Reuven says, 'The oath that it is a pledge in my hands rests on me, but I do not wish to swear. I reverse it so that you alone swear that you sold it to me, and I will give you three dinarim.' Shimon likewise says, 'You alone swear that it is in your hands as a pledge for one sela, and I will give you one dinar.' The Bach explains similarly, and the Shach adopts that reading. The Taz explains in essentially the same manner.
