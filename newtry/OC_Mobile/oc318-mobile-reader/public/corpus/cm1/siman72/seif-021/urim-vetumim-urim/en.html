He is not believed to say. See Tumim, where I brought proof against the Shach that even where there are no witnesses of current possession and he therefore has a migo, he nevertheless has no chazakah in the property. The item remains legally in the possession of its original owner, and his migo would be a migo used to extract property from that prior ownership.
And if he established possession. The Sema explains that this refers to slaves and not to animals. See below, siman 135, for what I wrote there.
And he shall swear a hesset oath, etc. See Tumim. The reason he swears only hesset that they were transferred to him in satisfaction of the debt, and not an oath while holding a sacred object as under the Geonic enactment, is that this clause concerns slaves, as the Sema explains. The Geonic oath does not apply to them at all. Even with ordinary collateral, the Sages did not enact a sacred object oath in this particular claim.
