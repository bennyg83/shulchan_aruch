And if he possessed them for three years, etc. The wording appears to apply also to the animal mentioned there. This is the view of Rashi and the Tur below in siman 135. Baal HaIttur and Rambam, however, disagree. The Mechaber in Shulchan Aruch rules like them there in siman 135, seif 2, writing that three years of possession are effective regarding a slave, but he does not state this concerning the animal discussed immediately beforehand. See the Maggid Mishneh on Rambam, whose words I cited there in the Perishah and Sema. Since this is not the principal place for the law of presumptive possession of movable property and animals, but is mentioned only incidentally, the Mechaber wrote generally here, 'if he possessed it for three years.' Each authority applies that statement according to his own view. According to Rashi and the Tur it also applies to an animal, while according to Rambam and Baal HaIttur it applies only to slaves. The Mechaber relied on what he writes in the proper place, siman 135. See there.
