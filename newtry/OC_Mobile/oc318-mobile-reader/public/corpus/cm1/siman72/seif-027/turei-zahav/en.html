(Ibid.: All the Geonim ruled, until “Rabbi Yitzhak’s rule applies only when the pledge was taken after the loan, and this requires study.”) There is no difficulty. To the extent of the debt, everyone agrees the lender is a paid custodian even when the pledge was taken at the time of the loan, for the Mishna expressly states that one who lends on a pledge is a paid custodian and the discussion there concerns a pledge taken at the loan. Rabbi Yitzhak’s statement as explained by Tosafot concerns the special proprietary effect needed for returning a pledge and for betrothing a woman with it; that effect exists only when the pledge was taken after the loan, as the Rif and Rosh explain in Kiddushin. Paid-custodian liability, however, applies even to a pledge taken at the time of the loan. The Ran states this expressly according to Rabbi Yitzhak in Shevuat HaDayanim.
