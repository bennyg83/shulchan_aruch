And he swears hesset. The Sema explains that a Torah oath of custodians does not apply because the parties dispute only the amount of depreciation. The Shach objects and writes that even where the depositor claims with certainty that the custodian was negligent, this does not necessarily create the ordinary oath of custodians. The primary explanation is that even with a definite allegation, the oath of custodians can apply, but here the oath is being used to collect rather than merely to exempt, and therefore it is not a Torah oath.
