A lender who claims. Specifically where he makes a definite claim, as written in seif 14. Sema and Shach.
(Likkut) A definite claim, etc. Specifically where his claim is definite, for then it is like other monetary claims and the borrower is believed through a migo that he could have said, 'I have nothing of yours,' because a custodian's oath cannot be used to extract money. This is unlike a doubtful claim, as written in seif 14. End.
