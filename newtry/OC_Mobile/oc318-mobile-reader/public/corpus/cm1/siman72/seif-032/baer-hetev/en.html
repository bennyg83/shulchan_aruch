He then swears and collects his debt, because at this point he is only an unpaid custodian: he has not yet lent the money, and he is not obligated to air or shake the object because its owner is in the same city and can do so himself. Nor can we say that it is as though he expressly stipulated that losing the pledge would cause him to lose his debt, because when the pledge was lost he had not yet lent anything. Shach.
