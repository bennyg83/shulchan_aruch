If Reuven had money held by Shimon, Levi asked Reuven to lend it to him, Reuven agreed and received collateral, and Reuven then instructed Shimon in writing to give Levi the money, which Shimon did, then if the collateral is lost in Reuven's possession, Reuven has the law of a lender upon collateral and is responsible for guarding it. His responsibility begins only when the money reaches Levi. If the collateral was lost earlier, Reuven is exempt. Therefore, if Reuven swears it was stolen before Levi received the money, Levi must return the money. If Reuven is uncertain, the money is not removed from Levi.
