And Reuven became obligated in guarding the collateral. Meaning, according to each authority's view. According to those who hold that he is a paid custodian, he is responsible for guarding the collateral even with respect to the amount by which its value exceeds the loan. According to those who hold that he is an unpaid custodian, he is responsible for the collateral up to the amount of the money lent, as explained above in seif 2. See there.
And if the collateral was lost. Meaning, if it was lost before that time, etc. According to the opinion that he is a paid custodian, the reason is that at that earlier time he was not obligated to air and spread out the collateral, because its owner was in the same city and could air it himself. After the loan, however, the lender does not trust the borrower to take the collateral back into his hands merely to air it. This is explicit in the Tur at the end of this siman, and I also cited the Tur's wording in the Sema there.
