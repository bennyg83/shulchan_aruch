If the lender denies ever receiving collateral while both parties admit the loan, the lender takes a heses oath that no collateral was left with him. The borrower takes a heses oath that he is retaining property of the lender equal to, or greater than, his debt. Even if that property is worth more, the lender is exempt from paying the excess because he already swore concerning it. If it is worth less, the borrower swears to the value of his collateral and pays the balance.
