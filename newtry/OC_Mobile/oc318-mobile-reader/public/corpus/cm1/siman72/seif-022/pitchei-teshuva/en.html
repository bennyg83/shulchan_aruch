And he takes his pledge. See Baer Heitev, where the wording is reversed. The correct law is that the borrower first takes his pledge and afterward takes a hesset oath. Dagul Merevavah writes that below, seif katan 110, Baer Heitev cites the Shach himself as saying that if the borrower first takes the pledge, the case is no longer one of immediate payment and becomes a partial admission, requiring a Torah oath.
