Seif 39. 'Because Levi told him: my partner Shimon owes him.' Note: It appears to me that this applies specifically where Shimon has no knowledge at all whether Reuven owes the partnership or the like. In that case one partner cannot seize. But if Reuven also asserts with certainty that Reuven owes the partnership, then not only may he seize for his own share, but his seizure is effective even for his partner's share. Since they are partners in this debt, and we rule that each partner may demand the entire debt, each is considered a litigant regarding the entire debt, as though the whole debt were his. His seizure therefore takes effect for the entire debt. See siman 71 regarding credibility and the Shach there. Here it appears clear that the seizure of one partner is effective to collect the entire partnership debt, and he swears and collects all of it.
