The lender, etc., and he is exempt. See Shach, and Menachot 109a, where conduct is treated as if one expressly stipulated, etc.
(Anthology) The lender, etc., and at the time, etc. This requires study from Rosh Hashanah: one who says, 'I undertake a burnt offering on condition that I not be liable for it,' still violates the prohibition of delay. According to this opinion, he could even destroy it, which is difficult.
