(Anthology) One who lends, etc., if, etc. He remains obligated to air it out, and there is the perutah of Rav Yosef. See Sema and Shach. This follows Rosh's approach. According to Ramban, Rashba, and Ran, however, even collateral taken at the time of the loan is acquired under Rabbi Yitzchak's rule, and the sugya there is merely a rejection, as several sugyot prove. Since for this reason the mishna says he is a paid guardian, he should be exempt here even before the due date, as the gloss states according to the opinion that he is an unpaid guardian. This is not so, however, because he is at least no worse than one who finds a lost object, as the sugya states there, although Ramban writes that this reason is unnecessary.
