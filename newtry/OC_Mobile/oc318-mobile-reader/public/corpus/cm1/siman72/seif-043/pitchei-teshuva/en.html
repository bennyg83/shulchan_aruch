Ketzot HaChoshen disagrees with the Shach. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, siman 57, regarding one who lent another one hundred gold coins on collateral, repayment to be made in installments of twenty five every quarter. After three installments had been paid, leaving only twenty five due, the collateral was lost while in the creditor’s possession. The judges maintained that the seventy five already collected need not be returned, following the plain meaning of Rama, seif 43, and the unqualified ruling of the Sema. Although the Shach challenged this, he did not present his view as compelled or as a settled ruling. For that reason Tumim did not reproduce it, and Ketzot HaChoshen expressly disagrees with it. The rabbi who asked the question sought to uphold the Shach for other reasons. Chatam Sofer rejects those arguments at length and concludes that there is no basis to require the creditor to return what he already received. See there.
