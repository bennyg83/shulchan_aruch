If a borrower repays a loan secured by collateral and asks for it, but the lender says, 'Go now and return tomorrow, and I will give it back,' and it is stolen, then if it was stolen before the appointed return time, the lender is liable; if afterward, he is exempt. Therefore, if witnesses establish that the borrower came at the appointed time and the lender did not return it, the lender is liable. Without witnesses, the lender swears the borrower did not appear at the appointed time and is exempt. {Rama: This follows the view that a lender upon collateral is a paid custodian. According to the view at the beginning of this siman that he is an unpaid custodian, he is liable only for negligence.}
