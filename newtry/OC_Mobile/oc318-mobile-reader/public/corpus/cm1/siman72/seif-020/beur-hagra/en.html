The one in possession of the collateral, etc. Sefer HaTerumot cites proof from the Yerushalmi in Ketubot, brought by Rosh, that recovery is limited to the value of the collateral. The latter opinion holds that this limitation applies only when extracting property from another, not where the creditor is already in possession, since had he claimed purchase he would have been believed and is therefore believed through migo. The Ketubot case proves nothing because the debt there was known; here the first opinion may still be correct.
And some say that the law. Sefer HaTerumot cites this in Ramban's name. Ramban interprets the Yerushalmi as concerning the rent of a widow's dwelling, and Rosh rules similarly. Rosh proves that the possessor prevails from Ketubot 91b: the passage implies that unless he is paid the entire debt he cannot be removed, even though Rava holds that a purchaser may ordinarily discharge a creditor.
