That it was worth only this amount. He must at least admit that even that amount exceeds the debt. Then he is a partial admitter and must swear. Likewise, where he says he does not know the value, the case means he knows with certainty that it was worth more than the debt but does not know how much. He then must swear and cannot, so he pays. If he does not know at all whether it exceeded the debt, the lender swears that he does not know and is exempt, because it is a case of 'I do not know whether I owe you.'
