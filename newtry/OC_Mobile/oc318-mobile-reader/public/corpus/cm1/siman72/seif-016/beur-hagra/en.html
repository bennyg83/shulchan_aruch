(Collected note) The lender... This entire seif concerns a case where the item in the money changer's possession is worth more than the debt. See the Sema. (End of note.)
But... and if. As stated in seif 12. (Collected note) And if the lender does not know... This refers to the phrase "but... and the lender..." He does not know the exact value, but admits that it is not worth less than the item in the money changer's possession. So explains the Shach. (End of note.)
