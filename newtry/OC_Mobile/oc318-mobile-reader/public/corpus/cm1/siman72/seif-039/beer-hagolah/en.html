n) He explains there that a migo applies only to a claim the person himself could have made. A claim another person could have made does not create a migo for him, because perhaps that other person would not have wished to lie by saying, "There never was such a matter," or "I returned it to him." See Terumat HaDeshen, siman 335, concerning Levi who deposited property with Reuven and Shimon, and Levi demanded his deposit from them. Reuven answered, "I spent such-and-such according to your written instruction," and Shimon made the same claim, saying that separately he too spent money according to Levi's writing. Levi disputed the amount of those expenditures. See there.
