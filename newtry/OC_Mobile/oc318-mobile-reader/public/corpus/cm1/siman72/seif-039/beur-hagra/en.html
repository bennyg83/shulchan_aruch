When does this apply. See Baer HaGolah, and as stated in Bava Metzia 11a.
(Anthology) When does this apply, etc. This follows Tosafot's explanation there. Rambam explains it like Rashi, and rules likewise in siman 275:28. Nevertheless, there is no practical difference, as Tosafot write regarding Rashi's explanation. One must say that the case is, etc., as Maggid Mishneh also writes on Rambam there. The only difference is in explaining the Gemara: Rambam adopted the explanation that it refers to the pledge, while Tur here adopted Tosafot's explanation that it refers to the owner of the courtyard.
