When he gives. The Sema notes that Responsa Rosh and the Tur read "when he gave." According to that reading, the incident was that Shimon re-pledged the books to Levi for the amount he had lent Reuven, and when doing so directed Reuven to Levi and told him to redeem them from Levi for that sum. The Mechaber's wording "when he gives" appears to be an emendation based on the Rosh's conclusion that Shimon was leaving the city and therefore deposited the pledges with Levi until Reuven came to redeem them and pay Levi the amount of Shimon's loan.
Authorized me. The Shach understands the case as one where Levi claims only that Shimon personally authorized him to study from the books. If, however, Levi claims that Shimon told him that Reuven had authorized whoever lent on the books to study from them, Levi is exempt, even if no deduction from interest was stipulated. The Shach further inclines to exempt where Reuven authorized Shimon and Shimon in turn authorized Levi, contrary to the Ir Shushan. He cites proofs and refers to Responsa Maharshdam 79 and 101.
