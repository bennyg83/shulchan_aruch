When he gives him the money. In the Rosh's responsum, and in the Tur that cites it, all editions read: 'when he would give him the money for which they were pledged.' In the Perishah I wrote that according to that version, the facts must have been that Shimon repledged the books to Levi for the same amount that Shimon had lent Reuven. At the time Shimon pledged them to Levi, he directed Reuven to Levi and told Levi to allow Reuven to redeem them for that sum. The Mechaber writes, 'when he gives him the money,' apparently because he had a different reading. The Rosh concludes in the responsum with the wording, 'when he gives him the money,' and earlier, in Levi's claim, the text reads, 'and he instructed me that when you give me the money.' The words 'he instructed me' imply that Levi spoke as Shimon's agent. The actual circumstances were that Shimon needed to leave the place where he lived, so he left the collateral with Levi and said it should remain with him until Reuven came to redeem it and pay the amount Shimon had lent him. This is clear.
