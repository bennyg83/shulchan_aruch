If Reuven claims from Levi books he had pledged to Shimon, saying Shimon directed him to Levi to receive them upon paying the money, and when Levi produces the books Reuven says they were damaged in Levi's possession and demands compensation, while Levi says the person from whom he received them authorized him to study from them while they remained with him, Levi is liable. Because he knew the books belonged to Reuven, he had no right to follow Shimon's authorization and must compensate Reuven for all damage caused through his use.
