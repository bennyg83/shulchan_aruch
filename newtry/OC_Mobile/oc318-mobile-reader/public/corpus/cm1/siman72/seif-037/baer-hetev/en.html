Mortgage. Even where the mortgage is for a fixed term in a place where the debtor cannot redeem it early, if the convert dies during the term the holder has accomplished nothing. So writes the Bach. The Shiltei Giborim, however, writes that this is true only in a place where early redemption is allowed. In a place where it is not, the land is effectively sold into the convert's possession until that time, and when he dies his lien is not extinguished; therefore whoever first takes possession acquires it. The Bach's view appears more correct. Shach.
