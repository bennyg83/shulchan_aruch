[Shulchan Aruch] One who lends to another upon collateral. Note: See the Taz, who writes that because the Mechaber omitted the rule that even where the lender needs the collateral he performs a mitzvah and becomes a paid custodian under Rabbi Yosef’s principle, and the Rif and Rambam likewise omit it, this implies that they hold that according to the view that a lender is a paid custodian, he certainly acts for his own benefit. However, the Nimukei Yosef and Baal HaMaor state explicitly that he is a paid custodian even when he needs the collateral. See there. Tiferet Shmuel also states the matter exactly this way. See Shach, seif katan 11 and seif katan 36.
