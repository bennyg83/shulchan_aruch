Seif 10. 'The lender says: I lent you a sela upon it,' etc., 'the lender shall swear,' etc. Note: I am uncertain whether, if the lender brings one witness who testifies that the collateral was lost from him, we say that the lender is exempt from this oath, which is not imposed upon one who merely asserts a definite claim, since we rule that a supporting witness exempts from an oath. In addition, the concern that the lender might later produce the collateral after the borrower swears does not apply, because one witness is believed in matters of prohibition, and with respect to this concern we should likewise rely upon the witness who testifies that it was lost. Or perhaps, since there is a concern that God's Name may thereby be desecrated through a false oath, we do not rely on one witness for such a concern. This requires study. For now I have found no explicit ruling on this point.
