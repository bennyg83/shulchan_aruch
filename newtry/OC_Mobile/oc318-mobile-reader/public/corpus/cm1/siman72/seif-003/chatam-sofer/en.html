There, Sema, seif katan 50, 'however, they must swear.' Note: The same applies when the lender is alive and no one makes a definite claim against him: he must swear. This is explicit in the Rambam's wording and likewise in the Nimukei Yosef. Nevertheless, where he is also holding a document, it is possible that he is regarded as one who is already in possession, as the Sema writes below in siman 108, seif katan 16. See Ketubot 85a regarding 'do you have witnesses that he demanded it from her,' etc.; see there. See also what the Sema writes in this siman, seif katan 49, in the name of the Ran. It is possible that in such a case, where there is no definite claim against him, one should rule accordingly.
