The lender may compel him, etc. The poskim write this concerning Shmuel's statement cited by the Tur at the beginning of this siman: If one lent another a thousand zuz upon the handle of a sickle as a pledge and the handle was lost, his money is lost. That applies specifically when the pledge was lost. So long as it still exists, the lender may compel the borrower to pay him the thousand gold coins and then return the pledge to him. See there. See also the beginning of siman 73 and seifim 12 and 13, where the law concerning a pledge equal in value to the debt is stated, including when the lender may compel the borrower to redeem it.
