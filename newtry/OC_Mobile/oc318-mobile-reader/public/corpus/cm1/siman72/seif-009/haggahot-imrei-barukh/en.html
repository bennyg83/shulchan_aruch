(Rama, seif 18) Rather, they had seen it earlier, etc. Note: See below, siman 193:3, where I noted that the Rashba's novellae cited in Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Batra 46 concerning the deceivers of Pumbedita do not accord with the Rosh's responsum, siman 106, which proves the rule later cited in his name in siman 133 from that sugya. According to the Rashba, if the artisan claimed, "you fixed two for me," before he showed the item to witnesses, and then took it out and showed it, he is believed by a migo. The Pumbedita deceivers are different because they originally denied the entire matter, and after that they were no longer believed to claim, "you fixed two for me."
