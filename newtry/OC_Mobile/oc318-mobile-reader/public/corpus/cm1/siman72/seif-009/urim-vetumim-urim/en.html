The custodial oath that it is no longer in his possession. Even though the lender pays by forfeiting the corresponding portion of his debt, he must nevertheless swear that the collateral is not in his possession, lest he have coveted it. See what I wrote above in Tumim, seif katan 46. This oath is called a custodial oath, but it appears that this particular oath is only rabbinic.
