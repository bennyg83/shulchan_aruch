Collateral belonging to a Jew. Even if it was taken after the time of the loan, when we rule that a creditor acquires a possessory interest in collateral, he nevertheless has only a lien upon it and not full ownership. The Shach is uncertain whether, according to Rashi's view that the creditor's possessory acquisition makes him liable even for unavoidable accidents, we would still say that he has only a lien. In Chiddushei Rashba to Bava Kamma it is explicit that even according to Rashi, the creditor's lien terminates upon the death of a convert who has no heirs. See there.
