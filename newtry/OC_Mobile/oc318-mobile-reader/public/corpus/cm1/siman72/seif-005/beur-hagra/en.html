The community is not liable. They do not have the status of a lender with respect to the collateral, because they owe one another nothing; each person merely pays his own share. See Bava Kamma 113b and Rashi there.
Nor is the communal trustee. Although he is an administrator and an administrator can be liable, that is only when acting for the beneficiaries, not for the public generally, as shown in Bava Kamma 39a. Moreover, an administrator is liable only for negligence as an unpaid guardian, not as a paid guardian, as indicated in Gittin 52b. Finally, the basis for an administrator's liability is the need to preserve his reputation for reliability, which does not apply in the same way to communal trustees.
