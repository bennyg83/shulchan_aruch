He is merely putting him off. If he later sells the object to another person in the owner's presence and the owner remains silent, he can no longer protest and say that his earlier words were only meant to delay. This is like the case of stones removed in the owner's presence. See Tumim for the distinction between this rule and the law below in siman 336 concerning an employer who tells a worker, 'Take what you made as your wages,' where if the item is already in the worker's courtyard the employer cannot retract.
