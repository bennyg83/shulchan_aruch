Merely postponing him. This law requires study. Nimukei Yosef derived it from the rule concerning a worker told, "Take what you produced as your wages," that the employer may retract so long as the worker has not removed it, contrary to the Rashba, because the statement was merely a delaying tactic. But the Tur and Shulchan Aruch themselves rule in siman 336, seif 2 like the Rashba that once the item is already in the worker's possession the employer cannot retract. It would seem the same should apply here to a pledge, and it is difficult to distinguish. Nimukei Yosef himself explicitly makes the two rules dependent on one another and says that according to the Rashba, if the owner later seeks to reclaim the pledge he is not heard. The matter requires study. Shach.
