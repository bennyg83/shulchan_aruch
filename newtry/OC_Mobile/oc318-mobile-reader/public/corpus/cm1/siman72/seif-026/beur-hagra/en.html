(Anthology) One who, etc. In Bava Metzia 118a, Rashba infers from the omission of 'take your expenses' in the latter clause that clearing is required only in the first clause. Since stones are not ordinarily removed immediately, the owner's reliance becomes complete. Ran disagrees, arguing from the Gemara's statement that both clauses were necessary, which implies that they are equivalent. Some derive from here the law of collateral, and Nimukei Yosef writes that according to Rashba the creditor acquires it immediately. In siman 336:2, Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule like Rashba. Bach and Sema offer a different distinction between a worker and a garden, overlooking Rashba's explanation. The matter still requires study, though the ruling may rest on Nimukei Yosef's concluding qualification.
