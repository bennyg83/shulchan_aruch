(Tur: Whether he lent produce or money.) According to the Rif and Rambam, who rule like Rabbi Yosef, they should also have ruled that the lender is a paid custodian even when he needs the pledge. Yet they omit that qualification. They apparently understood that the Gemara offered it only as a deflection to avoid making Rabbi Yosef’s rule depend on a tannaitic dispute. In truth, Rabbi Yosef follows Rabbi Akiva, and even Rabbi Akiva agrees that where the lender needs the pledge he is not performing the mitzvah that creates paid-custodian status. This answers Tosafot’s objection to the Rif. Baal HaMaor and Nimukei Yosef, however, state expressly that according to Rabbi Yosef he is a paid custodian even where he needs the pledge.
