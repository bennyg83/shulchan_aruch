He does not acquire it, because he merely puts him off. The Sema writes that the Tur and Rama state this law in siman 73. See there. In fact, however, it is not in the Tur. The law written here by the Mechaber genuinely requires study. The Nimukei Yosef derived it from his own view regarding a worker: if the employer tells him, "Take what you made as your wages," the employer may retract so long as the worker has not removed the item, contrary to the Rashba there, because the employer is merely putting him off. Yet the Tur and the Mechaber themselves disagree with that position below in siman 336, seif 2, and rule like the Rashba that if the item is already in the worker's possession the employer may not retract. It would therefore appear that the same should apply here regarding a pledge. It is difficult to distinguish between the case of a worker and the case here. Moreover, the Nimukei Yosef himself makes the two rules dependent upon one another and expressly writes that according to the Rashba, if the owner of the pledge later seeks to retract with respect to the pledge, we do not listen to him. See there. This requires further study.
