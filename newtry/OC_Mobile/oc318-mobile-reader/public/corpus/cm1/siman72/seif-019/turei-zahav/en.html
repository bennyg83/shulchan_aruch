(Seif 35) “Their father instructed that it was pledged for one hundred.” The Sema reads this too as a doubtful claim. That is difficult, for how can the borrower be required to take heset in the concluding case? In the preceding seif the heirs hold a document. Here they hold neither a document nor the pledge for the excess amount. It therefore appears that the phrase “their father instructed” is used precisely to make their claim certain, and the borrower accordingly takes heset.
