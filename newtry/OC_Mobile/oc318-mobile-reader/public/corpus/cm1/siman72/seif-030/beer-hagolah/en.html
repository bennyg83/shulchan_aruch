There is such a name in the gate that is mentioned in part of the G-d and so on
Baal HaTerumot writes there in the name of Rabbi Yaakov, from the statement, 'Let him say to his mother: go pay,' Bava Metzia 42b. The reason is that she is liable to her sons who entrusted it to her. Otherwise, anyone entrusted with a deposit could sell it to his wife, she could consume it and rejoice, and he would escape liability. This case is the same. If Levi cannot pay, Shimon who entrusted the item to him must pay in his place.
