Meaning even regarding the second dinar, as stated in the Yerushalmi cited by the Rif at the end of Shevuos: either give it to him, etc. Certainly this applies where the second claim arises independently rather than through gilgul. (Collected note) Therefore Shimon is one obligated to swear but unable to do so. The wording implies liability for both dinarim, since with the first dinar alone the principle would not be necessary. The entire seif comes to teach the law of a bailee who transferred the object to another bailee. The Shach's objection that one witness should not be more stringent than two is not decisive, because this opinion necessarily regards the secondary oath as attached once the first oath rests upon him. See siman 94, seif 7. If two witnesses established the first claim, no oath would be necessary on the second; but where one witness creates an oath on the first, he must also swear on the second or pay. So too siman 75, seif 19, "some say that since..." although two witnesses on the first claim would not require payment on the second. (End of note.)
