If Reuven pledged collateral to Shimon, Shimon repledged it to Levi or gave Levi his interest in it, and it was lost through unavoidable accident in Levi's possession, Reuven may demand the collateral from Shimon. If Reuven says it was worth one sela and secured only two dinars, demanding two dinars, while Shimon says it was worth three dinars and owes only one, Shimon partially admits. He must swear it was worth only three dinars and also that it is not in his possession and was lost unavoidably. He cannot swear to the accident because it did not occur in his possession, and Levi's oath cannot exempt him because Reuven may say Levi is not trusted. Shimon is therefore obligated to swear but cannot and must pay. The same applies if the collateral depreciated, the parties dispute the amount, and Shimon admits depreciation but does not know whether it was unavoidable. He may impose an unspecified ban upon anyone who knows the collateral was not worth the amount claimed. If witnesses establish that Levi guarded it properly and it was lost unavoidably, Shimon is exempt. Even without witnesses, if Reuven ordinarily deposits his property with Levi, Levi swears it is not in his possession and was lost unavoidably, and Shimon is exempt. {Rama: If Levi negligently lost it and cannot pay, Shimon must pay Reuven because Shimon is Reuven's contracting party, even if Reuven ordinarily deposits with Levi. See below, siman 291.}
