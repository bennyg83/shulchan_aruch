Shimon is therefore one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear, and must pay. See Sema and Shach. The primary ruling is as the Shach and Taz state, that he pays only the third dinar. What Tumim writes, that the reason is that he cannot take the oath that the collateral is not in his possession, is incorrect. Certainly, giving it into another person's hands is no worse than throwing it into the sea, in which case he pays only its actual value. Ketzot HaChoshen writes the same.
And Shimon is exempt. See Shach, seif katan 133, who writes that if there are witnesses that the collateral was lost through unavoidable circumstances, Reuven must pay his debt, and he concludes with uncertainty regarding whether this follows through Levi's oath. The Biurim writes that there is no doubt here: since Shimon has been exempted from responsibility for guarding the collateral, Reuven must pay his debt. See there. In my humble opinion, in light of what I wrote above in siman 66, seif katan 12, that collateral taken at the time of the loan cannot be sold in a manner transferring a personal claim against the borrower, but only so as to retain the collateral until the debt is paid, here, once the collateral is lost the lender's right is lost. This is specifically where the collateral itself was transferred as a gift. But where the collateral was pledged onward to Levi, so that Shimon is liable to pay Levi and Shimon in turn claims the debt from Reuven, there is indeed no doubt. Ketzot HaChoshen writes that with collateral one collects only from the collateral and not the underlying debt. I do not know his intent, unless he meant what I have written. As for his question that even in the first clause, where the collateral is in the hands of a person with whom the holder does not ordinarily deposit, the borrower should nevertheless have to repay Levi because Levi acquired the debt, there is no difficulty. The first party transferred to the second only whatever right would come into his own hands. Since Shimon, when unable to swear, cannot collect from the collateral, Levi likewise cannot collect from it.
