Even his son becomes a paid custodian. According to Mechaber, he is responsible for the entire value of the pledge because the mitzvah benefit of Rabbi Yosef applies: he must air and maintain it. According to Rama, the responsibility extends only up to the debt, under Shmuel's principle. Nimukei Yosef explains that he is a paid custodian because he holds the pledge against the debt. In Tumim I wrote that this too appears to mean Shmuel's principle. Value above the debt is not covered according to any view, and this is the practical law. If the father instructed his son to return the pledge for no compensation and it was later lost, the heirs are only unpaid custodians. Maharashdam and Shach, correctly. If the son was a minor, there is no custodial obligation at all because a minor cannot become a custodian.
