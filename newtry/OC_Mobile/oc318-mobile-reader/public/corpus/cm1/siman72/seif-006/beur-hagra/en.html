(Anthology) The lender, etc. Although Tosafot qualify the rule, Shach writes that their qualification applies only at the time of the loan, unlike the case of his son. Furthermore, according to Rif, he is in any event a paid guardian because of Rav Yosef's perutah, which applies to the son as well, since he too must air the object.
