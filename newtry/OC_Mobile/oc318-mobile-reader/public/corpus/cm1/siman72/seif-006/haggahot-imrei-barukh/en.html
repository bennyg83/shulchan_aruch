(Shach, same passage) This is the language of the Ran. Note: This requires study. The Ran on the Rif himself writes in the sugya of one who must swear but cannot and therefore pays, Shevuot 44, that if a borrower says, "I remember fifty, and as to the remainder I do not know," heirs are exempt in such a case because they are not expected to keep the matter so carefully in mind.
