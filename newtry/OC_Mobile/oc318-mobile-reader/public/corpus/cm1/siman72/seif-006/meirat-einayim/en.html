His son too becomes a paid custodian over it. Nimukei Yosef concludes that the reason is the benefit that he retains the collateral as security for the money, and the same is true of an artisan with the tool on which he worked. See below siman 89 and siman 306. According to the 'some say' cited above in seif 2 that the lender is only an unpaid custodian, his son likewise bears only the consequence of losing from the debt the value of the collateral.
