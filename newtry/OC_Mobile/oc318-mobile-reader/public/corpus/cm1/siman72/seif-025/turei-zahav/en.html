(Related to seif 3 above) “Judgment favors Shimon.” This implies without an oath. The wife is not believed to say that her husband pledged the objects without her permission, because we do not presume people to be wicked; the Beit Yosef cites the Mordechai accordingly. This is difficult, since in Even HaEzer 96, seif 14, the Rama rules that the husband is believed with an oath, and the Beit Yosef cites the Rashba similarly. The Beit Yosef also cites an anonymous opinion that even with an oath the husband is not believed; rather, the wife swears to the borrower. The principal practical ruling appears to be that the husband is believed upon taking an oath.
