Shimon is exempt. What difference does it make to Reuven whether Shimon borrowed the money or not? In any event the gentile became exempt once the collateral burned, because he was not liable for unavoidable accidents. Why should Reuven care whether Shimon profits? See Beurim: if Shimon does not know what happened and knows only that the gentile says it burned, Shimon must pay Reuven under Rabbi Natan's rule, because Reuven owes the gentile while the gentile is legally obligated to Reuven.
