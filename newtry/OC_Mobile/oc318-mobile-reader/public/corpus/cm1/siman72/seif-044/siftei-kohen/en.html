Shimon is exempt. Even without Shimon’s loan, the collateral would have remained in the non Jew’s house. Moreover, Shimon performed no act upon the collateral itself that would place it under his responsibility and make him liable for it. So writes Maharam there.
From paying Reuven the value of the collateral. Reuven cannot argue to Shimon that Shimon benefited because his own debt was discharged through Reuven’s collateral and therefore must pay him. Why should Shimon pay? If the burning of the collateral caused the non Jew’s lien to lapse under their law, that is not Shimon’s doing. Heaven favored Shimon. This is the language of Maharam there.
