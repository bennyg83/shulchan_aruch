(Anthology) For the borrower does not, etc. If the borrower certainly contradicts him, the lender swears hesset, as stated in seif 29, because a guardian's oath does not apply to a definite claim, as explained in seif 27. It is then treated like an ordinary monetary claim. Furthermore, a guardian's oath serves only to exempt, not to collect, as taught at the beginning of chapter 7 of Shevuot and chapter 9 of Ketubot. Even the oath imposed on partners, patterned after a guardian's oath, follows this rule. Therefore, the reason must be that it resembles the claim, 'You lent me,' etc., where one collects without an oath under siman 75:9; any oath here is only the guardian's oath.
