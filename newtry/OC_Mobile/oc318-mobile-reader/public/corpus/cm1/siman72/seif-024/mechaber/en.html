If Reuven borrowed seven dinars from Shimon against collateral and later said, 'I paid you two dinars once, another two a second time, and one dinar a third time,' while Shimon answers, 'I remember only the one dinar,' Reuven prevails. The collateral itself belongs to Reuven; when he claims it, Shimon effectively says he is uncertain whether any debt remains secured by it, and Shimon cannot assert a doubtful claim against Reuven's property. {Rama: See above in this siman, seif 14, note 12, and siman 259.}
