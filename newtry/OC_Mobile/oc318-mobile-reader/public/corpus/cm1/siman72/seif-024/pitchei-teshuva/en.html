See Baer Heitev, through his statement that if after saying that the garment belonged to so and so the possessor remained silent, etc. Dagul Merevavah writes that the unqualified wording appears to include even a spontaneous admission made before any demand. Yet below in siman 81, seif katan 56, the Shach discusses at length a person who admits spontaneously and holds that he can retract even after the time of immediate speech, based on the principle that the mouth that prohibited is the mouth that permits. Perhaps here too the Shach means a case where there had been a demand, or perhaps producing and displaying the object in beit din is different, though that distinction is difficult.
