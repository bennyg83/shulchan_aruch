And he gave him, etc. Bava Metzia 29a. Although Rav Nachman elsewhere says that if unavoidable accident occurred he is not liable, see Rosh's resolution: with a lost object he is already a paid guardian in any event, and the same applies here according to Rif and Shulchan Aruch. Rosh offers another resolution as well.
(Anthology) And he gave, etc. Without payment, as above, for otherwise he would not be a borrower. The ruling is expressed as merely possible because the two sugyot have several resolutions. Independently, here he may be deemed a borrower because permission to use is equivalent to taking possession, unlike seif 1, where he merely said that he may use it when the need arises.
