He has the status of a borrower and is liable. The Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe cite another responsum of the Rashba from which it appears that even if the lender already used the collateral, he does not become a borrower so as to be liable for unavoidable accidents, because all the benefit is not his. He is permitted to use it in consideration of having lent the money. They write that the Ran's responsum also appears to support this. It is surprising that the Mechaber and Rama stated the matter without qualification. Since both are responsa of the Rashba, they should have brought both. According to Rama's ruling in seif 2 that money is not extracted in a case of doubt, the same should be said here: the borrower is exempt from the amount of the debt, while the lender is exempt from the amount by which the collateral exceeds the debt.
