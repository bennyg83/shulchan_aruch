Give him permission. Not at the time of the borrower and the gift of the loan must be given to him permission, and he shall be recognized as a resident:
It is possible. The doubt may be that perhaps because of the loan already extended, the borrower now gives him permission to use the collateral, making him like a renter. With any other unpaid or paid custodian who is given permission to use the deposited item, he would be a borrower according to everyone. Shach. See Beurim, seif katan 19: the main doubt is where the borrower merely grants permission and the lender remains silent, whether silence counts as acceptance. If so, the same doubt can arise with an unpaid or paid custodian. If the custodian expressly protests, everyone agrees he is not a borrower. If he expressly agrees, everyone agrees he is a borrower. This applies where the owner said explicitly, "Use it for free." If he merely said, "Use it," we assume he meant for payment.
