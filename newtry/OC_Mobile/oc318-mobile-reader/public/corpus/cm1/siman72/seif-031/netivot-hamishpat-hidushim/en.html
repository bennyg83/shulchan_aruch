He must return it into his hand. This applies specifically to a borrower and lender. With other custodians, return to the owner's son counts as return. Sema. The Shach writes that there is no distinction between a depositor and borrower, and return is effective only to a wife who conducts the household business. Thus, if the custodian's wife was negligent and has no means to pay, according to the view in siman 291 that the custodian is liable where he regularly deposits with such a person, the same applies here, because this was not valid return. If the item was lost from her without negligence, he is exempt, because giving it to someone with whom the owner regularly deposits is no worse than ordinary entrustment to such a person. Tumim, and this is straightforward.
