He is called his father
The author wrote to the R. R. R.R. that it was so true that he would return to Duffy if he was a great son of a crime computer
So writes the Beit Yosef in siman 340 in the name of the Rashba, and the Rama there in seif 1 in the gloss. The Bach writes that returning a deposit likewise requires the owner's consent. Mordechai in chapter HaMafkid, cited by the Rama in siman 291 seif 21, says that where a depositary returned the deposit to the depositor's wife he is exempt. That case concerns a competent wife who conducts household business, as Mordechai explicitly writes, concluding that a person trusts his wife more and places all he has in her hand. The Rama writes this briefly, perhaps relying on what he wrote at the end of siman 120, that if a borrower repaid the lender's competent wife he is discharged. This means one who manages household business, as explicit in Responsa Rashba, siman 1090. We may learn the unstated from the explicit.
