It was even large only a small snorkel that would have worked in the Rashi answer:
He must return it to his hand. This applies specifically to a borrower and to a lender holding a pledge. For ordinary custodians, returning the deposit to the owner's son can sometimes count as valid return, as Rama states below in siman 291 concerning returning a deposit to the depositor's wife. Shach holds that even with an ordinary deposit the custodian is liable unless the recipient conducts the owner's household affairs. See Tumim, where I clarify a distinction between an unpaid and paid custodian, and all the more a borrower.
