For an ordinary sword of the world. The Tur and Mechaber write something similar below at the end of siman 378, in a responsum of the Rosh concerning one who damaged another person's property by means of a non Jew's mule that was in his possession. The Rosh concludes: if the non Jew causes him extraordinary loss because of this, the person who caused the damage cannot be charged that full amount, since he did not intend it.
