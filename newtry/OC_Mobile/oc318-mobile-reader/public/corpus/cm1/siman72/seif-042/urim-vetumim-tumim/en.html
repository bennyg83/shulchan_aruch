From the produce of the land. The Sema explains that all the produce is appraised at full value and credited toward the debt. He asks why the creditor should not simply consume all the produce under a deduction arrangement. The Shach answers that the mortgage term has already ended and the borrower can now remove him. This still does not resolve the matter. Why should the creditor be compelled to wait year after year, taking produce toward the debt? Let beit din appraise and award him the body of the land. The correct explanation follows the Mechaber's position that a mortgage arrangement is permitted only in the form of the Sura mortgage: after the fixed years end, the land returns without further payment. The novelty here is that the creditor is believed concerning the underlying debt although the land is in his possession under that Sura arrangement. Until the debt is repaid and the fixed mortgage years conclude, he consumes all the produce according to the predetermined reduced valuation. Thus "from the produce of the land" means according to the reduced valuation fixed by the mortgage arrangement, after which the land leaves his possession without money. The property is effectively leased to him until that time. This is clear.
