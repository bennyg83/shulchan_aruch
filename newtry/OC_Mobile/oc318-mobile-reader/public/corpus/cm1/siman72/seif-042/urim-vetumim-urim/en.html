The lender is believed with a hesset oath. Although we follow the Geonim that one who seeks to collect up to the value of a pledge must ordinarily swear while holding a sacred object, that applies to movable property, where Torah-level oaths generally apply. Land is different. There is no Torah oath concerning land at all. Therefore even one who seeks to collect against land through a migo, although he is technically extracting, takes only a hesset oath. Preserve this principle. It is an important general rule that applies in many places. Sema and Shach agree.
