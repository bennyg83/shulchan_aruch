He said, “D. Hashem’s promise of the land that Hashem does not bring land only from the ground to the ground is only a mortgage:
With a hesset oath. Although with ordinary movable collateral the lender must swear while holding a sacred object, that is because movable property can generate a Torah oath. Land does not generate such an oath. Therefore one who comes to collect through a migo, though he is taking property, swears only hesset. Sema and Shach.
