And he takes an oath according to the law of one who admits part of the claim, etc. This means a Torah oath. Although a Torah oath is not taken concerning real property, as the Tur and Mechaber state in siman 95:1, this case is different. Since he has not yet held the land for three years, the land has not left the borrower's possession, and since the claimant has no mortgage document upon it, his claim is comparable to an oral monetary claim, regarding which a Torah oath is taken when part is admitted. Alternatively, this case is different because both parties agree that the land is held as a mortgage and their dispute concerns only the amount of money. This is therefore not considered a claim concerning land. Rama likewise writes this in siman 95:3.
