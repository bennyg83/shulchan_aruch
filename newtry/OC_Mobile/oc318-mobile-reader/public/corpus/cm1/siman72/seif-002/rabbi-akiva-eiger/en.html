[Sema, subparagraph 5] That it was not damaged, alternatively. Note: This is puzzling to me. What difference does it make that he would be considered one who made unauthorized use of the deposit and thereby be disqualified from taking an oath? He claims that the item was not damaged and returns the deposited object itself. He is therefore not obligated in a custodian’s oath at all, since he claims that he is returning the deposit exactly as it was entrusted to him. This requires study.
