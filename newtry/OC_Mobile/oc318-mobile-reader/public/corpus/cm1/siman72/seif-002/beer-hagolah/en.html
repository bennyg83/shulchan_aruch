The language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 10 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, from the Mishnah there 80b, in accordance with the first Tanna.
The Tur, and so rule the Rif and Geonim. The Ran at the end of Shevuat HaDayanim writes in the name of the Ramban, according to the Geonim who hold that the Gemara's statement, 'Say that Rabbi Yitzchak said this only regarding collateral taken not at the time of the loan,' was merely a rejection. In truth, it applies even to collateral taken at the time of the loan. This also appears from Pesachim 30b and Gittin 37a.
Like the cited Mishnah in Bava Metzia, in accordance with Rabbi Akiva, and the baraita at the end of Shevuot 43a, in accordance with Rav Yosef, who says a finder of lost property is like a paid bailee because of the reward of performing a mitzvah. A lender holding collateral likewise performs a mitzvah, Shevuot 44b. So write the Rif and other Geonim.
According to the Gemara's interpretation at the beginning of 44a: where the collateral is not worth the amount of the money, all agree that Shmuel's rule, 'If the handle of a sickle was lost, one thousand zuz are lost,' does not apply. So rule the Rif and the other Geonim, and the Tur in their name.
This too follows the Rif and Geonim, unlike Shmuel. So write the Ramban and Rashba, as cited by the Maggid Mishneh.
