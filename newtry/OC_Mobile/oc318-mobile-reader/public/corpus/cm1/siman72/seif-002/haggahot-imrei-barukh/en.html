(Sema, seif katan 19) She would be believed with an oath. Note: See Even HaEzer, end of siman 73, Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel, and siman 90, Beit Shmuel seif katan 49. One must say that the Sema holds that the market enactment does not apply here, since it is known that these are his wife's garments and she has practical control over them and can take them without his knowledge. See Even HaEzer, siman 86, seif 2, Rama, and Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel there.
