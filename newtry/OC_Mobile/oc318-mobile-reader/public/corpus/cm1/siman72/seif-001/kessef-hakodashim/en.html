See Shach, seif katan 33. In seif 5 the Shach cites Ir Shushan that when certain persons paid a communal tax, their payment is treated as a loan. Later, at the end of seif katan 35, the Shach writes that once they paid, the debtor is liable to the community. The distinction depends on the form of the obligation. If the wording shows that the claim is against the community collectively, then after the selected representatives pay, the debtor owes the community. If the original obligation is against each individual separately, then even where designated leaders paid on behalf of the group, their payment is merely a loan to the others.
