If he stipulated. The source is the sugya concerning a lender who needs the collateral for his own use. Such use can be permitted only by express stipulation. The Sema writes that the stipulation helps only if made at the time of the loan. Yet the same sugya includes a case of a loan evidenced by a document, where the collateral is taken after the loan, and the dispute is nevertheless explained through the lender's need to use the collateral. This proves that the stipulation can be effective even after the loan. Taz therefore correctly rules that a later stipulation can work.
