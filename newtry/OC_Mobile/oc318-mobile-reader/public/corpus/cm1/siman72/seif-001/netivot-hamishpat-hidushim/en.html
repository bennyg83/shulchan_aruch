In Yoreh Deah siman 172 Rama cites an opinion permitting, even at the outset, use of books where the interest prohibition is only rabbinic, because a rabbinic interest prohibition can yield in a mitzvah context. That applies specifically where a deduction arrangement was made, so the prohibition is only rabbinic. Without such an arrangement the issue is theft, and even books may not be used. See Beurim. It is forbidden to take books without permission in order to study from them because use causes wear. But one may occasionally take another's tallit or tefillin to use for the mitzvah, because a person is ordinarily pleased that a mitzvah be performed with his property.
