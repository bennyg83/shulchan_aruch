(Collected note) According to the authority... This follows his approach, and therefore the Shulchan Aruch omitted it. (End of note.)
(Collected note) The lender shall swear... This follows his view in siman 294, seif 2, where he also requires gilgul that the bailee did not make unauthorized use. The phrase "that he was not negligent" means specifically where there are no witnesses regarding negligence; if there are witnesses, no oath about unauthorized use is required beyond that, as explained there. This is the meaning of "there were witnesses..." Although witnesses establish the theft, he still swears according to that approach. (End of note.)
