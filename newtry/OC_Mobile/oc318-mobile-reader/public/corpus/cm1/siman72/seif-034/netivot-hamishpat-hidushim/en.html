That the pledge was lost through unavoidable accident. Meaning, perhaps it was lost through unavoidable accident. If he made that claim with certainty, how could he also swear that he did not return it? Shach:
That he did not return it. The Shach asks that this is merely an uncertain claim. He answers that the clauses refer to different cases.
