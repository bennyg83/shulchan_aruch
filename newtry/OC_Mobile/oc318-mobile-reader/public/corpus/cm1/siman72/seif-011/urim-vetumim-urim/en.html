And he includes in his oath, etc. Ordinarily one who says, 'I do not know whether I repaid you,' does not require the claimant to swear at all. Here, however, the claimant must in any event swear that the pledge is not in his possession, so the uncertainty is included through a rolled oath. Therefore, if witnesses establish the loss or the borrower trusts him and no oath that the pledge is absent is required, this additional uncertain matter is not sworn upon.
