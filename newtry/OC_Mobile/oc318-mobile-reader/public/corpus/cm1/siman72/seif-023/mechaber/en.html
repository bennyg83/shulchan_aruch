If Reuven borrowed ten dinars from Shimon against collateral, and one witness knows of the collateral and loan but not the loan's amount, while Shimon claims he lent twenty, then if Shimon produced the collateral before a beit din or witnesses before the dispute arose, Reuven prevails. The single witness obligates Reuven to swear, but Reuven agrees with the witness and cannot take an oath contradicting him. Shimon therefore returns the collateral and must prove the additional money before collecting; Reuven takes a heses oath concerning the ten he denies. If the dispute arose before Shimon produced the collateral in beit din, Shimon is believed upon taking an oath while holding a sacred object and collects.
