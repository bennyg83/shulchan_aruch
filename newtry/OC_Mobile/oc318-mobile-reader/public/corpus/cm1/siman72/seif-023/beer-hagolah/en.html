The Ramban (C.)
Meaning, concerning the ten dinars that Reuven denies. If Shimon has no proof, Reuven takes a shevuat hesset, etc.
He explains there that when the claimant first asserted in beit din that the defendant owed him twenty dinars, he was immediately believed through a migo that he could have said, 'I returned it.' Since that trustworthiness arose then, even though he later produced the item and it was seen in his possession, he does not lose his original credibility.
