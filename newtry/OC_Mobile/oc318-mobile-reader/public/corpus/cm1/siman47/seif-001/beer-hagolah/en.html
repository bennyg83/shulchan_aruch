The Tur, and likewise Baal HaTerumot in gate 21. The Sema likewise writes that if one says of money in his possession, 'It belongs to so-and-so, who deposited it with me,' and it is known that he held deposits from others, he is believed through a migo that he could have given it to that person. He brings proofs for this.
There, and likewise the Rosh there. Otherwise he would be believed through a migo that he could have hidden it. See the wording at the beginning of siman 94.
