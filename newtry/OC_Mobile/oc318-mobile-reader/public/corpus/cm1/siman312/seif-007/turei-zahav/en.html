(Seif 15) The intercalated month belongs to the tenant. The Sema reconciles the Tur’s ruling here with what he writes in Yoreh Deah by saying that the Yoreh Deah case concerns one who makes the statement in the middle of the year. This is difficult, for the Tur should have drawn that distinction there. It appears to me that the Tur himself was uncertain, like the Rashba cited by the Beit Yosef. He therefore ruled stringently in each context. Here, in monetary law, we say that land remains in the possession of its owner. In Yoreh Deah, concerning a prohibition, we rule stringently lest the intercalated month also be included. This appears correct.
