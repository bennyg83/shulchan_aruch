So too the renter must notify him. The Beit Yosef concludes that a shopkeeper renting a shop cannot leave without notifying the owner twelve months in advance, so that the owner has time to find another shopkeeper to rent it. The outgoing shopkeeper himself ordinarily cannot leave the premises until he first collects the credit he extended to customers, and the period allowed for that collection is twelve months. This appears to be the reason.
The correct reading is: thirty days in towns, or beforehand, etc. This is likewise the reading in the Rambam.
