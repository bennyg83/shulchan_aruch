If he already lived there, he is not removed. The Gemara leaves the question unresolved, which makes Rambam's ruling difficult because land is normally left in the owner's possession in doubt. The answer is that if the tenant entered the lower house with the landlord's knowledge, he already acquired that accommodation. Once knowingly placed there, he cannot be reduced to the inferior upper story. If he entered without the landlord's knowledge, however, the landlord can remove him to the upper story because the land remains in the landlord's presumptive possession.
