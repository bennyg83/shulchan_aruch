g) The language of the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 2, from the Mishnah in Bava Metzia 102a. The Maggid Mishneh explains that the Rambam's view applies even when the dispute arises in the middle of the year, unlike the Rashba's view cited by the Tur. The plain sense of the Mishnah in chapter HaShoel supports the Rambam. Hagahot Maimoniyot and the Kesef Mishneh write that the Rashba stated his contrary view only as a theoretical ruling, but in practice acted in accordance with the Rambam.
