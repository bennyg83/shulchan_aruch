Until he gives thirty days' notice, etc. This is also Rashi's view, unlike Raavad. The Mishna's phrase 'from the festival, etc.' means that both from the festival onward and during summer, thirty days' prior notice is required. Otherwise the mention of summer would be unnecessary. The Mishna necessarily means that during the rainy season he may not remove the tenant at all from Sukkot until Pesach, and its later statement of thirty days must refer to notice before Sukkot. Summer therefore has its literal meaning. The latter clause, 'in large cities, both in the rainy season and in summer,' also implies that the opening clause addresses summer as well. See 81.
