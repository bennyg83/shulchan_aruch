One who rents a house to another without specification cannot remove him, etc. It appears that "without specification" is not literal. Otherwise, after one day or even less the owner could argue that he never intended a longer rental, because land remains in the possession of its owner. Proof appears from Rama at the beginning of siman 212, where one who says, "Let so and so dwell in this house," without fixing a term, may mean even one hour. The same principle would place the renter at the weaker position. Rather, the case is where he rented the house saying, "You will pay me such and such each month." The renter relies upon this as a continuing monthly rental, and therefore the owner must give thirty days notice before removal. The Maggid Mishneh explains the Rambam this way in chapter 6 of Hilchot Sechirut. This is specifically where the arrangement has no stated fixed end. If a definite term was set, the owner may remove him immediately at the term's conclusion without additional notice.
When is this said? In the summer, etc. The meaning is as follows. If the owner tells him during the summer, "Leave my house," beit din says that he must let the renter remain for another thirty days so that he can seek another place. This thirty day period applies when the demand is made in summer. But if the owner seeks to remove him during the rainy season, we do not merely give thirty days. He must let him remain until Pesach. The rainy season begins at Sukkot, and the owner cannot remove him during it unless he gave notice thirty days before Sukkot. Notice given thirty full days before the rainy season is sufficient. The ruling therefore concludes that if he failed to notify him thirty days before Sukkot, even by one day, his law is as though he gave no timely notice at all, and the renter remains until Pesach. The reason is that during the rainy season it is difficult to move from house to house, and people ordinarily rent one residence for the entire season. Therefore suitable housing is not readily available in the middle of that period.
