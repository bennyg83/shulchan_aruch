Or he may place another in his stead. Netivot HaMishpat notes that the Shach's reference to Responsa Mahara Sasson belongs here. Mahara Sasson writes that if the tenant already prepaid the rent, he may not substitute another tenant. Mishneh LaMelech cites this ruling, but it is not universally accepted, as discussed above in siman 212 and below in siman 316.
