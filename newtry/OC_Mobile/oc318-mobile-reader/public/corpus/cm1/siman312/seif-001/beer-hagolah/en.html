a) The language of the Tur, based on Bava Metzia 101b, where the Gemara says regarding negligence when the landlord's own house fell, 'You are no better than I am.' The Rosh writes there in his rulings that the entire discussion concerns an unspecified rental term, but if he rented it for a fixed term, it is like a sale. He brings proof from the Yerushalmi to this chapter and from Pesachim, chapter Makom Shenahagu, and writes that his teacher, Rabbeinu Meir, likewise ruled for him in a responsum. So too the Rambam writes at the end of chapter 6 of Hilkhot Sekhirut.
b) Ibid., seif 3 in the Rosh's rulings, from the Yerushalmi in Pesachim, chapter Makom Shenahagu, which states that if the renter prepaid the rent and the owner subsequently consecrated the house, the renter dwells in it without additional payment. This law is written by the Mechaber below in seif 17.
