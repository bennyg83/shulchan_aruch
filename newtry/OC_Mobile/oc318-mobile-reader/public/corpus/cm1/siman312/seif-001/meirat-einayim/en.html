Even if no time was fixed. The Beit Yosef derives this from the Tur's wording. He asks why the Tur needed to add that if the renter prepaid the rent, etc. The opening rule already teaches a stronger point, that even without prepayment the owner cannot remove him before the agreed term ends. The Beit Yosef answers that the later clause speaks even where no fixed term was specified. In the Derishah I wrote that this inference is not compelling. The clause can still refer to a fixed term like the opening clause and also to a case where the property was sold to another. Although the first clause teaches that neither the original owner nor the purchaser can remove the renter during the term, the second adds that if the renter prepaid the original owner, the purchaser cannot remove him before the end of the term even though the purchaser receives no rent at all from him. These are the words of the Rosh based on the Yerushalmi. For this reason the Mechaber appears to have abbreviated and not explicitly written that the case applies where no term was fixed. The rule may nevertheless be correct independently of the Tur's wording. See below.
