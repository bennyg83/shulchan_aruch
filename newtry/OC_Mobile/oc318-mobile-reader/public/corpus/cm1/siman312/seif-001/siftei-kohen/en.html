Even if he did not fix a term. The Sema is correct that this refers even to a lease without a fixed term; otherwise Maharam's proof from the Yerushalmi would not establish anything. However, the Sema's further suggestion that the law may be independently inferred from the amount paid is incorrect. The Gemara states that one who rents a house “for this year” obtains only until the year's end even if the price might suggest a longer period; Tosafot explain that the money does not determine the meaning of the words. Although one might distinguish where the wording does not contradict the price, the accepted rule regarding one who sells a yoke and claims that the price proves the oxen were included shows that money does not establish the interpretation even where the words could bear that meaning. All the more so here, the price alone does not fix the term.
