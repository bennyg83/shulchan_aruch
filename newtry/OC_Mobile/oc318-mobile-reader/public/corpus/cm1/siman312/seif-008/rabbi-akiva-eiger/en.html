[Sema, letter 23] And I resolved everything. Note: In my humble opinion the matter can be explained simply. The Tur in Yoreh Deah rules that the added leap month is prohibited because of the Rashba's responsum, siman 965, who wrote that in practice he is concerned to be stringent for the view of Rambam and therefore prohibits during the intercalated month. But regarding rent, the Tur follows the essential law that the added month is not included. The Rashba's wording itself implies that he did not absolutely abandon his own view for Rambam's, but merely was concerned for the latter. Such stringency applies in matters of prohibition, not monetary law. Moreover, if this creates a monetary doubt, the land should remain in the possession of its owner, and the tenant therefore has no right to the added month. One may still ask why the Tur rules that one who says simply a year is prohibited during the added month, since there is a double doubt: perhaps the Talmudic question is resolved that a year means like this year and the prohibition lasts only until Rosh Hashanah; and even if it means one full year, perhaps the law follows the Rashba that the added month is not included. This can be resolved. Afterward I found that Responsa Mahari ben Lev, book 2, siman 21, says this, though he then cites another responsum of the Rashba concerning a lessor, where the Rashba writes that in practice one should act like Rambam. See Responsa Maaseh Chiya, siman 1.
