He may remove the renter from his house. This appears to apply specifically after the renter has already lived there for thirty days. During the first thirty days of the rental, since there is ordinarily no rental for less than thirty days, it is like a fixed term. The Mechaber writes immediately afterward that one cannot remove a renter during a fixed term.
Until you find a place. Meaning: Shall I wait for you thirty days in summer, or until Pesach in winter, while you search and make efforts to find a residence, and meanwhile I myself be cast out with no place to live throughout the entire period you are seeking housing?
