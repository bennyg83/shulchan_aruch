i) Ibid., in the Mishnah.
j) Because it is a marketplace to which everyone is drawn to reside, and houses are not readily available for rent there, Rashi.
k) Because a shopkeeper extends credit to others, and if he moves elsewhere they will not know where to find him.
l) Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel's ruling there in the Mishnah. The Rif and Rosh rule likewise. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Rambam did not rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, and the Ittur likewise. The Beit Yosef writes that we follow the Rif and Rosh. In the Bedek HaBayit he writes that nowadays we observe that twelve months suffice, and perhaps for this reason the Rambam omitted the ruling of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. In my humble opinion, this gloss should properly have been stated in the name of some authorities.
