He fixed, etc. I do not understand the word 'fixed' here, and it is difficult to reconcile. Rambam first says he fixed thirty days and then speaks of time remaining, and further requires notice although notice was already given. See Derisha. In seif 5 he also omits the requirement of thirty days before Sukkot and does not mention notice before Pesach. Tur follows Rambam, but it is clear that Rambam's text contains a scribal error. It should read: 'The landlord, etc.; but, etc., until Pesach unless he notified him thirty, etc. If there remained, etc.' Meaning that if he did not notify him on the fifteenth of Elul, he may not remove him. 'Provided that he notifies him, etc.' refers to one who gave no initial notice. In place of the word 'fixed' the text should read 'unless he notified him.'
