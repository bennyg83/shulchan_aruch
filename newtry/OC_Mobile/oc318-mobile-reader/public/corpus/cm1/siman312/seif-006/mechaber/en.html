If he fixed a thirty-day term before Sukkos, and even one day of the thirty remains, he may not evict him until after Pesach, provided he gives thirty days' notice. This applies in towns. In large cities, whether in summer or winter, twelve months' notice is required. For a shop, whether in a town or large city, twelve months' notice is required, and for a baker's or dyer's shop, three years' notice is required.
