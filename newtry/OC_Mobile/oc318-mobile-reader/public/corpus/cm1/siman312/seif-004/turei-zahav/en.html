(Seif 9) And he became his enemy. It appears to me that this applies specifically where the tenant caused the enmity through his own misconduct, similar to the case in chapter HaShoel of the woman who gave a man a place to store his wine on condition that he marry her, and he later sent her a bill of divorce. The Gemara there applies the principle, “As he did, so shall be done to him.” This implies that had he not been at fault, the woman could not have removed the wine.
