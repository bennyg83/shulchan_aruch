The Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of Rashba, and the Rosh likewise writes in responsa, klal 1, siman 6. Rashba explains that if the landlord says, 'I rent you this house for such-and-such per year,' then from the second year onward he may not terminate without giving the notice period fixed in the Gemara, as explained above in seifim 5 and 6.
