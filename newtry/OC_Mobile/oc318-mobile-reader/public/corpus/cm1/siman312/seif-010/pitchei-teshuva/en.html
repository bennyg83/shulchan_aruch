A house for a year. The Shach writes that the rental year begins with Rosh Hashanah, the first of Tishrei. Thus one who rents a house 'for a year' counts twelve months from day to day, but if he says 'for this year,' even if the rental began on the first of Elul the year ends with Rosh Hashanah. Shevut Yaakov I:175 ruled this way in practice. He further rejected the tenant's argument that the high price proved a longer term, citing Tosafot that the price does not establish the term. Even if the excess is beyond ordinary error, there is no law of onaah in land rental. Therefore the tenant must leave unless local rental custom establishes otherwise, as stated later in siman 312.
