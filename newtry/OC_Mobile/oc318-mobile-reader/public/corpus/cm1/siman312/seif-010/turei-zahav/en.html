(The house burned), etc. The Sema correctly emended the text here by adding “and some say,” for words are missing. Nevertheless, the halachah appears to follow the first opinion, that a burned house has the same law as one that collapsed. What distinction could there be? The second opinion also holds regarding collapse that the tenant must pay the entire rent, interpreting the Gemara's statement, “If he said, this house, and it fell, it is gone,” to mean that the tenant must nevertheless pay all the rent. Since we rule that rent is deducted proportionately when the house falls, the same applies when it burns.
