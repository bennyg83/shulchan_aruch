Shevuat heset. Although he admits part of the claim, since their dispute concerns residence in real property, its law is that of land, regarding which only a shevuat heset is taken. Rental of land is like land with respect to ona'ah, as stated in siman 227, seif 52, and with respect to acquisition at the beginning of siman 190 in the gloss and in siman 155. The same applies to other matters. This is also evident from the Maggid Mishneh, chapter 5 of Hilkhot To'en, law 5, who concludes: "This is likewise explained in chapter 7 of Hilkhot Sechirut, because rental of land is regarded as land." You will not find in chapter 7 an explicit proof regarding partial admission other than these very words of the Rambam that the Mechaber copied here. Shach.
