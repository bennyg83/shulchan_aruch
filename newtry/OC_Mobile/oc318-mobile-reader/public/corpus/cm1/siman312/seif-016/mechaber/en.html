If the landlord says, 'I rented to you for a fixed term,' and the tenant says, 'I rented without a fixed term,' or for a longer term, the tenant must bring proof. If he does not, the landlord takes a rabbinic oath and evicts him. Rama: The same applies to every uncertainty between one residing in another's house and the owner, unless the occupant has established a three-year chazakah, in which case he is believed through the claim that he purchased it.
