(Seif 5) To another person without qualification. This means that he fixed a monthly rent but did not state for how many months, as the Maggid Mishneh explains. It appears to me that if he said nothing beyond, “I rent you this house,” the rental is presumed to follow the local custom, and it is as though he fixed a definite term, as stated in seif 8. The Sema, seif katan 4, writes that one who merely says, “I rent it to you,” is comparable to one who says, “So-and-so shall reside,” for which even one day suffices. In my humble opinion, rental is different, because one is presumed to rent according to the local custom.
