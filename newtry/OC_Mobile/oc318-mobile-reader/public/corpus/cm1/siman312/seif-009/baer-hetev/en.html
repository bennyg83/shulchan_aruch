And likewise. The Rama adds that even where the tenant originally rented for a fixed term and remained afterward without a new agreement, neither a subsequent rise nor fall in rental value changes the rate for the additional period. Rivash, siman 475, writes likewise in the name of a responsum of the Rashba. The Rama's gloss refers to the Mechaber's words, "even though the term has passed"; consider this carefully. See Responsa Mahari ibn Lev, vol. 4, p. 74. Shach.
