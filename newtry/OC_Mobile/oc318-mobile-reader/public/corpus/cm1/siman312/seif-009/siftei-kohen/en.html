And similarly, if he rented it for a fixed period, etc. The Mechaber himself writes this rule immediately afterward in seif 10. The Sema therefore writes that the Rama overlooked it in the flow of his writing. In my humble opinion, however, the Rama did see it and adds a new point here, referring back to the Mechaber's statement, "even though the period passed." Meaning, even if he rented it for a fixed period and then continued living there without a new agreement after the period ended, if rental prices subsequently rose or fell, the landlord cannot change the amount due for the additional time to match the new market rate. Rivash, siman 475, writes likewise in the name of a responsum of Rashba. Examine carefully.
