[Shach, subparagraph 13] He counts twelve months from day to day. Note: This applies even in a leap year, which is extended for the tenant according to the Rambam and the plain meaning of the Mechaber. If the parties said simply "a year," without saying "this year" or "one full year," the Gemara in Nedarim leaves unresolved whether an unspecified year means "this year" or "one year." Accordingly, as a practical matter, if the lease is made in the middle of the year for "a year" without further qualification, one can say that the tenant may remain only until Rosh Hashanah, because land remains in the established possession of its owner.
