He mortgaged it, etc. This is the Jerusalem Talmud according to Rosh's explanation above. Although Rosh and Rashba write that the owner lacked money to redeem it, Tur holds that this is merely the usual case, not an essential condition. Rosh distinguishes between rental and mortgage.
The purchaser may not, etc. See Baer HaGolah and Beit Yosef. This is unlike Bach, because a mortgage is not identical to a rental. See seif 14.
