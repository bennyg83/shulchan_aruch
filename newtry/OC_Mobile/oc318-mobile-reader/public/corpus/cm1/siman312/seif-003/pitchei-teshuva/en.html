And even if he did not fix a term, etc. See Baer Heitev. The Sema only rejected Beit Yosef's proof, not necessarily the ruling itself. The Shach, however, rejects the ruling and argues from Rosh Hashanah that where a house is rented 'for this year,' the rent paid does not establish a longer term, just as price does not establish that cattle were included in a sale of a yoke. Shevut Yaakov I:175 rules like the Rama. Where money was paid without any stated term, it is unreasonable to assume the parties left the period wholly undefined; rather, they relied on the amount paid as fixing the duration. This differs from a transaction where a term was expressly stated and the question is how to construe it. See Ketzot HaChoshen and Netivot HaMishpat.
