Therefore he gives the stolen property. See Sema, seif katan 12, through his statement, "and not like the Beit Yosef." I do not know who told him this. The Beit Yosef likewise does not explain Tosafos in accordance with Rambam except regarding the phrase "to his children or his brothers." But regarding Rambam's explanation of "and if he does not want it, he has no," Tosafos is not his companion at all, as is clear from Tosafos, who explicitly writes that he borrows and afterward gives it to his children. This is unlike Rambam. There is no indication in the Beit Yosef otherwise. The Sema further reports that he was told that the Bedek HaBayit says Tosafos does not hold like Rambam. I looked there and found nothing of the sort, because the matter is obvious. The Bedek HaBayit only says there that the Tur's difficulty is not a difficulty against Rambam. The Kesef Mishneh likewise writes that the phrase "to his children or his brothers" is out of order, because Tosafos also explains it like Rambam. What they told the Sema is false, and the matter is clear. Perhaps the Sema means only that this is unlike the Beit Yosef's equation of Rambam's explanation with Tosafos, and if so that equation is indeed incorrect.
