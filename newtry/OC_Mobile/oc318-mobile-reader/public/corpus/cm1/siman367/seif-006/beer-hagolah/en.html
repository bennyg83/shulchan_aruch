There, law 4. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the dispute of Rabbi Akiva and Rabbi Yose HaGelili in the baraita there, and Rambam rules like Rabbi Akiva. In my opinion, according to Rabbi Akiva it makes no difference whether the amount was converted into a debt or not; once the stolen property is removed from the robber's possession, that suffices even if he does not actually give it to the kohanim. The Gemara mentions converting it into a debt only to teach that even after doing so he must still remove the stolen property from his possession. Tur writes that he must give it to charity. Sema writes that it should be divided equally among the poor, like the returned guilt offering was divided among the kohanim in the time of the Temple.
