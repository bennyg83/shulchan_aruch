It goes without saying that if he gave it to beit din, he has discharged his obligation. This applies where he gives it to beit din so that they may send it to the victim whenever they choose, not where the agent or beit din merely holds it until the victim comes personally. This resolves Tosafot's statements on Bava Kamma 104a and 103a, and also answers Maharsha's question. See Sema, seif katan 6.
