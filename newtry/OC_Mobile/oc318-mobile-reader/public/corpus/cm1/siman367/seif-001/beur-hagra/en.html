And he must inform them. This is the view of Rosh and Tur. See Shach, seif katan 3. His words are unconvincing, for Rambam and Shulchan Aruch clearly hold that notification is unnecessary, since in the case of an oath they explain the reason as the owners' despair. They therefore did not have the Gemara's reading, 'our Mishna, since, etc.' Rama, however, follows Tur and our reading.
