A robber who wishes to return the stolen property, and one who robs his father. It contains six seifim.
One who robbed another and denied it, but did not swear, need not pursue the owner if he later admits. He may retain the property until the owner comes, provided he informs him, and the owner takes it. If he swore falsely regarding a perutah or more, he must pursue the owner and return it even to distant islands, because after the oath the owner despaired and will no longer come to demand it.
