y) Tur in the name of his father the Rosh, in the name of some commentators. The Nimukei Yosef writes that this is also the Rashba's view, and Baal HaTerumot likewise writes in gate 32.
k) There, according to his father the Rosh: regarding a deposited document, we do not suspect collusion or that he wrote it to borrow in Nisan but did not borrow until Tishrei, because his careful preservation of the document indicates its validity. This differs from a document that fell from his possession.
