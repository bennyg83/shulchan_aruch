As they say. The Sema writes that he must nevertheless swear before the document is returned to him. The Shach disagrees. He further writes that the lender can tell the borrower: "Either swear and they will return the note to you, or I will swear and they will return it to me." If the borrower refuses to swear, the note is returned to the lender. Likewise, if the borrower makes the same proposal to the lender and the lender refuses to swear, the note is returned to the borrower. See Beurim, seif katan 9.
