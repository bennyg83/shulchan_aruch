Even if it is still within its due date. Although there is a presumption that a person does not pay within the due date, as stated in siman 78, since there is an evident defect arising from the loss of the document, we say that perhaps an unusual circumstance occurred and he paid early, and for that reason the creditor was no longer careful to guard it.
And even if the borrower or creditor states a hole, etc. The reason is as the Tur writes: the document remained for some time in the creditor's possession, so he knows its identifying mark, yet it is possible that it fell from the borrower after it had been paid. Likewise, a borrower who writes a document, even when the creditor is not with him, pays attention to this identifying mark while the document is still in his possession, yet it is possible that it later fell from the creditor.
