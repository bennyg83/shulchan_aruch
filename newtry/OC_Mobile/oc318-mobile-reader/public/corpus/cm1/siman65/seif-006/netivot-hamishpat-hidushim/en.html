Its date. Since the note fell, its status is impaired and we suspect repayment even within the due date. If the borrower says, "I never borrowed," and the lender gives a distinguishing sign, the borrower is established as a denier and the lender collects from him. But if the borrower says, "I paid in Nisan and immediately received the note back," and the lender brings witnesses who saw the note in his possession in Iyar, even so he cannot collect, even from the borrower. See Beurim, seif katan 12. If the borrower was already established as a denier concerning a fallen note, then if that status arose before the note fell, such as where he said, "I never borrowed," the note was produced, and beit din told him to pay, so that he is not believed to say, "I later paid," nevertheless if the note then falls he may claim, "I paid afterward." But if he asserted another false claim after the note had already fallen, and was thereby established as a denier, such as saying, "I never borrowed," while witnesses prove that he borrowed and wrote the very note now fallen, then not only can he not claim earlier repayment, but even if beit din told him to pay he cannot later claim subsequent repayment, because he is established as a denier and the fall of the document no longer proves later payment. See Beurim.
