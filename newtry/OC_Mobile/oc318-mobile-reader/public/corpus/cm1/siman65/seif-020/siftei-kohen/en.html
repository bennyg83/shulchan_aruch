Even at its edge, etc. This is the Rambam’s language. The Sema’s explanation, that part of the receipt must be written opposite the text of the document and part below it, is incorrect. The correct reading is ‘even at its edge,’ as Baal HaTerumot expressly quotes the Rambam. Otherwise the Rambam would have omitted the Gemara’s rule of a receipt written after the witnesses’ signatures and substituted a case not mentioned there. Hagahot Ashri, the Bach, and the Ramban all rule that we do not accept the migo that the creditor could have cut off the receipt. If the debt had not been paid, he would not have impaired his own document in this manner.
