Even in part. Sema says the receipt must be written in a manner such that cutting it off would visibly reveal forgery. If it could simply be cut away without leaving evidence, the holder has a migo that he could have cut it away and is therefore believed to say the receipt has no force. Shach objects that this is a migo against a presumption. See Tumim. I show that the matter is disputed among major authorities and remains an unresolved monetary doubt. The burden of proof is on the claimant.
