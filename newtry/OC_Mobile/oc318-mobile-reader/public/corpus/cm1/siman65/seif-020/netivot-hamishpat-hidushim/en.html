In part of it. Meaning, part of the receipt is written opposite the text of the debt document and part below it. This excludes a receipt written entirely below the document, which according to this view has no effect because the creditor is believed through a migo that he could have cut it off. This applies specifically when the creditor is alive and makes a definite claim that he was not paid. If he died, we do not advance such a claim for the heirs. The Shach and Taz agree that even a receipt written entirely below is valid, including while the creditor is alive, because the migo is opposed by a strong presumption.
