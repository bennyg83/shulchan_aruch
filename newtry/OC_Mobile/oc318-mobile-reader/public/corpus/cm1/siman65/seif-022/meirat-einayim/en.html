That person has no share in it. This writing is no stronger than if the decedent had said the same thing before witnesses without saying, "You are my witnesses," in which case we say that he spoke merely so as not to appear wealthy. See also siman 81 for what I wrote further on this. This is unlike a debt instrument upon which a payment notation is written. There, the writing detracts from the force of the document itself, because when the document is produced in beit din for collection, the payment notation is visible on it. Therefore we say that the creditor would not have written it unless it were true. See there.
