(Netivot HaMishpat, same passage) Before its defect became apparent. Note: See Rambam, who writes that payment is collected for them and they remain in their presumptive status, and we do not suspect them. One may say that his reasoning is that once the document comes into the lender's possession, we sustain his right on the basis of the original presumption and do not invalidate the document because of a mere doubt of payment. With a get there is no comparable presumption. On the contrary, until delivery of the get the property remained in the husband's possession.
