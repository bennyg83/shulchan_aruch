Who are able. The Sema writes: Why do we not suspect that this debtor owes other creditors whose documents are later than this one, and that he therefore says to return this document to the present creditor in order to give him priority? The Tur and Mechaber make a similar concern in siman 37, seif 15, regarding one who sold a field without warranty and is therefore disqualified from testifying about it. Here, however, a valid document before us supports his admission, and therefore we are concerned only where there is a definite loss to purchasers. There, by contrast, he seeks to defeat the words of a challenger who brings testimony and proof against him. The Shach challenges this and writes that all the sugyot of the Gemara and the authorities clearly show that we are concerned for creditors and purchasers even when none are presently known. Rather, the case here must be one where it is definitely known that he owes no one else, such as where a proclamation was made to determine whether he owes others, or where there is a municipal enactment that any document not entered in the scribe's register is invalid, or the like. This is clear. The Taz also cites a difficulty in the name of Maharshal and resolves it differently; see there.
Authentication. In that case there is no concern for collusion, because if he wished he could write a new document. Although one might suggest that perhaps this document had already been paid and he wishes to borrow against it again, while avoiding the scribe's fee, that concern is not accepted. The lender would not agree to this, since he gains nothing from the earlier date and would fear that it might become known that the document had been paid and its lien extinguished, causing it to be invalidated after he lends on it again. Thus the Sema. The Shach writes that the Maggid Mishneh, in the name of the Rashba, wonders why authentication should be necessary at all. One who examines the Gemara will see that the primary ruling follows the Rashba and the Maggid Mishneh, and likewise Tosafot and most authorities, that authentication is not required.
