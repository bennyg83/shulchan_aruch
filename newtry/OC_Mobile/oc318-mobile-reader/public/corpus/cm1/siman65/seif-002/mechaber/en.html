Likewise, a custodian's heirs should not return any document found in their father's possession unless they know its circumstances. Some say that if the lender himself took a document before it reached another person's possession, we do not rule that it remain deposited, even if he took it from an heir's house before the heir took possession. {Rama: If an heir produces a woman's kesubah that had been deposited with his father, he returns it to the woman and need not fear that it was paid (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ritva).}
