[Rama] The smaller amount is waived, but not the greater. Note: This is because the uncertainty concerns how much he waived. But if the amount waived is known and the doubt is a legal one, whether the waiver is effective, it would seem that the possessor is exempt because of the doubt. However, the Beit Shmuel, siman 66, seif katan 9, indicates that he does not agree, and in my humble opinion this requires study. See Nimukei Yosef in the first chapter of Bava Batra concerning one who established a right to beams, from which it appears that even where the doubt concerns the waiver itself, whether he waived, and the matter is a doubt in law, the possessor prevails. Likewise, in Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer, beginning of siman 106, even where the claim is certain but the issue is an unresolved question whether she waived her ketubah, the Rif indeed holds that because of the doubt she does not lose her ketubah.
