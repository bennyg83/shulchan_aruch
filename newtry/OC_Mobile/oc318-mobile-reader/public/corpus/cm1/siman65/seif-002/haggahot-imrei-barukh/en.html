(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 15) If so, that document is like a shard. Note: The Mechaber omitted several details. The Ritva's language is that the principals revoked their agency, and meanwhile the signed document remained with the scribe. The document therefore became void like a shard, as though its lien had been forgiven. Now the borrower wishes to borrow anew on the basis of a document whose lien has already been extinguished.
