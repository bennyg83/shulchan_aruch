If a person returned a document that the law did not permit him to return, some say collection may be made with it, while others say it may not. Some say that even if a beit din collected with it and witnesses later establish that it had been lost, the beit din reverses the collection and removes the money from the creditor for the borrower. If the borrower voluntarily paid the creditor or his heirs, however, the money is not removed from them. {Rama: See the beginning of this siman, seif 1, and siman 12.}
