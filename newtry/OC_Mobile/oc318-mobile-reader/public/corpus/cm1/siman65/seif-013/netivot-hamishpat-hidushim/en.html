Proof. The Sema holds that even if he brings witnesses who saw the document in his possession before it was lost, that does not suffice, because perhaps it did not come into his possession at the date written in the document. He must prove that it was delivered to him at the time stated in the document. The Shach holds that proof that witnesses saw it in his possession before the loss is sufficient, and this is the primary ruling:
