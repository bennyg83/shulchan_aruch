If a deed of manumission is found and the master does not acknowledge it, it is returned to neither the master nor the servant. If the master admits that he gave it to the servant to free him, it is returned to the servant. If the servant later seeks to use it to seize property the master sold after the document's date, he must prove that it reached him on that date. {Rama: Where the signatories acquire on his behalf through their signatures, as explained above in siman 39, he need not bring that proof. See also siman 6.}
