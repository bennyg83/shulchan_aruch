To the borrower. The reason is that the lender is the one who ordinarily has the document validated, and once validation is completed he usually takes it from beit din rather than leaving it there. Since these notes are from three different lenders, we cannot say they fell from those lenders as a group, because who tied them together? We therefore say the borrower paid all three lenders, bound the notes together, and then lost them. But if the notes are not validated, perhaps each lender came to beit din to validate his note and they fell before validation was completed. There is also a possibility they fell from the borrower, so the notes are returned to whoever gives a valid sign, namely the sign of the binding together with testimony that beit din tied them, not a hole or similar sign.
