The name of his father's prayer is the same
He brings proofs that we follow circumstantial assessment from the incident where Rabbi Bana'ah said, 'Go strike your father's grave,' Bava Batra 58a; from the case of a document designed to evade creditors, Ketubot 78b; and from the case of one who wrote his property to another and afterward discovered he had a son, Bava Batra 132a, among others. He writes that this principle has a foundation from the father of wisdom, King Shlomo, who commanded, 'Give her the living child,' etc.
