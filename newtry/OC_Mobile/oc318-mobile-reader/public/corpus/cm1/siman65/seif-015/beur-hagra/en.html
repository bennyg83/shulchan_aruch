The Gemara's mention of the date was omitted because there is another practical difference where witnesses of delivery are used, as Tosafos state there in the name of the Yerushalmi. The Tur also writes this. The Gemara did not mention it because at the end of Gittin Rabbi Elazar says that the halachah does not follow Rabbi Elazar except regarding gittin. The Tur therefore stated the rule generally, and the Mechaber included both. (End of note.)
