Its value. Some debt documents cannot be collected for their full face amount because of the debtor's poverty or violent character. The creditor cannot say, "Write a receipt for half and leave the document in my possession." Rather, the debtor pays half of the document's appraised market value and takes his debt document back. If the document is appraised below face value only because its due date is still distant, however, the creditor may say, "Write a receipt for half the document. I prefer to wait for the other half and do not want an appraisal." The requirement that the debtor pay half applies when they came before beit din while the document was already being litigated and only after the court had begun did the debtor claim that it was paid. If immediately upon coming to beit din he said that the document was paid, and before entering court he could have destroyed it while it was in his possession, he is believed through a migo that he could have burned it.
