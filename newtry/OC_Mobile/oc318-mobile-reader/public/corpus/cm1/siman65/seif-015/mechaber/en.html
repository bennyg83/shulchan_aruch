If two people hold a document, the creditor saying, 'It is mine and I produced it to collect from you,' and the borrower saying, 'I paid it and it fell from me,' then if the document can be authenticated, each swears that his interest in its value is not less than half, and the borrower pays half the value of the documented debt. If it cannot be authenticated, the borrower takes a heses oath that he paid and departs. Some say that when it is authenticated and both hold it equally, whether both hold the essential text, both hold the standard text, or both hold the margin, and even if the essential text lies closer to one, each swears that his share is no less than half and the borrower pays half, even if the document grants the creditor credibility. If one holds the essential text and the other the standard text, the holder of the essential text takes the additional value that portion has over the other, and they divide the balance equally upon oath.
