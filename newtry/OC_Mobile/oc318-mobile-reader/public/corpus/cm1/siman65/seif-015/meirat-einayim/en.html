The Tur adds: if there are witnesses to delivery, then whether one has the standard text portion or the essential operative portion, each piece is valid for collection even without the signatures of the witnesses. Nevertheless, the standard text portion is worth more because of the witnesses signed on it, in case the delivery witnesses die or travel overseas. Therefore, we appraise the additional value of the standard text portion because of its witnesses, and the additional value of the operative portion because of the date. The difference between these values is deducted from the principal debt, and the remainder is divided between them after an oath. This is the Tur's language. I explained it in the Perishah, and in the glosses to Darkei Moshe I also explained why the authorities and the Mechaber omitted the law concerning witnesses to delivery. They likewise omitted part of the law concerning the added value of the operative portion because of the date. See there carefully.
