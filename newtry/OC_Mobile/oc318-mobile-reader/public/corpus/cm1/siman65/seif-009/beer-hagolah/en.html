t) Tur, according to Rashi's explanation there, and so write Tosafot there 20b. The Rosh writes that although Yevamot 120b implies that we are concerned an item was borrowed, that applies in matters of issur. In monetary matters we do not entertain that concern. Even if the concern for borrowing were d'oraisa, the Sages can nevertheless institute that in monetary matters we do not take it into account, etc. See there.
