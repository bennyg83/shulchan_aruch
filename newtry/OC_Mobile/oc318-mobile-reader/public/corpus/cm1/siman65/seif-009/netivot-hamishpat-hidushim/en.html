An identifying mark on the container. Even if he does not know that a document is inside the container, and we do not ordinarily suspect borrowing of containers in monetary matters, the identifying mark suffices. Likewise, if he says that the document is located in a particular type of container, even without giving a specific mark on the container, that may suffice when documents are not ordinarily stored in such a container. Regarding collection from encumbered property, the Shach holds that he may not collect, because we fear collusion and that the borrower supplied him with the identifying information. See Beurim, that here we do not say the document's loss itself weakens the creditor's claim due to careless guarding.
