Moreover, the documents are not ordinarily instruments that later become paid. An appraisal document is an exception in that an appraisal can later be reversed when the original owner repays, as explained in siman 103, but even that document is returned. If repayment had actually occurred, the owner caused his own loss by failing to obtain a new acquisition document from the creditor. Thus the Sema.
