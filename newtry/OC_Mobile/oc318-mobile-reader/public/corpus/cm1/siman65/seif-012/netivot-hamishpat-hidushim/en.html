We do not say that perhaps the party changed his mind and decided not to deliver it, because beit din writes only a matter that has already been legally established, and these documents are also not instruments awaiting collection. Appraisal documents are returned even though they can lead to collection, because an appraisal can later be reversed. The debtor caused his own loss, for if he had actually paid he should have obtained a new document.
