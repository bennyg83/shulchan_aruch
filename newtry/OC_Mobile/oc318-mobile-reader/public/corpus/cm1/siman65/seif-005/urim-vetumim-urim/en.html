Likewise, a gift deed, etc. Sema understands this even where the gift deed includes a kinyan. Shach disagrees and says it applies specifically without kinyan. Without kinyan, absent the document being tied or otherwise defective, it would not necessarily be returned to the giver because it is unknown whether it came from him. With a kinyan, however, the recipient acquired the gift even though the deed never reached him, so even if the deed is tied it should not be returned to the giver. This is correct. Shach extends the same reasoning to a debt instrument with kinyan. I disagree there, because Rosh distinguishes a debt with kinyan from a gift. See Tumim.
