If Reuven died and documents belonging to his sister were found tied together in his possession, some favoring her, such as her dowry document and deeds to her land, and some adverse to her, such as a gift deed by which she gave her husband land she owned, then although her dowry document resembles an ordinary debt document, its being tied with deeds to land certainly hers establishes that she deposited them. For the same reason, the gift deed to her husband is presumed deposited by her. All are returned to her or her heirs.
