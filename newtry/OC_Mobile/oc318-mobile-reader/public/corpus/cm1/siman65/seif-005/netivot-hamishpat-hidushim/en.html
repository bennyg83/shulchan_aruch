Bound together. The Shach asks that perhaps the shalish himself tied them together. This requires study. The Sema holds that even with a gift deed containing a kinyan, and even one without a kinyan, if it is not tied together with others it is returned to the donor. The Shach disagrees: if we do not know from whom it came, even a gift deed without a kinyan is not returned to anyone; with a kinyan, even if it is known that it came from the donor, it is returned to the recipient, provided it is known that the donor made the kinyan unconditionally and did not say, "Give it into my hand." With a debt note, it is not returned because of concern for repayment. See there and Beurim.
