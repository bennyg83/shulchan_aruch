Tied. The Shach writes that the Rosh derived this law from the rule that where one finds an object inside a container, or produce inside a vessel, one returns both based on identifying signs of the container. The proof is not conclusive. There, we have no reason to assume the attached item ever belonged to another person; since the container is certainly the claimant's, we infer that the item tied or contained within it is his too. Here, however, perhaps the deceased third party tied together all matters that had been deposited with him by different people. The law therefore requires study. The Shach also objects to the Sema's explanation that this passage concerns a gift document accompanied by a kinyan, for that is not correct, as he writes in seif katan 8.
