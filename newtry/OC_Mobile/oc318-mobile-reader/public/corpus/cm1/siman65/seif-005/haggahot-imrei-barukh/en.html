(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 21) And it appears that his intention is. Note: See Derishah UPerishah, Even HaEzer 110, who understands that even where a receipt is found written on a ketubah document and we do not return the receipt to the husband, the wife cannot collect the ketubah because the husband may claim, "I paid, and this receipt was mine and was lost." Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel there disagree. Since the receipt's credibility was impaired by being lost, the ketubah document remains in its prior presumptive status and may be collected from the husband.
