But we do not advance this claim for them. The Sema explains that taking such a document as collateral is uncommon because the document itself has no intrinsic monetary value. This is imprecise. The Rosh's responsum states instead that it is uncommon to lend money against documents that cannot be collected. Accordingly, if the documents are written in a manner permitting collection, then even when we know they did not belong to the heirs' father, we advance for them the claim that he took them as collateral.

According to the authorities who disagree there, as explained below in siman 108, seif 4, and simanim 257 and 361, the same applies here and we do advance the claim for them. This requires study.
