We do not advance such a claim for them. Specifically with a note that cannot be collected without written transfer and delivery do we not advance this uncommon claim for heirs. But with a bearer note payable to whoever produces it, we do advance the claim for them. See siman 67. With a note that cannot be collected in that way, even the father himself is not believed through a migo, as I wrote there. The Shach questions this because in siman 108 we rule that we advance even uncommon claims for heirs. Tumim resolves the difficulty there.
