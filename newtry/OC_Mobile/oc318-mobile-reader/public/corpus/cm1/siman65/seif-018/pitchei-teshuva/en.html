Regarding the legal force of the debt document upon which this receipt was found, whether it remains effective for collection, see Even HaEzer, siman 110, Chelkat Mechokek and Beit Shmuel, who explicitly write that the document retains its force and may be collected. See Responsa Mishkenot Yaakov, siman 29, who notes that Responsa Penei Yehoshua likewise agrees. He himself, however, writes that this is not the view of all the later authorities, including Sema, Shach, Bach, Taz, Responsa Avodat HaGershuni, Urim, and Tumim. See there. See also Shaarei Mishpat regarding this matter.
