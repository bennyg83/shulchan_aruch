(Shulchan Aruch, seif 2.) Before it came into another person’s possession. The Sema correctly explains that this means before uncertainty arose for the other person concerning the document. Mere physical arrival does not matter if no doubt arose. The Rashba states expressly that the instrument remains deposited only where the escrow holder himself became uncertain. Similarly, “before it came into the heir’s possession” refers to awareness of the disputed document. Even if physically located in the heir’s house, it is like an object that fell into another’s courtyard and was found before the owner became aware, and no defect arises.
