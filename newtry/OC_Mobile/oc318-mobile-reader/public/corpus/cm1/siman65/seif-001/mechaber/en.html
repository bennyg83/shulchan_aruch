The law of documents that are found, and of a receipt found in the possession of a third party. This siman contains 24 seifim.
If one finds a document in his possession and does not know its status, whether the lender deposited it, the borrower deposited it, or both entrusted it to him as a third party, and the debt has been partly paid, it must remain deposited until Eliyahu comes. If he returned it to the person named as creditor, he violated the words of the Sages. If the person who now holds the document denies the statement of the one who gave it to him, the creditor could collect with it. But if he admits that the other person gave it into his hand, he must return it to the neutral custody in which it is to remain until Eliyahu comes. Further details will be explained in this siman. See seif 16. If a mortgage document is found in his possession and he does not know its status, even if the mortgagee is presently in possession of the land, that possession gives him no valid claim. The document remains deposited until Eliyahu comes, and the land is returned to its owner. Rama: This applies specifically to a person who has no special legal relationship with either the depositor or the person for whom it was deposited. But a son who finds his father's documents in his possession and does not know their status, or a father who finds documents belonging to a son who is supported at his table, and likewise all persons who have no chazakah against one another as will be explained in siman 149, are treated like any other person. It is as though the document was found in the owner's own possession.
