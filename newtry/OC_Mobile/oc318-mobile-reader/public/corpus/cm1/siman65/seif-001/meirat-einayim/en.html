He could have collected with it. The Tur likewise uses this wording in the name of the Rosh. In the Perishah I explained that the phrase "he could have collected with it" indicates that collection is not automatic. The reason is that he cannot collect until he takes a stringent oath resembling a Torah oath against the person who says that the document had previously been in his possession. A third party, even after the item entrusted to him is no longer in his possession, has the status of one witness, as stated in siman 56. He does not, however, take an actual Torah oath, because all those who take Torah oaths swear and are exempt from payment, whereas this person swears and collects, as explained in simanim 89 and 108. Nor is this included in the rule that only a hesset oath is taken concerning documents, as the Tur and Mechaber write below in siman 95, because here he is not swearing against the document itself, but against the witness who says that the document was delivered to him.
