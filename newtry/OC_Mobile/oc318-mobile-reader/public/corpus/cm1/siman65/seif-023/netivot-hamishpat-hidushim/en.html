Waived. Waiver is like a gift, and the one claiming the waiver seeks to extract from the original presumption, so the burden of proof is upon him. Sema. This applies specifically where it is known that the lender waived the debt gratuitously. But if the borrower asserts with certainty that the lender was obligated to waive it, the case resembles repayment, and the larger amount is treated as waived. Shach.
