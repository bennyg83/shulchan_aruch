Forgiven. The Sema explains that forgiveness is like a gift, and the beneficiary seeks to extract property, so the burden of proof is upon him. The Shach writes that this applies only where it is clearly known, or the borrower admits, that the lender forgave the debt gratuitously. But if the lender merely said, 'It is forgiven to you,' and the borrower makes a definite claim that the lender was obligated to forgive it, it is treated like repayment, and the larger debt is deemed forgiven.
