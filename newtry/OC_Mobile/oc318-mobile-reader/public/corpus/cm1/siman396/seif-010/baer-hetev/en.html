The first. Nevertheless, the injured party may, if he wishes, litigate with the second guardian. The point is that he also has a claim against the first, because the first was negligent in entrusting the animal to the second. The owner may say, "I trust you to swear, but not the other person." This differs from the preceding law, where the owner of the ox lawfully entrusted it to the guardian. Likewise, in the following case, where the guardian entrusts it to his son, the transfer is authorized, because anyone who deposits does so with the understanding that the bailee's wife and children may handle it.
