He is exempt. See Shach above, siman 77, seif katan 1. It is clear there that he is exempt as a damager, but nevertheless may be liable as a guarantor where those primarily liable cannot pay, contrary to the Rama in Responsa, siman 27. According to this, the same applies, and all the more so, in the opening case where the property cannot be adequately guarded except by all five together: the one who was negligent is primarily liable, but if he cannot pay, the others must pay by reason of their mutual guarantee. This requires analysis.
