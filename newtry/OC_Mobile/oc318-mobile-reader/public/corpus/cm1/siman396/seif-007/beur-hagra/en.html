These too, etc. Rambam follows Tosafot and Rashba, who explain that one who adds fuel is liable where his addition itself is capable of spreading and causing damage. Rosh and Nimukei Yosef disagree and exempt where the original fire remained under another's control. This conflicts with rulings elsewhere, as noted in simanim 381, 383, 388, and 418.
(Supplement) These too are liable. Maggid Mishneh says all the commentators explain Rambam in this manner. Rashba answers Tosafot's objection by limiting the case to fuel capable of advancing and damaging on its own. Rosh, however, exempts entirely in the sugya and this remains difficult in light of the other rulings. See 85, 381, 383, 388, and 418.
