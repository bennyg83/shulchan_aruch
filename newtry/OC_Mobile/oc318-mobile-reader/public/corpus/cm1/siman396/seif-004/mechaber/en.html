If one breaches a fence before another person's animal and the animal leaves and causes damage, then if the fence was sound and strong, the one who breached it is liable. Some say he is not liable for the animal's damage unless he struck or drove it toward the place of damage, and this is the primary ruling. Some also say that even for the loss of the animal itself he is exempt if it wandered away. If the wall was already unstable, he is exempt under human law but liable under the law of Heaven.
