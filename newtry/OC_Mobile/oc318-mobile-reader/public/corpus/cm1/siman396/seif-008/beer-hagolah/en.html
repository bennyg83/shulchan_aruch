tet) The Rambam likewise writes there, law 4.
yud) The Maggid Mishneh writes this because in these cases the owners remain liable even when the animal was guarded with excellent care. This resolves the Tur's question in seif 7. The Kesef Mishneh writes that the sages of Lunel asked the Rambam why the owners should be liable if the guardians guarded it properly, and he answered that there was a scribal error. The correct version is: if it went out and caused damage, the guardians are exempt. If they guarded it with inferior care, then if he is an unpaid guardian he is exempt and the owners are liable; if he is a paid guardian, renter, or borrower, he is liable.
