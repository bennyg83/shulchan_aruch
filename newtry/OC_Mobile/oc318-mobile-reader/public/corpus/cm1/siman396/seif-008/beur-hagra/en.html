If they guarded it properly, etc. The text here contains an error. Rambam's responsum states that with superior safeguarding all custodians and owners are exempt in every case. With inferior safeguarding of a forewarned ox, or of tooth and foot, the owner is exempt and the custodian is liable; with an innocuous ox in the hands of an unpaid custodian, the owner remains liable.
