And liable. Tosafot writes that he is liable only for the animal that caused the damage, not for the wall, because the wall was already unstable. The Rosh writes that perhaps he is liable even for the wall, since it could have remained standing another day or two until workers were found to repair it. The Tur and Mechaber appear to agree, because they simply write that he is liable. According to the Rambam, he is liable in human courts even for an unstable wall. Sema.
