There is a name for Dr. V. B. R. B. B. A.D. and a fisherman named Alibaba
The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam explains the case as an ox tied properly, yet liability still applies because it is the nature of such animals to play with their restraints and free themselves. Thus the beginning was negligent, and even if the end was unavoidable, such as digging through, liability applies. Rashba endorses this explanation. Tosafot likewise explain the statement that an ox is prone to break loose.
