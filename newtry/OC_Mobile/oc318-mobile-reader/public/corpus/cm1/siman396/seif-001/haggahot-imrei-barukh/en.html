(Mechaber, seif 2) But if it was breached during the day he is liable, because the matter becomes known, etc. Note: What is the law if the owner transferred the animal to a guardian who did not accept responsibility to prevent it from causing damage, and the guardian placed it in a stable, the stable was breached during the day, and the animal went out and caused damage? Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Metzia 14 cites the Rashba and Rosh as holding that in such a case even the animal's owner is exempt, because the animal was not in his home and he did not know that the breach occurred. This requires study in light of the Mechaber's statement here that such a breach becomes known. Perhaps the report reaches the animal's owner as well. See Shitah Mekubetzet there, which also cites the Raavad as holding that in the case described above the owner is liable even if the breach occurred at night, because his initial negligence consisted in entrusting the animal to a guardian who did not accept responsibility for damage caused by it.
