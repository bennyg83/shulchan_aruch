[Seif 4] Some say that he is not liable, and so forth. This wording implies that according to the Shulchan Aruch he is liable for damage to the animal, which contradicts seif 3, where those who acted in order to destroy it are exempt. It therefore appears to me that the text should read “Some say,” without the conjunctive “and.” The Rama is not introducing a new dispute with the preceding ruling. Rather, he is explaining the words of the Shulchan Aruch, which are the Rambam's words, whose exact object of liability is unclear, as the Beit Yosef cites from the Raavad's objection. The Rama therefore says: “Some say that he is not liable,” and so forth, as an interpretation of the earlier ruling.
