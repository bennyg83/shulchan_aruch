And the Mechaber, who ruled here like the dissenting view, etc. The version of the Tur before us caused the Rama to write this. For the correct text, see Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter 3, siman 26. The Bach did not notice this and therefore emended an already mistaken version. The Sema also did not mention the textual issue. Nevertheless, he correctly understood the Rosh's actual position, which is also the position stated in the abridged rulings, based upon Maharshal's correct reading.
