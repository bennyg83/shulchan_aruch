Fifty against fifty, etc. Dagul MeRevavah writes that all these laws, as well as what the Sema states in seif katan 2, apply where the second damage occurred before the first injured party was able to bring witnesses. At that time he had not yet become a partner in the damaging ox, as explained by the Shach in siman 401, seif katan 1. But if he was already able to bring witnesses, he becomes a partner. Any later damage suffered by the damaging ox must then be borne by him in proportion to his ownership share, aside from what he himself must pay the other owner for the damage his own ox caused to that owner's share. The accounting is therefore different and depends upon the size of each party's share. See Sema at the end of siman 404, citing the Tur.
