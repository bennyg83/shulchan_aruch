One with whom funds for the poor or for redeeming captives were deposited, who negligently guarded them and they were stolen, is exempt, for the verse says, 'to guard,' and not to distribute to the poor; this is money that has no specific claimant. This applies when the money was not designated for the poor of that locality or for particular captives. If it was designated for these particular poor people or captives and their shares were fixed, it is money with claimants, and he must pay if negligent, or swear that he was not negligent, like any other bailee. Likewise, if the charity collector said to the bailee, 'Guard it for me,' he is liable like any other bailee.
