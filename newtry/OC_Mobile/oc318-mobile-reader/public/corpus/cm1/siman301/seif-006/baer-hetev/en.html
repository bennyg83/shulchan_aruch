Exempt. Meaning, although money of the poor does not have the law of hekdesh, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the end of siman 95 and in siman 212, he is nevertheless exempt because the verse, etc. Meaning, the Torah imposed a custodian's liability only where property came into his hands for guarding for some period and was then to be returned to its owner. Here, the one who gave him the funds to guard does not want them returned to him later. One might ask that he should then be considered the custodian of the poor, charged with distributing the funds to them. To this the ruling answers that it is money with no individual claimant. Sema.
