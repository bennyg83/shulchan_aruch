[Gloss] Like any other guardian. Note: So too writes the Nimukei Yosef in Perek HaGozel. See what I wrote in the gloss to siman 292, seif 9. See Responsa Chavot Yair, siman 199, and the Mordechai there, who concludes that the charity collector must nevertheless return it to the poor and may not profit from it, for why should he conduct commerce with charity funds? We rule like Rabbi Meir, that he returns the cow to its original owners. In the source of the law in the Mordechai it states that if the collector said to him, Guard it for me, and if you are negligent you will be liable to me, he is liable; this is also copied in the Darchei Moshe. So too in Maharikash it implies that if he merely said, Guard it for me, he is not liable. It appears to me that he is liable only to pay, but if he claims that he was not negligent he need not swear. See Shach, siman 291, seif katan 49. Here it is even stronger, since he said, If you are negligent, and this wording creates only a monetary obligation, not an obligation to swear. I have written about this in a responsum, with the help of Hashem.
