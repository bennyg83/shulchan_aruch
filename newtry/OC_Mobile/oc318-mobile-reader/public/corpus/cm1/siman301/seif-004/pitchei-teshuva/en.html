One who borrows a house, because it is land. See Baer Heitev, who writes that for this reason the borrower is exempt even if the unavoidable accident was caused through his own circumstances. Responsa Pnei Yehoshua II, siman 106, indicates a practical ruling in accordance with Rama above, that the excluded categories are exempt even for negligence, unlike Shach cited in Baer Heitev. Responsa Zichron Yosef, siman 1, cited above in siman 296, seif 5, seif katan 5, likewise treats this at least as an unresolved monetary question so that payment cannot be extracted from the possessor. This is also the view of Ateret Tzvi and of Responsa Beit Shlomo Acharon, siman 6, as cited in siman 66, and Responsa Shav Yaakov, Choshen Mishpat, siman 12.
