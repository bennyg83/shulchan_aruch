If they made a kinyan with him obligating him, etc. Without a kinyan, however, he does not become obligated merely through his words. Although an unpaid bailee may stipulate to have the law of a borrower and thereby assume liability merely through speech, as the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 291, seif 27, there he is at least included within the category of bailees, and therefore can obligate himself verbally to a greater degree of responsibility than the law would otherwise impose. These categories, by contrast, are altogether outside the laws of bailees. So write the Maggid Mishneh and Beit Yosef. See below, siman 305, where the Tur and Mechaber write in seif 4 that a condition among bailees takes effect merely through speech, whether to increase liability or to exempt. For exemption no special rationale is needed, because we rule that waiver requires no kinyan, as the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 12 and elsewhere.
