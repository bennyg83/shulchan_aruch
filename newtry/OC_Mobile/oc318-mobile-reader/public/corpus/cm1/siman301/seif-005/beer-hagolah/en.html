The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam rules like the Sages in Shevuat HaDayanim, Shevuot 42b, and the commentators explain that this is so even when the produce no longer needs the land at all. This does not contradict siman 95, seif 2, and the end of siman 193, where produce standing ready to be cut is treated as movable property. Regarding a guardian's oath, since the grapes were entrusted to him to be returned together with the land, they are treated as land, as the Tur writes in siman 95, seif 6, in the name of Rabbi Yosef HaLevi.
