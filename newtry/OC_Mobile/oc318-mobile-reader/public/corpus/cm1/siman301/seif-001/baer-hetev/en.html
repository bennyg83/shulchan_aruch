Liable. The Shach objects to the Rama here and writes that the Rosh's responsum concerns only one who gave the property entirely to the second person, not one custodian who transferred it to another custodian. In the latter case the Rosh does not disagree with Maharik. The Rosh himself writes in chapter HaMafkid that a custodian who transfers to another custodian is not considered negligent even regarding negligence. This is also evident in siman 291, seif 26, where all authorities and the Tur and Mechaber rule that if the first custodian brings witnesses, etc. See there.
