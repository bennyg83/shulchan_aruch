And he must pay if he was negligent. See Responsa Noda BiYehuda, second edition, Choshen Mishpat, siman 54, on why Rambam and Shulchan Aruch mention specifically negligence. At first glance, according to their ruling in siman 267, seif 16, following Rav Yosef that one who guards a lost object is a paid custodian, the same should apply here, as Tosafot in Shevuot 42b imply, and he should therefore be liable even for theft and loss. See there for the resolution.
