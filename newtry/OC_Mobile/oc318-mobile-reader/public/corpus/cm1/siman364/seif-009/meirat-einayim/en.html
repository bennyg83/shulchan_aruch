Who seized collateral without authority. Meaning, anyone who takes collateral without legal authority has the law of a robber, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the beginning of siman 90 and in siman 359.
Since there are no witnesses. The Rambam concludes, and the Tur cites him, that the mouth that creates the prohibition is the mouth that releases it. Since his own admission is what establishes possession of the pledge, he is believed within the scope of that admission, but because he swears and collects he takes a serious oath while holding a sacred object. See siman 72. Some authorities there hold that one who claims, "I hold this as a pledge for such and such," swears only hesset because he is in possession. The Mechaber rules there that he takes a serious oath.
