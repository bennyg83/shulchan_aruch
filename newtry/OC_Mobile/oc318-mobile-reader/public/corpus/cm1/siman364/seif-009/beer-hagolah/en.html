There. The Maggid Mishneh writes that from the Mishnah, 44b, and the sugya there, this law is clear. The Mishnah specifically says they saw him, meaning that witnesses are present.
He explains there that the mouth that created the prohibition is the mouth that permits, and he swears while holding a sacred object. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Geonim agree that even one who holds collateral and is believed up to its value must nevertheless swear while holding a sacred object, like all who swear and collect.
