This is the incident of Rabbi Abba's ingot in Bava Batra chapter 2, end of 33b, and Shevuot chapter 7, 47a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's words appear to imply that one who robbed his fellow in the presence of witnesses must return the object in the presence of witnesses. Later authorities disagree, as explained at the end of siman 361.
