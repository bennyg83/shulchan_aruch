There's enough
Raavad objects: if the homeowner's claim is not a valid claim, how can the defendant be obligated to swear and why should he have to pay? The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam holds that an oath based on one witness can be imposed even on a doubtful claim, whereas an oath for partial admission requires a definite claim. The latter rule is agreed upon, and this answers the objection.
