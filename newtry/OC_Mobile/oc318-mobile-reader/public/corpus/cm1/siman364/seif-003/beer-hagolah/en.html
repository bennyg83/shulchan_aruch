Raavad questions this, since the homeowner was not present and therefore has no definite claim. The Maggid Mishneh responds that some authorities hold that an oath imposed by one witness applies even to a doubtful claim, because wherever two witnesses would impose payment, one witness imposes an oath. Even if one rejects that, Raavad himself suggested the answer: once the object is found in the defendant's hand, the claim becomes definite, even though the owner did not see him remove it from the house and only the witness saw it.
