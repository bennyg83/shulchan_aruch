Pays. The Tur and Mechaber write in siman 90, seif 3, that where one witness testifies against him, he swears that he did not rob. There the witness did not see what he removed, and even two witnesses in such a case do not entitle the injured party to collect without an oath. Therefore we do not apply the rule that wherever two witnesses obligate payment, one witness obligates an oath, followed by payment when the defendant cannot swear. Here, however, the witness saw what he took. If there were two witnesses, they would obligate payment where he entered outside the owner's presence or entered to seize collateral. Therefore, against one witness, he is one who must swear and cannot swear, and he pays. Sema.
