They saw him, etc.; for every, etc. Otherwise the rule in chapter 3 of Bava Batra concerning livestock that ordinarily wanders would be inexplicable: if one is believed where he certainly seized the animal, all the more so where seizure itself is uncertain and possession could be invoked. The same is true of goats that ate barley and of one holding a sickle, where credibility rests on the presumption that a person would not brazenly act in that manner. This resolves Tosafos's difficulty. I found the same in Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of the Ritzba. Although, etc.; and although, etc. Meaning that although in the owner's presence he may be believed in such circumstances, as stated in the chapter Kol HaNishba'in and above in siman 90. And you, etc. As above concerning roaming livestock and the other cited cases. Or for a debt, etc. As above regarding the goats, etc. There is no, etc.; and afterward, etc. As stated at the end of chapter 9 of Bava Metzia in the case of one who seized a pledge, etc.; the same applies here.
