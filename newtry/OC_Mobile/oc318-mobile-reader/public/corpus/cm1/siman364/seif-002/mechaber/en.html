If witnesses saw a person enter another's house in the owner's absence and take utensils, even openly, and even if the owner usually sells his utensils, and the owner claims they were taken by robbery while the taker claims permission, purchase, gift, or seizure for a debt, the taker is not believed. One who enters another's house in his absence, takes utensils, and removes them before witnesses is presumed a robber. He returns the utensils without an oath because the witnesses saw what he took. After returning them, he may assert whatever claim he has against the owner, and their dispute is adjudicated.
