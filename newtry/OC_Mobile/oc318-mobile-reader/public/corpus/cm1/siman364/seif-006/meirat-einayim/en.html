And concerning the remainder he swears hesset. The Rambam explains that he does not swear a Torah oath as one who partially admits, because he has not admitted liability to anything. He says, "What I seized was mine." The Tur cites this. This differs from Rabbi Chiya's rule that one who denies everything and witnesses establish part is treated like one who admitted part, because witness testimony is no weaker than his own admission. There, at least the witnesses establish part of the claimant's monetary claim. Here the witnesses do not know how much he seized, and there is no admission of liability because he says everything he took belonged to him. We therefore impose payment only for what his own words establish and do not create an additional Torah oath on the rest.
