Every person may bring manure and refuse into the public domain during the established season for removing manure. Outside that season, it may not be placed there for storage, though it may be brought out for immediate removal. Clean wastewater and other liquids that do not remain in the public domain may be poured out even in summer. The same applies to foul water if the person intends to remove it immediately. Even where one makes a depression less than three handbreadths deep, the public cannot prevent him where the use is lawful. During the manure season, the refuse may be piled there for thirty days so that it is trampled by pedestrians. Nevertheless, if it causes damage, the owner must pay. Since trampling does not improve the refuse, the Sages did not penalize him by transferring ownership of it to the injured party. Rama: Some say that after damage occurs, whoever first takes the refuse acquires it.
