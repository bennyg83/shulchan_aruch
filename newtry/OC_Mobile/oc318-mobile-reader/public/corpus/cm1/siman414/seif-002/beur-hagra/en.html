Nevertheless, etc. Although Rabbi Yehuda disputes even this, the halacha does not follow him here. In Bava Metzia the Gemara places his view among a group of rejected positions, and Bava Kamma cites the baraisa differently. Likewise, Bava Kamma 6a establishes the Mishna in accordance with that baraisa. The Rosh and other poskim.

(Collected note) And some say that afterward, etc. From Bava Kamma 21a: "shall we say concerning the pit," etc. See Rashi there. Although produce does not ordinarily improve in the pit, the case must concern circumstances where it does, which is why the Gemara speaks of a pit. This also resolves Tosafos' difficulty there concerning Rav, and the problem according to Rabbi Yehuda. (End of note.)
(Collected note) And some say that afterward, etc. The Gemara says Zeiri can answer, "I said," etc., which indicates that after damage he certainly acquires the object even where there is no improvement, since Zeiri's reason is not improvement. All the more so Rav, who takes the stricter view. Although the Gemara frames Zeiri's answer according to Rabbi Meir, it later explains that according to Rav the Rabbis disagree with Rabbi Meir only regarding horn, whose possession began permissibly, but here, etc. (End of note.)
