Everyone has to take the rubbish out. Isabel is not as damaging as a straw he slips and is also long and fall by a stick:
There is no oxen and hindrance, but rather liquid and out of itself immediately after they have been evacuated from the same place and spread and are not harmful:
So that it will be rubbed away. The Gemara explains that Yehoshua apportioned Eretz Yisrael to the Jewish people on the condition that people not be particular with one another concerning this. See siman 274, where the Tur writes that the Rif disagrees.
Nevertheless, if it causes damage he is liable. Yehoshua's condition did not include exemption from paying for damage. The condition was still necessary, because otherwise people could object from the outset. A person does not want his property to be damaged and then have to collect compensation through litigation. This is unlike the Ir Shushan, who writes that Yehoshua permitted it only on condition that each person guard his manure. That is not correct. Even if he does not guard it, the permission can apply, but he must pay for any damage it causes.
