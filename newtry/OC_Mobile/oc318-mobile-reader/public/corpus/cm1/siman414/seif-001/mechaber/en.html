When a person may place refuse in the public domain. It contains two seifim:
A person may not place his straw or stubble in the public domain so that people will tread on it and it will become fertilizer. If he does so, the Sages penalized him by treating it as ownerless, and whoever takes it first acquires it once it has been trodden and improved. Some say, however, that one should not instruct a person initially to take possession of the material itself, only of the added value; but if the owner was warned and did not remove it, the entire material is declared ownerless. Tur, seif 4. If someone took possession of it immediately after it was placed in the public domain, it is not removed from him. Some say that taking possession does not help. Ri, Rosh, and Tur there. See above, siman 388. Although it is treated as ownerless, if a person or animal is injured by it, the one who placed it there must pay.
