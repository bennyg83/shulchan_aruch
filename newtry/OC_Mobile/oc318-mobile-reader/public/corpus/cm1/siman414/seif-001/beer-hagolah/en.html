a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 13 of Hilkhot Nizkei Mamon, law 14, from the Mishnah in the third chapter of Bava Kamma 30a and Rav Ashi's interpretation there. The meaning is that the material is placed there to fertilize fields and vineyards.
bet) Ibid., in the Mishnah, following Rav that liability concerns both the substance itself and its increase in value, on 30b.
gimel) Straightforward from the Gemara there according to Rav Nachman's answer.
dalet) The Rosh writes this in his rulings from the Gemara's interpretation. The Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh write that the Rambam views that interpretation as a mere rejection. In my humble opinion, although there is an explicit statement of Rav Huna in the name of Rav there, the Rambam understood that Rav retracted it in the sugya at the beginning of 51a.
heh) The Gemara's interpretation there.
vav) An unresolved question there. Following his general approach in uncertainties of monetary law, the Rambam holds that if the claimant seized, it is not removed from him. The Tur writes likewise in the name of the Ramah.
zayin) Ibid., in the Mishnah.
