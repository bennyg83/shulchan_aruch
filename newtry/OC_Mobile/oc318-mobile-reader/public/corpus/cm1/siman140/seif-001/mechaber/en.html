The law of one who possesses another's land, and the law of presumptive ownership of houses and shops. It contains 15 se'ifim.
Land remains under the presumption of its owner. Thus, if witnesses know that land belonged to Reuven and was in his possession for even one day, but Shimon now possesses it, consumes its produce, and claims he purchased it, while Reuven protests that Shimon stole it from him, Reuven is believed. He takes a heses oath and recovers his property, unless Shimon established proper presumptive ownership together with a valid claim. In that case, the land is not removed from Shimon; he takes a heses oath and retains it. If Reuven has no witnesses that the land belonged to him, Shimon need not establish presumptive ownership. He takes an oath and retains it even without having held it for the required period. See above, the beginning of siman 95, where it is explained that only a heses oath is taken concerning land.
