Land remains in the presumption of, etc. See Responsa Rabbi Aharon Sasson 115, Mahari ben Lev 71, Maharashdam 228, and other responsa for many applications of these rules. The Shach also cites numerous cases.
Known land, etc. Rashba writes that even if witnesses do not know with certainty that the land belonged to Reuven, but they saw Reuven or his father living there, we presume it was his and remove it from the later possessor unless the latter has three years of possession or evidence of purchase. The same can follow from a strong public reputation of ownership. Sema cites Rosh for reputation. See Tumim. I wrote that relying on reputation alone to extract requires study. Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe cite Rashba as implying that mere public reputation does not suffice. Rosh's responsum used reputation only together with additional circumstances. One should not extract solely on rumor.
