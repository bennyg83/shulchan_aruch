The tenants' testimony that they lived in the house by day and night is effective only when the rent is still in their possession and they say, 'We will give it to whichever party prevails in judgment.' Although the possessor has not yet received the house's rental income, their living there under his authority constitutes his benefit from its produce. This applies even if he lent it to them without charge. If they already paid the rent to the possessor, they cannot testify because they have an interest in the matter: they wish to keep him in possession of the house, since if it is removed from him they will have to pay the rent a second time to the protesting claimant. Nevertheless, even if they already paid the possessor, he may return the rent to them so that they can give it to whichever party prevails. They then have no interest in the testimony. Some disagree.
