Some say we require, etc. This differs from itinerant merchants and other traders who may be away from home for substantial periods yet still establish possession after three years. Their periods of absence are irregular and unpredictable. Shopkeepers, however, have a known regular period of nonuse each night. A known recurring interruption is therefore counted differently. The same is not true of synagogue seats, which have no ordinary use except during prayer times, so possession is measured according to the nature of that property. A shop, by contrast, is a room fit for use day and night. Sema.
