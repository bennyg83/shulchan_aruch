The language of Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilkhot To'en, law 3, from the statement in Chezkat HaBatim, Bava Batra 29b, that Rav agrees regarding the shops of Mechoza that are used during the day, according to Rambam's explanation that three years of daytime use suffice. Maggid Mishneh writes that Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash appears to agree with Rambam, and some other commentators write likewise. Tur cites this in seif 11.
Those who disagree hold that the shops were not destroyed at night, but that despite the intermittent pattern the possession counts. Still, complete years are required, and therefore the possessor needs six calendar years so that the daytime periods alone add up to three years.
