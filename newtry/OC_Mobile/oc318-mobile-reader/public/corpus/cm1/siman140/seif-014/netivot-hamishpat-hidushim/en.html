Merchants' shops. This differs from traveling peddlers and merchants, who need not make up for time absent from home, because their absences have no fixed schedule. Shops, however, have known closing times. Nor is this like synagogue seats, where there is no other normal use beyond sitting there when one comes to pray, and therefore it resembles a field where each form of produce use is assessed according to its nature. A shop is at least fit for habitation at night, and therefore the missing nights must be made up. Sema.
