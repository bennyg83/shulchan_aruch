If the claimant does not know, etc. The Shach correctly writes that if the claimant does know the facts, the witnesses can become obligated in hesset. Tosafot and Ramban explain the same. See Tumim. I discussed why this is not simply a migo dependent on two people, and rejected Perishah's and the Shach's earlier explanation. I offered two better possibilities: where two persons coordinate on a financial benefit, they can function together for migo analysis; or the case is one where both rented jointly and each is guarantor for the other's obligation, so they are themselves litigants rather than ordinary witnesses.
