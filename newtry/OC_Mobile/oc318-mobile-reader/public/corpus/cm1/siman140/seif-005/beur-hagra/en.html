The Jerusalem Talmud, Shevuot, chapter HaDayyanim, likewise states that one who comes with both a deed and possession cannot rely on possession after retracting and claiming that the deed was lost. Sefer HaTerumot records the same. The Jerusalem Talmud adds that one who possessed on the strength of a document later found invalid has no possession. The dispute parallels Rabbi and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, and the dispute concerning two sets of witnesses in Sanhedrin 23b. According to Rif's interpretation, the deed must be produced, and where the claim was based on both deed and possession, the possession is nullified when the deed fails.
