But if they forgot. See Sema, seif katan 16. Netivot HaMishpat writes that there is a scribal error and the text should read, 'Therefore we require two,' not 'therefore we require a deed.' The Sema's question is why two tenants are needed here, whereas one purchaser suffices. His answer is that there are no witnesses other than the tenants themselves, so two are necessary. Dagul MeRevavah offers another explanation. He writes that these words of the Sema belong at the end of the preceding seif katan and constitute a second answer to the Raavad. Earlier the Sema answered that the challenger may say, 'I felt secure.' Here he adds that the challenger did not fear that the tenants would claim they themselves had purchased the property, because he knew there were no other witnesses and they could not testify for themselves; nor did he know of the landlord's claim, since they had rented without a written lease.
