What constitutes presumptive possession, etc. Responsa Maimoniyot, at the end of Hilchot Ishut, siman 5, states that three years of possession operates only against one who claims title to the body of the land. A person who has only a debt lien or ketubah lien upon the land does not lose that lien through another person's years of possession. Thus a creditor or ketubah claimant may collect from the purchaser even though the purchaser held the land for the prescriptive period, because the lienholder can say: I did not bother to protest since the land itself was not mine, and perhaps I would ultimately find unencumbered property from which to collect. Darkei Moshe cites the extended discussion.
