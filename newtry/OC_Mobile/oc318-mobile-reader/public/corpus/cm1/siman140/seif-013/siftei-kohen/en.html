Even if the two did not both reside there, etc. We rule like Rabbi Yehoshua ben Korcha that witnesses in monetary matters may combine, even regarding land. See above, siman 30. The Sema adds that where one lived there in years one, three, and five and the other in years two, four, and six, all agree that chazakah is established even if they rented without a written contract. He means even according to Rashba. According to the Raavad, however, a written contract is required, as explained above. The Sema's wording is therefore imprecise. See the Beit Yosef, who cites Rashba in agreement with Rabbeinu Yonah, and Responsa Rabbi Shmuel Kohen, volume 2, siman 6.
