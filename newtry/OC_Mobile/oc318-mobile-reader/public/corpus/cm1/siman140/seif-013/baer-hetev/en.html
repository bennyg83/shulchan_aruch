Together. See above, siman 30. It appears that if one occupied the property in years one, three, and five, and another in years two, four, and six, all agree that even if they rented it without a written lease their possession establishes chazakah, since each occupied it for three years and their periods combine as though they had occupied it together. See what I wrote in siman 145:2. The following statement, however, that even six persons may establish the possession, follows each opinion according to its own rule: according to the first view a written lease is required, while according to the alternative view it is not. Sema. See Responsa Rashach II, 6.
A path. The Taz writes: One may ask what proof this provides, since perhaps when the seller sold the property he reserved a path for himself. The answer is that the possessor bears the burden of establishing his chazakah in a manner that leaves no plausible basis at all to invalidate it, even a mere possibility. See the Taz's further discussion.
