Together. See above siman 30. It appears that if one tenant lived there in years one, three, and five, and another in years two, four, and six, then according to all opinions it is a valid chazakah even if they rented without a written lease, because each one lived there for three years, and by combining their periods it is considered as though they lived there together. See what I wrote in siman 145, seif 2. But the Mechaber's following case, 'even six,' etc., is according to each opinion's own requirements: according to the first view, with a written lease, and according to the alternative view, even without one. See Sema.
