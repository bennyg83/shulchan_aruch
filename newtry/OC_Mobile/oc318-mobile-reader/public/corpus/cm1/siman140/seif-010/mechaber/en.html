Even if the tenants already paid the rent to the possessor, if the possessor comes to beit din before the protesting claimant appears and says, 'Let the tenants testify before you that they lived there for three years,' his request is valid. At that time, they have no interest in the testimony because no protesting claimant is before us. {Rama: Although testimony is not ordinarily accepted outside a litigant's presence, here the witnesses are presently eligible, and if they wait until the protesting claimant arrives, they will have an interest in the testimony. Their testimony is therefore accepted in his absence, as stated in Responsa Rashba 1026. See note 111.}
