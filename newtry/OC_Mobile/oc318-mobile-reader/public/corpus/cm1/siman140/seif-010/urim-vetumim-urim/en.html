Even if they already paid him. See Tumim. I left this ruling requiring study because the tenants appear interested witnesses. Their testimony is needed only if a claimant later appears, and if he does, the tenants have a financial interest in validating the possessor so they are not exposed to a second rent claim. Bach's comparison to siman 37, where a remote possibility of future enrichment does not create interest, does not persuade. I offered alternative readings of Tur in Rabbi Yonah's name, but Mechaber's unqualified rule is difficult if rent was already paid.
We accept their testimony. Rabbeinu Yerucham cites Responsa Rashba: since no opposing claimant is presently before us, their testimony may be accepted. Shach.
