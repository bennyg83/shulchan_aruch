They disagree with all of this. Their disagreement concerns the Mechaber's rule that if the challenger adds a particular claim, testimony of the tenants themselves is specifically required. Rabbeinu Tam and the Rosh hold that neighbors' testimony always suffices. According to the Sema, neighbors' testimony is always specifically required and unrelated witnesses do not help. The Shach disagrees and holds that if the possessor says generally, "Witnesses will testify for me," ordinary witnesses may also suffice.
