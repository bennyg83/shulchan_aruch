He would place, etc. In my humble opinion, the primary interpretation of the Gemara is that Rav Nachman, in Rabbah's resolution, retracted his first resolution, unlike the Tur. I further consider the rulings of Rambam and Rif, and the resolutions of Rav Pappa and Ravina, primary. Ravina's resolution appears to be missing from the Rosh, for otherwise it is astonishing that he would disregard Rav Pappa and Ravina, who are later authorities. This also appears from Bartenura. The Beit Yosef's discussion here does not appear primary. See Rivash, siman 248, who likewise writes that Rav Nachman retracted his first resolution.
The owner of the courtyard claimed. It appears to me that the text should read, 'against the owner of the courtyard.' See Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 94, and Responsa Mabit, volume 1, siman 109.
