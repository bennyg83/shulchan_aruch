And even. The Sema writes that the Rama's formulation is difficult. He writes without qualification, 'and even if he had a share,' until, 'it constitutes a chazakah,' which implies that the chazakah of partners regarding a matter over which people ordinarily object is like the chazakah of an unrelated person. This is not so, for the Rambam and Ri Migash hold that a partner acquires an immediate chazakah regarding a matter over which partners ordinarily object when the other partner did not object, because we say that he certainly waived his right entirely. It is possible that the Rama wrote as he did because he rules in accordance with what the Rivash wrote in responsum 241, that even a partner does not acquire an immediate chazakah. Since the Mechaber did not state the Rambam's opinion explicitly, the Rama likewise did not introduce his ruling as 'there are those who say.' See Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 94, and Responsa Mabit, part 2, siman 109.
