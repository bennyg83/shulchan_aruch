In my humble opinion Rambam rules according to Rabbi Yochanan in the name of Rabbi Bena'ah that partners may object to one another regarding all such uses, Bava Batra 57b. Since his fellow used it for three years with a claim of right, it becomes a chazakah. Therefore Rambam does not distinguish between an ordinary courtyard and the open area behind the houses according to Rav Nachman's limitation there. Rif likewise writes there. Rambam apparently understands the Mishnah's rule that making a partition establishes a right as discussing a case where there was not three years of possession. He records that rule in Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 5, law 5. The Maggid Mishneh writes there that the Mechaber's words concern a case without three years of chazakah. See there.
