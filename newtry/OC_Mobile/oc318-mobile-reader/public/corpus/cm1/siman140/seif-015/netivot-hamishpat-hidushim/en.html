Whether he erected a partition there. The general rule is as follows. In another person's courtyard there is no difference between erecting a partition and merely placing something there. Three years of chazakah together with a proper claim are required. Without them, even a partition does not help, because we say he was permitted temporarily to place it there. Since he seeks permanent exclusion of the owner from the land itself, a full chazakah and claim are required. In a jointly owned courtyard the law is reversed. Erecting a partition creates immediate chazakah because partners ordinarily object to a partition, and silence indicates waiver. There the partner does not claim ownership of the land itself, but only a right to keep animals in that place, and silence concerning something partners normally object to is treated as waiver. But if the partner claims ownership of the actual ground so that the other partner may no longer use it at all, then three years of chazakah and a proper claim are also required, in the manner explained in siman 149 concerning chazakah between partners.
