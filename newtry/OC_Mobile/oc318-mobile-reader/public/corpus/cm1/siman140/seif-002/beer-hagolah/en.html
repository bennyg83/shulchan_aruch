Rashba writes this in a responsum. In my humble opinion it refers to the second case in the preceding seif: if Reuven has no witnesses, Shimon does not require a chazakah but merely swears. The Mechaber then adds that where the challenger has no witnesses and the possessor has held the property for three years, he is not even subjected to an oath. Sema likewise offers this as his first reconciliation, while describing Rama's gloss as strained.
