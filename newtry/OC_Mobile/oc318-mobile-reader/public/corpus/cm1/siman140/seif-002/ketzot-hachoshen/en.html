Maharam, Rashbam, and Rashba hold that where the co owners have not yet established one against the other, the court does not place the matter into an oath framework. This resolves Tosafot's difficulty in Perek Chazakat HaBatim. Rashi explains that the parties needed the special arrangement because they had no witnesses of partnership. Tosafot asks that if there were no partnership witnesses, the one who possessed first should simply claim the whole property. Under the present rule, however, so long as neither has established himself against the other, he does not confidently deny because a denial would expose him to an oath. Once each has already possessed against the other, the court no longer gives one side the opportunity to gain the whole merely through an oath. The arrangement is therefore understandable.
