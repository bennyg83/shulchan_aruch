Hagahot Ashri to the chapter Chezkat HaBatim, however, cites Ra'ach that if a person purchased land from another and has a document, and within three years the seller claims, “You later sold it back to me,” while the purchaser remains in possession of the land, the purchaser need not take a hesset oath. Even if he has no document, where one person claims against another, “You sold me this land,” but is not in possession and has no deed, provided the defendant responds, “It never happened,” the same discussion applies. See there. The Mordechai in the chapter Shevuat HaDayanim cites a similar ruling from Rabbi Chizkiyah. The matter requires study.
