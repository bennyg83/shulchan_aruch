That witnesses come. This differs from siman 70:2, where one who says, 'I paid before so-and-so and so-and-so,' may be required to bring them only initially and, if they do not come, can swear and be exempt. There payment need not have been before witnesses at all, and the request merely seeks clarification. Therefore even if the witnesses later contradict him, he may remain exempt with hesset because incidental details are not always remembered. Here a prescriptive title is ineffective unless he claims he had a deed and lost it. If he says there is a deed, and now one is produced, it must be authenticated. Ran and Sema.
