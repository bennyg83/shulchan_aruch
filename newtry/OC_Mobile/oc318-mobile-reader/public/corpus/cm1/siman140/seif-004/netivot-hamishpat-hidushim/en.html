There he need clarify the witnesses only initially, to avoid an oath, and even if they came and contradicted him it is ruled there that he is exempt because people do not remember incidental details. A chazakah, however, is effective only when he claims that his document was lost. If the document is actually in his possession, he must authenticate it.
