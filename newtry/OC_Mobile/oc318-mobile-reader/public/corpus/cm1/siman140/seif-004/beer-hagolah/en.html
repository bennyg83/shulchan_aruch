Rambam likewise writes at the beginning of chapter 15 from the baraita recording the dispute of Rabbi and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel at the end of Bava Batra 161b, following the Gemara's final explanation on 171a that they dispute whether a litigant who has made a specific factual assertion must clarify it. The conclusion is that the halachah follows Rabbi, and even Rabbi requires clarification only to establish the truth.
The Halachot explain likewise, and Tosafot at the beginning of 171b write that Rabbeinu Tam interprets "to clarify" as requiring verification of the facts and establishing the truth. If the witnesses are found and say, "We never saw this," the claimant is liable, but if the witnesses cannot be found he does not lose merely because of that, because it is unreasonable to make his claim fail solely because he cannot prove a fact that he was not required to mention in the first place.
