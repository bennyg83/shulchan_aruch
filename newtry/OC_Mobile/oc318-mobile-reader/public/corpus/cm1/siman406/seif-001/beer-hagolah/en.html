A. A.D. is a matter of b.
Rambam writes in chapter 8, law 5, that the reason is that gentile law does not obligate a person for damage caused by his animal, and therefore we judge them according to their own law. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is Rambam's own reasoning. In response to Raavad's objection, he writes that perhaps in the time of the Gemara gentiles did not collect such damages at all.
As the Rambam, this fine is to the UN because it is not cautious in the plagues and does not remove the damage, and if it does not commit to damage to the provision, it does not preserve them and loses much of the barrenity
