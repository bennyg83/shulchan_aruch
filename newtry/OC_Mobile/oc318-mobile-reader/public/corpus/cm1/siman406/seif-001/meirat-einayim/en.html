If it gored an ox belonging to a gentile, he is exempt. Scripture says "his fellow's ox," excluding the ox of a gentile. On that basis one might have expected the reverse as well, that a gentile's ox which gores a Jew's ox should be exempt. The Sages derive liability from verses describing Hashem's appearance from Teman and Paran. In Bava Kamma they explain that the nations accepted the seven Noahide commandments and did not observe them, and Hashem therefore permitted their property in this respect. Rabbi Yochanan derives the point from "He shone forth from Mount Paran," referring to the nations' refusal of the Torah. It is surprising that the Ir Shushan combined the two verses and interpretations as though they were one derivation.
