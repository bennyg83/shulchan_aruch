Tur seif 2, from Ravina's establishment and the baraita in the name of Rabbi Yehudah there. In my humble opinion this requires study, because the passage concerns a forewarned animal, as Rashi and Tosafot explain. But with an innocuous animal, just as the victim collects from a purchaser, as written at the beginning of the following siman, so too he should collect from one who acquired it after the owner declared it ownerless.
