They are exempt. See the Tur, who writes that if the animals have established a pattern of goring, beit din appoints a guardian for them. The Mechaber omitted this, and the Rama likewise did not record it. It appears that the reason is that this law is not practiced in our time, for an ox cannot become formally forewarned in Babylonia. Even if it became forewarned in Eretz Yisrael, came to the Diaspora, caused damage, and the injured party seized payment, so that it is not removed from him, this is still an uncommon case. Therefore they did not record it and did not mention the law of a guardian here at all. Shach.
