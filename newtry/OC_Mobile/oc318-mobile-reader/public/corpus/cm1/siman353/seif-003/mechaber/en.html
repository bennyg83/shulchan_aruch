When the owner's despair is joined by a transfer of possession, whether the despair preceded the thief's sale or followed it, the purchaser acquires the object, though some disagree. The purchaser need not return the object itself, but if he bought from a notorious thief he must return its value. Some say he need not pay even the value because he acquired it completely. If the seller was not a notorious thief, he returns neither object nor money because of the marketplace enactment, as explained in siman 356.
