The Maggid Mishneh wonders there, for according to Rav Zevid's establishment Rabbi Yochanan holds that change of possession before despair does not effect acquisition. Perhaps Rambam holds that since Rav Pappa, who is later, establishes the case differently, where there was no despair at all, we return to the general rule that despair together with change of possession effects acquisition regardless of which came first, so long as both ultimately occur. This is somewhat supported by Merubah, 65a.
