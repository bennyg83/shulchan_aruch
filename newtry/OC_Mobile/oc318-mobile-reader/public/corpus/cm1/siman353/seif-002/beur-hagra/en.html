A change of possession without prior despair does not acquire according to any view. Rosh and Tur nevertheless rule stringently like Rabbah, and Rama rules likewise in Even HaEzer 28. See 28 and 968.
And if there is, etc. Tosafot in Sukkah and Bava Kamma teach that a weak change of name may join with despair, but without despair it does not acquire. Rosh, however, speaks of despair together with an inferior change of name. Tosafot offer two approaches: either only a substantial and irreversible change of name acquires, or even an inferior change of name acquires without despair. Under both approaches a reversible change of name does not acquire. Tur's formulation follows Tosafot in Sukkah, not the passage cited by Beit Yosef. (End of supplement.)
