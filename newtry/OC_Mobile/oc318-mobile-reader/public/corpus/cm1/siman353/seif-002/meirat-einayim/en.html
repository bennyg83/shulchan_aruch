The Tur concludes that the Rosh held: Since Rava was uncertain whether this acquisition works by Torah law or only rabbinically, although we do not follow Rava in his doubt as to Torah acquisition, but rather Ulla and Rabbi Yochanan who hold that he did not acquire by Torah law, if he used the object to betroth a woman she nevertheless requires a get out of doubt, because perhaps he acquired it rabbinically. It is surprising that Rama did not cite this even as a dissenting view. Presumably he relied upon the ruling in Even HaEzer concerning such betrothal.
