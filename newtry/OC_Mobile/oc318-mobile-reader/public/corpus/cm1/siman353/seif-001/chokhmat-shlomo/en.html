Seif 1. "The name of the stolen object changed, etc." Note: A reversible change does not effect acquisition. See my novellae to Hilchot Lulav, siman 649, in my work on Orach Chaim, section marked 102, page 124, where I explained the Yerushalmi at the beginning of the chapter Lulav HaGazul and proved that one must distinguish between a reversible change where the object can be restored while the change remains, and a case where it cannot be returned to the victim until the change itself is undone. See there and examine carefully.
