[Shulchan Aruch] Some say that he acquires it. Note: This is also the view of Tosafot in Sukkah chapter 3. The Baal HaMaor and Ramban in Perek HaGozel UMaachil, however, hold that a change which can revert to its original state is ineffective even together with despair of recovery. Regarding a complete change of name that cannot revert to its original state, all authorities hold that it effects acquisition even without despair, except the Baal HaMaor, who holds it ineffective. See Derishah, who writes simply that according to Tosafot and the Rosh even a physical change that can revert to its original state effects acquisition together with despair. With due respect, he overlooked Tosafot in Sukkah, who prove that for this purpose a physical change is weaker than a change of name.
