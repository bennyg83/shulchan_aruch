A ram. Maharshal, chapter Merubah, questions this ruling, because the Gemara there explicitly concludes that although a change of name can effect acquisition, a lamb that becomes a ram is not considered a change of name, since even a one-day-old animal may be called a ram. See there at length. The Shach writes that there is no difficulty. In every area of law we follow ordinary human usage, even where that produces a lenient result, and our own observation shows that people do not call a one-day-old animal a ram or an ox. The Gemara there likewise implies this. See there, where he brings an additional proof from Nedarim.
The theft. In this particular change, where a lamb becomes a ram, the thief also pays the double payment according to the value at the time of the theft. The laws governing other changes, and cases in which the value rose or fell, will be explained in siman 354. See there. Sema.
