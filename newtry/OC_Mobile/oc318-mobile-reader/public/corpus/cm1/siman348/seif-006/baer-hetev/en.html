The second. The Rosh concludes, as cited by the Tur: The rule that where an item was robbed and the owners had not despaired, they may collect from whichever party they wish, applies specifically where the second person robbed it from the first. Since the owners had not despaired, it is as though the second person robbed it directly from the owners. But where he did not come to steal, and merely assisted the first thief after the first had already acquired it, this does not apply. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 1, siman 178.
