One who, etc. As stated in chapter 10 of Shabbos, that one who assists, etc. The Rosh writes that this is unlike the rule, “He robbed and the owners did not despair,” where the owner may collect from either party, because there the second person robbed it from the first. He does not mean specifically that the second forcibly robbed it from the first, for the same rule is stated concerning an heir in Bava Kamma 111b, a purchaser there 115a, and in Chullin 138a. Rather, he means that the second intended to acquire it.
