[Sema, subparagraph 8] And in a courtyard belonging to both of them. Note: From the plain meaning of the Mechaber below, at the end of siman 351, regarding one who dragged an item and took it into the public domain, and from his requirement that the thief joined his hand beneath three handbreadths, as well as from the Tur cited by the Sema, it appears that they follow Ravina’s interpretation that meshichah in the public domain is effective at least for purposes of theft. See Tosafos there.
