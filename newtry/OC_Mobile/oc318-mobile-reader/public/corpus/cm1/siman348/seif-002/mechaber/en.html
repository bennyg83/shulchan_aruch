Anyone who steals even property worth only a perutah violates the prohibition of "You shall not steal" and must repay it. This applies equally to stealing the property of a Jew or of a non-Jew, and to stealing from an adult or from a minor. Rama: A mistake made by a non-Jew, such as misleading him in an accounting or avoiding repayment of his loan, is permitted provided he does not become aware of it, so that no desecration of the Divine Name results. Tur, seif 3. Others say that one may not actively mislead him, but if he made the mistake on his own, it is permitted to benefit from it. Mordechai, chapter HaGozel Batra.
