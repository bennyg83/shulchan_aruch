For the prohibition of "you shall not steal." Meaning the verse "you shall not steal" in Parashat Kedoshim, which concerns theft of money, as learned from its context, since it is adjacent to "you shall not deny falsely" and "you shall not lie." The prohibition "you shall not steal" in the Ten Commandments concerns kidnapping, which carries capital punishment, likewise inferred from its context next to "you shall not murder" and "you shall not commit adultery."
Or one who steals the property of a non-Jew. This is analogous to robbery from a non-Jew, which is prohibited.
Some say that it is also prohibited to deceive him, and they derive this from the fact that even deceiving a non-Jew's mind is prohibited.
