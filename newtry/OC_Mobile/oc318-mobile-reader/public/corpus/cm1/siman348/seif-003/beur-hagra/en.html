Who is a thief, etc. This is Rambam's wording. Theft is specifically secret taking. Otherwise, even if the owners do not know at the moment, the taker is a robber. The phrase 'the owners do not know' is not absolute, because an armed bandit may still be classified as a thief despite being seen. Raavad, Tosafot, and the Gemara's other discussions support this distinction. Rambam's example of seizing the spear states the clear case, while 'the owners' excludes mere onlookers. Tur and Sema explain likewise. The concluding clause excludes an armed robber. See siman 501.
