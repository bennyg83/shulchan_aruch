To pay. Although Shimon had already lifted the stolen item, removed it, and thereby acquired it, while in the preceding seif Shimon was exempt for that reason, the cases differ. There he was exempt for the assistance he gave in removing it, and after it had been removed the thief came and took it from him. Here, however, Shimon himself returned the stolen item to Reuven, even though he could have retained it and returned it to the owners. Therefore he is liable under the law of one who safeguards a lost item. Sema.
