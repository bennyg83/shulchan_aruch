For it is forbidden to deceive him. See Baer Heitev and Shaar Mishpat, who writes that even according to Maharshal, if a non Jew gives money to a Jew to purchase merchandise on his behalf and the Jew makes a profit from it, although it is initially forbidden to act this way because deceiving a non Jew is prohibited, the Jew need not return the profit. Since there is no halachic agency for a non Jew, the Jew is treated as having purchased the merchandise for himself. The profit is therefore from his own property. He brings proof from Orach Chaim, siman 450, seif 6. See there.
