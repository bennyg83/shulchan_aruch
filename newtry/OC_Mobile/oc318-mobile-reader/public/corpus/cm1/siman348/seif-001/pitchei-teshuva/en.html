Even by way of jest. Shaar Mishpat cites Shitah Mekubetzet, siman 261, in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, that one may not even steal back his own property from a thief in a manner that makes him appear to be a thief. This is an explicit teaching in Bava Kamma at the beginning of HaMeniach: Ben Bag Bag says, "Do not enter your fellow's courtyard to take what is yours without permission." Where loss would otherwise result, however, one may enter another's courtyard, as Tosafot and Hagahot Ashri explain there. Shaar Mishpat expresses surprise that Rambam, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch omitted this law.
