(Seif 8: He is not subject to liability) The Sema cites from the Darkei Moshe two opinions, but the proposed proof from David and Uriah is unpersuasive. David was not exempt from responsibility merely because there is no agency for a transgression on the theory that Joab was liable. Joab did not know that Uriah's death was unlawful; he believed David had validly condemned him. That ignorance is enough to preserve David's responsibility, but it does not make Joab liable. More fundamentally, it is unreasonable to punish an agent who, according to the facts known to him, performs a permitted act. Rashi and Tosafot in Merubah agree with the Mordechai on this point, and that is the halachah.
