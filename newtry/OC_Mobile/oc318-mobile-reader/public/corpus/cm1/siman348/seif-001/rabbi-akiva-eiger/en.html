[Shulchan Aruch] Anyone who steals. Note: If one stole an article from another person while that article was itself borrowed in the victim's possession, and the victim compromised with the lender for a small amount, the thief need pay him only the amount for which he settled with the lender. Shiltei Giborim, Bava Batra chapter 1. See Responsa Bnei Yaakov, siman 13.
