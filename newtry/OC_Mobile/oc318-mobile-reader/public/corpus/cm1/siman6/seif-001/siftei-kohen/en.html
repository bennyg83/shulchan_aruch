Less than a perutah, etc. With respect to utensils, beit din addresses a claim even worth less than a perutah, as stated below, siman 88, seif 3. So writes the Ran in chapter Shevuat HaDayanim, cited by Beit Yosef at the beginning of siman 88. This also appears from Rabbeinu Yerucham, netiv 3, part 2, and from Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilchot To'en, whose language the Mechaber cites in siman 88, seif 3. However, according to the authorities cited there by the Sema in seif katan 5, who hold that less than a perutah is not considered money even in the case of utensils, it appears that the same rule applies here. See there carefully.

They conclude the case even for less than a perutah. The Semag rules likewise. The Sema, however, rules in accordance with the Rosh that even if they originally convened for a claim worth a perutah, they do not conclude the case for less than a perutah. Rabbeinu Yerucham, netiv 3, part 2, also writes so. See Imrei Binah, page 74.
