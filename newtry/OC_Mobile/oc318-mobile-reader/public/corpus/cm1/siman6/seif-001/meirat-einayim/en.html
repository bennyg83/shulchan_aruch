Less than a perutah. Such an amount is not called money for purposes of theft. See the Derishah.
They complete their judgment even for less than, etc. This means that even if after the original claim was adjudicated, the defendant now counterclaims for less than a perutah, beit din hears and completes that matter. The reason is that it remains one judicial proceeding, because if the defendant prevails his amount will be deducted from what the claimant is recovering. Rashi and Kesef Mishneh explain it this way. The Mechaber therefore changes Rambam's singular wording 'they complete his judgment' to the plural 'their judgment,' teaching that the defendant's claim is also heard and completed.
Even for less than a perutah. The Mechaber rules like Rambam and not the Rosh, as the Tur cites. The Beit Yosef brings proof for Rambam from the Gemara. In the Derishah I argued that the Gemara's wording in fact supports the Rosh and that the Beit Yosef's proof is not decisive. Accordingly, in my opinion the halacha follows the Rosh, that even after beit din has already become involved over a claim worth a perutah, they do not complete a separate claim for less than a perutah.
