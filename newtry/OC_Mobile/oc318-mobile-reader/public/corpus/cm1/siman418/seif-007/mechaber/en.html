One who entrusts a fire to a deaf-mute, mentally incompetent person, or minor is exempt under human law but liable under the law of Heaven. This applies when he handed them a coal and they fanned it meaning that they blew it into a flame, because a coal ordinarily goes out on its own before traveling far enough to ignite other property. But if he handed them an open flame, he is liable because his own act caused the resulting damage.
