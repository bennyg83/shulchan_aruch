The Sema writes that the same would apply if Reuven told an agent who is not legally liable, such as a deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor, to tear Shimon's garment, and the agent did so before Shimon while Shimon remained silent: Reuven would be liable because Shimon had no reason to protest. The Shach finds this surprising, for the Mishnah states that one who sends a fire in the hand of a deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor is exempt. See Tosafot, Bava Metzia 10a, and what I wrote at the beginnings of simanim 182, 292, and 348. Shach.
