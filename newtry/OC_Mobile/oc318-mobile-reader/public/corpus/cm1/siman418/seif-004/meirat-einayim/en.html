For this is a blow from Heaven. This is the correct text and the Rambam's wording. The reason for exemption is that this event is considered a heavenly occurrence, even though ordinarily one who leaves a damaging object with permission can still be liable, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the end of the preceding siman. See what I wrote in the Derishah to siman 410:5 in the Rif's name concerning the distinctions among these cases.
