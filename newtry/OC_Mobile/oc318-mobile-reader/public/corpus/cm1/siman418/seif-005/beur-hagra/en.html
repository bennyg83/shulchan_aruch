(Supplement) When does this apply, etc.; it has no fixed estimate, etc. This appears to refer to the adjacent discussion of height, as also implied by the earlier ruling that sufficient distancing exempts without distinction. Tosafot, Rosh, and other decisors, however, apply it also to width, because the baraita's phrase 'even one hundred mil' necessarily concerns width. Rif makes no distinction between an upright flame and one bent by wind because we follow Rabbi Shimon.
(Supplement) With a blazing fire. Semag and Hagahot Maimoniyot write that this is the reading in old manuscripts, and it is also the Yerushalmi's text.
