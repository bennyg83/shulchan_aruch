Seif 18, some say a minor slave, and so forth. The Sema writes that one is liable for a minor slave when the master is present, just as for an ox. This is difficult, since the slave will eventually become obligated in mitzvot, and the master will certainly remove him from danger. I am also puzzled by the statement that one's ox is inferior to one's slave. We rule in siman 272 that preventing animal suffering is a Torah obligation. Just as one relies on the master not to leave his slave to burn, one should likewise rely on him not to leave his animal there and thereby violate the Torah prohibition of causing animal suffering. This requires study.
