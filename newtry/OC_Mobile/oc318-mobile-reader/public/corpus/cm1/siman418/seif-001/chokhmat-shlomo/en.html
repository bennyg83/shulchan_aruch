Seif 1. "Fire is a primary category of damage, as it is written, 'If fire goes out and finds thorns,' etc." Note: We rule that one's fire is treated as one's arrows. In my work on Orach Chaim, Hilchot Chanukah, volume 95, siman 56, I proved from a sugya in Menachot that even according to the rule that fire is like one's arrows, this is so only when the person brings the fire to the combustible object, thereby acting upon the fire itself. If the reverse occurs, and he brings the combustible object to an existing fire without acting on the fire itself, then according to all opinions the fire is treated as his property rather than as his arrows. See there, where with the help of Heaven I brought a compelling proof. Examine carefully.
