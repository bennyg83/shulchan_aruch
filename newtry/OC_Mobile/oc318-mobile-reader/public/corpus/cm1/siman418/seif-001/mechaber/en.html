Liability and exemptions for damage by fire, concealed property damaged by fire, and all related laws. It contains eighteen seifim:
Fire is a primary category of damages, as the verse states, "If fire goes out and finds thorns." It is one's property that travels and causes damage at a distance through the wind that carries it. Therefore, anything similar, namely one's property that travels and causes damage, is a derivative of fire and has the laws of fire, including the exemption for concealed property.
