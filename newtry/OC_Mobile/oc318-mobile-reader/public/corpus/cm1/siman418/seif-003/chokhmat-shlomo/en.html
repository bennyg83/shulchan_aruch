Seif 13. "A camel that was loaded with flax, etc., and even if it was a Chanukah lamp, etc." Note: There is room to question this in light of the Rambam's view that there is no mitzvah specifically to place the Chanukah lamp within ten tefachim, as he rules in Hilchot Chanukah. If so, the Rambam could seemingly have explained the owner's exemption on the ground that the lamp should have been placed above the height of the camel and its rider. See my work on Orach Chaim, Hilchot Chanukah, siman 671, in the section marked 102, where I resolved this point and discussed several additional observations concerning this law. See there.
