With wheat. Rashi explains that he gave permission to stack barley, not wheat. The point must be that one might have thought only in the first case, where permission was for barley and the other person stacked wheat, changing entirely from the owner's authorization, is he exempt for wheat. But where barley was stacked, perhaps he should at least be liable for the wheat covering it. The ruling teaches otherwise. Ibid.
