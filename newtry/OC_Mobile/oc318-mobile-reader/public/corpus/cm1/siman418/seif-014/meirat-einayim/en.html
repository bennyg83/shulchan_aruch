Meaning that the lender of the place made a fire, etc. Meaning, he kindled the fire on his own property and the force of his original act had already ceased, as explained above. The ruling teaches that we should not say that because Reuven gave Shimon permission to stack produce in his courtyard, it is as though Reuven accepted responsibility to guard the stack. One might have thought that even Rif and Rambam, who disagree in siman 398 with Rabbeinu Yitzchak and Rosh and exempt the courtyard owner when his ox damages an ox that entered with permission, would concede here because he nevertheless lit the fire with his own hands. The ruling teaches otherwise.
