Brothers who wish to divide an inheritance, and all laws of the adjoining landowner's right of preemption. This siman contains sixty-three seifim.
If not all the brothers are present and the brother before us wishes to divide the estate, he may divide it in the presence of three persons, even ordinary laymen, provided they are trustworthy and knowledgeable in valuation. If he divides it before fewer than three, the division is ineffective. If the estate consists of money, all of one currency and equal in value, three are unnecessary; two witnesses suffice merely as evidence. Rama: Likewise, if the assets are deposited with one person and some of the brothers appear, their shares are given to them in the manner stated above.
