In the words of the column, it is written, and there is an end to the end of the page, and it is not included in the reciprocality of it:
Ba'al HaItur writes this in his first formulation. The Maggid Mishneh cites it in his name in Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 12, law 5, and says it also appears from Rambam.
There in the Gemara: if one of them preceded and purchased, his purchase is valid. Rambam's wording is followed here. The students of Rashba explain that the purchase is valid whether he bought from the original seller or from the purchaser. Although we treat him as the neighbor's agent, we do not say he became agent for all the neighboring owners. Once he bought for one of them, that suffices. The Maggid Mishneh writes in chapter 12 that this is Rambam's view.
Tosafot in the name of Rabbenu Tam likewise write this regarding the incident of Runya, Bava Batra 5, and Rashba in Responsa siman 91.
These are Rambam's words in chapter 12. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is obvious according to Rambam's approach.
