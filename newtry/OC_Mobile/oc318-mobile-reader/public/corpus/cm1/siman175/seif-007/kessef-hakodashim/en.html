Seif 27: A row of date palms. A right of passage adjacent to the property being sold gives the holder the status of a bar metzra, just like one whose property is directly adjacent to his house. He can expand his boundary into any area subject to his right of passage and build there. Even if he has windows there, he can say that he will close his own windows and build. And even if his fellow has windows there facing him, if he can lower the structure on that side so that there will be no invasion of privacy, the properties are considered joined and the row of date palms does not constitute a separation. If, however, the right of passage is designated for the public, or jointly for him and his neighbor in a manner that prevents building there, he does not have the status of a bar metzra, particularly against one whose wall actually adjoins the property.
