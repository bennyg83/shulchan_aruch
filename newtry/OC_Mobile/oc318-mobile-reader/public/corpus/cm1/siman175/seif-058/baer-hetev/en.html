The mortgage. See Responsa Raanach, siman 82, from folio 125 onward, where you will find satisfaction on this issue. See also there, simanim 106, 114, 119, and 125, and Responsa Maharashdam, siman 228. Shach.
To remove him. The Beit Yosef follows his own view that the Rambam disagrees with the phrase 'or mortgaged,' as explained in seif 45. This is not compelling, because the meaning may be: Perhaps this field is mortgaged to you and the borrower will wish to buy the field adjoining his own. This is the Rama's view when he rules principally that a creditor has the status of an adjoining owner. Yet in seif 45 the Shach agreed with the Mechaber. Thus far the Shach. The Sema and Taz understand that the Rama did not decide the matter in that broad sense. Rather, he ruled only that if the creditor himself bought the adjoining field, the original adjoining owner cannot remove him, but not that the creditor can remove someone else who already purchased it. See their explanations there.
