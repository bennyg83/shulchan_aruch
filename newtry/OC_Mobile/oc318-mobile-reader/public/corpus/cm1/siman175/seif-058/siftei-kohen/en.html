If at the outset of the mortgage, etc. See Responsa Rabbi Eliyahu ibn Chaim, siman 82, where you will find satisfaction from page 125 onward, and simanim 106, 114, 119, and 125; and Responsa Maharashdam, siman 228.
The lender cannot remove him from his possession, etc. The Beit Yosef follows his own view that the Rambam disagrees with the phrase, 'or the field is mortgaged,' but this is incorrect. The meaning may be: perhaps this field is mortgaged in your possession, while the borrower wishes to purchase the field adjoining his own. This is the Rama's principal view, namely that a lender has the status of an adjoining owner. Above in seif 45 he nevertheless agrees with the Mechaber's ruling. Understand this.
