[Shach, subparagraph 27] And another version at length. Note: Their words are copied at length in Responsa Masas Moshe, siman 66. The same applies where the seller and purchaser are in one place and the neighboring owner is in the place where the house is located; there too the law of a neighboring owner does not apply. Maharikash writes: "If the neighboring owner is in the city where the land lies, while the seller and purchaser are in another city, Maharil wrote that the neighboring owner may remove the purchaser. Others disagreed with him, even concerning a partner. Since this is a dispute among the authorities, we do not remove the property from the purchaser." End quote.
