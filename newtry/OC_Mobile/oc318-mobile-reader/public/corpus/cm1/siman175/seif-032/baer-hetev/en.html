In judgment. If the adjoining owner learned of the sale during Chol HaMoed and brought the money immediately after the festival, he does not lose his right. Although cases are adjudicated during Chol HaMoed, beit din does not sit there as regularly as on ordinary days. So writes Responsa Mabit, siman 86. See Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 84; Responsa Maharam Alshakar, siman 118; and Responsa Mahari ibn Lev, part 3, siman 63. Shach.
