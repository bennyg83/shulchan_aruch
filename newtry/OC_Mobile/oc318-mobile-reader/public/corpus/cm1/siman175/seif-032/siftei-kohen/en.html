The time required for him to go and bring money, etc. If he learned of the sale during Chol HaMoed and brought the money immediately after the festival, he does not lose his right. Although judicial proceedings may be held during Chol HaMoed, a beit din is not regularly convened then as on ordinary days. This is stated in Responsa Mabit, siman 66. See also Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 84; Responsa Maharam Alashkar, siman 118; and Responsa Mahari ibn Lev, part 3, end of siman 63.
