Seif 51: If one of them sold his share. The Beit Yosef asks, according to the Rosh and Rabbeinu, who hold that even a right to rebuild after the house falls is considered a limited right for a known period, how the Rif can say that the owner of the building prevents the landowner from selling. What right remains for the landowner to sell? He answers that the building occupies only part of the land, while the other half remains his. This is difficult. Why does Rabbeinu call him a partner who is superior to an adjoining owner? At the beginning of this siman, partnership is superior only where the boundaries are not identifiable and every step is jointly owned. Here, with respect to the remaining land, he is merely an adjoining owner.
