Rambam there, law 11, and Tur likewise writes in his name. Maggid Mishneh explains that responsibility toward the neighboring claimant rests upon the purchaser. Since if the neighbor does not wish to buy, the purchaser cannot force him to do so, the purchaser remains responsible for the land. If the land stays with him and is later foreclosed, he returns to the original seller; if the seller lacks funds, the purchaser bears the loss. Therefore even after the neighbor removes the purchaser, if the land is later foreclosed the purchaser remains the party responsible for warranty, because it is as though he resold it to the neighbor. Kesef Mishneh gives another reason; see there.
