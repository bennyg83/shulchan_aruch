(Supplement) This applies, etc.; but, etc. Just as one is not believed regarding a gift carrying responsibility to say, 'It was a gift,' even with an oath, because there is a presumption that a person does not accept responsibility, etc., so too here. This also explains the statement that it was written in the deed, etc., for there is likewise a presumption that one does not accept responsibility for more than he received. So too in the laws of an adjoining owner. See Baer HaGolah. (End of supplement.)
