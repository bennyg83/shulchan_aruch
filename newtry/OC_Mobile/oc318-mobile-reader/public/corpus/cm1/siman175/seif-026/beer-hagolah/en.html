The language of Rambam there, chapter 14, law 1, from the Gemara on 108b: where one party wants the land for planting and another for houses, bar metzra law does not apply. Rashi explains likewise, and Maggid Mishneh writes that this is true even if the one who wants it for planting is the adjoining owner. Baal HaIttur and the Halachot likewise rule so, according to the textual version cited above. Beit Yosef writes that this appears to apply equally in Eretz Yisrael and outside it, because the Gemara does not say that houses have priority due to settling Eretz Yisrael.
Tur, seif 43, in the name of the Rosh, from the statement on 101b that regarding a house we listen to him, but regarding a field we do not, because of settlement of Eretz Yisrael.
