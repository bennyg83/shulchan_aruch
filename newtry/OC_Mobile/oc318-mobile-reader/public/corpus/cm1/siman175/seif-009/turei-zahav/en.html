Seif 12: The same applies if he purchased it. In Rabbeinu's text the phrase is “and it need not be stated,” while the Beit Yosef questions this wording and substitutes “the same applies.” Rabbeinu's wording can be explained. The first clause teaches the strength of a doubtful claim even against a definite claimant. It therefore need not be stated that a doubtful claimant prevails against another person, even though that person is currently in possession, since Rabbeinu does not base the rule upon the distinction between one who is in possession and one who is not.
