Where the debtor himself voluntarily gave it to the creditor, the rule is that an appraisal does not later revert, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch state in siman 103, seif 10. Consequently, when the adjoining owner removes the creditor in such a case, he likewise need not return the field to the debtor. Ibid.
