He is in the field of the field:
The land returns forever. This refers to land that beit din appraised and transferred to a creditor in collection. If the borrower voluntarily transferred the property to the creditor, we hold that the appraisal does not later return to the borrower, as stated in siman 103:10. Accordingly, a neighboring owner who displaced the creditor in such a voluntary-payment case also need not return the field to the borrower, because the borrower himself voluntarily gave it to the creditor.
