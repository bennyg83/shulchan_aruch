And there is one who says. The fact that he was not known to possess such means would not, by itself, have caused us to reject him, as in the incident of Bar Ziza in the Yerushalmi. Rather, there were also grounds for suspicion because he did not purchase immediately. Tosafos similarly write in Kesubos 85b, s.v. "and furthermore," that a sign is required as well, creating grounds for suspicion.
