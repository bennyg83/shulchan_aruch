And specifically... This follows the law of indirect causation in damages, Bava Kamma 60. They infer from the statement that he is placed under a ban until he accepts, etc., that otherwise he is exempt. See Beer HaGolah. He is nevertheless placed under a ban to remove the harm because causing damage indirectly is prohibited, as stated in the second chapter of Bava Batra. The first opinion holds that these are like his direct arrows. See siman 155. Although he is liable, he is placed under a ban to remove the harm because the other party may say, "I do not wish to trouble myself litigating with him afterward," as stated in Bava Kamma 15b.
