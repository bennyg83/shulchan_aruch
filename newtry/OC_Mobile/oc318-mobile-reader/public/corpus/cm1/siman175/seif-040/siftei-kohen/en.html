One who sells to a non-Jew, etc. See Tosafot to the chapter Lo Yachpor, Bava Batra 21a, s.v. 'and not to a gentile scribe,' the Rosh there, and the Maadanei Melech to the chapter HaGozel Batra.
Any loss caused by force that may come, etc. If one purchased from a non-Jew a house adjoining a Jew's property, he may rent it to a non-Jew, but he may not sell it to one. See Maharshal, Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 10, siman 104, where he follows his own reasoning; Responsa Mahari ibn Lev, part 3, siman 118; Responsa Rama, siman 86, page 169, column 4; and Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 233 and 281.
