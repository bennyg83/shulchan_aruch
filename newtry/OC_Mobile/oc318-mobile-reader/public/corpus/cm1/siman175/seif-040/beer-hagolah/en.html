Tur, seif 59, in the name of a Gaon. Beit Yosef writes from section 23: if one resident of an alley sold his share to a gentile and accepted liability for coercive losses caused by the gentile, and the other residents later claimed that the gentile forcibly took their money, but they have no witnesses, since an ordinary gentile is presumed coercive and the seller cannot swear that no loss occurred because he does not know, and all the residents make the claim together, we do not say they all joined to rob him. Rather, they swear what the gentile took and collect from the seller. Sema cites this ruling.
