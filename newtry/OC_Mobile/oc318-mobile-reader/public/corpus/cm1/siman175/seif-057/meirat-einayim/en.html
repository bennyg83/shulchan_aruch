Nimukei Yosef writes that even according to the opinion that a lender holding a mortgaged house may displace a neighboring owner who purchases land adjacent to that house, which Rama adopts as the primary view at the end of the following seif, the law here is different. There the mortgaged house in his possession stands upon the adjoining land, so it is as though everything is one property. A cellar beneath the house, or another house standing on land that was not mortgaged to him, gives the lender no such connection to the land. Darkei Moshe likewise records this cellar law after first ruling that a lender has the status of a neighboring owner.
