Shaar Mishpat writes that the wording of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, "that they colluded with one another," implies that the seller also says that he sold it for two hundred. This is difficult in light of Rama in siman 87, seif 6, who rules that even where one takes payment by oath, a supporting witness exempts him from the oath so that he collects without swearing. Why, then, should the seller not be treated as a supporting witness, as explained in siman 222? It therefore appears more correct that the present case concerns a seller who is disqualified from testimony or is related to the purchaser.
