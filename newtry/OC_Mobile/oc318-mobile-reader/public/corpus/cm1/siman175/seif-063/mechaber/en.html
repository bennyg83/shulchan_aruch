If a person rented his house to one person and later sold it to another, even if the purchaser is not an adjoining owner, the tenant cannot remove him. Rama: If Reuven owns land from which he pays Shimon an annual tax, and Shimon sells the right to that tax to Levi, and Reuven claims the right of an adjoining owner, then if Shimon sold the tax right permanently, Reuven prevails. If it was sold only for a fixed number of years, it is merely the sale of a debt, to which the law of adjoining ownership does not apply.
