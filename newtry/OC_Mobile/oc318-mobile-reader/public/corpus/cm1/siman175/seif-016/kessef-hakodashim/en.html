For his own maintenance. Some say that a sale for one's own basic subsistence is not subject to the law of bar metzra. By reason, the same should apply to the subsistence of one's wife and dependents whom he is legally obligated to maintain, not merely expenses assumed to avoid embarrassment under social custom. Even where the obligation is not fixed but communal pressure would publicly shame him for failure to support them, perhaps this too resembles his own subsistence, since Chazal regarded shame as extremely severe. The exemption should nevertheless apply only to minimal necessary support, without which he cannot manage even under hardship, not to increased household comfort. The term 'support' includes clothing, as in siman 60. Perhaps medical expenses likewise remove the bar metzra rule, though there may be a distinction between treatment with a fixed cost and open-ended expenses. If he sells in order to repay money borrowed for his own sustenance, perhaps this too is outside the bar metzra rule.
