One who appointed him. Kesubos 98a, regarding a widow, and as stated above in siman 185 seif 2.
And when he sold to others. There, where he said to him, "Go and sell..." This applies specifically when he said so to him. But if he himself sold it, it does not apply, because a person may happen to make a statement, whereas an act is different, as stated in Bava Batra 32a: "Did he not consult you?" According to whom, according to Admon? Even according to the Rabbis, etc.; see there.
