Seif 53, in the gloss: The law of adjoining ownership applies to houses. It appears to me that if the houses are not actually contiguous, but a public walkway separates them, the law of adjoining ownership does not apply. This follows from the Beit Yosef's reason in the name of the Raavad that adjoining rights apply to houses because it is common to connect them by doorways. That reason does not apply where a walkway separates them.
