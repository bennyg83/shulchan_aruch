And he cannot say, 'Give me what I paid,' etc. The Mechaber follows his position in the Beit Yosef, seif 39, that the Rambam does not disagree with the Ramah. Therefore he wrote above, seif 23, that the same applies where the purchaser paid first: the adjoining owner can remove him only by tendering money of the same kind as that paid. This too was written in my youth.
