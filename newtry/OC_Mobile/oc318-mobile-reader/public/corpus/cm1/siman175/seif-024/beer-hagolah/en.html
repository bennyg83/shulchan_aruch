Rambam, Hilkhot Shekhenim, chapter 13, law 4. The Maggid Mishneh writes that from here it is clear that Rambam follows the view that the law of a neighboring landowner applies even to houses, as noted in his name above. The Ittur likewise writes that the site of a synagogue is treated as land, and anything subject to chazakah is subject to the law of a neighboring landowner. The law concerning exchange is straightforward, since the neighboring owner cannot give the purchaser the particular courtyard the seller wishes to receive.
So writes the Kesef Mishneh there.
