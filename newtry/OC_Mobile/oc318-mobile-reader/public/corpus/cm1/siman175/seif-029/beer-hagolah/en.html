A. The Rambam is named after Hashem
As the Nehardeans said, and the halachah was ruled in accordance with them there, 108a.
The Ran and Nimukei Yosef explain that the neighboring owner is regarded as though he has some degree of acquisition in the body of the land. Nevertheless, an act of kinyan with him is sufficient, even though it resembles a mere verbal kinyan.
