His agent against his will. This is difficult, because the seif does not say that the seller specifically consulted the adjoining owner and told him, "Go buy it." It implies that if he merely consulted him about whether to buy, this would not be enough. The case must therefore be that the seller said to the adjoining owner, "Acquire it and I will give it to you," and the adjoining owner said, "I do not want it." The purchaser then acquires free of the adjoining owner's claim.
