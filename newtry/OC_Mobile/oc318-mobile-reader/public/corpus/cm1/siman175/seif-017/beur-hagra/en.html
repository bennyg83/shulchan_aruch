The purchaser died. This resembles the rule that an appraised property returns, which likewise rests on the requirement to act properly and uprightly. The Gemara at Bava Metzia 35a states, 'If he inherited it,' etc.
Some disagree. There the Gemara explains that the heirs originally entered the transaction with that understanding. That reasoning does not apply here, because even the purchaser certainly acquired the land intending to keep it, yet we nevertheless remove him under the law of the adjoining owner. This resembles the case at Bava Metzia 101b: 'If he sold it or bequeathed it,' etc.
