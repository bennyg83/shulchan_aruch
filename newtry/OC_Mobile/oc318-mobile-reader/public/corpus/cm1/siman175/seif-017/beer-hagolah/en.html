Tur, seif 31, in the name of a Gaon. Ramah likewise writes so, and Baal HaIttur cites Rav Hai. Beit Yosef explains the reason: the law of bar metzra is only because of "You shall do what is upright and good." It is therefore reasonable that a person wants his heirs to remember him through the inheritance he left them, even if he left them other property; the more he leaves, the more they remember him. Likewise he desires that a gift recipient remember him through the gift, whereas money may simply be consumed. There is another reason with a gift recipient: since the recipient himself is not subject to the special bar metzra duty in the same way, beit din does not remove him. The Sages placed the duty of uprightness upon the party holding the land. See Mordechai in Eizehu Neshekh.
