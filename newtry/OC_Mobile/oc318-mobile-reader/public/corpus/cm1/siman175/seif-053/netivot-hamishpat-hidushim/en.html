Synagogue seats. If the judge sees that uprightness and goodness require not removing the purchaser, he may rely upon the authorities who hold that the adjoining owner's law does not apply to synagogue seats. Sema. The Shach disagrees. If two seats cannot be joined into one usable seat, there is no adjoining owner's law.
