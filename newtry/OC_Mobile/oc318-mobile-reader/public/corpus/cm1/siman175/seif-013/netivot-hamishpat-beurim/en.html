It appears that even if the two fields are adjacent, the purchaser cannot say, "Since you are leaving me one field, I am now an adjoining owner just as you are," because at the time of the sale he was not an adjoining owner. This applies where he bought both fields at one time. But if he bought them one after the other, the adjoining owner can remove him only from the field purchased first, not from the second field when they are adjacent, for by virtue of the first field the purchaser became an adjoining owner with respect to the second just as the original adjoining owner is.
