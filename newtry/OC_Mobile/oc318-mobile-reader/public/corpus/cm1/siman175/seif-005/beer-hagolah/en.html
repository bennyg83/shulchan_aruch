The language of Rambam, chapter 13 of Hilkhot Shekhenim, law 4. Maggid Mishneh cites Rav Nachman's statement in HaMekabel, Bava Metzia 108a, concerning one who acquired, etc. There are other explanations, but according to all of them the Mechaber's ruling is correct.
Tur, seif 5, in the name of his father the Rosh, who writes in that chapter and sugya according to Rashi that this is reasonable. He also cites Rabbi Yehudah Barceloni. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that this is the principal ruling. Baal HaIttur likewise derives it from Rav Nachman's rule that ordinary bar metzra law does not apply in that case, yet brothers and partners nevertheless may remove the outsider and recover the property. Rashba likewise writes in a responsum.
