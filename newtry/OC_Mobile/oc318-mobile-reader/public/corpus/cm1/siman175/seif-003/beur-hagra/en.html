And even though he had... Tosafos write there that their dispute necessarily concerns such a case. Otherwise Shmuel would not have said that they reduce proportionately, for why should the absent brother lose by taking without a lottery? Rather, they should cast a lottery, and if that field falls to him, they should reduce proportionately. Yet even in such a case Rav says the division is void, because it was made in error. This is what is meant by "and even if he consented..." as stated there on 103b concerning a marshy field, etc.
