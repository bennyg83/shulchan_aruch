Brothers. See the Shach, who cites Hagahot Ashri at the end of the chapter HaKones regarding the Yerushalmi incident involving the sharecropper of Bar Ziza, who deposited a litra of gold with a certain person. He discusses the matter at length and concludes: Whenever such a case occurs, where one dayan ruled one way and gave half to the adult heirs, and afterward another dayan ruled the opposite way, it is a doubt of law whether the adult heirs must give to the minors. If the minors seize property from the adults, it is not removed from them. The same applies wherever such a situation occurs. See also the end of siman 62. Shach.
