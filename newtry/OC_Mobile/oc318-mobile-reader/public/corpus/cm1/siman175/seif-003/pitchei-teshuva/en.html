The partner removes him. See Baer Heitev concerning the case in which the seller was permitted to retract because no kinyan had yet occurred. Neot Deshe, end of siman 20, infers from the Shach that once a valid kinyan has taken place, the seller cannot use this argument to retain the field for himself. The underlying reasoning was sufficient only to release him from an oath, not to undo an acquisition. Once the seller's proprietary power in the field has ended by kinyan, he is legally like a stranger to it. There is no basis to distinguish whether the kinyan preceded the adjoining owner's protest or followed it. He nevertheless concludes that the matter still requires study.
