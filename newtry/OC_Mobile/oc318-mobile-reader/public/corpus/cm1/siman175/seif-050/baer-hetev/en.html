Acquired. The Sema writes that the Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh cite the Itur that a Torah scholar can remove a neighboring resident, and the Hagahot likewise state this. It requires study why the Mechaber omitted their view. It appears to me that today we do not apply the formal status of a Torah scholar for this law. Nevertheless, initially one should sell to a Torah scholar even nowadays. Taz.
Who lives. The Rama writes similarly in siman 253, seif 15. See there.
Who disagrees. This requires study. I have not seen anyone who disagrees, and the marginal reference to Rashi in the chapter HaMekabel is incorrect. Shach.
