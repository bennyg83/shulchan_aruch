Seats in the synagogue. Concerning neighboring-owner rights from seats in the old synagogue to seats in a new synagogue: if the new synagogue is in another location, there is certainly no right of preemption. Even if the old synagogue was demolished with the intention of constructing the new one, that does not create such a right. It appears that even if the new synagogue is built on the very site of the first and contains precisely the same number and arrangement of seats, the law of neighboring ownership still does not properly apply. Nevertheless, an equitable compromise should be arranged.
