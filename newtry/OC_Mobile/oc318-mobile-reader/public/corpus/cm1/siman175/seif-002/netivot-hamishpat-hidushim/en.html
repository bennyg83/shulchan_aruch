We listen to him to undo it. This depends upon the dispute in siman 174:5 whether a division made by lot can later be undone through an offer of additional money. Shach. The Shach further rules that where some heirs litigated before one judge and prevailed, while other heirs later litigated the same issue before another judge and also prevailed, in circumstances where the first heirs are not obligated to return what they obtained because they acquired it through possession, the authorities dispute whether the first heirs must share with the others. One view treats their gain like a found object or outside gift that need not be shared. Another treats it like a gift that effectively came through the father and therefore must be divided among all heirs. If the later heirs seized their share from the first heirs, it is not removed from them.
