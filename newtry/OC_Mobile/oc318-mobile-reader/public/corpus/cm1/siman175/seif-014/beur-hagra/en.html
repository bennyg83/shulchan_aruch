A husband. A husband is deemed a purchaser with respect to his wife's property, Kesubos 95b, "she arose and married..." See also Bava Metzia 35a and Bava Batra 139a to b.
Even if they acquired. For his right is stronger than hers, Kesubos 83a; and if she sold, the sale is ineffective, there on 78.
She arose... and likewise the slave. According to their respective laws of sale, as Rambam writes in chapter 30 of Hilchos Mechirah; see there. This law is straightforward, like one who purchases for another without the latter's knowledge, as stated in Bava Kamma 102a: Rabbi Shimon said, this is what it means, "one who purchases..." If because of this, there is no difficulty, etc.
