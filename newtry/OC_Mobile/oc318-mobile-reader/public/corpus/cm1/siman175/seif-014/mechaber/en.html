A husband has the right of an adjoining owner with respect to his wife's property. Thus, if she owns melog property and adjoining land is sold, the husband may remove the purchaser. Even if the wife formally waived her right in favor of the purchaser, the waiver is ineffective and the husband may remove him. If the wife herself removed the purchaser, or if a servant who conducts business with his master's assets removed the purchaser, the husband or master may ratify the act if he wishes. If he does not ratify it, the property returns to the purchaser, who receives his money back.
