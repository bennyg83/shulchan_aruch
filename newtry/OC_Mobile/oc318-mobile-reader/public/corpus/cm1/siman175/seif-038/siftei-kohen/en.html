The adjoining owner cannot remove him, etc. Even though the purchaser subsequently rents it out, even to that same non-Jew, he may nevertheless say, 'I drove the lion away from the property itself.' So writes the Beit Yosef in siman 157, citing the Mordechai in the name of a responsum of Maharam. If one purchased from a Sadducee, namely those called Karaites, Rabbi Eliyahu ibn Chaim discusses the matter at length in responsum 125 and likewise inclines to the view that the adjoining owner cannot remove him.
