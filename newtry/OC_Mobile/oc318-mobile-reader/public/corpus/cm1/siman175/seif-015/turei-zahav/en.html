Seif 32: If the adjoining owner delayed. Rabbeinu, citing the Rosh, writes that the Rambam implies that so long as the adjoining owner did not see the purchaser demolish and rebuild, he may remove him even after a long time. The Rosh disagrees and rules as the Mechaber does here. The Rosh's inference from the Rambam rests on the Rambam's specific mention of demolition and rebuilding. The Rambam's reason that otherwise no one would buy land does not refute this, because the purchaser can protect himself by requiring the seller to warn the adjoining owner and ask whether he wishes to buy at that price, as stated in seif 31.
