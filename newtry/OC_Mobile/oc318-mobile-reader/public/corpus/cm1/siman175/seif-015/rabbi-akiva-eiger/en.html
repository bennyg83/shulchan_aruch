[Shulchan Aruch] One who sells to a woman. Note: Responsa Maharanach, part 1, end of siman 119, wrote that since they sold it for their sister's dowry, it is equivalent to a woman selling, to which the law of the neighboring landowner does not apply. See there. It follows all the more so that if the purchaser bought and designated the land in order to give it as his daughter's dowry, to transfer the land to her betrothed, this is equivalent to a woman purchasing, to which the law of the neighboring landowner does not apply.
