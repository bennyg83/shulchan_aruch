If the property was sold to a non-Jew, a non-Jew is not subject to the law of bar metzra. Rama: If one purchases land adjoining that of a non-Jew, some say that the adjoining Jewish owner on the other side cannot remove him under bar metzra. Beit Yosef. Others say that one should not rely on this alone unless there is another independent reason why he cannot be removed. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 338.
