Some say that an adjoining owner cannot place another person in his stead to remove the purchaser under the law of adjoining ownership unless he first transfers to that person an ownership interest in the original adjoining land on account of which the claim is made. This limitation applies only to actually taking the property from the purchaser. For purposes of standing in the adjoining owner's place to tender the money to the purchaser on time, however, even if the money was sent through an agent appointed before witnesses, the agent is regarded as standing in his place.
