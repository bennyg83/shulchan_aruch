For his own sustenance. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, siman 95, who writes that this means that he sells property for money needed to purchase food. But if he sells in order to conduct business and earn his livelihood, that is not included in a sale for sustenance, because even if we say that he must delay selling until he offers the property to the adjoining landowner, no real harm results.
