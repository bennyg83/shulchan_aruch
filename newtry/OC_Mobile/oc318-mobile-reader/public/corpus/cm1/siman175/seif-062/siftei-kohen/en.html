[“The renter of his house, O Lord, see the answers of his heart, and he shall see him.”
In which he resides. Even if the other person already purchased his share, the first may remove him from that share. So writes the Beit Yosef. This is coherent according to the Rama, but the Beit Yosef's own rulings conflict. In responsum 28, reflected in seif 63, he rules that a tenant cannot remove another purchaser, whereas here he writes that one tenant may remove the other even after the latter purchased his share. The contradiction may be resolved only with difficulty.
