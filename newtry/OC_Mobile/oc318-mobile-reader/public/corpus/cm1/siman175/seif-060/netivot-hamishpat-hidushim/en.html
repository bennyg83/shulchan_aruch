Rental has the law of an adjoining owner. This means that if the tenant first purchased the adjacent field, the adjoining landowner cannot remove him. But the tenant himself also cannot remove an outside purchaser. This concerns a field adjacent to the field he rents. With respect to the very house he rents, the Taz concludes that the first tenant may remove a later tenant who rented it for a period beginning after the first lease ends. Beit Yosef writes that if the second tenant rented it only after the first lease had already expired, and the owner had not yet renewed the first tenant's lease, the adjoining rule does not apply.
