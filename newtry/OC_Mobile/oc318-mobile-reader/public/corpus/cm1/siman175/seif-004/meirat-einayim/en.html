For example, if he made it an apotiki, etc. The Tur also writes this. Otherwise, the creditor ordinarily would not collect his entire debt from only one heir. The law of an apotiki is explained in siman 117. Even without an apotiki there are cases where the creditor collects the whole debt from one heir, for example where the amount due is no more than the value of one field, in which case that heir cannot force the creditor to collect half from the other, as stated at the end of siman 107. Another example is where the other heir received his inheritance elsewhere and the creditor is not required to travel to property left by the borrower in another place, as explained in siman 111. The Tur and Mechaber abbreviate here and rely on those primary discussions.
