Alternatively, his debt amounted only to the value of one field, in which case the debtor cannot defer him by telling him to collect half the debt from the second heir, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch state at the end of siman 107. Another possibility is that the second heir took his inheritance elsewhere, for a creditor is not required to collect his debt in other places, as stated in siman 111. Here they abbreviated and relied on what was explained at the source of the law. Sema. (See what the Taz writes on this matter.) The laws of the adjoining landowner's right of preemption follow.
