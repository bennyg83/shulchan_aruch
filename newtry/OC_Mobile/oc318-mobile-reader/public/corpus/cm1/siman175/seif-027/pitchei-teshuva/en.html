One who has a house under lease, etc. See the Taz, who discusses this at length and concludes that with respect to leasing that same house, the first tenant certainly has priority over a later tenant who seeks to displace him after the first lease has expired, and it appears that even the Rambam agrees to this. Netivot HaMishpat likewise cites this. Ketzot HaChoshen writes, however, that this applies only where the first tenant does not have a larger household than the second, for if his household is larger the law of bar metzra does not apply. Netivot HaMishpat writes that the Beit Yosef indicates that if the second tenant leased the property only after the entire first lease had already expired, and before the landlord had renewed it with the first tenant, there is no law of bar metzra in his favor.
