He removes him. The Tur writes: Even where the adjoining owner made a kinyan in favor of the first purchaser, he may remove the second purchaser. The second purchaser cannot say to the adjoining owner, "Be silent, or I will return the deed to the first purchaser," because he gains nothing by returning it. We therefore say: When you actually return it, the consequences will follow, as explained in simanim 118 and 119. Sema.
