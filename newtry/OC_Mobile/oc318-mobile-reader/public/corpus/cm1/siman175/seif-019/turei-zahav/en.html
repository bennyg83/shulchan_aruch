(Seif 42: the law of a neighboring landowner does not apply.) The Sema writes in seif katan 73 that this applies even if he borrowed money for one of these purposes, etc. This is very surprising. Tosafos and our master write that even a loan is included only regarding taxes, food, and burial, which are indispensable human needs. This is unlike the cases of inferior and superior land, which involve only some monetary loss. This is obvious. The Beit Yosef's wording likewise implies this, for he writes that our master understood taxes and food to be analogous to burial, showing that the same rule does not apply to the other categories. Further proof is that our master writes the extension to loans only concerning those specific matters. Although Rambam lists all these matters in one clause, that proves nothing because Rambam is not discussing whether one borrowed for those purposes. It therefore appears obvious to me that there is a scribal error in the Sema and that his statement belongs to the following law.
