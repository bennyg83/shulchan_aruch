The Tur adds that even where the neighboring owner made a kinyan releasing his right to the first purchaser, he may nevertheless displace a second purchaser. The second purchaser cannot say, "If you remain silent, fine, but if not I will return the deed to the first purchaser," because he gains nothing by the return. We answer him: Return it when you actually do so, as explained above in siman 118.
