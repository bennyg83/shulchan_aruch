There, seif 21. Rosh writes this in his rulings and derives it from the following law, and more.
There in Bava Metzia 108b: with a woman, orphans, and a partner, the law of bar metzra does not apply. Rosh cites an explanation that where land adjacent to a field of two partners is sold to one partner, the other neighboring owner cannot claim, 'I am certainly adjacent while you may ultimately receive a portion elsewhere.' The partner is treated as certainly adjacent because he owns a share throughout the field. Hagahot Maimoniyot in Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 12 cites this explanation in the name of Rabbenu Tam.
Tur writes this as an obvious extension. Beit Yosef writes that it is not an a fortiori but an equal application. Ran writes this law in the name of Rabbi Yehudah Barceloni regarding one who acquired land among brothers, etc. The scholars of Nehardea say that even if he did not buy the partner's actual field but another field adjacent to the partnership field, the partners may remove him because each owns a share in the partnership field adjacent to that person. We rule like the scholars of Nehardea.
