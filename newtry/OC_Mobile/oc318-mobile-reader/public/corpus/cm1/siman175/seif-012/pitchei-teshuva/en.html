For it and the other one. See Baer Heitev regarding the dispute whether the rule differs when there are two properties or more. Netivot HaMishpat sides with the Shach. Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, siman 95, however, rules like the Taz and the implication of the Sema, and writes that the words of Sema and Taz should not be set aside because of the Shach's youthful suggestion to emend the text from "another" to "its sister."
