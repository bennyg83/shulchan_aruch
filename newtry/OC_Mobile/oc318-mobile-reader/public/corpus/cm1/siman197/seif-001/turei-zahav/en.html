Seif 5: In the public domain he does not acquire. The Rambam's text of the Gemara reads that in the public domain he acquires, apparently for every person, and Rashi explains that the rider fears the public may separate him from the animal. The Tur's text distinguishes an important person in the public domain. The Sema correctly questions the Rama's addition, “or in the city, in the public domain.” I am also surprised that he did not question the Rama's first gloss, “in a field or alley,” because the Rambam's essential distinction between an important person and another person applies specifically in an alley, while the Rama rules that everyone acquires there.
