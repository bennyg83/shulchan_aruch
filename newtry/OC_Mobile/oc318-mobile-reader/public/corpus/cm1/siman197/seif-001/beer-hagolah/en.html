So writes the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 2 of Hilkhot Mekhirah regarding Rambam's statement that it is acquired by meshichah. This is the conclusion of the sugya in the first chapter of Kiddushin, 25b: both this and that are acquired by meshichah, but not by mesirah, for meshichah is superior to mesirah. This is the principal ruling. The Beit Yosef likewise writes in the name of Ri and Rashbam, and notes that this appears to be the view of the Rif and Rosh, who omitted the baraita describing acquisition by mesirah, Kiddushin 22b. This also appears to be Rambam's view, as stated above in the name of the Maggid Mishneh. Ramban at the beginning of HaSefinah writes that this is the view of the Rif, Ri HaLevi, and Rav Hai, and he agrees. The Semag and Rabbenu Yerucham likewise rule that this is the principal view.
