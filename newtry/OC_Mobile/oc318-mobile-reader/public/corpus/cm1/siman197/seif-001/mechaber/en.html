The acquisition of large and small animals. It contains seven seifim.
An animal, whether small or large, is not acquired by mere delivery but by meshichah, and lifting is unnecessary. Rama: Some say that delivery also acquires a large animal, and some say even a small animal, when done in the seller's presence. See siman 198, seifim 8 through 13, for the laws of delivery. If the seller instructed the purchaser to acquire specifically by meshichah, and the purchaser changed either to an inferior mode such as delivery or to a superior mode such as lifting, he does not acquire; he must use the mode specified by the seller.
