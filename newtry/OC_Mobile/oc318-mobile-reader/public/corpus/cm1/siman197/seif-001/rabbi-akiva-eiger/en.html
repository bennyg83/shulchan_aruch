[Shulchan Aruch] One acquires an animal. Note: Regarding a bound animal, see Nimukei Yosef in the first chapter of Bava Metzia, who writes that the Gemara leaves it doubtful whether one acquires it in this manner, since this is not the ordinary way of acquiring it. According to the Rosh and the Tur there, it appears obvious to them that he does acquire. Analyze carefully.
