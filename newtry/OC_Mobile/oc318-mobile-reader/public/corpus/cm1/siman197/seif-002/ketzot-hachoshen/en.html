Meshichah through lifting acquires everywhere. Sema explains that even if the object is in the public domain or in the seller's domain, by lifting it in his hand the buyer brings it into his own domain, because whatever is held in a person's hand is regarded as though it were placed in his house.
It appears that even where he does not physically lift it in his hand, but the object is raised through his force in an upward movement, this too constitutes hagbahah although it is not actually in his hand. Tosafos write similarly in chapter HaGozel, Bava Kamma 98. Even though the lifting by his force occurred in the public domain, that is effective because only meshichah requires an alley or similar place, whereas hagbahah acquires everywhere, even in the seller's domain and the public domain. There is no distinction between lifting the object in one's hand and having it raised through one's force. This is clear. See what I wrote in siman 273, seif katan 4.
