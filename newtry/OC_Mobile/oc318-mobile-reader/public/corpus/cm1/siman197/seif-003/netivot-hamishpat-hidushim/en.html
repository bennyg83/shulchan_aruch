According to the view that riding acquires, the four cubits around a person do not acquire for the rider, because by choosing to ride he reveals that he does not wish to acquire through four cubits. Therefore, if one person rides and another leads, even if one entered the four cubits first, they divide. According to the view that riding alone does not acquire, however, if the rider entered the four cubits first, he acquires according to the opinion that four cubits can acquire even in a purchase and sale:
Or he led it by voice. The Sema concludes that both meshichah and leading acquire all animals, unlike the Rosh and Tur, who hold that in one category one of these methods may be ineffective. The Rambam also holds that meshichah and leading acquire all animals. His qualification concerns only a case where two people attempt to acquire simultaneously, one by pulling and one by leading.
