Seif 7: From now and after thirty days. This requires careful study. Tosafot in Yevamot writes that according to Rav, who is uncertain whether such language constitutes a retraction, the fact that the animal stands in a meadow after thirty days does not help. The Tur in Even HaEzer rules like Rav in the name of the Rosh, not like Rabbi Yochanan, who says that successive betrothals can all take effect. Why then does the Tur rule here that standing in the meadow helps, which follows Rabbi Yochanan's view that the original drawing continues? An even greater difficulty concerns the Beit Yosef's citation, followed by the Rama, that “from now and after thirty days” remains effective even if the deed is torn during the thirty days. Tosafot expressly identifies that result with Shmuel, whose position is not accepted, since the authorities in Even HaEzer dispute only whether the halachah follows Rav or Rabbi Yochanan, not Shmuel. I cannot presently reconcile this. The Rosh's separate ruling concerning “Draw the cow now, but do not acquire until after thirty days” is not difficult, because everyone may agree where the speaker expressly withholds the conveyance until later. The dispute concerns the ambiguous phrase “from now and after thirty days.”
