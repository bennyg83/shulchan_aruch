The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam apparently learned this from the discussion in the first chapter of Bava Metzia: Rabbi Elazar asked, 'Pull this animal to acquire the utensils upon it, what is the law?' The Gemara objects, 'to acquire? Did he say acquire?' Rather, it means, 'and acquire the utensils upon it.' Rambam understands that 'to acquire' implies future acquisition and therefore does not acquire, while 'and acquire' means acquire now at the moment of meshichah. It does not require that acquisition occur only afterward. This is also the Maggid Mishneh's understanding of Rambam's formulation.
