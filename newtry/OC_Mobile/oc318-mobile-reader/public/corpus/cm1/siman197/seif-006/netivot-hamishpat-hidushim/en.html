The same applies if he says, "Pull it." Rama apparently holds that both "pull" and "acquire it after thirty days" have one rationale: the wording implies acquisition only in the future. The Tur gives another reason: "Pull it so that you may acquire" can imply merely, "Perform an act of pulling suitable for acquisition, but I am not presently transferring it to you." A practical difference arises if he says, "Pull it to acquire it now." According to Rama it works; according to the Tur it does not. Taz.
