The same applies to one who tells him, "Pull the object," etc. The Mechaber's phrase "the same applies" indicates that he sees one reason in both cases, namely that the wording "pull it to acquire it" implies acquisition only later. The Maggid Mishneh writes this explicitly in chapter 2 of Hilchot Mekhirah. The Tur, however, does not say "the same applies," but rather "and even more than this," and gives a different reason: the phrase means, "You pull it in order to acquire, but I am not presently transferring it to you." In the Perishah I explained that the Tur does not understand "pull it to acquire" as necessarily referring to a future time. The Ir Shushan combines the Mechaber's wording with the Tur's reason, thereby mixing two distinct explanations.
