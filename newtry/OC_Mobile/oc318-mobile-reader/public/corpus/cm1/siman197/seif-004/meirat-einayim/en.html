He sold him one book, etc. Hagahot Maimoniyot writes: Reuven sold a book divided into two parts. He delivered the first half to Shimon, and Shimon later lifted the second half in Reuven's absence. Shimon did not thereby acquire the second half. This differs from an animal that follows a lead animal, because people do not ordinarily hold one half of a book on account of possessing the other half.
