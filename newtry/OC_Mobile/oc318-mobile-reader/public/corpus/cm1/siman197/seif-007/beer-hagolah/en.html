Nun) There, halachah 9, from a statement of Rabbi Yochanan, Ketubot 82a.
Samekh) The Gemara's explanation there.
Ayin) The Maggid Mishneh writes that the intent is an area that is not his own domain and therefore does not acquire for him. The same applies to the public domain. The Gemara merely used the ordinary case of a marsh, which is a grazing place. The Sema did not explain it this way.
Peh) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is established in many places as the accepted halachah, and it appears in Kiddushin, chapter HaOmer, 60a.
