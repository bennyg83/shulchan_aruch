Why did the Sages not treat the words of a shechiv mera leniently by interpreting him as though he had said that he gives the house for purposes of residence or the palm tree for its fruits, just as they were lenient regarding a promissory note above in seif 20 and treated him as though he had written and delivered it? The Maggid Mishneh explains that residence without a house is not an independently existent object at all. A document, by contrast, is physically delivered to the recipient even if the lien embodied in it was not transferred by writing and delivery. See there. Sema.
