That he allowed him to reside and fed him, etc. The Tur and Mechaber already stated these laws in siman 212. Why did the Sages not interpret a dangerously ill person's statement “give so and so residence in this house” as though he had said “give him an interest in the house for purposes of residence,” just as they interpret “give so and so this promissory note” as though the requisite writing and delivery had occurred? The Maggid Mishneh explains that residence without an interest in the house is not itself a tangible thing subject to acquisition. A promissory note, by contrast, is a tangible document capable of delivery. Even though the lien it represents ordinarily requires writing and delivery to transfer fully, the deathbed enactment can supply that missing legal formality.
