[Shulchan Aruch] Give two hundred zuz to my son, so and so. Note: The Tur later cites a responsum of the Rosh concerning Yaakov, who instructed because of impending death that two thirds of his property be given to his daughter and that the remaining third remain for the person legally entitled to inherit him, and he had a son. The son must pay the creditor. Even if the testator used language of a gift with respect to the son, it is treated as inheritance. See Derishah, who resolves the matter by noting that he did not assign a fixed sum to his son, but rather one third of the estate. See Responsa Raanach, siman 31, Responsa Avkat Rochel, siman 44, and Responsa Yachin UBoaz, siman 20.
