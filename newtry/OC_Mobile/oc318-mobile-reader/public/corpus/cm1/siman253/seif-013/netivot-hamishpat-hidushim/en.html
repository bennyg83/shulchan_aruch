And it was sold and the proceeds were lost. But if the wine became vinegar, the recipient does not lose. The dying person instructed that the recipient receive the proceeds of the wine, meaning after it was sold, and therefore even if all of it became vinegar the designation remains. This is specifically where he said, "from the proceeds of my wine," which resembles giving the tree for its fruit. But if he said, "two hundred zuz when the wine is sold," that concerns something not yet in existence, as explained in siman 200, seif 4, in Rama's gloss. See Tosafot, Gittin 65.
