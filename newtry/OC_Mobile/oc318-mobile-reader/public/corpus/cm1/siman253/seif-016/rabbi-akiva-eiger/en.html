[Rama] And if it was lost, they must give. Note: In the source of this law in Beit Yosef it is evident that the recipient bears the loss proportionately with the entire estate, and that this is unlike the case of "give him two hundred zuz from my wine." However, Responsa Ramban, siman 62, clearly holds that it is entirely comparable to that case and that the recipient loses nothing. The Beit Yosef cites this in new matter 8. This is likewise evident from Responsa Rashba, siman 1055, and from the Ritva as cited by the Beit Yosef here. The Ran, however, holds that saying "from my property" is equivalent to saying "from what is mine," and he transfers only a specific sum in coined money. If the coined money is lost, the recipient loses the entire amount. The Mechaber rules accordingly below in seif 19. This requires study.
