That is in the possession of so-and-so, give it to so-and-so. See Baer Heitev, seif katan 42. The citation there is printed incorrectly. It should refer to the Mechaber’s statement, “Similarly, if he said, ‘the debt instrument, etc., give it to so-and-so.’” The Shach edition of the Shulchan Aruch also contains an erroneous reference to seif katan 29. Baer Heitev cites the Shach that, in his view, the Ran’s words are primary. See, however, Shach above, siman 66, seif 42, seif katan 133, where he did not write so. See also what I wrote there under “and if he is a person on his deathbed,” citing Noda BiYehuda, second edition, siman 45, that even according to the authorities who require the words “it and all its liens,” that requirement applies when the dying person says that the documents themselves are to be given. If he says instead that so-and-so shall take the debt represented by the document, this debt is certainly no worse than an oral loan. See there.
