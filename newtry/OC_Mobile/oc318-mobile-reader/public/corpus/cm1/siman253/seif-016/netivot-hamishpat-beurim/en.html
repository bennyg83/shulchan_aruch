There is no bread to give until she marries. See Sema, seif katan 35. Ketzot HaChoshen and the Gaon R. Ozer in the second Amsterdam edition of Shulchan Aruch rightly question his explanation. If the donor did not say "Give," the recipient does not acquire at all, as Rama states in seif 2. The primary distinction is therefore this: if he said, "Give her two hundred zuz for a dowry," the reference to dowry merely identifies the purpose and the money is given to her immediately. But if he said, "Give her objects for a dowry," the very objects are tied to the marriage purpose, and they are not delivered until she actually marries. This is the primary ruling.
