If she gave birth to a male and a female, etc. See Rashbam, s.v. "this is the reading: some explain, etc." The Rosh likewise rules so at the end of responsa, rule 81. Regarding Rashbam's question from the Gemara's statement, "but he did not say male and female," the Rosh writes that this applies specifically when one gives to another and makes the gift depend on what she gives birth to. But when one gives to his own children, both are dear to him. This is the meaning of seif 28, "if he said, etc.," for there he gave to the brothers and made it depend on his children.
If she gave birth to a tumtum. Unlike Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. Rif and other poskim. This is the meaning of "and if the sex was later revealed, etc." See there 157a.
