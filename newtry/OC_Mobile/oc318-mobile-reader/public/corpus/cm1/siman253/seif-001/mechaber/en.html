Which expressions are effective for a deathly ill person, and the law when he says, "Give one maneh to one person, two hundred to another, and three hundred to another." It contains thirty-three seifim.
If two people enter to visit a sick person and he gives instructions before them, they may write the will but may not act as judges. If three enter, they may either write or constitute a beit din. Rama: They may establish each recipient in what was commanded and decide every doubt arising in the will. No heir or recipient may demand another beit din. This applies by day. At night, even three may write but may not adjudicate.
