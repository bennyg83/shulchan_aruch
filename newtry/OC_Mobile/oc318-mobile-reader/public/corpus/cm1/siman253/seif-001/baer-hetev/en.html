By day. This follows the rule governing other judges. If they themselves see or hear the matter at a time when adjudication is permitted, namely by day, they may judge it because hearing from another cannot be stronger than seeing it themselves. But if they saw or heard the matter at night, when the initial stage of a case may not be adjudicated, they have the status of witnesses, and a witness cannot become a judge of that matter even by day. This is explained clearly in siman 7 in the words of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch. Ibid.
