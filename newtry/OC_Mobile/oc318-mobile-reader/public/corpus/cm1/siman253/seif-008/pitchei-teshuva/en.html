To my creditor, as is fitting for him, etc. See Responsa Panim Me’irot, part I, siman 34, who writes that the qualification “as is fitting for him” is necessary only where he expressly said, “Give to my creditor.” If he did not identify him as a creditor but merely said, “Give so-and-so one hundred zuz,” then although he in fact owed that person money, it appears clear that he did not mean payment of the debt, and the recipient takes the amount in addition to the debt. I found this reasoning as well in the glosses to Mordechai at the end of Bava Batra. See there.
