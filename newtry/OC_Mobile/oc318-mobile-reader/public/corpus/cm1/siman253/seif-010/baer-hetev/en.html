From the recipient. A deathbed gift is treated like an inheritance. Accordingly, if a creditor produces the recipient's handwritten acknowledgment of debt, he may collect from him according to this view. So writes the Beit Yosef at the beginning of siman 69 in the name of the Ritva. See Responsa Rashach, part III, siman 24, and Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 203 and 328. Shach.
