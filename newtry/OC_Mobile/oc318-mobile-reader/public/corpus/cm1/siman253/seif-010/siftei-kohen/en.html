He collects the remainder, etc. The gift of a dangerously ill person has the status of inheritance. Therefore, according to this view, if the claimant produces the recipient's handwritten admission, he may collect from him. The Beit Yosef writes this at the beginning of siman 69 in the name of the Ritva. See Responsa Rabbi Shmuel Kohen, part 3, siman 24, and Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 203 and 328.
