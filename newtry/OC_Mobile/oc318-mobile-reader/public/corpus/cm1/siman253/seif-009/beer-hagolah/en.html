Bet) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah, halachah 13, from the baraita in Bava Batra 138a.
This is also Rambam's wording there. The number nine hundred was stated because of the latter clause.
Gimel) Rambam explains there that in every case, since he did not say "and after him," all recipients are equal.
Dalet) The Maggid Mishneh explains that the amount the deathly ill person gave him was insufficient to satisfy the debt.
Heh) Rambam explains that if the giver personally transferred the gifts to the recipients, it is recognizable which recipient he transferred to first, and that person acquires first.
