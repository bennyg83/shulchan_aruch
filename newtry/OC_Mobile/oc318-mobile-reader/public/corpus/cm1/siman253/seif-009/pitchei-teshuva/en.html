An additional zuz. See Baer Heitev, who writes that we interpret an extra expression, etc. This is from the Gemara, Bava Batra 138b. See Beit Yosef above, at the end of siman 61, who cites a responsum of Rashba that we have no authority to rule by analogy from this to another case. Rather, only where the Sages stated such a rule do we apply it. See there. See Responsa Pnei Yehoshua, siman 101, on this matter, partially cited above in siman 42, seif 10, seif katan 10. See also Responsa Noda BiYehuda, siman 3, cited above in siman 13, seif 1, seif katan 2, and Noda BiYehuda, second edition, Even HaEzer, siman 100, cited in Pitchei Teshuva to Even HaEzer, siman 66, seif 11, seif katan 6.
