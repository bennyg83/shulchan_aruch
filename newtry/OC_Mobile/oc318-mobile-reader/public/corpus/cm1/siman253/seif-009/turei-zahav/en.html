(Siman 12, two wills.) I am astonished, for the conclusion of Terumat HaDeshen there, siman 350, is that for this reason one would have said here that it was a condition and a single testamentary disposition. Rather, he ruled as he did because of the first reason stated there, namely, that we assess his intent to be that his affection was directed toward his granddaughter, and he resolved and gave unconditionally. His instruction that she purchase a belt was only to display special affection for her, showing that at the time of his death he remembered her adornments. According to this, it is not specifically where he said, “and she shall purchase with them,” but even where he said, “that she shall purchase with them.” Beit Yosef, seif 24, likewise quotes the words of Terumat HaDeshen as “to purchase a belt,” and the like.
