[Shulchan Aruch] And likewise if it decreased in value. Note: Ramban, in his novellae to Gittin, holds that if one says, "Give him two hundred zuz from the proceeds of my wine," and the wine later decreases in value, the beneficiary’s amount is reduced proportionately. He writes the same in Responsa Ramban, siman 62, cited by Beit Yosef in this siman, new matter 8.
