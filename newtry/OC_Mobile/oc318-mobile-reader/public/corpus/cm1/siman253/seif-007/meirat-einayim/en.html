One of his daughters, etc. Above in siman 246:6 the Mechaber states the same rule, following the Rif and Rambam. Rama there cites those who say that a daughter among sons or daughters does not acquire, the view of the Tur in the name of the Rosh. Yet the Tur himself states the present rule without qualification like the Rif. In siman 246 I distinguished the cases. Here the Tur speaks where the donor gave without specifying either language of inheritance or gift. Even the Rosh agrees that in such a case there is no relevant distinction between a son and a daughter. See there and the Perishah here.
