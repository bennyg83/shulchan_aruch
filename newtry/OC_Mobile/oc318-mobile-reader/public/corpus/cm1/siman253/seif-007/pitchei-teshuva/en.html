To attribute it to a scribal error. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, siman 138, concerning an objection to the wording of a will that stated, “I will give a gold chain to so-and-so.” A person on his deathbed should say “give,” not “I will give.” He writes that this is an evident error arising from the wording of the document itself and is a scribal defect. The document says that after her death “I will give this object to so-and-so.” How could she say that after her death she herself will give it? See Tumim, siman 49, seif katan 5.
