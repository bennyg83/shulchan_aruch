[Shulchan Aruch] In this house. Note: Even if one sold a house to Reuven without writing that the sale extends from the depths of the earth to the height of the sky, so that the airspace was not acquired by the purchaser and remained with the seller, the rule is that the seller transmits that right to his heirs but cannot sell it to others, as stated above in siman 214, seif 5, Rama. Likewise, he cannot give it by way of a shechiv mera gift. So writes Beit Yosef here in the name of the Ran.
