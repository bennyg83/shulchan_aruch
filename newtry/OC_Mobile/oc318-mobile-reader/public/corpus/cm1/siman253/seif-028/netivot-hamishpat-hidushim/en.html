The gift is void. This is unlike seif 27. There the birth itself was not made a condition; rather, he personally undertook a liability of one amount for a male child and another amount for a female child. Therefore, when both are born, each receives the designated amount. Here, however, the birth of a male or female was made an actual condition. When twins are born, the event does not conform to the stipulated condition, and therefore the gift is void. The same rule applies even if the recipient was his own son or daughter.
