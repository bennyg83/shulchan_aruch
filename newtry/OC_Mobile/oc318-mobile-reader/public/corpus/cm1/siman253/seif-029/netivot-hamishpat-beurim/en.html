My property to Toviah. See Beit Yosef on the Tur in siman 247, who writes that if one says, "my property to my relatives," whether male or female, this includes relatives who are disqualified from testifying for him.
That he owes Toviah. See Shach, seif katan 39, who disagrees with Ritva and holds that even with a gift, where the recipient is regarded as though he already has a partnership interest in the property, the debtor may still defer each claimant. See what I wrote above in siman 76, that only with a debt consisting of a personal obligation can he defer them. With a deposit, as where one of you deposited a maneh with me, if he had no duty to remember which one and both claimants are uncertain, the rule is that they divide it and he cannot defer each one.
