There, seif 4, in the name of the Rosh. He explains that since this wording is effective for a dying person, all the more so for a healthy person when accompanied by a kinyan. The Ramban likewise writes so.
There, seif 3, in the name of the Ramah. The Ramban likewise writes in the name of Ibn Migash that even if they wrote or made a kinyan, it is merely a kinyan on words. Rambam, his student, likewise writes that one who says 'pull and you will acquire,' or any language indicating future acquisition, does not acquire, chapter 2 of Hilkhot Mekhirah. See the Raavad and Maggid Mishneh there.
