The same applies that these expressions are effective for a healthy person. Meaning, for a healthy person when the recipient also makes a kinyan on this language of gift. The Tur likewise writes this in the name of the Rosh.
Some say that they are not effective. They hold that only with a shechiv mera, whose mind is unsettled and who may not speak in precise language of gift, did the Sages act leniently so that his mind not become distraught.
