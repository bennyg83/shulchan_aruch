They shall give to each one. See the Sema, seif katan 50, who writes that the two hundred zuz are given jointly to both of them. See there. This also appears from the responsum of Ramban cited by the Beit Yosef in siman 258 concerning a flask of water. However, where a person regularly attends synagogues in two cities and says, 'Give a Torah scroll to the synagogue,' the plain implication may be that each synagogue receives a Torah scroll, because 'to both of them' would then mean one to each. This requires study.
