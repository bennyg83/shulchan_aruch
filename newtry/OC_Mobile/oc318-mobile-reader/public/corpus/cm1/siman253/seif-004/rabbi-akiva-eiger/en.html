[Shulchan Aruch] It is language of a gift. Note: See Responsa Tashbetz, Chut HaMeshulash, first column, siman 23, who writes in the name of the Rashba that the same applies if he says, "I leave to so and so." See there concerning these laws. See also Torat Chaim of Maharachash, part 1, siman 2, in the name of his teacher, who holds that "I leave" is weaker than "I set aside" and is ineffective.
