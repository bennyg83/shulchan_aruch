[Shulchan Aruch] And if a debt instrument is produced against him, the creditor collects. Note: If among the gifts there was also a gift to consecrated property, it may be said that consecrated property takes effect immediately, making it a gift of a healthy person, while the other gifts are deathbed gifts that take effect only after death. The creditor would therefore not collect from the consecrated property. This depends on the unresolved question whether one who consecrates all his property may retract if he recovers. See siman 250, seif 3, and Tashbetz, part 4, second column, siman 7. Analyze carefully.
