One who says, etc. The Tosefta, chapter 9 of Bava Batra, teaches: one who says, 'I have two hundred dinars in the hand of person X, three hundred in the hand of person Y, and five hundred in the hand of person Z,' and a wife's ketubah or a creditor's claim is presented against them, collection is made even though all the funds have not yet been gathered. But if he then said, 'and person X shall take two hundred dinars,' then if all the funds were gathered, he takes it; otherwise he takes only proportionately. This must be the Tosefta's reading according to this opinion. According to our Tosefta text, however, the distinction is different: if he said, 'and person X shall take two hundred zuz,' he collects like the ketubah holder and creditor; but if he said, 'and afterward he shall take,' he receives only proportionately.
