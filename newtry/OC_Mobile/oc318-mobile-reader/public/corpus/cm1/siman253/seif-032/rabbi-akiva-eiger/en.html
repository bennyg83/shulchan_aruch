[Shulchan Aruch] If one of them is a Torah scholar, the Torah scholar takes precedence. Note: If he says by way of gift, "My property to my relatives," it may be that one must likewise give it to relatives who are Torah scholars even if there is another relative who is closer in degree. So writes the Ramban in his novellae cited by the Beit Yosef, folio 33. See Nimukei Yosef, chapter Mi Shemet, in the name of the Ritva, that if one says, "My property to my relatives," all relatives who are disqualified from testifying for him are included. This is cited by the Beit Yosef above at the end of siman 246. See Ketzot HaChoshen there.
