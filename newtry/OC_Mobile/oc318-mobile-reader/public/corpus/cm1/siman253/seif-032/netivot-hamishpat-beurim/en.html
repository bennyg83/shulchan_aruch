Documents recorded in secular courts. See Sema. The reason is that the document serves merely as evidence, while the shechiv mera's words themselves create the acquisition. Even if he instructed the secular officials to write and deliver the document, this does not create the same problem that exists with Jewish witnesses where we sometimes suspect that the donor intended the transfer to be completed only through the document. That concern can arise even with movable property, where a deed is not itself a mode of acquisition. It is merely a concern that the donor was particular that the document reach the recipient. Accordingly, once the document actually reaches the recipient during the donor's lifetime, the gift is effective even though the document itself has no independent power of kinyan.
