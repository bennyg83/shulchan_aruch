If a deathly ill person says, "Give two hundred zuz to so-and-so, and he shall marry my daughter," these are two independent gifts, and the recipient may choose either. He may take the money without marrying the daughter. If the donor says, "He shall marry my daughter, and they shall give him two hundred zuz," the marriage is a condition, and he receives no money unless he marries her. Rama: If one says, "Give one hundred zuz to my daughter so that she buy a belt," and she dies, the money is given to her heirs. It is as though he made two directions; although purchase of the belt can no longer be fulfilled, the first gift remains valid.
