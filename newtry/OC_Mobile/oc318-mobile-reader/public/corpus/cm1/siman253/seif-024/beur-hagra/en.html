(Supplement) For if he said, etc. Rashbam there, s.v. 'give,' and Rosh there challenge this from the Tosefta: one who says, 'Give, etc.' Rosh answers that the Tosefta concerns a deathly ill person distributing his estate, whereas the Gemara concerns a healthy person. Rashbam answers that the Tosefta concerns one distributing to his sons, whereas the Gemara concerns one who has no sons, or one who said, 'and the remainder to so-and-so.' Tur adopts the second answer and also Rosh's limitation to a deathly ill person. This is likewise Rama's formulation, 'a deathly ill person to his sons,' combining Rashbam's two answers. In practical law, the two answers amount to the same rule. (End of supplement.) See 6.
