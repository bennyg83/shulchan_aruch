There, law 7. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is a baraita according to Sumchus there: one who says, 'Give so-and-so a share in the cistern,' etc.; for a small vessel, no less than one sixteenth. Some explain that when he says merely 'a share,' we assign the smallest of the stated measures, namely one sixteenth. But the Gaon held that the first clause of the baraita, which says 'give,' means one quarter, and the Rif records this as primary. The Tur likewise writes in seif 40 in the name of the Rif and Rashbam.
