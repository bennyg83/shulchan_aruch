They do not eulogize him, etc. The expression "they do not eulogize" implies that it is prohibited to eulogize him. The reason is that a eulogy is only for the honor of the deceased and his relatives, and he waived his honor. It is a mitzvah to fulfill the words of the deceased. This differs from one who says not to bury him. He has no authority to require that the corpse remain overnight and not be buried. Even if his intention is that his own estate not pay for the burial, but that charity or the community bear the cost, we say he has no authority to enrich his children and shift his own burial onto the community. All the more so if he directs that the eulogy be paid from charity.
