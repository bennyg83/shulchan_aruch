They may do so. See the Sema, seif katan 77, concerning a dangerously ill person who admitted that he owed money to a particular person. The Bach rejects the Sema's explanation and distinguishes as follows: Ramban and Rashba discuss witnesses who, on their own initiative, wrote a memorandum during the person's lifetime or after his death. The Rosh, however, discusses a case in which the dangerously ill donor himself instructed them to write a document that would effect the transfer, and they wrote it merely in the language of a will. The Bach's distinction is correct. Carefully examine Ramban's language, 'If the witnesses wished,' and this will be clear.
