They may write. A will document recording a gift of a dying person does not have the ordinary legal status of a debt instrument, and the heirs can say, "We paid." Therefore the witnesses may write it even if the deceased did not say, "Write." In siman 255, seif 6, the Mechaber discusses an acknowledgment document, which has the full status of a legal instrument: the heirs cannot say "We paid," and collection can be made from purchasers on its basis. Even so, the witnesses may write it, because we rule that when a dying person makes an acknowledgment he need not say, "Write."
