[Shach, letter 31] And the Bach explained it differently. Note: See Responsa Avkat Rochel, siman 12; Responsa Maharit, part 2, Choshen Mishpat, simanim 32 and 95; Responsa Tashbetz, part 2, siman 235; and Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 15, halachah 11 of Hilchot To'en, s.v. "from now I retract." According to the Bach's reason, since a debt cannot ordinarily be transferred through maamad sheloshtan when the debtor denies owing it, such a transfer is unavailable in the ordinary case. It would appear, however, that if the creditor holds collateral, it can be effective, because he can transfer the collateral, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 169, seif 8.
