There, law 9. The Rif writes that the measures specified in this teaching concerning a barrel, pot, and small vessel are not sufficiently explained in their rationale, and therefore we do not derive practical law from them.
In the name of the Rashba's novellae, citing Rabbi Meir on this baraita of Sumchus, that the rule applies specifically to a gift, etc. He writes that the Gaon likewise stated in Sefer HaMekach that if one says, 'I sell you a share in this field for such-and-such,' he means one share in the field proportionate to the money paid, calculated precisely. It appears to me from this that Rabbenu Chananel, Rav Hai, and the students of the Rashba agree with the Rif, Rambam, and Rashbam, who rule like Sumchus.
