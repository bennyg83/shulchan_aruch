[Shach, letter 24] The words of the Sema are not clear to me. Note: I do not understand the difficulty; the Sema's words are quite clear. Specifically where he expressly states that he is giving as a deathbed gift, even a gift of only part of the estate requires no kinyan, as stated there in siman 250, seif 9. This is therefore included in the Mechaber's words that this law applies when he gives because of impending death or when he distributes the entire estate, and the same follows automatically where he expressly says that it is a deathbed gift. Thus, when the Mechaber later says, "but where he did not transfer," the meaning is that he did not expressly say it was a deathbed gift. The Sema's question is therefore well founded.
