Mem) There, seif 28, from the incident in the fourth chapter of Bava Batra 71a and Rav Ashi's statement to Mar Zutra that one who gives a gift gives generously. Rambam likewise rules there, halachah 22. The Beit Yosef writes that this applies specifically where one is holding one hundred and twenty, because people commonly confuse one hundred with one hundred and twenty, but not where the discrepancy is greater.
