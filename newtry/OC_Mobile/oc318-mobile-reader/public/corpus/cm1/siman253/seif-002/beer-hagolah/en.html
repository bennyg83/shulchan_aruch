All these are unresolved questions there at the beginning of 149a. Since they remained unresolved, we do not remove the property from the heirs, because it is in their possession. Regarding the phrase 'that he benefit from them,' the Gemara is uncertain whether he meant the entire estate as a gift or merely some benefit. In my humble opinion, if a kinyan was made and he died, the recipient acquires the smaller amount necessary for some benefit. The Sema gives another interpretation; the reader may choose.
