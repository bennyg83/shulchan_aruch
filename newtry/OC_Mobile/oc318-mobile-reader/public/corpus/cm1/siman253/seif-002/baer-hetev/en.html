Benefit. With wording of this kind we do not say that the words of a shechiv mera are regarded as written and delivered. Even if a kinyan was made regarding it, some say that it is merely a kinyan concerning words, while others say that it is effective. See Darkei Moshe. Sema.
I leave. The formulation 'I leave' is effective specifically for a shechiv mera, because that is the customary language of a dying person who leaves portions of his estate after him to one person and another. For a healthy person this wording is ineffective according to everyone. The Mechaber first states that 'I leave' is effective for a shechiv mera, and in the following seif says regarding the other formulations that some say they are effective even for a healthy person. This should not be understood as referring also to 'I leave.' Regarding that wording all agree that it is ineffective for a healthy person. The Tur therefore places the rule that 'I leave' works for a shechiv mera after the dispute whether the other formulations work for a healthy person, and his arrangement is clear. So writes the Sema.
