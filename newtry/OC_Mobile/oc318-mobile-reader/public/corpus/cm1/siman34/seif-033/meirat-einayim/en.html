Specifically those who violated oaths. Beit Yosef writes that Rambam and the Tur appear to speak even of oaths involving denial of money. Hagahot Ashri, in the name of Or Zarua, writes that the rule refers only to vain oaths and ordinary verbal oaths. Regarding a false monetary oath, perhaps this added repentance is not necessary because the person must return whatever he gained through the false oath.
Specifically where they did not remove him, etc. Rama writes this according to Beit Yosef's understanding of Tosafot's statement that the sinner should perform some stratagem so that he is returned to his occupation. I wrote in Perishah that this is not Tosafot's intent. Rather, whenever he again becomes qualified for the very matter through which he had been disqualified, that itself is called "his occupation." There is therefore no distinction whether he was formally removed from the trade or not.
