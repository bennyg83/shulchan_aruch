When does one who violated an oath regain fitness? When he comes before a beit din that does not recognize him and says, 'I am suspected of violating oaths,' or when he becomes liable to take an oath over a substantial amount before such a beit din and chooses to pay rather than swear. {Rama: This applies specifically to one who violated oaths. For other transgressions, it is enough that he accept before the beit din of his city not to repeat the offense. This is so only if he was not removed from a profession because of the offense. If he was removed, his law is like that of the butcher explained below.}
