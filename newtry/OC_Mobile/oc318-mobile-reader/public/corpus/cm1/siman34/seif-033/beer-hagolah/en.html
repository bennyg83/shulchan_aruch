This is also the name of the Rambam from Hiroshima and wrote the S. R.R
Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 2 part 2, concludes that the opposing party swears and collects.
The Kesef Mishneh in the Beit Yosef writes that the Rambam apparently learned this from the nearby case of the slaughterer. Hagahot Ashri, chapter Zeh Borer, writes that he must return what he gained through the false oath.
