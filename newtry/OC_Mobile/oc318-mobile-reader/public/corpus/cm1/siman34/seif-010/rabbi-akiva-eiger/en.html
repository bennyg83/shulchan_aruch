[Shulchan Aruch] A thief, and likewise a robber. Note: Regarding one who steals the property of a non-Jew, the Radbaz, siman 258, writes that he is disqualified by Torah law. Maharikash writes: “It appears to me that he should be deemed qualified, because people do not understand that this is forbidden, and this is the proper ruling.” From this wording it appears that the Radbaz holds that he is disqualified from testimony and Maharikash disagrees. In truth, however, the Radbaz, siman 28, writes that stealing from a non-Jew is prohibited by Torah law, yet nevertheless the offender should be deemed qualified because people do not understand that stealing from a non-Jew is forbidden.
