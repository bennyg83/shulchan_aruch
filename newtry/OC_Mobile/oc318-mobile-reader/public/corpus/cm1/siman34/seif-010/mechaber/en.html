One who lends with interest is disqualified as a witness, and the same applies to the borrower. If the interest is fixed interest prohibited by Torah law, both are disqualified by Torah law. If it is interest prohibited only rabbinically, they are disqualified rabbinically. Rama: Some say that with avak ribbis only the lender is disqualified, not the borrower. See Yoreh Deah at the beginning of siman 161.
