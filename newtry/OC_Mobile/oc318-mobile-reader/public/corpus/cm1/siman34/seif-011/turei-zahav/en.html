(The Rambam wrote: even though the thief and robber returned what they took, etc.) See the Beit Yosef’s explanation. It appears to me otherwise. The Rambam speaks even where they returned the property willingly. The Torah’s special designation of a “witness of violence” teaches that although such offenders are not subject to lashes, they are nevertheless disqualified, as stated above in seif 12. If the robber had not yet returned the property, there would be no novelty, for the reason he is exempt from lashes is that the offense is remediable by restitution, and until he returns the property he has not yet left the category of one liable to lashes. The verse must therefore teach that even after restitution he remains disqualified until he performs repentance.
