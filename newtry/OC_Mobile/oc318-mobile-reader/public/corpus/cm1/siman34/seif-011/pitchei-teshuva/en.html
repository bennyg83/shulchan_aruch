In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: If he struck another person, even where the blow caused damage worth a perutah and the law is that he pays money and does not receive lashes, he is nevertheless disqualified by Torah law. The reason is that this prohibition carries lashes when the blow causes damage of less than a perutah, as stated in siman 420, seif 2. Therefore, even when the damage is worth a perutah, the offender is disqualified. See Beit Yosef, responsum concerning laws of kiddushin, siman 2. End of quote.
