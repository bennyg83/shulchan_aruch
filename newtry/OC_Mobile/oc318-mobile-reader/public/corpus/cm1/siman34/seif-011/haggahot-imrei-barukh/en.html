(Mechaber, seif 23) Before they were publicly proclaimed, etc. Note: See Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot Malveh VeLoveh 4:6, and Responsa Maharit, Even HaEzer, siman 43. Regarding disqualification from a rabbinic oath, whether public proclamation is required, see Ran's novellae to Sanhedrin 25 in the passage beginning "since he saw," while Tosafot, Bava Metzia 5b, at the end of the passage beginning "without money," appear to indicate that proclamation is not required.
