See Baer Heitev and Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 172, concerning one who swore regarding a past event that he had given an item to a certain person on Rosh Chodesh Adar in a particular city, and it was later established that on the first day of Rosh Chodesh he was not in that city. The question was whether he should be treated as one who violated his oath and is therefore disqualified. Shevut Yaakov rules that he should not be disqualified on that basis. This is so even according to the Shach in siman 73, seif katan 31, who holds that one who swears to pay on Rosh Chodesh Adar must pay on the first day of the new month, not merely because of doubt but as the primary law, since people call that day Rosh Chodesh. Nevertheless, after the fact the witness should not be disqualified, because he can explain that he intended the second day of Rosh Chodesh, and not everyone is expert in these legal distinctions.
