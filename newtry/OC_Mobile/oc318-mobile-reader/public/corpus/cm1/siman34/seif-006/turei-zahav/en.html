(Seif 18: payment to go and observe.) This implies that if he already saw the event, he may not take payment merely to go and testify. It appears to me that this applies where there is no significant effort. Where effort is involved, he may take compensation for the travel. At the end of Perek HaIsh Mekadesh, one who takes payment to sprinkle purification water renders the sprinkling invalid, yet the Gemara states that one may take payment for transporting the water from place to place and for drawing it, because that exertion is not independently incumbent upon him. The Gemara explicitly compares this to payment for judging and testifying and to payment for sprinkling. Therefore, one may receive compensation for the effort of traveling, and this does not fall within the principle, "Just as I teach freely, so must you." This appears to me correct.
