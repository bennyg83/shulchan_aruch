From the incident of that slaughterer, etc., whom Rav Nachman placed under nidui, Sanhedrin 25a. According to the Mechaber's understanding of the Rambam at the end of chapter 12 of Hilkhot Edut, the Rambam mentions one who inspected for himself only because he does not regain qualification until a significant non-kosher animal emerges from under his hand. But one who inspects for others regains qualification through repentance alone.
