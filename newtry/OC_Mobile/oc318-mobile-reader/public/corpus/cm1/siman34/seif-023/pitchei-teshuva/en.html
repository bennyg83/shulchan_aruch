Even though they pay money. The Sema explains that those who pay are not thereby violating the prohibition of coveting, because "you shall not covet" implies taking without payment. He writes that this is indeed the truth and that Tosafot say so. See there. Tumim challenges him, noting that this is contrary to the ruling of Shulchan Aruch below, siman 359, seif 10, and to the Sema's own words there, seif katan 14. He similarly questions the Kesef Mishneh, Hilchot Edut, chapter 10. See there. Beit Shmuel had already raised this difficulty in Even HaEzer, siman 28, seif katan 2; see there.
