Before they announced his disqualification, etc. Since this is only a rabbinic disqualification, it is not automatically publicized. To prevent loss to people who relied on such a person as a witness without knowing of his defect, the Sages enacted that he is not disqualified until after a formal public announcement. When the Gemara says that ordinary customs collectors and ordinary shepherds are disqualified, it does not mean without announcement. Rather, even without proof of a particular theft, their occupation creates a presumption of improper conduct, so an announcement may immediately be made concerning them.
