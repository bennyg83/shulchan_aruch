That they announced. Nimukei Yosef writes that for the very testimony because of which he became disqualified, he is disqualified even without a public announcement. Thus writes the Sema. The Shach objects that Nimukei Yosef said this only according to the initial premise that a person may disqualify himself for testimony by his own statement. According to the conclusion that a person cannot render himself wicked and we divide his statement, this case does not seem possible. If others testify that he borrowed at interest, that is a separate testimony. Why, then, did the authorities record this rule? This requires study. It appears to me that this can be somewhat reconciled based on Rashba's responsum cited by the Sema in seif katan 64, which distinguishes between saying, "Your wife committed adultery with me," and saying, "I committed adultery with your wife."
