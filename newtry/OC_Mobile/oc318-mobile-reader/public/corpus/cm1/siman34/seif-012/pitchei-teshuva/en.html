And even if he violated a cherem. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot HaTur, letter 18, writes: One who utters the Name of Heaven in vain is disqualified from testimony, according to Rabbi Moshe Isserles, siman 12. Rabbi Naftali, however, in siman 13 there, writes that there is room to validate him. See what I wrote in Pitchei Teshuva to Even HaEzer, siman 42, seif katan 18. See also below, siman 87, seif 39, and Birkei Yosef, letter 17, who objects at length to Knesset HaGedolah on this point.
