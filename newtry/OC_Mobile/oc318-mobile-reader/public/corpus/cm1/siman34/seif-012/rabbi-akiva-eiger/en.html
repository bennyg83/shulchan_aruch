[Shulchan Aruch] He becomes disqualified from the time he testified. Note: This applies also to testimony he gave immediately beforehand, within the time required for an utterance, of the testimony regarding which he was subsequently rendered an eid zomem. This is explicit in the sugya in Bava Kamma 73a. See also Ramban, chapter Chazakat HaBatim. However, the Shitah Mekubetzet there in Bava Kamma cites the view of many Rishonim that this applies specifically where the two testimonies concern the same plaintiff and defendant. But where the two testimonies concern two different persons, the first testimony remains valid. From Maharit, part 1, siman 138, it is also clear that if, within the time required for an utterance after his testimony, the witness becomes disqualified through a transgression, his testimony is void. The practical ruling requires study.
