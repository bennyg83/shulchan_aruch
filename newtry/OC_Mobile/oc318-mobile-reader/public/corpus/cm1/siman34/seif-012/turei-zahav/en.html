(See the Beit Yosef, novella 2, from “Know, etc.” through “those who raise small livestock in Eretz Yisrael are disqualified, and I wonder at the Rif, etc.”) The Bach writes that the Beit Yosef overlooked the fact that the Rif and the Rosh did cite this ruling; see there. It appears to me, however, that the Beit Yosef’s principal question is why the Tur did not cite it here in the laws of testimony. According to this, the Beit Yosef’s answer is not entirely satisfactory, though it can be reconciled with some difficulty.
