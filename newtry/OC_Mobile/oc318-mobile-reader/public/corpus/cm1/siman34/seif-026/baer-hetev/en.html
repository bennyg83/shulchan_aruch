Who lent. Rambam likewise writes, "The borrower said: he lent to me at interest," etc., excluding Mordechai in the chapter Zeh Borer, who writes that the witness must say that the lender lent at interest but did not take anything from him. Thus writes the Sema. The Shach, however, agrees with Mordechai and writes that Rambam's words can also be interpreted that way. See there.
