Since he does not receive lashes, he is disqualified. See seif 2 in the gloss; this requires study. The gloss means that there it says one who violates a prohibition without lashes is disqualified only rabbinically, whereas here the implication is biblical disqualification. This can be reconciled: here the offense itself carries lashes, but he was not warned. He is therefore disqualified by Torah law.
