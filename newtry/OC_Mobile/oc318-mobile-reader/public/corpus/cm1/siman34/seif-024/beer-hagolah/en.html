o) The wording of the Rambam there at the beginning of chapter 12, from the incident of those gravediggers, etc., there. It appears from the case that they had not been warned, because the Gemara says they thought they were performing a mitzvah. Were it not for that reason they would have been disqualified even without warning. It also implies that wherever one can attribute the conduct to ignorance of the prohibition, the person is not disqualified.
