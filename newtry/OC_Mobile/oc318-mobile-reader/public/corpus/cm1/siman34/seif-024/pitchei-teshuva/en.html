One who takes payment to testify, etc. In the glosses of the Gaon Maharam, author of Tefillah LeMoshe, to Shulchan Aruch it is written: Regarding whether he must return the payment, see Bach, siman 9, seif 9, and what I wrote in my glosses to Shach, siman 208, and in Even HaEzer, siman 17, seif 3. The law is not as the Bach states; he must return it. End of quote. See what I wrote above in siman 9, seif 5, seif katan 8, and see there further.
