(Further there: a person is not disqualified through a transgression, etc.) The discussion continues through “his claim is a valid claim.” The nearby incident involving Bar Binyatom, where one person said, “He lent me money with interest,” may be different, since perhaps he was not demanding return of the interest, as the Beit Yosef explains in the name of the Mordekhai. The Tur therefore cites the Baal HaTerumot to teach that the statement is effective even when advanced as a legal claim. This is unlike the astonishment expressed in the work Binyan Shlomo, which asks why the Tur cited the Baal HaTerumot for what is already explicit in the Gemara.
