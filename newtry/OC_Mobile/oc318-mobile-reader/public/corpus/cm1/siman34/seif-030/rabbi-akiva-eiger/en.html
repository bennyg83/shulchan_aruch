[Taz, s.v. But a butcher who was found, etc.] Because of desire for money. Note: See Rabbeinu Yerucham, cited in Beit Yosef, who implies that an element of monetary wrongdoing is required only for a rabbinic transgression. For a Torah transgression, even where the witness is disqualified only rabbinically, such an element is not required. See Penei Yehoshua, Bava Metzia 72, on Tosafos s.v. "A document containing interest," who writes that the Riva disagrees with Rabbeinu Yerucham. The implication of all the authorities in this siman is that monetary wrongdoing is not required. See also Mishneh LaMelech.
