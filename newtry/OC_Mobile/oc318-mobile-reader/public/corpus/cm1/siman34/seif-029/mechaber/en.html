Anyone liable to lashes regains fitness to testify once the beit din administers the lashes. Other disqualified witnesses, whose disqualification arose from money they extorted or stole, require repentance even after repaying it. They remain disqualified until it is known that they abandoned their evil course. When do lenders at interest regain fitness? When they voluntarily tear up their loan documents and completely repent, refraining from lending at interest even to a non-Jew. They must return all interest taken to its owners; if they do not know from whom it was taken, they must use it for public needs. {Rama: Some say this applies specifically to habitual thieves and robbers. One who stole or robbed only on an isolated occasion has repented as soon as he returns what he took, provided he returned it voluntarily. If he returned it only under compulsion of beit din, repayment does not suffice until he repents (Tur, siman 15, and Responsa of the Rosh, klal 59). This appears correct to me.}
