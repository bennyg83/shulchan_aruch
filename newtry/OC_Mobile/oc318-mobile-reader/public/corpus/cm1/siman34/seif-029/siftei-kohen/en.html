Until it is known that they have retracted. The Ran writes in the second chapter of Ketubot that, according to Rabbeinu Chananel and the Rif, a robber becomes qualified only from the time witnesses testify before beit din that he repented. Any documents he signed in the interim are invalid. The Ran finds this difficult, since once witnesses testify that he was already fit when he signed, it is retroactively established that he was qualified. The Sema cites this and asks why the Mechaber did not rule explicitly. There is no difficulty, for the Mechaber's ruling below, siman 46, seif 26, as the Sema himself explains there, follows Rabbeinu Chananel and the Rif. The Kesef Mishneh likewise derives this from the Rambam. Nevertheless, I wrote in siman 46 that the Ran's view is primary. It is possible that the Rif and Rambam agree in practice, in accordance with the Ritva cited there. See there.
