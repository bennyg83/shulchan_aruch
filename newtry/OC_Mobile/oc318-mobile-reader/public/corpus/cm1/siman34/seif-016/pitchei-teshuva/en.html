A thief, etc. In the marginal notes of Rabbi Akiva Eiger: Maharikash writes that regarding one who steals money from a non-Jew, Radbaz, siman 258, rules that he is disqualified by Torah law. Maharikash himself writes that it appears proper to validate him because people do not generally perceive the act as forbidden, and this is the appropriate ruling. I have found that Radbaz himself, part 4, siman 205, ultimately reaches the same conclusion and validates such a person for this reason, so long as he was not first warned and informed that this conduct would disqualify him from testimony. See there. This is cited in part by Baer Heitev to Even HaEzer, siman 141, seif katan 27.
