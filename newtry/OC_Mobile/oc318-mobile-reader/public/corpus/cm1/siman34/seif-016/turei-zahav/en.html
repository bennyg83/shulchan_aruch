(Further there: one who violates an oath, etc., through “nevertheless, our master contradicted his own words, etc.”) Rivash, responsum 266, writes that regarding testimony even Rashi agrees that such a person is disqualified because he violated Torah law. Rashi’s distinction was stated only regarding the administration of an oath. This is also implied by the Tur, who rules here without qualification, while in siman 92, concerning an oath, he records two opinions. This is the primary ruling. Rashach likewise writes so in responsum 172.
