And afterward witnesses came. Birkei Yosef, note 1, writes: When a person requests that a ban be proclaimed requiring anyone who knows of a disqualification in the witnesses to come and disclose it, if he speaks only as a possibility, we do not heed him and he deserves rebuke. If he asserts it with certainty, we hear him. Everything depends upon the beit din's assessment. This is from Bnei Aharon, siman 53. See also Ra'anach responsa, part 1, siman 111. It appears that this is derived from below, siman 92, seif 6.
