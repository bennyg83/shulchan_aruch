(Further there.) Rabbi Yosef Yakar writes concerning one who swore to contradict a single witness, through the inference from the incident under discussion. His text of the Gemara reads, “He stole from me a handle of a spear,” and this is likewise the reading of Rivash, responsum 339, and the Ramah in the chapter Hezkat HaBatim. Our printed texts, however, read, “He stole a handle of a spear in my presence.” See the Rosh there.
