A person is not disqualified, etc. See what I wrote below at the end of siman 46 and in the rules of migo in siman 82 at length.
As a witness initially. Meaning that one should not deliberately designate him and entrust the matter to him as a witness. Shach.
On the basis of a rumor, etc. Even a continuous, unbroken rumor does not disqualify a person from testimony. Sema in the name of the Rosh, excluding Rashba's view.
