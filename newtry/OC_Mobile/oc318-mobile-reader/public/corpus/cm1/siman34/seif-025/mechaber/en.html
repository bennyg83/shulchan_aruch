A person is not disqualified for a transgression by his own admission, but only through witnesses who testify against him, because a person cannot render himself wicked. {Rama: Nevertheless, he should not be selected as a witness initially, as stated below in siman 92, seif 5. Nor is one disqualified by rumor or mere suspicion. For example, a person suspected of sexual immorality because he regularly associates privately with forbidden women and is the subject of rumors remains fit for all testimony except testimony permitting a woman to remarry (Tur, siman 9). See Yoreh De'ah, siman 119, regarding persons under suspicion.}
