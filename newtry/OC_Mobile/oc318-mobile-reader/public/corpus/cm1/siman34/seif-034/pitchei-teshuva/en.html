Kenesset Yechezkel, siman 89, offers an important application. Suppose three people testify that a fourth lent to them all together at rabbinic interest. With respect to each borrower there are two other witnesses, so each borrower is disqualified. If rabbinically disqualified witnesses require no proclamation, then their testimony against the lender cannot be accepted after they themselves have become disqualified, and the lender remains qualified. If proclamation is required, however, the borrowers remain qualified until proclamation and their testimony disqualifies the lender. See there. Sha'ar HaMelech, Hilchot Gerushin 4:4, gives two additional applications. Baer Heitev's partial resolution based on a responsum of the Rashba is also given by Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 14.
