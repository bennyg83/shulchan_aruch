[Shulchan Aruch] For a person does not render himself wicked. Note: If he comes to deny the accusation and excuse himself, saying, "I did such and such," because it appeared to him that he had not sinned thereby, but the beit din recognizes from his own words that he did sin, until ultimately he concedes to the words of the beit din through the course of his own argument, we do not apply here the rule that a person does not render himself wicked. Responsa Devar Moshe, Orach Chaim, siman 5.
