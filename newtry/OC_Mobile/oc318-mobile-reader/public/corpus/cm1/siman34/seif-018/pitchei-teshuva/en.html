From the time he testified, etc. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: The same applies to testimony he gave immediately before the testimony concerning which he was later shown to be a zomem, as is explained in Bava Kamma 73a. See the novellae of the Ramban at the beginning of the chapter Chezkas HaBatim. Shitah Mekubetzes in Bava Kamma there, however, cites many early authorities who hold that this applies only if the two testimonies concerned the same claimant and defendant. If the two testimonies concerned different people, the earlier testimony remains valid. End of quote.
