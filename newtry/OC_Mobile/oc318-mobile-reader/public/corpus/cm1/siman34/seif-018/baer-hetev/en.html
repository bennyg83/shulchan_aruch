To go. The Taz writes that this implies that if he has already seen the event, he may not take payment for going to give testimony. It appears to me that this applies where no exertion is involved. Where going entails effort, he may accept payment for the trip. Proof may be brought from the chapter HaIsh Mekadesh concerning one who takes payment to sprinkle purification water, etc. See there.
A gift. The Rosh writes that if one exempts a judge from a tax, it is permitted, because a Torah scholar is in any event exempt from taxes. See there. The Sema writes that the reason requires explanation, and also asks why the Rama placed this gloss here rather than in the laws of judges.
