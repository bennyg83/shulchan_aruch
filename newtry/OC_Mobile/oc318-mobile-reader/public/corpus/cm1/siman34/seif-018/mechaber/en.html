People of degraded conduct are rabbinically disqualified from testimony. These include people who walk about eating in the marketplace before everyone, those who go naked in the marketplace while engaged in degrading labor, and similar people who are indifferent to shame. Such people are regarded like dogs and are not concerned about giving false testimony. Included among them are those who publicly accept charity from non Jews even though they could obtain sustenance privately. By degrading themselves and showing no concern for their dignity, all such people are rabbinically disqualified. Rama: Anyone who accepts payment for testifying has void testimony, just as one who accepts payment for judging is disqualified, as explained above in siman 9, seif 5. No public declaration of disqualification is required. Rather, the judgment or testimony is void automatically. If he returns the money, his judgment and testimony are valid, because this is not like other disqualifications that require a declaration and repentance. The Sages imposed a penalty that his acts be void as long as he accepts payment. This applies to witnesses who have already seen the event and are obligated to testify, just as a person may be obligated to judge between one person and another. One who is not obligated to testify, however, and accepts payment to go and observe a matter so that he may become a witness, may do so. A judge who has already ruled a case and whose successful litigant afterwards wishes to give him a gift because he argued in that litigant's favor may not accept it. Any testimony given because of suffering or fear has no substance.
