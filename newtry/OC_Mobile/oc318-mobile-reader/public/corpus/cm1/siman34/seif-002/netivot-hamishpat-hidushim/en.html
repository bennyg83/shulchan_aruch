An offense for which there are no lashes. See Netivot, seif katan 1, for which transgressions without lashes nevertheless create Torah disqualification and which create only rabbinic disqualification. One who has relations with a betrothed woman without the required blessing is rabbinically disqualified. One who has relations with a gentile woman is likewise rabbinically disqualified. If he does so publicly or takes her in a marriage like relationship, he violates the prohibition of intermarriage and is disqualified by Torah law.
