He committed a transgression. This appears from the Rambam's language above. There, "Master, the yoke is bitter on my shoulders," implies specifically, and he infers from the Mishnah concerning the lender with interest. In Rosh Hashana 22 it says that the persons in the Mishnah are disqualified only rabbinically. Tosafot's difficulty there is therefore answered: specifically lashes render him Torah-disqualified, whereas interest is rectified by a positive commandment, as stated in Eizehu Neshech and Temurah, "and your brother shall live with you, etc.," and if he acted, the act is not legally effective.
