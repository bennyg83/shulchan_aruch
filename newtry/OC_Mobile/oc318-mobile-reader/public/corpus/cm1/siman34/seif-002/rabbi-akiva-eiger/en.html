[Shulchan Aruch] Disqualified from testimony. Note: We do not proclaim a ban in the synagogue requiring anyone who knows that a witness is disqualified to come and testify. See below, siman 92, seif 6. But one may proclaim a ban requiring anyone who knows testimony that would subject the witnesses to hazamah to come forward. Responsa Raanach, part 2, siman 19.
