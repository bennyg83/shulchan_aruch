Disqualified rabbinically. The same applies to one who has relations with his betrothed before the marriage blessing, because a bride is prohibited to her husband before the blessing; the disqualification is rabbinic. One who has relations with a gentile woman is likewise ordinarily disqualified rabbinically. But if he does so publicly or takes her in a marital manner, he violates the Torah prohibition against intermarriage and is disqualified by Torah law. Sema.
