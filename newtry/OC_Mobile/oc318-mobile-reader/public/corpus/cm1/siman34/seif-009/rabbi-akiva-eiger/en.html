[Sema, subparagraph 16] Note: See the answer given to this in the novellae of Mahari, of blessed memory, cited in the Taz. It is clear from his words that for any transgression that does not carry lashes, where the disqualification is rabbinic, an element of monetary wrongdoing is required. However, Rabbeinu Yerucham, cited in Beit Yosef, implies that such monetary wrongdoing is required only for a rabbinic transgression, whereas for a Torah transgression, even where the disqualification itself is only rabbinic, it is not required. See Penei Yehoshua, Bava Metzia 72, on Tosafos s.v. "Shtar," who writes that the Ribam disagrees with Rabbeinu Yerucham. The implication of all the authorities in this siman is that monetary wrongdoing is not required, and Mishneh LaMelech likewise writes so in chapter 4 of Hilchos Malveh.
