(In the name of Mahari in the Tur: even a valid witness who knows that his fellow witness is wicked.) The Beit Yosef wonders why the Tur does not derive this from “Distance yourself from a false matter.” One may answer that the Rambam understood the Gemara as discussing a case in which the wicked witness had already testified before the beit din and the valid witness does not accompany him to court. Even so, the valid witness may not join the testimony because of “Distance yourself from a false matter.” Where, however, he comes together with the wicked witness as one group before the beit din, the prohibition is derived from the more severe verse, “Do not join your hand with a wicked person,” a negative commandment forbidding one to assist the wicked person by becoming a witness together with him, whether they observed the event together or separately.
