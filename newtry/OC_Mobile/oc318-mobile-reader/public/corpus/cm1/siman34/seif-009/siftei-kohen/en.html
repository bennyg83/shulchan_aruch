Until they state it before beit din. The same applies if they wrote in a document that it was written at its proper time. So states the Ran at the end of the second chapter of Ketubot.
For they may have written it and postdated it. See the Gemara at the beginning of Echad Dinei Mamonot. The Bach explains that according to the Baal HaMaor, refuted witnesses concerning a document are punished only through personal disqualification, not monetary payment. The Ran appears to understand him otherwise, but the Bach's reading is supported by the Baal HaMaor's wording and avoids placing him against the Gemara. On this basis, the Rif too may agree with him, contrary to the Ran. The Baal HaMaor's ruling is therefore correct and may be undisputed.
