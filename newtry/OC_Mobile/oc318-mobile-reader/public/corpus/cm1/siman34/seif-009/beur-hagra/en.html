There are no witnesses. Sanhedrin 32a. See the Rif, Bava Kamma, chapter 7.
But if there are no, etc. This is learned from the Gemara cited above, for in a case of uncertainty we attribute matters in a way that validates the document. See Tosafot there.
Witnesses who signed. Proof is brought from the Jerusalem Talmud cited by the Ran at the beginning of Rosh Hashanah: 'They are its witnesses,' etc.; 'we signed regarding this,' etc. Ramban explains it where their signatures are not otherwise authenticated. Even so, they are believed only because they say, 'We signed regarding this,' as stated in Ketubot 18b: 'They taught this only,' etc., because otherwise they are not believed. See Tosafot, Bava Metzia.
