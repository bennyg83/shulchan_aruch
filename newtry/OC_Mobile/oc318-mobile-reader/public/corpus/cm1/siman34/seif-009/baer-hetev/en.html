And they postdated it. The Baal HaMaor holds that with a document, witnesses shown to be false witnesses are not punished with bodily or monetary penalties. The Shach agrees and writes that this ruling is correct and undisputed. See below, simanim 43 and 239.
Who know. This means that they saw the witnesses sign, but did not see the date and place written in the document, for otherwise they too would be witnesses subject to hazamah. Sema. The Taz writes that the Sema's words are difficult and explains the matter differently. See there.
At the time. For this reason they become wicked and disqualified from that time, because it is as though they testified then. They do not become disqualified from the date of signing written in the document, because there is no testimony as to when they actually signed; they may have wickedly antedated the document, as the Mechaber concludes. Sema.
They signed. This means that he had a document written without witnesses' signatures, which remained in his possession for several years, and today the witnesses signed it. Thus the date of writing precedes the signing. Sema.
Disqualified. Rashba writes in a responsum that one who hires witnesses to testify for him does not thereby disqualify them until the moment they actually testify. Sema.
