(Shach, seif katan 8) The Maharshal speaks of such a case. Note: One may say that only with respect to witnesses is there room to claim forgetfulness after sufficient time has passed, because a witness is not continuously obligated to keep the matter in mind. If he later swears that he does not know testimony, his oath may be truthful. By contrast, when a person has accepted an oath upon himself, he remains obligated to remember it continually so that forgetfulness not cause him to violate his oath. See Sema, seif katan 30.
