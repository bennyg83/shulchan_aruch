But regarding an oath concerning the future. Rivash rules that if one swore not to eat and then ate, he is disqualified because he performed an act in violation of the oath. If he swore that he would eat and then failed to eat, where he committed no act, he is not disqualified. Maharam Mintz writes that the halachah follows this mediating view.
