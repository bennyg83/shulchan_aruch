m) The Rambam at the end of chapter 10, also cited by the Tur, from what Rabbi Abahu taught before Rabbi Yochanan, Avodah Zarah 26a and 26b, which the Gemara there establishes as referring to an apostate who sins spitefully.
n) He concludes there that these have no share in the World to Come, and therefore the Sages did not need to enumerate them. The righteous among the nations of the world do have a share in the World to Come. The Kesef Mishneh writes that this follows Rabbi Yehoshua, whose view is accepted against Rabbi Eliezer in the chapter Challah.
