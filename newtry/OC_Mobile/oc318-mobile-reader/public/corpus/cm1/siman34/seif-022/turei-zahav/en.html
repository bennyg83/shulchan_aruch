(Further there: one who violates a rabbinic prohibition of theft, etc.) Know the Rambam’s position. See also siman 359, seif 9, where the Rambam’s language in chapter 1 of Hilchot Gezeilah is cited: one violates “You shall not covet” even if he pays the price and the owner says, “I consent.” See the Beit Yosef there. This requires study. The primary explanation is that the Rambam holds the witnesses may be mistaken about the prohibition, and therefore they are disqualified only rabbinically.
