Repentance. Regarding those who acted under duress on a day of massacre, see Responsa Rosh, rule 24, and Rivash, simanim 4, 11, and 12. Terumat HaDeshen explains why an apostate who accepts repentance is immediately accepted: he had been mixed among gentiles who have access to every desire, and now comes to separate himself from them and repent, so the Sages did not impose a further delay. Thus quotes the Sema. The Shach writes that this wording might imply that the rule applies only to an apostate to idolatry, but that is incorrect. The true reason is that we presume he will fulfill what he has accepted, and the same rule applies to every apostate.
