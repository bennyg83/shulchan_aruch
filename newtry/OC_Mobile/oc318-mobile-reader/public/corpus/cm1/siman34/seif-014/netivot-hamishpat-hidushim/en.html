Tax collectors. A customs collector takes according to his own estimate and is therefore presumed disqualified. A fixed tax collector, by contrast, collects a predetermined amount for the king and is not presumed disqualified without specific evidence:
Even one time. Meaning, if we know that he took unlawfully on one occasion, we presume that he likely took improperly on other occasions as well. If it is known that he did so only once, the Sema leaves unresolved whether that single act disqualifies him:
