And a minor. This acquisition is only rabbinic, enacted for the sake of peaceful relations, as explained at the beginning of siman 270. The Mechaber specifically speaks of a found object, because no other person's intent is involved in transferring ownership to the minor. Sema.
Large livestock. The Mechaber does not write that one who raises small livestock is disqualified even when he keeps it at home, because that prohibition applied only in Eretz Yisrael and Babylonia when most Jews owned fields and livestock, as the Tur writes in siman 409. This does not apply in our times. See the end of siman 425 concerning shepherds of small livestock, of whom the law says we neither lower them into danger nor rescue them. Large livestock is not mentioned there, which indicates that regarding the severe rule of refraining from rescue, the Sages did not extend the disqualification so far. Thus the Sema.
