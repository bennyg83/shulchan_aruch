(In the Tur, seif 10: it is like one who eats non-kosher meat for appetite.) The Rambam does not include the words “for appetite,” and in truth they are unnecessary. The Tur’s point appears to be that although the prohibited act has not yet been completed, since the forbidden food has not yet been eaten, the witness is nevertheless disqualified because he is suspected of giving false testimony. The Tur compares him to one who eats non-kosher meat, whom the Gemara disqualifies according to all opinions because just as hunger leads him to eat forbidden meat, so too hunger for money may lead him to accept payment and testify falsely. Here as well, since falsehood does not trouble him and he is willing to cause another person to eat what is forbidden, he likewise will not hesitate to remove money from another through false testimony.
