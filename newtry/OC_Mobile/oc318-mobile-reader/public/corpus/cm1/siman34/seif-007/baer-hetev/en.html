That they returned it. The Tur writes that this applies specifically where they did not pay of their own accord but only under compulsion of beit din. Although with one who lends at interest merely returning the money is insufficient, and he must additionally undertake restrictions upon himself by forbidding even what is permitted, as explained below in seif 29, that applies to one who has habituated himself to the practice. If it occurred only once and he returned the money voluntarily, that alone suffices. So writes Darkei Moshe. Sema.
