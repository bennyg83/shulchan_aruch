Even though he returned it, etc. The Tur writes that this applies specifically where he repaid only after compulsion by beit din. In the case of one who regularly lends with interest, mere repayment is insufficient and he must accept upon himself an additional restriction, forbidding even what would otherwise be permitted, as explained in seif 29. That stricter requirement applies only to one habituated to the sin. But one who committed theft or a similar offense only once and then voluntarily restored what he took has done enough to demonstrate repentance.
