Even though he returned it. See Rama at the end of this siman, seif 29. Mere return is insufficient where it occurred only through compulsion of beit din or where the person is already established as a thief.
One who shares with a thief, etc. See Tumim, where I leave this rule difficult. At minimum, the case must be where the stolen property has already left his possession. Even if beit din compelled him to surrender it, the theft is no longer in his hands. But if stolen Jewish property remains in his possession and he refuses to return it, he is certainly a wicked person because of violent monetary wrongdoing.
