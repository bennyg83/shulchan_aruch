(Further there.) The Nimukei Yosef presents another approach, through “and from his words we learn, etc.” This is incorrect, for in the chapter Eizehu Neshekh he explicitly rules regarding a document containing interest that the witnesses are disqualified because they are liable to lashes. One must therefore distinguish the borrower: because he is the one who suffers loss, he may err and is not disqualified, unlike the witnesses.
