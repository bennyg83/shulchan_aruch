A mamzer, etc. Meaning, one might have thought to derive a kal vachomer from a woman, who is fit to enter the congregation, just as the Gemara there derives regarding a slave, but this can be refuted because a woman is not obligated in all mitzvot, as stated there 88a; see Tosafot.
(Collected note) A mamzer, etc. Meaning, even though they are disqualified from entering the congregation, proof that they are fit is from the statement, "there is one who is fit to judge monetary cases, etc.," and the Gemara says this includes a mamzer. It further states there that anyone fit to judge is fit to testify. The same applies according to the other opinion, and all the more so to an uncircumcised Jew, etc., as stated at the beginning of Chullin that he is a full Jew. (End of collected note.)
And an uncircumcised Jew. At the beginning of Chullin 4b: "what is the case of this uncircumcised person? etc.; he is a full Jew."
