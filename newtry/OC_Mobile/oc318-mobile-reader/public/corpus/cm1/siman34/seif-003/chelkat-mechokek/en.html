After completing the blessing, he should perform the kiddushin. Meaning, ideally the primary mitzvah is to recite the blessing before performing the act, excluding certain authorities who hold that ideally the blessing is recited after the kiddushin. Concerning this, the Rosh writes in a responsum that they are mistaken. But if he forgot and did not recite the blessing before the erusin, he should recite it after the kiddushin, especially according to our custom in which another person recites the blessing rather than the groom. See Hagahot Maimoniyot at the end of chapter 3 of Hilchot Ishut.
