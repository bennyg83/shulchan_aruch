(Further there: see Rivash, responsum 339, etc., through “he has not yet brought him to judgment, etc.”) This means that the witness admits that he took interest from him, but the borrower is not presently suing him. If he had already sued him, the lender would already have been released from the claim and the borrower could no longer disqualify him. This disagrees with Raavyah. According to Raavyah, if the lender already took the interest, the borrower is an interested witness because he says this in order to sue for recovery of the interest. Rivash writes explicitly in responsum 339 that Rashba disagrees with Raavyah, contrary to the Beit Yosef.
