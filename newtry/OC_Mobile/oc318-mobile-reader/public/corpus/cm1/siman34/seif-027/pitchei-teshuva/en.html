A judge who already judged, etc. This is implied by the Rosh, etc. See Pilpula Charifta of the author of Tosafos Yom Tov there, entry 21, who writes that another major rule is learned from the words of the Rosh: accepting a bribe is forbidden even regarding a matter that is not a Torah judgment but merely a discretionary communal penalty. He adds: I have written this to instruct communal trustees that even though their decisions are not Torah judgments and they were not appointed for Torah adjudication, they must nevertheless be careful not to accept gifts in connection with their decisions. End of quote.
