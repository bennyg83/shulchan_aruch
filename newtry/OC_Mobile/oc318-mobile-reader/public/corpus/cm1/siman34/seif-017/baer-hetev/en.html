And not. The Sema writes that there are three categories. If it is known that a person lacks all three qualities, he is entirely disqualified, even after the fact. If it is certain that he possesses all three, then even if we do not know that he actively performs mitzvot, he is presumed to do so and is valid even initially. If ordinarily we know that he has not studied, but it is unknown whether he conducts himself properly and is observant of mitzvot, then we do not entrust testimony to him initially, but after the fact his testimony is not invalidated.
