One does not transmit testimony to an ignoramus, etc. The Beit Yosef questions why the Tur omitted the view of his father, the Rosh, who held that nowadays we accept testimony even from an ignoramus so that he should not go and build a private altar for himself. See the Bach. It appears more correct to say that nowadays most ordinary people are familiar with proper civil conduct and occupy themselves with worldly affairs. Therefore, if there is an individual who lacks these qualities, his testimony should not be accepted. The concern that such people will go and establish their own system does not apply merely because of an isolated individual when the majority conduct themselves properly. Magen Avraham writes similarly in Orach Chaim, siman 199, seif katan 1.
