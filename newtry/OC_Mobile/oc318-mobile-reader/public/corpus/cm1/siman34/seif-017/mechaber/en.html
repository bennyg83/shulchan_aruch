A person who is versed in neither Scripture nor Mishnah and does not conduct himself properly is presumed wicked and is rabbinically disqualified from testifying. Therefore, testimony should not be entrusted to an unlearned person and should not be accepted from him unless he is established as one who performs mitzvos and acts of kindness, follows upright ways, and behaves properly, even though he remains unlearned in Scripture and Mishnah. Thus, every Torah scholar is presumed fit until disqualified, while every unlearned person is presumed disqualified until established as following upright ways. One who accepts an unlearned person's testimony before that presumption is established, or before witnesses testify that he observes mitzvos and proper conduct, is an incompetent who will ultimately be held to account, for he causes Jewish money to be lost on the testimony of wicked people (Tur in the name of Rambam).
