It is in the power of evil, because he does not know what the good way he will do and what is the bad way that he laughs from:
Therefore, etc. If we know that a person is neither learned in Scripture and Mishnah nor conducts himself properly in worldly affairs, he is certainly disqualified, because such a person cannot reliably preserve the way of mitzvot. If he is learned and also conducts himself properly, he is presumed fit even if we have not independently established that he carefully observes mitzvot. If, however, we know only that he lacks Torah learning and do not know whether he nevertheless follows proper conduct, there remains a possibility that his upright way of life keeps him within the practices of decent and observant people. Therefore we do not initially entrust testimony to him until his observance is established, but after the fact we do not invalidate his testimony merely from lack of learning. Meirat Einayim.
