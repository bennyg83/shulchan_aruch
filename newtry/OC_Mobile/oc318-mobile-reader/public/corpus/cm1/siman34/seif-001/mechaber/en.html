Witnesses disqualified because of transgression. This siman contains 35 seifim.
A wicked person is disqualified from testimony. Even a valid witness who knows that his fellow witness is wicked, while the judges do not know of his wickedness, may not testify together with him, even though it is testimony in the language of the Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Edut, that is true. It goes without saying that a valid witness who knows testimony concerning his fellow and knows that the second witness with him is testifying falsely may not testify together with him.
