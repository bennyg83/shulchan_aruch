Even if the other witness is valid, and even if the testimony is true and was not invalidated by the disqualified witness because they did not see the event together, it is nevertheless forbidden for the valid witness to testify together with him. The disqualified person counts only as one witness, while beit din, unaware of his wickedness, will regard them as two valid witnesses and extract money unlawfully. Urim VeTumim, unlike the Shach.
