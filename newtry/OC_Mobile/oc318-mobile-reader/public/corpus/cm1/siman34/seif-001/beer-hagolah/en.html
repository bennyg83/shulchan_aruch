The Shach writes that this law requires great study, because it is far from reason that one who knows the matter is true should refrain from testifying. He concludes that since we rule that where three witnesses testified and one is found related or otherwise disqualified, the entire testimony is void even though two valid witnesses remain, the same applies here. Although the matter is true, it is a Scriptural decree that because there is a disqualified witness the entire testimony is void, and therefore he may not testify.
