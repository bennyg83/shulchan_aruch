[Shulchan Aruch] A wicked person is disqualified. One who knows that he deliberately committed a transgression that disqualifies him from testimony, while beit din is unaware of his disqualification, may not testify even truthfully. Responsa Chavos Yair, additions, now printed in siman 15, s.v. "HaOmnam." Regarding one who repeatedly told his adversary that if the latter did such and such he would convert out of Judaism, see Responsa Mahari ben Lev, part 3, siman 6.
