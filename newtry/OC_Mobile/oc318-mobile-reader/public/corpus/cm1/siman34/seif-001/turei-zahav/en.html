(Seif 7: even though they returned it.) Many question the Tur's surprise at Rambam's ruling, where he writes, "I do not know why they should not become qualified," etc., since the Tur himself writes at the end of this siman that repentance is required. It appears to me that the disqualifications under discussion are by Torah law, as Rambam states explicitly, and for this reason our master lists them among Torah disqualifications. He therefore asks why, once the stolen property is returned, they should not at least regain Torah qualification, as explained in siman 360 that theft is a prohibition repaired by a positive commandment through returning the stolen property. The answer is that where he returned it only under compulsion, this is not considered a true return.
