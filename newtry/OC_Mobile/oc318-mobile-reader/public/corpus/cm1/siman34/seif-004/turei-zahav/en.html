(Seif 14: tax collectors are presumptively disqualified.) It appears that according to both the Tur and Rambam, a customs collector retains for himself what he collects. He is therefore suspected even without specific proof, because he has relatively little fear of the government when taking more than the fixed amount. A government tax assessor, however, is trusted by the king to deliver the tax he collects, and fears the government if he takes more than the fixed assessment and keeps it, since he would be stealing the king's purse. We certainly do not suspect that he gives the excess to the king as well, because a person does not sin without personal benefit.
