[Rama] Against his fellow to strike him. Note: But if the blow caused damage worth a perutah, in which case the rule is that he pays monetary compensation and does not receive lashes, he is nevertheless disqualified by Torah law. Since the prohibition carries lashes where the blow causes less than a perutah of damage, he is therefore disqualified even when the blow causes damage worth a perutah. So writes the Beit Yosef in a responsum concerning the laws of kiddushin, siman 2.
