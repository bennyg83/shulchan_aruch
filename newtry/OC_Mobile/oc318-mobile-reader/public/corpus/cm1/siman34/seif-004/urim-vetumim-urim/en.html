Because they think, etc. The same applies to one who lent money against holy books and then studied from them. Even according to the view that such use is considered unauthorized use by a bailee, he is not disqualified from testimony, because he thinks he is performing a mitzvah. Sema in the name of Darkei Moshe. See below in siman 72.
One who raises his hand, etc. Although raising a hand to strike is prohibited by Torah law, there is no lashes for the act itself. Therefore the person is disqualified only rabbinically. Even if the one threatened never brought a complaint, the aggressor remains disqualified until he repents from his wickedness. Sema in the name of Mordechai.
