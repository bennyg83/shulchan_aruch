Not merely a verbal retraction, but he writes: I, etc. The baraita in chapter Zeh Borer states this regarding people who dealt in produce of the Sabbatical year. Rashi explains that verbal repentance means merely saying, "I will not do this again." Rather, there must be a recognizable act of repentance, such as distributing the Sabbatical-year produce in their gardens to the poor. The baraita says only that he states his undertaking. Rambam and the Mechaber require that he write it, as an added reinforcement of the commitment he accepts upon himself.
