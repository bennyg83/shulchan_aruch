(Anthology) From when, etc., from the time he testified, etc. But not retroactively from the moment of the act about which he testified. The Jerusalem Talmud states: If witnesses stood and testified on the tenth of Nisan that he stole the ox on the first of Nisan and slaughtered or sold it on the tenth, and they were shown to be conspiring witnesses on the fifteenth, all testimony they gave from the tenth through the fifteenth is invalid. Rashba, explaining Rashi.
