[Shulchan Aruch] Before a public declaration was made concerning him. Note: See Responsa Avodas HaGershuni, siman 67, who writes that this rule also includes one who commits a transgression that does not carry lashes and is therefore disqualified only rabbinically, so that he too requires a public declaration. Maharit's view, however, in responsa part 2, Even HaEzer, siman 43, is that in such a case no declaration is required. See Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 4 of Hilchos Malveh.
