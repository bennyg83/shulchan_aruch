The produce, etc. Produce follows the law of improvement. The sugya calls it improvement and asks from the case of collecting for consumed produce. Raza explains why the Gemara initially did not ask from the first clause, but later asks from the baraita.
(Supplement) The produce, etc. Rosh likewise writes that the law concerns the purchaser against both the dispossessed owner and the robber, and that the purchaser receives his expenditure. The reading that the land was stolen full of produce is difficult and appears to be a mistaken student gloss.
(Supplement) The produce, etc. Rambam likewise did not have that reading, as shown by his wording at the beginning of siman 372. See 81.
