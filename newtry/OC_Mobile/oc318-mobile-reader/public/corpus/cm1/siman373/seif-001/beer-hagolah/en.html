a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilkhot Gezeilah, law 6. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the conclusion in the first chapter of Bava Metzia 15b: the purchaser has reimbursement for the purchase money and for the improvement. The distinction the Rambam makes regarding which is greater, the improvement or the expenses, is explained in the Rif.
b) Ibid., law 7. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this too is explained there, in the dispute of Rav and Shmuel on 14b, with Rava ruling like Rav on 15b, and so in the Rif. Tosafot likewise write this at the beginning of 14b.
gimel) The dispute of Rav and Shmuel there on 15b, with Rava ruling like Rav that he has reimbursement for the purchase money but not for the improvement.
dalet) This is written in the Rif and is straightforward, Maggid Mishneh. Tosafot likewise write on 15b. The Bach writes that the Baal HaMaor agrees, but the Ramban disputes him and rules like the Rosh, and that this is the Rif's view, as explained in Milchamot. The Nimukei Yosef cites both opinions. Therefore, in a matter of unresolved law, property is not taken from the party in possession.
