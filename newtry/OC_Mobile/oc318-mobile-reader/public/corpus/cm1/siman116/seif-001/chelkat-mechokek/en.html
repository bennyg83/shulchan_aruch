One who marries a woman prohibited by a negative commandment. But if he marries a woman prohibited by karet, kiddushin do not take effect and she does not require a get, so the ordinary ketubah obligation does not arise. Perhaps, however, she receives the additional ketubah if he knew of the prohibition, because he knowingly undertook financial loss out of desire for the relationship. It is also possible that even if he did not know, she receives the additional ketubah just as a woman prohibited by a negative commandment or an aylonit may receive it, because he accepted the risk that the relationship might still be pleasing to him, as Ran explains. This is not comparable to a woman who violates religious practice and similar cases. See Ran. According to the Tur, a chalutzah has the same rule as a woman prohibited by a negative commandment even though the prohibition is rabbinic. See Beit Yosef on the nature of the dispute.
