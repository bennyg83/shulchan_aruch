(Shach, seif katan 3) And why should he pay, etc.; and so wrote the Maggid Mishneh, etc. Note: This is explicit in Nimukei Yosef in Bava Metzia in the sugya of appreciation. If a creditor forecloses, the seller need reimburse the purchaser only according to the land's present value. But if the true owner dispossesses the purchaser because the land was stolen, the seller must return the full purchase price even if the land depreciated. See Kiddushin 26a, Rashi at the end of the passage beginning "Rav Ashi said"; Ketubot 44a, Rashi, passage beginning "the law of a creditor"; and Shitah Mekubetzet to Ketubot 92, in the passage citing the Ramban.
