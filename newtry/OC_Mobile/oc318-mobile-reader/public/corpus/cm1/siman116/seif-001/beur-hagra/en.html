And the creditor does not... Meaning, where the purchaser bought without responsibility, he would need a power of attorney to litigate, as explained below in seif 2. Here, however, he collects by virtue of the seller's responsibility for the sale. That is the distinction between a purchase with responsibility and one without it. Even an oral responsibility established by witnesses enables collection from encumbered property, as stated in Bava Basra 42a. If a power of attorney alone sufficed in all such cases, the Gemara's distinction would be unnecessary.

As stated in Bava Metzia: the seller's undertaking can cover the purchase price, the value of the land even if it was bought below market, and its improvement even if it appreciated, as explained at the beginning of siman 115. This reading is not entirely compelled, for the Gemara's wording may combine the purchase and value into one measure. See Shach, seif katan 3.
And all the law... He has the legal status of a creditor toward the seller, except that no after-acquired-property clause is needed for the seller's ordinary responsibility, as above.
Some say specifically... See Rashi, Bava Basra, who explains that this principle applies to a purchaser but not necessarily to a gift recipient.
And that which he collects... The concern is double collection. Bava Basra 169a says that a second document is not written where it could enable repeated collection. Kesubos similarly worries that a woman might first use witnesses and afterward produce her ketubah, or repeatedly use evidence of bridal status. The remedy is to write a receipt, and Bava Basra concludes that the Sages generally permit receipts.
Nevertheless, beit din... With the original borrower there is no comparable concern because a receipt can be written, as in Kesubos and Bava Basra, and we rule in Bava Basra 171b that a receipt is written. If a later document is used against the borrower, he can plead that a substitute document had already been issued. Thus beit din writes an appropriate record even without surrender of the original document.
