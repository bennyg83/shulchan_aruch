Even if he did not expressly stipulate responsibility. See Baer Heitev, who writes that this is also explicit in the Rashbam. Shaar Mishpat writes that the matter is expressly discussed in the Rashba's novellae to the first chapter of Kiddushin in the name of the Ra'avad, and that the Rashba disagrees. The Ramban, in his novellae to the chapter HaMokher Et HaSefinah, likewise cites this view in the name of "some explain" and disagrees. See there. See also the Shach at the beginning of siman 225. The Shach here further cites Responsa Mabit as holding that the same applies if the buyer has not yet paid the seller the purchase price: the seller may collect the purchase price from encumbered property, etc. The Shach disagrees. Shaar Mishpat writes that the Shach overlooked the words of Baal HaItur, article 4, Achrayut, who explicitly writes in accordance with the Mabit. See there.
