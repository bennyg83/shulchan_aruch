Anyone who resides in the city, etc., for repair of the wall, etc. The Mechaber carefully lists these and similar obligations for which residence of twelve months creates liability. Matters of tzedakah and mitzvah have different periods stated in the Gemara: thirty days for the communal food plate, three months for the charity fund, six months for clothing, nine months for burial, and twelve months for city defenses, as the Tur and Mechaber write in Yoreh Deah 256.

For example, if he became ill, etc. Maharam of Rothenburg writes in his collected rulings that pregnancy is not considered unavoidable compulsion preventing a woman from traveling, because women in the seventh or eighth month sometimes ride and travel. If he claims that the community permitted him to remain until his wife gives birth, he need not swear; rather, a general ban is proclaimed. He further writes that where the community claims a person resided or conducted business there, they must swear or bring proof. If they bring neither and refuse to swear, the defendant swears.
