The ruler has no power. See Sema, seif katan 37. There is a scribal error and the text should read that before the unlawful levy was imposed, if the ruler exempted him on his own authority, dina d'malchuta applies and he is exempt. Sema disagrees with Shach, seif katan 16, and follows the second opinion in Mordechai that even where the ruler himself grants the exemption, the exemption belongs to the partnership. Sema's statement that concerning an idle person the exemption belongs to him personally, although not stated explicitly in Mordechai, is derived from Mordechai's treatment of people who were merely assistants and therefore were not full partners. An idle person who contributes only as an assistant is analogous.
