Torah scholars are exempt from taxes. Sefer Chasidim, siman 1013, states: This applies specifically to those who study day and night and have no other occupation. If, however, one studies and also engages in worldly work, he is like everyone else and must participate in every levy imposed by the community. The authorities do not rule this way, as explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 243; see there. See Responsa Mabit, part 1, siman 25; Responsa Maharshaldam, siman 360; Responsa Mahari ibn Lev, simanim 43 and 47; Responsa Rabbi Levi ibn Chaviv, siman 130; and Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 52.
