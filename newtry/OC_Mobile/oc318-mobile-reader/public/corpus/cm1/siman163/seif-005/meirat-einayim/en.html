The synagogue cantor is exempt, etc. The same applies to others occupied with the needs of the synagogue. Strictly speaking, all of them are liable, for we have found an exemption only for a Torah scholar. Maharam of Rothenburg also records a custom that one who does not possess one zekuk of silver, meaning five pounds of Vienna currency, is exempt from tax. Regarding a teacher, servant, and scribe, the custom is that if he does not have two zekukim he pays nothing, but if he has more than that, he pays from all that he has.
