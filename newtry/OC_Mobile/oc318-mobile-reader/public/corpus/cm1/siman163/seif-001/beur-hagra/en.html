To build... and to purchase. The Tosefta at the end of Bava Metzia.
The same applies to every. The Tosefta says that one who owns a courtyard in another city still contributes to certain public needs.
See Orach Chaim... This too is a communal need, like a synagogue.
The residents of the city compel. Bava Basra 8a regarding thirty days for the soup kitchen. See the Rosh.
Regarding expenses. This too is among communal needs, and those who remain must follow the communal determination.
All communal needs... all taxpayers. Bava Kama 116b says caravan members may make binding conditions among themselves. Rabbeinu Chananel explains this even without a kinyan, because partners in their common affairs have authority resembling beit din. The butchers in Bava Basra 9a likewise may make rules and impose penalties. The same applies to all who share the tax burden.
They must accept upon themselves. Sanhedrin and Yevamos teach that such communal authority does not extend to uprooting Torah law. See siman 2.
