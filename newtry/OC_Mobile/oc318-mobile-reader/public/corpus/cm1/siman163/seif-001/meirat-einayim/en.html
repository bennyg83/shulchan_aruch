So that everyone may read from them, etc. This is the Rambam's language in chapter 6 of Hilchot Shekhenim. In the Perishah I wrote that in our times, when books are readily available among us, the practice is not to compel townspeople to purchase communal books, except for a Torah scroll. Nevertheless, to prevent neglect of Torah study, beit din may compel residents to lend books for others to learn from, as the Rosh writes in responsum, klal 93, cited by Rama at the end of siman 292. There is support for that rule here as well.
