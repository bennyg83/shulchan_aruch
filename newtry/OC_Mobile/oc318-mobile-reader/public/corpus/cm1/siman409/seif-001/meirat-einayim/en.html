One may not raise small livestock, etc. The Tur writes that it is forbidden even inside one's house, and even if the animal does not belong to him. Although a person ordinarily does not sin for another's benefit, small livestock commonly escape and cause damage. The Sages therefore prohibited raising them out of concern that they might leave without the shepherd's knowledge and damage others.
