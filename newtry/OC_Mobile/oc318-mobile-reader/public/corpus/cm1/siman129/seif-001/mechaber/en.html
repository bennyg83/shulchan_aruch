The law of a guarantor under a document or orally, and when collection may be made from the guarantor. It contains 22 se'ifim.
If one person lent another money and a third person afterward said, 'I am the guarantor,' or if the creditor sued the borrower and another person said, 'Leave him alone, and I am the guarantor,' or the creditor was physically pressing the borrower in the marketplace to pay and another person said, 'Leave him alone, and I am the guarantor,' the guarantor has no liability. This is so even if he said before beit din, 'I am the guarantor.' {Rama: If he paid the lender at the borrower's instruction, however, the borrower must reimburse what he gave the lender. If a kinyan was made with him obligating him as guarantor for this money, whether before beit din or privately between him and the lender, he becomes obligated. A person who tells another, 'I am your guarantor for one day,' says nothing legally effective. See note 111.}
