The language of Rambam at the beginning of Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh chapter 25. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this clause and the clause in seif 2 are the halachic ruling at the end of Get Pashut, Bava Batra 176a and 176b. Rambam interprets a guarantor appointed by beit din as one who became guarantor directly through the beit din, and this is the primary interpretation.
