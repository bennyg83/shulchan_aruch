There, and likewise in Ba'al HaTerumot. This is also the text of Rambam before us in chapter 25, law 4, and the textual version of the Maggid Mishneh, as Beit Yosef writes. The Maggid Mishneh further writes that this is Ramban's view, unlike Raavad's gloss there. He also writes in the name of Rashba that whenever the borrower has property, the creditor may not first collect from the guarantor, because the guarantor's words are mere encouragement and do not displace the borrower's primary liability.
