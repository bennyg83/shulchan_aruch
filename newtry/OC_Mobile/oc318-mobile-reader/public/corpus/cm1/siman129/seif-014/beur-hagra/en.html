The Gemara's phrase, 'When does this apply, where he has no property,' refers to the opening case where the creditor did not stipulate that he could collect first from the guarantor. He must first demand payment from the debtor, but afterward may collect from the guarantor if the debtor has no assets. If assets exist, he must collect from them. But if he expressly stipulated direct recourse, the condition governs and he may always collect from the guarantor first. See citation 40.
