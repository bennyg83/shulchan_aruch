[Shulchan Aruch] Perhaps he repaid him. Note: If the lender already litigated with the borrower and guarantor, and the borrower admitted that he had not repaid and had no means to pay, and afterward the lender sued the guarantor, who argued, "Perhaps the borrower subsequently repaid you," the guarantor is obligated to pay. Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 42.
