He must first pursue, etc. Raavad interprets the deficient text in Bava Batra to mean that the creditor must first demand payment from the debtor and require him to swear that he has no property. If the creditor stipulated otherwise, and no known assets exist, he may collect from the guarantor. But where known assets exist, even such a stipulation does not permit direct collection from the guarantor. See citation 40.
And he must include, etc. Even where an independent oath would not be required, this issue is included through oath-roll, as the Jerusalem Talmud teaches. See siman 94:1 and citation 94.
