[Shach, letter 32] Money from the guarantor. Note: Maharikash, siman 176, writes: Reuven and Shimon were partners who jointly bought silver from Levi. After some days Reuven claimed that he had paid for the silver, Shimon did not know whether payment had been made, and Levi denied Reuven's claim and demanded the price of the silver from Shimon, or at least the price of Shimon's half share. Reuven must swear that he paid the price of the silver, and this exempts Shimon, because Levi sold the silver on the understanding that whichever of them was found in their shop would pay him. This is the substance of his words.
