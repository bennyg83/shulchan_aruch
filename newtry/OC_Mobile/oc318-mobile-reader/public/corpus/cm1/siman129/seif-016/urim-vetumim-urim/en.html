There is an opinion, etc. It appears to me that the Mechaber writes 'there is an opinion' because the kablan's claim is uncertain while the borrower admits that he did not pay. But if the borrower too claims that he paid, it is obvious that the kablan is exempt. See Responsa Rama, siman 89, cited by the Shach. See Tumim for what I wrote concerning this opinion from the Gemara in Bava Metzia and the strained resolution I proposed. The matter requires study.
