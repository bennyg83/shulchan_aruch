To stipulate. The Sema writes that in light of the Tur and Mechaber at the beginning of siman 130, where a borrower who claims that he already repaid owes the guarantor nothing, the present ruling must mean that because the kablan failed to stipulate otherwise, he caused his own loss. He must pay the creditor, but the borrower need not reimburse him, since as against the kablan the borrower is believed to say that he paid. The Shach cites Sefer HaTerumot, which states clearly that a kablan is never bound beyond the extent of the borrower's own obligation. If the borrower claims, “I paid,” the borrower swears and the kablan is discharged. Responsa Ra'anach, beginning of siman 21, agrees. See also siman 83 seif 3. Baal HaItur cites differing views and does not decide. Therefore, the Shach concludes, money should not be extracted from the kablan. Bach ruled otherwise, but had he seen all these authorities he would not have written so.
