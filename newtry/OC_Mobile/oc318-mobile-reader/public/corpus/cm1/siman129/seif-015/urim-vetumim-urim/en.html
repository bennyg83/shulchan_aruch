He wishes. Meaning, even if the borrower has only inferior land while the kablan has intermediate land, the kablan can direct the creditor to the borrower's inferior land. This is explicit in a responsum of the Rashba. See Sema.
For the kablan should have stipulated. The Sema writes that according to the Tur and Mechaber at the beginning of siman 130, where a borrower claims that he already paid, the guarantor has no claim against the borrower. Therefore the present law means that because the kablan failed to stipulate, he caused his own loss and must pay the creditor, while the borrower need not reimburse him because as against the kablan the borrower is believed to say that he paid. The Shach, seif katan 32, disagrees with this ruling and cites several major authorities who reject the Rama. He also disagrees with the Bach and writes that had the Bach seen all the authorities he would not have written as he did. See Tumim, where I agreed with the Shach that the Rama's ruling cannot be sustained as law.
