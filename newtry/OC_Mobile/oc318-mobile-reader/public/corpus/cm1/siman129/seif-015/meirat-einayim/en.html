But only if the borrower wishes to repay him. Meaning, then even if the borrower has inferior-quality land and wishes to pay the creditor from it, while the creditor wishes to collect from intermediate-quality land, the absolute guarantor can direct him to the borrower's inferior-quality land. So it is stated explicitly in Rashba's responsa, at the end of siman 892; see there and Hagahot Darkei Moshe there.
For the absolute guarantor should have stipulated. According to what the Tur and Mechaber write at the beginning of siman 130, that where the borrower claims he already paid, the guarantor owes nothing, we must explain similarly here: the absolute guarantor should have stipulated, and because he failed to do so he caused himself the loss and must pay the creditor, since the borrower is not believed to say that he paid the creditor. Yet the borrower need not reimburse the guarantor, because against him the borrower is believed to say that he already paid, as explained in siman 130.
