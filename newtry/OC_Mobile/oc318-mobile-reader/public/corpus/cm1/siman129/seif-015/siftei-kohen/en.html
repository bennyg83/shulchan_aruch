The borrower is not believed to say, etc. The Maggid Mishneh writes that even from a kablan one may collect only upon clear proof, unless the kablan personally received and transferred the money. Responsa R. Eliyahu ibn Chaim, beginning of siman 21, rules likewise. The Baal HaTerumot, gate 35, writes explicitly that a kablan is never bound beyond the borrower’s own obligation. If the borrower claims that he paid, the borrower takes an oath and the kablan is exempt. He also cites a responsum of the Raavad to this effect. What I wrote above, siman 83, seif 3, also implies this, as do the Tur and Mechaber below at the beginning of siman 130. The Baal HaIttur presents both views and does not decide. Therefore, in my humble opinion, money may not be extracted from the kablan. I later saw that the Bach rules against the Maggid Mishneh, but had he seen all these authorities he would not have written so.
