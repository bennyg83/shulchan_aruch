The language of Tur, from the Mishnah at the end of Bava Batra 175b. According to Ramban and Rashba, it discusses a case without a kinyan. According to Ramah, Tur, and Rambam, it discusses a case with a kinyan, as explained above in seif 4. Therefore the Mechaber stated the law generally and did not copy Tur's wording that expressly mentions a kinyan.
Statements of Rav and Rabbi Yochanan, as explained by the Gemara there, 176a.
According to Rambam, even in this case a kinyan is required, as explained above in seif 5.
