A broker sold Reuven's garment to Levi on a specified condition and required Levi to provide a guarantor to ensure performance, otherwise the garment would be returned. Levi wore the garment before providing the guarantor, and afterward Shimon became guarantor for Levi. This is treated as a guarantor who undertook the obligation at the time the money was given, because Levi did not acquire the garment, despite wearing it, until Shimon became his guarantor to Reuven. This is from a responsum at the end of Chazeh HaTenufah, section 1, cited by Sema.
