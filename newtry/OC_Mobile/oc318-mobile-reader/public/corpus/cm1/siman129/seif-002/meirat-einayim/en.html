We do not need a nest. In G-d, I will tell you what I have written about my son’s words:
Because he is solvent, etc., and this was false. Meaning that the person was not solvent when the assurance was made. If he was solvent then and only later suffered financial reverses, the person who gave the assurance is exempt, because the statement, He is solvent, is not itself language of guarantee. Mahari Weil states this explicitly. See also siman 306 and Shiltei HaGibborim to Bava Kamma.
