I found written in the margin of a handwritten get procedure. It was the procedure of Mahari Margoliyot in the handwriting of my maternal grandfather, the eminent Rabbi Zenvil. He writes: An incident occurred with a man who signed his name Pinchas without a yod. They likewise wrote it in the get without a yod, by agreement of all the scholars of the city. They did not distinguish between this case and the names Eliyah or Yonatan mentioned in this siman, although one could distinguish. The Shulchan Aruch explicitly says to follow the signature specifically in cases like Eliyah and Yonatan, whereas regarding David and Binyamin it does not say so. Nevertheless, the plain implication of Terumat HaDeshen, siman 233, is that we follow the person's regular signature in all such cases. Maharam Yaffe likewise implies this. See there.
