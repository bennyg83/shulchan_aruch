For he is reliable. See Shach, seif katan 7. It appears that the liability here is not based on guaranty but on dina d'garmi. A practical difference arises according to the opinion that dina d'garmi is treated as a penalty, so that it is not imposed upon the wrongdoer's children after his death, as explained in siman 396. Shach's statement that where he is an expert, such as a partner, he is exempt, follows the rule of one who shows a coin to an expert money changer. An expert is exempt because the error is considered unavoidable. Likewise, one knowledgeable in the matter is comparable to an expert and is exempt because the mistake is an ones. In Shach, seif katan 5, there is a scribal error. It should read: 'One who did not know this is considered under ones and is exempt.'
