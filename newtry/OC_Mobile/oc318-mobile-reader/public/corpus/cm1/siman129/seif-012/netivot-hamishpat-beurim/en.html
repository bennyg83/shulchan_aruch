The borrower takes a heset oath. See Shach, seif katan 30. Tumim writes that regarding a kablan all agree that he is like an agent appointed in the presence of witnesses. This is correct. Sema explains that the creditor can tell the ordinary guarantor, 'You are not my litigant that I should be required to answer your claim.' That rationale does not apply to a kablan, and the distinction is clear.
