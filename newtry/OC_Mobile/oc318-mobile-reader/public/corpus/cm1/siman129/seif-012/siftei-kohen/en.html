["He takes a heset oath and is exempt through a migo, etc. Teshuvot Maimoniyot gives another reason, that this is no worse than an agent appointed before witnesses. The Ir Shushan challenged the Rama, but the Sema answers that these are not the Rama's own words; he merely copied Teshuvot Maimoniyot, as recorded in Darkei Moshe. I say that agency appointed before witnesses has no relevance here, and the Ir Shushan's challenge is sound even against Teshuvot Maimoniyot itself. Maharshal also disputes Teshuvot Maimoniyot and rules that the borrower is not released merely because he paid the guarantor. This is the primary law. See Migdal David."]
