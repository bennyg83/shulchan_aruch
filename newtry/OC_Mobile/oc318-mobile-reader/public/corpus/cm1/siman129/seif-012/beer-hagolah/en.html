Rambam there, chapter 27, law 5. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this law is not explicit in the Gemara, but it is obvious. Even from the borrower's own property one cannot collect an oral loan from heirs without proof, all the more so from a guarantor. He adds that even from a kablan one cannot collect without proof, because whenever there is no document we must be concerned that payment was already made, since the borrower himself would have been believed by oath without producing a receipt.
