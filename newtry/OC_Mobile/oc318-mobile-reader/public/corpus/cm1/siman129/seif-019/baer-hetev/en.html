Forgave. The Sema writes that whenever it is not sufficiently established that the borrower has no assets, Rabbi Natan's lien has not yet attached to the person obligated to the borrower, and the kablan may therefore forgive that person's debt, as explained in siman 86:2 and 86:5. The Shach rejects this. Nimukei Yosef follows the first resolution of Tosafot in the second chapter of Ketubot. Likewise, the Beit Yosef cites Rashba as uncertain whether a guarantor's forgiveness is effective because it harms another, Rashba adding that his teachers did not agree with him on this matter. We rule according to Tosafot's latter resolution, as stated and proved at length in siman 86. Therefore the kablan cannot forgive the debt, contrary to the Sema's reference to siman 86, where no such rule is established. Shach.
To the guarantor. This refers to a guarantor who received and delivered the money himself, so the creditor has no claim against the borrower. Sema.
