Unless the kablan released the borrower, etc. This is incorrect. Nimukei Yosef follows Tosafot’s first answer in the second chapter of Ketubot. The Beit Yosef’s citation of Rabbenu Yerucham in the name of the Rashba, that it requires study whether a guarantor who released the borrower has acted effectively, follows the Rashba’s own view, cited by the Beit Yosef above in siman 66, that there is room to consider whether a release is effective when it harms others, although his teachers disagreed with him. We rule in accordance with Tosafot’s second answer, as explained above in siman 86. Consequently, a kablan cannot release the borrower, as I demonstrated at length above, siman 86, seifim katanim 11 and 12. This is unlike Sema, seif katan 42, who refers to what was written above in siman 86:2, although this point was not established there. His statement is incorrect, as I explained there.
