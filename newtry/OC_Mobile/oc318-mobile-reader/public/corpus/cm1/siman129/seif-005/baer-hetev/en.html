Ketubah. An ordinary guarantor for a ketubah does not become obligated because he assumes he is merely performing a mitzvah and has caused the husband no monetary loss, since it is preferable for a person to live as a couple. A kablan, however, is liable even without a kinyan. With a guarantor for a dowry there is likewise the rationale that he is merely performing a mitzvah, and the matter is disputed in Even HaEzer. Nevertheless, all agree that a guarantor for a dowry may retract before the marriage even if a kinyan was made, because no act was yet performed in reliance on him. This follows all the more so from the Tur at the beginning of siman 131. The Rama states this plainly in Even HaEzer 102:6. Sema. See Responsa Chacham Tzvi 51.
