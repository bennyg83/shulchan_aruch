Just as an ordinary guarantor becomes obligated only through a kinyan, at the time the money is given, or before beit din, an unconditional guarantor becomes obligated only through one of these methods. There is no distinction between them except regarding the obligation secured by a ketubah, or a dowry promised to a bridegroom, as explained in the Tur, Even HaEzer, siman 102, seif 6. Whenever a kinyan is required and none was made, collection cannot be made even from unencumbered property, although he gave the lender a document. {Rama: This applies even if he gave his hand as a pledge in a place where that is not customarily used as a kinyan.
