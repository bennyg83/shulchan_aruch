Regarding the lien of a ketubah. An ordinary guarantor for a ketubah does not become obligated, because he assumes he is merely performing a mitzvah and the woman suffers no financial loss by marrying, for it is better to live together than alone. A kablan guarantor, however, becomes liable even without a kinyan. For a guarantor of dowry there is likewise the rationale that he assumes he is performing a mitzvah, but the 'better to live together' consideration does not apply. Therefore there is a real dispute in Even HaEzer, and Mechaber omits the matter here. Everyone agrees, however, that a guarantor for dowry may retract before the marriage, even if a kinyan was made, because no action has yet been taken in reliance on him. This follows all the more from Tur and Mechaber at the beginning of siman 131. Rama likewise says this plainly in Even HaEzer 102:6. Our customary guaranties are generally for contractual penalties, and there is no right of retraction because of the embarrassment that would otherwise result; they are not guaranties for the dowry itself. Sema.
