Tur, siman 130, in the name of Rabbenu Yonah. Ran likewise writes this regarding the incident of that guarantor and that document, Bava Batra 32b.
Although the promissory note is in the guarantor's possession, the borrower did not obligate himself to the guarantor through that document. Rashba likewise writes this in the name of his teacher Rabbenu Yonah, and Ba'al HaTerumot writes it in Gate 35.
