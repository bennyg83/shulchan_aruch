But if he said to him, ‘Lend him,’ etc. See Responsa Rama, siman 131; Responsa Mabit, part 1, siman 102, and part 2, siman 118.
Some say that the words ‘Give him, and I am a kablan,’ etc. See Responsa R. Eliyahu ibn Chaim, siman 60.
This also appears correct to me. Mordechai at the end of Get Pashut writes that the same applies in a foreign language: whenever the guarantor uses neither wording of a loan nor wording of repayment, he is a kablan. The Sema also cites this. This is the primary interpretation of the Gemara and is also the view of the Semag and Hagahot Maimoniyot.
A document in which it is written, etc. Maharashdam, siman 9, rules in accordance with the Baal HaTerumot and the Baal HaIttur that he is only an ordinary guarantor. See there.
