There is one who says, etc. See Tosafot in Gittin. Rashba explains Rashi differently, because the special language 'give him and I will give you' is required, and that formulation does not fit every case. Nimukei Yosef, Maggid Mishneh, Rambam, and Shulchan Aruch in Even HaEzer disagree. According to Rashi, the rule in Kiddushin that one who says, 'Give a maneh to so-and-so' is bound under the law of guarantorship must be understood in this limited manner. See siman 190:3 and citations 102 and 190.
