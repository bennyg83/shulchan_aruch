And he is exempt by virtue of a migo. See Baer Heitev, who cites the Shach as saying that the objection is correct, and notes that Maharshal also disagrees. See Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 16, who discusses the matter and concludes that in his view it is difficult to extract money from a party in possession. This applies, however, only if the guarantor accepted responsibility for unavoidable accidents to the same extent as the borrower. In that case, since the guarantor is liable, the borrower can indeed become exempt, as where property is delivered to a person with whom one regularly deposits it. But if the guarantor is only an unpaid or paid custodian and the property was lost through unavoidable circumstances while in his possession, so that the guarantor is exempt, how can the borrower become exempt?
