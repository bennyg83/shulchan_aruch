Other. A. For the other, Hashem’s promise is to give up all the things that He has commanded to do with him and that He has commanded him to do what He has sold with him. A:
Kablan. The same applies to an ordinary guarantor according to the opinion in seif 19. The Mechaber's reference to purchasers is not precise, for the creditor cannot even collect from the borrower's unencumbered property after the kablan took and gave the money himself. There is no practical difference regarding unencumbered property, because after the creditor collects from the kablan, the kablan can in turn collect from the borrower. This differs from property sold to purchasers, against whom the kablan cannot proceed unless the creditor transfers the debt document to him through the formal mode of transferring documents, as explained below and in siman 130.
