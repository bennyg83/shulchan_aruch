Tur, seif 24, in the name of Baal HaTerumot. Nimukei Yosef likewise writes at the end of Bava Batra in the name of the commentators.
He explains there that since the creditor has authority to collect either from the borrower or from the arev kablan, from whichever he chooses, the lien has attached both to the borrower's property that he sold and to the kablan's property. Therefore the creditor may collect from whichever he wishes.
This is explained in what was written above in seif 19.
