Vilna. An incident occurred here and they wrote the city's name with two vavs. The reason is that wherever the sound is not read like the letter peh, we write two vavs. Wherever the sound sometimes tends toward peh, such as in Feivush or Frida, we write a single vav. I also found these two preceding rulings written in the handwriting of my grandfather and teacher in the margin of the same get procedure.
