A guarantor whose undertaking appears after the witnesses' signatures on the document, meaning that after the witnesses signed he wrote, "I am guarantor," and his handwriting is recognizable, or witnesses testify to it, according to some becomes obligated without a kinyan, though collection from him is limited to unencumbered property. Others say that he is not obligated without a kinyan. According to the Rambam, even a guarantor whose undertaking appears before the witnesses' signatures requires a kinyan whenever he became guarantor only after the loan money had already been given.
