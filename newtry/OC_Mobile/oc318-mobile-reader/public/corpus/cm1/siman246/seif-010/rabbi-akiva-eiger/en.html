[Rama] That he gave it to him as a gift. Note: Rambam, at the end of chapter 7 of Hilchos Zechiyah, writes: One who sends a gift to his fellow, or gives him money when he is in financial distress, and the recipient refuses to take it, while the giver swears that he will not allow him to refuse and presses him until he accepts it, and similarly in all comparable cases, even if he did not explicitly state so, these are gifts. He cannot later retract and demand repayment unless he explicitly stated that it was a loan. End quote.
