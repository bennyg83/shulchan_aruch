To his son it is likewise considered a gift. See Sema, seif katan 17, who explains that the reason is that otherwise he should have written, "all my property to my son." I find this difficult. Why was this rule not stated regarding gifts of a shechiv mera in siman 250, seif 13, where part is given to the first person and the remainder to the second, and the first acquires? See Sema and Shach there, who explain that this is so even without an interruption, because the expression "the remainder" itself proves the intent. According to this reasoning, if one says, "half to so and so and half to so and so," we should similarly infer that he intended the first transfer to be a gift of a healthy person, since he did not say, "all my property to so and so and so and so." Likewise, above in siman 111, seif 5 in the Rama, two views are cited concerning this matter. It will be explained there, with Hashem's help.
