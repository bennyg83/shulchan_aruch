Vav) There, halachah 2. This is straightforward from the Gemara on Bava Batra 131b concerning the statement of Rabbi Yochanan in the name of Shmuel.
Zayin) An unresolved question at the beginning of 132a. The Maggid Mishneh cites Rav Yosef ibn Migash that we therefore rule leniently and treat the person only as an administrator.
Chet) A statement of Rav Chanilai bar Idi in the name of Shmuel, 131a.
Tet) The Tur, seif 7, in the name of Rabbenu Yonah. The Beit Yosef writes that his reasoning is sound.
Yod) This is the language of the Rambam there and the Gemara on 150b, from Rava in the name of Rav Nachman regarding five cases where a person must write all his property, etc.
Kaf) The Tur there in the name of Rabbenu Yonah. The Nimukei Yosef and Rosh likewise write this, and the Maggid Mishneh cites Rav Yosef ibn Migash.
Lamed) Beit Yosef there in the name of the Rashba.
Mem) The Rashba likewise writes in the name of a responsum of the Raavad and agrees with it. He states that no kinyan is made for an administrator, and that this is a halachah without stated reason that should not be extended beyond its precise case. The Maggid Mishneh likewise cites this as a "some say" opinion in halachah 3. See seif 7, where a dispute on this point is recorded.
Nun) The Tur in Even HaEzer, siman 107, likewise writes this in the name of a responsum of the Rosh.
