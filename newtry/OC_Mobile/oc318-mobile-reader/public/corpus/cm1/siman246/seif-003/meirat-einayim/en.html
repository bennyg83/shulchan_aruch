The same applies to a healthy person who wrote away all his property. The Beit Yosef writes that even the alternative opinion in the preceding seif, which says the rule there applies specifically to a dangerously ill person, agrees here because this healthy person needed to flee and therefore resembles a dangerously ill person. In the Perishah I wrote that this is not compelled. On the contrary, the words of the Ramah cited by the Tur imply that one who disagrees regarding a healthy person who heard that his son died also disagrees in this fleeing case. See there.
