“An open gift.” To the knees of Tamir, and to the extent of the column and the author of the USSR:
Any amount of his property. Here "any amount" literally means even a very small reservation according to all opinions. This differs from a dangerously ill person who gives away all his property. If he recovers he may retract, while if he retained something he may not, and the authorities dispute how much must be retained, as explained in siman 250. Some require enough for his livelihood. Although the Gemara discusses the laws together, the present case is different, as explained there.
