Alef) This is the language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 6 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah U'Matanah, from the baraita in Bava Batra 146b in the name of Rabbi Shimon ben Menasya. The Rif and Rosh read that Rav Nachman said the halachah follows Rabbi Shimon ben Menasya. This is explicit in Bava Batra 132a. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes that the halachah is established this way in the chapter Mi SheMet.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that the baraita explicitly says "all his property." One should not object from Rav Nachman's statement on 150b listing five cases where all property must be written, where this case is not listed, because he already included a deathly ill person. The Mishnah there is established according to Rabbi Shimon ben Menasya, 146b, showing that their laws are equivalent.
