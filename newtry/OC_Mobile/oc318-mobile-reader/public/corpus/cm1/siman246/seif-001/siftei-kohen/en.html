We always assess the donor's intent, etc. If a person wrote all his property to another to take effect after his death because he had no offspring, and he later had a child, see Responsa Maharam of Lublin, siman 108; Responsa Rabbi Aharon Sasson, siman 124; Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 253 and 299; and Responsa Rabbi Eliyahu ibn Chaim, siman 38.
