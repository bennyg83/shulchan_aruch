[Shulchan Aruch] And he heard that he had died. Note: The language of the Gemara is, "and he arose and wrote." Perhaps this means that at the very moment when he heard that his son had died, he arose and wrote, meaning that he acted directly as a result of that report. This constitutes a compelling presumption of intent, similar to what Tosafot write in Ketubot 109a, s.v. "she shall remain."
