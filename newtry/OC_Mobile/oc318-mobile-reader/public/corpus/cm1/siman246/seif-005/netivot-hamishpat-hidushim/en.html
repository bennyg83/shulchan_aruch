One who writes all his property. According to Rif and Rambam, this rule applies specifically where he writes the gift, because one can say he intended only to honor the recipient by causing a public record to be made in his name. But where a dying person merely states the gift orally, it is a complete gift. This resolves apparent contradictions in the Gemara. The Rosh and Tur do not distinguish between writing and oral statement. Rather, they distinguish between language of gift and language of inheritance. This is also the view of the Mechaber and Rama.
