The details of this law are explained in the Tur, Even HaEzer siman 107. The Mechaber writes there that even if a kinyan was made, whether the husband was healthy or dangerously ill, if he gave all his property to his wife he made her only administrator over his heirs. This applies whether the heirs are sons from her, sons from another woman, brothers, or other heirs. "Other heirs" includes nephews and more distant heirs. Nimukei Yosef writes that there is no distinction among types of heirs. This differs from a responsum of the Rosh, cited by the Tur there, that if the only heirs are nephews we do not say that he intended merely to appoint his wife as administrator. See the Sema there.
