Siman 107. There the Mechaber writes that even if a formal kinyan was made from a healthy person or from a dangerously ill person transferring all his property to his wife, he is presumed only to have appointed her administrator over his heirs. This applies whether the heirs are sons from her, sons from another wife, his brothers, or other heirs. By "other heirs" he means, for example, nephews or more remote descendants. The Nimukei Yosef in the chapter Yesh Nochalin likewise writes that there is no distinction between one class of heir and another, contrary to the responsum of the Rosh cited by the Tur in Even HaEzer there, which holds that where the only heirs are nephews we do not presume that he merely appointed his wife as administrator. Sema.
