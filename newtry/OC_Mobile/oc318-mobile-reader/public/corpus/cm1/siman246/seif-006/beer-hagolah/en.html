The Maggid Mishneh writes that this distinction is established by the Rif from the force of the sugyot according to the approach of the Geonim. They asked the reason for the distinction and answered that it is simply an accepted halachah without stated reason, as in Mi SheMet, Bava Batra 144a, and one may say only what the Sages themselves said.
