When does this apply, etc. This is Rif's view in resolving Rashbam's difficulty cited above.
And some authorities say, etc. We rule like Ravina there. This is certainly so regarding a daughter among daughters, where all agree, since the Gemara's question concerned only a daughter among sons. Rif, however, does not read there 'a daughter among the sons.' In Rav Avira's statement he reads only 'a wife among the sons, and a wife, etc.' This explains the Gemara's statement on 132b, 'and Rav Avira establishes all of them, etc.,' meaning even a wife among the brothers, unlike our version. See Rashbam there.
