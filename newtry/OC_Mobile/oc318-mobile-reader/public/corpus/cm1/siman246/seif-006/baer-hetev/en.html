To a daughter. The Sema writes that this applies specifically where he wrote or gave the property to her using the language of gift. If he used the language of inheritance, no authority holds that a daughter acquires among sons, since she is not an heir where sons exist. This is explicit in the Mishnah and Gemara, and the Tur and Shulchan Aruch state it at the beginning of siman 281. See Responsa Rashach, part III, simanim 12 and 13.
