For a healthy person. The Sema writes that the Ran was uncertain about a healthy person who heard that his son had died and then gave his property to others from now until after death, whether this is considered a case in which he left nothing for himself, since he cannot sell the property during his lifetime. The Beit Yosef likewise cites this in the Ran's name. In truth, however, the Ran expresses no uncertainty. He states categorically that since the father did not leave himself without support, he likewise did not disinherit his son, and therefore the son acquires. See Responsa Maharam of Lublin on this matter. Shach.
