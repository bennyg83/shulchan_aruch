And an earlier deed emerged against him, etc. Meaning a deed earlier than this gift, even though it is later than the date of the wife's ketubah. The Tur writes simply that in such a case she loses the ketubah. In Even HaEzer he qualifies the rule to where the husband wrote the property to an engaged or divorced wife, where it cannot be interpreted as appointing her administrator. The same applies where he used one of the other formulations mentioned there that cannot reasonably be interpreted as an appointment to administration.
