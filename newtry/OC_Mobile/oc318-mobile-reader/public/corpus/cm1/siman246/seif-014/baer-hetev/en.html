Earlier. Meaning a debt document that predates this gift, even though it postdates the ketubah. The Tur writes without qualification that in such a case she loses her ketubah. In Even HaEzer, however, he explains that this applies where the woman is his betrothed or divorced wife, because in those cases we cannot say that his intent was merely to appoint her administrator. The same applies where he wrote the transfer in any of the other formulations that legally effect an outright grant, for those too cannot be interpreted as an appointment to administration. Sema.
