See Maharshal, chapter 8 of Bava Kamma, siman 68, and Terumat HaDeshen in the name of Mahari Chaviv, who brings a proof from the case of "tear my garment," where the owner did not say "on condition that you be exempt." This proof itself requires study, because in that case, if the garment did not come into the person's hands under a duty of safekeeping, he is exempt even though the owner did not expressly say "on condition that you be exempt," as stated in the Gemara and authorities at the end of siman 301.
