Rambam there, chapter 8, law 13. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is straightforward so long as the gift was not made in error and therefore void from the outset. A proof is one who expressly gives on condition that it be returned, where the recipient consumes the fruits during the term of the gift. Certainly an unspecified gift is no stronger than an express conditional gift.
A.R. and he who does not enter as a way that puts the guest in:
He said, “And there was more than usual in his life, and he was given to his sister, who had been sent to him for years, and more than that of his son, and he could say to his brother, I would like to give you and your father, if the other brother is better than the first, and his son, his wife:
He brought evidence of the people who went to the land of the sea and went to one another, and he gave his wife his hands on the horn of turtles and turned out in the E.C. and wrote the rebelliousness of his father's name to another:
He explains there that since the property originally came into his hands through waiver, the donor can no longer retract.
