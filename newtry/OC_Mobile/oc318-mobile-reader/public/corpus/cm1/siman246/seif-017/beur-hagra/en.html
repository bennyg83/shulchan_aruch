All who give, etc. See Beer HaGolah; Rambam also states this explicitly there.
One who says, etc., but not, etc. As stated in Kesubos 107b regarding one who went overseas, etc., and there 109a: "two matters, etc." In the Yerushalmi Rabbi Chaggai says, "I did not say that another may collect, but a father may collect"; Rabbi Yose says, "whether a father or another, he may not collect." Since no halachic ruling was stated, money is not extracted because of doubt. This is the Mordechai and Hagahos Maimoniyos. This applies specifically to supporting another's wife, because one who pays his fellow's debt cannot recover. But if he provided for the person himself, that person is certainly liable. See the Ran to Nedarim 33b, s.v. "gezeirah." The Rashba writes, etc., but his words are not compelling, etc. The Maggid Mishneh likewise rejects his proof, yet writes that the Rashba's ruling remains correct where one supports an ordinary person, and his proofs for that rule stand. See Maggid Mishneh and Ran there.
