And he wrote to his wife any amount of land, etc. The Tur here states simply that she loses her ketubah. In Even HaEzer the Shulchan Aruch and Darkei Moshe explain that this means specifically the ketubah obligation and not property she brought into the marriage. The reason is that when she hears her husband divide all his property among his sons and give her only a small parcel of land, yet she does not protest, "From where will I collect my ketubah?" we infer that she waived the ketubah because he honored her by giving her some share among his sons. Nevertheless, property the husband later acquires, or a share returning because one of the sons dies, may still be used to satisfy her ketubah, as explained there.
