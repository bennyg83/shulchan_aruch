The language of the Tur, from Reish Lakish's statement that roaming livestock have no chazakah, in Chezkat HaBatim, Bava Batra 36a.
The Mechaber omitted what Tur wrote, that possession is effective if held for three years, based on Rava's statement there and Rashbam's explanation that it also applies to roaming livestock, as Rama writes in the gloss. Rambam, however, in chapter 10 of Hilkhot To'en, law 4, holds that Rava's statement concerns slaves only and not roaming livestock. Maggid Mishneh writes that the Mishnah supports Rambam because it mentions chazakah regarding slaves but not animals. Baal HaIttur likewise appears to follow Rambam.
