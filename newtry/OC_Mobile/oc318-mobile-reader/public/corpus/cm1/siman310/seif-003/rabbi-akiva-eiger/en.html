[Shulchan Aruch] And if its value is insufficient. Note: Even if the carcass is worth a small amount equal to the increase in cost by which half the remaining work has become more expensive than the agreed rate, he may not take the carcass and add it to what he otherwise would have had to pay this owner as rental. His right is only to rent another animal using the carcass and still pay the original owner his rental, such as where the carcass is worth a substantial amount, but not to rent using the carcass without paying him rental. Certainly he may not add to this from the rental already earned for the distance traveled. See Tur and Derishah, and examine carefully.
