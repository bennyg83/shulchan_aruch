Reuven, etc. As Bava Metzia 77a states: where work was completed, the workers receive, etc. On 76b the Gemara distinguishes whether the employer inspected the field. Here it is as though he did not inspect it, since the rental was not for a specified destination. The latter clause, where it was rented for a particular place, resembles inspection and the case where a river intervened. See siman 334:1 and siman 335:1 regarding unavoidable circumstances. See 70.
