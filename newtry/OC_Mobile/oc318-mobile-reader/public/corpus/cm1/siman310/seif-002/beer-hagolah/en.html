The Raavad objects that there is no distinction between riding and carrying. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes that Rambam's position cannot be reconciled with the versions of the Gemara that read, 'one who rented a donkey and it died halfway, and there is no donkey to ride.' The Beit Yosef writes that our manuscripts read, 'one who rented a donkey to ride,' which is the version of the Rif and Rambam, and that manuscripts omitting 'to ride' pose no difficulty. He therefore questions the Maggid Mishneh. In my view the Maggid Mishneh's difficulty still requires study: if there truly is a distinction between riding and carrying, when the Gemara asks how the case is defined, whether he said 'this donkey,' etc., it could have established the case as one hired for carrying.
Tosafot likewise write this in their second answer at the beginning of 79a, as cited by the Beit Yosef.
