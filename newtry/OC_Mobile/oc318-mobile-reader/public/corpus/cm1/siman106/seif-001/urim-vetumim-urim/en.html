We send and notify him. Perhaps he has a receipt or other proof that he already paid.
And he collects it in addition to his debt. Since this expense was incurred for the debtor's benefit, because by strict law the creditor could collect from him in his absence, the debtor must reimburse it. But expenses of summons and similar matters are not the debtor's responsibility. See Shach.
We collect his debt for him. See Tumim for why this does not contradict the Mechaber below in siman 164 concerning a collapsed house whose owner is overseas.
His debt immediately. If the loan itself was made to the debtor while he was overseas, and the creditor later comes to this country and asks beit din to descend to the debtor's property, see Tumim. I wrote that it appears beit din does not collect from his property in that case.
After he swears. He takes the Mishnah oath while holding a sacred object.
Whether from movable property. We do not fear that the debtor may later come and prove payment, while in the meantime the creditor might conceal the movable property, as we feared above in siman 98. Here the debtor is not claiming payment, and we therefore maintain the note in its presumption of nonpayment. There is no basis for an independent concern that it was paid.
And if the note contains a credibility clause. See what I wrote above in siman 71 at length.
To swear. If the debt is still within its term, the creditor need not swear. If the due date has passed, however, he must swear even if the debtor left the country while the loan was still within its term, because the debtor may have sent payment with an agent who left during the term and instructed him to pay after the due date.
But not from his heirs. This means that the debtor's own property is overseas, while heirs who inherited that property possess separate property of their own in the present country. Beit din does not collect from the heirs' separate property, because it was never encumbered to the creditor. Only the debtor's property, which remains overseas, was encumbered.
