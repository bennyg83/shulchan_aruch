In addition to his debt. The Sema explains that reimbursement is due only if the defendant was found liable. If the defendant was exonerated, the claimant does not recover the expense. Likewise, one who sends a summons requiring another to appear in beit din ordinarily cannot charge the other party for the messenger. This case is different because the expense serves the debtor's own benefit, since the creditor could otherwise collect in his absence. See Maharshal, Bach, and above siman 14, seif 5.
