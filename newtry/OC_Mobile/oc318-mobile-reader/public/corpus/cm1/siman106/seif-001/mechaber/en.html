The law of one who comes to collect while his debtor is absent. It contains 3 se'ifim.
If a lender seeks to collect with an authenticated promissory note while the borrower is absent, and beit din can send a messenger to notify the borrower so that he can appear for judgment, they send and notify him, provided the messenger can travel there and return within thirty days. The lender pays the messenger's fee and adds it to the debt he collects. If the borrower is in a place from which the messenger cannot return within thirty days, the debt is collected immediately after the lender takes an oath, whether from land or from movable property. We do not fear that the borrower possesses a receipt. If the document expressly grants the lender credibility even when he collects in the borrower's absence, or grants credibility regarding the borrower and his successors, the lender need not swear. {Rama: Some say collection in the debtor's absence is allowed only to the lender himself, not to his heirs, who must instead go after the debtor. See Rivash 353, above in siman 73, seif 10, and siman 14, seif 1.}
