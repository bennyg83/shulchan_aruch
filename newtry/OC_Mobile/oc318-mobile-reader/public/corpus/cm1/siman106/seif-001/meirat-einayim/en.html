They send and notify him. Perhaps the borrower has a receipt showing that he already paid the debt.
And he adds it to his debt. The borrower's cost of sending notice is added to the debt because the notice is for the borrower's protection. By contrast, expenses of obtaining enforcement and final transfer documents are borne by the creditor because they are for his benefit, unless the parties stipulated otherwise.
Added to his debt. This applies where the defendant is ultimately found liable. If the defendant is exonerated, the claimant who summoned him must pay the messenger's fee.
Where the messenger cannot go, etc. This excludes the view of Ramah cited by the Tur that 138 days are granted.
After the lender swears. This is the Mishnah's oath, taken while holding a sacred object, which beit din itself imposes on one who comes to collect with a document from a debtor's property in the debtor's absence.
Whether from movable property. We do not fear that the lender will hide the movables and leave the debtor unable to recover if he later appears with a receipt. In siman 98 such concern exists because the debtor had already said, "Give me time and I will bring evidence invalidating the document." Mere abstract possibility is not enough to delay collection beyond the ordinary period.
Against him and those who come in his place. Beit din, which advances claims on his behalf, is included among those who come in his place.
But not from his heirs. Rivash explains that this permission concerns the debtor's own property. If the debtor died and his heirs hold their own property, even if they are obligated to pay their father's debt because they inherited other assets from him, the creditor's lien is not on these independently owned assets and he may not collect from them in their absence. Rama's gloss must therefore be understood to permit collection in their absence only from property inherited from the father that itself remains subject to the creditor's lien.
