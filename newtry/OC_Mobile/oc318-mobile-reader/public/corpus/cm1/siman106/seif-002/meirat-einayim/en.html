The Tur, citing the Rosh, says that they were in the possession of that person until the very day the court descends upon his property, even though it is unknown how they came into his possession, whether by inheritance or purchase. In the Perishah I explained the practical difference between inheritance and purchase, as will be discussed in siman 112. According to the Rambam, if the property is inherited we presume that it was already in the debtor's possession before he borrowed, whereas where it is known to have come to him by purchase, that presumption does not apply.
