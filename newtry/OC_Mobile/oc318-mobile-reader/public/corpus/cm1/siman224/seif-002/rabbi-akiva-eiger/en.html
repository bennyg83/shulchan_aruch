[Rama] That he did not know of the defects. Note: Where one party should have known and the other could not have known, he may not collect without an oath. See above, siman 31, in the gloss. If he does not wish to swear, the other party is exempt, because the meshichah was performed in error. Mordechai there. See Responsa Tashbetz, part 4, Chut HaMeshulash, second section, siman 22.
