It is not a Rashi who first died of a cow’s attraction to the expropriation of the husband of the victim to the husband of the cow, but rather the “c” and did not attract the grave until he found a dead man and was not known if he had previously withdrawn the breach or so as the column. A:
The cow. The reasoning is that since the doubt arose (meaning, the death became known) only after meshichah, at a time when the cow was in the possession of the owner of the cow, that owner bears the burden of proof. If he cannot prove his claim, we preserve the prior presumption that the animal was alive as before. Why do we not say the same at the beginning of the preceding siman, where one exchanged a cow for a donkey and the cow gave birth, and it is unknown whether she gave birth before or after meshichah? There, one might say that at the time of meshichah the cow was in the possession of the donkey's owner and had the presumption of being pregnant; when doubt later arose because she was no longer pregnant, we should preserve her prior presumption of pregnancy. The distinction is that here he exchanged the donkey for a particular known cow, and through meshichah of the cow the other party acquired the donkey. Therefore the animal is considered to have been in his possession. In the earlier case, however, where the cow gave birth and they dispute the offspring, it was never established whether the offspring was included in the acquisition at all, for perhaps she would not produce a viable offspring, or perhaps she was not pregnant, so the acquisition could have taken effect without the offspring. Sema. Taz writes that the question does not arise at all, because Sema overlooked Tosafot in HaMadir, who distinguish between a doubt that arises in a favorable direction there and a doubt that arises to the detriment here. With due respect to Taz, he suspected the innocent. Sema did not overlook these Tosafot, for he cites them in Perishah. He merely writes that one can distinguish the cases even in the manner he proposed. See there.
