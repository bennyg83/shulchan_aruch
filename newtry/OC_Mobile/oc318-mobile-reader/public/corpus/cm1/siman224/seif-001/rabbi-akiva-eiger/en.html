[Shulchan Aruch] Upon the owner of the donkey. Note: If he makes a definite claim, he does not need proof even if the cow is not in his possession, because his definite claim together with the presumption concerning the donkey's body is sufficient to extract property. Kesef Mishneh in the name of the Ramban and Rashba. See also the Ran and Raah in chapter 7 of Ketubot, in the sugya concerning defects.
