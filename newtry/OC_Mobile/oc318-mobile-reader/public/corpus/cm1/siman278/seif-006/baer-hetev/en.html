An ordinary son cannot say, 'Give me a portion of the land in exchange for my share of the appreciation,' even if the land is large enough to be divided. This is evident from the Gemara in the chapters HaGozel Kamma and HaMekabel and from the poskim. Rashi explicitly explains this in Bava Kamma 95b, s.v. firstborn and ordinary son. This is also clearly the intent of the Rambam and the Mechaber and is explained even more clearly by the Levush, unlike the Sema, who writes that the second half of the appreciation is assessed in money and divided between them. Perhaps he means where the firstborn agrees to this, but the plain meaning of his words does not indicate so. See Responsa Mabit, volume 2, siman 284. Shach.
