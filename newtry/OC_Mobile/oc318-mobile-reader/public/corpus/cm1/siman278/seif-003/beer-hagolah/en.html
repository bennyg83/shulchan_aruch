Dalet) This is the language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 3 of Hilkhot Nachalot. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is explained in many places, principally the Mishnah in the eighth chapter of Bekhorot 52a: a firstborn does not take a double portion in property merely expected to come into the estate as he does in property already possessed, including property of the grandfather, as explained there on 52b.
Heh) A statement of Rav Yehudah in the name of Shmuel in the eighth chapter of Bava Batra 124b.
