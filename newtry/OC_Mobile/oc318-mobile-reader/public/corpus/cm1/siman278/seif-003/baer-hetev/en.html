From the one who bequeaths to him. Although at the end of siman 277 it was explained that even if the firstborn dies during his father's lifetime, the firstborn's sons stand in their father's place to receive two shares, that case is different because a son is regarded as an extension of his father, and it is therefore as though the firstborn were alive and inheriting property already present with his father. Here, however, the father of the firstborn died while the property of the person from whom he would inherit was not yet present in his possession at the time of his death, but remained in the possession of the firstborn's grandfather. The firstborn is not the firstborn of his grandfather, only of his father, and the property was not present in his father's possession. Sema.

Responsa Rashdam there writes that the reference is not to merchandise aboard the ship, but to the ship itself. Mishpetei Shmuel, siman 96.
