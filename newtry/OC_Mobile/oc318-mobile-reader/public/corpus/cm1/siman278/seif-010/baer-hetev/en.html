Nimukei Yosef there concludes: where the custom is that the king does not sell land for unpaid taxes, but instead leases the land for three or four years until the tax is paid, if the owner of the field dies during those years, the firstborn receives a double share. See Responsa Maharam Sasson, siman 149, who discusses this law; Responsa Divrei Rivot, siman 208; Responsa Rashach, volume 1, siman 22; and Maharashdam, siman 375, regarding what is considered property merely prospective with respect to a firstborn.
