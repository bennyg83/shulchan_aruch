Vav) There, halachah 2, from the baraita at the end of Bava Batra 123b. The Maggid Mishneh explains that the Gemara's initial establishment on 124a according to Rabbi Meir was before Rav Pappa's statement concerning a palm tree that grew thicker. Initially one might have thought that the Sages disagree even regarding appreciation that occurs automatically. After Rav Pappa teaches that even the Sages agree to automatic appreciation where the object itself did not change, the baraita concerning a cow can also follow the Sages. The calf is not considered a changed object even though initially it was an embryo, because an embryo is regarded as part of its mother and therefore the property is treated as not having fundamentally changed.
