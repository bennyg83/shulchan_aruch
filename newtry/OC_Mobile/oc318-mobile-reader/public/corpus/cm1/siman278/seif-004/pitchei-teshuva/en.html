And some disagree. Shaar Mishpat raises a doubt wherever the authorities dispute whether a particular asset is considered presently held property or merely prospective property for the firstborn's double share. Should beit din deny the firstborn any additional share because the case resembles one where we are uncertain whether a person is a firstborn at all, in which case he receives nothing from doubt? Or is that case different because there the uncertainty concerns whether he has firstborn heir status, whereas here he is certainly the firstborn and the only uncertainty concerns the classification of the asset, perhaps requiring division as in a general monetary doubt? He leaves the practical ruling requiring study.
