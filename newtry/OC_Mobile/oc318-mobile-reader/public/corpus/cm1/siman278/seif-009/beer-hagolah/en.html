Alef) A statement of Rav Huna in the name of Rav Asi at the beginning of Bava Batra 127a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's explanation is evident from his wording. Rav Yosef ibn Migash had already given this explanation, as did Rabbenu Chananel. Rashbam likewise cites it in his name, applying the statement to a firstborn who protested and thereby showed that he was not waiving his additional firstborn share.
Bet) As Rabbah says there, "it is reasonable," etc. Since in disputes between Rabbah and Rav Yosef the halachah follows Rabbah, this is the ruling. The Kesef Mishneh raises a difficulty with this in chapter 3 of Hilkhot Nachalot, halachah 6, and leaves it requiring study. This is not the place to elaborate.
