But if they pressed them and divided equally. This ruling is difficult. If the firstborn protested concerning grapes but then divided olives equally, one can understand that he waived a new category of property and therefore waived more broadly. But once he already agreed to divide the grapes equally, he cannot later retract from that waiver as to those grapes. After the grapes become wine, the wine was produced from grapes already belonging to the brothers equally. It therefore seems that the firstborn must divide the wine equally even if he now wishes to protest. This requires study.
