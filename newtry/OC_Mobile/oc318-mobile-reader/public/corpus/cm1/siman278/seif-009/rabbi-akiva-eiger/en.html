[Sema, letter 29] He cannot remove the son. Note: This is not conclusive. Rabbi Yosef HaLevi wrote that he must attend to collecting it by appraisal. It is possible that the Mechaber agrees with the Rashba and Nimukei Yosef that for this purpose the ordinary son inherits against his will and cannot say, "I do not wish to inherit, let my younger brothers take it," so as to prevent the creditor from collecting. Nevertheless, he can say, "Place the creditor into possession of the property and make an appraisal for him."
