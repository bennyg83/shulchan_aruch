Although she left him sons. See Rashbam, Bava Batra 128a, s.v. u'mesah bito, who writes that the case is, for example, where he had no sons from her, as taught in the chapter Zeh Borer. Rabbi Akiva Eiger, in his glosses to the Gemara there, questions this, because there Rabbi Yehuda says so, whereas the first Tanna holds that even if he has sons he is qualified, and this is how we rule. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot Beit Yosef, entry 18, and Birkei Yosef, entry 20, had already raised this difficulty. See also Tumim, seif katan 7, regarding what he writes on this matter.
