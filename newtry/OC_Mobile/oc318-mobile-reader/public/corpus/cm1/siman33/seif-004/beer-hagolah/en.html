What is written in Hirol, O Lord
The Rosh in his rulings there, from the Bavli's ruling like Rabbi Yose. The Rif cites the Yerushalmi as proof regarding second-degree relatives through marriage, but it may disagree with our Gemara. In Responsa, rule 58, the Rosh writes that the ruling rendered against the Rif is decisive and unanswerable, and that all the sages of Ashkenaz and France judge accordingly.
