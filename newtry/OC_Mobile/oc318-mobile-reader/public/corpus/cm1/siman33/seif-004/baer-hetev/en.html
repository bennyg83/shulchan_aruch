It is also evident there that the same applies to judges sitting together, and that if one is related to one litigant as a second-degree relative through two marriages, he should not serve initially. Rama writes similarly in Even HaEzer, siman 169, seif 1, concerning chalitzah. Accordingly, a witness who has such a relationship to a litigant should likewise not sign the document initially. Shach.
To rule. It appears to me that according to the Shach's position, that judges related in this way to one another or to a litigant should not serve initially, while testimony was treated more leniently, then certainly in the case here of a first-degree relative to a second-degree relative through two marriages they are initially disqualified from serving with one another. The reason testimony was treated more leniently than judges appears to be as the Sema explains at the beginning of this siman. See there.
