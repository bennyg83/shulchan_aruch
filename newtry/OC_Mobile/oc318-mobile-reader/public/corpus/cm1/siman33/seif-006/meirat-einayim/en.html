It appears to me that after the fact his judgment stands. See Darkei Moshe to siman 7, where Rama cites Maharik, shoresh 21, who validates such a judge, and concludes that after the fact even Baal HaItur agrees that the judgment stands, as Rama writes here. The reason is that this is not actual love in the full disqualifying sense. See above, siman 7, seif 7, regarding a friend and enemy. I heard some distinguish as follows: Rama's statement here that they may not judge applies only when they judge one another's case. They may nevertheless sit together with a third judge to adjudicate other people, even if they are very close friends. Only two judges who hate one another may not sit together to judge someone else, as the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 7, seif 8. Two friends are different, because affection between them is less likely to impair deliberation in another person's case.
