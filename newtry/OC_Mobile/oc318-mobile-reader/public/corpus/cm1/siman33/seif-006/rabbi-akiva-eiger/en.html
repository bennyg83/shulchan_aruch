[Shach, letter 9] Afterward to claim from him further on account of this. Note: So too the Raah explicitly proves in his novellae to Ketubot, s.v. "Rav Huna said in the name of Rav," in the name of "some say," that persons disqualified from testimony who testified concerning a matter in which they were disqualified, and afterward became qualified with respect to that same testimony, are not believed, because they are considered interested parties, since they already testified. See Responsa Maharit, part 2, Even HaEzer, siman 37, at its end; Responsa Avodat HaGershuni, siman 3; Responsa Noda BiYehuda, first edition, Even HaEzer, siman 27; and Responsa Maharibal, part 3, siman 96.
