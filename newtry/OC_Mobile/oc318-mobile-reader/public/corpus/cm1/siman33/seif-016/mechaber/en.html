Witnesses who are related to a guarantor are disqualified from testifying concerning the borrower. This applies whether the borrower seeks exemption by denying the loan and they testify that he borrowed, or he claims to have paid and they testify that he admitted he had not paid. {Rama: Relatives of a murder victim may testify against the murderer. Likewise, relatives of a person who was beaten may testify against the assailant so that he be expelled from the synagogue or receive a similar punishment, because the victim derives no monetary benefit from their testimony.}
