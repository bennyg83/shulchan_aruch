Relatives of the murder victim. Their testimony gives no financial benefit to the murderer, and the desire to take revenge against him is not considered a disqualifying interest. Therefore even the victim himself may testify, provided he is not a tereifah. But if he remains alive and testifies in order to recover loss of work and medical expenses, he has a financial interest and is disqualified.
