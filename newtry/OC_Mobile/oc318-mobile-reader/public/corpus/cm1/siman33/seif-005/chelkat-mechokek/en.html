We are concerned that perhaps he intended the intercourse for kiddushin. Above, siman 27, even with money kiddushin, where his intention was for kiddushin, giving in silence may be effective. We must therefore say that here the case is even where he himself says he did not intend kiddushin. He is not believed against the presumption that a person does not engage in promiscuous intercourse. See above, siman 27, what I wrote. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that inadvertent intercourse does not acquire a wife; Beit Yosef cites this at the end of this siman. See Yevamot 55 and 56 and Tosafot at the beginning of 56, passage beginning 'regarding the matters stated.'
