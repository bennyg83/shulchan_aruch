(Rama, seif 16) Relatives of the murdered person may testify, etc. Note: This requires study, for the Yerushalmi, Sanhedrin, chapter Zeh Borer, halacha 9, and tractate Shevuot state: Rabbi Yishmael taught from 'the congregation shall judge,' that the congregation must not be relatives of the assailant or of the victim. Rabbi Yose said, etc., and from this you find that beit din are the avengers of blood, teaching that judges may not be relatives of those being judged. From there it follows that witnesses likewise may not be relatives of those being judged: the Torah says to execute upon witnesses and to execute upon judges, so just as the judges may not be relatives of those being judged, neither may the witnesses. However, one can say that this applies only according to Rabbi Yose, whereas according to Rabbi Akiva, who derives the disqualification of relatives from the verse 'fathers shall not be put to death because of children,' we do not derive a disqualification for relatives of the murdered person.
