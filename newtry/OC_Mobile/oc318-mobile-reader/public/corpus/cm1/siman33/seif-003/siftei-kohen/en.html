Nevertheless, initially they should not sign together. It is evident from Terumat HaDeshen there that the same applies to serving together as judges, or to the relationship between a witness and one of the litigants. Initially, relatives at the degree of second with second, and two husbands whose wives are related in that degree, should not serve together regarding chalitzah. See there. The Rama likewise writes this in Even HaEzer, siman 169, seif 1, concerning chalitzah. Accordingly, a witness who bears that degree of relationship to a litigant should likewise not initially sign the document. Examine this carefully.
