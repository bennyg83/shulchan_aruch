Who are relatives of the judges. The concern is that those judges would not accept hazamah testimony against their relatives, making the original testimony one that cannot be subjected to hazamah. Darkei Moshe cites many authorities who hold that in monetary law testimony capable of hazamah is not required. Everyone agrees that if the witnesses answer "I do not know" to the formal interrogations they remain valid, because the requirement is not that every testimony actually be framed in a way permitting hazamah. The dispute concerns testimony which, even if the witnesses gave a fully precise date and were later contradicted by hazamah witnesses, the judges could not accept the hazamah because the witnesses are their relatives, producing distorted judgment. Urim VeTumim, unlike the Shach. A witness who is a tereifah is valid in monetary law. Shach. Regarding witnesses related to the judges, many authorities validate them, and there is room to rely upon that at least after the fact. Shach.
