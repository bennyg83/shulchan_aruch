The witnesses. The Ran writes at the end of the second chapter of Ketubot that although the Gemara's reason based on the law of conspiring witnesses is rejected in Sanhedrin 28a, the ruling itself remains correct. See there. It is likewise stated in the Tosefta and Jerusalem Talmud, cited by the Rif and Rosh in chapter 3 of Sanhedrin.
(Anthology) The witnesses, etc. Gemara there, 28a-b.
(Anthology) Or, etc. See Tosafot, Bava Kamma 92b, s.v. kegon, who disagree with this. This is also as stated in Sanhedrin 27a, that conspiring testimony from another matter disqualifies them, unlike the Jerusalem Talmud. See the Ran, chapter 2 of Ketubot.
