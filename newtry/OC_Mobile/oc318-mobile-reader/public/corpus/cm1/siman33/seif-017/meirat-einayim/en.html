Or witnesses who are relatives of the judges are disqualified. The reason is that we require testimony capable of being made the subject of hazamah, and these judges would not accept hazamah testimony against their own relatives. Darkei Moshe cites Rabbeinu Yerucham and Nimukei Yosef, who hold that in monetary law testimony capable of hazamah is not required. For that reason they validate witnesses related to the judges, and even a witness who is a tereifah. Rama abbreviated because many authorities disagree.
