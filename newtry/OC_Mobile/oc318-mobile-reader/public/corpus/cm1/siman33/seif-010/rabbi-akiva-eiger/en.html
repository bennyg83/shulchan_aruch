[Shach, letter 16] Note: In my humble opinion there is some proof that in monetary law we do not require testimony that could potentially be refuted by hazamah. This follows from the discussion in Bava Batra 155 concerning minors, and the statement there that "they must stand," and so forth. Why did the Gemara not answer simply that such testimony is incapable of hazamah, since a minor who causes damage is exempt? It must be that in monetary law we do not require testimony capable of hazamah. As a matter of Torah law, a minor is nevertheless disqualified independently, because the term "men" is not superfluous, since it is already needed to exclude women, as stated at the beginning of chapter 7 of Shevuot. Rather, the Gemara says that minors are thereby automatically excluded because they are not included in the term "men." This is clear.
