Not because they are presumed to love one another, etc. The Tur concludes: therefore, one who loves or hates a litigant is competent to testify, as stated at the beginning of this siman. This supplies another proof. If relatives were disqualified because they are presumed to love one another, then any friend should likewise be disqualified, whereas the halachah is that a friend is competent to testify.
