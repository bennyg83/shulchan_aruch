He knew testimony for her. It appears that a man who divorced his wife may testify for her only regarding testimony he observed before she married him. He may not testify about something he observed during the marriage, because both the beginning and end of the testimony must be in a state of qualification. This applies even according to the Raavad and those who follow him below, siman 34, seif 26. See what I write there in my critique of Responsa Rivash, siman 339.
