Passed a prohibition for which there are no lashes. This rabbinic disqualification does not include a prohibition whose lack of lashes stems from its greater severity, such as a prohibition serving as warning for a capital offense. Nor does it include a prohibition repaired by a positive commandment, since lashes can apply if the repair is actively nullified. It also does not include offenses involving monetary greed, where disqualification can arise even without lashes because he is a wicked person of dishonest gain. Conversely, where violation can occur only under an uncertain warning such that lashes are structurally unavailable, the person is not disqualified on that basis. Mere failure to perform a positive commandment likewise does not ordinarily disqualify a witness unless it reflects an active rebellious breach or wholesale rejection of obligation.
