And they testify that he admitted. Meaning, that he admitted he had not paid. Alternatively, the lender holds a note and the borrower brings witnesses that he paid, but those witnesses are relatives of the guarantor. Rabbeinu Yerucham records this explanation. The Beit Yosef says the Tur wrote in accordance with all the explanations, but that is incorrect. This third explanation is not mentioned by the Tur; it is from the Ramban's Milchamot and reverses the Raavad's explanation. It is a correct explanation.
