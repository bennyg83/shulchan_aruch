[Shulchan Aruch] When he was his son-in-law. If he signed a document before becoming his son-in-law and afterward became his son-in-law, the case may be adjudicated on the basis of his signature. This is not considered a case whose beginning was valid and whose end was disqualified, because witnesses signed on a document are regarded as though their testimony had already been examined in beit din, and therefore its end too is considered valid.
When he was his son-in-law and she died. Even if his daughter was then dying, nevertheless, since she had not yet died, it is not considered that their testimony began while they were valid. Hagahos Ashri, chapter 4 of Shevuos.
