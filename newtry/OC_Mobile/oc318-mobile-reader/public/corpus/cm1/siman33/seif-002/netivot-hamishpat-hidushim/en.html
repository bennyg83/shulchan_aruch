The brothers. This is derived from the verse, "Fathers shall not be put to death because of children." The Gemara interprets the "fathers" as brothers and the "children" as the sons of those brothers, so that relatives in the second degree are disqualified:
A third degree relative to a first degree relative is disqualified. According to this view there is a dispute whether the disqualification is from Torah law or rabbinic law, and it may be only an asmachta:
Maternal relatives. The same applies to relatives through marriage. The Shach concludes that the disqualification is from Torah law.
