For a convert who converted is like a newborn child. The Beit Yosef was uncertain where their conception was not in sanctity but their mother converted while pregnant and they were born in sanctity: are they also called ordinary converts and treated as newborns, or not? Darkei Moshe cites this and writes that their birth too must have been outside sanctity, so that they themselves converted after birth. However, if one of them was born while the mother was still a non-Jew and later converted, that is enough, and he may testify regarding his Jewish brother even if that brother's conception and birth were both in sanctity, for the law follows the reason of the rule.
