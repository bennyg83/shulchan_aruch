(In the name of Mahari in the Tur: therefore, two men married to, etc.) According to my father the Rosh, etc. The Bach asks that he is valid even for her granddaughter herself. See his answer. It appears to me that the Tur did not come to teach the law of two marital links, for he would be valid even without that principle. Rather, he means: therefore, because father and son are considered first with first, and we do not say they are first with second as Mar bar Rav Ashi held when he validated a paternal grandfather, we find no other case of validation except this one, namely the granddaughter. According to Mar bar Rav Ashi, however, who considered father and son first with second, we could find another case of validation according to my father the Rosh, who allows two marital links even in first with second, namely the relationship between the woman's mother and daughter.
