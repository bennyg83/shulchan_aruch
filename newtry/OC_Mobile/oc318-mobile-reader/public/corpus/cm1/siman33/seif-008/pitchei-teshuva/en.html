In the matter and testify. See Baer Heitev, and see Shach below, siman 37, seif 18, seif katan 32, who cites many authorities who disagree and hold that even a disqualification because of financial interest requires that the witness have been qualified both at the beginning and at the end. He concludes that the party in possession may say, 'I rely on those authorities.' See there. This is also cited by Baer Heitev there, seif katan 32, and see what I write there. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, siman 36, who writes: Regarding the requirement that a witness be qualified both at the beginning and at the end, there are three categories. First, disqualification because of kinship. In this there is no dispute at all that qualification is required both at the beginning and at the end, as explained in Bava Batra 128a. Second, disqualification because of financial interest. Many authorities are lenient and do not require qualification at both stages, for three reasons: first, because the witness can remove his interest at any time; second, because this disqualification does not render him unfit to testify for everyone and therefore is unlike kinship; third, because a relative was an unfit witness from the outset and does not later become qualified, whereas one who has a financial interest was initially a litigant rather than a witness, and only when he removes his interest does he become a witness, so his beginning as a witness is in a state of qualification. Nevertheless, some authorities disagree and hold that financial disqualification also requires qualification at the beginning, and the Shach in siman 37, seif katan 32, concludes that the party in possession may rely on that view. Third, disqualification because of transgression. This is not mentioned explicitly in the Gemara, but the Shach in siman 34, seif katan 33, takes it as obvious that the requirement of qualification at the beginning and the end does not apply. From the arrangement of his words, it appears that he said this only concerning a transgression that does not involve robbery. End of quote. See there, and see what I write in siman 34, seif 29, seif katan 46.
