Regarding monetary disqualification, etc. The reason is that specifically with disqualification by kinship, even after the relationship ends, the person testifying is still the very same person who was once disqualified from testifying for him, and therefore the prior disqualification remains relevant. By contrast, where the disqualification depended on a monetary interest, once he has removed himself from that benefit and another has entered into it, he is treated as though he never had that interest. Moreover, a monetary interest did not create an inherent status of disqualification in him from the outset, since he could immediately have removed himself from the interest. This differs from kinship.
