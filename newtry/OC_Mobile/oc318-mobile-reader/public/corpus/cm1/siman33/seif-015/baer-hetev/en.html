And he may testify. The Sema writes that the reason is that where the disqualification is monetary, the status of disqualification due to personal interest does not attach from the outset, since he was able immediately to remove his own interest. This is unlike disqualification due to kinship. See Responsa R. Eliyahu ibn Chaim, end of siman 29 and siman 103, and Responsa Maharit, simanim 26 and 105.
