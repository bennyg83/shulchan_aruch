(In the Beit Yosef, beginning: the Mordechai wrote, etc., through: it follows from here that a tereifah, etc.) The Darchei Moshe writes that this applies specifically to capital cases, but perhaps a tereifah is valid to testify in monetary cases. Even according to the authorities who require testimony capable of refutation in monetary law as well, the witnesses can still be refuted and made liable for money. After all, even regarding capital liability there is a dispute in the chapter Hanisrafin. This is not conclusive, however, because perhaps that discussion concerns witnesses who were not tereifot when they testified. See Kesef Mishneh, Laws of Testimony 20:6. The matter requires study. Thus far from Maharshal.
