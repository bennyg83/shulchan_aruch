Maternal brothers of brothers may testify, etc. The Tur gives the example. Reuven married Rachel and she bore him Yamin. Reuven also had a son from another wife named Nemuel, who is Yamin's paternal brother. Reuven died, Rachel married Shimon, and she bore him Ohad, who is Yamin's maternal brother. Nemuel and Ohad are competent to testify for one another, even though Yamin is a brother of both.
