A convert who converted is like a newborn child. The Sema writes that the Beit Yosef was uncertain where conception occurred outside holiness, and that the Darkei Moshe requires birth also to have occurred outside holiness. The Darkei Moshe cites the commentary of Rabbeinu Shimshon at the end of Sheviit as explicit proof. In truth, the expression 'converted' there refers to one whose birth was outside holiness as well. The ruling should therefore be understood accordingly, and the relatives are not treated as relatives only where both conception and birth preceded conversion.
