The brother of the brother, etc. Reuven had a son Yosef from Rachel, and Leah had a son Yehudah from Yaakov. After Rachel and Yaakov died, Leah married Reuven and bore Shimon. Shimon is therefore brother to Yosef and brother to Yehudah, but Yosef and Yehudah have no kinship to one another; they merely share a half-brother. The law teaches that Yosef may testify for Yehudah.
