If judgment was already rendered, etc. Meaning that they testified before beit din, were found to have been relatives, their testimony was rejected and judgment was rendered; afterward the relationship ceased and they wished to testify again. Even if their original beginning was in fitness, their later testimony is not accepted because they are likely to reinforce their first statements. This is Baal HaIttur's explanation and Shach's. In Tumim I explain that the original witnesses thought beit din knew of their kinship and therefore did not carefully formulate the first testimony. Even so, I conclude that this law itself remains doubtful and disputed.
