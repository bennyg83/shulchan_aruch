(See there in the Tur: but his wife's paternal grandfather is valid for him.) The Beit Yosef asks that he is first with second through one marital link, and therefore disqualified according to all opinions, and concludes that the text must be emended. It appears that the Beit Yosef's emendation is correct. Otherwise, the Gemara's statement 'his stepson alone' would imply that no other first-with-second relationship is valid, whereas according to this reading one's wife's paternal grandfather would also be first with second and valid. We must therefore emend the text, contrary to the Bach. Thus far from Mahari.
