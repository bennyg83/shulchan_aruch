We do not require both its beginning and end to be in a state of qualification. Responsa Rabbi Eliyahu ibn Chaim, end of siman 29, rules that the Mechaber's conclusion is decisive. In siman 103, however, he writes that it remains a doubt in law. See there, and see below, siman 37, seif 19, and Responsa Maharit, simanim 26 and 105.
