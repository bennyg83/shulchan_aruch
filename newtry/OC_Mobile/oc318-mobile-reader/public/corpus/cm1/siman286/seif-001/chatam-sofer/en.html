Rama, seif 3, "one who redeemed him." Note: In my humble opinion, this applies specifically where the son was wealthy at that time. But if he was poor then, he need not repay, because a father is obligated to redeem his poor son, as is clear in Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh Deah, at the end of siman 252. See there, and see what I wrote there in siman 253, seif 4.
