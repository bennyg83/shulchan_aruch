Zayin) The cited source concludes in siman 105, in the name of a responsum of Maharam, that if the prospective groom and brothers do not wish to fulfill what their father undertook, they are exempt from the penalty. See Even HaEzer siman 13, seif 6 in the gloss, cited by the Sema.
Chet) He writes that the principle of fulfilling the words of the deceased does not apply because the property was not deposited with a third party, as explained in the relevant siman. Mahari Weil similarly writes in siman 42 concerning Reuven, who arranged his daughter's match and fixed a dowry for her but died before marrying her off. See there. The Sema cites this ruling.
