(For he did not support him.) Mahari Weil writes there, responsum 104, that even if the father formalized the undertaking by kinyan at the time of the engagement conditions, the kinyan does not take effect in favor of that son. Rather, it is made toward the other party, who betrothed his daughter to the son, so that if the father violates the agreement and does not give what was stipulated between them, he must pay the penalty to the other party. See Even HaEzer, siman 20, seif 6.
