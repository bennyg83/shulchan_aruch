(We do not heed them.) Although regarding a daughter's dowry we say in Even HaEzer, siman 113, that we follow the marriage settlement of the first daughter whom the father married off during his lifetime, and the heirs must give the second daughter the same amount, that case is different. By rabbinic enactment a daughter is entitled to one tenth of the estate. Thus, what the father gave his daughter during his lifetime reveals that he intended it in place of that one tenth, and presumably he would likewise give the second daughter the same amount. What a person gives his son, however, is merely an ordinary gift and not an obligation. It is therefore possible that under the present circumstances he has no wish to give a gift, and instead leaves him his inheritance.
