[Shulchan Aruch] And if they protested against him. Note: This is difficult to me according to the first view in seif 1, which holds that even there the rule applies where they protested. If so, what difference is there between the eldest brother and an ordinary brother? Both laws originate in the Rashba and are cited in Maggid Mishneh in his name. I later saw that the Derishah was troubled by this and wrote in the name of Nimukei Yosef, in brief, that with the eldest brother the others must protest initially, when he begins clothing himself at the estate’s expense. If they did not protest at the beginning, they waived the matter even prospectively, and when division occurs they do not appraise even the clothing then upon him. With ordinary brothers, however, they can protest even afterward.
