Some disagree, etc. The Tur holds that wherever Rabbi Meir and the Sages dispute, the halachah follows the Sages, except concerning fully ripened produce, where the halachah follows Rabbi Shimon, just as with a creditor who has no rights in produce, a dispute discussed in siman 115.
