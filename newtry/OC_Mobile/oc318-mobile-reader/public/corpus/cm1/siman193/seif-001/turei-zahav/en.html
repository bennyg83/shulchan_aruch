Seif 1: But if he does not need the land. The Sema discusses at length whether the phrase “does not need” here and in siman 95 means that he needs none of the land or merely not all of it. I do not know what compelled this distinction. The Rambam follows Rabbi Yosef Halevi, his teacher, whom Rabbeinu cites, and the rule depends on whether the movable property serves the land in a genuine and necessary manner. There is no basis for creating separate definitions from the wording alone.
