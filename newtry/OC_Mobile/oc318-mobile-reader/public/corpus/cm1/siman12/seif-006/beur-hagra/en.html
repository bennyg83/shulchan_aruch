One whom they are suing, meaning: although they said in Bava Kamma 42, "let him say to him, etc., I would have hidden it, etc.," and see Tosafot there, s.v. "Hava Ma'arikna," where it appears that this is permitted, that is not so. Rather, this is what he means: "What claim do you have against me?" But in truth even that conduct is forbidden, as stated in Shevuot 31a, "Come and stand together with me, etc.," and as written below in siman 21, seif 1, even if he says to him, "Come, etc."
And if he transgressed. Bava Kamma 56a; see Tosafot there, s.v. "Mamona, etc." See there.
