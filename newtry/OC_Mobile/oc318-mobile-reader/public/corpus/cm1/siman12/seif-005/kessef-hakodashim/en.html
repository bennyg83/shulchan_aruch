Seif 9. And I will not fulfill the compromise. If an arbitrator ruled that a seller who wishes to withdraw must pay a stated amount, but afterwards the seller decides not to withdraw, he need not pay merely because market prices later fell and that change influenced his decision. Payment is required only if the compromise explicitly fixed both consequences, namely that the sale is canceled and the seller must pay. Where the wording was not explicit, the seller has the stronger position, especially if he is already in possession.
