A compromise between them. See Baer Heitev, through the words that the Shach wrote in the name of Shiltei Gibborim, etc., that if the judges inform them of the nature of the compromise and appease them, etc. See Birkei Yosef, entry 9, who writes concerning this: Knesset HaGedolah also wrote that our custom follows the words of Ri'az; see there. In Eretz Yisrael and in Egypt, however, they did not follow this practice, and the implication of the authorities is also not in accordance with his view, especially the wording of the Rambam and the Mechaber. The Bach likewise writes this and concludes that the ruling of Shiltei Gibborim is not accepted; see there. This is also implied by the wording of the Agudah in the first chapter of Sanhedrin. End of quote. Tumim likewise questions the ruling of Shiltei Gibborim; see there. See also Netivot HaMishpat on this matter.
