And he may not allow the law to leave his hand divided, etc. The Rosh concludes in Responsa, rule 67, siman 6: the Sages gave a judge authority to rule according to what his eyes see where the matter cannot be clarified through proofs. Sometimes he may decide according to what appears proper to him even without a specific proof or formal assessment, which is the concept of shuda dedayanei, and sometimes by way of compromise. Darkei Moshe cites this. The meaning is that in all these situations the judge may act even against the will of the litigants.
