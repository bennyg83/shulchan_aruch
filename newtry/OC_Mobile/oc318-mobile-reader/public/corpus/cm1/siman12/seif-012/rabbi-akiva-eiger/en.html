[Shulchan Aruch] Without a kinyan. Note: In Responsa Masat Moshe, siman 62, he quotes Responsa Maharashach, part 2, siman 113, concerning creditors who agreed to compromise with the borrower while one creditor does not consent with them: "For several reasons I hold that the one who refuses should be compelled to compromise like the other merchants, who constitute the majority, because the custom of merchants is to compel one who does not wish to enter into a compromise, and they have authority upon whom to rely from the case of Istata."
