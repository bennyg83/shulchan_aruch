And this one built part of it. The case is one in which they dispute upon whom the obligation to build rests. Therefore, if only one of them built, that does not suffice, because according to the other party's claim the obligation to build rested on that person in any event. Accordingly, both must build. See Beurim, seif katan 6, that even if both built, the rule that they may not retract applies only to what they have already built. They may retract with respect to what has not yet been built.
