And he built, etc. Although the Tur writes below at the beginning of siman 157 that where an item is not divisible by law and a kinyan is needed for the division, possession by one party in his portion confirms the division and the other cannot retract, the present case is different. There, each originally owns an equal share in the entire property, and the division merely allocates existing rights. That is why the Rosh says possession by one works even without the other being present and without being told "take possession and acquire." Here the parties' prior rights were different, and the settlement creates new obligations between them. Therefore, even the Tur agrees that both must perform the agreed construction before the settlement is irrevocable. Darkei Moshe cites Rashba's responsum, the source of this law, and one will see that it is not analogous to siman 157. This is unlike the Ir Shushan, who writes that here too one party's construction suffices.
