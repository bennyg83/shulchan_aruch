Further, it states In that siman, “specifically before two.” Beit Yosef explains Rambam and Rif this way, but Hagahot Maimoniyot says that two is not exact and one suffices; Nimukei Yosef says the same regarding Rif. It therefore appears that no authority is concerned for the stricter reading, and an act of acquisition before even one person suffices. So ruled my revered father-in-law of blessed memory.
