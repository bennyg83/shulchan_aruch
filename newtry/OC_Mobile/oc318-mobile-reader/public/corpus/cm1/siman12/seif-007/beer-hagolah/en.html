tz) Conclusion of the Gemara, Sanhedrin 6a.
k) Like the Sages in the baraita there. Tur, and so rules the Rosh there. Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who says in the baraita on 5b that compromise may be effected by two, likewise permits one, as the Gemara establishes there on 6a.
r) So writes the Beit Yosef, and so appears according to the Rambam there and according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel in seif katan 63. The statement that the same applies to one person was not stated merely to teach a side point so that one should not think that three Tannaim disagree on the matter. It is surprising that he did not rule like the Sages. In my humble opinion, it appears that the Rambam too holds that the same applies to one person where the parties made a kinyan, as the Gemara says. It mentions two laymen because of the law that even two do not constitute a valid court, and also so that there will be witnesses to the agreement, as stated there in the Gemara. I later found this also in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot and in the Bach.
This is the proper reading, and the Mechaber writes it in seifim 12 and 13.
