Even though the litigants agreed, etc. Even after the arbitrators have stated the compromise, if no kinyan was made the parties may retract. So appears in the Maimonidean Responsa, Hilchot Sanhedrin, and in Mordechai, chapter Zeh Borer. See Responsa Maharashdam at the end of siman 11.
They may retract. This applies even to a claimant who agreed to yield to the defendant and waive part of his claim. Although an ordinary waiver requires no kinyan, that is because the person waives voluntarily on his own initiative. A waiver made pursuant to a compromise is different, because he waives at the direction of the judges. To prevent him from later claiming, 'The judges misled me with their words,' a kinyan is therefore required. Thus writes the Sema. See Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 34; Responsa Maharshal, siman 4; the Bach; and Ayelet Ahavim, chapter HaMekabel, pages 34a and 75a.
