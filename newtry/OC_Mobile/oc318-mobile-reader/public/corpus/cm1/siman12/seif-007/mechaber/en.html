Even after the litigants have agreed before beit din to a compromise, they may retract as long as no kinyan has been made from them, because a compromise requires a kinyan even when made before three judges. Once a kinyan has been made, they may not retract even if the compromise was made before a single judge. Some say this applies specifically before two. Rama: A kinyan is not literally required in every case. The same applies if he gave a document obligating himself, or used any other valid mode of acquisition. Some say that when making a kinyan on a compromise, he must transfer an interest in the actual object so that it not be merely a kinyan on words, as will be explained below at the beginning of siman 203.
