One who. As explained above in seif 8.
(Anthology) One who became obligated, etc. Although he did not state his undertaking explicitly, this resembles one who was speaking with a woman concerning her divorce and then gave her the document without an explicit declaration. We rule like Rabbi Yose, as stated in the first chapter of Kiddushin. Similarly, if one person said, 'I am hereby a nazir,' and his fellow heard and said, 'And I,' the declaration is effective. See the Mordechai in the first chapter of Sanhedrin.
