Further, in seif 12 “And this one built a little.” The Sema’s distinction between this case and siman 157 is obscure. There, the possession created by building benefits both parties, and through it the second party also acquires his portion; therefore one party’s building is effective. Here they dispute which party must build one section and which must build the other, while both are obligated to build. One party cannot compel the other to complete the remainder merely because he himself built part. If instead they disputed who would acquire a particular portion, that would be the case of siman 157.
