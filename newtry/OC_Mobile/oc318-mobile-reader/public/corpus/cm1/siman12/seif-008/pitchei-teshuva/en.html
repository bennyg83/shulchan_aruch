It is forbidden to seek stratagems. See Tumim, seif katan 5, regarding his explanation of Tosafos in Bava Kamma 40b so that their words not contradict this ruling. He also cites there the words of Terumat HaDeshen and the Maharshal on this matter. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part I, end of siman 163, who distinguishes in another way: Certainly, if the defendant actively seeks pretexts in order to force a compromise, this is forbidden. There, however, where he says, 'I would drive it away to the marsh,' this is merely passive inaction, namely, refraining from guarding the ox that is subject to his fellow's lien in order to bring about a compromise. This is certainly permitted. See there further. Some of this will be cited below in siman 93, seif 1.
