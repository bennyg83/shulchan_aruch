A waiver requires no kinyan. The reason is that once he waives, the waiving party immediately relinquishes his claim. This is specifically where he does not still hold the debtor's promissory note. If he retains the note, then so long as he does not return it immediately, even a complete waiver is ineffective without a kinyan. Thus writes the Sema, and the Tur writes likewise in the name of Rabbi Yeshayah. This requires study, for below, siman 241, seif 2, the Rama does not rule so; see what I wrote there. The Bach writes that Rabbi Yeshayah holds that the law follows Beit Shammai, that a document standing ready for collection is considered already collected. His words are astonishing, for we do not rule like Beit Shammai, as is evident from the Gemara at the end of chapter Kol HaNishba'in and from all the decisors. See Responsa Maharam of Lublin, siman 47, and Responsa Mahari ibn Lev, part 1, general rule 17, beginning of siman 57.
