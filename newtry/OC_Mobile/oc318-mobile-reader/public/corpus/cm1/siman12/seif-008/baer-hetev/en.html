Waiver. The Sema writes that this applies only where the person granting the waiver is not still holding the debt document. If he retains the document, then so long as he does not immediately return it, even a complete waiver without a kinyan is ineffective. The Shach writes that this requires study, since in siman 241, seif 2, the Rama does not rule this way. The Taz raises the same difficulty. See Even HaEzer, siman 105, seif 6, in Beit Shmuel and Chelkat Mechokek, who likewise rule unlike the Sema.
