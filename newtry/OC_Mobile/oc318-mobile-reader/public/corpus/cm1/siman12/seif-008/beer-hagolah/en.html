The Tur. This means an ordinary waiver, not a waiver produced by compromise, as Tosafot and the Rosh write regarding the worker whose payment was assigned, Bava Metzia 112a. The Rambam likewise writes in chapter 5 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, as do the other poskim, that waiver through compromise is different. Even though he knows what he is waiving, it resembles a mistaken waiver because he waives on the advice of the compromisers, as Tosafot write there.
