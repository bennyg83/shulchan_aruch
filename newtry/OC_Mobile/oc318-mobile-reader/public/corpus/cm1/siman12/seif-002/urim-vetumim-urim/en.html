So and so, you are entitled, etc. Once the judge has announced the ruling, he may no longer say, "Go and compromise, perhaps I erred and other judges will overturn my decision." Sema. See Tumim for my discussion. Shiltei Gibborim, cited by Shach, limits the prohibition to a compromise imposed according to the judges' own view without the litigants' consent. If the judges explain a proposed compromise and gently persuade the parties, without coercion, to waive claims toward one another, this is permitted. In Tumim I question this ruling, and Bach likewise disagrees with Shiltei Gibborim.
