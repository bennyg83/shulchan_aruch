[Shulchan Aruch] So too, he is cautioned not to distort a compromise. Note: Just as, with a distortion of judgment, if the judge erred in the law the judgment is reversed, so too with a distortion of compromise, if he erred in the compromise it is reversed. Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, siman 98.
