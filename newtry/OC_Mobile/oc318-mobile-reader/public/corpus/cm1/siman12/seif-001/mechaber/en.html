A Judge Before Whom a Compliant Litigant and a Powerful Litigant Appear, and All the Laws of Compromise. Nineteen seifim. If two litigants come before you, one compliant and one powerful, then before hearing their words, or after hearing them but before knowing toward whom the law inclines, you may say, 'I will not involve myself with you,' lest the powerful litigant be found liable and then pursue the judge. Once you have heard their words and know toward whom the law inclines, you may no longer say, 'I will not involve myself with you.' If the judge is appointed to serve the public, he must attend to their case. {Rama: Likewise, if both litigants are powerful, he must attend to their case (Hagahos Ashiri, beginning of Sanhedrin, and Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos). Some write that nowadays the practice is nevertheless not to protest against transgressors when doing so poses a danger that they will inform upon the protester to the government (Mahariv).}
