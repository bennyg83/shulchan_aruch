A kinyan of mere words. Baer Heitev cites the Sema that this concern applies specifically to the plaintiff, but for the defendant it is enough that a kinyan be made from him “to waive” whatever the arbitrators determine. Taz below, siman 207, disagrees and holds that a kinyan “to waive” is likewise only a kinyan on a future act, comparable to “I will give,” and is ineffective. Tumim and Netivot HaMishpat agree. The defendant must therefore say expressly, “I hereby waive up to the amount the arbitrators will determine.” Responsa Noda BiYehudah, end of siman 31, likewise rejects the Sema, noting that the Mordechai at the beginning of Sanhedrin, the source of this rule, states explicitly to the contrary.
