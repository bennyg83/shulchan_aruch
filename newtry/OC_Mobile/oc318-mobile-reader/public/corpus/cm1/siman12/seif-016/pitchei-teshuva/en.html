Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes that the Beit Yosef’s citation here from the Rashbam, part III, siman 217, is brought by the Sema below at the beginning of siman 23; see the Shach at the end of that siman. Birkei Yosef writes that where litigants appointed two arbitrators in writing and by kinyan, and the arbitrators deliberated and orally agreed between themselves on a compromise, even stating that agreement before witnesses, but did not yet announce it to the litigants, they may still alter their agreement, increase or decrease the terms, and issue whatever final compromise they deem proper. Once they pronounce the compromise before the litigants, and certainly once they write it and deliver it to them, they have no authority to alter it, because their agency has ended. Anything they say afterward is as though stated by one who was never appointed for the matter. This is from an unpublished responsum of Maharikash, siman 42. See below, end of siman 23.
