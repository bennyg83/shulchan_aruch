The language of the Tur, seif 13, from the conclusion of the Gemara in Ketubot 100a: the halachah is that an agent has the same rule as a widow. Maggid Mishneh, chapter 13 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 9, writes that most commentators explain this as referring to an agent of beit din, all the more so to other agents.
Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, and he writes that the Rosh agreed with this in his rulings there.
