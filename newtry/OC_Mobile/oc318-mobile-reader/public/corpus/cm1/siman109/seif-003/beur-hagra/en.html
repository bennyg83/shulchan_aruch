Some say that if... Erchin 24a states that although certain property is ordinarily sold in a particular way, consecrated property may be treated differently. The Rishonim apply the principle here.
And some disagree. Rashbatz distinguishes that case because the sale there occurred at a time generally fit for selling, whereas selling during an abnormal depressed period is like selling at night or outside the ordinary hours when laborers enter and leave, effectively a sale without proper announcement. The examples of slaves, cows, and pearls concern items that do not ordinarily improve merely by waiting, whereas here the market may recover automatically when the crisis passes. The first view is the Beit Yosef, who challenges this from the case of a cow whose value may rise with waiting. Rashba likewise holds that appraisal is based on present value, as stated in Bava Kamma 7b.
Although they announced. According to the Rif and Rosh's text, both clauses involve announcement.
And some say... According to our text, the case is where no announcement was made. The Rosh explains that even his own version can be understood as requiring announcement.
And if they wished... This appears to be inferred from Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel's statement that otherwise "what strength has beit din?" His dispute concerns whether beit din has greater finality than ordinary parties, but surely it is not weaker. Hence the rule here that a private party's rights should not exceed those of beit din. Some authorities in Even HaEzer disagree, relying on Kiddushin and Kesubos, where a division made specifically according to beit din appraisal may have stronger finality. Tosafos' explanation suggests that without announcement even refunding the difference may not cure the defect, because a sale invalid as to the orphans is likewise invalid as to the buyer. The Rosh uses this reasoning against Rambam.
And it is deemed a waiver. Unlike Tosafos' explanation, this follows Rabbeinu Tam's interpretation concerning the agent of judges, which the Rosh accepts.
