And a beit din that announced the sale. See the Sema, seif katan 11. The Kesef Mishneh understands the Rambam as presenting two separate clauses, and interprets the first clause's statement that the court did not examine the appraisal to mean that it nevertheless announced the sale. This is difficult, because according to that reading the Gemara should have answered that the latter clause expressly concerns a formal letter of appraisal, while the first also involved announcement but not such a letter. It should not have been forced to answer that additional procedures are required. Therefore, the Maggid Mishneh's explanation appears primary, although the matter requires study.
And if the beit din wished, etc. See Even HaEzer, siman 104, seif 5, and the responsum of Maharshadam, siman 374.
