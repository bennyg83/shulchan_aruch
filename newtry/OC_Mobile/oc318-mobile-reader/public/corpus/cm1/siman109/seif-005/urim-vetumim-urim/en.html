The same law applies to a guardian. Not completely. With beit din, an error below one sixth leaves the sale intact. A guardian is only like an ordinary agent, whose sale can be voided even for a slight error. Therefore even Rabbi Yonah, who disagrees in the first case because a purchaser can say, 'I relied on beit din and assumed they did not err,' agrees that a guardian is no better than an ordinary person and the purchaser cannot invoke that reliance.
