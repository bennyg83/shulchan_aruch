That they sold. Meaning even if the heirs sold the property in order to pay a creditor. This is unlike what might be inferred from the Bach, namely that where they sold specifically in order to pay a creditor the law is as though they had directly placed the creditor into possession. That is not so. See Maggid Mishneh, chapter 13 of Hilchot Mechirah, halachah 11. Shach.
