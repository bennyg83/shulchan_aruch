They sell without public announcement. The Sema writes that this is governed by the preceding seif, where they sell for maintenance and the head tax, for the reason he explained there. This is imprecise. Here the reason is that the estate stands in the place of a litigant who wishes to pay the debt himself. In truth, if the creditors do not wish to accept payment voluntarily from the estate, the matter depends upon the dispute in siman 103, seif 1. See there, and see the Beit Yosef.
