At the time workers enter. At that time the owner can hear from workers what they observed in the field, or they have time to inspect it. In the morning the owner tells workers to look at the field when they have leisure or pass that way. This is relevant to fields, not houses, yet the Sages did not distinguish, and the custom applies to houses as well.
