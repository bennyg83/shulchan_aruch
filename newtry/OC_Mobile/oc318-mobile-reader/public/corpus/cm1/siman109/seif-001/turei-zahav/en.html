If the item is a pledge in the creditor's possession, the creditor can say, “At the outset I lent more than its market value because it was especially dear to me.” It appears that although Rama rules in siman 72, seif 20, regarding this very claim, that a possessor cannot prevail merely by saying that he originally intended to retain the article if the debt was not paid because it was dear to him, nevertheless here the heir is heard, because the heir is the possessor. Rama has already ruled there that the burden of proof rests upon the party seeking to extract property from another.
