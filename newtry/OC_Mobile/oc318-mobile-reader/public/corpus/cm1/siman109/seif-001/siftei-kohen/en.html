We listen to the heir. The Sema explains that it is obvious that if the heir presently has money and wishes to redeem the property, we listen to him. Even if he presently lacks money, we appraise the property so that if he later obtains money he need pay only its appraised value. This is also implied by the Rosh and Tur. See siman 114:7. All this follows the Tur and Rosh. I have concluded, however, in siman 115 at the end of seif 1, that the Ramban, Raavad, Baal HaTerumot, Beit Yosef, and Rivash are correct: neither heirs nor purchasers may remove the creditor unless they pay the entire debt. Otherwise, the creditor may say, 'The land is worth to me the amount of my entire debt.' This is the primary ruling. See the whole of this siman in Even HaEzer 104.
