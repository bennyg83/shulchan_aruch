To the heir. The Sema writes that not only where the heir presently has money and wishes to redeem the land do we listen to him, but even where he presently has no money we appraise the land for him, so that if he later obtains money he need pay only its appraised value. This is implied by the Rosh and Tur. See siman 114, seif 6. The Shach, however, rules in siman 115 in accordance with the Ramban, Raavad, and Baal HaTerumot, and notes that the Beit Yosef likewise appears to agree. Rivash, siman 483, rules that in every case neither heirs nor purchasers may remove the creditor from the land unless they pay the full amount of his debt, for otherwise the creditor may say, 'The land is worth to me the amount of my entire debt.' This is the primary ruling. See Even HaEzer siman 104. The Taz notes that although in siman 72, seif 20, the Rama allows a holder of collateral to say that from the outset he intended to keep the object if unpaid because it is dear to him, here we listen to the heir because he is in possession, and the burden of proof rests on the party seeking to remove property from its possessor.
