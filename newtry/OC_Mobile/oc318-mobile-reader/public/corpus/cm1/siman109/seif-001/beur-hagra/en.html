They appraise, etc., and afterward announce, etc. Bava Metzia, the Yerushalmi, Ramban, and Baal HaTerumot establish that appraisal precedes public announcement. Although the Bavli limits the mishnah to places or items without announcement, the procedural order remains clear. See Tosafot and Rashi in Arakhin. See citation 333.
If he says, etc. Since an appraisal may later be reversed in favor of an heir, the heir's claim must be considered. See Sema.
