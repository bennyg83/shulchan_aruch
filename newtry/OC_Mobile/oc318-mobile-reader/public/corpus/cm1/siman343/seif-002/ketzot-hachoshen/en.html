A paid custodian for a fixed period. Ritva writes that when the stated custodial period ends, a paid custodian remains a paid custodian until he tells the owner, "Take your property, for I no longer wish to guard it." The mere arrival of the date does not automatically reduce him to an unpaid custodian. This is analogous to an artisan who has completed the work but has not yet told the owner to take the article.
