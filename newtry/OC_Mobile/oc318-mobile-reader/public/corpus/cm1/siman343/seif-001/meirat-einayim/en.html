Immediately when the term ends, etc. The Tur and Mechaber already wrote this at the end of siman 340. There the novelty is that even if the borrower sent it back through a messenger and an unavoidable accident occurred on the way, he does not remain liable as a borrower merely because one might call the sending negligent and the final accident a consequence of negligence. Here the novelty is that even if the item remains in his own house after the loan term ends, he is still only a paid custodian regarding theft and loss. The Mechaber repeated the rule here because he followed the Tur's arrangement.
