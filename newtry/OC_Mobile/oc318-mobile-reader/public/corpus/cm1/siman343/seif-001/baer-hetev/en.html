When the term ends. The Tur and Shulchan Aruch already wrote this at the end of siman 340. The Tur states it twice because there the point is that even if the borrower sent the object back through his agent and it was lost by an unavoidable accident on the way, we do not say that the beginning was negligence in entrusting it to an agent and the end was an accident, so that he should remain liable for accidents. Here the point is that even while the article is still in his house, after the borrowing term ends he remains liable for theft and loss as a paid bailee. Since the Mechaber wrote both laws at the end of siman 340, one must say that here he merely followed the Tur's wording. Sema.
