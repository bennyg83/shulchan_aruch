There, from the incident of the planter who wished to ascend to Eretz Yisrael, as Rav Ashi ruled. Rif and Rosh likewise rule in the name of the Raavad. The phrase 'when you reach slaughtering of sacred offerings, come question me' means that after you become wiser and have learned the entire subject you may challenge me, but at present you are not yet fit to do so. Although the Gemara says Rav Ashi initially thought otherwise, we find at the beginning of Bava Batra that an initial thought may remain as a viable reasoning.
Rambam likewise writes so there, based on the Gemara.
