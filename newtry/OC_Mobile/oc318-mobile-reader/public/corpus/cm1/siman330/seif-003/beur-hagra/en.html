(Supplement) He planted, etc. The ruling follows Rav Ashi. Rav Acha holds that the owner merely takes his half, while Rav Ashi holds that if the planter is removed before the agreed time he receives only the amount by which a planter ordinarily exceeds a sharecropper. The discussion supports Rav Ashi from the case where a quarter was washed away by the river, because that occurred before the term, unlike the case of an old stump. Rosh explains that Rav Acha permits division of the land, while Rav Ashi permits division only of the produce, because the planter cannot demand division of the vineyard before the term. This is the basis of Rama's gloss. The river case nevertheless must be explained as above, perhaps because the inability to divide before the term shows that the planter entered on the understanding that he would receive only a quarter. (End of supplement.)
