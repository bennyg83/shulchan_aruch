And he’s stuck and barked and lost. P. was charged for the time, A. Z.Z. and a little bit of them lost, and with the praise and loss, the loss remained in full praise for the loss and those who were more ignorant than they did or lost in another matter:
And we deduct from him what he caused to be lost. The Tur adds that he is also removed. The Mechaber copied the Rambam, who did not state removal here. Nevertheless, the two authorities need not disagree. The Gemara explicitly teaches that a planter who undertakes to plant another person's trees and causes loss must pay and may be removed, as the Tur and Mechaber already write at the end of siman 306. The Rambam himself also states that general rule at the end of chapter 10 of Hilchot Sechirut. The Mechaber therefore relied upon the earlier ruling.
