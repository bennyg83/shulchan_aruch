And they deduct. The Tur adds that the incompetent planter is removed. The Mechaber copies the Rambam, who does not state this explicitly. Nevertheless, they do not disagree. The Gemara in HaMekabel expressly rules that a planter who plants the owner's trees and causes loss must pay and is removed, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the end of siman 306. The Rambam himself states that general rule in chapter 10 of Hilchot Sechirut, and the Mechaber relies here upon what he already wrote in siman 306. Sema.
