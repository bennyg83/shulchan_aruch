Likewise, if two people deposited two utensils, etc. The Mishnah in HaMafkid teaches both cases: one deposited one hundred and the other two hundred, and two deposited utensils, one large and one small. The Gemara explains that the second clause adds a novelty where both utensils were deposited together. The custodian gives the small utensil to one claimant and to the other gives property equal to the value of the small utensil, even if this means breaking the large utensil and giving him a portion. One might have thought that to avoid destroying the large utensil everything should remain untouched. The Mishnah teaches otherwise. The Rambam and Mechaber copy the Mishnah. The Tur omits the case because after the principle is known it is straightforward.
