(He shall pay both of them.) Regarding what the Sema writes in the name of Nimukei Yosef, Tosafot do not agree. In the chapter HaMafkid, page 36, the Gemara challenges the incident involving a certain pious man who accepted an item and asks: "Did he not wish to fulfill his obligation toward Heaven?" According to the Sema's explanation, there would have been no difficulty at all, for in that case he had no duty to investigate carefully.
