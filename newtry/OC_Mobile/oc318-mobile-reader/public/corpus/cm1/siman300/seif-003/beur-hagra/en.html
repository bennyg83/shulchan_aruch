And likewise one who, etc. The Mishnah there, according to the initial understanding that they demanded from him. The Gemara established the case of admission and liability toward Heaven only according to Rabbi Tarfon, whereas we rule like Rabbi Akiva.
And if they themselves, etc., but, etc. There in the conclusion, "here, etc." The Rosh writes that this applies specifically to his father, since the Gemara does not say "one of you." See Beer HaGolah and the end of siman 77, where others disagree. This is also implied by Tosafos cited above, s.v. "hatam, etc."
