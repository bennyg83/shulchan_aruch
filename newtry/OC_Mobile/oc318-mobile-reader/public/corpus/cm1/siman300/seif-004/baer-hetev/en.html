To both of them. After they swear. In this respect there is no distinction even if they deposited together and the deposits were tied to one another. The custodian cannot excuse himself by saying, "I saw that you did not object to one another," because each can respond: "What could I have done? I had no way to protect myself against the other's deceit, for even if we had deposited separately, the other would see the animal grazing in the field and could come and claim that it was his."
