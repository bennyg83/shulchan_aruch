Shach, seif katan 10, 'as I wrote above.' Note: In siman 222, seif katan 35, and from what I wrote there, it is clear that he holds that even in the case of a robber the property remains literally in his possession. There is no distinction between robbery and a deposit. For this reason the Shach in the preceding seif katan brings proof from Tosafot in chapter HaGozel, which deals with robbery, because those who disagree do not accept such a distinction.
