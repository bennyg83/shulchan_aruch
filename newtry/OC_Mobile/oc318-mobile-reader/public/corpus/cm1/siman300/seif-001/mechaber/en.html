Two people deposited one hundred and two hundred, and each claims the two hundred. It contains four seifim.
If two people separately deposited with one bailee, one one hundred and one two hundred, and each claims he deposited two hundred while the bailee does not know, each swears that he deposited two hundred and the bailee pays two hundred to each, losing one hundred of his own because he was negligent in failing to label each purse. If they do not make definite claims, he is exempt in court but must pay to satisfy the demands of Heaven. If they jointly delivered three hundred in one bundle and later each claims the two hundred, he gives one hundred to each and the balance remains deposited, according to some with beit din, until one admits or forever. He may say that their joint delivery showed they did not insist on separation, so he was not required constantly to remember whose portion was larger. In that case he is exempt even morally, though some impose a moral obligation when they make definite claims.
