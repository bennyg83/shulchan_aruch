Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilkhot She'eilah U'Pikadon, halachah 4. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is explained in the Mishnah and sugyot of HaMafkid, Bava Metzia 37a, and in the Rif.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is unlike a case where one claims, "You have one hundred of mine," and the defendant answers, "I certainly know that fifty is yours, but regarding the other fifty I do not know," where the defendant pays the full hundred without an oath, as explained above. Here it is certain that through his negligence one of the claimants would otherwise lose one hundred wrongfully. It is therefore proper that the recipients swear before taking. The Rashba writes similarly. Moreover, this law applies even if the custodian says to each claimant, "Here is one hundred for you."
Gimel) This is the language of the Maggid Mishneh there in the name of some authorities, and he writes that the Gemara compels this conclusion.
Dalet) Rambam there and the Mishnah there, according to Rava's explanation.
Heh) Rambam rules like the first Tanna there.
