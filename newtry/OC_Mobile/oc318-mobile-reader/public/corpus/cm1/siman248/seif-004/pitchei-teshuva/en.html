But what they write, and so forth, and some disagree. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 122, who writes that the phrase "and some disagree" does not refer to the immediately preceding statement concerning "what they write," which is from the Ran in the chapter Yesh Nochalin. The Ran's position there is in fact definite and accepted. Rather, "and some disagree" refers back to the earlier rule concerning one who says, "and after you to your heirs from now." This is also explicit in Beit Yosef. See there, and see what I wrote below, siman 281, seif 7, seif katan 12.
