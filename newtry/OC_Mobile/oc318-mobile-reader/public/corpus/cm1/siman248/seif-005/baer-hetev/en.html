We collect. Beit din does not commit a prohibition by collecting from the body of the property itself, because selling it to alter the donor's intent is prohibited. It nevertheless appears obvious that if the borrower himself transferred it to his creditor in satisfaction of his debt, or to his wife for her ketubah, his act is effective. This is no worse than where he violated the rule and sold it. One cannot say that the rule of returning an appraisal applies and that the creditor has only a lien, because where the borrower voluntarily transfers property to the creditor it is explained in siman 103 that the appraisal is not returned.
