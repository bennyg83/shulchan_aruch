It is as though he expressly said to her, etc., and not to the husband. This is the Rambam's language. According to the Rambam, an express statement of this kind is effective. The Rosh and Tur hold that one who wishes to give property to his daughter in such a way that her husband receives not even the produce must also limit the daughter's own use to a specified purpose, as explained in Yoreh Deah siman 222 and Even HaEzer siman 85. According to them, the present law must be understood as though the donor imposed that more restrictive formulation. It may be, however, that their stricter requirement applies specifically where the daughter is to own the gift permanently. Here she does not own it forever, because the donor said, "and after you to so and so." See Even HaEzer siman 91.
