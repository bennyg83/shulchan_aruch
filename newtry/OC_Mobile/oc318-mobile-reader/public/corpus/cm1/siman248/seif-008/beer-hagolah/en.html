Dalet) This too is Abaye's statement there.
Heh) The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara's statement that the husband removes the property from the purchasers, etc., is not to be understood as requiring beit din first to transfer it from the purchasers to the husband and then from him to the subsequent beneficiary. We do not burden beit din with a pointless circular transfer. Rather, the property remains with the purchaser. It appears that even if the subsequent beneficiary wants it to belong to the husband, his statement is ineffective.
