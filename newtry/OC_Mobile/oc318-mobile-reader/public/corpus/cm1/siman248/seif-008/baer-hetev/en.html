To a woman. See the Tur and Shulchan Aruch in Even HaEzer at the beginning of siman 91, the Beit Shmuel there, and Responsa Rashach, part II, siman 135.
Explicitly. This is the Rambam's language, and according to him an explicit stipulation is effective. According to the Rosh and Tur, however, one who wishes to give property to his daughter while preventing her husband from receiving even the fruits must specify that the gift is not given to the daughter for all purposes, but only for a particular designated use, as explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 222, and Even HaEzer, siman 85. Accordingly, here too one would have to understand the statement to the woman in that form. It is nevertheless possible to distinguish: there, because the father gives the property to his daughter permanently, he must use such a stipulation if he wishes to exclude even the husband's right to fruits. Here, however, the property was not given to her permanently, since he expressly said, "and after you to So-and-so." Sema.
