It is as though he expressly said so. Even according to the Rosh and Tur, who hold that when one wishes to give to a married woman in a manner excluding her husband's rights he must say that he is giving it only for a particular purpose, here it is treated as though he said that. Alternatively, since he is not giving it to her forever, for he said, "and after you to so and so," no such additional language is required. See Beurim.
