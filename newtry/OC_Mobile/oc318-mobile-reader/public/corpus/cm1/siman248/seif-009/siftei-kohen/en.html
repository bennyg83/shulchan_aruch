To her heirs, etc. This is the text of the Rambam. See the Maggid Mishneh there, and this is likewise the reading of the Mechaber. Otherwise, the Rama's gloss would be difficult, as the Sema writes in seif katan 24. Accordingly, the phrase 'during the lifetime of her father' is incomprehensible to me. It should read 'during the lifetime of her mother.' I do not have authority to amend all the editions. Examine carefully.
