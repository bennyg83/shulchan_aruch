To her heirs. This is the Rambam's wording. See the Maggid Mishneh there. The Mechaber likewise appears to mean this, for otherwise the Rama's gloss would be difficult. Accordingly, the phrase 'during her father's lifetime' is unintelligible to me and should read 'during her mother's lifetime.' I am not prepared to emend all the editions. Shach. See Responsa Shav Yaakov, Choshen Mishpat, siman 17, on this matter.
