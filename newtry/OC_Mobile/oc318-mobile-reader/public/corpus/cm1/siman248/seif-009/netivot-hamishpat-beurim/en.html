My property to my mother. See Shach, seif katan 10, who writes that the phrase "during the lifetime of her father" is imprecise and should read "during the lifetime of her mother." According to our text, however, which reads "and he had a daughter," the wording is correct, for his daughter is also among his mother's heirs. Nor can we say here that the statement is treated as though he said "from now," for the reason I explained above.
