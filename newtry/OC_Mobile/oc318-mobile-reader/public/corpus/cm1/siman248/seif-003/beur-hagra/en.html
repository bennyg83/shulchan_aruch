(Supplement) If he transgressed, etc. The halacha was decided like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. One should not say that Rabbi Yochanan follows his own view while we rule like Reish Lakish, for Abaye also agrees with Rabbi Yochanan there, as he says, 'Which is, etc.' The same is true at the end of chapter 10 of Ketubot. Rava himself, who ruled like Reish Lakish, agrees with Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel on 125b. See Rashbam 137a, s.v. as it was taught, and s.v. they disagree only, etc. Rosh, Rif, and the other authorities write likewise. This is the meaning of seif 7: 'One may not, etc.' See what I wrote there. (End of supplement.)
