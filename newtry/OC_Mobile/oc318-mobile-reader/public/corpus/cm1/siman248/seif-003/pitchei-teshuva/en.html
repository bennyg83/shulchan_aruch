Except for what he retained. See Baer Heitev in the name of the Shach, who writes that the same applies to a deathly ill person if he said "from today," and that the Rama likewise writes in seif 7. See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, end of siman 132, who writes that the Shach's proof is difficult. The Rama there discusses one who says, "and after you to so and so from today," where "from today" is effective because the second recipient acquires retroactively, so the first recipient's interest can be interrupted. Here, however, the issue is that the first recipient himself was given the property "from today," and one might say that inheritance cannot be interrupted. Nevertheless, the ruling itself appears correct on logical grounds. See there.
