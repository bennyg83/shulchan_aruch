[Shulchan Aruch] And he specified and said. Note: See Maharit, part 2, Choshen Mishpat, siman 74, who writes that this applies specifically to one son among several. But with his only son, even in this formulation the inheritance is not uprooted from him and cannot be interrupted. He writes likewise there in siman 6. As to the Rambam's basic rule that by using the formulation "not by way of inheritance" the inheritance may be interrupted, Rivash, siman 167, writes that the Rambam's reasoning is unclear, that the Raavad already challenged him, and that the Rashba also appears to disagree. The Gemara likewise appears to indicate the opposite of the Rambam's reasoning. See Responsa Tashbetz, part 4, Chut HaMeshulash, second column, siman 2, at length.
