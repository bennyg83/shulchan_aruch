But what they write, etc., and some disagree. Shevut Yaakov, part II, siman 122, explains that Ramah's phrase “and some disagree” does not refer back to the preceding rule concerning the customary wording of documents, which comes from Ran in the chapter Yesh Nochalin. Ran's position on that point is definite and accepted. Rather, “some disagree” refers to the immediately preceding rule concerning language such as “and after you to your heirs from now.” This is also explicit in Beit Yosef. See there, and see what I wrote below, siman 281 seif 7 seif katan 12.
