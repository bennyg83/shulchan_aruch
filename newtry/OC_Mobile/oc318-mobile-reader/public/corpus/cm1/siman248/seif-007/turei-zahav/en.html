(Seif 13: since he did not write, etc.) In our master's text it says, "since there was no delivery here." The meaning appears to be this. Although he transferred it to her by kinyan, and one might say that the kinyan served in place of the written phrase, "acquire it for yourself," there still was no delivery. Necessarily, then, the kinyan must be understood as taking the place of delivery, and consequently there is no writing. This explains the following statement: although Rashbam rules that writing is unnecessary, meaning that then we can properly treat the kinyan as equivalent to delivery, our master adds a further point. Even if one says there is both writing and delivery, the writing is still deficient because the required phrase, "acquire it for yourself," is absent.
