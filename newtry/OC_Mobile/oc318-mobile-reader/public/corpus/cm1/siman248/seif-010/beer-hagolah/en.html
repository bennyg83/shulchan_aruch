The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara leaves unresolved whether slaves are treated as land or movable property. The Rif, however, rules simply that they are like movable property. In any event, where he did not say 'all movable property,' since even wheat and barley are not included, all the more so slaves are not included.
