[Rama] To me or to my heirs. Note: This applies specifically to actual heirs. But if he says, "after you to my grandchildren," while his children are alive, a sale by the first recipient is effective. So writes Riva in the Hagahot Mordechai to Bava Batra. See Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer, siman 107, seif katan 17, and Chelkat Mechokek there.
