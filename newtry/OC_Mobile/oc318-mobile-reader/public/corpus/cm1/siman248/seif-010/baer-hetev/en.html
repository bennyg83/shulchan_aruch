And the like. Meaning, other kinds of merchandise. The ordinary expression 'movable property' refers only to household utensils unless he expressly says 'all my movable property.' Tur in the name of the Ramah. Ibid.
Everything. Except a Sefer Torah, as stated in seif 12. Responsa Mabit rules that other sefarim likewise are not included in the ordinary term 'movable property,' and proves this from the Tur's wording. Shach.
The movable property. Although elsewhere slaves are compared to land, with a gift we follow ordinary speech, and in ordinary speech slaves are included among movable property because they move. Sema.
The lower. People do not ordinarily move the lower millstone after it has been fixed in place. Even when it needs dressing, it is dressed in its place. The upper millstone, however, must be moved and removed from its place when it is dressed or repaired. Ibid.
