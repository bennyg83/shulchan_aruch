C. St. St. St. S. B.:
He explains there that since the words of a dying person are treated as written and delivered, this is like a healthy person who wrote a transfer concerning promissory notes and also like one who actually delivered the notes. See Responsa Rashba siman 910, the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 10 of Hilkhot Zechiyah, law 2, and Responsa Rosh, klal 83, siman 4.
