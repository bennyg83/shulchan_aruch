The gift of a dangerously ill person, etc. The Mechaber's words are astonishing. Since he retained five gold coins for his heirs, this does not have the law of the gift of a dangerously ill person, as explained below, siman 250, seif 4. There is therefore no difficulty in requiring writing and delivery. The Beit Yosef likewise cites at the end of siman 66, novel ruling 49, the following responsum of the Rashba: 'The transfer of a promissory note may be effected as the gift of a dangerously ill person by a mere oral declaration, provided that the gift includes all his property.' The Rashba's responsum cited by the Tur concerns the ordinary case in which nothing was retained. See there.
