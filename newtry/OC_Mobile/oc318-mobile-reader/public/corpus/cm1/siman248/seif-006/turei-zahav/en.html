(Seif 11: He said to give her her garments.) It appears to me that this applies specifically when he said, "her garments," which implies all the garments belonging to her. If he said merely, "Give her garments," she receives only two garments, because the minimum plural of garments is two, just as in siman 218, seif 24, the minimum plural of fields is two. Although the responsum of the Rosh uses the wording, "concerning the garments that the husband instructed be given to her," it appears that this phrase is the Rosh's description, not the testator's exact wording. The testator himself must have said, "her garments." Were the law otherwise, it would parallel the case in seif 11 of one who says, "My property to so-and-so," where even his cloak is included, as the Rosh explains in the chapter HaMokher et HaBayit.
