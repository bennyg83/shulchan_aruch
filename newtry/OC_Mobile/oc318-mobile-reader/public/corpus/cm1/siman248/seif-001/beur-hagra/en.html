One may not, etc. Bava Batra 137a: the halachah was decided like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. The sugya there 125b also follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, as does the end of chapter 10 of Kesubos.
Such as, etc. As stated there according to Rabbi Yochanan ben Berokah, and Rabbi Yochanan ben Berokah speaks precisely of such a case, as stated there 130a.
Even if, etc. This follows from the Gemara's challenge there from Rav Acha's message and from the first baraisa. Necessarily the third recipient also was fit to inherit him, for otherwise the original difficulty remains, since the term "inherit" was used.
And likewise, etc. For it is not his property.
For every, etc. The Geonim likewise rule so, unlike Rashbam there, who writes that this applies only where he did not state it in the language of a gift. See there 133a, where the reading "for the language of gift does not, etc." supports the Geonim.
And if, etc. See the first chapter of Gittin.
But a healthy person, etc. This is what Rav Huna says there 133a: "a deathly ill person, etc.," in accordance with what was sent, etc.
