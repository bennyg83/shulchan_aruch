We do not listen to him until his fellow comes. This was explained in the Derishah at the end of siman 77. The Yerushalmi that is the source of this law shows that only payment of the deposit or loan need be withheld. He may still sue regarding his own share and litigate even if his fellow is not in the city, where there is a dispute between them, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the end of siman 122 and in siman 176. One cannot distinguish those cases as partnership law, because the Yerushalmi itself also concerns partners and nevertheless says the custodian need not hand one partner his share while the other is absent. See my comments in simanim 77 and 122.
