Two people who deposited with one person, and one of them comes to claim his share. It contains one seif:
If two people deposited property with one person and one of them comes to take what belongs to him, he is not heard until the other comes as well. This applies when the second depositor is not in the city. If he is in the city, knows that his partner made the claim, and does not come, he is presumed satisfied with what his partner is doing and the partner acts as his agent. Further details of this law are explained in siman 176, seif 25, and siman 122. If one deposited with two custodians, it is treated like lending to two borrowers. Nimukei Yosef, beginning of chapter HaMafkid. See above, siman 77.
