The language of the Tur, from Rav Yehudah's statement concerning clearing a river channel, and the baraita in Bava Metzia 108a. Rambam likewise writes in chapter 3 of Hilkhot Shekhenim, law 7. Maggid Mishneh explains that one field cannot receive water until the one above it does, so all owners participate in repairing the upper section.
