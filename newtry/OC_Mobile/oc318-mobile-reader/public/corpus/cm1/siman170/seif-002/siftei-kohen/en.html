They irrigate in order, etc. See Sema, seif katan 2, through his explanation that when the Mechaber says, 'Those situated along a river irrigate in order,' he means where an irrigation channel runs alongside the fields and they draw water from it in the direction of its flow. Rashi and Tosafot explain it this way, as do the Remazim and Rabbeinu Yerucham, netiv 31. This is unlike the Bach's explanation of the Tur and Rambam. Examine this carefully. The Gemara's objection from its failure to establish the case as one in which the water merely passes onward also proves this. What the Bach writes in response is astonishing, because according to his interpretation the answer remains difficult: there is greater reason to say that when the channel passes by one's cistern the upper owner should drink first than in a case where the water simply flows onward. Examine this carefully.
