Those who draw irrigation water from a river do so in order. If one wishes to dam the river so that the water turns toward him and he irrigates first, after which he will open the dam, while another wishes to irrigate first, whoever prevails first acquires the right. A cistern situated close to an irrigation channel, into which the river flows in the ordinary course of its movement, is filled first for the sake of peace. Some say this applies only if the cistern existed before the channel, but not if the channel preceded the cistern.
