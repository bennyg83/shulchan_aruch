This is the Gemara's explanation according to Rav. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam omitted it because it was stated only according to Rav and not Shmuel, while the Mechaber presents the Mishnah in its plain sense, as Raavad writes, in order to preserve peaceful relations. Rif likewise omitted this explanation. Beit Yosef writes that since we remain uncertain whether the halachah follows Rav, we follow the Mishnah's plain meaning. In my humble opinion Rama should have written this as 'some say.' Sema likewise notes this.
