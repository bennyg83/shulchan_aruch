There, in the Taz, s.v. 'for he did not acquire.' Note: This accords with what the Shach writes in seif katan 13. Maharit, siman 39, writes that this is derived from the verse, 'it shall be a house of God.' This nevertheless requires study, for if so, from where does the Rambam derive that a vow can take effect upon something that has not yet come into existence from 'I will surely tithe to You' and 'which you vowed to Me as a vow'? Perhaps the vow refers only to 'it shall be a house of God,' while Eretz Yisrael is regarded as already held, whereas 'I will surely tithe' was not a vow. This requires study. See my Responsa Chatam Sofer, Yoreh Deah, siman 243.
