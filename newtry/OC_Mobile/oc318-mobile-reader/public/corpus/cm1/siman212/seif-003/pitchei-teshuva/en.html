So-and-so shall reside. See Baer Heitev through his citation of the glosses to the Shach. Responsa Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, siman 9, writes that the Sema's position is the correct one. Although Rav Nachshon Gaon held otherwise, Rav Hai Gaon and most of the authorities who follow his approach maintain that even when one transfers a house to another for him to reside in, without a specified term the right is only momentary. Therefore, Rav Nachman's statement that one acquires "a house in which to reside" must refer to a case where the grantor expressly fixed a period or said "forever." This is also indicated by the Rambam, Hilchot Zechiyah U'Matanah 10:15, who distinguishes between a person on his deathbed saying, "So-and-so shall reside in this house," and saying, "Give this house to so-and-so so that he may reside in it until a specified time," as codified below in siman 253:21. The Rambam's requirement of a stated term shows that without one, the right is only for a single moment. See Chatam Sofer further, and Netivot HaMishpat for his attempt to resolve the dispute.
