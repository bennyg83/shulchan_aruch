That he reserved for him, etc. According to Rashbam’s explanation there.
(Collected note) From the upper story to the courtyard. According to Rashbam. Rabbeinu Tam explains the reverse. See Tosafos there, s.v. “on condition, etc.” The Rosh and Rabbeinu Yonah agree with Rashbam. The Gemara’s question, “Granted according to Rabbi Zeira, etc.,” required teaching that although one who sells the airspace of a courtyard ordinarily conveys nothing, here his reservation is effective, even without express wording, like the reservation of tithes. This explains the ruling here. See Rosh. (End of note.)
And if he reserved, etc. As stated in Bava Batra chapter 8: whether he wrote it at the beginning, etc., if it is phrased as a gift his words are effective. We rule there that this applies even to two people and two fields. See below siman 281 seif 6.
(Collected note) And if, etc. As stated in Bava Batra 63, if he says “for me and my sons, etc.,” his son also acquires. See what was written in siman 209 seif 7 in the gloss. (End of note.)
