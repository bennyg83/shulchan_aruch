(See there, in the gloss: He similarly reserved it for another.) In Ramban’s responsum cited by the Beit Yosef, he writes that if a person said, “I reserved a dwelling for myself and for so-and-so,” the other person does not acquire it, because there is a reservation here but no gift. It must therefore be that the case here is one where he said, “I reserved for so-and-so a dwelling that I give him as a gift.”
