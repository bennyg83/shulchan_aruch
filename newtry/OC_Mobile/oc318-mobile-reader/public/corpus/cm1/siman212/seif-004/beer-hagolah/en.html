Tet) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 23 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 5. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is straightforward.
Yod) There, halachah 6.
Kaf) The Maggid Mishneh writes that in his view Rambam compares this to a sale during the period when the Jubilee is observed. Just as one who buys during the Jubilee cycle, whether near or far from the Jubilee, may use the land as he wishes even though it is known that it will return to its owner at the Jubilee, so too one who purchases for a fixed term. The ordinary nature of a Jubilee sale is itself equivalent to an explicit fixed term. Therefore the same rule applies to an ordinary sale for a defined period. The Raavad's objections are not conclusive, while the rest of the Rambam's words and the explanations of the commentators are clear and straightforward.
