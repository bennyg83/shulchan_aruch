(Seif 7: Some say that he did not acquire.) This “some say” is the Rosh. He maintains that whether one says, “It shall belong to hekdesh,” or, “It shall be given to hekdesh,” the statement does not even constitute a vow. If he says, “I will give it,” thereby placing an obligation upon himself, it is a vow. Rambam, who is the first authority cited, maintains that even if one says, “It shall be given to hekdesh,” or, “It shall belong to hekdesh,” it is a vow. This resolves the Beit Yosef’s difficulty with the Tur. In the chapter Arbaah VaChamishah, in the case of one who struck another, the Gemara’s language appears to me to accord with the Rosh, and Tosafot likewise appears so. This is my view.
