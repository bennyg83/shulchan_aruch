[Shulchan Aruch] One who sells the substance of the land. Note: In the Ran's novellae to Gittin 84a, he writes in the name of the Raah that one who gives another land, its substance and produce, for one day, acquires the land forever, for we conclude that once it terminates, it terminates. This requires study. See Responsa Bnei Aharon in the novellae to the laws of gifts, siman 241, and Urim Gedolim, studies 47 and 48.
