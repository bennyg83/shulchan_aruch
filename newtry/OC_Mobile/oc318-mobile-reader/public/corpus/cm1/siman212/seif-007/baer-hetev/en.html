I will give it. The Tur writes in the name of Rambam, 'or I will give it to charity.' It is specifically on that formulation that the other authorities disagree, as Rama concludes. But where he says merely, 'I will give it,' all agree that he is obligated to fulfill his words by force of his vow. So writes Sema, and so writes Responsa Mahari ibn Lev, part 1, siman 59. See Responsa Ra'anach, simanim 30, 103, and 108, and Responsa Mahari ibn Lev, part 3, siman 43. Shach.
