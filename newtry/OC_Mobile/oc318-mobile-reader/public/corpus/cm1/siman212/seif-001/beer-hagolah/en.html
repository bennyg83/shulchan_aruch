Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 22 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 13.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is agreed by the Geonim and is explained from the statement in Bava Batra 147b: if a deathly ill person says, "Let so-and-so live in this house," etc. The reason is that residence is not a tangible object. The Gemara says there that a disposition unavailable to a healthy person is likewise unavailable to a deathly ill person. This is straightforward, agreed, and also explicit in the Yerushalmi.
Gimel) See above, siman 60, seif 3 in the gloss, and at the end of siman 163 in the gloss, where a different ruling is stated. See the Sema's distinction and the Hagahot Mordekhai there. This is not the place to elaborate.
