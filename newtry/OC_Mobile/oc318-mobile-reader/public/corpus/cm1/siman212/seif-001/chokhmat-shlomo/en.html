Seif 1. In the gloss: One who says, "So-and-so shall reside in this house" includes even one hour, etc. See my Torah writings to Parashat Chayei Sarah, year 5605, where I wrote that this applies specifically to a gift made to a wealthy person. If the beneficiary is poor, the donor is obligated because of a vow, as stated in siman 125 and elsewhere. In the law of vows we interpret ambiguous language stringently, and an unqualified statement therefore means indefinitely. This may also resolve the Mechaber's words in siman 60. See Even HaEzer siman 50, where a marrying couple are treated as poor for this purpose. I discussed this at length in the cited Torah essay.
