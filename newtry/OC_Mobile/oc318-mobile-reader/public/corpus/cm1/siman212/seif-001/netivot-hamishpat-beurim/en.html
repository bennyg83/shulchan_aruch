It is only an hour that Hashem has given you the house to the apartment, but when he says, “It is only one hour that he will give you the house to the apartment.” At the time of the day of the day, I will report that the Sages of Delhanna Diagon had written:
And they made a kinyan from him concerning this. Taz asks from siman 211, seif 6. I do not understand the question. Here the principal reason is that when a kinyan was made we interpret it as taken from the body of the land itself. There, where the transferor does not own the thing at all, a kinyan cannot create property from nothing, and proof of some other effective basis is required. According to Taz’s approach, even here a document that expressly says, “and we made a kinyan sudar from him,” identifying the formal kinyan, would be ineffective, yet no posek says this. The distinction is not the mere presence of a kinyan but whether the transferor presently owns a body of property from which the kinyan can be interpreted as operating.
