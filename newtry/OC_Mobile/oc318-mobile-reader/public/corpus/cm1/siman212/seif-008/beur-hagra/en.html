He acquired, etc.; some say, etc. As stated in Shevuot 26b regarding 'that which has gone forth from your lips, etc.'
Some say, etc. The Gemara asks whether the rule can be derived from consecrated property, but ordinary property is not derived from consecrated property. Kiddushin likewise says that consecration is distinct because it may take effect by thought. The first opinion maintains that although other matters are not derived from consecration, every vow is included in 'every generous-hearted person.' Hagigah derives this verse for vows generally. This is Rabbenu Tam's position, followed by Rosh and many authorities. See Yoreh Deah 258:13, Orach Chaim 562:6, and the discussions in simanim 3, 258, 553, 562, and 608. Ran adds that according to the Geonim, who generally rule according to an 'if you wish to say' conclusion, the passage in Nedarim establishes that charity is like consecration in all respects. See the end of siman 199 and Yoreh Deah 258:2.
