If one purchases land intending in his heart to consecrate it but says nothing, some say that because he fully resolved in his heart to give it to charity, he is obligated to give it. Others say that although the verse states, "Every willing heart brought offerings," we do not derive the law of ordinary property from consecrated property. Nowadays, moreover, what is called consecrated property does not have the status of property dedicated to the Beit HaMikdash but is simply charity. Therefore, unless he expresses the commitment verbally, it has no effect. One should be stringent in accordance with the first view. See Yoreh Deah, end of siman 258.
