Shin) There, siman 14, and this too appears in that responsum.
Tav) In my humble opinion this law follows from what was written above. The words beginning "and there is one who says" through the end of the siman are all the words of the Rosh in that responsum. The division marked as seif 9 is not a separate dispute introduced by the Mechaber. He did not cite the Rosh's view above because he considered the Rambam's view primary. However, when he later cited the law of one who acquired land, which came from questions addressed to the Rosh, and brought the first "some say" that when a person resolved firmly in his heart, etc., and the second "some say" that even though, etc., both of which are from the Rosh, he then completed the entire passage of the Rosh's responsum. Even though he did not rule that way, since it was included within the view he had already introduced as "some say," he did not omit the remainder.
