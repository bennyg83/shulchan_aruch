If one says, "When I acquire this land, it shall be consecrated," his words are ineffective, because a person cannot consecrate something that has not yet come into existence. If, however, he says, "When I acquire it, I will consecrate it," or, "I will give it to the Temple treasury," he has undertaken a vow and must fulfill it. Rama: This is the view of the Rosh, who disagrees with the Rambam. The Rambam holds that even if he said, "When I acquire it, it shall be consecrated," he must fulfill his words, as explained in seif 7. The Mechaber's ruling above without qualification like the Rambam, and here like the Rosh, is therefore inconsistent.
