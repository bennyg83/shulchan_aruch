The Mechaber's words, where he ruled above without qualification like Rambam, etc., are not correct. In the Derishah I wrote a sound reconciliation. Rambam holds that regarding "whatever my cow will bear" or "whatever my tree will produce," matters that arise automatically, the vow takes effect and must be fulfilled. Here, however, acquisition of a house is something outside his control and does not arise automatically, and therefore the vow does not take effect. In this way I also reconciled the Kesef Mishneh and Beit Yosef, who write that the Rosh agrees with Rambam concerning the earlier law. According to the Rosh, specifically with collecting a debt, which likewise does not occur automatically, the vow does not take effect unless he says, "When it comes into my hand, I will give it." See there and examine carefully.
