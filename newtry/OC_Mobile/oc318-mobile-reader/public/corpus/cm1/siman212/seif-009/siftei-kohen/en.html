For if he says, 'When I acquire it, it shall become consecrated,' etc. This is incorrect, for Ketubot 59a expressly states that if one says, 'This field that I sold to you shall become consecrated when I repurchase it from you,' it does not become consecrated. One must therefore explain as the Sema does in seif katan 22. In my humble opinion, however, the Rama's words are correct. The Sema's distinction between something not yet in existence and something merely outside one's possession cannot be sustained, for Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilchot Arakhin, clearly makes no such distinction. The Sema's question from Ketubot presents no difficulty. There the declaration does not create actual consecration, but the Rambam nevertheless holds that the declarant must fulfill it by the law of vows. A practical difference arises if he seeks release from his vow. This is explicit in the Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilchot Arakhin. See also the Maggid Mishneh, chapter 22 of Hilchot Mechirah, and Yoreh Deah 257.
