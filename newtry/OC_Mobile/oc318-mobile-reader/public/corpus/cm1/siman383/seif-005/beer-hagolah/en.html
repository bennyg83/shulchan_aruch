zayin) Ibid., law 12. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this law is disputed by Ravina and Rav Acha bar Rav Pappa in Sanhedrin at the end of 76b. The Rambam rules like Ravina, who imposes liability because we adjudicate dina d'garmi. Maharam likewise rules so, and the Ramban writes the same in his work on dina d'garmi. The case of 'one who presses down' is where the victim fell into water on his own and the other held him down and did not allow him to raise his head. The Rambam states, 'or he fell and the other prevented him from emerging until he died in the water.' This law is taught there in the Mishnah regarding homicide.
chet) This is the incident disputed by Rav Acha and Ravina.
