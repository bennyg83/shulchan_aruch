gimel) The language of the Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilkhot Chovel uMazik, law 6. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is from a baraita in the third chapter of Bava Kamma 28a. The phrase 'in the injured party's domain' teaches that if it occurred in the upper owner's domain, certainly not.
dalet) Ibid., law 6, and in that baraita, as the Gemara explains it there.
