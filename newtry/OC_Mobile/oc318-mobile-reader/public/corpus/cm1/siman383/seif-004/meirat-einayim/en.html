The last one is liable. He cannot say, "You should have removed your bundles when I placed mine there," as we say above in siman 381 concerning five people sitting on a bench. There the bench would have broken after some time even without the final person. Therefore the final person can argue: since you would eventually have had to stand, you should have stood immediately. Here, however, the earlier bundles would not have caused the loss without the final bundle. The same applies to overloading a ship at the end of siman 380, as explained in the Derishah to siman 381.
