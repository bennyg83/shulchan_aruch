According to the Rambam's reading, the Gemara asks: if without him the fire would have spread, it is obvious that he is liable; if even without him it would not have spread, what did he do, and he is exempt. Rashi and the Arukh, however, have a different reading and explanation, as explained in siman 418, seif 10. The Rambam's reading and explanation are correct.
