a) Rambam, at the end of chapter 4 of Hilkhot Chovel uMazik. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this was an unresolved question that was resolved at the end of the second chapter of Bava Kamma 27a, and the Rambam explains it like Rashi and Rabbeinu Chananel.
b) In the Rambam there and in the Tur in this siman, the words 'when he is bound' do not appear. Likewise, in siman 418 the Mechaber does not mention that he is bound, following Rashi as the Maggid Mishneh explains. In my humble opinion, the words found in some books are a copying error. If the Mechaber intended the view of Tosafot and the Rosh that the victim is bound, as discussed below in siman 418, he would not have omitted Tosafot's qualification that even if the owner is standing next to the ox the person is liable. For if the owner is not present, it is obvious that there is liability where the victim is bound. This is not the place to elaborate.
