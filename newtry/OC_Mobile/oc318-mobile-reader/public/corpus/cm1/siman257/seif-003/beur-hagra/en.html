Or his heirs. Rashbam, s.v. 'he sold,' and as stated in seif 5.
And if they were, etc. Rif rules this from the statement there, 'but was it not taught that we appraise, etc.' Meaning, we appraise for the purchaser who bought from the father, while the son who takes the corpus from the purchaser must pay the purchaser the value of the fruits. The Gemara answers: this is no difficulty; here, etc. Meaning, an unrelated purchaser does not waive his rights in favor of the seller's son.
And some authorities say, etc. This follows Rashbam's approach: the purchaser cannot have greater rights than the person through whom he comes. Maggid Mishneh. This also appears from Rashbam, s.v. 'and he answers,' indicating that we follow the nature of the original gift.
