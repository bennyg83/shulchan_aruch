One who consecrates land. The rule that the property was not yet consecrated, regarding one who consecrated to take effect after thirty days, refers to a case where he retracted before the thirty days elapsed. We treat the statement as uncertain whether it was a condition or retraction and therefore leave the property in the chazakah of its original owner. Ketzot HaChoshen writes similarly, and the matter is straightforward. See also what I wrote above in siman 197. This applies specifically to bedek habayit hekdesh. Charity consecration has the law of an ordinary person's transfer for this issue.
