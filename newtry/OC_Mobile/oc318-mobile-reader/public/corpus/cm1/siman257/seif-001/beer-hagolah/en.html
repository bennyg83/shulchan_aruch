Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah, halachah 13. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Mishnah in Yesh Nochalin, Bava Batra 136a, and explains that the giver transfers the body of the property from now, while the fruits pass after death.
Bet) This is the language of the Mishnah there. Rashbam explains that the father cannot sell the body and fruits completely because the body has already been transferred from now and only the fruits remain with him until death. The son likewise cannot make a complete sale. Together, however, they can effect a complete sale immediately.
