(Seif 6, he does not acquire the right to consume the produce.) Rambam writes this lest one think that the giver retains the power to retract throughout his lifetime, as with the gift of a dying person. The Gemara in the first chapter of Bava Metzia states generally, “What is a gift of a healthy person that is acquired only after death? It is like the gift of a dying person.” One might therefore have thought that this case too is included, and Maggid Mishneh cites such an opinion. Rambam teaches that it resembles the gift of a dying person only regarding the giver’s right to consume the produce, but not regarding retraction. This resolves Beit Yosef’s difficulty with the Tur for citing these words in Rambam’s name. The Tur therefore continues: “Accordingly, the recipient acquires the principal immediately, and the giver cannot retract.” This is straightforward.
