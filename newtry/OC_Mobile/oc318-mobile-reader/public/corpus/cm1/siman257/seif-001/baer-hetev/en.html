From today and after death. If the property includes movable items or debts, he must sell them, and so forth. This is the correct text, and it is the wording of the Nimukei Yosef. He concludes there: 'If the principal suffers a loss, the loss remains; the profit is not encumbered to restore it, for this was said only regarding partners.' Meaning, where one conveyed property to his son from today and after death, while retaining the fruits for his lifetime, we do not say that the fruits and profit enjoyed by the father during his lifetime become encumbered to the corpus of the property, such that if the corpus decreases while in his possession from its value when he conveyed it to his son, he must replenish that loss from the fruits he takes. It teaches that this is not so. See below, siman 176, seifim 15 and 17, where the law that profit is encumbered to principal among partners is explained. Sema.
