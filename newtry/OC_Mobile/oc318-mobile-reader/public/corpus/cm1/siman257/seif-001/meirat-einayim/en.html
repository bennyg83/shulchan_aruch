One who writes all his property to another from today and after death, and it includes movable property or debts, must sell them, etc. This is the correct reading and is the language of Nimukei Yosef. If the property written to the son from today and after death yields produce during the father's lifetime, the father may consume that produce, and if the principal later diminishes while in his hands, he need not replenish the loss from the profits he consumed. Profit is not pledged to restore principal in this case. That rule was said only regarding partners. See siman 176:15 and 17.
