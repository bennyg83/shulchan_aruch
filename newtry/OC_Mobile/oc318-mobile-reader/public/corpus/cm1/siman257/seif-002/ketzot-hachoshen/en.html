A grandson is treated as though the gift were given to a son. Darkei Moshe derives this from Terumat HaDeshen. Yet Rama elsewhere follows Rosh that a grandson is treated like an unrelated person for the doctrine that a person's intention is especially close to his child, specifically in the law of acquisition for a fetus. The present rule was likewise derived from that very doctrine of special closeness. Therefore the two rulings appear contradictory. Shach seems to have alluded to this difficulty. In my view, if the grandson is treated as another person in the fetus rule, the same should apply here. The matter requires study.
