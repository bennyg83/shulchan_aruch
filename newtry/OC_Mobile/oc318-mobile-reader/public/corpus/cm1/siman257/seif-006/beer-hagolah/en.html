There, law 14, from the baraita in chapter 8 of Bava Batra, 135b, and in the first chapter of Bava Metzia, 19a, as Abaye explains it in both places. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rashi, Rashbam, and Ramban explain in accordance with Rambam: this is not like a gift of a dying person, from which he may retract until death. It merely resembles such a gift in that the recipient does not acquire the fruits immediately.
