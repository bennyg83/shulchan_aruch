And to be supported from them. At first glance this is difficult. If he stipulated that the recipient should be supported from the assets, why should it matter that he later has property from another source? Rashba's responsum cited by Beit Yosef implies that the condition was that he should receive support from these assets when he lacked other means of support. The novelty is that even if he later acquires property from elsewhere, he need not reimburse the estate for support he consumed during the period when he had no other property.
