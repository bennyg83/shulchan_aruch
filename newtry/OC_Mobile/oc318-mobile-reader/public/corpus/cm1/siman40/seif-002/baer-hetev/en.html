His hand. The Sema notes that the word "his hand" does not appear in the Rambam or Tur. He explains that the Mechaber means that the document must be in the borrower's own handwriting, because then it is not easily falsified. The Rambam writes regarding such a document, which has no witnesses, that it must be written in a manner that cannot be altered. A document signed by witnesses creates a greater fear of falsification. See the beginning of siman 42. The Shach writes that no forced explanation is necessary. The primary reason is that since no witnesses are signed, there is concern that the holder may alter it and add text. When witnesses are signed, however, nothing can be added after the signatures. Therefore the writing must be resistant to alteration, so that no additional matter can be inserted.
