They signed and testified, etc. This is Rambam's wording. Beit Yosef explains that it does not mean they sign the document in the usual manner. Rather, they write in the document that the borrower delivered this document to the creditor in their presence and they sign testimony to that delivery. In other words, "they sign" means they sign the testimony that this document was delivered before them. In Perishah I offered another explanation as well.
