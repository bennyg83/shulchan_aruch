[Shach, letter 1] Or where the lender admitted that the other does not owe him. Note: See Urim, who writes that this applies specifically where the admission was made in error. Otherwise, one may say that he intended to transfer the right through an admission, which is a complete mode of acquisition, as is evident from the incident of Isur Giyora. It appears to me that even without this, the law would be that he must pay, as stated in siman 245:4 regarding one who says, "I gave," where we can say that perhaps he transferred it through another person. This is the sugya in Gittin 40b. So too here, perhaps he transferred to him, through another person, an obligation concerning something he did not previously owe. Even according to Reish Lakish, at least through a kinyan one can undertake such an obligation.
