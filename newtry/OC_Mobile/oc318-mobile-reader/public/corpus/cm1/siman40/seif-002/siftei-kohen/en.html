And he gave it to the lender before witnesses, etc. It is valid according to the opinion that delivery witnesses give legal force. Other authorities disagree, as explained at the end of siman 51. See there.
Provided that it is in his handwriting, etc. The word "his" does not appear in the Rambam, Tur, or Rabbeinu Yerucham. The Mechaber apparently means only to explain that there must be the obligor's handwriting or some other known writing that cannot be altered. The reason is that where no witnesses are signed, we fear that the holder will add text. If witnesses are signed, he cannot add anything after their signatures. This is not, as the Sema strains to say, merely because one is afraid to forge a witnessed document. The requirement is a form of writing in which no further text can be added.
