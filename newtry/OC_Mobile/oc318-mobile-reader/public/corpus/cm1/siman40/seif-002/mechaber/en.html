If a borrower writes a document in his own hand, has witnesses attest it, and gives it to the lender, it is a valid document. Likewise, if he writes a document without signed witnesses and delivers it to the lender before witnesses, it creates a documented loan, provided that the handwriting cannot be forged and the witnesses to delivery read it. Some Geonim ruled that he must tell the witnesses before whom it was delivered, 'Sign and attest that it was delivered before you.'
