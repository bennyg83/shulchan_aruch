[Shulchan Aruch] Be witnesses for me that I owe. Note: The Shach below, siman 81, subparagraph 49, holds that even where a person made a full admission in a manner where joking is not applicable, and the admission was made in the claimant’s presence so that the defense of attempting to induce a false admission does not apply, he still cannot obligate himself unless he says, "You are my witnesses." Certainly, where he could claim that he was joking, even if he admits that he truly intended by his admission to obligate himself, it is ineffective. The Sema, subparagraph 2, writes this explicitly. Urim VeTumim there disagrees and holds that saying "You are my witnesses" adds nothing to the creation of liability. The essential point is that whenever he intended a genuine admission without joking, he can obligate himself. See there.
