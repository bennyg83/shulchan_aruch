One who says to witnesses, "Be witnesses for me," etc. In Derishah I explained the source Rambam relied upon for this law and that many authorities disagree. They hold that an oral statement of this kind can help only as an admission of an already-existing debt. To create a new obligation now through speech alone is ineffective unless he writes such an undertaking in a document or says, "I owe you one maneh by document," which Rambam himself accepts. The Tur, although quoting Rambam's language, omitted this opening rule because he did not accept it. It is therefore surprising that the Mechaber and Rama state it without recording any dissent.
