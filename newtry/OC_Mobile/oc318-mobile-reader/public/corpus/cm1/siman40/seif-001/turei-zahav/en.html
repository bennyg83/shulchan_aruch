In the Beit Yosef. In the passage beginning, "One who says to his fellow, I owe you," etc., until "and it is not his handwriting, meaning," etc. The wording can be understood literally. Below, siman 69, seif 5, the Shulchan Aruch rules in the name of Ramban that one's handwriting is effective even without a signature. The Beit Yosef there, in seif 1, cites Baal HaTerumot in the name of Ramban that Rashi explained likewise. Accordingly, Rambam's intention here is that even though it is not his handwriting, it is effective. If it were his handwriting, it would be like a signature. When Rashi explains in Perek HaNose that had he signed it, etc., his meaning is handwriting, which is equivalent to a signature. Examine carefully.
