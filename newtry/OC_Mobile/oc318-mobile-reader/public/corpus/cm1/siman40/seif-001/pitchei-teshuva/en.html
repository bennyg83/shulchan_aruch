As to the substance of the Shach’s ruling, the Baal HaTerumot writes the same at the end of Gate 64, and the Beit Yosef cites him at the end of this siman. See the Taz, in the gloss of Maharashak, who writes concerning this Baal HaTerumot that the Rif and Rosh do not appear to agree, for otherwise why would they have written that the Yerushalmi disagrees with our Gemara, when the Yerushalmi plainly speaks of a case in which an error occurred?
