A gift ordinarily carries no responsibility, and the giver is liable only to the extent he expressly accepted. But if he accepted general responsibility without qualification, he must exclude responsibility for the possibility that the property was stolen. Otherwise he has a financial interest in defeating the present adverse claimant, because if that claimant wins he must reimburse the donee. He need only exclude liability toward this specific successful claimant; he may remain responsible for other third-party claims and still be disinterested as to this litigation. Sema.
