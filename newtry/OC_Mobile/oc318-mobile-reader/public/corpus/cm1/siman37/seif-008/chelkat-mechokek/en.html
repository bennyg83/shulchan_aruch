Or through herself. According to those who explain the reason as though the father said, "Whoever gives you a maneh, I consider it as though he gave it to me, and become betrothed with it," the same should apply even to another minor girl, for she is no worse than a dog designated to receive the money. But the Rosh writes that although a minor generally has no agency, for her own benefit and acquisition she may act as agent. According to this, the rule applies specifically through the daughter herself and not through some unrelated minor. See the Rosh, who also writes that this is not comparable to the law of a guarantor and distinguishes between kiddushin by money and by document, and between whether the father spoke with the man performing the kiddushin or did not. See also the Ran at the beginning of Kiddushin.
