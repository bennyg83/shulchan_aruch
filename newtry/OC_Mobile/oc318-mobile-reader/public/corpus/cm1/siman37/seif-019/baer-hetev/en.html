The synagogue. The same applies to sacred vessels and gold ornaments used to beautify a Torah scroll, and similar items. Thus writes the Shach in the name of Rashdam, siman 16. The Sema writes that nevertheless a synagogue is not exactly comparable to a Torah scroll. Regarding a synagogue one must remove one's personal interest even if there is another synagogue, unlike a Torah scroll, as explained in siman 7. See there.
