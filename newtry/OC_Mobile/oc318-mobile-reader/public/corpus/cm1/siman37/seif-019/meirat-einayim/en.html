Where they have another Sefer Torah, it is permitted. In that case even withdrawal from the communal interest is unnecessary. He may listen to and read from this Sefer Torah, since another Sefer Torah is available and mitzvot were not given for personal benefit. So the Rosh writes in responsum, rule 3, siman 13. According to this, Rama's statement that the same applies to similar matters, such as a synagogue, is not an exact parallel. I already wrote above in siman 7, citing Rabbeinu Yerucham, that with a synagogue withdrawal is required even if another synagogue is available. See there.
