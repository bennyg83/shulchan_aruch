If a Sefer Torah belonging to the townspeople was stolen, since it is intended for communal reading and no individual can divest himself of his interest in it, the case may not be adjudicated by judges from that town, nor may proof be brought from residents of that town. The same applies to every similar case. Gloss: See above, siman 7, seif 12, that if they have another Sefer Torah it is permitted. The same applies to every comparable matter, such as a synagogue or other books and the like: the townspeople may serve as judges concerning them where the stated condition is met.
