Even if the money was consumed before he heard. Although the kiddushin money is no longer extant, and one might ask upon what the kiddushin can now take effect, nevertheless she becomes betrothed by the benefit of having his will fulfilled. This is analogous to the benefit of forgiveness of a loan. This is the view of the Ramah, author of this opinion. See Responsa Maharik, shoresh 32, page 23, who likewise explains the Ramah's view and brings several proofs that we find similar cases in which kiddushin take effect even after the money has been consumed.
