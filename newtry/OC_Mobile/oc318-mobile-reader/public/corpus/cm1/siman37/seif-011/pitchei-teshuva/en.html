They are not judged by the judges of that city. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot HaTur note 24, writes that the baraita's rule that the case is not judged by that city's judges and proof is not brought from the city's residents was said only with respect to outsiders. As for the residents themselves, since we are witnesses that from the outset they accepted these judges and trusted these residents, they are plainly believed. This is the view of Re'em, part 1, siman 12. Birkei Yosef, note 4, writes that Perach Mateh Aharon, part 1, siman 4, questioned this ruling; Darkei Noam, Choshen Mishpat, siman 22, struggled to defend it; and Rabbi Mahari, in a manuscript responsum, wrote that all the decisors disagree with Re'em on this matter. See there, and what I write below in seif 22, seif katan 16.
