Shimon, one of the witnesses to the gift, is disqualified. Meaning the gift itself is void as against the donor's attempt to retract, even though another witness joined Shimon, where the donor claims Shimon has an interest because he wishes to testify in order to free himself from dealing with the first litigant and prefers the second. This is the explicit case in the Tur. It differs from the earlier rule regarding one witness to a debt document that was sold to him, where the concern is whether the debtor can say the witness signed falsely so that he could collect. There the seller is not seeking to undo the sale. Rama refers to siman 66 because both rules are arranged there next to one another.
