There is no substance to that admission. Once witnesses have testified they cannot retract. We do not say that they are believed at least as to their own shares and must return them because they now admit the earlier testimony was true and have a migo of silence. The reason the source says they are not believed to harm their fellow's rights because of the rule against retraction, rather than simply saying their new claim is supported by migo, is that the law applies even before they collected the debt, when such a migo would be a migo to extract. As to their own portions, if they have not yet collected they plainly cannot collect contrary to their admission. If they already collected, however, we do not remove the money from them. This explains why the Shulchan Aruch and the responsum of Chazeh HaTenufah say nothing about returning their own shares, and it is evidence against Yam Shel Shlomo cited above at the end of siman 32.
