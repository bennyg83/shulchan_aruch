Invalid. Meaning that the gift is void even though another witness testified together with him. This applies where Reuven seeks to retract the gift and argues that Shimon was an interested witness. This differs from seif 7, where one sells a promissory note to another and the ruling is that the arrangement is valid. There the meaning is that the purchaser may collect from the borrower, and the borrower cannot argue that the witness signed falsely merely so that the purchaser would be able to collect from him. Thus the Sema.
