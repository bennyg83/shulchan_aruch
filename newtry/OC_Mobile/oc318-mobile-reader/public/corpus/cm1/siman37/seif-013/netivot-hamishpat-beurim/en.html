A borrower against whom others challenge, etc. This disqualification applies specifically where the property in question is land subject to a lien. Movables are different. The basis of disqualification is that one who testifies concerning an object encumbered to his creditor is effectively testifying about something tied to his own obligation. Ordinary movables are not subject to that continuing lien. Therefore if a person has no land and comes to testify concerning movables, he is not disqualified merely because a creditor may later wish to collect. Otherwise anyone owing even a single dinar would be disqualified from virtually every commercial or movable-property testimony. The same logic applies to an oral creditor, who has no specific lien on property in the ordinary sense.
