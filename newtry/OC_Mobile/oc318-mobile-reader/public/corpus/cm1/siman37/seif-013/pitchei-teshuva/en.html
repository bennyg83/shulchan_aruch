Any possibility of benefit in this testimony. Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes in his gloss: If a witness tells a litigant, "I do not wish to fix any fee for myself from you. If you prevail, it will be up to you whether you wish to give me something," he is an interested witness, because it is certain the litigant will pay him. This is from Beit Yosef at the end of this siman. See Pitchei Teshuva, Even HaEzer, siman 17, seif katan 19. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot HaTur note 46, writes in the name of Mahari ben Lev, part 1, siman 34, that even if their only benefit is the satisfaction of having their will accomplished, they are called interested witnesses. In siman 41 Mahari ben Lev writes that where it is evident to everyone that the witnesses testify because of hatred and quarrel between the parties, they are considered interested; Rashdam, Yoreh De'ah, siman 111, writes similarly. Birkei Yosef, note 6, observes that Admat Kodesh, Choshen Mishpat, siman 25, challenged Mahari ben Lev's statement in part 1 from Mahari ben Lev's own responsa, part 3, simanim 119 and 121, and the matter requires resolution. See Ketzot HaChoshen, seif katan 2 and 4, and Sha'ar Mishpat, seif katan 1.
