He is not faithful to his wife, O Lord, who is afraid of Hashem’s promise, and he will not be saved. And there is no one here, and there is no thanks to Hashem’s mercy, and now he says, “And he will be faithful to him, and he will be faithful to him, and he will be blessed with him, but he will not be able to do so, but he will not be able to do so with him, and he is not like a single one.”
