A kablan, etc. Rashbam at the beginning of Bava Batra 47a appears to define a kablan as one who received the money from the lender and gave it to the borrower, allowing the lender to collect first from either party. I doubt Rashbam wrote this. In such a case the original borrower is released from the lender and the kablan himself is the borrower. The Gemara at the end of Gittin Pashut defines a kablan as one who says, 'Give him and I will give you,' or, 'Give him and I will repay.' This is the definition stated by all the authorities and explained below in siman 129:18-19.
Some say, etc. From the words of the Rambam and the Mechaber it appears that even a guarantor who undertook direct liability may testify. Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 2, part 5, writes likewise, as does the Beit Yosef. This also appears to be the view of the Baal HaIttur, who writes at the end of folio 39 that some say he may testify and some say he may not, and that we rule leniently. His meaning is that the land remains where it presently stands, and the witness may testify because the burden of proof rests upon the claimant.
