The kiddushin grew with her. See Maharik, shoresh 32, who discusses this rule at length, as well as the law of an orphan girl whose kiddushin continued as she matured, whether her status becomes kiddushin by Torah law or remains rabbinic. I have not seen a responsum of Rashba that disputes this. Rashba merely states the clear case that if he had marital relations with her after she matured, we certainly regard her as betrothed by Torah law; he does not address the case without relations.
