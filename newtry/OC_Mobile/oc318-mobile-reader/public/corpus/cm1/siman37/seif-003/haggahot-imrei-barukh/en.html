(Shach, seif katan 10) In truth, the responsum of the Ritva does not say so, etc. Note: Rashi in many places describes the benefit of discretion as "a small amount." It may be that we do not suspect a witness of lying for a small benefit, as also appears from the Sema below, seif katan 29, in the name of the Tur. But if the benefit of discretion itself has substantial monetary value, a witness related to the charity treasurer is like a witness related directly to a litigant, because for the status of a litigant there is no distinction between a small and a large financial interest.
