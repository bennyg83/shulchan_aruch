Before they testified, etc. The Sema cites the Darkei Moshe in the name of Meisharim that a litigant cannot join an outsider to disqualify witnesses testifying against him, even where another set of witnesses is available and he therefore appears to lack an interest. This formulation is too broad. Meisharim means that even if the opposing party originally claimed to have two sets, the defendant remains interested in disqualifying the first, because the claimant need not produce the second set and the defendant may hope no other set exists. Thus he cannot join another to disqualify the first set. But if the second set is actually before us and independently proves the claim, the defendant may no longer be interested. The Gemara in Sanhedrin supports this distinction.
