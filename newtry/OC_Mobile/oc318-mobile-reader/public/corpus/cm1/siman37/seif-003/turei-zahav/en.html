(Seif 7: to one of the signatories.) Tosafot in the chapter Elu Terefot, folio 47, write that a scrupulous person should nevertheless distance himself from this. Darkhei Moshe cites Rivash that if the witnesses have not yet signed, even if the matter was already entered in the scribe’s memorandum and one witness subsequently acquired an interest, he may not sign afterward.
