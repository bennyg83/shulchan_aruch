And he alone will be liable for all of it. See VeShav HaKohen, end of siman 44, who expresses surprise at the Shulchan Aruch. Since Shulchan Aruch rules in siman 77, seif 1, that two people who borrowed together are ordinary guarantors for one another, not full contractual guarantors, and we hold at the end of siman 49 that whenever the borrower is exempt the guarantor is likewise exempt, even when the guarantor knows the borrower is lying, how can the present ruling stand? He leaves the matter in serious difficulty. See Tumim at the end of siman 49 and siman 129, seif katan 6, and what I write there in siman 129, seif 8, seif katan 6.
