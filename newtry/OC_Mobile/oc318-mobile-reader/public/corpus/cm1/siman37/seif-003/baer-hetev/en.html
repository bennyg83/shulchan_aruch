Before. Meisharim writes that a litigant may not testify that witnesses who come to obligate him are disqualified, even where he has no personal interest in that particular testimony, such as where another set of witnesses also exists. This is likewise implied in chapter 2 of Sanhedrin. Thus the Sema. The Shach writes that this applies specifically where the other set is not presently before us. If the other set is already present, the litigant may testify that the first set is disqualified, and this is also implied by that Gemara. The Taz writes that there is a scribal error in Meisharim and that the reference should be to chapter Zeh Borer. He therefore rejects this rule as stated. See there.
