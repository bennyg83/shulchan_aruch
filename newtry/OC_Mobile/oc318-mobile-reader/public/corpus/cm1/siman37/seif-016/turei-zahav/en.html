(See the Beit Yosef, beginning “The Rosh wrote: provided he owns land corresponding to his debt, etc.” through “and Hagahot Ashri contains the first answer, etc.”) This is imprecise. There are not two answers regarding a guarantor. Tosafot’s distinction concerns testimony of a lender for a borrower, a topic not discussed at all in Hagahot Ashri. Regarding a lender, however, the Tur follows Tosafot’s first distinction. The Beit Yosef abbreviated Tosafot’s words. May his Master forgive him.
