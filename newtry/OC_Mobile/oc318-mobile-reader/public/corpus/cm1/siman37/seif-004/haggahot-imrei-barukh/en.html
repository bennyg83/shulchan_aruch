(Mechaber, seif 10) Even though if he becomes wealthy, etc. Note: See the Yerushalmi at the end of chapter 2 of Yevamot: 'and all of them who had wives,' etc., because a person is not ordinarily found sinning for a benefit that may come only later. Korban HaEdah explains: a person does not sin now because of a doubtful benefit that may come to him in the future.
