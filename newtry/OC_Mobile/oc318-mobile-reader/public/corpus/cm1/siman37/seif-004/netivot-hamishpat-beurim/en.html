One denied and the other admitted. Urim VeTumim writes that the one who admits may testify through a migo where the lender's own claim is uncertain. I do not agree. The admitting partner is not merely a neutral witness. If the other partner does not pay, he himself can become liable, and whatever he pays may generate a reimbursement claim against his fellow. He is therefore also a potential claimant. A migo does not transform such a litigant into an ordinary witness, particularly where the result would help him extract money. This differs from a neutral agent whose migo removes all personal interest.
