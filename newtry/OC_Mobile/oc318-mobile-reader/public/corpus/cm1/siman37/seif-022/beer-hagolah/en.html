Responsa of the Rosh, rule 5, siman 4, and likewise Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 32 part 1, Responsa Rashba, Hagahot Maimoniyot relevant to Hilkhot Edut, and Responsa Rivash, simanim 191 and 161.
The Tur writes in siman 55 that one who comes to testify in a matter in which one litigant has a grievance against him is an interested witness. See there. Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 2 part 5, writes that a litigant cannot testify that witnesses coming to obligate him are disqualified, even if he has another group of witnesses, and even if he initially said, 'I have two groups of witnesses.' This is explained in Sanhedrin 23b.
