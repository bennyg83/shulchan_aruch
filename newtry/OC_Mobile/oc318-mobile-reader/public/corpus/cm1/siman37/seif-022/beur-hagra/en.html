Now. Sanhedrin 24a.
Any matter dependent. Since those disqualified for ordinary testimony and a single witness are fit, as written above, and Yevamot 117b states: Rav Nachman said, "wherever the Sages believed, etc."
Likewise we do not say, as stated at the end of chapter 3 of Eruvin 41a, "and there was no person who protested, etc."
