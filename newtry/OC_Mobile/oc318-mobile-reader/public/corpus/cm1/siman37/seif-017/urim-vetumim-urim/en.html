To one of the signatories. But not to two of them. See below, siman 66. Darkei Moshe says witnesses who signed a sale may later purchase the property, though the Sages teach one to distance himself from conduct that appears improper. Regarding a judge who adjudicated the matter and later wishes to acquire it, Tosafot in Chullin records two approaches: perhaps a judge who ruled alone may purchase because his judicial role itself rebuts suspicion, and the stricter rule concerning one who prohibited a woman by vow and then wishes to marry her reflects special concern that he had set his eyes upon her; or perhaps even a sole judge should not purchase. The two Tosafot answers dispute this.
