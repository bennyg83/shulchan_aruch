If he sold the stolen garment to Levi, etc. Tosafot infer from Rashbam's statement that Reuven sold it to Levi without warranty that if the sale carried warranty, Shimon would be an interested witness. Their reasoning is that even after the owner's despair and a change of possession, Shimon could remove the article from Levi because Levi would suffer no loss, since he could recover from Reuven. This does not appear correct. Why should the absence of financial loss allow Shimon to remove an article that Levi already acquired through despair and change of possession? Levi may want the article itself rather than its value. He may also wish to avoid litigation with Reuven, or Reuven may be unable to pay. Furthermore, the authorities everywhere state without qualification that despair and change of possession effect acquisition, which includes an ordinary sale carrying implied warranty. Even were Rashbam discussing a sale without warranty, the same objection would arise from the market regulation, under which the original owner reimburses the purchaser. The truth is that once the purchaser acquires through despair and change of possession, the acquisition is complete even if the seller later returns his money. Rashbam's mention of a sale without warranty teaches an additional point, not a limitation. Nimukei Yosef cites Rabbi Yehudah son of the Rosh as explaining that with warranty there is no true change of possession and the object remains as though in the robber's domain. I cannot accept this, for warranty has no bearing on change of possession. All the authorities in simanim 353, 356, 361, and elsewhere state the rule without distinguishing between a sale with or without warranty. The Rambam, Tur, and Mechaber here likewise specify 'without warranty' in the preceding seif but omit that qualification here. Thus there is no distinction, and this is the correct ruling. The Bach wondered why Tosafot's qualification is not cited by the poskim. The answer is that they do not accept it; rather, Tosafot's position itself requires study. 350
