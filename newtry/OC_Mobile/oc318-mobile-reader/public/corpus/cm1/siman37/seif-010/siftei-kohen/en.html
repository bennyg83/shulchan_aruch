Whenever at present, etc. The Sema asks that the Gemara nevertheless disqualifies the witness because he wishes to avoid being called a wicked borrower who does not pay. He suggests a person known not to care about such a reputation. I hesitate, because this is a matter of the heart and cannot readily be established; the Gemara and authorities treat the concern as general. Rather, the ruling has application where that concern does not arise for an objective reason, such as a seller without creditors whose only possible claimants are tort victims. According to authorities who allow tort claimants to collect from encumbered property, he may testify because he never borrowed from them and is not called a wicked borrower. Other similar cases are also possible. See below, siman 123, seif katan 23.
