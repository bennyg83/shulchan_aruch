If it presently has no produce, etc. Although produce will grow later and he will take his share, he will not testify falsely merely for that reason, since many other fields are available for him to work and take a share as an aris. This is the view of Ramah and Rosh. Rashbam disagrees and holds that because he already invested labor in this field he may not testify. If he receives from this field more than other sharecroppers normally receive, it appears that all agree he may not testify. The Rama may have intended this qualification when he wrote, "if he has no other benefit." Sema.
