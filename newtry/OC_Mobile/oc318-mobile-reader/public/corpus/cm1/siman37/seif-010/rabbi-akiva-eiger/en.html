[Shulchan Aruch] Since they accepted them upon themselves. Note: Responsa Yachin UBoaz, part 1, siman 5, writes that this applies only with respect to adult orphans, but they are not believed against minor orphans. See at length the responsum of Rabbi Yaakov Buli at the end of his Sefer Ezras Nashim, siman 2, and Responsa Re’em, part 1, siman 12.
