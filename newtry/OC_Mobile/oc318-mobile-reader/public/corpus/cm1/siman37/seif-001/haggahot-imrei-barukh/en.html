(Sema, seif katan 5) As we say concerning brothers who divided, etc. Note: The Taz, siman 175, seif 4, distinguishes and rules decisively that when partners divide and one partner's share is later taken from him, he has no claim against the other. This requires study. If the property is taken because it was stolen property, the division was certainly made in error. The reason the law concerning brothers who divided requires the principle that a son stands in his father's place applies instead to collection by a creditor who collected from only one of them.
