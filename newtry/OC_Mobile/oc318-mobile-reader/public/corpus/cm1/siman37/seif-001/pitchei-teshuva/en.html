An interest in the matter. See Baer Heitev, who cites Sema's explanation that the disqualification of an interested witness is because we suspect him of falsehood, contrary to the Levush. Although the Shach considers the possible source of the Levush's position, he ultimately agrees with Sema. Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes in his gloss that Ezrat Nashim, siman 17, seif katan 30, proves the same from siman 140, seif 9. Netivot HaMishpat explains a practical difference. If a witness signed a document before becoming interested in its outcome and only later became interested, then if the disqualification is not because of suspicion of falsehood but is analogous to kinship, other witnesses may authenticate his signature, just as where one signed before becoming the signatory's son-in-law, siman 46, seif 35. If the reason is suspicion that he may lie, however, even others cannot authenticate his signature, just as where one signed before becoming a robber, siman 46, seif 34. The principal view is that of Sema and Shach, that an interested witness is disqualified because of suspicion. Ne'ot Deshe, siman 68, rules similarly and challenges Ketzot HaChoshen, siman 34, seif katan 4. He also notes that this follows from Shulchan Aruch, siman 66, seif 21, and from Bava Batra 159a concerning one who signed before property fell to him by inheritance. This refutes those who say that an interested witness is disqualified merely as a relative rather than because of suspicion. He concludes that in the very case discussed, where a person signed before becoming interested and later became interested, it is clear that others may not authenticate his signature unless witnesses saw the document before he became interested. In other cases where there may be a practical distinction between treating an interested witness as a relative and treating him as suspected of falsehood, it remains uncertain whether the party in possession can plead kim li according to the view that treats him as a relative. See there, also Mishkenot Yaakov to Even HaEzer siman 17 in the addenda, seif 3, and Noda BiYehuda, Even HaEzer, siman 27.
