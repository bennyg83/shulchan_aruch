The first purchaser may testify for the second purchaser. For example, Reuven sold a field to Shimon in Nisan, and afterward sold another field to Levi in Iyar. If someone comes to remove Levi's field, whether by witnesses that Reuven had stolen it from him or by a debt document establishing that Reuven owes him money, Shimon may not testify for Levi to preserve that field in Levi's possession unless another field remains in the possession of Reuven, or of another purchaser from Reuven, worth enough to cover the value of Shimon's own field should a claim later arise against it. Otherwise Shimon is an interested witness, because if Levi's field is taken, there may no longer be sufficient property from which Shimon can recover his purchase price if his own field is later taken.
