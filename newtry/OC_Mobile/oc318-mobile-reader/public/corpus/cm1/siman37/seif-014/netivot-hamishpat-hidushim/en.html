The first purchaser. For example, Yaakov sold a field to Reuven in Nisan and another to Shimon in Iyar. If someone comes to remove Shimon's field, Reuven, the first purchaser, cannot testify in support of Shimon unless another field remains with value sufficient to cover Reuven's purchase. Otherwise Reuven is interested in preserving property against which he can exercise his warranty if his own field is later taken. If another sufficient field remains, his warranty remains protected and he may testify. Sema.
