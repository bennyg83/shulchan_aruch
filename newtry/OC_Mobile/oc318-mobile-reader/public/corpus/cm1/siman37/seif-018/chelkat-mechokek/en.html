Explicitly after the kiddushin. This is Rambam's view, and according to some explanations also the Rif's. The Ran is uncertain about the Rif and suggests that even if the father consented at the time of the kiddushin, perhaps she is still not betrothed. See Maharik, who distinguishes a case where she was betrothed in the father's presence and he remained silent, and only afterward expressed consent; there the kiddushin are effective.
