If Reuven sold a field to Shimon without a guarantee and Yehudah challenges Shimon's title in order to remove the field from him, Reuven may not testify for Shimon about it. Although Reuven gave no guarantee, he wants the field to remain with Shimon so that Reuven's creditor can later seize it for his debt, thereby preventing Reuven from becoming a wicked borrower who does not repay. {Rama: This applies specifically when Yehudah challenges the title derived from the seller. If he challenges it on account of the buyer, claiming that the buyer owes him money, the seller may testify concerning the field (Tur).}
