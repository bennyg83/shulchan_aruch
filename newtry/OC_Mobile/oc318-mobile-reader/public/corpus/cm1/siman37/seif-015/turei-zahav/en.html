(In the Beit Yosef, beginning “The Hagahot Ashri write according to Rashi and Rashbam, etc.” through “but according to Rabbenu Hananel and the Rif, etc.”) This requires study, for the Rif copied the Gemara’s resolution that the case is where they made a kinyan from him. Rabbenu Yonah and Rabbenu Yitzhak likewise write so at the beginning of the chapter HaKotev.
