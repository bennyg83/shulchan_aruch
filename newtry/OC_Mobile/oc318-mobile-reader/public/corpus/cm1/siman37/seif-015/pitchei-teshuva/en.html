To receive witnesses from the community. Birkei Yosef, note 9, writes that when Rashba and Rosh state that the custom is to accept witnesses from the city and not require witnesses from another city, this is not limited to a city. The same applies to members of an association: testimony may be accepted from members of that association without requiring witnesses who are not members. This is from Ra'avad Shimon ben Tzemach, in a responsum of Mahari Ashkenazi, Torat Chaim, part 3, siman 23. See there further.
