(Seif 3: before they testified.) The Sema’s citation in seif katan 15 from Meisharim, based on the second chapter of Sanhedrin, contains a scribal error. The reference should be to the chapter Zeh Borer, where the matter is disputed. The wording of Darkhei Moshe is therefore imprecise, for if the witness is not interested, why should he be unable to disqualify the opposing witnesses? In Zeh Borer he is regarded as interested because he need not clarify and present every item of proof; see there.
