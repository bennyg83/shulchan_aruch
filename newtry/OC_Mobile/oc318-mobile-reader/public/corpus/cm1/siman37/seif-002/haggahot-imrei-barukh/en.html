(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 3) Since brothers who divided are treated as purchasers, etc. Note: This requires study. Before partners divide, if a creditor comes to collect from one partner, he can collect only the half belonging to the debtor. If beit din gives him the property, the field is divided: half remains with the non-debtor partner and the creditor takes the debtor's half. Why, then, if the partners themselves divided, should the creditor gain a right to collect even from the share now held by the partner who owes him nothing?
