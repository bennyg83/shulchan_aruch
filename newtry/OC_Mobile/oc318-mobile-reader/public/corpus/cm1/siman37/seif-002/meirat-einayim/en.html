If it has fruit. Yes, he is the final and has many meanings in his column:
If it has no produce, he may testify. Even though produce will later grow and he will receive his share as an agricultural tenant, this does not make him interested in the testimony, because there are many other fields in which he could work and receive the usual tenant's share. This follows Ramah and the Rosh as cited by the Tur. Rashbam is stricter even where he merely labored in the field, while Ri is more lenient and allows testimony even where produce is present unless he was promised more than ordinary tenants receive.
