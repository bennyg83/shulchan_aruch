For kiddushin do not take effect with a nonviable newborn. The Yerushalmi's statement that a one-day-old girl may be betrothed refers to a case where it is known that she completed the full term of gestation, or where she survived thirty days after the kiddushin, thereby revealing retroactively that she was not a nonviable newborn. It appears that in the case of a doubtful nonviable newborn the kiddushin themselves are doubtful. Thus, if a man betrothed the girl's sister and afterward betrothed the sister's newborn daughter, both would require a get out of doubt. See below at the end of siman 40, where according to Rambam even a recognizable fetus can be betrothed.
