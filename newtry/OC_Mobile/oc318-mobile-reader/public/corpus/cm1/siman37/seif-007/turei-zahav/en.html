(Seif 17 in the gloss: because he admitted the matter.) Why can he not later say, “I admitted only so that the claimant would not take it,” as in siman 47 regarding a trust document, where one may say that he made the statement merely to ward off his creditor? There the document remains in his possession, so the more plausible assumption is that it has not been paid, and his statement is understood as intended to delay his creditor. Here that presumption is absent.
