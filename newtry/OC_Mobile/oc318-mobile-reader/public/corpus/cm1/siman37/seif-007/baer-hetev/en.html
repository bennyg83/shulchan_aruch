Of the signatories. This means after they signed the document. If they had not yet signed it and had only signed the scribe's memorandum, and before the formal document was signed the lender transferred the debt to one of the witnesses, that witness may not sign afterward because he is personally interested in the testimony and is therefore unfit to sign initially. So writes Rivash. Darkei Moshe writes that regarding a judge who adjudicated the matter and later wishes to acquire it, see Tosafot in the chapter Elu Terefot. Thus writes the Sema. The Shach writes that below, siman 66, seif 21, he proved that even if the claim was sold to two witnesses it is valid after the fact; nevertheless they should not both acquire it initially because of suspicion. See Responsa Maharit, siman 89.
