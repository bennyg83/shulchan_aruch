[Shulchan Aruch] The witness has a side of benefit. Note: If the witness said to the litigant, "I do not wish you to set a fixed fee for me, but if you prevail, it shall be up to you whether you wish to give me anything," he is considered interested in the matter, because it is certain that the litigant will pay him. Beit Yosef, end of this siman.
