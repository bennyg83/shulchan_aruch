(Sema, seif katan 24) But this requires study, etc. Note: The Sema's difficulty can be answered in several ways. By way of example, consider a city where the wealthy are compelled to contribute to the needs of the poor. They cannot testify that a certain person said, 'Give one hundred to the poor of the city.' But persons who are neither wealthy nor among the poor receiving support may testify, even though if they later become wealthy they will benefit from their testimony, because at that point they would have an obligation to contribute to the needs of the poor and their required contribution would be reduced because of the amount gained by the poor through this testimony.
