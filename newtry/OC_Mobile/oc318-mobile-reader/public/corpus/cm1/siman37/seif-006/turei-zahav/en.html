(Reuven may testify for him, etc.) One might object that Reuven benefits by avoiding the purchaser’s resentment, as Tosafot state in Hezkat HaBatim, folio 43. From there the Tur proves that the case must involve a sale without responsibility and that Shimon knew the property did not belong to the seller, as the Beit Yosef explains. Indeed, this case too must involve a purchaser who knew. Although the Tur writes that according to the Rosh such knowledge is unnecessary, that applies only to exemption from repayment under Rav Pappa. To remove the concern of resentment, knowledge is still required. This distinction is relevant generally to whether the purchaser may recover from the seller, and it is necessary according to Tosafot, since the Gemara’s case of the cow and cloak in this seif is explicit.
