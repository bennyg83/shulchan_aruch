a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 12. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Mishnah, baraita, and Gemara in Bava Metzia 105a. The ruling follows Rabbi Yannai and Reish Lakish's explanation of his words. Below that minimum amount he is not obligated to tend the crop, because the undertaking 'I will set a heap before you' is not fulfilled.
b) In my humble opinion this requires study, because all the authorities wrote this only regarding the next law, where he failed to work the field and left it fallow. Therefore the Mechaber omitted there the formulation found in the Mishnah, Rambam, and Tur, 'Thus he writes to him: If I leave it fallow and do not work it, I will pay from the best,' meaning that this applies even if it was not expressly written, as Tosafot and the Rosh explain there, and as the Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of the Ramban, Rashba, and Rabbeinu Yitzchak of Barcelona. All of them discuss only where he left the entire field fallow, and this also appears from the Gemara there.
