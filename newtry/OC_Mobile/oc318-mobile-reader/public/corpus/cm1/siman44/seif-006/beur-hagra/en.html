(Collected note) And some invalidate. This depends on the dispute above in seif 5. There the Mechaber states the first opinion without qualification. In siman 45, seif 11, however, he states the latter opinion without qualification, writing "and if they began, etc." These are the Rosh's words, following his own approach. Although one can distinguish, the truth of the matter points in this direction. In Even HaEzer, siman 125, seif 19, the Mechaber states the first opinion without qualification, following the Rambam there. See Shach, seif katan 13. (End of collected note.)
