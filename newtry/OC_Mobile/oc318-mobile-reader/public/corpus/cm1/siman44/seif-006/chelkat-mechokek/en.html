Because kiddushin do not take effect with them. Above in siman 15 it was explained that if one betroths the sister of his betrothed, rabbinic kiddushin are nevertheless recognized lest people say the first kiddushin were conditional. The same applies to the betrothed of one's brother, in accordance with Rabbi Akiva in Yevamot 94, as ruled by Rif and Rosh.
