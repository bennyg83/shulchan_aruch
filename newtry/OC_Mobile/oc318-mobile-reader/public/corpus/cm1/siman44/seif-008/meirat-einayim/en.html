Perhaps he erased it and wrote a forged matter. In Perishah I wrote that even if no benefit to the document holder is presently visible at the location of the erasure, the document is still invalid. Perhaps he erased something above that was adverse to him, or wrote some benefit for himself there. And even if neither is presently visible, once the document is susceptible to forgery or alteration in his favor it has the status of a document capable of forgery and is invalid.
