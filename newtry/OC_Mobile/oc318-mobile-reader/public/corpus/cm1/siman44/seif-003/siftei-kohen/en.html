[" We do not derive from half a line, etc. The reason is stated in Tosafot, Mordechai, and the Rosh in the chapter Get Pashut: witnesses are not exacting and may leave one full line of space. Therefore, when they leave one line and sign in the middle of the second line, since only the thickness of one line remains between the witnesses and the text of the document, nothing may be derived even from the complete portion of that line:"]
[" And specifically when they signed, etc. Meaning, if something is found written there, we do not derive from it for the reason explained above, since the witnesses could have left that amount of blank space and the document would still have been valid. But if they signed one after the other, we do derive from it, because the entire document would be invalid had the witnesses left such a space, as explained below in siman 45, seif 10. They therefore certainly left nothing blank, and we derive from what is written there. The words of Ir Shushan may likewise be explained this way. Sema, seif katan 5, explains it differently, but in my view the explanation is as I have written. In practical law, however, the result is the same. See there. Likewise, what Sema wrote against Ir Shushan raises no difficulty, because the words of Ir Shushan can be explained precisely in accordance with the Rama, both according to Sema’s explanation and according to mine. See there, and this is clear:"]
