And it is not proper to do so. The Maggid Mishneh explains that because she is not fit for marital relations or for chuppah in that state, it is not proper to betroth her then. See below, siman 60, where even with respect to chuppah the preferred practice is that she not enter until she is pure; if he does marry her while she is a niddah, the groom is first informed that she is a niddah.
