Although in the final line we write, "and we made a kinyan from him," while earlier in the document it may say only that he instructed the witnesses to make a kinyan and never expressly state that they actually did so until the final line, this is not treated as an entirely new matter and may still be learned from the final line. The Shach writes that this is disputed among the poskim.
One may not derive from it. The Sema holds that even if there is an additional blank line, one still may not derive from the last line. The Shach writes that according to Ramban it is valid.
