It must be validated first. This means that if the validation appears in the last line, we do not derive from the last line after the repetition at the end of the document, Shach.
And it is unnecessary to write, "and the matter is established." The phrase "valid and established" has the same force as "and the matter is established." Nevertheless, the practice is to write both expressions, as is also implied by the Tur.
They do not invalidate the document. Shach challenges this ruling as unreasonable. See Tumim for what I wrote on the matter. Beit Yosef, in responsum siman 9, writes that if most of the standard text of the document was erased, it is certainly invalid. See Tumim for what may be inferred regarding this from Rashi in Bava Metzia.
