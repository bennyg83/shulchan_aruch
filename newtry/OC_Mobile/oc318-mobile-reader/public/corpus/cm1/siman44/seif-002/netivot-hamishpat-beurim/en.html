But whether one may rely on the last line of his handwriting to collect from encumbered property depends on the dispute whether we fear collusion in a place where the document did not suffer a defect through falling. This is similar to the issue in siman 43 concerning whether a handwritten obligation requires a date. It is surprising that Urim VeTumim here rules stringently for collusion while there he concludes otherwise. A distinction can be drawn: with respect to purchasers or other encumbered property we are more concerned for collusion, while as between the direct parties or ordinary creditors the concern can be weaker.
