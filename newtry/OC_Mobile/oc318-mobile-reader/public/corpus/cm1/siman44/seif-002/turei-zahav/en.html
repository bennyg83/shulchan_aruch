(Seif 9: Two occurrences of “firm and established” are valid.) It appears that this concerns a document containing a blank line, as the Sema writes, yet the Tur nevertheless disqualifies it. His reason seems to be that when we observe the defect that “firm and established” was written twice, we suspect that the witnesses left two blank lines. This defect is more serious than the erasure at the end discussed below, where one blank line leaves the document valid. The Barceloni validates it because he holds that there is no defect at all, since even initially one may write “firm and established” several times.
