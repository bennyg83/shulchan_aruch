There is an authority... Bava Metzia 38 and 39 discusses property of a captive and whether another may be placed in possession. Rav Huna's principle is that no chazakah is established against the property of a minor. Even the opinion that does not place a relative there does so only from fear that he may damage the property. Without that concern he would be placed there, showing that his possession cannot establish ownership against the minor.
Nor against the property of mentally incompetent persons. The same passage treats one who flees like a captive, and Kesubos 48a treats a mentally incompetent person as one unable to act knowingly.
