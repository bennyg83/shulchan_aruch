Abandoned property. The Tur also adds property whose owner was forced to abandon it. This refers specifically to one who left because of danger to life. If he left because of financial pressure, possession can establish chazakah. "Abandoned" in the other sense refers to one who departed voluntarily and we do not know where he went, creating concern that he may be in a place where caravans are not common. In that case there is no chazakah.
