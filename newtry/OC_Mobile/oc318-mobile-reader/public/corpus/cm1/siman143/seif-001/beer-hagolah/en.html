Mishnah, Bava Batra 28a.
Tur, from the Gemara's explanation on 38a that a protest made outside the possessor's presence is nevertheless effective. This implies that even where the possessor occupies in the owner's presence, he still requires three years.
According to the Gemara's explanation there that the Mishnah's requirement that the owner be in the same province applies in wartime.
From the incident before Rava on 30a. Rashbam explains "outside markets" as distant lands where caravans are uncommon. Rif likewise writes in the name of Rav Hai, and Rambam holds this in chapter 11 of Hilkhot To'en, law 2.
Tur explains so, and Beit Yosef writes that it is straightforward.
There, and Rashbam likewise there.
There in the name of Rambam, law 3. Maggid Mishneh writes that the reasoning is sound. Kesef Mishneh questions it, noting that there is greater distraction at town markets, etc. Perhaps "markets" means great fairs, called fairs in the vernacular, where village inhabitants gather in particularly large numbers because the market is especially expansive.
