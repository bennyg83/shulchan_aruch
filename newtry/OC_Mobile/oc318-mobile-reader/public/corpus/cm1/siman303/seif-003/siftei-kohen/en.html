Exempt from unavoidable accidents. The Maggid Mishneh derives from the Gemara’s discussion of consecrated property that a paid custodian is exempt from unavoidable accidents generally. If such an accident occurred, he does not forfeit his wage; he swears that the loss was unavoidable and collects his pay. This is established in Bava Metzia 58a and is also indicated above in simanim 66 and 301.
An armed robber, etc. This is so even though the robber hides from people. See Responsa Maharshadam, siman 430.
