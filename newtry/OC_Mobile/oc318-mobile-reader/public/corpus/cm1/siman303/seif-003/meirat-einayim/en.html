Even if, had he been there, he could not have saved it. Although below in seif 10 the Tur, Mechaber, and Rama rule that where one entered the city at the ordinary time and a lion attacked, if even had he remained he could not have saved the animal he is exempt according to all opinions, this case is different. There the event bears the legal character of an unavoidable attack by a wolf or lion. Here it bears the legal character of theft. Therefore his actual presence is specifically required. In the Perishah I gave another explanation based on the Tur's wording.
