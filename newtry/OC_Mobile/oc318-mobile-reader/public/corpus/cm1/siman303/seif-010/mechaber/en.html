If a shepherd left his flock and entered the city, whether at a time when shepherds normally enter or when they do not, and a wolf tore or a lion mauled an animal, we do not simply say that had he been there he would have saved it. Rather, we assess whether he could have saved it with other shepherds and staffs. If so, he is liable; if not, he is exempt. If this cannot be determined, he must pay. Rama: Some say that if he entered at a time when shepherds do not normally enter, he is liable in all cases, because the incident began through negligence and ended through an unavoidable accident. See above, siman 291, seif 12.
