And if the matter is not known, etc. The Beit Yosef explains that presumably he could have saved the animal by hiring shepherds and staffs; the Ir Shushan and Sema therefore exempt a gratuitous custodian. This is difficult, for how can such ability be presumed, and where did the Rambam derive it? Rather, because the facts are unknown, the paid custodian is obligated to swear but cannot swear with certainty. Every custodian must swear that he knows clearly that an unavoidable accident occurred. One who can say only that he is uncertain is one obligated to swear who cannot swear, and must pay. The same reasoning applies even to a gratuitous custodian. This is why the Rambam did not distinguish between them.
