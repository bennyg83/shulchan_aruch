The wording of Rambam there. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Mishnah's phrase 'it climbed to the tops of cliffs,' etc., and the Gemara in HaMafkid, 36b, establishes it where the animal overpowered him and climbed. Although that answer was stated according to Abaye, whose view is not followed, Ramban and Rashba explain at length that as a matter of halachah this interpretation is true according to all. 'Cliffs' means sharp, high mountains, and 'overpowered him' means he held it but it was stronger than he.
