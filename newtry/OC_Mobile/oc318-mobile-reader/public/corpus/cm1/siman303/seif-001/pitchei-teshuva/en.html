A paid custodian. See Baer Heitev. Machaneh Ephraim, laws of custodians, siman 31, writes that Responsa Rashach, volume 2, siman 85, indicates that a household servant has the status of an unpaid custodian toward the employer if property is entrusted to him and then stolen. Although the servant receives wages from the employer, those wages are for household service, not specifically for guarding. See there. Ketzot HaChoshen above, siman 291, seif 21, appears to disagree.
