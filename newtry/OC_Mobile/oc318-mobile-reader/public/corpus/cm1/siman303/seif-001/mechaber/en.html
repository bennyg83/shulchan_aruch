For what a paid bailee is liable or exempt, and when his liability begins. It contains fifteen seifim.
A paid bailee likewise does not become responsible for guarding until he performs meshichah in a place where meshichah is effective, as explained in siman 291 regarding an unpaid bailee. Rama: Some say that if a person sends an item with another to bring it somewhere, and meanwhile sends him a gift, the carrier becomes a paid bailee. If Reuven says to Shimon, 'You have a certain item to sell; entrust it to me and I will sell it,' and Shimon says, 'Give me such and such an amount and the excess will be yours,' Reuven becomes a paid bailee even if he remained silent when accepting it.
