A paid custodian, etc. It appears that he becomes a paid custodian only if the wage is worth at least a perutah, as is explicit in the Rosh and in the Tur below, siman 346, seif 12, citing Maharam. The Gemara teaches that a worker hired by the day to guard property may not receive separate Shabbos wages; consequently he is not liable for an item lost on Shabbos. If he was hired by the week, month, year, or seven-year period, Shabbos is included in the larger wage, and Shabbos responsibility rests upon him. This is explained in Orach Chaim 306:4.
