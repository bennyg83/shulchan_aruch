Wages. It appears that one is not a paid custodian unless his wage is worth at least a perutah. This is explicit in the Rosh at the beginning of HaShoel and in the Tur, siman 346, seif 12, in the name of Maharam [Ramah]. See there. We learn in HaZahav, Bava Metzia 58, and in Ein Bein HaMudar, Nedarim 37: one who hires a worker to guard, meaning, as Rashi explains, a worker paid by the day, may not pay him Sabbath wages; therefore, if the guarded item is lost on Shabbat he is not liable for it. If, however, he is hired by the week, month, year, or seven-year period, he may receive wages inclusive of Shabbat, and therefore responsibility for Shabbat rests upon him. This is explained in Orach Chaim, siman 306, seif 4. Shach.
