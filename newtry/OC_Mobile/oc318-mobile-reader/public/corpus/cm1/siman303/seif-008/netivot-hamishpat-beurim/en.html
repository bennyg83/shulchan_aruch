He is negligent and liable. See Shach, seif katan 5. The text should read, "and the same applies to any other person." Tur says that if the custodian had to give the rescuers the full value of the animal, the owner must reimburse him for the effort and expense of buying a replacement animal. Perishah cites Raavad that he need not. Tur's statement is difficult. Even an armed robber or ordinary thief pays only the market value of what he took, not more than its value. The same should apply here. The owner should not be required to reimburse additional procurement inconvenience merely because the custodian had to spend more in the rescue process.
