[Shulchan Aruch] To say that it was torn. Note: According to this, where there is no migo, for example where the deposit is before us or its value fell, and the custodian claims that he saved it while prices were high at the higher value, he is not believed. Nevertheless, perhaps this applies only where it is entirely unknown whether he saved it for a fee. If it is known that he saved it for a fee but the amount he spent is unknown, one can say that since he spent with authorization for his fellow’s benefit, he is believed with an oath even without a migo, like one who incurs expenses on his wife’s property and like anyone who incurs authorized expenses. See Responsa Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, siman 83.
