Except that a gratuitous custodian calls shepherds, etc. Nevertheless, if even a gratuitous custodian first paid others to rescue the property, the owner must reimburse his expenses. Tosafot, the Rosh, Mordechai, and other authorities rule this regarding one who drives away a lion. The same applies to an ordinary person who was not a custodian and paid to rescue another’s property. See Responsa Maharshadam, siman 220.
