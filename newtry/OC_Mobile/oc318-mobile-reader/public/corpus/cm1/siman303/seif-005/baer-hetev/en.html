From unavoidable accidents. The Maggid Mishneh writes at the beginning of chapter 2 of Hilchot Sechirut that from the discussion concerning a paid custodian of consecrated property one may learn that a paid custodian, who is ordinarily exempt from unavoidable accidents, does not lose his wages when such an accident occurs. Rather, he swears that an unavoidable accident occurred and collects his wages. His words are correct, as is evident in HaZahav, Bava Metzia 58a, and also above in siman 66, seif 2, and at the beginning of siman 301. Shach.
