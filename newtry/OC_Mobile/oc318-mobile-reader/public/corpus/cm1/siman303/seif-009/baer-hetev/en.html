Without payment. Nevertheless, even an unpaid custodian who first spends money to rescue the deposit must be reimbursed for what he spent. So appears in Tosafot, chapter HaKones, Bava Kamma 58, and in the Rosh, Mordechai, and other poskim there regarding one who drives away a lion. The glosses to the Rosh in the chapter HaPoalim say likewise. Tosafot and the Rosh there further state that the same applies to any ordinary person who is not a custodian: if he rescues the property at his own expense, he may recover those expenses from the owner. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 202. Shach.
