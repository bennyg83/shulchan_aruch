Liable. The Beit Yosef explains that ordinarily he could have rescued the animal by hiring shepherds and sticks, and the Ir Shushan and Sema follow him, writing that an unpaid custodian is exempt. This is surprising, for how can we presume that he could have rescued it? In my view, the reason is that since the facts are unknown, the paid custodian is obligated to swear, yet he cannot swear because he does not know. Every custodian must swear that he knows with certainty that an unavoidable accident occurred. If he himself is uncertain, he is one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear, and therefore must pay, as explained above in siman 291 as written there in seif katan 16. This rule is straightforward in many places. Accordingly, the same law applies to an unpaid custodian, and this is why the Rambam makes no distinction here. This appears clear to me. Shach.
