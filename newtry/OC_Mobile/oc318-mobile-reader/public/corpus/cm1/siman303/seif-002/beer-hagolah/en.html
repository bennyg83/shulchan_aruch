(b) Changing P.D
Tur seif 2, because so it appears in the Yerushalmi. Tosafot in Bava Kamma 57b cite Ri and conclude that it is difficult. The Rosh likewise rules that every case called theft obligates a paid guardian even if it came through a great accident, because all theft is close to unavoidable accident, as stated in Bava Kamma 94b. Thus there is no distinction between a greater or lesser accident. Armed robbery, however, is captivity, and from that the Torah exempts a paid guardian. The Sheiltot likewise writes so, and Maharik, shoresh 104, writes that this is also implied by the Mordechai.
