`Sh. M&D in several places:
And some say, etc. See Tosafot, Bava Kamma 57a, s.v. 'for example,' and Rosh there.
(Supplement) And some say, etc. Tosafot and Rosh cite support from the Jerusalem Talmud, final chapter of Shevuot: Rabbi Elazar said in the name of Rabbi Yochanan, 'Safekeeping was stated, etc.' Yet none of their proofs is conclusive, and Tosafot themselves conclude that the explanation is strained. Hagahot Maimoniyot disagrees and rules that every complete accident exempts the guardian. Yam Shel Shlomo likewise rules this way and discusses the matter at length. The words of Sheiltot also require great study. The Jerusalem Talmud does not contain the wording attributed to it by Tur, 'even if they surrounded it with an iron wall, etc.' See the relevant sources and Derisha and Perisha. (End of supplement.)
