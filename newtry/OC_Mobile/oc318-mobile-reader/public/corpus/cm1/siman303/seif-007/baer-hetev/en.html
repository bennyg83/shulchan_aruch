Meaning, even for payment, as the Tur and Mechaber write in seif 8 regarding a shepherd who must save the flock through other shepherds, even for payment, and afterward collects the expense from the depositor. So writes the Sema. The Shach writes: even though he saves his own property and as a result the article he was guarding burns, we do not say that because he could have saved the article the loss was not unavoidable, merely because his own property would otherwise have burned. So writes the Rama in Responsa, siman 106, at length and with sound proofs. It appears from his words that if he were an unpaid custodian he would be liable, because this would be considered negligence. Nevertheless, the law requires study. See Responsa Shav Yaakov, Choshen Mishpat, siman 23.
