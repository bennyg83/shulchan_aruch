The wording of the Tur, seif 12. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes so.
There and in Rambam. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the dispute of Abaye and Rava in HaMafkid, and Rambam rules like Rava. The Halakhot explain at length that although chapter 1 established that where negligence begins the sequence and an unavoidable accident follows one is liable, here the accident did not result from the negligence at all, and there was no means to avoid this accident.
