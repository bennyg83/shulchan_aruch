Shach, seif katan 17, s.v. 'he further wrote,' etc., 'specifically.' Note: See Teshuvot Maimoniyot to Sefer Kinyan, siman 31, which derives a worker's right to withdraw from the law of monetary deduction applicable to a Hebrew servant who fled. One must say, however, that monetary deduction still requires a payment and therefore is not considered fully within his power to redeem himself, unlike a worker. The Ran writes this distinction, and the Magen Avraham cites it in siman 441, seif katan 7. See Hagahot Mordechai to Bava Metzia, siman 860, and Responsa Chavat Yair, siman 140; see there.
