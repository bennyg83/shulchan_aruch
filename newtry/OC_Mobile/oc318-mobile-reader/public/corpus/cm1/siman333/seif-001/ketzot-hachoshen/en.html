Only a grievance. Tosafot says that even where the workers can find other employment after the employer retracts, they retain a grievance because they now must exert themselves to locate it. Rosh and Tur rule similarly. The Shach asks from the case of a ship unloaded midway, where the Gemara says that if the shipowner can find another cargo there is no grievance. Shitah Mekubetzet already answers that in the ship case the customer has a substantial reason to terminate, because he no longer needs the ship. Where one retracts for a legitimate strong reason and the other party can find equivalent work, there is no grievance. Ordinary unexplained retraction is different. Thus the existence of grievance depends not only on financial loss but also on whether the withdrawal was unjustified and imposed needless trouble upon the other party.
