(Seif 1: If the employer pulled, etc.) The same applies if the employer did not physically pull the craftsman's tools but the craftsman placed them in the employer's courtyard. The employer's domain acquires for him just as formal pulling does. Once the employer acquires the craftsman's tools for the work, that acquisition is not undone merely because the craftsman later takes them back into his hands; property previously delivered as a deposit is different. I also found in an old collection that whenever the Sages say one receives the wage of an idle worker, this means half the ordinary wage, as stated in a responsum of Rashi and transmitted by Rabbeinu Chananel and the Or Zarua.
