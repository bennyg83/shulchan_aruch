[Shulchan Aruch] When does this apply? When they did not begin traveling. Note: If a group hired a teacher, or an individual acted on behalf of the group, then even if the teacher did not yet begin the work, they cannot retract, because a communal undertaking does not require a kinyan. Three people are already considered a group for this purpose. Darkei Moshe, subparagraph 5. Maharikash writes similarly. See Responsa Rama, siman 50.
