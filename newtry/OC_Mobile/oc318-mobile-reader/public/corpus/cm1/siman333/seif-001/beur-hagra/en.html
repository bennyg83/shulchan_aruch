And some say, etc. See Tosafot 48a, s.v. 'but surely,' Nimukei Yosef, Mordechai there, and the beginning of chapter HaUmanin, section 318.
Nevertheless, etc. Nimukei Yosef and Maggid Mishneh explain that where the employer holds the workers' property, the rule that he may hire replacements applies up to forty or fifty zuz only for work whose loss is irretrievable. Against the amount already owed to the original workers, however, he may hire replacements even for ordinary work. This is learned from the chapter HaZahav, where misuse of consecrated property shows that the wages have already been acquired by the worker. Rashba disagrees with Tosafot and permits retraction, while allowing the employer to retain the worker's property. Rama derives that even according to Tosafot the same retention applies to hired labor. See Shach.
