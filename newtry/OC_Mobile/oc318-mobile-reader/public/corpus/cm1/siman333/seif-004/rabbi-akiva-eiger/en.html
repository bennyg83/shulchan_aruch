[Shulchan Aruch] If they can find someone else to hire them. Note: If, when hiring them, the employer said that if he retracts he will pay their full wages, one can say that this additional wording comes to include even a case where they find someone else to hire them, and he must still pay them. So writes Ramban in Responsa Toldos Adam, siman 1. Nevertheless, perhaps that applies specifically to the case there, involving a teacher, where the ordinary reduction for an idle worker does not apply because even without this student he learns with others, as Ramban explains there. The added wording must therefore teach that even if another student is found in place of the first, the original employer must still pay him. For an ordinary worker, however, it is more reasonable that the added wording means only that he must pay full wages rather than the reduced wage of an idle worker, but only if the worker cannot find alternative employment. This appears to me.
