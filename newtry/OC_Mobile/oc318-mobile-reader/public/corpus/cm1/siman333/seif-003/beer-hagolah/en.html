h) The Ritva writes in a responsum that the rule permitting a worker to withdraw applies only when he hired himself by mere verbal agreement. But whenever he further obligated himself, that additional undertaking means that he may not withdraw. Beit Yosef, siman 334. Accordingly, if he withdraws, he has the lower hand like a contractor.
i) Three years has the status of a hired term, as Scripture states in Isaiah, 'like the years of a hired worker.' According to Tosafot in Bava Metzia 10a, where one may withdraw during the agreed term, the arrangement is permitted.
