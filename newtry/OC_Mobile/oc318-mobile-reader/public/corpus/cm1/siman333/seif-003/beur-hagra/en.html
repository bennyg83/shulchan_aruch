Even if he received, etc. For employment is compared to a Hebrew slave, as stated below from the verse, etc. A Hebrew slave leaves by deduction of the redemption price. Kiddushin 16a asks, "this document, what are the circumstances, etc.?" This is the meaning of "and there is no, etc."
For this reason, etc. See Tosafos there 17a, who cite the verse in Isaiah, etc. Therefore if one hires himself for more than three years he leaves the category of hired worker and resembles a slave, and it is prohibited, as stated there 22b. This is the meaning of the statement in the chapter Mefanin that one hired himself for three years. The text here is erroneous and should read "more than three years." Mordechai disagrees, and Tosafos 10a, s.v. "ki, etc.," likewise disagree.
Even a teacher, etc. See seif 5 in the gloss, "he hired himself for a fixed period, etc."
