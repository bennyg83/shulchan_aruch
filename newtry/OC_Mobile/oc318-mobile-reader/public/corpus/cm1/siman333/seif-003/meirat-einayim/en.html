The worker began, etc. The Mechaber follows the Rambam's wording. The Rambam too does not explicitly write that once the worker begins, the employer is forbidden to retract even if the workers have not left their place. The baraita does state this, and the Tur records it. Rama alludes to it below when he says that the employer's retraction has the same law as the contractor's. In my view the Rambam omitted it because he understood that it is already learned from the law of "they went." The employer's liability there rests on the principle that going to the job is considered the beginning of the work. Certainly actual commencement of the work has the same or stronger effect.
