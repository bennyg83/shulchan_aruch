Some disagree. In my view there may be no disagreement. The Terumat HaDeshen states that if the worker cannot find employment except at work heavier than the original work, he may choose not to engage in it and receives his wage as an idle worker. This can mean that the employer need not add payment for the heavier work. One should not argue that the worker must perform the heavier work and that beit din should appraise the additional compensation, because the case may be one in which the appraised addition would exceed the idle-worker wage. The employer therefore declines to add it. The worker may then either accept the heavier work at the offered rate, in which case the original employer owes nothing further, or decline it and receive the idle-worker wage.
