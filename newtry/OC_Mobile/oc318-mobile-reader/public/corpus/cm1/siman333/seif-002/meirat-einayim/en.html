They are paid to reduce what is hired today less than the one who pays it according to the value of an expropriation by yesterday:
If they went, even though, etc. The Tur likewise writes in the Rosh's name and adds: the same applies if they did not actually travel but the employer delayed them until they could no longer find other work. He must pay them like idle workers because he caused them a loss. The source states the ordinary case. Ordinarily, if they have not traveled they can still find other work, whereas after traveling they often cannot.
