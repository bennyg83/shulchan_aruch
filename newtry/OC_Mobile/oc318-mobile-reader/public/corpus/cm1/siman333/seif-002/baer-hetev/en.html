The shortfall. The wording implies that if the workers can find other employment, the employer need not pay them the full idle-worker rate, but only the difference. This also appears from the Tur, who writes that if the employer says, "Go hire yourselves elsewhere and I will supplement your wages up to the amount I agreed with you," they must do so. Thus the worker cannot insist, "I will remain idle and you must pay me as an idle worker." The choice belongs to the employer. The Bach, seif katan 2, writes that the choice belongs to the workers: if they do not wish to hire themselves elsewhere, the employer must pay them as idle workers, but if they do not wish to lose anything they may work elsewhere and the first employer supplements their wage. In my humble opinion, the first explanation is correct. Shach.
