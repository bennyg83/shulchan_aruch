The baraita there, as Rav Nachman establishes it: the case is where their bundle is in his possession. Rashi explains this as their tools of trade. The Maggid Mishneh cites an opinion that where the employer holds their tools, even for work that is not subject to loss, he may hire replacement workers against them up to the amount of the original wages if labor became more expensive, but no more. This is Rashba's view and appears also to be Ramban's.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that the baraita says 'up to forty or fifty.' Rambam explains that this refers to a single worker for a single day and that the numbers are exaggeration. This is reasonable.
