Teachers have the law of other workers. Even where the local custom permits either householders or teachers to retract until Rosh Chodesh Iyar or MarCheshvan because the new term is not considered begun until then, a handshake undertaking customarily made between them may establish that they intended no right of retraction from that point. Whether viewed as a covenant or as part of the employment transaction, it can serve as evidence that the employment is to be binding. If the handshake is treated only as a covenant, some opinions might allow release from it, but as a commercial commitment it appears effective. The customary uncertainty of a teacher's hiring before Rosh Chodesh may apply particularly to town teachers; a head teacher or one hired for a village may sometimes have a more settled engagement even earlier.
