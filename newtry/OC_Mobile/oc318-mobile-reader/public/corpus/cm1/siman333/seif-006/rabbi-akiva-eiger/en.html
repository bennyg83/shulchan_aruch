[Shulchan Aruch] Unless he was prevented by circumstances beyond his control, such as illness. Note: If one gives an item to an artisan to weave or perform similar work as a contractor, and the artisan is prevented by circumstances beyond his control, one can say that the owner may not take the item away from him. The artisan can say, "I am not a day laborer. I will perform the job after the unavoidable circumstance passes." The exception is where it is evident that the owner needs the work completed specifically by that time, such as for a festival. Tiferes LeMoshe, Yoreh Deah, siman 380, on Shach subparagraph 17.
