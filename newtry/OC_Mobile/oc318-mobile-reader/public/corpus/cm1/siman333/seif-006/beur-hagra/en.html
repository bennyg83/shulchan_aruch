Up to the amount, etc. Meaning, up to the entire wages of the first workers. The Rosh, unlike Rashi, brings proof from the Yerushalmi.
And if not, etc. For they never became bailees, and one who is not a bailee is not liable for negligence. There.
Others say, etc. The reason is that the employer relied upon them. Since their tools are in his possession, they will not hire themselves elsewhere. Rashi likewise explains "bundle" in this sense, though he holds that the specific object is not essential.
Specifically, etc. This is based on dina d'garmi. Nimukei Yosef seemingly disagrees, as above regarding "if he did not hire, etc.," but the Rama accepts his view only where the subject is not monetary.
(Collected note) And if, etc.; specifically, etc. Hagahos Ashri, s.v. "and if it is a matter, etc."
