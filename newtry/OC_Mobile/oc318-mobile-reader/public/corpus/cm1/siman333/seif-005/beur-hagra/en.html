Teachers have the law, etc. As stated at the beginning of chapter HaPoalim. The Yerushalmi there says, "this teaches that custom overrides the halachah." Rif and Rosh cite it.
(Collected note) Teachers have the same law as other, etc. Hagahos Ashri, s.v. "and they also follow, etc."
And it is prohibited, etc. As stated in the Tosefta cited by Rif and Rosh, chapter 5: one who seats his fellow in a shop on a half-profit arrangement, if he is an artisan he may not engage in his craft, because his attention will not remain on the shop. In Bava Batra 8b Rav found Rav Shmuel bar Shilas, etc., after thirteen, etc.
Or, etc. Yerushalmi Demai 7:3: One may not plow with his own cow and rent it out during the day; one may not work on his own property at night and hire himself out by day; he may not starve or afflict himself, because he thereby diminishes the employer's work. Rabbi Yochanan came to a place and found a teacher who was fasting. He asked why. They told him the teacher was fasting. He said: it is forbidden for you. If the work of flesh and blood is protected so, all the more so the work of God.
