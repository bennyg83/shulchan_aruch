The Rosh writes in his rulings on the conclusion of the baraita on 76b, 'when there are no workers available to hire,' etc. He asks that this is obvious and explains that it refers to deceiving the original workers. If other workers are available and he tells the first workers, 'go and hire them,' then if he deceived them he must give them the additional amount. The Maggid Mishneh cites Rashba likewise.
