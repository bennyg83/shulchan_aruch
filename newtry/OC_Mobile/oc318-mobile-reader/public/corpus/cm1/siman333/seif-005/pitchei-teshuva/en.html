A worker may retract even in the middle of the day. The later authorities explain that the worker's right of retraction does not mean every financial consequence disappears. Where he retracts under circumstances for which the special protection of a worker applies, his hand is on the upper side; where he retracts for an improper motive, such as merely to obtain a higher wage while remaining willing to serve, he may still be able to stop working but his hand can be on the lower side. See the Shach, Sema, later responsa, and the distinctions discussed in the preceding paragraph.
