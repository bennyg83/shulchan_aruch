(Collected note) He said to an artisan, etc. As stated in the Tosefta and below siman 335:3, where he hired him to bring apples, etc.
A prayer leader, etc. As above siman 312:9.
But if, etc. From Kesubos 90a, where we rule like Rav Huna that she does not receive the additional ketubah amount. See Shach.
The father, etc. See Shach.
A teacher, etc. As stated in Tosefta Kesubos chapter 10: one who obligated himself to support his wife's daughter cannot release himself from the obligation, because one may confer a benefit upon a minor but cannot create a liability for a minor. Chapter 12 there likewise teaches that one who promised money to his minor daughter and then refused to pay is compelled to give it, because one may acquire on behalf of a minor but may not impose a liability upon her.
Nevertheless, regarding other, etc. Kiddushin 16a: "let him say to him, etc." A worker's body is not acquired by the employer, as Tosafos write there 17a, s.v. "chalah," unlike Mordechai in the name of Maharam there.
