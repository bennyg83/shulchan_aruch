Even though. See Tosafos, Sanhedrin 5a, s.v. "for example," etc.
(Likkut) Even though, etc. But if they accepted him upon themselves, even an ordinary judge may judge alone initially, as stated in the Yerushalmi at the beginning of Sanhedrin: R' Abahu sat alone as judge in the old synagogue of Caesarea. His students said to him, "Did our master not teach us, 'Do not judge alone'?" He answered, "Since you see me sitting alone in judgment and nevertheless come before me, it is as though you accepted me upon yourselves." It likewise teaches: when does this apply? When they did not accept him upon themselves; but if they accepted him, he may judge even alone. End.
