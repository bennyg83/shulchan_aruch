[Shach, subparagraph 8] And the principal view appears to be that of the Rambam. Note: Seemingly, even if they accepted him upon themselves, this does not help with respect to an admission. Knesset HaGedolah likewise writes in his glosses to the Tur, subparagraph 11, in the name of the Raah. However, there, subparagraph 21, he cites a manuscript responsum of the Rashba that if the parties accepted a single judge upon themselves, and one denied the claim before him, and witnesses later came, he is established as one who falsely denied a debt. Knesset HaGedolah understands that the Rashba in fact holds this even regarding a single expert judge.
