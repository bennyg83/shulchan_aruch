Some say that he is believed to be called first to the Torah and to raise his hands for the priestly blessing. The Ramach, who holds this view, wrote only that he may be called first to the Torah. He explains that we are not concerned if he is called first, meaning that there is no prohibition involved, only a consideration of maintaining peaceful relations, as stated in the Mishnah in HaNizakin, Gittin 59b. But raising the hands for the priestly blessing involves a positive-commandment prohibition, as explained in Ketubot 24b, and in that matter we are not lenient even according to the Ramach.
