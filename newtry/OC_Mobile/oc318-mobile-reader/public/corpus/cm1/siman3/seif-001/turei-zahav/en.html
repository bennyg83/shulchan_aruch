Shulchan Aruch, seif 3. “Although a single expert,” etc. This refers to a case where the litigants did not accept him. If they accepted him, however, he may even initially adjudicate alone. My grandfather, Rabbi Yitzchak son of Betzalel, ruled this way in practice. The Maharshal disagreed with him, but Rabbi Yosef Kohen of Krakow already rejected the Maharshal's proof in She'erit Yosef. I also found this stated in the Jerusalem Talmud, first chapter of Sanhedrin, concerning Rabbi Abba, who judged alone. It appears there that when the litigants came before him without qualification, this was considered an explicit acceptance. My father-in-law likewise cites this as the halacha.
