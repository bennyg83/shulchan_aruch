[Shulchan Aruch] And they adjudicate. Note: Responsa Magen Gibborim, siman 5, cites the Rashach, part 2, siman 229, regarding a dispute between a claimant and defendant. One says that he wishes to litigate according to Torah law, while the other says that he should litigate only before other judges, because that is the custom in the place where the transaction was made. The law follows the latter, since in the place where the transaction was made the custom is to adjudicate according to mercantile practice and not according to Torah law. Custom overrides the halachah. He adduces the law of situmta; see there.
