a) From the Mishnah at the beginning of Sanhedrin and the Gemara there, 3a, following Rav Acha son of Rav Ika, that on Torah law one judge suffices, but because of those who sit at crossroads three were required, as the Rama writes in the gloss. So too explains the Rambam in chapter 2 of Hilkhot Sanhedrin, as the Kesef Mishneh there writes in the name of the Rashba. The Bach writes that ideally three judges who are learned are required. Regarding towns whose residents are all unlearned, see below at the beginning of siman 8 in the gloss.
