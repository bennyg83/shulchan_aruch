[Shulchan Aruch] And they may change their claim. Note: See Responsa Maharit, part 2, Choshen Mishpat, siman 46, whose view is that one may not change his claim and then plead anew. With respect to the principle that a person does not reveal his claim except before a beit din, there is no distinction between a single expert judge and a beit din of three. What the Rambam writes, that an admission before them is not regarded as an admission before a beit din, means only that the admission does not acquire the status of a written instrument. And what the Rambam writes, "therefore, one who denies before them," refers to his ability to say afterward, "I paid," as the Shach writes in seif katan 9.
