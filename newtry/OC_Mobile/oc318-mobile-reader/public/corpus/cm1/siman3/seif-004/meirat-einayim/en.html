It is preferable that the judgment be decided by eleven, etc. When there are many judges, all of them examine the matter closely and the ruling is more likely to be true. The reference to ten is not literal, since ten is an even-numbered court. See the Perishah for why the figures ten and eleven were mentioned.
All those sitting in beit din must be Torah scholars and fit. This refers to all ten or eleven people actually sitting as members of the court, not to a student sitting before his teacher. The Beit Yosef implies that such a student need not himself be a Torah scholar, provided he is not an ignoramus.
