(Anthology) Fewer, etc. The anonymous Gemara in many places follows Rabbi Abahu: Sanhedrin 30b, concerning a deed of admission; the second chapter of Ketubot; the chapter Get Pashut, concerning a court session of three where one was absent; and the second chapter of Bava Metzia, 'Go and bring three.' Rava at the beginning of Sanhedrin likewise follows Rabbi Abahu. So do Rabbi Yochanan and Reish Lakish in the Yerushalmi, as Tosafot cite on 2b.
(Anthology) Fewer than three, etc. See the Mordechai and other authorities, who prove this from the deed of admission in the third chapter of Sanhedrin, from three who sat to validate a document and one died, according to the first chapter of Bava Batra and the second chapter of Ketubot. Tosafot in Sanhedrin explain that even Shmuel agrees regarding document validation and a deed of admission. The phrase, 'Perhaps the Rabbis of Rabbi Abahu follow Shmuel,' means that they follow Shmuel only generally. Their words are compelled according to the Rif and Rambam, who hold that even an expert cannot adjudicate alone, although elsewhere his expertise is effective.
