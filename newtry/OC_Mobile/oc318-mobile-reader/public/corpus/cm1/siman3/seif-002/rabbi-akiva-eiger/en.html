[Shulchan Aruch] If the defendant refuses. Note: If he was found not liable by a beit din, and afterward the opposing litigant returned and sued him before another beit din, he is not required to descend into litigation with him, and the second beit din is not permitted to hear his claims at all. See Beit Yosef below, siman 12, in the name of Responsa Chazeh HaTenufah. Maharikash writes there: It appears to me that if there are no witnesses to the matter, he is believed to say that he already litigated with him over that same oral claim and was found not liable. He is not even obligated to take an oath, because even if he were to admit the allegation, he would not thereby become liable for money, but only to litigate with him. It is possible, however, that a ban is imposed upon him that he not plead falsely, and that he admit it if he has not already litigated with him over this claim. The Sema, siman 19, seif katan 2, cites the aforementioned words of the Beit Yosef.
