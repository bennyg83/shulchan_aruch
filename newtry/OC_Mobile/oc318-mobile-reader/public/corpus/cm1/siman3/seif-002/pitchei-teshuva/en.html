And they judge the person against his will. Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes in his glosses to Shulchan Aruch that Responsa Magen Giborim, siman 5, cites the Rashach, part II, siman 229, concerning a dispute between plaintiff and defendant where one says that he wishes to litigate according to Torah law and the other says that the dispute must be decided only according to merchants' law, because that is the custom in the place where the transaction was made. The law follows the latter. Since in the place where the transaction was made the custom is to adjudicate according to commercial practice and not according to Torah law, the custom overrides the otherwise applicable law.
