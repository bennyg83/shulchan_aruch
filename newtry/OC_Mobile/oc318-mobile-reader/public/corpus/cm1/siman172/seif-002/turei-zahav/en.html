Seif 2: And the one to whose portion it fell. The Sema writes in seif katan 6, following the Beit Yosef, that the same applies if the house with the eight-cubit entrance falls to one partner. In my humble opinion this is not reasonable. If so, even when they divide equally without any additional payment, we should say that had the second portion not fallen to him he would have had to add more. It appears to me that this reasoning is considered part of the additional payment only where the owner of the two entrances actually pays an increment. We may then say that this factor is included in the money he pays. It cannot be used merely to reduce the accounting by claiming that otherwise he would not have paid so much.
