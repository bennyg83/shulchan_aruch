[Shulchan Aruch] He broke away its doorposts. Note: If there were no doorposts at all and he sealed the opening, Maharach Shmuel, siman 14, wrote that from Rashi's language, "he did not break away his doorposts," meaning that he did not remove his mezuzah and so forth, thereby revealing his intent not to withdraw, it follows that an affirmative indication is required that he intends ultimately to reopen it. If he simply seals it, we say that he lost his right. However, from the language of Maggid Mishneh, who wrote that whenever he breaks away the doorposts his intent is apparent that he is removing himself, it follows that an affirmative indication is required that he is relinquishing his right. See there. Responsa Maharik, siman 191, likewise wrote to prove that the primary view is that we require an indication that he intends ultimately to reopen it. See there.
