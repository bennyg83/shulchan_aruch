The name of his father is the name of his father:
This is not literal. The same applies if the owner of one entrance paid money to the owner of two entrances. Since there was an appraisal and adjustment, we may say they also appraised the advantage of the four cubits in the courtyard. If they appraised that the owner of one entrance should pay his fellow ten gold coins, perhaps if he too had only one entrance they would have appraised twenty. Beit Yosef infers this from Rosh. The Maggid Mishneh writes similarly in Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 2, law 1, in the name of Rashba and says it also appears from Rabbenu Chananel.
