There, from the baraita in the first chapter of Bava Batra 11b. Maggid Mishneh cites Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash, who explains that refuse is apportioned according to the houses because it originates from the houses, and the more houses one has the more refuse is generated. Rashi explains that it is divided according to doorways for carrying refuse to the fields, because refuse was thrown through the entrances until a heap formed.
The Gemara raises the question there and resolves it from a supporting baraita. Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam follows the second explanation cited in the Halachot, and Rashi's wording accords with it. This is the second explanation cited in the gloss.
