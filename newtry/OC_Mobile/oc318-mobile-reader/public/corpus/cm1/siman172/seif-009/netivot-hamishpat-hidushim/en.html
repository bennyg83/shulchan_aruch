Meaning the refuse that is left in the courtyard. According to the first interpretation, which understands "refuse" as referring to division of the refuse left in the courtyard, "royal lodgers" likewise refers to division of the refuse left by the king's guests. The second interpretation understands "royal lodgers" as referring to expenses that residents must contribute for hosting the king's guests, and accordingly understands the first clause concerning refuse as the expenses of removing refuse. Thus, under each interpretation the first and second clauses form opposite types of obligation, and the Mechaber's word "but" is precise. Sema:
