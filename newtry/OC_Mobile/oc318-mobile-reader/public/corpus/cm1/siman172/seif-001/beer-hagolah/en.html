Rosh writes in his rulings in the name of Riva in the name of the Geonim, deriving it from the rule that brothers who divided have no rights of way, windows, or ladders against one another, 7b. Just as a window does not acquire a right to require four cubits of setback opposite it, an entrance does not acquire four cubits in the jointly owned courtyard. Beit Yosef writes in seif 2 that from Rambam at the beginning of chapter 2 and the Maggid Mishneh in the name of Rabbi Yosef HaLevi it appears that Rabbi Yochanan's rule that four cubits are in addition to the area of the entrances applies specifically to those who acquired from ownerless property. He discusses at length how to reconcile the Gemara accordingly.
