[Shulchan Aruch] Rather, they divide equally. Note: If a courtyard is five cubits by five cubits, the owner of two doorways cannot say, "Since my share will leave only four cubits for one doorway and four cubits in the courtyard, the property is not divisible, and I will not divide unless the courtyard is six times four by four so that I receive three areas of four by four." Whenever he receives four cubits for one doorway and four cubits in the courtyard, it is considered legally divisible. So rules the Rosh.
