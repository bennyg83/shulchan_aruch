This too is there, law 15, from Rav Bibi bar Abaye's challenge concerning rock, etc., regarding chazakah, there 29a. The Raavad objects. The Maggid Mishneh explains that Rambam does not require an act that benefits the land as an acquisition where the buyer himself derives benefit. If the buyer benefits and another person is intentionally transferring ownership, he acquires. A stronger example is locking a house, which is chazakah because he bars everyone else as an owner does. All the more so eating produce or any act through which owners benefit from land or use it in its normal manner. The Ittur likewise rules so, as explained in the next siman.
