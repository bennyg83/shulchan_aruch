Rambam there, law 10. The Maggid Mishneh derives it from the incident of the bill of divorce on Shabbat in Gittin, chapter HaZorek, 77b. From the statement, 'let her go and lock and open,' he writes that she locked it and then reopened it.
So the Beit Yosef explains the view of the poskim cited by the Tur, that locking the door is sufficient. The Rosh likewise writes so in his rulings in Gittin, as do Tosafot in Bava Batra at the beginning of 53a and in Bava Kamma at the beginning of 52a. The Maggid Mishneh also cites some commentators who say so.
