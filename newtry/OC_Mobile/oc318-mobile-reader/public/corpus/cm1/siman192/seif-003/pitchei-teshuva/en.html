Go, take possession and acquire. See Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Sechirut, siman 13, who writes that from Responsa Radbaz, siman 122, it appears that a partner is treated like any other person, and when the act of chazakah is performed outside the transferor's presence he must tell him, "Go, take possession and acquire." Tosafos, however, in the first chapter of Bava Batra 3a, s.v. Rav Ashi, write that when the land belongs jointly to both parties, an act of chazakah performed with the other's consent acquires even in his absence although he did not say, "Go, take possession and acquire." The Rosh writes likewise. In my humble opinion, this does not contradict the Radbaz. First, Ramban disagrees with Tosafos there, as Beit Yosef writes above at the beginning of siman 157. Second, even according to Tosafos, the chazakah is effective there specifically because the matter is merely a clarification of their respective shares, as Sema writes there in seif katan 6. For this reason Hagahot Maimoniyot, cited by Shach there, holds that even walking the boundary alone is sufficient. This differs from a case in which one partner sells his share to the other.
