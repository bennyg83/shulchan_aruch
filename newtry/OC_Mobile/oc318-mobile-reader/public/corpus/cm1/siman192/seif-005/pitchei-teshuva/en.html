And some disagree, and so forth. Shaar Mishpat writes that this appears to apply specifically where the purchaser has not paid the money. If he paid the money, then even in a place where deeds are customarily written, so that money alone does not acquire, in a case such as this an act of chazakah consisting of eating the produce or the like should nevertheless be effective. We see that chazakah by itself, without money and without a deed, acquires even in a place where deeds are customarily written, as stated at the end of this siman. The early authorities explain the reason: once the property has come into his possession, his mind is settled upon the acquisition.
