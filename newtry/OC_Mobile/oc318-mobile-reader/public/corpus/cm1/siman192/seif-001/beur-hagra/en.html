How does one sell, etc. See Baer HaGolah. The Gemara there similarly discusses one who sold ten fields, etc.; Bava Batra there asks, 'And what did he sell?' etc.; see also the fifth chapter of Bava Kamma and other passages.
And it was effective. There the Gemara states that he placed a bundle and it was effective, etc., and asks: what are the circumstances? If from the outset, etc.
