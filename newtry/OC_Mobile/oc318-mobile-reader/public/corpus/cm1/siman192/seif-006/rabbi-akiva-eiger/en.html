[Shulchan Aruch] Only corresponding to his money. Note: Nimukei Yosef in the chapter HaSocher holds that if he gave part of the price of each individual field, he acquires all of them. The Ran in the first chapter of Kiddushin, and likewise the novellae of the Rashba, hold that even in such a case he acquires only corresponding to the amount he paid. Only where he gave part of the price of the particular field upon which he took possession is it effective, and even if he paid nothing at all, if the seller is not pressing for payment it is effective for that field upon which he took possession.
