Chazakah, etc., and even. The Rosh and Ran in Kiddushin there, based on the Gemara’s statement, “When does this apply? When he gave, etc.” This requirement is only for ten fields; for one field it is unnecessary. The case must therefore be where documents are ordinarily written, for otherwise what need would there be for chazakah? This is unlike the Baal HaIttur, who writes that chazakah alone does not acquire. His proof is from the chapter HaOmanin, where Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says, “We teach, etc.” If chazakah or another kinyan sufficed, why not acquire through them? This is not a proof, because wherever the purchaser repeatedly enters and leaves over the price, the act is not a completed acquisition, as stated above in siman 190. This is the meaning of “wherever he enters and leaves, etc.”
Nevertheless, etc. As stated in Kiddushin: “and if he expressly stipulated, etc.” The same applies conversely.
