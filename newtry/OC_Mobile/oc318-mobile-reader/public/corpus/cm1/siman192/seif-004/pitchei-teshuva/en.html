Because he removed the damage. See Baer Heitev, through his citation of Rashbam's explanation that this resembles one who returns a lost object, and so forth. See Shaar Mishpat, who cites Chiddushei Ramban. Ramban writes that this is obvious not only where one acts in another person's property in the owner's absence, for then he is merely returning a lost object and performing a mitzvah that rests upon him. Rather, even if the owner is present, or even in the property of a convert who died without heirs, where no mitzvah of returning lost property applies, the person merely acts diligently so that he should not be called one who causes a loss.
