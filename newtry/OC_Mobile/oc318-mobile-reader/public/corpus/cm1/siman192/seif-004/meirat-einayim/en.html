Even if it is less than ten, etc. The Tur writes that some say the Gemara's case, where the wall was initially less than ten handbreadths high and he completed it to ten, merely states the ordinary circumstance. Whenever he completes it to ten the result is this, meaning that before the work people could ascend comfortably and afterward only with difficulty. The same law applies even below ten whenever the change has that effect. This refers, for example, to a sloping area outside a wall built upon a mound, where even a slight partition prevents further entry. See the Perishah.
