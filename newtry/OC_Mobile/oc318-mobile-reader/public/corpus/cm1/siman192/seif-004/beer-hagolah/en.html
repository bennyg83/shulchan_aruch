The Mishnah in chapter 3 of Bava Batra 42a.
The language of Rambam there, law 11.
In accordance with Shmuel's statement there and on 43a.
As explained there in the Gemara. Maggid Mishneh writes that if the field remains at the same elevation as before, he has accomplished nothing. Kesef Mishneh writes that the Gemara's measure of ten is significant because anything less than ten may still be easily ascended.
