Likewise regarding rental, etc. Tosefta, Bava Batra chapter 2, cited by the Rosh in chapter 3, siman 60: if one purchased ten fields from another, once he made chazakah in one he acquired all of them; if he rented ten fields, once he made chazakah in one he acquired all of them; if he purchased some and rented some, and made chazakah either on one purchased or one rented, it is a valid chazakah for all.
And regarding rental, etc. This requires study according to the Rosh, who requires that the money first be paid, and even for a gift he holds acquisition does not occur. Why should rental be stronger? The Tur also writes that he paid the money. Although the Beis Yosef strained to explain this as referring to sale, the wording does not indicate so, since it says there, “whether regarding rental, and he paid the money, etc.” The reasoning also does not incline to that explanation.
