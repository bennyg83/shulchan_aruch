Some disagree regarding a gift. Their reason is that a donor receives nothing, and absent payment we presume he wishes to retain the option to retract. In a rental, by contrast, one act of chazakah upon one property acquires all of them even before payment, because rent is not payable until the end. It is as though the lessor agreed that the rent should remain as a loan until the end of the term. Sema. The same applies where he takes the property in satisfaction of a debt, because no money remains to be paid. Once he performs chazakah on one of them he acquires all. Tosafot, Kiddushin 27. Even in a sale, if he has not paid the price of all the properties but the seller says, "Make chazakah on one of them and acquire all of them," he acquires all.
