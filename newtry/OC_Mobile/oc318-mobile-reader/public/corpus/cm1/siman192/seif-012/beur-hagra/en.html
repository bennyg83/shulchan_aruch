(Collected note) Some disagree regarding a gift. Tosafos Bava Batra 53b, s.v. “that, etc.,” the Rosh there, Hagahos Ashri, and the Tur in the name of the Rosh. The Rosh follows his own approach, for in Kiddushin he copied the Rif’s version of Rava’s statement, “This was taught only, etc.,” regarding ten fields, etc.; this is also the Rambam’s reading. See above, siman 2 there, seif katan 2. According to our text, however, it refers to movable property, and movable property is acquired in this manner even as a gift, showing that a gift is like a sale. (End of note.)
