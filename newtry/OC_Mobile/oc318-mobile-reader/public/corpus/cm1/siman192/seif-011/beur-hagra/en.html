Some disagree, etc. Meaning they disagree with all four preceding seifim from seif 8 onward. The Yerushalmi cited above, regarding one who piled produce into it, etc., concerns the rule that he need not say “Go make a chazakah and acquire,” for there it teaches similarly regarding a key and latch, etc. See Raavad and Darchei Moshe. The Raavad holds that this is not a universal rule, as indicated in the Gemara. When they challenge, “But what of eating produce, etc.?” the same applies to every similar case, since all share one reason: such acts do not effect acquisition of land.
If there was a mound in the field. The text should read “in the house,” because the act constitutes building, as stated at the end of chapter 8 of Shabbos, like fencing and similar acts. In a field, however, it is only an act of plowing, and only where the field is intended for cultivation, as stated below.
