The language of Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 16. Maggid Mishneh explains that Rambam holds that any use that would acquire by three-year chazakah against a seller who denies the sale effects acquisition immediately when the seller admits and says, "Go establish possession and acquire," even though among the examples the Mechaber lists are uses that do not create three-year chazakah, as explained in siman 141. The reason is that he used the land in the ordinary manner of an owner.
