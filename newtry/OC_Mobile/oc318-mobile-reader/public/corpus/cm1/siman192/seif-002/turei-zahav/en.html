Seif 3: He installed doors. The Beit Yosef asks why Rabbeinu here follows his father's view that installing doors suffices without locking them, while in siman 275, concerning ownerless property, he requires locking as well. The answer is that ownerless property has no other mind conveying it, so a stronger act demonstrating ownership is required. In an ordinary purchase, the seller's intent to convey joins the buyer's act, and installing the doors alone is sufficient. This is the proper distinction.
