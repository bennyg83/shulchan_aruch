Make a chazakah and acquire. Machaneh Efrayim, Hilchot Sechirut 13, writes that Radbaz 122 appears to hold that a partner is like any other person, so that if the acquisition is made outside the seller's presence the seller must tell him, "make a chazakah and acquire." Tosafot, Bava Batra 3, however, writes that where the land belongs jointly to both, one partner's chazakah with the other's consent works even in the other's absence and even without those words. Rosh writes similarly. In my opinion this does not necessarily contradict Radbaz. First, Ramban disagrees with Tosafot there, as Beit Yosef notes at the beginning of siman 157. Second, even according to Tosafot the rule there concerns only the clarification and division of already joint shares, as Sema explains. For that reason Hagahot Maimoniyot, cited by the Shach there, holds that even merely walking the boundary may suffice. This is different from a partner selling his proprietary share to the other partner. The matter therefore requires study.
