Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah, halachah 19, from the dispute between Rav and Shmuel in the incident of a particular gift in Bava Batra 153a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam rules like Rav, because the Gemara there expressly rules that way.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this concerns one giving instructions because of impending death. But with an ordinary gift, if a kinyan was made it is certainly like the gift of a healthy person, while without a kinyan it is ineffective.
Gimel) There, chapter 12, halachah 16.
Dalet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rav and Shmuel disagree only regarding a gift of a deathly ill person. Rav explains that the phrase "during life" is merely an encouraging expression, indicating hope that the giver will recover from the illness. With a healthy person, even Rav agrees that the phrase has its ordinary legal meaning. If the deed says "from today and after death," the law is explained in siman 257.
