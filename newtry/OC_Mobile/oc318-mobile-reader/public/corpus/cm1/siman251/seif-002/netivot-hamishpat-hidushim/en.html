If he does not bring proof, the property remains in the presumptive possession of the heirs, and they are exempt even without an hesset oath, because heirs do not swear hesset merely to be exempt, even where the recipient asserts a definite claim. If the recipient seized the property, the same rule applies in the later case where the giver says he had been on his deathbed and the recipient says he had been healthy. Seizure is effective, and the recipient is believed where he has a migo; without a migo he is not believed (Shach).
