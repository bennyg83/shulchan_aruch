Meaning, although we rule that despair alone does not effect acquisition, and even though when it reaches the recipient there is both despair and change of possession, nevertheless it came to him through prohibition. Here the case is different because it is comparable to a sale: he gave him the stolen item in exchange for his garment, so the recipient does not benefit gratuitously from the stolen property, and at the time it was given to him he did not know it was stolen. This is the implication of Kesef Mishneh.
