If only a minority of his property is his own, etc. See above at the beginning of siman 358, where the Tur, Mechaber, and Rambam rule that one may not buy from a thief an item most of which is presumed stolen. In the Derishah there I distinguished the shepherds and similar people mentioned there, who have the established status of thieves so that whatever they possess is ordinarily presumed stolen. Although there is a minority of shepherds who are not thieves at all, the person offering the goods is presumed part of the majority. Here, by contrast, the case is one where it is known that this particular person possesses a minority of money that is not stolen. See what I wrote there further concerning this.
