As for the Rambam's ruling in chapter 6 of Hilkhot Geneivah that if the majority of a certain type of merchandise is stolen, one may not purchase it, as the Mechaber also writes at the beginning of siman 358, it appears that there he rules like Rav. Alternatively, the distinction is between a majority of the items and a majority of the money: where most items are stolen it is forbidden, but where most of the seller's money comes from theft it is permitted. This is the principal explanation.
