The wording of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 5 of Hilkhot Gezeilah. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is explicit in the Mishnah and Gemara in HaGozel U'Ma'akhil, Bava Kamma 113a, and is explained by what is written in siman 356.
There, law 7. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's position is supported by the fact that regarding one who recognizes his vessels and books in another's possession, Bava Kamma 115a, the Gemara cites Rav Chisda's rule that if one robbed and the owners had not despaired, and another person came and consumed it, the owner may collect from either, 111b. Some disagree and say that an ordinary robber is presumed like a notorious thief because news of robbery spreads. Even that is not decisive. The primary rule is not to remove the object from the purchaser without reimbursing him unless the thief was notorious.
