(Seif 6: like one who steals from a robber.) It appears to me that if a person cannot avoid paying an excessive amount except by withholding payment entirely, he may withhold even the portion lawfully due. He is not obligated to hand his money over to a robber, and the robber caused his own loss. Nevertheless, he should attempt to include the proper amount in some other accounting or payment so that the lawful tax eventually reaches the official.
