For the presumption of this occupation is that it is robbery. This is the correct reading in Rambam as well, and it appears in both large and small printings. "This occupation" means the occupation of customs collectors. The Beit Yosef cited a version of Rambam reading, "for the presumption of this food is that it is stolen," and distinguished food as more strongly presumed stolen. That reading is incorrect. See the Derishah, where I further reconciled Rambam's words.
One may not exchange dinars from their collection box, etc. Only regarding currency exchange, where there is little benefit, do we distinguish between exchanging money from their box, which consists of the tax collections themselves and is prohibited, and exchanging from money they have in their home, which is permitted. With other substantial benefits, as stated immediately before this, it is prohibited to benefit from them even from property in their house. This is straightforward.
