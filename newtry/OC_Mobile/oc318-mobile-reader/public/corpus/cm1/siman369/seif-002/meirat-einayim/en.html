He must pay rent to the owners. Land remains in the legal possession of its owners, as explained in simanim 363 and 371.
He robbed palm trees, etc., until it is forbidden to pass over it. Rama's gloss printed in the following seif belongs here. It states: Nevertheless, if he transferred it to the public, it is permitted, etc. I cited the Maggid Mishneh's language in the Perishah. In such a case it is permitted even in a place where the law of the kingdom does not otherwise govern this issue. I found the same gloss in Rama's own handwritten notes attached specifically to the law of stolen palm trees.
