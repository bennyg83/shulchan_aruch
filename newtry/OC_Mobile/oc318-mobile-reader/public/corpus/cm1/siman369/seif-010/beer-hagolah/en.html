The Maggid Mishneh writes that this too is in Bava Batra 55a, where the king says that whoever pays the land tax may possess the land; alternatively, it follows from Bava Metzia 73b, that land is pledged for its tax. The meaning is that where the tax collector sold the land, the sale is certainly permanent; the discussion is where someone took possession on his own and paid the king's tax, Maggid Mishneh.
