For he is like one who steals from the robber. Concerning this people say, "One who steals from a thief tastes the taste." In the Perishah I wrote that where customs officials rely upon merchants' declarations of how much merchandise they have, it is permitted to state matters in a way that prevents the official from taking more than the amount legally fixed. For example, if a merchant has forty cubits of cloth and under the law he owes only thirty large coins in customs, but if the official knows that he has forty cubits he will force him to pay forty large coins, the merchant may tell the official that he has only thirty cubits and then pay only the thirty coins actually owed. The same applies in comparable cases. This is why the Mechaber carefully writes that it is forbidden to evade the fixed amount of the tax. What is beyond the legally fixed amount may be evaded.
