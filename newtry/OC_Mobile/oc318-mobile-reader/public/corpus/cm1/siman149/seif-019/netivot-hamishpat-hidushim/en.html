Some say it is not a chazakah. Even if he held the property for three years after the minor became an adult and claims that he purchased it after majority, he is not believed according to this view. We assume that he never purchased it and that the former minor, even after adulthood, did not know that the property had belonged to his father and therefore did not know to protest. Sema. See Beurim: if the minor later dies, an adult heir inherits him, and the possessor then holds it for three years in the adult heir's presence, chazakah is established, because the adult heir knows the property of his own ancestors.
