After the guardianship passed. See Tumim. I proved that the case is where it is known that the guardian already delivered some of the orphans' fields to them because his period of appointment ended, but it is uncertain whether this field was also delivered. If he then possesses this field for three years, the possession can establish title, because had it belonged to the orphans we would expect him to have delivered it as well and the orphans would not have remained silent. But if it is not known that he delivered any of the other property, then even after the formal period of guardianship ended, his possession does not establish title, just as above where beit din appointed him. The matter requires study.
