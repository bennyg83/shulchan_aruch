Whenever the Sages say that a particular person has no chazakah, this refers to a case where witnesses establish, or we otherwise know, that the item belonged to Reuven and is now held by Shimon, who says it is his or that he purchased it. Otherwise the rule plainly does not apply, because the possessor would be believed by a migo that he could have denied the matter entirely. Maharshdam, Choshen Mishpat siman 213. The compiler says that this is necessarily so. Where the authorities dispute the laws of chazakah, see Maharit, Choshen Mishpat simanim 28 and 75, Rashach part 1 siman 126, and Mahara Sasson siman 34, concerning who is considered the established possessor.
