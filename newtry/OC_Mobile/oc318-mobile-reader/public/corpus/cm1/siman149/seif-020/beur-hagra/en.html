The rule that no chazakah is established... According to Ri Migash, Rabbah bar Sharshom had not possessed the property for three years during the father's lifetime. Had he done so, he could have claimed that he had purchased it. Rather, his three years of possession began after the father's death. Although ordinarily no chazakah is established against a minor only when it is known that the property belonged to his father, here the public report that it had belonged to the father undermined the chazakah, because the possessor knew that a minor cannot protest and therefore should have preserved his document. This is the meaning of "no chazakah is established against a minor," and why the Gemara says he could not claim purchase. See also Kesubos 17b, where in the first case the rule of "the mouth that prohibited is the mouth that permitted" applies.
If he established possession. This follows Rashbam's explanation and the Gemara in Kesubos, and accords with Ri Migash as above.
