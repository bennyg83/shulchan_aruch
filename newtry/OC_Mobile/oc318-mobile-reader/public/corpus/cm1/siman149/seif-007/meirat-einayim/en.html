And they all have one common purse. The Rashba writes in a responsum, cited by the Beit Yosef, that brothers are considered partners in property they inherited jointly from their father, and therefore do not establish presumptive ownership against one another in that inherited property. This implies that even if they do not maintain a common purse, they do not establish possession against one another with respect to that inheritance. The Mechaber teaches here that where they do share one purse, they do not establish possession against one another even regarding property originally belonging to only one of them. This is also evident from the Tur later in this siman, seif 37, where he cites Ri Migash and compares brothers to father and son. Between father and son, neither establishes possession against the other even regarding property in which the other had no original share. See the Perishah.
