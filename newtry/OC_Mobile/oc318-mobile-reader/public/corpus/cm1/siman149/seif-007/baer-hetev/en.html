Against all of them. The Beit Yosef cites a responsum of the Rashba that property inherited jointly by brothers is treated as partnership property, so they do not acquire chazakah against one another. It appears there that this is true even when they do not share a common purse. The Mechaber teaches here that where they do share one purse, they do not acquire chazakah against one another even regarding property that originally belonged exclusively to one of them. This is also evident from the Tur in this siman, seif 37. Sema. See Responsa Maharam Alashkar, end of siman 40.
