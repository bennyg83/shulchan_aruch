The charity administrators have a share, etc. The reason is that the poor should not suffer a loss, etc., merely because the administrator failed to protest. This is similar to what Maharam wrote in seif 24 concerning a guardian who failed to protest. The Mechaber ruled there without qualification, and likewise ruled here without qualification; see Sema. See Tumim, where I wrote that his words are not compelling. I also discussed the substance of the Rama's ruling, which was taken from a responsum of the Rashba, and showed that this conclusion is not necessarily established by the Rashba's own words. See there also the reason I gave for why the Rama nevertheless ruled this way.
