The language of Rambam at the end of chapter 13, from Rabbi Yochanan's statement on Bava Batra 46a defining a robber, etc. Maggid Mishneh writes that some explain it as where witnesses testify that he stole the land from the owner, while others explain it as where he stole it and beit din removed it from his possession, and now it is again seen in his hands. Tur likewise writes so.
In accordance with Rav Chisda's statement there.
Maggid Mishneh writes: know that according to everyone, one who has been established as a robber concerning this particular field may nevertheless acquire chazakah in other fields. But one who kills over monetary disputes has no chazakah in any land in the world. This is straightforward.
