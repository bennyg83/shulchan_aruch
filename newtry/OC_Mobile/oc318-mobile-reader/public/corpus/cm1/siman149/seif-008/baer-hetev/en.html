That she lent. The Taz explains that the wording means she claims that she lent it, for if it is known that she lent it, the statement that people ordinarily lend such an item would not be relevant. This also follows from the language of the Terumat HaDeshen cited by the Beit Yosef. The Shach writes that the wording implies this rule even when she is not supported at his table, but the practical ruling requires study. See Responsa Rashach, part 2, siman 6.
