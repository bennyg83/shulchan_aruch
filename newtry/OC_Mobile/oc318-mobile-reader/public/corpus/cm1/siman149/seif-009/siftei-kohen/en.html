And he built and demolished. See the Sema, seif katan 10. The Bach writes that building and demolishing are not legally identical to digging pits. See there, and see Imrei Emet, pages 87 and 72.
The same applies to a chazakah concerning nuisances, etc. See the Sema, seif katan 12, through the words, 'according to the Rosh.' There is a scribal error there, because the Rosh requires an accompanying claim. The text should read, 'according to Rambam.'
