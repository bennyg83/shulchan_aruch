A father possessing his daughter's property, and a daughter or son possessing the mother's property, have the same law as a son possessing the father's property and a father possessing the son's property. {Rama: The same applies to other relatives who do not object to one another's use, as the judges determine from the circumstances. See Responsa Rashba 950, Responsa Ramban 93, Rivash 252, and notes 8, 46, and 57.}
