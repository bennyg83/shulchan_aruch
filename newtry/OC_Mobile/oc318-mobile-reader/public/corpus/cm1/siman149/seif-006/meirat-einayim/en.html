The correct reading is: "Likewise, the daughter," etc., with the Hebrew letter kaf in "likewise," and not "and the son." The point is to teach that a daughter or son with respect to their mother's property has the same law as a son with respect to his father's property, as stated above in seif 3.
The same applies to other relatives. Some editions place this gloss in seif 7, but that is not its proper place. It belongs here, where the Mechaber states the law of a father with respect to his daughter's property. Rama adds that the same applies to other relatives. The Rashba states these matters together in one responsum, which Darkei Moshe cites.
