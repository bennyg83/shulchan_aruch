Rambam says one in this position collects without an oath. Raavad argues that he should have to swear like a creditor holding collateral under the Geonic rule. Maggid Mishneh answers that here the subject is land, and oaths are not imposed concerning land. See the discussion of this issue in siman 72, seif katan 31, and the Shach concerning Rava bar Sharshom, where a person holding a document could in any event swear and collect even without possession.
