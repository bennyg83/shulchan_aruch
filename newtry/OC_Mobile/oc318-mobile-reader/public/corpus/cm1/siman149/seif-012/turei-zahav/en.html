(Seif 22) Without an oath. I see no basis for Sema's distinction between this case and Rama's ruling in siman 133 that heirs are considered possessors. When the articles are ordinarily lent or rented, their possessory presumption is removed for all purposes. It appears that Rama also follows the Rosh's ruling in siman 150, seif 6, that an oath of hesset is required.
