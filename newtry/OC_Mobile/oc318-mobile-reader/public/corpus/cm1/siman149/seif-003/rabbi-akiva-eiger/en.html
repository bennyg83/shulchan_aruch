[Shulchan Aruch] One who has been established. Note: The Tur writes that even if it is not known that on this occasion he came upon the property by robbery, once he was previously established as a robber with respect to it and it left his possession as property obtained by robbery, he can never thereafter establish a chazakah in it. Chelkat Mechokek likewise writes this in the name of some authorities. See further in the Tur regarding a case where it is known that he repented.
