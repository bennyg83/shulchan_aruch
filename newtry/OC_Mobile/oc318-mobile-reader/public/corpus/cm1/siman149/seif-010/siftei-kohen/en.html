If she was divorced, even if the divorce is uncertain, etc. Although he remains obligated to support her and did not designate another field for her maintenance, she nevertheless can establish chazakah. Since he now hates her, it is presumed that he would not simply give her the produce for her maintenance without legal obligation. Therefore, if he allowed her to consume it freely, she must have acquired it. Rashbam writes this, and the Sema cites him; the Rosh writes likewise. Tosafot, however, write that even here the case is one in which he designated another field for her maintenance.
