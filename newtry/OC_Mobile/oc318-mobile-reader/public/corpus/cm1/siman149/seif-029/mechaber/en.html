Guardians cannot establish presumptive ownership, whether they were appointed over this field or over other property. {Rama: Some say a guardian may establish ownership of other fields over which he had no authority.} This applies whether beit din appointed them, the orphans' father appointed them and the orphans retained them after reaching adulthood, or a person appointed a guardian over his expenditures and receipts. Since they use the property with permission, they cannot establish ownership. {Rama: But the owners may establish presumptive ownership of property held by the guardians.} See note 40.
