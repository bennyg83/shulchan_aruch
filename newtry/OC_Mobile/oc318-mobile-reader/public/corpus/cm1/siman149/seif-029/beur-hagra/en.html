Whether appointed by... or... Bava Metzia 39a. Tosafos asks why Rav Huna's rule is needed if a guardian in any event cannot establish chazakah. The answer is that guardians never establish chazakah even against an adult, while an ordinary possessor could in principle establish chazakah against a minor absent the special rule. Gittin likewise distinguishes an appointee of beit din, yet both kinds remain fiduciaries.
