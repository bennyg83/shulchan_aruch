Maggid Mishneh cites the Mishnah concerning guardians, etc. The Gemara there does not explain the matter explicitly, but HaMafkid, Bava Metzia 39a, shows that a guardian appointed by beit din has no chazakah even after the minor matures, all the more so one appointed by the father. The other details are learned from Chezkat HaBatim 52a: one who manages the household and has deeds and documents issued in his own name bears the burden of proof. All the more so he has no chazakah. Perhaps Rambam found this stated more explicitly elsewhere.
