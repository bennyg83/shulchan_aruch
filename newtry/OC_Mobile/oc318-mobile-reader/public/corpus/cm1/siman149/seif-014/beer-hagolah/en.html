Maggid Mishneh writes that these words are clear: a gentile's claim is not a claim that creates an obligation to swear, and we do not find anywhere in the Gemara an oath imposed solely by a gentile's allegation. Raavad, Mordechai, and Ran write that if the Jew possessed the property for forty years, this constitutes chazakah by virtue of the rule regarding Persian sharecroppers, Bava Batra 55a.
