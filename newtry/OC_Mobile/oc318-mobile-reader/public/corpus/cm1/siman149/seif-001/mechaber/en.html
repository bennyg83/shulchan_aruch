Those who cannot establish presumptive ownership: partners, sharecroppers, guardians, robbers, brothers, a deaf-mute, a mentally incompetent person, and a minor. It contains 31 se'ifim.
If Reuven consumed the produce of Shimon's field for the years establishing possession and claims that he purchased it, and Shimon brings witnesses that the field is known to belong to him and also witnesses that Reuven is known to be his partner, sharecropper, or guardian, which explains why Shimon did not protest, the field is returned to Shimon. Shimon takes a heses oath that he neither sold nor gave it away. But if Shimon brings no proof that Reuven was his partner or sharecropper, and Reuven himself admits, 'Yes, I was his partner, and he sold it to me,' Reuven is believed through a migo that he could have said he was never Shimon's partner. {Rama: If two people jointly own land and another person possesses it, then although his possession does not establish ownership against one partner, such as because he was that partner's sharecropper or guardian or because that partner was outside the country, his possession is nevertheless effective against the other partner with respect to the latter's share. See notes 40 and 50.}
