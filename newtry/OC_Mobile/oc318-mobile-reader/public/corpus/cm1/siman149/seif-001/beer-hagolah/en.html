The language of Rambam, chapter 13 of Hilkhot To'en, law 3, from the Mishnah in Chezkat HaBatim, Bava Batra 42a.
This applies where the property is not subject to division, as explained immediately below in seif 2. Tur writes likewise.
Maggid Mishneh concludes there that if one says, "I was his partner, then I purchased it from him and consumed its produce for the years of chazakah," he is believed. It is explained there that a migo is accepted in many circumstances.
