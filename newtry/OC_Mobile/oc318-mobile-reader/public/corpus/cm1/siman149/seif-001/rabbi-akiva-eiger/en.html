[Shulchan Aruch] In the possession of both of them. Note: If one of the partners rented out the entire house and collected all the rent, this constitutes a chazakah, because with respect to division of the rental proceeds the matter is susceptible to division. If he has not yet collected the rent, then even if the property is susceptible to division, one may say that this does not establish a chazakah, because a partner is treated as one who enters with permission, and what he does is on behalf of both. So writes Maharit, part 2, siman 26. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 3, siman 161.
