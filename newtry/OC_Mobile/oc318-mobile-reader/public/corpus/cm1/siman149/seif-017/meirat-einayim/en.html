I purchased it from you after I converted, he is believed. The Tur, citing the Rosh, gives the reason: he is like a Jew who derives title from a gentile and is believed to say, "I purchased it from you." The Mechaber carefully writes that the convert claims he purchased it after conversion. This excludes the Ir Shushan's statement that he is believed even to say he purchased it before conversion. See the Derishah.
