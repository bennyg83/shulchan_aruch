After. The Sema writes that the Mechaber was precise in writing, 'after I converted,' unlike the Ir Shushan, who writes that he is believed to say, 'I purchased it even before I converted.' The Taz agrees with the Ir Shushan. See there. If the possessor claims that the objector acted as a broker in his purchase from the non-Jew, it is as though he claimed that the non-Jew purchased it in his presence. This is stated in Responsa Mabit, part 1, siman 214, and cited by Knesset HaGedolah.
