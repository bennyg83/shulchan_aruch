Therefore, all the produce that he consumed, etc. This is also the language of the Rambam. The Tur, however, writes: 'The seller or donor takes the produce until the date of the second deed.' The Sema writes that in Derisha he explained a practical difference between them. In his view, the Tur holds that the seller or donor takes only produce that still exists, but the purchaser or recipient need not pay for produce already consumed, whereas the Rambam holds that he must pay for it. The Bach did not write so, and his words are astonishing, because the Rosh and the abridgment of his rulings explicitly indicate that the Rosh's view is as written by the Tur.
