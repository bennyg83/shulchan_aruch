[Shulchan Aruch] And if he did not add. Note: If in the first deed he wrote, "on condition that you do a certain act," and in the second he wrote, "on condition that you give me two hundred zuz," we say that he gave him the choice to fulfill either of the two conditions. But if in one he wrote, "on condition that you give me two hundred zuz," and in the second, "on condition that you give me three hundred zuz," we say that the second nullified the first. This is evident from the sugya in Gittin 76a and Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer, siman 163, seif 10. If the two gifts were made orally, one witness to the first condition and one witness to the second condition do not combine.
