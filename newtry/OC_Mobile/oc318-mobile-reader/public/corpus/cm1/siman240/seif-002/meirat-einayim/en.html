He wrote the second deed only as an addition. The Tur writes that the purchaser collects the added amount from the date of the second deed, while the principal right is collected from the date of the first. In the Derishah I cited those who disagree and explained at length why this differs from two ketubot issued by one husband to one wife, where we rule like the dissenters. One distinction is that with ketubot, when a husband adds an amount it is customary to write explicitly, "and I added for her from my own property." If he did not write that, the omission indicates that he did not intend an addition. In a sale deed there is no comparable standard wording. Therefore, even if the second deed does not say explicitly that it is supplemental, we interpret it as adding to the first. The principal right dates from the first deed.
