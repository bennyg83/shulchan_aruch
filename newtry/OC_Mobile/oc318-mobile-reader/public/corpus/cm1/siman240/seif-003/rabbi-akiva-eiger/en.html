[Sema, letter 8] From the date of the first, they are invalid. Note: And the same witnesses signed it. This is because we are uncertain whether the halachah follows Rafram or Rabbi Acha, and therefore we maintain the deed in its existing status. So writes the Derishah. See the Ran, who writes that where both documents are gifts, Rabbi Acha also agrees that we say he acknowledged the earlier gift, because with a gift there is no concept of waiving the purchase price. Accordingly, in such a case all of his deeds are invalid. Tosafot, however, write that even with a gift there can be waiver.
