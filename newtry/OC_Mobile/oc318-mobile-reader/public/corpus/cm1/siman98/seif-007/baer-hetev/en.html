Appropriate. Whenever beit din fixes a period, Shabbat and Yom Tov are included even though the person cannot perform on those days the act for which the time was granted. So writes Shiltei Giborim. It appears to me, however, that everything depends on the judge's assessment. If they grant him three days immediately before Yom Tov, and it is obvious that he can do nothing during those three days, they surely mean three days after Yom Tov. See these laws in Responsa Maharanach, part 1, siman 28; Maharshadam, Choshen Mishpat, simanim 86 and 396; Maharashach, part 2, siman 156; Responsa Maharshal HaLevi, Even HaEzer, siman 14; and Maharam Alshich, siman 17.
