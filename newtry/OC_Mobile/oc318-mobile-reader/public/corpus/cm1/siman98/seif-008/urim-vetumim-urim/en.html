That one must wait twelve months. Sema writes that this is not an absolute period; everything depends on what appears necessary to beit din. If the judges see that more time is genuinely needed, they may grant more. Chiddushei Rashba to Bava Kamma does not appear to say this. Nevertheless Sema's view is primary. Rif, Rosh, and Rambam omit the fixed rule entirely, which indicates that twelve months is not an inflexible measure. It is obvious that when beit din sees that immediate collection would cause the borrower significant loss, it can delay, just as with movables. See what I wrote in Bava Kamma. Gidulei Terumah likewise rules this way.
