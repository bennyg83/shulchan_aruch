In which it is not written, “We tore it,” etc. In siman 103 I explained the reason: perhaps beit din will inadvertently leave the promissory note in the lender’s possession and the lender will then go to another court and ask for an authorization against a different field. When the authorization states that the note was torn, however, beit din certainly would not write that statement until the note had in fact already been torn. I wrote a similar point in siman 41.
