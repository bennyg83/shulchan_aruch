[Shulchan Aruch] From the time the days of public announcement have concluded. Note: See Maharshal at the end of the chapter HaNose et HaIshah, who writes that this applies specifically where the debtor has no money with which to pay, or where the creditor comes to collect from a purchaser. But if the debtor refused to pay and beit din descended to his property, the creditor consumes the produce from the time the writ of authorization reaches his hand. Tosafot and the other authorities there, however, indicate that they do not accept this distinction. See there.
