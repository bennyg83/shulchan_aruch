When does this apply? When he was, etc. This refers back to the previous ruling that they wait ninety days.
They immediately write an authorization to seize property. Here "immediately" means literally immediately: even the first thirty days preceding the ninety-day period are not granted, because he refuses to appear before beit din, as explained in siman 100. But in the following clause, concerning a document of deposit, where the Mechaber says that they "immediately" write against his property, "immediately" is not literal. He is still given the initial thirty days when he claims that he has evidence invalidating the deposit document. The Tur and Mechaber use "likewise" only to teach that in a deposit case they do not wait the full ninety days.
Likewise, if the document concerns a deposit, etc. A deposit is not subject to the reasons for waiting ninety days, because the deposited item was given with the expectation that it be returned in specie.
