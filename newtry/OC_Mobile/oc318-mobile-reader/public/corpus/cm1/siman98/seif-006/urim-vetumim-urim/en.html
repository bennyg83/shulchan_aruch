Concerning a deposit. A deposit must ordinarily be returned in its existing form, so what need is there to grant extended time? Nimukei Yosef writes that this applies where the defendant denies the deposit. But if he admits receiving it and says he was negligent and is therefore liable for its value, time may be granted. See Tumim. The meaning is not ninety days but thirty days, like the rule below in siman 100, because he does not claim the document is forged. I also wrote there that Maggid Mishneh appears to disagree with Nimukei Yosef, so this ruling requires study.
