There Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam holds that an announcement made concerning unencumbered property has no fixed duration, but is left to the judges' assessment. From the Mishnah's statement that appraisal of orphans' property is announced for thirty days, it follows that this fixed period applies specifically to orphans. The same rule applies when collecting from purchasers, since they are like orphans in that collection from them requires an oath.
