The possessor begins eating fruits only from the stage of the final court authorization and transfer. Shitah Mekubetzet explains that the preliminary writ of authorization is not enough because the creditor still does not know which land will ultimately be collected and there has been no appraisal or acquisition. The Shulchan Aruch in siman 103 likewise indicates that until the land has actually been transferred and confirmed, appreciation or depreciation remains in the borrower's domain. Thus the creditor acquires fruits only after actual judicial transfer.
