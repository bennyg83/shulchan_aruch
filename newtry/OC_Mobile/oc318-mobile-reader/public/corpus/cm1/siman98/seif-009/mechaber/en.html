How is the writ of authorization written? If the creditor is placed in possession of unencumbered property, they write: 'So-and-so was held liable in judgment to so-and-so for such-and-such amount and did not pay voluntarily. We have therefore written this authorization concerning his specified field.' Three appraisers then assess for the creditor a portion of that field corresponding to his debt, and it is announced for the period they deem proper, as also stated in the Tur, siman 102, seif 2. When the bidders cease adding to their offers, the creditor is placed in possession of the portion assessed for his debt, and the promissory note is torn if one exists. If the debtor has no unencumbered property, they write: 'So-and-so was held liable to so-and-so for such-and-such amount under the promissory note in his possession and did not pay his debt. We found no unencumbered property belonging to him. We have already torn the note against him and authorized so-and-so to search and investigate, extending his authority over any property he may find belonging to the debtor. He may collect his debt from every parcel of land the debtor sold from such-and-such date onward.' After this authorization is written, the creditor searches. If he finds unencumbered property, it is assessed for him from that property. If he finds property encumbered by a sale made after the date of his note, he seizes it. The writ of authorization is then torn and a writ of seizure is written for him. How is it written? 'So-and-so son of so-and-so prevailed in judgment and is entitled to seize, for the debt of such-and-such amount owed him by so-and-so, the specified field purchased by so-and-so for such-and-such amount at such-and-such time. We have torn the authorization he held and authorized him to seize such-and-such amount from this purchaser.' After the writ of seizure is written, three experts enter the field and assess for the creditor a portion corresponding to the principal of his debt and half the appreciation. The property is announced for thirty days, as is done with the property of orphans. The borrower then swears that he owns nothing, if he is present in the country. The seizing creditor swears while holding a sacred object that this debt was not paid, forgiven, or sold to another. He is then placed in possession of the purchaser's property at its assessed value, and a writ of placement is written. How is it written? 'After we assessed property for so-and-so under the appraisal he held, announced it for thirty days as required, and administered the oath to this seizing creditor and creditor, we placed him in possession of the specified field to use as a person uses his own property.' From when may the creditor consume the produce of this field? From the end of the announcement period. If the writ of authorization contains an error, he may not consume its produce even after the announcement period has ended. See notes 40, 140, 149, and 506.
