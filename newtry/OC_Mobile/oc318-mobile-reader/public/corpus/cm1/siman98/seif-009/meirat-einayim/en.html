Until those who increase their bids cease, etc. The Tur cites this in siman 103:8 in the name of the Rambam and comments that the Rambam’s language appears to require a thirty day proclamation only when selling property of orphans. The Tur argues that there should be no distinction, because whenever property is sold against its owner’s will by order of beit din, it requires appraisal and proclamation just as in the case of orphans. The Baal HaTerumot likewise writes this in the name of several leading authorities, and the Beit Yosef cites them there. It is therefore surprising that here both the Mechaber and Rama state only the Rambam’s formulation without mentioning the dissenting opinions at all.
