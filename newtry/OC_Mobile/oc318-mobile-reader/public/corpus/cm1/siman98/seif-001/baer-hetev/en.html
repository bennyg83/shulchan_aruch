Although this type of conduct is effective regarding a wife's ketubah, as the Tur and Mechaber write in Even HaEzer, siman 106, the Gemara states that they were lenient specifically regarding a ketubah. Since the husband honored her by allotting her a share together with the sons, even though she did not receive as large a portion as the sons, we say that by remaining silent she waived her claim. With other creditors, however, Chazal were not lenient enough to infer a waiver merely from silence. Sema.
