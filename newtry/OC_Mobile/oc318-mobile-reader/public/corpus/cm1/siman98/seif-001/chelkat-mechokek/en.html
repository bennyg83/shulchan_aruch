A vow and an oath. The language is not specific. The same applies if he merely said she was exempt, because every exemption or waiver can be effected by speech alone, as appears in the first chapter of Kiddushin. Nimukei Yosef in chapter HaKotev, citing Ritva, says similarly. Ran at the beginning of chapter Af Al Pi writes that if he exempted her from the ketubah oath, he also exempted her from an oath concerning the additional ketubah.
