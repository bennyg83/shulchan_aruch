The procedure for collecting a debt, and one who requests time to invalidate a document but does not return at the appointed time. It contains 11 se'ifim.
The procedure for collecting a debt is as follows. When a lender produces an authenticated promissory note, we tell the borrower, 'Pay.' Even if the lender waited many years without demanding payment, we do not say that he forgave the debt because he delayed so long. Even if we heard that he entirely despaired of the debt and said, 'Woe for my financial loss,' this is not abandonment. {Rama: Even if the lender saw the borrower transfer all his property to his sons, leaving the lender only a small parcel of land, and remained silent, we nevertheless do not say that he forgave the debt.} Even if the promissory note is no longer in the lender's possession, he may collect when the debtor admits the debt.
