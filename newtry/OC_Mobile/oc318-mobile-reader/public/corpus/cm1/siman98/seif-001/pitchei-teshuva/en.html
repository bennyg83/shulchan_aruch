We do not say that he waived it. Shaar Mishpat writes that the lender collects without an oath, and even a general ban need not be pronounced, because even if he waived the debt in his heart he may retract so long as there is no compelling circumstance demonstrating waiver. Responsa Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, siman 45, rules that waiver in the heart is not a waiver unless there is a clear circumstance proving it. Although the Bach in siman 146 appears otherwise, Maharit's view is primary. See the extended discussion there and what is written above in siman 12, seif 8, seif katan 14.
