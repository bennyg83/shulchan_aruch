The language of Rambam, chapter 13, law 2 of Hilkhot Malveh, cited by the Tur in seif 6. Maggid Mishneh writes that this is Rava's statement that a document may be authenticated even outside the presence of the litigant, even if he stands and protests, there in Bava Kamma. Rambam evidently understands that the document is authenticated and collection may proceed upon it even while he protests, although at times the court grants a delay when he requests time, etc.
From the Gemara there: if he is a violent person.
In the name of the Rosh, and Baal HaTerumot writes likewise in the relevant Shaar.
