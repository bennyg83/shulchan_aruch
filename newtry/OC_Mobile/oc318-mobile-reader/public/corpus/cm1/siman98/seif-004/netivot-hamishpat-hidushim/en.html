We set a time for him. According to beit din's assessment, depending on whether the witnesses are nearby or far away and whether caravans are commonly available. The Sema writes that if the lender is violent and once he collects it will be impossible to recover from him, the debtor is given ninety days. See Tumim, who writes that this is a scribal error and that the primary law is simply that the period depends upon beit din's judgment.
