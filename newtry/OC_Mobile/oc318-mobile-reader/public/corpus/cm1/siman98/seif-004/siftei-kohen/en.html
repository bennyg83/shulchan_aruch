It is deposited with a third party. See the Sema, who writes that the debtor is initially granted ninety days. His words imply that these ninety days are granted immediately when the debtor claims that the document is forged. This is incorrect. Plainly, the ninety days mentioned here begin only after the thirty days granted before the writ of authorization is issued, as stated in the Gemara. According to the Sema's reading, Baal HaTerumot would contradict the Gemara.
