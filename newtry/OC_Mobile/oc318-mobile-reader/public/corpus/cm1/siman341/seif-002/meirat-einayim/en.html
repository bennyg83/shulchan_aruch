Some say that for a house of mourning he may not, etc. The Tur cites this in the Rosh's name. It is surprising that here the Mechaber rules simply like the Rambam, while in Yoreh Deah siman 360 he states the Rosh's formulation. Rama notes the inconsistency there. It seems possible, however, that the Rambam does not disagree. His phrase that one may borrow clothing for a house of mourning 'to go and return' can mean for the full seven days of mourning, during which the borrower remains there, and afterward returns it. Likewise, 'for a wedding feast for that day' can refer to a feast lasting one day, while if local practice is for several days the loan lasts until the celebrations end. The Tur says only that the Rosh brings the baraita in another wording, which suggests a difference of formulation rather than law.
