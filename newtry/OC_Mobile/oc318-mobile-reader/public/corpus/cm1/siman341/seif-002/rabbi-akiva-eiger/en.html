[Shulchan Aruch] They may use it. Note: If a renter dies and the heirs wish to use the rented item, it appears to me that even if their father left them no property, they must pay the rent from their own funds. Although they have no greater right than their father, their father likewise had no right to use it for free, so they too should have no such right. Yet one may say that immediately upon renting it the father became a debtor for the rental price and acquired the right of use. The heirs can therefore say: we inherit our father's right of use, while we do not pay his debt. This resembles one who bought movable property without paying, then died, where the heirs acquire the property but need not pay his debt. So too, rental for a day is considered a sale. This may be derived from Responsa Rashba, siman 1028. If their father left property subject to liability, see above, siman 334, seif 1, in the Rama. Afterward I found explicitly in the Ritva, Ketubot chapter 3, that if their father rented an item and died, the heirs may use it throughout the rental term and need not pay the rent if he left them no property subject to liability. See Responsa Bnei Aharon, siman 29.
