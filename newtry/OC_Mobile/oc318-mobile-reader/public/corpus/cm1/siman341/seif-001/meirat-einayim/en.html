The lender may demand it at any time, etc. Only an ordinary monetary loan, which is given for expenditure, receives the default thirty-day term fixed by the Sages, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write at the beginning of siman 73. A borrowed object, by contrast, must be returned in its original form. This excludes Rabbeinu Tam's view that compares an unspecified borrowing to an unspecified loan. See the Derishah.
