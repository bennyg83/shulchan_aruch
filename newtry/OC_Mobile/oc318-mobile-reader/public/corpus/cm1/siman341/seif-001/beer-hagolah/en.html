The wording of Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilkhot She'elah U'Pikadon, law 5.
Rambam there says 'he returns it.' The Maggid Mishneh explains that the statement in Makkot chapter 1, 3b, that one who lends without specifying a term may not demand payment before thirty days, applies only to a loan given for expenditure. A borrowed object, which is returned intact, has no fixed term. Rashi likewise explains in Shabbat, chapter HaShoel, 147a, regarding 'lend me,' that it is not ordinarily written in a document. Ramban and Rashba prove this as well, and it is also evident from Tosafot. This is primary.
