[Shulchan Aruch] They may return it. Note: But the borrower may retract within the term, provided that he actually returns the item to the owner's domain. Before it is returned to the owner's domain, even if he retracted from the borrowing, he remains liable for unavoidable accidents. This is not comparable to a case where the period of borrowing has ended. Nimukei Yosef, Bava Metzia 122a, at the end, in the name of the Rosh. See above, siman 293, seif 2.
