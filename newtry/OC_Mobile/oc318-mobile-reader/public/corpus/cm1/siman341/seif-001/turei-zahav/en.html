(Seif 6: Meaning, you are not, etc.) The Sema's resolution of the Rosh's objection, cited by the Tur, is very forced. If the speaker merely intended ordinary courteous language, he should have said, “With your kindness, lend it to me.” Why did he place “with your kindness” after “lend it to me”? The phrase therefore modifies the legal character of the loan: he asks that this loan be granted with a degree of generosity beyond ordinary loans.
