Wherever he wishes. The Gemara states, "He digs and proceeds until he happens upon it," and so it appears in the Rambam and the rulings of the Rosh. Nowhere is it stated that the borrower may choose to dig in any place he wishes. On the contrary, we rule that the hand of the holder of the document is at a disadvantage. It therefore appears that the Tur and Shulchan Aruch also mean that he digs successively in one place after another until he finds water in one of them sufficient for the purpose for which the property was lent. Shach.
