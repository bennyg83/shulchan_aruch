He may build wherever he wishes. The Gemara says that he digs and continues through however many pits in the land are necessary until he happens upon water. Rambam and the abridged rulings of the Rosh say the same. Nowhere is it stated that the borrower has an unrestricted right to dig at any location of his choosing. On the contrary, we rule that the holder of a document has the weaker hand, whether he is a purchaser or recipient of a gift. Therefore it appears that the Tur and Mechaber's phrase "he builds wherever he wishes," meaning digs, likewise means that he may dig one pit after another in various places until he finds sufficient water for the purpose for which the land was lent to him. See the Perishah.
