The wording of Rambam there, law 8, from Rav Nachman's statement there, 103a.
M.M. M. M. Darabi, Mary, is the father of Samuel:
A.M.R. Darb Mary Brea, I will be there and I will show you
The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rashi explains there that he returns the broken pieces, which accords with Rambam, Tur, and Rosh. Some explain that even if it breaks, he repairs it and continues using it, and ultimately returns only the handle.
The Tur writes in the name of the Ramah that nevertheless he has no permission to lend it to others, under the rule that a borrower may not lend to another.
