If one borrows an implement and says, 'Lend me this as an act of your generosity,' meaning that the lender is not particular about time, and a formal acquisition is made, the borrower may use it forever until it is no longer fit for its function, then return its broken remains. He may not repair it or remake it. Rama: Some say that saying, 'Lend me this implement while it remains good,' creates a perpetual loan as long as it remains usable. Even if returned, he may take it again when needed, provided a formal acquisition was made. Without such an acquisition, he cannot retake it after returning it. If it breaks, he returns the pieces.
