[Shulchan Aruch] They thought it was. Note: Responsa Ginas Veradim, Choshen Mishpat, rule 1, siman 3, cites an early version in the Meiri in the name of an authority: "They thought it belonged to their father," and so forth. If they were heirs who were guarantors, they swear that they did not know that it belonged to the claimant and are exempt from those three payments. It appears that when they swear, they take an oath while holding the object. End quote.
