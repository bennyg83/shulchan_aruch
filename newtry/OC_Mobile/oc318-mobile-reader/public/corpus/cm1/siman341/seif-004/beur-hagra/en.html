Properties. In G-d, property liability and the SS are responsible:
(Supplement) And they slaughtered it, etc. See Be'er HaGolah. Proof comes from Bava Metzia 37b: if one says, 'I have one hundred in your possession, etc.,' and the obligation already existed, it would be a case of 'I do not know whether I returned it,' for which all agree he is liable. Rather, liability arises only when the loss occurs. Nimukei Yosef writes so in Bava Kamma.
(Supplement) And if, etc., and they slaughtered it. This applies only in such a case, but not to the opening case in seif 3, following the latter version and Rav Pappa. See Be'er HaGolah, Nimukei Yosef, and Tosafot. (End of supplement.)
