The Tur in the name of Rosh, following the second version there. This is Rav Pappa's view that where the owner dies the heirs are exempt because liability for unavoidable accidents does not rest upon the borrower until the accident occurs, and at that time the borrower is no longer alive. The Maggid Mishneh writes likewise in the name of Ramban and Rashba, though he leaves Rambam's ruling requiring payment from the estate even where the animal died as requiring study.
