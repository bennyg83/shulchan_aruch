Property. The Sema writes that the Mechaber states simply "property," which includes movable property as well, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the end of siman 218 and in siman 241. The reason is that after the Sages instituted collection from orphans even from movable property inherited from their father, as stated in siman 107, there is no longer a distinction for this purpose between landed property subject to liens and movable property. The Tur likewise writes this. The Shach writes that this is explicit in the Maggid Mishneh cited by the Beit Yosef; see there.
