And even a widow. See Shach, seif katan 4, beginning with the words, ‘And the Baal HaTerumot wrote.’ Regarding the question raised there by Gidulei Terumah, that in any event this would be a migo involving brazenness, which he leaves unresolved, this is not precise, for a migo is indeed said in such a case even though the alternative claim would involve brazenness. See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, siman 130, beginning with the words, ‘And even according to the formulation,’ who writes in defense of the Gidulei Terumah that here her credibility when she says, ‘You gave it to me as a gift,’ rests on the presumption that she would not brazenly make such a claim. We must therefore regard this as a strong presumption that she is unable to make such a brazen false claim, and consequently there is no migo, for by our own premise she cannot brazenly advance that alternative claim.
