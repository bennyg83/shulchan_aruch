He recites the blessing over beer. If beer is unavailable, he may recite the blessing without a cup. This appears from Rambam, Hilchot Ishut chapter 10, and Maggid Mishneh writes that it is obvious that the cup is not indispensable. Rosh and Tur, however, cite Rabbeinu Nissim that one cannot dispense with a cup. I do not know his reason. The Gemara in Ketubot discusses the blessing of erusin and the blessing of nisuin together and mentions a cup for neither.
