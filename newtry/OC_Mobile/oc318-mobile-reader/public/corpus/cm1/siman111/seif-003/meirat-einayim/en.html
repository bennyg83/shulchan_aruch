The condition is effective. The Tur explains that a donor may impose whatever condition he wishes upon his gift. The recipient cannot transfer to others more than the donor intended to give him. If a creditor could seize it, the condition would not be fulfilled, the gift would be void retroactively, and the property would revert to its original owner. The creditor would therefore be taking another person's property for his debt.
