Collect. The Tur writes that if the creditor could seize the property, the condition attached to the gift would thereby fail, the gift would be void retroactively, and the property would return to the original owner. The result would be that the creditor was collecting another person's property for his debt. Shach.
The seller. The Sema explains that in a sale the seller received money from the purchaser. If the seller wishes to void the sale because of the condition, he must return that money, and the creditor can collect those returned funds. Thus when the law says that the creditor collects from the seller, it means up to the amount of the field's value. Ordinarily the seller also has no practical concern whether the land remains with the purchaser or passes to the creditor. See Responsa Mahara Sasson 121.
