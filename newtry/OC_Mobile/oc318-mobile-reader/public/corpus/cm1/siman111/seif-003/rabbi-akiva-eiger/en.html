[Shulchan Aruch] Consecrated property has the same status as encumbered property. Note: If one declares his property ownerless and another person precedes others and acquires it, any loan that may be collected from purchasers may also be collected from this person. See below, siman 275, seif 31. If the creditor himself acquires the ownerless property, however, his debt has thereby been extinguished and he cannot collect the loan a second time. So writes Nimukei Yosef, cited by the Sema below, siman 245, seif katan 18.
