Gidulei Terumah and Knesset HaGedolah write that if the available property is more than three parasangs away, the debtor certainly cannot force the creditor to pursue it; within three parasangs the matter depends on beit din's assessment of burden. Their words are not compelling. If there is no significant burden, why should distance itself matter? Rashba's use of three parasangs was by analogy to the rule of going to a higher court, and Mechaber and Rama omitted that distance-based rule above in siman 13. One should therefore not depart from Rama's formulation. See Tumim. According to authorities who hold that property liens are not Torah law, there is in any event little room for this distinction; the creditor should first pursue available unencumbered property.
