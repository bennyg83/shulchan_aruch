If he acquired and bequeathed, etc. The heir's domain is regarded as the borrower's domain, because a son stands in his father's place. As for the words "some disagree," the Tur and Mechaber state without qualification at the beginning of siman 107, in the following seif here, and at the end of siman 112 that a son stands in his father's place regarding despair and change of possession, and they do not treat an heir's domain as a different domain. See also siman 37 and below simanim 353 and 361. The fact that the Mechaber here writes that some disagree must therefore be because he followed the language of the Baal HaTerumot at the end of Shaar 43, who records such a view.
