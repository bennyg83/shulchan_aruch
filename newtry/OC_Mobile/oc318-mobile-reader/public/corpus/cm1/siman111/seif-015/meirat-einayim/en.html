And he need not take half a field, etc. The Tur and Mechaber likewise write above in siman 107:10 regarding collection from heirs. The reason is that heirs and purchasers are no better than the borrower himself, who may satisfy the lender from whichever intermediate quality property he chooses. He cannot, however, give half of one field and half of another. He could give one whole field here and another whole field elsewhere, and is not required to satisfy the lender in one location. The same rule applies to heirs and purchasers.
