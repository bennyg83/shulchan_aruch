If he gave them, etc., just as, etc. Kiddushin 60a states, according to Rav Huna, ‘and he will give,’ which Rashi explains as meaning whenever he wishes. This parallels the Mishna’s later case, ‘on condition that I give you from now until a stated time’; in the first clause, however, performance may occur whenever he wishes.
