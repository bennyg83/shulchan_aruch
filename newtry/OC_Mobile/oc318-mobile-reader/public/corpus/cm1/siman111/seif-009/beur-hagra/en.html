Reuven, etc. Ketubot 69a states that property may be taken for a daughter’s support whether the heirs sold or mortgaged it. Ameimar explains that a daughter is treated as an heir, and the Sages treated a deathbed gift like inheritance, as Bava Batra 149a states. Rav Ashi disagrees with Ameimar only because he classifies her as a creditor.
We do not suspect, etc. Were such a suspicion entertained, every document would be suspect. It is considered only when a document was found after being lost, because its reliability was impaired, as explained in the first chapter of Bava Metzia.
