For collusion. The Sema explains that this means the heirs already transferred the property to the recipient and left the testamentary document in his possession, or returned it to him, so that he could seize property from purchasers and divide it with them. As long as the testamentary document remains in his possession, we do not undermine other people's documents. See the gloss to siman 41, seif 3, and the end of siman 253.
