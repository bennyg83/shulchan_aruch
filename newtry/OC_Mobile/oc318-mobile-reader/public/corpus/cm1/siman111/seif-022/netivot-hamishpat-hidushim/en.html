If he sold. See the end of siman 85 and you will understand this seif. See Tumim: if no after acquired property clause was written, there is a distinction between a case where the movables have already been collected, as in siman 85, where such a clause is required even if the earlier creditor sold his debt note, and a case where the purchaser of the debt note has not yet collected from the debtor. In the latter case, the creditor may seize the body of the note itself even without an after acquired property clause.
