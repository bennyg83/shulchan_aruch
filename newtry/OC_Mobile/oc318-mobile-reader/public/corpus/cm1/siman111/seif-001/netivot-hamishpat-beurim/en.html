But a creditor by document. See Shach, seif katan 2, through his statement that the Bach likewise holds that he collects nothing. The reason for the distinction appears to be as follows. Where another person gave the wife land, the produce has the status of nichsei melog; therefore, the husband's creditor does not collect from it because of the benefit to the household. But where the husband gave his wife a gift, since by law she acquired it and the husband does not consume its produce, then in the present case, where he gave it to her in a manner under which the husband would consume the produce, his consumption necessarily means that he retained the produce for himself. Consequently, this produce no longer has the status of nichsei melog, but belongs entirely to him. Therefore, the creditor does not collect from it. The law concerning whether an oral creditor collects from gifts of a shechiv mera is explained in Even HaEzer.
