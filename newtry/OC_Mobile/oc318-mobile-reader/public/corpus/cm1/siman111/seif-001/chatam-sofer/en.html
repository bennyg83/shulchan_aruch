Mechaber, seif 13, 'men of force.' Note: This means Jews, but not non-Jews. So writes the Chiddushei Rashba to Nedarim 26b, stating that this is also the reading in Tosafot, Bava Batra 44a; see there. Accordingly, there is a scribal error in Responsa Rashba, siman 1039, where it reads 'a violent gentile'; it should read 'a violent man.' The Shach's difficulty is thereby resolved, as is the Taz's difficulty below in siman 163 concerning Maharik; see there.
