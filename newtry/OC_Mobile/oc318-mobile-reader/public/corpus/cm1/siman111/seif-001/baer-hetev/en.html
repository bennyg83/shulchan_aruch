From gifts. Had the recipient not given him satisfaction, he would not have given him the gift. A gift is therefore treated in this respect like a sale. See above, simanim 115 and 235. Sema.
In a document. The Shach writes that the same applies if the debtor admitted before witnesses and instructed them to write a document. So writes Baal HaTerumot. See the laws in this siman in Responsa Raanach, siman 100, and Responsa Chavot Yair, siman 78.
