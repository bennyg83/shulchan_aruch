(Sema, seif katan 9) The son who borrowed from him, for, etc. Note: Study is required. Once the creditor collects this right from the land, the gift is automatically voided by the condition. The son should then be obligated to return to the estate shared by the brothers all fruits he consumed from that land from the date of the gift onward. The brothers would become creditors for their claim to their respective shares of the produce attributable to the portion of land that falls into their brother's inheritance.
