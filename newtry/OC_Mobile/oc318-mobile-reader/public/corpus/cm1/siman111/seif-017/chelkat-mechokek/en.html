But if she waived her ketubah to her husband, etc. I already wrote in siman 105 that even if she did not waive the ketubah itself but waived all or part of a ketubah condition, she may do so. This differs from one who obligated himself to support his wife's daughter, where the mother's waiver does not release that obligation. In the following siman the Rama cites Raavad as disagreeing concerning daughters' support, and Maggid Mishneh likewise leaves the matter unresolved. See Ran.
