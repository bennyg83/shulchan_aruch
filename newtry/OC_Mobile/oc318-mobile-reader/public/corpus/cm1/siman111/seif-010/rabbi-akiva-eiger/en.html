[Sema, subparagraph 31] And they wrote at the beginning of siman 107. Note: See Mishneh LaMelech at the beginning of chapter 31 of Hilchos Malveh, who writes that there is no proof from there, because that law concerns the period after the Geonic enactment allowing collection from movable property. Such property was therefore included in the rule that after acquired assets may be collected, as Raanach, part 1, siman 100, writes.
