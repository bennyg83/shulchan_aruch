[Shulchan Aruch] And they received land from him. Note: If one sold or gifted land before borrowing and said, "From today, if I do not retract," and he then borrowed, the creditor can seize the land. Since the borrower retained the power to retract the prior transfer, his creditor likewise has access to that power, and it is as though the borrower retracted. So writes Shitah Mekubetzes, first chapter of Bava Metzia, in the name of the leading authorities.
