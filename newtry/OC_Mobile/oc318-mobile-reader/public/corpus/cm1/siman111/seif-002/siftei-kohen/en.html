Consecrated property has the status of encumbered property, etc. I find a clear proof for this in the Gemara, chapter Shevuat HaDayanim, Shevuot 42b. It is likewise evident from Tosafot, Baal HaMaor, Ramban, and the other authorities at the end of Bava Batra concerning the rule that a person does not collude against consecrated property, and from the Tur and Rambam below in siman 255, seif 3.
