[Rama] And some disagree. Note: This is also the opinion of Tosafot, Ketubot 92a, s.v. “Reuven who sold a field,” see the end of their comments and Maharam Schiff there. One may perhaps distinguish that there the purchaser can subsequently collect from minor orphans. Therefore, the creditor cannot collect from the purchaser, because that would effectively amount to collecting payment from minor orphans. In an ordinary case, however, where the purchaser would not be able to return and collect from the minor orphans, the creditor may collect from him. See above, siman 110, seif 3, and the Sema there. Examine this carefully.
