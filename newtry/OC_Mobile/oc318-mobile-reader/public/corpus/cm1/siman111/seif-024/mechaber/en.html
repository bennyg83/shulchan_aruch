If Reuven held a promissory note against Shimon and transferred it to Levi by writing and delivery, and when Levi sought to collect, Yehudah produced an earlier note against Reuven than Reuven's note against Shimon, claiming that the note Reuven gave Levi belonged to Reuven's creditor and that Yehudah acquired it by priority, the law follows Yehudah. His note is earlier, and Reuven encumbered movable property together with land to him. The enactment protecting ordinary commerce does not apply here. See below at the end of siman 113 and note 205.
