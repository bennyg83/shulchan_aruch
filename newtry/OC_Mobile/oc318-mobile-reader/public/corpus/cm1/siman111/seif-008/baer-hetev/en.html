They divide. The Sema explains that since the donor gave to all three in one document, we assume that he intended all the gifts to take effect together, but a person cannot physically utter two things at precisely the same instant. This differs from siman 104, seif 10, concerning three creditors owed unequal sums who share equally, because there each creditor lent his own money and the debtor's assets became equally subject to each creditor at the time of his loan. A voluntary donor is different. Where his words show an intention to give more to one recipient, that recipient should receive more. Therefore they divide proportionally.
