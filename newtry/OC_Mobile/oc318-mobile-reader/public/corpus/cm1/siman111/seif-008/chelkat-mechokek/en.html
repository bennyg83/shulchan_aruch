Even though he did not leave more than the two ketubah amounts. The same applies if he did not leave enough for two complete ketubot, but only one dinar more than one ketubat benin dichrin. Even though after one set of sons takes their mother's ketubah as a debt the other set will not receive its ketubat benin dichrin in full, the enactment still applies, because it can apply proportionally. So writes Maggid Mishneh, as partially cited above, and similarly in the following seif 9.
