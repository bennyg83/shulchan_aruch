An excess over the first ketubah, even one dinar, etc. This follows the "some say" in seif 7. I have already written that it needlessly uses the language "some say," because Rambam appears to agree, as Maggid Mishneh explains. Rambam's own wording likewise suggests this. In halachah 7 he says, "her sons precede regarding inheritance of her ketubah." The word "precede" implies that even if the other sons will not receive their own ketubat benin dichrin in full, the first sons take priority.
