Seif 1. Note: Ketzot HaChoshen writes here that we interpret a later transaction in light of an earlier one only where the parties had a grievance or dispute with one another. See my responsa, sixth edition, Even HaEzer siman 139, where I noted that he overlooked the explicit words of Ran in Gittin, chapter Mi SheAchazo, s.v. "The Rabbis taught: he said to her before two witnesses," which state the opposite. The law follows Beit Shmuel.
