Tur in the name of Raavad in his gloss, that this matter is judged according to prevailing custom and that we learn from gentile commercial practice. Maggid Mishneh cites some who strained to establish Rambam's rule differently and writes that no proof for Rambam can be brought from the law of brothers who divided inherited property and a creditor later came, Bava Batra 7a. They instead bring proof from the Tosefta: one who lends to his fellow through two guarantors, etc.; if he said, "I may collect from whichever I choose," etc. This is Ramban's view, and Tur writes likewise in his name. Rashba, however, agrees with Rambam and distinguishes between two guarantors and two joint borrowers.
