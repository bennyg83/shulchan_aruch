From the unresolved question that Avuha bar Geniva sent to Rava at the end of Yesh Nochalin, Bava Batra 139a. Rif and Rosh write that since the question was not resolved, an oral loan cannot be collected from her, because the lender caused his own loss by lending without a document. Rambam likewise rules so there, law 13, and adds that if the actual loan money itself is still present, it is returned to the lender. Maggid Mishneh writes that this follows the words of Rav Sherira and Rav Hai, and perhaps they derived it from the Gemara's wording there, "she borrowed and consumed it," implying that the question arises specifically after the money was consumed, whereas if it remains intact it is returned to the lender.
