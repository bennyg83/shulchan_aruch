A slave or wife of a man shall be paid. He is in a way that they must pay if they had a visa:
Until they are freed, etc. The Tur and Mechaber write similarly below in siman 424:9 concerning damages they cause to others. The reason is that a slave and a married woman are persons capable of obligation and acquisition, but at present have no independent property, because whatever a slave acquires belongs to his master and whatever a married woman acquires ordinarily belongs to her husband. A minor, by contrast, even if he undertakes as an absolute guarantor, is not legally capable of obligating himself or conveying in a matter from which he derives no benefit, even after reaching the age at which a minor's ordinary transactions are recognized. Therefore he remains exempt even later. See the Perishah, siman 96, and below simanim 235 and 349 concerning a minor's loans, sales, and gifts.
