(Seif 5: when the guarantor pays the lender, etc.) Rambam's wording is, "when he pays the lender." Meaning, the guarantor, at the time he pays the lender, must tell him on behalf of which borrower he is paying, so that that borrower will become obligated to reimburse the guarantor and cannot say, "You did not pay on my behalf." The lender himself does not care on whose behalf the guarantor pays. It appears to me that if the guarantor paid without specifying, he cannot sue either borrower until he pays the second debt as well or obtains authorization from the lender.
