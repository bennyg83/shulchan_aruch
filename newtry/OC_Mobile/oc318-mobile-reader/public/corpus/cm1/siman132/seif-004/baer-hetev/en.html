And pay. The Sema says the text should read “and pay,” not “and I will pay.” Although an ordinary guarantor who paid the creditor cannot recover from the borrower unless the borrower had first told him, “Become my guarantor and pay for me,” here those words are unnecessary. Since the creditor may collect from whichever obligor he wishes, it is as though each one told the other, “Become guarantor for me,” and as though the creditor litigated with the borrower and the borrower swore that he had no means, in which case even a guarantor who pays voluntarily may recover from him. The same applies here. Compare the rule of a kablan in siman 130 seif 2. The Shach cites a lengthy responsum of Mabit concerning three people who jointly purchased merchandise, wrote a debt document binding themselves, and became kablanim for one another. When the due date arrived, the seller released one of them from his guarantee for the others on condition that he pay only his own share. See there for the resulting practical ruling.
