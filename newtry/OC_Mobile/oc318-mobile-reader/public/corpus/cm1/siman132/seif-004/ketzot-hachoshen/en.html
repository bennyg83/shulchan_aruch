If one co guarantor waived his right against the borrower and the creditor later collected the whole debt from the second guarantor, the second guarantor cannot recover a contribution share from the first. Mabit ruled so. I explained in siman 73, seif katan 8, that this is correct. The first guarantor's release can remove the internal contribution obligation before the second guarantor pays, and the second cannot revive it merely because the creditor chose to collect from him.
