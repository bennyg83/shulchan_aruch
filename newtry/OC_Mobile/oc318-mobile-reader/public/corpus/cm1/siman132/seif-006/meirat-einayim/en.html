The creditor may claim the entire amount from the second guarantor. The Mechaber rules similarly in siman 77:8. The reason is that he follows the Rambam, who holds that where two guarantors exist, the creditor may initially collect the entire debt from either one. Therefore, even if the creditor had not forgiven the first guarantor, he could have collected the entire debt from the second if he wished. But with two borrowers who borrowed from one creditor, if the creditor releases one of them, the Mechaber rules in siman 77:6 that he may collect only half from the other. This follows the Mechaber's view that even without the release the creditor could not have collected the entire debt from either borrower alone. Even according to the Rosh, who does permit collection of the entire debt from either of two borrowers, release of one borrower does not allow collection of the whole from the other as it does here with guarantors. The difference is that here the entire debt remains against the borrower, and therefore against the remaining guarantor. The Tur makes this distinction in the name of the Rosh and writes here that the whole debt remains against the guarantor and the borrower.
