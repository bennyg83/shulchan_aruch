When the creditor is paid, he should notify him, etc. The Tur's text reads "the creditor" with the definite article, while the Rambam reads "to the creditor." The Mechaber used similar language above in siman 77:3. Whichever reading is adopted, I proved in the Perishah and Derishah that the meaning is that the creditor must inform the guarantor to which debt he applied the money received, so that the guarantor can return against that particular borrower. The creditor has the choice to designate the debt to which the payment is applied, as the Tur and Mechaber write above in siman 83:3. See there and the Derishah.
