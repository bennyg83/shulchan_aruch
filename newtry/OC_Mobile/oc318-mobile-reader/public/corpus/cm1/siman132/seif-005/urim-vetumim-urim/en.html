The lender should notify him, etc. Sema understands that the lender must tell the guarantor for which borrower's debt he is accepting payment. The wording, however, 'for which of them he pays,' more naturally means the guarantor must tell the lender on whose behalf he is paying. Otherwise, after paying one debt without specification, he may be unable to recover from either borrower because each can redirect him to the other. The novelty is that notification by the guarantor to the lender suffices. Shach and Taz interpret this way. Taz adds that if the guarantor paid without specification, he cannot sue either one until he also pays the second debt or obtains authorization from the lender.
