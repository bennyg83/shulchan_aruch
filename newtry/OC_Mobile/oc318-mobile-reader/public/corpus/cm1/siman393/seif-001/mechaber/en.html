One who brings produce into another person's courtyard without permission and the courtyard owner is injured by it. It contains four seifim.
If one brings produce into the homeowner's courtyard without permission and the homeowner's animal eats it, the homeowner is exempt. This is so even if the homeowner was a miller and the owner brought wheat for him to grind for payment, because it is still considered entry without permission. If the homeowner's animal slipped on the produce and was injured, the produce owner is liable. If the produce was brought in with permission, the produce owner is exempt. Some disagree, as explained at the end of siman 398. If the homeowner expressly accepted responsibility to guard the produce, the homeowner is liable for its loss.
