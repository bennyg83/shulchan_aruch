If the produce was brought in with permission, and the courtyard owners left the entrant alone there to guard himself and his property, and the homeowner's animal ate the produce and was injured by eating it, the produce owner is liable. Since he saw the animal eating something harmful and allowed it to continue, he is responsible, because the courtyard owners were absent and could not remove the animal. Thus, a woman once entered her neighbor's house to knead and bake. The household withdrew so they would not look at her while she worked. The homeowner's goat ate the dough and died, and the Sages required the woman to pay its value. The same applies in comparable cases.
