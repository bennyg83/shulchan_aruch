There was an incident involving a certain woman, etc. The Mechaber first copied the Rambam's law where the courtyard owner withdrew with the produce owner's knowledge and thereby left responsibility upon him. In this incident he adds that even if the courtyard owner withdrew without expressly informing the produce owner, where the produce owner engages in a private act that ordinarily causes the courtyard owner to leave, and he does in fact leave at that moment, it is as though he notified him. The produce owner then becomes responsible for guarding the courtyard owner's animal, even to prevent it from overeating and injuring itself.
