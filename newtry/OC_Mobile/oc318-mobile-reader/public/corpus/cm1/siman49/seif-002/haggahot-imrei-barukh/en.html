(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 21) At the end of the passage: and it appears that his reason is, etc. Note: It is incorrect to read this into the Shach's words. The Shach himself explicitly explained in his work Tekfo Kohen that his reason is that a claim based on a whichever-way-you-look-at-it argument involving two different people cannot be collected from property. The Tumim therefore properly questioned him.
