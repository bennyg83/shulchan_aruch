When does this apply. This requires study, for it is contrary to the Gemara there. See the Tur, Bach, and Derisha and Prisha.
(Likkut) When does this apply, etc. Since the Mishnah states, 'If one was found,' etc., and afterward, 'What shall they do,' etc., it implies that the remedy applies to this case as well. Although according to R' Yirmiyah's answer, 'What shall they do' refers to distinguishing the names on the receipt, because the discussion concerns a document whose parties are distinguished, according to Abaye's answer it cannot be said to refer to the receipt.
