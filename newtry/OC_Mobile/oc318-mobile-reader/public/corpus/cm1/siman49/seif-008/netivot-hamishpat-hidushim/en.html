The holder of the receipt must bring proof. The Shach concludes like the Tur that the law favors the holder of the receipt. See Beurim: the primary ruling follows the Mechaber, that an untripled receipt is ineffective against a tripled document. The lender whose document is tripled swears and collects, while the lender whose document is not tripled swears and loses because of the untripled receipt. See there for the reasoning.
