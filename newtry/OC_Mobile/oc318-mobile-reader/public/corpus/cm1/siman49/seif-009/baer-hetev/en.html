Authenticated. The Shach writes that the word 'authenticated' should be deleted here. We rule in siman 65, seif 18, that either one factor is sufficient: either the receipt is authenticated, or the document is found among torn documents. The Bach's distinction, that here there is a defect in the receipt and therefore both are required, is incorrect in light of the Gemara. The Taz likewise rejects this answer and notes that even according to the Ramah this is not considered a defect. Anyone who studies the end of the first chapter of Bava Metzia will see that such a distinction cannot be maintained.
