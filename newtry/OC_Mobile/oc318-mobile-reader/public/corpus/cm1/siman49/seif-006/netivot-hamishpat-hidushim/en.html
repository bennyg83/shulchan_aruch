An error. This means a definite error, not a possible error, because perhaps this document is valid and the witnesses have already completed their agency, Shach. If one witness signed and the document was lost before the second signed, they may write another document, because until both have signed they have not completed their agency. The dissenting opinion brought by the Rama concerning a document without a kinyan, which holds that the principle of completed agency does not apply in the same way, also speaks of a document that was lost, and does not actually dispute the first opinion on that point. If the first document was not lost, they may not write another. If they did write another and the borrower afterward says, "Give it," the second document is valid. See Beurim:
With documents. According to this opinion, even if the first document was not lost they may write another, because the principle that the witnesses completed their agency does not apply to the writing alone. But if the document was lost after it had already been delivered, they may not write another, because this view holds that the essential completion of their agency is the delivery. See Beurim. Regarding the Mechaber's statement that if it is clarified that the mistake was a scribal error the document is valid, see Beurim.
