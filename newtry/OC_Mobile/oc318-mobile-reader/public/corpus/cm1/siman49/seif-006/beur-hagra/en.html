And some say that they do not. This is a responsum of the Rashba, derived from Bava Batra 171a, where it states that witnesses may write even, etc., and from Bava Kamma 98b, "let them write for him," etc. He holds that this applies even to witnesses. As for the statement in Bava Batra there, "Rather, witnesses who have performed their agency," etc., see Tosafot there.
