If there is only doubt, the witnesses may not rewrite the document, because perhaps the original document is valid and they have already completed their agency, as explained in Even HaEzer at the beginning of siman 122. Their agency. See above, siman 41, and what is written there in seif katan 2.
With documents. The Sema explains that the principle that witnesses may have completed their agency applies specifically to a get, where the Torah says that he must "write and give," so the husband or his agent must commission the writing. Once the witnesses have carried out that agency, it may be exhausted. Other documents are different. The Shach disputes Rama and writes that the law of the Rashba was not understood correctly. The Rashba states in a responsum that we do not say the witnesses completed their agency except regarding a get, etc., but that statement excludes only a gift document or a sale document without warranty. Regarding a sale document containing warranty and regarding a loan document, the Rashba agrees that agency may be exhausted, as his words explicitly show. The Shach proves this from the Gemara and poskim and concludes that according to all opinions, only when the witnesses made an error may they write another document without concern that their agency was completed. If there was no error, we do say that their agency was already completed. There is therefore no real dispute. This appears primary and clear, contrary to Rama. The Shach also expresses surprise that the Ir Shushan and Sema followed Rama's formulation. See also the beginning of siman 41 and beginning of siman 54.
