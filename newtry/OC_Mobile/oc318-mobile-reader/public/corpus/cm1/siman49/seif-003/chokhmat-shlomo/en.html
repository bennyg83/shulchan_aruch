In the gloss: If there are two men named Yosef ben Shimon in one city, and the father of one of them died, and the document states, “Yosef ben Shimon, may he live,” or “may his light shine,” and the like. See what I wrote in my work on Even HaEzer, later edition, siman 11. There I wrote that the doubt whether the witnesses testify to the entire formulation applies specifically to the name of the borrower. With respect to the name of the lender, however, there is no doubt that they do not testify to every element of the description. See there, where with Hashem’s help I explained the reason for this distinction and also brought a proof for it. Examine this carefully.
