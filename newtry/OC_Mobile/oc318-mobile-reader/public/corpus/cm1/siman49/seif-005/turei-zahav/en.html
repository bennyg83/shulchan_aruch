(In the name of Maharshal, Beit Yosef beginning, “Nevertheless, testimony is unnecessary.”) The Rosh and Nimukei Yosef write so. The Beit Yosef’s proofs are not understood according to the interpretation that the woman says, “It was not I, but another woman bearing my name.” Perhaps she originally brought two witnesses to her name, while the husband failed to examine her appearance closely enough to recognize her later. The Baal HaTerumot instead explains that she may have assumed another woman’s name. This indeed proves that valid witnesses cannot be required, for witnesses who knew her identity would prevent her from later retracting.
