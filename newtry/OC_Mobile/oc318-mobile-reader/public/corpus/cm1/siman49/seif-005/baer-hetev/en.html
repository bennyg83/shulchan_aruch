As for the concern that a fraudster may have adopted the lender's name, even where two people have identical names we do not suspect such fraud, for we rule that two people with the same name living in one city may each produce a debt document against another person. The Shach writes that perhaps the reason also applies to the latter clause when the document is not in the lender's own possession, as stated in the gloss to seif 7. If two people with that name were already known, the debtor can say, 'I owe the other one,' whereas if no second person was known he cannot say this.
