Shak. R. R. R. D. H. B. B. A.D. in his uncle's most jealous field. “Third:
To the second. The Shach writes that the same applies where one person with a given name guaranteed the debt of another person with that same name. Just as the creditor cannot recover from the borrower because of the ambiguity, he cannot recover from the guarantor. See the gloss to siman 129, seif 8, and Responsa Rashach, part 2, siman 11.
