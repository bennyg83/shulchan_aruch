That he purchased. The same applies where one person of a given name inherited a field from another person of that identical name. See Beurim: specifically where the heir does not know independently about the decedent's loan and relies only upon the document. But if he admits that he knows the decedent borrowed and did not repay, he is liable.
To two people with the same name. The same rule applies where one such person acted as guarantor for the other. See Beurim: here too the guarantor says he does not know independently about the other's loan, but only that he said, "Lend to so and so by document or witnesses and I am guarantor," and afterward the lender produces the document or witnesses showing a loan to someone of that name. He is exempt, because such a document proves nothing and is mere worthless paper. But if he admits that he knows which loan occurred, he must pay.
