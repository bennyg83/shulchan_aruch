Who purchased from Yosef son of Shimon, etc. The same law applies where one inherited a field from his uncle Yosef son of Shimon, for the same reason. I proved this further at the end of Tekfo Kohen.
It follows that one who guaranteed for both men named Yosef son of Shimon, etc. The same applies where a Yosef son of Shimon guaranteed for another Yosef son of Shimon. Just as the creditor cannot identify and sue the borrower, he cannot sue the guarantor. See responsa Maharash Kohen, book 2, siman 11, and my comments below to siman 129, seif 8, in the gloss.
