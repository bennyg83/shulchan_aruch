Without a kinyan, neither of the two persons with identical names may collect from the other. See Beurim. Even within the fixed term, the possessor may say, "I returned it to you when you paid me." If he says, "It is true that I am the borrower, but I already paid you," he is likewise believed through a migo that he could have said, "I returned the document to you when you paid." Where he may collect from free assets, he may also collect from encumbered property. In a document that does not name the lender but merely says, "I borrowed from you," all agree that neither person with the identical name may extract from the other, even if the document contains a kinyan.
