If people call a person by a particular name and he answers to it, he is presumed to bear that name to his detriment. For example, if he called himself Reuven and a document was written stating that Reuven borrowed from Shimon, and he now claims, "My name is not Reuven," then if people call him Reuven and he answers, he is liable even though he had not previously been established under that name for thirty days.
