(Seif 9: An authenticated receipt was found with the creditor.) See what I wrote in siman 65, seif 18, explaining why here both authentication of the receipt and placement of the loan documents among the creditor’s cancelled documents are required, whereas there one of these indications suffices. Some answer that the receipt is defective because the loan document distinguishes the parties by a third generation while the receipt does not. This is mistaken, for even according to the Ramah that is not a defect here, as explained above. Anyone who studies the end of the first chapter of Bava Metzia will see that this answer is impossible. See siman 65, seif 18.
