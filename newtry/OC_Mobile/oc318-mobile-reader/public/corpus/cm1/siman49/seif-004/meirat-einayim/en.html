And a document is written against him, etc. It appears the correct reading is 'and a document is written against him,' with the conjunction. Meaning, the lender produces a document stating that Reuven owes him, and Reuven wishes to deny liability by saying, 'My name is not Reuven.' We do not believe him, since people called him by that name and he responded. This is certainly his obligation. It does not mean that this Reuven himself went to the scribe and witnesses to have a debt document written stating that he owes Shimon one hundred, for in that case the fraud discussed above could occur: he might assume the name Reuven in order later to extract money from another Reuven.
