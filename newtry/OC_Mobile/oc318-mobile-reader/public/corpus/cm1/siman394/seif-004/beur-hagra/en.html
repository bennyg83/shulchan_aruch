(Supplement) How, etc., and likewise, etc., even, etc. This follows Rabbenu Chananel and Raavad: only a full se'ah is valued within sixty because it is a substantial amount and one should not unduly burden the damager; less than a se'ah is valued independently. Rabbi Yannai extends this to half a se'ah, while Hezekiah says even a single stalk.
(Supplement) How, etc. This explanation resolves why the Gemara's objection is directed specifically against Hezekiah. The law follows Hezekiah because the entire sugya, including the baraitot and incidents, follows him. Rambam omits the separate rule for a sapling because he holds that the Sages dispute all the preceding views. Rosh disagrees and also deducts the natural deterioration of the vine. See Tosafot and Rosh.
