And its limbs were not crushed, etc. This language implies that if no limb was crushed and the animal suffered only pain, no payment is required. If there is doubt whether crushing occurred, he must pay what the owner would have paid to save the animal from that risk. The language of Tosafot and the Rosh, however, does not clearly support this. See Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 6, siman 13.
