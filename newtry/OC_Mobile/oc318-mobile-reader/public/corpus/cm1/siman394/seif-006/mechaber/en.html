If an animal ate the fruit of one date palm, or a person cut and ate another's dates, then if it was a Roman date palm or similar tree whose dates are not especially fine, the damage is assessed together with sixty comparable units of land. If it was a Persian date palm or similar tree whose dates are exceptionally fine, the palm is assessed independently, according to its value before and after the damage. Rama: Some say that if the fruit had not completed its growth, it is assessed with sixty, but if it was fully grown it is assessed at its actual value.
