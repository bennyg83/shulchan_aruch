One who places another person's animal upon a third person's standing crop must pay for the damage it causes. The same applies if he strikes or drives the animal until it goes to the crop and causes damage. Rama: He is liable even if the produce was in the public domain, because he is considered to have caused the damage directly. This applies where he actually drove the animal. If he merely stood in its path so that it turned toward the crop, he is exempt because the damage was only indirectly caused.
