One who places another person's animal, etc. All the more so if it is his own animal.
Even if the produce is in the public domain. Although it was explained in siman 391 that for damage by shen or regel in the public domain the animal's owner pays only the value of the benefit, here, since he deliberately placed the animal there, he is considered as though he damaged the produce directly with his own hands, as the Mechaber concludes. Therefore he pays full damages.
