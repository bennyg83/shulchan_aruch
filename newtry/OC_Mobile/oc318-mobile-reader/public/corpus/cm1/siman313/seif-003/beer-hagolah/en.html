The Gemara's resolution of the Mishnah: it was needed for the landlord's courtyard and oxen that came from outside. The Maggid Mishneh explains Rambam to mean that even though the courtyard is rented to the tenant, since it belongs to the landlord and the oxen came from elsewhere, the manure belongs to the landlord. The Raavad and Rashi instead establish it in a landlord's courtyard not rented to the tenant. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's explanation is somewhat supported by the Gemara's choice to say 'the landlord's courtyard' rather than 'a courtyard not rented to the tenant.'
