(Seif 3: Belonging to others) The Beit Yosef asks that a lease for its term is considered a sale. It appears to me that the Rambam follows Tosafot in Bava Metzia 56, who hold that a lease does not transfer ownership generally; only with respect to overreaching does an additional verse treat rental as a sale. Here too, the tenant acquires only the use of the premises, not every incidental benefit. There is therefore no contradiction from the Rambam and Tur at the beginning of siman 315, where “rental is a sale” refers to the essential acquisition of the use itself. The Sema's objection from siman 260 will be resolved below.
