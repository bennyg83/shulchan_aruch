(Seif 1: In the garden plot) Rashi explains in the Gemara that this is a small garden in which various grasses grow and whose appearance is pleasing to the eye. This implies that the garden itself is not ordinarily used; it is intended only for visual enjoyment. Therefore Rashi mentions use of the garden wall. A courtyard, by contrast, is itself used, and Rashi accordingly speaks of use of the courtyard itself. The Sema did not attend to this distinction, and that is why he found difficulty where the matter is straightforward.
