There, Rav Nachman himself said, etc. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam has 'the courtyard's garden plot' in place of 'the mansion's garden plot' in the Gemara. His teacher Ibn Migash likewise explains it. A tarbitz is a small garden area for air, where grasses grow and the appearance is pleasant.
