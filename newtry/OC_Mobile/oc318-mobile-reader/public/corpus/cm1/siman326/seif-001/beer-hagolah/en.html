a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 14, from the incident involving sesame and Rav Ashi's statement to Rav Kahana, Bava Metzia 104a.
b) The Maggid Mishneh explains that the tenant gives the owner his share of the wheat and cannot say, 'I did not weaken your field as much as sesame would have, so deduct the difference for me.' Rather, nothing is deducted. This is clear in the Gemara. The Rashba concludes that the same law applies both to fixed-rent tenancy and to sharecropping.
c) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is straightforward, because since he deviated from the agreement he must pay the excess.
d) From the incident there, as Rav Acha of Difti said to Ravina.
