One who receives, etc. See Be'er HaGolah. The disagreement between Rashi and Ramban corresponds to the dispute at the end of siman 324. Rashba's statement that the matter is not legally significant is independently necessary, because Rashi explains the Mishna on 105a and the case on 106b as applying to a sharecropper, although he says that the rule was unnecessary in a sharecropping arrangement. On 110b Rambam and Maggid Mishneh apply the law both to a fixed-rent tenant and a sharecropper. See Lechem Mishneh.
And not from the appreciation, etc.; but, etc. This follows the incident involving Rav Pappa. Rambam omitted the dispute between Abaye and Rava because the rule follows a fortiori.
(Supplement) And not from the appreciation of the trees. This is a scribal error. The text should read, 'and not from the appreciation,' referring back to the preceding clause, 'and not from trees that emerged, etc.' The continuation is, 'but they calculate, etc.' (End of supplement.)
(Supplement) But they calculate, etc. He said to him: What loss did I cause you? (End of supplement.)
And some say, etc. This follows Tosafot there, s.v. 'there is no.'
(Supplement) And some say, etc. See the Gemara and Rashi, s.v. 'he said to him, from the fact that he said.'
(Supplement) And some say, etc. Tosafot add that the land had become suitable for palms. The codifiers omitted this because in Babylonia a tenant may change the crop as he wishes, as explained in the preceding siman. On this view 'Babylonia' is not literal, but includes comparable lowland regions, unlike Eretz Yisrael's mountains.
(Supplement) But if, etc. This follows Tosafot's first answer. Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch follow the second and therefore draw no distinction. Tosafot's qualification applies to a fixed-rent tenant, but Rambam holds that a sharecropper may not change the crop. The Gemara concerns a fixed-rent tenant, whereas Rambam begins with 'one who receives,' meaning a sharecropper, unlike Rashi. Tur and Rama follow the first opinion at the end of siman 324. (End of supplement.) See 444.
