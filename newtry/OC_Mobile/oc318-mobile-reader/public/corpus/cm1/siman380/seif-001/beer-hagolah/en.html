The wording of Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilkhot Chovel U'Mazik. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Mishnah in Bava Kamma chapter 8, 92a, and the Gemara's challenge. Rabbi Yochanan answers that sometimes 'yes' means no and sometimes 'no' means yes. The baraita likewise teaches this. He explains that where 'yes' is said as a question it means no, and where 'no' is said as a question it means yes. The Mechaber did not explicitly mention the questioning tone because it is obvious. Rashi explains likewise. The law is phrased as liability regarding bodily injury and exemption regarding money because ordinarily a person waives his money more readily than his body.
Tosafot likewise write there, 93a, that it is presumably said as a question, since initially he told him to tear his garment, etc.
