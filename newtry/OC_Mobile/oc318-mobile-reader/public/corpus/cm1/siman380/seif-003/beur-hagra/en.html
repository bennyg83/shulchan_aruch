(Collected note) One of the forbidden relationships, etc. Even one punishable by kareis. The Tur, and so too the baraisa and Gemara. (End of note.)
Since he pursued, etc. See Beer HaGolah and Rashi there. This is why the rule includes all forbidden relationships, even those punishable by kareis, as the Tur explicitly writes. The reason is that in all such cases the victim may be saved even at the pursuer's life, as explained in Sanhedrin. See Tosafos Bava Kamma 22b concerning the kid, and Riva's answer.
