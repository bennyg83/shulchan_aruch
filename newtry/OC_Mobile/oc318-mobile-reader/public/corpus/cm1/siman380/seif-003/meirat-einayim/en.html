The Tur writes: whether the prohibition carries karet, a beit din death penalty, or whether he pursued a male in order to violate him. The point is that even where the pursuer is not subject to a beit din death penalty but only to karet, he is exempt from paying for utensils broken in the course of the pursuit because the rule of the more severe liability applies. He is liable with his life before Heaven. See the Perishah.
