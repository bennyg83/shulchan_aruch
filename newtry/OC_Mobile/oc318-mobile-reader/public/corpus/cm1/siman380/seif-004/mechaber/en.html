If a ship is in danger of breaking from excessive cargo and one person lightens it by throwing cargo into the sea, he is exempt, because the cargo is like a pursuer threatening their lives, and he performed a great mitzvah by saving them. Rama: Some say this applies only when the ship was overloaded. If it was not overloaded and a wave threatened to sink it, all must compensate according to cargo weight, not value, even if only one person's cargo was thrown. If someone brought a donkey onto a ship and it jumped about, threatening to sink it, and another threw the donkey into the river, the thrower is liable if bringing donkeys aboard is customary; otherwise he is exempt because the donkey is a pursuer. The same applies in similar cases.
