A ship, etc. Bava Kamma there. Raavad objects from the case where a wave threatens a ship. Maggid Mishneh answers that a wave does not arise because the ship was overloaded, whereas here the cargo itself acts like a pursuer. Nimukei Yosef objects that Rambam should then have specified that the last cargo loaded was thrown overboard. Mordechai explains that if bringing the donkey onto the ship was dangerous from the outset, one who throws it overboard is exempt. But if donkeys are ordinarily brought aboard and it later begins jumping, one who throws it overboard is liable, since he saved himself with another's property, as in siman 329:4 and the wave case. This is the meaning of 'one who brought a donkey onto a ship.' Beit Yosef's objection to Mordechai is therefore unfounded.
