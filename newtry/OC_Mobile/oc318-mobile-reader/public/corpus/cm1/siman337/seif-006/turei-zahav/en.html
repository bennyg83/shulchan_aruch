(Seif 19: he weakens his strength.) This refers to other workers, not to a worker in a vineyard. A vineyard worker has a Torah right to eat from the vineyard, and since he can sustain himself properly from it, he may certainly make himself hungry beforehand so that afterward he can eat grapes to satiety. This is like the rule in siman 338 that a person may withhold food from his animal beforehand. The Sema's explanation, applying this rule to a vineyard worker, is not acceptable to me.
