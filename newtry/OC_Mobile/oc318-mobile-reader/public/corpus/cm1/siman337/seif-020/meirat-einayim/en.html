For they were particular regarding zimmun and the fourth blessing, etc. The Tur's text appears to read that the Sages were particular both regarding zimmun and regarding the fourth blessing. Meaning, workers were not required to wait for others to form a zimmun if that would delay their labor, and they were also treated leniently concerning the fourth blessing. The Ir Shushan appears to have read it as one phrase, but the language does not support that.
