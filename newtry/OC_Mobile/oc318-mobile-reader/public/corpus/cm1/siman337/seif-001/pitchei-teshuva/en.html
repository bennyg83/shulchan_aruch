Divrei Mishpat questions Shach's ruling, and Shaar Mishpat likewise questions it at length. He concludes, however, that he found a major authority supporting Shach, namely Ritva as cited in the chapter HaPoalim. It appears that Ritva did not have the Gemara text reading, "This is why a verse was needed to permit it." The same version is found in Sefer Yereim of Rabbi Eliezer of Metz, mitzvah 164. Nevertheless, according to the version before us and the explanations of Rashi, Rif, and Rambam, the primary ruling appears not to follow Shach in this matter. See there.
