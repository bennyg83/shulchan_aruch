And it is prohibited, etc. The Mishnah 93a says one may stipulate, etc. This is permitted specifically through an explicit stipulation, as Rashi writes there 92b, s.v. "kotzetz," and only for those with legal understanding and with their consent, as stated in seif 17.
And if they muzzled them, etc. As above, specifically by stipulation, and likewise after the fact. As stated there 91a regarding a cow, certainly here, where he does not receive lashes.
