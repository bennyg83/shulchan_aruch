A worker may eat when working with produce grown from the ground while it is attached and at the completion of its labor, such as harvesting grapes, olives, figs, or dates; and with detached produce before completion of the final labor that creates its obligation. One working with products not grown from the ground, such as milking, churning, or cheesemaking, may not eat. Nor may one working with attached produce before completion, such as hoeing vines, covering tree roots, or thinning onions and garlic, eat, because the principal work serves the larger plants whose labor is not complete.
