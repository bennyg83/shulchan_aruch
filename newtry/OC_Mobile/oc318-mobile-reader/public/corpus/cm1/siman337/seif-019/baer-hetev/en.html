His food. Meaning, his own food, from which he sustains himself at home. Although the Tur and Mechaber write at the end of siman 338 that the owner of a cow may leave it hungry so that it will eat much while threshing, that case is different. The animal's principal food and satiation come from grain, so even if it is initially left hungry, as soon as it enters to thresh it eats from the grain it is threshing and regains its strength. A worker, by contrast, does not derive his principal nourishment, from which he has strength to work, from the vineyard. Therefore he may not starve himself of his principal food. Sema.
