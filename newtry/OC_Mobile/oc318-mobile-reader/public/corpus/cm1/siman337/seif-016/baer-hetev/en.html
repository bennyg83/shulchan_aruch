A nazir. Meaning, one might think that since the employer knows that the worker is a nazir and will not eat the food, it is as though he stipulated with him that whatever food is available should be given to his wife and children. Therefore the ruling teaches otherwise. These are the words of the Sema. In the Gemara, however, the case of a nazir is even more straightforward, for he cannot make such a claim because of the principle "Go, go, we say to the nazir: go around the vineyard and do not approach it." This is clear. Shach.
