Even a nazir who was working. The Sema explains that one might have thought that since the employer knew he was a nazir and could not eat, it is as though he stipulated with him that what he was entitled to eat should be given to his wife and children. The ruling therefore teaches otherwise. In the Gemara, however, the case of a nazir is even more obvious, because he cannot say that the employer should have told him to avoid taking this work. This is clear.
