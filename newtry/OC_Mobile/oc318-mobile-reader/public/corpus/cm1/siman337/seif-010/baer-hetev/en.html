We do not remove. The Sema writes that it seems evident from the Gemara and Rashi that even with figs and grapes, if while working with the figs he worked and ate from the grapes, we do not remove what he ate from him, even if the vine was not trained over the fig tree. This is unlike the Ir Shushan, which writes that where a vine is trained over a fig tree, or vice versa, if he eats from the trained plant we remove it from him. In my view the Ir Shushan is correct. See the Gemara carefully; the Sema objected improperly. Shach.
