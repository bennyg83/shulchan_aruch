Some say specifically, etc. The Sema explains that the Rama's 'some say' is directed against the Mechaber, although both formulations appear in the Tur, and that it excludes the Beit Yosef's understanding of the Rif and Rambam. This is not entirely precise, because the Beit Yosef himself writes that the Rif and Rambam also accept Issi's rule, as shown by their omission. See there.
