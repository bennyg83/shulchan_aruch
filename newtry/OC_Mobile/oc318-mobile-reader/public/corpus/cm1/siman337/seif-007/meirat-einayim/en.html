He may eat a cucumber or dates worth a sela, etc. This is also the language of the Gemara and the authorities. It appears that "one cucumber" is not literal, and he may eat many, provided the total amount is permitted. The Gemara contains an opinion that even if he was hired to work only with a single cucumber, he may eat that cucumber even if it is worth more than his entire wage. Because of that opinion, the Tur and Mechaber also use the example of one cucumber, even though we do not rule like that authority, as Rama concludes.
And some say specifically, etc. Rama's use of "some say" on the Mechaber's ruling is noteworthy because both positions are recorded in the Tur. He intends to exclude the Beit Yosef's view that Rif and Rambam do not hold this limitation.
He may not eat one cucumber. Because a worker may eat only while he is placing produce into the owner's vessel, and in this case that cucumber will never be placed into the owner's vessel.
