And some say. The Rama's phrase "and some say," written after the words of the Mechaber, even though both rulings are stated by the Tur, is intended to exclude the view of the Beit Yosef, who wrote that the Rif and Rambam also hold this way. This is the explanation. Editor's note: It appears to me that this is the correct reading, and the Shach's difficulty is thereby resolved; see there.
