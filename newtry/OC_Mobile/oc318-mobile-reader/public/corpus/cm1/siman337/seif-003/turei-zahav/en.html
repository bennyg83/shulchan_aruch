(Seif 10: A vine trained over another plant, etc.) The Sema disagrees with the Levush regarding one who eats figs and grapes from intertwined plants. It appears to me that the Levush is correct. When there are two species, even though one is trained over the other, the worker is not eating from the same species that he is placing into the employer's vessel, as the Gemara explicitly requires. The Gemara's unresolved question concerning trained vines does not establish permission for two distinct species, and no authority applies the leniency beyond one vine trained over another vine. Thus two species remain prohibited whether intertwined or not.
