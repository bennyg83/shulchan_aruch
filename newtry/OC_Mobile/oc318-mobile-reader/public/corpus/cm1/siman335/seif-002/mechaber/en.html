If one hires a worker to carry out an errand from one place to another, and he went but found nothing to bring back, he receives his full wage, because he completed the errand. Rama: If he was sent with a letter, found the recipient, and returned without delivering it, he receives nothing unless the employer, rather than the messenger, should have known of the obstacle. If the item to be carried was heavy, the employer may deduct because returning empty is easier than returning loaded, unless the worker is accustomed to strenuous labor.
