To bring him a mission, and in the name of Rashi, he was given to him that he would serve on his journey and die in the middle of the road, giving him all his wages and the people he had given to him. (And if he had sent after his wife and did not want to pay for the Rashi in his return, he did not want to pay the petition.)
For the messenger already performed his mission. The Maggid Mishneh explains why this differs from a worker hired to bring produce who went and found none, who receives only the wage of an idle worker. There he was hired for repeated trips or for a whole day, and it is unreasonable to pay him the full wage for all those trips when he accomplished nothing. He receives idle worker compensation because the employer prevented him from hiring himself elsewhere. Likewise, one hired to plow a field for the day who finds it too wet is paid for the day as an idle worker. But this messenger was hired for one specific trip and completed the mission for which he was hired. See the Beit Yosef and Derishah.
But if he sent a messenger with a letter and found the person to whom it was sent, etc. Rama's wording is difficult. The source in Mordechai speaks of a paid letter carrier who encountered danger halfway, apparently hostile troops or conflict, and therefore had to turn back. In that case the loss is the worker's, whether the danger was known to both or unknown to both, and he receives nothing even for half the journey because the sender derived no benefit from the mission. This differs from a hired donkey that dies halfway, where the owner may still have received usable transport to that point. Rama seems to have read the source as though the messenger met the merchant himself halfway, but that is difficult. Such a meeting is not an unavoidable accident, and the purpose of the mission, delivering the letter, was achieved, so why should he not receive his full wage? The Ir Shushan added the word 'not' and explained that the messenger failed to find the recipient, but that too does not fit the source well. The primary meaning is the danger case just explained.
