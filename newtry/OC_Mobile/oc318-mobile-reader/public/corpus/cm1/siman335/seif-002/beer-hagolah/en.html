The wording of Rambam there, law 8. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is a Tosefta and appears in the Halakhot in HaSocher Et HaOmanin.
And there is no such name to walk away from Green and not find a grain mentioned above that there is no reward for the same move, but for Day A and will be abolished all day, but it has already made its work paying
The Sema already writes that Rama's words here are confused and unrelated to the Mordechai in HaOmanin. There the case was that the agent encountered war and turned back halfway because of the unavoidable danger. The same appears in Mordechai to Kiddushin. Rama's wording seems to have interpreted 'tegar' as a merchant whom he met halfway, but that cannot fit the Mordechai. Even as a matter of law, the statement that the agent receives nothing is incorrect, for he must at least be paid for half the journey he traveled, as the Mechaber wrote immediately above. Even if the employer himself met the merchant and no longer needed the agent, the agent must still be paid for the distance traveled. The Levush added the word 'not,' but the Sema rejected that reading for the reasons above. This is not the place to elaborate.
The Beit Yosef in seif 3 cites a responsum of Rashba: if Reuven hired Shimon to serve him during a journey and Reuven died halfway, Shimon receives his full wage. The Sema cites this from Darkhei Moshe.
The Maggid Mishneh writes so there from the baraita concerning donkey drivers who traveled and did not find what they expected, which concludes that one who arrives loaded is not comparable to one who arrives empty, Bava Metzia 76b. The Raavad likewise writes this in his glosses. The Tur's words must be explained similarly, as the Beit Yosef writes.
