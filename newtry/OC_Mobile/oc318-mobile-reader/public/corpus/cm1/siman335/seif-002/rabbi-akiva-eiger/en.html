[Sema, letter 7] They give him his full wages. Note: See there, where this is compared to an unspecified ship with this wine. At first glance, the law in siman 334, seif 4, is unlike the Rashba. Also regarding a ship, Rama above, siman 311, records an opinion that the full wage is not paid. Even according to the Mechaber there, this is because the owner placed his ship at the renter's disposal, as the Sema explains. If so, apparently here too he should not receive his full wage. This requires study. See Responsa Mishpetei Shmuel, siman 338, and Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Sechirut, siman 5.
