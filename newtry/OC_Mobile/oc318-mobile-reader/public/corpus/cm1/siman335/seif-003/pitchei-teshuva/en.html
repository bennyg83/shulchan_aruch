He receives his full wages. Lechem Mishneh, Hilchot Sechirut, chapter 9, law 8, asks from the law of one who descended to rescue and did not succeed, in siman 264, seif 4, where he receives only the ordinary wage, whereas here the employer must pay the full amount he stipulated even though the mission was not accomplished. Divrei Mishpat answers that here, when the messenger was sent, the requested item was not in fact available at the destination, although the employer believed it would be. The accident therefore befell the employer's undertaking rather than the messenger's performance. See there.
