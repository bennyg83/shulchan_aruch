Some say he must pay. See Baer Heitev, Beit Hillel to Yoreh Deah, siman 245, seif 4, and Yad Shaul there. See also Shaar Mishpat here, who concludes that if the teacher is holding property belonging to the father, and he taught the son, then according to all views he may retain that property for his teaching fee, as explained in siman 128, seif 1, in the gloss and in Shach, seif katan 8.
