An employer who hired, etc. This follows Bava Batra 153b, where Rav Nachman said: "if he is healthy, etc." The halachah follows Rav Nachman because he is a judge, etc. The same logic applies here: since he knows now, he presumably knew initially. Even according to the rejection in Kiddushin 79b, Rav Nachman disputes only, etc. Here all who hire themselves are presumed to know their own skill, and certainly one who expressly stipulated it. People do not lie about something that will readily become known. See Tosafos to Bava Batra there, s.v. "Rav Nachman, etc." All this follows Tosafos's approach there, s.v. "keman, etc.," and their rationale. Rif and other poskim, however, rule unlike Rav Nachman, as stated above siman 221:2.
