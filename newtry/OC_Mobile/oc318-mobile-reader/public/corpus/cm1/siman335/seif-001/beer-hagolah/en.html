a) The language of the Tur, from Rava's statement in Bava Metzia 77a regarding one who hired workers for a task.
b) The Rosh writes in his rulings that if the hiring was unspecified, the employer may change them from light work to heavy work. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes in chapter 9 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 7, in the name of the Ramban and Rashba, because had he wished he could have assigned them difficult work in the morning, as the Mechaber writes at the end of the seif.
c) The language of the Rambam there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this appears in the explanation of the laborers of Mechoza mentioned in the Gemara.
d) Tur, seif 1.
e) This follows from what was explained above. The Sema writes that if a teacher was hired without specification to teach five or six boys, the employer may later substitute another boy for the first even without an unavoidable circumstance, because this resembles hiring a worker for unspecified labor.
f) Hagahot Mordechai to the sixth chapter of Bava Metzia writes that a group who hired a teacher cannot withdraw even before he began working if he cannot find other employment, because any matter undertaken before three persons cannot thereafter be revoked. It further writes in the name of the Semag that one who hired a teacher subject to a penalty must pay the penalty even without a formal act of acquisition.
g) See siman 285, seif 3.
