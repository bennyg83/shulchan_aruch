Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, halachah 6. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is straightforward in many places. The baraita in the first chapter of Bava Kamma 15a teaches that Scripture equates a woman with a man for all civil laws of the Torah. Numerous incidents in the Gemara apply the laws of custodians to women. Although the verse says, "If a man gives to his fellow," Scripture speaks in the ordinary manner.
