The wife. A wife who negligently breaks her husband's utensils is exempt from paying because of domestic peace. So write the Rosh and Tosafot in the chapter HaKotev, citing the Yerushalmi, and the Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule likewise in Even HaEzer, siman 80, seif 17. See Nimukei Yosef, chapter HaShoel, cited by Beit Yosef in siman 346, seif 10. If she broke them intentionally or gave them to another person, however, she is liable. Gloss of the Shach.
