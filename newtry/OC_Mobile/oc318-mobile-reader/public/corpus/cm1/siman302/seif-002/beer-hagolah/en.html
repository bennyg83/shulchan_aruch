Bet) There, halachah 7. Rambam explains in the name of his teachers that this custodian does not swear in response to a definite claim of the minor, because the Mishnah in Shevuat HaDayanim, Shevuot 38b, says that one does not swear on the claim of a minor. The special oaths of custodians, however, are inherently imposed even where the claimant does not know what occurred. The Maggid Mishneh writes similarly in the name of Rav Yosef ibn Migash and discusses the matter at length.
