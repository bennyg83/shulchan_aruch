Seif 10, gloss: He exchanged money and movables together, etc. Note: See my responsum to Choshen Mishpat siman 198, where I initially suggested proof from Bava Metzia 48, in the case of pulling an artisan's tool, that where one component of a combined acquisition fails, the entire acquisition is void. I later rejected that proof. Still, the passage appears to show that where everything was included in one statement, or where one component depends upon the other, all agree that the whole transaction fails. In my work on Even HaEzer, however, I later proved from the Gemara in the opposite direction, that even within a single statement the valid portion can take effect. See there.
