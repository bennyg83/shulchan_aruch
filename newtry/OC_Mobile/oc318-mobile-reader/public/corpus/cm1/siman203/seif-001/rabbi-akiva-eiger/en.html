[Shulchan Aruch] Except for coin. Note: Responsa Maharam bar Baruch, siman 257, states: except for coin and an item that is not specifically defined. This responsum appears verbatim in Responsa Rashba, siman 1104. See the Beit Yosef here in the name of the Ran, who holds that an item not specifically defined is acquired through chalipin, and Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Kinyan Chalipin, siman 3.
