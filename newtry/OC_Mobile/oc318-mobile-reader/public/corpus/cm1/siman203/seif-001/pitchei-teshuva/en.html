Responsa Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, siman 153, initially writes that bank notes are acquired by chalipin, because their physical substance is not coin; they represent an obligation against the assets of the sovereign and therefore are transferable by chalipin. In siman 187, however, written later, he rules that bank notes are not acquired by chalipin. His later view is cited above, siman 95, seif 1, seif katan 1.
