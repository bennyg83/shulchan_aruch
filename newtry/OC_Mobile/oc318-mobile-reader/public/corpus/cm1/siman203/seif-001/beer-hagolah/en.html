Alef) This is the language of the Tur, and Rambam likewise rules at the beginning of chapter 5 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, based on the Mishnah, Bava Metzia 44a.
Bet) This was explained in siman 195.
Gimel) As Rav Nachman states there, Bava Metzia 47a.
Dalet) A statement of Rav Pappa at the end of 45a according to Rav Nachman. Although Rav Pappa's statement was rejected on 46a, that rejection concerned only his claim that currency can be acquired through chalipin. His statement that produce can be acquired through chalipin even according to Rav Nachman remains accepted. Rambam likewise rules this at the beginning of chapter 6.
Heh) The conclusion of the Gemara there, 46a.
Vav) The Tur, and Rambam likewise at the end of chapter 5. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is agreed upon and learned from the discussion at the beginning of Bava Batra 3a concerning division of a courtyard: "Even if they made a kinyan, what of it? It is only a kinyan concerning words." The same applies to all similar cases.
Zayin) As in Rava's statement there, 47a.
Chet) The Mishnah at the beginning of Kiddushin 28a, as the Gemara explains there on 28b: there are forms of monetary value that function as chalipin, etc.
Tet) Rambam at the beginning of chapter 5 gives the example of wine for oil. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam follows Tosafot in the name of Rabbenu Tam: Rav Nachman's rule that produce cannot serve for a kinyan applies only to chalipin in the manner of kinyan sudar. But where the exchange is based on equivalent value, as in these cases, even Rav Nachman agrees that produce can function as chalipin. The Beit Yosef writes that the Maggid Mishneh indicates this is also the Rashba's view, and the Tur likewise appears to agree. Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 9, part 3, states this in the name of Rav Hai, and his language also indicates agreement.
Yod) The Maggid Mishneh there in the name of some commentators.
