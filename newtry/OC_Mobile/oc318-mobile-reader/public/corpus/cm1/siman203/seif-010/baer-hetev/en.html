He exchanged. The Sema explains that Reuven gives Shimon his sudar and says, "Take my sudar and give me in exchange money and movable property." In such a case there is a dispute, as the Rama concludes. One must explain why the Rama records three opinions here, whereas in siman 209, seif 4, concerning one who transfers something not yet in existence together with something already in existence, he records only one view. To resolve this, one must first introduce several principles, as the Sema explains there at length. See Responsa Maharashdam, siman 251, and the work Imrei Emet, page 95a.
