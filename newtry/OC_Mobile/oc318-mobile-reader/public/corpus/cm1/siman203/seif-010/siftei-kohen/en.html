He exchanged money, etc. See Responsa Maharashdam, siman 251, and Imrei Emet, page 95a.
Some say that it is effective, etc. It appears to me that the Terumat HaDeshen also agrees with the Rivash that an asmachta is ineffective. In siman 311 he discusses only a case in which the acquisition itself is valid, where waiver is effective as to the person himself and certainly as to Levi. He does not address asmachta. His case must therefore involve a legally effective form, such as an acquisition before an eminent beit din. See there and what I wrote in siman 209.
