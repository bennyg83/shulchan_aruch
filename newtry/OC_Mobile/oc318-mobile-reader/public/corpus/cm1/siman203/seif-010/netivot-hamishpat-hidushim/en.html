He exchanged money and movable property. To summarize the Mordechai's discussion, he brings all three opinions. In siman 210, seif 4, the ruling follows Rabbeinu Simcha that half of the transaction is acquired, and the halachah here likewise follows that opinion. We also rule in siman 210 concerning "you and the donkey" that the valid half is acquired. The dissenting view here, which holds that the entire acquisition remains valid, applies only where the invalid component is at least a type of property to which some acquisition could theoretically apply, such as money, which can be acquired by hagbahah or meshichah. We then say that since the kinyan takes effect on the movable property it can extend to the money as well. But with an item not yet in existence, to which no present kinyan can apply, we do not say such an extension. Only the property presently in existence is acquired.
