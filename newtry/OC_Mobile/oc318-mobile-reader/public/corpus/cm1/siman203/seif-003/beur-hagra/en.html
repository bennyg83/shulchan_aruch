Although Reish Lakish disputes Rabbi Yochanan and initially holds that even produce cannot be redeemed onto dinars, first, the halachah follows Rabbi Yochanan against Reish Lakish; second, Beis Shammai’s view against Beis Hillel is not authoritative; and third, we generally rule like the latter version, which explains the concern as delay because of one’s attic.
