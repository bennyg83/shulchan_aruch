Mem) Rambam there, chapter 6, halachah 2. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is explained in the Gemara, Bava Metzia 45b, where the reason is stated: people's intent depends upon the stamped form, and stamped forms are liable to be canceled. Unstamped metal pieces have no such form. Moreover, this follows a fortiori from coins invalidated by the government, as explained below in seif 8.
Nun) This is explained by what was written in seif 1.
Samekh) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is disputed among the Geonim. Rav Hai disagrees and holds that a gold coin is treated like produce for all purposes. The Rambam follows Rabbenu Chananel and the Rif. Their proofs are explained in the Rif on HaZahav.
