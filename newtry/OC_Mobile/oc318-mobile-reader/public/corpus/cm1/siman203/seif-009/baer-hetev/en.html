That he acquire. This refers to a gift. In a sale there is a method by which he can acquire, as explained above: if Reuven performs meshichah on Shimon's movables, he becomes obligated to pay Shimon their value. Sema. See Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 16.
Deposited. See Responsa Rabbi Levi ibn Chaviv, siman 94; Responsa Raanach, siman 77; Responsa Rabbi Moshe Alshich, siman 87; and Responsa Rama, siman 92.
The debt. See Mordechai, chapter HaGozel Kama, where many poskim hold that a debt can be transferred through acquisition together with land. Mordechai in chapter HaKotev likewise states this in the name of Rabbi Eliezer son of Rabbi Shimshon. This view is brought in the Rama to siman 66, seif 26. This requires study. Shach.
