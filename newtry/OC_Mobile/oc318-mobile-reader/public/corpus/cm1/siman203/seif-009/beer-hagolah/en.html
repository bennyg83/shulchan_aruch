Chet) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 6. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is explained in Bava Metzia, HaZahav, at the beginning of 46a. The law of acquisition together with land is explicit in the incident of Rav Pappa there.
Tet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Rambam's reasoning is correct. The incident of Rav Pappa involved a deposit, but an oral loan cannot be transferred through acquisition together with land. The Rashba likewise writes in HaGozel Etzim that the version in the incident of Rav Abba should not read that money was owed to him, because an oral loan cannot be transferred together with land. This is the primary view. Some bring proof from the beginning of Bava Batra 148a, where a deathly ill person says, "I lent so-and-so," etc., and the Gemara establishes it through ma'amad sheloshtan rather than acquisition together with land. This is also the view of the Ramban.
Yod) This is the language of the Mechaber, as written in siman 211, seif 7.
Kaf) There in the Rambam, and it is explained by what was written above.
