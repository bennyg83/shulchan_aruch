She'erit Yosef inferred from Nimukei Yosef that only ordinary movable deposits can be consecrated while in the custodian's hands, not deposited money, because loose deposited money may be used by the custodian. This is not correct. The Shulchan Aruch here expressly rules that even deposited money can be transferred while the specific coins remain intact. So long as the custodian has not actually used them, and the depositor demands return of his deposit, the custodian cannot substitute other coins merely because he was permitted to use these. The original coins therefore remain the depositor's property. Nimukei Yosef's reference to movable deposits did not mean to exclude deposited money that is still intact.
