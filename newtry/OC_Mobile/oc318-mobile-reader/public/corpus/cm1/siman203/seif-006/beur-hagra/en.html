Some say, etc. Although the halakha does not follow Rabbi's earlier view and the several tannaim who agree with it, all agree regarding copper. The order of the Mishna is that each later item is inferior to the preceding one and is considered produce relative to it: copper relative to gold, defective coins relative to copper, and an unstamped token relative to defective coins. Rosh. The Yerushalmi at the beginning of HaZahav likewise states the general rule that whatever is inferior to another acquires the other. Although the authorities disagree concerning silver and gold, and we do not rule as stated there, they do not disagree regarding the other cases. See cited source 96.
