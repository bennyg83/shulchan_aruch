[Shulchan Aruch] They are like other movable property. Note: This is the language of the Rambam, and it implies that their law is like produce, which may be acquired through chalipin. Kesef Mishneh explicitly writes so in chapter 5, and the Gaon in Lechem Mishneh, chapter 6, halachah 2, writes that this follows all the more so from disqualified coins discussed below in seif 8, which are merely regarded as produce. It must therefore be that when the Rambam writes here that they acquire one another, he means as an exchange of equivalent value, which is effective even with produce, as is clear from his words at the beginning of chapter 5 and as the Maggid Mishneh writes there. This is what the Rambam intended by writing here that they acquire one another "by way of exchange, as we explained," meaning that it is analogous to the beginning of chapter 5, where an exchange based on value is effective even with produce. Since the Mechaber did not cite the rule that an exchange based on value is effective even with produce, he should not have copied here the statement that they acquire one another. This requires study.
