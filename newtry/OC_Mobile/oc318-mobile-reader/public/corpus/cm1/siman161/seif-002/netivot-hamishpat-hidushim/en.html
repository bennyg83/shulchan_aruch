Not other things. Meaning, a gatehouse. Beit Yosef. The reason appears to be that a gatehouse is required because of visual damage, as is evident in Bava Batra 2. If he does not live or use the courtyard, there is no visual damage from him. But a bolt and lock protect the houses themselves from thieves who might take windows or break into the building, and therefore he must contribute even if he does not live there.
