According to wealth. Although a gatehouse is required because of visual intrusion, as Bava Batra 22b indicates when deriving from this law that visual intrusion is legally cognizable damage, and siman 157, seif 3, states that regarding visual intrusion partners contribute equally even if one owns twice as much property, the cases can be distinguished. There, the injury is caused by each neighbor to the other. Each person is himself the source of damage, and the one causing damage bears the duty to remove it. The same distancing is necessary whether the property exposed is worth a small or large amount. Here, by contrast, the danger comes from outsiders. The person who stands to suffer a greater loss therefore must contribute more toward the protective measure.
