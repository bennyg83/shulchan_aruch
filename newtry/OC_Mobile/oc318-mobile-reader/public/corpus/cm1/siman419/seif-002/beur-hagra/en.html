If there is a quaint as a quaint in C.C.:
(Supplement) But if, etc. This is Tur's ruling in accordance with Rosh, who holds that the injured party cannot compel the damager even to give medium or inferior land. The Gemara's objection, 'if so, you have impaired, etc.,' concerns such a case. The same applies where the damager owns two grades of superior land. See Rosh there and siman 102, seif 2.
