Now. The Eir Shushan disputes this. See Migdal David and the Sema, who challenge him, but their arguments are not compelling, and the Eir Shushan's position is more correct. Maharshal, Bava Kamma chapter 1, siman 11, disputes the entire seif and writes that the injured party always collects from the damager's best property, even the very best parcel that he chooses, since the law grants him payment from superior land. Nevertheless, the judges should exercise their judgment. If they see that one parcel is advantageous to the injured party and another to the damager, this is included in the requirement to do what is upright and good. See there. Shach.
