From the best land among the damager's properties. See the novellae of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Gittin 48b. He writes that this apparently refers to the properties the damager owned at the time he caused the damage, because at that moment the obligation to pay from his best property took effect. Property acquired after the damage but before judgment must indeed be used to satisfy the personal debt, as stated in Bava Batra 157a, but it may not be subject to the special requirement of payment from the best quality property. He concludes that the practical law requires further study. See there.
