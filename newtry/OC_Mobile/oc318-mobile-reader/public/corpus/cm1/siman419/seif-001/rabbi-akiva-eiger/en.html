[Shach, subparagraph 3] I have not reached the end of his reasoning, for we do not find anywhere that Rav Kahana was a student of Rava. Rather, in many places Rav Kahana appears as a student of Rav. Note: See Toras Chaim, Maharam Lublin, and Maharam Schiff, who write that there were two sages named Rav Kahana, one in the days of Rav and another in the days of Rava. Otherwise, Rav Kahana’s statements would contradict one another, because there he derives the law from the verse, "If it was torn by beasts, he shall bring it as evidence." Rather, it is certain that this Rav Kahana is not the one mentioned earlier. Consider carefully.
