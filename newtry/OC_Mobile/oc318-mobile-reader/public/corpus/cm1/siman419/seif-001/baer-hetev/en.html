We attend to the claim. Maharshal, Bava Kamma chapter 1, siman 16, rules that damages may be collected from encumbered property even if the defendant had not yet stood in judgment, because damage claims become publicly known. This is also implied by Tosafot there on 14b according to Rabbeinu Tam, on the view that liens arise by Torah law. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes similarly, as does the Maggid Mishneh in Hilchot Malveh, chapter 19, in the name of some authorities. The Sema likewise writes this in siman 118, seif 3. There are, however, dissenting opinions. See what I wrote in siman 66, seif 35. Shach.

The Shach explains that this means even if the damager has cash, he need not pay in cash and may instead pay from inferior movable property, unlike an ordinary creditor as discussed above in siman 108. This is the principal view. Nevertheless, if the damager wishes to satisfy the claim with cash, he certainly need not give the injured party his movable property or land. This is obvious.
