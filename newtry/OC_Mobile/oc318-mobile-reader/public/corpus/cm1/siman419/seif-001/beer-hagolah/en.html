a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilkhot Nizkei Mamon. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is explained in the first chapter and in the sugya concerning the Mishnah's phrase 'property worth money' on 14b. Earlier there is also a dispute of Amoraim, and the Rambam rules like Rav Pappa and Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua on 17b. The Rif likewise holds this view. The Tur and Rosh write the same in their rulings.
bet) The language of the Tur, seif 1, from the statement of Rav Pappa and Rav Huna cited above.
gimel) Ibid. The Rosh writes in his rulings regarding a creditor that this rule is intended so that the door not be closed before borrowers. Although that reason does not apply to damages, the Tur understands that the Sages did not distinguish between them for this purpose.
dalet) The baraita there on 6b, according to Rav Acha bar Yaakov's interpretation. The poskim rule like Rabbi Akiva, because the halakhah follows Rabbi Akiva against an individual colleague.
