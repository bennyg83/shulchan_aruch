The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is a dispute of Amoraim in Gittin chapter 5, 50a. Rambam rules like Rav Nachman, whose view is halachah in monetary law, and like Abaye who says so, not like Rava. Thus all the poskim rule. These heirs include both minors and adults according to them. Rashba, however, writes in the name of Rabbenu Yonah that only minor heirs pay from inferior land, whereas adult heirs pay from superior land.
