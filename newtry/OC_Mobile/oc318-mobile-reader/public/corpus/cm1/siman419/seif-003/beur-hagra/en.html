(Supplement) If, etc. This refers to the present era. In Talmudic times movable property of orphans was not collected, as stated there on 14b. Today, however, damages are collected just as an ordinary debt is collected. Rif rules this way. Rosh records that Rabbi Ephraim disagrees, maintaining that the enactment applied only to ordinary debts and a ketubah, whereas damages are uncommon. Rosh agrees with Rif, though for a different reason: in the present era this is the law itself, as Tosafot explains in Ketubot 67a, s.v. 'camels,' etc. Rabbenu Tam disagrees, and Rosh records his position there.
