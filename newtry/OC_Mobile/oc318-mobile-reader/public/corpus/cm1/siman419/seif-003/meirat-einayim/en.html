From the movable property of the orphans. The Tur explains that this refers to the period after the Geonic enactment that a creditor may collect from orphans' movable property. The same applies to a tort claimant. In another respect too, a tort claimant resembles a creditor collecting from heirs: although the debtor himself would have had to pay from intermediate quality land, one who collects from the debtor's minor heirs takes only inferior land. The Mechaber's unqualified wording here appears to say that even from adult heirs he collects only from inferior land. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the Rambam's view, while Rashba distinguishes between minors and adults. Yet at the end of siman 108 the Mechaber himself follows the Rosh and Rabbeinu Yonah and distinguishes between minor and adult heirs, for the reason I explained there in the Sema. The Tur also writes here without qualification that a tort claimant does not collect from heirs' movable property unless subject to the enactment, but only from their land and from its inferior portion. That can be explained as speaking specifically of minor heirs. He mentioned the inferiority rule incidentally while distinguishing collection from the tortfeasor himself from collection from heirs. Were it not for the Maggid Mishneh's explicit statement, I would say the Rambam also meant minors, because his wording elsewhere parallels the Tur. The Mechaber's present formulation, however, cannot easily be read that way and requires study.
