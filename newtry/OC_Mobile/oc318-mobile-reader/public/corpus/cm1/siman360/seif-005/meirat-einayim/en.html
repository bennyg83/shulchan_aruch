He robbed earth and made it into bricks. In the Perishah I wrote that Rashba limits the rule to bricks dried in the sun, not fired in a kiln, because kiln fired brick cannot be crushed back into fine earth as it originally was. In the Derishah I argued that the Tur's language implies even kiln fired brick. Since earth itself is not a significant article, people are not particular that it return to precisely the same fineness. This differs from felt made into wool, where the wool is itself valuable and useful for its original type of work.
