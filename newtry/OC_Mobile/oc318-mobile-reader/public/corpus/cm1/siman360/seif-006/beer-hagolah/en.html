There is a name in G-d that makes it impossible for him to pass the color and so that the column is brought to him:
The Gemara has no opinion that mere beating of the fibers alone constitutes a change. Regarding bleaching, Rav Chiya bar Avin establishes the view of the Sages as bleaching with sulfur to whiten it thoroughly. Tur writes likewise. Rambam, however, has 'or he beat it and bleached it.' In my humble opinion this requires study, and this is not the place to elaborate.
Rambam and Tur say 'a garment.' Kesef Mishneh and Beit Yosef object that the Gemara says this is a reversible change. Rav Ashi establishes the acquisition there as where he made felt. This is what the Mechaber means by 'or made it into felt,' which can no longer return to its original state of separate fibers.
