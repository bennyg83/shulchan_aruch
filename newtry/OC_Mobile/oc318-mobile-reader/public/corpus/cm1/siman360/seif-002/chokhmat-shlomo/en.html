Seif 8. In the gloss: 'It is not called a change unless the name of the stolen object changed because of the physical change.' Note: See Shach. In my Orach Chayyim I distinguished that where the physical change occurs by itself, a change of name is also required, but where the change is made actively by hand, a change of name is not required. I brought a proof from the sugya of the merchants. I later wrote another distinction to resolve the Gemara in Temurah. See there.
