[Seif 4] Even though, and so forth. The Tur writes in the Rambam's name: “There is robbery here, but there is no restitution.” The Beit Yosef discusses the phrase at length. It appears to me that the Tur infers from the Rambam's wording. The statement “there is robbery here” is not literally accurate, and the Rambam therefore explains it by the concluding words “there is no restitution.” The act retains the legal consequence that the mitzvah of restitution was not fulfilled, although the object itself is not presently held as stolen property. This accords with the Ramah.
