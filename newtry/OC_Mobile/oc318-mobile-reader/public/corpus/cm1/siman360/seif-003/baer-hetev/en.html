The third. All the more so, if he stole one perutah and it later fell in value, he must return the item itself or the original value of one perutah. The case of three bundles is taught as a stronger point: even though he already fulfilled the mitzvah of restitution by returning two, he must still return the third. So write Tosafot. The Tur concludes that all the more so if he stole two bundles worth two perutot, they declined to a total of one perutah, and he returned one bundle, he must return the second as well, since he has not yet fulfilled the mitzvah of restitution at all. Sema.
