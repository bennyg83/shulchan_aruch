Three bundles worth three perutot, etc. All the more so, if he robbed one item worth a perutah and it later decreased in value, he must return that item or its present equivalent of a perutah, corresponding to what it was initially worth. The case of three bundles is stated for an additional novelty: even though he already fulfilled part of the mitzvah of restitution by returning two of them, he must nevertheless return the third. Tosafos write this, and the Tur concludes: all the more so if he stole two bundles worth two perutot, they later fell in value so that together they are worth only one perutah, and he returned one of them, he must also return the second, for he has not yet fulfilled the mitzvah of restitution at all.
