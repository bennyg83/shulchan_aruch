Seif 1. In the gloss: 'One who stole land,' etc. Note: See what I wrote in a responsum to the community of Zloczow, in my Choshen Mishpat, third edition, concerning one who darkens his fellow's property by building and whether the market enactment applies, and whether it applies when he did not know the property was stolen. I also explained the Sema's wording there. See also Netivot HaMishpat, who asks from Temurah, which challenges the view that an unlawful act is ineffective from the rule that a change acquires stolen property, even though the change can occur by itself. See my novellae to Bava Kamma, chapter HaGozel, where I resolved this, and my Yoreh Deah, sixth edition, siman 3, where I also discussed it.
