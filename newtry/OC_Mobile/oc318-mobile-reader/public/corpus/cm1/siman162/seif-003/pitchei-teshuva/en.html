But if the king gave them permission, etc. The Rama writes similarly below, siman 417, seif 1, in the gloss, concerning projections and space beneath the public domain. At first glance, here the permission applies only if the king already granted it. Initially, however, it would be forbidden to petition the king for authority to install doors, because the rule that it is forbidden to damage a boundary applies regardless of the manner in which the damage is achieved. By contrast, in siman 417 it may be that one may even initially request such permission. This requires study. See what I wrote there, siman 417, seif katan 1.
