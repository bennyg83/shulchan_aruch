(Rama, seif 7) And the synagogue members come, etc. Note: Responsa Tzemach Tzedek, siman 94, discusses this at length and concludes that where they cannot find any places to rent at all, even at high cost, because all are occupied and there is no other solution, they may compel expansion. He rejects the suggestion that additional benches alone suffice, because if necessary they can even compel construction of a synagogue. See there, and Responsa Maharam Shik, siman 4.
