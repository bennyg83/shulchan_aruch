Because at times people are pressed, etc. Meaning that on market day, when many people and animals from other towns fill the public domain and cannot all remain there, they press into the alleys. The Tur concludes that the law is not limited to an alley opening directly to a public domain, but applies as well to one opening to a karmelit or a side street. This is what Rama means in seif 1 when he says that only smaller alleys opening into those that themselves open to a public domain or side street are permitted to close. It follows that an alley opening directly to a side street may not do so.
