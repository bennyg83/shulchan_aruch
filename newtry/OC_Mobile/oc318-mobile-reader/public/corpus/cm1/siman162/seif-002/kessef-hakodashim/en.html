With respect to altering a public road, where the governing prince or king has granted permission to build, there may no longer be an objection on that ground. Perhaps this also means that one may approach the ruler in advance to object to a proposed construction even before first litigating in a Jewish beit din, because the Jewish judges themselves will ultimately have to take the ruler's permission into account. By approaching first, he merely seeks to prevent the ruler from granting the permission that would alter the legal position. This requires further study.
