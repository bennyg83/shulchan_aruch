The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's text follows the Halakhot: one of the residents of an alley who wishes, etc., and the question is whether billeting is divided according to number of people or according to number of entrances. The Gemara concludes that the residents may prevent him, because billeting is divided according to people. Billeting means the king's soldiers and servants. Kesef Mishneh likewise writes in the name of Ramban in the name of some commentators.
