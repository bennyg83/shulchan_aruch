Closed. The Tur states, "He had an open entrance and it was closed." Rivash, siman 248, writes that this applies specifically where he has witnesses that the entrance was originally open to the alleyway. If he has no witnesses to that fact, the existence of the closed opening does not prove that he possesses rights in the alleyway, even though he did not remove its doorposts, for perhaps it was lawfully closed. See Rivash there, who discusses the matter at length. He also writes that perhaps another person closed it, which constitutes waiver even though he did not remove the doorposts, as stated in siman 154, seif 12. Sema. See Responsa Rashach, part 2, siman 195, and Responsa Mabit, part 2, siman 233.
