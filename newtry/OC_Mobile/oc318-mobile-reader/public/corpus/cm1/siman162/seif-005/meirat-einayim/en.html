He had an entrance that was closed. The Tur says, 'He had an open entrance and it was closed.' Rivash, siman 248, likewise writes that specifically where there are witnesses that it was originally open to the alley does failure to remove the doorposts preserve his right. If there are no witnesses that it was originally open to the alley, the remaining structure proves nothing, because perhaps it was lawfully closed. Rivash discusses the matter at length and also notes that perhaps another person closed it, which can constitute waiver even without removing the doorposts, as explained above in siman 154:12.
