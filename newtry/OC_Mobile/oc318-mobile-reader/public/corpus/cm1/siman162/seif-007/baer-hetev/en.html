The second. The Sema writes that the text specifically says "the second" to teach two points: the outer resident cannot object, while those who live farther inward than the second resident may object. From this we also learn that if the outer resident built inward of his own position, all the residents may object to him, which is obvious. See Responsa Rashach, siman 231; Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 85; Responsa Maharashdam, siman 412; Responsa Mabit, part 1, siman 39; and Responsa Masat Binyamin, siman 4.
