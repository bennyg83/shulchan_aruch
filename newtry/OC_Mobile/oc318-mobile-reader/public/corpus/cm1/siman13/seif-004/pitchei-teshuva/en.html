And in every place, etc., he likewise cannot say, etc. Responsa Beit Yaakov, siman 15, writes that this limitation applies only to the litigants. The judges themselves, even after they have begun deliberating, may add judges if all of them agree. Regarding his question from the Sifrei cited by Rashi to Devarim on “How can I alone bear your trouble,” see Responsa Shav Yaakov, siman 1, Divrei David of the Taz to Parashat Devarim, Birkei Yosef below, siman 18, letter 3, and Tumim, seif katan 2. Beit Yaakov also discusses whether one judge, seeing that his colleagues outvote him, may say “I do not know” so that additional judges will be added. This will be explained below, siman 18, seif 1, seif katan 4. See also what I wrote above, siman 3, seif 1, seif katan 3.
