And a judge may not. Rivash.
Nevertheless. This disagrees with the Shulchan Aruch.
(Anthology) Nevertheless, if, etc. Since they consented, there is no concern that the other party will fabricate arguments of his own invention, and it is as though they accepted him. What the Shulchan Aruch writes, that a judge may not accept, means even with the judge's own consent, when the opposing litigant has not consented.
