See there: “They rely on Rashi’s words” The Rosh’s wording indicates two advantages in the procedure where each litigant selects a judge. First, each litigant believes his judge will favor him even beyond strict law. Second, the judges themselves ensure that no valid argument is overlooked. Rashi’s two clauses support both. The Gemara says that through this method “the judgment emerges in truth.” “Emerges” refers to the litigants’ willing acceptance, because each thinks he gained. Yet “in truth” adds the objective advantage that no valid claim escapes the judges.
