Whomever he wishes. Whether the selected judge is greater or lesser than the other judge, since the parties have entered this form of adjudication, each may select whomever he wishes, provided that he is fit. Thus writes the Sema. Beit Yosef, letter 3, in the name of Rabbi Yerucham, implies that the selected judge must be learned, or publicly accepted as competent; otherwise the other party may object.
