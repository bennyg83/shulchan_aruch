The language of the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot Sanhedrin, according to the Semag's text, which the Kesef Mishneh likewise found in an old manuscript, from the Mishnah 23a, in accordance with the Sages.
The tongue of the column
(b) There are radars
D) There in the Final
According to Rav Pappa's later interpretation there. As the Tur explains, the case is where one party has already selected a judge, in which case the other may also select one. But as long as the expert judge has not been selected, he can compel both parties, as explained below in siman 3. The Kesef Mishneh writes that this applies specifically where the two judges are in the same city. Then even the borrower may say so, as written in siman 14.
All the more so the claimant, as the Sema writes.
