These are two judges, etc. Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes in his glosses to Shulchan Aruch that if the two judges selected by the parties are related to one another, the defendant has the advantage and may compel the plaintiff to select another judge. See Responsa Rama, siman 104. Rabbi Akiva Eiger also cites that responsum somewhat more fully in his glosses to the Mishnah at the beginning of Zeh Borer, Tosafot Rabbi Akiva Eiger, letter 5.
