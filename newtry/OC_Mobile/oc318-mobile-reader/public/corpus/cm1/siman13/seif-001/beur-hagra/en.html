If they are not, etc. Rosh there.
(Collected note) If they are not, etc. As Tosafot write there, 5a, that the rule of zeh borer lo echad applies when one says, "I will not litigate before this one, etc." Otherwise, a single expert judge may compel him even against his will. The same applies to city leaders, who stand in place of an expert judge, as explained in siman 2. Since the litigants cannot reach agreement, they compel him. Tosafot there write that the same applies to three lay judges. This is the meaning of "if there are no leaders, etc." (End of collected note.)
