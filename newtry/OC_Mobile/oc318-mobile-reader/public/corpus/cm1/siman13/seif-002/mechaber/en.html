They write, 'So-and-so selected so-and-so, and so-and-so selected so-and-so.' Until this is written, either party may retract; once it is written, neither may retract. Therefore, it is written only with the consent of both parties, and both pay the scribe's fee. {Rama: The same applies if the litigants affirmed their selections through a formal act of acquisition (Nimukei Yosef, first chapter of Bava Metzia). Some say that once they have presented their claims before the judges, they may not retract even if nothing was written (ibid., in the name of the Yerushalmi). It appears to me that in a place where the practice is not to write a record of the selections, all agree that once the litigants have presented their claims they may not retract. Wherever they may not retract, they also may not demand that more judges be added (Rabbeinu Yeruham, netiv 15, part 3; see also siman 86).}
