Seif 6. 'And they may ask without the knowledge of the litigants.' Note: See my work on Orach Chayyim, Hilchot Beit HaKnesset, siman 153, where I wrote that this applies specifically where they selected only two arbitrators in a manner that allows those arbitrators to choose a third. In such a case they may also seek guidance without the litigants' knowledge. But where they cannot choose the third without the litigants' knowledge, they likewise cannot ask another authority without their knowledge. See there, where I explained this well, with God's help. Since the Sema writes that this law is derived from that case, it is enough that the derived law be like its source. See Shach, seif katan 200. This is clear.
