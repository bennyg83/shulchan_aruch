Once they have presented their claims. This is somewhat difficult in light of siman 12, seif 7, where the Mechaber rules that even after the litigants agreed to compromise before a beit din, they may retract; the Sema and Shach write there that they may retract even after the compromise was announced, and the Rama raises no objection. Here, however, the Rama writes that once they have presented their claims before the selected judges they cannot retract according to all opinions. Perhaps one may distinguish between agreeing to compromise before an ordinary beit din and doing so through the procedure in which each party selects one judge, but this distinction is difficult.
