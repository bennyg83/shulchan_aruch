They divide the rent. This differs from one who rents an unspecified donkey and it dies halfway, where the owner must supply another, and if he does not, the renter pays nothing. There the merchandise for which the donkey was hired still exists. It resembles the case where one party brings the wine and the other fails to bring the ship. Here both parties are equally prevented from completing the original agreement. At first glance it appears that even if they traveled less than half the route, or more than half, they would still divide the rent, because neither one's existing possession gives him a decisive advantage and each must give the other half when both stand equally regarding fulfillment of the condition.
