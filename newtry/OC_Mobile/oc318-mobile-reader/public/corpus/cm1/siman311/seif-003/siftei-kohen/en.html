Some say that he pays only the rent for half the journey. This is the Rosh's view, based on the rule that when an unavoidable event affecting the employer was no more foreseeable to him than to the worker, the worker bears the loss. Tosafot already answer that the cases differ. There the worker lost nothing except the opportunity to work; here the shipowner lost his ship. Hagahot Mordechai, Hagahot Maimoniyot, and Terumat HaDeshen give the same answer. The primary ruling therefore follows the Rif, Rambam, Mechaber, and those who agree with them, as indicated by the plain Gemara and by Maharam, the Agudah, Nimukei Yosef, Maggid Mishneh, and later authorities.
