Some say. This is the view of the Rosh, who challenges the Rif from the rule at the beginning of siman 334 that when an unavoidable event occurs to the employer and the employer had no greater reason to know of it than the worker, the worker bears the loss. Tosafot, chapter HaOmanin 79a, already addressed this and answered in the name of Rivan that there the worker loses nothing tangible and merely remains idle from his work, whereas here the lessor loses his ship itself. Hagahot Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot cite this answer in Rivan's name, and Terumat HaDeshen rules likewise. Therefore the primary ruling follows the Rif, Rambam, Mechaber, and those who agree with them. This accords with the plain meaning of the Gemara, and is also the view of Maharam in the responsum cited by the Mordechai, the Agudah, and apparently Nimukei Yosef and other later authorities. Shach.
