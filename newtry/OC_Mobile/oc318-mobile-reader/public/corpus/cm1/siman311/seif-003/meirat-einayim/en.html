Rent for half the journey. "Half" is not literal. He pays according to the proportion of the journey actually traveled. Their rationale is that whenever a loss was no more foreseeable to the renter than to the owner, the loss falls upon the owner, who seeks to extract payment, as explained in siman 310. If the shipowner already received all the rent, Tosafot writes that he need not return it because he remains ready to fulfill his side of the agreement. The Mechaber follows the Rif and Rambam, who do not compare this case to an ordinary employer and worker or ordinary renter and owner. In those cases the worker's loss is merely lost wages. Here the shipowner lost the ship itself. Therefore, so long as he is ready to fulfill his obligation, he is at least entitled to compensation like an idle worker. See the Derishah.
