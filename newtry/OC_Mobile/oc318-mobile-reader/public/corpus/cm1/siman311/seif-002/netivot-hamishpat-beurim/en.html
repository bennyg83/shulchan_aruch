A major objection. See Shach, seif katan 1. The text should read "because both sank," rather than "with wine that sank." His explanation requires that both the ship and the wine be lost. Since the wording as printed appears to make the rule depend only upon the wine, the correct reading is "because both sank." The Shach's criticism of the Sema's words, where the Sema says that "it" means the ship, likewise concerns the awkwardness of the wording. The ruling refers generally to this ship and this wine, where substitution of another wine is impossible, and the Sema's explanation does not fit.
And if it is possible to recover it. This follows the view of the dissenting authorities cited by Rama in siman 310, seif 2. According to the Mechaber there, once the ship was rented for carrying cargo, the renter does not become a renter merely through payment of money.
