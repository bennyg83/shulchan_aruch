The Tur writes likewise here. So writes the Sema. The Shach disagrees: since the Mechaber writes here without qualification that he must pay wages for the entire journey, it appears that in this case there is no deduction at all, as the Beit Yosef writes in the name of Maggid Mishneh. Nevertheless, Tosafot, chapter HaOmanin 79b, plainly rule like the Tur, as does the Ir Shushan. This appears to be the primary practical ruling from the Gemara there 77a, the poskim, and below at the beginning of siman 335. Shach.
