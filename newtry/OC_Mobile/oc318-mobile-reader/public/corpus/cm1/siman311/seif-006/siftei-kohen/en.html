He pays the rent for the entire journey. The Sema says that he nevertheless deducts the owner's saved expense, as the Tur and Mechaber rule above. The Mechaber's unqualified language here indicates otherwise. The Maggid Mishneh explains that nothing is deducted because the ship still exists and the owner can say, “I am ready to perform.” This differs from an unspecified ship and specified wine where the ship sank, for there the ship no longer exists and an expense deduction applies. Tosafot and the Tur nevertheless state that the saved expense is deducted, and the Ir Shushan agrees. This latter view appears primary in practice, in light of the Gemara and the rulings cited at the beginning of siman 335.
