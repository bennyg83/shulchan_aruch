The wording of Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 4, as explained by the Gemara there. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Gemara: if he did not find another renter, must she pay him the entire rental? The commentators explain that she deducts nothing because the ship remains intact and the owner can say, 'I am ready.' This differs from a case of a specified ship and specified wine where the ship sank, in which he deducts because the ship no longer exists.
This is explained in the Gemara there. The Raavad also objected to Rambam on this point, and the Maggid Mishneh writes that the Raavad's position is indeed the truth as explicit in the Gemara.
There and in the baraita, according to the Gemara's resolution.
