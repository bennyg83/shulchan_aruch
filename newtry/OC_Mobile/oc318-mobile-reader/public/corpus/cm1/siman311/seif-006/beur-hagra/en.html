And he must pay, etc. Rif and Rosh rule this way, in accordance with the Gemara. Rambam holds that the claim of increased wear was valid only under the Gemara's initial assumption, but under the conclusion it is unnecessary. His text apparently read, 'If so, the claim, etc.,' meaning that it is no claim at all, even as grounds for resentment. Rambam therefore mentions only resentment, as in the latter clause. Tosafot follow Rif and Rosh. See their discussion, s.v. 'when he unloaded it.' See 81.
