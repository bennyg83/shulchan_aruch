He pays the hire for the entire journey. Maggid Mishneh explains that nothing is deducted because the ship still exists and the owner can say, "I am ready with my ship." Mishneh LaMelech asks why this differs from workers who receive only an idle-worker wage when the work falls away. Shitah Mekubetzet, citing Rabbeinu Yonatan, supplies the distinction: if the cargo is removed in mid-route and the vessel is left idle, the shipowner suffers from the idleness itself, like the workers of Mechoza who weaken when idle. He can therefore demand the full fare. If the original ship sank, that special claim no longer exists, even if another vessel could be procured. This is the intent of Maggid Mishneh.
