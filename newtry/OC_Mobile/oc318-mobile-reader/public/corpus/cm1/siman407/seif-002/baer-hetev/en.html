This is the principal view, because we rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel that one who impairs another's lien is liable, as the Rambam writes in Hilchot Chovel, chapter 7, and Hilchot Malveh, chapter 8. The Mechaber likewise rules in siman 117, as do the Hagahot Ashri in HaManiach and Maharshal there, siman 22. From the Mechaber's subsequent words, 'if he gave it as a gift,' one should not infer that he is exempt from the loss caused by slaughter, as the Gemara infers in its formulation. There the inference is proper because it states, 'he slaughtered it or gave it as a gift, what he did is effective.' The Rambam and Mechaber, however, first wrote that if he slaughtered it, the injured party collects from the meat. Their later statement that if he gave it as a gift means that the gift is effective for purposes of using it for plowing. The Eir Shushan writes similarly. See what I wrote above in siman 386. Shach.
