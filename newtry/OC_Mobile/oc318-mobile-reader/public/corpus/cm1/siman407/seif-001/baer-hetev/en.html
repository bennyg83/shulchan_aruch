Sold. Maharshal questions this and writes that according to Rabbi Akiva the ox is not sold even for plowing, and this is also the Tur's view. Tosafot on Bava Kamma 33a, s.v. vehikdisho, write likewise. This appears to be the principal view in the Gemara, since we rule like Rabbi Akiva that the parties are partners. This is what the Rama means in seif 2, 'and similarly, if he used it,' but in my view it was not placed correctly and should have been written here as an alternative opinion. Shach.
