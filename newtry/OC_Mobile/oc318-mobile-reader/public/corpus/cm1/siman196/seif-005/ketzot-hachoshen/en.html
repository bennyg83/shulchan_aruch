He called it and it came to him. Sema writes that the same should work if the first master tells the slave, "Go to the purchaser." I do not see the basis for this. Meshichah works when the object moves because of the purchaser's action, like one who leads an animal. If the slave moves because of the seller's command, the purchaser has done nothing. The Shulchan Aruch's wording, that the purchaser calls and the slave comes to him, appears deliberate.
