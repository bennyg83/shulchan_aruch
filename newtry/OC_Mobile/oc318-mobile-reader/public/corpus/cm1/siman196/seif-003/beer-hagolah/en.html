The Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of the Rashba that even according to our version of the Halakhot, where the matter is presented as a dispute, we rule that the slave is acquired by lifting. Rabbi Shimon's position is accepted, since acquisition is effective even by meshichah according to Shmuel and Rav Ashi there, all the more so by lifting. The Ran likewise writes so.
