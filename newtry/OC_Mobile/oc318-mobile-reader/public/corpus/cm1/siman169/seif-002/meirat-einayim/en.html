And don't put any fruit into it:
And the outer owner may sow, etc. Although sowing somewhat interferes with passage to his garden, since the path runs through the middle of the field he would suffer a substantial loss if he could not sow it. Presumably the path was granted on that understanding, so that he would retain permission to sow there. This differs from a path running along the side of the field, where the loss is much smaller. There the path was presumably granted for walking alone and he may not sow it. Accordingly, the Tur concludes that neither of them may sow the side path. This is the Mishnah's language and is the explanation of Rashi, Nimukei Yosef, and the other authorities. The Rambam likewise writes that each may restrain the other from sowing the path that was given at the side. The printed Shulchan Aruch reading that both are permitted to sow is therefore a scribal error. It should read that they are not permitted, as in the older editions.
