He explains that even in a courtyard there is concern for increased traffic of people entering and leaving, all the more so in one's house.
Maggid Mishneh cites Rashi: when the path runs through the middle, we are witnesses that the owner did not completely waive the path so that the other may plant it. But when it is on the side, he loses less, and since it was established with the consent of both, we assume he fully waived that strip for passage and not for planting.
