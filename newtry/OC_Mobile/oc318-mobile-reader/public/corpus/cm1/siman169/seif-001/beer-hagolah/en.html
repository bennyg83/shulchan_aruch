As the Gemara explains the reason there: the cistern owner requires a key to protect his water, and the courtyard owner requires one because of suspicion regarding his wife, so that the cistern owner should not enter the house unless the courtyard owner is present. Bach writes that the rule is not limited to a cistern; the same applies to a room, where each should have a key because of suspicion. The Mishnah mentions a cistern because of the separate law concerning his animal.
