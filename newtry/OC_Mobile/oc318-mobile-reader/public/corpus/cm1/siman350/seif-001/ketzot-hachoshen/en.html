He stole its forbidden fat. See the Shach's discussion whether the right to choose the recipient of a gift has monetary value. Mordechai in chapter Shevuat HaDayanim writes that this right is not considered money, following Ulla and Rav Asi in the second chapter of Kiddushin. Although Rava is later and explains a Mishnah in Nedarim according to the view that the benefit is money, he did so only to avoid another difficult interpretation; Rava himself holds that the benefit is not money. The Shach asks where we find that Rava himself holds that it is not money. It appears that Mordechai refers to Rava's statement at the beginning of Bava Metzia, 11a. Rava says that Rabban Gamliel properly did not use chalipin, because although they certainly had a scarf with which to perform chalipin, the benefit of choosing the recipient is not money that can be acquired through chalipin. The same reasoning indicates that such benefit is not money for acquisition through land either. See what I wrote in siman 275, seif katan 1, and siman 278, seif katan 1.
