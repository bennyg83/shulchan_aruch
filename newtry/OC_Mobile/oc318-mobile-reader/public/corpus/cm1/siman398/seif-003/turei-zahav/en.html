Exempt from damage to vessels. The Sema asks a question here and answers it. In my opinion there is no question. Simanim 403 and 410 speak specifically after the injured party became aware of the danger. Here, even if he did not know that the ox had fallen, the owner is nevertheless exempt because the damaged items were vessels. Nor may one ask from seif 2, which rules that if the entering ox injures the courtyard owner's ox, its owner is liable. Why can the owner of the entering ox not say that the courtyard owner should have removed it? The courtyard owner may answer that he assumed it would not injure him and therefore did not forcibly remove it. This is straightforward.
