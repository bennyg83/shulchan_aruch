If, etc. The Gemara there derives this from the stated reason. Even Tosafos agrees, though Tosafos rules according to Rava's answer, as noted in the gloss.
And if, etc. Because we rule like Shmuel in monetary law.
And some say, etc. This follows the Rabbis, since the sugya proceeds according to them. See Tosafos 48b, s.v. "and Shmuel," and s.v. "the reason," where Tosafos explains that according to Rava even Rabbi would impose liability when permission had been given, contrary to the earlier assumption. Rava therefore establishes the Mishna according to the Rabbis. The Rif, however, cites Rava while holding that the ruling applies even according to Rabbi, and merely wishes to establish the Mishna according to the Rabbis as well. The Rosh questions this, for if so the baraisa could have been established wholly according to Rabbi. Hence the Shulchan Aruch writes, "if he brought it in," etc. According to the Rif this is accepted by all; according to Tosafos it is Rava's ruling according to the Rabbis.
Specifically, etc. This is an unresolved question on 47b, and therefore we rule leniently. See Tosafos there, s.v. "then." The Rosh, Tur, and Rama appear to follow Tosafos' first answer; Or Zarua and Hagahos Ashri follow the second, as does Yam Shel Shlomo.
