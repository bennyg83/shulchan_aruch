The Maggid Mishneh writes in his name and in the name of the Rif that the words of Tosafot appear correct and accord with the Rambam: when one brings his ox in with permission, it is as though the parties expressly stipulated that each is exempt from damage caused by his own ox's goring and from other damages. This is the principal view.
