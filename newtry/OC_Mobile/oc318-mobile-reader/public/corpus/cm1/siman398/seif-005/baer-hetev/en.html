From elsewhere. The Shach cites Maharshal as explaining that this refers to oxen that entered the owner's courtyard and damaged the unauthorized entrant there. If the entrant instead went outside and was injured, the courtyard owner is liable. The same applies if the entrant strangled himself within the courtyard, and if he went out and caused damage to others the courtyard owner is liable. The Rama's wording does not naturally imply this, but Maharshal's ruling is primary; examine the Rosh carefully. See above siman 291 and the beginning of siman 393. Shach.
