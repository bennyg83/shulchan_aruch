And if an ox caused damage, etc. There must be a textual error in Raavad’s words, as the Maggid Mishneh explains, unlike the version in the Tur and Bach. This is evident from Raavad’s gloss at the end of chapter 3 of Hilchot Nizkei Mamon. See the Gemara, Tosafot, and Lechem Mishneh in chapter 7. The matter still requires study.
Because it came to him from elsewhere, he is not liable, etc. Maharshal explains this as a case where oxen entered his courtyard and injured the animal. If the animal left the courtyard and was injured, the courtyard owner is liable; likewise if it strangled itself in the courtyard, or left and injured others, the courtyard owner is liable. Maharam’s wording does not clearly imply this, but Maharshal’s ruling is primary. See the Rosh carefully.
