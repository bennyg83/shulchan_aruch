Its law is as though it damaged in the public domain, etc. Do not say that even a tam should pay full damages for keren in the injured party's domain by an a fortiori argument from shen and regel: although shen and regel pay nothing in the public domain, they pay full damages in the injured party's domain. The ruling teaches that we do not say this. Rather, we apply the principle of dayo: since keren in the public domain pays only half damages, it pays only half damages even in the injured party's domain. See what I wrote in siman 389.
