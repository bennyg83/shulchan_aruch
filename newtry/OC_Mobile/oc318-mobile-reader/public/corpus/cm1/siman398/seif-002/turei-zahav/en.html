[Seif 4] At the time of the fall he is liable. The Sema explains that this refers to an innocuous ox or alternatively, and so forth. This does not appear correct. Here the fall is not treated as an accident, for if it were, the owner would be exempt as stated in siman 394. Rather, because the ox entered without permission, its conduct is treated as intentional negligence. It is therefore liable for the entire damage, like the case in siman 394 where an animal descended in its ordinary manner, caused damage, and was injured by the fall, and pays the full loss. The reason is that this is treated like tooth and foot, whose damage is common. The same applies here, and even an innocuous ox pays full damages.
