In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger, he observes that the Maggid Mishneh, Hilchot To’en chapter 14, appears not to accept this distinction and understands the Raavad to hold that even a definite claim based upon information received from another is treated as a definite claim. As for the Tur’s statement in the name of the Raavad that when the witness is not before us no oath, not even hesset, is imposed, Responsa Devar Shmuel, siman 28, writes that this Raavad is the author referred to in Baal HaItur, not the Raavad who wrote glosses on the Rambam.
