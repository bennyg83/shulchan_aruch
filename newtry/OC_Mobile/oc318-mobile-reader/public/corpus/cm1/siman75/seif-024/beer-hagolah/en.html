The Rambam at the end of chapter 14 of Hilkhot Malveh, also cited by the Tur at the end of the siman. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Geonim derived this from Shevuot 43b, where the lender swears first lest the borrower swear and then the lender produce the collateral, etc. Although this is not a complete proof, it is somewhat analogous, and the rule is intrinsically sound.
