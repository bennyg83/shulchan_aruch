We tell the lender, "Nullify every document, etc." In the Perishah I wrote that this law is derived from what the Tur and Mechaber state in siman 72:15. If the borrower is required to take an oath, the lender must first swear that the pledge is not in his possession, lest the borrower swear and the lender afterward produce the pledge, showing that it was not worth the amount claimed, resulting in a desecration of the Divine name or rendering the oath one taken in vain. The same reasoning applies here.
