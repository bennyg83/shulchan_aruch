[Shach, letter 82] The Raavad distinguishes between “my father told me” and “one valid witness told me.” Note: However, from Maggid Mishneh, Hilchot Toen VeNitan 14:5, it appears that he does not accept this distinction and understands the Raavad to hold that even where the definite claim is based on another person’s statement, it is considered a definite claim. As to what the Tur cites in the name of the Raavad, that where the witnesses are not before us we do not compel the defendant to swear even a heset oath, Responsa Devar Shmuel, siman 28, writes that this refers to the author called “the Rav Av Beit Din,” not the Raavad who wrote the glosses, as is explicit in Baal HaTerumot.
