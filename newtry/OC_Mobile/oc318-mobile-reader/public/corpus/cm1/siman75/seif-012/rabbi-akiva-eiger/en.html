[Shulchan Aruch] But he says, “It is mine,” he is one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear. Note: If he has a supporting witness who testifies that what he seized was indeed his, see Ketzot HaChoshen, siman 87, seif katan 9, who cites Shitah Mekubetzet in Bava Metzia that such a witness is effective.
