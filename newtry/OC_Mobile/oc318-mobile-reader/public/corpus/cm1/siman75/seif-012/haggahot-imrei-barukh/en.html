(Shach, seif katan 31) And proof from one who was claimed against for wheat, etc. Note: At first glance one can distinguish the case of wheat, which concerns a claim in beit din. Since the claimant did not demand the barley there as well, this is viewed as a certain waiver. Here, however, the case presumably did not occur in beit din, since the second party had not yet been aroused to sue him. If he merely answered, "I certainly do not owe him," one can say that he had not carefully reviewed his account and later remembered. Yet Biurim, seif katan 29, appears to understand that this case too concerns a statement made in beit din. This requires study.
