The interpretation of the baraita taught by Rabbi Ami, Shevuot 47a.
Meaning, whether your father repaid you or not. We do not say "since he cannot swear, he must pay" except regarding the litigant himself, who should know the facts, but not regarding an heir who had no reason to know.
There in the name of his father the Rosh and Baal HaIttur.
This is clear there in the Gemara regarding Rabbi Ami's baraita. The Rambam likewise rules in chapter 4 of Hilkhot To'en. The Maggid Mishneh writes there that this follows Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov, Shevuot 42b, according to the explanation given above that his rule applies only where the son makes a definite claim, and this is the position of the Halakhot, etc. So too writes the Rosh there in his rulings.
