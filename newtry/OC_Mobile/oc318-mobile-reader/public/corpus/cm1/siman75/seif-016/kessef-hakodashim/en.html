He is not considered interested. A trusted accountant in a store gives the proprietor a definite claim based on the statement of a person who is not interested. No general acceptance of a ban is required, and the defendant cannot reverse the oath onto the merchant. If a person makes a definite claim based on what a non-Jew told him, although the Shach in siman 149 writes that a hesset oath can be imposed on the basis of a definite claim learned from a non-Jew, perhaps such a claim is no stronger than a claim learned from an interested party, which does not impose hesset. This requires further study.
