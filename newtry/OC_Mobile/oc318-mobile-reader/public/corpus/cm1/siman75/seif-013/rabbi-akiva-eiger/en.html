[Shulchan Aruch] And the claimant need not swear. Note: If the defendant has one witness who testifies that he owes only fifty, although the defendant must nevertheless pay because this is a case of one who is obligated to swear and cannot swear, which is treated as a direct monetary obligation, nevertheless the claimant must first swear because one witness testifies that the excess has been paid. This is clear from the responsum of the Rashba cited in Beit Yosef, siman 75, new matter 24.
