If so, when one claims wheat and the defendant admits barley, the claimant should be able to say, "I did not remember the barley, and therefore I did not claim it; now, from your own words, I have considered the matter and remembered that you also owe me the barley." Even if he still does not actually remember it, perhaps he should be able to claim the barley on the basis of the defendant's admission, since he never said that the barley was certainly not owed to him.
