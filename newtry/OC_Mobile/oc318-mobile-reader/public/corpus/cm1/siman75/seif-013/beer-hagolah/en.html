The Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilkhot To'en, law 8.
This law concerning the claim 'I repaid you' is not found in the Rambam except where he was established as a denier, in the manner written by the Mechaber at the end of the seif. Otherwise he would swear to contradict the single witness and be exempt, for what difference is there whether he contradicts the witness regarding the loan itself or regarding repayment? So writes the Maggid Mishneh in the name of the Rashba. One can explain that the Mechaber's phrase, 'Likewise, if he initially claimed,' refers back to this first case of 'I repaid you,' where it occurred in a manner that made him an established denier, so that he became obligated to swear but could not. The Sema initially writes similarly, but was troubled by the Beit Yosef's statement in seif 11 in the name of the Rambam that if he claims with certainty that he repaid, he is one obligated to swear, etc., upon which the Maggid Mishneh explains that this must concern testimony that he did not repay, such as where the witness never left him, or where it is within the term. This differs from the other poskim. The Bach writes that a textual error entered the books and proposes corrected readings. He explains the Beit Yosef's citation of the Rambam to mean that he claims repayment through what the lender owes him, for the reason explained there.
