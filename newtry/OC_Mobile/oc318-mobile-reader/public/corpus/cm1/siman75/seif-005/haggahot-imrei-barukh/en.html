(Mechaber, seif 4) He became established as one who falsely denies. Note: This is also clear from Tosafot, Bava Metzia 5a, passage beginning "if so." In Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Metzia 4a, passage beginning "what is common to the two," the Ramah is cited as holding that if witnesses testify to fifty of a deposited amount in circumstances that establish the defendant as a false denier regarding that fifty, there is no Torah oath on the remainder. Since he is already established as a false denier, the obligation cannot be derived from his own admission, and the rule that one who is obligated to swear but cannot do so must pay does not apply. The Rosh disagrees in his interpretation of the Gemara. The Ritva cited there appears to understand Tosafot in accordance with the Ramah.
