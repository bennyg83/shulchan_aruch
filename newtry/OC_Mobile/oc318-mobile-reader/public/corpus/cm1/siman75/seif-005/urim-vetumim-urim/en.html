He must clarify, etc. Bava Batra 70a discusses one who says, 'My field is sold to you except for half of such and such field.' Rashbam explains in astonishment that the seller sold one field, not the rest, and that an absurd additional phrase should not be used to infer that he sold what he never intended to sell. Nimukei Yosef derives from this that we do not overanalyze a person's words in order to use them against him where he did not expressly say the damaging implication. We may say that an extraneous phrase was spoken foolishly. Sema cites this. The Shach says he searched and did not find it, but it appears at the cited place.
