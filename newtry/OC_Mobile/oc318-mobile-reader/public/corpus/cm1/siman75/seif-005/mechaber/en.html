The claimant says, "You owe me one hundred from one loan and another one hundred from a different loan made at another time." The defendant answers, "I owe you nothing, because from the first loan I repaid such and such and from the second I repaid such and such." If, from the parties' own accounting, beit din determines that twenty dinars from the two loans remain unpaid, this is treated like the testimony of two witnesses that he has fifty in his possession, because beit din itself witnesses the admission that a balance remains. He must therefore take a Torah oath regarding the remainder. Rama: This applies specifically where beit din determines that, according to his own accounting, he admitted that a portion remains. If he becomes liable for part only because the law does not accept some aspect of his explanation, that is not considered a partial admission. See below, siman 87, seif 5.
