You have one maneh of mine... As stated there, 3a: "But here... we are witnesses..."
And specifically... but... For example, where he is one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear, as in the cases listed below in seif 13. Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch, siman 364, seif 6, explain that perhaps he is telling the truth, and therefore this does not constitute an admission. This applies specifically as stated there, "we are witnesses..." and the Gemara says there that the admission of his own mouth should not be more stringent, etc., by a kal vachomer from his own mouth. With an admission of this sort he is exempt, as stated above in siman 364, seif 6.
(Collected note) But if... For example, where he granted the lender credibility, or told him, "Do not repay me except before witnesses," or where he is one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear. See siman 364, seif 6. (End of note.)
