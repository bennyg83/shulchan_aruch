He is exempt even from a heset oath. Since the heir himself did not see the loan or admission, he is like one who returns a lost item, who is exempt even from heset as explained above in seif 3. The same is true if he admitted nothing at all, according to the Rambam and those who follow him.
And some say he is liable, etc. I wrote above that Rabbeinu Baruch exempts him from a Torah oath but requires a heset oath even when he denies everything. The consensus of the later authorities follows the Rambam and those who agree with him.
