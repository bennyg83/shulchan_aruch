Only fifty, etc. This is the classic oath of partial admission, derived from the verse, 'For he says concerning it: this is it.' The Gemara asks why a partial admitter must swear. The answer is that a person ordinarily lacks the brazenness to deny everything to his creditor. He would like to deny all, but cannot be so brazen; he would like to admit all, but is temporarily pushing off payment until he has money. The Torah therefore imposes an oath.
