And one does not remove a person from Eretz Yisrael to outside the Land. This phrase is seemingly unnecessary. If one can even compel ascent to Eretz Yisrael, certainly one cannot compel departure from it. The Gemara, Ketubot 110b, says, "and not everyone may remove," to include a slave who fled from outside Eretz Yisrael to Eretz Yisrael. See Tosafot there regarding the continuation, "everyone may compel ascent to Jerusalem, and one may not compel departure.
