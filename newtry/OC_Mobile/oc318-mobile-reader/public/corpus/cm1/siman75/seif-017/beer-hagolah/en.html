The language of the Tur, and likewise the Rambam in chapter 1 of Hilkhot To'en, law 7. The Maggid Mishneh writes that all the poskim agree, from the statement regarding Rav Nachman that we impose a shevuat hesset because a person is presumed not to make a claim unless he has some basis, Shevuot 40b. This shows that a definite claim is specifically required. So too Baal HaTerumot at the end of gate 38. See below, siman 87, seifim 25 and 26.
The Tur and Baal HaTerumot, gate 36, in the name of Rabbi Avraham bar Yitzchak.
