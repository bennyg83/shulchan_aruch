And witnesses testify, etc. See Tumim. According to Sema, the witnesses must testify clearly that they know the debt still exists and was not repaid. Shach holds that once the defendant says, 'I never borrowed,' he himself admits that he never repaid, and the witnesses need only establish the loan. I wrote in Tumim that this is a dispute among major authorities, and Rambam and Tur appear to support Sema.
