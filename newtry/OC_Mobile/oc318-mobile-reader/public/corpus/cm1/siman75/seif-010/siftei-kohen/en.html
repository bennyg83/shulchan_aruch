He did not claim from him, etc.; and I do not know whether I returned it to you, he is exempt. Even if the other party later replies, “I am certain that you never repaid me, although I do not know of the original loan,” the defendant remains exempt. This follows from the authorities' unqualified language and from the Gemara in HaGozel Batra. Otherwise the Gemara could have preserved the plain meaning of the statement that he is liable by establishing that case. Nevertheless, it appears that he must take a heset oath. This differs from siman 88, seif 32, because there he claims definite repayment and has a migo, so he is regarded as one returning a lost object. Here he says, “I do not know.” Moreover, I question the ruling in siman 88 itself, as explained there.
