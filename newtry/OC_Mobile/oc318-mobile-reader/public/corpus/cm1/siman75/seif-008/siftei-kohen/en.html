The same applies if he says, “You stole from me.” Although according to the claimant the defendant is suspected of dishonesty, he may nevertheless be required to swear, since he was not established as suspect through witnesses, as explained in siman 92. The Beit Yosef cites the students of the Rashba concerning a dispute among the leading authorities whether one may impose an oath upon a person accused of taking interest. The same dispute appears in the Ritva at the beginning of Bava Metzia. The accepted ruling is that he may be required to swear, as explained in Yoreh De'ah at the end of siman 169 and below in siman 82, seif 10. See also siman 87, seif 25, and my comments there.
