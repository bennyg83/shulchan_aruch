(Shach, seif katan 19) And now he became obligated for part, so one who is obligated to swear but cannot do so must pay. Note: This requires study, because the Rashba holds that in such a case we do not apply that rule when the defendant seeks exemption by claiming payment. The Shach himself, below in seif katan 38, cites the Rashba's responsum brought by the Beit Yosef, and the same ruling is mentioned by the Sema below, siman 79, seif katan 20.
