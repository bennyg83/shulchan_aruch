Although the claimant says that the defendant robbed him and one witness also testified against him, one witness does not disqualify a person. We say perhaps there is some doubtful loan the defendant believes is owed to him, and he therefore took the money intending to return it after reviewing his accounts and discovering that it is not owed. For this reason the Sages imposed an oath upon him, since he certainly will not swear falsely on the basis of mere doubt.
