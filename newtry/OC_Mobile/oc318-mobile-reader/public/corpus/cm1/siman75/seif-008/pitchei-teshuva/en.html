To satisfy Heaven, he should pay. See Baer Heitev, which cites Rivash, siman 392, in the name of Ramban, regarding a defendant who seized property. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger: See Shitah Mekubetzet, first chapter of Bava Metzia, in the sugya of “a kohen who seized,” in the name of Ramban. It appears there that this rule depends upon the general dispute among the poskim whether certain seizure after the doubt has arisen is effective. Beit Meir, Even HaEzer, end of siman 177, likewise notes this and questions the Shach for citing the Rivash in the name of Ramban without qualification as halachah, because Ramban himself is the principal authority who holds that seizure is ineffective in cases of legal doubt, and Rivash follows that view. According to the opposing authorities the result would differ. See below, siman 388, Shach seif katan 31, and Dagul MeRevavah here, and what I wrote above, siman 28, seif 1, seif katan 6.
