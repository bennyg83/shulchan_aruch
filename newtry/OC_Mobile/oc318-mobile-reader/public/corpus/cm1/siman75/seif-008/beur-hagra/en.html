You have a maneh of mine... which you robbed from me... We do not say that one who is suspected concerning money is thereby suspected concerning an oath, as stated in Bava Metzia 6a: we are concerned that perhaps he is uncertain, etc. Although a robber established by witnesses is disqualified from taking an oath, as stated there, without witnesses he is not, as Tosafos there, s.v. "safek..." and the Rosh explain. In the incident of the silver ingot of Rabbi Abba, liability arose specifically because he did not contradict the witness, making him one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear. Had he contradicted the witness, he would have been believed with an oath. Certainly this is so if there was not even one witness. See Bava Basra 34a, Rashbam, and Tosafos, s.v. "havei...".
