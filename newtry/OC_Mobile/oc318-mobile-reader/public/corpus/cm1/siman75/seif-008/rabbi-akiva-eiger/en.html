[Shach, letter 26] That if the claimant seized the property in the presence of witnesses. Note: See Shitah Mekubetzet, first chapter of Bava Metzia, in the sugya of “the priest seized it,” in the name of the Ramban. It appears there that this law depends upon the dispute among the authorities regarding all cases of doubt, whether seizure is effective when accompanied by a definite claim. I later saw that Beit Meir, Even HaEzer, end of siman 177, also arrived at this point. See above, siman 388, Shach, seif katan 31, and examine this carefully.
