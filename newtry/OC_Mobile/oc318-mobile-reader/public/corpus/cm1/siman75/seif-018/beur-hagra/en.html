He claimed against him, etc., and he fulfills, etc. One cannot fulfill his obligation toward Heaven unless the claimant advances a definite claim.
And some say, etc. Their dispute depends on Bava Metzia 37a concerning the father of one of them. The Gemara establishes it where no claim was made and the purpose is to fulfill one's obligation toward Heaven. Sefer HaTerumot cites an opinion that the same applies where one of you deposited with me, while Ramban limits it specifically to the case of a father, as stated above in siman 76:3 and below in siman 300:3 in the gloss.
