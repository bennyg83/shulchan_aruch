For what does he owe you? A person may not claim two hundred when only one hundred is owed merely in order to force the defendant into a partial-admission oath and then roll another claim onto it. Likewise, a defendant may not say, 'If I admit the part I truly owe in beit din, I will incur a Torah oath, so I will deny everything and pay that portion privately.' If the claimant has only one witness, he may not say, 'Come, you and I will testify.' And if three people are owed money, one may not become the claimant while the other two falsely pose as witnesses. All such conduct is forbidden. Gemara, decisors, and Shach.
