(He shall consume the produce until what he consumes repays the debt.) The creditor may say that it suits him to cultivate and consume the produce of the entire field, not only part. Therefore, with movable pledges, if the debtor seeks to redeem half and leave a proportionate half, he should be heard where the creditor loses nothing. The Rosh’s house case supports the Ramban only where partial redemption causes loss of the agreed benefit. Where there is no loss, refusal is the trait of Sodom.
