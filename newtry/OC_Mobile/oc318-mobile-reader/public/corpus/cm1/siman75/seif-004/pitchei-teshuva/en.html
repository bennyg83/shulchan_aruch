And he shall swear by Torah law concerning the remainder. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger, of blessed memory: If he says concerning the remainder, 'I do not know,' it appears to me that we do not apply the rule that one who cannot swear must pay, because this oath is derived by an a fortiori inference from an oath imposed through gilgul, and many poskim hold that with an oath imposed by gilgul we do not say that one who cannot swear must pay. See Besamim Rosh, siman 17. See Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, siman 9, which cites this Besamim Rosh and writes that it cannot be accepted as authoritative and did not issue from the Rosh. He extensively proves that the Rosh did not hold this view. See there.
