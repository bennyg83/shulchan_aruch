Seif 8. 'You have one hundred in your possession that you robbed from me,' etc. Note: See my novellae to Choshen Mishpat, siman 298, where I raised the question of one who says, 'I do not know whether I lent you,' but inwardly believes the claimant. Is he obligated to pay? May the claimant impose a rolled oath upon him, or a cherem, that he does not inwardly believe him? Or perhaps even if he inwardly believes him he is not obligated to pay. This requires study. See there.
