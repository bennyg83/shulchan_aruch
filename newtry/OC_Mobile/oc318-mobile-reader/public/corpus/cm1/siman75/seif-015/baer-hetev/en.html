It likewise appears to be Ramban's view and is explicit in Maggid Mishneh, that here even with a gilgul oath we apply the rule that one who cannot swear must pay. Maggid Mishneh qualifies that when the primary claim itself is doubtful, we do not apply this rule through gilgul. In siman 344 I wrote that Ramban also appears to hold this. The authorities mentioned above do not agree with that qualification. In their view, even when the primary claim is doubtful, a gilgul can create the rule that one who cannot swear must pay.
