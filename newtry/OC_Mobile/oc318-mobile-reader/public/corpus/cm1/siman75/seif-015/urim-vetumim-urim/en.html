From those objects, etc. The Rosh was asked about a defendant accused of receiving two hundred zuz as a deposit to invest and improve. He denied the entire matter, while witnesses testified that he had admitted before them to fifty. The Rosh ruled that he is established as one who falsely denied and cannot say that he was merely delaying, because the claimant did not entrust him with a particular identifiable object but gave him money to spend and invest, which he indeed did. See Tumim. I discussed at length why we do not say that perhaps the invested property was lost through the custodian's negligence and he presently lacks money to reimburse it, so that the denial was only temporary delay. I wrote that the underlying rule appears to be disputed between Tosafot and Rashi and therefore remains a doubt in law requiring study.
