And an oath imposed through extension. If the defendant says he does not know the matter added by extension, we do not apply the rule of one who must swear but cannot. He swears that he does not know and is exempt. See Beurim: the primary rule is that if the claimant asserts with certainty that the defendant knows and the defendant says he does not know, the defendant must take a Torah oath through extension that he does not know. But if the claimant does not assert with certainty that the defendant knows, one cannot extend an oath of "I do not know." However, when the principal oath itself arises from an uncertain claim, such as the oath of custodians, one may extend an oath concerning lack of knowledge even without the claimant asserting certainty.
