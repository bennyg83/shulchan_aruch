(Seif 9.) "I do not know whether you lent me." We do not say that a certain claim is stronger so as to extract money. The Tur writes likewise in siman 223. The claimant does not need to swear. Although in siman 91, seif 3, the Rama rules that whenever one party knows and the other does not, the party who knows must swear, there the knowing party expended a certain amount with the other's authorization and is believed by oath regarding the amount spent. See siman 79 and Tur siman 126, seif 17.
