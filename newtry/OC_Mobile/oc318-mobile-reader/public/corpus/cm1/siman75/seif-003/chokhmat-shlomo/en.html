Seif 7. “You have a maneh of mine in your possession.” He answers, “I have nothing of yours,” even though two witnesses testify that he borrowed from him, etc. Someone raised an empty objection by comparing this to a condition stated in affirmative form, where the party relying upon fulfillment bears the burden of proving that he fulfilled it. I explained the underlying reason for that rule in my seventh-edition responsum on Yoreh Deah, Hilchot Sefer Torah, siman 28. The comparison therefore does not create a difficulty here. See there.
