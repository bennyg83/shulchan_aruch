Sema, seif katan 31, "this is difficult, for even." Note: here too it appears to me as follows. One witness testifies that the lender warned him, "Do not repay me except in the presence of this witness," and the borrower says, "I repaid you before another person." Ordinarily he should be believed through a migo that he could have said, "You never stipulated this with me at all," as the Sema wrote above, siman 70, seif katan 11. Nevertheless, since there is one witness against him, he is one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear, and therefore pays. This appears correct.
