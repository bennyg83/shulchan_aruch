The wording of the Rambam there in chapter 4. The Maggid Mishneh derives it from the baraita containing the dispute of Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov and the Sages, Shevuot 42a. Even according to the poskim who rule like Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov, he said his rule only where the son makes a definite claim that he saw the defendant admit the debt to his father. The Sages hold that it is considered a definite claim only where the son says that the loan itself occurred in his presence. Many commentators explain it this way.
There is no shevuat heset on an uncertain claim.
