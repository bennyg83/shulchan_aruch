[Shulchan Aruch] And he shall swear by Torah law. Note: If with respect to the remainder he says, "I do not know," we do not apply the rule that one who is obligated to swear and cannot do so must pay, because the additional oath arises only through gilgul shevuah. Many authorities hold that the rule of payment for inability to swear does not apply to such a derivative oath. Responsa Besamim Rosh, siman 17.
