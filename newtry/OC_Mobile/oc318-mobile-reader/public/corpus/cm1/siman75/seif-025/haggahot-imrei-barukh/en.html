(Same Shach) And he brings proof from what we say in chapter HaMafkid. Note: This wording is imprecise. The rule is explicit in the Gemara in Kiddushin 43, "and now that the Sages instituted the oath of agents." Terumat HaDeshen explains that the reason is analogous to the rule that we require him to swear that the item is not in his possession.
