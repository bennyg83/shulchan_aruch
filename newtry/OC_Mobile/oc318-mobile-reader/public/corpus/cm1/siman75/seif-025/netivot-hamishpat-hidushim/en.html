We do not listen to him. With a document, it is understandable, for the claimant certainly knows whether he has a document or other proof. With witnesses, however, perhaps he truly does not know whether witnesses exist or is presently unable to bring them. If he later discovers that there are witnesses, why should his proof be nullified for no reason? The Shach infers from this that if he specifies particular witnesses, naming this person and that person, then in a setting where beit din tells him, "Go and bring them or your testimony is void," their status is like that of a document and he must expressly nullify his reliance upon them.
