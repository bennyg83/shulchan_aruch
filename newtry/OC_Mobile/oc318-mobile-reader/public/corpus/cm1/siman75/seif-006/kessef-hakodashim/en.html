For a partial admission to arise from an obligation established by beit din, perhaps the principle of 'truth according to its truth' applies, meaning both according to the halacha and according to the factual picture. Where liability emerges from both, it is a partial admission even if the liability exists only because of a Torah-law rule such as interest.
