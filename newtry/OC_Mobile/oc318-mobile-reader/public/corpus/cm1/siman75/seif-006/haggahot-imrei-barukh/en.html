(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 3) And he answers that if so, why, etc. Note: See Shitah Mekubetzet there and Ritva. Their principal reasoning is that since he claims regarding one item that it was lost through unavoidable circumstances, why did he deny liability regarding the second? He could have claimed unavoidable loss for the second as well, making one claim regarding both. Why then did he vary his claims?
