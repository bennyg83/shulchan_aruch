See Shach, seif katan 27. The Shach and Urim VeTumim write that where there is doubt whether an earlier payment preceded the later loan, the case is treated as "I do not know whether I repaid." It appears that the reason is our rule that where each party has an independent debt against the other, each collects his own debt. In a partnership, however, where the partners need not convert every advance into a separate loan but instead make a final accounting of what each spent, an uncertainty of this kind may be treated as "I do not know whether I ever became obligated.
