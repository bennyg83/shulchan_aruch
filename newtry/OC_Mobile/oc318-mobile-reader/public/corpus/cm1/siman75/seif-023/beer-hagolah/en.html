He writes that this is also the Rif's view from the responsum of a Gaon cited in chapter Kol HaNishba'in concerning one man who said to another, etc., where the Gaon ruled that he is obligated to swear, etc. The Ramban decided like the Rif, and the Rambam likewise writes so in chapter 4.
