To settle outside Eretz Yisrael. If he has not yet divorced her and later dies outside Eretz Yisrael, she collects her ketubah and addition from his estate. If she too dies before collecting, her heirs collect in her place. But if he divorced her immediately when he went to Eretz Yisrael, even if he later returned outside the Land, it appears she has no further claim against him after divorce. Likewise, if he wished to move her from one city to another and she refused, her law is that she leaves without a ketubah. Even if he returned to his original city before divorcing her, she has already lost the ketubah. This differs from ascent to Eretz Yisrael. There, if before his death he himself rejected Eretz Yisrael and settled outside it, she did not lose her ketubah so long as he had not divorced her. But in an ordinary relocation case, the moment she refused his legitimate request she failed to fulfill her duty and lost the ketubah. His later return to his former place does not restore it.
