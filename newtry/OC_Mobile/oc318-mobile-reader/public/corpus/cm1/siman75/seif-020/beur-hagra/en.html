He claimed, thinking, etc. As stated in Shevuot 42a, 'Can you say,' etc., where the Gemara distinguishes a case in which he did not say so. All the more so where he claims against the heirs, for the Sages exempt them there even from a definite claim.
But if he claimed with certainty, etc. This follows Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov there, according to Tosafot and Rif, where an adult claimant makes the claim. Rif proves it from 47a, where Rava says that Rabbi Eliezer's view is more reasonable. Since two Tannaim dispute and the sugya follows one of them, this is treated as a reasoned halachic determination. See Rif there.
