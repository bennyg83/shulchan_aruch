See Shach, who concludes that where the claimant says to the heir, "You know," and the heir admits part of the claim, the heir is obligated to take a Torah oath that he does not know. This contradicts Shach's own statement in seif katan 57 to the contrary. The primary ruling is as Shach concludes in seif katan 56, that the heir is exempt from a Torah oath. See there.
