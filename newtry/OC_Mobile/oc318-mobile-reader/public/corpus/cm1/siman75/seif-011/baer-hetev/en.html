Definitely. The Sema says that the word "definitely" is essential. The Shach disagrees and thinks it is not literal. Mordechai proves the rule from one who was sued for wheat and admitted barley, where he is exempt; Rashi explains that the plaintiff effectively waived the barley by claiming only wheat. The Rama's responsum 33 likewise indicates this. The Taz agrees with the Shach against the Sema.
Waived. The Sema derives from the Tur that even if the plaintiff later says, "I miscalculated at first; after checking I now see that you owe me," he is not believed, because the earlier omission is treated like an express waiver of what was owed and cannot be retracted. The Bach disagrees. The responsum of Maharam cited by the Tur and Hagahot Ashri appears to support the Sema. See also Responsa Ramban 117, Mabit II in the second group of questions 111, and Maharshdam 328. Shach.
