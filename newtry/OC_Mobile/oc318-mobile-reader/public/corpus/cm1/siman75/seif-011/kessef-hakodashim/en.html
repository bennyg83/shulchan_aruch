See Shach, seif katan 26. The Shach cites Responsa Rivash, siman 392, in the name of Ramban, that seizure does not help where one is obligated only to satisfy a heavenly obligation. In my opinion, according to our ruling that where one makes a definite claim, seizure after the doubt arose can be effective even without permission, as explained in Tekfo Kohen and Urim VeTumim, the same should apply where a lender claims with certainty, 'I lent you,' against a borrower who says, 'I do not know whether I borrowed.' This differs from Rivash, who follows his own view that such seizure is ineffective.
