He explains there, for example, where the defendant admits part but the amount denied is less than two silver ma'ah; or the claim lacks a matter defined by measure or weight; or the admission is not of the same kind as the claim; or he says, "here it is." The same applies to those matters regarding which one does not take a Torah oath, such as servants, documents, and land. Likewise regarding an oath imposed by one witness, where two witnesses would not obligate him to pay money except through an oath of the claimant.
