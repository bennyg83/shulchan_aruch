[Shach, letter 47] And one should not ask that the Tur wrote. Note: I do not understand the beginning of the objection. There he is not believed to say, “I paid,” except by virtue of a migo that he could have said, “I never hired you.” If so, he must swear just as he would have had to swear had he claimed, “I never hired you,” for in that case he would certainly have been obligated in a Torah oath, like one who claims, “I never borrowed,” against one witness. This is straightforward.
