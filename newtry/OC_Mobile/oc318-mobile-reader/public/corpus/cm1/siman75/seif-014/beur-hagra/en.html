(Anthology) We do not say, etc. See Tosafot to Ketubot 18b, s.v. 'and regarding all of it': with a rabbinic oath, etc., as implied in the first chapter of Bava Metzia, etc. This means the statement, 'an enactment upon an enactment,' etc.; he is exempt, and we do not say he must pay. Yet there, s.v. 'and an enactment,' Tosafot explain according to Reish Lakish, as in s.v. 'the opposing party,' that with a disqualified person we do not apply the rule because we do not permit him to take the oath, even regarding a Torah oath. Hagahot Ashri combines the two Tosafot, and this requires study. In truth, the rule is clear from 'You have one hundred of mine and the other says, I do not know,' where he is exempt. We do not say he must pay as in a case of fifty known and fifty unknown. Rav Nachman, who holds that he is exempt, nevertheless accepts the hesset oath.
