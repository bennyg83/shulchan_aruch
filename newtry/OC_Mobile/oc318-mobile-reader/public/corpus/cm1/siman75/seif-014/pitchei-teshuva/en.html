He claimed, “You seized it.” This is a case of one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear, and therefore must pay. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger: If he has a supporting witness that the item seized was in fact his own, see Ketzot HaChoshen below, siman 87, seif katan 9, who cites Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Metzia that such a supporting witness is effective.
