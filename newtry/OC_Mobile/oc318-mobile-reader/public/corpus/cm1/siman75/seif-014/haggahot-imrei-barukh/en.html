(Shach, seif katan 34) Although he has a migo. Note: Meaning that if he had denied the very occurrence to which the witness testifies, he would be exempt from the oath, according to the view that a migo may exempt from an oath. If so, there would be no possible case here in which a Torah oath could be imposed, so why do we say that one who is obligated to swear but cannot do so must pay?
