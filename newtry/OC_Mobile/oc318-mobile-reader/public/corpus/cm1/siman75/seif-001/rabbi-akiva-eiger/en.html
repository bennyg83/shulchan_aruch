[Sema, subparagraph 7] And at the end of siman 84 he also wrote. Note: If the defendant says, "I do not know whether I ever became obligated," and one witness supports him by testifying that he did not borrow, then according to Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 5 of Hilchos She’eilah, it would appear that the witness does not exempt him where the defendant himself does not make a definite claim. However, the Ramban in Milchamos, chapter 1 of Bava Metzia, and the Rosh there indicate that no such distinction exists.
