They differ in their language. Although Yehudah, Galil, and Ever HaYarden presumably all spoke the holy language, regional variations could still exist, as Eruvin says that Galileans were not precise in their speech. Darkei Moshe writes: if Silesia, the Rhine region, and Franconia are considered different lands, how can Eretz Yisrael, which measured four hundred by four hundred parsah, have contained only three lands? Accordingly, the classification cannot depend on linguistic difference alone. Silesia and the Rhine region presumably have some difference in speech, yet territorial scale also matters. In practice, what counts today as another land requires study. Rivash, siman 177, writes that a husband cannot compel his wife to move to another kingdom, but within the same kingdom relocation may be compelled.
