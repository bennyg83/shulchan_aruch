[Shulchan Aruch] Therefore we collect. Note: Urim VeTumim, in the laws of seizure, letter 124 at the end, writes that one cannot plead, "I follow the Raavad," who holds that an oral loan is not collected from a deathbed gift. Perhaps, however, regarding movable property given as a deathbed gift, one may say, "I follow that view," because in such a case the enactment of the Geonim may not have been made. See Even HaEzer, siman 53, seif 20, in the gloss, and Beit Meir there.
