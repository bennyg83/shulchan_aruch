Seif 1. I was asked by the community of Konymia about a testament stating that the testator owned a residence worth four hundred reichsthalers, from which a certain amount was to be given to one person and "after them" another amount to another person. The house was ultimately sold for less than that valuation. The questioner compared this to siman 253, seif 9, where the last recipient bears the loss. Another person objected based on the Rashba cited here in seif 11. I answered that the questioner ruled correctly. The matter depends upon the wording and order of responsibility. Since the testator said "after them," the later recipient receives only what the earlier recipients leave. The ruling is therefore correct and straightforward.
