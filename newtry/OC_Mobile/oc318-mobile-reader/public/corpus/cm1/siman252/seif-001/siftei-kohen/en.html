[Ps 119:105; 119:105; 119:130]
Under the ketubah, etc. The Rivash writes in responsum 247 that a widow who made an act of acquisition in favor of a dangerously ill person to uphold his will concerning his property nevertheless collects her ketubah first, and only afterward must she fulfill the will from the remaining property. These words are cited by the Darchei Moshe and the Sema, seif katan 3. See the Rivash for his reasoning. The Rama's words in Even HaEzer, siman 106, end of seif 2, are an abbreviated copy of the Rivash. See there.
