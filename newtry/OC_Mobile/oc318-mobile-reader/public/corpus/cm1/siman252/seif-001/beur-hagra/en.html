A gift, etc. Bava Batra 135b, 137b, 152a, and Bava Metzia 19a.
Whether, etc. Land, 137a, "and he concedes, etc.; what is the reason, etc." Movable property, 135b, "write and give, etc."
Therefore. 133a: "now, etc."
Even, etc. Meaning, even debts that cannot be collected from a gift made by a healthy person, as stated at the beginning of chapter Af Al Pi and the beginning of chapter HaNizakin. In Gittin 50b it is resolved that a gift is also called encumbered property. Although the Gemara deflects that proof, it is merely a rejection, as the Rif writes there. See Rashbam in Bava Batra there, s.v. "ve'im lav, etc.," and Tosafos to Kesubos 49b, s.v. "hu, etc."
For indeed, etc. As stated there 137a.
Specifically, etc. As stated in Gittin 50b, which calls inherited property "encumbered property."
And one who gave, etc.; some say, etc. See Even HaEzer 106:2 in the gloss and Beis Shmuel there.
