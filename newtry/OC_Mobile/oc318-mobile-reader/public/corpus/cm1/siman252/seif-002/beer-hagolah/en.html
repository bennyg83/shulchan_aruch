Tur, seif 2, at the end of chapter 6 of Ketubot, 69b, ruling like Rav Nachman that the halachah follows Rabbi Meir, who says so. In the first chapter of Gittin, 14a and 15a, it appears that the same principle applies even to a healthy person. Tosafot and the Ran write so there. See Meisharim, netiv 24, where three distinctions are set out between the mitzvah to fulfill the words of the deceased and the rule that the words of a dying person are regarded as written and delivered. The Rivash, siman 247, writes that a widow who accepted a kinyan from a dying person to fulfill his testament from his estate nevertheless first collects her ketubah, and only afterward must she fulfill the testament from the remaining property. Sema in the name of the Darkhei Moshe.
