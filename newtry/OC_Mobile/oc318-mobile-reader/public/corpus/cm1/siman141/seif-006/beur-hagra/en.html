If he spent upon it. Kesubos 79 and the chapter concerning one who spends on his wife's property: if he spent and did not consume produce... The Gemara distinguishes where he consumed produce rather than capital. Rabbi Yehudah's ruling there compares this directly to consumption of produce. Therefore the same applies here to sowing a kor and consuming the field's produce. But if the expenditure came from elsewhere and the field produced more, the case resembles the Mishnah's law of spending much and consuming little.
