The language of Rambam there, law 17. Tur likewise writes in seif 21 from Rav Pappa's statement there on 36a. The phrase "half the land" is not literal; it means the land needed beneath and between the trees. Tur likewise writes in the name of Rashbam's explanation. Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam understands the sugya as serving two subjects, one concerning seller and buyer and the other concerning three-year chazakah. Tosafot likewise write so, and Rashba agrees, although they differ in explaining "half the land," as Tosafot discuss.
