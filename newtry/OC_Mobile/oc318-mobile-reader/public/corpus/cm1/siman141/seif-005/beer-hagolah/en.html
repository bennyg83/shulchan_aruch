Maggid Mishneh writes that this is what Rav Nachman means there on 36a when he says that opening and preparing the land does not constitute chazakah. Rav Hai Gaon likewise explains it in Sefer HaMekach VeHaMimkar. There are other explanations, but this is the principal one.
Meaning, he opened and plowed the land and then leveled it after plowing with an implement, as villagers do. This usage appears in Isaiah chapter 28.
