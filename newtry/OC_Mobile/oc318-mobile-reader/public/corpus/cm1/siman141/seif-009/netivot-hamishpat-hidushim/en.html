Some disagree. From the Mechaber's wording it appears that they disagree only regarding plowing, since at least he also consumed produce during that year. But regarding rebuilding a ruin, where he derived no benefit during that year, they agree that it does not establish chazakah. It nevertheless appears that they disagree there as well. Even if he rebuilt until close to the end of three years and then resided there only briefly, they hold that this can establish chazakah, similar to the law of tending bushes in seif 17. Sema.
