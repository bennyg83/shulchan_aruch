They divide. From the arrangement of the Mechaber's and Rama's words it appears that the dissenting opinion disagrees only in this case, because plowing, sowing, and eating are all one Mishnah. In the preceding seif, however, all agree that since he did not benefit during the entire year in which he built, that year does not count toward the years of chazakah. Nevertheless, it appears to me that the dissenting opinion disagrees there as well, and even if he built until near the end of the three years, they hold that it is a chazakah, since he is building in order to sit and dwell in that building immediately upon completion, similar to the case of the shuch fig trees in seif 16.
