If he built. This is like plowing, concerning which they said that each act of improvement counts, following Rabbi's view.
Or one year. As stated there concerning plowing: he plowed one year and sowed for two years.
And likewise if he improved. This is Rav Hai Gaon's explanation of 'opening the land,' cited by Shulchan Aruch in seif 5.
And likewise if he sowed it. This is Rashbam's explanation in the name of Rabbenu Chananel of 'opening the land.'
