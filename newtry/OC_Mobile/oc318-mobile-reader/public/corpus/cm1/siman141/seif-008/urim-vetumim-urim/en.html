If he built, etc. Sema says no authority disputes this. That assumption led him to question Tur's citation of Ramban. See Tumim. Tur was correct. Ramban explicitly records a dispute on this very rule. Had Sema seen that passage, he would not have said the matter was unanimous. See there at length.
Irrigation of the water. Sema says Mechaber already stated a similar rule earlier. See Tumim. The point here is different: he performs the irrigation in one year in order to enable planting and growth in the following year, and repeats the process in the second and third years. Rama therefore adds something beyond Mechaber's earlier rule. See there.
