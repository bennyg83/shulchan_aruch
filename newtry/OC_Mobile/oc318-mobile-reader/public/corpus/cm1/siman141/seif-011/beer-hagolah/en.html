Maggid Mishneh writes that Rav Yehudah said there: if he consumed produce of orlah, this constitutes chazakah; a baraita likewise teaches that if he consumed orlah produce, etc., it constitutes chazakah. This is the version of the Geonim and the Halachot. Tosafot likewise write so in the name of Rabbeinu Tam.
Raavad in his gloss follows what Maggid Mishneh cites in the name of some commentators. Regarding kilayim they were forced to explain that he sowed between the vines and the fruit increased while the branches did not. Tosafot likewise write there and bring proof from the incident of Rav Yehudah involving bundles of branches, Ketubot 80a.
