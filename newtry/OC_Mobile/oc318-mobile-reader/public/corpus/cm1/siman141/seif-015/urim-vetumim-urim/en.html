It drops its fruit, etc. Tur cites Rosh in the name of Rav Hai Gaon that this is not a presumption where the tree sheds its fruit by itself rather than the possessor gathering it. Beit Yosef sought to align this with Rabbeinu Chananel, who explained the case as gathering fruit before ripening. Sema correctly notes they are not the same: according to Rav Hai, even fully ripe fruit does not establish possession if it merely falls by itself and was not actively harvested. See Tumim. Mechaber's unqualified adoption requires study. Rabbeinu Chananel and Ramban disagree, and under Ramban's reading the law can be the opposite. There is at least a legal doubt. I also noted tension with Mechaber's earlier rule that three harvests can suffice. The matter requires study.
