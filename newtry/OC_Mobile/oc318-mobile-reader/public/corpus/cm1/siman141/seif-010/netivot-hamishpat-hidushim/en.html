In the third year. Regarding unripe grain, the Sema explains this according to Rama's opinion in seif 1 requiring three full years from day to day. The case is one where he consumed three complete crops and also unripe grain at the end of the three year period. For example, possession began in Tammuz while the crop was still unripe, and he consumed the fully ripened crop in Elul two months later. In each of the next two years he consumed a full crop in Elul, thereby consuming three full crops in two years and two months, and then in Sivan of the third year he consumed unripe grain. The novelty is that he need not wait to consume every crop that grows during the full three years in its fully ripened state. The Taz explains differently: both at the beginning and at the end he ate only unripe grain, while in the middle he ate two full crops. Even so, chazakah is established, because once the third year was complete there was no reason for the challenger to wait longer before protesting.
