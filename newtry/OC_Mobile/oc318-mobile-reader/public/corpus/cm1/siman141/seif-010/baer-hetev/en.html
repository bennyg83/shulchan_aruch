In the third year. This implies that in the first two years he did not consume the crop as fodder, and therefore it is a chazakah. The case is where he already consumed three fully ripened crops, and at the end, when the three years were concluding, he consumed a crop as fodder. The ruling teaches that one should not think he must specifically consume every crop that grows during the three years only after full ripening. This differs from the first clause, which concerns a case in which he consumed at least one of the first three years as fodder, and therefore there is no chazakah. Thus writes the Sema. The Taz challenges his explanation and interprets the case differently. See there.
