If he consumed the crop as fodder before it reached one-third of its growth, he did not establish presumptive ownership. If the local practice is to sow for fodder because it commands a high price, this does establish presumptive ownership. {Rama: Similarly, if the land came into his possession while the crop was fodder, then upon reaching the third anniversary from day to day he establishes presumptive ownership even though in the third year he harvests it only as fodder.}
