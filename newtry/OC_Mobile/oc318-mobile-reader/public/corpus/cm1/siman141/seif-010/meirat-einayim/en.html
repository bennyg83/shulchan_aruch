Because his blood is precious and the tongue of the column and the places they have innumerable, and your way to reap the rains that you have a strong flame:
Even though in the third year he consumes only fodder. The Tur uses this exact language. His precise formulation implies that during the first two years he did not consume the crop as fodder. This is why presumptive possession exists only in such a case. The case is one where he has already consumed three complete crops, and at the end of the three years he consumes fodder. For example, suppose the field came into his possession in Tammuz while the crop was still immature fodder. He did not consume it until Rosh Chodesh Elul, when the crop had fully ripened. Thus in the first year he consumed a complete crop two months after the field came into his possession, and similarly in the second and third years, each time in Elul when the crop ripened. By the third Elul he has already consumed three full crops, yet the field has been in his possession only two years and two months. When the full three year period is completed, at the end of the following Sivan, he consumes the next crop while it is still fodder. The novelty is that we do not require every crop growing during the three years to be consumed only after full ripening. By contrast, in the first clause, where he consumed fodder also during the initial three crops or during one of those years, there is no presumptive possession. This appears to be the meaning of the Tur and Rama. According to this explanation, however, it is somewhat difficult that Rama states this without qualification, because according to the Mechaber's ruling in seif 1, three crops from three agricultural years suffice even if he did not possess the field for three full years from day to day. What novelty is then added by saying that even though he consumes only fodder in the third year it suffices? It may be that the novelty is that even according to the alternative view Rama cites there, which requires three full years from day to day, they agree in this case. The Tur also states this law because he himself requires possession for three full years from day to day.
