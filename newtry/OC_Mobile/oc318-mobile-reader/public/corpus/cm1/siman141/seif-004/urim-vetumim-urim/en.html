Some say it is a presumption. This is Rabbi Yitzchak's view in Tur. Bach writes that Tur did not fully grasp Rabbi Yitzchak's position and that Rabbi Yitzchak speaks where all local fields are regularly left fallow. See Tumim. I proved that if only some fields are left fallow and some are not, even Tur agrees with Rabbi Yitzchak. I further wrote that Mechaber's presentation requires study because Rambam appears not to accept Rabbi Yitzchak, and below in siman 145 Mechaber rules like Rambam. In practical law, Tur's position that there is no presumption appears primary.
