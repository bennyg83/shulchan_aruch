There is someone who says. D.C. does not belong to nor does it belong here, nor is it useful:
And there is one who says. This follows the discussion on Bava Batra 28b concerning land that lies fallow. Rav Huna's admission is explained where local practice is to leave fields fallow. There, consecutive years certainly establish possession, as the Gemara says he should have sown the land. The first opinion infers from the questioner that the case concerned a place where everyone leaves fields fallow.
