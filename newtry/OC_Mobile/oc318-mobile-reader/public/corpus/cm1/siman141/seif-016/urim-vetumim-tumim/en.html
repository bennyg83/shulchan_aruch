The Mechaber should not have stated the rule without qualification, for the Tur's words clearly indicate that this law depends on the earlier law of plowing, namely whether those days count toward the three years. The Rosh, who accepts this law regarding the white fig, also holds that plowing counts toward the three years. The Mechaber, however, brought as the primary ruling above in seif 9 that plowing does not count, as also appears from his wording in seif 8 and as I wrote there. He therefore should not have cited the law of the white fig, for according to his approach three actual harvests are required. This is explicit in the Tur and requires study.
