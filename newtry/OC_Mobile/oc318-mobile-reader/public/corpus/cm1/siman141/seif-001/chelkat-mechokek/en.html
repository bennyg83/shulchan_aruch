Because a minor cannot appoint an agent. I found written in the handwriting of my eminent grandfather, Rabbi Yonah, in the margin of a get procedure: We agreed that this is not an actual requirement, but merely an additional precaution. Whichever way one looks at it, if he is truly a minor, his kiddushin were not kiddushin in the first place. It also appears to me that this applies specifically where he is visibly young. We do not require that he have reached the majority of his years; it is only a concern for appearances where he is young and looks like a minor.
