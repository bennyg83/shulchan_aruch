And olive presses. They too are considered to produce continuously, because some people store olives throughout the year and press them gradually in small amounts. So write Tosafot in Bava Batra. See Sema.
That walk. This excludes very young slaves who do not walk, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the end of siman 135. The text also specifies a slave in order to exclude an animal, for which there is no chazakah even after three years, as the Mechaber writes there. See Sema. The Sema above in siman 135 likewise distinguishes between a slave and an animal. The Shach there disagrees and holds there is no distinction, as the Mechaber writes in siman 72, seif 21. See what I wrote there at length.
Properly spaced. This is the Rambam's wording. The Tur writes, 'even if they are planted less than four amot apart,' and the Mechaber says the same in seif 18 below. That is why he repeats it there. See Sema.
