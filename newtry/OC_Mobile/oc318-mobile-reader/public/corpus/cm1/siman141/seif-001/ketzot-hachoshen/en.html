These are like three years. Knesset HaGedolah writes in the name of Maharshal that according to Rashbam the halacha follows Rav, who requires complete years. This is surprising, for where in Rashbam do we find that the halacha follows Rav and requires three complete years from day to day? Rashbam wrote only that according to this formulation even Shmuel does not reduce the period below three years, and this is how we rule. That language can be understood to mean that we rule like Shmuel. Moreover, even that language is not Rashbam's but Rabbeinu Chananel's. Rashbam may still hold that the halacha follows Shmuel, so that three harvests create a chazakah even in less than three years. Maharshal says that he found no one who rules like Rav except what he himself wrote in explanation of Rashbam, and he already rejected that reading.
Yet I am astonished, because Rashbam writes explicitly, s.v. 'the words of Rabbi Akiva and Rabbi Yishmael,' that the Sages say their chazakah is three years from day to day, and that the opening sugya explains the reason for three years as, 'until three years a person is careful with his document, afterward he is no longer careful.' Rashbam then says that we rule like these Sages according to Rav, as is demonstrated by the sugya, which does not follow the gathering of three fruits from three years but requires three complete years in every case, because a person does not preserve his document for more than three years. Ramban likewise writes in his novellae that the statement, 'until three years a person is careful with his document,' compelled Rashbam to rule like Rav. See there.
