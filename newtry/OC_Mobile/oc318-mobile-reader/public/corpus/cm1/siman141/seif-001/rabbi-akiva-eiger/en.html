[Shulchan Aruch] That it is not a chazakah. Note: According to what the Sema writes below in siman 145, subparagraphs 2 and 3, that the Rambam there speaks of a place where fields are ordinarily left fallow and the witnesses do not contradict one another, it appears to me from the fact that there is nevertheless no chazakah, even though according to each witness separately there would be a chazakah, that the Rambam follows the Tur’s view that in a place where fields are left fallow, continuous eating of the produce does not establish chazakah. Once the testimony of both witnesses is combined, it emerges that he consumed the produce continuously, and therefore no chazakah is established.
