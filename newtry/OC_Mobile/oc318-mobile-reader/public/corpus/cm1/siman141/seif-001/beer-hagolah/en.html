The language of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 12 of Hilkhot To'en. Maggid Mishneh cites the Mishnah there, "The chazakah of houses," etc., Bava Batra 28a. Some of the cases Rambam lists are explicit in the Mishnah and Gemara on 29a, and others follow from the general rule that anything that produces fruit continuously acquires chazakah in three years without requiring full day-to-day occupation.
There, as Shmuel says: this is the view of Rabbi Yishmael and Rabbi Akiva, but the Sages say, etc.; we rule like Shmuel according to the Sages.
