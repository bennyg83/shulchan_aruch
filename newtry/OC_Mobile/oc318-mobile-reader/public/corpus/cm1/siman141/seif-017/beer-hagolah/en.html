The language of Rambam, Hilkhot To'en chapter 12, law 19. The Maggid Mishneh cites Abaye's statement there, 36b: from Rabbi Yishmael we may infer, etc.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that this law is learned from Rabbi Yishmael's rule regarding an orchard, where one gathers the grain, etc. The grain is certainly scattered throughout the field, as are the olives and summer fruit, for this is the usual manner of cultivation. Each type therefore establishes possession for the others. The same applies to these trees, specifically where they are scattered. This is Rambam's view.
There is a page for the addition of the appendix, named after a report, such as the girls who have been abandoned until their summer and ten years, who had eaten in the first time, had been vaccinated for years and a second, and of the year, Genoto, the first year that started to hold and as the column:
The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara there says, 'provided the people plundered the rest.' Rambam explains this literally, that the populace took the other produce. Raavad explains the expression as meaning scattered, that he ate three trees here and three there. Rashbam, Tosafot, and Rosh likewise explain that each year he ate from three trees scattered through each beit se'ah. Tur writes this as well. I do not understand why Ramah did not gloss upon Rambam here.
