Shintoy L. Illines to the house of Saha as a captain and see the C.T. column if he planted a number of old illances inside the Jasein house and ate them scattered J every year:
He wanted more than anyone to go to Sa's house. He did not eat all of them each year, but ten and another
Unless they were within four cubits. The Tur writes in the name of the Ramah that then they are regarded as uprooted. Although if he consumed all of them continuously throughout the full three years there would be presumptive possession, as the Mechaber writes in the following seif, here, where he consumed only ten of them each year, there is presumptive possession only when the trees are viable. In the Perishah I wrote that 'within four cubits' means that there are not four cubits between one tree and another, not, as the Ir Shushan writes, that all ten trees stand within one area of four cubits.
