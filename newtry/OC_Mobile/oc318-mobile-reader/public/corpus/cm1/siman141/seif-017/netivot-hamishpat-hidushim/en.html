He established chazakah only in what he consumed. According to the Sema, this means that he keeps the produce he already harvested and consumed, but may not take produce of future years because he did not establish chazakah over it. The Shach disagrees and holds that where he has no chazakah, both land and produce must be restored, including the produce already consumed. The Mechaber's phrase means instead that if he ate from ten trees in one place for three consecutive years, he establishes chazakah only in those trees from which he actually ate, not the rest. In the Perishah the Sema explains that if in the third year he ate from trees whose fruit ripens only once in three years, chazakah can arise in that manner as explained in seif 16, unless he never locked or fenced the land.
