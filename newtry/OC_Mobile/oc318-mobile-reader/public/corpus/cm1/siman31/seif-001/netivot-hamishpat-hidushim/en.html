If there are three witnesses and they contradict one another in those interrogations, for example two say the event was in Nisan and the third says it was in Iyar, all testimony concerning that event is invalid. Nevertheless, only the third witness, who is contradicted by two, is disqualified from other testimony. Where they contradict one another only in secondary examinations, both remain qualified. If there are four witnesses and they agree on part of the testimony but contradict one another on another part, for example in the case in siman 146 where all testify that the property belonged to the ancestors of one party but they contradict one another regarding years of possession, the testimony regarding ancestral ownership remains valid.
