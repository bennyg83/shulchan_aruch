The language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 22 of Hilkhot Edut, from the dispute between Rav Huna and Rav Chisda, Shevuot 46b. As Rashi and the Rif explain there, they do not disagree regarding a single witness, etc.
A man says he killed and one says he did not kill
There, in accordance with Rav Huna, who was Rav Chisda's teacher, as Tosafot write there. So is the consensus of the poskim.
