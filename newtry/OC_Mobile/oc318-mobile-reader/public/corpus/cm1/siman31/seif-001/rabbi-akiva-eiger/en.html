[Shulchan Aruch] Two sets of witnesses. Note: If a beit din acted on the basis of a litigant's admission before them, and afterward two witnesses testified before another beit din that he had not admitted before the first beit din, this is not treated as two contradictory sets of witnesses. On the contrary, the later witnesses are disqualified. See Responsa Tashbetz, part 1, siman 1, p. 6b, where he discusses those who contradict them. See Responsa Maharibal, book 2, siman 8; Responsa Chavot Yair, siman 159; and what I wrote in the margin below at the beginning of siman 91. If the members of the second set are relatives of the first set, see Responsa Maharibal, siman 25, seif 3.
