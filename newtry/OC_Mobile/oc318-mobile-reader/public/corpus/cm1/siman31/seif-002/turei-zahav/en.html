(There: that he should burn the small one.) I find it difficult why the Sages gave advice in this case. How is it different from siman 248, where one who advises another to sell property is called wicked because he thereby deprives the second party of money? One may answer that there, the sale itself is forbidden even according to the seller's own knowledge, since he may not sell it initially. Here, however, according to the lender's own understanding that the debt is truly owed to him, there is no prohibition.
