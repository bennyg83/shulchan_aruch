However, if judgment was already rendered, etc. Shach rules that even before final judgment the witness cannot later testify because a person tends to maintain his prior statement. Urim VeTumim asks from cases where testimony given improperly outside a litigant's presence can later be repeated, and where testimony initially given together with a relative or disqualified witness can sometimes be given anew. The distinction is that where the first statement had no legal force and the speaker knew it had none, a later formal statement need not be suspected as merely preserving a prior position. But where the witness believed his original statement would be relied upon by beit din, or he did not know his own disqualification, he has a reputational incentive to maintain what he said. That is the concern of 'a person maintains his statement.'
