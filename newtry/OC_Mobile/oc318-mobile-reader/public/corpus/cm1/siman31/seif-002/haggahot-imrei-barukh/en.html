(Sema, seif katan 2) To swear against him a Torah oath, etc. Note: See Penei Yehoshua, part 4, Makkot 6a, on Tosafos, s.v. 'Rabbi Yose says,' etc. Know also that according to one answer of Tosafos, Sanhedrin 31a, s.v. 'Beit Shammai,' we do not say that if one of a group of witnesses is found disqualified the entire testimony is void unless the disqualification was known. If so, here too the two witnesses should not be invalidated through that rule, and an oath should be imposed on the basis of one of them who is certainly valid. Even according to Rashba this requires study where the two did not testify together within the time of immediate speech.
