Each one shall take a hesset oath and be exempt. The reason is that beit din sees in the hands of one lender these two documents together and knows that one of them is invalid. Each borrower makes a definite claim that he owes nothing and that the document produced against him is the invalid one.
He collects from unencumbered property. Meaning, but not from encumbered property. Since the document has no full legal status, for had the debtor denied the debt he could not have collected with it, the debtor's admission cannot prejudice others.
