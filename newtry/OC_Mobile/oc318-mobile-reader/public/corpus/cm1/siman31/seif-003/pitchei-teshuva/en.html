One of them is false. See Baer Heitev in the name of the Sema, who writes that where the testimony is void, one need not take a Torah oath against the witness. The Sema concludes that this follows Rashba's responsum cited by the Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe in siman 36. Marot HaTzoveot on Even HaEzer siman 17, seif 70, challenges this from Tosafot in Sanhedrin 31, who indicate that where a witness's disqualification is not known, his participation does not invalidate all the testimony. Rashba there is no proof, because he discusses a known disqualification. He leaves the matter requiring study. As to Baer Heitev's citation of the Shach that the same applies with two witnesses, Dagul MeRevavah refers to Beit Shmuel on Even HaEzer siman 115, seif katan 28, and writes that upon close analysis a distinction can be drawn. See also Shevut Yaakov, volume 1, siman 79; Shemen Rokeach, volume 1, siman 72; and Divrei Emet, kuntres Trei uTrei, siman 3.
