[Shulchan Aruch] This set comes by itself. Note: If afterward both sets testify together concerning some matter, according to the Sema, seif katan 2, it appears that this falls under the rule that if one of a group of witnesses is found to be a relative or otherwise disqualified, the entire testimony is invalid. Tosafot in Sanhedrin, however, write according to one answer that it is not comparable to a case where one is found to be a relative or disqualified, since the disqualified witness is not clearly and definitively identified.
