(In the name of Maharshal in the Beit Yosef, s.v. It is written in Meisharim, Netiv 2, part 4, etc.) See seif 5. The explanation is somewhat as follows. Rav Nachman states in Perek Chezkas Habatim: although the witnesses were contradicted regarding the eating of the produce, they were not contradicted regarding ancestral ownership. We rule in accordance with him, as explained below in siman 146.
