(Further there: a creditor who holds a claim against a borrower, etc., through: even according to the second formulation of Rabbi Natan, etc.) Meaning, even according to the second formulation, which requires a monetary basis specifically for a rabbinic oath, that requirement applies where the original claim is for money. Here, however, his initial demand is that the other party produce the document, not that he pay money. Perhaps even the second formulation concedes that no prior monetary basis is required. The Beit Yosef did not understand it this way, and according to his understanding of the Rosh, the explanation requires study.
