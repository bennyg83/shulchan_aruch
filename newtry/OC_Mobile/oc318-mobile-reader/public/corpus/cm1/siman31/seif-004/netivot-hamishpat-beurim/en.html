Unless the witnesses to the document themselves came. See Shach, seif katan 4, where there is a scribal error. It should read that the case is one where they remember the loan itself. The novelty is that one might have thought that since they do not testify that they remember signing the document, but only that they remember the loan, perhaps we should be concerned that the witnesses are mistaken and merely think these are their signatures, so that the document should have only the status of an oral loan. The ruling therefore teaches that whenever they remember the loan, and so forth, the document is valid as explained there.
