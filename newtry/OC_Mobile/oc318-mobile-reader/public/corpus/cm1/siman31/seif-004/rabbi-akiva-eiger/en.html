[Shulchan Aruch] We accept each one of them. Note: If the second set contradicted the first only after the first set had died or was overseas, there is room to rule that later testimony of the first set, given after their original testimony that was contradicted, is void. We do not say here that they retain their presumptive status, because perhaps had they been before us they would have conceded to the second set. See Ketubot 20 and Tosafot, s.v. "Rather, Rav Nachman said."
