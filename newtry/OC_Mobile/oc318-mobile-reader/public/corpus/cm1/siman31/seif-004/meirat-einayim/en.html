Danny by P. D. D. D. D. The prosecutor was held to return to false witnesses to all of Israel, who were held to testify falsely, and therefore discussed by Eleon in his vision:
And they testified, etc. Rambam writes this at the end of Hilchot Edut. The Tur records it below in siman 63 and cites Ramah as disagreeing. Ramah holds that we do not rely merely upon the witnesses saying, "This is our handwriting," because perhaps the forger exerted great effort and copied it. Their identification helps only if they also remember that they actually signed that document with that handwriting. Rama cites the dissenting view in siman 63 and says nothing here, relying upon the law stated in its proper place.
