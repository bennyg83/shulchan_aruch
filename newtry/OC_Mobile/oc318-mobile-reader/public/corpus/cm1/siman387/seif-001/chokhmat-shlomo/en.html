Seif 1. 'Therefore, one who pushes his fellow's coin until it falls into the sea is liable.' Note: See Shach, seif katan 8, who cites Maharshal and disagrees with him. See my responsum in Choshen Mishpat, third edition, siman 51, where I explained the basis of the dispute between Maharshal and Shach regarding one who steals an object in order to destroy it, and whether he is considered a robber.
