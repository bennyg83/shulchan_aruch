We assess, etc. Bava Kamma 11a, and 10b concerning the source of this rule. It concerns direct human damage, like theft and robbery, and the Gemara's incident where an object was broken.
(Collected note) We assess, etc. Tosefta chapter 3 gives the general rule: if one killed another's ox, tore his garment, or cut his plantings, the injured party cannot insist on receiving a new replacement while leaving the damaged item with the tortfeasor. Rather, we assess its value before and after the damage, and the difference is paid. (End of note.)
