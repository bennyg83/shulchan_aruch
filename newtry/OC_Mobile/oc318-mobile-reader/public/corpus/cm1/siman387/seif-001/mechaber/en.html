The broken remnants are assessed for the injured party, and the damager pays the difference. It contains one seif.
When a person directly damages property, the assessment is made in the same manner as when his animal caused the damage. Thus, if he killed another person's animal or broke his utensils, beit din assesses the value of the animal before the damage and the value of the carcass afterward, or the value of the utensil while whole and its value in its broken condition. The damager pays the reduction in value, and the injured party keeps the carcass or broken utensil, as explained regarding an ox that was damaged in siman 403.
