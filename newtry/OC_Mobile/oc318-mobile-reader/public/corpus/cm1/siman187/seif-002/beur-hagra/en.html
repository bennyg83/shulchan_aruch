(Supplement) From money that was, etc. This is stated because of the concluding clause, that concerning a concealed matter he swears only in such a case. A custodian's oath serves only to exempt him from payment, not to enable him to collect, as stated at the beginning of the chapter Kol HaNishba'in: all Torah oaths, etc., and as derived from, 'Its owner shall accept,' etc. The same applies to rabbinic oaths except for those specifically enumerated there. See above, siman 93, seif 13. A person may collect only with proof. (End of supplement.)
