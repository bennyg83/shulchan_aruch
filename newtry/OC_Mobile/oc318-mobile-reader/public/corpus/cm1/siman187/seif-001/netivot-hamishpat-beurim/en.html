In a place where witnesses can be brought. This is not limited to a case where it is independently known that the event occurred in a place with witnesses. Even if the custodian himself admits that he was in a place where people could see, he must bring proof. He is not believed through a migo that he could have said the event occurred where there were no witnesses, because this is a migo against witnesses. This is contrary to Maharit in responsa. Although with payment or return of an object one is believed even where witnesses were present, showing that the matter is not literally treated as though witnesses already testify against him, there is a different rationale in payment cases, as I will explain below.
