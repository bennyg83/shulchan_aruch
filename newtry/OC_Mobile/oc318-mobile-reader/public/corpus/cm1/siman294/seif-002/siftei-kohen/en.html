He must swear that matters are as he says. He must swear that the item is no longer in his possession because it was stolen or lost. This oath is of Torah law, since by it he exempts himself from payment. This is explicit in the Gemara in Perek HaMafkid and in the authorities.
That he was not negligent in guarding it. Rabbeinu Yerucham, cited by the later authorities, writes that we require him to swear that he was not negligent. It is insufficient for him merely to swear that the loss was unintentional, because such wording may itself imply that he admits carelessness and failed to guard properly.
