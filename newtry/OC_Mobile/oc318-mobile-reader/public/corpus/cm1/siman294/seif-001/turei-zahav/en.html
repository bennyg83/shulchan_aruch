His words are correct, as is evident from siman 58, seif 2, where if the creditor says, "There never was such a matter," he is established as a denier. We do not interpret his words as meaning merely, "It is untrue that you repaid me; rather, you gave it to me as a gift." The same applies here. Although in siman 67 the Tur and Mechaber speak of a migo that he could have said, "There never was such a matter," there the case concerns witnesses who saw the item in his possession. His claim means: "Your statement that these objects are held by me as a deposit is untrue; rather, they are collateral in my possession." One must distinguish that there the phrase "there never was such a matter" is the terminology of the decisors, meaning that the custodian may deny the essential distinction between their claims. In siman 58, by contrast, where the litigant himself says, "There never was such a matter," he certainly denies the claim entirely.
