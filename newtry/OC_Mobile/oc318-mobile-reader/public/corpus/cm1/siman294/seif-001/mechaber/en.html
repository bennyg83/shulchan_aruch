One who denies a deposit or claims it was stolen. It contains six seifim.
Once the depositor demands the deposit and the bailee denies it, the bailee immediately becomes a robber, is liable even for unavoidable accidents, and is disqualified from testimony and oaths. This applies when witnesses establish that the deposit was still in his possession when he denied it. Without such witnesses, he may have lost it and denied temporarily only to delay until he could find and return it.
