If a deposit was stolen through unavoidable circumstances and the thief was later identified, both a paid and unpaid bailee must litigate against the thief rather than take an oath. If the bailee already swore and the thief was then identified, an unpaid bailee may either rely on his oath or pursue the thief; a paid bailee must pursue him. Rama: Reuven procured a secure debt for Shimon and received a brokerage fee. If the debt later deteriorates and effort with the ruler is required to collect it, some require Reuven to perform that effort because he received compensation, analogous to the identified thief.
