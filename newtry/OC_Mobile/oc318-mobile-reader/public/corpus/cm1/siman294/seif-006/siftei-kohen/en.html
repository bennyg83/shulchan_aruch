The deposit was stolen, etc. The Sema explains that the Mechaber mentions unavoidable accident even regarding an unpaid custodian because in an unavoidable robbery there is no double payment recoverable from the armed robber. This overlooks the Gemara, which discusses double payment in circumstances of armed robbery as well. The precise distinction follows the Gemara and the authorities: the owner's transfer of the prospective double payment depends upon the custodian's undertaking to pay, not upon whether the initial event is classified as theft or unavoidable accident.
