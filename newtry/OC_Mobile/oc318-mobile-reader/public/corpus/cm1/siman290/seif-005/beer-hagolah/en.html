Lamed) This is the language of the Rambam there, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Nachalot, halachah 7, from the incident mentioned there.
Mem) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is a dispute between Rav Huna and the academy of Rabbi Sheila. Rambam understands that the dispute concerns only a guardian appointed by the orphans' father. A guardian appointed by beit din can be removed according to all opinions, because beit din must select someone trustworthy and capable of preserving the estate.
Nun) The Maggid Mishneh writes that where beit din appointed the guardian, we do not attribute losses to an unlikely accident. This is Rambam's view. The Ramban likewise writes that these words are correct and primary. The Beit Yosef cites the Nimukei Yosef that when beit din appointed him, once there are grounds to suspect that he is causing loss he is removed immediately. The Raah likewise states in the name of the Ramban that beit din or its agent must not allow an improperly administered matter to remain unresolved.
Samekh) This is written in the name of the Geonim and is explained in the Rif.
Ayin) Maharik, shoresh 53, writes that if they cannot recover the property, the guardian has been negligent even if he is otherwise an honorable and suitable person. See there at length.
