The orphans. This excludes a guardian who wastes or squanders his own property, for he is not removed on that account. It may be that he is careful with the orphans' property, since a person is willing to pay considerably in order to be known as trustworthy, as stated in the Yerushalmi. This is the wording of the Rashba. Sema. A guardian who does not invest the orphans' money profitably but leaves it idle and consumes it little by little is removed, as stated in Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 24. Knesset HaGedolah writes that plainly, if he does so for the orphans' benefit because he cannot find a profitable investment and no trustworthy person is available who will take the funds on profit, he is not removed. See Responsa Divrei Rivot, siman 120, and Rashdam, siman 432. Bnei Chayei.
