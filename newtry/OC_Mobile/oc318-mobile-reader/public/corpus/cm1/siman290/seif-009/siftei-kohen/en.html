It appears to me, however, that the Rosh disputes the Rambam and holds that even here, where loss threatens from both sides, permission of the beit din is required. His reason is that Ravina likewise obtained permission from Rav Ashi. The Rambam's reason is that since Rav Ashi permitted it, this demonstrates that Ravina acted properly, and therefore no other person is thereafter required to obtain permission. I found this stated explicitly in Responsa Rashdam, siman 46. See there and examine carefully.
