[Shulchan Aruch] Orphans. Note: See Knesset HaGedolah above, siman 128, Beit Yosef glosses, letter 21, who asks that the Mechaber appears to contradict his ruling in Yoreh Deah, siman 253, seif 5. In my humble opinion, a case in which they relied upon him is stronger, because he has the status of a guardian who supports the orphans. Terumat HaDeshen, end of siman 348, writes this explicitly. Responsa Maharam Alshech, siman 70, likewise writes that the Rashba's words concerning a case where they relied upon him follow only the Rashba's own view, but not that of the Rif, who exempts one who supports orphans. In my humble opinion this is not compelled. See Maggid Mishneh, chapter 12 of Hilchot Ishut, in the name of the Rashba, where he brings proof from orphans who sold property to support another man's wife without qualification, implying that there is no distinction. Examine this carefully.
