For a long period. See Sema, cited also in Baer HaGolah, who writes that if during that period the orphan encumbered the property, etc. Dagul MeRevavah writes that at first glance this appears to contradict Rama above, siman 235, seif 2, in the name of Ran. But one who studies Beit Yosef here sees that the will explicitly provided that during the stated period the heir could take no action concerning the property. The two rulings may therefore be reconciled. See there. See also Netivot HaMishpat on this point.
