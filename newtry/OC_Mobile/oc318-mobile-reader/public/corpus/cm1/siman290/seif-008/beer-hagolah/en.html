See there. The Raavad derived this from his interpretation of the incident of Rav Safra in Bava Batra 144a, where he was an apotropos. He writes that this interpretation is not clear, but nevertheless the law is learned from it; the Ritva likewise writes so. In the margin of my father's Shulchan Aruch I saw noted that this gloss appears to contradict siman 292, seif 19, where one who sells a deposit by authority of beit din sells it to others but not to himself. He concluded that the Tur's concern for suspicion is primary. In my view there is no difficulty. There we fear that people will suspect him of buying cheaply, whereas here it is money that he receives for investment at half profit according to what the judges deem beneficial for the orphans, as the Mechaber writes in the Beit Yosef.
