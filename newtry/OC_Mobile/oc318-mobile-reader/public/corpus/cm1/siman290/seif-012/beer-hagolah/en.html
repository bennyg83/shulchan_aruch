Kaf) This is the language of the Tur, seif 18. Rambam likewise rules there, halachah 7, from the baraita and the Gemara's explanation. The Maggid Mishneh explains Rambam: it cannot be that guardians are permitted to litigate and, if they win, the judgment stands, but if they lose, the judgment is void. Such a rule would distort justice, because the claimant could lose but could never gain. Therefore the Sages said that they may not litigate with a claimant at all in such a case. Since they were not authorized to litigate, if they nevertheless did so and were held liable, the judgment is ineffective.
