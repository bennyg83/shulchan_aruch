A baraita in Pesachim 49b states six matters concerning an am ha'aretz. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Halakhot imply that this was said only of an am ha'aretz who possesses neither Scripture, Mishnah, nor proper conduct. This is Rambam's view. Others disagree and hold that even if he has proper conduct and mitzvot, an ignoramus does not fear sin and may rationalize that he is merely taking compensation for his labor. This is Rashi's view.
