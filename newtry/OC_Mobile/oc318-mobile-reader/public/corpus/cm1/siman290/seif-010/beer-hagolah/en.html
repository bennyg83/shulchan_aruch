The wording of the Maggid Mishneh, chapter 11 of Hilkhot Nachalot, law 3, from the incident of Ravina, who held beer belonging to Ravina the orphan, etc. He asked whether to leave it lest it sour, and was told that the orphan's property is no better than his own. Thus they permitted transporting it where there was a risk of accidental loss only when there was concern that if left there it would spoil before sale. Rambam records this in law 3.
