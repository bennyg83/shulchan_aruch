Once he began attending to their affairs, he may not retract. The Tosefta states this concerning a guardian. At first this seems different from an ordinary worker, who may retract even in the middle of the day because the Jewish people are servants of Hashem and not servants of servants. I wrote in siman 293, citing Ritva, that even a custodian appointed for a fixed period can retract within the period for that reason. A guardian is different. His activity is itself a mitzvah. Once he has begun, compelling him to continue does not make him the servant of another person. He is carrying out the service of Hashem. Maggid Mishneh writes that beit din may not force a person initially to accept guardianship of orphans, because the mitzvah does not authorize forcing a person to undertake the office against his will. But once he has voluntarily begun, the court can require him not to abandon it. The doctrine of "not servants of servants" therefore does not permit retraction after commencement.
