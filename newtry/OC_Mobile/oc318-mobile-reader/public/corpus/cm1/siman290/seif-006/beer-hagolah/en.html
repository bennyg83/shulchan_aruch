Meaning, since his condition changed for the worse after the father's death, he is like an apotropos who causes loss and is removed. But if he was already in that condition during the father's lifetime and the father nevertheless appointed him, beit din does not remove him, for the father accepted this. In Rambam the text reads 'and words of suspicion.' Possibly this is a scribal error caused by interchange of similar letters.
