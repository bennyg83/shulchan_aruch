For a long period. Sema, cited by Be'er HaGolah, writes that if during the restricted period the orphan encumbers property, the result depends on the will's terms. Dagul MeRevavah notes that this appears to conflict with Rama above in siman 235, seif 2, in the name of Ran. A review of Beit Yosef here shows, however, that the will expressly provided that during the stated period the heir could make no disposition at all, and therefore the cases need not conflict. Netivot HaMishpat also discusses this point.
