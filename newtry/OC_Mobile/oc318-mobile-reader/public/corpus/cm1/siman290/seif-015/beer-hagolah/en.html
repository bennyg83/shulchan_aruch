Samekh) There, halachah 10, from the baraita. The Maggid Mishneh writes that all of these laws apply whether the guardian was appointed by the father or by beit din. Rambam and Rashba write that whenever guardians are ordinarily forbidden to perform one of these acts, they may do so if they first obtain permission from beit din. This is explicit in the Tosefta.
Ayin) This is the language of the Tur, seif 21, from the first chapter of Bava Batra 8a, the incident involving Rava and orphans.
Peh) Rambam there, halachah 11. The Maggid Mishneh cites the statement in the fourth chapter of Ketubot 48a concerning one who became mentally incompetent. Rambam equates one who became deaf with one who became mentally incompetent for this law.
