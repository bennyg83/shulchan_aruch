What buys slaves. Dovma is found to buy a tablet:
They work first for houses. The responsibility of bees is better than a slave to feel her mother:
They do not sell and leave the proceeds idle. The reason is that the money may be stolen. Another reason given by Rashi is preservation of the honor and beauty of the father's household. In the Perishah I explained that Rashi's concern that the money may be stolen refers to the period before it is given to a trustworthy person for investment. Afterward the recipient is liable at least as a paid custodian for theft and loss.

The parents are the most important. The Gemara Darbi Yossi called for a field:
To transfer the orphans' money by ma'amad sheloshtan, etc. Terumat HaDeshen explains that the effectiveness of transfer in the presence of all three parties is a special rabbinic rule without a fully sufficient conceptual basis. Therefore the Sages said, in effect, that one should not extend the enactment beyond its stated case. It applies when a person transfers his own money, not when a guardian uses it to remove the orphans' money from their legal possession. Even if the guardian intends the transfer for their benefit, removal from their possession is ordinarily disadvantageous, and the Sages did not distinguish. They left the matter under ordinary law. By contrast, when others transfer property to the orphans, that is a benefit. The orphans can acquire through their guardian, whose hand functions like theirs.
