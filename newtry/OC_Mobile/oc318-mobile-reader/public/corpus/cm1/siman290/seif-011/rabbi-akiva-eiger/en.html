[Shach, letter 25] See the responsum. Note: See Rashach, part 1, siman 64, who writes that where a person performed with his own hands an act that the Sages did not permit, such as sending orphans' property by ship over the open sea, he is considered to have caused damage directly with his own hands and is liable according to all opinions. See there. Pri Chadash, in his responsa Mayim Chaim, siman 13, writes concerning this that it is not conclusive that such conduct falls within the category of direct damage itself.
