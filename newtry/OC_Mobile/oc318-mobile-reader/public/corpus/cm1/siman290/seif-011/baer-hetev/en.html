Three. The Terumat HaDeshen explains that the acquisition of ma'amad sheloshtan, transfer in the presence of all three parties, has no fully sufficient conceptual basis for why it should work. Therefore, the Sages said in effect, 'Enough, do not extend it further.' It was enacted only for a person transferring his own money, not for a guardian to divest orphans of their property by this mechanism. Although the guardian transfers it for the orphans' benefit, since divestment is ordinarily harmful the Sages made no distinction and left the matter under the ordinary law. By contrast, where others transfer property to orphans, the transaction benefits the orphans, and therefore their guardian may acquire it for them, because his hand is like theirs. Ibid.
