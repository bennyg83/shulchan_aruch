He Who rides upon the highest heavens. In the Perishah I wrote that the verse uses this title of Hashem here in order to frighten the guardian with awareness of Hashem's exalted power and His providence over orphans. It resembles Rashi's explanation of the verse in VeZot HaBerakhah, "Who rides the heavens to your aid, and in His majesty the skies." Even if the weak person is above and the mighty below, the mighty fears the weak because of the other's advantageous position. All the more so Hashem, Who is mighty above while the weak orphan is below, watches over the orphan. Therefore the Tur, Rambam, and Mechaber cite the opening of the verse, "Extol Him Who rides upon the highest heavens," rather than bringing only the latter phrase, "Father of orphans." The continuation is, "Father of orphans and Judge of widows is God in His holy habitation."
