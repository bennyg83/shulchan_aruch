When the orphans mature, the guardian gives them the inherited property remaining in his hands and need not provide a detailed accounting of receipts and expenditures. He says, 'This is what remains,' and takes an oath while holding a sacred object that he stole nothing. This applies to a guardian appointed by beit din. A guardian appointed by the father or another testator does not take an oath merely on an uncertain claim, unless there is a definite claim or other basis for an oath.
