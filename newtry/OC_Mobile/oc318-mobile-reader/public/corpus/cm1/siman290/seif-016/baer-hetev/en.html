Certain. The Tur quotes the Rif: If the orphans grew up and asserted a definite claim against him and he denied it, in such a case he certainly swears. The Mechaber states the law generally in order to include the same rule where beit din advances a definite claim. If there are two judges, they are believed against him; if there is one, he has the status of a single witness and the guardian swears against him. Sema.
