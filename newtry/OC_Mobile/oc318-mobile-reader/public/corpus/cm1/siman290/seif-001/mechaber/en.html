The appointment, conduct, and laws of a guardian. It contains twenty-eight seifim.
One who dies leaving minor heirs, a pregnant wife, or both minors and adults should appoint a guardian to manage for the minors until they mature. If he did not appoint one, beit din must do so. Beit din may appoint a man or woman, relative or stranger, but should select a trustworthy, capable person and avoid one whose conduct creates suspicion or conflict of interest.
