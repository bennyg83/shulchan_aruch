Alef) This is the language of the Tur. Rambam likewise rules in chapter 10 of Hilkhot Nachalot, halachah 5. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is explained in many places: if the father of orphans appointed a guardian, no one may overturn his acts, and if he did not appoint one, beit din must do so. The principal source is Gittin, chapter HaNizakin, 52b.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh explains that this applies where the adult heirs do not wish to remain in partnership with the minors, or where they are not improving the estate. But if the adults improve the estate and wish to remain in partnership, they may do so. Rambam agrees with the Raavad on this point.
There, halachah 6. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this follows from the rule that the commercial transactions of pe'utot are valid, as explained in siman 235, seif 1, when they have no guardian. This shows that beit din does not appoint a guardian in every circumstance. All the more so, from the following law that a father may appoint a person as guardian, he may leave the property in the hands of his own minor sons when appropriate.
Gimel) A baraita in Gittin there.
