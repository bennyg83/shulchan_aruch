A certain claim. Although the case here concerns a guardian appointed by beit din, and beit din may impose an oath even upon an uncertain claim, as stated in seif 16, the text specifically mentions a certain claim because upon such a claim an oath is imposed in all events even if the claimant does not allege two silver ma'ah and a perutah. By contrast, on an uncertain claim, denial of two silver ma'ah and admission of a perutah are required, as stated in seif 19. These are the words of the Sema. See Responsa Rashdam, simanim 49 and 445, and sefer Be'er Sheva, 81d.
