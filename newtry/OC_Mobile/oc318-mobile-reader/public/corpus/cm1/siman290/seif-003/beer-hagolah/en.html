Tur seif 5. The Maggid Mishneh in chapter 11 of Hilkhot Nachalot, law 4, writes that the Rashba cited the Itur that only at the end do we not require an accounting from them, but initially beit din must make an accounting with them. The Beit Yosef likewise cites the Ran.
The Tur in the name of the Raavad. The Maggid Mishneh likewise cites the Rashba. This is called a shtar shimush, and its text appears in the Itur, Hilkhot Apotropos.
