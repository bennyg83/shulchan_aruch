Because on a definite claim he must swear. Although this case concerns a guardian appointed by beit din, whom beit din can require to swear even on a doubtful claim, as explained below in seif 16, the text mentions a definite claim because an oath is always imposed upon it even without a claim of two silver coins and an admission of one perutah. A doubtful claim has the additional minimum requirements explained below in seif 19. See the Perishah.
