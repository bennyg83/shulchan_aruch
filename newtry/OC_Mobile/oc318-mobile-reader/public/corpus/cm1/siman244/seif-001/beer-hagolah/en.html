The language of Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilkhot Zechiyah UMattenah, law 10. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rav and Shmuel disagree in the third chapter of Gittin, 29a, whether a gift is like a get in this respect, and Rambam rules like Shmuel.
The Tur writes in the name of Rambam that we do not say concerning a gift that one who says, 'Tell the witnesses,' is like the law of a get. The Maggid Mishneh writes that some hold that even with a get this is valid, and certainly then the same applies to a gift. The Mechaber follows his own view, as he writes in chapter 3 of Hilkhot Gerushin that where one says 'tell them,' the get is close to being void. The Beit Yosef likewise writes in the name of the Ran citing the Ramah.
