One who instructs two people to write a deed of gift for another. It contains one seif.
A gift is like a get in that a person cannot appoint an intermediary merely to transmit instructions to another agent. Thus, if he says to three, "Tell so-and-so and so-and-so to write and sign a deed of gift and deliver it to so-and-so," the instruction is ineffective. If those persons follow the message, write, and deliver the deed, the recipient does not acquire. Rama: All the more so if the three themselves sign it. Likewise, if he says to two, "Write and sign a deed of gift and give it to so-and-so," they may not direct a scribe to write it; they themselves must write. Some validate the transaction when he says, "Tell so-and-so and so-and-so to write and sign a deed of gift and deliver it to so-and-so."
