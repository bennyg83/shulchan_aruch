If the lender instructed, 'Repay me only before so-and-so and so-and-so,' but the borrower paid him privately and the money was then lost through unavoidable accident, the lender cannot successfully claim, 'I accepted it only as a deposit until those witnesses arrived,' and demand payment again. Since he admits receiving the money and the borrower did not deliver it as a deposit, it constituted payment; the lender's contrary words have no effect.
