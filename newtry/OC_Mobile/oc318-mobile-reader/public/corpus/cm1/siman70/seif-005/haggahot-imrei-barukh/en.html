(Shach, seif katan 21) And they cannot give him a written statement that he repaid, etc. Note: This is difficult to understand. The lender can authorize them to write, or he himself can give the borrower a handwritten receipt. Later authorities have already raised this. In my humble opinion the borrower may say that he is not obligated to preserve a receipt. Although where a document is lost we write a receipt, that applies only where the document was actually lost, not where repayment is being postponed until particular witnesses return. This is comparable to siman 54:3. According to this, however, a borrower should be believed to say that he paid before a particular beit din even if he has no written proof from them, because the condition "do not repay me except before so-and-so and so-and-so" excludes only payment before other ordinary witnesses, not payment before a specifically identified beit din. Yet if so, why does the Mechaber say that the court writes for him that he paid before them?
