And some disagree. This is Ramban’s opinion, who rejects both the ruling and its rationale, as stated in siman 39, seif 3. See Ketubot 102b and Gittin 51a, where collection is from encumbered property. Generally, whenever the debtor can plead repayment, collection may not be made from encumbered property because of concern for collusion. According to Ramban, the exceptional case must concern a debt still within its term.
And it appears, etc., although. See Bava Batra 5b, where the halakhah follows Reish Lakish. Some seek to prove that one is not believed to claim waiver, for otherwise he would be believed to claim payment through a migo of waiver. The answer is that waiver is an inferior claim and is accepted only through a migo of payment, as the Rosh writes in Shevuot concerning the claim that payment was applied to another debt.
