There are those who disagree. The first opinion is primary. It is the view of most authorities, indeed almost all of them, as I explained in my work. Mahari Trani likewise rules so, and this is implied in many passages and from the words of the authorities and the Tur, including siman 39, seif 3, the beginning of siman 51, siman 75, seif 4, and the Rambam and Tur below in siman 87, seif 31, and at the end of siman 88. Although the dissenting view is that of Raavyah in the Mordechai, and the Ramban in the Ran appears to agree, the practical law follows the first view. The Ramban himself writes in Bava Batra that a kinyan without a written note may collect from encumbered property, and that the authors relied upon this to say that the debtor cannot plead payment. He does not disagree there.
