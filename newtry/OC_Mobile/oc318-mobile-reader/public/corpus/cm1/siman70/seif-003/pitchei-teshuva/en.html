If he repaid without witnesses, he is not believed. See Responsa Mayim Chayim, Choshen Mishpat, end of siman 17, who writes that even if the borrower claims that the lender later canceled the condition requiring repayment only before witnesses, because the lender feared he might urgently need the money when no witnesses were available and the borrower would refuse to pay without them, or for some other reason, the borrower is nevertheless not believed. See there, and see what I write below in siman 176, seif 11, seif katan 14.
He is believed. See Shach, seif katan 14, and Responsa Knesset Yechezkel, siman 100, regarding this matter.
