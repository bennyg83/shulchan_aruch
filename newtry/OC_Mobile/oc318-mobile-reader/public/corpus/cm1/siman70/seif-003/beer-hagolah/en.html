y) Rambam at the beginning of chapter 15 of Hilkhot Malveh, following his teacher Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash. This applies even if the borrower did not expressly accept the condition, because "a borrower is servant to the lender." The views of the Rashba and Ramah also appear to agree. The Maggid Mishneh and Ran explain that whenever the lender says, "Do not repay me except before witnesses," the borrower is not expected to repay without witnesses. We therefore regard the borrower's contrary claim as demonstrably false.

The Rosh likewise writes this in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel. The Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of Rabbi Meir HaLevi that this reading is found in accurate and ancient copies and in the manuscripts of the early authorities.
