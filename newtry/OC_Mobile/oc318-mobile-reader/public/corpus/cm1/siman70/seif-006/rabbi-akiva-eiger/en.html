[Ibid.] If the specifically designated witnesses are relatives of the lender or borrower, and the debtor says that he paid in their presence while they deny it, it may be argued that he is believed. He says that he fulfilled his condition, and they are disqualified from testifying that he did not pay before them. In any event, if at the time the condition was made they were not relatives, and they later became relatives through marriage, he is not believed to contradict them.
