To clarify. See the Sema, who challenges the Rama. According to the authority who states without qualification that one must clarify his words, it is obvious that an oath cannot help when the clarification is contradicted. The Rama therefore should not have appended this to the Mechaber's words, which are the Rambam's, since according to the Beit Yosef the Rambam requires clarification without qualification while the Mechaber concludes that he is believed with an oath. The Shach writes that this is not correct. The Tur first states without qualification that one must clarify his words, and later concludes that he is believed even if his clarification is contradicted. Tosafot likewise write this at the beginning of Shevuot 42, and so do other authorities. The Taz likewise writes this.
