(Shach, same passage) But if he claims that nothing ever happened and witnesses came who testified that he paid before them, etc. Note: One who studies Mahari's novellae in the Maggid Mishneh carefully will see that even if the witnesses testify after repayment that the lender said, "none of those coins reached me," the lender is believed. Thus he does not require the lender to claim that the money was applied to another debt.
