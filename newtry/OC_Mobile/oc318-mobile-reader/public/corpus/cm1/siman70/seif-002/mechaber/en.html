If the borrower says, 'I paid you before so-and-so and so-and-so,' he is told to bring them and be exempt, because he must clarify his statement. If they do not appear, or even if they appear and contradict him by saying the matter never occurred, he takes a heses oath and is exempt. If he says that he formally designated them as witnesses to payment and they contradict him, he must pay. {Rama: Some say there is no distinction whether he designated them; whenever witnesses appear and contradict him, he must pay, although he was not initially required to clarify his words. If he cannot bring the witnesses, this causes no concern. See below, beginning of siman 75.}
