But where she does not eat with him. The Tur states this law according to Rambam, who holds that the husband may say that she will not regularly eat with him except on Friday night, even if she does not agree, as explained in the next seif. According to the Yerushalmi, however, and according to the Rama's ruling in the gloss that the husband cannot force her to eat separately unless she accepted that arrangement, there is no independent need for this rule. The Rama must therefore have inserted it for communities that follow Rambam's practice.
