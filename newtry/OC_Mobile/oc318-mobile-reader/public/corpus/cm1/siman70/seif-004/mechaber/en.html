If the lender said, 'Repay me only before so-and-so and so-and-so,' and the borrower says he paid before others, then if those others appear and testify that he paid before them, he is exempt. {Rama: The lender may nevertheless say he accepted that payment toward another debt, because the borrower did not pay before the specified witnesses.} If the other witnesses died or went overseas, the borrower is not believed. If he says, 'I paid before the specific witnesses you designated,' but they died or went overseas, he is believed upon oath, because he claims to have fulfilled the condition. If he admits he did not pay but says, 'I will wait until the designated witnesses arrive and then pay before them,' he is not heeded. He pays before a beit din, which writes for him that payment was made in its presence.
