And a little wine to drink. See the Ran, who wonders why Rif omitted the Gemara's rule: if she was accustomed to drink two cups in her husband's presence, one cup is allotted when she is not before him; if she was accustomed to only one cup before him, she receives no wine at all when apart from him, because wine stimulates desire. Rambam therefore writes merely that she is given a little wine. This somewhat implies that Rambam too is speaking of a woman eating separately from her husband.
