Accordingly, we say that the lender designated particular witnesses only so the borrower could not make that claim, but did not intend to disqualify all other witnesses. According to the Rif, however, who holds that the borrower is not believed in any event to say that he repaid before deceased witnesses, the only purpose of saying, "Do not repay me except before so and so and so and so," must have been to disqualify other witnesses. Therefore, even if other witnesses testify that he paid, they are not believed. The Shach writes that this applies where the lender claims that the payment was for another debt. Even if these witnesses say that the payment was for this very debt, we divide their statement. But if the lender admits that he has no other claim against the borrower and says simply that these witnesses are lying, then even according to the Rif we need not presume valid witnesses to be liars.
