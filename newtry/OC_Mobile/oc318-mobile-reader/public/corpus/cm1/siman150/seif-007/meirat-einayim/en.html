Even if the lender claims that there was a mortgage document, etc. The Tur writes this in the name of Rif and Rosh, excluding Rabbeinu Yonah, who holds that the lender is believed only when he claims that there never was any mortgage document. If he admits there had been one, there is a weakness in his claim, because people ordinarily preserve a mortgage document carefully as proof against a borrower's assertion that the mortgage term has already expired. See the Tur and Perishah, where the matter is explained.
