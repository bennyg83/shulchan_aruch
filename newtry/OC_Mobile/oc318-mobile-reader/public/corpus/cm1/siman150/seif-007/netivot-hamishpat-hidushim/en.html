I had a mortgage document. The Shach disagrees and rules like Rav Pappa in Bava Metzia 110 that he is not believed through a migo that he once had a mortgage document. Since such a document is used for collection, a person is careful to preserve it. His failure to preserve it strongly indicates falsehood, and a migo does not help. The Shach concludes that this remains a doubt of law. Therefore, regarding produce not yet consumed, the land remains in the presumption of its original owner and the burden of proof rests upon the party seeking to extract. This applies specifically to a mortgage with deduction, where the document stands for collection of the debt. With a Sura style mortgage or a lease, however, the document does not stand for debt collection and a person may not carefully preserve it. He is therefore believed through a migo, even regarding produce already consumed. Concerning produce already consumed he is believed even with a mortgage with deduction. See the Beurim:
