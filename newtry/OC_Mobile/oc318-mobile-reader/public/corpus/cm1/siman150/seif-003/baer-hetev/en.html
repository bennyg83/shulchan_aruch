Forever. This applies specifically to a pledge and similar cases, where the property's legal designation and nature have not changed. By contrast, with the son of a craftsman, an agricultural tenant, and similar cases, the status and circumstances later change. Although they had no chazakah when they first entered the property, if they subsequently possess it for three years and claim, 'In my presence you admitted the craftsman's or tenant's title, and I know that you sold it to him,' they are believed by virtue of the migo that they could have claimed, 'I purchased it from you.'
