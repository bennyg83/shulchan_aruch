Nimukei Yosef likewise writes in the name of Ran that the general rule is that anyone who enters property with permission has no chazakah. This is the reason an artisan and sharecropper have no chazakah, for it is as though there were witnesses that the item was entrusted and presently seen; even one who is not an artisan would not be believed in such a case, as in Chezkat HaBatim 45-46. Tosafot write likewise there and on Bava Batra 33a, s.v. "he concealed it," etc.
