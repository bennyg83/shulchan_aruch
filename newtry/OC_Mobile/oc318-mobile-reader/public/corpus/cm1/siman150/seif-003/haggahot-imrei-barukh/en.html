(Same Shach) And from the two answers of the Ramban, etc. Note: According to the Geonim, the intent of Rava bar Sharshum was to conceal the matter until he had consumed produce equal to his debt. He would then inform the orphans that the land was theirs. Since he would not demand anything from the orphans before completing collection of his debt, he would no longer be obligated to swear, because he had already collected and was not within the rule governing one who comes to collect.
