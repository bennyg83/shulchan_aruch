To claim. The Taz writes that the word 'every' is not literal, for in siman 77 we find that we do not advance for orphans every claim their father could have made, and likewise concerning an iska document in siman 108, seif 4. The reason is that those are weak claims. Regarding the Shach's statement that this requires study in light of siman 108, seif 12, it appears to me that there is no difficulty. There the creditor has a pledge document and also possessed the property. We therefore say that even had he not possessed it, he could have sworn and collected, so now that he also possesses it, he collects without an oath. Here, by contrast, he has no document and therefore must swear. See also the Shach in siman 149, end of seif katan 24.
