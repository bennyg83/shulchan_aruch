He collects without an oath, etc. This requires study. The Mechaber rules in many places that we do not say a migo in order to exempt a person from an oath. How, then, can he collect from orphans without an oath, when we rule that one who comes to collect from the property of orphans may not collect except with an oath? The Rosh has already concluded that this is so even where the creditor seized property, and the Mechaber likewise rules above in siman 108, seif 3. Even if we force the explanation that the present ruling applies only where he claims that the property was purchased, in which case against heirs he is exempt from an oath because they have no definite claim, there is still a difficulty. We would then have to explain the Gemara’s discussion of Rabbah bar Sharshom, and the migo he asserted, in accordance with the Rosh’s second answer, as relating only to the waiting period and not to exemption from the oath against orphans. After all, one who collects from orphans must take an oath, and we do not use migo to exempt from an oath. We would then need to adopt the Raah’s explanation that Rabbah bar Sharshom reasoned: since even if I wait I will not become exempt from an oath, why should I wait? This itself proves that when one claims, “He owes me,” by a migo that he could have claimed purchase, he still must take a hesset oath, because Rabbah bar Sharshom’s reasoning was that even though his present reliance on migo would subject him to hesset, that makes no difference, since even after waiting he would still have to swear. It follows that this supports the view that one must take a hesset oath even against orphans, despite their uncertain claim. We must therefore say that the Mechaber followed the Tur here, and the Tur does not accept the rule that migo is ineffective to exempt from an oath, and the Mechaber merely copied his words. Alternatively, perhaps the Mechaber follows the opinion that one must swear even against orphans. This is difficult, however, because above in siman 133 he rules that no oath is required at all. This requires study.
