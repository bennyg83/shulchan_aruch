[Shach, subparagraph 3] And what he wrote, that the land remains in its owner’s possession, is not exact. Note: We have merited the illumination of Shitah Mekubetzes to Bava Batra, where the words of Rabbeinu Yonah are cited. It is clear there that he distinguishes between land and movable property. With movable property, the possessor may retain it as collateral until he is paid, through a migo that he could have retained it claiming purchase. With land, however, which remains in the established owner’s possession, there is no concept of seizure as collateral. See there.
