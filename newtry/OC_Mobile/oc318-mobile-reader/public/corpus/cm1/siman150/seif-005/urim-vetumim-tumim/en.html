And he wishes to retain the land until he has consumed, etc. See Bach, who asks that before he has consumed the amount due, the preceding seif already states that he is not believed. See Perishah for his answer. According to what I wrote above distinguishing between a loan evidenced by a deed and an oral loan, there is no difficulty. Here we are dealing with a loan evidenced by a deed, as in the case of Rabbah bar Sharshom, which concerned such a loan. Yet there is another difficulty. Since the rule that although he is believed to claim purchase despite the public report, he is nevertheless not believed to claim that a debt remains due by a migo of purchase, is derived from Rabbah bar Sharshom, because that would be a migo contradicting a public report, how do we know the present rule that the report itself functions like a protest and prevents chazakah if it arises within the three years? In the case of Rabbah bar Sharshom, the Gemara does not explain “you cannot claim purchase” because the report functions as a protest, but because the migo contradicts the report, as explained above. It is difficult to say that Rabbenu Yonah derived from his own reasoning that a report is no weaker than a protest. What is the source for that, given that a public report has no substantive legal force? Perhaps, according to what I wrote above in connection with Shach in siman 103, the orphans in the case of Rabbah bar Sharshom certainly had a guardian. If so, there remains a difficulty. Rabbah bar Sharshom should have had a migo that he purchased the field from the guardian, since the Mechaber rules above that such a claim can establish chazakah. This migo would not contradict the public report, because the report established only that the field had belonged to the orphans’ decedent; it did not establish that the guardian could not later have sold it. We must therefore conclude that a public report preceding the chazakah prevents the chazakah from taking effect. Yet besides the fact that this inference is strained, a report of this kind should not nullify chazakah at all, because the chazakah being asserted is against the guardian, while the report concerns the orphans’ father. One can understand the distinction.
