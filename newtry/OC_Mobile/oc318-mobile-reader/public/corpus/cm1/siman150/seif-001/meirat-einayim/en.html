If there is no name, but a voice. P. Among the bars is not clearly famous but a voice of thirst. But the occupier himself admits and says that in a row, he comes next to him and that he is so jealous and if he says he has more debt from the field than that of this law, he wrote next to him:
Like the case where he mortgaged an orchard for ten years, etc. This is the case stated in the Gemara at the end of chapter Eizehu Neshech. Below, in siman 255, a similar incident is mentioned and the period there is stated as three years, from chapter Chezkat HaBatim. It appears that these are essentially one type of case. The Rambam and Mechaber also write ten years there. The Tur writes ten years here in order to teach that the owner should protest during every three year period. There, where the discussion concerns coercion, three years suffice for the point being made. This is clear.
