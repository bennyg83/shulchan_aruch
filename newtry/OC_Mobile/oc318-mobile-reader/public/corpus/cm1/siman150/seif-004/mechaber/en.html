If Reuven entered Shimon's field under a mortgage and also had a separate oral claim against Shimon that he could not otherwise collect, and the mortgage term ended, and Reuven wishes to retain the field and consume its produce up to the amount of that claim and then return it, then if it is known that he originally entered under a mortgage, he is not believed even if he held the field for three years. Once the mortgage term ended, he must return it immediately. If it is not known that he entered under a mortgage, he is believed concerning the other claim through a migo that he could have concealed the mortgage deed and claimed, 'I purchased the field,' provided he takes an oath. This applies specifically if he already consumed produce equal to the amount he claims. If he comes to claim before consuming the produce, he is not believed to retain the land.
