The Maggid Mishneh writes that he did not find this explicit, but observed that the Gemara mentions only Torah prohibitions or rabbinic prohibitions from which benefit is forbidden, such as yayin nesech, meaning ordinary gentile wine. But with rabbinic prohibitions of eating, once the buyer consumed and benefited from the food he returns nothing. The Kesef Mishneh writes likewise in the name of the Rivash. Although Rambam lacks a completely explicit proof for this law, none of the later authorities is found to disagree with him.
