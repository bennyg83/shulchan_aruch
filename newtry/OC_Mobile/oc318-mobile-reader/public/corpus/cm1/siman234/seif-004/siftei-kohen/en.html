All objects from which benefit is forbidden, etc. See the Sema, seif katan 6, through the words, 'This applies only to others, but the seller himself, who is a party to the matter, is forbidden to sell it,' etc. Rashi writes this in the chapter Rabbi Yishmael, and Tosafot write likewise at the beginning of the final chapter of Avodah Zarah. Rashi and Tosafot also write so in Chullin concerning the leaven of transgressors. This is the principal ruling, unlike what is written in Maadaney Yom Tov in Bekhorot. See there. See also Orach Chaim, siman 450, and the Bach there and what I wrote there.
