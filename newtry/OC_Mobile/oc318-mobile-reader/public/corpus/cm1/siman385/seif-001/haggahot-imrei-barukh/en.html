(Mechaber, seif 1) They imposed the penalty only where he acted intentionally. Note: Rambam, Hilchot Chovel U'Mazik 7, writes: "Likewise, one who causes damage inadvertently or under compulsion is exempt, for they imposed the penalty only upon one who intends to cause damage." The same is clear from his words in Hilchot Maachalot Assurot 13:28, where he writes that because this gentile intended to harm him and to render his wine prohibited, etc. See Pri Megadim and Beit Yosef to Tur, Yoreh Deah 132, and Derishah there. The same is also clear from the Raavad's gloss to Hilchot Chovel U'Mazik 7:4: even when the act was intentional, if his intention was for his own benefit and not to cause loss to his fellow, he is not liable in human courts.
