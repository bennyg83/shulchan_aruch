Shin) There, halachah 7, from the Gemara's conclusion on 61b. The Gemara asks how the matter is determined, whether a structure called a house is included under the local term "house," and similarly concerning a yoke of cattle on 77b. Everything follows the terminology used in that locality. The Tosefta likewise teaches that where there is no established custom the formal rule applies, but where there is a custom, everything follows local custom. The Maggid Mishneh explains this clearly.
Tav) There, halachah 8. The Mechaber repeats this in siman 210, seif 19, and siman 220, seif 15.
