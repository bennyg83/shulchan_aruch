Zayin) This is the language of the Rambam there, chapter 25, halachah 7, based on the Mishnah there on 65b and the Gemara's phrase "the sea of the olive press." The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam used this term in place of the word "sea" in the Mishnah. Rav Yosef ibn Migash explains that it is like a millstone built upon a platform on which the olives are crushed.
Chet) This is explicitly explained in the Gemara regarding the vertical posts mentioned in the Mishnah.
Tet) A baraita there. It means the pit into which one descends, which is hollowed out and made like an opening.
Yod) This follows the version in our texts of the baraita, "he sold the boards," and the Rashbam's explanation that, in his view, they were attached.
Kaf) There in the baraita.
Lamed) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this appears in the baraita, and the Mishnah's correct version likewise seems to be so, although some texts differ.
Mem) The Bach writes that since the Rishonim dispute the matter and the textual versions are uncertain, the burden of proof rests upon the claimant.
Nun) A baraita on 68a concerning a bathhouse.
Samekh) The incident there before Rav Yosef, and Rav Ashi's response.
Ayin) The Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of Rabbenu Shmuel that their principal use is to sell bread and wine upon them, and olives are also spread upon them to dry before producing oil.
