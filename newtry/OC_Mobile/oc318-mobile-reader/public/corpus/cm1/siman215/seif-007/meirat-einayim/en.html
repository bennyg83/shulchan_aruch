Likewise, brothers who divided an estate. Since they came to divide, with one taking this field and the other a different field, they intend to separate themselves from one another completely with respect to everything fixed and standing within each portion.
And one who takes possession of the property of a convert, etc. Meaning, through a single act of chazakah performed in this field, he also acquires a grafted carob tree, a sycamore stump, and similar items. This is like two fields with no boundary separating them, where the acquirer obtains both through one act of chazakah, as I write below in siman 275.
And one who consecrates, etc. One who consecrates property is presumed to consecrate entirely and generously. Otherwise, what compelled him to consecrate it at all? This is the same rationale as with a gift.
