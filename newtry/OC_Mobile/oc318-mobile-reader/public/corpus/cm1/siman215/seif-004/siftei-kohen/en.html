One who sells a city, etc. The Mishna in the chapter HaMokher Et HaBayit records a dispute whether the santer is included in the sale. The Gemara discusses the meaning of santer. The Rif rules against Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, as do the Rambam and Rabbi Ovadiah of Bartenura in his commentary on the Mishna. The Rosh expresses surprise at the Rif, but there is no difficulty. In the chapter HaSefinah the Gemara places this opinion within a group of exceptional views, and in every such case the law does not follow any member of that group, as the Rif and Rosh themselves state there. The Rif and Rosh rule similarly at the end of the chapter HaBayit VehaAliyah and at the beginning of the chapter concerning Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel's view that wording merely indicates a place. The Rosh's abridged rulings also state plainly, in siman 6, that a change in the form of a plain or folded document invalidates it. This appears correct.
