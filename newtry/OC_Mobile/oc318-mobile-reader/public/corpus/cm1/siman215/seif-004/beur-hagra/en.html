(Supplement) One who sells, etc. Rif writes that the halakha does not follow Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. Rosh objected, but overlooked Rif's own explanation that this general rule is not absolute, especially where the view is grouped in a rejected line of authorities. Rambam explains 'santar' as the city watchman, consistent with the common usage in Gittin, the Tosefta, Yerushalmi, and Midrash. The irrigated field within the city and adjoining plots are included; known outlying fields are a separate category. The Gemara's alternative versions are resolved accordingly. See siman 40.
