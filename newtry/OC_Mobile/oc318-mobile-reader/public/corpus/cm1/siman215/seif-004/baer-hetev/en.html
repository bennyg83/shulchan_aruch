The city. In the Mishnah in HaMocheir et HaBayit there is a dispute whether the city official called the santar is included in the sale. See the Gemara there for its explanation. Rif rules that the view of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel is not accepted, and so rule Rambam and R. Ovadiah of Bartenura in his commentary on the Mishnah. The Rosh questions Rif, but there is no difficulty. Indeed, I am surprised at the Rosh, for in HaSefinah the Gemara establishes the cited opinions as a rejected line of authorities, and in such a case the halachah does not follow any of them, as Rif and Rosh themselves write there. They write similarly at the end of Bava Metzia, and also in Gittin regarding Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel's view that a stated place merely indicates location. The Rosh also rules this way in his Kitzur Piskei HaRosh concerning a simple or folded get whose form was altered, that it is invalid. See there. This appears correct to me. Shach.
