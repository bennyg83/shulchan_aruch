It is astonishing that the Ir Shushan, instead of the word meaning the remnants or outskirts of the valley, wrote “ornaments and chains within it,” evidently mistaking the word for jewelry. Not the part she has in the sea. P.R., one part of the sea should be awakened and named as the city but standing in the sea:
Nor the portion it has on the mainland. Rashi explains that this portion too is on the mainland like the city, but is separated from it, although it bears the city's name. Rashbam's version of the baraita reads that the portions in the sea and on the mainland are sold together with the city. The Rambam's version reads that they are not. The Maggid Mishneh explains Rashi and says the Rambam disagrees with Rashbam, without suggesting a different textual version. The Mechaber follows the Rambam. The Tur omitted this entire clause.
