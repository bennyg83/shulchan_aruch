There, chapter 9, law 4, and there in the Mishnah. The Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of the Rashba that the trees which were reserved do not require a right of way, while the Rashbam writes that even the trees require one. In my humble opinion the Mechaber omitted their dispute and stated the law without qualification. It appears to me that Rambam's view follows the Rashbam, for he did not mention trees separately.
