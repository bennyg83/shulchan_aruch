The Maggid Mishneh writes that a pit and cistern are included in the sale of a courtyard because a courtyard is used for washing and watering animals, unlike a house. The Rashba's reasoning is similar. Ibn Migash writes that courtyards normally contain pits and cisterns, whereas houses do not.
