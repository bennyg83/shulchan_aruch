The foregoing limitations apply to a sale, because the purchaser should have specified what he intended to acquire and did not do so. He therefore receives only the items expressly included. With a gift, however, the recipient acquires everything, whether the subject is a field, house, courtyard, or olive press. The general rule is that one who gives land thereby gives everything attached to it unless he expressly excludes it. Rama: This applies only to what lies within the thing given. Items outside it, such as an external gallery, chamber, or similar structure, are governed alike in a sale and a gift.
