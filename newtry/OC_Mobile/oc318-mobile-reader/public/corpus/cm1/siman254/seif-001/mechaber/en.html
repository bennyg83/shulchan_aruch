A person on his deathbed who requested that a kinyan be made for his gifts. It contains one seif:
Although a deathbed gift ordinarily does not require a kinyan, if the giver requests that a kinyan be made, it may be made even on Shabbat, because the kinyan is legally unnecessary. Some say that even where the kinyan has practical legal effect, such as where he writes away all his property and expressly provides that he will not retract if he recovers, the kinyan may be made on Shabbat. Rama: Some say that even a partial gift, which ordinarily requires a kinyan, may be formalized by kinyan on Shabbat. Although the fact that he gives only part of his property indicates that he may not be acting from fear of death, nevertheless the kinyan may be made. Rashbam, chapter Mi Shemet. Others disagree. Tur in the name of Rosh.
