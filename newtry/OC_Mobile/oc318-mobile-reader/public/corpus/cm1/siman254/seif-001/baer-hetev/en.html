On Shabbat. Ordinarily it is prohibited to make a kinyan sudar on Shabbat because it resembles commercial activity, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch write at the end of siman 195, the end of siman 235, and Orach Chaim, siman 339. Here it was permitted because the kinyan is legally unnecessary, since the words of a shechiv mera are regarded as written and delivered. We nevertheless perform it in order to reassure the dying person's mind. Sema.
