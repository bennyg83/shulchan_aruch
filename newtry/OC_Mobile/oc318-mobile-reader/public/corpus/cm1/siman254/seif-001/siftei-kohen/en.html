Even on Shabbos. Ordinarily it is forbidden to make a formal act of acquisition on Shabbos because of the rabbinic prohibition against commercial transactions, as the Tur writes above at the ends of simanim 195 and 235, and in Orach Chaim, siman 338. Here it was permitted because the handkerchief acquisition is legally unnecessary, since the words of a dangerously ill person are treated as though written and delivered. It is performed only to calm his mind. These are the words of the Sema.
