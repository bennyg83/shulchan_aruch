A. A.M. M.R. Dr. Levi S. D. Kendall:
Rambam writes in chapter 8 of Hilkhot Zechiyah, law 3, cited by the Tur. This concerns one who gives all his property, which requires no kinyan.
A column called his father Rashi:
And there is a name given by all of his assets from D.M., he gives and is entitled to the darkness of judgment if he does not say:
As well as as the addition of the name, and bringing evidence from the chapter of Zecharin to the Shabbat, “I will buy an authority on the Shabbat, and I will not be able to receive a permit on the Shabbat.”
