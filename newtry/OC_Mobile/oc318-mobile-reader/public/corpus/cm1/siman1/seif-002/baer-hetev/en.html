Loss of value. Although at the beginning of HaChovel the Gemara says that this assessment is common and also involves monetary loss, the explanation is that the Gemara calls only the assessment itself common, namely the difference in value between a virgin and a nonvirgin, because everyone is familiar with that matter and it is not included in an assessment of a person as though he were a slave. The act itself, however, namely raping or seducing virgins, is uncommon. The Mechaber's reference to loss of value concerns rape and seduction, and it takes the place of full damages, as Rashi explains in Bava Kamma 84b. Likewise, kofer applies only to an ox that killed a person. The Mechaber was merely concise and grouped these matters together. Thus the Sema.
They collect. This means that for any bodily injury they collect even for severing a hand, which is uncommon. Since compensation for loss of work and medical expenses also applies to other common blows and wounds, no distinction is made between one injury and another, and these payments are collected in all such cases. Sema. The Shach likewise rules so in the name of Maharshal.
To appease him. The Sema writes that Rama also includes medical expenses in the amount paid to appease the injured party, and this is obvious.
