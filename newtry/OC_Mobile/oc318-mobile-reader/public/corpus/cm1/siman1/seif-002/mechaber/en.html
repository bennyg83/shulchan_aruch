When one person injures another, judges who do not possess semichah in Eretz Yisrael do not collect payment for damage, pain, depreciation, humiliation, or kofer, but they do collect compensation for loss of work and medical expenses. {Rama: Some say that they do not adjudicate medical expenses or loss of work either (Tur in the name of the Rosh). I have not seen the practice to be exacting in this regard; rather, they compel the assailant to appease the injured party and fine him as they deem appropriate (Darkei Moshe, following Maharam in his rulings, siman 208), as will be explained shortly in seif 5.}
