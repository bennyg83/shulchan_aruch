One may not sell a Sefer Torah, etc. In Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chaim 153, it is ruled that one may sell a Sefer Torah in order to provide support for students of Torah or to use the proceeds to marry off orphans. This is from the Rivash, cited by the Beit Yosef there, who concludes that this is comparable to selling for the sake of Torah study or marriage. Accordingly, this applies specifically to a male orphan who wishes to fulfill the mitzvah of procreation but lacks the means. One may not sell a Sefer Torah for the sake of marrying off a female orphan, since a woman is not commanded in the mitzvah of procreation, as ruled below in seif 13. For the other details of this law, such as where one lacks food, redeeming captives, and whether there is a distinction between a privately owned Sefer Torah and one belonging to the public, see Yoreh Deah 270 and 282 and Orach Chaim 153. This is not the place to elaborate further on the laws of selling a Sefer Torah.
