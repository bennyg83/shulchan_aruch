And even if he married a woman and remained with her for ten years, etc. The Rama derived this rule from a responsum of the Rivash, but one who examines the responsum will see that he wrote the opposite. His words are: 'If she remained with him ten years and did not bear children, etc., nevertheless we compel him. And although she is already married to him, all the more so if she has an established status as barren, that if he comes to marry her we prevent him by compulsion from marrying her.' See there.
