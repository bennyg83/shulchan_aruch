If the injured party seized. See Baer Heitev, who writes that this refers to proceedings under Jewish law, etc. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: There, the Maharshal did not write this with respect to a case after the fact. Rather, he wrote that the injured party is permitted initially to seize forcibly from the tortfeasor, and concerning this he wrote that nevertheless it is forbidden to seize anything through non Jewish courts. End of quote. After the fact, however, one can say that the seizure is effective, and it is entirely comparable to what was stated above in siman 4 in the gloss: If he transgressed and acted, where he could not have saved his property in another manner, what he did is effective. End of quote.
