Although judges who do not have semikhah in Eretz Yisrael do not collect fines, they place the offender under nidui until he appeases the injured party. Once he gives him the amount that is appropriate for him, they release him from the nidui, whether or not the injured party has actually been appeased. Likewise, if the injured party seized the amount that he is entitled to receive, it is not removed from his possession. Rama: If the injured party says, "Assess my damages for me so that I will know how much I may seize," they do not heed him. Rather, if he has already seized, they assess the matter for him and tell him how much he may retain and how much he must return. This applies specifically to fines prescribed by law. However, fines that the Sages themselves impose in order to uphold their enactments may be collected everywhere, as will be explained in siman 2.
