And they caused payment to be extracted. This applies specifically where money was actually extracted through their testimony, not merely under the law of "as he conspired," because that is a penalty. It also applies specifically where the witnesses were shown to be plotting witnesses. If they were merely contradicted, then even if money was extracted through their testimony we do not obligate them to reimburse it, because why should the later witnesses be believed more than the first? That differs from formal hazamah. See Biurim seif katan 3: where the payment of "as he conspired" does not apply, they are exempt even from liability under the laws of direct causation, because the lashes exempt them from monetary payment.
