(Further there.) Rav Hai writes that a beit din must have these items prepared, etc. One may ask what Rav Hai is teaching, since this is stated in the Gemara. If his purpose were to exclude a shoe because it is not commonly available, he should have written that Rav Hai says a shoe is unnecessary. It appears to me that he comes to explain that one should not understand the verse, "which you shall place before them," regarding the judges' implements, as referring to placing these items before Israel, with Hashem commanding Moshe to place such implements before them so that fear would fall upon them. Rather, he teaches that the phrase "before them" refers to the judges themselves, meaning that these implements must be ready at the sitting of beit din.
