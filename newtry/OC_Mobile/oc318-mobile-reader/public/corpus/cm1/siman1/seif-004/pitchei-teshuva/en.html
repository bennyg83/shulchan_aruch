And they extracted money, etc. See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part I, siman 147, that if they did not actually extract money on the basis of their ruling, but merely exempted him from an oath, we do not obligate them to pay. We do not say that the moment they exempted him it is considered as though they extracted money, so as to obligate them under the law of garmi, for this is not garmi but only grama, and they are exempt. See there, and see what I wrote below, siman 28, seif 3, seif katan 11.
