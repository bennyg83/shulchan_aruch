The practice is not to compel him in this matter. This is somewhat implied by the Rosh in chapter HaBa Al Yevimto, seif katan 16, who writes: 'And it would likewise be proper, regarding an unmarried man who has passed the age of twenty and does not wish to marry, for beit din to compel him to marry.' This implies that such compulsion was not the practice in his time. There is no proof, however, from the Rivash, from whose words the Rama derived this ruling, because the Rivash discusses not compelling him to divorce, or not preventing him from marrying a woman incapable of bearing children. He does not discuss a man who remains entirely without a wife.
