His honor. The Yerushalmi, cited by the Tur, states that one who humiliates an elder must pay a litra of gold; some explain this as 36 gold coins. Thus concludes the Sema. The Shach writes that in Yoreh Deah, siman 243, seif 2, the Rama rules that nowadays a Torah scholar does not have the status of a talmid chacham for purposes of the litra of gold. Responsa Maharit HaChadashot, end of siman 47, disagrees. See there.
