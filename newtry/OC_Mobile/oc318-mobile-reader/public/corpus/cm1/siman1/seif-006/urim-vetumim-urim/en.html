Accordingly. Mechaber writes in Yoreh Deah that one who humiliates a Torah scholar can be fined a litra of gold. Rama says that scholars today do not have this status. Maharit questions that limitation. Even if we do not impose the historic fixed fine of a litra of gold, a judge should nevertheless exert himself greatly to punish one who humiliates a Torah scholar. In our sins, Torah scholarship has already been diminished. If beit din fails to punish contempt for scholars, Torah itself will become despised and students will lose the desire to devote themselves to it.
