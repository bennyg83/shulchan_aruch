We do not remove it from his possession. Rabbi Akiva Eiger notes in his glosses to Shulchan Aruch that it becomes fully his property, and even if the damager subsequently seizes it back from him, the seizure is ineffective. See Shitah Mekubetzet to the first chapter of Bava Kamma, siman 43. See also Netivot HaMishpat on this matter.
