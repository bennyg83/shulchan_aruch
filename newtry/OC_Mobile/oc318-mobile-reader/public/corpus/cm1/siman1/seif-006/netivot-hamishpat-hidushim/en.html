According to his honor. Yoreh Deah states that one who humiliates a Torah scholar is fined a litra of gold. Rama writes that Torah scholars of our time do not have this law. Maharit already questioned that ruling. Nevertheless, a judge should exert himself greatly in this matter and impose a very severe punishment upon one who humiliates a Torah scholar. On the contrary, especially because, due to our sins, Torah wisdom has already declined, if beit din does not involve itself in penalizing those who disgrace Torah scholars, Torah will become greatly despised and students will become disgusted with studying it, Heaven forbid. Urim VeTumim.
