According to his honor. Gloss: The Yerushalmi, cited by the Tur, states that one who humiliates an elder pays him a litra of gold. The Sema cites this. Rama rules in Yoreh Deah, siman 243, seif 2, that a Torah scholar nowadays does not have the status of a Torah scholar regarding a litra of gold. This is based on Terumat HaDeshen, and Maharik, shoresh 163, writes similarly. Responsa Maharit, new responsa, end of siman 47, strongly disagrees and holds that he does have the status of a Torah scholar regarding a litra of gold. See further there.
