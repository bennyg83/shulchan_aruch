One who humiliates another verbally. The Tur cites this in the name of Rav Sherira and the Rosh. It is an enactment of the Geonim. By strict law, verbal humiliation carries no monetary payment, and even ordained judges would not collect damages for words alone, as stated in chapter HaChovel, Bava Kamma 91a. This is unlike Ir Shushan.
According to his honor. The Yerushalmi, cited by the Tur, states that one who humiliates an elder pays a litra of gold. A litra is the weight of thirty-five gold dinars, as Rambam explains and the Tur cites below in siman 420, seif 30. Some explain it as thirty-six gold coins, as cited in Yoreh Deah siman 334 in the name of Maharam of Rothenburg.
