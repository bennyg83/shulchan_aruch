Or robbery. Although in the chapter HaChovel it says that robbery and bodily injury are uncommon and therefore we do not act as agents of the ordained judges, this does not contradict the many places in the Gemara where thieves and robbers were judged. Tosafot explain that uncommon robbery connected with bodily violence is not judged, but ordinary robbery is judged. This is the intent of the Rambam and the Mechaber. Rama in Darkei Moshe cites another answer, based on the Beit Yosef, distinguishing uncommon forcible snatching from common cases such as denial of a deposit. Sema. Shach objects that this cannot be the Mechaber's intent because the Beit Yosef did not cite that distinction. He explains instead that according to the Rambam, when the Gemara says robbery is judged, it means that the principal is collected everywhere but the statutory double payment is not. This is why the Rambam carefully says that only the principal is collected. See Biurim seif katan 2, which distinguishes that when ordained judges existed in Eretz Yisrael, unordained judges outside Eretz Yisrael did not adjudicate robbery, but now, due to our sins, when there are no ordained judges, robbery cases are adjudicated. See there for the reasoning.
