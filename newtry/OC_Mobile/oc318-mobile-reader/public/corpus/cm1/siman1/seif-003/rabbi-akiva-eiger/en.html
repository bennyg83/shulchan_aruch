[Shach, subparagraph 15] In Yam Shel Shlomo, first chapter. Note: Maharshal there did not write this regarding a case after the fact. Rather, he wrote that the injured party is initially permitted to seize forcibly from the tortfeasor. On this he wrote: "Nevertheless, it is forbidden to seize anything from him through the laws of the gentiles." End quote. But after the fact, one can say that the seizure is effective, and this is exactly comparable to what the Rama writes below in siman 4.
