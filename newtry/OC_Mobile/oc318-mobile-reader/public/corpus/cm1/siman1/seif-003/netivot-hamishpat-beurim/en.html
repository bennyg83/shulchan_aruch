Witnesses who testified falsely. One should not ask that they are liable to lashes and that lashes exempt from payment. The Gemara says that Rabbi Meir treats plotting witnesses as paying rather than receiving lashes only with respect to the payment created by 'as they plotted.' The liability here is not from that verse but from the law of causation, so apparently the lashes should exempt them. There is no difficulty. They are in any event liable to payment through 'as they plotted,' and where ordained judges exist the rule is that they pay rather than receive lashes. Accordingly there is no separate lashes liability that would exempt them from this other monetary liability. This also supports Tosafot's view that in monetary matters we do not apply the limitation 'as they plotted but not as they did.' Where the payment of plotting witnesses does not apply, however, as will be explained in the laws of testimony, then liability under causation may indeed be displaced by the lashes.
