And some say specifically thefts, etc. Nimukei Yosef writes this to reconcile the relevant Gemarot. Tosafot, Bava Kamma 84, Gittin 88, and Sanhedrin 3, distinguish differently: only thefts that arise through bodily injury are not adjudicated. This is the Mechaber's view when he rules simply that theft is adjudicated. See Darkei Moshe, seif 4.
