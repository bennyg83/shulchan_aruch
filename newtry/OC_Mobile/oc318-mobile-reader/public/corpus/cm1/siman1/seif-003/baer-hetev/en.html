Robberies. The Sema writes that in the Mechaber's view all laws of robbery are adjudicated, except robberies arising through bodily injuries, which are not adjudicated in the present era. The Shach, however, holds that even robberies arising through bodily injuries are adjudicated today. When the Gemara in the chapter HaMegaresh states that cases of robbery and bodily injury are not adjudicated because they are uncommon, it refers to penal payments associated with robbery, such as double payment, fourfold and fivefold payment, and the like. His words appear difficult to me, for all this does not pertain to a robber. Examine this carefully. As for the Mechaber's statement that only the principal is collected from him, the Bach explains that only the principal is collected, but not the increase in value that accrued before the owner's despair. The Shach disagrees and maintains that even that increase is collected; the exclusion concerns only the robber's penal liability, etc. See there.
