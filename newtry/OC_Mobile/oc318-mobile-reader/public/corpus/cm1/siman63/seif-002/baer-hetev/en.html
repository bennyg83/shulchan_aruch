We retain it. This applies specifically here, where there is a defect because he was seeking a forged document, and possession would not help him. Therefore it is not left in his hands, lest he collect with it in another beit din. In siman 38 concerning a claim of side payment, and in siman 46 where two witnesses say that the witnesses are disqualified, although there too we neither collect nor tear up the document, we nevertheless leave it in his hands because possession would help in those cases. We therefore do not fear that he will collect in another beit din, since there is no practical difference between that and possession. Sema.
