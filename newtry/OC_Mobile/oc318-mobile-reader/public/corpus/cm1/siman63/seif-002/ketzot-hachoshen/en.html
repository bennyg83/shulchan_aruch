Forever. Maharam cites Rabbeinu Tam in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel that when the Gemara merely says a document is "weakened," the ordinary consequence is that the holder must swear before collecting. Tumim inferred that even the document described in Ketubot as especially bad could still collect with an oath. That is incorrect. Tosafot in Shevuot expressly distinguishes the ordinary use of "weakened," which can mean collection only with an oath, from the special document in Ketubot concerning one who was known to seek forged documents, where the Gemara says, "We do not collect with it." That document is not collectible even with an oath. The comparison there to a woman whose bad reputation deprives her of fine and seduction payments likewise shows a permanent bar. Thus the phrase "weakened" standing alone may mean oath, but the explicit rule of "we do not collect with it" means no collection at all.
