One does not collect with it. Bach writes that this applies where the document had not been seen in the person's possession before he began seeking help to forge. If it had previously been seen with him while he still had a presumption of fitness, the document is not retroactively invalidated. If witnesses say that he asked them to forge but do not know when, the same rule follows Rama in siman 34: where witnesses establish that a person sinned but cannot determine whether the offense preceded or followed his testimony, we preserve the prior presumption of fitness and say the deterioration occurred only now. Therefore the earlier documents remain valid.
