We do not collect with it. See Sema, seif katan 3, who explains that we say this person is not presumed to be a forger, but perhaps he has an old oral loan. His intent is that there is a doubt of an old loan, for even if the debtor claims that he never borrowed, we do not collect. See Shach, seif katan 2, who writes that even if witnesses saw the documents before the due date, they do not identify them unless they have a distinctive identifying mark. This follows his position in seif katan 3 that testimony that the witnesses saw the loan or saw the signing of the document is ineffective. According to Sema, seif katan 5, who holds that such testimony is effective, no distinctive identifying mark is needed, because their testimony is equivalent to testimony that they saw the signatures being made.
