And up to. This means inclusively. The reason is that at a distance of less than four cubits the trees do not draw sufficient nourishment and are considered as though uprooted, whereas beyond sixteen cubits the land is not subordinate to them, since they do not require so much area for nourishment. What was stated in the preceding seif, that he acquires the trees between them, concerns trees situated at least four cubits from the three trees that he purchased, for otherwise they would be deemed uprooted. Nimukei Yosef. Ibid.
