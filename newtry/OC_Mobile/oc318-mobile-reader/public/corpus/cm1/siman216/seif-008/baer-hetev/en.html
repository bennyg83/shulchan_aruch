Proof. The seller is in possession of the field. We do not say that if the trees had grown larger the seller would immediately have forced the purchaser to cut them down, because perhaps the seller did not notice the matter at that time. Ibid. It appears to me that all the more so, if the seller says, 'I sold them one after another,' while the purchaser says, 'I bought them all at once,' the purchaser must bring proof. So writes Taz. See there, where he thereby resolves the question of Rashba and Ritva against Ramban.
