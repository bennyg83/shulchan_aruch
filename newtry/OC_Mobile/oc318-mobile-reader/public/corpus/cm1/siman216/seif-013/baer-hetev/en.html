And took possession. See Sema, who sought with difficulty to resolve Maggid Mishneh's question against Rambam, namely that Rambam's words here contradict his ruling explained in siman 141:19. Sema distinguishes between the present case and the case there. Taz objects that both rulings are derived from one statement in Chezkas HaBatim: 'He sold the land to one person and the trees to another; the purchaser of the trees has half the land.' How then can the two cases be distinguished? Taz.
