Some explain and hold, etc. According to Tosafos there, s.v. “this, etc.” and s.v. “shall we say, etc.” Rashbam, however, explains here too that he receives all the land needed, as stated in the preceding opinion. The Tur records the dispute between Rashbam and Ri here, while earlier he ruled without qualification like Rashbam, as reflected in the gloss. Tosafos does not explicitly discuss the earlier case, so perhaps they disagree there as well. Indeed Tosafos appears to explain the entire sugya as concerning the right to plant another tree in place of one that dies. If so, the first clause must also concern that matter. This requires study. It also requires study why the Rama did not comment above in siman 141 seif 19, since the Tur records the same dispute there.
