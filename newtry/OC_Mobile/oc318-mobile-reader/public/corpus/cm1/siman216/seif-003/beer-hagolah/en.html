There, law 11, from the Gemara 69b: 'except for a palm tree,' etc. The Maggid Mishneh cites the Rashba's novellae in the name of Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash that this does not mean the very best or the very worst, but one among the good or one among the bad. He writes that this appears to be the Mechaber's intent. The Kesef Mishneh likewise writes so. It makes no difference whether he reserved the worst of the worst or there is another worse than it; the law is the same. The Ran likewise writes so, and this is also the view of the Raavad.
