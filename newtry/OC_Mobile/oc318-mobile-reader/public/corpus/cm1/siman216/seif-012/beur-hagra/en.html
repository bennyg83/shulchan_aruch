(Supplement) Likewise, if he reserved, etc. This follows Rav Huna on 71a, including the statement 'even though, etc.' Although the Gemara initially associates the ruling with Rabbi Shimon, that understanding is not maintained in the conclusion. See Rashbam there, s.v. And he answers, and on 72b, s.v. This is the reading, through the end of s.v. The Sages said to him. See also Baer HaGolah. (End of supplement.)
