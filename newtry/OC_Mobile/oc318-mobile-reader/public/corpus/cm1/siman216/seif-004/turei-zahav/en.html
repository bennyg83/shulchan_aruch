(Seif 11.) One who sells land. In the words of Rabbeinu it appears necessary to emend the text as follows: “The Rambam wrote that he has half the land, etc. Likewise, if he retained two trees, he has the land required for them. This does not appear correct to me.” This, in my humble opinion, is the proper reading. Concerning the first ruling Rabbeinu wrote, “It appears to me, why should he receive half the land when he retained the trees?” Rabbeinu then cited in the Rambam's name the law that one who retained two trees has the land needed for them, although Rabbeinu had already written that law himself. He did so lest one mistakenly explain the Rambam's statement that where the seller retained trees he receives half the land as referring to a case in which all the land is required by those trees. Rabbeinu therefore cited the concluding clause, which necessarily does not concern such a case, since there the Rambam says only “the land they require,” whereas in the first clause he says “half the land.”
