And growing up from the race, the Sages gave a lesson in each of the idols according to what it would give him a lesson that could return and grow:
Two shoots. This is the Mishnah's language and also the Rambam's. The Tur writes that he leaves two branches close to the ground so that they will grow again. Rashbam explains similarly. See below siman 274.
In the quaint of the quake, we will be found in the S. Ruts and the quaint is called as long as we have not yet been harvested, i.e. during his choosing and Snowden, which is called after Xikin and has already been cut off once a:
From the knot and upward. This is the Rambam, Rif, and Rosh's wording. The Tur writes, “from the lower knot downward.” In the Perishah I explained that all refer to the same act of cutting. The knot is the lower joint of the tree. He cuts from that knot upward, leaving the knot itself. The Tur's statement means that from the knot downward remains for the seller, including the knot.
