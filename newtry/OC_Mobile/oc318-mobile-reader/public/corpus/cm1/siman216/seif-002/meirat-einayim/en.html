Likewise one who sells land, etc. In the Perishah I explained that this means: even where the seller sells a tract standing within a large valley containing enough area for several fields, with no boundaries separating them, and says, “I sell you this field,” where the law is that he acquires the whole tract and the seller cannot limit him to one field, nevertheless it is advisable to write explicitly, “I have retained nothing,” etc. Otherwise the matter may come before an inexperienced beit din that will award him only the standard measure of one field, namely nine kav. The explicit language prevents such an error.
