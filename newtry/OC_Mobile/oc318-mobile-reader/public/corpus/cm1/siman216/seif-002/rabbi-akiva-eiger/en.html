[Rama] Although there are date palms. Note: Tosafos, cited briefly by the Shach, hold that if there are many date palms in this field, he gives the purchaser only two palms, because the plural "date palms" implies no more. The principle of interpreting a document favorably does not apply here, unlike the case at the beginning of this siman, because that principle applies specifically to written language, whereas here the statement was oral. Furthermore, there the document says "date palms and other trees," while here he says only "date palms," so there is no basis for that interpretation. End quote.
