(Collected note) One who says, etc. See Beer HaGolah seif katan 40, regarding one who sells, etc. The Rif in our editions cites this as an opinion, but he himself does not accept that the rule depends on a thing not yet in existence. Rather, he writes in accordance with the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch. His statement that “we also rule that one may contract, etc.” concerns a different rule. The Rambam himself explains likewise, as stated above siman 209 seif 6. (End of note.)
