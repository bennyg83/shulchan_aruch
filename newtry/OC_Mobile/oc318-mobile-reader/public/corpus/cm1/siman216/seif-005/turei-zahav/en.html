(Seif 13: together with half the land, etc.) According to the distinction the Sema seeks to make in seif katan 98 in Rambam's view between this case and the rule at the end of siman 141, I am astonished. Both rules are derived from one and the same statement of the Gemara in Perek Chezkas HaBatim: "He sold the land to one and the trees to another, the owner of the trees has half the land." How can one distinguish between them? The Maggid Mishneh already leaves Rambam's words requiring study.
