There is a judgment and a name
There in the Mishnah, as Rabbi Yochanan says, 'he cuts it,' and Rabbi Yochanan likewise states this there, 82a. The Maggid Mishneh, after citing Ibn Migash and the Rashbam, wished to explain that the Mishnah's phrase 'he shall not trim' refers to spreading branches, as the Rama writes. Likewise, what grows above the trunk means the branches, even a newly sprouted branch, because the concern mentioned there exists only with what rises from the trunk, etc.
