See Shach, seif katan 3, who writes that the blessing HaTov VeHaMeitiv was instituted in Yavneh and is only rabbinic, so that together with the blessing over the wine there are only four blessings of consequence. Ketzot HaChoshen objects: if so, why must he pay for the blessing over the wine either, since it too is only rabbinic? All blessings are rabbinic except Birkat HaMazon, which is biblical, and according to some poskim the blessing over Torah study. See there. Ar'a DeRabbanan, letter 627, records the same objection in the name of a sage, and the glosses there offer a partial resolution. See there.
