With permission. Meaning, even though he did not actually obtain permission, since his fellow benefited from it, it is considered as though it was done with permission. See below, siman 375. It appears to me that the same applies to the law in siman 157, seif 10, where one party raised the wall above four amot and the other later built a wall opposite it. There too, if the first party cannot prove his expenses, he swears how much he spent on the additional height and collects it. Ibid.
Proof. Because the obligation is not sufficiently obvious that the other party would necessarily pay it on his own, as explained in siman 157 there. See there. Ibid.
