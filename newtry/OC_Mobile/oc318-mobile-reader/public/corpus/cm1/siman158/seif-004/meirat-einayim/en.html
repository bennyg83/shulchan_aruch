Thus, if the Wall had fallen, then, because the front was a sign that Hashem himself and a conference in his own was therefore when the Wall fell into his own place:
And they make a facade marking on both sides. The Gemara asks why neither should make any marker. It answers that otherwise one party might enter the other's courtyard, plaster a marker there at the end of the wall, and later claim that the entire wall is his. Therefore the Sages required each party to make a marker. One cannot answer that the other could simply scrape away the false marker, because, as explained, the place from which plaster was scraped is recognizable.
