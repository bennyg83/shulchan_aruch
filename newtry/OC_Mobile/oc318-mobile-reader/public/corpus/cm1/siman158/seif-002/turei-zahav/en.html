(Seif 5, there is an authority who says). This is difficult. When the Gemara asks, “Let neither this one nor that one make a frontage mark,” and answers that the Mishnah is not according to Abaye, it could have established the ruling according to all authorities and explained that the frontage mark is needed lest the wall fall. It appears that this opinion holds that making a frontage mark would not help after the fence fell, because it would then no longer be recognizable. The principal function of the frontage mark is therefore while the wall still stands, and concerning that the Gemara asks why neither party should make one.
