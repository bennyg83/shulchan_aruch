The Rosh writes so, and Maggid Mishneh likewise writes in chapter 3 of Hilkhot Shekhenim, law 3, from the Gemara's question: in a place where the custom is not to fence, if the surrounding owner built fences on all four sides, why must the enclosed owner pay? What did the Sages accomplish with their enactment, since one person could simply build first and obligate the other? They answer that if the enclosed owner protested when construction began, he need not pay.
