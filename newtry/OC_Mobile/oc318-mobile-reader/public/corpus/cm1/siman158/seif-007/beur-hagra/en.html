The rule that costs are apportioned. Ramban derives from the Yerushalmi that the assessment follows the construction of the fourth wall. If the surrounded owner built the fourth wall of stone arches, he pays for arches even if the surrounding owner built the first three walls of bricks, and the surrounding owner must accept this arrangement, undertaking to rebuild the first three with arches if they fall. Thus, if the surrounded owner built the fourth wall of reeds, he need pay only for reeds. This is Rav Huna's statement in the Gemara, 'everything according to what he fenced,' meaning according to the fourth wall. This is the meaning of 'according to whether the surrounding owner or the surrounded owner,' etc.: everything follows the fourth wall, for that is when liability for all four arises.
