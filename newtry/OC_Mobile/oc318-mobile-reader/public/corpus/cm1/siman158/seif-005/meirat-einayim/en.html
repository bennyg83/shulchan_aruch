It is presumed to belong to both of them. The reason is that by strict law they were not required to make any marker at all when both built it, as is evident from the Gemara cited above. The dissenting view holds that once the Sages instituted a marker and one party did not make it, that omission creates an adverse indication. Since the wall is physically in the other party's courtyard, he is the possessor, and the rule is that one who seeks to extract must bring proof. See the Perishah.
