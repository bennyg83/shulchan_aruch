There is one who says. Their reason is that once the Sages enacted that both parties should make a projecting marker, the absence of such a marker creates a defect in the claim of the one who is not in possession. Therefore the possessor is believed. Sema. With a courtyard wall above four cubits, if it falls into one party's property and there is no marker, it belongs to that party. Shach.
