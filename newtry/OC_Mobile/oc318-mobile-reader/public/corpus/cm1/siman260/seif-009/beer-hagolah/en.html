There, at the beginning of chapter 15 of Hilkhot Gezeilah VaAveidah, from the Mishnah concerning one who found something behind a fence, etc., there 25b.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that it appears from Rambam's words that this was his reading at the end of the sugya: rather, one may say that someone placed them there, and it is therefore a doubtful placement. This explanation contradicts the earlier explanations. Nevertheless, from the Rif, our textual reading, and Rashi's explanation, it appears that the rule applies specifically to an item without an identifying mark. The Rashba and Ramban likewise write so. The Kesef Mishneh writes that Rambam may hold that since Rabbah bar Zavda stated generally, 'any doubtful placement should not initially be taken,' he meant both an item with a sign and one without a sign. He explains the sugya at length. This is not the place to elaborate; see there.
