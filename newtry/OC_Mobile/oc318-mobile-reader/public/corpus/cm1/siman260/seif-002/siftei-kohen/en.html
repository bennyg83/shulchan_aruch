If the purse itself proves, etc. This implies that even when the objects fill the entire thickness of the wall, we follow the indication furnished by the purse or knife. The Tur also implies this. The Bach ruled otherwise, following what he understood as the Rambam's view, but his words are astonishing. Without doubt, the Rambam and Semag agree as well, as the Kesef Mishna and Rabbi Avraham Stein explain. This is also proven by the Gemara, for otherwise its question 'Is this not obvious?' would not arise. Maharshal explains the Semag this way too. Some mistaken student added an explanation stating that the wall is filled from the purse and knife onward. That addition does not reflect the author's view and may be a printer's error; it apparently belongs earlier in the passage.
