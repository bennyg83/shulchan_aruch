The Bach did not rule so, but rather understood the Rambam differently. His words are exceedingly difficult. Without doubt, the Rambam and the Semag also hold as stated here, as the Kesef Mishneh and Mahara Stein write. This is likewise proven from the Gemara, for otherwise what is the Gemara's question, 'Is this not obvious?' See there carefully. Maharshal, in his commentary to the Semag, also explains it this way. Some mistaken student added an explanation there, saying that the wall is filled by the purse and knife and the like, because he understood the matter according to Mahara Stein; perhaps there is a printing error there.
