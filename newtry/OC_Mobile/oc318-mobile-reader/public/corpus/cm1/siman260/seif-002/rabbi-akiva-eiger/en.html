[Sema, letter 25] So that the animals should not enter. Note: This is Rashi's explanation of a fenced enclosure, namely that because of the enclosure crawling creatures do not go there. But the language of Rambam, the Tur, and the Mechaber implies that ordinarily there is presumed despair, whereas by enclosing the place with partitions he demonstrates that he is particular about it and does not despair. A practical difference arises where one erected a fence and then died. According to the Rosh's reason, whatever falls there even after his death is prohibited, since crawling creatures still do not enter. According to the Mechaber's reason, however, one may say that an express indication of non-despair is effective only for that person himself, but not for his heirs after him, whom we judge like ordinary people who despair.
