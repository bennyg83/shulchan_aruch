The language of Rambam there, law 4, from the Mishnah there, 26b.
The Kesef Mishneh writes that Rambam's reason appears to be this: the Gemara asks why the Mishnah specifically teaches that what is found between the chair and the moneychanger belongs to the finder. The same question applies to a shop: why teach that what is found between the box and the shopkeeper belongs to the shopkeeper? It could have taught that what is found on top of the box belongs to the shopkeeper. This implies that what is on top of the box belongs to the finder, etc.
The Tur in the name of Ri. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes that this is because many gentiles are commonly present there. Tosafot and the Raavad likewise explain so.
