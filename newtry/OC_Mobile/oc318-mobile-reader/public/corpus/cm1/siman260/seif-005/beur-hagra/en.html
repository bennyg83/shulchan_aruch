(Supplement) Even if there is, etc. As Shulchan Aruch states below, even if the coins were tied, etc., following the textual reading: Rabbi Elazar said, even if tied and placed, etc. Being tied is an identifying mark, as stated in the Mishna, 'if they were tied,' and there on 24a. See Tosafot 26b, s.v. 'even if tied.' This is the meaning of 'provided they are, etc.' Rosh, however, writes that even where most are Jews the finder may keep it because the owner despairs. Rashi does not read 'tied.' (End of supplement.) See 660.
