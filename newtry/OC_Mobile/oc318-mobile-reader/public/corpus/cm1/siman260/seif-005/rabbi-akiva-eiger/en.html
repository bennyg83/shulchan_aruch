[Rama] Even an item possibly placed there intentionally, if it has an identifying mark. Note: The word "even" is difficult, for the greater novelty is that even if it was certainly placed there intentionally, he may take it and announce it where the place affords only partial protection, as the Tur states explicitly. One must therefore say that "even" refers to the latter clause: even where it is only possibly placed there intentionally, if it has no identifying mark he should not take it. The Ran in Shitah Mekubetzes holds that one should not take it unless two favorable factors are present, namely, that it is only possibly deposited there, the place affords some protection, and the item has an identifying mark. But if it is only possibly deposited yet fully protected, or certainly deposited and only partially protected, he should not take it.
