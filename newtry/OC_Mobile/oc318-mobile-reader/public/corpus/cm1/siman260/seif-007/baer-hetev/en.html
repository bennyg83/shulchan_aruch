The threshing floors. So writes the Tur. He holds that the law depends on the place where the produce is found. Since it was found in the area of the threshing floors, even after the threshing season we attribute it to the owners, because when they threshed the grain they did not wish to trouble themselves to gather a small quantity of produce scattered at a distance of four amot. This excludes the view that makes the law depend on whether it was found during the threshing season. See below, siman 262, seif 7. Ibid.
Amot. Tosafot explain, according to one answer, that this means an area of two amot by two amot. If it is spread over two by four amot, it belongs to the finder. See there and examine carefully. Shach.
