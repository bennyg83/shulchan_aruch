A find from a thief. See Shach, seif katan 16, who refers to siman 268 where there are two opinions whether a courtyard acquires something for which payment must be given. Even according to the opinion that treats it like a found object, the item must at least be resting on the ground of the courtyard. If it is still physically in the seller's hand, the buyer's courtyard does not acquire it, as Sema, seif katan 15, cites from a responsum of Rosh.
