A thief. The Raavan writes in the chapter Elu Metziot, at the beginning of page 94: If a thief entered Reuven's house intending to transfer a found object to Reuven, while Shimon was sitting there, and the thief thought that Shimon was Reuven the homeowner and transferred the found object to him, Reuven's house acquires it for him whether Reuven is present in the house or not. This is so even though it is a house through which many people pass and is not guarded, because where another person's intent transfers ownership, a guarded courtyard is not required. Here too, the thief entered intending to transfer it to Reuven. Shimon must therefore return it to Reuven. See there, and see below, siman 268 seif 3 in the gloss. Shach.
