(Seif 7, or over a larger area.) The Sema asks that this is obvious. It appears that the phrase was included because of the latter clause concerning one kav of dates or sesame seeds. Even when they are scattered over more than four cubits, the finder may not take half a kav scattered within two cubits, because completing the collection of all the scattered produce would not involve excessive effort. By contrast, with a kav spread over four cubits, completing the collection would entail substantial labor, and therefore he does not gather any of it, not even half a kav within two cubits. Tosafot give this as one of their answers.
