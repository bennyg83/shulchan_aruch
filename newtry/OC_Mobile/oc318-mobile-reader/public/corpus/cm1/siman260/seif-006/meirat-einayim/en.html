Because the owners despair of them. The Gemara says that figs and dates commonly fall from the tree. Therefore the owners despair of fruit that falls even before the actual fall. Otherwise it would be despair without knowledge, and the object would first come into the finder's hand while still prohibited to him. With cut figs laid out to dry, the owner notices the loss immediately when he gathers them to bring home, because they are valuable, and he despairs before the finder takes them. See the Perishah.
