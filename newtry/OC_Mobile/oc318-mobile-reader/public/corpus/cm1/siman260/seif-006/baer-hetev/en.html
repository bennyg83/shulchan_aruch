They despair. The Gemara states that figs and dates normally fall from the tree, and therefore their owners despair of them even before they fall. Otherwise, this would be despair without knowledge, and the finder would have taken possession while it was still prohibited to him. Likewise, with dried figs, when the owner comes to gather them and bring them home, their importance causes him to notice immediately if some have fallen, and he despairs before they reach the finder. Sema.
