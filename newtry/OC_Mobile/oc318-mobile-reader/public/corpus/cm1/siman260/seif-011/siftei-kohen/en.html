When is this said? When he found it in a refuse heap, etc. The Sema, following the Tur, writes that where the object was deliberately abandoned it belongs to the finder. The Rambam omitted this. It is not necessary to say that the Rambam disagrees. The Gemara asks, 'Is this not deliberate loss?' meaning, why must he proclaim it rather than keep it. The Rambam merely abbreviated and stated the conclusion, omitting the refuse heap never intended for removal that appeared only in the question. He may therefore agree with the Tur, and this is likely Rama's reasoning. Although the Rambam rules differently below in siman 261, seif 4, the wording here is not decisive; alternatively, the two forms of deliberate loss may be distinguished. In practical law, the Tur and Rama are plainly correct.
