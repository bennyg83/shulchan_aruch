(The wooden barrier.) This means a wall enclosure made of wood or reeds, whereas a fence is made of stones. Although at the beginning of the siman, regarding an article beside a fence, the implication is that one should never touch it even if it has an identifying mark, that case is different. Laborers commonly place their cloaks beside the fence where they work, so the location is guarded. Tosafot and Rosh state this.
