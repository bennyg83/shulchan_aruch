But if he acknowledges that it is a found object, etc. The reason is what I already wrote, that a person's courtyard does not acquire a lost object for him. We would otherwise have said it belonged to him or his ancestors; he now admits that it is a found object.
Therefore, if he rented it, etc. The Tur writes this as a separate ruling. The Mechaber copied Rambam at the end of chapter 6 of Hilchot Gezeilah with the word "therefore." It appears the meaning is this: since when the owner admits it is a found object it belongs to the finder because his courtyard did not acquire it, therefore if he rented the premises to three non-Jews, an item found there belongs to the finder. The householder cannot say, "My courtyard acquired what fell from a non-Jew." This explanation is somewhat strained and requires study.
It belongs to the last tenant. Meaning, if he is Jewish. The earlier Jewish tenants certainly searched and checked the house and removed everything belonging to them when they left.
If he rented it to three non-Jews. This is the Gemara's wording. The Tur says, "if two non-Jews and one Jew lived there together." In the Perishah I explained that the Tur follows the Rosh, and Tosafos has the same intent even though it says that fewer than three do not constitute a majority. It means three including the householder who lives with them. The finder may take the item because the owner certainly despaired, reasoning that the non-Jews found it and would not return it. Alternatively, we attribute the lost item to the majority non-Jews rather than to the one Jew living with them. It is possible that Rambam and the Mechaber mean the same and that "three non-Jews" is not literal. Alternatively, they interpret the Gemara as a case where the Jew does not live with them, in which case three would be required. The rationale for the exact number is not clear. See the Derishah.
He has made it into an inn. Even if it is true that he hid the item there, he surely despaired of recovering it, assuming the non-Jewish occupants found it.
