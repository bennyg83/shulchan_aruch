When does this apply, etc. Because the wall does not acquire it for him. See Tosafos there, s.v. "deshatich, etc.," and as stated below, "therefore, etc." This is the meaning of seif 1, "for they do not remember, etc." It is not limited to an Amorite wall. Even if a Jew who was not his ancestor built it, we attribute the object to him when it is deeply corroded, since there was already despair of recovery.
Therefore, etc. The Gemara there: "and why, etc.?" In an old wall the reason is that it is deeply corroded. This is the meaning of the challenge, "are there still Amorites, etc.?" Meaning, why not follow the last possessor?
And if he rented it, etc. See Tosafos, s.v. "to three, etc.," unlike Rashi, s.v. "three non-Jews, etc." The Rosh and Tur, however, rule like Rashi.
Non-Jews. See Beer HaGolah. This follows the Rif and Rambam, who hold that Rava disagrees with Rav Nachman. See siman 262:2.
