Until the end of the fifth hour. This is not exact. According to Sema the court may sit until close to the end of the sixth hour, provided the judges begin eating before the sixth hour fully ends. Once they begin before then, they may continue the meal afterward without it being considered eating at an improper time. According to Mechaber, who fixes the end of the fifth hour, these hours are seasonal hours varying with the length of the day, as in Orach Chaim. According to Sema's approach, the decisive point is midday itself and therefore does not vary in the same manner.
