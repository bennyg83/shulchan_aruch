Until the end of the fifth hour. Beit Yosef writes this as well. I explained in Derishah and Perishah that the poskim's wording differs. The Tur says until midday, Rambam until the end of the sixth hour. Beit Yosef objects because the sixth hour is the meal time of Torah scholars and from then onward eating is considered like throwing a stone into a flask. Therefore Rif and Rosh write until meal time, namely the sixth hour. I wrote that Rambam and Tur are correct. Their wording, "until midday" or "until the end of the sixth hour," means up to but not including the end. They should sit nearly until the end of the sixth hour, leaving enough time to begin the meal at the end of that hour and consume the main meal in the seventh. The small amount eaten at the end of the sixth hour is no worse than a taste, concerning which the Sages said that one who tasted something has no concern.
