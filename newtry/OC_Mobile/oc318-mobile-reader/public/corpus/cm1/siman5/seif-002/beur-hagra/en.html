(Collected note) To one who is not in the city. Ramah. Proof is that at the beginning of chapter 3 of Makkot we say, "they judge his case, etc." Beit Yosef, which implies even against his will. See below. (End of collected note.)
And some say. Bava Kamma there, concerning those attending the kallah assembly, etc.
And there are places. They found support for this in Shabbos 9b, "and if they began, etc." Likewise, a case already begun may be concluded at night, as explained above.
And some say that if they transgressed, etc. Yerushalmi, cited by Tosafot in Yevamot 104a, s.v. "Mar." However, see what is written in siman 28, seif 24. We rule in accordance with Tosafot, and this is necessarily implied by the Gemara in Yevamot there and Sanhedrin 11b.
