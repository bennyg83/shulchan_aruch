But if they sat down close to minchah ketanah, they interrupt. This requires study. Here the Rama rules this without qualification, while in Orach Chaim, siman 232, he cites the view of the Baal HaMaor that even a large meal is permitted close to minchah gedolah. If so, when the Mishnah says that one should not begin eating or judging, it refers to close to minchah ketanah. Yet the Mishnah then says that if they already began, they do not interrupt. It follows that even close to minchah ketanah they do not interrupt. How, then, can the Rama rule here without qualification that they do interrupt? This requires study.
