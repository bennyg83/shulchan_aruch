In the Shulchan Aruch, seif 1: Cases are not judged on Shabbos. It is written in Even HaEzer siman 64 that one who has a dispute may assemble three men and arrange his claims before them, and this may be done even on Shabbos. It is further written there, “If they summoned a litigant,” etc. It appears that the novelty is that in this situation the litigants need not show deference to beit din by appearing and presenting an excuse before them, as the Rama requires in siman 11.
