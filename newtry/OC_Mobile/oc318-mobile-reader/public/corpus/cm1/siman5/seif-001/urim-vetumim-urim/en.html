It is forbidden to adjudicate on Shabbat and Yom Tov. The reason is concern that the judges will come to write the claims or the decision. Gemara and Sema. Tosafot in Beitzah 36b writes that if everything was already written before Shabbat, one may complete a capital case on Shabbat because there is no longer concern of writing. That does not necessarily apply to monetary law. In capital cases the written judgment is not produced in the same manner, whereas in monetary cases the judgment itself is written. Therefore even final judgment can remain prohibited on Shabbat.
