It is forbidden to adjudicate on Shabbat, etc. The Sages prohibited judicial proceedings lest the judges come to write the claims and the judgment, as explained in Orach Chaim.
And if he transgressed and adjudicated, etc. This follows the Tur and Mechaber here. The Tur and Mechaber rule similarly below at the end of siman 235 concerning a sale transacted on Shabbat, in siman 208 concerning a sale performed through a prohibited act, and in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 175.
