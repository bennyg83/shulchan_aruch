(Shach, seif katan 3) But afterward they set a date. Note: So rules the Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, HaGozel, siman 17. Responsa Shevut Yaakov, siman 159, cites the Gedulei Terumah that no summons is issued during the entire month. Nevertheless, Shevut Yaakov concludes that where the custom is to issue summons immediately after the festival, the custom should be maintained.
