And so ruled the later authorities. In the Responsa Mahariv there it is written that even according to the view that we do not remove the money from the wife, that applies only to money lent to her husband that never came into her possession. But where the money actually came into her possession, etc., otherwise lenders would be deterred from extending credit, for she could retain all of the money and he would emerge free of debt.
