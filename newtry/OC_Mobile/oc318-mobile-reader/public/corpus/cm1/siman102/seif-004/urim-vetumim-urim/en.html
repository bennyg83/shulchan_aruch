And he sold it, etc. Since the debtor already owned intermediate land at the time of the loan, the creditor's lien attached to it at that time. Maharshal holds that this is true only if the debtor sold it after the due date, or in an unspecified loan after thirty days. If he sold it earlier, the creditor does not collect intermediate land from the remaining assets. The Shach says this is not implied by the Gemara or decisors. See Tumim. I brought proof from Tosafot and the Gemara against Maharshal. Likewise, if he owned superior land at the time of the loan and it was later swept away by a river or stolen by robbers, the creditor may collect intermediate land. There is no difference between involuntary loss and sale. See Tumim.
