For example, where he did not transfer movable property to her together with land. Although he wrote that she may collect from movable property and land, so that she collects the movable property as a matter of law because of a monetary stipulation and not merely because of the enactment of the Geonim, nevertheless a creditor takes precedence. Rambam explains the reason: the creditor advanced his money, while the wife did not suffer a monetary loss. But if he transferred movable property to her together with land, the movable property has the same law as the land and the rules of priority apply to it.
