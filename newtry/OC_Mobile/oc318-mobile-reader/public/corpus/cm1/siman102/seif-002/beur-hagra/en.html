And some say that he cannot, etc. This follows the foregoing rule. Rosh is cited. The first opinion reasons that although a creditor ordinarily cannot demand a specific intermediate-quality field, he may do so when the alternative is inferior land in another's hands. Rashba distinguishes between equal-quality fields and intermediate versus inferior land. The second view relies on Bava Kamma 8b, but that proof is not conclusive. See citation 119.
