Ketubot 110a, in the Gemara's explanation of the dispute between Rav Nachman and Rav Sheshet. According to Rav Nachman, appraisal is made relative to the borrower's own property. Thus, if the borrower has a field that is medium quality for everyone generally, but for him it is his best field because his other fields are inferior, the creditor does not collect from it. Rif and Rosh rule like Rav Nachman, because the halachah follows Rav Nachman in monetary law. Rambam likewise writes this in chapter 25, law 10.
