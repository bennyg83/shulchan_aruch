The seller cannot, etc. This applies where the purchaser was not overreached by enough to void the sale, because then it is not certain that the purchaser will retract. If the buyer was overreached by the amount that voids the transaction, so that ordinarily he will rescind and there is effectively no binding sale, Rabbeinu Yonah, the Rosh, and the Tur hold that the seller too may retract. The Tur states this in siman 227 and relies upon it here. The Mechaber and Rama, however, do not cite that view there because the Rif and Rambam hold that even in such a case the seller cannot retract.
