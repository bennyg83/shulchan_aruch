He sold him meat, and he returns the amount of the overcharge. VeShav HaKohen, siman 64, writes that Terumat HaDeshen, the source of this ruling, expressly indicates that “he returns the onaah” applies even where the difference is less than one-sixth; we do not say that such a small discrepancy is automatically waived. He further writes that the ruling of Terumat HaDeshen itself requires considerable study, as Bach already observed. See also Shaar Mishpat.
