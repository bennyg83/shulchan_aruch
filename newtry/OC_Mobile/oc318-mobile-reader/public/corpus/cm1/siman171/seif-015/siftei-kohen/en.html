We do not heed him, etc. The Tur concludes in the name of the Ramah that if the defendant requests time, it is granted to him even though the claimant wishes to pay immediately; if, however, the claimant requests time, it is not granted. The Sema also cites this. The case must be one in which the claimant swore not to retract, for otherwise there is no practical difference, since the claimant could simply retract, as stated below in the name of the Ramah. See further Sema, seif katan 41, through his reference to the Tur and Perishah. See also the Bach; the first explanation he gives appears more correct. The Tur further writes in the name of the Ramah that if the defendant says, 'Conduct the buyout arrangement now, effective after thirty days, so that the claimant cannot retract, while I sell my property cheaply for this purpose,' we heed him. The Sema cites this nearby.
