Tur writes in the name of Ramah that if the defendant requests time, beit din grants it, even if the claimant is ready to pay immediately, because perhaps during that period the defendant will obtain money and be able to buy out the other party. Tur further writes in seif 31 in Ramah's name that if the claimant says, "Let me acquire his share now, subject to the condition that if the defendant does not bring money within thirty days my acquisition will take effect retroactively now and finally after thirty days," we do not listen to him, even if he deposits the money immediately with beit din. Rather, if the defendant fails to bring money after thirty days, the parties return to the original status and the claimant may again demand "buy or sell." Tur further writes that when one party invokes "buy or sell," even after the other agrees to buy or sell, either may retract until they complete a valid kinyan with one another. Sema notes that it is surprising the Mechaber and Rama omitted these rules.
