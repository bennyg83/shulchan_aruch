We do not listen. If the defendant asks for time, it is granted even though the claimant offers immediate payment. If the claimant asks for time, it is not granted, and the defendant is not compelled to enter the "buy me out or I buy you out" process until the claimant actually produces the money. If the claimant later brings the money and the defendant then asks for time, see Beurim as to whether that time is counted from then or from the original demand. Even if the claimant proposes, "Let the defendant acquire from now, effective after thirty days, on condition that if he does not bring the money within thirty days the property becomes mine retroactively from now," we do not listen, even if the claimant immediately deposits the money. Even after both agreed to the "buy me out or I buy you out" process, either may retract so long as no kinyan was made. Therefore, if the defendant says, "I do not wish to trouble myself to obtain money unless you first make a binding commitment that if I bring it within thirty days I acquire," his claim is valid, because perhaps he will sell his assets cheaply and then the claimant will retract. If he did not raise that argument, beit din does not advance it for him. One may retract only from buying to selling or from either choice back to continued partnership. He may not retract merely in order to raise the price. After retracting to remain partners, however, he may then make a new higher offer. Sema. See Beurim.
