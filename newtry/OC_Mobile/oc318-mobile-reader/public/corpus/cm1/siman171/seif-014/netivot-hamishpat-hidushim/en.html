A house against a house of hearers so that they will not lose themselves in the division of each house
“If there is no house in any house, and it is not possible for him,” he said, “and if there are any of them in order to divide Hashem or union only on the same that it does not have in order to divide and divide the rest in partnership and abroad.”
Who are destined to collect their money. The same applies to a debt instrument. This is unlike what the Tur and Mechaber wrote at the end of siman 66, where with a debt instrument we apply the rule of "you buy me out or I buy you out." I explained the reconciliation there. Sema.
Like a rental. One can distinguish. A rental is different because the parties pay for use and do not ultimately collect money back. Therefore ordinary division does not apply, and we use the rule of "you buy me out or I buy you out." Sema.
