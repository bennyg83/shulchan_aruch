But from the words of the Rosh in a responsum it appears, etc. See the Perishah, where I wrote that I do not think the responsum proves what is attributed to it here. The Tur cites that responsum in siman 28. There the matter depends upon the son's preference. If he does not wish to remain in partnership with the widow, he cannot say to her, "Either buy the whole house or I will sell the whole house and give you half." Rather, in such a case the widow is correct that the shop and the house must be divided against one another, with an equalizing payment between them. But if the son wishes to remain in partnership with the widow, the widow cannot compel him to divide in this manner. See the Perishah for further discussion.
The rule of buy or sell does not apply, etc. The same category includes a mortgage or similar arrangement under which they are ultimately to collect money. It also includes a promissory note, from which money is ultimately to be collected. According to this view, the rule of buy or sell does not apply to such a note. This differs from what the Tur and Mechaber wrote at the end of siman 66:41, where the Mechaber rules without qualification that buy or sell does apply to a promissory note, and Rama cites no dissent there. I wrote there a possible resolution.
It is like a rental, etc. In Hagahot Darkei Moshe I wrote that the Maggid Mishneh indicates a distinction between the two according to the Rambam. Rental is different because the parties rented the property and pay rent, and they will not ultimately collect money from it. Therefore actual division does not apply, and the rule of buy or sell does apply. See there.
