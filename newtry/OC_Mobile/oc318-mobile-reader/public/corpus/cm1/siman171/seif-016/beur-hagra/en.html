Where there is no... Kiddushin 42a, according to the explanation of Rambam and the Rosh, that it concerns adults and minors, as stated in siman 289 seif 1. Tosafos there, s.v. "and they select," write that from the expression "and they select," Ri infers that it means without a lottery. He further proves this from the statement that they may protest, whereas in Bava Batra 106b to 107a, when the division is by lottery, they cannot protest. See Tosafos there, and see siman 175 seif 2 and what is written there. According to the explanation of Rambam and the Rosh above, it is impossible to explain the passage as Rabbeinu Tam does there. We thus learn that the authority of guardians is like the authority of the orphans themselves, and the same applies to gud o agud.
And some say that he may not. Rabbeinu Tam there writes that "they select" means by lottery; see there. See also Tosafos, Gittin 34a, s.v. "and the beit din," who prove this from the Gemara's question, "What does it mean that they may protest? With respect to the directions," rather than saying with respect to gud o agud.
