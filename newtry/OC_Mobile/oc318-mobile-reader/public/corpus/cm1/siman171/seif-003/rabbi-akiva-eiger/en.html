[Rama] He cannot afterward. Note: Beit Yosef, new matter 3, cites the Rashba as follows: Even where the property is legally divisible, if one partner used it for one, two, or three years, he need not pay rent, because he was using his own property. Likewise, the other partner may say, "Just as you used it for three years, I too will use it for the same period," since they made no prior stipulation otherwise. End quote. Maharikash cites this as well.
