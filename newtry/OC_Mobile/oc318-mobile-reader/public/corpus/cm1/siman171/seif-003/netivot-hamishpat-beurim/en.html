It will reach at least. See Sema, seif katan 5. The words "all of them can prevent" are a scribal error and should read "all of them are prevented." For when the party whose share does not contain the minimum measure for division is agreeable, the others certainly cannot object. There is also a scribal error at the end of Sema, seif katan 9. It should read: "and they must make a compromise among themselves, or all remain in partnership because of the fourth party, whose portion would not retain the minimum measure for division." Therefore he concludes and writes, etc. The words "and if" and the word "not" are scribal errors.
They are no better. At first glance this is difficult, for it is explained immediately below that if two parties do not have the minimum measure for division and two do have the minimum measure, the latter two take their portions. Why, then, is the reason needed that they are no better than the person through whom they come? However, according to what the Sema writes in seif katan 9, that if one party seeks to take his portion we listen to him, this reason is necessary: here, even in such a case, he cannot prevent the division.
