There: So that I remain a partner in it. Rabbeinu writes that he uses one quarter of his portion. The meaning is that one partner takes three cubits, while the remaining cubit is used jointly by both. That cubit is called one quarter of his portion when counted together with the other three cubits, meaning one quarter of the portion used by the other. There is no need for the Sema's explanation in seif katan 19. All this applies where they use the area at different times. A difficulty remains: if they own the four cubits equally, why can one not say, “I will take three cubits and share the fourth cubit with you”? It appears that where their interests are equal, one partner cannot insist on taking at least three cubits and then invoke buy or sell regarding the remainder. The other may insist that buy or sell apply only to the whole. Where, however, one owns four cubits, he may say to the other, “You cannot place yourself on equal footing with me, since in any event you are using one of my cubits.”
