This too is there, seif 4, in the name of Ramah. Nimukei Yosef likewise writes in the name of Raah: needless to say, they must divide the property in a way that leaves each one practical access, so that he is not forced to fly through the air, buy access for an enormous sum, or construct a new structure. The rule that they have no right of way against one another applies where each already has access from another place or consented to the division. The Rosh and other authorities agreed.
