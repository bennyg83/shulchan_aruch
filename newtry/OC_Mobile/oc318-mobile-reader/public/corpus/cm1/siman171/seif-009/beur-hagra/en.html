One who rents, etc. As stated in the Yerushalmi, Bava Batra chapter 2: It was taught, regarding his neighbor, he may retract; once he accepted, he may not retract. Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says that even after he accepted he may retract. Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel follows Rabbi Meir, who says, "I thought that I could tolerate it, but now I cannot tolerate it." The same applies here, even though they initially rented it in order to use it together. Although the first Tanna disputes Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, and likewise Rabbi Meir, that is only after he accepted, as it states regarding his neighbor that he may retract.
Others disagree and hold... For the reason above, and they hold that since he rented it, this is considered as though he accepted.
