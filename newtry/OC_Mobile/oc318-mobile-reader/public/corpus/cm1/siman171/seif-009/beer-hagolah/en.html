There, chapter 1, law 3. Maggid Mishneh writes that Raavad objects sharply to this ruling. Rashba, however, writes in a responsum to uphold Rambam: one can say, "When I rented or bought jointly with you, I assumed I could tolerate partnership with you, but now I cannot." Since they entered the partnership or rental without a special stipulation, he can make this claim. Rashba writes that this is correct. Maggid Mishneh adds that one may also say, "I relied upon the law of buy or sell when I entered partnership with you."
