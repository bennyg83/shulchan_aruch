[Shach, letter 1] See the Tur. Note: Responsa Maharanach, part 1, siman 16, rules likewise. See there, that if those who purchased it later left it to their heirs, the heirs cannot assert the claim of "I was merely acting as an agent," since they derive their rights from purchasers. Knesset HaGedolah copied this in his glosses to Beit Yosef, letter 26.
