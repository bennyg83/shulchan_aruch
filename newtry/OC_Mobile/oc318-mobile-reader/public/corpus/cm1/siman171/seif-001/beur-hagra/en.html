Reuven and Shimon. As stated in seif 2. Taz writes that even where there is no visual damage, partners can compel a partition. But the Rosh's responsum here says that where one cannot compel the other, the builder must withdraw into his own property.
Likewise. The responsum derives from Bava Basra 21b concerning opening a shop beside another. Even though the first shop may lose value, one generally cannot object where the other acts on his own property. Yet the Gemara ultimately recognizes an objection in some cases, and this point requires study.
