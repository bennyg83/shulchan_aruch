Or two who purchased from one person, etc. Rabbi Yosef HaLevi, the Raavad, the Nimukei Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, and the Raah maintain that the law of 'buy me out or I will buy you out' does not apply to partners who jointly purchased the property. See the Tur and Beit Yosef below, seif 33, and Responsa Maharan Sasson, siman 81.
"You can't go back to them, and you're not going to be able to come back in the face of a great deal, and I'm sure you're not going to be able to take a seat in the field, and that's why you're doing so."
