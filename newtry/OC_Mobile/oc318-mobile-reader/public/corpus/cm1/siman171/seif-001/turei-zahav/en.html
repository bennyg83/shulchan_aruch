Seif 2: so that they do not cause damage, etc. The Sema is correct to disagree with the Mechaber's explanation that the case concerns visual intrusion. At the beginning of Bava Batra it is clear according to the opinion that visual intrusion is not considered damage that every shared area nevertheless requires a partition ten handbreadths high so that one who crosses it may be treated as a thief. The same therefore applies to a field, where visual intrusion is not relevant. But the Sema's further statement that no real partition is needed, only a boundary marker so that one does not mistakenly use his fellow's land, appears incorrect to me. At minimum a partition ten handbreadths high is required so that an intruder can be treated as a thief. The Maggid Mishneh writes explicitly at the beginning of Hilchot Shechenim that where there is no visual injury, a partition ten handbreadths high is required.
