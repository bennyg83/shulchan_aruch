When the whole item is unfit. At first glance this is difficult. If so, why does the Gemara raise its question concerning a firstborn and an ordinary son specifically according to the view that the rule of gud o agud does not apply? Even according to the view that gud o agud does apply, the Gemara's question should arise where a father left a firstborn and an ordinary son an item that is not fit for the use of even one of them. Perhaps the Gemara did not discuss this because such a case is uncommon.
