They divide. The Tur is the dissenting opinion. Nevertheless, he concludes in the name of the Ramah that if the area is less than three amot, since it is not fit for complete use but only for spreading fruit or the like, even after division each party receives an area fit for that limited use. They therefore divide it, and it can properly be said that after division its original designation remains. Although the Tur then cites the Rosh as disagreeing, that disagreement is specifically regarding a courtyard in front of houses, where neither gud o agud nor division applies. In other places it appears that no one disputes that they divide in such a case. Sema.
