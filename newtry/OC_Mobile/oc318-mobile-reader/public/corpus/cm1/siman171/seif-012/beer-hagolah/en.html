Seif 17 in the name of Ramban. He writes this at the beginning of Bava Batra, and the Maggid Mishneh cites it in his name in Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 5.
Tur in the name of Rosh: in such a courtyard gud o agud does not apply, because a house cannot function without a courtyard. Tur further writes that a party can say he does not wish to buy, for money, something too small for his use. Beit Yosef writes that Rambam would agree and that Rambam speaks only of an open area behind houses or an independent ruin.
