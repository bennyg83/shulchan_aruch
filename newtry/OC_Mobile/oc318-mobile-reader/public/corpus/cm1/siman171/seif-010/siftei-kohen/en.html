There are those who disagree in this matter, etc. The Rosh writes concerning the Rif that we may not determine the reasoning of a lone authority by our own conjecture. It appears to me, however, that the Rif's position is based on the rule that wherever Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel taught a ruling in our Mishna, the halacha follows him. The Rif writes in the chapter Get Pashut that this rule applies only where there is a sound reason for his view, as I write below, siman 386, seif katan 12. Therefore the Rif writes here that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel's reasoning is compelling. One cannot answer that the rule applies only to a Mishna, because Tosafot in the chapter Eizehu Neshech, Bava Metzia 69a, s.v. Ela SheAta, explicitly indicate that the same applies to a baraita; see there.
