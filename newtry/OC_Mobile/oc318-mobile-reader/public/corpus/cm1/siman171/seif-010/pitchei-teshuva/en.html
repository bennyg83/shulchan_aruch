To receive a gift. See Baer Heitev, and see Agudat Ezov of Rabbi Moshe Zev, rav of Bialystok, who offers a decision and strong support for the view of the Rif and Rambam from Gittin 38 and Chullin 45. See also Shaar Mishpat, who raises a question where one of the partners is a distinguished person and the giver derives benefit or honor from his acceptance of a gift, as explained in siman 190:6. If one partner seeks division and offers the other the larger share as a gift, perhaps the recipient cannot invoke the principle that one who hates gifts will live, because in the case of a distinguished person the giver himself gains honor from the acceptance. On the other hand, that honor exists where the distinguished person voluntarily accepts the gift. Here the other partner is trying to force him to accept it as the price of division; if the distinguished person does not wish to receive it, he may perhaps say that he refuses the gift and cannot be compelled. Shaar Mishpat leaves the matter for study.
