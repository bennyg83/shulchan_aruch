The language of Tur, seifim 13 and 14.
A baraita there, 13a, following Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel as the Gemara explains, 'the case is always as stated,' etc. Rif rules this way because his reasoning is more plausible. Rambam likewise rules so at the end of the cited chapter, and Tur writes in their names.
There, in the name of Rabbenu Chananel. Rosh writes likewise in his name and agrees with the authorities who hold this, because since we rule that the halachah follows the majority against an individual, we cannot decide in favor of the individual's reasoning based on our own logic. The Gemara's general approach follows the first Tanna. Rosh agrees, and the Maggid Mishneh cites this in the name of some authorities.
There, seif 15, in the name of Rabbenu Yonah. The Maggid Mishneh writes that some authorities held this, and that this is Ramban's view.
