There. Maggid Mishneh writes that it was already explained above that whenever they remain partners, they rent the premises and divide the rent.
Maggid Mishneh cites the Gemara there on 13a: according to the view that does not apply "buy or sell," there is division by alternating periods. Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash writes that even though the halachah follows the view that "buy or sell" applies, when neither partner demands it we use division by periods.
Maggid Mishneh likewise writes in the name of Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash, and Rashba likewise rules, that a dwelling is not divided into alternating use for periods shorter than a full year at a time.
Tur, seif 9, in the name of Responsa Rosh, rule 98, siman 4.
The language of Rambam there, law 2. Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's teacher also ruled so and elaborated on the reason, bringing proof from the baraita concerning a bathhouse cited above, involving a poor and wealthy partner, where there is no division by days because both can use it simultaneously.
Maggid Mishneh likewise writes in the name of his teacher and the commentators that since the partners do not agree either to sell or to divide, there is no alternative except division by periods, and each can compel the other to accept it.
