Gimel) Rambam there, halachah 2. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's rule that there is no asmachta where the subject of the transaction is already held by the acquirer follows the view of some commentators who agree to this distinction. He discusses it at length there. The Netivot HaMishpat explains that the reason is learned from Bava Metzia 65b, concerning one who lends money on a field: if the borrower said, "From now," and made a kinyan, the lender acquires even though he had not yet taken possession of the land. Accordingly, whenever a person has taken hold of an item and acquired it through one of the valid modes of acquisition, this is no worse than saying, "Acquire from now." This also appears from Nedarim 27b, where the Gemara asks according to Rav Huna that the case is an asmachta and answers that there the documents of right had already been seized. The later statement that asmachta can be acquired when a kinyan is made does not refer back to that case, but to a case where he says, "I will give," etc.
