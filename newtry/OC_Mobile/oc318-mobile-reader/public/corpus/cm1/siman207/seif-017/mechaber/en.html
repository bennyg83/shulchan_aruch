If two people agree to perform a matter and give earnest objects as a penalty, so that whoever withdraws will forfeit his earnest object to the other, one should not formulate it merely as, 'If I withdraw, I will give you such-and-such, and this object is security for the money I will then owe.' Rather, he must make a kinyan sudar, without asmachta and before an authoritative beit din, creating a legally binding penalty if he withdraws. He may then give the object as security, so that the lien attaches once the penalty obligation arises. Alternatively, he may say, 'If I withdraw, you shall acquire such-and-such monetary value in this specific object.'
