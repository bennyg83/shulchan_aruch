In the article itself. This is not comparable to seif 11, concerning one who gives earnest money, where some disagree and hold that even if the buyer retracts, the seller does not acquire the pledged article itself. There the reason is that, since if the seller retracts he himself does not become liable for the penalty, the buyer likewise is exempt. Sema. The Taz objects that the Beit Yosef in seif 15 cites a responsum of the Rosh in the name of Maharam stating that asmachta applies to a pledge, and adduces as proof the case, "If I do not repay you, collect from this wine." That case is exactly like this one, where he says, "Acquire the article itself." It therefore appears obvious to me that here, when he says, "Acquire the article itself," he means "from now." Tosafot at the beginning of Kiddushin, p. 8, explicitly write that when people customarily make marriage agreements one must be careful to say, "I hereby transfer to you the article itself from now." The wording of the Mordechai likewise implies this. Taz.
