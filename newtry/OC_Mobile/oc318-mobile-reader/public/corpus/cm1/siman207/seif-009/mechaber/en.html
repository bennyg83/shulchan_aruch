If one lends another one hundred gold coins against a field worth more, and says, 'If you do not repay me within three years, the field will be mine,' this is an asmachta and the lender does not acquire. Rama: Some say it is an asmachta even if the field is not worth more. If he says, 'If you do not repay me within three years, it shall be mine from now,' he acquires. Rama: Some say that if, when the mortgage was first created, the lender insisted upon taking only this particular land as security, he acquires it even without the words 'from now.' Likewise, if the borrower says, 'If I do not repay you, you shall have recourse only against this field,' it becomes a designated mortgage, and the borrower cannot remove the lender except by paying money.
