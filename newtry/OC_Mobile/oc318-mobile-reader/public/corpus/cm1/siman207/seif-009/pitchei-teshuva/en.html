The sale at a specified time. Chavot Yair, in the additions at the end of his responsa, writes that although some laws require warning or reminder, where a person voluntarily undertakes a time-limited legal arrangement, no reminder is necessary. For example, one may sell land or movable property while reserving the right to return the money within one year and take the property back, or formulate a similar arrangement. The other party need not remind him, much less formally warn him. Once the period passes, he cannot claim that he forgot, because he voluntarily accepted the deadline, and forgetfulness is not an unavoidable accident.
