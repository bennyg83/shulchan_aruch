Some say, etc. As stated there, “Every ‘if’ does not acquire.” Likewise, “If he said, acquire it for collection, etc., it is an asmachta.” See Tosafos there, s.v. “and if, etc.,” and s.v. “if,” where they explain the case of insistence, etc.
Some say that if, etc. There, Rav Pappa says that if he was particular, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. “if,” who explain accordingly.
Likewise, etc. There, “rather, as an apotiki, etc.” The Raavad and Rashba explain: if he said, “If I do not repay you within three years, you will collect only from this property,” the apotiki takes effect even without “from now,” because regarding an apotiki the condition merely defines the source of collection. Rav Huna said to him, etc., and we rule that whenever the Gemara says one authority “reasoned” and another said “the halachah is,” the halachah follows the express ruling.
