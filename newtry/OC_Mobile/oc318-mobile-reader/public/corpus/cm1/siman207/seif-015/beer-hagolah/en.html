Although every kinyan operates from now and it was already explained that asmachta does not apply where one says 'from now,' the commentators write that an important beit din is required specifically when one deposits his legal rights. In such a case he is not voluntarily transferring them, but the court fixes a deadline to compel him not to evade the proceedings. Therefore they take a kinyan from him and require an important beit din, because this resembles judicial confiscation of property.
