Provided he deposited his rights, etc. The Geonim and Rif understand the Gemara’s concluding “and the halachah is” in Nedarim as referring to the specific incident there. This is why the ruling is placed there rather than on the Mishnah in Get Pashut. An important beis din helps only in a case like that incident, where he deposited his legal rights not voluntarily but because beis din required him to do so. The important beis din can then use hefker beis din, and “from now” is unnecessary. By contrast, in private conditions between litigants, an important beis din adds nothing; only “from now” avoids asmachta, and no important beis din is then necessary. See Responsa Rashba siman 917 and Rivash siman 335.
