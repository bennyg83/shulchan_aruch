They disagreed with him. In siman 60 and at the end of siman 151, Rama writes that we follow the dissenting authorities. This requires study, because in siman 232 he cites only Rambam's view that one who says, "on condition that you have no recourse against me because of a defect," gains nothing because the obligation is unquantified, without citing the dissent. One cannot answer that there is a separate rationale there derived from the laws of overreaching, because Beit Yosef writes that Rambam's main reason is precisely that the obligation is unquantified and cannot be derived from overreaching.
