(Seif 17: “If I retract,” etc.) The Sema, seif katan 20, asks from the dissenting opinion in seif 11 and answers that there the seller, etc. This is difficult, for in responsum 108 of the Rosh, cited by the Beit Yosef in seif 15, the Rosh writes in the name of Maharam that asmachta applies even to a pledge. He proves this from the case where one says, “If I do not pay you, collect from this wine.” That case is precisely of the present type, where he says that the creditor should acquire the body of the object, yet the creditor nevertheless does not acquire it. It appears to me that the answer is simple. Here, when he says, “You shall acquire the body of the object,” he means “from now.” This is also the wording of Tosafot in the first chapter of Kiddushin, page 8: “When people customarily arrange engagements, care must be taken that he say, ‘I hereby transfer to you the body of the object from now.’” The wording of the Mordechai implies the same.
