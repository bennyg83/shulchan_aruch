Transfers ownership. Meaning, he made the acquisition dependent upon the future act, saying in substance, "If you do this, you will acquire," rather than formulating it as a condition upon an acquisition taking effect now, "You acquire now on condition that later you do such and such for me." In the former case it is an asmachta, because whatever is already in the buyer's possession is treated, for this purpose, as being in another's possession. It must be that the item being acquired remains in the acquirer's possession until fulfillment of the condition, for otherwise there would be no valid acquisition at all even absent asmachta, since the kerchief used for the kinyan would already have returned to its owner. The Tur follows his own view that even something in one's own possession can involve asmachta. Taz.
