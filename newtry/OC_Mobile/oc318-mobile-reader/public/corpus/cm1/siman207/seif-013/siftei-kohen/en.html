See the Bach, who attempts to explain why the condition involving the tribes of Gad and Reuven was effective even though it depended upon the will of others. His words are astonishing. He writes that by Torah law an asmachta acquires and brings proof from a guarantor, but perhaps a guarantor is different because of the benefit he receives, as the Gemara states explicitly.
