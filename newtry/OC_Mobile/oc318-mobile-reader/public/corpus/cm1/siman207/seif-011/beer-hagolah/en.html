Rambam, chapter 11 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 4. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is a baraita in HaZahav, 48b. Rabbi Yehudah disputes only a case where one says, 'I will double your deposit,' but where he says, 'my deposit is waived to you,' Torah law effects acquisition. The wording, 'it is enough that he acquire corresponding to his deposit,' supports this. The reason is explained above in seif 3.
