A woman once sent an agent to buy land for her from Shimon, her relative. Shimon told the agent Reuven, 'If I later have money, will my relative return this land to me?' Reuven replied, 'You and she are as close as brother and sister,' meaning that it was likely she would return it and would not object. The Sages ruled that the agent acquired nothing, because the seller did not rely conclusively upon the agent's words. Since the agent gave no definite answer, the seller never fully resolved to transfer the land.
