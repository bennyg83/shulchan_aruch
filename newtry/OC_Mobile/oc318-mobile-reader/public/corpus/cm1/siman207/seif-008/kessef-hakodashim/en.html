Seif 13. If he did not exaggerate the amount. A condition that is not exaggerated is not subject to asmachta, as stated in Shulchan Aruch siman 207, seif 13 in the gloss and seif 16 in the Mechaber, and similarly in Beer HaGolah there. Nevertheless, if one obligated himself to pay a sum if he failed to deliver merchandise by a specified day, and the merchandise later fell in price, that loss may be considered an exaggerated liability if such a decline was unusual. Even if such a decline is common, where the decline caused no actual loss because the merchandise would have been held in any event, it may still be viewed as exaggerated. If the wording 'to deliver by such and such a day' is ambiguous whether it means specifically during the daytime or includes the following night, the obligor should be exempt because of the doubt. Ordinarily, people sometimes refer to the ensuing night as part of the preceding day. Likewise, if he was delayed so that weighing could not be done during that day, and he did not know that weighing would also not be done that night, he may not be included in the obligation.
