Seif 7. If he sold it to him without specification, and the purchaser voluntarily told him, etc. Note: The Beit Yosef, citing the Ritva at the beginning of this siman, makes clear that the purchaser himself must come, and that performance by his sons does not suffice. See my responsum written to the community of Buchach, siman 330, where I concluded that this is so only when the purchaser volunteered the condition. If the seller was the one who stipulated it with him, performance by the purchaser's sons does suffice. See there for my proof.
