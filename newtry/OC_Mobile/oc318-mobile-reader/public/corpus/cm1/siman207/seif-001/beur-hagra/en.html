One who transfers ownership, etc., whether, etc. Bava Metzia 65b; Bava Batra 63a and 137b; and elsewhere. See Beer HaGolah and what was written below, siman 209 seif 8.
Nevertheless some say, etc. Bava Metzia there; see Tosafos, s.v. “mere words, etc.” Some write that the Rambam does not hold this way, as will be explained in seif 7.
But if, etc. Tosafos, because most conditions operate this way, as in the case of one who betroths a woman and says, “on condition that I am a Kohen, etc.” See Even HaEzer siman 38 seif 8.
(Collected note) But if at the outset, etc. From Kiddushin 27a: “on condition that you write, etc.,” even though this condition is for the buyer’s benefit. Ran there in the name of Ramban. (End of note.)
Likewise if, etc. This is a separate matter. Compare the chapter HaMadir: one who betrothed on a condition and then married without restating the condition. Even according to the opinion that she requires a get, Abaye says there that one should not say Rav’s reason is, etc.
