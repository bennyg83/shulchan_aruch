A vow and an oath. See Baer Heitev, through his citation that the Bach disagrees. Responsa Noda BiYehuda, siman 31, writes that with respect to the Sema's ruling that an oath can itself effect the acquisition, no litigant may say, "I rely on the Sema," because the authorities who disagree are numerous, including the Bach, Taz, and Shach, and the plain language of the Rama also supports them. Moreover, the proofs of those who disagree from the Rivash himself appear correct. One cannot plead reliance on a lone opinion, as explained in Knesset HaGedolah. As for the further point cited in the name of Mahari Weil, that perhaps the rule applies only where one gives a formal handshake to that very person, see Responsa Shevut Yaakov II, siman 173. He writes that anyone who studies Mahari Weil will see that Mahari Weil himself did not establish this as the halacha, but used it only as an additional consideration alongside other grounds. The Shach also overlooked that Darkei Moshe in Yoreh De'ah, siman 239, disputes Mahari Weil and writes that the other authorities do not agree. The Rama therefore omitted this position from Shulchan Aruch, and because of the severity of oaths one should not rely on it.
