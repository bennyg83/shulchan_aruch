Ayin) Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 11 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, in the name of Maharam. Many biblical vows were formulated conditionally, such as "If God will be with me" and "If You will indeed deliver this people into my hand," and many similar examples.
Peh) The Bach writes that although beit din compels him to fulfill his oath, they do not descend to his property. Likewise, if he later obtained release from the oath, or if he died, he is not compelled to pay, as the Rama writes in siman 209, seif 4. The Bach further rules concerning one who obligated himself to fulfill a sale both by a solemn handshake and by a monetary penalty: if he pays the penalty, then because he did not state explicitly that the penalty would not discharge the oath, one may say that he intended the penalty to free him from the oath. In my humble opinion this requires study.
