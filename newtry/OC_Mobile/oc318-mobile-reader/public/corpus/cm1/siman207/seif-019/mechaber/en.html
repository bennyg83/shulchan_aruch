A vow, an oath, or a formal handclasp is effective even where the underlying undertaking is an asmachta. Rama: See below, siman 209, seif 4. The same applies if the document states that he accepted the obligation under a severe ban and Torah oath, like the acknowledgment discussed above in seif 15 concerning an authoritative beit din. This is my view, although some disagree. If one vows under an asmachta to give something to charity or consecrated property, the obligation is effective, as explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 258, seif 10.
