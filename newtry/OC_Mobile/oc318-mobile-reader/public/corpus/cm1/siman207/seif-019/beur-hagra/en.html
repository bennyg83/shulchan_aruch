(Supplement) A vow, etc. In the second chapter of Nazir the Mishna states, 'The cow said, etc.' Tosafot explain the expression 'I am a nazir.' The Gemara establishes the case where he said, 'I am a nazir,' and Beit Shammai do not treat the statement's core, etc. Although it is an asmachta, Beit Hillel disagree only because that is not the speaker's principal formulation. According to Rabbi Yehuda, the cow itself becomes the offering. (End of supplement.)
