But if the document states, "This document was made in a manner that contains no asmachta," it is effective, because the party acknowledges that there was no asmachta at all, meaning that it was executed in an important beit din. A litigant's admission is equivalent to one hundred witnesses. This is stronger than the case of one who merely instructed that a promissory note be written, discussed in seif 15.
