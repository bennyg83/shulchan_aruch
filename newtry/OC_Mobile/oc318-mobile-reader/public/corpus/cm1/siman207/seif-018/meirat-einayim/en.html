This does not remove it from the category of asmachta, etc. The Tur writes: since we see that the undertaking is in fact an asmachta, what does he accomplish by writing that it is "not as an asmachta"?
Some disagree, etc. Their reason is that by writing in the document that the undertaking is not an asmachta, he reveals that he fully resolves to transfer the property. See Darkei Moshe.
