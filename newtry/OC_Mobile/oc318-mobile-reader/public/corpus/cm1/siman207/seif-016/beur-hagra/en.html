When they were, etc. This is stated for completeness, because according to the Rambam a waiver does not constitute asmachta, and certainly not when made “from now.”
And the manner, etc. This excludes the requirement of an important beis din except where legal rights were deposited with beis din, as explained above according to the Geonim.
There are places. These are the Rambam’s words, though here they concern reciprocal penalties. See Even HaEzer siman 50 seif 6 in the Shulchan Aruch.
Some say, etc., and likewise, etc. Tosafos, Bava Metzia 46a, compares this to “if I leave the field fallow and do not work it, I will pay with the best,” which is not exaggerated. Here too the amount is reasonably related to the undertaking. See Rosh in Nedarim there.
Nevertheless, etc. Even the obligation for leaving a field fallow is ordinarily written in a document, but according to Tosafos there, s.v. “it was,” customary practice itself can function like a kinyan. The same applies here where such stipulations are customary.
