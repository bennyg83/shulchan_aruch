And there are places, etc. See Even HaEzer 50:6 in the gloss; Responsa Mabit, part 2, siman 78; and Responsa Rabbi Shmuel Kohen, part 2, siman 63, and part 3, siman 54.
Nevertheless, an act of acquisition is required, etc. This follows what is written in Darkei Moshe and Beit Yosef, seif 21. Yet in the chapter Eizehu Neshekh, when the Mordechai records Rabbeinu Tam's view, he writes that the same applies to betrothal arrangements: although there is no eminent beit din and he did not say 'from now,' once they made an acquisition, etc. In my opinion, however, all agree that in betrothal arrangements no acquisition is required. This is explicit from Tosafot and the poskim, who explain that the penalty is imposed so that one who withdraws will be liable for the shame he caused, comparable to the rule, 'If I leave the field fallow, I will pay from the best.' Hagahot Mordechai to Bava Metzia likewise states explicitly in the name of the Semag regarding a teacher that even without an acquisition he is liable for the penalty, and even one who requires an acquisition for gambling concedes here. Responsa Rosh also provides no proof to the contrary. Examine carefully.
And no teacher was found, etc. This means after the teacher withdrew. See Responsa Maharam Alshich, end of siman 39.
