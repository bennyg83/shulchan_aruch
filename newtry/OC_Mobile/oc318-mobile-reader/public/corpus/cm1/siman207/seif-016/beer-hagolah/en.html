Zayin) There, halachah 18. The Maggid Mishneh writes that from here one learns that Rambam and those sages hold that asmachta applies even where a kinyan was made. For that reason the condition had to be formulated as a waiver, as explained at the beginning of the chapter and above at the beginning of this siman. The Kesef Mishneh questions this, since Rambam wrote in this chapter, as explained above at the beginning of the siman and in seif 14, that a kinyan made "from now" is not subject to asmachta. He explains instead that they could not transfer currency directly because money is not acquired through chalipin, and since most people transact by fixed sums of money, they would first make a kinyan obligating one party to owe the other a certain sum, and afterward make a second kinyan from the other party, etc.
Chet) So writes the Beit Yosef in Even HaEzer, siman 50, seif 7.
Tet) The Tur, seif 11, in the name of his father the Rosh. Tosafot likewise write this in Bava Metzia 66a. The Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 11 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, cite the Sefer Mitzvot Gadol, Rashi, and Raavyah.
See siman 243, seif 7 in the gloss, and Even HaEzer siman 50 in the gloss.
Yod) The Beit Yosef there in the name of Tosafot, the Mordekhai in HaMekabel in the name of Or Zarua, the chapter Zeh Borer, the Hagahot on chapter 8 of Hilkhot Sekhirut and chapter 11 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, and other poskim.
