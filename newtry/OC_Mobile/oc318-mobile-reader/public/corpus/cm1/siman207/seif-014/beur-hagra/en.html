Some say that “from now,” etc. Every ordinary kinyan takes effect from now, as stated at the end of Nedarim chapter 5, yet in Sanhedrin chapter 3 an asmachta still requires an important beis din. The case of lending on one’s field is different. See Tosafos, Bava Metzia 66a, s.v. “and Minyomi,” where Ri explains, etc., and Ran in Nedarim. There is no proof from the end of Nedarim, for ordinary kinyan indeed takes effect immediately, but a formulation such as “if I do not come” does not mean “from now”; it takes effect only after the specified day.
Some say, etc. As stated in Gittin chapter 7 and Kiddushin chapters 1 and 3.
Some disagree, etc. Only where the condition requires performance of an act do we say it is equivalent to “from now,” because the legal act takes effect immediately and the condition is subsequently fulfilled, as in Kiddushin and Gittin. This does not apply to a penalty. See Sema.
