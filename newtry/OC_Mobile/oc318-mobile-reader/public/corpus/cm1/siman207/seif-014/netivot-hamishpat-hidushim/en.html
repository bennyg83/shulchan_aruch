Before an eminent beit din and from now. According to the Sema, "from now" is not literal here. An eminent beit din alone is sufficient, as indicated below in seif 15, where the Mechaber mentions only an eminent beit din and Rama does not gloss. The purpose here is merely to exclude a case where he expressly says that it should not take effect from now. There is a scribal error in the Sema, and the text should read that it excludes where he said explicitly, "not from now." Alternatively, Rama refers back to the Mechaber's statement that "from now" alone is effective and clarifies that "from now" alone is not sufficient unless there is also an eminent beit din. In seif 9, where one lent money upon a field, everyone agrees that "from now" alone suffices because the lender gave him the benefit of the loan and the property also initially came into the lender's hand to some extent as an acquisition. Sema. Taz disagrees and requires both "from now" and an eminent beit din. See Beurim: with other conditions everyone agrees that "from now" alone is sufficient. Only with a condition in which he subjects himself does this opinion require both an eminent beit din and "from now."
