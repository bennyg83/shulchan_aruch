"He who has given his name is," he said
And likewise the seller or the purchaser. See the Sema, seif katan 11, for his explanation of this seif. The Bach explains that the original acquisition was made without qualification, and afterward they made a further acquisition from the purchaser obligating him to return the property, as stated later in this siman. The Kesef Mishneh writes likewise.
"And he did not give him a look at him," he said, "and he should look at the heart of his father."
