There, law 5. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the Mishnah in the chapter Get Pashut, Bava Batra 168a, and we rule like Rabbi Yehudah as decided in the Gemara. The reason is that he made the result depend upon return of the document, which is not in the lender's hand but with a third party. The Maggid Mishneh suggests that according to Rambam, if the document were in the lender's possession and the borrower said, 'if I do not repay you,' etc., the lender could collect the entire amount, since both the document and the money are in his possession. This is why the Mishnah specifically speaks of placing it with a third party. See above at the beginning of siman 55.
