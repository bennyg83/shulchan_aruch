“And thou shalt be thrust down to him, and shalt be thrust down to hell, and shalt be thrust down to him, and he shall be thrust down to him.”
This is an asmachta. Even though he stipulated that the money should become a gift, and Rambam holds that asmachta does not apply to something entirely within his control, this case is different because he made the matter depend upon return of the document, which was in the shalish's hand. If instead he gave the document into the lender's own hand and said that in the stated event the money should become a gift, then according to Rambam there would be no asmachta. Shach.
