And similarly, if he sent. See Baer Heitev, through its statement that since at the outset the recipient acquired the funds for the designated person, he can no longer retain them for himself. Dagul MeRevavah writes that this requires study, because if so, even a migo that he could have denied the transaction entirely should not help him, since he admits that he already received the funds and acquired them for the designated person. See siman 125, Shach, seif katan 51. See Tumim and Netivot HaMishpat concerning this, and Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 51, which discusses the matter at length.
