Compensation for lost labor and medical expenses belong to her husband. The Tur explains why compensation for lost labor belongs to the husband: since her handiwork belongs to him, her injury causes him to lose her labor. According to this, if she is a woman who has no obligation to work except to sit in dignity and perform only a small amount of work so that idleness not lead her to immorality, as explained above in siman 80, it would seem that not all of the compensation for lost labor should belong to the husband. However, I already wrote in siman 80 that Rashba holds that even where she is not obligated to work, if she does work her handiwork belongs to her husband. Thus, with every woman, the compensation for lost labor belongs to the husband, because he can say, "I would have persuaded my wife to work throughout the day." How compensation for lost labor is assessed where bodily damage also exists is explained here in the Tur and in Choshen Mishpat 420.
