If the borrower... This is clear from the incident of "for the side," where the creditor needed a migo only because he did not admit the second loan. It is likewise clear there on 91b in the incident involving the small parcel of land, and from the statement, "we said this only..." The reason there is specifically that they are not obligated to pay. See Rashi there, s.v. "the money..." which says it is like any other sale. But in a case of this kind, even if he says otherwise, the law is as stated here. This is the meaning of "and even if he expressly says..."
And even if he remained silent... As stated in Kiddushin 13a, where one says to a woman, "Take..." and she did not wish to accept it, while if she remained silent, etc. See Terumas HaDeshen, siman 37, and siman 103.
And likewise if... As stated in Bava Metzia 9b: where one was riding, etc., he acquires it.
