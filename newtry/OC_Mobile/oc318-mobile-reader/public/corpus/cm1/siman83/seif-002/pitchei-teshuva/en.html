And likewise if he sent it. See Baer Heitev through the statement that once he initially acquired the money on behalf of the other person, he can no longer retain it for himself. Dagul MeRevavah writes that this requires study. If so, even a migo that he could have denied the matter entirely should not help him, because he admits that he already received the money and acquired it on behalf of that person. See Shach, siman 125, seif katan 51. See also Tumim and Netivot HaMishpat on this point, and Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 51, which discusses the matter at length.
