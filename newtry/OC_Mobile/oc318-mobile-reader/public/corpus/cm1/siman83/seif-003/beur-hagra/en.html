He is believed... See the Sema.
And the guarantor is exempt. See the Sema, and as stated in Bechoros 48a.
If he did not repay... Even if he denied the second loan, and that second loan was oral, he would be believed, as stated in Kesubos there in the incident of "for the side."
And even if... Only in the case of "for the side" did he need a migo, because the matter was not known and the claim was not strong. That is not so here. See the Rosh in chapter HaDayanim, 42a, siman 19: "This too is no proof, because although..." and "and if you ask..." One must say that the reason there is, etc., which does not apply here. Therefore the Tur resolves here the uncertainty raised by Sefer HaTerumos in accordance with his own approach, because in siman 58 the Tur rules like the Rosh there.
