[Shach, letter 5] One may answer that this case is different, since the lender admits. Note: The difficulty remains, for the lender should be believed against the guarantor by means of a migo that he could have said, “It never happened.” Since the lender asserts a definite claim and the guarantor only an uncertain one, we apply a migo even to extract money, as the Shach writes in the rules of migo, letter 15.
