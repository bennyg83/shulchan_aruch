From the incident before Rabbah and Rav Yosef. The halachah there was ruled like Rav Yosef, that this document is merely a shard and the money remains where it currently is, Bava Batra 32a.
The Rambam likewise explains this in chapter 14 of Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh, and so writes the Tur. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this also appears to be the view of the Geonim, and Rabbeinu Yonah likewise rules so. As for why the holder is not believed through a migo that he could have claimed the document was completely valid, Tosafot there write in the name of Rivam that we do not use a migo to extract money. This is explained in the preceding siman, seif 12. Tosafot there write other reasons in the name of Rabbeinu Yitzchak.
