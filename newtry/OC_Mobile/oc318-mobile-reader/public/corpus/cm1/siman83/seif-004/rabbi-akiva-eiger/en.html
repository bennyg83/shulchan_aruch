[Shach, letter 7] The words of the Sema are astonishing. Note: In my humble opinion, since we explicitly see that Rabbi Yosef’s reason is that there is no migo where the claimant must first make a demonstrably false statement, the straightforward explanation is that when we say the halachah follows Rabbi Yosef with respect to money, we mean that it follows him for his stated reason. Why should we say that the halachah follows Rabbi Yosef but not for his reason, and instead only because this would be a migo used to extract money? Tosafot were compelled to explain, in accordance with the Rashba, that Rav Adda’s distinction between land and money reflects uncertainty whether the halachah follows Rav or Rabbi Yosef. Alternatively, because we rule like Rabbah, they were compelled to say that where one must first lie, there is nevertheless a migo, or that the ruling like Rabbi Yosef regarding money is a legal conclusion without adopting his reason, but rather because it is a migo used to extract money. Once so, according to the dissenting authorities who hold that a migo may be used to extract money, one must explain the distinction between land and money as a doubt whether the halachah follows Rabbah or Rabbi Yosef. This is parallel to the Shach’s explanation that those dissenting authorities follow the Ramban and speak of a document that has not been authenticated. Alternatively, the distinction between land and money reflects a doubt whether the halachah follows Rabbah, that even with an unauthenticated document there is a migo, or Rabbi Yosef, that with an unauthenticated document there is no migo, as the Shach explicitly writes at the end of seif katan 9. If so, one may likewise say that the dissenting authorities accept Rabbi Yosef’s reason, namely that where one must first lie, there is no migo, and Rav Adda distinguishes between land and money because of a doubt whether the halachah follows Rav or Rabbi Yosef regarding whether one who must first lie has a migo. When the Sema writes that Tosafot say so, he means that Tosafot identify this as Rabbi Yosef’s reason. Since according to the dissenting authorities it is clear that the practical halachah remains in doubt between Rav and Rabbi Yosef, for otherwise there would be no basis to distinguish land from money, the ruling regarding money is unfavorable because perhaps the halachah follows Rabbi Yosef, under whose view one who must first lie has no migo. The Sema’s words are therefore correct, just as according to the Shach there is a doubt regarding an unauthenticated document. So too, according to the Sema, there is a doubt whether one who must first lie has a migo. Examine this carefully.
