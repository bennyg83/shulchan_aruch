And the ruler seized it. The Shach in siman 388 writes that if one returned collateral under compulsion he is liable, because physically handing it over is treated as direct handling and liability can apply even under compulsion. See Beurim: this applies specifically where Reuven originally received the collateral into his house without Shimon's consent. If he became a custodian of the collateral with Shimon's consent, he is exempt, because an ordinary custodian is exempt for unavoidable accident even where he physically hands over the item under the circumstances explained in siman 292.
