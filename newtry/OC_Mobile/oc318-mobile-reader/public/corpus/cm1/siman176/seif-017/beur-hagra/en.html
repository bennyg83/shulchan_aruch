If there was a fixed time. Gittin 31b.
Collection: If there was a fixed time... Bava Metzia 105a, according to Rif's explanation that it concerns partners who entered partnership without specifying a term, and the like. Tosafos likewise rule in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel in Gittin 31b, s.v. "to my partner..." Rashi explains it as a case where they fixed a term. This is what is stated in seif 15, "the partners..." See the Rosh there. End of collection.
