Seif 14. Note: If one partner sells his fellow's share without permission, may the buyer claim that perhaps the partner had authority? See below, siman 185, seif 5, where two views are recorded. In a responsum concerning Zvihel I concluded that with movables, where the buyer is in possession, all would agree that he may raise this claim. With land he may not, because land remains in the presumptive possession of its owner. A form of possession treated in our times as movable property has the law of movables. If it is customary for one partner to sell on behalf of both without specific consultation, his act is valid even without the other's knowledge, as the Beit Shmuel writes in Even HaEzer siman 86.
