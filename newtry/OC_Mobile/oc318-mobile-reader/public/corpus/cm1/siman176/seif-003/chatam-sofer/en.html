Seif 12, "and similar matters." Note: if they initially stipulated that all profit accruing to them because of the capital would be divided, this excludes only finds and similar windfalls. But if they earned profit through brokerage work that consumes time and detracts from the partnership's business, the profit is shared equally. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 168, and Responsa Pnei Moshe, part III, siman 14.
