There: If he performed meshichah. Meshichah is not literal in every case, for an object ordinarily lifted must be acquired by lifting, as stated in siman 198. I do not understand the Sema's assertion that this is like exchange. Exchange means using one object to acquire another, whereas drawing coins in order to acquire those same coins can certainly be effective. The gloss explains that the Sema meant that each party's drawing the other's coins cannot transfer ownership, because it is merely exchanging positions. Whether the black maneh belonged to Reuven and the white to Shimon before the act, or the reverse afterward, neither has acquired a claim against the other. The printed word in the Sema should therefore read “by exchange,” and such an act is ineffective.
