And some say that even a formal act of acquisition is unnecessary, etc. This plainly applies also to the beginning of the siman, concerning other partners. The law is stated here regarding craftsmen because their arrangement concerns something not yet in existence; all the more so does it apply to ordinary partners who contribute funds to a common purse. This is explicit in the Beit Yosef in the name of the authorities who adopt this view, namely the Ittur, Mordechai, Hagahot Maimoniyot, and those who follow them. So I found written. See Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 106, 114, 153, 154, 177, 395, and 413.
