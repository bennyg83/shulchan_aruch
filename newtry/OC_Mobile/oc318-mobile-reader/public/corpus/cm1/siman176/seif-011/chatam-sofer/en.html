Ibid., Sema, seif katan 1, "but in Ramban." Note: in the Responsa attributed to the Ramban, siman 21, he writes: "This law applies only to brothers who are partners." This implies that he comes only to exclude brothers who are not partners, while ordinary partners are plainly governed by this law, as the Sema states. In the Beit Yosef, however, the text was mistakenly printed in reverse, "only in a partnership of brothers," which would imply exclusion of partners who are not brothers. That reading is incorrect.
