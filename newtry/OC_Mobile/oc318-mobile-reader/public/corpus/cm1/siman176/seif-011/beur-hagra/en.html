(Supplement) Therefore, etc.; for commerce. In siman 183, seif 5, he writes, 'whether for commerce or for consumption,' and both formulations are Rambam's and Rif's. Their reason is that they rule like the scholars of the West, for Rabbi Abba reconciles the mishna in Arakhin according to their view. The Gemara likewise asks, 'Shall we say it disputes the scholars of the West?' They explain that this limitation applies only to a partner, whereas regarding an agent the agent's hand is always like the principal's hand. Therefore, the Gemara does not challenge Rabbi Yehuda from the mishna concerning the principal's acquisition of the improvement in the wool. Rosh and the other authorities, however, rule unlike the scholars of the West. Accordingly, Rosh, Tur, and the other authorities hold that even regarding a partner the rule applies both to commerce and consumption. Rama rules this way in siman 183, seif 5. This is why Rama writes here, 'See below, siman 183, seif 5.' (End of supplement.)
