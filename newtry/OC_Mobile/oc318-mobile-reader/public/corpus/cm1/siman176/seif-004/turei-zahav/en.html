There: They entered into partnership and began. The Sema explains that they mixed the money. It appears to me that the Beit Yosef's citation from the Ittur in the name of the Rif, requiring the money to be mixed, applies where they had not yet begun commercial activity. Here, however, they already began buying and selling. The essential act creating the partnership is their commencement of business, each acting for the partnership's benefit. The Beit Yosef therefore cites the Rivash that they are regarded as hired workers for one another and require no further acquisition once they begin the work.
