Seif 18: He accomplished nothing. The Beit Yosef compares Rabbeinu's ruling to one who was given money to buy wheat and bought barley instead. The cases are not comparable. In the wheat and barley case, there is no proof that the agent acted from a mistaken legal assumption. He may have thought that if the principal wished to share in the barley transaction, well and good, and otherwise he would keep it himself. Here, by leaving half the money idle, his conduct can only be explained by his mistaken belief that the division was legally effective. The Sema's explanation is therefore entirely incorrect.
