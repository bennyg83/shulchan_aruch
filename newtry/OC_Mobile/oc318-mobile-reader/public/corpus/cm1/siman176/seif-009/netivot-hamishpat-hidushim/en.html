The same applies to ordinary clothing of wives and children, because they do not ordinarily come before beit din over such matters and therefore certainly waive against one another. But Shabbos clothing can be sent to beit din and therefore is not presumed waived. Sema:
Clothing for their wives. See the Shach, who leaves unresolved whether Shabbos clothing is appraised against the oldest brother. One can say that if the reason Shabbos clothing is appraised for minors is that it can be brought before beit din, whereas clothing of the oldest brother is not appraised so that the other brothers will listen to him, then Shabbos clothing of the oldest brother should likewise not be appraised. But if the reason it is appraised for minors is that people do not ordinarily intend to transfer ownership of such expensive clothing, then even the oldest brother's Shabbos clothing should be appraised:
