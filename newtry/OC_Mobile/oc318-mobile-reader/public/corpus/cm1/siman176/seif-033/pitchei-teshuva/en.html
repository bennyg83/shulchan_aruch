If one of them died, etc. The other shall swear. In Nachalat Tzvi I cited a great authority who argued that the Shulchan Aruch contains a scribal error and that the word "not" is missing, so that it should read, "the other shall not swear," based on the Baal HaItur, source of this ruling. In my view this is not correct. The text before us in the Shulchan Aruch, requiring the oath, is the principal reading, as I explain there at length.
