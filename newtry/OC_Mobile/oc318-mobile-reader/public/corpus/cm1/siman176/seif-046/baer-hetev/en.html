The others. Some editions of the Shulchan Aruch read "the brothers," and the Levush has the same reading. This is an erroneous version and must read "the others," as here. The substantive ruling is also incorrect, as I proved in siman 62, seif katan 17 and 19. Only with promissory notes, since they are written in his name, is there an indication that they belong to him. With ordinary money, however, we do not advance on behalf of orphans a claim that their father himself could not have made. This also appears from the Sema there. See Responsa Mahara Sasson, simanim 134, 135, and 136. Shach.
