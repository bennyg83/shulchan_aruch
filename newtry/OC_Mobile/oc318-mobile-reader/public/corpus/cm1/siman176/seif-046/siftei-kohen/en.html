(He who is not a quaint.) In the house check:
The others must bring proof. Some editions of the Mechaber read, 'the brothers must bring proof,' and the Ir Shushan has the same reading, writing that if he died and they have litigation with his sons, the brothers must bring proof. This is an erroneous version and must read 'the others,' as it does here. The stated law is also incorrect, as I demonstrated above in siman 62, seif katan 17. Only with respect to documents, because they are written in his name, is there an indication that they belong to him. With respect to money, however, we do not advance for orphans a claim that their father himself could not have advanced. This also appears from Sema there, seif katan 8. See there.
