If he said, "I am rescuing this for myself," etc. The Sema says that in all these cases he must make the statement before three people. This does not appear correct. The authorities imply that even a private declaration to himself is sufficient. This is explicit in a responsum of the Rashba cited by the Beit Yosef in newly collected responsum 19: if he received payment before witnesses and said before them, "I am rescuing it for myself," he acquires it; if he received it privately, he is believed to say that he rescued it for himself through a migo that he could have said he received nothing at all. No proof can be brought from seif 18 concerning a partner's division, because there the money is known not to be equal, unlike here.
