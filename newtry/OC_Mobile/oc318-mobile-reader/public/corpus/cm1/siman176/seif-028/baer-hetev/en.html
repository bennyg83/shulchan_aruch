A non-Jew. The same law applies when they have a debt owed to them by a Jew. So writes the Tur in the name of the Rosh. Sema.
Salvages. It appears that in all these cases as well he must make this declaration before three people. Sema. The Shach writes that this does not appear correct. The implication of the poskim is that even a declaration made privately is sufficient. This is also explicit in the Rashba's responsum cited by the Beit Yosef in section 19: If he received payment before witnesses and said before them, I am salvaging this for myself, he acquires it. If he received it privately, he is believed to say that he salvaged it for himself, through a migo that he could have said he received nothing at all. The ruling in seif 18 concerning a partner's division is no proof to the contrary, because there the money is known not to be equal, unlike here. Shach. The Taz likewise challenges the Sema on this point. See there.
