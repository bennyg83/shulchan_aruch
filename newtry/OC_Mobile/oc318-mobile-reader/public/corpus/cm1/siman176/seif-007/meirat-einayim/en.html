They divide according to the money. Since the money itself did not change from what it originally was, it is treated as though it merely increased or decreased in value while still intact. The preceding seif already explained that in such a case division is according to each person's investment.
And he shall have one third of the profit, and they acted accordingly. Meaning that they collected the debt with Shimon's assistance. For example, the debt was four gold coins and accrued three gold coins of interest, the interest being the profit. Shimon therefore had one third of the profit from that debt and Reuven two thirds of the profit, while the principal of the debt itself belonged to Reuven. Nevertheless, whatever they later earned from the merchandise they bought and sold together is divided equally, like two people who placed into a common purse, one contributing one hundred and the other two hundred.
