Seif 6: We do not say that he must pay from his own funds. In siman 93 Rabbeinu cites the Rambam, whose words imply that the partner must personally cover the loss, although Reuven is not believed to extract that amount merely by his oath. The Mechaber rules accordingly there in seif 13. Here, the Rema's view appears to disagree, and the Beit Yosef likewise cites a responsum attributed to the Ramban. There is no contradiction in the Mechaber's rulings. In siman 93 his purpose was only to teach that an oath cannot itself extract money. Here, where the substantive liability is at issue, he records the Rema's view concerning the obligation. I have seen a proposed distinction, but it has no basis. See what I wrote in siman 93.
