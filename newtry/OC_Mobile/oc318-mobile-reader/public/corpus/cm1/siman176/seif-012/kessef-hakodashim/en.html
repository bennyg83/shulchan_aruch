And afterward an accusation arose. In siman 176 it is written that an accusation that follows the law of the kingdom or the legal rules binding on non-Jews may fall upon the partner who accepted the arrangement. If one sold on credit to a non-Jew what he himself had purchased on credit, and the non-Jew later paid with movable property, and thereafter the authorities took back that property from the original creditor because that is their law, it requires study whether this loss must be borne by the one who extended the credit.
