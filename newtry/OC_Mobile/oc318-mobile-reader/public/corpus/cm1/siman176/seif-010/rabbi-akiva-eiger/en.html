[Shulchan Aruch] And he agreed to his actions. Note: Mishneh LaMelech cites numerous responsa of the Geonim who dispute whether silence is considered an admission of consent. Maharit, part 2, siman 112, writes that if he responded, "What you have done is already done," this may not constitute consent, for he may mean only: "Since you have already acted, there is nothing to answer, because after the fact nothing can be changed. What you did is effective to my benefit, but not to my detriment." See there, and Responsa Bnei Aharon, simanim 40 and 112.
