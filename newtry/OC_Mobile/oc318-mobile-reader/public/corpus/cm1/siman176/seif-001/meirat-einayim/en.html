Rather, they joined in partnership and began, etc. In the Perishah I explained that this means they formed the partnership by placing their property together and commingling it, and then began doing business. In such a case the partnership is effective even to prevent either one from retracting with respect to the future until the time they initially fixed between themselves. This differs from a case where they did not place and commingle their money together, but merely began by each purchasing separately with his own money. That does not prevent either one from retracting with respect to future activity. See there in the Derishah and in my novellae, where I wrote four approaches to these laws.
