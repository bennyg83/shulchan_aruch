The partners. Bava Batra 42b: "partners are paid bailees..." See Bava Metzia 99 and Bava Kamma 79, where Rabbi Elazar says, "just as they instituted..." through "rental of land." See Tosafos there. Accordingly, the rule that partners may not retract follows the law of purchasers.
The currency. Bava Metzia 46a.
And they wrote. Bava Metzia and Bava Kamma there: movable property is not ordinarily subject to a written deed.
And they place money. Rambam holds that they must specifically place it into one purse and lift it, and the Rif likewise wrote in a responsum. See Bach, Derishah, and Perishah.
And they place. Kesubos 93a.
And they lift. Bava Batra 86a. It is also stated in the third chapter of Kesubos, and we learn from the case of one who steals a purse on Shabbos, etc., if he dragged it, etc.
And some say. This is the Tur's view, and so it appears. See Bach and Derishah. The Tur holds that neither placing the money together nor both parties lifting it is required.
And even... and they began. Bava Metzia 99a: Rav Huna said, one who borrows an axe, etc.
One who lent. Bava Kamma 93a: "and a contradiction was raised, 'to guard'..." We see that whenever property entered his possession under the law of guarding, he is not released merely by a verbal statement.
