How a partnership is established, how the partners conduct themselves, how they separate, and what happens if one dies. It contains fifty-one seifim.
When people wish to become partners, the partnership is not completed by words alone. Thus, merely saying, 'Let us enter a partnership on such-and-such terms,' does not prevent either from withdrawing. It is established only through an act of acquisition, and each type of property requires the acquisition appropriate to it. Therefore, because coin cannot be acquired by barter, a partnership in money is not established by a kinyan. Even if each made a formal kinyan obligating himself to bring his money, and they wrote a document and had witnesses sign it, this is ineffective. Rather, each must bring his money, place both sums into one purse, and they must lift the purse together. Some say it is also sufficient for each to draw the other's money into his possession. Some further say that even if neither was done, once they joined together and began buying or selling in the partnership business, the partnership is established. Rama: If one lent money to another and later told him, 'Keep it as an investment with profits divided equally,' and the money was lost through an unavoidable accident, the borrower remains liable for all accidents as before, because his original responsibility is not removed by words alone.
