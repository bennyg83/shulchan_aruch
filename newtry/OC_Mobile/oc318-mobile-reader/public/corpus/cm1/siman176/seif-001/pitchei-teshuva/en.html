Rather, they entered into partnership and began, etc. Baer Heitev refers to the Taz. The Taz disagrees with the Sema and holds that even if they did not first combine their money, once each purchased with his own money for purposes of the partnership he can no longer withdraw even prospectively. Netivot HaMishpat cites both views without deciding between them. Shivat Tzion, responsum 107, considers whether both partners must actually begin dealing or whether the activity of one suffices, and concludes that both must begin. If only one began, then even if the funds had already been combined, the partnership is effective only with respect to what was already purchased, in which both acquire rights, but not prospectively, and either may withdraw for the future.
