If a ruler owes money to Reuven and Shimon, and Shimon accepted responsibility for collection without Reuven's participation, but Shimon lives under that ruler's authority and cannot safely press the claim, Reuven cannot require him to provoke his ruler in order to collect Reuven's share. If Shimon receives a tax exemption because of the debt, however, the amount of annual tax he would otherwise have paid is appraised, and he must give Reuven the proportion corresponding to Reuven's share in the debt.
