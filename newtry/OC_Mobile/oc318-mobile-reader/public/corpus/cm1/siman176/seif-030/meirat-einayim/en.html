Or Shimon released Levi, etc. The text deliberately says "or Shimon released Levi," not that both Reuven released Shimon and Shimon released Levi. If Reuven released Shimon from an oath and Shimon released Levi, Reuven cannot then require Levi to swear. This is evident from the Baal HaItur cited by the Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe. At first glance, the reason is that the Baal HaItur holds that Shimon, as partner, was authorized to enter a further partnership with Levi whenever he saw this as beneficial to the partnership. Reuven's only claim against Levi is therefore the same partnership oath he would have had against Shimon, and Shimon cannot release Levi from that oath so as to improve Levi's position against Reuven beyond Shimon's own position. But if Reuven first released Shimon from the oath, Shimon can likewise release Levi. This differs from a deposit or pledge that Reuven placed with Shimon and Shimon then deposited or pledged with Levi and it was lost. There Reuven can say: Shimon is trustworthy to me upon oath, but Levi is not, as explained in simanim 72 and 291. In a partnership, however, Shimon can say that he acted for the partnership's benefit. The Baal HaItur himself explains that Reuven may require Levi to swear and Levi cannot say, "You are not my litigant," because Reuven may answer: This is my money, and Shimon acted as my agent. Thus Shimon's delivery to Levi is treated as Reuven's agency. There is a difficulty because the Rambam, Tur, and Mechaber rule earlier in this siman that one who enters a partnership without specification may not bring another person into the partnership. It cannot simply be that the authorities dispute one another, because the Mechaber rules earlier like the Rambam and Tur and here like the Baal HaItur. We must therefore say that even though Shimon was not entitled initially to enter the partnership with another, once he did so Reuven takes half the profit and may also require Levi to swear. Once Reuven accepts Shimon's act by taking half the profit, then if Reuven released Shimon from the oath and Shimon released Levi, Levi is also released even though Shimon initially acted improperly by joining Levi to the partnership.
