An oath for one of them. See Beurim: even if Reuven wishes to add matters by extension of the oath against Levi, he cannot do so. Once Levi was exempted from the principal oath, he is also exempt from matters added by extension. This applies even if Reuven was not in the city when Levi swore.
Or perhaps Shimon. The Sema holds that if Reuven released Shimon from an oath and Shimon then released Levi, the release is effective. The Shach disagrees. The Taz writes that if Shimon informed Levi that he was a partner with Reuven, then even where Reuven released Shimon and Shimon released Levi, Reuven may still make Levi swear. If Levi was not informed of the partnership, then even if Reuven never released Shimon, once Shimon released Levi, Levi is also exempt against Reuven. It is possible that the Shach agrees with the Taz on this distinction.
