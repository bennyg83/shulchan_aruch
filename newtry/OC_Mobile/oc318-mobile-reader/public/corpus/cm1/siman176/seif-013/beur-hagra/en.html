(Supplement) For he says to him, etc. The Yerushalmi at the beginning of the third chapter of Bava Kamma discusses an ox that climbed upon another ox and the owner of the lower ox came to pull his animal away, etc. The other cannot say, 'Had you left it, I would have paid your damages,' etc. Consider the case where it was a forewarned ox: he may answer, 'You do not have the right to force me to pursue you through the courts.' (End of supplement.)
