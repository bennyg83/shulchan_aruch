A debt owed by others. See the Beurim. This applies specifically to debts whose due date has already passed. If payment is not yet due, the partners cannot divide the debts. There is no distinction between oral and written debts. No separate kinyan or ma'amad shelashtan is needed when the division is made by appraisal before beit din. Reuven and Shimon who received non Jewish debts as an iska and the debts later lost value need pay only according to what those debts were worth when received. Sema. He transacted. This applies specifically where they took the loan during the partnership term. Since they fixed the repayment date after the partnership's original termination date, it is as though they expressly extended the partnership until the loan falls due. But if they borrowed the money at the very beginning when the partnership term was fixed, then even if the repayment date extends beyond the partnership term, neither can prevent division once the originally fixed partnership term arrives.
