The partnership is dissolved, etc. A similar law is stated below in siman 329 regarding one who accepts a field from another for a fixed term. This differs from a borrower who borrowed a cow for a fixed term and died, whose heirs need not return it until the borrowing period ends, as the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 341:3. There the borrower receives all the benefit, so his sons inherit the right he possessed. In a partnership both parties receive equal benefit. Therefore when one dies, the other can say, 'I partnered with your father because I knew he was skilled in commerce,' or for some other personal reason, 'but I did not agree to partner with you.' It appears that the heirs likewise may retract and say, 'Our father partnered with you, but we are not comfortable continuing with you.' See the Perishah.
