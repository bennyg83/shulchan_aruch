The community took the promissory notes for taxes, etc. The responsum of the Rosh explains that the notes were in Reuven's possession and the community seized them from him. The Tur quotes the responsum at the end of siman 128, and Darkei Moshe cites it here. It also appears that the notes were written in Reuven's name alone, although the funds belonged to the partnership and Shimon owned half of them.
Reuven bought merchandise and left it with Shimon, etc. See responsum of the Rosh, klal 89, siman 13, concerning Reuven who entered a tax farming partnership in which Shimon was to receive one quarter, and afterward joined Levi, giving Levi one sixth while telling him that Shimon too had only one sixth, and Levi conveyed the same to Shimon. The Rosh nevertheless ruled that Shimon must receive his original one quarter. See there at length.
