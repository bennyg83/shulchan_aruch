He wishes to return. The case is one where Yehudah cannot prevent Shimon and Levi from withdrawing, for example where Reuven formed a separate partnership with each of them individually and not with all of them collectively. Beit Yosef citing the Tur:
Where many people are partners together, one cannot withdraw. This means he cannot merely sell his share to the remaining partners. See the Beurim. Even if one of the partners agrees to assume the withdrawing partner's potential loss, withdrawal is still unavailable because the number of people actively managing the business has decreased. But he may divide off his own merchandise and take it home when the conditions of seif 18 are met:
At the time he transports it. The reason is that the recipient cannot sell it earlier, so the business effectively begins only when he transports it, whether the merchandise rose or fell in value. Even if a fixed amount was stated when he received it, the merchandise is valued according to its worth at the time he transports it. Sema. See the Beurim. The reason is that it is forbidden to calculate it above its value because of interest. Such a transaction is like a sale made in a prohibited manner, where the sale remains valid at the permitted market rate. See the Beurim.
