(Seif 5: the loss is shared equally, etc.) I am surprised that the Rama did not cite as a "some say" the opinion of Rabbeinu Yitzchak and the Rosh in our master's words, that even if they bought an animal for slaughter and actually slaughtered it, the gain and loss are shared equally when they did not stipulate otherwise at the outset. There is a compelling presumption that they intended everything to be shared equally.
