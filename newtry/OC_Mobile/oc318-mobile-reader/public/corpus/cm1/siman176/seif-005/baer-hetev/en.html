I am surprised that the Rama did not cite the view of Rabbeinu Yitzchak and the Rosh that even if they purchased the animal for slaughter and actually slaughtered it, the proceeds are nevertheless divided equally. Since they made no stipulation at the outset, there is a compelling presumption that they intended everything to be shared equally. Taz.
The currency. The taste would add if he remained the currency in the hands of anyone who came to them as a result or loss. A:
In produce. The Sema explains that the produce itself rose or fell in value. Although produce is intended for eventual division from beginning to end, each partner nevertheless receives the profit or bears the loss in proportion to his investment. See Responsa Maharam Padua, siman 128; Responsa Maharashdam, siman 375; and siman 442.
