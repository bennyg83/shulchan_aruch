And all the more so if they became partners in produce and the produce is still extant, etc. The phrase 'all the more so' follows the Tur in seif 9: each person purchased produce with his own money, and even if they became partners in money, etc. The marginal citation to the Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel and the Rif in paragraph 8 is a scribal error. Here, where they became partners in produce rather than in money, all agree that each takes according to the amount of money he contributed. Rabbeinu Chananel and the Rif dispute Rabbi Yitzchak and the Rosh only where they used jointly owned money to purchase produce. In the 1607 edition of the Shulchan Aruch, the margin cites only the Tur. See Responsa Maimon? siman 129 and Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 375 and 442.
