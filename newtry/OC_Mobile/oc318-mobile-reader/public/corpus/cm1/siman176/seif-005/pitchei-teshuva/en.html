Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, responsum 123, writes that money cannot be extracted against the opinion of Ri and the Rosh. VeShav HaKohen, responsum 65, discusses the issue at length and concludes that with produce or merchandise capable of division, if it was purchased for joint resale, the profit is divided equally. The same applies to an ox purchased for slaughter: if it was slaughtered in order to sell the meat jointly, the result is shared equally. If, however, the merchandise was purchased in order to divide it, or the ox was purchased so that the partners would divide the meat, then even if the items were later sold, according to the Rif as understood by the Rosh and Ran each partner receives according to his capital contribution. The Rambam's view in the specific case of an ox purchased for slaughter and division of the meat still requires study. See also Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, responsum 29.
