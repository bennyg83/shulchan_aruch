Unless there is a custom. Regarding the food expenses of one who travels to receive partnership merchandise, the Shulchan Aruch says such expenses are included in the partnership account only where this is customary. The Sema explains that this applies specifically where only one partner is occupied in the partnership business at that time. The same should apply here. If the partner receiving the merchandise is also away from his home, then both are occupied outside their homes at the same time. If the receiving partner has separate personal business, one must distinguish. If his personal affairs alone would not have kept him away from home that long, while the partnership business alone would also have required him to remain away, then both are still treated as occupied outside their town at the same time because of the partnership. If his personal business alone would have delayed him and the partnership business alone would likewise have delayed him, the case resembles the law in siman 264 concerning one who incurs expenses for both himself and his fellow. See Responsa Yad Eliyahu.
