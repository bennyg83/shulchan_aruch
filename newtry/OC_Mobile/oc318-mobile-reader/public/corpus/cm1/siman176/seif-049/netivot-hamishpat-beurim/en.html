If Reuven does not wish, he collects from Shimon. It appears that Shimon's liability is based on shelichut yad, which creates liability even when committed through an agent. As I wrote in siman 292, even where the agent is a non Jew, the custodian can become liable for shelichut yad. Therefore, even if Shimon proves that the property was later lost through ones in the second person's hands, he remains liable. But if Reuven merely instructed Shimon to deposit the property with Levi, and Shimon did so properly, Shimon is exempt when he proves that the property was lost through ones, as explained in siman 291 concerning a custodian who entrusts an item to another custodian.
