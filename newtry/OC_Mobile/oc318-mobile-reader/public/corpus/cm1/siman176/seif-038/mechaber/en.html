If Shimon bought merchandise from a non-Jew and misled him in the accounting, then placed the amount gained through the error into the partnership, and he fears the non-Jew may later remember the mistake, or if pledged items belonging to others are involved and he fears a resulting loss, Reuven should acknowledge before witnesses that the money gained through the mistake and the value of the pledges entered the partnership. The witnesses should write and sign the acknowledgment and deliver it to Shimon. Rama: See above, seif 12 of this siman.
