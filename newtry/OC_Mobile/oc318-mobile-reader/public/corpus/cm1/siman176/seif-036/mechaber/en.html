If two people jointly hold a debt against a non-Jew, and he pays part but withholds the balance on the claim that one partner owes him an offsetting sum, while that partner denies it, the resulting loss is borne by both partners. Rama: It appears to me that the same applies if the debtor is a Jew and must swear to his counterclaim. This differs from a non-Jew, who need not swear and may be subjected only to a general ban.
