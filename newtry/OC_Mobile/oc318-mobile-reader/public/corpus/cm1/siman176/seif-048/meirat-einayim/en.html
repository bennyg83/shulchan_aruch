Two partners who bought silk, etc. The responsum concerns Reuven, who sold Shimon ten pounds of silk on condition that when Shimon sold it, profit and loss would be divided equally. Shimon received and weighed it, but when he later came to sell it, it was short by half a pound. Reuven claimed that he had accepted profit and loss, not a deficiency in the silk itself. The Rosh ruled that deficiency is also included in loss. Shimon must swear that he did not make unauthorized use of the silk and was not negligent in guarding it, and Reuven then bears his share of the deficiency.
