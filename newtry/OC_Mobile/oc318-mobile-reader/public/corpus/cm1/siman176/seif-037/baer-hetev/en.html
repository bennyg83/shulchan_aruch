Shimon became obligated to the non-Jew only if the Jew failed to pay him. So long as the non-Jew did not establish that, Shimon was not legally liable to him, and the non-Jew merely imposed an unjust demand upon him. A person is not held liable for another person's unjust exaction. See further in siman 93, seif 15, where I wrote that a distinction must be made. Ibid.
