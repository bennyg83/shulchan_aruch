Reuven and Shimon were partners. Shimon bought garments from a non-Jew and sold them to a Jew, with the Jewish buyer obligated to pay the non-Jew by a specified date. Shimon gave the non-Jew a written undertaking that if the Jewish buyer failed to pay by that date, Shimon would pay. Reuven and Shimon later dissolved their partnership. Reuven must give Shimon a written undertaking that if Shimon proves by witnesses that the Jewish buyer failed to pay the non-Jew and Shimon therefore had to pay, Reuven will reimburse his proportional share of every expense and bribe that Shimon proves by witnesses he incurred in connection with the matter.
