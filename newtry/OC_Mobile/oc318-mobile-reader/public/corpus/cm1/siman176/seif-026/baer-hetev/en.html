As for the Rama's earlier ruling that when they are defendants one partner's conduct does not prejudice the other at all, one must say that the earlier case is different because they were initially defendants, and the plaintiff then counterclaimed against him in the same proceeding. Yet this distinction requires study, for it initially appears that the Mordechai disagrees with the Rosh. If one has a partner overseas and tells another person, Write to your partner that he should buy a certain item for me and send it with your merchandise, and he does so, but the ship sinks, see the Bach, who cites a responsum of Rav Hai, also brought in Responsa Maharashdam, siman 106. Liability rests upon Reuven only if he expressly said, The responsibility is upon me. If he did not mention this, he is exempt from payment. See there.
