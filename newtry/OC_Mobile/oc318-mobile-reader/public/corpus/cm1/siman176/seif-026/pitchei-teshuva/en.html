And when the debts are paid. See Nachalat Tzvi regarding Reuven and Shimon, partners who divided while Shimon remained in the business. A debt of ten gold coins remained owing from Levi. Levi later came to Shimon, gave him ten gold coins, and at the same time took new merchandise worth ten gold coins. Reuven claimed that Levi had thereby paid the old debt and that Reuven was entitled to half. Shimon claimed that the old debt remained outstanding and that the ten coins were payment for the newly purchased merchandise. Pitchei Teshuva writes there that it appears the law favors Reuven.
