When does this apply? When he did not become liable. See Shach, seif katan 43, through the statement that if he did not mention it to him he is exempt from payment. This is difficult. As soon as Shimon bought the merchandise, Reuven acquired his share through agency. If Reuven then instructed Shimon to send it, that is like saying, "Send it through so and so," in which case the sender bears responsibility. The principle that mere words cannot be delegated to another agent, as in siman 244, applies to execution of a document or similar verbal agency, not to this case. Reuven instructed Shimon to spend partnership money, and Shimon did so at his command. Why should Reuven not bear responsibility? We must therefore say that the case is one where Reuven merely instructed Shimon to buy the goods, while Shimon would receive a profit on the merchandise rather than acting as Reuven's agent. In that arrangement Shimon is not a simple agent, and therefore he may be exempt from responsibility. Beit Yosef at the end of this siman cites Rav Hai explicitly in this form.
