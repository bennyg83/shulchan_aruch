(Seif 25, in the gloss: but when they are defendants, etc.) The Sema, seif katan 59, limits this to property that does not require collection through legal process. This is surprising. If that were the distinction, the Rama should have divided between land and movable property, as Ramah does. It therefore appears to me that the Rama rules broadly like Mordechai, whose reason is that a defendant commonly evades appearing for judgment until he is actually sued. That reasoning applies to every defendant. This appears to be the primary practical ruling.
