If no duration was fixed for the partnership, or the fixed term ended, and one partner wishes to divide without the other's consent, he may divide before three people, even ordinary laymen, provided they are trustworthy and know how to appraise the property. A division made before fewer than three is ineffective. Rama: If he later suffered a loss in the portion assigned to him, he may set aside the division. Some disagree. This applies when they divide produce or other goods. If the partnership property consists of coins of one currency and equal value, he may divide without beit din and deposit the other partner's share with beit din. If the coins are not equal, they are treated like other goods and may be divided only before beit din.
