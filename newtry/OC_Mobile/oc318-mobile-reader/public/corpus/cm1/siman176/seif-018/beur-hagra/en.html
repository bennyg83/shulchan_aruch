Collection: And some disagree. This is like one who gives money, etc., where some say that if he changed the instructions, he alone bears the loss. Certainly the same applies here. This is what is meant by "if he lost..." But if he profited, everyone agrees that the profit is divided equally. See Beer HaGolah. The first opinion holds that the other case is different, because what he did nevertheless takes legal effect, whereas here the division is entirely ineffective. End of collection.
