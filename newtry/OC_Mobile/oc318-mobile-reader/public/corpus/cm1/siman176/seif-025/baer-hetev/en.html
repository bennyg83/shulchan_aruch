Were defendants. See the Tur, who writes in the name of the Ramah that if one partner was sued concerning land and was found liable, his partner cannot reopen the judgment, presumably because collection from land requires no further act of seizure. It appears that all agree to this. The Rama here is speaking of a monetary claim that still requires collection. Sema. The Taz writes that this is difficult, for the Rama should then have distinguished between land and movable property, as the Ramah does. Rather, it appears that he ruled generally in accordance with the Mordechai, whose reason regarding a defendant is that a person ordinarily avoids appearing in court until he is actually sued. This applies to every defendant. This appears correct as practical halachah.
