Tur, seif 2, in the name of his father the Rosh, who cites Rabbeinu Yonah and Raavad in his rulings. Nimukei Yosef likewise writes in the name of Rashba from the rule that one may raise the valuation against the property of Bar Maryon. The Rosh writes that this is reasonable because all partners benefit from the increase in valuation, Bava Batra 12b.
Beit Yosef there cites Ramban and his student Raah, who hold that where there is no actual increase in value, one does not nullify the lottery because the lottery itself creates the legal division. The rule of increasing valuation was said only regarding "buy or sell" where the item is not legally divisible. Ritva, Ramban's student, inclines to this view.
