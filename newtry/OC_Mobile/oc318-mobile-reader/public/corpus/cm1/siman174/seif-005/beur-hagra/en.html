The brothers. This is learned from what is stated in siman 175 seif 2, from Kiddushin 42a, that they may protest concerning the directions. Ri challenged this from Bava Batra 106b, "If so..." and answered that Kiddushin concerns a case where the additional valuation nullifies the lottery.
And some disagree. They maintain that an additional valuation was stated only where the property is not subject to division, as stated there on 13b, "and if not, they raise the value..." implying that otherwise they do not. The case in Kiddushin concerns a division made without a lottery, and therefore they may protest, as Tosafos explain in Bava Batra there, s.v. "and Shmuel"; see there.
