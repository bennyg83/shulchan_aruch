To compel. The wording implies that even when the portions are equal he cannot compel them. This is difficult, since the Mechaber ruled in seif 1 that when they are equal one compels the other on account of the trait of Sodom. The Tur cites this ruling in the name of the Rosh, who follows the "some say" there. The Maggid Mishneh likewise states these rules and writes that in this case the law is like that of a yavam, whereas in the first case, where two people acquired jointly, the law is like that of a firstborn. This is understandable if he thereby alludes that one portion is superior to the other, for according to the Rambam the firstborn has an advantage even in that case. Therefore, in the latter case of three who acquired together, one cannot compel the others because the portions are unequal. The Mechaber's unqualified wording, however, remains difficult. Taz.
