There, law 3, from the Gemara at the beginning of 12a, according to Rabbeinu Chananel and Rambam's teacher, as Tosafot cite Rabbeinu Chananel. Rambam explains "nigra" as a road, because land near a road is more valuable than land farther away. Beit Yosef.
Maggid Mishneh writes that this follows Rambam's and his teacher's approach, as explained.
