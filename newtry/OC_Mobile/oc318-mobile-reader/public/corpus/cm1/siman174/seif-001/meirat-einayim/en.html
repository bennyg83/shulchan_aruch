Or close to the road. This is the Rambam's language. The Gemara says "close to the irrigation channel." The Beit Yosef writes that the Rambam interpreted the word as road, based on the expression for public roads, because a field near a road has an advantage. In the Perishah and Derishah I wrote that it appears that the Rambam too understood the word to mean a canal or stream, as the other authorities do. Owners of fields around a river dig channels and draw water from the river into their fields, and the path taken by the water through those channels is itself called a road. See there.
