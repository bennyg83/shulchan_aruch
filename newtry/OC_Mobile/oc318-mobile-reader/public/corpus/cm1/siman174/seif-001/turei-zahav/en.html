Seif 1: This is the trait of Sodom. The other partner loses nothing by giving his fellow the portion adjacent to the latter's boundary. This is Rashi's approach. The Beit Yosef questions the Rambam, who says that where both portions are superior land, one is compelled because withholding would be the trait of Sodom. It appears that the Rambam does not explain the case like Rashi. Rather, one portion is better than the other, yet the owner may still be compelled to give the adjoining portion to his fellow at a beit din appraisal. Rav Yosef, however, holds that the owner may say, “I value this property as though it belonged to Mar Marion,” meaning that just as those properties were especially valuable to their owner, he too specifically desires the more valuable parcel. The halachah follows that view, as Rabbeinu writes in seif 5.
