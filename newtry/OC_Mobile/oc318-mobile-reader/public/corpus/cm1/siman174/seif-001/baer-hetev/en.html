For him. Meaning, he may say: Let us divide by lottery. He maintains that this does not constitute the trait of Sodom, because he has a legitimate interest in the matter. If the lot should give him the parcel between the two fields, he may refuse to exchange it except for a high price. Therefore they cast lots, since perhaps that advantage will fall to him. The Tur writes: Nevertheless, if he is astute he can say, "I will pay such and such for it, slightly more than its value, or you may take it at that price." They will not wish to take it, and it will remain his at that sum. Sema.

Rather, we apply the rule that whoever is stronger prevails, because perhaps the halachah follows Rashi. This is the Mordechai's language. The Rama abbreviated the ruling. Here it is different, because this is not considered a gift. He prefers receiving inferior land so that his fields will adjoin one another rather than receiving superior land as his share. Sema.
