There, law 2, from Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua. The Maggid Mishneh explains that according to Rambam, if there are two average portions and a middle superior portion, and the firstborn cannot receive his whole share together unless he takes the entire superior portion, he takes it with a monetary adjustment. Rambam's teacher likewise ruled.
Rambam rules like Rava there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that according to Rambam, if all portions are equal, even a yavam takes one contiguous share, consistent with Rambam's approach and his teacher's view. This was explained in the preceding seif.
