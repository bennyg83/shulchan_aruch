[Shach, letter 5] That he said before them. Note: This is perplexing. The Shach appears to require that he make the statement before witnesses, whereas a statement made privately between himself and his Creator would be ineffective. Yet above, siman 176, seif katan 45, the Shach concludes that even a statement made privately between himself and his Creator is effective. One might suggest that here, since it appears he is believed with an oath even without a migo, he must claim that he made the statement before witnesses, whereas above there is a migo. But this distinction is not persuasive. Since you believe him under oath that he made such a statement before witnesses, even though no witnesses are presently before us, we should likewise believe him that he made the statement privately between himself and his Creator. This requires study. See Mishneh LaMelech regarding this, and Toldot Adam of the Rashba, siman 190.
