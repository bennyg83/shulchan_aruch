One of them. See Baer Heitev, which writes, "when they cannot save it." This means even when they are unable to save it, and the point is straightforward. As for the words, "and Beit Yosef in the section beginning...," that is indeed how they appear in the Shach, seif katan 3, but it is a printing error, as Netivot HaMishpat notes. After the words "they divide equally" seif katan 3 ends. Everything printed after that point belongs to seif katan 5, corresponding to Baer Heitev seif katan 6. After the words "that he said so before them" one should read: Maharshal there, and Beit Yosef in the section beginning, and so forth, through the end of that comment.
