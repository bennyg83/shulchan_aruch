He thereby withdraws from the employment. See Sema, seif katan 5, through his citation of the verse, "For the children of Israel are servants to Me." This implies that the case concerns a day laborer. With a contractor, to whom that reason does not apply, as explained in siman 333, the profit would seemingly be shared. However, the Shach writes there in seif katan 4 that only where there was a formal kinyan or meshichah is a contractor unable to withdraw completely. Without a kinyan he can withdraw, although he may be at a disadvantage in the accounting. Since he can withdraw, the subsequent profit is his own once he does so. A laborer, by contrast, may withdraw even after a kinyan, because of the principle cited by the Sema, "For the children of Israel are servants to Me."
