Likewise, one who hires a worker to save property. See the end of siman 270, where Rama cites a dissenting opinion that even where he hired the worker generally and merely showed him the found item, the item belongs to the employer. One must say that siman 270 is the primary place for the laws of a worker, and therefore all distinctions are recorded there. Here Rama writes only the rule accepted by all opinions.
He thereby retracts from his employment. Meaning, a worker is permitted to retract, because the verse says, 'The children of Israel are servants to Me,' as the Tur and Mechaber explain below in siman 333 and as was also explained above in siman 176.
It belongs to him. This applies specifically where the owners themselves could no longer have saved the property, so that we presume they certainly despaired. Even then, if the worker did not expressly retract, the recovered property belongs to the employer, because we presume he continued acting as an employee and saved it for the employer, since he had no independent share in the property.
