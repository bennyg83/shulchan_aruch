This is also the name of the author and the author of the author, and if he said and so on, he saved it as “Modam” we were as much as his part:
The Maggid Mishneh writes that this cannot refer to a case where they can save the property, because then he certainly has no authority to appropriate it. Rashi explains the case as a worker hired by the caravan, where they cannot save the property, so he may retract and acquire from ownerless property. See similar laws at the end of siman 270.
