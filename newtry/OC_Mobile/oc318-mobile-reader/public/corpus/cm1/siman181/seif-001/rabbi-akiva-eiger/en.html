[Shulchan Aruch] And once they sat down. Note: This is difficult to me. Despair alone does not effect acquisition, and there is no change of possession here, since the robber did not transfer it willingly. Rather, this person rescued it and took it from the robber against his will, as stated below, siman 361, seif 5. This requires very great study. Much later I found the same point in Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Gezeilah, siman 28, but his resolution is not understandable to me.
