There, seif 7, in the name of Ramban from the words of Baal HaTerumot in Shaar 67.
Beit Yosef explains Baal HaTerumot in this manner.
He explains there: if the property is lost through circumstances beyond control, who must pay him? If you suggest placing it with beit din, who required him to do so? And is property held by beit din itself not also subject to unavoidable loss?
