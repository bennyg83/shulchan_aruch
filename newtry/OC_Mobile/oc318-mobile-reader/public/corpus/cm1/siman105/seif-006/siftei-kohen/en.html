The limitation that Rabbi Natan's lien is judicially enforced only where the intermediate debtor lacks assets protects the intermediate debtor from loss under rabbinic policy. When the creditor or bailee voluntarily wishes to pay, he may do so initially because Torah law already subjects the debt to the ultimate creditor. This is the primary ruling.
