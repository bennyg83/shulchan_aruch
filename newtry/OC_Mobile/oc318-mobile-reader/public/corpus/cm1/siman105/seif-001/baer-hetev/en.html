An agent. See the Shach, who reconciles Rashi's words in Bava Metzia and derives from them that if a person hired another to seize property for him from a debtor, and the agent seized it, the seizure is effective even where this disadvantages other creditors. It appears that all the poskim agree with this ruling in practice. See the responsa cited by the Shach, including Rabbi Moshe Alshich, Maharashdam, Mahara Sasson, Mabit, Raanach, Maharashach, Maharshal, Maharam Galanti, and Maharit.
