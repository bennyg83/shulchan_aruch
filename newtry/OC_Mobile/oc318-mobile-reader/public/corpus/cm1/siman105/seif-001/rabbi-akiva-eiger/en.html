[Shulchan Aruch] For anyone who seizes property for a creditor where doing so causes loss to others. Note: In cases of doubt where seizure is effective, if another person seizes on his behalf, one may say that the seizure is effective, since it is not certain that he thereby causes a loss to the other party, for perhaps the law is indeed that the property belongs to this creditor. See Responsa Mekor Baruch, siman 60. See also Responsa Pnei Moshe, part 1, siman 91, which suggests that seizure based on a claim of “I hold” is effective only when made by the litigant himself, not by another on his behalf.
