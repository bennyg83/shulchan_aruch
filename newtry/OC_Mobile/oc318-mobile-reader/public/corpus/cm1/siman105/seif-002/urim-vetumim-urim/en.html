If the debtor owes him. Sema holds that if the debtor owes the person who seized one hundred, and that person seeks to seize two hundred for another creditor, his seizure helps only up to his own one hundred. He cannot seize beyond the amount of his own debt. All the more so if he already seized property for his own debt and now seeks to retain the excess for another creditor. The Shach disagrees and says that once he has a legal hand in part of the property, he has a hand in all of it. In Tumim I brought clear proof for Sema. One should not depart from his ruling. Masat Binyamin and Knesset HaGedolah likewise agree.
