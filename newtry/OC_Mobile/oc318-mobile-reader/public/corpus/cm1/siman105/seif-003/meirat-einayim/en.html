Acquire this object for so and so. The Tur says, "Acquire this object on behalf of so and so, my creditor." In the Perishah I wrote that "my creditor" is not essential. Rather, the case is simply that he acquires it for another person, as is implied by the Mechaber here, who does not say "creditor." The Mechaber also concludes that none of the creditors can collect these items because "another person" has already acquired them, again implying that the recipient need not himself be a creditor.
