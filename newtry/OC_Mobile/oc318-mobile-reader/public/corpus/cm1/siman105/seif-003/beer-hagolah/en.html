Tur, and Rambam likewise writes there in chapter 16, based on the rule that anyone who says 'give' is regarded as saying 'acquire on his behalf.' Rashi explains that this is not an ordinary case of one who seizes for a creditor, because the owner appointed him as agent to acquire it for him. Gittin 11b.
