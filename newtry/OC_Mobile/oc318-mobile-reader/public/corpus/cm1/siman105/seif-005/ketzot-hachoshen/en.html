One who seizes for a creditor where he does not disadvantage others. It appears that this applies specifically where he seizes money, or where he seizes a pledge in circumstances in which taking the pledge is itself permitted. Where taking the pledge is forbidden, as with an ordinary loan under siman 97, the seizure should not work. According to Tosafot and the poskim, the power to seize for another rests on agency, and there is no agency for a transgression. Examine carefully.
