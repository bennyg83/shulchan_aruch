If. The Sema writes that the text should read 'and if,' referring back to the view introduced by 'some say.' He understands that the Mechaber's wording was visibly deficient and that the Rama supplied the correction by adding 'and some say.' The Shach writes that the Sema overlooked the Beit Yosef's Bedek HaBayit, which expressly states that the Ittur and the Rosh do not disagree. Rather, the Rosh speaks where the creditor explicitly says, 'Accept responsibility upon yourself.' This still requires explanation, because once he is liable in responsibility, why is an explicit acceptance needed? The Shach suggests that the intent is that he expressly accept it so that the claimant will have against the seizer the status of a written loan.
