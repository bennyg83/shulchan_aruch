If the debtor has no other assets, etc. The authorities explain that seizure for another creditor is effective only when the debtor lacks sufficient property for all creditors. Where sufficient assets exist, no one is harmed and the issue does not arise. See the cited responsa for applications of this rule.
He must accept responsibility. The Sema explains that the recipient must expressly accept responsibility for the property. The Beit Yosef already states that Baal HaItur and the Rosh do not disagree: the Rosh concerns an express acceptance. Although a bailee who transfers property may already bear responsibility, an express undertaking is needed so that the original owner has a documented claim against the person who seized it.
