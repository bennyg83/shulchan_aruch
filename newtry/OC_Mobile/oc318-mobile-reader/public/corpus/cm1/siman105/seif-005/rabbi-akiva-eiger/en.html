[Shach, letter 2] Wherever he acquires something for himself, he acquires it for his fellow as well. Note: See Tosafot, Bava Metzia, s.v. “And partners who stole,” which implies that they hold this only where the partners stole together, but not where one steals for himself and for his fellow. See Machaneh Ephraim, Hilchot Shutafim, siman 8.
