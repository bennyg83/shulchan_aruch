Also to the one who seized. See Baer Heitev, which records that the Shach rejected the opposing views and agreed with Beit Yosef. Korban Netanel, Gittin chapter 1, letter 70, refutes the Shach's arguments and concludes that the primary ruling is like the Sema; Tumim agrees. Noda BiYehudah, however, at the end of siman 17 rules that one may say, "I rely on the Shach," especially because Beit Yosef and Levush also rule that way. Noda BiYehudah further discusses a debt whose due date has not yet arrived and concludes that the principle "since he could acquire it for himself, he may acquire it for another" still applies. A debt not yet due is not regarded as lacking an act of acquisition merely because its time has not arrived. See also Chatam Sofer, Choshen Mishpat, end of siman 100.
