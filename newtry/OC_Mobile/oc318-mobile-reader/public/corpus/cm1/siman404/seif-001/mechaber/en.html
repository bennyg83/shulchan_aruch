An ox that injures another ox, or where either the injured or damaging animal appreciates or depreciates. It contains two seifim:
If an ox worth two hundred gores another ox worth two hundred and reduces its value by fifty, and by the time of judgment the injured ox has appreciated and is now worth four hundred, whereas without the goring it would have been worth eight hundred, whether the increase resulted from fattening or occurred on its own, the tortfeasor pays only according to the damage as valued at the time the injury occurred.
