If the injured animal becomes emaciated because of the wound by the time of judgment, so that its loss is now one hundred, the tortfeasor pays according to its condition at the time of judgment. If the damaging animal appreciates before judgment, and it appreciated because its owner fattened it, collection from that animal is limited to what it was worth at the time it caused the damage. Some say that if the appreciation exceeds the owner's expenses, the owner of the damaging animal takes reimbursement for his expenses and compensation for his labor, while the injured party takes his share of the remaining appreciation.
