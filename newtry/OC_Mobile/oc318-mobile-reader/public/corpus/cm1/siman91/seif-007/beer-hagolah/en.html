There and there, part 2, law 7.
The Sema writes in the name of Maharshal that where the workers died and only the shopkeeper comes, etc., he does not collect without an oath. The present case concerns where the employer knows that he gave them something but does not know how much. Above in seif 2, according to the dissenting authorities cited by the Rama, the case is where the employer does not know whether he gave them anything at all.
Meaning, the workers contradict the shopkeeper and say that he did not give them as much as he claims, and that the employer did not owe them that much either. Sema.
