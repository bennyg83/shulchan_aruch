If he did not give... This is like a case where the amount was fixed above in seif 2. Because he relied on what the workers would say, their later statement functions like a prior fixing of the price. We rule in Bava Basra that if one pulled the merchandise without fixing a price he did not acquire it because his mind was not settled, but once the price was fixed his mind is settled. Similarly, Avodah Zarah states that if the value is to be determined by three appraisers, the acquisition is effective. Thus reliance on their valuation is equivalent to fixing the amount.
And some say that even... See Taz and Shach, who explain that there is no dispute. The text should read, "And if the workers..." As explained above, this is treated as a fixed amount. Baal HaTerumos writes that the employer should have specified how much he would pay and what their wages were; by failing to do so he caused his own loss and is regarded as having accepted whatever amount the workers state, just like an agreed price.
