And there are grounds supporting the matter. Baer Heitev implies that a ledger is not relied upon at all in this context and therefore cannot itself create a right to impose an oath. Tumim concludes otherwise: for purposes of requiring the opposing party to swear concerning the entire ledger, the account holder's ledger can constitute a definite claim. The additional requirement of supporting circumstances applies where he seeks to collect money on the strength of the ledger. Tumim writes that this is the accepted ruling of halachic authorities, and Netivot HaMishpat cites it as well.
