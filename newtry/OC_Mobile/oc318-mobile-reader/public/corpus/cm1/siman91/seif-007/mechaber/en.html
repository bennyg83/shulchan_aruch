If the employer did not specify to the storekeeper how much to give his workers but said, 'Give them what they need,' then if the workers are not before us or have died, the storekeeper is believed to say, 'I gave them such-and-such,' and collects from the employer without an oath. {Rama: Some say that even if the workers are present and the storekeeper gave them more than their wages, since no limit was placed on what he should give them, the storekeeper swears and collects.}
