But if the householder advanced money to the shopkeeper and said, 'Give my workers one sela.' It is possible that the denomination of the money he advanced does not circulate as readily as the sela.
A similar distinction is found regarding a money changer who sells a minted dinar for unminted money.And the workers consented.
The word 'consented' is not exact.
It is enough that they remained silent and did not explicitly say that they had no dealings with the householder and would look only to the shopkeeper.
Mere silence does not necessarily release the householder from his underlying obligation unless the circumstances clearly show an agreed substitution of debtor.
The rule therefore depends on whether the workers expressly accepted the shopkeeper as the sole person responsible for payment or merely accepted the arrangement as a convenient method of payment.
