There is a name called R. D. in the name of the mountain
There in Baal HaTerumot in the name of Rabbi Yosef ben Plat, who answered that we learn this from Kiddushin 43b, concerning one who tells two people, 'Go betroth a woman for me,' etc. Now that the Sages enacted shevuat hesset, the witnesses swear, etc. The same applies with one witness: the agent swears that he paid and the lender swears that he did not receive, etc.
The Tur in the name of the Ramah. So too Baal HaTerumot in the name of the Rif in tractate Kiddushin.
Baal HaTerumot, gate 29, in the name of Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash: since the shopkeeper receives nothing from the householder, the Sages did not enact this rule even for the workers. Rabbenu Ephraim likewise challenged the Rif.
