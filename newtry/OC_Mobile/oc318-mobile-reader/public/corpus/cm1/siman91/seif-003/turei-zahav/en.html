(See there) “The worker has no claim.” This means that where the employer did not first advance funds to the shopkeeper, the law of a transfer in the presence of all three parties does not apply at all. Where he did advance the funds, such a three-party transfer is stronger here, because it is possible that the shopkeeper already paid the worker, as Sema writes in seif katan 10. This is contrary to Rabbi Yeshayah, cited by the Tur, who gives such weight to the possibility of prior payment that even without an advance this case is stronger than an ordinary three-party transfer, and the employer is exempt.
