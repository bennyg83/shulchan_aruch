For he pays only one payment. See Shach, seif katan 11, who concludes that even according to Rambam and the Mechaber, an oath is required although the payer will ultimately make only one payment. This appears to be the primary ruling. It is proved from seif 8, concerning a minor who hired workers, where the workers swear even though the shopkeeper does not receive an additional payment. Evidently, when the parties directly contradict one another, an oath is required even where the payer makes only a single payment.
