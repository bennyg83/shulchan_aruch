He said. The Shach discusses these laws at great length and offers several interpretations. Chacham Tzvi, both in his glosses to the Taz and in Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 143, challenges the Shach and rejects all of his explanations. One who wishes to determine the matter accurately should study their discussions; this is not the place for a lengthy treatment. See also Responsa Rashach, part 3, siman 56, which likewise discusses at length how to interpret the Mishnah and Gemara differently from the Rif.
