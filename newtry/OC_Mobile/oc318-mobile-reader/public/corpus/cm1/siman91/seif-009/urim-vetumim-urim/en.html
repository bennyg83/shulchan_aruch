The psalmist swore that the fruit they would see for them to take away, and for this reason, they would only take care of them, and that it would take them to swear, and that the fruit was sworn in:
And I brought them, etc. The shopkeeper says, 'I never sold you these particular fruits.' Had the fruits still been in his own domain, he would be believed with hesset. Because they are now lying in the public domain, he must take the stronger oath described here. See Tumim.
