(In the Shulchan Aruch, seif 1.) "How so? The householder," etc. The Mishnah's wording does not mean that the householder says, "It is written in my ledger that you owe me such and such." Rather, he says, "Give my son," etc. Meaning, a storekeeper's ledger has evidentiary force only where the householder admits that he originally trusted the storekeeper to make payments on his behalf.
