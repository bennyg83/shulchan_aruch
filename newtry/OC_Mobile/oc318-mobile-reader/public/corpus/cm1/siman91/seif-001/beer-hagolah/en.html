From M.M. M.M., a page is brought in:
The Tur. So writes Baal HaTerumot, gate 29, and the Rambam in chapter 15 of Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh, law 5. The Maggid Mishneh writes that some hold the Mishnah's law applies only to a shopkeeper. With other agency, if one says, 'Give so-and-so such-and-such money for me,' and the agent says, 'I gave it,' the agent does not swear and collect but must bring proof, because we are uncertain whether he was actually appointed agent. Others say an agent has the same law as the shopkeeper. See there. The Bach likewise infers this from the Tur's wording and rules accordingly.
