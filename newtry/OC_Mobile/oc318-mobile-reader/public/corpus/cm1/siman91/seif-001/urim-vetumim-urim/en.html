Give my workers a sela. The same applies if one says, 'Give so-and-so a gift,' in a manner where, had the shopkeeper not given it, the owner would have no independent obligation to do so. If the recipient says, 'He gave me nothing,' the shopkeeper must swear while holding a sacred object and collect. Although the owner ultimately pays only once, he nevertheless suffers loss if the shopkeeper did not pay, because absent payment the owner had no liability at all. Therefore the oath is required.
