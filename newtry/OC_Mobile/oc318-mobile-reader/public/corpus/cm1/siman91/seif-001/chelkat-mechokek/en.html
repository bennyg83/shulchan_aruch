A woman who brought a debt document into the marriage. The phrase "brought into the marriage" implies that she appraised the debt document as part of her dowry and that the husband accepted it as nichsei tzon barzel. But if the document remained hers as nichsei melog, she should be able to waive the debt because the principal remains hers. Yet I saw in Sefer HaTerumot in the name of Ramban that even regarding nichsei melog, the husband's hand is like her hand and he acquires rights in the debt so that she cannot waive it. This matter is left unresolved in Choshen Mishpat 66:28.
