Meaning that if the householder admits that he instructed the payment, the claimant swears and collects; if the householder denies the instruction, he takes a hesset oath and is exempt, as explained in seif 10.Because he relies upon it as true. The cited ruling therefore allows him to swear to a non-Jew concerning a pledge recorded in his ledger as securing fifteen gold coins.
The fact that the incident involved a non-Jew is incidental; the same principle applies to an Israelite where the law of the oath is otherwise applicable. A regularly maintained account book can create a definite claim when the person genuinely relies upon it as accurate business memory.
