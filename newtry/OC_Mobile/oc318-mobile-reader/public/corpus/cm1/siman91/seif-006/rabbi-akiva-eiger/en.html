[Shach, letter 33, fifth explanation, s.v. “It further appears”] Nevertheless, it requires study whether we apply a migo in such a case. Note: In my humble opinion, the doubt is that according to his own account, he says he purchased the homeowner’s fruit but claims that the homeowner owes him the value of the fruit. It may therefore be a migo used to extract money. See what I wrote in siman 138, seif 8, in the gloss.
