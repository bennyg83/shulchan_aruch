For he knows. This means that where the law allows a claimant to collect despite the other party's uncertainty, he must ordinarily swear before collecting. The fact that in seif 7 he collects without an oath appears to be because Rama's requirement applies only where the claimant was not negligent in lacking knowledge, for example where it was impossible to fix the expenses in advance. In seif 7, however, the employer knew what wage was due to the workers and failed to stipulate it. He caused his own uncertainty and therefore brought the loss upon himself, as Taz writes there. The claimant accordingly collects without an oath.
