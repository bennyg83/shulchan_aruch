Whose law is the same as that of an adult. The Tur writes that a worker collects because the minor derives benefit from the worker's labor, and the shopkeeper likewise collects because he can say, 'I gave the worker what you told me to give him for your benefit.' The Rambam, however, writes that the shopkeeper does not take an oath and collect, because the minor derives no benefit from the shopkeeper himself, since the minor was already obligated to pay his workers, and the shopkeeper caused his own loss by giving his money on the minor's instruction. The Mechaber rules without qualification like the Rambam in siman 96:3. See there.
