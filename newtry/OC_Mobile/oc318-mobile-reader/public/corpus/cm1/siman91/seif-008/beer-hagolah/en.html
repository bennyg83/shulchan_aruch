There in his name, at the end of chapter 5 of Hilkhot To'en. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is explicit in chapter HaNizakin, Gittin 59a, that minors' purchases and sales of movable property are valid, and in chapter HaChovel, Bava Kamma 87a, that a minor who injures others is forever exempt from payment. From these two laws some Geonim learned that everything depends upon whether the minor receives benefit. Where he benefits, his acts are effective once he reaches the stated age. Where he receives no benefit, he is exempt, analogous to tort liability. This is a sound distinction and the principal ruling. In siman 36, end of seif 3, the Mechaber rules without qualification like the Rambam.
