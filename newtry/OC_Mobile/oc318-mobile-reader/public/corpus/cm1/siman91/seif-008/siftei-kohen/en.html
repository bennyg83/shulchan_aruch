Whose law is the same, etc. The Tur writes that the worker collects because the minor receives a benefit, and the shopkeeper also collects because he can say, what you instructed me to give the worker I gave for your benefit. The Rambam, however, writes that the shopkeeper does not swear and collect because the minor receives no benefit from the shopkeeper, since the minor is already obligated to pay his workers, and the shopkeeper caused his own loss by giving his money based on the minor's instruction. At the end of siman 96, seif 3, the Mechaber rules without qualification like the Rambam. See there, and see above in siman 89, seif 2.
