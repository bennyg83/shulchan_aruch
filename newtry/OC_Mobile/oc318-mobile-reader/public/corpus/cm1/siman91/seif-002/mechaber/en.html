When does the preceding rule apply? When both the storekeeper and the workers are before us and claim from the employer. But if the storekeeper died and only the workers claim, or the workers died and only the storekeeper claims, the remaining claimant collects without an oath, because the employer loses nothing; he makes only one payment. {Rama: Some say that only when the storekeeper died and the workers come to collect do they collect without an oath, because the employer certainly became obligated to them and does not know whether he paid them. If the workers died and the storekeeper comes to claim what he gave them, however, the storekeeper collects only upon taking an oath, lest he have given them nothing. See note 4.}
