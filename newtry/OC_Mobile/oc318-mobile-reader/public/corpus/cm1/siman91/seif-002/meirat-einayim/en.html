For he pays only one payment. Meaning, he admits that one payment is unquestionably owed. The Rambam and Tur write likewise. This differs from the Maggid Mishneh's report in the name of the Ramban that if the workers died, their heirs may collect from the employer by taking the heirs' oath that they were not instructed otherwise. According to that position, the employer could ultimately face two payments even here. See the Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe.
And he does not know whether he paid. Strictly speaking, the worker should collect from the employer without an oath. It was only because of the employer's potential loss, and in order to appease him, that the Sages enacted that the workers take an oath before collecting. That enactment applies specifically where the employer may also have to pay the shopkeeper. That concern is absent here. Examine this carefully.
