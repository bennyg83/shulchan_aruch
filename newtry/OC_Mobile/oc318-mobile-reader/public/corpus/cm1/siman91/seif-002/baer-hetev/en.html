Died. Baal HaTerumot writes that death is not literal; the same applies if the other party went overseas. Although he may return today or tomorrow and say that he paid, since he is not presently before us the remaining claimant is not required to swear. If the defendant wishes, however, he may impose an unspecified ban upon anyone who took this money and now claims it a second time. The other authorities and the Rambam rule likewise, as does the Mechaber in siman 121, seif 10. It appears that if the absent party is nearby and it is known that he will return, the employer may say, 'You will suffer no loss; wait a little until he comes, or swear and collect immediately.' It also appears that where the party went overseas and the claimant therefore collected without an oath, even if the absent party later returns and contradicts him, forcing the employer to pay the other party as well, the first claimant still need not swear because he has already collected. The Sages imposed this oath only upon one who is coming to collect; once he merely seeks to retain what he already received, there is no oath. Shach.
