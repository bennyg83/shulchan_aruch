That what is written in the ledger is true. One who regularly records all his business dealings in a ledger without omitting matters can, if another claims one hundred and he finds only fifty recorded, swear that he owes only fifty. Had he owed one hundred, he would have entered one hundred. Everything depends on the nature of the ledger, the person's habits, and the judgment of beit din.
