The Maggid Mishneh writes that he also did not find this explicitly stated, but there is some support from Sotah 37b, which speaks of a guarantor and a guarantor for a guarantor. Although the first guarantor gives no money, the second does not require a kinyan, because the moment of his undertaking is treated like the lender's giving of money. This is the primary view.
