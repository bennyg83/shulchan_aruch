This mixture is for Philly. As the Ramban and the Sages put it on this matter, the column wrote:
As though he said, "Lend him." This means that the second guarantor becomes obligated even without a kinyan. The first guarantor's own obligation, even if undertaken after the money was advanced and through a kinyan, is treated as the equivalent of the moment when the original money was advanced. The second guarantor therefore becomes obligated to the first without a separate kinyan. So writes the Maggid Mishneh.
