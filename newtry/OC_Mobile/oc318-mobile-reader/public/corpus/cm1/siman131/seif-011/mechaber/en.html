Reuven purchased a field from Shimon, who wrote him a document. Reuven asked that Shimon also arrange a document concerning his wife, who was not present. Levi guaranteed that when she came, she would make such a document. When she arrived, she refused. Reuven then demanded that the guarantor write him a document against his own property, while the guarantor claimed that he was obligated only to return Reuven's money. The law follows the guarantor.
