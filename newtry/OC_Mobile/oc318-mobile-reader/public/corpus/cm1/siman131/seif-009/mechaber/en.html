Some have written that if Reuven sold a field to Shimon and Levi accepted responsibility for its warranty, Levi did not become obligated. But if a kinyan was made with Levi that he guarantees payment of the purchase price whenever Shimon demands it, Levi is obligated. {Rama: Some say there is no asmachta when a guarantor accepts responsibility for a warranty at the time the money is given, and he becomes obligated.}
