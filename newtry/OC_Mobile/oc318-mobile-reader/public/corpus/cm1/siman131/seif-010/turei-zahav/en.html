(Seif 13) This guarantor is not liable at all. In the Tur's text of Raavad the words are, “if so, neither much nor little.” His meaning is that the guarantor certainly did not obligate himself beyond the borrower's means, whether those means were much or little. At least we should therefore estimate that he did not undertake more than the borrower possessed, and likewise not less than the amount presently needed. One might ask why Raavad does not similarly use estimation in siman 60 regarding an obligation for an unspecified amount. Maggid Mishneh raises this and gives no answer. Here we know at least one boundary with certainty, that he did not undertake more than the borrower owned. Once a definite limit exists, we follow the evident assessment throughout, even downward. In siman 60 there is no such objective indication.
