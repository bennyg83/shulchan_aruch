The clarification will be... Meaning as stated in the gloss, "and the guarantor is not believed..." See what is written there.
This is specifically... Since he drove the lion away from him, he is like another person who was seized because of his fellow, as stated in the Yerushalmi and above siman 128, seif 2, and below siman 388, seif 6.
The guarantor is not believed... He is no better than an informer, regarding whom the Gemara at the end of HaKones asks whether the enactment for one who was robbed applies to an informer, and leaves the question unresolved. We therefore rule leniently for the defendant.
Rather... and then... Meaning that the witnesses know why he entered the settlement, but do not know for what amount or whether it was because of the guarantee. See below siman 388, seif 7, in the gloss. If he knows... The reason is that where there are circumstances supporting the claim, he collects with an oath, as in the rule of the shopkeeper on his ledger. The phrase there does not mean that in every case he collects by oath, but specifically where there are supporting circumstances. This is unlike Sema, seif katan 17, who explains that it is as though he spent with authorization. If that were the reason, even in the first case, where they do not know how much he paid, he should collect with an oath, as stated above siman 91, seif 3, in the gloss.
