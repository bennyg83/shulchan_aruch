(Seif 8: and they became guarantors for one another.) This implies specifically in such a case. Ordinary partners do not become liable for one another's loss resulting from an accusation by authorities merely because they are partners. The wording of Mordechai at the end of HaGozel supports this, where one party can say, "You caused a lion to crouch at my boundary." Without an agreement of mutual guarantee that claim would not apply. See siman 176, seif 12, in the gloss.
