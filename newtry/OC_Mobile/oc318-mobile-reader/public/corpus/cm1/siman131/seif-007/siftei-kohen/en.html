["Or that he wrote between the lines, etc. The Sema writes that the text should read without the conjunction, and it refers back to the previous clause. The Bach disagrees, because perhaps he colluded with the non Jew and therefore cannot be believed merely with an oath. He explains that the notation and the interlinear writing were entered in their official court record. See the Sema, who argues that this cannot be because Shimon still holds the debt instrument. That is not decisive, because perhaps he had only partially paid and therefore had to record the payment while leaving the instrument outstanding. See responsa of the Rama, siman 15."]
