(Seif 9) Levi did not become obligated. Sema explains that a person may buy land even for one day. It is difficult, because Shmuel says in Bava Metzia 14 that omission of a warranty is treated as a scribal error only in a loan document, not in a deed of sale, since a person may buy land for a single day. Yet on page 15 Rava rules that we do not follow Shmuel. It appears that this principle is rejected only regarding the direct warranty between purchaser and seller, but remains relevant to the obligation of a guarantor. The distinction still requires study.
