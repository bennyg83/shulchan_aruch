Once they performed a kinyan with him, he cannot retract. Shaar Mishpat raises a question. Since with an ordinary kinyan sudar concerning a gift of movable property a party may retract while the parties are still engaged in the same matter, does the same apply to this guaranty, or is it comparable to maamad shelashtan at the beginning of siman 126, where the transfer becomes final immediately? He leaves the issue for consideration.
