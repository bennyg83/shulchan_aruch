One who agreed... As stated in Yevamos 93a regarding one who sells the future fruits of a palm tree. Although Rav Huna there follows Rabbi Meir, the same principle applies according to the Sages where the obligation is one he is legally capable of undertaking. See above siman 112, seif 1, in the gloss.
Collected note: One who... Even the lender may retract, as stated at the end of siman 39 from Bava Basra, chapter Get Pashut, concerning writing a document for the borrower, and from the second chapter of Kiddushin. The same applies to the borrower, guarantor, and kablan. Teshuvos HaRashba, siman 158.
