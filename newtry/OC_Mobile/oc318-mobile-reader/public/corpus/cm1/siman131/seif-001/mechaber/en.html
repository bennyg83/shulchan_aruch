One who retracts after agreeing to become a guarantor. It contains 14 se'ifim.
If a person was persuaded to become a guarantor before the lender gave the borrower the money, he may retract even if a kinyan was made with him. If the lender disregarded the retraction and gave the borrower the money, he has no claim against the guarantor. But if the guarantee was undertaken after the money was given, once a kinyan was made with the guarantor he cannot retract. {Rama: In this respect, there is no distinction between an ordinary and an unconditional guarantor.}
