See above siman 129:8. Rama compares this to the case there where one guarantees return of a borrowed object and the borrower's obligation is itself subject to asmachta. Even though the borrower may be exempt, the guarantor can still be liable. The comparison is not complete. One can accept liability there yet still exempt the guarantor in the present cases, because the asmachta there is no greater than that of ordinary guaranty and is overcome by reliance. The asmachta here is stronger, as explained above. Sema. See Tumim for a lengthy discussion.
