There, in the name of Raavad in his gloss. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes there in the name of many Geonim, following the first view cited by Rambam that he must pay. Some even wrote that if the debtor was brought before the guarantor on Friday at twilight and escaped after Shabbat, the guarantor is not thereby released. He cites responsa of Rav Nachshon Gaon and Rav Tzemach and discusses Raavad's objections at length, concluding that on the principal legal question he is not qualified to decide between them.
