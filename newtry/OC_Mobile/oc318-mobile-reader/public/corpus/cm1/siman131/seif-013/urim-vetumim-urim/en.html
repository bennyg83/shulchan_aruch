And all those who come, etc. Sema says Mechaber exempts because Rambam generally holds that an undertaking of an indefinite amount is ineffective, as above in siman 60. He argued in Perishah that even authorities who reject Rambam there may agree here, because guaranty adds the further weakness of asmachta. The Shach disagrees and follows Beit Yosef, Maggid Mishneh, and Rivash that those who disagree with Rambam there disagree here as well. See the earlier simanim and Tumim, where I raised a serious difficulty on Maggid Mishneh in chapter 25 of the laws of loans.
