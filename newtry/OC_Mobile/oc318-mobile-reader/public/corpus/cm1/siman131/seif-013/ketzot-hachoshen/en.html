Rambam exempts a guarantor who undertakes liability for an amount not fixed. The Shulchan Aruch understands this as part of Rambam's general view that a person cannot obligate himself in an unquantified future amount. Accordingly, the authorities who disagree with Rambam in siman 60 and permit a person to undertake an unquantified obligation likewise disagree here and would bind the guarantor. See what I wrote in siman 60, seif katan 2.
