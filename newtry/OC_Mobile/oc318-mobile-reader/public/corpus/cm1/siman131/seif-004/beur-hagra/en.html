A guarantor who came, etc. As explained in the preceding siman, the law of a guarantor is like that of encumbered property. Just as a creditor may collect from purchasers when the debtor's unencumbered property was ruined, even where collection was delayed, so too here. Ramban explains that the Gemara denies collection because of negligence only where the debtor sold the property and the creditor expressly wrote that he waived his rights. Mere delay does not forfeit the claim, as explained in siman 111:12 and 111:14. See citation 40. See citation 444.
