(Seif 7) Wrongfully, and the funds were then lost through accident. Sema asks from siman 93 and answers that there the principal reliance was on Levi. I do not understand this, because there too the first partner entered the guaranty for Levi. There is no difficulty. That case concerns the possibility that Levi actually paid the gentile and was released in your presence, but the gentile later pursued you through a fabricated claim. In that situation your own misfortune caused the loss, as Rama writes nearby. See what I wrote in siman 93, seif 15.
