There, seif 4, in the name of Ramban in a responsum. Rabbeinu Yerucham likewise writes in his name.
The Mechaber's language follows what he wrote in Bedek HaBayit: In my humble opinion it is astonishing why the guarantor should not be able to claim, "My guarantee was only until a specified time. During that period you could have collected your debt and did not; you caused your own loss." I am close to saying that Ramban did not sign this responsum, analogous to the statement in Yevamot 21a that Mar son of Rav Ashi did not sign that ruling. Sema writes that for this reason the practice developed to make a new kinyan whenever the time for marriage obligations is extended. Perhaps Ramban would agree there, because it does not concern a direct monetary liability but only embarrassment.
Sema and Bach write that they did not find anyone who explicitly disputes this; see what they write.
