The language of Rambam, chapter 26 of Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh, law 8. Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's teachers and other commentators rule that the guarantor's claim against the borrower has the law of an oral loan, so the borrower may say, "I repaid you." But if the creditor wrote to the guarantor, "I have received payment," and transferred his rights to him, the guarantor stands in the creditor's place and has the creditor's law. If the creditor held a written loan, the borrower cannot say, "I repaid." Maggid Mishneh writes this from Rashba and states that it is straightforward: the guarantor's position should not be worse than that of an unrelated third party whom the creditor authorized. These rules were explained in the preceding siman and in seifim 1 and 2.
