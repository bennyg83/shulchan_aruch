Squandering. The Tur and Mechaber write the same in siman 73 concerning a lender and borrower, excluding those who disagree in both contexts. See there, and see the Tur in this siman and the Sema. See what I wrote above in Urim, siman 73, seif katan 39, where I agreed with Yam Shel Shlomo and the Shach that this applies specifically where he is dissipating assets improperly. Since we see that he is thereby causing loss to other people's money, beit din may intervene to preserve the creditor's property. If, however, the assets are being lost because of difficult economic conditions or similar circumstances, beit din has no authority to restrain the property before the due date. See there. See also Tumim, seif katan 12, concerning whether one who is preparing to flee is treated like one who is squandering assets.
