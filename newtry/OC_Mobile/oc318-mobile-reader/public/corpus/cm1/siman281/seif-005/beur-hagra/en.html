If, etc. Rif, Rosh, and Rav Hai Gaon rule this way, unlike Rabbeinu Chananel. Rif explains that the question was not conclusively resolved and therefore the lenient rule applies. Rabbeinu Chananel understood the final discussion as resolving it, but Rif and Ramban explain that the give-and-take itself remained uncertain. The issue turns on whether the enactments were made by one court or by two courts and whether Rabbi Yochanan ben Beroka's rule applies to a healthy person. See the full discussion in the Milchemet and the commentaries. See 100.
