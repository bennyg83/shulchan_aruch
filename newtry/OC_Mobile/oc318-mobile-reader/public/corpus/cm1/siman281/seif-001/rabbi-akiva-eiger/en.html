[Shulchan Aruch] A person cannot. Note: Likewise, a son cannot remove himself from his father's estate so as not to inherit, even by a kinyan. See Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer, end of siman 69. This is plain from the view of all the Rishonim in HaMaor, Ramban, Ran, and Rosh at the beginning of chapter HaKotev, and in Maggid Mishneh, chapter 23 of Hilchot Ishut. See also Responsa Chavot Yair, siman 50.
