My brother. “If he said so about his son and his son, like many sons, but in his son, there is a quaint between a cult, or he said, as he says, ‘I am told, ‘I will change a lot of inheritance and not healthy, and as such a column called the Rabbi of the island and the Rashi.’ A:
To his son. The Mechaber follows his own ruling in siman 246 that we say he appointed the recipient merely as an administrator only when the recipient is his son, not his daughter. With a son, there is no distinction whether he wrote to him in the language of a gift or of inheritance. The Ramah, Rosh, and Tur disagree, as Rama writes there in seif 6. See also there in seif 4. Here Rama did not mention this dispute because he relied on what he wrote there, which is the source of this law and of the rule based on assessment of intent. Sema. See Responsa M.A., siman 58, Responsa Rashach, volume 2, simanim 161 and 164, and volume 3, siman 12.
