The second wording does not repair the first. Nimukei Yosef concludes there: “Even concerning one person and one field, we say either that the first expression controls or that the last controls.” This means that where the speaker’s statements contradict one another, as in “by measure with a rope,” the law depends on whether we follow the first or the last expression. Regarding one who first says “He is my son” and then, in siman 279, “He is my slave,” the statements contradict one another, for saying “my son” is equivalent to saying “not my slave.” This also answers the Tur’s ruling in siman 279, seif 3, regarding retraction within the time required for speech, as I wrote there.
