[Shulchan Aruch] And field so and so shall be given to so and so. Note: If there is some defect in the transaction such that this recipient did not acquire the field, the wording of inheritance used for the other recipient does not help, since the gift language was ineffective for the recipient himself. Responsa Pnei Moshe, part 1, siman 34. He further writes there that it is doubtful whether the gift language helps only the immediately preceding or immediately following clause, but not one two clauses removed. Thus, if he said, "Reuven shall inherit field so and so, Shimon shall inherit field so and so, and field so and so shall be given to Levi," one may say that Shimon acquires but Reuven does not. Likewise, it may not help one two clauses later. Thus, if he said, "Field so and so shall be given to Levi, Reuven shall inherit field so and so, and Shimon shall inherit field so and so," one may say that Shimon does not acquire.
