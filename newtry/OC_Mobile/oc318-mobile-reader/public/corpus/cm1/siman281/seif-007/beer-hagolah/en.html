The Maggid Mishneh writes that Reish Lakish means that he never acquires until the testator says, 'So-and-so and so-and-so shall inherit field so-and-so and so-and-so, which I have given them as a gift, and they shall inherit.' The Halakhot establish the case where the gift language followed after the interval of tokh kedei dibbur. Rambam's view is that Reish Lakish mentioned gift language in the middle, but the same applies if it is at the beginning or end. His teacher wrote likewise. The Rashba wrote differently, but the words of Rambam and his teacher are primary.
