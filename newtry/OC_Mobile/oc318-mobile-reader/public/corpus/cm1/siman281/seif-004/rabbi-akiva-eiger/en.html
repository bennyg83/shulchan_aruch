[Shulchan Aruch] He said, so and so. Note: The Darkei Moshe writes: "Nimukei Yosef wrote that if one said concerning his ordinary son that he should take a double portion, he has the status of a firstborn with respect to prospective property; and there are those who disagree." Beit Yosef cites the words of Nimukei Yosef in the name of the Ritva, who says that it appears that the rule that such a declaration is effective for one son among other sons applies specifically when he distributes all his property, but not when he distributes only part of it.
