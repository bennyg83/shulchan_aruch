Tet) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 3. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is explained there that even Rabbi Yochanan concedes regarding a firstborn because the Torah says, "he may not give the birthright." Know that all the rules stated above, that one can direct inheritance among sons or descendants by saying, "He shall inherit all my property" or "he shall inherit half," apply only where no firstborn is among those from whom the inheritance is being redirected. If a firstborn is present, the statement is ineffective with respect to the firstborn's statutory portion when expressed in language of inheritance, even if the firstborn was not expressly mentioned. This is explained in seif 6.
