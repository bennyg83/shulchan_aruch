When does this apply, etc. This is the Mishna on 126b and the Gemara on 130a, and it applies even according to Rabbi Yochanan ben Beroka.
And even in a place, etc. A valid custom may govern commercial practice, but a custom without support in Torah is treated as an error in legal judgment. All the more so, a defective custom cannot uproot a Torah rule. See Tosafot and Masekhet Soferim.
(Supplement) And even, etc. As stated in the fourth chapter of Bava Metzia: can a Torah rule be displaced by local custom? (End of supplement.)
