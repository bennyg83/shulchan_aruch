[Shulchan Aruch] Whether he said it orally. Note: See Responsa Bnei Aharon, siman 56, in the name of Maharit, that with respect to property that will come to him afterward he cannot cause another to inherit it, because that which he has no power to give he likewise has no power to transmit by inheritance. This also appears from Responsa Rif, siman 11.
