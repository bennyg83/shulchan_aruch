Regarding the expression "her descendants," see Ketzot HaChoshen and what Netivot HaMishpat writes against him. In my humble opinion, the proper conclusion is that the daughters do not acquire and cannot extract anything by force, because a person cannot transfer ownership to one who does not yet exist. Consequently, the males inherit everything. However, if the heirs wish to pay the daughters their share under the shtar chatzi zachar, the males cannot reject them. The condition between the parties was that if he gives to her descendants, which includes daughters, he is discharged from the debt. We rule that a person may make a condition concerning one who does not yet exist. Therefore, if he fulfills the condition and gives the daughters their proper shares, the condition is fulfilled and he is discharged from the debt, and the males have no claim against the heirs. This is clear. See above at the end of siman 104 in the Sema and Shach regarding property that is only prospective versus property already in possession for purposes of a shtar chatzi zachar.
