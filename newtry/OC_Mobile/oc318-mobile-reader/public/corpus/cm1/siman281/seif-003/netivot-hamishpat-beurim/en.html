And Shimon shall not inherit. See Sema, seif katan 5, who explains the rule on the basis that once he said Reuven should inherit, Reuven immediately gained the designation. Anyone who examines Tur and Beit Yosef will see there is no difference whether he first said, "Reuven shall inherit," and afterward, "Shimon shall not inherit," or the reverse. The only ineffective formulation is where he links the statements, such as, "Reuven shall not inherit, rather Shimon." There the meaning is that because Reuven will not inherit, Shimon remains the sole natural heir; he did not affirmatively designate Shimon. But where the statements are not linked, his words imply an affirmative designation of the one named as heir.
