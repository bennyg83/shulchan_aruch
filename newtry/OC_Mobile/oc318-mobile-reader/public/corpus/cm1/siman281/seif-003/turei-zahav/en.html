(He may sell it.) The cited authority concludes with the reason: the testator did not write that the recipient should acquire from today and after death a fifth or sixth share in the property. Had he done so, he could not afterward give that share to others. Instead he said, “You shall take a share like one of my heirs.” When he gives portions to others, the shares of all the heirs are reduced, and the son-in-law’s share is likewise reduced, because the testator gave him only the status of one ordinary heir. For this reason the Rama writes immediately below that a creditor takes precedence over this gift. Since the daughter’s gift depends on the son’s inheritance, it does not take effect until the debt has first been paid.
