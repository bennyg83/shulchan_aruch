And he increased one person's share using inheritance language, etc. The Tur writes that when one increases one heir's share or equalizes the firstborn with the ordinary sons, Ri Halevi says that just as his words are ineffective regarding the firstborn, so they are ineffective regarding the ordinary sons. The whole distribution is disregarded and they divide anew as though he had said nothing. The reason is that those to whom he gave extra must make up the firstborn's required double share from the additional amount he attempted to give them. Since their shares are thereby reduced from the amounts he designated, the testamentary distribution itself has failed.
