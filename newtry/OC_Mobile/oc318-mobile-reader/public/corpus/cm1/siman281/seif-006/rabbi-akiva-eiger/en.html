[Shulchan Aruch] But with a firstborn. Note: Ramban, in his commentary on the Torah, writes that this applies specifically during the firstborn son's lifetime. But if the firstborn died during his father's lifetime, although his own son inherits his firstborn share, the elderly father may nevertheless say concerning that grandson, "He shall inherit such and such," and concerning another son, "My son so and so shall inherit such and such." This is cited in Responsa Rama of Padua, siman 44. Responsa Bnei Chayei by the author of Knesset HaGedolah, Choshen Mishpat, part 2, siman 79, writes that we do not rule this way, and Responsa Pnei Moshe, part 3, siman 43, agrees.
