The Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of the Rashba.
He explains there that although the son was excluded from the property, so long as it was not given to another person he inherits automatically. The sisters and their children are complete strangers in the presence of the brother's son who is legally fit to inherit. There is no wording in the will indicating that the remainder belongs to the sisters' children. Moreover, even if he said, 'Write and give to my sisters' children, for I give them all the rest of my property,' if he dies, the document is not written and delivered, because a document cannot take effect after death.
