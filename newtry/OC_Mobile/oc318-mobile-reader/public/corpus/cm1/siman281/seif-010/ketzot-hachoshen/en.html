If the gifts take effect one hour before their deaths. Rosh requires the reciprocal gifts to be structured from today and one hour before death. This cannot mean that the body is transferred today and the fruits only one hour before death, because then all parties would presently own the bodies and the circular gifts would have no workable structure. Nor can it simply mean an acquisition beginning today and completing one hour before death, like "from now and after thirty days," because if one of the parties dies first, another party could not have transferred onward property she herself had not yet fully acquired. The correct understanding is as a condition: the gift takes effect retroactively from today if the donor does not retract before the final hour of life. When one sister dies, the surviving recipients are found to have owned by virtue of the earlier transfer. At that point the surviving sisters can still retract from the gifts they had conditionally made to the deceased, because those gifts were likewise structured with a continuing power of retraction until the relevant final hour. The same result can be achieved by expressly conditioning the gift upon that sister dying first, with the property then belonging to the surviving sisters. This avoids the future property problem because the transfer is made now subject to a future condition.
