[Shulchan Aruch] Or brothers or daughters. Note: This is the Rambam’s language. It appears to prove that a brother inherits his brother directly, not through the intermediate inheritance of their father, as appears to be the view of Beit Yosef above in siman 253. According to Darkei Moshe there, subparagraph 7, however, a brother inherits through his father. If so, how can a person say of one brother that he should inherit everything? The deceased is not directly causing his brother to inherit. Rather, their father in the grave inherits him and then transmits the property to his sons, all of whom are equal in that process. Consider this carefully.
