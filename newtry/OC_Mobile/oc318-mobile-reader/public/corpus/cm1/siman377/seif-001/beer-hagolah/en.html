The wording of Rambam at the end of chapter 13 of Hilkhot Nizkei Mamon, from the Mishnah in Bava Batra chapter 7, 99b.
The Gemara concludes there in the name of Rav Yehudah that a boundary path acquired by the public may not be damaged, 100a. Rashbam explains that where the public leveled and repaired it for walking and the owners knew and remained silent, the owner may not later damage it, because he certainly waived it to the public. All the more so in the Mishnah, where he gave it knowingly and the public took possession. See Responsa Rashba simanim 1 and 152 regarding the extent of their chazakah, and Nimukei Yosef cited by Beit Yosef.
