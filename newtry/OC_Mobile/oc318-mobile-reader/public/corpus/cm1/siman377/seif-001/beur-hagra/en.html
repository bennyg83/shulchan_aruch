A quaint. G-d is there and VAT:
With permission. Tosafot, Bava Batra 12a, s.v. metzar.
(Supplement) A boundary, etc., with permission. This is specifically so, as is evident from Bava Kamma 28a, where the Gemara asks from the baraita and does not answer that Rabbi Yehuda's rule concerning a boundary applies, as it answers regarding a path that one granted. See Tosafot there. Rashi's explanation there is difficult to sustain.
(Supplement) A boundary, etc., with permission. See Oholot 18:4. This qualification is necessary only for the reason stated there. Rashi in Moed Katan does not explain it so, following his view that it applies even without permission, as he writes at the beginning of HaManiach. Yet Rashi's words are difficult, since the area is pure even for sacred food, as stated in Oholot, and his comments in HaManiach are likewise difficult.
