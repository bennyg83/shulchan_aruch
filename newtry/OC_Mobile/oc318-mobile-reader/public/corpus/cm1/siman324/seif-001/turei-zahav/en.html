(Seif 1: Some disagree and hold, etc.) This is difficult, for even under fixed rent the tenant must ordinarily give the agreed species grown in the field, as stated in siman 322 in the name of the Nimukei Yosef, because the owner may say, “I want the wheat of my land.” Perhaps these dissenters, representing the Ramban's view, hold that the Gemara ultimately rejected that rationale. The reason concerning seed is different: once the seed has fallen into the field, it is lost.
