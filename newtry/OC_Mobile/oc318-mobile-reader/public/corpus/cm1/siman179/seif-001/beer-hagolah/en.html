The language of the Tur. Rambam likewise writes in chapter 5 of Hilkhot Shutafin, law 8. Rif and Rosh write so regarding the Mishnah's rule, "Therefore, if the wall fell," etc., according to the Gemara's explanation on Bava Batra 4a that the case is where the wall fell, etc.
Tur, seif 2, in the name of Raavad. Baal HaTerumot likewise writes in his name in Shaar 49, and Nimukei Yosef in HaBayit VeHaAliyah. They infer it from the case of a house and upper story that collapsed, Bava Metzia 113b: at the time of collapse we do not know how many whole stones belonged to each and how many broken stones to each. If one party says, "Most of the whole stones are mine," he is not believed even after clearing them into his own domain and holding them. Rather, they divide equally. The same applies here.
