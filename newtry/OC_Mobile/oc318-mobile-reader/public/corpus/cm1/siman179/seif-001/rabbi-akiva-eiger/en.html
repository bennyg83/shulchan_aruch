[Shulchan Aruch] They divide it equally. Note: The Raavad derived this from the case in the chapter HaBayit VehaAliyah, cited above in siman 164, seif 3, and brought by Baal HaTerumot and Beit Yosef here. Gidulei Terumah expressed surprise: if we compare our case to that one, the Raavad should have derived an even broader rule, namely that even where we know that one party owns two thirds and the other one third, but each claims, "Two thirds are mine," the law should likewise be that they divide the disputed portion, just as there, where the complete units certainly belong to only one of them. He further asks that, regarding the disputed portion itself, namely the amount between one half and one third, the law should be that they divide it. See there.
