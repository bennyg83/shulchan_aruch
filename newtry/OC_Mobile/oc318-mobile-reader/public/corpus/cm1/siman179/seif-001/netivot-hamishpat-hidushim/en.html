Even if it remained in his possession. The Sema writes that even if it remained there for a long period during which partners ordinarily object, he still is not believed to claim sole ownership. The Shach distinguishes: during a period so long that partners ordinarily would object, he is believed. The Taz follows the Sema, and this is primary. See Beurim. If there are witnesses that the property originally came to this partner as a deposit from the other partner and there are witnesses who saw it in his possession, even the Shach agrees that lapse of time does not help.
