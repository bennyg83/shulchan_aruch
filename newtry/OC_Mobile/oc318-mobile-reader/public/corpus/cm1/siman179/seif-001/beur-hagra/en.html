(Supplement) Even if it remained in his possession for a long time. This is the Tur's ruling. The Tur writes likewise in siman 157, based on Rosh in the first chapter of Bava Batra, section 6, who writes that partners are not particular with one another even if the property remained for a long time. This is unlike Tosafot and Hagahot Asheri there, s.v. ve'im, etc. Rosh also does not explain the Gemara's phrase, 'It is obvious,' etc., as Tosafot do, nor does he distinguish between a long period and a short one, unlike Sema and Shach. His view accords with Rabbenu Yonah in siman 149, seif 5, who disputes Tosafot. See Maggid Mishneh at the end of chapter 2 of the Laws of Neighbors, who writes that Rif's approach is unlike Tosafot. This is also implied by Rif, who writes, 'We learn from here,' etc., and by the gloss to Rashi there in the name of Rabbenu Chananel: 'We learn from this that every comparable case,' etc. (End of supplement.)
