Seif 5. "If the robber sold it to a non-Jew and the non-Jew improved it, the improvement returns to the owners." Note: See my work on Yoreh Deah, fifth edition, in the responsa there. I concluded that although it is explicit here in the Shulchan Aruch, based on Bava Kamma 96, that a non-Jew does not acquire the improvement because the Sages did not enact their protective ordinance for a non-Jew, nevertheless according to those who hold that despair of recovery effects acquisition by rabbinic law, even though that rule likewise rests on a rabbinic enactment as the Shach writes here and as appears in Bava Kamma 66, it can operate even for a non-Jew by rabbinic law. See there for the reasoning, which is sound, and examine carefully.
