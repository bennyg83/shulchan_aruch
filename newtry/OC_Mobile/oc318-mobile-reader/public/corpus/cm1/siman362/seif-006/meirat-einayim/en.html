A non-Jew sold it to a Jew, etc. This is an unresolved question in the Gemara: since the second Jew comes through the non-Jew who sold it to him, perhaps he is no better than the non-Jew; or perhaps, because the property originally belonged to a Jew, the rule differs. The question was not resolved, and therefore the burden of proof rests on the claimant.
If the victim seized it, etc. So write the Rosh and Tur. In the Derishah I explained that although in ordinary monetary uncertainty they hold that seizure does not help, they agree that seizure can be effective in an unresolved Talmudic question. See what I wrote there and examine carefully.
