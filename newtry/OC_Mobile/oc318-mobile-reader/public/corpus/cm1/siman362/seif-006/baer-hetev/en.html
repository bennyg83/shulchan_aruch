The Sema writes that the Rosh and Tur rule likewise, and that although they generally hold that seizure is ineffective in a monetary doubt, they agree that seizure is effective in a doubt left unresolved by the Gemara. This is mistaken. The Rosh did not write this, and the claim that the Rosh and Tur concede in a Gemara doubt is also incorrect. On the contrary, in such a doubt they hold that even if one seized the property, it is removed from him. See what I wrote in siman 346, seif katan 14. See also siman 388. The truth is that the Tur copied the Rambam's language here and relied on what he wrote in simanim 388 and 390. Maharshal likewise writes this in HaGozel Kamma, siman 6. The same must be said regarding the Tur above in siman 352, seif 8, where he copied the Rambam and relied on what he wrote in those simanim. Shach.
