(Collected note) If it was, etc. Bava Metzia 43a discusses the case, and even where he later broke or drank it, the act of theft is fixed at the time of unauthorized taking, as shown by the Gemara's valuation question. This is certainly true if it broke by itself. (Collected note) Whether he broke it, etc. This follows all the more from a case where it broke on its own, so that the sinner should not benefit from the subsequent breakage. Bava Kamma 65a states the point explicitly.
