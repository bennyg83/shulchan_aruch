Seif 8. 'One who lives in his fellow's courtyard,' etc. Note: There is room to ask whether, if he caused a loss worth less than a perutah, he must pay the entire benefit, or whether, since less than a perutah is not legally money, he need not pay at all. See Tosafot, chapter 3 of Ketubot, s.v. 'which can be restored with difficulty,' from which it appears that even if he caused a loss of less than a perutah, the entire benefit is charged to him. This is a novel ruling. Seif 7. 'Some say that when the courtyard is not intended for rental,' etc. Note: See what I wrote questioning this from Bechorot 37a, regarding defects that do not disgust a person, where the price is reduced accordingly. This would support those who disagree here. I discussed the matter at length in my work on Yoreh Deah.
