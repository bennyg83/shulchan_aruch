But some disagree. Meaning they disagree with the Rambam's rule that the robber does not acquire appreciation that accrued before the owner's despair. They hold that he does acquire it. More than that, even a reversible change can entitle him to the improvement. It appears that the same applies where there is no physical change at all, such as where he robbed a pregnant animal or one laden with wool and the fetus or wool increased while in his possession. Even if the owner removes the animal while still pregnant or wool bearing, the robber pays the amount of the increase that accrued in his possession. See what I write in siman 363:5.
