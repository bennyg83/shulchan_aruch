Although they despaired, etc. Maharshal rules that if the object appreciated after despair, the increase belongs to the robber. This does not appear correct. The Rambam rules that appreciation after despair still belongs to the robbed party, and the Tur above in siman 354 states without distinction that if the stolen object increased in value or grew fat, payment is according to its present value. Since an ordinary theft is presumed to involve despair, the Tur's unqualified ruling necessarily includes appreciation after despair. The Semag and Rabbeinu Yerucham agree, and this is the principal ruling.
