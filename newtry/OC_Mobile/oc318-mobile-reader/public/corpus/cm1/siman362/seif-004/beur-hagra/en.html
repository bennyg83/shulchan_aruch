One who robs, etc. This ruling is extremely difficult. Maggid Mishneh objects that if the robber himself does not acquire the improvement, how can a purchaser or heir acquire it? Raavad likewise rejects the derivation. Moreover, an heir is not treated as a purchaser, and change of possession without despair does not acquire. The text is therefore corrupt. The clause beginning, 'the injured owner returns,' should be omitted, and the discussion should resume with improvement produced by expenditure, following Rif in Bava Metzia. It belongs after seif 9.
