[Seif 12, or seif 10] Or it was lost. The Sema explains that exemption applies only where it was lost by itself, while ordinary loss is negligence. He states this more fully in siman 354. This does not appear correct. His words imply that any ordinary loss occurring while traveling requires payment. Yet the Gemara says that when one broke or drank the wine he became a robber through that act. If he performed no act but merely left it and it was lost in the ordinary manner, he did not become a robber at that time. This is included in the ruling in siman 354 that where it was lost by itself he pays only one zuz. The earlier ruling that if he broke or lost the vessel he pays four refers to actively causing the loss, such as leaving it in an unguarded place and it was stolen. This is clear.
