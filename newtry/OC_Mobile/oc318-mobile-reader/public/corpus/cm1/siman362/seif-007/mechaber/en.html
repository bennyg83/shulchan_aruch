It has already been explained that appreciation occurring after despair or after a change in the stolen object belongs to the robber, even if it arose on its own. Thus, if he stole a cow that became pregnant in his possession, whether it gave birth before he was sued or has not yet given birth, or stole a ewe that grew wool in his possession, whether it was shorn before suit or not, once the owners despaired he pays according to the value at the time of theft. If it gave birth or was shorn, the offspring and wool belong to him. If not yet born or shorn, the appreciation is assessed, he receives its value from the victim, and returns the animal itself.
