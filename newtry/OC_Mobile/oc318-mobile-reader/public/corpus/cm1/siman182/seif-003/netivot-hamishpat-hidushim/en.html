The purchase is void. See the Beurim. This applies specifically where the agent disclosed that he was acting for a principal. If he did not disclose this, the purchase remains valid so long as the error is below the legal threshold of ona'ah, or until the period in which the agent could show the merchandise to a merchant or relative has elapsed. The agent cannot say that he was prevented from showing it to a merchant because he immediately transferred the item to the principal; in that case the principal is treated like the purchaser himself. Likewise, if the agent delivered it to the principal and the principal retained it for the period needed to show it to a merchant, the principal can no longer claim against the agent. Whether the purchaser can rescind when the agent was overcharged by any amount after disclosing that he was an agent is explained below in siman 185.
