And he bought at an excessive price. If an agent or partner purchased at a price above market by less than one-sixth and the overcharge cannot be recovered from the seller, it requires study whether the agent must reimburse the principal. Merchants generally do not object to a variance of less than one-sixth, while as against a seller who knows that the buyer is an agent the law of overcharge may apply even to a smaller amount. It is reasonable to consider whether the same standard governs the relationship between agent and principal. This requires study.
