If an agent erred and bought for more than the proper price, even by the smallest amount, the transaction is void, whether it concerns land or movable property. The principal may say, 'I sent you to improve my position, not to harm it.' Therefore, if the principal expressly appointed him to act whether advantageously or disadvantageously, then even if the agent sold property worth one hundred for one dinar, or bought property worth one dinar for one hundred, the principal cannot rescind and must perform according to the stipulation. Rama: If one instructed another to handle a matter and the agent incurred expenses, the principal need not reimburse amounts exceeding what is ordinarily spent on such a matter, because he could not have anticipated such expenditure. Ordinary expenses must be reimbursed. For disputes between them, see siman 91, seif 3, and siman 93, seif 4.
