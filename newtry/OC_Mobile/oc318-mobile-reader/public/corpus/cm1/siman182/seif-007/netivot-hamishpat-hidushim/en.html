At the low market rate. Ordinarily, if one gives money without stipulating that goods will be supplied at the later lower price, the seller may supply according to the price at the time the money was given, even if prices later fall, though if he retracts he may incur the "Who exacted payment" curse. Therefore the principal must stipulate that if the market price falls, delivery will be according to the lower rate. If he acted through an agent and the agent erred by failing to make that condition, the rule follows accordingly. Sema.
