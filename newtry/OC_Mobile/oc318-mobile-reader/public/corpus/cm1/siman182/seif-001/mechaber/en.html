How an agent is appointed, the law when he mistakenly buys too dearly, and the law when he disregards the principal's instructions. It contains twelve seifim.
Rama: In every matter, a person's agent is legally like the person himself, except with respect to transgression, because there is no agency for a transgression. This qualification applies when the agent himself is subject to the prohibition. If he is not personally subject to it, agency may operate even for the prohibited act. If one tells an agent, 'Go sell land or movable property for me,' or, 'Buy for me,' the agent may sell or buy and carry out the agency, and all his acts are valid. Appointing an agent requires neither a kinyan nor witnesses; a verbal appointment between principal and agent suffices. Witnesses are needed only to prove the appointment if one later denies it. Rama: Therefore, if one tells another, 'Buy this merchandise and I will become your partner,' and the other buys it, the first cannot withdraw, because the buyer acted as his agent.
