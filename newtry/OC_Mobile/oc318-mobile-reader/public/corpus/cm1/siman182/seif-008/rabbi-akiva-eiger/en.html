[Shulchan Aruch] And the purchase is acquired. Note: This refers to a matter that can be corrected, such as where the agent sold an item worth one hundred for two hundred. The purchase can stand and the agent can pay the additional one hundred from his own funds, and similarly in the reverse case. But if it cannot be corrected, for example, where he was appointed to rent out the principal's courtyard for one year and instead rented it for three years, the transaction is void even if he did not notify the other party that he was an agent. This is like the case in seif 5, which certainly concerns a situation in which he did not notify him. Mahari ben Lev, part 1, siman 79. See also Responsa Tashbetz, part 4, siman 22.
