Or when he sold them in two deeds, etc. This is Rabbeinu Tam's view, distinguishing whether the agent himself completed the documentation, in which case no additional burden remains for the principal, from where he did not. See the Derishah in Rabbeinu Tam's name. Beit Yosef wonders why the Tur did not make this distinction. In the Derishah I wrote that the objection is unfounded because the Tur follows Rabbeinu Yitzchak, who does not accept that distinction. Instead he distinguishes between a sale made through two deeds, where even if the agent completed them the sale is void despite the principal never having expressly revealed an objection, and a sale in one deed. I proved from the Gemara that Rabbeinu Yitzchak's view is primary. It is therefore surprising that the Mechaber rules here that even when the agent sold in two deeds, the sale stands if the agent completed the documentation, and also surprising that Rama does not cite Rabbeinu Yitzchak's view at all, although Darkei Moshe records it in seif 14.
