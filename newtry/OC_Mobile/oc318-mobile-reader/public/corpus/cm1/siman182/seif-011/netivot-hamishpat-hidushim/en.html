The Sema writes that Rabbi Yitzchak's view in Tosafot is primary: if the agent sold in two documents, even if the agent himself signed them, the sale is void because of the burden of multiple documents. See the Beurim. The principal ruling is that the Rif and Rambam hold that when the principal specified a kor and the agent changed the terms and sold a letekh, the sale is void even where multiple documents create no inconvenience. We rule according to the view that the principal's wording expresses insistence. Whenever the agent departs from the principal's instructions, even in a matter that does not practically burden one side more than the other, the sale is void. For the same reason, where the principal specified sale to one person and the agent sold to two, the sale is void. But if the principal merely said, "Sell a field," without specifying anything further, the agent may sell even to many people together, even where multiple documents result.
