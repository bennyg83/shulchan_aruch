And had them sign. The Sema writes that the primary view is that of Rabbeinu Yerucham, who maintains that there is no distinction based upon whether the agent had the documents signed. Rather, the distinction is between a sale made through two documents, where even if the agent had them signed the sale is void although the principal never disclosed that he objected to this, and a sale made through one document. The Bach likewise takes this view. See there. It is therefore surprising that the Mechaber rules here that if the agent had the documents signed the sale remains valid even through two documents. It is also surprising that the Rama does not mention Rabbeinu Yerucham's opinion at all, although he cites it in Darchei Moshe. Sema.
