That he stipulated with him. Specifically with an unpaid agent do we presume that he conditioned his agency so that he would not be personally liable if he deviated, since he receives no payment. With a paid agent we do not make that presumption. Sema. The Shach writes that if the deviation resulted in very substantial overreaching, then even for an item not ordinarily subject to overreaching the transaction can be void because we presume the principal is not a fool. See Beurim, where the Shach's statement requires analysis.
