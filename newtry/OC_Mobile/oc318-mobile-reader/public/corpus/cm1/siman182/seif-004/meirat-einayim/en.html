That he stipulated with him whether for proper execution, etc. Although the principal asserts certainty and the seller who is in possession says he does not know, we still apply the rule that one who seeks to extract must bring proof, as the Tur and Mechaber explain below in simanim 223 and 224. There is also a logical basis: ordinarily the agent will protect himself by stipulating that his agency remains effective even if he makes an error, so that he will not become personally liable for correcting the mission, especially since he receives no wage for acting as agent.
