The seller may say, etc. It appears that according to the Ramah this applies only where the deviation was not so great as to constitute nullification of the sale. We then say that the principal presumably appointed the agent with authority whether to improve or to impair, because he ordinarily made him as his own representative. It does not apply where an item worth one hundred was sold for one dinar, for a person is not presumed foolish enough to stipulate that even an extreme distortion should remain valid. Examine carefully. See below siman 185, seif 5.
