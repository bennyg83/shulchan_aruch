Shach, beginning of seif katan 6. Note: see Maharshal on Tosafot 99b regarding an agent appointed to sell. Even if the agent did not inform the purchaser that he was acting as an agent, the purchaser cannot return the item, since what was sold was not the agent's own property. This rule applies specifically to an agent appointed to purchase. See there. From the glosses of the Ketav Sofer.
