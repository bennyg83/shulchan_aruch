There, chapter 2, law 4. Kesef Mishneh writes that Rambam learned this from the incident before Rav Nachman in Get Pashut, Bava Batra 169b, where a sale made by an agent who purchased without warranty remains valid. In Almanah Nizonet, Ketubot 99b, however, the Gemara says that if an agent errs, the sale is void. Rambam understands the latter as where the agent disclosed that he was acting as agent; where he did not disclose it, the sale remains valid.
