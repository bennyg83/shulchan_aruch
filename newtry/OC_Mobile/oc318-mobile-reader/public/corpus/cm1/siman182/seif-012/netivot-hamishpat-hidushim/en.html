It was explained in Tur Yoreh Deah. There the Mechaber rules that produce consumed under the mistaken arrangement is recoverable through beit din. Beit Yosef explains that although the issue is only rabbinic interest, it can still be recovered because the waiver was based on mistake. The Sema disagrees. Even a mistaken waiver does not create judicial recovery here because the arrangement was fundamentally a sale. Only where the transaction began as a loan does mistaken waiver support recovery. With a sale, that doctrine does not apply.
