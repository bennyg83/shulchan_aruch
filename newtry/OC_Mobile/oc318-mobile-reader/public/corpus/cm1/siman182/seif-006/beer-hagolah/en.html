The language of Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilkhot Sheluchin, law 3, from the incident before Rav Nachman in Get Pashut, Bava Batra 169b.
Meaning that the document expressly states that there is no warranty against him. If it were unspecified, omission of warranty is treated as a scribal error, as explained above at the beginning of siman 39.
The incident before Rav Nachman must be explained as one where the agent did not disclose the agency. Raavad writes so according to Rambam, and Maggid Mishneh likewise writes that if he had disclosed that he bought for the principal, the sale would be void.
