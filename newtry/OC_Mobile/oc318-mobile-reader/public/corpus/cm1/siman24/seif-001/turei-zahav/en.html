In the gloss: “Although we know he has witnesses who know,” etc. Siman 16 allows this even where his property is not depreciating. The Sema forces both cases to involve depreciating property, but the distinction is simpler. There the defendant seeks witnesses to refute the claimant’s very demand. Here he does not deny that demand but seeks to bring a separate counterclaim; therefore depreciating property is required before delaying the first claim. Maharshal gives this answer.
