This is specifically where Shimon raises an affirmative counterclaim, so there are effectively two claimants and we therefore address the original claimant first. But if Shimon says merely, 'I paid,' and the matter is one where witnesses are known to exist, additional time may be granted as explained at the beginning of siman 16, because the second party is not making a separate claim but only defending himself. Urim VeTumim similarly writes regarding a claim that collateral depreciated, meaning that such a claim resembles payment rather than a new independent claim. This also accords with siman 88:21, where a matter framed as a separate demand does not count as partial admission. Ketzot HaChoshen misunderstood this distinction.
