The plaintiff first. See Baer Heitev and another explanation in Responsa Shevut Yaakov, volume 1, siman 144. Birkei Yosef, letter 2, writes in the name of Rav Levi that Maran deliberately used the Talmud's negative formulation, 'we attend only to the plaintiff,' to teach that even if one imagines some ground of advantage to the defendant, beit din nevertheless attends first to the plaintiff, except where delay would cause the defendant's property to depreciate. Thus in no ordinary circumstance do we attend first to the defendant.
