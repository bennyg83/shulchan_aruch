Even though he presently has no witnesses, since witnesses may ultimately arrive, all the more so in such a case. He therefore agrees with Riva's explanation, as written below, and the same applies if the claimant wishes, etc. This is explicit in Tosefta, chapter 6: "How is judgment conducted? The judges sit, those being judged stand, and the claimant opens his words first, as it says, 'Whoever has a matter shall approach them.'" (End of collected note.)
