And this matter, etc. As stated in seif 19: if the damager says, etc. Even if he had offered to heal him himself the injured party could refuse because he may say, "you are like a lion crouching over me." The same applies to a physician who treats for nothing. This is why the Gemara says that if growths developed because of the wound, etc., and the rule refers to this circumstance. So too seif 19 says, "if he did not," etc. See Sema.
