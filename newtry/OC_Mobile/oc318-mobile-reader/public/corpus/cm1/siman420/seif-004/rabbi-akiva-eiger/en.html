[Rama] He said this because of kinship. Note: This implies that where one called another a mamzer there is no obvious way to reinterpret his words. If he said it to someone who does not live in that place, it requires study whether he may explain that by "mamzer" he meant that the person is a stranger and foreigner there, as Kiddushin 72b interprets the verse "A mamzer shall dwell," referring to Jews who were regarded there like foreigners. The practical ruling requires study.
