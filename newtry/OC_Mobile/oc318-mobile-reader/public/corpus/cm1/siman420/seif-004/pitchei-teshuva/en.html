We assess how much his value was reduced. Dagul MeRevavah explains that the tortfeasor need not pay the victim's full market value as a skilled artisan, because despite the injury he remains capable of that occupation. Nevertheless, he must at least pay the full amount of depreciation measured as though the victim were not a skilled artisan. See Sema, seif katan 17. If, however, the reduction in value specifically attributable to his occupation exceeds the full value he would have had as an unskilled person, then the tortfeasor must pay that greater occupational depreciation.
