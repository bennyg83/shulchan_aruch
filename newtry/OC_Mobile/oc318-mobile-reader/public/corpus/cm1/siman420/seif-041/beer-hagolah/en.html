The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara's term for one form of blow is 'senokeret,' valued at thirteen. Rambam explains it as striking with gathered fingers. Rashi gives another explanation, which the Maggid Mishneh considers correct. Nimukei Yosef cites Raah and Arukh: kicking with the foot is valued at five, because he struck with the five toes; a blow with the knee at three, because it has three joints; a backhanded blow at thirteen, corresponding to the thirteen finger joints involved besides the thumb joint.
This is one explanation of the Mishnah's statement that one who 'tokea' his fellow pays a sela, there in chapter 8, 90a. Most commentators explain it as shouting loudly into his ear, or according to another explanation striking beside his ear, as Rashi explains. The law follows the first Tanna. Maggid Mishneh.
