This was an unresolved question in the Gemara there, 90b, and was resolved from Rav Yehudah in the name of Rav: every mention of money in the Torah means Tyrian currency, while rabbinic amounts mean provincial currency, Bava Kamma chapter 4, 36b. Rambam likewise writes in chapter 3, law 10, that these sela'im are of the silver currency of Eretz Yisrael at that time, each sela containing half a dinar of silver and three and a half dinars of copper, etc.
