He is exempt. See Baer Heitev. Responsa Shvut Yaakov, part I, siman 180, cites Maharshal, chapter HaChovel, siman 38, who writes that he is nevertheless liable under the laws of Heaven, since this is no better than frightening another person and other forms of indirect causation. Shvut Yaakov disagrees. Frightening another is different, because a person should anticipate that people are ordinarily frightened by such conduct. He also adduces proof from the Gemara in chapter HaKones. Rather, in a case such as this one he is entirely exempt, in accordance with the unqualified language of the Gemara and all the poskim, none of whom state that he remains liable under the laws of Heaven; see there.
