peh) The language of the Rambam there, chapter 1, law 18, from a question that was resolved in the Gemara.
tzadi) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is straightforward.
kuf) Explicit in the Mishnah on 90a and in Rav Pappa's statement in the Gemara on 91a, but not where the injury is concealed by clothing.
reish) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is not explicit in the Gemara, but it is correct for the reason given by the Raavad: even where witnesses do not know whether the homeowner injured him or he injured himself, the Sages said he swears and collects, because people do not ordinarily injure themselves and we follow presumptions. Here, where witnesses saw that he was injured and a small stone would not ordinarily cause such an injury, all the more so.
