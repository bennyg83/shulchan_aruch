Even if he struck a Canaanite slave, he receives lashes. See Baer HaGolah, who writes that the Tur and Rambam read: “the Canaanite slave of another,” and that the Beit Yosef expressed surprise at this; see the Sema and Bach on the matter. See also Responsa Shvut Yaakov, part I, siman 181, who writes that all the later authorities erred in this matter because they overlooked the wording of the Rambam, Hilchot Rotzeach U'Shemirat HaNefesh 2:12. He concludes that, in light of all this, the words of the Tur and Rambam are entirely correct and free of difficulty; see there. Likewise, in Responsa Chacham Tzvi, Lviv edition, in the collected responsa at the end of the volume, siman 54, he answers the Beit Yosef on this point and writes: “It is clear to me that one is exempt for striking his own Canaanite slave, whether the injury is worth a perutah or less than a perutah”; see there. See also the commentary of the Vilna Gaon.
