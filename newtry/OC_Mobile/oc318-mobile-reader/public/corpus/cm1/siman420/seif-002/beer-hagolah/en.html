The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara concludes in Bava Kamma chapter 8, 88a, that a slave is obligated in mitzvot like a woman. The law is also explained in Makkot chapter 3, beginning of 9a. Tur and Rambam there read 'another person's Canaanite slave.' Beit Yosef wonders why it must be another person's slave, for even one's own slave may not be struck, since he is obligated in mitzvot. Rambam likewise writes at the end of chapter 15 of Hilkhot Sanhedrin, though Kesef Mishneh there gives another reason. The Mechaber therefore omitted the words 'another person's.'
