[Gloss] For he can explain that he meant “disqualified.” Note: Responsa Rashdam writes that the same applies to one who calls another person a slave, for he can explain his words by saying that he meant a Hebrew slave, unless he expressly called him a Canaanite slave. See Responsa Mahari Beit Levi, rule 5, siman 48; Responsa Tzemach Tzedek, siman 54; Responsa Chavot Yair, siman 62; and Responsa Pnei Moshe, part 3, siman 10.
