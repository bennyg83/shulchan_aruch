For five. These five categories are collected from the best of the defendant's property, like all damages. This is stated by the Rambam at the beginning of Hilchot Chovel, and by the Semak. The Maggid Mishneh explains that the law is explicit at the beginning of Bava Kamma, which states that all derivative categories are like the primary categories with respect to payment from superior property. The Rif, Rosh, and Nimukei Yosef rule likewise at the beginning of Bava Kamma, as does Maharshal there, siman 6. The same rule applies above in simanim 385 and 388, seif 2. Shach.
