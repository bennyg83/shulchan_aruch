If the injury, etc. The rules from "and if there is no," through seif 9 derive from the Yerushalmi cited by the Rif and Rosh there.
Some say, etc. Rashi on Bava Kamma 91a, s.v. "and medical expenses." In the Yerushalmi at the beginning of the chapter, Ben Azzai says that the injured party is compensated for the increase in his food needs. It explains: originally I ate lentils and vegetables, but now I can eat only meat and chickens; the additional amount is given to him.
