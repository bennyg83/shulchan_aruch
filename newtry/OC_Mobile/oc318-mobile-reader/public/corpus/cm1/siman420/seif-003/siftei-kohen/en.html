He is liable for the five categories of damages. These five payments are collected from the best-quality portion of his property, like all tort damages. This is the Rambam's ruling at the beginning of Hilchot Chovel. The Semag states the same, and the Maggid Mishneh explains that it is explicit at the beginning of Bava Kamma, where all derivatives of the primary categories of damage are said to pay from the best property. The Rif, Rosh, Nimukei Yosef, and Maharshal likewise rule this way, as do the rulings above in simanim 385 and 388, seif 2.
