We assess how much his value has diminished. Dagul Mervavah writes that this means he need not pay the entire value that the injured person is worth by virtue of his trade, since he remains capable of practicing that trade. Nevertheless, he must at least pay the diminution measured against the full value he would have had if he were not a skilled tradesman. See Sema, seif katan 17. However, if the diminution caused to him specifically in his trade exceeds his entire value as an ordinary person, the tortfeasor must pay that greater diminution.
