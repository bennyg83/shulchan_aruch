(Sema, seif katan 56) Ri explains that it is permitted to strike him, etc. Note: This is according to Rabbeinu Yonah, who explains the phrase "he descends to his life" as referring to the jaw, as cited in Shitah Mekubetzet. But Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 1, siman 42, writes that even if the other person first insulted him with a degrading name and he then struck him, the one who struck still violates a prohibition.
