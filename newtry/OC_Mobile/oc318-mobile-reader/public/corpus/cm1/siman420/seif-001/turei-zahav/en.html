(Seif 2: a Canaanite slave.) The Sema's explanation is extremely difficult. If it is his own slave, the master should in every case be liable to lashes whenever no monetary payment is due, because where the injury is worth less than a perutah there is no monetary liability to displace lashes. Rather, it appears that liability applies specifically to another person's slave. With his own slave he is exempt because he has authority to strike and discipline him, as the Rama writes in Yoreh Deah, siman 421, seif 13. Therefore he is exempt even if he injures him, just as a teacher is exempt from exile if he accidentally kills his student through a disciplinary blow, as stated in Perek Eilu Hen HaGolin: this excludes a teacher disciplining his student.
