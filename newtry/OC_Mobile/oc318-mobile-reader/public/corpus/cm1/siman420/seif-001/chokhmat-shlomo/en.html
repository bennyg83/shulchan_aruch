Seif 38. In the gloss: 'One who says to his fellow, You act like a mamzer, or You are like a mamzer, has done nothing.' Note: See the holy Zohar, Parashat Mishpatim, on the verse 'You shall be holy people to Me.' It supports the first opinion, that one who says 'like a wicked person' has not committed a prohibited insult. It brings proofs from the verses 'she has become like a widow' and 'God was like an enemy.' See there.
