Sema writes that the same applies wherever later authorities mention that communities imposed a ban concerning this matter: there is no need to place the offender under a new ban, because one who violates their enactment is already under the existing ban. He further writes that since Rama says generally that 'they must release him,' this implies ordinary release from a vow by three people. The stricter requirement of one hundred people from three communities in three countries applies only where one wishes to marry a second wife while the first is alive, etc.
In my humble opinion the text should read 'to carry out judgment upon him.' The wording there is: on condition that he accept upon himself whatever beit din determines.
