And some say, etc. This is inferred from the Gemara's citation of Rabbi Elazar. See Tosafot at the end of s.v. 'he humiliated him,' etc. Rif and Rambam apparently did not have that reading. This is supported by the first baraita, which already states Rabbi Elazar's view concerning a deaf-mute and a minor; otherwise the later baraita should have resolved the question. Bach's forced explanation is unconvincing.
