A Torah scholar. Community leaders and communal officers have the status of Torah scholars for this purpose, as stated in Responsa Mahari Beit Levi, siman 29. See there at length. The gloss adds that the work Bnei Chayyai copies in this siman and the following siman many rulings cited by Knesset HaGedolah from responsa of early and later authorities concerning fines, humiliation, and similar matters. They are too numerous to reproduce here, and one who wishes to examine them should consult them in their original locations.
