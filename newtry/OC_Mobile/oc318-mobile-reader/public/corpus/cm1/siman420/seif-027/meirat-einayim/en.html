But if he merely embarrassed him, they give, etc. In the Perishah I proved that when embarrassment accompanies an injury for which the other categories of compensation are collected immediately, no additional time is given for payment of the embarrassment. Since damage and other monetary losses are being collected, pain and embarrassment are collected together with them even though they are not themselves direct pecuniary losses. Only where the sole liability is embarrassment do we give the tortfeasor time to pay. The same should apply to pain standing alone, for example where a hot spit causes pain privately without visible embarrassment or economic loss. The Tur, Rambam, and Mechaber mention only embarrassment because the incident before Rabbi Akiva involved a woman whose head covering was removed and who was embarrassed.
