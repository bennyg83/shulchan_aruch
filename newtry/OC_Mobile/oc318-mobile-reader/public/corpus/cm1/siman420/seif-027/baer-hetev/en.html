Who humiliated him. In the Perishah I proved that no extension of time is granted for humiliation that accompanies bodily injury. Since no extension is granted for the other payments collected for bodily injury because they involve monetary loss, pain and humiliation are collected together with them even though those two categories do not themselves involve monetary loss. Only where the sole claim is for humiliation is additional time granted. It appears that the same is true of pain standing alone, for example where one burns another with a skewer in a place where no one sees and therefore there is no humiliation. Since there is no monetary loss, the defendant is granted time to pay. The authorities discuss this rule specifically regarding humiliation rather than pain because the incident in the Gemara involved the woman before Rabbi Akiva whose head covering was removed and who was humiliated.
