He is exempt. See Baer Heitev and Shevut Yaakov, volume 1, siman 180. He cites Maharshal, chapter HaChovel, siman 38, who writes that the actor is nevertheless liable toward Heaven, no less than one who frightens another or other forms of indirect damage. Shevut Yaakov disagrees. Frightening another is different because a person should anticipate that people are naturally susceptible to fear. He also brings proof from the Gemara, chapter HaKones. He concludes that in a case like the one here the person is completely exempt, consistent with the plain wording of the Gemara and all the decisors, none of whom mention any heavenly liability.
