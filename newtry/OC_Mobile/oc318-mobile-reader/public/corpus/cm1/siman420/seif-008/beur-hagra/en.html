With the kerchief in his hand, or, etc. This is Rambam's wording. The Yerushalmi reads 'tumos,' meaning a document, as stated in the Tosefta at the end of chapter 9: if he struck him with the back of his hand, with paper, a ledger, untanned hides, or a bundle of documents in his hand, he pays four hundred zuz, not because the blow causes pain but because it is degrading. The Tosefta cites verses concerning striking the cheek. The same applies to a kerchief. Rosh, citing Arukh, explains it as parchment.
