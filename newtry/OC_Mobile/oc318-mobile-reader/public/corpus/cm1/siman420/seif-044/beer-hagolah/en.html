Beit Yosef writes that in the Rif text in our possession it states that he agreed with the other Rabbis that this entire sum is the victim's. Thus he explicitly agrees with Rambam, and we rule accordingly, especially because the law is to retain money in possession. Beit Yosef further writes that if the person struck him with such force that the blow caused substantial physical damage, it appears Rambam would agree that medical expenses and loss of labor are also paid.
