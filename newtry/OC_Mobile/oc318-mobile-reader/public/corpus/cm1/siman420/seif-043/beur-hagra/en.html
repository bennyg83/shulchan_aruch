(Supplement) When does this apply, etc. This applies even to the first three cases mentioned in the Yerushalmi cited by Rosh. The baraita there teaches fixed amounts for a kick, etc., indicating that the rule concerns a respectful person, as in the Mishna. Although the Yerushalmi differs from our Gemara regarding the measure and does not use the toes, it measures the hand by the thumb: its three joints equal the two joints of the other fingers.
Some say that these, etc. Our text of Rif is the reverse and shows that he agreed with those who disagree, like Rambam in seif 41. Rif brings proof from the case of Hanan on 37a, where only half a zuz was imposed. That proof is not conclusive, because a blow of that kind ordinarily entails neither medical expenses nor loss of employment. Rosh cites Rif in the opposite direction, in accordance with another reading, but that proof too is inconclusive.
