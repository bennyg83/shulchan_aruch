The Maggid Mishneh writes that this distinction, from one who strikes his fellow through this point, is explicit in the Mishnah following the first Tanna against Rabbi Akiva, as explained in the Gemara 91a. But the first three categories are not explicit as applying only to a person of stature. Rambam equates them, which is correct. Tosafot likewise write so in the name of Ri at the end of 27b.
