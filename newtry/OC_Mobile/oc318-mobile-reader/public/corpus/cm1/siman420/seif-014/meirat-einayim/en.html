Only a scar. Meaning, the wound healed but left a permanent mark upon the skin. The Maggid Mishneh, cited by the Beit Yosef, questions whether liability for such a scar should apply in every location. Regarding one who injures his daughter, the law appears to concern specifically a scar on her face, where appearance is especially significant. Here the Mechaber writes without qualification. In the Perishah I suggested that a daughter may be less particular with her father about scars in less visible places, whereas one unrelated person is particular even about such a permanent mark caused by another.
