There, law 13. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the dispute of Rav Acha and Ravina. Rambam rules that a gift is like a sale, perhaps because the reasoning of the one who equates them is more persuasive. Tur likewise writes in the name of the Ramah, explaining that the purchaser is certainly in possession while the seller's claim is doubtful, and a doubt cannot remove a certainty. It also appears to me that since whenever Rav Acha and Ravina dispute the halachah follows the lenient view, here too this is a leniency for the defendant, namely the purchaser from the robber who presently holds the land.
