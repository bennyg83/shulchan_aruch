And the robber told him, "This one," etc. The Tur writes: "and he did not wish to collect his debt except from this field." In the Perishah I wrote that this is not literal. Even if the creditor collected this field without expressly insisting upon it, we still assume the robber intended to preserve the field for the purchaser. Therefore the Mechaber carefully omitted the words, "he did not wish except this one." In the later clause it is specifically where the victim has no other land except this one that the mechanism fails. This is unlike the Ir Shushan, who preserved the Tur's nonliteral wording.
