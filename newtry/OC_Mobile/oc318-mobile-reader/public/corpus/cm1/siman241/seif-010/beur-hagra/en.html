One who says, etc. Gittin 74a states that she may not marry another until the affirmative condition is fulfilled. Ran asks that Gittin 83a implies that conditions are generally not feared and she may marry immediately. He concludes that a passive condition creates no concern, whereas an affirmative act must first be performed. Rashi in chapter HaMegareish distinguishes a condition involving the recipient's father, but Ramban extends the rule to any person. Ramban explains that for an affirmative condition she must prove fulfillment, whereas for a negative condition the other party must prove violation. This explains the concern that she may already have had relations. Reference 100.
