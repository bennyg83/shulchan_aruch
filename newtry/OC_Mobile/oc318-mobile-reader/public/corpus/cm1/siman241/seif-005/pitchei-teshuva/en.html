And some disagree. See Baer Heitev in the name of Taz, who rules like this dissenting opinion. See Responsa Avodat HaGershuni, siman 114, who writes that this is also the view of the Mechaber. Although Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 93, states a general principle that the Mechaber ordinarily places the more accepted opinion first, that is where the first opinion is stated anonymously as the rule. Where even the first opinion is introduced as "some say," as here, and afterward the Mechaber writes "and some disagree," it is more likely that he does not accept the first view. See the introduction of Pri Megadim to Yoreh Deah, general rules, siman 4. Avodat HaGershuni further explains the first opinion, that of Rambam, in a manner limiting the disagreement. Rambam can agree that if one says, "I sell you a house in my house," the description sufficiently identifies the subject even if he does not specify which particular house. Likewise, "a palm tree among my palm trees" identifies the class sufficiently.
