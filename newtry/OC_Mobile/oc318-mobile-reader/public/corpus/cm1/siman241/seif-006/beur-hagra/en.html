One who gives, etc. Bava Batra 137b, "take this esrog, etc."; "this ox, etc."; Kiddushin 6b.
He may consume, etc. Bava Batra there, the case of that woman, etc., and likewise conversely.
Whether with land, etc., and he may consume, etc. For at the end of Eruvin 31a we say that a sale during the Jubilee year is comparable to interest because it is on condition that it be returned. And Toras Kohanim, Behar, chapter 5, interprets "and he shall redeem the sale of his brother" to include one who gave it as a gift.
And this is, etc. The Gemara in Bava Batra there.
If he did not set a time, etc. Presumably he may fulfill the condition whenever he wishes, as stated in Kiddushin 60a concerning "on condition that I give, etc., from now until, etc." We infer that in the first case he may do so whenever he wishes. See Rashi there, s.v. "amar Rav Huna."
And if it is, etc. As stated in Bava Batra there, "we examine, etc., something fit, etc." The same applies to an esrog, as stated there, specifically where there is an esrog, etc.
