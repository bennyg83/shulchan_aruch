S) We must return the fruits that we eat as a “chiat” and we will also learn from the esoteric system, and not the pig has not come out:
Meaning, he made the vow according to his fellow's understanding, and therefore he must give him a complete gift. But if he simply vowed or swore to give a gift, he may give even on condition that it be returned, as stated in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 238. Sema.
