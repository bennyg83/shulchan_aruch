Ketzot HaChoshen, seif katan 4, cites Rosh in Lulav HaGazul and Ritva in Kiddushin, from whom it is clear that when property was given as a gift on condition that it be returned, the recipient must perform a new act of conveyance when returning it. Ketzot writes that absent their words he would have thought no new kinyan necessary, because a gift on condition of return is full title for a limited period, with the donor retaining a remainder interest that automatically restores possession at the end. Netivot HaMishpat disagrees and concludes that the primary halachah follows Rosh, Ritva, and the other poskim: a new kinyan is required upon return. Chemdat Shlomo likewise rejects Ketzot. See also Pitchei Teshuva, Even HaEzer siman 28, citing Chatam Sofer, that where the donor later waives the condition, no new act of meshichah is needed merely to eliminate the condition.
