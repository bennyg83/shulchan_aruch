Seif 3. One who forgives, etc. Note: Someone asked me why this is not a forgiveness concerning something not yet in existence. There is no difficulty. Above, siman 209, it is explicit that if the recipient takes possession, acquisition of something not yet in existence can become effective. This is an explicit Gemara. Here too, since the creditor did not retract before the other party had already taken the property, that party is now in possession, and the forgiveness is effective. For this very reason the same mechanism does not work with land. Physical seizure does not create the same possession against the legal owner in land, so the purported forgiveness remains one concerning something not yet in existence. This is simple and correct.
