Mechaber, seif 3: one who waives to another whatever he will receive. Note: See Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilchot Mechirah, halacha 11; Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 3 of Hilchot Zechiyah U'Matanah, halacha 2, who notes this issue; see there in Mishneh LaMelech. See also above, siman 209, end of seif 4; Responsa Maharit, page 46, end of question 20; and Tosafos to Bava Metzia, chapter 9, page 36, s.v. 'I declared it ownerless.'
