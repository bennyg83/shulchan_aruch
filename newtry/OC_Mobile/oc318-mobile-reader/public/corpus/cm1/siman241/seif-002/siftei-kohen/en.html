Siman 204, seif 5, etc. This implies that just as above, siman 204, seif 5, the sale is not acquired by reason of the collateral, so too one who gives a gift and leaves an object as collateral for it does not effect acquisition. Although Baal HaItur writes in the laws of acquisition, second discourse, page 5, at the end of column 3, that acquisition is effected, Tosafot, the Ritva at the beginning of Kiddushin, and all the other poskim write that it is not. See also the Ran in the chapter Shevuat HaDayanim and the Nimukei Yosef at the beginning of Bava Metzia. This is the principal ruling.
