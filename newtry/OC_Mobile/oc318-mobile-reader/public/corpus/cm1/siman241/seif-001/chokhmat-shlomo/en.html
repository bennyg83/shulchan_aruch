Seif 1. One who gives a gift to another. Note: Regarding one who gives another person the actual object as a gift, can the donor retract within the brief interval of tokh kedei dibbur? Taz holds that he cannot retract, and all the later authorities express surprise at him. See my responsum in Even HaEzer to the community of Zolkiew, eighth edition, siman 85, where with the help of Hashem I explained Taz's position properly.
