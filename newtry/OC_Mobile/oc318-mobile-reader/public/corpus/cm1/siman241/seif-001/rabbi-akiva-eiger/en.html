[Shulchan Aruch] One who gives a gift. Note: If one gives a gift to the "offspring" of a particular person, and that person also has sons who are mamzerim, they are not included, because disqualified offspring are not included in the ordinary term "offspring." Likewise, one can say that a mamzer is not called "offspring" for this purpose. See Parashas Derachim, Derech HaKodesh, derush 7, and Poras Yosef in Sefer Teivas Gome, Parashas Vayechi, subparagraph 2.
