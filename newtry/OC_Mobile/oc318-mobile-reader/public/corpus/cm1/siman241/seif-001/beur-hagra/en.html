One who gives, etc. The Mishna in Bava Batra 156a states that a healthy person who distributes property must make a formal acquisition. The Sages disagree only regarding a dangerously ill person. Likewise on 151a the Gemara says: but he did not perform meshicha, etc.
(Supplement) One who gives, etc. Tosefta, chapter 10 of Bava Batra: a dangerously ill person who distributes his property orally, whether on a weekday or Shabbos, his words are carried out; a healthy person must transfer by money, document, or possession. (End of supplement.) Reference 6.
