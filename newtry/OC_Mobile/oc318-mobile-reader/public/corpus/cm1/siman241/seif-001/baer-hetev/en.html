It appears that just as there the sale is not acquired by virtue of the pledge, so too, where one gives a gift and leaves an item as security for it, the recipient does not acquire the gift. Although the Baal HaItur writes that he does acquire it, Tosafot, the Ritva, and the other authorities write that he does not, and this is the primary ruling. See the Ran in the chapter Shevuat HaDayanim and the Nimukei Yosef at the beginning of Bava Metzia. Shach.
