Alef) This is the language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 5 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah U'Matanah. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is straightforward in many places.
Bet) This is the language of the Tur, seif 2, and Rambam there, halachah 4, based on the incident of Mar Zutra and Rav Adda Sava before Rav Ashi. The Gemara concludes that witnesses were created only to prevent falsehood, Kiddushin 65b.
Gimel) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 1, from the statement of Rabbah bar bar Chanah in the name of Rabbi Yochanan in Bava Metzia 49a, as the Gemara concludes that the recipient's mind is not settled upon acquisition.
Dalet) Rav Pappa says there that Rabbi Yochanan concedes, etc.
Rambam explains that the recipient acquired only in order to fulfill Reuven's words, and Reuven's words themselves never took legal effect.
