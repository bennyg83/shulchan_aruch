Therefore, if one gives an ox to another on condition that he return it, and the recipient consecrates it and then returns it, the consecration is valid. If the donor said, "on condition that you return it to me," the consecration is invalid, because the condition requires return of something fit for the donor's use. Rama: If one says, "This field is given to you on condition that you give me two hundred zuz," and the donor dies, the recipient may give the money to his sons and the gift remains valid. Although "to me" ordinarily excludes heirs, here the donor intended only that the payment be made, and it was made. One who conditions a gift upon receiving a particular object may be given its monetary value, unlike conditions in gittin.
