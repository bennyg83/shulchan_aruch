Tzadi) The Beit Yosef writes in siman 207, citing the Rashba, concerning one who says, "My field is given to you on condition that you give me five gold coins each year." The recipient must give them during the donor's lifetime. If the donor dies, he need not give them to the heirs for future years, but any sums that became due to their father for past years must be paid to his children. The Nimukei Yosef in HaMokher Peirot writes similarly. The Sema cites this from the Darkei Moshe.
The reason is that in the laws of gittin, an unspecified condition is presumed to be intended to cause her personal inconvenience, and therefore the specified object must be given to him personally. See Sema.
