With gittin. It appears to me that one who says that a gift is given on condition that it be returned may return even its monetary value. The Rosh so rules in a responsum cited by the Tur in Even HaEzer, siman 28. See Responsa Maharam ibn Sasson, siman 63, who writes that the Rivash disagrees, and see the Rosh in the chapter Lulav HaGazul. Shach.
