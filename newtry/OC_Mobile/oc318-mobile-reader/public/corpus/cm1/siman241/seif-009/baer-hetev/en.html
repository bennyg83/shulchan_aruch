The giver. See above at the beginning of siman 207, where the Rosh distinguishes a case in which the seller stipulates a condition for the buyer's benefit. Such a statement is not treated as a binding condition but merely as words of encouragement unless it was stipulated at the beginning of the sale, for then one may say that the buyer purchased specifically on that understanding. The same must apply here. This is so whether the giver stipulated, "on condition that you give me such and such in return," or the recipient stipulated and said, "Give me this on condition that I give you, or do for you, such and such in return," or where the condition was made at the outset of the transaction, for then it may be said that the gift was given only on that understanding. Sema. See Responsa Maharam ibn Sasson, siman 118.
