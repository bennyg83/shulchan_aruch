He may give him the monetary value of the object. Panim Meirot, I, siman 92, initially understands this to mean that even if the specified object still exists, the obligated party may substitute its monetary value, and he notes similar language in Tur, Even HaEzer at the end of siman 28. He then expresses surprise because the Gemara, Gittin 75a, appears to require delivery of the specified object itself, as does Rosh in Lulav HaGazul. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 311, indeed states expressly that where the object itself still exists, one must give the very object that was stipulated. Ketzot HaChoshen and Netivot HaMishpat cite this ruling.
