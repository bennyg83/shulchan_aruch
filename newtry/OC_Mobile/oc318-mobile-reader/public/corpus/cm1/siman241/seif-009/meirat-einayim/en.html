Whether the giver stipulated the condition or the recipient stipulated it, etc. See above at the beginning of siman 207, where the Rosh disagrees in a case where the seller stipulated a condition that benefits the purchaser, holding that it is not considered a true condition but merely conciliatory words, unless he stipulated it at the outset of the sale, for then one can say that the purchaser bought only on that understanding. We must therefore say that here too the case is of that kind: either the giver stipulated, "on condition that you give me such and such in return," or the recipient stipulated and said to him, "Give me this on condition that I give you, or do for you, such and such in return"; alternatively, he stipulated at the outset of the transaction, so that one can say that it was specifically on that understanding that the giver gave him the gift.
