When does this apply, etc. See seif 2 concerning the four requirements of a condition. We rule like Rabbi Meir because Shmuel and Rava follow him in Gittin 75b. The requirements are a doubled condition, the positive clause before the negative, the condition before the act, and a condition capable of fulfillment. Another requirement is that the act be performable through an agent, as explained in the laws of chalitza, and another that the act and condition concern distinct matters, as Tur and the gloss in seif 4 write. Rambam omits the last point. The authorities dispute whether 'on condition that' requires all these formulations because it operates like 'from now.' Rif and Rambam rule that it does not require doubling, while others understand that the Sages dispute Rabbi Meir concerning all the formal requirements. A further dispute concerns monetary conditions: some Geonim and Rambam's cited authorities hold that doubled conditions are required only in marriage and divorce, while Rambam and Tosafot object that the condition of the tribes of Gad and Reuven was monetary. Ramban and Rashba answer that Rabbi Meir requires the forms everywhere, but we follow him strictly only in matters of prohibition; in monetary matters ordinary contract interpretation controls. Other passages and the Yerushalmi are analyzed at length by Tosafot, Rosh, Ran, and Maggid Mishneh. In any event, the condition preceding the act and practical possibility of fulfillment remain necessary according to the anonymous Mishna and the express ruling like Rabbi Yehuda ben Tema.
