One who waives for another whatever he will take. This is difficult, because language of waiver is ineffective for a physical object. The Sema answers that the case is where he also used language of gift. The Shach answers that here language of waiver is also appropriate, because if the other takes without permission the owner would have a claim against him, and he may properly waive that claim. This is unlike a case where one's object is in another person's possession and the other has no right to take it without permission, where there is no present claim to waive. He further explains that the giver does not know what the recipient will take, whether money or an object.
