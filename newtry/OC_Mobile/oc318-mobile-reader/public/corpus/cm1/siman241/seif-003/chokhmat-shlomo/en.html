Seif 6. In the gloss: If he fixed no time for return, the recipient may return it whenever he wishes, etc. Note: Concerning the apparent difficulty from siman 42 and a condition to pay "on Pesach," see my responsum to Rohatyn, third edition, siman 243, and the practical conclusions there. See also Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer, laws of gittin, siman 143, seif katan 6. See the Sema here, seif katan 16, who explains "after the festival." In my third edition, siman 330, I explained the Sema's intent and derived when a formal demand is required and when it is not. I also discussed there several distinctions relevant to this gloss, including when performance by the recipient's sons suffices.
