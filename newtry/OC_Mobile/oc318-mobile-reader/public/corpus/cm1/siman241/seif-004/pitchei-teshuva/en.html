Zichron Yosef, siman 4, cites a challenge from Shaar Ephraim: Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit disagrees with Tur there, and Shulchan Aruch omits Tur's rule, so perhaps Sema's comparison contradicts Beit Yosef. Zichron Yosef answers that the challenge is unfounded. Sema cites Tur only for the general proposition that a mistake is not presumed where the facts do not indicate one. Beit Yosef does not dispute that proposition; he disagrees only with Tur's conclusion that in that particular case the mistake was not evident. Beit Yosef thought it was evident there. Thus Beit Yosef can fully agree that where the circumstances do not indicate mistake, the waiver or compromise is not treated as mistaken. Teshuvot Rama, siman 74, states this expressly.
