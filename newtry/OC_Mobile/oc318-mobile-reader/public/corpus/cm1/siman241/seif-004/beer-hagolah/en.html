The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's teacher, Rabbi Yosef HaLevi, derived this from the sugya in Gittin 9a and from the Rif's statement there that where the property was not specified, acquisition does not take effect, etc. Later authorities dispute this based on the law of 'a document of yours in my possession is paid,' explained in siman 65, the law of 'my field is sold to you' in siman 218, and the law of 'a house from among my houses' and 'a slave from among my slaves' in siman 214. The Maggid Mishneh leaves the matter requiring study. The Kesef Mishneh writes at length to reconcile Rambam so that those cases do not contradict him. See there.
