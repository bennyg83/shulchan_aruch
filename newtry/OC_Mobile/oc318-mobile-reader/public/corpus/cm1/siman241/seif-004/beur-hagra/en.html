Some say, etc. This follows Rashi's explanation in Gittin 9a, s.v. "ad," and so rule the Rif and Rash. They do not dispute that as to the property he did not acquire it. Although that point does not remain in the conclusion, the ruling itself is nevertheless correct. See the Maggid Mishneh.
But if, etc. Bava Batra 62b, "half of the land, etc.," and there 107b, the Mishnah: "one who says, etc." This is the meaning of "from the inferior portion, etc."
Others disagree, etc. This follows from the Mishnah and Gemara cited above, for they do not accept the distinction he makes. Similar expressions appear at the end of Kesubos, in the case of one who said, "a palm tree to my daughter, etc."; at the end of Menachos 108b, "a house in my house, etc."; and at the end of Bava Batra, "if so, [when one says] my field is sold, etc." See Tosafos to Gittin there, s.v. "le'olam," and the Maggid Mishneh, chapter 3 of Hilchos Zechiyah U'Mattanah.
