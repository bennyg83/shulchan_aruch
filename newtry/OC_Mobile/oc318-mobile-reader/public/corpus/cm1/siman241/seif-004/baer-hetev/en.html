This. See the Tur, siman 232, seif 6. It appears that the halachah follows the dissenting opinion. According to the Rambam, one who transfers four cubits of land to another together with movable property by means of acquisition incidental to land must explicitly identify those four cubits, as the Maggid Mishneh explains. Yet the established practice is to transfer movable property incidentally to four cubits of land without identifying any particular parcel. It necessarily follows that we rule according to the dissenting opinion that no identification is required. Taz.
