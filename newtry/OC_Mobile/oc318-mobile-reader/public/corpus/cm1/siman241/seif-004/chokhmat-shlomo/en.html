Seif 7. 'Therefore, one who gives,' etc. Note: See the Sema in the gloss in the name of Rashba. See Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer siman 114, in the name of the Taz, regarding one who gave his solemn handshake to make a gift and died before the time. See also Yoreh Deah siman 228, seif 20, in the gloss, where it is clear that if he died he must give it to his children. It is also clear there that even if he promised not to engage in business without the other's permission, that right passes to the children. Yet see Choshen Mishpat siman 276, which states that a person cannot bequeath to his children something intangible or a mere benefit of discretion, implying that such a right does not pass to them. See also Choshen Mishpat siman 149, seif 31, in the gloss, concerning Reuven and Shimon who inherited from their father a right that no one could build in the synagogue without their permission, which implies that the heirs do inherit such a right. This requires study. Perhaps there the grant expressly included the heirs as well. The entire matter still requires study, for the sources appear contradictory.
