Even if he had a deed, etc. Baal HaItur likewise writes this on page 93b. Examine carefully, so that you do not infer otherwise from the law of one who forgives a debt above, siman 66. He cites a Yerushalmi in which the authorities disagree and rules leniently. According to this, the matter remains a monetary doubt. Examine carefully. This Yerushalmi appears at the end of the first chapter of Gittin, halacha 4: If one forgave a debt evidenced by a deed, Rabbi Chananya and Rabbi Mana disagree. One says that it is forgiven, and the other says that it is not forgiven until he transfers the deed to him. See Responsa Maharashdam, simanim 235 and 278, and Responsa Rabbi Avraham ibn Chaim, siman 77.
