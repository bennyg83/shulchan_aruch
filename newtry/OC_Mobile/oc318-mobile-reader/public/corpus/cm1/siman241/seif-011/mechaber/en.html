Reuven gave his property to his two sons, giving more to one and less to the other, on condition that neither sell anything until the younger reached twenty. The elder sold before that time. By violating the condition, his gift was void from the outset and his share remained with the father. Upon the father's death, both sons inherited it. The younger son's original gift remains valid, and he also inherits half of the portion that had been given to his brother.
