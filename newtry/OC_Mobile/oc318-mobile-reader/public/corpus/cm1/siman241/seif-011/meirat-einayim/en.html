The gift is void retroactively and remained in his father's possession. The Tur writes that anyone who says "on condition" is considered as saying "from now," and if the condition is fulfilled the gift takes effect from the time it was given, while if the condition is not fulfilled the gift is void retroactively. The Mechaber's wording here is likewise the wording of the Tur, only abbreviated. In the Perishah I explained that he needed to write that it is void retroactively and remained in the father's possession because the case concerns a gift of a shechiv mera. With a shechiv mera we rule that even if he gives in the language of a gift, it is as though he gave by way of inheritance, which cannot be interrupted, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the beginning of siman 248. Thus here too one might have said that once the gift left the father's possession for this son, who later failed the condition, it should not leave him. He therefore teaches that this case is different, because the gift was void from the outset, as though it had never left the father's possession. The Rosh likewise writes this in his responsum there.
