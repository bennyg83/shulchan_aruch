From the outset. The case concerns a gift of a person dangerously ill. We rule that even if such a person gives property using the language of gift, it is treated as though given by inheritance and therefore cannot be interrupted, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the beginning of siman 248. One might therefore think that the gift should not leave the possession of this son merely because he violated the condition. The ruling therefore explains that here the gift was void from the outset, as though it had never left the father's possession. Responsa Rosh likewise writes this. Sema.
