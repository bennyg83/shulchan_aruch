Rashi explains that after one began to fall, if he then intentionally directed his fall onto another person in order to harm him for his own benefit, he is liable for shame even though he did not specifically intend to shame him. The Maggid Mishneh writes that there are three levels: where there is complete negligence, such as an ordinary wind, but no intent, he is liable for the four payments except shame. Where there is no complete negligence but also not a complete accident, such as an unusual wind, he is liable for damage but exempt from the other payments. In a complete accident the commentators write that he is exempt entirely. Rabbenu Yerucham writes that any accident comparable to theft is exempt. Tosafot likewise write so.
