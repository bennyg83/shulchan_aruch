He gives the adult the embarrassment value of a minor. Siman 420:37 explains that a minor receives embarrassment compensation only if he is old enough to feel embarrassment when shamed. The Mechaber there also writes that embarrassing a minor is not equivalent to embarrassing an adult. Here the tortfeasor pays only the amount appropriate to a minor because that is the degree of embarrassment he intended. He did not intend the greater embarrassment due to the adult, and there is no liability for unintentional embarrassment.
