The wording of Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilkhot Chovel U'Mazik, law 17, from the Mishnah in Bava Kamma chapter 3, 32b.
P. is there
The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam follows the latter version of Rabbi Yose bar Chanina regarding the baraita there. Some explain 'without permission' as where the entrant is known to the owner, like the preceding case; Tur cites this in the name of Rashi. Others explain that even if the owner did not know he had entered, a craftsman's shop is different because people commonly enter craftsmen's premises, so entry without explicit permission is like entry with permission. Tur cites this in the name of Rivah.
