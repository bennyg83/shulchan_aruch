Specifically, etc. As stated there 27b regarding one hundred zuz, etc., and certainly in such a case. See also 28a concerning "and she shall cut off her hand," which applies only where she could have saved him otherwise.
Immediately. This is a scribal error. This explains the continuation, "and the same applies regarding insults," etc. See Sema.
And Shimon then retaliated, etc. Meaning immediately, as above, for then he is exempt.
Nevertheless, he must, etc. He is no less than a pursuer, as in Sanhedrin 74a, unlike cases involving oxen.
And so too is the law, etc., and likewise one who, etc. As stated there 28a in the name of Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak.
(Collected note) And the same law applies to a person, etc., and likewise, etc. See Rashi in Arachin 16b, s.v. "could one," etc. (End of note.)
