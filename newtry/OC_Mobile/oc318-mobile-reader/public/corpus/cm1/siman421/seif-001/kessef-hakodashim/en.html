See Sema, seif katan 24. From the language of the Sema in siman 421 it appears that when one person begins a quarrel, the consideration that his heart becomes inflamed applies even with respect to striking, although the first person did not strike him. Nevertheless, it appears that this applies only where the quarrel was begun through curses and insults that are improper according to Torah law and that involve the prohibition of verbal mistreatment. This is not so with words spoken for the sake of Heaven, or with words that do not take the form of prohibited verbal mistreatment. The Shulchan Aruch itself writes only of striking that follows an earlier striking. Although the Sema's wording there is stated according to the initial assumption of the discussion, it nevertheless appears that the same is true according to the conclusion there.
