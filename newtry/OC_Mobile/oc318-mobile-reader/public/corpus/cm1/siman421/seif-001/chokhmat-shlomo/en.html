Seif 3. 'A person is always considered forewarned, whether unintentionally or intentionally, awake or asleep,' etc. See Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 21 of Hilchot Ishut, Hilchot Chovel U'Mazik, and Hilchot Sheluchin, where he wishes to distinguish within the Raavad's view in chapter 21 of Hilchot Ishut that, regarding guarding while the owner is present, the rule that a person is always considered forewarned applies only through the law of negligence, and where he did not intend to cause damage he is exempt when the owner is present. See my Even HaEzer, fifth edition, siman 17, in a responsum to the community of Ostroh, where I distinguished between a bailee and an ordinary person. Specifically a bailee is exempt for damage when the owner is present, but not another person. See there for the reason, which is sound, with God's help.
