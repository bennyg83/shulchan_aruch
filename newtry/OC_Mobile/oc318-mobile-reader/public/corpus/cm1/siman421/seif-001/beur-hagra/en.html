(Supplement) Likewise, one who, etc. The statement on 27a, 'if he turned over, etc.,' means specifically that he intended to injure him, as the Gemara says, 'since he intended to injure.' This rejects Rashi's explanation that he turned for his own benefit. Yam Shel Shlomo. This is not comparable to the dispute in siman 388, seif 5, because regarding the four additional heads of personal-injury payment one is certainly liable even without intent, as stated there, and all the more so for bodily or property damage.
