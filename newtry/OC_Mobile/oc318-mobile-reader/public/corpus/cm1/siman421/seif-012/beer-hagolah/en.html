There, chapter 8, law 11, from the Mishnah in Bava Kamma chapter 8, 92a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam understands Rabbi Yochanan's statement on 93a, that sometimes 'yes' means no, as an independent law and not as explaining the Mishnah's law of major limbs, unlike Rashi. According to Rambam, with major limbs, even if the victim calmly said 'yes,' the assailant is liable. Rabbi Yochanan's rule applies specifically where one says 'strike me' or 'wound me.' This is why Rambam distinguishes major limbs from ordinary injury. He did not cite the questioning formulation because it is obvious.
