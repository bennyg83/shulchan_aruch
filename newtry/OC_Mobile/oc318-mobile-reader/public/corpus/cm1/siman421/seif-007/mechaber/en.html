If a person enters another's courtyard without permission and the homeowner does not know he entered, and the homeowner unintentionally injures him, the homeowner is exempt. If the homeowner is injured by the entrant, the entrant is liable because he entered without permission. This applies where the homeowner neither knew nor saw that he entered. If the homeowner saw him and nevertheless injured himself through the entrant, the entrant is exempt because the homeowner caused his own injury.
