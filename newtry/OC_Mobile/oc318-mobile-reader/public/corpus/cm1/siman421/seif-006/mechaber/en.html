Whenever one intentionally injures another, he is liable for all five categories of payment. This is true even if the injured person entered the injurer's property without permission and the owner injured him while removing him, because although the owner has the right to remove him, he has no right to injure him. If the intruder refuses to leave, however, some say that the owner may even injure him as necessary to remove him. Rama: Likewise, one who has a servant and fears that the servant will steal from him may dismiss him before the end of the hiring term. If the servant refuses to leave, he may strike him until he leaves. Mordechai, chapter HaManiach.
