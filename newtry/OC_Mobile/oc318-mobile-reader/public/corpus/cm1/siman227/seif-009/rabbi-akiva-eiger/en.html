[Shulchan Aruch] The purchaser may retract. Note: But if he sold an item worth five for six, or in another case where there is no cancellation of the sale, he cannot retract. See Tosafot in our sugya, who write in one answer that if the article subsequently increased further in value, so that the seller would now have a claim exceeding the measure of onaah, the purchaser may retract. See there. This requires study.
