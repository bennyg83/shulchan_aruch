The language of Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 2. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Mechaber records four cases. Two are agreed upon in the Gemara, Bava Metzia 49b: an item worth six sold for five, and an item worth six sold for seven. But five for six or seven for six is disputed by Rav and Shmuel there, 50b. Rambam rules like Shmuel because a baraita supports him.
