(Seif 6: there is room for doubt.) The locus of the doubt, as explained by the Sema in seif katan 14, requires study. The Gemara in Perek HaMokher Et HaPeirot, page 95, states explicitly concerning onaah that initially the parties intended exact equality, yet a discrepancy of less than one sixth is not ordinarily known and a person waives it. The Rosh concludes there concerning one who fears Heaven, etc. It appears to me that if the buyer is not certain whether the discrepancy was less than one sixth and wishes to return it, the seller should not accept it, because perhaps the amount was legally waived. If the returning party too is God-fearing, he should give the money as a gift.
