The language of Rambam there, law 9. The Maggid Mishneh writes that in Kiddushin 42b an agent can say, 'I sent you to improve matters, not to harm me,' and in Ketubot 100a they say that an agent is like a widow, whose sale is void for even the smallest discrepancy. The Rif likewise rules so.
According to the explanation of most commentators, the Ketubot passage concerns an agent of beit din. An administrator appointed by beit din is their agent. Alternatively, it follows by reasoning that an administrator's authority cannot be stronger than that of an ordinary agent. This is the Ramban's view in Kiddushin.
