There. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam rules like Rav Kahana, there 55a. He understands Rav Kahana's statement to mean that ona'ah does not apply to a discrepancy of exactly one perutah, but only when it exceeds one perutah. The anonymous Mishnah supports Rav Kahana because it does not list 'ona'ah of one perutah,' although the baraita expounding 'when you sell' follows Levi, who disagrees. Since the halachah follows an anonymous Mishnah, Rambam rules this way. Other explanations exist, but this is primary.
