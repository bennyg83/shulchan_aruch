Provided that it circulates with difficulty. The Tur writes the same. In the Perishah and Derishah I demonstrated that the meaning is that if the coin cannot circulate even with difficulty, then even as a matter of piety the recipient need not exchange it after the normal time for claiming has passed. This should not be surprising. We find a stronger rule that after the time for showing the item to a merchant expires, even one who sold an item worth one hundred for two hundred need not return the overcharge. This differs from the Ir Shushan, who writes that if the coin does not circulate even with difficulty the seller must certainly exchange it even after a very long time. Although Rambam's language appears to support that view, the Maggid Mishneh already questioned him and writes that the Gemara does not imply so. For this reason the Mechaber, contrary to his usual practice, did not copy the Rambam but copied the Tur's wording, which supports the explanation I have given.
