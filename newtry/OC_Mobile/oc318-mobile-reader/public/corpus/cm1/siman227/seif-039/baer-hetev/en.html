The Tur concludes in the name of the Rosh: Even if Shimon says, "I will remove from you the complaints of the residents of the alley," Reuven may respond, "I do not wish to acquire an adversary for myself by opening this entrance." Moreover, since at the time of the division they erred, believing that he could open the entrance when in fact he cannot, the original division was made in error and is void. The Tur and Mechaber similarly rule at the beginning of siman 175, seif 3, regarding two brothers who divided an estate and then a third brother appeared, that their division is void for this reason.
