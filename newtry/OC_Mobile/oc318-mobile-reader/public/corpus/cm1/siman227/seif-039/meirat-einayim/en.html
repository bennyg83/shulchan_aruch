The Tur concludes in the Rosh's name that even if Shimon says, 'I will remove from you the grievance of the alley residents,' Reuven may answer, 'I do not wish to create an enemy for myself by opening this entrance.' Moreover, because at the time of division they erred in assuming that the entrance could legally be opened, whereas it cannot, the original division was made in error and is void. The Tur and Mechaber likewise write at the beginning of siman 175 concerning two brothers who divided and later a third brother appeared, that their division is void because it had been based on a mistake. The reference here to judges who erred is not exclusive. The same applies if other people made the mistaken division. The incident in the responsum of the Rosh happened to involve judges. See there.
