Regarding seif 7. The period of 'enough time to show it to a merchant' should be measured from the completion of the acquisition. Before the kinyan, the buyer may still have been inclined not to purchase and therefore had no reason to seek an appraisal. Even if the merchandise had been in his house for a long time before the acquisition and he had not indicated any intention to refuse it, it may still be reasonable that he did not obtain an appraisal then, because people often decide only after the transaction is actually completed. Even if he knew that the seller would immediately travel away with the money, the Sages did not appear to distinguish such cases. The fixed period therefore begins with the kinyan.
