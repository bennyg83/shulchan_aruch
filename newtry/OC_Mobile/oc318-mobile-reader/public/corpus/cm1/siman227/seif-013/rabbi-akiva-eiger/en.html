[Shulchan Aruch] Up to half is permitted. Note: If the deficiency is less than half, it is forbidden to keep it, as stated in the Gemara. Rashi holds that even the slightest deficiency makes it forbidden to keep. Most authorities, however, including Nimukei Yosef and the Rambam, disagree and require a deficiency of less than half together with the measure of its onaah. This is also cited in Shitah Mekubetzet in the name of the Rashba. In my opinion, with respect to less than one sixth in ordinary transactions, it is permitted even initially to charge the higher amount. See above, siman 66. Here, however, one certainly requires both that it be deficient and that the deficiency reach the measure of onaah. See Maharsha and analyze carefully.
