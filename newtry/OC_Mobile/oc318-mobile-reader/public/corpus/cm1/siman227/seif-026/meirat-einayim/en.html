A non-Jew has no law of ona'ah, etc. The same principle applies to an ox belonging to a Jew, even one established as dangerous, that gores an ox belonging to a non-Jew: the Jewish owner is exempt, because the verse says, "If a man's ox gores the ox of his fellow," as the Tur and Mechaber write below in siman 406. There too Jewish law is stronger on both sides, since if a non-Jew's ox gores a Jew's ox, even if the Jewish ox was ordinarily harmless, the non-Jew pays full damages. See what I wrote in the Derishah on this matter.
