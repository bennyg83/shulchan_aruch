To sow a field for him. See Sema and Responsa Amudei Or, siman 107, concerning one who hired another as a contractor to build a house. Before the construction was completed, the contractor realized that he had been underpaid and sought to retract. Amudei Or discusses the matter at length and concludes that this is exactly the unresolved question of Rava in Bava Metzia 56b. It therefore remains a doubt, and property cannot be extracted from the current possessor. However, it appears that the shaping and preparation of the wood should be appraised separately, because ona'ah certainly applies to that labor, while the work of attaching and constructing it into the land should be appraised separately, similar to the rule of hiring a worker together with a horse above, seif 33 in the gloss.
