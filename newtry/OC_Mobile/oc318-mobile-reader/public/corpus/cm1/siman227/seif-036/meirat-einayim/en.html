Each of the two, whether the contractor or the owner of the garment, may retract forever. It appears that this means whichever one of them was wronged may retract forever, even the owner of the garment who presently holds the garment. We do not say he may retract only for the time needed to show it to others, as with an overreached purchaser, because rental and contracting are different. The party who caused the overreaching, however, certainly cannot retract once the wronged party has agreed not to do so, just as in ordinary sale. Maimonides and Tur explicitly conclude here that the right to retract forever is like that of a seller in the relevant case.
