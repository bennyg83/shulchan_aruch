He violates a prohibition. Scripture says, 'When you sell a sale to your fellow, or buy from the hand of your fellow, one shall not wrong his brother.' This prohibition is included within the category of robbery. Just as there are no lashes for robbery because it is remediable by restitution, as Scripture says, 'He shall return the stolen object,' so there are no lashes for this prohibition because overreaching too is remediable by returning the amount of the overcharge. So writes the Tur.
