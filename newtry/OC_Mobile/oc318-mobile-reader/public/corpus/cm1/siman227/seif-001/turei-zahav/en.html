(Seif 3: an item worth seventy sold for sixty.) Regarding the difficulty the Maggid Mishneh raised against the Rambam and the Tur, as cited by the Sema in seif katan 5, it appears to me that whenever the discrepancy can be termed one sixth of the money paid, we follow that measure. When it cannot be called one sixth at all, we follow the value of the merchandise. The principle is that whenever the discrepancy bears the name “one sixth” from any perspective, we follow it. We do not derive by a fortiori inference from a smaller amount to a larger amount, for the Sages established a uniform measure and made the matter depend upon one sixth. Therefore, where an item worth sixty is sold for fifty and one perutah, since the overcharge cannot at all be called one sixth, we consider only the value of the merchandise, and the discrepancy is waived. This is also the implication of the Mordechai cited by the Rama in seif 4. The Sema's surprise in seif katan 11 that the Rama wrote nothing here concerning the Mechaber is itself surprising. What could the Rama have written, when he saw that the Mechaber stated a rule accepted by all opinions, including the Maggid Mishneh, and the Mechaber did not state any exclusion regarding three and one perutah? The Rama therefore added it here.
