More. This is difficult. The Rosh and Tur write in the name of Rabbeinu Tam that only where the deviation is less than half is there no ona'ah, but at exactly half the law of ona'ah does apply, and they prove this from the Yerushalmi. Although Tosafot in Bava Kamma and Bava Metzia cite Rabbeinu Tam in wording similar to the Rama, one must say that their wording was not exact; their purpose was only to exclude the view that these transactions have no law of ona'ah at all, as maintained by the Rif and Rambam. Accordingly, the Rama should not have written as he did, but should have followed the Rosh and Tur's explanation of Rabbeinu Tam. This is the Sema's ruling. The Shach writes that in his view Tosafot spoke precisely, and this is explicit from Tosafot in Bava Kamma and Kiddushin 42b; see there. He concludes: Nevertheless, as a matter of law I established at the end of siman 66 that the primary ruling follows the Rif and those who agree with him. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 58, page 53d, who writes that it is an unresolved question of law. See also Responsa Ibn Lev, kelal 20, siman 108; Responsa Maharit, end of siman 93; and Responsa Ra'anach, siman 69.
