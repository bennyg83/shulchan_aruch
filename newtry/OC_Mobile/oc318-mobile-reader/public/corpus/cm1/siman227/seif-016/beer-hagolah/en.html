The Mishnah there at the beginning of 52a, according to Rabbi Shimon and according to the reading that has Rava say: who is the tanna concerning a garment? Rabbi Shimon, as appears in the Rif.

It's an old man, and he's already in the end
Meaning, for example, he gave the sela for twenty-one or twenty-seven. But if he gave twenty and one perutah, this is not waiver, because he was still overcharged by more than the ratio of seven to six. So it appears from the Maggid Mishneh.
The Maggid Mishneh writes likewise there. It is explained below at the beginning of siman 232 that one returns the ona'ah.
According to the reading that has Rabbah, they rule like Rabbi Yehudah concerning a sela. A sela differs from other items, as Abaye explains there. Maggid Mishneh.
