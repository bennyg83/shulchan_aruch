With coins. Although the verse says, "When you sell a sale," and the use of a coin is not ordinarily characterized as a sale in the same sense, the ruling teaches that the laws of ona'ah apply to coins as well. So writes the Sema. See Responsa Rashach, part 2, siman 167.
One twelfth. The reason is that money is given to be spent, and people will not accept a deficient coin from him. Therefore the Sages were exacting and said that waiver does not occur until the deficiency is less than one twelfth. Sema.
