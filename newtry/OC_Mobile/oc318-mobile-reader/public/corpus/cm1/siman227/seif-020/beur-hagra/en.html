Like one who exchanges, etc. Bava Metzia 46a records Rabbi Abba's reasoning that the case resembles barter. Rabbah disagrees only because the language 'sell to me with these' indicates a sale.
Even, etc.; and likewise, etc. The Yerushalmi distinguishes an exchange of one animal for another from an exchange of one heap for another. Rambam applies the passage to overreaching, because for acquisition he holds that produce may be acquired by barter, as stated above in siman 203. The Yerushalmi's language, however, appears to concern acquisition, and Raavad objects accordingly. The proper explanation is that the dispute concerns a heap of coins: Rav permits even one heap for another, while Rabbi Yochanan limits barter to an unusual coin serving as an object rather than currency. Kiddushin likewise says that an object requiring appraisal does not operate as money.
