There is no law of overreaching. Utensils can serve for barter, as indicated by the verse concerning a shoe. Barter is not subject to overreaching, because Scripture says 'when you sell a sale or buy.' Produce, however, does not function as the symbolic object of exchange in the same way, and where its value is appraised the transaction takes on more of the character of a sale.
