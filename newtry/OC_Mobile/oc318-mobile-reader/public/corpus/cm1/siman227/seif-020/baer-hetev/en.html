With utensils. For many years I was uncertain whether this rule was stated specifically regarding utensils intended for use, or even when the parties wanted them for merchandise. I concluded that it applies even when they were intended for merchandise, based on the reason cited by the Beit Yosef in the name of the students of the Rashba: the law of ona'ah does not apply to barter, only to a sale, because the verse says "sale." I later found that Maharshal Rashdam, siman 383, discusses this question and reaches the same conclusion. See also Maharshal Rashach, part 2, siman 218; Responsa Bnei Shmuel, siman 22; and Radbaz, siman 269. There, Bnei Chayya notes that the Radbaz disputes the Rashdam just cited.
