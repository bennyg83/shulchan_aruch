The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Mechaber follows some early authorities who hold that where the discrepancy exceeds one sixth, the party who caused the ona'ah cannot retract unless the injured party demands his ona'ah. The Kesef Mishneh writes that this can likewise be learned from the Rif, as the Rosh and Tur write in his name, and that this was also the view of the Rivam.
Relative to the value of the item the discrepancy is less than one sixth, while relative to the money received by the seller it exceeds one sixth. The Maggid Mishneh disagrees with this approach, as he explains at length there in law 3. The Beit Yosef likewise disagrees. The Sema discusses the matter at length, while the Bach supports the Mordechai from Tosafot at the end of 51a, s.v. 'two pundeyon,' ruling that we measure relative to the value of the merchandise.
