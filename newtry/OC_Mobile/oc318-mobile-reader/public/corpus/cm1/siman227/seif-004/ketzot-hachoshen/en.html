For people make errors in a purchase. This is from Mordechai in chapter HaZahav. Shitah Mekubetzet there records a question: where the overcharge is more than one sixth relative to the object but less than one sixth relative to the money, such as an item worth five dinars sold for five dinars and eleven peshitim, how do we calculate it? Rabbeinu Yehudah says it is treated as ordinary ona'ah. Since it is neither a case of waiver nor one of voiding the transaction, it must be ona'ah. Mordechai disagrees. Ritva cites Rabbeinu Yehudah and rejects the approach: how can we create ona'ah when there is no one-sixth disparity according to either side? Why should we impose an arbitrary compromise between the two methods of calculation? Ritva concludes that the sounder view is to calculate according to the value of the merchandise both according to Rav and Shmuel. The principal category is ona'ah of the merchandise, and their dispute concerns ona'ah of the money. He nevertheless leaves the matter requiring study.
