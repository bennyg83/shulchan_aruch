If the overcharge exceeds one-sixth by any amount, such as selling an item worth sixty for fifty less one perutah, the sale is voidable. The injured party may return the object and cancel the transaction entirely. The party who caused the overcharge cannot withdraw if the injured party wishes to accept the transaction. Rama: Some say even the party who caused the overcharge may withdraw unless the injured party once expressly accepted the sale or remained silent beyond the period explained in seif 7. If an item worth sixty was sold for fifty-one, or the reverse, so that from one perspective there is no precise one-sixth overcharge, while from the other there is either cancellation or waiver, the law follows the value of the merchandise, both regarding cancellation and waiver, because people err in valuing merchandise rather than in counting money.
