There, law 15. The Maggid Mishneh writes that he already explained Rav Chisda's statement in a comparable case of ona'ah by one sixth. Rav Chisda said that where an item worth five was sold for six and then increased in value, the buyer may retract but not the seller. The Ramban explains that the buyer may demand his ona'ah while the seller cannot retract. We, however, rule that neither party may retract and the sale stands, and all the more so each may demand the return of ona'ah. This is straightforward in light of what was explained.
