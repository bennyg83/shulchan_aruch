This is the Mishnah there, following the first Tanna.
Alef) Rambam there, halachah 11. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam derived this from the Gemara, Bava Metzia 52b: regarding a garment, every person knows its value, whereas regarding a sela only a moneychanger is expert. Accordingly, the same rule applies to anything comparable to a sela whose value is not generally known. This is unlike a sela in villages, because such a coin is ordinarily exchanged on Friday afternoons.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this reasoning is likewise correct.
