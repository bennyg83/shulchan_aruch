Even books, etc. In the Perishah I explained that the authorities singled out these items to exclude Rabbi Yehudah, who says there is no overreaching for a Torah scroll, animals, or pearls. A Torah scroll has no fixed price, while animals and pearls are often purchased above ordinary market value for purposes of matching. A poor ox can impair a good ox when they are yoked together for plowing, and one who owns a fine pearl may buy another at an inflated price in order to match it with the first in a gold setting. See Bava Metzia 58b.
