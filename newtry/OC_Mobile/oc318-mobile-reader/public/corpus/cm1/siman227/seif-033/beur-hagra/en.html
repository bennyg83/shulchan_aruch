One who hires, etc., because, etc. As stated there: “Is ‘sale’ written, etc.? This person, etc.”
And slaves, etc. Although the slaves excluded there because they are compared to land are Canaanite slaves, a Hebrew slave also resembles land in this respect, as stated in Kiddushin chapter 1, 28a.
He hired a laborer, etc. As stated in Gittin chapter 1 regarding “yourself and my property,” according to Rava.
And we assess, etc. One cannot say that any amount should be assigned to the laborer’s personal service because there is no ona'ah there, while all the remainder is assigned to the horse. Rather, the market rate for hiring each is assessed, as in the chapter HaOmanin: “How are laborers hired, etc.?” The total is then apportioned according to the relative values.
