And servants are not subject to overreaching. Although the verse, 'You shall hold them as an inheritance,' cited above, concerns Canaanite slaves, nevertheless one who hires himself as a laborer bears the designation 'servant.' These are the words of the Sema. Responsa Rabbi Yosef Beit Levi, page 26, writes likewise and cites Terumat HaDeshen, siman 318, who also rules so. See there.
