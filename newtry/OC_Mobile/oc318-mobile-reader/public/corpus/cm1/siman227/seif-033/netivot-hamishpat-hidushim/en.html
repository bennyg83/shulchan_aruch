One who hires his fellow. This applies specifically to a day laborer, but a contractor does not have the legal status of a servant, as stated below in seif 36.
And servants are not subject to ona'ah. Although the verse “and you shall hold them as an inheritance” is written regarding Canaanite slaves, nevertheless one who hires himself as a worker bears the legal designation of a servant.
And the amount by which he was subjected to ona'ah. This implies even more than one sixth, specifically where the work has already been performed (Sema).
