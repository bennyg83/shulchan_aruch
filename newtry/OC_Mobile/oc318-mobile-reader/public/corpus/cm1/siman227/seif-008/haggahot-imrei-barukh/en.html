Sema, seif katan 57: rental for a day is considered a sale. Note: Although by Torah law a day's rental is not literally a sale, nevertheless the law of ona'ah for rental of movable property requires a specific scriptural inclusion. It follows automatically that there is no ona'ah in the rental of land. See Tosafos, Bava Metzia 56b, s.v. 'and these vessels,' and Maggid Mishneh, Hilchot Mechirah, chapter 13, halacha 14.
