On condition that you have no claim of ona'ah against me. Although with a stipulation concerning a monetary provision of the Torah the condition is ordinarily effective, here the other party did not know the amount that he was waiving. See Beurim: if he said, “on condition that he waive the ona'ah for me,” the condition is effective, and if he afterward refuses to waive it, the sale is void even where the disparity is only one sixth.
He has a claim of ona'ah against him. If the disparity is one sixth, the transaction stands and the amount of the ona'ah is returned. See Beurim.
That there shall be no ona'ah in it. This implies a condition that the law of ona'ah shall not apply, comparable to a condition that the Sabbatical year shall not cancel the debt, which is ineffective. Consequently, less than one sixth is deemed waived, while at exactly one sixth the transaction stands and the ona'ah is returned (Sema).
