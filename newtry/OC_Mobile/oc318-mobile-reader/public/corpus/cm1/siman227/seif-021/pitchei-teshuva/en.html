Does the prior waiver leave only an amount less than one sixth, which would itself be waived, or does the transaction remain void? Radbaz answers that it is clearly a cancellation of the sale. Less than one sixth is waived because people ordinarily forgive only that small amount; when the discrepancy reaches one sixth, the entire onaah is returned, including the portion that would otherwise have been waived. Likewise here, the parties waived one sixth but not more than one sixth. Since there was no waiver of the larger discrepancy, the transaction is void rather than merely adjusted by returning the excess.
