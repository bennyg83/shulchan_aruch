Kaf) There in Rava's statement.
Lamed) The Tur, seif 9, infers this from the Rosh's wording. The Sema likewise infers from the Tur in the name of the Rosh that even if the coercive circumstance later ceased and the buyer did not immediately show the item to a merchant, he may still retract and explain that at first he was prevented by circumstances and afterward did not think to investigate until now.
Mem) This follows from the incidents of the one who took silk garments to sell and the one who took jewelry to sell, Bava Metzia 51a. The Bach proves that here too the buyer stated before witnesses, before the purchase, that he did not waive his rights. If he did not say so before witnesses, he is presumed to have waived them.
