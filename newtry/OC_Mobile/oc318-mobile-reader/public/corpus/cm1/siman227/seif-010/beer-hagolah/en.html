Tzadi) This is the language of the Rambam, halachah 13. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is learned from Rav Chisda's statement at the beginning of Bava Batra 84a. Although Rav Chisda spoke of ona'ah of one sixth according to the Mishnah's view that the injured party may rescind, whereas we rule that with exactly one sixth the sale remains valid and the ona'ah is returned, the principle of Rav Chisda's statement still teaches the law of cancellation of a sale, as explained at length in the Rif. I already explained above that according to Rambam, in a case of cancellation the party who committed the ona'ah cannot retract so long as the injured party does not demand his remedy. The same applies here. We learn from this that even where a later change in market price turns the disadvantage toward the original wrongdoer, he cannot thereby demand rescission.
