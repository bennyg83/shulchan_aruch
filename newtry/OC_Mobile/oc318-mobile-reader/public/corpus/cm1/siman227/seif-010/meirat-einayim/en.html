But not the seller. Even according to the opinion cited by Rama above in seif 4 that the party who caused the overreaching may also retract until the wronged party accepts the sale, this case is different. Once the price rises, we presume that the wronged purchaser will no longer retract. The purchaser himself nevertheless retains the right to retract until the period for showing the item to a merchant expires. Even after the price rises, we presume that so long as he still has time to retract, he has not fully resolved to finalize the transaction. See the Perishah.
