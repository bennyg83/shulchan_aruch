The seller. This is true even according to the opinion cited by the Rama in seif 4 that the party who committed the ona'ah may also retract until the injured party accepts the sale. Here it is different, because once the item rises in value we presume that the injured party will no longer retract. Nevertheless, the injured party himself may retract until the period in which he could show the item to a merchant has elapsed. Even when the item has risen in value, we say that as long as he still has time to retract, he has not finally resolved to complete the transaction until that period expires. Sema.
