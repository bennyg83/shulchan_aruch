See Sema, seif katan 34. According to the Sema, onaah applies when fruit is sold by rough estimate. Nevertheless, where one sells the future contents of a beehive before they are removed, it appears that onaah does not apply, similar to the rule in the laws of ribbit concerning 'what my hive will produce,' see siman 173, seif 9. Perhaps the distinction is that the contents of a hive cannot be estimated precisely in advance, whereas an expert can estimate with reasonable accuracy the number of measures in a heap of fruit sold by rough estimate. See also siman 211 concerning 'what my net will bring up.' This requires further study.
