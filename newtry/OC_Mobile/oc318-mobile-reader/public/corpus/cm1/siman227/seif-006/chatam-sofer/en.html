There, Mechaber, seif 20, 'one who exchanges.' Note: Maharai Yaavetz, part 2, siman 161, considers it obvious that when an exchange is made by appraisal, the law of ona'ah applies, unlike the Sema. In my opinion it is at least clear that where he is not merely exchanging a needle for armor, but the exchange is accompanied by an additional cash payment, then all authorities agree that ona'ah applies to that excess payment. Independently, the Raavad at the beginning of chapter 13 of Hilchot Mechirah, and the Ramban as cited in the Maggid Mishneh there, maintain that ona'ah applies even to barter; see there and the Beit Yosef here. This is straightforward.
