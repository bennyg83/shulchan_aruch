He hired him to sow a field for him. Meaning, Reuven hired Shimon as a contractor to sow his field using Reuven's seed, and Shimon is responsible only for the labor of sowing. Since the overreaching concerned how much wheat the land required, it remains a question whether this resembles a contractor working on land, to whom the laws of overreaching do not apply, or a contractor working with movables, to whom they do apply. See Beurim. If he hired him to sow using the worker's own wheat, and the overreaching concerned the market value of the wheat, then according to Ramban this remains an unresolved question whether the transaction is treated like land, because the overreaching arose in a sale connected with land, or like movables. According to Tosafot it is certainly treated like movables, because the overreaching concerned the market value of the wheat.
