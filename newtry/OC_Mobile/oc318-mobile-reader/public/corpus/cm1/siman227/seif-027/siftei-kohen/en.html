See the abridged rulings of the Rosh to Perek HaZahav and my own discussion. It appears from the Gemara in Perek HaZahav that just as one who conducts business on trust is not subject to the ordinary law of onaah, neither is his transaction subject to rescission for excessive onaah. The baraita groups one who trades on trust together with one who expressly stipulates, "you shall have no onaah claim against me," and states of both that there is no onaah. According to Rava's conclusion, where the amount is expressly disclosed, even a transaction ordinarily subject to rescission is not rescinded under such a stipulation, as stated in the Gemara and in seif 21. The same therefore appears true for one who trades on trust. Nevertheless, this requires deliberation before practical application. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 433.
