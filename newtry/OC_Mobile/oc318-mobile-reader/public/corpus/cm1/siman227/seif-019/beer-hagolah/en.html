Nun) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 13 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 2, from Rabbah's statement in the name of Rav Huna, Bava Metzia 46a and 47a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the term achsara is the language of the Mishnah in Zeraim, at the end of chapter 2 of Demai. Rambam rules like the Rif that ona'ah applies. The Kesef Mishneh writes that it appears from here that if the parties did not use language of sale, but one merely said, "Exchange your cow for these," there is no claim of ona'ah.
Samekh) There in the rulings. The matter is uncertain whether the halachah follows Rabbah in the name of Rav Huna, as above, or Rav Abba in the name of Rav Huna, who says there is no ona'ah. See the beginning of siman 209 and the Beit Yosef's discussion of the source of the law there, where the meaning of achsara is likewise a dispute between Rabbah in the name of Rav Huna and Rav Abba in the name of Rav Huna.
