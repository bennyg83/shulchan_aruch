This dissent concerns specifically a purchaser who acquired goods for an undetermined lump sum price, because that is not an ordinary mode of acquisition except in an exchange. But produce sold for a lump sum is subject to overreaching according to everyone, as explained above at the beginning of siman 209 regarding "this heap of wheat," which is subject to overreaching. Sema. The Taz writes that the dissent disagrees in both cases and relies here upon what he wrote in siman 209. He concludes that the primary law follows the Mechaber, that both are subject to overreaching. See Beurim: if no language of sale was mentioned at all and one merely says, "Here is my cow in exchange for these," everyone agrees there is no overreaching.
