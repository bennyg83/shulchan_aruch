[Shulchan Aruch] For which there is no law of onaah. Note: The Ramban in his commentary on the Torah and the Raah in Sefer HaChinuch write that nevertheless the prohibition itself applies even to these categories. Scripture excluded them only from the monetary law of onaah and cancellation of the sale. See Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot Malveh, chapter 4, halachah 1, who writes that Tosafot at the beginning of chapter 5 of Bava Metzia plainly disagree and hold that even the prohibition does not apply.
