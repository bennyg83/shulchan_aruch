He has no ona'ah claim against him. See Baer Heitev regarding rescission of the sale. Responsa Shav Yaakov, part 3, siman 169, writes that it is obvious that if a transaction was conducted on trust and an actual defect is afterward discovered, the purchaser may retract. The Gemara and poskim say only that there is no ona'ah, but a defect creating a mistaken sale is different. Otherwise they should have stated the greater novelty. He found the same ruling in Responsa Maharit Tzahalon, siman 25, with the agreement of Maharam Galanti. This applies where the defect could not have been discovered at the time of purchase.
