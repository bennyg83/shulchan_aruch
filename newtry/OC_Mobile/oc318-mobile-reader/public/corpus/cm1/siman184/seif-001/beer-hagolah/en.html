The language of Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 13, from Rabbi Abba's statement at the end of Eizehu Neshekh, beginning of Bava Metzia 74b.
This is explained in that very statement. See Sema, who writes that the condition existed both initially at the time the money was given and finally at the time of acquisition.
He writes at the end of the gloss that this is not literal: whenever the funds are kept separate, whoever made the purchase acquires, even if they were not sealed or tied. The Gemara merely describes the usual case, because when money is kept separate a person commonly wraps and seals it separately. Otherwise the inference from Rambam's opening clause would contradict the inference from the closing clause. Bach writes that this is correct.
