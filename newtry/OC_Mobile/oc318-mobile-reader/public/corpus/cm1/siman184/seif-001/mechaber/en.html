One who purchases a field in another person's name and later says it was for himself, and the law of an agent appointed jointly by three. It contains two seifim:
If three people give money to one agent to make a purchase for them, and the money is mixed together, then if the agent buys with part of the money, the purchase belongs to all three and they divide it according to their respective contributions, even if the agent intended that the item be acquired for only one of them. Rama: If he expressly stated that he was buying it for one of them, however, he thereby withdrew from his agency for the others, and the person for whom he bought acquires it. Tur in the name of Rabbi Yeshayah. If each person's money was separately wrapped and sealed, then even if the agent intended to buy the item for all of them, only the person whose money was actually used acquires it.
