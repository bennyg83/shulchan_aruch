He didn’t even say yes to the seller. He didn’t say that he was buying:
Rather, he told the witnesses, etc. See the end of siman 60, where the Tur and Mechaber state this law regarding a loan document and also discuss the case where Levi comes and says that Reuven purchased with Levi's money.
The seller cannot retract. The Hagahot Mordechai writes that afterward Shimon the seller wished to retract, but could not, even though Reuven had acted without Levi's authorization and Levi had not appointed him as agent. If the agent claims that he purchased with his own money, the dispute between them is governed as explained there.
