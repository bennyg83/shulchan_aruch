If Reuven bought a field from Shimon, told Levi that he had bought it, and had the deed written in Levi's name, then later told Shimon, 'I bought it for myself; write another deed in my name,' the seller cannot be compelled to write a second deed. If Reuven stipulated initially that he was buying for himself and was writing Levi's name only so that people would not know he was the buyer, he may compel the seller to write another deed in his own name. The same applies even if he did not say this to the seller but told the witnesses before the seller, before the first deed was written, that they would later have to write him another deed in his own name. Rama: If he initially bought land in Levi's name, although Levi never appointed him as agent, then if he bought it with Levi's money he cannot retract.
