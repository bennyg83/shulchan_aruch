If he purchased land. Bava Kamma 102b: Rav Sheshes said, this is what it means, etc.
With Levi’s money. There: “This implies that he transfers ownership, etc.; if because of this, etc.” Here the case is not where he informed him, and therefore this applies specifically with Levi’s money, for when it is his money he need not inform him, since the issue of the Western scholars does not apply, as stated earlier in the Gemara there. This is unlike the case above, siman 183 seif 4, “and the principal does not, etc.,” because there he appointed him an agent, as stated above in siman 190 seif 4 in the gloss. See also siman 183 seif 4, “but, etc., in a name, etc.”
