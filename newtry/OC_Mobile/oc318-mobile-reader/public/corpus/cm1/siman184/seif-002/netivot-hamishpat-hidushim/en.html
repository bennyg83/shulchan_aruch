He cannot retract. See Sema and Taz. Where Reuven used Levi's money to buy property but Levi had not appointed him as agent, the seller certainly may retract, because a purchase is not necessarily a benefit to the purchaser. Bach therefore emends the text, as cited by the Shach, to read that Reuven bought with his own money, not Levi's. That is, Reuven bought using his own money but told the seller he was buying in Levi's name. The seller then cannot retract. If the principal later sues the agent and claims that he appointed him and that the purchase was made with the principal's money, while the agent denies it, their dispute is adjudicated accordingly. See Beurim. The Sema's phrase, "if the agent claims," is a scribal error and should read, "if the principal claims."
