Again. See the Taz's explanation. He writes that as a matter of halachah there is no power to compel the seller to write another deed unless both elements were present, namely an evident indication and an express condition. The condition is effective even if it was stated only before witnesses. See there.
To write. See the end of siman 60, where the Tur and Mechaber state this law regarding a loan document. There the law is also clarified where Levi appears and claims that Reuven purchased with Levi's money. Sema.
To retract. Meaning, the seller. The Hagahot Mordechai state: If afterward Shimon the seller wished to retract, he cannot retract, even though the purchase was made without Levi's authorization and Levi had not appointed him as agent. If the agent claims that he bought with his own money, the dispute is between them. Thus far the Sema. The Shach writes that the Bach emends the Rama's wording. See there. The Taz labors at length to reconcile and explain the Rama, and concludes that apparently no halachic ruling can be derived from this gloss because it is obscure and has no satisfactory explanation.
