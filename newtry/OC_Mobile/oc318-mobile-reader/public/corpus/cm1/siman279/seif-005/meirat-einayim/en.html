Slaves and maidservants are not called, etc. The Rambam writes this in chapter 4 of Hilchot Nachalot, based upon the baraita in Berakhot, chapter Hayah Koreh. The Maggid Mishneh cites it there.
So that no mishap result, and this son be found, etc. The mishap is that the son's lineage will be damaged. If someone calls a maidservant "mother," people may conclude that he is actually her son and therefore a slave. If he calls a male slave "father," it does not necessarily follow that he is a slave, because his mother could have been Jewish, as explained above. Nevertheless, there is still a defect in lineage, because betrothal does not take effect with a slave and the child would be regarded as born from a prohibited nonmarital union.
