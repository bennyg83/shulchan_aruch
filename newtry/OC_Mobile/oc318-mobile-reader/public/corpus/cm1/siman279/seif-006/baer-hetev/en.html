A son. The Rashba writes in responsum 610 concerning a man who had an improper relationship with an unmarried woman, expelled her from his house, later brought her back, and declared that her child was his son. He rules that the man is believed, and we do not suspect that he merely spoke on the basis of an assumption or personal estimate. The Rosh writes in responsa, klal 87, siman 2, that this applies specifically in such a case, where she served him and was regularly with him, and therefore we attribute the child to him. The Darchei Moshe cites both rulings. See there. See also Responsa Rashach, part II, siman 92, and Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 3, folio 5a and folio 8b. See Even HaEzer, siman 4, seif 26, and the Beit Shmuel and Be'er Heitev there.
