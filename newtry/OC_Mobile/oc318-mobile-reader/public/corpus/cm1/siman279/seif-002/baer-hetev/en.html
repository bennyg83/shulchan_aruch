Lineage. Regarding lineage, it is impossible that his son should be disqualified as a mamzer while his son's son would be kosher. With respect to inheritance, however, the son's son has no present share or connection to the inheritance, and the grandfather can also divest him of it during his lifetime by declaring that all his property shall belong to someone else.
