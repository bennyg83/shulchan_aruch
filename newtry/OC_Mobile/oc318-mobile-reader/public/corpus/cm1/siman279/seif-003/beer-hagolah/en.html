A baraita there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the reason is that although he could have given him half his property as a gift, once he previously identified him as a slave, he cannot simply retract that identification. His later claim is therefore like a migo against witnesses.
This is the Kesef Mishneh's reading in Rambam. He writes that Rambam apparently read in the Gemara, 'there they called him a slave, son of a maidservant,' while our books read 'son of one hundred zuz,' like the Gemara's standard wording. The Tur likewise writes according to our reading.
