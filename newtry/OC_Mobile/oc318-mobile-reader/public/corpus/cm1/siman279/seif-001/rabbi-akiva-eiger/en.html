[Rama] And he is believed. Note: Responsa Bnei Aharon, siman 57, cites the words of Maharik. In the course of his discussion he writes: "I saw that Rabbi Shmuel de Medina, Choshen Mishpat, siman 306, concludes that this is a dispute among the authorities, and he is not believed to remove property from the possession of the definite heir with respect to property that will fall to him afterward." I have now merited to obtain the works of Rabbi Shmuel de Medina, and I saw that he rules there that even where we do not know whether he had such property when he said, "This is my son," we leave everything in the possession of the definite heirs and do not apply the presumption that what is found here was here previously. See above, end of siman 250.
