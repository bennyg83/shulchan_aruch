Seif 3. One who says, "This is my son." Note: See Sema citing the Tur that a retraction within immediate speech is believed. In the Tur itself, however, Rashbam's view appears to be that even within immediate speech he is not believed. See Bach's explanation that because this is not a direct retraction, it is not governed by the ordinary rule of immediate speech. See my responsum to Krakow in the additions to Nezer Yisrael, siman 210, and Sema, seif katan 12. See also my Even HaEzer, fifth edition, siman 122, responsum to Brody, where I explained Sema in a manner that does not contradict Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer siman 4, seif 30, and Rambam.
