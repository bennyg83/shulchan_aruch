(Seif 3, and he later said, “He is my slave.”) The Sema writes that the father is believed within the time required for speech only where he says that the person was born from a maidservant, and the like. I do not know what compelled him to limit the law this way. All the credibility discussed here concerns whether that person inherits him. For that purpose, the father’s later statement “He is my slave” is effective to establish that he does not inherit him.
