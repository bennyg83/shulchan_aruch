Even. The novelty is that there are two disadvantages. First, the subject is something not yet in existence, which a person generally cannot transfer. Second, when he is moribund he is not legally capable of making an acquisition even through testing and gestures, for he is nearly dead. Nevertheless, by acknowledging that this person is his son or his heir, the person automatically inherits from him from that moment. Sema.
