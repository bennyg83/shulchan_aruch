Definitive identifying marks. See Sema, seif katan 4, through his statement, "if his utensils were of such and such a kind," and so forth. It appears that if he gives definitive identifying marks in his utensils, those marks are effective for monetary matters. Tosafot in Yevamot 120, s.v. kelav, write that in monetary law we do not suspect that the garments were borrowed. Sema means only weak identifying marks, such as saying they were white or red.
