A creditor who produced. Bava Metzia 21a, concerning one who found a document, etc. The Rishonim explain that this refers to the creditor. According to Rashi there, the phrase "and if there is with them" must be understood as presenting a separate case, as Rashi explains there from the Gemara's wording, "we learned: if there is with them," etc. According to the other explanation, however, the entire passage concerns the creditor. Some Geonim establish it specifically where the creditor's heir is involved, because the Gemara appears to discuss a receipt found among torn documents. With the creditor himself, however, it need not be found among torn documents, because he was negligent in failing to remember, as the Ramban writes concerning the Mishnah there on 36a, "the father of one of you deposited," etc. It says specifically "the father," but if one of them himself had deposited it, there would be no need to give it to them, since he himself admitted the uncertainty and they were negligent in not remembering.
An authenticated document. Otherwise the debtor would in any event be believed, as explained above in siman 46, seif 1.
And if he later said. He is believed to say, "I remembered," as in Ketubot 22a, where one said to him, "Do you remember?" etc., and eventually he remembered, etc.; he said to him, "Do you think," etc., "I am the one," etc.
