One who marries off his adult son in a house. Rambam's wording in chapter 6 of Hilchot Zechiyah is, 'one who marries off his adult son to a virgin in a house.' This is also an explicit Gemara in chapter Mi Shemet. I do not know why the word 'to a virgin' was omitted here. It is obviously a scribal error, and the text should read, 'to a virgin in a house.' Rama therefore should not have presented this law in the name of 'some say,' since it is an explicit Gemara. Rather, he should have inserted the words 'to a virgin' into the Mechaber's text.
