Wrapped. Birkei Yosef cites the Shelah, page 215, who writes that the practice of wrapping oneself is not observed today. He did, however, see his teacher, when sitting in judgment, put on the outer garment in which he went to the synagogue. See Orach Chaim, siman 232, seif 2, and the Sema above at the end of siman 5.
