For it is a mitzvah to treat him lightly. The Sema writes that Scripture calls such judges “gods of silver,” analogous to idolatry, which it is a mitzvah to treat with contempt. Birkei Yosef writes that according to this, deriving benefit from such a judge resembles deriving benefit from idolatry; gifts given to him are not merely unlike first fruits, but are comparable to providing benefit to idolatry, and honoring him is comparable to honoring idolatry. See Keneh Chochmah, page 105b.
