It is forbidden to treat even an officer of the beit din disrespectfully. If someone causes him distress, the beit din may administer lashes for rebellious conduct. The officer is believed like two witnesses to testify that a person insulted him, so that the offender may be placed under a ban. {Rama: The officer may report the matter to the beit din, and this does not constitute lashon hara (Maimoniyos, chapter 25 of Sanhedrin, and the Gemara, chapter Eilu Megalchin). He may also personally enforce the law against one who resists him and strike him. If he damages the offender's property while doing so, he is exempt (Rabbeinu Yeruham, netiv 31, part 2, and Nimukei Yosef, beginning of chapter HaManiach). See above, beginning of siman 11.}
