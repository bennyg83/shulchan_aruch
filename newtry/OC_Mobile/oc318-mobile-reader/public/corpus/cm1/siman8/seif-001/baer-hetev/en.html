And is not. The vav in "and is not learned" is disjunctive. Two separate cases are stated: either the appointee is unworthy in conduct even though he is a Torah scholar, or he is not a Torah scholar even though his conduct is proper, as the continuation states. Sema.
He will ask. Because perhaps he will fail to ask and will thereby distort the judgment. Rashi. This is unlike the reason given by the Ir Shushan. Sema.
