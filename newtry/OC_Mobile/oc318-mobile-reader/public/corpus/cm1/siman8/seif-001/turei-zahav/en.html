Shulchan Aruch, seif 3 “And they press them,” etc. This is difficult from Kiddushin 76, where the host of Rav Adda the convert, whose mother was Jewish, quarreled with Rav Bibi over who should administer the city’s affairs, and Rav Adda reconciled them. How, according to Rambam, could Rav Bibi disregard the Sages’ instruction and not wait until others urged him? It appears that one must wait only where it is known that people will urge him after he initially refuses. There, however, the convert was pursuing authority himself.
