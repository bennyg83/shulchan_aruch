a) The wording of the Rambam in chapter 3 of Hilkhot Sanhedrin, and so writes the Tur. The Rambam explains there that it is derived from the verse, "You shall not recognize faces in judgment," etc. The Semag writes in negative commandment 194 that this is taught in the Mekhilta.
b) There, from the Yerushalmi at the end of Bikkurim, and so writes the Tur. The Bach writes that it appears the meaning is that he is not learned in Torah wisdom. But if he is learned in Torah wisdom, even though there are greater scholars than he in the city, there is no prohibition in this, and he brings proof for this.
