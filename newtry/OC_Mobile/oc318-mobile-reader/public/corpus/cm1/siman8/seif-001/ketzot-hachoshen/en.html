Every judge. Bach brings proof from the episode of Rabbi Yehoshua ben Gamla that if someone paid money in order to be appointed to a judicial or communal position, even if he is actually greater in wisdom and number than the alternatives, it is forbidden to rise before him in honor. But if he himself paid nothing and others appointed him partly because he is wealthy, then so long as he is a Torah scholar he is entitled to the honor due his learning.
