Who is unfit. Sanhedrin 7b.
And is not learned. There, concerning the household of the Nasi, etc.
Even though he is. Sifrei, Parashat Devarim, on "you shall not show favoritism," etc., that one should not say, etc.
(Collected note) Even though he is wholly desirable, etc. Sifrei, Parashat Devarim, on "you shall not show favoritism in judgment": this refers to the official appointed to seat judges. Perhaps you might say, "So and so is handsome, I will seat him as a judge; so and so is powerful, I will seat him as a judge; so and so knows every language, etc." See there. (End of collected note.)
And it is forbidden to appoint. Shabbos 139a; see Rashi there.
And cities. Sanhedrin 23a, concerning secular courts, etc.
And once. There, "When does this apply? When, etc.," and even regarding such courts.
(Collected note) And every judge. Yerushalmi, end of Bikkurim: Rabbi Mana cursed those who are appointed through money. Rabbi Ami applied to them the verse, "You shall not make for yourselves gods of silver or gods of gold." Rabbi Yoshiyah said: "The cloak upon him is like a donkey's saddlecloth." Rabbi said: "A judge appointed through money, one does not rise before him and one does not call him Rabbi; the cloak upon him is, etc." (End of collected note.)
To stand before him. It should read "before him," and so is the text in the Yerushalmi.
