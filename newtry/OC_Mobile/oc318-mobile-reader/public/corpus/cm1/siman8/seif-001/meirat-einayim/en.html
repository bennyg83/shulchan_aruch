And he is not learned, etc. I explained in Perishah that the connecting vav in "and he is not learned" is disjunctive, presenting two separate faults. One may not appoint a judge unfit in conduct even if he is a Torah scholar, nor one who is not a Torah scholar even if his conduct is proper. This is why the Mechaber concludes that even if he is wholly desirable in other respects, the appointment is improper.
