With awe. Because of the honor of the Shechinah, as it is written, "God stands in the congregation of judges."
Judges. It should appear to him as though he were judging before the Holy One, blessed is He, as it is written, "for the judgment is not for man," etc. Sema.
Truthfully. Meaning that he judges according to the truth appropriate to the circumstances. At times this means according to strict Torah law, and at times according to the needs of the hour in order to erect a safeguard. So writes the Sema. See the Bach and Derishah for additional explanations.
