Seif 5 “Even concerning an agent of beit din,” etc. The Beit Yosef expressed surprise; yet the sugya in Kiddushin states that a man insulted an agent of the Sages, Rav Yehuda excommunicated him, and Rav Nachman asked why he had not merely administered lashes. Rav Yehuda answered that he had imposed the more severe sanction. This very exchange explains the novelty. The passages mentioning sometimes excommunication and sometimes lashes mean that both are available according to the judge’s assessment. Excommunication is spiritually more severe, though people ordinarily find lashes harder to bear. The agent is believed even regarding the more serious sanction, and certainly regarding the lesser. The Sema’s assertion that he is not believed regarding striking is therefore difficult, for Rav Nachman’s question presupposed that lashes could be imposed on the agent’s report. Alternatively, Rambam mentioned “So-and-so insulted me” only regarding the agent’s credibility, while the sugya teaches that excommunication may actually be imposed. On the explanation above, all is resolved.
