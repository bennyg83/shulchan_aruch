The judges must sit with awe and fear, wrapped appropriately and with gravity. They may not act frivolously or sit in beit din conversing about idle matters. A judge should regard himself as though a sword rests upon his neck and Gehinnom is open beneath him. He must know whom he is judging, before Whom he is judging, and Who will exact payment from him if he deviates from the line of justice. Any judge who does not render a true judgment causes the Shechinah to depart from Israel. Any judge who takes money from one person and gives it to another unlawfully causes the Holy One, blessed is He, to exact lives from him. Any judge who renders a true judgment according to the truth, even for one hour, is considered as though he repaired the entire world and causes the Shechinah to dwell among Israel. Lest a judge say, "Why do I need this trouble?" Scripture states, "And He is with you in the matter of judgment." A judge has only what his eyes see.
