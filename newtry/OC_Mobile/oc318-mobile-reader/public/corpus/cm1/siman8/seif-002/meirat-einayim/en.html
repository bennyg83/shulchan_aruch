The judges must sit in awe, etc. Because of the honor of the Shechinah, which rests among them, as it is written, 'God stands in the congregation of God.'
As though a sword, etc. This refers to punishment in this world.
And as though Gehinnom, etc. This refers to punishment in the World to Come. Both are expounded from the verses in Shir HaShirim concerning Shlomo's bed, surrounded by sixty warriors, all holding swords because of fear in the nights. The bed alludes to the place of the Shechinah; the warriors allude to Israel; the sword at one's thigh signifies the threat of punishment, and the nights allude to Gehinnom.
He should know whom he is judging. He should view himself as though he is judging before God, as it is written, 'See what you are doing, for you do not judge for man but for God.'
And before whom he judges. Meaning that Hashem is present with them in judgment, as stated, 'God stands in the congregation of God.'
He is destined to exact punishment from him. As the verse says, 'in the midst of judges He judges,' meaning the God mentioned at the beginning of the verse will judge among the judges themselves and exact punishment from them.
That the Shechinah may withdraw from Israel. As it is written, 'Because of the oppression of the poor, because of the groaning of the needy, now I will arise, says Hashem.'
He takes his soul from him. As it is written, 'He takes the soul of those who take from them.'
Who judges a true judgment according to its truth. Meaning he judges truthfully in light of the needs of the time: sometimes by ordinary Torah law and sometimes through extraordinary measures to fence a breach. See the Derishah at the beginning of siman 1 for additional explanations.
It is as though he repaired the entire world. When people see that justice is enforced, they refrain from wrongdoing. This is learned from the verse, 'the people stood before Moshe from morning until evening,' as explained in the Perishah at the beginning of siman 1.
And he causes the Shechinah to rest. As it is written, 'God stands in the congregation of God.'
