From the Mishnah, 'robbers took his garment,' etc., Bava Kamma 114a. Here the owners did not despair of recovering the items, and the same follows from the baraita above. Hagahot Mordekhai in Bava Kamma likewise writes this. But if the purchaser bought it in the marketplace, the enactment protecting ordinary commerce applies and the owner must reimburse the purchase price, as explained below in siman 356.
