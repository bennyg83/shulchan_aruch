And even the craftsman himself. In Sema, in the parenthetical gloss stating that the choice rests with the person to whom it was delivered, it appears to me that there is a scribal error. Why should he have such a choice? Rather, Tur means that the law follows the nature of their dispute. At times the excess is returned, such as in a case of one sixth overcharge and similar matters, while at times the garment itself is returned. The ruling follows the claims and circumstances of the case.
