One whose garments were exchanged in a house of mourning or at a celebration. It contains 2 se'ifim.
If a person's garment was exchanged at a craftsman's shop, he may use the garment he received until its owner comes and takes his own. This applies only if the craftsman himself gave it to him, not if the craftsman's wife or children gave it. Even when the craftsman gave it, this applies only if he said without qualification, 'Here is a garment.' If he said, 'Here is your garment,' the recipient may not use it.
