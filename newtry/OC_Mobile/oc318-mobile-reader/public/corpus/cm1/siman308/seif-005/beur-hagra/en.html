One who hires, etc. This follows from the Gemara's statement, "to bring a letech, etc.," implying that the first clause does not refer specifically to a letech. This is also implied by the general expression "to bring, etc.," meaning any weight upon which he stipulated. Rava's calculation likewise implies that the volume of barley is equivalent to an addition of one thirtieth over wheat. See siman 309:3, where the Rosh holds this view. Rashi, Tosafos, Ramban, and other poskim, however, hold that he is liable only when he adds beyond the load the animal is capable of carrying, in accordance with the plain meaning of the Mishnah, which speaks of a letech.
