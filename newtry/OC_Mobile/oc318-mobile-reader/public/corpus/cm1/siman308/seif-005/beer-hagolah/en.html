The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Rambam appears to hold that even though the animal would ordinarily carry more than the load placed upon it, once he added one thirtieth beyond the weight for which he hired it, he is liable. The Ramban disagrees. The Beit Yosef writes that he found in the teachings of the Rashba's students that where the lessor told him that he was hiring it for half a letekh, if he added beyond that amount he is liable, because it is evident that the lessor knew his donkey was weak. But if the renter said, 'Hire it to me to carry half a letekh,' and the lessor rented it to him without qualification, it is possible that if he added only up to its ordinary carrying capacity he is exempt. These are sound words. The responsum of the Rosh accords with this, and the Rambam's words can also be explained this way.
