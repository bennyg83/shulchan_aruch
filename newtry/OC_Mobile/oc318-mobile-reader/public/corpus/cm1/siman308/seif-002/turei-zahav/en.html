(Seif 2) Pregnant or nursing. The Tur writes in the name of the Ramah that if he specified “this woman,” she may not make any change at all, even to an equivalent load and even if the owner accompanies her. If he did not specify “this woman,” but merely a woman said, “Rent me your animal,” she may change to an equivalent load. If neither the owner nor the renter accompanies the animal, but it is handed to another person, even a minor change is prohibited, because a renter is not authorized, etc. The words, “meaning that the renter,” refer to the clause concerning “this woman.” This follows the manner in which the Tur writes in siman 211, seif 3, the same three rules in the name of the Ramah. The Beit Yosef’s difficulty is thereby resolved.
