He may place any woman upon it. The Tur cites the Ramah that this applies only when it was rented for “a woman” without identifying her. If it was rented for this particular woman, he may not substitute another woman. Likewise, if it was rented for this particular load, he may not substitute another load even if it is no heavier. Others permit substitution when the replacement load is not heavier, and the Rosh agrees. Rabbeinu Yerucham, the Ramban, and Rashba also follow the permissive view regarding “this wine.” The later authorities rule accordingly, and this is primary.
