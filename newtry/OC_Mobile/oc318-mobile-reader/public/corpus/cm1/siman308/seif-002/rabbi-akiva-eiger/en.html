[Shulchan Aruch] He is liable for its damage. Note: Likewise, regarding the additional burden, he must compensate him, even when it was less than the measure stated here, as in seif 5. So writes the Bach. See Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban, who wonders: is he a guardian of the animal's body that he should be liable to him? If he rented a slave from another, even then he is exempt for negligence; and if liability is as a tortfeasor, the damage did not come through his direct force, even if he loaded it. See Shitah Mekubetzet in the name of the Ritva.
