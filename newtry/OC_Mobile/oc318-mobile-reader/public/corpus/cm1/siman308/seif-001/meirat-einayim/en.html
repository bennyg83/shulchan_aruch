He may not place a woman upon it. Because she is heavier.
The same applies to a non-Jew. Because an ordinary non-Jew is not careful with a Jew's property.
He is not liable to pay. Since an animal is ordinarily fit to carry a woman as well, even though this renter told the owner that he would place a man upon it, the deviation is not regarded as negligence. Had he asked to rent it for a woman, the owner would also have rented it to him, though he might have demanded a somewhat higher rental. Therefore this renter must add to the rental payment but is exempt from liability for accidents.
