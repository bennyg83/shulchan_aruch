Some disagree. The Darkei Moshe identifies the dissenters as the Rosh's responsum cited below, siman 309:3, and Riva in the Mordechai. If so, it is somewhat difficult that the Rama there rules without qualification like the Mechaber. The cases may be distinguished. There the owner specified that the animal be returned the next day, so the renter should have realized that the owner knew his animal's limits. Riva may likewise concern an owner who expressly said, “I rent it to you for a Jew to ride.” Here the animal was rented simply for a man. If it was rented without specification and the owner expressly stated that it was being rented for a man to ride, the renter would be liable for substituting a non-Jew, because an ordinary non-Jew is not careful with a Jew's property and this is full negligence. This distinction is stated by the Rashba's students and accepted by the Beit Yosef.
