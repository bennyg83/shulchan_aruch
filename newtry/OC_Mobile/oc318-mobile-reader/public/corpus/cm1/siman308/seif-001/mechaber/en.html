How much may be placed upon an animal rented for riding. It contains seven seifim.
If one rents an animal for a man to ride, he may not have a woman ride it, and the same applies to a non-Jew. Some say this restriction applies only initially, but if he transgressed and put a woman on it, he is not liable even if the animal was damaged. Others disagree. All agree that where local custom charges more for women riders, he must add according to custom. If he rented it for a woman to ride, he may have a man ride it.
