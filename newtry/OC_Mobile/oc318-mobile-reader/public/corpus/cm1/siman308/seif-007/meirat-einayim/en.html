The shoulder added is . If a man’s reward is one of those who have a shoulder to the place of P. and have made a portion of Hashem more than he is accustomed to a single time, and the worker has thought that he has been wrong with the custom and that he is not allowed to learn from his shoulder, and that he may not be able to do so
For he feels the weight of the burden. Meaning, one might argue that when he felt the burden was too heavy he should have thrown it off, and since he did not, he knowingly accepted the risk. Nevertheless, the renter is liable. We say that because the worker did not know an excessive load had been placed upon him, even though he felt unusual heaviness he thought: I am accustomed to carrying a load of this type. Why is today different? I must simply be temporarily weak, and the weakness will pass shortly. Therefore he did not throw off the load, and in the meantime he was injured by it. For this reason the renter remains liable.
For this is a condition of beit din. Meaning, whenever a person is suddenly confronted by a loss, beit din established that he may take his fellow's property in order to save his own, provided he compensates his fellow for the property taken, and similarly in comparable cases. See simanim 264 and 265 for the details. It is somewhat difficult that Rama writes here without qualification that he may take it, while in siman 264:5 he writes that some disagree, and at the end of siman 274 he likewise notes a dispute. The dissenters are the Rif and Rambam, cited by the Tur there. We must say that Rama understands that even they disagree only with the opposing position of the Rosh and those with him, who hold that the owner of the wine can be compelled to pour out his wine against his will. But if the owner of the wine is absent and has already shown that he does not consent, perhaps even the dissenters agree that the owner of the honey may pour out the other's wine on condition that he pay for it. Rama's point here is only that he is not called a robber, because we say that perhaps had the owner been present he would not have objected to this arrangement in order to save his fellow from loss.
