There, where Rav Chiya bar Yosef instituted the rule, according to Rambam's explanation. The Maggid Mishneh explains the reason: the two workers should not have joined together to carry a load unless each one could carry it alone. Since the load was too heavy for each individually, there was reason to require full payment, but since it was light for both together, there was reason to exempt them. Therefore they pay half. This is Rambam's view, as explained in the second clause.
