But most, etc. This follows our Gemara's reading, that he returns five, and Raavad and Rosh's explanation that 'this was said only, etc.' refers to repayment on market day. They omit the phrase 'he should have sold it.' A market day means the actual market day; before then he may say that he needs money. Maggid Mishneh offers another reason, unlike Rashi. Raavad explains that this applies only when the barrel was broken on market day. If it was broken on an ordinary day, he pays only four, its value when broken, under the rule that robbers pay according to the time of robbery. Maggid Mishneh writes that all commentators accepted Raavad's explanation.
(Supplement) The porter, etc.; this too is the wording, etc. There are three approaches. Rashi and Tosafot hold that monetary payment is always required, though before the first market day passes he may repay with a barrel of wine. According to them, 'this was said only' refers to ordinary days, when he pays five because the owner could not sell it. Rosh limits the rule to a barrel broken on market day; otherwise payment is based on its value at the time of damage. Therefore he cannot accept Rashi's interpretation about the first market day. Tur likewise writes that even if several market days passed, the rule remains. Rif reads that on ordinary days he returns four. Nimukei Yosef, citing Raavad, explains: if he broke it on market day and repaid on market day, he returns a barrel or five; if he broke it on an ordinary day, he pays four. This is also Rosh's approach. Rambam likewise follows Rif's reading but interprets it, like Rashi, with reference to the time of repayment. He holds that a barrel may always be tendered, but if money is paid its amount is assessed at the time of repayment. Only when the owner has no other wine on market day does he receive five. Maggid Mishneh explains 'this was said only' as referring to market day, like Rosh. Tur, however, understands Rambam's condition as referring to the day of the breakage, like Rosh, and rejects the alternative interpretation. (End of supplement.)
