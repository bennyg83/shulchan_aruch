Zayin) There, halachah 3, from Rava's statement on Bava Metzia 99b. The Maggid Mishneh explains that the case is where the damage occurred through negligence, or where witnesses were present and the custodian failed to bring proof, etc.
Chet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this version is found in some texts, though Rambam's textual reading differs from ours. Rambam's version apparently reads that on other days they return three. Some manuscripts of the Rif likewise read this way. The intent is that because they must reimburse him with barrels of wine, when they instead pay money the value is calculated according to what the wine was worth at the time of return.
Tet) There in Rava's statement.
Yod) According to our version of the Gemara and the Rosh's explanation, where they pay four this applies specifically if the barrels were broken on market day. The Maggid Mishneh writes similarly in the name of the Raavad, Rabbenu Yonatan, and Rashba.
The Sema explains that the case is where he purchased replacement wine after the time of the breakage.
