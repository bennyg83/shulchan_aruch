From here, etc. As written there, regarding a beam he pays the entire amount, in accordance with his approach that even if two people carried it, the same law applies here.
(Likkut) From here you learn, etc. This follows the Rosh's version, which does not read that regarding a beam he pays the entire amount. Likewise, Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch did not mention that rule above, and therefore they needed to derive it from here. End.
