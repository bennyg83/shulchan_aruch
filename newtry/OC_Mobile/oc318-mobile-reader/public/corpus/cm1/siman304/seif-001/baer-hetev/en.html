To the place. The Mechaber copied the Rambam's wording, which does not distinguish between one who stumbles on a slope and one who stumbles on level ground. The Rif and Rosh likewise appear not to distinguish. The Tur, however, distinguishes: one who stumbles where there is no slope is considered negligent and is legally liable even if he is an unpaid custodian, because he should have been careful while walking on level ground. Nevertheless, there is a mitzvah to act beyond the strict letter of the law with workers, and if they are poor it is even a mitzvah to pay their wages when they have nothing to eat. The Gemara at the end of HaOmanin likewise appears to distinguish between a sloped place and other ground. The Shach, however, writes that, on the contrary, the Gemara supports the Rambam, Mechaber, and those who agree with them.
