For it is an obligation upon Israel to support, etc. This is derived from the verse concerning the Kohen Gadol, 'greater than his brothers,' which Chazal interpret as requiring his brethren to make him great from their own resources.
Or unspecified consecrated funds. This excludes funds consecrated for a particular designated purpose, which may not be diverted to another purpose, as explained in Orach Chayyim siman 153 and Yoreh Deah siman 256. See the Perishah.
And it is better to collect, etc. This refers back to the Mechaber's statement that the money may be collected at the beginning or end of the year.
