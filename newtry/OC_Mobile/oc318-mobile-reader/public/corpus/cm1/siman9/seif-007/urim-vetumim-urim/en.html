A meritorious argument for a poor person, etc. Sema, citing Maharshal, says the same duty applies to a wealthy person. The text mentions a poor person because one might have thought that due to the dignity of a great judge or fear of a wealthy litigant the judge should remain silent. Taz, citing Maharal of Prague, says specifically a poor person creates a duty to speak, whereas in favor of a wealthy person the judge is not similarly obligated. Maharshal's position appears stronger. See Tumim for the precise reading of the Gemara.
