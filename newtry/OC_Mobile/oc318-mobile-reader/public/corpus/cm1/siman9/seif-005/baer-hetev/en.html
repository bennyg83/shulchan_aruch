Forbidden. The Shach writes in the name of the Bach that it is permitted to take payment for arranging a compromise. The Sema writes that if the judge has a trade, then even if he is not actually engaged in his trade when the litigants come before him, since the matter depends upon his availability, although accepting payment is improper initially, after the fact his judgment remains valid.
