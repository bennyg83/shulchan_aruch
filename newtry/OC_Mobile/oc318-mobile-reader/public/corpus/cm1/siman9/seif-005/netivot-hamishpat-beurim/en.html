One who takes payment to judge. Knesset HaGedolah, in the name of Maharit, writes that this applies specifically to a judgment dependent upon judicial discretion. In my humble opinion, however, the Sages removed from him the status of a judge altogether. It is therefore as though the litigant made his claim outside beit din, so he may change his claims, and an admission made before that person is not an admission, because he is not a judge.
Compensation for lost work. If he did not stipulate it, see Sema and Shach. Ketzot HaChoshen writes that this resembles one who says, "Pay my debt," and the like. This is incorrect, because there he expressly said "pay," which is equivalent to saying, "Give your money." Here, however, he merely said, "Judge for me," without mentioning money at all. It is possible that the judge is content to perform the mitzvah at his own expense and to reckon the reward of the mitzvah against its loss.
