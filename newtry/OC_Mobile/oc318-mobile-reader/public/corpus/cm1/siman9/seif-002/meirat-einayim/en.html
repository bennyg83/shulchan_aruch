From the trait of piety. Mahardach, bayit 26, writes that the matter is merely pious restraint only where the favor was done for the judge outside the presence of the opposing litigant. If it is done before the other litigant, it is certainly forbidden because it will close that litigant's mouth and impede his arguments. See Darkei Moshe.
