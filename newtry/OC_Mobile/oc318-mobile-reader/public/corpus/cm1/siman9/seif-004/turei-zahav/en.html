In the name of Rabbi Yonah of blessed memory: “A student who sits and sees a valid argument for the poor litigant” The text specifies poor and rich in light of Rashi’s explanation of “You shall not fear”: the judge must not think, “How can I impair the rich man’s honor? I will rule for him now, and outside I will tell him to pay.” Thus the teacher is not mistaken in law and does not seek a permanent distortion, but only a temporary accommodation to the rich man’s honor. Even so, the student must not remain silent.
