Shulchan Aruch, seif 1 “When he claims it from him,” etc. The Sema distinguishes this from interest, but he overlooked Rashi in Bava Metzia 61: fixed interest is compelled by beit din to be returned if the borrower demanded it during the lender’s lifetime. Thus interest too requires a claim; the novelty is that it is judicially recoverable once claimed. A case arose where a person had no property except fixed interest taken from him that had not yet been returned, and his creditor sought to collect that interest directly under Rabbi Natan’s rule. It appears he need not pay it, because absent a claim by the borrower for its return, it is not yet legally his property. Moreover, Yoreh De'ah 161 states that return is compelled to fulfill “that your brother may live with you,” meaning return it so that he may live. Here the interest has not reached the borrower so as to sustain him, and therefore it is not extracted under Rabbi Natan’s rule.
