(Anthology) Rather, he should say, etc. Sanhedrin 35a expounds, 'You shall not answer,' etc., according to Tosafot's first interpretation there, s.v. dinei, which applies also to monetary cases. See Sema, siman 18, seif katan 2, in his explanation of Tosafot.
