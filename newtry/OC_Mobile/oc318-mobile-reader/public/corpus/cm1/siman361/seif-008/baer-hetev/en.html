That he must. The Maggid Mishneh writes this in the Rambam's name. The Shach writes that in his view nothing can be proven from the Rambam, and the Kesef Mishneh also says so. As a matter of law, it is clear that we rule that he need not do so, for this is the view of many great authorities. Rivash likewise writes that those who disagree with the Rambam are exceedingly numerous. The Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit, siman 50, also writes that since major authorities hold he need not do so and there is no proof from the Rambam, we adopt that ruling. This is the primary law. The Sema likewise rules this plainly in siman 107, seif 5, and see what I wrote there in seif katan 8, unlike the Bach there, who treats it as an unresolved question of law.
