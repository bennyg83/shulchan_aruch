peh) The Maggid Mishneh, chapter 4 of Hilkhot Gezeilah, law 14, in the name of the sages of the generations, and the Kesef Mishneh there in the name of the Rivash citing many authorities. The Ramah responded similarly to the Ramban, who wrote of him, 'His lips should be kissed.' In chapter 4 of Hilkhot To'en the Maggid Mishneh was uncertain as to the Rambam's view, and the Ran in chapter Kol HaNishba'in cites both opinions regarding the case of one who seized a bar of metal from another.
tzadi) The Maggid Mishneh so understands the Rambam, and the Kesef Mishneh likewise writes that Rabbeinu Yerucham and the Rivash in responsum 392 state that the Rambam holds this view.
