And some say, etc. This is learned from the case of the metal bar of R' Abba. See siman 75, seif 13 in the Rama's gloss, from the statement that he is like a robber. The first opinion explains that case as one where there are witnesses who saw the object. A proof is from Kesubos 18a: 'Let it teach,' etc., 'but we rule,' etc. If the other view were correct, it should have taught the case of a robber. The latter opinion is inferred by Maggid Mishneh from Rambam's words cited by Shulchan Aruch in siman 364, seif 4. See Be'er HaGolah there. Meaning, Rambam did not specify that there were witnesses who saw it. This is not conclusive, for Rambam merely used the Gemara's formulation. See what I wrote in siman 357, seif 1, and see Shach.
(Likkut) And some say that he must, etc. The Rosh likewise writes there: if the adult heirs say, 'We know,' etc., and the case concerns, etc., since it is known, etc., and it remains in the father's possession, etc. End.
