[Shulchan Aruch] And he consumed it, he is exempt. Note: Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 4, halachah 12 of Hilchos Malveh, s.v. "Maran wrote that the Rashba," is uncertain whether, if one sent an agent to steal and the agent brought the item to him, and he then consumed it after ye’ush, he is likewise exempt.
