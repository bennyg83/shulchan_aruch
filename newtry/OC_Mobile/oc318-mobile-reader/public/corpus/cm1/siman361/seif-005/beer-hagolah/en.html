There, seif 7, from Rav Chisda's statement: if one robbed and the owners had not despaired, etc., Bava Kamma chapter 10, 111a. The Maggid Mishneh writes in chapter 5 of Hilkhot Gezeilah, law 4, that some commentators hold that even if the robber fed the stolen item to another person, the victim may, if he wishes, collect from the one who ate it. Rambam does not distinguish otherwise. Nimukei Yosef likewise cites this in his name.
