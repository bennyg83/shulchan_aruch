By mistake. This is likewise stated in the Rashba's novellae to HaNizakin. However, it states there that if it was given to him as an exchange for his own property, it constitutes a change of possession. This also appears below in siman 369, seif 5; see the Beit Yosef there. Yet Baal HaMaor in HaGozel Batra expressly proves that even a mistaken transfer constitutes a change of possession, and Ramban there appears to agree. But Responsa Rashba, siman 968, gives one resolution according to which even an exchange is not a change of possession when the item was given to him against the giver's will. See there. All this requires study. Ibid.
