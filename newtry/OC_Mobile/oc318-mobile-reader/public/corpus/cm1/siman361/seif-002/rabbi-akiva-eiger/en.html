[Shulchan Aruch] From the first or from the second. Note: Reuven saw Shimon enter Levi’s house and steal an article. That article later came into Reuven’s possession, and Reuven returned it to Shimon. Levi cannot recover it from Reuven in beit din, since Shimon said that he lawfully took it from Levi’s house on account of a claim he had against him. Responsa Rosh, rule 64. It is further stated there: Reuven stole an item, and after he had lifted it, Shimon assisted him in bringing the stolen item into Shimon’s house. Shimon is exempt. See there.
