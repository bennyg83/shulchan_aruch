Seif 5. "But if another person came and took it from the robber's house, etc." Note: The apparent difficulty from the Mishna and from the law in siman 368 concerning one who rescues property from bandits, the explanation of the Rama's words here, and the distinctions of law that arise in this matter are discussed in my work on Even HaEzer, sixth edition, responsum 47, written to the community of Briegel. See what I wrote there and examine carefully.
