[Shulchan Aruch] For their father would not have been required to swear. Note: If the heirs claim on the basis of a document and the debt was still within its fixed term, one may say, in light of what the Sema wrote above, siman 78, seif katan 2, in the name of the Mordechai, that the presumption that a person does not pay within the fixed term exists only where the claimant asserts the matter with certainty, but not where heirs assert it only as a matter of doubt. Perhaps the law here is that they must take the oath that their father did not instruct them otherwise. The rule that a person cannot bequeath a claim requiring an oath would not apply, since their father would not have been required to take the oath imposed on one who collects from the property of orphans, because the debt was within its fixed term. This resembles the case of an express waiver of the oath requirement. See further.
