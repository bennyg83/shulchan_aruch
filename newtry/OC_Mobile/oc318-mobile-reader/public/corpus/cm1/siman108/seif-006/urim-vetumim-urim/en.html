That our father did not instruct him, etc. If he says the father did instruct him, then if the debt document is in his hands and he could burn or conceal it, he is believed. If not, he is not believed. Perhaps while he is still a minor he cannot be sued, but the document remains until he matures, at which point the witness is like one witness testifying that the document was paid. See Tumim, where I wrote that the entire law requires study.
If beit din appointed him. He need not swear that the father gave him no instructions, because the father could not have known that beit din would later appoint him guardian. Although guardians sometimes must swear and we do not exempt them for fear people will refuse the role, that applies where the oath reflects suspicion that the guardian wronged the orphans. Here he swears only in order to benefit the orphans and collect their document, so he is performing a mitzvah and will not refuse because of that. Tur.
