Beit Yosef writes: it appears to me that this law is almost explicit in the Gemara there, because the Gemara explains, "This is what it means: orphans collecting from orphans do not collect except with an oath," and does not establish the case as one where the borrower himself claimed, "I paid your father, so swear to me," because the Mishnah implies that in every circumstance they collect only with an oath, whether a claim is raised or not.
