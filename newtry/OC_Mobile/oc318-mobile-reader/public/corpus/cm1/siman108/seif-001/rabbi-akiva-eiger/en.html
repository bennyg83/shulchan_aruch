[Shulchan Aruch] An oral loan is not collected from heirs. Note: If there is an oral creditor and a creditor holding a document, and the document creditor admits that the oral creditor was not paid, then if the estate is insufficient for both debts, the oral creditor receives his share because the document creditor admits his claim. But if the estate is sufficient for both debts, the document creditor collects his debt, since enough property remains from which the oral creditor could theoretically collect, even though the heirs may not believe the document creditor’s admission and may therefore refuse to pay the oral creditor. So writes Beit Me’ir, siman 102, seif 1.
