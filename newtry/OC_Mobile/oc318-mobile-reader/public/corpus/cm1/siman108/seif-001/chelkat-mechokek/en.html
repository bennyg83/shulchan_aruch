Or a healthy person from whom a kinyan was made. This view is not universally accepted. Choshen Mishpat 253 explains that whenever the wording is prospective, such as "he shall take," "he shall acquire," or "he shall possess," there is a dispute whether it helps for a healthy person even with a kinyan. Rambam and Ri Migash hold that this is merely a kinyan on words. The Rama must therefore mean a healthy person from whom a kinyan was made using language effective according to each authority, as Maggid Mishneh explains for Ri Migash, namely actual language of conveyance rather than "he shall take."
