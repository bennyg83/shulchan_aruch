Trusted. The borrower trusted them only to say that payment had been made. Here, however, they admit that no payment was made. This applies where the borrower's heirs assert with certainty that their father told them he never borrowed, as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch state in siman 75, seif 16, because their definite claim is treated as though it concerned facts they personally knew. Sema. The Shach notes that the Beit Yosef leaves unresolved whether the same applies where minors make such a claim.
