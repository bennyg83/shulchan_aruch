[Rama] And they collected on its basis, what they did is effective. Note: But in the reverse case, if judges acted in accordance with one authority and another judge later acted in accordance with the opposing authorities, the latter ruling is ineffective to overturn the judgment of the first judge. Responsa Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, simanim 11 and 12. See Ketzot HaChoshen, Kuntres HaTefisot, principle 4, siman 8.
