And even if he stipulated... See Beer HaGolah and the Rosh, who explains that Rava is a lone view against the others and that the various answers forced according to him are strained. Likewise the attempt to establish Avram Chozaah according to Rabbi Yishmael is not halachic. See there.
And if he wrote... As stated in Perek HaKosev regarding one who writes, "free from vow..." The general waiver does not necessarily extend to heirs, but if he explicitly writes that the heirs too shall be believed without oath, that stipulation is effective according to the Rif. See the Rosh.
