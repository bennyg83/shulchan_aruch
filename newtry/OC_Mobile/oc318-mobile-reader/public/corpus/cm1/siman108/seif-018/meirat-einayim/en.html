Only from inferior land. The Sages restored the rule to the Torah baseline, under which a lender collects from inferior land. They enacted collection from intermediate land only because of concern that otherwise people would be reluctant to lend, as the Tur and Mechaber explain in simanim 101 and 102. They were not concerned that lenders would cease lending merely because of the possibility that the borrower might die and collection would then be from inferior land. A person collecting damages, however, is entitled under Torah law to superior land. Therefore, from adult heirs as well he collects superior land, as the Tur and Mechaber state at the end of this siman.
