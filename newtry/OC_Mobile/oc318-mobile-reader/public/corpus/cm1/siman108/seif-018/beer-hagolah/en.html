There in Gittin, the Gemara's conclusion reads 'from the orphans,' whereas Rambam at the beginning of chapter 19 writes 'from the heirs.' The Maggid Mishneh explains that Rambam means not only sons but all heirs. This is somewhat evident from the Gemara. Tur, seif 36, writes likewise, as do Ran and Ba'al HaTerumot in Gate 4. They write that throughout the Talmud the word 'orphans' is not limited to children but includes all heirs, and they bring proofs for this.

Regarding Rif's ruling like Rava, Ba'al HaTerumot writes in Gate 4 that Rabbi Yonatan testified that Rif retracted and ruled like Rav Nachman. Raavad rules likewise and writes that the standard ketubah phrase 'from the best and choicest' does not help against orphans. Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 23, part 3, writes that even if he designated ordinary property as an apotiki, he does not collect from the orphans. Sema in the name of Darkei Moshe.
