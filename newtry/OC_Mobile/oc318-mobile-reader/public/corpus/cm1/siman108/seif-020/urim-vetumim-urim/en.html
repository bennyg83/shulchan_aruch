And there are embodied and medium-sized liquors and even a medium-sized G-d is due to him that the medium-sized species is being done with the hands and height of the orphans and is made with him medium-sized and simple:
He may retain it. The reason, as explained in Tumim, is that since he stands to collect intermediate-quality land and that very land is already in his hands, it is treated as though already collected. This applies specifically to land, which is subject to his lien. With movables, under Talmudic law heirs' movables were not encumbered, so mere possession does not make them collected during the debtor's life unless he expressly said he was holding them toward the debt.
For example, if he acquired it afterward. During the borrower's lifetime it was not yet treated as collected because the borrower did not own it then. After death, the creditor has no lien on the intermediate-quality category in a way that would make it automatically collected. See Tumim concerning Baal HaTerumot.
