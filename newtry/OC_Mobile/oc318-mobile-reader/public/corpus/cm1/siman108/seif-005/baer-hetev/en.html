Said. The Rosh writes in the first chapter of Bava Metzia that if the heirs have one witness who heard the father say that this promissory note had not been paid, they collect without an oath. Shach.
I found. According to this, it is possible that even a child born after the father's death must take an oath, and the Tur and Maggid Mishneh write so in the name of Maharam. See Imrei Noam, page 84, which writes that according to the Rambam we do not rule like Ri and no oath is necessary. The Shach says that this appears correct. The Sema writes that even where the father did not communicate the matter to them through another person, they need not swear. When the children are too young for him to instruct directly, a person does not ordinarily send them a temporary message through someone else; rather, he records the matter for later or entrusts it to their guardians.
