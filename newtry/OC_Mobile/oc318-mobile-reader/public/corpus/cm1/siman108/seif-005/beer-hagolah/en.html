Rambam likewise writes this there. The Maggid Mishneh explains that this is the oath of heirs mentioned in the Mishnah, chapter Kol HaNishba'in, Shevuot 45a. As for the Gemara's statement there, 'Now their father would have collected without an oath,' etc., that applies where the borrower did not claim, 'Swear to me.' But where he did make such a claim, since the father would have been required to swear, his heirs too must take the oath of heirs. Rav Hai and Rif likewise write this in responsa, and this is the primary view. As for why the Mishnah was not established as a case where the heirs collect from the borrower himself and he claims that they should swear, the Maggid Mishneh answers as explained at the end of seif 1 in the name of Beit Yosef.
