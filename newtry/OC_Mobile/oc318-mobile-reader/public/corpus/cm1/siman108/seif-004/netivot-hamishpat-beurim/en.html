Or if he trusted the managing partner. Tumim asks that siman 71 explains that a general clause of credibility is ineffective against heirs unless the creditor was expressly trusted even against the heirs. There is no difficulty. Regarding an oath, the Sages' requirement that the creditor swear against heirs is not merely because we advance for them any defense their father could have made. The oath based on the claim, Swear to me, according to many authorities is not a Mishnah oath, whereas the oath against heirs is a Mishnah oath, and in many respects the heirs' oath is more stringent. Therefore, even if the debtor granted the creditor credibility and exempted him from the oath based on the claim, Swear to me, he remains obligated in the heirs' oath unless expressly exempted even against heirs. Here, however, the creditor holds a promissory note and the heirs can avoid payment only because we advance for them the claim their father could have made. Their father could have claimed, I returned the funds, through a migo that he could have claimed they were lost through unavoidable accident. Once he trusted the managing partner, however, their father could no longer make that claim, and therefore we no longer advance it for the heirs. This is even more compelling according to the Sema's reasoning in seif katan 17, that the answer to Why is your document still in my possession is that the debtor could say, I relied on my migo. Meaning, he thought he would be believed to claim that he returned the funds through the migo that he could have claimed unavoidable accident. One cannot say that he relied on the possibility of falsely claiming unavoidable accident and therefore returned the money without taking back the document, since surely he would not plan to swear falsely. Rather, he relied on being believed on the true claim of repayment through that migo, as is also evident from Tumim. Once he granted credibility and therefore no longer had such a migo, he had nothing upon which to rely. The creditor can then say, Why is your document still in my possession? On what did you rely when you failed to take it back?
