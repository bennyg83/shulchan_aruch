Its law is as previously explained. The Sema cites siman 82, but the Tur's language is: 'If he does not wish to swear, we do not extract it from him.' The Rosh likewise writes this in Bava Batra, chapter Chezkat HaBatim, siman 18. The Beit Yosef and later authorities did not identify the Tur's source because they overlooked this passage of the Rosh, as I wrote in siman 82, seif katan 17. The Sema suggests that greater stringency was applied here because the claimant seeks to collect from heirs. This is not convincing; the primary ruling is as I wrote there in siman 82.
