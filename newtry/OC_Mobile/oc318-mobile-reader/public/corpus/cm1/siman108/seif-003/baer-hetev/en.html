Minors. Baal HaTerumot writes that there is no distinction whether the heirs are sons, brothers, or other heirs, and this remains so even if they have a guardian. Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe, siman 110, seif 2. Sema.
A minor. Even if another litigant is present, such as a widow on whose account beit din needed to receive testimony, testimony is nevertheless not accepted in the presence of the minor. Maharam Alshich 127. See also Maharashdam, Choshen Mishpat 201 and 202 and siman 37; Mahari ibn Lev I, 57 and 95; Raanach I, 94; Maharit I, 144; and Tumat Yesharim 152. Knesset HaGedolah.
His heirs. If the borrower granted credibility to the lender's heirs, the heirs of those heirs are also included. Radbaz II, 104. Knesset HaGedolah.
To swear. Rabbeinu Yeruham writes that even if the creditor, after the debtor's death, finds in the debtor's possession the very movable property that he had originally given him, he still does not collect without an oath. The Raavad and Ri write likewise. Darkei Moshe at the beginning of this siman. Sema.
