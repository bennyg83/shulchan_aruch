There, seif 5, from the Mishnah, "From the property of orphans she may collect only with an oath," Ketubot 87a. The reason is that perhaps, had their father been alive, he would have claimed, "Swear to me that it has not been paid." Therefore, although the orphans themselves do not make this claim, she must swear to them, because since they are orphans beit din advances the claim for them, as stated at the beginning of this siman in the name of Maggid Mishneh.
