Rather. Even here the cases are not entirely identical. Against the borrower himself, if he does not demand, "Swear to me," the lender's heirs collect without an oath. Against the borrower's heirs, however, even if they are adults and make no such demand, beit din advances the claim on their behalf. This is the Talmudic rule that orphans collecting from orphans do not collect except upon an oath. Sema. The Shach cites Baal HaIttur that this is specifically where the other heirs claim, "Our father told us that he paid." If they make no such claim, beit din does not advance it for them, because we do not advance a claim for one heir to the detriment of another heir.
