Rather... and if... This explains Kesubos 88a, which says the rule applies specifically where the heirs said.... The Mishnah was not simply established in an unqualified case because the heirs' own assertions matter. This is unlike Baal HaIttur, who held that beit din does not plead for heirs against other heirs.
