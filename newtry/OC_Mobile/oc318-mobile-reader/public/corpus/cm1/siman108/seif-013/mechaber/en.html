When do we say that the lender's heirs do not collect where the borrower died during the lender's lifetime? Where the document contains no trustworthiness clause. If it contains such a clause and the borrower made the lender trustworthy against himself and against his successors, the lender's heirs swear and collect, because their father would not have been required to swear either to the borrower or to his heirs. If the borrower also made the lender's children trustworthy, they collect without an oath. This applies even if the lender had become obligated to swear to the borrower in connection with a different claim.
