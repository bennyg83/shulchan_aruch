Or from purchasers. Responsa Pri Tevuah, siman 41, writes that the same oath requirement applies as between an earlier creditor and a later creditor. He adds that he is accustomed to say that the position of a later creditor holding a note is weaker than that of purchasers, based on the rule in Shulchan Aruch siman 104, seif 13. At the very least it is certain that a later creditor's rights are no stronger than those of purchasers. See there also regarding Beit Shmuel in Even HaEzer siman 96, citing She'erit Yosef, that heirs may say they do not desire their mother's oath, and see Pitchei Choshen there, seif katan 3, as well as Tumim here, seif katan 12, and Netivot HaMishpat.
