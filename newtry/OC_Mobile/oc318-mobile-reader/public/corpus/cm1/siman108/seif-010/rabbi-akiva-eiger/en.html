[Shulchan Aruch] We do not advance on their behalf the claim that the property was lost through unavoidable circumstances. Note: Maharach Shashon, siman 32, writes that perhaps only unavoidable loss is not advanced on their behalf, whereas theft is common and may be advanced. This also appears to be the opinion of Maharit, part 2, siman 120. See Shach, siman 121, seif katan 61.
