A receipt dated after his father's death, etc. The Tur cites Rabbi Yosef: Even if the document's due date had not yet arrived during their father's lifetime, if the receipt were genuine the debtor would have produced it while their father was still alive, meaning he would have shown it to beit din or to others to establish that the note in the lender's possession had already been paid. When the heirs become adults, if they can invalidate the receipt they may collect on their document; if not, their document is torn up.
