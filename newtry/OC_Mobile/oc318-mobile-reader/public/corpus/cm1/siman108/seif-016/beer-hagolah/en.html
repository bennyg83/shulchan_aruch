In the name of the psalmist, Hashem’s name is in the name of Hashem
The Maggid Mishneh there in the name of Rashba. He further writes in the name of Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash that even if the father did not claim this debt during his lifetime and even if its due date had not arrived, we are concerned for this receipt, because the borrower should have summoned the lender before beit din and compelled him to tear the document. Tur writes likewise in his name and says that his father Rosh agreed. When the orphans grow up, if they can invalidate the receipt, they collect on their document; otherwise the document is torn up.
