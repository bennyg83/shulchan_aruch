Because there is no... Bava Kamma 112.
And even if it can be validated... Without validation the document would be worthless even against the borrower himself, as stated at the end of the first chapter of Bava Metzia. Ramban similarly explains in Bava Basra that so long as the document was not formally produced it lacks legal effect.
And even through... Meaning, even where ordinarily a weak document would be accepted upon a certain form of confirmation, here it is still defective. Although a document can generally be validated outside the presence of the litigant, in this case a compromised document does not regain full status merely from signature verification. It requires witnesses who say, "We signed this document." Thus even if its signatures can be technically verified, it may still be treated as no valid document, as explained above in siman 63.
