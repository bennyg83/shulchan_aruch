A receipt dated after his father's death. See Shach, seif katan 31. There is a textual error in the phrase. The correct reading is that once the due date of the note has arrived, we require that the creditor have demanded payment during the debtor's lifetime. By contrast, while the debt is still within its term, the chazakah that a person does not repay before the due date remains effective even where the evidence against the note is weak, such as where no demand was made during the father's lifetime, and the note is therefore not torn up.
