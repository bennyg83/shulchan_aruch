Who says. The Shach writes that he does not know why the Mechaber presents this as the view of "some say." Since we rule that an oral loan is collectible from heirs even where the debtor had not first stood in judgment, the case must concern one of the three circumstances mentioned above, and then plainly no one disagrees. Its application to purchasers is likewise elementary from siman 39 and many other places, and in any event it does not belong specifically here in the laws of heirs. Perhaps the Mechaber recorded it here because of the latter clause imposing a penalty. The matter requires study. See also siman 388.
