(Mechaber, seif 8) And there is an authority who says, etc. Note: See siman 258, seif 8, and above, siman 56, seif 7, in Responsa Rosh, rule 56, ruling 8, concerning a woman who admitted tearing debt documents because she knew they had been paid. The Rosh rules that she must pay. Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot Zechiyah, chapter 6, halachah 8, derives from the Rosh a distinction between a deposit and a loan. But one who studies the Rosh's responsum will see that his point is that because the documents were not deposited with her, she is not believed. Therefore, even without witnesses who saw the documents after the death of her husband, the heirs' father, once she admits that she tore them after his death she is liable as a damager. See there. One must distinguish this from the rule below in siman 295:8 that where she could have taken the items she is believed. See also the language of Nimukei Yosef cited in Derishah, siman 255, seif 23, and Responsa Rosh, rule 14, ruling 5.
