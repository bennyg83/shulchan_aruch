One of three ways. Shaar Mishpat writes that where the law itself is uncertain as to whether the defendant would be believed to say "I paid," as in the cases below in simanim 157, seif 11; 158, seif 8; and 375, seif 9, the ability to collect from minor heirs depends upon the dispute among the authorities. According to Rambam and the Mechaber in siman 157, seif 11, the claimant must take a Scriptural-style oath while holding a sacred object and then collect. On that view one does not collect from minor heirs. According to Ramah, Ramban, and the Shach there, seif katan 16, the obligation in such cases is even more strongly presumed unpaid than an ordinary debt within its term. On that view one may likewise collect from minor heirs, just as one may collect a debt whose due date has not yet arrived.
