The Sema writes that in siman 93, seif 14, the Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule that if Shimon holds a promissory note against Levi for a partnership debt belonging to Shimon and Reuven, and Shimon says Levi paid, Levi is exempt by virtue of Shimon's admission. That case differs because the note was written in Shimon's name. Even though it expressly states that the debt belongs to the partnership, since the note is in his possession and written in his name, he is believed to exempt Levi, though Shimon must then pay Reuven his half. Here, by contrast, the promissory note was written in their father's name and was publicly established as their father's document. The person presently holding it never had the authority to return it. The Shach writes that this requires study. See siman 56 and siman 71, seif 23.
