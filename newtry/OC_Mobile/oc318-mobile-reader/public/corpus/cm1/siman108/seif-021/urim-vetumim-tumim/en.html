If the injured party, etc. The Sema asks that below, at the end of siman 419, the Mechaber appears to rule without qualification that even from adult heirs an injured party collects only inferior land. It seems possible to distinguish the cases. Here the heirs possess movables as well, and as explained above in siman 107 they have the right to satisfy the claim with inferior land instead of the movables. Since the strict rule where movables are available is that the claimant may collect the movables and need not accept land at all, we do not extend the heirs' leniency so far as to let adult heirs substitute inferior land. They must give superior land appropriate to a tort claim. Below in siman 419, by contrast, the Mechaber expressly speaks where the heirs have no movables. The claim must therefore be satisfied from land, and even adult heirs pay only from inferior land. This distinction reconciles the rulings.
