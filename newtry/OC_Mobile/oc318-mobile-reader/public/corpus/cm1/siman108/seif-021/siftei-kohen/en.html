If the injured party comes to collect his damages, etc. The Rambam, at the end of chapter 8 of the laws of property damage, appears to hold that even from adult heirs he collects only inferior land. The Maggid Mishneh writes this in explaining his view and says that leading authorities ruled so. The Ran at the beginning of HaNizakin appears to agree. The Baal Halachot Gedolot, at the beginning of Bava Kamma, p. 86d, states this explicitly, as does the Semag, p. 145. The Mechaber himself rules this way at the end of siman 419.
If they are adults, etc. The Rosh and Tur write this in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, and the Rosh agrees. The Rashba in his novellae to HaNizakin, the Maggid Mishneh at the end of chapter 8 of the laws of property damage, and the Ran at the beginning of HaNizakin also hold this view. The Rambam, however, appears to disagree, as I wrote in seif katan 39. The Ran says this is proved by the Gemara, apparently following the Rashba's explanation. Yet according to the other authorities, one can explain that when the Gemara concludes that 'orphans' includes adults and certainly minors, it states a uniform rule for all cases, not merely the preliminary case discussed there.
