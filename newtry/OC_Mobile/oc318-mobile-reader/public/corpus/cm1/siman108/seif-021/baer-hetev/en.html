Adults. The Rambam appears to hold that even from adult heirs a creditor collects only from inferior land. The Maggid Mishneh explains his view accordingly, and this also appears to be the view of the Rif and explicitly that of Baal Halachot Gedolot. The Mechaber himself rules this way at the end of siman 419, and the Semag likewise appears to follow the Rambam. Shach.
