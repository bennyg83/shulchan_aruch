g) The Maggid Mishneh explains it this way, and the Tur, seif 6, writes likewise in the name of the Ramah. The Rivash writes in responsum 176 that whatever a person undertakes as wages, even orally and without any act of acquisition, the employer must pay once the hired person performs the work, because a worker's hire becomes binding with the commencement of the work. This is explained in siman 333. See Terumat HaDeshen, simanim 323 and 324, regarding laws of hiring.
