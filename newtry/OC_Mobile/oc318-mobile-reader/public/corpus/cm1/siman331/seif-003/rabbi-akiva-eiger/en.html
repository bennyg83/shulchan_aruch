[Gloss] But something that is not. Note: See Orach Mishor, later edition, on Darchei Moshe, Yoreh Deah, siman 1, who writes that this applies specifically where we observe the practice two or three times. But where it is clearly known that the practice was established by agreement of the elders, even if the circumstance occurs only once in a jubilee, it is still proper to say that this is the established custom.
