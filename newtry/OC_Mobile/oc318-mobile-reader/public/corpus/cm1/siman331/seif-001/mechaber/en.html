An employer of workers must follow local custom. It contains three seifim.
If one hires workers and tells them to begin early and finish late, in a place where workers do not customarily do so, he cannot compel them even by adding to their wages, because he did not stipulate this when hiring them. Rama: If there is no local custom, or he said, 'I hire you according to Torah law,' they must leave home at sunrise and work until nightfall. On Friday they return early enough to fill a barrel of water, roast a small fish, and light a lamp. If the city has no custom but most residents came from a place with a custom, follow the custom of their place of origin. A worker who moved between places follows the custom of the place where he was hired. A practice is called a custom only when it is common and repeated many times, not when it occurred once or twice.
