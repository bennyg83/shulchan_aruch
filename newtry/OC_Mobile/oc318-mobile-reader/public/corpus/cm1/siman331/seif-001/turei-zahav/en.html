(Seif 1: Since he did not stipulate) The Gemara says that the employer increased their wage so that they would perform superior work. One might ask why we do not inspect the work itself, as the Gemara asks elsewhere. The answer is that here the employer personally promised an increase in order that they exert themselves to the utmost and not become lax. In the other case, the employer said nothing; one worker merely misrepresented to another that the employer would pay four. There, the worker can claim the higher amount only by proving that his work was actually worth more than ordinary labor.
