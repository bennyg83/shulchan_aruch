The wording of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 9 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, from the Mishnah at the beginning of Bava Metzia chapter 7, 83a.
. . . . . . . . . . .
P. for the rest of the workers
A name and a name called R
From Reish Lakish's statement, derived from the verse 'the sun rises, they gather in,' etc., followed by 'man goes forth to his work.' The Gemara concludes that this concerns hired laborers and alternatively one who said, 'hire workers for me according to Torah law,' on 83b. Rif and Rambam omit this discussion. The Beit Yosef explains that they may interpret Reish Lakish like Rabbenu Chananel, as Tosafot cite, in which case he merely teaches the workers' custom already included in the rule that everything follows local custom. Alternatively, even under Rashi's interpretation, the statement applies only to a new city or to the unusual case where he expressly hired them according to Torah law, and therefore they omitted it.
