Small. The Tur does not include the word "small," nor does Hagahot Ashri at the beginning of chapter HaPoalim when quoting this Yerushalmi. It appears that the Rama inserted it because in Orach Chaim, siman 256, the Tur describes the six blasts sounded before Shabbat and says that after the third blast they waited the time needed to roast a small fish. That is no proof here. There the discussion concerns householders who were in town every Friday and had already baked, cooked, and prepared their needs beforehand. Workers, by contrast, were in the field and came home close to Shabbat without yet having prepared their Shabbat needs, and therefore require more time than merely roasting a small fish. Proof is that the passage there gives no measure for filling a barrel of water, and says that the lamps were lit before roasting the small fish, while here they are lit afterward. Necessarily, these activities of roasting fish, drawing water, and lighting lamps were performed somewhat earlier by the workers than by other people. So writes the Sema. See also the Bach.
