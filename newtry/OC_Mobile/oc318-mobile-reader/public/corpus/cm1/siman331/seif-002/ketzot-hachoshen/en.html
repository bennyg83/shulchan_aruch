One who hires a worker. Rosh distinguishes an informal dependent servant from a fixed-term hired worker. A dependent who lives in the householder's home, has no fixed wage, and sometimes receives food, drink, or clothing can be required to return the clothing if he leaves voluntarily. A fixed-term worker who receives clothing and leaves during the term can likewise lose it, because the gift was made on the understanding that he remain. But if he leaves at the end of the agreed term, the employer knew that ordinary workers then depart. If the employer wished the clothing returned, he should have stipulated. The gift is therefore complete. Rif records the law of the dependent servant, but Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch omit it, and I do not know why.
