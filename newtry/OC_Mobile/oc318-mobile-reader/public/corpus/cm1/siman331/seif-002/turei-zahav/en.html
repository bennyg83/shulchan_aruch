(Seif 2: Everything follows local custom) The Tur records that the Ramah rules like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel: even if the employer expressly promised food, we do not infer that he intended an unlimited enhancement merely because the workers would receive ordinary meals in any event. This seems unlike the Rashba's responsum in siman 315, where a clause permitting a tenant to mortgage or sublet was read as adding permission to sublet even to a larger household, since otherwise the clause would add nothing. Here, however, Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel reasons that an unspecified enhancement of food has no definable limit. Had the employer intended an addition, he should have specified it. Therefore the clause refers only to the customary meal.
