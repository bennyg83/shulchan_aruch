Since he believed him, and so forth. Shach explains that even without the special credibility clause, the creditor can ordinarily collect the full amount from the debtor. If the creditors are partners, each collects his own share, but an oath may be required. The credibility clause removes that oath. What oath is this? The debtor admits he did not pay the present creditor. He says only that he paid the creditor's partner. If payment to the partner legally discharges the debt, then the present creditor's personal credibility should not help. The debtor says, "I agree that I did not pay you personally. I paid your partner, and that payment discharged me." If payment to the partner does not discharge, then even without credibility there is nothing for the present creditor to swear about, because the debtor himself admits he never paid him. The case must therefore be that the debtor says: "I gave the money to your partner, and he told me he transferred it to you." That gives the debtor a definite claim against the present creditor based on the partner's statement, much like the storekeeper-on-instructions structure where one party's report can generate a definite claim even though that reporting party has an interest. Without the credibility clause, the present creditor may therefore need to swear that his partner never delivered the payment to him. Once the debtor expressly granted the present creditor credibility, however, the debtor cannot use the partner's report to impose that oath. This also answers the argument cited from Maharanach. Maharanach inferred from the language "he has no standing" that if payment to the partner were independently proven, the debtor would be discharged. But the present discussion need not concern the legal effect of payment to the partner itself. The actual disputed point can be whether the partner subsequently transferred the money to the present creditor. In that dispute, ordinary credibility removes the creditor's oath. If witnesses actually prove the partner transferred the money to him, the result can of course be different.
