[Shulchan Aruch] Even within the fixed term. Note: The source is the Baal HaTerumot, who follows his own view that on behalf of heirs we advance even an uncommon claim. According to the authorities below, siman 108, however, who hold that we do not advance for heirs a claim of unavoidable accident, the same applies here. Although their father could have claimed, “I paid you within the term,” that is an uncommon claim, and we do not advance for the heirs the claim that it was paid within the term. See Gidulei Terumah and Knesset HaGedolah; Shach, siman 69, seif katan 26; and what I wrote in the margin below, siman 78.
