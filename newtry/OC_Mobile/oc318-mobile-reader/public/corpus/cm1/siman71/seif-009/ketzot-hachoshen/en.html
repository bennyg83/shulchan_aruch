The law follows the borrower. The Shach is uncertain whether, according to our ruling in siman 58 that where a creditor says a payment was applied to another debt the document is neither collected nor torn, but if the creditor seizes property we do not remove it, the same applies here. In my view the question exists even where there is a written receipt supported by witnesses. Perhaps seizure is ineffective because a receipt places the holder in the stronger evidentiary position and his hand is considered superior. The matter requires study.
