The law favors the borrower. The lender cannot say, "That payment was for another debt," even if he had been granted credibility equivalent to two witnesses, because no such credibility can overcome the debtor's own handwriting. Shach, correctly. The Shach remains uncertain whether the deed should actually be torn or merely left uncollected, like other cases involving a claim that payment was applied elsewhere.
