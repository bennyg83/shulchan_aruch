The law favors the borrower. The lender's claim that the money received was for a different debt does not avail him when he received the money in the presence of witnesses, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the beginning of siman 58. A handwritten receipt is equivalent to witnesses. In the responsum and in the Tur it is written that this is so unless the borrower granted the lender credibility equivalent to two witnesses. As for what the Tur and Mechaber write in seif 4, that meticulous people make a handwritten receipt and that it is effective even where the lender was granted credibility like two witnesses, as written there, that case concerns a receipt which explicitly states that on the particular promissory note in my possession against this borrower, for such and such sum and witnessed by such and such witnesses, I received such and such amount. Here, by contrast, the handwriting does not state that the payment was received on account of that particular debt. See the Perishah, and examine carefully.
