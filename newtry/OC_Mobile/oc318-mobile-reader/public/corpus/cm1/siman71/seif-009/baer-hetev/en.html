The borrower. A claim that the money was received for another debt is ineffective when the lender accepted the money before witnesses, as stated at the beginning of siman 58, and a signed writing is treated like witnesses. Sema. The Shach leaves unresolved whether, according to the opinion in siman 58, seif 2 that the document is not torn up and money seized by the lender is not taken from him, the same rule applies here or whether a signed writing is stronger.
