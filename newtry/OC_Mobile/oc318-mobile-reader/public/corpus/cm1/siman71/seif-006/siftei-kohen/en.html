Not to proclaim a general ban. The Sema explains that because a general ban does not curse or accuse the creditor directly, the creditor cannot prevent it. This applies where the creditor was expressly exempted from accepting a ban. But if he was merely exempted from an oath, the debtor may compel him to accept a general ban before beit din, since the exemption from the more severe oath does not necessarily include the lighter ban. This differs from a vow, which is included in common oath language or is not ordinarily imposed by beit din.
