Not to impose even a general ban. Certainly, if the debtor did not stipulate that the lender would be believed without any ban at all, the debtor can require him to accept a direct ban in his presence. But where he expressly exempted him from such a ban, he need not accept one directed personally against him. A general communal ban may nevertheless be declared. A contractual clause cannot prevent that. Why? We can say: if you did not wrong him, what do you care about a general ban? And if you did wrong him, return what belongs to him. Shach.
A vow is included. Likewise, if he exempted him from a vow, an oath is included. In ordinary monetary law the Sages did not institute a special formula in which a person must vow, "If I wronged you, produce is forbidden to me," in the way they instituted such vows regarding a ketubah. Therefore in ordinary usage "vow" and "oath" function as one category for this purpose. Nimukei Yosef in the name of Ritva in *HaKotev*.
