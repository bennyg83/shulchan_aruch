(From the words of the Tur.) At the end of Even HaEzer, siman 75, the Tur cites a question to his father, the Rosh, concerning Reuven who claimed before the marriage, etc. From there we learn a rule: if a borrower stipulated with the lender that the lender would have no special credibility, and later at the time of the kinyan the borrower made the kinyan without qualification in a place where documents are ordinarily written with credibility clauses, the law follows the borrower's stipulation and the lender does not receive such credibility.
