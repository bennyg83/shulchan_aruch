A written proclamation of curses authorizing a public ban is issued only for heirs who advance their father's claim, or for a guardian, when they say, 'We do not know who owes anything to our decedent.' If someone seeks to leave the synagogue while the ban is proclaimed, the beit din may ask why he is leaving. If he refuses to remain, he is not prevented; even if he leaves during the proclamation, the ban applies to him. {Rama: See above, siman 16, seif 3. Some say every person may have a ban imposed concerning testimony, and this is the practice.}
