They are given to every person. The Sema distinguishes: if he says with certainty that he has witnesses but does not know who they are, the general ban is imposed for any person. But if he does not know at all whether witnesses exist, it is imposed only for orphans. The Shach distinguishes differently. There the issue is imposing a ban upon anyone who possesses testimony, and that is done for every litigant. Here the issue is imposing a ban upon anyone who owes him money, and that is done only for orphans.
