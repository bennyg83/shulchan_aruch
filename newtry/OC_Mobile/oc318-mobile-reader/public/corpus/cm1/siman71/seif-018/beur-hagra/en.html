If the borrower died... Meaning that without a credibility clause the creditor could not collect at all, as stated in Shevuos 48a.
(Collected note) If the borrower died... Meaning that the borrower granted credibility even against his heirs, but did not extend that credibility to the lender's heirs. This follows the Mishnah there: "I have no vow or oath against you or your heirs..." but his heirs, etc. Mordechai, siman 320, uses this distinction to answer Tosafos' question in Bava Batra 158a. Here one cannot answer that she swore and then died, because why would she swear during her lifetime? Tosafos themselves make this point. Although they ultimately reject that answer, this is not conclusive, since the purpose there was merely to teach who has the stronger presumption, in a case where no oath was necessary. There can also be a case where specific movables were designated for her, as stated at the beginning of Af Al Pi and in Mordechai. (End of note.)
