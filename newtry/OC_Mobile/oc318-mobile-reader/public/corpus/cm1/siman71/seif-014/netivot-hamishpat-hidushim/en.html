Since it is not the custom. The Shach holds that even if the custom is widespread, a credibility clause arising from custom is ineffective if the borrower did not know of the custom. If the borrower did know the custom and instructed the scribe to write an ordinary document, his intent is that the document include all customary provisions. This is the primary ruling:
