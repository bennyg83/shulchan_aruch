There, seif 27, and the Mishnah there: one may stipulate with an unpaid bailee to be exempt, etc., Bava Metzia 94a. See what I wrote above in seif 2.
The Bach writes that specifically for trustworthiness equivalent to two witnesses we require that it be stipulated other than at the time of the loan and with a kinyan, as in siman 22 regarding accepting disqualified persons as judges. But ordinary trustworthiness creates an obligation even if not stipulated at the time of the loan and without a kinyan. He brings proofs for this.
