(Collected note) But he must... This requires study from seif 1, where even with an oral loan the creditor collects without an oath. Sefer HaTerumos writes that an ordinary credibility clause means without an oath, and the later authorities raise the same objection. The answer is that here the clause is understood only to defeat the evidentiary force of the document, leaving the claim like an ordinary oral loan. In seif 1, however, there is no comparable basis for limiting the credibility clause. (End of note.)
