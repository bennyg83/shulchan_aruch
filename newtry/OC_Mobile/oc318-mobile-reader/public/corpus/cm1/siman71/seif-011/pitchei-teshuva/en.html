Through witnesses, the credibility clause is void. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: See Responsa Avodat HaGershuni, siman 19, that this is not limited to actual witnesses. The same applies where the circumstances are such that by our own legal presumption we consider him to be lying, even though there are no literal witnesses. Once he is established as a liar, the credibility clause is void. End of quote. See what I write below in siman 91, seif 3 and 4.
