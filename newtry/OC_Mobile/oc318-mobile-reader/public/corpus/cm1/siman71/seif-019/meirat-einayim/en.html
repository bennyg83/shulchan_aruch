The same applies to an earlier creditor, etc. Meaning, the earlier creditor must also swear to a creditor who lent money afterward to the same borrower and who therefore also has a lien on the borrower's property. It is explained in the chapter Mi Shehayah Nasui that the later creditor may require the earlier creditor, when the latter seeks to collect under his prior lien, to swear that the debt is still owed to him and that he is not acting in collusion with the borrower.
