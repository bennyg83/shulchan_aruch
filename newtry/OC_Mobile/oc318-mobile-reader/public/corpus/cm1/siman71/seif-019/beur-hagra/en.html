(Supplement) The trust clause does not, etc. Baal HaMaor, Rosh, and other authorities challenge Rif and the Geonim, arguing that the Ketubot case applies only where the lien preceded the trust clause. They cite the Tosefta defining those who come by her authority as persons to whom she sold or gave the right. Rif and the Geonim, however, understand that one is generally not believed to prejudice third parties, even regarding property presently under his control. Abba Shaul’s rule concerning heirs supports this. Compare Bava Batra 175b and siman 47 concerning a debt that has been established.
