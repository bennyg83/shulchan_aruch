And if the borrower dies within the term, etc. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: The source is Baal HaTerumos, who follows his own view that we advance on behalf of heirs even an uncommon claim. According to the authorities below in siman 108, seif 4, however, who hold that we do not advance for heirs the claim of unavoidable accident, we likewise would not advance for them a claim that the debt was paid within its term. See Ginat Veradim and Knesset HaGedolah, and see Shach, siman 69, seif katan 26. End of quote.
