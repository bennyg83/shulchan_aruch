There is nothing to fear. See Baer Heitev through its statement that this applies specifically when the writing states that it relates to a particular document. See Shach below, seif 9, seif katan 24, who disagrees and holds that even with an ordinary handwritten acknowledgment the debtor cannot claim that the payment was for another debt. The Taz, Tumim, and Netivot HaMishpat agree. Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, at the end of siman 8, however, writes that Responsa Rif, Livorno edition, siman 278, shows that the Sema's position is correct as halachah, contrary to the Shach. See there, and see what I wrote above, siman 58, seif 2, seif katan 3.
