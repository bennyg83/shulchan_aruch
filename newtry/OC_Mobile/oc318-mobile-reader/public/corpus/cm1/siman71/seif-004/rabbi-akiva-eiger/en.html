[Shach, subparagraph 11] But he is believed to say, "I paid." Note: Had he also been granted credibility regarding payment, the document should simply have said that he is believed, without qualification. So writes the Rashba. Responsa Lechem Rav, siman 14, derives from this that an unqualified clause of credibility includes all matters, including a claim of a trust arrangement, interest, and payment. If, however, the document states that the creditor is believed so long as the document remains intact, meaning it has not been torn with a beit din tear and no payment has been recorded upon it, this reveals that the credibility was granted only regarding payment. See there. See also Bnei Yaakov on the Itur, maamar 1, page 26, and Responsa Maharam Alshakar, siman 13.
