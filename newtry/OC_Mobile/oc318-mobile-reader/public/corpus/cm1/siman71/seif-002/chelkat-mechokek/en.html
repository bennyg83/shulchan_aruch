But we do not compel him to marry off his daughters, etc. The wording is awkward. The intent is that he is not compelled regarding marriage support in the same manner that one is compelled to give tzedakah. He is, however, compelled to provide her with a husband. If he finds someone willing to marry her without clothing or property, he may do so. If no one will marry her without anything, he must give her the minimum support necessary for someone to marry her. It is nevertheless a mitzvah to give an appropriate dowry in order to match her with a suitable husband. I have already written about this above in siman 57.
