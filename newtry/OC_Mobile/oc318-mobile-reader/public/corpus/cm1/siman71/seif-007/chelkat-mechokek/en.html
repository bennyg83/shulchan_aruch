If he admits that the child is his. But if he does not admit it, although the woman claims with certainty that he is the father and he says only, "Perhaps others had relations with you," we apply the monetary rule: one who claims, "You owe me a maneh," and the other says, "I do not know," is exempt because the presumption of possession is stronger. Although she is believed regarding the child's personal status so as to validate the child, she is not believed to extract money. See Responsa Rivash, siman 41.
