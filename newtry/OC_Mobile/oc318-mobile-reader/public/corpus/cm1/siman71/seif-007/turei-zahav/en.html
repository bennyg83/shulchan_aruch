(Seif 14) “Except because they would see.” The Beit Yosef objects from siman 61, citing the Rosh, that anything customarily written is treated as though it had been written expressly. He answers that here there was no universal local custom to write this clause. This is difficult, for the Tur says, “as they are accustomed to write in all documents.” It appears to me that, regarding a credibility clause, the parties had to stipulate expressly. Other clauses were inserted by the scribes on their own as improvements to the document. Here the witnesses erred and assumed that this clause too was merely a standard documentary improvement that did not require the debtor’s consent.
