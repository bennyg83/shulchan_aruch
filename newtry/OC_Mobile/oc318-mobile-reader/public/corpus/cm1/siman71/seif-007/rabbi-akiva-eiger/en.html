[Shach, letter 38] And the Razah, the Rashba, and the Baal HaTerumot. Note: Chiddushei HaRitva, chapter HaKotev, writes concerning these words of the Razah that they are not clear. Yet Chiddushei HaRitva, chapter Kol HaNishba’in, writes concerning the same words of the Razah that this is a plausible reason. See Responsa Perach Mateh Aharon, part 1, siman 39, and Chacham Tzvi, siman 73.
