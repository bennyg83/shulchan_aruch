Effective. The Sema explains that this follows the rule in siman 41 regarding a borrower who says that the document fell from him and it is now in the possession of another person. Since the document is not in the lender's possession, the usual evidentiary presumption is absent. The Shach adds that this assumes the third person does not know whether the borrower actually owes the debt, as stated in the Rosh's responsum. If he is a neutral custodian who knows the status of the document, and is therefore believed as explained in siman 56, he may return the document to the lender, and the ne'emanut clause plainly benefits the lender.
