(Collected note) Some say... From the Mishnah's discussion of one who partially admits payment, it appears that of all the oaths listed there, only that case is singled out. The Ra'avad similarly infers that an ordinary credibility clause does not defeat the oath arising from one witness. The Rosh, siman 18, disagrees and says that the Mishnah mentioned the first case as an example and the same applies to all of them. Proof is that before Rabbi Shimon's dispute, the Mishnah interrupts the list of those oaths, including partial payment, etc., but concerning heirs it expressly states that the clause is ineffective. The same would apply to purchasers. According to the Rif, however, credibility is never effective against purchasers, so this explanation cannot be used. (End of note.)
