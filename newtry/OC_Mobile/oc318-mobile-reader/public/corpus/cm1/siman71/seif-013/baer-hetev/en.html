Established as a denier. The Tur writes that although one who has been established as a false denier concerning this money is not thereby established as such regarding another matter, in this case he nevertheless loses the benefit of the ne'emanut clause and may collect only upon an oath. The Shach cites Gidulei Terumah that if he had already been established as a denier in beit din before the ne'emanut was granted, judicial matters are public knowledge, and the other party knowingly accepted the risk.
