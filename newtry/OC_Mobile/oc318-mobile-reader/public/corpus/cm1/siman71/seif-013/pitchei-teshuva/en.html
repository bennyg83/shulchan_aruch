With witnesses, the credibility clause is void. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger, of blessed memory, he notes from Responsa Avodat HaGershuni, siman 19, that this is not limited to actual witnesses. The same applies whenever the circumstances are such that, according to law, we hold that the creditor is lying. Even without literal witnesses, he is established as one who falsely denied, and the credibility clause is void. See what I wrote below, siman 91, seif 3.
