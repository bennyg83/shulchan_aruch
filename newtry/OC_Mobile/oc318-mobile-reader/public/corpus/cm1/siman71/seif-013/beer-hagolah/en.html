The Tur in the name of a responsum of Rabbenu Kalonymus, recorded in Sefer HaTerumot in the name of Rabbi Meshullam bar Kalonymus. He explains that when he trusted him, it was because all Jews are presumed trustworthy. Once he became established as a denier, his trustworthiness was impaired. An Ashkenazic responsum brings proof from the case where a woman says, 'So-and-so the sage ruled this blood pure for me,' and it is found false, Ketubot 72b. There too she is believed by Torah law, yet once she lies she is no longer believed and loses her ketubah entirely.
