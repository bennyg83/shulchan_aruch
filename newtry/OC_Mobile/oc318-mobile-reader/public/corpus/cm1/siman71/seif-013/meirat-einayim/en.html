A denier concerning one matter, etc. The Tur writes: if he was established as a denier concerning another matter through witnesses, although we rule that one established as a denier concerning this money is not thereby established as a denier concerning a different matter, nevertheless here his special contractual credibility is canceled and he collects only with an oath. In Perishah, citing Baal HaTerumot, I explained that the presumed reason the borrower originally trusted him was because the lender was regarded as trustworthy in his eyes. Once he has been established as a liar in another matter, that trust has been impaired. From this I proved that the correct reading in the Tur and Mechaber is, 'a denier concerning another matter.' Meaning, even if he was not shown false in any aspect of this particular document but only elsewhere, he still cannot collect without an oath. This is also evident below in siman 79; see the Tur and Mechaber there, seif 5.
