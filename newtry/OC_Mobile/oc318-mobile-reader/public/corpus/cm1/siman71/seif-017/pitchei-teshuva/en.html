Unless he explicitly exempted him, etc. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: Responsa Maharach Shabbetai, siman 3, writes in the name of Responsa Maharshdam and Maharashach that whenever a document states 'credibility, etc.,' the word 'etc.' includes the full standard credibility clause, namely credibility for the lender and his heirs against the borrower and his heirs, as scribes commonly specify in documents, but here the scribe abbreviated and included it in the word 'etc.' End of quote.
