Unless. With a ketubah we are more concerned that the husband may have left money or valuables for payment, and therefore even an express exemption does not necessarily help against the heirs. With an ordinary loan, however, an express exemption regarding the heirs is effective. Even if the heirs claim, "We ourselves paid you," they are believed where the exemption applies. Likewise, if the borrower granted credibility to the lender and his heirs, the clause is effective even if the heirs say, "We paid you." Shach, unlike the Sema:
