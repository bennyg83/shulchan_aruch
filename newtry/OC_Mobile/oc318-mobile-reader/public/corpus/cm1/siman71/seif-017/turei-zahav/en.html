(In the name of Maharshal in the Tur) The witnesses are believed to say, “We signed only for this purpose,” even when their handwriting is authenticated from elsewhere, as the Tur rules in siman 43, seif 8, unlike the Ramban cited there and unlike Rabbi Yitzhak and the Rosh cited by the Tur. It therefore requires study why the Tur presents the ruling here anonymously in the name of Sefer HaTerumot as though no one disputes it. The matter can be reconciled.
