He does not collect from orphans. Even if the debtor had granted the lender credibility equivalent to two witnesses. See below, siman 110. If a deed contains a credibility clause and the lender gave it to an agent to collect, and the borrower says he paid the agent, the agent must swear concerning his conduct. But this does not destroy the lender's contractual credibility. Even if the agent says, "I received the payment and handed it to the lender," while the lender says, "That never happened," the lender is believed without an oath because the borrower granted him special credibility. All the more can the lender say the payment was applied to another debt. The agent, however, must swear regarding his own claim, whether he denies receiving payment or says he received and delivered it to the lender in circumstances where he was not negligent in allowing payment without obtaining a receipt, for example because the borrower instructed him to do so. This is from Responsa Rosh, rule 71, siman 5, cited by the Shach.
