If a document contains a credibility clause and was given to an agent for collection, and the borrower claims that he paid the agent but did not take the document back, the agent must swear even if the document contains credibility in favor of the lender's representatives. The lender may then collect the debt without swearing if the agent did not transfer the payment to him.
