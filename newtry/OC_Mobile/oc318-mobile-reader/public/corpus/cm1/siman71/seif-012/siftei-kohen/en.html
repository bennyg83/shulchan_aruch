Even though the note contains credibility as two witnesses. The Rambam and Mechaber in siman 110 imply this expressly. The Rosh ruled concerning a note in which Reuven empowered himself and anyone acting through him, and later authorized Yehudah to collect. If the borrower claims that he repaid Yehudah, Reuven's credibility does not exempt Yehudah from an oath. Reuven himself, however, remains exempt, even though Yehudah acts through him. If the borrower claims only that he repaid Reuven or sent payment through another person, he is not believed. Similarly, if he says that he paid Yehudah after Yehudah became Reuven's authorized agent, Reuven may collect without an oath, while Yehudah must swear.
