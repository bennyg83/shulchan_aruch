He stipulated. Kesubos, Mishnah 86b, and Shevuos 42a. The language indicates that even an unstated general clause works, like "You are trusted by me," Sanhedrin 24a.
(Collected note) Even... This is like saying, "Do not repay me except before witnesses," which works even orally, as the sugya there shows. Likewise the Mishnah, "You have a maneh of mine... exempt... Do not give it..." Certainly a credibility stipulation is effective, since it can even prevail against witnesses, unlike the former rule, as stated in siman 70, seif 4. (End of note.)
