(Shach, seif katan 39) This too is difficult, that a loan should be stronger, etc. Note: There is no difficulty. A sale of land contradicts an earlier sale, but it does not contradict an earlier lien. Both can coexist: the land can remain encumbered to Reuven for his debt and simultaneously be sold to Shimon until Reuven actually forecloses on it. Since the later sale is not a retraction of the earlier lien, once the document is delivered to the lender after the sale we can apply the principle that the witnesses' signatures acquire the right for him retroactively.
