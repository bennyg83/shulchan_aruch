Collection from purchasers, lest, etc. In the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger to Shulchan Aruch it is written: If the witnesses orally testify that the borrower told them, 'Write and sign,' the loan has the status of a documented loan. If the borrower admits that he told them to write and sign, and the witnesses have died, he is believed to say, 'I repaid,' by virtue of a migo that he could have said that he never instructed the witnesses to write and sign. If the witnesses are alive, however, he is not believed. See Maharach Shabbetai, siman 1. End of quote.
