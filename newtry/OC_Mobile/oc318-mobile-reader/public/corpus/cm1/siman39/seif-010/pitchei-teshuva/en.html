A document for the borrower, etc. Dagul Merevavah writes: A mortgage document, however, may not be written unless the lender is present. See siman 65, end of seif 1, in the words of the Mechaber. Nevertheless, according to what I wrote there in the margin, which will be cited there in seif katan 2, one may indeed write a mortgage document for the borrower even without the lender present. End of quote.
