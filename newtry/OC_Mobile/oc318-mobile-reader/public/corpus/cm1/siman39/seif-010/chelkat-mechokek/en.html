And he healed her. Meaning, even if he healed her before the husband learned of the defect, and in a comparable case involving vows she would be betrothed, nevertheless regarding bodily defects she is not betrothed, because she remains repugnant in his eyes when he remembers that she had those defects. However, if at the time of kiddushin the defects that had previously been present were already healed, it appears that she is betrothed, because he did not stipulate, 'on condition that you never had defects.' His concern is only that she not presently have a defect. If the defect existed at the time of kiddushin and was healed only afterward, she nevertheless remains repugnant in his eyes.
