If there is. Bava Metzia there, the rule concerning one who pleads, etc., according to the Geonim and the Rif's explanation above.
(Collected note) If there is, etc. As stated there, "one who pleads after an act of beit din, etc." The Geonim explain this as a final judgment, and so in the Yerushalmi at the end of Sheviit, "all acts of beit din" means judicial decrees. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) If there is, etc. From what is stated in Gittin chapter 9: every authorization for seizure, etc. An authorization for seizure means a final judgment. Mordechai, beginning of Bava Metzia, siman 318. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) And if this one, etc. See Knesset HaGedolah, where there are authorities who disagree. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) And the judges, etc. Specifically a kinyan is ordinarily intended for writing, unlike a final judgment, which is not ordinarily written. Therefore, if he did not say to them, etc. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) And the judges, etc.; and if, etc. As above in seif 7, where they do not write until they send, etc. The reason is as the Sema writes there, seif katan 19: he does not contemplate that they will write it for him. See there. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) And the judges, etc.; and if not, etc. As above in seif 9, that when he left and returned they do not write. Hagahot Maimoniyot holds that this is specifically when they did not tell the defendant. (End of collected note.)
(Collected note) And if they forgot, etc. For even regarding witnesses we say there is a presumption that witnesses do not, etc. (End of collected note.)
And there are. Those who explain "one who pleads, etc." like Rashi. The Rif above explains as the Rama writes in the preceding opinion.
(Collected note) And there are those who disagree, etc. From the Tosefta at the beginning of Bava Metzia: one who finds judicial decrees and prozbul documents shall return them. The reason is that the borrower can say "I repaid," as stated there, siman 321. (End of collected note.)
