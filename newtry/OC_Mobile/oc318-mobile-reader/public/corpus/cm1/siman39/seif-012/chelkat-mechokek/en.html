On condition that there shall not be defects in her. The Bach already writes that the Beit Yosef had a corrupt text, and that the correct Tur text is, "on condition that there shall be no defects in you." Nevertheless, even though the precise case is not mentioned in the Tur, there is room for doubt. Since he altered his wording and said, "that there shall not be," rather than, "that there are not," perhaps he means that even if defects exist now he objects only if they remain at the time of marriage. This is certainly so if he expressly explains that intention. At minimum the kiddushin should be treated as doubtful because the wording admits that interpretation. See the later supplement.
