For example, they wrote, "and one of us was absent," or, "so and so said to us," or the document states that the proceeding took place before a recognized expert who would not have erred by treating two judges as a beit din. Thus states the Tur. Sema. The Shach writes that this assumes that even today there is such an expert for this purpose. The concern that perhaps the judges erred applies only where merely two signatures appear. Where it is certainly an act of beit din, however, we do not presume that beit din erred, as stated in siman 46, seif 9, and at the beginning of siman 255. See also Responsa Mahari ben Lev, part 1, rule 20, siman 106.
