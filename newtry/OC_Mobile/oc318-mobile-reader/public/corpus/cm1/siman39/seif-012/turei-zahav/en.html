(In the name of Maharshal on the Tur, Even HaEzer.) The phrase “perhaps he does not want, etc.” is explained in Rosh responsa, rule 65, and in the rulings cited by the Beit Yosef in seif 7: a written obligation depreciates a person’s property, and people do not want documents written against them. This applies even to a document of sale rather than a loan, as the Tur writes in siman 243.
