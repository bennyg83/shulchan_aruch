A borrower who comes to beit din holding a promissory note and seeks to compel the lender to lend him the money until the term stated in the document cannot compel him, even if it is established that the document was written with the lender's consent, because the lender may retract. However, if he agreed to lend against collateral and took possession of the collateral, he may no longer retract. The borrower pays the scribe's fee, even when the document is an iska agreement. If the lender lost the document and a replacement must be written, the lender pays the fee.
