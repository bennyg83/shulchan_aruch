(See there: they write a document for the borrower, etc., through “because it is an injustice, etc.”) That reason is unrelated. The cited case concerns witnesses testifying that the borrower instructed them to deliver the document to the lender as a trust document. The true reason here is that he explicitly said, “Give it into my hand.” If he merely said, “Give it to the lender,” they deliver it. Baal HaTerumot, gate 52, states this reason explicitly in the name of Rabbi Natan.
