[Rama] And he took possession of the collateral. Note: Responsa Rashba writes similarly regarding a mortgage: if the lender entered upon the mortgaged property and acquired the land, he cannot retract, because through taking possession of the land he already became obligated to provide the money, for it is comparable to a sale in which the seller is not pressing for the money. But if he has not yet acquired the land, he has incurred no obligation. If reliance is placed on the mortgage document, land is not acquired by a document alone, but by money together with a document. If reliance is placed on the kinyan recorded in the mortgage document, some hold that even a document accompanied by a kinyan does not acquire without money. End quote. See below, siman 191, seif 1.
