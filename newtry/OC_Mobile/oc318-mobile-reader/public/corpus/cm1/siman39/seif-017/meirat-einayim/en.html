A borrower came to beit din with a debt document in his possession, etc. The Tur gives the case: Reuven undertook to lend Shimon one maneh and told him, "Go to the scribe and witnesses and have them write and sign that you owe me one maneh. Deliver the document to me and I will lend you the money." Shimon did so and brought him the document, but Reuven then retracted. Ramban writes that Reuven is obligated to lend him. The Tur disagrees and concludes that writing the document did not obligate the lender to advance the money. The Ir Shushan writes that in any event the lender should reimburse the borrower for the scribe's fee under the laws of causative damage. That is reasonable.
