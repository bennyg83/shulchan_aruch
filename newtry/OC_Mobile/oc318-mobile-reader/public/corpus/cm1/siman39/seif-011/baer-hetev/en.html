An admission. The same applies to a loan document, because with a loan too the witnesses may not write until the debtor tells them, "Write and sign." Shach.
Purchasers. But collection may be made from the person who made the admission himself, because we do not suspect the witnesses of fabricating the underlying event. Thus the Sema. The Shach objects: we rule "from their mouths and not from their writing," as the Mechaber writes in siman 28, seif 11. If so, this writing should not be testimony at all. The matter requires study. One must say that the Shach understands that since the document was written without the debtor's authorization it is not a full legal document and therefore is treated as a written memorial of oral testimony, as he writes above in seif katan 1. Otherwise, the Mechaber rules in siman 28, seif 12, that monetary judgments may indeed be based on testimony contained in a valid document.
