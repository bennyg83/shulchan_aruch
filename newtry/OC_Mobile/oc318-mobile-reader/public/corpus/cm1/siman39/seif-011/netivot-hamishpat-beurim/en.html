Regarding foreclosure from purchasers. Shach asks that if the writing was unauthorized enough to lack purchaser-enforcement force, it should also be testimony from writing even against the borrower himself. There is no difficulty. To avoid the defect of testimony from writing, it is enough that the borrower authorized the witnesses to write and sign. For purchaser collection, however, he must also have authorized delivery. The concern is therefore that he may have said only, 'Write and sign,' and the witnesses mistakenly assumed that was enough to create full collection rights. We do not suspect that they wrote without any authorization at all, because that would make them direct liars about the central event.
