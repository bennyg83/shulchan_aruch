A. The writers. As a millimeter there:
(Anthology) With regard to collection from, etc. According to the first opinion in the gloss to seif 3, even collection from the debtor himself is excluded, because the document is testimony from writing and there is no valid oral testimony at all. The first opinion there is primary, and Shach rules accordingly.
(Anthology) With regard to collection from, etc. The same applies to the plea, 'I repaid.' Shach, seif katan 33. He cannot, however, deny the transaction entirely and say that it never occurred. Hagahot Ashri there. This follows the latter opinion in the gloss to seif 3 and likewise seif 12. Therefore it has the law of a document. See seif 6.
