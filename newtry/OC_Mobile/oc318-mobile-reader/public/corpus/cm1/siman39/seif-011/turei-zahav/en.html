(In the name of Mahari in the Tur: we rule that omission of responsibility is a scribal error.) According to the view that property liens are biblical, why is the rationale of scribal error needed? Even an oral loan should by strict law be collectible from encumbered property, and the Sages restricted this only to protect purchasers. A written document provides publicity, so the restriction should not apply. One may answer that ordinary oral testimony lacks sufficient publicity; therefore one might have thought the Sages required express writing of responsibility, and absent such wording no publicity exists. The rule of scribal error teaches otherwise.
