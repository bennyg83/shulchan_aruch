And he went to a physician. At first glance this means that he sought treatment before the woman learned of the defect, because once she became aware of it the kiddushin would already have been void, as explained above in seif 2. It is possible, however, that a woman objects only to a defect that cannot be cured. This differs from the man, who may object even to a comparatively minor matter and, as soon as he learns of it, say that he cannot live with a woman subject to vows and does not wish her to seek annulment from a Sage.
