Even if they are deposited items. All the more so, the witnesses may not write concerning movable property that was borrowed, for which the borrower is liable, or money lent, and similar obligations. This is the implication of all the authorities, contrary to the Tur, who writes that where one accepted liability for movable property the witnesses may write a document. Lechem Mishneh, chapter 7 of Hilchot To'en, and the Bach already challenge the Tur. See there.
