He admitted regarding land. Even though no substantive debt arises from the admission itself, if the witnesses write it without authorization there is still a problem of testimony through writing according to Rashi and Baal HaMaor, because written testimony must be produced with the knowledge of the person obligated by it. Ran answers that with an admission concerning land, writing causes no ordinary harm or disadvantage to the one who admitted. We therefore presume that he consents to the writing, as though he expressly instructed them to prepare a document. Rashba gives the same rationale. It follows that if he explicitly protests and tells them not to write his admission concerning land, they may not write it. If they do so anyway, the writing is testimony through writing without his authorization and does not have the legal status of testimony.
