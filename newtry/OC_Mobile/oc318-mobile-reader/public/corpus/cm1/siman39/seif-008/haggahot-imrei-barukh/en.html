(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 13) Once he said, "give," their testimony was complete, etc. Note: In my humble opinion these words are not correct according to the authorities who explain that a written document is not disqualified as testimony from writing because it was written with the debtor's authorization. The witnesses act as his agents, as though he wrote it himself. The Ran's discussion there is also according to Rabbi Elazar, who holds that delivery witnesses effect the document. According to the opinion that signing witnesses effect it, the signatures themselves create the document.
