(See the Beit Yosef, beginning “If he did not acquire through a kinyan, etc.” through “some rule like Mar bar Rav Ashi, etc.”) Tosafot in Sanhedrin cite Rivan in Rashi’s name as ruling like Mar bar Rav Ashi regarding an admission, but this is a scribal error. Rashi in Hullin explicitly rules that the halachah does not follow Mar bar Rav Ashi regarding an admission, and Tosafot there cite Rashi that way. The Rosh in Sanhedrin and the Nimukei Yosef in Hullin likewise report Rashi’s view. The Rosh’s proof from the chapter HaKotev establishes this clearly.
