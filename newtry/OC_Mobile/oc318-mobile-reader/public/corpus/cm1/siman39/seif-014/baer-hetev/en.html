He acquired. The Shach writes that this requires study. One who carefully examines the Baal HaTerumot will see that he discusses a person who obligated himself generally to two people, said nothing to the witnesses, and the witnesses delivered the document to one of them. Even in that case the Baal HaTerumot remained uncertain. But if the obligor himself delivered it to one of them, it appears that only the recipient acquired rights through it. By what basis would the other acquire? Perhaps the Tur likewise means that he delivered it to one of them in a manner intended to confer the other person's share as well. This requires study.
