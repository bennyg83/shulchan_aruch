[Shach, letter 41] For one may acquire a benefit for a person in his absence. Note: At first glance this appears to depend upon the two opinions below, siman 243:11. According to the first opinion there, that he may retract because a person cannot transfer something that has not yet come into existence, as explained in the Tur there, it would not help here for the borrower to tell the witnesses to acquire on behalf of the creditor so as to remove the problem of an antedated document. The witnesses cannot acquire for the creditor, because at the time the borrower tells them to do so the relevant right has not yet come into existence. This requires study.
