And the obligor delivered the document. Shach leaves this rule requiring study. According to Mechaber, where a kinyan already gave the recipient a retroactive right but the obligor said, 'Give the document to me,' his language is best understood as conditioning final effect on the instrument leaving his hands. Once he himself removes it from his possession and transfers it to the beneficiary, even through an intermediary, the condition has been fulfilled. But in a non-kinyan document where acquisition occurs only through receipt of the document itself, every beneficiary who must acquire through the deed must actually receive it. Thus a single document intended to transfer to two recipients must reach each according to ordinary acquisition law.
