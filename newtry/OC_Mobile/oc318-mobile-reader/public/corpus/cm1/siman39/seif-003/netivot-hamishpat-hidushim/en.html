The date of the kinyan. This refers to a document that states only part of the usual responsibility clause and does not state that after acquired property is included, so omission of that clause cannot be treated as a scribal error. If the date of the kinyan is not recorded, there is concern that the loan occurred in Nisan, the kinyan was made in Iyar, and the borrower sold property in Tishrei, while the witnesses postdated the document and wrote a date in Sivan. Had it been dated from Nisan, it would have been evident that the property was acquired only after the loan and therefore could not be collected, since this is after acquired property.
