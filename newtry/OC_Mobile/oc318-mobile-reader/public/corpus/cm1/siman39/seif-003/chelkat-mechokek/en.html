If she is an important woman. Since we rule that she is doubtfully betrothed, it would seem that we should rule that she is definitely betrothed, because one cannot say that the man does not wish this because he thereby becomes prohibited to her relatives, since in any event she requires a get because of the doubt. One must therefore say that not everyone is learned in the law. A person who does not know the law does not effect definite kiddushin, because he assumes that she will not require a get. Since the law does not distinguish between one who knows the law and one who does not, in all such cases the kiddushin are not definite.
