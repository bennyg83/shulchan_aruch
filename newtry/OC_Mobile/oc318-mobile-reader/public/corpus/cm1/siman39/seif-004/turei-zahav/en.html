(There: even if he did not say, “You are my witnesses.”) The Sema’s explanation in seif katan 19 is unsatisfactory, for saying “You are my witnesses” would be less effective, not more, since it does not appoint them as a beit din, yet the Sema treats the case as an a fortiori inference. The Tur means only to contrast an admission before ordinary witnesses, where one must say “You are my witnesses” to prevent the plea, “I was jesting with you,” as stated in siman 32. Before a beit din no such formula is needed, because one cannot plead jesting before a court.
