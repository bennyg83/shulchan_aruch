Only when they made the kinyan before the lender. In the Perishah and Derishah I proved that the Tur agrees, and that he alluded to this in two places in this siman, seifim 18 and 21. See what I write below in the Sema, seifim ketanim 35 and 36.
And some disagree. Nimukei Yosef writes there that although an ordinary kinyan is understood as intended for writing, it is not understood as authorizing the insertion of a warranty clause and other strengthening clauses. The principle that omission of warranty is a scribal error applies only when they expressly stipulated it. Nimukei Yosef in Bava Metzia, however, does not write this. One must therefore say that what he writes in chapter Get Pashut refers specifically to a guarantor, who derives no benefit from the loan. Regarding a borrower or seller, who received benefit, we assume their undertaking included all ordinary strengthening clauses. See Hagahot Darkei Moshe and below, siman 61, seif 5.
