The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Rif rules likewise in the name of Rav Hai, like Mar bar Rav Ashi, etc. I saw the same in Tosafot there, in the name of Rivan in the name of Rashi, in the name of Rabbeinu Gershom, and that Rabbeinu Chananel rules likewise.
z) The Kesef Mishneh writes that Rabbeinu explains "until they establish a place" to mean that the judges are sitting in their fixed place. Even if the debtor did not gather them himself, if they summoned him to beit din they may write the admission.
ch) From the Mishnah, "provided they know the parties," Bava Batra 167a.
