[Shach, letter 15] The language of the Rashba: and the law indeed dictates so, etc. Note: This is difficult to me, for we rule that a receipt may be written, and because of this very concern he will have to obtain a receipt. Moreover, there is no proof in any event, for perhaps the rule is that the witnesses are not permitted to write unless all of them write together at one session, as where one designated ten witnesses, and as where one sells a field before witnesses and all of them must write. The reason may be precisely that with respect to purchasers, a receipt is not available. This requires study.
