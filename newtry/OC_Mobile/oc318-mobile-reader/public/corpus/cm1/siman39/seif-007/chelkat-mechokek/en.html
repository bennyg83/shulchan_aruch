Because the daughters do not go to the bathhouse. Meaning, all the more so they do not ordinarily go outside at all except for the bathhouse. The case concerns one who betrothed her through an agent and did not see her at the time the kiddushin were given. Therefore, even a visible defect nullifies the kiddushin. See Mordechai, who writes that during erusin it is possible that a man does not know even of a visible defect, and examination in the bathhouse is not relevant before erusin. The entire Mishnah concerns nissuin. See there and the Bach.
