And they did not send for him. See Baer Heitev, who explains that this means either that the judges were not a permanently constituted beit din or that they did not send for him. His intention is that both conditions are required: the court must be permanently constituted and they must also have summoned him. Only then may they write the document, as the Shach writes in seif katan 19. Therefore, if either one of these two elements is lacking, it does not suffice until both are present. Misgeret HaShulchan, Ateret Tzvi, and Maamar Kaddishin erred on this point and understood that the Sema does not require both conditions and therefore disagrees with the Shach and Taz. This is incorrect. Tiferet Yaakov, seif katan 12, has already noted this. See there.
