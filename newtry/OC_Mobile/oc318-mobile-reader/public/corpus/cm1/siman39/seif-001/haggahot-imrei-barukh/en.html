(Shach, seif katan 2) He collects from encumbered property, etc. Note: In the novellae of Rashbam to Bava Kamma 104b, cited in Shitah Mekubetzet, Rabbi Shimon rules like the view that liens are not Torah law. He concludes, however, that we rule that liens are Torah law. In one answer there he writes that Rabban Gamliel, who was a student of Rav and Shmuel, follows the position of his teachers that liens are not Torah law. See further there in Shitah Mekubetzet.
