And the same applies to vows between him and her. This does not mean every vow classified as between husband and wife in Yoreh Deah 234, just as not every form of personal affliction is necessarily a material objection. Rather, it refers to serious matters, such as a vow of the kind "I am removed from the Jews," and similar matters to which most people object. So writes the Ran.
