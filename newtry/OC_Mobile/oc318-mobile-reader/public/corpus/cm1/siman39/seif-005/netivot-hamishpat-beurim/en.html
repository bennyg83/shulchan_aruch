When he summoned others for his kinyan. Even if he did not summon anyone, unrelated persons may not simply write him a deed, because otherwise any two people could fabricate one. The reference to having summoned others is relevant to whether the absence of a document weakens a claim of nonpayment during the term. If he had expressly summoned witnesses for the kinyan and still no deed appears, that circumstance can weaken the claim and support a plea of payment even within the term. If he never summoned witnesses who were entitled to write, the absence of a deed proves nothing.
