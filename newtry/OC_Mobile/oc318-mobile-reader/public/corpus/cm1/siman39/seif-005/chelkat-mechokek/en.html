Bad odor and perspiration. The Tur does not have the words "bad odor"; the Mechaber follows Rambam. Regarding a woman who urinates in bed, or who is afflicted with leprosy, the Beit Yosef writes that it is obvious that these are defects. Darkei Moshe comments on the first that although it is obvious to the Beit Yosef, he himself has a substantial doubt, and discusses the matter at length. He concludes that inability to share a bed is certainly a major defect, no less serious than a vow between husband and wife. He then adds that perhaps only a vow concerning an obligation she owes him, such as a vow that deprives him of marital relations, can void the kiddushin. The matter requires study.
