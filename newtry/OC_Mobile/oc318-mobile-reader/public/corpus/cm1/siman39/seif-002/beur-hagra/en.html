Until he says. B.B.:
And give it to him. As stated in Ketubot there: 'and give it to him,' etc.
(Anthology) And some say, etc. This is inferred from Sanhedrin there, which states only 'write,' and is limited specifically to a marriage contract and similar documents. The first opinion infers from the phrase 'give it to him,' rather than 'give it to her.' Rashi guarded against this inference and wrote that the case was, for example, a gift of land. See the Ran there. Regarding the earlier wording, 'write, sign, and give it to him,' however, this second opinion does not disagree, for Sanhedrin there likewise states this concerning that acknowledgment document.
