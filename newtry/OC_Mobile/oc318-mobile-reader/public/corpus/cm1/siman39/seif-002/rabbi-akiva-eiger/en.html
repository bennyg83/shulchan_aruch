[Shulchan Aruch] And some say that they do not require it. Note: This is the view of the Raavad and those who follow him, that only with a ketubah and a deed of gift must the witnesses consult the obligor before writing. The Beit Yosef writes that Responsa HaRosh implies that he agrees with the Rambam that in every case they must consult him, and he wonders why the Tur did not mention this. In my humble opinion, the difficulty is even greater, for at least everyone agrees that with a deed of gift they must consult him, and this is the subject of an extended sugya. The Tur should therefore at least have brought this law regarding gifts in siman 243. This requires study.
