f) Rambam, chapter 11 of Hilkhot Malveh VeLoveh, from the conclusion of the Gemara: the halachah is that he must consult him, Ketubot 55a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rabbeinu explains this as applying even to an admission.
g) The Raavad there in his glosses, and the Maggid Mishneh in the name of Baal HaIttur and other commentators, explain that the passage in Ketubot concerns one who writes a ketubah for his wife or gives a gift, because in those cases the document itself effects the transfer. Here, however, the document merely reveals the matter. The Beit Yosef writes that this also appears from the Rosh, rule 58.
