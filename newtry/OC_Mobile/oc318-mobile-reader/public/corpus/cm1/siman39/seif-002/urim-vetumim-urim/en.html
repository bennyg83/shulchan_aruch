He must consult him again. See Tumim. If the debtor retracts so that the witnesses may no longer deliver the deed to the creditor, nevertheless if the deed remains in the witnesses' hands the court may adjudicate on its basis. It is not invalid written testimony, because the debtor expressly told them to write. I also conclude that witnesses should not write before they are told to do so on condition that they will later ask the debtor whether to deliver it. See below, seif 13.
Some say they need not consult him again. The same applies if the creditor tells the witnesses in the debtor's presence, “Write,” and the debtor remains silent. His silence is treated as agreement to the creditor's instruction and they may write. Baal HaIttur, cited by Shach.
