But if they made a kinyan from him. Dagul Merevavah writes: If a kinyan was made before one witness, or even in the presence of the opposing party without any witnesses at all, and he admits that he accepted the kinyan but now refuses to perform and seeks to retract, does the kinyan take effect even though it was not made before two witnesses? See the Shach, siman 81, seif katan 22, who discusses this at length. See also Tumim, seif katan 17, who writes that we rule in accordance with the Shach’s conclusion that a kinyan made privately is also effective. See there further.
