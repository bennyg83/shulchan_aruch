(Shach, seif katan 6) And even according to the latter opinion, etc. Note: This requires study. From the Ramban's words cited by the Ran, and even more clearly from the Ramban cited by Shitah Mekubetzet to Ketubot in the discussion of one who writes down his testimony, it appears that the debtor is believed to say, "I paid," even without any independent clarification at all.
