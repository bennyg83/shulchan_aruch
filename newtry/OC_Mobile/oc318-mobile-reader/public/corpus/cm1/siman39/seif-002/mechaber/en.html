When one lends money to another in the presence of witnesses, the witnesses may not write their testimony and give it to the lender, because they would thereby transform an oral loan into a documented loan, unless the borrower tells them, "Write a document, sign it, and give it to him." Even after he says this, they must, according to one view, consult him again after signing before giving the document to the lender. Some say they need not consult him again.
