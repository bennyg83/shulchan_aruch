One who moves a boundary, etc. Sifrei explains the verse "you shall not move your neighbor's boundary": since theft is already prohibited, this verse teaches that one who moves a neighbor's boundary in Eretz Yisrael violates two prohibitions. The phrase "in your inheritance which you will inherit" limits the second prohibition to Eretz Yisrael. Outside Eretz Yisrael he violates only the ordinary prohibition of theft. See Beer HaGolah seif katan 2 and the gloss to siman 360 seif 1.
