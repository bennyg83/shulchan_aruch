If in Eretz Yisrael he moved the boundary, etc., until "and by the prohibition of 'you shall not move a boundary.'" The end of the verse says, "in your inheritance that you will inherit in the land, etc." Scripture therefore warned against moving land boundaries specifically in Eretz Yisrael.
He violates two prohibitions, etc. Rambam likewise writes this. The Tur, however, does not mention the prohibition of "you shall not rob" or "you shall not steal," because land is not legally stolen, as explained in siman 371. Nevertheless, the act is sinful, as stated in siman 359. Likewise, regarding other lands the Tur does not say that one violates "you shall not rob," as he says in Eretz Yisrael that one violates "you shall not move a boundary"; rather he says only that if he takes it by force he is a robber.
