He is a robber. See Baer HaGolah, who cites the Rashba's responsum to the effect that the enactment made for penitents was instituted only for movable property, but not for land. The Beit Yosef cites this in the Rashba's name in this siman. The Beit Yosef above, at the end of siman 371, likewise cites it in the name of Meisharim, netiv 31, part 6, and the Bach rules likewise in his name. See there. The Rama also rules so above in siman 360, seif 1, in the gloss. See what I wrote there, seif katan 1, concerning a case where the encroachment was not committed knowingly.
