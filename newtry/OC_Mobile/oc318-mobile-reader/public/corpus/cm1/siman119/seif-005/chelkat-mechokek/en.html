Even if he does not have the means to pay her ketubah. This law is not universally agreed upon. Rashba, siman 1254, writes that a man may not divorce his wife if he cannot pay her ketubah. As for the Rosh's statement in Responsa, klal 42, that since by law she is obligated to accept the get, payment of her ketubah is like any other debt and he pays as his means permit, it is possible that the Rosh wrote this only where she is obligated to accept a get because he advances a claim of a serious defect or the like. But when he divorces her voluntarily, perhaps the Rosh agrees with Rashba. Nevertheless, the words of Rivash, siman 91, somewhat imply that she must accept her get and afterward claim her ketubah, and that she cannot delay the get until payment of the ketubah is ready, because before the get she has no claim regarding the ketubah.
