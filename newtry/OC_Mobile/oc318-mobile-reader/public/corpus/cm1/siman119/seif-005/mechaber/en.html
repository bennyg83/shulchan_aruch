If the debtor sold the parcels successively to one person, the purchaser takes the owner's place. When does this apply? When he acquired the superior land last, or acquired all the parcels at once. But if he acquired the inferior land last, all claimants collect from the inferior land. {Rama: Some say that even when he acquired the superior land last, this applies only if the seller died. If the seller is still alive, the purchaser can return the deed for the inferior land to the seller, after which all claimants must collect from the seller. The same principle applies when he acquired the inferior land last: even if the seller died, everyone collects from the inferior land. If he acquired intermediate land last, injured parties and creditors collect from the intermediate land, while a ketubah is collected from the inferior land.}
