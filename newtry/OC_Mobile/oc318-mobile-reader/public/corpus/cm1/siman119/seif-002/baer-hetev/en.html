Torah law gives such a claimant a lien upon superior land, yet if the debtor sells the superior land, the injured party collects only from inferior land. The Bach asks for the source of that proposition, but the Shach writes that the question is misplaced. It is an explicit baraita and Gemara in the first chapter of Bava Kamma and is accepted by all the authorities.
