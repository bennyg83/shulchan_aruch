A creditor does not seize superior-quality land. The Tur compares this to a tort claimant, whose biblical right is to superior land, yet if that land was sold he collects only from inferior land remaining with the debtor. Bach asks for the source, but this is an explicit baraita and Gemara in the first chapter of Bava Kamma and is accepted by all authorities in the remainder of this siman.
From the superior-quality land that he sold. The Sema criticizes Ir Shushan as though it said that the creditor collects intermediate land because the debtor promised superior land. The criticism is unwarranted. Ir Shushan says that the creditor collects from the best of the intermediate land still in the debtor’s possession. Ordinarily the debtor may choose which intermediate parcel to give, but because he undertook to pay from his best property, the creditor may choose among the intermediate parcels. That is clear.
