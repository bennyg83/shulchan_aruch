A borrower who stipulated... His stipulation cannot make the creditor stronger than a tort claimant whose Torah lien is on superior land. Yet Bava Kamma says that all claimants collect from the last parcel sold, and Gittin similarly rules concerning a debt document against orphans. All the more this applies to purchasers. The Rif and Rosh accept this distinction. Some authorities distinguish orphans from purchasers, explaining that purchasers are analogous to guarantors, and an ordinary guarantor is not sued before the principal debtor. Baal HaTerumos develops this point. See Beer HaGolah and Rashbam in Bava Basra.
