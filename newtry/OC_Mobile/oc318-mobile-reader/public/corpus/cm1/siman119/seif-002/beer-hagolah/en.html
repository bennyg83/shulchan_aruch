Ba'al HaTerumot likewise writes in Gate 4 and explains that purchasers are like guarantors, and the Mishnah states that one may not first demand payment from the guarantor, Bava Batra 173a. Furthermore, it resembles a lender who wrote that the borrower is believed regarding the document, which is effective against orphans but not against purchasers. Most decisors, including Raavad, rule this way.
