He should appease the purchasers. The same applies to a creditor. The rule that one does not collect from encumbered property where free property is available exists only because of the enactment protecting purchasers. If the purchaser waives that protection, the enactment no longer applies. See Beurim: nevertheless, the creditor may not appease a purchaser merely in order to obtain superior land beyond his legal entitlement. If an earlier ketubah exists and the superior land was sold last, the injured party may appease the purchaser of inferior land and thereby leave the inferior land for that purchaser.
