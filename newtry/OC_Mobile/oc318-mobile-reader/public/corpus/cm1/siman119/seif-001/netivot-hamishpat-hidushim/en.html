Superior or inferior land. The creditor can say, "Why did you distance my lien?" See the Beurim. Even if the intermediate quality land in Shimon's possession later deteriorated and became inferior, the creditor still cannot collect from the second purchaser, because at the time of the sale that purchaser carefully bought land that was not then suitable for collection against him. Likewise, if he bought inferior land that later improved to intermediate quality while the intermediate land in the first purchaser's possession deteriorated to inferior quality, the creditor still cannot collect from the second purchaser, because we follow the status at the time of acquisition. But if the seller left intermediate land of the same quality in the first purchaser's hands, then we follow the status at the time of collection.
