And the injured party collects from superior-quality land. Although a tort claimant has no written instrument, damage creates publicity comparable to a deed, according to the Sema and Ir Shushan, based on the Maggid Mishneh. Tosafot and Maharshal imply the same where biblical liens are recognized. This creates difficulty for their statements in siman 66. Other authorities disagree, and Hagahot Ashri rules that a tort claimant does not collect from property sold before judgment. The matter requires practical study. Ir Shushan offers another reason, that an obligation written in the Torah is like a written loan. That is not correct as practical law, since we rule that a Torah-imposed debt is not treated as a written loan, as explained in Yoreh Deah siman 305.
