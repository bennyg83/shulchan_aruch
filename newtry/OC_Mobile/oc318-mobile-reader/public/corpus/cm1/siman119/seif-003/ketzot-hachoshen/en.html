A person injured by another collects from superior quality land. Sema says that although tort claims do not ordinarily have a written document, the event itself has publicity like a document, so a purchaser should know of the possible lien. Tosafot indicates that this reasoning depends on the view that property liens are biblical. If liens are only rabbinic, publicity alone does not automatically create a purchaser lien in an unwritten tort claim. Thus the ability to collect superior land from purchasers is bound up with the broader theory of whether the tort lien exists by Torah law.
