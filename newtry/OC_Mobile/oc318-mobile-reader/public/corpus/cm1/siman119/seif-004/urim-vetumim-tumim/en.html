And he wrote them an after-acquired-property clause, etc. This itself disproves the Shach's statement above in siman 115 that an after-acquired-property clause is ineffective for a wife's ketubah. One of the claimants here is a ketubah holder, yet the baraita assumes that the clause gives her collection rights. The rule that where two claimants would otherwise divide we favor the weaker claimant does not answer this, because that principle applies where the debtor's assets are insufficient to satisfy both. Here the only distinction concerns whether collection is from inferior or intermediate land, so the weaker-claimant rule is not the basis of the result.
