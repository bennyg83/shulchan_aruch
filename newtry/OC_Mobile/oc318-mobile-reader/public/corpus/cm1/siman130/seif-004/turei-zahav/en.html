(See there: Even if the promissory note is in his possession). Sema is correct in disagreeing with Levush. There is explicit proof from Bava Batra 32 in the incident of the guarantor. The lender had written “I have received payment,” as Nimukei Yosef states expressly. If the borrower could simply say, “I paid the guarantor,” why did the Gemara debate whether he is believed to say, “You did not first receive the money from me”? Once he says “I paid you,” the guarantor's document would be ineffective and the later claim that the guarantor returned and collected again would be only an oral claim. Necessarily, once the lender wrote a receipt to the guarantor, the borrower cannot claim that he paid the guarantor.
