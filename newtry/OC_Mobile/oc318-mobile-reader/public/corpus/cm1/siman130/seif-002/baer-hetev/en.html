The Tur and Sefer HaTerumot write that only where the borrower told him, “Become my guarantor,” is the borrower liable even if the guarantor paid voluntarily before the creditor litigated with the borrower. If the borrower did not tell him to become guarantor, the borrower is exempt. This appears to be the primary ruling, and Responsa Mabit, part I, end of siman 42, implies the same.
