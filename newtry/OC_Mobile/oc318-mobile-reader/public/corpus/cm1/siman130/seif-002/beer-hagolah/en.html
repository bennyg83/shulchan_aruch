The language of Rambam, Hilkhot Malveh chapter 27, law 6, cited by Tur.
As Rabbi Hamid puts his hand on his son, and the taste of this is because he has entered a contract from the words of the Levites and the lender, he is able to sue and replace him if he or she does not wait for him to return
This is also the name of the Rambam, who is there, or who has said to him that he is an Arab, and that he is not allowed to pay him anything
And in the name of the taste, it is not that the loan cannot be achieved, but after the prefixion of the loan in the law, and this is pre-existing and the judgment of the evening, in order to make another man who violates the debt of his heirs who do not have to pay as the above
Tur in the name of Raavad in his gloss there, according to the Maggid Mishneh's explanation.
