There is an authority who says... This is like one who pays another's debt without authorization. So writes Sefer HaTerumos. Accordingly, the incident involving that guarantor must be speaking where the borrower said, "Become my guarantor and pay."
But... See Sema, seifim katanim 7 and 8.
If... but... Since the borrower was necessarily required to pay him, as indicated in Bava Kama 58a concerning one who drives away a lion. See Tosafos there, s.v. "Alternatively," who explains that where a person is compelled and suffers a loss, reimbursement is due. Tosafos' statement there in the name of Ri regarding one who pays another's debt likewise concerns either a voluntary payment or a case where the payer suffers no loss.
Some say... Since the guarantor has a lien against the borrower, his payment is not considered voluntary. See above siman 129, seif 1, in the gloss. Sefer HaTerumos writes that even according to this view, this applies where the borrower told him, "Become my guarantor."
