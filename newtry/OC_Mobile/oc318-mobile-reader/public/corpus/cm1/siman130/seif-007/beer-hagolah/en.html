This too is there in the cited chapter 26, law 7. Maggid Mishneh writes that it is explicit in the Gemara there: with a gentile, because their law follows the guarantor, etc. He explains that this rule applies only in a place where gentile law is indeed so.
Beit Yosef writes: meaning, when the guarantor paid without informing the heirs, the presumption that the deceased left designated funds remains in force. When he informed them, that presumption is removed.
