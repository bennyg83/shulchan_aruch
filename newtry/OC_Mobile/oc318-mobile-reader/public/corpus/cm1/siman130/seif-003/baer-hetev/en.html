Even. If the creditor wrote to the guarantor, "I have received payment," see the Shach's lengthy discussion. See also the Sema and Taz concerning whether the borrower can claim that he paid the guarantor and their practical disagreement.
I have received. If the guarantor has witnesses that he paid the creditor and the creditor's promissory note is in the guarantor's possession, even though the creditor did not write "I have received payment," the guarantor is believed and is placed in the creditor's position, since it is known that he served as guarantor. Sema. This is also explicit in siman 132:4. See Responsa Maharashdam 25. Shach.
