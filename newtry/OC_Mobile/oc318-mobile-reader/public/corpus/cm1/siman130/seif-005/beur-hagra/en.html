A guarantor who paid... This is comparable to the rule in Kesubos 85a concerning payment by an agent. Although where the principal did not instruct him to take the document the agent is exempt, as the Rif and Tosafos explain there from Bava Metzia 3a, that is because the principal can say, "I trusted you," since he did not say to him, "Give it only upon receipt of the document." Here, however, the circumstances are different and the guarantor is responsible to secure the evidence of payment.
