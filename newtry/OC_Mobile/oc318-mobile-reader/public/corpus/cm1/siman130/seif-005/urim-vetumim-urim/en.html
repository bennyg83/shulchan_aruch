Which he left. Sema notes that here Mechaber does not require that the guarantor specifically mention taking the debt document from the lender, unlike above in siman 58. Since the guarantor knew the loan was documented and the document remained with the lender, he should have understood that payment without receiving the document exposed him to renewed collection. The Shach writes that although Baal HaTerumot, cited by Beit Yosef above, exempts the guarantor unless the lender expressly told him to take the document, that applies where the guarantor intended to recover from the borrower and therefore should not have paid without securing the document. This distinction is correct.
