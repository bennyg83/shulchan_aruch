Here the Mechaber does not say, as in siman 58 seif 1, that the borrower must specifically have reminded him to take the note, because the guarantor knew that the loan had been made by document and that the note was still in the creditor's hands. Although Sefer HaTerumot, cited by Beit Yosef there, states that even when the debtor mentioned the note the payer is exempt unless the debtor explicitly told him to take it, a guarantor is different. He pays his own money intending to recover it from the borrower, and therefore should not have paid without obtaining the note. He cannot extract money from the borrower without the note. Sema.
