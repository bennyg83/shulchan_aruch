If the borrower died and the guarantor hastened to pay the debt before notifying the heirs, then if it is known that the borrower did not pay the promissory note before his death, such as where he admitted it before dying, was placed under a ban and died while under it, or the loan had not yet fallen due, the guarantor may recover from the heirs everything he paid. {Rama: Some say all this applies specifically to minor orphans. Adult heirs must pay when the guarantor holds the document, in the manner explained in seif 4. This appears to be the primary view.}
