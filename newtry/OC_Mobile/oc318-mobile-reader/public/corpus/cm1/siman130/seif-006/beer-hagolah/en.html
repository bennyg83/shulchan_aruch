The Maggid Mishneh writes that the source is the incident at the end of Get Pashut, Bava Batra 174a, concerning the guarantor for an orphan, following Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua, whose view is ruled as halachah there on 174b.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam interprets the case as applying even to adult orphans, which is why he did not write 'minor orphans.' This is the view of his teacher and of Raavad. The reason is that whenever a guarantor pays without informing the heirs, it appears that the debtor had already set aside money for payment. Some commentators explain the case as minor orphans, but the first view is primary.
