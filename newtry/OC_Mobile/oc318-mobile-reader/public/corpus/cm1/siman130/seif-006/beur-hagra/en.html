And some say that all this, etc. This follows the explanation of Raavad and Ramban. If the creditor did not write a receipt, the guarantor cannot collect even from adult heirs because of the concern that the debtor had set aside funds, and because the reimbursement is only an oral loan. Rambam, however, sees no distinction between minor and adult heirs. Where the debtor admits the debt, collection is possible even from minors; where he does not, collection is unavailable even from adults. The receipt only defeats the claim that the document fell into the guarantor's hands or was transferred to him. See siman 108 and citation 108.
