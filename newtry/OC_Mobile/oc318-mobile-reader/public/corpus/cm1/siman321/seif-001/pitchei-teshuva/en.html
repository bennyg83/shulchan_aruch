And if the house fell, etc. The authorities discuss whether a landlord who loses his own dwelling may remove a tenant from another property, and how this depends on the nature of the tenancy and local custom. The practical rule follows the distinctions stated in this siman and the cited responsa: a vested tenancy is not displaced merely because the landlord's circumstances became difficult unless the applicable condition, custom, or specific halachic ground for removal is present.
