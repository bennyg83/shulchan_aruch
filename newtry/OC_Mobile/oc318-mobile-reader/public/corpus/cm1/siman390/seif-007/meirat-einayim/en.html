In a place where it was impossible for it not to scatter pebbles. This too is an unresolved question in the Gemara. There is stronger reason here to classify the conduct as ordinary foot, even though the pebbles were scattered as a result of kicking, because in this place the animal would have scattered pebbles even without the kick. Nevertheless, since the question remained unresolved, its law is the same as the preceding doubt concerning kicking and scattering pebbles where it could have avoided doing so.
Seizure is effective, etc. According to the dissenting authorities, the Tur writes that if the damage occurred in the public domain, anything seized is removed from the injured party, while if it occurred in the injured party's domain and he seized the body of the offending animal, he may collect one quarter damages from it. The reason is straightforward according to the rule that the burden of proof rests upon the claimant. Since seizure is ineffective for such a doubtful law according to them, the injured party is always in the weaker position.
