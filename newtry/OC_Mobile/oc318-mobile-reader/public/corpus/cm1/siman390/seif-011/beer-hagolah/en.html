ayin) The language of the Tur, seif 4, from the baraita at the beginning of 18a.
peh) The Gemara's interpretation there.
tzadi) Tosafot and the Rosh explain it this way, and the Maggid Mishneh likewise writes in the name of the Rashba's Chiddushim.
kuf) From the Gemara's sugya and the explanation of Tosafot and the Rosh.
reish) Rav Bibi bar Abaye's interpretation there. The Rambam, law 14, states the law of one who digs about in another wording, and the Maggid Mishneh writes that this follows the Rashba's approach.
