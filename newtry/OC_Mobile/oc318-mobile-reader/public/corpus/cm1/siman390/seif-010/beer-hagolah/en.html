The wording of Rambam, law 10, from the Mishnah there, 17a.
Beit Yosef omitted these distinctions stated by Rambam in the preceding law because Raavad challenged them and the Maggid Mishneh found no support. He writes that Rambam based them on the sugya relating to this Mishnah, 19b, but according to our text he does not know why. The preceding clause copied by the Mechaber was not challenged. In my humble opinion it corresponds to the Gemara's statement, 'rather, the Mishnah concerns one that propelled it along,' which Rambam explains as where it became entangled in the cord, rolled, and broke elsewhere. That is pebbles and incurs half damages. The challenge concerns only Rambam's further distinction depending on whether a person tied it, etc., which the Mechaber did not explain. The Mechaber also did not set out all the detailed laws according to our Gemara and the approach of Tosafot and Rosh, as Tur does and Rama cites. It appears he relied on the fact that these cases are uncommon, as Beit Yosef says Rif omitted Rav Huna's statement because it is not common. Perhaps that was true of his text, though our Rif contains Rav Huna's statement.
