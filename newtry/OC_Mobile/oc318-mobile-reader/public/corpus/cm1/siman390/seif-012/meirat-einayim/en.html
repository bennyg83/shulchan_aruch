We do not say that because the owner was initially negligent in allowing them the opportunity to jump in their normal manner, which would generate full liability, he must therefore pay full damages even when they caused injury through an unusual action. The Torah imposes only half damages for damage caused through an unusual act, like an innocuous ox that gores. Since this behavior is not their normal manner, it is treated as an unusual act.
