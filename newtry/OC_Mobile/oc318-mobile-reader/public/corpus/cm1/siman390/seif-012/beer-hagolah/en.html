shin) The language of the Rambam there, law 15, from the Mishnah on 21b.
tav) Explained in the Gemara there.
a) Tosafot and the Rosh write this from the baraita and the Gemara's interpretation. Tosafot further write that if the objects were neither close nor distant, the cases of falling and jumping are combined. In my humble opinion, one may say that the Rambam and Rif, who do not distinguish between items near the wall and far from it, discuss this middle case; alternatively, they had another textual reading, as the Maggid Mishneh writes.
bet) The Gemara's interpretation there.
gimel) Explained in what is written immediately below.
dalet) Ibid., law 16. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this follows the Rif's reading of the baraita there, as Rav Pappa explains it at the beginning of 22a. The meaning is that it digs its claws into the wall, which is not its normal manner and is therefore an unusual act.
heh) Ibid., in the baraita at the end of 21b.
vav) Tur, seif 6. Tosafot write this there, s.v. 'a person and a rooster,' and so do the Rosh and the other poskim.
