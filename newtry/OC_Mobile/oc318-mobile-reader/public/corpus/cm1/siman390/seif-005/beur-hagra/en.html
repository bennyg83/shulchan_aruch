There is, etc. Since Rav Ashi's question on 19a concerning the force of a force was posed only according to Sumchus, it follows that for the Rabbis the law was obvious. For Sumchus the question is only whether the received halacha limits liability. One cannot say that it was obvious to him that a secondary force is unlike one's direct force because of the halacha, since the halacha comes to reduce liability, as explained in Sukkah according to all but one answer in the opinion of Rabbi Shimon. This is why Bava Kamma 17b says "it is always like his body," and does not say the opposite. So writes the Rosh. Likewise, from 18a, "if it is Sumchus, say the latter clause," it appears that according to the Rabbis the matter is clear and could be resolved from the latter clause alone. The Rambam does not agree, as stated in siman 392 in the Shulchan Aruch. The Rosh also cites Tosafos this way; see Tosafos 22a, s.v. "and Rabbi Yochanan."
