According to, etc. The Rashba also rules so; see Beer HaGolah. The Rambam, however, holds that because the Gemara asks, "since, then," it is evident that excessive tail swishing is not its normal manner, and the original question returns, "if so, horn also," etc. The Rosh explains that the Gemara resolves the issue by saying that even excessive swishing is normal, and its challenge concerns ordinary swishing, asking how one could ever have entertained the question at all.
