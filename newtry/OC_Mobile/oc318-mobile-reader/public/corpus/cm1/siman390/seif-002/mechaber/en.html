If an animal repeatedly and unusually lashes with its tail and causes damage in the public domain, or lashes with its male organ in the public domain and causes damage, the owner is exempt. If the injured party seized property, he may collect half-damages from what he seized, because there is an unresolved question whether these acts are derivatives of horn, for which liability applies in the public domain, or derivatives of foot, for which there is exemption there. Rama: Some hold that seizure is ineffective in every unresolved question of law, and therefore even if the injured party seized property it is removed from him. According to Rosh, lashing with the tail is not doubtful at all and the owner is certainly exempt.
