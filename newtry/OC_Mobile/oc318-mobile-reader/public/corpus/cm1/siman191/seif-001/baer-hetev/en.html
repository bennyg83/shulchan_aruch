The shard. The Sema writes that although pottery and a leaf are materials susceptible to alteration, and for that reason a promissory note may not be written upon them even for immediate collection, as stated by the Tur and Shulchan Aruch at the beginning of siman 42, that case is different. There Scripture teaches that the document must be capable of lasting for many days. A deed of acquisition, however, need not be durable; it need only effect the acquisition at that moment. The same is stated explicitly at the beginning of siman 42 in the Rama, citing the Ran, and my father-in-law the Bach questioned the Sema without cause. Taz. See Responsa Maharashdam, siman 265.
