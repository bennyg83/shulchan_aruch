[Shulchan Aruch] Until he gives the money. Note: The Ran writes in Kiddushin that even where the seller is not pressing for payment, the buyer does not acquire. If he paid part of the price and the seller is not pressing for payment, he likewise does not acquire, because the money was not given for the purpose of acquisition. Therefore, even though the seller accepted it, this does not prove that he is agreeable that the buyer acquire through it. Consequently, the buyer does not acquire even corresponding to the amount paid, because acquisition by deed does not operate partially. See above, siman 190, seif 15, in the Rama. See also Ritva on Kiddushin, who writes that this is specifically in a place where money alone does not acquire. But in a place where it is not customary to write a deed, although he does not acquire by virtue of the deed because he paid only part, he nevertheless acquires by virtue of the money. Thus, if the seller is pressing for payment, he acquires corresponding to the money received, and if the seller is not pressing for payment, he acquires the whole. See there.
