Seif 4: Or with money. The Sema correctly questions this ruling. An even stronger objection arises from the explicit Gemara at the beginning of HaOmer regarding betrothal. Rav and Shmuel both say that if a man gives money and says, “You are betrothed to me after thirty days,” she is betrothed even if the money has already been spent. The same principle should govern a monetary acquisition. The distinction suggested here therefore requires careful study, since money can create the acquisition now with its legal effect deferred.
