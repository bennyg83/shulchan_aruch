And he wrote in the deed that he acknowledged receiving it. This is the ordinary formulation. Wherever money is paid before the deed is written, the deed naturally states that the seller received the money. Indeed, if the deed did not say so, that itself would be a defect suggesting that he had not been paid. Nevertheless, even where the deed says that he received the money, there are places where such language is written in anticipation of money to be paid in cash immediately after the deed is executed. Therefore both the actual payment practice and the wording of the deed must be considered.
Or with money after thirty days. Darkei Moshe cites this in the name of the Beit Yosef, but I searched and did not find that the Beit Yosef wrote it. On the contrary, at the end of siman 190 and again at the end of siman 192 he writes explicitly in the name of Tosafot that money effects acquisition even if the parties did not say "from now," because if the sale did not take effect the money would have to be returned, so it is considered as though it remains present. Nimukei Yosef writes the same explicitly. One can reconcile Rama with difficulty by saying that when he writes that the money was "lost," he means lost in a way that makes repayment impossible. He inferred this from the Beit Yosef's statement that where the money is lost the acquisition remains because if the sale were void the seller would have to return it. That implies that where return is impossible, the sale would not take effect. Alternatively, the text may be emended by removing the word "or," so that it reads, "and with the money." Then the concluding statement, that if he did not say "from now" he does not acquire, may concern specifically a land transaction in a place where both money and deed are required and the deed was lost. Darkei Moshe indeed writes explicitly in the latter clause that the deed was lost and does not mention loss of the money. The opening phrase that "even the money was lost" would then be an additional novelty: even if the money too was lost, acquisition is effective because he said "from now."
