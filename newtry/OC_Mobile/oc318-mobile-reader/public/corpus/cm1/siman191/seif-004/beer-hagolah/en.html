The Rashba, with proof from the end of the final chapter of Ketubot, 110a. Since this is the accepted practice, the buyer is believed to say that he paid the money once the validated deed is in his possession, as stated in seif 11.
See Yevamot 53a and the Nimukei Yosef on the baraita, 'What I will inherit from my father is sold to you,' where they write that even if the money was already consumed, the acquisition is valid. So is ruled in Even HaEzer at the beginning of siman 40, and it is explicit in Kiddushin 59. See what the Sema writes on this.
