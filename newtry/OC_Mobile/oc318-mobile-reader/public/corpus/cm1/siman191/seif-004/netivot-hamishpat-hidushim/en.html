Or with money. The Sema asks: where one says, "Acquire after thirty days," even if the money was already consumed he acquires after thirty days, even without saying "from now," because if the acquisition failed the seller would have to return the money. He reluctantly suggests emending the case to concern a deed and money, where money alone does not acquire and the deed was torn. The phrase "or the money was lost" would then teach that even so, if he said "from now," the sale remains effective. He also suggests that the money was lost in a manner in which the seller cannot later recover it, such as unavoidable accident. See Beurim: even where the money was lost through unavoidable accident, whether one may retract depends upon a dispute among the authorities.
The same applies to acquisition through chazakah, and likewise to acquisition through barter. Shach.
