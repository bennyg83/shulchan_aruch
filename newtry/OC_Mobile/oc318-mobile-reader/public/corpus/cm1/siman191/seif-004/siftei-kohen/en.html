Or with money after thirty days, etc. See the Sema, seif katan 9, who cites Tosafot and writes that, to the contrary, their words imply otherwise. Tosafot in Yevamot 93, Ketubot 82, and Kiddushin 63 indeed state that money effects the acquisition even if he did not say 'from now.' One may resolve the matter with difficulty by explaining that the statement that the money was lost means it was lost in a manner in which it can no longer be recovered. This is also stated by the Ran in Kiddushin, Perek HaOmer, by the Hagahot Ashri at the end of Perek HaIshah Shenaflu, and by the Mordechai in Perek HaOmer. The Rama himself draws this distinction in Even HaEzer, beginning of siman 40, citing the Ramban, Rashba, and Ran, as brought by the Beit Yosef there.
The same applies to acquisition by chazakah, etc. The same also applies to acquisition by chalipin. This is stated by Tosafot in Perek HaIshah Rabbah and in Kiddushin, Perek HaOmer, by the Ran and Hagahot Ashri at the end of chapter 5 of Nedarim, and by the Tur and Mechaber below in siman 195. This is unlike the Mordechai in Perek HaOmer, who writes that chalipin is like acquisition by money and does not require the words 'from now.' See also the end of siman 197.
