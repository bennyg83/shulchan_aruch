Because of her late wife, she must have given her permission to take care of a bill so that she cannot return:
Until he gives the money. Meaning the entire price, as Rama wrote in the previous siman, seif 16, for the reason explained there. But if the purchaser acquires a field in satisfaction of a debt already owed to him by the seller, and the seller writes, "My field is sold to you," the purchaser acquires through the deed alone. The seller clearly resolves to transfer the field in exchange for the existing debt. Rashba writes this in a responsum cited by the Beit Yosef.
