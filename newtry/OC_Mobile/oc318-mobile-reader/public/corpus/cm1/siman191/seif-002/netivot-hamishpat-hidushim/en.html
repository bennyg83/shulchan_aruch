Until he gives the money. Meaning, all the money. If the seller transfers a field to him in satisfaction of a debt already owed by the seller, the purchaser acquires through the deed alone, because there is no remaining monetary deficiency. Sema. See Beurim: if he stipulated that acquisition should occur through the deed alone, that condition does not help even where the seller is not selling because of the field's poor quality. Likewise, if he swore not to retract, that is like such a condition. But if the purchase price was converted into a loan owed by the purchaser, he acquires through the deed alone. Conversely, in a place where deeds are ordinarily written, money alone without a deed does not acquire, even where the seller is selling because of the land's poor quality.
