From Shmuel's statement, "They taught this only concerning a deed of gift," etc., in the Gemara's discussion. Maggid Mishneh writes: know that even if the seller is not repeatedly pursuing the money, the buyer does not acquire, etc. If it is a gift and the giver stipulated that the recipient acquire only by deed, the condition is valid, as explained above in siman 190. If the seller transfers the field in payment of a debt he owes the buyer, the buyer acquires through the deed alone, because the seller certainly completes the transfer in satisfaction of his debt. Beit Yosef cites Responsa Rashba; Sema.
