[Sema, letter 3] Which contains no witnesses at all. Note: I am astonished. If a scribe wrote it and there are no witnesses signed upon it, it does not have the legal status of a deed, so by what does he acquire? And if it is in the seller's own handwriting, which is like his signature and is equivalent to one hundred witnesses, how could the Sema write in seif katan 1 that even though earthenware can be forged? A document in the seller's handwriting cannot be forged in that manner, as Tosafot write in Bava Batra 51a.
