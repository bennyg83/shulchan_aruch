This resembles the case of a string tied to a rooster in siman 390, seif 10, in the gloss, and a dog that took a cake with a coal in siman 392, beginning of the siman, where the owner of the string and the owner of the coal each pay half. The same rationale applies there and here: none of these actors is legally responsible. A human being, however, is responsible; therefore, when the objects were rolled by a person, that person alone pays the entire damage. Sema.
