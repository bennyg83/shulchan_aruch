A pit is exempt for utensils. But if the courtyard owner himself is injured by the excrement, the animal owner can be liable because the excrement is his pit. Although Scripture says, "if a man digs a pit," excluding an ox that digs a pit, here the animal owner owned the excrement at the time it was deposited, and the duty to remove the danger rested upon him. It is therefore treated as though he himself dug it. Although siman 313 states that if Reuven's animal deposits dung in Shimon's courtyard, Shimon's courtyard acquires the dung, this case differs because the courtyard owner does not want his courtyard to acquire something dangerous that may injure him. If it is clear that he already knew of the dung and was pleased to have it, then his courtyard acquires it. If he is later injured by it, the animal owner is exempt.
