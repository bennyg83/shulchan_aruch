If it propelled, etc. Rosh writes all these laws. They are explained in siman 390, seif 10, in the gloss. 'It propelled' concerns an innocuous ox together with a pit according to Rav Nachman on 53a-b. The liability applies after the object came to rest, as in the case of the cord on 19b, likewise according to Rav Nachman. See 390.
