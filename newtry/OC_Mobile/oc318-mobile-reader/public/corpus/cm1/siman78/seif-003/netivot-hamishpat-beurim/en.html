He demanded payment on the day the term ended. See Shach, seif katan 16, through his statement that this would be a migo involving brazenness. Tumim writes that although we generally rule that even a migo involving brazenness can be used to exempt a person from monetary liability, here we do not apply it because payment on the very day the term ends is itself uncommon. Thus, an uncommon payment claim is not strengthened by an alternative claim that would require unusual brazenness.
