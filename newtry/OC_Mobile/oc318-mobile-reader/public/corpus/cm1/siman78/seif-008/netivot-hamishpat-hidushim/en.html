This applies specifically where he fixed a time. But where beit din merely granted him time to pay, even if the debtor himself requested that judicial extension, he is believed to say he paid during the period. See Beurim: arbitrators who fixed a date as part of a compromise create a situation like the lender voluntarily granting extra time, which the Shach treats as a claim of payment within the term. With rent and obligations concerning a courtyard wall, since the obligation itself may be uncertain, it can have the law of a payment claim within the term even without a fixed date.
