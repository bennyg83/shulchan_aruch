See what I wrote above, siman 69 seif 2, in the name of Responsa Noda BiYehudah, concerning a document whose date was intentionally postdated with the knowledge of both borrower and lender. He discusses whether the presumption that a person does not repay before maturity applies during the interval between the actual writing of the document and the later date written in it, and distinguishes between a witnessed document that creates a lien on land and a handwritten obligation that creates no lien on land.
