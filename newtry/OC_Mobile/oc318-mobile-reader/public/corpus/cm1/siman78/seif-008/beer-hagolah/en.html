The Tur, seif 5. So write Tosafot, the Rosh, Ramban in chapter 1 of Bava Kamma, Baal HaTerumot, gate 16, and the Shulchan Aruch. The Rosh further explains in Responsa, rule 71, siman 53, that with an ordinary loan it sometimes happens that the borrower obtains money and repays the same day. Therefore, if he says, 'I repaid you that very day,' he is believed.
