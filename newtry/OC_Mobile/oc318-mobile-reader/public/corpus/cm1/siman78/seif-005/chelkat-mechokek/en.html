They descend to his property and sell it by public announcement. Although we rule that every moment one delays redeeming a captive is as though he sheds blood, as explained in Yoreh Deah siman 252, nevertheless the property is sold by public announcement. The Gemara exempted only three matters from public announcement: the royal tax, support, and burial. It nevertheless remains difficult why such a great mitzvah should be inferior to those three matters.
