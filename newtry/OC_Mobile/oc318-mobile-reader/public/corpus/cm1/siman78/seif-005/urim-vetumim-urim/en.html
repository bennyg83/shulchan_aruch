And he claims that he paid at its time. Meaning that he says he paid within the term, as appears from the Shach. Sema asks what this seif adds, since the preceding seif already says that at the end of the due-date day he may claim earlier payment through a migo. One possible novelty is based on what I wrote: if the claimant seized property in the presence of witnesses, we may not remove it from him because of the underlying legal doubt. One might then say that the borrower could refuse to swear unless the claimant first accepts that if he later seizes property in the presence of witnesses he will return it and not claim certainty like the authorities who support seizure. The law teaches that this is not required. The borrower takes the hesset oath now.
