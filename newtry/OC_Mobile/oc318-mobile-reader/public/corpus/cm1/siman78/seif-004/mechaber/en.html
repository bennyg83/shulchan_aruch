Some say that the rule that a borrower who is sued on the day the term expires and says, "I paid you today," is believed applies only when he is sued at the end of the day. Earlier in the day he is not believed. If, at the end of the day, he says, "I paid you before the term expired," he is believed through the migo that he could have said, "I paid you today." Some say this claim is advanced only when the borrower himself makes it, but we do not advance it on behalf of his heirs.
