The correct reading is: the lender died within the term, his heirs produced the promissory note, and the borrower produced a written receipt. This is a responsum of the Rosh, cited by the Tur in siman 71:15. It is also recorded in Chazeh HaTenufah and cited by Maharik in shoresh 66. The Beit Yosef likewise quotes it here in this form. The Mechaber's text before us is therefore erroneous, for his marginal reference itself identifies the responsum as appearing in Chazeh HaTenufah and as cited by Maharik there.
