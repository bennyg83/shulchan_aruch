[Shach, letter 15, s.v. “And regarding the practical ruling”] But indeed he must take a heset oath. Note: There, in my humble opinion, when there is one witness he is obligated to take a Torah oath after payment, for the presumption that a person does not pay within the fixed time does not exempt him from the oath generated by one witness. With respect to an oath, one witness is like two witnesses with respect to monetary liability.
