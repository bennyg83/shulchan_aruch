(Shach, seif katan 7) That I would not be believed, etc. Note: One can say that we tell him: because you thought you would later be believed regarding the second payment if he denied it, you unnecessarily paid him, since you would have been believed in your original claim that you paid within the term through a migo that you could have said, 'I paid now.' However, from the Sema's words it appears that the primary reason is that the presumption shows that his original claim in beit din was false. He is therefore established as a false denier and can never afterward exempt himself from that same claim without proof.
