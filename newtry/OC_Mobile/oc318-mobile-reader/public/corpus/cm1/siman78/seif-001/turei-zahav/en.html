(Shulchan Aruch, seif 2) “Some say the creditor must swear that he was not paid and then collect.” The gloss is difficult. If the oath precedes collection, it should be a severe oath while holding a sacred object, like every claimant who swears and takes, yet the Rosh, source of this view, specifies heset. If the intention is that he collects first and later takes heset, like the employer in siman 317 who claims payment within the term, then the following view that no oath is required appears to exempt him even afterward. Sefer HaTerumot likewise concludes that the creditor need not swear merely to retain what he collected. Why then does siman 317 require heset afterward? The distinction requires study.
