Even minors. Bava Basra 174a says that we are concerned that the debtor may have set aside a bundle of money for payment. One could say that even before the due date he may have set aside such money, as in the law that a widow may collect, etc., discussed in Gittin 34b, where Rav does not, etc., because he is concerned about money set aside for payment. There we are concerned even before the due date, since a kesubah is ordinarily not collectible during the husband's lifetime. The reason is that a dying person commonly sets aside money shortly before death because he does not want his widow to suffer humiliation in beit din, as stated in Kesubos 97b. Proof comes from Bava Basra 158a regarding a house that fell upon a person, etc. Tosafos there, s.v. "and Beis Hillel...," ask according to the opinion, etc. But according to what I wrote the matter is clear, because in sudden death there was no opportunity to set aside money. So too Tosafos there write in the name of Rabbeinu Shimshon. See the Rosh, chapter 1, there.
