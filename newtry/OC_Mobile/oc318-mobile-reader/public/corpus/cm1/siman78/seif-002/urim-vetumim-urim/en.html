And this is primary. See Tumim. I concluded that the two views do not really disagree about whether a hesset oath can ultimately be required. They differ only in the immediate court ruling. According to one, beit din says, 'Pay him, but afterward the claimant will take a hesset oath concerning the payment claim.' According to the other, beit din simply orders payment. Once payment is made, if the borrower then sues and says, 'You received payment twice,' he can require a hesset oath. Everyone agrees to that. This is the case below in siman 317.
