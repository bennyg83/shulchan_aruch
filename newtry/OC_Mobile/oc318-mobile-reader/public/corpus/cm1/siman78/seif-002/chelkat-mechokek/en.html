We do not obligate the husband, etc. The wording implies that if he wishes to redeem her for more than the ordinary amount, we do not stop him. If so, the ruling should have said that according to some authorities he is actually obligated to redeem her to the extent of his ability. But there is a deeper difficulty. According to the authority who follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel in Ketubot 52b, redeeming for more than her market value is prohibited because of the public policy concern. Especially according to Rambam, who rules like the opinion at the end of HaSholeach that excessive redemption encourages captors to demand more, one may not redeem for more than the captive's value. See Yoreh Deah 252.
