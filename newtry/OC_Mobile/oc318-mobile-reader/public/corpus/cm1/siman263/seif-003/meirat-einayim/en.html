And they forbid him to return it. I already cited the Rosh, who writes that it is prohibited for an elder to demean the honor of his Torah where the Torah did not obligate him to do so.
He should pay from his own pocket. The Gemara brings the incident of Rabbi Yishmael son of Rabbi Yose, who encountered a man carrying bundles of wood. The man said to him, "Load them for me." Rabbi Yishmael asked, "How much are they worth?" He answered, "Half a zuz." Rabbi Yishmael gave him half a zuz, etc., and declared the wood ownerless. See there. The Tur cites this in siman 272:9.
