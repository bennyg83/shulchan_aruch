(Rama, gloss to seif 5) Others disagree and hold that if, etc. Note: Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 99, writes that there is no proof that the Rosh disagrees with the Rashba. The Rashba discusses expenses incurred in proceedings before non-Jewish courts, whereas the Rosh discusses expenses incurred there in order to compel the other party to appear with him before a Jewish court.
