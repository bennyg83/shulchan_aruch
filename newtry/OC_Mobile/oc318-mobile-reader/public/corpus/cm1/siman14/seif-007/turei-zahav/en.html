In the name of Maharshal, in the Beit Yosef, s.v. “Sefer HaMitzvot wrote: If there are two Torah scholars in one city,” through “without refuting his proof” There is no difficulty. The Tur explains the Gemara as Nimukei Yosef does: a borrower may object even where both judges are experts, such as preferring Rav Huna over Rav Chisda because Rav Huna was greater. “Am I troubling you?” applies in that direction, not the reverse, unlike Semag’s explanation. This is also explicit in Rashba’s responsum cited by Beit Yosef.
