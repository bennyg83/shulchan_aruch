And certainly if. There, 31b.
(Collected note) Some say that if, etc., and certainly, etc. Meaning, even if he does not suspect the judges. This follows Tosafot in Sanhedrin there, s.v. "veIm," "however, etc." According to the first opinion, however, this is the same as the statement "however, some say, etc." Nevertheless, it is possible that even according to this opinion, if he suspects them they must write the reasons for him even if he did not explicitly request them, and Tosafot initially did not enter into this distinction. (End of collected note.)
However, some say. Tosafot there, from the wording "we compel, etc.; and if he said, etc."
(Collected note) Some say, etc.; however, etc. See Yoreh Deah, siman 177, seif 23, where the Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule in accordance with the alternative explanation. (End of collected note.)
