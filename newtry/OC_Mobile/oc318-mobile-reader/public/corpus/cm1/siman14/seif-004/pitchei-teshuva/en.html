A father who has a claim, etc. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot Tur, letter 8, writes that the same rule applies to a rav and his student. Urim VeTumim cites this and explains that it means one’s primary rav, as defined in Yoreh Deah, Hilchot Talmud Torah. Rabbi Yehoshua Heschel Ashkenazi notes that the same should apply to husband and wife: the wife should travel to the husband’s location because she is obligated to honor him, and because travel is particularly difficult for a man. In my opinion this is not persuasive, and Responsa Brit Avraham, Even HaEzer, siman 92, which I cite in Pitchei Teshuva, Even HaEzer, siman 75, seif katan 3, likewise does not appear to support it.
