There: “Some say” According to Baal Halakhot Gedolot, only the defendant is heard when he demands the great beit din. One might object that every defendant could thereby evade payment. The answer is that a person can protect himself by dealing only with one whom he trusts not to wrong him, whereas a defendant cannot guard against anyone who may bring a fabricated claim. Nor need we fear that every poor claimant will summon a rich defendant to the central tribunal merely to force settlement. The phrase “he may spend one hundred to recover one hundred” is not exact there; ordinarily the central tribunal is not as distant as the great beit din, and the concern is only to spare the claimant any unnecessary expense.
