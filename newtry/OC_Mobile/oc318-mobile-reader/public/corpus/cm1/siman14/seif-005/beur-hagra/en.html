There is one who, etc. Because this is indirect causation, as explained in Bava Kamma, siman 1, and as Tosafot distinguish between garmi and grama.
And there are those who disagree. As above, but if he was refusing, etc., it is garmi.
(Collected note) There is one who, etc.; and there are those who disagree, etc. This depends on the dispute of Ri and Ritzba in Tosafot, Bava Batra 22b, s.v. "Zot." The first opinion follows Ri, that garmi is where the person himself performs the causative act and the damage occurs at the time of the act. The latter opinion follows Ritzba, that the Sages imposed liability for common forms of damage, and here too it is common. (End of collected note.)
One who said. As above.
(Collected note) One who said to his fellow, etc. This resembles one who refuses to appear, who is liable as above; this requires study. (End of collected note.)
It is impossible that he swear. For we say that he swears only when he incurred an expense with authorization, Ketubot 79b, or when he enhanced the property, as stated in siman 375.
