Tur in the name of the Semag, from the statement, "Am I troubling you? Let us go and be judged before Rav Chisda," etc., Sanhedrin 23a, even though Rav Chisda was not greater than Rav Huna.
Meaning, one litigant can prevent the other from choosing an unsuitable court, and they must select a beit din acceptable to both.
