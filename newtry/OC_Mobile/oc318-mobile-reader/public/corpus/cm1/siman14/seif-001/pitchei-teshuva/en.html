And afterward they extract. Birkei Yosef writes: Reuven and Shimon litigated, and the judge ruled that Reuven must swear in response to Shimon’s claim. Reuven demanded that the judge first give him a written ruling explaining why he was obligated to swear, and only afterward would he swear. The law is with Reuven. This is also implied by Ramban’s statement that “they write and afterward extract from him.” This is from an unpublished responsum of Maharikash, siman 28. Plainly, the intent is a case where Reuven was compelled to litigate before that court. See Baer Heitev, seif katan 1.
