After it was written, etc. Whatever is formally written in the record was certainly stated with precision. Therefore one cannot later retract from it. It appears that if a party remains silent in response to the opposing party's claim, that silence is likewise treated as an admission according to all views, because the excuse that he was still considering how to answer does not apply once every response is being written in the record. Indeed, even an explanatory excuse does not help in such a case.
That beit din should write, etc. Meaning that this requires the consent of both parties. Nevertheless, beit din should endeavor to obtain their agreement to have the claims written, so that there be no later retraction and no end to empty and shifting claims.
