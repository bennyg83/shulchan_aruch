(In the name of Maharshal, on the Beit Yosef’s citation of Nimukei Yosef in Chezkas HaBatim) Nimukei Yosef and the Rashba follow their own positions. The Rambam disagrees, as explained in siman 79, seif 2. Perhaps, however, everyone agrees in Nimukei Yosef’s case because the claimant’s deed contradicts the possessor’s account of the sequence of years and is equivalent to witnesses proving that his earlier possession preceded the required three-year period. The Rama’s gloss should therefore not be followed; the Mechaber correctly omitted it. The Sema’s comments are likewise difficult.
