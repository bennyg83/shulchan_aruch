[Shulchan Aruch] He cannot retract and advance a different claim. Note: See Maharit, part 2, Choshen Mishpat, siman 36, who writes that nevertheless he is not established as a denier. Thus, if beit din obligated him and he later said, “I paid,” he is believed; and with respect to his present claim as well, the claimant must take an oath. See there. Responsa Rama, siman 30, does not appear to hold so.
