After it was written. Once a claim has been reduced to writing, a litigant is presumed to have been precise in formulating it, and retraction is therefore not accepted. It appears that if he remains silent in response to the opposing claims, such silence is likewise considered admission according to everyone. The reason that he may ordinarily remain silent while formulating an answer does not apply here, since all responses are being written into the record. In this setting, even an explanation for the silence does not help. Urim VeTumim.
That beit din should write. Meaning, the consent of both parties is required. But beit din should encourage them to agree to writing, so that there will be no endless cycle of retractions and frivolous arguments.
