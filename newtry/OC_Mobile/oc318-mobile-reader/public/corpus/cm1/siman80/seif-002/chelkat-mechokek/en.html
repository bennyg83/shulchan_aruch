And she produced more than what was required of her. According to Maggid Mishneh, even if she performed the additional work at a time when ordinary people sleep, and all the more so if she did many tasks simultaneously without unusual exertion, the surplus belongs as discussed there. See the Tur, which cites the differing opinions of Rabbeinu Tam and Rav Hai. The Rosh did not decide between them. Therefore, the woman can say, "I rely on the authority who holds that the surplus belongs to me." The Bach writes that this is the practice in these lands, not to remove from the woman's possession any surplus produced through unusual exertion.
