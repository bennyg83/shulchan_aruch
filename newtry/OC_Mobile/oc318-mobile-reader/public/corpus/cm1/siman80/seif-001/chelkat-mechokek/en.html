Flax harms the mouth, and spinning wool is the work especially associated with women, as Scripture says, "Every wise-hearted woman spun with her hands," in Rambam's language. Although Ketubot 61b states that not every flax is harmful, but only Roman flax, Beit Yosef answers that since we do not know which flax is Roman flax, all flax remains doubtful. This uncertainty works leniently for the woman, so that she cannot be compelled to work with any flax. It is also possible that Rambam understood from the Gemara's categorical formulation, "with wool, yes; with flax, no," that she is not compelled with any type of flax. Because some flax is extremely harmful, the Sages did not distinguish and ruled that only wool may be imposed upon her, not flax. This differs from Tosafot there, passage beginning "and this applies to Roman flax." See there.
