To the second. This differs from siman 176, seif 28, regarding two partners to whom one debt is owed. There they lent jointly. Here, with a jointly owned house in which they lived together or which stands for rental, once the first partner rented it, he may say to the second: live together with this renter, or rent your share to someone else. In any event, there is no difficulty, because the present law addresses only whether the first partner's tenant must also pay the second partner his share; it does not address the partners' reciprocal rights.
