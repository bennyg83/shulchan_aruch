One who steals animals or slaves that deteriorate, and one who resides in another's courtyard without permission. It contains eleven seifim.
If one steals an animal and it ages or suffers irreversible deterioration, such as an incurable illness, or steals a coin that cracks or is invalidated by the king, or fruit that entirely rots, or wine that sours, it is like breaking a stolen utensil and he pays its value at the time of theft. Some say that even a coin invalidated by the king may be returned as is. But if an animal suffers reversible deterioration, slaves age, a coin is invalid only locally but circulates elsewhere, fruit partly rots, terumah becomes impure, chametz remains over Pesach, an animal is used for a transgression or becomes unfit for sacrifice, or is condemned to stoning, he may say, 'Here is yours before you,' and return the object itself.
