The wool and markers who sing as well, but if there were no semanin, but a sway of color as he was, and he was a player and a cracker and jealous of his teeth:
He is exempt, and if the other seized, etc. The Gemara leaves unresolved whether visible appearance is legally significant enough to constitute proper restitution. Therefore the burden of proof rests upon the claimant. See what I wrote in the preceding siman in the Perishah and Sema, that even the Rosh agrees that where the doubt is an unresolved Talmudic question, we do not remove property from one who has seized it.
