The wording of the Tur, seif 8, from the unresolved question in Bava Kamma chapter 9, 101a, explained there according to the first version. Since it remained unresolved, the ruling follows the law of monetary doubt.
In my humble opinion this follows Rambam, who writes similarly at the end of chapter 3 of Hilkhot Gezeilah, law 10. According to Ri and Rosh, however, if the claimant seized property it is removed from him, as Rosh writes at the beginning of Bava Kamma chapter 2 and in many places. Tur and Rama likewise cite this repeatedly. This must also be the meaning in siman 362, end of seif 7. Beit Yosef writes similarly at the end of siman 391 concerning the Tur. Sema explains it differently, but the reader will see and judge. What is written in siman 262 that Rosh says so appears to be a scribal error; I did not find it there.
