And if it is ordinarily rented. Since he took it without the owner's permission, he is at least a robber. The owner therefore has the choice either to treat him as a robber and collect the depreciation, or to accept the compensation that the taker was willing to pay when he seized it. If he expressly entered under a claim of robbery, however, he is governed solely as a robber. This is implied by the Tur and Rambam, who speak of taking without the owner's knowledge. Rashi explains the case as taking it against the owner's will.
