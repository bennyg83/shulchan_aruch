If so, if, etc. The latter clause of the Mishna is Rabbi Yehuda's statement concerning the owner of the upper story, meaning that from that point onward he no longer benefits. Rashba uses this to refute Rif's view stated above in seif 6. This is the dissenting view cited by Rabbenu Yerucham and Rama. At first glance it depends on the dispute between Rif and Tosafot concerning a case where one does not benefit but the other suffers a loss. Yet how could Rif disagree with an explicit Mishna? One must say that Rif concedes where the loss is slight, especially since some authorities hold that even where liability exists one pays only the loss. See Nimukei Yosef and 81.
