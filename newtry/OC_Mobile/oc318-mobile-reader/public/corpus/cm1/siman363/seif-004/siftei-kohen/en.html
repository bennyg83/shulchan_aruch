And if he prevented him from other work, etc. The Sema writes, based on the Perisha, that even according to the Rif, who treats robbed slaves as movable property, where the slave was taken only for labor and not as an act of robbery the taker must pay for the lost work. The Gemara does not appear so. Once the Rif revised his view and treated slaves as movable property, he also necessarily revised the related ruling concerning one who forcibly employs another's slave. Shiltei Giborim says this expressly, and Ma'adanei Yom Tov and Lechem Mishneh discuss it. In practice, however, nearly all authorities reject the Rif's revision and treat slaves as land; Ramban too preferred the Rif's earlier ruling, and that is the principal law.
