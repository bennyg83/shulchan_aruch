The owner of the pit is liable. The Tur concludes that the owner of the covering need not notify him that he removed it. Darkei Moshe reconciles this with the Rosh's ruling, cited by Rama in siman 383:4, that one who removes his stone from beneath another person's barrel and thereby causes the barrel to break can be liable. There the barrel breaks immediately, so removing the support is effectively direct damage. Here uncovering the pit does not immediately cause harm. Damage occurs only later if another person's animal falls in, and the harm is not nearly as certain.
