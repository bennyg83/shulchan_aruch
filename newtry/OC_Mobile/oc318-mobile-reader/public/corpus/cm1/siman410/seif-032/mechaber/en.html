If an ox pushed an animal into a pit and it died, then if the ox was forewarned, the pit owner pays half and the ox owner half. If the ox was innocuous, its owner pays one quarter from the body of the ox, and the pit owner pays the remaining three quarters from the best of his property. The carcass owner may say to the pit owner: 'The reduction in the carcass's value is chargeable to you. Although my animal was large and alert, once it was pushed it is treated like one that fell at night. Whatever I can collect from the ox owner I will collect, and you must pay the rest.'
