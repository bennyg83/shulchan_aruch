(Supplement) Because the owner, etc. This follows the latter version, which is generally authoritative. The earlier sugya likewise follows Rav Nachman. This is unlike Tosafot, who rule according to the first version and understand the dispute involving an ordinary ox and an ox disqualified from sacrifice accordingly. Hagahot Maimoniyot and Yam Shel Shlomo explain that the later version can also fit. Nevertheless, seifim 34 through 36 require study. Yam Shel Shlomo concludes that in all these cases each participant pays only his proportionate share.
