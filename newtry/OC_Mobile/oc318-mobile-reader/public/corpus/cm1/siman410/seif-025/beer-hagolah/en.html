There, law 7. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is an explicit Mishnah in HaParah, 51a, and handing over the bucket is explained in the Gemara, 51b. Rambam rules like Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov, whose view is established as halachah in Nedarim chapter 5, 46b. Rosh in HaParah, cited by Beit Yosef, writes that Rashi's text says the second is liable, while Rif reads the first is liable. There is no practical difference between the two versions. Under Rashi, the second is liable where the first left him using the pit; otherwise both are liable. Under Rif, the meaning is that even the first is liable, lest one think only the second, who passed by last, is liable. In my humble opinion there is no doubt that Rif's version was also that of Rambam and the Maggid Mishneh, and that Rambam likewise held that the first remains liable, as is clear from the Gemara when established according to Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov. Beit Yosef already questions Tur for rejecting Rambam without explaining his words the way Rosh explained Rif.
