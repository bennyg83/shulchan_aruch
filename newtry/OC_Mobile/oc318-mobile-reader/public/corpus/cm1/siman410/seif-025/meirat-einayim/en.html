Some say that both are liable. Rama writes this as "some say" because from the Mechaber's formulation, "the first is liable," it appears that only the first is liable. If "the first is liable" meant merely that the first too is liable, as the Rosh explains the Rif, then the following phrase, "until he transfers his bucket to the second," would necessarily mean that at that point only the second becomes liable. In that case the subsequent statement, "one who transferred his bucket to the second, etc., the second became obligated to cover it," would be redundant. For this reason the Tur also cites the Rambam's formulation, which is like the Mechaber's, and says, "It does not appear so to me." I explained this in the Derishah, unlike the Beit Yosef who questioned the Tur.
