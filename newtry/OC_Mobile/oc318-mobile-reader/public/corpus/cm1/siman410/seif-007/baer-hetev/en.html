The public. The Ramah writes that if one originally dug for his own need and later decided to transfer the pit for public use, the digger is not exempt until he fills it. The Tur objects: once he has now transferred it to the public and they need it, what difference does it make that he originally dug it for himself? Ibid.
Permission. Even for a public need one may not dig in the public domain at a place that burdens the public or similarly causes them harm. Ibid.
