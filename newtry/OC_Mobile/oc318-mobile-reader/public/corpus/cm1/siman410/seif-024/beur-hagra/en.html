(Supplement) Some distinguish, etc. This is Rabbenu Yitzchak's explanation in Tosafot, unlike Riva. Although in siman 291, seif 9, we follow Riva, Yam Shel Shlomo distinguishes between a case where the accident itself is treated as negligence and a case that began with negligence. Here the negligence and the accident concern the same event, namely falling into the pit. There, the negligence concerns wolves and thieves, while the accident is death. See 291.
