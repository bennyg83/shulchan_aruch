Whether, etc. This follows Rabbah, for the halacha follows Rabbah against Rav Yosef, and Rabbi Akiva against an individual colleague. The Rif, Rosh, and other poskim rule so. They further note that the anonymous Mishna accords with Rabbi Akiva.
One who digs, etc. The Mishna, 50b.
Or one who digs a pit, etc. The Mishna, 49b.
Into another's domain. According to Rabbeinu Yitzchak and the Rosh, where the owner did not know. The Tur, as above.
And he abandoned ownership, etc. Rashi, as indicated by the Gemara: Rabbi Akiva speaks where he abandoned ownership, etc.
But if, etc. Tosafos 28b at the end of s.v. "but," and 51a, s.v. "a pit," together with the Rosh and other poskim, unlike Rashi on 50a, s.v. "owner of the obstacle."
