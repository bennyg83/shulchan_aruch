If the first person dug a deep pit and another later widened it, and an ox fell in and died, then if it died from the pit's vapor, the second person is exempt because widening reduced the vapor. If it died from the impact, the second person is liable because he brought the danger nearer. Likewise, if the ox fell from the side widened by the second person, he is liable because he brought the danger nearer, even if the ox died from vapor. If it fell from the side dug by the first person, the first is liable, because the second reduced the vapor. Rama: Some say the distinction is not between vapor and impact. Rather, if the ox fell from the widened side, the second person is liable in every case; if it fell from the other side, the second is exempt in every case.
