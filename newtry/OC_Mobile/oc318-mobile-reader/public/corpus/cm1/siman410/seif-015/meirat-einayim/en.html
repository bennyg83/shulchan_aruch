This is one handbreadth, etc. This is an unresolved question in the Gemara. One person added a handbreadth to a pit so that his addition alone made him the owner of the dangerous pit for liability. He later removed his added handbreadth. Does he remain responsible even for damage caused by the original pit of less than ten handbreadths, because once his addition made him the legal owner of the pit the first person's act ceased to control even for that lesser depth? Or, once he removes his addition, does the matter return to its original state, making the first digger liable and the second exempt? Since the issue remained unresolved, the Mechaber applies the rule that the burden of proof rests upon the claimant. According to the dissenters, seizure does not help in such a doubtful case, as explained in seif 2.
