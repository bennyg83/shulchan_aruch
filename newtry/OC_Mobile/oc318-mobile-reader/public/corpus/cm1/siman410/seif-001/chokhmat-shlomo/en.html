Seif 1. "A pit is one of the primary categories of damage, etc." Note: Bava Kamma 29 says that two things are not legally in a person's possession, yet the Sages treated them as though they were in his possession: chametz on Pesach and a pit in the public domain. See Shitah Mekubetzet, who asks why the Gemara did not also list an ox condemned to stoning. In my work on Orach Chaim, volume two, Hilchot Pesach, page 126, in my responsum to the community of Czestochowa near Krakow, I resolved this with the help of Heaven. In my first edition on Choshen Mishpat to this siman I also cited the Rashba concerning attached horn damage, where the Gemara asks whether perhaps it should be considered fully forewarned. The Rashba writes that a doubt in damages is treated stringently. I raised a difficulty with his words and later resolved it, and I discussed the matter in several other places in my novellae. See also my novellae to Bava Kamma, chapter HaGozel, 99b, where I wrote another resolution of the Rashbam and brought a proof for our approach. See there.
