To become uncovered. See above, siman 396, seif 6, regarding one who gives his ox to a deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor. Even if the ox was tied with a rope, the owner's conduct is considered negligent because an ox commonly loosens its tie. Tosafot explains that the deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor themselves may untie it. It appears the same applies here. Since they are not legally responsible persons, we are concerned they may uncover the pit, which can easily become uncovered. The Gemara indeed states these two laws together. Ibid.
