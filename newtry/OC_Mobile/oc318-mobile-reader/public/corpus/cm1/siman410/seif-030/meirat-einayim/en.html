Because the pit is liable to become uncovered. See above, siman 396, seif 6, regarding one who entrusts his ox to a deaf-mute, incompetent, or minor. Even if the ox was tied with a rope, it is considered negligence by the owner because the ox may become untied. Tosafos explain that this means the deaf-mute, incompetent, or minor may themselves untie it. The same reasoning applies here. Since they lack mature judgment, we are concerned that they will uncover a pit that can easily be uncovered. The Gemara states these two laws together. See there, where I cited the Gemara's wording.
