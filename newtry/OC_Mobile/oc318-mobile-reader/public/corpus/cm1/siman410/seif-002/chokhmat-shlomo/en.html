Seif 25: “Once he handed his bucket to the second person to draw from it, the first is exempt,” etc. Note: The source of this law is the Gemara in Bava Kamma. I find this very difficult. What is accomplished by handing him the bucket? The Gemara there appears to base the matter on the law of a shomer: once he handed him his bucket, the second person became its shomer. But we rule that the laws of shomrim do not apply to land. Why, then, should the second person become liable to pay by virtue of a duty of safekeeping? This requires substantial study.
