(Seif 17: even if it did not die, etc.) The Sema asks that since this follows Shmuel, who imposes liability even for impact damage, the law should have stated impact damage, and all the more so vapor damage. I do not understand what difficulty the Sema sees in the wording of the poskim when the Gemara itself uses precisely this formulation: "Shmuel says, for its vapor, and all the more so for its impact."
