The same applies to one who opens a pit. In Mishpatim Scripture places these cases together: "If a man opens a pit, or if a man digs a pit and does not cover it, and an ox or donkey falls there, the owner of the pit shall pay." From "and does not cover it" we learn that covering exempts him. Consequently, the phrase "if a man opens a pit, the owner of the pit shall pay" teaches that one who removes the covering is himself called the owner of the pit for liability for whatever later falls into it.
