He is liable for the other four categories of damages. As for the fact that he is liable for humiliation, even though the case necessarily concerns a person who pushed him unintentionally, as I wrote in seif katan 34, one must explain it as Tosafos does: for example, the person became aware of his fellow's fall at the time and then turned over intentionally. Maharshal, siman 42, rules that he is exempt for humiliation unless the ox first pushed him and afterward the person came and added to the injury. See there, where he discusses this at length.
