Matters. Although he is liable for humiliation, even though the case necessarily involves an unintentional push as stated in seif 34, one must explain as Tosafot do: the person became aware at the time of the other's fall, and that awareness is treated as intent. Maharshal, siman 42, rules that he is exempt from humiliation unless the ox first pushed the victim and the person then came and added to the harm. See there at length. His words are difficult. Shach.
