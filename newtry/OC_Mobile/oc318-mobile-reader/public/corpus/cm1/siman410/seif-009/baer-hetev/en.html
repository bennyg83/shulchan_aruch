Exempt. This differs from the Tur and Mechaber in siman 414, seif 2, regarding placing refuse in the public domain, and from the end of siman 417 regarding placing stones there for construction. Although those acts are also performed with permission, if they cause damage the actor is liable. There he performs the act in the public domain itself. Here the pit remains within his own property, and it is only temporary, since immediately after the excavation he will fill it with the building stones to be laid in the foundation of his wall. For the same reason this differs from one who builds an oven in his house and distances it from the upper story by the prescribed measure, yet is liable if a fire escapes and causes damage, as stated at the beginning of siman 155. There the activity is regular and ongoing, while here it is temporary. Thus writes the Rif, according to the Sema. The Shach, however, writes that Maharshal rules this is an error and that he is liable. The Gemara, in Maharshal's view, concerns a case where the wall was moved inward into his own property and the excavation was within the recessed area. He proves this at length, contrary to the Sema, and the Bach agrees. The gloss adds: see the Derishah there, who cites Maharshal's words and disputes them at length.
