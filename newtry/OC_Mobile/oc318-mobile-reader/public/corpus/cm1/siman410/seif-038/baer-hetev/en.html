Exempt. Because he himself caused his own loss through digging.
From the heirs. Meaning, not only if the digger is alive must he pay the owner of the ox, but even if the digger dies, his sons who inherit him must pay for the damage to the ox. Although an oral debt of their father is generally not collected from heirs after his death, here the father died only now, at the time the ox fell in, so he certainly had not yet paid the value of the ox.
