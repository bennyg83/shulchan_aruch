The Maggid Mishneh writes that a baraita concerning this is brought in the first chapter of Arakhin 7a, and the Rambam rules according to the baraita's plain meaning. It also appears at the end of chapter Get Pashut. The interpretations offered there on Bava Batra 175b follow the view that an oral loan is not collected from heirs, but the halakhah does not follow that view, as explained above in simanim 107 and 108.
