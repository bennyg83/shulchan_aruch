The owner is exempt. Dayo caused the loss to his soul in his lap:
From the heirs of the pit owner. Meaning, not only if the digger is alive must he pay for the ox, but even if he dies, his heirs must pay the damage. Although an oral debt is ordinarily not collected from heirs after the debtor's death in every circumstance, this case differs because their father died at the very time the ox fell into the pit. It is therefore certain that he had not previously paid the value of the ox. See the Perishah.
