Whether one digs, etc. Mekhilta there: "the owner of the pit shall pay," including every owner.
(Collected note) Whether one digs, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 5: I know only one who dug it. From where do we include one who bought, inherited, or received it as a gift? The verse says, "or if he digs," etc. (End of note.)
Likewise if, etc. Bava Kamma 19b, where it asks who is liable if it became attached by itself, etc.
Or if another dug it, etc. Bava Kamma 48a.
Likewise if, etc. Tosafos there at the end of s.v. "although," and see what I wrote in siman 413, where the Ramban disagrees. See also Tosafos 49b, s.v. "in private property." The Rosh explains like Rabbeinu Yitzchak, and the Tur answers that the case there is one where the courtyard owner did not know. Hence the wording, "and it became known," etc.
(Collected note) Likewise, etc., and it became known, etc. Nimukei Yosef likewise explains Tosafos 49b, s.v. "in private property," and answers Tosafos' difficulty by saying that the owner did not know. (End of note.)
