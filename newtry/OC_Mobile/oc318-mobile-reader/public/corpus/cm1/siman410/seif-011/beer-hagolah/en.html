The wording of Rambam there, chapter 12, law 11, from Rabbah bar bar Chanah's statement in chapter 5, 51b. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam's text apparently read: a pit ten handbreadths deep, of which one handbreadth is water, is liable, because each handbreadth of water is like two handbreadths of dry depth. The Gemara then asks about a pit eight handbreadths deep containing two handbreadths of water: perhaps because it is not deep there is insufficient vapor, or perhaps the larger amount of water creates dangerous vapor. If you say the greater water creates vapor, what of a pit seven handbreadths deep containing three handbreadths of water? The question remains unresolved. Beit Yosef and Bach write that Tur follows Rambam, but that is not so in the Tur text before us. This is not the place to elaborate.
