Who disagrees. Sema suggests that the reason is that the rationale stated in the beginning of siman 129 for a guarantor at the time money is advanced does not fully apply here. There, the guarantor becomes obligated without a formal acquisition because the lender relied on him and parted with money on his account. Here Shimon did not part with money because of this guaranty. Shach objects that the Mechaber's words here and in Beit Yosef require study. In his view no authority disputes liability, because there can be no greater loss than that, relying on this person's assignment and guaranty, the original creditor released the assigned debtor. See Shach for the distinctions he draws.
