[Shach, letter 76] Nevertheless, he still owes him a loan. Note: See Tosafot, chapter HaGozel Etzim, page 99a, s.v. "Rather, with what does he betroth her?" Accordingly, one may say that the Rosh in his responsum accepts the second answer of Ri there, and there is no difficulty. Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer, siman 28, seif katan 38, likewise writes as obvious that according to Ri's reasoning there, one cannot transfer it through maamad sheloshtan. However, the view of the gloss there, which is from the Mordechai, appears at first glance to disagree with the Rosh and to maintain that one can transfer it through maamad sheloshtan. Beit Shmuel there was aware of this and reconciles it with difficulty. See there. This requires study.
