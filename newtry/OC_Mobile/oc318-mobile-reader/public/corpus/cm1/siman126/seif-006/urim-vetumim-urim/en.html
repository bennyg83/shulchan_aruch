He transferred him, etc. The Shach gives several distinctions in Mechaber's view. One important case is where witnesses establish the underlying debt and the debtor transferred payment to Levi. Without witnesses, if the debtor immediately claims in beit din, 'I already paid you, despite having transferred you to Levi,' he can be believed through a migo that he could have denied the debt entirely. Sema instead explains Mechaber as speaking where Reuven did not in the interim return to Shimon and say, 'Levi did not become obligated to me, so pay me yourself.' The Shach agrees with this point, and it is correct.
