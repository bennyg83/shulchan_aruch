To extract. We say that the substitute debtor paid the initial amount in order to uphold his word. Even if he knew that no enforceable obligation had arisen, he may have wished to preserve his credibility, and only now does he retract regarding the unpaid remainder. So writes the Tur. The Sema explains that the case is where he initially admitted the obligation not before witnesses, yet is still not believed to say "I erred" in order to recover money already paid. Had the admission been before witnesses, he would not even be believed regarding the unpaid remainder, because a person carefully verifies matters before admitting in front of witnesses. The Shach writes that Responsa Rosh does not require this reading and may speak even of an admission before witnesses. See there.
