And he is obligated to give, etc. The Rashba responsum cited by the Beit Yosef explains that the assignment is effective even against the will of the debtor or custodian. The dissenting view follows the “some say” cited by the Tur and other authorities, who hold that one cannot assign against the third party’s will. This creates a difficulty with the Mechaber. In seif 7 he rules without qualification that assignment is effective even against the third party’s will, and the Beit Yosef likewise decides so, yet here he cites the dissenting view. This requires study. The Aruch HaShulchan omitted the dissent, apparently for the reason I have stated.
