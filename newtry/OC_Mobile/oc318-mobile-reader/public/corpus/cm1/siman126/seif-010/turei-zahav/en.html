(Seif 23) A merchant who said. It appears that the incident was as follows. A merchant sold an article to a householder and, in a three-party setting, directed the householder to pay the broker. The broker undertook toward the merchant to bring him the money from the householder. The householder later withheld the money because of a claim against the broker. The ruling was that if the broker's undertaking was indeed as stated, he must pay the merchant, because it is as though he had already received the money from the householder. If the broker claims that the householder's allegation is false and that the withholding is theft, and that he never undertook to reimburse what the householder might wrongfully seize, he is exempt after swearing. This resolves Beit Yosef's difficulty.
