A transfer in the presence of all three parties does not work through an agent, such as where the giver sent his agent to the custodian to have the money given to the recipient. {Rama: Similarly, the guardian of orphans is treated as their agent and cannot transfer the orphans' money through a transaction in the presence of all three parties, as stated in Terumat HaDeshen 347.} If the giver tells the custodian in the presence of the recipient's agent to give the money, some say the transfer is effective, provided the recipient appointed that person as his agent. But if the recipient did not appoint him, and Reuven merely says to Shimon in Levi's presence, 'Give the one hundred of mine that you hold to Levi so that he will acquire it for Yehudah,' Reuven may retract.
