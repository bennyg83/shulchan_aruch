Except where there is concern for agunah. Above, where Rosh Chodesh is only one day, seif 4 states that some refrain from giving a get on Rosh Chodesh. Here, where Rosh Chodesh is two days and there is a dispute among the authorities, as Beit Yosef cites the views of Rashba, Ran, and Rosh, one should not write a get on the first day of Rosh Chodesh except where there is concern for agunah. The second day involves only an added concern, like an ordinary one-day Rosh Chodesh. Beit Yosef himself writes that even refraining on the first day of Rosh Chodesh is an excessive stringency, and that he saw people who did not take it into account.
