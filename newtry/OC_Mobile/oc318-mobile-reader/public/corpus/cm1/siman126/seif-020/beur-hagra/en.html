Ma'amad sheloshtan... This is learned from the incident involving Issur, where Rava said, "If it were by ma'amad sheloshtan..." Although he could have sent an agent, that would not have sufficed.
Likewise, a guardian. For he is only an agent, and in one respect even less than an ordinary agent, as stated at the beginning of the second chapter of Kiddushin 41b.
If he said... This follows from Bava Kama, beginning of chapter 4, "We are the hand of the poor," which is explained as operating through ma'amad sheloshtan. See Tosafos there. The Rif and Ritva in the second chapter of Kiddushin likewise write this.
Collected note: There is an authority who says... Maharshal disagrees, asking why the agent receiving should differ from the transferor, since here we do not say that a person's agent is exactly like himself. See below siman 105, seif 1, "even if he appointed..."
This is where he appointed him... The Tur writes so, deriving it from Sefer HaTerumos, which disagrees with the Rif and explains that in that case the funds had already been placed in Rav Yosef's hand. However, in Yoreh Deah 228 the Shulchan Aruch rules like the Rif. One may answer that beit din or a charity collector is different, as reflected by "We are the hand of the poor." See there, and below siman 290, seif 11, in the gloss.
