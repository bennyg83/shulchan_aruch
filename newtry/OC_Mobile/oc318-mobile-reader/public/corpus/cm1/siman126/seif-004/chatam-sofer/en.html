Ibid., Shach, seif katan 58, "clear, and examine carefully." Note: in my humble opinion, the words of Maharit are correct. Where no one is bringing a claim against the agent and we merely suspect that he has an interest affecting his testimony, it is reasonable to say that he is not interested, for what difference does it make to him whether liability falls on this person or that person? But where a claim is brought against him and he seeks to exempt himself through a migo, Maharit's position is sound. Understand this distinction.
