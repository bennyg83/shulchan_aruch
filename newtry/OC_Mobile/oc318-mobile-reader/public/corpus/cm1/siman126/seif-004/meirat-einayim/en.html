And if what he had with him was a loan, etc. The reason is that a loan is given for expenditure. At the time of the original loan, the creditor advanced the value with the understanding that the borrower would later repay fifty dinars from whatever property he then had, whether wheat or money. The wheat originally lent was valued in money and a definite monetary sum was fixed for repayment. Therefore, when he later says in the presence of all three, Give him fifty dinars in money, this does not prove that his original claim was specifically for money. A deposit is different, because a deposited object must ordinarily be returned in its original form. This reason is stated explicitly by the Tur. I have only expanded his language for clarity.
