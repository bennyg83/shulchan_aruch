From the wheat that you have, etc. The same applies if he says, 'Give him fifty dinars, for you hold a kor of wheat of mine.' Since he first stated what he has in the other's hands, it is as though he said, 'Give him fifty dinars from the wheat in your possession,' which is equivalent to transferring wheat worth fifty dinars. Baal HaTerumot and Maharit. Maharit adds that if he expressly says the other party should pay from his own money and hold the deposit merely as collateral until reimbursement, or says, 'Lend him this amount and you may rely on the deposit in your hands,' the recipient does not acquire through maamad sheloshtan. Shach.
