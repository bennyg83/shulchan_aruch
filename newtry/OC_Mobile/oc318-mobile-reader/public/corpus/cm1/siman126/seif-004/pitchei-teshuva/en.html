Shaar Mishpat writes that there is room for doubt if Reuven simply told Shimon, "Give one hundred to Levi," while Shimon held only fifty belonging to Reuven. Does Levi acquire half, because we presume that as to fifty Reuven instructed Shimon to give what Shimon already owed him and as to the other fifty asked him to lend it? Or, since regarding fifty Reuven's intent necessarily involved a loan, perhaps even this opinion concedes that we are concerned he intended the entire one hundred as a loan, in which case Levi acquires nothing.
