Reuven had, etc.; if they were, etc. See siman 253, seif 19, that even heirs need not give the money unless it remains in their possession in its original form. The Ran explains this from the Gemara's concern that perhaps the maneh was buried. This is the meaning of "Even if he says," etc.
But if he said, etc. As stated in the incident involving the theft, "Give," etc.
And if they were, etc. See Sema. As the Gemara says, "What difference is there between the original property and its proceeds?" This follows the explanation there that the basis of ma'amad sheloshtan is that it is as though he says to the third party, etc., although that explanation is not retained in the Gemara's conclusion.
Or wheat. Meaning, he is not specifically obligated to give money; anything of equivalent monetary value may be given. See there.
At the rate, etc. Meaning, he need not give according to what the wheat was worth at the earlier time merely because the obligation attached then; rather, he gives according to its present market rate. See there.
