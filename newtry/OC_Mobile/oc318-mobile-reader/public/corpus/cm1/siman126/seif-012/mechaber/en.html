If Reuven tells Shimon, in the presence of all three parties, 'Give Levi the promissory note of mine that you hold,' then as long as Reuven has not retracted, Shimon must give it to Levi. If Reuven retracts, however, Shimon need not give it. But if Reuven wrote to Levi that he conveys the note and all its liens, and directed Levi to Shimon, with whom it was deposited, Reuven cannot retract.
