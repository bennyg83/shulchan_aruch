The Tur, following a responsum of Rosh, says a claim for unpaid rent cannot be transferred by three party arrangement before the rented object is returned, because rent is payable only at the end. The Shach objects that rent accrues from beginning to end and is therefore already a loan, merely not yet due. He compares it to an ordinary loan within its term, which certainly can be transferred. The objection is not correct. Bava Kamma and Kiddushin show that even according to the view that rent accrues from beginning to end, the employer or owner does not yet have an existing loan while the work or use continues. Rashi explains that when the object is finally returned, the accumulated rental obligation becomes a loan retroactively. Tosafot says similarly that until the item is returned the worker cannot use that accrued rent for kiddushin because no present loan yet exists. Thus the distinction between the views whether rent accrues from beginning to end concerns retroactive characterization once the service is completed. It does not mean that at every intermediate moment there is a present collectible loan. While the item remains out, there is no definite debt in the sense required for the language, "One hundred of mine in your hand." When the item is returned, the obligation becomes a loan retroactively. This is why the case differs from an ordinary loan before maturity. In a loan before maturity there is already a definite debt and only the time of payment is delayed. In an unfinished rental relationship, there is not yet a presently fixed debt at all. It resembles a ketubah, which can later relate back to its original document for purposes of liens yet is not a present certain debt while the marriage continues. Since three party transfer requires a definite present obligation, Rosh's ruling is correct. Beit Shmuel makes essentially the same distinction regarding kiddushin through benefit of forgiving rent. Until the work is completed, the relationship is not merely an ordinary loan within its term. This accords with Rashi and Tosafot and resolves the Shach's difficulty.
