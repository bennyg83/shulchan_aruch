For rental payment... See Bava Metzia 65a and 110b. This requires study, since we rule in the second and third chapters of Kiddushin, the ninth chapter of Bava Kama, and the first chapter of Avodah Zarah that wages accrue continuously from beginning to end, so they are a loan in his hands. If so, the transfer should take effect as a transfer of a debt. The statement that wages are payable only at the end is not relevant here, as Tosafos writes in those places. I also found that the Shach raises this difficulty and leaves it requiring study.
