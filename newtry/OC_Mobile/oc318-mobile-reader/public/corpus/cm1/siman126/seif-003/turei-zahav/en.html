(Seif 22) Since he consented. It appears that even the later view in seif 9, under which the recipient does not fully release the transferor, agrees here that he did release him. Under gentile law the gentile was already discharged from the transferor, and this may be learned from the following ruling that if the recipient is a gentile from whom the depositary cannot escape, the depositary is exempt. Here too the recipient himself caused the transferor's loss.
