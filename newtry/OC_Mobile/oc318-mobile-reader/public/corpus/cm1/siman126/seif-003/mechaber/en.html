If Reuven says to Shimon without qualification, in the presence of all three parties, 'Give Levi one hundred,' without saying, 'Give Levi the one hundred of mine that you hold,' and Shimon admits that he holds one hundred belonging to Reuven but says, 'Since he did not specify the one hundred he has in my possession, he did not mean that money. He meant that I should lend him one hundred and give it to you,' Shimon's claim has no substance. We presume Reuven referred to the one hundred Shimon held, and Levi acquired it. Some say Levi did not acquire it.
