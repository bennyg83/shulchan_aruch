The Tur cites the Rosh: Since transfer in the presence of all three is a rabbinic enactment without an apparent reason, we have only what the Sages expressly enacted, namely, 'I have a maneh in your possession, give it to so and so.' If the maneh itself is not mentioned, the statement appears merely like a guarantee undertaken after the original extension of credit, which is ineffective without a kinyan.
