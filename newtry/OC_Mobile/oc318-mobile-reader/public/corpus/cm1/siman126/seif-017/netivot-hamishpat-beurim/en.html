With Reuven's consent. See Shach, seif katan 74. Tumim writes that Bach's ruling is primary, and in truth Shach's words are difficult to understand. Shach objects that the broker must take the more stringent oath based on siman 121, but this objection is surprising, for siman 121, seif 5, explicitly states that in such a case he takes the more stringent oath. See my novellae there for the reason: because the debt was in his possession with power to discharge it, it is treated as though the other party's money was certainly in his possession.
