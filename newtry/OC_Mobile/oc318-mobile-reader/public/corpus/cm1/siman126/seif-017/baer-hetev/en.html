And became obligated. Meaning through a kinyan, a document, or another binding form that created a full monetary obligation. This therefore differs from the following seif, which concerns wages. The phrase "for a specified time" is stated as an even stronger case and because that was the actual event in Responsa Rosh. Certainly the same law applies if he undertook to pay immediately. Shach.
Willingly. It appears that this means even though Reuven himself also agreed to cancel the lease, as in the case in Responsa Rosh. If the lease later ended for some other reason, even against Reuven's will, the obligation still remains because when he instructed payment the commitment had already arisen. This likely explains why the Mechaber later says simply, "even though the lease was later canceled." The Shach does not know why the Rama added the word "willingly." Perhaps he understood that voluntary cancellation is an even greater novelty, or perhaps the text should read "with their consent," meaning that the rule applies not only if Reuven canceled but even if the communal authorities agreed to the cancellation. See the Shach's further discussion of Responsa Rosh regarding a merchant who told a broker to collect money for him from a money changer, where he explains the responsum differently from later authorities.
Who said. The Tur writes in the name of the Rosh: Since Reuven became obligated to the community because the lease had been completed in a manner that could not be canceled without everyone's consent, Shimon acquired the right. If Reuven is a violent person who does not obey judgment, or has no assets, the community must pay Shimon, because the community was not released merely by the recipient's silence unless he explicitly released them. This follows the second view stated by the Mechaber in seif 9. Sema.
