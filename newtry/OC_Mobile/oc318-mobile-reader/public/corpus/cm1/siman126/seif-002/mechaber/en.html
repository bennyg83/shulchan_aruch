The foregoing applies when Reuven had one hundred zuz in Shimon's possession. If Shimon did not owe or hold one hundred zuz for Reuven, but Reuven said to him, "Give one hundred zuz to Levi on my behalf and I will repay you," Levi does not acquire it, and each of the three may retract. Even if the creditor forgave the debtor, he may retract so long as the matter was not effected by a kinyan. Rabbeinu Yerucham, Netiv 59. Even if Shimon paid Levi part of the debt, the arrangement is not thereby irrevocable. Nevertheless, if Shimon actually gave him the money, Shimon may collect it from Reuven, since he gave it at Reuven's direction. Rama: Likewise, if the recipient fully released the giver from liability, the person holding the money must give it to the recipient, for this is no less than an ordinary guarantor. Nimukei Yosef and Mordechai, chapter Eizehu Neshech.
