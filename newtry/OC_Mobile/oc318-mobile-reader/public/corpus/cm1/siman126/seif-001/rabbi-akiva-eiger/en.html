[Shulchan Aruch] The one hundred that I have in your possession. Note: I am uncertain about a borrower who says to the lender, "The fixed interest that I have in your possession, give it to so and so." Is a maamad sheloshtan transfer effective for this? One can say that since there is no ordinary lien upon the recipient, only a mitzvah obligation upon the lender to return the interest, which is why beit din does not descend to his assets, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 161, perhaps the acquisition of maamad sheloshtan does not apply. See also Mishpat Shalom, siman 9. The practical ruling requires study.
