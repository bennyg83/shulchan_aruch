Seif 1. 'Reuven had one hundred in Shimon's possession.' Note: See the Shach in this siman, who disagrees and holds that although wages become payable only at the end, the obligation nevertheless accrues continuously from beginning to end. See my work Avodat Avodah on Avodah Zarah 65b, in the addenda, where I brought a proof from the Gemara to the contrary. See there, for it is a clear proof, with God's help.
