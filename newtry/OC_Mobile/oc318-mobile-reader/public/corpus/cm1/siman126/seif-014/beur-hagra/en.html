If the transferor... As stated in Kesubos 19a, a lender is not believed to say that a document was a trust document where doing so harms others, since he lacks power to waive it. The same applies here.
Even if there is... See Beer HaGolah. As stated in the chapter HaDayanim regarding one suspected of violating a rabbinic oath, we do not institute one rabbinic enactment upon another. The meaning is that he collects without an oath, since he cannot swear.
If it is a gift... As above, because he is thereby prejudicing others.
