Beit Yosef writes that Rabbeinu holds that even according to the Rosh, whose view Rama cites in the gloss above in seif 9, that the transferor is not generally released, this case is different. Since the transferor caused the creditor through his testimony to lose the ability to collect the debt, the other party is already released from him.
