Reuven must bring proof. Even if Levi pleads uncertainty, Reuven must bring proof. Tumim, seif katan 14, writes that the claim 'he was wealthy and later became poor' is a weak claim. As Tumim proves, otherwise what benefit would there be in a written instrument? The usual argument 'why is your document in my possession?' has no application to such a claim. Rather, the claim is inherently weak and is believed only through a migo that he could have claimed payment. When Levi pleads uncertainty, he has no such migo. See Sema at the end of seif katan 42, where there is a scribal error. It should read that if he cannot swear to contradict the witness, he does not lose. See also Shach, seif katan 56, where the text should read: 'The first rationale was stated only...' In Shach, seif katan 57, the text should read: 'There remains no liability upon the transferor or borrower that is called guaranty...'
