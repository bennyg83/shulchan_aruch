If Levi claims that Shimon was poor and that Reuven deceived him, while Reuven says Shimon was wealthy at the time and only later became poor, then if Reuven's debt to Levi is documented, Reuven must bring proof. If he brings none and the document grants Levi credibility, Levi collects without an oath. If it does not grant him credibility, Levi swears and collects. If the loan was oral, Reuven is believed upon taking a heses oath that Shimon was wealthy at that time.
