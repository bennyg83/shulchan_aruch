The writing. Since a three-party assignment is ineffective through the assignor's agent, all the more so it is ineffective merely by a written instruction. There is then no actual presence of all three parties; the assignor merely communicates to the depositary that he should give the property to a specified person. Rivash. The same appears true if the recipient communicates only in writing, and indeed that is even weaker than a writing from the assignor. A three-party assignment is effective in the presence of a debtor's agent when the debtor's money is actually in that agent's possession on the debtor's behalf. Maharam di Trani I, siman 15. Knesset HaGedolah.
