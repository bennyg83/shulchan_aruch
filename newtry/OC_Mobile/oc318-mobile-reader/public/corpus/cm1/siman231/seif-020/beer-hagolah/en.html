Tav) This is the language of the Tur, seif 26, from a statement of Rav Chama in the name of Rabbi Yitzchak, Bava Batra 89a.
Alef) From the Rambam, chapter 14 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 2. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this distinction regarding necessities of life is not stated explicitly, but appears comparable to the rule there that one may not hoard produce in Eretz Yisrael when it is a basic necessity, etc. The Kesef Mishneh writes that anything used as human food is included among necessities of life. This also appears from Rambam's wording, where he excludes spices and roots, indicating that he excludes only items that are not human food at all.
Bet) A statement of Shmuel there on 90a.
Gimel) So writes the Rosh in the third chapter of Bava Metzia concerning the Gemara's statement on 40b that otherwise the amount exceeds one sixth, etc.
Dalet) So write the Rashbam and Rosh there.
Heh) In the name of the Ramah. This can likewise be inferred from Rambam at the beginning of chapter 14 of Hilkhot Mekhirah.
