And one eighth of a quarter. The Tur likewise has this reading. See the Derishah, where I wrote that Rashbam rejects this version.
So that it not be confused with a quarter-se'ah measure. People can confuse a measure that differs by a quarter, but they do not ordinarily confuse measures differing by a third. Therefore we are not concerned that a quarter-se'ah measure, which is one and a half kav, will be confused with a one-kav measure.
And one third and one quarter of a hin. Although people may err with quarters, since these measures existed in the Beit HaMikdash, Chazal did not protest their use in ordinary commerce either. In the Beit HaMikdash there was no concern because Kohanim are diligent and would not make such mistakes. This is stated in the Gemara and Rambam.
