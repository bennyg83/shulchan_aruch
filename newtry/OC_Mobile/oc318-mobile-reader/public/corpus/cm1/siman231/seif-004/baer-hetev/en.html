By a quarter. People can err by one quarter, but they do not err by one third in measures. Therefore we do not fear that a quarter-seah, which is one and a half kav, will be confused with one kav. Ibid.
And a quarter. Although people can err by one quarter, these measures existed in the Beit HaMikdash, and the Sages did not abolish them for ordinary use. In the Beit HaMikdash there was no concern because kohanim are diligent and would not err. This is stated in the Gemara and Rambam. Ibid.
