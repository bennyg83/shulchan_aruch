Produce may not be exported, etc. The Rosh and Tur did not record this law, because they hold that the prohibition was enacted specifically in Eretz Yisrael for the sake of maintaining settlement there, and not in the Diaspora even in a place where most inhabitants are Jewish. Alternatively, in our countries, due to our many sins, a Jewish majority is generally not found, so this is treated as a law not presently practiced and therefore they omitted it.
