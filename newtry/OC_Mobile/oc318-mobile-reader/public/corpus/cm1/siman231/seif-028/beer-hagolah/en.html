The Maggid Mishneh cites a Tosefta: bakers may establish a rotation among themselves, etc. It appears to me that the term means rest or respite, namely: you rest on such-and-such day and I on another day, etc. This is also explained in the Gemara there at the beginning of 9a in the incident involving those butchers.

(b) There in G-d in the works of the city
The Kesef Mishneh writes in the name of the Rivash that with municipal enactments this applies even without the approval of an important person. This also appears from Rambam, who mentions the requirement of an important person only for craftsmen's guilds. He explains the reason at length.
