Craftsmen are permitted, etc. Bava Batra 9a. The Tosefta teaches that wool merchants and dyers may agree that every purchase coming to one of them shall belong to all as partners. Bakers may also establish turns among themselves. Ramban explains that this means dividing selling times: do not sell during my allotted period and I will not sell during yours.
And the rule concerning members of, etc. Ramban writes that all craftsmen within their trade have the same status as members of the city. This also appears from the Tosefta, for otherwise by what legal mechanism would they acquire a share in every purchase?
Or of, etc. This appears from the Gemara there, in the challenge of Rav Yeimar, etc., and its answer.
