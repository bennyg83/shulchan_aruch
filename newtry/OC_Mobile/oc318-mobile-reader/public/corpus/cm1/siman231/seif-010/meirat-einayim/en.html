Weights should not be made, etc. Rambam in chapter 8 of Hilchot Geneivah explains that metals develop rust and thereby lose weight. According to that reason, one must be careful even with weights used for dry goods. Rabbeinu Tam explains instead that moisture sticks to metal, dries upon it, and makes it heavier. That is the basis of the alternative view permitting metal weights for dry goods.
