"The Rambam has a name from the 'distribution'."
The language of the Tur, seif 18, according to his explanation of the baraita there: twelve handbreadths are the length of the pole and twelve are the length of the cords. This is unlike the Rashbam's explanation there. Rambam merely records the words of the baraita. The Beit Yosef writes that the Tur's interpretation appears correct, for otherwise the baraita should have explained rather than stated it without qualification.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that because the baraita there states, concerning gold, only the pole, while he does not know the required length of its cord, Rambam therefore wrote as he did.
