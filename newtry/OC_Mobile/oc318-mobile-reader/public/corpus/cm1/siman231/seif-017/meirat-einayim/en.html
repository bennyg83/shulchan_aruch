The four cubits adjoining the irrigation ditch are measured leniently. This is the language of the Gemara in Bava Metzia 107b: Rav Yehudah said that with respect to the four cubits alongside an irrigation channel one may be lenient, but alongside the river itself one should not measure precisely at all. Rashi explains that an irrigation channel is drawn from the large river through the fields across the valley. The owners of each field refrain from sowing along its banks for a width of four cubits so that its edge will not be damaged. They measure the entire strip together and mark it so that they will not plow beyond the mark. By contrast, beside the river itself one does not measure precisely, but leaves an estimated width so that it is visibly intact and broad.
