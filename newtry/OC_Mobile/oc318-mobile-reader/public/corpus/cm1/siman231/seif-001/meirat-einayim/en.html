Or even to a non-Jew. Although with respect to ona'ah the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 226 that one does not violate the prohibition in a transaction with a non-Jew, false measurement is different. Ona'ah contains the term "your fellow," and there is also no ordinary theft where the purchaser saw what he bought. With measures, by contrast, the purchaser relies upon the seller's trustworthiness to give a full measure. See the Perishah for what I wrote further at the beginning of this siman and in seif 24.
