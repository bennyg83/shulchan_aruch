Alef) This is explicit in the Torah. Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilkhot Geneivah, halachah 8.
Bet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that the law regarding a gentile is stated in Bava Kamma 113b, where the Amoraim teach that stealing from a gentile is forbidden. False measurement is itself a form of theft, as appears at the beginning of the fifth chapter of Bava Metzia 61a. I saw in Yalkut, Parashat Tzav, at the end of siman 504, in the name of Tanna Devei Eliyahu, an incident in which a man related that he cheated a gentile when measuring dates that he sold him. He afterward bought oil with all the proceeds, the jug broke, and the oil spilled. The teaching concludes: Blessed is the Omnipresent, before Whom there is no favoritism. The verse says, "You shall not oppress your fellow and you shall not steal." Theft from a gentile is theft.
