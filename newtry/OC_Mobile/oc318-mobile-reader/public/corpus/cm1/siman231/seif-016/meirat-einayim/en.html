In writings of geometry, etc. This is Rambam's wording at the beginning of chapter 8 of Hilchot Geneivah. Maggid Mishneh explains that "writings of gematria" means the science of measurement and calculation. Concerning this they said that calculations of seasons and gematriot are appetizers to wisdom. The meaning is that they are appetizers to the science of astronomy, which is called wisdom and understanding. These are his words.
