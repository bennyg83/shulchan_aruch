With eggs, up to double. Rashbam gives two reasons. First, eggs are not so essential to basic sustenance. The Tur and Maggid Mishneh likewise write this, unlike the Beit Yosef. Second, for a poor person who travels from town to town buying eggs, the labor is substantial and the profit small.
