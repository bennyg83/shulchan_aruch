Since they did not similarly state, regarding the rule in the preceding seif prohibiting commerce, that the same applies outside Eretz Yisrael in a place with a Jewish majority, it implies that they maintain that there it is permitted even under such circumstances outside Eretz Yisrael. Since the Sages set a limit that one may not profit more than one sixth, commerce itself is permitted. This also explains why the Rosh and Tur omitted that other rule. It is further evident from their statement that one may not profit twice on eggs, implying that on other items it is permitted, as the Rashbam writes at the end of the chapter HaSefinah. See there.
