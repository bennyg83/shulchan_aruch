One may not store up, etc., where the majority are Jews. The Tur and Rosh also write this. Since they did not similarly qualify the preceding rule that one may not trade in essential foodstuffs by saying that the same applies outside Eretz Yisrael where the majority are Jews, it appears that they hold that even there such trading is permitted outside Eretz Yisrael. Since the Sages fixed the limitation that one may not profit by more than one sixth, commerce itself is permitted. This also explains why the Rosh and Tur omitted the baraita that would prohibit such trade entirely. It is further supported by their statement that one may not profit twofold in eggs, implying that with other commodities ordinary profit is permitted, as I wrote in Rashbam's name. This requires study.
