Alef) This is the language of the Tur.
Bet) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilkhot Gezelah VaAveidah, halachah 4, from the Mishnah in the second chapter of Bava Metzia 30b.
Gimel) There in the Gemara on 31b.
Dalet) The Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of the Rashba that if one returns a lost object at a time when work is plentiful and is delayed long enough that he could have earned one sela, he is not paid according to that high wage. Rather, he is paid according to the wage he could earn at a time when work is scarce while otherwise sitting idle, for example half a sela. Rabbenu Chananel, the Raavad, and the Rif explain this way, and the Ramban agrees.
Heh) This is explicit in the Mishnah there in Bava Metzia 30b.
Vav) There in the Mishnah.
