Except with the borrower’s consent. With the borrower’s consent a replacement may be written. Rabbeinu Yerucham says that it may be written with the consent of both parties. One might object that this creates a risk of collusion where the borrower owes other creditors and causes two instruments to be written so that this creditor receives two shares. The case may therefore be one where a single instrument states that if other debts reduce the creditor’s distributive share to fifty, the instrument gives him no additional recovery. Alternatively, we do not ordinarily suspect collusion because the lender generally does not know of the borrower’s other debts. Such suspicion is raised only where the document’s loss creates an independent defect, such as when it was found in the marketplace.
