In this circumstance. It appears that the same rule applies if there are witnesses that after the seller sold the property to the challenger, the seller himself lived there for one day. The present possessor is then believed that his own purchase preceded the challenger's purchase, through a migo that he could have claimed that the seller later repurchased the property from the challenger. This is explicit in Chiddushei Ramban. Shach.
Believed. Even if his witnesses testify only to three years of possession and no more, he is believed. Although the seller later sold the property to another by document, he is believed that his own purchase came first. The later sale was therefore wrongful and does not constitute a valid protest. Shach.
