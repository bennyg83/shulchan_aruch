An agent. Even if he says, 'I purchased it from you, not through an agent,' he is believed by virtue of a migo. The Tur writes likewise in the name of the Ramah. Outside a time of emergency, even if he says, 'I bought it from you on such and such a day,' he need not bring proof, because of the migo that he could have said, 'On another day I was together with you,' or, 'You transmitted the matter to me through an agent,' and the like. From the phrase 'on another day I was together with you' it appears that even if witnesses establish that the claimant was not here at all, he can say that he himself was at the objector's location and purchased it from him there.
