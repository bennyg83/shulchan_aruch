Maggid Mishneh writes in the name of some authorities that proof concerning the final three years is not enough to believe his claim that he occupied for seven years through a migo that he could have claimed, "I bought it from you and possessed it for the years of chazakah." There is no migo, because when he gave his initial response he did not know the nature of the challenge being made against him. Rambam's wording appears to indicate this, and this is the principal view. Tur writes likewise.
