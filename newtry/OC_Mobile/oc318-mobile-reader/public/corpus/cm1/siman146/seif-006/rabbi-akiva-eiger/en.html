[Rama] Or the borrower are believed. Note: Even if the seller claims, "I purchased it from so-and-so," and that person did not reside in it even one day, so that according to the seller's own claim this is a chazakah without a supporting claim and the challenger should prevail, the seller is nevertheless not believed by his claim to the detriment of others. This is explicit in the cited responsum of the Rosh. See Responsa Lechem Rav, siman 128.
