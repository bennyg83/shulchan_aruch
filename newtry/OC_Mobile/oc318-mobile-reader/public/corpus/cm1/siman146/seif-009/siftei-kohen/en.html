If he produced witnesses, the property returns, etc. The Tur writes that he swears and recovers, and the Sema quotes this. The reason for this oath appears to be that he seeks to remove land from one who possessed it for three years, and is therefore considered one who takes property. One who takes property must swear even in response to an uncertain claim, as stated above in simanim 91, 357, and 375. Responsa Maimoniyot, Sefer Kinyan, siman 7, likewise notes that many cases require one who collects to swear even on an uncertain claim. See there, and below, siman 422, Sema seif katan 10. The Sema suggests that the Mechaber relied on his earlier ruling concerning a protester against a possessor who must swear, though perhaps the cases can be distinguished. Had the Sema seen the Mechaber's words in Bedek HaBayit, he would not have written this, for the Mechaber states there that he does not know the reason for this oath. See Responsa Rabbi Shmuel Kohen, volume 2, siman 78.
