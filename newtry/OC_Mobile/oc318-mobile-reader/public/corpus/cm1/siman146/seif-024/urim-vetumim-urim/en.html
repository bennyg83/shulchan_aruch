Sema says this opinion does not follow Rif and Rambam's explanation of the Gemara's statement, 'I was in the inner rooms,' but rather Rashbam and Rosh. I do not agree. The Shulchan Aruch immediately brings another opinion limiting the rule to a time of emergency, which shows that the first opinion can still follow Rif and Rambam but understands their Talmudic case as one where a specific day was named. If no specific day is alleged, the possessor need not clarify further. See Tumim for the reasoning.
