The language of Rambam there, chapter 15, law 6, from the case at the beginning of Bava Batra 31a where each says, "It belonged to my ancestors," as Abaye said to Rava.
Rama inserted the Tur's words into Rambam's ruling, but Maggid Mishneh did not explain Rambam this way. He writes: why do we not advance for the one who has witnesses to chazakah the claim that his ancestors bought the property from this challenger or from the challenger's ancestors, since we normally advance claims for an heir? Some answer that here there is no proof that his ancestors possessed the land even for one day. Others say that whenever an heir himself makes a definite claim of any kind, we do not advance a different claim for him, and Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the principal explanation. Tur writes similarly in seif 39. In my humble opinion, the Mechaber's later wording, "or he said it belonged to my ancestors who bought it from your ancestors," follows Maggid Mishneh's explanation.
