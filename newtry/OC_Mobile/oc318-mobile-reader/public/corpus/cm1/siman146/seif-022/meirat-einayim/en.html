They are the years themselves who have testified about them. For years after this, the judgment gives that it is given to those who have held it in the last G-d if it is now in the other hand:
We leave it in their hands, etc. 'In their hands' is not literal, because the case is one where neither person is presently in physical possession. Rather, the meaning is that the property remains under the legal authority and claim of both parties, and whoever proves stronger prevails.
And all the overcomers fell in it. "We were a man who was given to him," he said, "that there is no evidence for each of them, and that the third one of them does not have any evidence, and that the third one of them does not have any evidence, and that it is the case of the third, and that it is not the same as any of them."
