[Shulchan Aruch] For we are concerned that they taught him to make a false claim. Note: Tosafos are uncertain about this. It is possible that where he says, "It belonged to my ancestors, who purchased it from your ancestors," so that his later words contain no real contradiction to his earlier words, the explanation is effective even after he left beit din. The language of the Rashbam appears to hold plainly that it is effective even in such a case. See there.
