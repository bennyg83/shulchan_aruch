The law of a purchaser and creditor, etc. A purchaser or heir need not prove that he is in fact a purchaser or heir. Once he possessed the property for three years, that suffices and beit din advances the proper claim on his behalf. The Tur, seif 27, writes this in the name of Ramban and the Rosh, and the Maggid Mishneh agrees in chapter 14 of Hilchot To'en. See Responsa Rabbi Shmuel Kohen, volume 2, simanim 183 and 232, and Responsa Maharit, siman 9.
The same applies if he established possession, etc. See the Bach, Responsa Maharan Sasson, siman 41, and the end of Masat Binyamin. Citation references: 40.
