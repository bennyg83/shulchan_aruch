A protest before two. The Shach writes that even if the two witnesses heard the protest separately, they can combine. He cites Nimukei Yosef in the name of Raah and rules this primary. See Tumim. I do not agree readily. Beit Yosef cites Responsa Rashba that separate witnesses do not combine, and it is surprising that the Shach ruled simply against him. Although I found a possible reason supporting the Shach, it remains strained, especially because Rosh may agree with Rashba. The matter requires study.
