And the law. A purchaser or heir need not bring proof that he is in fact an heir or purchaser. Once he has possessed the property for three years, that is sufficient, and beit din advances the appropriate claim on his behalf. The Tur writes this in the name of the Ramban and the Rosh, and the Maggid Mishneh agrees in chapter 14 of Hilchot To'en. See Responsa Rashach, part 2, simanim 183 and 232; Responsa Maharit, siman 9; Responsa Maharshdam, siman 255; and Responsa Mabit, part 2, siman 211, that we do not advance such a claim for a renter.
