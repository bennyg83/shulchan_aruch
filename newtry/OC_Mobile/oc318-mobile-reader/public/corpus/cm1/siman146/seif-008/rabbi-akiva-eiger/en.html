[Shulchan Aruch] He resided in it even one day. Note: If he did not reside there even one day, but the seller appears before us and claims that he purchased it, one may say that the present possessor has a valid chazakah. The requirement that the seller have resided there one day may apply only when beit din advances the claim on behalf of the purchaser. See Nimukei Yosef and what I copied in my gloss above, siman 140, seif 8, which does not appear to support this. The practical law therefore requires study.
