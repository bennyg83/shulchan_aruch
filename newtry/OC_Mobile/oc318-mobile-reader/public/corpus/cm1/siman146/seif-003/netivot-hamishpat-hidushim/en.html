They will tell others. He did not say to him, but others did not warn him
It is not a protest. Even if the witnesses violated the owner's instruction and actually told the possessor that he had protested, once they report that the challenger told them not to let the matter become known, chazakah remains. See Beurim: even if they did not tell the possessor that the challenger had said this, the underlying act still was not a valid protest. It is comparable to two people falsely telling the possessor that the owner protested when in fact he did not. Logically, chazakah remains.
