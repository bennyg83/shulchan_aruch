So that they will tell others, etc. For he told them only not to tell the possessor himself. He did not warn them not to tell other people.
A matter that people do not ordinarily take care to remember, etc. Meaning, whenever a matter is not something that a person must attend to, and the two alternatives are equal in his eyes, whether to do it or not, or whether to say it or not, he may later state whichever alternative happens to come to his mouth without having consciously fixed his attention on one side rather than the other. See the Perishah.
