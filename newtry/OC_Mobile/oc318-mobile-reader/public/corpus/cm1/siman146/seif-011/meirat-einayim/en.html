And at that time I was not in the country, etc. See above, siman 140:13, where I wrote that the Rif and Rambam explain in this manner the phrase, 'I was in the inner neighborhood,' stated at the beginning of chapter Chezkat HaBatim. The Rashbam and Rosh explain the phrase instead to mean, 'I was living with you in the house.' The first opinion stated by the Mechaber here, which requires the possessor to prove that the challenger was in the country at the relevant time, follows the Rif's explanation. In the Gemara the possessor was required to bring proof without qualification, implying that this is required in every case. The alternative opinion cited by the Mechaber does not interpret 'the inner neighborhood' that way, and therefore holds that if such a case arises independently, the possessor need not bring proof except when he himself specified the particular day on which the challenger sold him the property. According to the second alternative opinion, this requirement applies specifically in a time of emergency. See the Perishah, where I explained the Tur's presentation of all these opinions.
