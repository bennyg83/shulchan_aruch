And they should write in the wording, etc. Meaning, the expression 'he caused us to testify' is language of commissioning, that he caused us to bear witness to this matter. Although he did not expressly command them to write, nevertheless, since he designated witnesses for the matter and acted so that the matter would become known, presumably his intent was that they write it, because writing it in a document publicizes the matter. Therefore they should not write, 'So and so sent us,' or, 'made us agents,' because in truth they are not actual agents, for if they wish they may remain silent, and nevertheless it is a valid protest, as explained above; see Sema.
