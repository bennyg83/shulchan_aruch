The language of the Tur from the conclusion of the Gemara, Bava Batra 38a.
As Rav Nachman says there at the end of 39a, and the halachah follows him in monetary law.
There on 38b in the name of Rav, according to Rashbam. The Rosh writes that although Shmuel disagrees with Rav and the halachah generally follows Shmuel in monetary law, on this point the later Amoraim follow Rav.
The Gemara there says: your friend has a friend, and your friend's friend has a friend, meaning that information naturally spreads.
