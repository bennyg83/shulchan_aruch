A protest made before two people may be written even if the owner did not tell them to write it. {Rama: They must write in the language of agency: 'So-and-so caused us to attest concerning himself, so that this protest should be written for him.' They must not write, 'We heard that he protested,' because that would be written hearsay.} Once he protested in the first year, he need not protest again every year. But fewer than three complete years must elapse between one protest and the next. If he waited three years between protests, the earlier protest is ineffective.
