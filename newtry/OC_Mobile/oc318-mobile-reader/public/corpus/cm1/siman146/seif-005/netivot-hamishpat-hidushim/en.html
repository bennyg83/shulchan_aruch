Before two. Even if the protest was made before them separately, their testimony combines. Shach:
He did not tell them to write it. Although ordinary documents may not be written except with the consent of the person against whom they operate, here the Sages enacted that a protest may be preserved through even minimal testimony in order to defeat a chazakah. Sema:
So and so appointed us as witnesses. The word "appointed us as witnesses" denotes agency. Even if he did not expressly tell them to write, once he designated them as witnesses it is presumed that he intended them to record it. Nevertheless, they should not write, "He made us his agents," because they are not actual agents and may remain silent if they wish. Even so, the protest is effective. Sema:
From their writing. But if they write, "So and so appointed us as witnesses," which is document language, it is effective like any ordinary document, where written testimony is effective. According to Rabbeinu Tam, cited by the Tur in siman 28, that witnesses who are able to speak may testify from their writing, they can write, "We heard." Sema. See the Beurim. Even according to Rabbeinu Tam, they should specifically write, "So and so appointed us as witnesses," because perhaps the witnesses will die. We require that the witnesses be fit to testify orally at the time the writing reaches beit din. Tumim writes that after the fact even the wording "we heard" is valid.
