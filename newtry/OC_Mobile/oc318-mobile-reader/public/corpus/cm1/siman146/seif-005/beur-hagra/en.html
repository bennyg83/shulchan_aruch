They should write in the language... Tosafos there asks what novelty there is and answers that the witnesses may record it in the form of a document even though he did not explicitly say, "Write."
But not... Tosafos explains that this is a special enactment. The novelty is precisely that it may be written with documentary language, because the Gemara says he need not say, "Write," showing that ordinary testimony rules alone would not have sufficed.
