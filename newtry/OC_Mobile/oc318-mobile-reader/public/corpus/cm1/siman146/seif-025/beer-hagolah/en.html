The language of Rambam there, chapter 15, law 8. Maggid Mishneh cites the incident on Bava Batra 32a-b where one said to another, etc. Rabbah said: why should he lie? He could have produced a valid document, etc. The Gemara rules like Rabbah in the land case, leaving the property where it currently stands. Rambam explains the case as one where the present document is authenticated.
There in a responsum it is explained that proof was brought from the town ledger showing that the acquisition had been canceled.
