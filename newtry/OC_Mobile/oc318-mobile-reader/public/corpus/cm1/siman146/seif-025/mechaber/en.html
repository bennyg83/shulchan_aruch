If the protesting claimant brings witnesses that the field belongs to him, while the person occupying it claims, 'I purchased it from you, and here is my deed,' and produces an authenticated deed, and the claimant says the deed is forged, and the holder of the deed admits, 'That is true, but I had a valid deed that was lost, and I obtained the one now in my possession only to intimidate him into admitting that he truly sold it to me,' the holder is believed. Since he could have stood by his authenticated deed had he wished, the field is not removed from his possession, and he takes a heses oath. {Rama: If a person possessed property for the years establishing ownership, and the protesting claimant brings proof that the sale was canceled, the possession is nevertheless valid. The possessor may have purchased it again afterward. Furthermore, a written cancellation of this kind does not annul the sale until the parties make a complete new kinyan.}
