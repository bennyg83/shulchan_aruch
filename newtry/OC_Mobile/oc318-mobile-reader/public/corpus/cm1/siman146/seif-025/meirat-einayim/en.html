The Mechaber follows the language of the Rambam in chapter 15 of Hilchot To'en, where the case is stated with an authenticated document, and therefore uses that formulation here as well. The Tur, however, writes the greater novelty, that even if the document has not yet been authenticated but can be authenticated, the same rule applies. See the Perishah.
