The responsum states in summary that the city register recorded that the acquisition was canceled, but since a kinyan had already been made it was not actually canceled until the original recipient transferred it back through another kinyan. We rule that one who gives a gift and later merely returns the gift deed has not thereby returned the gift until a new acquisition is made. See siman 243. Sema.
