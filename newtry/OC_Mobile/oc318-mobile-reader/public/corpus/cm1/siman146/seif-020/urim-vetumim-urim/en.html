The law of a purchaser and a creditor who collected land. See Tumim. Rama's precise wording says a creditor 'who collected' land. Only after the creditor is already in possession do we make claims for him comparable to a purchaser. Before collection, because we rule that a creditor acquires only prospectively, he is still seeking to extract and we do not make the same claim for him. Responsa Maharshal seems to extend the rule even before collection, but I concluded that Rama's precision is correct. This also resolves an apparent contradiction between two Responsa Rosh.
