But when the first one sold it, etc. One need not protest. These are Responsa Rashba's words. See Tumim. I showed that Rashba's proof depends on the view that a new protest is required every three years so the possessor cannot later claim, 'After your earlier protest you sold it to me.' This is one of the interpretations cited by Rashbam and also followed by Rabbi Zerachiah HaLevi. That explains Rashba and answers Maharit. But Mechaber's adoption of Rashba without qualification is difficult, because Mechaber repeatedly rules against that interpretation in this siman, following Rashbam, Ramban, Rosh, Nimukei Yosef, and apparently Tosafot. The Shulchan Aruch's ruling therefore requires study.
