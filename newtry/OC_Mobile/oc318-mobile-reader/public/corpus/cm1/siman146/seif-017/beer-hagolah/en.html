In every such case where he merely spoke and did no act, we apply this rule. But if he performed an act, we do not, as stated at the beginning of siman 147. Sema writes that nevertheless he acted improperly by giving the other party advice for his own benefit, causing him to stumble and saving his own money at another's expense. To such conduct Chazal applied the verses, "Do not place a stumbling block before the blind," and "Do not mislead the blind on the road."
