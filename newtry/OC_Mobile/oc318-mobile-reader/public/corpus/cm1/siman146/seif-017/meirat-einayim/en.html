You are more convenient for me, etc. There are many instances in the Gemara and poskim where such an argument is legally effective. Nevertheless, in my view he did not act properly by advising another person in a way that benefits himself while causing that person to stumble and saving his own money through another person's money. Concerning conduct of this sort the Sages apply the verses, 'You shall not place a stumbling block before the blind,' and, 'You shall not mislead a blind person on the road.'
