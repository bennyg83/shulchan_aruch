Bar Kappara taught this at the end of 39. Rosh explains that by changing his story he admits that the first protest, made at the end of the first three years, was false, so the original three-year possession stood without protest. Rashbam says the same where two protests within three years contradict one another: by the second protest he admits the first was false, and the second is also ineffective because he has established himself as a liar. Rosh rejects this, because a person is established as a liar only by witnesses, and he may say he erred in his first protest. Tur.
