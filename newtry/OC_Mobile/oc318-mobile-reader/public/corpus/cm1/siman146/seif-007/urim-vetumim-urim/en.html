And if he claims against me that I sold it, etc. This is Rambam's language. According to Rama's earlier opinion that the protest need not say, 'Tomorrow I will sue him,' all the more so here. Indeed, where the protest alleges theft there is even more reason to mention future suit, so listeners do not ask, 'If he was truly robbed, why is he not suing now?' With a loan or rental claim, the protest concerns only preventing future possession and no such question is as strong. Sema.
