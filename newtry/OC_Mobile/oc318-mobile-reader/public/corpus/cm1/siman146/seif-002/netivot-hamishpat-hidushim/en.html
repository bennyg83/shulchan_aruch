He may say, "You did not protest." Even though one witness testifies to the protest, the possessor is not treated as one obligated to swear but unable to swear, because there is no Torah oath based upon one witness concerning land. Consequently there is also no separate oath obligation concerning the produce, because the produce derives from the land and if the land is his the produce is his as well. There is another reason: since the protest was not made before two witnesses, it resembles a meaningless joke, and the possessor may say that he therefore did not preserve his deed. Thus, even if the parties dispute only the produce, the possessor is believed. Sema, Shach, and Beurim.
