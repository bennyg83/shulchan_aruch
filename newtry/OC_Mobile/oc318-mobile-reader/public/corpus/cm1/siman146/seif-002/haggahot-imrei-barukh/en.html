(Rama, seif 10) From the woman, and she sold it publicly. Note: Beit Yosef cites Rashba as follows: 'One can say that if Shimon was in a place where he knew that the mother sold her houses to his brother Reuven, and they publicly announced in the synagogue that she had sold the houses to his brother, this supports a chazakah, for Shimon would not have remained silent. He therefore possesses the years of chazakah by virtue of this sale. All the more so if there is an enactment among them that anyone who does not assert his right within the period of the announcement loses that right,' etc. See Knesset HaGedolah, seif katan 26.
