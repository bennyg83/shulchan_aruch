Seif 8. One person came and said, etc. Note: See my Choshen Mishpat, third edition, siman 122. The ruling applies specifically where he says that the property belonged to so-and-so and was merely deposited with their father. But if he says only that he heard from their father that the father owed so-and-so a certain amount, he is not believed even if he could have taken the money himself. I explained the reason there at length.
