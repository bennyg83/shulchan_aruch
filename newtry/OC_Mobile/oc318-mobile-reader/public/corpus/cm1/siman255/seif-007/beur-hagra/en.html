Others say, etc. This is evident from the Gemara's conclusion, "he retracted, etc.," even without saying "give." The reason is migo, as Tosafos write in the cited s.v.: "they too could say, etc." The statement that they are not believed, etc., is therefore explained accordingly. This is also what Tosafos mean in s.v. "ela lo ba'i, etc." Certainly, according to the Gemara's answer, Rabbah's words prove that in every case they are believed to say "we paid," for otherwise there would be no migo. Rather, the Gemara should have stated the converse and would not have needed this. This is unlike the Shach, who writes that Tosafos's words in s.v. "ela" are merely a marginal gloss.
