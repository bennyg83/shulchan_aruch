If the deceased said, "I hold one maneh belonging to so-and-so," and the orphans say, "We paid it," they are not believed, because he did not say, "Give it," and there is no reason they would have known they were obligated to pay. If he said, "Give it," and the orphans say they paid, they are believed after taking a heset oath that they paid. Rama: Some say they are believed to say they paid in every case.
