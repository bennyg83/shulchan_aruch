Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Zechiyah, law 10, from Rabbah's statement concerning a dying person who says, 'one hundred to so-and-so,' etc., and the Gemara's response, 'which way does the scale incline? The opposite is more reasonable,' etc. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Mechaber agrees with his teacher Ibn Migash, who interprets the phrase accordingly. He further compares it to the rule, 'Go and give it to him,' in the first chapter of Bava Metzia, beginning of 17a. The Tur likewise writes in seif 6 in the name of the Ramah and agrees with it. Baal HaMaor writes similarly in the name of the Rashba, and Tosafot also write so there. I proved this because the Gemara did not simply say that the statement was reversed, but rather knew the tradition as stated. The Maggid Mishneh rejects this proof, explaining that the Gemara preferred not to say the statement was reversed because that would attribute to the first transmitter the extraordinary error of switching yes and no, which is uncommon. He concludes that he is unworthy to decide.
