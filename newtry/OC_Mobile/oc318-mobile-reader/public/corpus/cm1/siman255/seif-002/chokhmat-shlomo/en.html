Seif 3. A dying person who consecrated property. Note: See Shach, who discusses why, since we rule like Beit Hillel that consecration is subject to release, we nevertheless fear collusion. Regarding whether retraction within immediate speech, discussed in this siman, is Torah law or rabbinic, see my additions to Nezer Yisrael, siman 210, in my responsum to the community of Krakow.
