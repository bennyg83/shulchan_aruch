Bet) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Zekhiyah, halachah 3, from the statement of Rav and Shmuel there in the tenth chapter, Bava Batra 174b.
Gimel) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is explained by the Rif from the force of the sugyot.
Dalet) The Kesef Mishneh explains that the case can be where he admitted the debt before the claimant, or like the incident of Issur Giyora on 149a, where the concern that he was merely trying not to make his children appear wealthy did not apply because he had no heirs.
Heh) The Tur, seif 4, in the name of Responsa Rosh, rule 65, siman 1.
Vav) The Rosh explains that a person would not go to such lengths merely to avoid making his heirs appear wealthy.
