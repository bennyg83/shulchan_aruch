An admission. This is the Rambam's view, that an admission is effective even without saying, 'You are witnesses,' when he says it in the presence of the recipient, as is evident from siman 81, seif 14, where even according to the Rambam he must have admitted in the presence of the claimant. As for the Mechaber's ruling there in seif 8, that if he made a full admission he must pay even though the claimant is not present, I wrote there that the Rambam said this only insofar as he cannot claim, 'This never happened,' but he may claim that he said it merely so as not to appear wealthy. See there. The Mechaber rules here accordingly. Thus writes the Sema. His words are not compelling, and they contradict what he himself wrote in siman 81, that according to the Rambam, when one admits before the claimant he does not require the formal mode of an admission. This is also evident from the Tur there, seif 14, and I discussed this at length there in seif 14. See there. Shach.
