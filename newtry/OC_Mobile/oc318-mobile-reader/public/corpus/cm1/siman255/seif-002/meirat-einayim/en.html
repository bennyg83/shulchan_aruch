By way of acknowledgment. The Rambam holds that a full acknowledgment can be effective even without the words “you are my witnesses” when it is made in the claimant's presence. This is reflected in siman 81. Although the Mechaber there says that a complete acknowledgment can obligate even when the claimant is not present, I explained there that according to the Rambam such a statement at least prevents the acknowledger from later denying that he ever admitted, but he may still argue that the statement was made merely so that he or his children should not appear wealthy. The Mechaber follows the Rambam here, unlike the Rosh and Tur, who require “you are my witnesses.” Although the Rambam elsewhere holds that beit din does not ordinarily advance for a living acknowledger the defense that he spoke only to avoid appearing wealthy if he himself did not raise it, here the acknowledger is dangerously ill and no longer available. We advance for his heirs any defense their father could have asserted.
