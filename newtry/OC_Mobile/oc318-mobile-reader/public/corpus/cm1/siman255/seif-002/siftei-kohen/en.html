By way of an admission. The Rambam holds that a formal admission is effective even without the words, 'You are my witnesses,' when it is made before the recipient. The Sema writes this here and again in seif katan 15. His words are not persuasive and contradict what he himself writes above in siman 81, seif katan 33 and seif katan 41, namely that according to the Rambam, when one admits before the claimant there is no need for the special form of an admission. This is also evident from the Tur in siman 81, seif 18, according to the Rambam. The Bach's words at the end of this siman are likewise difficult. I discussed the matter at length above in siman 81, seif 14, seif katan 34. See there.
