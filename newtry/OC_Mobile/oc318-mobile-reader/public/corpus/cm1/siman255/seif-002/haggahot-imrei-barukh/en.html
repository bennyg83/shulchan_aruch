(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 2) s.v. 'Furthermore, he brought,' it appears to contradict the answer. Note: The Shach himself writes that where one transferred property to hekdesh through chalipin or another formal acquisition, there can be no retraction. There is therefore no need to establish the case as one where the assets were physically delivered to the treasurer. Rather, he transferred all his property to hekdesh through an act of acquisition and now says that he holds one hundred belonging to a certain person as a deposit.
