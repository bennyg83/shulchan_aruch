He came back to us and said to us, "Our father," and he said, "And he did not know that after his announcement, he did not distinguish between him and his father, and that he did not testify to him, and that his father had not been seen as one of them
But if he said, "Give it," etc., they are not believed. This requires study. The Tur writes in the name of Ri that this applies specifically if the heirs claim that he paid before making the admission. If they say that he paid after the admission, they are believed even though he had first said, "Give it." The Mechaber gives no qualification. From his wording it appears that in the first clause, where he did not say "give," he follows Ri, but in the second, where he did say "give," he follows the Ramah.
