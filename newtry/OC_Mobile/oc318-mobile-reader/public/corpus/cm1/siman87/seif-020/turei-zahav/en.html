(In the name of Maharshal) In Beit Yosef, beginning with the words “It is written in the writings of Mahari,” through “and we reverse it upon him,” the case can be found in the oath imposed upon partners, where he shifts the oath to the other party, requiring him to swear that he truly suspects him of such conduct, as the Tur wrote in seif 17. This appears correct according to those authorities whose view we follow, and Sema wrote likewise. Regarding siman 88, it is written in Mahadura Batra, part two: Rashi explained, etc., through “but if the defendant maintains his claim,” etc. This applies where he did not make the claim at a known time, for otherwise the Mishnah’s question would be difficult, as the Rosh wrote against Ramah. This requires study. Examine it carefully.
