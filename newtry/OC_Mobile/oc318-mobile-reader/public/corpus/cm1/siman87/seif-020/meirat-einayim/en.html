In any language that he understands, etc.
The Tur cites Rambam: 'Our teachers ruled that oaths are administered only in the Holy Tongue.
It is not proper to rely upon this ruling.
Although the custom in all Jewish courts is to administer even a hesset oath in the Holy Tongue, one must nevertheless inform the person swearing so that he understands the language of the oath.' The meaning is that even according to those teachers who require Hebrew, the person must understand exactly what he is swearing.
Thus an oath recited in words the litigant does not comprehend is not acceptable merely because the formula itself is in Hebrew.
The essential point is that the oath's meaning and scope be made clear to him so that his acceptance is knowledgeable and the warning has its intended force.
