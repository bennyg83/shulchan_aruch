And if there is... As stated in Nedarim 25a and Shevuos 29a, and at the beginning of Bava Metzia, "this one shall swear that he owns no less than three quarters." See the Rosh there.
Likewise, there are... As written in siman 15 seif 4; see what I wrote there.
Or to frighten him... See the Ran. Tosafos in Gittin 35a also writes this in the name of the Aruch, based on Vayikra Rabbah, chapter 10; see there at length.
(Collected notes) Or to frighten him... As stated in Vayikra Rabbah 6, they would administer an oath using inflated skins. The Geonim, cited by Tosafos in Perek HaKosev, inferred from this that such was the law for a Torah oath, but we do not rule that way. (End of collected notes.)
