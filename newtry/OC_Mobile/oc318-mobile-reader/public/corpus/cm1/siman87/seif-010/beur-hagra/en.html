(Collected notes) Some say that he is liable... The reason is that where one swears a rabbinic oath, we do not descend to his property, because money which rabbinic law does not extract is not extracted through another rabbinic mechanism, as with finds of a deaf-mute or minor and similar cases, and likewise rabbinic interest. The same applies to those who swear and collect: since Torah law would allow collection without an oath, the Raah rules accordingly. On this reasoning, however, it applies specifically to one who by law collects without an oath, such as a woman who has impaired her ketubah. See Shach. (End of collected notes.)
