[Shach, subparagraph 12, s.v. And it is difficult] And so wrote the Ran at the beginning of chapter Shevuas HaDayanim. Note: This is also explicit in the Rashba on Shevuos, in the discussion of one who lends his fellow money before witnesses. He cites a Tosefta that if witnesses testify that the borrower owes fifty, he is exempt from an oath. The Rashba explains that the witnesses testify that he in fact did not pay, and since the debt is secured by land there is no oath. Rabbi Eliezer’s baraisa, however, speaks where the witnesses testify only that he borrowed. See there.
