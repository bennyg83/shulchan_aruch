Where there is reason to suspect that the matter will later become clarified. This wording implies that he should not swear because the truth may later be established contrary to his oath, and his oath would then be found false. The Ir Shushan writes similarly, adding that if the claimant does not wish to wait, he should nullify all witnesses he has concerning this matter and may not later produce them in order to disqualify the defendant. But there is no reason to refrain from imposing an oath merely because it might eventually prove unnecessary. Perhaps the matter will never become known, and the claimant would lose his claim.
