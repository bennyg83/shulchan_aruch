To one. In Baer HaGolah the Sema's comments on this law are cited. See there. The Taz writes concerning them: It appears that his words apply only where one claimant sues many persons, each of whom is individually liable to him. If, however, he claims that they jointly owe him as partners, it is sufficient for one of them to swear and for the others to answer amen. This is properly considered an oath on behalf of all, because the claim rests on all of them together. If one is not liable, then none of them is liable, since the liabilities are interdependent.
