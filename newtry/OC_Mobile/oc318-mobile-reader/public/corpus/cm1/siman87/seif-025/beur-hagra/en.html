Yerushalmi, Perek Kol HaNishba'in, halachah 7: among matters concerning which no oath is taken are claims that are not monetary claims. Rabbi Yishmael derives from the verse that the claim must resemble the specified cases, namely a monetary demand. Thus one who says to another, "Give me two hundred zuz that you said you would give me and did not," is excluded; likewise one who says, "You cursed me or embarrassed me," is exempt; and penalty payments such as fourfold and fivefold payments are excluded because they are fines.
