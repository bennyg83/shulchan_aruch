[Sema, letter 60] Meaning, by virtue of what do we exempt him? Note: According to the Bach in siman 22, if one says, “I will not swear,” he can no longer reverse the oath and say that his intention was for the opposing party to swear. Accordingly, it may be explained simply that this is why the Mechaber writes “and he pays,” to teach us that as soon as they exempt him from taking the oath he must pay, and the claimant need not swear. The Sema, however, follows his own view above that the oath may still be reversed, and therefore he is forced into a strained explanation here. Examine this carefully.
