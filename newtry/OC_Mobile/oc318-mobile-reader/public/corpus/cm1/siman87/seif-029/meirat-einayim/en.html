And he is further established as suspected through two witnesses. In the Perishah I explained that two witnesses disqualify him immediately. But when there is only one witness together with the litigant to whom he swore, he is not immediately disqualified through them after the oath, because until then that litigant remains financially interested. Only after he has already sworn and been discharged can that litigant join with the witness, come anew to beit din, and testify that he swore falsely, thereby disqualifying him, as the Tur and Mechaber write at the end of siman 34.
