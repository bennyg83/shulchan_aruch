One who denied... See Beer HaGolah. Although there the Gemara speaks of witnesses who came afterward, the rule is the same, because an oath does not acquire the money for the oath-taker. Even though he contradicted the witness through his oath, the witness is subsequently believed. This is like the statement in Perek HaKosev, "If he is clever..." The Raavad explains it as applying after she already swore concerning her ketubah, as explained above in seif 14.
