[Shulchan Aruch] If the claimant afterward seized property belonging to him. Note: Likewise, where the defendant was obligated to swear and had a supporting witness, but the claimant seized without witnesses, the claimant is believed to contradict the witness. So write Responsa Avodas HaGershuni, siman 100, and Shevut Yaakov, part 1, siman 151. Where the defendant reversed the oath, the claimant swore, and the defendant afterward sued him saying, "I know that you swore falsely, and I now wish to swear," the defendant is believed by virtue of a migo that he could say, "I have now paid." Responsa Mekor Baruch, siman 22. See Responsa Tzemach Tzedek, siman 7, which appears to disagree.
