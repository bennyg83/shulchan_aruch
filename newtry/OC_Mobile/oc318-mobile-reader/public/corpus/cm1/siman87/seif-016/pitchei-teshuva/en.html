In a matter involving an error in an explicit Mishnah, the judgment is reversed. See Baer Heitev, and see the Shach in the passage beginning "and thus it appears to me," where he writes that he sees a further proof from what is taught at the beginning of Bava Metzia concerning that shepherd, and asks that if this were so, what would the Gemara's question be. In Rabbi Akiva Eiger's glosses he writes: "I am surprised. Even if Rabbi Chiyya's rule applies, he can claim: I am like any other claimant, entitled to impose a heset oath upon you. Since you are disqualified from swearing, I will take the heset oath myself without holding a sacred object." This is straightforward.
