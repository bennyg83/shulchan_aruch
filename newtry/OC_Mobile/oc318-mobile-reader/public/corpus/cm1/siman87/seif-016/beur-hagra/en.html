Two... Some say... and some say... Their proof is from Gittin, Perek HaSholeach, where the Gemara says that Rav concedes in a case where she jumped and swore on her own initiative. See Rashi there. The first view follows Rabbeinu Chananel, who explains that she is required to swear again, and there is no concern that people will refrain from administering an oath to her, since she has already sworn. Moreover, even according to Rashi this case differs, because here the oath concerns the future and that oath is not as severe for him, since he is not disqualified on account of violating it, as stated in Shevuos 46b. The second view follows Rabbeinu Tam in Tosafos there.

See Rashi in Gittin 35a, who explains that if she jumped and swore, she has already sworn and therefore collects without another oath. The Ran agrees and cites the Yerushalmi. Rabbeinu Chananel, however, rules that they administer the oath to her again. He brings proof from Bava Kamma 107a, although the Ran rejects the proof because there the oath does not qualify as one accepted by the owner, since the person jumped and swore on his own initiative.
