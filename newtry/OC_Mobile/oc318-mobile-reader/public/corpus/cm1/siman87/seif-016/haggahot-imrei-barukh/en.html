(Shach, seif katan 68) The borrower is not believed to say, 'You waived it for me,' etc. Note: One can say that there the claim of waiver is inherently weak. Why would the creditor waive the debt? He therefore cannot be believed without a migo. But regarding an oath, the claim is not weak. The defendant says that once the claimant saw he was prepared to swear, and the claimant knew that his own claim was false, he did not wish to make the defendant swear needlessly and therefore waived the oath.
