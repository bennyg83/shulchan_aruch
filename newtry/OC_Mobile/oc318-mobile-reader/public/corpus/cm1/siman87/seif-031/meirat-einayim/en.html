I paid, or I owe you nothing, and he swore, etc. This is where he did not initially claim, before swearing, that the claimant should nullify his documents, as explained above in siman 75:24.
And afterward witnesses to the kinyan came. At the beginning of siman 70 it was explained that one who lends to another with witnesses to a kinyan must repay him before witnesses, even though the lender holds no document. Although Rama writes there that some disagree, the Mechaber here follows his own position when he writes, "or by a document," meaning that the ruling follows each authority according to his view.
