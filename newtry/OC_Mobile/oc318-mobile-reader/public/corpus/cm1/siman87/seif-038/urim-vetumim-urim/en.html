That the claim appears fraudulent. The source case was a destitute person claiming that a very wealthy man had borrowed money from him, where the circumstances made it obvious that the purpose was merely to harass the wealthy person and force him to swear. Beit din has authority to exempt the defendant from the oath. However, Responsa Maimoniyot makes clear that this leniency was stated where there was no meaningful monetary basis for the claim, a situation in which Rabbeinu Tam in any event often ruled according to the more lenient second version of Rav Nachman. It was not stated where there is genuine monetary substance to the dispute. Mechaber's unqualified wording therefore requires study.
