(In the Tur, seif 10) Rashi explained that one who denies the entire claim is exempt by Torah law only in the case of a loan, etc. This is difficult, for Rashi himself explained, and Beit Yosef cites him below regarding the rule of “here it is,” that this rule applies specifically to a deposit, as Ran wrote in his name. It appears to me that there is a dispute in the Gemara, in the chapter HaGozel, between Rami bar Hama and Rabbi Hiyya as to whether partial admission applies to a deposit. Rabbi Hiyya cited above, who holds that one who says “here it is” is exempt, follows Rami bar Hama, who taught that the four classes of bailees require denial of part of the claim. As a matter of practical law, however, Rashi ruled in accordance with Rabbi Hiyya bar Yosef, who holds that the Torah passages are intermingled here.
