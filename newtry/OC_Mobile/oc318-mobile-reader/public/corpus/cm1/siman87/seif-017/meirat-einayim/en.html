The order of the judges' oath is as follows.
The Tur writes that a judicial oath, whether Torah or rabbinic, whether on a definite or uncertain claim, is administered with the one swearing holding a Torah scroll, etc.
The Mechaber abbreviates because seif 1 already included all these categories within 'the judges' oath.'By an oath or by a curse. Meaning, whether through an oath or through a curse, and whether the oath or curse is spoken by the person swearing himself or imposed upon him by another.
The legal form is effective in either manner when the person accepts it and understands its content.
