Because it is based upon a definite claim. Since the claimant stands before him and asserts with certainty that he owes him, there is some basis to suspect that this denier might swear falsely, and therefore they frighten him. This differs from a claim based on uncertainty, such as the oaths of partners and sharecroppers. Likewise, regarding an oath of hesset, although the claimant makes a definite claim, the oath is lighter, since according to all opinions it is at least taken without holding a sacred object, and therefore no special intimidation is required.
