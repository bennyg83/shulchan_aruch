If the defendant was suspected, etc.
This is the correct text.If the defendant was suspected of false oaths, he is exempt without an oath.
The reason is that we do not make an enactment upon an enactment against the defendant's will. The defendant's rabbinic oath was itself an enactment for the claimant's benefit.
Once the defendant is disqualified from swearing, Chazal did not make a second enactment that the claimant should be compelled to swear and collect against the defendant's will.
They did so only where the defendant himself chooses that the claimant should swear and collect.
The result depends on the kind of oath involved and whether the original obligation was Torah law or merely rabbinic.
