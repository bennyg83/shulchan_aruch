And so it is proper to rule. Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 80, writes that a supporting witness need not literally testify to the same factual account as the defendant. For example, the defendant says that he paid, while the witness says that the claimant admitted before him and another person in a complete admission that the defendant owes him nothing. Even though the defendant does not know whether the witness's statement is true, the witness nevertheless exempts him from an oath. Likewise, if the defendant says, 'I do not know whether I ever became obligated,' and one witness testifies that the claimant admitted before him and another person that the defendant owes nothing, while the defendant himself does not know whether the witness is truthful, the witness still exempts him from an oath.
