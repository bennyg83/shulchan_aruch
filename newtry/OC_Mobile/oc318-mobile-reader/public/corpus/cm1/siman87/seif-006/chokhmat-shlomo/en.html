Seif 22: Anyone who became obligated to take an oath in beit din, etc. Note: It appears that even according to those who disagree with the Taz regarding one who swears and collects, in the case of a widow’s oath against heirs the heirs certainly do not need to make the formal acceptance required in ordinary oath proceedings. We do not otherwise find such an obligation imposed upon heirs, and it is possible that the Sages did not impose this stringency upon them. Moreover, this is a case of one who swears and collects, and the Taz holds generally that the formal acceptance does not apply in such cases. Even if one says that it does apply to one who swears and collects because the Sages made no distinctions in their enactment, the widow’s oath is taken outside beit din and does not have the ordinary form of a judicial oath. In that setting the principle of making no distinctions does not apply. It therefore appears that we certainly rule like the Taz and do not require the heirs’ formal acceptance in this case.
