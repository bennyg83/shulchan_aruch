Proof. The Sema writes that nevertheless, if the defendant claims, "You waived the oath for me," he is believed. See siman 82, seif 11. This requires study, for the cases are not comparable. Certainly the borrower can require the lender to swear regarding such a matter, but that the borrower himself should be believed without an oath is unreasonable. It would also undermine the law, because every person would make this claim and no oath would ever be administered. Rather, he is not believed to say that the oath was waived except where he has a migo that he could have said, "I already swore." I proved likewise in siman 75, seif 7, that a borrower is not believed to claim waiver except where he has a migo. Here, since he cannot claim, "I already swore," because oaths are administered in the presence of the court attendant, he likewise is not believed to say, "You waived the oath." Perhaps, regarding a hesset oath, if he claims waiver he is believed because one rabbinic enactment is not reinforced by another. This too requires study. It appears more likely that the Sema means only that he does not require independent proof of his statement, but he must nevertheless swear that the other party waived the oath. Even this requires study, because perhaps such an oath does not help. His credibility rests only on the migo that he could have said, "I swore," and just as that claim would not be believed even with an oath in the present case, the waiver claim should likewise not be believed. One cannot say he should be believed with a migo that he could have said, "I swore before the court attendant," because if he made that claim he would not be believed even with an oath. It is as though the parties had accepted the court attendant as two witnesses. Even if one were to say that he would be believed with a Torah oath to contradict that witness, nevertheless, now that he claims waiver we do not apply such a migo, as I wrote in siman 75, seif 13, in the name of Tosafot and the authorities, that where one would have been compelled to swear against a witness, the alternative claim does not create a migo. See there. This requires study. Shach. The Taz also disagrees with the Sema on this point. See there.
