See also what I wrote above, siman 24, seif katan 4, in the name of Birkei Yosef, who writes that from Teshuvot Tashbetz, part II, siman 2, it appears that our case concerns one in which the judge recognizes that the claimant's purpose is to impose many separate oaths upon the defendant. The responsum of the Radbaz, part IV, siman 210, likewise implies that a judge should not compel a litigant verbally to state all of his claims at once unless he recognizes him as acting deceitfully and seeking to bring the other party to numerous oaths. Otherwise, he should not compel him; see there.
