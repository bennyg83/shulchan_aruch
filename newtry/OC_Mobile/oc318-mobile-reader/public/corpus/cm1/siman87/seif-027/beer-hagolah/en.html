The Tur, seif 42. So write the Rif and Rosh regarding Rabbi Yochanan's statement, 'One who owed his fellow an oath,' etc., Bava Metzia 17a.
To my father, if he had to say to himself in the oath that I would swear to you and sue for me and not a father, and then I swore that I would not say what I did not need to do with the D, and that it was not merely a rejection of thirst and not absolved and regretted by the G-d
This excludes those who swear and collect, who collect only with clear proof. There in Nimukei Yosef. The Tur writes that if one was obligated in a Torah oath and claims that he already swore, he takes a shevuat hesset. So too Nimukei Yosef in the name of Rav Hai, with whom the Ramban agreed, and Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 3 part 4.
