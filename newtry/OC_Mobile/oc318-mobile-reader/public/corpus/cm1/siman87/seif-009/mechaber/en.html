The following are differences between a Torah oath and a rabbinic oath. If one is obligated to take a Torah oath and refuses, beit din descends to his property and gives the claimant what he claimed. If one is obligated in a rabbinic oath and refuses to swear, then if he is among those who swear and are exempt, such as one who swears in response to a doubtful claim or takes a heset oath, he is placed under nidui for thirty days. If after that period he still does not come and seek release from the nidui, he is given lashes of rebellion and the nidui is released. Beit din does not descend to his property. Rama: It is permitted to call him a transgressor, because he violates the words of the Sages.
