If he is among those who swear and are exempt. The rule is stated specifically for a defendant who would otherwise swear and be exempt. For one who swears and collects, if he does not wish to swear he simply receives nothing. If a defendant was obligated in a hesset oath, refused to swear, and the claimant seized some of his property saying, "Either swear the hesset oath or I will retain what I seized," the property is not removed from the claimant. Responsa Radbaz, part 1, siman 216.
