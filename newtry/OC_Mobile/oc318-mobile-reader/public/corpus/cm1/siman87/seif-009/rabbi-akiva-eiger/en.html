[Shach, subparagraph 14] Nevertheless, an unspecified cherem is imposed upon him. Note: If without the witness there would have been only an unspecified cherem, one can say that a supporting witness exempts him even from that cherem. See Responsa Mekor Baruch, siman 52; Shach, siman 75, subparagraph 36; and Knesset HaGedolah, siman 75, glosses to the Tur, subparagraph 19.
