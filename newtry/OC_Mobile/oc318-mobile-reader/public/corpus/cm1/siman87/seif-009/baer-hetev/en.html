See the Sema, who discusses this at length and concludes: See what I wrote at the beginning of siman 82 in the Perishah, where it appears to me that the Rosh and Tur hold that one who possesses a validated promissory note, seized property of the defendant, and does not wish to swear, is not compelled to return what he seized, and that we do not even place him under a ban for this. In my humble opinion this is incorrect. Rather, according to all opinions we place him under a ban, as I wrote in siman 82, seif 6. See there. The Sema further writes that in the Derishah he cited the Ran as holding that only if he seized money do we not remove it from him, whereas if he seized movable property we do remove it. The Mechaber likewise rules explicitly in Even HaEzer siman 96, seif 11, that if she seized money we do not take it from her. Ibid.
