He concludes that, according to the Rosh and Tur, one who holds a validated document and has seized the defendant's property but refuses the required oath may retain what he seized. This conclusion is not established. The effect of possession depends on whether the oath is a condition of the claimant's right or merely a procedural safeguard, and on whether the seizure occurred with witnesses or under a claim of right. The authorities must be applied according to the specific category of oath.
