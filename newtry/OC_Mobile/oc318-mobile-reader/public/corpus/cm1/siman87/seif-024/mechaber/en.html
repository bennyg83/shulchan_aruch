If a person makes many claims against another, the defendant takes only one oath covering all of them. If he incurs two oaths on two claims, one less severe and one more severe, he takes the more severe oath and the other matters are appended to it. {Rama: If one claims that another owes him and says he has additional claims that he does not wish to bring now, we tell him to present them all together. If he refuses, and the defendant says that he owes him nothing, the defendant swears and is exempt from every claim the claimant had against him through that day. See Responsa Maimoniyot, Sefer Mishpatim, responsum 34.}
