And then he need not, etc. The entire reason that he must swear in the other party's presence is that he is more ashamed to swear falsely in front of one who knows his falsehood. This applies only when the other party knows that he is lying. Therefore, even if the witness died, he need not swear in the presence of the litigant, since the litigant does not know his falsehood. See Tumim, where I wrote that this applies only to one who swears and is exempted, but one who swears and collects must always swear in the presence of the other party. See there. If he swears on an uncertain claim, he need not swear in the presence of the other party, since he is not ashamed before him. The same applies when he swears by virtue of the oath of the Geonim, for he need not swear in the other party's presence. So writes Mahari Weil in responsum 154.
