(Shach, seif katan 63) He is demanding money from him, etc. Note: One can say that with a hesset oath we do not descend to his property, because the defendant is exempt through his denial and is merely placed under a ban to compel compliance with the Sages who imposed the oath. Once he claims that he has complied with the Sages and maintains his claim that he owes the claimant nothing, we do not punish him. This differs from a Torah oath.
