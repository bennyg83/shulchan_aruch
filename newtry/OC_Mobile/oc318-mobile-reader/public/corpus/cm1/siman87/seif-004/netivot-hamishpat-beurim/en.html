This is not my utensil. It is evident from here that if a person includes in his oath that this is the very utensil he is obligated to return, he must return that utensil, even though the claimant admits that it is not actually his original utensil. At first glance this resembles one who was sued for wheat and admitted barley, who is exempt even from the barley, and also the rule in siman 88, seif 13, regarding an admission concerning a utensil. The distinction will, with Hashem's help, be explained there.
