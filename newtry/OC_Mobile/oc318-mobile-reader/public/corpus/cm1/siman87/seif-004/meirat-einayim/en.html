According to the one who says that a pledge is considered immediate payment, it is no worse, etc. See Perishah on the Tur's words, 'Perhaps he holds, etc.,' where I explained the Tur's uncertainty. Rama's phrase, 'it is no worse,' is not conclusive. One can argue that it is worse because the defendant seeks to leave the claimant with property that is not his and that he does not want, whereas a pledge is intended to be redeemed from him.
