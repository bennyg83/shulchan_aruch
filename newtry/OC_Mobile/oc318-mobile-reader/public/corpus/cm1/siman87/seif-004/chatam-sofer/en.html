Note: See there for the reason, because any person may lessen the severity of the oath and say, 'Swear to me by the life of your head,' as stated in chapter Zeh Borer. It seems to me that his intent is that when Chazal instituted the oath of the Mishnah, the enactment itself included the possibility of imposing anything from that oath downward in severity, such as a hesset oath, an oath by the life of one's head, or a cherem, and all of these were included in the enactment. See Taz, siman 93, seif 4, and below, siman 290, seif 19.
