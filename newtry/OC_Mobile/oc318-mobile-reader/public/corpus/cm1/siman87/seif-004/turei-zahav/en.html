(Seif 10) “We do not remove it from him.” It appears to me that this means that money is not extracted from him until he takes the Mishnaic oath imposed upon one who collects, as in other cases of persons who swear and collect. Nevertheless, the other party may sue him and require a heset oath that he holds none of that person’s property, and we place him under a ban until he swears, as stated in seif 9 and above in siman 82, seif 7.
