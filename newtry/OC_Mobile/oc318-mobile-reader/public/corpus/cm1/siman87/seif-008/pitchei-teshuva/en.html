One who swears and collects. See Baer Heitev in the name of the Shach, who excludes a person suspected of false oaths. Otherwise, anyone who had one supporting witness could extract money from a suspect, and this is the reasoning of Responsa Maimoniyot. Noda BiYehudah, second edition, siman 27, writes that the Shach did not quote Responsa Maimoniyot accurately. See there. See also Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, siman 171, and Responsa Shemen Rokeach, part 1, end of siman 75.
