Every oath... As stated in Perek Kol HaNishba'in: "All who take oaths..." and the Mishnah enumerates three classes of oaths. Two groups are those who swear and collect on a definite claim, including a worker and the other cases there; another group is introduced by "and these swear..." Thus the text here says, "and among rabbinic oaths..." and gives examples such as the oath of..., and one who impairs a document....
And there are... in later generations... As stated in Kiddushin 43b: "Now that the Rabbis instituted a heses oath..."
And even though... The practical difference concerns holding a sacred object. An oath administered by beit din is taken while holding a sacred object, as is a Mishnah-level oath, but only when sworn before beit din, as Rashi explains in Gittin 35a. See below, seif 13.
