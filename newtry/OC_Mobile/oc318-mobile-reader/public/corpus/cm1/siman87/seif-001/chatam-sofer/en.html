Shach, seif katan 10, 'who teaches it on the latter clause.' Note: See there, 41a. It appears from the Ran that specifically with an oath instituted by the Sages he is exempt, but with a Torah oath he must swear where the ruling is subject to a dispute. However, if the judge himself is uncertain, he cannot obligate him out of doubt, even to a Torah oath. This is demonstrated by Tosafot on 40a, s.v. 'to teach its force,' etc.; see there.
