[Shulchan Aruch] If he is one who admits part of the claim, he is obligated in a Torah oath. Note: If one knows the truth himself, it is forbidden for him to accept the oath upon himself and say, “I will swear,” merely in order to frighten the other litigant, even if he has no intention of actually swearing, as it is written, “You shall not take,” and the Mekhilta expounds: “You shall not accept upon yourself.” So writes Rabbeinu Yonah in Shaarei Teshuvah, section 45.
