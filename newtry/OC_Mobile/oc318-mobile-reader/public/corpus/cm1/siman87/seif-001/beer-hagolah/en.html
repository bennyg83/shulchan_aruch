Mishnah at the beginning of Shevuat HaDayanim, Shevuot 38b. The Ran writes that if one claims one hundred, fifty as a loan and fifty for bodily injury, and the defendant denies everything, if the claimant has already sworn regarding the injury, this is considered an admission to part of the claim because we are legally certain he owes that amount. It resembles one who claims one hundred, the defendant denies all, and witnesses testify that he owes fifty, under Rabbi Chiya's first rule, Bava Metzia 3a.
Ketubot 87b derives it from the verse, 'One witness shall not arise against a man,' etc. If one witness testifies like the lender and another like the borrower, the law is explained at the end of chapter 2.
