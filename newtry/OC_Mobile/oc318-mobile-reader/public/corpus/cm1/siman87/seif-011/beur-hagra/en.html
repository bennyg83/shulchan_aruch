If there is no... Voluntary reversal of an oath is effective, as stated in Bava Basra 127b-128a regarding one who says, "You stole my slave..." See Rashbam there.
(Collected notes) Likewise, those who swear and collect... See the Rif in Perek HaKosev. The Ramban writes that even with a heses oath, which the original enactment placed on the defendant, he may say to the claimant, "Swear and collect, or collect nothing." All the more so with oaths of those who collect, where the original enactment placed the oath on the claimant, we do not listen to him if he seeks to reverse it. He must swear or not collect. Only a defendant seeking to retain money may say that if the claimant does not wish to swear, he shall not collect; one seeking to extract money cannot say, "Swear or give me the money." (End of collected notes.)
