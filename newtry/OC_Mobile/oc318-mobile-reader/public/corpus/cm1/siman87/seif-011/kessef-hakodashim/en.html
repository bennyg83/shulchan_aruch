(See Shach, seif katan 58.) The Shach cites the Bach that a vow to give payment for immoral relations concerns conduct carrying the penalty of karet, and answers that we do not rule like Rabbi Nechunya ben HaKanah. I do not understand this. Even if we do not rule like Rabbi Nechunya ben HaKanah regarding exemption from monetary liability, according to the claimant's own account the defendant should at least be regarded as suspect regarding oaths. Here the usual concern that he may merely be withholding money because of an old loan does not apply at all, as the poskim discuss and as the Sema writes here. This requires careful consideration.
