[Shach, subparagraph 16] However, where he is obligated to swear and cannot swear. Note: See Responsa Das Esh, siman 18, who writes that this applies specifically where the defendant incurs a monetary obligation without an oath by the claimant. But if the defendant is disqualified from swearing, in which case the opposing party ordinarily swears and collects, a supporting witness exempts the defendant from payment. It is apparent from his words that where both parties are disqualified and the defendant must pay outright, a supporting witness does not exempt him.
