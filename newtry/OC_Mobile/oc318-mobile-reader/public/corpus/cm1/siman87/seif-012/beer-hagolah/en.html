There, seif 20, in the name of the Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilkhot To'en VeNitan, law 4. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is sound reasoning: one of those whom the Sages allowed to swear and collect may, if he wishes, reduce his claim and be treated like an ordinary claimant, in which case the defendant takes a shevuat hesset and is exempt. Surely his position is no worse than that of other claimants. His law is therefore like theirs, including the defendant's ability to reverse the oath onto the claimant. Rabbi Meir HaLevi likewise writes this, and it is the principal ruling.
