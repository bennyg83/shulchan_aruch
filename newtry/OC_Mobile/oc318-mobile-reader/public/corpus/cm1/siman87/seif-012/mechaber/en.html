Although claimants who swear and collect cannot reverse the oath to the opposing party without the defendant's consent, if the claimant says, 'I do not want the enactment the Sages made for my benefit; instead, I wish to be like other claimants, and let the defendant take a heses oath for me,' he is heard. If the defendant then reverses the oath to the claimant, he is heard. If the claimant does not wish to swear, the defendant is exempt.
