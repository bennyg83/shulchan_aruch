There is an authority who says... See Tosafos, Shevuos 46b. The Behag's text and explanation are cited there. The Mordechai's gloss explains that the case concerns one suspected regarding oaths because witnesses heard her utter God's Name in vain, thereby transgressing the prohibition of taking the Name in vain. This is treated like a false oath, as stated in Berachos, and renders her suspected as in Perek Kol HaNishba'in. From here one may derive the rule stated here, as Rava ruled.
He claimed from him... Maharam writes that entitlement to a matchmaker's fee follows the law of one who enters another's field without permission. If the field is one ordinarily planted, the owner pays what a person would normally pay to have such a field planted, as stated in Bava Metzia 101a. The matchmaker is comparable to a broker, as explained above in siman 185 seif 10 in the gloss, and a broker must be paid, as stated in Bava Metzia 63b.
