Likewise, if the claim appears weak to him. In the Amsterdam edition of the Shulchan Aruch, Mahariv, siman 30, is cited here, but this is a printing error and should be deleted. The citation refers to the clause below, "we do not impose an oath," etc. The meaning is that where the claim is weak and there is also no basis for the rule of Rabbi Dimi, no oath is imposed. This also resolves the apparent difficulty from seif 1 above, where it is explained that a heset oath is imposed even where the rule of Rabbi Dimi does not apply, as explained there by the Sema and the Tur, and likewise in siman 75 and many other places. This is also explicit in Darkei Moshe, letter 3. See there.
