A Torah oath, and it later became known, etc. This is not limited to a Torah oath. The same applies to a rabbinic oath of those who swear and collect, as below in siman 92, seif 13.
And he shall pay. That is, after the opposing party swears, as below in siman 92. Nevertheless, it is difficult why the Rama wrote this here, since the Rambam, Tur, and Mechaber already state it explicitly below in siman 92, seif 13. Moreover, what the Hagahos Mordechai and Darchei Moshe call an enactment of the Geonim does not appear so from the Rambam, Tur, and Mechaber there. Rather, their words indicate that it is strict law. The Maggid Mishneh at the end of chapter 2 of Hilchos To'en, cited by the Beit Yosef at the end of siman 92, explicitly says that this is clear, because it is no less than the rule that a judgment based on an error in an explicit Mishna is reversed. They likewise write there regarding a rabbinic oath by one who swears and collects that if it becomes known that he was disqualified from taking an oath, he must return what he collected. See there. If so, the Rama should not have written here only, if he took a Torah oath, etc., because the same rule applies to those who swear and collect by a rabbinic oath.
