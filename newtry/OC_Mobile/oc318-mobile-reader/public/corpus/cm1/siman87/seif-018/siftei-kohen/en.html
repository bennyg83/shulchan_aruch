And they said that a heset oath is not, etc. The Sema writes: see Meisharim, Nesiv 1, chelek 5, where he records an opinion that a heset oath is only a general ban. His wording is abbreviated. He means that it is not taken with a Torah scroll at all, but that one places him under a general ban in his own name, whereas an ordinary general ban is neither with a Torah scroll nor in his name. That is how Meisharim states it. It appears that "in his name" means in the person's own name. Examine carefully. Nowadays, however, the practice is to administer heset with an oath in which he says, I swear that I owe this person nothing.
