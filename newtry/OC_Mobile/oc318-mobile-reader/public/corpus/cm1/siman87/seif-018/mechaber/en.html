According to the Rambam, even a heses oath must employ the Divine Name or an appellation and be phrased either as an oath or as a curse. Its only distinction from an oath administered by the judges is that a heses oath is not taken while holding a sacred object. The other authorities did not agree with him. They hold that a heses oath employs neither the Divine Name nor an appellation, but is phrased simply as an oath or with the words, 'May he be cursed.' See Rivash 237.
