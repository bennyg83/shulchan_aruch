It is not. The Sema cites Meisharim, Netiv 1, part 5, as saying that shevuat heset is only a general cherem. The Shach explains that the Sema's wording is too brief. Meisharim means that heset is not administered with a Torah scroll. Rather, one places a general cherem in the person's name. A general cherem itself is neither with a Torah scroll nor by invoking the individual's name, but is stated generally. The Shach suggests that "in his name" may mean the person's own name. In contemporary practice, however, heset is administered as an actual oath in the form, "I swear that this person has nothing of mine in his possession.
