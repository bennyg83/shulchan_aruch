Either by an oath or by an imprecation. Meaning, whether hesset is administered in the form of an oath or in the form of an imprecation, according to this view the person must mention the Divine Name or an appellation in either formulation. The Mechaber then concludes that the authorities did not accept this view. Rather, hesset is administered without mentioning the Name or an appellation, whether phrased as an oath or as an imprecation.
