And I have not seen the custom follow this. Noda BiYehudah, siman 13, discusses a noblewoman who claimed weakness and inability to leave her home and wished to swear there before the synagogue attendants. He writes that the local custom was for all oaths to be taken specifically in the Old New Synagogue. Nevertheless, impossibility is not judged by ordinary custom, and because she could not attend she could swear at home. She should, however, swear before ten people, as stated in the Rama to siman 87 seif 17. Although the Rama writes that this was not the prevailing custom, Noda BiYehudah reasons that once he had departed from custom to permit her not to swear in the synagogue, he likewise departed from custom on this point and restored the strict law requiring ten people. See Baer Heitev below, siman 124 seif katan 4, in the name of Radbaz, and what I write below in seif 19, seif katan 20.
