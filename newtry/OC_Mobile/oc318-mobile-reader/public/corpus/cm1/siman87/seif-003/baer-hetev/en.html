Acting deceptively. Wherever there are two opinions whether a person is obligated to swear, the Rosh writes in chapter HaKotev, and the Tur in Even HaEzer siman 96, that we do not issue a ruling imposing the oath. This is straightforward and is explained by the authorities in many places. See Responsa Maharit, siman 151, at length, that where the leading authorities dispute whether an oath is required, the defendant is exempt because he can say, "I rely on the authority who exempts me." The same appears in Responsa Ibn Lev, Maharashdam, Mahari Beit Levi, siman 3, and Responsa Maharik, siman 99. Shach.
