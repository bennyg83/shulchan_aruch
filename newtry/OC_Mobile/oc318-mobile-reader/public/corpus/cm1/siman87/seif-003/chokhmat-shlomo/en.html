There, seif 12: Although those who swear and collect, etc. Note: It appears that if a litigant made this declaration in beit din, he cannot later retract and say that he wishes to return to the ordinary law. Similarly, if he was holding property of the other party and the law was that he must swear and collect, or in the case of a lender holding collateral, once he says, “I accept the status of an ordinary claimant,” he cannot later retract. This follows the law explained in siman 22. The same applies to the preceding seif in the gloss, where it says, “Nevertheless the defendant may,” etc. This appears clear to me.
