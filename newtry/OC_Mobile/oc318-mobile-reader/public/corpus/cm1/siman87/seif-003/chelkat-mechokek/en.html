But regarding a presumption arising from damage, etc. In Choshen Mishpat 149, Rama cites this law in the name of "some say," because it is not universally accepted. According to Rambam, even if one dug into and damaged the land, the husband still acquires no presumption against his wife. All the more so regarding opening windows, the wife can say, "I never waived this use for all future time; I merely did not insist upon it temporarily while dealing with my husband." Ritva expressly writes so, cited by Beit Yosef to siman 149.
