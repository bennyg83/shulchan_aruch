An answer. The Tur writes that he swears regarding the remainder and is not thereby disqualified. This means that he now takes an oath regarding the remainder. So write the Ir Shushan, Sema, and Bach, who disagree with the Beit Yosef. See there. If the claimant wishes, however, he may himself swear and collect against the defendant's will, as stated in siman 92, seif 5. Ibid.
