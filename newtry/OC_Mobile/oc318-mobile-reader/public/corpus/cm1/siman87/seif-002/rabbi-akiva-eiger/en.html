[Shulchan Aruch] He said to him, “Here is the portion I admit.” Note: If he was sued for one hundred, admitted fifty, and said “here is twenty,” it requires study whether we should say that he has a migo, since he could have admitted only twenty and tendered that amount immediately. See Or Zarua, who asks according to the opinion that immediate tender exempts in every case, why the defendant is not believed by a migo that he could have admitted only a perutah and tendered it. He answers that perhaps that amount was not available to him. See there. According to that reasoning, in our case there would be a migo. Tosafot in Bava Kamma 107 do not appear to agree. See there, and also their words in Bava Metzia 5, s.v. “And perhaps it is immediate tender.” Examine this carefully, and see the following seif 2.
