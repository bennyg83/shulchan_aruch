Seif 6: He admitted part of the claim and one witness supports him, etc. Note: See the Rosh in the first chapter of Bava Metzia, who rejects Rabbeinu Yonah’s proofs concerning whether the term “witnesses” can include even a single witness. See also the proof I wrote in my novellae to Bava Kamma, chapter Merubah, concerning the Mishna about a thief established by two witnesses. See my third-edition work on Choshen Mishpat, siman 68, where I explained the Rosh’s words in Bava Metzia, including his statement “some ask,” and resolved the difficulty of Maadanei Yom Tov. I also discussed the Beit Yosef in siman 28, cited by Urim VeTumim here, from which it appears that a supporting witness can help even in a case governed by the rule that one who is obligated to swear and cannot swear must pay. See there carefully.
