In the same seif. (A pledge.) Even according to those who hold that a pledge is not considered immediate payment because it still requires guarding, the converse should also follow. If by virtue of the pledge the lender is deemed to admit part of the borrower's claim, because the lender must eventually return the pledge while the borrower claims that the pledge contained additional movables, the lender can nevertheless regard the entire admitted portion as immediately available. Although he presently withholds the pledge until the debt he claims is paid, his admission is not that he must hand over part immediately. Rather, he admits that once payment is made he must return the pledge, and at that point it is fully ready for return. This should therefore be considered immediate payment.
