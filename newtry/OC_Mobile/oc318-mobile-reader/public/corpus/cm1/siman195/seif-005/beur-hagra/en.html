But if, etc. As stated above, siman 131 seif 4 in the gloss, and see what was written there. Tosafos in Yevamos and Kiddushin 63a writes that according to Rabbi Yochanan, even “from now” does not help because it constitutes a reservation. Tosafos in Kiddushin adds that one must examine, according to Rabbi Yochanan, how a kinyan sudar can take effect after time even with “from now.” Rabbeinu Meir answers that perhaps “on condition” is effective even according to Rabbi Yochanan and is a condition rather than a reservation. Therefore it is preferable to say “on condition, from now.” See the beginning of chapter 9 of Gittin regarding the dispute concerning “except, etc.,” and Beer HaGolah in the name of Beis Yosef.
