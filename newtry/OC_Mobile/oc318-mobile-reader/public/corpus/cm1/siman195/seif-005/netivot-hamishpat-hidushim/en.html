On condition that you acquire for me from now. "From now" alone is not enough according to the Sema, because it indicates a partial present acquisition that is not completed until after thirty days. With sudar kinyan, by the time the acquisition completes the scarf has already returned to its owner. But if he says, "on condition," there is no delayed completion, only a condition attached to an acquisition effective now. Strictly, saying "on condition" alone would suffice because that phrase is equivalent to "from now." The Sages nevertheless instituted saying "from now" as well, to publicize that the acquisition is immediate. The Shach disagrees and holds that "from now" alone is sufficient. See Beurim in siman 198, where this is primary.
