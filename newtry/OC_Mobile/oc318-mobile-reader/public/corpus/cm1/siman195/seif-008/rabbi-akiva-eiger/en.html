[Shulchan Aruch] After the time of toch kedei dibbur. Note: But within toch kedei dibbur he may retract even from an acquisition effected by meshichah or chazakah. Beit Yosef in the name of the Rosh, and Nimukei Yosef likewise writes this in the name of the Ritva, though he records that some disagree. See Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 101.
