But if he said to him, acquire on condition, etc. In the Perishah I wrote that the reason for saying "from now" is that it constitutes a reservation within the acquisition. The Sema continues that strictly speaking, an expression of "on condition" is effective even without explicitly saying "from now"; the Sages instituted saying "from now" as well to publicize that the transfer takes effect immediately, and the Beit Yosef writes likewise. Their words are difficult, however, because here it is the recipient who says "on condition," as is explicit in the poskim. The language at the end of siman 191 shows that saying "from now" alone suffices, and the Tur here also indicates this. The Tur's use of "on condition" is merely habitual language, since every acquisition by sudar is made on condition that it effect the transfer. Tosafos in Kiddushin also holds this way according to Shmuel, whose ruling we follow. See also the end of siman 197. I discussed this at length in my responsa. See the Rosh in Perek Mi Shemet and the Tur below, siman 210, and Responsa Maharit, siman 46.
