Perhaps the Sema's unresolved question concerning a perutah used for acquisition by money, and whether one may retract while the parties are still engaged in the same matter, can be explained as follows. Money given as part of a fixed purchase price may be a stronger acquisition in the object itself, because failure to pay the balance can affect the validity of the sale. Penei Yehoshua writes distinctions concerning chalipin where the exchange itself takes the form of a sale. If there was an acquisition by money together with a handshake, and one party retracts while they are still engaged in the same matter, perhaps the customary handshake acquisition is void while the monetary acquisition remains effective only to the extent that retraction subjects him to mi shepara.
