As a gift. Responsa Mahari Weil, siman 4, writes that even with a gift one can say that the recipient may not desire it because "one who hates gifts shall live." He therefore concludes that one should not distinguish between a gift and a sale. Although in siman 171, seif 10, the Tur and Shulchan Aruch record a dispute regarding this point while here the Mechaber rules without qualification, the meaning appears to be that acquisition outside the recipient's presence is found only regarding a gift, according to the authority who validates it, whereas in a sale all agree that it is ineffective. Mahari Weil further writes that in both cases there is no acquisition unless we know that the recipient desired it at the time of the acquisition. Even if we know he desired it earlier and also desires it now when he hears of the acquisition, perhaps the giver retracted in the interim and later changed his mind again. It appears that under the common practice in which the buyer sends witnesses to the transferor to make the acquisition on his behalf, there is no concern that he retracted in the meantime, since they act as his agents.
