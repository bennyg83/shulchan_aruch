See Baer Heitev, who explains that this means three fingerbreadths by three fingerbreadths, for at that size it has the status of a cloth utensil with respect to impurity, and Rashi writes likewise. See Responsa Be'er Yitzchak, siman 5, where the author was asked by the eminent Rabbi Eizeh of Shavli: We rule that only wool and linen fabric requires merely three fingerbreadths by three fingerbreadths to contract impurity, whereas fabric of other materials requires three handbreadths by three handbreadths, as stated in Shabbat 126a. If so, where the scarf used for chalipin is made of another material, should it not require three handbreadths? The author replies that Tosafos in Bava Batra 173b, at the end of s.v. Asurei Mechsera, expressly write that there is no distinction between leather and sackcloth in the rule in Bava Metzia that a piece measuring three by three suffices. The abridged rulings of Tosafos there and Hagahot Ashri write likewise. The reason may be explained in several ways. First, although other fabrics of only three by three fingerbreadths are not included in the laws of impurity, wool and linen of that size are included because they are fit for use by the poor, as Bartenura explains in Keilim 27:2. It follows that such a size has the status of a utensil generally and can therefore be used for chalipin. When Rashi in Bava Metzia refers to the law of impurity, his intention is to establish the minimum measure, for below three by three fingerbreadths there is no utensil status anywhere. Alternatively, the matter may be explained from Zevachim 54a, where Abaye says that a cloth smaller than three, according to Rambam's reading, distinguishes between the views: according to the one who requires that it be fit for use, this cloth too is potentially fit, because if one wished he could designate it. A further explanation may be derived from the Gemara there, 95a, which answers that because the pieces are attached to their parent garment they are significant; Rashi explains that when attached to a garment, the garment lends significance to its own three by three portion.
