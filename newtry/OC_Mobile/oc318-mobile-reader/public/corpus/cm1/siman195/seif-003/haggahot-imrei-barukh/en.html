(Mechaber, seif 7) In other methods of acquisition. Note: See Tosafot, Kiddushin 57a, passage beginning "and its place," and Shitah Mekubetzet to Bava Kamma 66a citing the Ritva, at the end of the passage beginning "unless he said, knowingly." The discussion there concerning the deathly ill person implies that even with a get, after delivery to the woman, so long as they are still engaged in the same matter, a condition may still be imposed. This requires great study. See Even HaEzer 38 and Taz there, seif katan 2, also cited by Beit Shmuel seif katan 2.
