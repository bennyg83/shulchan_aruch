Seif 9: Some say they are not acquired. The Beit Yosef questions why Rabbeinu records a dispute regarding rental. It appears that Rabbeinu learned it from the Rosh, who asks why they did not lend the parties a place. He does not ask why they did not rent them a place. Rental would offer no practical advantage, since they would still have to transfer the money later. A loan of the place, however, would eliminate the need for payment. Although Tosafot mentions rental as well, Rabbeinu followed his father's wording. This resolves the question.
