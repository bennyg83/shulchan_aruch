Judges for a compromise. What is stated in Choshen Mishpat 195, that where a kinyan sudar was made to accept the compromise of judges the parties cannot retract even while they are still engaged in that same matter, may perhaps apply only where there are three judges. With a single judge, although a kinyan sudar is effective, perhaps it has no greater force than an ordinary kinyan sudar for a gift or sale. The Gaon wrote the foregoing specifically regarding a kinyan sudar before three valid judges. At present I have not found a decisive proof, nor do I know the source from which the Gaon derived this. If he derived it from the general language of the Sages regarding one who says, “So-and-so is acceptable to me,” that he cannot retract, perhaps some inference can indeed be drawn from there. This requires careful study.
