[Shulchan Aruch] And not with produce. Note: Nevertheless, it appears that if one gave another produce on condition that the other give him a particular utensil, and he stated that he was selling the produce for that utensil, then once the recipient performed meshichah on the produce, the other party became obligated to give that utensil as a personal obligation. The utensil is the consideration he must give in exchange for the produce, although the utensil itself is not acquired directly, but is owed as an obligation. It appears that this is Rashi's intent at the beginning of the chapter HaZahav, in the Mishnah stating that all movable property acquires one another, whether by way of chalipin or by way of payment.
