But other movable property, etc. The Hagahot Mordechai to Bava Metzia writes that anything made by human hands is included in the category of a utensil, whereas something produced naturally is classified as produce. The Ittur writes likewise. This is the language of the Sema. This requires study, for this appears to be Rashi's view, and if so it is inconsistent with the Rama's ruling that an animal is called a utensil. Examine carefully.
