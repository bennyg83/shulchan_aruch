Some say there is no, etc. Regarding a get the reason is the requirement of complete severance.
The seller does not, etc. In accordance with Rav Nachman at the end of chapter 5 of Nedarim. Although Rav Ashi says, “And who, etc.,” that is only a rejection, for Rav Ashi himself holds in Kiddushin chapter 1 that in all these cases one acquires except a woman, etc. This shows that chalipin is a gift on condition of return. Ran and Rosh there.
One does not effect a kinyan, etc. How can one transfer another person’s utensil to someone else? This is like Kiddushin, where one who betroths with stolen property, etc., and the same applies to a sale, which is compared to kiddushin there.
(Collected note) One does not effect a kinyan, etc., therefore, etc. From Bava Metzia chapter 1: “Learn from here, this sudar, once he holds it, etc.” Hagahos Mordechai there, siman 532, in the name of Tosafos. (End of note.)
Some say even, etc. If even a borrower may not lend the borrowed item to another, all the more so he may not give it away, even as a gift on condition that it be returned.
Unless, etc. It was given to him in a manner that legally enables this, as stated at the end of chapter 7 of Bava Batra 137: “But here, if it does not leave his possession, etc.” See Rosh, Kiddushin chapter 1, siman 2.
