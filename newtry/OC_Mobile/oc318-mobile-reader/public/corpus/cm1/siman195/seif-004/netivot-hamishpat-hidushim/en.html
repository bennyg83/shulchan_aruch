The measure of three fingerbreadths. Meaning three by three fingerbreadths, because that size has the legal status of a cloth utensil regarding ritual impurity. From this we learn that when acquiring with other utensils, the recipient must grasp enough of the utensil that if the portion in his hand were separated, the remaining or grasped portion relevant to the act would still have the legal status of a utensil regarding impurity. Sema:
To pull away the entire utensil. "Entire" is not literal. It means enough of the utensil to constitute a utensil, namely three by three fingerbreadths. Sema. Although his ability to pull it away does not suffice to acquire the whole item when finding ownerless property, as explained in siman 269, seif 5, perhaps for kinyan sudar undertaken in order to transfer ownership, the ability to pull away the requisite portion is sufficient.
