One may not effect acquisition, etc. This appears from the end of the ninth chapter of Bava Batra 156b, where Reish Lakish says that one may make a kinyan from a dangerously ill person, etc.; from Bava Kamma 70b; and from siman 208 below. The Tosefta in Bava Batra states that on Shabbos one should not do so, but if one did, the act is effective. See Tosafot there, s.v. Rav Chanina, etc. The Yerushalmi, fifth chapter of Yom Tov, cited by Rif and Rosh, states that all who performed such acts, whether deliberately, etc. Rif and Rosh infer from this, etc. See below, the end of siman 235, and Bava Metzia 9a: if so, one who lifted, etc.
