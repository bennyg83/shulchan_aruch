[Rama] A borrowed utensil. Note: See Ritva in the discussion of a coin, on the statement that one who lacks a kerchief may nevertheless perform chalipin. He writes explicitly that chalipin may be performed with a borrowed kerchief, because since it is acquired only in order to transfer ownership, any minimal possessory right suffices. See Shitah Mekubetzes there in the name of a student of the Rif, who holds that one cannot effect the transfer with a borrowed object, because the kerchief is treated as though it had been cut, and the owner did not consent that his kerchief be cut.
