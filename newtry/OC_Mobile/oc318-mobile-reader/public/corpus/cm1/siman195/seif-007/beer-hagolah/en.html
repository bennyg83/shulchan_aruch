Kuf) Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 10. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the proof is from the statement there, Bava Batra 86b: "A kor for thirty," etc., where each portion is acquired as it is measured, even though they are still engaged in the same matter. The Tur likewise rules this way. The Rosh writes that it is specifically with a kinyan that the Sages allowed a brief period for retraction, because people commonly finalize a matter suddenly through a kinyan sudar; therefore they allowed him a moment to reconsider.
Reish) So writes the Rosh there and the other poskim, based on the statement in the chapter Yesh Nochalin, Bava Batra 129b, that speech made within tokh kedei dibbur is regarded as part of the same speech, except regarding idolatry, etc.
