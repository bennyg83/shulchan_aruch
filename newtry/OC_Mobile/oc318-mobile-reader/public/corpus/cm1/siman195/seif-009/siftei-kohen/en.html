Through the other modes of acquisition, etc. See the Sema, seif katan 17, who criticizes the Levush for offering a reason of his own. This criticism is unjustified, for the Levush's explanation is correct and is taken from the Rosh, as cited by the Beit Yosef. These are the very words stated by the Tur and Levush at the beginning of siman 126: only where the acquisition is not performed upon the object itself may one retract while the parties are still engaged in that matter, because people commonly act suddenly in such a case. But where acquisition is performed upon the object itself, their intent has already become settled; otherwise the owner would not have allowed the act of acquisition to be performed upon the object. The wording of Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, cited by the Beit Yosef, likewise implies this. Without this explanation there is no basis for distinguishing between a sudar kinyan and other acquisitions. Examine this carefully.
