(Rama, seif 4) An ordinary kinyan is on condition that the utensil be returned. Note: This is based on the Rosh and Ran in Nedarim. One who studies them will see that according to the Ran the transferor acquires no right at all in the utensil, while according to the Rosh it is a gift with a condition to return it. See Tosafot, Kiddushin 6b and 26, which indicate that they rule like Rav Sheshet that, if he wishes, the transferor can retain the sudar. The Rashba in his novellae there disagrees, yet still writes that the transferor acquires a temporary right in the sudar. The Ran in Nedarim clearly holds that there is no acquisition at all, even temporarily. Tosafot Rid to Kiddushin 3 and 6 states that in chalipin by a utensil the transferor acquires it completely, like a case where the item is actually cut off and retained.
