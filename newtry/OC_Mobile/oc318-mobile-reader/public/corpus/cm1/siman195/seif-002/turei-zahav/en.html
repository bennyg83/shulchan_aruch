Seif 5: On condition that you acquire for me from now. Tosafot in Kiddushin states that the phrase “on condition” is effective according to all opinions, and then says that it is preferable to say, “on condition, from now.” Their intent appears to be this. The phrase “on condition” already functions like “from now,” since without present effect a statement such as “Acquire this scarf if you convey your object to me after thirty days” would not work. The familiar principle is that one who says “on condition” is as though he said “from now.” But “on condition” must also serve its ordinary role of introducing a condition rather than a reservation in the sale. It is therefore preferable to add “from now” explicitly, leaving “on condition” to express the condition alone. The Beit Yosef's explanation is strained.
