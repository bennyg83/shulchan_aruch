The language of Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, law 5. Maggid Mishneh writes that the halachah follows Rav that chalipin is performed with the buyer's utensil, as concluded in HaZahav, Bava Metzia 47a.
The Rosh, Responsa rule 67, siman 4, cited by Beit Yosef from section 24, discusses Reuven who had animals for slaughter and sold the hides to Shimon by kinyan chalipin. While the animals were being taken to slaughter they escaped and drowned in a river. The Rosh ruled that the loss of the hides falls upon Shimon. Sema cites this. A similar rule appears in siman 199, seif 3, concerning a meat purchaser.
