Let the buyer learn from the name of a man who is offended and who is permitted by the brides of a buyer and who is not only a vessel and is permitted to receive the pleasure of Domyahu
Once he performed meshichah. The Rosh writes concerning Reuven who had animals to slaughter and sold their hides to Shimon through sudar kinyan. The animals later drowned in a river before slaughter. The loss of the hides falls upon Shimon because he had already acquired them through the kinyan, just as with one who buys meat as in siman 199:3. Sema. The Taz adds that even if Reuven expressly said he would give the hides only after slaughter, the loss still falls upon Shimon.
And there was no one else who was forced to deal with the same thing or who was changed in the Middle East
