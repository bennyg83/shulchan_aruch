They are not acquired through a loan. The reason is that rental for a day has the legal status of a sale. Below, in siman 204, the Tur and the Mechaber write in seif 10: if one person has a debt owed by another and says to him, "Sell me these movable items in payment of the debt you owe me," meaning through the preexisting loan, the acquisition is ineffective. There it is explained that he can acquire through the benefit created by forgiving the loan, and the same applies here. The "mortgage" mentioned by the Mechaber here means that he lent money against land that remains pledged in his possession, with the lender eating the produce while deducting its value from the debt, until the borrower returns the money or until a fixed time. This is explained in Yoreh Deah siman 172. A mortgage of this kind has the legal status of rental and sale.
