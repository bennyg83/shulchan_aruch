(Seif 3: The Tur wrote) The Tur cites the Ramah that this applies only where there is no other means of payment. It appears that, at minimum, the worker must have a means of collecting what is due him, for the employer accepted liability for his wage. If the materials are worth less than the wage, the employer must pay the balance. If they are worth more, the worker acquires only the amount of his wage, unless the employer expressly transferred all of them. The principle is that the employer cannot force the worker to accept an indefinite or valueless substitute, but where identifiable property has been accepted and brought into the worker's domain, it serves as payment to the extent of the debt.
