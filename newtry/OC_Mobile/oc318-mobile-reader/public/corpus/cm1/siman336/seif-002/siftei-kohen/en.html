Take what you made as your wage, etc. Even if the employer owns nothing else, he must exert himself to sell the article and obtain cash for the worker. Tosafot, Ramban, Nimukei Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, the Rosh, the Ittur, Baal HaTerumot, and the Ritva all rule this way. A worker is entitled to monetary payment and may not be compelled to accept the product of his labor in lieu of cash merely because the employer lacks other funds.
