h) Ibid., in the Mishnah. The Rambam likewise writes in chapter 9 of Hilkhot Sekhirut, law 10, reproducing the Mishnah's form. The Maggid Mishneh writes that from here the authorities learned that regarding wages, even if the employer has no money, he is told to make the effort to sell property and give the worker cash, because the worker hired himself on that understanding. The employer may not pay him with movable property, for 'to it he lifts his soul.' This is the principal view. The Mordechai, however, writes at the beginning of Bava Kamma in the name of Maharam that regarding edible goods such as wheat and barley, the worker must accept them. Hagahot Maimoniyot to chapter 9 of Hilkhot Sekhirut likewise writes this, cited by the Sema in the name of Darkei Moshe.
