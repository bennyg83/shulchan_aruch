In his possession. The Ran and Nimukei Yosef at the end of Bava Metzia hold that he may retract so long as the objects have not been removed, as above in siman 166 regarding a garden wall; they write that according to this, in the case of collateral he does not acquire it. This requires study, for the Mechaber himself rules this way concerning collateral in siman 72, seif 26, and the Rama likewise rules so in siman 73, seif 17. See there. Regarding the Sema's words here, one may answer that this is not comparable to siman 166. Here he is already obligated to pay the worker's wage, and when he tells him, "Take this for your wages," the worker acquires it immediately. There, however, there is no prior wage obligation before he removes the material. See Nimukei Yosef there, who offers a somewhat different answer. Shach.
