He gives him his full wage. The Sema proposes emending the wording cited from the Ramah, but the proposed word is absent from the Gemara and all the authorities and has no textual basis. In my view the Ramah refers to the end of the baraita: the worker returns and recovers from the challenger the value of the benefit conferred, meaning the value of the labor to an ordinary sharecropper. Consequently, the sharecropper has no disqualifying interest if the possessor did not agree to pay him more than the customary local rate. No emendation is required.
