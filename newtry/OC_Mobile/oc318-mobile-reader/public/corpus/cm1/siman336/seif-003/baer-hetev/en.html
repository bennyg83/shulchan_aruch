And he should acquire it. Meaning, this is sound advice for the watchman: he should promptly acquire the ownerless item for himself by lifting or pulling it. If another person precedes him and acquires it, he will lose on both sides, for since he knew that it was ownerless, his case is like one who was hired to work in another person's field where the worker knew the field belonged to that other person, as stated in seif 1. These are the words of the Sema. See the Tur regarding what he writes on the words of the Ramah cited there.
