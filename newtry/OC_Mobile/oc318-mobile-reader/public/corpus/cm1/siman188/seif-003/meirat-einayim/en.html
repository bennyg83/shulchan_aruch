One who says to his fellow, "A maneh, etc." This is the Rambam's wording in chapter 2 of Hilchot Sheluchin. The Kesef Mishneh explains that until this point the Rambam dealt with an agent sent to make a purchase, while here he addresses one sent to deliver a debt or deposit. More likely, the Rambam's purpose is to teach a further novelty. One might think that only when the principal explicitly says, "Send it to me with the minor," as above, is the sender exempt, but if he merely says, "Send it to me with so and so," perhaps the sender remains liable because he did not know that the person was a minor. The ruling teaches that even if the named person is a minor, since the sender fulfilled the principal's instruction, he is exempt.
