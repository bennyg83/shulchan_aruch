176. There the Rama records a dispute whether a principal must redeem one who served as his unpaid agent. It is possible that only there he rules that some authorities impose liability, because we say that the agent's body is, as it were, borrowed to the principal, and therefore one opinion obligates the principal to redeem him. Here, however, where the agent suffered a loss to his own property, it may be that all agree the principal is exempt. For this reason the Rama states the rule here without qualification. Ibid.
