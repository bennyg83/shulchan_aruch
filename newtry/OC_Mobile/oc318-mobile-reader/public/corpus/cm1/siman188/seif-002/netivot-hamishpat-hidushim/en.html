They cannot become agents. See the Beurim. If one hired people who lack legal capacity for agency to perform an act of acquisition, then with hagbahah, if the worker raised the item three handbreadths, the employer acquires it, because the force of a worker is treated as the employer's own force, as though the employer raised it through him. The same applies to chazakah, where improvement to the house performed through the worker is treated as performed through the employer's force. With meshichah, if the worker pulls the item into the employer's domain, it is effective, because it is as though the item were drawn there through the employer's own force. But if the worker pulls it into the worker's own domain, it is ineffective, because the domain of a worker who lacks capacity for agency does not become the employer's domain. Likewise, if with hagbahah he lifts it less than three handbreadths, the employer does not acquire it.
