An agent. If an apostate has adopted idolatry, whether he has the legal status of a non-Jew for purposes of agency is discussed in Responsa Beit Yosef, Laws of Gittin, siman 14, where authorities are arrayed on both sides. Riaz, in his glosses surrounding the Rif in the chapter HaMevi, on the Mishnah stating that all are qualified to bring a get, writes that such an apostate is fit to act as agent to bring a get to a Jewish woman. See Pnei Moshe, volume 2, siman 38; Responsa Mabit, volume 2, siman 145; and Responsa Maharashdam, siman 58. Bnei Chayei.
