[Shulchan Aruch] A gentile does not. Note: See Responsa Masat Binyamin, siman 97; Responsa Nachalat Shivah, siman 30; Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer, siman 5 at the end; Tiferet LeMoshe, Yoreh Deah, siman 169, s.v. "in the Beit Yosef," page 167; the Shach above, siman 123, seif katan 30; and Maharikash, siman 192, ruling 14. If a Jew built through a gentile, this constitutes chazakah even though there is no agency for a gentile. See there. Regarding whether there is agency for an apostate Jew, see Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer, simanim 123 and 141; Magen Avraham, siman 189; Responsa Beit Yosef, siman 36; and Shach, siman 126, seif katan 92.
