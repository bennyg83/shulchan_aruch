A gentile cannot serve as an agent for any of these matters, etc. We learn this from the verse concerning terumah, "also you." The Sages expound "also you" to include your agents, showing that an agent can separate terumah in place of the owner. Since the word "also" was not needed merely to teach the general law of agency, which had already been learned from Pesach and gittin, they used it to compare the agent to "you": just as you are members of the covenant and obligated in mitzvot, so your agent must be. A gentile is therefore excluded. Alternatively, had the law of agency not already been known from Pesach and gittin, we could have assigned the extra word "also" to another teaching. Once agency is independently derived, the extra word is available to teach that an agent learned elsewhere must be comparable to the principal in this respect. The Tur and Mechaber's further statement that a Jew cannot be an agent for a gentile is learned from the same comparison, because principal and agent must be within the same legal category. The Gemara states this explicitly.
