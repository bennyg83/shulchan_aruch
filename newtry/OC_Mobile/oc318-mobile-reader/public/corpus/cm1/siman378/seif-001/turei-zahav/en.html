[Seif 2] In an unusual wind. The Sema's attempt to reconcile the Tur is difficult, for what source has the Tur for lowering liability by one degree when, regarding damage, a person and vessels are governed alike? It appears to me that in siman 451 the Tur imposes liability only where the roof had no parapet, because one should not ascend a roof without a parapet where an unusual wind could cause a fall. Here, however, the roof had a parapet, but the person was thrown beyond it by a storm so violent that it lifted and cast him. There is no greater accident than this. This also explains the Raavad's statement that he is exempt even in an unusual wind, meaning this very kind of extraordinary wind.
