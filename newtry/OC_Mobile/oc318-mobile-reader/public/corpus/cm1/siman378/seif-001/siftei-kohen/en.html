And specifically when he is not under compulsion, etc. The wording is difficult. It should say: "And some say, specifically when he is not under complete compulsion." The language of Rambam and the Mechaber implies that there is no distinction, and the Maggid Mishneh likewise writes that according to Rambam there is no distinction.
When he is not under complete compulsion, etc. See what I write below at the beginning of siman 386. It appears that this applies specifically where he caused damage unintentionally. But one who intentionally causes damage is liable even under complete compulsion, like a pursued person who breaks vessels, as explained below, siman 380, seif 2. See there.
