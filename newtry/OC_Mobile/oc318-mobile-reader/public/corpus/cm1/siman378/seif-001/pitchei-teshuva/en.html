Whether he acted unintentionally. See Responsa Chavot Yair, siman 169, concerning a guest who went to sleep in his room, forgot to extinguish a paid-for wax candle, and left it burning, whereupon the host's table and a chest containing linens were burned. The same responsum discusses a maidservant who negligently failed to remove wood from atop an oven while it was burning and thereby caused a fire that destroyed some household vessels. He ruled that they must pay, because a person is always forewarned with respect to damages he causes. See there.
