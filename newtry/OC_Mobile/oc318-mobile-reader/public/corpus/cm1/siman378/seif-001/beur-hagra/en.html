It is forbidden, etc. Even in a dangerous circumstance, as stated later in seif 2, and even indirect causation is forbidden, as Bava Batra 23a teaches.
And if, etc. The Mishna in Bava Kamma 26a.
Specifically, etc. See Tosafos there 27b, s.v. "and Shmuel," the Rosh there, Tosafos Bava Metzia 82b, s.v. "and he holds," Tosafos Sanhedrin 76b, s.v. "a murderer," and the Rashba. This is also the meaning of the Gemara on 48a that when the owner caused the damage he may be exempt, and the Maggid Mishneh explains similarly. The phrase "as will be explained" refers to seif 6, and the same approach appears in seif 3 in the Rambam's language.
