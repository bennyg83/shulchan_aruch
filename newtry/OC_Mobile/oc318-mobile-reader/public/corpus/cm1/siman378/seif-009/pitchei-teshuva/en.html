The damager is not obligated to pay more. Dagul MeRevavah writes that the Tur, citing a responsum of the Rosh, concludes here, "since he did not intend it." This implies that if he did intend it, he must pay everything the non-Jews subsequently imposed because of dina de-garmi. See also siman 72, Shach at the end of seif katan 40, and Baer Heitev there, seif katan 53, and what I wrote there.
