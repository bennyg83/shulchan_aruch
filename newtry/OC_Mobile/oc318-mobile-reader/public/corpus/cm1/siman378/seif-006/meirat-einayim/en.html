Wherever one causes damage, etc. This is the wording of the Rambam and Tur. It appears the text should read "in every place," with the preposition included.
And he damaged him. Whenever the language says "he damaged," it means the tortfeasor caused the injury by an act, even without intent. Whenever it says "he was damaged," it means the injured party was hurt by coming into contact with the stationary damaging object. Thus the damaging object remained in its place and the injured party approached and was injured. Rashi explains this, and see the Perishah.
