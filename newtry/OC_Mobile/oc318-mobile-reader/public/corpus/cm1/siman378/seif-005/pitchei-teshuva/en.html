The tortfeasor is not required to pay more. Dagul MeRevavah notes that the Tur, citing a responsum of the Rosh, concludes with the reason that the tortfeasor did not intend the consequence. This implies that if he did intend it, he must pay the full amount that non Jewish authorities subsequently imposed, under dina degarmi.
