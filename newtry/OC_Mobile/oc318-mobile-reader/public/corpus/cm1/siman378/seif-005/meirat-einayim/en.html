Therefore it is forbidden for a person to stand over another's field, etc. The Tur and Mechaber state this particular form of visual damage rather than the visual damage discussed in siman 154 because there the seeing itself does not directly damage anything. It merely causes the other person to alter his conduct in the courtyard out of embarrassment, or creates concern about encroachment and similar matters. By contrast, looking at standing crops can itself exert a harmful influence and cause direct loss. As for prohibition, however, the cases in siman 154 are all the more certainly prohibited, because regarding looking at standing crops there is even an opinion in the Gemara that it is only a matter of pious conduct. See the Perishah.
