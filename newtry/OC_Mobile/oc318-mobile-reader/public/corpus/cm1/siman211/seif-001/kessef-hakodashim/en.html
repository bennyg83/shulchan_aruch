Seif 5. He may sell from today. Machaneh Ephraim, discussing the Bnei Aharon who understood the Yerushalmi to mean that when fruits belong to another the owner cannot sell or consecrate the underlying property, writes that this is difficult to understand. Perhaps the Yerushalmi was compiled through Rabbi Yochanan's teachings, and Rabbi Yochanan holds that ownership of fruits is equivalent to ownership of the body. On that basis the Yerushalmi is understandable and does not contradict the later authorities who permit the owner to sell, since we rule that ownership of fruits is not ownership of the body.
