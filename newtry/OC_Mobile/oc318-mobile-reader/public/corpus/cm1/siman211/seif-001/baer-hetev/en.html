My castle. He said, “I don’t believe that if my back is poor.” A:
Specified. Since he specified and identified the subject of the transaction, and inheritance comes automatically, we do not regard it quite as much as a transfer of property not yet in existence. Sema. This is also the wording of the Rosh. Meaning, however, that if he says, "this field that I will purchase," it is property not yet in existence even though he says "this," as is evident in Bava Metzia 16 and many other places, contrary to the Ir Shushan. See Responsa Ibn Lev, vol. 1, siman 63. Shach.
