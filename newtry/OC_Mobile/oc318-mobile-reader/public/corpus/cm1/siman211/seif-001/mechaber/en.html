The law of property not in one's possession, and one who writes his property to his sons to take effect after his death. It contains seven seifim:
Something that is not in the transferor's possession cannot be acquired and is treated like something that has not yet come into existence. For example, if one says, "What I will inherit from my father or from another person from whom I will inherit. Students of Rashba. is sold to you," or, "Whatever my net catches from the sea is given to you," or, "This field, when I purchase it, is acquired by you," he has said nothing. The same applies to similar cases. Rama: Some say this applies only when he speaks generally. If he specifies and says, "This particular field that I will inherit from my father is sold to you," the buyer acquires it. Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Tam. See above, siman 209, seif 5.
