[Shulchan Aruch] And if he denied it. Note: See the Rashba's novellae to Bava Kamma 70a, s.v. "they do not write an authorization." It is evident that he holds that in the case of stolen property whose owners have not despaired of recovery, and likewise movable property that the possessor denies owing, although the owners cannot consecrate it, as we derive from the verse "if a man consecrates his house," meaning that just as his house is in his possession, nevertheless he can sell it. See there. The Rashba writes similarly in his novellae to Shevuot 33a. It appears to me that this is also the view of the Rashbam in Bava Batra 43a, s.v. "to remove himself." I discussed this at length in a responsum.
