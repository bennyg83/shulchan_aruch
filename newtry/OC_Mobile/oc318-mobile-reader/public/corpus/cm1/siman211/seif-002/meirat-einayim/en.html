There is no Maddak if he sold more. P. But there is no need to sell too much for his burial if he does not find anyone who will buy him a little and soak:
Because of his livelihood. The Rosh and Tur write, "because of his livelihood for that day." The Rif and Rambam write simply "because of his livelihood." In Hagahot Darkei Moshe I explained that even according to the Rif and Rambam one may transfer or sell only what his trap will bring up that day. He may sell that day's anticipated catch even if it will provide him sustenance for several days. This is unlike the implication of the Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe.
