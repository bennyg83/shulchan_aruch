Note: If partners own a field and one sells "my half of the field on the eastern side," saying, "If by the lottery this area falls to my share, it shall be acquired to you," this is a transfer of something not yet in existence. The exception is where he sells his undivided share wherever it is located, so that the purchaser simply takes his place as partner with the other owner. Responsa Rosh, rule 99, siman 3.
