[Shulchan Aruch] That which I will inherit from my father. Note: If his father did not die that day, the money returns to the purchaser. Likewise, regarding "what my net will bring up today," if it brought up nothing, he returns the purchaser's money. But if it brought up even a mere shard, he acquires the money. Responsa of the Geonim, simanim 201 and 202.
