Zayin) A statement at the end of Bava Batra 158b. The Beit Yosef writes that the Gaon established the case where the father is a goses, for otherwise even the seller himself could recover the underlying property because the sale never took effect, as explained above. Nevertheless, he must return the purchase money, as the Maggid Mishneh writes there in the name of the Rashba.
Meaning, the money was already spent and was not used for the father's needs.
Chet) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 7.
Meaning, the father of the person who died during his lifetime.
