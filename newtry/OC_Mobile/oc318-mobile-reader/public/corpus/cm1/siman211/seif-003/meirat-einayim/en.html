And he need not return the money, etc. It appears that the case is where the purchase money was consumed only after the son's death, in such a way that the grandfather of the present heir derived no benefit from it, neither for burial expenses nor for anything else. Otherwise there would be no reason to say that the descendant may recover the property from the purchasers without returning their money merely because he inherited his grandfather. The grandfather himself, having benefited from the purchasers' money, would have been obligated to repay it. I discussed this at length in the Derishah.
