One who had a deposit, etc. Regarding one whose collateral is held by others, Tosafot in Bava Metzia 73b, in the incident of Rav Mari bar Rachel, writes that the excess above the debt may be consecrated or sold, though the creditor first collects his debt. See Hagahot Ashri there. Several poskim indeed hold this view, while others maintain that even the excess cannot be consecrated or sold. See my Shach to Yoreh Deah 221, seifim ketanim 42 and 44, and Nekudot HaKesef there, where I discuss this at length and reject Tosafot's proofs from Eizehu Neshekh. One may perhaps distinguish between consecration and sale and say that all agree a sale is effective. The matter requires study. See Responsa Rabbi Aharon Sasson, siman 152, and Mabit, part 1, siman 107.
