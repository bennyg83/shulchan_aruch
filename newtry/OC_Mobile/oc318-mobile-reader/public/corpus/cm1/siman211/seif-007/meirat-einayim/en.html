Reuven borrowed from Shimon, etc. The Tur cites this in the Raavad's name at the end of siman 54. There it is explained more fully that during the period in which Reuven still had the right to redeem the field, Reuven ate the produce, for otherwise the arrangement would involve interest. Reuven later sought to recover the field from Yehudah because Yehudah had purchased it before Shimon acquired rights through the document. The Raavad ruled that Reuven is not heard. See there.
