And Levi gave Shimon the document in his possession. See Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 48. He writes that the delivery of the document is not essential; that detail merely reflected the facts of the case. In truth, even if the third party did not physically hand the document to Shimon, Shimon acquires retroactively. This is also implied by the Levush at the end of siman 54, who compares the matter to one who sells his field effective immediately and the creditor says to the debtor, "When you have money I will return it to you." If payment is not made at the stipulated time, the sale remains effective from the original date. See there also for his explanation of the Beit Yosef at the end of siman 54 and his disagreement with Gidulei Terumah. In siman 49, VeShav HaKohen raises a further doubt. Reuven rented a house to Shimon for one year and, during the lease term, sold him the house with the sale to take effect after the year. During that same year Shimon sold the house to Levi. Does Levi acquire, or since Shimon's purchase would take effect only after the year and he sold before his own acquisition vested, is this analogous to saying, "This field shall be acquired by you when I acquire it," which is ineffective? Or perhaps, because most authorities regard a lease for its term as a form of sale for all legal purposes, once Shimon held the property under lease and the permanent sale was set to take effect immediately upon expiration of the lease, the house may already be treated as his for purposes of conveying it onward. He brings an apparent proof from the Tosefta at the end of the chapter HaMafkid concerning one who rents a cow and it is stolen, and analyzes that source at length. His tentative conclusion is that if one leases property for a year and subsequently sells it to the lessee effective at the end of the year, a resale by the lessee during the lease term should be valid. Nevertheless, he concludes that the matter still requires practical study because the earlier authorities did not cite that Tosefta and there are additional considerations.
