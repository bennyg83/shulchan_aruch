One who had, etc., and the same applies, etc. Bava Metzia 38a, where the Gemara challenges from one who deposits produce, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. “therefore, etc.,” and Beer HaGolah.
But a lender, etc. From Bava Batra 147 and 148, where the Gemara discusses Rav Nachman’s view. Although the rule does not apply to a healthy person, the Gemara derives the shechiv mera rule and invokes Rav Huna, showing that for a healthy person a loan cannot be transferred except by ma'amad sheloshtan; another kinyan does not work. Likewise 152a and Kesubos 55b, “he mounted it on two steeds, etc.” See Tosafos there.
And if it was, etc. There, 76.
Reuven, etc. As stated in Bava Metzia 65b and 66b, every “from now” effects immediate acquisition. See Rashi 66b, s.v. “alternatively,” who explains that it is a complete acquisition, etc., accepted by the other party. See below seif 5 and siman 363 in the name of Raavad.
