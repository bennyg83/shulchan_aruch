By a sale. Regarding one who has collateral in another's possession, Tosafot in Bava Metzia 73, in the incident of Rav Mari bar Rachel, write that whatever exceeds the debt may be consecrated or sold, although the creditor first collects his debt. See the Hagahot Ashri there. This is indeed the view of some authorities. Others disagree and hold that even the excess cannot be consecrated or sold. See Yoreh Deah 221:7, in my Siftei Kohen and Nekudot HaKesef there, where I discussed this at length. The proof of Tosafot in Bava Metzia may be rejected, see there. One may also distinguish that this applies specifically to consecration, whereas with respect to sale all agree that he may sell. This requires study. See Responsa Mahara Sasson, siman 152, and Mabit, part 1, siman 107. Shach.
