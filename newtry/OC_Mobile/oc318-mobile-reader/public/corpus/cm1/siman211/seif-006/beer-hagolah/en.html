Mem) Rambam, chapter 23 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 8. The Maggid Mishneh writes that in Kiddushin 27a the Gemara asks whether movable property must be physically gathered upon the land in order to be acquired together with it, and concludes that it need not be. If property that is not presently in existence could also be acquired this way, the question would have been obvious. Necessarily, acquisition together with land works only when the movable property is presently in existence.
Nun) The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is straightforward, because the burden of proof rests upon the claimant. The Rashba writes similarly.
