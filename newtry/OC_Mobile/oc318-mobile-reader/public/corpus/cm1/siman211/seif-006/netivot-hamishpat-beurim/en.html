See above, siman 60. Sema, seif katan 12, intends to distinguish between the cases as follows. There the document used the past tense, “I gave,” which is language of admission. Here the donor says in the present tense, “I give land, and together with it movable property,” which is language of acquisition rather than acknowledgment, so proof of an effective agav transfer is required. See what I wrote in siman 60 from Rama’s responsum that the word “I gave” can bear both meanings, and where possible we interpret it as an acknowledgment. Without this distinction, Sema’s words are difficult: there is no inherent difference between knowing that the transfer was by agav here and knowing that it was by kinyan sudar in siman 60.
