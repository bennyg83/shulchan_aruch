(Seif 2: A wine storehouse in Eretz Yisrael). The Tur does not distinguish between Eretz Yisrael and Babylonia. It appears that he understood Rav Yosef's statement, “Our wines are harmed even by the smoke of a lamp,” to refer to all wines, including those of Eretz Yisrael, for we find that after the destruction of the Temple the flavor of fruits was diminished. Beit Yosef's explanation is strained. See there: If it had already been established, and so forth. Meaning, even according to Rashi and Rif, whom the Tur cites in seif 47, that one may not create a potentially harmful use even before the vulnerable object exists, a cattle shed is different because it is a residence. Since the harm is not immediate, one need not remove his dwelling. Rashi explains it this way.
