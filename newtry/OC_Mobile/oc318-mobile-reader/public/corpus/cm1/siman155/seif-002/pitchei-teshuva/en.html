Under his fellow's storeroom. Responsa Noda BiYehuda, second edition, siman 37, considers whether the activities prohibited beneath another's storeroom are likewise prohibited beneath another's residence. Ramban, in his commentary to Bava Batra 18, records a dispute and concludes by an a fortiori argument that they are prohibited beneath a dwelling, citing the Yerushalmi. Knesset HaGedolah also cites Rabbeinu Yerucham that the resident above may object. Noda BiYehuda analyzes Rif, Rambam, and the Yerushalmi at length and ultimately concludes that the upstairs resident cannot prevent the lower owner in the case before him, especially because there was an air space between the lower vault and the upper floor, making it comparable, according to Rambam's explanation, to a separate upper story.
