If Reuven's wall adjoins Shimon's wall at a right angle, and Reuven wishes to build a second wall parallel to Shimon's wall, so that the walls form three sides, Shimon may prevent him unless he leaves four cubits between them. The space between the parallel walls must be wide enough for the public to tread there and compact the ground. {Rama: Even if a wall stood there for many years and fell, when it is rebuilt it must be distanced.}
