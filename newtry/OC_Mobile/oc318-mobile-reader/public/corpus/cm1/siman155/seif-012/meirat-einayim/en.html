Reuven whose wall was adjacent, etc. In the Derishah I proved that the case is stated as an additional novelty. Even though Reuven already had a wall there shaped like the Hebrew letter gimel, which prevented most people passing there from walking close to Shimon's wall, since sensible people would not walk close to it only to have to go around the entire length of Reuven's adjacent wall and then turn back to continue their route, nevertheless when Reuven comes to build a second wall opposite Shimon's wall he must distance it four cubits. This is contrary to the Ir Shushan, who writes that according to the Rambam distancing is required only with three walls and not with two. His words are astonishing and contradict the Gemara and what I wrote in the Derishah. Rama likewise cannot hold that view, for nearby in seif 14 he writes in his gloss concerning Reuven's wall running from east to west, and the reason there is that with such a small obstruction we do not fear the loss of foot traffic that strengthens the ground, as the Tur explains. This proves that when one builds a wall directly opposite another wall, even with only two walls, distancing is required.
And even if it stood there, etc., and fell. The Rosh and Tur write this according to their interpretation and textual reading of the Gemara, which includes the case where it fell. The Rambam interprets and reads the passage differently, as I explained in the Perishah and Derishah. Nevertheless Rama writes this qualification upon the Mechaber's words, even though the Mechaber follows the Rambam, because Rama understood that the Rambam agrees with the resulting law.
