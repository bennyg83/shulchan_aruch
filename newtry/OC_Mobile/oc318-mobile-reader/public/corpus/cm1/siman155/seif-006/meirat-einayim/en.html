On dry rock one may urinate, etc. At first glance this appears limited to urinating alongside the wall, but if urine collects in a pit beside it, even on rock it should be prohibited. Beit Yosef, however, understands the Tur to hold that according to Rambam even a pit requires no distancing where the ground is dry rock. See the Perishah and my responsum, where I discussed at length the analogous case of excavating a privy beside a stone wall jointly owned with one's neighbor, where the wall rests on a hard rocky foundation, and whether three tefachim of distance are required.
