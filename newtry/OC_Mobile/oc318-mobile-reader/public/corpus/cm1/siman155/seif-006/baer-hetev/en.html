Rock. At first glance it appears that the ruling speaks only of urinating directly beside the wall, whereas a pit containing urine would be prohibited even where the rock is dry. The Beit Yosef, however, writes that according to the Tur's understanding of the Rambam, even a pit requires no distancing when the rock is completely dry. In a responsum I discussed this at length and derived from it the law of excavating a privy beside a shared stone wall standing on a hard foundation comparable to bedrock, whether in such a case one must distance it three handbreadths or not. Sema. See what the Taz writes concerning dry rock.
