(Seif 5, in the gloss: and with a pit, etc.) This requires study according to Rambam's view that the Mishnah's phrase "urine" refers to a pit containing urine, as the Maggid Mishneh explains, and that this is the "wastewater" mentioned in the Gemara. Why then does the Tanna list urine together with seeds and plowing, where the requirement of plastering with lime applies, as the Beit Yosef writes? It should have listed urine together with the water channel and pickling pool in the opening clause.
