Nevertheless, initially. See Sema, seif katan 83, who writes that in a place where there is no right to protest there can be no chazakah. This is difficult here. If he has no legal right to object from the outset, no chazakah is needed at all. The present statement must instead concern the preferred initial conduct even where later objection or a future change of circumstances could matter. One should therefore not derive from Sema's general formula that every inability to protest necessarily means that no relevant neighbor right can ever arise.
