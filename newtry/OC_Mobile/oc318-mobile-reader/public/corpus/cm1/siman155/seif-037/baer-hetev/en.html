They said. See Responsa Ibn Lev, siman 46, and Responsa Maharit, simanim 88 and 114, page 146. If he says, 'I will arrange a pipe to carry out the smoke so that it will not harm you,' the law is with him. Responsa HaGeonim, siman 107. The compiler states that the same applies to every form of damage: if he remedies it in a manner that causes no harm to his fellow, the fellow may not prevent him. Maharashdam, Choshen Mishpat, siman 266, likewise writes so. Smoke and a privy do not acquire a chazakah; all the more so is this true of hazards involving danger. Maharashdam, Choshen Mishpat, siman 284; Maharib Lev, part 3, siman 46; see also Maharashach, part 2, siman 172. Where there is doubt whether the condition is among those forms of damage for which no chazakah can be acquired, the party presently exercising the harmful use is considered the possessor. Maharashach, Responsa, siman 16. Knesset HaGedolah.
Nevertheless. The word 'nevertheless' should be understood as though written with the prefix meaning 'because,' that is: because at the outset he was able to prevent it, once he thereafter remained silent he can no longer object. Otherwise, we have an established rule that wherever protest is unavailable there can be no chazakah. Sema.
