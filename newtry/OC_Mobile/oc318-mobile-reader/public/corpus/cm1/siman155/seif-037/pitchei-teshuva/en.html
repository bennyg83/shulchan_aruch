And so it appears to me to rule. See Baer Heitev, which notes that the Shach disagrees. Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, end of siman 151, nevertheless rules in accordance with Rama. He distinguishes the case from the earlier question of sweeping and sprinkling beneath a storeroom. There the damaging force is certainly attributable to the actor because the injury is renewed each day; the uncertainty is only whether the Sages exempted one who preceded the storeroom, and therefore one may not create the doubtful injury initially. Here, by contrast, the authorities dispute whether the act is legally classified as damage at all. Since the person acts entirely within his own property, he may proceed initially.
