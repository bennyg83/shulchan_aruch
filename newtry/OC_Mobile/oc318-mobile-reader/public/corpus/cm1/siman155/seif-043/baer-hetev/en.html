Who established a chazakah. See Responsa Ibn Lev, part 3, siman 46; Responsa Maharashdam, siman 296; Responsa Rabbi Moshe Alshich, simanim 94 and 171; and Responsa Maharit, simanim 114 and 133. The Taz writes that it appears from here that where no chazakah can be acquired for the odor of a privy in one's house, covering it does not suffice. The neighbor may say that tomorrow at times you may fail to cover it and I will have to trouble myself with litigation and judges.
