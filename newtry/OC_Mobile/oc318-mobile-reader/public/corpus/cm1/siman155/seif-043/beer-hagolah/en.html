The Maggid Mishneh writes that although this law is not explicitly stated in the Gemara, it is obvious according to the Geonim, who hold that rights relating to damage are acquired immediately through silence. Since this person is presently in possession of the right, his possession is evidence for him. This chazakah functions here like three years of chazakah in land accompanied by a claim of sale or gift. When the other party comes to protest, he seeks to remove an established right, and the burden of proof is upon him.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that whenever a monetary claim is made and the defendant responds with a definite denial, a hesset oath is imposed by rabbinic law. This is obvious.
