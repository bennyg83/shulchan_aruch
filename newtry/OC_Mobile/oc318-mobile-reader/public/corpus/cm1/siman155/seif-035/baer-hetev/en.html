Immediately. He said, “I will be strong and strong, and if you see it, you will be able to do so with it, and you will be able to do so with it, and you will not be able to do so.”
To rule. See Responsa Ibn Lev, part 3, siman 46, and Responsa Rashach, part 2, siman 208. The Taz writes that the Sema labored unnecessarily in siman 153, seif 16, to distinguish the Rama's ruling here. It appears rather that the Rama deliberately waited until the end, after presenting this dispute, to decide that the burden of proof rests on the claimant and that three years are not required in any such case. This appears entirely clear, without any doubt or alternative interpretation. See also what the Taz writes in explanation of the Tur's words.
