With respect to all the required separations stated above, if one did not distance the source of damage and the other person saw and remained silent, he is deemed to have waived his objection and may not later compel him to distance it, provided his conduct demonstrated waiver, such as where he immediately assisted him, told him to proceed, or saw him act alongside his property without the required separation and remained silent without objecting. In such a case, the person causing the condition acquires the right to maintain it. Rama: See above, siman 154, seif 12. This is the proper ruling, although some say that three years of established use together with a supporting claim are required.
