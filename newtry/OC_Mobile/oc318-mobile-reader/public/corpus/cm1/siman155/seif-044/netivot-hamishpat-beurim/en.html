Some say that the law is with him. See Shach, seif katan 22, who distinguishes the case of a vineyard partition on the ground that the damage there is certain. This is difficult, because the laws of distancing harmful uses do not turn on certainty of damage but on whether the harm is considered one's direct force. A proof is the gloss to seif 4, where if the water descends only gradually the person is exempt, and the same principle appears in many places. The primary explanation is that failure to maintain the vineyard partition is considered one's direct force because the harmful effect proceeds from one's property in a legally direct manner. Thieves, by contrast, are independent actors and are not considered the owner's direct force.
