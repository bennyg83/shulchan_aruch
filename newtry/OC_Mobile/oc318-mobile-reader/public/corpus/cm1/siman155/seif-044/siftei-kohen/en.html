Some say that the law is in his favor, etc. The Bach likewise rules this way in siman 157, seif 5, and compares it to the partition of a vineyard that was breached in the first chapter of Bava Batra. In my eyes, his proof is like a tiny measure against a large barrel. There the damage of kilayim is certain once the partition is breached, whereas who can say that thieves will actually come here? Even the damage caused by breaching a fence before another person's animal is more certain. This is clear.
