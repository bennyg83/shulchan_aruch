(Seif 35, who assisted him by hand). This is the correct reading, and I have found it so. The statement proceeds from the lesser case to the greater: not only where he assisted by hand, but even where he assisted verbally. The Tur writes that according to the Rosh three years are required, which implies that this applies even where the injured party assisted him physically. This is difficult in light of siman 142, where assisting him in bringing in the produce creates an immediate chazakah. It may be answered that there, if he says, “I allowed him to descend only for the produce,” he is believed. Here too, he may say, “I waived the injury only for a fixed period,” and he is believed.
