The language of Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilkhot Shekhenim, law 8. Maggid Mishneh explains that once the owner has established a right to the gutter, he acquires use of his neighbor's area to the extent that the neighbor must leave four cubits so that a ladder can be raised to repair it. Although the neighbor may build beneath the gutter, as explained in siman 153, building beneath it does not prevent ladder access, while building immediately adjacent does, unless he distances himself. Rashba agreed with this in his later years, holding that a gutter's chazakah includes everything necessary for its maintenance, because without maintenance it will no longer drain. This is analogous to a window made for light, which requires the neighbor to distance his wall four cubits. The same applies to a gutter.
Tosafot write likewise where one sold or granted the right to stand a ladder in his courtyard. Nimukei Yosef writes in the name of Ramban that the same applies to brothers who divided property and adjusted the valuation because of the gutter: the owner of the gutter acquires a right in the courtyard to stand a ladder.
