Who disagree. The Sema explains that they hold that for a stone-lined pit a distance of one handbreadth is sufficient, while for an unlined pit three handbreadths suffice. The Taz writes: I do not know who the dissenting authorities are. The Sema appears to allude to the Mordechai's responsum concerning Reuven who made a drainpipe, which is the case cited by the Mechaber in seif 20, where the Rama rules that only three handbreadths are required. It appears to me, however, that even that authority agrees with the Mechaber here regarding smoke and a privy, for the Mordechai itself cites this law. A drainpipe is different because the injured party can build a barrier against the water, as stated there explicitly. It therefore appears that one should not depart from the Mechaber's ruling, which is the position of Maharam Gaon, the Rosh, the Mordechai, and Rav Amram. The dissenters did not state their view explicitly.
