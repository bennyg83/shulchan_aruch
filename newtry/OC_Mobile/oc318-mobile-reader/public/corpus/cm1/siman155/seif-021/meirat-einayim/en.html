Therefore he must distance the privy, etc. This refers to a privy not dug into rock, but in a place where the soil is soft. The ruling concludes that a privy should be distanced, as an ample precaution, fifty cubits because its contents are corrosive and foul smelling. The Tur likewise writes in the name of Rav Amram that, as a generous precaution, it should be fifty cubits away, like a tannery. Other wells and pits do not require so great a distance, but where the ground is soft they must at least be distanced more than six handbreadths. This is evident from the Mechaber's wording that one must distance more than six handbreadths, and I likewise proved it in the Derishah from the Tur and the Rosh.
