There is an authority who says that the Sages stated that a separation of six handbreadths between one pit and another suffices only in mountainous land. In a place where the soil is loose, a greater distance is required. Therefore, a privy must be distanced from another person's pit until people can discern that moisture from the privy does not pass into the pit. As an added precaution, he should distance it fifty cubits. Rama: Some disagree with this ruling. Wherever one causes damage, he must distance himself, even when a public domain separates the houses, such as two courtyards situated on opposite sides of a public domain. Even so, he must distance the source of damage.
