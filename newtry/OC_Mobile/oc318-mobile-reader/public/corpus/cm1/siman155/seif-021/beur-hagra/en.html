And for the rest of it. It is better than Bursky from the city than it is purely, and here it is pure and dull and thick:
There is one who says. The Rif writes regarding a soaking pool and vegetables that according to R. Yose one need distance only three handbreadths because that is the range of his direct force. Beyond that, although damage results, no distancing is required because the water's direct action has ceased before the later effect. The same applies here, even though a soaking pool also creates odor. The Tur writes that all the Geonim agree with the first view; those who disagree here also dispute the adjacent ruling.
