(Seif 21, gloss: Some disagree). I do not know who the dissenting authority is. The Sema's statement in seif katan 46 appears to allude to Mordekhai's report that Rabbi Meir was asked about Reuven who made a drainage pipe. This is the responsum that the Tur cites in the name of the Rosh and that the Mechaber records in siman 20. Maharam ruled that only three handbreadths of separation are required. Nevertheless, it appears that he agrees with the Mechaber's present rule concerning smoke and a privy, for Mordekhai himself also cites that rule. A drainage pipe is different because the injured neighbor may be able to erect a barrier against the water, as the responsum states explicitly. It therefore appears that one should not depart from the Mechaber's ruling, which is the position of Ramah the Gaon, the Rosh, Mordekhai, and Rav Amram. The dissenters did not state their disagreement expressly.
