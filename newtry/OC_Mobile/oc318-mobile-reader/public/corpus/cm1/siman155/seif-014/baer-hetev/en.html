The king. The Beit Yosef writes that since he purchased the property from the official, he presumably acquired the four cubits required for foot traffic that strengthens the wall. Therefore, his fellow who later comes to purchase from the official may not build there, because those four cubits already belong to the first purchaser. Otherwise, however, one need not distance his building and encumber his own land merely in order to strengthen his fellow's wall. Ibid.
