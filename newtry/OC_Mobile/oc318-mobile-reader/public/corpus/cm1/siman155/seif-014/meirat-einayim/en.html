Only when they acquired the land from the king. Beit Yosef explains that since he acquired it from the king, we presume he also acquired the four amot needed for strengthening the wall. Therefore a neighbor who later purchases adjacent land from the king cannot build within that space, because it already belongs to the first owner. Otherwise, however, one is not required to distance his building and encumber his own property merely to strengthen his neighbor's wall. See Darkei Moshe.
