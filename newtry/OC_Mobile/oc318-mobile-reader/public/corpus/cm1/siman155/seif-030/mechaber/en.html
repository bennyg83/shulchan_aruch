If Reuven dug a pit and, upon descending, found roots of Shimon's tree within his own field, he may cut them and continue digging, and the wood belongs to him. If the roots are within sixteen cubits of Shimon's tree, they belong to Shimon; Reuven cuts them and gives them to him. If Reuven does not need to dig a pit and Shimon's roots extend into his field, he may dig to a depth of three handbreadths so that they do not obstruct the plow. Any root found within those three handbreadths may be cut, and he need not be concerned that the other person's tree may dry out, because he is digging within his own property.
