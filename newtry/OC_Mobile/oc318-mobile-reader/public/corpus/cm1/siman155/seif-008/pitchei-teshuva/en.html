And the same applies to any great damage. See the Tur at the end of this siman, who cites a responsum of the Rosh. Reuven owned houses, and recently the butchers began selling meat outside the courtyard near his house. Reuven objected that he could not tolerate butchers near his home because of the foul odor of the meat and the dogs and pigs commonly found there. The Rosh ruled for Reuven. This was so even if the butchers claimed that they had purchased the place from Reuven. See the Beit Yosef, Derishah, and Bach there.
