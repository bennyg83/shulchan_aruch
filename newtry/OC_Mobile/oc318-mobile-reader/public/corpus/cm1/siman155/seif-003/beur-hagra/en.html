(Supplement) In all these, etc. This is Rambam's approach to cases of doubt. The Rosh disagrees here and holds that one need not distance on account of uncertainty. See the gloss below in seif 18. A similar dispute exists regarding a doubtful lost object. Rambam's reason is that since causing damage is biblically forbidden, doubt is treated stringently; this differs from requiring removal after the act, where the possessor retains the property. The Rosh holds that although the prohibition is biblical, monetary uncertainty is treated leniently. Rambam distinguishes between taking something from an established possessor, where the claimant bears the burden, and a person who is now coming to cause damage. (End of supplement.)
