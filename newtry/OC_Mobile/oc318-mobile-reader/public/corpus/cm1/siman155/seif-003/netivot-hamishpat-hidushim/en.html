A hollow space. The Rambam interprets the Talmudic question concerning building an upper story over one's house as referring to the shop owner creating an intervening hollow space. Rashi explains it as referring to the owner of the storeroom first constructing upper stories in his house, and whether that shows his intention to use it as a storeroom. Practically, both rulings are true. Sema.
