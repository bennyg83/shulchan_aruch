There, law 13, from Abaye's unresolved question there.
The unresolved question of Rav Huna son of Rav Yehoshua there.
This is the text in Rambam and in the Maggid Mishneh there. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this explains the Gemara's phrase, 'he built an upper story over his house.' Rabbenu Chananel and Rambam's teacher explain it this way. In the Venice printing of Beit Yosef I saw the word 'partition.' Rashi explains differently.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam holds that because of doubt one may not damage his fellow. If he nevertheless acted, the burden of proof rests upon the claimant seeking to extract. Rambam's explanation in all these cases follows his teacher in the name of Rif.
