Even. As stated by the Gemara at 19b regarding wastewater. Rashi explains that here too there is no distinction between a stone wall and a brick wall, just as throughout the mishna.
(Supplement) Even without, etc. Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch hold that this applies specifically to a pit. See Baer HaGolah. The Rama's statement regarding a pit differs from their view, for according to them the mishna itself concerns a pit and does not add plaster here as it did earlier. Rambam holds that where the location is not fixed, unlike a cistern or pool, no distancing is required even if there is a depression, except for urine in a depression. According to the other commentators and the Rama, however, the mishna concerns wastewater without a depression, while if there is a depression one must plaster it for any liquid. (End of supplement.)
