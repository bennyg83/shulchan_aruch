There in Rambam, from the Mishnah there.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Mishnah says 'urine,' while the Gemara, 19b, explains this as pouring. Rashi and Ibn Migash explain that it refers to urine collected in a vessel, so that he should not pour it all at once. Rambam's intent is specifically where there is a pit into which it is poured, etc. He concludes that it is reasonable that even ordinary water requires distance. One can say that although other water may collect and remain, since it is not constant like the water of launderers, the same distance is not required.
