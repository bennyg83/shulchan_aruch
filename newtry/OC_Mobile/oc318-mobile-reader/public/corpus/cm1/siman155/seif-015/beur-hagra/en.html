It shakes... until. Meaning that the barrel and its cover stand on the courtyard ground. Thus the ruling says "to the courtyard," and does not say the barrel stands upon the wall, unlike Rashi.
Some say. Tosafos there, s.v. "at the mouth," in the name of Ri.
At the time of the shaking. Bava Basra 22b, "even if you say... sometimes at the same moment..."
He must pay. Bava Basra 6b says that if he caused damage he pays. Fire is treated as one's arrows in Bava Kama 23, and this damage likewise comes directly from his force. Tosafos Bava Basra 26a and the Gemara there show that where the harm comes directly from his force he is liable. This is why the text says, "because it comes from his force." If it did not, even Mar bar Rav Ashi would agree to exemption. See the Rif and seif 34.
Even if it was built. From the case "he was poor and then became wealthy." Tosafos, Hagahos Ashri, and the Rosh explain that even an activity established earlier does not permit direct damage. See seif 36.
The same applies if. Bava Basra 23a and Tosafos there. See seif 39 and seif 41.
