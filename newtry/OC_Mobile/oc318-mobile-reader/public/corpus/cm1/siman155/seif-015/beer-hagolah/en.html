The language of Rambam there, chapter 11, law 3, from the incident involving the oil press workers, etc., at the end of 25.
There, beginning of 26. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rashi explains that if a jug with its cover is placed on the wall, the cover will move because of the vibration. Ibn Migash likewise explains this. He writes that if the cover does not move, even though the courtyard itself vibrates, this is not considered damage and we disregard it. This is Rambam's view.
