The language of Rambam at the beginning of Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 11.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that this follows the incident there according to the Halakhot. Regarding the flax beaters, the Gemara concludes that this is like one who winnows while the wind assists him. Rashi explains that just as for Shabbat this is considered his force, so too for damages it is treated as his force.
Rif likewise writes this from Rav Ashi's statement that it is merely indirect causation, and one is exempt for grama in damages, Bava Kamma 60a.
