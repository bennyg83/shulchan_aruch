One who made. Bava Basra 24b, according to Abaye's answer. Although the Gemara leaves a question, that only means the Mishnah need not be established in this fashion; the substantive rule remains correct. Ri Baal HaTosafos supports it. Thus the Shulchan Aruch later distinguishes a permanent threshing floor, while a nonpermanent one must still be placed far enough not to cause damage.
