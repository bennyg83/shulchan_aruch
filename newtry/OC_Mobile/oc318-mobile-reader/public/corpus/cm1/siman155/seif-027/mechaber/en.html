A tree that extends over the public domain must be cut back sufficiently to allow a camel and its rider to pass beneath it. Rama: The same applies if one wishes to project a ledge above the height of a camel and its rider. Even in a public domain, and certainly in an alleyway, the residents cannot prevent him. He may not, however, construct a bridge from one house to another, because it darkens the public domain or alleyway. Even a single ledge is forbidden if it causes darkness or if wastewater is poured from it into the middle of the road.
