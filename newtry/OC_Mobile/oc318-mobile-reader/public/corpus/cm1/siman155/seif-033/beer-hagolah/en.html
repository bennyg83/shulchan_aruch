Tur, seif 49, in the name of Raavad, analogous to the exemption given in the case where people were beating flax and the chaff was carried by the wind, at the beginning of Bava Batra 26a.
There, seif 3, in the name of Baal HaIttur concerning the Mishnah's rule, "and if he caused damage he must pay," Bava Batra 20b. He writes that with all the listed distancing requirements, if damage nevertheless occurred even after he distanced properly, he must pay where the damage is identifiable and demonstrably caused by him. He writes that the Rosh ruled likewise, except for damage caused by a mongoose after a ladder was placed nearby, Bava Batra 22a, and damage from beating flax cited above, because in those cases the damage was not a direct natural result of his act but occurred through the wind or the animal.
