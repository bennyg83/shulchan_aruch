And for a stove, one handbreadth, etc. Regarding the required height above a stove, the Tur records a dispute, while the Mechaber rules without qualification. It appears that the Mechaber holds that with respect to height all the cases listed are equal and require a distance of four cubits. Since the concern is the flame that rises upward, a substantial separation was required, and beyond four cubits there is no concern in any of these cases. This is why the Tur and Mechaber also state without distinction the required height for an oven and for bakers' stoves, because they hold that all are equal in this respect. See the Perishah, where I wrote that this is likewise the view of the Rosh and Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash.
