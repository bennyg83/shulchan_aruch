Under his fellow's storehouse. See Noda BiYehudah Tinyana, siman 37, concerning whether one may perform these activities beneath another person's dwelling. He cites the Ramban's novellae to Bava Batra 18, where there is a dispute on this question. The Ramban concludes by an a fortiori argument that beneath a dwelling it is forbidden and brings proof from the Yerushalmi. Knesset HaGedolah likewise cites Ri'a that the owner of the upper dwelling can prevent it. Noda BiYehudah discusses at length the views of the Rif and Rambam and the interpretation of the Yerushalmi, and concludes that the resident above has no power to prevent the resident below. This is especially true in his case, where there was some empty airspace above the lower vault between its ceiling and the upper floor, which according to the Rambam is comparable to having built an additional upper story.
