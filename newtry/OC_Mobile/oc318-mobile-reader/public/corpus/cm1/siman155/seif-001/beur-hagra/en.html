The house and upper story belonging to... This is the wording of the Rambam and Tur and follows Tosafos in Bava Kama 61b.
Apart from... Rashi in Bava Basra there.
Collected note: Even though... This is unlike Rabbi Shimon, whose position is rejected at the end of Bava Metzia. The Rif writes this expressly. The Rosh notes that the general rule of majority against an individual would suffice, but the Rif addresses the apparent conflict with the passage in Bava Kama by distinguishing a continuously maintained fire, where greater care is required, from an occasional fire. Thus the rulings are consistent.
Every person. Rashi indicates that even an upper-story owner must distance dangerous activity, and Tosafos agrees. Maharik proves this from the wording, which does not limit the rule to a neighbor's upper story.
