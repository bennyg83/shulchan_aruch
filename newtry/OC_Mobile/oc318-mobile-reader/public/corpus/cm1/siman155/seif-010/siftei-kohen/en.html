“No man shall break up in a hole, and he shall see in his return.”
And he must plaster the wall of the cistern with lime. The Sema explains that according to the Rambam this applies even to the neighbor’s masonry wall, whereas according to the Rosh and Tur, with a masonry wall one need only distance the harmful use three handbreadths and need not plaster with lime. I do not understand this. The Rosh and Tur rule leniently on the unresolved question, and in their view even the wall of a cistern need not be plastered. There is no distinction.
