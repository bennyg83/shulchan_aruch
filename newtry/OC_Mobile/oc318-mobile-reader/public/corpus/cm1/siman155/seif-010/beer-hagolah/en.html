There in the Mishnah, requiring coating with lime. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara, 19b, raised the question and initially said it was obvious that both measures were required. Although that proof was rejected, the commentators write that since the Gemara regarded both as obviously necessary, this remains the halachah. This is Rambam's view.
