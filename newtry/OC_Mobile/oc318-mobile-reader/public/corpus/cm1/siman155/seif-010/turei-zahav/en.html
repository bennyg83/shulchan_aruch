(Seif 18, in the gloss: and some disagree and say one of them suffices.) This is the Rosh's view cited by our master in seif 18. He holds that the Gemara's question was not resolved whether distancing a pit or cistern requires both three handbreadths and plastering with lime, or whether either one suffices. Because of the unresolved doubt, we cannot compel him to do both. The first opinion is that of Ramah, who follows Rambam and holds that since the Gemara initially says, "It is obvious that the Mishnah reads, and he plasters with lime," we do not disregard that conclusion merely because of the later rejection mentioned by the Maggid Mishneh. The Beit Yosef asks in seif 12 why our master did not cite the Rosh there as disagreeing with Rambam, whom our master cites in seif 12. According to what I have written, the answer is clear. Our master postponed the Rosh's opinion until the end because it all concerns the same issue. Accordingly, the Rama should have written this "some say" above in seif 10, on the Mechaber's phrase, "and he plasters with lime," etc.
