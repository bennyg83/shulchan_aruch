From the wall. The Rambam holds that the same law applies even to a constructed wall belonging to his fellow. The Rosh and Tur hold that for a constructed wall a distance of three handbreadths is sufficient and it need not also be plastered with lime. This is the Sema's language. The Shach writes that he does not understand this, because according to the Rosh and Tur, who rule leniently on the unresolved question, even with a wall there is no need to plaster with lime, and there is no distinction.
