There is a practical difference for the initial permissibility of creating the condition. Since the Gemara leaves the issue unresolved, one must at least refrain from doing it initially, as in seif 3. With an ordinary masonry wall, however, according to the Rosh it is permitted even initially. The distinction is therefore clear.
