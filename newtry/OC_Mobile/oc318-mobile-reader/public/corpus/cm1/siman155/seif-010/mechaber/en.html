A person may not dig a cistern, ditch, or cave, bring a water channel, or make a pool in which clothes are soaked for washing next to his neighbor's wall unless he distances it three handbreadths from the wall and lines with lime the side of this cistern, pool, or channel wall facing his neighbor, so that the water will not be absorbed and damage the neighbor's wall. {Rama: Some say that even if the water does not remain there constantly, such as a pipe that pours water from a house outdoors, it must nevertheless be distanced. Others disagree. With urine, however, all agree it is forbidden, as explained in seif 5. The same applies to a pit containing no water at all: it may not be dug next to the neighbor's wall, and protective measures must be made as expert craftsmen consider appropriate. See note 4.}
