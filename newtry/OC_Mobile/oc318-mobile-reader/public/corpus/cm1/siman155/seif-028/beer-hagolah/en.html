Tur, seif 41, in the name of a responsum of a Gaon. He explains that the neighbor cannot say, "You caused your own damage by raising your roof." He further writes that the branches leaning into Levi's property belong to Levi and he may eat their fruit by the law of a tree leaning from one property into another, Bava Metzia 107a, in accordance with Rav there because this was the accepted boundary custom, as Rav Yehudah said to Ravin there. However, Tur at the end of siman 167 does not rule this way, nor does Rambam below.
