The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is how Rambam's words must be read, because the Gemara explicitly says so, and it is compelled by the baraita requiring two cubits between trees. That baraita can only concern trees relative to trees.
The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rava bar Rav Chanan had certain date palms, etc. Ibn Migash explains in the name of Rif that Rava had distanced them only two cubits, as required in Babylonia, but Rav Yosef told him that between trees and vines four cubits are required as in Eretz Yisrael. Tur likewise writes this in Rif's name, and Ramban upholds this approach.
Tur, seif 35. Rosh writes this there, all the more so regarding an open field. Hagahot Ashri likewise writes this, and all the more so with trees or vines next to crops, from Tosafot there, s.v. 'both vines,' etc.
