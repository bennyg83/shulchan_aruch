To distance four amot. See Sema, seif katan 57, through his statement that the person can say, "I have already established my chazakah." According to this reasoning, many poskim would require three years together with a claim of acquisition, just as with a window in siman 154. When the Sema adds that this applies even if the neighbor says he does not intend to plow, his intent is that the neighbor wishes to erect a separating fence. Without such a measure, he cannot simply say, "I do not plan to plow," because the other owner can answer, "I am not required to post a guard to watch you," as Ramban explains in his novellae to the sugya.
