And the same applies to every substantial injury. See Tur at the end of this siman, citing a responsum of the Rosh. Reuven owned houses and butchers recently began selling meat outside a courtyard adjacent to his home. Reuven objected that he could not tolerate their proximity because of the foul odor of meat and the dogs and pigs attracted there. The Rosh ruled for Reuven. This remains so even if the butchers claim to have purchased the location from Reuven. See Beit Yosef, Derisha, and Bach there.
