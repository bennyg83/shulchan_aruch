One who established a use to do, etc. This is the correct text, as in Rambam, chapter 11, law 5, and not the printed text in some editions of Shulchan Aruch, 'one who distanced,' which forced Ir Shushan into a strained explanation.
And they destroy his produce, etc. This refers to a delicate person who is repulsed by fruit stained with blood. Ordinary people can wipe the blood off and eat the fruit. This is why the text later says, 'his produce is spoiled for him.' The word 'for him' indicates specifically a person of his sensitivities.
