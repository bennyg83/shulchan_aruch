The language of Rambam, chapter 11, law 8. Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam learned this from the incident involving Rav Yosef at the beginning of Bava Batra 23a.
Maggid Mishneh explains that even though ordinary people are not particular about such a matter, this person is unusually sensitive and objects. It is explained there that he may protest and that chazakah does not work against him. The commentators write that this applies where it is established and known that he cannot tolerate the disturbance. The Mechaber rules so below in seif 41.
