From digging a pit. The reason was explained in the preceding seif katan. Rashi and Rif disagree and hold that with other nuisances too one may not initially place the damaging activity nearby when the potentially damaged use is not yet present, if the neighbor is expected to establish it later. This is the dissenting view cited by Rama. Sema. See Beurim: even according to the opinion permitting one who lawfully established his use first, if the damage is renewed every day through an affirmative act after the damaged use later appears, such as pounding groats, he must afterward distance it. Regarding a window, this depends upon the dispute above in siman 154:15 whether visual damage is treated as a renewed direct injury. Conversely, even according to those who require later distancing where the first use was initially lawful, if the first person established his use next to ownerless property and only afterward the other acquired that ownerless property and created the vulnerable use, the first need not distance. Practically, the primary law follows the Mechaber and the first view cited by Rama.
