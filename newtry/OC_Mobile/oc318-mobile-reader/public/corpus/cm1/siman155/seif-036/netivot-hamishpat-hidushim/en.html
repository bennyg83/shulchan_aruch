Even if the damager maintained his use for three years, and even according to the view that the neighbor could have protested, it nevertheless does not establish chazakah according to the Ramban in siman 154, seif 16. Even according to those who hold that when one established a use permissibly before the vulnerable use existed, the later injured party must distance himself, as many authorities hold above in seif 19, smoke and a privy are different: the damager must later distance them. And if they made a kinyan from him that he waived. Without a kinyan, waiver does not help. It resembles one who says, "Tear my garment and be exempt," who may retract even after the other has begun tearing. A kinyan is therefore needed to strengthen the commitment. Bach. See the Beurim. Even with other types of damage, waiver without a kinyan is ineffective for the same reason. This applies specifically where he waived after the vulnerable use already existed. But if he gave permission to establish the damaging use before anything vulnerable was there, even with smoke or a privy the permission operates as waiver without a kinyan. It does not resemble saying, "Tear my garment." He simply can no longer demand that the other refrain from establishing the vulnerable use. The distinction between smoke or a privy and other damages exists only where the damager independently established his use before the vulnerable use arose, according to the view that permits him to do so.
