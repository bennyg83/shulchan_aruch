And damp sand, etc. I am surprised. According to the Mechaber, who follows Rambam, in damage caused by dampness he rules the unresolved question stringently and requires both distancing and plastering with lime, as he writes in seif 10. Damp sand is damage from moisture and resembles a water channel, as stated in the Gemara, and this is why the Mishnah did not list it separately. Why, then, did he include damp sand among cases that do not require both measures? The Tur is also somewhat difficult. Rambam does not mention damp sand at all, relying on the law of a water channel and the formulation of the Mishnah. Examine carefully. This appears to me to require study.
