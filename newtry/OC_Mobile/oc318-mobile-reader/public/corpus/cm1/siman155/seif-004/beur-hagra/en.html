Collected note: All this... The Gemara distinguishes a case where the damaging force does not come directly from him. Although indirect damage is forbidden, that case differs from one where he himself causes the material to fall, and from one's stone, knife, or burden negligently left on a roof. See siman 164, seif 1, in the gloss. The Bach explains that here the rainwater entered without the upper owner's negligence, such as an exceptional downpour, whereas defects in his own roof remain his responsibility.
