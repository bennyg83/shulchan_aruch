The language of the Tur from the incident in HaBayit VeHaAliyah, Bava Metzia 117a, and Rav Ashi's statement, "When I was in Rav Kahana's academy," etc. This is explained by Rif and Rosh there.
The language of Rambam, chapter 9, law 2, from the Mishnah in Bava Batra 17a.
Meaning stones from which heat radiates; all of these are damaging to a wall because they emit heat.
Tur, seif 8, from the teaching of Rabbi Oshaya and the Gemara's explanation on Bava Batra 19a.
There, and in Rambam. The Mishnah states that one plasters with lime, and the Gemara asks whether the text means that one must both distance and plaster or may either distance or plaster. The question is unresolved. The Rosh therefore writes that if one performed either measure, beit din does not compel him to do more, because in a monetary doubt the defendant has the advantage.
