The witnesses are believed. Even if the third party holding the document has a migo, we do not accept a migo against witnesses:
With a condition. Even if the parties appointed the third party in the presence of witnesses and those witnesses later died or traveled overseas, one party cannot say, "I did not rely on you because I appointed you before witnesses." Since witnesses can sometimes die or become unavailable, the parties necessarily relied on the third party and trusted him:
Contradicted. Although in siman 63, regarding witnesses, a contradiction is treated leniently and the claimant bears the burden of proof, that case concerns a contradiction in secondary details, which does not disqualify the witnesses. See the Taz there. With a third party, if we were to treat him as contradicted he would lose his credibility. Even if the contradiction concerns secondary details, the third party has the status of a litigant and can be undermined by such a contradiction. We therefore reconcile his statements where possible so as not to remove him from his established credibility. See Beurim, seif katan 12:
That the lender. This assumes that the borrower makes a definite claim. If he claims only uncertainty and knows the matter solely from the third party, while the lender contradicts the third party, that person does not have the special credibility of a third party, Shach. See Beurim, seif katan 5, that this limitation applies specifically to a document. With movable property, even where the claimant knows the facts only from the third party, the third party is believed.
