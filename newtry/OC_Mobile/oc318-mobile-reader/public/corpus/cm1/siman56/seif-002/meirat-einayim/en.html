We advance that explanation for him so that he will not be contradicted by the witnesses. One may ask that siman 30:3 says that if witnesses testified without sufficient detail we treat the matter as contradictory in the manner most favorable to the current possessor, because the burden of proof rests on the one seeking extraction. Here the custodian already possesses the document together with a presumption of reliability, and the witnesses seek to remove him from that position. We therefore reconcile their testimony with his words if possible. There, by contrast, the money remains in its owner's possession and the witnesses seek to extract it, so in the absence of complete testimony we treat them as contradicted. See Perishah for more.
