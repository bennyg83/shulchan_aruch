Although in a case of two conflicting groups of witnesses we ordinarily resolve the doubt leniently for the party in possession, as above in siman 30, there the rule is that the claimant must produce proof. Here the matter itself remains in the third party's possession. If we say that the testimonies contradict one another, we would be removing it from that established possession. It is therefore preferable to reconcile the statements so that the matter remains in its status quo, Sema and Shach. See what I wrote above in siman 30.
