Upon a condition. The same applies if he was appointed as a third party upon that condition before witnesses, but the witnesses are no longer present because they died or traveled overseas. One cannot say, 'I did not rely on you because I appointed you only before witnesses.' Since witnesses may die or travel away, the parties necessarily relied upon the third party and trusted him. This is explicit in Tosafot, the Rashba, and the other poskim. Thus concludes the Shach.
Contradicted. Although in siman 30, seif 3, where witnesses testify ambiguously we treat the testimony as contradictory in order to leave the money with its possessor, there the reason is precisely that the claimant bears the burden of proof. Here, if we were to treat the statements as contradictory, we would be removing the third party and the item in his possession from his established presumption of credibility. We therefore advance the argument on his behalf. So write the Sema and the Shach.
