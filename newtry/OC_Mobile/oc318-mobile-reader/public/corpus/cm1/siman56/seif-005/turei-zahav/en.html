(Seif 6: The borrower came with another person.) This phrase does not address the escrow holder’s credibility, for the Rama already ruled that it makes no difference whether the creditor alone placed the item in escrow. Rather, it means that the borrower agrees to the underlying agency and amount with the other person who claims to be the creditor’s agent. This had to be stated, for otherwise the escrow holder would not be believed, as explained in seif 1.
