(Netivot HaMishpat, same passage) They are considered deposited in Reuven's domain. Note: This requires study. According to the bailee himself, the property does not belong to the depositor but came into the depositor's hands through robbery. Why should it be considered as resting in the robber's domain? On the contrary, the bailee should deliver it to the victim in order to fulfill the mitzvah of restitution, since he knows that the robber himself will not return it. In my opinion this follows all the more strongly from the rule the Rosh states plainly in responsum 68, cited by the Beit Yosef at the end of siman 255.
