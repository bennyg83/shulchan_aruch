(Collected note) And this, etc. Meaning, a grievance concerning the fact that he returned the item. If the dispute concerns the money itself, he is not believed even while it is in his possession, as explained above in seif 1. Shach.
(Collected note) And that which he is believed like one witness, etc. Meaning, they have no grievance concerning the return itself, but, etc., as Tosafot write in Ketubot 84b, s.v. "migo," etc.
And likewise if he was. As stated in the Tosefta, he is like any other person. Sanhedrin there likewise states that Rav Nachman did not believe him once, etc., because it was then as though the item had left her possession.
