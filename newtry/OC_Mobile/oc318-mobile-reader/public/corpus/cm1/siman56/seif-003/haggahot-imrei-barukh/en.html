(Netivot HaMishpat, seif katan 18) He has a migo that he could say, "I am a stakeholder," etc. Note: One may answer that the person appointed as stakeholder is competent to testify about this. It was in his power to testify that the messenger was not himself appointed as stakeholder, since the messenger brought the pledge or document to him merely as an agent. Had he accepted it, he would have become the stakeholder. If the messenger now claims that he had already been appointed stakeholder, he is contradicted by this witness and would have to swear against the witness. He therefore has no valid migo.
