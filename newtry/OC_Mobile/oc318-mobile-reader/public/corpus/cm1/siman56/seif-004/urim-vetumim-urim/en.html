He is not believed. If he is otherwise fit to testify, Sema holds that the litigant need not swear to contradict him, because the litigant can still claim that his earlier admission was joking or made merely to avoid appearing wealthy so long as he never said, "You are my witness." Bach disagrees and says those explanations do not apply to admissions involving an escrow holder. The Shach agrees with Bach, and this is correct. See Tumim, seif katan 13. If the litigant instead claims payment, in a matter where that plea does not itself contradict the escrow holder, he is not believed through a migo of forgery. See there.
