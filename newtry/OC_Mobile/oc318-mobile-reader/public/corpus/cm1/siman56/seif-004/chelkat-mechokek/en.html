Or she became a niddah. There is no distinction between this occurring at her expected menstrual period and outside that period. She cannot say, "his field was stricken by misfortune," for even if he became ill or she became ill, it is an unavoidable circumstance. The Gemara in Ketubot 2a mentions such a distinction only within the conditional discussion whether, if he or she became ill, he must provide her support.
