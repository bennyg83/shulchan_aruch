(Netivot HaMishpat, there) On condition of such and such, etc. Note: This requires study. Since the third party knows that the debtor fulfilled the condition, the document is effectively paid, and he has the ability not to return it to the lender but to deliver it to the debtor. The same applies where the lender had to perform something, with the document to be void if he failed, and it is known that the lender violated the condition, as stated in the Sema.
