(Seif 4: The claimant shall pay the escrow fee.) The Beit Yosef cites this from the Hagahot Mordechai, whose old editions read that Rashi required the claimant to pay the escrow holder, relying on the principle that one who suffers pain goes to the physician. That is no proof. The Sema says the escrow benefits the claimant, but that is not necessarily so; the claimant may prefer direct delivery. Newer editions read “the messenger’s fee,” referring to the rule that if consultation is required, the question is sent elsewhere. In that context the proof is sound, although the Rosh holds that both parties pay the messenger. As for an escrow holder’s fee, the party requesting the escrow should pay; ordinarily it is presumed to benefit both parties.
