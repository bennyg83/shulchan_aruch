One who dies leaving adult and minor sons. It contains three seifim.
If one dies leaving adult and minor sons and the adults improve the estate, all improvement is shared, and the adults receive no labor compensation. If they first say before witnesses, 'See what our father left us; we will work and consume the increase,' the one who improves it receives the increase, provided the estate was suitable for division. If it was not yet suitable for division, the increase remains common.
