Lamed) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 5, based on the incident of Rav Safra. The Maggid Mishneh writes that some explain that all of the profit was his and that we do not say he receives merely a manager's half-share on behalf of his brothers. He writes that this is Rambam's view. The Beit Yosef concludes that even today we judge this way for a Torah scholar of comparable stature, contrary to the Nimukei Yosef.
