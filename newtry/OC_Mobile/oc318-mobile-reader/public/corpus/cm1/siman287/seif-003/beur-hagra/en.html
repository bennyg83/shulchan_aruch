And if they were adults, etc. Rabbi Ami's statement shows that the restriction depends specifically on minority. He did not accept the first objection, as Abaye explained that in the comparison case the parties knew of one another. This is the meaning of 'since it was not known, etc.' See 61.
And likewise a brother, etc. The same applies when one descends into the property of an adult brother. Since he knew of the other heir, he is compensated as one who entered without permission, as stated in seif 1 in the gloss. See 25.
