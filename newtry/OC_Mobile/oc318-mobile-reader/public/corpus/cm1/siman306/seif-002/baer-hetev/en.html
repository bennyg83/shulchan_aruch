Acquires. The Tur writes this here and in siman 339. See Even HaEzer, siman 28, where he writes in the name of Ri and the Rosh that the law follows the view that a craftsman acquires an interest in the improvement of the article, while immediately beforehand he writes that the Ramah holds that the law follows the view that a craftsman does not acquire such an interest. In Yoreh Deah, at the end of siman 120, the Tur also writes that authorities disagree on this point. The Shiltei Giborim and Maharshal already noted this contradiction, as I wrote in the Derishah on Even HaEzer. Sema. The Shach writes that in his view the practical ruling should be that this remains a doubt of law. See there, where he discusses the matter at length.
