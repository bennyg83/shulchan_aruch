[Shulchan Aruch] He gave it to craftsmen. Note: If one said to a craftsman, Make such and such an article and I will take it from you tomorrow, and the craftsman made it, but the customer then withdrew and refused to take it, he must compensate the craftsman for the entire loss under the law of garmi. So writes the Rosh in Responsa, end of klal 104, and so is ruled below in Shulchan Aruch, siman 333. See Sema above at the end of siman 39 regarding one who says, Slaughter your animal, and if it is found tereifah I will pay such and such for the meat, and if kosher such and such. If it is found tereifah, the purchaser may withdraw even if he gave collateral, as the Tur rules below, siman 207, in the name of a responsum of the Rosh. See Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot To’en VeNit’an 6:6.
