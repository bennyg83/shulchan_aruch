Without payment. If the slaughterer was an expert, he is exempt. He is considered expert after slaughtering small fowl correctly three times. Maharshal writes this there. I saw in a large edition of the Rif with Shiltei Gibborim, printed in the margin of Mordechai at the beginning of Perek HaMokher Peirot, the following: "I found it written that if a person sends something to another to slaughter, and it becomes a neveilah through his hand, the slaughterer is not liable. Although at the beginning of Perek HaMokher Peirot it is taught that one who brings an animal to a slaughterer and it is ruined must pay because he is a paid custodian, that applies specifically to a professional whose occupation is slaughtering, who does no other work and receives payment for this. He is therefore like a paid custodian. But if he does it only in exchange for incidental benefit, he is an unpaid custodian and exempt, for he is not negligent. If he were negligent, why would the Gemara in Bava Batra say that he is liable because he is like a paid custodian? He should be liable because of negligence. I am uncertain, however, whether we regard him as a paid custodian because of the blessings that he recites at the time of slaughter and thereby gains those blessings." It appears to me that some mistaken student wrote this and overlooked an explicit Gemara in Perek HaGozel Etzim. There the baraita from Perek HaMokher Peirot is established as referring to one who takes payment. Without payment, an expert is exempt, whereas an ordinary person is liable even without payment because he is negligent. See there.
Who take the entrails, etc. Although they take nothing from an animal found to be tereifah, nevertheless, since had it not become tereifah he would have received them, this constitutes his payment. Moreover, the entrails they receive from kosher animals are themselves compensation for the tereifah animals as well. This is the language of the Tur cited by the Sema. Based on the second reason, Maharshal writes that he is also considered a paid custodian regarding chickens. This is clear.
