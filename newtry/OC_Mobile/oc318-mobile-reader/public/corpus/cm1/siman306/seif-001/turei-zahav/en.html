(Seif 1, according to some texts seif 5) With respect to monetary liability, etc. The difficulty raised by the Sema, seif katan 10, from siman 232 is no difficulty. The case there is different, because the item remains forbidden, and there can be no greater error than that. The purchaser may say: Had I known this, I would not have given you my money, just as in every mistaken transaction. Here, by contrast, one seeks to impose liability upon another because of a doubt. The Rivash writes this explicitly in responsum 298, cited briefly by the Beit Yosef in Yoreh Deah, siman 119, seif 14. See our comments there. The Sema strained unnecessarily to resolve the matter.
