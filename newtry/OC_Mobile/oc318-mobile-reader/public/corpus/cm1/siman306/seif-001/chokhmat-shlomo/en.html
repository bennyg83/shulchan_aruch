Seif 1. Note: See Sema and Shach, seif katan 1, and see my fourth edition on Choshen Mishpat, responsum 191, together with Netivot HaMishpat there. The Gemara disputes whether one who deviates from the owner's instructions thereby becomes a robber. In my fourth edition on Yoreh De'ah, responsum 182, second responsum, I discussed this in reconciling Rambam's laws of Megillah and several related matters. Seif 2: An artisan does not acquire the improvement he creates in an article. I discussed whether, when two artisans jointly take an article to repair, the rule that an artisan acquires the improvement applies to them as well, and whether the artisan acquires only the value of the improvement or an ownership interest in the body of the article itself. A practical difference arises if the article appreciates or depreciates, because that determines how the original body is valued. See my work on Bava Kamma, Perek HaGozel, page 99, where I cite the views of the Rishonim at length. I also noted there that Ketzot HaChoshen apparently did not see the Rashba I cited, for had he seen it he likely would not have written what he did.
