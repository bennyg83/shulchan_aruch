The foregoing applies when the slaughterer certainly rendered the animal a neveilah. If he caused a possible tereifah, such as by pausing during slaughter when only a minority of the simanim remained, he is exempt from monetary liability. Likewise, if he finds the knife nicked after slaughter even though he checked it beforehand, since it is possible that the knife was nicked by striking the neck bone, money cannot be extracted on the basis of doubt. Rama: Even though our practice is to prohibit the animal in such a case, he is nevertheless exempt from monetary payment. This appears correct, despite an authority who disagrees. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 186. In any event he forfeits his fee, because perhaps he slaughtered with a defective knife. Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham and Rosh.
