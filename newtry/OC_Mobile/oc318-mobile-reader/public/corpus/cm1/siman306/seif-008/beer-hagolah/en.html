b) Ibid., at the end of the cited chapter, from the statement concerning a teacher of children in Bava Metzia 109a.
c) From the incident of Ronya, Ravina's gardener, and so too the Raavad writes there. The Maggid Mishneh likewise cites the incident of Ronya. He explains that if one slaughtered improperly without pay, he is removed so that he may no longer do so either for pay or without pay. Alternatively, even if he slaughtered for pay and reimburses the loss, he is removed because of the embarrassment caused and the value of the guests' embarrassment, or because at times meat is urgently needed. Maggid Mishneh; Chiddushei Rashba in the name of the Raavad.
d) According to the Rif's explanation in the Halakhot.
e) According to Rashi's explanation there: once an error has entered, it has entered. The Maggid Mishneh writes that according to Rashi this means where the teacher himself errs in the matter and does not know it. But if he knows the matter and merely fails to supervise one of the children when that child states it incorrectly, he is not removed.
