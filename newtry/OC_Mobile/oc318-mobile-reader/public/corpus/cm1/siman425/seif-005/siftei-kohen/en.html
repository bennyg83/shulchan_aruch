It is a mitzvah to rescue him, etc. Nevertheless, one is not obligated to redeem him if he swore in a manner that produced the predicament. The point is only that if someone wishes to redeem him, there is no prohibition in doing so. See the Beit Yosef and Rama in Yoreh Deah, siman 251, seif 2, and siman 252, seif 6, and the Bach there.
