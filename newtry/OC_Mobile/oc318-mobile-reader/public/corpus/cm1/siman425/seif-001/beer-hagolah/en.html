a) From Rabbi Elazar ben Yaakov's statement, 'I heard that beit din may administer corporal punishment and impose penalties,' Sanhedrin 46a in chapter Nigmar HaDin.
bet) Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilkhot Rotzeach, law 6, and Tur, seif 3, from the Mishnah and Gemara in Sanhedrin at the end of chapter Ben Sorer uMoreh, 72b and 73a.
gimel) Rav Huna's statement there.
dalet) A baraita in the name of Rabbi Yonatan ben Shaul there at the beginning of 74a and in several other places.
heh) The Maggid Mishneh writes that the meaning must be: since the pursuer had not yet killed, he did not otherwise deserve death, yet he may nevertheless be killed in order to save the pursued person. But once he has already killed, others may not kill him merely on that basis.
vav) Mishnah and Gemara in Sanhedrin, chapter Ben Sorer uMoreh, 73a and 73b.
