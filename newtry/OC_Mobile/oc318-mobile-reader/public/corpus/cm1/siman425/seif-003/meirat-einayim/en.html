At the end of chapter Ben Sorer U'Moreh, the Gemara derives that one may save a victim at the pursuer's life only in cases of forbidden relations involving degradation and injury to the pursued person, such as a betrothed girl who would be violated, or a male who is not designated for such use and would suffer great disgrace. This is learned from the verse, "to the girl you shall do nothing... there was no one to save her." Scripture is written one way and read another, allowing the male case to be derived as well, while the extra wording includes other forbidden relations. The passage also contains an exclusion, from which we exclude one pursuing an animal for intercourse or one pursuing another to desecrate Shabbos, where there is no comparable personal degradation or injury to a victim.
