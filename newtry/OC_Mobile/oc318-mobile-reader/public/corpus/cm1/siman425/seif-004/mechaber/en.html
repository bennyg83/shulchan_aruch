If a man was pursuing a woman to violate a forbidden relation with her, and others were pursuing him to save her, and she said to them, “Leave him, so that he will not kill me,” they do not heed her. Rather, they frighten him and prevent him by striking his limbs; if they cannot stop him by striking his limbs, they may even take his life. Rama: One who has relations with a non-Jewish woman in public, in the presence of ten Jews, may be struck down by zealots, who are permitted to kill him. This applies only during the act; once he has separated from her, it is forbidden to kill him. It applies only where they warned him and he did not desist, and only where the zealot comes to kill him on his own initiative. If he asks the beit din, they do not instruct him to do so.
