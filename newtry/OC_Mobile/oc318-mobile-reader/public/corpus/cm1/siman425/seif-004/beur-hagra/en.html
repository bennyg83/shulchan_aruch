Before ten Jews, etc. As stated in Sanhedrin 74b regarding whether public transgression requires ten; the Gemara says, "is this not obvious," etc.
Specifically where they warned him, etc. The Raavad comments on the Rambam's statement that zealots who strike him are praiseworthy and zealous: this applies where he was warned. He does not explain that without warning the act becomes permitted in the same praiseworthy sense. The Raavad derives this from seif 1, "and they warned him," etc.
