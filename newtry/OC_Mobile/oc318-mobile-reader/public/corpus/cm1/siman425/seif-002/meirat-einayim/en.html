This is the nature of the world. The Mechaber needed to say this lest one argue that the fetus is pursuing the mother and should therefore be killed under the law of a pursuer. Since difficulty in childbirth is a natural process, the fetus is not assigned the legal status of a pursuer. Nevertheless, while it remains inside the mother it may be cut up to save her even though it is alive, because before it emerges into the world it does not yet have the full legal status of an independent person. Proof appears from the law that one who strikes a pregnant woman and causes the fetuses to die pays monetary compensation for them and is not treated as a murderer. See siman 423.
