The ruling teaches that since this is the natural process of the world, the fetus does not have the status of a pursuer. Nevertheless, while it remains in her womb it may be cut apart even though it is alive, because until it emerges into the world it does not have the legal status of a nefesh. The proof is the law of one who strikes a pregnant woman and causes her children to emerge and die: he pays the value of the fetuses but is not liable as a murderer or subject to death, as explained in siman 423.
