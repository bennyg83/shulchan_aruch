"One who hates gifts shall live." This is a verse in Proverbs 15:27, and the Gemara expounds it this way at the beginning of the third chapter of Kiddushin. The Sages' intent is that people ordinarily pursue money, thinking that by doing so they sustain themselves and their households. The verse teaches the opposite: one who hates gifts shall live, because a person who is eager for gifts must flatter people and does not rebuke them for the wrongful conduct he sees in them.
