(Seif 1: one who says he will give, etc.) In Perek Lulav VaAravah, Rabbi Zeira says that a person should not tell a child, "I will give you something," and then fail to give it, because this trains the child in falsehood, as the verse says, "They trained their tongues to speak falsehood." If one gives a gift to his fellow and only after it has reached the recipient's possession imposes some condition upon the recipient, the condition is ineffective even if stated within the immediate span of speech. I demonstrated this in Even HaEzer, siman 38, seif 1.
