A small amount. But not a large amount. This distinction applies specifically to a wealthy person. With a poor person there is no distinction, and even a large amount is acquired by virtue of the law of a vow, as explained in siman 125, seif 5, and in simanim 204 and 243. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 94, and Responsa Mabit, part I, siman 122.
