Bet) A baraita at the end of the second chapter of Avodah Zarah 64b, following Rabbi Yehudah. Rambam writes in chapter 14 of Hilkhot Issurei Biah that a ger toshav is accepted only when the Jubilee is observed. The Beit Yosef writes that in this law there is no distinction between ordinary gentiles and Yishmaelites.
Gimel) Tosafot there in the name of the Tosefta. The same applies where a gentile accompanies him on a journey, based on the incident in Eruvin 64b where one said, "Take this cake," etc.
Dalet) From the rule that we support poor gentiles, etc., in the baraita in Gittin 61a. Tosafot write that an act done for the ways of peace is not considered a gratuitous gift.
