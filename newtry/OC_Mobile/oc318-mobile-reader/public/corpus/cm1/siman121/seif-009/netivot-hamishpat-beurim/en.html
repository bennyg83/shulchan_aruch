The debtor is not believed. See Shach seif katan 30 through his discussion of the condition "do not repay me except before witnesses." This is not comparable to one who says, "I repaid you before so and so and so and they have gone away," even though repayment can be made through the creditor's handwriting just as it can before witnesses. Here the writing is before us and cannot be authenticated, which weakens the debtor's claim. He therefore needs specifically a migo that he could have said "I repaid you." Since under the stipulation he cannot claim ordinary repayment, he is likewise not believed to say that he recognizes the writing as the creditor's handwriting.
