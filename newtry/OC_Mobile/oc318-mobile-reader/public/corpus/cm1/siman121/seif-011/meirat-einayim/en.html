The law is the same as explained where he says, "carry it," etc. The Tur says there is no distinction here between "carry a maneh" and "give a maneh to so-and-so." Although for ordinary acquisition there is a distinction between "carry" and "give," here there is not. The stronger explanation is that one might have distinguished between "give him what you owe me," where the debt is not physically present and perhaps the debtor will delay, and "carry these actual coins" already placed in the messenger's hand, where there is a presumption that an agent performs his agency. The ruling rejects the distinction.
