I will not be like him. There:
But they do not testify to Hashem’s name and plan:
And he places under a ban, etc.; and some say, etc. Shulchan Aruch already ruled this in seif 4 and repeats it here to teach that delivery is not invalid because the messenger was never appointed at all. Rather, the creditor is believed to deny that the writing is his handwriting, as Rambam and Shulchan Aruch explain.
