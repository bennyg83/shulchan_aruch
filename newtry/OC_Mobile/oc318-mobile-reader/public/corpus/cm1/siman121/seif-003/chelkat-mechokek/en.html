See above, siman 119. Meaning that above it was ruled concerning a woman who alternates between lucidity and insanity that she is not divorced unless it appears she will remain lucid. If she has a fixed cycle of lucidity and insanity, and he divorces her during a lucid period knowing she will return to insanity, some authorities hold she is not divorced, as Mahari Weil writes in the name of Rashba and Maharshal discusses at length. This stringency was stated specifically regarding a woman because the Sages were concerned that she would later be exposed to abuse or neglect. Even though she is lucid now, that concern remains because she will return to insanity. But regarding a husband who gives the divorce, if he is lucid at the relevant time, why should it matter that he may later return to insanity?
