Shimon must pay. The Tur disagrees and rules that Shimon swears and is exempt. See the Shach, who explains that the Tur inferred this from the Rambam. The Rambam's preceding language concerns a borrower who asserts definitely that he sent the agent to collect fifty. If so, why should the borrower have to pay? He should swear and be exempt. See the Shach's lengthy discussion, which is correct. The Rambam, however, can be explained as discussing a debt evidenced by a document, in which case the borrower is not believed. This is why the Rambam did not state that he must swear, since the same law applies generally to all documented debts. This is simple and correct.
