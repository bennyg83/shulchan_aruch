If Levi came as Reuven's agent to collect fifty dinars owed by Shimon and collected all fifty, but Reuven says, 'I sent him to collect only twenty and to bring me only twenty,' Shimon must pay the balance after the lender takes an oath while holding a sacred object that he sent Levi to bring only twenty and received only twenty. The agent takes a heses oath to Shimon that he gave all fifty to the lender.
