Or a mute who hears but does not speak. A mute here means one who was born that way. See Beit Yosef, who writes that such a person does not require examination, because just as he entered marriage through gestures, he leaves it through gestures. Bach writes, however, that if a yevamah fell to him from his brother, where the bond is Torah law, he requires examination like one who became mute after having entered a fully Torah marriage.
