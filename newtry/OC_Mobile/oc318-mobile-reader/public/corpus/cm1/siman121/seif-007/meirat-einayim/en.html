The agent is not believed, etc. Meaning he is not believed so as to be exempt without an oath under the special law of a third-party custodian, even though he has a migo that he could have said the borrower never gave him the money, nor is he believed so as to exempt the borrower from the general ban.
Because they are interested parties. A third-party custodian against whom there is a complaint must swear. Here too, had the messengers said that the borrower never gave them the money, they would have had to swear. Even after the two messengers take hesset oaths that they delivered it to the lender, they are not treated as independent witnesses sufficient to exempt the borrower from the ban, because anyone who must swear about his own role in the event does not have ordinary witness status for that purpose.
