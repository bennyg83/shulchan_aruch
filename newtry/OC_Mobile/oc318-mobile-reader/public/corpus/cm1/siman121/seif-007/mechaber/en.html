When does the preceding rule apply? When the agent admits that the money reached him and claims it was lost through unavoidable accident. But if the dispute is between the agent and the lender or depositor, with the agent saying, 'I gave it to you,' and they saying, 'We did not receive it,' then even if the lender and depositor are two people, they are not believed because they have an interest in the matter. The agent takes a heses oath and is exempt, while the borrower or custodian accepts an unspecified ban upon anyone falsely claiming that the money was delivered to the agent.
