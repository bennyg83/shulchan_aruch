The creditor need not tell the agent, 'Tell so-and-so to send through you the money he holds for me.' It is enough that he says before witnesses, 'I have money in so-and-so's possession. Go tell him that you intend to come here, and if he gives it to you, bring it to me.' If the borrower then gives it to the agent and it is lost on the way, the borrower is exempt. But if the creditor did not appoint him as an agent and merely said, 'I have money in so-and-so's possession, and he does not send it to me. Perhaps he cannot find an agent through whom to send it. Present yourself to him, and perhaps he will send it to me through you,' the person is not an agent. If the borrower gives it to him and it is lost, the borrower remains responsible.
