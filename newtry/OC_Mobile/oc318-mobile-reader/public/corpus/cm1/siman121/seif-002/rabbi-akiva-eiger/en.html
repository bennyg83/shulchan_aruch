[Sema, subparagraph 14] It is an actual Torah oath. Note: It appears to me that this is specifically where the depositor’s handwriting serves as the evidence, because by Torah law such handwriting requires authentication, as discussed in siman 69, Shach subparagraph 12. But where witnesses’ handwriting is involved, there is no Torah oath, because by Torah law it requires no authentication, and the custodian is believed by Torah law to claim unavoidable loss even without a migo.
