Or a minor, deaf-mute, or incompetent. Perhaps the rule stated here applies only where the principal sends such a person to make payment. If he sends a minor to borrow for him from someone else, and the minor is not sufficiently sharp and competent to safeguard the money, all the more so an incompetent person who destroys property, the matter may fall into the dispute in siman 380 concerning one who says, 'Throw a maneh into the sea and I will pay you.' With repayment, however, perhaps everyone would agree that the principal is exempt. This requires further study.
