If the agent... This resembles the Kiddushin rule cited above, but there the creditor and agent swear to the borrower where the person had not been made an agent. Here the alignment is reversed. Compare Bava Metzia 58a concerning whether the communal contribution had already been taken. Ran in Kiddushin explains that where the borrower and lender each make a definite claim their positions can combine in the relevant way.
In the presence of... in the presence of... As stated in Shevuos concerning the shopkeeper and workers, and in the Yerushalmi that both parties swear in one another's presence.
To the borrower or depositary. The text should read "to the lender or depositor," because the oath is taken for their benefit. Bava Metzia says that the custodians swear to the treasurers, not to the owners. See Shach, seif katan 40.
