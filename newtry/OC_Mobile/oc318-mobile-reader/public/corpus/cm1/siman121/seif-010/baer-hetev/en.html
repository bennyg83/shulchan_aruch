With certainty. Although the debtor claims that the messenger told him with certainty that he had paid the creditor, the messenger did not make that definite claim before beit din against the creditor. See above, siman 75, seif 23. If the messenger had made a definite claim before us, then even if he later died or went overseas, the creditor would have to swear. This follows from siman 91, seif 2, concerning a shopkeeper or workers who died. There the reason no oath is required is that the employer is paying only once. Here, however, the debtor already paid the messenger and must now pay the creditor or his heirs, so he pays twice. See above in siman 91 and Responsa Rashach, part III, siman 51. Shach. See also Responsa Shav Yaakov, Choshen Mishpat, siman 5.
