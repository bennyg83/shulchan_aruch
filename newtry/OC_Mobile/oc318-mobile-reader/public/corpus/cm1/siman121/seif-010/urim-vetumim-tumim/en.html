He cannot require him to swear, etc. The Shach writes that even if the borrower says, "The agent told me that he paid you," nevertheless, since the agent is not before us, the borrower cannot require the lender to swear. He correctly explains the Mechaber according to the latter's view that one cannot impose an oath on a claim based merely on "I was told." But according to what I wrote above, that this agent is not considered monetarily interested, and according to our ruling that one may require an oath on the basis of "I was told," the borrower can likewise require the lender to swear in this case.
