There, seif 5, from the Gemara's conclusion: now that the Sages instituted a heset oath, etc., Kiddushin 43b.
It is as though the debtor says with certainty, "I repaid you," because once he gave it to the creditor's messenger it is considered as having reached the creditor's hand. The creditor says, "I do not know whether you repaid me." The creditor therefore has only the right to impose a general cherem upon anyone withholding money.
