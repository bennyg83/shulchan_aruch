If the claimant admits. This law applies both to an oral loan and to a loan documented by a note, because once the claimant admits the facts, the force of the note is nullified. See Shach.
The messenger swears, etc. The Shach, seif katan 37, cites the Mordechai that where Reuven sent money to Levi and an unavoidable accident occurred, the messenger need not swear to Levi until Reuven comes, because perhaps Reuven will repay the lender again, in which case there is no oath to administer. The Shach concludes that if Reuven died, however, the messenger must swear. See what I wrote in Tumim, seif katan 10.
