If the claimant admits... See Beer HaGolah. Compare above, siman 72 seif 14 in the gloss. Once the debtor paid according to the creditor's instruction, he is discharged, like the employer who says, "What claim do I have against the workers?" The special liability in the shopkeeper case exists because the workers contradict the shopkeeper, which is absent here. This is the meaning of the next seif's distinction.
