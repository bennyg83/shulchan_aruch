Sending a debt through an agent. It contains 12 se'ifim.
If a person sends a debt or deposit through an agent, and the lender told him to send it through that agent, the sender is exempt if it is lost. This applies even when the lender did not tell him face to face but wrote, 'Send it to me through so-and-so,' and the sender sent it through that person, provided the sender recognizes the lender's handwriting. It applies even if the agent is a non-Jew, a deaf-mute, a mentally incompetent person, or a minor. Even if he did not identify a particular person by writing, 'Send it through so-and-so,' but instead wrote, 'Send it to me through whomever you wish,' the sender is exempt if he sends it and it is lost or the agent denies receiving it, provided he used a trustworthy person and a route by which people ordinarily transport money. If he sent it through a person established as a denier, or by a dangerous route through which the public does not ordinarily transport money, he was negligent and is liable, because the lender did not authorize him on that understanding unless he expressly did so.
