Seif 9. 'When is this so? When he appointed him as an agent to bring what belongs to him,' etc. Note: See my Choshen Mishpat, third edition, siman 269, where I wrote that even the borrower's son, or a borrower whose entire business is conducted with his father, has the same status as any other person. The lender swears and collects. Even with a depositor, although a person deposits with the understanding that his wife and children may handle the deposit, and they are believed to say that it was lost through unavoidable circumstances, nevertheless they are not believed regarding its return. The law is as one who says, 'I do not know whether I returned it,' and as explicitly ruled here, the lender swears and collects. See there for the reason.
