Even if the agent is a non Jew. See what I wrote above in siman 106, seif katan 1, distinguishing between throwing the money on the ground and sending it through a deaf mute, a mentally incompetent person, or a minor. Ketzot HaChoshen brings a proof from Tosafot in Bava Metzia that one should be liable when sending through a non Jew, but his proof is difficult and, in my view, incorrect.
