This is not my handwriting. This applies specifically where he denies the handwriting itself. But if he admits the handwriting and merely says, 'I wrote it to send and then changed my mind, and this person found it,' or, 'I signed my name on a blank sheet and this person found it and wrote above it,' he is not believed. See Shach, contrary to the Bach.
