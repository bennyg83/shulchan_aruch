Into the hand of your agent. But he must take a severe oath. There is an obvious and necessary omission in the text. It first says that the defendant is believed with an oath of hesset and then concludes that he must take a severe oath. There is also no reason to impose a severe oath where the deposit was made without a document, because had he denied the deposit entirely he would have been exempt with hesset. The text also becomes repetitive. Moreover, it begins by addressing a deposit made orally and without witnesses but never introduces the law of a deposit documented by a writing. The correct text therefore is: 'Into the hand of your agent. And if it was deposited with him by document, since he could have said that unavoidable circumstances occurred, he is believed to say, I delivered it into the hand of your agent, but he must take...' This is explicit in the Tur. Once the text is corrected this way, Rama's following gloss, 'specifically with a loan, but with a deposit,' is unnecessary.

After the lender takes a severe oath. This is a Mishnah level oath, like the oath of one who swears and collects, discussed above in simanim 82 and 87.
