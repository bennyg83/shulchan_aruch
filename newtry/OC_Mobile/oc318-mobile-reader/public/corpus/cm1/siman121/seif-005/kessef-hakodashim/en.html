Seif 8. (If the messenger denies it.) In siman 121, where the messenger says that he did not receive the full amount, the borrower says that he gave it, and the lender says that he did not receive it, a Torah oath can fall upon the borrower. Perhaps if the borrower says that he gave the full amount and the messenger says that he does not know exactly how much he received, but knows that nothing remains in his hands, while the lender says that he received only half, the messenger may become one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear. If so, unless the messenger can say "here it is" with respect to the admitted portion, there may be no Torah oath upon the borrower merely through combining the lender's statement with the messenger's uncertainty. This requires further study.
