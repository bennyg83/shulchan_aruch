He said, "I have money." In Shach, seif katan 11, the words "the borrower is not liable" are a textual error and should read "the custodian is not liable." A depositor's custodian is exempt when he transfers the deposit to another custodian to whom the owner regularly entrusts his property. A borrower, however, remains liable even if he gives the money to the lender's wife, as explained in siman 120.
