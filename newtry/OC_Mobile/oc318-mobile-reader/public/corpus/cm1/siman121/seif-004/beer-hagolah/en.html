In accordance with Rav Yehudah in the name of Shmuel there, as cited in the preceding seif.
Since he does not know whether the handwriting is genuinely his, it is like a creditor saying, "You owe me one hundred," and the debtor saying, "I do not know whether I repaid you."
Kesef Mishneh writes that the reason must be that perhaps he wrote the instruction and forgot. The Sages imposed an oath on him so that he would examine carefully all that he wrote, analogous to HaKotev concerning one who admits partial payment of her ketubah, Ketubot 87b: one is precise when paying but not always precise in recalling payment, and therefore the Sages imposed an oath.
