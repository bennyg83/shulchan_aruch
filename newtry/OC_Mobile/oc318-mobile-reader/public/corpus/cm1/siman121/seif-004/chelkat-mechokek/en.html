That he be lucid at the time of writing. All the more so he must be lucid when he gives the instruction to write, for that is the essential point. There is no concern, however, if he becomes mentally incompetent between writing and delivery. Beit Yosef writes that perhaps if they failed to examine a dying man and wrote the get on his instruction, it is valid after the fact. Perhaps even where he became mute through illness and they failed to examine him, everyone would agree that it is valid after the fact where no other possibility existed, as in the case performed by Baal HaItur cited by Beit Yosef in this siman. But if he became mute before they ever asked whether he wanted the get, formal examination is certainly required, as explained in the following seif. Without examination the case remains doubtful. See also Beit Yosef regarding a case where his mind became disturbed in the middle of writing, they stopped, he later recovered, and they completed the get. The get is valid.
