f) Tur, and so it appears from the words of the Rambam there, law 4. So too writes the Ramban in Responsa, siman 279, that in such matters one may judge on the basis of related witnesses, hearsay testimony, and the like, when it appears that the matter is true. Full witnesses were required only for Torah law, but not for one who violates communal regulations. Likewise, no warning is required. The Rashba writes that in capital matters they must nevertheless be cautioned to act with the agreement of the elders of the city, only when there is great need and with deliberation. Sema.

The Ran writes, however, that with a Torah scholar, "cover him like night," for Reish Lakish said that a Torah scholar who sinned is not placed under public excommunication, Menachot 99b.
