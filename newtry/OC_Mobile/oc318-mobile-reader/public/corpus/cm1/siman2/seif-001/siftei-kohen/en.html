Every beit din, etc. See Responsa Maharam of Lublin, siman 138, regarding measures taken to safeguard the law.
When the people are lawless, etc. Even when only one individual is lawless, the judges may punish that individual as they deem proper. This is explicit in the Tur, the other authorities, and the Gemara. The Sema writes likewise.
Even when there is no complete testimony, etc. Even without prior warning, as stated in Responsa Ramban, siman 279, cited by the Sema. In capital matters, however, they must be cautioned to act with the consent of the elders of the city, so that they proceed only in a case of great necessity and with deliberation. Beit Yosef writes this in the name of the Rashba, and the Sema writes likewise.
