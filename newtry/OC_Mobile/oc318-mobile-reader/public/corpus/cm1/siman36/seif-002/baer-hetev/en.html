Recognized. The Sema writes that this applies specifically when the witness himself volunteers that he did not recognize the disqualified person; beit din does not ask him whether he recognized him. The Shach challenges this and concludes that beit din must ask him. See there.
Who testified. This follows the second view in seif 1. According to the first view there, the same rule applies even if the disqualified person did not testify with him in beit din, once they saw the act together and both intended to testify. Shach.
