He pays half damages. Maggid Mishneh explains that even if the dog was already forewarned, it pays only half damages, because it did not bite on its own initiative but only because it was incited, making the act unusual.
If he incited it against himself. Meaning, Reuven incited Shimon's dog until it went and bit Reuven, the inciter. Shimon is exempt, because Reuven first acted unusually by inciting it, and whenever one party deviates and another then responds with an unusual act, as here where Shimon's dog responds, Shimon is exempt.
