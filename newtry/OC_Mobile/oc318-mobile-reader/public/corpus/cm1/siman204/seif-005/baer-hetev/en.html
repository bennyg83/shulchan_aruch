A pledge. The curse was enacted only when money was actually given. Although the formula of the curse speaks of one who does not stand by his word, nevertheless, because money acquires by Torah law, one should not retract after payment. Nimukei Yosef in chapter HaZahav cites the Yerushalmi: if one gives a ring as earnest money and wishes to retract, he may retract without Mi Shepara. Rabbi Zeira asked Rabbi Abahu: what if he gave a gold coin? He answered: a ring is different. What distinguishes a gold coin from a ring? A gold coin is ordinarily given to be spent, while a ring is expected to return intact. Meaning, if he gave a gold coin as security he must accept Mi Shepara because sometimes a gold coin is given as actual payment, whereas a ring is always returned as the same object. Bach.
