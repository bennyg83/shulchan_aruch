The Yerushalmi at the end of Sheviit states that one who gives a ring as earnest money may retract without Mi Shepara. Rabbi Zeira asked Rabbi Abahu why gold differs from a ring. He answered that gold is subject to fluctuation, whereas a ring remains the same object. Meaning, when gold is given as security, Mi Shepara applies, unlike a ring. The Yerushalmi in HaZahav similarly states concerning the incident with silk that a ring does not constitute earnest money, and anyone who negotiates only through words is not subjected to Mi Shepara.
