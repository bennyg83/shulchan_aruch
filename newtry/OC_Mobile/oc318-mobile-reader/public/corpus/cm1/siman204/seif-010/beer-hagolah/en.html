(b) The Rambam is there. It is written that the author’s opinion, and so on, is said to him, and that they are likened to him, and that he has been circumcised in his name
The Raavad in his gloss, from the statement in Kiddushin, 6b, that they agree regarding a sale, etc. The Maggid Mishneh likewise cites the Yerushalmi and writes that the Ramban and Rashba rule so. He concludes that the approach of the later authorities is primary. The Tur, seif 7, likewise writes in the name of his father the Rosh that this applies even while the money remains intact. The Maggid Mishneh further writes that if the seller wrote him a deed, the buyer acquires by the deed because it is like a gift. Other authorities write so as well.
