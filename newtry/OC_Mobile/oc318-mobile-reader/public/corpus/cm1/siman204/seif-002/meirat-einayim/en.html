Because he is afraid to lose means that you have not lost at all, and the seller can tell him when you lose me, I will give you a dirty fee and no time you have lost, you don’t have to return because the receiver can say I won’t be able to recover with you later in Dain and Dana, even if I lose you won’t have anything to hurt me:
To lose the entire transaction. The wording is precise. This excludes a case where he fears only a partial loss, for example where he bought wine and fears that some of it may sour. Some authorities write this distinction. See the Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe for the differing opinions.
