He gave, etc. There, 47b: the enactment was made lest a fire occur, etc. The Rif writes that we learn from here, etc., and the Rambam rules likewise. In Chullin and Eruvin, regarding the four periods of the year, etc., the rule is that if the animal dies, etc.; this implies that even Mi Shepara does not apply. The Gemara there likewise asks from the four periods, “But he did not perform meshichah, etc.”
Some say, etc. This is derived from the incident involving the household of Parzak Rufila, there 49b, where Rav Chisda did not require them to accept Mi Shepara. See Tosafos 47b, s.v. “if.” Rabbeinu Tam explains differently. The first opinion holds that in truth Mi Shepara is required, but the question there was only whether he may retract at all. This is like the Gemara there on 48a: “But does he not have to accept Mi Shepara?” and the answer, “Here too.” The first case did not mention it because that was not the question being asked.
