It is proper for him to stand by his word. Our Sages expounded the verse "a just hin" to mean that one's yes should be just and one's no should be just. According to the Rambam and Mechaber, even if the market price later rises, one should not retract, and therefore they state the rule without qualification. The Rosh and Tur hold that if the seller retracts because the price rose, or the buyer because it fell, there is no such moral reproach. See their words and Darkei Moshe.
