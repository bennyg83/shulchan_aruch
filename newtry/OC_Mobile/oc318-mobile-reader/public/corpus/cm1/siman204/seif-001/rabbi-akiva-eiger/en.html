[Shulchan Aruch] Whether the buyer. Note: It appears obvious that even if the seller said that he would not have the ability to object, this remains mere words and he may retract. However, if the buyer said that he would not have the ability to retract if the price falls, Pnei Yehoshua, Bava Metzia 68, Tosafot s.v. "if it is seized," inclines to the possibility that this is effective, because it is as though the buyer said that the money should be deemed waived if he retracts. See there. See also the Rama above, siman 158, seif 5.
