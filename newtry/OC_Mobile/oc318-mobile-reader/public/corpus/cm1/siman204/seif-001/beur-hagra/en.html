He did not act, etc. There, 48b, deriving from the verse “among your people, etc.” See Rashi and the Rif’s wording. It follows that conduct of this kind is not considered proper Jewish conduct.
And even, etc. See Beer HaGolah. The Yerushalmi states this explicitly: a certain person gave dinars to silk merchants and then retracted. The case came before Rabbi Chiya bar Yosef and Rabbi Yochanan. Rabbi Chiya bar Yosef said: either he must give him according to the amount of his earnest money or be subjected to Mi Shepara. Rabbi Yochanan said: either he must complete the entire purchase or be subjected to Mi Shepara.
