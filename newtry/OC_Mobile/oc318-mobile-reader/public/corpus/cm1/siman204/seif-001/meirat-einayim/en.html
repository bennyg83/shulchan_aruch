To accept who is disturbed near S.D., the author wrote:
Even if he gave only part of the money. This means even if he wishes to retract only from the unpaid remainder. As explained in siman 190, with land a partial payment acquires the whole property when the seller is not pressing for the balance. The same principle applies to the sanction of "He Who exacted punishment" in movable property, as the Tur and Mechaber write in siman 190:17.
