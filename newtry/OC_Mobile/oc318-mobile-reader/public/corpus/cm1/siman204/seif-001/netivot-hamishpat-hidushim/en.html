And he must accept "He Who exacted punishment." The same applies where one party was overcharged by exactly one sixth. The sale stands and the ona'ah must be returned. If the party who caused the ona'ah is willing to refund it but the injured party refuses to uphold the transaction, the injured party is subject to "He Who exacted punishment." Shach. The Shach further writes that whenever a person has the right to retract from the transaction and denies that any transaction ever occurred, he cannot be compelled to take a hesset oath, because he is not denying an enforceable monetary obligation, as he could retract in any event. See the Beurim. The same applies where he did not give cash but gave his own promissory note, or where the seller said, "Give one hundred to so and so, and my movables will thereby be acquired to you."
