How is the curse of 'He Who exacted payment' administered? Beit din curses the withdrawing party and says: 'He Who exacted payment from the generation of the Flood, from the generation of the Dispersion, from the people of Sodom and Gomorrah, and from the Egyptians who were drowned in the sea, will exact payment from one who does not stand by his word.' Rama: Some say beit din addresses him directly: 'He will exact payment from you if you do not stand by your word.' Some say the declaration is made publicly.
