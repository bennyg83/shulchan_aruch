Alef) This is the language of the Rambam, chapter 23 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, halachah 9. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is straightforward from the Mishnah in Bava Batra 80a. Rambam explains why this is not treated as the sale of something that has not yet come into existence.
Bet) The Kesef Mishneh writes that a dovecote and beehive are subordinate to their future produce, and ordinarily are not worth nearly as much as the produce. Therefore selling the dovecote or beehive for its produce is treated as though the structure itself was sold for that purpose. But the produce of a field is subordinate to the field, so selling the future produce does not amount to selling the field for its produce unless this is stated expressly.
