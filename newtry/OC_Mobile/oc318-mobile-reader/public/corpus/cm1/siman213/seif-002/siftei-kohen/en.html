He beats upon the dovecote. Darkei Moshe writes that Rashi implies that if the bird once hovered above the fledglings, he acquires them, unlike the apparent implication of the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 23 of Hilchot Mechirah. The Sema also cites this. Examination of the Maggid Mishneh shows that this inference is incorrect. See also the Bach.
