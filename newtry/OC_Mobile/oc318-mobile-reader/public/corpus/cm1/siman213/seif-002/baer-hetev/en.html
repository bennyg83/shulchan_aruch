The mother birds. The same applies if the chicks have flown away. The case here concerns small chicks that cannot fly even if one taps the nest, similar to eggs. They have no remedy until the mother bird flies off them. At that moment his courtyard immediately acquires the chicks and eggs for him. Thereafter, even if the mother returns and sits upon them, he may take the mother together with the young. Although our Sages expounded, 'You shall surely send away,' even one hundred times, that applies where the nest is not in his courtyard. There, even if the mother flies away, so long as he has not taken the nest into his hand and acquired it, it is not considered prepared for him, and the prohibition returns as before. When it is in his courtyard, however, as soon as the mother flies away his courtyard acquires the nest and it becomes prepared property. Sema.
