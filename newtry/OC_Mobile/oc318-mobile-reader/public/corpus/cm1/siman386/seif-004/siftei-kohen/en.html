The Nimukei Yosef at the beginning of Keitzad HaRegel writes in the name of the Ritva, citing his teacher, that since Rava remained uncertain, both parties are exempt because the burden of proof rests upon the claimant. The Bach cites this at the end of this siman. The Shiltei Gibborim in chapter 25, pages 13 and 14, likewise cites it and discusses the matter. The Baal HaMaor also writes in chapter 25 that although Rabbah regarded the law as certain, since Rava was uncertain we must take his doubt into account. He rules there that the money is subject to doubt and should be divided. According to our rule, however, that in every monetary doubt the burden of proof rests upon the claimant, both are exempt. The Ramban in Milchamot responds to the Baal HaMaor, but his arguments are not compelling. See there.
