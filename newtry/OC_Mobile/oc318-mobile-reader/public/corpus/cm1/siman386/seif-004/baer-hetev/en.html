Liable. The Nimukei Yosef at the beginning of Keitzad HaRegel cites the Ritva as ruling that because Rava was uncertain, both parties are exempt, since the burden of proof rests on the claimant. The Bach cites this at the end of this siman, and Shiltei Gibborim discusses it as well. Baal HaMaor likewise writes that although the matter was clear to Rabbah, since Rava remained uncertain we take the doubt into account. He himself rules that doubtful money is divided, but according to our rule that in every monetary doubt the burden of proof rests on the claimant, both parties are exempt. See Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 134. Shach.
