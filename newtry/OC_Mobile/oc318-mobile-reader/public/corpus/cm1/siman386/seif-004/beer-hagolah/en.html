chet) The language of the Tur. The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes this, and so does the Rambam there, law 12, from Rabba's statement at the end of the second chapter of Bava Kamma 26b. The Bach cites the Nimukei Yosef in the name of the Rosh: if one threw a stone or arrow toward a vessel, since the thrower did not yet perform an act upon the vessel itself, if another person broke the vessel first, the breaker is liable. This is from Tosafot on 17b, s.v. 'he threw a vessel,' and this is the ruling we follow.
