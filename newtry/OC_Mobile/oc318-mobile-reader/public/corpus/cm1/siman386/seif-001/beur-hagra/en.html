(Collected note) Specifically, etc. Bava Kamma 48b. The Rif omits this statement. The Ramban explains in his treatment of dina d'garmi that the Rif's text read Rabbah, who holds that garmi is exempt, whereas accurate texts read Rava, who holds that garmi is liable. Even so, this rule remains clear: indirect acts caused by an animal are mere grama, because the animal itself does not perform the human legal act. (End of note.)
