a) The language of the Tur, from Reish Lakish's statement to Rabbi Elazar and those who follow his view, Bava Kamma 100b.
b) Ibid., in the name of the Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilkhot Chovel uMazik, law 11. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rabba exempts in Bava Kamma 98a because he does not accept dina d'garmi, but the commentators write that according to our ruling one is liable in all these cases. This is also the view of Rabbeinu Chananel, the Rif, and the other poskim. This applies even if he throws the coin into clear water where it remains visible and is not truly lost, but he causes the owner to lose the wage that must be paid to someone skilled enough to dive and retrieve it. If the owner can retrieve it by paying that wage, even Rabba requires the one who caused the loss to reimburse it.
gimel) The language of the Rambam there, law 9, following Rafram, who compelled Rav Ashi to pay, there on 98b.
