Although the Tur holds that this injury too resembles smoke, Perishah writes that it is not quite as severe as smoke. I do not know why he omitted the Rosh at the beginning of Bava Batra, who writes that although one who tells another, "Tear my garment," can retract his permission and the other becomes liable for damage done after the retraction, the present case is different because the obligation to build a wall is a monetary claim that can be waived. Perishah himself cites this Rosh. According to the Rosh's explanation, which compares ordinary neighbor damage to the case of "tear my garment and be exempt," a mere waiver without kinyan would not be effective for other forms of damage unless they can likewise be characterized as a monetary claim subject to waiver.
