He takes an oath while holding a sacred object, etc. The Tur writes that the Ramah disputes the Rambam and holds that the claimant collects without an oath. The Sema explains that because this claimant seeks to extract money from another, the Shulchan Aruch ruled like the Rambam. I do not understand his words. Even where the defendant says, 'I paid,' the claimant extracts money from him and nevertheless collects without an oath, as the Rama rules above at the end of siman 78, seif 2, and as the Beit Yosef rules there. The Gemara in Bava Batra 5b likewise implies that here the presumption of nonpayment is stronger than in the ordinary fixed-time case above. The Mechaber and Rama therefore require study for omitting the views of the Ramah and Ramban. The Nimukei Yosef also cites Ramban's view as his conclusion.
