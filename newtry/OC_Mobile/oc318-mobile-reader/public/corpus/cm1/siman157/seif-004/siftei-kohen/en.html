Some say that the custom is not followed. This means that if the other party does not consent, he may say: I do not wish to accept an inferior fence that will fall and require me to trouble myself again with rebuilding it. Tosafot and the Mordechai write at the beginning of Bava Batra and in the chapter HaSocher et HaPoalim that one does not follow a defective custom. These are the words of the Sema. See the responsa of Rabbi Eliyahu ibn Chaim, siman 29; Rabbi Shlomo Kohen, book III, simanim 47 and 48; Rabbi Moshe Alshich, siman 57; and Rabbi Shlomo Kohen, siman 84.
