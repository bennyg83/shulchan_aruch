Regarding a light partition, one party can say, "If you build entirely within your own property, I will have no right to support beams upon it." Even if the other says, "I will permit you to support beams," he may answer, "If you want to sell me that right, I do not want to buy it, and if you wish to give it to me, I do not want a gift, for it is written, 'One who hates gifts will live.'" See below, siman 173, seif 13. Sema. The Taz questions the Rama. We never find that the Sages obligated both parties to build a wall for the purpose of supporting beams. The obligation exists because of visual damage. Once one owner builds within his own property and removes the visual damage, who can object?
