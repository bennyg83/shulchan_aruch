The Gemara's explanation there.
Maggid Mishneh cites the Halachot: from here we learn that an item known to belong jointly to two partners does not leave the other's chazakah merely because it is presently in one partner's possession, because partners are not particular with one another. Therefore neither has chazakah against the other except through proof or after division.
There, according to the Halachot. Rashba and Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash likewise write that if there are no witnesses testifying concerning these items, the possessor is believed by migo, because he could have said, "I returned them." Tur, seif 13, writes likewise in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah.
