Likewise, if this one went by himself. The Maggid Mishneh explains that the Rambam's phrase "by himself" means that one partner did not tell the other, "Go take possession and acquire," and that each took possession without the other's presence. Even so, the division is effective, although such possession would not ordinarily be effective in a regular sale, as explained in siman 192. The reason is that here no transfer from one owner to another is occurring, only clarification of which portion belongs to each. According to this, when the Mechaber concludes, "some say," the added novelty is that even if both did not take possession of their respective portions, but only one took possession of his part, the other also acquires his. The Maggid Mishneh reports that some interpret the Rambam's own words "by himself" this way, reading "and this one took possession" as though it meant "or this one took possession." The Mechaber's phrase "some say that even if one took possession" does not mean that these authorities necessarily read the Rambam this way. Rather, it states their independent view that the division works even with all these deficiencies.
