[Shulchan Aruch] The width of the wall. Note: The view of Ramban, the Rosh, and Nimukei Yosef is that local custom governs only whether one wishes to build with uncut stone or hewn stone. As to the width, however, the measure is that expressly stated in the Mishnah for uncut stone, hewn stone, and bricks. We do not follow custom either to reduce or to increase that width.
