Regarding a courtyard wall between two partners, if one claims that he built it alone and the other says that he assisted with the construction, the latter is presumed to have assisted. Even if it is known that one built it alone and he demands that the other repay his share, the other is believed, upon taking a heset oath, to say that he paid him. He is believed even if he claims that he paid before the wall was completed. Even if witnesses testify that the builder demanded that his partner assist and the partner refused, the partner is still believed to say that he paid afterward, unless witnesses testify that he was with them in a particular place continuously from the day construction began and they know he did not pay, or witnesses testify that he was brought before beit din, was ordered to assist with the construction, and refused to comply with the order of beit din.
