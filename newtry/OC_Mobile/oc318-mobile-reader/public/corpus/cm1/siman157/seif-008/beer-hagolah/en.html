Rambam likewise writes this in Hilkhot Shekhenim chapter 20, law 2: since the legal obligation to assist with building the partition is publicly known, he is believed to say that he paid, subject to a hesset oath. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is obvious because every denial of a monetary claim entails a hesset oath.
