Even if he claims that he paid before the wall was built, etc. See Sema, seif katan 21, who writes that the Mechaber abbreviated here and that the case concerns a claim made after the due date had elapsed. In my humble opinion, this is not correct. Rather, the Mechaber addresses the same case as the Mishna and Gemara, and there the ruling necessarily applies even where the claimant has no migo. The Mechaber therefore states without qualification that even if he claims that he paid before the wall was built, he is believed in every circumstance. Examine this carefully.
