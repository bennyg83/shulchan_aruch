This applies when witnesses know that the claimant alone built the entire wall. If that is not known, the wall is presumed to belong to both parties. If the defendant admits that the claimant built it but says, "I already paid you," he is believed through a migo that he could have said, "I built it together with you." Rama: Some say that if he originally consented to raising the wall, he is believed to say that he paid his share. Tosafot and Nimukei Yosef. As soon as he says that he will pay half the expense, he acquires half the wall; the other party cannot object, and he is compelled to pay. Maggid Mishneh, chapter 2 of Hilchot Shechenim, and Nimukei Yosef.
