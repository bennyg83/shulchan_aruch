The language of Rambam there. Maggid Mishneh explains that this is Rav Nachman's statement in the Gemara, Bava Batra 6a: for that which he has connected he has established the right, and for that which he has not connected he has not established it.
Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam uses this to explain the Gemara's phrase concerning setting beam sockets. Sema writes that nevertheless one must determine that the neighbor himself made the sockets. Otherwise he can say that the owner who raised the wall made them without his knowledge to protect the wall, anticipating that perhaps later he would persuade him to build upon it.
This is the explanation of the term "aprizah" there, and Rashi likewise explains it so.
