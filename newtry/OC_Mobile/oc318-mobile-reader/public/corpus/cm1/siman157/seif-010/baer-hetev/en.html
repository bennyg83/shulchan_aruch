That fell. The Sema writes that the text says "that fell" to teach a greater novelty: even if the wall between them was originally higher than four cubits, once it falls, neither can compel the other to rebuild it to that full height, but only to four cubits. See Responsa Mabit, Choshen Mishpat, second set of questions, siman 38, page 110.
