[Rama] He is believed that he paid. Note: Ramban in Milchamot holds that even if he merely adjoined a temporary structure to the wall, he is believed to say that he paid his share. Even if he placed only a single ceiling upon it, he is believed to say that he paid for the entire wall. See there. The Rivash likewise wrote this in a responsum cited by Kesef Mishneh, chapter 8 of Hilchot Nizkei Shechenim, halachah 8. See there.
