Nimukei Yosef is the first opinion, holding that the neighbor may say, “I do not wish to litigate with you repeatedly whenever the wall falls.” The Rosh is the dissenting opinion cited by Rama. He holds that the Sages did not require a wall lasting for generations. Only where the wall belongs to both may one say, “I do not wish to waste money on a light partition.” But where the other builds alone and presently removes the visual intrusion, that is sufficient. It will be explained below in seif 5 that this is the principal ruling.
