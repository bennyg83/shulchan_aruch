(Seif 11: he swears while holding a sacred object.) This is difficult. At the beginning of Bava Batra, page 6, the Gemara seeks to prove that even where there is a chazakah that a person does not pay before the due date, we do not accept a migo of "why would he lie?" The answer there is that the situation differs because he could say, "Who says the Sages will ever obligate me?" This shows that the reasoning "who says the Sages will obligate me?" is an even stronger basis for disbelief than the presumption that one does not pay before the due date. Yet concerning payment before the due date itself, the Beit Yosef in siman 78 cites Rambam as holding that the creditor collects without an oath. Here, despite the additional reasoning that the defendant could have thought the Sages would never obligate him, the claimant must take an oath. This requires reconciliation.
