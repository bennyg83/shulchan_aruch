Seif 4 The text should read: “He gave it to him unlawfully; if the judge did not personally transfer it, the judgment is reversed, and if restoration is impossible,” etc. The author of Kesef Mishneh corrected it this way in his own copy, but from great integrity did not alter Rambam’s printed wording. He often reproduces a demonstrably mistaken text exactly as found. The Sema tries to preserve the present reading through many strained explanations, as he himself acknowledges, so I have not reproduced them.
