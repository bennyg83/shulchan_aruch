There, in the gloss “Nevertheless they should retract.” This is difficult under the Rosh’s approach, which Rama adopts, because according to Rosh the judgment stands in every case, even if a greater authority could reverse it. How then can the judge be instructed to retract? Mordechai’s statement fits his own adoption of Ramah, under whom even discretionary error may be reversed. Rama’s wording must therefore be forced to refer back to the Mechaber rather than to Rama’s immediately preceding gloss, though this is strained.
