In the Tur: Ramah wrote, “A judge erred, and the claimant had not yet managed,” etc. According to this, why did the Gemara not resolve the contradiction between the Mishna that a mistaken judgment is reversed and the baraita that what was done stands by distinguishing whether the claimant had already collected? The Mishna could concern a case before collection, and the baraita after collection, when the judge must pay from his own funds. Rav Hai’s distinction through Baal HaItur, whether the party is present, presents no such difficulty, because “what was done stands” cannot meaningfully describe an absent person. Ramah’s distinction, however, leaves the question unresolved.
