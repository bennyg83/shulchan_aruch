By mistake. Darkei Moshe cites Maharik: if one compromised his inheritance share for a small sum because he was told that the estate was not large, and afterward learned that the estate was substantial and that his proper share would have been almost twice as much, the compromise was made in error. Beit Yosef writes concerning one who had a claim against two people and compromised with one of them, after which the second died, that he need not give anything to the second person's heirs. Thus concludes the Sema.
