a) The wording of the Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilkhot Sanhedrin, from the Mishnah, Sanhedrin 32a, according to Rav Sheshet's interpretation there, 33a.
The Shach notes that the Rambam precisely wrote "every judge," rather than "everyone who judged," to indicate specifically one who is called a judge, such as an expert judge or one accepted by the litigants. But a layman who was not accepted by them is not a judge at all, and his ruling has no validity even if he did not err, as explained below in seif 4.
b) From what Rav Huna said to Rav Ashi, etc., there.
c) Tur in the name of his father the Rosh, based on the Gemara's explanation that they were not merely reed cutters in the marshes. The Kesef Mishneh likewise derives this from the Rambam's words.
d) According to Rav Chisda's interpretation there, and so rules the Rif.
