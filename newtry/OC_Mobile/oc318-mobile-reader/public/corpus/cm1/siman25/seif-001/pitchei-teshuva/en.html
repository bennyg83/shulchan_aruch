Who judged monetary cases and erred, etc. See Tashbetz, part II, siman 9, who writes that the judge is not disqualified from judging in the future merely because of this error, for every person is liable to err, and even the greatest Tannaim erred in their judgments. If, however, he erred because he was not careful in his learning and many of his rulings were erroneous, it may be possible to disqualify him. Even then, the disqualification would be only from judging alone as an expert, but not from being counted as one of a panel of three. See there.
