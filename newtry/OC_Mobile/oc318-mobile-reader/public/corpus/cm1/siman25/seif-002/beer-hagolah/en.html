h) From Rav's statement, "One who wishes to judge," etc., there 5a. Even if he personally took and transferred the property, he is exempt, because property declared ownerless by beit din is ownerless.
i) From the incident of Rabbah bar bar Chanah, who judged a case and erred, etc., and likewise Mar Zutra, etc., there. These two were expert judges. This also follows from the Mishnah, "one who judged the case," etc., Bechorot 28b, which the Gemara in Sanhedrin 6a establishes as a case where the litigants accepted the judge upon themselves. The latter clause teaches that if he is an expert for the public he is exempt. Likewise, from the incident of Rabbi Tarfon there in the Mishnah in Bechorot, whose case is comparable to one where they accepted him, he is exempt even if he personally transferred the property.
