Sema says that according to Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash the lower owner assists the upper owner above only when the upper courtyard is a full four amot higher. If the difference is less, then because the lower owner must in any event build upward from below, he must also help with the section above. I do not see a basis for that distinction. Ramban states explicitly that whether the upper courtyard is one, two, three, or four amot higher, the same rule applies. The upper owner assists below because the wall needs a foundation, while the lower owner need not compensate for his natural lower position. If the height difference is three amot, they build jointly from below to one amah above the lower ground, so the lower owner ends up with his required four amot and the upper owner then completes four amot above his own courtyard alone. If the difference is a full four amot, the lower owner need do nothing beyond his own four amot, while the upper owner constructs the full wall above his courtyard. The Tur's citation of Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash follows this same structure.
