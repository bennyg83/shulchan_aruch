Where two courtyards are situated one above the other, with the ground of one higher than that of the other, the owner of the upper courtyard must assist the owner of the lower courtyard in building from below, and the owner of the lower courtyard must assist the owner of the upper courtyard until the wall rises four cubits above the ground of the upper courtyard. Therefore, they remove earth from the ground of the upper courtyard to the width of half the wall, and build the wall on land belonging to both of them until it rises four cubits above the ground of the upper courtyard. Rama: If the upper courtyard is very high, or the lower courtyard is not broad enough to permit viewing from a distance, and a smaller partition will suffice to eliminate visual intrusion, it is enough to build a partition that people recognize as preventing further visual harm.
