Rambam writes so in chapter 3 of Hilkhot Shekhenim, law 6. Maggid Mishneh writes that the Gemara answered according to Rav Nachman: "What does it mean that he is not obligated? He is not obligated to four cubits, but he is obligated to a ten-handbreadth partition," as Rabbi Yosef ibn Migash explains. Since Rambam ruled above according to Rav Nachman, it is surprising that he writes that the upper resident has no obligation at all toward the lower. This requires study. Raavad likewise objects and writes that the matter is not clear.
Tur, seif 2. The Rosh writes likewise.
