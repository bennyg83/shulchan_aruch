Note: Beit Yosef explicitly wrote that even regarding a roof adjoining a courtyard, if it is a covered roof, the owner of the courtyard must assist in constructing the parapet. Moreover, regarding the Sema's principal rationale, that the owner of the roof causes more harm to the owner of the courtyard than the owner of the courtyard causes to the owner of the roof, Ramban at the beginning of Bava Batra explicitly wrote the reverse. He wrote that a covered roof is comparable to the privacy damage of a house, which according to all opinions is deemed damage. See there.
