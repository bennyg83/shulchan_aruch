Exempt. This means exempt from the Torah oath imposed upon one who admits part of a claim. In seif 12 it will be explained that he is also exempt regarding legumes. The reason the ruling specifically mentions legumes is in contrast to a claim for grain, because the other five species of grain are all included in the term "grain." But where the claim is for a kor of wheat, if he admits barley or another species of grain, the admission is likewise not of the same kind as the claim. It appears that the correct text is, "and he admitted half a kor of barley or legumes," contrary to the Beit Yosef. So writes the Sema. The Shach writes that wine and vinegar are considered one species, as Rashbam explains in Bava Batra 85b.
