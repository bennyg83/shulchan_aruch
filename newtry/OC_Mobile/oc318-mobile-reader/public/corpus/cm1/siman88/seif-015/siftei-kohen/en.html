There is no admission here except a partial one, etc. The reason is as I write below in the Ran's name. Rabbeinu Yerucham likewise writes that a partial admission creates a Torah oath only when it follows the claim. Accordingly, even where both sides discuss the same kind of money, he is exempt if the admission precedes the claim. For example, they come before beit din and the defendant first says, “I owe you fifty zuz,” after which the claimant responds, “You owe me another fifty,” and the defendant denies the additional amount. He is exempt from a Torah oath. Ramban and Baal HaTerumot state this explicitly, and I cited them above, siman 75, seif 3, seif katan 9. See also what I write below, seif 32.
