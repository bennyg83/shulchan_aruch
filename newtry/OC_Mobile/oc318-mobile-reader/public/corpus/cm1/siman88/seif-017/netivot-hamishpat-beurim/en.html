And he answered that he does not know whether it was wheat. See Shach seif katan 35, who questions this from the end of HaManiach. According to what I wrote in seif katan 3, it is resolved. When the defendant says, "I do not know whether it was wheat or barley," his uncertainty necessarily concerns a single day and a single transaction, for he does not know which commodity was involved at that time. Where he affirmatively says "barley," it is possible to interpret the wheat and barley as arising at different times. In the present case, because the uncertainty concerns one transaction, the plaintiff can proceed against the barley by mima nafshach. In HaManiach, by contrast, he cannot proceed against the tam by mima nafshach for the reasons explained there at length. This is clear and correct.
