The oath of bailees, etc. Bava Kamma 107a states that the passages are intermingled, and we rule like Rav Chiyya bar Yosef. Therefore, the phrase 'money or utensils' governs a loan claim and not the oath of bailees.
And some say, etc. Tosafot hold that there is no distinction between a loan and a deposit except where the particular claim can arise only in a deposit. Their proofs show that the requirement of two silver ma'ot applies equally. The Gemara does not present bailees as a greater novelty even though the verses are written in that context, and it requires a separate verse only for one witness, implying that the remaining oath laws are learned from one another. Shevuot likewise requires two silver ma'ot for the rabbinic oath imposed upon partners by analogy to bailees.
