Full, etc. This follows Rif's version, which reads 'in your possession' in all clauses. In the first clause the word 'full' precedes the container, in the middle clause it follows, and in the final clause it is omitted. We rule like Admon in those of his seven rulings that were accepted. Tosafot explain the parallel rule in Shevuot accordingly.
But if, etc. This follows Rosh's version. The difference between the clauses is whether the claimant says merely 'a vessel' or specifies 'vessels.' Rosh's abridgment, however, accords with our version and Rif. See Derishah and Sema.
Only the vessels, etc. See the gloss to Baer HaGolah. This reading is primary.
