If one claims, 'You have ten jars full of oil belonging to me,' and the defendant says, 'I have only ten jars without oil,' he is exempt, because the claimant claimed oil and the defendant admitted only earthenware. If one claims, 'You have ten jars of oil belonging to me,' or says, 'You have ten jars full of oil belonging to me,' and the defendant answers, 'I have only ten empty jars,' or admits only the oil, he must take a Torah oath, because the claim concerned both the jars and the oil. {Rama: But if he said, 'You have ten oil jars belonging to me,' he claimed only the jars. If the defendant admits the oil, the admission is not of the same kind as the claim.} See note 40.
