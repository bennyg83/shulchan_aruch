(Seif 18) “Full of ten.” Sema correctly inserted the word “full” into the Tur’s text. He should, however, have made an additional correction, for the wording has been transposed. It should read: “Rather, you have ten jars of oil belonging to me, and he admitted the jars but denied the oil.” This is also the reading in Sefer HaTerumot, gate 7. One who examines the Rosh and the Remezim will see that their wording likewise requires correction. There is no need to elaborate. According to what I have written, the Tur and Rosh follow the view of Rashi and Rif. This is compelled, for otherwise they should have cited the interpretation of Rashi and Rif and disagreed with it. Rama’s words are difficult, as Sema wrote.
