He claimed from him a debt, etc. Tur attributes to Raavad the view that where the claimant demands one person's debt and the defendant admits another person's debt, it is like wheat and barley, and Tur disagrees. The formulation is difficult. If the claimant also concedes that the second debt exists but merely does not presently demand it, the same would be true even in wheat and barley once both are claimed. If he denies that second debt entirely, why should it be paid, since he has waived it? The statement concerning a hesset oath is likewise unclear, because the result depends on whether the admitted amount equals the claimed amount. The wording in Sefer HaTerumot does not match Tur's citation, and the matter requires great study.
