This applies only to a loan. A loan is given for expenditure, so the creditor does not demand the exact coins originally delivered but only equivalent value. With a deposit, by contrast, the deposited item itself must be returned, so the precise thing deposited remains the subject of the claim. See Tumim. I wrote that in our times, where every householder effectively has the status of a money changer with respect to ordinary currency, the law of a monetary deposit is often the same as that of a loan for this purpose.
