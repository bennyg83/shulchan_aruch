See Baer Heitev, who writes that in any event he must take a heset oath, etc., until: "and for that reason he wrote there explicitly that he is exempt from heset, whereas he did not write so in the first ruling, and the reason is," etc. In Rabbi Akiva Eiger's glosses to the Sema he writes: "It can simply be said that here, since had he claimed two he would have been liable to a heset oath, therefore even when he claims three he must take a heset oath. Otherwise one could say that he is acting deceptively in order to exempt himself from heset. This is straightforward." See Tumim.
