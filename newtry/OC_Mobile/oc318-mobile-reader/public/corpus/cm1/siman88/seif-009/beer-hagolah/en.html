There in the Gemara according to Rav and Rashi's explanation: since the claimant specified a weight, he plainly meant that exact amount.
So write the Rosh and Ran in the name of the Ramban.
There, from the baraita, "a gold dinar of gold," etc., there 40a.
They explain there that although we rule like Rav that an unspecified "silver dinar" means its value, nevertheless here the defendant is exempt. The reason is that the claimant stated an exact object while the defendant admitted only value, meaning: "I do not owe you that exact object; I owe you half a dinar or its value." But when the claimant claims monetary value, whatever the defendant admits is an admission of the same kind as the claim, because there is nothing that does not have monetary value.
