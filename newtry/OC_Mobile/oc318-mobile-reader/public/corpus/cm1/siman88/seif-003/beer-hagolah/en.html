There. So too the Rambam there, from Rav Anan in the name of Shmuel, Shevuot 40b.
There, in the name of the mutter, tools have come out for what they are
The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is the view of the Geonim, unlike the Yerushalmi, and that it is the principal ruling, to which the Rashba agreed. The Beit Yosef likewise writes in the name of the Ran, citing Rav Hai Gaon and Rabbi Yosef HaLevi, that the view and agreement of the Geonim is Torah.
