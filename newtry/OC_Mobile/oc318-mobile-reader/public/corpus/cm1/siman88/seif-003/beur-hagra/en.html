But even, etc. The language of the Gemara indicates that even a utensil of minimal monetary value is sufficient. Ri Migash reads the verse as comparing money, which must possess the halachic significance of money, with utensils, for any genuine utensil is significant even if worth little. Although the Yerushalmi requires two perutot, its discussion does not accord with our Gemara. The rule that beit din does not adjudicate less than a perutah applies to ordinary money, not to a utensil. Some explain 'a significant utensil' to exclude broken fragments or an object unfit for acquisition, as discussed concerning symbolic acquisition.
