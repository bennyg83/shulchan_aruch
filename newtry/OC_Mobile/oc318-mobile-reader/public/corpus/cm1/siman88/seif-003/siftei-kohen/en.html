Ten needles, etc. Rav Hai and Rambam write this, but the Rosh and Tur disagree and hold that each item must be worth at least a perutah, because less than that is not regarded as money. Ramban agrees, and Tosafot in Shevuot and Kiddushin 11b write likewise in Rabbeinu Tam's name.
If he admitted a perutah, he is liable. According to the Rosh and those who agree with him, either the utensil admitted or the utensil denied must in any event be worth a perutah. The Tur states this expressly.
