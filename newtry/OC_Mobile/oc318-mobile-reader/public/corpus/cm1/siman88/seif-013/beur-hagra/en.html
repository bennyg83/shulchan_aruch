One who claims against another, etc. Bava Kamma discusses one innocuous ox and one forewarned ox, where the injured party attributes the larger damage to the forewarned ox and the defendant attributes it to the innocuous one, leaving the claimant without recovery on his formulation. This parallels a claimant who says, 'I lent you a maneh,' while the defendant admits a different obligation. The reverse case is similar. See siman 400:3, where Rambam disagrees.
