Even if he claimed, 'This house full,' etc. Even if the house is not completely full, ordinary people may still call it 'full,' so the claim is not sufficiently defined.
Alternatively, the rule requires that the demand itself mention a quantified measure, as Tosafos explain.Up to the projecting beam.
Rashi explains that a ziz is a beam projecting from an upper story into the house.They rotted through his negligence.
Although the Mishnah, Tur, and Mechaber state that he says, 'what you left you take,' the point is that where the loss occurred through the custodian's negligence he cannot rely on the uncertain description of quantity to escape liability. The claimant must still define the claim enough for the relevant oath rules, but known negligent loss changes the monetary consequence of the uncertainty.
