If they arose together, he says the claimant cannot avoid the effect of his original admission merely by saying he is not presently asking for the barley. In Tumim I wrote that according to Tur and Mechaber he is believed even where the claims arose together. As practical law, however, I concluded like the later authorities that in no case is the admitted barley automatically lost merely because the claimant answered quickly in this fashion.
