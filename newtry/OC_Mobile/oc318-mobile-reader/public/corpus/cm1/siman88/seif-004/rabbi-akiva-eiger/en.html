[Shulchan Aruch] It therefore emerges that he admitted only what was written in the document. Note: I am surprised why the early authorities did not cite as practical law the other resolution in the Gemara, that he is exempt because the document supports him. The practical difference arises where the document contains no credibility clause. According to all views, his admission would ordinarily obligate an oath, yet in a case of "selaim" where he says two, he is exempt because the document supports him. This requires great study.
