(Seif 9) “Even if he admitted owing a coin.” The Tur likewise wrote this, but there is no basis for stating it here, because this point is the dispute between Rashi and Rabbenu Hananel. According to Rashi, even if he admitted owing a silver coin, the admission is of the same kind as the claim, as the Tur later writes, because all coins are treated as one kind. Why, then, did the Tur write this here? It appears to me that in the haste of composition the Tur followed here the arrangement of his words in the Remezim, where he first recorded the dispute and then wrote, “even if,” etc.
