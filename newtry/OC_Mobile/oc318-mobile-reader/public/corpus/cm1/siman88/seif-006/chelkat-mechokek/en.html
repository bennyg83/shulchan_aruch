The same applies to the offspring of maidservants and animals that are nichsei melog. Meaning, although while she remains married all offspring belong to him, nevertheless, if at the time of his death or divorce the husband still has an offspring of her maidservant or an offspring of her animal that he did not sell, she pays him its value and takes it because of the honor of her paternal family. This wording implies that the honor of her paternal family applies even to the offspring of an animal. The plain meaning of Ketubot 79b, however, where Chananya concedes that if she is divorced, refers only to the offspring of a maidservant. Rambam explicitly writes in chapter 22 that she pays money because of the honor of her paternal family, and the Maggid Mishneh writes likewise there. The same appears in Rivash, siman 21, and this was ruled above in siman 85, seif 16. Here, however, the Mechaber followed the Tur's wording and did not recall what he had ruled in siman 85.
