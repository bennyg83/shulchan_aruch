Two ma'ah. Rashi derives this from the feminine form 'two,' showing that the unit is ma'ah. Bava Metzia likewise establishes that a sela contains four dinars and that four silver units for overreaching means ma'ot. Kiddushin states that six silver ma'ot equal a dinar, and the Yerushalmi expressly identifies the minimum silver coin as a ma'ah.
The measure of a perutah. See Rif and Rosh at the beginning of Kiddushin.
And the measure, etc. As stated in Kiddushin 12a: one ma'ah equals two pundeyon, and one pundeyon, etc.
