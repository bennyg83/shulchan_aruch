A perutah is the weight of half a barleycorn, etc. See the Bach, who found it written in the name of Maharam of Rizensburg that the five sela'im for redemption of the firstborn equal five lots of silver and one quarter lot, and that one should be stringent according to this measure. This calculation is close to that in Ma'adanei Melech on Bekhorot, page 268a, where he weighed it and found each sela to weigh one lot, more than the silver in three reichsthalers. Nevertheless, three reichsthalers suffice because of their copper content and the premium of coinage. I too weighed 384 average barleycorns while in Moravia. A sela is four dinarim, each dinar six ma'ot, and each ma'ah sixteen barleycorns, namely thirty-two perutot. The 384 barleycorns came close to one Moravian or Viennese lot, which contains the weight of five red gold coins, lacking about one tenth of a lot, according to my careful weighing. See what I wrote in the Shach on Yoreh Deah, siman 305, seif katan 1.
