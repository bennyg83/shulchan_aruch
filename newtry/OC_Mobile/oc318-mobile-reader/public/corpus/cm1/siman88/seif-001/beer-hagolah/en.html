Rav said there, 39b, and Rabbi Chiya taught a baraita supporting Rav, 40a. The Yerushalmi gives the reason from the verse, 'If a man gives money or vessels,' etc. The smallest silver coin mentioned in the Torah is a ma'ah, which is a gerah, as translated from 'twenty gerah' as twenty ma'ah. Just as 'vessels' means two, so too 'money' means two.
