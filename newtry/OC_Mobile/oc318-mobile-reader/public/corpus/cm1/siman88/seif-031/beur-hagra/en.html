He claimed a maneh from him... This was inferred from the Rif's words in seif 28, where he says that no oath is imposed by an admission contained in a document, even though a loan in a document and an oral loan are otherwise two distinct categories. The Rif's source is the Yerushalmi, second chapter of Kesubos: "My uncle concedes regarding a document." How so? One claimed a maneh, the defendant denied it, and the claimant produced a document establishing fifty; he has only the fifty. See what I wrote above in siman 75 seif 2.
