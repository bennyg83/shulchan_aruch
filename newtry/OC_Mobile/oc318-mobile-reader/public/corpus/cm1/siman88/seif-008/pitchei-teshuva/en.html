In Rabbi Akiva Eiger's glosses to the Shulchan Aruch he writes: "I am surprised why the early authorities did not codify the rationale of the other answer in our sugya, that he is exempt because the document supports him. A practical difference arises with a handwritten note that contains no clause of trustworthiness. According to all opinions, his admission would obligate him to swear. Nevertheless, in a case of 'selas are stated in the document and he says two,' he would be exempt because the document supports him. This requires great study."
