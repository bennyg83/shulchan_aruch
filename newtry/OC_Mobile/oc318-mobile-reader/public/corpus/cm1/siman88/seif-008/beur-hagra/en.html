He did not claim... from all that.... This is as explained in the preceding seif, where he claimed a kor of produce and the defendant admitted legumes. It is comparable to a claim for value and an admission in a different item. Rosh and Ran.
But if... As stated in Shevuos 40a concerning a gold dinar. This follows Tosafos' text there, which reads that he is exempt. The Yerushalmi likewise supports Rav, because specifically where the claimant says "a minted gold dinar" does he mean that very coin; otherwise his claim is for its value. The continuation there, where the defendant says that he owes only a silver dinar and is exempt, must therefore be understood in the same way.
