Two claimants, etc., since he did not, etc., but rather, etc. Had he said this at the time of his admission, it would be as though he said, ‘I have clothing or utensils of yours in my possession,’ a case in which he is exempt, as stated in the Tosefta and explained above in siman 75. Here, however, he did not say so when he admitted. Everything follows the moment of admission. Raavad derives this from Shevuot: where one answers unintentionally, he is exempt because he did not admit after the claim was made. Even though he does not deny the claim afterward, his admission did not occur then. Thus, everything depends upon the time of the admission. See Sefer HaTerumot.
