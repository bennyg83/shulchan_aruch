Nevertheless, according to Rambam it appears that the bundle must be worth a dinar. Feeding it to his animal is not considered an honorable form of consumption. According to the Rosh, however, Rav Yehudah disagrees with the other two Amoraim and holds that benefit worth even a perutah is sufficient.
