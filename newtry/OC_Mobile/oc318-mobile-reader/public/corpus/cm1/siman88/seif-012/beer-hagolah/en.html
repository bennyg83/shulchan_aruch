In the name of the Rambam there, law 10. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the Rif derived this from the discussion there and the Geonim ruled accordingly. Nimukei Yosef likewise writes so and says this is the Ramah's view. Nimukei Yosef writes that even seizure before witnesses is effective.
In the name of some authorities, and likewise Meisharim, Netiv 3 part 2, and Sefer HaTerumot, gate 7, in the name of Tosafot. Rashi writes, according to the Sema, that even seizure not before witnesses is ineffective because it is like a migo against witnesses: we are legally certain that since he did not claim barley, he waived it. Nevertheless, seizure without witnesses helps him collect the value of the wheat he claimed, as the Mechaber writes above in siman 87.
