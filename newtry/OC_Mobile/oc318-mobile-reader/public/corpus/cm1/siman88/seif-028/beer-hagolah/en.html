Tur, seif 6. This is the conclusion of the Rif and Rosh at the beginning of Bava Metzia from the statement there, 4b, that one does not swear over denial of a lien on land, meaning that a document constitutes a lien on land. Likewise, an admission concerning it does not create an oath, because land was excluded from oaths, as stated in the chapter Shevuat HaDayanim, Shevuot 42b. So rules the Rambam in chapter 4 of Hilkhot To'en, law 4.
