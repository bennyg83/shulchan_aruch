Money tied in a bundle. Even if he admits that the bundle contained money, he is exempt because the amount is not a defined measure. Shach:
He is exempt from an oath, and so forth. We do not say that since he at least admits a perutah, he is one who cannot swear and therefore must pay, as in the case of a pledge in siman 72, seif 13, where he says, "I know it was worth more, but I do not know how much." There the item has already been lost and he cannot be obligated beyond a perutah unless the amount can be fixed, so the claim is treated as one concerning a definite number. Here, however, the amount can still later be determined, and therefore it is not presently a matter of definite number. See the Beurim. This is specifically where the measure cannot ultimately be determined except from the deposit itself, so that if the deposit is lost through unavoidable circumstances the amount will no longer be ascertainable. But if he said that the deposit is worth whatever another identified item in another person's possession is appraised at, that is a matter of definite measure.
