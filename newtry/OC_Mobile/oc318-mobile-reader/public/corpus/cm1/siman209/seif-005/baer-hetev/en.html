In his possession. Nimukei Yosef writes in the name of the Itur: To what is this comparable? To one who sells an object to his fellow on the representation that he possesses it, and it turns out that he does not. He is obligated to supply the purchased object. Responsa Maharashdam, siman 123, comments that perhaps this applies specifically where the seller knew and the buyer did not, so the buyer reasonably assumed the item was certainly in the seller's possession. But in his case, where perhaps neither party knew, the buyer did not give his money with that understanding. See there further. Shach.
