The sale is not void because of this, etc. The reason is that when the seller speaks in the language of reservation, he does not make the sale conditional upon receiving the produce. He simply retains the produce out of what he sells. But if he says, "on condition that you give me," he does not reserve part of the object. Rather, he imposes a condition upon the sale, and if the condition is not fulfilled the sale is void. In the Perishah and Derishah I wrote that according to the Rosh, even if he does not say "give me," but says, "on condition that I have such and such amount of produce each year," fulfillment of the condition remains essential to the sale so long as he did not use language of reservation.
