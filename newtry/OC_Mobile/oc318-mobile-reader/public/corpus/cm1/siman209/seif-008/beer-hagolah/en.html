Alef) The Tur, seif 11. The Rosh writes this there, because the Mishnah could have taught: if he gives him the first tithe every year, he acquires, and if not, he does not. Since instead it states, "the first tithe is his," we learn that the seller has reserved the right itself.
Bet) This too is stated there and in the Rosh, for we rule that the phrase "on condition" creates a true condition.
Gimel) The reason is that the lien already exists, unlike the case above in seif 4, as explained there in seif katan 3.
