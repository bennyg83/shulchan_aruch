And reserved. Meaning, in a legally effective manner. For example, he said: 'The field is to Reuven and its produce to Shimon.' Otherwise the reservation is ineffective in any event. See below, siman 212, seifim 1 and 3. Ibid.
Anything. It appears that even if he sold the produce to Shimon, Reuven nevertheless takes it together with the field without payment. Sema.
A person dangerously ill. Meaning, he reserved the right for himself and also for his heirs. Some disagree regarding a gift of a person dangerously ill and hold that he reserved it only for himself. See Responsa Mabit, part 1, siman 118. Shach.
Like a condition. See below, siman 241, seif 7 in the gloss.
An upper story. In such a case the upper story was never included in the sale at all, and remains his and his heirs' forever. Sema.
