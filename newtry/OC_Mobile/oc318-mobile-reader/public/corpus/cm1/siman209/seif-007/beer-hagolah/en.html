Tzadi) This is the language of the Tur, seif 7, based on Rav Nachman's statement in Bava Batra 148a: if one gave the palm tree to one person and its fruits to another, etc.
Kuf) There, in Rav Nachman's statement, both the ruling and its reason are stated.
Reish) The Tur, seif 8, from the baraita concerning a Levite who sold a field to an Israelite, Bava Metzia 63a.
Shin) This is explicit in the baraita there, as the Rashbam explains.
Tav) This follows from the preceding baraita. Rashbam explains that because the law is derived from an unnecessary extra expression, we extend that extra expression only as far as its explicit implication, namely for the seller himself. Ramban explains that because he did not reserve a defined item, the reservation has the character of a condition. With conditions, one who says "to me" means "to me and not to my heirs," as in Kiddushin, chapter HaOmer, concerning one who says, "On condition that you give me two hundred zuz," and he dies; if she did not give them, she remains subject to yibbum, according to the first Tanna against Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, Kiddushin 60a.
