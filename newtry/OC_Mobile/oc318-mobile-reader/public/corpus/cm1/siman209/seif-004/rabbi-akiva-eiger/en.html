[Rama] If they have begun to ripen. Note: If at that stage he sold the produce to take effect when it would be detached, then since at present the produce is not capable of acquisition by meshichah, the law is that money acquires it. Although the acquisition takes effect only when it is detached, at which time the produce is capable of meshichah, we nevertheless follow its present status. This appears to me to follow from Terumat HaDeshen, siman 253, in its explanation of Hagahot Ashri in chapter 4 of Bava Metzia, regarding one who sells the fetus of his cow to a gentile. There the case concerns one who says that the sale should take effect when the animal gives birth, and nevertheless it is regarded as a case in which meshichah is impossible.
