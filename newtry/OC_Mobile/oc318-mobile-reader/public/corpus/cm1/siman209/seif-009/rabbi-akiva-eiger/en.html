[Rama] Likewise, if he sought release from his oath. Note: According to Rabbeinu Tam, it may be said that where one made a vow for the benefit of another and a sage released the vow, after the fact the release is effective and the act is permitted. This is stated in the Rivash and is brought briefly in the Sema, siman 129, seif katan 16. See Yoreh Deah, siman 228, seif 20, and Taz above, siman 121.
