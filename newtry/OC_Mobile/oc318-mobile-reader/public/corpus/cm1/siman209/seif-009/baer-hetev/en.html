To another. Above, in seif 7, the law is stated where he reserved some of the produce and gave it to another. The novelty there is that even though he initially transferred produce rights to the owner of the field, he still cannot transfer part of the reserved produce to another. Here the novelty is that even if he reserved all the produce for himself, his reservation is effective. Thus, although he transferred the tree to the buyer, the buyer acquires only the trunk once it dries up, as explained in the following seif. Sema. See the Taz, who explains this differently, distinguishing between a field and a tree.
