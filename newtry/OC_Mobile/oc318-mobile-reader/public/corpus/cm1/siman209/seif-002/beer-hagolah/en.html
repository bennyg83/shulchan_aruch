See Terumat HaDeshen siman 320 concerning two people who shook hands and accepted upon themselves a charitable penalty to exchange all their property with one another. He ruled that they must fulfill the agreement unless they mutually waived it. The Sema writes that here their intent to transfer is stronger because neither knows what the other owns, unlike the law of one who says, 'everything in this house,' etc. See Yoreh Deah siman 228, where one who vowed to charity before the charity administrator whatever another person would become obligated to pay him cannot later waive that debt.
