(Seif 2, concerning one who gambles with dice.) This is difficult. The principal reason for the rule regarding dice is asmachta, yet here the matter is plainly within his control, as stated, since he first took possession and drew it to himself. In siman 207, seif 15, the Mechaber writes in the name of Rambam that anything within a person’s control is not an asmachta. One may answer that what is “within his control” here is of no significance, because he does not know whether the object is straw or gold. Alternatively, the statement that he took possession may refer to a case where the matter nevertheless remains outside his control, such as where he drew it into an alleyway. This is clear.
