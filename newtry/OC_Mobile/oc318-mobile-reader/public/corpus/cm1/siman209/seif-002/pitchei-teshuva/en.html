And this is merely like jesting. See Baer Heitev in the name of Shach through his statement that we therefore do not need Sema's distinction. According to Shach, even if the seller himself does not know what is in the house, such as where he himself acquired the house together with whatever it contained and then sold it to another, the purchaser still does not acquire the unknown contents. According to Sema, by contrast, the purchaser would acquire in such a case, as Netivot HaMishpat explains. See there.
