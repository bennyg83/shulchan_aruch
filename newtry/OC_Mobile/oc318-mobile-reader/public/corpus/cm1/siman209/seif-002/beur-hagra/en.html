(Supplement) But one who says, etc., because his mind did not rely, etc. As stated at Bava Metzia 16a, where Rav Sheshet raises an objection. There the seller's mind does not rely upon the transaction because he does not know how much he will inherit or what the trap will catch. (End of supplement.)
Some say that the same applies, etc. According to Tosafot, this does not involve asmachta, like gambling in siman 207. The rule stated by Shulchan Aruch is different because the seller knows while the buyer does not, and therefore all agree. This is why Rama writes it as 'some say.' See Sema and siman 203.
