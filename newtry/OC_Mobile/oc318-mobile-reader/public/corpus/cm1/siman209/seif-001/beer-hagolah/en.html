The language of Rambam at the beginning of chapter 21 of Hilkhot Mekhirah. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is obvious that he acquires, and this is explained by similar cases in the Gemara in HaMokher Peirot, Bava Batra 95a.
The Kesef Mishneh writes that this can be learned from Rav Huna's statement, 'sell to me with these coins,' where the acquisition is valid and ona'ah is returned, Bava Metzia at the end of 46. The baraita concerning one who goes to milk his goats and says, 'what my goats will produce,' etc., 64a, appears to indicate that the acquisition is valid and even an excessive price is permitted. One could answer that Rambam speaks only of goods with a known market price, whereas milk and fleece have no fixed market price and therefore no law of ona'ah. This requires study. In my humble opinion, because that milk and fleece cannot presently be determined by measure or weight, the sale is by estimate, which is not subject to ona'ah, as below in siman 228, seif 19.
