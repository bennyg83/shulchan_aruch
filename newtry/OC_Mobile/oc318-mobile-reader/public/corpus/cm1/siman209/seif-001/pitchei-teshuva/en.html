Everything that is in this house I sell to you for such-and-such. See Responsa Be'er Yitzchak, Orach Chaim, siman 6. He writes that it is clear that the rule applies specifically where the seller states a fixed total price. In such a case the buyer does not fully rely on the transaction because the contents may not be worth the stipulated amount. But if he says that he sells everything for whatever value three appraisers will assess, as discussed above in siman 200:7 and as is customary in the sale of chametz, the concern that the buyer lacks full reliance does not apply, because the appraisers will certainly not obligate him to pay more than the property's value. See there for his proofs.
