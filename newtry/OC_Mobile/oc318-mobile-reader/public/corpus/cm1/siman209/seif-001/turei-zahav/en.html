(Seif 1: and the laws of onaah apply to them.) The Beit Yosef asks from Perek Eizehu Neshech, where one may say, "What my goats milk today is sold to you," implying that the transaction is permitted even if there is onaah. His forced answers are unnecessary. According to Rambam, the case there likewise concerns an amount that does not reach the threshold of onaah. If the discrepancy reaches the statutory measure of onaah, the formula, "what my goats milk," does not prevent the remedy. According to our ruling, however, which follows Rabbeinu Tam regarding gambling, that because each party desires to acquire he fully transfers his own rights, as the Rama writes in siman 207, the same reasoning applies to milk whose amount neither buyer nor seller knows. Because each party wishes to acquire, he commits fully to the transaction.
