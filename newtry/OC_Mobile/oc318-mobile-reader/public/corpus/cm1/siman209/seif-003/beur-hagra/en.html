The seller, etc., gives him, etc. Bava Metzia 72b in the Mishnah: when the market price has been established, etc. This applies even though they did not explicitly set the price, as the Gemara says there regarding contracting at the higher market price. Rabbi Yehudah says, etc. If they had explicitly fixed the price Rabbi Yehudah would not disagree. See Rashi there, s.v. “give, etc.” The implication is that according to the first Tanna the seller is subject to Mi Shepara. Likewise 74b: “If you fixed the price, etc.; I am, etc.; if, etc.”
