A person cannot transfer ownership. See Baer Heitev, who writes that he may nevertheless obligate himself, and so forth. See Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger at the end of siman 141, who cites Knesset HaGedolah, siman 60, as ruling that even if the wording is one of personal obligation, if the giver dies before the fruits come into existence, the heirs are not obligated to deliver them.
