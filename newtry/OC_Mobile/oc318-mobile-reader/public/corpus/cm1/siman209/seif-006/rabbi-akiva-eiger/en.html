[Shulchan Aruch] And if he sold the tree. Note: Mishneh LaMelech discusses whether one who gives money for future produce acquires. See Responsa Maharam Alshich, siman 7; Maharash Chayun, siman 35; Penei Moshe, part 1, siman 34, and part 2, siman 35. See also Toras Chaim, part 2, whose words indicate that it is effective, like transferring a palm tree for its fruit. End quote. See Shach, Yoreh Deah, end of siman 204, and what I wrote there in the margin.
