Even, etc., whether, etc. This was explained in siman 348, seifim 1 and 2.
And if, etc. This appears from the Yerushalmi.
(Likkut) The Yerushalmi proves that taking a trivial item to which people ordinarily do not object is technically permitted, though the Sages acted stringently for themselves lest universal repetition destroy the owner's fence or food supply. See 14 and 50.
