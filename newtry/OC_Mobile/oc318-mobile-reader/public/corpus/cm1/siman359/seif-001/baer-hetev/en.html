From a gentile. The Kesef Mishneh writes that the Rambam deliberately says, 'it is forbidden to rob him,' rather than 'one transgresses,' to indicate that the prohibition is not biblical. The Rambam's language at the beginning of Hilchot Geneivah, copied by the Mechaber at the beginning of siman 348, does not imply this; see what I wrote there. Maharshal, chapter HaGozel, likewise writes that these words of the Rambam and the Semag imply a biblical prohibition. Nevertheless, as a matter of law, Maharshal rules like Rashi's explanation in Sanhedrin that it is only rabbinic. See Responsa Rama, beginning of siman 9. Shach. See also Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 26.
