It is forbidden to rob or oppress. The beginning of Bava Kamma implies that a robber pays from superior-quality land like one who causes damage. The Rif, Rosh, Nimukei Yosef, and Maharshal rule accordingly.
Whether from a gentile. The Kesef Mishneh notes that the Rambam carefully writes that it is forbidden to rob or oppress a gentile, but does not say that one violates the biblical prohibitions stated concerning a fellow Jew. His intent is that the act is forbidden, though the particular negative commandments are formulated regarding Israel.
