He is a robber, etc. The author of Bedek HaBayit writes that the Rambam's language implies that a person who uses another's property without permission is not thereby disqualified from testimony, because he may not realize that he must first obtain permission. This requires qualification: where he knowingly takes and uses another's property against the owner's will, he is a robber and is disqualified; where he relies on an assumed permission, the matter differs.
