"And there are those who say to him that they are not allowed to steal from them, and they are not allowed to pay for their sins, and that the other one knows this."
And he should confer title upon the owners, etc. This applies only where the acquisition is certainly beneficial to the owner, such as property standing for sale. It does not apply to ordinary household possessions. The Tur writes this, and the Mechaber's language should be understood accordingly.
