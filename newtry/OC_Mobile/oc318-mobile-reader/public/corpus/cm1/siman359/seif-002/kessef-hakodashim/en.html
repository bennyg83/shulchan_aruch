See Shach, seif katan 2. According to the view that theft from a non-Jew is not prohibited by Torah law, one must say that the principle 'can there be something permitted to a Jew but forbidden to a Noahide' does not apply here. Perhaps that principle is limited primarily to food prohibitions, whereas in monetary law each society may bear obligations toward its own members because of the structure of mutual dealings. Still, perhaps evasion of a debt among Noahides is itself prohibited at least by the obligation to establish courts and follow rational justice, even before the matter reaches a judge. Ona'ah may perhaps be more severe among them as a matter of interpersonal justice. This requires further study.
