I say. According to Dr. Damer, he is not allowed to steal in order to pay the doubles of their deaths when he is appointed by his mother, by the other hand, he is immediately referred to as an accidental theft that the other knows this in the report. Name:
A benefit. This applies only where it is known to be a benefit to the owner, such as an item standing for sale, meaning it is clearly known that the owner does not intend to keep it for himself but will sell it. It does not apply to ordinary household possessions. So writes the Tur. His words imply that the Rosh speaks of such a case. This resolves all of Maharshal's questions against the Rosh in chapter HaKones, siman 27, and also shows that Tosafot there, page 61, do not dispute the Rosh as a matter of law. See there carefully.
