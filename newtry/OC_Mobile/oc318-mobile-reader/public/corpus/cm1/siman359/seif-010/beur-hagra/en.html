Anyone, etc., who desires, etc.; you have learned, etc. The Mechilta, cited by the Rambam in Sefer HaMitzvos, teaches that “you shall not covet” requires an act, as derived from coveting silver and gold, whereas “you shall not desire” creates a separate prohibition for the desire itself. Desire leads ultimately to coveting, and coveting can lead to coercion and robbery, as the verse says, “They coveted fields and robbed them”; see there.
His servant or, etc. The Mechilta expounds the general and particular terms of “you shall not covet”; see there.
