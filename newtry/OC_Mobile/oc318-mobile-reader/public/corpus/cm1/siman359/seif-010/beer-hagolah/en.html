In Bava Metzia chapter 1 the Gemara says that a person rationalizes, 'I am paying money,' yet he still violates 'You shall not covet.' Thus, one who buys for money, so long as the seller did not initially wish to sell and the buyer obtained it through persistent pressure, violates the prohibition of coveting even though the seller ultimately hands over the object willingly.
