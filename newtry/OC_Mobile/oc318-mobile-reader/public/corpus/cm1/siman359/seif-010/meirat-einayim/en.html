Whoever covets his servant, etc. The Rambam and Mechaber begin with the prohibition of coveting before discussing the prohibition of desiring, even though desire in thought precedes the act of coveting. The reason is literary. They begin by explaining the prohibition with which the preceding seif concluded. Seif 9 ended with one who compels his fellow to sell and thereby violates "You shall not covet." They therefore first define that prohibition, and afterward explain the earlier stage of "You shall not desire."
