(Sema, seif katan 2) It is a Torah prohibition, just as it is forbidden, etc. Note: See Pnei Yehoshua to Kiddushin 46, passage beginning "but it is a loan," who writes that perhaps even if one first stole less than a perutah and later stole another amount less than a perutah, there is no obligation of restitution because each amount is successively waived by the owner. If so, one can say that with theft the rationale of combining amounts does not apply. Independently, this requires study because the Ran on the Rif to Ketubot 44, in the Gemara's question concerning hiring workers for less than a perutah, implies that there is no Torah prohibition of theft involving less than a perutah. He writes that interest of less than a perutah is rabbinically prohibited because it is no less serious than verbal interest. See Tosafot there, passage beginning "the appraisal comes," and Tur, Yoreh Deah, siman 161.
