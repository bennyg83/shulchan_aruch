[Shach, letter 4] For example, with an item that is available. Note: See the Ran, chapter 3 of Moed Katan, page 86a, in the name of the Raavad, that with an item that stands for sale there is no prohibition against taking it with intent to pay. This implies that even if he does not transfer ownership of the money to him through another person, it is permitted. See Responsa Kol Eliyahu, Even HaEzer, siman 3, and Machaneh Ephraim, Laws of Robbery, siman 5.
