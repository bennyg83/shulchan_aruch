The Rosh writes in his rulings regarding Rav Huna's statement about the Jew's stacks of barley that the question was certainly not whether it is permitted to burn them to save a Jewish life, for that is obvious, since nothing stands before danger to life. Rather, the question was whether one may burn them with the intention of being exempt from payment, and the answer was that this is prohibited.
