But he does not have the status of a robber so as to be disqualified by Torah law. Although he violates "You shall not covet," he is not thereby disqualified from testimony or an oath. Ordinary people understand "You shall not covet" to mean taking without payment, and therefore one who pressures another into a paid sale is not treated by society as a robber in the full evidentiary sense. This is unlike the Ir Shushan, who writes that he does not violate a Torah prohibition at all. See siman 34.
