By Torah law. Meaning, although he violates the prohibition of "you shall not covet," as stated in the next seif and in the Tur, he is not thereby disqualified from testimony or an oath because people understand "you shall not covet" as referring to taking without payment. This is unlike the Ir Shushan, which writes that he violates no Torah prohibition. So writes the Sema. His words are surprising, for the Ir Shushan is correct: one who pays yet takes by coercion, a chamsan, violates "you shall not covet" only rabbinically. See above, siman 34, seif 10. The next seif's statement that he violates "you shall not covet" means rabbinically. Shach.
