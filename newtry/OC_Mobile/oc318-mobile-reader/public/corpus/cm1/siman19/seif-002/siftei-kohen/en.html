Gloss: If Reuven and Shimon litigated and one emerged victorious, and the other litigant later sues him before a different beit din, the successful party need not descend into litigation with him or answer his claim. The second beit din is not permitted even to hear the matter, because after one has been vindicated by a beit din, one beit din does not scrutinize the decision of another. So writes Darkei Moshe in the name of a responsum of the Rosh, cited in the glosses to the Sema there. Even if the students see that the ruling was erroneous, they may not tear it up until the master, who knows more than they, arrives. Rabbeinu Yerucham, cited in the glosses to the Sema there.
