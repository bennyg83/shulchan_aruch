[Sema, subparagraph 2] In the name of the responsum at the end of Sefer HaTerumos. Note: Beit Yosef writes similarly above in siman 12 in the name of Sefer HaTerumos and a responsum of the Rosh. Maharikash writes there that if there are no witnesses to the matter, the defendant is believed to say that he already litigated this matter with the claimant and was found exempt, and he need not swear, since even if he admitted the claim he would not thereby owe money, but only be required to litigate again. It is possible, however, that a cherem is imposed upon him not to claim falsely that the matter was already adjudicated.
