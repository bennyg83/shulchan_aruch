How the Ruling Is Delivered and the Order in Which It Is Written. Three seifim. After the judges have deliberated, the litigants are brought in, and the senior judge says, 'So-and-so, you are entitled; so-and-so, you are liable.' A judge may not say after leaving the beit din, 'I ruled in your favor, but my colleagues outvoted me.' One who does so is among those described as, 'One who goes about as a talebearer reveals secrets.' See also siman 40.
