And the greatest of the judges, etc. The reason is so that the litigants will not discern which judge ruled in their favor and which ruled against them. Thus writes the Sema. See Tosafot Yom Tov to Sanhedrin 3:2.
It is included in the prohibition against talebearing, etc. Mahara Stein writes under negative commandment 9 that the prohibition “You shall not go as a talebearer” also includes telling a litigant, “The judge acted unlawfully toward you by finding you liable,” as the Gemara says in the chapter HaGozel concerning an improperly slaughtered animal. The Bach cites him. His proof from HaGozel is not conclusive; see the Gemara there.
