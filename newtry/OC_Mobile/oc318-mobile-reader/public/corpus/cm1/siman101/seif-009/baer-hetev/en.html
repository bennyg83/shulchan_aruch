Immediately. The Sema writes that "immediately" is not literal. In siman 103 the Tur and Mechaber state that land is first appraised and then announced for sale for thirty days. If no purchaser is found willing to pay the appraised value, the creditor must accept it at that valuation. The same applies here. The point is only that the land is valued as if a person who needs it and wants to buy it were presently before us. The fact that no one is currently willing to pay that amount is not proof of a lower value, because people may see that the owner must sell in order to pay his debt and therefore assume he will be forced to sell cheaply. The ruling excludes valuing the property based on what it might fetch if transported elsewhere or if sale were delayed until prices rise. The Taz disputes the Sema. See there and what he writes in siman 109, seif 3.
