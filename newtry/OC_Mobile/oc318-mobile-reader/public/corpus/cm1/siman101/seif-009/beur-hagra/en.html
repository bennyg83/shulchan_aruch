Everything that... Bava Kamma 7b says that anything may count as the best property because the debtor can sell it wherever he wishes. Erchin 24a says similarly regarding consecrated property.
Whether land or movable property. As above. The same applies to land, as shown by the discussion of attracting buyers. Although damages are collected from superior land and creditors by Torah law from inferior land, the principle at issue here is the same, for otherwise borrowers would be deterred from obtaining credit.
