If the borrower holds promissory notes against others, they may be assigned to the creditor as payment. Their value is appraised according to the debtor named in each note, including whether he is a violent person, and according to whether his property is superior or inferior land. {Rama: Even if the borrower owns other movable property, he may pay the creditor with the notes at their appraised value.} See notes 6 and 40.
