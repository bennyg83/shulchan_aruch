To assign them for collection. See the Shach, who extensively challenges the Mechaber and Rama regarding this law and concludes, with proofs, what he regards as the practical ruling. The Taz asks why the debtor can satisfy the debt by assigning a promissory note even if he has other movable property, since there is a concern that the original lender may waive the assigned debt, as stated in siman 66. He answers that the case is where the transfer was made in the presence of all three parties, in which case the debt cannot be waived, as stated there in seif 29, or where the debtor owns land, so that if he waives the assigned debt the creditor can collect from his land.
