(Seif 6) In the case of one who sells merchandise. According to Ritva's reason, that we are witnesses to his intention, as Sema writes in seif katan 10, the distinction is difficult. Tosafot explicitly write at the beginning of HaMokher Peirot, page 52, regarding a creditor, that he lent the money with money in mind. From where, then, is there a basis to distinguish between a sale and a loan?
