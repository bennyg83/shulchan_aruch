(Collected notes) If he has... See the gloss citing the Rashba and the responsa attributed to Ramban, which are Rashba's. In strict law, debt documents may not themselves be subject to collection because they are intangible claims. Rashba in Bava Kamma likewise writes that one who purchases a debt document cannot fully protect himself from the seller's power of waiver. He asks why beit din cannot simply appraise a woman's ketubah and assign it to the creditor, and answers that collection itself does not operate directly on the debt document. Rather, when the debt matures the court may take from the debtor and give to the creditor under Rabbi Nasan's rule. Kesubos 110a also supports this. (End of collected notes.)
