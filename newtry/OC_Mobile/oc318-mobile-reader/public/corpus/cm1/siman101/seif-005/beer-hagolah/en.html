There, seif 6, in the name of his father, the Rosh. Rashba likewise writes at the end of siman 829: I see that even in the era of the great decisors, collection was made even from loan documents. Responsa attributed to Ramban, siman 22, likewise states: I would say that documents are not collected by beit din because they are intangible rights, but what can I do when I see that the practice has spread everywhere for beit din to appraise and collect them? I bow my head to the custom of Israel.
