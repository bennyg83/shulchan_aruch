When he comes to pay, if he has money, etc. The same rule applies to collection of a ketubah. If the heirs possess cash, they must pay her in cash, as indicated by the Tur in Even HaEzer, siman 100, and stated expressly in the responsa of Maharshadam in the name of Rabbi David HaKohen. Indeed, even if the heirs possess land, they must give movable property when available, as explained below in siman 419.
