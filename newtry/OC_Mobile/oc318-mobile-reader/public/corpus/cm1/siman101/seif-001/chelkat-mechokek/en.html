Whether or not they provide her support, etc. Even if they provide her no support at all, she may still collect her ketubah so long as the ketubah document remains in her hand. Rashba writes in a responsum that even if she is elderly and no longer likely to remarry, beit din will still act to collect her ketubah. Beit Yosef cited this responsum above in siman 96, and Darkei Moshe cites it here.
