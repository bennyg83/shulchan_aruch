The Bach writes: If he has money, movable property, and land, but owes debts to gentiles, and he says that if he does not pay them money they will imprison him and he will be held captive, and he will also become liable for double under their laws, we heed him and collect for the Jewish creditor from movable property or land, even though he has money. The Bach brings proofs and concludes that this is the practical ruling.
