Unlike those who say that he must swear concerning this. See Darkei Moshe at the beginning of siman 99, where Rama writes that this is also stated in a responsum of the Rosh cited by the Tur in siman 61:5 and in siman 96. In siman 61 I explained in the Perishah that the case there is specifically one where the debtor claims that he has absolutely nothing from which to pay. Rama further writes there that the same appears from a responsum of the Rashba, siman 1026. But that responsum too is no proof, because it concerns a person who is presumed to be wealthy. See my gloss to Darkei Moshe there. Rama himself concludes expressly that the Mordechai requires only a general ban and not an oath.
