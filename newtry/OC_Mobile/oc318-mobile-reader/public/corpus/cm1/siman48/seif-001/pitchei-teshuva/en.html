Since it was forgiven. See Shach, seif katan 2, where he writes further, citing the Sema, and explains that the case concerns a loan made before witnesses. This is unlike what appears from Responsa Maharam Lublin, siman 35. It would be possible to interpret Maharam Lublin as speaking where the loan was known through witnesses, but this does not appear from the end of his responsum. See Noda BiYehudah, second edition, siman 26, regarding this matter.
