(Collected note) Specifically, etc. According to Tosafos 92a, s.v. “let us see, etc.,” even according to the alternative manuscripts the case of half and half is different because possession stands against the price indication. Where the majority usage supports the price, even if possession points the other way, the money resolves the meaning, as in the case of an ox for plowing. Rashbam, however, holds that money proves intent only where everyone calls an ox together with its yoke by the broader term, even though the yoke alone is also called by that term. Since the price does not contradict the language used by everyone, the price is probative. See Rashbam there and the Rosh at the beginning of chapter 6, who follows Rashbam. Rashbam’s explanation corresponds to Ri in Tosafos 61b, while Ramah follows Rivam, under which price information is unnecessary. This is why Ramah and the Rama explain the clause “if everyone, etc.” as they do. (End of note.)
