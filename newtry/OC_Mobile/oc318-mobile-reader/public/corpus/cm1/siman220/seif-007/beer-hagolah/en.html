Kaf) There, halachah 4, as Ulla says, "the dispute is," etc., on Bava Batra 71a.
Lamed) The Maggid Mishneh writes, following his teacher Rav Yosef ibn Migash, that because Ulla stated the rule without qualification, it follows that these items are always included in the sale.
Mem) This follows the Sages in the Mishnah there, as Ulla explains.
Nun) The Gemara raises an unresolved question whether the Sages concede in this case. The Rif therefore rules that these items are not included, because the burden of proof rests upon the claimant. The reason a saddle blanket and saddle are more readily included than a sack is that they are riding equipment, and a donkey is ordinarily intended for riding. According to Rambam, the same applies when one sells another riding animal: its ordinary riding equipment is included.
