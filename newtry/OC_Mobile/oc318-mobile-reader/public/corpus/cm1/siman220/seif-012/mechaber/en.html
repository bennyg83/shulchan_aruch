If one says, "I sell you the head of this slave," or, "the head of this donkey," he has sold one-half of it. The same applies to every organ upon which life depends. If he says, "I sell you the hand of this slave," or, "the hand of this donkey," the parties make an appraisal between them. Rama: They appraise the organ according to the work for which it is useful, and the purchaser receives a corresponding share of its value. The same rule applies to every organ upon which life does not depend.
