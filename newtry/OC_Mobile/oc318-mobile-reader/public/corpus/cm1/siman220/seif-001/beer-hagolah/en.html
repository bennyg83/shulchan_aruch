Alef) This is the language of the Rambam at the beginning of chapter 27 of Hilkhot Mekhirah, based on the Mishnah and baraita in Bava Batra 73a.
Bet) The baraita there reads askala. Rashbam explains that this is the gangplank of a ship by which one boards it, a Greek term. Rabbenu Chananel and Rav Hai explain similarly. The Sema writes that in his view there are two kinds of gangplank: one used to board the ship from land and disembark from ship to land, and another used to descend to the lower deck and ascend from there to the upper deck. He discusses this at length.
Meaning, large sacks in which merchandise carried on the ship is placed.
