An unspecified maidservant and cow, etc. It is true that the Rosh's rulings contain such wording. The Tur, however, cites the Rosh as saying that even if he says, “this cow, maidservant, or donkey,” without further qualification, etc. In the Derishah I wrote that the Tur's reading is correct. The Rosh ruled that the offspring is sold together with the mother only when he says “this maidservant,” because “this” means the maidservant in the condition before you, and he sees that she is pregnant. If he merely says “a maidservant,” the seller may show him whichever maidservant he wishes, and the pregnancy is not necessarily included. This also follows from the remainder of the Rosh's language, which I cited in the Derishah. The Ir Shushan followed Rama's wording, but what I have written appears correct.
