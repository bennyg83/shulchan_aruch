Without specification. It is true that the Rosh writes this. The Tur, however, cites him as holding that even if one merely says, 'this cow' or 'this maidservant,' the rule applies. The Tur is correct, because he understands that the Rosh wrote that the offspring is included without further wording only when one says, 'this maidservant.' The word 'this' means, 'as she stands before you,' and the purchaser sees that she is pregnant. If one simply says 'a maidservant,' by contrast, the seller may show him whichever maidservant he wishes. This is also evident from the Tosefta. Bach writes likewise. See there. Ibid.
