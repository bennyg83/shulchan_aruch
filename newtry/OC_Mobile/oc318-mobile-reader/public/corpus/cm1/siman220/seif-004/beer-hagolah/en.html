This is the correct reading, and so appears in Rambam. The Maggid Mishneh writes that it is clear from the Gemara that since not everyone, or even most people, call it by that name, it is not included in the sale.
In the name of Ibn Migash's explanation regarding one who sold the yoke, etc., deriving the law from the wagon and mules. The Rashba writes that the Ramban held likewise, because the tanna of the baraita taught the law concerning a wagon, and the same applies to a yoke, since the rationale is identical.
