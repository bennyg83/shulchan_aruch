(Collected note) In all these, etc. Meaning all the rules stated in this siman. They are based on Rashbam 73a, s.v. “saddlebags, etc.” The Rosh writes likewise. This is why this chapter was arranged separately after the rules beginning “When does this apply to a seller,” and the Tur likewise placed this siman separately after completing the laws of land, writing “and in all these, etc.” (End of note.)
As stated above, etc. The Shulchan Aruch there rules likewise.
