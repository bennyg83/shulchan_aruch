Rambam there, law 10, following the first Tanna of the baraita there at the end of 79a, as Rava says: our Mishnah is an individual opinion, Rabbi Natan. The Rif writes that because it is an individual opinion we do not rule like it. The Rashba writes that the halachah follows the Mishnah because an anonymous Mishnah prevails over a dispute in a baraita. To uphold the Rif and Rambam, one may answer that otherwise the Gemara should simply have said, 'Who is the author of our Mishnah? Rabbi Natan.' Since it instead says, 'Our Mishnah is an individual opinion,' this clearly comes to say that because it is an individual opinion the halachah does not follow it. The Nimukei Yosef writes that if one sells a well of living water, everyone agrees that the water is included, because the well is named for the water. So writes the Ritva, cited by the Sema in the name of the Darkhei Moshe.
