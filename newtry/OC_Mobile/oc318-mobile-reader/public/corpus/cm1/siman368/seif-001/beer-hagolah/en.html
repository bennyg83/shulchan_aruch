a) The language of the Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilkhot Gezeilah vaAveidah, law 3. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is explicit in the Mishnah in chapter HaGozel uMa'akhil, Bava Kamma 114a, and the Gemara there explains the distinction between Jewish robbers and gentile robbers.
b) The Bach writes that the statement in the second chapter of Kiddushin that with property robbed from a Jew there must be despair by the owner, discussed in Even HaEzer at the beginning of siman 28, concerns Torah-level kiddushin. It is not Torah-level kiddushin until we know that the owner despaired. Rav Ashi's distinction between a gentile robber and a Jewish robber is only rabbinic. For monetary law, despair alone effects acquisition rabbinically for a Jewish robber, and likewise for kiddushin it effects a rabbinic acquisition. The Bach develops this at greater length in his responsa.
