Know. The Sema writes that the Tur and Mechaber do not distinguish here between non-Jewish judges and Jewish judges, even though the reason they give depends on the judges. Presumably, any Jewish bandit or thief sued by the victim is sued before Jewish judges, whereas a non-Jewish bandit, even when the victim is Jewish, must be sued before non-Jewish judges. See also the Sema's distinction between this case and siman 369, seif 5, regarding customs officials taking clothing. See Responsa Rashdam, siman 157.
With a non-Jew. See the Shach, who objects at great length to the Rama and brings many proofs from the Gemara and authorities.
