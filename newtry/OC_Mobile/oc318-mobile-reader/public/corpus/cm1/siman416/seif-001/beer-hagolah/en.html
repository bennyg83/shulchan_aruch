The wording of Rambam, chapter 13 of Hilkhot Nizkei Mamon, law 19, from the Mishnah in Bava Metzia chapter 6, 117b, cited in Bava Kamma chapter 1, 6b. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam speaks where the damage occurred at the moment of the fall, and therefore he is exempt even though he later abandoned the materials. This is also evident in Bava Kamma from Rambam's stated reason. The phrase 'even though he abandoned them' is difficult to reconcile, because if he did not abandon them there would be even more reason for liability. Sema suggested emending to 'even though he did not abandon them,' but that also cannot work because when the damage occurred during the fall, abandonment is not relevant. The Maggid Mishneh's own explanation of the existing text is strained. In my humble opinion a copying error occurred: these two words were omitted from their proper place and later inserted in the margin, after which a copyist placed them in the wrong location. They should be removed from here and placed at the end of the seif, which should read: 'because he left them there, even though he abandoned them.' Rambam thus teaches that although one who abandons damage after an unavoidable accident is normally exempt, here he is liable because he allowed them to remain after the time fixed by beit din. This appears completely correct to me. I did not alter the text because all books have it as presently printed, but I am confident any careful reader will see that this emendation fits perfectly.
The Maggid Mishneh likewise writes so there according to Rambam, as explained above in siman 412.
