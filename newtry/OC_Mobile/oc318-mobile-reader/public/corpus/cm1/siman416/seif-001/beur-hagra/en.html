(Supplement) Some say that if, etc. Tosafot limits the exemption to where he had no time to remove the obstacle. Rashba and Rosh write that even if he had time, he is exempt if he declared the objects ownerless, and Tosafot also agrees. The rule is that exemption may apply in four situations: at the moment of falling, immediately afterward before knowledge, before there was time to remove the object, or after abandonment. After sufficient time, lack of knowledge or time is treated like the moment of falling, while abandonment has its own rules. See Tosafot 29a and 23a. See 411 and 412.
