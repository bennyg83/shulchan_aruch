The language of Rambam there, law 3, from the Mishnah on Bava Metzia 117a, ruling like the first Tanna.
Maggid Mishneh writes that Rashba asked why the owner of the upper story cannot compel the owner of the house to rebuild. He writes that the Yerushalmi establishes the case where the owner of the house went overseas and beit din does not descend to his property. Ramban likewise writes this, or alternatively the upper owner does not wish to compel him. Maggid Mishneh says this appears to be Rambam's view. Rashba writes that the owner's other property was never liened to the upper resident; only the house itself was subject to his right. Therefore he can never compel the owner personally. Instead, the upper resident builds the house and lives in it.
From Rabbi Natan's baraita there on 117b. Rabbah concludes: hold Rabbi Natan's ruling firmly, because he was a judge who descended to the depth of the law and held, etc.
