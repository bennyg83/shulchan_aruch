The owner of the upper story then builds the house, etc. But we do not compel the householder to rebuild it with his own money, since the owner of the upper story can build below and reside there. See what I wrote at the beginning of this siman.
And he resides in it until the other pays him. This is not regarded as interest, because the land itself is also subject to the upper owner's right, and in any event the householder is not residing there during this period.
Taking a third of the land in the Gem will be plucked because of the increase lost third of the house, according to the House, it would be worth nine years now that the increase and the frequencies in which it is by the House is not standing but a year that takes one third in the land:
Likewise, if they sell it with the consent of both, etc. The Tur likewise requires the consent of both. The householder plainly cannot sell the land against the will of the owner of the upper story even if he is willing to give him one third of the sale proceeds, because the upper owner may say: I prefer the land itself, so that I can build a dwelling upon it later. If the householder wishes to sell while stipulating with the buyer that the buyer will build an upper story above the house, he may obviously do so. What difference is there to the upper owner whether his upper story rests over this person's house or another's? He cannot say that he prefers the temperament of this owner. Tosafot states this explicitly in the chapter HaBayit VeHaAliyah. See also my comments in siman 154.
