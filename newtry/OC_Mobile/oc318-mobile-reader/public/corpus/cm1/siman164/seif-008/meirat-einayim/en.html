And there is one who says that this applies only, etc. The two opinions cited by the Mechaber here are brought by the Tur in siman 157:26. The Tur explains the reasoning. Some say that both owners have equal rights in the common wall and each may build upon it according to his needs. My master the Rosh holds that the other may prevent him and say: I do not want you to diminish the airspace of my courtyard, because I wish to use the space above my own portion. The Tur concludes that the matter depends upon local custom. See there.
