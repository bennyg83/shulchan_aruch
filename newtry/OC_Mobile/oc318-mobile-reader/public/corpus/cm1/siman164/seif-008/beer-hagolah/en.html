Nimukei Yosef in the name of Rashba, and Rashba likewise writes in Responsa siman 931, that these rules were said where one owns the house and another the upper story. The upper owner has no ownership in the house's stones or building, only a servitude enabling him to build above it. Therefore he may not add weight or increase the servitude. This differs from ordinary neighbors where the place and stones belong to both.
