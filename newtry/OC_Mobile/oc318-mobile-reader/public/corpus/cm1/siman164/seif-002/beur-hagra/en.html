They did not become detached, etc.; the expenses, etc. This follows Ramban, the Rosh, and the Tur, based on the reading, 'come and let us rebuild it.' This is the meaning of 'they did not become detached.' See Baer HaGolah, seif katan 9. Rambam follows his own approach stated in seif 1 and therefore explains 'became detached' differently. See Baer HaGolah, seif katan 5.
