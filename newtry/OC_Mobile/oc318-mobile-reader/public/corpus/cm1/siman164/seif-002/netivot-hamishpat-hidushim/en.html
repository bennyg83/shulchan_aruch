Within ten handbreadths. The space within ten handbreadths belongs to the lower owner, who can say to the upper owner, "You have no right to push me out of my domain." Sema:
He demolishes and rebuilds. Meaning the lower owner does so, because only the ceiling sank while the walls remain standing. The Mechaber follows his view that repair of the ceiling is the lower owner's responsibility. According to the dissenting view in seif 1 cited by the Rama, which places ceiling repair upon the upper owner, the cost of raising the ceiling here should be shared, since the lower owner also lacks a proper dwelling and must therefore assist. It is somewhat difficult that the Rama writes that the cost of raising the structure is shared specifically where the ceiling sank because the house walls settled into the ground.
