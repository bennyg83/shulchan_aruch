And concerning some of them he says, I do not know, etc. He is like one who admits part of the claim but cannot swear regarding the rest. The opposing party therefore recovers without an oath, under the rule that one who is obligated to swear but cannot swear must pay, where the matter is one he should have known, as explained in siman 75. Here too, a person ordinarily pays attention to the stones with which his building is constructed and therefore should know. Tosafot states this. See the Perishah.
