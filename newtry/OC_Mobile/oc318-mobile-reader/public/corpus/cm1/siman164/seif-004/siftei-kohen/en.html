And concerning some of them he said to him, etc. He is regarded as one who admits part of the claim, and because he cannot swear concerning the remainder, the opposing party collects without an oath, under the rule that one who is obligated to swear but cannot do so must pay. This cannot be excused as a matter he was not expected to know, as stated in siman 75, because here as well a person ordinarily pays attention to his stones and to what he is building with them, and therefore he was expected to know. This is the language of the Sema, seif katan 11, based on Tosafot at the beginning of the chapter Shor Shenagach et HaParah and in the chapter HaShoel, Bava Metzia 97b. The Nimukei Yosef in the chapter HaBayit VeHaAliyah, however, writes in the name of the Ran that from here we learn that even where he was not expected to know, the rule remains that one who is obligated to swear but cannot do so must pay. I likewise concluded above, siman 72, seif 12, seif katan 51; see there.
