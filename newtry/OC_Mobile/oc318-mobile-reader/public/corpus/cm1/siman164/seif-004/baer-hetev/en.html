The opposing party therefore collects without an oath under the rule that one who is obligated to swear but cannot do so must pay, where the matter is one that he should have known, as explained in siman 75. Here too a person ordinarily pays attention to which stones are his and with which he is building, so he should know. Thus writes Tosafot, as cited by the Sema. This refers to Tosafot at the beginning of the chapter Shor Shenagach Et HaParah and in the chapter HaShoel, page 97b. Nimukei Yosef, however, in the chapter HaBayit VehaAliyah, writes in the name of Ran that from this law we learn that even where he was not expected to know, the rule remains that one obligated to swear who cannot do so must pay.
