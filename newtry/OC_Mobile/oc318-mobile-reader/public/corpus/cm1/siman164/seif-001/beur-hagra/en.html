The ceiling. This follows the Mishnah concerning an olive press and similarly a house and upper story. In partnership cases, the lower owner supplies the structural ceiling and the upper owner the finishing layer. This resolves Tosafos' difficulty, because the dispute in the rental case need not apply identically to co-owners.
Collected note: The house... and the ceiling. The ruling follows Rabbi Yosi and treats the same principle as applying to divided ownership. The Rif concludes that where the water directly falls from above, the upper owner repairs it, consistent with Rabbi Yosi's allocation: the lower supplies the ceiling, the upper the plastering layer. The Gemara's structure and the anonymous Mishnah support him. Tosafos and the Rosh differ, and the same disagreement appears in rental law in siman 312.
Some say. They distinguish an olive press, where the lower owner actually needs the ceiling, from a house, where the upper roof already protects the structure and the upper owner cannot remove it. See Sema and the Rosh.
