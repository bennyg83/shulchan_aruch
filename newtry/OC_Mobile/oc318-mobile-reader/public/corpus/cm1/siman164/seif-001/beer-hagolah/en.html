The language of the Tur. Rambam likewise writes at the beginning of chapter 4. Maggid Mishneh writes that HaBayit VeHaAliyah, Bava Metzia 117a, explains that the lower owner must maintain his house. Kesef Mishneh infers from the Mishnah's phrase, "the owner of the upper story says to the owner of the house: build," that Rambam understands the lower owner to be obligated to rebuild the entire house without assistance from the upper owner. The Mishnah later says that he returns the other's expenses, not half the expenses. Rashi likewise explains this way.
The Mishnah there, Bava Metzia 116a, according to Rabbi Yose, whose reason is explained on 117a: the plaster layer serves to level depressions, and leveling depressions is the upper owner's responsibility. The plaster layer is the coating of clay placed over the ceiling.
