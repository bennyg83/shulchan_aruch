Maggid Mishneh cites the Gemara's distinction: if it collapsed inward or fell outward. Rashi explains that if the house sank from its foundation and the wall collapsed downward in place, it is evident that the lower stones broke; but if the wall fell outward like an upright pole toppling, with its upper portion landing far away, the upper stones broke.
This is explicit in the Mishnah and Gemara there.
