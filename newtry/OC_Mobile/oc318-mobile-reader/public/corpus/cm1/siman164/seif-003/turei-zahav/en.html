(Seif 3: They divide the timbers, etc.) It appears to me that this case too must be understood where the possessor has no claim of credibility through a migo, as I explained in seif katan 57 on seif 5. Namely, the stones were seen in his possession. The principle that partners ordinarily are not particular with one another does not establish his ownership; it merely prevents the other party from being treated as one extracting property from his fellow. This appears from Rashi’s explanation of the Gemara there.
