Because of him. The Tur in the name of the Ramah reads: 'and not money known to have come because of him.' The meaning is that it is not known to us with certainty that this money came from Reuven's property. This differs from the first case, which concerns property definitely known to have come from Reuven. So writes the Bach. The Sema explains differently. Shach. See Responsa Avodat HaGershuni, siman 110.
