Meaning, a son does not inherit his mother after death in the grave so as to transmit her estate to his paternal brothers, nor does a woman inherit her father in the grave so as to transmit it to her husband. Rambam rules like Rabbi Akiva in the Mishnah there, 158a, as the Amoraim explain on 158b, and as Abaye explains there, because the inheritance had already been established in that tribe. The Halakhot and the Maggid Mishneh likewise rule so.
The Maggid Mishneh writes there that this appears correct to him.
