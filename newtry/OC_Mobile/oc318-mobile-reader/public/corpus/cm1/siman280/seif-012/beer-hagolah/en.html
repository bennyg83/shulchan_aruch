Law 8 there and Tur seif 10, from the Mishnah on 157a. The Maggid Mishneh writes that we rule like Beit Hillel, who say the property remains in the possession of the heirs. The Gemara concludes that this applies to all heirs, even where a son and father died and it is unknown who died first. The son's children, who inherit the elder, do not pay their father's debts from that estate.
