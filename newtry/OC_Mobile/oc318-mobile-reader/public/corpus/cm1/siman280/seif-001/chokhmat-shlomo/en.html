Seif 2. Other property fell to Levi and they divide it. Note: This is an explicit Mishna in Bava Batra, chapter 8. Rashbam and Bartenura explain that the property fell to Levi during his lifetime. Tosafot Yom Tov questions why they say so. In my opinion there is no difficulty. From whom could Levi inherit? If from his father's family, then identifying that family would itself clarify whether the claimants are his brothers. The Mishna must therefore concern property coming from his mother, whose identity can be known while his father remains uncertain, or from his wife. Such property can come to him only during his lifetime, because a son does not inherit his mother posthumously in order to transmit her property to paternal brothers, and certainly a husband does not inherit his wife if he predeceased her. Thus the case necessarily concerns property that fell to him while alive. This is simple and correct.
