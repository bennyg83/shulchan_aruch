Tet) There, chapter 5, halachah 1. The Maggid Mishneh writes that the law of a tumtum is explained in the Mishnah in the ninth chapter of Bava Batra 140a. The law of an androgynos follows Rambam's view that an androgynos is an independent halachic category. This is also the Rif's view in Yevamot, chapter HaArel. A baraita in Bava Batra likewise implies that a tumtum and androgynos are treated alike in this respect.
Yod) The Kesef Mishneh writes that Rambam is discussing inheritance only. A tumtum nevertheless receives support from the estate, because whichever status applies, there is a right to maintenance. This is explained below in seif 2.
