If one dies leaving a son and a tumtum or androgynous child, the definite son inherits everything because the other child's status is doubtful. Rama: In every case of certainty against doubt, the doubtful claimant cannot dispossess the certain heir. Thus, if Reuven's son went overseas and it is unknown whether he lives, Reuven's brothers may not descend to the inheritance because the son is the certain heir and they are only doubtful heirs.
