Heirs. Meaning, where it became established before beit din that Reuven and his wife both died. It also appears that the case is one where it was established that Reuven's wife died first and her husband inherited her. Otherwise, her heirs would inherit her ketubah. The author is brief here because these laws are not the present subject; his sole purpose is to teach that we do not suspect that she gave birth. Sema.
