Appreciation that reaches the shoulders. See Sema, seif katan 5, who says there is no difference whether the appreciation arose automatically or through labor. In my view this is very difficult. Tosafot in Bava Batra 42 write explicitly that appreciation reaching the shoulders also refers to property that improved by itself. Likewise, Bava Batra 135 asks about property that appreciated automatically and then distinguishes appreciation that does not reach the shoulders. This proves that the uncertainty concerning appreciation not reaching the shoulders is only where the appreciation arose automatically. Where appreciation came through labor, the heirs certainly divide equally. Tosafot explain that since the deceased did not perform that labor, why should the firstborn receive a double portion in the labor of the surviving heirs?
