There and there in the Gemara, 158b, it is straightforward according to Rava. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this follows the version of the Halakhot, which is also the version in our books. Its meaning is improvement that has reached the stage of being carried on the shoulders, meaning that it is ready to be removed from the land and carried away. The Tur likewise writes this in the name of Rabbi Yosef HaLevi.
Rava's question there remained unresolved. The Maggid Mishneh writes that Rambam ruled in this unresolved question as though the issue had been resolved, etc. The reason is that this claimant is certain and the other is doubtful, as will be explained in chapter 5, below seif 7.
