Increase that reaches the shoulders. Meaning, at the father's death it had already grown fully and, by way of example, reached a person's shoulder height. It is then treated as an independent item and no longer bears the name of the land. All the brothers therefore divide it equally. According to this explanation there is no difference whether the increase came by itself or through labor. This differs from Rashbam's explanation that "reaches the shoulders" means increase produced through physical labor carried on one's shoulders. See the Tur, which cites his explanation.
