[Shulchan Aruch] The father's heirs divide it. Note: If the deceased was indebted to others, it may be said that the creditor collects from the portion taken by the father's heirs, with the argument: since you acquire through him by claiming that his maternal grandfather died first, you must consequently pay his debts. After the creditor collects, there is room to consider whether the father's heirs can recover that property from him, with the result that the law would circle back repeatedly. See Sefer Ketivah, siman 162, letter 13.
