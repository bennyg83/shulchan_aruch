(Sema, seif katan 2) Since there is no publicity regarding how much he lost, etc. Note: This requires study. Regarding payment for produce consumed by a robber, the law should not be classified as a debt written in the Torah. The Torah did not need to create a new obligation for it, for why should he not pay? It resembles repayment of an ordinary loan, which is not called a debt written in the Torah even though Scripture says, "the man who is your debtor," as Tosafot write in Kiddushin 14b.
