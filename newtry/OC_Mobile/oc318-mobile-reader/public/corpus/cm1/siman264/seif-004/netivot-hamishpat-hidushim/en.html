His donkey came up on its own. The case is where he stipulated with the owners. If the owners were present and he made no condition with them, he is not entitled even to ordinary hire. In all the preceding laws, it is not essential that an express condition be made. If the owners were not present, even without an express condition it is treated as though they stipulated. Sema. The Sema disagrees with Rama and holds that if the donkey came up on its own, then even if the owners were present, if they stipulated he receives his entire agreed wage in full. The Shach, in the name of Maharshal, agrees with the Sema. See Beurim: the primary law follows Rama.
