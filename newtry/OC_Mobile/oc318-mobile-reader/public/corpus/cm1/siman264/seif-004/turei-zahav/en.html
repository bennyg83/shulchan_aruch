(Likewise, any person.) The “act” or “benefit” mentioned by the Rama does not mean merely saving another from loss, for that would be the case of one who drives away a lion from another’s property. Rather, it means that he rendered a positive service by overseeing the other’s property. Tosafot write in HaKones 58 that such a case is not included in the rule of driving away a lion. Ran likewise writes this in Shenei Dayanei Gezerot, the source of this ruling, concerning one who arose and supported another without specifying that it was a gift. We do not presume he intended it as a gift.
