"And if it is not so bad." In his book, he said, “And he shall not be saved or saved, if he did not have such a thing as he had written with him, and he shall not be saved or saved, for granted, if he did not have any such abomination with him, and he shall pay him whatever he had done.”
If the second person went out, etc. If one person was seized and another redeemed him with money, the former must reimburse him up to his own value, but not more. If he was seized in a capital matter, he must reimburse even more than his value. Likewise, if one fled and the official seized his sacred books, and another redeemed them, he must repay everything paid because of the disgrace to sacred books. All this is established with proofs in Yam Shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma, chapter 6, siman 15.
