If he did not have to increase his expenses, etc. Responsa Beit Yaakov 148 writes that even where the rescuer did have to increase his expenses, if the other owner expressly protested and told him not to incur them, that owner is not liable. Only where a person's life is endangered by drowning, as below in siman 426 and as explained in Responsa Maharam of Rothenburg 39, is the endangered person's protest ineffective. In rescuing property, however, an express protest prevents liability.
