Nevertheless, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'with that intention,' etc. This case differs because it is like an object swept away by the sea. The Gemara compares it to Rav Safra's case only regarding the duty to pay, as in Rav Safra's case the others did not receive a share in his donkey even though they contributed their own donkeys.
And if there is no, etc. Tosafot on Bava Metzia 31b, s.v. 'if,' and Haggahot Mordekhai and Rosh there, seif 28, explain that although the owner is absent, once the rescuer is paid as an idle laborer he must be compensated for his entire loss. The same applies where his donkey was swept away. See Rosh, siman 265, and Tosafot to Bava Kamma 57a. See 265.
