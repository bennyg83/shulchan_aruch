Chet) This is the language of the Rambam there, halachah 3, from the Mishnah in the tenth chapter of Bava Kamma 115b.
Tet) The Maggid Mishneh writes that even if the owner is not present, Rambam derived this from the Mishnah in Eilu Metziot, Bava Metzia 30b, concerning making a stipulation before beit din. The Raavad writes in his commentary to Bava Kamma that the Mishnah concerns a case where the owner could have saved the property. If he could not, the rescuer can say, "I acquired it from ownerless property." This is explained in the sugya there.
