And see Yoreh Deah, siman 242. There the Rama writes that some say this applies specifically to one's teacher who taught him without payment. But if his father hires a teacher to teach him, then his father's lost object takes precedence over every other lost object. The Rama writes there that this appears to him to be the primary ruling. This is from Sefer Chasidim, siman 585, and the Sema also cites it. Sefer Chasidim further writes there that the same applies to another person who is not his father: if that person hires a teacher for him, the lost object of the one who pays the teacher takes precedence over the lost object of his teacher.
