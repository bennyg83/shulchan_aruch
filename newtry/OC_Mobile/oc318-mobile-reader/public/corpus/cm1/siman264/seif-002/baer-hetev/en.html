242. There the Rama writes that some say this applies only to one's teacher who teaches him without payment. But if his father hires the teacher who teaches him, his father's lost object takes precedence in every respect, and the Rama writes that this appears to him to be the primary ruling. This is from Sefer Chasidim, siman 585. Sefer Chasidim further writes that the same applies to another person who is not his father: if that person pays for his teacher, the lost object of the one who pays takes precedence over that of his teacher. See there, and see the Shach.
