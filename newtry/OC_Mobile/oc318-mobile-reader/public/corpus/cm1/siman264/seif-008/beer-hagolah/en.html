Beit Yosef in the name of Responsa Rashba, siman 1000 and 240, and the Mordechai in chapter HaCholetz. Maharik, shoresh 195, writes concerning Reuven, who promised Shimon to use his efforts without charge. When the effort was almost complete, Reuven retracted and Shimon had to pay him. Later Reuven's money came into Shimon's hands and Shimon said, 'I took what was mine because I was coerced when I appeased you with payment.' Maharik ruled in favor of Reuven. Hagahot Mordechai to Kiddushin likewise discusses one who claims that he promised payment because of fear. The Sema cites this in the name of the Darkhei Moshe. See Maharik, shoresh 173, regarding whether one is believed under oath concerning expenses for bribes.
