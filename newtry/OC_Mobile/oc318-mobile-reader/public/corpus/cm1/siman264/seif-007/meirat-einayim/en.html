In the Perishah and Derishah I proved from the Rosh, Tur, and Rambam that this means literally whatever amount they agreed upon, even if it is not evident that he would actually have earned so much by fishing. Since he had a real opportunity to fish, and it is possible that his net would have yielded enough fish to equal the agreed compensation, the owner must pay the full stipulated amount.
