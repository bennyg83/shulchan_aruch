One who escaped prison and found a ferry may promise the ferryman a dinar to take him across, yet owes only the normal fare. If the ferryman was a fisherman and the fugitive said, 'Abandon your nets and take me across,' he must pay the full amount stipulated, and likewise in similar cases. Rama: Some say that limiting payment to the normal wage applies only where unusually high payment is not customary. For services commonly commanding high fees, such as adjuring spirits or medical treatment, he must pay all he stipulated. For matchmaking, however, he receives only the customary fee even if a larger amount was promised.
