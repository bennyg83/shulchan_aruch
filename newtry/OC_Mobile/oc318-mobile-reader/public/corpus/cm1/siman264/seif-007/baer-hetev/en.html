And in matchmaking. The same law applies to a broker, contrary to Responsa Divrei Rivot, siman 326. Responsa Mahari ibn Lev likewise writes that it applies specifically to a matchmaker and not to a broker. See there. This is incorrect, and the matter is clear. A large amount. Maharshal, in the first chapter of Bava Kamma, siman 38, rules that one gives him the entire amount that was stipulated. It appears to me that there is no disagreement: Maharshal discusses a place where matchmaking fees have no fixed customary rate, as also appears from his words there. See there. Shach.
