He has only his wage. See what I wrote above, seif 4, seif katan 1, from Responsa Chavot Yair. It is clear there that if the amount stipulated is not substantially greater than a proper wage, he receives the amount stipulated. Responsa Chut HaMeshulash, part IV of Tashbetz, third column, end of siman 20, likewise states that where the increase is close to what he reasonably deserves, the promisor must pay what he undertook. Netivot HaMishpat cites this and writes that "close" appears to mean within one sixth.
