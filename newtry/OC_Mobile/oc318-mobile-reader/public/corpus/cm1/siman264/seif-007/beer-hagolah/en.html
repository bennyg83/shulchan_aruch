It appears that Rambam holds that even if one promised more than the other party actually lost, he must pay the promised amount. In my humble opinion this follows the poskim who do not rule like Rabbi Yishmael, as explained above in seif 5 in the gloss and at the end of siman 264. But according to those who rule like Rabbi Yishmael, who requires a person to pour out his wine in order to save another's honey, he need not be paid more than his actual loss and appropriate effort. A hunter is different because he might genuinely have earned that amount.
Meaning, payment for the physician's professional expertise. But if the patient, because of the urgency of his illness, promised an excessive amount merely for medicinal ingredients, the physician receives only their actual value. The Maggid Mishneh writes this in the name of the Rashba, and the Mechaber rules accordingly in Yoreh Deah siman 336, seif 3.
