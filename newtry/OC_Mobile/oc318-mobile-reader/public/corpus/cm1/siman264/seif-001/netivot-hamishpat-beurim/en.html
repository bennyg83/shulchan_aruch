If rescue is impossible altogether, the rescuer acquires from ownerless status, as Bava Kamma 115 indicates. Also, the rule requires that without the rescuer there would have been definite loss. If the property would probably have been saved anyway, even ordinary rescue wages may not be due, like one who merely drives away a lion, as Tosafot in Bava Metzia 31 explain.
