In such a case the other owner can be exempt even where the property could not have been saved without these expenses, because the rescuer descended solely for his own benefit. This is one benefiting while the other suffers no loss. Rama therefore concludes that if he descended intending to benefit both parties, the other must contribute. The reason is that where the property cannot be saved without such expenditures, each owner can compel the other to contribute, like a caravan attacked by a band in siman 272. As I wrote in siman 178, whenever a repair is necessary to both parties and one cannot secure it without the other, they can compel one another to share the expense.
