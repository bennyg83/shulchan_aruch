Ateret Tzvi, and similarly Maamar Kedushin, write that according to what is explained in siman 259:4 in Rama and Shach, the same rule applies here: nowadays such property must be returned in every case. The governing enactment in our times is the sovereign's decree in every locality that items seized during a fire must be restored, whether or not the owners were present. Responsa Beit Ephraim, Choshen Mishpat, end of 58, likewise records that this is in fact the daily judicial practice. See also what I wrote above, siman 259:7:3.
