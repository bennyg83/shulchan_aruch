The appropriate amount. Here too the Gemara and Tur explain the reason. The owner of the honey was present, and the owner of the wine should have stipulated with him that he would rescue the honey only on condition that the owner reimburse him for the value of his wine. Since he did not stipulate, the honey owner may say: had you not rescued it, I could have found someone else who would have done so for this wage. It follows that if the owner of the honey was not present, he must reimburse the full value of the wine, as the Rama writes in seif 3, and so states the Rosh. Since the present seif concerns two people who encountered one another, the owner of the lost property is present, so the law where he is absent was not stated explicitly here; the authorities relied on what was written above.
