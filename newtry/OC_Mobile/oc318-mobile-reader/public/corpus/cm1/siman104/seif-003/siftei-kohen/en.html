There is no law of priority among them. The Bach cites Hagahot Maimoniyot and rules that where other creditors are known, the court should reserve their shares until they appear, unlike the Beit Yosef, who would distribute to those presently before the court. A responsum attributed to Ramban appears to say that if a creditor is reported to have died overseas, beit din may sell land for a widow's ketubah despite concern for earlier creditors. That text requires study and appears corrupt. It should be understood in accordance with the Rama's ruling above, based upon Rashba.
