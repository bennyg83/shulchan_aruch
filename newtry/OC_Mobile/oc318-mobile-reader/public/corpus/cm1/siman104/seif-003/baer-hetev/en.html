Priority. The Bach rules in accordance with the responsum of Maimonidean authorities that if it is known that there are other creditors, the beit din sets aside their share until they arrive, unlike the Beit Yosef, who rules that collection is made for those presently before us. See there. The Shach cites a responsum of the Ramban, siman 82, brought by the Beit Yosef in Even HaEzer siman 102, concerning sale of land for a ketubah despite concern for earlier creditors, and writes that the text of that responsum requires study. He suggests that there is a scribal error and that its meaning is as the Rama writes in the gloss to seif 1 in the name of the Rashba. See also Responsa Mabit, part 2, siman 213.
