This is not considered seizure of money. Debt documents are merely evidence of the underlying debt. Sema gives the reason that the original creditor can waive the debt, but according to that reasoning, where waiver is impossible, as above in siman 66, seizing the documents should help. In truth that is incorrect. Even then the possessor has not seized the underlying money itself. See Tumim. If beit din transferred the debt documents to him through formal collection, they are treated as collected and an earlier creditor has no right in them. Likewise, if the debtor refuses to pay until the physical document is returned to him, possession of the paper itself counts as full seizure regarding the paper, as explained above in siman 64.
