Here, however, both debt instruments can be maintained by saying that the borrower incurred two separate obligations on the same day. We do not assume that a borrower foolishly ordered two documents written for one loan. Levush's explanation based on a presumption that witnesses do not sign twice for one debt is insufficient, because the two documents may have different witnesses.
