He collects both of them. See Sema, seif katan 30, through his rejection of the Ir Shushan, who tied the rule to the witnesses and to the chazakah that witnesses do not sign twice for one debt. The Sema's criticism is not correct. The Ir Shushan means that it is obvious that if each note bears different witnesses, both are collected, because witnesses would not sign on an improper duplication. He adds that even if the same witnesses signed the second note as well, both are collected, and we do not suspect that the witnesses forgot or erred and wrote a second note for the same debt, because there is a chazakah that witnesses do not sign twice for a single debt. The Ir Shushan states this rule explicitly.
