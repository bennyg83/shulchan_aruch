Or sixty days on Mondays and Thursdays. Although if one counts only the actual proclamation days there are merely eighteen, because the period extends over a long time people hear about it. This is the Gemara's language. The eighteen days are calculated as follows: beginning on a Monday, eight weeks amount to fifty-six days, with two proclamation days each week, Monday and Thursday, totaling sixteen. Four more days remain to complete sixty, Monday through Thursday, adding two more proclamation days, for a total of eighteen.
