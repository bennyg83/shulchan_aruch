(Mechaber, seif 2) By a document of the municipal authority, etc. Note: Ritva's novellae to Ketubot, cited in Shitah Mekubetzet on 100, state: 'We learn from this that where beit din sold by public announcement, even if the announcer says that anyone who does not present his right will lose it, that statement has no effect. However, in some places the townspeople enacted conditions concerning announcements, and their communal agreement is binding with respect to townspeople and those attached to them, provided they are adults. They have no authority to impose such a condition upon minors or upon persons who are not residents of the city.' This is from our teacher, of blessed memory.
