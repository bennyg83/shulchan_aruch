[Rama] All this applies to a loan evidenced by a document. Note: Where a creditor holding a document and an oral creditor come to collect from orphans, if the documentary creditor admits the oral creditor’s debt and the estate is insufficient for both, they divide it, because of his admission. But if the estate is sufficient for both, the documentary creditor takes his entire debt, for he can say, “The property is before you,” even though the oral creditor cannot collect from the orphans on the strength of the documentary creditor’s admission. So writes Beit Meir, siman 105.
