The one who came first and collected acquired. See Baer Heitev. In the Vilna Gaon's glosses to the Shach, on the Shach's statement that it is like seizure and therefore effective, he writes: "Examine this carefully. The Beit Din himself wrote in his Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer siman 102, seif 3, that with land seizure is ineffective unless effected through beit din."
