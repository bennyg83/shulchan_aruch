In his possession. In that case the first lender's lien immediately attached to that field before the second loan. If the borrower acquired the field only afterward, the liens of both creditors attach to it together. The Sema adds that if the borrower acquired it between the two loans, after borrowing from the first but before borrowing from the second, and the first lender had not received an after-acquired-property clause, the second lender alone may collect from it, since it was already in the borrower's possession when the second loan was made, unless the first lender had such a clause. See Even HaEzer siman 102 regarding a wife's ketubah and a creditor. The Shach disagrees and holds that where the property was acquired between the loans, the creditors divide it. See Responsa Maharashdam, siman 210.
