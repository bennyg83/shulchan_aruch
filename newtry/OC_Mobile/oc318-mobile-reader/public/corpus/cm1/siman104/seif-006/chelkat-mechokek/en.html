And they did not write an audit document, meaning a precise record of the appraisal and announcement. If they made no announcement at all, the preceding law already explained that even without valuation error the sale is void. If the case were in a place where announcements are not made, the Mechaber shortly afterward says that if they sold property when they did not need to, etc. Rather, this case is where they did announce, but did not write the audit document recording the exact appraisal and announcement. Then if they erred by one-sixth the sale is void. Kesef Mishneh explains it this way, unlike Maggid Mishneh.
