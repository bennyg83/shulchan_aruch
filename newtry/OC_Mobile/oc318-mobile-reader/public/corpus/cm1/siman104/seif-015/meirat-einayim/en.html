Interest that accrued from the date of the document, etc. Meaning interest on Shimon's documents, as stated in Rashba's responsum.
Shimon's argument has no effect. Above in siman 86 Rama cites Mahari Weil that Shimon may redeem a non-Jew's collateral from Reuven by paying the principal and interest accrued until that day, and then collect his own debt from the excess. Rama rules that way against the dissenting Beit Yosef. There is no contradiction. Here Shimon seeks to prevent Reuven, when Reuven comes to collect his document, from collecting interest that had already accrued on the non-Jew's property retroactively from the time Shimon borrowed. In siman 86 Shimon is willing to pay Reuven all principal and interest accrued to the present and only seeks to stop future interest by redeeming the pledge now.
