In siman 86, seif 1, the Rama writes that according to one opinion Shimon may redeem from Reuven collateral that belongs to a non-Jew by paying Reuven the principal and accrued interest, and may collect his own debt from the excess, and that this appears to be the primary ruling. See the Sema there for his distinction explaining why that case is different from the present one. See also Responsa Bach 26.
