Unless beit din appraised them. See Tumim, who writes that with land, since collection requires beit din, appraisal is certainly required. With movable property, however, seizure is effective without appraisal. Ketzot HaChoshen writes that seizure is effective because the creditor acquires the item as a pledge, even if he seized it forcibly. It is explained in siman 97, seif 16, that even a pledge taken forcibly is not treated as ordinary movable property in the hands of the creditor's sons and is not released by Shemittah. This shows that he acquires the pledge. See there.
