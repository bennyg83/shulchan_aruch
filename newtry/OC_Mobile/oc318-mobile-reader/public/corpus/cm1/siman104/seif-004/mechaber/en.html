It is not considered collection unless beit din appraised the movable property and actually placed it into the creditor's possession. If beit din merely closed the debtor's shop for the benefit of a later creditor, that is not collection, and an earlier creditor may collect from those movables. Rama: If beit din attached Reuven's money held by Shimon because Reuven owed Levi, and Shimon violated the attachment and gave the money to Reuven, see above, siman 86, seif 5.
