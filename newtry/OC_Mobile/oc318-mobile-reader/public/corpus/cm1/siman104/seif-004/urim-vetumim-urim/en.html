With movables, no formal collection through beit din is required, and even a beit din appraisal is unnecessary. With land, however, authorities dispute whether formal beit din collection is required. According to those who require beit din collection, an appraisal is obviously necessary, because there is no such thing as beit din transferring land without valuing it.
