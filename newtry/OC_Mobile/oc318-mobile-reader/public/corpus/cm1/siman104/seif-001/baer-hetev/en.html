If a later creditor wrongfully collected first through a secular court, and the earlier creditor had no power to prevent it, and the later creditor then sold the land to another Jew, the earlier creditor may not remove it from that purchaser. Responsa Rama, siman 109. If a community enacted that communal sacred funds take priority over creditors even when their claim arose later, the enactment applies only to unencumbered assets, not to property already encumbered to others. Responsa Maharit, Choshen Mishpat, siman 101; see also siman 8, Responsa Mabit, part 1, siman 111, part 2, simanim 196 and 212, part 3, siman 94, and Responsa Maharshadam, simanim 108, 109, 135, 145, and 285. This applies where it is certain that the creditor is later. Where it is doubtful whether he is later, the property is not removed from his possession. Responsa attributed to Ramban, siman 22.
