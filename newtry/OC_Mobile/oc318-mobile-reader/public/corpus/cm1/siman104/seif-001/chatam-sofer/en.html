Mechaber, from seif 1, "we remove it from his possession." Note: if the later creditor wishes to remove the earlier creditor by paying him money, see Ketzot HaChoshen, siman 107, seif katan 5, who writes that he cannot do so. This is also explicit in Tosafot, Ketubot 94a, s.v. "where one was found not to be his." See there. From the glosses of the Ketav Sofer.
