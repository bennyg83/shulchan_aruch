[Shulchan Aruch] And the house fell upon him and upon those from whom he inherits. Note: Sefer HaTerumos, gate 48, seif 8, writes that this applies specifically where the person from whom he inherits is his father, because then we say that he comes by virtue of his father’s father. If, however, he inherited from his paternal uncle, his father’s creditor collects from him. See there, and Responsa Shav Yaakov, Choshen Mishpat, siman 18.
