A loan evidenced by a document, etc. See Be'er HaGolah and Bava Batra 176a.
(Supplement) An oral loan, etc. Ramban disagrees and holds that a documented loan has priority, and that property seized by an oral creditor may be removed from him. He adduces proofs from Ketubot concerning judicial sale for one of two obligations and the priority of maintenance claims. Rashba rejects these proofs, but Ramban's arguments remain strong and are accepted by Rif, Raavad, and Ran. Beit Yosef nevertheless follows Rashba because Maggid Mishneh reads Rambam that way. See citations 2 and 61.
