There, seif 13, in the name of Rav Hai. Rashba agrees in Responsa siman 914. For according to the view that a lien is d'oraisa, by strict law even an oral loan could be collected from encumbered property. The Sages were concerned for the protection of purchasers because an oral loan has no publicity. But with a written loan we leave the Torah law in force: since it preceded the other claim in time, it also has priority in collection, for we rule that a lien is d'oraisa, as in Bava Batra 171b.
However, if the later creditor already collected first, then we say that what he collected remains collected, as Rama writes above in seif 1. Rashba writes likewise there in the responsum.
