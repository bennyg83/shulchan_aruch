And he wrote to him, "what I will acquire in the future." If a debtor borrowed twice, wrote an after acquired property clause to one creditor but not the other, and later acquired property that remains free in his hands, Beit Yosef and Bach hold that the creditor with the after acquired clause has priority. The Shach says they divide, arguing that as against the debtor himself no after acquired clause is needed. I have already explained why the Shach's view is difficult. Tumim seeks support for the Shach from the rule of improvement collected by a creditor. If the seller wrote an after acquired clause to the lender but the purchaser did not receive one from the seller, the creditor collects the improvement. Tumim argues that the seller's obligation to reimburse the purchaser arises only when the land is actually taken, so how can property have been subjected earlier to a debt that did not yet exist? He proposes that one may subject property now to a future obligation. That cannot be correct. A person's assets cannot be subjected where his body is not yet obligated. Rosh in Kiddushin applies the rule, "There is no maneh, therefore there is no collateral." If no personal obligation exists, there is nothing for the property to secure. Rashi's statement that one can write a document now that will secure a later loan is different, because the document creates a present conditional undertaking: if the money is later lent, the personal obligation and property lien relate back under the present agreement. One cannot create only a property lien now while leaving the personal debt to arise independently later. Nor can one say, "My property shall become encumbered when I later become obligated," without confronting the problem of something not yet in existence. Tosafot itself asks how one can designate an object for a fine that has not yet become payable. Therefore when a seller accepts responsibility for a buyer's loss, the correct understanding is that he undertakes the responsibility personally from the time of sale, conditioned upon later dispossession. His body is already obligated conditionally, and his property can be subjected from that time. This is the only coherent structure.
