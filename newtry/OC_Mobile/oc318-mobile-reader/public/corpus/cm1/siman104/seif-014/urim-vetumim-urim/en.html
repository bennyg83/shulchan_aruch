Beit din does not collect, etc. Although we rule that if the earlier creditor first sold the property, the later creditor has no claim upon it, Rambam holds that this applies specifically to a sale. Merely encumbering the property is different. Even making it an apotiki, or encumbering it for a wife's ketubah, does not remove it in the same manner. See below, siman 248, where I discuss this.
