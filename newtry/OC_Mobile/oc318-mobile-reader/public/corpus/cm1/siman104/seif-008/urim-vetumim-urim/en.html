All on one day. See Tumim. Where the document includes a kinyan, one might have applied *shuda deDayanei*, as Ran and Ritva write. Nevertheless, because the Shulchan Aruch states unqualifiedly that all divide, I concluded that division is the rule. The reason is not that all liens necessarily take effect simultaneously. Rather, the Sages adopted division in order to prevent closing the door to borrowers, and because there is no reliable basis to determine which creditor was actually first. See there, unlike Sema, who says all liens arise from the previous evening and therefore occur simultaneously.
