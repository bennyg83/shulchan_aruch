Two hundred. The lien of the creditor owed one hundred extends over all the available property just as does the lien of the creditor owed eighty, contrary to Rabbeinu Tam, cited by the Tur, who holds that they divide proportionally according to the amounts owed. Sema.
How. The Sema writes that the Mechaber could seemingly have said simply that each receives according to the amount of his debt. The more detailed formulation is necessary because there is a practical difference in how intermediate, inferior, and superior land are allocated. In his view, they first divide the intermediate quality land. See there. See also Even HaEzer 102, seif 4, and Gidulei Terumah, Shaar 43, part 4.
