Maggid Mishneh writes that wherever an earlier and later creditor divide the property, a purchaser likewise takes his share and the creditor does not seize everything, etc. This is straightforward. Kesef Mishneh writes this in the name of Tosafot and Ramban, and it is compelled by the law.
There, following the first view. Maggid Mishneh writes that this division follows the Halachot in Mi SheHayah Nasui, which rule like Rabbi in the baraita, Ketubot 93a. The law of this division is explained there at length. Many later authorities agreed with this view, and we act according to the Halachot and live by their rulings. This also appears from Rambam in chapter 21 of Hilkhot Malveh and in chapter 21 of Hilkhot Ishut. Tur likewise writes that the Rosh agreed with this view.
