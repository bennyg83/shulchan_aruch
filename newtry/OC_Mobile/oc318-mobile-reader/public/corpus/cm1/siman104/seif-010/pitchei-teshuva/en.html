Where one person holds two debt instruments, whether distribution is calculated by persons or by instruments is discussed by the Shach above, siman 53, seif katan 2, cited there in Baer Heitev. His language plainly assumes that the holder of two notes receives two shares. See also Responsa Bach, siman 35; Responsa Tzemach Tzedek, siman 15; Responsa Shevut Yaakov, volume 1, siman 156; Responsa Givat Shaul, siman 20; and Responsa Neot Desheh, simanim 18 and 19. See at length there, and see also Tumim and Netivot HaMishpat on this issue.
