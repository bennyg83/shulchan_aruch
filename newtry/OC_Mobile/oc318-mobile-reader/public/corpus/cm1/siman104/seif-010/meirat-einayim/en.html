If six hundred are found, they divide three hundred equally, etc. The reason the Mechaber does not simply say that each creditor takes according to the amount of his debt is that this method of division affects which grades of land each receives. In my view, they first divide the intermediate quality land, because a creditor ordinarily collects from intermediate land. When that is exhausted, they divide the inferior land, as applies where the debtor owns only superior and inferior land, as explained in siman 102. When the inferior land too is exhausted, they divide the superior land. See the Perishah.
