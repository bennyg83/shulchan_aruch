[Shulchan Aruch] If, when the money found is divided. Note: See Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 1, siman 156, who rules principally like Tzemach Tzedek that if one creditor holds two debt documents, distribution is made according to the number of documents. If, however, they are commercial bills whose customary form provides for payment according to capital, then since mercantile custom is to distribute in proportion to the amount, and that custom has a basis in the view of Rabbeinu Chananel, one who accepted commercial custom regarding payment must follow it. See there. See also the same responsa, part 2, siman 162, concerning two or three merchants, each of whom gave a chest of merchandise to a wagoner to transport to market. The wagoner suffered an unavoidable loss on the road, the merchandise had unequal values, and all three sued him in a secular court, which imposed a compromise payment. They disputed how to divide that payment. He concludes that they divide it in proportion to the value of their merchandise, comparable to the law of a gift below in siman 111, seif 8, and according to the reasoning of the Sema there, subparagraph 18. The same applies here.
