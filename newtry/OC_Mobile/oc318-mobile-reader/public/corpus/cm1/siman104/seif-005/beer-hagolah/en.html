Bach writes in the name of Maharshal that the word 'agav' should be deleted, and that he found this in an accurate copy corrected by a great scholar. This accords with Tur's words, 'and he returned and acquired,' which otherwise have no clear meaning. Bach himself attempts to uphold the printed reading; see there.
