By means of movables together with land. The source of this law is explained in siman 60 and below in siman 113. There it is explained that even nowadays, where a borrower borrowed from one creditor and later from another, property is taken from the later creditor when the earlier lien included movables by way of land. The protection of market transactions applies only where the movables were sold. See there for the rationale. Sema. See also Responsa Maharashdam, siman 102, simanim 100 and 110, and Responsa Maharit, siman 128.
