For it is not. Rosh at the end of Bekhorot and Ramban derive from this mishnah that damages are not collected from property merely anticipated to come into the debtor's possession, even though the obligation is treated like one recorded in a document. They also cite the Tosefta and Yerushalmi. Baal HaTerumot records dissenting views, but the principal ruling is as stated here. Tosafot dispute the rationale in certain inheritance cases, while Ramban maintains that the decisive factor is that the property was not yet possessed. See citations 80, 100, and 107.
