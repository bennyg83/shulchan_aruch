It is a matter in the “Nach” page, and the property is in possession, which means that the possession of heirs does not have a “higher” of them
Mordekhai, chapter Yesh Nochalin, writes that for this reason a creditor cannot collect from wages for labor that never came into the debtor's possession before he died. Tur, siman 106, writes that a creditor does collect from a loan receivable left by the debtor after death, even though it is considered 'prospective property' with respect to a firstborn, as explained below in siman 278.
