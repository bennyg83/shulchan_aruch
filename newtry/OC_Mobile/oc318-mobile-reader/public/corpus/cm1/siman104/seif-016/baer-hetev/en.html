Property merely expected to come to him. See Maharshal's responsa concerning a half-male inheritance document that states that the beneficiary shall take from both presently held property and property expected to be acquired. Maharshal rules that it is effective even as to property that will later fall to his father-in-law by inheritance after the latter's death. He there cites a responsum of the Rama that does not rule so and disputes it. See there. Sema. The Shach discusses this at length and cites many leading poskim and responsa that analyze these laws in detail. See there.
