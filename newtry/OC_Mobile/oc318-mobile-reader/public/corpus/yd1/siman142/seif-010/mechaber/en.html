The shade of a house of elilim: inside it, and opposite its entrance within 4 amot, is forbidden; the shade that is behind it is permitted. And even inside it, if it is a public thoroughfare where the road preceded it and afterwards they built there a house of elilim, it is permitted to pass through there; but if the house preceded the road, it is forbidden. And there are those who forbid in every case (Remez).
