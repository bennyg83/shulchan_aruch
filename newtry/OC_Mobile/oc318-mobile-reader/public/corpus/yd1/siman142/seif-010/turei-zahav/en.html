The shade of a house of idol worship. This means that specifically an asheirah, which is made to provide shade, is forbidden in every circumstance. A house, however, is made only for dwelling within it. Therefore, the shade cast behind it is permitted, as we say in the chapter Kol Shaah concerning the Sanctuary building. This is the Ra'avad's opinion cited by the Tur. Tosafos, however, write according to one resolution that idol worship is more stringent. Accordingly, even the shade of a house of idol worship is forbidden in every circumstance. This is the opinion introduced by the Mechaber at the end of the seif as “there are those who forbid.” At first glance, it would appear that this opinion forbids even where the object encroaches upon the public domain. Yet no authority says this, for an explicit Mishnah permits it even regarding an asheirah, and the Mishnah likewise records Rabban Gamliel's statement, “It came into my boundary.” It therefore appears to me that the stringent opinion refers only to the beginning of the seif, which permits the shade of a house of idol worship.
