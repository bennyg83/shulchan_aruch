Shade of house of idols. Meaning: specifically asherah made for shade forbidden in all respects; but house is not made except to dwell in — therefore shade after it permitted as we say in chapter "every time" in the Temple hall — Raavad's opinion in Tur; but Tosafot wrote as one stringency of idol that it is different; therefore even in house of idol shade forbidden in all respects; and this is "there are forbidding" the mechaber brings at end of seif; and at first glance appears "there are forbidding" forbids even if it robs the public — and there is no one who says complete Mishnah permits even in asherah; and we also say above in Mishnah in Rabban Gamliel "it comes within my boundary"; but it appears to me it refers only to the first part of the seif permitting shade of house of idol.
