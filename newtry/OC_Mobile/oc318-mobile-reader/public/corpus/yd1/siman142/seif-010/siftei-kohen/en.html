Within four cubits it is forbidden. This is because one must distance oneself four cubits from idolatry, as stated below at the beginning of siman 150.
The shadow behind it is permitted. This means sitting in its shade, because it was not made for shade. Merely passing through even the shade of an asherah is permitted, as the Rama writes.
It is permitted to pass through there. Even without running, as stated in seif katan 20, provided he has no other route. If another route exists, even if it unlawfully encroaches on the public domain, passing there is forbidden even at a run. This follows from the Gemara's question, “If another route exists, would saying ‘Make me run’ render it permitted?” Rav Sheshet's asherah must therefore have obstructed the public way.
Some prohibit it in every case. This refers to the opening clause: they prohibit even sitting in the rear shade of a building, because they equate the shade of an asherah and that of a building. No authority prohibits the other cases. This is clear from Beit Yosef.
