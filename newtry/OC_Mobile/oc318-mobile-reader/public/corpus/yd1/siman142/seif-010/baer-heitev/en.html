Behind it. Since that area was not created for shade, one may even sit there. Merely passing through the shade of an asherah is permitted in any event, as Rama writes. Shach.
To pass. Even without running, provided there is no alternative route. If there is another route, passing is forbidden even if the asherah occupies the public road and even if one runs. Shach.
Forbid. This refers to the first clause: they forbid even sitting in the shade behind the house, because they equate the shade of an asherah and a house. In the other cases no authority forbids. Shach and Taz.
