If there is a bathhouse in which there are elilim, it is permitted to bathe in it, because it is made for ornamentation and not to worship it, as it says, “their gods,” meaning when they treat it as a divine entity and not when they disgrace it — such as this, that it stands on the sewer and everyone urinates before it. But if its manner of worship is such, it is forbidden (the language of Rambam).
