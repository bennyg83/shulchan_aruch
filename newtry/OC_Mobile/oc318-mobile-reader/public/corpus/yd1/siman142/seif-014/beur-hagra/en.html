A bathhouse, etc. This is the Rambam's language. His reading in the Mishnah treats “another matter” as referring to the second clause: Another matter, one does not say, “It was made as a bathhouse,” etc. It is an ornament for the bathhouse, for if they offered you money, etc. This explains the statement that it serves as ornamentation and is not worshipped.
And if it was customary, etc. The Gemara there states: Rabbi Elazar said, “A response,” etc.
