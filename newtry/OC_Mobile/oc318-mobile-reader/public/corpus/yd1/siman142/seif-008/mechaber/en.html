Chicks that nested in an asheirah and do not need their mother are permitted; but eggs upon it, and likewise chicks that need their mother, are forbidden. And the nest itself that is on its top is permitted, because the bird brings its wood from another place. (The language of Rambam, Hilkhot Avodat Kochavim 7.) (And one should not climb on the asheirah to take them; rather, he should knock them off with a reed and they will fall) (Tur).
