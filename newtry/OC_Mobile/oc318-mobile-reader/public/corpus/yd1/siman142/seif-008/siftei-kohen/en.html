And likewise chicks that still need the tree are forbidden. Because they need the tree and the Rabbis decreed about them as about the tree itself — Rashi. And the Raavad explained: because they are like the growths of an asherah.
Because the bird brings its wood from elsewhere. For if it took its wood from the asherah, even though it broke off, we rule below siman 146 seif 11 that an idol broken by itself is not nullified.
And he shall not ascend the asherah etc. Meaning: even those that do not need the tree, one shall not ascend the asherah to take them; and thus those that still need the tree — even if he knocks them down with a stick and they fall, forbidden; and the Magen Avraham wrote: chicks that still need the tree, since he cannot take them without benefiting from the asherah — ascending it or leaning on it — they are considered like growths of asherah and forbidden for benefit rabbinically — end; and I do not know why he forced it unnecessarily, for even knocking them down with a stick when he does not ascend the asherah is forbidden; and what Raavad wrote that they are like growths of asherah means: since they still need the tree, they are considered like wood of the asherah — and one should understand this.
