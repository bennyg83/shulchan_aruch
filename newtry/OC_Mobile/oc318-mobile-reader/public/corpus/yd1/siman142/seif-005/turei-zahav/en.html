Bowls, etc. These utensils are used without any subsequent contribution from a permitted cause, unlike a new oven that was cooled and then heated again. However, it is difficult that pots are also mentioned, for a pot is exactly like a new oven that was cooled, and the Gemara explicitly mentions an oven. It appears that the Rambam is discussing small pots from which people drink, which is why he lists them together with cups and flasks. Pots used for cooking are like a new oven, concerning which we say that it should be cooled. The reason is that it requires a subsequent permitted heating before bread can be baked in it, as I wrote shortly according to the Rambam's view.
