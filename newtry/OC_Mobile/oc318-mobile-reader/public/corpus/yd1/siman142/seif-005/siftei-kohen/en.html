And what the mechaber wrote regarding bowls and cups etc., behold these are forbidden for benefit. The reason is that bowls and cups are subsequently used without a permitted cause; and a pot too, although one cannot cook in it without wood, nevertheless the way is to place water and meat in it and afterwards set it on the fire — if so he uses it without a permitted cause; so it is in the Gemara. And accordingly, where it is clear to him he did not use it until after setting it on permitted wood, the dish is permitted — but ab initio forbidden in every case, it appears to me. Nevertheless one should wonder at the Rambam and mechaber: from what he wrote regarding bowls, cups, and pots — different from a new oven baked in its heat where the bread is permitted — behold when baking in it too there is no permitted cause at all; and one can distinguish between ab initio and after the fact, or that bowls etc. whose main making is for the sake of fire — and this is what they wrote “for a forbidden-for-benefit thing made them new”; but all this is forced; therefore it appears the oven is better, since after scraping the forbidden wood away, since no ash or heat of forbidden wood remains but only the oven itself is forbidden and we rule zeh v'zeh gorem permitted — which is not the case for bowls etc. — investigate.
