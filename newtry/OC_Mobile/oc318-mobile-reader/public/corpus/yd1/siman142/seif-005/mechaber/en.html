Bowls, cups, pots, and dishes that a potter fired with wood of elilim — these are forbidden in benefit, for something forbidden in benefit made them new. (The se’ifim 5, 6, 7 are the language of Rambam, perek 17 of Hilkhot Ma’akhalot Asurot, except that the Mechaber wrote “wood of elilim” instead of “peels of orlah”.)
