Forms made only for decoration and not worship, however, are permitted, as Tosafot and the Rosh explain. Shach.
Intends. Shach writes in the name of the authorities that even if another route is available, it is permitted where he does not intend benefit, provided he could block his ears, close his eyes, or stop his nostrils so as not inevitably to benefit from the sound, sight, or smell. If benefit is inevitable, it is forbidden. If he intends benefit, it is forbidden even when no alternative route exists.
