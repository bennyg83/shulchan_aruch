This matter of beauty means the avodah zarah objects themselves that were worshipped: it is forbidden to gaze at them in order to see their beauty. But forms made for decoration and not for worship are explained by Tosafot and the Rosh to be permitted. Thus the Shach.
Intends. The Shach writes in the name of the poskim that even if it is possible for him to go elsewhere, it is permitted when he does not intend to benefit. The case is where he is able to stop his ears, close his eyes, and block his nostrils so that he does not benefit from the sound, sight, or smell; then it is permitted when he does not intend to benefit from them, since it is not a psik reisha. Otherwise, it is forbidden. But when he intends to benefit, even if it is possible for him to go elsewhere, it is forbidden. Thus his language.
