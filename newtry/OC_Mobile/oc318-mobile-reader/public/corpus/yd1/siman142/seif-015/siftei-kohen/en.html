Forbidden to hear etc. And likewise to smell the smell of idols, as proven in chapter "every time" (Pesachim 25b) — see there; and see what I wrote at end of siman 108.
Of the ornament of idols. Meaning: in idols themselves that were worshipped it is forbidden to gaze at them to see their beauty; but forms made for ornament and not for worship — it is explained in Tosafot and the Rosh that it is permitted.
However, an unintended matter is permitted. This is an explicit Gemara in Perek Kol Shaah, Pesachim 25b, and Rif, Rosh, and Ran ruled so there, as did Rambam in chapter 12 of Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot law 12. I do not know why the Rav wrote it in the name of Hagahot Riaz. Perhaps one may say that the Gemara could be explained regarding other prohibitions and not avodah zarah, so the Rav wrote it in the name of Riaz; but the plain matter there implies even avodah zarah, and Rashi explicitly explained so there; see there. So too wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham, netiv 17 part 5. It is explained there in the Gemara and poskim that even if he can go to another place, it is permitted when he does not intend. It is explained in Tosafot, Rosh, and Rabbeinu Yerucham there that it speaks in a case where he can stop up his ears, close his eyes, and block his nostrils so as not to benefit from the sound, sight, and smell; and it is permitted when he does not intend to benefit from them, for it is not a psik reisha. Otherwise it is forbidden. But when he intends, it is explained there in the Gemara and poskim that even if he cannot go to another place, it is forbidden.
