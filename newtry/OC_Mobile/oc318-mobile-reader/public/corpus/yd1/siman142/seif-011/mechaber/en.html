It is permitted to plant vegetables under an asheirah, whether in the summer, when they need shade, or in the rainy season, because the shade of the asheirah — which is forbidden — together with the ground — which is not forbidden — causes these vegetables to grow; and any time something forbidden and something permitted together cause it, it is permitted in every place. Therefore, a field that was fertilized with fertilizer of elilim is permitted to sow, and a cow that was fattened with vetch of elilim may be eaten; and likewise anything similar.
