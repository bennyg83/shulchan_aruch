Bread that cooked on pig coals etc. The Rav goes per his reasoning: as we said regarding orlah, so here regarding idolatry; and therefore he challenges from what we say at beginning of this siman idol coals forbidden; and he answered there it deals only ab initio; and we already proved at beginning of siman it is not so but complete prohibition in idol coals even after the fact and even dim because "nothing shall cleave"; and here regarding idolatry likewise forbidden per Ramah and Rosh's ruling regarding idolatry even in zeh v'zeh gorems bread and beam forbidden; likewise certainly forbidden cooking on idol coals; so appears per my humble opinion.
