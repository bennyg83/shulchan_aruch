Bread cooked over coals, etc. The Rav follows his own reasoning. Just as this is stated regarding orlah, as explained above, he holds that the same applies to idol worship. He therefore asks from the ruling at the beginning of this siman that a coal of idol worship is forbidden, and answers that there the prohibition applies only l'chatchila. We have already proven at the beginning of the siman that this is not so. Rather, there is a complete prohibition concerning a coal of idol worship, even after the fact and even when the coals are dim, because of the verse, “Let nothing of the banned property cling to your hand.” Here too, regarding idol worship, it is forbidden according to the ruling of the Ramah and the Rosh that in cases of idol worship even zeh v'zeh gorem involving bread and a shuttle is forbidden. Certainly, then, cooking over coals of idol worship is forbidden. So it appears in my humble opinion.
