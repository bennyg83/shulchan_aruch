Damages. This is not considered benefit, because while alive the animal was useful for breeding, labor, and food. Rashi.
New. The case is where he cut wood for idolatry with the knife. Otherwise the knife is permitted, because accessories of idolatry are not forbidden until actually used, as stated at the beginning of siman 139. Shach.
Forbidden. Shach understands that the entire animal is forbidden and sending the value of the knife's benefit to the Dead Sea does not help. The benefit from slaughter is substantial, because the animal might otherwise have died. Cutting meat with the knife gives less benefit, so discarding that benefit suffices. The case involves destructive cutting, such as pieces intended as a gift.
