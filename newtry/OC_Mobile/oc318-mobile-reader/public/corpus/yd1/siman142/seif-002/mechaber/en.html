If one slaughtered with a knife of elilim, it is permitted, because he ruins it; (and if the knife is new or it is old and he whitened it from the forbidden matter in it, even l'chatchila it is permitted) (above siman 10). But if the animal was mesukenes, it is forbidden, because he improves it; and this improvement is benefit from the appurtenances of elilim. And likewise, it is forbidden to cut meat with it, because he improves it; but if he cut in a way of loss and ruin, it is permitted.
