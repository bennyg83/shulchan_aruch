He slaughtered with it etc. Deals with an old knife not kashered from its prohibition; therefore although from appurtenances of idolatry one may slaughter ab initio since he is damaging it, nevertheless from [being] absorbed-from-idolaters it is forbidden to slaughter ab initio, as above seif 10; and as the Rav concludes.
Because he damages. This is not benefit — in its lifetime it is designated for three things: breeding and birth, plowing, and eating — end Rashi.
And if the knife is new. Such as where he cut a piece of wood for an idol — for otherwise appurtenances of idolatry are not forbidden until they are used, as explained at beginning of siman 139; and so it is in Chullin 8b.
A way of loss and destruction. Such as when the pieces are destined as gifts and similar.
