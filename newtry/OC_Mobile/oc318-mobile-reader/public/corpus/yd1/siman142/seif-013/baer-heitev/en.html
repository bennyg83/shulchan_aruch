Ornament. And although ornament of idols is not forbidden until used, as above siman 139 — this standing before idols is their use. Shach. And Turei Zahav wrote: it appears even to sit in their shade forbidden; and although Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit in the Rashba's name wrote permitted to sit in their shade — for it is only an offering — we do not rule so, for the Tur concludes it is because of ornament — end.
