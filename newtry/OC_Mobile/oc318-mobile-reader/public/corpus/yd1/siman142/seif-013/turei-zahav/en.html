It is no less than an ornament. Accordingly, it appears that even sitting in its shade is forbidden. Although the Beit Yosef writes in Bedek HaBayis in the name of the Rashba that it is permitted to sit in their shade because they are merely offerings, we do not rule accordingly. The Tur cites that view as “there are those who say” that they are offerings and are permitted because they are not comparable to offerings brought within the Temple. He then concludes that they are forbidden because they serve as ornamentation. Therefore, no proof can be brought from the Rashba concerning this matter.
