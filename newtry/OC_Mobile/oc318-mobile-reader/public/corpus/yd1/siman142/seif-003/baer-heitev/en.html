The garment. Sending only the benefit of the shuttle to the Dead Sea does not suffice; the value of the whole garment must be discarded. This implies that before mixture, discarding the value of benefit does not help, and the same applies to bread in seif 4. Levush's contrary ruling concerning bread baked with chametz coals is incorrect. Shach nevertheless proves from the Yerushalmi and other authorities that one may sell the garment or bread to a non-Jew excluding the value of the prohibition, provided the non-Jew cuts it before the Jew so it cannot be resold to another Jew. Discarding the benefit does not help before mixture because idolatry has no redemption. Sale excluding its value is permitted because the Jew derives no benefit from the idolatry and no longer possesses the forbidden object.
