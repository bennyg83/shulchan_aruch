The garment. And leading the benefit of the beam to the Dead Sea does not help — only the value of the entire garment; and see siman 110 and siman 140; and it appears that whatever was not mixed, leading benefit to the Dead Sea does not help; and so regarding bread in s.k. 14; and not like the Levush in Orach Chaim siman 445 seif 2 who wrote regarding bread baked on coals of chametz that one leads the benefit of the prohibition to the Dead Sea and it is permitted for benefit — end — does not exist. However it appears to me proven from the Jerusalem Talmud and several decisors that one can sell the garment and bread to a gentile except the value of the prohibition in it when the gentile will cut the garment and bread before the Israelite in a manner impossible to sell again to another Israelite — like regarding stam yeinam above siman 134; and that which leading benefit to the Dead Sea is forbidden is because all the time it was not mixed and there is idol prohibition in the garment or bread — no redemption for an idol; but when sold to a gentile except the value of prohibition in it — he has no benefit at all from the idol and the prohibition itself is not in his hand — permitted. And see end of siman 23 and siman 57 s.k. 21, siman 84 seif 5, end of siman 86, and siman 134 seif 11 — end of Shach's language.
