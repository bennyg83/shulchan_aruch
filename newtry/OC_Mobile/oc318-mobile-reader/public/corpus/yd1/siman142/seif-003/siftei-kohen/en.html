The garment is forbidden for benefit — if mixed etc. It appears whatever was not mixed, leading benefit to the Dead Sea does not help; and so regarding bread in seif 4; and so explained in Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh; and not like the Levush in Orach Chaim siman 445 seif 2 who wrote regarding bread baked on chametz coals that one leads benefit of prohibition to the Dead Sea and permitted for benefit — end — does not exist. However it appears proven from the Jerusalem Talmud and several decisors one can sell garment and bread to a gentile except prohibition value when the gentile cuts garment and bread before the Israelite in manner impossible to sell again to another Israelite like yayin nesech above siman 134 — sell to gentile except yayin nesech value; and that leading benefit to the Dead Sea is forbidden is because all time not mixed and there is idol prohibition in garment or bread — no redemption for idol; but when sold to gentile except prohibition value — no benefit from idol and prohibition not in his hand — permitted. And we hold above siman 134 s.k. 11 that certainly yayin nesech mixed in wine does not help selling to gentile except yayin nesech value even though certainly Israel will not again buy wine from gentile — there different for actual prohibition; but bread and garment actual prohibition not recognizable; and although we say "there is wood benefit in bread" — meaning to say it is more stringent than this-and-that-causes, for prohibition benefit is recognizable; but not that it should be actual mixture; rather forbidden to sell garment and bread whole in manner he can sell afterwards to Israel — as explained in Orach Chaim siman 467 regarding grain sunk in river forbidden to sell to gentile before Passover much together from benefit; and see end of siman 23, siman 57 s.k. 21, siman 84 seif 5, end of siman 86, siman 112 seif 11, and siman 134 seif 11.
