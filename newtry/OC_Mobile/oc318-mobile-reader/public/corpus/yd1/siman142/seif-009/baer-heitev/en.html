Worshipped. Meaning it was planted initially to be worshipped, as explained in siman 145. Shach.
In its shade. The Gemara states that even the shade of its shade is forbidden. Rashi explains that until the length of the shadow exceeds the tree's height, the shadow is dense and dark and called its direct shade. Beyond that point it is thin and faint and called the shade of its shade.
Run. The Gemara and Rabbeinu Yerucham imply that if he has no alternative route, he need not run. Shach. The Gemara also implies that permission without an alternative applies specifically where the asherah has seized the public road.
