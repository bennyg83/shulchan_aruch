Whether it was worshipped. Meaning: that it was planted from the outset to be worshipped — but if not so, no; and as will be explained in siman 145 — see there.
Whether an idol was placed beneath it. Meaning: that it is currently placed there — therefore forbidden to sit in its shade; but if it was removed from beneath it, permitted for benefit; and as will be explained in the aforesaid siman seif 6; therefore permitted to sit in its shade.
Forbidden to sit in its shade. Beit Yosef: and it is included in “he shall not sit in its shade” — even one whose shade extends very far is still called its shade — end.
And if he has a way, etc. See the Gemara, which implies that specifically when it steals from him it is permitted if he has no other way. So too it implies in Rabbeinu Yerucham, netiv 17 part 5. It also implies in the Gemara and in Rabbeinu Yerucham there that where he has no other way, he does not need to run. See above siman 149 regarding the law of a city or courtyard that contains avodah zarah, whether one may pass there.
