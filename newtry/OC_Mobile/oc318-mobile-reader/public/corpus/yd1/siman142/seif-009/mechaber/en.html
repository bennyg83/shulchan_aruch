An asheirah — whether it was worshipped, or whether elilim were placed beneath it — it is forbidden to sit in its shade (the language of Rambam). And if he has another route, short like it, it is forbidden to pass beneath it; but if not, he passes beneath it while running. (And this is specifically to pass beneath it, but to pass through its shade, in every case it is permitted) (Ran, perek Kol HaTzelamim).
