Forbidden to sit in its shade. This is Rambam's language; and in Gemara we say even in shade of shade forbidden; and Rashi: all time shadow length not exceeding tree height — shade thick and dark called its shade; from here onward shade thin and weak is shade of shade; and so Tur; and the Ran and Kesef Mishneh wondered on Rambam for not forbidding shade of shade; and for halacha certainly forbidden for it is set Talmud.
But to pass in its shade etc. For even sitting has no prohibition by law since he does not intend — only perhaps he will tarry sitting and come to intend; therefore passing in general permitted; but beneath it forbidden because of impurity of idols.
