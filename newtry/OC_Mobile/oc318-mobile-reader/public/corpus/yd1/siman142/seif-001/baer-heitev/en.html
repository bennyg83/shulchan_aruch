Burned [serefah]. This refers to an Israelite; but a non-Jew who burned it has annulled it. Taz.
From its flame. Taz explains that this refers to flame alone. If the flame remains attached to a coal, heating oneself from it is certainly forbidden, even more than from the coal itself. Flame is permitted only because it has no substance. Even dim, non-glowing coals are forbidden because they have substance, and there is no distinction between great or slight benefit, for the verse says, 'Nothing of the banned property shall cling to your hand.' Taz therefore rules that even after the fact benefit from the coals is forbidden. Shach agrees.
