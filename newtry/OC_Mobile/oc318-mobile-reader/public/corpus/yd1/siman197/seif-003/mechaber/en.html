She is forbidden to immerse in the day of the 7th. And even if she delays immersing until day 8 or 9, she cannot immerse in the day, because of the “trace of her daughter” (meaning: the daughter’s attachment and closeness, to do like her mother’s practice — that she will immerse in the day like her — and she will not discern that her mother immersed after seven and not on the seventh itself). {Rama: And kallos who immerse before the chuppah may immerse in the day, for they do not come to the chassan until the night; but after the chuppah their law is like other women (Mahari”l).}
