She is forbidden to immerse during the day, etc. Explanation: By Torah law, a niddah who immerses on her seventh day, even though those days were not clean, immerses specifically at night, for her status depends specifically upon the completion of days. A zavah, however, who counts 7 clean days, is subject to the principle that part of a day is considered like the entire day. Once she has counted part of the seventh day in cleanliness, she may immerse even during the day. We derive this from the verse, "and afterward she shall be pure," meaning that after the act of counting she may purify herself. The Sages, however, forbade immersion during the day lest she have relations while it is still day and then see blood later that same day, retroactively invalidating her count. It would then emerge that he had relations with her while forbidden. Therefore, the Tur wrote that our women, although they are possible zavos, meaning that they count 7 clean days, and consequently there are always at least 8 days including the day of the sighting, such that she should be permitted to immerse during the day because the 7 days have already ended, nevertheless she may not immerse during the day. This explains the words of the Shulchan Aruch here. The reason is because of her daughter's possible error, meaning that her daughter may see that her mother immersed on the eighth day and mistakenly think that she immersed on the seventh day, and she too will do so.
