That she is forbidden to immerse. See in responsum Chut HaShani siman 70, who wrote that bediavad, if she immersed — if unintentionally, she is permitted immediately; and if intentionally she immersed on Friday night, one should be stringent to forbid her to her husband on Shabbat; and in what he wrote "she is permitted" — and if it is Yom Tov, it appears halachically that she is permitted immediately; but not lemaaseh, for it is fitting to be stringent with one who acted intentionally, because of making a fence around the matter. See there. And it appears to me that in any case she is permitted in all kinds of closeness and affection [now I saw in Minchat Shlomo and S.T. that their view is to be lenient on Yom Tov even lechatchila, even in places where they practiced to be stringent on Shabbat; and regarding Shabbat, Minchat Shlomo's view is like the aforementioned Chut HaShani — that if she immersed intentionally she is forbidden for that Shabbat; and S.T. disagrees, etc.; and per my humble view it appears to be lenient bediavad, even on that same Shabbat, etc. See there. And so it is somewhat implied from a responsum of Chakham Tzvi that I brought above s.k. 1, in what he wrote "she did a prohibition," etc. See there.] And see further in responsum Chut HaShani siman 82.
