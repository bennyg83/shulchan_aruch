And so if she transgressed and immersed on the seventh day, etc. Bach ruled to be stringent like Maharil and Hagahot Maimoniyot and Hagahot Sha"D in name of Maharam — that on the seventh day the immersion did not take effect and she needs a second immersion at night; and it is good to be stringent where possible, since so is also the view of Raavad and Hagahot Maimoniyot and R. Avraham in name of She'iltot.
