If she transgressed and immersed on the eighth day in the day without an oness, even so the immersion counted for her. And likewise, if she transgressed and immersed on the seventh day in the day, the immersion counted for her. {Rama: Nevertheless, she should not have tashmish even on the eighth until the night, and she should conceal her immersion from her husband until the night (Beit Yosef in the name of Agur).}
