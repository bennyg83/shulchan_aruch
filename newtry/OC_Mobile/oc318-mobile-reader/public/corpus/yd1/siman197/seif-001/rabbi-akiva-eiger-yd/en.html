(Siman 197 s.k. 3 in the hagahah) They may immerse by day. Where they practice to immerse a kalah on day seven, she should not immerse until after sunrise; and bediavad, from dawn it is effective, as stated in the mishnah at the beginning of chapter 2 of Megillah. And in Tur it is written: but a zavah from the Torah immerses on day seven after sunrise. This wording is somewhat difficult, for from the Torah, after dawn she is permitted to immerse.
