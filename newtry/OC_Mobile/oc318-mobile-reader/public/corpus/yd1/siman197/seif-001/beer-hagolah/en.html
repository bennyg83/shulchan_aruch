The wording of Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilchot Issurei Biah, from the baraita in Shabbat 64b in the name of Rabbi Akiva. Rav Hai Gaon explains that Torah law derives immersion for a niddah by an a fortiori inference from one who touches her, as it states, "One who touches her bedding shall wash in water." Rabbeinu Tam explains that it is derived from the vessels of Midian, where it states, "Only through the water of niddah shall it be purified," which the Gemara interprets as water in which a niddah immerses. Tosafot, Yevamot 47b, and the Rosh at the end of Niddah cite these explanations.
