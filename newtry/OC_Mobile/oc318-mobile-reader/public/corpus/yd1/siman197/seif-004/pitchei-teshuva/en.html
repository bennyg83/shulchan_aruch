After childbirth. Ba'er Heitev wrote: specifically, etc., until that is, after thirty-three for a male and sixty-six for a female. And see in Dagul MeRevavah, who disagrees on this, that even within the days of tohar, if she saw and could not immerse only because of the stringency that the poskim were stringent — it is called her proper time, and she is permitted to immerse on Friday night. See there. And so in Sefer Levushi Serad siman 86. And he wrote further that if she already immersed once within the days of tohar after childbirth, and afterward saw within the days of tohar and her time of immersion arrived on Friday night — in this too Shach agrees that she is permitted to immerse; for either way, if we hold this blood to be tamei, it is an immersion of mitzvah; and if not, there is no prohibition of immersion at all. See there. And know that in all that I wrote above, siman 196 s.k. 13, that she can count seven clean days immediately — but she was stringent on herself and did not begin counting until after five days; and through this her night of immersion fell on Friday night — she is forbidden to immerse, and this is simple.
