After childbirth. Dagul MeRevavah permits Friday-night immersion even during postpartum purity days where she could not immerse earlier only because of later halakhic stringencies. Levushei Serad adds that if she already immersed once after childbirth and later saw again during the purity days, everyone should permit. But where she voluntarily delayed counting because of a personal stringency, Friday-night immersion remains forbidden.
