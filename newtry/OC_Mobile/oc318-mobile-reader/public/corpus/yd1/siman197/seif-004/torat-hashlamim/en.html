Where there is a compelling circumstance. See Be'er Sheva, page 116, who writes that women whose time for immersion falls on Friday night sometimes immerse while it is still fully daytime because they do not wish to desecrate Shabbos. They do not act properly. Tzemach Tzedek likewise writes in responsum 66 that even where they are embarrassed, for example, because other residents besides the members of their household live with them, and they therefore hurry to immerse while it is still fully daytime so that they can return home before the meal without others noticing, they do not act properly. One should protest and rebuke them. See there. See also Responsa Maharshal, siman 6, and Rama, siman 21.
