Beit Yosef, per the view of Rashi that the Tur brought — that tevilah at its proper time is not a mitzvah except when her husband is in the city, as Tosafot wrote in Niddah page 36 side a and Yoma page 8 side a. And from Yehoshua we find that he was punished for preventing Israel from being fruitful and multiplying for one night. And he wrote further: and if she delays immersing in order to distress her husband, it is a sin in her hands. And in the name of Sefer HaZohar — a woman who does so causes many evils in the world.
