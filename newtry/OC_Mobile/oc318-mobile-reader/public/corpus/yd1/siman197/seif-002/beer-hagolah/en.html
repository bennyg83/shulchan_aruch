Beit Yosef, following Rabbeinu Tam's view cited by the Tur, that immersion at its proper time is not itself a mitzvah except when her husband is in the city. Tosafot writes likewise in Niddah 36a and Yoma 8a. Yehoshua was punished for causing Israel to neglect procreation for one night. Beit Yosef further writes that if she delays immersion in order to distress her husband, she commits a transgression. In the name of the Zohar, he writes that a woman who does so causes many evils in the world. See there.
