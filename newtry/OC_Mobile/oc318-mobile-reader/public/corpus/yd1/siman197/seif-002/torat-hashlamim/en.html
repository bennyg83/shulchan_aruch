Now, certainly what he brings there as proof from a responsum of Rashba siman 485, brought in Beit Yosef in Tur Orach Chayyim siman 503 — that on Yom Tov we do not forbid at all if one transgressed and cooked — is no proof at all, for the words of Rashba are not agreed to by the majority of poskim, as is explained and proven from the words of the Rosh and other poskim regarding this inquiry of one who transgressed and baked, brought in Beit Yosef siman 527; and similarly Rambam in chapter 23 of Hilchot Shabbat, that one who raises terumot and maasrot on Yom Tov intentionally should not eat until Motzaei Yom Tov; and so regarding ola on Shabbat in siman 503.
