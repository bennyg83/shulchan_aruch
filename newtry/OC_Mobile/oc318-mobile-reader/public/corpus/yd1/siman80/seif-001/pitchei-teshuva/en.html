And it is certainly a pure chayah — Avodat HaGoren s.k. 2 in the name of Shach; and see in responsum Beit Yaakov siman 41 where he wrote that for these signs — split, twisted, etc. — we also require a sign of purity even though it chews its cud and splits its hoof; and only in beheimah does chewing cud help, etc., unlike chayah; and he wrote he would bring clear proofs later and none were found, only that thus is implied by Tur and Shulchan Aruch who wrote "it is certainly a pure chayah" — see there; and in my humble opinion this is not correct; on the contrary from the Mechaber's conclusion "and if one of these three signs is missing its milk is forbidden" it is clear that at least it is pure and its flesh permitted; and so above siman 79 seif 1 signs of beheimah and chayah were explained in the Torah, etc., implying chewing cud and split hoof also help chayah for purity sign — and this is obvious; and see there further whether we require these signs in both horns or one horn suffices.
