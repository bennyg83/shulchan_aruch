(Lekut) Regarding its horns — and not in hooves, unlike Perishah there in Tosafot s.v. these, etc., but as Rabbenu Tam explained there — why did the Gemara not explain signs of hooves; Tosafot HaRosh and Shach (end).
(Lekut) Twisted, etc. — and there are in them explanations of Rashi and Tosafot and Shach and other versions — see there (end).
(Lekut) If, etc. and if, etc. — twisted, etc. — Rashi explained in a second language that twisted means round and pointed that they are sharp, but in the first language he explained that round ones are round and twisted means made in layers upon layers, and so Rashba and Ran and Tur; and grooved — Rashi explained full of notches and grooves, and so Tur; but Rashba and Ran explained pressed that the layers are pressed and do not peel; and Rambam in his commentary explained in one place, and split — Rashi explained that many splits emerge from them, and so Rambam in his commentary; but Rabbenu Tam explained in Tosafot there s.v. and behold as split in two, meaning bent; and so Rashba (end).
And that their grooves interlock, etc. — Rambam and explains what he wrote, and this is a doubt, etc., unlike Tosafot and Shach who do not interlock — for in truth they interlock only they are not interlocked like other horns, and it is doubtful to them whether this interlocking suffices:
(Lekut) and this, etc. — but Tosafot and Tosafot HaRosh and Shach wrote that we do not require interlocking of their grooves (end).
