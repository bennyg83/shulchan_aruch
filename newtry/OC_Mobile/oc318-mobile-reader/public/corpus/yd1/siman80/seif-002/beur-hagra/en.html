In what matters, etc. — explained in the Torah: (Lekut) in what matters, etc. — as written in chapter 1 these goats of Beit Baltin what is it, etc., since we translate torabla, etc. (end).
Seven species of chayah — even though there in chapter 1 we say that to'o is wild ox and is a species of beheimah, as written in siman 63 according to Rabbanan — he relies on Yerushalmi (chapter 5 of Kilaayim) who said that one who translates torabla follows R' Yosi, and so in Tosefta (chapter 1 of Kilaayim) five things were said about wild ox: forbidden due to meat and milk, and its milk forbidden, etc.; R' Yehuda ben R' Ilai: this is the to'o mentioned in the Torah and permitted regarding meat and milk, and its milk permitted, etc.; and so the sugya there, unlike Rav Huna bar Chiya there who said perhaps a species of to'o, etc.; and Tosafot there s.v. and perhaps, etc. — and it is strained that it should enter the dispute as a lone view; it should have said another chayah; and with this what they challenge on him is resolved — why did he rule like Rabbanan when the sugya is like R' Yosi.
