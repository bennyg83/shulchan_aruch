Wild ox is a species of beheimah — it implies that even for covering blood it is not required; and the Rav wrote in siman 28 seif 4: and there are those who are uncertain regarding buffalo that it is wild ox, perhaps it is chayah — therefore it is good to cover it without a blessing, end of his words; and one must say that what he did not emend here is because he relied on what is above, for the main place of the laws of covering is there.
