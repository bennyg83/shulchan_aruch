Wild ox is a species of beheimah and re'em is a species of chayah — behold Tosafot Zevachim daf 113b s.v. orzila, etc., wrote that buffalo we call re'em does not exist and its milk is forbidden, for re'em is certainly a chayah species and its milk permitted; but buffalo in our place are small so one cannot say they are from re'em; if so what is the difficulty — how did it stand, for there is no difficulty — lions of the upper story how did they stand, for there were small lions that could insert, etc.; and I heard one challenge: what proof — the Gemara answers this difficulty how the lion stood; R' Yannai inserted its cub and the Gemara challenged but Rabbah bar Chana said he saw, etc.; thus from the lion of the upper story one cannot challenge that perhaps it inserted its cub; and further one cannot challenge that a lion's cub does not see — but from the lion one can challenge well, for there is no cub — for Rabbah bar Chana saw the lioness one day old, etc.; and the conclusion is that if we say a lion has large and small in its species and likewise the lioness has small and large and small ones do not become large — otherwise still how did it stand, for small ones were inserted and later became large — we must say one does not become large from small; and with this one must say it is called one species, otherwise still impossible to permit buffalo milk since re'em is chayah — they are two species; and so the conclusion is one species; nevertheless small ones do not become large; thus Noach necessarily inserted male and female cubs from large ones and male and female from small ones, for there are large and small in the world from what Noach inserted and they bore fruit; therefore he inserted four; but a lion is a tame species and the Torah said from an impure species bring only two by two; thus impossible; unlike re'em one can well say there are small and large all one species and small does not become large; nevertheless he inserted male and female from this; and not from small ones; and the four is because buffalo is a pure species and in pure ones he brought seven; and Tosafot's words are correct; and in truth Tosafot did not need to challenge how the lion of the upper story stood — only briefly: he needed to bring four in one tame species since necessarily small ones do not become large except that it is preferable to challenge how it stood; and according to what I wrote it is clear the Tosefta's challenge why not challenge from the lion of the upper story, or they challenged how the re'em stood; and if they challenged from the lion, why not challenge from re'em; and according to what I wrote it is clear the Gemara's challenge on re'em can answer he inserted its cub; and must say the Gemara's challenge "I saw," etc., unlike if challenging from the lion one could not answer he inserted its cub as I wrote; and this is clear; investigate.
