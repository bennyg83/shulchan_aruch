By majority. Rashi wrote at the end of Temurah: If one asks from Niddah, where they said that a garment into which kilayim was woven may not be sold to a non-Jew, why should that thread not be nullified in the majority, this is not a difficulty. With kilayim we do not say it is nullified in the majority, for the entire prohibition comes through mixture, and nullification by majority is not relevant to it. End quote. Nor should one ask from what the Tur wrote in siman 303 regarding rabbinic kilayim, that one should remove one thread; why is it not nullified in the majority, since it is rabbinic? One can say that regarding nullification by majority the rabbis treated it like Torah kilayim: anything extant prohibits, as by Torah law, for what the rabbis instituted they instituted in the pattern of Torah law. But when one removes one thread, so that perhaps the prohibited one is no longer in existence, they were lenient. End quote from Taz.
