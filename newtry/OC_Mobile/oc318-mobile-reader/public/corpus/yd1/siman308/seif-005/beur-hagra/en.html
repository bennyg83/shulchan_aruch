Or it should be buried. The Tur ruled that it should be buried. See Tosafot 32b at the end of s.v. “pasak,” etc.: if you ask, how is this different, etc.; they answer that there is, etc., and there is, etc. The Tur holds like the answer that establishes it according to Rabbi Yehudah, but we rule like the sages; therefore it specifically should be buried. The Rambam holds like the first answer here, etc., and therefore ruled that it should be burned. So too the Rash in chapter 3 of Orlah adopts that answer. But the Rav's words are astonishing, for he wrote “or,” while we do not rule like Rabbi Yehudah, who is an individual view. Perhaps his reason is that the sages hold like him in Pesachim chapter 2, 21a: the sages say, he may even crumble, etc. See what I wrote in Orach Chayim at the beginning of siman 445.
And some say specifically, etc. Temurah there. But the Rambam rejected it from halakhah because of the sugya in Bekhorot 25b: here regarding the fleece, etc., and with fleeces it is impossible in the morning. So too wrote the Rash in the last chapter of Orlah, mishnah 3 there. But he wrote there that even in that case in Bekhorot “any amount” is not precise, rather a full sit. The Rambam and Shulchan Aruch in seif 4 wrote according to its plain meaning; see Raavad, Hilkhot Bekhorot chapter 3.
