It is permitted to pluck. See Magen Avraham 438:23 and Shaar HaMelech, chapter 3 of the Laws of Yom Tov, halachah 3. He questions the Tur and Mechaber, for Chullin 137b indicates that plucking is not shearing only regarding sheep's wool and the like. With goats' hair, whose normal removal is by plucking, plucking is equivalent to shearing. Why, then, did they state without qualification that one who slaughters a firstborn may pluck its hair, without distinguishing an ox from a goat? A firstborn goat should be forbidden because its normal method is plucking. He leaves the matter requiring study.
