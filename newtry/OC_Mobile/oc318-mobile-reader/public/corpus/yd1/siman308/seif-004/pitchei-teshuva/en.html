And it sanctifies with any amount. See Noda BiYehudah Tinyana, Yoreh De'ah siman 193, who wonders at the Mechaber, whose words imply even less than the full sit measure; see the Shach above, siman 110 s.k. 2, and examine carefully. Yet regarding weaving it into a garment in the next seif, the measure required is a full sit. The Rama too changed his wording: regarding weaving into a garment he brings the dissenting view requiring a significant amount, which implies that here no one disagrees. In truth this is not so. See there, where he discusses the matter at length and brings the Rash's view distinguishing between an unblemished bekhor, which forbids in any amount, and a blemished one, which requires a full sit. If one sheared it by hand, according to the Raavad's second answer it forbids in any amount, while according to the first answer even there it requires a full sit; see there.
