And it sanctifies with any amount. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Yoreh Deah, siman 193, who questions the Mechaber because his words imply that even less than the width of the sit measure is prohibited. Yet in the next seif, where the wool is woven into a garment, he requires the width of a sit, and the Rama there cites the dissenting opinion that only an important amount is prohibited, implying that here there is no dissent. In truth this is not so. He discusses the matter at length and cites the Rash, who distinguishes as follows. Wool from an unblemished firstborn prohibits in any quantity, while wool from a blemished firstborn requires a sit. If it was sheared by hand, according to the Raavad's second answer it prohibits in any amount; according to his first answer, it too requires a sit. See there.
