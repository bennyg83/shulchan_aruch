It is permitted to pluck. See Magen Avraham, siman 438, seif katan 23, and Shaar HaMelech, chapter 3 of Hilchot Yom Tov, halachah 3, regarding his words. He further challenges the Tur and Mechaber here. In Chullin 137b it is explicit that the principle "plucking is not shearing" applies specifically to sheep wool and similar material. But with goat hair and the like, where plucking is the normal manner, plucking is equivalent to shearing. Why then did they state without qualification that one who slaughters a firstborn may pluck, without distinguishing between a firstborn ox and a firstborn goat? It would appear that plucking a firstborn goat's hair is forbidden because its normal manner of removal is by plucking. He leaves the matter requiring study.
