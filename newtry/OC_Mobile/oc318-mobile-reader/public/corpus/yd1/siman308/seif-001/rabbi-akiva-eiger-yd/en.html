(Siman 308, seif 1) It is forbidden in shearing and labor. If a firstborn fetus extended its head and withdrew it, it is considered born and labor with the mother is forbidden because of the sanctified fetus. See Beit Yaakov 7 and Shevut Yaakov, part 1, responsum 73. A firstborn, whether unblemished or blemished, may not be used for breeding, since this is labor. See Moed Katan 12a and Rambam, Laws of Meilah 1:9. A blemished firstborn also presents a kilayim prohibition because Scripture compares it to a gazelle and hart. See Makkot 22 and Mishneh LaMelech.
