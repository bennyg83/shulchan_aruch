(Siman 308, Shulchan Aruch seif 1) See Eshel Avraham, who copied Responsa Beit Yaakov regarding an animal having its first birth, where the fetus extended its head and withdrew it, permitting labor to be done with its mother. The sugya in Temurah 11 and the Rambam, Hilkhot Meilah, escaped him, for they prove that its mother is forbidden for work because it weakens the fetus. Later I found that Sefer Shevut Yaakov already challenged him.
