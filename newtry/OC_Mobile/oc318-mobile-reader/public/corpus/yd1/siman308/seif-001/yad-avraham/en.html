(Siman 308, seif 1) See Eshel Avraham, which cites Responsa Beit Yaakov concerning a first-bearing animal whose fetus extended its head and then retracted it, permitting labor with the mother. He overlooked the sugya in Temurah 11a and Rambam, Hilchot Me'ilah, from which it is clear that the mother is prohibited in labor because such work weakens the fetus. I later found that Shevut Yaakov had already raised this objection.
