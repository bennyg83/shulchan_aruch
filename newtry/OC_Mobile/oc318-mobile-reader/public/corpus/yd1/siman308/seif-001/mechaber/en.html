The prohibition of working a firstborn and the prohibition of its shearing. In it are 5 seifim.
The firstborn is forbidden in shearing and work; and even if he transgressed and sheared it, or plucked by hand, or even if the wool fell off by itself—it is forbidden in benefit forever. Whether it died by itself, or whether they slaughtered it afterward—even by an expert—slaughter does not permit wool that was plucked while alive; but attached wool, slaughter permits it like its flesh.
