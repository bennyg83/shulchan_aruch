After seven days. Lachmei Todah 30 rules that the limitation to after seven days applies only when changing clothes for pleasure. If the change is necessary because the garment is dirty or causes discomfort, it is permitted even on a weekday during the seven days, by analogy to washing, which is forbidden only for pleasure. Its omission from earlier authorities reflects its obviousness. See siman 381, seif katan 2. [Citation 25.]
