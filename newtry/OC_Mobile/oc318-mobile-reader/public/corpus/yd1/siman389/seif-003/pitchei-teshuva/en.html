It is forbidden to wear. Panim Meirot, part 2, responsum 123, permits a woman within thirty days of mourning who has given birth and reaches the customary day of returning to synagogue, which is like her personal festival, to wear Shabbat clothing on that Shabbat, but not festival finery or gold jewelry, lest she become lighthearted and forget her mourning. She need not change her usual seat. See siman 353:4. Shevut Yaakov, part 2, responsum 102, permits a woman within mourning who attends her son's pidyon haben to wear at least Shabbat clothing during the ceremony, since several authorities regard it as a festive occasion.
