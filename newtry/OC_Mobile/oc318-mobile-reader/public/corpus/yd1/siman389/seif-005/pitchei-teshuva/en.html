That the festival arrive after thirty, and his fellows rebuke him. This implies that we require three things: after thirty, and the festival arrived, and the rebuke. However, in Teshuvah MeAhava volume 1 siman 124 letter 20 he wrote that the Shulchan Aruch did not mean specifically [all three]. It is not to say that we require these three, but rather: the festival does not cancel like the rebuke; also thirty alone does not suffice, rather we specifically require rebuke, and after thirty; but certainly rebuke after thirty suffices and we do not require a festival. And why the Tur used the language "festival" is as they stated it in the present reality, as the Rosh wrote in Piskei HaRosh, Zoltsbach edition, siman 53, because on the festival it is customary to wear ironed garments, etc.; see there.
