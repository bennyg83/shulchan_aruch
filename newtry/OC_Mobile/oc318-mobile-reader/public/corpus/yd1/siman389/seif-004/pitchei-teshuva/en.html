That a festival arrive after thirty days and his friends rebuke him. This implies that three things are required: after thirty days, the arrival of a festival, and rebuke. However, Teshuvah MeAhavah I siman 124, note 20, wrote that the Shulchan Aruch did not mean this precisely. It does not mean that all three are required; rather, the festival does not cancel like rebuke does, and thirty days alone does not help, but rebuke specifically is required after thirty days. Certainly rebuke after thirty days helps and a festival is not required. The reason the Tur and Shulchan Aruch mention a festival is that they speak in the usual case, as the Rosh wrote in Moed Katan, Sulzbach edition siman 53, because on a festival it is customary to wear pressed garments, etc.; see there.
