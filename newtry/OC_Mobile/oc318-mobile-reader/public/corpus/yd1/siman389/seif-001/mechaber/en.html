A mourner is forbidden to launder his garments and the law of pressing. And it contains 8 seifim. A mourner is forbidden to launder his garments all seven days, even in water alone; and after seven permitted; and just as forbidden to launder, so forbidden to wear what was laundered before:
{Rama: (and after seven permitted; and the world practiced prohibition (as Rivash and his supporters); and the custom is another person wears them first and afterward the mourner wears them — custom is primary (Tur in name of Semak and so Mordechai and Kol Bo); and so in these lands after seven; and if another wore them only one hour, that suffices) (Kol Bo and Semak);} and needless to say forbidden to wear new garments; and also sheets and bed linens forbidden to launder and spread laundered ones; and likewise hand towels, even though permitted to launder on chol haMoed; and likewise all those we said permitted to launder on chol haMoed — one leaving captivity, prison, excommunicated person released, one who asked a sage and was permitted, one coming from overseas who went to profit and had no time to shave — forbidden on mourning days; if one of these occurred before mourning and he entered mourning immediately — forbidden to launder; unless one occurred and two mournings pressed one after another — then permitted to launder even with lye and potash even within seven, provided done in private in his house; and after two mournings pressed one after another he launders in water but not lye and potash.
