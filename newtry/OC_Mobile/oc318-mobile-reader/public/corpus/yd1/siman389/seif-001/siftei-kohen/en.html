And after seven it is permitted. This is where it is not pressed, or for us, where our pressing is like their laundering, as below at the end of the siman.
And the world practiced it as forbidden. That is, they are concerned for the view of Riva and his supporters, who forbade laundering throughout thirty days. It is implied in Tosafot to Moed Katan 23a that he forbids only what is washed with neter and sand, but not with water alone. The Bach wrote: according to this, if it was washed with water alone, another person need not wear it first, and it is obvious that it is permitted immediately after seven days.
He wears them first. Even if they were also pressed with a smoothing stone, it is permitted in this manner; so wrote the Bach.
Only one hour. Meaning some amount of time, and it need not be a formal hour. So it is implied in the poskim, and so is the custom, unlike what is written in Atzei Zahav, “an hour or two”; perhaps he too was not speaking precisely.
Who went to earn profit. This excludes traveling for pleasure; see Orach Chaim siman 531.
And he did not have time to shave. The same applies here to laundering.
