An hour. This means some period of time; a full seasonal hour is unnecessary. So the authorities imply and such is the practice, unlike the Levush, who wrote one or two hours. Perhaps he too was not precise. This is the Shach. The Bach writes that a garment washed only in water, without cleansing agents or sand, need not first be worn by another, and it is obviously permitted immediately after seven days.
