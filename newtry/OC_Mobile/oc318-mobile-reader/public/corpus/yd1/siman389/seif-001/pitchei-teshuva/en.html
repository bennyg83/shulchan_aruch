After seven days. Responsa Lachmei Todah, siman 30, rules that the restriction on changing garments until after seven days applies specifically when changing for pleasure. If the garment is dirty or causes discomfort, one may change even during shivah, just as washing is prohibited only for pleasure. He writes that the authorities did not state this expressly only because it is obvious from the laws of washing and anointing. See what I wrote above in siman 381, seif katan 2.
