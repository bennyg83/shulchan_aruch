If wick fell from candle onto snuffbox and extinguished — responsum Beit Efrayim CM siman 17: I rule to be lenient and stringent: if head of wick already burned to ash it does not forbid; what is not yet ash certainly forbids; if rested on vessel until yad soledes bo it is absorbed through fire and needs libun; if not rested — hag'alah or scraping; here kli rishon does not apply since wick is moist in milk; Maharatz Beit Tzalel in gloss.
