(there gloss in Beit Yosef) However what our teacher wrote that if there is not sixty in the milk even though he preceded and removed, also the milk is forbidden — is not intended in my view, etc. — see there; and in my humble opinion Tur's words are Rosh's words (siman 23): an olive-size of meat that fell into a cauldron of milk — meat forbidden and milk permitted; it deals when it fell into a boiling cauldron and he preceded and removed before it rested from boiling — what it absorbs it does not discharge; even though milk absorbs fatness discharged from the meat it has no measure to forbid it; but if there is not sixty in the milk against the meat and even if he removed immediately everything is forbidden — end; explained explicitly as Taz wrote; and so for Rambam and Rashba who require tasting for a gentile — we infer he holds that nevertheless it discharges of itself before removal — only since min b'eno mino he wrote kafila suffices; and so Ran and other poskim — see there; and requires study.
