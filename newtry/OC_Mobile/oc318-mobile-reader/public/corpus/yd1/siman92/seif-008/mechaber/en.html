A milk pan placed on a stove under a meat pot — the steam rises and is absorbed in the pot and forbids it. {Rama: If there was milk in the pan, we require sixty in the food in the pot against the milk in the pan (private opinion). All this is when the pan is uncovered and the steam rises from the food itself to the pot above it (Arukh). And also when it is so close that yad soledes bo in the steam where it touches the pot; but if yad soledes bo does not apply in the steam, everything is permitted — therefore they hang meat to dry on milk pots and we are not concerned for rising steam (Piskei Mahar"i siman 103). And likewise if the pan is covered, everything is permitted, similar to two pots touching each other, for they do not forbid one another by contact, all the more by steam (Mordechai ch. 25) — nevertheless l'chatchila one should be careful in all this (Hagahot Sh"D).}
