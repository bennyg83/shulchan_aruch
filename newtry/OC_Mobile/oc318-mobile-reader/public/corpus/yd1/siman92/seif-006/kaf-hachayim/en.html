(6) And there is another difference between Rashi and R' Yehuda (Mishnat Zahav section 2): if the piece was partly outside the gravy and the drop fell in the gravy, and there is not sixty in what is in the gravy — Rashi holds that part outside does not join to sixty; R' Yehuda holds it joins. Per our ruling above we are stringent like Rashi here too, and that part does not join to sixty — Zivchei Tzedek there.
