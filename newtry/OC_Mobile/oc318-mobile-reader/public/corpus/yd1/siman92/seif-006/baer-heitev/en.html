The gravy. Meaning even leaving until it cools does not help. Shach wrote: Maharai who was last and great in his generation investigated all customs and wrote they practice to permit through cooling as in Semak, and so Maharshal; however even per this custom it appears specifically in an old pot, but in a new pot certainly if there is sixty in the food against the drop they did not practice to forbid; and likewise in an old pot that is not beit yomo, or even beit yomo with sixty times sixty-one slightly less against the drop — in all this they did not practice issur and it is permitted for all, nevertheless wait until it cools.
The pot. And in Terumat HaDeshen he wrote one should be stringent l'chatchila to forbid the pot on erev Shabbat.
There is. We fear perhaps it did not steam all inward but something remained in the pot wall; and if perhaps it remained in the pot wall and spread in width until there is not sixty in the wall against it — then need sixty in the food against that wall that was forbidden; if it spread in width it spread in all until sixty, and if it did not spread in the whole vessel presumably it entered inward and was nullified — therefore the food is permitted but in the pot we fear perhaps a little remained in thickness that did not enter inward and it is forbidden; therefore if he cooked another dish in it its law is like the first time and sixty suffices if it fell opposite the gravy like the first time — Shach.
And he should pour. And not leave it to cool — thus after it fell opposite the gravy we fear lest it steam more from here onward (Orach Chaim).
