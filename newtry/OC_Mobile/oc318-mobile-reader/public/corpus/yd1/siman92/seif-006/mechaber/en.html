People are accustomed to forbid when it fell on the wall not opposite the gravy — and specifically when it falls on the side that is not opposite the fire; but if it fell opposite the fire it is permitted, for the fire burns and dries it (and then the pot is also permitted) (Hagahot Sh"D and Arukh general rule 31) — and specifically a small quantity such as a drop; but if a lot fell one may not permit even if it fell opposite the fire, unless opposite the gravy and by sixty. {Rama: And then the pot is forbidden even if there is sixty in the food against the drop that fell — and he should pour the food out immediately on another side, not opposite the drop; and if he cooked another dish in the pot, its law is like the first time (Arukh general rule 31).}
