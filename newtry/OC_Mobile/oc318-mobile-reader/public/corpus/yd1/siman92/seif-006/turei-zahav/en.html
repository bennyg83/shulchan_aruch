People practice to forbid, etc. Meaning even leaving until it cools does not help; and so it appears from Orach Chaim general rule 31 that wrote: people practice to forbid the food even if there is sixty in the food against the drop, since it fell opposite the empty place — then certainly it only steams on the pot walls alone, and we fear perhaps afterward once the pot boiled it touched that part of the wall that was forbidden, or perhaps it touches now at the time of pouring before it cools — end of his words; and in Perishah it implies nevertheless there is permission even per the custom to leave until it cools, and he only was stringent to pour before cooling — and the matters are clear as I wrote, and so Terumat HaChacham general rule 55.
But if it fell opposite the fire. Meaning while it still stands by the fire; but after they removed it, although still boiling, nevertheless it does not burn and dry; and although this is simple, I saw one who erred in this.
