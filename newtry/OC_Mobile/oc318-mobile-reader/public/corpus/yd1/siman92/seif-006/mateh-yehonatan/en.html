(There in the hagahah seif 7) And if milk spilled, etc. And the food is permitted, for the lower prevails — meaning the pot stands in the place of kelipah; and per our holding that if the kelipah is removed sixty is required against the kelipah — one must say he did not place the pot by the fire afterward, for then it conveys absorption more than kedei kelipah; but if he places the pot afterward by the fire, sixty is required against the kelipah.
