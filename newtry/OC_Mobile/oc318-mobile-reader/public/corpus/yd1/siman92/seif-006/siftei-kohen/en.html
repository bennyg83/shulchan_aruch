People practice, etc. Maharai who was last and great in his generation investigated all customs and wrote they practice to permit through cooling as in Semak, and Hagahot Sh"D siman 55, and so Maharshal in Orach Chaim and his book there.
When it fell on the wall, etc. It appears specifically in an old pot they practice to forbid when it fell not opposite the gravy, for the reason Orach Chaim general rule 31 din 3 wrote: we fear perhaps afterward once the pot boiled it touched that part of the wall that was forbidden — end of his words; but in a new pot certainly if there is sixty in the food against the drop they did not practice to forbid, for even if it touches there is no issur here; and likewise in an old pot that is not beit yomo; and likewise in an old pot beit yomo with sixty times sixty-one slightly less against the drop — in all this they did not practice issur and it is permitted for all, nevertheless wait until it cools.
And then the pot is also permitted. And in Terumat HaChacham there he wrote in din 3 one should be stringent l'chatchila per Orach Chaim to forbid the pot — see there.
And then the pot is forbidden, etc. Because we fear perhaps it did not steam all inward but something remained in the pot wall; and one should not challenge: perhaps something remained in the pot wall and spread in width until there is not sixty in the wall against it — then need sixty in the food against that wall that was forbidden; if it spread in width it spread in all until sixty, and if it did not spread in the whole vessel presumably it entered inward and was nullified — therefore the food is permitted; but regarding the pot we fear perhaps a little remained in thickness that did not enter inward and it is forbidden; therefore if he cooked another dish in it its law is like the first time and sixty suffices if it fell opposite the gravy like the first time, for there is only concern lest a little remained — and certainly not more than a drop; nevertheless since we do not know how much remained, in the second time need sixty against the whole drop — requires study; and see Orach Chaim.
And he should pour immediately. On the other side and not leave it to cool — thus after it fell opposite the gravy we fear lest it steam more from here onward — so Orach Chaim.
