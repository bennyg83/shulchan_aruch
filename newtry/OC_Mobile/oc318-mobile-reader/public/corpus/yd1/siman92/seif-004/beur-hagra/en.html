They did not say chatichah na'asah nevelah, etc. — as ch. 5 Terumot and ch. 1 Temurah, ein meduma, etc.; Avodah Zarah 73, vinegar in a pit, etc. — see entire sugya; and ch. 5 Terumot, a se'ah of terumah, etc. (Yerushalmi: all Torah issurim that a shogeg added are permitted — siman 99 seif 5). Ch. 5 100a, "before," etc.; Tosafot s.v. before; R' Efraim; and if you ask that now, etc. — it appears, etc.; Yerushalmi there and learn; but Rif and some poskim hold first tanna and Beit Yosef disagree with first tanna explaining that the name chagav is not required (end).
And then even the piece, etc. — we do not say afshuto metukaso assur in other issurim; sugya Avodah Zarah as stated; Tur here disagrees with Rashba siman 106; but Rashba and Shulchan Aruch there hold the piece is forbidden because it can be squeezed and is forbidden — distinction between what blends and what does not; no contradiction with Avodah Zarah.
Some say in all issurim, etc. — R' Yitzchak's view in Agur; Ran: rabbinic in other issurim; brought siman 22 s.k. 10.
Specifically if attached, etc. — Rashi and Tosafot dispute Tur: Rashi forbids even partly outside gravy; Mishnah "partly outside" — but Tosafot s.v. drop: entirely outside; 97b, "if there is," etc. — see there; Rav ruled stringently in basar b'chalav but in other issurim like R' Yehuda; all poskim in basar b'chalav like R' Yehuda.
If the issur is attached, etc. — we fear it was once entirely outside the gravy or remained alone in the pot — Darkei Moshe in Orach Chaim; and as siman 73 s.k. 26 from spit that was cooked, etc. — see there.
But if part of the piece, etc. — even if he later stirred and covered, and as at the start "or the whole piece," etc. — this case; and "but in basar b'chalav," etc. — also when stirred; as Darkei Moshe s.k. 2, not like Ran.
Nevertheless one should be stringent, etc. — Rashi's view: he was lenient only regarding other pieces, as R' Yehuda, since some say chatichah na'asah nevelah only rabbinically; but that piece is forbidden even per R' Efraim; even if stirred and covered later — as siman 106 and gloss there; main view, not like those who disagree.
Some say we do not say chatichah na'asah nevelah if wet issur, etc. — from Avodah Zarah; no need for Tosafot and Rosh's forced explanations — Mordechai. Ran challenged from 108b: why is milk permitted, etc.; ruled stringently in basar b'chalav even wet-wet; rabbinic in other issurim; see there; Rav ruled stringently for great loss.
And if dry mixed, etc. — resolves Terumah ein meduma; Tosafot ch. 5 — they did not distinguish basar b'chalav from other issurim; but wet-wet, all hold chatichah na'asah nevelah from Avodah Zarah sugya.
In any issur, etc. — even basar b'chalav; not like R' Yehuda who distinguished when partly in gravy.
Vessel, etc. — the pot itself is as if forbidden; new vessel — siman 95 seif 5 if known, etc.; what is absorbed in walls is not chatichah na'asah nevelah — siman 98 seif 5; proof from 97b kli that cooked terumah — but old vessel: absorbed issur is chatichah na'asah nevelah — siman 98 seif 5 and 97b, Tosafot s.v. but; only regarding the pot itself, not chatichah na'asah nevelah.
