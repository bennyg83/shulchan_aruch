Wet permitted. Ba'er Heitev s.k. 13 in name of Maharshal Piskei Hagahot siman 60: specifically when mixed not through cooking; if through cooking we say chatichah na'asah nevelah — see responsum Noda BiYehuda 2 YD siman 49 on eight hides of sheep whose fat was rendered and mixed into five measures sheep fat and all rendered together then put into fifteen measures goose fat — permitted in great loss though Maharshal forbids if through cooking; from Rama's words it does not appear so; from Shach s.k. 15 it appears Shach leans lenient in wet-wet for great need — see siman 56 that Shach leans lenient in wet-wet in basar b'chalav for great need; perhaps Maharshal agrees in great loss; in any case one may rely on Rama especially only doubt of treifot — see siman 50 there.
