A gentile tastes. See below siman 98 that we require specifically kafeilah or mislaleta — see what is written there.
When is this said — when he preceded, etc. For then there is only on account of the meat taste itself; and since a gentile tasted and said there is no taste, it is permitted; but if he did not manage [in time], etc. — then it is possible the milk itself absorbed in the meat and the piece became nevelah, went out and mixed in the rest of the milk, and cannot be tasted — for milk in milk it is; therefore one needs sixty in the milk against the whole piece, for we do not know how much was absorbed and discharged from it.
