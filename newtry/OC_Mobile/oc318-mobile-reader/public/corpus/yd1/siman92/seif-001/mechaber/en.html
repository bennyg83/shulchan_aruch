Laws if milk fell into a meat pot. In it are 9 seifim: An olive-size of meat that fell into a cauldron of milk boiling — a gentile tastes the pot: if he said there is taste of meat, it is forbidden; if not, it is permitted even in less than sixty; and that piece is forbidden. When is this said? When he preceded and removed the piece before the milk it absorbed would discharge — meaning before the cauldron rests from boiling. But if he did not manage to remove it until it could discharge the milk it absorbed — even though a gentile tasted it and there is no taste at all — it is forbidden unless there is sixty.
{Rama: And see below siman 98 that we do not practice relying on gentile tasting and we require sixty in every case.}
