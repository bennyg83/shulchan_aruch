(siman 92 Shach s.k. 3) Since we always estimate at sixty. It appears to me: nevertheless when the whole pot has sixty against the drop, that piece should also be permitted through netilah if it is fatty everything joins to nullify; if lean, netilah suffices even if sixty in the piece — by law only netilah not sixty; therefore still need pot-steam reasons that thin issur spreads in whole piece — see Peleti.
