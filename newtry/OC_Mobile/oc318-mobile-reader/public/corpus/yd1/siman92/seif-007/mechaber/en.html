Some permit in a time of pressure, such as erev Shabbat — even not opposite the gravy, even not opposite the fire — by sixty. {Rama: And so is the custom (Arukh). If milk or other issur boiling was spilled on the ground and a hot pot was placed on it — if what spilled is not by the fire, it is only a kli sheini; therefore the pot is forbidden, for it absorbs somewhat, and the food is permitted, for the lower prevails (Hagahot Sh"D siman 25). Drippings from a boiling pot that went to a cold pot — if the stream broke from the boiling pot before reaching the cold one, it is also like kli sheini (so appears in Sha'arim from responsum of Maharam). If it did not break, it is like iruy and the cold pot is forbidden if yad soledes bo in the stream touching the pot (Terumat HaDeshen siman 185), and the food inside is permitted, for iruy forbids only kedei kelipah; but if the pot is hot and is kli rishon — all the more if it stands by the fire — even if the stream is cold, everything is forbidden, for the lower prevails and it is like cold into hot, all is forbidden (so appears from Beit Yosef), as explained above siman 91. A drop falling on the cover of a pot — its law is like falling on the pot opposite the gravy, when the pot began to boil, for then the steam always rises, reaches the cover, and descends from there to the gravy (Arukh general rule 31 and Sh"D).}
