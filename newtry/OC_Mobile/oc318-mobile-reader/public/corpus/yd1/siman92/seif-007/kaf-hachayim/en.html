But if he stirred from beginning to end, etc. — Rambam's language; it appears only in that case it is permitted; but if he stirred and covered at the start and not at the end, not — as Beit Yosef wrote; but Tur on Rambam wrote one need only stir or cover immediately at the time of falling, even if he later stopped stirring — end; and so in the gloss. But since Shulchan Aruch brought Rambam's language, it appears Rambam holds only if he stirred from beginning to end; Beit Yosef so explained Tur, and Raavad agrees with Tur — but Beit Yosef understood Rambam differently. Practical difference for Sephardim who accepted Shulchan Aruch: we permit only if he stirred or covered from start to end. However, one need not stir constantly until the end — once he stirred three or four times so it certainly mixed, or covered long enough that it mixed, suffices, as the Gemara implies (Chullin). And so Zivchei Tzedek section 7.
