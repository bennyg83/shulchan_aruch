A drop of milk that fell on the pot beside the fire on the outside — if it fell opposite the food, one needs only sixty against the drop, for it steams inward and is as if it fell in the food. If it fell in the empty place and it steams on the pot wall until near the gravy so there is not sixty against the drop, that place of the pot is forbidden; and if he pours the food through that place of the pot that was forbidden, the food becomes forbidden. The remedy is to leave it so and not touch it until it cools. {Rama: And specifically if the pot is old; but if it is new, in every case one does not need sixty except against the drop that fell on it, as below siman 94 regarding a ladle.}
