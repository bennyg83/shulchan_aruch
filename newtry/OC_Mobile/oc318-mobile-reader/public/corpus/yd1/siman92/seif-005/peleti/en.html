A drop of milk, etc. So Semak wrote: a drop that fell on the vessel wall — we are in doubt in ch. Dam Chatat whether it steams through the whole vessel or not; therefore when it fell opposite the gravy and there is sixty in the gravy from the drop it is permitted either way — if it steams through the whole vessel it is nullified, and if it does not steam then it should not forbid (meaning if it does not steam through the whole vessel it should not forbid because presumably it steams inward and is as if it fell in the food — so Beit Yosef explains); but when it fell in the empty place, perhaps it steams on the pot wall until near the gravy so there is not sixty from the drop, and that part of the vessel becomes nevelah, and when he pours the food, etc. — end of his words; and per these words they are the Mechaber's words, only abbreviated.
Only sixty against the drop. Meaning to permit the food, but the pot is nevertheless forbidden and he should pour the food immediately on the other side.
