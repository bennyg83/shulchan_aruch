Milk fell, etc. — through end of seif — Rambam's language.
And they estimate, etc. — 97b, 100a. And the same for what the pot absorbed — ch. 15 law 24; Gemara language 100a is used, and see siman 99 seif 4.
The piece is forbidden. Like Rav against Shmuel and R' Yehuda against R' Elazar there — Rosh.
When is this said, etc. — Gemara there 109a, "the master said," etc.
And likewise if not stirred, etc. — text error; should read: and likewise if there is not sixty in the piece against, etc., as there 108b, "what if he did not stir," etc., and Mishnah there 1.
And if he stirred, etc. — Rambam used Gemara language meaning until the absorption of the drop finishes.
Provided he stirred immediately, etc. — as stated above; otherwise he did not stir at the beginning, etc.
And if it fell, etc. — variant texts in Mishnah: a drop of milk fell on the piece — stir the pot; if, etc. We do not say chatichah na'asah nevelah since it is unknown which piece — as Terumot ch. 4 two boxes and two granaries, and as siman 111 s.k. 47; only one must stir and mix so one does not taste the issur.
Some say, etc. — they hold we say in Terumot Mishnah only min b'mino, not min she'eino mino, since it can be established otherwise; as R' Akiva Eiger: we do not know what fell, etc. — those without taste do not join to nullify the white; this is min b'mino; not min she'eino mino in noten ta'am — Terumot ch. 6 law 5 in Yerushalmi Nedarim, etc.
