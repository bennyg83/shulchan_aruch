The upshot: a piece forbidden due to davar she'yesh lo matirim and chametz after six hours and every matter that forbids in mashahu in its species — if that piece afterward fell to other pieces and was cooked with them even if also its species — it does not need sixty against the pieces and all the more it does not forbid in mashahu; and all the more when it fell to what is not its species it needs sixty only against the piece.
