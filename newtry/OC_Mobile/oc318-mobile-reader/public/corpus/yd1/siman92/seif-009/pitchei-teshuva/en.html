Of milk. Ba'er Heitev of Mahari"t: may one light tobacco pipe from milk candle — see Bnei Chayyim siman 105 osef 4 brought proof forbidden by law not mere piety — see Hagahot there he retracted — see responsum Shabbetai Kohen: proof from Rashi Shabbat 22b lighting candle from candle — pipe absorbs oil and "it appears" refers to light; see YD 274 Taz and Nekudat HaKesef on Rashi; leniency if only detriment in milk itself; but l'chatchila lighting pipe from milk candle — detriment l'chatchila forbidden as several places; see Taz siman 47. Responsum Ma'aseh Moshe 4: one who is not careful may not light from milk candle for one who is careful — see.
But milk. If wick fell from candle onto snuffbox and extinguished — responsum Beit Efrayim CM siman 17: I rule to be lenient and stringent: if head of wick already burned to ash it does not forbid; what is not yet ash certainly forbids; if rested on vessel until yad soledes bo it is absorbed through fire and needs libun; if not rested — hag'alah or scraping; here kli rishon does not apply since wick is moist in milk; Maharatz Beit Tzalel in gloss.
