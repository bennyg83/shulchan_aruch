Of yours. Shach wrote: meaning a candle called kreuzel that burns from rendered-fat milk and is boiling from the fire; but milk of yours boiling in a pot that the pot boiled by the fire, scraping suffices, for it is not considered iruy since the stream broke as above seif 7 and siman 98 seif 10 in the gloss. Taz wrote: milk of yours — meaning from its mouth where the wick burns that side is considered through the fire itself; but if dripped from another side of the vessel where the wick is inside it is not called boiling to forbid what drips on it. Greater still Maharam wrote: if a drop of boiling milk fell on a knife or other cold vessel, only scraping where the drop fell suffices since the drop ceased at falling and did not come in a stream; specifically when falling on wood, but if on iron stab ten times in the ground and enough.
