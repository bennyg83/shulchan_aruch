Candle, etc. — meaning in milk that is not "of yours" where the drop does not fall by the fire itself but by heat of the fire; and iruy forbids only in a stream as in Sefer HaKadmonim.
But milk of yours, etc. — meaning like our candles where milk drips by the fire itself; in such a case even if the stream broke it does not cool so much and forbids like iruy kli rishon; and it appears a proof from what they challenge ch. Keitzad 76a: we taught a drop from gravy, etc., and we would establish in cold earthenware and even though the stream broke — as Tosafot Zevachim 96a s.v. poured and Torat HaDeshen that in such a case use is through the fire as Tosafot ch. 1 Chullin 8a and end Avodah Zarah in name of RIBaM — see there.
