(1) [Seif 1] One may salt many pieces one on top of the other, etc., and as long as it emits its brine it does not absorb, etc. — so is one answer in Tosafot that Beit Yosef brings here; except that there is another answer there that holds it does absorb, but it has permission to return and re-emit when the brine in it emits — and so it appears from what Rama wrote in seif 6 that one returns and salts it, etc., implying that in any case it already absorbed. Taz s.k. 1.
(2) There. And even beef with goat and lamb meat, and even with poultry, etc. — from here you learn that poultry with animal and wild animal meat may be salted together. Maharam MiRotenburg chapter 5 of Magen Avraham. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahat Tur, section 2, letter 2.
(3) There in the hagahah. A piece salted twice is permitted, etc. — meaning without rinsing between them, and specifically if one salted; but l'chatchila it is forbidden to do so — rather one must rinse it well before the second salting. Shach s.k. 2. Peri Chadash section 1 for the hagahah, section 1 in Levush, section 1 in Kereti, section 1 in Shafan Damim, section 2 in Shulchan Gavoah, section 4 in Chokhmat Adam general rule 32, section 21. Beit Yitzchak chapter 1 section 2, Mikveh Mayim section 4, Zivchei Tzedek section 2. And it appears specifically after it remained in the first salt for the time of salting it is forbidden to salt a second time; but within the time of salting, to add salting — there is no concern. Mikveh Mayim there.
(4) There in the hagahah. And we are not concerned that the second salt absorbs the blood, etc., and even if one salted after twenty-four hours it is permitted. Menachem Mani on Tosafot Chullin general rule 15 section 44 — not like Taz s.k. 2. Peri Chadash section 2 in Shafan Damim section 2 and section 3, Beit Yitzchak there in Amud HaZahav section 4, and likewise in Sefer Kanfei Yonah section 7. Mikveh Mayim there. Zivchei Tzedek section 3.
(5) There. Before the poultry emits its blood — meaning before the poultry finishes emitting its blood. Peri Chadash section 3, Kereti section 2, Mikveh Mayim section 5.
(6) There. And if one transgressed and salted them together, etc. — whether salted together side by side, whether one salted poultry on fish or fish on poultry — it always applies to say the fish are forbidden as a peel's thickness and the poultry permitted, because since they are busy emitting they do not absorb — not like Bach — Shach s.k. 4. Peri Chadash section 4, for the hagahah section 2 in Levush section 2 in Shafan Damim section 4 in Chokhmat David section 2, Beit Yitzchak there in Amud HaZahav section 8, Mikveh Mayim section 6. And this is specifically according to Shulchan Aruch; but according to Moreinu the Rambam, specifically if the fish are on top, peeling suffices; but if fish are below or from the side, sixty is required as explained in the hagahah.
