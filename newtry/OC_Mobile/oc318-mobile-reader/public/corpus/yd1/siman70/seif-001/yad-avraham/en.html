(Siman 70 seif 1 in the hagahah) A piece that one salted twice is permitted. And the view of Magen Avraham general rule 15 s.k. 14 that even after brine emission it is permitted b'dieved, for regarding slight brine we say it slides off — not like Taz. And likewise in responsum Makom Shemuel.
(There) But meat with fish — even poultry meat with fish is forbidden to salt; and if one transgressed and salted together, poultry permitted but fish require a peel; and if scales were not removed when salted — permitted, because scales are in place of a peel. And even for us who assess salting at sixty — nevertheless scales help, because then they are constricted and do not emit quickly, and since they are busy emitting they do not absorb. And it is explained in Or Zarua, Tur, Or Zarua, and Tosafot Chullin general rule 20 that scales do not help in place of a peel except if the fish is whole; but if cut one must peel at the cut. It is astonishing why the Mechaber omitted this.
