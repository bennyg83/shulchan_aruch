The law of salting many pieces of meat together. And it has 6 seifim:
One may salt many pieces one on top of the other; even though the bottom piece finishes its emission before the top one, we do not say that it returns and absorbs from the blood of the top piece, because it remains a long time to emit brine; and as long as it is emitting its brine it does not absorb. And even if much brine collects and stands in the hollow between the pieces — it is permitted. In what cases is this stated? When salting meat with meat — and even beef with goat and lamb meat, and even with poultry — because they cannot finish emitting all their brine until the beef finishes emitting its blood. {Rama: Nevertheless, the custom is to be stringent l'chatchila so that every piece that has a receptacle, such as a whole cavity, they turn it over so the blood will drain; but b'dieved one need not be concerned (Or Zarua). A piece that one salted twice is permitted, and we are not concerned that the second salting absorbs the blood remaining from the first salting (Piskei Maharai siman 66).} But meat with fish — even poultry meat with fish — is forbidden to salt together, because the fish emit all their brine before the poultry emits its blood. And if one transgressed and salted them together, the poultry is permitted but the fish require removal of the thickness of a peel; and if their scales were not removed when they were salted — they are permitted. {Rama: And some forbid all the fish (Or Zarua general rule 13) if there are not sixty against the poultry, for we assess salting at sixty — and such is the custom. And this is specifically when they have no scales, for their skin is soft and they emit immediately; but if they have scales they are permitted, for they do not emit immediately and do not absorb from the poultry (Rosh there), because since they are busy emitting they do not absorb — like two pieces salted together.}
