Emits. Taz wrote there is another reason for permission — that it returns and re-emits when the brine in it emits; and so it appears from Rama's words in seif 6 that one returns and salts it, etc., implying that in any case it already absorbed:
Absorbs. Shach wrote: and that which we forbid in siman 69 regarding meat salted without first rinsing — because salt absorbs the blood on it — that case is different, for there is blood in the eye; alternatively, this case is different because since it was salted first and not rinsed afterward, it is still standing to emit brine, and therefore we say the second salt does not absorb the blood. And Taz added and wrote that if after it emitted all its brine — meaning after twenty-four hours — and it was still not rinsed and one returned and salted it, there is reason to forbid it, for we hold below that if it fell into brine after this measure it is forbidden because the brine will absorb; and likewise it will absorb here, end of his words:
Together. Shach wrote: whether one salted them together side by side, or salted poultry on fish or fish on poultry — the fish are always forbidden a peel's thickness and the poultry permitted, for even though blood does not seep upward, nevertheless peeling is required, as written in siman 69 seif 18 that what is outside the brine one cuts a peel's thickness; and poultry are always permitted because since they are busy emitting they do not absorb:
Their scales. For scales are in place of a peel; and Shach wrote that Rabbenu Yerucham also agrees to this; and what he said the reason is soft skin — the practical difference is if fish were salted with another prohibition that is not of blood — even though we do not say there since it is busy emitting, etc., the fish are permitted, for even though we hold all salting at sixty, nevertheless Rabbenu Yerucham wrote in siman 105 regarding a prohibition that has no fat at all, such as chametz on Pesach and the like — for all opinions salting forbids only a peel's thickness; and if so in such a case here the fish are permitted without a peel, for scales are in place of a peel.
