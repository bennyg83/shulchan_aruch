Avodat HaGershuni wrote in Shemesh Tzedakah: a fat piece never salted placed in a vessel with two meat pieces salted and rinsed properly and placed in non-perforated vessel and salted for preservation and stood thus twelve hours — at first glance there is room to permit, for blood in fat is minute in veins and arteries, and certainly there is in the meat twice sixty against the fat; and for law he ruled to forbid — one may not add from reasoning; but the author's son wrote in the hagahah there to permit through rinsing and second salting and rely on Tzedek siman 9 that we do not say through rinsing emission holes closed — Peri Chadash, Menachem Mani agreed; and although in responsum Mahari HaLevi brother of Taz siman 35 and responsum Me'il Shemuel wrote to forbid — nevertheless in a dispute one may rely on presumption of sixty, etc. — end of his words. And it appears b'dieved, especially in great loss, one need not forbid if he wants to roast not intending long storage but immediate eating within two or three days — the reason to forbid can be pushed aside — I shortened.
