In what cases is this stated? When both were salted together, or the poultry was salted and the fish unsalted; but if the fish was salted and the poultry unsalted and one placed them next to each other or one on top of the other — even the fish are permitted without peeling. {Rama: And likewise if the fish are placed on the poultry they are permitted even if they were salted together, for blood does not seep from below to above, and it is no worse than a piece placed in brine — what is above the brine is permitted (Or Zarua general rule 13). And likewise if one did not place the fish next to the poultry but only after the poultry remained in their salting for the time of salting — even the fish are permitted although both are salted (an individual opinion, and so it appears in Or Zarua). And if fish were salted in a vessel in which meat had been salted — the fish are permitted, for fish do not absorb the blood in the vessel, for salting does not apply to vessels (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba siman 129). And that which we say that if the fish was unsalted and the poultry salted they are forbidden — that is specifically when the fish already emitted its blood and was rinsed; but if it has not yet emitted its blood and was never salted — it is permitted, for since it will emit its own blood it will also emit the blood absorbed from the poultry (his own reasoning, and so it appears in Or Zarua general rule 13 in the name of Semak) — like a piece that fell into brine before salting, which is permitted for this reason, as will be explained below in this siman. And see below siman 91 regarding in which salting we say it is considered as boiling.}
