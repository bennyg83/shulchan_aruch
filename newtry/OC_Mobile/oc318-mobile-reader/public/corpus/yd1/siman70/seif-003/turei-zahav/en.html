Kosher meat that was salted, etc. — in Shaarei Dura chapter 10 it is written: and if there is a doubt whether shechitah meat was salted with nevelah meat, it is a Torah doubt for stringency, end of his words:
And there is one who forbids, etc. — this is the Ran, who was challenged: even with pure salted and impure unsalted one may say the pure's salting heats from the impure until it emits and absorbs into the pure; and he does not hold the answer I wrote at the beginning of seif 2 in the name of Rashba that it does not heat so much. Therefore he answered: they permitted only when not touching, only that emissions touch; and even in this, if the pure is unsalted and the impure salted — forbidden:
