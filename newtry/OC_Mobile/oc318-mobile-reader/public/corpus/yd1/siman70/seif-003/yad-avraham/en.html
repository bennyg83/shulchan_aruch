(There seif 3) Or the treifah salted and the meat unsalted and they touch — forbidden. Implies in the conclusion too regarding pure salted and impure unsalted touching, as Shach wrote. The Rosh challenged and Beit Yosef brings: since the pure was salted and placed on the impure, the impure was also salted from salt on the pure. He answered: the pure was salted on all sides except where it touches the impure. Therefore similarly with impure salted — even if not salted where it touches the pure — forbidden. And although according to the Rosh's second answer the pure was salted on all sides even where touching impure, nevertheless the impure was not salted where touching salted. And so Taz, hagahah, and Shulchan Aruch HaRav; if so similarly with impure salted it is also salted on all sides. Nevertheless it seems to be stringent, and so Tosafot Chullin end of general rule 20 — brine is considered boiling — see there. And see siman 91 seif 5 in the hagahah.
