Kosher meat that was salted with treifah meat, or the treifah was salted and the kosher unsalted, and they touch one another — it is forbidden to the thickness of a peel, for even though it does not absorb blood from the treifah it absorbs from its brine. But if the kosher was salted and the treifah unsalted — it is permitted with rinsing without peeling, whether one placed the kosher on top or below. And there is one who forbids when they touch one another, and holds that they were permitted only when standing close so that the emission of one touches the other. {Rama: And when the treifah is salted and the kosher unsalted — even in such a case it is forbidden. And see below siman 105 regarding these laws.}
