Touching. Written in Shaarei Dura: if there is a doubt whether shechitah meat was salted with nevelah meat, it is a Torah doubt for stringency; and Shach also wrote: brine of nevelah, treifah, and sheratzim is Torah law; whereas brine of impure fish is rabbinic, as below siman 83 seif 5; and likewise brine of meat forbidden because of blood is also only rabbinic; and practical difference regarding doubt. And he wrote further what the Mechaber wrote here "and they are touching," etc. — as a surplus; the end mentions this to include that if the kosher is salted and the treifah unsalted, even when touching one another it is permitted; but in truth if the treifah is salted and the kosher unsalted — even though they are not touching one another but only standing close enough that this one's emission touches that one — forbidden, for in any case the brine drips on the kosher — what difference whether touching or not, end of his words:
Close.
