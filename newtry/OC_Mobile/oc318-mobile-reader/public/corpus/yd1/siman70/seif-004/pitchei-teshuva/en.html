What is above the brine is permitted. Avodat HaGershuni end of s.k. 35 wrote we do not hold thus but all is forbidden. And Machatzit HaShekel erred and understood what Shach wrote end of s.k. 46; but for us who hold all salting at sixty, obviously all is forbidden immediately — meaning even what is above brine; but in truth Shach did not mean "all" precisely but to say not only a peel is forbidden — all that is inside brine; but what is above brine is certainly permitted, and even fat inside brine does not forbid above brine only a peel, as Shach explained in the previous s.k. and in many places. I again found in responsum Pri Tevuah siman 18 in the hagahah that he challenged him on this.
