That I will not eat. This refers to a period during which it is possible to remain without eating. Otherwise, it would immediately be a vain oath because fulfillment is impossible, as stated in siman 232, seif 5, regarding one who says, “All the fruits in the world are forbidden to me.” So too wrote the Rosh at the end of the third chapter of Shevuos.
That I threw into the sea, or that I did not throw. This means either formulation. He violates his oath according to whichever statement he made.
Oaths of expression. These are included in the verse, “When a person swears, expressing with his lips,” etc. This means any matter within his power to perform. The verse states, “to do harm or to do good.” Just as doing good refers to a voluntary act, so doing harm refers to a voluntary act affecting himself, such as not eating. This excludes something dependent upon another person, discussed later. Likewise, doing harm to another is not included in the verse of an oath of expression, but within a vain oath. There is no offering for it, though he has committed a transgression.
