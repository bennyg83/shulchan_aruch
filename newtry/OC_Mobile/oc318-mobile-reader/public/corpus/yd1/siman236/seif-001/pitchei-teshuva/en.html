To express with the lips. See above siman 232 seif 12 in Be'er HaGolah regarding shevuah in writing; and see on this responsum R' Eliezer Mizrachi siman 72, responsum Mahari Betzalel siman 9, Mayim Rabim part 4 siman 68, Chut HaMeshulash siman 154, Shev Yaakov siman 49, Noda BiYehudah part Yoreh Deah siman 66 and part Choshen Mishpat siman 244 letter 14, responsum Brit Avraham part Yoreh Deah siman 59, and Sefer Shemen Rokeach part 2 siman 27 — what they expanded on this. See responsum Tiferet Tzvi part 4 siman 62 in the name of the gaon Maharam Mordechai Benet who wrote regarding agreements rabbis give on printing books that another may not print this book — one may say it does not take effect, for shevuah in writing does not help; and he disagreed, for a decree in writing that great ones decree certainly takes effect — only he wrote that if the first printer already sold his books, he has no power to block others from printing and preventing Torah work even within the time rabbis bound with their cherem, since he has no loss — see there (and in responsum Kovetz Yeshurun the famous gaon Maharam Efraim Zalman Margoliyot — I saw his view is not so, that even after sale he still has full right until end of time; he expanded in proofs; and I heard that in practice the leading gaons of our generation disputed this matter and it is not known who prevailed and how the matter fell). Also in Noda BiYehudah there he wrote likewise that even if shevuah in writing does not help, acceptance in writing certainly helps — see there. He also wrote in siman 66 regarding shevuah: one's agent is like himself, since one may swear conditionally — see there; and see responsum Shemen Rokeach there who disagrees — his view is shevuah through an agent does not help; and see Noda BiYehudah Tinyana part 4 siman 47 — responsum from the author's son who expanded to prove his father the gaon's view that shevuah takes effect through an agent; and he wrote that also in Piskei Maharai siman 234 it is clear thus, and he resolves all objections — see there [and see Beit Meir Even HaEzer siman 120 seif 4 — it is obvious to him shevuah cannot be through an agent, for "express with lips" is written; and he wrote on the above Noda BiYehudah that Ramban in Bava Batra 126 was hidden from him — see there more on this; and he did not see that Noda BiYehudah himself in Tzelach to Berakhot noted this, and also Noda BiYehudah Tinyana there and siman 148 expanded on this. Also in responsum Chatham Sofer simanim 220 and 227 he expanded greatly and explained many details and distinctions regarding shevuah, neder, and konam in writing; and difference whether written in past, present, or actual verb tense; and law of shevuah and konam through agent and whether condition applies in writing; and distinction between laws of man and laws of Heaven; and distinction between a document that is only narrative and a kinyan document — see there at length and you will find rest. See further that responsum siman 328 what he explained on Ramban's words].
