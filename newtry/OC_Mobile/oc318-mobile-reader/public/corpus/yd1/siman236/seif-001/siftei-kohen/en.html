And I will not sleep, etc. — meaning when specified a day or two days; but three days or general is a false oath, as below seif 4. And likewise without "I will eat." And Bach wrote: it seems even in general, if he says that in his mind it was today, he is believed — it is not a false oath. And if you ask: if so, ask him what was in his mind — one can say such as he said that in his mind it was whatever the meaning of my language implies; or such as he forgot what was in his mind, as Tosafos wrote chapter 3 of Shevuot 20b, and above siman 208 s.k. 35, and siman 232 s.k. 35.
