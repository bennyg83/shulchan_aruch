That I will not eat. This means for a period during which abstention from food is possible. Otherwise it is immediately a vain oath, as with one who forbids all produce in the world. Taz.
I will sleep. This applies where he specified one or two days. Three days, or an unspecified period, creates a vain oath. Bach writes that if he says he intended only the present day, he may be believed unless he forgot his intent.
