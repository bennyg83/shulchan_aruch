To maintain. Taz rejects Rama's gloss as lacking basis. Shach understands Rama consistently with the Mechaber in siman 232:4.
Forbidden. His oath takes effect because he included all matzah, even matzah not used for the mitzvah. Similar principles govern inclusive oaths that combine permitted and mitzvah items. Several cited responsa discuss charity and future mitzvah obligations.
The sukkah. Levush writes that to exempt the oath he must specify mitzvah matzah or a mitzvah sukkah. If he said merely that he would not eat matzah or sit in a sukkah, the oath includes nonmitzvah cases and therefore takes effect.
A positive commandment. Shach explains that the true distinction is not simply positive versus negative commandments. An oath cannot compel an active Torah transgression, but an inclusive oath of passive abstention may take effect even regarding a prohibition, as where permitted dates are included together with neveilah and treifah.
