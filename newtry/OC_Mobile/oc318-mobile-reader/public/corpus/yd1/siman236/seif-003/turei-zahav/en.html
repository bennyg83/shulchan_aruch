He must be careful regarding his oath. So wrote the Rambam. The Tur objects: I do not understand, for a person cannot prohibit his own property to another except through a konam. The Rambam may be reconciled according to the Ramban at the end of siman 206, who holds that a vow expressed in oath language and an oath expressed in vow language are effective. The person certainly knew that his fellow was not obligated to accept the oath. Rather, he intended to prohibit his property to the other person as a vow, but expressed it in oath language. The other person must therefore be careful. The Rambam concludes that if the other person enters, he is the transgressor, while the one who swore is considered under duress.
