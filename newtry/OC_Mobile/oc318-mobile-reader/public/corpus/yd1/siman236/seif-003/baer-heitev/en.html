His. Taz wrote: and even though a person does not forbid his own on another except with konam language — it must be said that Mechaber holds that a vow they said in oath language, and an oath in vow language, is effective, as above siman 206. And therefore he certainly knew the other is not obligated to accept his oath; rather he intended to forbid in vow language, only he expressed it in oath language — therefore the other must be careful. And Rambam concluded: if he enters, he transgresses, and the one who swore is under duress — end quote.
