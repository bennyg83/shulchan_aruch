In another matter, etc. — Beit HaGadol; and Maggid Mishneh brought proof for Rambam's words from what is written in Niddah 46b: since we find, etc., for intentional oath; and we establish there such as he devoted and others ate.
But if, etc. — so is Tur's version, meaning he swore that his fellow not enter — to impose an oath on his fellow is certainly impossible; and so in our version. And Tur also explains Rambam's words thus, not like Maggid Mishneh.
Must, etc. — meaning Shimon; and in our version it says he is obligated. And Maggid Mishneh already challenged him, that even in vows he wrote in chapter 5 of Hilchot Gerushin that in such a case he is not obligated. And Ran in Nedarim 15a, s.v. Halacha, etc.
