Exempt. Taz derives a general rule: when fulfillment of a condition is essential to the undertaking, failure of that condition prevents the obligation even if caused by circumstances beyond control. Where the timing is secondary, unavoidable delay does not cancel the principal obligation. He applies this to commercial delivery dates and marriage agreements, and disputes Bach concerning whether a postponed wedding oath remains binding. Nekudot HaKesef supports Bach. Taz further discusses penalties and communal cherem in engagement agreements.
