If he swore to change what is known to people—such as regarding a man that he is a woman, or regarding a woman that she is a man—he is liable for a shevuat shav. And similarly if he swore to establish the truth about a matter known to everyone, such as regarding "two that they are two," and the like; or regarding a matter that is impossible to do, such as that he will not sleep for three days, or he swore that he will not eat for seven days, or he swore that he will not eat any of the fruits in the world—it is a shevuat shav, and they flog him, and he sleeps immediately and eats immediately. {Rama: If he swore without specification that he will not sleep and will not eat, it is as if he swore that he will not do so forever, and it is a shevuat shav. (Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe end of siman 213 in the name of R' Yerucham, and similarly Rosh chapter 3 of Shevuot and Tur end of siman 219.)}
