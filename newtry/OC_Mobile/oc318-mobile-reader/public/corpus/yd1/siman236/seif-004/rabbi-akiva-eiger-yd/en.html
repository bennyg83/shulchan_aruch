(Seif 6) Two who swore — Rabbi Chayyim Alfandari z"l in his book Esh Dat al HaTorah wrote in parashat Korach that it appears the same applies if a Jew swore to do something with a non-Jew: since the non-Jew is not subject to [the laws of] oaths, the Jew is exempt from that oath — end quote.
