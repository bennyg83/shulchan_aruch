In this manner. Shach questions the scope of Rivash's ruling. If the woman refuses to marry him, he is released because his oath meant to marry her if she agreed. If she later agrees, the matter is uncertain. Taz adds that an oath not to marry anyone except a named woman may still require annulment even if she refuses, because the oath took effect inclusively when she might have consented.
