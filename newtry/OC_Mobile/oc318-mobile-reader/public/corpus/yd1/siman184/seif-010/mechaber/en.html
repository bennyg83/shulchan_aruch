One who wants to go on the road must take leave of his wife, even close to her veset. {Rama: And even with tashmish it is permitted (Tur in the name of some; and Beit Yosef in the name of Rashi and Ra’avad and Rashba and Rabbeinu Yerucham). Nevertheless, one who is stringent not to take leave of her except with words of appeasement — and this is praiseworthy (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag). And it was already explained that all types of closeness and affection are permitted, except tashmish. And if he goes for the sake of a mitzvah, he need not take leave of his wife (Maggid Mishneh, perek 4 of Hilchos Issurei Bi’ah, and in the glosses of Shulchan Aruch). And some say: if a person wants to go on the road and his wife is a niddah and she will immerse within one onah, he must wait (Nimukei Yosef, perek HaBa Al Yevimto).}
