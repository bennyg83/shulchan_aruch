That they are twenty-four months. See responsa seif kaf chet who wrote in Even HaEzer siman 13 seif 3 that the intercalated month does not count in the number; and in truth there the Mechaber's opinion is the intercalated month counts in the number — only Rama was stringent only in old Shach Krakow edition who wrote "some say" and intended Rama's opinion. And in truth it appears even the opinion of those who say there agree here she is not included — for all its force there is from what we learned in Ketubot "and her cleansing two years"; and if so intercalation is included — eat all here we rule like rabbis who do not depend on nursing at all, only limbs disintegrate and do not return to strength until twenty-four months; and year is not mentioned at all, only twenty-four months — from where to say year? And Rabbi Meir who holds it depends on nursing — if she nursed even five years she is not in presumption of seeing — if so twenty-four months excludes rabbinic matter who hold even if died at twenty-four months nevertheless called nursing; and on this he said not because of death — twenty months she is not in category of nursing. But he did not limit time at all to twenty-four months; and rabbis who limit and do not depend on nursing at all but on limbs disintegrating, fixed time twenty-four months — if so from where to add? And especially since it is unlikely in reality: if twenty-four months pass in the year for this reason limbs would be weak without returning to strength until intercalated month passes — what that would not be except intercalation; and this requires premise from what they expounded in Yerushalmi on the verse, etc., that God agrees reality of nature with fixity of Israel's sages in intercalation; and we have no reason to say this except where we find explicitly so in Chazal's words; and if kabbalah regarding blood we accept, but otherwise not — and therefore it is clear here is only twenty-four months whether in regular year or leap year; and there is no dispute.
