Once her pregnancy became apparent, etc. In Siman 189 it will be explained how long a pregnancy must be for it to be apparent.
Twenty-four months. See in Even HaEzer, Siman 13, Seif 11, that the intercalary month does not count in this number.
She need not separate, etc. I found in the glosses of Maharshal to the Tur, where he wrote: "It seems to me that this refers to a case where she did not establish a fixed cycle three times during her pregnancy or during her nursing period, but she is concerned about her first [pre-established] cycle. Reflect on this." So wrote the Hagahot DeRi as well, in Seif 15. And see below, Siman 189, Seif 33.
Near, etc. The Perishah wrote: this implies only near [the time of the cycle], but at the time of the cycle itself she must separate. And similarly above, s.k. 12, regarding a minor. However, this is not a fully precise inference, etc. And it similarly appears from what the author and the Ateret Zekenim wrote — that even during her cycle itself it is permitted without inspection. And so it appears from the words of the Rosh and other authorities.
