He need not separate etc.
I found in Maharshal's gloss to the Tur: it seems to me this deals with where she did not establish a veset three times in her days of pregnancy or in her days of nursing — only that he is concerned for her first veset — investigate — end of his words; and likewise in Hagahot Darchei Rachel seif 15; and see below siman 189 seif 33.
Near etc. Perishah wrote it implies only near; but at the time of her veset itself he must separate; and likewise above s.k. 12 regarding a minor; but this is not a fully precise deduction, etc.; and so it appears from what the Mechaber and Eitan HaEzrachi wrote that even during her veset it is permitted without checking; and so it appears from the words of the Rosh and other poskim.
