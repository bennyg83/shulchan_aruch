Such as jumping and similar. Meaning: eating garlic and other hot things, or other action she does that causes sighting by coercion that is like jumping (see above siman 189 seif 23 in the gloss) — and this is simple. But establishing veset through an occurrence in her body such as yawning and sneezing that do not depend on her action — if so one should be concerned for days and veset of bodily veset, as explained later siman 189; and Shach understood Beit Yosef's words to refer even to yawning and sneezing — therefore he elaborated to challenge him and concluded: perhaps Beit Yosef also admits regarding veset of the body, etc., and deals only with veset of the body that comes without action similar to jumping, but not other veset such as yawning and sneezing; and it is strained that the plain meaning of his words implies he deals with all veset of the body; and further we do not find in Talmud and poskim any veset of the body through action except jumping, end of his words. Behold what he wrote that we do not find any veset of the body through action except jumping — this is certainly not so, for Beit Yosef followed Rashba here, and Rashba counts also eating garlic and similar as jumping, as Beit Yosef wrote later siman 189; and certainly it was hidden from him there Beit Yosef's words. Also what he wrote that the plain meaning of his words, etc. — this too is not so; on the contrary, since he wrote veset depends on action, say she did not jump, etc. — it implies and is explained explicitly to the reverse; and Beit Yosef's words are correct — investigate.
