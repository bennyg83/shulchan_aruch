The first. If she saw twice on Rosh Chodesh and the bleeding continued into the second or third day, but the third occurrence began only on the second or third, the beginning of the sighting is normally controlling. Chavot Daat distinguishes continuous bleeding, which is one sighting, from intermittent bleeding, where each day may require concern. Once the initial day itself has been firmly established, bleeding during the subsequent niddah days does not independently create another expected time.
