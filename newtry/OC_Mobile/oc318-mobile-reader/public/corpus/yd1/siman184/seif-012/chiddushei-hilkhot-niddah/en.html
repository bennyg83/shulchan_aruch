Regarding bodily veses: what the Shach elaborated upon in seif katan 32 — the Machatzis Yosef already wrote that the view of the Shulchan Aruch is also regarding this matter specifically — a veses that depends on an action, such as eating garlic and the like. But a bodily veses that comes by itself incidentally — it is obvious that one must be concerned that perhaps the day arrived and she had such occurrences; and this is straightforward.
