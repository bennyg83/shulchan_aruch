She changes her cycle. Noda BiYehudah understands Rama as describing a woman who is certain to see within a span of two or three days but has no fixed day inside that span; all those days become expected times. He derives a stringent rule for one who repeatedly cannot complete seven clean days, but Chavot Daat rejects the proof and explains Rama as referring to a woman whose established dates gradually shift earlier or later, so several dates remain concurrently significant.
