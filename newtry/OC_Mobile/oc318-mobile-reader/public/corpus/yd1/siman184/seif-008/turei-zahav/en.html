Thus Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba, that even ab initio she is not concerned — and this is like a pregnant woman in seif 7, except that the Tur wrote here that she is concerned for veset except that there the day passed, etc., and as the Rama said afterward in the name of "some say." And Beit Yosef challenged the Tur, for in the Gemara Rabbi Yochanan derives the law of a pregnant woman from the law of one who was in hiding who does not need a bedikah — how can we distinguish between them? And he elaborated on this pilpul. And Maharshal wrote that the Tur and Rosh had a different version in the Gemara; and in my humble opinion the matters appear according to their plain meaning: regarding removal of blood due to fear, there is reason to be concerned that perhaps at the hour she needs her husband the fear departed from her heart, and since it is her veset hour it is possible she will become impure then, for her regular time comes; and they did not say in her case that fright removes blood unless she was in fright the entire veset hour and it did not cease from her — and this is a clear matter: if she was in fear at the beginning of her veset hour and departed from the fear within that veset time, then she is like other women whose nature returned to its original place — therefore she may not have relations until after the entire veset hour passed. Unlike a pregnant woman who is already cleared of blood and there is no concern to forbid her even at veset time — it follows there is no resemblance of pregnant woman to one in hiding except after the fright ended, meaning after the veset measure which is certainly removal of blood; and this is each according to her way — in this case after the fright passed, and in this case even immediately. And therefore the plain meaning in the Gemara regarding a pregnant woman ab initio from one in hiding: for the entire veset time afterward she does not need a bedikah — and this is correct and clear. And I am astonished at the commentators who did not notice this matter, for it is a correct reasoning and fitting to establish the halakhah thus.
