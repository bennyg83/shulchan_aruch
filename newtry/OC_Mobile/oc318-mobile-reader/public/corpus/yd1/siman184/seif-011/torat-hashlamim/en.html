It is forbidden for him to have relations with her. The Tur states: One who returns from a journey, and likewise one who remains with his wife and the time of her veset arrived, and so forth. This is the language of the Mishnah in Perek Kol HaYad. The Gemara asks why it is necessary to teach the case of one returning from a journey. One might have thought that the ruling, namely that before the time of her veset she is permitted without inquiry, applies only when he was in town, but when he was away and she was not mindful to examine herself, it does not apply. The Mishnah therefore teaches otherwise. The Mechaber abbreviated and did not write the law concerning the period before her veset at all. He therefore also writes here without qualification, with all cases included.
He may have relations with her. Nevertheless, he should not have relations with her immediately upon returning from a journey, because this can result in weak children, as stated in Gittin 70a. Rambam rules likewise, as cited in Tur Even HaEzer siman 25. See above, seif katan 18.
