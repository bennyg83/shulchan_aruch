And her veset time arrived. But before the time of her veset and likewise when she has no veset before thirty days — he need not ask her.
And he need not etc. Whether she is awake or asleep, whether young or old — Tur from the Talmud; and Tosafot wrote that not fully asleep, but not so awake that she knows to answer that she is pure; but if she is fully asleep — even though there is no niddah prohibition, nevertheless it is forbidden to come to the sleeping woman, as in Nedarim; and the Acharonim bring it.
