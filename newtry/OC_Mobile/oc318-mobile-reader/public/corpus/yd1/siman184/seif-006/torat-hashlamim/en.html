She need separate only during the first onah. This is not comparable to seif 5, where she must separate for the duration of the continuation. There, the continuation is brief, and it may be considered a single sighting. When the flow continues for two or three days, however, it cannot be considered a single sighting; rather, it consists of additional blood that was added. The Ateret Zahav and the Derishah write this. See the Bach and Shach, who distinguish the cases in another manner. We must indeed draw a distinction so that the Rosh's own statements in Perek HaIshah do not contradict one another. See there. The same is necessarily true in the words of Rabbeinu Yerucham. The Taz writes: “It nevertheless requires study what source the Shulchan Aruch has for making this distinction.” I do not understand his difficulty, since the distinction is necessary, as is explicit in the Rosh there. See Ma'adanei Melech at the end of Perek HaIshah, and see there.
