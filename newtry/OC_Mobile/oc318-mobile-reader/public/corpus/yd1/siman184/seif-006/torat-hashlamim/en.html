And it appears for seif 5 she must separate for the duration that lasts in it — for there the continuation is a minority matter; it is possible to say it is one sighting, unlike in continuation of two or three days — it is impossible to say it is one sighting, rather extra blood that was added — so Pri Chadash and Darkei Moshe. And see Bach and Shach who further distinguish in another matter; and so we are compelled to distinguish so that the Rosh's own words in chapter HaIshah do not contradict each other — investigate. And so is compelled in Rabbeinu Yerucham's words; and Taz wrote: except I have doubt from where Shulchan Arukh has to distinguish thus, end of his words — and I do not know, are not his words compelled as explained explicitly in the Rosh there? And see in Magen Avraham chapter HaIshah — investigate.
