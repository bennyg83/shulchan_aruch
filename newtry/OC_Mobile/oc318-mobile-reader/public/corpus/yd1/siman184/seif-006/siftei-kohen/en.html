She need not separate, etc. And it appears for seif 5 she must separate by day for the duration that lasts in it — for there she sees prolonged sighting from after sunrise until within the day; but here it is not one sighting, rather the sighting she sees first is the beginning of her veset, and all she sees afterward is extra blood that was added to her — so Bach and Perishah wrote: specifically above where the continuation is a small matter, it is reasonable to say it also joins; unlike when it is two or three days and similar — so wrote Pri Chadash — investigate.
