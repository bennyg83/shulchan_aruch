Who has not reached the years of youth. This means twelve years and one day. Before then, even if she developed signs of maturity, we say that they are merely moles. If she did not develop signs, even after reaching the years of youth she remains a minor. Taz and Shach. This is also explained below in siman 189 and at the beginning of siman 190. The primary laws of a minor and her signs are thoroughly explained in Even HaEzer siman 155. See there.
