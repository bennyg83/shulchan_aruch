To separate from her. See in responsa Noda Biyhudah part 2 simanim 55 and 56 who raised that what they said veset derabbanan means: regarding that we do not say she already saw at her veset time because it is against the presumption that regular time comes — there is presumption of purity; but regarding separating from one's wife near her veset, it is from the Torah that we are concerned lest she see, and we do not say regarding the future to establish her in presumption of purity — as if she will never see. And it is similar to that which they said: perhaps he died we are not concerned, and perhaps he will die we are concerned — investigate. And see in Noda Biyhudah part 2 siman 103 what he wrote on this; and see what I wrote siman 187 seif kaf vav in his name. [And see in responsa Chatam Sofer siman 170 and seif kaf tet who wrote that Noda Biyhudah intended this reasoning from his own mind, and in truth it is earlier in Beit David on Rosh HaShanah; and so his teacher the Gaon received to distinguish thus; and therefore he is very concerned for this reasoning and treats it as Torah doubt — investigate. And so too in several responsa there. However in siman 175 there he brought Ra'ah's words at some length; and according to what is stated there it appears only the body of the reasoning to distinguish between "she saw" and "perhaps she will see" is earlier in Ra'ah, but for practical law there is great distance between them. And these are his words there in the name of Ra'ah: although veset is not deoraita, in order that we establish her certainly impure after a moment of veset passed — nevertheless from the Torah she is forbidden to have relations in the onah near her veset after the moment of veset passed; for certainly if she is accustomed to see in the middle of the day, and we forbid her to have relations from sunrise before she reaches the moment of veset, because perhaps through heat of relations the regular time will advance — this is derabbanan. But after midday when the moment of veset already passed — although we do not establish her as certainly having seen, since veset is derabbanan and we do not say she already saw — nevertheless from the Torah she is forbidden to have relations the second half of this onah from midday onward, lest she see now due to heat of relations, since the moment already arrived when she was fit to see then, end of his words — investigate.]
