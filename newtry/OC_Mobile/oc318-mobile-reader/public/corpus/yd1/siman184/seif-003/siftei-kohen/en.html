She has not reached the days of youth. That is, twelve years and one day, as below in Siman 189, s.k. 63.
And she has not produced, etc. The vav is disjunctive — meaning: or she has not produced signs. So wrote the Perishah and the Bach, and so it is below in Siman 189, Seif 27.
