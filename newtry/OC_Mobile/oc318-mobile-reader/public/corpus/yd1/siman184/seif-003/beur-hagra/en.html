In what case, etc.; and the same applies, etc. This follows from the Gemara there: 'He asked him,' etc.; 'here too, her blood,' etc. The same applies to all four classes of women listed in the Mishnah there, 7a, since the halachah was decided in accordance with Rabbi Eliezer. Regarding a virgin, however, this cannot apply, as the Mishnah there states: 'Concerning what did they say,' etc. We rule on 10b that this refers to a virgin and an elderly woman, unlike a girl who has not yet reached the age at which menstruation is expected, as explained there.
