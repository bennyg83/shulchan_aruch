And she did not manifest the signs. This explains the phrase, "But regarding a minor." That is, there are two forms of minority. One is that she has not reached the years of adolescence. There is also an indication of minority when she has not manifested the physical signs; even if she has reached the years of adolescence, she is still a minor. Conversely, if she has not reached adolescence but has manifested the signs, she is still a minor, because those signs are regarded as a mere mole. See siman 189.
He need not separate from her near the time of her veset. It appears obvious that the same applies at the actual time of her veset, for the reason is identical, as written in seif 7 concerning a pregnant woman. The wording "near" was used only because this siman is discussing that subject.
