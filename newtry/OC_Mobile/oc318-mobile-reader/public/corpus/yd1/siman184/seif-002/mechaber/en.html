At the time of her veset, one must separate from her one “onah,” and not from other forms of closeness, only from tashmish (the bed) alone. If it is by day, he separates from her that entire day, even if the veset is at its end, and it is permitted immediately the night after it; and likewise if it is at its beginning, he separates the entire day and it is permitted all the night before it. And so is the law if it is at night: he separates the entire night, and it is permitted on the day before it and after it — whether she established the veset 3 times or 1 time. {Rama: And all this is only regarding a veset that depends on days, but not regarding a veset that depends on a change of the body (Beit Yosef in the name of Ra’avad); and see above siman 189. And a woman who changes her veset to advance it by 2 or 3 days, or to delay: when the time of her veset arrives, one must separate from her 2 or 3 days before, or after it (Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 1 of Hilchos Issurei Bi’ah), and see above siman 189.}
