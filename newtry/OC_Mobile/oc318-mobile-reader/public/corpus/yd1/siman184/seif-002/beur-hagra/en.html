Whether she established, etc. Niddah 64a.
And all this, etc. The Mishnah, 63b, states: 'If she was accustomed,' etc.; Rabbi Yose says: 'Even,' etc. Rabbi Yehudah disputes only concerning days and hours, but he does not dispute the first Tanna.
And a woman who changes, etc. The Mishnah there states: 'If she was accustomed,' etc.; all the more so in a case such as this.
