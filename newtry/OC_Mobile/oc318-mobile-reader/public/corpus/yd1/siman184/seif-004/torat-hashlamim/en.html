She is forbidden only by day. The reason: since veset derabbanan, we go to be lenient and say in them that it certainly was during her niddah days — so Rosh and other poskim in the name of Sefer Baal HaNefesh to Raavad. However in Sefer Baal HaNefesh before us, Venice edition, gate of fixing veset, he wrote: where she is accustomed to see at sunrise itself, or if she is not established in the matter — I do not know whether before sunrise or after sunrise; certainly we rule them stringently the entire night and entire day, end of his words — behold explicitly Raavad ruled simply to be stringent; and also it implies from his words that at sunrise itself is called a doubt in itself. And see Bach who also wrote to be stringent.
