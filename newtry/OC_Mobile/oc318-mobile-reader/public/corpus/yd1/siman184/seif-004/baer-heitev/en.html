By day. For by day she is certainly impure, for even if she saw before sunrise, nevertheless the day was certainly during her niddah days — unlike the night which was in doubt. Rosh. And Shach wrote accordingly: if she is accustomed to see at twilight and we have no established law whether before night or after night, she is forbidden only at night. And Bach wrote it is fitting to be stringent from doubt to forbid by day and night, and Shach agreed with him.
