Tiferet LeMoshe permits relations on a woman's immersion night even when it coincides with her established cycle, where her pattern makes any earlier immersion impossible, because otherwise she would be permanently forbidden. He is even more lenient where the expected bleeding is the following day. Chatam Sofer similarly permits leniency where changing cycles would otherwise prevent normal marital life, and also allows shortening the customary waiting period after bleeding immediately following immersion when no relations occurred.
