One onah. Avnei Tzedek. And see in Tiferet Lemoshe who wrote: a woman who established a veset on the night of her immersion and cannot immerse until the night of her veset — such as she established veset from night of the fifteenth to night of the fifteenth, and her way is to see all seven days and she must observe seven clean days two weeks, and immerses on night of the fifteenth — she is permitted to have relations even though it is her veset time, for veset is derabbanan (see in the seif kaf before this), and they were not stringent to forbid her to her husband forever; and all the more if her way is to see on day fifteen, one need not be stringent on the night of her immersion on night fifteen because of the onah before her veset onah, as Or Zarua's opinion — investigate his proof. And it implies specifically in such a case where her way is to see all seven days — and if so it is impossible to advance immersion from night fifteen. But if not, and she established veset from night thirteen to night thirteen — although Rama wrote in siman 196 seif 11 that one does not begin counting seven clean days except from day six, and if so the night of immersion is her veset time — nevertheless it is preferable she begin counting from day five or day four (meaning if she fixed for night twelve), for this is only a stringency. Indeed if her veset is on day thirteen, in this one can say it is better to be concerned for Rama's stringency than Or Zarua's stringency not mentioned in Shulchan Arukh at all. [And see in responsa Chatam Sofer seif kaf tet, a question similar to this regarding a woman who changed her veset twice every month in a way that it is difficult and unlikely she will chance to immerse for her niddah — for until the first passes the second hurries to come; if she chances a night of immersion it will be at the onah whose veset is tomorrow in its day; and according to the Shach one must be stringent like Raavan to separate the night before that onah — then this woman will end up divorced from her husband, Heaven forbid, if two leniencies are not found for her: one to have relations on the night near the day of her onah; two, if it happens she sees immediately after one immersion before relations, to be lenient that she need not wait six onot and count seven clean days immediately when she stops seeing. And he answered regarding the first law whether one must practice like Or Zarua and Raavan's stringency — it appears to be lenient, since their entire reason is only because of "do not distinguish" even though veset derabbanan; and even for Ra'ah who holds the onah near veset is stricter than veset itself (in the previous seif kaf) — nevertheless that is for a fixed veset three times; but veset that is not fixed, such as this woman who always changes her veset and is concerned for her day of seeing — is veset near veset derabbanan; if so, one may be lenient with his "do not distinguish" and permit in the onah near the onah of seeing so she not be divorced and the husband fulfill peru urevu. And also in the second law it is simple to be lenient, since that which they practice to wait six onot even though she did not have relations is only because of "do not distinguish" between she had relations and she did not; and one need not be stringent with "do not distinguish" like in the body of the law itself, as Terumat HaDeshen wrote and brings from Magen Avraham siman 197 — if so one may be lenient in such a case; and at minimum she should wait three onot, for behold for the Rambam who ruled like the rabbis of Rabbi Yosi, etc., only Israel are holy. But where there is concern she leave her husband immediately because of this, it is obvious one should be lenient at minimum after three onot; and if she immersed at twilight and saw after immersion before relations, she should begin counting seven clean days on day three and stop in purity at twilight of day two approaching three, end of his words — investigate. And see what I wrote later siman 196 end of seif kaf gimmel in the name of several great ones that in such a case if she saw after immersion before relations, one may be lenient in every manner and even count immediately — investigate.]
