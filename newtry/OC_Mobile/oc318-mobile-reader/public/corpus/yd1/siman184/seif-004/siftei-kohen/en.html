She is forbidden only during the day. Since vestot are rabbinic, this is a rabbinic doubt and we rule leniently. She need separate only during the day, which was certainly within her niddah days. This is the Rosh. That is, even if she saw before sunrise, nevertheless the day was certainly within her niddah days, whereas the night was in doubt. So the later authorities explained. According to this, if she is accustomed to see during bein hashmashot and we do not know whether it was before or after nightfall, she is forbidden only at night.
Only during the day. The Bach wrote that it is proper to be stringent because of the doubt and forbid both the day and the night; according to what I wrote above in s.k. 7, all the more so here.
