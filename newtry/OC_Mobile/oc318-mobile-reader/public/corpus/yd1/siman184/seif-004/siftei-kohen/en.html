Forbidden only by day. Since vestot are rabbinic — it is a rabbinic doubt and we are lenient, and she need only separate on the day that was certainly in her niddah days — Rosh. That is to say: even if she saw before sunrise — nevertheless the day was certainly in her niddah days, unlike the night which was in doubt; and so the Acharonim explained. And accordingly if she is accustomed to see at twilight and we do not know whether it is before or after night — she is forbidden only at night.
But by day. And Bach wrote it is proper to be stringent due to doubt and forbid both by day and at night; and according to what I wrote above in s.k. 7 — all the more so here.
