Since she certainly saw at the end of the night, we follow after the beginning of her veset and say the essence of veset is at night, only its time extends further part of the day — therefore she is forbidden also in that part of the day. And therefore if she is accustomed to see from before night until night, she is forbidden the entire day and at night for the duration that lasts in it. And Shach wrote according to what I wrote seif kaf zayin: if so, at sunrise she is forbidden here the entire night and entire day, and on the day after for the duration that lasts in it; and before night she is forbidden the entire day and entire night before it, and on the night after for the duration that lasts in it — investigate.
