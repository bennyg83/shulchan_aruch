Forbidden at night, etc. For we follow after the beginning of her veset — so too the poskim. And therefore if she is accustomed to see from before night until night, she is forbidden the entire day and at night for the duration that lasts in it. And according to what I wrote above seif kaf zayin: if so, (at sunrise) [before sunrise] she is forbidden here the entire night and the entire day before it, and on the day after for the duration that lasts in it; and before night she is forbidden the entire day and entire night before it, and on the night after for the duration that lasts in it — investigate.
