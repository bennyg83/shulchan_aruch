If, etc. Certainly, throughout the actual time of her fixed period she is forbidden even according to Rabbi Yose, as stated in the Mishnah there. Rabbi Yehudah comes only to impose an additional stringency. Nevertheless, she is not forbidden for the entire day. This follows from the statement there: 'If she was accustomed,' etc., 'at sunrise,' etc.; Rabbi Yehudah says, 'The entire day is hers.' The phrase 'at sunrise' means immediately at sunrise, and it is impossible that the sun should not rise during the span of sunrise itself. Moreover, the Mishnah teaches the rule without qualification and does not distinguish between a brief flow and a prolonged one. See the two passages there.
