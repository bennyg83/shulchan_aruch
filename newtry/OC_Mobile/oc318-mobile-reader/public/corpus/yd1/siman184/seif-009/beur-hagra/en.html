Other women, etc., passed, etc. For we rule veset derabbanan; and Tosafot there s.v. veRabbeinu Nissim, etc.; and Tosafot 15:1 s.v. afilu.
And some say, etc. Those Tosafot; and as is written; and the first reasoning of Rav and Shmuel in their dispute; and in the end she checked because of Shmuel — for it was stated; and their reason from what is written 15:1: Rav Bibi said: even if it arrived, etc.; and it implies even immediately after veset, only the onah passed — unlike what Tosafot wrote there s.v. afilu; and Rabbi Yochanan who said "he counts her veset days," etc., holds veset is deoraita — and we do not rule like him; and see in Shevuot.
Or it is, etc. Her onah when she has no veset is like veset, as is written: Rabbi Levi in the name of Rabbi Yonatan, etc.; and see Rashi there s.v. within, etc.; and as is written 9:2.
