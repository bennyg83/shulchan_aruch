If she has a fixed veset. This means that she established it three times. If she did not establish it three times and it is shorter than the average interval, such as when she saw from the twenty-fifth day to the twenty-fifth day or the like, then even if she did not examine herself, she is pure without an examination, provided she did not sense a discharge of blood. The Tur writes this at the end of this siman. See the Perishah and the Taz regarding how they resolve this so that it does not conflict with siman 186.
