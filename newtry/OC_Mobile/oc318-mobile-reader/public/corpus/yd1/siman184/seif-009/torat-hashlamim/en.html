If she has veset. Meaning she established it three times; but if she did not establish three times and it is less than intermediate onah — such as she saw from twenty-fifth to twenty-fifth and similar — although she did not check, since she did not sense blood she is pure without bedikah — so Tur end of this siman. And see in Perishah and Taz what they reconcile here so it should not be difficult from siman 186.
