Until she examines herself. This means even if she did not examine herself until a long time afterward. We do not say that perhaps the blood fell to the ground at the time of the veset.
If she has a veset, etc. However, if she has a veset that she has not yet established three times, and it is shorter than the average interval, such as when she saw from the 25th day to the 25th day or the like, then even if she did not examine herself, she is pure without an examination, since she did not feel a discharge of blood; end quote from the Tur at the end of this siman. The Perishah raised a difficulty, for siman 186 states that a woman who has no veset is forbidden to have relations without an examination. He therefore discussed many resolutions at length. It appears to me that there is no difficulty at all. Certainly, a woman who has no veset, but has already become accustomed to many sightings with no equal interval at all from one to the next, retains a presumption that she may have seen until she examines herself. Here, however, we are discussing a woman who has a veset like other women, but on one occasion changed and saw from the 25th day to the 25th day. She must therefore be concerned when the 25th day arrives, as stated in siman 189. Regarding this case we say that since she did not feel a discharge on the day for which she was required to be concerned, and that day passed without her examining herself, she is afterward pure. This is because she has not established through three sightings that she sees at that time; it is sufficient that she be concerned l'chatchila. This is straightforward.
