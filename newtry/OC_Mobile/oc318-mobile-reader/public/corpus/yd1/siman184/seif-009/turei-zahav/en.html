If she has veset, etc. But if she has veset that she has not yet established three times and it is less than intermediate onah — such as she saw from twenty-fifth to twenty-fifth and similar — although she did not check, since she did not sense blood she is pure without bedikah, end of Tur at end of this siman. And Darkei Moshe challenged: behold it is found in siman 186 that a woman without veset is forbidden to have relations without bedikah; and because of this he elaborated greatly in answers. And in my opinion it is not difficult at all: certainly a woman without veset but already accustomed to many sightings without equal measure at all — each time she is in presumption of seeing until she checks. But here it deals with one who has veset like other women, only she changed to see from twenty-fifth to twenty-fifth once; and she must be concerned when day twenty-fifth arrives, as is found siman 189 — regarding this we say: since she did not sense on that day she is concerned, and that day passed and she did not check afterward — she is pure, for she is not established three times in sighting at that time; and it suffices that she be concerned ab initio — and this is simple.
