(Shach seif kaf lamed dalet) The measure that she can count and immerse. In my humble opinion one can say: if the veset composed of jumping was from twenty-fifth to twenty-fifth, and the husband stayed until thirty-three days passed when she is permitted by presumption — doubt perhaps she did not see at all; or if you say she saw — perhaps she jumped on day twenty-fifth and saw, and now immersion time has arrived.
