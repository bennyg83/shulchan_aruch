Seif 1. See Maharshal siman 1 on the Shach who holds that in a woman who has no veset, within thirty days is called not at her veset time and she does not need a bedikah — and he challenged: if so, you will not find a case requiring her husband to do a bedikah. And in my humble opinion he spoke well for the law; but regarding the Shach there is no grasp here, for according to his approach he goes — for he holds later in siman 186 not like the Mechaber, and his intent is to equate most poskim like the Rif, that in truth her husband does not need a bedikah even in a woman without veset; and regarding taharot the Shach did not speak here at all. And in truth it appears one can say he holds a reason in the matter: for a woman does not become impure from the Torah except through sensation — and if so it is only a rabbinic doubt, and a doubt is resolved leniently, and one need not even clarify, as explained in the general rules of Shach — that although the presumption is not so strong that a woman stands in presumption of purity since she has no fixed veset, nevertheless there is more presumption of purity here regarding prohibition. But all this is within thirty days; but on day thirty, since most women see on day thirty, we follow the majority to be concerned and she needs a bedikah, and not before — as in my weak opinion, to speak in defense of the Shach.
