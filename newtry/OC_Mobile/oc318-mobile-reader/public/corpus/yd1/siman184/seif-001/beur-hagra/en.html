And every woman, etc. Mishnah, 15a; the baraita, 11b; and the Gemara, 12a. See also siman 186.
And she need not, etc. This is the Rambam's opinion; see the beginning of siman 186. All the other authorities disagree with him, however, and do not require an examination even after relations, as explained there. This is the intent of the gloss, 'also,' etc. Its source is the Gemara there, 12a: Rabbi Zeira asked him, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'He asked,' etc., and the passage there in which Rabbi Abba asked him, etc.
But not, etc. Torat HaBayit explains the Mishnah at the beginning of the second chapter in this manner.
