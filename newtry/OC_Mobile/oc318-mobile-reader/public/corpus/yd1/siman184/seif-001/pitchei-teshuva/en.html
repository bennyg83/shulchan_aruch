Or from thirty days to thirty days. Shach implies that before a woman establishes a regular cycle, within thirty days is considered outside her expected time and she need not examine herself. Kreti U'Pleiti disagrees, while Sidrei Taharah and Chavot Daat uphold Shach: at the beginning, before any pattern is known, no examination is required within thirty days; once she is established as having no fixed cycle, she always requires examination.
