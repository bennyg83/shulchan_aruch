He had relations with her not at the time of her veset. The Tur states that he may have relations with her as he wishes. This means whether she is awake or asleep. It refers to a case in which she is not completely asleep, for otherwise it is forbidden to have relations with her, as stated in Nedarim. Rather, she is not sufficiently awake to answer whether she is pure or not. So wrote Tosafos in Niddah 12.
