That one must separate from a woman one “onah” before her veset; it contains 12 se’ifim. Most women have vesatos (meaning: a fixed time, common among women) to see at a known time — for example, from 20 to 20 days, or from 30 to 30 days. And every woman who has a fixed veset: one may have relations with her not at the time of her veset, and she does not need a bedikah before tashmish (Rambam).
{Rama: She also should not be stringent to check herself, neither before tashmish nor after tashmish, so that his heart not knock and he separate (Tur and Mordechai, beginning of Hilchos Niddah, and most poskim).} But not at the time of tashmish, whoever increases checking, behold this is praiseworthy.
