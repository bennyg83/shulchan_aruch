Such as from twenty to twenty. Or to twenty-five or thirty — Beit Yosef, and it is obvious.
Or from thirty etc. And the standard veset is thirty days — Tur; and so it is in the Talmud and poskim, and later end of seif 9 and beginning of siman 189; and that is: if she did not establish a veset, then her veset is presumably every thirty days, and within thirty is called not at the time of her veset.
That she has, etc. But if she does not have a veset, her law is explained in siman 186 seif 2. And she does not need, etc. This is according to Rambam whom the Mechaber brought at the beginning of siman 186. But according to most poskim brought there, and according to the Rav there and here, she should not be stringent at all to examine, neither before relations, lest his heart strike him with concern, since when he sees his wife examining he thinks that if she had not felt something she would not examine and he separates, nor after relations, lest his heart strike him with concern and he separate from then on. That which we say in the Gemara and below in siman 196 seif 9, that any hand that checks frequently among women is praiseworthy, refers to not at the time of relations. So wrote the Tur, Rashba, and other poskim.
