He had relations with her not at her veset time. In the Tur he wrote that he has relations with her as he wishes — meaning whether she is awake or asleep; that is, she is not asleep entirely, for if so it is forbidden to have relations with her as is found in Nedarim; rather she is not so awake that she can answer whether she is pure or not — so Tosafot in Niddah 12a, end of his words, Taz; and so Bach; and similarly the Tur and Beit Yosef wrote in this siman explicitly regarding one who waited after veset the time to count and immerse that the Mechaber brought nearby seif 11. And the Mechaber who ruled anonymously here and in seif 11 appears he holds the main reasoning that the Talmud distinguishes in Niddah 12a between awake and asleep — because asleep she is not fit for taharot, unlike awake who is fit for taharot; therefore more bedikah is required. And so Rashi explained there for one explanation. And if so for us where there are no taharot — if so, the distinction between awake or asleep is no longer relevant — and it is simple.
She also has no difficulty checking herself. In my letter, Plyg M. Moore, with the author’s opinion, Daina needs a test, but if she wanted to get worse, and after she had a check-iner, he said, “The Rambam should not be examined at all, and the Rambam’s opinion should be made clear to him.”
