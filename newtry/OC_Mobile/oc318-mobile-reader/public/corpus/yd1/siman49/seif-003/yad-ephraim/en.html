Its law is like a needle found in ha-meses. See Be'er Heitev; and see Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 64 regarding a needle found in the kurkavan at the time of eating that Taz forbade in place of Hagahot Perishah; but for the honor of Shabbat one may be lenient, for we lean more that it came through the esophagus, since he did not see at salting a companion from outside nor at washing — presumably it was not punctured outside — see there. And Mahari Levi brother of Taz wrote in his responsa siman 25 regarding an incident where they cooked chickens and geese and gave the kurkavan to a lad to eat, and when he bit it he screamed and they looked — behold a needle stuck.
