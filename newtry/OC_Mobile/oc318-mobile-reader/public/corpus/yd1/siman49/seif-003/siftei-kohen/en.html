Stuck in it from within and not punctured entirely — its law is like the law of, etc. And so is Tur's language, and Darkei Moshe wrote on it and so are his words: it appears from Tur's language that specifically regarding puncture from within and not punctured entirely its law is like ha-meses and beit hakosot; but if punctured entirely its law is not like ha-meses and beit hakosot, for even if no koret dam was found around — treifah entirely; and so is implied in Terumat HaDeshen that he did not write that there was koret dam around the needle, and nevertheless it is implied that if the flesh were not this minority it would be considered sealing — treifah, end of his words. (And that which the Rav omitted this in the hagahah appears to be because he wrote above siman 48 seif 8 that even in beit hakosot one should practice prohibition when punctured from both sides even if there is no koret dam, for we are not expert; but in ha-meses and beit hakosot whose membrane is soft one can say that after slaughter something so slight was punctured. But the flesh of the kurkavan is thick — one does not say that perhaps it was punctured in such a brief moment; rather it was entirely punctured while alive. But if punctured from within alone it is simple that one must investigate whether there is koret dam outside opposite it — forbidden; and the same applies even if the needle merely rests inside it as written siman 48 seif 8 regarding ha-meses. Except that then one need not check from outside whether there is koret dam there, since we have no companion for it inside — which is not so when stuck from within.) And Derishah brings it, and many poskim omitted it, and Hagahot Maimoniyot end of chapter 6 of Hilchot Shechitah wrote and so are his words: and if the needle is discernible from both sides in the kurkavan, koret dam is found on it — it is known that before slaughter no koret dam was found on it; it is known that it was inserted from the other side after slaughter, as above regarding a needle found in the thickness of beit hakosot, end of his words. And so too Hagahot Mordechai and Semak daf 91a and so are his words: and when the needle was inserted in the kurkavan from within and koret dam is found on it from outside opposite the needle — treifah; and if the needle is discernible from both sides and there is no koret dam on the needle or on the flesh after slaughter — it went out to the outside and is kosher, and we are not concerned perhaps it passed from life to the outside side, end of his words. And so is implied explicitly in Orach Chaim general rule 91 law 21; and so is implied in Sefer HaTerumah siman 14 (and so is implied from Bach's words who wrote and so are his words: from what Tur wrote that its law is like a needle found in beit hakosot — it is implied that for all their laws they are equal, also for the law of koret dam and rust that was explained in the preceding siman — and it is simple, end of his words). And that which Tur wrote "and not punctured entirely" — this is because if punctured entirely it is obvious that it is like beit hakosot; but without puncturing entirely there could be a mistaken impression that it is like the liver where we distinguish above siman 41 between the pouch and inside or outside — it teaches us that in the kurkavan too we say food and drink pressed it as in beit hakosot, and therefore even the pouch outside is kosher; and so is implied explicitly in Tosafot and in Asheri end of chapter 8 of Avodah Zarah — and this is clear. And from Terumat HaDeshen too it is no proof, for there too it deals with beit hakosot, and he did not mention koret dam only because certainly for its obviousness he did not need to mention it; or perhaps because he holds like one acharon who declares treifah nowadays even in beit hakosot without koret dam because we are not expert — and see above siman 48 s.k. 12.
