Be'er Heitev of Maharit in name of Shach that even if the needle is stuck in the puncture, etc.; and see in responsum Toldot Yitzchak siman 6 who wrote that one may be lenient only when the needle does not go out at all to the outside into the fat; but if the needle goes out a little to the outside into the fat, even though there is much fat on the point of the needle — one must forbid; and it is fit to expound this law publicly, for many instructors erred in this — see there where he elaborated. And what he further wrote in name of Be'er Eshek: in fattened poultry there is much fat and it is not protection, etc. — see in Tvu'at Shor who also agreed to this; and see in questions and responsa Toldot Yitzchak siman 7 who doubts this and raised to be lenient in Hagahot Perishah. And see in Dagul MeRevavah what he wrote on this. And from his words it appears that if it is known that the puncture preceded the fat — it is fine sealing and kosher. And see in Sefer Chomot Yerushalayim end of siman 70 who wrote that according to some poskim even fat that is not of its kind seals; one may be satisfied how the law is in an egg that laid previously, for even though the hen returns to kosher status — one may say the egg remains in its prohibition — see there; and according to the reason he wrote in Dagul MeRevavah, that since the sealing of fat was revealed, it was never treifah, etc. — see there; it is obvious that the egg too is kosher. [And see on this topic in Sefer Levushei Serad siman 6 and in its explanation there s.k. 17, and brought a little below siman 81 seif 2.]
