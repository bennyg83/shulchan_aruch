Its law is like the law of a needle, etc. See end of siman 45 what Darkei Moshe wrote in his name: here one need not have koret dam when punctured from both sides; and if punctured from within alone it appears simple that one must investigate whether there is koret dam from outside opposite it that is forbidden; and the same applies even if it merely rests inside as written siman 48 seif 8 regarding ha-meses. Except that then one need not check from outside whether there is koret dam there, since we have no companion for it inside — which is not so when stuck from within.
