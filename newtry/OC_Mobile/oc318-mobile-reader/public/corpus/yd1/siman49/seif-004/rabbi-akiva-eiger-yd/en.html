(Siman 49 Taz s.k. 3) On the contrary it is a fortiori. In my humble opinion according to what is written here: since the view above holds only where the needle is still stuck, for in this case if it had punctured outward the needle would be stuck outward. But if the needle is not stuck in it, the view above also holds that we are concerned perhaps it healed. If so, here according to the side that the puncture is through a needle — since the needle is not before us stuck — it is much more common and regular that the outer was also punctured than what there is to be concerned for with a needle in the body cavity that perhaps inner organs were punctured.
