See in responsum Noda BiYehudah Yoreh De'ah part 2 siman 18 that the same applies if one of them was removed — kosher; and likewise if one of them rotted entirely — also kosher; and not like one sage who wished to forbid this and said the reason that rotting is not similar to puncture, for through rotting in the end both will spoil — and he disputes him; see there at length.
