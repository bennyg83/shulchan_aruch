(1) [Seif 1] The kurkavan has two membranes, etc. Explanation: the pocket inside is one membrane, and all the flesh is the second membrane. Taz s.k. 1. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur ot 4; Peri Chadash ot 1; Lifnei Periyah ot 1; Beit Levi ot 1; Kereti ot 1; and he wrote that one is the pocket inside that they peel from the kurkavan and discard, and one is with all the thickness of the kurkavan's flesh. Mishnat Zekenim ot 1; Maharam Chaviv ot 1; Anaf Yosef there general rule 30 ot 1; Turei Zahav siman 86 ot 1; Beit Yitzchak ot 1; Zivchei Tzedek ot 1.
(2) In a goose there is no inner peel by itself; rather they scrape and peel with a knife, and after scraping a white, smooth, somewhat hard skin remains, and afterward red flesh. Mishnat Zekenim there. And I investigated this and they told me that in these places geese and bar-geese and poultry — namely chickens of their kinds — are all equal in their measures, for in the kurkavan there is a pocket in which food is collected and they discard the pocket, and there is no distinction among them. Maharam Chaviv ot 2; Anaf Yosef there; Zivchei Tzedek ot 2.
