Membranes. Be'er Heitev; and see in responsum Shetei HaLechem siman 39 who wrote regarding a kurkavan punctured through illness from the outside going all the way inward until the membrane that women remove when removing filth — but that membrane was not punctured: his view is to declare treifah, for that membrane is not counted at all, and we properly call it punctured through-and-through; and not like one teacher who erred in this — see there. [And see in responsum Chatam Sofer siman 50 who disputes him, that the teacher did not err at all, for the truth is so that even that green membrane inside protects; and so appears from Peri Megadim's conclusion, and so teach the language of Kereti and Peleti; and one who is stringent may be stringent for himself, and one who is lenient for others does not lose — see there.]
