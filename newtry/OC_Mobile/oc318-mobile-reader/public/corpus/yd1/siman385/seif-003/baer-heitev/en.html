Portions. The Bach cites Rabbeinu Yehudah that a mourner within thirty days may neither invite others nor accept an invitation to join them. The Taz writes that the same applies throughout the twelve months of mourning for a father or mother. Maharil likewise forbids a mourner to serve drinks to guests. This applies even on Shabbat in places where people do not exchange greetings with mourners on Shabbat. Darkei Moshe. Magen Avraham writes in Orach Chaim, siman 696, that the Rama implies that where mourning practices are not observed on Purim, Purim has the law of Shabbat, and sending gifts is permitted in a place where people customarily greet mourners. The Taz there does not consider this inference reliable. Reference 656.
