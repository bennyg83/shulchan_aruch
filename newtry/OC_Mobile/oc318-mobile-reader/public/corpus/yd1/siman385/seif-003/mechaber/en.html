A place where they practiced to ask welfare of mourners on Shabbat — they ask; and Rambam wrote the mourner gives peace to everyone on Shabbat, for it is among public matters: {Rama: Forbidden to send portions to a mourner for father and mother all twelve months, even on Shabbat in a place where they practiced not to ask his welfare on Shabbat (responsum of Maharil siman 31); but in a place where they practiced to ask his welfare on Shabbat, this too is permitted.}
