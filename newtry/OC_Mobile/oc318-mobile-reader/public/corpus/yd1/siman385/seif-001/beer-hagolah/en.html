Moed Katan 15a. It derives this from the fact that the Merciful One said to Yechezkel, “Sigh in silence.” Rashi explains: In this matter you shall observe mourning, and you shall not extend greetings. As Tosafot write there, Yechezkel was required to display certain signs of mourning, although he did not observe the laws of mourning in full, since his conduct served only as a symbolic example:
