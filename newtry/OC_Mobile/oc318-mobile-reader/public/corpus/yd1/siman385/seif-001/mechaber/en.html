The law of greeting a mourner. And it contains 3 seifim. A mourner is forbidden in greeting — how so? The first three days he does not ask peace of anyone; and if others did not know he is a mourner and asked his welfare — he does not answer them but informs them he is a mourner. From three until seven he does not ask; and if others did not know he is a mourner and asked his welfare — he answers them. From seven until thirty he asks peace of others who are at peace, and others do not ask his welfare; and all the more he answers one who asks his welfare. After thirty days he is like any other person. In what matter? For other relatives; but for father and mother he asks peace of others after seven, and others do not ask his welfare until after twelve months:
{Rama: And since forbidden in greeting, all the more forbidden to speak much (Rambam chapter 5 and Kol Bo); and if doing so for honor of the public, such as many coming to comfort him — permitted to say to them, Go to your houses in peace, for honor of the public is permitted (Nimmukei Yosef chapter Elu Megalchin and in Hagahot Maimoniyot and Mordechai Laws of Mourning); and some are lenient nowadays for a mourner to greet after thirty, and they have no reason unless to say what we practice is not called greeting as in their days (reasoning of the Rav); and see Orach Chaim siman 83.}
