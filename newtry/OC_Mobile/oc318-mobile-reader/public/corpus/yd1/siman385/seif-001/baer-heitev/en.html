(However, it is possible to say that since most of our greeting of shalom is nothing but that one says "good morning," and this is permitted, for it is not an actual greeting of shalom, as is in the Beit Yosef in Orach Chaim siman 85, and he brought it in the Shulchan Aruch there. And the Beit Yosef there further brings in the name of Ri that spreading peace is like bowing, but shalom by mouth is permitted, like "a good morning of my master" and the like, see there. And he wrote there in Darkei Moshe that in the Zohar Pekudei daf 103a it implies that it is forbidden only when one mentions the Name, and this is said regarding not giving shalom to his fellow before he prays; and we learn from this that all this that we practice is not included in greeting of shalom, as it appears to me.)
