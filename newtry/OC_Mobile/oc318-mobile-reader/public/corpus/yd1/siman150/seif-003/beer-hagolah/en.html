(°) In the Gemara the text does not read "before idolatry" but in [large] cities in general; but the Mechaber took the wording of the Rambam, chapter 3 of [the laws of] Avodah Zarah, and Semag, negative commandment 19; and likewise in Behag at the end of the laws of Avodah Zarah. The reason is that the face [of the idol] was made as an ornament for idolatry, and one who kisses the face is as if he kissed the idolatry itself. Bach.
