"Because it appears as if he bows, etc." It is written in the hagahah of Perishah: and according to what we hold that anything forbidden because of marit ayin is forbidden even in innermost chambers — so too here, even if no person sees him it is forbidden; and so it is explained in Semag siman 19 — until here his language; and so in Tosafot (first chapter of Avodah Zarah 12 end of 11a); and in Hagahot Ashri there R. Yuda is stringent in the name of R. Yitzchak; and so it appears in the Tur explicitly; and such are the words of the Mechaber; and see Orach Chaim siman 301 seif 45 and siman 305 seif 11:
