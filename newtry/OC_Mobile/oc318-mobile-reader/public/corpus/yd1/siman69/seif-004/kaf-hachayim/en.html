(12) There in hagahah. And if he did not do so, it is as if not rinsed at all. And if he cut after rinsing into two, or a piece not rinsed only on one side — permitted b'dieved with great loss. Shaarei De'ah seif 3 — and this is according to what is written in seif 4 regarding a piece salted only on one side the Shulchan Aruch permits and in hagahah some forbid; and the later authorities wrote there that with great loss one may permit if already cooked — as explained — and in our words there. And the same if he removed hooves of legs after rinsing: estimate if above the hooves there is corresponding to what was in the hooves, it is as a piece salted on one side.
(13) However if he cut only a little, one may permit even without great loss, for we are not meticulous about this because of what Shach wrote s.k. 21. Shaarei De'ah there; margin of Maharsha. And this is when he cut a little, meaning a small amount; but if he cut much, it is as cutting each piece in two, and as salted on one side one may not permit except with great loss as written in the previous seif; with this the doubt Pitchei Teshuvah wrote in seif 4 is resolved.
