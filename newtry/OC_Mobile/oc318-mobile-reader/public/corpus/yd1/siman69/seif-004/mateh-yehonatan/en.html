(seif 9 in hagahah) Even if placed only in a kli sheni, etc. Since there is salt and tzir, etc., and even though we hold melicha requires cooking like ox meat, etc., salt does not hasten to cook, etc., and one may challenge the Rosh in chapter Kirah brought in Taz s.k. 23 who wrote that if old salt, perhaps forbidden, etc., and even if salted anew, although the meat does not cook, nevertheless the salt and moisture and blood in it cooked, etc. — how does he say that salting in it cooked? Melicha requires cooking like ox meat! One must distinguish: where he says cooking required, that is salt in its strength and in the eye; but if it dissolved somewhat on the meat and its power ceased, it cooks properly and also hastens to cook through the power of salt and tzir even in kli sheni. What Taz wrote "his words are astonishing," etc. — not necessarily compelled; one may say the Rosh deals with Shabbat, and what he concludes that it returns and absorbs, etc., is to say the meat is forbidden because of cooking on Shabbat — if it had not absorbed the moisture of blood cooked on Shabbat the meat would be permitted, for the meat does not cook at all in kli rishon; if so no prohibition was made in this meat at all, therefore he says the moisture of blood that cooked is absorbed and the meat is forbidden. What Rama wrote in Teshuvot HaRosh, etc., Rabbenu Yerucham permits unsalted meat — one who examines Rabbenu Yerucham Netiv 15 part 5 sees explicitly he deals when there is no water, dry stuck to vessel walls; but if there is water, one may say he agrees forbidden as the Rosh said.
