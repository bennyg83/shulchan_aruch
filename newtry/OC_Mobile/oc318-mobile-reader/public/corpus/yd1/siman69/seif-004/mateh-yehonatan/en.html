(there) And one should not leave meat three days, etc., for we are concerned lest they cook it. He challenges from a chicken with sixty treifot doubts permitted to leave only one day and we are not concerned for mishap. Some want to say it is different there because slaughter is required and it is a major act remembered within the time it is treifah; but this is not so, for permitted to leave even eggs one day without this reason; one must say that regarding sixty definite prohibitions with no heter, people separate themselves and mishap does not apply; but here, since permitted to roast, since there is heter for roasting, people do not separate and properly concerned for mishap.
