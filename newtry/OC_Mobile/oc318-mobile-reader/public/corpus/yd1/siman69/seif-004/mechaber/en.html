He shall spread salt on it so no place remain without salt, and salt so it not be fit to eat with that salt, and he need not add more salt than this; and salt it from two sides, and birds must be salted also from inside; and if he salted them only from inside or from outside, and likewise a piece that was salted only on one side — permitted. {Rama: And some forbid even b'dieved (Maharai in hagahot of Shach Devarim and HaAroch general rule 6) and so is the custom if not for need; and specifically if already cooked thus; but if not yet cooked he shall not cook it thus unless within twelve hours of salting he returns and salts the side not yet salted and cooks it afterward; and if after twelve hours then he shall roast it, for fire draws and the side already salted does not absorb from the side not salted (there).}
