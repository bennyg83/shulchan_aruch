(There Shach s.k. 40) Or one finds a case where they were rinsed by rainwater. Kereti challenged this text that Shach brought, that it deals where they were rinsed in rainwater that are not mekarev. But their rinsing pleases him and post-intent watering is mekarev. And one may say Shach holds like Tosafot Bava Batra (daf 97) that rainwater is not called watering by default until one intends them for watering and they do not help what pleases him. And see Chullin (daf 87). And see in Tel Melach and Chiddushei HaRamban. And it seems to me like what I wrote. And one may say it deals where the owners were wicked and there is no intent without action. However, the view of Tosafot and Chiddushei HaRamban is that they do not require specifically the owners' intent, for another's intent is also mekarev, unlike the Rambam.
