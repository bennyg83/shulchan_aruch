(Beit Yosef) And I wondered on Rama in siman 75. In my opinion requires further study: there melicha on the inner side certainly does not help since there is no blood there, therefore if cooked, even with great loss forbidden because of blood in the fat; but to return and salt is permitted since in fat there is only a little blood; but here for Rashba that melicha on one side helps — no blood remains at all, and preferable to that below; and for Maharai that melicha on one side does not help — much blood remains and inferior to that below; therefore in great loss we rely on Rashba and there is no blood at all; but without great loss we are concerned for Maharai that melicha on one side does not help and consequently much blood in the piece and returning to salt does not help — clear with God's help.
