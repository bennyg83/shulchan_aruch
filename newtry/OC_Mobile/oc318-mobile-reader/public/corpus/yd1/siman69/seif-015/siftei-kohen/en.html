Meat soiled with blood, etc. So is Rabbenu Yerucham's wording (Netiv 25) brought in Beit Yosef; and it appears to me he mentioned specifically 'soiled with blood,' as stated explicitly in Semak siman 205 (daf 56b) in the name of R' Meir and in Orchot Chaim general rule 2 din 5 in the name of Mordechai and R' Meir — and it is reasonable to them that it is not forbidden because of the meat itself that became pickled, for although it absorbs and expels, nevertheless it is not better than kli sheni that has remedy in melicha, as Beit Yosef brought Mahari in the name of R' Meir; but meat soiled with blood is visible blood that does not exit, neither through melicha nor through roasting — even without the reason that pickling is literally like cooking, as written above and in siman 76; and so Orchot Chaim wrote there explicitly that this blood is visible blood that does not exit through melicha or roasting — examine; indeed Beit Yosef brought Hagahot Shach Devarim what our rabbis practiced to forbid meat that remained in water — that is when there is much meat and there is not sixty in the water against all the pieces; but if there is sixty in the water against all the pieces, then the blood that exited is already nullified in the water, and if it is absorbed back in the meat it does not harm; and what remains in the piece exits through salt, end of his words — appears explicitly that even if not soiled with blood he forbids it because of the piece itself that became pickled, like cooked; and so appears from Shach Devarim and Maharai and Agur; and Maharshal in Orchot Shabbat and chapter 25 siman 91; and the Rav in Torat Chatat general rule 3, and above seif 1 in hagahah, see there — and so is the view of all other Acharonim; and the Mechaber too, who wrote 'soiled with blood,' must mean he does not disagree about 'soiled,' rather it is the way of speech he mentioned 'soiled,' because therefore they soak it; and nevertheless requires further study how he wrote only through roasting, for this is not found in any posek — that meat soiled with blood that soaked from above should be permitted for roasting; and it is astounding, for since it was absorbed in the flesh through pickling, roasting no longer helps, for visible blood that was absorbed does not exit through melicha or roasting, as explained above and in siman 76 and in many places; and Shach Devarim and Agur, who mentioned there are those who permit roasting, did not mention soiled with blood in their words, and they hold pickling is not better than melicha and is not literally like cooking; and Rabbenu Yerucham and other poskim who mentioned soiled did not mention permission in roasting; and if so the Mechaber is giving advantage to two against one, and perhaps the Mechaber's view is that even if soiled, nevertheless since it soaked and the blood mixed in the water and became pickled, it is no longer considered visible blood (and not like what I wrote above, also unlike Orchot Chaim) — requires further study, and see s.k. 66:
Unless there is sixty in the water against it. Meaning then the piece itself is also permitted; and so is the Rav's view in Torat Chatat general rule 3; and he wrote there it is not comparable to cooked (above seif 11 in hagahah), for they did not say pickling is like cooking for all matters, only that it absorbs and expels but does not mix the taste of the piece like in gravy; and there it is only a custom to forbid that piece — therefore one should not be stringent in such a case, end of his words; and Maharshal there forbids that piece — certainly; and in any case one may rely on Hagahot Shach Devarim and the Mechaber and the Rav to permit it for roasting, since in any case there are those who permit pickling for roasting; and further, in Shach Devarim responsum of R' Shlomo ibn Gabirol it appears thus, for he gave the reason of meat that soaked in water from above: because blood returns and is absorbed in the flesh, see there — and it appears for the permissive view even if soiled with blood it is permitted, since there is sixty in the water against it, the visible blood on it is nullified in sixty:
