Bloody meat, etc., for roasting — Shach challenged how will roasting help on filth that is visible blood and kevush is like cooking, certainly sixty without doubt. And I wrote: every piece sixty against visible blood on the piece; and if they said so for blood in second rinsing, all the more in first rinsing, known that more blood smeared in second than first, and piece kevush in water on all sides cannot be unless water more than piece — behold here twice thirty against blood nullified on all sides — to reconcile. However, in the body of the matter how will roasting help after soaking, since kevush is like cooking, and Pri Chadash challenged Mechaber himself seif 18 ruled in unperforated vessel what was kevush in brine, roasting does not help — however on this question one may say Mechaber is uncertain whether kevush is fully like cooking and roasting does not help, or not, and here that it soaked in water many hold in water is not kevush specifically in vinegar and brine, and many hold only three days; therefore he combined this with that and permitted roasting, but below in brine he did not want to rely on the lenient view that roasting helps in kevush alone; but the essence of the law needs teaching what roasting helps — it seems Mechaber holds kevush acts to cook to expel or absorb, but to expel and absorb together is judged like kevush like boiling pot that did not cool from its soaking and is like a pot that did not cool from boiling, and many poskim hold it only acts to expel or absorb, not both together — therefore they are lenient in roasting, unlike below seif 20 where brine full of blood is before you and piece is kevush in it and soaking acts to absorb brine blood and one action makes clear forbidden, therefore roasting does not help.
