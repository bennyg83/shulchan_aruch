Meat soiled with blood. Meaning it did not yet expel its blood through melicha — and this law Rama wrote seif 1 — see what is written there:
Unless there is sixty in the water against it. That sixty helps here; and above seif 11 Rama concludes some forbid that piece — behold here, since it is pickling, it is like cooked; above Rama distinguished in Torat Chatat that nevertheless it is not literally permitted like cooking; and Rashal disagrees and wrote do not rely on him to permit, although R' Meir wrote to permit — perhaps R' Meir holds even cooked without melicha the piece itself is permitted, as his student Rosh ruled; but we, who forbid, likewise for roasting; end of his words; and it appears Rama follows his view, that he ruled even for cooking to permit in great need; therefore for roasting one may be lenient in all respects; but Rashal ruled for cooking stringently in all respects, therefore he was stringent also for roasting; and it appears one may rely to be lenient as Rama ruled here:
