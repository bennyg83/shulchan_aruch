And if, etc. According to Ra'osh's explanation of what is written: a bowl in which he salted, etc. — it deals with an unperforated vessel.
And if he used, etc. As stated in Pesachim 76a:
(Likkut) And if he used it, etc. Even according to Ra'avad, who wrote that a bowl is always hot even after rinsing, and they are in it — nevertheless since both are absorbed, peeling suffices, as stated in Pesachim chapter Keitzad Tzolin (76a) that this coating is different, etc.; and see in Tur 73a and there all the difficulties in this book are explained at length, end of his words; and he wrote there that according to Ra'avad, if he put something wet like gravy, all of it is forbidden even cold gravy — but Tosafot wrote that after rinsing the bowl is cold and peeling suffices, and only hot; and so it appears, for forbidden to eat hot in it he said — but cold is permitted, etc.; and what is written "and if it is," etc. — meaning hot, and according to Ri in Tosafot there (end of Likkut).
And if, etc. According to Ri in Tosafot there s.v. tania.
(Likkut) Sixty are required, etc. As everywhere there, and according to him peeling, etc.; and Rashba challenged there: may one l'chatchila put in it and taste peeling — and he answered because forbidden implies even b'dieved; and on this he asks "and according to him," etc. And from "and according to him" it implies his measure is rinsing, and that is such a case (end of Likkut).
