And what we say there is no melicha for vessels — Beit Yosef explained in the name of Hagahot Shach Devarim: what entered does not exit through melicha, but through cooking it exits; although salting is like hot — meaning like hot of roasting, not like cooking, end of his words; and the reason forbidden to use that vessel hot specifically, and cold permitted: he holds the bowl after melicha is considered cold — therefore permitted cold without rinsing. And one may distinguish for a knife in which he slaughtered above — there through knife pressure it expels; and some hold the bowl is considered hot from melicha, and the law is like a slaughtering knife — there knife pressure, here melicha's boiling; and Torat Chatat wrote: and to me it appears the law of this bowl is like other treif vessels in which permitted was placed — the lower prevails applies; therefore if the bowl is cold and he puts hot in it, peeling suffices; and if gravy, we require sixty against peeling; and if heated below or both hot, everything forbidden if there is not sixty against peeling of the whole bowl, end of his words:
