To salt in it a second time. In Chamudei Daniel siman 39 wrote that salt of arnunit that dried is forbidden to salt meat in it, and even b'dieved one should forbid, for it is inferior to salt in which meat was salted, since it became pickled from above in tzir — see there. [And see in responsum Shavot Yaakov part 3 siman 66 regarding water in which meat was rinsed that spilled on salt — what to do with the salt; he ruled that he should leave it to dry and afterward may salt meat in it to expel its blood, but to put in food forbidden; and if it is much salt he should not leave in his house lest they err to put it in food — see there.]
