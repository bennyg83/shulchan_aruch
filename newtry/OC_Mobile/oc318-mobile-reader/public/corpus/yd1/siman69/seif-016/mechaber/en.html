One may salt only in a perforated vessel or on straw and stubble or in a sloped place such that if water is poured there it exits immediately. {Rama: And a smooth board — water flows from it — one need not place it on a slope; but if it is not smooth one must place it on a slope so the water exits (Shaarei Dura and in Beit Yosef in the name of Teshuvot HaRosh siman 172). And even in a perforated vessel one should be careful l'chatchila that the holes be open (HaAroch general rule 3). Therefore they do not stand it on the ground, for it is like a non-perforated vessel (see below siman 70). And likewise they are somewhat stringent to place straw or stubble inside the perforated vessel, for the meat will block the holes (hagahot of Ashiri part 25 and HaAroch general rule 1). And b'dieved one need not be concerned for all this. (Another opinion). }And if he salted in a non-perforated vessel, forbidden to use that vessel for something boiling. {Rama: And if he used it, requires peeling if it is a dry matter (Rashba). And if it is a wet matter we require sixty against the peel from the bowl (Beit Yosef in the name of one authority). }And some say that even cold it is forbidden to use without rinsing; and if he used without rinsing, he rinses what he used. {Rama: But permitted to return and salt meat in it after perforating it, or even without perforating if the meat already remained in its melicha and was rinsed (Beit Yosef in the name of hagahot of Shach Devarim and HaAroch).}
