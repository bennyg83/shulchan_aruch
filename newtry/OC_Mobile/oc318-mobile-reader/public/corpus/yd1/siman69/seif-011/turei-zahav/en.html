For what exited was nullified in sixty, and what remains in it is blood of limbs that did not separate; and Maharach challenged: behold in breaking the neckbone of an animal siman 67 even Rosh holds forbidden because it separated from place to place — and the same should be forbidden here; and he answered there — see on him; and one may distinguish: here in cooking, whatever separated exits outside, and whatever did not exit outside does not separate at all:
And some forbid, etc. Because the piece was cooked in its blood; and one may answer: behold blood from outside that fell on a piece in a pot part of which is in the gravy is permitted even what is outside the gravy — here is different, for the prohibition is because of itself, the taste remained in its place and is forbidden; and further, it is considered adherent prohibition, and one should be concerned lest it once rose outside the gravy before expelling all its blood, end of his words — Orchot Chaim:
For honor of Shabbat, etc. In Torat Chatat general rule 2 he wrote: in a chicken that is hollow, one should be concerned it separated from place to place and is forbidden in such a case even in pressing need and great loss:
And it was not salted properly, etc. Orchot Chaim wrote two laws: one, salted and did not remain the measure of melicha; two, if he salted only one side — in both it is as if not salted at all — and these are the forbidders Rama brings seif 4; but Rama decided there b'dieved permitted with one side salted, and likewise if he did not spread salt everywhere; and if so why is sixty required here, as if not salted at all?
