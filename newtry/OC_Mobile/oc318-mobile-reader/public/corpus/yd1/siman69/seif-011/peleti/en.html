Meat that was cooked — a woman cooked meat and forgot if she salted it first before cooking, and the Rav Taz permitted, for it is only d'rabbanan safek lekula; and also like safek in reading in the second chapter "that they may increase," etc., we say she is accustomed — so this woman in her habit certainly salted; and Rav author of Noda BiYehudah challenged, for itchezk issura; and see Pri Chadash. And behold, if itchezk issura, does d'rabbanan safek lekula help — see below general rules on double safek what I wrote there, and from there seek; and it seems if the woman attributes her error to something that is certainly without doubt why she forgot and how this safek came — it seems clear to answer we say in many places Pesachim 16: chaver died and left produce — they are in a presumption of being fixed, and the Gemara inferred from this safek removes certainty; and Tosafot Niddah 15b s.v. chaver wrote this safek removes certainty is a good safek, and he is accustomed, therefore it pushes off certainty; and the Gemara also said therefore presumption of being fixed, for certainly he does not remove from his hand what is not fixed; and also this too is safek that perhaps he put in refuse, etc. And clearly if we find a pot of cooked meat and the cook is not before us to ask if she salted the meat, obviously the meat is permitted to eat — we never heard she must ask a woman cook if she salted; and as with chametz, presumption the house was checked — all are colleagues regarding salting to salt meat; if so, behold safek removes certainty, even though presumption was initially not salted and safek it was salted — like presumption fixed to separate tithes from what I wrote to salt, and like there they permitted, so permit here, and there is no distinction at all. And if we say here is a flaw — how did she forget a great action of salting — say on the other side, how did safek arise whether she salted; what brought it to this safek? Say she did a great action; but go learn from safek whether he prayed and certainty he did not — he does not return to pray, prayer is d'rabbanan; although certainly obligated, if safek whether he prayed, how does certainty remove safek? Also how forget such great action whether he prayed — requires favor of heart; do they speak of feeble-hearted outwardly? All the more liable to remember salting which hands are busy all day. And if you say prayer is different lest he bless in vain — here concern for blessing in vain; challenge one explanation Pesachim that even in d'rabbanan safek does not remove certainty; if so why exempt prayer and Kriat Shema — strain that when they enacted d'rabbanan like prayer and Kriat Shema they enacted for certainty not safek; in any case in such a case safek removes certainty, and all the more according to Tosafot there that "his animal" is all d'oraisa, but d'rabbanan nevertheless obligated in tithes and certainly so, and Gemara concedes in d'rabbanan safek removes certainty. And Taz's words are clear. However, still in its place as Shach wrote, what is the alarm — take kafila to taste whether taste of blood, certainly she forgot, and if not certainly she did not forget; and because we are stringent not to rely on kafila in this we are lenient in the deed? One must say as I wrote, every piece is full of blood but they forbade only blood smeared on top; therefore even if taster tastes blood, no proof it was not salted; and according to what I wrote this is category safek removes certainty, no question, for he can clarify by asking her mouth; in any case no need to clarify, no need kafila tasting. And so Chullin 97, Gemara challenged: if so, if it fell into another pot it should not be forbidden; Tosafot: even though safek whether all exited in first pot or only part, d'rabbanan safek lekula. Understand: in falling into another pot that Gemara forbids, he tasted kafila — what d'rabbanan safek here, kafila tasting would decide; one must say Gemara's question where no kafila, and if we rely on d'rabbanan safek; if so even for us who do not rely on kafila, as if no kafila, rely on d'rabbanan safek — requires further study to be lenient, for one may reject: Gemara's question if pot increased now no milk taste, nevertheless forbidden since initially less than sixty and nevela; unlike if safek d'rabbanan lekula, never forbidden; but regarding tasting, in truth we do not rely — if possible to taste; therefore requires further study. Tzitz Eliezer brought in Kereti: if safek whether milk salted with meat — d'oraisa safek, species in different species forbidden; Pri Chadash agreed. And I say: if he peels meat — permitted, for milk spreads in salting per most poskim does not spread, peel suffices; see Turei Chayim general rule 38, below siman 105 Shach s.k. 33 many authorities hold does not spread more than peel; even stringent did not decide; certainly to spread until stops even in sixty requires removal; see siman 105. If so, double safek: perhaps no milk contact, and if contact perhaps does not spread more than peel; if unknown which place touched until peel place unknown, one in two nullified, see siman 92 Shach. What I wrote in Peleti, do not doubt. However as Shach wrote, milk in meat is species in different species, clarified by gentile tasting — how say lekula we do not rely on him? It seems from poskim salting taste is weak, impossible to taste; see Rambam Hilchot Maachalot Assurot 15:34 — salted nevelah mixed with slaughtered meat forbidden because salt became nevelah, etc., impossible to stand on taste or issur — requires further study. Tzitz Eliezer: if safek milk safek fat, species in species nullified d'oraisa, only d'rabbanan safek lekula; Pri Chadash questioned two safekot to be stringent, perhaps salted together, perhaps milk salted not fat — I did not understand: in the end safek whether milk salted — if not, permitted, d'rabbanan safek — what difference two safekot or not, why be stringent in d'rabbanan issur?
That piece — lest not all exited outside the piece, only spread place to place — and one should understand: nevertheless blood in cooking is d'rabbanan, if so d'rabbanan safek lekula, and itchezk issura did not apply at all, for from here we would say spread place to place — behold blood is absorbed in place. And strained to say reason they forbid: perhaps can be clarified through kafila and tasting — not mentioned in poskim. Therefore one may say certainly blood exited as nature of cooking, only we are concerned before exit it remained in the piece and spread place to place and the piece became nevela, and even after exit, possible to squeeze forbidden — therefore that piece remains in its issur, therefore kafila taste does not help, for it is not there, only exited exited; and this reasoning Tosafot wrote Chullin 96b s.v. vekhelev: chelev in chelev before it exited from chelev forbids the meat — the same here. And do not challenge: if so in every piece salted before all expulsion finished, spread place to place and nevela — say: even though melicha power to expel all blood is blood that did not spread, but to absorb taste of blood it is not absorbed through salish, only kli rishon, and issur vel heter does not apply.
