Meat that was cooked without melicha requires sixty in the dish against that meat, and then all is permitted. {Rama: And some forbid that piece even where there are sixty against the piece (Tur in the name of one authority and hagahot of Maimoniyot in the name of Semak and hagahot of Shach Devarim and HaAroch and Maharai and Mordechai in the name of Raavan) and so is the custom if not for need such as honoring Shabbat or honoring guests, for then one may rely on the lenient authorities (Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim and another opinion). And if a piece was salted and not salted properly, its law is as if not salted at all; and likewise meat that remained three days without melicha — even if salted, its law is as if not salted; and if cooked, requires sixty against it (Orchot Hayim).}
