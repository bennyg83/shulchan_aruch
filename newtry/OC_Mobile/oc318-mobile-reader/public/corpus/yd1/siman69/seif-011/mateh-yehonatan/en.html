(there seif 15) Soiled meat, etc., they say to eat it only roasted, etc. And although below seif 18 that if pickled in tzir it never comes out through roasting, and what is written here that it comes out through roasting — one may distinguish: there, since already expelled through melicha, the expulsion did not come through tzir only absorption; therefore we say absorbed so strongly it does not come out through roasting; Rama holds not to distinguish in such a case.
