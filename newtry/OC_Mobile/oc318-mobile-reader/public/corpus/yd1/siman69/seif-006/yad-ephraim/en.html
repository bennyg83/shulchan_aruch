Approximately one-third of an hour. Be'er Heitev; and see the words of R' Alexander Ziskind in chapter Shabbat in Pesachim, that what Taharat HaAdam brought from there as proof for salting period being one hour is no proof at all, for although Rosh wrote like roasting period, nevertheless roasting itself does not require an hour, only with its preparation we give an hour — see there; and see Be'er Yaakov; and see in Chavot Yair that regarding mil period in salting one needs like walking two-fifths of an hour, which is twenty-four minutes — see there. And it seems since the reason is to be stringent d'oraisa as written in Mevaser Shemesh according to Rambam and Raavad in Pesachim, and we hold blood in cooking is only d'rabbanan, we are lenient.
