(There in the hagahah) And in one of them it suffices, or in speaking offhand, etc. Or going out and coming in, for he is afraid. And it is obvious that for a mumar speaking offhand does not apply when he knows that issur and heter depend on this, unless one finds cases as Taz and Shach wrote s.k. 42 — see there. And it seems to me that fear also does not apply to a mumar, for he supposes they hold him as a full Jew and will not check after him, as Tosafot and Rosh and Shach wrote at the beginning of the first chapter of Chullin (daf 3) regarding knife inspection for a mumar. And here is a kal v'chomer from there, for there it is a d'oraisa issur and nevertheless the mumar supposes they will not check after him because they hold him as a Jew even for a d'oraisa issur; all the more here, where it is only a d'rabbanan issur as I wrote, that it is blood that was salted — see there.
