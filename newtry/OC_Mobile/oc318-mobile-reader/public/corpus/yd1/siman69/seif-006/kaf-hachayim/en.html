(19) There in hagahah. But if he did not soak, only rinsed well, it suffices for him. And accordingly it appears that for honoring guests or erev Shabbat when time is short, which is b'dieved as below seif 6 in hagahah, it suffices to rinse well by hand on all sides and to salt. Mekor Chaim seif 12. And likewise Chayei Adam general rule 30 seif 3: for great need where he has no time to soak half an hour, he rinses very very well in water and rubs well and returns and rinses in water a second time until the waters remain white. And likewise Darkei Teshuvah seif 22 in the name of several poskim; especially according to what is written above seif 15 that according to the Shulchan Aruch soaking is not required — if so, wherever there is need and no time, one may be lenient only to wash and rub the meat well until rinse-waters are white, and also see if there are hardened blood drops on the meat to remove them at the time of washing.
