(there seif 12) [Hagahah of Rav the third, etc., R' Shlomo HaKohen, Vilna. And this is his wording.] Behold in this law it is explained in Beit Yosef in the name of Semak who wrote another reason because he is concerned for cleanliness; and Tur omitted this reason and one must understand why; it appears at first glance difficult: why does the reason of cleanliness help — rinsing once suffices for cleanliness! However for the law we require two rinsings as poskim wrote from "and rinse it very well." However in truth Semak goes according to his view that b'dieved one rinsing suffices as in Hagahot Maimoniyot on his name brought in Beit Yosef. However Tur may hold that even b'dieved two rinsings are required, as appears from Hagahot Ashiri that it is required even b'dieved, therefore he omitted this, for according to his view this reason does not help at all. And further: there is concern that even though he rinsed, perhaps he placed the meat in the vessel before putting water, and Tur's view on this is that even b'dieved forbidden, for he wrote that if he salted in coarse salt and did not shake or rinse in water first, not forbidden because water nullifies the power of salt, etc. — implying only when there is water in the vessel permitted b'dieved; if not, not. Therefore he omitted it. Unlike Semak who may hold that even if no water at all in the vessel, also not forbidden b'dieved as Rama wrote in hagahah in the name of Shach Devarim and Orchot Hayim, and the reason of cleanliness helps for his view. End hagahah of Rav R' Shlomo HaKohen.]
