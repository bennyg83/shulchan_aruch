The measure of staying in salt is not less than the span of a mil, which is approximately a third of an hour. {Rama: And on this one may rely b'dieved or even l'chatchila for honoring guests or Shabbat need; but otherwise the custom is to remain in melicha the measure of an hour and one should not change (Maharai in Teshuvot HaRosh siman 167 and hagahot of Shach Devarim and Orchot Hayim general rule 1).}
