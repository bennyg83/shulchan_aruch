Be'er Heitev, that Shach brought and in s.k. 96 the adder added from Taz, and they are words contradicting each other; and what he wrote in s.k. 96 that Taz is lenient — see in the Acharonim. And in Rav Meshulam siman 15 in Likutei HaGaon, the author of Chavatzalet HaSharon wrote he saw written in the name of the Gaon Rabbi Moshe of Radin, may his memory be blessed: a piece of meat salted on one side is permitted only if the salted side is on top, but if below — forbidden, for blood does not spread upward — and this is an error; and between going and coming it was switched for him, for certainly salt draws blood and pulls it to the place where it is, whether from below to above or the reverse; and the sense testifies, but regarding spreading, meaning to convey from it onward, blood has no power to go from heat of salting from below to above, only to move from above to below — see there, and it is clear the listener heard and erred and hung empty words on the Gaon Rabbi Moshe, may his memory be blessed; and we merited to discuss it in Sefer Tiferet Moshe, and the viewer will see he never had such a thing in mind; his words refer to what is said here that after beforehand there is no repair in second salting, for the unsalted side will absorb — Taz wrote there is repair in salting again and the salted side should be below so it does not spread upward, only he must know for certain it did not flip, for by default it tends to flip — see there, what he wrote regarding intestines salted: there is repair to return and salt from inside, and we are not concerned the inner side will absorb since there is no brine, therefore there one does not need even l'chatchila to salt only the outer side alone — see there. And in Shiyurei Berachah s.k. 23 he stood on this and wrote it depends on the dispute of Shach and Mei Yisrael regarding the reason of spreading a peel's depth — see there. And in my humble opinion, in such a case where it is unknown, one may be lenient because of the peel, only Shulchan Aruch does not deal with repair such as needing to stand over it lest it flip at all; nevertheless if it is great loss, one may do so with care not to flip, since otherwise the Mechaber's view is to be lenient regarding salted on one side.
