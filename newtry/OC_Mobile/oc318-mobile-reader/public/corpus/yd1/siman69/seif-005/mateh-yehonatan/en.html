(seif 10) An idolater serving in an Israelite's house, etc. Shach s.k. 42, and according to his words one must say he does not disagree with siman 134, etc. In haste it is not comparable to there where presumption of prohibition was established, unlike here where there was no prohibition on the meat at all, and before explaining even blood was permitted; nevertheless since presumption was established in blood, properly called presumption of prohibition; what compelled this was necessarily called impossible, otherwise why need the reason of fear and masiach lefi tumo — otherwise permitted as rabbinic doubt leniently; nevertheless one must say presumption of prohibition, and where impossible, only rabbinic doubt leniently.
