If he cut — Pri Chadash challenged these things Mordechai wrote: through knife pressure it expels, but he and Tosafot and many authors hold specifically with heat of the slaughterhouse we say so, not knife pressure alone. And one may say the essence there is to absorb and expel inside, and from a hard thing like a knife; but to expel and absorb only regarding soft meat we say even without heat of the slaughterhouse it absorbs, and do not challenge this view that holds when he cuts he must return to rinse because of knife expulsion — if so how did the Gemara say the butcher needs two knives, one to cut meat and one to cut chelev, for with one knife we fear he cuts chelev first; and Ran wrote it deals where they slaughtered in the slaughterhouse, so no further rinse is needed — otherwise what of it that he cut chelev first, in any case he needs initial rinse and wipes the chelev; and according to this view that when he cuts he always needs initial rinse, Ran's question on the Gemara is difficult, that always if he cuts he needs rinse — what is there to fear, and one must say these authorities hold like those who hold below siman 76 that roasting does not require initial rinse, for fire draws even visible blood — see below siman 66; and if so one should fear he cuts chelev first and afterward cuts meat with it and chelev smears on it and afterward he roasts the meat and there is no rinsing at all — examine.
