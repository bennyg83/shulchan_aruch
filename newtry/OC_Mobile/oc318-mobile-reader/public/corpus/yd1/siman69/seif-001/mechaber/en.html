Laws of melicha and hadacha. And it contains 21 seifim. One must rinse the meat before melicha (and if the butcher rinsed it, one need not rinse it in the house) (Tur). And if after he rinsed he cut each piece into two or three (or removed the hooves of the legs after the rinsing) (HaAroch general rule 4 and 17) he must return and rinse them.
{Rama: And if he did not do so it is as if not rinsed at all (there). The rinsing of meat l'chatchila he should be careful to soak it for up to half an hour and to rinse it well in the soak-water; but if he did not soak it, only rinsed it well, it suffices for him; and afterward he should wait a little until the water drips off before salting so the salt not dissolve from the water and not expel blood (hagahot of Shach Devarim and hagahot of Ashiri part 25 and in HaAroch general rule 1). And they are accustomed not to use other things in the vessel in which they soak meat; and if the meat was delayed in its soaking from time to time, the meat and also the vessel are forbidden — and see below in this siman; but less than from time to time one need not be stringent; and even where the vessel became forbidden, it is permitted to return and soak in it (HaAroch) — law of meat full of ice — how they conduct with its soaking — see above siman 68.}
