(Siman 69 seif 1) If after rinsing he cut each piece into two or three, he must rinse them; and if he did not do so it is as if he did not rinse. The reason in Mordechai: through knife pressure it expels visible blood. According to my reasoning he holds the reason for initial rinsing is visible blood on the surface, that salt becomes full from it and does not expel blood; therefore it seems sufficient with thorough rinsing without soaking, for the meat is already softened, and one need only remove the visible blood from the place he cut. Now it is clear there is no contradiction with what the hagahah wrote at the end of this siman seif 20, that one may cut during the salting period if he wishes to rinse there and return to salt there — see there and examine. And so too in Lechem HaPanim and Bedek HaBayit. And if he cut only from one side, presumably there is sixty as I wrote.
