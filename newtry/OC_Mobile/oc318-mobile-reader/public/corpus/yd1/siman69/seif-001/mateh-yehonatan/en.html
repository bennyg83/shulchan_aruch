(Siman 69 seif 1) The Mechaber's wording "and if after he rinsed he cut each piece into two," etc. This law is from the Mordechai, and his proof from that stated in chapter 1 of Chullin that through the pressure of the knife it absorbs — even according to the one who says the place of slaughter is cold, likewise here through the pressure of the knife it expels. He challenges: did he not himself rule above that even according to the one who says the place of slaughter is cold — i.e. the place of slaughter is not completely hot but nevertheless is somewhat hot and through pressure it absorbs — not so here that it is completely cold; because of the pressure of the knife he says it does not expel. One may say that here, since it need only expel from meat that is soft, it is different. One may say that the pressure of the knife alone also expels, and his proof is thus: as in hot somewhat the pressure of the knife expels completely what was absorbed, likewise here that it is not absorbed so much in it, the pressure of the knife suffices to expel even when completely cold.
