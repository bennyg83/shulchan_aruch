Permitted to salt in it. See in Chamudei Daniel siman 10 who wrote on this: one must put salt on the meat that it be somewhat thick; and if coarse salt is available, forbidden to salt with fine salt even if he puts much, for the first part of the salt that drew near to the meat enters the meat and does not act in it. He further wrote that one must be careful that through grinding much of the salt becomes like flour, and when he salts with all the salt together, much of the meat does not receive from the fine salt; and regarding b'dieved it appears from his words that even if he salted all the meat with fine salt alone and did not add until somewhat thick, permitted — see there.
