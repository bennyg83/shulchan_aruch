For the water in the vessel. Be'er Heitev; Noda BiYehudah part 2 siman 41: meat still in its salting period that fell into water in which the meat soaked before salting, within the salting period — certainly permitted, not to speak of if there is no concern of unperforated vessel such as salting on a grate over wood as Shach wrote; but even if it fell on the grate's edges where water is inside, permitted because water nullifies the salt's power. However, even if it fell after the salting period, it seems to permit, and one whose heart struck him in any case should salt again even though it already expelled its blood, for since expulsion holes sealed, etc., and this is not so agreed; and see Tzitz Eliezer siman 8, and fit to rely on it in such a case; but he should be careful to salt alone and not with other meat. And see Beit Yosef s.v. an incident that was salted, etc., and in Pri Chadash end of siman 70 he permits too, and the posek may rely on this plainly — see there.
