And the whole pot combines for sixty. The Mechaber's words too must be explained thus; and what he wrote 'there must be sixty in it' does not mean there must be sixty in it alone against the salt on it (as Maharshal's view, which I will write below) — for even according to those who hold a piece becomes nevelah even in other prohibitions, and when the prohibition is adherent we require sixty against the whole piece, as below siman 72 seif 3 in hagahah regarding heart attached to a bird: if there is not sixty in the bird against the heart, we require sixty against the whole bird — nevertheless here it is not called adherent prohibition, as Mordechai chapter 25 and Hagahot Shach Devarim wrote, see there — Ra'avan in Sefer Tzafnat Pa'aneach and Orchot Chaim general rule 11 din 4 and Torat Chatat there: as soon as the piece enters water, all the salt and blood on it melts and falls into the pot; and further, the Mechaber holds we do not say a piece becomes nevelah except in meat and milk; and Tur also wrote there must be sixty in the pot to nullify all the salt, and sixty against the whole piece are not required, end of his words — and so many poskim; and Maharshal in Orchot Shabbat chapter 8 and in his book chapter 35 siman 30 expanded greatly to rule that according to what we hold, in adherent prohibition we require sixty against the whole piece — here too we require sixty in the pot against the whole piece, and nevertheless the piece itself is forbidden; and he pressed greatly against the lenient rulings, see there — and in my books I rejected all his words, and he did not aim well at Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of R' Meir and at Maharai's words on which he built his foundation; and the main point: R' Meir holds in the piece itself there is sixty to nullify the blood and salt on it, and one need not combine the gravy in the pot; and the forbidders hold there is not sixty in the piece itself, therefore one needs combination of the gravy — and so we are accustomed; and in this all his words were rejected — examine; and I proved this from Hagahot Shach Devarim in siman 1 and added clear proofs to rule like the Rav's words; also I brought there Shach Devarim and Semak and Mordechai and Kol Bo and Orchot Chaim and Maharil, who ruled in general in adherent prohibition one requires sixty against the whole piece and the piece itself is forbidden — and here they ruled like the Rav; also I wrote there it appears Maharai's view agrees, see there — and so all decisors are accustomed:
