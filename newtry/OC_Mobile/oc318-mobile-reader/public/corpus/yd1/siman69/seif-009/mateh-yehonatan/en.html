(seif 13) Meat that was salted and a doubt whether salted within three days — permitted. One may be lenient according to what Taz wrote s.k. 4, incident of a woman who cooked meat and forgot if she salted, and she was permitted because blood in cooking is rabbinic and sixty rabbinic leniently; if so, even a doubt perhaps not salted at all is also permitted, for blood that was salted is only rabbinic and doubt leniently; if so why say specifically within three days permitted because it is stringency of Geonim — even without this reason permitted because rabbinic doubt leniently. One may say: since we hold melicha forbids only as much as a peel, salt has no more power than a peel, and we do not say blood that was salted is definitely rabbinic; therefore without the Geonic stringency I would say sixty stringently; and what Taz wrote sixty leniently is specifically after cooking. Therefore one may answer what Maharshal challenged on Rabbenu Yerucham that he need not give reason that blood in cooking is rabbinic, for from melicha too it would be d'oraisa forbidden — according to my words one may say Rabbenu Yerucham wants a reason that the idolater is believed even on salting meat, i.e. if the idolater says he salted, the law is thus; if so compelled to reason it is like blood in cooking, for if because of melicha more than a peel, not blood that was salted; requires further study.
