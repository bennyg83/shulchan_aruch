Meat that was salted and cooked without a final rinsing requires sixty against the salt on it. {Rama: And the whole pot combines for sixty (Shach Devarim and see s.k. 34). And if in the pot there is as much as the piece that was salted and not rinsed, all is permitted, for certainly there are sixty against the salt on the piece, for the piece itself is certainly thirty against the salt on it (HaAroch general rule 11 law 4). And if there are not sixty in the pot against the salt, even if placed only in a kli sheni, all is forbidden, for since there is salt and tzir there, even in a kli sheni it cooks (there law 1 and in Mordechai). And dry meat may be permitted even in a kli rishon, for certainly there is sixty against the salt on it since it already dried (Mordechai chapter 25 and Ramban and Beit Yosef in the name of hagahot of Ashiri and Shach Devarim). But l'chatchila he should be careful even with dry meat not to cook it or rinse it in water where yad soledes bo applies without a final rinsing (Shach Devarim there). And all this deals when he did not rinse finally at all; but if he rinsed only once and cooked thus, permitted, for b'dieved it suffices with one rinsing finally (Maharai in hagahot of Shach Devarim). Salt that meat was salted in once is forbidden to salt in a second time (Kol Bo and another opinion and the opinion of most poskim). And all the more so forbidden to eat the salt after meat was salted in it.}
