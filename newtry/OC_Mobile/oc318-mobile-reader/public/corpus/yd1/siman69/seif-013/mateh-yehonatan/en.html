(there seif 20) Meat that was salted and remained, etc. Taz s.k. 4 holds that after measure of melicha it is no longer called boiling and even tzir is not boiling; in my opinion it appears from Rosh that at least tzir is boiling even after measure of melicha — in Rashi's incident the reason not to forbid tzir is only mohal, etc., and although salt on meat forbidden because blood mixed in it, therefore must rinse meat well, etc. — either blood in salt dried inside and has no power to exit, or power ceased because it expelled and salt no longer considered boiling; hastily one may answer the latter: why say above that after measure melicha not blood only mohal, permitted because no boiling at all; nevertheless after measure tzir is boiling, therefore say tzir is only mohal and nullifies boiling power of tzir on meat that exited during measure of melicha; therefore proved not like Rama and Taz as written.
