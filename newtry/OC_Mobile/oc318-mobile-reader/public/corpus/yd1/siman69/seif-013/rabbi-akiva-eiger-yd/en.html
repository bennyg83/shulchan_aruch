(there) He returns and salts the other side. In my opinion one should examine: what helps melicha of the other side? For the side already salted and remained the measure of melicha — the power of salt ceased, and if so it is as if not salted; if so, now in the second melicha also only salted on one side! It was fitting to salt the whole again on both sides, for it is clear we do not say that salting one side helps for thickness, for if the piece were cut in two it would be permitted — this is not implied; rather we say salt's power enters only a little into thickness sometimes like the thickness of a finger, etc.; or one may say the first melicha expelled blood from thickness of a finger, salt's power ceased, and now when salting the other side it also expels like thickness of a finger and the middle remains in its blood — requires further study.
