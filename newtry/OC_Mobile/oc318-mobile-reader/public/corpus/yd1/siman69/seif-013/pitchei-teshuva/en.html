For honoring guests. He wrote in Solet LaMinchah general rule 15 in the name of Turei Even: where honoring guests is mentioned, that is such as Torah scholars and men of good reputation and orphans, or a poor man of good family, or wealthy men fit to honor who do not do evil — not the wicked or misers who pass through for prey and food; they are in the category of charity — see there. He further wrote that called "guests" only those who lodged in his house, but not when he invited his friend. And he disagrees on this and concludes that l'chatchila he should not invite his friend if he knows he must be lenient regarding rinsing or melicha; but if he already invited him, then one may be lenient as because of other guests — end of his words, see there.
