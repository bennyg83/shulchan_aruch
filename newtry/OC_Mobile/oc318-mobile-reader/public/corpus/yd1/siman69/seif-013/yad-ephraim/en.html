Be'er Heitev; and see Turei Chayim general rule 2, who wrote thus; and although above he wrote regarding great loss it is permitted, nevertheless here plainly, and it is possible he holds here one should forbid that piece even in great loss, since its blood congealed inside it — properly one should be concerned lest cooking softened it somewhat so that through cooking it spread from place to place, and nevertheless it does not exit outside; and even the Mechaber concedes in this according to Taz's reason, what spread exited outside; even according to what I wrote below Taz's reason — requires further study. And the essence reason is as Rosh ruled, that spreading place to place in the piece itself is permitted; if so, here too one may permit in great loss. And so Pri Chadash, that for the Mechaber the piece itself is permitted; and see in s.k. 96.
