Safek whether it was salted, etc. — and if the safek is in the animal when it was slaughtered, such as by way of parable day four meat before us and we do not know if slaughtered day one and three days passed, or day two and still within three days — say establish the animal on presumption of life, and now that it died; and so in sugya Niddah, Ketubot, Kiddushin, and many places: establish live on presumption of life; all the more if we saw it alive before — it is as if we saw it alive from evening. And in this I will answer: since it is only stringency of Geonim, they were lenient, for such is the law.
