(Taz s.k. 15) Since there is heter otherwise in roasting. In my opinion: if so, after twelve hours why not salt? Let us also say since this matter of measure of tzir expulsion twelve hours is not agreed — the primary view is more like poskim that its measure is twenty-four hours; here too, since there is heter otherwise in a place of great loss, rely on them that measure of tzir expulsion is twenty-four hours.
