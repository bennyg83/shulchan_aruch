Be'er Heitev; and in Shiyurei Berachah part 1 regarding goose fat that was not rinsed for three days, one may be lenient to roast through salting as its law; and in any case scrape the skins well from the meat stuck to the fat, also cut and rinse and salt them — see there, brought in Turei Meir siman 75. However, in Noda BiYehudah siman 24 he scolded the posek for permitting this, and wrote that if he ruled to permit the meat he is certainly intentional to feed forbidden matter, for even Shiyurei Berachah permits only the fat alone and not the meat, and there was no room to err; and they scold him and remove him from ruling further. However, even the posek who permits the fat alone should be scolded, and if the act was done and the fat was rendered and cooked, the vessels and all mixtures are forbidden, unlike Shiyurei Berachah, for although fat is not presumed with blood, nevertheless there is blood in it; and further, goose skin is not all fat, for even a thin goose has a skin membrane, and that membrane certainly has blood, and the Acharonim spoke only regarding fat in intestines that is only fat alone; and Shivat Tziyon wrote all poultry skin is fat — this is not so, etc. — see there; and see below siman 75 what I wrote there regarding Elazil salted on one side. And in responsum Or Ne'elam siman 19 regarding fat of a hen that remained three days without salting and rinsing, and the woman salted and rinsed and rendered the fat and made foods for Shabbat and also sold to others who made dishes for Shabbat, and it became known it remained three days — he wrote to permit the remainder of the dishes and vessels because of double safek: perhaps there was sixty in the dishes, for perhaps there is not so much blood in the dish; and also perhaps it exited through salting; and in any case not so much remained that it cannot be nullified; and this is not safek lacking knowledge that is safek for the whole generation; and also it is d'rabbanan safek as Taz wrote in safek whether she salted; and although Noda BiYehudah disagrees because itchezk issura, one may distinguish: there the safek is on the piece, unlike here where the fat dissolved in the pot. And it seems Noda BiYehudah may also concede here; and as is clear from his words regarding fat in intestines there is more reason to be lenient, for there is no skin; and perhaps in great loss one may permit the mixtures; and in any case one who relies to be lenient to permit the vessels after prior use certainly did not lose; and in Givat Shaul siman 2 regarding a fat goose that remained three days without salting and has no heter except roasting — forbidden to place a vessel under it to receive the grease; and b'dieved if they placed a vessel to receive the fat and it was already half roasted, permit b'dieved like cooking after roasting b'dieved; and although Beit Yosef gives reason of nullification in gravy, here perhaps there is no sixty against the blood that exits in the grease; nevertheless b'dieved one may rely on Beit Yosef's reason that all its blood exited through roasting and therefore permitted after cooking, and the same here. And see Machatzit HaShekel what he wrote on Bedek HaBayit and Pri Megadim and raised that regarding goose that remained three days one should forbid even in great loss; but if he did not give initial rinse, be lenient in such a case — see there; and see in the small seif after this.
