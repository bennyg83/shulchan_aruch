Meaning: if it mixed with other pieces that had not yet remained three days and were not yet salted — permitted to cook all of them when he salts all of them; or it deals when one was salted after remaining three days and the others were salted within three days — permitted to cook all without melicha, and we do not say it is a davar sheyesh lo matirin in roasting, for we hold even in a thousand it is not nullified — for roasting it was never forbidden; and what is permitted to cook all of them — specifically each by itself, or even all in one pot when he increases permitted over it up to sixty — if not so, not as below siman 109 seif 2 — see there; and why Rama did not write here to throw one away or give to a gentile — because this is only stringency of the Geonim, one should not be stringent so far — Shach's wording:
To honor. For its prohibition is only from blood absorbed in it. (And blood is not fit to honor; and why it is not davar sheyesh lo matirin: because even though it has permission for roasting, nevertheless there is no permission for what is forbidden now, meaning for a pot):
Melicha. Shach wrote: requires further study, for we hold below in siman 109 that min b'she'eino mino requires sixty because if he cooks them it gives taste — if so, why does majority suffice here, for blood will give taste when he cooks them; granted for the Mechaber one may answer, for he holds since meat that remained three days is only stringency of the Geonim they did not be stringent so much; but Rama wrote that such is the law when cooked without melicha, that this is forbidden by law — difficult why it should be nullified in a majority; and it is possible it only comes to inform us it is not forbidden because of a piece fit to honor, and 'majority' is not precise rather sixty is needed — requires further study.
