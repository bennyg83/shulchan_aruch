An idolater serving in an Israelite's house placed meat in a pot and it is not known whether he rinsed it — if the idolater knows Jewish practice, we rely on his words if there was an Israelite going in and out or any minor of understanding. {Rama: And one of these suffices (Tur). Or through masiach lefi tumo that he rinsed it well, or an Israelite even a minor going in and out, for he is afraid since he knows Jewish customs; however if he warned the idolater not to rinse without permission and he transgressed his words, forbidden, for we see he is not afraid and one may not rely on his words either (hagahot of Shach Devarim and Orchot Hayim).}
