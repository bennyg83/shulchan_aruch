Even b'dieved. Be'er Heitev and in Chokhmat Adam siman 36 wrote regarding an incident where they salted meat in salt mixed with groats a third or quarter — forbidden, since they are accustomed otherwise that there is empty space, and when the place of groats joins, there will be much empty space and in that place it is eaten because of its salt, and this hinders b'dieved according to all — therefore if not yet remained the measure of melicha, he should rinse and return to salt with proper salt, for forbidden to salt while salt and blood are on it; and if remained the measure of melicha, he should rinse slightly and salt, for if he rinses well the holes of expulsion close and the place that was salted properly absorbs from the place not salted; also he salts on a clean place, not on that vessel in which he salted the first time — see there.
