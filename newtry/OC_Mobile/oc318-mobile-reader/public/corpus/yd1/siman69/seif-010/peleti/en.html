In ms"l't — behold, one should understand: blood in meat is species in a different species and can be clarified by tasting — if so, let the gentile taste for us, as explained below laws of mixtures; and what need for reliability to rely on his tasting? And strained to say: for whether it was rinsed we rely on him, but for reliability we need he is expert in distinction — not so from Mechaber, beginning siman 98 — see there; if so, why need reliability — give him and let him taste us. And one may say Ran, chapter Kol HaBashe, stood on what they said salting period mil — we see the mohal exiting from meat after this period much redder than the first; and answer: certainly salting comes only to remove blood on the meat's surface and near it; for absorbed blood in the piece we are not concerned, for it is blood of limbs that did not spread, permitted; and further answer: after salting period there is no blood, only mohal reddens, etc.; and according to this view every piece is full of blood, only they did not forbid except blood on the piece's surface, not what is in the essence of the piece; if so, how does he say he does not know from his tasting, for he does not feel taste of blood; if so, we judge it was rinsed — what of it, there is still blood from the congealing place; all the more this answer that it is mere mohal, certainly equal in taste to blood — if so, how do we rely on the gentile saying taste of blood? Truthfully as his words, and nevertheless it does not forbid, for it is mohal — so Rashba in his novellae to Chullin Bei Duga, for impossible for the drip to taste blood, for also fat drip tastes like blood and impossible to distinguish, and all the more meat mohal. And with this the challenge on Rama is resolved, who ruled even in d'rabbanan issurim we do not rely on ms"l't, and in this law he wrote one may rely on ms"l't — it is resolved, for here the piece is in its blood, since issur is only blood on top and inside the piece is permitted, it is majority permitted blood and species in species is nullified in majority permitted taste, or forbidden taste is nullified in majority permitted taste; or say when it cooks, added afterward water more than sixty against blood, and nevertheless issur vel heter forbids, but impossible to taste, for taste already nullified in sixty — if so, also concern that cooking them will give taste is gone, for taste of blood already nullified in more than sixty, and only taromet mixture of species in species remains — to reconcile, examine. And further one may say: here the gentile fears lest we taste him and the blood inside will be discovered, since he knows it was not rinsed and salted; also many poskim hold the piece itself sixty against absorbed blood, only disputants are not clear to me, and from uncertainty we do not extract; and there is here double safek safek: perhaps it was rinsed, and if you say not rinsed, perhaps there is sixty — see below general double safek that in such a case we do not invert; if so, at least ms"l't helps. And further one may say: here d'rabbanan, for Rama holds below siman 98 that even in a d'rabbanan matter ms"l't does not help — it is fine according to what I wrote, that here there are two d'rabbanan matters.
