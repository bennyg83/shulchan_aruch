(10) There in hagahah. Or he removed hooves of the legs after rinsing. But if he did not remove them, all is considered one piece of meat and rinsing them as they are suffices, since there is no visible blood. Shach s.k. 4; Shaarei De'ah seif 4; Mekor Chaim seif 7. And regarding if they were salted thus without removing hooves whether what is inside the hooves is forbidden — see above siman 68 seif 8 and our words there.
