(S.k. 32) Maharshal wrote, etc. Maharshal's words need examination, for reason suggests thus that meat expels a little. Also what he wrote 'and I saw one teacher who permitted in this pleasantly kevul'o kach polto' — it appears he erred, etc.; and to me it appears he ruled well, for this piece that remained three days without melicha is stringency of the Geonim and no hint is mentioned in the Talmud, and also Rivash siman 86 waved it aside — therefore give it to him, do not add to it, and you have only what they were stringent about; and further one may say that even the Geonim did not say it does not exit at all through melicha, for the matter is seen to the eye that not all its blood exits — and if so, since in any case a little blood exits, we say regarding absorbed blood kevul'o kach polto — so it appears to me:
