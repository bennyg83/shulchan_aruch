(There seif 13) And there they soaked it in water within three days, etc. Taz and the Acharonim wrote one should soak it well; but passing water over it does not suffice unless he pierced the meat for blood grooves — then in b'dieved one relies on this, etc.; nevertheless afterward needs another rinse before salting, even b'dieved, etc. And it seems this applies also to "soaked it well" within the three days; nevertheless afterward needs another rinse before salting even b'dieved, for it already dried. And Taz and Shach wrote that if it was not yet cooked, he rinses and salts again; and so in Mei Yisrael general rule 7 s.k. 1. And what Shach wrote "suffices b'dieved" means even if not yet cooked; and as Shach himself wrote s.k. 14. However, Minchat Yisrael on Turei Chayim general rule 5 siman 3 and general rule 15 siman 12 challenged Shach's words and wrote that permitting b'dieved with slight rinsing is only after cooking, but if not cooked, only salted, since there is repair in rinsing and salting again it is not called b'dieved — see there. And this is that they disagree with what the hagahah wrote below seif 2, that if he did not rinse only a little before salting it is permitted b'dieved even before cooking or after cooking, and there in note 48 for law — see there.
