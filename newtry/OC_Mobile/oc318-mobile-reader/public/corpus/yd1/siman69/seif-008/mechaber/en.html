If he did not shake off the salt on it and did not rinse it, there is no prohibition because the water in the vessel nullifies the power of the salt. {Rama: And even if the water is scant, nevertheless they nullify the power of the tzir (another opinion and HaAroch general rule 6 and Maharai). And some permit even if there was no water at all in the vessel and he placed the meat in it, for one need not be concerned in such a short time while busy rinsing the meat (Shach Devarim and Orchot Hayim general rule 1 and 10 and see above siman 60). And one may rely on them, and in all this one need not be concerned only in the first rinsing; but after the first rinsing one need not be concerned (as appears from Mordechai and Ran). And there is no difference whether they rinsed in a kosher vessel or treifah or a non-Jewish vessel; and if there was a little water in the vessel that nullifies the power of the salt so it is no longer considered boiling, even if the vessel is a non-Jewish vessel or treifah, still in prohibition permitted (HaAroch).}
