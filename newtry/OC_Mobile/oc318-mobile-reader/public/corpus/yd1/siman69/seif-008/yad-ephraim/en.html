Permitted to rinse meat in fruit juice. Be'er Heitev; and see Radbaz part 2 siman 37, that rinsing meat in all kinds of liquids, even not of the seven liquids. And see Machatzit HaShekel, that from Pri Chadash it is clear l'chatchila one should not rinse even final rinsing in fruit juice; and likewise as Kereti wrote; and in responsum Knesset HaGedolah he is stringent in final rinsing even b'dieved.
