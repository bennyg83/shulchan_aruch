To salt in it again. Be'er Heitev; and in Shiyurei Berachah part 3 siman 66: water in which meat was rinsed before salting that reddened and there was not sixty against the blood in them — let it dry and it is permitted to salt meat in it to expel its blood, but forbidden to put into forbidden food; and if it is very salty, do not leave it in the house lest they err to put from it into food, for although b'dieved it does not forbid, l'chatchila good to be careful — see there.
