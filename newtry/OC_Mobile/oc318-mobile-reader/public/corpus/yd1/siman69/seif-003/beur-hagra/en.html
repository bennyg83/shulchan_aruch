One should not salt, etc. Rambam. And as stated in the Gemara there, Rav Dimi, etc., and he explains that he states the law.
Like flour. So Ri'az explains the words of Rambam.
And not, etc. Ri'az.
And if, etc. For all poskim disagree with Rambam — Ra'avad explains that Rav Dimi salted with coarse salt so he need not rinse first in a perforated vessel. And regarding coarse salt it is not applicable; and if there is no, etc. But in truth it is not an impediment, as the poskim and Darkei Moshe and Shach: (Likkut) And if not, etc. From that Rav Dimi alone did thus. Tur Orach Chaim 451:1 in the name of Ra'avad — and even l'chatchila, end of his words (end of Likkut).
