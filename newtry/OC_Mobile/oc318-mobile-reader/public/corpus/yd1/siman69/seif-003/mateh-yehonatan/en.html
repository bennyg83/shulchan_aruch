It appears to me that this certainly applies to pickling from above that is like cooking — not that very moment at the end of above causes absorption, but an hour or two before the above begins and is not finished until end of twenty-four hours. And if they put water or something else in the pot within the above in which meat was cooked and remained there above, and afterward puts forbidden fat after the above of the first cooking, or even other prohibitions — for those poskim all is forbidden since at the start of absorption it was within above, etc. — if so the absorbed joins with meat pickled in it, it is meat in milk and forbidden even if not meat in milk — long reasoning on pickling.
