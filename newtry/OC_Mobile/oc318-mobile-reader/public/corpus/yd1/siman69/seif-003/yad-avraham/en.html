(Seif 6 in the hagahah) Regarding honoring guests, he wrote in Sulam LaMinchah in the name of Torat HaEsh that wherever "for honoring guests" is mentioned, they are those fit for honor, and paupers who pass through for scraps and food are in the category of charity and not in the category of honoring guests. He further wrote that l'chatchila one should not invite his friend when he will need to be lenient on his account; but if he already invited, one may be lenient even for townspeople. And regarding great loss he wrote there in general rule 38 that everything is according to the estimation of the posek's mind according to time and place — the rich according to his wealth and the poor according to his poverty — and also according to the matter of the ruling whether there are many sides to be lenient or not.
