In salt. Taz wrote in the name of Maharshal: what meticulous people are accustomed to be careful not to place any meat that was not rinsed on any board or special table for that purpose, lest there be salt on the place where meat is placed without his knowledge — end of his words; and from this one learns not to place salt on the place designated to place meat there when they bring it from the slaughterhouse (and Peri Chadash wrote: and one should not place meat in baskets that are not designated for that, for many are lenient to place also in that basket vegetables and the like, because blood congeals in the baskets and they eat the fruits placed there afterward without rinsing at all — and even if they rinse the baskets or the fruits brought in them it is nothing, for it is not the usual manner of rinsing, as below siman 91 — end of his words):
