(Beit Yosef at end) And requires further study. In my opinion one may say Turei Even's intent is certainly Tosafot's view that both first and final rinsing are permitted in fruit juice; and since Tosafot hold so, it is reasonable without compulsion, for we find no one who disagrees; and this is for final rinsing; but for first rinsing there is no proof from Tosafot, for perhaps they hold like poskim that first rinsing's reason is visible blood, therefore permitted in fruit juice; but for softening one may say fruit juice does not help — requires further study.
