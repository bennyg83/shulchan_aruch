And one should be careful l'chatchila. Even in other vessels; and b'dieved it is permitted even in earthenware — Torat Chatat general rule 11 din 7; and Maharshal's view there: in other vessels permitted to eat hot from them even l'chatchila since they are perforated; in earthenware, even perforated, forbidden even b'dieved; and it appears to decide: in other vessels forbidden l'chatchila and permitted b'dieved since they are perforated; in earthenware forbidden even b'dieved; and so Bach; and Orchot Chaim general rule 3 din 4 certainly cold is permitted to use even l'chatchila even in earthenware perforated, even without rinsing, only with good wiping — and see responsum of Maharal ibn Chaviv siman 91:
Permitted to eat from it even hot. Because since the vessel is perforated it did not absorb anything — blood as it absorbs so it expels:
