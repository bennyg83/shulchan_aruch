Hot. Because a perforated vessel does not absorb anything and m'sharek sharek:
Permitted. And Shach explained in the name of Turei Zahav: b'dieved it is permitted even in an earthenware vessel; and Maharshal's view is that in other vessels it is permitted to eat hot in them even l'chatchila since they are perforated, and in an earthenware vessel even b'dieved it is forbidden even if perforated — and it appears to decide that in other vessels it is forbidden l'chatchila and permitted b'dieved since they are perforated, and in an earthenware vessel even b'dieved it is forbidden; and likewise Bach; however cold it is permitted to use even l'chatchila even in an earthenware vessel according to all, if it is perforated, even without rinsing, only with good wiping — end of his words:
