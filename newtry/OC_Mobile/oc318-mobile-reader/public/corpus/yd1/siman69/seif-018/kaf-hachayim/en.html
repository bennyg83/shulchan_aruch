(18) Meat that was soaked in a vessel in sea water — one sage arose to forbid, and it has no further remedy to salt it, since sea water is very salty, it is as meat salted in a non-perforated vessel; and Rav Moshe Mat part 1 siman 74 rejected his words and permitted for several reasons — see there. Shevet Sofer seif 1; Sdei Chemed seif 8.
