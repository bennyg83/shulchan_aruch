(There seif 18 in the hagahah) But the other pieces on it are permitted. So too Ohr HaChaim, and it is explained there that even a peel is not required; and so wrote Taz. And in my humble opinion it is impossible to permit them without a peel. And clear proof from the Gemara chapter Kol HaBasar (daf 112) regarding fish and birds salted one with another — forbidden; and so Taz siman 70. And the Gemara challenged there: how is it? If in an unperforated vessel, why fish and birds — even birds with birds should be forbidden. And now what is the question — for these birds above do not even require a peel? And these fish and birds — even the fish above require a peel, as Shach wrote siman 70 s.k. 4 and the Acharonim there and Shach. Rather it is forced that birds with birds is also so. However, Mei Yisrael left it in requires further study whether birds above require a peel — see there in general rule 2 s.k. 22.
