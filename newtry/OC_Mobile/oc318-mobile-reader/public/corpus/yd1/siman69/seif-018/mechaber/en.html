Meat salted in a non-perforated vessel and remained there for the time that they would put water on the fire and it would begin to boil — all that is in the tzir is forbidden to eat even roasted; and the part of the piece outside the tzir is forbidden from it only as much as a peel, even if there is fat in it. {Rama: And some forbid the whole piece even what is outside the tzir (Tur and Mordechai in the name of Maharif). And even if he salted only a little as is the way they salt for roasting, and even if he did not remain for the measure of melicha only a little until tzir is seen in the vessel (Mordechai end of chapter 25 in the name of Rabeiah and hagahot of Shach Devarim and HaAroch general rule 10). And so is the custom and one should not change; nevertheless it is forbidden only that piece placed below in the vessel touching the tzir; but other pieces salted above it with it are permitted (there in HaAroch and in Kol Bo) and so is the custom.}
