Meat that remained three days from time to time without melicha — its blood dried inside and will no longer come out through melicha, and one may not eat it cooked, only roasted; and after roasting it one shall not cook it; and if they cooked it, permitted (and one should not leave meat three days without melicha, for we are concerned lest they cook it (Beit Yosef in the name of Piskei Maharai siman 191).}
