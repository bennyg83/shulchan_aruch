And one may not eat it cooked. Even if they place it in lukewarm water so its blood awakens to exit — it does not help — Torat Chatat general rule 4 din 4, and Maharshal in Orchot Shabbat siman 4 and chapter 25 siman 85:
Only roasted. And for roasting it is permitted without melicha, only l'chatchila one should salt it a little like other roasting — as below siman 76 seif 2 in hagahah; but we do not say that since it remained three days without melicha we require actual melicha even for roasting — Torat Chatat beginning of general rule 4; and it is further explained there that even if he did not pierce it from the paths of the blood in it, it is permitted for roasting; and above siman 64 seif 18 it is explained that l'chatchila one should pierce the meat from the fat in it within three days, and b'dieved if he pierced afterward it is permitted even for a pot — see there:
He should not cook it. For perhaps not all its blood exited through roasting and afterward it will exit through cooking, for cooking acts more than roasting; and if they cooked it, it is permitted, for we suspend and say everything exited, and what remains and did not exit will not exit again through cooking. And one may distinguish for liver siman 73, that likewise it has no remedy except through roasting, and it is permitted to cook it after roasting even l'chatchila — for there melicha does not help because of abundance of blood in it, and presumably everything was expelled in roasting, and what was not expelled in roasting will not be expelled in cooking; but here melicha does not help because its blood already dried inside, and it is possible roasting does not act to soften the blood so everything exits, whereas cooking softens it:
