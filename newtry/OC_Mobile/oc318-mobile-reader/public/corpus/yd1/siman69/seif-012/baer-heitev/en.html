Cooked. Shach wrote: and even if they place it in lukewarm water so its blood is aroused to exit — it does not help:
He cooks it. Shach wrote: lest not all its blood exited through roasting and it exits afterward through cooking, for cooking acts more than roasting; and if they cooked it — permitted, for we suspend to say all exited, and what remained and did not exit will not exit again through cooking; and one may answer for liver in beginning of siman 73 that likewise it has no permission through melicha except through roasting, and it is permitted to cook it after roasting even l'chatchila, for there melicha does not help because of abundance of blood in it and presumably all was expelled in roasting, and what was not expelled in roasting will not be expelled in cooking; but here melicha does not help because its blood already dried within, and it is possible roasting does not act to soften the blood so all exits, and cooking acts to soften it. And he also wrote that for roasting it is permitted without melicha, only l'chatchila one should salt it somewhat like other roasting; and even if he did not pierce it from the paths of blood in it, it is permitted to roast; and see above siman 64 seif 18, explained that l'chatchila one pierces the meat from fat in it within three days, and b'dieved if he pierced afterward it is permitted even for a pot — see there. And Taz wrote in the name of Maharshal on meat that remained three days without melicha and afterward they salted it in a perforated vessel, etc. — see there; and Taz also wrote: and so it appears if he salted meat that remained three days without melicha with other meat that was salted to expel its blood — that meat that remained three days is forbidden, lest it absorb from the blood exiting from the other meat, for the meat is not so hardened to say it will no longer absorb anything within; therefore we rely on nothing for leniency; and likewise if he salted meat that remained three days with other meat already salted and rinsed in a non-perforated vessel — the other meat is also forbidden since it rests in a non-perforated vessel. And I found in an old collection that if after it remained three days they salted it the time of melicha and rinsed it and roasted it — again permitted to cook (and see Tzitz Eliezer siman 8 and Beit Yosef siman 171):
For we are concerned. Meaning: one should not leave it to roast it, for we are concerned lest he forget and cook it:
