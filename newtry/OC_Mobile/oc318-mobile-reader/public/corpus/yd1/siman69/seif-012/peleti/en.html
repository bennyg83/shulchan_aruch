And one should not leave it, etc. — and that which they permit safek treifah poultry and even an egg that needs cooking, there they abandon it and it has no remnant, we did not decree lest he eat from it; but this meat he does not abandon, for it has whiteness in roasting — we decree; and further, there benefit in leaving it, for anyway safek will be clarified, or it will lay eggs and the like; but here what benefit in leaving for cooking — never will be permitted, and for roasting immediately permitted — if so, why need to leave and come to concern for mishap?
