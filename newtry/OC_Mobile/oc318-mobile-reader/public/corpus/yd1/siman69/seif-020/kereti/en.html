Permitted — for gravy after it remained the measure of melicha is not blood, only molten blood.
And some forbid — who hold gravy is blood and absorbs k'dei klipah; however if he rinsed only once it is no longer considered blood, or hot.
K'dei klipah — and Maharshal disagrees and forbids everything in the gravy; and if there is sixty it is permitted; but what is outside the gravy does not combine for sixty, for blood does not spread — and Rama, who requires only peeling, peeling does not help with sixty.
And there is residue in it — but if not soiled, there is no melicha for vessels and it is permitted.
But great loss — they permit even soiled.
Until he rinses it — certainly for all views the second rinse is required; and its root is from the Gemara — only for the first view the second rinse need not be immediate, and he may leave it a while in an unperforated vessel, for it is not hot; and afterward rinse it — unlike the second view, which forbids in an unperforated vessel and requires immediate rinse; also for the first view, if he peels the flesh around until there is no visible blood smeared, the flesh is permitted to eat — but for the second view what would it help, behold it expels blood and rinsing is needed to cool and nullify the expulsion.
Must hag'alah — for the second view; but for the first view not needed — he ruled the power of the heat of salt and blood.
