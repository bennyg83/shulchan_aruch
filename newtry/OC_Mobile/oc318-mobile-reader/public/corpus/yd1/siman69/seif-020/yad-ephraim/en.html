And the vessel into which that brine fell. Be'er Heitev; and in responsum Pri Tevuah siman 23: salting brine that fell on a porcelain vessel at the vessel's rim — he wrote to permit for several reasons, since it fell outside, and also it is in a high place; when the hot items reach opposite the vessel's ear that they use it through pouring into it, then there will be sixty, etc., as a thing whose way is to use abundantly, etc.; and also because of irui it will not expel from the vessel's outer wall, and especially porcelain — some wrote it is somewhat like glass, etc.; and especially according to Rashi, brine after salting is considered cold, etc. (and what he wrote on Pri Megadim interpreting Shach's words that in every case the whole is forbidden — these are idle words; for Pri Megadim himself brings there Turei Chayim general rule 11 several times, etc. — see there, who brought from Ohr HaChaim who holds so). And see in Pri Megadim several laws on this; and see below siman 91.
