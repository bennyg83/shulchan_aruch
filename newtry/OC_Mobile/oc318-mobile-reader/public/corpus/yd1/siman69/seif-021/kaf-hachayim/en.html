(21) But if he salted, even slight rinsing suffices b'dieved. Shach there; Peri Chadash there; Lechem HaPanim there; Lechem Panim there; Turei Da'at there; Chayei Adam general rule 32 seif 1; Beit Yitzchak there. And what Shach wrote "it suffices b'dieved" means even if not yet cooked, as Shach himself wrote s.k. 14. However Minchat Yitzchak on Turei Chaim general rule 5 seif 3 and general rule 15 seif 12 challenged Shach's words and wrote that permitted b'dieved with slight rinsing is after cooking; but if not cooked, only salted — since it has remedy in rinsing and melicha a second time, not called b'dieved — see there. And this is that they disagree on what is written in hagahah below seif 2 that if not rinsed only a little before salting, permitted b'dieved whether before cooking or after cooking; and there in seif 6 is explained for practical law — see there.
