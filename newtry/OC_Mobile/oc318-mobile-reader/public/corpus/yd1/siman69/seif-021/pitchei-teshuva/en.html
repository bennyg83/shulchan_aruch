Meat that remained. See in Be'er Heitev of the Rav Maharit who wrote below siman 75 s.k. 5 in the name of responsum Shaviv to be lenient with goose fat that was not rinsed within three days. And see in responsum Noda Biyehudah part Yoreh De'ah siman 25 who disagreed with Shaviv here, who permitted the forbidden, and wrote that even according to Shaviv, specifically fat that was stripped; but if the fat was not yet stripped from the goose, he too agrees that even the fat alone is forbidden to cook; and one who ruled to permit even the fat alone deserves rebuke with nezifah; and if the act was already done and he stripped the fat and cooked, the vessels and all the mixture are forbidden — see there. Also in responsum Maor HaGolah Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 44 disagrees with Shaviv on this. And see in Chokhmat Adam who also wrote that with fat the law is like with meat, except he wrote that one may permit through cooking in water with sixty after melicha, since in great loss we rely on the Mechaber in seif 11, and the water that absorbed prohibition he pours out, and this is not nullifying prohibition because the fat will return and absorb from the water — this is not nullification of prohibition for absorption comes after nullification; and further our intent is to melt. Fat is somewhat similar to siman 84 seif 63; but to mix it with other fat until sixty appears forbidden — end of his words. (And it appears that in this manner even with meat permitted; however for the second reason Chokhmat Adam wrote it is not nullifying prohibition for our intent is only to melt the fat — possible that with meat forbidden for the reason Taz wrote in siman 137 s.k. 4 that not permitted without intent for that except where impossible otherwise — see there; and likewise with meat possible through roasting, but with fat impossible otherwise for in roasting the fat is consumed.) And see in responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 38 who raised to be lenient in this with great loss — namely, that initially he salts the fat as required, and after melicha and rinsing he mixes with other fat that was salted and rinsed as required — see there for his reason.
