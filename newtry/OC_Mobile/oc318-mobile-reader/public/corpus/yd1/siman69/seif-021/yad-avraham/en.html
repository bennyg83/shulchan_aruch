(There seif 21) In a place where salt is not found, they roast the meat until half its roasting and afterward cook it — thus Tur. And Beit Yosef showed its source from Hagahot Ashiri. And this is not that Tur did not bring Hagahot anywhere; but in truth it was hidden from Beit Yosef the words of Rosh, chapter HaRoeh siman 7, and Rashba in Taharat HaAdam, who wrote: and they sent from the yeshiva in a place that has no salt to roast it on fire. And after it reaches half, if he wishes to cook it in a pot, it is fine — see there. Behold it is clear explicitly as Tur wrote, and he did not need to make a weak inference from Hagahot as Beit Yosef wrote.
