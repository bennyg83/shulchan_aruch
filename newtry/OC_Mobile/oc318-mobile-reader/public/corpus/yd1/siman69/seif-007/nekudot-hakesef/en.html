(There s.k. 29) In Turei Zahav, etc. It is like that hunter who finds a large animal and takes small ones and takes — and I in my book challenged Turei Zahav on this, and thus I wrote: Mordechai wrote in the name of responsum of R' Shimon son of Abraham that even for one who permits in meat, in a whole chicken that was cooked one should be stringent even though there is sixty in the water, because of the liver and intestines, as one who places them in a basket of water and blood expelled from them is absorbed in the chicken and no longer exits, etc. — end of his words; and this responsum is in responsum of Maimonides; and Turei Zahav wrote that likewise if there are no intestines, it is forbidden in chicken according to all, for since it is hollowed blood spread from place to place and forbidden in such a case even in time of pressure and great loss — end of his words. And likewise in Etz Chaim; and it is not clear, for he mentioned specifically liver and intestines — if not so it is no worse than a piece of meat; and even though it spreads from place to place, nevertheless from the moment it spread into the hollow it was nullified in water; and even in liver and intestines it is not forbidden except when they adhere to the bird — if not so, not as Rokeach siman 339 in the name of many great ones; and even though we do not hold like Rokeach there even in adhesion, that is because he follows his view that he has no issur davuk — but we do not hold thus; but when not adhering it is obvious we hold thus, as the Rav wrote in hagahah itself at end of siman 73, only we say since it is not adhering, therefore from the moment it was expelled it was nullified in water — and all the more so regarding the chicken itself one may say thus.
