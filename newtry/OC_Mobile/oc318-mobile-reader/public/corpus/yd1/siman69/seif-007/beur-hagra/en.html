Or he should rinse, etc. For Rashba wrote that therefore he salted with coarse salt, because he shakes it off — not so with fine salt, where shaking is impossible and he must rub; and the reason is as follows.
And he rinses twice. As stated there, and "he rinses it well, well" implies two rinsings. Ran.
And he washes, etc. For the reason of rinsing: the first time removes the salt and blood; the second removes the moisture of the first that remained, so clear water exits — as Ra'osh and Rambam wrote; therefore he must wash so the salt and blood not adhere to the flesh.
And some say, etc. As stated "well, well" — meaning he rinses well; therefore three times are needed, as stated in chapter 4 of Tamid: and they rinse the innards, etc.; and Tosafot Avodah Zarah 33b s.v. kesi, therefore Ri explained, etc.
Therefore he should wash, etc. Difficult to reconcile; and Peri Chadash already challenged all the Acharonim.
And l'chatchila, etc. For Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote the reason that he shakes it off so the salt not be abundant in the rinse water and become like roasting and forbid the vessel and flesh — and Ra'avad's words: first rinse in a perforated vessel if he did not shake off.
Permitted, etc. Tosafot Chullin 33a s.v. venichalin, etc.
