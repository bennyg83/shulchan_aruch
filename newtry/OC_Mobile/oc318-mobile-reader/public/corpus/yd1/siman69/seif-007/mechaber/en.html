Before putting the meat in the vessel in which they rinse it, he shall shake off the salt on it or rinse it in water; and afterward put the meat in the vessel in which they rinse and rinse it twice and wash the vessel between rinsing and rinsing. {Rama: And some say they need to rinse the meat three times (Chiddushei Agudah in the name of Or Zarua and Maharai and Orchot Hayim chapter 21 law 15 and hagahot) and so they are accustomed l'chatchila; therefore he shall rinse it or shake off the salt from it and rinse it twice, for this is as rinsing three times; or he puts the water in the vessel and afterward places the meat in it and rinses it three times, and so is the custom; and l'chatchila he puts much water in the first rinsing so the power of the salt in the tzir be nullified (another opinion). Permitted to rinse the meat in fruit juice and water is not required (Ran chapter of the slaughterer and Tosafot there).}
