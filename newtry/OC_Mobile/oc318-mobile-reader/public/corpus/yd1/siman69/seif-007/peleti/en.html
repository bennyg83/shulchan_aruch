In fruit juice — Rama in responsum wrote: from Ran's words regarding the slaughterer who did not find blood, it implies initial rinsing in fruit juice does not help, for they congeal it; and Taz, Shach, and Pri Chadash wondered: we find no such thing in Ran, only on Tosafot's question on "and they are eaten with soiled hands" that it was kashered through rinsing, and Ran answered it was rinsed in rainwater — see there; if so how did Rama learn from this regarding fruit juice, which they never mentioned? And it seems without intent for Rama, who lives by roasting — spoke without knowledge, Heaven forbid; so one should understand Ran: he holds rinsing in rainwater does not kasher them — behold they said in the mishnah: whatever begins or ends with intent is mekarev; here their intent is to rinse the meat and prepare it for eating, and there is no greater intent than this, and they are mekarev; and one must say his intent was to roast it, not for rinse water, and if so his intent was without moistening the meat in water and he is not pleased with this rinsing at all, and if so it is not for intent and they are not mekarev; but nevertheless, if when he salted in second rinsing in fruit juice it was kashered — if in rainwater this certainly pleases him that he cannot do without rinsing, then even rainwater is mekarev and his answer is nothing; and needless to say certainly second rinsing is discussed, that it was rinsed in fruit juice and rainwater is not needed, only initial rinsing that fruit juice does not help — difficult; and on this answer rainwater, and it is not pleasing to him that his intent was to eat it and they are not mekarev — examine.
