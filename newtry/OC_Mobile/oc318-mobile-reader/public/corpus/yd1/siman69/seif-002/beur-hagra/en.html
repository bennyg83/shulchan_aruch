Like the reason of Ra'ah, that rinsing is so the salt not absorb the blood on its surface, and it no longer expels; and the first reasoning holds that as it absorbs so it expels, and likewise in Chullin 111a. And as stated there 113a, s.v. ein, etc., and likewise we do not say as it absorbs so it expels.
And if not, etc. For according to Ra'ah it suffices thus; but l'chatchila he must rinse it well because of Ran's reason — only that b'dieved it is not applicable to forbid according to his reason; and this is what he wrote "and likewise," etc.
And if he salted, etc. For regarding the others it is considered expelled blood, and as it absorbs so it expels.
