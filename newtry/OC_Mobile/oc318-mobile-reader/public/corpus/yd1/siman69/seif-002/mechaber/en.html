If he salted and did not rinse first, he shall rinse it and salt it a second time; and some forbid. {Rama: And so they are accustomed even if he only salted a little as is the way they salt for roasting, and even if he did not remain in his salt the measure of melicha (HaAroch and Shach Devarim and Semag); however in a place of great loss there is to permit (Beit Yosef). And if he did not rinse only a little before he salted it, permitted b'dieved; and the same law if there were sixty in the piece against the blood on it (hagahot of Shach Devarim in the name of Maharai). And if a piece was salted without rinsing with other pieces, the other pieces are permitted and it is forbidden (HaAroch general rule 4 law 8).}
