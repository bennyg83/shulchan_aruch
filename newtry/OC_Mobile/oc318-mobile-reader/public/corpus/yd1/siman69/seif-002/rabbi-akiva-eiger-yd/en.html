(Taz s.k. 8) That impossible even melicha for roasting. It is difficult for me: for if so, even if he rinsed first, melicha for roasting should be forbidden, for it expels a little and returns and absorbs, for it is not busy expelling much since salted only a little.
(Taz s.k. 9) Even if remained in his salt. It is difficult for me: for at first glance, specifically when it remained; but without remaining, since there is heter in roasting as below (siman 76 seif 2), not called great loss according to Taz nearby s.k. 15.
(Seif 2 in hagahah) And the same if there was sixty in the piece. Nevertheless requires peeling.
(Seif 4 in hagahah) Even b'dieved fat salted on one side permitted. See below (siman 75).
(Taz s.k. 15) Since there is heter otherwise in roasting. In my opinion: if so, after twelve hours why not salt? Let us also say since this matter of measure of tzir expulsion twelve hours is not agreed — the primary view is more like poskim that its measure is twenty-four hours; here too, since there is heter otherwise in a place of great loss, rely on them that measure of tzir expulsion is twenty-four hours.
(Beit Yosef) And I wondered on Rama in siman 75. In my opinion requires further study: there melicha on the inner side certainly does not help since there is no blood there, therefore if cooked, even with great loss forbidden because of blood in the fat; but to return and salt is permitted since in fat there is only a little blood; but here for Rashba that melicha on one side helps — no blood remains at all, and preferable to that below; and for Maharai that melicha on one side does not help — much blood remains and inferior to that below; therefore in great loss we rely on Rashba and there is no blood at all; but without great loss we are concerned for Maharai that melicha on one side does not help and consequently much blood in the piece and returning to salt does not help — clear with God's help.
