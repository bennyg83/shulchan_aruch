For roasting. Bach permits with slight melicha and without waiting, and such is not the view of Taz — see there:
Waited. Darkei Moshe wrote: that is for a pot, but for roasting it is permitted if it did not remain the time of melicha, as written in siman 76 seif 2; and Maharshal and Bach forbid even for roasting even if it did not wait — and Shach's conclusion is like Darkei Moshe, and he wrote that also what Bach wrote that when it waited it is forbidden even in a place of great loss even through melicha and rinsing again is not clear:
B'dieved. Shach wrote: and it implies that since it was salted, even though it was not yet cooked, it is considered b'dieved and he need not return to rinse and salt it:
Sixty. The wording of Turei Zahav: and even though you have no piece that has sixty against the blood on it — that is in a general case; but if we know there are sixty in it, certainly it helps b'dieved. And if that piece was cooked we require sixty against the whole piece and the piece itself is forbidden:
Permitted. For regarding the others it is called blood of expulsion and kevul'o kach polto:
