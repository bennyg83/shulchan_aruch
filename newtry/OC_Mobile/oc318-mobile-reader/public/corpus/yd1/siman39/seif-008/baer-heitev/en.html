Examination. And the reason is that dependent sircha is only peeling of the lung lobes and does not come from a place of puncture. (Even though there is no sircha without puncture — here it is permitted, because the sircha on account of puncture adhered to another place. Maharival ch. 2 siman 72: sirchot found hanging on the animal's wall and it is not known whether they were thus hanging from the outset or were stuck to the lung and afterward were uprooted — they declared kosher, for we establish the slaughterer in his presumption — see Peri Chadash siman 127 and see there siman 37 seif 2.) And Taz wrote: even though he concluded here that examination is not required — nevertheless according to what Rama wrote, that the custom is that when there is sircha passed by rubbing they practice inflating it — the same applies here, that examination is required; for it is one reason: dependent sircha and sircha passed by rubbing — both of them we maintain as peeling of lobes — end:
Kosher. And Taz wrote: it is implied that dependent sircha that is adjacent applies; but when stuck to another place there is no leniency at all, because there are "two for ri'uta," and rubbing and reduction do not help there at all; and see above siman 37 seif 2; and it appears the same applies if below half it emerged from a tinnir, for it is not called "two for ri'uta" even though it stuck on two sides; and so is in Hagahot Bedikot to declare kosher in this case even if it emerges from a boil; and it appears from this that rubbing is required as with other sirchot:
