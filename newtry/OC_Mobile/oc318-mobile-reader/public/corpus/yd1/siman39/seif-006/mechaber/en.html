The attachment cord of the rose lobe — wherever it becomes sircha'd — treifah, even to its pocket, and even if the whole thing adheres to it; and the same law applies to every excess strand — wherever it becomes sircha'd — treifah. {Rama: However if the attachment cord adhered below at its root with its pocket — one may declare kosher, for this is their reclining (Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari ibn Chaviv).}
