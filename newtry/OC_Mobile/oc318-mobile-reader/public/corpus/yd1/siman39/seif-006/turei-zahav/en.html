To every place it becomes sircha'd, etc. For in every place it would be on its back not in order. Thus in the name of Or Zaru'a: an incident with an animal that was very fat, and the heads of the onot were swallowed in fat from abundant milk; and Rabbenu Yoel separated the fat from the onot and no fat of depletion remained on the lung, and it rose in inflation and he declared kosher — end.
