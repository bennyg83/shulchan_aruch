Treifah. And Shach wrote: and specifically an extra lobe found in front; but an extra lobe among the paths of the ona — it is obvious the law of ona applies to it and in ksederan it is kosher. And Taz wrote: an incident occurred with an animal that was very fat, and the head of the onot was swallowed in the abundant fat; and Rabbenu Yoel separated the fat from the onot and no fat remained on the lung, and it rose in inflation and he declared kosher — end (and specifically in front; but an extra lobe among the paths of the ona — it is obvious the law of ona applies and in ksederan kosher.
