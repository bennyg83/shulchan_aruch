(19) And Rashba wrote in responsum siman 112: wherever examiners are accustomed to be stringent even not according to halachah — I say thus the sages of the place practiced, and it is forbidden to transgress their words until we know they truly erred in ruling and erred in prohibition — end. However, where the matter is clear they erred in ruling, the custom may be nullified. Peri Chadash letter 1. Lechem HaPanim letter 3. Beit Lechem Yehudah letter 2. Einat Nachum there letter 2.
