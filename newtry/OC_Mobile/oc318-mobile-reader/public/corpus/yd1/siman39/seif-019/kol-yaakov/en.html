(19) And if she sewed and covered [them] — and the same for making housings with a press, Melechet Shlomo there — the tefillin are disqualified, for this is among repair of their making; for shin is included in sewing and covering. But if he scraped glue between letter and letter, or made other repair — kosher b'dieved. And the same if she sewed the [parchment] — nevertheless for clarity, if possible to scrape the sides of letters where touched and rewrite them — he should do so, for this is called l'chatchila. And specifically in [Torah] scroll; but in tefillin it is called b'dieved, for they require ksavari. Magen Avraham there in the name of R' Moshe of Lublin siman 68.
