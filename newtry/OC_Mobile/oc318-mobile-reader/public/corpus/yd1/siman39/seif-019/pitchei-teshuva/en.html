Or to flesh and to bone. See responsum of Radbaz HaChadashot siman 328, who wrote: wherever the rib is exposed it is called bone; and wherever flesh covers the bone it is called flesh — not like some slaughterers who say it is not called bone and flesh except when sircha-adherent to flesh between two ribs where there is no bone at all; but if sircha-adherent to flesh on the bone — since the middle of the rib is exposed and both its sides covered in flesh, and if sircha-adherent to that flesh it is called [adhesion] to bone since bone is beneath it — treifah.
