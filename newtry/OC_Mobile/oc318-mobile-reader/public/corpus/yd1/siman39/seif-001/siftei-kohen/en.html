And they also practiced to inflate. But if it was lost without inflation, according to all it is kosher since it was examined; and this is simple and proven in the Talmud in many places and all the poskim, and the Bach likewise. Only that he understood from Maharshal, first chapter of Chullin siman 21, that one who is stringent for himself on a large animal, if the lung was examined and lost without inflation — but he did not analyze his words well, for on the contrary it appears the opposite from his words; and why should one who is stringent be stringent in a matter whose permission is simple in the Talmud and all the poskim, rishonim and acharonim:
Only if there was sircha in it, etc. And the same applies to all other flaws, as we say in the Talmud regarding the lung that he inflated, and regarding a blocked lung, and likewise regarding a lung whose upper membrane peeled — above, siman 36; and so in several places. And so wrote the Rambam at the end of chapter 11 of Hilchot Shechitah. And most places do not inflate, for nothing was born that causes concern; and they never inflated the lung in Spain and the west except if something was born that they are concerned about — end of his words.
