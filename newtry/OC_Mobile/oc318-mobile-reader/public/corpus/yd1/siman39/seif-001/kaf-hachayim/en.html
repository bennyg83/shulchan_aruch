(6) On examination of treifah one need not recite a berachah, for it was slaughtered standing in presumption of permissibility. Rokeach siman 366. And in Hagahot Laws of Examination of Mahari Weil he wrote one does not bless on examination, for we are concerned perhaps treifah will be found and he will make a berachah in vain. Beit David, Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz siman 29 letter 3, wrote this reason is weak, for he can bless afterward when he finds kosher; rather the main reason is because examination is one of the divisions of slaughter — slaughter is to eat when he finds kosher, and since he blessed on slaughter he need not bless on examination — see there. And Machazik Berachah in this siman letter 2 wrote this is not a rabbinic mitzvah on which we bless like other rabbinic mitzvot, but a rabbinic decree not to encounter treifah prohibition — see there. Although Erech HaShulchan letter 3 wrote for other poskim one should bless — nevertheless the simple custom is not to bless, as the aforementioned poskim, and also because it is difficult for me to resolve.
