The laws of examining the lung. In it are 25 seifim. One need not examine after any of the treifot of the ordinary [cases] except the lung — one must examine in cattle and wild animal if there is sircha (Rivash and R' Yitzchak ibn Chaviv and so stated). And whoever breaches the fence to eat without examination — the snake will bite him. {Rama: They also practiced to inflate every lung even if there is no ri'uta in it (R' Shimon siman 92 and examinations and so stated). And in some places they are lenient not to inflate it except if there was sircha passed by rubbing; and so is primary (Rambam ch. 11, Mordechai, and so stated).}
