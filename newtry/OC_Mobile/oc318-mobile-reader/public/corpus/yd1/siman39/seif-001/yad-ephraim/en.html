And Pri Megadim wrote, part 2 siman 89: there are places among Sephardim whose custom is to declare kosher sircha into the wall, cutting the sircha and checking by inflation — this is mistaken custom; and all the more sircha not in order on the lung itself — no posek ever thought to permit; one must protest against them, and if they do not listen it is forbidden for Ashkenazim to eat from their slaughter and examination, and one must distance even from their utensils — see there. And see siman 192 where he wrote: in Belgrade there was an ancient Sephardic community and Ashkenazim settled there who did not wish to return — they may adopt their custom even leniently; but now that Ashkenazim are a holy community unto themselves, they practice their custom. And if a seudat mitzvah occurs, the host must announce that something occurred whereby they practice leniently, such as regarding veredah and the like; and each community holds its custom, lenient or stringent — and they are literally two courts in one city, and we are not concerned for "do not cut yourselves." See Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 60: sircha that passes by rubbing and before inflation was torn or lost — it appeared to declare kosher, especially with great financial loss; and so Kaf HaChayim part 2 siman 42. And so it appears to me in what Taz wrote that examination is only as stringency; nevertheless one may not be lenient except when the sircha was pressed between his fingers by gentle rubbing, as the Rosh wrote — then examination is stringency. However, in places where they are lenient and press somewhat forcefully, and also in Prague where examiners press sircha and lung until the sircha peels completely from the lung — for complete sircha does not peel completely from the lung, etc. — the sages of the generation closed their eyes; if so, when it is pressed somewhat, the act of inflation is primary. And if it was peeled and torn or lost under duress — behold two leniencies that contradict each other; Heaven forbid to be lenient in this. And likewise when a bone is found on the lung, inflation is required by law and one may not be lenient without inflation according to all — see there.
