Baer Heitev; and see in my responsa Beit Avraham siman 16: a cow that was slaughtered and sircha was found in it — they forbid milk within three days; those three days are counted retroactively, since the matter is natural that the mouth of the wound does not scab except over three days, and one cannot give the matter to measures that in this one it scabbed in 50 hours and in this one in 72 hours. And see there what I wrote to distinguish between this case and a woman who gave birth regarding Yom Kippur fast, where we do not count retroactively, as written in Orach Chaim siman 617 — I brought there several similar places — see there. And see in my book siman 23: milk that was milked from an animal that became treifah from sircha together with a boil, and there was also a wrinkle in the lung, and milk milked one day before slaughter was mixed into milk milked from four kosher animals and they used the aforementioned mixture milk in various utensils l'chatchila — one may not rule permissibility, and especially since it appears there is no great financial loss — sell the milk to a non-Jew and kasher utensils that can be kashered; and if there is great financial loss on account of utensils that cannot be kashered and were used with kli rishon hot water — at least let them stand retroactively.
