The rib broke. See responsum Mahari Weil part 2 siman 9, who elaborated to distinguish against slaughterers who said one may declare kosher when sircha-adherent to a rib that broke and returned and reattached — considered a wound although the break was in one place and the sircha beyond it, for that entire rib is in presumption of ri'uta; they brought proofs for their words, and he rejected their proofs and brought that Heaven forbid to be lenient in this.
