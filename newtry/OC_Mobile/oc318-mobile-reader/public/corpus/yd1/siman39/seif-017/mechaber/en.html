One who buys a lung and says he found in it sircha in a place where it is treifah, and the seller says it was in a place where it is kosher — since both of them came at once to court, there is no testimony here, and behold it is like an animal whose lung was lost before it was examined. {Rama: And likewise in places where they slaughter with a non-Jew and left the animals with a non-Jew between slaughter and examination — one need not be concerned that perhaps the non-Jew inserted his hand and detached the sirchot, for examination of the lung is d'rabbanan and a doubt we lean leniently (Rashba siman 298). However if gentile butchers are accustomed to do so — one should be concerned even b'dieved; and l'chatchila one should be careful in every matter.}
