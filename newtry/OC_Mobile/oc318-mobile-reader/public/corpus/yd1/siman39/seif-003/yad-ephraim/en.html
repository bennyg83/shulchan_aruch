However, if the veredah, etc. And see in my book responsa Beit Avraham siman 3: that which they declare kosher when the veredah stuck at its root — specifically when one knows clearly that only the root stuck alone; but when it is not clear that it is the root alone — one should be stringent — see there. And see there further siman 9, where he wrote that Rama meant: if the whole [veredah] is stuck it is treifah; only if the adhesion is not over the entire base of the veredah but only at its root — the place where it begins to grow, at its connection to the ona — meaning if it sticks at the bottom of the pocket at its end, i.e., its lower part specified at the place of connection of the veredah between the cut of the ona and the uma.
