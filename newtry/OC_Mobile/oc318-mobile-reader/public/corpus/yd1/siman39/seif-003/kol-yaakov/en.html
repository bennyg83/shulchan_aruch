(3) We do not rely on counting years except through valid witnesses, as written in Orach Chaim siman 169 seif 11. And although there it says in the name of letter 11 to rely if he was established before neighbors — Pri Megadim, Orach Chaim siman 25 wrote one may not rely on this except in time of pressure. Melechet Shlomo there wrote simply: in our texts there is not so much pressure; therefore one may not be lenient via presumption of neighbors and voice — only via clear proof — see there.
