(3) And if one wishes to be stringent for himself and examine everything — there is nothing wrong, and this is not called adding to the words of the Sages; this is only like adding fences for purity of his soul. And one who forbids himself safek — all this is called sanctifying oneself in what is permitted; and you find that early chasidim distanced themselves fifty gates so as not to enter the fiftieth gate that has prohibition. All this is specifically for himself, but not to instruct contrary to the early permissibility. Perishah there. Zivchei Tzedek end of letter 1.
