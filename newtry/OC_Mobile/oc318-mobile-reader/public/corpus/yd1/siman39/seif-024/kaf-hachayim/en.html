And although nowadays in most places treifot are more common than kosher — the reason is because they are very stringent with stringencies not from core Talmudic law; also many matters require examination and we are not expert in examination and declare them treifah; also many animals on account of safek we declare treifah. Peri Chadash there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav letter 1. She'erit Menachem there. Zivchei Tzedek letter 7. And it implies: in places where majority are treifot — by law one may not declare kosher. And so Beit Lechem Shmuel letter 14: if sometimes in some region some treifot are very common in animals until almost the majority are treifah, and at least half-and-half as we sometimes see happen for some reason — then treifah when lost, even with great financial loss, since there is no majority of kosher status; and even in kids and lambs, if such is common there — the law is thus, for in such a case it is safek d'oraisa: all that was not examined — end.
