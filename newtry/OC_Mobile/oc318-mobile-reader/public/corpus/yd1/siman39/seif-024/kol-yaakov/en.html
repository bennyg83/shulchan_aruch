(24) Seif 4: Tefillin that an apikoros wrote, etc. — tefillin presumably are not written for idolatry, unlike a Torah scroll; see Yoreh Deah end of siman 281. Magen Avraham s.k. 8. And explanation of his words from Machazik Berachah: meaning here he brought two views — some say burn, some say bury; and in Torah scroll in Yoreh Deah stam burn. The reason: granted tefillin — an apikoros's way is not to put on tefillin, and certainly he wrote them to sell to Israel; therefore we are not so concerned he wrote them for idolatry — therefore some say bury. Not so a Torah scroll: if an apikoros reads from it, possible he wrote for his own need, and if so certainly for idolatry — therefore for all burn. So Rabbeinu Yerucham. And see Bach — end.
