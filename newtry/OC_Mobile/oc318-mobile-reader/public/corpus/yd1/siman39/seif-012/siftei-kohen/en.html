It is good to give drink, etc. So Kol Bo: he received from his teachers, and from the expert sacrificers, that an animal to which water was not given before slaughter is entirely full of thin and thick sirchot, etc. And the Gaon, may his memory be for a blessing, said: it appears to me that this came out for them from what we say in the Talmud at the end of Beitzah (daf 40): why state "give it to drink and slaughter"? A matter incidentally teaches that a person should give his animal to drink and then slaughter, on account of sircha from thirst. And Rashi and the Ran explained that it should be easy to spread, because the adhesion sticks more to the flesh. And it appears that they explained on account of sirchot that detach on account of giving drink.
