(12) And when he comes to insert his hand he cuts gently to open the treifot cavity and tilts toward the ribs, and not more than needed for hand entry so he not scrape some sircha there. Some do not cut the treifot cavity with a knife but with a fingernail on account of this concern. Some cut only a small hole to insert a finger and examine around, and afterward widen the hole.
