(12) However, whoever does not put on tefillin — even if he does not do so to anger — is disqualified, for he is not in binding; and this is when he does not put them on because he is not concerned for them at all. But if he does not put them on out of appetite, such as one who goes after his business — fit to write. Magen Avraham s.k. 3. Razah there.
