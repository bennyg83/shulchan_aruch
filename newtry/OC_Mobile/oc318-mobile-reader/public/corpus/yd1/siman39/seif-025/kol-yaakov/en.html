(25) There: and some say bury them — behold wherever Maran wrote stam and afterward wrote "and some say" — according to Kenesset HaGedolah and Damiei, to inform there is concern for this view, as Machazik Berachah wrote Yoreh Deah siman 55 end of letter 4. If so, here too one should be concerned for the view of "some say," and let it be Maran's view to rely on the view he stated to permit definitively. Nevertheless in practice we are stringent, as Machazik Berachah brought there from Zivchei Tzedek part 1 siman 69 letter 29. If so, here too we are stringent.
