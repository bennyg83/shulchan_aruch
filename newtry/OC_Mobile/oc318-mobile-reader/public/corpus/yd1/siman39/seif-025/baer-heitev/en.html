In inflation. Taz wrote: I heard that one teacher declared treifah a lung in which discolored flesh was found; he peeled it and beneath it was like a scar — meaning wrinkles — for he understood that whatever is not beautiful is treifah even if not treifah otherwise; and it is not clear at all that we may add to the treifot, and the Rokeach never intended this prohibition, etc.; and see above siman 35 seif 8 what he wrote in the name of Maharshal (and see Peri Chadash: a lung lacking on the back of the uma like a crack while the skin and flesh remain, and opposite it on the wall it appears the rib was broken and healed — this is ri'uta and treifah.
