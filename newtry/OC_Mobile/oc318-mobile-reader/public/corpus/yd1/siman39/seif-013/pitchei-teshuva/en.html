And see responsa Chinuch Beit Yehudah siman 42: nevertheless, if it is impossible to check afterward by inflation, such as when a non-Jew made a puncture in the lung according to his understanding — one may be lenient — see there. And so I wrote above s.k. 1 in the name of responsa Givat Shaul. (And see above siman 36 s.k. 2.) And so ruled Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 60: sircha that passed by rubbing and before inflation was torn or lost — one may declare kosher, especially with great financial loss. However, he wrote: nevertheless one may not be lenient except when the sircha was pressed between his fingers by gentle rubbing. Indeed, in places where they are lenient and press somewhat forcefully, etc. — one may not combine two leniencies.
