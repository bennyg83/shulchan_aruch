And some permit, etc. And thus Maharshal, Piskei Treifot siman 25: "And the custom in all Ashkenaz is to press sirchot with the fingers, although the Rashba wrote that in this we did not hear from the sages in any place; and all who practice thus are as if feeding treifot to Israel." Such is it according to his view. But "I did not hear" is not proof. And regarding the words of those who permit, I say: blessed is He who chose them and their words, for the concept is sensible and accords with reason that it is so, etc. And likewise, since the Rosh wrote that the practice in all the land of Ashkenaz is: every sircha that detaches when the butcher inserts his hand gently to examine the lung — we say it is not sircha but mere moisture that spread from the moisture of the lung, and it is kosher. And the sircha — if a person presses it all day between his fingers — goes and strengthens — end of his words. Thus, if it detaches by this it is kosher. Indeed, I heard that some congregations who are not sons of Torah take a thick cloth and press with it; such people certainly feed treifot to Israel openly. And it is fitting to warn the butchers not to press so forcefully, but only to roll it between the fingers, and somewhat in a minimal manner — end of his words, Maharshal. And as the words of the Rosh, R' Yerucham also wrote, and thus: "And likewise those that detach upon the butcher's inserting his hand are not sirchot but the moisture of the lung; and so they practiced in Ashkenaz and in most places," etc. "And certainly it is so: every sircha that detaches when the butcher inserts his hand gently is moisture and the moisture of the lung, and it is kosher; for the sircha — if he detaches it all day in his fingers — behold, it goes more and strengthens" — end of his words. And he also brought in the Beit Yosef poskim who are lenient in this — see there:
Every place that one presses, etc. And after one presses, they practice examining it in spit or lukewarm water to see whether it does not bubble; and so all the acharonim:
