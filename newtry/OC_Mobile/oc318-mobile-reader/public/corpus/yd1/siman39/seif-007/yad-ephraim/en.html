Baer Heitev; and see in my book responsa Beit Avraham siman 24: regarding what some practice to inflate the lung before removing sircha to see if it rises in inflation — in my humble opinion there is no need, for if the sircha rises in inflation, after all the membrane is punctured and it is obvious it will bubble after removing the sircha; and one may say that after removal one no longer recognizes the exact place where the sircha stood, because in the reduction they practice they absorb much. That is: since in any case examination by reduction is required and they rely on reduction, examination for bubbling is only as a stringency, as Taz wrote — this suffices. And also the place of the sircha always remains recognizable and known to the examiner, and he can aim to examine that place well whether it bubbles there; therefore one need not [inflate first] except where they are accustomed — let them hold their custom — see there:
