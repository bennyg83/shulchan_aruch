Adhesions, etc., until entirely, etc. It implies that he holds like the conclusion of the Beit Yosef's explanation: that even though there is no window, any limb that is not entirely adhered along its length is treifah. And so it is implied in Darkei Moshe. But there are those who permit adhesion to a lobe or to a fat pedicle, even if there is a gap such that it split apart — as long as there is no window; that is, it is sealed on both sides and the gap is only between them. And this view was mentioned in the Beit Yosef; and so R' Meir in Simchat Habayis; and so some expert examiners practice:
From their heads to their ends. Requires study: what he wrote regarding adhesion to a lobe — that it is kosher as long as one cannot insert a finger between them. And although in the case of Ravyah in the Mordechai, and in the hagahah of Ashiri, and in the agudah, who permitted — one could not insert even a needle — nevertheless, there too they wrote in the name of Ravyah: one can insert a finger between them. Learn from this that less than this is kosher. And so it is implied in the Beit Yosef, that the measure of these adhesions is one. And it appears that the Rav was drawn after the words of Mahariv: regarding a lobe he wrote the measure of a finger, and regarding a pedicle he wrote: an uma adhered from its root to the end of the fat pedicle, etc. And the reason is perhaps because it is certain to them according to the Geonim: here this is their reclining, and here this is their reclining; and we do not say in treifot, "this is similar to that."
