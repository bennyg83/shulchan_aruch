If the ona or uma became sircha'd to a lobe or to the chest or to the fat of the heart or to its pocket or to the pericardium of the heart or to the pericardium of the liver or to the pericardium of the attachment cord that is the end of the pericardium of the heart, or to the womb, or to the alma'ra that they call mulliga, or to the membrane descending between the two arugot (R' Yitzchak ibn Chaviv and so is my view), or to the heart stalk or to the fat of the membranes or to the liver — treifah; and even if it adheres to one of these places without fenestration (there and other acharonim explained). {Rama: And there are those who are lenient if the upper onot adjacent to the lobe are actually adhered to the lobe and there is no gap between them at all such that one could insert a finger between them (Mordechai Perek Aleph Tav in the name of Ravyah, BeHag, Mahariu, and Agudah). And likewise if the onot or umas were fully adhered from their heads to their ends to the fat descending between the umas (there, Rabbenu Baruch, and R' Chaim). And likewise if the entire lung adhered to the spine — for they say this is the way of their reclining thus and this is not sircha (Mahariu). And one may not permit all this without asking a sage and expert who renders rulings; and one may not learn from these places to other places where the lung became sircha'd, for in other places there is no distinction between fully adhered or not (so is my view from Beit Yosef).}
