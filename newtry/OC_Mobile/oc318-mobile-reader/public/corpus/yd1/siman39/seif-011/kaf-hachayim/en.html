(11) There: except for the lung one must examine, etc. — this examination means the examiner inserts his hand inside gently and feels all parts of the lung softly with moderation, and returns his mind to feel whether there is deficiency, exchange, sirchot, or other treifot dependent on the lung. This examiner must be expert — not only expert in all laws of lung examination, but also expert in hand and feeling, knowing every form of the lung, its place, and manner of placing it inside the live animal.
