If he was uncertain whether it is, etc. — this is Rashba, and he gave the reason: it was slaughtered, it was permitted, and he established it in presumption of permissibility. And according to what Rama wrote below siman 50: wherever there is ri'uta we strike — we do not say "it was slaughtered, it was permitted." If so, what helps? And especially difficult on Rashba, who himself regarding charutah bidei shamayim, etc., wrote for himself: one may say ri'uta that arose in lifetime — we do not say "it was slaughtered, it was permitted." One must say: since we were revita (extra lobe), if it is at the cut we were revita and there is no ri'uta at all; therefore it is not clear to us that ri'uta arose in lifetime, and therefore we rely and establish on presumption — not so regarding charutah: even though it is bidei shamayim, nevertheless there is ri'uta, only it returned healthy; if so we do not establish on presumption since ri'uta was from lifetime. Examine well.
