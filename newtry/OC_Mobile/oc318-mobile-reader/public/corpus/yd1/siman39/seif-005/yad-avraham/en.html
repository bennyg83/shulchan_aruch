(Shulchan Aruch in the hagahah) However, if non-Jewish slaughterers are accustomed to do so, there is concern even b'dieved. It appears that if no sircha leak is found, it is no worse than what the Rambam wrote and Shulchan Aruch brought above seif 3: a non-Jew or Israelite came and removed the lung before examination while it exists — they inflate it. And according to acharonim who declare kosher there without great financial loss — likewise here. However, when he left it intentionally it is as intentional nullification; it appears from Shach's words it is forbidden for all. Not like Peri Megadim who wrote that for others it is permitted, as in every intentional nullification. And so in Hagurat Shmuel, brought in Lechem Shlomo. And it is not so: for Rivash wrote in responsum siman 498 regarding nullifying lung examination — it is as though nullified for the entire community, for whoever wishes to buy; even though it was not done for a specific person but stam for whoever wishes to buy — and behold it is as though done for them specifically. And proof from that which is in the second chapter of Eruvin regarding techumin: if he came to sell also for Israelites — likewise as though it came for them specifically. And again I saw that Tevuot Shmuel siman 16 and R' Luzzatto derive this from the Gemara in Bekhorot, and he too did not see Rivash already preceded in this. All this was hidden from acharonim here.
And in judgment: sircha to the treifot cavity and it happened he did not examine — the view of Noda B'Yehuda siman 15 is to forbid even with great financial loss. But if he examined and found a needle placed and not embedded — he permits; and likewise any needle found inside with a drop of blood outside.
