(5) And if he reached twenty years and did not bring two hairs, and signs of saris developed in him — behold he is an adult. Choshen Mishpat there seif 2. And see Orach Chaim siman 155 seif 12: for stam Maran, age nineteen years and thirty days, his law is like twenty years; and some say he is not considered twenty years until twenty less thirty days.
