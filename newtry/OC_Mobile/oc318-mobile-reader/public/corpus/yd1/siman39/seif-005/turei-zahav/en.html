On the back or on the cut. Meaning: it became sircha'd on the lip of the ona in order, only there is doubt whether it is from cut to cut and kosher, or from back and treifah — we follow the majority of animals that are kosher; and since there is doubt how it was when inflated in the animal's lifetime — whether much or little — we establish it in the presumption of permissibility; so Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba, from the reason "once slaughtered, it was permitted"; and see what I wrote from this in seif 17.
