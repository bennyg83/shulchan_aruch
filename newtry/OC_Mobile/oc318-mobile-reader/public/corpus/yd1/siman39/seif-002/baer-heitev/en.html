A slaughterer who slaughtered and has fat from it — may he l'chatchila tell a non-Jew to take the lung from it and throw it to dogs, and sell the meat to a non-Jew and rely on the presumption that it was kosher, and eat the fat from it? It appears that if he has milk that was milked within three days it is forbidden even b'dieved, for we are concerned for sircha; but for what was milked before three days it is permitted to do so, for even if sircha is found in it we do not forbid retroactively except [milk within] three days — and specifically where no ri'uta was found in the ribs. But if after they threw away the lung a great wound was found in the ribs whereby the lung had become sircha-adhered to the ribs — in such a case it is forbidden, for certainly it is an old wound.
