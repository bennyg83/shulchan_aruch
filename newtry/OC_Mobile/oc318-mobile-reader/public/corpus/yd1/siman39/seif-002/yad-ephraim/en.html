And some say to declare it kosher. (In Shulchan Aruch with Baer Heitev it is printed "and one may declare kosher," etc. — and this is a printing error.) And it is simple that in ksederan below half there is no distinction between animal and chayah, kids, lambs, and tender calves — in all of them one may permit in such a case. And see Har HaKarmel siman 1. And see in my book responsa Beit Avraham siman 10: I elaborated to clarify for the questioner the meaning of Rama's words and Taz's and Shach's words there; and I answered in Teshuvot VeHanhagot and Mishbetzot there — all with fine explanation.
