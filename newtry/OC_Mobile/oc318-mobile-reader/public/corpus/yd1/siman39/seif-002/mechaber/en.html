One who tore open the belly of the animal and before the lung was examined a dog or non-Jew came and removed it and went — behold it is permitted, and we do not say perhaps it was punctured or sircha'd. {Rama: And there are those who are stringent if the lung was lost (BeHag, Ra'avan, R' Yitzchak Levi, Mordechai, Hagahot Ashiri, and so stated); and one may not permit except in a place of great loss, or in kids, lambs, and chayyot in which sirchot are not common (Tur in the name of Ba'al HaEitan).}
