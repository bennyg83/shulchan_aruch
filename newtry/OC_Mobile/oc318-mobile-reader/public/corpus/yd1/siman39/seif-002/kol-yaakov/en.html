Amudei Shlomo there. And Machazik Berachah wrote there is a printing error. And what Peri Megadim in Orach Chaim wrote: when his beard was filled — it is a sign he presumably brought two hairs; and in siman 53 seif 8 in hagahah it implies "his beard was filled" means from age eighteen — see there; Melechet Shlomo wrote on this: in Machazik Berachah he erred in reading, for there it only says that below eighteen he is not called "beard filled" out of respect for the community; but to say age eighteen is Rava's presumption even leniently — this was not said nor hinted there; therefore these words of Magen Avraham stand in wonder, as truly all the aforementioned great ones disagree with him; and wonder on Chayei Adam klal 14 who copied them — end. And so Chayei Olam on Magen Avraham s.k. 1 rejected Peri Megadim's words — see there. And what Shemen HaMor wrote — what Magen Avraham wrote "until he is eighteen" means also his beard must be filled — see there; requires study, for according to his words the main [requirement] is missing; rather the meaning of Magen Avraham's words: since he reached eighteen without beard filled — fit to write. On this the aforementioned great ones wondered.
