(Siman 39, seif 2, in the hagahah) And one may not permit except in a place of great financial loss. And if one has a dangerous animal available to him and a slaughterer who knows how to slaughter — but he is not expert in lung examination — requires study whether one may permit him to slaughter and eat without examination on account of monetary loss; for if he waits, it will die or the meat will spoil. Meshiv Davar, Orach Chaim, siman 498, s.k. 4.
