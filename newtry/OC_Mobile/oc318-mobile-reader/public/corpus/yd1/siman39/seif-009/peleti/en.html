Sircha from place to place, etc.; and the view of Maharshal is to forbid according to the view of Rashi who holds there is no sircha without a puncture — requires study. If so, why does it teach "two onot that became sircha-adherent not in order" — to teach us a greater matter: even in that ona it is treifah, and all the more from ona to ona in a place that is not revita. Perhaps one may say for Maharshal: from ona to ona there is no examination at all, for certainly there is a puncture; but in that ona examination helps — only examination is required, and we are not expert in examination; if so, it is not difficult from Kol Bo that he holds we are expert in examination — requires study.
