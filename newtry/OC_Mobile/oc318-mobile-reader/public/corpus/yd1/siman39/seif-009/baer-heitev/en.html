Treifah. And Taz wrote: Maharshal disagrees on this and wrote: if it becomes sircha-adherent in the ona or in the uma itself, it is not called dependent sircha (and Shach rules like Shulchan Aruch; and this reasoning that Rama mentioned — from lung flesh longer than the sircha — is no proof at all, for perhaps even now it is equal [in length] but in its lifetime it was more inflated and was shorter. And Taz wrote further: according to what Rama wrote that examination is required even in ksederan, and we are not expert in examination — if there is sircha between the cut of this arugah and the kna, and all the more from the cut of this arugah to the cut of the arugah beside it — reduction and rubbing are required in every sircha; and if it does not bubble, then specifically kosher; but if it becomes sircha-adherent from the back of this arugah to the back of another arugah — treifah without examination — end):
Treifah. And the reason is: if it rises in inflation of the lung — treifah; so in Shach, because we say the lung was certainly punctured and therefore wind entered into the sircha and it rises. And sircha that detaches by inflation — R' Moshe Alsheich wrote in responsum siman 3 that it is treifah, and compares it to reduction; and it is implied according to us that we are lenient in reduction — likewise here also kosher.
