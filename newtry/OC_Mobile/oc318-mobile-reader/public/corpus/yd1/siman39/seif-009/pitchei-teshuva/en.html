That beneath it wrinkles. Shach: the reason is because in the end it will detach. And behold, if one cut the sircha and the wrinkle remained thus — it appears one may permit, for one cannot say perhaps in the end it would spread and tear, for we see its place remained. However, Tevuot Shmuel s.k. 52 and Peri Megadim wrote to forbid on account of treifah from two ri'utot, and further perhaps in lifetime the wrinkle would spread and reduction and rubbing would not help — see there. And see responsa Noda B'Yehuda, Chayei Adam there, who disagrees and concludes to declare kosher when the sircha passed by reduction and rubbing — see there. [And see responsa Chavat Daat siman 37, who elaborated and his view agrees with the aforementioned Noda B'Yehuda, concluding: although one who is lenient did not lose, nevertheless one who is stringent — may Heaven help him — see there.] Further Noda B'Yehuda wrote there: were I not afraid, I would say Rama forbade only when it does not pass by reduction and rubbing — for then we say it impedes the lung from inflating properly; but when it passes by reduction it has no power to impede the lung; but I fear to be lenient, etc. And as long as when they remove the sircha the wrinkle spreads — see there. And see Peri Megadim who wrote on this: with great financial loss one may side to be lenient with reduction — see there well. [And see responsa Chavat Daat siman 35 regarding lungs whose lower parts are made of flaws — one enters and one exits, and a lip of skin encircles the lung and connects the flaws, and sometimes they appear wrinkled thereby, but never does it make a window — only the lip of skin goes in and out equally with those flaws. I will tell the truth: all my days I saw thousands of such evidences and never heard anyone who doubted them; nor did any safek arise in my heart that this is only thinning and the lip of the lung skin itself cracking at the end of the lower part — until I came here I heard a certain gaon already arose on this and shook the world and investigated in large cities, and all agreed this is only thinning of the lung. And yet if they wrinkle the flesh much come and ask me, and my heart beats greatly on such, and I have nothing accepted to rely on, and I refrain from such — but I do not instruct others to prohibition — end.]
