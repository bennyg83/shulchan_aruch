Or in the uma itself, etc. So Kol Bo; and Beit Yosef's reason: if it were on account of a hole it would adhere to another ona; rather it is peeling of moisture; and it is implied from Rama's words that he agrees to this, only he distinguished whether it is not shorter than the flesh of the lung, etc. But Rosh and Tur wrote explicitly that it does not become sircha'd to another place — implying that if it became sircha'd to any place it is not called hanging sircha. And so Rashal ruled for halachah that it should not become sircha'd to any place — then specifically it is called hanging sircha. And what Beit Yosef wrote: since it did not become sircha'd to an ona, etc. — is not sound reasoning; for to whichever place it first touches in its adhesion it becomes sircha'd — end; and likewise the reasoning Rama mentioned from lung flesh longer than the sircha is no proof; for perhaps even now they are equal, but in its lifetime it was more inflated and was shorter. Rashal also wrote: so in the name of Mordechai — if it became sircha'd from the roof of this arugah to the roof of another arugah — treifah; and they wondered at him — why did he write it, for it is simple? It appears he was precise: specifically from roof of arugah; but below it, from cut of this arugah — kosher, because there the two arugot are in the narrow of the spine and do not separate, and we do not consider it an exposed place, for they are between two arugot; and know that otherwise from cut of this arugah to cut of another arugah that is treifah — it should have said so — end. And in my humble opinion: the people of Candia hold that in every in-order case examination is not required, as Shulchan Aruch seif 4; therefore they maintain sircha from cut of the arugah to the stalk between the arugot as sircha in order, and forbid only sircha from roof to stalk or roof to roof. But according to what Rama wrote there that examination is required even in order, and we are not expert in examination — here too one may say thus: if there is sircha between cut of the arugah and the stalk — and all the more from cut of this arugah to cut of the arugah beside it — reduction and rubbing are required as with every sircha; and if it does not bubble, then specifically kosher — simple.
