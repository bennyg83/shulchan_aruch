(9) If sircha is found in fowl lung — wherever it is sircha-adherent to ribs it is kosher; but if sircha-adherent toward the face — treifah. Nevertheless we are not beholden to examine except beheimah and chayah. Rashbatz in Sefer Yavin Shemuah daf 16. Machazik Berachah letter 6, part 4 there. Beit Lechem Shmuel there. Einat Nachum there. Pithei Teshuvah letter 2. Beit Yitzchak there. Kometz there. Zivchei R' Baruch in Temidim there. Manot Yosef there. Zivchei Tzedek letter 36.
(9) Sometimes through geese their lung appears to have sircha and it is not sircha, for geese' lungs are commonly thus. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz letter 2 from expert examiners. And in responsa Ohel Yosef he doubted this; and certainly one may not declare kosher except through a very expert God-fearing person who will say this is their nature. Machazik Berachah letter 7.
