From place to place, etc. The Beit Yosef wrote: even according to the one who says there is no sircha without a hole — for if it were truly sircha'd, it would adhere to another ona; and since it adheres to that ona itself, it is certainly not sircha but only the peeling of the moisture of the lung, and it did not come on account of a hole. Maharshal, in his examinations and in Piskei Treifot, argued against him without a necessary reason. And his view is: perhaps as we rule, there is no sircha without a hole — from place to place is treifah. And the view of the Mechaber and the Rav appears to be primary, for Kol Bo is the source of this law that from place to place is permitted. He holds there is no sircha without a hole. And moreover, Mahariv also permits from place to place; and so the acharonim; and so they practice:
But if it is short, etc. The reason was written by Mahar'r Avraham of Prague: because it is destined to detach. And so it is in the examinations of the acharonim and in the Eitan Tzvi. And Darkei Moshe wrote: requires study — what of it that it is destined to detach? Is this not sircha not on account of a hole but only suspended? It appears to me the reason is: since it cannot rise in inflation, it is like a blocked lung — end of his words. And this reason is forced. Rather, it appears to me as Mahar'e and his supporters wrote: and although this sircha is not on account of a hole, nevertheless when it detaches there will be a hole — like the one who says there is sircha without a hole, that not in order is treifah because it is destined to separate and there will be a hole. It appears clear to me:
That it also rises, etc. For then certainly the lung was punctured; and therefore the wind enters inside the sircha and it rises — end of his words; old examinations in Darkei Moshe. And sircha that detaches through inflation is written in the responsum of R' Moshe Alshkar, siman 2, that it is treifah; and he compares it to contraction. And it implies that for us, who are lenient regarding contraction, the same applies here:
