Sircha that goes from place to place in an ona or in the uma itself — its law is like the law of hanging sircha. {Rama: And specifically when the sircha is not shorter than the flesh of the lung when they inflate the lung; but if it is short such that on account of the sircha the flesh beneath it wrinkles and does not rise well in inflation like the rest of the lung — treifah (Mahar'r Avraham of Prague and examinations of the acharonim). And every hanging sircha that also rises with inflation of the lung, or that has a wound on the wall opposite it — treifah (old examinations).}
