If the uma became sircha'd to the wall — if there is a wound on the wall — kosher, and it does not require examination; and if there is no wound on the wall — treifah, and examination does not help for it. And specifically for sircha to the wall do we declare kosher through a wound; but if sircha'd to other places such as chest, lobe, fat of the heart, pericardium, and the like — even if there is a wound in them — treifah, and examination does not help for it.
