And Rama: if that ri'uta he felt inside would render treifah if found afterward, and he did not examine outside — treifah; but if it would not render treifah and he did not examine outside — b'dieved kosher. Practical difference for us: if lung was lost after he examined inside and did not examine outside by inflation — kosher if that ri'uta would not render treifah.
