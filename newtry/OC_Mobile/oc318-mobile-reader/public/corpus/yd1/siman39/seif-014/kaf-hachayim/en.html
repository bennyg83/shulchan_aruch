(14) Also for this a wise heart should tremble when coming to examine the lung: he should set before his eyes where he is fit to declare kosher food for many souls; his way is hidden and none sees, for Hashem is high and low sees; he should set all his eyes on the lung to examine gently and with great alacrity, and not insert his hand with arm and mighty hand forcefully. Know what is above you: behold Hashem stands over him to repay the man whose way is hidden for every hidden matter, and He will requite his hands. The essence of all: be alacritous to examine in fear of Hashem on his face, in awe and dread; and he should not speak during examination with any person, nor incline his ear to another matter — for with his soul he speaks; perhaps he will detach some sircha unknowingly and he will not know, and he bears guilt and sins of many depend on him. When he feels all parts of the lung he should feel gently and with great moderation, be expert in all laws of the lung, all its statutes and judgments; examination is only to tell a person the straight path in examination, requiring great intent; fear of Hashem is his treasure. The examiner should be kosher and God-fearing from many; who is he that will guarantee saying he is kosher and God-fearing? A wise person will hear and add learning; more, children — beware! And there will be no disaster — also blessed he will be. Toldot Zahav siman 7 letter 4. Zivchei Tzedek letter 5.
