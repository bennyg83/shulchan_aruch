The first is believed, etc. Perhaps the second examiner inserted his hand forcefully and moved the sircha, or the first moved it when withdrawing his hand — end of his words, R' Yerucham; and the Rosh's responsum. And Darkei Moshe wrote: and it appears to me that even for us, who permit all sirchot that dissolve through handling — nevertheless it is treifah, lest he moved it forcefully — end of his words. And so Maharshal, Piskei Treifot siman 25. And the Beit Yosef wrote: from the words of Rabbenu Yerucham one can imply the opposite of what the poskim wrote — that sirchot that detach when the butcher inserts his hand and examines are not sirchot.
