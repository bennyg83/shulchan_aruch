Nevertheless, one may say specifically in adhesion it must detach from both ends, for otherwise even complete sircha easily detaches when the repelling hand comes; but here, since it was already cut — if it were complete sircha it would not pass through inserting his hand, for it does not impede his hand — behold sircha that passed by reduction — see there.
