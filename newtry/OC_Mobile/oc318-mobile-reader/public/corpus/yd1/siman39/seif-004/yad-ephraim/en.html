Sircha. See Har HaKarmel siman 2, where he wrote: when the middle lobe is large, long and thick, and sircha stuck going out from it to the heart fat and passed by reduction — in a place where the custom is not to declare treifah, one may declare kosher with great financial loss. And it does not appear to me in this that reduction is a great leniency; if so, one may say it is treifah from two ri'utot, attributing that even the largeness of the middle lobe is not natural but illness caused it to swell and enlarge; and likewise if the sircha is long and thick, one may say illness caused it to shrink, and the stuck sircha indicates this — and all the more according to what Taz and Shach wrote in the name of Maharshal himself: 24 illnesses one must declare treifah for these alternatives. And what [they] say to call this illness lenient, that sircha by reduction is a different illness — for sircha is always common, etc. And in Machazik Berachah these words are muktzeh from the mind, as acharonim explain; and what he doubted on Bach's words to support the law of treifah from two ri'utot from the beginning of Niddah — he wrote to distinguish between things that exist, etc.:
