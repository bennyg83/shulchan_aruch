The statement of Rava there on daf 47.
(1) Rashi explains: because there is no sircha without a puncture, etc., and a little goes out little by little through the puncture and becomes a membrane; even though the puncture is sealed, the membrane that arose on account of a wound is not a [true] membrane, for in the end it will come apart; but when it is sircha-adherent in ksederan (aligned order), its fellow seals it and protects the puncture, and the membrane grows stronger. And Tosafos explained: that sircha is accustomed to come healthy without a puncture, because it draws all liquids, etc.; and that which is treifah when not in order — this is because in the end it will come apart and is considered like a puncture since in the end it will be punctured.
