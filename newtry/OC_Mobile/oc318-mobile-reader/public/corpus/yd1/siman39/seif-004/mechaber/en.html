An ona that became sircha'd to an ona or to an uma — whether sircha'd a little or entirely — not in order — treifah; in order — kosher (Ran and chiddushim of Rashba) even without examination (Rosh and so stated) (however if they examined it and a hole was found — treifah) (Beit Yosef). And it is not called "in order" except when two [adhesions] are one next to the other from cut of one to cut of the other, and even if it is diagonal and even if there is fenestration — but from back of one to back of the other, or even from cut to back, or from lower slopes to lower slopes, or from lower slopes to cut, or even from point of one to point of the other that is adjacent, or even from point of one to cut of the other that is adjacent — is called not in order. {Rama: And some say that even in order examination is required that there is no hole in the lung (Semak, Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Ritzba, Tosafot in the name of Rabbenu Gershom and more Geonim and Kol Bo). And since we are not expert in examination — wherever there is ri'uta one should declare treifah every sircha (Hagahot Maimoniyot, Semak, Semag, Kol Bo). And some say to declare kosher if it is from the root of the onot until halfway (Mahariu). Even if fenestrated — and so they practice to declare kosher thus in order. And likewise if there is like a type of membrane between ona and ona or between uma and ona — in order — from its root until its middle one does not declare treifah, for it is the way to be found thus and this is not sircha (Semag, Hagahot Maimoniyot, Semak, Kol Bo). And this way of membrane is not found except between ona and ona or between ona and uma — but not in other places (Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe). And if found in another place it is only sircha, and wherever sircha is kosher — even if there is hanging sircha also on those sirchot — and it is called double sircha — kosher (Beit Yosef).}
