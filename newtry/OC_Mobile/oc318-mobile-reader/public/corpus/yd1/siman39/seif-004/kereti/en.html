In ksederan it is kosher. Shach brought two views: either the reason is there is no sircha without a puncture, or in the end it will be punctured. See Peleti what I wrote regarding the practical difference between "in the end it will be punctured" versus "it was already punctured."
If it is from the root of the ona — for these laws are confused in the authors, each crying here and there, and most slaughterers rely on everything on reduction — I did not wish to elaborate, for the words of the Mechaber and Rama suffice, and it is permitted to rule from Mans' practical halachah.
