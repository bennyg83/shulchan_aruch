Permissibility. The reason we say "it was slaughtered, it was permitted"; and what was written in the previous seif — a slaughterer who inserted his hand, etc., they declare it kosher — it appears this deals when the slaughterer explicitly says he found treifot in the lung; but if he said stam "treifah" he can say he found some puncture in the courtyard of the liver that the second did not find, for the examiner is not obligated to examine afterward; and even if he says "I searched in the courtyard of the liver and did not see a puncture there" — "I did not see" is not proof, for perhaps the first saw some small puncture that this one did not see — end, Taz:
