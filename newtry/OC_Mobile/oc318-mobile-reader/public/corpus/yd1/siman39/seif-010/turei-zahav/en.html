Every place they forbade, etc. So Rashal: regarding shaking — if we can shake it even though it does not detach by reduction of the hand — the custom is to permit it in an animal of Israel, and specifically a large animal; and so in the name of Maharil: when they shake the lung and the sircha remains in the flesh of the lung — treifah, because there is no sircha without a hole; but if it remains in the heart fat — kosher, and we judge it as ordinary moisture — end. And this is straight reasoning: if it remained in the flesh of the lung and did not detach except from the fat — the matter is revealed that it is from the lung; but if it detached in the middle — there is no proof here that it came from the lung — end. And he wrote further that one may shake even many times, and they practice cutting the liver before shaking — end. And it is explained in Beit Yosef that permission to shake applies only when sircha'd to heart fat specifically; and it is simple that examination is also required for this permission. But Beit Yosef wrote the matter is simple that we are not expert in this shaking — therefore they did not bring it in Shulchan Aruch; accordingly a person should not rely on shaking except with a great sage.
