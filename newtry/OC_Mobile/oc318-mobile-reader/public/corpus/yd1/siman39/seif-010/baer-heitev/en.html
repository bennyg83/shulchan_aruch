For Israel. And Taz wrote in the name of Maharshal regarding shaking: if one can shake it even though it does not detach by reduction of the hand — the custom is to permit it in animals of Israel, and specifically in a large animal; and in the name of Maharil: if one shakes the lung and the sircha remains in the flesh of the lung — treifah, because there is no sircha without puncture; but if it remains in the heart fat — kosher, and we judge it as ordinary lung; and he wrote further that one may shake even many times, and they practice cutting the liver before the shaking; however the matter is simple that we are not expert in this — therefore a person should not rely on this except [with] a great sage (and Shach testified that we do not practice shaking at all):
