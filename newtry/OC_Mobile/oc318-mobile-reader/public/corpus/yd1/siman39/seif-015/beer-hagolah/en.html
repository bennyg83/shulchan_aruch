Rambam there, Rosh, and Rabbenu Yerucham wrote: even according to those who hold there is no sircha without a puncture — because sircha that comes on account of a puncture, on account of moisture going out through the puncture, becomes thick and strong and becomes sircha-adherent to whatever place it encounters; but dependent sircha is only spreading of moisture of the lung and does not come from a place of puncture.
