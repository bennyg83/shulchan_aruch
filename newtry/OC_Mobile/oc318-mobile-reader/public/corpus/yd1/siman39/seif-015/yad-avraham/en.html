(Shulchan Aruch seif 15) A slaughterer who inserted his hand and said "treifah," and afterward it became clear the treifot was not opened — they declare it kosher. This deals when he explicitly says he found treifot in the lung (as Taz wrote). But when he says stam "treifah," one may say he found a flaw on the knife after slaughter that the other does not know — for such is the custom among slaughterers: when a flaw is found on the knife they attribute the treifah to the examination.
