By reduction. Baer Heitev in the name of Shach. And see responsa Har HaKarmel, Chayei Adam end of siman 1, who wrote: according to Shach, in calves we require specifically that it nurses; the time given for nursing he explained means: not to call it nursing — i.e., after it ceased nursing above; if so, there is remedy in tender calves even within a year while nursing — withhold them above without nursing, and again sircha therein becomes permitted by reduction — see there. [And in the name of the Rav Gaon R' Moshe Zev z"l, who was av beit din of the holy community Bialystok, I heard he did not agree to this and said: the measure of nursing he explained means not to call it nursing — i.e., after it ceased three days above, as regarding an infant below siman 81 seif 7.] And what Baer Heitev wrote; and see Peri Chadash who disagrees — meaning he holds the law is the same in calves even if not nursing, as long as they are called calves in human language; and so the view of Tevuot Shmuel. And see responsa Har HaKarmel there, who wrote this depends on custom; and where there is no known custom one may rely on Bach's words: within a year and not nursing, again reduction helps — see there. And see responsa Givat Shaul siman 58: an incident occurred before him — lung of a calf that already ceased nursing, safek whether within its year, wherein sircha passed by reduction and rubbing — he declared kosher and wrote: specifically in such a case of safek whether within its year; but certainly within its year, even if not nursing — certainly one may not permit — see there. And what Baer Heitev wrote in the name of Bach. See responsa Givat Shaul there, who wrote this is specifically kids and lambs, not calves; and even in kids and lambs one may doubt whether to rely on this custom, since this view is not found in any acharon — see there.
