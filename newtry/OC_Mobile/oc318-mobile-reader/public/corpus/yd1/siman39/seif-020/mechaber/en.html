That which we declare kosher — specifically when the hole is opposite the wall; but if it was punctured from inside, or even in the lower slopes of the lung and sircha emerges from there to the wall — treifah. And if sircha emerges from the lower slopes of an ona and spreads on the back of the ona and becomes sircha'd to the wall — if the majority of the sircha is on the back of the ona — kosher; and if not — treifah.
