If the majority of the sircha, etc. Tur wrote that Rosh agreed to this; and Beit Yosef challenged: behold above Tur wrote in the name of Rosh that when opposite the ribs and flesh it is forbidden, since it is also opposite the ribs; and they answered many answers — and I do not know what the original difficulty was; for there the hole was clearly opposite the ribs and opposite the flesh — therefore sealing of the flesh does not help, since the portion of the hole opposite the ribs is not sealed; unlike here where there is sircha — even though there is no sircha without a hole, nevertheless the hole is not as large as the sircha, but the hole raised moisture around it until it enlarged; nevertheless we say: if the majority of the sircha is from the ona — then the portion of the sircha in which the hole is — is from there, only it expanded and made sircha also opposite the uma where there is no hole; and likewise the reverse — so it appears to me, simple. And so if hanging sircha emerges at the lower slopes of the uma and at the end of the sircha it is thick and hangs on it like a button and appears like a full boil — nevertheless it appears kosher, for it is peeling of a lobe; nevertheless examination is required — end. And I say: it was a boil at the lower slopes — treifah. In my youth I saw an incident with one expert lung butcher: one thin bone, slightly long, rested on the lung from outside and adhered to its skin in width; he peeled it from it and inflated and it did not bubble, and he declared kosher on the grounds that examiners say from the animal's ribs it peeled and adhered to the lung beneath the ribs; and afterward I saw this law printed in the name of one great authority. And to me it appears treifah, since it is possible to puncture the lung — examination is required by Talmud law like a lung where he felt a needle in siman 36 seif 16; and we are not expert in examination, as Rama wrote this siman seif 4; and according to what I wrote in every matter whose kosher status depends on examination — by law; and further one should be concerned perhaps there is also a hole in another place; and so I heard from Maharam Yafeh z"l.
