And rubbing. Shach wrote: and likewise all the sirchot explained in this siman — in all of them they practice pressing; and this is the rule — keep in your hand: all sirchot in the world they practice pressing and examining afterward whether it rises in inflation, except ksederan — meaning from cut to cut below half toward their root; and dependent sircha; and sircha attached to the windpipe or to the fat of the spleen or to the spinal column in the manner explained in this siman — kosher; and likewise if the veredah stuck below at its root in seif 3 — all these are kosher without reduction and without any examination, because their majority is thus; so they practice in these lands, and so is in the words of the acharonim (and Peri Chadash wrote: we do not practice pressing; and in ksederan we declare kosher in every manner even above half; and likewise attached to the spinal column; also veredah stuck at its root we declare kosher; but attached to windpipe or fat of the spleen they declare treifah — end. And Pithei Teshuva wrote: Heaven forbid to press or rub in a place of "two for ri'uta." And see Beit Yosef who wrote: if the animal was fat and the fat of the breast hangs from the chest on the wall and became sircha-adherent there — kosher). And Taz wrote that one should not press sircha attached emerging from a boil or from a tinnir; and wrote further that he saw an incident with one expert slaughterer: a thin long bone lay on the lung outside and stuck to its skin along its width, and he peeled it from it and inflated and it did not bubble, and he declared kosher because examiners say that from the animal's ribs it peels and sticks to the lung beneath the ribs; and it appears to me treifah, since it is possible to puncture the lung — examination is required by Talmudic law as with a lung where one felt a needle, siman 36 s.k. 96; and we are not expert in examination as Rama wrote in this siman seif 4; and further one should be concerned lest it was also punctured in another place; and so I heard from Maharam Yafeh z"l — end (and Peri Chadash declares kosher, for presumably it is only from the animal's ribs. If found on the lung fat called wurtzlin — one must peel from the lung gently; and if the lung beneath the wurtzlin is fine and whole — kosher. Pithei Teshuva):
