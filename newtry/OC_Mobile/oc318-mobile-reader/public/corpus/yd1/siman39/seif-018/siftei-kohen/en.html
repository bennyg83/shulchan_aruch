And it was already explained, etc. And likewise all the sirchot that will be explained in this siman — in all of them they practice pressing. And this is the rule — take in your hand: all sirchot in the world, they practice pressing and afterward checking whether it rises in inflation — except not in order, that is, from cut to cut below their halves toward the side of their roots; and hanging sircha; and sircha adhered to a lobe or to the fat pedicle or to the spine in the manner explained in seif 7 — then kosher. And likewise if an attachment cord adhered below at its root — seif 6 — all these are kosher without pressing and without any examination, because this is their reclining.
