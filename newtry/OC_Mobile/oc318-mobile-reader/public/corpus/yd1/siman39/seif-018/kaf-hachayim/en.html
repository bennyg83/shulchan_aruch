(18) There: except for the lung one must examine, etc. — although from Shulchan Aruch's language it implies one examines only after sirchot, i.e., b'dieved; l'chatchila one must examine for all treifot in it. Tevuot Shmuel letter 3. And so Beit Lechem Shmuel letter 2: all agree lung must be examined for all treifot in it. And so Toldot Zahav siman 8 letter 3: not specifically sirchot but even other treifot such as boils, disqualifying appearances, and other prohibitions from siman 35 until siman 49 and their offshoots require examination, for they are common, and one may not follow majority of healthy lung at all — see there.
