(18) And likewise blackening their straps by a woman is kosher, for it is not among repair of their making; but a minor requires leshem and an adult must stand over him. Knot of the dalet — and also of the arm — should appear; one should not have a woman do it, for she is also included in writing. Peri Megadim, Orach Chaim letter 6. And Mishnat Zahav siman 33 letter 5. Melechet Shlomo klal 1 letter 4. And what Melechet Shlomo wrote in Binah letter 10 regarding blackening straps by a woman — requires study; aside from answering his words, one may not leave Peri Megadim's certainty on account of his safek.
