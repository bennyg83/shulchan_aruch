One who deveined and left, and we do not know whether he is proficient in deveining, and there is no one here proficient in deveining to show him — it is permitted: {Rama: And specifically when we know that he completed his deveining; and if not, it is forbidden {Rama: (perhaps he did not complete the deveining) (Ran in the name of Rivba)}. And if it is possible to show a proficient person, one should not rely on the deveiner (Beit Yosef from the implication of the Rosh) unless we know him to be proficient in deveining and established in kashrut; and even though most of those found with deveining are experts, nevertheless it is good to be stringent (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba).
