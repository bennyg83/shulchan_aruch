Perhaps he did not, etc. — see Peleti what I wrote on this:
Nevertheless it is good to be stringent, and especially in our time when stringencies multiplied from Baal HaItur and the like, and the effort is heavy on him, and they are not mostly Torah scholars, and they are stringent and lenient where it is forbidden. And behold in my time there was one deveiner, even a Torah scholar and very expert, who became confused in his mind to say about another sinew that it is the true sinew; and until this day we and our fathers erred in a sinew that is not the sinew that the Torah forbade, and he went and returned throughout the land of Ashkenaz and alarmed the creatures until he came to Prague and presented his words before me and before the sages of the city, the Geonim. And I investigated and examined the matter and found that this sinew is only in male animals and not in female animals; then I showed him Semag, which wrote that gid hanasheh applies to males and females, and thereby his words were silenced; and in any case one should not rely on deveining except on a proficient person who fears God; from many I stood on my view that I learned the laws of deveining to be proficient in them and in their names — I did not rely on a deveiner except for what I deveined myself and toiled as I ate:
