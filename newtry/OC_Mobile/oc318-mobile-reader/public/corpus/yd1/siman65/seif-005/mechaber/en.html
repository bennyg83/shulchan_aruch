The sciatic nerve applies to domestic animals and wild animals, even if their spoon is not round; and it applies to the right thigh and the left thigh; and it does not apply to birds because their spoon is not round; but if it has a round spoon, it applies to it; and one need not check afterward whether it is round: {Rama: And there is no distinction between a wild animal and a domestic animal in anything forbidden due to the sinew or due to blood; and likewise in a bird if it had a spoon — therefore everything that is deveined in a domestic animal must be deveined in a wild animal as well (Mordechai, end of chapter HaShochet); only what is forbidden due to milk need not be deveined in a wild animal:}
