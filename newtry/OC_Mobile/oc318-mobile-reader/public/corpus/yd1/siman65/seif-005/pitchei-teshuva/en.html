The sciatic nerve. [See in responsa Chavot Yair siman 69 regarding what the Kohen-Priest deveiner alarmed the world that gid hanasheh is one sinew that is found only in male animals and not in females, and the sages of Prague found no answer until he showed him in Semag, which wrote that the mitzvah of gid hanasheh applies to males and females — if so, it is not that sinew. And many wondered — this is a mistake of Semag: it deals with that gid hanasheh applies to Israelite males and females, but it does not deal with whether the sinew is found in males and females. Also in Sefer Tolodot Adam part 2 he counted this among errors, to teach that the trap of mistake is spread over all the living (small as great); and since it is a mistake and he rejected this with a reed, the words of the deveiner stand, and one should not eat hindquarters unless two sinews were deveined from them. And he wrote to explain the words of the Kohen-Priest above with good reason — Heaven forbid he erred at all in this, and the words of the sages stand, see there]:
