(Siman 65, hagahah seif 11) In the Tur he wrote: therefore a person sends a thigh to a non-Jew, etc., and if he does not give it to a non-Jew, etc. The Tur deals anonymously — that he did not explain to the non-Jew that he sells it to him with the presumption of kashrut and deveining; and therefore there is no concern for stealing the mind of orphans — lest he give it to him in the presence of another Israelite; we are not concerned for this either, as the Rosh wrote to Rashi on this, and as the Tur wrote in siman 117. And Beit Yosef, after he brought the Rosh's words, wrote, and these are his words: and such are the words of Rabbeinu, who wrote that even though he holds a favor for him, it is permitted because there is no stealing the mind in a gift; and he also wrote: and if he does not give it to a non-Jew in the presence of an Israelite, he may give it to teach us that we are not concerned lest he give it in the presence of an Israelite, etc., end of his words. And certainly he understood that the Tur deals anonymously; and on this he wrote that the view is like Rashi's view — anonymously; and he wrote that a person sends to a non-Jew a thigh, etc., whether it is whole or cut, and we are not concerned for stealing the mind that he holds a favor for him because he sent him a cut thigh — for as long as it was fit for himself he sent it to a non-Jew, and nevertheless it is permitted, as Rashi explained; and nevertheless it is permitted because anonymously we are not concerned for stealing the mind. And he also wrote: and if they do not give it to him in the presence of an Israelite, etc., and one should not be concerned lest he give it in the presence of another Israelite — rather certainly as we said, that anonymously we are not concerned; this is Beit Yosef's intent; and also what he wrote that there is no stealing the mind in a gift — he used usual language, for the Rosh also used such language; but in truth there is no distinction between sale and gift, and not like Bach who challenged Beit Yosef; and according to what I wrote, it is fine; and requires further study:
