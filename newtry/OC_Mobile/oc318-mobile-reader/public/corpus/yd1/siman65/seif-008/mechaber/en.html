There are two sinews in one thigh — an inner one adjacent to the bone and an outer one adjacent to the flesh; and both are forbidden; and one must scrape after them; only the inner one is forbidden from the Torah, and it is the one that extends along the entire thigh; and the outer one is forbidden rabbinically; and the tendons in both of them are forbidden rabbinically; and one must scrape after them; and their fat — Israel, the holy ones, treated it as forbidden: {Rama: And the heads of the tendons enter into the heads of the bones; therefore the one who deveins must break the heads of the bones in order to remove the tendons from their root (Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Ashiri).
