(Seif 3) The wing of a bird is like the hand of an animal; therefore one must cut the wing bones, etc. Hagahah: and there are those who say one need not remove in a bird, etc., and such is the custom, etc. — and such is the simple custom also in our time. Shach s.k. 7. For the Prisha, letter 6. Bina VeDa'at in Shaarei Perakim, letter 5. And so Shulchan Gavoah, letter 14 — the custom is as the Rav wrote in the hagahah. And Radbaz siman 303 wrote that they cut the head of the wing in a bird, and thereby blood exits; and so the foreleg. Machazik Berachah, letter 4; and so Zivchei Tzedek, letter 8.
