The wing of a bird is like the hand of an animal; therefore one must cut the wing bones and salt them, and likewise the cheek bones in a bird: {Rama: And there are those who say that one need not remove these veins in a bird because they are thin and the blood exits from there through salting (Ran, chapter HaShochet, in the name of R' Yitzchak), and such is the custom — that one does not remove any veins from a bird except the neck veins, and in those one must be careful to remove them from there or cut them with the neck vertebra in two; nevertheless, b'dieved, if only the head was removed from the bird it suffices (the Long in the name of Or Zarua). And it is customary to cut at the thighs of birds at the lower knee joint because sometimes red veins are found in the legs of birds; and if one did not do so, it is permitted b'dieved even if they were cooked thus, even if red veins were found (all of this in Mordechai, end of chapter HaShochet) — for it is only mere fat:}
