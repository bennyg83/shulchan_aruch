(Shulchan Aruch seif 14) One does not buy meat from any butcher who slaughters for himself and sells to others unless he was established in kashrut. It implies that by law it is forbidden. And it requires further study — for above siman 18 seif 8 the hagahah wrote that they practiced in some places that a person does not slaughter and check for himself, only those appointed by the community — implying that by law it is not forbidden; and so the later authorities state there explicitly.
