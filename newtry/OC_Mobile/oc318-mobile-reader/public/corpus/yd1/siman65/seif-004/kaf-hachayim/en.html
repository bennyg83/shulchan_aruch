(Seif 4) Male eggs, etc. — forbidden for a pot without peeling. And the same — cutting and salting help with them. Shach s.k. 9. Peri Chadash, letter 10. For the Prisha, letter 8. Beit Lechem Yehudah, letter 4. Shaarei Dura, letter 9. Bina VeDa'at in Shaarei Perakim, letter 7:
Kenesset HaGedolah wrote in Hagahot Beit Yosef, letter 23 — specifically eggs of a kid and lamb; but eggs of an ox — even within thirty days one must devein the membrane on them; and according to custom, even kid and lamb they practice thus; nevertheless b'dieved one need not forbid. Shaarei Dura there. Bina VeDa'at there. And this is if they are within thirty, as stated below letter 16:
There. But for roasting they are permitted — even without cutting, since they are exposed and stand with their faces on the fire. Shach s.k. 10. For the Prisha, letter 9. Shaarei Dura, letter 10. Bina VeDa'at in Shaarei Perakim, letter 8:
