Taz s.k. 2 wrote that it is permitted to roast liver when it is wrapped in paper on the fire, and we do not say the paper interposes between the liver and the fire and it is like cooking. But Mishnat Hasidim, letter 2, doubted this; and even the Gaon Maharar Ozer wondered at Taz in this — for it is implied from his words that the same applies to meat rescued in paper; and he wrote that it is obvious that meat or brain rescued wrapped in paper is forbidden even b'dieved, and so with liver. Machazik Berachah, letter 2; and so in Binat Adam in the section Orach Chaim, letter 41 — that they already wrapped in paper and roasted — forbidden, not like Taz.
