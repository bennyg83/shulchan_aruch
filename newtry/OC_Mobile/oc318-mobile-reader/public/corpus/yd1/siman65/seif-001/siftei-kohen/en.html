And if one did not remove them, etc. — In my book I proved from the words of Rabbeinu Yerucham, N'tivat Chayim, letter 25, regarding the dispute of R' Efraim and the Rosh — that in the neck sinews one does not need sixty, since one sections the animal limb by limb; only that l'chatchila one must remove them. And I explained there that this is indeed the meaning of the Rosh's words in chapter HaShochet; and thereby the Rosh's words do not contradict each other — for in chapter HaShochet he challenged R' Efraim and wrote that we do not find veins forbidden due to blood except the hand and the foreleg, which do not squirt out — namely the neck sinews; and in chapter Gid HaNasheh he wrote that those which squirt out — namely the neck sinews — require cutting for a pot; only that in chapter HaShochet he wrote that they are not forbidden due to blood and are permitted in cooking b'dieved, only that because of themselves they require cutting l'chatchila; and so it appears from the words of Shaarei Dura siman 34, see there; and so too the Tur in siman 22 regarding veins — that if one sections it limb by limb it is permitted in cooking. And regarding this one of my study partners aroused me — the exalted Rav R' Mordechai Ashkenazi, may his light shine — and if so, one must say that the Rav in the hagahah does not refer to neck veins; and that which he wrote anonymously is because in neck veins too one sometimes finds this law when one did not cut them limb by limb.
