(Seif 1) There are veins forbidden due to blood in them, such as veins in the hand, etc. — and even when he slaughtered the neck veins, as stated above siman 62, these veins are forbidden because they are not drawn out through the path of the veins — Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba and Ran. Shach s.k. 1.
