The forbidden veins due to blood and due to milk, and the laws of the sciatic nerve. And it has 14 seifim:
There are veins that are forbidden due to the blood in them, such as the veins in the hand, and in the shoulder, and in the lower jaw on the side of the tongue from here and from there, and in the tongue, and in the neck, and the heart veins, and the veins of the tail, and the veins of the testicles that are inside the fat of the testicles, tangled together like a spider's web: {Rama: And it is customary to remove also three long veins near the breast from the inside (Or Zarua and the Long), and likewise they remove veins behind the ears (the order of deveining and the Long, and Kereti — and it is only a mere stringency) (in my humble opinion):} And the membrane of the brain that is in the skull, and the membrane on the testicles — salting does not help with them unless one cuts them first {Rama: (and cut underneath) (Ran in the name of Raavad and Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba and Tur siman 66 in the name of — there are Gaonim)} and afterward one salts them; and this applies to cooking them in a pot; but for roasting they do not require cutting nor salting, because fire draws and extracts. And there is one who says that specifically when the faces of the veins are toward the fire, but if they are embedded within the meat the fire does not draw unless one cuts them {Rama: (Rashba, and see above siman 67 in Beit Yosef):} {Rama: And if one did not remove them and cooked them without cutting, one needs sixty per each of the veins (Rashba); and for roasting, peeling suffices (Hagahot Ashiri and Hagahot Shaarei Dura in the name of Maharai siman 33). And see above siman 22.}
