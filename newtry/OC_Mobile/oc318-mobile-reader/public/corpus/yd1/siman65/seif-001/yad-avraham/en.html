(Siman 65 seif 1) There are veins forbidden due to blood in them, such as veins in the hand, etc., and veins of the testicles that are inside the fat of the testicles, etc., and salting does not help with them unless one cuts them first. Incidentally, in his flow he mentioned here the veins of the testicles to say they are forbidden due to blood; but this does not refer to "unless one cuts them" — for with veins of the testicles cutting does not help, only deveining; and as Rashba and Tur end of siman 75 wrote, see there; and Raavan in Sefer Yereim in the name of Rav Yehudai Gaon:
