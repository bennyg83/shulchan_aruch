**In the animal.** See responsum Ma'or haGolah Rabbenu Akiva Eiger z"l siman 51 who wondered on this—for in mishnah it teaches only “and the nail” and Gemara explains because attached; and perhaps deals with nail of slaughterer himself—for man is compared to ground as explained Shach Choshen Mishpat siman 95; or rabbinic stringency not to pass slaughterer's work on his nail—for certainly it is ground; but with animal's nail one may say kosher for slaughter like all above that are as movable objects; and he concluded in any case he did not know clear proof to disqualify shechitah with animal's nail—see there.
