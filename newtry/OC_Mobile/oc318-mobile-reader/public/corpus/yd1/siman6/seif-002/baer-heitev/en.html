**Attached.** As it says “and he took the knife to slaughter”—something taken immediately hand to hand like a knife.
**Were severed.** Taz and Shach in name of all later poskim disagree and hold even b'dieved forbidden if severed—and so Ra'osh and Rashal.
**After severed.** Taz wrote customary language—but in truth if rooted and not severed—also forbidden.
