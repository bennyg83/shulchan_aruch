**(seif 2) Such as nail and tooth attached to animal.** And I wondered why Shach raised as primary in Choshen Mishpat siman 95 that man is compared to ground and verse “and you shall take possession”—even though regarding Canaanite slave it is written, nevertheless we derive from it man of the world who has no redemption; if so from where is our ruling that slaughter with nail attached to animal is invalid—for mishnah teaches only “and the nail” and Gemara explains because attached; and perhaps deals with slaughterer's nail or one holds another's hand and slaughters with his nail—and this is like attached to ground as we say everywhere slave compared to ground for ona'ah and me'ilah—and so man is like ground and nail attached to ground; but with animal's nail one may say kosher for slaughter like all above that with living creature are as movable; and why do we rule shechitah departed from all these; and also what we say daf 20 to exclude tooth and nail and Rashi explained kosher for melikah and invalid for shechitah—this is also with his own tooth and nail—for in melikah he comes to it automatically in such manner as Tosafot wrote there; and especially per Rif that rishonim explained tooth and nail is reversed—kosher in shechitah and invalid in melikah—and deals with detached; or tooth detached and refers to kosher in shechitah, and nail attached and refers to kosher in melikah—if so plain he holds it deals with man's nail; and all the more for poskim that man is not like ground—but who will tell us animal's nail is invalid; and so one may analyze somewhat from language of Rosh (chapter 4 law 3) who permit; and main difficulty of Rosh there may be resolved per what I wrote man is not like ground and in shechitah they decreed because of slave; but in permitting on contrary it is a stringency that he is not judged as ground and from Torah was permitted; and possible truly with slave whose hands are washed not permitted, or rabbinically permitted not to pass on living creature whose hands are washed of man of the world—in any case I did not know clear proof to disqualify animal nail for shechitah.
