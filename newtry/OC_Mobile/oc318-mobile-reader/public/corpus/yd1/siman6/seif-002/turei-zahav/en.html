**With an attached thing.** As it says “and he took the knife to slaughter”—specifically something that is taken hand to hand like a knife.
**His shechitah is kosher.** This is Rambam's view; but Ra'osh forbids even b'dieved—and Rashal wrote this is primary.
**After they were severed.** Customary language—but in truth if rooted and not severed—also forbidden.
