If one stuck a knife in a wall (or in a detached thing) and passed the neck until it was slaughtered—his shechitah is kosher (Rambam's language chapter 2 Hilchos Shechitah law 8)—provided the animal's neck is below and the knife above; for if the animal's neck were above the knife, perhaps the animal would descend by the weight of its body and cut without holacha u'va, and this is not shechitah. And even if he said it is clear to me I did not press—his shechitah is invalid (Tur and poskim). Therefore if it was a bird—whether its neck was above the stuck knife or below it—his shechitah is kosher.
