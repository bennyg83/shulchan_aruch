**And tooth and nail when solitary.** But with two his shechitah is invalid as in seif 3.
**Or glass.** This requires further study—from where is this, for in the Gemara and Tur and all poskim it is stated explicitly that with glass one slaughters l'chatchila; and also what relevance have splinters separating in glass, since it is stronger and harder than lake reed; and it appears this is a textual error and this “glass” should be above in the Mechaber's words after “whether with flint”—and so it is in the Tur.
**That splinters separate, etc.** Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe wrote in name of Ba'al HaIttur that therefore b'dieved also invalid—and they mean when lost after shechitah and they did not check; but if they checked and it is clear to him splinters did not fly from it—kosher b'dieved, as Rabbenu Yerucham and Maharshal wrote chapter 1 of Chullin siman 34—and this is not like Bach who understood Ba'al HaIttur argues with Rabbenu Yerucham.
**And on Yom Tov when impossible, etc.** And all the more a knife that has a sharp point on its head—permitted to slaughter with it on Yom Tov by inserting the point of the point into a splinter—and likewise in a time of pressure as Maharshal and Bach wrote.
**If he wraps, etc.** Kol Bo wrote this means specifically when the knife has a full neck's measure besides the neck without the flaw—and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe and other acharonim bring this; and seemingly difficult—it is obvious; and it appears it teaches us that even though we hold in siman 8 and siman 24 seif 2 that when he performs holacha u'va a knife of any size suffices—here we require a full neck's measure besides the neck like holacha or hava alone, for we are concerned if he performs holacha u'va back and forth perhaps thereby the cloth will detach from its place and he will slaughter on the flaw.
