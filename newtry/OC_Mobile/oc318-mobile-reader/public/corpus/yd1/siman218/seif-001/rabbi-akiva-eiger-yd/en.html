(Siman 218, se'if 1, in the gloss) But concerning one who makes a vow to another person: if a man swore to his wife that he would not remove her from a particular city, and he performs acts that force her to leave, this is considered actually removing her. He did not swear merely that he would not carry her out on his shoulder. Moreover, since she administered the oath to him, we follow her intent. Thus writes Maharikash.
