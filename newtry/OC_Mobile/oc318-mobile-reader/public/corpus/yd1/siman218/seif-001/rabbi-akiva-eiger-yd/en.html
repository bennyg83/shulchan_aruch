(Siman 218, seif 1, gloss) But regarding one who vows to another. If a husband swore to his wife that he would not remove her from a certain city, and he performs actions that compel her to leave, this is considered actually removing her. He did not swear merely not to carry her out on his shoulder. Further, since she imposed the oath upon him, we follow her understanding. Maharikash.
