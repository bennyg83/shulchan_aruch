"And he's sorry for his wife." But we will not be sorry for the suppression of proof and not even for deception and cover. And it was written in the reply of the M.B.C., who entered the ship with his wife to walk in the sea and set up a storm on them, and who would not be afraid to go and swore that he would no longer enter with his wife on a boat and then he would be forced to walk on the boat to another generation in another city that would not be seen in the house And eating one sender and having a business in partnership and being in the shop and the housework and the store fell between them, and one of them, and swore that we would not sit together in one house and that we would not eat and that we would not drink together, we shall not break it apart, but by an explanation, and no one shall be allowed to go together in one house, but he is allowed to eat at home "And thou shalt see thee with the rod," and he was dressed in the palm of his hand, and he said, "I am the Lord of the Lord."
And all this is specifically, etc., unless there is a clear assessment, etc. It is written in responsum Mabit, part 4, siman 57, page 46d, that this distinction applies to a matter that has a monetary element; but where there is no monetary element, even though they administered the oath without specification, we follow the assessment, etc. This is not compelling. Also, from the plain wording of the Rav it implies that there is no distinction.
