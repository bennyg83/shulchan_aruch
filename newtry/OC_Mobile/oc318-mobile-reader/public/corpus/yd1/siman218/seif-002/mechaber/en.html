One against whom a false accusation was made because he sold wine to Ishmaelites, and on account of this he swore that he would not make wine to sell — and he had wine already made beforehand — it is forbidden to sell it. {Rama: For even though he said “that I not make,” we follow his intention (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashbatz). And all the more so a community that enacted an ordinance like this — they are forbidden to sell even what they had beforehand (the view of the Rav from the implication of Rashbatz; and not like Terumat HaDeshen, siman 481).}
