An agreement that the ruler commanded to cancel in part — the agreement is not nullified on account of his command. {Rama: A community that placed a cherem on someone that he not dwell with them, and afterward he wants to sit there in a shop — they judge according to the matter for which they vowed against him: if they intended only regarding permanent residence with his wife and children, a shop is permitted; and if not, everything is forbidden (Ramban, siman 257).}
