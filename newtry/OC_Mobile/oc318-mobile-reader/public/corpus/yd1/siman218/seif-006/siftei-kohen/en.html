That he would not reside, etc. See above, siman 217, seif 32, in the gloss. Mabit writes in part 1, siman 188, page 78, column 3, concerning one who swore that he would not eat outside his home. We do not follow his claim that he intended only that country and not another country. Following the intent of the person making the vow applies where the expression can bear two meanings, as in the case of cherem discussed above in siman 208. But one who vows concerning a particular matter is not believed to say that he intended to forbid it only in this city and not elsewhere. An unqualified personal vow applies everywhere, and he should have specified otherwise. See there for additional laws. However, when the circumstance that prompted the vow is evident, such as where wicked people lived in that city and he vowed not to eat in their homes, or a similar case, we follow that circumstance.
