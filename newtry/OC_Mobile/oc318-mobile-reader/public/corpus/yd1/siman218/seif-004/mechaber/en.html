If one was pressing his fellow to eat at his home and the fellow refused, and he said, “Konam your house that I not enter, and a drop of cold water that I not taste” — he is permitted to enter his house and to drink cold water for him, for he intended only that he not eat and drink with him at this meal. And likewise all similar cases. (One who vowed charity or another vow on account of a sick person who died — see below, siman 220.)
