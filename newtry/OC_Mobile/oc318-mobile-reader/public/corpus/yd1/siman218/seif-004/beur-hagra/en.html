It is permitted, etc. Meaning, entering and bathing are permitted, but eating and drinking with him are forbidden, as stated in Tosefta, chapter 4: it is permitted to enter his house and bathe with him in cold water, but it is forbidden to eat and drink with him. There is a textual error there and the correct reading is "to drink with him in cold water." This is unlike the Rosh and Tur, who permit even eating and drinking because his mouth and heart are not consistent, as stated in chapter 3 of Terumos.
