Shalom. Shach wrote: and most of the world are not careful about this; and so in responsum of Rosh it is permitted (and in Netivot HaKodesh he concludes: nevertheless in Tosafos first chapter of Sotah 10a it is proven forbidden; and it is stated there explicitly: great is this, for Shalom is among names that are not erased; and that it is not taught with the seven names that are not erased — because the tanna taught and omitted — see there; and nevertheless difficult for practical law, for from other poskim' words it does not appear so, that one of them would not have written thus; and difficult for practice — end quote; see there).
