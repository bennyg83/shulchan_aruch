Even in the word 'shalom,' etc. Most people are not careful about this. The responsa of the Rosh, klal 3, siman 15, state that it is permitted. For additional laws pertaining to this siman, see Orach Chaim, siman 32.
The final heh of the Name, etc., may be erased, etc. Everyone sees that the joined letters form a chet and no longer constitute a Divine Name, so erasing it is not considered erasing the Name. If the letters touch only slightly, however, it is still recognizable as the Name, and erasing it appears to be erasing the Divine Name.
