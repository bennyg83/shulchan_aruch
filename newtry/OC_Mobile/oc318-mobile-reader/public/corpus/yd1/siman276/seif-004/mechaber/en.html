When he dips the quill to write the Name, he should not begin immediately with the Name to write, lest excess ink be upon it and the writing not come out straight; rather he should intend to leave one letter from what he writes before the Name, and with that letter he should begin to write. (And if he did not do so, he should seek a letter that needs ink, fill it, and write the Name) (Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai).
