He wrote other gods there. See in responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 89: in a Torah scroll missing the letter heh in the word "to their gods" in the verse "for so they do to their gods," and it was written Elohim — possible he erred and sanctified, thinking it is essentially sacred; and therefore wrote Elohim; and he suggested: even though Rama wrote it is like sanctifying blemished offerings — one can say here it is like writing the Name not in its place, and not comparable to other gods, for that is entirely profane and cannot be sanctified, for its blemish is visible; but here, etc.; therefore must scrape the entire word Elohim and write Eloheihem — see there further; and as Shach s.k. 1 in the name of responsum Shevut Yaakov regarding the name "they sacrificed to demons not God" — explained there that even where the blemish is not visible, sanctification did not take effect; and even the author of Mishnat Chachamim did not dispute him there except regarding adding yod — see there [and see in Chamdat Shlomo siman 28 what he wrote on this].
