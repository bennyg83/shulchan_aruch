If a nonroot suffix was omitted, it may be suspended between the lines. But omission of a root letter within the divine name cannot be repaired by suspension and may require replacing the sheet or, where that is impractical, removing and rewriting the name. A secular word spelled like a divine name may be erased. A sacred name written without sanctifying intent may be erased for correction, while one properly sanctified may not be removed even for correction.
