After them. Prefix letters attached before a divine name, such as the lamed in 'to Hashem' or bet in 'with Hashem,' may be erased. Suffix letters attached after the name share its sanctity. If a nonroot suffix was omitted, it may be suspended between the lines. But omission of a root letter within the divine name cannot be repaired by suspension and may require replacing the sheet or, where that is impractical, removing and rewriting the name. A secular word spelled like a divine name may be erased. A sacred name written without sanctifying intent may be erased for correction, while one properly sanctified may not be removed even for correction.
Eloah. Tur also lists Elohai, because the yod is part of the root of that divine name. Taz rules that where the vav of Eloah was omitted in the verse, it cannot be inserted by suspension because it is part of the name itself; the name must be removed and rewritten.
