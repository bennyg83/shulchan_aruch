It is forbidden to erase even one letter of the seven Names that are not erased, nor from the letters attached after them, such as the final kaf of "elohecha" and the final mem of "eloheichem." And these are the seven Names: the Tetragrammaton; the Name Adnut; El; Eloah; Elohim; Shaddai; Tzevaot. And some also read Ehyeh—"Asher Ehyeh" (Tur).
