It is permitted to write the Divine Name, etc. This somewhat implies that writing over an erased or scraped area is preferable to suspending the Name above the line, as the Beit Yosef explains according to the Rosh, and as some authorities also maintain. The Tur similarly writes below in siman 279, seif 4, that scraping is not as disgraceful as suspension. Therefore, whenever the defect can be corrected either by suspension or by writing over an erased or scraped area, the scribe should not suspend it. I believe this is the accepted practice.
An erased or scraped area, etc. 'Erased' means that the ink was removed while still wet. 'Scraped' means that it was scraped away after it had dried.
Part of the Divine Name, etc. This requires study. In chapter HaBehemah HaMeaksheh, Chullin 75b, Rabbi Yochanan is challenged: 'He stated this according to Rabbi Shimon Shezuri. But did not Ravin bar Chanina say in the name of Ulla, in the name of Rav Chanina, that the law follows Rabbi Shimon Shezuri, and furthermore, wherever Rabbi Shimon Shezuri taught, the law follows him?' He answered: 'I hold like Rabbi Yonatan, who said that the law follows Rabbi Shimon Shezuri only regarding an animal in danger and terumat ma'aser of demai.' All the authorities rule against Rabbi Shimon Shezuri in chapter HaMeaksheh. They therefore hold that the general rule, 'Wherever Rabbi Shimon Shezuri taught, the law follows him,' is not accepted. Yet in Hilchot Ketanot, the Rif and Rosh write regarding one who erred while writing a Divine Name that the law follows Rabbi Shimon Shezuri, and furthermore, wherever he taught, the law follows him. The same difficulty applies to the Rambam, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch. In his Commentary on the Mishnah and at the end of chapter 5 of Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot, the Rambam rules that an animal whose limbs have spread upon the ground still requires slaughter, contrary to Rabbi Shimon Shezuri. Yet in chapter 1 of Hilchot Tefillin he rules that an omitted entire Divine Name may be suspended, whereas part of it may not be suspended. The Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule similarly in siman 13. I have discussed this at length in my work.
