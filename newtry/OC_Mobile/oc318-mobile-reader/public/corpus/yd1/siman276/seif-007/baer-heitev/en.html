The erasure. Shach implies that writing on an erased or scraped place is preferable to suspending a word between the lines. Where either correction is possible, suspension should not be chosen. This appears to be the custom.
And it should not. Shach says Rama's gloss belongs above: suspending part of a divine name is invalid even in other sacred books. The Mechaber's statement that the name should not extend beyond the column means into the margin beyond the line. Scribes who distort neighboring letters merely to avoid placing a divine name at the line's end act incorrectly, as do those who add marks after the name.
