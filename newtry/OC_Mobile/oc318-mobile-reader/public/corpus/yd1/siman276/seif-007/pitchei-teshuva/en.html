And none of them protrude at all. See in responsum Even Shetiyah siman 41, who wrote: this is only initially; but post factum do not invalidate for this — see there. And see in responsum Pa'er HaDor to Rambam siman 69, who wrote explicitly that even post factum it is invalid and must replace that sheet, store it, and write another — see there. And see in responsum Be'er Sheva at end of the book, who also wrote even post factum invalid (and see Magen Avraham siman 32 s.k. 47). And he wrote further: not specifically names of Havayah — the same law for all seven names that are not erased; and even all attached to the full Name after it, such as final mem of Eloheikhem and final mem of Eloheihem — equal in this law that all must be within the margin — see there. See in She'eilat Ya'avetz part 1 siman 154: he doubts whether letters attached to the Name after it also need to be within the margin; and further doubts whether letters attached before the Name, such as letters of "in all," may be written outside the line since even to erase them is permitted; or perhaps for this purpose even in attachments before there is concern, because it is intentional. And his view: be stringent both in attachments before and after — initially do not write them outside the line; nevertheless post factum do not invalidate. However for part of the Name — difficult for practice, both hanging and outside the line even post factum — see there.
