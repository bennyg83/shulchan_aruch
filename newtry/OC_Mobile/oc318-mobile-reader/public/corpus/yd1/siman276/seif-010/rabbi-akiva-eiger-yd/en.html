(Seif 11, gloss) Or to scratch the leg. He should begin scratching from below upward, because if he begins from above, after scratching only a small amount the form of the letter heh will immediately appear. Responsa Menachem Azariah, siman 36. See Responsa Chavot Yair, siman 16; Responsa Maharal of Prague in Chinukh Beit Yosef, siman 75; Responsa Maharlbach, siman 97; and Gan HaMelech, siman 16.
