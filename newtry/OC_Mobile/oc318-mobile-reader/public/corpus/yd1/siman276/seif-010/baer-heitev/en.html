Elohai. In Tur he also wrote Elohai; and so in some of Rambam's books; and the reason: the name Elohai is also the essential name, and the yod is part of the root of the name; and so Kesef Mishneh, since we find in the name Elohim that the yod is also root — the same is in the name Elohai — end quote Taz. And he wrote further: it is found in a Torah scroll in the verse "and he forsook Eloah" — he wrote deficient vav; and he ruled it does not help to repair and hang the vav, since it is from the body of the Name in that place; rather must scrape the Name and write another in its place — end quote.
