And yes, Avery. A name like our formula:
And there is, etc. — meaning in aleph-dalet; and per R' Chananel's version in Tosafos there, s.v. B'aleph, etc.; but in Yerushalim first chapter of Megillah it is stated explicitly like the first view: he wrote aleph-dalet-mem from four letters, aleph-hei from Akik, shin-dalet-lamed from Shadai, tzadi-bet-hei from Tzevaot — behold these are erased, etc.; he wrote Y-K from four letters, aleph-lamed-mem from Elohim — it is not erased at all; the principle: any similar name that is established elsewhere is not erased; and this is what he said above "or aleph-hei," etc.
And the name that they write, etc. — since it is not letters of the Name, and even though hinted at the Name, as Rosh wrote in Shevuot there "every complete," etc. — he does not speak regarding that it is not erased, rather to know its meaning, etc.; and so in responsum general 3 siman 15; and so Rambam and Shulchan Aruch, for only these seven names mentioned are counted; and even though the name Elohim and similar — many of them are profane — nevertheless he counts them and does not count those, only he was in doubt in his rulings there from Tosafos' words there that aleph-dalet are not erased, since they are from the unique name; and the first letter is beginning of the essential name and is like the head of the word; and even though not comparable to a name, for a name is two letters and also nothing is written after them — not so here; nevertheless be stringent not for great need — see there; and this is what he said "if not," etc.
