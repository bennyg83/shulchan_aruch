To erase. R' Yonah wrote in the name of Mahari Chagiz: the name El with a tzere vowel point — it is sacred; if he pointed under it another point and made it invalid, removing it from sacred to profane — there is room to doubt if it is like erasing the Name; and regarding Shabbat what is its law; and it is possible since he did no act in the body of the letters, it is nothing — see there. And it must be said he speaks of a Name that was sanctified; or also not for the sake of repair — Beit HaTov s.k. 9; and as Shach s.k. 13 in the name of responsum R' Akiva Eiger.
