And likewise alef-dalet from Adonai or alef-hei, etc. This gloss belongs after seif 10. The point is that only “El” and the two-letter Name are sacred Names independently. Thus, even if he intended to write a longer Divine Name and did not complete it, the letters already written may nevertheless retain sanctity.
And some are stringent concerning this. This means even regarding alef-dalet and alef-hei. Tosafos explains that these incomplete forms are treated more stringently than shin-dalet, because they are connected to the uniquely sacred Name. The Levush explains alef-hei according to Kabbalah, but Tosafos makes clear that the stringency applies to alef-dalet as well.
