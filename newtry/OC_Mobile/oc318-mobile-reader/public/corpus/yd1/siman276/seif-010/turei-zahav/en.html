And likewise aleph-dalet from Adonai or aleph-hei, etc. This gloss is placed after seif 10; and the reason: specifically El and Y-H are also names in themselves; and he informs here: even though he intended to write Elohim and his thought was not fulfilled, nevertheless it is sacred — as written.
And some are stringent about this. Meaning: also in aleph-dalet and aleph-hei; and the reason is explained in Tosafos chapter Shevuat HaEidut (Shevuot 35) that they were more stringent here than in Beit Din; and it is difficult: since this is the unique name; and in Levush he gave a reason: according to Kabbalah, aleph-hei is like Y-H; and per his words "some are stringent" applies only to aleph-hei and not aleph-dalet; and he did not see Tosafos' words I mentioned, that it is clear they are also stringent in aleph-dalet.
