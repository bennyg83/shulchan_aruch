If he wrote "El" from "Elohim," "Yah" from the Name—it is not erased; but "Shad" from Shaddai and "Tzev" from Tzevaot are erased. {Rama: And likewise "Ad" from Adnut, or "Eh" from Ehyeh (Yerushalmi, first chapter of Megillah). And some are stringent in this (Tosafot, chapter Shevuat Ha'eidut, in the name of R' Chananel). And the Name that they write in siddurim with two yuds and one on top of them—it is permitted to erase if for a need (Piskei Maharai siman 148).}
