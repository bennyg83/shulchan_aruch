Of the names that may not be erased. Beit Yosef cites Mahari Iskandari, who argues from the Yerushalmi that all sacred names require writing with proper intent. Beit Yosef rejects the proof and says only that it is proper to write all nonerasable names with intent. This implies that failure to do so invalidates only initially, not after the fact. Radbaz likewise indicates that only the Tetragrammaton absolutely requires such intent.
