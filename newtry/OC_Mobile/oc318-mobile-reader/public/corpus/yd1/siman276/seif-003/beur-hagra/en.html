When he writes, etc., and if, etc. — Tosefta chapter 3 of Berachot: one who writes the Name — even if the king asks of his welfare he does not answer him; if he was writing two names or three, once he finished one of them he answers. And so in Mishnat Soferim chapter 5 halacha 6; and from here R' Yitzchak Eskandari proved: even in other names that are not erased, for their sake is required — for three names of Havayah together we do not find. And Beit Yosef wrote: there is no proof, for one can say per Nissim Gaon's explanation that he left on the sheet many names to write in purity, etc. — and it is not so, for in Yerushalim there it is explicit as his words: taught — if he was writing the Name, even if the king asks of his welfare he does not answer; if he was writing two or three names, such as El, Elohim, Hashem — this one finishes one of them and answers the greeting of peace.
