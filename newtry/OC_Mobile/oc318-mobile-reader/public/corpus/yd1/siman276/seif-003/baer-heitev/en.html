He shall answer him. Shach wrote: and if he answered, it is not invalid — so implies Beit Yosef; and he explained what is written in tractate Soferim: "he paused in the middle of the Name — invalid," meaning he made one Name like two names, such as "see, son" — end quote; meaning this Name is not a sacred name, rather every word is called a name, as we find many times in the Gemara "word" in the language of name; and not like Bach who understood it is a sacred name — end quote. And if a king — God save him — asks of his welfare, permitted to answer him, as written regarding prayer in Orach Chayim siman 104.
