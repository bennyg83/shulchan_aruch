That he writes for the sake of holiness. See in Sefer Bnei Yonah, who wrote: if he wrote two or three letters of the Name in general and did not sanctify them for the Name, and remembered when he wanted to complete it and sanctified it — it is fit, for his sanctification and completion come together (and he brought that Leket Yosher also wrote so; and "even though we do not learn from him, nevertheless it joins to another view). But if initially he expressed intent for another word, such as he was to write the Name and intended to write Yehudah, and remembered before the final dalet that should be here the Name, etc., for the Name — it does not help. And see in Sefer Mishnat Chachamim negative commandment 3, who wrote so in the name of Rama miPano siman 30: if he did not sanctify the Name until the end — it helps; and he brought proof from that which he needed to write the Name, etc., that it helps if he makes from dalet heh — see there; and see what is written below s.k. 24 in the name of Levush and Tiferet Moshe, that there it speaks where it is known he sanctified it — it does not appear they hold thus.
