Part of the Name, etc. — difficult, for in chapter HaBehemah HaMeaksheh (Chullin 75b) we learn: Rabbi Yochanan said to him — per R' Shimon Shezuri he speaks; but did not Ravin bar Chinnana say in the name of Ulla in the name of Rav Chanina: the law is like R' Shimon Shezuri; and not only, but wherever R' Shimon Shezuri taught, the law is as he says? He said to him: I hold like this, that R' Yonatan said: the law is like R' Shimon Shezuri in a dangerous situation and terumat ma'aser of demai — end quote; and all poskim ruled not like R' Shimon Shezuri in chapter HaMeaksheh; if so, this general principle does not exist — wherever R' Shimon Shezuri taught, the law is as he says. And in Hilchot Ketanot, Rif and Rosh wrote on that which one errs in a Name, etc.: the law is like R' Shimon Shezuri; and not only, but wherever R' Shimon Shezuri taught, the law is as he says; and also on Rambam, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch it is difficult thus, for in explanation of the Mishnah and in chapter 5 of Ma'achalot Assurot he ruled spreading over ground requires slaughter; and in chapter 1 of Hilchot Tefillin he ruled: the whole he hangs, part he does not hang; and so Tur and Shulchan Aruch in siman 13; and here in Sifrei I extended on this.
