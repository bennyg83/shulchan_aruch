Part of the Name, etc. This requires examination in light of the rule of Rabbi Shimon Shezuri, for in one place it appears that even part of a Divine Name has sanctity, while elsewhere the implication is that the prohibition depends on a complete Name. One must distinguish between a part that was written as part of the Divine Name and still carries its sanctity, and letters that by themselves do not form a Name. According to this, there is no contradiction.
