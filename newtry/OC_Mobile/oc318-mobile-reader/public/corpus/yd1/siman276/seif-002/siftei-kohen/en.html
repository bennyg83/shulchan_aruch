He must say, etc. The Bach rules that if the scribe intended and wrote for the sake of sanctity, even though he did not articulate this with his lips, the writing is not invalid after the fact. See there. The Rama likewise rules in Orach Chaim, siman 32, seif 19, that after the fact it is sufficient that the scribe mentally intended to write the Divine Names for their own sanctity, since at the beginning of writing the Torah scroll he verbally declared his intent. See there, and see above at the beginning of siman 274.
If he did not do so, it is invalid. Passing the pen over the letters afterward with the proper intent does not remedy the defect.
