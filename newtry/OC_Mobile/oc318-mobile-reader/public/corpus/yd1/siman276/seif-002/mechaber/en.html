Even though he says at the beginning of the book that he writes it for the sake of the sanctity of a Sefer Torah, each time he writes one of the Names that are not erased, he must say that he writes for the sake of the sanctity of the Name; and if he did not do so, it is invalid (so it appears from the Rosh, and likewise Semag and Sefer HaTerumah). {Rama: If he wrote names of other gods for the sake of sanctity, it is not invalid, for it is like one who dedicates blemished animals to the altar, which are not holy (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashbatz).}
