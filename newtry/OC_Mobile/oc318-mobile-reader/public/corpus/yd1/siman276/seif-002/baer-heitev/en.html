Defects. Taz infers that if a divine name was mistakenly written in the wrong place but with proper sanctity, it remains sacred and requires burial. A name written without intent for sanctity has no sanctity.
Invalid. Passing a quill over the divine names afterward with proper intent does not repair them. Shach. Bach holds that if the scribe had intent for sanctity, failure to verbalize it does not invalidate after the fact when he verbally declared the sanctity of the Torah scroll at the beginning. Taz requires verbalization for each divine name unless the scribe initially declared that every divine name would be written for its sanctity. Nekudot HaKesef objects from Rama in Orach Chaim.
