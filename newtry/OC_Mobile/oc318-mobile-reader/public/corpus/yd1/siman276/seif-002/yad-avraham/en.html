(Rama, seif 11) Or to scrape the leg and repair it. Gan HaMelech explains that he should begin scraping from the bottom upward until none of the leg remains, and then rewrite it. If he begins from the top downward, the form of the letter heh is immediately recognizable; if he then scrapes away the entire leg, he has erased a Divine Name. If he leaves part of it, the repair constitutes engraving the interior of the letter. There is no prohibition here under the strict law, however, because the letter is invalid due to engraving its interior, and one may therefore erase it completely in order to repair it.
