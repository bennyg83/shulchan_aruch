(There.) Elohim. How when adjacent together "Elohei haElohim" in this "Elohim" is mundane, for it refers to judges of the world and He is the Judge over them — for high is Hashem — so Beit Yosef wrote here in the name of Maharshal and in Joshua (22:22) "El Elohim Hashem" Rashi explained: El — for all elohim He is Hashem; and likewise Rashi (Tehillim 50:1). Therefore in those verses it is permitted to erase the word Elohim; however in Yerushalmi (chapter 1 of Megillah, halacha 9) and in Midrash Rabbah (Bereishit chapter 88, siman 16) it is explained they are all names — requires study. "And you shall be as elohim knowing good and evil" — mundane; therefore "I have seen your face as seeing the face of Elohim" — mundane; "Machaneh Elohim" — holy. Massekhet Soferim.
