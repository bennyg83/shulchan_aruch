He wrote the mentions, etc. Atzei Levanon wrote: specifically the mentions; but the rest of the Torah scroll, even though they write only in black ink and not in any other matter (as above siman 271 seif 6) — nevertheless if he wrote in ink and threw gold upon it, it is fit — end quote. And it is not clear, for since the reason throwing gold invalidates is because the upper writing is writing and nullifies the lower, as in Beit Yosef — if so, what difference mentions or rest of Torah scroll; only therefore he mentioned mentions, for in other places in the Torah scroll he has repair by erasure and scraping, not so with mentions that are invalid and repair does not help, for it is forbidden to erase; and so in the braita: he shall store; and so Beit Yosef in Orach Chayim siman 32; and so in Shulchan Aruch there seif 3; and so Levush himself there: also in other writing invalid; only in a mention there is no remedy — see there. And in Derishah seif 4 it appears like Atzei Levanon, and it is not correct, as written.
