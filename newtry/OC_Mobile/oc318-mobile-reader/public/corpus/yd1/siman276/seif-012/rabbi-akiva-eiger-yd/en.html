(Ibid., seif 12) He should suspend the dalet above. 'For he did not intend to write the Divine Name,' writes Ateres Zekeinim. It appears to me that this teaches that had he intended to write the Divine Name, it would be forbidden to suspend the dalet. Since the word would then be read Yehudah, this would constitute erasure of the Divine Name. See Eliyah Rabbah, Orach Chaim 142, seif katan 3, and Responsa Rashdam, Yoreh Deah, siman 187.
