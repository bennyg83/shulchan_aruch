(Seif 11, gloss) Or to scrape the leg and repair it. Gan HaMelech explains that one should begin scraping from the bottom upward, so that the entire leg does not remain. If he begins from the top downward, the form of a heh becomes immediately recognizable. If he then scrapes away the entire form, he is erasing the Divine Name. If he does not, the correction is chak tochot. There is no Torah prohibition in erasing the whole form for the purpose of correcting it, because in its invalid form it is not a valid Divine Name.
