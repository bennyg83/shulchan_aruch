If a letter stuck to its fellow in the letters of the Name, he may scrape it. {Rama: Ink that spilled on the Name—it is permitted to erase it in order to correct it, for this is not a manner of erasure but of correction (Beit Yosef in the name of Maseches Soferim and Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 6). If the hei of the Name bent so its leg touches the roof—if it truly touches until it appears like a chet, it is permitted to erase or scrape the leg and correct it; but if it touches only slightly, there is doubt whether it is permitted (Beit Yosef in the name of R' Yitzchak Alexandri).}
