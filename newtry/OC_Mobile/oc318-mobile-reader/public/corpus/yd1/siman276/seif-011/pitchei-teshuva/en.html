From seven names. Per Shach above siman 279 s.k. 11, who wrote plainly: a name written in profane language, such as GaT in foreign language — permitted to erase; but in responsum Chavot Yair siman 106 he wrote: if he wrote GaT in Assyrian script, possibly forbidden to erase due to intent at time of writing with the holy image — see there; and be stringent; and as written s.k. 16 in the name of Bnei Yonah. And see in responsum Sha'ar Efraim siman 90: one who was forced — God save him — and experts told him to write an amulet on gazelle parchment, and he found no gazelle parchment, only a Torah scroll — if permitted to cut a piece of Torah parchment for the amulet, since he must scrape the writing and erase several names; and he brought proof from that which is stated in tractate Sukkah and Sanhedrin regarding David, who stopped the deep — he said: is there one who knows if permitted to write the Name on a potsherd; and he learned a fortiori: and what to make peace between man and wife, etc. However one can distinguish — end quote; and per my humble view I do not know the place of doubt here: if he is dangerously ill — plainly permitted; if there is no danger — plainly forbidden, since it is a Torah prohibition; and difficult now.
