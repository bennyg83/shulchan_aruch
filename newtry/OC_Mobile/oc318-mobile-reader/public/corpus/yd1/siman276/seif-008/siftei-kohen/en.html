There is no distinction, etc. This gloss is arranged here in all printed editions, and so it appears in the Levush. In truth, however, it does not belong here. Why would one think to distinguish here between a Torah scroll and the other sacred books? Since it is permitted in a Torah scroll to write over an erased or scraped area, it is certainly permitted in the other books. Rather, this gloss belongs above in seif 6, where the ruling that part of a Name suspended above the line is invalid applies even to the other sacred books. One may not write part of a Name suspended above the line in them either. This is also stated in Sefer HaTerumah. See there.
