On the face side. Shach wrote: the reason is not that l'chatchila one must salt from two sides — for if so, why is it permitted in seif 3 through piercing bone and membrane? Rather certainly, since he salts from outside on all sides it suffices that it not be only one thick piece; and this is what it means: one who wishes mitzvah min hamuvchar to salt head and brain cuts the head in two and salts well on the face side because of the brain, etc.
