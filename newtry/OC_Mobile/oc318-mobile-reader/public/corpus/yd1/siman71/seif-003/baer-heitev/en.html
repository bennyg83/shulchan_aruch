Below. Shach wrote: but it does not help when placed on beit shechitah or nostrils and something stuck in — as what helps for roasting.
L'chatchila. There it is explained that l'chatchila they practice removing brain before salting from the skull and cutting the skull (two-and-evening).
Forbidden. For it is like salting in a non-perforated vessel; and he wrote in Torat Chatas that the same applies to fowl.
Flesh. Shach wrote: because the skull bone separates between blood inside and flesh; and therefore even other flesh salted with it and already emitted all blood and gravy — where "as it absorbs so it releases" does not apply — nevertheless permitted. And Taz wrote one need not forbid because it was not salted from two sides, since when the head is whole it is like a thick piece.
Salting. Shach wrote: if cooked without salting, brain is forbidden (because we presume blood in brain, and likewise all cooked with it is forbidden if there is not sixty against brain in bones); nevertheless it appears if there is sixty in pot against brain in bones, even brain itself is permitted — and see siman 70.
With other. But with their own meat they practice every day to salt even l'chatchila, and this is fine — end Shach. (And Peri Chadash wrote: we practice l'chatchila to salt with other meat because we presume blood in them like other meat.)
