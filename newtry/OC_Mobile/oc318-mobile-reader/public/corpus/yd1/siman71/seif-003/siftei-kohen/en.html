The membrane and brain are forbidden. For it is like salting in a non-perforated vessel, as written in Torat Chatas general rule 15 law 18 — and the same law applies to fowl — see there.
And the rest of the head is permitted. Because the skull bone separates between blood inside it and flesh; and even if a little blood emitted and entered the flesh — as it absorbs so it releases — so wrote Rashba in Torat Chatas HaAroch daf 75b, and the Rosh chapter 5, and Or Zarua general rule 17 law 7; and it appears to me the first reason is primary, and for clarity of the matter they wrote the second reason; and also Ran in chapter Gid HaNasheh wrote only the first reason. And according to this, even other flesh that was salted with it and already emitted all its blood and gravy — in a matter where "as it absorbs so it releases" does not apply — nevertheless it is permitted (if the flesh was removed from the head outside on the skull). And the rest of the laws of salting, roasting, and cooking of head and brain are explained in siman 68 — see there.
Require salting. Meaning: if it was cooked without salting, the brain is forbidden because we presume blood in the brain; and for this reason if it was salted b'dieved with other meat it is permitted. Nevertheless it appears that if there is sixty in the pot against the brain in the bones, even the brain itself is permitted — and see siman 70 s.k. 8.
And salting the bone helps for the brain inside it. And there is one who says regarding brain in the head — for there the red threads of the membrane are like blood enclosed inside; but blood in limbs, with a little salting it is emitted and extracted — end of Rashba daf 76a.
However l'chatchila one should not salt them with other meat. But with their own meat they practice every day to salt even l'chatchila, and this is fine; and so in Torat Chatas there law 11.
