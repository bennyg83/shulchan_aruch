And rest of head permitted — Shach s.k. 8 gave two reasons: bone separates, or as it absorbs so it releases; Shach agreed to first reason. I say it is necessary: since we hold ein hana'asur yachol le'asor in other prohibitions too, and it is known that skull bone separates only for emission of blood but not for fatty prohibition — if treifah head is cooked, does skull bone block its emission? Therefore since it becomes nevelah and forbids fatty prohibition to remainder — for which bone does not separate — it must be ein hana'asur cannot forbid because blood did not emit at all; and if as it absorbs so it releases — behold it emits — so ein hana'asur does not apply; and this is necessary — requires further study.
