The hooves — one cuts, etc. Rashal wrote in chapter 25 siman 12: since we pierce the hooves — that is, the shoe — why must salting precede singeing? On the contrary, afterward is better; and Yoreh De'ah did not say so regarding hooves, etc., but that salting before singeing helps — unlike those who are stringent; but mitzvah min hamuvchar is to salt afterward once the shoes are pierced first, even though the custom is not so (there).
