They enroll the children. Kol Bo, siman 74, writes that instruction begins with Vayikra, because the Sages said: Hashem said, let the pure children come and occupy themselves with the laws of purity. This remains the practice. Kol Bo and Rokeach, siman 296, also record customs observed when bringing a child to school. Those customs are no longer generally practiced, but where they are practiced they should not be changed, as Rokeach writes there.
