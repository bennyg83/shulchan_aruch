If the custom in the city was that one takes a teacher of children for pay, he is obligated to teach him for pay until he reads all Written Torah; and he is not obligated to teach him for pay Mishnah, Gemara, laws, and aggadot; and this is when his time is pressed; but if it is possible for him, it is a mitzvah to teach him Mishnah, Gemara, laws, and aggadot.
