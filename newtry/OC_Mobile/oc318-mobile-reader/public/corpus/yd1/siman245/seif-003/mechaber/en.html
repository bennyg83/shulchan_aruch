Just as there is a mitzvah to teach one's son, so there is a mitzvah to teach one's grandson, as it says, "And you shall make them known to your children and your children's children"; and not only to one's grandson, but every Torah scholar of Israel is obligated to teach students, for they too are called children — except that the mitzvah is to give precedence to one's son over one's grandson, and one's grandson over the son of one's fellow.
