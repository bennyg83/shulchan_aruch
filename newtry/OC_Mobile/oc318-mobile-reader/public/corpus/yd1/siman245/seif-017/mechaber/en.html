A teacher of children who leaves the children and goes out, performs other work while responsible for them, or is negligent in their studies is included in the verse, “Cursed is one who performs the work of Hashem deceitfully.” Therefore, one should appoint only a God-fearing teacher who reads fluently and carefully. Rama: A teacher should not remain awake excessively at night, lest he be sluggish in teaching by day. He should likewise not fast, excessively restrict food and drink, or eat and drink excessively, because all these impair his ability to teach properly. A teacher who departs from these conditions is at a disadvantage and may be dismissed. See Mordechai, chapter HaUmanin, citing the Yerushalmi. For the other laws of teachers, see Choshen Mishpat, simanim 334 and 335.
