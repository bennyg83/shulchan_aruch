A teacher of children who leaves the children and goes out, or does other work with them, or is lax in his teaching — behold he is in the category of "cursed be he who does the work of the Lord deceitfully"; therefore one should not seat a teacher unless he is God-fearing, quick to read and to parse: {Rama: And a teacher should not stay up at night too much, lest he be lazy by day to teach; and likewise he should not fast, withhold food and drink, or eat and drink too much — for all these cause that he cannot teach well; and whoever changes — his hand is on the bottom and they remove him (Mordechai, chapter HaUmanin, Yerushalmi); and other laws of a teacher — see Choshen Mishpat simanim 334 and 335.}
