If there were more than twenty-five, etc. The Rosh and Tur explain that up to forty students no assistant is required. From forty to fifty an assistant is needed, and for fifty students two teachers are required. Rabbeinu Yerucham, nesiv 2, section 1, rules likewise. It appears that this depends upon the teacher and the students, whether they learn little or much, and everything is assessed according to the circumstances.
The townspeople are not obligated, etc. This means that they cannot compel one another to hire a teacher.
