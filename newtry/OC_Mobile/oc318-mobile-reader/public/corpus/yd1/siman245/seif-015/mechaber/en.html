Twenty-five children suffice for one teacher; if more than twenty-five up to forty, they seat another to assist in their learning: {Rama: And even if the community hired a teacher for all children unstated, he seats another to assist at their expense (Nimmukei Yosef, chapter Lo Yachpor); and some say if there are not twenty-five children in the city, the townspeople are not obligated to hire them a teacher (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 2, Tosafot, Rabbeinu Yerucham); and some say even with fewer they are obligated (there in Nimmukei Yosef).} If more than forty, they appoint two.
