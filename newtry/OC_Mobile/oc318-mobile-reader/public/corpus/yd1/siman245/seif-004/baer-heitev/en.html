For his son. The Kesef Mishneh writes: It is possible that one must also hire a teacher for his son's son, and likewise for his great-grandson. I am uncertain, however, whether his daughter's son precedes another person's son, for perhaps priority over another person's son exists only where he was obligated regarding the father, which cannot be said regarding a daughter. The Shach writes: It appears to me that his daughter's son does precede another person's son. See siman 6.
