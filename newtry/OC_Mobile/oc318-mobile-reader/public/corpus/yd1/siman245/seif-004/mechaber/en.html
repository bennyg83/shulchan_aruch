One is obligated to hire a teacher for his son to teach him; but for the son of one's fellow he is not obligated to hire: {Rama: And they would compel him to hire a teacher for his son; and if the teacher is not in the city and he has property — if possible they inform him, and if not they descend to his property and hire a teacher for his son (Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 1 of Laws of Torah Study, in the name of R' Meir).}
