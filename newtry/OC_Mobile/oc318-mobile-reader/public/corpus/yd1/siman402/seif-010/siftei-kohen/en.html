**If not one who was at burial and not at death.** Eliyahu Zuta challenged this — for reason we fast is danger, bad luck on day father or mother died; if so no distinction and always fast death day — so he wrote. So responsa Mahari Brin in Bach; responsa Meshech Benjamin siman 84 challenged Rabbenu — two brothers in one city, one at death other not in city when father died, before burial second came — one does yahrzeit death day second next day; lo titgodedu and mockery concerns; we require pleasant ways — end. Other Acharonim imply always death day not burial. Maharam Benet wrote specifically second year onward; first year yahrzeit from burial lest not complete twelve months — e.g., died erev Shabbat Rosh Chodesh Shevat near Shabbat no time bury before Shabbat buried Sunday 3 Shevat; yahrzeit on death day Rosh Chodesh Shevat mourning stops that day since practice not mourn past yahrzeit — improper since mourning from burial not death and must complete to 3 Shevat — end. Never heard distinguish yahrzeit years; all Acharonim plainly death day implies first year; Sefer Tahara below also first year yahrzeit death day. His difficulty: in any case difficult — sometimes death far from burial, e.g., brought elsewhere; mourning only from burial as above siman 375 and yahrzeit from death; strained twelve-month mourning follows death; strained if such case do yahrzeit before twelve months. Sefer Tahara implies always yahrzeit at end of mourning completion; always count twelve-month mourning from burial — wrote one whose father died Rosh Chodesh Nisan: by law once next Rosh Chodesh Nisan enters mourning complete since twelve months done — only world practice even Rosh Chodesh Nisan that day not cancel twelve-month law; perhaps Kaddish Barchu fasting like day father died therefore observe mourning that day — by law not required. Relevant: one fasting Kaddish Barchu death day dies that day buried morrow — mourning always from burial — end. Accordingly always fast death day; but if death three or four days or more from burial, first year fast burial day for yahrzeit at mourning completion since impossible otherwise and uncommon — rely Maharam Benet burial day; second year onward death day. One whose father died and buried same or next day — fast death day even first year — examine. But Taz ruled always yahrzeit only death day even far from burial — what if not mourn twelve months if impossible; none concern — end.
