**Not obligatory they tell him.** Chamudei Daniel wrote: one came to brother within thirty of sister's death and did not want brother informed — appears permitted to wear Shabbat garments so need not tell him. See above siman 393 in name of Shivat Tzion regarding within seven.
**They practiced to inform.** See responsa Avraham Zacuto part 1 vol. 2 siman 191 — wrote on festival when must observe private matters forbidden to inform due to preventing festival joy; brings proof from Gemara Moed Katan 7a. And there siman 199 ruled likewise on Purim not to tell until after Purim — see there.
**At burial.** Be'er Heitev in name of Shach — if death day distant from burial three or four days, fast first year on burial day so yahrzeit at completion of mourning; appears if leap year fast on death day since mourning already completed at twelve months end as stated siman 391 seif 2; then this reason does not apply — plain.
**And if it occurred.** See responsa Avraham Zacuto part 1 vol. 2 siman 198 — regarding kohen who has yahrzeit and that day a son was born for pidyon haben he must complete and he redeems that day; but regarding blessing that custom that kohen blesses on wine (as stated siman 305 end in hagahah) — appears he should not bless and give others to drink as at circumcision on fast day where impossible otherwise; but here better others bless; and if they want kohen at meal do pidyon near Minchah and kohen completes fast and comes to meal at night — see there. See responsa Beit Shmuel OC siman 561 who disagrees and wrote even if pidyon haben time delayed kohen may eat; but regarding father of son — doubt. He also wrote there if feast of mitzvah on yahrzeit day and they appeased him not to fast and come to meal and he refused due to yahrzeit though otherwise would count with them — behold he established vow that even on Purim he will not eat; and if yahrzeit occurs and he has Purim like it — forbidden and needs release; therefore careful in such case to say he does so without vow — so he wrote. See Pri Megadim OC siman 573 in name of responsa Devar Moshe that groom does not fast yahrzeit during seven days of feast — see there.
**Tachanun.** Taz OC 568 s.k. 5 — likewise if son's bris on his yahrzeit day he does not fast; if wants to fast need not complete — see there. Appears likewise mohel and sandek need not fast yahrzeit — see OC 559 s.k. 8 hagahah and responsa Har HaKarmel OC siman 10 — wrote even initially permitted to accept sandek or mohel on yahrzeit day — see there.
**First.** Be'er Heitev; see responsa Givat Shaul siman 73 — father died leap year on Rosh Chodesh second Adar which is 29th of first Adar — fast plain year on 29 Adar not 29 Shevat — see there.
**391.** See there s.k. 8 in name of responsa Makom Shmuel.
**All his days.** Be'er Heitev of Mahari"t; see Taz there — meaning Taz there s.k. 3 disagrees, wrote not called vow path unless intends always forever — lenient did not lose; especially one distressed — see responsa Chut HaShani siman 66 — wrote when yahrzeit first on erev Shabbat need not complete when also later on erev Shabbat; but when falls weekday must complete — see there; also even when first weekday, if later erev Shabbat not complete since presumably acceptance not on erev Shabbat etc.; see responsa Nachalat Shivah siman 58 — also when weekday, if later erev Shabbat need not complete; as first yahrzeit on erev Shabbat so all his days; as Rema wrote not like those who err all depends on first yahrzeit after twelve months — rather on first yahrzeit on erev Shabbat he speaks — see there; see responsa Chinnuch Beit Yehudah siman 23 did not write thus — see there. See Pri Megadim Mishbetzot wrote to be lenient when first (meaning after twelve months) on erev Shabbat; his view even if occurs weekday not complete since generations weak — see there.
