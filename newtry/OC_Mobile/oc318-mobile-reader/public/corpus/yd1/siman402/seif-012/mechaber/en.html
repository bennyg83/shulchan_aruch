One whose relative died and he did not know — not obligated they tell him, even father and mother; on this it says one who brings forth slander is a fool; permitted to invite him to betrothal and wedding meals and all joy since he does not know; however if asked — may not lie and say alive, as it says keep far from false matter: {Rama: Nevertheless for sons custom to inform so they say Kaddish; for daughters no custom to inform (Mahariw siman 13); mitzvah fast day father or mother died (Kol Bo in name of Rambam); fast day of death not day of burial (Mahariw siman 121 and Maharai siman 296 and later authorities); unless one was at burial not death (kabbalah from elder Maharam Landa); if that day is day tachanun not said — do not fast at all; if died in Adar and year intercalated — primary to fast in first Adar (Terumat HaDeshen siman 294 and R' Yehudah Mintz) though some dispute, so is primary; if died leap year in second Adar — fast also second Adar in leap year (same there); see Orach Chaim siman 568 seif 7; see above siman 391 not eat meal night father mother died; if fast falls erev Shabbat its law like other fasts see Orach Chaim siman 249; if first time completed — practice all his days.}
