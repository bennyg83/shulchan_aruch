**He has no lie, etc.** Appears say language bearing two faces — not explicit he died but language bears life and death; proof R' Chiyya asked Rav Abba alive — said mother alive; Tosafot explained wonder language until you ask father ask mother — R' Chiyya understood father died without explicit death:
**Death day not burial day.** Written responsa Maharam Benjamin appears specifically second year onward; first year always yahrzeit burial day not death day lest not complete twelve months — e.g., died erev Shabbat Rosh Chodesh Shevat no time bury until Sunday 3 Shevat; yahrzeit death day Rosh Chodesh Shevat mourning stops that day since practice not mourn past yahrzeit — improper mourning from burial not death must complete to 3 Shevat — end. I do not believe these words from that tzaddik — certainly no one counts twelve months only death day; one who did not hear father died until half year counts only half year mourning as siman 394 all that year law stretched from death not mourning start; certainly completes mourning 6 Adar and then yahrzeit; what if not mourn full twelve months if impossible; rather wonder he would not mourn more than 6 Adar when twelve months complete and would distance yahrzeit from mourning end — none concern. May Merciful repair breach with Israel and comfort them rebuilding Jerusalem speedily in our days amen so be His will:
