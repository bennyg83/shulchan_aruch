**Close.** Shach wrote: and he begins to count seven and thirty from the hour he heard. Bach and Darishah wrote in the name of Maharshal that therefore where one died on day one and was buried on day two — even though relatives at the burial count from burial day when the grave was sealed — nevertheless those who were not at the burial and did not know he died until day thirty from burial need not observe mourning, since their day of tidings is day thirty-one from death and it is distant tidings; for regarding tidings law the day of death is primary — end. So too Taz.
