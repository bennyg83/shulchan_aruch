Ten days after Sukkot heard one died erev festival — even if counting one hour before festival as seven and seven festival days and Shemini Atzeret as twenty-one and ten more days would be forty-one — this is not distant tidings law but close tidings, for festival does not count for one who did not observe mourning before at all; and all the more one who did not know relative died (Rabbeinu Yerucham in name of Baal HaMaor and so Ramban).
