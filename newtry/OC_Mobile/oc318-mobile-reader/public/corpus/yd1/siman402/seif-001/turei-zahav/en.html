**Rule: day of his tidings, etc.** Explained in Beit Yosef this siman in name of Ramban that on day of close tidings one does not put on tefillin as burial day:
**But regarding thirty-day decree.** Meaning what depends on other relatives in thirty count — laundering and haircut — this applies to father until they protest etc.; and what he wrote "counts from death day" means matters forbidden twelve months:
**Observes only one day.** And even that day we say part like whole; so Ramban; so too OC siman 548:
