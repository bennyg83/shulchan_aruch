One who hears distant tidings need not observe all mourning laws — ink suffices by removing shoe; need not wrapping or overturning bed; permitted work, bathing, anointing, use of bed, Torah study; if no shoes on feet — must overturn bed or wrap head, for need act recognizable as mourning; if engaged in Torah or work or bathing and anointing and tidings came — pauses one hour for mourning and returns to his activity; but if wearing tefillin — need not remove them; nevertheless does not fulfill mourning by this pause; need recognizable mourning act such as removing shoe or overturning bed or wrapping — one suffices.
