**Tomorrow.** Taz wrote: and it appears that if he heard close tidings at the end of the thirtieth day after Ma'ariv prayer and it is still daytime — we certainly do not say it is night by force of prayer; for to be strict we say so regarding not letting the past day count toward seven — but not to be lenient and say it is distant tidings; rather he must mourn; similar to what is written in siman 262 regarding circumcision that we do not care about prayer; and at least he counts from day of tidings not from the morrow lest there be two contradictory matters — end (and see above siman 375).
