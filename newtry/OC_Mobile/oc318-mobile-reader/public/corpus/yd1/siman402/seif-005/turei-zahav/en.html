**But one day.** Meaning one hour; but if heard tidings on Shabbat when twenty-ninth — observes seven and thirty after Shabbat. Maharshal. Reason: does not observe mourning after Shabbat even though began Shabbat and is thirtieth — since not full mourning that Shabbat only private matters, therefore no mourning decree after — so Beit Yosef Rashba; Ramban wrote that Shabbat and festival private matters forbidden all day and we do not say part like whole since obligation next day one hour — how say mourning stops and returns:
