**But close tidings law.** And begins counting seven and thirty from hour heard; Bach and Darishah wrote siman 399 in name of Maharshal — therefore where died day one buried day two: even though relatives at burial count from burial when grave sealed, those not at burial who did not know until day thirty from burial need not mourn since their tidings day is day thirty-one from death and is distant tidings; for tidings law death day is primary — end:
