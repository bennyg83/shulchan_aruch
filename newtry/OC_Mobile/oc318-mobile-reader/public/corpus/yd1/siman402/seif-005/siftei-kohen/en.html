**The hearer, etc., but one day.** Meaning one hour; and from here Bach ruled to sit on Motza'ei Shabbat one hour on ground and observe mourning; and even if seventh falls on Shabbat must observe mourning one hour on Motza'ei Shabbat — for here even though private matters apply on Shabbat, not released thereby and must do on Motza'ei Shabbat act recognizable as mourning — so he ruled. In my humble opinion I do not agree in general that here, since Sages obligated mourning properly at least one hour, and on Shabbat mourning observed only in part — therefore never observed mourning properly even one hour; therefore obligated Motza'ei Shabbat or Motza'ei festival one hour. But one who observed seven days and within seven Shabbat fell — why obligated Motza'ei Shabbat? Even if negligent some days — ruled above siman 397 need not complete; all the more Shabbat when observed as required. Bach also elaborated to remove R' Yechiel's words from plain meaning and ruled if seventh of mourning on Shabbat must observe private matters all day since on Shabbat observes only private matters; brought proof from what we rule seif 2 must do act recognizable as mourning — and every examiner judges rightly that R' Yechiel's view: also on Shabbat we say part of day like whole and observe part of Shabbat day and no more; none disagree; and so plain Talmud and poskim language — one should not depart to split; and I do not agree to above seif 2 for reason written nearby:
