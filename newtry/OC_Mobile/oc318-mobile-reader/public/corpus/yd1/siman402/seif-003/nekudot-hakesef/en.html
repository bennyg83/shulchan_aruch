(Taz s.k. 9) He wonders at Maharam Benet not properly; on contrary his words are astonishing — what he wrote "one who did not hear father died until half year" is irrelevant — there he did not hear and what can he do; once half year passed he is released from mourning — certainly need not mourn more than wicked sentence in Gehinnom twelve months; when twelve months complete for deceased there is no mourning here; here intentionally would not mourn twelve months since wicked sentence is from burial day, therefore mourning begins only burial day; even one buried weeks later mourning begins burial day as above siman 375; when he begins mourning day one must also stop day one since must mourn full twelve months of his judgment days from burial — clear: May death be swallowed forever and God wipe tears from every face etc.
