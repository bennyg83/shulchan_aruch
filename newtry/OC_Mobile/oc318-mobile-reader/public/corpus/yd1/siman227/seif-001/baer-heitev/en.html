Readily marketable. This means merchandise that merchants eagerly purchase. Merchandise 'lying on its face' is difficult to sell. Ran writes that if he prohibited their property upon himself, every transaction is forbidden, whether he receives more or less than market value, because the prohibition already rests upon their property. If he prohibited only his benefit upon them, purchasing readily marketable goods remains forbidden even if acquired after the vow, because the prohibition rests upon his own benefit.
He prohibited them. If he prohibited them upon himself and also prohibited himself upon them, every transaction is forbidden, and he must deal with members of another nation. Rambam adds that neither side may buy, sell, borrow, lend, ask, or lend objects to the other.
Upon him. He can prohibit his property to others only regarding assets already in existence. Once prohibited, even selling an item worth one hundred for two hundred is forbidden. Shach.
