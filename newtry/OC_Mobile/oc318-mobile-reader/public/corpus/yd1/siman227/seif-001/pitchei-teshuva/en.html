He purchases it for a higher price. See Noda B'Yehuda, Tinyana, Yoreh De'ah, siman 149, who writes that even in such a case it is permitted only if he pays immediately. Purchasing on credit, however, is itself a benefit, for the seller extends credit to him, and he thus benefits from something prohibited. See there.
