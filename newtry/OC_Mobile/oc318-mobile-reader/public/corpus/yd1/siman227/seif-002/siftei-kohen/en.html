He may lend them objects, etc. We do not decree that he might come to borrow an object or money from them. Likewise, regarding sales, we do not decree that he might come to buy from them, as stated above in siman 221, seif katan 35. The case here is different because he vowed regarding all Jews, and the Sages did not impose such a decree. This is from the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilchos Nedarim, as cited by the Tur, and the Ran writes likewise. The Rambam's wording makes clear that this is not limited to one who vows regarding all Jews. The same applies regarding an entire nation, for the decree was made only when he vows regarding an individual, not when he vows regarding a whole people.
