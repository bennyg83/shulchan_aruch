(Likut) Who, etc., and the same law, etc. — apparently from what he wrote "and likewise," etc., he holds that tovat hanaah is not money (see there in Choshen Mishpat s.k. 112), but it is not so, for then the Gemara's question is difficult — for then these kohanim too would take; perforce he ruled here like Rava, and what he wrote "and likewise," etc., he wrote so according to the Yerushalmi there, and its language: the Yerushalmi there — Rav Chaviva said a person may give his tithes as tovat hanaah, and Rabbi Yochanan said a person may not give his tithes, etc.; the Mishnah disputes Rav Chaviva: "Konam kohanim and leviim have benefit from me" — they take — perforce he explained it as saying "I do not wish to give a gift at all"; know that it is so, for we learned "these kohanim, these leviim" — others take; but this is uncertain, for it contradicts his own words in chapter 2 of Laws of Theft halachah 5: a thief of terumah of Israelites — he does not pay double payment, for they have in it only tovat hanaah, and tovat hanaah is not money (and likewise in chapter 5 of Laws of Marriage), and it is uncertain; and also uncertain that he contradicts his own words, for he ruled like the first answer in Bava Metzia 11:2 regarding movable property by way of land — he acquired it for him, and it is proven that tovat hanaah is money; and likewise difficult are the words of the Rif against each other, for the Rif also ruled like that first answer, and at the end of chapter 3 of Pesachim he concludes that tovat hanaah is not money — see there in the Rif, and it is uncertain; but on the first question it was possible to say (as the Kesef Mishneh wrote) that there is a distinction in nedarim, for even vaday is forbidden, for although tovat hanaah is not money, nevertheless it is forbidden; and the implication of the Yerushalmi is so, that it does not depend on the dispute whether tovat hanaah is money, rather whether he can give as tovat hanaah; but from our Gemara it does not appear so, for it depends on the dispute of tovat hanaah; only that he seizes the Yerushalmi as is his way everywhere (end quote).
