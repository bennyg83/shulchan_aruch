His terumot. The Gemara explains that since terumah is fit only for kohanim, when he forbids it to them it becomes like ownerless dust and they acquire it from hefker. Ran extends this to one who prohibited his property upon himself, but Maharshal distinguishes: others may not seize his property by force, and he may direct it to charity or mitzvot. If they already consumed it, they are exempt, unless he later annulled the vow while some of the prohibited property remained, in which case they must pay.
The poor. Such as leket, shikhechah, and peah. The same applies to charity: beit din may seize property and distribute it to the poor, because charity obligations are enforceable. Beit Yosef.
