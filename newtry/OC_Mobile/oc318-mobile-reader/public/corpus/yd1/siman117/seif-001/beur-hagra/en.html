And a donkey is raised for its labor, and a camel is raised for its labor. R' Hoshaya carried and gave in this morais; R' Huna carried and gave in this chilteis— meaning, they would engage in buying and selling with chilteis and morais, which are by rabbinic law. And likewise, in the Tosefta, chapter 5: one should not bring dogs, kofrin, squirrels’ weasels, cats, and a hedgehog to sell them to a non-Jew, and to ride upon them; but morais, and the cheeses of Beit Huniki, and their bread and their oil, it is permitted to sell them and to lie upon them.
