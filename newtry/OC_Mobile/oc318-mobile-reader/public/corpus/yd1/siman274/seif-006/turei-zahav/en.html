One must be careful in forming the letters and their crowns, etc. The Tur writes that the Rambam does not invalidate a scroll when the crowns on the letters of the group known as Shaatnez Gatz were omitted, while the Rosh invalidates it because the Gemara says that these letters require crowns. The Rambam's view is supported by the Gemara's common use of the word “requires” for matters that are only initially required but do not invalidate after the fact. The practical ruling therefore follows the distinction stated by the Shulchan Aruch.
