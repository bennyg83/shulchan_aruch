Vowel-pointed sefer invalid. The reason is that we establish "there is a yod for mesorah"; and since it is vowel-pointed there is here only Scripture. But it is difficult: why does it not help if he removed the pointing? And why should this be worse than one who wrote open mem and it stuck, which helps if he scrapes the stroke alone and it remains like a nun and afterward writes a new stroke, as written in Orach Chaim siman 32 — and there correction helps even in the body of the letter; and it appears here it is worse because the scribe's intent appears that he is not concerned for mesorah at all, and certainly for this purpose they wrote it, and it is as if forged from within and entirely invalid — unlike where he wrote some error unwittingly; so it appears to me.
