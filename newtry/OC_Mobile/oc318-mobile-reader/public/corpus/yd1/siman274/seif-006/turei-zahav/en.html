A vowel-pointed Torah scroll is invalid. We follow the written tradition as well as the reading. Once vowels are inserted, the scroll presents only one reading and disregards the transmitted written form. Removing the vowels does not necessarily cure the defect, because their placement reveals that the scribe wrote the scroll with an intention contrary to the Masoretic tradition. It is therefore comparable to an internal falsification, unlike an accidental malformed letter that can be corrected.
