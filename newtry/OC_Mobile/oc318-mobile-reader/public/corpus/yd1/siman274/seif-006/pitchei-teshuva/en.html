In correcting letters. See responsum Maharam Galanti siman 124 regarding open kaf — Beit Yosef Orach Chaim siman 36 wrote it must be rounded above like reish; one sage wrote like dalet; ruled whoever acts like one authority acted — depends on custom of that city — see there. [His words also brought in Magen Avraham siman 32 note 86; in Maharil responsum implies invalid; likewise Chatom Sofer siman 261 in name of his teacher the Gaon — see there.] See Shaarei Efrayim siman 81 regarding a sefer Torah where zayin was written like vav shape but tagged with tagin of sheatnez gimel tzadi; and nun peshuta long is literally like long vav shape only tagged; and open mem is not like two vav — what is this sefer Torah's law — see there. See Noda Biyehudah Choshen Mishpat siman 74 — if they took out a sefer Torah on Shabbat and found chetiyot made between two vav shapes or between dalet-vav shapes — do not take out another sefer; but on a weekday all sifrei Torah may be corrected. See there siman 80 — sofer widened end of line closed mem only upper side (like final mem) — do not invalidate; if easy to correct — good; but if similar exists in a holy Name such as Elohim — leave as is and it is valid — see there; and see responsum Brit Avraham Choshen Mishpat siman 10 and Yeshuot Yaakov from Gaon of Lvov Orach Chaim siman 36 note 3. Avnei Nezer Panim Me'irot part 1 siman 66 — alefim written inverted as yudin — do not invalidate; likewise shin and tzadi written inverted as yudin — valid b'dieved — see there; likewise Noda Biyehudah second series Choshen Mishpat siman 171 — see there. [See Chatom Sofer siman 258 — asked regarding chetiyot as required two zayinin and roof per R' Tam and left per Rashi; lenient except where sofer widened excessively unlike Magen Avraham siman 36 note 3 that chet in two-zayin form invalid when widened; but if wide roof like Rashi — valid — see there.] From now one may ask: permitted to scrape chetiyot and make wide roof per Rashi and then extend them, or not because of two contradictory practices since whole sefer written per R' Tam and these chetiyot would be per Rashi? He answered: no concern of contradiction; proof from Rabbeinu Yerucham Orach Chaim siman 36 that Sephardi scribes write all chetiyot with wide roof like Rashi except chet daleth with roof pointing back (see there sweet reason); he explained at length that both Rashi and R' Tam have chet form whether as rod like Rashi or roof like R' Tam, but they disagree whether we need hint of chay; nevertheless no remedy here to erase chet written per R' Tam and write Rashi form in its place — though Magen Avraham wrote R' Tam chet cannot be widened unlike Rashi chet; in Eliyah Rabbah in name of Taz wrote opposite — see there; and though Taz wrote Rashi chet invalid if widened because needs measure of two zayinin — I do not agree, nevertheless I do not dispute that also per R' Tam wide chet may be valid — how erase valid R' Tam chet and write without roof which for R' Tam lacks hint of chay barom shel olam and is invalid (meaning even l'chatchila may write like Rashi, but writing on erasure forbidden since no elevation in holiness, as he explained siman 261, brought below siman 275 note 8). If we leave chetiyot as they are — invalid per Magen Avraham; therefore if another sefer exists do not read from this; in pressing need if no other sefer — rely on Taz to read. If possible scrape chet with some letters or words before it until the Name, remove patch, rewrite chet per Rashi wide form — for in my opinion Rashi does not need measure of two zayinin and no contradiction. That is good — see there. There siman 265 regarding old worn sefer with crooked twisted letters and strange abundant tagin — explained there if sofer added tagin beyond sheatnez gimel tzadi letters — l'chatchila not proper if done, does not disqualify provided attached to letter; if not attached — erase, even in holy Name letters. Also bet without heel — do not invalidate since not rounded behind like kaf but like dalet and yud without left protrusion above — do not invalidate, for some say no need for protrusion actually bulging outward and extending down, only corner above and below unlike round — see there (see Seder Gittin end of Beit Shmuel letter peh; Noda Biyehudah second series Even HaEzer siman 113 brought in Pischei Teshuvah Even HaEzer siman 125 note 16 s.v. ktzo). There siman 269 regarding sefer where all zayinin and nunin peshuta improperly — line drawn from side not center then bent toward center; and all yudin literally like lamed shape — wrote plainly these forms disqualify the sefer though not disqualify for change of forms mentioned in Beit Yosef siman 36, but such changes plainly disqualify — not only where resembling lamed (appearing as another letter) but even zayinin and nunin disqualify, for certainly not only another letter but another form though not from alef-beit, whenever change causes it unrecognizable to most people as our accepted letter form. To fix the sefer — requires study because of yudin in holy Name, etc.; nevertheless if yud appears literally as lamed to all — may scrape so only dot body remains then add upper and lower thorns — see there. See Chinuch Beit Yehudah siman 74 asked regarding left yud thorns extended more than right — how fix and what regarding yudin of Names; he raised this depends on dispute of Hagahot Maimoniyot and R' Yitzchak of Alexandria brought in Beit Yosef Orach Chaim siman 36 — per Hagahot need only scrape all left thorn and rewrite left thorn properly; per R' Yitzchak must scrape also right leg and rewrite right leg and left thorn; likewise permitted to scrape and fix yudin of Names per each authority — see there (see below siman 276 note 21 in name of responsum Hashiv R' Eliezer).
