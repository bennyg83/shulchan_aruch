A sefer, etc., and even, etc., and likewise, etc. Maseches Soferim, chapter 3, halacha 7: "a sefer," etc., "he scraped it," etc.
Specifically, etc., because it does not contain the measure of a closed paragraph. In Maseches Soferim, chapter 1, halacha 10, "a continuous text that he made staggered," etc., means leaving the measure of a closed paragraph.
(Likkut) Specifically, etc., all, etc. This follows from what is stated in chapter 12 of Shabbos 103b, "he made it like an ordinary passage." Regarding the Song, Maseches Soferim says that one must leave the measure of a closed paragraph, as the Rosh writes in their name in seif 14. Rivash likewise writes this in siman 286 from Rambam's words. The phrase "he made it like an ordinary passage" implies specifically leaving the measure of a closed paragraph. See below, siman 273, seif 3: "and if there is not," etc., "he leaves a blank," etc.; and there, seif 4, "if it occurred," etc., "and if not, he leaves," etc. End.
