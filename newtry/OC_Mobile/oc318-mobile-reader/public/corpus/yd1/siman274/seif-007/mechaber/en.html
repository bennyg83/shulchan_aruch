A book with vowel points is invalid—even if the pointing was removed from it. And likewise a book that has punctuation of verses is invalid. {Rama: And specifically when he made the break with ink; but if he left air between the verses, as long as there is not the measure of a valid parashah, it is valid (responsum of the Rivash). And a Sefer Torah in which letters are mixed up as in a get is not invalid, but l'chatchila one should not do so (there, siman 146).}
