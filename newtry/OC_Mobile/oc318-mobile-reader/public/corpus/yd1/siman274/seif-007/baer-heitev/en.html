They removed it. Taz distinguishes this from correcting a malformed open mem by scraping part and rewriting. Here the scribe's deliberate disregard for the received tradition makes the text appear internally falsified and wholly invalid, unlike an ordinary accidental writing error.
Verse divisions. If verse divisions were inserted, they can be scraped away. This differs from vowel points, which alter the form and meaning of the words. Taz also condemns drawing decorative ink lines beside a divine name at the end of a row and requires their removal. Missing special dots over certain words do not invalidate reading from the scroll, since those dots are a later enactment.
