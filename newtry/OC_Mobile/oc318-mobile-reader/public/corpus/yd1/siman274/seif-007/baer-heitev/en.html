Remove. The Bible says, “Why do we get rid of the Lord’s eyes open and stick to me if the Lord is not afraid to give up at all, and he is left as a nuclei, and then he is re-examined by Hashem’s words, and he is certainly written and removed from him and completely removed from him instead of a mistake he wrote.”
Verse divisions. It appears that if it had verse divisions there is a remedy to scrape them; and it is not like vocalization where it creates invalidity in the body of the word because its meaning changed between Scripture and mesorah, but in verse divisions of verses the explanation of the words does not change — Taz. And he wrote further: I saw in many sifrei Torah that when a Divine Name happens at the end of a line they make next to the Name two lines in ink like this, and there is a transgression here because they make a change from what was in the Torah of Moses; therefore one should scrape those lines entirely; and see siman 276 seif 1. Further it appears to me regarding a sefer Torah that does not have the vowel points that should be on certain places, such as on "elav Isha Sarah" or other places — we do not disqualify from reading in it even though it has not yet been corrected, for the vowel points are only Ezra's enactment, as Tosafot wrote in Zevachim chapter R' Yishmael — end quote [should read in Tosafot rulings chapter Kol Korbanot siman 232].
