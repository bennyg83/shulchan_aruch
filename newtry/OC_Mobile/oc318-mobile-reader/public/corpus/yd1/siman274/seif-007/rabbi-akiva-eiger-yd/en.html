(Seif 7) Vowel-pointed sefer invalid. In Shakh Nachalat Yaakov brought in the name of Turei Berakh that specifically if he wrote the Sefer Torah with vowel points; but if after writing someone came and made vowel points — if the pointing was removed it is valid, see there; and in doubt whether he wrote with pointing or the pointing was made afterward, see at length in Turei Berakh (siman 36).
