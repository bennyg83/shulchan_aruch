Vocalized. See Baer Heitev, seif katan 6, citing Taz regarding a Sefer Torah lacking the special dots, and see Bnei Yonah, who remains uncertain. He further writes that omission of the traditional large and small letters certainly does not invalidate, because these are included among scribal ornaments that are only a preferred mitzvah practice.
