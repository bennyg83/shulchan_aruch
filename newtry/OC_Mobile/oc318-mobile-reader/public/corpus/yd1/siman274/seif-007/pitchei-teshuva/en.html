The vocalized scroll. See Baer Heitev, seif katan 6, citing Taz concerning a Torah scroll that lacks vowel points, etc. See also Bnei Yonah, who remains uncertain about this. He further writes that omission of the enlarged and diminished letters certainly does not invalidate the scroll, for they are included among the other scribal adornments that are only a preferred fulfillment. Likewise, if the inverted nuns were not made, it may be argued that the scroll remains valid. See there.
