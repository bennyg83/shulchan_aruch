(Seif 7) A vowel-pointed Sefer Torah is invalid. Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah cites Responsa Mekor Baruch that this applies specifically if the Sefer Torah was written with vowel points. If someone added vowel points after it was written, it becomes valid when the vowel points are removed. See there. If there is uncertainty whether it was written with vowel points or the vowel points were added afterward, see the lengthy discussion in Mekor Baruch, siman 36.
