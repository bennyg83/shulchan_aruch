Taz wrote that this measure is only l'chatchila, but b'dieved it is not invalid except when they are literally as one according to a child who is neither smart nor foolish; and all the more if a letter is missing at the end of the word and he hangs it between the lines — not invalid because it is as if the hanging letter were written in the line, and then there is no break between words at all — invalid? That is not so, for in any case we now see they are two words; and one sage already erred on this — end quote.
