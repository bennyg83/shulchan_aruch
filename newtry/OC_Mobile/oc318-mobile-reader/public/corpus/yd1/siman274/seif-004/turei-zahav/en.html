Or if he wishes to roll it, etc. The Rosh was asked about a corrector who needed to roll a Torah scroll while the ink was still wet and removed some ink with his pen so the word would dry more quickly. He ruled that there is no degradation of the mitzvah. This differs from lighting an ordinary lamp from a mitzvah lamp, where the ordinary lamp is improved by weakening the mitzvah lamp. Here the written word is not harmed; a small amount of excess ink is merely removed. Even with a lamp, weakening it without benefiting an ordinary lamp would not necessarily be prohibited. Nevertheless, one should remove only what is needed for the scroll and not use the ink for an unrelated purpose.
