To take ink. See Sefer Bnei Yonah who wrote — permitted to discard that ink taken from the letter, but forbidden to write mundane matters with it; and where by taking ink one diminishes the blackness of the letter — plainly forbidden to take because of nullification of mitzvah; but to write a letter in a sefer Torah — possibly permitted. And from names that are not erased — all forbidden unless much ink remains on them that one must fear smudging — see there.
