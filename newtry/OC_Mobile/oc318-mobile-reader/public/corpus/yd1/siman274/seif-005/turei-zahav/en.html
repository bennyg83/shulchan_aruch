One must be careful about correcting letters and their adornment, etc. In the Tur it is written that Rambam does not invalidate if he omitted the zayin adornments, and Rosh invalidates since it is stated in the Gemara: Rava said shin-ayin-tet-nun-zayin gimmel-tzadi zayin-yod-nun and kuf — end quote; for it is stated in Gittin at the beginning of chapter "One who brings two" — one view says he must say, and if he did not say it is valid — it teaches us an extra clause that it is invalid; thus the language "must" does not teach except l'chatchila; and here Rava used the language "must," and this supports Rambam.
