Ink. Taz permits taking excess ink only for writing the Torah scroll. Shach reads the source more broadly, even from a divine name, but questions using it for mundane writing because of degradation of a mitzvah. The preferable permission is when ink is removed only so that the writing dries faster and is not reused.
To roll. Rosh's case concerns wet ink that had to dry before the scroll could be rolled. Taz permits drawing off excess ink with the quill and reusing it in the Torah, but objects if sanctified ink will simply be discarded. Nekudot HaKesef responds that no degradation exists because the act itself protects the Torah.
