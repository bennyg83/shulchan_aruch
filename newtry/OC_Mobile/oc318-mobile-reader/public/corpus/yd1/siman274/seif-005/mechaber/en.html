Care must be taken that the form of no letter be impaired until it is not recognizable, or similar to another letter—whether in the main writing, whether in a tear, whether in a hole pierced in it, or in smudging. (And he may take ink from a written letter when he needs ink (Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 2), or when he wishes to roll the book) (Rosh, general rule 3).
