Every letter is required. See Noda Biyehudah second series Choshen Mishpat siman 169 — he ruled regarding a sefer Torah where a hole was found next to the roof of the yud of the Divine Name, so that through this hole the yud is not surrounded by parchment at that spot; he wrote if this hole was made after it was already written properly — no correction needed; if doubtful when it was made — also valid because of double doubt perhaps made afterward; and if you say it was made at the beginning of writing — perhaps the halacha is that not surrounded by parchment only harms when touching another letter, but to fix by scraping part of the yud is not correct, for forbidden to erase even part of a letter when not for repair; and even if certainly from the beginning of writing — hard to permit, for perhaps halacha is that not surrounded does not harm, and then scraping is not needed repair — see there. And see Noda Biyehudah first series siman 75 — if all parchment between one letter and another was removed and no parchment remains between letters, or in that letter between right and left — it is like attached — see there.
