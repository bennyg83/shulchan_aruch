It is forbidden to write, etc. The Ran writes in chapter 2 of Megillah that the Yerushalmi indicates that if one wrote without copying from a written text, it is forbidden to read from that scroll except in pressing circumstances. Rabbeinu Manoach writes that this restriction applies only initially, but if one transgressed and wrote without copying from a written text, the scroll is not invalid. It requires study whether the passages found in tefillin may be written in a Torah scroll without copying from a written text, since the scribe is fluent in them. End quote of the Beit Yosef, cited by the Darchei Moshe. See Orach Chaim, siman 32, seifim 29 and 51.
