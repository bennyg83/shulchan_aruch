(There, se'if katan 2) And then it is invalid. Even if the first word ends with one of the final letters mem, nun, tzadi, pei, or kaf, such as in min haaretz, so that it is recognizable that they are two words, it is nevertheless invalid. So writes Ezrat Nashim by the author of Kappot Temarim, in Shemot Gittin, entry 2, in the name of Ben Tzion.
