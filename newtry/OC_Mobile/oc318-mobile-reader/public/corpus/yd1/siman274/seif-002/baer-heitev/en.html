That it is forbidden. Ran wrote it is proven from the Yerushalmi that if he wrote not from a written text it is forbidden to read from it except in time of pressing need. Rabbeinu Menachem wrote specifically l'chatchila, but if he transgressed and did so it is not invalid. And there is doubt whether those parshiyot in tefillin one may write in a Sefer Torah not from a written text since he has memorized them — end quote. And see in Orach Chaim siman 32 seif kaf tet and nun aleph. And Taz wrote in the name of Semag: even though he said at the beginning of writing that he writes for the sake of the sanctity of a Sefer Torah, he should also say "and all the Divine Names in it for the sake of the sanctity of the Name" — end quote. And it appears this is correct, for perhaps he will forget afterward to sanctify the Name in its place — end quote.
