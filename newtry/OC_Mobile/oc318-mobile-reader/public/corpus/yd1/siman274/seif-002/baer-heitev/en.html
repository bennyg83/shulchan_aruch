That it is forbidden. Ran derives from the Yerushalmi that a Torah scroll written from memory may not be read except in pressing circumstances. Rabbeinu Manoach treats this only as an initial requirement and does not invalidate it after the fact. It remains uncertain whether passages known by heart from tefillin may be written into a Torah scroll without copying. Taz adds in the name of Semag that at the outset the scribe should declare that all divine names are being written for their sanctity, lest he later forget to sanctify one individually.
