A lung where a murmuring sound is heard when they inflate it — if the place from which the murmur is heard is recognizable, they place upon it saliva or straw or the like; if it shakes it is known that it is punctured and treifah; and if the place is not recognizable they place it in lukewarm water and inflate it — if the water bubbles — treifah; and if not, it is known that only the lower membrane was punctured and the air goes between the two membranes and for this reason a murmuring sound is heard at inflation — and kosher. {Rama: And if they placed it once in hot water (according to all) or cold — it has no further examination even in lukewarm (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran).}
