In lukewarm water. In lukewarm water of summer days that stood in the house a while after drawing and warmed somewhat from air heat — Tur and Rashba in Teshuvot in the name of Rabbenu Tam and Ittur and BeHag, brought in Beit Yosef also in this section; and above siman 188 s.k. 14 explained that lukewarm means like the warmth of saliva; and the vessel — Rabbenu Yerucham wrote it should be covered with a limb, brought in Beit Yosef; and Darkei Moshe; and this is specifically in summer days; but in winter days it must specifically not be covered with a limb, as below seif 15 — see there; and in BeHag daf 128 43a I found in summer days place them in cold water.
Lukewarm, etc.; and if they placed it, etc. Language of the Talmud in lukewarm in warm — no, for they contract (Rashi: they contract and the puncture is sealed and this is not good examination); and in cold they expand; and if they placed it once in hot or cold — it has no further examination even in lukewarm; and so is implied in Taz and Bach and other acharonim; and even though Rabbenu Baruch wrote in hagahah seif 15 and Bach and Pri Megadim that at first it helps even now — nevertheless it appears from their words that at first it helps only when not placed in hot or cold; but if placed in hot or cold at first — it has no examination even in lukewarm, as Shulchan Aruch and Taz and Bach and Pri Megadim wrote — see there.
