(Seif 8 in hagahah) Lacking flesh from inside even if the lack holds up to a quarter — kosher; more than this — treifah. What Maharshal and Bach distinguish between this and poured like a pitcher that here the lack is in one place worse — does not appear so in the Talmud Piskei Tosafot (daf 48) regarding needle in lung: we say according to the view internal lack is not called lack — kosher even if found in flesh and iron's nature lacks flesh when it remains there; and no distinction between quarter and more was mentioned there, and the lack there is in one place. And so is implied explicitly in the Talmud and Rashi and Tosafot who compare that lack to bite lack; and they raise from the mishnah regarding poured and conclude permitted there; and they answer for R' Shimon it was needed and do not answer simply the mishnah deals with lack in one place more than quarter called lack. Rather we learn there is no distinction at all between this and poured. And I wonder on the hagahah who ruled to forbid more than quarter — for regarding needle in lung why permit stam in great financial loss in seif 16 and should say examine if lack more than quarter since iron lacks flesh when it remains; requires study. Therefore it appears we should rely on the implication of the Talmud, Rashi, Tosafot, and Rashba in Mishmeret HaBayit; and so Rambam, for he omitted Rav Nachman's statement in the Talmud from the baraita of lack in one place even holding a quarter kosher; and wrote only poured law; and if he held a distinction between this and poured he would not omit. And so Taz, unlike Nekudot HaKesef.
