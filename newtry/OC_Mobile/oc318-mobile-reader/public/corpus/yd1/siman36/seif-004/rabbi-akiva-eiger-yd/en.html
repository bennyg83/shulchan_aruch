(Siman 36, s.k. 1) From one wind. And per Shach s.k. 8, in the reason of those who forbid — also from two winds it is treifah.
(Shach s.k. 9) For since we are not expert in examination. Not understood: for what we hold there to forbid above half — we judge this in the category of deficiencies, and by law examination does not help.
(Be'er HaGolah at the end) In examination. And nevertheless in peeled we hold we are expert in examination, as explained in Nekudot HaKesef s.k. 2.
