(There) And examination is thus: inflate first properly to see it is whole and rises in inflation in all places; then place in lukewarm water specifically — meaning like warmth of saliva leaving mouth or somewhat colder; for if placed in hot or cold the puncture seals and inflate properly to know there is no puncture; if any puncture — water bubbles; if not examined thus — treifah from doubt. Semachot there. Einayim Yeshayim there. Pithei Teshuva ot 3. Pri Chadash there ot 3. Kometz there ot 4. Pri Chadash siman 29 ot 2. Zivchei Brit siman 5 ot 2. Mateh Yehonatan ot 5; however Levush ot 11 wrote no examination in lukewarm needed, only inflation; nevertheless concluded l'chatchila one should heed Turei Zahav; b'dieved if examined by inflation alone — kosher — examine. And so Pithei Teshuva: in financial loss rely to be lenient if examined only by inflation and rose properly and cannot examine again in lukewarm because torn or lost, etc. And so Ba'er Heitev in Levush ot 3. Kometz there. Mateh Yehonatan there.
