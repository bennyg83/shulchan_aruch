How do they examine it (if the matter is doubtful from what cause it came) (Ran's view): they place the lung in water from time to time; if it is cold season they place it in lukewarm water in a vessel where water is not drawn from its back and runs off so it will not cool quickly; and if it is hot season they place it in cold water in a vessel where water is drawn from its back so it remains cold; if it returned to its form — this is by heavenly causes or by creatures and it is permitted; and if it did not return — by human action and treifah (the entire seif is Rambam's language there law 10). {Rama: And it appears we should not rely on this examination, for we are not expert in everything requiring examination; and if it appears it came by heavenly causes one may not permit without this examination — in this one may rely on our examination (so wrote Maharshal several times).}
