If the matter is doubtful, etc. This is Ran's view (unlike Rashba who was uncertain); examination is mentioned only in doubt; but if known it came by human action, such as another slaughtered before it — no examination helps.
And if it appears it came by heavenly causes, etc. And Maharshal's view: if it appears by heavenly causes — no examination at all; however certainly where they examined and it did not return — treifah; and where it returned — permitted without examination, as Rama wrote — see there.
And if it appears that it came upon her by the hand of Heaven, etc. Maharshal's view there is that if it appears that it came upon her by the hand of Heaven, no examination is needed at all. However, certainly where she was examined and did not return to her normal state, according to everyone she is a treifah, for the matter proves that previously they had also frightened her by human hands.
