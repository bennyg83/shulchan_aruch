A lung where one felt with the hand that a needle is inside it — if it is whole they inflate it; if it rises in inflation — kosher — whether thin or thick, whether it entered through its point or through the box (meaning through the side of the thickness); and if it is cut — treifah. If there is a drop of blood opposite the needle outside — treifah even if it is whole. {Rama: And nowadays one should declare treifah any lung in which a needle is found, and we do not judge it as whole (Beit Yosef in the name of Semak, Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 7, long Arukh, and Maharai sha'ar 30), unless in a place of great loss — then one may be lenient and examine by inflation (Shach siman 92 in hagahot, Beit Yosef, Maharil, and so is the implication of all poskim).}
