(Shulchan Aruch seif 6) If one bronchus was punctured into lung flesh it protects and is kosher. And it appears to me this deals when the flesh was not also punctured opposite it, for if it were punctured opposite it she would lose sealing. As we say in the Talmud Piskei Tosafot (daf 48) and Shach brought in siman 42 regarding sealing of the lining to the ona and sealing of liver to mara. And it is difficult: if so how does Taz permit in seif 16 a needle found in lung flesh — when the needle exits the bronchus to the flesh the flesh is also punctured opposite and is not protected, especially a needle whose way is to lack flesh. As in the Talmud there (72b) a needle in the lung is kosher only according to the view that internal lack is not called lack — see Rashi there. And I found in Peri Megadim in the name of Turei Zahav who wrote we attribute that as soon as the lung was punctured the needle passed inside the lung and did not remain and did not lack — for the lack comes because it remained as Rashi wrote there. And I wonder: even without lack there is a puncture here and it cannot be protected; puncture of the sealed is puncture of what was sealed as proven in the Talmud there, which asks why not teach puncture of lining and liver. And you already know every puncture in any amount even without lack as Rashi, Ran, Ra'avad, and Shach wrote at the beginning of Piskei Tosafot; and so is proven in the Talmud and Rashi (daf 47b) regarding lung punctured or lacking — punctured means without lack — this is clear. And what Peri Megadim wrote that lung flesh is soft — requires study; this would be a membrane that arose from a blow that is not a membrane for all punctures whether sealed or unsealed, as Peri Megadim himself wrote in siman 42 regarding sealing liver to mara that if punctured opposite and afterward a membrane arose — it does not help, for we hold in chapter HaMekasheh (daf 68) treifah has no remedy. And also requires study what the acharonim wrote that if the needle is partly in the bronchus and partly in flesh — treifah, for flesh does not protect because the needle prevents. And one could say: if so, even if found in flesh certainly at first when it exited the bronchus into flesh it was partly in flesh and cannot be permitted — treifah has no remedy — requires study. And say: afterward I found this difficulty in Peri Megadim and did not find a resolution. And truly these are great difficulties; therefore one should not permit a needle in the lung at all even in great financial loss, unlike the hagahah; requires study. However in Sefer Torat Yekutiel siman 44 he proved all treifot have remedy and return to kosher through themselves except a membrane from a blow is not a membrane for it will tear. And so in Chiddushei HaRashba chapter HaMekasheh who wrote treifah has no remedy by a person such as cutting above, etc.; but disqualification that returns through itself or through remedies returns to kosher.
(Shulchan Aruch seif 16) A lung where one felt by hand that a needle is inside, etc. — if there is a drop of blood opposite the needle outside — treifah even if whole. This is learned from a needle found in the body cavity on one side below siman 48 seif 8. And according to Ra'osh there one need not examine if punctured from both sides; and so for a thorn stuck in the esophagus from inside in his view as written in seif 33 s.k. 3. If so all the more here one need not examine after a blood drop, for here it is better than stuck from one side, for the needle is found in lung flesh and is not stuck even in inner skin. However I wrote there for esophagus examination is needed l'chatchila; and so Hagahot Ashiri beginning Aleph Tav — lung equals esophagus. And the reason for treifah for a thick needle found in a cut piece of lung and coffer inside is concern perhaps it healed; meaning perhaps the needle exited outside, punctured, and returned backward as we are concerned for esophagus for the Mechaber siman 33 seif 7 and so Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba (daf 36). And even though the hagahah ruled leniently we are not concerned it healed — it appears his reason: since found stuck inside lung flesh, if punctured so much we are concerned it punctured more. And these matters appear in Sefer Beit David for Ra'ah (daf 33a). And accordingly when found in the large bronchus without being stuck it is the law of found as explained for esophagus siman 33 and siman 48 — kosher when whole. And if it already exited into flesh and returned — who aligns to that bronchus; therefore visible matters prove it was not punctured at all. This is learned from Rashi Piskei Tosafot (daf 48). And it appears therefore one may be lenient when found in the large bronchus even with a blood drop or adhesion outside kosher from outside, similar to needle found in the stomach — see siman 41 seif 8 in hagahah and Ba'er Heitev there and siman 48 s.k. 10; examine. And see what I wrote regarding needle in lung in this siman seif 6 that one should not permit at all even in great financial loss unlike the hagahah here unless found in the large bronchus and whole — kosher even with blood drop or adhesion that is anyway kosher.
