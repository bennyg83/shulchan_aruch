P) There scribes practice tagging, etc. Letters in "detailed" CHaTaTZ must have tagin but small only so letters not ruined; letters malach sofer remain without tagin — see there.
And if did not tag even SHaTNaZ GaTZ — not disqualified. And even though Beit Yosef permitted only in distress when none knows to make tagin — one may say then remembrance from Rabbenu Yerucham did not come; but afterward Rabbenu Yerucham saw Rabbenu Hai Gaon and colleagues permit when no SHaTNaZ GaTZ tagin; presumably this is Rambam's ruling plainly kosher; thus all Bach z"l's wonders in Yosef Ometz there are settled.
