(There seif 7) A lung poured like a pitcher, etc. — if the bronchi stand in their place, etc. — kosher; and the appearance of the water must be clear; but if murky or putrid — treifah. Such is the view of Ri"f and Rambam. And in truth Ri"f did not write regarding poured that they must not be putrid — only that they must not be murky; and regarding bubbles he said the opposite — putrid treifah and did not mention murky. And know all commentators did not reach the end of Ri"f's view here, therefore they multiplied difficulties on him; therefore I shall expand somewhat.
