C) Tefillin in Ashkenazic script: there is variation in Assyrian letter forms between Ashkenazim and Sephardim — Ashkenazic tefillin disqualified for Sephardim — Rav Maharam ben Chaviv responsum Orach Chaim siman 4; and so Rav HaChasid R' Yaakov Molkho responsum Orach Chaim siman 110 in Birkei Yosef ot 2, brought in Yad Efraim on laws of tefillin and straps ot 26: however in distress when Sephardic tefillin unavailable he dons Ashkenazic so as not to nullify tefillin and dons them privately — end. But in his book Beit Meir he disagreed and forbade; and so Birkei Yosef and acharonim — see there at length.
