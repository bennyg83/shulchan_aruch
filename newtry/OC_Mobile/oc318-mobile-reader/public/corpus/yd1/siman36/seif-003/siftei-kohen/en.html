And it sealed — treifah. For all punctures of treifot are not helped even by superior sealing; and there is no distinction for lung punctured and the lining sealed — siman 39 seif 18; and likewise mara punctured and liver sealed at beginning of siman 41; and likewise other sealings that help — those are sealing from the outset (meaning: as soon as the puncture was made there is immediately something to seal, as explained in examination seif 2); but here it is sealing that arose after time — Beit Yosef in the name of Maggid Commentary; and Ohel Moed general rule 51; and Rashi Piskei Tosafot daf 43 end of 43a.
