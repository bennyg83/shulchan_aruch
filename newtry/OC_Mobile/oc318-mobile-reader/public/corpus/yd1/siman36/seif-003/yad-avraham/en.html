(There seif 7) A lung poured like a pitcher, etc. — if the bronchi stand in their place, etc. — kosher; and the appearance of the water must be clear; but if murky or putrid — treifah. Such is the view of Ri"f and Rambam. And in truth Ri"f did not write regarding poured that they must not be putrid — only that they must not be murky; and regarding bubbles he said the opposite — putrid treifah and did not mention murky. And know all commentators did not reach the end of Ri"f's view here, therefore they multiplied difficulties on him; therefore I shall expand somewhat. Know Beit Yosef siman 37 wrote in the name of Sefer Orach Chaim in the name of R' Yonatan: wherever it did not putrefy outside we are not concerned it putrefies inside; and Beit Yosef wrote this does not appear so from the gemara and poskim. But in my humble opinion the words of Orach Chaim in the name of R' Yonatan are clear and necessary in the Talmud according to Ri"f's view in the version in Sukkah chapter Lulav HaGazul (daf 36): Rava asked regarding an etrog on which signs of treifah were born, etc., and concludes that he asks like Ulla said — R' Yochanan: lung poured like a pitcher is kosher; and Rava said: when the bronchus stands; here what — perhaps there it does not rule over it air and returns healthy; but here air rules and it putrefies. If not — etc. And from Rashi it is proven Rava's question deals with poured from inside, namely the seed chambers where seeds are inside and still did not putrefy. And so appears at first glance from Rava's words: but here air rules and it putrefies — meaning not yet putrefied at all. And it is astonishing: if so how does he resolve from the baraita that teaches putrefied inside when Rava's question deals with not yet putrefied. And in Kereti Peri Chodosh challenged Ri"f: if already putrefied even healthy is disqualified and all the more an etrog; and he was not precise, for even those who disagree with Ri"f and hold even putrid lung is kosher — how does he want to resolve here when this deals with already putrefied and his question is not yet putrefied. And one cannot say thus he resolves that etrog is stricter than lung in any case even putrid lung kosher — for if so what did Rava answer "not both from outside" when lung even in such case kosher and etrog treifah; therefore it is proven etrog is stricter with the reasoning air rules and putrefies. And one should not ask why Rava does not ask when already putrefied since lung even in such case kosher and etrog in May — say Rava knew from the baraita bringing etrog putrefied disqualified and only asks when not yet putrefied. Truly the first difficulty is huge how he resolves from the baraita. Also Ri"f omitted the question in Sukkah implying leniency — difficult, for it is a doubt in the Talmud and he should be stringent; and Ra'osh stood there and what he wrote it is a rabbinic doubt — strained that we know these disqualifications are rabbinic. But truth guides that Ri"f's view is even poured lung if it did not putrefy inside and outside only inside alone is not treifah. And Rava asks on etrog in such a case poured and putrefied inside alone from May: if we say since air rules it putrefies completely even outside — since outside air rules there it putrefies. Now he asks to resolve from the baraita etrog putrefied disqualified — what, not putrefied inside — thus even putrefied inside alone is treifah; and he rejects not both from outside, for there even lung is treifah. Since not resolved he rules stringently putrefied disqualified whether inside alone or outside. But poured without putrefaction at all he did not rule stringently, for he did not ask at all — this is clear. Thus proven from all the above for lung we require putrefaction outside as Orach Chaim wrote in the name of R' Yonatan, but not putrefied inside alone; and thus poured where the sign is inside even if putrefied we have no concern unless lung putrefied outside; and it is not treifah unless appearance murky as Ramban wrote in Milchamot Hashem the reason in murky one cannot examine bronchi for white threads. And this is the hagahah: one expert to examine bronchi did not melt may rely; and because of murky he wrote thus here expertise is needed. But for bubbles outside on the lung Ri"f wrote plainly if putrid treifah and did not mention murky since bronchus examination is not needed for bubbles as above siman 37 unlike Rambam. Thus all difficulties on Rashba, Ra'osh, Ran, and Magen Avraham are resolved — see Beit Yosef who brought them; examine well.
