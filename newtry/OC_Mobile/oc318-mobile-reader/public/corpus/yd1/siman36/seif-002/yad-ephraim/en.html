See in my responsa Bayit Avraham siman 15: there was on the lung a little peeling of the upper membrane, and on the membrane a blow — a break in a rib joined break to break, only the break was not opposite the peel but several fingers away; even after inflating the lung it was far as above; when examined by inflation as required the slaughterer saw at the peel site something like membrane — slaughterers call it shlufrik — one must remove membrane and examine by inflation; he did so, peeled little by little gently, found fine after examination except one place where the slaughterer's son broke a rib and made a puncture with his fingernail. The teacher feared perhaps in life it was more swollen and peel was opposite the break and membrane was treifah; slaughterer says it is shlufrik and testified they do not hold this as defect — therefore, being great loss and Yom Tov honor, he permitted. I answered: first fear is not a fear — we do not presume defect. But from membrane there is concern; slaughterer's shlufrik claim cannot be relied on — shlufrik means something moist melting like water as Hagahot HaRatz wrote here. See my books siman 160: (Truth all slaughterers commonly call shlufrik the visible membrane; they know the name. But they err, not understanding the matter, interpreting the word as they wish. Truly this word is Ashkenazi for something moving without firmness in one place, like phlegm not holding one place and slipping. In our language when saying something loose in our hands we say shlufrik in beit hadegusha, as known in language mixture changing emphasis degush to rafeh or reverse and in vocalization. Hearing most explain shlufrik as membrane and mislead teachers thinking it accepted — I wrote this root:) but membrane clinging in lung mesh needing peeling appears adhesion place to place; with peel it is treifah; even gentle peel — doubtful if helps for place to place though Taz is lenient on hanging blister passing by smoothing; may say place to place is worse — many acharonim forbid; but sides to be lenient especially gentle peel until past peel and tear somewhat far — since came out permitted, especially great loss and Yom Tov honor — see there. See my books siman 38 s.k. 49 and siman 39 s.k. 190 — even shlufrik not lenient without examination; somewhat from my books' language defect exists; with another defect — two defects.
