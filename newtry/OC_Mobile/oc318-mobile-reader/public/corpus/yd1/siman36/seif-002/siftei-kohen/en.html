Some say if they inflated it, etc. It appears to deal when they inflated in the usual way; but if they inflated excessively — kosher, for we say while alive it was not so swollen; and so is practice every day that they permit, and in examinations of the R"tz and other acharonim it is written thus: and if the membrane broke from inflation — it is dry and treifah; meaning the membrane broke in inflation around the dryness — it is dry and no examination is needed to see if it is dry — end; and it is possible in such a case the old examinations deal; and the Rav Gaon z"l explained that BeHag and Rabbenu Baruch deal when there is some flaw in the lung such as appearance, etc., that require inflation to see if it rises; and if the membrane broke and there is no flaw — treifah, for we say in any case: if we judge it as before inflation — treifah; and if as after inflation — behold the membrane broke; and Beit Yosef was omitted by Bach at end of siman 39 seif 15 in the words of Rabbenu Baruch in this hagahah — see there.
