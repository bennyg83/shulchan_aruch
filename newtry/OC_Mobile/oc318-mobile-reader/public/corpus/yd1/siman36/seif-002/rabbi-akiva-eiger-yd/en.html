(Seif 2 in hagahah) If a blister is found. And likewise adhesion out of order on the place of peeling — treifah, and smoothing does not help (Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav, siman 39 s.k. 18); and even hanging adhesion — it appears to me — treifah.
(There) On the place that was peeled. It implies to clarify that even if only the upper skin was peeled in one place and there is a blister there — treifah.
