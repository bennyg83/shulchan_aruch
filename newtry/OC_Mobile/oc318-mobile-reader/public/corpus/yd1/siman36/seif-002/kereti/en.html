From inflation — treifah. Meaning they did not inflate it excessively; but if they inflated excessively we say excessive inflation causes [damage] — Shach. And this is to be lenient; but the reverse we also say below: if there was no ring of flesh and others came and inflated with force and there was a ring of flesh — kosher, for we always go to be lenient, for the animal stands in presumption of permissibility.
