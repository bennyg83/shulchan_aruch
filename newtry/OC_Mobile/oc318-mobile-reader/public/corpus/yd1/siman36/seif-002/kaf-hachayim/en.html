(Seif 2) Even if entire upper membrane peeled, etc. As in gemara (Chullin 46a): this lung that peeled like a red onion — kosher. Rashi: peeled — upper membrane removed and exposed; red onion — remains on lower membrane red like a date.
(There) If this lung was placed once in hot water — lukewarm examination no longer helps except for great need. And if not placed at first except in cold — one may permit in financial loss and examine again in lukewarm; but if placed once sharp liquid like wine or vinegar, etc. — no examination even for great need. Kometz there ot 5.
