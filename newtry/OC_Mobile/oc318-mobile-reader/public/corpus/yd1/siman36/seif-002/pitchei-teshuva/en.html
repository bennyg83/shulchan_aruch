Treifah. Ba'er Heitev: what Rama wrote deals when there is some suspicious matter, etc.; and see in responsum Brit Avraham Yoreh De'ah siman 16 ot 1 who wrote that if the suspicious matter is specifically from adhesion, if it does not pass through smoothing it is suspicious matter and the either-or of Shach applies; but if the adhesion passes through smoothing and examination is to be stringent, one may say properly that when inflated excessively we say it was not so swollen in life and we judge it as before inflation; but what can I do, for Turei Zahav in siman 39 was stringent also for adhesion that passes through smoothing and forbids even b'dieved if not examined by inflation — thus always the either-or of Shach applies; if so when inflated it is treifah. [And per what is written below siman 39 in the name of responsum Givat Shaul to permit b'dieved in great financial loss, and so Peri Megadim there unlike Turei Zahav; and also responsum Chinuch Beit Yehudah siman 42 wrote one may be lenient — then the law stands.]
