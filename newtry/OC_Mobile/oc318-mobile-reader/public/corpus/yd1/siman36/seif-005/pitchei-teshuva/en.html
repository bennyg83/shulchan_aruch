To a wolf. Ba'er Heitev: what he wrote that kosher even without comparison, etc.; and see in Dagul Mervavah who wrote from Rama's language at the end of the seif that we do not rely on comparison to permit implies there is comparison to forbid; if so it is implied not like Hagahot Mordechai. And see responsum Maharit part 1 siman 37: asked on this and answered to permit, for where there is room to attribute we attribute even without resemblance; and wrote there that even if at first they forbade because they compared and it did not resemble, and it left presumption of kosher when they forbade — nevertheless when afterward known there is room to attribute by touch or wolf they permit it — see there.
