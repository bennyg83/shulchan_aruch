(There in hagahah) More than this — treifah. See Taz s.k. 14. This ruling is very wondrous: if so, why did the Talmud not answer as I wrote above in his words. It may be said: more than a quarter is not treifah in itself, but treifah from concern bronchi were punctured; for poured lung has great damage — we say damage did not reach bronchi, as seif 6; unlike here without such great damage, properly concern bronchi punctured. Therefore cannot establish "lacking" as quarter, for lung lack in mishnah is counted among 18 treifot. Lack more than quarter is under lung puncture, for bronchus puncture is lung puncture; all treifot more than quarter are on account of punctured bronchi. If so, one of eighteen treifot is missing.
