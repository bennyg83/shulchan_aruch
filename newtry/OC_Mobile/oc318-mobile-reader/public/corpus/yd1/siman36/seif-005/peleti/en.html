View of most authors: worm must be found on lung; even if not inside puncture, at least adjacent to puncture — we rely it punctured from worm; but when no worm on puncture, only worm inside lung — we do not rely; puncture is treifah; unlike attribution to slaughterer where elongated puncture attributed, round not — see Zevachim 72.
