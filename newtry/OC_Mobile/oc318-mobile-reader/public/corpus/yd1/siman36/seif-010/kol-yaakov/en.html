Y) Recently a Torah scroll came to me with very narrow letters so letter heads could not hold tagin; sofer minimized tagin until mostly had to glue together; sometimes one branch from roof and two branches from it to complete tag count; I erased from buyer's hand for impossible to repair; though without tagin at all I would be lenient b'dieved when no sofer to make them; when I wrote this two-three years ago to Rav Achai Maharam Mat z"l newly printed Livorno in Sefat Emet daf 71b — nevertheless absence better than bad presence; therefore Noda Biyehudah worse — core laws siman 7 ot 8.
