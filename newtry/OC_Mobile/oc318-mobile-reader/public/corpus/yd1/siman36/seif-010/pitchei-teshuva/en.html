Lacking flesh from inside. See responsum Noda Biyehudah Tinyana Yoreh De'ah siman 23 who wrote that if adhesion is found on such a place — forbid even not from two suspicious matters, for even if this hollow is not considered suspicious matter at all it is treifah, all the more if adhesion was stuck elsewhere; smoothing we are extra lenient; if flesh under adhesion is whole but flesh is gone the adhesion has nothing to hold inside, only skin — disconnects by smoothing even if not abstract moisture, only full adhesion — treifah from adhesion; even hanging adhesion treifah like hanging blister — under blister flesh is gone, no adhesion suckling inside, does not hold elsewhere (blister testifies puncture; not held because no flesh to suck from) — end. See responsum Bach siman 130 — rib broken opposite, healed — repeated damage; my books on two suspicious matters; kontrass responsum copied Maharav Moshe Gaon z"l to honored grandfather on peel — Bach responsum brought; he wrote Bach's words wondrous, my custom to permit.
