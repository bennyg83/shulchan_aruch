How many types of lung treifot. In it are 17 seifim. The lung has two membranes; if they were punctured one without the other, or one not opposite the other — kosher until both were punctured opposite each other (Tur in the name of Rashba). {Rama: And some declare treifah if one was punctured not opposite the other (Hagahot Ashiri ch. 1, Rabbenu Yerucham, and the long [Arukh], and most custom is so.)}
