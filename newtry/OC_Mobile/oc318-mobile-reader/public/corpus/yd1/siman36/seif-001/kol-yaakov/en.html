A) Seif 1. One must be precise in writing the letters so the form of none changes, etc. Regarding letter forms our master z"l in Beit Yosef brought from the pamphlet of R' Yitzchak Baruch and other poskim — know that in those forms there are matters of Ashkenazic script and there is a difference between Sephardic and Ashkenazic writing; and so Noda Biyehudah Tinyana siman 80 wrote Sephardic difference; see Leket Yosher siman 13 ot 3; therefore in every other letter we bring in brief Beit Yosef's words in the name of early and later Shulchan Aruch and the Sephardic form as Mahari Weil wrote and brought Leket Yosher in that siman:
A. The upper point shall be like a yod with a small tag on it; its face with the tag inverted upward; the thigh of the yod attached to the roof of the body at the middle of the roof; the end of the roof curved upward slightly behind it; the lower point distant from the head of the body by the thickness of a quill and a half; the lower point shall have a small tag downward on the right side because its form is like a yod hanging by its tag on the body of the alef; the left tag of the upper point aligned opposite the right tag of the lower point — Beit Yosef in the name of Shulchan Aruch. And the Sephardic point on the alef is not literally a yod; rather its head slopes and rises; its thigh goes out from it downward near the right side but not at the end, for its roof passes over it slightly behind; also it does not touch the body in its middle but slightly below the middle; and the point beneath it has no tag on the right but has a tag on the left going out from the curve downward. Leket Yosher there; and according to the kabbalah of the Ari z"l in Sha'ar HaKavanot in the drushim of tefillin drush 2: all alefs in tefillin shall be in the form of yod — the point above the vav in yod form and the lower point in the form of dalet.
If the upper yod was written inverted — if it can be repaired easily, repair it. Kessef HaChaim siman 5 on the form of alef. And it implies: but if impossible to repair, leave as is — namely in tefillin; so wrote general rule 5 ot 4; and so Pithei Teshuva siman 474 ot 5 in the name of Peri Megadim ch. 1 siman 66 in a Torah scroll where alefs were written as inverted yods — do not disqualify for this; and likewise shin and tzadi written as inverted yods — do not disqualify b'dieved — see there; and so in responsum of Noda Biyehudah Mahadura Tinyana Yoreh De'ah siman 171 — see what he wrote there.
And if the first part of the alef is like simple lines such as this א — disqualified. Forbidden to correct in a Torah scroll because of "for the honor of Heaven," and likewise anything similar where letters must have heads on them and were made like simple lines — disqualified. So wrote there; and see in our words to siman 32 ot 106.
B. It shall be square and have a sharp tag above on the right side leaning toward the alef and a thick heel below; and a small staff above on the left on its face [because its completeness is like dalet within the throat of vav] — Shulchan Aruch. In Sephardic script it has no sharp tag above on the right; on the contrary it is slightly round on the outside on one side like a reish but has a corner inside; its thigh attached below in its seat near the right end but not at the end — only the seat passes under it slightly like quill-thickness on the right. But also for the Radbaz in Sefer Magen David Luratah dalet-vav — Leket Yosher there. And our custom here in Baghdad, may it be built — we do not make for it a staff above on the left on its face.
And if the bet was made without a heel below such as this ב — meaning it does not go out from behind it below the bottom line — one may not disqualify except that it not be round. Pithei Teshuva siman 474 ot 5 in the name of Chavot Shalom siman 265 — see there. And if it appears like kaf — disqualified; and if doubt — show to a child — R' Zalman on the form of bet, Kessef HaChaim there on the form of bet; and see Peri Megadim in the introduction to siman 32 on the form of kaf and Kessef HaChaim there in Leket HaKemach ot 15.
G. Its head and body shall resemble zayin. Its head thick and the right leg descends slightly more than the left thigh; the thigh drawn thick and somewhat sloped and somewhat low; and three tagin on its head — Shulchan Aruch; and wrote in Sefer Mahari Weil for Magen David its form vav; and for Sephardim its form is as Shulchan Aruch's words. Leket Yosher there; and what Shulchan Aruch wrote "the thigh drawn thick and somewhat sloped and somewhat low" — explanation: they used to write nun kafuf; its lower seat drawn toward the left more than its head for the measure, so that if straightened it would be a straight nun; and so they wrote kafuf tzadi as below; and if they wrote nun or kafuf tzadi equal in length to other letters and not more, when supporting one letter to it they would have to distance upward such as [diagram] which is incorrect; therefore the fix is to make them longer so the second letter can be supported above too such as [diagram]. Behold they also used to write gimel with left thigh extending left more than head, for its form should appear like kafuf nun as Beit Yosef wrote; therefore it too must be made longer than other letters; however our books do not practice thus but as Sefer HaEshkol and Riaz. Kessef HaSofer there in Leket HaKemach ot 7 — extend body slightly right and need not extend left nor lengthen more than other letters.
D. Its roof long and leg short [for if leg longer than roof it resembles simple kaf]; and on the right a good heel; and a sharp corner alone is insufficient, for its form is like two vavs closed; the heel opposite the head of one vav and a tag on its face opposite the second vav; leg straight slightly sloped like gimel — Shulchan Aruch. In Sephardic script the leg is not sloped right but equal like the roof — Leket Yosher there.
H. It shall be like dalet only with a point hanging on it like dalet-vav; that point shall not touch the roof above; the point shall not be irregular but thin above and slightly thick below curved down on the right not left lest it resemble tav; not distant from roof more than quill-thickness; not so close that an average person cannot distinguish the gap from four amot; not centered on roof but on the left at the end of the roof; small staff on left on face — Shulchan Aruch. In Sephardic script the right leg straight like dalet; in Ashkenazic also straight back like dalet; hanging point a small straight line slightly thick in middle, not curved at all — form dalet-vav; likewise Magen David and Mateh Shmuel dalet-vav; upper yod tag very thin appearing to touch but not touching per the secret — Mor Uktzia, Leket Yosher there. If made like reish with point inside — kosher — so wrote general rule 5 ot 4.
What Beit Yosef wrote "not so close that an average person cannot distinguish from four amot" — Torat HaRema siman 36: not literal; less suffices that an average person recognizes well from above a Torah scroll on the word while reading — Magen Avraham s.k. 1; so wrote there Leket Yosher there in Beit Yosef there. B'dieved any separation from roof even hairbreadth — kosher; disqualified only if actually touching — Bach.
If the roof of heh was lengthened and the leg written in its middle — disqualified; specifically when roof overly long; if slightly — no concern. If this heh is from the Name Havayah — even if overly long do not touch, for some say even leg in middle is kosher [see Magen Avraham siman 32 s.k. 33; it appears in any case distinguish by child if read properly — Kessef HaChaim siman 5 on form of heh]. In other words drag left leg from where written and write at end of roof — so wrote in form of letters Leket Yosher there in Beit Yosef in form of heh, Kessef HaChaim there general rule 5 ot 4; and see Peri Megadim introduction siman 32 [end of siman] on heh if leg in middle of roof may drag roof equal to leg; no concern of erasing; so also in the name of Atzei Levanon; see below on kuf "if."
If heh made with two legs inside — permitted to drag middle leg even in the Holy Name — see there in the name of Mishnat Avraham; may also leave as is — kosher; and Binat Olam siman 54 doubts — brought in Mishnat Avraham — see there; therefore be stringent.
If this heh made with these two legs on the right side — disqualified, for it is change of letter. And if heh made like vav with point in place of heh — appears like gimel — disqualified — see there in the name of the aforementioned; make left leg of heh equal right leg, for Peri Megadim [Orach Chaim siman 32 ot 33] doubts if such unequal heh permitted (in print leg slightly cut) and correcting is erasing; do not make left leg distant from right and line like this ד — see there.
V. Round on the right side so it not resemble zayin; though zayin head passes both sides, nevertheless concern child may read zayin and disqualify. Head short so not resemble reish; faces equal — if diagonal would not be proper writing but broken [text should read "only meshita" broken script as in full Shulchan Aruch and Leket Yosher general rule 26 ot 11 in Binah]; leg long so not resemble yod; straight equally — not thin broken in middle but whole and complete; thickness tapers gradually until sharp below — hinting secret — Shulchan Aruch.
V. Head slightly sloped and tag on it. And Mateh Shmuel vav — head body tag. Mahari Weil, Leket Yosher there in the name of Mateh Shmuel — so in Sha'ar HaKavanot in that drush; namely in tefillin not Torah scroll as noted in Mateh Shmuel daf 31a and Sha'ar HaKavanot in that drush; and so R' Zalman and Kessef HaChaim on form of vav.
If vav leg found long like straight nun though head round — concern child may call it nun and disqualify — see there Leket Yosher there in Beit Yosef there. Form of severed vav — see in our words in laws of Torah scroll siman 474.
Z. Head thick and square passing over body on both sides; leg not long lest resemble straight nun — Shulchan Aruch. In Sephardic script head not square but slightly diagonal upward; passes over body both sides but not equally — thigh exits right near end; beginning of thigh above at attachment very thin, descends thickens curves back, returns sharp forward. Behold this zayin head with upper point of alef with its line — Mor Uktzia, Leket Yosher.
If zayin leg drawn from side not center but returned bent to center like this [diagram] — disqualified. Chavot Shalom Yoreh De'ah siman 269; Pithei Teshuva siman 474 ot 5 — see there.
If made like vav even with tagin — disqualified. Sha'ar Efrayim siman 81, brought in Pithei Teshuva there — see there.
Ch. Its two legs shall resemble two zayins literally, not like dalet-zayin; for we say in HaBonah: if remove roof of chet and make two zayins with high roof in middle — we see scribes of Dokni curve chet roof high meaning "living in height" — Rabbenu Tam from language of camel-hump; also "high roof in middle." Rashi: staff from left; we do both and quill-thickness between two zayins so chet square length like width — Shulchan Aruch. In Sephardic script not two zayins but wide roof with thigh like reish; somewhat like dalet — Magen David form dalet-vav or vav-dalet; inverted dalet; left leg straight line slightly thick, head thin where touches roof above. Mahari Weil, Leket Yosher; our Baghdad custom to make two zayins as Beit Yosef in Shulchan Aruch.
What R' Zalman and Kessef HaChaim wrote per kabbalah of Ari z"l: right leg like vav in tefillin and hump line on right leg thick and left thin — Sha'ar HaKavanot in that drush brought letter forms with tefillin differences — not mentioned except Mateh Shmuel daf 31a and Mishnat Chassidim tikun tefillin ch. 1 ot 9. Do not rely on kabbalistic books Chayei Adam and Mateh Chaim etc. except Ari z"l and Maharach z"l whom Rav relied on — see Nefesh HaShalom daf 60b; see there daf 58a.
If chet made as two zayins and wide roof [meaning two zayins distant and wide roof over them — Peri Megadim Orach Chaim] — disqualified for lack of hump — Magen Avraham s.k. 3; see there Leket Yosher; Kessef HaChaim there in Leket HaKemach on chet; some permit — R' Akiva Eiger ot 22 in the name of Mateh Yehudah; brought Chavot Shalom Yoreh De'ah siman 258; in distress if no other Torah scroll rely on Mateh Yehudah to read — Chavot Shalom there; likewise do not take out another Torah scroll — Kessef HaChaim Leket HaKemach ot 11; even for forbidders specifically if overly long; slight — no concern, kosher — see there; and so from responsum Darkei Noam Orach Chaim siman 1.
And likewise if chet made like two dalets, or like dalet-zayin, or like two vavs with hump — some disqualify; in tefillin no repair because of "for the honor of Heaven"; some permit b'dieved all forms of chet and do not take out another Torah scroll — see there in the name of Turei Zahav, Ehr HaGolah, Zichron Yosef, Noda Biyehudah Kama, Torat Maggid from the beginning siman 1, Tolodot Yaakov brought in Mishnat Avraham; but on weekdays one may repair all scrolls — see there in the name of Noda Biyehudah, Kessef HaChaim, Leket Yosher, Beit Aharon; tefillin we cannot disqualify and bury holy Names; therefore my view is comfortable to permit b'dieved — see there from Mishnat Avraham in the name of Be'erot HaMayim siman 20.
And if chet made like dalet-zayin — if hump touches middle of dalet line like this [diagram] — even in Torah scroll permitted to drag slightly from middle of dalet line to make zayin; no erasing concern; but if hump touches beginning of dalet like this [diagram] — forbidden to drag from dalet; even to thicken hump beforehand on right on dalet then drag middle — forbidden in Torah scroll — see there.
If chet made per Rashi's view — kosher; Beit Yosef in the name of Maggid Commentary; Magen Avraham there; see there Leket Yosher; see there only make tag at end on left to indicate intent of sages "living in height" — see there; and if no tag on left — Peri Megadim introduction siman 32 on chet doubts; what he wrote rules to forbid; but in Beit Yosef in Maggid Commentary implies only l'chatchila; and in Chiddushei Ra'ak proved entire hump matter is only mitzvah not me'akev; b'dieved not me'akev; if easily repairable — repair, for then like l'chatchila — see there; brought Kessef HaChaim Leket HaKemach ot 10; even for forbidders it appears in Torah scroll if tag not made at writing time permitted afterward; no "for honor of Heaven" like tip of yod since child reads chet — see below on yod.
This chet per Rashi — even if lengthened — kosher b'dieved; and even if not square — Magen Avraham there, Sha'arei Efraim there, R' Zalman there, Leket Yosher there, Chavot Shalom there, Kessef HaChaim there; and even though some say Rashi chet if lengthened is disqualified — view of R' Akiva Eiger and colleagues brought in that ot — view of permitters is primary, for they are many and such is our custom.
If in Torah scroll some chets with hump and some without — no contradiction, for even per Rashi chet with hump is kosher — Chavot Shalom there, Kessef HaChaim Leket HaKemach ot 10.
If in Torah scroll wide-hump chet of Rashi — forbidden to erase and write in its place wide Rashi chet, for it is lowering — Chavot Shalom there — see there.
If in Torah scroll chets like inverted nun on right and kafuf nun on left with hump above like this [diagram] — disqualify; nevertheless do not take out another Torah scroll — Kessef HaChaim Leket HaKemach ot 12 Imrei Shefer there.
If hump made on other letters such as heh or tav or closed mem or other letters — do not permit; however it appears one may repair even in Torah scroll; no "for honor of Heaven"; no erasing concern since superfluous and all superfluous is as removed — Imrei Shefer there.

T. Head somewhat long, for its form like kaf-zayin; lowers head slightly because body drawn diagonally beneath improves kaf form; also then head round above and not fit to place two tagin — for if first head equal and suitable for tagin like second they would not skip it to place two tagin on second head, similar to not passing over mitzvot; reason tagin on right are yod-like — ayin, tzadi, shin all faces upward and tag of head drawn slightly inward; but do not bend much — Shulchan Aruch. In Sephardic script right head lowers into it and slopes greatly — Magen David two forms: kaf or inverted nun. Roof low and depressed as if broken — Mor Uktzia, Leket Yosher there.
What R' Zalman and Kessef HaChaim wrote in that siman per kabbalah of Ari z"l: left head like straight vav in tefillin — per Mateh Shmuel daf 31a; but per Maharach in Sha'ar HaKavanot in that drush even in tefillin like zayin; do not deviate from his words as written above.
If did not bend tag of head inward and did not make left head like zayin — kosher and may repair afterward; no "for honor of Heaven" — Peri Megadim introduction siman 32 on tet.
Y. Head and face equal so writing appears whole not meshita; small leg not large lest resemble reish; leg bent to improve form; tag on face; small tag descends below as in HaKometz — even tip of yod is me'akev; Rashi: this is right leg bent; Tosafot challenged — obvious; Rabbenu Tam explained left head of yod bent down — thus Rashi and Rabbenu Tam require leg on right; and Rabbenu Tam surely does not argue Rashi who said in Pesikta: what is yod — one point below hinting wicked descend, one above hinting they will ascend — implies lower tag and corresponding upper tag; lower shorter than leg lest resemble chet; therefore also bend yod leg — Shulchan Aruch. In Sephardic script head and face unequal — head from halfway bends upward in thickness until sharp and tag on it continues by itself; no descending tag opposite; all letter heads like yod are equal in this — yod on alef, zayin heads, tet, lamed, ayin, tzadi, shin; legs from these heads mostly exit right near tips; some scribes exit from middle except left head on tzadi unique; Mateh Shmuel yod has three tags like Shulchan Aruch Mahari Weil Leket Yosher; what written in Mateh Shmuel three tags — so in Zohar Vayikra daf 11b and Sha'ar HaKavanot: all have three tags one above one middle one below — end; and what wrote "like Shulchan Aruch" — also Gurei Orach general rule 2 siman 11: above is upper tag, middle from upper downward, below is yod leg called tag since after mentioning remainder of tags no concern calling lower tag or leg — examine; but Mishnat Chassidim tikun tefillin ch. 1 on yod: three tags are upper, body, leg — see ot 9 and ch. 2 ot 1; and from his words Zohar daf 120-121a; but Zohar Vayikra daf 10b implies like Gurei Orach — examine.

K. Kafuf shall be round all around with no corner; faces equal above and below — Shulchan Aruch.
If corner made on back above — kosher if round below. R' Zalman; and so implied in Beit Yosef ot 81 dalet-mem ot 7; but Peri Megadim introduction siman 32 wrote any corner above or below — disqualified; Kessef HaChaim siman 5; and see there brought two explanations; wrote Leket HaKemach ot 15: rely to permit b'dieved if found in Torah scroll at reading time not to take out another — see there; appears likewise permit in Torah scroll in such case b'dieved not to require burial; see in our words above on chet "and likewise," and siman 32 ot 65; but if corner below though round above — do not permit — Leket HaSofer there.
Simple kaf: short roof lest resemble reish; therefore do not extend at end of line to lengthen; though in Hagahot Maimoniyot explained not to extend any letter — that is l'chatchila; if done — not disqualified, for widespread law in Israel that in get they lengthen letters "according to law of Moses and Israel"; but simple kaf if roof longer than leg — disqualified; and Ra'avan wrote all simple letters must be long [l'chatchila R' Zalman and Kessef HaChaim] measure that if doubled would be kafuf; therefore do not make corner above on simple kaf but round like reish — if bent becomes kafuf, no difference except simple and kafuf — Shulchan Aruch. If corners made above like dalet — some disqualify, some permit; see Magen Avraham in that ot; see there Leket Yosher; appears l'chatchila write round like reish; b'dieved do not disqualify — Leket Yosher there in Beit Yosef there, Kessef HaChaim there; and our custom permit b'dieved; and even stringent may repair in Torah scroll by adding ink to make round; not "for honor of Heaven" since form on it is called as Peri Megadim wrote [introduction siman 32 on kaf]; unlike R' Zalman who was stringent like Riaz Leket HaKemach ot 16.

Beware in simple peh inner point not inverted outward lest resemble tav — Beit Yosef in the name of Riaz; see there Leket Yosher; if inverted outward — Peri Megadim there on tav doubts; if on kafuf peh — difficult — see there.
Simple peh need not be double roof length — somewhat suffices — Peri Megadim there; see there; and see above on simple kaf.
Kafuf tzadi: first head like yod face slightly up as on tet; thigh attaches middle of neck; beware not attach low lest resemble ayin; second head like zayin; neck somewhat long to support letter; seat drawn well left — body form like nun; in curves round like all kafuf letters — Shulchan Aruch; what wrote "neck somewhat long" — see above on gimel. In Sephardic script right head like inverted yod, left straight yod; legs extend back until touch near heads on body beneath like nun or bent kaf — see there.
Simple tzadi: heads like kafuf tzadi; thigh descends below head attachment [l'chatchila R' Zalman Kessef HaChaim] to be fit for kafuf tzadi — Shulchan Aruch.
If simple tzadi yod and zayin connected and no leg drawn after — requires study; but if full yod form — kosher — Peri Megadim introduction siman 32 on tzadi — see there.
Quf: equal roof; back thigh bent well — leg form kaf hanging; leg above somewhat thick tapering — form like straight nun; therefore length like three and a half quill-thickness — Shulchan Aruch.
If left leg touches roof or thigh — disqualified — Kessef HaChaim there; even if attachment very thin — Leket Avraham general rule 27 on qof; see above siman 32 ot 101.
If in Torah scroll quf with long leg in middle of roof — dispute of Radbaz and Gan HaMelech brought; appears on Shabbat if found do not take out another; rely on Givat HaShaul; and lean toward Kenesset HaGedolah and R' Akiva Eiger siman 32 s.k. 40; but afterward repair for eighteen Radbaz etc. — see there.
Torah scroll where all quf legs attached to roof — drag entire leg and repair; no concern of erasing Yachin and Boaz ch. 1 siman 24 — Yeshuot Yaakov, Leket Yosher there.
Reish: truly round behind lest resemble dalet; faces equal — not meshita writing; long roof short thigh lest resemble simple kaf — Shulchan Aruch; reason long roof lest resemble vav — Beit Yosef there.
Shin: first head like yod face up; second likewise; third like zayin as explained on tet; body slopes down to third head and attaches there with slight heel on right face; three tagin on first head — Shulchan Aruch.
If middle head of shin like zayin — kosher; if inverted — Mahari Pigo permits — see there; and see above on alef "if."
If wrote shin with four heads — even if child knows to read it — no repair by dragging one yod to leave three heads; must nullify form and complete, otherwise becomes chet; in Torah scroll if written below — no repair because "for honor of Heaven" — Peri Megadim there on shin.
Tav: like dalet literally; thigh like inverted dalet; end of thigh opposite end of roof; right leg short lest child read dalet-lamed-tet for simple peh; left leg touches above — all letters must be whole — Shulchan Aruch.
If tav roof overly long and leg in middle — law like heh and quf — Peri Megadim there on tav, Kessef HaChaim there on tav.
If in tav two legs in middle — change of letter and disqualified; not similar to heh with two legs where roof unaffected — see there in the name of Mishnat Avraham in the name of Sefer STaM siman 62.
If left leg like simple line — disqualified, resembles chet per Rashi z"l — see there.
