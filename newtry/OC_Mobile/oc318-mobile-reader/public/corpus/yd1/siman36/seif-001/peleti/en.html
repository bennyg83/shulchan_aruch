Punctured one without the other, etc. — one should understand on this view above siman 33 regarding needle stuck: we are concerned perhaps healed — meaning membrane from blow healed; if so why not concern upper membrane healed; and do not say here it deals entirely from illness but there from blow — for there too main concern is healed membrane.
And some forbid not opposite one another. Shach s.k. 1 gave reason: sometimes wind enters here and exits there; if so even from two winds one should be stringent; and Taz s.k. 1 gave reason: sometimes they happen to align; therefore all permit from two winds. I did not understand his words: Shach's reason is better.
