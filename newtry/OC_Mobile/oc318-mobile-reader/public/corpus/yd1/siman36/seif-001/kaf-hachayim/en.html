(Siman 36 seif 1) The lung has two membranes; if punctured one without the other or one not opposite the other — kosher, etc. And the reason Beit Yosef wrote: behold the two skins are attached to each other and wind does not enter between them for the most part; and even if you say wind enters here and exits there — since they are not aligned opposite each other we have no concern.
(There) If punctured one without the other, etc. — kosher. His fellow protects him; and one must remove the lung and inflate it and place saliva or lukewarm at the puncture to see if it does not bubble, so it will be known clearly the second was not punctured; for whatever can be clarified we clarify and do not rely on presumption of kosher.
(There in hagahah) And some declare treifah when punctured not opposite, etc. And so Bach wrote in the name of several poskim that such is our custom, unlike Beit Yosef who ruled leniently in Shulchan Aruch; and so Shach in Nekudot HaKesef in the name of several poskim unlike Maharshal; and so Pri Chadash ot 1 in Levush ot 1; Shulchan Gavoah ot 2.
(There) However even for those who forbid — this is when both from one wind; but from two winds — kosher. Taz s.k. 1. Pri Chadash there. Levush ot 1; however Shach in Nekudot HaKesef challenged Taz's words and his view: even from two winds — meaning one from back and one from front — one may not permit.
(There) However if one punctured in right arugah and one in left arugah — no concern. Turei Zahav ot 3 on seif 4 there. Ba'er Heitev on seif 7 ot 2. Pri Chadash ot 1. Kometz there. Mateh Yehonatan there. And see Pithei Teshuva there.
(There) Even if entire upper peeled, etc. And the same if blisters on entire upper skin and peeling on lower has no blisters — law is as peeled; and specifically after inflation. Levush in hagahah to Semak ot 3. Ba'er Heitev in Levush ot 2. Pri Chadash ch. 1 to siman 36 in Ba'er Heitev ot 2.
(There) And majority is as all. Pithei Teshuva ot 3. Ba'er Heitev on seif 7 ot 2. Pri Chadash siman 29 ot 2. Kometz there ot 4.
