If lacking from the body of the lung and it was not punctured — kosher; and some forbid. {Rama: And the custom is to permit if the deficiency is like a simple spoon; but if the deficiency is like a bent spoon such that one could place a thumb in that depression — it is called a deficiency and treifah; and if not — kosher; and all this when the deficiency is visible from outside after inflation; but if from outside it appears like other lung except that flesh is lacking inside — even if the deficiency holds up to a quarter — kosher; more than this — treifah (Mahari Weil, old examinations, and see practice, Shas, Tur, poskim, and Beit Yosef in the name of Ohel Moed).}
