(s.k. 11) All his words are far-fetched, as one who understands knows. There is no wonder on Tur.
(s.k. 21) But Rama's view is doubtful, etc. No difficulty he did not write "if the matter is doubtful" — only to teach that even in doubt examination does not help for us; and certainly if clearly by human action examination does not help even per Mechaber; but if clearly by heavenly causes he relied on what he wrote afterward and did not want to permit entirely without examination like Rabbenu Nissim, since Rashba disagrees; nevertheless he holds one may be lenient via examination — clear.
(Hagahah) See Beit Yosef who objected to Hagahot Ashiri on daf 25a regarding requiring examination if it does not crumble under fingernail; Bach and Maharshal lengthy to settle Hagahot Ashiri; it appears Hagahot Ashiri in the name of the Geonim is concerned for sealed — therefore requires this examination; in Shas it is not because of sealed but because of shriveled — therefore this examination does not help and examination from above is needed; Hagahot Ashiri also agrees we require entire shriveling for examination from above, but says even if partially dried it needs this examination for sealed — see BeHag daf 128 beginning of 3a and you will find as my words — end.
