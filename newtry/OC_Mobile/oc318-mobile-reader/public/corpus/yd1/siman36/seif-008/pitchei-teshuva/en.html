See responsum Noda Biyehudah part 2 siman 5 in the middle of the responsum who brought proof for Rambam's view to forbid in every case; and wrote that so appears to rule practically to forbid any external lack whether like bent kaf or simple kaf in any manner — requires study for practical law. [And in responsum Chavot Shalom siman 35 he wrote: though the law is not like Rambam for internal lack, nevertheless teachers should not treat his reasoning lightly, for if several reasons combine there is room to be stringent; however those small internal lacks visible on the lung like punctures visible to a person registered from illness in hospital — it is simple there is no house of concern even for Rambam, etc. — see there.] And see what is written below siman 38 s.k. 2 in the name of responsum Givat Shaul siman 86.
