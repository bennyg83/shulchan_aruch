Ch) Seif 3: must tag, etc. — tagin must touch letter body and not each other; if not touching letter body or touching each other — disqualified — Rav HaRif Meir Abulafia siman 38, Kenesset HaGedolah Hagahot Beit Yosef siman 41 s.k. 3; see there on touching letter to letter yod, Leket Yosher siman 12 ot 10, Beit Meir on the matter ot 17, Kessef HaChaim siman 5 ot 4, general rule 5 ot 5; and see Yosef Ometz siman 41; and below ot 8; and even per Rambam tagin not me'akev — when none at all; but when wrote and did not join — extra letters and disqualify — Rama there, Kenesset HaGedolah there; Magen Avraham abbreviated; if not touching body — disqualified; hence responsum Zichron Yosef siman 18: tagin disqualified need repair but scroll not disqualified — no worse than no tagin at all; and so appears R' Zalman's view in his Shulchan Aruch; b'dieved even if none at all — kosher implies; truth explicit in Rama when has tagin unjoined worse than none — Leket HaSofer ot 28; Yosef Ometz there; however if known expert wrote — attribute separated afterward and permit — Rama there, Kenesset HaGedolah there; likewise attribute touched afterward — Magen Avraham there; see there Leket Yosher, Beit Meir; Rama's reason: not worse than unbound sheets siman 32 seif 16 permitted if written in kosher hide — Levush; thus Peri Megadim's difficulty resolved unlike Ya'avetz and Leket Avraham; if separated from start — may join afterward even in Torah scroll; no "for honor of Heaven"; Yad Efraim: where tagin not touching from start — do not repair to join, for small letter added on letter — see there; we do not hold thus — no worse than extra word in STaM siman 32 ot 122 permitted to join to prior word; practice daily joining separated tagin from start — no objection.
