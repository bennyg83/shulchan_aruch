Lacking from body of lung. Explanation: from birth; and alternate text: Rambam's view that even internal lack is called lack; nevertheless specifically after inflation the skin stands and covers; if not — treifah.
If the deficiency is like a simple kaf. Whether in middle of lung or at its slopes; and not as the R"tz wrote in examinations — specifically at slopes; there is no distinction; but I am astonished at Rama who forbids more than quarter — requires study.
More than this — treifah. This is written in Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer Ohel Moed: it appears to me more than quarter is treifah, for so we infer from "even a speck does not return" — end; and from this Maharshal and others were stringent.
