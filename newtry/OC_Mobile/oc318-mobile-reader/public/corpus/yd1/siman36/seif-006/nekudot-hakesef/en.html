(Taz s.k. 16) And in my books I wrote it appears simple Tur's view is like Rashba and Mahari Weil — tear the membrane alone immediately adjacent to the sealed place; for if flesh too is punctured, certainly air exits; perhaps from there downward it is blocked. Tur wrote "tear the sealed place" — meaning tear the membrane that is sealed; not like Maharshal chapter Aleph Tav siman 40 — see there.
