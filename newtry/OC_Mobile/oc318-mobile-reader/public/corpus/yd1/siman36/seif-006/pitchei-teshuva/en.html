Worms. Ba'er Heitev s.k. 13 [and see responsum Chavot Shalom siman 44 regarding rams in his country whose liver was very wormy and many times lungs from those rams were brought before him; he saw red stains and many red dots; they examined the lung by inflation and water and it bubbled and many recognizable worms were found on those lungs from the liver worms — if to attribute these punctures to those worms he elaborated and concluded: if a puncture is found at a red dot or red stain — do not attribute for any matter and it is treifah; however if found at plain lung appearance one may permit if worms are on the lung too even if recognizable as from liver worms — see there; and see Turei Zahav and Peri Megadim]. And see responsum Givat Shaul siman 85: asked regarding a bubble on the lung with a worm inside — what is the law; he answered where there is no puncture in the bubble it is simple to permit and this is not two suspicious matters; but even if puncture is found in the bubble and the worm is partly in the puncture and partly outside — it appears to permit; but if the worm is not in the puncture only on the lung — one may question the permit, since Maharshal and Bach forbid even without a bubble, and though we practice to permit, regarding a bubble one may say it is two suspicious matters — see there.
