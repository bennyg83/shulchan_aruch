(There seif 17) A needle found in the large bronchus of the lung — kosher even in a cut piece. This implies even when whole one can feel; and do not challenge how one can feel by hand when it is in the large bronchus — say such as it fell through the trachea but is still whole. Bach wrote in the name of Bach: even if the needle is stuck through the lung beyond beyond, if there is no blood drop at all we say after slaughter it was pressed and passed like needle in body cavity siman 48. Shach challenged him and Mateh Yehonatan settled it there. Nevertheless they left it requires study perhaps similar to esophagus siman 33. And in my humble opinion the reason is clear: we say liquids washed it; since the lower membrane of the lung is red as in the Talmud and poskim, a blood drop is not discernible in it as regarding esophagus siman 33. And where examination is impossible — treifah as below siman 48 seif 8. And one may say here too perhaps blood remains inside and is not visible. And I still question Bach's view: though we are not concerned for lung in such a case that we say after slaughter it punctured, nevertheless we should be concerned for esophagus with a thin needle or coffer outside, for since lung is punctured it is not better than cut, for we declare treifah from the reason. Granted when lung is whole before us we do not strengthen suspicious matter to say already punctured and membrane arose as in Piskei Tosafot; but when punctured before us this does not apply.
