The membrane alone. And not the flesh, for if not when he cuts the sealed place air would exit and perhaps from there downward it is blocked — see Mahari Weil and Beit Yosef and Rashba.
If moisture is found in it, etc. Because of moisture it did not rise in inflation; and in the animal's lifetime the moisture could exit; and in Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe in the name of Rokeach: likewise murky blood or putrid flesh — see there.
And if not — treifah. For in the animal's lifetime the lung hovers on the heart; and if there is a sealed place it cannot draw air and inflate — Rashba; and further poskim wrote stringency when putrid inside — see Taz and Peri Megadim.
