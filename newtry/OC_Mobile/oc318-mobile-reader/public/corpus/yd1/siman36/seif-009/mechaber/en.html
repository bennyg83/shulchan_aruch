A lung in which a sealed place is found — whatever size (Rambam ch. 7 law 6) — where air does not enter and it does not rise in inflation — they bring a knife and tear (the membrane alone) (Mahari Weil, Beit Yosef in the name of Maggid Mishneh, and Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba) immediately adjacent to the sealed place; if moisture is found in it that prevented air from entering there — permitted; and if no moisture is found they place a little saliva or straw or down upon it; if it shakes — kosher, for behold air enters there; and if not — treifah. {Rama: And it is only called sealed when its appearance is similar to other lung except that it does not rise in inflation; but if its appearance is similar to mucus it is only tinnir and kosher without examination (Hagahot Maimoniyot, Semag, and Beit Yosef in the name of Rashi); and even for sealed one should first place the lung in lukewarm water or put lukewarm water into the tube and shake it (Hagahot Ashiri), or feel much at the sealed place (Rokeach) and afterward pour out what is in the tube — if it then rises in inflation it is kosher; and even if the lung was already cut, if one can examine via a tube that the sealed place rises in inflation one may permit (Mahari Weil and Arukh entry afi, explained as inflation in Shas).}
