(Lekut) Any fowl that, etc. — there are three signs, etc. — Mechaber abbreviated and poskim agreed per Rambam bar Yosef who explained in mishnah: any seizing fowl is tame — meaning certainly tame, for no pure fowl seizes; and any with extra toe, etc. — if it has all three signs certainly pure, for no tame fowl has three purity signs — all twenty-four tame have only one purity sign; raven has two; vulture has none; and any known not to seize need no other sign and certainly pure, as written 62a Ameimar ruled, etc., and it is specifically that it does not seize; and as explained; and version 61a Tanna R' Chiya: fowl, etc., and learn from verses, etc.; if so raven that Scripture wrote, etc.; answer: if so other birds unless raven; and there learned twenty-four, etc.; three return in all of them — twenty-two have only one sign; three signs in body — extra toe, crop, gizzard peels — return in all twenty, each has one; raven has two; fourth sign not in all twenty-four — none seize; peres and ozniyah also only one sign — one of them has sign of not seizing; Tosafot there s.v. saw it, etc. (end).
(Lekut) That seizes — Rashi: holds with claws and lifts from ground what it eats — see there 59a s.v. the seizer, etc.; Tosafot: eats alive — see there 61a s.v. the seizer and so Tosafot HaRosh 62a and Tosafot Bava Kama 16b s.v. seized, etc.; and Tosafot HaRosh brought proof cannot mean literal seizing, else wolf too — we taught wolf's seizing; and so Maharam chapter 9 (end).
And if known, etc. — 62a Ameimar said, etc.; per Geonim' view known it does not seize alone is one sign; and this is what Mechaber wrote here and did not write with one sign permitted because commentators multiplied; and Perishah and Lechem Mishneh and Ribash siman 191; nevertheless for us no difference as explained; and even though it has, etc.; and Perishah 62b s.v. saw it, etc.
There are three, etc. — though one sign suffices as explained, because commentators multiplied per Tosafot there s.v. and it is specifically this sign alone that it does not seize; but with another sign we fear perhaps raven; and for Rambam specifically with another sign and with two signs all agree we fear; but with all four signs no concern for anyone.
(Lekut) And even though it has three signs, etc. — because there are versions and other explanations; Tosafot HaRosh; meaning Rashi's explanation and Perishah 62b s.v. saw it, etc.; per his view requiring four signs known also not to seize — impossible to be expert like marsh chicken; but per poskim and Tosafot, whoever has three other signs is known not to seize — no concern; Tosafot there s.v. saw it, etc. (end).
