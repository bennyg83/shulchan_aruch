Rashal chapter 1 siman 115. Shach s.k. 1. Kenesset HaGedolah end note 40. Peri Chadash note 3. Beit Lechem Yehudah end note 2; see below note 13.
(4) There. And hunter is believed, etc. — here regarding fowl one does not recognize, therefore eaten by hunter's tradition; unlike local traditions: where some places eat it, it is known; where they do not eat, they know other places eat — still we see they do not eat and one may argue they practiced prohibition due to similar tame or divided in acceptance.
