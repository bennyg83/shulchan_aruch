The hunter — Shach wrote: but he is not believed to say this fowl my master the sage permitted; nevertheless if a Torah scholar testifies on a fowl accepted as pure he is believed.
Seizes — Shach wrote: meaning it inserts claws into living creatures and seizes them; Taz explained in Tosafot's name that it does not eat the bird alive but tears until it dies first then eats.
The air — Taz explained: when they throw food it catches in its mouth from the air and eats and does not place on ground before swallowing — unlike chicken that also catches from air but places on ground before swallowing — so is Rashi's view, end.
Toe — meaning behind the toes; others explained toe before it is large and extra — so Ran.
Perhaps — and even though we see it does not seize now, still we fear perhaps it will seize another time.
Their fathers — Shach wrote: it appears if they have tradition they need not check further; but certainly if found it seizes or splits legs one may not rely on tradition — mistaken acceptance.
