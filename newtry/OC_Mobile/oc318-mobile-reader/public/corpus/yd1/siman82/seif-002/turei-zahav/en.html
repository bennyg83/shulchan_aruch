Any fowl that seizes and eats — Tosafot explain it does not eat the bird alive but tears until it dies first then eats afterward.
Or catches from the air — when they throw food it catches in its mouth from the air and eats and does not place it on the ground before swallowing — unlike a chicken that also catches from air but places on ground before swallowing — so is Rashi's view.
Extra toe — behind the toes; and though in most birds it is called extra because not in order with fellows, others explained the toe before it is large and extra — called extra toe; so Ran.
