Even if he intends to return — and this is not applying stringencies of the place he left, for what they do not eat there is not from prohibition but lack of acceptance from fathers and they were not taught prohibition — so Rosh.
Permitted to eat — Rashal wrote: from Rosh's words he may eat only because of his tradition, but not they; even from Rosh and Tur it is clear all places may rely today on tradition of those who received permission — namely places that did not practice prohibition but only lacked tradition; but where they practiced prohibition one should not rely on their acceptance; and so Rashba, etc.; and with this what is implied in the next seif that even one who forbids agrees here it is permitted.
