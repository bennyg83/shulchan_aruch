A majority of stores sell slaughtered meat and a minority sell nevelah meat; one took from one of them and does not know from which he took, and it became mixed with others and is not recognizable — nullified in the majority because of a double safek: {Rama: And some forbid in such a case, because wherever the issur is in its place it is considered half-and-half, and since the first safek is forbidden by Torah law there is no further safek here to permit — only mixtures are not called a double safek (Arukh general rule 25) — and so is the custom.}
