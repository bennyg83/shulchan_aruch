(There seif 7 in hagahah) And even to eat them two-by-two — it is forbidden for another person to eat all of them. Peri Chadash challenged this from the fact that regarding the altar R. Eliezer also permits offering two-by-two. See in Imrei Tzrefah on Yoma, who resolved that sacrifices are different because the Sages were not as stringent — as Tosafos in Zevachim (74) wrote.
