"And it became mixed with others." — and they challenged: behold, the Mechaber himself rules; and likewise Rashba shows this law — that there are not one safek in itself and one in mixtures; and see Peleti that one must force to explain that it deals with that this seller of treifot — it is not recognizable which animal; and by Torah law one in two is nullified, and it is only kavu'a d'rabbanan; but this is forced; only the correct view is as Shach wrote — kavu'a is a novelty, and you have in it only its novelty, and it suffices to forbid with one safek but not with two safeks; and see Peleti; and according to this, if nine stores sell nevelah meat and one slaughtered, and there was kavu'a like half-and-half, and it became mixed with others — even the Mechaber concedes that double safek does not help, for on the contrary, aside from the novelty it is certainly forbidden, for the majority is forbidden.
