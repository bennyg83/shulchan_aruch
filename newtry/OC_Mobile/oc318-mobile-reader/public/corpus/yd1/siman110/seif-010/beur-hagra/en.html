Regarding the lung, etc. — for we establish animals in a presumption of kashrus, as in every safek that we establish on its presumption; and even regarding a married woman, as written in chapter 4 of Yevamot (80, 11) and other places; and all the more so here, as written in the first chapter of Chullin (9a) "it was slaughtered in a presumption," etc. — behold it teaches us, etc.; and all the more so that one witness testifies, and in Beit HaGedolah.
