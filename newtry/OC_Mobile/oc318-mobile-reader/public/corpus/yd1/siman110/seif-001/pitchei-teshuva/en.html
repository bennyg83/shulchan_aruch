Tamid. Avodat HaTaharah. And see in Minchat Yaakov general rule 42 s.k. 42 that wrote in the name of Taz that one should be lenient b'ein acher makif regarding an egg that is nullified; and wrote that thus one should practice — see there. And see in responsa Teshuvah MeAhavah volume 1 siman 113 that one teacher relied on Minchat Yitzchak's words that an egg is nullified b'ein acher makif; and wrote that it is not good that he ruled, for Minchat Yitzchak's words are nullified — for what Taz wrote that b'ein acher makif it is permitted, that is something sometimes counted; but an egg even b'ein acher makif is forbidden nowadays (also Pri Megadim challenged him on this); and nevertheless after he already ruled to be lenient, he sided with this according to what Rashi wrote in Zevachim side 2 s.v. "everything that is customarily counted," and even though there are those who are not particular and add extra, etc.; and likewise in s.v. "but according to the one who says" — see there; and now in these countries they add extra for the buyer — it is also not customarily counted — see there.
