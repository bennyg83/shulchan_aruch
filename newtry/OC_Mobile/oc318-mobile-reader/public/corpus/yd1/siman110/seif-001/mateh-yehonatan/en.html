(Siman 110 in the Shulchan Aruch, seif 1) An important item forbids in its kind in any amount, etc. Behold, the Rav, author of Peri Toar, explained that the matter of "not nullified in sixty" applies to something forbidden for benefit; but regarding something forbidden only for eating, even an important item is nullified — and his words are entirely incomprehensible, for we find that live animals are never nullified even for something forbidden only for eating: in a gored animal that became mixed, Rabbeinu Tam ruled that it is permitted through suppressing them, since they move, and we say "whatever separates, etc." — it appears that otherwise live animals are never nullified even though there is no prohibition of benefit.
