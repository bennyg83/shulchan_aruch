Important thing, etc. All of this is the language of the Rambam, and it appears to mean specifically in species in its kind, not as he wrote above in siman 101, seif 6, in the hagahah. And even though one can push back, because one does not find a dry mixture of a species not in its kind that is not discernible, nevertheless it is superfluous language — only here does he say specifically. And behold, ostensibly there is proof for the words of the aforementioned hagahah from what is written at the beginning of Yevamot (54), and in chapter 8 of Zevachim — the Tanna of the case of a liter of dried figs, etc.; and behold, for R' Yehudah without blilah, species in its kind is not nullified. And likewise what he says there at the end, "she rolled it in a circle," etc., s.v. ya'alu. And likewise in the chapter Nezikin (54b), "they fell and split," etc. — the words of R' Yehudah; only here it deals with not in its kind. But already Tosafot explained in Zevachim 73, s.v. Rabbi Yehudah — R' Yitzchak asks that species in its kind is not nullified except wet with wet. And with this it is also resolved the chapter Notel (141b) — R' Yitzchak says even we raise, etc. And behold, here it is species in its kind, that not in its kind is nullified in sixty, as written in chapter 2 of Orlah to be lenient and to be stringent — species not in its kind, etc. And likewise in Yerushalmi chapter 6 of Nedarim: "and anything that has no permitted form, such as terumah," etc., and not in its kind nullified in sixty. And that passage in chapter 5 of Yevamot (38b) that says wheat and wheat are not nullified according to R' Yitzchak. And likewise that which is said in Menachot 23 regarding the mishnah "two meal offerings," etc. — it is according to R' Yitzchak in flour that is wet with wet. R' Yehudah, etc. Nevertheless, the words of Tosafot are not necessarily that he holds like the Rambam, but because one does not find a mixture of not in its kind in dry, as noted above. And it is possible he explains according to R' Levi because the sugya at the beginning of Yevamot (and in chapter 8 of Zevachim) according to R' Levi who says "this Tanna," etc., that even regarding d'rabbanan, etc.; and he is according to R' Levi who holds the reason of R' Meir is because it is d'rabbanan; but R' Yochanan holds it is d'oraisa, and according to the Rambam's explanation that all fruits is d'oraisa except vegetables — not like Rashi and Tosafot. Nevertheless the difficulty stands in its place — why did he rule like R' Yehuda that even regarding d'rabbanan it is not nullified; and furthermore, at the conclusion in Yevamos there where he says "This is according to the Rabbanan and I who say like R' Yosi" — it applies even to R' Yochanan, which ostensibly is difficult since R' Yitzchak holds like the Rabbanan — what forced him to establish that case of the circle according to R' Yosi?
