The law of safek treifot that occurred in meat. In it are 10 seifim: An important thing forbids in its kind in any amount; and they are seven things, and these are: peeled nuts and Badan pomegranates and sealed barrels and turnip stalks and cabbage heads and Greek gourd and loaves of the master of the house; and likewise important animals are not nullified; but other things — even though their way is to count them — behold these are measured by their measure: {Rama: And some say that every davar sheb'minyan — meaning whose way is always to count it — is not nullified (Tur in name of R"I and Beit Yosef); and so is the custom. That an important thing is not nullified is only d'rabbanan, and we follow its doubt leniently (Arukh general rule 25 and Rambam chapter 17 of Forbidden Foods and Tosafot Zevachim end daf 73 and Agudah there and Rosh chapter 3 and Rashba in Teshuvot and Nimukey Yosef chapter HaAreil and many other poskim as will be explained in this siman in several places):} Every thing that is important to the people of a place among the places — such as peeled nuts and Badan pomegranates in Eretz Yisrael in those times — forbids in any amount according to its importance in that place and at that time; and these were mentioned only because they forbid in any amount in every place; and the same applies to everything similar to them in other places.
