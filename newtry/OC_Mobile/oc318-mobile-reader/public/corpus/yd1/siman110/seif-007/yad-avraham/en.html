(There, seif 7 in the gloss) And even to eat them two by two, it is forbidden for another person to eat all of them. The Peri Chadash raised a difficulty on this from that regarding the altar, where R' Eliezer also permits to offer two by two. And see in the book Imrei Tzerufah at the end of Yoma, who answered that the case of a korban is different, for the Sages were not so stringent, as Tosafot wrote in Zevachim (daf 74).
