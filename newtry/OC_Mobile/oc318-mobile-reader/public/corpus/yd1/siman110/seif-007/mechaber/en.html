A thing that is not nullified because of its importance, such as animals and beriah and a piece worthy of honor, and a thing that has matirin that became mixed with others and one of them was eaten b'shogeg (whether he himself ate b'shogeg or others ate him b'shogeg) (Terumat HaDeshen siman 175) (Kereti) or it fell by itself into the sea in a manner that it is lost from the world — the others are permitted, for we attribute to say the issur went away; and specifically when eating the remainder two by two together, for from your soul there is one that is permitted; but to eat them one by one — forbidden: {Rama: And even to eat them two by two — forbidden for one person to eat all of them; and even two people may not eat all of them at once (Beit Yosef per view of Tur).}
