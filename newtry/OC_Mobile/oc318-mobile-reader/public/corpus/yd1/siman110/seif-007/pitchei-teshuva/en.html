"And one of them ate." — See in Tiferet L'Moshe that wrote that likewise if one separated one of them and it was cooked or became mixed with issur in a matter that is not nullified and forbidden d'oraisa (and requires study if it is a matter that is not nullified d'rabbanan — what is its law) or was sold to a non-Jew — for behold you need not judge upon it — see there; and so too Or Zarua in responsa Panim Me'irot volume 1 siman 105 in the middle of the responsum that wherever one need not judge upon the piece — such as it fell to issur and there is no sixty to nullify; and likewise regarding live animals — if one animal became tereifah while alive — such as its brain membrane was pierced and the like — or one of them died — we rely on it the issur and the rest are permitted; however where it died by a person — one can say it does not help to permit the rest, for when one fell to the sea we require specifically that it fell — but if one threw it, even b'shogeg we penalize on account of meizid; and if it died by a person it is like throwing b'shogeg; however if this person who killed did not know of the mixture — it is permitted, for it is not applicable to penalize him and he is not called shogeg — only the mixture was known but he thought that thereby the rest would not be permitted and the like; but where this person did not know — even though another person knew — it is literally like falling by itself — end of his words — see there well.
