One who took meat from the slaughterhouses (meaning the butcher shops) — and even a piece worthy of honor — and a treifah was found in the slaughterhouses and the treifah pieces were not known and he does not know from which he took — all that they took from the slaughterhouses before the treifah was found is permitted, for the safek did not fall in a fixed case but only after it separated; and since the majority is kosher — permitted; but what he took from then on is forbidden, and even a piece not worthy of honor — for not everyone is expert in this and they err between worthy of honor and not worthy of honor.
