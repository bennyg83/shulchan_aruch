(There in the Taz, se'if katan 5) And if you say that in butcher shops where many are common we decree more — yet the opposite is taught in the chapter "Chavit" (daf 147): but ten people, etc.; and Rashi explained: since they are many, they remind one another. And see in Nekudot HaKesef who challenged him: that case is different, for the ten people are in one gathering; therefore we say they remind one another, etc.; but here one should be concerned that they will come to take one after another. And it appears to me that this distinction is compelling from that at the beginning of Eruvin (daf 3) regarding part of a beam within twenty and part above twenty, etc., that Rabbah said regarding a mavoi it is invalid, and Rava explained as a messenger the reason of a mavoi: it is for the many — they rely on one another and do not remind; and raise a difficulty from that which is taught in the chapter "Chavit" (there), that on the contrary, with many they remind one another. However, according to what Nekudot HaKesef distinguished between whether the many are in one gathering or not — that of Eruvin too is well, for there too sometimes there are not many in the mavoi, and an individual comes to use at that time and there is no reminding. And nevertheless there is no difficulty from there against the Taz, for this distinction is only according to that version; but according to the first version there, that Rabbah said the many remind in every case — we say the many remind one another. And so is the main [view], as I wrote in my novellae on Eruvin (daf 18), and from there there is support for the opinion of the Taz.
