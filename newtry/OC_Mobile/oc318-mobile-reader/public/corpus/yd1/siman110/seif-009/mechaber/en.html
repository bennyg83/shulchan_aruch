Safek treifah that became mixed with others — all are forbidden until there is in the heter enough to nullify the issur, if it is among things that are nullified; since the first safek was in its body — one may not permit it through double safek: {Rama: And some say the reason it is forbidden is because the safek (Taz) is a d'oraisa issur and we cannot say another safek that there is no issur here only that it became mixed with others — is not called double safek and is forbidden (there); but if there were two doubts whether there was any issur here at all (Gra, Baer Hetev, Kereti) and both doubts became known together — they permit double safek in every place even in d'oraisa issur and the body of the issur; and even if it had a presumption of issur, such as a chicken standing in a presumption of issur and its wing broke or fell off — safek whether from life or after slaughter; and if you say from life — perhaps the lung was not perforated (Taz) — one may permit through double safek (Gra) even though one could clarify through examination of the lung — one need not be concerned (Taz, Baer Hetev); and see above siman 53.}
