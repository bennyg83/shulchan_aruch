9) And regarding an important item that forbids in benefit, which one sells except for the monetary value of the forbidden portion within it — it is certain that we require selling everything together; but to sell them one by one and discard the last, or discard the first, is forbidden, lest the buyer receive the monetary value of the benefit prohibition. Not so when he sells them together and discounts toward the non-Jew the portion of the prohibition. Shulchan Panim ibid. Zivchei Tzedek note 6.
