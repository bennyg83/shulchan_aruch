All the others are permitted. See in responsa Or Zarua Panim Me'irot volume 1 siman 105 that was asked: if issur of tereifah arose in the butcher shop and among them was a piece worthy of honor, and several pieces are known to be from one animal — by way of parable: the tongue of an animal was hanging with the shoulder in one place, and likewise each animal's pieces are placed all in one place alone — and a dog came and ate one tongue of an animal — whether there is room to permit the other tongues to eat two by two or not — for it is impossible to say the other tongues are of heter, since the meat remained in issur; and he answered regarding issur: specifically when one piece was lost entirely we rely on it the issur; but if some remained there is no room to say this is the issur to permit the others; on the contrary we say the issur is in the majority; therefore all the tongues are forbidden — see there at length.
