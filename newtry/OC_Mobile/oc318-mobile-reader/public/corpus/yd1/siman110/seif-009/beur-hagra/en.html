Even though, etc. In Tosafot, Avodah Zarah 38b, s.v. i, etc., "and the reason is because it is safek safeka," etc., and the Rosh wrote there siman 38, "and if you ask — according to his reasoning it is doubled; one can say that since it is safek safeka they did not trouble him," etc., and as written in Niddah 66a, "and she should examine," etc., and Tosafot there, s.v. vetivdok, etc., and R' Tam, etc., "and one can establish," etc., and Semag explains that this is what the Gemara means: because there is no, etc., "good advice" — it teaches us that now it is permitted without examination because of safek safeka, lest it is not from the source; and if you say from the source, lest it will not come in a second husband — for not all fingers are equal — which is not so when she examines, that it will be clarified that it came from the source and she will be forbidden to a second [husband].
