A thing that is not nullified because of its importance that became mixed with others and one fell from this mixture to two others and one fell from the three to two others — behold these others are permitted, for behold the one of the first mixture was nullified in the majority; and if one of the first mixture fell into a thousand — all are forbidden, and we do not say nullified in the majority except to permit their double safek — that if it falls from the second mixture to another place it does not forbid. (Language of Rambam chapter 16 of Forbidden Foods law 9): {Rama: And in any case one person may not eat all of them; and specifically when the issur that became mixed is a definite issur; but a safek issur that became mixed and fell from that mixture to another place — the second mixture is permitted (Arukh general rule 26); some say a thing that has matirin one may not permit through double safek (there general rule 25) — and it is good to be stringent if not for need, since he has a permit in any case.}
