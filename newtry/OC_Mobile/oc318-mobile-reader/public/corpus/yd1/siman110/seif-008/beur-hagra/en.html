An item that is not [nullified in the majority], etc. According to the Rambam's view, as noted, and so in siman 110 — and unlike the Rambam there, but like the Ra'avad's critique there; but all the poskim agreed to Tosafot's and the Rashba's view, and Shach, and the Shulchan Aruch and hagahah in this siman sealed it several times.
And it fell, etc. When it was separated to a known place, according to the Rashba's view in seif 6.
And nevertheless there is no [need to say it was nullified in the majority], etc. He derived this from Tosafot's view regarding two mixtures, and the same applies to the Rambam regarding three according to his view that the first mixture is considered definite — and this is what it means, "and we do not say nullified in the majority," etc. Nevertheless it is not necessarily so.
And specifically [regarding a Torah prohibition]. So decided Ohr HaChayim — that certainly one should rule like the Rambam, and in doubt like Tosafot and the Rashba; and that is in doubt of a Torah prohibition, even though it does not combine with the mixture, as written in seif 9 in the hagahah. But doubt of "their words" — even for the Rambam it is permitted in two mixtures, so that Tosafot's view applies in one mixture; and in seif 9 he ruled that in such a case even one mixture suffices — and this is Tosafot's and the Rashba's view.
There are those who say that a davar she'yesh lo matirin, etc. So too the Mordechai at the end of chapter 3 of Avodah Zarah, from that [passage] at the beginning of Yevamot, "and a doubt," etc., and according to R' Yitzchak's explanation as noted, and Rav Ashi concludes because of davar she'yesh lo matirin, etc., and even for a rabbinic prohibition — and in Orach Chaim siman 367, seif 2 in the hagahah. And so too the Rosh at the beginning of klal 2 in the name of the Rambam regarding chadash, and he challenged him — for there the reason is because of one doubt in the mixture, as noted, and siman 393, seif 3 in the hagahah, and even for a Torah prohibition — and in Avodat HaKodesh siman 397, seif 4. And this is according to the Rashba's view, and the Shulchan Aruch in seif 9; therefore the Rashba and the Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 7 regarding davar she'yesh lo matirin, etc. But according to the poskim and the hagahah in seif 9, that in a case of doubt it combines — therefore one must distinguish regarding davar she'yesh lo matirin, and this is what he writes here according to his view; and he also disagrees in seif 7, and Shach s.k. 57.
Unless it is for a need, etc. And this is that he ruled like the Rosh in siman 393.
