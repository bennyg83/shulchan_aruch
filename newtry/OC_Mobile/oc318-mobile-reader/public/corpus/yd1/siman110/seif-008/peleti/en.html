"To permit double safek." — Shach challenged: for below siman 100 regarding a ring of avodah zarah, etc. Rambam permits even with two safeks, and here he requires three safeks. And Shach answered in the name of Maharam Gaon Ashkenazi: the Gemara's difficulty is that Rav holds regarding a ring of avodah zarah double safek alone is permitted, and we say he holds like R' Shimon — but behold R' Shimon requires specifically three safeks according to Rambam, and he explained it in the Gemara. Therefore Rambam holds to distinguish between a matter that has no connection to eating, only benefit, such as a ring and the like — they permitted with two safeks; but a matter that has eating, such as pomegranates in the granary — we require three safeks; therefore in this siman that deals with eating we require three safeks, unlike below siman 100 where eating does not apply and two mixtures suffice — end of his words. And there is much to question in this resolution — for behold in Laws of Yom Tov, an egg that became mixed on Yom Tov that is davar sheyesh lo matirin that became mixed in a thousand — it is forbidden; and if its safek became mixed it is permitted — see there; nevertheless his version is: if it became mixed in a thousand it is forbidden certainly and not its safek — see there; and behold there are only two safeks and even the first safek is from itself — nevertheless Shulchan Aruch permits; why does he forbid here when it is a matter of eating? And further — from where do we know for Shmuel to say regarding a ring double safek does not help from R' Yehuda who forbids regarding pomegranates in the granary in safek lest — for there it is forbidden eating, and even for R' Shimon we require at least three safeks; here R' Yochanan is stringent that double safek does not help, but in prohibitions of benefit like a ring that even three safeks we do not require for R' Shimon — from the outset there becomes a distant dispute, and let us say according to R' Yochanan double safek even in a thousand does not help at all — if so from where do we know for Shmuel to forbid all double safek even regarding a ring? And further it is difficult — this that it answers: and this is impossible, it is R' Eliezer — and they explained two explanations; and it is difficult for Rambam from all these that there is no need like the first explanation that therefore it teaches three and not two in order that two remain, because regarding what remains it requires two — for behold Rambam does not hold so; and he explained two by two regarding falling that is recognizable, but in a mixture it can be that one remained. And if like the second explanation of R' Eliezer that a loaf is — that two mixtures do not suffice — behold there it is a matter of eating; and if we say that there in a loaf there is only wood value and mere benefit it is not called as Tosafot wrote, or one can say it deals with a thick garment, etc. — behold according to Rambam's view that there is a distinction between two safeks and three safeks — there is no proof for Shmuel at all from R' Eliezer that double safek forever forbidden — for we do not find for R' Eliezer except in two safeks; and still one can say in three safeks R' Eliezer concedes and is stringent because of the stringency of avodah zarah — in two safeks even regarding a matter of benefit alone forbidden like other eating issurim. And in truth — for in my Chiddushim by way of pilpul I said to resolve this that he says like R' Eliezer according to Rambam's view — and it is that the mishnah teaches from many to three and from three to two; and the Gemara investigates: why does it teach to three and not also to two; only one can say regarding falling from three to two — those two with the one that fell into them are permitted to eat them in rabbinical court; if so it entered his mind that also the first three would be permitted — for this is what he explained into two and it was eaten — it is like falling into the sea, and better than those eaten b'shogeg that all were permitted — all the more so this that was eaten with permission; and regarding "they die" in the mishnah that the Gemara compares to falling to the sea — and Rashi wrote: even though they kill it by hand — Maharshak wrote: since it was done by command and permission of rabbinical court it is for him like falling automatically; if so also this is like it, for behold it was permitted to eat and it is like one of them ate b'shogeg; if so also these are permitted — and it teaches us in the baraita that nevertheless they are forbidden and only the last were permitted; and it teaches much that it is not called and is not comparable to one of them ate, etc. — for we permit to eat it that we rely on permission; and how can we still say issur of separation — and it teaches much; and this is now that we say one fell to three —if so in a mixture of four rings and R' Eliezer holds two by two — meaning that falling must be two according to Rambam's explanation; if so these the mishnah teaches fell to two and in total three — if so that in truth regarding one of them ate it is like falling to the sea and all are permitted; but here it is impossible, for there must be two to fall — if so in the second mixture there is remainder only one — how will it be permitted, for there is no double safek as I wrote: how can we say safek issur fell or not, and if you say the issur did not fall — perhaps this is not issur, for there is only one; if so if so regarding falling to three in truth all are permitted — therefore it teaches to three; and nevertheless not all are permitted — end of his words; the Gemara answered according to Rambam's view. However with all this Tosafot's difficulty stands in its place; therefore it appears Tosafot there daf 72 s.v. "were mixed" wrote Rabbeinu Tam's view that which R' Yochanan holds double safek does not help — that is specifically regarding an important matter like pomegranates in the granary; but in other matters that are not nullified even for R' Yochanan double safek helps; therefore R' Yochanan mentioned pomegranates in the granary; and they challenged — behold in the baraita it teaches for R' Yehuda pomegranates in the granary — why were they taught, etc.; it should have amended that therefore they were taught because double safek does not help in it — see there. And with this one can resolve Tosafot's difficulty that they challenged Rashi who said like R' Eliezer regarding the loaf he baked — behold also his colleagues the Sages hold so — for at first glance there is room to challenge: perhaps because R' Eliezer holds double safek does not help — that is regarding an important matter as entered his mind for Rabbeinu Tam regarding R' Yochanan — but regarding a ring where there is not so much an important matter R' Eliezer concedes and his supporters that double safek helps; and Shmuel who says regarding a ring has no support — however according to what we say regarding six matters that sanctify and R' Akiva adds even homeowner's loaves — and these too R' Eliezer holds — at least he should have said R' Eliezer and R' Akiva adds — for R' Eliezer is earlier and teacher of R' Yochanan — and perforce one must say he does not hold; if so end of his words that d'rabbanan R' Eliezer there is no proof that perhaps the Sages are R' Akiva — if so the loaf is an important matter and he challenged well as I wrote: from where for Shmuel regarding a ring; therefore he says like R' Eliezer who does not hold homeowner's loaves are important except because of stringency of avodah zarah — end of his words; investigate. Also this matter of the egg is resolved — for an egg is not nullified not because of importance but because of davar sheyesh lo matirin; therefore Rambam does not concede regarding it that two safeks alone help. And with this one can resolve what I challenged at the beginning of the siman to Rambam who holds chametz is davar sheyesh lo matirin — what did the Gemara challenge: let it teach homeowner's loaves regarding chametz on Pesach — behold even baker's loaves that are not important — nevertheless are not nullified — nevertheless the Gemara challenged to consider homeowner's loaves is called important matter and there is practical difference regarding mixture whether we require three — investigate. However — both in my answer and in Shach's answer that the Gemara in Zevachim needs Rav's matter who said regarding a ring R' Elazar's matter who said regarding a barrel of terumah — it is difficult: behold Tosafot wrote that therefore they were permitted when one fell to the sea that it is double safek — therefore Tosafot forbade to eat all of them; and accordingly these Rav's matter alone that is regarding a ring and suffices for Shach's view with two safeks even regarding falling permitted that is also two safeks; but regarding barrels of terumah that is eating and double safek does not help — if so regarding falling too double safek does not help — it teaches us R' Elazar that nevertheless regarding falling they were lenient even with double safek alone; and so too it is difficult to what he also wrote — if so regarding a ring that it is not important matter and helps with two safeks even regarding falling helps — but barrels that deals with sealed barrels and it is important matter — if so they do not permit regarding falling — for double safek alone does not suffice. However this can be rejected — since R' Elazar said barrels stam — for R' Elazar we still do not know it deals with sealed barrels that is important matter; and that it is not nullified because it is "everything that is customarily counted" we learned (and nevertheless it is not in the category of important that would require three safeks, and so it appears in Tosafot specifically six matters are important and in them double safek does not help); if so the need is well established; but on Shach's answer it is difficult — investigate.
"Forbidden for one person." — Shach and Pri Chadash wondered regarding the third mixture: why may one person not eat it; and it appears according to what I wrote it fits that Rama holds like Rambam who requires three safeks — it is because of the importance of the mixture as I wrote; and if you do not say that one eats as one person — if so there are not at all only two safeks: perhaps this that separated is from the majority of heter, and likewise this that separated to three is heter; but this safek — perhaps this that he ate is from two of heter — there is not, for he eats all as one; therefore one must not eat as one and there will be three safeks. And in my Chiddushim I said: why does it require three safeks from a multitude to three, etc.; and one can say that if so the first safek is falling from a multitude, or safek lest the ring of issur did not fall; and the second safek — perhaps this that he ate — perhaps it is not what fell from the multitude; and the intent is — perhaps it is not of issur; and accordingly behold also in the category of "one name of compulsion" — the first safek if it fell from the majority the ring of issur; and the second safek too if this is the ring of issur — that is the first safek whether this is a ring of issur or heter — it is every safek whether it is the ring of issur; and one can say: it is true that all safeks are all whether it is a ring of issur; but the safeks are not comparable, for the safek in falling to the third — if this ring is what fell — if so it is forbidden to eat it as one, for if so the second safek goes away; but in falling to two — behold he can eat it as one; and nevertheless two safeks remain; and since they are not comparable it is not called "one name of compulsion."
"Davar sheyesh lo matirin, etc." — Shach challenged: if so when one of them fell to the sea — the reason as Tosafot wrote is from the category of double safek — perhaps issur fell, and if you say heter — perhaps this is not issur; behold in davar sheyesh lo matirin falling does not help, for double safek does not help; and above the Mechaber wrote davar sheyesh lo matirin when one fell to the sea from mixtures that all are permitted — and Rama did not emend. In truth it appears clear we lock in regarding falling what Tosafot wrote from the category of double safek — is not the primary reason, only Tosafot placed support and reason. And in particular for Rambam who requires three safeks in mixtures — and behold regarding falling there are only two safeks — all the more so it is strained to say for R' Yehuda who holds double safek does not help — if so Rav's master would disagree regarding one falling to the sea; and in one version in Rashi as I wrote — he says like R' Eliezer who R' Eliezer holds regarding a loaf double safek does not help — and the same applies in other issurim — see there in Tosafot; and it is difficult how it is possible double safek does not help — if so Rav's law would disagree regarding one falling to the sea; and in one version in Rashi as I wrote — he says like R' Eliezer who R' Eliezer holds regarding a loaf double safek does not help — and the same applies in other issurim — see there in Tosafot; and it is difficult how it is possible — behold R' Eliezer holds: if the head of one was offered, all are offered — and that is Rav's law of falling; and behold R' Eliezer does not need double safek — only clearly the primary heter we derive from a verse "blemish in them," etc. — and this is Tosafot's reason only as a mere branch; therefore the Gemara challenged: from where for R' Eliezer — and it answered he derives it from "blemish in them"; and what is the difficulty and why need a verse — behold double safek is a general principle in the entire Torah; or that the Gemara holds it is not a complete double safek — therefore it needed to derive from a verse — and double safek is a mere branch; if so even though double safek does not help, falling is permitted — for we have the verse "blemish in them."
