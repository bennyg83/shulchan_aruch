"Meat that is found, etc." — See in responsa Beit Efrayim volume 4 siman 40 regarding whether in one store of nevelah there is more meat than in nine stores of kosher — whether one follows what is found after the majority of stores or after the majority of meat; and he wrote that there is room to be lenient and to be stringent: whatever was not sold from each store except one or two animals and all the rest of the meat remained in its place — we follow the majority of stores — for what will this meat help us that is much in the store, since it remained placed in its place; but if in one there are ten nevelah animals and in nine each has one kosher and they all sold all the meat — it is obvious that in such a case we follow the majority of meat sold today — for what will the majority of stores help us since one seller sold more than all the other sellers together; and he expanded on this; and see also in Chokhmat Shlomo s.k. 23; and already Beit Efrayim challenged him in his responsa there — see there; and see in responsa Knesset Yechezkel siman 24 in s.v. "and I saw in Ran" that wrote plainly that since we do not distinguish that sometimes in one store there is much meat that is the majority of pieces against nine that sell slaughtered meat — it appears there is no practical difference in this and each store and store is considered as if all are equal — see there; also in responsa Noda B'Yehuda second edition part 1 siman 71 he investigated this too and wrote that from the language of the Gemara it appears everything depends on the majority of stores and not on the amount of meat — and nevertheless he does not insist on this matter to be lenient, but to be stringent certainly there is reason to be concerned — see there.
