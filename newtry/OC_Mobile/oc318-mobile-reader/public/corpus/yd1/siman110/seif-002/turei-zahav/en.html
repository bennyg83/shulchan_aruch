Specifically small animals. This distinction was compelled from what Tur wrote in siman 101, that a whole sheep is not nullified — and these "small" here must mean small birds, not a chicken, for above he wrote that a chicken is not nullified. Maharshal chapter 35 siman 11 resolved this: above deals with a lamb that was flayed and is still whole; but here deals with one that was not flayed — automatically it lacks great preparation, and is similar to what he ruled above regarding a chicken in its feathers, that it is nullified — and all the more so here, that a lamb in its wool lacks great preparation even more. To me it appears that the importance of a living animal is importance in itself — that is, if it is a weak thing not fit for eating, automatically it has no importance from the side of a piece worthy of honor when slaughtered; only while it is alive does it have importance because it is a living creature. On this Tur said that here, where it is important as a living creature, its importance is nullified when slaughtered; but if it has importance as piece worthy of honor — that they already wrote in siman 101.
