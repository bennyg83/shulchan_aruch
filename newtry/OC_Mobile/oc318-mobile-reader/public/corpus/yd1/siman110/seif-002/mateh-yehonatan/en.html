(There, seif 1) But other things, even though their way is to count them, etc. These are the words of the Rambam; and he challenges him from that which is in the chapter HaAreil, daf 82: one who had bundles of fenugreek of kilayim of the vineyard — they must be burned; if they became mixed with others and others with others, all must be burned — R' Meir; and the Sages say one in two hundred suffices; for R' Meir used to say: anything whose way is to be counted we learned — if so, since R' Yehuda and R' Levi disagree regarding davar sheb'minyan, whether "you count its way" or "anything whose way is to be counted" we learned — infer that davar sheb'minyan is not nullified in sixty; if so, how did the Rambam and Mechaber explain against R' Yehuda and R' Levi? And one may say (this is the dispute): behold, that which the Rambam wrote — any item that is important to people in some places, such as peeled nuts and Badan pomegranates in Eretz Yisrael, etc. — if so, one may say that R' Levi and R' Yehuda dispute according to the view of the rabbis, who hold that an important item is not nullified in sixty; if so, the rabbis hold that davar sheb'minyan is nullified — and that is since counting does not depend on their importance at all, but only because of the purchase, that counting informs the sale; but if it is something sold by estimate and within that species there is one type sold by counting — for example peeled nuts, since other nuts are sold by estimate and peeled nuts by counting — if so, certainly because they are more important than other nuts they are sold by counting, and even for the rabbis it is not nullified — not because of davar sheb'minyan but because it is an important item. Accordingly, one may say that R' Yehuda and R' Levi dispute according to the rabbis: R' Yehuda holds specifically that if it is "you count its way" it is an important item; but if that species — namely peeled nuts — is sometimes sold by estimate, it is not an important item; and R' Levi holds that even in such a case too it is an important item. It remains difficult against the Mechaber and Rambam — investigate.
