"Live animals, etc." — See responsa Radbaz HaChadashot siman 573 that wrote that even though we do not maintain like R' Tam who permitted even certainly gored that became mixed — by force of "let us suppress them since they move," etc., and whatever separates from the majority — and we do not decree lest he take from the fixed case — that is specifically regarding certainly gored; but regarding safek gored — such as we do not know whether it gored by hand or by tooth and it became mixed with others — one should rule like his words and permit by force of "let us suppress them since they move," etc., and whatever separates from the majority; and the last two are forbidden — and we do not decree here lest he take from the fixed case — for even if he takes it is double safek: perhaps this is not it and perhaps it is not gored; and even though R' Yochanan wrote (this is what the Mechaber wrote seif 9) that in such a case it is not double safek — that is regarding taking from the mixture; but to decree even where it separated from the majority because lest he come to take from the fixed case — this is a great stringency and the Torah showed concern, etc. — and on this one may rely for practical halachah. And he wrote further that even according to those who are stringent there is a remedy for the matter, etc. (this matter Beit HaTosafot already wrote in his name above end of siman 57) — see there.
