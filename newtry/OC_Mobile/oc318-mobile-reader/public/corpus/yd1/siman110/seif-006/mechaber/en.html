Animals and other important things that became mixed in heter that are not nullified even in a thousand — even if one of the majority separated after the mixture became known — forbidden; and that is when he separated them intentionally; but if it separated by itself — permitted: {Rama: And that which it is forbidden if he separated them intentionally — that is when he separated only a little and some of the issurim remained in their place, for then we fear lest he also take from the fixed quantity; but if all of them separated together and none remained in their place, and at the time of their removal some of those that separated — then those that separated are permitted (words received from his father-in-law the Gaon Mahar"R Shachna z"l); only the last two are forbidden (Tur end siman 57); and if a treifah chicken became mixed with kosher ones and an egg was found among them — the egg is permitted, even though chickens are important and are not nullified regarding an egg — we follow the majority (Arukh general rule 26).}
