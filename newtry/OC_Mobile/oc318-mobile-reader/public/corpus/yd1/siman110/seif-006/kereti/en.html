"They separated" by itself — meaning not before us; but if before us, some say it is a fixed case d'rabbanan and some say it is d'oraisa. See Peleti.
And that which is forbidden, etc. — the Pri Chadash and Maharshal sought to resolve: therefore the Mechaber ruled above in siman 57 regarding oto v'es beno that became mixed — "we suppress them" — since it is a davar she'yesh lo matirin; tomorrow it will be permitted, so we are not concerned lest one take from the fixed quantity. The egg is permitted, for it is a case of separation and the majority separates; and we do not decree lest one take from the fixed quantity, for from an egg to a chicken we do not decree — Shach. And according to us, who hold that the explanation is "by itself," we also do not decree lest one take, etc. In any case there is no difficulty, for here too the explanation is "by itself.
It appears permitted even if the egg is also an important item; nevertheless it is permitted, for the majority separates and the majority are kosher. And accordingly one must not witness the laying of eggs — for then it is considered as if he saw and explicitly separated, which is forbidden, for he is one who takes from a fixed quantity. But where an egg is not important, if so it is nullified by itself even if it separated before us, for there is no mixture here.
