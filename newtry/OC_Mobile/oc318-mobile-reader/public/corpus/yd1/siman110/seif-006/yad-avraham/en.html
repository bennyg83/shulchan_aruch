(Laws of double safek in Shach letter 2) He wrote in the name of Rashba's responsum that regarding a rabbinic issur where something has a permitted aspect, we follow stringency when there is presumption of issur. Eben Ha'ozer chapter 2 of Shabbos disagrees, for we do not establish like R. Yehudah in safek tevel, as Rambam wrote — the anonymous mishnayos are not like him. So too Maggid Mishneh chapter 4 of Bechoros, and Ran in Sukkah (46b); see Avnei Yashpe and Chochmat Adam. However, the anonymous ruling in Taharos refers to safek tumah where there was no presumption of issur, not to tevel, as Tosefta wrote. Rather our author's reason is that we do not establish R. Yehudah against his colleagues, as in Magen Yeshuah. What he wrote in Beit Avraham — that this depends on two resolutions Tosafos wrote in Pesachim (9) — wondered why the later authorities did not mention them; they already preceded this in Shaarei Teshuvah on mikvaos klal 7 and in Tzitz Eliezer on Berachos; and his words there are impossible according to Rambam, as in Magen Avraham. See Seder Taharah. What Avnei Yashpe wrote as proof for issur from the fact that we are stringent in safek muchan — there it is something that has a permitted aspect. What Beit Avraham wrote as proof from Eruvin (32) — I omitted Maggid Mishneh's words in Laws of Bechoros; see there. What Chochmat Adam wrote as a question to forbid from safek impure hands d'rabbanan — Peri Megadim likewise, but it is not proof, for there even though impure issur was not established, the reason is they made it like Torah law to render a person impure, to burn terumah and kodshim upon him they also made it like Torah law to render safek impure. Rather requires study what Taz wrote siman 201 s.k. 86, explaining there the Mechaber's words that he forbids like R. Yose. That is also Rambam's language in Laws of Mikvaos. What Kereti Ufleiti wrote to bring proof to permit from Menachos (68b) regarding sixty days of waving — presumption of issur was pushed aside in Avnei Yashpe and Chochmat Adam, for there it is preferable to other safek because we are expert in the fixed month. There is a question on their words from what Rambam wrote chapter 10 of Laws of Forbidden Foods that sixty is forbidden from double safek — and if this were like all sixty it would be forbidden from the Torah since presumption of issur was established in sixty, a Torah issur. Regarding double safek from one place — he wrote in responsa Tur HaEven siman 41 that this is like there regarding ones regarding seduction of a minor — automatically it is ones; and if it were not ones presumably she would not be a minor; but if in one safek there is a dispute and in one safek all permit, there is not one place that is like one permitting more than his colleague. Levushei Serad wrote that the view of poskim not brought in Shulchan Aruch are not combined even for double safek — and similarly I saw Or HaChaim wrote in laws of seizure that one cannot say "I am certain like the view not brought in Shulchan Aruch." Mari argued on this — can the views of great poskim be nullified based on Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe's ruling? The Gaon already wrote thus in Noda B'Yehuda EH siman 47.
