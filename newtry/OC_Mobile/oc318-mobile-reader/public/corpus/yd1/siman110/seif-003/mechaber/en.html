Nine stores sell slaughtered meat and one sells nevelah meat, and one took from one of them and it is not known from which of them he took — behold this is forbidden, for every fixed case is considered half-and-half; but meat found in the market or in the hand of a gentile — permitted, since most stores sell slaughtered meat, for "whatever separates from the majority separates" — this is Torah law; but the Sages forbade it even though all the slaughterers and all the sellers are Jewish: {Rama: And see above siman 63; and that which we say "whatever separates from the majority separates" — that is when it did not separate before us; but if it separated before us, or one sees when the gentile took it — it is as if he took it from there in his hand (Tur in name of Rashba and Tosafot chapter 3 daf 95 and Ran there and Hagahot Orach Chaim in name of R"I and Tosafot and Agudah chapter HaTaarovot and Rosh in responsum general rule 20 siman 15 and Semag end daf 52 and Semak siman 214 and ShaDa siman 46 and R' Yerucham net 15 and Maggid Mishneh chapter 5 of Forbidden Foods in name of the commentators).}
