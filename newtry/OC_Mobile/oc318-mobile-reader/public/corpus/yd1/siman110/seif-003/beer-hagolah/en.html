The Rambam in chapter 15 of Hilchot Maachalot Assurot, and not like Rabbeinu Meir there in the mishnah — although R' Yochanan and R' Levi disagree in the explanation of R' Meir in Zevachim daf 72, Beitzah daf 3, Yevamot daf 80 — it is not necessary to say that he holds the halakha is according to him; and if so, an egg is also nullified even though its way is to count it. And so too the Rashba in Torat HaBayit, and in the name of the Ramban; and it is written in Beit Chadash that the view of most poskim is like the Ramban; so too in Kesef Mishneh there, and in Beit Yosef in this siman. And they wondered about the Mechaber above in Shulchan Aruch in siman 86 seif 3, who ruled that an egg even in a thousand is not nullified; however, it appears to us in our humble opinion that this latter is primary according to the Mechaber's view — for in siman 86 in Beit Yosef he did not descend to explain the view of the disputants on this, only wrote in siman 110 with a hint that it will be explained that there are disputants against him, end of his words; and likewise he did in this siman — he descended to count the view of the disputants. And it seems to us to reconcile his words: that which he wrote above that an egg is not nullified is not because its way is to count it, but rather because the Rambam holds so regarding an egg with a chick forming because of the living creature, and even in a thousand it is not nullified; and the Raavad likewise regarding any egg because of the living creature — therefore the Mechaber divided there between an egg with a chick forming and other eggs.
