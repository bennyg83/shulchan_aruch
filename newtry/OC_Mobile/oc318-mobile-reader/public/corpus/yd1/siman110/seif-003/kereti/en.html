"And one sells." — it appears it deals with one who sells treifot openly and everyone knows that this store sells treifot; for if not so, how is it possible that he does not know from whom he bought? Behold, the seller did not sell to him stam — are we dealing with wicked people? And since he told him that it is treifah, how is it possible that he does not know from whom he bought? And it goes without saying that it deals with a well-known store; and if so, one need not tell him that it is treifah, for it is well-known in this regard, and he forgot afterward whether he bought from that store at all; and see what I wrote, Peleti, that it is possible it deals with that the store is a non-Jew, or that at the time he bought he did not know that there are treifot in the store.
"In the hand of a non-Jew." — but if found in the hand of an expert minor that one may rely on in his treifah — it is like found in the hand of a Jew, and treifah; Pri Chadash.
"Permitted." — see what I wrote, Peleti; and the essence is that that store selling treifah meat — behold, a non-Jew.
"But the Sages." — forbade it. See above siman 63 at length; also I clarified there that even if found near a treifah store, nevertheless we follow the majority; and see there whether majority of the world also helps or only majority of that city and its vicinity — see there what he wrote in name of Rashba.
"But if it separated." — according to Ran, in "we saw it separated before us" — nevertheless it is only kavu'a d'rabbanan; and Baal HaTerumah and Rashba hold that since a safek arose in a place of fixation, it is considered kavu'a d'oraisa.
