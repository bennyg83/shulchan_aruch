Be'er Heitev; and see below s.k. 7; and see in Shevut Yaakov part 3 siman 64: a goose found like an extra intestine in the dekin should be forbidden unless the fat entered and covers the extra, even though it comes from the stuffing — kosher; and see there siman 82: when it emerges from the dekin like a branch from beit habad — permit per Shulchan Aruch's ruling — see there. And Shemesh Tzedakah siman 40 wrote: one who opened a goose and removed the intestines forcibly because it was emerging from the dekin like a swollen extra, thick, closed above, and stuck very much to the goose's wall; and also the mouth of the extra like intestines, closed, and it was not known clearly that it had a passage to the rest of the dekin; and he opened the extra and found inside a thin, sharp bone like a thorn and needle.
