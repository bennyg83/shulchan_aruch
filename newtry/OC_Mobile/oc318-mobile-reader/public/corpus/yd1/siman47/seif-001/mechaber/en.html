The law if extra intestinal ducts are found. In it are 5 seifim. An animal in which two intestines are found is treifah — such as when it has an extra intestine from its beginning to its end, or two intestines are found side by side from beginning to end in the intestines of a fowl; or when the intestine emerged like a branch from the trunk and behold it is separated — whether in fowl or in an animal. But if it returned and became mixed with the intestine and became one from two heads, and behold both are separated in the middle — behold this is permitted and there is no extra here. Some say: when a small intestine emerges from the dekin like a branch emerging from the trunk — if at the head of the kivah, the place from which the dekin emerge, there is a complete finger-width before this intestine begins to separate, and also below, after it connects with the rest of the dekin, there is a complete finger-width — kosher. And if not, that there is not a complete finger-width above and below — treifah. (And such is the essential ruling) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba and Ran).
