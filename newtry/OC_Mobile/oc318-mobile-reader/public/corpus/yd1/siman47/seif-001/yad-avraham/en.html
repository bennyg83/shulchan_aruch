(Siman 47 seif 1) An animal in which two intestines were found, etc. If it returned and became mixed with the intestine and became one from two heads — permitted, and there is no extra here; and it is simple that if that extra intestine was perforated, this is perforation of the dekin — meaning those main intestinal ducts were perforated, for behold they are open to the extra intestine; and since it is perforated, behold they are perforated. And as the hagahah wrote in this s.k. And this is similar to a hadra or other extra organ — if perforated, treifah; and as Rambam wrote chapter 8 of Hilchot Shechitah that a hadra that was perforated is treifah. And so is proven explicitly in Rashi and Tosafot and Rosh and Rashba and Ran and Tur siman 39 seif 6, who wrote that hadra — wherever it is attached — treifah. And it is difficult: what of it — for according to their view a missing hadra is kosher. And we hold siman 50: everything that if perforated is treifah, likewise if missing is treifah; and if kosher when missing, also when perforated it should be permitted. And so is stated in Darkei Moshe siman 45. And further: since it is an extra, why is it treifah. Behold Shulchan Aruch ruled siman 44 that if the windpipe was diminished, the extra is kosher; and so Be'er Heitev there and siman 43 regarding the spleen. And it must be said: regarding lung and intestines it is different, for since the breath of the lung enters the hadra — if it is perforated, behold the lung is perforated; and so with the intestines. This is clear. And again I found so is proven explicitly in Mordechai Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 276 — see there. And if so, perforce the hagahah in siman 35 does not permit except regarding reversal — if the small extra was reversed; but if perforated, one may not permit there for the reason above. However, regarding other organs one may permit treifot of extras, as Tzitz Eliezer wrote regarding the spleen — see above siman 43.
