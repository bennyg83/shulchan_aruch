(1) [Seif 1] An animal in which two intestines were found, such as when it has an extra intestine from beginning to end, etc. Behold in this law interpretations multiplied; and one must be concerned for all of them; therefore to permit an extra in the dekin three conditions are required. One: before they begin to split, the intestine should be finger-width. Two: the length of the emerging branch should not be more than a finger. Three: it should return and become mixed below near the refuse house for a finger-width; and if one of these three conditions is missing — treifah. Peri Chadash ot 3; for great loss ot 4; Beit Levi ot 4; Sha'arei De'ah end ot 1 — and this is with minor loss; but with great loss one may be lenient if half of the two is forbidden — namely even if the emerging branch has more than a finger and even several fingers, all that there is above finger-width and below finger-width connected together one may permit; and this is the first opinion that Shulchan Aruch wrote. Beit Levi ot 1; Turei Beit Shmuel ot 3; that there is great loss ot 4; Machatzit HaShekel ot 2; Zivchei Tzedek ot 1; Turei Zahav siman 47 ot 1; Zivchei Berit ot 1. And what Sha'arei De'ah wrote there in the name of Turei Beit Shmuel that with great loss the length of the split must be finger-width — the Ani Tzaddik there wrote this is not so, see there. And so is Machatzit HaShekel's and Turei Zahav's view there, not like Sha'arei De'ah. And although some acharonim brought Sha'arei De'ah's words because they did not see the poskim's words above, and further since such is Shulchan Aruch's view and our Rabbi's z"l in the hagahah — it is fit to rule thus with great loss, per the poskim above.
