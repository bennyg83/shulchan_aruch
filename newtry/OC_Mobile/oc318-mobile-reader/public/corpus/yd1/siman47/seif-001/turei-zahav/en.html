Like the intestines of fowl. In the Gemara it is stated that in an animal it is treifah, and corresponding to them in fowl — kosher; Rashi explained: in fowl sometimes it is so, and even though we do not find them so, they established it; and some explain: every fowl whose way is thus, because from the zefek until the kurkavan there is one intestinal tract, and that they called "intestinal children," and at the head of the kurkavan complete intestinal children emerge — end of his words. It turns out that according to the first explanation, all extra dekin in fowl are kosher; and according to the second explanation, all that is not the usual way in fowl is treifah. And this view that Shulchan Aruch brings is Rambam's view; and there is a stringency in fowl according to Rashi's second explanation, for he wrote "whether in fowl whether in animal"; and according to this, what the Gemara said "and corresponding to it in fowl — kosher" means with two complete intestines, not an intestine like a branch from beit habad. And also in an animal there is a stringency here, that he forbids if the extra is like a branch emerging from beit habad; and so is Rif's view, as Beit Yosef wrote, and not like Ramban whom Mordechai brings, that they forbade only when two intestines emerge from two places, not the thin one emerging from the dekin. And explicitly so Mordechai — that Rif disagrees with Ra'avan. I wrote this because there is a difficulty here on Tur and Shulchan Aruch in the law of hadra dekaneta in seif 5, as will be written there.
