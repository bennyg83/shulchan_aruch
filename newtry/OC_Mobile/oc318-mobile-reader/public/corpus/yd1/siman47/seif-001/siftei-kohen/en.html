Like a branch from the trunk. Specifically when they emerge from the kivah or from the kurkavan, and it is recognizable that it is an extra intestine similar to two intestines emerging; but if it emerges from the dekin — kosher, for since from the kivah only one intestine emerges, therefore the thin one emerging from the dekin is not considered extra, as the Shach wrote in s.k. 7:
Whether in fowl, etc. And even though in seif 2 he permits in fowl if several branches emerged from it — there it deals with those that return and become mixed with the dekin, unlike here where it is separated; and so is implied in Taz in seif 2 that he deals with those that return and become mixed:
There is a complete finger-width — kosher. And the Rokeach wrote: the same applies to two beit hakosot or two kivot emerging in one place and afterward becoming mixed — finger-width — kosher:
And such is the essential ruling. In Shulchan Aruch Ran is copied here, and in truth so it is in Darkei Moshe; but it requires further study, for on the contrary Ran's view is like Rambam whom the Mechaber copied — that even if they did not become mixed but only somewhat above and likewise below it is kosher; and he wrote that so is Rif's view and so is brought in Beit Yosef, and he wrote that his words are very correct — and it requires further study:
