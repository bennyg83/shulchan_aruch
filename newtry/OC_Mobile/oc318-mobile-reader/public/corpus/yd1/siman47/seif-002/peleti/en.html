In what matter is this stated in an animal — I already wrote that per what Rama wrote, and so is essential, that this law too is essential to distinguish between fowl and animal; and Bach wanted to be stringent if they are separated from the beginning — treifah. And I do not know whence the wise man found reconciliation of the matter: if like Rashi's second explanation everywhere treifah — what difference fowl and animal; and if like Rashi's first explanation, behold on this "and corresponding to them in fowl kosher" that the tanna teaches "in what matter when they emerge in two places," etc. — behold corresponding to them in fowl he teaches on two places; and it is simple.
