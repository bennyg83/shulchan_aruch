In what matter is this stated in an animal, etc. This is the conclusion of the first opinion that Shulchan Aruch brings in the preceding seif; and it is Tur's view, who holds that in fowl every extra in the dekin is kosher — and this is Rashi's first explanation that I mentioned nearby; and he disagrees with the first view, which is Rambam's view. And it is implied from Rama's words that he rules thus leniently in fowl; and so Maharshal — in fowl kosher in every manner.
