The hadra, etc. That Ra'avan explains "in what matter is this stated" that emerge, etc., that two intestines emerge from the large intestine and two more emerge — and we say the extra is considered as removed, and it is a perforation of the dekin, the place of their removal. And proof for this explanation: that it says "and corresponding to them in fowl," and so too in fowl in truth; and from "two intestinal children" it implies that one emerging from the intestines is not extra, for such is its way.
And all this, etc., "but," etc. Therefore two beit hakosot — treifah, perforce.
