Therefore there is a difficulty on Tur and Shulchan Aruch who wrote to rule this, since they themselves ruled not like him in seif 1 — that even like a branch emerging from beit habad is treifah; and Maharshal already challenged thus, and said that on Rosh himself there is no difficulty, even though he too rules like Rif in his rulings that it is forbidden even in a thin one from the dekin — for it is possible that in his composition in his rulings he retracted from what he wrote in his responsa. But on Tur's view it is difficult, and so on Shulchan Aruch. And it appears to me to reconcile their words: not for naught did they compare the extra intestine to a branch emerging from beit habad, but to teach the reason for the prohibition — namely when the extra hangs and stands in the air of the animal like a branch from beit habad; even though refuse can enter there and return into the intestine, nevertheless it is with difficulty. Unlike that extra in the hadra dekaneta, where the dekin lie around the kaneta — namely the fat — and that extra also lies among them, and refuse goes from there to the extra with ease and returns to the intestine; therefore the extra does not disqualify there, for we consider it as one. And even though Rosh did not mention any of this in his responsa, nevertheless Tur and Shulchan Aruch ruled thus for halacha, and it is not satisfactory for them to say that Rosh in his rulings retracted from what he wrote in his responsa, as above in reconciling their words; but it was not done in practice, because we compare only in the hadra dekaneta treifah for extra; and so is Maharshal's conclusion.
