The hadra dekaneta that emerges from its middle, an extra one hanging on the dekin like a small intestine, the length of a finger — kosher. {Rama: And all this is when it is closed at its head and refuse does not emerge from it; but if it is open at its head it is treifah, for refuse falls into the body (thus is implied from Mordechai end of chapter Elu Treifot and so in Ra'avan siman 21 and in the hagahah at the end of Orach Chaim kelal 55 din 6, and it is simple); and further that it is a perforation of the dekin.}
