And behold all the poskim challenged Shulchan Aruch's words against one another, for in seif 1 he ruled that even like a branch emerging from beit habad is treifah; and Shach answered: in seif 2 he deals specifically when two intestines emerge as one; but here behold only one intestine emerges, only that afterward, far from the kivah, a small intestine emerges from that intestine — therefore (it is considered the thin one emerging from the dekin) and kosher. And he wrote further: nevertheless, where there is no great loss, one should forbid the thin one emerging from the dekin (and all this deals with an intestine that has refuse in it; but if it has no refuse, even though it is hollow it is not extra but a vein extending from the liver, and sometimes it gets tangled in the intestines and appears like an extra intestine — Peri Chadash).
