An intestine that emerges from beit hakosot (meaning: the end of the kereis is shaped like a hat and is called beit hakosot, and the meses is attached to it, and food enters from beit hakosot to the meses, and this is the esophagus; and because food is digested the meses is called from the term "dissolved, crushed") to the meses — kosher; and one that emerges from beit hakosot to the kereis — treifah.
