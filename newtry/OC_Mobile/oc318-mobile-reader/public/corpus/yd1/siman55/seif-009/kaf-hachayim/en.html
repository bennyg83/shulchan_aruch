(9) There. And likewise if cut within the joint etc. between the arkum and the shank — treifah according to this view. And he means: if cut between that small bone children play with and the shank — treifah per this view; but if cut in that small bone itself — Maharshal in Perek HaMekasheh permits, for its law is like the hock sold with the head; and so is Taz s.k. 1; however Shach s.k. 1 brings a dispute — some say its law is like above the hock and concludes to forbid; see there; and Beit David Kereti ot 2 wrote in great loss one may rely on those who permit; and so Mechaber ot 8 Anaf Yehonatan there ot 2; Machazik Berachah ot 2; Zivchei Tzedek ot 3.
