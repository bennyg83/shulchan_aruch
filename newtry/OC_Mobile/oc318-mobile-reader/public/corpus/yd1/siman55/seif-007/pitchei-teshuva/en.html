That it returned and rejoined. See in responsa Noda BiYehudah part 10 siman 20 on a bird whose kulyah bone broke near the body, returned and rejoined, and was salted with two other birds because they did not notice the break until after salting and rinsing; he wrote: since the leg broke and rejoined, the main concern is consumption of projections — it is a general stringency; in great loss Rama permits; and even not in great loss — at least per those who permit in siman 56 seif 10 when rejoined, here too permitted; and even if forbidden — the prohibition is from safek; therefore if salted with its kind and there is majority against it — one may permit what was salted with it in the shell even if fat; and if cooked afterward without shelling — b'dieved not forbidden; good to peel after cooking if easy; see there at length.
