(7) There. And there are those who forbid when the middle bone is cut anywhere etc. (hagahah). And the custom in all Ashkenaz and France is like the latter view etc.; and one should be stringent; however when there is another safek or very great loss — one may rely on the plain ruling of Shulchan Aruch. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 47 and so in his book Shaarei De'ah in Ein HaChayim siman 79 — only there he did not write "great loss," only "great loss" plainly. Shaarei De'ah ot 5; and so agreed the Mechaber end ot 4 Anaf Yehonatan siman 37 ot 1; Turei Zahav there ot 6; Zivchei Tzedek ot 2.
