At the end of the ankle between the ankle and the shin, etc. This matter is from Rosh's words in Perek HaMekasheh brought by Beit Yosef, and these are his words: Where the leg was cut in the ankle between the joints — I saw commentators who permitted; to me it appears to forbid, because one can challenge from precision of head to tail in the mishnah: in the head it teaches "from the ankle and below — kosher" — behold in the ankle itself treifah; in the tail it teaches "from the ankle and upward — treifah" — behold in the ankle itself kosher; one of them is specifically precise and the other not — we go stringently; so Rashi explained in Pesachim on baskets whose legs broke in the ankle or above the ankle and the bone does not protrude — evidently within the ankle and above the ankle they share one law. And from "in which ankle did they say" — in the ankle sold with the head — evidently the lower bone is called ankle; "below the ankle" mentioned means from the start of the ankle downward; "above the ankle" means from the end of the ankle upward; now no difficulty from precisions contradicting each other, end of his words. I saw two explanations on plain "ankle" mentioned here: First — Issur VeHeter wrote general rule 50: Above it is a small bone standing between joints connecting lower bone to middle bone — opposite place called kniya in foreign tongue; cut with hooves when flaying animals; Rashi ruled like Rav explaining mishnah ankle — that small bone; and wrote: likewise if that ankle itself is cut — treifah; so Rosh, etc., end of his words. Behold he explains the ankle Rosh discussed is the small bone children play with called teshi"ch; so Rosh responsum general rule 20 siman 16; therefore prohibition for Rosh even if cut in teshi"ch itself or even below it between where teshi"ch ends and start of bone sold with head — otherwise precisions still contradict. Again I saw second explanation in Hagahot Or Zarua end of book: Mahariash said ankle is gelayid between bones — in animal and bird; end of his words; therefore plain ankle does not apply to small bone called teshi"ch — he includes bird too and bird has no teshi"ch nor bone separating lower from middle — only place separating bones called in foreign tongue gelayid; he said if cut there between joints — treifah; therefore in teshi"ch itself and below it kosher; only on gelayid — place between teshi"ch and middle bone — he rules to forbid; teshi"ch belongs to lower bone and from it begins lower bone; even though nowadays that bone not sold with head — no proof perhaps in their days it was sold with head as Maharshal siman 11 wrote — I will write his words shortly; found prohibition Rosh "between joints" is literally between middle bone and teshi"ch; as I will write shortly in name of Shulchan De'ah; also from Tur words implies so — wrote above it immediately treifah — implies as soon as lower bone ends; if teshi"ch belongs above — "above it" means on teshi"ch and treifah there; why then say afterward "even within joint between it and shin treifah" — should say greater novelty even in teshi"ch treifah; if Tur meant call teshi"ch in language "joint" — should write greater novelty treifah if cut between teshi"ch and lower bone; perhaps among all he calls "within joint"; Mordechai implies calls ankle teshi"ch — even so permits opposite teshi"ch wrote small bone connecting them called lower kufitz; in mishnah Bechorot arukum; lower ankle law like below it — no concern opposite it for tendon severance, etc., end of his words; behold he adopts first explanation on ankle — nevertheless permits teshi"ch itself because no tendon severance concern; holds tail precision primary "from ankle up" specifically treifah; Shulchan De'ah siman 87 wrote: ankle itself — concern lest one tendon severed for there is tendon junction; appears explains ankle per second explanation above — if first explanation teshi"ch — no prohibition in teshi"ch itself. In my humble opinion understood Shulchan De'ah calls teshi"ch ankle and even so forbids from tendon junction though no tendon prohibition siman 57 — nevertheless forbids because we are not expert to distinguish well; to me appears as I wrote — Shulchan De'ah wrote explicitly there is tendon junction in its name; simply explains per second explanation I mentioned. Maharshal explained Rosh's words on teshi"ch itself stringently per first explanation — but disagreed from his own reasoning: not reasonable to declare treifah there in cut in ankle itself — even tendon junction in ankle if cut not treifah; why declare more in bone cut — its law like below ankle sold with head; even though nowadays not sold with head — no proof perhaps in their days sold with head; he elaborated and brought Mordechai above; end: cut or extra standing leg in ankle does not disqualify at all; so appears practical halachah to be lenient — for Rif and all his camp and most great ones explain like Ula — obvious kosher in that ankle itself, end of his words; also Beit Yosef in name Mahari ibn Chaviv from Rabbenu Yerucham: no prohibition in ankle itself only between it and shin — as written here in Shulchan Aruch; Darkei Moshe brings it.
