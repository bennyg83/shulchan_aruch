The laws of treifot in the leg and its joints. In it are 13 seifim. There are three joints in the leg. The lower one — that which is cut with the hooves when flaying the animal — is called the hock (arkeivah) sold with the head. Above it is a second joint called the shank, and the junction of the sinews at its bottom near the hock sold with the head. Above it is a third joint connected to the body, called the kulyah (hip joint). In the lower joint, wherever it is cut — kosher. The bone connected to the body called kulyah — wherever it is cut — treifah. In the middle bone, if it was cut not at the place of the junction of sinews — kosher; and if it was cut at the place of the junction of sinews — treifah, and it is not forbidden because the leg was cut at this place, but because the sinews were cut; therefore if the bone broke at the place of the junction of sinews and protruded outward and skin and flesh do not cover most of it, and one examined at the junction of sinews and they were found intact — kosher. And there are those who forbid when the middle bone is cut anywhere, even not at the place of junction of sinews; and according to them, if the bone broke at the place of junction of sinews and protruded outward and skin and flesh do not cover most of it — treifah, even if one examined at the junction of sinews and found them intact. And likewise if it was cut within the joint between the hock sold with the head and the shank — at the end of the arkum — whether between the arkum and the shank — treifah according to this view (Beit Yosef in name of Mahari ibn Chaviv). And if it was cut within the joint at the place where the kulyah bone joins the shank — treifah:
{Rama: And the custom in all Ashkenaz and France is like the latter view (Rosh and Tur and Rabbeinu Yerucham), and one should not change; therefore everywhere it was cut from the arkum and upward — treifah, even if the junction of sinews remains intact.}
