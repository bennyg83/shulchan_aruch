See Be'er Heitev who wrote in Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 59: in an animal that walked straight and no defect was visible during life or after slaughter — then two men came and said they wanted to buy this animal they know in Ashkenaz, only the seller told them it limps a little because the thigh fell from the socket called sisal; even if the seller was an Israelite who said so — it is only in appearance that he says so from estimation, for he saw it limp a little and thought it was pushed from its place; and in truth it may be it was not pushed at all, only perhaps weakened by illness in its legs and its throat was caught; and since there is no slipped thigh before us, even if two witnesses testify the thigh once slipped — since it returned healthy before us, certainly projections were not severed and not consumed — all the more it is only according to estimation and he says in appearance; and in such a case we say in Ketubot daf 9 that if one could say from presumption he does not know — he is not believed to forbid it; all the more the animal walked straight and after flaying no defect was seen; however if the owner knew of this defect and heard from the posek inspection is needed and he transgressed and did not inspect as required — he should be fined; and see there regarding an animal whose legs were injured and became lame in one leg — inspection is needed on the leg that limped, as written in siman 34 regarding spinal cord severance known to have fallen — we are concerned; and behold although there is no such concern since it did not fall only was injured — nevertheless there are grounds to say it became slipped thigh and projections severed and consumed and the animal became treifah; and Mordechai wrote in article on milk from animals whose boka de'atma was pushed from its place called in Yiddish (schnarbein) etc.:
Therefore one must forbid any slipped thigh perhaps.
