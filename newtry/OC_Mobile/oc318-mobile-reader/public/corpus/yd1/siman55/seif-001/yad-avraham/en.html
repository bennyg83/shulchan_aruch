(Siman 55 seif 1) Three joints in the leg etc. And there are those who forbid when the middle bone is cut etc. even not at the place of junction of sinews. And likewise if cut between the arkum and the shank — treifah. Taz ruled if cut specifically between the teshi and the shank — treifah, but when the teshi itself is cut etc. — kosher, for the teshi itself belongs to the lower hock. And see Be'er Heitev that there are acharonim who hold the teshi belongs to the shank and if cut there — treifah; and in my humble opinion the essence is like Taz, and so is proven in Gemara and Tosafot Perek HaMekasheh (daf 76) regarding that which Ulla challenged Rav Yehudah who said that which the mishnah teaches there that its legs were cut from the hock and below — kosher, from the hock and upward — disqualified — deals with the lower hock, and immediately above is disqualified, meaning in all the middle bone. And he challenged: if so what does it teach "and likewise the junction of sinews was removed" — for when the middle bone is entirely cut, behold the sinews are automatically cut. And it answers: it teaches "hock without junction of sinews," such as the bone broke and the sinews remain. And he challenged: but it teaches "were cut." He was silent. Tosafot wondered: what is the difficulty — perhaps he holds like those who say the junction of sinews is from the protruding part and inward, and above the hock it is not treifah because of the junction. And they answered Rav Yehudah speaks nearby "where butchers pluck" etc., meaning above the arkum which is still higher — end of his words; and if Rav Yehudah's arkum is included in "from the hock and upward" of the mishnah — if so why was he silent; Ulla should have said to him the mishnah teaches from the hock and upward immediately treifah, meaning in all the arkum where there is not yet treifah in the junction, only above the arkum treifah of the junction begins; and as Tosafot challenged — requires further study; rather it is known to say that even for Rav Yehudah the arkum itself is not included above the hock, only from the end of the middle bone treifah begins from bone cutting; and there treifah of the junction also begins; therefore he challenged well "what 'and likewise junction removed'" and was silent. It is also compelled from that sugya that cut between arkum and shank is treifah, unlike Rosh who was uncertain in this; for if between them there is no treifah from the bone, if so why was he silent — he should have said it was necessary to teach "and likewise junction removed," meaning even where there is no treifah from cutting, there is treifah from the junction.
