Between the arkum and the shank — kosher; but in the arkum itself — kosher as Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe wrote in name of Mahari ibn Chaviv who explained thus Tur's words and learned from Rabbeinu Yerucham — this is Raavan's view in the hagahah; but in truth it is proven from Rosh's words in Perek HaMekasheh that when cut within the arkum it is treifah, and so is Tur's language, and so Lechem Chamudos daf 185a, and so Bach, and so appears from Rabbeinu Yerucham in Netiv 15 ot 18: if the leg was cut in the lower hock itself between the joints — Tosafot forbade it, and I wrote the measure of junction of sinews from above the arkum — and it is the small bone connecting the joints; and so Rosh in responsa kelal 20 siman 16: know my son that the lower bone connected to the hooves is not called hock but there are small bones between the lower and middle bone called hock, and in that hock itself it is treifah per R' Yehuda — like above the hock — end of his words; and he wrote there several times; if so, even if junction of sinews remain — treifah, for it is treifah from the reason it is as cut above the hock; and Bach wrote and so we hold; but Maharshal in Perek HaMekasheh challenged Rosh — his view is that when cut in the arkum its law is like below the hock; and his words are unclear, also his proof from Mordechai is unclear, for those are Ra'avyah's words in Mordechai who hold like Rambam that in the shank it is kosher whenever junction of sinews were not cut; and he only spoke that in the arkum there is no junction of sinews, therefore if cut there — kosher; and Orach Chaim ruled like Rosh; however in Semak siman 86: in the arkum itself treifah from concern one sinew was severed, and even if they did not protrude — if so even if break healed joining fragment to fragment — treifah since the reason is concern for junction of sinews as below siman 56 s.k. 12; and see what I wrote on this in siman 56 s.k. 4:
