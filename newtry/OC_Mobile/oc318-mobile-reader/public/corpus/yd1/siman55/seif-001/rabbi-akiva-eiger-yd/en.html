(Seif 5 in the hagahah) Nevertheless the custom is to declare treifah. For they decreed lest sometimes there be a hole in skin and flesh — then forbidden according to Rashi even when skin and flesh cover most of it, lest the break once protruded through a hole; and combined with that even for other poskim, nevertheless since in any case it requires flesh covering, we say we are not expert — therefore we forbid when skin and flesh are pierced, and even without piercing from decree; in great loss they are lenient, meaning without piercing skin and flesh; but with piercing skin and flesh we forbid even in great loss. So is the conclusion of the acharonim. And I still doubt whether one may be lenient in great loss without piercing skin and flesh — meaning they did not decree lest piercing, since when pierced itself we forbid only because we are not expert in covering; but where there is truly no covering, namely peeled etc. — one may say even in great loss it is forbidden; or in great loss we do not say the decree lest piercing at all — requires further study for practical law:
