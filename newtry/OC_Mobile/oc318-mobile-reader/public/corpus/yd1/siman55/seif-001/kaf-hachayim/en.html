(1) [Seif 1] There are three joints in the leg. The lower one — that which is cut with the hooves etc. — and even though there are more joints in the hock sold with the head, he counts them as joint 1 because their law is the same.
(2) There. Three joints in the leg etc. — but if the forelegs of an animal broke — it was explained above siman 53 seif 1; and in our words there at length.
(4) There. And above it is the second joint called the shank and the junction of sinews at its bottom etc. Behold in the form of these bones many posekim are confused and are not expert — if they come before them after cooking whether it is the shank bone or the kulyah; also they do not know its head and end if it connects to the hock — and it is the leg in a bird where the place of junction of sinews is inside, or its end is the place of connection to the kulyah; also in the kulyah bone they do not know head and end and place of projections; through this many are lenient not according to law and sometimes stringent not according to law — and there is great practical difference in the law as known; therefore I took trouble to give signs in them. Know: the beginning of the leg called in an animal arkeivah — next to it in a bird connected with the shank bone like bukna in asita; and that shank bone is much longer than the kulyah bone; and the head of this bone at the place it connects to the hock is made like a gutter sunk in the middle on one side — and there the junction of sinews begin and go upward on the other side whose head is made like a thick button and is not sunk and adheres to the kulyah bone. And the kulyah bone at the place it connects to the shank is also made like a gutter sunk in the middle like the shank bone mentioned — only much shorter than the shank; and above near the body also made like a button but smaller than the shank button; and on its head it has about three small protrusions and connects to the body with projections like bukna in asita. Now the posek who fears Heaven will see with his eyes and understand in his mind to be expert in it; in his heart he will understand. Beit Yitzchak part 1 in Ta'aruvot HaBayit ot 1, Binyan Av ot 1; and there is another sign that the shank is not equal in thickness — rather its head connected to the hock where the junction of sinews is — is thin, and its second head connected to the kulyah is thick; but the kulyah bone does not have such difference in thickness and is almost entirely equal in thickness. Zivchei Tzedek ot 4.
(5) There. In the lower joint wherever it is cut — kosher. An incident with a lamb created lacking in its hind legs — all the lower joint sold with the head, only a small piece remained at that joint place connected to the shank with hooves fixed — and the lamb walked like with complete legs — one may declare kosher. Chinuch Beit Yehudah siman 53. Marginal note Maharsha on Shulchan Aruch. Tur HaZahav siman 83 ot 52. Zivchei Tzedek ot 54.
(7) There. And there are those who forbid when the middle bone is cut anywhere etc. (hagahah). And the custom in all Ashkenaz and France is like the latter view etc.; and one should be stringent; however when there is another safek or very great loss — one may rely on the plain ruling of Shulchan Aruch. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 47 and so in his book Shaarei De'ah in Ein HaChayim siman 79 — only there he did not write "great loss," only "great loss" plainly. Shaarei De'ah ot 5; and so agreed the Mechaber end ot 4 Anaf Yehonatan siman 37 ot 1; Turei Zahav there ot 6; Zivchei Tzedek ot 2.
(9) There. And likewise if cut within the joint etc. between the arkum and the shank — treifah according to this view. And he means: if cut between that small bone children play with and the shank — treifah per this view; but if cut in that small bone itself — Maharshal in Perek HaMekasheh permits, for its law is like the hock sold with the head; and so is Taz s.k. 1; however Shach s.k. 1 brings a dispute — some say its law is like above the hock and concludes to forbid; see there; and Beit David Kereti ot 2 wrote in great loss one may rely on those who permit; and so Mechaber ot 8 Anaf Yehonatan there ot 2; Machazik Berachah ot 2; Zivchei Tzedek ot 3.
(10) There. Therefore if the bone broke at the junction of sinews etc. (hagahah). And the custom in Ashkenaz and France is like the latter view that from the arkum and upward is treifah even if junction of sinews remains; and one should not change. Kereti ot 4. Machazik Berachah ot 5. Zivchei Tzedek ot 5.
(11) [Seif 2] The kulyah bone that separated from its place at its joint with the body, namely boka de'atma that was pushed from its place — treifah, both in animal and bird; and this is when its projections were consumed. Chochmat Adam klal 31 ot 1. Shaarei De'ah ot 6. Zivchei Tzedek ot 6.
(13) There. But some say we are not expert in consumption of projections; therefore forbid any slipped thigh (hagahah: and likewise if broke near the body even if rejoined well — treifah, perhaps projections were consumed; we estimate four fingerbreadths in large animal and two in small, and in bird large according to size and small according to smallness). Chochmat Adam there ot 3. Zivchei Tzedek ot 8.
