And requires further study whether to be lenient in great loss from double safek: safek after slaughter and if you say during life — perhaps it did not protrude outward; and even though there is lack of knowledge, in great loss they are lenient in such a case as Rama wrote (siman 110); see Shach above (siman 53 s.k. 15 and in siman 55 s.k. 4) in name of Ramah that double safek does not reverse.
