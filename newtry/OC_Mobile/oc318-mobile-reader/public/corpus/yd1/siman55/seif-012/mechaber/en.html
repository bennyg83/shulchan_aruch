If the bone broke in a place that makes it treifah and returned and rejoined, and it is not known whether most of it protruded or not — kosher, provided it returned to its original place and the fragments joined together adhering: {Rama: And even if it is still thick at the place of the break and it is still recognizable that it broke — kosher (Orach Chaim HaAruch);} but if it did not return to its original place but the two bone fragments lie one on the other, one drawn upward and one drawn downward — treifah: {Rama: And all this deals when not at the place of junction of sinews; but at the place of junction of sinews see below siman 56.}
