(Shach s.k. 6) One may be lenient in great loss. Meaning only when inspecting projections; but since we are not expert in inspection here, they are lenient in great loss; but without inspection — likewise even in great loss it is forbidden, for above (siman 53 seif 3) they are stringent like the poskim that broken is like slipped even in great loss; if so it should be proven they are lenient even without rejoining, though otherwise forbidden without great loss when there is no defect in skin and flesh — and it is two leniencies in great loss, unlike what I wrote for this; one may say more that Shach deals with well rejoined: from the side of leg breaking there is no prohibition and they are lenient in great loss even without inspection at all, as Shach concludes above (siman 53 s.k. 7); requires further study:
