But other poskim hold regarding "its wings broke" — since for Israel there is only mitzvah of separation, permitted to a non-Jew l'chatchila; and there is no "before a blind person" prohibition:
When cutting the forbidden limb, etc. Meaning the place of cutting where it is now connected — because impossible to trim precisely; and so poskim — place of cutting forbidden; but certainly not at the cut place one need not cut above the break — what relevance above the break; Maharshal and Bach understood poskim forbid above the cut even if bone and flesh separated from the dangling limb — therefore they challenged; matter is clear as I explained; for us, since even with flesh covering the dangling limb is forbidden — we are not expert what is called flesh covering as Tur HaChadash kelal 87 end din 1 — place of cutting all flesh around the break forbidden; but from permitted bone above — need not cut anything:
And one must cut it before salting, etc. And where there is safek whether protruded outward — even though custom is to cut the limb and discard — nevertheless if salted or roasted or cooked with that limb — do not forbid even without sixty; as poskim wrote where safek — lenient; so Bach and his words are correct; if so all the more where no defect in skin and flesh and it rejoined but not fragment to fragment) — permitted the dish and unlike responsa Masat Binyamin siman 40; and even where certainly protruded needing sixty in cooked or roasted and salting — against it do not be stringent where no sixty in the piece — we do not say the piece became nevelah through adhering prohibition (as below siman 72 regarding heart and siman 73 liver) since this limb has only mitzvah of separation; but where no sixty in the dish — need sixty against entire dish since dish became from outset nevelah; only where sixty in dish but not in piece with adhering limb — do not be stringent since many poskim hold even adhering prohibition all the pot joins as below siman 72 seif 3; and in adhering one may say since rabbis considered this dangling limb as if cut — therefore forbidden as limb from living animal which biblically is permitted while attached slightly — do not be stringent to consider adhering prohibition contradicting two stringencies; but where no sixty in dish — cannot say thus:
To declare every break treifah, etc. This is a great stringency not mentioned by any posek; acharonim and Maharai and Orach Chaim did not write thus — only Agur wrote custom is so; Beit Yosef disagrees; Maharshal in Orach Chaim siman 86 wrote we do not practice like Agur; also Agur's reason — for Rambam concern junction of sinews throughout middle bone — is clear if broke not at junction — kosher even for us; therefore it appears despite custom to declare all cases, when rejoined even without fragment to fragment — permit when skin and flesh whole and no visible defect — for otherwise from poskim when rejoined it is permitted even without fragment to fragment; Bach permits in great loss; if so here even without great loss one may permit; perhaps therefore Rama wrote "but if returned and rejoined — custom is to permit" and did not write "and rejoined fragment to fragment."
