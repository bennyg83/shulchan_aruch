See Be'er Heitev who wrote in Noda BiYehudah siman 20: a bird whose kulyah bone broke near the body and returned and rejoined like a stylus and was salted with two other birds because they did not notice the break until after salting and rinsing — since it broke and rejoined, the concern is consumption of projections; and Rama permits in great loss; and for those who permit in siman 56 seif 10 even without great loss — permitted; and even for those who forbid it is only safek that perhaps projections were consumed; therefore if salted with its kind and there is majority against it — one may permit what was salted with it in the shell for all, even if it is fat; and if cooked afterward without shelling — permitted b'dieved; and good to peel after cooking if easy to peel; and see in books in Kuntras HaTeshuvot siman 14 what I raised on this; and see in Bach responsa siman 139: kulyah bone in a bird broke and rejoined well but fragments lie one on the other and there was no defect until eating flesh above the bone revealed the break — they salted together flesh and many birds; one who is stringent for himself — holy may he be called; for others one need not be stringent at all, all the more in great loss or for Shabbat need; one who is stringent should not be stringent except for that pot in which the bird was cooked, but other meat found — even though there is no sixty against the bird — one need not be stringent, for by law the bird too is permitted, only because it emerged from Mahariik's mouth "do not add" to be stringent in salting which is also a stringency; see there and see siman 23 regarding kulyah bone in a bird found broken and the break rejoined well but not fragment to fragment — the chicken was already eaten; metal vessels not Beit Yosef are permitted, for even if safek the flesh appearance changed — it is a d'rabbanan safek; nevertheless metal vessels require hag'alah as if like hag'alah expenses are minor as Shach wrote siman 102; and so Chokhmat Adam in name of Be'er Sheva; see there and Pri Megadim part 2 siman 109: bird whose shank bone broke above the junction of sinews and flesh and skin were whole and they did not recognize it while alive; after cooking and eating a bit they found the break diagonally and fragments were not adhering fragment to fragment — one may declare kosher from double safek: perhaps it did not protrude outward, and if you say it protruded and skin and flesh did not cover most of it — perhaps the halacha follows Rif and Rambam that in the middle bone above the junction there is no prohibition; and in rejoining even without fragment to fragment one may combine this view to permit even without great loss as Shach and Pri Chadash; and see Chavot Tzvi and Pri Chadash to be lenient even in kulyah near the body and not rejoined fragment to fragment if no defect in skin and flesh — but his words are not compelled; go out and see what the community practices and sufficient for us to be lenient even in middle bone not at junction of sinews; and once we were at a circumcision meal and a break was found in the kulyah in a place that makes treifah and there was no fragment to fragment — we investigated and there was no defect in skin and flesh and we permitted the dish because of great loss and mitzvah meal; see there and siman 120 regarding a chicken whose shank bone broke above half the bone and immediately sent it to the posek while alive and felt two protrusions and skin was whole — they slaughtered and inspected and found skin whole on all sides; he wrote that according to custom to declare treifah without return and rejoining lest one come to permit if the break protruded during life — he wrote there is no reason for prohibition that perhaps it protruded once, for we do not establish defect; rather the reason lest one come to permit etc.; and in my humble opinion it is wondrous to lose Jewish money because of a decree lest the posek come to permit if it protruded during life — one should not compare to rabbinic decrees where sometimes they decreed in such a case; rather the reason is as plain: perhaps it will happen that it protruded etc.; and even though we do not establish defect, we are stringent in this because it is somewhat common that when broken by force at the moment a bit protrudes outward — if so in our case where immediately after the break they inspected and found skin whole — this reason does not apply; and also the second reason that perhaps flesh was peeled — one need not be so stringent when there was no interval after the break, only immediately slaughter — I shortened; and it appears to be lenient even in slight loss in such a case; and also for the second reason that we are not expert in flesh covering — also here where he saw immediately these reasons apply etc.; therefore also here one should not overturn custom and one should be lenient only in great loss or for an important matter that is like great loss; see there and so in Shemesh Tzedakah siman 26 regarding kulyah bone that broke and rejoined; and see there siman 55 seif 9 in this law that returned and rejoined but not fragment to fragment and was salted with many birds;
