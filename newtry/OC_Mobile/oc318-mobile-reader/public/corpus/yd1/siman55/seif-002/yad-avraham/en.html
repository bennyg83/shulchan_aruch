(Seif 3 in the hagahah) And some say specifically when skin and flesh cover most of it. See Mateh Yehonatan kelal 8 explained from his words that if the skin outside is whole we do not require skin and flesh covering in slipped thigh for all — and so is practical halacha; and also projections were not severed. And see Kereti. And greater than this Maharshal wrote chapter 5 of Ma'achalot Assurot halacha 8 in name of Rashba's responsa that in slipped thigh we do not require covering at all and it is always permitted; but what Maharshal cited from Rashba on Rambam's words there as if one language and one matter — is not so, as Shach challenged below siman 62 s.k. 13 on the hagahah there that cited Rashba's words for halacha — Rashba follows his reasons permitting eggs that were uprooted; but Rambam ruled there halacha 7 that a limb that slipped etc. is forbidden to eat from custom that all Israel practiced beforehand, for it is similar to a limb from a living animal:
