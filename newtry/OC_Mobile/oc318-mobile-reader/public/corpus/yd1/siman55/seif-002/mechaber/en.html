The kulyah bone that separated from its place at its joint with the body, namely boka de'atma that was pushed from its place — treifah, both in an animal and in a bird; and this is when its projections were consumed (meaning rotted) — they are the fibers in the spoon-bone that emerge on this bone and hold it; but if they were severed not due to consumption — kosher: {Rama: And likewise if they were not consumed but only a minority of them and the majority remain — kosher by law (Rashba in responsa and in his novellae); but some say we are not expert in consumption of its projections; therefore one must forbid any dislocated thigh, perhaps its projections were consumed; and likewise if it broke near the body, even if it rejoined well — treifah, perhaps its projections were consumed; and we estimate four fingerbreadths in a large animal and two in a small one, and in a bird the large one according to its size and the small according to its smallness (all in Orach Chaim general rule 50 and hagahot Semak daf 92b and hagahah of Rashba siman 86); and it is good to be stringent where there is no great loss. If the thigh did not slip out and its projections were consumed — kosher (Mordechai).}
