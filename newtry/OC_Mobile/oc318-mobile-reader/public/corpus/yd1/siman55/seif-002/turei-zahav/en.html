And this is when its nivin dissolved. In Tur he wrote "or dissolved"; Beit Yosef in name Rivash it is scribal error; in my humble opinion no scribal error — Tur's version is like Rif and geonim Beit Yosef brings who read in Gemara "these matters that dissolved"; therefore Talmud says even if not entirely severed but only dissolved — treifah; a fortiori entirely severed even without dissolution.
And likewise if broke near body even though, etc. In broke without protruding he distinguishes kulit; if protruded — treifah in entire kulit bone as in cut; for cut or broke and protruded — all forbid entire kulit; so wrote explicitly in Or Zarua general rule 50.
