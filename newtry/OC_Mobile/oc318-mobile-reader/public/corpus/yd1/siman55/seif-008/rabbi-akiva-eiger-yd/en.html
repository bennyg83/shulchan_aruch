(Shach s.k. 21) For here the safek is because of lack of knowledge. And requires further study whether to be lenient in great loss from double safek: safek after slaughter and if you say during life — perhaps it did not protrude outward; and even though there is lack of knowledge, in great loss they are lenient in such a case as Rama wrote (siman 110); see Shach above (siman 53 s.k. 15 and in siman 55 s.k. 4) in name of Ramah that double safek does not reverse. Nevertheless if it is from the kulyot and below — ostensibly one may argue to be lenient since one may combine the view of Rif and supporters that prohibition is only in kulyot; if so even if certainly there was skin and flesh, or in cutting — one may say it is double safek after slaughter and safek like Rif's view; and in any case in safek whether it protruded outward, and especially with whole flesh covering — it appears there is reason to be lenient even without great loss:
