If the flesh covering most of it was not in one place but collected a bit here and a bit there around the bone, or it is in one place but is flattened or peeled off the bone or its lower third was removed or pierced or cracked — they do not save; and likewise if the covering flesh dissolved and became like flesh that a doctor scraped — it does not save: {Rama: And we are not expert in flesh that a doctor scraped; therefore everywhere the appearance of the flesh changed — it does not save (Hagahot Maimoniot).}
