Great loss. See Be'er Heitev and what he wrote in name of Pri Chadash; see in responsa Batei Kohanim siman 11 who brought: in safek severance, even when the manner was that we could not inspect until a claim of lack of knowledge would fall per contract law — nevertheless one must forbid even in great loss.
