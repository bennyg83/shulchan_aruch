Forbidden from safek. And see siman 36 seif 5 that we hang on wolf — there there are grounds to say; unlike here; so wrote several poskim; and some wrote to distinguish: here safek is from lack of knowledge; Maharai in hagahot Rashba siman 86: where bone broke or slipped in treifah place and unknown if during life or after death — sometimes we can clarify if blow black — known during life; wherever we cannot clarify — forbid from safek; we do not say slaughtered permitted it like wolf; here safek depends on lack of wisdom — if expert could distinguish; with defect we forbid from safek; and all such cases — end of his words.
