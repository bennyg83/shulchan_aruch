Forbidden from doubt. As written siman 50 seif 1 in hagahah; but found in Semak wrote in name Behag as Tosafot challenge there: every doubt stringently only where legs to say done after slaughter; so Ran in name Behag chapter 4; so Hagahot Maimoniot ch. 1 Hilchot Shechitah; so Mordechai and Shach and Piskei; so Gemara challenges there 51a:1 and answers Shegruna common; specifically because common; and what written (49a:1) punctured where butcher's hand uses, etc. — not so; likewise do not wrap from thin to thick and from segment to segment of segment (51a:1); and do not wrap in bubbles (46b:2); Tosafot Perek Arba Achim — for Gemara conclusion as Shach siman 50; but every treifah doubt until permitted more common like Shegruna; so Tosafot there from doubt dog doubt cat as Rashba wrote cat not common that tramples; so Ran chapter 1 in name R' Yonah every doubt stringently and proved from doubt of drusah as Tosafot in challenge and from that of Shegruna as above; so poskim.
