To stand. Meaning the face of the blow was not blackened — safek perhaps it was made during life and the flesh healed (Or Zarua wrote: this that we hang to be lenient — specifically in a bird; but in the legs of an animal, which are thick — we do not hang; but Tur HaChadash general rule 87 ot 28 and Darkei Moshe and Darkei Moshe 144 and Bach disagree and hold that even in an animal we hang on crushing of limbs; and know also that in a bird there are three joints: kulit, thigh, and the third bone that has scales, which is opposite the ankle sold with the head in an animal).
From safek. Shach wrote: and see siman 36 seif 5 that we hang on a wolf — there there are legs to the matter; or else here it is safek from lack of knowledge and is not called safek.
