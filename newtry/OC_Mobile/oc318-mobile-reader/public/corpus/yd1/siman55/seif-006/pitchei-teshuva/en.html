Broke near the body etc. See in responsa Batei Kohanim siman 11 who elaborated greatly and brought that Orach Chaim and Rama z"l deal when it is not known whether pushed or not — such as found in a child's hand and he does not tell if it was slipped or struck as usual; they wrote we are concerned perhaps due to the break projections were severed and consumed; and if consumption — it is likely it was pushed and it is a safek close to vadai; but if broke near the body and we see it was not pushed — not only per the rabbis who hold even in boka de'atma if projections consumed and not pushed we are not concerned; but even per my master the Rav z"l (Pri Chadash brought below s.k. 11 and in Be'er Heitev of Maharit z"l s.k. 7) who holds the law is like slipped — nevertheless in a break where the defect did not arise in boka de'atma one may be lenient even if certainly severed, all the more in safek; however if we see it was pushed and there is room to doubt perhaps after slaughter — such as when brought to slaughter it was not slipped (like that which Be'er Heitev brought s.k. 3) — and we also find break near the body; since there are two grounds for defect — even if not severed, neither in boka nor at the break place — nevertheless one must forbid; see there:
