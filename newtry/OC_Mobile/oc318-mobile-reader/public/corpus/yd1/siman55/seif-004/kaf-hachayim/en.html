And above it is the second joint called the shank and the junction of sinews at its bottom etc. Behold in the form of these bones many posekim are confused and are not expert — if they come before them after cooking whether it is the shank bone or the kulyah; also they do not know its head and end if it connects to the hock — and it is the leg in a bird where the place of junction of sinews is inside, or its end is the place of connection to the kulyah; also in the kulyah bone they do not know head and end and place of projections; through this many are lenient not according to law and sometimes stringent not according to law — and there is great practical difference in the law as known; therefore I took trouble to give signs in them. Know: the beginning of the leg called in an animal arkeivah — next to it in a bird connected with the shank bone like bukna in asita; and that shank bone is much longer than the kulyah bone; and the head of this bone at the place it connects to the hock is made like a gutter sunk in the middle on one side — and there the junction of sinews begin and go upward on the other side whose head is made like a thick button and is not sunk and adheres to the kulyah bone. And the kulyah bone at the place it connects to the shank is also made like a gutter sunk in the middle like the shank bone mentioned — only much shorter than the shank; and above near the body also made like a button but smaller than the shank button; and on its head it has about three small protrusions and connects to the body with projections like bukna in asita. Now the posek who fears Heaven will see with his eyes and understand in his mind to be expert in it; in his heart he will understand. Beit Yitzchak part 1 in Ta'aruvot HaBayit ot 1, Binyan Av ot 1; and there is another sign that the shank is not equal in thickness — rather its head connected to the hock where the junction of sinews is — is thin, and its second head connected to the kulyah is thick; but the kulyah bone does not have such difference in thickness and is almost entirely equal in thickness.
