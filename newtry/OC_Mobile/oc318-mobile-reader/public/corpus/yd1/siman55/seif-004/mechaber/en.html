If it has three legs — treifah, for every extra limb is considered as removed and it is as if lacking one leg; and this is when this extra limb is from the head of the lower bone and upward: {Rama: And specifically when this extra limb has the appearance of a leg and stands in the place of legs; but otherwise it is not called an extra limb and is only a protrusion and kosher (Beit Yosef in name of Mordechai in name of Raavan and in Sha'arim siman 89 and Orach Chaim there); and if created lacking one leg — treifah (Gemara Avodah Zarah daf 58 and Bekhorot daf 40 and Rambam chapter 8 of Hilchot Shechitah, and not like Beit Yosef, and all seized upon him in this).}
