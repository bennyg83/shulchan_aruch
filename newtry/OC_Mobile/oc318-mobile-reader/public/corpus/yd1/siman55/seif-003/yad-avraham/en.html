(Seif 10) The flesh covering most of it was not in one place etc. or peeled from the bone or its lower third was removed — they do not save. This is a question not resolved in Perek HaMekasheh (daf 77), and in my humble opinion it is proven explicitly in Gemara there and all poskim — meaning peeled and lower third removed in the majority; but if not peeled or removed only a minority — in an animal, or half in a bird with half connected — long discussion; for in Gemara there they seek to resolve from that which Ulla said R' Yochanan said skin is like flesh, that skin alone saves; and since here there is only skin it is kosher, all the more when there is flesh but it was peeled or a third removed — rejected: perhaps the stalk of my hand. Rashi: Ulla deals where there is no flesh such as near the hock and skin adheres well to the bone and holds it. Tosafot: there are secretions between skin and bone. So too Taz seif 8 that in such a case skin alone saves. And in seif 7 he wrote minority of skin joins in animal and bird even half skin kosher; meaning even where there was flesh and all flesh was removed because of the skin — nevertheless since half the flesh was not peeled and not removed — kosher. And so in the next seif pierced flesh in a circle etc. kosher when flesh adheres around the ring. Now one might say it is impossible to permit if flesh does not adhere but was peeled; rather we learn that without piercing, even if peeled up to half — kosher in a bird. However Rambam although explained skin alone saves, nevertheless explained the question of lower third removed that it is forbidden.
