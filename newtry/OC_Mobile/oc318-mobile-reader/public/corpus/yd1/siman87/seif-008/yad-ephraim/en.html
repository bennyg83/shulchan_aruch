And therefore one should also forbid using the pots. Baer Heitev — and what he wrote: if so, how does he permit l'chatchila, etc. — see Tzemach Tzedek siman 98, who wrote that in truth one should not take ash to rinse utensils from the stove where they cook, but one takes ash from the oven where they bake bread and the like — see there. And in Magen Avraham end of seif 6 he wrote that he saw people are not careful about this and gave a reason that it is only a mere concern, and presumably all moisture in the ash has already been burned; it is also nullified in the water and gives noten taam lifgam through the ash; and rinsing eating utensils is only through iruy — and likewise see there. And see in Shulchan Aruch HaRav that one should be careful not to draw water in a meat vessel and afterward in a milk vessel, for sometimes they are dirty and there is no noten taam bar noten taam of permission; and see there that he wrote one should protest against those who do so.
