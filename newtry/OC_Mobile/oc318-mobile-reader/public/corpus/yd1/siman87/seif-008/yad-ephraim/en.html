Baer Heitev — and he wrote in Mishna Berurah in the name of Beit David: for a sick person whom the physician commanded to cook a whole chicken in water dripping from moist unsalted cheese that is nesyovei without salting the chicken in order to extract the water in an alembic — and this is his cure to drink that water — thus he should do to be saved from prohibition: roast the chicken first, and first cook the water dripping from the cheese and filter it, and take the clear water, and cook the chicken with that water in the alembic, and the sick person should drink, even though there is no danger.
