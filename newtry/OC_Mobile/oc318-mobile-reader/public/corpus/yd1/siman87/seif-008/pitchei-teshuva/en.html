Permitted in cooking. See in Chumdei Daniel 18 that he wrote that it is permitted to cook meat for a non-Jew in a pot of milk — it is not a kli rishon, for whatever is forbidden only d'rabbanan they did not decree on cooking and benefit; and here there is no marit ayin — who would know whether it is a pot of meat or milk? Nevertheless one should be stringent because Rama wrote in seif 6 that it is forbidden to stoke, etc. And one may say because initially it was pure milk they were somewhat stringent — see there.
