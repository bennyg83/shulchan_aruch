And for benefit. Shach wrote: but in eating without qualification forbidden d'oraisa because impure meat or impure milk — not basar b'chalav; Bach wrote rabbinically forbidden because of basar b'chalav; practical difference piece becoming nevelah — Mechaber siman 92 only meat and milk; likewise piece fit to honor guests; Taz challenged: R' Ephraim's reason in basar b'chalav is each alone permitted, mixtures forbidden becoming one body — not applicable to one impure species; piece fit to honor guests not applicable; Shach disagrees with Bach — see there.
Rabbinically. Bach ruled fowl meat in milk is d'oraisa; Shach disagrees and brings Rambam: one who says fowl in milk d'oraisa violates bal tosif.
Prohibition. Taz wrote nevertheless do not eat fish in milk because of danger as Orach Chaim siman 173 — error; only fish with meat forbidden for danger; fish with milk unheard and unseen; daily practice cook fish in milk; Gemara chapter Kol HaTzlamim explicit no danger — plain — end Shach.
Almonds. Shach wrote: fish blood siman 66 permitted entirely; nevertheless in vessel forbidden marit ayin unless scales show fish blood — there blood d'oraisa; viewer may think animal blood d'oraisa; but actual fowl in milk rabbinic — no marit ayin; therefore must speak of cooking; otherwise domestic meat no d'oraisa prohibition without almonds; Maharshal ruled fowl in almond milk forbidden unless almonds placed; primary since even rabbinic marit ayin in many places — Orach Chaim siman 305 pair plugged lest appear going to market to sell — marit ayin though commerce Shabbat rabbinic — end; Taz: l'chatchila place almonds even by fowl; where no almonds do not forbid eating — all we can fix we fix.
