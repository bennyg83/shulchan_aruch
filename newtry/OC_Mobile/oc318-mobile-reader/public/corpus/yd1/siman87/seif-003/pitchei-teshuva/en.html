By way of cooking. Beit HaTaharah — and see Peri Chadash that frying is also included in cooking; but Magen Avraham in Seder Limud general rule 5 and Maharam Schiff at end of the book on Chullin inclined to be lenient that frying is not included in cooking and is not forbidden for benefit. And in Peri Megadim one may be lenient. And hot in hot without gravy — Pri Megadim wrote in the introduction in name of Rashal that it is not by way of cooking and is permitted for benefit — see there. [And see in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 97 what he wrote in our time; nevertheless even in these that are only d'rabbanan and permitted for benefit one must give them to a non-Jew thus and not cook initially, for then one violates cooking and it is forbidden for benefit d'oraisa, for although the piece absorbed not by way of cooking, nevertheless afterward the meat cooked with the milk absorbed in it — this is obvious; and so in responsum Noda Biyehudah Yoreh Deah siman 26 s.v. and from this.]
