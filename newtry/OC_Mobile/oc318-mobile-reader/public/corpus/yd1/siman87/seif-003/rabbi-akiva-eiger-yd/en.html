(Shach small seif 5) Again came to hand the book Be'er Sheva. And see in his book Tzeda LaDerech parashat Tzav.
(Taz s.k. 4) Like woman's milk. This is astonishing — behold with woman's milk even b'dieved it is forbidden and is permitted only if it fell into a dish that is not recognizable; also per this proof it would be proven that with domestic animal meat too they do not forbid eating it; and so what Taz wrote, and so Rama's wording implies — one may place, etc.
(Shach s.k. 6) But because of marit ayin it does not deal. And so I found explicitly in responsum Tolodot Adam of Rashba (siman 200).
(Be'er Sheva) That one may cook for medical need. I have difficulty — nevertheless why permitted for benefit? Let us be concerned for marit ayin; requires study.
