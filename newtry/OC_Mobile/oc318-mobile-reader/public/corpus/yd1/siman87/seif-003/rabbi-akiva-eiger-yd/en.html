(In the hagahah) Permitted for benefit. And that he did not write permitted in cooking — this is because Beit Av considers it through pickling and salting; in this, cooking is forbidden since pickling and salting absorb milk in it, and when he cooks he now cooks meat with milk inside it and violates cooking, and it also becomes forbidden for benefit because d'oraisa basar b'chalav was created; investigate. And to cause thus — to mix meat in milk for benefit — see in Menachot general rule 5 s.k. 9.
