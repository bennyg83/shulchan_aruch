It applies only to meat of a pure domestic animal in milk of a pure domestic animal; but pure meat in impure milk or impure meat in pure milk is permitted in cooking and benefit; and meat of wild animal and fowl — even in pure milk — is permitted in cooking and benefit, and even for eating it is forbidden only rabbinically; but fish and locusts have no prohibition even rabbinically: {Rama: And the custom is to make almond milk and place fowl meat in it, since it is only rabbinic; but meat of domestic animal one should not place near almond milk because of marit ayin, as explained above siman 66 regarding blood (logical opinion).}
