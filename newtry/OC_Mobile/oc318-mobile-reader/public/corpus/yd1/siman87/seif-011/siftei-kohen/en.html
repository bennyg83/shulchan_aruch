If there is meat taste. Meaning: when there is not sixty in the cheese against the skin; and Mechaber follows his view that below siman 98 we rely on gentile taste — therefore he wrote anonymously 'if there is meat taste'; but we do not rely nowadays on gentile taste, as the Rav wrote in hagahah there — therefore we always measure with sixty; and this is what the Rav wrote in hagahah — the wording is 'if there are sixty' — investigate — end.
But one who sets rennet in nevelah stomach skin. The distinction in this poskim wrote: kosher stomach skin, since permitted in itself and has no prohibition except because of its joining with cheese — whenever it does not impart taste it is not basar b'chalav but each stands alone; but nevelah skin and the like, forbidden in itself — when it sets we see the prohibition as if in existence — end; and Maharshal ruled in chapter 25 siman 106 that even one who sets in nevelah stomach skin nullifies in sixty; and his words are not required — end.
But if there was also permitted setting agent, etc. It appears specifically when the prohibition cannot set by itself without the permitted — if not so, forbidden; and requires study for practical law; and see below siman 143 seif 4 and seif 11 and what is written there — end.
