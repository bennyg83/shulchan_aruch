If one set cheese in the skin of a kosher stomach — it has taste of forbidden meat; and if not — it is permitted; but one who sets in the skin of nevelah, treifah, or impure animal — forbids in any amount: {Rama: Because it is a forbidden item in itself and setting even in a thousand is not nullified (so Beit Yosef per Rashba and Ran); and specifically when there was no other rennet there only the forbidden; but if there was also permitted rennet — it is this-and-this causes and permitted if there is sixty against the forbidden (from implication of Mordechai).}
