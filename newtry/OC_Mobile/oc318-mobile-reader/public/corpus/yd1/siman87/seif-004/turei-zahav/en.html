And it appears accordingly, etc. Darkei Moshe left Rashba's words on woman's milk as requiring study: why is impure milk permitted in cooking? His view here is very astonishing, for Rashba in Torat HaBayit wrote explicitly impure milk is permitted in cooking and benefit and mentioned no marit ayin prohibition; and it appears to me this is not a fortiori — they did not decree marit ayin except in something permitted for eating each alone, then it resembles pure meat and pure milk — therefore with almond milk in cooking one should place almonds l'chatchila; and woman's milk also permitted when separated as in siman 81 — therefore forbidden to cook with meat because of marit ayin; unlike impure milk or impure meat which do not resemble pure basar b'chalav — therefore no decree there for marit ayin — clear to me, except I cannot be lenient where Rama was stringent; nevertheless it is plain that accordingly all the more one must forbid marit ayin to cook fowl in milk which the Sages explicitly forbade as basar b'chalav, and for one tanna it is truly d'oraisa; and so Darkei Moshe — fowl in milk is worse than impure in milk, not as Leket Yosher that here it is permitted for marit ayin because recognizable — sometimes cut unrecognizably; the viewer will not distinguish fowl from domestic meat — dispute of tannaim; and Maharshal wrote in chapter 25 siman 52 on fowl in milk — see there.
