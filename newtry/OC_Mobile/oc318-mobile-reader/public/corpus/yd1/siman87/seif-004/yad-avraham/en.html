(There, seif 3) It applies only to meat of a pure domestic animal with milk of a pure domestic animal. He wrote in Beit Yosef HaMekor that blood that cooked treifa meat or nevelah meat in milk is forbidden from the Torah, and he brought proof for this from Chullin (daf 115), where the school of R' Eliezer taught: a Torah prohibition — every nevelah, etc. And the refutation is: perforce R' Eliezer holds that prohibition applies upon prohibition, and we do not rule thus.
