(There) One may not mix water in which basar b'chalav utensils were rinsed and give it before one's animal, for they are forbidden in benefit. It states matters separately: mixing alone is also a prohibition, for it is cooking basar b'chalav. And if he mixed, they are forbidden in benefit. And see Shach that the hagahah deals when he mixed them boiling, for that is the manner of cooking — see this siman. And see what is written there: one must say the utensils were each rinsed in a kli rishon and were also mixed in a kli rishon; for if through iruy it is not cooking.
