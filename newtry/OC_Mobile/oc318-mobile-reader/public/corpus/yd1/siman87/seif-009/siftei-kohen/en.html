And some forbid. Meaning: when clear, as written above siman 81 seif 6; and on this the Rav wrote in hagahah and so they practice; and he distinguished in hagahah in seif 10 between dried and clear; and it appears from the sage general rule 65 that l'chatchila it is permitted when dried — see there; however regarding clear his view is that even b'dieved it is forbidden, as he wrote in hagahah in seif 11; nevertheless it appears l'chatchila it is forbidden even when dried because of marit ayin, as written in seif 4; and see s.k. 7 and Maharshal chapter 25 siman 107 who ruled even dried is considered actual milk by law, as Rashi; and so appears Ran's view (and so Rokeach siman 461, and requires study, for in siman 464 it appears he distinguishes dried and clear — see there); and so Orach Chaim beginning of general rule 46 — end.
