Baer Heitev — see in Shulchan Aruch HaRav that even hard bones are forbidden, not as implied in the responsum at the end of Magen Avraham that only marrow bones have a prohibition; and see in responsa at the end of his book: a hollow bone found inside a milk food, and there is doubt lest there was brain in it and it came out and mixed in the food — one should be stringent; and in a responsum at the end of Eliyah Rabba he disagreed and wrote to permit, for we do not establish a presumption of prohibition — see there. And it appears that those bones that are routinely known to have brain and it is their way to come out in cooking — one should be stringent in a small dish where there is no sixty.
