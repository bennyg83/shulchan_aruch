Baer Heitev — see Peri Chadash who wrote that in Yerushalmi Shabbat it was resolved: a question was asked whether smoked is considered cooking for Shabbat that one is liable — and likewise for basar b'chalav it is considered cooking; and in Peleti he rejected this, for one can say this is not the manner of cooking through heating in smoke. And see Mishna Berurah who challenged this: if so, why did the Rambam omit it regarding Shabbat? And see in Yeshuot Yaakov regarding Tiberias hot springs that he wrote it is because of a decree lest it be confused with derivatives of fire; and Mishna Berurah wrote that it is a Torah doubt as implied from chapter 5. And regarding roasted meat in milk, the view of Peri Chadash; and in Sefat Emet to Menachot general chapter 5 in the name of Tosafot Yom Tov, Mateh Yosef, and Peleti and Mishbetzot that it is forbidden from the Torah; and Mishna Berurah elaborated on this — see in Peri Chadash regarding roasted in a pot and frying whether included in cooking. And see in responsa Sha'ar Efrayim regarding butter that became forbidden through basar b'chalav because it was cooked in a meat Beit Yosef pot — whether permitted to kindle a Chanukah candle, because mitzvot were not given for benefit; and he also discussed roasted and frying. And I brought this in my responsa on the laws of Chanukah — see there. And see in Sefat Emet regarding candles of milk that gentiles mix butter in time of distress — permitted to kindle, for frying forbidden from the Torah is permitted in benefit, and also there is no olive-size at one time; and see in Petichah to Peri Megadim he wondered on this — see Mishna Berurah who also wrote thus, that the principal matter here is as Peri Chadash that it is forbidden from the Torah, and there he brought from responsa Ohel Yosef who also permits. And Mishna Berurah elaborated and brings among them the Gaon Moharach Abualefia who forbade candles that were a mixture of butter and milk that Jews bought, and commanded to bury them, and great ones agreed with him; and so Mishna Berurah concludes there — see there, for Peri Megadim brought in the name of Mohar"m Shif who inclined that frying is permitted, and he concludes in Peri Megadim to permit; and Mishna Berurah wrote that the principal matter is as Peri Chadash and Mateh Yosef that frying is forbidden from the Torah — see there. And see in responsa Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 60, where they challenged him on what he wrote in Sefat Emet his reasons for permission on account of frying, etc. — for it teaches that milk in milk has no prohibition of benefit; and he answered that certainly it is forbidden in benefit even for the Rambam, as is the view of Tosafot — see there. And see in Mishna Berurah that from the Rambam's commentary on the Mishnah in Keritot, in the wondrous note, it is clear that it is permitted in benefit; and see in Maggid Mishneh chapter 5 of Hilchot Yesodei HaTorah and in Sha'ar HaMelekh there; and see in Teshuvot HaTashbetz part 3 siman 295 who wrote that it is forbidden to cook butter alone to throw it as it is in a hot pan — one transgresses the negative commandment of cooking, for iruy in a kli rishon is like a kli rishon, and a negative commandment regarding benefit if he sells it, etc. — see there. And see in responsa Noda BiYehuda siman 29: liver fried in forbidden butter is forbidden — not to speak of roasted liver that does not discharge, it is nevertheless forbidden on account of fat prohibition; but even living liver is forbidden even according to Rashi for this reason. And what he wrote — and I already wrote what Peri Megadim brought in the name of Mohar"m Shif; and in Mishna Berurah he wrote one may not be lenient, for frying is like cooking and forbidden in benefit from the Torah — see there.
