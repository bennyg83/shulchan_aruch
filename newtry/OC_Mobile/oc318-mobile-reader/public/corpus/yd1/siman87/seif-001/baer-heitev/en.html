Cooking. Meaning: but not by pickling or salting, which is not a way of cooking. {Rama: And roasting is included in cooking; and cold into hot is also a way of cooking; and pouring from a cooking vessel at least the measure of a shell; and smoked is like cooking; and cooked in Tiberias hot springs is not forbidden from the Torah. Peri Chadash}
For benefit. Even rabbinically; Maharshal disagrees and rules forbidden for benefit; Shach challenged and wrote his words are not required; Taz also wrote we have only Rishonim and Acharonim. {Rama: Therefore fowl meat in milk without sixty may be given to a gentile; rennet forbidden for benefit — agreement of Rishonim and Acharonim unlike Maharshal stringent for benefit. Radbaz was asked about practice putting pure milk in sugar when cooking — craftsmen say two per hundred — permitted to eat sugar with meat because nullified in water and sugar — see Peri Chadash}
