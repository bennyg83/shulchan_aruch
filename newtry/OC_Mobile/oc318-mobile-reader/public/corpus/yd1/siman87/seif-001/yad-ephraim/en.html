But fish and locusts have no prohibition. Baer Heitev — see Kenesset HaGedolah that according to physicians there is danger also in fish with milk; and in Mor uKetzia siman 73 likewise in the name of Rabbenu Bachya; and see in Eliyah Rabba in Orach Chaim siman 173, who wrote on Kenesset HaGedolah's words: what he wrote — I have not seen people careful about this. And see in Kenesset HaGedolah that Mahari"t his teacher and also some rabbis would take between fish and cheese; and Mishna Berurah agreed that an error fell in Beit Yosef, and in place of milk it must read meat; and in our lands the common people cook fish with milk and are not careful at all. And regarding cooking fish in a meat vessel — whether sixty helps will be explained in siman 116.
