In which meat does the law of basar b'chalav apply and how is cooking called. In it are 11 seifim: It is written in the Torah 'You shall not cook a kid in its mother's milk' three times — one for prohibition of cooking, one for prohibition of eating, and one for prohibition of benefit; and it derived eating in the language of cooking to say that it is not forbidden from the Torah except by way of cooking; but rabbinically it is forbidden in every manner (all basar b'chalav that is not forbidden from the Torah is permitted for benefit (Tur and Arukh general rule 40)).
