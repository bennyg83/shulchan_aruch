By way of cooking — to exclude pickling or smoking. And regarding roasting there are opinions; see Peleti that one should be stringent.
Cooking — see Peleti: if one cooked olive oil with milk together with one olive's worth of meat, even not in the usual way of cooking as Rashi wrote, nevertheless it is forbidden for benefit from the Torah.
All basar b'chalav — Peri Chadash's view: if not cooked as food of the court, there is no Torah prohibition, for we say in which cooking did they speak — cooking that others eat, meaning the court; and see Peleti that I wrote there is no such thing except whatever comes to heat — all is Torah prohibition — see there.
Permitted for benefit — and whatever is forbidden for benefit is buried and one may not burn it; their ash is forbidden and burned ash is permitted; permitted to cast into a foul place and the like; and see Peleti.
