Almond milk. Beit HaTaharah — what Maharshal ruled, etc. And see in Nitzotz that I wrote that nevertheless one should not forbid placing fowl meat in almond milk without placing almonds beside it, except if before viewers as at great feasts where there is full marit ayin; but in one's house it is permitted — see there. And what Beit HaTaharah and Taz wrote, etc. — one should not forbid eating, etc. — Taz implies from his words that the same law applies to domestic animal meat.
