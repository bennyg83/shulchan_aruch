Salted. Shach wrote must stand in salting until placed on fire and boils siman 69 seif 18; salted until inedible from salt siman 91 seif 5; even for us salting forbids immediately — nevertheless b'dieved if set rennet when not salted so much — many poskim consider excrement even clear — end. {Rama: Peri Chadash: even for us must stand in salting to forbid all; forbid peel measure immediately; if forbidden amount can set alone — permitted}
Day. Meaning by itself — like pickled like cooked.
Sixty. Shach wrote apparently difficult why sixty suffices — setting agent even in thousand not nullified; say only setting agent forbidden in itself like nevelah not nullified; here forbidden only by basar b'chalav mixture — nullifies; even actual basar b'chalav by cooking considered substance — here only salting/pickling rabbinic — Rav holds not substance for any amount setting {Rama: see Darkei Moshe Knesset HaGedolah resolutions}
Lenient. Shach wrote permit even kosher stomach nursed tereifah when clear congealed in great loss — many poskim permit clear — rely on them; though some stringent even dried — here clear congealed lenient in great loss.
And they dry. Shach wrote same for other innards dried like wood; l'chatchila should not.
