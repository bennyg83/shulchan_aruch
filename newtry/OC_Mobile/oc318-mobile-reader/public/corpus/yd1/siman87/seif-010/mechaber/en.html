Milk found in the stomach (l'chatchila one should not place it in the stomach until the milk congeals inside the stomach (Arukh general rule 18 in name of R' Simchah and hagahah Ashiri); but b'dieved one need not be concerned until) that was salted in its stomach or stood in it one day — (then) forbidden to use for rennet: {Rama: And if one used it for rennet — if it is the clear part it forbids all the cheeses until there is sixty in the milk used for rennet against the forbidden stomach; and if there was sixty in the milk all is permitted; and if the stomach was dry it forbids nothing even if there was not sixty in the milk against the stomach (per Rabbenu Tam); and if the stomach was clear from the start and became hard it has the law of clear (Beit Yosef in name of Rashba and poskim); and some are lenient (ShaDa and Beit Yosef in name of Mordechai); and where there is loss one may be lenient; stomach skin — sometimes they salt it and dry it until it becomes like wood and fill it with milk — permitted, for after it dried it is merely like wood and has no meat moisture (Beit Yosef in name of Shibolei Leket).}
