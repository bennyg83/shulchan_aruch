Smoked meat and meat cooked in Tiberias hot springs — one is not flogged for it; and likewise one who cooks meat in milk-water or in milk of a dead animal or male animal, or who cooked blood in milk — is exempt and one is not flogged for eating it because of basar b'chalav: {Rama: And male milk is not called milk at all; and if it fell into a meat pot it does not forbid; but milk of a dead animal and milk-water forbid the food like milk itself, and even for cooking there is room to forbid l'chatchila (so appears in Arukh general rule 41); and some say forbidden to stoke fire under a gentile's pot because they sometimes cook milk sometimes meat and stoking under their pot leads to cooking meat in milk (hagahah Mordechai chapter Kol HaTzlamim); they also wrote one should not mix water used to rinse meat vessels with water used to rinse milk vessels and give before an animal, for they are forbidden for benefit (Mahariu); they also wrote the vessel used for head-washing water — one should not use it, for they make it from ashes on the stove and it is customary for meat and milk to mix there (Maharil); therefore one should forbid using winter-house oven pots because meat and milk sometimes splash on them from pots cooking in ovens (Mahariu); and b'dieved one need not be concerned in all this; and even l'chatchila this is only a general stringency and one who is lenient has not lost.}
