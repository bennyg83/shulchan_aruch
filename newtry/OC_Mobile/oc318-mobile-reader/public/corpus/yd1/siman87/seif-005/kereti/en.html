Even though entangled in sinews — although above siman 86 poskim hold when one struck a chicken on its tail and laid eggs they are forbidden because of eiver min ha'chai; eiver min ha'chai is different, for even if not called meat it is eiver min ha'chai since it draws from the living; but for basar b'chalav what difference if entangled in sinews — it is not called basar b'chalav; and see Peleti on Shach and Peri Chadash.
Behold — and Rashbam's view: as long as the outer shell is not hard, forbidden to cook in milk; Beit Yosef wrote one should practice thus l'chatchila; and see Shach and Peleti settling Rashbam's words without contradicting the Gemara and resolving in Tosafot.
But if it has only, etc. — and Shach wrote even if not entangled it is forbidden; and in Rashba Torat HaBayit it does not appear so; and see Peleti in Beit Yosef that we have no source to forbid with great stringency in rabbinic prohibition.
