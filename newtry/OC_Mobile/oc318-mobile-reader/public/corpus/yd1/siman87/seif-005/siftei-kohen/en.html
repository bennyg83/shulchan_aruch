Even though entangled in sinews, etc. And what appears above siman 86 seif 4 — when he struck the chicken on its tail and it laid eggs forbidden because of eiver min ha'chai if entangled in sinews — although completed in white and yolk, since entangled it is considered chicken meat; this case is different regarding basar b'chalav of fowl which is rabbinic and they were lenient; or this case is different since each alone is permitted — so Acharonim wrote; and this distinction is mentioned in Tosafot and poskim and Rashba from their words to distinguish between nevelah egg above siman 86 seif 3 which is forbidden and here; and it appears to me without this there is no difficulty from striking the chicken on its tail — for although not considered meat, even actual milk would be forbidden because of eiver min ha'chai if not that the verse revealed 'a land flowing with milk and honey' — as above siman 81 s.k. 11; and if so although the verse revealed milk is permitted because not attached to anything and not separating from meat — but eggs entangled and attached certainly remain in their prohibition; greater still some forbid even milk-water because of eiver min ha'chai — as above siman 81; and although we do not hold like them for those reasons explained there — nevertheless certainly entangled eggs are forbidden by all because of eiver min ha'chai; and Rabbenu Tam also wrote even actual eggs would be forbidden because of eiver min ha'chai if not that the verse permitted — as Sha'arim wrote siman 64 and other poskim in his name; and therefore certainly when entangled, since the verse did not reveal, they are forbidden because of eiver min ha'chai — and this is clear.
But if it has only yolk, etc. It appears even if not entangled — all that did not complete except the yolk is like meat itself, as Rashba wrote; in my book I expanded on this and brought that l'chatchila one should not eat in milk even those completed in yolk and white while the shell is still soft even if not entangled — per Rashbam, Mordechai, Agudah first chapter of Beitzah, Sha'arim siman 76, Orach Chaim, and Mahari Weil in hagahah ShaDa; but b'dieved if cooked with milk it appears permitted; but if entangled, even b'dieved forbidden, since Beit HaGefen daf 35 side 3 ruled like R' Yaakov, and so in all Rif manuscripts before us in first chapter of Beitzah, and Sheltei HaGibborim chapter 25 daf 295a in name of Rif; and Ran's view is clear if not misread in chapter 25; and so R' Yonatan brought in Orach Chaim and Kol Bo siman 106; and Rashbam and his supporters clearly hold halachah is like R' Yaakov — otherwise cannot resolve the sugya in first chapter of Beitzah per their words; and Agudah part 81 explicitly; and Yerushalmi ruled explicitly like R' Yaakov; and our Gemara somewhat implies they return to establish the baraita like him, unlike Semag there who left a difficulty that Rif ruled like Ra'avyah; also Beit Yosef and Acharonim wrote plainly all poskim ruled like first tanna — and so I wrote; and I proved 'complete' means completed in yolk and white — if soft shell entangled, even b'dieved forbidden; if not entangled, permitted; but small ones that completed only in yolk, even not entangled, forbidden even b'dieved as like meat itself — and so in the sage general rule 70; except he did not distinguish entangled or not, and from his words there those completed in yolk and white, even entangled, are permitted b'dieved — primary as I wrote; also requires study why he did not emend here; nevertheless it appears even if only yolk completed and entangled, one may be lenient where there is great loss — and such is worthy to rely on Rashi, Tosafot, Rosh, Tur, and Rabbenu Yerucham in such a case; also it appears per them 'entangled' means small ones completed only in yolk entangled in spine meat — not as Beit Yosef wrote per Tur in name of Rashi when separated from spine meat with red streaks; afterward Taz wrote Rashi himself did not explain so in either version; both Gemara versions clearly contradict Beit Yosef — rather as I explained; Maharshal's words chapter 25 siman 52 are wondrous on this and contradict each other and what he wrote first chapter of Beitzah siman 19 — see there; all this is law; but Mahari Weil wrote women do not practice leniency even when fully complete and outer shell fully hard — see there; Maharshal and the Rav in the sage followed this custom; however from Mahari Weil's and their words it is mere stringency; therefore where there is no custom, no concern at all; Bach sought to find the custom is law — his words are not required — see there.
Forbidden to cook them in milk. This wording Taz also wrote; he is uncertain — for to cook alone is obviously permitted, for even fowl itself was explained above siman 63 permitted in cooking even rabbinically; rather 'to cook them' means to eat them. Law of salting eggs found in fowl was explained above siman 87 — see there.
But if he ate them by themselves. Even if entangled still in the cluster — so in Orach Chaim there; and it appears there even if only yolk completed and entangled in cluster, permitted to eat cheese or milk afterward — see there; and 'entangled in cluster' Rashi explained: attached in spine meat.
