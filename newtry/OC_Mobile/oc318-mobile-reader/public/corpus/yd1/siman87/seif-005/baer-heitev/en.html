Entangled. Shach wrote: above siman 86 seif 4 striking chicken — eggs forbidden eiver min ha'chai if entangled — considered meat; different here rabbinic basar b'chalav leniency; also no question — without verse 'land flowing with milk' milk would be eiver min ha'chai; verse permitted milk not attached; entangled eggs remain prohibited; never called meat for this — clear — end; Taz in name of Maharshal: there not taste-dependent — Maharshal himself wrote strained; whenever entangled considered meat for all matters; Mahari Weil: women forbid even hard outer shell; l'chatchila only; b'dieved when shell complete even white membrane suffices — no meat law — end; Shach: nevertheless if only yolk completed and entangled — lenient in great loss.
To cook them. Shach wrote wording imprecise — cooking alone obviously permitted even fowl siman 63; 'to cook them' means to eat them. {Rama: Custom: Acharonim forbid any eggs found in fowl with milk even hard shell — Maharshal, Bach, Beit Yosef; b'dieved completed in white membrane suffices — Taz in name of Mahari Weil}
After them. Shach wrote: even still attached in spine — permitted to eat cheese afterward; salting these eggs explained above siman 87 see there.
