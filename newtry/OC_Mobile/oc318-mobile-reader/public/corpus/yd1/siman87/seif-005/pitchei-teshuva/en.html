In meat. See Maharam Schiff at end of Chullin that he wrote regarding liver that they wanted to fry it in fat and fried it in butter and inclined to be lenient — liver is blood and "kid" the Torah forbade, not blood (he also inclined there that frying is not cooking). And see Pri Megadim that wrote that for this alone one certainly should not rely and be lenient based on "kid" and not blood; and Maharam Schiff did not write this except as a mere side point. And see in responsum Shamayim Rosh siman 285 in Hagahot Kesef Nivchar there what he wrote on words of Maharam Schiff.
