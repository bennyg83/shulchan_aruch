Eggs, etc. — see Shach s.k. 10 who brought Rashbam, Sefer HaTerumah, Agudah, Issur VeHeter that as long as eggs are not complete with hard outer shell one forbids in milk; Rama in Beit Yosef general rule 70 practices l'chatchila like them. Understand their words: baraita says one who slaughters a chicken and finds complete eggs in it — permitted in milk; R' Yaakov says if entangled in sinews forbidden — we see if first tanna holds complete means hard shell, R' Yaakov forbids if entangled — he is lenient from first tanna; how say Beitzah 7a first version — investigate Peleti settling Rashbam and Gemara.
Even though entangled in sinews — Shach challenged s.k. 9 regarding eiver min ha'chai: even if yolk and white completed, if entangled forbidden for eiver min ha'chai; without Torah permission even laid egg would be forbidden for eiver min ha'chai; we have only when not entangled; Peri Chadash agreed; if chicken died and egg entangled — forbidden, eiver min ha'chai prohibition — where does it go without shechitah; perhaps death purifies for tumah but not for eating while it was in eiver min ha'chai — see Peleti on Tosafot nevelah stringency vs. basar b'chalav.
But if it has only yolk, etc. — Shach wrote even not entangled forbidden; Rashba Torat HaBayit does not appear so — he challenged Gemara's resolution with cluster of eggs; Maharshal holds so; per my view why cite cluster if not entangled forbidden as meat with tumah in belly — see Peleti on Rashba permitting small unentangled yolk-only eggs; stringency when small lest people think entangled.
