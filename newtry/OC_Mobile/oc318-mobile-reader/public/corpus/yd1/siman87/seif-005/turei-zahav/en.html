And permitted to eat it in milk. Maharshal challenged: why different from Rashba brought by Tur siman 86 when one struck a chicken on its tail — if entangled in sinews it is like eiver min ha'chai; and he answered that case is different because there is d'oraisa prohibition with no room to distinguish; and he resolved: what Mechaber wrote 'not completed' means not fully completed though they have yolk and white and could be eaten in milk even entangled — because they have no meat taste; but when struck until it laid, while entangled it is like eiver min ha'chai and it does not depend on taste — end; and he wrote this is forced for the halachah; rather as long as entangled in sinews it is considered meat for all matters; and in name of Maharai — women practice prohibition even if outer shell hardened completely; one should not be stringent l'chatchila but b'dieved once shell completed even with white membrane alone suffices and it is not meat at all — end; and see siman 75 regarding salting with meat in name of Rashal.
