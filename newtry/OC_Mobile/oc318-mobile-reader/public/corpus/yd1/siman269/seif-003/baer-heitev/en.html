Concerning the wife of, etc. From Tosafot and Rosh it appears that one should likewise be stringent concerning his father's sister, in accordance with the opinion that this relation is forbidden to Noahides. Bach likewise ruled this way. These are the words of Siftei Kohen.
She is permitted. She is not considered his brother's wife, for his brother's marriage to her did not take effect according to Jewish law when he married her as a non-Jew. Levush writes that in the case of a maternal relative, it appears to him that even if he married her, he must divorce her, since the marriage was contracted in violation of a prohibition and Jewish law is more stringent.
