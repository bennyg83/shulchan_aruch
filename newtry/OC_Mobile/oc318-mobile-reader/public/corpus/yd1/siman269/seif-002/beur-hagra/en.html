So that people should not say, etc. There. See Tosafos there, s.v. “Forbidden relations,” etc. Rambam, however, does not accept their explanation, for he maintains that relatives on the father's side are permitted, as stated in seifim 2 and 3. So it appears there. This is incorrect, for even according to Rambam one must adopt Tosafos' explanation. A mother's sister and the wife of a maternal brother are permitted to a non-Jew but forbidden to a convert, necessarily because of possible confusion. According to Rambam, relations with one's mother and maternal sister are prohibited because people might say that one left a more severe sanctity for a lesser sanctity, whereas a mother's sister and the wife of a maternal brother are prohibited because of possible confusion. Here the Mechaber listed only those women who had already been forbidden to him while he was a non-Jew. The practical difference is that with those women he must divorce even after the fact, whereas with the latter women he need not do so, as stated in seif 2. See what I wrote there.
