His mother, etc. In Choshen Mishpat at the beginning of siman 7, the Rama writes that the same applies if his father is Jewish. See the Sema there.
He is disqualified from judging a Jew. This applies even to monetary law, and specifically when exercising coercive authority. If the litigant accepts him as a judge, however, it is permitted. Thus wrote the Rosh and the Tur, and likewise the Atteres Zahav.
And for chalitzah, etc. Meaning, to serve as a judge on a chalitzah panel.
Unless both his father and mother are Jewish. In Even HaEzer, siman 169, seif 2, it is explained that some say he is qualified if his father is Jewish.
