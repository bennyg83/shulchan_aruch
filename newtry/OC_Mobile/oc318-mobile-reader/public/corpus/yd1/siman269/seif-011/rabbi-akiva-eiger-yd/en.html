(Siman 269 seif 11) Disqualified to judge Israel. Rambam wrote (chapter 4 of Hilchot Melachim halachah 4): We do not appoint a king from the congregation of converts until his mother is from Israel; and not only for kingship but for any authority in Israel, etc.; and even one appointed over the water distribution that they divide from it to the fields — and all the more so a judge or nasi, etc., even after many generations. Copied in Maharikash — see Hagahot Maimoniyot.
