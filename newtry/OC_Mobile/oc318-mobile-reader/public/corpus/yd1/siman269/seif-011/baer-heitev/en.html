His mother. At the beginning of Choshen Mishpat, siman 7, Rema writes that the same applies if his father is Jewish. See Sema there.
To adjudicate. This applies even to monetary law, but only when he would adjudicate coercively. If the litigants accept him as their judge, it is permitted. Thus wrote Rosh and Tur, and Levush likewise ruled.
And for chalitzah. This means serving as a judge for chalitzah. Even HaEzer, siman 169, se'if 2, explains that some authorities hold that if his father is Jewish, he is eligible. See there.
