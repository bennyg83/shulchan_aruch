The secondary forbidden relations, etc. See Even HaEzer, siman 15, regarding which relations are forbidden by Torah law and which are rabbinic secondary relations.
Or her great-granddaughter through her daughter. This is the reading in the editions of the Mechaber and the Rambam. The Atteres Zahav emended it to read, 'or her granddaughter through her daughter,' because he found it difficult that the first clause mentions her mother's mother. He therefore wrote: A man may marry a female convert together with her mother's mother or her daughter's daughter. End quote. This is an error, for her mother's mother and her daughter's daughter are forbidden by Torah law, as explained in Even HaEzer, siman 15, seifim 13 and 15, and Scripture explicitly states, 'You shall not take her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter.' Rather, it is obvious that the first clause should read: A man may marry a female convert together with her mother's mother's mother. The Derishah also cites the Rambam's wording this way. This is clear.
