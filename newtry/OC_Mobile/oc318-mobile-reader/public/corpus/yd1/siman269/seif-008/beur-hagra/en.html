And some forbid, etc. As Rashi explained above — he challenges also from his father's wife and answers "this is R' Meir," etc., and Tosafot in Yevamos there s.v. eshet aviv, etc.; however, etc., and so the Tur, and it is good to be stringent according to both opinions; nevertheless there is no difference whether his conception was not in sanctity and his birth in sanctity, or his conception and birth not in sanctity, and a son whose conception was not in sanctity, etc. — so Ramban agreed, and so is Rambam and Shulchan Aruch unstated, and Tosafot there s.v. nasa, etc. (Likkut) And some forbid his father's wife. This is from the Tur's words and as written above; but the Rav did not examine here, for the Tur disputes Rambam greatly — for according to him who ruled also like R' Eliezer, it is forbidden also with his sister, father's sister, and mother's sister from the father, as written in the braita there; and there is no leniency on the father's side except from the side of marriage, such as his father's brother's wife and a son of his father's brother's wife from a convert mother, as Tosafot wrote and as above; and further that his sister, father's sister, and mother's sister from the mother he must divorce, as written above, and likewise his father's wife he must divorce since he ruled like both opinions; and further he ruled like R' Shimon that from the father and from the mother it is forbidden like from the mother alone, unlike Rambam who wrote two twin brothers, etc. (end).
