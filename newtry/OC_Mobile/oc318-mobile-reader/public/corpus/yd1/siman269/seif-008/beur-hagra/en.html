**(Collection) And some forbid regarding a brother's wife.** This is from the words of the Tur, and as written there; but the Rav did not examine carefully here, for the Tur disputes the Rambam a great deal — for according to him, who also ruled like Rabbi Eliezer, it is forbidden also with his sister, and the father's sister, and the mother's sister from the father, as written in the baraita there; and there is no kula on the father's side except from the aspect of marriage, such as the wife of his brother from his father; and likewise regarding her — the sister of a convert-woman from the father, as Tosafot wrote, and as it appears to me. And further: his sister, and his father's sister, and his mother's sister from the mother — he must divorce, as written there; and likewise with his father's wife he must divorce, for he ruled according to both of them. And further: he ruled like Rabbi Shimon that from the father and from the mother it is forbidden like from the mother alone — unlike the Rambam who wrote “two twin brothers” etc. (end of quote).
