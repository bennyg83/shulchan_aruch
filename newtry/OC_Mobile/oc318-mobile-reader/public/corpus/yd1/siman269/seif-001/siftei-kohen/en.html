By Torah law, etc. Meaning, by Torah law a convert may marry his mother or his maternal sister, and certainly a paternal relative, because a convert who converted is regarded as a newborn child. The Sages, however, prohibited maternal relatives, since descendants of Noah are also forbidden to maternal relatives, so that people should not say, Heaven forbid, that they have passed from a more stringent sanctity to a more lenient sanctity. They did not decree regarding paternal relatives, as stated below in seif 7, because a non-Jew has no legally recognized paternity. This is the view of the Rambam and the Mechaber.
