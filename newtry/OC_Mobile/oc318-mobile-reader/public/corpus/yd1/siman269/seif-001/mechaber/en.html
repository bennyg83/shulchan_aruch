Prohibition of forbidden relations with converts and their law regarding testimony and chalitzah. And it contains 11 seifim.
By Torah law it is permitted for a convert to marry his mother or his maternal sister who converted; but the Sages forbade this matter so they should not say, "We came from a severe sanctity to a light sanctity"; and likewise a convert who had relations with his mother or sister while she was still a gentile — behold it is like one who had relations with a gentile woman.
