They are liable because of a brother's wife. The Beis Yosef maintains that even if they are not twins, since they were born in sanctity they are liable for the prohibition of a brother's wife. This is also the view of the Bach. This requires study, for the Mechaber's words clearly imply that this applies specifically to twins. The Atteres Zahav likewise implies this, and it is also explicit in the Rambam and the Maggid Mishneh at the end of chapter 4 of Hilchos Issurei Biah. See there.
But they do not perform chalitzah, etc. By Torah law they are brothers only through their mother and not through their father. Although they are twins, they were conceived outside sanctity, and there is no legally recognized paternity here. For chalitzah and yibbum, paternal brothers are required, as explained in Even HaEzer, siman 156. If both their conception and birth were in sanctity, however, they are fully Jewish in every respect. The Tur writes likewise, and this is obvious.
