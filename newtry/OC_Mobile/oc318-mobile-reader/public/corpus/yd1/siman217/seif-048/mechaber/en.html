One who vowed or swore that he would not laugh any laughter is forbidden to cast lots and to gamble with any person on his account; and likewise forbidden to wager with his fellow in what they call apostat. (Responsum Chazeh HaTenufah.) {Rama: A community that decreed not to pray in a minyan on account of some matter — they are forbidden to read from the Torah, for every holy matter is called prayer (Responsum of Raash, kelal 3, siman 9). One who vowed not to accept a deposit is forbidden to lend on a pledge to an Israelite (Maharil). However, it appears that we follow his intention, as will be explained in siman 218 (my humble opinion). One who vowed or swore to fast a set number of days, and an obligatory fast occurred — it counts for him toward the number of days. And likewise one who vowed to go to the cemetery or to a place, and it happened that he went there on business — he has fulfilled his vow (Responsum of Maharil, siman 118). And see above, siman 239, more on this.}
