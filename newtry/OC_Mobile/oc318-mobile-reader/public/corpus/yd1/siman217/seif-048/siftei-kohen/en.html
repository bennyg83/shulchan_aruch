Tur: one who vows from "the born" or from "those who will be born" is forbidden whether in those already born or those who will be born afterward; and he is permitted only in birds and fish that lay eggs and do not bear live young — end. This is from chapter 4 of Nedarim. And Perishah wrote: even though it is something not yet in the world — and I wrote above siman 215 in Derishah regarding one who vowed to give tithe from profit that there is nothing to it since it is not yet in the world — perhaps this is considered more "in the world" since it will certainly come, for such is the way of the world — end. But there is no need to strain, for even though a person cannot consecrate something not yet in the world, a person can nevertheless forbid to himself something not yet in the world, as above end of siman 204.
