He is permitted fish brine and fish sauce. This applies specifically to liquid that had already emerged from the fish before he made the vow. Liquid that emerges afterward is forbidden if he said, “These fish are forbidden to me as a konam to taste,” “to eat,” or “these fish are a konam,” as stated above in siman 216, seif 9. The Bach writes this, as does the Perishah in the name of the Maharshal. This is the primary ruling.
