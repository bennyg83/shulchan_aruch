And he is permitted in kids, etc. Although above, siman 177 seif 38, we say that one who gives money to his fellow to buy fruit may buy with it whatever kind he wishes, even living creatures, here it is different because he said “fruits of the year.” So wrote the Bach, and so the Perishah concludes; this is primary.
He is forbidden in all of them. The Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Ran that this is specifically where he forbade them upon himself only for a period he can endure. But forever, it is not a vow at all, because he cannot fulfill it; the later authorities brought this. Since the Ran and later authorities wrote this regarding the latter clause of “growths of the year,” it implies that in the first clause it is a vow even forever, because since he is permitted in kids and lambs he can fulfill his vow. So I wrote below, siman 232 s.k. 8, in the name of Teshuvot Maimoniyyot; see there.
