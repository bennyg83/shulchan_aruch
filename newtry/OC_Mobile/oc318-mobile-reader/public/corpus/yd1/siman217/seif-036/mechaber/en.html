One who vows from sun-seers is forbidden even in the blind, for he did not intend except regarding one whom the sun sees; excluding fish and fetuses. {Rama: And even if he says he did not intend thus, it does not help (Beit Yosef in the name of Raash), since the language is certainly so. And he is permitted in everything that is not a living being, even though the sun sees it (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot).} And if he vowed from re'im hachamah, he is permitted regarding the blind.
