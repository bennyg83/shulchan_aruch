Whoever he is, etc. — for in vows we follow the time of utterance, not the time of the vow taking effect; and see above siman 234 seif 34. And it appears that in all other matters the law is likewise — e.g., if he said "after such a time the inhabitants of such-and-such city shall be forbidden to me," and in the interim one person moved his residence from that city, he is forbidden, since at the time of the utterance he was from that city. And likewise if at the time of utterance he was not there, even though at the time the vow takes effect he is there, he is permitted. And so for all similar cases.
And even if he said, etc. — see above beginning of siman 208 and siman 232 seif 14 s.k. 30.
