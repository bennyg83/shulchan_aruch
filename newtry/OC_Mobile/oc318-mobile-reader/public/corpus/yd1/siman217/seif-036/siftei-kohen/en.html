And even if he said, etc. See above at the beginning of siman 208, and siman 232 seif 14, s.k. 30.
And if he vowed from those who see the sun, etc. The Bach wrote that there is no distinction between one who vowed from “those seen by the sun” and “those who see the sun,” and he is permitted in blind people only where he vowed from “those who see the sun”; see there.
