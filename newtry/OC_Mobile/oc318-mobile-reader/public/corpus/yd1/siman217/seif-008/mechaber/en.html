In a place where if a person sends an agent to buy meat for him, the agent says to him, “I found only fish” — if he swore or vowed in that place regarding meat, he is forbidden even in fish meat; and likewise everything similar. And in every place, one who vows from meat is forbidden in the meat of birds, sacrifices, head, legs, windpipe, and heart; and permitted in locusts. And if at the time he vowed it is shown that he did not intend except regarding animal meat [or only poultry and animal meat], he is permitted in fish meat, even in a place where the agent would have consulted regarding them. (And even poultry meat is also permitted, for he intended only animal meat) (so the language of Tur appears, and so they lean on the words of Ran).
