He said: seafarers shall be forbidden to me after thirty days. Meaning that after thirty days seafarers shall become forbidden to him.
Whoever is... In vows we follow the time of the declaration, not the time when the vow takes effect. See siman 234:34. The same appears true in other cases. If one says, “After a certain time the residents of a particular city shall be forbidden to me,” and meanwhile one resident moves away, he remains forbidden because at the time of the declaration he was a resident. Conversely, one who was not then a resident remains permitted even if he lives there when the vow takes effect.
