It is forbidden to look by the sunlight. Meaning, it is forbidden to benefit from the light of the sun. This is also implied by the Beit Yosef there and by the Darchei Moshe.
The Tur rules that one who vows to derive no benefit from those born or from those who will be born is prohibited regarding both those already born and those born afterward. He is permitted only regarding birds and fish, which lay eggs and do not give birth in the ordinary manner. This is from chapter 4 of Nedarim. The Perishah asks that future offspring are something not yet in existence. He answers that they may be regarded as more nearly existent because their coming is certain in the ordinary course of the world. This difficulty is unnecessary. Although a person cannot consecrate something not yet in existence, he can prohibit upon himself something not yet in existence, as stated above at the end of siman 204. [Citation 215.]
