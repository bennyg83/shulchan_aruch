One who vowed or swore regarding salted food — if their custom is to call all salted items “salted,” he is forbidden in all salted items, even if it is salted only to preserve for a time. And if their custom is to call “salted” only salted fish, he is forbidden only in salted fish (Tur, Raash, and Ran in the name of Yerushalmi). One who vowed or swore regarding pickled food — if their custom is to call all pickled items “pickled,” he is forbidden in all of them; and if their custom is to call “pickled” only pickled vegetables, he is forbidden only in pickled vegetables. And likewise everything similar. If some of the people of the place call it thus and some do not call it thus, we do not follow the majority; rather, behold this is a doubt regarding vows, and any doubt regarding vows is resolved to be stringent.
