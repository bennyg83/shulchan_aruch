The law of one who vows regarding cooked food, salted food, pot-cooked food, and the like, and how we follow vows according to common speech; it contains forty-eight se’ifim.
One who vowed or swore regarding cooked food — if in that place, in that language, and at that time they call even roasted and boiled food “cooked,” he is forbidden in all of them. And if their custom is to call “cooked” only meat that was cooked in water and spices, he is permitted in roasted and boiled food. And likewise regarding smoked, fried, and food cooked in the hot springs of Tiberias and the like — we follow the language of the people of the city.
