And to parboil. — meaning partially cooked but not fully cooked; but overcooked is considered mevushal everywhere — so Tosafot and Ran; and Bach ruled thus; and this is the main point.
Rabbeinu Yerucham: in vows we follow the language of people; and if there is no human language, such as one who vowed in the holy tongue in a place where people do not speak the holy tongue — we follow the language of the Torah; so is the plain meaning in chapter HaShutafin — end, brought by Beit Yosef in his last section.
