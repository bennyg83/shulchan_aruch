See Orach Chaim 179. This appears in all editions and in the Levush, but it is an error. It should read siman 570.
After the Rambam sets forth in chapter 9 all the laws written in this siman, he states that in all these matters and those like them, the law of one who vows and one who swears is the same. The Beit Yosef cites this, and it is self-evident. See above, siman 239, where it is explained that the law of an oath equals that of a vow in every respect except for several distinctions explained there. [Citation 12.]
