If he said, “Konam vegetables are upon me,” he is forbidden only in what is eaten raw. If he said, “Vegetables of the pot are upon me,” he is forbidden even in what is pickled in it. If he said, “Vegetable that is cooked in a pot,” he is forbidden only in what is cooked in it; however, he is forbidden in everything cooked in that pot, even gourds (meaning: a type of vegetable on which large fruits grow; Arukh). For anything regarding which the agent consulted, it is included in that species. And if he said to his agent, “Bring me a vegetable,” and he finds only gourds, the agent may consult to say, “Are gourds acceptable?” But if he vowed from gourds, he is permitted in other vegetables.
