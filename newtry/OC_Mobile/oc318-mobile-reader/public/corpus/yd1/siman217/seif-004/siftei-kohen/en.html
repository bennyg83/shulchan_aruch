Vegetables, etc. See below, s.k. 15.
Only those eaten raw. This means vegetables that are also eaten raw.
Forbidden even in pickled, etc. meaning even those eaten only when pickled; but in any case those vegetables are also forbidden cooked; so Maharshal wrote: "even in pickled ; meaning and all the more so forbidden in cooked vegetables, whether they are eaten as they are raw or are not eaten raw; but plain vegetable is certainly permitted to eat" ; end. And Perishah challenged him from the Rosh's language in seif 10 ; and it is no challenge, for the Rosh means: one who says "vegetable pot upon me" is not forbidden in vegetable eaten only by cooking, such as squash; but what is eaten by pickling is forbidden in every case. And Bach also brings Maharshal's words and writes "and it is obvious"; I have since seen in a last pamphlet that challenged Perishah on this.
Nevertheless, he is forbidden, etc. Squash is not included in the unqualified term “vegetables” or “pot vegetables,” which denotes vegetables eaten raw or pickled, whereas squash is eaten only after cooking. The Derishah writes likewise.
