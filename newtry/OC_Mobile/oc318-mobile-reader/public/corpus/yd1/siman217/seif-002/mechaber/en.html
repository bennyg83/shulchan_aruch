One who vowed from pot-cooked food is forbidden only in matters that are boiled in a pot, such as groats and pancakes and the like. If he forbade himself from everything that goes down into a pot, he is forbidden in everything cooked in a pot. One who vowed from what goes into an oven is forbidden only in bread; and if he said, “All oven work is upon me,” he is forbidden in everything made in an oven.
