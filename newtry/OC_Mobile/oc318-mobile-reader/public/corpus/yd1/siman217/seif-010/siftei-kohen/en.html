He is forbidden a thick dish of groats. This is a cooked dish made from groats.
He is forbidden garlic. The term makkafah includes anything congealed and thick. Garlic is also prepared as a thick food, since it is mashed with a small amount of water and eaten in that form. Ran.
Spices, etc. Anything added to a pot to impart flavor, such as garlic, onions, and similar items, is included in the term “spices.” With these items, it is relevant to distinguish between raw and cooked forms. Regarding pepper and similar spices, however, no such distinction applies. Beis Yosef and the later authorities.
He is permitted field vegetables. It appears to me that nowadays he is also forbidden field vegetables, because ordinary speech includes them in the term “vegetables.”
