For us. For we speak in the vernacular; accordingly, if one vowed from tirosh in the Holy Tongue, he is forbidden in wine and permitted in all kinds of sweets, for we do not call tirosh anything but wine in the language of the Torah — end of the words of the Ran. And the same law applies if one vowed from tirosh in the vernacular: he is forbidden only in wine and not in other kinds of sweets, for they are not included in the language.
