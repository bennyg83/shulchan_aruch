For us. For we are accustomed in the gentile language — accordingly, if he vowed from tirosh in the holy tongue, he is forbidden in wine and permitted in all types of sweetened beverages, for we do not read tirosh except as wine in the language of the Torah — so said the Ran. And likewise if he vowed from tirosh in the gentile language, he is forbidden only in wine and not in other types of sweetened beverages, for they are not included in that term.
