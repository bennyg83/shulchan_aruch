One who vowed from milk is permitted in whey (meaning: water separated from milk when it is curdled, called sirvo) (Rambam, perek 9 of Nazir, halacha 8). And there is one who says that if they call whey by the name of milk, such as if they call it "whey of milk," it is forbidden (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Ran). From milk he is permitted in cheese; from cheese he is permitted in milk; and he is forbidden in it whether salted, unripe, moist, or dry.
