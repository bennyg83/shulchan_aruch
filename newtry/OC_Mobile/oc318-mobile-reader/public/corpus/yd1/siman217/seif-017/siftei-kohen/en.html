For us. Since we ordinarily speak the local non-Hebrew language, if a person vows against tirosh using the Holy Tongue, he is forbidden wine but permitted all kinds of sweet foods and beverages, because in the language of the Torah we use tirosh only for wine. End quote from the Ran. Likewise, if he vows against tirosh in the local language, he is forbidden only wine and not other sweet foods and beverages, because they are not included in that usage.
