He is forbidden all of them. The Beis Yosef writes in the name of the Ran that this applies specifically when he forbade them to himself for a period he is capable of enduring. If he forbade them forever, it is not a vow at all, because it is impossible to observe. The later authorities cite this. Since the Ran and the later authorities state this regarding the latter case of “the produce of a year,” it follows that in the first case the vow is valid even forever, because he remains permitted kids and lambs and can therefore observe his vow.
