One who vowed from dates is permitted in date honey. {Rama: Even though the name of dates is upon it and it is called date honey, since their form changed from what they were initially — unlike whey of milk (Ran in the name of Rashba). And furthermore, what is called date honey is only to distinguish it from other honey (Ran).} From date honey he is permitted in dates. From istoniyot (meaning: bad grapes left on the vine in autumn days, from which they make vinegar) he is permitted in the vinegar that comes from them.
