A law of a non-Jewish and Jewish midwife; it contains 2 se’ifim.
A non-Jewish midwife should not deliver for a Jewess in private, even if she is an expert. And likewise, she should not nurse a Jewish child in her house, even if others are standing over her. But in the house of a Jew, it is permitted for her to deliver and to nurse if others stand over her or go out and come in — provided that he does not leave the child alone with her at night.
