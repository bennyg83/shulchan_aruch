Even if she is, etc. Tur. This is also implied by the Talmud there: "And Rabbi Meir," etc.; "like that woman," etc. It follows that even an expert is prohibited. The Sages disagree only when others are standing over her.
And even other people, etc. This means both in the case of a midwife and in that of a wet nurse. Rosh writes there that the midwife must necessarily be in the Jewish woman's domain, yet we still require others to stand over her. The same applies to a wet nurse. These are also the words of Tosafot there, beginning with the words "Foreign worship," etc. Their words imply, however, that even in the non-Jewish woman's domain the only requirement is that others stand over her, for they write: "This means even in the Jewish woman's domain," etc.
Or, etc. Tosafot and Rosh there. This is equivalent to having others stand over her, as stated there in Avodah Zarah 61a and in Chullin 3a.
