Assist in childbirth. It is clear that here too both conditions are required: others must be standing over her, and it must specifically take place in a Jew's house. Taz.
An expert. We do not say that she would not damage her professional reputation, as we say below in siman 155 regarding an expert physician. So wrote the Beit Yosef. The Bach explains that a midwife can excuse herself by saying that the child was a stillbirth and the like. The Shach writes: This astonishes me, because in the Jerusalem Talmud, in the chapter Ein Maamidin, it states that just as an expert physician is permitted, the same applies to a skilled midwife. Nor does any posek state that an expert midwife is forbidden. This requires further study. End of the Shach's words. See below, siman 81, seif 7, in the Rama's gloss.
