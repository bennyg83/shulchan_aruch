Child. The Torah is my religion in the midst of the Holy Spirit, which is to say that others are standing in the house of Israel. TJ:
Expert. We do not say that she will not damage her own reputation, as below siman 155 regarding an expert physician. So writes the Beit Yosef. The Bach writes that the reason is that a midwife can say it was a miscarriage and the like. This is astonishing to me, for in the Yerushalmi, Perek Ein Maamidin, it says that just as with a skilled physician it is permitted, so too with a wise midwife. Nor is it mentioned in any posek that an expert midwife is forbidden; this requires study. Thus the Shach. See above siman 81 seif 7 in the gloss.
