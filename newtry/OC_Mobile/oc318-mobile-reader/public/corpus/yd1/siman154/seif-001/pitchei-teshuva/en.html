Expert. Be'er Heitev in the name of Shach; and see responsum Beit Yaakov siman 104, who wrote that what the Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote — forbidden with a midwife even if she is expert — deals with one who is not expert only for midwifery and not for other matters; therefore she ruins her reputation, as appears from Tosafot in Avodah Zarah 27 — even if he is expert for a sick person, if he is not expert for other matters it is forbidden because he ruins his reputation; so too the law with a midwife. Therefore there is no difficulty in what Shach challenged from the Yerushalmi that with an expert midwife it is permitted — there it deals with one who is expert also for other matters (and as written above siman 155). He also wrote: just as they said in the Gemara that an important person may be healed, so too the law with a midwife — if she is an important woman it is permitted; see there.
