A Jewess should not nurse a non-Jewish child, even for payment (unless she has much milk and it causes her pain — it is permitted for her to nurse him) (Mordechai and Agudah, at the beginning of perek Ein Ma’amidin). And she should not deliver for a non-Jewish woman unless she is known as a midwife, for then it is permitted; and this is specifically for payment, and on a weekday. (It is forbidden to teach a non-Jew a trade) (Hagahot Ashiri there, and Yerushalmi).
