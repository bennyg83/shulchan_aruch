Be'er Heitev; and see responsum Chasam Sofer siman 131 regarding the district governor in the land of Turkey over all cities and villages to hire them an expert midwife who learned before their sages specifically, and even though they have alert animals — nevertheless they specifically need to hire her who stood in trial before the sages of the doctors. Behold some villages did not find by the hour a learned one except a Jewish woman, and the questioner asked if she is permitted to hire herself to them for midwifery whether on a weekday or Shabbat; he answered: since they have several expert animals, otherwise it is obvious it is permitted even without the reason of enmity, as Maharil writes in the name of the Ramban who acted in practice. For halacha one should rely on the Ramban who acted in practice. If so, in our case it is permitted even on Shabbat in what there is no Shabbat desecration — i.e., with one sitting on the birthstool who already uprooted the fetus, as Magen Avraham siman 330 s.k. 6 writes. Indeed to cut the umbilical cord, which is a Torah labor, command the healthy gentile woman standing at her side to cut; and if in this enmity there is concern of danger to life, there is to permit even Torah labor — although from Magen Avraham siman 334 it implies specifically extinguishing which is a labor that all the more so was permitted, and not a full labor; however one who examines Eruvin 44b in the mishnah and Shulchan Aruch there will see explicitly that even Torah labor was permitted if impossible without it; so his words; see there.
