It is forbidden to teach, etc. The Jerusalem Talmud, cited by Hagahot Ashri there in se'if 6, in the passage beginning, "It is explained in the Jerusalem Talmud," etc. The Jerusalem Talmud concludes with the incident of two guilds in Gero, glassmakers and cupmakers. The cupmakers taught their craft and were uprooted; the glassmakers did not teach it and endured. Ran cites this at the beginning of the second chapter.
