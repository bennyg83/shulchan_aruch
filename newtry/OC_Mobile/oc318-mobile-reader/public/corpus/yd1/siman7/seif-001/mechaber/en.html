**One who fixes a knife in a wheel—whether it is permitted to slaughter with it. And it has one seif:**
A person may fix a knife in a wheel of stone or wood and spin the wheel with his hand or foot, and place there the neck of the animal or bird until it is slaughtered around the wheel. And if the water is what spins the wheel and one places the neck opposite it at the time it spins and it is slaughtered—this is invalid (Rambam's language there law 13). And if a person released the water until it came and spun the wheel and he slaughtered around it—this is kosher b'dieved, for behold it comes from the person's power. In what case are these matters stated—in the first revolution that is from the person's power; but from the second revolution and onward—it is invalid, for behold it is not from the person's power but from the water's momentum.
