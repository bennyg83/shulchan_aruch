The Tur in the name of Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilchot Talmud Torah, law 3, from the baraita there. Rambam explains that outside three parasangs one may rule even without permission, so that the baraita does not contradict the incident in which Rav Huna did not rule in Charta of Argiz. Tosafot resolves it as Rama writes in the gloss.
