The greater person is on the right. He walks slightly behind him and angled to the side, while the lesser person is on the left. Beyond four cubits all is permitted. The Beit Yosef cites the Rashba that some communal leaders stand when the prayer leader raises the Torah scroll and shows the writing to the people, because this is part of honoring the Torah scroll, and this is practiced in most places. Ramban writes similarly on the verse, 'Cursed is one who does not uphold this Torah.' See Orach Chaim, siman 146.
