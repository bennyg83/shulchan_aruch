Three who were walking on the road — the rabbi in the middle, the greater to his right, the lesser to the left: {Rama: That they do not honor on roads except at the entrance fit for a mezuzah — when each goes his way and they are not one group; but if one group, they honor on the road (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 6, Tosafot, chapter Bameh Behemah); and in a place of danger one need not honor at all (Mordechai in the name of Rabeiah, chapter Sheloshah Sheachlu).}
