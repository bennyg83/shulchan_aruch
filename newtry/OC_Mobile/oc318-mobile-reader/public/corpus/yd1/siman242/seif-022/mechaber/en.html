If he saw his rabbi transgress words of Torah, he says to him, "You taught us, my rabbi, thus and thus": {Rama: And if he wished to transgress only a rabbinic prohibition, he must still protest (Terumat HaDeshen siman 43). One who sees his rabbi doing an act about which he can challenge — if a Torah prohibition, he challenges before the act; if rabbinic, he lets him do the act and afterward challenges, since he does not know certainly he transgresses but only that he can challenge (Teshuvat Or Zaru'a netiv 2).}
