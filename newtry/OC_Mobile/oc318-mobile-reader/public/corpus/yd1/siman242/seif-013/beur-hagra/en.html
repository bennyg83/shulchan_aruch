Or capital cases, etc. As stated there: according to whom does this go, etc. The Rambam ruled like Rabbi Yehudah. See Rashba, who wrote at length about this, and the Kesef Mishneh brought him there. Nevertheless, the law here is simple from Rav's statement in Ketubot 10b: one who ate dates should not issue rulings. See Tosafot there and in Keritot there. Perhaps because of Tosafot's difficulty, the Rambam was compelled to rule like Rabbi Yehudah even though we rule like Rabbi Eliezer.
