A student who did not reach the level of ruling yet rules — behold he is a fool, wicked, arrogant; and concerning him it is said, "for she has cast down many wounded": {Rama: And young students who jump to rule and sit at the head to aggrandize themselves before amei ha'aretz — they increase dispute and destroy the world and extinguish the lamp of Torah (Tur from Rambam); and every person should be careful not to rule when intoxicated with wine or other intoxicants, even in a plain matter if not clear in decisors and "go read at the rav" applies (Terumat HaDeshen 42, Maharik shoresh 170, and see Choshen Mishpat siman 7).}
