Before his teacher. Shevut Yaakov advises every qualified decisor to consult books before issuing a ruling, because the accepted works of the sages are one's teachers. This prevents error and suspicion, especially in places lacking learned people. Pri Megadim gives similar guidance.
If he is distant from his teacher. Beit Yaakov discusses a ruling completed while the teacher was outside three parasangs and the teacher then arrived. If the decision was already completed, no further permission is required; if deliberation was still underway, the student should consult him. Even where formally permitted, piety favors asking.
