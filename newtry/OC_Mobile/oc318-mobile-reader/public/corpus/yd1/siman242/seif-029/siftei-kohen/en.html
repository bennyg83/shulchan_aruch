Specifically his phlegm, etc. Rabbeinu Yerucham explains the Gemara's expression as referring specifically to phlegm. Rashi, however, explains that even ordinary spitting in one's teacher's presence is forbidden; one should move away or absorb it in his garment. Nedarim teaches that one should not spit anything before his teacher, except after eating gourd or porridge, which may create a dangerous lead-like strand; in such a case he should spit it out because of danger to life.
