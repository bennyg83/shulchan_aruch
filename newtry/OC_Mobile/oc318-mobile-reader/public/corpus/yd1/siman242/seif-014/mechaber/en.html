Any sage who reached the level of ruling and does not rule — behold he withholds Torah and places stumbling blocks before the many; and concerning him it is said, "and many are those she has struck down": {Rama: The matter of ordination practiced today is so people know he reached the level of ruling and what he rules is with his ordainer's permission; therefore if his rabbi already died he need not ordination, and likewise for a student-colleague as explained above where permission is not needed, ordination is not needed (Rivash siman 271, not like Nachalat Avot, chapter Shenu Chachamim); and some say one not ordained — we do not teach from him and his gittin and chalitzot are nothing; and one should be concerned for gittin and chalitzot he gave unless known to all he is expert to the public but from humility does not seek greatness (Maharad Cohen siman 20, Mahariv 85, 122); and some disagree and are lenient (Teshuvat Rivash there); and in a place of agunah one may be lenient if he already gave gittin and chalitzot — but not otherwise, for Israel's custom is Torah — so it appears to me; and further it appears permitted to give ordination to one to arrange gittin even though by ancient ordination law it would not be so — now it is only taking permission and permitted.}
