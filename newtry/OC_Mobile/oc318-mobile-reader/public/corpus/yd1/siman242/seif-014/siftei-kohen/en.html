And although from the law of semikhah in earlier days. The Rambam wrote in chapter 4 of Hilkhot Sanhedrin: this is where he is fit for all matters. How so? An outstanding sage who is fit to rule in the entire Torah, the court may give him semikhah and give him permission to judge but not to rule in issur and heter, or give him permission in issur and heter but not to judge monetary cases, etc. Semikhah nowadays is only the taking of permission, and for whatever he is fit, he is fit. Darkhei Moshe. See at the end of Responsa Maharlabach, Responsa Mahari Bei Rav and Responsa ibn Chaviv, several kuntresim concerning semikhah.
