Although according to the law of ordination in former times. The Rambam writes in chapter 4 of Hilchot Sanhedrin that the candidate must be fit for the authority granted. A preeminent sage fit to rule in the entire Torah may be ordained with authority to adjudicate but not rule in ritual law, or to rule in ritual law but not monetary law. Today's semichah is merely the granting of permission, and it applies only to matters for which the recipient is qualified. See the responsa of Mahari Bei Rav and Maharal ibn Chaviv concerning semichah.
