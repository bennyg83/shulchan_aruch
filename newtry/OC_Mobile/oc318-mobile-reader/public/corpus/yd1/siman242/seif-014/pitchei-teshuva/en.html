Pitchei Teshuva suggests that after Taz, Shach, Magen Avraham, and later commentaries explained the reasons, responsible rulings from Shulchan Aruch together with those works may be acceptable.

Shelah says that once the answer is clear, the decisor should respond immediately. All agree that one must not delay a ruling needed to permit a woman to her husband.
