Who establishes. The Bach wrote the reason: since he establishes a study session for himself, he is thereby disputing his teacher's authority.
Halachic ruling. The Shach wrote that this is not implied by Maharik's words, for the proof they brought for this law, that such is the way of Torah from the days of the Tannaim, Amoraim, and Geonim in many places, may be understood as referring to when permission was taken, or when he died, as below in seif 4. This requires study; end quote.
