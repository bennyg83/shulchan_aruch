And not after his death. For Abaye, when quoting in the name of Rabbah — even when he was with Rav Yosef — would afterward say "the Master did not say so." Similarly Rav Mordechai to Rav Ashi, for they were their preeminent teachers. See Gittin 59a and the implication there. This is what is stated at the end of Shabbos and its implication: "I said before Rebbi — and who is he," etc.; the Gemara explains who he was. Similarly regarding his father, as stated in Kiddushin (31b): "a sage changes," as in this case, etc. After his death — it was that Mar bar R' Ashi sat at the head; and there: "after his death, how?" etc. Rather, thus he said: "my father my teacher said," etc.
