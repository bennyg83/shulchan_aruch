It is forbidden for a student to call his rabbi by name, in life or death; and even to call others by his name is forbidden if it is a remarkable name people are not accustomed to use: {Rama: But a name people are accustomed to — permitted to mention when not before his rabbi (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam); and all this when mentioning only his name alone; but permitted to say "Rabbi, my teacher so-and-so" (so implied from Rashi, chapter Chelek).}
