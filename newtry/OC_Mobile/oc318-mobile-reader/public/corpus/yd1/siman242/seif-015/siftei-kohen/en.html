It is permitted to mention, etc. Meaning, to use that name when calling other people. See above, siman 240, seif 2, seif katan 3.
But it is permitted to say, etc. This appears to apply only outside his presence. The Darkei Moshe derives it from Rashi concerning Elisha, where the discussion is outside the teacher's presence. In his presence one should not mention his name at all, but call him 'Rabbi.' This is the practice.
