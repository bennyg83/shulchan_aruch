One who says to his fellow, "I do not accept from you unless you are like Moses" — they flog him for contempt: {Rama: And a Torah scholar who stated a halachic matter in a matter pertaining to himself (Tosafot in the name of Rabbenu Tam, Nimmukei Yosef) — if he said it before the act, they listen to him; if not, they do not listen (Gemara, chapter HaArel, beginning Yevamot 77, Tosafot, Nimmukei Yosef there, Mordechai, end of siman 245); and specifically if he said "thus I received"; but if he says a matter by reasoning and shows faces for his words and it appears so, they listen to him (Nimmukei Yosef in the name of Ritva); but they do not listen to him alone lest he compare matters that are not similar; but if it is plain, they listen to him (Rabbeinu Yerucham end of netiv 2, in the name of Rosh).}
