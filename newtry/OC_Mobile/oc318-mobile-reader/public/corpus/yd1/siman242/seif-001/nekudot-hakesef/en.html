(Siman 242, Taz note 9) He challenged him — for what purpose did they take leave twice etc. This is nothing — certainly the Gemara was not precise except from the fact that they took leave a second time, since they had already taken leave on the 22nd; but that they took leave on the 22nd is no difficulty — perhaps they feared they could not take leave tomorrow on the 23rd, or because they were taking leave of him on the 22nd in the joy of the festival they took leave from him also for what he said "one must emend to the 24th" — the language is not corrected; and further, if so, what he said afterward "but on the 23rd" etc. also needs emendation — but emend to the 23rd. And further — the simple meaning of the verse in Divrei HaYamim, as written "on the 23rd of the seventh month he sent the people" etc., there it is written "the people," meaning they took leave of him, and afterward "they blessed the king and went to their tents"; but here it is written "on the 23rd he sent the people to their tents" etc., implying they set out on their journey on the 24th; and even though in Melachim it also says "on the eighth day he sent the people" etc. — there it says he sent them to their houses on the 23rd, and so Radak wrote on the verse — his words: "on the 23rd" etc., and in Melachim it says "on the eighth," which is the 22nd of the month — Rabbis explained that on the eighth they took leave of him and did not travel that day because it was the festival, and the next day, the 23rd, when they traveled, they again took leave of him — end of his words; so too appears explicitly from Rashi there, see there; and so I found in Tosefta Sukkah chapter 4 — its language: "Bless for himself" — it says "on the eighth day he sent the people and they blessed the king" — one might think they need not stay overnight — Scripture teaches "on the twenty-third of the seventh month he sent the people and they went to their tents" — how so? They took leave of him while it was still day, agreed, and went — end; implying they went to their houses on the 23rd.
