Not to rule before one's rabbi, and the law of a rabbi who waives his honor. And it contains 36 seifim. A person is obligated in the honor and fear of his rabbi more than the honor and fear of his father:
{Rama: His father who is his outstanding rabbi — he calls him in his speech "my rabbi"; but for a rabbi who is not outstanding he calls him "father" (the rabbi's reasoning from Tur and Gemara, chapter HaZahav).}
