If the ordainee did not receive ordination immediately from that ordaining rabbi but through other rabbis and he became their branch — that rabbi has no dominion over him at all if he is not his rabbi: {Rama: But if he ordained him alone, the custom is that the ordainee is somewhat subordinate to his ordainer (Maharik shoresh 117, 113); and likewise one who learns in a yeshiva for a time — the custom is to say he is a student of the yeshiva head, even if the yeshiva head may hear more novellae from him; and these customs have basis (Piskei Maharai).}
