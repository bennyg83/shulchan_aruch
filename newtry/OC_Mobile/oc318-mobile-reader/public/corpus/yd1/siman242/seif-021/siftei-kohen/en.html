Unless his teacher, etc. Even if the teacher does not personally honor his student, but the teacher is honored by others honoring the student, they may honor the student even in the teacher's presence. The Derishah and Bach write this in the name of Rabbi Simchah, as do Hagahot Maimoniyot and the Mordechai. They further write that a teacher need not stand before his student even if the student is a very great Torah scholar. Nevertheless, Hagahot Maimoniyot reports that his master would show respect even to students who were not especially distinguished. The Rama rules likewise below, siman 244, seif 8.
This applies specifically where the teacher is also, etc. Responsa Mahari Kohen of Krakow, siman 19, writes that the honor of one's teacher's teacher, even if one never studied before him, exceeds the honor of one's own teacher. If he also studied somewhat before the senior teacher, he should set aside his own teacher's honor and not honor him in the senior teacher's presence. See there.
