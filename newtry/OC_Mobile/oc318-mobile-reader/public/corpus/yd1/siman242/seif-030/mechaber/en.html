All these matters we said one must honor his rabbi with — they said only regarding an outstanding rabbi — that is, most of his wisdom is from him, whether Scripture, Mishnah, or Gemara: {Rama: And in these days rabbinical authority does not depend on who taught him pilpul and distinctions practiced now, but on who taught him the halachic ruling, analysis, and established him on truth and uprightness (Maharik shoresh 170).} But if he did not learn most of his wisdom from him, he is not obligated to honor him in all these matters; but he stands before him from when he reaches four cubits of him, and tears for him as for all the dead he mourns — even if he learned from him only one matter, whether small or great he stands before him and tears for him: {Rama: (And see below siman 340 seif 8).}
