Not everyone whose teacher died, etc. This gloss belongs at the beginning of seif 13 and should read: 'Not everyone whose teacher died may sit and issue rulings unless he has reached the level of ruling; and any student who has not reached the level of ruling, etc.' This is the text in the Tur in the Rambam's name and in old editions of the Shulchan Aruch. Below, seif katan 46, it will be explained when one is considered not to have reached the level of ruling.
