From the incident of Tanchum, son of Rabbi Ami, there, and according to Tosafot's explanation in Eruvin 62b. Even permission does not help when one's teacher is nearby, because success is not granted in such circumstances. This is learned from the incident of Abaye's sharecropper, Ketubot 60b, according to Rashi and Tosafot. Within three parasangs is considered nearby. Maharik, shoresh 173, therefore writes that permission helps only beyond three parasangs.
