Every scholar, etc. See Maharsha in Chiddushei Aggadot, chapter 3 of Sotah, where he wrote: "In these generations, those who rule halachah from the Shulchan Aruch — and they do not know the reasoning of each matter without first examining it carefully from the Talmud, which is the [true] service of Torah scholars — a mistake has crept into their rulings and they are in the category of 'those who destroy the world,' and therefore they must be rebuked — see there."
