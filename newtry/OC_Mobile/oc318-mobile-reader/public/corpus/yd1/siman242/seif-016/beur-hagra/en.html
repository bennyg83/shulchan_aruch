It is written as 'some say' because the Rif omitted this ruling, apparently holding that R' Elazar's statement that any Torah scholar who does not rise, etc., disagrees with R' Yannai. We rule like R' Elazar, who expounds 'You shall fear' to include fear of one's teacher like fear of Heaven. One who repeatedly receives the Divine Presence would certainly have to rise each time. Rosh. Rambam and the other poskim, however, record R' Yannai's statement. Semag explains 'he is not permitted' as meaning 'he is not obligated,' and this is also the reading of the old Tosafos.
