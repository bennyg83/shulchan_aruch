A student should not initiate greeting. Panim Meirot writes that passing before one's teacher without greeting him is boorish conduct. This resolves a question raised by Yeshuot Yaakov.
To stand before him. Shevut Yaakov exempts standing before one's teacher on Tisha B'Av. Birkei Yosef challenges him, and the widespread custom in Eretz Yisrael and elsewhere is to stand for rabbis and Torah scholars as on other days, though custom alone may not prove strict obligation.
