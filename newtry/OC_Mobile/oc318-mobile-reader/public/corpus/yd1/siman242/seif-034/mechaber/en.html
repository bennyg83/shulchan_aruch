His father's lost object and his rabbi's lost object — his outstanding rabbi's lost object takes precedence; and if his father equals his rabbi, his father's lost object takes precedence; if his father and rabbi were carrying a load, he puts down the rabbi's first and then his father's; if his father and rabbi were captives, he redeems his rabbi first and then his father; and if his father was a Torah scholar, he redeems his father first and then his rabbi: {Rama: And likewise unloading his load takes precedence over his rabbi's, even if his father is not equal in wisdom to his rabbi; but he does not return his father's lost object first unless equal to his rabbi (Tur in the name of Rosh, Hagahot Maimoniyot, Beit Yosef proved so on the matter); some say rabbi before father is only when he learns from him free; but if his father hires a teacher and teaches him, his father takes precedence in everything (Sefer Chassidim); and so appears to me as essential.}
