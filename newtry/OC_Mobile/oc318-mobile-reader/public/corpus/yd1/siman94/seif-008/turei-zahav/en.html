Into an oven of panada. Meaning a bowl of meat — they make in it a meat dish called panada.
It forbids only the amount of a peel. For there is no gravy there, and it is not like hot meat in seif 7, for here there is no knife pressure, only it rests on it; and further there is sometimes dry fat on it not visible, unlike in a bowl — so Beit Yosef.
