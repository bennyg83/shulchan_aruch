Cheese. Meaning cheese fell on hot panada.
Peel. Shach wrote: one may say for siman 105, issur that fell on hot heter requires removal, for cheese does not enter so much; if so the cheese itself requires removal; and Taz wrote: one may say for hot meat in seif 7, for here there is no knife pressure, and further there is sometimes dry fat on it that is not visible, unlike in a bowl; and Shach wrote: that which a peel suffices here is in something completely dry; but if there is a little moisture we rule in siman 69 regarding salting in a kneading trough that hot meat in a hot bowl requires removal; and even though in siman 105 regarding food, if both are hot, even if both are dry, all is forbidden — nevertheless in a vessel it is lesser since the issur is not in actual form; and even though there seif 7 implies a vessel forbids even non-fat issur even without gravy — one may say although it is better that it exudes without gravy, nevertheless it does not exude more than a peel; and nevertheless investigate — end.
