And Taz wrote in siman 95 seif 3 it will be explained that also by pouring kli rishon onto two vessels, one of milk and one of meat, and they are soiled, there is issur like kli rishon itself; therefore if one also poured kli rishon onto a vessel soiled from meat it is considered as if he used that vessel in kli rishon. And Maharshal wrote: I am inclined to be lenient with kli rishon standing by the fire — as long as it is hot, even if it is not yad soledes bo, it is fit to forbid, for they made distance for kli rishon as in the Yerushalmi — end; but Taz rules like Beit Yosef that even in kli rishon on the fire it does not forbid if it is not yad soledes bo, and so is primary.
