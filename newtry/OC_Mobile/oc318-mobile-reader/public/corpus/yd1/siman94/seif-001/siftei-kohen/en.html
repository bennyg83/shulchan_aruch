They estimate for all that was inserted, etc. — for we do not know how much came out from it; and see above siman 98 seif 5 if we do not know how much was absorbed; and Maharshal wrote in Orach Chaim s.k. 61 and Piskei HaGahot siman 41 that if it is not clear to him how far it was inserted they estimate in the default manner of insertion — until the head of the spoon — end.
That is, they used it in a kli rishon. Milk; but if they used it in a kli rishon with water it is not counted ben yomo; but in a forbidden spoon, even if they used it in a kli rishon of water, it is counted ben yomo when there was not sixty in the water against the spoon, as below seif 6 and siman 103; and see there.
And the first view is primary. For even though part of it is hot, all of it is hot — nevertheless it does not carry its absorption throughout, as most poskim hold; and see above siman 121 s.k. 16; and Mechaber's view too is that the first view is primary, as I wrote in this siman s.k. 28; therefore he wrote the last view in the name of "some say"; and see above siman 98 seif 4.
