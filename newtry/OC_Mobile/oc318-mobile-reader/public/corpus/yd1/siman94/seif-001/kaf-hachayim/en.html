(1) [seif 1] One who inserts a dairy spoon into a meat pot, or the reverse — they estimate for everything that was inserted, etc., because we do not know how much came out from it. Tur. Beit Yosef. Levush. Shach s.k. 1. However, where it is known how much was absorbed — such as when it was new (or not a ben yomo) and one shook out an olive's worth of milk from it and afterward (within twenty-four hours) inserted it into a meat pot or the reverse — since it is known it absorbed only an olive's worth, one need not estimate except against the olive. Beit Yosef and so below in Shulchan Aruch siman 98 seif 5 — see there.
(4) And if it is not clear to him how far it was inserted, they estimate in the manner of one who inserts a spoon into a pot — that is, the head of the spoon. Rashal there. Shach s.k. 1. Peri Chadash section 1; Levushei Serad section 1; Chavot Ya'ir there. And this means against the entire spoon except the handle. Rashal Piskei HaGahot there siman 41. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef section 4; Minchat Yaakov on Taz general rule 85 section 1; and he wrote this means the head of the spoon, which is the round part, not the handle — end. And what Shulchan Gavoah wrote in Maharsham section 1 to explain Rashal's words — it seems because he did not see Minchat Yaakov's words; nevertheless it seems everything is according to appearance, for sometimes the pot is full and necessarily part of the handle was inserted, and sometimes one can see on the spoon how far it was inserted from the moisture it absorbed from the dish; therefore everything is according to appearance.
(5) And likewise if a woman said "certainly it was inserted" or "until here was the dish in the pot" to estimate with sixty, or if she said the pot was not yad soledes bo — she is believed, for a woman is believed even in issur; Orach Chaim Tashbetz IV Chut HaMeshulash siman 42. Erech HaShulchan section 3, unlike Rav Beit Yehudah I Yoreh De'ah siman 41. And so Yad Efraim on this seif that a woman is believed, unlike Rav Beit Yehudah — and so Zivchei Tzedek section 5: in practice every day we believe women whether in basar b'chalav, salting, or absorption of issur, since handling the dish is in their hands and only women are at home, and we never found anyone who doubted this — end.
