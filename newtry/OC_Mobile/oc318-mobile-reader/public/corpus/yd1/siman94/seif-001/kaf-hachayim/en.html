(1) [seif 1] One who inserts a dairy spoon into a meat pot, or the reverse — they estimate for everything that was inserted, etc., because we do not know how much came out from it. Tur. Beit Yosef. Levush. Shach s.k. 1. However, where it is known how much was absorbed — such as when it was new (or not a ben yomo) and one shook out an olive's worth of milk from it and afterward (within twenty-four hours) inserted it into a meat pot or the reverse — since it is known it absorbed only an olive's worth, one need not estimate except against the olive. Beit Yosef and so below in Shulchan Aruch siman 98 seif 5 — see there.
