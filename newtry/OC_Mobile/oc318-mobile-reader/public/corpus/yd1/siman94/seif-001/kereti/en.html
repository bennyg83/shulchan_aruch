What was inserted, etc. — not what was inserted in the food, only what was inserted in the pot; even if outside the food, since it is within the steam of the pot it joins to forbid — see Peleti where I clarified the matter in his reasoning.
Within twenty-four hours — meaning milk; but if they used water or other things within twenty-four hours it is nothing, for it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am of the permitted.
Hot in part of it, etc. — and likewise absorption and emission in all of it. And that which is brought "if he cooked in part of the vessel" — hag'alah is not needed for the whole vessel — is because as its absorption so its emission; and see what I wrote in Peleti.
And the first view is primary — for "hot" we say, but it does not absorb in all of it; but for carrying emission from outside the insertion into the place of insertion we do not say. And further I wrote to distinguish: if it is hot in any case it absorbs and emits in the entire vessel; but if it is only cold and hot from insertion into the hot pot — "part of it hot is all of it hot" — we do not say both to heat all of it and to emit and absorb from all of it — investigate; see Peleti.
