See responsum Noda BiYehudah II Yoreh De'ah siman 16: it is explained there that even if there is no recognition on the spoon how far it was inserted, if he says how far it was inserted he is believed, and we do not say in this "a matter not dependent on him," etc., for in a matter the person himself does he is not likely to say so, since anyway he knows there is sometimes a difference in the matter and he knows how he does it; specifically regarding one who slaughters who did not learn — if after learning he says "clearly I slaughtered well" it does not help, since at the time of slaughter he did not know there is sometimes stringency regarding delay of pressing — see there; and per his words it is difficult from siman 18 seif 4 regarding confused slaughter and Shach there s.k. 15 and Taz siman 6 s.k. 3 regarding a flaw above half the knife — see there and what Noda BiYehudah wrote there; and the proof he brought from R' S siman 94 in Shach that we ask, etc. — Shach has no mention of this at all; possibly he meant Shach s.k. 28 what he disagreed with Maharshal regarding a knife — see there; and he should have written from end siman 94; but even from there there is no proof for spoon insertion, for that case is different, since it is a rabbinic doubt when there is no gravy, as Peri To'ar wrote there and Peri Megadim brought. And see Peri Megadim in Orach Chaim in hora'ah customs section 2 letter 13: it is also explained that if it is not recognized how far it was inserted one may doubt whether "clearly to me" helps, since it is a matter not dependent on him — see there and investigate. [And see Knesset HaGedolah who wrote one must estimate against the part of the spoon inserted into the pot even against what is above the dish, for it is hot from the pot's steam; and Chavot Da'at wrote on him: although what compels this from the dispute of Rashi and R' Yehudah in siman 92 is not compelled, nevertheless there is room to be stringent as he wrote — see there; and see Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot s.k. 1.] See also responsum Chasam Sofer II siman 82 who wrote per Peleti's reasoning it seems there is no relevance, and so is the practice of poskim that Maharshal brought in Shach s.k. 1 — see there.
