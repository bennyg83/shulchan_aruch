That was inserted. See responsum Noda BiYehudah II Yoreh De'ah siman 16: it is explained there that even if there is no recognition on the spoon how far it was inserted, if he says how far it was inserted he is believed, and we do not say in this "a matter not dependent on him," etc., for in a matter the person himself does he is not likely to say so, since anyway he knows there is sometimes a difference in the matter and he knows how he does it; specifically regarding one who slaughters who did not learn — if after learning he says "clearly I slaughtered well" it does not help, since at the time of slaughter he did not know there is sometimes stringency regarding delay of pressing — see there; and per his words it is difficult from siman 18 seif 4 regarding confused slaughter and Shach there s.k. 15 and Taz siman 6 s.k. 3 regarding a flaw above half the knife — see there and what Noda BiYehudah wrote there; and the proof he brought from R' S siman 94 in Shach that we ask, etc. — Shach has no mention of this at all; possibly he meant Shach s.k. 28 what he disagreed with Maharshal regarding a knife — see there; and he should have written from end siman 94; but even from there there is no proof for spoon insertion, for that case is different, since it is a rabbinic doubt when there is no gravy, as Peri To'ar wrote there and Peri Megadim brought. And see Peri Megadim in Orach Chaim in hora'ah customs section 2 letter 13: it is also explained that if it is not recognized how far it was inserted one may doubt whether "clearly to me" helps, since it is a matter not dependent on him — see there and investigate. [And see Knesset HaGedolah who wrote one must estimate against the part of the spoon inserted into the pot even against what is above the dish, for it is hot from the pot's steam; and Chavot Da'at wrote on him: although what compels this from the dispute of Rashi and R' Yehudah in siman 92 is not compelled, nevertheless there is room to be stringent as he wrote — see there; and see Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot s.k. 1.] See also responsum Chasam Sofer II siman 82 who wrote per Peleti's reasoning it seems there is no relevance, and so is the practice of poskim that Maharshal brought in Shach s.k. 1 — see there.
In a kli rishon. See Beit HaTosafot s.k. 2 in the name of Rashal; and see responsum Chavot Yair siman 101 who wrote: if they boiled milk in a pot and it stood until after it cooled many hours passed and they emptied the milk from it, and the next day they cooked meat in it — if it was close after twenty-four hours from boiling such that yesterday at this hour it was still hot, more than lukewarm whose measure is like the heat of spit — it seems to forbid, for although it was removed from the fire, nevertheless kli rishon remained there and they made distance and a fence; and all the more regarding that kli rishon absorbs and carries — so much more what is in it; and it needs twenty-four hours after it comes to lukewarm temperature — see there; and see below siman 105 s.k. 5 what is written there.
Primary. Beit HaTosafot of Rav Maharit who wrote in the name of Beit Yosef: even though part of it is hot is all of it hot — that is for absorption, but not for emission, for it emits only what was inserted from it into the pot; and there is a difference: if the metal spoon's handle was inserted ben yomo into a dairy pot, sixty is needed in the pot against the handle that was inserted (end); and behold Beit Yosef wrote he heard so from the author of Magen Avraham — see there; and indeed so in Magen Avraham Orach Chaim siman 452 s.k. 24; however Shach's view here s.k. 3 does not appear so, but also for absorption we do not say all of it is hot, for it does not carry its absorption throughout; and so below siman 121 s.k. 17: primarily as Maharam Mintz — "all of it hot" is regarding if the vessel became hot at one end and issur touched the other end it is forbidden, but not that if issur fell at one end it spreads to the other side; therefore if permitted matter was used in part of the other end it is permitted b'dieved — see there; and so in Solut LehMincha general rule 85 law 1: if the spoon's handle from meat was inserted into dairy food, even if the spoon was used without the handle within twenty-four hours, one need not nullify it at all b'dieved; and so I brought in Pesach laws siman 452 s.k. 40, unlike Beit HaTosafot in the name of Magen Avraham; but nevertheless the spoon is forbidden to use again until hag'alah — end; and see responsum Ahavat Shalom I siman 83 who expanded on this matter and brought that where the whole vessel is not hot — yad soledes bo is not in it — we do not say part hot is all hot; and if the spoon's handle or knife was inserted before kashering, sixty is not needed to nullify it; and if the whole vessel is hot we properly say it spreads throughout the whole vessel like a hot piece; and if the other side was inserted into food before kashering, sixty is needed to nullify it; except for kashering, part use suffices — see there. And Shach wrote there in the name of Orach Chaim general rule 37 law 2: specifically when hot from cooking; but when hot from fire itself it carries and emits its absorption throughout — see there; therefore if a metal vessel was heated by actual fire in part and part was inserted into the pot, one must estimate against the whole spoon, for all of it is truly hot and emits from everything.
