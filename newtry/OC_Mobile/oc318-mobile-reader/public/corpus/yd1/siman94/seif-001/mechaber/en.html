The law of one who inserts a dairy spoon into a meat pot. In it are 9 seifim: One who inserts a dairy spoon into a meat pot, or the reverse — they estimate for everything that was inserted from it into the pot (if the spoon is a ben yomo, that is, they used it in a kli rishon within twenty-four hours) (Arukh general rule 37). And there are those who say that if the spoon is of metal they estimate for all of it because part of it became hot and all of it became hot (and the first view is primary, and so is the practice) (Mordechai and Hagahot Shmuel David and Arukh) (and see above siman 98):
