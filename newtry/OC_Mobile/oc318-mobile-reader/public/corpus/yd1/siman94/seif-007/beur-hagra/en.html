Hot meat, etc. — unlike Rabbenu who wrote that a peel suffices and brings proof from perek 1 (8b) that a knife "peels to one side" because the place of shechitah is hot; and Semak rejected it, for there it is not hot except the peel near the place of shechitah.
Or if he does not know, etc. — as in Avodah Zarah 38b and Tosafot there s.v. if, etc., and as stated above siman 122 seif 7; and see Tosafot perek 1 of Chullin 8b s.v. one who slaughters must stand, etc., and so Ra'ash there.
Only, etc. — as Semak wrote there in the name of Rabbenu, that the case of slaughtering with a forbidden knife even when not ben yomo requires only a peel; and so Rashba; and as Rashi 112a explained per the first view. And with this one need not resolve Semak that even when all of it is hot it does not forbid from slight grease stuck to it except a peel; and as in perek Keitzad (76a) that salting is different, a little, etc., and this is "the amount of a peel."
The knife requires hag'alah — and likewise a knife that is not ben yomo, but then it is permitted to use for other things as above.
The meat requires, etc. — like a forbidden knife: the place of shechitah is hot in kli sheni only from grease, and as stated.
And the knife is inserted — as at the end of Avodah Zarah regarding a gentile's knife to use it cold even though cold does not absorb and rinsing suffices, as in perek 28 107b regarding a knife; likewise here if he cut with it cold; and likewise per the first view in perek 1 (8b) that in the place of shechitah it is cold nevertheless he peels because of knife pressure; but Rashba wrote that nevertheless the reason is also that the place of shechitah is somewhat hot combined with knife pressure; and this is what is stated here in kli sheni; and that at the end of Avodah Zarah is for established use in something cooked hot or to cut something sharp, as in end of siman 121.
And even if, etc. — as above; and in the place of shechitah it is cold but somewhat hot as above, and likewise even per the first view as above.
