Hot meat, etc. — even if knife below and meat above, we do not say "the lower prevails," for through knife pressure it absorbs much — clear in Orach Chaim.
The entire piece is forbidden, etc. — and why do we say place of shechitah is hot and peel suffices even with gentile knife forbidden from grease, yet only peel — there not all animal hot, only place of shechitah; but piece entirely hot. Tur in R' Peretz; Maharshal Piskei HaGahot siman 59: difference regarding knife that slaughtered — issur from blood, the throat releases, absorbs little from much blood, peel suffices; unlike absorption of meat or milk. I did not grasp end of his view — plainly not like kosher slaughter knife where certainly no peel needed, for busy emitting blood does not absorb — as in sugya, explained siman 10; permitters compare to gentile slaughter knife — Chullin 8b even per place of shechitah hot, peel suffices; Semak siman 205 explicitly — end; here no blood issur, only grease issur — cannot distinguish; plain.
If there is no sixty, etc. — and even though siman 105 seif 4 Taz: issur fell on piece in pot outside gravy, removal suffices — here different through knife pressure spreads throughout; or default knife absorbs butter grease; or fatty meat; siman 96 radish only removal — radish sharpness only; some hold radish sharper than hot, makes ben yomo for benefit — for spreading in cooking, hot stronger.
Like the place of the knife. Continues first view in seif 1 which is primary; even part hot all hot, did not carry absorption throughout; Rama "against knife" means place that touched meat; only when knows clearly how far cut; default cuts with whole knife — sixty against whole knife except handle; siman 105 seif 7 Tur: issur outside gravy, removal suffices — different knife pressure; or default knife butter; fatty; siman 96 radish removal only — radish sharpness; some radish stronger than hot for ben yomo benefit — hot stronger for spreading. Maharshal Orach Chaim 41 and Piskei HaGahot 42: must estimate against whole knife except handle — not mindful how far cut; Rav and Shach primary; only when knows clearly; default cuts whole knife — sixty except handle.
Or if he does not know that it is ben yomo. Siman 10 s.k. 6 I proved Mechaber: if slaughtered with gentile knife, no difference — even if knows not ben yomo, peel needed for grease on face; Mechaber siman 96: even not ben yomo, peel for grease; Rav Orach Chaim siman 447: default knife not clean; grease does not forbid more than peel; siman 96 radish: if not ben yomo and not wiped, forbids removal — radish sharpness and knife pressure make peel and face ben yomo up to removal; Taz: even first part with sixty against knife place, peel required; siman 105 seif 5: whatever needs sixty needs peel; if not ben yomo, no sixty against grease — all forbidden; lenient only among astonished. Siman 10, 96, Orach Chaim 447: default not clean; grease only peel; siman 96 radish implies not ben yomo not wiped — removal; radish sharpness and pressure; Taz first part with sixty still peel; 105 seif 5 sixty and peel; not ben yomo no sixty against grease — all forbidden; lenient astonished — end.
And all this with hot meat in kli rishon, etc. until "and the knife requires inserting." In Shulchan Aruch Orach Chaim long general rule 36 — not in printed Darkei Moshe. Behold Orach Chaim stringent: hot meat on strainer forbidden while very hot from kli rishon; Maharshal chapter 3 siman 44: kli sheni only water and gravy and liquids that mix and cool quickly; not meat/fish/solids — while yad soledes bo stays hot, exudes and absorbs — rice and porridge after pouring from pot to bowl, while yad soledes bo, like kli rishon for basar b'chalav: ben yomo spoon from meat, porridge absorbed milk — food and spoon forbidden from basar b'chalav, forbid another pot if inserted; if dish already forbidden from absorption, spoon not likewise forbidden without gravy; all the more if food milk or meat spoon not likewise forbidden afterward — end. Darkei Moshe siman 105 brings Rashal, disagrees: hot gravy holds heat more than thick meat — no proof; here lesser since knife pressure like radish siman 96 we forbid from taste. Prove from R' Peretz in Tur: some permit hot meat cut with dairy knife through peel like place of shechitah — who would be lenient in kli rishon with peel — if peel sufficed in kli rishon, how forbid from taste? Plainly kli sheni; nevertheless stringent sixty since yad soledes bo; if not, whole forbidden; Me'orei Or siman 105: hot meat with dairy knife in kli sheni yad soledes bo — knife forbidden meat exudes, meat not forbidden — kli sheni no power to exude knife absorption into meat. Stringent where knife pressure; without pressure lenient as Rama.
And the knife requires hag'alah. Written regarding ben yomo knife hag'alah required, not if not ben yomo — absorbed basar b'chalav, forbidden meat or milk; not ben yomo permitted fish and vegetables as siman 93 gloss; ben yomo knife became nevelah — hag'alah required. "Knife requires inserting" — even if meat soft, if cut hard thing like hard cheese, cold knife requires inserting as Orach Chaim general rule 58 dalet; see siman 89 s.k. 20; siman 121 inserting law.
