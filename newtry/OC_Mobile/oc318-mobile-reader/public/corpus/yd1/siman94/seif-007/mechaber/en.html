Hot meat that one cut with a dairy knife — the entire piece is forbidden if it does not have sixty against the place of the knife that cut the meat; but if it is not a ben yomo, or if one does not know that it is a ben yomo, it does not forbid except the amount of a peel: {Rama: And all this is with hot meat in a kli rishon; and then if the knife is a ben yomo and there is no sixty in the meat against the knife, everything is forbidden; and the knife too requires hag'alah; but if it is a kli sheni, the meat requires peeling and the knife is inserted in the ground (Arukh general rule 37), and so is the practice; and even if the knife is not a ben yomo one should peel the meat a little because of the knife's grease (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Semak):}
