(2) There. They estimate for everything inserted from it into the pot, etc. — this applies when the vessel is hard: one estimates only against what was inserted; but in soft food one estimates against all of it — e.g., one inserted a cold piece of meat into a dairy pot and not all of it entered the gravy, only part — one estimates against all of it. Orach Chaim general rule 28 end law 3; Maharshal; see below siman 105 in Biur, section 9; Zivchei Tzedek section 1; and see below siman 105 section 11.
(3) There. In a vessel, what was inserted from it into the pot, etc. — it implies that if he says clearly to me "until here it was inserted into the pot" he is believed, and we do not say in this "a matter not dependent on him," etc.; and so Rashal Piskei HaGahot siman 42 and Orach Chaim siman 65. Bach in this section. Noda BiYehudah II Yoreh De'ah end siman 16. Chavot Ya'ir general rule 46 section 6; and so agreed in Zivchei Tzedek section 2, unlike one who doubted this and wrote that also in practice every day when a question of Orach Chaim law like this comes before them they believe the questioner until where it was — and no one ever doubted the matter — end.
(6) There. They estimate for everything inserted from it into the pot, etc., and one must estimate with a good measure — by measuring: fill a vessel with water, then insert the spoon into it gently, and the water that overflows is the measure of the spoon. Orach Chaim general rule 37 law 3 end section 1; Zivchei Tzedek section 4; and see above siman 93 sections 3 and 4.
