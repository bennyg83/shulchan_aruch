Against the place of the knife — from here it is proved Mechaber holds we do not say "part of it hot absorbs all of it" like Acharonim; and see Peleti there is no proof from here.
Place of the knife specifically — when he knows the knife's place that cut; but from the default we say he cut everywhere — Rashal and Peri Chadash; and nevertheless even with sixty one must be stringent with removal.
In a kli rishon — and Maharshal's view distinguishes between solid and liquid; see siman 105 what is written at length.
Insertion — and if he cut something hard, insertion is required even without kli sheni and entirely cold — Peri Chadash.
