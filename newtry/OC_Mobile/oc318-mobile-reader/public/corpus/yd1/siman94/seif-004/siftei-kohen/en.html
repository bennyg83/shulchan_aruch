And the spoon is forbidden l'chatchila whether with meat or with milk. Since it absorbed from meat and milk; but with other things it is permitted, as above siman 93 s.k. 3.
But b'dieved it does not forbid, etc. Beit Yosef wrote: specifically if he returned and inserted it in the type he inserted the first time; but if he returned and inserted it in the type absorbed in it initially within twenty-four hours of insertion — obviously it forbids — end; and all the more if he returned and inserted it in other things such as water and vegetables — permitted b'dieved even within twenty-four hours of first insertion.
