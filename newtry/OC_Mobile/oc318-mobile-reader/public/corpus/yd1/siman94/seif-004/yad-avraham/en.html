(seif 2 in the gloss) If the spoon was inserted twice, one time of sixty suffices. And it is simple that specifically if the same spoon was inserted, one time of sixty suffices, since there is no taste; but if another spoon was inserted, one must nullify both, for we rule it returns and is stirred if the first issur was added, as siman 99 seif 6 in the gloss; and see siman 98 seif 5 in the gloss.
