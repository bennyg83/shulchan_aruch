(4) And if it is not clear to him how far it was inserted, they estimate in the manner of one who inserts a spoon into a pot — that is, the head of the spoon. Rashal there. Shach s.k. 1. Peri Chadash section 1; Levushei Serad section 1; Chavot Ya'ir there. And this means against the entire spoon except the handle. Rashal Piskei HaGahot there siman 41. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef section 4; Minchat Yaakov on Taz general rule 85 section 1; and he wrote this means the head of the spoon, which is the round part, not the handle — end. And what Shulchan Gavoah wrote in Maharsham section 1 to explain Rashal's words — it seems because he did not see Minchat Yaakov's words; nevertheless it seems everything is according to appearance, for sometimes the pot is full and necessarily part of the handle was inserted, and sometimes one can see on the spoon how far it was inserted from the moisture it absorbed from the dish; therefore everything is according to appearance.
