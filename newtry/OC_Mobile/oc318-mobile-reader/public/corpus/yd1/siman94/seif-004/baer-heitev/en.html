L'chatchila. The difference between pot and spoon is in Beit Yosef, for the spoon absorbed milk at the time of its benefit, therefore even after it is spoiled it is forbidden l'chatchila like a pot that is not ben yomo; unlike the pot which did not absorb milk taste until after it was spoiled.
B'dieved. Shach in the name of Beit Yosef: specifically if he returned and inserted it in the type he inserted the first time; but if he returned and inserted it in the type absorbed in it initially within twenty-four hours of insertion — obviously it forbids; and all the more if he returned and inserted it in other things such as water and vegetables — permitted b'dieved even within twenty-four hours of first insertion.
