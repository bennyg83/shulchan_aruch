(Mechaber seif 3) All forbidden in benefit, fruits and cold permitted. Source Tur; Darkei Moshe: unlike Pesach vessels Orach Chaim 451 — there want to benefit from issur after Pesach; here different — Avodah Zarah 32: want permanence through other — forbidden in actual issur; bottles permitted — wine not absorbed much, actual issur; vessels absorbed more — no actual issur; if so why Orach Chaim forbid rent chametz vessels — there gentile uses hot chametz, actual issur, wants chametz permanence; here only cold use, no actual issur — permitted. Rama there permits heat chametz vessels for needs; here did not gloss — even hot forbidden; there not normal use; basar b'chalav even not normal use forbidden; how hag'alah pot basar b'chalav absorption — issur during hag'alah — forbidden to hag'ael basar b'chalav vessel until not ben yomo — resolves Tosafot challenge how hag'ael large cauldron — forbidden until not ben yomo; long gloss Maharash Cohen Vilna on Darkei Moshe — Magen Avraham siman 452 Rambam/Tosafot 43b eating required, Meiri forbidden from Torah not eating — gloss missed Rambam/Tosafot.
