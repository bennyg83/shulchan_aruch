But the spoon is forbidden. Bach challenged Tur who ruled like Rashba above siman 93 that cooking is hag'alah — if so here too milk should be hag'alah. See Beit Yosef; and it fits that they strained themselves; for me it is difficult, for the spoon absorbs from meat and truly in the pot there is sixty against milk, so the spoon that absorbed milk — the milk is not noten ta'am, for the whole pot combines; but meat absorbed in the spoon immediately becomes noten ta'am — what of it that milk has sixty — nevertheless the meat is noten ta'am as in another large pot, and if so it is issur and hag'alah in milk will not help since issur was absorbed — investigate.
All this when it is a ben yomo: if absorbed from meat and within twenty-four hours inserted into milk and within twenty-four hours of that insertion inserted into a dish — even though from meat absorption twenty-four hours already passed, for milk absorption twenty-four hours did not pass and milk became noten ta'am and is forbidden, for we count twenty-four hours from the last time it became noten ta'am — Peri Chadash.
If it is not a ben yomo — and even per what is written siman 87 in Beit Yosef, even noten ta'am lifgam is forbidden; and possibly here since there is sixty in the pot we are not so stringent to say noten ta'am and forbid noten ta'am lifgam — investigate.
