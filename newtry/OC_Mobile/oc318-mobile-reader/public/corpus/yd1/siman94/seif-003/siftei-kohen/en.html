The spoon is forbidden. Per R' Avraham in siman 95 seif 3, even if there was only water or other things in the pot, if the pot was ben yomo and absorption was initially through the body of the meat the spoon is forbidden, and so we rule; but per Mechaber there the spoon is forbidden only when inserted in a pot in which they are now cooking meat in actual form — see there.
Whether with meat or with milk. And likewise with other things, for the spoon is noten ta'am; and see above siman 98 seif 5, and in Magen Avraham there.
As long as it is a ben yomo. Meaning from the time of insertion, even if it is not ben yomo from when milk was cooked in it, since it was ben yomo at first insertion it became noten ta'am and forbids afterward even b'dieved if it is ben yomo from insertion; and this is clear, as Orach Chaim wrote in several places and Darkei Moshe siman 66 so explicitly, unlike Bach who wrote in seif 3 plainly the opposite — plainly an error; but if it is not ben yomo at second insertion everything is permitted, for although noten ta'am, now it is noten ta'am lifgam and permitted; but certainly l'chatchila it is forbidden to insert it in anything even if not ben yomo, since it became noten ta'am initially.
Everything is forbidden in benefit, and even the pot, etc. Hagahot Ashiri at the end of Avodah Zarah wrote if he afterward cooked when it is not ben yomo he throws the pot's value into the river and the food is permitted — end; and so Maharai and the Rav in Taz; and Maharshal in Orach Chaim and in his book Piskei HaGahot siman 46 disagreed and rejected his proofs.
Fruits or cold. Beit Yosef wrote it implies even a moist thing, as long as it is cold, is permitted — end; and see siman 91 s.k. 2 where I expanded on this.
