In a vessel, what was inserted from it into the pot, etc. — it implies that if he says clearly to me "until here it was inserted into the pot" he is believed, and we do not say in this "a matter not dependent on him," etc.; and so Rashal Piskei HaGahot siman 42 and Orach Chaim siman 65.
