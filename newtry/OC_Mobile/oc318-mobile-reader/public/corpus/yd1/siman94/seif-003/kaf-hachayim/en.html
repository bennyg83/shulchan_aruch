(7) And if it happened they cooked a dish in a dairy pot and put a little milk in the dish such that there is sixty in the dish against the milk, and inserted a ben yomo meat spoon into that dairy pot — everything is permitted, for the milk was nullified since there is sixty in the water against it, and it is also as if absent, as if there were only water, and it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am. Therefore the posek must ask and investigate, for they asked him when inserting a ben yomo spoon into a dairy pot whether there is milk in the pot or not, and also when there is milk how much — for sometimes they put only a little; thus he will rule rightly. Birkei Yosef in Sha'ar HaYichud section 1; Zivchei Tzedek section 6.
(9) A barley dish into which they put one part butter in forty of the dish and stirred it with a spoon and inserted the spoon into a meat pot that does not have sixty against the butter put in the barley dish — the teachers ruled forbidden, unlike those who permit. Responsum Penei Aryeh Chadashot siman 48. Birkei Yosef there section 2; but Erech HaShulchan there wrote that per what all hold, the spoon forbids only per the calculation of one in forty, and if so the meat dish is permitted. And so Peri To'ar siman 95 section 1.
