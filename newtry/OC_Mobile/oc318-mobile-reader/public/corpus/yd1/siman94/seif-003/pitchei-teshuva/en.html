In benefit. Beit HaTosafot. And what is written "but not before a dog even if not his" — that is Taz's words who wrote so in the name of Orach Chaim and Maharshal and Taz; however the Gaon author of Mekor Chaim siman 348 s.k. 10 is not so, and so Biur HaGra there — see there; and some proof from below siman 134 seif 2 — see there and investigate. And see responsum Penei Yehoshua Even HaEzer siman 12 and Sefer Be'er Yaakov siman 29 on this matter.
