(there) And some say if the spoon is metal they estimate for all of it, etc., and the gloss: the first view is primary, etc., for even though part of it is hot all of it is hot — it does not carry its absorption throughout — Shach. And it appears to me primary as I wrote in Chiddushei perek Dam Chatat (96b) that it is clear in the Gemara that obviously it spreads throughout when he cooked in part, since all of it is hot; and nevertheless in other issurim it is not asked, for obviously rinsing and scouring of the whole vessel is not required as in kli rishon; and as Rama wrote in the gloss siman 121. But in kodashim it is properly asked whether we say so, for we see in several matters they were stricter than other issurim until the conclusion that truth is so, for the verse revealed to us "and if in a copper vessel it was cooked," even in part; otherwise it is impossible to resolve that passage at all, however much Tosafot wondered there; and so in Ran perek Kol HaShir; and I did not descend to the end of one of the Acharonim who wrote it carries absorption throughout but does not emit from all of it — so in Magen Avraham and so in Beit Avraham. Rather primary as from the Gemara: we say it carries its absorption throughout and emits from all of it. And it seems to say: specifically what absorbed through use of part of it — the general rule as its absorption so its emission; but what absorbed through use of all of it does not emit through insertion of part of it. And since there is no way to use so, we are not concerned presumptively. With this the Shach's challenge from Orach Chaim in Beit Avraham is resolved: a vessel forbidden from issur absorption that fell in part into a pot — one need nullify only that part; it deals where they did not use part only all of it, and in such a case it does not emit from all of it. But if we know that within twenty-four hours part was inserted into kli rishon, one estimates for all of it — investigate. Long afterward I found Peleti Mahadura Kama agreed with my resolution of the passage in perek Dam Chatat. And so agreed for practical law; only he wrote specifically when the whole vessel is hot — see there. Again I saw in Beit Avraham he disagrees with Shach that it does not carry absorption throughout, but wrote it carries absorption throughout but does not emit from all of it even when hot. It seems he did not see responsum Penei Moshe mentioned above.
