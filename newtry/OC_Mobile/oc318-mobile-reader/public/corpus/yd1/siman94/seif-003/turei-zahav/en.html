Fruits or cold. Taz wrote in general rule 85 and Sha'arei Dura that even hot water to rinse the head or to do things not needed for eating and drinking is permitted, and so is the practice — unlike Mordechai perek Ein Ma'amidin that forbids using a moist thing; and he further wrote: per this practice one may be lenient also if a pot was forbidden from basar b'chalav and after twenty-four hours they cooked food in it — one need not throw the pot's value into the river as Maharai ruled — until here; and Rashal perek Kol HaBasar siman 46 disagrees with Rama, for the practice is not to warm but only to rinse the head in general, not water fit for any food and drink; and there is proof that rinsing the head is permitted even l'chatchila, etc.; therefore one must throw the pot's value into the river — until Rashal's words. And all this in a forbidden pot; but forbidden food — Issur VeHeter HaAroch and Rashal and Taz wrote one must throw it specifically in the latrine, not before a dog even if not his.
