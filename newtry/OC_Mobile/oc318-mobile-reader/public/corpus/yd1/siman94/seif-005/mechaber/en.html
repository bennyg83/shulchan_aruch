If they cooked water in a new pot and inserted into it a dairy spoon, and afterward they again cooked water in it another time and inserted into it a meat spoon, and both spoons were bnei yoman, and at none of the times was there sixty in the water — it is forbidden to use the pot, neither for meat nor for milk; but other things are permitted to cook in it since it was new and they had never cooked in it before: {Rama: However, if one transgressed and cooked meat or milk in it, it is permitted because it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 9). A pot in which they cooked vegetables or water and inserted into it a ben yomo spoon and the pot is not a ben yomo, or the reverse, or there is sixty in the food — everything is permitted; and they are accustomed to be stringent to eat the food like the type of the vessel that is a ben yomo and to forbid the vessel that is not a ben yomo (Arukh general rule 37); and this is only a stringency in itself, for by law everything is permitted:}
