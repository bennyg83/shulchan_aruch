If they cooked, etc. — until the end of the seif: in truth Mechaber did not intend well here, and the Rav too did not get to the point; therefore I must expand, for thus wrote Semag (siman 213 daf 103a): A case came before my teacher R' Yom Tov in a new pot in which they cooked water and inserted a ben yoma dairy spoon and there was not sixty in the water to nullify; and after a day they cooked other water in it and inserted a spoon used for meat, likewise ben yoma, and again there was not sixty in the water to nullify; and he ruled that taste was absorbed from basar b'chalav in a spoiled manner and the pot is forbidden to use for meat or milk, but to cook fish in it is permitted. It seems since the pot was new and they never cooked milk in it in actual form, even though a ben yoma dairy spoon was inserted and there is no sixty in the pot, nevertheless the pot does not become dairy thereby to forbid if they afterward cook meat in it the same day, for it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am: taste going out to the spoon, from the spoon to the water in the pot, and from the water to the pot itself — and it is still permitted. And likewise if after twenty-four hours from inserting the dairy spoon they heated water in it and inserted a ben yoma meat spoon, it is permitted to use the pot l'chatchila for meat, and milk taste absorbed in the pot is not considered taste, as explained. But truly as the Rav ruled, a pot absorbed from basar b'chalav even in a spoiled manner is forbidden l'chatchila for meat or milk; you find this if a dairy pot was used for milk in actual form and afterward meat was used — the meat is permitted but the pot is forbidden l'chatchila for meat or milk because it is absorbed from spoiled basar b'chalav — end (so is the law in Semag books printed in Krakow and copied in Kol Bo siman 106 daf 122b and Hagahot Shmuel David siman 85 and Orach Chaim Maharshal there and in Semag printed in Venice — the law is mistaken). Explanation: the case was they cooked water in it again after twenty-four hours, as he concludes "and he ruled absorbed from spoiled basar b'chalav"; and Rif challenges Maharai's ruling and says: it seems since the pot was new it does not become dairy, etc.; therefore why is it permitted l'chatchila for meat — milk taste is not taste; and the Rav's law that in basar b'chalav it is forbidden and other things permitted you find if meat was cooked in a dairy pot used for milk in actual form when not ben yomo; and so is clear in Kol Bo there explicitly, and in Orach Chaim general rule 35 explicitly, and it is plain. If so one may wonder on Mechaber why he forbids cooking meat specifically, especially since he permits below siman 95 noten ta'am bar noten ta'am in cooking; and further how he wrote "since it was new," etc. — implies if he first cooked milk in actual form it is forbidden to cook other things, unlike below siman 95 seif 3 that meat bowls rinsed in a dairy cauldron with boiling water, even if both ben yomo, everything is permitted as noten ta'am bar noten ta'am; and on Rama too one may wonder, for he rules plainly like Mechaber, and for us in three noten ta'am all agree; nevertheless it is not difficult — why not permit even milk, since one must establish one use for this pot, therefore establish meat use, for more of the last taste remains primary; and all the more if they used actual meat in this pot into which a ben yoma dairy spoon was first inserted — permitted to cook meat afterward; but after first spoon insertion we would not permit the pot for meat l'chatchila, since one must establish a use — from milk absorbed — end. And certainly a great refutation is on Beit Yosef and Rama; it seems to resolve with difficulty: spoons were ben yomo and presumably grease sticks as in siman 95 seif 3 — say clearly no grease; if so actual taste, nevertheless Rama holds b'dieved permitted as noten ta'am bar noten ta'am — two times, as stated. And if water was cooked again after twenty-four hours, etc. — implies if within twenty-four hours from meat cooking it is forbidden even if water was cooked in between, and we do not say absorption weakened; unlike Tur in Rashba siman 93 regarding vegetables; Beit Yosef wrote no difference between vegetables and water; I wrote above to resolve. And Rama: if one transgressed and cooked meat or milk — permitted because noten ta'am bar noten ta'am. Pot in which vegetables or water were cooked and a ben yoma spoon inserted — everything permitted; they are accustomed to be stringent to eat the food like the type of vessel that is ben yomo and forbid the vessel that is not ben yomo — only a general stringency, for by law everything is permitted. Taz: although food is not forbidden, one may not eat it with meat or milk since one can eat without that — like l'chatchila; good to be stringent with food not to eat with meat or milk, but to place in a vessel of one of them no need to be particular. Rashal: practice is to eat from the pot with a new spoon; since only general stringency, practice follows Rama; nevertheless l'chatchila pour into vessel like the ben yomo one. Stringency: consequently Shulchan Aruch "two spoons in new pot" forbidden — also only general stringency; if water cooked in meat vessel not ben yomo and ben yoma dairy spoon inserted — water forbidden as Rama siman 95 seif 3. For us who rule noten ta'am in all issurim — if cooked water in pot within twenty-four hours, count twenty-four hours from heating water; three noten ta'am all permit l'chatchila even to eat in dip; Rosh chapter Kol HaSha'ah: even for those who forbid fish cooked in meat pot in dairy dip — here permitted, for taste from vessel to water and back is not considered taste when it returns to cook on Pesach; Tosafot end Avodah Zarah; Shulchan Aruch HaRav siman 75; Tosafot end Avodah Zarah daf Bat Yoma; Shmuel David siman 85; Mordechai Piskei HaGahot: if water heated in dairy pot within twenty-four hours not considered ben yomo from heating — even those who forbid cooked items agree in three noten ta'am; Hagahot Maimoniyot there; Beit Yosef Orach Chaim siman 452 in Shulchan Shel Leket: even for those who forbid cooked, agree in three noten ta'am — end; Orach Chaim divides general rule 34 between two and three noten ta'am; on Rama one may wonder who rules like Mechaber — for us not so even in three; nevertheless not difficult — establish meat use; all the more actual meat after first insertion — permitted; but after first insertion not l'chatchila for meat — establish from milk — end. Rif permits noten ta'am bar noten ta'am in cooking and rinsing when one not ben yomo; pot here stands on first matter; Rif after twenty-four hours not specifically — within twenty-four hours agrees to Maharai; truly as Rav ruled, etc. — you find dairy pot with milk in actual form, etc. Rif: new pot does not become dairy, etc. — why permit l'chatchila for meat; Rav's law — meat in dairy pot not ben yomo; Kol Bo explicit; Orach Chaim explicit; plain. Wonder on Mechaber; below 95 permits; "since new" implies milk first forbids other things — unlike 95 seif 3; wonder on Rama; for us three noten ta'am agree; not difficult — establish meat; actual meat after dairy spoon — permitted; after first insertion not l'chatchila meat — milk — end. Refutation on Beit Yosef and Rama; resolve: ben yomo spoons, no grease per 95 seif 3; actual taste; Rama b'dieved noten ta'am bar noten ta'am. Printed Semag mistaken case: new pot, water, dairy spoon; after days water again; "it seems pot new never milk" — not objection above but at beginning if second spoon within twenty-four hours; after all objections not resolved; plain Semag Krakow/Kol Bo correct; Venice mistaken; great stretch to say Rif holds noten ta'am l'chatchila within twenty-four hours forbids even other things — he permits noten ta'am bar noten ta'am l'chatchila; if dairy pot with milk in actual form why permit other things l'chatchila — say noten ta'am lifgam forbids l'chatchila — not noten ta'am l'chatchila; plain as I wrote; even Maharshal stringent on fish in dip permits here pot for meat after last insertion; Rosh Kol HaSha'ah; three noten ta'am; water taste not taste on Pesach; Tosafot; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 75; Tosafot Bat Yoma; Shmuel David 85; Mordechai: water heated in dairy pot within twenty-four hours not ben yomo from heating — three noten ta'am; Hagahot Maimoniyot; Beit Yosef 452 Shulchan Shel Leket; Orach Chaim 34 two vs three; Rama like Mechaber — for us not so in three; not difficult; meat use; actual meat permitted; after first not l'chatchila meat — milk.
