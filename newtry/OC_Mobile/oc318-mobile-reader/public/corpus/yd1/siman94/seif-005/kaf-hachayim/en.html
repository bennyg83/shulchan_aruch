(5) And likewise if a woman said "certainly it was inserted" or "until here was the dish in the pot" to estimate with sixty, or if she said the pot was not yad soledes bo — she is believed, for a woman is believed even in issur; Orach Chaim Tashbetz IV Chut HaMeshulash siman 42. Erech HaShulchan section 3, unlike Rav Beit Yehudah I Yoreh De'ah siman 41. And so Yad Efraim on this seif that a woman is believed, unlike Rav Beit Yehudah — and so Zivchei Tzedek section 5: in practice every day we believe women whether in basar b'chalav, salting, or absorption of issur, since handling the dish is in their hands and only women are at home, and we never found anyone who doubted this — end.
