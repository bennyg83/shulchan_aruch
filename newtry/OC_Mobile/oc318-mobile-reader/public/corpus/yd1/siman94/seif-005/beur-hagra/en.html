If they cooked, etc. — Shach and Peri Chadash challenged Shulchan Aruch and explain Semag's words in one answer and as in perek 8 (111b) that noten ta'am bar noten ta'am is permitted; and even per Tosafot there that only "they were cooked" is not so in three noten ta'am — all agree; see Tosafot at the end of Avodah Zarah 76a s.v. daughter and s.v. from here, etc., and Chullin 108b s.v. that fell, etc., and Ra'ash perek 2 of Pesachim and Sha'ar HaPesach; and all the more Shulchan Aruch ruled in siman 95.
Everything is permitted — for without ben yomo it receives spoiled taste, and that which is not ben yomo receives noten ta'am bar noten ta'am.
In the type of the vessel — as should be read — Shach there.
And it is only, etc. — as stated above siman 95 seif 2 in the gloss; but to place in a vessel, etc., and there seif 3 in the gloss unless one, etc.; unlike Tosafot 112a s.v. the law and if the one is not ben yomo, etc.
