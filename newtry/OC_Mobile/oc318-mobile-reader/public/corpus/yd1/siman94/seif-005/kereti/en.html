Not meat, etc. — and even though it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am, since it is a new pot and it is not known in which type it will be used, meat or milk, they are stringent l'chatchila without using it for meat or milk.
Like the type of the vessel. And primarily: if possible to eat without meat or milk fat they eat only like the type of the vessel; but if impossible to eat so, it is b'dieved and they eat with meat or milk, for this b'dieved of having no choice to eat without fat.
