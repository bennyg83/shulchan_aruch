For it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am. From the spoon to the water and from the water to the pot — both times to the permitted element; for even the second time it is permitted by law, and what is forbidden l'chatchila is because the practice spread to forbid what is not ben yomo, as stated afterward — so Taz; and it seems the reason they were stringent in the pot here is because they place the food in a vessel like the vessel that is ben yomo; therefore it is not correct to permit the vessel that is not ben yomo since they pour from it, as Rama intended; and one may challenge Tur who wrote: and after it stood twenty-four hours from cooking the water, etc. — it implies if within twenty-four hours from meat cooking it is forbidden even if within that time they cooked water, and we do not say the absorbed taste was weakened; and this is not as Tur wrote in the name of Rashba siman 93 regarding vegetables that the absorption was weakened from the vegetables; and Beit Yosef wrote there is no difference between vegetables and water; and I wrote above to resolve this.
Or there is sixty in the food — everything is permitted. Even if both are ben yomo. And Taz challenged per the first reason in siman 95 seif 3 on those who forbid: we are concerned lest bowls touched the pan and likewise lest the spoon touched the pot — what does sixty help, the spoon still absorbs and there is a second taste; one must say he holds like the second reason there, concern lest water was forbidden, etc.; and here there is no such concern since there is sixty.
And they are accustomed to be stringent to eat the food, etc. Taz general rule 57 wrote: although the food is not forbidden, one may not eat it with meat or milk, since one can eat it without that — it is like l'chatchila; therefore it is good to be stringent with the food not to eat with meat or milk, but to place it in a vessel of one of them there is no need to be particular, as stated — end; and Rashal perek Gid HaNasheh siman 43 wrote the practice is to eat from the pot with a new spoon; and since it is only a general stringency, the practice follows Rama; nevertheless l'chatchila one should be careful to pour into a vessel like the one that is ben yomo specifically — so appears to me.
And it is only a general stringency. Consequently also what Shulchan Aruch wrote "if two spoons in a new pot," etc., that the pot is forbidden — this too is only a general stringency, for it is one reason with this law; and if water was cooked in a meat vessel that is not ben yomo and a ben yomo dairy spoon was inserted, the water is forbidden as Rama wrote in siman 95 seif 3; and see the reason I wrote there.
