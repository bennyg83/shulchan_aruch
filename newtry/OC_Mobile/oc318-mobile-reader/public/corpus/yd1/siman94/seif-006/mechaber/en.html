Onions or vegetables that absorbed from meat and they cooked them in a dairy pot — if it is known how much meat was absorbed in the onions and vegetables, sixty is not required, only against the meat: {Rama: For it is not relevant to say the piece becomes nevelah since it is still entirely permitted; therefore one need only estimate against what was absorbed; and all the more so in a dairy pot in which they cooked water within twenty-four hours and afterward cooked meat in it — we do not say that one must estimate against all the water, only against the milk that the pot absorbed (meaning of Tur and Beit Yosef):}
