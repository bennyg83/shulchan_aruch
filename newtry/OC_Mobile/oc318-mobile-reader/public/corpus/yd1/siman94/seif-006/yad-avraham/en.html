(there) Everything is forbidden in benefit, even the pot — for it is cooking and forbidden from the Torah, therefore forbidden in benefit. Erech Shai perek 7; if so forbidden l'chatchila to insert a ben yomo dairy spoon in a meat pot or the reverse, for it is cooking in basar b'chalav. And we say in the Gemara Piskei HaGahot (97) and Zevachim perek Dam Chatat (96) and so Taz siman 93: a pot in which meat was cooked — one may not cook milk in it. He speaks of actual cooking that is forbidden, for it is cooking milk within fat of meat emitted from the pot to the water. And do not challenge from what I wrote above siman 87 seif 6 that in absorbed matter in a vessel cooking does not apply — one may say that is where there is no actual matter, for meat and milk were both absorbed in the vessel; but where meat is in actual form and milk is absorbed, it is cooking meat within milk emitted to water, or the reverse. And one must say this is stronger than roasting a pot and types of frying that are not cooking, as in Solut LehMincha general rule 85 per Tosafot Sanhedrin — that only through water is it considered cooking, unlike where not liquid like water.
