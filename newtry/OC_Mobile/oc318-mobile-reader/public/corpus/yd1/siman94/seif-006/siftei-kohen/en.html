Onions or vegetables, etc. — Meaning from the meat itself, or he cut them with a meat knife finely; but if he cut in one place of them — see above siman 96 what the law is.Tur wrote in Sefer HaTerumah: even in other issurim, a pot of issur that stood twenty-four hours without issur — if hot water was heated in it within twenty-four hours it is considered ben yomo, for issur taste in the pot emitted into the water and there was no sixty to nullify; we estimate for the whole pot and it becomes ben yoma and needs twenty-four hours after heating the water; but in basar b'chalav it is not so: if they cooked meat in it and within twenty-four hours heated water and afterward cooked milk — permitted even within twenty-four hours of meat cooking, for still entirely permitted; therefore only estimate against meat absorbed in onions; Semag (and Beit Yosef) reason: water in the pot itself is issur from noten ta'am, unlike basar b'chalav where still all permitted — noten ta'am bar noten ta'am; Beit Yosef end siman 103 for poskim who say noten ta'am only in basar b'chalav — this law not found in other issurim (see end siman 103 in Shach and gloss). But Tur wrote in Sefer HaTerumah generally and below siman 121 generally — he does not hold so; he ruled siman 92 we do not say noten ta'am in other issurim; rather other reason: noten ta'am bar noten ta'am does not apply to issur matter; Ran chapter 25: noten ta'am bar noten ta'am only where permitted cooked with meat in basar b'chalav — when second taste still permitted, since so much joined not considered to forbid; but in issur matter wherever taste exists it forbids forever — end; also clear there even for those who do not say noten ta'am in other issurim. See siman 95 s.k. 5; Shmuel David siman 85; Tosafot Avodah Zarah 76a end d.h. bat yoma — we count twenty-four hours from last cooking; Rosh end Avodah Zarah: if cooked therein, needs twenty-four hours from last cooking; Ma'adanei Melech: seems Rosh holds noten ta'am in all issurim and Tur/Beit Yosef missed Rosh — not so; matter clear as I explained; Rashba responsum 526 like Ran (and so Rosh chapter Kol HaSha'ah); Rashba holds noten ta'am not in other issurim; Beit Yosef siman 95 all poskim agree in issur matter noten ta'am bar noten ta'am does not apply; Taz general rule 57 plain from Ran's reason. Beit Yosef end siman 103: where absorption from basar b'chalav cooked together or alternately, all agree when water heated within twenty-four hours considered as issur cooked again — count from heating — end; he followed his view; not clear — poskim' reason in basar b'chalav each alone permitted — meat becomes issur — unlike here where absorbed in pot already forbidden, noten ta'am does not apply; for us who rule noten ta'am in all issurim no relevance — end. Difficult: why sixty here against milk if water cooked within twenty-four hours — noten ta'am bar noten ta'am milk to pot to hot water back to pot as Sefer HaTerumah? Say not known all taste went to water — doubt; if pot not ben yomo permitted — if all to water noten ta'am bar noten ta'am, if not noten ta'am lifgam; in other issurim why count ben yomo from heating and not say in any case whether pot emitted or not — perhaps emitted less than sixty and nullified, noten ta'am only rabbinic, rabbinic doubt lenient? Say rabbis enacted since doubt how much emits — stringency lest all emitted and all became nevelah; siman 98 pouring min b'mino permitted from rabbinic doubt — specifically pouring where one could estimate; here impossible to estimate — enact estimate for whole pot — investigate; water in issur vessel within twenty-four hours — noten ta'am and count from heating — see end siman 103. All the more below siman 96 Rama: only sixty against place removed.
That absorbed from meat, etc. — I would not challenge other issurim; this comes to exclude onions all absorbed from milk then meat — not enough sixty against meat alone; for us who rule noten ta'am in all issurim, specifically absorption from meat; Maharam of Lublin responsum: if sixty against meat in onion, onion permitted even not crushed; but if cut with forbidden knife, even with sixty separate onions by straining, for we hold "possible to squeeze" forbidden — end; see siman 96.
