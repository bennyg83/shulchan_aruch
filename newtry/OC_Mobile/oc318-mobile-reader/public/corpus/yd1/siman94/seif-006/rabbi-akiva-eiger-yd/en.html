(seif 6) And they cooked in a dairy pot. At first glance it would seem the precise wording is for that reason and he did not write "they cooked in milk" — the case is a pot of milk, but there is no milk in the pot only something else; thus sixty against the meat helps and the onions themselves are permitted, for there is no concern milk taste entered the onions and forbade them and would forbid the dish afterward if there is no sixty against all the onions, for milk taste in the dish before entering the onions is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am of the permitted; and if there is no sixty against the meat that is forbidden; even per Mechaber (siman 95 seif 3) here it is forbidden, for from onions to water it is not called noten ta'am bar noten ta'am since it was not absorbed in a vessel and did not become attached; but if they cooked in milk, one may say sixty is needed against all the onions, for milk entered them and became noten ta'am and went out and forbids, and the onions remain in their issur; but from Maharam of Lublin's words it does not appear so, and so from Shulchan Aruch gloss (siman 95 seif 2) — investigate:
