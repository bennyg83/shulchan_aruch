Only against the meat — see Peleti where I challenged that one must estimate against all the onions that became noten ta'am from basar b'chalav — see what he answered there.
Not required, etc. Maharam of Lublin wrote the onions too are permitted; see Peleti where I left it in doubt; and for practical law it seems the onions are forbidden.
Against, etc. — and if the onions absorbed from basar b'chalav, Beit Yosef holds the onions are noten ta'am and sixty is needed against the onions even for Rashi; and Shach and Peri Chadash hold onions are like other issurim, for "each alone is permitted" applies only when permitted absorbs taste from issur and there is no noten ta'am — per Rashi; and so is primary.
Within twenty-four hours — then we count twenty-four hours from meat cooking, not from water cooking; what was emitted to water is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am of the permitted and is entirely permitted, and we are concerned only for meat absorbed that did not emit in water and needs twenty-four hours from that time; specifically if meat was cooked; but if milk was cooked and afterward water — per those who say noten ta'am in other issurim, we count twenty-four hours from water cooking for all; but per those who do not say noten ta'am in other issurim, Shach still raised that it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am of issur, and what milk emits to hot water is issur and from that time it forbids; see Peleti: we forbid only according to the measure of absorption, not more, for if we required against all absorbed milk one would need twenty-four hours from milk cooking.
