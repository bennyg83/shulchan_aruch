Because it is noten ta'am, etc. Mordechai there on this law wrote the reason it is permitted because grease in honey is noten ta'am lifgam, and he concludes: but if it were for benefit it would forbid; and Beit Yosef thinks the reason for permitting this honey is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am; and there is a difference in Mordechai's reason: even if there were actual meat in the pan in which they cooked the honey, then it would be forbidden and not noten ta'am bar noten ta'am; nevertheless here it is permitted because it is noten ta'am lifgam; if so Shulchan Aruch should not have omitted Mordechai's reason.
