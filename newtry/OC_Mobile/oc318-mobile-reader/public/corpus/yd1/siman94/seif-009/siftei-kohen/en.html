Of the permitted element. Mordechai there on this law: grease in honey is noten ta'am lifgam; concludes: if for benefit would forbid; Beit Yosef: reason permitting is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am; difference in Mordechai: even if actual meat in pan where honey cooked — forbidden, not noten ta'am bar noten ta'am; here permitted because noten ta'am lifgam; Shulchan Aruch should not omit Mordechai — end. Clear case: poured and stream did not stop; otherwise permitted siman 98 seif 6; only honey permitted, bowl forbidden per Rama siman 95 seif 3; cannot say permitted per Mordechai lifgam — Rama siman 103 seif 4: without great loss stringent grease in honey not lifgam; possibly beverage from honey — all agree lifgam; Mechaber implies bowl permitted; perhaps double advantage — many poskim permit much cooking in dip, some grease in honey lifgam — Rama permits even bowl; siman 103 seif 2 gloss.
