(9) A barley dish into which they put one part butter in forty of the dish and stirred it with a spoon and inserted the spoon into a meat pot that does not have sixty against the butter put in the barley dish — the teachers ruled forbidden, unlike those who permit. Responsum Penei Aryeh Chadashot siman 48. Birkei Yosef there section 2; but Erech HaShulchan there wrote that per what all hold, the spoon forbids only per the calculation of one in forty, and if so the meat dish is permitted. And so Peri To'ar siman 95 section 1.
