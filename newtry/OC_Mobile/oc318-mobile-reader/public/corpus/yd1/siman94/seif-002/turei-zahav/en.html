In Terumat HaDeshen siman 283 he gave a reason to require two times of sixty: we are concerned lest the spoon did not first emit all the forbidden absorbed matter and part of sixty remained and permitted matter in the pot entered in parts and the absorbed matter in the spoon became forbidden and the whole became nevelah, and another sixty is needed against what renewed in the spoon from first insertion [that emits within the pot on second insertion]. And he rejected this, for forbidden absorbed does not forbid permitted absorbed; and he further sought a reason to permit one time of sixty. And Rashal perek Gid HaNasheh siman 40 was stringent to require two times of sixty — we have only the practice Rama wrote to be lenient, both in basar b'chalav and other issurim.
