If the spoon was inserted twice, etc. — the reason: Hagahot Maimoniyot on Chullin and Maharil responsum siman 161 and Terumat HaDeshen siman 283 wrote: initially sixty is needed against the milk, for we are concerned lest a little milk remained; and when they removed it from the pot the entire spoon became nevelah from basar b'chalav, therefore the spoon must be nullified the second time. It seems therefore Taz wrote if inserted twice, etc., requires two times of sixty, and they did not write generally; and if inserted many times, sixty is needed each time — because they follow their view that we do not say noten ta'am only in basar b'chalav, for there each alone is permitted by itself and through joining they become forbidden, unlike other issurim, as I wrote in siman 92 s.k. 10; therefore when inserted a second time there is no longer room to say noten ta'am, since the spoon was already forbidden; even if inserted many times, two times of sixty suffice; and Semak who holds noten ta'am in all issurim truly did not write in siman 213 this language that two times are required — see there and investigate.
And it was not known in the interim. For if known in the interim the first was already nullified, and the second time too was nullified in those sixty; and even though Beit Yosef's view seems below end siman 99 that if an olive of issur fell into sixty olives of heter and afterward another olive of issur fell — it returns and is stirred even min b'mino even if known in the interim — nevertheless here there is no reason at all, for there is sixty against the entire spoon and the spoon would be all milk, only from noten ta'am two times of sixty are needed (and even though min b'mino also does not impart taste, nevertheless since it can impart taste when not min b'mino it is forbidden, and this does not apply here — investigate, end); as Ramban and Ran wrote at the end of Avodah Zarah and Beit Yosef brings there, and this does not apply here — investigate.
And some say one time of sixty suffices. For many poskim hold that in absorbed matter we do not say noten ta'am, as below siman 98 seif 5; and so too in Terumat HaDeshen siman 283 that we rely on this reason; and even though we rule there stringently, nevertheless in such a case there is no reason to be stringent; and even regarding the issur itself that fell into the pot many times we hold one need only one time of sixty, as below siman 98 end seif 4 in the gloss; and Maharshal and Bach have other views here but we do not rule so; and see siman 99 s.k. 21.
