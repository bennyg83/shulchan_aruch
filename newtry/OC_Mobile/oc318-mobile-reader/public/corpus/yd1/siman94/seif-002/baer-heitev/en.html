Maharil's reason: initially sixty is needed against all the milk, for we are concerned lest a little milk remained; and when they removed it from the pot the entire spoon became nevelah from basar b'chalav, therefore the spoon must be nullified the second time. It seems therefore Mechaber specified "if inserted twice," etc., and did not write generally that many times require sixty each time — because he follows his view that we do not say noten ta'am only in basar b'chalav, for there each alone is permitted by itself; therefore when inserted twice there is no longer room to say noten ta'am, since the spoon was already forbidden, even if inserted many times two times of sixty suffice; specifically if not known in the interim; but if known in the interim the first was already nullified and the second time too was nullified in those sixty; and even though Beit Yosef's view seems below end siman 99 that if an olive of issur fell into sixty olives of heter and afterward another olive of issur fell — it returns and is stirred even min b'mino even if known in the interim — nevertheless here there is no reason at all, for there is sixty against the spoon and the spoon would be all milk, only from noten ta'am two times of sixty are needed (and even though min b'mino also does not impart taste, nevertheless since it can impart taste against it when not min b'mino it is forbidden, and this does not apply here — investigate, end); see end siman 92 what Taz challenges from here on what Maharil wrote in Pesach laws regarding hag'alah from kli rishon — see there.
That suffices. Shach wrote even regarding the issur itself that fell into the pot many times we hold one need only one time of sixty, as below siman 98 end seif 4 in the gloss.
