They estimate for everything inserted from it into the pot, etc. — this applies when the vessel is hard: one estimates only against what was inserted; but in soft food one estimates against all of it — e.g., one inserted a cold piece of meat into a dairy pot and not all of it entered the gravy, only part — one estimates against all of it.
