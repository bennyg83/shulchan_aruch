(8) And know: if one inserted a meat spoon or an issur spoon into a permitted pot — that the poskim wrote one must estimate for the entire spoon applies when it was inserted into the issur itself, where everything in the first pot was issur, e.g., only milk was cooking in the first pot into which the spoon was inserted, for then all it absorbed is issur and we do not know how much it absorbed, therefore we estimate for all of it. But if the dish was permitted cooking in the pot and milk or issur became mixed in it and the spoon was inserted there and the spoon was forbidden because of that, and afterward they inserted it into a permitted pot — then one need not estimate against the entire spoon; rather we estimate as if a portion of that dish was taken — if the spoon holds a liter, for example, we consider as if one liter of that issur dish fell into the permitted dish, then we calculate how much issur reaches that liter of dish. The analogy: in pot 1 they cooked ten liters of dish and ten ounces of issur fell in — each liter has one ounce; if one liter of dish was taken and fell into a permitted pot, certainly one need not estimate except against the one ounce in it, for it is simple there is no noten ta'am le'heter; therefore this spoon, even if we consider it all dish, one liter in that pot's dish need not be estimated except against what it absorbed. Rav R' Shlomo Amar z"l in Piskei Dinim 20. Birkei Yosef there. Erech HaShulchan section 2; Zivchei Tzedek section 7; and he wrote so is our practice to rule.
