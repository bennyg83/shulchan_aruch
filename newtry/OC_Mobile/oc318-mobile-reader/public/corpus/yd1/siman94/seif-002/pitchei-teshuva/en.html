In a kli rishon. See Beit HaTosafot s.k. 2 in the name of Rashal; and see responsum Chavot Yair siman 101 who wrote: if they boiled milk in a pot and it stood until after it cooled many hours passed and they emptied the milk from it, and the next day they cooked meat in it — if it was close after twenty-four hours from boiling such that yesterday at this hour it was still hot, more than lukewarm whose measure is like the heat of spit — it seems to forbid, for although it was removed from the fire, nevertheless kli rishon remained there and they made distance and a fence; and all the more regarding that kli rishon absorbs and carries — so much more what is in it; and it needs twenty-four hours after it comes to lukewarm temperature — see there; and see below siman 105 s.k. 5 what is written there.
