Regarding chametz on Pesach, hagalah suffices, since heter was absorbed; and so the reason appears in Beit Yosef, and so it appears from the Mechaber's language here; and so too Pri Toar; and accordingly also in a pan of milk or meat hagalah suffices, since heter was absorbed. But in the responsum of Menachem Azariah siman 96 he wrote the reason: because a pan of gentiles — most of their use is without water but through other liquids such as oil and the like, etc.; not so with a pan of chametz, etc. — end of his words; and accordingly also in a pan of milk or meat libun is required, since it absorbs without any intermediary.
