(In another place) Rather it means that it absorbed only in part. In my humble opinion there is no need to force this, for in its simple meaning one may say he stated the reason as it absorbs so it emits, to teach that if he cooked in a pot in part of the vessel — which is the whole — nevertheless the absorption spread throughout; nevertheless he need not kasher all of it, only the part in which he cooked, for as it absorbs so it emits.
