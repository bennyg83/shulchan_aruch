On Pesach. Shach wrote the reason: since heter was absorbed; and accordingly also in a pan of milk or meat, hagalah suffices since heter was absorbed. But in the responsum of Menachem Azariah he wrote the reason: because a pan of gentiles — most of their use is without water but through other liquids such as oil and the like, etc.; not so with a pan of chametz; and accordingly also in a pan of milk or meat one needs libun, since it absorbs without any intermediary. (And in Orach Chaim siman 499 seif 5 Magen Avraham wrote that the world practiced prohibition to kasher a meat vessel for milk or the reverse; and the reason he wrote in the name of the Gaon Rabbi Benjamin of Pozna — see there.)
