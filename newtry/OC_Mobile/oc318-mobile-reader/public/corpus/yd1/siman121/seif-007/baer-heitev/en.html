L'chatchila. Shach wrote: but b'dieved, if he used it boiling after scouring, even if it is ben yomo it is permitted as after hagalah. Levush wrote: if he kashered two ben yomo vessels or more in water that did not have sixty against both of them but there was sixty against one of them — if he kashered them at once, certainly their hagalah did not succeed, for there was not sixty against both; and if he kashered them one after another, some say their hagalah succeeded, for the taste of the prohibition was already nullified in the water before he kashered the second, and the water remained permitted when he kashered the second; and some forbid even in this because the prohibition returns and is reawakened and forbids the second as well — end of his words; and so is the essential view, since the water is forbidden by law, why should the vessels not be forbidden? And what they kasher on Pesach many vessels in a cauldron is no proof, for there they are not ben yomo — end of Shach's words.
