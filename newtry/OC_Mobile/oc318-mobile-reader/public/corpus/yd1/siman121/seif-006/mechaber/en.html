With metal utensils: even though there are those who say that if one used issur in part of it, the whole becomes forbidden, because “the hot of part of it is the hot of all of it” — nevertheless, regarding its kashering, it does not become kasher until he kasheres all of it, whether regarding hag'alah or regarding libbun. {Rama: And this is specifically if he used it in all of it; but if it is known that he used it only in part of it — k'bol'o kach polto (Tur).}
