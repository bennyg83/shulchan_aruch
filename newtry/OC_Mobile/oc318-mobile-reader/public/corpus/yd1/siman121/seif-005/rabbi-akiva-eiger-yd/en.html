(Shach s.k. 15) That they buy from the market. It implies that if he bought in a gentile's house it is forbidden from doubt; and see in Mishbetzot Zahav (Orach Chaim siman 451).
(Shach s.k. 17) But Maharam Mintz wrote. And even though at the time of libun the entire vessel becomes hot and we might say that before the absorption burned throughout the vessel and afterward only in the place of libun was burned — it must be that absorption in the vessel does not spread from place to place in the vessel itself through the heat of the vessel without gravy.
(In another place) Rather it means that it absorbed only in part. In my humble opinion there is no need to force this, for in its simple meaning one may say he stated the reason as it absorbs so it emits, to teach that if he cooked in a pot in part of the vessel — which is the whole — nevertheless the absorption spread throughout; nevertheless he need not kasher all of it, only the part in which he cooked, for as it absorbs so it emits.
(In another place) It also carries its emission throughout. And this is with oil, for in weak roasting it does not forbid the vessel only thus; and it is possible that in a vessel as well we say we are not expert, as for emission according to Shach's view (siman 105 s.k. 123).
