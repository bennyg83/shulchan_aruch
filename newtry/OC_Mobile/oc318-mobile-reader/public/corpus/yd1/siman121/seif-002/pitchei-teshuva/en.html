Of metal. [And see in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 113 regarding iron pots made in the community of Krakow, which are coated in smelting (Gussmetall) with a thin white coating less than the thickness of a peel, and the body of the smelting is very hard and appears like iron; and the world lacks knowledge of what the Gussmetall is made from, because the craftsmen conceal their work; and he wrote that if treifot occurred in them, one should not perform hagalah on them, for libun is required from doubt; however to fill with coals is considered fine, for presumably we need not be concerned that they burst; and if they burst, behold it is earthenware, for it burst through fire; but if they did not burst, we cannot say that hagalah does not succeed for them, because we are lenient on them since it is only a coating and also there is doubt whether it is earthenware — permitted to rely on this; and if he boiled so much that the knife burned from outside, the iron was also kashered and is permitted — see there.]
