And to immerse them. Shach wrote: it appears one should return and immerse them without a blessing. Levush wrote: and if he does not wish to use them except cold, he may immerse them and use them cold; and when he afterward wishes to use them hot, he purges the vessel and the immersion that he already immersed them suffices for everyone, since at first they immersed them in heter, he does not need immersion again — end of his words. And it appears that where they immersed them with intent to eat in them hot, since he must return and immerse them for the first opinion, if so even cold is forbidden to eat without a second immersion.
