If he purchased from them utensils that were used with hot, whether they are of metal, or of wood, or of stone, he performs hag'alah on them, and afterwards immerses them if they are of metal, and they are permitted. And if he immersed them and afterwards performed hag'alah on them, they are permitted. And there are those who say that one must return and immerse them again. {Rama: The law of bone utensils — see the laws of Pesach, siman 451. One should not perform hag'alah on any utensil so long as it is ben yomo (Tur). And one should not use the water of hag'alah (Arukh). Any place that requires hag'alah, it does not help if he peeled the utensil with craftsman’s tools (Terumat HaDeshen siman 130, and in Mordechai and Hagahot ShaD); and see above siman 108, the law of a meradeh of issur.}
