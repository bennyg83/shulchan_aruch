He kashered them, etc. And the Tur wrote: how is hagalah performed? He scours the vessel well to remove the rust in it, and afterward he kashered them, etc.; and see in Orach Chaim siman 451 seif 3 and seif 5.
And some say, etc. For it is like one who immerses while a sheretz is in his hand; Pri Toar wrote: and if he does not wish to use it except cold, he may immerse it and use it cold; and when he afterward wishes to use it hot, he purges it and the immersion that he already immersed them suffices, even for those who require a second immersion; for here it is different, since at first his intent was not to use it except cold, and for cold immersion suffices for him; and since at first they immersed them in heter, he does not need immersion again — end of his words. And it appears that where they immersed them with intent to eat in them hot, since he must return and immerse them for the first opinion, if so even cold is forbidden to eat without immersion; and so it appears from the words of the Rosh and Tur.
To return and immerse them. It appears one should return and immerse them without a blessing; and see above siman 120.
One may not kasher, etc. See above siman 94 and siman 95, and in Orach Chaim siman 452.
