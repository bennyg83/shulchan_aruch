He pays. Since it is forbidden to raise small domesticated animals in Eretz Yisrael, as stated in Choshen Mishpat siman 419, we fine him so that he takes only half. Alternatively, since it is forbidden to raise small domesticated animals in Eretz Yisrael, he would have had great trouble keeping them in forests and deserts where there is no settlement; therefore half the value of a large domesticated animal is enough for him. Shach. The Taz wrote: it appears to me that he should return it to the seller and the seller should bury it. It is obvious that if the seller wants this, he is obligated to give it to him, for the seller wants to fulfill the mitzvah. Even if he does not want it, nevertheless the burden is placed on him, since he caused this. One should not say that he should not give it to him because he is suspected in this matter, for he is not suspected of eating it, as stated in siman 119 seif 2. End quote.
