And he then reimburses him. Even if the Jew could not have discharged his obligation to the king except with wine, it is permitted, since he did not explicitly instruct the non-Jew to give the king ordinary non-Jewish wine. So writes the Ateres Zahav, based on Tosafos as cited by the Beis Yosef.
Be in my place, etc. This implies that even if he could have appeased the king with other things, nevertheless, once he says, “Be in my place, etc.,” it is forbidden according to all opinions. Bach. See further there.
