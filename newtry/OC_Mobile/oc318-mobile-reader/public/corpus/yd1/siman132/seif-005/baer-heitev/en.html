Appeased. Even where the Jew could not extricate himself from the official except with wine, it is permitted, since he did not explicitly tell the non-Jew to give the official yayin nesech. End quote from Levush, and it is from Tosafot. But where he said “be in my place,” the Bach wrote that it implies that even if he could have appeased him with other things, nevertheless since he said, “be in my place,” etc., it is prohibited according to all.
