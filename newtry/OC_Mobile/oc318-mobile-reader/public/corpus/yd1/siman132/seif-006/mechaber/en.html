A minister who would distribute his wine to the people and take its value from them: a Jew may not say to a non-Jew, “Here are 4 zuz; go in my place into the minister’s storehouse,” so that the non-Jew will take that wine that is written in the name of the Jew and the non-Jew will give the money to the minister; but he may say to him, “Here are 4 zuz, and save me from the storehouse.”
