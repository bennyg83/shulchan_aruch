And enter in my place. Perishah wrote that likewise in this case it is forbidden — even if nothing was yet written about him — only that he says "enter in my place so they do not write about me."
But, etc. And Tur concludes: and some say if they already wrote about the Jew's portion — it is forbidden in any language he says to him; and there is no permit except as long as they have not written — where it is permitted to say to the gentile: appease them on my behalf so they do not write about me — end; and likewise Atzei Zahav.
