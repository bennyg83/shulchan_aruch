But, etc. The Tur concludes: “Some say that if his share has already been recorded against the Jew, it is forbidden regardless of the wording he uses. Permission applies only so long as it has not yet been recorded, in which case he may tell the non-Jew, ‘Appease them on my behalf so that they will not record it against me.’” The Ateres Zahav rules likewise.
