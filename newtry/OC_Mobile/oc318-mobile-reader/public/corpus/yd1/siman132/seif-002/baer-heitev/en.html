It therefore appears that the money is being taken as sale proceeds, not compensation for damage. Moves it. Shach writes that according to Rama in siman 124:17, mere movement without lifting does not forbid. He nevertheless questions why the money is forbidden here, since in siman 126 we rule that a connecting stream forbids only drinking, not benefit, and here the concern appears to be only a connecting stream. He also questions the Mechaber's assumption that the vessel necessarily moves when the non-Jew holds it. The gloss explains that according to the view that a connecting stream joins the wines, when the non-Jew moves the lower vessel the upper stream becomes forbidden through the lower wine. The Gemara's discussion, however, follows the view that a connecting stream is not a connection, and therefore the movement does not matter while the wine is still in the air.
A small amount. If it has any monetary value and belongs to the Jew, it is forbidden. If there is sixty against the wine residue, all the wine is permitted even for drinking, as stated below in siman 134:4. Shach.
That he touched. Shach explains that a connecting stream is not relevant here for benefit, since ordinary non-Jewish wine is joined through a stream only to forbid drinking. He reconciles Rama's rulings by distinguishing between a non-Jew who gently holds the vessel so that the wine falls into its center, where only the first drop at the bottom becomes forbidden, and one who moves the vessel so that the wine touches its rim before entering. Alternatively, the Mechaber's distinction concerns a case where the price was not fixed: if the non-Jew touches the wine, it is forbidden; if the Jew measures into his own vessel without touch, it is permitted. Into the non-Jew's vessel, or a Jewish vessel held and moved by the non-Jew, it becomes forbidden immediately, and the Jew must first receive payment. Beit Yosef adds that if the Jew rents the place to the non-Jew, the wine is permitted, provided it is removed before becoming forbidden, so that the Jew does not receive rent for yayin nesech.
