(There in seif 2) For then perforce he is swinging. See Shach seif 16, who challenges that it is against Gemara chapter "one who hires workers" 71b; and he left it uncertain; and it seems to me: certainly all the proof of R. Eliezer, who said: and if you think drawing by a gentile acquires — from drawing he acquires, and it is libated wine until he touched — all this is according to the one who says stream is not connection; but according to the one who says stream is connection, one may well say drawing by a gentile acquires; and nevertheless if he did not take the money first his money is forbidden; and that is: since the gentile grasps the vessel, and what is within the vessel's interior is libated wine, the stream above is forbidden even before it enters the vessel; and libated-wine money is taken — only according to the one who says stream is not connection; if so, even if the vessel swings and the wine in the vessel's interior is forbidden, nevertheless the stream is not forbidden; and if so, when the stream enters the vessel the gentile acquires through drawing; and if so, it is not libated-wine money; or drawing does not acquire by a gentile — therefore there is no place for Shach's difficulty; and the matters are clear.
