(There in Hagah) However if he measured into a gentile's vessel, etc., that immediately when they came to the air of the vessel he acquired it and the wine was not forbidden until he touched, etc. See Shach seif 24; and it seems to me here it is because Tur certainly holds: where be-di'eved it is permitted even in actual mixture, one may permit in stream even le-chatchilah — see there; and ostensibly his words are astonishing — if so, as we hold above siman 127 that stream is connection; and that is if he pours into a vessel that has gentile wine, the wine in the vessel from which he pours is forbidden — it is like lower mixed with upper; and even though we hold like R. Shimon ben Gamliel that it is permitted to sell everything except libated-wine money in it — if so, mixtures are permitted be-di'eved; and nevertheless we say stream is connection; and one must say Shach certainly holds: where the libated wine is recognizable — as above siman 126 — and it is called prohibition on it; in such a case we well say stream is connection; but here, where it is only a little residue of wine not worth a perutah — on this it is not called prohibition at all; and in such a case we do not say stream is connection. But on what he answered regarding Tur's words, that when the gentile grasps the vessel in his hand it is permitted — he explained; and this is his wording: or because since even in mixture it is permitted be-di'eved, it is not forbidden because of stream connection — these words have no dawn at all; for behold there are two reasons in stream connection: one — if he pours into a vessel that has libated wine, the stream connects them and it is as if libated wine is mixed in what is above; and reason two — if the gentile touched the wine below, the stream connects as if he touched above; and the practical difference is this: if we say it is forbidden from mixture reason — we say sell everything except libated-wine money in it; but where he touched below and what is above is also as if he touched — everything is forbidden and it does not apply to sell everything except libated-wine money in it; therefore it is difficult on Shach — for behold where the gentile grasped the vessel in his hand, it is forbidden from the reason that inevitably he will swing and everything is forbidden and it is as if the gentile also touched above; it seems more to me to answer Rama's words: since otherwise in great loss we hold stream is not connection — like Rabeinu Tam — if so, likewise here regarding receiving the money one may well rely on them even not in great loss.
