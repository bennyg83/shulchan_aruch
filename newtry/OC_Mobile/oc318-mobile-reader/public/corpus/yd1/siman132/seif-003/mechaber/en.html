Jewish workers who did work for a non-Jew, and he sent them a barrel of wine as their wages: if it was before they received it as their wages, it is permitted that they say to him, “Instead of sending us this barrel, let it be yours and give us money in exchange for it” (Ran). But if it was after they received it as their wages, it is forbidden (R' Yerucham and Rosh) (and see above siman 123, that even after they received it, in our times it is permitted).
