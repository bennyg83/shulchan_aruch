The money is forbidden. Avodat HaTalmid; and see responsum Tashbetz part 1 siman 14: a Jew sold a gentile a barrel of kosher wine on condition that before the gentile draws and libates them he should write the Jew a promissory note and afterward he draws and does with them as he wishes — it is forbidden only when he sells a little to libate; but when selling barrels for commerce there is no prohibition except regarding payment — that there should not be libated-wine money in the Jew's hand; and one must know how to conduct — see there.
