From that same gentile. For it is not a sales fee but damage money; and even if the gentile does not wish to give the money until the Jew gives him the wine — it is permitted — so Ran wrote and Beit Yosef brings it.
Who forbade. It implies that even if they forbade inadvertently — in which case he is exempt from paying as in Choshen Mishpat siman 385 — nevertheless one may take payment from that gentile, for he caused damage regardless; and so Rashba ruled; and so Bach ruled.
The wine owner who sold it — their money is forbidden. As a penalty — as stated in Rabbeinu Yerucham whom Beit Yosef brought; and likewise in the Rosh there; and see above siman 215 seif 1.
To others, permitted. For since after the gentile acquired the wine through drawing he paid the money — it is not considered money of libated wine — Tur and Rosh there.
