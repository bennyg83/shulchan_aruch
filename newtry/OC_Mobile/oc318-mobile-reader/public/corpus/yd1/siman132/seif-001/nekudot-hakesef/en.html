(Siman 132, Taz seif katan 3) Therefore he writes here, "there is one who says," and so forth. Taz explains that others are permitted to benefit, identifying this as Rabbenu Yerucham's view cited by Beit Yosef, and says the permissive rule is an explicit Gemara. He is mistaken. There is no explicit Gemara permitting this. On the contrary, many poskim disagree. Tosafot and Rosh at the end of Avodah Zarah cite R. Elchanan, who limits the Sheviit case to where the prohibited object is no longer in the purchaser's possession. Regarding yayin nesech as well, they permit only where the gentile sold the wine first and afterward paid the Jew. Ran discusses this at length at the end of Avodah Zarah, concludes similarly, and rejects the proofs for permission. Rabbenu Yerucham also cites the prohibiting opinion. All this is explained in Shach, seif katan 3.
