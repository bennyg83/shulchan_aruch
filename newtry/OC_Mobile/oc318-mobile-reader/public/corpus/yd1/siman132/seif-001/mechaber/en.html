One may not benefit from yayin nesech; it contains 7 se’ifim. A non-Jew who touched our wine — even though it is forbidden in benefit — it is permitted to take its value from that non-Jew who forbade it; or that non-Jew may sell it and give its value to the Jew. And if a Jew sold it to another non-Jew: if he gave him the money before the Jew gave him the wine, the money is forbidden in benefit; but if the non-Jew took the wine first and afterwards gave the money to the Jew, there is one who says that even though to the owner of the wine who sold it the money is forbidden in benefit, to others it is permitted.
{Rama: And all this speaks only of non-Jews who pour libations, but in our times all of their touch does not forbid except for drinking (Rashi in the name of the Geonim), as was explained above in siman 123.}
