This is permitted. The Tur writes: That which we say, that money received for idolatry while in the hand of a non-Jew is forbidden, applies specifically when he sells the idolatry in order to purchase articles needed for idolatry with its proceeds. The money then retains its forbidden status, end quote. Although we generally rule that mere designation is not legally significant, this case is different, because the money came from idolatry and is destined to return to it. It is explained in siman 144 that even where this is uncertain one must be concerned that perhaps he sold it in order to purchase idolatry.
