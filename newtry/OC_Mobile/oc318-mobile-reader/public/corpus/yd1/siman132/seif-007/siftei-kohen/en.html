From the words of Bach it seems: where it is not known that he sold it for his own needs — even with libated wine one should be concerned lest he sold it in order to buy more libated wine — as below end of siman 144 regarding idolatry; but Derishah in siman 144 distinguished between idolatry and libated wine — for with libated wine we are not presumptively concerned that he took it to return and buy libated wine — see there.
And some say. Raavad distinguished: when it is in his power to press him and he waits out of good will — it is forbidden, for through him idolatry is sustained; but when it is not in his power to press him and he therefore waits — it is permitted — end of Tur; and the words of Raavad are brought by Rashba, Ran, and Rabbeinu Yerucham in their conclusion.
And see above siman 124. And likewise it is in Atzei Zahav; and in my humble opinion it is a scribal error and should say siman 144.
