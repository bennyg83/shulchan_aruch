Permitted. Shach writes that Bach appears to hold that where it is unknown whether the non-Jew sold it for his own needs, even with yayin nesech we should fear that he sold it in order to buy more yayin nesech, as in siman 144 concerning idolatry. Derishah distinguishes between idolatry and yayin nesech: regarding wine, we do not ordinarily suspect that the proceeds were taken in order to buy more forbidden wine.
