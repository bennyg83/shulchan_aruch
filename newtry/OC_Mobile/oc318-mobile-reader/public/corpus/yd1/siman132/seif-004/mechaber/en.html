One who hired non-Jewish workers and set food for them may not give them to drink yayin nesech. But if he told them, “Go out and drink at so-and-so the shopkeeper, and I will pay,” if he did not advance him a dinar it is permitted, even though he set aside a dinar and it is intact in the hand of the homeowner. But if he advanced him a dinar, it is forbidden; and this is specifically when he said to him, “Let this dinar be in your hand until you give the workers to drink, and I will settle accounts with you in the end.” But if he gave it to him to spend now if he needs it, it is permitted. (And likewise, one who invites a non-Jew to him, it is permitted to give money to his servant to buy for them yayin nesech) (Kol Bo).
