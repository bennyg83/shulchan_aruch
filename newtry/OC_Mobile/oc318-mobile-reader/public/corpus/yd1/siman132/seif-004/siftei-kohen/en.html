[He preceded a dinar to him — forbidden. For now they eat from the house of the harlot:]
[But if they gave him, etc. Tur challenged on this, thus: and it does not seem — for since he preceded the dinar to him, the wine was immediately acquired for him through agency, and they gave it to them perforce; and Beit Yosef wrote there is no doubt Tur did not see Torat HaBayit's words — only the short he saw; and therefore he disagreed; but if he saw the long he would not disagree — for what difference from hire that Rashba brings; and in my humble opinion Tur's words are correct: even though money does not acquire — that is with a gentile according to Tur in this siman; but Jew from Jew — money acquires by Torah law — as in chapter HaZahav; and if so, Jew from gentile — all the more he acquires with money — as in Choshen Mishpat siman 126: if Jew from Jew effects in the presence of all three — how much more from gentile; here too likewise; and since he preceded the dinar for the wine, he acquired the wine corresponding to it with his money; and you might ask: what do we care about this acquisition — behold the Jew does not benefit from it; for this he said that the gentile gives it through the Jew wine owner's agency to his workers; and this does not apply to hire — to say he acquired something with that hire; therefore hire has no relevance to bring afterward; and therefore there it is permitted; so it seems in my humble opinion to settle Tur's words; but Rashba did not consider this — that he acquires libated wine from him — for he speaks anonymously that he should give them food and drink, and did not mention libated wine:]
[Permitted to give money, etc. Bach wrote: meaning he gives his slave a dinar that he should acquire for these gentile workers — he effects to the gentile the dinar through his slave, and he becomes agent to buy wine on behalf of the gentile; and it implies; but if he said anonymously "acquire wine such-and-such" — the slave does not acquire the wine except for the master, and it is forbidden; and it seems specifically his slave; but forbidden to say thus to another Jew — for he wants the existence of the libated wine until he performs his agency for the gentile — as below siman 133 seif 5 in the law of mixing for a gentile for free — see there — end:]
