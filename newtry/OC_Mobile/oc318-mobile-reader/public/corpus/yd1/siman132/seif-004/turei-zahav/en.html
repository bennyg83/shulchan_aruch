And specifically when he said to him: let this dinar be in your possession, etc. Rashba in Torat HaEretz proved this — for it was challenged to him: he preceded the dinar to him — what of it? Behold from when he gave it to him the gentile acquired it — for anonymously they gave it to him to take out; and at the time of giving the money there is no prohibition — for they do not take libated wine; and when the workers take afterward, the dinar was not the Jew's; and it is like one who gave a lamb as hire and afterward came upon her — the hire is permitted; therefore he explained it deals as written here; and specifically, etc.; and Tur wrote on this: and it does not seem — for since he preceded the dinar to him, the wine was immediately acquired for him through agency, and they gave it to them perforce; and Beit Yosef wrote there is no doubt Tur did not see Torat HaEretz's words — only the short; and therefore he disagreed; but if he saw the long he would not disagree — for what difference from hire that Rashba brings; and in my humble opinion Tur's words are correct: even though money does not acquire — that is with a gentile according to Tur earlier in this siman; but Jew from Jew — money acquires by Torah law — as in chapter HaZahav; and if so, Jew from gentile — all the more he acquires with money — as in Choshen Mishpat siman 126; here too likewise; and since he preceded the dinar for the wine, he acquired wine corresponding to it; and you might ask: what do we care about this acquisition — behold the Jew does not benefit from it; for this he said the gentile gives it through the Jew wine owner's agency to his workers; and hire has no relevance; therefore there permitted; so it seems to me to settle Tur's words; but Rashba did not consider that he acquires libated wine from him — for he speaks anonymously regarding food and drink, and did not mention libated wine.
