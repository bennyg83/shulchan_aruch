When he finishes the Torah, he must finish in the middle of the line at the end of the page; and if much of the page remains in lines, he shortens and ascends (and makes letters long from bottom to top so that one word holds four or five lines) (Tur), and begins from the beginning of the line at the end of the page and does not finish the line, and intends until it be before the eyes of all Israel in the middle of the line at the end of the page.
