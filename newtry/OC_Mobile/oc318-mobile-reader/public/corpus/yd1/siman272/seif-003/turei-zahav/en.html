And not more than eight. Thus it should read; and it is explained in Tur that the primary beauty is from four or five, only there is no prohibition except with two or nine. And in Semag Laws of Sefer Torah he wrote this law of making a sheet of three, etc., and all he mentioned here — concluded: all this is for the mitzvah but not to invalidate — end quote; and so in Mordechai Laws of Ketanim. If so, if only two sheets were written in a sheet it is valid after the fact; and so appears in Rambam chapter 10 of Hilchot Sefer Torah that he does not count this among disqualifications; surprising on Beit Yosef and Rama that they did not bring this; and it appears what Shulchan Aruch wrote in siman 275 seif 5 "all these matters" applies also to this siman.
