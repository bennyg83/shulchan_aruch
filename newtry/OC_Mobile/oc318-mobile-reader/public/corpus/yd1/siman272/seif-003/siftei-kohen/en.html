"There is no less fire than G-d, no more than eight. "Assad,"
Even one verse. Even though the last verse does not have words equal to the number of lines — he makes letters long from bottom to top so one word holds four or five lines in order to finish at the end of the page — Tur. And it implies here there are many verses one can write in each line one word equal to the number of lines; however all this is at the end of the Torah since impossible otherwise; but in the middle of the Torah one should not do so — neither extend letters in length nor width — rather write as prescribed and as below siman 275; however if the scribe wrote thus it is not disqualified for this, and so appears in Perishah.
