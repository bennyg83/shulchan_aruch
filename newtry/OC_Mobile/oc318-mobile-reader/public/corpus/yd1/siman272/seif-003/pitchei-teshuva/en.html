Less than three sheets. See in responsum Givat Shaul siman 55 regarding a Torah scroll whose sheets were damaged by mouse eating and the deficiency reached within five lines in a way that cannot be fixed as explained in siman 280 — only in one sheet one sheet was damaged and the two adjacent sheets remained whole; and he ruled not to replace those two sheets and bury them in vain, only that damaged sheet — namely to make one more sheet to write anew; and those two sheets would be one sheet. For two reasons: one — making less than three sheets is only for the mitzvah and not to invalidate; and further because it is written in gross script and if he wrote in fine script in each line there would be only thirty letters like lemishpachoteichem three times — he could make from those two sheets four; and the reason not to make less than three — Rashi explained so the stitches not be too close; if so what difference how many he makes — only two sheets from gross writing since stitches are not close — see there in the last pamphlet (and see in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 256).
