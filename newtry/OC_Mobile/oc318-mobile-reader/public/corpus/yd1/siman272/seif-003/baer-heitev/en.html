Fewer. Tur indicates that four or five columns per sheet is the most beautiful arrangement; only two or nine is formally objectionable. Semag and Rambam imply that two columns in a sheet remain valid after the fact. Taz.
A verse. Even if the final verse lacks enough words to fill the required lines, the scribe should elongate letters so that one word occupies four or five lines and the scroll ends at the bottom of the column. If many verses remain, one word may be written on each line. This applies only at the end of the Torah, where no other arrangement is possible. In the middle, letters should not be stretched in length or width, though an accidental deviation does not invalidate the scroll. Shach.
