'According to your families' three times, etc. Hagahos Maimoniyos cites Rabbeinu Chananel that this is the measure of one handbreadth. It further cites him that this applies specifically to fine script. With large script, everything depends on aesthetic appearance, and even more than a handbreadth is permitted. The Tur and the other authorities write likewise. His eyes will not wander because the writing is large. This is the custom.
So that his eyes should not wander, etc. When the lines are long, he may err at their beginnings.
