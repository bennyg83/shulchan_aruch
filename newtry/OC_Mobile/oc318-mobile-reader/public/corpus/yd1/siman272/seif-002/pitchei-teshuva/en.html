Six handbreadths. And after the fact it is valid with less. See siman 275 seif 5. And see in responsum Rashbash siman 321 who wrote: nevertheless this is not proper honor to take out for the congregation to read — and what honor will the congregation give it when a person sees with his eyes; and this matter I leave according to what my eyes see of the congregation's important people — see there; and it is plain the same applies if its length is like its width but it does not have six handbreadths — such as if he reduced the writing — one should not read from it in the congregation for this reason.
