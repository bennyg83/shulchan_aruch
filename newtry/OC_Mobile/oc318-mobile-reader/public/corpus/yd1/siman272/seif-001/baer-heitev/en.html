A thumbbreadth. Rambam writes that every thumbbreadth mentioned in these measurements and throughout Torah law means the width of an average finger. Careful measurement showed it to equal seven medium barley grains pressed side by side, or the length of two barley grains placed loosely.
That is within. Taz emends this to 'at the end.' The final roller is included in the calculation because after rolling it remains within the scroll, whereas the first roller rests above it and is not counted in the scroll's thickness. Shach notes that Rambam measures six handbreadths excluding the rollers and explains how to prepare a Torah scroll whose circumference equals its height.
