A thumbbreadth, etc. The Rambam writes in chapter 10 of Hilchos Sefer Torah, halachah 9: The width of the thumb mentioned in all these measurements, and in all other Torah measurements, is the width of an average finger. We have carefully measured it and found it to equal the width of seven medium barleycorns pressed side by side, and the length of two barleycorns placed with space between them. End quote. The Mabit cites this in part 2, end of siman 173, page 69, at the beginning of the fourth column. See there.
Some say, etc. Nevertheless, allowance must be made for the thickness of the rollers, as implied by the Rosh and the Tur. The Rambam's view in Hilchos Sefer Torah is that the circumference of six handbreadths excludes the rollers. See there, where he discusses at length the procedure for one who wishes to write a Torah scroll whose length will equal its circumference.
