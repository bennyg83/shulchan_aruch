One who remarries his divorced wife must count seven clean days. It appears that the same applies if he divorced her while she was pregnant and then remarried her. She must count seven clean days, because although a pregnant woman is presumed not to menstruate, we nevertheless suspect that she may have seen blood because of desire, just as we do regarding a minor at the beginning of this siman. It is explained above at the end of siman 189 that a minor is less likely to see blood than a pregnant woman. If he transgressed and married her during this period, the Taz wrote that she does not require guarding, since he has already had relations with her.
