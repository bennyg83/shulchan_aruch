An incident involving a widow who had a set time by agreement with her groom for the wedding, but the groom told her it was possible he might advance the wedding — and so he did; she said she relied on this, counted seven clean days, immersed, and he came to her. In my view: even though the widow acted improperly — since she had no certainty about the advancement of the wedding until the groom came and took her immediately — it turns out that at the moment she became certain, a new desire arose, and she should go back and count seven clean days. Nevertheless, since she already transgressed and did this, one does not separate them on account of this — and the proof is from what is written in siman 196 seif 12: if she erred and immersed, etc., she should wait six intervals and then immerse.
