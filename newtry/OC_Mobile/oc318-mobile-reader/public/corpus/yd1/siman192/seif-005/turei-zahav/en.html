One who remarries his divorced wife, etc. It appears that if he transgressed and married her within the required period, supervision is unnecessary. Since he already had relations with her when she was his wife the first time, his inclination is not now so powerful, and supervision is unnecessary. So it appears to me.
An incident occurred involving a widow whose marriage agreement fixed a specific wedding date with her groom, but the groom told her that he might advance the wedding, and he did so. She said that she relied upon this possibility, counted seven clean days, immersed, and then had relations with him. It appears to me that although the widow acted improperly, since she had no certainty that the wedding would be advanced until the groom arrived and immediately married her, the desire was newly aroused when she first became certain. She therefore should have counted seven clean days anew. Nevertheless, since she already acted in this manner, they should not be separated because of it. Proof may be brought from what is written in siman 196, seif 12, that if she erred and immersed, etc., she waits six onos and immerses. This is certainly true here, where the concern for desire is only rabbinic, as stated at the beginning of this siman. See what I wrote at the end of siman 196.
