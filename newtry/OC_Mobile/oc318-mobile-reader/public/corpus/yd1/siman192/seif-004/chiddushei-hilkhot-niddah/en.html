He transgressed and entered, etc. Need not guarding — this is Torat HaShlamim's view who holds what we say in Gemara "but a husband need not guard" means fit for relations; and see Taz s.k. 7 on what he wrote; and from Rambam's words who holds chuppah of a niddah is not called chuppah and holds chuppah means isolation fit and handed over for relations — if so what we say in Gemara "and likewise one who spread a niddah woman — he sleeps", etc. — according to chuppah was before she became niddah, for afterward chuppah does not apply; and also then he was isolated with her, for otherwise chuppah does not apply; if so behold he was fit for relations; nevertheless we say he sleeps among people, etc. — learn our law of Torat HaShlamim does not exist; and therefore Shulchan Aruch wrote well — one who is stringent, praise to him. And the custom is to take a minor, etc. Shach wrote in s.k. 14 they must know the reason for relations and do not hand themselves over for relations; and Rav Mahari"y challenged him — here his wife guards him, and even if she hands herself for relations there is no concern of isolation; and one can say Bach holds as explained in Even HaEzer siman 22 that at night even in a city we require three; if so certainly with a husband there is no concern, for his wife guards him; but here they isolate a minor with the groom, and that minor also deals — he knows the reason for relations as Bach wrote explicitly — namely he is nine years and one day; if so he too is forbidden to isolate; and the husband is only one guardian; if so behold the minor isolates with the bride and another one-year-old great daughter; and at night one guardian does not suffice; therefore requires that that minor not hand herself for relations; if so no concern of enticement; and for ones there is no concern for she will cry out and the guardians will awaken; if so behold regarding minor against bride two guardians — the minor and the husband; and one resolves not so when she hands herself for relations regarding minor there is only guardian the husband; and at night we require two guardians — and this is the reason for custom that they take specifically minor and minor.
