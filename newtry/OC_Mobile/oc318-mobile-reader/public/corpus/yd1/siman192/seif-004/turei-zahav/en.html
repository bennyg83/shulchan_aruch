Some say if she was tehorah, etc. In Torat HaShlamim there he brought view of those who forbid as anonymous language of Talmud who said if husband need not guarding; and there are those who permit for what Talmud says "husband" means fit for relations; and Torat HaShlamim decided for permission; and his proof from ox mu'ad we saw oxen three times and it did not gore — returned to its nature — apparently we do not say this time its desire rested and today or tomorrow its desire returns; so too in this case. And nevertheless one who is stringent, praise to him — end quote. And in my humble opinion the case is not similar to the proof, for what he brought proof from ox mu'ad is not proof, for we hold end of first chapter Bava Kamma page 15 — ordinary oxen stand in presumption of guarding, etc. — found every ox that is mu'ad to gore is different from nature of ordinary oxen; and therefore all we see it saw oxen and did not gore — its change was nullified and it became like other oxen; not so here where ordinary man his desire is strong over him and he does not establish himself as Tosafot in first chapter Ketubot page 9 s.v. mai lav, etc. wrote — and so: since he isolates certainly he has relations with her, for a person does not establish himself; and although regarding yevamah we say until thirty days he establishes himself — most times he does not establish himself even one hour, etc. — end quote. Found this groom who did not have relations is changed from nature of the world; and we do not say because he changed one hour or few days it will be so forever; on the contrary all time we see he changed behold he changed; and at time we do not see he changed the matter returns to human nature which is majority of world; and you have in it only its novelty; and from its novelty we do not derive; on the contrary there is proof to the contrary from what Tosafot wrote that with yevamah until thirty days he establishes himself and no more — behold he was established thirty days in this that he did not have relations with her; and even so we say afterward desire is strong and he does not establish himself; likewise here you have in it only its novelty; therefore it appears to me like words of those who forbid as simple meaning of Talmud language that permission is only when he had relations; and this is not in category of "one who is stringent, praise to him" but complete law that guarding is required.
