That his daughter-in-law menstruated, etc. And it was explained in Even HaEzer end of siman 60 that the custom is clearly established to enter and make a marriage even when she is a niddah.
He no longer requires supervision. For we see that his desire is not strong since he has not had relations with her until now.
And one who is stringent, etc. And it seems that nowadays, since the custom is not to have relations until two or three days after the wedding — even though it is a foolish custom with multiple prohibitions involved and it would be proper to abolish it — nevertheless since the custom is so, if she menstruated certainly supervision is required, for the reason he did not have relations at first was due to the custom; and they are accustomed to require supervision in such a case.
And one should not distinguish, etc. That is to say, lest you think that regarding a widower who has already had relations his desire is not strong — therefore it teaches us; and so implied below beginning of siman 195 that there is no distinction in this.
And the custom is to take a minor, etc. And see above siman 182 where I wrote in the name of Bach that it should be ones who know the meaning of relations but would not give themselves over to relations.
