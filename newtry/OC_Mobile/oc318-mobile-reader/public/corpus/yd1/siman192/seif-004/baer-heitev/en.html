Supervision. Since he has not had relations until now, his desire is evidently not overpowering. Taz nevertheless rules that supervision is required by law. Shach writes that where custom delays first relations for several days after marriage, if she becomes a niddah before relations, supervision is certainly required because the delay proves nothing.
For a widower. This teaches that even a widower who has previously had relations requires the stated rule. Or a virgin. After a virgin has had the first relations, seclusion is permitted even if she did not see niddah blood, because virginal blood is treated with the same stringency as niddah blood. Shach.
A minor. Bach writes that the supervising children must understand the nature of relations and yet not be willing participants in it.
