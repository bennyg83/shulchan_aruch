If he transgressed and brought her in within this time, and likewise a chassan whose kallah became a niddah before he had intercourse with her: he should not be alone with her; rather, he sleeps among the men and she sleeps among the women. {Rama: Some say: if she was tehorah when he married her and he did not have intercourse with her, and afterward she became a niddah, she does not need further guarding (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 253, and Hagahos Alfasi), and one who is stringent will be blessed (Terumas HaDeshen there). And one should not distinguish in this between a bachur and a widower, or between a virgin and a widow (Rabbeinu Yerucham, nesiv 26). Some say that she is forbidden to be alone with him in the day as in the night, and there is no need for two guards; rather, either he among the men or she among the women (Tur in the name of Rosh). And if they do not sleep in one room, they do not need guarding at all (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba). And some say that at night two guards are needed, and in the day it is permitted to be alone (Raavad). And the custom is to take a minor boy by the chassan and a minor girl by the kallah, and they are not alone in the day without a minor boy or minor girl.}
