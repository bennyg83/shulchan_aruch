One who is stringent will receive blessing. See the Taz, who writes that according to the strict law one must be stringent. The Shach, in Nekudos HaKesef, questions his words. See also the Shach, who writes that nowadays the custom is not to consummate the marriage until two or three days later. Although this is a foolish custom and it is proper to abolish it, nevertheless, if she menstruates, guarding is certainly required. The reason he did not have relations was not that he had subdued his evil inclination, but because of the custom. The accepted practice is therefore to require guarding.
The custom is to take a minor boy, etc., and a minor girl. The Shach wrote: "Above in siman 342 I wrote in the name of the Bach that they must understand the nature of intercourse and yet not submit themselves to intercourse." End quote. I saw that the Bach there derived this from the ruling in Even HaEzer, siman 22. In truth, however, there is no proof from there. Seclusion with another woman is forbidden unless the girl is a minor who does not submit herself to intercourse, because if she does submit herself to intercourse it is forbidden for one man to be secluded with two women. Here, however, it is possible that even a girl who would submit herself to intercourse is permitted, because we are not concerned that he will lie with her when his wife, who is entering the chuppah with him, is present, and his wife guards him, as explained there in Even HaEzer. Although one may distinguish between the cases, there is nevertheless no proof from there. This is readily understood.
