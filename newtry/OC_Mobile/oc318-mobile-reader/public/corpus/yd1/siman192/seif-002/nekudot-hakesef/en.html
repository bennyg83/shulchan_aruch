(In Taz, seif katan 7) He objected to Terumat HaDeshen without justification. Here too, ordinary people retain a presumption of proper conduct. This is not comparable to Tosafot's statement that since he secluded himself with her, he certainly had relations with her, for there the woman was permitted to him. The case there was in Yehudah, where a man would seclude himself with his betrothed, and similarly with his yevamah. Since she was permitted to him, he would not restrain himself. That is not so here.
