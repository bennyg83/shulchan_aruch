These seven days they count, etc. All this is from Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba; and Rashba wrote in Mishmeret HaBayit page 172a — and so: custom of Israel is Torah, and in all our places they practice to prepare festive Shabbat meals before Shabbat of marriage, and this is called claim, and from it they count these seven — end quote. And his words are according to custom they practiced to enter on Shabbat eve and immerse at entry of Shabbat as explained in Alei HaShulchan siman 63; and one must say immediately on Friday eve before Shabbat of marriage when they prepare for the meal it is called day of claim, for otherwise we do not find 7 clean days — and requires study. And now that they practice per majority to immerse on night of sixth, day of claim must be called two and three days before Shabbat per what we say at beginning of Ketubot that a man should trouble three days.
