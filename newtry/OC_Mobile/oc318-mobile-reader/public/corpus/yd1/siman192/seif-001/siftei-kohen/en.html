And does not need the cessation, etc. And nowadays the custom is to observe cessation in purity even for a virgin, and they examine for all seven clean days; and so Maharshal, brought by Perishah and Bach; and he wrote however di'eved even if she examined only once within the seven it suffices — and this is obvious.
And yet she requires, etc. Beit Yosef’s language: and per Rosh’s view lechat’chilah she must examine each day of the seven; but di’eved once that she examined within seven suffices, for she is not superior to one who saw with certainty — end quote. It seems he means she is not superior to one who saw with certainty, for Rosh holds below siman 196 di’eved one examination within seven suffices; accordingly for us who hold there in seif 4 it only suffices if she examined on day one and day seven — the same applies here; but this does not appear to be the Rav’s and Acharonim’s view; rather it seems he holds here since she did not see with certainty it is even better.
