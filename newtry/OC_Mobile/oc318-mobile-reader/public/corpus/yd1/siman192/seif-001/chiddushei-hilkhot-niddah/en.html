She was claimed for marriage, etc. Let us say from desire, etc. See Taz s.k. 1 that his view is if immediately when they reconciled she was with him in bed she is permitted to him — and the matter requires study; at first glance from where is this novelty, for there is concern immediately at time of claim she sees a drop of blood and it is lost — and who gave a measure how long she should stay? And Rav Baal Sha'ar Efrayim challenged Taz on this. A) And I found justification in Tosafot in chapter HaChalitz and Yoma that challenged on what we say "Rabbis — yichudei — he isolates her for him" — behold forbidden to isolate; and answered not "isolate for him" only general isolation so they have bread in his basket — if so there is no desire — see there. And this matter is difficult — from bread in his basket, behold it is impossible for them to take at all for the day, for immediately when he wished to take her there will be a new claim, for initially he did not take her only for isolation — if so afterward when she claims for relations, behold a new claim and she needs 7 clean days and is forbidden to him — if so there is no bread in his basket at all; and what did they accomplish by isolating them, for never will he be permitted to have relations with her until he waits 7 clean days and there is no bread in his basket; and it is clear like Taz's view that if she claimed and immediately he had relations he does not need 7 clean days; and if so one resolves that initially there was no claim for her husband and bread in his basket exists here — at night when he desires to have relations he will claim her and immediately have relations, and there is no concern of desire and she is permitted to him and it is properly bread in his basket. And perhaps Tosafot's view is like Ra'avyah who holds if she checked herself immediately at claim and did not find, she is permitted; and his words must be that why does the Gemara challenge on those who announce "who will be for the day" — behold Rav said "she was claimed for marriage", etc. — perhaps she was immediately checking; the Gemara's question would be they should announce thus and not simply "who will be for the day"; and perhaps women would not notice they answer to check immediately — they should announce who will be for the day they should check themselves; if so the Gemara answers properly that therefore they did not announce, for they only isolated and there is no heat and desire here; nevertheless there is bread in his basket, for if he wishes to have relations he will claim her and tell her to be careful to check immediately and she is permitted to him like Ra'avyah's view. And behold Taz challenged on Boaz and Tamar; and regarding Tamar plainly then this decree did not exist; and Boaz we do not find he had relations then at all — and I do not know what is difficult for him; on the contrary this is Boaz's strength. However regarding David it is difficult for him — possibly all delay that was on Bathsheba in divorce document and the like — nevertheless behold she was claimed for marriage; but also this requires study, for before R. Zeira's stringency on the day of her 7th of niddah she is permitted in the evening — what of it that she saw blood? Even if flowing all 7, only at evening she stops, immerses at night and is permitted; if so David when she was claimed for Boaz was on the 7th day as written "and she bathes from her impurity" — she had combing; if so even though then she had desire and saw, nevertheless at night when she immersed in water she is permitted to David — requires study. But one can challenge — why does the Gemara challenge "who will be for the day" — behold Rav said "she was claimed for marriage", etc. — perhaps the announcement was thus "who will be for the day" — namely the woman whose today is 7th of her niddah; if so she can marry him and at night be permitted to him in such case; and this is the precision in "who will be for the day" — the day happened ready she can become betrothed to him. And one can resolve — certainly they did not take an adult woman lest she become pregnant and be in category of sons of a divorcee in heart — for their intent was to divorce her, for although not in category of "do not devise evil against your neighbor", etc. when marrying with intent as known — nevertheless in category of sons of divorcee as Ran wrote, for although not hated; and it is clear they take minors not fit to bear; and the Gemara's question — a man should not marry a woman in this province, etc. — nevertheless difficult, for we do not distinguish in their decrees as known; if so the Gemara's question properly — who is she whose time is the day? For it deals with a minor; and a minor is not presumed to see as known; but per this there is no place for Tosafot's question on yichudei miyachadei leih — one can say in conclusion we say they married an adult, for truly he did not have relations at all and sons of divorcee in heart does not apply; if so with adult woman properly one can say "who will be for him for the day" as stated — who is whose 7th of niddot is today as stated; requires study.
