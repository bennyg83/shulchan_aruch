To count seven clean days. Shaar Efraim rules that a yevamah who counted before chalitzah must recount once marriage becomes possible, because her mind was not settled upon the new marriage. Chamudei Daniel requires written engagement terms before the count. Beit Shmuel and Chatam Sofer discuss insanity and a widow who counted before meeting her intended husband, generally inclining to leniency where the marriage decision was already fixed.
