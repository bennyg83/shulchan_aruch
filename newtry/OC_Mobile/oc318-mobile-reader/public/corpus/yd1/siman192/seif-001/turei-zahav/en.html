And furthermore it seems to me to answer: regarding Tamar and Ruth there was no concern that she might see from desire — for as soon as Judah and Boaz became reconciled with them, they went to bed with them immediately; and it is like one who has bread in his basket, which is different from all other brides. And she does not require a hefsek, etc. The reason is that she is not established as having seen blood, for she certainly did not see.
