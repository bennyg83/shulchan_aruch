The laws of a kallah entering the chuppah; it contains 5 se’ifim.
They asked her to marry and she agreed — she needs to sit 7 nekiyim, whether adult or minor, and even if she checked herself at the time of the request and found [herself] tehorah, lest because of desire she saw a drop of blood like a mustard seed and did not feel it. And she counts 7 from the day after the day of the request. And she does not need a hefsek taharah, for even though she did not check on the day of the request to stop in taharah, she counts from the next day 7 nekiyim. Nevertheless, she needs bedikah during the 7 (each day l’chatchila (Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh, and Bach that this is also the view of Ramban); nevertheless, b’dieved if she checked herself only one time during the 7, it suffices) (Beit Yosef and the later authorities).
