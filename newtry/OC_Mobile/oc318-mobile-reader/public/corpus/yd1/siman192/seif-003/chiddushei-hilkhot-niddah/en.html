If marriage was postponed, etc. Although she checked, etc. Rav Shach in s.k. 7 brought in name of Mas'at Binyamin that if time was set for Rosh Chodesh Nisan and afterward a messenger came that an ones befell the groom on the road and in a day or two he will come — and so it was — she does not need new clean days even if she did not check herself in the days between, for she did not despair of the first 7 clean days and relied, etc.; and these words have reasoning; and as Shach wrote one need not be stringent in these matters. However what Mechaber conditioned that there not pass 7 days without bedikah, and Shach cited and Mechaber in responsum doubled the words — see there. B) This matter is not understandable to me at all — these boundary days, what is their case from a doubt? If you make for the groom's claim a new claim if not even one day passed, behold a new claim and new desire; and if we say he did not despair from the first claim — if so even though 7 days passed and she did not check, nevertheless behold he already counted 7 days at first time; and if you require bedikah afterward — if so on the contrary one who married in less than 7, more. C) There is concern for prohibition lest she saw and 7 days of Torah niddah did not pass at all — after she counted 7 clean I do not know what difference if she passed 6 days and did not check and immediately married she is permitted, and if 7 days passed and she did not check she is forbidden to marry — what is six of seven? On the contrary in passing 6 there is Torah prohibition concern, not so in seven where there is only R. Zeira's stringency. Certainly if Mechaber's view were that she must wait 7 days, bedikah on one of the days would be fine; but since she is permitted to marry and need not wait 7 days — if so this that she passed 7 days without bedikah forbidden — I do not know what reason in the matter; and from where is this law established, for it has no root and essence among the Rishonim.
