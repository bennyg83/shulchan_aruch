If marriage was postponed due to some reason. It implies specifically when marriage was actually postponed; but if marriage was not postponed only there is some quarrel between them due to dowry; and likewise if they postponed first time and immediately in that session agreed to set second time — there is no new desire; and so Bach and Taz and Shach agreed, not like Atzei Arazim; and Atzei Arazim further wrote if from the quarrel the bundle separated and in that session the bride agreed to marry another man and immediately entered chuppah that day — plainly there is a new claim; and even if she checked herself and found tehorah she needs to count 7 clean days anew — perforce; and so all later authorities agreed. And if time was set for wedding on Rosh Chodesh and she counted 7 clean days; when Rosh Chodesh arrived groom did not come, and next day messenger came from groom that an ones befell him on road and immediately a day or two afterward he will come — and so he did and chuppah was made 2 or 3 days after Rosh Chodesh — Taz's view we require 7 clean days anew; however in responsum Mechaber siman 42 he ruled simply she does not need 7 clean days; and after him Shach followed and his words are reasonable, for essence of this law to require 7 clean days anew is a great stringency as Mechaber elaborated in responsum to uproot Mahari"l's words — see there; and see Shach s.k. 7 (and nevertheless it appears to me she needs to check herself at that hour the messenger came and found herself tehorah).
To return and sit 7 clean days. And even though she immersed she needs 7 clean days and immerse again — so Shach in name of Mordechai; and if she transgressed and had relations she needs to wait 6 complete onot and afterward count one clean day and immerse as explained below siman 196; and we do not say she needs to separate from him 2 days to distinguish between seed sown in holiness and seed sown not in holiness, since here is only rabbinic prohibition — so Taz this siman and siman 176 — see there. And in truth without this it appears no distinction applies here, for even son of a niddah is like a complete Israelite as explained in Alei HaShulchan siman 7; and so Shach in Nekudot HaKesef siman 196; and so it appears to me somewhat proof from mishnah chapter 4 brothers (Yevamot 33) — two brothers who betrothed two women and at time of their entry to chuppah exchanged this one's for that one's — behold they are liable for a sister-in-law; and if brothers for a brother's wife; and if she was niddah for niddah; and we separate them three months lest they are pregnant and their sons mamzerim; and we require distinction between kosher seed and invalid seed lest fetuses rely on their husbands — perforce; and if so one can challenge — behold even without exchange we require distinction if niddah had relations? Rather certainly with niddah we do not need distinction; and so meaning of all poskim who did not mention distinction regarding niddah, only regarding convert male and female where there are several legal ramifications as explained in laws of converts.
