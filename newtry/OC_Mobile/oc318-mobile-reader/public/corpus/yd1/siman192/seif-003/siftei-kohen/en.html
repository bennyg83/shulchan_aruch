Although she checked, etc. And in responsum Mas'at Binyamin siman 42 he elaborated to disagree and holds she need not return to sit 7 clean days except when she did not check herself; and concludes at end — nevertheless one should be stringent per Mahari"k and Rav; and nevertheless regarding a bride who wore whites and counted 7 clean days with intent for marriage set for Rosh Chodesh Nisan; when Rosh Chodesh Nisan arrived he did not come, and the next day a messenger came from the groom that an ones befell him on the road and immediately he will come in a day or two; they made chuppah 2 or 3 days after Rosh Chodesh Nisan — in this certainly she does not need other 7 clean days even if she did not check herself in the days between, if 7 days did not pass without bedikah; and even Rav and Mahari"k agree, for since marriage was not postponed except due to some ones that delayed the matter, certainly she did not despair of the first 7 clean days and relied that groom will come after day or two, and relied on herself and gave her mind she would be clean and not come to concern of desire — end quote. And in truth one need not be stringent in these laws, for anyway many poskim hold 7 clean days not required; and also di'eved one bedikah within 7 suffices for all; and also the entire essence of this law is from their words.
