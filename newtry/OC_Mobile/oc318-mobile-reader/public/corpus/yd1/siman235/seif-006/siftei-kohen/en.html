It has no effect. A person cannot prohibit another person’s property to a third party. This applies specifically when he makes the prohibition dependent upon something that is within his power to forbid, such as the case above in seif 3: “Your marital relations are forbidden to me if you go to your father’s house,” or, “Benefit from me is forbidden to you if you go to your father’s house,” as stated above in siman 220, seif 22.
