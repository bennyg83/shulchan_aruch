For one month. Taz writes that an explicit vow for one month is valid and may be maintained while he lives with her in the city. An unspecified vow is treated as longer than one month. Where he lives elsewhere, Tur's longer period depends upon whether she ordinarily frequents his home.
Immediately. She reasons that since he heard and did not annul, he must hate her, and she cannot continue living with him. Taz applies this whether or not she linked the vow to marital relations. Perishah distinguishes cases and explains that when she prohibited food or adornment while remaining married, he may need to divorce immediately so that the prohibition will end.
And heard. Shach wrote, and it implies that whoever did not tie in relations he must divorce and give ketubah, since it is not in his power to annul; for this is not a vow of affliction of soul, like not going to a house of mourning or a house of celebration. And so Bach; and it is difficult, for above siman 234 seif 62 Mechaber ruled it is affliction of soul. And it seems here he incidentally used the Tur's language; and so must be said in Even HaEzer siman 74 seif 65. And Taz wrote that truly here it speaks even without tying in relations; only he mentioned tying in relations because nevertheless he is obligated to divorce immediately and they do not give seven days, since she vowed and he upheld.
