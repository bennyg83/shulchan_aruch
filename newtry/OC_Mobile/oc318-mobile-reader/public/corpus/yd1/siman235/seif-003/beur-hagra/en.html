And he tied, etc., or, etc. See there the sugya 71a–71b — we taught: one who forbids, etc., until "it is repugnant to me"; see Tosafot there s.v. bishelema and s.v. shenadrah; and Ran explained more that the Gemara's difficulty — "we taught," etc. — perforce cannot say he forbade plainly "you shall not taste," etc., for that is not in his power as above Nedarim 59a; rather he forbade his property: if you taste, etc.; and if so, "taste and wait thirty days" — although for tasting fruit it is impossible to wait at all, but to benefit him she may wait thirty days like the first clause; and so in Yerushalmi: one who forbids his wife not to taste, etc.; and there is one who forbids his wife saying: if you eat such-and-such item you are forbidden my property — she eats that item and is forbidden his property; when she vowed and her husband heard and did not annul — our Gemara and Tosafot s.v. bishelema explain he tied it in marital relations and asked: "eat and wait seven days"; and there is no difference except Tosafot explain like the sugya below — "she tied," etc.; and Ran explains like Yerushalmi sugya and answers: Beit Hillel's case, e.g., she vowed, etc., and did not tie at all — impossible to wait at all, as above; you might ask: what gain in a get? One may say: nevertheless one may say he does not want a man who keeps vows; and Tosafot s.v. shenadrah explain she vowed while under him; in conclusion we say only Beit Hillel's case, e.g., she tied in relations or to benefit from his property, nevertheless he divorces immediately; and even though this resolution is only R' Yosi according to R' Yehuda, Hagah, R' Yitzchak, etc. — we do not hold thus; nevertheless we need this change even for the rabbis, since the end "on condition she say to so-and-so," etc., is not affliction nor matters between them — it must be thus; and Tur wrote all mishnayot there — not to go to father's house, mourner's house, feast — all are not beh beh and he cannot annul; Even HaEzer see s.v. The rule is found: when he forbids, if like tied in relations she waits seven days; but when she vowed and he was silent or upheld, whether tied or not, he divorces immediately — we hold like R' Yosi and R' Yehuda who gave a get, etc., and our stam mishnah; per this the entire siman is explained.
