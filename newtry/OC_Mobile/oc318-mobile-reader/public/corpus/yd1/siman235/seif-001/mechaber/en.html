One who forbids his wife from marital relations, or from going to her father's house, and the like. It contains 6 se'ifim: One who forbids his wife from marital relations, having said, "[Konam,] your marital relations are forbidden to me"—he must uphold it for seven days, whether he forbade her explicitly for seven days or more, or forbade her without specification; and after seven days he divorces her and gives her her ketubah.
