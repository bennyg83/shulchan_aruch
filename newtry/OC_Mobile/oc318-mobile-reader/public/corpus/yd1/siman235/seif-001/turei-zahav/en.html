He shall maintain her for seven days. This means that if he explicitly prohibited relations for seven days, he need not divorce her at all. If he prohibited them without qualification, or explicitly for more than seven days, he must give a get if he does not obtain release, but he is granted seven days because a woman ordinarily remains without relations during her seven niddah days. The statement here, “after seven days he shall divorce,” applies only to an unspecified vow or one expressly exceeding seven days, not to a vow expressly limited to seven. Rashi explains the Mishnah in Kesubos 61a similarly. Beis Shammai says that if he prohibited relations for two weeks he waits, but if longer he must divorce and pay the kesubah. Thus, where the vow itself is limited to two weeks according to Beis Shammai, or one week according to Beis Hillel, there is no obligation to divorce at all. Hagahos Ashri likewise writes that if a woman vowed and made her vow dependent on relations for one week, he need not divorce. I will cite this in seif 3. The same applies below concerning her going to her father’s house for one month, as I will explain in seif 3.
