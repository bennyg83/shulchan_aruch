He shall uphold her seven days. Meaning: when he forbade explicitly seven days, no need to divorce at all; but if he forbade generally or more than seven days explicitly, then he must give a get if he does not release the vow, except they give him seven days' time — since otherwise a woman waits during her niddah days seven without relations. And what is written here "after seven days he divorces," etc., refers specifically to a general vow or more than seven explicitly, not a vow on seven explicitly. And so explained in Rashi on the mishnah, chapter Af Al Pi (Ketubot 61), in the mishnah HaMedir his wife from relations — Beit Shammai: if he forbade two Shabbatot he waits; if more, he divorces and gives ketubah — end quote; explained that for a vow of two Shabbatot, and likewise for Beit Hillel one Shabbat, there is no obligation to divorce at all. And so written in Hagahot Ashri, chapter HaMedir: for a woman who vowed and tied in relations one Shabbat, he does not divorce; and I will bring it in seif 3; and likewise below regarding "go to her father's house one month," as written in seif 3.
