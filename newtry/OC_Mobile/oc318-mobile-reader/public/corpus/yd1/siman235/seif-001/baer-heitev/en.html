Seven. For thus is the way of a niddah woman to be seven days without relations. And Shach wrote that all this speaks only when he stands in his vow; but certainly he can release through a sage, as above siman 234 s.k. 23. And the reason he does not divorce immediately is lest meanwhile he find an opening for his vow. And Rashba wrote in a responsum in the name of Rav Hai Gaon that even if he swore to divorce his wife, it is not an oath — like one who swore not to pay a creditor, since his words have no substance. And Shach does not hold thus; rather it is a proper oath, only they release him on condition he brings proofs for his words.
