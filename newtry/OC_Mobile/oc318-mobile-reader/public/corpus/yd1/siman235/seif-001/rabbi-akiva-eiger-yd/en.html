(Siman 235, seif 2) It does not take effect, for it is, etc. If he imposed the vow upon her while she was betrothed, the vow takes effect to prohibit her even after the marriage. Regarding language prohibiting her from deriving benefit from him, the view of Tosafos is that marital relations are not included in such language, whereas the Ran holds that she is forbidden to him also with respect to marital relations, and the Ra'ah is uncertain about the matter.
