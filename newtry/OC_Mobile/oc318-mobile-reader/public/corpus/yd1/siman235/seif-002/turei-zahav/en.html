For behold he is enslaved to her. In Tosafos beginning HaMedir, s.v. Hachi Nami, they challenged: behold we find he is not enslaved to her without saying "go out, work of your hands," etc. — such as he tied in relations, saying "your relations are forbidden if I sustain you." One can say: if so, it should not say "more than thirty days he divorces," but rather after thirty he enjoys and is forbidden — meaning he need not divorce until after seven days after thirty. And one cannot say he said "your relations forbidden upon me from now if you enjoy from me," since then forbidden to enjoy, like konam my eyes with sleep today if I sleep tomorrow — a person is not careful with conditions, as written above siman 213 seif 3; and below seif 3 will be explained. And what Tosafos wrote: a virgin in relations shall wait thirty-seven days — this is specifically in this case, that until thirty days it is possible to forbid from enjoyment without tying in relations, since it is possible to sustain through a provider; but after thirty days it is impossible, therefore the vow is not effective afterward at all; rather, since he tied it in relations, prohibition of relations is upon him, and it is like one who forbade relations alone, and he waits seven days and divorces. This is not like what is said later in this section; therefore "until thirty days," etc., implies the vow is effective afterward without tying in relations, and he must divorce — since there the vow is fully effective for small matters without tying, not obligated while she is with him; only after the vow took effect she needed to go to her father, and then the obligation of those matters was born; therefore they gave thirty days, possible through a provider for small matters; after thirty he divorces immediately. Not so here, where the obligation is from now to sustain by himself, not through a provider — only within thirty days she waives this; if not effective after thirty unless he tied in relations, automatically she needs seven more days after thirty, as the law of one who forbade relations.
