The Shach wrote that the husband must afterward pay him, and likewise above in siman 221 seif 8, regarding one who says, "whoever feeds," etc. Specifically where he went to a shopkeeper who is accustomed to deal with him, he is not obligated to repay him unless he wishes, for he appears as his agent; and so wrote the Bach. But this is not the view of the Taz, who ruled here too that there is no obligation upon him to pay, as he wrote there in siman 228; see there.
Without specification. This is when he vowed her off without specification or for more than thirty days; but if he explicitly vowed her off for thirty days, he may maintain her. Shach. The reason is that thirty days is the measure during which she is not particular to be sustained by a trustee; beyond that, the matter is degrading for her before people.
