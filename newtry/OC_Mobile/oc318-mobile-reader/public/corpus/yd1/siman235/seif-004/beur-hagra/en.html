If she vowed, etc. These two seifim follow the wording of the Tur, in accordance with the explanation above. Although one could explain here as well that she vowed and made the vow contingent upon marital relations, as the Ran writes, nevertheless, since the text does not say “one who imposes a vow” but rather states that he said to her, “on condition that,” etc., it implies the Tur's interpretation.
