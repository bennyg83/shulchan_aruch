And he heard and did not annul. See in Sefer Bnei Ahuvah chapter 13 of Hilchot Ishut halacha 10, s.v. "behold in Tur who wrote difficulty on Shach above siman 234 s.k. 84," who wrote that Tur's view is like Riyvo: that where she tied the vow in a matter requiring action, and not matters with affliction of soul or private matters, he cannot annul until the vow takes effect — how then can the husband annul the tying of relations, since he has not yet lent the utensils and the vow of relations has not yet taken effect? One must strain and say the case is where on the same day he heard, he lent the utensils; and if so, the prohibition of relations takes effect, etc. — see there.
