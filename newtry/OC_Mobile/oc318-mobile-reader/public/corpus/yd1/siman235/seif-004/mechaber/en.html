If she vowed a vow of matters between him and herself, or of affliction of the soul, and he annulled for her on condition that she say to so-and-so a disgraceful matter, or that she turn over after marital relations so that she not conceive, or that she fill ten kav of water and pour them into the garbage—and she does not wish to do one of these things and the vow was fulfilled—he divorces her and gives the ketubah.
