Collector, etc. In the Yerushalmi, last chapter of Peah: R' Elazar was a communal leader. One time he came down to his house. They said to him, "What did you do?" He said to them, "A group came and they ate and drank and prayed for you." He said to them, "That is not good reward." He came down another time. They said to him, "What did you do?" He said to them, "A group of poor people came and they ate and drank and cursed you." He said to them, "Now that is good reward." R' Akiva — they wanted to appoint him as communal leader. He said to them, "Let me consult with my household." They followed after him and heard his voice saying: "on condition of being burdened, on condition of being disgraced."
