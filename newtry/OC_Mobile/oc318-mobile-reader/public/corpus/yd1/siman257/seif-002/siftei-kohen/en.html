But one who is not trustworthy, etc. Even if he was appointed with the community's consent, if complaints are raised that he acted improperly and he is suspected of a matter that appears genuinely doubtful to the judge, he must plainly provide an accounting to anyone who challenges him. It is known that many communal expenditures may be disclosed only to discreet individuals. Mahari Weil, cited by the Darkhei Moshe.
And when the community wishes, etc. The Kol Bo writes that where the practice is to appoint known individuals for a fixed term, after which they leave office and others replace them, there is no suspicion when they are removed at the end of their term. The Darkhei Moshe cites this. This implies that during their term the community may not remove them merely because of suspicion. The Rama's words do not clarify this sufficiently.
