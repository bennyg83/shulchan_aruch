When the charity purse lacks funds, the collector should borrow; and when money is found later, he is repaid from it without needing permission from donors: {Rama: One who separates his tithes and lends to a poor person from his own — separates tithe and reckons this as what he lent, and withholds for himself what he separated. (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashi, chapter Kol HaGet.) Specifically while that poor person still lives; but if he died or became wealthy, he need not pay, for he was poor then and one does not tithe on lost property, and we do not suspect he became wealthy unless known. (Hagahot Mordechai, Bava Batra; end of Kol HaGet; Yerushalmi; Sefer HaTerumah, gate 65.)}
