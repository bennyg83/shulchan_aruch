Charity is included in vows; therefore one who says, "Behold I am obligated a coin for charity," or "This coin is charity," is obligated to give it to the poor immediately; and if he delayed, he transgresses "do not delay," for it is in his hand to give immediately and poor people are available. If there are no poor people, he separates and sets aside until he finds poor people: {Rama: All this is when he can distribute it himself; but when they vow charity in the synagogue to give it to a collector, or other charity he gives to a collector, he does not transgress even though poor people are available, unless the collectors demanded — then he transgresses immediately if poor people are available and the collector would distribute immediately; and if the collector did not know, he must inform him what he vowed so he can demand. (Mordechai, Bava Batra and Rosh Hashanah.) "Poor people available" means poor who need immediate distribution; but if there is no way to distribute immediately, it is not called "available." (There in Bava Batra.) And if he said he gives a coin to charity to so-and-so, he does not transgress until that poor person comes, even though other poor are available. (There.)} And specifically when separating charity unstated; but anyone may set aside charity money to keep and give little by little as he sees fit. (Tur in the name of Rosh.) And if he stipulated when vowing or donating that collectors may exchange and combine it into gold, that is permitted. (Rambam.)}
