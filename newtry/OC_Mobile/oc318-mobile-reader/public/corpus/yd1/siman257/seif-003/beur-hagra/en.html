And all this, etc. From what is stated in chapter 1 of Arachin (6a): "before it reaches the collector's hand," etc. The reason is that before it reaches the collector's hand, he is not permitted to distribute on his own, since he vowed with the understanding of the collector, as stated in the preceding siman. But when the collector does not demand it from him and he knows that he is obligated, he is permitted to redirect it, since there is no loss to the poor, as stated there: "R' Yannai would borrow and repay," etc. This is what is stated: "and if there is no," etc.; "and it is not considered," etc.; "and if he said," etc.; and all is as stated above — whenever it is in his power to distribute them, etc.
And likewise if he stipulated, etc. — Rambam; and plainly everything follows the condition; and Pri Megadim siman 254 seif 8.
