But not after they removed him. My revered father-in-law, of blessed memory, wrote that in an authoritative corrected text of the Mordechai it reads, “even after they removed him.” I likewise saw that Mahariv, siman 124, copied the Mordechai in this manner, and his words imply that he based a practical ruling upon it. Nevertheless, the versions may be reconciled. Certainly, if a substantial period passed after their removal, they are not believed, since they did not make the statement at the time of removal. This is what the version of the Shulchan Aruch excludes. The version of Mahariv concerns a case where they made the statement immediately after their removal. This appears to be the proper conclusion based on reason.
