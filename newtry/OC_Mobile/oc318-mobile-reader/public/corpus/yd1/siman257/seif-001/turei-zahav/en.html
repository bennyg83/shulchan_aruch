They may not separate from one another. Rashi explains that people should not say that the collector who is collecting alone intends to steal. It appears that his intent is as follows. Although in truth one person may not collect alone, as stated in siman 252, seif 3, because this is an exercise of authority, nevertheless an observer may think that since he separated from his colleague he is acting improperly, for collection requires two people. The observer will therefore conclude that he intends to steal. This resolves the difficulty that seif 2 states that accounts are not demanded from charity collectors. According to what I have written, the matter is clear. Here there is a defect in his conduct, because he separated from his colleague and acted improperly, and this brings him under suspicion.
Two at a time. So that people should not say that he places two coins into the collection but records only one.
