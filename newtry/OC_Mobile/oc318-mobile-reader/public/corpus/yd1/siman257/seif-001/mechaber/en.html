The order for collecting charity and the prohibition against delaying it. It contains eleven seifim:
Charity collectors may not separate from one another in the marketplace except enough (that they can still see one another. Tur. For example,) one may turn toward the city gate while the other turns toward a shop, and they collect separately. If a collector finds money in the marketplace, he should not place it into his personal pocket, but into the charity purse, and when he reaches his home he may take it out for himself. If the collector was owed one maneh by another person and that person repaid him in the marketplace, he likewise should not place it into his personal pocket but into the charity purse, and when he reaches home he may take it. He should not count the coins of the charity fund two at a time, but one by one, because of suspicion, as it is written, "You shall be clean before Hashem and Israel." (Rambam, Hilchot Matanot Aniyim chapter 9)
