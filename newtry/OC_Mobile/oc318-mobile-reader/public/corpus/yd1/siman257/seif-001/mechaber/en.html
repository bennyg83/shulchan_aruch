The order of collecting charity and not delaying it. And it contains 11 seifim.
Charity collectors may not separate from one another in the marketplace except so they see one another — such as one standing at the gate and one at the shop — and collecting. If a collector found money in the marketplace, he does not put it in his pocket but into the charity purse; and when he reaches home he takes it. If a collector was owed a maneh by his fellow and he was repaid in the marketplace, he does not put it in his pocket but into the charity purse; and when he reaches home he takes it. They do not count charity money two by two, but one by one, because of suspicion, as it says, "And you shall be clean before the Lord and before Israel" (Rambam chapter 9 of Laws of Gifts to the Poor).
