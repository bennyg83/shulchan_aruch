The order for collecting charity and the prohibition against delaying it. This siman contains eleven seifim.
Charity collectors may not separate from one another in the marketplace, except in a manner that allows them to remain visible to one another, such as one going to a gate and the other to a shop. If a collector finds money in the street, he should not place it in his own pocket, but in the charity purse, and remove it when he reaches home. If someone repays a personal debt to the collector in the marketplace, he should likewise place the payment in the charity purse until he reaches home. He should not count the charity funds two coins at a time, but one at a time, because of suspicion, as it is written, “You shall be clean before Hashem and Israel.”
