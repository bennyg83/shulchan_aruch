He may neither sell, etc. If the seller vowed not to benefit from the buyer, and the merchandise is worth exactly the money paid, both derive benefit and both are forbidden by strict law. If the merchandise is inexpensive, only the buyer who is prohibited by the vow derives benefit, and purchasing is forbidden. Selling is nevertheless forbidden by decree, lest he come to purchase. If the merchandise is expensive, purchasing is forbidden by decree because of selling. If the buyer vowed not to benefit from the seller, the reverse applies. See siman 227.
He may not lend him an item, etc. We decree borrowing because of lending, and taking a loan because of giving a loan.
