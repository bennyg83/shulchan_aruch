Reuven who forbade benefit upon Shimon may sleep (with him) on a small bed in summer days, but not in rainy days, because he benefits him. And on a large bed he is permitted, whether in summer or rainy days. And he may bathe with him in a large bath, but not a small one, because he benefits him when the water rises upon him. And he may sweat with him, even in a small bath. And he may recline with him on a bed and eat with him at a table, but not from one bowl, lest he eat from his portion. And if it is the custom of the host to return and refill the bowl after they ate everything that was in it — permitted. {Rama: Or if from the outset there was so much in the bowl that the remainder returns to the host — permitted (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran and Raavad).} If they were both digging in a vineyard together, he may not dig in the row near him, because he benefits him when he loosens the ground before him; but in a distant row it is permitted. And he may not sell to him, nor buy from him, nor lend to him nor borrow from him, nor ask nor borrow money from him.
