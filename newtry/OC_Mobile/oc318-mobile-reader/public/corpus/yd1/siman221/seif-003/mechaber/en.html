Reuven who forbade his property upon Shimon is forbidden to give him his minor or maiden daughter in marriage, since she is still in his possession and behold he hands him a maidservant to serve him. And if Shimon's property is forbidden to him — permitted to marry his daughter, even though he exempted him from obligation of her sustenance. And he returns his lost object to him, whether the property of the owner of the lost object is forbidden to return it, or the property of the returner is forbidden to the owner of the lost object. And in a place where they take wages for returning a lost object — if the property of the owner of the lost object is forbidden to the returner, he is forbidden to accept it; rather he returns it to him free. And if the property of the returner is forbidden to the owner of the lost object, he is not permitted to return it to him free. And if the property of both is forbidden one to the other, he cannot accept the wages, for if so he benefits; rather the wages fall to hekdesh.
