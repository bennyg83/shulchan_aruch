Hekdesh. Shach wrote: it implies that regarding hekdesh all agree he can forbid. It is surprising, for below siman 258 seif 7 Rama wrote: if he has collateral, some say even the surplus he cannot dedicate; yet here anonymously he wrote he can dedicate it; requires investigation. And Taz wrote: regarding practical law it is appropriate to be stringent, for in explanation he can forbid; but Shach wrote he is not important enough to forbid it upon another. And he concludes: nevertheless Ran's words appear correct that they would excommunicate him until he releases his vow. And see Shach there, who expands on this law. And he also wrote that he cannot dedicate except that portion he has in the house that is worth more than the rental price; but when it is not worth more he cannot dedicate it. And Taz wrote: it appears for us that even if he said "hekdesh," it does not take effect, for Rosh wrote that nowadays all our hekdesh is profane, it is only charity, etc.
