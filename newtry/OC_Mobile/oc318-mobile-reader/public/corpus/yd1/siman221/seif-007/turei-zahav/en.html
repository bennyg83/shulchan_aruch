He cannot forbid it upon him. This is the opinion of Rabbeinu Tam in Beit Yosef, and it implies even explicitly — and this is like the second "some say" nearby. And that Tur wrote the opinion of the first "some say" in the name of Rabbeinu Tam — meaning the first part alone, that one who rents a house to his fellow etc., the prohibition does not take effect on the house; but what he concluded afterward "and if he specified" etc. — this is not Rabbeinu Tam's opinion, for otherwise Rabbeinu Tam's words would contradict each other. And regarding practical law it is appropriate to be concerned for stringency of the first "some say" that in explanation he can forbid, and automatically here in the question he can forbid explicitly.
