Per my understanding, since regarding avodah zarah it is permitted even though it is written "and nothing of the ban shall cleave to your hand" — because its spoil exceeds its repair — all the more so regarding nedarim. And what he wrote that explicit proof etc., since we say that only regarding avodah zarah did the Merciful One reveal this and not elsewhere etc. — he was not precise, for Tosafot's intent is not to say that only regarding avodah zarah where the Merciful One revealed do we say thus, for then wherever we derive by "from where do we learn" we could not learn; rather Tosafot's intent is that only regarding avodah zarah is it not considered benefit since its spoil exceeds its repair, and if so we would not consider it benefit — meaning the spoil causes it and it is not considered benefit; but regarding Shabbat, although spoil causes it that there is no benefit, we would say he did melacha and certainly one who does melacha is liable even if he does not benefit; but that he be considered a spoiler and not doing melacha — we do not say this is Tosafot's intent. Also what he said that in nedarim too we follow the noder's intent etc. is nothing — after all there is no benefit here. Also what he brought from Raavad that forbidden in maddir benefit is nothing — there in the end there is benefit, but here since spoil exceeds repair there is no benefit. Also what he brought from Raavad is not proof — examine.
