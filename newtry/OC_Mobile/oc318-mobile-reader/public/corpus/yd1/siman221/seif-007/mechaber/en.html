One who rented a house to his fellow, and afterward the tenant forbade the landlord from his property — the prohibition does not apply to the house that he rented to him. And if he specified the house explicitly, some say the prohibition applies to it as well. And some say that even if he specified it explicitly, he cannot forbid it with the language of konam unless with the language of actual hekdesh. And the tenant may forbid him, for behold they are his fruits; and from a mashkon, his law is like a landlord, and the owner of collateral is like a tenant. {Rama: One who lends a house to his fellow cannot forbid it upon him for the entire time of the loan (Hagahot Maimoniot, perek 6). And the borrower may forbid it upon another for the entire time of the loan, for it is considered his (Beit Yosef, end of this siman, in the name of Rashba).}
