One forbidden benefit from his fellow may pay his debt, even if the lender had collateral in hand and this one paid on his behalf and took the collateral — he must return it to the borrower who is forbidden (the view of Rashi, Rivash, and Rambam). And he may feed his wife and children and servants, even Canaanite slaves. But forbidden to feed his animal, whether impure or pure. And forbidden to teach him Scripture, since it is permitted to take wages for it and he would teach him free — behold he benefits him. But he may teach him midrash, halachot, and aggadot, provided the forbidden one does not say to the forbidding party, "Teach me." And nowadays, since it is permitted to take wages even for midrash, everything is forbidden. But to teach his son, even Scripture, is permitted. And permitted to serve him with the cup of the house of mourning and the cup of the bathhouse. {Rama: And some disagree and permit even if he said to him, "Teach me" (Raash and Tur). And the same applies to every mitzvah matter, provided it is a matter requiring agency — such as separating his terumah: forbidden to be his agent (Beit Yosef in the name of Raash and Rabbenu Yerucham).}
