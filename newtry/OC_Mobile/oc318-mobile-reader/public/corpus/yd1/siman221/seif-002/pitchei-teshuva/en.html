[Eshel Avraham note 46, and see responsum Chatam Sofer siman 225 who brought from Maharshal in Yam Shel Shlomo Bava Kamma siman 34 who rejected the narga from the Mishnah in Arakhin 29, and wrote practically that if the tenant said anonymously "behold I dedicate it" it is not holy at all unless he specified explicitly that he dedicates the benefit he has in that house — and he (Maharshal) wondered at this and concludes there is no doubt he can dedicate it anonymously and it is dedicated until the time of rental payment; and there he expanded regarding most synagogues in his country that are only rented to the community by the city officer, that they have all sanctity of the synagogue for the entire rental period — see there.]
