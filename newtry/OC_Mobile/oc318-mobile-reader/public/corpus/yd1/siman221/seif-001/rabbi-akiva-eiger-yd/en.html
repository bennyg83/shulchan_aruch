(Siman 221, seif 1) And it is forbidden to chew wheat. This apparently proves that one who vows generally against a certain item is forbidden even when benefiting from it in an unusual manner. See Magen Avraham, siman 466, who states that chewing wheat and placing it upon a wound is an unusual mode of benefit. This is difficult in light of the Magen Avraham's categorical statement in siman 386, seif katan 20, that ordinarily a person who vows intends only the usual mode of benefit.
