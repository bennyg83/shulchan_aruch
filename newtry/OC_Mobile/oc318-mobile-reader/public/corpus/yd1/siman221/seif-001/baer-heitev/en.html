To pass through. Taz and Shach wrote: even if it does not shorten his route; it is nonetheless forbidden in any case; and this is unlike the Bach:
Which they rent. Since he waives the rent to him for that amount, the recipient can use the money to purchase food. Rosh.
To warm himself. Taz and Shach puzzled over this; why should it be forbidden here? And Levush wrote that it refers back to the earlier case of "the benefit of your food upon me"; and so it is correct:
Food. Shach cites the Yerushalmi that one may lend him cups, bowls, and serving dishes because they do not improve the food itself; the food is already prepared and is merely placed in them. Pots and pans are forbidden because the food is cooked in them and they improve it. The Yerushalmi also forbids lending him money, because this is equivalent to giving him money with which to purchase a loaf.
