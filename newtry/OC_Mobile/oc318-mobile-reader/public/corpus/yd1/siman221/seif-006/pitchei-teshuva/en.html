Healthy animal. See Beit HaTovim and see responsum Penei Aryeh siman 64 who raised like Rama and not like Taz, except he wrote nevertheless it is not permitted to slaughter for him a healthy animal except in a place where they do not give wages for slaughtering, or one who gives him his wages — but otherwise forbidden. And even so it is not permitted except when the forbidden one did not say to slaughter his animal for him, but rather this one comes and slaughters for him of his own accord; or the forbidden one said whoever slaughters my animal does not lose, or said whoever wishes to slaughter may come and slaughter — and there is also another who knows how to slaughter. But if he told him explicitly to slaughter his animal for him — we come to the dispute of Beit Yosef and Rama above seif 2. However in a place of mitzvah such as for Shabbat honor and similar, one may rely on Rama there. And see also there siman 66 where he wrote permitted to pierce meat to remove milk veins and blood of one forbidden benefit from him — for if the forbidden one wished to transgress the mitzvah he could eat it without piercing, and he does not hold him back; rather he wants to fulfill the mitzvah — behold he does not benefit except benefit of fulfilling a mitzvah, and mitzvot were not given for benefit. However to salt the meat to remove its blood is forbidden, for apart from mitzvah benefit he also benefits him, for salt sweetens the meat and gives it taste — behold he benefits him in what he does to make it fit food for his table — see there.
