He may sound it, etc. This implies that even the prohibited person himself may sound the shofar. This is surprising, because Orach Chaim, siman 586, seif 5, states that when one is prohibited from benefit from a shofar, another person sounds it and he fulfills his obligation, implying that he may not sound it himself, as the Beit Yosef writes in the name of Kol Bo. This requires study. For practical law, the Bach in Orach Chaim there cites a dispute among the authorities and rules that in pressing circumstances he may sound it himself. See there.
