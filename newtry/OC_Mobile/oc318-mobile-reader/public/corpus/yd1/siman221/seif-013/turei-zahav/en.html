He may use it to sound a mitzvah blast. In Orach Chayyim, siman 586, the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Kol Bo that specifically another person may sound it for him. The person prohibited from benefit may not sound it himself, for a person sometimes enjoys sounding the shofar. The Shulchan Aruch there ruled accordingly. Therefore, there is certainly no difficulty from seif 11, where one may not study from a sefer forbidden to him. Even without the reason I wrote there, the distinction is that Torah study certainly gladdens the heart, as evidenced by the fact that a mourner may not study Torah. This mitzvah is therefore unlike other mitzvos, concerning which we say that mitzvos were not given for benefit, for here actual pleasure reaches the person. One should not ask why, then, he may teach him Scripture, as stated in seif 2, since he receives joy from it. There, the teacher is obligated to teach him by virtue of the principle, “Just as I taught without payment,” etc. We therefore disregard the benefit. This is unlike lending sefarim, where he has no obligation, as written in the responsa of Maimonides above. So it appears to me.
