Permitted to blow in it a mitzvah blast. In Orach Chaim siman 586 in Beit Yosef he wrote in the name of Kol Bo that specifically after he blows for him, but the forbidden one himself may not blow, for sometimes a person enjoys what he blows; and likewise he wrote there in Shulchan Arukh. And according to this certainly there is no difficulty from that in seif 11 that it is forbidden to study in a book forbidden to him — not because of this reasoning that I wrote there, but because here the Torah certainly gladdens the heart, for it is forbidden to study during mourning days; if so this mitzvah is not comparable to other mitzvot where we say mitzvot were not given for benefit, except here benefit extends to the person. And if so, why is it permitted to teach him Scripture as stated in seif 2 — behold he has joy from this. One may say there the obligation is upon him to study with him by force of "what am I for free" etc. — therefore we do not care about benefit; unlike requesting books where there is no obligation upon him, as written in responsum Meimoni above — so it seems to me.
