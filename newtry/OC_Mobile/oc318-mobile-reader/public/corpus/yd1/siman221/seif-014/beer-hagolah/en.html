The Mishnah there on 33a, in accordance with Rav Oshaya, who said, "These are the words of Chanan," Ketubot 107b, and in accordance with Rashi, Rivash, Rif, and Rambam in chapter 27 of the Laws of Creditor and Debtor and chapter 6 of the Laws of Vows. They hold that Chanan addressed other debts as well, not only money lent to a man's wife for her support, as Tur wrote in their name. Rambam concludes there that nothing entered the possession of the person forbidden to benefit; the payer merely prevented a claim from being brought against him.
