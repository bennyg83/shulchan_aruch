On the second day he has fulfilled his obligation. For mitzvos were not given for benefit. He does not fulfill his obligation on the first day, however, because it is written, “You shall take for yourselves,” meaning from your own property and not from something forbidden for benefit. This object is forbidden for benefit and is not his property for any purpose.
It appears to me that one who vowed not to benefit from another may not give that person paid work, for the fact that he passed over other workers and hired him is itself considered benefit, as written in siman 160, seif 23.
