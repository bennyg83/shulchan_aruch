He has fulfilled his obligation. Because mitzvot were not given for physical benefit. On the first day, however, he has not fulfilled his obligation, because Scripture requires, “You shall take for yourselves,” meaning from your own property and not from property forbidden for benefit. The Rama writes likewise in Orach Chaim, siman 649, seif 2. The law concerning persons prohibited from benefiting from one another joining for zimun is explained in Orach Chaim, end of siman 196; see there.
