Two came out. Commandments were not given for benefit — but on the first day we require "and you shall take for yourselves from your own" and not from forbidden benefit; therefore the second did not go out. And see Orach Chaim siman 649 seif 2. And the law of those under vow of benefit joining in a zimmun — see Orach Chaim siman 196. And Taz wrote: it seems to me that one who vowed benefit from his fellow is forbidden to give him work for wages, for it is called benefit — what he saved others' wages for this one. As written siman 105 seif 23.
