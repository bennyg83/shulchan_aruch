One who gives a tablet to an artist to paint, and she forbade her husband after he painted it, before he paid him — she is permitted. One forbidden benefit from his fellow — the forbidding party may tell a shopkeeper, "So-and-so is forbidden benefit from me, and I do not know what to do," and the shopkeeper gives to him and returns and takes from the forbidding party if the forbidding party wishes to pay him (but he is not obligated to pay him). And likewise if the forbidding party had a house to build, a fence to erect, a field to harvest — he may tell workers, "So-and-so is forbidden benefit from me, and I do not know what to do"; they work with him and come and take wages from the forbidding party if he wishes to pay them. And likewise permitted to say, "Whoever feeds so-and-so or does with him — does not lose." But forbidden to say, "Whoever hears my voice shall feed so-and-so," for since it is language of command it is as his agent. And likewise forbidden to say to his fellow, "If you feed so-and-so you will not lose," for since he says so to an individual, he appears as his agent.
