“And he has nothing.” It is because he has no idea what he will be like and what he will be like
Behold it is hefker etc. And even though according to rabbinic words it is not hefker until one declares it hefker before three as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 273 seif 7 — here, since he has nothing to eat, they established it according to Torah law — so ends Bach. And one may say that even according to rabbinic words it is only regarding that the declarer of hefker can retract — so ends his words. And Bach resolved as Tosafot wrote at the end of Ein Bein haMedir: specifically regarding real property, but regarding movable property three are not needed — see there.
