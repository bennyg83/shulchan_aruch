And he has nothing, etc. Only here, because he has nothing to eat, did the Sages permit this. The Rosh rules likewise.
It is ownerless, etc. Although by rabbinic law property is not fully ownerless until it is declared before three people, as stated in Choshen Mishpat, siman 273, seif 7, here, because he has nothing to eat, the Sages applied the Torah law. Alternatively, even by rabbinic law the requirement of three applies only with respect to the owner's ability to retract. The Bach writes this. Another explanation follows Tosafot at the end of chapter Ein Bein HaMudar: the requirement applies specifically to real property, but movable property need not be declared ownerless before three.
