(Shach, seif katan 43) That is, the portion he owns in the house. In my opinion this is incorrect. That distinction belongs to the first answer, which therefore need not distinguish rent from debt, since Ketubot concerns the amount of the debt. The second answer adopts a different distinction: consecration can remove a rental right because the property will later be returned voluntarily, whereas sanctity of money does not remove a lien securing an ordinary debt, where the property need not be returned automatically.
