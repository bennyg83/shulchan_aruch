One does not make from them a mezuzah. In HaKometz (32) Tosafot wrote s.v. dilma lehashlim, etc.: "And further there is such as paragraph Shema at the end of the column and he added paragraph VeHayah if you listen in the margin below, or paragraph VeHayah at the beginning of the column and he added Shema in the margin above" — end quote. And it is difficult on the division that he wrote Shema above — behold this is out of order; and he wrote in beginning of siman 288 that it is invalid, and Beit Yosef brought so in name of Mekhilta; and if so, why do I need the reason "one does not lower," etc. — in any case it is invalid. And it appears Tosafot hold that out of order deals specifically within one paragraph itself, but not from paragraph to paragraph, since they are not adjacent in the Torah; and as written above beginning siman 288, and I brought proof from Orach Chaim siman 64. And accordingly it must be that per Rambam's view in Tur, who invalidates also when one precedes a paragraph — it does not deal here with the second dispute of Tosafot that we mentioned, but with the first dispute. And from here is strengthened the wonder that I wrote on Shulchan Aruch beginning siman 288 — and one may say that there it refers if he wrote first VeHayah and afterward wrote Shema below it; for then he should not have omitted this law of preceding paragraph (VeHayah) [Shema] in column order, but that he wrote first VeHayah. And further it is necessary — this is in the way of the first version where there is only change in writing but not change in earlier and later.
