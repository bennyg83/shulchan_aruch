Not to write it on gevil of a Sefer Torah. In it is one seif. A Sefer Torah or tefillin that wore out—one does not make a mezuzah from them, and one does not write it on gevil of a Sefer Torah, because one does not lower from a severe sanctity to a lighter sanctity.
{Rama: But it is permitted to repair the Sefer Torah itself with its gevil if it cannot be repaired another way and it would need genizah in any case (Maharam Padua siman 84). But if it can be repaired another way—even though the Sefer Torah is gevil and he wishes to paste klaf on it—he should not cut the gevil to repair it, for it is not forbidden to repair it from outside with klaf even though the Sefer Torah is gevil (same there).}
