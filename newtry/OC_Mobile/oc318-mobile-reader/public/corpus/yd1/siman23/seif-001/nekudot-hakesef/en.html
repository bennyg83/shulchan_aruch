(Siman 23, Hagahah, Shach seif kaf gimmel) He paused the time of shechitah by a hair's breadth more, etc. It implies: if he paused the time of lifting and laying down and a little shechitah, still needing much for majority — kosher; and it requires study why Rambam did not write this law; therefore it appears to Rambam: even if he paused the time of a little shechitah — even so the Gemara requires it, since the measure of shechitah is a small matter and a small measure is between slaughtering its majority and minority; therefore it must be asked whether it is treifah if he paused the time to lift, lay down, and slaughter somewhat; but if he paused less than this — meaning he did not pause the measure of all three, only paused the measure of lifting and laying down without shechitah, or vice versa — kosher; so it appears to me according to Rambam; and so is proven from commentators who challenged Rambam — for according to Beit Yosef's explanation there is no challenge here — end.
