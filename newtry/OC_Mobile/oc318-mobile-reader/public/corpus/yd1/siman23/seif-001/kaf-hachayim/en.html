(a) [Seif 1] Any slaughterer who does not know the five [laws] of shechitah, etc. And these are shehiyah, etc.; and furthermore there are two other laws he must know: checking the knife and checking the simanim after shechitah, as written above siman 1, seif 2, in Hagahah — see there; and in our words to the name, b'siyata diShmaya.
(c) And if he slaughters before us in a manner we can check the place of shechitah and we saw he did not do hagramah; we also checked and saw the simanim were not uprooted; also he did not do chaladah, such as we saw the feather or hair cut, as in siman 3, seif 1 — even though we found him slaughtering who knows only the law of shehiyah and drasah — permitted to eat from his shechitah, for three laws exist in examination; and if we examined and found them proper — kosher; and we also checked the knife and it is fine. But if he lacked the law of drasah or shehiyah — in any case forbidden. Perishah, letter 2; Zivchei Tzedek, letter 2. But Shach, letter 1, disagreed with Perishah's words above, and wrote he must also know chaladah — see there. And so Minchat HaZahav, general rule 15 in Asheron, letter 2; and Zivchei Tzedek, letter 3, agreed — except what he wrote in Asheron that he must also know hagramah; and he disagreed with Shach — Zivchei Tzedek wrote there this is not so, for hagramah one knows well, such as where we see he slaughtered in the middle of the neck and the heads of the simanim are still before us in their quality and he did not touch them at all — see there.
(d) There. And these are shehiyah, etc. And one should not ask reason for these laws, for all are halakhah leMoshe miSinai. Sha'arei Tzedek, letter 1; and one who is clever to give reason for these laws does not act properly, for damage may come from it, comparing matter to matter and thereby misleading the many. Turei Zahav, letter 1; Shach, letter 2; Asheron, letter 6; Chayei Adam, general rule 7, letter 1; Zivchei Tzedek, letter 4.
