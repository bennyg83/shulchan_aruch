[Strike it on its head, etc. And in Sefer HaTerumot he concludes: provided he does not break its neck-bone; and Maharshal wrote on this: it is excessive stringency, for it is obvious if he breaks the neck-bone with a knife it does not appear like shechitah — why should we be so stringent? — end of his words. Understand: it is forbidden because of shehiyah; and it is not so. Rather, the reason is as we learn in chapter 25: one who breaks the neck-bone of an animal before its soul departs — forbidden because of absorbing blood in the limbs; and it will be explained below siman 67 that l'chatchila it is forbidden to break the neck-bone even to eat it raw, as Beit Yosef wrote there in the name of Shibbolei HaLeket; and Maharshal himself ruled in chapter 25, siman 99; and so the Taz explained — see there.]
[And he should not return and slaughter. R' Tzvi wrote in Shechitot that it is permitted to slaughter afterward with a flawed knife, and it is not clear; and so it appears from Semak's wording, who wrote "he should not return and slaughter with a knife or a sickle," because he paused and an unspecified sickle is flawed; and moreover in any case it is forbidden because of ikkur in the last minority. So too Bach in siman 24, seif 12, in laws of ikkur; and he wrote: if he slaughtered with a flawed knife, even after he already slaughtered the majority with a valid knife — forbidden; and see siman 2, seif kaf 29, on laws of shehiyah in the last minority.]
[According to this there is no shehiyah in the trachea in fowl at all. For in the first minority of the trachea shehiyah does not apply, as above — like half a defective trachea is only doubt; not so in the esophagus, where shehiyah applies in the first minority of its perforation in any amount, as below siman 33; but the trachea is cut off in its majority, as below at the beginning of siman 34.]
