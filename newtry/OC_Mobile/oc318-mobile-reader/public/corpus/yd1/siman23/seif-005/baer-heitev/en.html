To kill it. And Shach wrote in the name of Sefer HaTerumot: only provided he does not break its neck-bone; and see siman 67 that it is forbidden to break the neck-bone even to eat it raw; and see siman 67 in Hagahah, seif 3, what must be asked on this and what Shach and Taz resolve there. See in Beit Yosef, siman 138: fowl called indik (and in another language "English hen") — most of the world prepares its head with an axe when it convulses; one should rebuke them not to do so until after blood comes out; and the same for other animals as long as blood spurts; and b'dieved it is permitted to eat its flesh through cutting and salting — Darkei Shalom, siman 37.
And slaughter. And Shach wrote: even not with a flawed knife, because of ikkur in the last minority.
