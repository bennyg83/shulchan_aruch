After he slaughtered the majority of one in fowl or the majority of two in an animal — shehiyah does not invalidate. According to this, there is no shehiyah in the trachea in fowl at all. And there is one who says that anything until the shechitah of both simanim is finished — shehiyah invalidates; and l'chatchila one should be careful to heed his words. {Rama: And even b'dieved the custom is to declare treifah; and therefore if after he slaughtered the majority of two the animal or fowl pauses to die — strike it on its head to kill it and do not return and slaughter (Terumat HaDeshen, according to Rashi and Semak and Mahari Weil and Or Zarua).}
