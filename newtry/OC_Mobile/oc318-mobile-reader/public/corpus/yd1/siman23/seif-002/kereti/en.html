And he raised — not precise; he only mentioned it as the ordinary way of speaking.
Until majority — Shach explains: even if he did not pause the time to slaughter the majority of simanim, only lacking any amount from the majority — nevertheless this was Rava's question according to Rambam's method, who slaughtered in a minority of simanim; and they explain "like a minority" with kaf — only that it was almost majority and now is minority; and it was resolved teiku; therefore this is doubt of neveilah; and the Mechaber should have mentioned it is doubt — good reasoning; only Shach was not so precise; and see Peleti, where I wrote another explanation.
Which is the valid shechitah measure — see Peleti: we follow the measure of one who slaughters with a fine knife; and even if he slaughtered with a bad knife and pauses in his slaughter — we do not follow his slaughter, only as one quick man with a fine knife in his hand.
Shechitah — and one may say stringently: the measure is one who slaughters two simanim, trachea and esophagus, as one, and not one after the other, for more time is needed; therefore I brought in Peleti: be stringent only if there is time to slaughter the esophagus and a bit of trachea — half a defective trachea; in such a manner the shechitah is valid, and the time for shechitah is shehiyah — see there.
And in fowl — see Peleti what I wrote on this to resolve Beit Yosef's difficulty on Ra'osh — see there.
Until blood comes out — certainly even if no blood, we are concerned perhaps he touched the esophagus; only when he says "It is clear to me I did not touch the esophagus" — here there is a difference: if blood comes out, behold defect is before us; not so when no blood — no defect; he is believed saying "It is clear I did not touch" — so Shach and latter authorities. And Peri Chadash wrote: all this is if the skin was cut; but if the skin was not cut through and through, even if blood came out — kosher, for it is skin blood; he did not cut through and through.
Comes out — and behold Ran's view, and so Rashba: the measure of shechitah is time to cut the skin and simanim, but not feathers. But in Peleti I wrote it does not appear so from Rambam's words and Terumat HaDeshen — see there; and one should be stringent.
In another place — and according to one who says in a known place the esophagus has examination from outside, even in animal one slaughters in another place — see below siman 34.
And afterward, etc. — and for us, who are not expert in examination, that fowl has no remedy; see Shach, Maharshal — his view is to be lenient in great loss; and do not be lenient lightly.
Any shehiyah — but if pushed in his hand at the time of shechitah from place to place, it is not called shehiyah — Taz.
Gomi — and the same for long grass: it is impossible that the siman and gomi be cut at one time equally; and the incident contradicts it; and he will not escape that he paused somewhat — Shach and Peri Chadash.
