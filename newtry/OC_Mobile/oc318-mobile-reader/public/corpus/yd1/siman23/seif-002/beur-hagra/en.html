And he paused, etc., and he or another came, etc. See there in the Mishnah and Tosefta.
(Likut) In order to lift, etc. Like R' Yehoshua ben Chananya, and not like Rashi's version, who reads "there is between them" only regarding standing to lay eggs alone; and that is its explanation according to his version. But according to Rashi's version, R' Yosi and Shmuel disagree, and lifting and laying down are not required — and this is what "some say," etc., without lifting and laying down (and that which he mentions fowl is because on this the first view disagrees in two matters; and also because he meant "and according to their words," etc. But in Likut below he explains plainly like Shulchan Aruch, that the first view is only in fowl). And in Terumat HaDeshen he wrote one should be concerned for Rashi's words in both — end.
Until the majority of simanim. Which is the Torah measure of shechitah even l'chatchila [and not like Rambam that they brought in Terumat HaDeshen (who explains) from that which it asks "he paused in a minority of simanim — what is the law?" if he paused the measure of a minority of simanim; and so in Terumat HaDeshen they contradicted it, for it should read "like a minority"].
And some say, etc. For he holds R' Yehoshua ben Chananya holds like Rav, since he holds small for small, all the more fowl for fowl; and Hagahot and first reasoning hold R' Yehoshua ben Chananya does not deal with fowl at all, and holds like R' Yochanan.
(Likut) And some say the measure, etc. This is Rashi's method, who ruled like Rav: measure fowl for fowl — R' Yehoshua ben Chananya is according to him, who says small for small, and if so the same fowl for fowl; and he also holds without lifting and laying down, even in large and small animal; and as the version of R' Peretz: standing to lay eggs — aleph bet; and he ruled like Shmuel and R' Yochanan in this, not like R' Yehoshua ben Chananya and R' Chanina; and so in Semag and Mordechai: we do not estimate at all in lifting and laying down, even for animal. But Ra'osh wrote: there is no such version reading aleph bet; and all agree with R' Yehoshua ben Chananya, only he wrote that R' Yehoshua ben Chananya, since he did not say "and fowl for fowl," implies that in fowl he does not require lifting and laying down — and this is what "some say," etc. But according to Rashi and Shas, even in animal we do not require, as above; and Ra'osh explained Rif's words, who ruled like R' Yochanan even in animal — meaning even large for fowl, and all the more large for small; only for lifting and laying down we estimate large for large and small for small; and in fowl, without lifting and laying down at all — Taz; and not like Be'er HaGolah, who forces an elephant into a basket of sin (i.e., distorts the sources); but R' Yehudah explains Rif's words like Rambam's words — and this is the first reasoning — end.
(Likut) Perhaps he slaughtered something, etc. For shehiyah invalidates in the first minority of the esophagus, as in the Tosefta that Tosafot brought there, letter 2, s.v. hachalid, etc.: it was taught "he slaughtered a minority of esophagus," etc.; and in the Gemara there, 30b, R' Hoshaya asked, etc. — end.
And if he comes, etc. As written above, 28a, that incident, etc.
And the custom, etc. Because it is impossible to be expert in the measures, they agreed to forbid in every case.
And if found, etc. For the simanim are stretched and slaughtered quickly, not so a thing lying in place; and even though he is occupied in shechitah it is called shehiyah, as written in seif daled — Terumat HaDeshen.
