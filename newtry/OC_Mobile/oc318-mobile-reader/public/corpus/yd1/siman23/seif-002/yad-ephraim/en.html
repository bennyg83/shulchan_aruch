Strike on its head to kill it. Ba'er Heitev. And see Tosafot Yom Tov in the name of Sha'arei Tzedek; and what Ba'er Heitev wrote in the name of Divrei Shmuel, siman 37 — textual error, should read 237; and they brought it in Likutei HaKadmonim. And see Beit Yaakov, siman 138; and Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 54: slaughterer slaughtered chicken, slaughtered entire trachea, gave fowl from his hand while presumed kosher; when brought home still alive, running as usual; ordered household member to cut esophagus in two so it dies; cut with flawed knife — forbid because of shehiyah and ikkur in last minority or not? Shach in siman 23, seif kaf 13, challenges R' Tzvi on this; and it appears to me to decide: certainly l'chatchila forbidden to do so; b'dieved, even though from Shach's words it implies forbid b'dieved, I do not think so at all: once slaughterer removed his hand completely from shechitah after slaughtering majority and it is called valid shechitah name on him — if afterward fowl pauses to die, do not invalidate b'dieved if he completes its death with flawed knife, since it already left slaughterer's hand in valid shechitah name — do not declare treifah further because of shehiyah. Such incident came regarding a sick person: they slaughtered fowl for him, slaughtered esophagus and trachea whole, still alive as before; I ordered leave until dies by itself, perhaps atonement for the sick; as long as chicken lived, sick lived; until one came and chopped its head — patient became moribund; as long as chicken convulsed, his soul did not depart — see there. And Meir Netivim, siman 17, on indik fowl wanting to be lenient: if clear to slaughterer one siman already completely slaughtered and he did not begin the second — permitted to chop head with axe; no concern of shehiyah in last minority, only where majority of simanim slaughtered — error in his hand; and explicitly Semak, siman 196 — heaven forbid to be lenient thus.
