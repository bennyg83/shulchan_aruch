And he wrote in Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 54: hard thorn stuck between esophagus and trachea from outside — declare treifah; for that which Rama mentions regarding lying in esophagus speaks of what is normally found through eating — see there, where he wrote regarding a chicken with thread coming from its mouth, pulled, felt it goes outside — how slaughter? He answered: slaughter trachea alone; even though l'chatchila two simanim, here b'dieved: if he slaughters esophagus he comes to shehiyah when cutting the thread; after shechitah see from where it came and investigate. They did so; found slaughtered head pulled through esophagus to crop and gizzard, knot of threads the chicken ate; no defect — validated. And in Divrei Shalom, siman 236: slaughterer slaughtered fowl, soft thin grass leaf found cut in two — be stringent because of shehiyah, as Shach wrote — see there. And in Ba'er Heitev, what he wrote in name of Or Zarua, siman 86 — textual error, should read tzadi vav; and what he wrote that for kashering utensils he leaned lenient — not so: he wrote those utensils may be kashered, but if mixture increased in those utensils they again used other utensils — no kashering needed — see there. And see what he wrote in name of Beit Yosef on cut of holder's finger; and so in Avnei Nezer, part 2, siman 42: one slaughtered fowl, friend held fowl at shechitah and also cut finger — validate, as we say in Chullin, daf 32: slaughtered cow and cut pomegranate with it — kosher; even though one may say specifically for them who require shehiyah measure, not for us forbidden like gomi found along neck length, not stretched, and knife sharpness pauses to cut it more than cutting siman — not so human finger holding forcefully: one may say cut esophagus and finger at once, cutting both equally — therefore I permitted — see there. And Zichron Yosef, siman 1: slaughterer slaughtered calf on special slaughter chair called shraagin; felt at shechitah knife touched shraagin wood during shechitah, but said "It is clear I did not pause at all, nor startled at touch, nor cut wood, only touch"; with extra honor checked wood — no cut; from speed of shechitah did not know if before or after finishing — he answered: even though Or Zarua, siman 66, wrote we do not judge safek shehiyah like other shechitah doubts, etc.; and also per Chinuch, Beit Yosef, siman 24, law if knife head pushed to wall or hard ground — shechitah invalid — one might say do not add; specifically pushed at shechitah, not in safek; nevertheless custom among slaughterers to declare treifah unless clarified clearly he did not slaughter after push; they do not distinguish light touch; and since he did not intend to finish shechitah, it is habitual from speed not to interrupt shechitah only to complete his act — not called safek if slaughtered after push; nevertheless he raised it is kosher since safek whether he cut or only touched without cutting and shehiyah — safek for leniency. And all the more in our case there is safek safeka, and also permission because he says "It is clear"; and even though in gomi found they declare treifah because there he errs thinking he paused cutting siman but actually paused cutting gomi; and so cutting finger from panic — sense testifies he certainly raised knife somewhat; and we can say same for knife head touching wall or hard ground — not so in our case: we can say since he knows shechitah laws and says "It is clear I did not pause" — we say certainly he did not cut wood and shechitah kosher — see there.
And I do not know if esophagus was perforated. Ba'er Heitev. And Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 65: slaughterer began slaughtering animal, slaughtered part of skin, paused, slaughtered afterward elsewhere; place first cut was washed well, checked, saw skin not entirely cut — if no blood, lenient plainly; but even if blood — lenient as Peri Chadash, seif 23: not like plucking feathers, for fowl skin is thin unlike animal skin where examination can stand; nevertheless not fitting for every slaughterer to do so — only ask teacher or join other expert slaughterers.
