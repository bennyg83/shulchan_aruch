This is invalid. Here too — doubt of neveilah that requires examination in the Gemara: he paused in a minority of simanim — what is the law? Teiku; and we stand stringently. The reason: since the majority of the siman was cut, it is as if the whole was cut; and what he does holacha u'va in the remaining minority is as if he does holacha u'va with its hand or foot; and therefore when he was delayed the measure of shehiyah in the last minority of the first siman — treifah in an animal; not so in fowl, whose fitness is in one majority. So too the poskim. And since the Rav did not emend here either "according to the custom in every case treifah" even if he was not delayed the measure of shehiyah — it implies he holds that here specifically we require the measure of shehiyah even for us; and so it appears in the Taz; and this is because he is occupied here with shechitah of the simanim. And see siman 18, seif kaf 11, what is written on this.
