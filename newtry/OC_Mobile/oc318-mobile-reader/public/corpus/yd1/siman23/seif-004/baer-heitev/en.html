And Shach wrote: and specifically in an animal, since one must cut both simanim in an animal; if so, when he slaughtered the majority of a siman as one, what he does holacha u'va in the last minority of the first is as if he does so with foot or hand — and it is shehiyah. Not so in fowl: when the majority of one siman is slaughtered it is immediately kosher. And since Rama did not write here "according to the custom declare treifah in every case," it implies he holds that here specifically we require the measure of shehiyah even for us; and the reason: since he is occupied here with shechitah of the simanim. However, certainly l'chatchila even in fowl one should not slaughter with it, since two simanim are required in fowl l'chatchila too — Mahari Weil.
