One who slaughters an animal, etc. Specifically in an animal: since two simanim require shechitah, and if he delayed while slaughtering the last minority of the first siman — even though he did holacha u'va in the way of shechitah, it is invalid; since he slaughtered the majority of the siman, the remaining minority is considered as slaughtered; and what was delayed in his slaughter is not counted as part of the shechitah, and it is as cutting with its hand or foot. Not so in fowl, whose fitness is in one siman. And see what I wrote on this at the beginning of siman 21.
