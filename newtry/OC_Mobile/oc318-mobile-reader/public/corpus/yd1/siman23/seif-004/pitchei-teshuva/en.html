And if after shechitah gomi is found. See responsum Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 61, where they asked about a chicken whose slaughtered head comes from its mouth and they pulled it and saw it is inside the esophagus and did not come outside — how slaughter it? He answered: practice like the case of blood spurting — slaughter trachea alone; for even though l'chatchila one must slaughter two simanim in fowl, here it is b'dieved: if he slaughters esophagus he comes to shehiyah when he cuts the thread; and after shechitah they will see from where this thread came. And so they did, and found the slaughtered head was pulled through the esophagus to the crop and from there to the gizzard, and there was the knot of threads the chicken ate; no other defect was found — and he validated — see there at length.
