Chumdei Daniel wrote: sometimes hard blisters are found in fowl between siman and siman; if one was cut with the simanim — declare treifah because of shehiyah; but soft things around simanim — no concern. And he further wrote: where one fears in fowl lest something be found in esophagus, such as it just ate, etc. — permitted to slaughter one siman l'chatchila, for time of distress is like b'dieved — end of his words; and see above seif kaf gimmel.
