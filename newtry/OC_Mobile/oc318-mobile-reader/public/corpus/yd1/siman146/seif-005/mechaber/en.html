A non-Jew nullifies elilim even though it is not his, and even if he does not worship those elilim, and even against his will, provided that he knows the nature of elilim. But a minor who does not know the nature of elilim, and likewise a shoteh, and likewise one who does not worship elilim, such as a ger toshav, cannot nullify it. (Ishmaelites, who are not worshippers of idolatry, cannot nullify) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam, perek 8).
