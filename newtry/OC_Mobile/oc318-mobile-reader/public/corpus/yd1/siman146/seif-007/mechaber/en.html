Elilim of a non-Jew: if he cut off the tip of its ear, or the tip of its nose, or the tip of its finger, or if he crushed it in its presence — even though it is not lacking — it is nullified. If he crushed it not in its presence and it is not lacking, or he spat in its presence, urinated in its presence, dragged it, or threw excrement at it, it is not nullified. {Rama: And there are those who say that even nullification by mere statement is nullification; but if he nullified it due to duress, it is not nullified until he does an act upon its body, as was explained (Mordechai, at the beginning of perek Kol HaTzelamim, in the name of Ravyah).}
