The convert. Inheritance by a convert is rabbinic, and the Sages were lenient so that he not return to his former ways. This applies specifically to a convert. If a father apostatized together with his son and then died, the son cannot tell his apostate brother, 'You take the idols,' and so forth. Likewise, one who entered a commercial partnership with a non-Jew and idolatry came into their joint possession may not say, 'You take the idols.' Shach.
