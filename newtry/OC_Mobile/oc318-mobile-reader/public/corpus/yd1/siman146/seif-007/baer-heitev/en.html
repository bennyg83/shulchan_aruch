Not. The Gemara derives this from the verse, 'When he is hungry, he becomes enraged,' and afterward, 'He looks toward the earth.' Although at first he denigrates his idol, he later turns back to it.
By declaration. This differs from sale or pledge below, where financial pressure compelled him. Bach. Darkei Moshe explains that there we do not know with certainty that he nullified it. Here the case is where he explicitly states that he wishes to nullify it by cutting off its ear or nose and the like. In sale or pledge he himself does not nullify it and assumes that the purchaser will not nullify it either.
