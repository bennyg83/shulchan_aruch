By a mere verbal declaration. The Beit Yosef wrote regarding this: I am astonished by the law stated shortly below concerning one who sold or pledged it, which implies that even if he sold it willingly, it is not nullified. The Darkei Moshe wrote that the case there is different, because the sale was not for the purpose of breaking it. But where he says, "I do not desire this idol," it is nullified through speech alone.
