Elilim of a non-Jew that broke on its own: its broken pieces are forbidden in benefit until they nullify it. Therefore, one who finds broken pieces of elilim — these are forbidden in benefit, perhaps the non-Jew did not nullify it. And if it was made of segments and a layman can restore it, he must nullify each and every segment of its segments; but if he cannot restore it, once he nullified 1 limb of it, all the broken pieces are nullified.
