Once a non-Jew has nullified it, it is permitted. We derive this from the verse, "the graven images of their gods," teaching that a non-Jew can invalidate his own god.
He has a partnership in it. The reason is that we say that the Jew worships with his own intention and not with the intention of the non-Jew. Therefore, when the non-Jew nullifies it, he nullifies only his own share.
