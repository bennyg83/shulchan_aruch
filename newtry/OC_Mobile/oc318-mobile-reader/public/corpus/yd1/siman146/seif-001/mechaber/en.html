Which idolatry has nullification and which does not; it contains 15 se’ifim.
Elilim of a non-Jew that a non-Jew nullified are permitted; but a Jew cannot nullify elilim of a non-Jew, and all the more so those of a Jew, which have no nullification forever. And even if the non-Jew has a partnership in it with him, the non-Jew’s nullification does not help for the Jew’s share.
