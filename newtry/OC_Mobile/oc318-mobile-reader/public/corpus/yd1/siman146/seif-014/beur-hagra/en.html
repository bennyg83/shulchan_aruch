(Collected) Mitzvah, etc. Tur: and thus the Rambam chapter 7 ; positive commandment to destroy idolatry and its accessories and everything made for it, as stated "you shall surely destroy, etc." "only thus shall you do, etc."; and in Eretz Yisrael commanded to pursue it until it is destroyed from all our land; but in chutz la'aretz we are not commanded to pursue it ; only wherever we conquer it we destroy all idolatry in it, as stated "and you shall destroy their name from that place" ; in Eretz Yisrael you are commanded to pursue them and you are not commanded to pursue them in chutz la'aretz; and this is in Sifrei parashat Re'eh on "and you shall destroy their name, etc." ; "one might think you are commanded to pursue them in chutz la'aretz; teach: and you shall destroy, etc. ; in Eretz Yisrael you are commanded, etc."; and the Tur holds that specifically to pursue one is not commanded, but one who finds is obligated to burn; and apparently the distinction between Eretz Yisrael and chutz la'aretz is that in Eretz Yisrael even in a place we did not conquer one is obligated to burn; but in chutz la'aretz only in a place we conquered; and so in Semag positive commandment 14: and in Eretz Yisrael commanded to pursue it even in a place we did not conquer until it is destroyed from all our land, etc.; but in chutz la'aretz we are not commanded to pursue it ; only wherever we conquer it alone we destroy all idolatry in it. And possibly this is the intent: to destroy what one finds is a mitzvah wherever "you shall surely destroy, etc." "only thus shall you do, etc." is stated; but to destroy from every place and pursue it so as not to leave any at all ; one is commanded only in Eretz Yisrael, for to pursue so as not to leave in the land is derived from "and you shall destroy, etc." and it is written "from that place, etc." (end).
