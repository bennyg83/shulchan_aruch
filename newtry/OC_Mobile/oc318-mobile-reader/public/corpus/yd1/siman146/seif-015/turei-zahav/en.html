And to give it a derogatory name. The Perishah writes in the name of the Maharshal that this applies specifically in Eretz Yisrael. This is astonishing. It appears that there is a scribal error here, and this comment belongs to the opening ruling, that one must root it out. That obligation applies specifically in Eretz Yisrael, which must be cleansed and purified. I later saw this stated explicitly by the Rambam in chapter 8: Outside Eretz Yisrael, we are not commanded to pursue it; rather, wherever we conquer, we must destroy every idol found there, end quote.
