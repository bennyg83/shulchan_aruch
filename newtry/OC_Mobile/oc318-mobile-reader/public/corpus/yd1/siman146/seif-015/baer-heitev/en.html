And to nickname it. Taz writes that Maharshal limits this to Eretz Yisrael, which appears difficult. Taz suggests that the words belong to the earlier clause requiring one to uproot it, which indeed applies only in Eretz Yisrael, as Rambam writes; outside Eretz Yisrael we are not commanded to pursue it. Nekudot HaKesef writes that there is no textual error. Both uprooting and assigning a degrading nickname are derived from the same verse, 'You shall destroy their name from that place,' and therefore both apply only in Eretz Yisrael.
