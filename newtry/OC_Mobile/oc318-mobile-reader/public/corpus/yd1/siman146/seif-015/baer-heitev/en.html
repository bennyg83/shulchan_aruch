And to name it. The Taz wrote that Rashi explained specifically in Eretz Yisrael — and this is wondrous; and it appears there is a scribal error here and it applies to the beginning that one must uproot it — this is specifically in Eretz Yisrael; and so is the Rambam's language; and in chutz la'aretz we are not commanded to pursue it, etc. — end. (And in Nekudot HaKesef he wrote there is no scribal error here; and so it implies in the Talmud: that which one must uproot it and name it, etc. — we derive from one verse "and you shall destroy, etc., from that place"; and just as uprooting is specifically in Eretz Yisrael — so too naming is only in Eretz Yisrael — end.)
