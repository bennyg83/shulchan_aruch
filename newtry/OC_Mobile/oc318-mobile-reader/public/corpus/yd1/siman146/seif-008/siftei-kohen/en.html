Even to a goldsmith etc. And all the more to a gentile goldsmith or to another Israelite who is not a goldsmith — in those cases according to all it is not nullified.
And some permit etc. Implying even in a pledge; and in Beit Yosef he wrote that even if he pledged it saying “if I do not bring you the money from now until such-and-such a day it shall be yours” and the time arrived and he did not redeem it — not nullified according to all, unlike the Ran who wrote that pledging is like selling — end; and so the Bach held as principal from the Mishnah, Gemara, and Rambam like the Ran — see there.
