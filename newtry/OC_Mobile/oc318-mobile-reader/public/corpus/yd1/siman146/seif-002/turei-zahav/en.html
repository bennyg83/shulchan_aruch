It appears to me that since the reason is only a decree, they decreed specifically where it came into his possession after he acquired it. But if it did not come into his possession, and he merely purchased it and gave the non-Jew the money, then although money legally effects acquisition from a non-Jew, as the Tur wrote in siman 132, "Immediately when he gave him the dinar, the wine was acquired for him," nevertheless, here they did not decree regarding nullification, since the idol originally belonged to the non-Jew. This is why the Tur and the Shulchan Aruch used the doubled expression, "once it came into the possession of a Jew and he acquired it," implying that acquisition alone is insufficient.
