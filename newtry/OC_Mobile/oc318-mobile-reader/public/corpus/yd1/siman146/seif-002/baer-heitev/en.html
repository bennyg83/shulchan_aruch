Nullification. The Taz wrote; and the reason in Beit Yosef in the name of Semag — a decree lest an idol of Israel; and it appears to me: since the reason is only because of a decree, they decreed only if it came to his hand after acquisition specifically; and if it did not come to his hand but he bought it and gave him the money — even though by law money acquires from a gentile — nevertheless here they did not decree regarding nullification — end.
