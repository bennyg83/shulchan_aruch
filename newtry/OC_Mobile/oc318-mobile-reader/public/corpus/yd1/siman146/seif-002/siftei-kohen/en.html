And the Bach wrote that even if he did not win it — such as where he found it before despair or stole it — even though by law it is the gentile’s idolatry and has nullification, nevertheless rabbinically it has no nullification, decreed because of a Jew’s own idol; and with this we say that appurtenances of idolatry that came to a Jew’s hands and a gentile nullified them are permitted; but if the Israelite won them, even appurtenances have no nullification — end; see there; and see below seif 13 in the glosses.
