(Collected) One who purchases, etc. Meaning, this does not follow the discussion at the beginning of chapter 2 of Bekhorot, where Ravina answers, “He neither paid nor performed meshichah,” etc.; rather, it follows the discussion in Avodah Zarah there, which mentions only Rava's answer (end).
If he paid, etc. Since it states there, “If, after he paid,” etc., it implies that both payment and meshichah are required. This is also evident in Bekhorot 13b: “And if you say that money effects acquisition,” etc.
(Collected) Although meshichah, etc. This is the Rambam's wording, and it is difficult, since according to the Rambam money also effects acquisition from a non-Jew, as he wrote in chapter 1 of Hilchot Zechiyah, and as stated above, siman 132, seif 2. Why, then, did he introduce this point here and not in the first clause concerning money? See Lechem Mishneh, who discusses this at length (end).
