He returns them to the gentile. And we do not say it appears like selling idolatry — for all know they were not acquired by him since he did not pull them.
And he did not give money. Although he agreed on a price — in such a case pulling acquires, as above siman 132 (and see above siman 138 and the Perishah) — nevertheless the pulling was in error since he did not know there was an idol among them.
He gave money etc. And likewise pulled first and then gave money; and the reason nowadays — since they were acquired by him, even though it is like a mistaken purchase — nevertheless when he goes back and takes the money it appears like selling an idol; therefore he shall lead them to the Dead Sea.
