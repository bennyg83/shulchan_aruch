One who nullifies an elil, its appurtenances are nullified (even if they are already in the hand of a Jew, they are nullified along with the idolatry that is in the hand of the non-Jew) (Mordechai, perek R. Yishmael; and so wrote Ran in the name of Ra’avad). If he nullified its appurtenances, it itself is not nullified.
