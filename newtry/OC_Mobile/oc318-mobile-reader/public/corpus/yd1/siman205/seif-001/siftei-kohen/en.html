Requires a query, etc. — Beit Yosef wrote in the name of responsum of Rashbatz: regarding one who was coerced to forbid a woman and attached it to a forbidden matter, one may say that under duress the rabbis did not require a query.
For regret alone does not suffice for him. His regret as below siman 208 — for if he does not regret from the root he needs to seek an opening as below siman 228; but here we are stringent with him such that even if he regrets from the root, regret does not suffice — Bach. And Ran wrote at beginning of chapter 2 of Nedarim that it must be from another place, and we do not open for him with “honor of your mother” — meaning: had he known there would be no honor for his mother in this he would not have vowed; lest you say since it is only d’rabbanan we open with this, we are informed — no — Rashba z”l.
Only in forbidding his wife to him. — since he came to annul procreation they were stringent — Perishah. And Ran wrote at beginning of chapter 2 of Nedarim: because it is his way to forbid his wife out of anger, etc.; but other vows, since they are not common this way, they were not stringent like attaching to a forbidden matter.
