The Rambam:
Opening. The Ran wrote that they do not open for him with the honor of his mother — meaning, if he had known there would be no honor for his mother in this he would not have vowed a Torah prohibition, and since it does not exist except rabbinically they open for him with this, he informs us. And Beit Yosef wrote in the name of teshuvah of Rashbatz regarding one who was compelled to forbid one woman and attached to something forbidden — one may say that in a case of compulsion the Sages did not impose stringency to require him to seek annulment.
In other matters. And Beit Chadash rules that now everyone agrees it applies even in other vows, and the words of Rabbeinu Yerucham appear to be primary. Shach.
