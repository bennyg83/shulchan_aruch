The Rambam:
An opening. The Ran writes that we do not open for him with his mother's honor, meaning, "had you known that this would not be honorable to your mother, you would not have vowed." One might think that since the matter is only rabbinic, we may open with it; therefore he teaches us otherwise. The Beit Yosef writes in the name of a responsum of the Rashbatz regarding one who was coerced to prohibit a certain woman and attached the prohibition to a forbidden object, that in a case of coercion the Sages were not stringent to require she'elah. With other. The Bach rules that nowadays everyone is like an am haaretz even with other vows. The Rav's words appear primary. Thus the Shach.
