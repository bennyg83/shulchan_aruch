You upon me like my mother. Avodat HaTammid of Rabbi Maharit z"l, who wrote in the name of Rabbi Elchanan: if he said "behold you are forbidden to me like my mother," it is an actual vow. And see on this in Teshuvot Rashbatz part 2 end of siman 25, who left this matter in doubt — see there; and see in Machaneh Ephraim siman 1, who expanded on this.
