And Turei Zahav wrote thus: and Beit Yosef brought teshuvah of the Rosh — one who says "my wine shall be forbidden upon all of Israel if I do such-and-such," and he transgressed and did it — he is not forbidden, for he cannot forbid unless he forbade it with konam or with something vowed, etc. And it is surprising — behold the Tur wrote at the beginning of siman 204 that language of prohibition is also a vow; and it is possible the Rosh held that language of prohibition is effective only upon himself but not upon others; and such is the meaning of the verse "to prohibit a prohibition upon his soul." And it seems this teshuvah is not authoritative; therefore the Tur did not bring it. End quote.
