And some permit. This is the opinion of the Rosh whom the Tur brought here at the end of the siman. And Beit Yosef brought another teshuvah of the Rosh, thus: one who said "my wine shall be forbidden upon all of Israel if I do such-and-such," and he transgressed and did it — he is not forbidden, for he cannot forbid unless he forbade it with konam or with something vowed, end quote. And it is surprising — behold the Tur wrote at the beginning of siman 204 that language of prohibition is also a vow; and it is written explicitly in siman 224 that a person forbids upon his fellow with language of prohibition. And it is possible the Rosh held that language of prohibition is effective only upon himself but not upon others; and such is the meaning of the verse "to prohibit a prohibition upon his soul." And it seems this teshuvah is not authoritative; therefore the Tur did not bring it.
