He should not vow until he has. Explanation — for it would be thought that whatever he does not have, nevertheless he accepts upon himself if he will have, as we find in Taanit in that which they decreed a decree and decreed not to sit in a fast, and they accepted upon themselves in a vow when it will be possible for them to fast they will fast, and they derived it from Daniel as it is said "from the day you set your heart to fast," etc.; if so here too let us say thus — he informs us it is not so, and he should not vow but wait until he has, and then give without a vow. And thus Beit Yosef concludes regarding the main point — at the time he has, he need not vow at all. And Maharach Or Zarua explained the language "he should not vow until he has" — meaning he should not make a vow if he will have, etc.; and the language "until" is not resolved according to his explanation.
