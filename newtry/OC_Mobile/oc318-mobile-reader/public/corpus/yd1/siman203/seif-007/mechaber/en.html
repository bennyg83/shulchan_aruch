One who vows vows in order to establish his traits and correct his deeds — this one is zealous and praiseworthy. How so? One who was a glutton and forbade meat to himself for a year or two years; or one who was mistaken with wine and forbade wine to himself for a long time, or forbade intoxication forever. And likewise, one who pursued money and was alarmed by wealth and forbade gifts to himself, or receiving gifts from people of this city. And likewise, one who was proud of his beauty and vowed nazir, and similar vows — all of these are avodah l’shem Hashem. And regarding these vows and the like, the Sages said: “Vows are a fence for abstinence.” And even though they are avodah, a person should not increase vows of prohibition and should not accustom himself to them; rather, he should separate from things that it is fitting to separate from, without a vow.
