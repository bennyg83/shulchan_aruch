(Shach there, seif katan 6) Even though it is an oath. There it is mentioned only concerning one who swore to nullify a mitzvah, such as one who swore not to sit in a sukkah. Likewise, an oath not to eat neveilos does not take effect, since one prohibition does not take effect upon another prohibition. But an oath that I will sit in a sukkah does take effect, for the requirement that it be expressed in the negative and affirmative applies only regarding an offering, not regarding the negative prohibition.
