It is permitted for him to vow, etc. However, even in casual speech, if he says "I will repeat" there is upon him the obligation of a vow, as below siman 213 seif 2; and so Rabbeinu Yerucham, part 3, and Semag daf 71 end of 73, and other poskim.
It is a mitzvah to swear, etc. — although an oath does not take effect on a matter of mitzvah, as below siman 239, nevertheless it is permitted for a person to urge himself. Shach.
