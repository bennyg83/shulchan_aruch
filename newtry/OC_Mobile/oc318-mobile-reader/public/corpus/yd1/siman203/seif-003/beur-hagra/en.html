Likewise if, etc. See Rosh, chapter 3, siman 2. He further writes that the Sages did not ordinarily attend to oaths, etc., and that he himself would act stringently, etc. Although this is not required by strict law, as the Rosh writes there, "all these are stringencies," and Mordechai writes likewise, nevertheless he states that one should be stringent. This is like what is stated at the end of chapter HaMadir: "Go see whether she sought release," etc.; and like the verse, "He swears to his own hurt and does not change," and chapter HaSholeach, 34-35, where they refrained from administering an oath, and similar cases.
