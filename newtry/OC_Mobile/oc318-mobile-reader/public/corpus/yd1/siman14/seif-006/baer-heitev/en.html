In them. And the Shach wrote: he mentioned these because the animal is not rendered treifah by them; and in the Talmud it challenges why different from fetus that is permitted to eat, etc., and answers that it is written "it" whole and not lacking.
Shechitah. And the Shach wrote: this is unresolved in the Talmud and deals with one that came out after its slaughter from the womb before mother's shechitah—for then even if nine months alive its slaughter is neveilah from doubt, lest shechitah is only after going out into the air of the world; but where it did not come out before mother's shechitah, it is permitted through mother's shechitah; and therefore since doubtful it is not called shechitah, forbidden to slaughter the mother after it on the same day, lest it be shechitah. However it requires study—for in any case it is chaladah—and examine.
