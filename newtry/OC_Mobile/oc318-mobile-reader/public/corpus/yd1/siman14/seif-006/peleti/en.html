In its mother's womb it is not called shechitah—or this law that implies even b'dieved treifah requires study, for the later authorities Taz, Shach, and Peri Chadash gave the reason it is unresolved and challenged Pri Megadim who gave reason of chaladah. Truly in Gemara Chullin daf 74b R' Eliezer asked: one extended his hand into animal's womb and slaughtered nine-month alive, etc.—what is the law; and R' Chanina resolved from mishnah: behold one born treifah from the womb—for it has no time of fitness; and if so, behold it has time of fitness—for if he wishes he puts in his hand and slaughters; Rava said to him: teach "formed treifah from the womb"—end. And Rashba cited in chiddushim and so Ran, and they wrote: since it was not resolved, we go stringently; but Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Tur, and Rashba in Torat HaBayit omitted it. And from this it implies they were not concerned. But go learn Tosafot's words in Beitzah daf 6 s.v. "and if what, etc."—they challenged why the Gemara says calf born from treifah—for it has no twilight on Yom Tov time of fitness, it is not prepared through its mother who is treifah; and Tosafot wrote: and it is astonishing—behold it is prepared, for we say in Chullin ben pakuah is permitted through four simanim through mother's shechitah or through its own; and if so he can extend his hand into its mother's womb and slaughter it and it will be prepared. And one may say the Gemara speaks according to Rav Oshaya who established there in Chullin whether ben pakuah is permitted through four simanim; or one may say since l'chatchila one should not slaughter in this manner, since he cannot see anything—as slaughtering at night that is forbidden l'chatchila—it is not called prepared—end. Here Tosafot's words need explanation—for what does it call to him, is it not plain it is unresolved and from such reasoning to challenge, and why Tosafot's challenge and resolve unresolved question there from this here—this matter in Beitzah is not a baraita, only question of R' Kahana and R' Eliezer to Rav, and whence resolve. And Tosafot's answer that it speaks according to R' Eliezer—for we do not find anyone disputes Rav Oshaya, and he is unresolved. And the essence answer: it is like slaughtering at night—what did they want with this, for from law it is forbidden even b'dieved—and see Piskei Tosafot Beitzah siman 22 who wrote one does not slaughter at night l'chatchila—end; behold he ruled for practical law b'dieved permitted, and it is against the Gemara above; and all Tosafot commentators did not address this. And what appears—that Tosafot and the other poskim who omitted this unresolved question hold that what Rava said "teach formed treifah from womb"—Rava did not intend to emend the mishnah from his heart, only such was the tradition in his hand that the mishnah must say "formed from womb"; and if so one may infer the opposite—why did the mishnah teach "formed from womb," which is uncommon, and not teach the common "born from womb"; nevertheless they had time of fitness—learn from mishnah that it holds one can extend hand into animal's womb and slaughter; and therefore it had to teach "formed from womb," for there is no time of fitness; and if so from Rava's words the unresolved question was resolved and therefore those poskim omitted. And this is Tosafot's view, and therefore they challenged there in Beitzah that it has time of fitness to slaughter in womb if like conclusion of the matter; and therefore answer in one resolution that they speak according to Rav Oshaya—for he does not have Rava's version in mishnah and it is doubtful to him and comes according to him; or that it comes according to conclusion like Rava, and nevertheless forbidden l'chatchila, for it is like slaughtered at night. And on this Piskei Tosafot hold that for practical law like Rava the unresolved question was resolved, only forbidden initially as slaughtering at night. And if so this law of Rama and later authorities is in great dispute—we say, and many poskim permit, until Rashba himself though in chiddushim he brought it in Torat HaBayit he omitted, and possibly Pri Megadim too holds the unresolved question was resolved like Tosafot, and holds Tosafot's reasoning that the reason of prohibition is similarity to chaladah, not that it is truly chaladah, as later authorities wrote it is not called chaladah only similar to chaladah and forbidden l'chatchila; and Tosafot's challenge in Beitzah is resolved that it is not called prepared since initially forbidden as above. And behold I found support for this in sugya of crushing limbs Chullin daf 61 that R' Nachman said: house of womb has no concern of crushing limbs; and the Gemara brought support from that which we taught: calf born on Yom Tov one slaughters it on Yom Tov; and it rejected exceptional birth and again brought support from that which we taught: and they are equal that if born with its blemish with it, this is from the prepared; certainly on spreading on ground. And all wondered why it did not answer immediately with first support on spreading on ground. And it appears what the Gemara answers on that baraita of exceptional birth is that otherwise it must be exceptional birth—for there on Beitzah page cited, Rav taught: calf born on Yom Tov is permitted for it is prepared through its mother in shechitah; chick born on Yom Tov is forbidden for not prepared through its mother; and the baraita is difficult: what R' Kahana and R' Eliezer asked Rav what difference between this and calf born from treifah—and if Rav was silent, how do we resolve; and see Shitah Mekubetzet what he wrote there. And one must say Tosafot's challenge to answer that one can extend hand into animal's womb to slaughter—and it is difficult, for l'chatchila forbidden like slaughtering at night for he does not see the shechitah. But if it deals with exceptional birth that otherwise anyway one does not slaughter on Yom Tov because of crushing limbs, and exceptional birth is that mother was cut and offspring removed—if so fine it is prepared like Tosafot's challenge to extend hand into animal's womb; and if slaughtering at night like cutting mother and womb place where born rests, then there is covering and eye sees how he slaughters and it is like slaughtering by day, and even l'chatchila permitted, and fine it is prepared—and examine. And this is clear as above, and so in Shitah Mekubetzet to Beitzah explained in name of Ritva also like Tosafot that forbidden to extend hand into its mother's womb to slaughter for it is like slaughtering at night—and examine.
