One who extends his hand into the animal's womb and cuts from the spleen or kidneys and the like and leaves the pieces inside its womb and afterward slaughters it—behold those pieces are forbidden because of eiver min hachai, even though they are inside its womb; but if he cut from the fetus in the womb and did not remove it and afterward slaughtered it—behold the pieces of the fetus or its limb are permitted, since it did not come out. (Rambam law 9.) {Rama: And if he slaughtered a fetus in its mother's womb, it is not called shechitah (Ran and it is an unresolved question in chapter Behemah HaMekishah and it was not resolved).}
