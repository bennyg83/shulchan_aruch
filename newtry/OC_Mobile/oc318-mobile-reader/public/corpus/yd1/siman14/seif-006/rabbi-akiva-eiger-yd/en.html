(Seif 6 in hagahah) It is not called shechitah. I am doubtful: if the mother died and he slaughtered the offspring in its mother's womb—in this one may say it is shechitah, for it only asks when the mother is alive, for then one may say the offspring is like part of the mother and not behemah separately; but when mother died, one may say the offspring is only like placed in a box and considered born. And as Ramban brought in Magen Avraham (siman 339 s.k. 15) that though Yisrael is not executed for fetuses, nevertheless when mother died the fetus is yelud and a door is locked before it. However according to Levush's reason that it is chaladah, also in such a case it is not shechitah. And in essence of the law it is difficult for me why Rama needed to bring this law, for Rama already wrote above (siman 13 seif 3) that if mother was slaughtered and found treifah, now the offspring has no remedy, for we are not expert whether its months were completed; if so automatically one cannot permit slaughtering offspring in mother's womb without permission of mother's shechitah, lest its months were not completed and it has no own shechitah. And perhaps Rama's intent is it is not shechitah at all even to be lenient that it is permitted to slaughter the mother afterward—for it is not oto v'et beno; and this is because it is double doubt: doubt lest months not completed, and if we say they were completed, perhaps shechitah in mother's womb does not help—and requires study (and not like Shach wrote who is stringent regarding oto v'et beno).
