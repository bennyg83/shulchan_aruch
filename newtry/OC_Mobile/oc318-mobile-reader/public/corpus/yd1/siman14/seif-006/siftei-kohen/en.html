And if he slaughtered a fetus, etc. This is an unresolved question in chapter HaMekishah (daf 74) and it is proved there that it deals with one that came out after its slaughter from the womb before its mother's shechitah—for then even if nine months alive its slaughter is neveilah from doubt, lest shechitah applies only after it goes out into the air of the world; but where it did not come out before its mother's shechitah, it is permitted through its mother's shechitah. And the Rabeinu Yerucham summarized understandably and relied on above (and therefore since it is doubtful it is not called shechitah, it is forbidden to slaughter the mother after it on the same day, lest it be shechitah). And the Pri Megadim did not know the source of this law and therefore wrote the reason that it is not shechitah because of chaladah—and the sugya there does not go that way at all according to this—see there; rather the reason is plain as I wrote, and so Rashi there; and the Lechem Chamudot already challenged on this daf 184 end of seif 1.
And he cut from the spleen and kidneys. He mentioned these because the animal is not rendered treifah by cutting them, as below siman 43 and 44; and in the Talmud (daf 69a) it challenges from fetus and answers that it is written "it"—whole—and not "it" lacking, unlike the Pri Megadim who omitted this sugya and wrote another reason—see there.
