(Seif 6 in hagahah) And if he slaughtered a fetus in animal's womb, it is not called shechitah. Unresolved in chapter HaMekishah and was not resolved. And I did not reach the end of the hagahah's view that followed the Talmud in this—for it is explicit in Talmud and Rashi (daf 74) and Tosafot (daf 75 end of seif 1) and Rashba and Ran that it is only a question regarding nine months alive, but eight months alive there is no question at all. And so Tosafot in first chapter of Beitzah (daf 6 seif 1)—for even if it went out into air of the world shechitah does not apply, for it is like dead; behold like cutting in slaughtered flesh as in Shabbat (daf 136)—see there. And if so the hagahah should not have written this law—for even if it went out into air of the world and he slaughtered it immediately, forbidden lest it is not nine months, as hagahah wrote above siman 13 seif 3—see there. However this is only from stringency side and it is not considered a doubt as written nearby; but this law is full doubt and practical difference for double doubt whether there are two doubts here: perhaps in mother's womb this is not shechitah at all, and if we say it is shechitah, nevertheless perhaps it is not nine months. However since we rule regarding wife of kohen that presumption of fitness applies, we follow majority of offspring.
