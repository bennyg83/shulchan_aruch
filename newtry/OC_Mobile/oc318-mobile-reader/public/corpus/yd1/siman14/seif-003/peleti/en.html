Most with minority of limb—Shach explained: with this minority there is most of it outside; and they said we follow the majority—meaning now with the minority it is majority outside; and he strained unnecessarily, for the plain meaning is there is majority outside aside from this small amount, only inside half and outside half and a bit; and if we throw minority of hand outside inside—for majority of hand outside, behold inside is majority six and a quarter; but now aside from hand there is majority outside, we do not rule on minority of hand inside—and behold outside is majority; and it is plain; and this is the unresolved question that was resolved; and we judge in it both leniently and stringently.
