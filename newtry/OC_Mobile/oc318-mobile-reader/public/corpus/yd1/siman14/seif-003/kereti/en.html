Half with majority of limb—came out half and half; and his daughter: the half that came out is majority of the hand; and if so, if we throw the minority that is inside after majority of hand outside, here is departure of majority—it is unresolved and we go stringently that mother's shechitah does not permit; and likewise in reverse, if afterward the mother was slaughtered, we say perhaps it is not yelud and therefore offspring's shechitah does not help further, for mother's shechitah already permitted it; and what is outside—all treifah.
Most with minority of limb—I wrote in Peleti by way of parable: outside six olives and minority of hand goes up three quarter-olives; and inside with majority of hand five and a half olives; and if we say we throw minority of hand inside—for majority of hand outside, behold inside is majority six and a quarter; but now that aside from the hand there is majority outside, we do not rule on minority of hand inside—and behold outside is majority; and it is plain; and this is the unresolved question that was resolved; and we judge in it both to be lenient and to be stringent.
