Half with majority of limb. For example, most of its hand came out; and when what came out from the fetus remains only half; and if one adds the minority of the hand inside after the majority of the hand that came out to complete majority of the fetus—it is forbidden; and this is an unresolved question in the Gemara and was not resolved, and we hold stringently; and therefore it is clear that its own shechitah does not help either, lest it not be considered yelud; but what remained inside is permitted through its own shechitah in any case—if yelud, plainly permitted; if not yelud, permitted through mother's shechitah, for it did not come out at all. But the second case—most came out with minority of limb—is plain in the Gemara that we follow majority of the fetus and not majority of limb inside; and if so, even leniently we hold yelud; and if so, its own shechitah helps even for what came out; and therefore he should not have written the same language for both "behold it is yelud"; also the Tur's language "and so, etc." is not precise, for they are not in one category.
