And the fetus brought out its head. — In Choshen Mishpat siman 277 it is explained that most of the forehead is as if its entire head came out; and even though in the Talmud at the beginning of chapter Yesh Bechor and in Rambam chapter 2 of Laws of Inheritance and in Shulchan Aruch there it says that if the forehead came out it is as if all of it came out—it is plain that this refers only to the law of forehead, which is like its head; but certainly most of the forehead is like all of it and is counted as if its entire head came out; and so the Pri Megadim and Semag there s.k. 6 and Perishah here; and so below siman 194 in Tur and Rambam and Bach in Choshen Mishpat deleted the word "most"—and for practical law, as I wrote.
