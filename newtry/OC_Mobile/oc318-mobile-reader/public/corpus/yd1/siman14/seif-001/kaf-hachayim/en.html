(1) [Seif 1] The animal straining to give birth. At time of shechitah. Rashi Chullin 68a. And he mentioned "straining"—see there that birth was with difficulty. Zivchei Torah siman 1.
(2) There. And the fetus brought out its head. And one need not all the forehead, only until most of its forehead. Perishah siman 1; Taz s.k. 1; Shach s.k. 1; Peri Chadash siman 1 for practical law siman 1; Levushei Serad siman 1; Kereti siman 2; Zivchei Torah siman 2. And specifically stringently one may say so, but leniently it is not yelud even with departure of entire forehead until one estimates majority of its head came out. Shaarei Deah siman 2; Pri Deah siman 1; and practical difference: if nine months dead, all forbidden lest it be yelud; if alive, what is outside forbidden lest not yelud, and what is inside permitted through its own shechitah in any case. Pri Deah there. Zivchei Tzedek siman 1.
