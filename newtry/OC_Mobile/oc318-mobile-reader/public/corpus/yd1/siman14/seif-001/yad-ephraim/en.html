And there is one forbidden limb. Avodat HaGershuni; and Netiv Chaim wrote in Mishneh Torah siman 54 according to Mishneh Lamelech what Mordechai wrote end of Chullin in name of Rabbenu Meshullam that a thing mixed from beginning of its creation is not nullified; and from this he wondered on what later authorities wrote in siman 14 that regarding outgoing limb milk is nullified in sixty—for this is mixed from beginning of its creation; and he wrote that therefore nullification does not help, for otherwise why did they rule the unresolved question stringently regarding majority—for certainly there is majority here, and it should be rabbinic doubt leniently like mixture of min b'mino and nispach; and he answered: they too hold in majority it does not help from beginning of creation, only regarding sixty where the matter depends on taste, and where it is known there are sixty it is permitted since there is no prohibition taste here, unlike where there is doubt whether there are sixty—it is Torah doubt; and if so it is not similar to nispach that from Torah is nullified in majority, unlike here that is not nullified in mixtures from beginning of creation; and nevertheless on Shach it is astonishing who wrote nullified in majority, etc.—see there, and he was astonished. And nevertheless I wonder—in my humble opinion Mishneh Lamelech wrote well that even sixty does not help: granted if we come to forbid because of taste we judge sixty to nullify taste, unlike here where we do not come on taste—for from min b'mino it is only because nullification does not help in a thing from beginning of its creation, and it is as if the substance of prohibition here without nullification—what does sixty help for substance of prohibition, and it is similar to min b'mino according to those who say not nullified even in a thousand—not nullified; and that sixty helps in min b'mino is because its substance without taste is nullified in majority and prohibition is only because of taste, and since there are sixty there is no taste, as explained in Rashba Chullin daf 98; but where there is no law of nullification at all, one may not permit even if there are sixty, whether min b'mino or min she'eino mino; and even though taste is not sensed, since it stands in substance without nullification, and like swallowing prohibition—even though there is no sensed taste here it is forbidden; and this too in min she'eino mino where mixture in sixty helped to remove prohibition taste, and all the more in min b'mino where its taste was not removed at all—and there is no nullification here; and what will sixty help him. And that which we say in Chullin daf 98 to derive from "arm when cooked" regarding sixty—even though there it is min b'mino—Tosafot already wrote there that certainly there it was nullified in majority, they only relied on the verse; end of his words. But the essence law of sixty has place only in min she'eino mino where the substance has law of nullification—sixty helps to nullify taste; but where there is no law of nullification at all, even sixty does not help—and requires study.
