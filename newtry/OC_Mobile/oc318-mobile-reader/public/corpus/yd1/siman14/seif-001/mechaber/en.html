The law of a fetus in an animal's womb. In it are 6 seifim.
An animal straining to give birth; and the fetus brought out its head—even though it returned it—behold it is like one born (yelud) and is not permitted through its mother's shechitah. Therefore if it is eight months—alive or dead—or nine months dead, it is forbidden. And if it is nine months alive, it is permitted through its own shechitah.
