(Siman 14 seif 5) Its milk is forbidden to drink. And if half came out with majority of limb, one may say the milk is permitted because of double doubt: doubt whether it is yelud, and there is no issur yotzei here; and if we say it is not yelud, doubt that perhaps regarding what came out the milk is also permitted; and ostensibly this is hidden from what the Taz wrote s.k. 6 regarding ben pakuah that came on superior animal that the offspring has no remedy through shechitah—the milk is forbidden—for this too is among the unresolved questions—see there; and behold this law of ben pakuah on superior animal that offspring has no remedy because we are concerned for father's seed is only a doubt, as we hold in siman 15 that for practical law we hold it is doubtful whether we are concerned for father's seed; and if so it is double doubt. However one may say: there regarding doubt whether concerned for father's seed, the offspring has presumption of prohibition that it is not slaughtered, and we judge from presumption that it has no remedy through shechitah; but here with half and majority of limb one may say we do not say it is certainly not yelud that shechitah does not help what came out—for regarding what remained inside the presumption of prohibition is reversed to judge it yelud, that if it were not yelud it would immediately be permitted through mother's shechitah; and from presumption of prohibition it is fit to judge it was not permitted; and if so there is presumption here and there; therefore one may say it is fine as double doubt.
