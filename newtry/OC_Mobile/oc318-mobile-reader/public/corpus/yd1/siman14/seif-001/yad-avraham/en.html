With this the wonder of Tosafot at beginning of HaMekishah s.v. "the reason, etc." is resolved—why did the Talmud not resolve that departure of head is yelud from what it taught regarding firstborn. And according to what I wrote to reconcile, and therefore he inferred from firstborn to inheritance that it did not depend on opening womb alone but that it be viable offspring, and learn that all of it is yelud; otherwise behold the head alone is not fit to be viable offspring—and see there the sugya and you will understand what I wrote is clear.
