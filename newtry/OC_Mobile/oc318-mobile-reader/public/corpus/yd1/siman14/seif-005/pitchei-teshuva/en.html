In kosher milk. Avodat HaGershuni; and see in Sefer Bnei Chiya who disagrees with Shach and Peri Chadash who agreed to permit the milk if there are sixty in the animal against the forbidden limb—for they omitted Mordechai's words in Chullin daf 560 who wrote there is no nullification except in a thing that was recognizable by itself at first and afterward mixed, but in a thing whose entry into the world began mixed, it is not nullified—see there; also Mishneh Lamelech chapter 1 of Laws of Tum'at Metzora law 14 brought what Mordechai wrote that a thing mixed from the beginning of its creation has no nullification, and wondered on the Ashkenaz later sages who debated here whether milk is nullified in sixty—see there where he elaborated; and see in responsum Noda Biyehudah Tinyana part Yoreh Deah siman 54 that there is this difficulty, and he wrote Mordechai's words are only regarding nullification that we come from Torah law of majority, but in a thing nullified in sixty whose reason for nullification is because then the taste is not sensed—the Mordechai's distinction does not apply—see there.
