Forbidden. And the Shach wrote: the same with ben pakuah that came on a regular animal—the offspring has no remedy, as in siman 13 seif 4, that milk of that offspring is also forbidden to drink—and not like the Bach.
Kosher. And behold the Bach and Shach permit if there appear to be sixty in the animal against that limb—it is a rabbinic doubt of min b'mino that from the Torah is nullified in majority, but rabbinically requires sixty; however the Taz ruled one should not be lenient (and Nekudot HaKesef disagrees), lest perhaps from this small limb more milk grows than from a large limb; and the Shach concludes it is a rabbinic doubt and we rule leniently; and so Peri Chadash.
