Also Shach's words who wrote forbidden to slaughter mother after it same day lest it be shechitah do not seem right—for certainly for need it is permitted to slaughter mother immediately after because of aforementioned double doubt; and after slaughtering eight-month alive certainly permitted to slaughter mother after it same day—for we hold eight months has no shechitah in its species and is not included in "and you shall slaughter from your cattle and flock," as explained in Talmud Rashi chapter HaMekishah (daf 72), and so Rambam chapter 2 of Laws of Fathers of Impurity, and Rashba in Torat HaBayit; and so Tosafot beginning chapter 3 s.v. "in offspring," etc., and Tosafot (end daf 100) that eight months is not called ox and sheep—see there. And if so it is not in "ox or sheep, it and its son you shall not slaughter," etc.—for here there is neither ox nor sheep nor shechitah, only like dead whose shechitah does not purify even from neveilah, as in Talmud there and Rambam as mentioned—see there. And such double doubt helps even in a thing that has permission tomorrow, as Shach wrote siman 110 s.k. 55; and see there seif 8 in hagahah.
