What came out from the limb to the outside is forbidden. For it is said "and flesh in the field, treifah, you shall not eat," and Chazal expounded: once flesh went outside its partition—that is, the womb which is the partition of the fetus—it is forbidden.

And all this, etc., only regarding that it be permitted, etc. Meaning: its mother's shechitah is what diminishes its power; for if it was not born before its mother's shechitah—even though it is nine months alive—the limb is forbidden and its own shechitah afterward does not help it; and see the reason in s.k. 11.
But if the animal later gave birth to it. Specifically before shechitah; but after shechitah—even if born through the birth canal—it is forbidden, as is proved in Tur and Beit Yosef; and so the Bach; and with this what was left in Darkei Moshe and Perishah in Beit Yosef's words requires study—see there.
