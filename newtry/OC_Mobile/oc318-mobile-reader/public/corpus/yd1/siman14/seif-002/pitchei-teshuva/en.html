That came out and was returned is permitted. See in responsum Beit Shmuel Acharon part 4 siman 9.
What came out from the limb. Here the law of issur yotzei is stated in the Talmud regarding ben pakuah that koshered it, that every offspring is permitted through mother's shechitah; therefore outgoing limb has no remedy, unlike when mother is treifah. Indeed Shach's view below s.k. 7 is clear that even when mother is found treifah issur yotzei applies and what is outside is forbidden. However Peri Chadash disagreed—see in Levushei Serad who wrote it appears the essence is like Peri Chadash, but for practical law one should be stringent and say if the limb that came out is a limb on which the soul depends, forbid all of it even what was inside, and when it grows its milk is also forbidden (and if there is another side of permission such as mixture, etc., one may permit—see in my explanations s.k. 7); and if it is a limb on which the soul does not depend, be concerned and forbid the outgoing limb in any case, and the rest is permitted through its own shechitah, and when it grows its milk is permitted—see there.
