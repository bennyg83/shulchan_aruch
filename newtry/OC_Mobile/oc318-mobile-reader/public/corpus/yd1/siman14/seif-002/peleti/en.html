But if it gave birth, etc.; and if the mother was slaughtered and found treifah, until the offspring found inside requires shechitah—for the Merciful One koshered four simanim—if offspring's shechitah is effective also for the outgoing limb: Bach's view it is not effective, and so Shach and Peri Chadash challenged that it is effective. And his words seem without proof, for he brought proof from that which we say to R' Meir there is no issur yotzei—learn from this offspring's shechitah helps for limb too—there is no proof, for plainly for R' Meir mother's shechitah does not work at all on offspring, plainly offspring's shechitah permits the limb; but with treifah where her shechitah helps even for outgoing limb to purify from neveilah status, as appears from mishnah there daf 68b—if so, mother's shechitah already took effect and offspring's shechitah no longer helps for eating permission. However there is proof from that which R' Yirmiyah asked what to be concerned for its seed, and the Gemara challenged: it implies it came on superior animal—why particularly this with issur yotzei—even superior ben pakuah, for Rav Mesharshia said according to those concerned for father's seed, ben pakuah on superior animal—that offspring has no remedy, etc.; and it is difficult what is the challenge—perhaps the question is in such a case that it brought out its hand and afterward mother was slaughtered and she is treifah—then offspring has shechitah but outgoing limb has no shechitah, and it came on regular animal—offspring has remedy for it has shechitah from every side, but limb forbidden for we are concerned for its seed. Fine, from that we say to R' Yehudah there is issur yotzei, there is prohibition of milk and blood—no difficulty, perhaps in such a case—and if so offspring needs and shechitah is effective and there is prohibition of milk and blood in it. Unlike regarding limb there is issur yotzei that is not permitted through shechitah. No difficulty—for it deals with one that came on daughter pakuah like it, and therefore not needed as above; rather this challenge from the outset does not exist, for daughter pakuah like it is not needed, only it deals in such a case. And truly according to Rashi and his supporters who hold that of Rav Mesharshia does not exist according to those who hold the Merciful One koshered four simanim—if so the Gemara that challenges R' Meir is not needed as above, since we do not hold four simanim, etc., in fetus found in treifah womb also treifah and has no remedy at all.
