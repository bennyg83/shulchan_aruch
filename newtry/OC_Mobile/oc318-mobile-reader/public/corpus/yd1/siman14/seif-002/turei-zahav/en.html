From the limb to the outside is forbidden. For it is written "and flesh in the field, treifah, you shall not eat"—once it went outside its partition it is forbidden; and this fetus's partition is the womb to permit it through its mother's shechitah; and since it went outside its partition—that is, into the air of the world—it is forbidden from the permission of its mother's shechitah.
And the place of the cut. For this is the distinction between inner and outer; and since it returned it, one need not cut toward the inside but only narrows and cuts, and the place of the cut is permitted—for we apply to it "in behemah you may eat"; but if it did not return, we do not apply "in behemah" at the place of the cut.
Even if it is its minority. For we hold: birth does not apply to limbs like R' Yochanan, and not like Rav who holds birth applies to limbs.
