(Siman 50 seif 1 in the hagahah) Every place where one cannot hang that it was done after slaughter — even though it has inspection, if they did not inspect it or cannot inspect it — behold it is like safek treifah and we forbid it. And he wrote in responsa Tur HaEven siman 41 that even the hagahah was stringent only if it was already slaughtered and the organ was lost so it cannot be inspected; but when it was not yet slaughtered he admits we go leniently — and therefore he permits slaughtering on Yom Tov a bird in which a defect was born. And therefore he permits milk from an animal that limps and it is not known if its nivin were eaten. And especially in this treifah that certainly was not born with it and it has presumption of its body — also Tur HaShalem is lenient. And moreover R' Dain: we do not establish from prohibition to prohibition in safek of reality from Yevamot (daf 119) — we do not learn presumption of a married woman from presumption of prohibition of a yevamah for the marketplace. And therefore only regarding throwing a get are we stringent because Tosafot wrote in Ketubot (daf 22) that we have a get before us — unlike a balanced treifot book where even though a defect was born from life that was in a presumption that it is not slaughtered — it is a different prohibition. And all the more there is a majority of kashrus and no other majority against it to remove it. And even in a book if the esophagus was perforated — nevertheless "she was slaughtered" before us. Only in a book where it sat or was gored we establish from life that it is one prohibition that does not leave, and also there is in it "I am certain" — organ 45 like intestinal children when it was not slaughtered properly — as one who places in a basket; from when it was slaughtered siman 1 and they did not permit unless it was slaughtered with slaughter that permits to Israel. And in siman 17 he agreed to Tzitzat Tzvi's view regarding a found needle — we hang that it fell; and the same regarding found broken — if it is not recognizable that it was done from life we establish the original presumption and now it deteriorated — and not like Tur HaShalem.
And see in Levushei Serad who wrote plainly to forbid lost buka without inspection, and he saw this from Mordechai forbidding without inspection. And there is no proof — Mordechai is according to his view who holds like the poskim Shach brings that we do not say "she was slaughtered, she is permitted" where there is nothing to hang on for permissibility, as Tur HaShalem wrote siman 29.
