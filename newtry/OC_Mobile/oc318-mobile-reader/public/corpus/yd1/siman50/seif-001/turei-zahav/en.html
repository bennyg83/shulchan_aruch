Tur wrote "in the liver and heart," and Beit Yosef wondered — behold perforation does not disqualify the liver; and he answered that it deals with the liver stalk, and Maharshal explained that it deals with the liver opposite the gallbladder — namely, the gallbladder was perforated and the place of the liver opposite it was also perforated; then it is forbidden, as above siman 42:
