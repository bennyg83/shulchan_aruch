See Peri Chadash and Tur HaShalem in what he wrote in the name of Penei Moshe: when water is found in the body cavity; and see in Noda BiYehudah part 1 siman 25: when water is found in the body cavity one must inspect the internal organs, etc.; therefore one must be stringent even in great loss. And in responsa Bach siman 157 regarding a chicken whose belly was swollen, etc., and it was copied in Likkutei HaKabbala; and see in Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 65 where he brought Bach's words and wrote: thus in water found in the body cavity in a chicken — in Bach's responsum siman 157 he ruled to permit when no defect is seen and there is room to hang on illness as is the way of all swollen ones that have water between skin and flesh; and one who wishes to be stringent may be stringent for himself and not for others. And see in my books Bach siman 39; and see in Peri Chadash regarding an animal similarly — he concludes one may not permit except in great loss; apart from that I heard from physician-sages that there is danger in eating such food — one must be concerned it will also lead to illness called (wassersucht); and similarly I heard regarding chickens whose legs are swollen — even in the lower bone one must be concerned it will lead to illness podagra, meaning gout; and those who eat come to this illness — and the matter is common in chickens; therefore one who guards his soul should distance himself from them:
