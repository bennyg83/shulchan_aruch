And in great loss one may rely on those who permit. Behold the Rav in Bach siman 54 wondered — above in siman 43 the Mechaber and Rama left undifferentiated that with two livers it is treifah — and this is Rashba's view, for according to Rambam it is kosher; and how did the Mechaber bring here Rambam's view too, and Rama wrote one may rely on him in great loss. And behold in truth in Rambam himself chapter 6 of Hilchot Shechitah din 20, who wrote: the spleen was removed or two spleens were found — kosher; and he did not write two livers — it implies he holds like Hagahot Gedolot that it is treifah; however from this there is no proof that he holds perforation of ducts within the liver is treifah; and if so, the same applies to liver deficiency — certainly the ducts were ruined, and therefore treifah; and one cannot challenge from Hagahot Gedolot's law. But for us, who hold not like them and perforation does not disqualify ducts — nevertheless we declare treifah with two livers, which implies not like Rambam; however, according to what is written in Ra'avad siman 43, one may say that in any case Ran decides not like Rambam — from that which is taught "all excess is considered as removed" and it is not taught "as deficient"; if there were a difference between removed and deficient, see there; and according to his words it must be said: even for Rashba it is difficult why it teaches "as removed" and not "as deficient"; and it must be said it is mere language, since there is no practical difference — and this did not please Rambam's eyes; therefore he holds on the contrary there is a difference between created missing and removed. However, the Sages had a tradition that in excess thus weakness prevails in the animal as if the organ were removed — for weakness is stronger than created missing; so excess of the weak organ prevails until it cannot endure and both become useless and go and are considered removed through weakness; and this is what he inferred in explaining their words: "all excess" — not like created missing, only like removed; for through excess that organ eventually becomes entirely gone and going and becoming deficient — and therefore it says specifically "as removed." And if so, this case of two livers that is treifah is for all; but the Rav in Tur HaShalem, according to what is written below siman 54, implies he does not hold thus — and see what I wrote there, for in my humble opinion it is clear as I wrote. And behold at first glance there is proof for Rashba who argues on Rambam from the Gemara Niddah 24a: sealed body does not become impure through childbirth — and what is a sealed body? Rabbi says: enough that one take from the living and it dies; R' Zekharyah says: until the knee; R' Yanai says: until its navel; R' Yosi says in the name of R' Yosi bar Yehoshua: until the place of its navel. And the Gemara says: between R' Zekharyah and R' Yosi there is a dispute whether treifah lives — some say treifah lives and some say treifah does not live; and Rashi explained: if cut from the knee upward — treifah; and he holds treifah does not live; and R' Yanai disagrees and holds treifah lives; and between R' Yosi and Yochanan there is a difference — that R' Eliezer says the thigh was removed and its cavity, and Rashi explained: R' Yochanan does not have R' Eliezer, etc., see there. And at first glance it is difficult — behold we taught "I gave you legs from the knee and upward," etc.; but deficient we did not teach, only cut — namely removed; and if so, created missing according to Rambam is kosher — and behold there the body is sealed, which is created missing; and if so kosher according to Rambam; and in the Gemara it implies for all treifah — only the disputant holds treifah lives; and this is at first glance proof for Rashba's words. But in truth one may say Rambam concedes regarding deficient leg it is treifah. However, after examination I saw that this is exactly one of the proofs Rambam had in this law — Rambam ruled in Hilchot Issurei Bi'ah chapter 10 halacha 11: sealed body that does not become impure through childbirth — this is until its navel per R' Yosi bar Yehoshua; and they already wondered on him — R' Yosi bar Yehoshua holds thigh and cavity removed is not nevelah and also treifah lives; but according to what he ruled that thigh and cavity removed is nevelah — if so he should have ruled until its navel; and the matter requires further study. At first glance, this that he ruled treifah does not live — there is no difficulty; one may say he holds like Rashba's novellae in the name of Ramban: since it lives, the offspring is viable; and it must be said that which we say "treifah does not live" there in Niddah is per R' Meir there in Chullin who does not live more than a novelty; but this is difficult. And therefore it appears it is difficult for Rambam — if so the tannaim argue on that of R' Eliezer who said the thigh was removed, etc.; why does the Gemara challenge in Chullin 32b and consider also that R' Eliezer's thigh and cavity were removed — nevelah; and it is difficult to establish the mishnah like R' Yosi bar Yehoshua who argues on R' Eliezer, and the anonymous mishnah like R' Yosi bar Yehoshua who is a tanna; and similarly the Gemara answers in chapter 8 as many tannaim, see there; therefore Rambam holds they did not argue on the body of treifot, only on this reasoning whether created missing and removal are one law, whether there are offspring in them; and therefore R' Yosi who said until its navel holds: just as removal is treifah or nevelah, so created missing; but R' Yosi bar Yehoshua holds: in truth it is nevelah, only this is in removal; but in created missing — treifah and not nevelah; and all these are created missing and there is no nevelah here; and therefore we need until the navel; and therefore the Gemara in Chullin cited cannot establish that mishnah — those treifot of R' Yosi bar Yehoshua who argues on R' Eliezer — for on the essence of the law in removal they do not argue, only argue on created missing like sealed body; and Rambam who decides this reasoning between created missing and removal — therefore ruled like R' Yosi bar Yehoshua; and one support for his friends — and the matters are clear — examine. And so is proven from Tosafot 57a s.v. dilema, etc., who wrote: and this should be said — even nevelah too is called if the thigh and its cavity were removed, as R' Eliezer said, and there is no one who disagrees; and so they wrote in the statement before it: why, etc., that it is not omitted to teach in one place dislocated thigh in an animal kosher — there is R' Yosi bar Yehoshua who does not have it; and therefore entirely kosher he holds; if treifah — R' Yanai holds treifah does not live; and if you will say R' Yanai and R' Yosi bar Yehoshua argue in two matters in treifah whether it lives and in R' Eliezer thigh removed, etc. — this is not the sugya of the Gemara. At first glance, from Tamid 11b where we say: R' does not have R' Eliezer who said removed, etc. — there is no difficulty for Tosafot — there one may say we do not need R' Eliezer who said nevelah, but treifah nevertheless; but here it is proven he holds entirely kosher — difficult; and therefore it must be said Tosafot hold like Rambam — strained to say they argue on the essence of the law that we do not find, etc., only if deficient in the category; but removed — certainly treifah; and the matters stand as they are, for Tosafot and Rambam agree as one — and who will open his mouth against them; and therefore, since Rambam has a good peg, Rama wrote one may rely in great loss; and so is the view of Tur HaShalem and Or Zarua; and especially where there is otherwise a branch to lean on; and even Peri Chadash who is stringent — clear:
