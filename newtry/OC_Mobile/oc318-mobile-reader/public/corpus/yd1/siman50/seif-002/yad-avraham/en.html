(Seif 2) And the meaning of what we said: if created missing — treifah, all the more if removed by hand; and everything we said: if removed — treifah, the same law applies to created missing; and some permit. This is Rambam. And in my humble opinion he deals with treifot that became treifah because of themselves — such as the liver was removed or flayed, which is in the category of removed, as Rashi wrote beginning of chapter 8 (daf 43) and Shach brings siman 29. But in treifah that is not because of itself — such as the upper cheek was removed, which Rambam declares treifah as written siman 33 seif 2 because of lung cooling. And if so, there is no distinction here between removed by hand or created missing — for if the cheek was created missing, this is lung deficiency that we taught in the mishnah in chapter 8, or that it was deficient — as above, clear. And with this the Rashba's challenge in Tur HaShalem (daf 43) on Rambam is removed. And see at length siman 41. And what Tur HaShalem wrote on Rambam at the end of siman 29 from the Gemara Niddah (daf 24) — he left in requires further study; also the Rav in Kiryat Sefer sensed this and they settled it — see there. Indeed, what he wrote that there is proof for Rambam from the above Gemara — in my humble opinion one may distinguish between person and animal, as written in novellae on Niddah and in Rambam's composition Hilchot Issurei Bi'ah.
