Perforated. Shach wrote: and even though above siman 45 some are stringent regarding perforation — whether kosher if removed is disputed — we are not concerned:
To created missing. (The same.) If created perforated. A goose whose skin was whole on its entire outside and a deep depression was seen two finger-widths wide in the middle of the belly along its length, and it was evident there was a deficiency inside under the skin; they skinned it and found that the middle bone protruding in the middle like a thin board — the flesh on both sides was cracked and split along its entire length and in its very middle; and in that break between them the heart and liver were seen from outside as they lie in the body cavity, and it appeared to have been done thus from birth — in Sefer Tzitzat Tzvi siman 76 he leaned to permit; but because it was a new matter not seen like it by the multitude of treifah inspectors, see there.
