That he forbade. The Taz and the Shach wrote that with an oath too the law is so, that it is forbidden forever, unlike nezirut, which is only thirty days (one who forbade himself to pray in so-and-so's synagogue and did not set a time is forbidden to pray there forever, Rashach I siman 36. Likewise, communal consent for which they did not set a time is forever, Rashach III siman 105).
