One who vowed, etc. That is to say, he made one matter contingent upon another, saying, “A particular item is forbidden to me if I do not marry a woman.” Otherwise, this is the language of an oath and is not a vow at all. Perishah. It nevertheless appears that nowadays it constitutes a vow with any formulation, as stated above at the end of siman 206. See above, siman 218, se'if katan 2. See there, where some also say that when one expresses a vow in the language of an oath, it is effective under the law of incomplete declarations.
And he did not fix a time, etc. This is not exclusive. The same applies if he fixed a time to marry within one year or the like. We compel him only to marry a woman who is suitable for him. So writes Responsa Mabit, volume 2, siman 104, page 50c.
