In liver of domestic chicken. The Rav Taz in s.k. 9 wondered: granted Rashba holds when intestinal children are lost and inspection impossible — kosher; therefore here it is like lost, we do not know if domestic or desert; unlike according to what we hold in seif 5 that lost intestinal children — treifah; if so, since we do not know domestic or desert, it is like lost and treifah; and he properly wrote that also above, who is lenient in green from R' Nasan, also proves he holds lost — kosher; but if lost — treifah; then it is not comparable to change of appearance where lung is lost — by law kosher, for what we hold treifah is only rabbinic enactment of Chachamim, as Shach wrote above siman 39; therefore in doubt whether from scald or from R' Nasan we hang on R' Nasan; but here the doubt is whether to hang on R' Nasan or not — it is like lost and treifah; therefore it must be he holds lost kosher. Behold the Rav in Nekudot HaKesef explained: when we say treifah in lost — all intestinal children; but here some intestinal children are before us with no defect, only domestic liver has scald — we hang on desert birds; reasonable matter; but if not, even in lost one could say thus; if so, Ran, who is teacher of this law, would learn from lung whose ribs protect in either-or: if no defect — obviously kosher; if defect — we see ribs did not protect; therefore it must be they are not before us; and the reason ribs protect — otherwise treifah; and difficult: other organs heart, gizzard, liver — what is their benefit if not before us, according to his law treifah, for they are not before us to inspect; and if before us with no defect — still difficult why ribs protect, behold they are before us elsewhere with no defect; therefore it is clear Ran's view is not to distinguish. However, Taz himself wrote from Ran's words there is no proof that every difficulty where lung did not change — we see ribs did not protect; and Rosh already wrote we hang change on chance, and Shulchan Aruch ruled; if so, Ran's law does not exist, only one could say they ruled by reasoning since it fell into fire it deteriorated and therefore needs inspection; and especially according to seif 6 that change disqualifies without falling into fire; if so, falling into fire is unnecessary; and it must be falling into fire alone obligates inspection; and therefore division is fine as Shach wrote — examine well.
