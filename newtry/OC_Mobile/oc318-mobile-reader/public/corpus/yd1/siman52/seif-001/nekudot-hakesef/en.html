(Siman 52 in Taz s.k. 5) He elaborated to wonder on Tur and Beit Yosef and wants to say green like grass — there is no one who disqualifies, for green like grass is kosher from R' Nasan without our seeing it fell; if so, in falling too we hang like R' Nasan, and there is no difficulty; and Tur and Beit Yosef's words are clear that Rashba answers initially: that which he counted heart, gizzard, and liver — not specifically these, but the same applies to lung if it contracted, and they spoke only regarding inspection; and for this change it is obvious green like grass disqualifies lung in falling into fire — for if green like grass were kosher, it is not possible to say "not specifically," but specifically they taught heart, gizzard, and liver, which are disqualified like grass, and lung kosher; and one cannot say it deals with gogel green, for it deals similar to heart, gizzard, and liver; and furthermore in gogel green one cannot say "not specifically," for gogel disqualifies lung even without falling into fire, and it is not relevant to mention here. Rather certainly when we say that which they said heart, gizzard, liver that turned yellow are disqualified — the same applies to lung that turned yellow, perforce like grass; and what Rashba said afterward "I still have to say it is impossible to count lung with them," etc., is another reason — specifically he counted heart, gizzard, and liver, and it is impossible to count lung with them because lung is kosher when green like grass; however for halacha Rashba does not hold this "I still have to say," rather as he explained initially; and so is proven from his words in the short Torat HaBayit, and as I will write shortly; and so Ran in chapter These Treifot explicitly — this is his language: that which lung was not counted with them — even though where it fell and turned yellow — treifah — for even though above we said green is kosher from R' Nasan, that is when it did not fall into fire; but in falling into fire we do not hang lung on R' Nasan, just as we do not hang liver on illness; this was the reason: because lung is not made to contract like heart, gizzard, and liver, since ribs protect it; and furthermore its law may not equal those, that heart, gizzard, and liver need inspection whenever it fell into fire, and bird lung does not need inspection; and Rashba wrote vegetation disqualifies in liver only like grass, etc., end of his words — this is Rashba's first answer; and if so, grass disqualifies lung in falling into fire; and so is proven explicitly from what he wrote in the short Torat HaBayit daf 23a: even though the Sages did not count lung among reds that turned yellow, it appears to me the same applies, and this is the reason — only because bird ribs protect it; and from the default inspection is not needed for lung even if it fell into fire before us; therefore they did not count it among those needing inspection after falling into fire; but if we saw lung turned yellow — treifah, for it is known ribs did not save it; and there are those who permit and their words were not clarified at all, end of his words — behold explicitly he does not hold halachically this "I still have to say," etc., rather like the first reason in the long Torat HaBayit; and if so, also gogel green he declares treifah, as Ran explicitly wrote; and therefore Tur properly aimed at Rashba's view and Rosh's view — Rosh obviously disagrees with Rashba, for Rosh wrote it implies even if lung changed we do not declare treifah, since ribs protect we hang change on something else, not on fire-fall, etc.; one cannot say since we see it changed it is treifah, for then lung should have been counted with heart, gizzard, and liver; rather certainly from not counting with them, change does not disqualify even in falling into fire, end of his words — all this is clear in my eyes, and I did not need to elaborate except because he elaborated to wonder on Tur and Beit Yosef and concluded this is one of the places commentators of Tur did not notice; in my humble opinion there is no wonder at all, and Tur and Beit Yosef's words are simple and clear, and commentators of Tur did well not to write anything here. I again saw in Rabbeinu Yerucham Netiv 15 chapter 5 ot 21 — it implies there explicitly also as I wrote, that for Rashba all green is treifah from the reason they said there is no lung for a bird to contract only regarding l'chatchila that inspection is not needed; but if we saw it contracted, even grass — treifah — see there. And further it appears to me: even if you wonder that Rashba declares treifah is specifically in gogel green, nevertheless let it be clear on Tur why he wrote Rosh disagrees — for it is proven from Rosh that even in gogel he permits, like the view of "there are those who interpret"; and if not, what does Rashba argue against "there are those who interpret" — perhaps they deal with grass, especially since it appears explicitly "there are those who interpret" are Tosafot who wrote thus on daf 26 top, and they did not mention morika green in their words, only unspecified — it implies they permit all green; if so, Rosh, who concludes thus, holds like them; and one should not challenge: if so, how does Rosh answer that which all treifot the Sages counted in animal — correspondingly in bird — this is not a difficulty, for Rashba already challenged "there are those who interpret," and as they answered, so Rosh answered. And further: let it be clear for their view they certainly were not unaware of that which all treifot the Sages counted in animal — correspondingly in bird; for regarding that very "there is no lung for a bird to contract" which "there are those who interpret" brought — it is so in the Gemara, version in chapter 1 daf 57a: what is "there is no lung for a bird"? If we say it has none at all — behold we see it has; rather it is not declared treifah; and Levi taught: treifot the Sages counted in animal — correspondingly in bird; rather it has no falling and contracting — what is the reason? Since most of its ribs protect it, etc.; rather it appears to me commentators certainly hold all eighteen treifot declared treifah from clear treifot such as perforated, deficient, removed, and the like — correspondingly in bird; but contracted, which is not certainly treifah except because ultimately it will perforate — in bird the Sages knew, since ribs protect it, even if it changed and contracted it will not ultimately perforate — so it appears clear in their view; and it appears clear Rashba also does not consider this a challenge on "there are those who interpret," for otherwise he should have included it in his other challenges; rather he did not write thus, only like a branch and proof for his words, meaning: since in animal it is declared treifah, correspondingly in bird it is declared treifah in them. But one may well say for "there are those who interpret"' view as I wrote — and it is clear; examine well. If so, it is also obvious that without falling into fire it is not declared treifah, for since in falling into fire when it became green it is kosher — all the more so without our seeing it fell into fire; and in animal, where green gogel is treifah even without falling into fire — perforce the Sages knew in animal in such a case it will ultimately perforate; and in animal certainly, since it is green in this appearance, lung certainly fell into fire; and even though Rosh holds we do not say when known it fell into fire and also contracted — this does not apply in animal regarding other intestinal children, but lung green gogel is scald, certainly from fire, or even if you say from something else — nevertheless the Sages knew it will ultimately perforate; and what Tur wrote unspecified above siman 38 that appearance of green gogel is disqualified and did not distinguish animal and bird — because he relied on what he wrote here in this siman that there is no lung for a bird in Rosh's name: even if it fell into fire and we saw it contracted — kosher; behold he explicitly excluded bird from animal regarding yellowing; and all the more so without falling into fire; so it appears clear in Tur's view; and therefore he wrote unspecified in Rashba's name: if we saw it is green — treifah, and Rosh disagrees and permits, and did not distinguish between green like grass and gogel, because for Rashba even grass is treifah, as I wrote above, and for Rosh even gogel is kosher.
