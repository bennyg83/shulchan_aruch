The law of a bird that fell into fire and change of appearance in organs. In it are 7 seifim. A bird that fell into fire and its intestinal children contracted, and the heart, gizzard, and liver — whose way is to be red — turned yellow, or the intestines — whose way is to be green — reddened: treifah, even in any amount of them that changed. And even if it did not reach the cavity — since they changed from fire burns, ultimately they will perforate. And the liver — the change does not forbid in it unless it changed opposite the intestines, meaning at the thin head of it and facing inward; or if it turned yellow opposite the bile; or opposite any place of its life-source. But the lung — we are not concerned for it because its ribs protect it. {Rama: And a spleen that turned yellow in its support — even only something — treifah, because ultimately it will perforate (Beit Yosef, in Rashba's view).}
