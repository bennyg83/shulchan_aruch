(3) R' Shmuel Kluger was in doubt in Ashelei Ravravi on Pri Megadim at end of the book whether boiling water mentioned means specifically while still on the fire, but removed from the fire — not, etc.; and he left it requiring further study — see there; and for practical law it is reasonable: as long as it is yad soledes bo, and even if removed from the fire — also called boiling water, as Shulchan Aruch wrote below siman 105 seif 2 and in Orach Chayyim siman 318 seif 9; and it is also reasonable that even kli sheini and yad soledes bo is also called boiling water for this matter, as Pri Chadash wrote siman 68 ot 18 — see there.
