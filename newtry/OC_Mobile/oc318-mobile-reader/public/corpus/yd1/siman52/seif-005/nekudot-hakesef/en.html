(Taz s.k. 10) He wrote on Maharshal that his words are wondrous and explained a strange meaning; but I wondered on Maharshal, for the mishnah teaches thus: fell into fire and intestinal children contracted — if greens — disqualified; if reds — kosher; trampled in a wall or animal crushed it and it convulses — if it remained from evening to morning and was slaughtered — kosher; if so, it should teach "and intestinal children contracted" because of reds kosher and also because of greens, for they are certainly disqualified; but when not before us it is only doubt, and it did not teach trampled in a wall, only to inform that if it remained overnight and was slaughtered — kosher. And I looked in Maharshal chapter 1 siman 92 and saw he wrote: otherwise why not teach fell into fire alone, as the mishnah taught at beginning of the chapter drusah alone and was not trampled and flesh reddened; rather certainly it is not relevant to teach because as soon as trampled it is treifah from doubt lest flesh reddened until inspected to remove it from treifah doubt, unlike falling into fire, end of his words — meaning lion and wolf drushah of chapter beginning, not as he understood that Maharshal meant trampled in a wall; nevertheless from this too it is not difficult, for there it needs to teach lion drushah in large animal and wolf in small animal and other tramplers, and dispute of R' Yehuda, unlike here where it needs to teach intestinal children contracted for the reason I explained; examine well:
