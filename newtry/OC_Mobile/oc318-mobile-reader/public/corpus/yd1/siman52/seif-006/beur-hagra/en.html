And there are, etc. According to their version that there is no text except "it is known that intestinal children contracted"; and even in Rif and Rambam's version, "it is known" refers to "contracted," and this means: it is known that since it fell, etc.; and proof from Rav Ashi: therefore do not eat, etc.; and therefore it is not in an unspecified case — for otherwise why would he be more concerned than all eighteen treifot? Rather it must be in falling into fire; and if the mishnah were unspecified, Rav Ashi would not need to say "unspecified." Ran.
