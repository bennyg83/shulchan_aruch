And even though in seif 6 we declare treifah if a change is found even though it did not fall into fire — this case is different, since the change is not seen except through scalding; and so he distinguishes in Rashba's novellae daf 60a; and so it appears in Beit Yosef, that in one law he wrote that Rashba wondered at Rambam who declares treifah when it did not fall into fire, and in another law in seif 6 he wrote that Rashba brought proof for his words and they agreed with him; and in this the question in Sela Chadasha daf 171a on the Mechaber was settled — see there:
And it happened that they were scalded, etc. Nevertheless it is permitted to eat them roasted, and we are not concerned if they were scalded that perhaps they would change — Tur and Gemara and all poskim; and so Or Zarua beginning of general rule 54: even if it is known that it fell into fire, one need not scald them lest they change, for we do not establish a prohibition, end of his words; and it is simple.
