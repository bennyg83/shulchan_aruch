[See responsa Chatam Sofer siman 54, who wrote regarding bulls fed refuse of cooking, which is hot water and sometimes boiling, and many drank and died: if one should be concerned in an unspecified case for those bulls that intestinal children contracted from boiling water — this is certainly that intestinal children contracted; we say only specifically in fire; and what caused several animals to die from drinking boiling — if there were medicine for them they would live; and to add treifot — certainly. If before cooking we find change in inner organs that we declare treifah in them — any change we are concerned for in the meat that the doctor drags and we declare treifah in any case; but if the organs are fine or were not inspected at all, and after cooking we find change — one should not hang on contraction at all — see there:]
