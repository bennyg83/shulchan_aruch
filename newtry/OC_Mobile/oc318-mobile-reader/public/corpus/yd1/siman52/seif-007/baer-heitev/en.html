And Shach wrote regarding an animal: if it fell into fire and is not before us we do not establish prohibition, for Maharshal permits even in a bird in such a case; however Bach wrote that inspection is needed l'chatchila in an animal where it is before us. Or Zarua: we are not expert in inspection of falling into fire; and Shach wrote it requires further study why acharonim did not write this, and perhaps it is included in "fallen."
