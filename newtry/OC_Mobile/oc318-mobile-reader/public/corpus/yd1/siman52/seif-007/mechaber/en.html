Treifot of change of these organs disqualify only in a bird; but in an animal, change of these organs does not disqualify even if we know it fell into fire. {Rama: And there are those who forbid also in an animal when it is known that it fell into fire and its intestines changed; and so is the custom (Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniot in the name of several great ones, Rashba, and Or Zarua).}
