When it is known that it fell, etc. But when it is not known — kosher in an animal even not in a place of great loss:
And its intestines changed, etc. It appears that therefore the Rav wrote briefly; and some say also in an animal, because he came to inform us that specifically when they changed before us do we practice prohibition in an animal; but if it fell into fire and is not before us we do not establish prohibition; and so is the essence, for Maharshal in Piskei Halachot siman 95 permits in such a case even in a bird; and furthermore, there are poskim who hold that change of appearance disqualifies also in an animal, and nevertheless they hold that inspection is not needed in an animal where it fell into fire, as Ran said and as Tur wrote in the name of Ba'al HaItur; however Bach wrote that inspection is needed in an animal, and that is l'chatchila where it is before us:
And so is the custom. Pri To'ar wrote that this means not in a place of great loss. Or Zarua general rule 56 din 13: it implies we are not expert in inspection of falling into fire, and it requires further study why the acharonim did not write this from it, and perhaps it is included in "fallen." And Taz wrote that Rashba is also among those who forbid in this, and if the Mechaber had seen his words he certainly would not have been lenient against him, end of his words:
