Ba'er Heitev in the name of Shach: however in fatty issur absorbed in a vessel, etc. — and see in Magen Avraham siman 458 s.k. 37, who ruled explicitly that even oil entirely absorbed in a vessel does not forbid all of it; and see in responsum Noda B'Yehuda part 4 siman 44, who wrote that one should be lenient like Magen Avraham's view — we do not abandon what is clear to Magen Avraham because of Shach's doubt — see there.
