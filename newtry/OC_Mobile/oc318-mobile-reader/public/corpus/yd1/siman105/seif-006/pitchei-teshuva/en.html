A kli rishon in which yad soledes bo. See Shach s.k. 5 who disagrees with Maharshal, who is stringent even when the yad does not soledet bo; and see in responsum Bach siman 20, who ruled like Maharshal's view because the Yerushalmi implies accordingly — for if like Shach's explanation in the Yerushalmi's words it is difficult, for from the law it is so and not because of distance; and the author of Shach himself conceded to him — see there; and see in responsum Teshuvat Chen siman 6, who elaborated on this and also raised for the law to be stringent like Rashal without great loss; but when not standing at fire — one need not be stringent at all — see there.
