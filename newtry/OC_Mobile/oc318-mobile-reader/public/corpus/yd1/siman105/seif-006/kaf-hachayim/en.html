Forbidden food that fell with permitted food for twenty-four hours in cold is called pickled, etc. And if the pickling was in stages — such as forbidden food fell with permitted food for twelve hours and they removed the permitted food outside and after some hours returned and placed it into the forbidden food for one hour — whether it is considered pickled in such a case where there was an interruption in between or not, see above siman 70 section 242 where we brought the dispute in this and wrote there that the consensus of most poskim is to permit after the fact if it already happened, and even one who wants to be stringent nevertheless in great loss it is permitted, see there.
