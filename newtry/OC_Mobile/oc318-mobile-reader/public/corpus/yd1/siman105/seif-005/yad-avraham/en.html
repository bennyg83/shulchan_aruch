(there) It is forbidden to pour from a vessel that has kosher fat for a burning candle that has milk, etc.; and b'dieved one need not be concerned. And one must say this is specifically in such a case where the issur is because of the flame of the candle that rises upward and forbids the vessel — therefore b'dieved one need not be concerned, for there is not so much issur in a candle flame, for it weakens the issur greatly as Peri Chadash wrote siman 108 and brought it end of siman 92 s.k. 42; but pouring from a pot of heter into a pot of issur so close that the hand burns from the steam — the steam rises and is absorbed in the pot and forbids it, as is written above siman 92 seif 5 and siman 108 in hagahah; and likewise Peri Chadash siman 122 that steam is considered cooked like the body of the tavshil; and even b'dieved it is forbidden; and here the Acharonim did not explain well.
