(seif 5 in hagahah) And specifically forbidden milk, etc., that oiliness applies to it; but issur to which oiliness does not apply and is certainly lean — forbids only kedei netilah. And in my humble opinion kelipeh suffices; and clear proof for this from Gemara Pesachim (daf 75): touched the earthenware of the oven — peel its place; and see there Rashi and Tosafot and Ra'avad and Rambam chapter 8 of Laws of Korban Pesach; therefore even in roasting if the issur is completely lean — kelipeh suffices; and as Shulchan Aruch wrote siman 69 regarding salting that roasting and salting are equivalent in this; and practical difference for us in d'rabbanan issur and issur completely lean that kelipeh suffices — for on this we establish the law of Gemara; and so I found in Maharshal and Taz and Erech HaTaanugim siman 65 s.k. 15, see there — and this is clear. And regarding milk whether it is considered fatty issur — there is room to derive from that which we say end of Kesuvos: "flowing with milk and honey" — fats from milk and sweets from honey.
