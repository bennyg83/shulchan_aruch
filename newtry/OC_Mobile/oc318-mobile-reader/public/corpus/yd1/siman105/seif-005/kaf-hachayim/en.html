(5) Also for one who holds there is pickling for vessels and the vessel is forbidden — and if it is a metal vessel it requires hagalah; nevertheless if it is an earthenware vessel one does not need breaking since although an earthenware vessel never leaves its absorption, this is where it absorbed through heat; but if it absorbed through pickling which is in cold, filling with water three times for twenty-four hours or hagalah three times suffices. Chakal Yisrael there siman 87. And likewise Arukh HaShulchan later siman 135 section 14 in the name of several poskim that an earthenware vessel in other prohibitions that absorbed through cold — filling and pouring help, and not like Shach there s.k. 33 who wrote that filling and pouring do not help except for wine or regarding chametz on Pesach and not for other prohibitions unless it is not absorbed from the beginning, see there. Nevertheless initially if possible one should be concerned for Shach's words and with him one should not do pouring for an earthenware vessel in other prohibitions except after twenty-four hours.
