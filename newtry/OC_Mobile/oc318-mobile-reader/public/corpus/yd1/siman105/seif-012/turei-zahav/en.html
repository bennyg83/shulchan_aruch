And likewise if it was salted, etc. In Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of Rashba, that it is permitted to salt meat in a vessel into which gentiles put wine for preservation, and he gave many reasons: one, that this does not emit issur; and further, that it did not absorb except something entangled like oil or brine, etc. — end of his words; and in siman 70 seif 2 I wrote that Rashal disagrees and it appears to him that salting does not cause a vessel to emit at all, even d'oraisa issur absorbed in a vessel.
