Forbidden. As Beit Yosef in name of Rashba responsum — permitted to salt meat in a vessel into which gentiles insert wine for preservation, because this does not expel issur; and also it did not absorb except something tangled like oil and brine, etc.; and in siman 70 seif 2 I wrote in name of Maharshal who holds salting does not cause a vessel to expel at all even d'oraisa issur absorbed in the vessel — end of Taz.
