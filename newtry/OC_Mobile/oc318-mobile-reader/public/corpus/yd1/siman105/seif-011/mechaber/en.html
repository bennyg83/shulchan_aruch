That which we said regarding salted issur and plain heter — that if it is fatty it forbids until there is sixty to nullify it — specifically when the salted issur was below and the plain heter above, because the lower prevails; but if the plain heter was below and the salted issur above, it forbids only kedei kelipah: {Rama: And some disagree and say in salting there is no distinction whether above or below (Tosafot and Rosh and Semak and Tur and Hagahot Rambam) — and such is practiced:}
