And some disagree and say in salting there is no distinction. Rather whether above or below — forever the salted imparts to brine and does not absorb from it; and the reason the poskim wrote: specifically in fire's boiling there is a distinction because through fire the overpowering one boils its fellow or cools it — for such is the way of fire's products; but through salting there is no power in the brine to make the salted brine, for salted is not like boiling until one salts on both sides and salts well well; and likewise brine has no power to make salted brine — as is said and one rinses well well; therefore with fire even if prohibition is cold the permitted is heated — unlike here; and even if prohibition is above, the lower has no power to make it brine — end of his words:
