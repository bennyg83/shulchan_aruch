Requires netilat makom. See in responsum Shemen Rokeach part 2 siman 15, who raised that if one cooked it thus without netilat makom and there is not sixty against the netilah — it is permitted (on this see siman 91 in Ba'er Heitev s.k. 1); however specifically in something that appears fatty; but in something that appears lean, netilah by law is forbidden without sixty against the netilah — see there; and likewise wrote Peri Megadim in Meshivtzot s.k. 11.
