And some disagree, etc. From the Gemara's distinction here between impure salted or pure brine and it does not distinguish between upper or lower; and in roasting the Gemara distinguishes between upper and lower and not between hot prohibition or permitted — the reason: granted with fire that which is hot overpowers by boiling its fellow or cooling it; but in salting the salting has no power to make the brine salted, for salted is not like boiling until one salts on both sides and salts well well; and likewise the brine has no power to make the salted brine — as is said and one rinses well well; therefore with fire even if the prohibition is cold the permitted is heated by them — unlike here; and even if the prohibition is above the lower has no power to make it brine.
