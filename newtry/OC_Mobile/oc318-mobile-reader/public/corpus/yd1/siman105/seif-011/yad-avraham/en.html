Shach wrote: even according to Taz — only when both are salted; but if issur salted and heter bland — it has no power to season the issur — end; and what shall I do, for it appears to me without examining Tur and Beit Yosef he wrote so, for Tur explicitly wrote and I brought his language shortly before this that even issur salted and heter bland and it is fatty — spreads and forbids all; and likewise Beit Yosef explicitly in s.v. Rashba wrote: specifically when the salted issur is below, etc., and these are his words: and greater than this Tur wrote that even lean issur if the heter is fatty — wherever issur salted and pure bland — forbids all — end, see there; and likewise Shulchan Aruch seif 11; and likewise Beit Yosef explicitly siman 91; and likewise in Sefer Peri Megadim he left Shach's words in requires-study in Orach Chayim, see there; and likewise Mateh Yehonatan general rule 23 s.k. 8 sensed what is written; and in this it appears to me to reconcile what is astonishing on the Mechaber Shulchan Aruch seif 10 who wrote, and these are his words: in what case when issur and heter are both salted, and even issur salted and pure bland — and it is very astonishing, for it is proven in Gemara and Rashi chapter HaBayit (daf 113) and Rif and Rosh and Ran and Tur above seif 9 that all the more if the impure is salted and the pure bland — forbidden; and according to what I wrote it comes out well: the Mechaber Shulchan Aruch in seif 10 refers to prior seif 9 that deals when one of them is fatty that forbids all — more reasonable to forbid when both salted than issur salted and heter bland and it is fatty; and therefore he wrote that even so all is forbidden — and understand. But Tur's words cannot be reconciled thus, for there it deals with kelipeh issur not yet dealing with one of them fatty; and he wrote in Shulchan Aruch language and even issur salted, etc. — contradicts what is written in seif 9 in reverse: and all the more if impure salted, etc. — great requires study.
