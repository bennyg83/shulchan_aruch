The law of an issur that fell into heter. In it are 14 seifim: An issur that stood with heter from time to time in cold is called kavush, and behold it is as cooked and all of it is forbidden; but less than this, rinsing suffices: {Rama: And wherever we say kavush as cooked, even what is outside the pickling is forbidden, for through the pickling below it exudes upward as in cooking; and there are those who are lenient regarding what is outside (Arukh general rule 40); and doubt of kavush is forbidden except with meat and milk, for we follow leniency, for from the Torah it is not forbidden except through actual cooking (there and Maharshal):} And if it is kavushed in brine or in vinegar (a lone opinion), if it stood long enough that one would place it on the fire and it would boil and begin to cook, behold it is as cooked; and in less than this measure it is not forbidden except the measure of klipah {Rama: (and see above siman 70 regarding the law of meat that fell into brine):}
