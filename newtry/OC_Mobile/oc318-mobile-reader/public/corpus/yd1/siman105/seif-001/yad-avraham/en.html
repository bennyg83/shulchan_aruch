(siman 105 seif 1 in hagahah) And doubt of pickling is forbidden. See what is written in Darkei Moshe in siman 104 and end of siman 87 and 95 and Taz's words here in requires-study, for here he disagrees with the hagahah and holds that doubt of pickling is permitted in all issurim; whereas above siman 104 regarding a mouse he agrees to forbid; and likewise in siman 83 s.k. 6 regarding fish — and Mateh Yehonatan brought it general rule 20 s.k. 14 — requires study; however in d'rabbanan issur all agree doubt of pickling is permitted; and with this there is no difficulty from what is written in Erech HaTaanugim in Orach Chayim siman 427 s.k. 34; and the essence of the law is as the hagahah; and doubt of pickling means: we see it was pickled only we do not know if it was pickled for the required measure as is proven siman 4 above; but if the doubt is whether it was pickled at all or not — permitted, for it is double doubt — perhaps it was not pickled; and if you say it was pickled — perhaps it was not pickled for the required measure as is proven siman 4 above, see there; and one should not challenge this from what Tosafot wrote chapter Kol HaBasar (daf 116) and Avodah Zarah (daf 34) regarding gentile cheese because they curdle them in hide of nevelah stomach — they specifically mentioned hide of nevelah stomach because there is d'oraisa issur; but because of ben yomo they would not forbid them from doubt, for cold in cold is d'oraisa not forbidden except through cooking; and likewise in Shiltai HaGiborim in Avodah Zarah there; and it appears even because they curdle in hide of nevelah stomach they should not forbid from doubt, for it is double doubt — doubt whether they curdled at all, for perhaps they curdled in a permitted thing; and if you say it was pickled — perhaps it was not pickled for pickling measure and perhaps the milk hardened within me'al le'al as is written end of siman 87 that one may say it is different there because they were accustomed to curdle in hide of nevelah stomach and it is a common minority as Tosafot wrote there; but heter where there is doubt whether it fell at all with issur — we do not establish issur; rather we say: here it is found and here it was; and this is certainly comparable to that mikveh Taz brought where there is no defect at all; and granted when we see it was pickled there is a defect — and one should not compare to mikveh as Peri Chadash wrote; and in Sefer Etz Yosef he wrote presumption of heter helps only when a defect arose from itself and not from mixture and another matter; but when we do not see a defect — certainly permitted. And regarding pickled blood see Peri Megadim whether it is like cooked and forbidden d'rabbanan; and see in Or Zarua general rule 6 siman 8 who wrote it cannot be d'rabbanan after it mixed in water and is not fit for offering.
(there) And if it is pickled within brine, etc. If it stood long enough that they place it on fire and it boils — so is in Hagahot Ashiri chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah; but above in siman 69 seif 18 Taz wrote the measure that they place water on fire and it begins to boil — and so appears to me primary; and that is a measure more than those boilings of brine that happen quickly; even through irui, all the more brine cooks as stated in Shabbat (daf 42); and see Pesachim (daf 40 side 2) and hagahah above siman 69 seif 9; and from this one may learn what is stated in Gemara Avodah Zarah (daf 32) brine burns fire — how much more so; therefore we compare the power of brine to the power of fire; and at the measure when water boiled on fire, brine also boils and expels and absorbs at such measure; and see Rashba and Beit Yosef and Tur siman 135.
(there in hagahah) And if already placed in kli sheni it does not forbid at all. He deduced to say "already placed" — for if not, that it was not yet at all in kli sheni but only the stream was interrupted — kelipeh is required as is written end of siman 92 and 93 regarding covering. And see what is written in Darkei Moshe in siman 92.
(there) It is forbidden to pour from a vessel that has kosher fat for a burning candle that has milk, etc.; and b'dieved one need not be concerned. And one must say this is specifically in such a case where the issur is because of the flame of the candle that rises upward and forbids the vessel — therefore b'dieved one need not be concerned, for there is not so much issur in a candle flame, for it weakens the issur greatly as Peri Chadash wrote siman 108 and brought it end of siman 92 s.k. 42; but pouring from a pot of heter into a pot of issur so close that the hand burns from the steam — the steam rises and is absorbed in the pot and forbids it, as is written above siman 92 seif 5 and siman 108 in hagahah; and likewise Peri Chadash siman 122 that steam is considered cooked like the body of the tavshil; and even b'dieved it is forbidden; and here the Acharonim did not explain well.
(seif 7) If there is in it issur only what it absorbed, etc. — it does not forbid another. Pri Megadim challenged from Orach Chayim end of siman 467 and reconciled: if the issur is above we are concerned perhaps it dripped — meaning from the wheat itself; but in absorbed issur where there is no issur apparent in any case it does not forbid — and not as Mateh Yehonatan wrote general rule 28 there: wherever the issur piece is above we are concerned perhaps it dripped and require scraping — for certainly absorbed issur never goes out at all.
(there) If it is known it only touched one of them and it is not known which — all are permitted, for one in two is nullified even though the meat absorbed milk. It appears the Mechaber holds: regarding species and its species we follow majority; and I already wrote siman 98 that milk stuck to meat is called fatty meat — and it is its species with the meat; and therefore nullified in majority dry in dry as I wrote siman 109; however Shach's and Peri Chadash's view: wherever the issur is nosen taam it is not nullified in majority, for if they cook it will impart taste; and therefore likewise it is not nullified in majority except we need sixty against the milk; and see what is written on Shulchan Aruch in Chidushei chapter 3 of Menachos (daf 23) — and understand; however since it is only d'rabbanan — for from Torah it is nullified in majority dry in dry in what is not its kind as Shach wrote siman 109 s.k. 10 — we follow its doubt leniently as Turei Chadash wrote general rule 39 end of din 4; and if so there is room to be lenient where it is not certainly fatty, all the more in lean — even for us who require sixty only as a stringency; if mixed afterward one in two is nullified: and understand intent of Shulchan Aruch: it deals when it touched a fatty piece as is written at the beginning of his words: if the meat salted with it is fatty, etc., see there; if so it is species and its species — milk and fat — for if they cook them the milk will not impart taste and therefore nullified in majority; and it deals when all pieces are fatty and there is majority of fat against the milk; and Shulchan Aruch already wrote siman 98 seif 2: if mixed in its species and what is not its kind we say remove what is not its kind, etc., see there; and know the Mechaber Shulchan Aruch refers also to gid fat or lean milk that suffices with kelipeh; and if known it only touched one and it is not known which — one need not peel all of them from doubt that one in two is nullified as stated explicitly in Mordechai and Hagahot Ashiri Pesachim and as is written siman 101 in Darkei Moshe; and if it touched one and its place is not known — one must peel all of it as in Mordechai and Hagahot Ashiri and as is written siman 72.
