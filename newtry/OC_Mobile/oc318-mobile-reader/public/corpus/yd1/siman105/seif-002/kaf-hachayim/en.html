(2) And what is the case — in a wooden vessel or earthenware vessel one should forbid, but in a metal vessel one should not forbid if forbidden food fell for twenty-four hours. Ohr HaChaim there. However Minchat Yosef on Tosafot Chadashim there section 12 wrote that there is no distinction between wooden, earthenware, or metal vessels regarding pickling. And likewise Beit Levi section 1; but Peleti end of section 1 brought support for Ohr HaChaim's words and likewise Arukh HaShulchan there that the main view is as Ohr HaChaim's words. And in a glass vessel one who is lenient in great loss did not lose. Mishnat Zahav there end of the words "and if". And this is according to his view that he forbids pickling of vessels in every case; but according to what was written in the previous section that in great loss one may be lenient there is no difference, and all the more so in metal vessels and glass vessels where there is a dispute.
