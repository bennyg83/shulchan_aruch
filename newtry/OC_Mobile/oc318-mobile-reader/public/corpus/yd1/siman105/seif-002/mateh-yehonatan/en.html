(in Shulchan Aruch seif 4) For R' Eliezer, etc., it does not forbid except kedei netilah, etc. One may say: behold the Mechaber does not distinguish between roasting and cooking without gravy — it appears both are equal regarding exuding; and he does not hold that pot steam exudes; and he also holds milk is like gravy, as Shach wrote in several places; therefore it is difficult why the Mechaber wrote it forbids kedei netilah — for we say there in the Mishnah a drop of milk, etc., if there is noten taam, etc. — it appears there is no noten taam in it, permitted entirely and not forbidden kedei netilah; and necessarily it must be said it is like gravy that immediately exudes throughout the piece; therefore if there is not noten taam, the whole piece is permitted; and on this it is difficult how we say in Pesachim "it dripped from its gravy onto the vessel," etc., peel its place — why do we not say it immediately entered throughout the Pesach offering, for it is like gravy, and certainly there was sixty against it; and it must be said the Mechaber holds as Rambam wrote there — all issur is because gravy drips to the offering and it is roasted because of something else; but certainly the body of issur is truly nullified, and properly it discusses peeling its place:
