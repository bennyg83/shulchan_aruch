And some say also, etc. And as is said in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Shabbos and chapter 1 of Maaser halachah 6 until where R' Yehudah bar Pazi R' Simon in the name of Rabbah bar bar Chanah — until that the hand may be put in it and it scalds — all agree in kli sheni that it is permitted; what is the difference between kli rishon and kli sheni? R' Yehudah bar Bon said: here the hand rules and here the hand does not rule. R' Yonah said: here and here the hand does not rule — rather they made a distance for kli rishon {Rama: (see Orach Chayyim siman 318 section 9)} and did not make a distance for kli sheni. R' Mana said: this law is supported by the rice pot and the groats pot supports father — that pot of groats that you carry from place to place until it boils. Meaning a bowl of rice and of groats which is kli sheni and it boils and it supports R' Yonah that yad soledes bo in kli sheni and nevertheless it does not cook. What if one puts spices below and pours over them from above? R' Yonah said forbidden — pouring is like kli rishon; R' Yonah's leniency is from this: whether one cooked or poured boiling into it. R' Yosi said: there earthenware absorbs — spices are not cooked. Meaning: nevertheless pouring is like kli sheni and there the prohibition is because of absorption and earthenware absorbs even in kli sheni — otherwise for Shabbos whose prohibition is cooking, R' Yehudah bar Bon taught and the baraita taught also in a copper vessel — you may say copper absorbs. Meaning copper certainly does not absorb in kli sheni — rather we learn pouring is like kli rishon. What if one pours with boiling? R' Chanina son of R' Hillel said: dispute of R' Yonah and R' Yosi — meaning it also depends on the dispute above. R' Yitzchak bar Nafcha asked R' Mana: if one did so on Shabbos is he liable for cooking? If in kli rishon is he liable for cooking? Meaning in pouring. He said to him: as R' Zeira said — what is clear broth? Whatever the fire passes beneath it; and here what is clear food? Whatever the fire passes beneath it — meaning but in pouring, even though it is like kli sheni, it is only that it absorbs and emits as is said in Zevachim daf 95 side 2 absorbs without cooking, etc. From all this it implies kli sheni also does not absorb; and also proved from the Yerushalmi above that only kli rishon where yad soledes bo; and so they said in chapter 3 of Shabbos {Rama: (daf 40 side 2)} one water and one oil, etc., and Tosafot there and therefore he said that the hand, etc., and he said and forbids the whole thing as above from that a drop of milk, etc., and he said kli rishon — meaning even if not on the fire as is said in Shabbos there the pot {Rama: (daf 42)} the kettle he emptied, etc. {Rama: (daf 41)} and many places there; and even though in the Yerushalmi above they said whatever the fire passes beneath it — meaning in a vessel where the fire had passed beneath it {Rama: (not like Ramban end of Avodah Zarah who strained in chapter Yerushalmi)} and so in Chullin daf 104 side 2 lest it rise in the first pot:
