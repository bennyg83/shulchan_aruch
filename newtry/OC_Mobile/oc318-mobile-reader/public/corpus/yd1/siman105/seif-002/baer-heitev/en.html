To beware. Shach wrote: even if it is something whose way is to rinse it afterward — then it is permitted to place it on cold issur as above siman 91 — nevertheless here one should beware l'chatchila in this matter; and he wrote further: nevertheless one should be stringent and forbid in kli sheni, all the more in kli rishon if yad soledes bo in a place where there is not so much loss; and likewise in a food item one should peel where there is not so much loss; but in any case one should not forbid all of it in kli sheni; and also Taz's view is to be stringent in kli sheni whenever yad soledes bo unless in great loss and an important matter; and if so all the stringencies mentioned in siman 94 regarding ben yomo apply in kli sheni as well, and not as Rama wrote there that unless in great loss and important matter one may rely on Rama; however in this matter Taz disagrees, for from his words it implies he forbids all of it; and Shach wrote he forbids only kelipeh {Rama: (and see what is written in siman 94 in name of Maharshal regarding a thick thing such as a hot piece of meat whose law is truly like kli rishon, and what is written there on this)}; and Shach wrote further: even in irui where the stream was interrupted it absorbs — nevertheless for all kelipeh; and even though from Rama's words siman 68 seif 10 it appears if one poured from kli rishon onto a chicken to pluck and the stream was interrupted — one need not even kelipeh, for it does not expel and absorb as one; but nevertheless it absorbs {Rama: (and requires study, for there it does not imply so at all, see there; and also in Nekudot HaKesef itself he challenged from siman 92 seif 7 in hagahah and wrote there is room to distinguish, see there)}; and he wrote further: in kli rishon standing at the fire, even though there is no yad soledes bo, one should be stringent and say it absorbs kelipeh even though from the words of the poskim it does not appear so — end of his words.
