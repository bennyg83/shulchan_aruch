Only as much as a peel — see Peleti that Shach's view is even if boiling stopped; and I wrote that we require specifically that boiling did not stop, unless in a solid item or heat at the fire as on Pesach when it was roasted in fire and dripped from its gravy — then even if boiling did not stop it forbids as much as a peel.
All the more it is not forbidden; and Maharshal is stringent with a solid item, for it is like a kli rishon — and see Peleti, for Tosafot's view is not like mine, and we have only Rama's words that he does not distinguish.
We say ten — see Peleti, for Maharshal's view in a vessel the law of tattei does not apply, etc. — and see there with earthenware vessels: one should be stringent.
Forbidden to pour, etc. — it is not forbidden except when the upper is cold and the lower hot; but both hot — permitted — see Peleti.
