A hot issur fell into hot heter of kli rishon, or even a cold issur into hot heter — all is forbidden, for the lower overpowers the upper and warms it until it emits in the lower; and needless to say cold heter into hot issur — all is forbidden; but if the upper is hot and the lower cold, it does not forbid except kedei kelipah, even if the upper hot one is issur: {Rama: And all this applies only to heat of kli rishon, such as immediately when removed from the fire one places it with the heter; but if it is already placed in kli sheni and afterward places the heter next to it or on it, it does not forbid at all, for kli sheni does not forbid as explained (so it appears from Ran's language); and if he placed them next to each other — if both are hot from heat of kli rishon, all is forbidden; and if one is cold, the heter requires kelipah in the place it touched (Arukh general rule 29 din 2 and Hagahot ShaDa); an issur that he placed in a vessel of heter, or the reverse — we say likewise the law of the lower prevails as with two pieces (there general rule 36); and see above siman 94 if one cut meat with a dairy knife. It is forbidden to pour from a vessel that has kosher fat for a burning candle that has milk or forbidden fat (likewise there and Mordechai perek 25, and see responsum Maharil and Pesakim Maharai siman 103) and b'dieved one need not be concerned (Arukh):}
