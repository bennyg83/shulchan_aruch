Placed. Darkei Moshe wrote: specifically if already placed in kli sheni; but if one placed an issur item in a bowl of heter — the bowl requires kelipeh; and likewise if one placed a heter item in a bowl of issur — the heter requires kelipeh, for it is like irui.
First. Shach wrote: Maharshal's view is that in a thick item such as a piece of meat or fish or rice or millet that one pours hot from the pot — if yad soledes bo its law is always like kli rishon; and his view also tends to agree with Maharshal, unlike Rama who wrote in siman 94 when cutting meat in a bowl with a milky knife we do not forbid, etc.; and see from this in siman 107; however certainly in rice and millet if it is clear and poured like gravy it appears one may rely on Rama; and perhaps Maharshal said only regarding rice and millet that are not clear — end of his words.
Kelipeh. Taz wrote: likewise regarding meat and milk it is so that cold requires kelipeh; likewise Or Zarua; and Maharshal wrote: it appears to me if the issur is hot and the heter cold and placed next to each other — it forbids in all of it like in salting; and if the heter is hot and the issur cold and placed next to each other — kelipeh is required; and it appears where there is not great loss one should be stringent thus, for even in salting Rama ruled so in this siman seif 66; and Shach wrote: meat and cheese that touched each other and one of them is cold — both require kelipeh; and if the issur is hot, even if both are hot — permitted also with kelipeh — end of his words.
Prevails. Shach wrote: Maharshal disagrees on this and his words are not plausible; rather the essence is as Rama's view — see there.
To pour. Because the vapor rises from the candle upward — thus the reason is proven from Mordechai and Or Zarua; Darkei Moshe wrote the reason: the lower prevails and connects in a stream; and in Darkei Moshe: pouring forbidden hot fat into permitted cold fat forbids, for through the heat all of it melts; and he proved this from the law of a mouse that fell into fat above siman 104, that it is forbidden even if the mouse is found at the bottom of the vessel — end of Taz.
