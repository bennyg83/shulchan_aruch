And if one is cold, etc. — the same law applies in basar b'chalav — thus it is written in Orach Chayyim general rule 29; and Rashal wrote in the chapter Kol HaBasar siman 74: it appears to me, if the issur is hot and the permitted cold and they rest one beside the other — it forbids all of it like salting; and if the permitted is hot and the issur cold and they rest one beside the other — a peel is required; and it appears that where there is not great loss one should be stringent thus, for even in salting Rama ruled thus in this siman seif 10.
Forbidden to pour from a vessel, etc. — this is the reason in Orach Chayyim, that tattei gavar, and he connects it in a stream; and Darchei Moshe wrote that one who pours fatty issur hot into fatty permitted cold forbids, because from the heat all of it melts; and he proved this from the law of a mouse that fell into fatty substance mentioned above siman 104, that it is forbidden even if the mouse is found at the bottom of the vessel.
