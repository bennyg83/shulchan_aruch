(4) Also in a cooked thing there is the law of pickled, and not like Rabbi Pri Tzadik siman 19. Arukh HaShulchan section 2; and this is such as if cooked forbidden food fell into a moist dish such as one that has gravy and it fell for twenty-four hours — the dish is forbidden because pickled is like cooked. And see later siman 106 section 15.
