[ Aside from meat with milk. Peri Chadash wrote: however to cook them is forbidden, for against cooking it is a d'oraisa doubt — see there; and likewise explained in Peri Megadim above siman 64 in Meshivtzot end s.k. 1 and in the introduction to the laws of Bach, and below siman 111 in Sha'ar HaDatz s.k. 3; and likewise explained from what I wrote above siman 87 s.k. 3 — see there. However in responsum Minchat Ani siman 35 he agreed to the ruling of the rabbi who ruled there to permit l'chatchila to cook in doubtful kavush, where the doubt is whether it was kavush from me'eit l'eit — and that is according to Taz's words, who permitted also in other issurim because of initial presumption, and also Peri Chadash and Nekudot HaKesef did not challenge him on the essence of the law, only on the proof he brought from the mikveh, and he elaborated on this and concludes that also Chavot Da'at who forbids in cooking means in certain kavush and the doubt is in two pieces, where it is not applicable to say establish on presumption; but in one piece and doubt of me'eit l'eit — permitted even in cooking a positive commandment — requires further study. ]
