And if it was cooked, etc. — that all of it became nevelah, for it is dabuk issur, and the membrane does not interrupt so there should not be dabuk issur; and one need not wonder, since we too require sixty in salting — if so, why mention cooked — behold from the time of salting it was forbidden; and if it was roasted without salting — behold it is forbidden because of blood, for we estimate against all of it; and it is possible there is a doubt whether it was cooked without salting because of blood — it is a d'rabbanan doubt; but because of milk — a d'oraisa doubt, for even though dabuk issur is d'rabbanan, nevertheless the substance of the issur is d'oraisa — requires further study.
