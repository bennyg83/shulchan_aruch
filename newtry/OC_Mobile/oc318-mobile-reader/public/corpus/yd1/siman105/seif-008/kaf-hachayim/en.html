(8) There hagahah. And wherever we say pickled is like cooked — even what is outside the pickling is forbidden, etc. And there are those who are lenient regarding what is outside. And likewise in Tosafot Chadashim general rule 22 law 6. Taz s.k. 2. Shach s.k. 1. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahat Turei Zahav section 4, Peri Chadash section 2, Peri Toar section 2, Lechem Pri Chadash section 2, Beit Levi section 2, Chokhmat Adam section 4, Shulchan Petach David section 1, Chokhmat Adam there. And even in forbidden oil — in salting what is outside the brine is permitted in pickling. Kenesset HaGedolah there. Minchat Yosef on Tosafot Chadashim there or Taz. Beit Levi there. But Shach there wrote that this is when the forbidden food is lean — but if the forbidden food is oil even what is outside the brine is forbidden. And likewise Peri Chadash there that if the forbidden piece is oil even what is outside the brine is forbidden for all — because the fat spreads. Kereti section 2, Chokhmat Adam there. Beit Yosef there section 4. However Arukh HaShulchan end of section 14 wrote that from Rama's wording it does not appear so but certainly in pickling for twenty-four hours also in oil it does not spread upward. And Shulchan Petach David there wrote that when there is no great loss one should forbid in oil like Shach in pickling for twenty-four hours. And such is the practical law since there is a dispute one should not be lenient except in great loss. And this is if there is not sixty in the pickling against the forbidden food — the permitted food that is outside is permitted in great loss; but if there is sixty in the permitted food inside the pickling against the forbidden food, otherwise everything is permitted even what is inside the pickling without great loss and all the more so what is outside.
