(seif 7) If there is in it issur only what it absorbed, etc. — it does not forbid another. Pri Megadim challenged from Orach Chayim end of siman 467 and reconciled: if the issur is above we are concerned perhaps it dripped — meaning from the wheat itself; but in absorbed issur where there is no issur apparent in any case it does not forbid — and not as Mateh Yehonatan wrote general rule 28 there: wherever the issur piece is above we are concerned perhaps it dripped and require scraping — for certainly absorbed issur never goes out at all.
