(seif 8 in hagahah) It was cooked thus the kidney, etc., and requires sixty against, etc. Mateh Yehonatan left in requires-study general rule 37 s.k. 8: why particularly if it was cooked — even in roasting and salting we also say the piece becomes nevelah; and he did not be lenient there general rule 38 section 2 except in great loss and specifically in salting because the multitude is lenient in salting even in fat that forbids only kelipeh — unlike roasting; and he wrote: possibly kidney is different since in any case many poskim hold the membrane interrupts — we are not stringent for chanan; and if so the Rav should not have permitted like roasting without great loss, for roasting of kidney is not better than salting in general; and because he sensed weakness in this his mind was not settled and he left requires-study; but this caused him to omit Ran's words here who wrote end of chapter Kol HaBasar: since chanan in other issurim is only because of taste in kli rishon — therefore we do not say it except in things that cook specifically — similar to kli rishon; and Shach brought it siman 22 and in his long book; and was aided from this the Rav ruled in Turei Chadash we do not say chanan in salting; and if so likewise in roasting — therefore he only permitted in salting because the multitude is lenient, etc.; and he did not see Shach's words there who wrote the Rav's intent is to say salting is lighter than cooking, for most poskim hold in salting it does not forbid in lean more than kelipeh; and if so one may say likewise it is not chanan; and he compelled that such was his intent, for in one law he wrote the multitude is stringent in salting with sixty, etc., as he explained; and if so also in roasting from this reason one may say it is not chanan — lighter than cooking for most people in lean; and if so one may say likewise it is not chanan even in fat. End matter: in my humble opinion according to Ran and Shach mentioned — I do not see room to distinguish between roasting and salting for chanan; and even though the Rav permitted in Turei Chadash only in great loss — in this one may say kidney is different, etc.; and actual roasting is also included in cooking as Acharonim wrote — truth that it is also included in "it was cooked thus the kidney" that he stated.
