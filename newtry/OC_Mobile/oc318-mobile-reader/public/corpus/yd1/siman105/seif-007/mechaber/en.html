That which a piece of issur forbids its fellow through contact — specifically when its issur is from itself, such as nevelah or basar b'chalav; but if it has no issur except what it absorbed from elsewhere, it does not forbid another touching it, even if they were roasted together. In what matters — when it absorbed issur that does not exude; but if it absorbed from things that exude by nature, such as fat — it forbids its fellow, whether in roasting or in touching one another hot with hot, or lower hot and upper cold, for the issur absorbed in it exudes and goes from piece to piece {Rama: (Rashba's novellae perek 25 daf 107a and Maharai in name of Or Zarua and Maharam): Rama: And all this is with two pieces; but a vessel that absorbed issur forbids heter touching it, even in issur that is not fatty (Hagahot ShaDa in name of R' Yosef of Ivra and Or Zarua general rule 36):}
