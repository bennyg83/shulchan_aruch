In milk. Taz wrote: Maharshal challenged, for we hold absorbed issur does not go without gravy even in kli rishon; and proof from a drop of milk that fell on meat in siman 92 — when that piece was forbidden without shaking and covering it does not forbid other pieces that touched hot one another if they did not separate from what is written here in kli rishon — meaning hot meat that touched cheese is considered issur forbidden from itself — end. And Shach wrote: this explanation is not the essence; rather it appears the Rav holds: even though we hold hot in hot without gravy forbids all of it — that is because we are not expert between lean and fatty; nevertheless what is not the body of the issur but only through absorption — we were not so stringent; and they established it on the law that it does not forbid when it does not spread; and milk issur that is lean is called and does not spread. And he wrote further he found in Or Zarua Maharshal printed in Lublin on page 33, he wrote there hagahah from Maharshal who brought on the contrary proof from Pesachim regarding kli rishon that forbids a piece to its fellow without gravy; and wrote there that so ruled his teacher and master the Gaon — see there — end of his words.
Together. And even kelipeh is not needed — Taz; and Shach wrote: even the piece in which the issur fat was absorbed — the fat and oil in it spreads, for the oil in it does not conduct the issur with it but only to the place where the issur itself can naturally go; and this that above seif 65 — even the issur piece lean and the heter piece roasted with it fatty — the issur fat spreads in all of it — is not difficult, for where the issur is forbidden from itself, since in any case it expels issur gravy and in any case forbids kedei netilah — therefore if the heter is fatty next to it it holds its expulsion and absorbs in all of it; unlike here where the piece is not forbidden from itself but swallowed issur. And Or Zarua wrote: salting is equivalent to roasting in this; and proven in Turei Chadash that blood is not considered spreading issur for this purpose; and although we hold in all places also regarding blood to estimate with sixty because we do not distinguish between lean and fatty as the Rav wrote in siman 69 — nevertheless where the piece absorbed blood it does not forbid other pieces through salting and roasting; but meat that was not yet salted and has blood of itself — it is simple in Gemara and all poskim that it forbids in salting and roasting — end of his words.
The things. Taz wrote: it implies milk does not spread; and above siman 87 seif 10 it is proven we say milk is called issur that spreads; and Shach wrote: in the bottom hot, etc. — it implies that if the bottom is cold, even kelipeh is not needed; and even though we hold hot into cold one peels — that is because it is impossible it did not absorb a little before it cooled; and that is specifically from the body of the thing next to it — it absorbs immediately a little from it before it cools since it touches it; but it does not go out from piece to piece before it cools.
Vessel. The reason is in Taz: specifically regarding food that has something to expel from its body — then absorption does not go without gravy; but a vessel that has no expulsion from its body — then what it absorbed goes out properly; and therefore it is simple a vessel is not forbidden from food that absorbed in it issur that does not spread such as liver or meat salted in what is not ben yomo, for this does not spread; and Shach wrote: and it appears specifically food of heter; but a vessel does not forbid a vessel without gravy as the Rav wrote end of siman 92 and 93; and Maharshal wrote: however a tavshil such as rice or fish in which meat or milk was absorbed — if one inserts a spoon of meat or milk — ben yomo — it properly forbids the absorption in the food and the food is considered as if all meat or milk and is forbidden with the spoon; and what Rama wrote that a vessel forbids heter, etc. — it is simple it forbids only kelipeh without gravy; however regarding oily issur absorbed in a vessel — possibly even when completely dry it forbids with sixty; and even though we do not distinguish between lean and fatty — that is regarding the body of the thing; but what became forbidden through absorption — not; and nevertheless requires study for the law.
