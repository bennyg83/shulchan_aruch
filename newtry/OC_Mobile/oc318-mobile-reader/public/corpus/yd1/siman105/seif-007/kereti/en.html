Basar b'chalav — and see Peleti: if the explanation is meat that was roasted with cheese; but meat that absorbed and was roasted with other things — there is no issur from itself and it does not forbid; or one might say basar b'chalav — since this alone would be permitted, it is issur from itself — and see Peleti that the main thing is like the first explanation — see there at length.
That touched — and in this we are expert to distinguish between lean and fatty; and blood is a lean issur — siman 87.
In the lower hot; but if it cooled, even a peel is not required, for adameikar does not apply, etc., since it is only absorbed issur — Shach; and Peri Chadash disagrees.
Even in an issur that is not fatty — and if there is fatty substance in it, whether it forbids only as much as a peel or spreads more — Shach was uncertain — requires further study.
