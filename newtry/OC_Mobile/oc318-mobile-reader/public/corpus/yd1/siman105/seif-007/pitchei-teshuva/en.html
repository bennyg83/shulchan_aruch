See in the book Bechor Shor in his novellae on Chullin daf 103, who wrote that since we are not expert in the measure of an infant's gullet being burned, therefore in any case in a d'oraisa matter it is fitting to be stringent with a kli rishon until the doubt leaves his heart that there is not the heat mentioned; and even in d'rabbanan one needs investigation on this — and not like the world's custom to estimate at a time when one cannot hold the hand because of heat — it is called yad soledes bo only when it exists — see there.
