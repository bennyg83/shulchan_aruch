(seif 9) Salted, etc. — its law is like boiling and expels to forbid kelipeh; and if it is gid fat, etc.; and if it is actual milk — requires sixty to nullify it; and if the meat salted with it is fatty — even in gid fat, etc. The language is not precise, for even in actual milk there must be fatty meat — then the milk is presumably fatty, or the piece that was salted in its milk is fatty; for if not — it is actual lean milk and does not spread like gid fat; and so is stated explicitly in Tur, and these are his words: if the piece is fatty and was salted in its milk, or issur salted and heter bland and it is fatty — it spreads and forbids all — end; and see what is written in Darkei Moshe in the discourse adjacent after this.
