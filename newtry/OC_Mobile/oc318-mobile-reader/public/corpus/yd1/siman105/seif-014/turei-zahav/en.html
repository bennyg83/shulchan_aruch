Even though the salt is noten ta'am li'fegam. See what I wrote in siman 98 seif 4 on the law of acting according to its taste, and what Tur wrote here at the end of the siman: one must estimate as if all the blood were salted — meaning that sixty does not help as long as the taste is sensed; and on this they said that even in a thousand it is not nullified, because of nosen ta'am — meaning any amount that gives taste, as opposed to initial intent; and likewise Rama wrote at the end of siman 98.
