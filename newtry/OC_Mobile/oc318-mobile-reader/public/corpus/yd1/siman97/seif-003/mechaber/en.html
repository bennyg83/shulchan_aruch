Bread that one baked with roasted meat and fish that were roasted in one oven with meat — forbidden to eat them with milk; and this is in a small oven; but in a large oven that holds twelve eifahs and its mouth is open — permitted; and if the roast is covered, and likewise pashkida whose opening is covered — permitted even in a narrow oven (and see below siman 108 how they practice).
