The reason wiping does not suffice is because the oven cannot be cleaned well and the tallow remains visible until one heats it so much — in Tosafot and likewise Ran in chapter 21 and in chapter 25; and one should not challenge from what I wrote earlier that the oven floor is like absorption in a pot, for here it appears there is visible prohibition, for here it is different because the tallow itself smears from it into the oven and that moisture is not cleaned well in the oven, unlike paladin above where there is no visible fat at all in the oven but only absorption from paladin; and this reasoning is written explicitly in Hagahot Ashiri chapter Kol HaBasar in the hagahah beginning "a lamb or chicken," etc.: "but regarding pashkida we see it does not emit at all but its place is dry," end of his words; and from what Rama wrote in seif 1 "lest it drip beneath it," which implies visible prohibition beneath paladin — there is no difficulty, for certainly we do not contradict visual sense that we see its place is dry; and furthermore both these laws are from Hagahot Ashiri in the name of Or Zarua; but plainly, ordinary paladin does not drip beneath it as we always see, only sometimes it occurred; and likewise Hagahot Ashiri that Beit Yosef copies here: "it is good to be careful lest perhaps it dripped," etc. — implies it is not certain that it dripped.
