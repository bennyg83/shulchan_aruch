One may not knead; and a mortar in which they grind spices for meat and milk — if meat was ground in it, forbidden to grind spices again before kashering, even to grind spices for meat — Taz; and Peri Chadash challenged that one may not add to the Sages' decree.
And if one kneaded, etc. — thus in the city and all Galician regions bakers bake all wheat bread with milk; when I came here to eat all bread, rolls, bread baked in tallow — woe, they breach it, for it is something the majority of the public cannot uphold; their permission in the Gemara deals with a happenstance case; but our city where all know and there is no baker who does not bake with milk and all bread is made thus — since it is known to many there is no longer error or concern, for all know it was made with milk; and even though Mahari"t in part 14 siman 18 rejected this reasoning, they rely on it without hearing protest. And because of milk that non-Jews milked and one does not see — one relies on Peri Chadash that where impure milk is expensive, from pure milk we are not concerned. In tallow, etc. — if the bread has taste and no tallow taste, see what I wrote in Peleti that one should be stringent; and if the bread has sixty against the tallow, permitted — so ruled the authorized Rav Mahari"t.
