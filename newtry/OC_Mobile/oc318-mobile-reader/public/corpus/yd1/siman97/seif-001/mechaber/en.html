Not to knead dough in milk. In it are 3 seifim: One may not knead dough in milk lest it come to be eaten with meat; and if one kneaded the entire loaf it is forbidden even to eat it alone; and if it was a small amount for eating at one time, or one changed the form of the bread so it is recognizable that one will not eat meat with it — permitted. Similarly, one does not bake bread in an oven whose surface was greased with tallow; and if one baked it, its law is like dough kneaded in milk: {Rama: Therefore the custom is to knead bread with milk on Shavuot and also with fowl fat in honor of Shabbat, for all this is considered a small amount; also because their form is different from other bread; and all the more so paladin or pashkida are permitted (Hagahot Sh"D). One may not bake any bread with paladin or pashkida in an oven, for we are concerned lest fat drip onto the bread; and if it dripped beneath it, its law is as if kneaded with it (Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Ashiri). The custom is to place a cover at the mouth of the oven (Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahat Sh"D). And even in a pan they are stringent l'chatchila.}
