(1) [Seif 1] One may not knead dough in milk, etc. — and likewise fowl fat and the like; and even though it is only d'rabbanan, nevertheless it is not a decree upon a decree, for it is close to error that man lives by bread and his way is to eat with meat and milk and spices. Shulchan Petach David section 1; Arukh HaShulchan section 3; Zivchei Tzedek section 1. And what if the tallow is a small amount absorbed in bread and not recognizable — forbidden to eat; but if the tallow is recognizable and visible on the bread surface there is no concern and it is permitted. Peri Chadash end section 3; Lechem HaPanim section 4; Beit Levi section 4; Shulchan Petach David there; Arukh HaShulchan there; Zivchei Tzedek section 10.
(2) A mortar in which they grind spices used with meat and milk, and once they ground garlic with goose-fat gravy — it appears that mortar is forbidden to grind spices again even for meat lest one eat with milk; it requires kashering specifically and even b'dieved forbidden without kashering. Taz s.k. 1; Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahat Turei Zahav section 2; Beit Levi section 1; Peri Toar section 1; Mishmeret Shalom section 1; Or HaYashar section 2. However Peri Chadash section 1 disagrees with Taz and wrote it is permitted to grind in that mortar spices again to eat with meat, and we are not concerned lest one grind for milk, for we do not decree decrees from our own reasoning — they were concerned only with bread because it is man's main sustenance and he is accustomed to eat with meat and milk, therefore they decreed; but this mortar one wants to designate for meat is permitted and we do not decree. And likewise Minchat Yehudah on Turei Chadash chapter 60 section 3; Lechem HaPanim section 1; Shulchan Gavoah section 3; Bris Kehunas Yisrael in Shavuot section 3; Chokhmat Adam general nun section 7; Arukh HaShulchan section 2.
(3) There. And if one kneaded the entire loaf — forbidden even to eat alone. A decree lest one eat it with meat. Shach s.k. 1. Chokhmat Adam section 3; Shulchan Petach David section 1; Arukh HaShulchan section 1.
