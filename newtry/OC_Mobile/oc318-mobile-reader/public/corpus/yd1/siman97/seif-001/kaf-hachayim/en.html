(1) [Seif 1] One may not knead dough in milk, etc. — and likewise fowl fat and the like; and even though it is only d'rabbanan, nevertheless it is not a decree upon a decree, for it is close to error that man lives by bread and his way is to eat with meat and milk and spices. Shulchan Petach David section 1; Arukh HaShulchan section 3; Zivchei Tzedek section 1. And what if the tallow is a small amount absorbed in bread and not recognizable — forbidden to eat; but if the tallow is recognizable and visible on the bread surface there is no concern and it is permitted. Peri Chadash end section 3; Lechem HaPanim section 4; Beit Levi section 4; Shulchan Petach David there; Arukh HaShulchan there; Zivchei Tzedek section 10.
