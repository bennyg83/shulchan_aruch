**Two who slaughter, etc.** Explanation: even though kosher one's intent alone suffices to slaughter and invalid one does not assist—nevertheless since he too slaughters at that time he forbids; therefore fine what I wrote siman 4 seif 4 for Ran who holds slaughtering friend's animal for idol worship makes neither issur nor heter—we could not permit two slaughtering since we do not need heter of slaughterer for idol worship.
**In what case when he had partnership, etc.** So Rambam chapter 2 Hilchos Shechitah after writing all invalid matters in shechitah whether idol worship or kodshim outside. Beit Yosef wondered on our master: why not mention this distinction here in kodshim invalidity—resolved he relied on what he wrote in invalidity for idol worship at beginning of siman where there is dispute; then challenged Ra'osh who holds with partnership also permitted—if so mishnah two holding knife and slaughtering, one for invalid matter one for kosher matter—shechitah invalid—refutation on Ra'osh—for above Gemara challenges from that mishnah one slaughtered for invalid idol worship—invalid, and answers there this invalid deals with mumar who certainly forbids; but here kodshim cannot say mumar—why invalid; and what Beit Yosef explained: they said person does not forbid what is not his only for issur from law like idol worship; but kodshim is marit ayin—for not his—what is it—viewer thinks it is his and comes to permit kodshim outside—therefore even without partnership forbidden per Ra'osh; but Rambam although he too forbids only marit ayin—nevertheless permits without partnership—for whoever slaughters another's animal for invalid matter has publicity; per this Rama ruled one may be stringent where forbidden marit ayin. Although I am not worthy to dispute them, I said their words are wondrous: one, we do not find anywhere issur marit ayin stricter than issur from substance itself; two, if new dispute born between Rambam and Ra'osh and Tur per Beit Yosef words Tur should have mentioned this dispute with Rambam; and what Beit Yosef challenged from Ra'osh and Tur appears tzarich iyun—for until here Tosafot and Ra'osh do not dispute Rambam and Rashi—they hold even partner does not forbid except idol worship—good reasoning Israelite does not worship idols but certainly intends to pain him; but slaughtering for kodshim certainly forbids with partnership—for he thinks he does mitzvah; if so Tur wrote well two holding knife and slaughtered one for forbidden matters—meaning each per its law: idol worship issur per Ra'osh with mumar and Rambam even Israelite with partnership; olah issur per all with partnership; but animal not his—cannot say doing mitzvah—for person does not consecrate what is not his; need not mention issur in partner's animal obvious what difference partnership or his alone; Rambam mentioned partnership distinction only for slaughtering idol worship where he divides partnership vs others; Tur already wrote; but invalid slaughter for kodshim certainly per all no issur in another's—for how could he consecrate another's animal—if one sees friend's animal in market and says behold it is hekdesh—can it enter mind animal forbidden marit ayin—certainly only because he thinks doing mitzvah and that is only when he has share in it as I clarified clearly for halachah; but not l'maaseh since Beit Yosef and Rama mouth forbid stringency—nevertheless if side to be lenient e.g. mixture—may combine heter we said per my view.
