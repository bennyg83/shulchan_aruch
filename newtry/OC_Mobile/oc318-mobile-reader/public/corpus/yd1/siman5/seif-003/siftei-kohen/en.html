**And likewise if they slaughtered one after the other, etc.** Appears here we require invalid one begin with matter making it nevelah—as above end siman 2; all the more if kosher one already slaughtered majority—he does not disqualify when other afterward slaughters for kodshim; so Maharshal; and so appears explicitly Beit Yosef s.v. umash shebem Rambam, etc.; Bach wrote even if one already made slaughter kosher and afterward another came and slaughtered for kodshim—forbidden because of marit ayin—and I do not know what marit ayin here—for behold slaughtered ox before you.
**In what case when he had partnership, etc.** This is Rambam's view Mechaber brought above siman 4 seif 4; and appears Mechaber's view: even per Ra'osh brought there that even with partnership not forbidden—here forbidden when partnership—for here he intends to pain him, whereas viewer thinks he intends mitzvah; but when no partnership—not forbidden, for whoever slaughters another's animal for disqualifying matter has publicity; so Peri Toar see there; and Beit Yosef wrote per Ra'osh even without partnership forbidden—wrote so per Tur who did not mention partnership distinction here; perhaps Tur holds so; but reasonable to rule here like Rambam for reason mentioned; think.
