Two who slaughter—whether both hold one knife or each has his knife in hand—and one of them intended a matter that disqualifies—this is invalid. And likewise if they slaughtered one after the other and one intended a disqualifying matter—it is invalid. In what case: when he had partnership in it; but if he has no partnership in it, it is not forbidden, for no Israelite forbids what is not his—he does not intend except to pain him. {Rama: some forbid in every case because of marit ayin (Beit Yosef in name of Ra'osh and Tur); and one may be stringent.}
