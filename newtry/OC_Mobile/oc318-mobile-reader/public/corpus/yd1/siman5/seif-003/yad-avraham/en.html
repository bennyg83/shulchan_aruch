**(Shulchan Aruch seif 3) Two who slaughter, etc., and one intended disqualifying matter—invalid; and if they slaughtered one after the other and one intended disqualifying matter—Rambam.** Shach wrote: appears we require invalid one begin with matter making nevelah—as above end siman 2—see there. Case not comparable to proof there regarding gentile slaughter—for there act of ape; here depends on thought for kodshim appearing like slaughter outside—even slaughtering only minority siman 1 appears like kodshim outside and they come to permit minority siman 1. We hold like R. Yochanan shechitah exists from beginning to end—as Rambam chapter 1 Hilchos Pesulei haMukdashim; if slaughtered minority simanim outside and finished inside—liable as Rambam there—and explained Shas chapter 2 Chullin (29); although minority simanim—even minority kaneh alone since shechitah from beginning to end—so Rashi chapter Merubah (72): shechitah exists, etc., and however much one considers counts as thought—end quote. Clear from “however much”—no distinction—even minority kaneh alone law is so; and afterward Teivat Shlomo and Peri Megadim explain so; and responsum Beit Yaakov siman 41; and Maharit on Tosafot Yoma (32b). That Rambam chapter 4 Hilchos Shegagot implies not liable for slaughter outside until slaughters all or majority—for where slaughter began for idol worship it was revealed retroactively cutting dust; but where finished in kosher manner certainly shechitah from beginning exists—as Rashi and Tosafot; think.
