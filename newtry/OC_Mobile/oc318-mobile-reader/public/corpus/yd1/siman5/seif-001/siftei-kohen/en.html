Even if it is baalat mum. — Sometimes a person covers the blemish and it is not apparent—Shas and Tur; and implies therefore even if not covered forbidden because lo pelug.
For todah. — And likewise nazir offering—even though he did not vow to be nazir—one may fear viewer will say he vowed in secret—Shas; and so Rambam chapter 2 and other poskim.
For temurah. — Specifically when he has no offering in his house; but if he has offering in his house and said for temurah of my offering that he substituted—invalid—Shas; and so Rambam there. Tosafot wrote: we are not concerned here perhaps he has offering in his house in secret, because he has publicity—he is accustomed to inform so they not come to me'ilah.
His shechitah is kosher. — For since it is not vowed and donated, it is known it is way of jest and he says so.
And if it became known he transgressed, etc. — Rashi explained: when not known—even if he said “for my chatat” kosher; Maharshal wrote: specifically when not before us in known matter; but if before us and says he is obligated chatat—admission of litigant like hundred witnesses—and invalid; Bach disagrees.
For my chatat, etc. — But if he said “this for chatat” without yud—kosher as Rashi and Rashba in Terumat HaDeshen—for implies he donates chatat now, and chatat does not come as donation; but “chatati” implies chatat he is already obligated; likewise if he said only “this is my chatat” without “for”—kosher as Bach and Maharshal chapter HaShochet; as proven in Tosafot chapter HaShochet s.v. veha lo amar, etc., and Piskei Tosafot there and Rambam printed Venice 5150 require specifically to designate for chatat—as Nedarim (beginning 13) “to Hashem what does he do with it—he needs to designate it for chatat and asham”; Rashi there: designates by saying “this for my chatat and my asham”—this appears Taz's view.
