Even if baalat mum—Gemara disputes R. Yannai and R. Yochanan; poskim ruled like R. Yochanan; wonder Beit Yosef why ruled like R. Yannai against his teacher R. Yochanan; appears we say Pesachim 53: R. Yannai said Rav: one who says “this meat for Pesach”—forbidden—for appears consecrating his animal, etc.; and does not distinguish whether animal unblemished or baalat mum; also challenges from gid hanasheh and does not answer “blemished”—even baalat mum—learn Rav holds forbidden in all cases; R. Yannai individual against Rav and R. Yochanan.
And if known, etc.—Maharshal's view: if before us and says obligated chatat—admission of litigant like hundred witnesses—forbidden; Bach disagrees—for sin was public and clear he lies; therefore Taz decides if he admits chatat done anew—then no time to publicize. Truly Taz's language if sin were public Bach's words justified; but from Torah we hold forbidden to publicize his sin; Rambam's view even famous sin—only Rashi wrote he did not conceal—and therefore would be heard—not that he publicized, only not concealed; and will not escape it was not publicized even without concealment; since he confessed we say it was not publicized. I ask: if they come at time of offering and he says he ate chelev and no witnesses contradict—is he not obligated to bring chatat even if not publicized at all; if so from Torah we permit animal; therefore we have only Maharshal's view—plain.
And some disqualify asham—Tur's view ruled stringently as one who donates asham talui daily. Understand day after Yom Kippur when we do not bring asham talui—as mishnah: wait until enters House of Doubt—if slaughtered Motza'ei Yom Kippur would be kosher; but this is not so—for we do not bring asham talui per all on 11 Tishrei; nevertheless could vow to offer tomorrow onward—donation matter—and marit ayin he slaughtered—like Pesach. However Orach Chayyim Tur ruled say “may it be will” on asham portion because donated—and did not write 11 Tishrei without saying perhaps holds halachah like Tanna Kamma Keritot who holds even 11 Tishrei he would donate lest at night he enter House of Doubt; but tzarich iyun—then he would not donate for cannot sacrifice then due to reduced eating; must hold like Ben Bava per R. Shimon; for us concern he separated on Yom Kippur to offer after Yom Kippur and then vow applies and even after atonement Yom Kippur he separated for after Yom Kippur. Saying asham portion on Yom Kippur—in any case on shelamim too cannot due to reduced eating.
