**Blemish.** Shach wrote: even if blemish in exposed place—sometimes covers blemish and not apparent.
**Todah.** Shach wrote: likewise nazir offering—even if did not vow nazir—viewer may say he vowed in secret.
**Invalid.** Taz wrote: although he did not say “for my olah”—for it is matter anyone may donate, unlike chatat.
**Kosher.** Reason: we say since not vowed and donated it is known certainly jest; Shach wrote: specifically when no offering in house—then kosher if said for temurah; but if offering in house and said for temurah of my offering that he substituted—invalid. Taz in name of Maharshal: here when not before us we establish presumption; but if before us and says explicitly obligated chatat—plainly forbidden; Bach disagrees. It appears distinction if he says he transgressed some time ago—certainly lying—for if true would publicize; but if possible not known until now or possibly now—truly spoke—end quote.
**Invalid.** Shach wrote: specifically if said “for my chatat” with yud; but if said chatat stam—kosher. Taz wrote: if says “for my chatat” and known he transgressed sin obligating chatat—forbidden in benefit like slaughter outside; so Maharshal ruled.
**And donation.** Shach wrote: although Orach Chayyim siman 1 did not emend Maharam at all because of doubt—goes here and there stringently; see there siman 7.
