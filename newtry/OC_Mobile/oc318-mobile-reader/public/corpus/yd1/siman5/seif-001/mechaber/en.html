**One who slaughters for kodshim—what is his law. And it has three seifim:** One who slaughters for kodshim that people vow and donate as such—even if it is baalat mum—his shechitah is invalid, for this is like slaughtering kodshim outside. If he slaughtered for kodshim that do not come through vow and donation—his shechitah is kosher {Rambam's language chapter 2 Hilchos Shechitah}. How so: if he slaughtered for olah, for todah, for Pesach—his shechitah is invalid, since Pesach can be separated at any hour he wishes—similar to vowed and donated. If he slaughtered for chatat, for asham vadai, for asham talui, for bechor, for maaser, for temurah—his shechitah is kosher. And if it became known he transgressed a sin obligating chatat or asham and he says “this is for my chatat” or “for my asham”—invalid:
{Rama: some disqualify asham talui in every case, for they hold it comes through vow and donation (Tur); and so appears Ra'osh chapter HaShochet and Rabbenu Yerucham see there and Rashba in Terumat HaDeshen; and one should be stringent.}
