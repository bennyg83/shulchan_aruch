And likewise turtledoves, etc.—but large turtledoves and small pigeon chicks invalid; and if said for olah of woman—kosher for woman does not come as donation unless known he has wife; I will not expand uncommon matters; once someone asked me in writing if he may take turtledoves for atonement—I did not agree—for saying “this is my atonement” implies olah—for atonement turtledoves are fit for olah.
