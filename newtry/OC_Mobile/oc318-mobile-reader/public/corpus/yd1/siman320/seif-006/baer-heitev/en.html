For then. The Shach wrote: because the poskim dispute whether meshikhah acquires and money does not, or money acquires and meshikhah does not. Therefore, to escape doubt, one must perform both kinyanim, meshikhah and money. This implies that even after the fact, if he performed only one kinyan, either meshikhah or money, he has not exempted it from bekhorah. The Bach wrote that even if he did both, meshikhah and money, but the non-Jew did not give the money immediately at the time of acquisition, only promised to give him such-and-such money and the Jew trusted him, then even if the non-Jew later gave him what he promised, that money does not acquire. Needless to say, if the Jew owed the non-Jew money and the non-Jew said, “Your cow is acquired to me with the money you owe me,” and the Jew transferred it, it is obvious that this is not a money kinyan. End quote. It implies that the same applies where a Jew buys a cow from a non-Jew: according to Rabbenu Tam, only meshikhah acquires; according to Rashi, money acquires. If the Jew performed meshikhah or gave money and it gave birth, it is obligated in bekhorah out of doubt. So it appears in the Rav's responsum, siman 87. He further wrote there that if one bought a cow from non-Jews with money and it was stolen, and the non-Jew agreed to pay the Jew for the cow, and afterward the thief was identified and the cow gave birth, it is exempt from bekhorah, for once the non-Jew agreed to pay, the cow was acquired to him wherever it was. In Piskei Maharai he wrote that although transferring the fetus to a non-Jew is ineffective because it has not yet come into the world, nevertheless there is reason to say it works if the cow is pregnant, similar to the beginning of chapter HaMafkid: it is as though he says, “My cow is acquired to you just before its theft.” If there is another basis for permissibility, such as where they saw it nursing previously, this can combine. And the condition with the non-Jew that he feed the cow in exchange for the calf is like giving money for it. End quote.
