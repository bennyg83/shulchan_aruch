Reuven asked that a goldsmith owe him twenty rubles. In the past, Reuven demanded the debt from the goldsmith, and the goldsmith told him that he had nothing to pay. Only if you wish, take from me a cow that has never given birth before, and this winter she will certainly give birth in exchange for fifteen rubles, and the rest will remain with me. And Reuven went to see the cow without his knowledge and saw it. Later, the king came to him and said that he would accept the cow in the aforementioned exchange only on the condition that the cow would be with him for his maintenance until she gave birth, and then he would take her to his house. Later, as time went by, Reuven was in the village and decided to take the cow before she gave birth so that she would give birth.
