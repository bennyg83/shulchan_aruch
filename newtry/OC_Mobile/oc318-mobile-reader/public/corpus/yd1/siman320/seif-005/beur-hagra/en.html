And even if he can, etc. Like the second version there. Rif and Rosh.
(Likkut) And even, etc. But the Tur wrote plainly like Shulchan Aruch. It appears that he was uncertain because of the other version that Tosafot wrote there s.v. “the animal of,” etc.: “and some books,” etc. Certainly that version is correct, as Tosafot there asked from the baraita there and it remained difficult. Furthermore, the Gemara should not have avoided asking, “but it is taught that it is obligated.” Also, it is not the way of the Gemara to say “another version” that is completely the opposite of the first; there it says that this was not asked at all. See Tosafot, first chapter of Yom Tov 13a s.v. “another version,” etc. Although the Rosh decided like the “some say” version and like the Rif, his proof is not compelling, for his proof there concerns only challah, since according to the first version it implies bekhorah and challah are equal. But according to the other version it is more understandable. His proof is also unclear: from where does he know the first version disagrees regarding challah? His proof from the Yerushalmi is not so clear even regarding challah; he should have brought proof from the Tosefta, where it is explicit that it applies even when he cannot remove him, as Tosafot wrote. I am concerned for the words of the early authorities, that the other version is clear and primary.
But if, etc. This is not comparable to what is stated in Bava Metzia 70b: since if not, etc.; see Tosafot there s.v. “and if,” etc. The same applies according to the second version, because the principal lien, and as above, the principal, etc., there.
