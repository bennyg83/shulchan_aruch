A flock that the king takes from it tithe—since the king's tithe is within it, the hand of a non-Jew is considered in the middle and it is exempt from bechorah: {Rama: And even if he can remove the non-Jew with money, the hand of a non-Jew is considered in the middle, since the main enslavement of the non-Jew is over the animals (Beit Yosef for Rif and Rosh and Piskei Mahariv siman 130); but if they stipulated from the outset that they will give him money for the offspring, it is obligated in bechorah (likewise there)}
