(Taz s.k. 7) Through the place is stronger. This implies that the Taz holds that the phrase "and he should transfer the place to him" does not mean that through this he acquires the animal by kinyan chatzer, but that he transfers it to him by kinyan agav. See what I wrote in the margin of the Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat siman 262.
