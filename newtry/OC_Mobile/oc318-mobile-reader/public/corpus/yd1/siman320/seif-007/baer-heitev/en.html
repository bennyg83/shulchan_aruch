Since there were both meshichah and payment. At the time of the stipulation the animal was in the non-Jew's domain, and his domain acquired it for him in place of physical pulling. Payment was also given through his undertaking to feed and care for the animals. One might object that the offspring did not yet exist, but proof may be brought from the chapter HaMafkid, where the arrangement is treated as though he said, “Acquire the animal with respect to its offspring.” Mahari.
