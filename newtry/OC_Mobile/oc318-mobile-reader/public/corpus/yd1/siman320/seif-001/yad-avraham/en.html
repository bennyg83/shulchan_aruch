And know that a kinyan of chalipin is also effective regarding bekhor according to Rabbeinu Tam, as Terumat HaDeshen writes in siman 270. This escaped the author of the Shach in Choshen Mishpat siman 123, who wrote that with respect to bekhor the poskim did not write the permission of chalipin, but Terumat HaDeshen already wrote it. Terumat HaDeshen also testified there that he did not find a Gaon who disagreed with Rabbeinu Tam; if so, one may rely on this at least after the fact. Later I found that Responsa Shaar Ephraim there noticed this, and so too at the end of Sefer Tzemach Tzedek.
