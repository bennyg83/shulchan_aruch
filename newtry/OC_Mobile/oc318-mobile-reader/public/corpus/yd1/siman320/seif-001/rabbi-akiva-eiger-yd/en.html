(Siman 320 seif 7) The acquisition does not take effect. If he said, "when most of it emerges, it shall be acquired to you by kinyan chatzer," in my humble opinion this does not help, because the sanctity of the bekhor and the sale arrive together and take effect at once; see Temurah 25a. Even if he said, "when a small amount emerges, it shall be acquired to you," see above siman 319 seif 2, and see Terumat HaDeshen siman 270. Examine carefully.
