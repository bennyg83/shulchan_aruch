They shall strike them and strip them. Maharshal asks how this can be reconciled with the teaching that Torah study is greater than saving lives. There is no difficulty: nothing supersedes danger to life. The Gemara means that one who merits uninterrupted Torah study has greater merit than one forced by circumstances to suspend study in order to save lives. Mordechai was ranked lower only after public rescue obligations compelled him to reduce his study. Likewise Ezra did not go up with Zerubbabel because the Temple construction proceeded without him. Derishah's suggestion that Torah study takes precedence when both cannot be fulfilled is certainly incorrect.
