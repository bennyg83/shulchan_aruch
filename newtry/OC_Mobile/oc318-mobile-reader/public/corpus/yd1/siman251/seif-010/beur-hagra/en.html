But if they recognize him, etc. The Tosefta at the end of Peah and the Yerushalmi there state: One may not give a poor traveler less than a loaf purchased for a pundion when four seah cost a sela. If he lodges overnight, one gives him the provisions required for lodging, namely oil and legumes. If he remains for Shabbos, one gives him food for three meals, oil, legumes, fish, and vegetables. This applies when they do not recognize him; when they recognize him, they provide even clothing for him.
