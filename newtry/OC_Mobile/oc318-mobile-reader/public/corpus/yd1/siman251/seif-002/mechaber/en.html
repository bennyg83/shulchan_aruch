One who is a transgressor to provoke — even for one mitzvah, such as one who eats nevelah where kosher meat is common — it is forbidden to redeem him if he was captured. {Rama: (So is explicit in the Gemara, chapter HaSholeach, and Tur end of siman 252) (But a transgressor out of appetite — there is no prohibition in the matter if they wish to redeem him, but they are not obligated to do so) (Beit Yosef deduced from the language of Rambam and Sefer Yereim siman 47).}
