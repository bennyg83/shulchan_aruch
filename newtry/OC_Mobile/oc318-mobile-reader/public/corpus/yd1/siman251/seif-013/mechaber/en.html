A community that needs to hire a rabbi and a prayer leader and does not have means in hand to give both — if he is an outstanding rabbi expert in rulings and laws, he takes precedence; and if not, the prayer leader takes precedence: {Rama: And one may not sustain the rabbi of the city from the charity fund, for he is their creditor and also for the townspeople; rather they shall make his provision from elsewhere (same source); but each individual may send him from his own charity — and this is the way of honor.}
