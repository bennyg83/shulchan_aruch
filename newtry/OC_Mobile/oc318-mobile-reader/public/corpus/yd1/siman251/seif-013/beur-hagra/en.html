And one should not support, etc. The Tosefta, chapter 6 of Niddah, states: Anyone who receives charity may receive the poor tithe; there are those who may receive the poor tithe but may not receive charity. Hagahot Mordechai explains in the name of Or Zarua Katan that this refers to a case of this type, where accepting regular charity would be degrading both for him and for them. The poor tithe, however, is sent from the threshing floor and is not conspicuous. Similarly, the Tosefta in the final chapter of Peah states that the poor tithe may be given to a chaver as a favor.
