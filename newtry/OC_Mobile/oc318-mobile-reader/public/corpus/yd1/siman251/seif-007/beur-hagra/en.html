He is obligated, etc. The Yerushalmi at the end of Horayot states: A man precedes a woman, etc. This applies when both require life-saving support, or both require clothing. But what is the law if one requires life-saving support and the other requires clothing? This may be derived from Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi's statement in the name of Rabbi Chanina ben Antigonus: Clothing for the wife of a chaver and life-saving support for an am haaretz; clothing for the wife of the chaver takes precedence over the life of the am haaretz because of the chaver's honor. They said this only regarding clothing for the wife of a chaver versus life-saving support for an am haaretz. But if one generally requires life-saving support and the other clothing, the one requiring life-saving support takes precedence.
