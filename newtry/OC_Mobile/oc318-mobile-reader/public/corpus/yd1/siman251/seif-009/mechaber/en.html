If many poor were before him and there is not enough in the purse to sustain or clothe or redeem all of them — he gives precedence to a kohen over a levi, and a levi over an Israelite, and an Israelite over a chalal, and a chalal over a shetuki, and a shetuki over an asufi, and an asufi over a mamzer, and a mamzer over a natin, and a natin over a ger, and a ger over a freed slave. When do these matters apply? When they are equal in wisdom; but if there was a mamzer who was a Torah scholar and an am ha'aretz — the mamzer who is a Torah scholar takes precedence: {Rama: (And even a scholar for clothing and an am ha'aretz to sustain life, and a scholar's wife like a scholar) (Beit Yosef from Yerushalmi).} And whoever is greater in wisdom takes precedence over his fellow; and if one of them was his teacher or his father — even though there is one greater than them in wisdom — his teacher or his father who is a Torah scholar takes precedence over one who is greater than he.
