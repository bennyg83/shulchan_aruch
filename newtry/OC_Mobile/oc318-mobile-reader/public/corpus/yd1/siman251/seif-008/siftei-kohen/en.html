Who came to request food, etc. The Mishnah at the end of Horayot states that a man precedes a woman with respect to saving life and returning a lost object, whereas a woman precedes a man with respect to clothing. This must mean that 'saving life' refers to rescuing them when they are drowning in a river or the like. Food, however, is not included in that category, but follows the same rule as clothing. The Tanna mentioned clothing, and the same applies to food. This is the wording of the Beit Yosef. Sefer Be'er Sheva, page 22b, writes likewise.
A woman is given precedence, etc. This is because a man commonly goes from door to door, whereas a woman does not. Even if she is presently going from door to door, since this is not normally her way, she is given precedence so that she need not continue doing so. Alternatively, perhaps she will be too embarrassed to approach others as well. This is also implied by the Perishah.
