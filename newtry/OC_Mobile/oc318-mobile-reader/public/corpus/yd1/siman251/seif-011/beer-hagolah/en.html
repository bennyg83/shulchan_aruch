Baraita, Ketubot 67a. 'They support her' includes both food and clothing, because the stated reason, that a man's way is to go about seeking assistance, applies to both. Rambam likewise rules this in chapter 8. The Mishnah at the end of Horayot stating that a man precedes a woman 'to sustain life' refers to rescue from mortal danger, such as drowning. Its rule that a woman precedes a man for clothing applies equally to food, because the rationale is the same.
