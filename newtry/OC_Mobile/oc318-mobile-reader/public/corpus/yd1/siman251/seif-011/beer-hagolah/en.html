Baraita Ketubot 67 and we sustain — as the baraita teaches, meaning whether food or clothing; for the reason is because a man's way is to go about, etc., which applies whether food or clothing; and so Rambam in chapter 8 — this that we taught at end of Horayot "a man precedes a woman for sustaining life" means to save them if they are drowning in a river; and what we taught "a woman precedes a man for clothing" etc. — it taught clothing and the same applies to food for it is one reason.
