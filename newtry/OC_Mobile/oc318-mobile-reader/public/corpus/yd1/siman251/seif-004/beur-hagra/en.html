They compel, etc. This follows from Bava Batra 174b, which says, "His son is different," meaning that he is obligated to support him. Beit din compels a person to give charity, as stated above, siman 248, seif 1. The support of his poor relatives is incumbent upon him, as stated in Nedarim 65b. This is the meaning of, "Likewise, etc." All the more so regarding his son, who precedes his other relatives, as stated above. The Sages also expound, Ketubot 50a, "He performs charity at all times, etc."
