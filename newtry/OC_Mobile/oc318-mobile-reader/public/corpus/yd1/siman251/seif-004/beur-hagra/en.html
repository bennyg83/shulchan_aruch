We compel, etc. From what is written in Bava Batra 174b "his son is different" — meaning he is obligated to sustain him; and we compel regarding charity as above siman 248 seif 1; and his poor relatives are an obligation on a person as written in Nedarim 65b — and this is what he says "and likewise," etc.; and all the more his son who precedes other relatives as above; and we say (Ketubot 50a) "do charity at all times," etc.
