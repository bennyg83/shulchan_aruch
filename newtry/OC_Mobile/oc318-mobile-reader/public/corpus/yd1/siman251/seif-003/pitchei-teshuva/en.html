His relative requires. Maharam Ziskind, citing Maharam Mintz, treats a wife's relatives like the poor of one's city, though a husband need not contribute to them as much as to his own relatives. He suggests one-third as an example and leaves related priority questions unresolved.
Another. Shemesh Tzedakah rules that the poor of one's own city, even if unlearned, precede Torah scholars from another city. Priority based on wisdom applies where both are from the same city.
And the residents of Eretz Yisrael. Chatam Sofer gives Jerusalem priority over other cities of Eretz Yisrael, but only after basic survival needs are equalized. One may not deprive others of bread to provide Jerusalem with comforts. He also expresses disapproval of the costly Lag BaOmer pilgrimage celebrations in Tzfat.
