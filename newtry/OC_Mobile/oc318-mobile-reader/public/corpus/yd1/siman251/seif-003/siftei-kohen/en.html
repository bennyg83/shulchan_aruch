That he is not obligated, etc. Meaning, the funds that exceed six.
His father, etc. If he has the means, a curse should come upon one who supports his father from charity funds, as stated above in siman 240, seif 5.
They precede the poor of another city. This implies even the poor of another city in Eretz Yisrael. The Bach likewise writes this.
And they precede his brothers, etc. The Sifrei states, and the Semag and Mordechai cite, that a paternal brother precedes a maternal brother. The Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe cite this.
