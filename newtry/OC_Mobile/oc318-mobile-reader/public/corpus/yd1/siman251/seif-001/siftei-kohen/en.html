A habitual transgressor. The term 'habitual transgressor' implies that he regularly transgresses. Likewise, the wording of the Tur and Semak is 'an apostate because of appetite,' etc. The term 'apostate' implies habitual conduct, as stated above in siman 2, seif katan 20. The Rama likewise writes in seif 2, 'but an apostate because of appetite,' etc. This is also logical, for one who transgressed once because of appetite does not thereby leave the category of brotherhood.
Together with the Jewish poor. This is not precise. Even gentile poor alone are supported for the sake of peaceful relations. Ran, cited by the Darkei Moshe. I likewise wrote above in siman 151, seif katan 19.
