For we assess his intention. Panim Meirot discusses a charitable endowment whose donor's heir later became poor. Before the funds reach the charity collector, the income may be given to poor heirs based on presumed intent, but the principal must remain intact. Once the funds reach the collector, the heirs have no preference over other poor people. Adnei Paz rejects a son's claim to all funds bequeathed generally to relatives merely because he later became poor.
