If he vowed charity without specification, etc. This refers to a vow made at the time of death, when we estimate his intention, etc. All the more so, during his lifetime he may give it to his relatives. But if he pledged charity together with the townspeople, he may not divert it to his relatives.
For we estimate his intention, etc. Responsa of Rabbi Eliyahu Mizrachi, siman 53, states that in all laws of vows and consecrated property we rely upon reasonable estimation, and adjudicate accordingly. We likewise follow an estimation concerning the poor. See there at length.
