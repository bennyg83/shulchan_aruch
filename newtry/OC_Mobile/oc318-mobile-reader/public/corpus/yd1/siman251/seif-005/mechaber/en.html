One who gave money to charity collectors — neither he nor his heirs have any power over it, and the community shall do what is good in the eyes of God and man: {Rama: But before it came into the collectors' hands, if he vowed charity unstated, they give it to his poor relatives, for we assess his intent that he intended for his relatives — and specifically if he had poor relatives at the time of the vow; but if they were wealthy then and became poor, they do not give to them; and all this is when assessing charity alone; but if he assessed charity with the townspeople according to the townspeople's understanding, he vowed and they shall do as they wish (all in Mordechai, first chapter of Bava Batra, Teshuvat Maharam).}
