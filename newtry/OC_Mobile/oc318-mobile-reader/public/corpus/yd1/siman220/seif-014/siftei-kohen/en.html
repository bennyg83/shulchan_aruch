There we are uncertain whether his intent followed the majority usage of the city's language or the majority of the city's supply, and we rule stringently. Here, however, it is certain that he regarded the people of his own city as decisive and used the unqualified term for what is most common there. Thus writes Atzei Levush. Bach writes similarly.
