She is immediately forbidden to benefit. Perhaps she will go, thereby causing the prohibition to have applied retroactively.
And she is permitted to go. She may benefit once Pesach has arrived. Here he did not say that she is forbidden to go before Pesach lest she benefit from him after Pesach, because a person is careful regarding the vow itself but is not careful regarding a condition attached to a vow.
