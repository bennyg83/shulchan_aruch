She receives lashes. The Ran explains that this refers to the woman. Although she did not vow anything, she nevertheless violated “he shall not profane,” meaning that the matter of a vow shall not be profaned. Since a person can impose a prohibition upon another, the other person is included in the prohibition of “he shall not profane.” This is unlike the Rambam, who explains that he receives lashes if he provides benefit to her, but she does not.
