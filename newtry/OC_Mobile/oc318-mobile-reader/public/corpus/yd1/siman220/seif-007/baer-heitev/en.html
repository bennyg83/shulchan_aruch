Forbidden. From the outset from when he vowed. And from the words of Semag in Choshen Mishpat siman 312 se'if kaf bet it appears explained: if he stands on Rosh HaShanah and said one year, he is forbidden only twelve months, even if the year is intercalated — for otherwise it should have said "this year." But when he does not stand on Rosh HaShanah he cannot say "this year," for then it would imply only until Rosh HaShanah. And in truth it means a complete year — see there. However, where he said one year from this day we count twelve months even if he stands on Rosh HaShanah.
