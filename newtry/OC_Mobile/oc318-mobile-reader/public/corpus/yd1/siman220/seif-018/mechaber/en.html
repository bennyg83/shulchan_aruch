One who forbids himself in a matter until the rain — behold, he is forbidden until the time of rains, which in Eretz Yisrael is Rosh Chodesh Kislev. When the time of rains arrives, he is permitted, whether rains fell or did not fall. And if they fell on the 17th of Marcheshvan, he is permitted. And if he said "until the rains" — behold, he is forbidden until rains fall; and this is when they fall in the time of the second rain, which in Eretz Yisrael and places adjacent to it is from the 23rd of Marcheshvan onward. {Rama: And in the diaspora, the time of rains is sixty days after the tekufah when they begin to ask; and the time of the second rain is seventy days after the tekufah (Tur).}
