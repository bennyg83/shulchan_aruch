From the seventeenth. Shach inclines to Rosh's view that the first rainfall period begins on the seventh of Marcheshvan and the second on the seventeenth. If he said 'until the rain,' he is permitted from the seventh whether rain fell or not. If he said 'until the rains' or 'until there are rains,' he remains prohibited until rain actually falls in the second period.
And in the Diaspora. The calculation follows the place where the vow was made. Shach explains that under Rosh's system the second rainfall period in the Diaspora is seventy days after the tekufah, while Rambam's dates would imply sixty-six days. He leaves Rama's combination of the views requiring study.
