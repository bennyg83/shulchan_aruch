Seventeenth. The view of Shach leans to rule like the view of Rosh that the first rainfall (*) — before Turei Zahav the text at the beginning was "they delay" and at the end "they hasten," and before us it was emended to the opposite according to Shach — the first is on the seventh of Marcheshvan and the second on the seventeenth thereof. And if he said until the rain, he is forbidden until the seventh of Marcheshvan, and then he is permitted whether they fell or did not fall. And if he said until the rains or until there will be rains, he is forbidden until they fall in the second rainfall. And one may wonder at the Rambam who ruled in Hilchot Tefillah that we ask on the seventh of Marcheshvan, and here ruled the first rainfall is on the seventeenth of Marcheshvan — only it may be resolved with difficulty, and it requires investigation. And it appears to me that for this reason they are accustomed to bless in the synagogue on the first Shabbat after Rosh Chodesh Marcheshvan, even though Mahari Weil wrote one should not begin to fast in the synagogue until the seventeenth of Marcheshvan which is the first rainfall. And so it is in Hagahot Minhagim. Nevertheless, since we hold the first rainfall is on the seventh of Marcheshvan, as is written — if so, one should bless at the first rainfall, and we never decree a fast in the synagogue before the seventh of Marcheshvan — end quote:
And in the exile. And all according to the place where he vowed. And Shach wrote: and it appears Rosh follows his own reason who holds the first rainfall is on the seventh of Marcheshvan in Eretz Yisrael and the second on the seventeenth thereof. If so, we see the two are ten days apart. And in the Gemara in Taanit they say: in the exile we ask sixty days after the tekufah. If so, the second rainfall is seventy days after the tekufah — for otherwise from where do they have this, that in the exile the second rainfall is seventy days after the tekufah? And according to this, Rambam and the author who wrote the first is on the seventeenth thereof and the second on the twenty-third thereof — if so, there are only six days between one and the other. If so, the second rainfall in the exile is only sixty-six days after the tekufah. And it is difficult on the Rav who wrote the words of Rosh after the words of Rambam and the author — and it requires investigation — end quote:
