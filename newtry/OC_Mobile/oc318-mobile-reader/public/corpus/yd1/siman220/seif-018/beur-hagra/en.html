One who forbade, etc. — Rambam's wording, and he explains until the rain: if they fell — until the first rain; if they did not fall — until the last rain; and the text must be another version in the gemara. And he ruled like R' Yosi that the halacha follows him (as written in Eruvin 46 side b and in the conclusion) — and in Taanit 6 side a R' Chagah said halacha like R' Yosi. But Rashi there has the text halacha like R' Yehuda — see there s.v. nireh li R' etc., and as I wrote R' Chagah said halacha like Rabban Gamliel, and so there 10 side a, and as written in Orach Chaim siman 117; and this is what he wrote in Hagahah: and the time of the second rain, etc., like R' Yehuda, as Rosh wrote in Nedarim there and Tur. And in siman 575 seif 1 he explained like R' Meir (and so the text in Taanit in R' Meir's words), as written in Nedarim there and in Taanit there — third for fasting; and there: and so R' Yosi would say, individuals do not fast, etc.; and Rambam and Shulchan Aruch ruled like the unattributed mishnah there 10 side a: the seventeenth arrived, etc. — and this is according to R' Meir, as I wrote on 3 Marcheshvan we ask, etc. (and see Rabeinu Nissim there).
And if he said, etc. — he ruled like the first tanna; and Baer Heitev and Beit Yosef and Avraham ibn Ezra and Rosh in chapter 10 of Bava Batra end of siman 46 — and the law is a general rule of 34, etc.; and their reason is that we say in Ketubot 87 side a and Bava Metzia 37 side b — they are amoraim, etc.; and Rava, R' Nachman disagree, and we hold like Rava there; and Beit Yosef lengthened in vain (and see Choshen Mishpat siman 223 note 3).
And in the diaspora, etc. — Taanit 10; and as Rosh wrote — first for asking.
