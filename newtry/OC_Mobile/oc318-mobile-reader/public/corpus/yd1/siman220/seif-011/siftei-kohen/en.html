One who forbids an item upon himself, etc. Bach notes that the Tur carefully wrote 'an item,' rather than simply saying that one vowed until Pesach. Until this point the siman discusses one who forbade wine, but this law applies specifically to other items. Regarding wine, even if he said 'until Pesach shall be' or 'until before Pesach,' he is forbidden only until Pesach arrives, because the halachah follows Rabbi Yehudah, who explains that he intended only until the time when people customarily drink wine, as ruled by the Rosh and Tur below. However, the Mechaber's omission of those words below implies that he follows the Rambam and those who agree with him, that even regarding wine he is forbidden until Pesach ends. Bach writes likewise below. Maharil, siman 131, writes concerning one who vowed from wine but excluded Shabbos and Yom Tov, that he is nevertheless forbidden from the wine of Havdalah. See there.
Only until it arrives, etc. Bach rules that whenever the prohibition lasts only until the event arrives, such as 'until Pesach' according to all opinions, 'until before Pesach' according to the Rambam, and 'until the rain' below in seif 18, release by a sage is required, lest he not know how to distinguish among these expressions.
