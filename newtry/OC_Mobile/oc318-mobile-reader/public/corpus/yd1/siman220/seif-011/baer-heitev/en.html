Bach wrote: wherever he is forbidden only until it arrives — such as until Pesach according to its simple meaning, and until "before me" according to the Rambam, and likewise until the rain (below se'if 18) — in all of them a release to a sage is needed, lest he not know to distinguish between them — see there.
