He is forbidden from day to day, etc. This means from the hour of the vow until the corresponding hour on the same date of the next month. Thus, if he vowed at midday on the fifteenth of Tammuz, he is forbidden until midday on the fifteenth of Av, although this is only twenty-nine days because Tammuz is deficient. It is nevertheless a complete month from day to day. The Rosh explains similarly. The Rambam, Semag, and Rabbeinu Yerucham write that if he said 'one month,' he is forbidden for thirty full twenty-four-hour periods. It appears that they discuss an ordinary full month, but they agree that a deficient month requires only twenty-nine days, as the Mishnah's phrase 'from day to day' indicates.
