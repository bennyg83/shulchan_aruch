A full twenty-four-hour period. From the hour at which he made the vow.
Wine that I taste today, etc. The same law applies if he said, 'Konam, wine that I taste this day.' It is treated as though he said 'today,' as stated in Semak and in the responsa of Mahari ibn Chaviv, siman 9, page 20, column 2, cited by Bach.
He requires release by a sage. He does not require a basis for regret, unlike one who vows for one day. Perishah. Another practical difference is that here release is required only rabbinically, whereas one who vows for one day requires release by Torah law. See above, siman 208, seif 2.
He does not require release by a sage. We do not decree because of the expression 'one day,' since everyone knows that the law of a fast differs from other vows, as the Sages said that its prohibition is not comparable to other vows.
