(Siman 220, Taz, se'if katan 2) He writes that if one said die Woche, Shabbos is not included. His words are not compelling, for Woche means a week, and Shabbos is therefore included. The expression die Woche indicates the entire week. His statement, “It appears that this is the common practice,” is no proof. Although the common practice is that a person who undertakes not to eat meat die Woche may eat meat on Shabbos, that is because he intended only the weekdays, not Shabbos, when he is obligated to take pleasure and eat meat. Regarding other vows that apply on Shabbos as well, Shabbos is included. Consider that people call a period of seven days Woche, which shows that without Shabbos it is not called a Woche.
