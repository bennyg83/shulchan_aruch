(Siman 220 se'if kaf vav se'if kaf bet) He wrote that if he said die Woche, Shabbat is not included. And his words are not necessary, for Woche is a week — if so, Shabbat is also included. And the language die Woche indicates the entire week. And what he wrote "and as it seems the custom is common in this" is not proof, for although the custom is common that one who accepts upon himself not to eat meat die Woche is permitted on Shabbat — this is because his intention was only on weekdays and not on Shabbat, which one is obligated to enjoy and eat meat. But other vows that also apply on Shabbat — Shabbat is also included. And consider for yourself: behold people call seven days Woche — is it not that without Shabbat it is not called Woche:
(There, seif katan 3) My teacher, of blessed memory, wrote this from his own reasoning, etc., and it does not appear so, etc. He criticized him improperly. He did not write it from his own reasoning, but ruled like the Bartenura and Rabbeinu Peretz, as I wrote in Shach, seif katan 10.
