This week. The same applies if he said, “Konam, wine that I taste this week,” or “this month,” or “this year”; his law is like one who says “this week,” “this month,” or “this year,” just as with a day where he says “today.” So wrote R. Levi ibn Chaviv in his responsum there. See below, end of s.k. 15. It is written in Responsa Mabit II siman 104, p. 50c, that if he said “this year,” with the feminine form, it also means until Rosh Hashanah, and this is simple. He further wrote there that the same applies if he said “until a year,” that its law is like “this year,” but this is not necessary.
And he is permitted afterward. The Bach ruled like the Rav in Semak and like the Bartenura, that with “this week” and “this month” too, she’eilah is required at the end of the week or month; see there.
He is forbidden for a full time-period. Meaning seven full days, twenty-four-hour periods, from the hour he vowed.
