Even when Rosh Chodesh is two days, etc., since in ordinary usage, etc. Accordingly, if he was standing on the first day of Rosh Chodesh and said 'this month,' he is forbidden from that first day of Rosh Chodesh onward, because ordinary people call it Rosh Chodesh, and it is therefore included in the coming month. Rashi explains this in the Gemara, page 60b, and the Rosh also cites this explanation. Although the Rosh gives another explanation, this ruling remains correct. See the Rosh there, and see above, siman 228, seif 48.
