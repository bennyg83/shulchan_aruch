Even two days of Rosh Chodesh, etc., since in language, etc. And according to this, if he stood on the first Rosh Chodesh and said "this month" — he is forbidden also from the first Rosh Chodesh onward, for since in the language of people they call it Rosh Chodesh — if so, it is included in the coming month. And so Rashi explained in the Gemara page 60b. And also Rosh there brought this explanation. And even though he explained there another explanation — nevertheless for the law it is true.
