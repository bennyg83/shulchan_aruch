If he was standing within the month, or before it, and said, "This month" — he is forbidden until the month is completed, and he is permitted on Rosh Chodesh, even a two-day Rosh Chodesh whose first day is for completing the past month — even so he is permitted in it, since in common speech they call it Rosh Chodesh. (And all the more so if he vowed to do something before Rosh Chodesh, that he must fulfill it before the first day.)
