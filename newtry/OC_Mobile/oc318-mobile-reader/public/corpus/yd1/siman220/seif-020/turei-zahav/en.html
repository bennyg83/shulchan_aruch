He is forbidden on the final Yom Tov. I found it written in the name of Maharar Leib of Prague that this proves one should say on Shemini Atzeres, “this day of the festival of Shemini Atzeres,” since it is called a festival, end quote. This proof may be rejected, because vows are different, for we follow ordinary speech, and in ordinary speech it is called a festival, though perhaps not in the language of the Torah. It appears, however, that one can bring a clear proof from the chapter “These are recited,” Sotah 41a: “Had the Torah written, ‘on the festival of Sukkos,’ I might have thought even the final Yom Tov; therefore the Torah writes, ‘when all Israel comes.’” We see that even in Torah language it is called a festival, for an additional verse was required to exclude it. This is a clear proof against which there is no answer.
