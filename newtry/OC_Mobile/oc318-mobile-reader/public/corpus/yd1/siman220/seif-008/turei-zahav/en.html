Until Rosh Chodesh of the first Adar. For the halachah follows Rabbi Yehudah who holds unspecified Adar is the first Adar. Nevertheless for "until the end of Adar" he is forbidden until the end of the second Adar, for we consider the two Adars as one — likewise Rosh. And it is simple that if he said until half of Adar — that is, until half of the first Adar — and we do not say he means until Rosh Chodesh of the second Adar, for there is no half falling on the second Rosh Chodesh; specifically regarding the end of Adar we consider them as one regarding that he intended until completion of the month mentioned by the name Adar, whatever it may be.
