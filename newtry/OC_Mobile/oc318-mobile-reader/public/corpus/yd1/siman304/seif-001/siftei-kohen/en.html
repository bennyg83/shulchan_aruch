It is permitted to make them, etc. — and what the Rav wrote above in siman 301 seif 2 that ab initio one may not make sha'atnez — that applies to things one must be concerned he will cover himself with; meaning garments made for themselves or for another Israelite; but here we deal with a situation where there is no such concern, such as a gentile's garment being made for gentiles, and the like.
