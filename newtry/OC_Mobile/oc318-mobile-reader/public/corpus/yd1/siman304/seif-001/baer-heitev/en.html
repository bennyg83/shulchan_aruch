To make them. Shach writes that Rama's statement above in siman 301 that one should not initially make items of kilayim refers to objects where there is concern one may cover oneself with them. That is where they are made for oneself or for another Jew. Here the case is one where there is no such concern, such as a gentile's garment being made for a gentile or the like. Shach. See siman 301.
