To make them. The Siftei Kohen wrote: what the Rav wrote above in siman 301 that ab initio one may not make sha'atnez — that applies to things one must be concerned he will cover himself with; meaning garments made for themselves or for another Israelite; but here we deal with a situation where there is no such concern, such as a garment of a gentile being made for gentiles, and the like — end quote.
