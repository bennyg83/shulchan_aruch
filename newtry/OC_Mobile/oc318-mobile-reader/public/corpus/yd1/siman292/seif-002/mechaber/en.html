A nest that is ready at hand, such as doves that are accustomed to be raised with people in houses, and geese and chickens that nested in a house—is exempt. But doves of a dovecote and an attic, and birds that nested in earthenware vessels (meaning: clay vessels built in walls where birds nest), and in pits—and geese and chickens that nested in an orchard—are obligated; and this is when the mother was not raised above the eggs at all from when she laid them. But if the mother was raised above them, if those places are his, his courtyard acquired it for him and it is ready at hand and exempt.
