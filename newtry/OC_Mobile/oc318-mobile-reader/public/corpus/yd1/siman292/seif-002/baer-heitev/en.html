Prepared. The verse says, 'If a bird's nest happens before you,' excluding a nest already prepared and designated.
In an orchard. Meaning an unguarded orchard. Once the birds rebel and are no longer under one's control, the nest is not considered prepared. If it is guarded and under control, the mitzvah does not apply. Shach.
Was lifted. Once the mother was raised, the courtyard cannot acquire the eggs and make them prepared, because one may not acquire the eggs while the mother rests upon them. Since the owner cannot acquire them, his courtyard cannot acquire them for him. Shach.
The mother. Shach explains that she was raised high enough to leave his hand, as later stated. Alternatively, she rose by herself, in which case it is enough that she no longer touches the nest, like a bird hovering above it.
