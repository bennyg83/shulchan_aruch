Two rows of eggs, etc. — Taz wrote he intended to take only the lower ones alone — and this is like Rashi's explanation; but in Rashba's novellae it is written it is not clear, for in any case the mother broods on the upper eggs; and even if he takes neither upper nor lower — we learn (and it is explained in seif 5) "I will take the mother and send away the chicks" — he said nothing; except here when the upper are eggs of impure fowl, or alternatively with ready eggs similar to a cloak, such as he put his hand to the nest and placed his eggs there to make separation between the mother and eggs — so it appears to him — end; and to me the plain meaning of the Gemara is main like Rashi, and so plain from Tur's, Rambam's and the author's words; and there is no difficulty from what we learn "I will take the mother," etc. — for here he does not want to take the mother but only the lower eggs alone; and the Gemara's question is whether the upper eggs are separation — if so these lower eggs are as though placed elsewhere and permitted to take before sending; or not separation and forbidden to take them; but the mother in any case is forbidden to take because she broods on the upper eggs even though he does not intend to take them; and it appears this is Tosafot's view and not like Sefer Torat Chaim who wondered on Rashi and Tosafot; and according to what I wrote for preliminary understanding, see there — so it appears to me.
