This one, etc. — for we are in doubt whether we say: since if he left it thus it would become teref and it would be "for you and not for your dogs," and he is exempt from sending and takes the mother permissibly and completes the slaughter; or we say: since it is in his power to complete the slaughter, "take for yourself" applies to him and he is obligated to send. And in Sefer Ma'amar Mordechai, chapter Shiluach HaKen, he wrote: and this is in a case where there was no measure of staying, and for us this does not apply since in any amount we render teref — end quote. And it seems to me the case is when at the time of slaughter he takes the mother — such as he slaughtered with one hand and takes the mother with one hand, or it is a mitzvah for another to take the mother — and this is that the Rav did not emend "for us this does not apply." And so Atzei Levanon and other acharonim wrote here in general this law. And furthermore, even if you say the case is where it stayed any amount, nevertheless since many poskim permit as above siman 283, only we are stringent — and this is regarding eating but not to be lenient regarding shiluach haken. And let it be clear.
