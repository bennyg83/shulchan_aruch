If he slaughtered part, etc. This is an unresolved question in the Gemara. Perhaps, since leaving the slaughter incomplete would render the offspring tereifah, they are excluded by “for yourself and not for your dogs.” Alternatively, because he can complete the slaughter and make them fit for himself, the mitzvah still applies. We therefore rule stringently. The Tur's text, “part of its signs,” is a scribal error; the issue concerns slaughtering part of the young.
