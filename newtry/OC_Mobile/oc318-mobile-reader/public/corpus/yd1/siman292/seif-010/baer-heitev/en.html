Slaughtered. Shach wrote: and even though for us this law does not apply, since in any staying we render it teref, it appears the case is when at the time of slaughter he takes the mother — such as he slaughtered with one hand and took with one hand, or it is a mitzvah for another to take the mother; or even if it stayed any amount, since many poskim permit as above siman 283, only we are stringent — and this is regarding eating but not to be lenient regarding shiluach haken — end quote.
