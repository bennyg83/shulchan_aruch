If her wings touch them. The verse says “brooding” rather than merely “sitting,” indicating that actual contact is required.
Even if they touch from the side. The Tur cites the Rambam that side contact creates liability, but the Ran challenges this from the Gemara, which appears to say that side contact is exempt. The Rambam must have understood the passage differently: the Baraisa's exemption concerns a case where the wings are beside the young without actually resting upon them. If the wings physically touch them from the side, it is still considered brooding and the mitzvah applies.
