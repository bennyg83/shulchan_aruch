If her wings touch. It is written by a gunman and does not say that he is sitting, but he does not say that he is swayed by a gunman:
Even though they touch from the side. And Tur wrote in the name of Rambam in this that if they touch from the side one is liable. Ran challenged: for we say in the Gemara, R' Yirmiyah — when the braita teaches touching from the side, exempt. And it must be said that Rambam would explain that the braita speaks of hovering from the side and touching there — exempt; but hovering over them, even touching from the side — liable.
