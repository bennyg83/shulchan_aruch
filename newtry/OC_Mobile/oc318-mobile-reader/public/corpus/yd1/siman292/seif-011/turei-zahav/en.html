If her wings touch. It is written by a gunman and does not say that he is sitting, but he does not say that he is swayed by a gunman:
Even though they are touching from the side. In the Tur it is written in the name of Rambam that if they touch from the side he is liable; and Ran raised a difficulty, since the Talmud says R' Yirmiyah taught that the baraita speaks of touching from the side as an exemption. One must say Rambam interpreted it as follows: the baraita speaks of one flying past from the side and touching there — which is exempt; but one who is flying directly over them [the priests], even if touching only from the side, is liable.
