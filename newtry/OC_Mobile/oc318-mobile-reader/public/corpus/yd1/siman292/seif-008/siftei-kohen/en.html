And they are pure — meaning the mother and the eggs are both pure, only they are not of its species. And since he did not distinguish, it implies that even with a female korah one is not lashed — and this is Rambam's view, as Beit Yosef wrote, and so Rif according to how Rosh understood his words; and after him followed Rabbeinu Yeruham and Bach in understanding Rif's view. But Rosh, Tur, Rashi, and Ran hold that with a female korah one is liable even though it sits on eggs not of its species, since both are pure. And so Rabbeinu Yeruham wrote that most poskim hold thus. And according to what Beit Yosef and Maharshal wrote at the end of the chapter Shiluach HaKen, Rif also holds thus, and so Maharshal wrote there. And the meaning of korah — Rabbeinu Yeruham wrote that it cannot be the bird called kokliyo, as some commentators explained korah as one that lays but does not give birth, for it is an impure bird; and it is simple to all that one is exempt — end.
