Pure. Shach wrote that since he did not distinguish, it implies that even with a female korah one is not lashed — and this is Rambam's view. But Rosh and the other great poskim hold that with a female korah one is liable even though it sits on eggs not of its species, since both are pure. And the meaning of korah — Rabbeinu Yeruham wrote that it cannot be the bird called kokliyo, as some commentators explained korah as one that lays but does not give birth, for it is an impure bird; and it is simple to all that one is exempt.
