And after he sent it away, it returned. This is difficult. Since the Tur writes that even in his own property he must send the mother away, why is he obligated again after it returns? At the moment he first sent it away, he could have acquired the young, and his courtyard should have acquired them automatically. It appears that his courtyard does not acquire them unless he consciously intends to acquire them, because automatic acquisition would cause the mitzvah to be lost. Therefore, if he did not take the young and the mother returned, the mitzvah applies again.
