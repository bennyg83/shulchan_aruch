Name in the Barthala:
This is the name of the Lord of Israel, who is given by the sea through the sea, and who has washed the water and was the nest in his head:
There, the question of the Pappunai before Rav Kahana, and he resolved for them from the verse "and I will put on his head," and Rashi explained: even though it was on his head it did not lose its name, etc. And now also on the ground we call it thus.
