If he took, etc. — he is not obligated unless it died or fled, and the like. Rambam, and as Rif's version in Makkot 15b: "if he fulfilled and did not fulfill" — meaning like this case; but for Rashi and Rosh, even though Rashi explained "did not fulfill" after the time of speech, nevertheless we rule according to the version "nullified and did not nullify" specifically that he slaughtered it. See Taz 1 s.v. "it works out," and see in Likutim (as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 72 s.k. 11 and siman 97 s.k. 36).
