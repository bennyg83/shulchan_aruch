He must send her away. He is exempt from lashes, since he still has the ability to send her away and is therefore not yet liable to lashes. Nevertheless, one may not act this way initially. If she died, or he slaughtered her before sending her away, he is liable to lashes, since he can no longer fulfill the mitzvah of sending her away. Rambam writes this at the beginning of chapter 13 of Hilchot Shechitah, and Ran writes likewise. Rambam further writes there that whenever a prohibition is linked to a positive commandment, one must fulfill that positive commandment, and if he does not, he receives lashes. If another person came and seized the mother from his hand, or she escaped from his hand without his intention, he receives lashes, for the verse states, “You shall surely send away,” meaning that he himself must send her away, and he did not fulfill the positive commandment.
