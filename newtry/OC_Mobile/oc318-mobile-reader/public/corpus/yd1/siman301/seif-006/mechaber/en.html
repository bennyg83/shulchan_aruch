Tailors of garments sew in their usual manner, provided they do not intend for warmth because of the heat or because of rain; and the modest sew on the ground. And likewise sellers of garments sell in their usual manner, provided they do not intend for warmth because of the heat or because of rain; and the modest hang on a rod. {Rama: And some permit even wearing kilayim whenever he does not intend for his benefit, such as one who wears kilayim to pass the tax through it, or who wears them to show their measure if he wishes to sell them and the like (Tur, Rosh, and Semag).}
