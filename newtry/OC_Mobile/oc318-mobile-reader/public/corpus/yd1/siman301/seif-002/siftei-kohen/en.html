If they are sewn with linen, etc. Although they are not shua, tavui, and nuz, nevertheless according to the Rambam they are forbidden by Torah law like felt. Even according to the Rosh and his school, who can tell us that these kapanag garments are hard? Therefore it is forbidden to spread them underneath him, even with ten mattresses one atop another and they underneath, as is clear from the Beit Yosef.
All hard garments. Although they contain Torah-level kilayim, they are permitted to spread underneath him. This is specifically when his flesh does not touch them, as above in seif 1. The Rav was brief, and his wording is also difficult, for he wrote "it is forbidden to wear them," when he should have stated the stronger novelty that it is forbidden to sit directly on them.
And some are stringent about wearing felt. The same applies to spreading it underneath him when his flesh touches it.
