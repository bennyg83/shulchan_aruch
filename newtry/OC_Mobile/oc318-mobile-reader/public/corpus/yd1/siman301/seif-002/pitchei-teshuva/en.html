Regarding a bed. This means a rope bed, which sinks under him and causes the sides of the cushion to rise around him. But wooden boards are hard and do not sink so much, as the Rash writes in the final chapter of Kilayim and Tosafot Yom Tov there. It seems to me that the same applies even to a wooden bed if the cushion is wide and the bed narrow, so that the sides of the cushion bend down at the bed walls and rise against him. See what I wrote above in siman 190 s.k. 26 and siman 195 s.k. 11.
