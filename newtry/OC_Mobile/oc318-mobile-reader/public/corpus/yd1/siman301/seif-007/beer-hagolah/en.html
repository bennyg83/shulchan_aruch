Beit Yosef explained: a garment made of felts. And according to the view of the Rambam that felts are forbidden d'oraisa — and I noted this above in seif 1 — even if they are as hard as the namta gamda of Narash, it is forbidden to place them upon oneself. And according to Tosafot and the Rosh who permit even placing them upon oneself if they have reached the hardness of the namta gamda of Narash — who can determine whether they have reached the hardness of the namta gamda of Narash or not? Therefore one should be careful, etc. (°) And provided that his flesh not touch them, as above in seif 1.
