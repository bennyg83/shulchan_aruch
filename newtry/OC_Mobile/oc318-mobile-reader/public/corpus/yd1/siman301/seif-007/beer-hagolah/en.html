The Beit Yosef explains this as a garment made of felt. According to the Rambam, who holds felt is forbidden by Torah law, as I noted above in seif 1, even if they are hard like the gamda felt of Naresh, it is forbidden to bring them upon oneself. According to Tosafot and the Rosh, who permit even bringing them upon oneself if they reached the hardness of gamda of Naresh, who can determine whether they reached that hardness? Therefore one must be careful, etc.
Only provided his flesh does not touch them, as above in seif 1.
