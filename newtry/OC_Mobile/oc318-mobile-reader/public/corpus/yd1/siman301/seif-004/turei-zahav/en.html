Although an adult does not, etc. Meaning that if an adult is not embarrassed and often goes to the market in this child's garment, it is obviously forbidden, for it is a full garment even regarding tzitzit. Rather, even if he is not accustomed to go to the market in it, nevertheless regarding kilayim every garment in the world is forbidden. The Tur and Rosh wrote here that an adult goes out with it temporarily to the market, and the Beit Yosef wrote this is a scribal error and should read "although an adult does not," etc. But he was not precise: the Tur and Rosh refer to present wearing, meaning a very small child's garment that is not fit at all for an adult to wear, and he would be embarrassed to go to the market in it, yet if he now happens to go out with it, it is forbidden. Proof is that in Orach Chayyim siman 16 the Tur discusses a child's tallit and mentions covering head and most of the body, and regarding an adult mentions not being embarrassed to go to the market in it; here he mentions none of that. Rather, he simply means that even a child's garment that does not cover an adult's head and most of his body is forbidden for an adult to go to the market in, even where he is embarrassed, for the Torah forbade kilayim in every manner.
