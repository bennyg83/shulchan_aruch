Such as, etc., that they cover, etc. In the Mishnah: "the brasin," etc.; see the Rambam in his commentary there.
But tziltzalim, etc. End of the first chapter of Yom Tov, according to Rav Ashi.
And a rag, etc. Tosefta brought by the Rosh: a bandage, poultice, and dressing have no kilayim prohibition because this is not a manner of warming. But in the Yerushalmi I cited at the end of siman 300 it implies it is forbidden. Perhaps R. Yirmeyah holds like Rav Pappa in the first chapter of Yom Tov, while the Tosefta follows Rav Ashi, and we rule like Rav Ashi.
In order to cause to bloom, etc. Gemara there: kalia peruchi, according to the Rif and Rosh's explanation there.
