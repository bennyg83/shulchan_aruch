There is no prohibition of kilayim except in garments that are worn in the manner of warming, such as a shirt, turban, pants, belt, cloak, and garments with which one covers the legs and the hands and the like; but small bells that people make on their wrists to tie money or spices in them, and a rag on which one places a poultice or a compress or a plaster and the like—these are permitted even though his flesh touches them, for there is no manner of warming in this. A leather or silk frontlet on which wool and linen threads hang loose upon a person's face in order to drive away flies—there is no kilayim in it, for there is no manner of warming in this.
