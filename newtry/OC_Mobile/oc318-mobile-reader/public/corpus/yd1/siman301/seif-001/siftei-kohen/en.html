And likewise it is permitted, etc. In all these cases mentioned by the Rav it is permitted even rabbinically. However, it appears this is only when he does not touch them.
And some say, etc. This requires study: what is the meaning of "some say," since no one disputes this, as is clear in the Beit Yosef and all the poskim. According to all, rabbinic kilayim may be sat upon when hard, and even wearing hard items is permitted. This is also proven from the Rav himself in seif 2, who wrote that hard felt garments are permitted to wear because even soft ones are only rabbinic. The stringent view there regarding felt is only because it holds felt is forbidden by Torah law too. But with rabbinic kilayim all agree it is permitted. It appears that the Kol Bo's statement that lechatchilah one should not make them of kilayim lest one come to cover with them speaks of Torah-level kilayim. This requires study.
