And kilayim on one side. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Yoreh Deah, end of siman 186, who discusses a garment made of camel wool with a patch of kilayim on one side where a sheep-wool thread is joined with a linen thread. He questions whether the entire garment thereby becomes kilayim, so that one may not cover himself even with the other side, as with a garment entirely of sheep wool that has a linen thread on one side; or perhaps only the actual joining of linen and sheep wool is prohibited, while the camel wool itself does not become prohibited. According to the Rambam above in siman 299, seif 2, he concludes that it appears prohibited. According to the Rosh there, the matter remains doubtful. See there.
