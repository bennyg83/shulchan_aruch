And kilayim from one side. See in the teshuva of Noda BiYehudah Tinyana, Yoreh De'ah, end of siman 186, who was in doubt regarding a garment of camel wool that has kilayim on one side, that a thread of sheep wool was joined there with a flax thread: do we say that the entire garment becomes kilayim and it is forbidden to cover oneself with the other side, like a garment that is entirely sheep wool and on one side a flax thread; or is that case different, that wool and flax forbid one another, but camel wool is not forbidden — only the joining of flax and wool is forbidden, and not the entire garment. And he wrote that according to the view of the Rambam above siman 299 seif 2 it appears to forbid, but according to the view of the Rosh there there is room to doubt; see there.
