And kilayim on one side. See Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Yoreh Deah, end of siman 186, who was uncertain about a garment of camel wool with kilayim on one side, where sheep wool thread was joined to linen thread: do we say the whole garment becomes kilayim and one may not cover with the other side, as with a garment entirely of sheep wool with linen on one side, or is it different because there sheep wool and linen prohibit each other, whereas camel wool is not itself prohibited and only the joined linen and sheep wool are forbidden, not the whole garment? He writes that according to the Rambam above in siman 299 seif 2 it appears forbidden, but according to the Rosh there is room for doubt. See there.
