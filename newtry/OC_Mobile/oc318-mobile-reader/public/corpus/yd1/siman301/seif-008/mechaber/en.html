A donkey's pack-saddle—it is permitted to make it from kilayim and to sit on it, provided his flesh does not touch it; and he should not place this pack-saddle on his shoulder even to carry manure on it. And specifically when the kilayim is recognizable and known in the garment; but a garment in which kilayim was lost and its place is not recognizable—it is forbidden to make from it a pack-saddle for a donkey, since its place is not recognizable—lest he forget and take from it a patch to sew atop his garment; and he should not sell it to a gentile, lest the gentile resell it to an Israelite.
