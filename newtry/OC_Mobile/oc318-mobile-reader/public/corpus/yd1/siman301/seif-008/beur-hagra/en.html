And it is, etc. See Beer HaGolah. The Rosh disagrees and writes that since it did not say "provided," etc., it implies that even if it touches him it is permitted, because pillows and cushions are not hard like a saddle, as in Zevachim 94b. However, Tosafot in Niddah wrote like the Rambam.
And this is only if, etc. Other poskim wrote that here even if his flesh touches it, it is permitted, and the plain Mishnah implies this because it did not mention the condition. But one may say it is because people do not usually ride naked.
And specifically, etc., lest, etc. See Tosafot Pesachim 40b, Semak, Rosh, and other poskim.
