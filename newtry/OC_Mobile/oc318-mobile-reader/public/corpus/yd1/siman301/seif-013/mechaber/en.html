Ardilirin (meaning: a vessel worn under the shoe; and some make them of wool against the heel)—they contain no kilayim, for they are hard. (From Rashi's explanation in the name of the Geonic responsum.) They contain no kilayim. {Rama: A shoe that is kilayim and has no heel—it is permitted to wear it, for the skin of the foot is hard and one does not enjoy it like other skin of the body (Tur).}
