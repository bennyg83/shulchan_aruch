A shoe etc. See Be'er HaGolah. But Tosafot Yom Tov wrote that it should say: and it has only a heel, and this is correct, and so it appears from the teshuva of the Geonim that the Rosh brought in Niddah there; but he explained it as referring to ardalin, as he wrote there. Nevertheless the Rambam explained it as a shoe of zerb, as he wrote in the commentary there.
