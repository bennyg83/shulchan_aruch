A shoe, etc. See Beer HaGolah. But Tosafot Yom Tov wrote that the text should read "and it has only a heel," which is correct, and so it appears in the responsa of the Geonim brought by the Rosh in Niddah there. He explains this as ardalin, as stated there; nevertheless, the Rambam explains it as a shoe of zerav, as he wrote in his commentary there.
