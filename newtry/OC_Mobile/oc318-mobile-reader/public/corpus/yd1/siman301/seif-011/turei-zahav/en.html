But in the Rambam's commentary to the Mishnah, final chapter of Kilayim, "book cloths" means the Torah scroll cloths held during the reading, and he says they involve kilayim. So too in Maimonides it says the Torah scroll cloth, etc. Therefore it is certainly forbidden to make the mantle of a Torah scroll, called menteli, or the cloth wrapped around the Torah itself, from kilayim, for the attendant often holds it and there is concern for warmth in cold weather. Hagahot Asheri speaks only of cloths in the aron kodesh itself, under or around the Torah, which do not go in and out with the Torah but remain in the aron kodesh permanently.
