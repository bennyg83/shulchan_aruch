Or around the Torah. Hagahot Ashri in Perek Bnei HaIr has this wording: a cloth spread in the Aron Kodesh beneath Sifrei Torah or around the Torah. Rambam's Commentary to the Mishnah at the end of Kilayim, however, explains "cloths for books" as cloths used to hold a Sefer Torah during reading, and says they are subject to kilayim. Mishneh Torah likewise speaks of the cloth of a Sefer Torah. Therefore, it appears clear that one may not make the mantle of a Sefer Torah, commonly called a mantel, or the cloth wrapped directly around the Sefer Torah itself, from kilayim. We see that the synagogue attendant frequently holds it in his hands, and there is concern he may warm himself with it in cold weather. Hagahot Ashri is not discussing such a cloth. It refers to a cloth permanently placed inside the Aron Kodesh beneath the Sefer Torah or around the Torah area, which does not enter and leave together with the Sefer Torah but remains permanently in the Aron. This appears correct to me.
