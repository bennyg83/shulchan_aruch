And if it was hard, it is permitted. This implies there is no prohibition in benefiting from spreading it against sun, rain, and wind. So the Bach writes, and so Maharshal writes in the first chapter of Beitzah, siman 45: only regarding clothing do we forbid even protection from sun and rain, but not regarding a curtain. What the Beit Yosef wrote here, unlike Tosafot who wrote that they said a curtain is impure but has no kilayim prohibition, and what the Maadanei Melech followed, wondering at the Rosh, is a mistake obvious to anyone who examines Tosafot there.
