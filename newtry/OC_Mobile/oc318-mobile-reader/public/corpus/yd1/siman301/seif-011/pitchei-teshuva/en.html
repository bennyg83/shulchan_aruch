If it was hard, it is permitted. See in the book Chomot Yerushalayim siman 74, who wrote that one can say that we are not expert in what is soft or hard, and likewise regarding the law of hard garments above seif 2 in the gloss; and as Tosefet Shabbat wrote that we are not expert in what a thick cloth is regarding holding the ever, so too here; see there.
