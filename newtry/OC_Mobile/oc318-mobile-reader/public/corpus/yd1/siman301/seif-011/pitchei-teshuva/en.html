If it was stiff, it is permitted. See Chomot Yerushalayim, siman 30, who writes that one may say that nowadays we are not expert in determining what is considered soft or stiff. The same may apply to the law of stiff garments above in seif 2 in the Rama's gloss, just as Tevuot Shor writes that we are not expert in determining what counts as a thick cloth for the law of holding the organ.
