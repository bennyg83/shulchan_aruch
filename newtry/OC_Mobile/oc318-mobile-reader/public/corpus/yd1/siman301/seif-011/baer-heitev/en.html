The cloths. The Taz writes that it is certainly forbidden to make the mantle of a Torah scroll, called menteli, or the cloth wrapped around the Torah scroll itself, from kilayim, for we see that the attendant often holds them in his hand, and there is concern for warmth in cold weather. The Rama does not speak of this, but of cloths made in the aron kodesh itself, under or around the Torah, which do not enter and leave with the Torah but remain in the aron kodesh permanently.
