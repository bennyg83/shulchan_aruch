Towels with which one wipes the hands, and towels with which one dries utensils and floors, and with which one dries after washing, and towels for books—are forbidden because of kilayim, since the hands touch them and they wrap upon the hand constantly and become warmed. {Rama: And likewise it is forbidden to make towels that are spread on the table at which one eats (Rosh, general rule 6, and Chiddushei Agudah end of Beitzah), or that one reads upon in the synagogue from kilayim (Hagahos Ashiri, chapter Bnei HaIr), because sometimes one wipes his hands with them and becomes warmed in them.}
