To evade the customs duty. See Shach, seif katan 8, who explains that the prohibition here is because the person's principal intent is that the item be worn in the manner of clothing. Accordingly, if customs duty is imposed even on clothing and he wears the item merely so the authorities will not see it, it appears permitted, because he does not intend that it function as ordinary clothing.
