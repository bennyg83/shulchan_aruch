The first. See above, siman 265, seif katan 8, citing Brit Avraham, Orach Chaim 14, that the first deceased takes precedence because the obligation arose immediately upon the first death. If both died on Shabbat, this reason would not apply. However, Brit Avraham also gives the reason that the one who died first should not decompose, and according to that reason there is no distinction.
