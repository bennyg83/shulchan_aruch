And some say, etc. So wrote Ran: that what is implied in chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah and in chapter 23 of Shabbat — that a mirror is permitted — is [specifically] in a place where men are accustomed to look in a mirror, where there is no issue of 'a woman's garment shall not be on a man,' etc.; but the Yerushalmi and Tosefta deal with a place where only women are accustomed [to use mirrors], where 'a woman's garment shall not be on a man' applies fully; and likewise he resolved the case in Nazir regarding shaving the pubic area and the armpits — and as stated above siman 182 — see there; and nevertheless he wrote that the punctilious abstain in all places, and for such a case they had to explicitly permit [it] for Rabban Gamliel there in the Yerushalmi and Tosefta; and likewise regarding shaving the armpits that we say in Nazir there — and see siman 182 seif 1 in the gloss: 'only,' etc. But his words require study: why did he not establish it as a place where only women are accustomed [to use mirrors]? For we say there explicitly: 'and a man — even on a weekday it is forbidden' — thus it is forbidden by law; and on this it is said: 'for the house of Rebbi they permitted,' etc. — and Tosafot of Avodah Zarah there cited it; and furthermore his words are not convincing — what he distinguished between a place where [men] are accustomed: for we say in Shabbat 50b: 'a person may scrape,' etc.; 'and if because of,' etc. — and even he who disagrees there [does so] only because of honor due to one's Creator, but in such a case certainly not; and we say (Shabbat 94b, Makkot 20b): 'to pluck white hairs [from among black ones] for women,' etc. And even, etc. Smag, Hagahot Maimoniyot, and Hagahot Or Zarua; and so it is in Tosafot of Avodah Zarah mentioned above: 'or because of eye pain it is permitted,' etc.; and so it is in Smag — see there.
