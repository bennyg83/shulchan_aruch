With a razor. For harm is common; but with scissors where harm is uncommon it is permitted if another is with him — Tosafot there; and the Bach explained that if another is not with him it is in any case forbidden to seclude with a gentile (as above siman 153 seif 2) — end; and so is explained explicitly in Tosafot there in the previous heading: but between him alone it is forbidden to have one's hair cut — meaning in a place where people are not often found; but actual seclusion does not need to be stated — for it is derivable that even without the haircut it is forbidden to seclude with them on account of bloodshed — end.
