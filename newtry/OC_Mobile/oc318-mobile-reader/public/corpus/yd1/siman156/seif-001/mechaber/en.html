Whether it is permitted to get a haircut from a non-Jew; it contains 3 se’ifim. One may not get a haircut from a non-Jew except in a place where people are commonly found, or if that non-Jew imagines that that Jew is an important person. {Rama: And some are stringent not to get a haircut from a non-Jew even in a public place, with a razor, unless he looks in a mirror (Tosafos and Mordechai, perek Ein Ma’amidin); and the practice is to be lenient like the first reasoning.}
