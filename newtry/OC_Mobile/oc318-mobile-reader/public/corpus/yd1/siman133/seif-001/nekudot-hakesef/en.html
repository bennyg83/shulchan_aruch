(Siman 133 in Taz seif kaf-heh) In Tur it is written in the name of Raavad, etc. It is astonishing — he inclined himself according to Raavad; why? Behold Rama, Ramban, Rashba, and Ran all disagree with Raavad; and likewise is the view of Rashi, Rif, and Rambam — as in Beit Yosef; and likewise Beit Yosef and the Acharonim; and I already wrote thus in Shach seif vav.
