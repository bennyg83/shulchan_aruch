One may not earn a profit from yayin nesech or make merchandise from it; it contains 7 se’ifim. Just as it is forbidden to sell stam yeinam and benefit from it, so too it is forbidden to earn a profit from it. Therefore, if a Jew was hired by a non-Jew to do work for him — even regarding stam yeinam — or he rented him his donkey or his ship to transport it in, or his cellar to store it in, his wages are forbidden in benefit. And if he took money from him as his wages, he must grind it and scatter it to the wind or cast it into the sea. If he gave him produce, he must take it to the Dead Sea, or burn it and bury the ashes in a cemetery. Even if he took land or an animal as his wages — which are not forbidden when one bows to them, and one serves them — they were stringent regarding these and forbade them in benefit.
{Rama: And see above siman 123, that in our times they are lenient regarding the permissibility of benefit from stam yeinam, and therefore one should not be stringent in these laws except regarding actual yayin nesech.}
