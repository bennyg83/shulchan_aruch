A perutah. The Taz wrote: this requires study, for the tanna in the Mishnah in chapter HaZahav, who lists five perutah amounts, should have included this too, that yayin nesech does not prohibit with less than the value of a perutah. In Nekudot HaKesef he answered that the tanna taught some cases and left others; see there. Barrel. The Taz wrote that here one need not cast anything into the Dead Sea; he may leave it with the non-Jew and receive the rest. This is unlike the first clause, where the name of prohibition already applies to the whole mixture.
Lechatchilah. Even if there is not the value of a perutah among them. Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba.
