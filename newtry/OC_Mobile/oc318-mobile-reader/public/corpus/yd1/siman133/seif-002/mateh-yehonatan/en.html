(There seif 6) Even if the gentile said to him: guard for free, etc. See Taz seif kaf-zayin, who wrote: this — that he did not state the reason that he holds a favor for him, etc. — see there; and in truth there is no difficulty — for that which he forbade above in seif 4 for free on account of holding a favor applies where he performed an action with libated wine, such as transferring it from place to place and the like; but here he performs no action, only sits and guards — why should it be forbidden? And the reason must be that he wants its existence; and the reason of holding a favor as above does not apply — and so it seems to me.
