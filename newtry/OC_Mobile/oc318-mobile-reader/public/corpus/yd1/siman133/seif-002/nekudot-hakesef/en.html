(There in Taz seif vav) It is uncertain, etc. It seems to me: the Tanna taught and omitted — for so too Rabba taught in Tosafot and Rosh in chapter Eizehu Neshech; and likewise Tur below siman 161 in the name of Rabbenu Yitzchak, that there is no interest in less than a perutah; and that which the Gemara challenges in the first chapter of HaZahav "and let it teach ona'ah with a perutah" — that is because in that Mishnah it deals with ona'ah; and likewise that which it challenges there "and our Tanna — why does he not acquire the sitting of judges" — that is because Levi counts it in the Mishnah; and likewise that which it challenges "why does it not teach ma'aser" — that is because since it taught hekdesh it should also teach ma'aser by implication; and further one may say: where it can be changed it changes; and where it cannot be changed one may say the Tanna taught and omitted.
