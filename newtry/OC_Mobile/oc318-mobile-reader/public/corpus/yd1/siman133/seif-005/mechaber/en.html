A Jew may not serve and pour for a non-Jew with yayin nesech; and likewise it is forbidden to be an interpreter for a non-Jew to sell yayin nesech. And likewise it is forbidden to place wine into a vessel in the hand of a non-Jew who proclaims it to give others to taste, since once the non-Jew touched it, it became yayin nesech, and when he proclaims it and gives it to taste (some say, and shows it; and so it is in Beit Yosef and in Kol Bo), the Jew benefits from yayin nesech.
