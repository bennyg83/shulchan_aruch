(Siman 328, seif 3 in the gloss) If we know that it is a benefit for the owner of the dough, etc., one may separate challah without his permission, etc. Ketzot HaChoshen 243 disputes this based on Tosafot and other early authorities in Nedarim 36. The principle of acting for another's benefit applies only when one separates from his own produce for another's. When separating from the other person's property itself, the owner's knowledge is required by the Scriptural derivation, 'Just as you act with your own knowledge.'
