One does not separate challah without the permission of the owner of the dough. {Rama: However, if we know it is to the owner's benefit, such as when the dough would spoil, it is permitted to take challah without his permission, for one may benefit a person in his absence; and likewise a maidservant in the house may take challah without permission since it is customary that sometimes the mistress of the house gives her permission (Terumat HaDeshen siman 288); two partners need not take permission from one another (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam, chapter 4 of Terumot); but another must take permission from both of them (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag); but if one gave permission to another to separate, even while still flour it is effective even though he is not yet obligated; and a guest may tell the mistress of the house to separate challah for him from every dough she makes for him while it is still in her house so she need not take permission each time.}
