(Siman 328, seif 1, gloss) Or to separate challah. He designates it by name and says, 'This is challah.' Raavad writes this in his glosses in the name of Rabbi Acha of Shabcha, and it is cited by Beit Yosef. In my humble opinion, this is also clear from Rashba's novellae to Chullin 94, s.v. hasholeach yerech lenochri.
