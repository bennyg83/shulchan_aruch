Or to separate challah. Maharshal wrote: therefore they are accustomed to say terumah challah, both of them, end of his words. (1) And I am astonished: for we should still say perhaps challah is the main thing and not terumah, and if so the word terumah would be an interruption between the berachah and the word challah. And the Semak explicitly wrote that there is no insistence here, and whichever one wants, one may say. And those women who change on Pesach regarding challah from the matzot from what they say all year — it is obvious that it is a mistake in their hands, for what change is there in this regarding the berachah. And it is implied here that they need not say from the dough, but it suffices when they say to separate challah; and why then did they become accustomed to say from the dough? And even though the law is so, to exclude separating challah from flour, nevertheless there is no need of it for the berachah; and do they need to mention the details of the laws in the berachah? And above, siman 28, regarding the berachah of covering that they say with earth, we wrote there the reason why they say the particular; and here I do not know a need for it.
