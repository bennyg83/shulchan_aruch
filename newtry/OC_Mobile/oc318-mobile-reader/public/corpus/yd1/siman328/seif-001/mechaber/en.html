The law of the blessing on challah and who is fit to separate it. In it are 3 seifim.
At the time he separates challah he blesses "Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to separate terumah" (or to separate challah) (Tur); therefore it is forbidden for a man to separate his challah naked, for he cannot bless while naked; but a woman is permitted—and this is when her face is downward toward the ground (and in a place where they separate two challot he does not bless except when separating the first) (Kol Bo).
