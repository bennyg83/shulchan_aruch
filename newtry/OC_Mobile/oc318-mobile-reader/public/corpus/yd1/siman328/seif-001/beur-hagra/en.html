Terumah. As the verse says, "you shall separate terumah." In Scriptural language, challah can mean the dough or loaf itself, as in "cakes mixed with oil" and many similar examples. In Mishnahic language, however, the separated gift is everywhere called challah. This is why one may say either formulation. Nevertheless, it is preferable to formulate the blessing in Scriptural language, as stated at the beginning of chapter 6 of Berachot, where R. Yehudah says "the various grasses" rather than the Mishnahic term "vegetables.
