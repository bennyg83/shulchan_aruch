That her face should be. It implies that one may recite the blessing while seated. See Magen Avraham siman 8 s.k. 2, who wrote that separating challah is not so much a mitzvah act, since one is only fixing one's food; therefore one may recite the blessing while seated, see there. According to this, even if she is clothed she may recite the blessing while seated. See there in Yeshuot Yaakov by the Gaon, my relative, the av beit din of Lemberg, who rejected this and distinguished: where it is possible to perform the mitzvah standing, one may not sit, and therefore for tzitzit one blesses standing. Here it is different, because she cannot stand naked; therefore she may bless sitting. The main reason one should not sit is that it appears to belittle the matter and as though the fear of Hashem is not before his eyes; but when one does so out of necessity, there is no belittling at all.
