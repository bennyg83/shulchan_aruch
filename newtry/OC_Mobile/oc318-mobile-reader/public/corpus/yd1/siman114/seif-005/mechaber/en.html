Pomegranate wine that they sell for medicinal use — it is permitted to take it from the merchant even not from the barrel, even though its price is dearer than wine, because since it has importance [as medicine], he does not deceive himself. {Rama: (And likewise anything one buys from a craftsman who does not deceive himself) (so it appears from the words of Ran Pesachim and Mordechai chapter "How does one bless").}
