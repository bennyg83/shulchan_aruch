(there s.k. 13) He expanded in matters that are not correct — for the matter is plain that Maharam in Issur VeHeter and Rama deal with a case where it is known there is one gentile who does not mix and it is not known which one; and thus is brought in Mordechai brought in Beit Yosef siman 123 and Agudah chapter Ein Ma'amidin. And now that they make bread with wine lees, R' Avraham wrote that the bread is permitted because it is impossible that one gentile should not make it with beer lees; and we say in chapter 2 of Ketuvos — if there is one hidden source it saves all the priestesses — end of his words. And so too in Issur VeHeter klal 22 din 15 which wrote — and presumably here too we say regarding stam that it is impossible there should not be one gentile in the city who does not mix in it; therefore. But where they recognize that gentile we do not say he saves. Also what he challenged — that there regarding a priestess it is b'dieved; to resolve for me that here too it is b'dieved since it was already done by a gentile, and as above siman 108 in hagahah in many places — all the more so here regarding stam gentile wine where we are lenient in many places. Also what he wrote "however he challenged what Rama wrote: whatever is not known etc. until end of the speech" — it has no point; and see in Shach.
