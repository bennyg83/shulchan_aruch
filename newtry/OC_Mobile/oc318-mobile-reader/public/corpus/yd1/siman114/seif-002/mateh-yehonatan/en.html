(there seif 4 in hagahah) Until there is not sixty to nullify it, etc. See Taz s.k. 1 who challenged why do we need sixty etc. for a cooked dish etc., like wine in water below siman 134 — see there. And to resolve for me: behold it is in the chapter Ein Ma'amidin daf 34 side b — artist's morayis: first and second time permitted, third time forbidden, for the first or second time there is much fat and he need not mix wine into it; from here on he must mix wine into it. This barrel of morayis that came to Acco — R' Abba placed a guard over it. Rava said to him: until now who guarded it? etc. What shall we be concerned for? If for mixture etc. — a kista of wine in four lugim. And he explains that this incident is in the third time when the wine improves it; and if so there is concern for mixture, unlike the first or second time when he need not improve it — even cheap wine too he does not mix in it. However Tosafos s.v. morayis etc. explained according to Maharsha who holds it deals with the first or second time; even though he need not improve it, if the wine is cheap too there is concern because of wine mixture — see there in Maharsha well. From now one could say that the Rama holds like Rashi's explanation that when he need not improve it, because of dear expense alone there is no concern lest he mix wine in it; if so here where the Mechaber wrote there is concern lest he mixed wine in it, therefore it deals with when the wine improves the beverages, for otherwise it would be difficult why they forbade because of mixture — behold because of dear expense alone we are not concerned for wine mixture at all; and therefore one must say that the wine improved the beverages. And now to resolve for me: certainly this law of wine in wine and other beverages in wine is nullified in six — that is where the beverages are not improved by the wine and there is only to forbid because of wine taste; and since there are six parts water there is no wine taste. But where the beverages themselves are improved and the taste of wine is recognizable in them — in such a case certainly there must be sixty — investigate.
