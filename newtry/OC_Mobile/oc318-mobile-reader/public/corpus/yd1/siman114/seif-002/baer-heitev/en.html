Leniency. Rashal wrote: it appears to me he speaks only of a place where they are lenient; but nowadays that in some places, with permission, there is complete permission, it is permitted to drink beer of a gentile there, only that he should not drink in his house — end of his words. And Turei Zahav explained his words that specifically in a place where they know there is prohibition in gentile wine but they practice leniency there, the Sages made a marker that he should not drink beer there lest it be treated like wine, so they will perceive not to be lenient further. But in places where they practice complete permission and they think there is no prohibition at all, it is found they do not know from what to separate, for complete permission is before their eyes; therefore no decree applies here, and it is permitted to drink beer there, whether from a Jew or from a gentile — end of his words.
