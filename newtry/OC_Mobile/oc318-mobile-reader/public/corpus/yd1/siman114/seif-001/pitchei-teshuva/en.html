Avodat HaTaharah of Maharit may his memory be blessed — what he wrote that one who was lenient regarding beer, likewise it is permitted to drink coffee of gentiles, etc. And this is from the words of Peri Chadash s.k. 6; and see in the responsum of Or Zarua Panim Me'irot part 2 siman 62 who disagreed with him that even regarding beer the main reason was not because the grain is nullified, rather the main reason was that their beer was not served on kings' tables; or as Bach wrote that wedding feasts do not apply so much to it since they do not invite them and there is not so much closeness of mind; but regarding coffee our eyes see that it is served on kings' tables and they invite them; therefore it is proper for a scrupulous person not to drink coffee of gentiles or tea of a gentile — see there. And see in She'ilat Ya'avetz part 2 simanim 142 and 143 from this; and see what I wrote siman 122 s.k. 4 in the name of Noda B'Yehuda.
