a) [Seif 1] All beer of a gentile after date beer etc. or of honey is forbidden etc. Also bee honey water like ours, for there is nothing new under the sun that was not in the world, and everything was included in the prohibition. Beit Chadash. Zivchei Tzedek gloss 1.
b) There. Or of barley or of grain. And there is no prohibition here because of bishul goyim, for just as the grain is nullified relative to the water regarding the blessing of shehakol, so too it is nullified regarding the prohibition of bishul goyim. Tosafot. Turei Zahav s.k. 1. Shach s.k. 1. Peri Chadash gloss 2. Shulchan Gavoah gloss 4.
c) And from here there is proof to permit that they practiced to drink the boza of gentiles, that is millet flour that they cook in water and make from it a beverage comparable to grain beer, that there is no bishul goyim in it since it does not rise by intent, and also the flour changes to become a beverage on which one recites shehakol. Shulchan Gavoah there.
d) There. But if he brought the beer to his house and drinks it there it is permitted etc. It appears specifically until he brings it home and drinks it there, but he should not drink at the shop entrance — and this is the language of Rambam chapter 17 from Ma'achalot Assurot, as Beit Yosef wrote; and in the Gemara (Avodah Zarah 31b) it states: Rav Papa — they take it out to him at the shop entrance and he drinks; Rav Achai — they bring it to his house and he drinks. And Beit Yosef wrote on the words of the aforementioned Rambam that he seized the words of Rav Achai and not Rav Papa, even though the latter was later and master of the Talmud more than Rav Achai — in order to distance from matters of gentiles; see there. It is found that the view of Rambam and Shulchan Aruch is that he ruled like him to be stringent like Rav Achai, and thus we hold, unlike Peri Chadash gloss 3 who ruled like Rav Papa and wrote "and unlike Shulchan Aruch." And so too Rabbi Mazmor LeDavid challenged the words of the aforementioned Peri Chadash and agreed to the ruling of Shulchan Aruch. And it appears that Rama too holds thus from his not disagreeing. And so too Lechem HaPanim seif 1, Chokhmat Adam general rule 66 gloss 14. Arukh HaShulchan gloss Yud-Dalet. Zivchei Tzedek gloss 3.
e) There. But if he brought the beer to his house and drinks it there it is permitted etc. This "to his house" that he says is not specifically — even if he drinks it in the market it is permitted, for they only forbade inside the shop and before the shop, and in other places it is permitted. Shulchan Gavoah gloss 6.
f) There. But if he entered the gentile's house and drank casually, incidentally, it is permitted. Explanation: we require both that it be casual, meaning not fixed, and incidental, meaning he is not accustomed to this. Peri Chadash gloss 4. And it appears that but with fixed intent, even once, is forbidden; and with regularity, even casually, is forbidden. And so too Knesset HaGedolah in Hagah Beit Yosef gloss Yoreh De'ah. And regularity means three times; but once or twice casually is permitted.
g) There. And one who lodges in the gentile's house is considered like his house. It appears specifically lodging is considered his house; but if he entered for an hour he is not considered his house, and it is forbidden to drink there with fixed intent, even once, and with regularity even casually.
h) There. Considered his house and permitted to send in the city to buy etc. — meaning and to drink there even with fixed intent.
i) There. And permitted to send in the city to buy etc. And even if the gentile is his host, he gives him to drink because of enmity. Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot. Shach s.k. 2. Knesset HaGedolah there. Peri Chadash gloss 5. Binyamin Zeev gloss 1. Lechem HaPanim seif 1 Chokhmat Adam general rule 66 gloss 14.
j) There in hagahah. And some permit beer of honey and grain. And Peri Chadash gloss 6 wrote that one who is lenient with grain beer transgresses the words of the Sages and deserves rebuke, for every type of beer is forbidden; but beverages of other fruits are permitted. Lechem HaPanim gloss 1. Binyamin Zeev gloss 2. Shulchan Gavoah gloss 9. And so too Chokhmat Adam general rule 66 gloss 14, that every careful person should distance himself from this.
k) There in hagahah. And so they practice to be lenient in these countries. That is in Ashkenaz countries; but for Sephardim it is forbidden because they accepted the stringencies of Shulchan Aruch. And so too Shulchan Gavoah there that thus we hold like the ruling of Shulchan Aruch. And so too Zivchei Tzedek gloss 8: we Sephardim have only the words of Maran, and every type of beer is forbidden — end of his words. And this that it is forbidden is to drink it in the place of its sale and with fixed intent, as stated in Shulchan Aruch. And see above gloss 6.
l) However it is permitted to drink coffee and likewise chocolate of gentiles in the gentile's house, for there is no bishul goyim in it. Peri Chadash there. Lechem HaPanim there. Binyamin Zeev there. Shulchan Gavoah there. Chokhmat Adam there. Zivchei Tzedek gloss 9, and he wrote unlike responsum Panim Me'irot volume 2 siman 62 that forbade — Pitchei Teshuvah brought it gloss 1, and wrote see there Zivchei Tzedek that thus they practiced in Baghdad provinces, yar'eich elokecha, that they drink coffee and tea in the gentile's house and no one protests — end of his words. And see above siman 113 gloss 21 and letter 22.
