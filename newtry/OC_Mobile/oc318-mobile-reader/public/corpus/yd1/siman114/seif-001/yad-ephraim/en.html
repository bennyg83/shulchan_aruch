See Yaavetz vol. 2 siman 142 regarding drinking coffee in the house of gentiles in a casual manner — one should not forbid because of gentile residues; but to drink coffee and chocolate in a fixed manner in the house of gentiles is certainly forbidden. And see above siman 113 in Eshel Avraham and responsum of Beit Yehudah siman 91 in what he wrote in Be'er Eshek siman 105 to permit coffee that has no concern of gentile residues, and he wrote seven reasons and they are not sufficient; and the essence of the reason is that which Peri Chadash also wrote because it is not brought to the table of kings to dip bread in it. And see in Peri Chadash siman 114 that he wrote that even if some people dip in it bread that comes in a napkin, the majority do not do so — see there. And see siman 22 regarding blessing on stam yeinam — there is no substance in his words — see there. And see in Be'er Heitev Kaf Hei chaf nun heh "one who is black".
