או של תבואה. ואין בזה איסור משום בישולי עובדי כוכבי' כי היכי דהתבואה בטילה לגבי המים לענין ברכה דשהכל הכי נמי בטילה לענין איסור בישול עובדי כוכבי'. תוספות: **** ENGLISH **** Or of grain. And there is no prohibition here because of bishul akum, for just as the grain is nullified relative to the water regarding the blessing of shehakol, so too it is nullified regarding the prohibition of bishul akum. Tosafot.

And regarding bread they were more stringent, because a person sustains himself on bread and requires more distancing — meaning because it creates more closeness; and Ran gave the reason because bread is women's work, all the more so his wedding feasts — end of quote.
