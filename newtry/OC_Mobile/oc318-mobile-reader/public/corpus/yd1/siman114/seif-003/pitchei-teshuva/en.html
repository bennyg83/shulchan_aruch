See in She'ilat Ya'avetz part 1 siman 157 that one teacher cautioned the people not to eat fresh beans that are taken from a gentile, because it was told to him that they launder them in lye in order to scour them; and he wrote, may his memory be blessed, that it is a stringency without basis, for lye gives taste for spoilage, and they do it only for the appearance of the beans that they should appear white and shiny, and appearance in such a case is certainly not a matter; and all the more so here that we do not know the truth whether it is so, and even if they do it to him they certainly do a little in the world and do not put a substantial amount inside it, rather only to remove filth — see there.
