(there seif 10 in hagahah) For whatever separates from the majority separates. Taz s.k. 14 and one may say: we decree lest he take etc. And one may say: according to what we establish that we do not say every fixed source is like half and half except when the prohibition is recognizable in its place; but when it is not recognizable we do not say every fixed source etc. If so it is difficult — behold here the prohibition is not known and recognizable; and one must say: since if we wanted to investigate and search immediately it would be possible to know and clarify the matter; if so the essence of the matter is recognizable and known; and if so well we say every fixed source etc. And now also Taz's challenge has no substance, since there is a reality that one can recognize the prohibition — there is no decree at all lest he take from the fixed source — investigate.
