I wondered why sixty is required — behold we establish in siman 134 seif 5 that wine in water is nullified in six parts, and the same applies in other beverages, for this is plain that they nullify the taste of wine more than water; and in Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe they copied here in the name of Mordechai in the name of Ra'aviah that sixty is required — from this there is no proof, for he holds like the opinion of Yesh Ma'amin that the Tur brings in siman 134 that sixty is required, but we do not establish thus; therefore certainly here too six suffices — so it appears to my view.
