All these beverages, and likewise vinegar of beer — it is forbidden to buy them from them if their price is dearer than the price of wine, for we are concerned lest wine was mixed into them. {Rama: (until there is sixty to nullify it) (Mordechai in the name of Ravyah)} In what case? When they sell in a shop. But if he sees that they draw it from the barrel, it is permitted, and we are not concerned lest wine was mixed into it, for if wine were mixed into the barrel it would spoil. {Rama: And even though they are accustomed to smear the barrels and vessels with pig fat, one need not be concerned, for it is giving taste for spoilage, and it is also nullified in sixty (Tur klal 44 and Mordechai Pesachim). Also one need not be concerned if these beverages were placed in wine vessels; and see below siman 137 regarding a place where they practiced giving blood or another prohibited substance into salt or ink whether it is permitted or forbidden.}
