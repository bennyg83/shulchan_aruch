Rashba. Shach wrote: specifically Rashba, who knew certainly that in all that land they sprinkled wine on it; but we who do not know certainly — if so we say it is impossible that there not be one who does not sprinkle, and he saves all of them. And see further there that he brings many proofs to permit; but what he wrote, even if there were threads of meat in it nullified in majority, he challenged this himself regarding this in his book Nachalat Binyamin, for behold it is a different type in a different type — see there.
