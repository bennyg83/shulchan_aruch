Types of small fish. That have no scales and are destined to grow after time, and we establish that it is permitted (as above beginning of siman 83) — Rashi, and so the Rambam explained in his commentary on the Mishnah and Bartenura. And according to this it appears that other small fish that have scales are permitted, for one can distinguish between them and the impure ones.

That we rely on the lenient side, etc. Meaning: and it is permitted to buy from all of them even from them in their houses, for each and every one — when I buy from him I say this one did not mix; and even if the majority's practice is to mix, since it is not known with certainty that they mixed, we say that the one who did not mix saves all of them, as we say at the end of Ha'ishah shenis'armelah regarding a city captured by an army (meaning: troops) — all the priestesses are forbidden, and if there is there one hidden one, meaning one who is presumed, she saves all the priestesses; and the same law applies to every rabbinic prohibition like it. And see below siman 129 end of seif 11 in the hagahah; and it requires study in the responsa of Maharil siman 86 regarding what he wrote there why they permit flour of gentiles that some of them leaven with lees, etc., until one must say that we establish [leniency] at the place of the fixed source — see there.
We follow the majority. For if the majority certainly mix, everything that separates from them is forbidden; and if the majority certainly does not mix, everything that separates from them is permitted. But it is forbidden to buy from them in their houses even though it is known that the majority certainly does not mix; and if he bought from them it is forbidden, for every fixed source is like half and half — as above siman 110 seif 3. And the same applies if the gentiles who sell have shops — behold it becomes a fixed source in their shops, and we do not say whatever separates from the majority separates as majority, except when the gentile goes to the market and sells — investigate.
