And if it is known etc. Like Chizkiyah there, for the halachah is not like a student in place of his teacher. Rif and Rosh.
And he sells all of it except etc. As he wrote Avodah Zarah 74a, halachah lema'aseh etc. (Collected) And he sells all of it etc. For what he wrote "all of it forbidden for benefit" — that is according to the plain Mishnah; but according to Rabban Gamliel he sells etc. Terumat HaDeshen chapter 7 seif 2 in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, and he disagrees with Ra'avad, and see siman 134 seif 5 in the gloss, and Mahariv and Rashba hold that since in the Gemara it was not mentioned to distinguish, we do not establish like the Yerushalmi, and so too there several times (end quote).
And if it is known etc. As he wrote in chapter 2 of Kesubos (27a) "if there is a hiding place" etc., and they were lenient regarding a captive, and the same applies here for it is only d'rabbanan — we rely leniently, as he wrote in Avodah Zarah 70b "say he took a bribe" etc.
And if it is known etc., but etc. As he wrote (Pesachim 9b and Rashi there) regarding ten stores, and in the end of chapter 10 of Kesubos 15: "if they go" etc., "to accompany" etc.; and in Tosefta chapter 4 of Demai: "The whole city sells certainly [tithed produce] and one [seller of] demai sold, and one bought and does not know from which of them he bought — this one is forbidden, he separates terumah and maaser" etc., "The whole city sells tithed produce and one sells what is not tithed and one bought" etc. "this one is forbidden", "The whole city sells ritually slaughtered meat and one" etc. "this one is forbidden", "The whole city sells pure wine and one" etc. "this one is forbidden"; in what case? In a time when he took and does not know from which of them he took; but one who buys from the market — we follow the majority. But here it is not applicable that one saves [the others], since it is known with certainty that they mix [wine].
