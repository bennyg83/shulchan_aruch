Like this in rabbinic prohibitions. See in the responsum of Noda B'Yehuda part 4 siman 34 regarding earthenware pots that when a crack comes in them they give them to a craftsman to repair and to plaster in the place of the crack so it should not leak; and now it became known that one craftsman does this plastering with sharp cheese, and he wrote that one may permit from stam since it also became known that there are craftsmen who do it with lime alone without cheese, as Rama wrote here. For here too there is no Torah prohibition, for a pot's way is to be used with abundance (see in the responsum of Pri Tevuah siman 23), and this cheese that is in the place of the crack is a small matter; and according to Rashba and the Mechaber above at end of seif 99 it is permitted; and even according to the dissenting views there is only a rabbinic prohibition; and therefore we rely on the lenient position. And also they should not use them until after twenty-four hours, for one should also combine the permissive views in siman 103 seif 5 — see there.
