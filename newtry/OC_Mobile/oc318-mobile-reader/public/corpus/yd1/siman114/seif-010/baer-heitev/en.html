Chalita. It is a type of fruit called in a foreign tongue, and they cut its crate with a knife, and it is forbidden because of the greasiness of the knife. Rashi.
Fish. That have no scales and are destined to grow after time, and we establish that it is permitted; and it appears that other small fish that have scales are permitted, for one can distinguish between them and the impure ones. Shach.

Turei Zahav wrote, and it deals with when one does not know which gentile does not mix, for then on each and every one I say this one did not mix, and even if the majority's practice is to mix, the one who does not mix saves all of them; but if it is known which gentile does not mix, he does not save the others whose practice of the majority is to mix.
