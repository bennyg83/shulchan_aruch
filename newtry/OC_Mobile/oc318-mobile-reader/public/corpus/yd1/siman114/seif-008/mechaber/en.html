Their capers, caplota'ot, and pickled locusts are permitted when he sees that they take them from the storehouse; but those sold in a shop are forbidden lest wine was poured on them. And likewise their pickled olives are permitted even when very soft until the pit slips out, and we are not concerned lest wine was poured on them, provided they were not cut with their knife. {Rama: For after they are sharp [with vinegar] they absorbed from the knife; but if pickled in vessels of a non-Jew they are permitted, for after there is water with them their sharpness is already nullified, and likewise anything similar (Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chayim siman 247); and see above siman 96.}
