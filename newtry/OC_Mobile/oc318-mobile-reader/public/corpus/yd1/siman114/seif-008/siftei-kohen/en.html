That they are sharp, etc. Issur VeHeter wrote, klal 38 din 9 and 15, that is specifically that they were cut after they were salted, for then they are sharp — see there. And that which we do not say the taste is nullified in the first two or three and nullified in majority was explained above siman 96 seif 20 — see there.
That there is water with them. Specifically when the majority is water; but if the olives are the majority, behold they are in their sharpness and are forbidden even though the vessels are not ben yomo, because he makes it for improvement — as above siman 95 seif 2 in the hagahah.
