Capresin. Meaning: guard for fruit of the caper species, and they are normally eaten raw; and some boil them. And caplutos are cut and eaten as they are, raw.
Lest he sprinkled wine on them. And even in a place where wine is dear, nevertheless we are concerned that he acted in measure to make for them a good aroma with a little wine that he sprinkles — we are concerned for this.
