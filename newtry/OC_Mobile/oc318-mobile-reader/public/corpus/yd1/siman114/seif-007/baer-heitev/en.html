Cooking or purging. Meaning: emission from the vessels; and the reason is that they are eaten as they are, raw; and meat and fat spoil in oil and honey, as above siman 103 seif 4. Alternatively, their vessels are not ben yomo, and see above siman 122. And the same applies to hot water, which is also eaten raw, and further that it does not change from its created state by fire — Rashi.
