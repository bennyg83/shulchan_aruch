There is not sixty in the beverage. But if there are sixty it is permitted; even though something that sets forbidden in any amount, as above siman 87 seif 11, nevertheless one could say that lees are not called something that sets, and they are not comparable to cheese, for there the prohibition is visible to the eye because the milk congealed on account of them; but not so here where the prohibition is not visible at all, for behold the lees only cause the beer to ferment on account of them, and there is no distinction between before fermentation and after fermentation except regarding taste; but regarding appearance — as its appearance was then, so now. R' Pinchas in responsa manuscript which they brought in responsa in Orach Chaim siman 448 s.k. 11. And for this reason he permitted wine lees that were placed into a barrel of beer and there are sixty — see there further on this.
