f) There. But if he entered the gentile's house and drank casually, incidentally, it is permitted. Explanation: we require both that it be casual, meaning not fixed, and incidental, meaning he is not accustomed to this. Peri Chadash gloss 4. And it appears that but with fixed intent, even once, is forbidden; and with regularity, even casually, is forbidden. And so too Knesset HaGedolah in Hagah Beit Yosef gloss Yoreh De'ah. And regularity means three times; but once or twice casually is permitted.
