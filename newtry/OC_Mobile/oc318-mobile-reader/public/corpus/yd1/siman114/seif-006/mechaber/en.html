One must be careful to inspect and inquire regarding beer and honey beverage that gentiles make nowadays whether they put wine lees into them. {Rama: And if their practice is to put lees into it, it is forbidden to buy from them if there is not sixty of the beverage against the lees (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot and Mordechai); and this is when it is not made for its taste, for when made for its taste even in a thousand it is not nullified (Tur klal 22 and Beit Yosef end of seif 134 in the name of responsum of Rashba; and see above seif 123 s.k. 21).}
