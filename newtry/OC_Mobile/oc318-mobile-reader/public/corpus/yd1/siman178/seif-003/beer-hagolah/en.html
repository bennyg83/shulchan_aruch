In chapter 11, Rambam writes that one leaves the hair loose behind, based on the Baraita in Bava Kamma 83a and Sotah 49: one who cuts his hair in the komei style follows the ways of the Amorites. He further writes that anyone who performs one of these practices or anything similar is lashed. Tur cites this. It is not a prohibition that includes several unrelated matters, because all of them are prohibited under one category. Beit Yosef writes that although Rambam did not write this in his Commentary on the Mishnah, his words in Mishneh Torah are more authoritative than his words in the Commentary on the Mishnah.
