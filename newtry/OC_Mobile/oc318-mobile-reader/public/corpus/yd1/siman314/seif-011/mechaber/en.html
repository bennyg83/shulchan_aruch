One does not show the bechor to an Israelite until a kohen is with him, lest the expert say to him "it is a blemish" and it is permitted to slaughter for it and he go and slaughter it for himself and not give it to the kohen—for although he is not suspected of eating kodashim outside, he is suspected of stealing the gifts of kehunah; therefore if he was a sage known to be scrupulous with himself, they show it to him (and likewise if) the blemish was evident to all, such as that its hand or foot was cut off—since he brought it to the expert sage, he is in the presumption of being scrupulous with himself; therefore they show it to him even though a kohen is not with him:
