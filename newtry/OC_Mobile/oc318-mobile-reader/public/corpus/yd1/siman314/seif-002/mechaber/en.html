For all blemishes that are fit to come through human action, the shepherd is believed to say they fell by themselves and were not made intentionally, and they slaughter for them. In what matter is this said? When the shepherd was an Israelite and the bechor is in the hand of the kohen; but if the shepherd was a kohen and the bechor is still in the hand of its Israelite owner—behold he is not believed and they suspect him that perhaps he inflicted it in order that he give it to him. (And some say a kohen shepherd is believed regarding an Israelite's bechor, and one may be lenient) (Tur in the name of the Rosh and Ramban):
