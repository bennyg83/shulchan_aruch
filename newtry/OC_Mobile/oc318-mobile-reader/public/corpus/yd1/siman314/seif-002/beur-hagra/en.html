In what case, etc. He ruled like the first version, because the sugya there 18b follows the first version. See Tosafot 35b s.v. “meimar,” who pressed this; Lechem Mishneh.
And some say, etc. For we rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, as stated there, “and the halakhah,” etc., and he disputes the first tanna even regarding a shepherd. See Rashi s.v. “regarding another's,” whether, etc., and all the more so according to the second version; see Rashi s.v. “regarding another's, a kohen,” etc., and the Rosh there. But the Rambam explains that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel disputes only what the Mishnah lacked, namely a kohen regarding a kohen. See the Rosh. It appears to me that this is why the Rambam ruled like the first version: in the baraita it says that his son and daughter are believed, and his wife is not believed only because she is like his own body; otherwise she would be believed. This shows that we are not concerned for a gift of food, unlike the second version, for Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel agrees with the first tanna regarding a shepherd, as I explained.
