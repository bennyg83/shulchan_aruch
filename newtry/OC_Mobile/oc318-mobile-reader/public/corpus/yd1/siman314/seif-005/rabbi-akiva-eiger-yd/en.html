(Siman 314 seif 5) She is believed to say, "in my presence," etc. Raanach responsa I siman 22 inferred from this lengthy wording, since it did not simply say "even a woman is believed," that it comes to teach that she is believed only when testifying from herself, but not when testifying from another, for that would contain two weaknesses: woman's testimony and testimony from another witness.
