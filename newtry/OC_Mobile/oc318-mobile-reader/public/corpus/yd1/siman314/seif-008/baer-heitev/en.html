A Jew. The Taz writes that according to the Rambam the case is one in which we know which Jew gave the firstborn to the kohen, but we are uncertain whether he gave it to him unblemished or already blemished. The kohen is therefore afraid that the Jew may be questioned. Nekudot HaKesef writes that this is incorrect. On the contrary, the Rambam holds that he need not identify a particular Jew. Even when we do not know who the Jew was, he is afraid that he will be asked which Jew gave it to him, and that person will then be questioned.
