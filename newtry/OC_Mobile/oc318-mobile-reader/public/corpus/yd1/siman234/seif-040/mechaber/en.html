One who annuls his wife's and daughter's vows must express it with his lips; and if he annulled in his heart it is not annulled. But one who nullifies need not express it with his lips — he nullifies in his heart alone and compels her. {Rama: All this is the Rambam's view; but according to the first view there is no distinction between annulment and nullification, and in both he is not annulled or nullified (Tur, Rosh, Tosafos, Ran, and Ra'avad) except when he annuls or nullifies in his heart and said to her "take and eat" — and such is the essential [law].}
