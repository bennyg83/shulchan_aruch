The husband may serve as her agent. The reason is that his wife is like his own body, as though she herself were present. Another person cannot serve as agent, because the one who made the vow must come before beit din, as explained in siman 228, seif 17.
Three already assembled. The Ran on 8a explains that since it is clear that the one who vowed should appear before beit din, and the husband’s agency is only a leniency, the Sages permitted it only if the three are already assembled. They did not permit him to take the additional step of gathering them initially for this purpose. The Tur first cites a different reason in the name of R’ Eliezer of Metz, according to his own approach there.
