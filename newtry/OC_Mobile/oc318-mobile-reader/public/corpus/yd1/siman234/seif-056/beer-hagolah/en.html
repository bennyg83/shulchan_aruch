The Tur in the name of Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilchot Nedarim, and Semag. The Ran likewise writes in the name of Ramban, from Rami bar Chama's question, 'If you say a husband may annul without hearing,' Nedarim 73a. Since the Gemara presents this as a conditional premise and connects it to Rabbi Zeira, they understand that this is the accepted ruling.
