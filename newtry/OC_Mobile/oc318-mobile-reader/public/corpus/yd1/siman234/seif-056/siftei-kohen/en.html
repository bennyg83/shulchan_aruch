The husband becomes an agent, etc. Although above in siman 228, seif 15, we rule that one cannot appoint an agent to seek release from his vow, a husband is different because his wife is considered like his own body. For the same reason, the husband cannot join two others to release his wife's vows, whereas a father may join to release his daughter's vows.
But he may not gather them. Perhaps she does not want the matter publicized through his gathering them. Ateret Zekenim writes that if she authorized him also to gather them and obtain release, he may do so. See Bach.
