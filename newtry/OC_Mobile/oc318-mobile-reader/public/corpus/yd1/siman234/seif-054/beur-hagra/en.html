How so, etc. There is a scribal error here. It should read: “How so?” etc., meaning, etc. He cannot annul for her, because this is like making his own vow dependent on hers. If he said, “I am hereby a nazir, and what,” etc., he may annul hers while his remains in force. If he said, “I am hereby a female nazirite,” etc. Lechem Mishneh and the other later authorities already noted this error.
