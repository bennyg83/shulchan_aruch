If he vowed for himself and caused her to vow like him and concluded in his heart to cause her to vow, and she said amen — behold he cannot annul. And if he vowed and caused her to vow by way of a question to know what is in her heart, such as if he said to her "Are you willing in this vow to be like me or not?" and she said amen — behold he annuls for her. How so? If he said to her "Behold I am a nazir and you" — meaning "and you are a nazir like me" — and she said amen, he cannot annul for her. If he said to her "Behold I am a nazir — what do you say? Are you a nazir like me?" and she said amen — behold he annuls. And if he annulled for her, his is void, because this is as if he made his vow dependent on her vow. If she said to him "Behold I am a naziress and you," and he said amen — he cannot annul.
