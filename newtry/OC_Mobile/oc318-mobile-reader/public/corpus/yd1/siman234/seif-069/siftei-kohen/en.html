'Konam, I will not derive benefit from you.' Meaning, 'I will not derive benefit from you.'
And these are matters between husband and wife. Tur explains that it is degrading to him that she becomes forbidden to benefit from her father or his father if she acts for him, and likewise that she may not act for either father lest she become forbidden to her husband. Ateret Zekenim writes similarly. It is difficult why this latter point must itself be classified as a marital matter, since the resulting prohibition to her husband is independently a marital matter or personal affliction regardless of what condition she attached. Rather, the ruling teaches that he may annul before the vow takes effect, and for that advance annulment the condition itself must involve a matter between husband and wife, as explained above in seif katan 45 in the names of Rabbeinu Yerucham and Ran.
