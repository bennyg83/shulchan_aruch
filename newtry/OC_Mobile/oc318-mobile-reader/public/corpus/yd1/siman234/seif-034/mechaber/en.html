All annulment depends on the time of the vow. How so? If at the time of the vow she was married, even though it will not take effect until she is widowed — such as if she said "konam this loaf upon me after thirty days" and he annulled for her and she was widowed in the interim — it is annulled. And similarly if she said "konam this loaf upon me after I am widowed" — behold it is annulled.
