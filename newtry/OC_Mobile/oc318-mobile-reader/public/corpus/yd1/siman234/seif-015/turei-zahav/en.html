And the father annulled before he died. Since the annulment was not completed during the father’s lifetime, even the father’s annulment is void, and the betrothed man must annul the entire vow.
The betrothed man cannot annul. The father’s authority is not transferred to the betrothed man. The Gemara derives from Scripture that only when the husband dies without annulling does his authority return to the father, but not the reverse.
