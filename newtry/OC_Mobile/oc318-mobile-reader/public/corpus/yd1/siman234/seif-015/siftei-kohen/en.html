And the father annulled before he died. Since the father's death now removes her from his authority, the betrothed husband cannot annul, because he can act only in partnership with the father.
And the father did not manage to hear, etc. This states the ordinary case, because had the father heard he presumably would have annulled. But even if he heard and failed to annul, although the betrothed husband annulled during the father's lifetime, the vow is not annulled and the husband cannot annul now after the father's death, because he can never annul without partnership. Tur also implies this.
