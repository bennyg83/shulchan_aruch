And proof from that which is stated regarding "if the husband died" etc., and they establish in the gemara "even if the betrothed heard," and similarly there in chapter 68 "when do" etc.; and therefore when both heard as stated above in seif 11 — if so, the same applies to the first clause "if the father died" etc. But the Rosh and Ran hold that even when he did not have time, the father returns and annuls "he did not have time," as stated in Shulchan Aruch siman 234; therefore they explain that whenever he heard and did not have time, the words of the baraita are all for amplification; and so too in Tur; nevertheless here for practical law there is no difference in this matter:
