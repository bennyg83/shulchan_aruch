"But if you don't hear him." "And thou shalt not hear at all."
However if, etc. So it is proven in the Gemara (daf 72). However, the Rosh concludes there in his commentary that he must say, "they are hereby nullified when I hear," and in another manner the nullification is not effective. Regarding a father, his daughter must be in his domain when he hears. So too appears from Ran there.
