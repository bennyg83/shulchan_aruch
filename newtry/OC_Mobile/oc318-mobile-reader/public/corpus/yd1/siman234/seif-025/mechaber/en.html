The husband (or the father) can annul without hearing if he is mentally competent; but if he does not hear and he is speaking, he cannot annul. {Rama: And some dispute that even a mentally competent person cannot annul until he hears (Tur and Rosh and Semak); however if he annulled and afterward heard, it is an annulment (Rabbeinu Yerucham and in siman 43).}
