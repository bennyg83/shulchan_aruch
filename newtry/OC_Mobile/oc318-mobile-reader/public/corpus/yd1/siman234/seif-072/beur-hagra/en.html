Before his cattle, etc.; and they are not, etc. See Rosh there, s.v. shelo eten, and Ran, s.v. shelo. Ran follows Rif’s text, while Rosh follows Rashi’s. See Rosh, Ketubot chapter 5, section 26. Tosafot have a different text; see Ketubot 61b, s.v. lo, etc. Rambam reads in Nedarim: “water before your animal and straw before your cattle.” Beit Yosef explains that Rambam follows Rif’s text and holds that she is obligated specifically to place straw, but not water, since animals are ordinarily watered at the river and “all the honor of the king’s daughter is within,” etc. Shulchan Aruch here mentions only cattle, following Rif, as he also rules in Even HaEzer 80:6, even if she brought in no maidservants, etc.
