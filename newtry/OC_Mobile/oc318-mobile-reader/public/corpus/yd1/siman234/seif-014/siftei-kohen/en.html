And he did not hear, etc. Had he heard and then divorced her, the divorce would be equivalent to confirmation, as stated nearby.
And he divorced her, and afterward, etc., that vow, etc. This is difficult, because according to the Mechaber he cannot annul even vows made now. Tur presents no difficulty, because Beit Yosef and the later authorities explain that 'he married her' there means only that she was delivered but did not enter the chuppah. The Mechaber, however, holds that delivery is like nisuin. It appears that the ruling means not only that he obviously cannot annul vows made now, but even the earlier vow made while she was under him cannot be annulled now by arguing that since he could have annulled it then he should be able to do so now.
