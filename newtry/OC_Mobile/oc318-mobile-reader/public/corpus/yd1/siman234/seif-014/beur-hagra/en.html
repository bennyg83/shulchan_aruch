He married her and she vowed, etc. Mishnah, Nedarim 69a, which the Gemara on 72a establishes as referring to a married woman. See Ran 69a, s.v. nadrah, etc. It must concern one who returned and became betrothed again but did not remarry, for otherwise what novelty would there be? Even if he had never divorced her after marriage, the husband cannot annul vows from the earlier betrothal, as explained above. Likewise, the Gemara’s phrase, “to include where he delivered her,” and Rosh’s explanation there, s.v. zeh haklal, must concern such a case. See Ran 72a, s.v. amri, etc.
And he did not hear, etc. For if he had heard, even during the betrothal, the rule is as stated below in seif 20.
