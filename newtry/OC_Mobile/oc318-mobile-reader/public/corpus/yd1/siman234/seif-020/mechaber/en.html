That which we say she returns to the father's authority is to annul what she vowed under the betrothed — and this hearing is specifically when the husband died from the betrothal; but if he divorced her, we are uncertain whether divorce is like upholding; therefore if he heard her vow and divorced her, the father cannot annul, neither alone nor in partnership with another betrothed, and not even with the first if he took her back on the day of hearing. But if the betrothed did not hear her vow and divorced her — her law is like one who did not hear and died for all matters.
