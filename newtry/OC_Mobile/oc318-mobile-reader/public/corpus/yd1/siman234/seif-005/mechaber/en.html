If she married [someone forbidden by] a lav violation or [a violation carrying] lashes for a positive commandment, and her husband annulled for her — behold it is annulled. From when she becomes betrothed until she enters the chuppah, the husband does not annul alone, even if he is obligated in her sustenance; nor does the father annul alone — rather both annul in partnership, even vows that were upon her before she was betrothed. And if one annulled without the other, it is not annulled; however the other's portion remains weakened, and the lighter prohibition is weakened so that there are no lashes in it. And both must hear on one day; but if one heard on one day and annulled, and the other heard on another day, he cannot annul. And some say that each one annuls on the day he heard, even though they did not hear on one day. (And if she was betrothed and has no father, the betrothed cannot annul.) (Rashba siman 166)
