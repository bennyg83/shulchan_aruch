(Shulchan Aruch seif 21) Except day they heard. Rosh Yosef siman 7 — vow takes effect after thirty days and husband heard day she vowed and was silent — cannot annul day vow takes effect; see Beit Yosef this siman s.k. 65 examine; Bach s.k. 66 wrote reason husband can annul before takes effect since must annul day of hearing — therefore can annul before vow takes effect — if not annul now cannot again — see there.
