Alone. Even if father annulled before she matured and husband did not annul — he cannot annul again when she matures; even per some above seif 5 each annuls day he heard — when father could annul without husband hearing — not here; maturity like death (below seif 15); likewise vow after maturity — betrothed cannot annul whether betrothed before or after maturity — once mature left father's authority betrothed cannot annul — only annuls in partnership — end quote Shach.
