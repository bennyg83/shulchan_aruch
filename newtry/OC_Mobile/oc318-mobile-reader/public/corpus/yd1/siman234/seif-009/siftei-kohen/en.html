That they did not hear, etc. This is not exact. Even if the father annulled before she reached majority but the husband did not, the husband cannot later annul after she reaches majority. Even according to the opinion above in seif 5 that each may annul on the day he hears, that applies only when the father is still capable of annulling on the day the husband hears. Here, reaching majority is comparable to the father's death discussed below in seif 15. Once she leaves her father's authority, the betrothed husband cannot annul. Bach writes likewise. The same applies to a vow made after she reaches majority. The betrothed husband cannot annul whether he betrothed her before or after majority, because once she has left her father's authority he can annul only in partnership, as explained below.
