She vowed, etc. See Ran there, 83b, end of s.v. amri, and certainly concerning attendance at a wedding feast, etc. See the Mishnah in Ketubot 71a, “One who forbids,” etc., and the Gemara there: “Granted, regarding a wedding feast,” etc. The Gemara establishes the Mishnah where she herself vowed, etc., and he holds, etc. Ran writes there that the latter clause of the Mishnah must likewise concern such a case, and that all these are forms of self-affliction, in accordance with Rabbeinu Yerucham. The Tur, however, writes in siman 235 that none of these is self-affliction. See what is written there in seif 3. Tur and Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer 74:4, rule similarly.
