The father, etc. A father and a husband may annul only vows involving personal affliction or matters between husband and wife, as explained below in seifim 55 and 58. A sage, however, may release all of her vows.
The vows, etc. The same applies to the oaths of his daughter and wife, as below in seif 37.
A maiden. Meaning, she is twelve years and one day old and has developed two pubic hairs.
Even if she vowed, etc. A husband may likewise annul his wife's vows even if she vowed with the consent of the public. This applies even if the husband waived his authority and gave her permission to vow with the consent of the public. Rosh. It appears that the same applies if a father gave his daughter such permission. Perishah writes likewise. See above, siman 228, seif katan 74.
She reached majority. Meaning, six months after developing two pubic hairs when she was twelve years and one day old.
