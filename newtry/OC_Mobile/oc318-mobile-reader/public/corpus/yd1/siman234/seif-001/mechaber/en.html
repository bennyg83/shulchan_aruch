When the father and the husband annul [vows], and when they annul together. And in it are 72 se'ifim: The father annuls his daughter's vows for all the time that she is a minor or a na'arah, even if she vowed with the consent of the many; when she becomes a bogeret (meaning: when a woman has passed twelve years and one day, then if she brought two [pubic] hairs she is called a na'arah until six months afterward, when she is called a bogeret, meaning full [growth of] hair, for the Jerusalem Targum translation of ish sha'ir is gever b'gar) or marries, he can no longer annul.
