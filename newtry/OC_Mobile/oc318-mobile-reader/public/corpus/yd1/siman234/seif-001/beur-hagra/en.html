All this, etc., or a maiden. Mishnah, Niddah 47a.
She reached majority. Ibid., and Mishnah, Nedarim 70a.
Or she married. Mishnah, Ketubot 48b; see there, 49a, concerning the teaching of the school of Rabbi Yishmael, etc. This is also clear in many places. Its primary source is the Sifrei on, “And if she shall indeed be married to a man,” etc. Scripture did not come to distinguish except with respect to whose authority she is under. So long as she is in her father’s house, her father and husband annul her vows jointly; once she marries, her father no longer annuls her vows.
