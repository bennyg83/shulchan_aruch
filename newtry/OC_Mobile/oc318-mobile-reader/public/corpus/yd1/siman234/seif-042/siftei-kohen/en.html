And when he saw, etc., 'I intended in my heart to annul,' etc. It appears that the same rule applies if he says, 'I annulled it for her earlier.' If, however, after witnesses testify he says, 'I annul it for her now,' he is believed. Although in monetary law such a claim would not be believed, as explained in Choshen Mishpat, siman 79, seif 5, there the reason is that he has established himself as a denier regarding that money. That concern does not apply to vows. If he claims that he annulled earlier, however, he cannot be believed after initially saying that she never vowed. See above, siman 232, seif katan 25.
