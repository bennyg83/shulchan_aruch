The wording of the Tur, based upon Mishnah Nedarim 71b. It says 'died' rather than 'was divorced' because the ruling must apply whether before or after the father heard. With divorce, the Gemara is uncertain whether it is equivalent to confirmation, and the doubt is treated stringently. The father would therefore not be able to annul. This requires comparison with the ruling above in seif 5 according to Rambam. See Taz.
