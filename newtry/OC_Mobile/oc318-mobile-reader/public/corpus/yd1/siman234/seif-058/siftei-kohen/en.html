Some say, etc. Bach rules leniently, preferring the dissenting opinion because it is stated without reason or proof. He overlooked Rama's discussion in Bedek HaBayit and Kesef Mishneh, where Rama rules according to the first opinion that a daughter is like a wife and supports it at length against Mizrachi. Tzedah LaDerech on Parashat Matot and Rabbeinu Yerucham at the end of netiv 27 also agree. This is the primary ruling.
