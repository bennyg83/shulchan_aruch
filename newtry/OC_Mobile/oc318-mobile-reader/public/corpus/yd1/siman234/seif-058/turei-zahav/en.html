Also father, etc. Thus Sifrei — from between man and wife and between father and daughter compare father to husband; some permit — Rambam wrote we do not hold this Sifrei since not in Babylonian and Yerushalmi Talmud; plain verse implies only husband needs self-affliction not other vows not father; some dispute — Rabbi Yechiel in Tur reconciles Talmud and Sifrei — Sifrei after betrothed since father does not annul with husband except self-affliction — even divorced returned to his authority father cannot annul what could not before — plain likewise between them alone like self-affliction for father — Sifrei compares father to husband — comparison not half; not as Perishah only self-affliction — labored reason; seems as we said; Rabbi Yechiel cited self-affliction from Mishnah last Nedarim — not only husband self-affliction — truly likewise between them alone; Rosh explicitly there.
