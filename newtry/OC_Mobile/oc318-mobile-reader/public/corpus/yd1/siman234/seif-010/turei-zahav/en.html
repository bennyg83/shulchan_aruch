Until he brings her in, he cannot annul. At that point she had already left the father’s authority through her marriage, and the husband cannot annul vows that preceded the marriage.
Vows made from the time I betrothed you shall be annulled. Although the betrothed man may also annul vows made before the betrothal together with the father, as stated in seif 2, the law speaks here of vows made “from the time I betrothed you,” because presumably the father had already annulled earlier vows before the betrothal, when he alone had authority.
