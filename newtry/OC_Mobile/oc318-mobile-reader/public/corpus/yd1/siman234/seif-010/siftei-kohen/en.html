From when I betrothed you, etc. Even though an arus can nullify even vows she vowed before she was betrothed, as above in seif 5, nevertheless the question posed to the sage is only to annul what she vowed after she was betrothed. Before then, presumably her father nullified for her, since it was in his power to nullify alone. Perishah. So too Bach wrote, and he further answered that only when the arus heard her vow can he nullify earlier vows; here, however, he did not know and did not hear what she had vowed about. And according to the dissenting view below in seif 25, nullification can work for when he will hear, even though he has not heard now; but here, for vows she vowed before she was betrothed, it does not help.
