He cannot annul. A husband cannot annul vows she made previously. Tur. Although a betrothed husband can annul earlier vows, as above in seif 5, that is because he and the father annul jointly. Once she has left her father's authority, the husband cannot annul them alone.
From the time I betrothed you, etc. Although a betrothed husband can annul even vows made before betrothal, as above in seif 5, the Torah scholar is required to revoke only vows made after betrothal. Earlier vows were presumably annulled by her father, since he then had sole authority. Perishah and Bach write likewise. Bach further answers that a betrothed husband can annul prior vows only when he heard the vow, whereas here he did not know which matter she had vowed about. Even according to the dissenting opinion below in seif 25, advance annulment can take effect when he later hears the vow, but it does not work for vows made before betrothal. See there.
