The husband cannot, etc. See Ran 8b, s.v. shema minah, and 81b, s.v. vegarsinan, etc. Rosh also writes so there. Although in the first chapter Rosh is uncertain whether the husband may participate together with other judges, even according to Rabbi Yehudah, Beit Yosef infers from the Tur’s language that even this is forbidden. The same appears from Rosh’s commentary there, s.v. haveh lei, etc.
