And there are those who say that bathing and adornment, etc. The Mishnah records a dispute. The first Tanna holds these are vows of self-affliction, while R’ Yosi holds they are matters between husband and wife. The Ran and Rosh rule like the first Tanna. The Rosh cites the Ramban as ruling like R’ Yosi. The Rambam also rules like R’ Yosi but distinguishes between them, ruling like the first Tanna regarding bathing and like R’ Yosi regarding adornment. This is difficult, and the Beit Yosef labored to explain it. Therefore, the Shulchan Aruch did not cite the Rambam’s exact distinction, but only the Ramban’s view. My revered father-in-law was troubled that the Shulchan Aruch equated bathing and adornment according to the alternative opinion, which appeared to follow no one, and he proposed a textual error. In my view, the Shulchan Aruch intended only to cite the Ramban. Seif 60 likewise depends upon the dispute whether the halacha follows the first Tanna or R’ Yosi.
