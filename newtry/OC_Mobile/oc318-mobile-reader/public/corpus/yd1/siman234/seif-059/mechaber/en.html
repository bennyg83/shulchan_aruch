What are matters that contain inuy nefesh? Such as bathing and adorning — such as if she swore not to bathe or not to adorn herself, or if she said "the benefit of bathing and adorning is konam for me if I bathe or adorn myself" — even if she did not specify except bathing and adorning of that day, and it is possible she will not bathe or adorn herself that day and will not become forbidden — it is inuy nefesh even though the vow takes effect only for one day. And some say that bathing and adorning are matters between him and her alone. (See se'if 73.) {Rama: And all this applies only to adorning the face; but adorning below, for everyone it is not a matter between him and her alone. (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Nissim)}
