He must intend for the name of the woman he annuls for and for the name of the vow he annuls. How so? His wife vowed and he thought his daughter vowed (for so they told him) (according to one version in the Gemara), or the reverse; or she vowed [to abstain] from figs and he thought she vowed from grapes (for so they told him), or the reverse — and he annulled for her with this understanding — it is not an annulment unless it became known to him within kedei dibur and he also consented to this annulment.
