To make agent. Written woman shall confirm, etc. — not his agent; though everywhere agent like person — here different verse revealed; Tur concluded even vows already vowed — wonder plain — agent better than himself annul until she vows; Gemara all vows my wife until I come from place — annul — taught woman shall confirm and annul — Rabbi Yoshiah; Rabbi Yonathan found everywhere agent like person — implies Rabbi Yoshiah forbade only agent does two things — hearing vow and annulment; one of them not taught; Tur — perhaps agent two things — hearing and afterward annul; even if he heard and agent only annuls — baraita teaches Rabbi Yonathan's power permits even this.
