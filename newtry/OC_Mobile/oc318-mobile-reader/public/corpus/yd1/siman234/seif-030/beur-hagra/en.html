(Supplement) There is no, etc. He did not write it in the language of the Gemara: "one who says to an agent: all vows, etc." — because in such a case all agree he cannot dissolve [the vow], for their dispute is only according to Rabbi Elazar's position, as written in Nazir 12b — and we rule like the Sages. See the Rosh in Nedarim there. (End.) Not for confirmation. As stated there: "her husband shall confirm it, etc."
