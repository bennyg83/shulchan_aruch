According to Rambam, etc. This resolves the difficulty raised above by Ran and Rosh. The statement that even if he says, “It is void, and eat,” he must still nullify it in his heart applies where she does not actually eat. If she eats, he need not say anything. This is the baraita’s meaning: “Beit Hillel say, either,” etc. See Rambam’s commentary to Nedarim there: “But they said, a man should not say to his wife,” etc. This is why Rambam writes, “she may eat,” etc. Lechem Mishneh explains similarly.
