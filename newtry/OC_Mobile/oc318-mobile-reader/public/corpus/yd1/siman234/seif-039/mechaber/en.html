As long as he has not annulled the vow for his wife, he cannot compel her to transgress the vow unless he said to her "take and eat" and thought in his heart the language of annulment. And according to the Rambam, one who nullifies his wife's and daughter's vows need not say anything and all vows are nullified; and what is nullification? That he compels her to do a matter she forbade herself; but annulment does not compel her — he annuls for her and leaves her: if she wishes she does, and if she wishes she does not. How so? She vowed or swore not to eat or not to drink, and he said to her "annulled for you" — behold it is annulled and she is permitted to eat and drink. If he took and gave her and said to her "take, eat; take, drink" — behold she eats and drinks and the vow is nullified by itself.
