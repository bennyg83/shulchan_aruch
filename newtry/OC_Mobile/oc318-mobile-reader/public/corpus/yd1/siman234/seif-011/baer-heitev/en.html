After. Shach: implies all while he lives can annul even after day of hearing; difficult; only per some above seif 5, not Rambam there.
And according to the Rambam. The Taz disagrees with the Mechaber and wrote that the Rambam's intent is only such as where she has a second fiancé who became betrothed to her on the same day; then the first fiancé stands in his place and the father has no power to annul without the partnership of the last fiancé; but if there is no second fiancé, the father properly annuls alone; thus he wrote; and the Beit Yosef wrote that even according to the Rambam it is specifically if he heard afterward, but if he heard during the father's lifetime, even though he did not annul during his lifetime he can annul afterward; see there:
