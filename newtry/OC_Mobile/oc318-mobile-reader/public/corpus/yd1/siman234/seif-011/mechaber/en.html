If the betrothed died while she was still a na'arah, she returns to the father's authority and he annuls, even what she vowed during the betrothed's lifetime and he heard and died — the father annuls alone on the day he heard, even if she has a yavam and he performed ma'amar upon her — the father annuls alone. And specifically when the betrothed died before they heard, or died on the day they heard and did not uphold the vow; and similarly if they annulled, the father must annul again. But if he upheld the vow before he died, or died after the day of hearing — the father cannot annul. And according to the Rambam: if the betrothed heard and annulled for her and died, and afterward the father heard — the father cannot annul.
