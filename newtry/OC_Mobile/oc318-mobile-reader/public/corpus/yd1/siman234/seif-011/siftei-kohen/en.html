"Or if the father died after the day they heard — the father cannot annul etc." It implies that all who did not die can annul even after the day they heard. It requires study — for this is only according to the "some say" above in seif 5, but not according to the Rambam there; and the Rambam teaches us here that if he died, even on the day the husband heard, the father cannot annul — consider.
And then he heard it. It means that he heard later, but he heard in his life, even though he did not violate his life, and so on in Joseph’s house, but that he would soon conclude that he had done so and that he had returned to him from this conclusion
