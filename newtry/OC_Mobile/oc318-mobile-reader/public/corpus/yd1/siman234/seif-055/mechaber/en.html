These are vows that the husband annuls: vows that contain inuy nefesh and matters between him and her alone; but vows that contain inuy nefesh — when they are released for her she is permitted in them forever; and matters between him and her alone — the release is only for himself as long as he has benefit from it, meaning as long as she is under him; and after she is divorced, as long as she has not remarried, because it is possible she will return to him; but after she remarries the vow takes effect also for him. And vows that do not contain inuy nefesh and are not between him and her alone — he cannot annul.
