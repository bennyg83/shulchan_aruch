(Shulchan Aruch seif 55) And after divorce; if vowed forbid adornment after divorce — for those holding adornment between them alone — see Ran chapter Af Al Pi page 85 — first answer cannot annul since not apply until after divorce not called between them — unlike above seif 34 there self-affliction; second answer there can annul afterward; found Yerushalmi chapter 18 Nedarim halachah 1 — even between them annulled forever; says what difference self-affliction and between them — resolves said konam my benefit on you when I leave your authority — not called between them since vow not apply until after divorce.
