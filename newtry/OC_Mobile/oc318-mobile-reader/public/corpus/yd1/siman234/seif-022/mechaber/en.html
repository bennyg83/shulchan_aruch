If his wife vowed and he upheld it for her in one of the expressions of upholding, and he thought they were not expressions of upholding, and on that day he annulled for her — his upholding is upholding. And the same law applies to one who knows he has [the power] to annul that vow she vowed but did not know he must annul on the day he heard — once the day of hearing passed he cannot annul. And if he asked about the upholding, his annulment that he annulled is an annulment and he need not annul again.
