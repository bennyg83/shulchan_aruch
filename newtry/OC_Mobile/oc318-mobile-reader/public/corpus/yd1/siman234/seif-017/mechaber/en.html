If she vowed while betrothed and the betrothed died, whether before hearing or after hearing, and the father heard and she returned and was betrothed to him on that day, even many times — the father and the last betrothed annul her vows. And if the father did not hear on that day and she was betrothed to others and heard afterward, even after many days — the father and the last betrothed annul together on the day they hear.
