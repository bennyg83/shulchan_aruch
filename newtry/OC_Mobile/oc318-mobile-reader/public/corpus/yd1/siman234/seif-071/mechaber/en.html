If she said "konam that I will act according to your word" — he annuls even though she is subjugated to him, lest he divorce her and then the vow will take effect and he will not be able to take her back; therefore we read it now as matters between him and her alone. And specifically when she said "may my hands be sanctified to your agents" (Beit Yosef, Tur, Gemara, and poskim; and see Eben HaEzer siman 81).
