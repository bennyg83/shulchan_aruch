My hands. Hands exist in world — else cannot forbid him — without specification; Beit Yosef in Tosafot — mentioned hands e.g. konam work of my hands on you — as if said my hands sanctified to their maker; see Even HaEzer siman 81 where Mechaber wrote Rambam must say my hands sanctified; Rav did not emend; Taz wrote specifically here needs annulment lest he divorce, etc.; but seif 67 saying benefit of my marital use on you and seif 73 vow not to wash — need not annul — divorce does not help except saying my hands sanctified — body not acquired only lien — if gave money for work exempt and can say not sustained and not work — sanctity removes lien — when divorces vow applies unlike body subjugated for marital use and washing — sanctity language does not remove obligation even if divorces — therefore must annul — end quote.
