The father cannot annul his portion. Although if the father had not annulled at all the betrothed husband's portion would return to the father, as above at the beginning of seif 11, the father's prior annulment makes the situation worse. The betrothed husband's portion was weakened and no longer has sufficient force to pass to the father.
