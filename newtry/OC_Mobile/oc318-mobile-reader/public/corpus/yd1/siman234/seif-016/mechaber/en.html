If her father heard and annulled for her and the betrothed did not manage to hear until the father died — the father cannot annul the betrothed's portion; and specifically if he died after the day of hearing; but if he died on the day of hearing, he returns and annuls everything together. And if she returned and was betrothed to him on that day — even though the first betrothed died after the day the father heard — he returns and annuls with the second betrothed. And some dispute this and say the father does not return and annul with the second betrothed.
