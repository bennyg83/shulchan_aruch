The father. Although an entirely unannulled vow may return to the father's authority, where the father already annulled during the betrothed's lifetime the betrothed's portion was weakened and cannot be inherited by the father. Shach.
Hearing. Meaning the husband died after the day on which the father heard. If he died on that same day, the father's earlier partial annulment is disregarded because he can now annul the whole vow. Taz.
Returned. Bach questions this according to Rambam, who requires both parties to hear on the same day. Derishah distinguishes the case where she returned and became betrothed again. Taz and Shach discuss the difficulty.
