And the get reached her hand. The Beit Yosef explains that this teaches that once the get reaches her hand, even if she is not yet divorced, such as when it was given conditionally or to take effect later, he can no longer annul her vows. This is difficult, for the next seif states this explicitly. It appears to me that this refers to one who appointed an agent to deliver the get. Even HaEzer, siman 140, explains that he may retract so long as the get has not reached her hand. During that time he may annul her vows, for she is still his wife since he can revoke the agency.
