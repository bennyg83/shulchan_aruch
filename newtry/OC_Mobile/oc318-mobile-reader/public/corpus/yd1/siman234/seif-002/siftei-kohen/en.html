Until she is divorced, etc. Bach writes that this teaches that so long as the bill of divorce has not reached her hand, even if he delivered it to an agent and cannot revoke it outside the agent's presence, she remains his wife because he can still revoke it in her presence. He may therefore annul vows made in the interim.
He may not annul for her. This implies that even after the fact the annulment is ineffective. Bach also appears to rule this way.
