That I will not benefit from people. This means “from people.” The first opinion follows the view in seif 60 that if she forbade the fruits of one individual upon herself it is self-affliction, because the halacha follows the first Tanna. The second opinion here follows the Rambam in seif 60, who rules like R’ Yosi. It is surprising, however, that the second opinion is the Rosh’s view as cited by the Tur, whereas the Rosh follows the first opinion in seif 60. This requires study. The Perishah distinguishes between expressly mentioning fruits and forbidding general benefit, but he overlooked the Gemara on 82a, which compares “the fruits of so-and-so are forbidden upon me” with “the benefit of so-and-so is forbidden upon me.”
