There is, etc. Tur.
There are those who say, etc. The Tur cites this in the name of his father, Rosh, based on Rosh’s ruling there that the law applies specifically because of self-affliction, etc. This is incorrect, because the Mishnah follows Rabbi Yosi, as explained above. Rosh himself writes so in his commentary to the Mishnah, s.v. konam she’ani, etc. Ran likewise writes this there, s.v. eino, etc. See Rosh 82b, s.v. deamar Rav Huna kulah, and Ran there.
