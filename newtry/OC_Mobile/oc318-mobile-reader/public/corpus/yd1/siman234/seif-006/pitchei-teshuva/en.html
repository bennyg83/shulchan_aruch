Husband annuls. See responsum Maharit part 1 siman 4 wrote husband can annul wife's nazirite Samson vow since nazirite Samson among self-affliction matters; though end Makkot nazirite Samson not in asking — specifically sage cannot release but husband annuls; found likewise responsum Radbaz siman 1 part 5 — see there; however Shach below siman 299 s.k. 28 brought responsum Maharit part 2 siman 45 in name of Mahari Bei Rav husband cannot annul what she vowed nazirite Samson — see there.
