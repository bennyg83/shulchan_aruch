If one confirmed, etc. These words are difficult, for the Gemara speaks only where one first annulled and confirmation then interrupted between the two annulments. See Ran and Rosh there. The wording of Shulchan Aruch is copied from the Tur, and the Tur refers to what he wrote above: if one annulled without the other, the vow is not released; if one confirmed, etc. Rambam, however, writes in chapter 13 that one confirmed for her, the other annulled, and afterward the one who confirmed obtained release from his confirmation. His text of the Gemara was reversed: “one confirmed and one annulled, and then the one who confirmed,” etc. His reading is preferable, for according to our text the words “and then the one who confirmed” are unnecessary, since the discussion already concerns him.
He cannot annul, etc. This accords with Rambam’s view, cited by Ran there, s.v. vechazar. Rosh also agrees in his commentary and rulings.
