The father and husband do not annul before she vows; but once she vowed they annul even though the vow has not yet taken effect, and even not in her presence. {Rama: And some say this is specifically a vow that is dependent on time, which takes effect automatically; but if it is dependent on an action, he cannot annul until it takes effect (Rabbeinu Yerucham end of chapter 27 in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah).}
