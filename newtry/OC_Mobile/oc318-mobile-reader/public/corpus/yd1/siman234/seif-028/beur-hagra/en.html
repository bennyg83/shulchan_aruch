But etc. Ran there s.v. u'l'inyan etc., and Rosh in his pesakim there:
(Anthology) And even not in her presence — from what is written (Nazir 23a and related sources): her husband annulled it and she did not know, etc. (end).
And some say, etc. Beit Yosef and the Rama abbreviated the words of Ri"v [R' Yonah] and created a stumbling block for scholars — for Lehem Mishneh erred and wrote that R' Yonah ruled like Ran, except that he holds like the first opinion that Ran wrote 90b in s.v. 'however,' etc. 'and one other,' etc. — and according to his view, the Rav should have emended below in seif 69 and in siman 228 seif 17. But one who looks at Ri"v will find that he there explicitly wrote like the Rabbis, except that he wrote: specifically [when] she made it contingent on something involving mortification or matters between him and her, etc.; and it is not comparable to 'one who says: after thirty days,' for there it takes effect automatically; and likewise Ran wrote there 80a in s.v. 'and R' Yosi,' etc., and raised a difficulty, etc., and answered, etc. — except that Ran added and wrote: 'alternatively, for example,' etc., and derived [it] from what is written there 89b: 'konam that I do not,' etc.; and likewise wrote there 90a in s.v. 'however,' etc. But Ri"v does not make this distinction — for he holds like the view of Tur and Rosh in their interpretation there s.v. 'konam,' etc. — that it involves matters between him and her; and as Ri"v wrote there explicitly; and as written in the Shulchan Arukh in seif 69: 'and matters,' etc.
