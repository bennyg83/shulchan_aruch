Some say, etc. Beit Yosef and Rama abbreviated Rabbeinu Yerucham’s words and thereby misled later readers. Lechem Mishneh erred in writing that Rabbeinu Yonah ruled like Ran but adopted the first possibility stated by Ran on 90b, s.v. mihu, etc. According to that understanding, Rama should also have added a gloss below in seif 69 and in siman 228, seif 17. Yet one who examines Rabbeinu Yerucham will see that he explicitly rules like the Sages, except that the condition must depend on a matter of self-affliction or a matter between husband and wife, etc. This is unlike one who says that the vow should take effect after thirty days, for there it takes effect automatically. Ran likewise writes in chapter 1, s.v. veRabbi Yosi, etc., where he raises a difficulty and answers it, etc. Ran adds another possibility, namely, etc., based on the statement on 89b, “Konam that I will not,” etc. He writes similarly on 90a, s.v. mihu, etc. Rabbeinu Yerucham, however, does not make this distinction, because he follows Tur and Rosh in their explanation there, s.v. konam, that this is a matter between husband and wife. Rabbeinu Yerucham states this explicitly, as does Shulchan Aruch below in seif 69: “and matters,” etc.
