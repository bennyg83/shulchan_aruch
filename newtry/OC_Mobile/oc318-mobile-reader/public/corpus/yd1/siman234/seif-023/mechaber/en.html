A husband who upheld his wife's vows and she went before three laymen and they released her — it is permitted. {Rama: And some say that a sage or three laymen cannot release [a vow] after the husband's upholding (Mahariv siman 15 and Mordechai on the chapter HaSholeach in the name of R' Yitzchak, and Hagahot Maimoniyot on Shabbos in the name of R' Yonasan); but if the husband did not annul for her and was silent toward her, a sage or three laymen can release [it] (Maharik shoresh 50). And it is good to be stringent that if the husband upheld for her, the husband should be asked about the upholding (and release) [and annul] for her; and if it is not on his day of hearing, a sage or three laymen may also release (Terumat HaDeshen siman 279). And see above seif 49. And all this is l'chatchila to heed the stringent view; but the essential [law] is according to the first view that a sage or three laymen release after the husband's upholding, according to the first view (which is the view of Hagahot Mordechai on Shevuos and Shabbos and Rashbam in the name of R' Yitzchak and R' Eliyahu Mizrachi, Beit Yosef in the name of Rashbatz, and Terumat HaDeshen).}
