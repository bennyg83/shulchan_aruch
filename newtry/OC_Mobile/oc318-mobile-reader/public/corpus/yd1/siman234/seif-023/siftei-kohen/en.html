And he remained silent toward her, a sage can, etc. All the more so if the husband never heard the vow, the sage may still release it for her.
And if it is not on the day he heard it, etc. Meaning, because some authorities below in seif 49 hold that the husband can seek release from his confirmation only on the day he heard the vow.
A sage or three laymen. Nevertheless, even after the day of hearing the husband should first seek release from his confirmation and then annul for her as well. Perhaps a husband can seek release from his confirmation even after the day of hearing, and perhaps a sage or three laymen cannot release her after the husband's confirmation. Therefore, initially, to avoid all disputes, everything possible should be done. The primary ruling follows the first opinion, however. Thus, if the husband is unavailable, three laymen may release her. See Responsa Maharam Mintz, simanim 18 and 111, who discusses this at length and concludes that in practice the husband should first have his confirmation released even after the day of hearing, then annul for his wife, and afterward the sage should also release the woman.
