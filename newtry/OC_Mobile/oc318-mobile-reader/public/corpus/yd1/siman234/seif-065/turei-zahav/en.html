The vow takes effect regarding him as well. At that point the husband is included among “people,” because she vowed not to benefit from anyone who is considered among people at the time of benefit. Annulment is effective because although it is not presently self-affliction, it will lead to self-affliction if she is divorced. So wrote the Rosh. It is obvious that the annulment is effective regarding others as well, like every vow of self-affliction.
