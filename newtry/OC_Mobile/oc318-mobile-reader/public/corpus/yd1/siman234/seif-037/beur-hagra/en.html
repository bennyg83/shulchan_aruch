Although she does not understand it. Regarding confirmation, this is because even confirmation in his heart suffices, as stated below in seif 41. The same applies to annulment, since annulment is effective even when she is not present, as stated below. So too with any expression.
Any expression, etc. On 72b the Gemara gives the example, “You did well,” etc. Rambam and Tur cite it, and I do not know why Shulchan Aruch omitted it. See Ran there, end of s.v. tanya: “It appears to me,” etc.
Even if he performed, etc. This follows a fortiori from silence, as stated there, 79a.
Or ‘void.’ Gemara there: “He should not say,” etc., “void,” etc. See also the Yerushalmi cited by Rosh in his rulings there.
Or ‘there is no,’ etc. Rambam’s text of the baraita was: “If he said, ‘It is impossible that you should vow,’ he has said nothing; if he said, ‘This is no vow,’ or, ‘You did well,’ etc., his words are effective.”
Whose meaning is uprooting, etc. See Rambam’s commentary to the tenth chapter on the Mishnah, “The annulment of vows is all day,” and his wording from the Sifrei, etc., because the concept of annulment, etc. See also his commentary at the beginning of chapter 4 of Nazir, Mishnah 1: “Among the matters you must know,” etc.; this is what he wrote here, etc. See Kesef Mishneh and Lechem Mishneh.
Whether, etc. As stated in Nazir 23a: “Her husband annulled for her,” etc. The alternative phrase, “There is no vow here,” appears in the Yerushalmi and is cited by Rosh and Ran. Ramban and Rosh also had that reading in the baraita.
‘Forgiven,’ etc. As stated there regarding a husband who said, etc. All these are expressions used by a sage, as stated in siman 228, seif 3. See Ran there, s.v. amar Rabbi Yochanan, etc., “and we expound,” etc. Certainly “forgiven” is included, since Scripture says that others may forgive it; “permitted” is likewise an expression of release. The Sifrei says: “A sage permits, but a husband does not permit,” etc.
There is an authority who says, etc. This follows our text, and the Tosefta has the same reading.
