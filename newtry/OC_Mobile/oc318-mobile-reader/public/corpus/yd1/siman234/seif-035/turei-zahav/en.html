Even though she married, etc. The Mishnah derives this from, “The vow of a widow and divorced woman,” etc. It is obvious that the vow stands, for who could annul it? Rather, the verse teaches that in such a case the husband cannot annul after marriage. It is surprising, for above in seif 8 we already applied this verse to a father who delivered his daughter to the husband’s agents. Tosafos in Kesubos already raised this question and left it unresolved.
