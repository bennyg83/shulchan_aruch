He may not, etc., and not, etc. This is explicit there on daf 72a, and similarly elsewhere. Its principal source is the Sifrei: 'But if on the day her husband hears he restrains her,' etc., which teaches, etc. Earlier there it states, 'But if her father restrains her,' etc. From where do we know the same rule regarding a father? The verse states, 'These are the statutes that Hashem commanded Moshe,' teaching that you compare the father to the husband, etc.
Specifically, etc. See Tosafot, Shabbat 157a, s.v. vehilkheta, etc. This is also Rosh’s conclusion in his rulings on Nedarim there, and Rambam and the other authorities rule likewise.
For if, etc. Mishnah, Nedarim there.
The time when he learns of it, etc. Ran there, s.v. yafeh, and s.v. Rabbi Meir, follows the latter rationale. Ran likewise explains at the end of chapter 10, 79a, s.v. vachakhamim, etc., and on 72b concerning the Sages’ disagreement, etc. Rosh also writes so there, s.v. Rabbi Meir, etc.
