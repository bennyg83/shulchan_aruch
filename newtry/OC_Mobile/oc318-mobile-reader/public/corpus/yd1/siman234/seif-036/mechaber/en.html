If she forbade herself figs and grapes, whether by vow or by oath, whether she forbade herself the entire species or said "these figs and these grapes," and he upheld [abstention from] figs and annulled grapes, or upheld grapes and annulled figs — what he upheld is upheld and what he annulled is annulled; and similarly anything like this. And we do not say regarding annulment that a vow partially annulled is entirely annulled. {Rama: And some dispute and hold that if he upheld part of it, the whole is upheld, and if he annulled part of it, it is not annulled at all (Tur, Rosh, Ramban, and Ran); and this is specifically one vow; but if she said "konam grapes upon me, konam figs upon me" — these are two vows and one is not relevant to the other. (So too in the Tur)}
