We do not say regarding annulment, etc. This is said only regarding release by a sage, who uproots the vow from its beginning. A husband merely cuts the vow off from that point onward. Ran.
If he confirmed part of it, etc. Regarding confirmation the verse says, “he shall confirm it,” which can be read as confirming even part of it. Regarding annulment, “he shall annul it” cannot be expounded that way; it means annulment of the entire vow.
