He must seek release from a sage. This means even after the day of confirmation. Since he cannot annul while the confirmation stands, his silence is like that of one who does not know he has authority to annul, and is not itself confirmation. He may annul even after many days, provided he does so on the day the sage releases the confirmation, which becomes like his day of hearing. The dissenting opinion holds that his express confirmation is no less than silence on the day of hearing, and afterward he can no longer annul.
