One who upheld his daughter's or wife's vows and regretted it — behold he is asked of a sage, who releases his upholding, and he returns and annuls for her on that day. But if he annulled for her and regretted, he cannot be asked of a sage in order to return and uphold. And some say he cannot be asked about the upholding except on the day of hearing. (And one should be stringent according to the latter view (Ran, Tosafos, and so Rabbeinu Yerucham and Terumat HaDeshen in the name of Semak), and a sage may also release as above in seif 23.)
