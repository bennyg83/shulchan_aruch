And he releases his confirmation. He may do so even after many days. We do not treat his silence as confirmation, because while he is unable to annul it is like a case where he does not know that annulment is possible, as above in seif 21, and his silence is not confirmation. He may therefore annul even after many days. Nevertheless, he must annul on the day he obtains release, which is treated as his day of hearing. Tur.
