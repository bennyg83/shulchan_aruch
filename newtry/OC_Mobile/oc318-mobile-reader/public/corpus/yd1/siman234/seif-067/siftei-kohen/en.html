She said, 'The benefit of relations with you is forbidden to me.' Although the benefit of relations is intangible and vows do not take effect upon an intangible matter, her words are interpreted as though she said, 'Your body is forbidden to me for the benefit of relations.' Ateret Zekenim, based on Rivash cited by Beit Yosef. See above, siman 213.
He need not annul, because she is obligated to him, etc. Nevertheless, he should annul, lest he later divorce her and she then become forbidden to him, as below in seif 71. Perishah writes likewise at the end of this siman.
