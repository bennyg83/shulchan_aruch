That which a vow of marital relations takes effect is specifically when she said "the benefit of your marital relations is konam for me"; but if she said "the benefit of my marital relations is konam for you" — he need not annul, because she is subjugated to him. And similarly if he said "[the benefit of] my marital relations is konam for you" — it is not a vow; but if he said "the benefit of your marital relations is konam for me" — it is a vow. {Rama: A husband who vowed not to approach his wife until a certain time — if he did so with his wife's consent the vow takes effect, and if not it does not take effect (Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 4 of Hilchos Ishus in the name of the Rambam). A woman who asked her husband not to have relations with her and the husband became angry and swore not to have relations with her — when the woman sees her husband is angry and wants to swear, she appeases him: the vow takes effect, since she asked him not to have relations with her, behold she forgave him his subjugation; and what she appeased him was only so he would not be angry (Mahariv siman 1).}
