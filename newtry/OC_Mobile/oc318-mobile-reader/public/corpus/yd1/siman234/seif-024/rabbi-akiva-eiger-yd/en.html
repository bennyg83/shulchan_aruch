(Shach s.k. 145) And even though it appears Ran wrote thus — in my view Ran's words are an arranged sugya (chapter HaMedir): "do not adorn yourself and do not forbid" — if so she is called disfigured; behold even for R' Yosi adornment is not etc.; nevertheless since through calling her disfigured she is likely to transgress and be forbidden in marital relations, he can annul immediately — and this is exactly Ran's view above — end of his words.
