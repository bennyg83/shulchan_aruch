And most. It is implied in the Talmud in Chullin that with an old person, even without most flesh, he transmits tumah in an ohel; so wrote Maharshal in the first chapter of Chullin. Its implication there is that if it became like a gistera, meaning it was cut across its width at the entire neck or at the spine to its cavity, or it was split in two, it transmits tumah in an ohel; end quote, Shach.
To be stringent. The Bach wrote that by law one must be careful, and there is no place here to rule leniently; see there. However, it appears that one does not need to wake him and tell him when there is a goses, as they must do when there is a dead person, as written in siman 372. Likewise, if they told him while he is naked, he may wait until he dresses himself and then leave; end quote, Shach.
