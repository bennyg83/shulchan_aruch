The laws of liver treifot. In it are 10 seifim.
If the liver was removed — treifah; unless there remained an olive-volume in the place of the gall and an olive-volume in the place of its vitality — which is where it depends on it — and these two olive-volumes are each whole in its place; but if it was gathered together, or like a strap, or flattened — treifah.
