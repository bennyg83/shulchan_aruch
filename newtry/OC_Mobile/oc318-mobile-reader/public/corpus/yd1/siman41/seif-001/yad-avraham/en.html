(Siman 41 seif 1) Liver removed unless olive remained, etc. Know statement of Rabbi Yitzchak bar Yosef who said Rabbi Yochanan in Piskei Avodah Zarah (daf 43): and so meaning of sugya (daf 46); and as Tosafot there wrote. But in mishnah of Rabbi Pinchas: liver removed and nothing remained from it — implies if anything remained — kosher and we do not require olive. And in Gemara there they challenge each other: in mishnah of "these are kosher" (daf 54) it teaches liver removed and olive remained from it; and they answer: this Rabbi Shimon, this Rabbi Chiya; and in Gemara there they challenge Rabbi Yochanan who ruled olive required, yet Rav Bibi said Rabbi Yochanan: law follows anonymous mishnah. And Rashi: what strength has latter anonymous against former anonymous? They answer: two tannaim according to Rabbi Yochanan. From all these it is proven we read in mishnah of "these are kosher" — olive. And Rif and Ran read in mishnayot of "these are kosher" — any amount. And Maharam of Rothenburg wondered on them in gloss of Rif — see his name. It escaped him Rashba's words in Tosafot (daf 39) who challenged this version and read in mishnah of "these are kosher" — any amount; and mishnayot do not contradict. And Gemara challenges from baraita that teaches olive required; answers: this Rabbi Shimon, etc.; and therefore challenges Rabbi Yochanan how ruled baraita and abandoned mishnah, yet Rav Bibi said law follows anonymous mishnah; answers two tannaim according to Rabbi Yochanan; and clear this is Rif and Ran's version. For practical law certainly no difference, for certainly olive required in place of gall, etc., as Shulchan Aruch wrote.
Know: liver removed treifah even in species that have no gall in liver and place of their remnant equal; and see acharonim siman 42 s.k. 12; and so proven explicitly in Tosafot Kiddushin (daf 57) s.v. shechatah, etc., who wrote regarding bird of metzora found treifah in intestines such as liver removed — see his name. Bird of metzora was turtle-dove as in Negaim chapter 14; Taz brings siman 82. And turtle-dove has no gall in liver as acharonim wrote in that s.k., for liver removed is not treifah from lack of gall, since possible all liver flesh removed and gall stuck in liver sinew as Tosafot beginning Avodah Zarah; and not as appears from Rashba in Tosafot (daf 43) whom Beit Yosef brings at end of this siman that liver removed — we come on account of lack of gall. And this is astonishing: how hang baraita on what baraita did not teach — this is explicit mishnah liver removed and nothing remained — treifah, and none disagree; and if gall lacking — majority of poskim permit as explained Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, Shach end siman 42; Rashba's words require study. And it does not appear so in any posek, for even in species without gall in liver — treifah if their liver removed, as written; and see Turei Zahav. And if liver lacking from start of its creation — explained Beit Yosef at end of this siman and Shulchan Aruch siman 50 s.k. 3: for Rashba and Ran treifah; for Rambam kosher. However Kohen Perot wrote: even for Rambam one may say treifah. And in Tosafot Kiddushin explained explicitly treifah, for they wrote found treifah in intestines such as liver removed — certainly from birth. And since Tosafot's view is to be stringent — do not be lenient, for Rambam is lone against Tosafot, Rashba, and Ran; and not like hagahah siman 50 s.k. 3 that ruled lenient in financial loss. And so Turei Zahav and Peri Chadash to be stringent even in financial loss — see his name. It escaped him Tosafot's view here — see his name.
