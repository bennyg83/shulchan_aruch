And in opening to siman 98 I will explain whether in such case we say one in two nullifies, etc. — end. And in much — Rav's words are astonishing, for in such case on contrary perforce two treifot; and I thought printing error and where remained must say removed; but I saw in opening to laws of mixtures chapter 1 investigation 1 he mentioned both removed and remained: there one in two nullifies if specifically from permitted clarified initially, etc.; parable: three liver olives (not specifically) from three animals one removed place of vitality, one from gall, one from kidneys — if in each one olive remained, now perforce two of permitted, etc.; and likewise in flayed three animals one remained in navel, etc. — see his name; behold explicitly he equates remained to removed; and in truth this is not. Again I heard while genius author was alive a sage from community Slonim asked him this himself and he admitted to him a slip escaped his pen in this.]
