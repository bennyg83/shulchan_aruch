Depends. Namely in the kidneys, as stated in Beit Yosef; and there are poskim who say place of its vitality is the place of its adhesion in the omasum; and Orach Chaim wrote: therefore three olive-volumes are required in these three places; and he brings it in Tosafot Chadashim to decide practical law — end; Shach. However in fowl one may be lenient if two olive-volumes remained, one in place of gall and one in place where it adheres to the heart — see Shaarei Yosher siman 51. A goose liver for roasting was on the lower side resting on the esophagus and reticulum like a pocket of flesh covered with liver skin; it was tall and somewhat harder than liver flesh; he tore the pocket and found inside a complete foxtail, also rotted and digested, also sand like that found in reticulum — treifah. Responsum Shaarei De'ah question 28 and Peri Chadash in Kuntras A; and see Radbaz siman 199. A stone found in liver of animal buried in flesh of liver is fit, for there it forms from food matter descending to the liver to form blood. Peri Chadash in name of Rikanti.
