A) [Seif 1] Liver removed — treifah; even if gall remained stuck in sinews of liver and simfonot — treifah. Tosafot Chullin 42a s.v. nikav. Peri Chadash ot 1; and he wrote: not like Rashba in long Tosafot 43b who wrote no prohibition of liver removal except from lack of gall that does not exist. Per this Orach Chaim ot 1; Likutei HaRambam ot 1; Kereti ot 1; Turei Zahav ot 1; Sha'ar HaTziyon ot 1; Zivchei Tzedek ot 1; Chokhmat Adam general rule 17 ot 9; Beit Yitzchak ot 1; Minchat Yitzchak in roots ot 1; Zivchei Tzvi ot 1.
C) There. Unless olive in place of gall and olive in place of its vitality that depends on it. Namely place of its hanging when exposed and stuck under kidneys — Rashi Chullin 46a; and Rashba and Rambam chapter 8 Hilchot Shechitah; and Beit Yosef wrote and so is essence; therefore ruled so in Shulchan Aruch. And Rivan and Rokeach wrote: in place where it is alive — namely place of hanging in omasum; and so Rashi there in foreign tongue; and Beit David Beit Yosef. Therefore Orach Chaim siman 51 ot 8 wrote: need three olives in these three places — meaning olive in place of gall, olive in place stuck under kidneys, olive in place of hanging in omasum. And Beit David Rama in his book Tosafot Chadashim general rule 89 ot 9 section ot 2; Taz s.k. 1; Shach s.k. 1; so is practice in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 3; Peri Chadash ot 2; for this Orach Chaim ot 2; Kereti ot 2; Turei Zahav ot 3; Sha'ar HaTziyon ot 3; Zivchei Tzedek ot 3. Machatzit HaShekel ot 5; Chokhmat Adam there; Anaf Yosef siman 30 ot 1; Tosafot Zahav siman 65 ot 2; Beit Yitzchak ot 1; Kometz general rule 5 laws of other treifot ot 3; Zivchei Tzvi ot 3.
D) However in financial loss or for honor of Shabbat — if olive in place of gall and olive in place of hanging in omasum — one may declare kosher. Masat Binyamin responsum 103; so is practice there; Minchat Yitzchak on Tosafot Chadashim there ot 25; and so in his book Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 51. But Machatzit HaShekel there wrote: in financial loss or Shabbat honor if olive in place of gall and olive in place of kidneys — declare kosher per Shulchan Aruch ruling; and wrote: though Moharach in Tosafot Chadashim brought Orach Chaim's words requiring three olives — here in hagahah he retracted since he did not emend Shulchan Aruch's words. And so Anaf Yosef there: in financial loss be lenient if remained olive in place of gall and olive in place of kidneys to rely on Shulchan Aruch ruling. Zivchei Tzvi ot 4; and he wrote: but if only olive in place of gall and olive in place of omasum — make treifah — end; and so appears from view of Rashi, Rambam, Rashba, Shulchan Aruch, and Rama who did not emend Shulchan Aruch.
E) And he wrote there Sha'ar HaTziyon: this requiring three olives is from doubt; accordingly three olives that fell into one dish from three animals — one remained olive in place of omasum, one in kidneys, one in gall — need only 60 against one olive; and in drying all permitted, for perforce two kosher and one treifah and nullified in majority. And Hagahat HaPanim ot 1; and wondered on him: in such case on contrary perforce two treifot; see his name; and Mekor Chaim ot 5 wrote what Sha'ar HaTziyon wrote "remained" is scribal error and must say removed — meaning one removed olive of omasum, one place of kidneys, one place of gall — need 60 only against one of them, for two of them kosher and we do not know which — end.
