Unless olive remained in place of gall, etc. Explanation: place where it rooted and hung in liver as explained Rashi Chullin 46a end s.v. matbil bah; and so in explanation not like Rabbi Azriel siman 371 who explained olive in place of gall means opposite gall — this explanation not brought in any posek.
