Even in large ox, etc. Chullin daf 44 s.v. kedei tefisat yad; and Tosafot on tefisat yad two explanations: Rashi four fingers; Bahag two fingers; afterward Tosafot brought Rivam: this is large ox; but in other animals, beasts, and fowl — estimate all according to value. Rabbenu Beit Yosef siman 20 above: Rivam spoke only per Rashi whose measures are large and impossible to say so in other animals; but per Bahag all animal and beast equal to large ox except fowl. And Rabbenu Beit Yosef learned this from Rambam and Rashba in Torat HaBayit daf 25: this measure of tefisat yad is for animal and beast; but in fowl according to value — namely poskim hold like Bahag two fingers not four; therefore no contradiction — Rashba daf 37 regarding tefisat yad at place of adhesion is tefisat yad — this measure for ox and small animal according to value of this measure; and seemingly contradiction — for above he wrote all animals equal; likewise daf 38 on two olive-volumes of liver: reasonable even in large ox and fowl all according to size; and what he said "even in large ox" implies other oxen and animals equal except fowl — they contradict. But in truth it resolves: where measure is large such as two fingers, and likewise liver (proper law only olive, but we are stringent from doubt to require two olive-volumes) — all animals that arise are equal; but where four fingers needed — not found in all arising animals — for all animals according to value; and what he wrote siman 46 on four fingers in large ox and small according to value — refers according to all that this is said in Gemara in shoe of ox and not said in animal — learn ox specifically; therefore difficulty for Rashba and Mechaber; therefore above siman 20 and everywhere Mechaber did not give measure according to value except in fowl. However Tur does not hold so: siman 20 after bringing Bahag who explained two fingers wrote: nevertheless Rivam's law — all according to value of large ox even animal; and Beit Yosef wondered; and indeed he followed Tosafot and Rosh who did not distinguish between Rashi and Bahag; also Rosh wrote explicitly regarding puncture like wing: obvious olive-volumes stated in fowl according to value, and in animal large and small estimate according to smallness as Rivam wrote regarding tefisat yad — end. If he held Rivam spoke only because four fingers required — how compare to liver and suffice with one olive that is only third of egg? Or he holds no distinction and not dependent on small and large measure; therefore Tur omitted "even in large ox" and wrote anonymously in fowl according to value — same in animal; only since Rosh wrote so in fowl he wrote also in fowl. If so, this law is disputed — be stringent. And I almost have proof for Rosh and Tur: if we say small calf and large ox — all measure two olive-volumes equal — then known: as animal grows so inner organs; if today liver lacking and only half olive remnant — treifah, and within eight days it grows according to animal value until half olive becomes full olive as way of large animals — used and kosher — we find treifah returns to kosher; impossible; and treifah does not improve; for this is not improvement but way of growth; if so treifah calf grazing in flock will not grow to ox — especially per those who say improves from doubt; perforce always estimate according to value; if so above case does not apply: necessarily according to value animal so treifot value; if then large was small — half olive liver called olive remnant also kosher; if grew afterward olive also grows; and this requires study. Perhaps included in what they said: whatever makes treifah within eight days does not improve; and so in liver.
