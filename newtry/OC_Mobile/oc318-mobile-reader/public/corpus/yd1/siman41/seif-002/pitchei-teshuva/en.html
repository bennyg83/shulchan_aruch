The two olives alone. See Turei Zahav who wrote language not precise; in truth even if one of them was consumed though all with second olive exists — Torah doubt, etc.; therefore for us who require three olives — if one dried or other flaw though two other olives with entire liver exist — treifah — end; and see responsum Toldot Yitzchak siman 2 who brought in name of his uncle genius author Panim Me'irot who wrote: specifically cited two olives; but in one of these not treifah — double doubt; and as Beit Yosef below siman 59 regarding flayed; and all the more here one may say perhaps law like Maharshal they brought; and he disagreed with Panim Me'irot and raised like Turei Zahav: even when one of these olives dried — treifah; but what Turei Zahav wrote at end regarding us who require three olives — in this he exaggerated to be stringent; and one may be lenient from double doubt — see his name — he raised so practically.
