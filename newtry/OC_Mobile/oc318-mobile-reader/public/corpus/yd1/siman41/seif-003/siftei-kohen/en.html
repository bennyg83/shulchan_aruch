And if these olive-volumes dried, etc. Thus is implied in Tur; and so Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 17; and so Orach Chaim there; and Taz hung this law on "it appears to me" as though he judges by sight; however Maharshal there challenged Tur and wrote: and these are his words — I do not know from where he took this, for from that which we ask (Shas daf 55b) regarding flayed: removed like a sela on the spine and at the ends of the limbs and the rest remains — what is the law (see above siman 59 regarding flayed) — we do not ask thus regarding liver; it implies in liver it is kosher; and so Rashba and Ran wrote only that if the liver dried and the two olive-volumes did not remain; but in any case if the entire liver exists or most of it and those two olive-volumes were removed or dried — kosher, as stated below for practical law and not for action — end of his words; and he did not intend according to law what he wrote "I do not know," etc. — the Rambam slipped his mind, chapter 8 of Hilchot Shechitah law 21, who wrote: removed from it the place on which it depends and the place of the gall even though the rest remains — as though removed — treifah — end of his words; and so is everyone; and so is proven explicitly from Rashba's words in the long Tosafot 39b; and in brief there regarding two livers adhered with an olive in the place of the gall and an olive in the place of its vitality — treifah — see his name; and Beit Yosef brings it; and Maharshal there siman 19; and so is proven from Rashba's words that Tur brought siman 52 regarding liver became yellow opposite place of gall and opposite place of its vitality; and as appears from Maharshal's own words there siman 94; and that which the Gemara does not ask thus regarding liver — this is because in liver it is obvious to forbid, since the essence of its vitality was removed; and all this is clear.
Melting of the liver, etc. And in the Gemara in Gittin (daf 69a) it appears that it is not treifah except when the two olive-volumes did not remain, as with removal; and it appears to me that so is Rokeach's view; and so in responsum Masat Binyamin siman 21 — see his name; and so Bach agreed for practical law; and so in Sefer Tosafot Chadashim daf 134a; and in Lechem Mishneh daf 68b; and Bach followed Rabbenu Yitzchak; and in my humble opinion, as written.
