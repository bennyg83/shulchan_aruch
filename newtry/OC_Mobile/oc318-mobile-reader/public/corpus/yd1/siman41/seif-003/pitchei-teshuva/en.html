To rot — all. Avnei Nezer in name of Taz and Shach; and see in this responsum his uncle Panim Me'irot part 1 siman 1; and see responsum Masat Binyamin siman 21 who also disagrees on Rama — see his name. [And in responsum Chatam Sofer siman 144 s.v. venava he wrote: in vain Mishnah Berurah challenged him; and worthy to be concerned for Rama's words — see his name.] Further he wrote there: also Rama was not stringent except in melting body from liver flesh; but if liver flesh exists and healthy only blood clotted and when scraping blood liver beneath exists — no concern at all even if liver flesh lacking measure of blood, etc. — see his name. And see responsum Geonei Batrai siman 12.
