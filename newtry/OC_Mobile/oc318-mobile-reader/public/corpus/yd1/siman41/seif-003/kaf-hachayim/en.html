G) There. But if gathered or strap, etc. — not specifically these forms only, but anything not whole as created — olive in those places; even if all rest whole — treifah. Turei Zahav ot 4; Beit Yitzchak in Amud HaYemini ot 3; Anaf Yosef there ot 2; and so Shach s.k. 3: if these two olives removed even though entire liver remains — treifah. Sha'ar HaTziyon end ot 3; Machatzit HaShekel end ot 14; Chokhmat Adam there ot 9; Anaf Yosef there ot 9; Zivchei Tzvi ot 8.
H) These olives — Kohen Perot ot 2 wrote flesh and sinews and simfonot combine to olive measure; and so is public custom; but Turei Zahav ot 2 wrote they do not combine; must be all olives from liver flesh alone. And so Sha'ar HaTziyon ot 3; Anaf Yosef there ot 4; Zivchei Tzvi ot 9; and see below ot 14.
