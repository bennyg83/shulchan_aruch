C) There. Unless olive in place of gall and olive in place of its vitality that depends on it. Namely place of its hanging when exposed and stuck under kidneys — Rashi Chullin 46a; and Rashba and Rambam chapter 8 Hilchot Shechitah; and Beit Yosef wrote and so is essence; therefore ruled so in Shulchan Aruch. And Rivan and Rokeach wrote: in place where it is alive — namely place of hanging in omasum; and so Rashi there in foreign tongue; and Beit David Beit Yosef. Therefore Orach Chaim siman 51 ot 8 wrote: need three olives in these three places — meaning olive in place of gall, olive in place stuck under kidneys, olive in place of hanging in omasum. And Beit David Rama in his book Tosafot Chadashim general rule 89 ot 9 section ot 2; Taz s.k. 1; Shach s.k. 1; so is practice in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 3; Peri Chadash ot 2; for this Orach Chaim ot 2; Kereti ot 2; Turei Zahav ot 3; Sha'ar HaTziyon ot 3; Zivchei Tzedek ot 3. Machatzit HaShekel ot 5; Chokhmat Adam there; Anaf Yosef siman 30 ot 1; Tosafot Zahav siman 65 ot 2; Beit Yitzchak ot 1; Kometz general rule 5 laws of other treifot ot 3; Zivchei Tzvi ot 3.
