If the liver dried until it crumbles under a fingernail — treifah; and this is when all of it dried and there did not remain an olive-volume in the place of the gall and an olive-volume in the place of its vitality. {Rama: And if these two olive-volumes alone dried — also treifah. Melting of the liver — that is, blood comes out from the flesh of the liver that melted and became blood — treifah (Rokeach); even though the two olive-volumes remained, for in the end it will rot — all (his opinion). There are those who say: a liver that is hard as a stone — treifah (Rokeach and Avraham ben David and Darkei Moshe); and this is when the two aforementioned olive-volumes did not remain, that they did not harden.}
