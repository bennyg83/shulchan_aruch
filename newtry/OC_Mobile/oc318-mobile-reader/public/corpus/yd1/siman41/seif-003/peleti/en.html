That in the end it will rot — all: Taz and other acharonim disagreed in this law whether from six days of creation to be concerned for this — only Rokeach deals when entire liver melted even two olive-volumes; but if two olive-volumes remain — kosher. I have nothing new except it is obligation to be beaten and kiss the dust — Rama. I said to jest somewhat: one must be precise in Gemara chapter "One who seized him": they said spitting blood — if lung disease — has remedy; if liver disease — no remedy; and Taz said reason: since from mouth — say perhaps melted and removed; and explanation needed: he said no remedy — do you decide it certainly to death? Then he said say perhaps melted — implies doubt whether melted; how decide certainly no remedy? Perforce intent: in the end there will be melting only for this we do not make treifah, for not yet melted; but this is only if there is limit that will stand; but all this time it will melt — if so, even though not yet melted, nevertheless it is as melted; and this is Gemara's intent "say perhaps melted" — every moment it was presumed to be melted.
Hard as stone treifah — Rav Beit Yosef doubted; and his intent: when we learn treifot one from another, and in Beit Yosef he learned law of dried that is also in its blemish animal — stated only: this case is different there air does not rule and returns healthy — we learn from lung that dried thus that crumbles under fingernail does not return healthy even if air does not rule; and this is principle everywhere. But dry as stone that was stated only regarding lung — how learn from it for us? If so, also in blemishes of firstborn: ear of animal hard as stone — disqualify like lung; for Gemara compares this more than liver; and all blemishes sages said in lung — also in liver should have taught; but specifically lung they said so, for all treifot as Rashi wrote: lest membrane puncture — this applies only in lung not liver; therefore in great loss certainly permit.
