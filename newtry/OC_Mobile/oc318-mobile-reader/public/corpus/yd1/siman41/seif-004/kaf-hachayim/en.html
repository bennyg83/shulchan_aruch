D) However in financial loss or for honor of Shabbat — if olive in place of gall and olive in place of hanging in omasum — one may declare kosher. Masat Binyamin responsum 103; so is practice there; Minchat Yitzchak on Tosafot Chadashim there ot 25; and so in his book Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 51. But Machatzit HaShekel there wrote: in financial loss or Shabbat honor if olive in place of gall and olive in place of kidneys — declare kosher per Shulchan Aruch ruling; and wrote: though Moharach in Tosafot Chadashim brought Orach Chaim's words requiring three olives — here in hagahah he retracted since he did not emend Shulchan Aruch's words. And so Anaf Yosef there: in financial loss be lenient if remained olive in place of gall and olive in place of kidneys to rely on Shulchan Aruch ruling. Zivchei Tzvi ot 4; and he wrote: but if only olive in place of gall and olive in place of omasum — make treifah — end; and so appears from view of Rashi, Rambam, Rashba, Shulchan Aruch, and Rama who did not emend Shulchan Aruch.
