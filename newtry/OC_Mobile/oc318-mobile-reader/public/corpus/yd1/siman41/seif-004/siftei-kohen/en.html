Even in the place of the gall, etc. Bach wrote: even if the entire liver was removed and only these olive-volumes remained and these that remained became wormy — kosher — see his name. And so is implied explicitly in Tosafot Chadashim general rule 89 law 9 — see his name and in the simanim. And Maharshal's view in Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 42 does not appear so, for he wrote: that which is kosher when wormy in place of gall and place of its vitality — this is when between all of them there is only the punctures of the worms like an olive-volume of flesh in place of its vitality and in place of the gall; and if not, it is obvious one may say the Gemara teaches us that since it became so wormy, in the end it will become wormy and spoil entirely; and Ran leans to be stringent per this reasoning — and this is the essence — end. And if so, when all was removed and the two olive-volumes that remained became wormy — there are not here two olive-volumes alone but worm punctures — and treifah; and so is Ra'ah's view in Sefer Beit Hashem; and perhaps also Tosafot Chadashim and Bach do not deal except when it became wormy in a case where the two olive-volumes were not lacking from their measure; and this requires study.
