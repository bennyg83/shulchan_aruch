Even if all of it is buried in the flesh of the liver — treifah. For we are concerned lest it entered via the esophagus to the thin parts and exited from the thin parts to the cavity of the animal and punctured, and from there entered the liver. Tur's language.
Facing outside. And Atzei Tzvi wrote: even if it is not buried in the flesh of the liver but only its point goes out into the cavity of the animal and its thick head is hidden inside the liver — kosher — end; and he followed Rashba's words that Beit Yosef brought, who reconciles Rashi's view and distinguishes: although we hold below siman 51, a thorn found in the cavity of the body is treifah lest perhaps one of the inner organs was punctured — that is specifically there where it punctured with force; but with a needle that entered gently we are not concerned for this. But Ran in Piskei Avodah Zarah concludes: where the needle goes out to the cavity of the body — treifah; and so Maharshal ruled Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 47; however in Semak and Orach Chaim general rule 51 law 17 it is written like Rashba; and so in Hagahot Shaarei Dura siman 94; and see there s.k. 3 and siman 55 what he wrote from this.
Kosher, etc. And Rashba holds: if its point faces toward the cavity of the body — treifah, for we are concerned for anything that entered until the place of the liver and its point downward, and it passes via a narrow path lest with its entrance it punctured the reed branching to the liver in the place where it is narrow — treifah, as written at end of siman 34; and he wrote that so is Rif's view; and so Maharshal and Bach.
In every case — treifah. For if it entered via the simfon it would not have been possible for it to bend entirely and lodge in the thickness of the liver — Beit Yosef; and what he wrote in Derishah siman 12 — see his name — Rabbenu Yerucham in this is an error, for on the contrary in Darkei Moshe he agreed to Beit Yosef, as written in the hagahah here.
But with a thin one there is no distinction. Whether the thick side of it faces the reed or toward the cavity of the animal — always treifah, even if all of it is inside the liver, for it is fit to puncture on its thick side; and if so, one must be concerned it entered via the esophagus; and see Tosafot and poskim who distinguished: why do we not distinguish above at end of siman 36 regarding lung between kofa inward or outward, that the simfonot of the lung if wide — see his name. (The law of the needle was explained in siman 48 and 49 and 51.)
