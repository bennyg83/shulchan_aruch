A needle found in the liver — whether it came before us whole or cut — if the thick side of it is toward the cavity of the animal, even if all of it is buried in the flesh of the liver — treifah; and if the thin side of it faces outside — kosher, for we attribute and say it came via the simfon. {Rama: And specifically when the thick side faces toward the simfon; but if it faces to the sides — in every case treifah (Beit Yosef).} In what case is this said? With a thick needle; but with a thin one there is no distinction and it is treifah; and the measure of thick is like the measure of a date pit.
