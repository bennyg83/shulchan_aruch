And if thick side — Gemara says regarding needle found in piece of liver: we see if kofa outward — punctured; and similarly from needle in synagogue we do not distinguish kofa outward; Gemara answers there: there are foods and liquids that pressed it; Rashi explains difference: if thick head leans toward liver and point toward body cavity — how possible from outside and cavity it came? Then thick head would puncture liver — difficult in reality; we attribute via simfon entered — kosher. But opposite: perhaps thick leans toward body cavity — how via simfon puncture? Thick head not punctured; if so from body cavity punctured liver and via body cavity came via esophagus — treifah; this is Rashi's view and supporters; and this is Shulchan Aruch's view here; and Rashba and supporters opposite — we never concern needle came via body cavity, only via reed and simfon; only when point leans to reed and simfonot and thickness toward liver — even though pressed and entered inside liver flesh — nevertheless needle has no power to puncture in its travel and expulsion, for its thickness and pouch impede — therefore kosher; opposite — concern via travel it came in point and punctured simfon and similar slight puncture; Maharshal and more acharonim concerned for Rashba; if so law of Gemara distinction kofa inward and outward in lung falls, since Rashi and Rashba explain opposite to opposite and concern for both to be stringent. If needle swallowed in liver both heads thick and no point at all — per Rashba kosher, for how puncture diagonally and bent travel; but per Rashi treifah, for we attribute esophagus puncture; and if impossible to puncture — also in simfon impossible to puncture. Third method Rambam: he does not concern for esophagus or reed, only simfon swallowed inside liver also slight puncture; therefore in needle concern for simfon puncture; therefore if point toward animal cavity — treifah, simfon punctured and exited; but opposite kosher, for in pouch impossible puncture simfon, only exited via open mouth of simfon to liver — correct he explains outward and inward per Rashba. In Gemara difficulty from abomasum — Rashba explains even per Rambam: since you said in thick place hard to depict puncture and travel — if needle stuck in abomasum and pouch leans toward abomasum cavity — treifah, for perforce from outside point punctured and passed skin thickness until pouch exited outside toward cavity; if from face side outward — how could pouch puncture? Answer Gemara foods and liquids pressed. Peri Chadash challenged: Rambam explains in synagogue on one side — we puncture outer not inner; if so if questioner intent kofa toward synagogue cavity — treifah, for certainly punctured inside going outside; opposite how can pouch puncture? What does Gemara answer foods and liquids pressed? On contrary this supports passed inside to outside. See Peri Chadash explains dachahu — stranger they pushed needle backward — rejected, not seen. But appears certainly on one side kosher including all cases: either punctured from outside and did not reach abomasum cavity, or opposite punctured inside and did not reach animal cavity. Rashba wrote in section this needle, etc., to establish Rashi holds: if needle head lodged in liver goes to animal cavity — not in category needle in body cavity treifah, for they said only thorn that punctures with force from outside and enters with pressure; in this matter concern lest with pressured entrance it struck and touched slight puncture, etc.; per this needle from outside to body cavity lodged in organ like abomasum from outside — via pressure it lodged; if so no difficulty if kofa into synagogue cavity how puncture — entrance pressured as Rashba wrote they pressed perforce; and this resolves. Only Gemara difficulty opposite inner puncture not outer that entered via travel in synagogue — thorn via eating lodged while still fruit challenges; Gemara answers since there are foods, etc.; and this resolves.
