F) There. And these two olives shall each be whole in its place; but if gathered, strap, or flattened — treifah. And likewise three olives we wrote above ot 3 — must not be gathered, flattened, or strap. Orach Chaim there. And if olive of excess or olive of kidneys flattened, etc., and behold two whole olives — declare kosher from double doubt. And whatever has olive of gall and another whole olive — even if third flattened whether in omasum or kidneys — kosher. Machatzit HaShekel ot 7; and he wrote: so proven from end of Peleti ot 3; and so is essence; not like what he wrote: if olive in place of omasum flattened — see his name. Anaf Yosef siman 30 ot 5; Tosafot Zahav siman 65 ot 4; Beit Yitzchak in Tosafot HaRishonim section ot 1; Zivchei Shaul ot 5; Zivchei Tzvi ot 7; and implies always olive in place of gall required, only regarding whole olive and flattened olive no distinction whether whole in kidneys and flattened in omasum or opposite.
