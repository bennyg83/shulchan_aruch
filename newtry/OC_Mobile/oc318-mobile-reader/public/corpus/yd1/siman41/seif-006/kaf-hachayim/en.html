And if olive of excess or olive of kidneys flattened, etc., and behold two whole olives — declare kosher from double doubt. And whatever has olive of gall and another whole olive — even if third flattened whether in omasum or kidneys — kosher. Machatzit HaShekel ot 7; and he wrote: so proven from end of Peleti ot 3; and so is essence; not like what he wrote: if olive in place of omasum flattened — see his name. Anaf Yosef siman 30 ot 5; Tosafot Zahav siman 65 ot 4; Beit Yitzchak in Tosafot HaRishonim section ot 1; Zivchei Shaul ot 5; Zivchei Tzvi ot 7; and implies always olive in place of gall required, only regarding whole olive and flattened olive no distinction whether whole in kidneys and flattened in omasum or opposite. B) There. Unless olive remained in place of gall, etc. Explanation: place where it rooted and hung in liver as explained Rashi Chullin 46a end s.v. matbil bah; and so in explanation not like Rabbi Azriel siman 371 who explained olive in place of gall means opposite gall — this explanation not brought in any posek. From this Orach Chaim ot 3; and in his book Gidulei HaKodesh ot 1; Zivchei Tzvi ot 2.
I) And olive measure — some say half egg, namely nine dirhams; some third egg, namely six dirhams. Sha'arei Teshuvah ot 3; and be stringent that it must be half egg. Kereti ot 2; Chokhmat Adam there. And this stringency for stringency such as liver removed and only olives remain; but for leniency such as liver exists and only olives removed — not half-egg measure, for that is leniency; rather since removed from them third-of-egg measure — forbidden. Sha'ar HaTziyon ot 1; Beit Yitzchak in Tosafot HaRishonim ot 1; Kometz laws of other treifot general rule 5 ot 4; Zivchei Tzvi ot 11. And so we wrote in Orach Chaim siman 486 ot 1 in name of several poskim: olive measure as half egg — to be stringent not to be lenient — see his name.
J) And it appears if third-of-egg measure exists from flesh alone and completion of half egg from sinews and simfonot — kosher, for double doubt in dispute of rabbis. And appears further: if fed in place of gall and olive in place hanging on kidneys — whole from flesh alone and half-egg measure for each; and third olive for omasum — its measure third of egg; and with sinews — kosher even without financial loss from reason I wrote; and likewise opposite. Anaf Yosef there. And his intent in "and likewise opposite": if olive in place of gall and olive in omasum whole in flesh alone and half egg for each; and third olive hanging on kidneys — third egg with sinews complete half egg — kosher even without financial loss; but if olive in place hanging on kidneys and olive of omasum whole from flesh alone and half egg each and third olive in place of gall — third egg and sinews do not permit; for as written "and likewise opposite" intent specifically regarding olive of omasum and olive of kidneys; but olive in place of gall — no permission; for so we find in law of flattened — need whole olive in place of gall and another with it; but if two olives of omasum and kidneys whole and olive in place of gall flattened — no permission as Machatzit HaShekel ot 7 — see his name. But it appears even olive in place of gall — third egg and sinews complete half egg — may declare kosher from above reason that flattened law is different, for Gemara asked even on one olive as Machatzit HaShekel wrote; but regarding half egg and sinews — dispute of rabbis — we do double doubt even on olive in place of gall. Zivchei Tzvi ot 12.
