If the omasum was punctured, etc. And Darkei Moshe wrote in name of Maharil: if the courtyard of the liver was punctured in the place where the butcher's hand rests — kosher; and if white threads like milk are found around it — then it is an old puncture and treifah — end; and see above siman 36 s.k. 14.
From the side of the liver, etc. For the omasum is wider than the liver, and on the right side of the omasum it is not covered by the liver, and there the puncture can be far from the liver — end; Rambam in Semag; and so in responsum Divrei Rivash siman 173: specifically from the side of the liver is treifah; but Bach proved that this "from the side of the liver" is a scribal error, for it was written "courtyard of the liver" and they erred and inserted "from the side" in place of "courtyard"; and so he emended in Gedulat Mordechai; and so it is in the new slaughterings printed in Lublin year 534 in name of Maharal of Prague and in name of several great ones; and so I found explicitly in Agur siman 1032.
But if, etc. The reason the poskim wrote: treifot is not from itself but because of the puncture, in the end the liver will be removed; and so it appears to me to prove from the Gemara Piskei Avodah Zarah (daf 48a) that courtyard of the liver is not treifah from itself — see his name; and examine well. And according to this, whether a membrane grew on it from a blow or other membrane — it is a sealing; and so in responsum Divrei Rivash there: when the puncture was sealed with fat — kosher; and this is not like Ratz in examinations; and Rambam already challenged him in Sefer Beit Hashem.
