With adhesion on rib — kosher. Avnei Nezer; and see in my book pamphlet of answers siman 30 and 31 I elaborated law of adhesion from omasum to synagogue; and found needle; and I raised: if stuck in walls of abomasum and synagogue lengthwise and all hidden within thickness of wall — make treifah even if no adhesion opposite; and all the more when adhesion opposite; even in financial loss. And where not stuck inside wall only placed lengthwise between folds of abomasum and needle point not opposite wall and adhesion outside where needle mouth ended — hard in my eyes to permit. But since usual and very common and mostly financial loss and honor of Shabbat — it appeared in my humble opinion to be lenient if adhesion passes via narrowing and actual touch, not via peeling with fingernail, only roll between fingers and gently feel adhesion. (And though perhaps they did not accept narrowing matter only in lung — if adhesion all day will not disconnect unlike other organs where adhesion disconnects, as written regarding calves; but it appears whatever said adhesion in organs — word applies also to narrowing — their law like lung and enough; and not like calves where from reasoning so, not so here that at least legs to matter — not from puncture since disconnected with light touch — great doubt to permit.) And so appears to be lenient when see flesh in omasum of liver at adhesion place is flawed like blow — then attribute flaw to flawed place, namely adhesion came from omasum; and in abomasum no consumption, etc. — see his name; and see his name I brought responsum Noda BiYehudah siman 15 and see his name siman 18. And if puncture open through or adhesion requiring check — if checked in spread and no needle found at all — no concern lest lost on account of majority of spread — see his name; and see Peri Megadim part 2 siman 129 on what Chut HaShani wrote to be lenient without checking spread — heaven forbid to practice so, clarified in many experiments it is very common. And if impossible to check all belly rooms — make treifah; and so I heard Ashkenaz practice; and in my opinion one who does not practice so feeds treifot — see his name; and he wrote in Noda BiYehudah siman 16: adhesion from omasum to synagogue — already discarded rumen or cut and salted and rinsed in way impossible to search for needle except woman says to her son there was no needle — make treifah, for since she did not yet know from adhesion — it is matter not dependent, etc. — see his name.
