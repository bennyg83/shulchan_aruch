If the omasum of the liver was punctured with a puncture open through from side to side from the side of the liver — treifah; but if it adhered with an adhesion on a rib — kosher. {Rama: And if the omasum of the liver adhered to the abomasum — one must examine the rumen, for certainly there is a thorn or needle that punctured and on account of this this adhesion came (thus I found in old examinations); and I saw an incident and so is the practice.}
