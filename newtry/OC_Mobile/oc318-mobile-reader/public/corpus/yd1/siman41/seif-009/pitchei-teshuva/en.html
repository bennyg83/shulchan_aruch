From side of liver treifah. Avnei Nezer; and see responsum Givat Shaul siman 55: in courtyard of liver was round puncture and lip around puncture in upper skin toward lung side; also flesh between two skins lacking in circle like skin; recognizable made while alive; in lower skin toward liver no such lack, only torn to several tears; slaughterer said lower skin stuck to rumen and torn from there with force; this puncture was not toward liver side — he wrote to permit for several reasons — see his name.
