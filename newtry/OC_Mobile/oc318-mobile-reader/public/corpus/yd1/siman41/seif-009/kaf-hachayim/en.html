I) And olive measure — some say half egg, namely nine dirhams; some third egg, namely six dirhams. Sha'arei Teshuvah ot 3; and be stringent that it must be half egg. Kereti ot 2; Chokhmat Adam there. And this stringency for stringency such as liver removed and only olives remain; but for leniency such as liver exists and only olives removed — not half-egg measure, for that is leniency; rather since removed from them third-of-egg measure — forbidden. Sha'ar HaTziyon ot 1; Beit Yitzchak in Tosafot HaRishonim ot 1; Kometz laws of other treifot general rule 5 ot 4; Zivchei Tzvi ot 11. And so we wrote in Orach Chaim siman 486 ot 1 in name of several poskim: olive measure as half egg — to be stringent not to be lenient — see his name.
