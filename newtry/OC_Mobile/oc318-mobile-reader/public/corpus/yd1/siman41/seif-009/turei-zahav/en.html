Needle found in omasum. Explanation: all covered by omasum; for otherwise it is obvious treifah, for it is no better than found in cavity of body. It appears to me the examiner need not check courtyard of liver whether it has a puncture or not, for we rely on majority like we need not check after other treifot except lung adhesions that are common; and even though I saw in some examinations to check afterward — it appears to me this is not obligatory; and a fortiori one should not punish the examiner for this as with other treifot.
