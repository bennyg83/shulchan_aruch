For any excess is considered as removed. This implies even if they are not adhered to one another in place of its vitality and in place of the gall — for any excess is considered as removed, meaning as though both were removed entirely; therefore wherever two livers are found — treifah; and so wrote Tosafot Chadashim general rule 89 law 11; and so Maharshal ruled Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 18. However Maharshal wrote there: if the two livers begin below that olive in place of gall and in place of its vitality — it is obvious it is kosher according to everyone, like excess of the leg that goes out below the knee (below siman 55 seif 4) — end; and one must say it is worse if excess of liver is found that is not adhered at all to the liver than if adhered below that olive, for since it begins below — from the place of its vitality and place of the gall it appears it is only liver tendrils; and this requires study; and see at end of siman 43 and siman 50 and 54 and 55 what I wrote from this.
