And some say that this is not practiced in these lands, etc. The Rokeach wrote: A mourner is obligated in wrapping, like now, when they pull the hat before the eyes all seven days, and on Shabbat privately but not publicly; end quote. My teacher and father-in-law, of blessed memory, wrote: According to this, immediately when the shaliach tzibbur begins “Barekhu,” the mourner should raise the hat, so that it not be a public matter; end quote.
