The custom. The reason is that the older practice of head covering brought great ridicule from the gentiles and from servants and maidservants among us. Nevertheless, some slight form of wrapping should still be observed, as is done today by pulling the hat down before the eyes throughout the seven days. On Shabbos this should be done privately, not publicly. Bach writes that therefore, when the chazzan begins Barechu, the mourner should lift the hat so that the mourning not be publicly evident.
