Obligated to stand. See responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 4, who wrote: even if engaged in Torah one is obligated to stand before a Torah scroll, for the essence of the law of standing they learned in the Gemara from a fortiori: before its students they stand before it — all the more so; and behold above siman 244 seif 11 it states one must stand before a Torah scholar even during study — if so, if a Torah scroll is greater, plainly one must stand before it even during study — see there.
