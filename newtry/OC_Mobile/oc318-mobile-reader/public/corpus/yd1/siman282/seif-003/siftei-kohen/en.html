And if he was afraid, etc. — and Bach extended on this and concludes: for practical law we rule here and there stringently, that not in a place of danger it is permitted only in his lap against his heart; and even in other books; and in a place of danger too it is permitted only to hang it behind him, but not to ride on it; and as Rabbi Yonah wrote to explain the words of Rav Alfasi, for thus the language implies as he explained — end quote; and certainly where possible to be stringent in honor of Torah is preferable; but where not possible — permitted even to ride on them.
