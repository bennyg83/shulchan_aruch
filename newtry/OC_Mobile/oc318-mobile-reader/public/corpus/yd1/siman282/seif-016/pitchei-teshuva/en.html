There is one who permits. See in Yad HaKatanah Hilchot Sefer Torah chapter 4 positive commandment 23 ot 9, who wrote: that which Shulchan Aruch brings here and also in Orach Chayim siman 133 seif 2 — two views on this; namely specifically a Torah scroll that an individual bought for himself, for we say regarding it that it is like snatching a mitzvah from the marketplace; but a Torah scroll that he wrote for himself or hired a scribe to write for him to fulfill the positive commandment of writing — certainly forbidden to sell it; and therefore he wrote above siman 270 seif 1 in general: and he is not permitted to sell it, even though there it speaks of an individual — see there.
