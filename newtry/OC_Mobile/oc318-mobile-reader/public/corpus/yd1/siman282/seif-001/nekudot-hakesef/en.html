(Siman 282 in Taz s.k. 4) "And it seems to me this should be taught," etc. — his words are not correct that he learned thus from Rama's responsum, where he wrote: behold here the ark is not considered except as one vessel unless the ark is large and comes out from the category of vessel and divides jurisdiction for itself — and this is explained in tractate Ohalot, and Rambam wrote in Hilchot Tum'at Met on this. However it was already explained that an ordinary ark is large and nevertheless is not considered except as one vessel, as explained — requires study — end quote; and therefore he also learned from tumah to here, and the matter is not relevant here, for there the reason is since it is not called a tent, therefore it is a vessel and does not interpose before tumah; for regarding tumah specifically we need a tent that interposes, but a vessel does not interpose since it receives tumah; and that we rule like Rabbi — this is because it is not called a tent; and Rabbi Rama did not bring from tumah except that if the name came out from the category of vessel, all the more so here; and he also left this requires study; but where the name does not interpose there is no proof here. And what he brought as proof from chapter 3 that one may not place it in a sack, etc. — not comparable, for there the reason is since he sits directly upon them — even though sacks interpose, nevertheless his body presses upon them and his body shakes the books; but here behold he sits only on the ark, and one cannot bring proof from what Tosafot wrote in chapter HaKometz (Menachot 32b) in the name of Yerushalmi that it is forbidden to sleep on a chest full of books — for there regarding sleep is different, as in Piskei Tosafot we are concerned he might exhale or might have relations — see there. And furthermore, Rabbi Rama left this requires study because our books are not Assyrian script for the Name.
