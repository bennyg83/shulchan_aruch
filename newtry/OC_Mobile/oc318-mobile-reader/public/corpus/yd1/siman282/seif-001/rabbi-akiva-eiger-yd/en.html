(Siman 282 seif 2 in gloss) Beit Yosef. In the name of Rambam. Per Kesef Mishneh that Beit Yosef brought from Rambam: even in Chumashim made as scrolls one need not stand before them; but in our books that are not made as scrolls — even if the whole Torah is written in them one need not stand. And Rashba's view: in Chumashim as scrolls their law is like a Torah scroll and by law one is obligated to stand before them; and in our Chumashim that are not scrolls — fit to be stringent; and see in responsum Or Ne'elam (siman 1).
