To conduct oneself with honor toward a Sefer Torah, and the law of its accessories. In it are 19 seifim.
A person is obligated to conduct himself with great honor toward a Sefer Torah; and it is a mitzvah to designate a place for it and honor that place and adorn it greatly. And he should not spit opposite a Sefer Torah; nor expose his nakedness opposite it; nor stretch out his legs opposite it; nor place it on his head like a burden; nor turn his back to it unless it is ten tefachim higher than him. Rather he sits before it with gravity, awe, and fear—for it is the faithful witness over all who come into the world, as it says, "And it shall be there with you for a witness"—and one honors it according to one's ability.
