Beit Hillel writes that it is forbidden to suspend a Sefer Torah in a case as tefillin are suspended. Rather, it must be placed down. He brings proof from the Gemara in Berachos 24. He likewise forbids suspending it from one's neck. At first glance, one can say that it is permitted to suspend it on one's arm, based on Sanhedrin 22 concerning the king's Sefer Torah, which he makes like an amulet and suspends on his arm. Rashi implies that this means literally on his arm, since the law is derived from the verse, “He is at my right; I shall not falter.” This is unlike Lechem Mishneh in Hilkhot Melachim, who writes that it does not mean literally on his arm. Rambam omitted this law, and I explained his reason in my novellae there.
