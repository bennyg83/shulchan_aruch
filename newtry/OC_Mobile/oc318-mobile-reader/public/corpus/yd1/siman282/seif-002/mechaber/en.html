One who sees a Sefer Torah when it is being carried is obligated to stand before it; and everyone stands until the one carrying it stands still and brings it to its place, or until it is covered from their eyes. {Rama: But before our chumashim one need not stand (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rambam); and some are stringent (Rashba siman 144). One who hears the sound of one carrying a Sefer Torah—even if he does not see it—is obligated to stand (Beit Yosef in the name of Rav Rabbenu Menachem).}
