Hearing. Shach wrote: so it is in all the books, but we do not practice thus; and so Beit Yosef wrote: they practiced not to stand before them, and never was heard one opening his mouth to protest that one must stand before a Chumash — and it seems the text should read thus: some are stringent and say "one who hears the sound of one carrying a Torah scroll," etc., and below it refers to this; and so Levush has the version — end quote. And Taz wrote: in Beit Yosef he brings in the name of R' Menachem that therefore they practiced to make bells — but it does not seem right to me, for if so on Shabbat one may not take out that Torah scroll, for it makes sound; therefore it seems to me one should not make those bells, and therefore Rama did not bring it; and we already saw this in a synagogue and they protested and removed them — end quote.
