Sound of one carrying a Torah scroll. In Beit Yosef he brings in the name of R' Menachem, and therefore they practiced to make bells; but it does not seem right to me, for if so on Shabbat one may not take out that Torah scroll, for it makes sound, as in Orach Chayim siman 305 seif 11 regarding a bell on an animal's neck and a coin in its neck — due to prohibition of making sound on Shabbat; and even though there in siman 339 Rama wrote: some say nowadays all is permitted, for we are not expert in making musical instruments, etc. — nevertheless since according to the first view there is prohibition, why should we do initially a prohibition, especially with a Torah scroll. And further it does not seem he is lenient there except regarding those things that are not really similar to musical instruments, such as clapping and slapping by hand; but regarding that bell that is more similar to a musical instrument — there is prohibition according to all; therefore it seems to me one should not make those bells; and therefore Rama did not bring it; and we already saw this in a synagogue and they protested and removed them.
