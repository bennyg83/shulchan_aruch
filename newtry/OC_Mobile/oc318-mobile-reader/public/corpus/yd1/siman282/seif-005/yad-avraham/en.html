He wrote in Beit Yosef: forbidden to hang a Torah scroll in a case as one hangs tefillin — only must place it; and Rabbi Magen from Berachot (24b); and so he forbids hanging it on the neck. And ostensibly one could say on his arm is permitted from that which is in Sanhedrin (22b) regarding a king's Torah scroll that he makes it like an amulet and hangs on his arm — and it implies from Rashi that on his arm literally he speaks, since he learns it from the verse "from my right I shall not move." And not like what Lechem Mishneh wrote in Hilchot Melachim that it is not literally on his arm he speaks. And Rambam omitted it, and I explained his reason in my commentary there.
