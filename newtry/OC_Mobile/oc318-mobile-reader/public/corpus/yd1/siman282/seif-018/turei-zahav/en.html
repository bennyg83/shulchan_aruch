If there were errors, etc. — this is from words of Rivash that Beit Yosef brings in Orach Chayim siman 153: if one letter is missing or one letter extra — since it is invalid it does not have sanctity of a Torah scroll and it is like Chumashim that the seven good men of the city sell in the presence of the townspeople — end quote; automatically the same law applies to an individual with his Torah scroll.
