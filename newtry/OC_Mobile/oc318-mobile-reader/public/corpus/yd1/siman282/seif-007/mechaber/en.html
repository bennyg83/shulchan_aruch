It is forbidden to sit on a bed on which a Sefer Torah lies. {Rama: And all the more so it is forbidden to place it on the ground; and the same law applies to other books (Beit Yosef in the name of R' Rabbenu Menachem and in the name of Orach Chaim and Kol Bo). And even on the steps made before the Holy Ark it is forbidden to place books (Hagahos Maimoniyos). And a person should not place a Sefer Torah on his knees with both forearms upon it (Maharil). And it appears to me that the same law applies to other books.}
