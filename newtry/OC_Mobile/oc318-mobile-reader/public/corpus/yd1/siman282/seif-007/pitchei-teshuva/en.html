To place it on the ground. Tiferet LeMoshe writes that if many books fell together and lifting them individually would cause significant delay, they may be lifted as one stack, though the comparison to Eruvin may be limited to Shabbat.
And he should not place. Bach permits sitting on a chest containing books in pressing circumstances, especially where the chest also contains other objects and is not their dedicated container. A Torah scroll requires greater stringency. Teshuvah MeAhavah strongly condemns using printed sacred pages for wrapping merchandise or bookbinding.
