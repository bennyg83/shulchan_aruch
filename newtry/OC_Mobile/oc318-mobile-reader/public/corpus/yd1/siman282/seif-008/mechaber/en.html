A house in which there is a Sefer Torah—one should not use it for his bed until he removes it; and if he has no place to remove it, he makes before it a partition ten tefachim high. But by placing a vessel within a vessel that is not designated, it is not permitted except for tefillin and other sacred writings and chumashim—but not for a Sefer Torah. And the Rambam permits even for a Sefer Torah. And if he spread his tallit over the chest in which it is placed, it is considered like a vessel within a vessel.
