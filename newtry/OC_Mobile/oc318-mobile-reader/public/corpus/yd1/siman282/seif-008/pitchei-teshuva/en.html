And see Tiferet leMoshe who wrote it appears there is some leniency where the cover of the box does not touch the books — see there. And see responsum Teshuvah MeAhavah part 1 siman 9 — he wrote regarding printed sacred writings that people treat with disrespect and sell in shops with food items — it is a criminal offense, for printing is sanctity like complete writing, as the Taz wrote above siman 271; and a court should protest to the printer not to sell them to shops; and so Magen Avraham in siman 154 note 14 wondered at the great ones of the generation who do not protest those who bind holy scripture books with pages — that is an absolute prohibition unlike Beit Aharon there who wrote they relied on what the Taz wrote note 6 — see there; meaning it is preferable to make a light accessory of sanctity rather than leave entirely idle and genizah — that is not so, for the Taz deals with an accessory of sanctity and not with sacred writings that are sanctity themselves — see there.
