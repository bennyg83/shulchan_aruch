Honor. Taz wrote: it seems to me those people who during study hold a book and want to raise it — stretch their hand and take another book and place under the book they study — there is prohibition due to disgrace, for thus he disgraces the book to be used for whatever one can do with wood or stone; unless the lower book is already placed — certainly permitted to place the second upon it. And in responsum of Maharil he brings in the name of Sefer Agudah: one may not make Torah implements from cloth used by a commoner; nevertheless it seems this is specifically something that is itself a sacred implement, such as those mentioned in Orach Chayim siman 154; but an implement of an implement, such as those mentioned there — permitted to make from cloth; and if already made a sacred implement from it — forbidden afterward to make from it an implement of an implement; we do not say to be lenient that holiness did not take hold in it — end quote.
