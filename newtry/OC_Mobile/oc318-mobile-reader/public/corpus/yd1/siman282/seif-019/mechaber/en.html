It is permitted to place a Sefer Torah on a Sefer Torah; and one places chumashim on Prophets and Writings; but one does not place Prophets and Writings on chumashim, nor chumashim on a Sefer Torah. {Rama: But Writings on Prophets (or the reverse) is permitted (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran in the name of Tosafot, first chapter of Bava Batra). And all this speaks of two bindings, each bound by itself; but in one binding, all is permitted (Mordechai, chapter HaShutfin). And see above siman 246 that it is forbidden to contemplate words of Torah in filthy places—and this is because of honor of Torah.}
