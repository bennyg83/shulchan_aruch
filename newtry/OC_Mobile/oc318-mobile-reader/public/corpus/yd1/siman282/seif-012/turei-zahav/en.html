And it was placed in it. Mere designation is ineffective; an actual sacred use is required. Likewise, if one says, “This silver is designated for an accessory of a Torah scroll,” the declaration does not create a binding consecration, since he may sell it. The rule of a binding verbal pledge applies specifically to charity.
