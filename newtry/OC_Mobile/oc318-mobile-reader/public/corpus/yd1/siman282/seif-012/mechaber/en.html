A case prepared for a Sefer Torah in which it was placed; and likewise the cloths, the ark, and the tower in which they place a Sefer Torah—even though they do not place a Sefer Torah in it when it is alone, but only when it is in its case; and likewise a chair prepared to place a Sefer Torah upon it and upon which it was placed—all are accessories of sanctity and are forbidden. And after they wear out or break, they are put in genizah.
