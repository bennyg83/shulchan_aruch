This is the 'ark' mentioned in the Gemara. Alternatively, it is the chest in which the Torah scroll is stored. The 'tower' is the table upon the bimah on which the reader places the Torah scroll during the reading, following the verse in Ezra, 'He stood upon a wooden tower.' The 'chair' is the seat mentioned in the Gemara, for some places prepare a chair upon which to place one Torah scroll when two scrolls are taken out.
