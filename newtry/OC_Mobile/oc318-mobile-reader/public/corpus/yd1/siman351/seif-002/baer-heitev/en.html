Who invalidate. The Bach wrote that one should not invalidate them; rather, one should tie them to one another or cover them under the corners. The Taz wrote: when Rabbeinu Gershom passed away, he said, "tzitzit outside," and they disagreed in explaining his words. Some say he meant that they should remove the tzitzit from the tallit, and others say that this is what he meant: they should place them outside the coffin; end quote.
