In a tallit that has tzitzit. She'elat Yaavetz, part 1, responsum 124, writes that one who was unable during life to fulfill tzitzit because his hands were disabled may perhaps not be buried in a tallit, based on Tosafot that one who was not careful with tzitzit in life is not buried in them because this mocks the helpless dead. Since Tosafot's reasoning is not conclusive, however, one who provides him a tallit loses nothing, especially if the deceased left funds for it. Yet one need not collect money or use the burial society's fund for this purpose.
