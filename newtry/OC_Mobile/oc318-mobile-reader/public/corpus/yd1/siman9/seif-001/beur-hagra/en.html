**(Likkut) If, etc.** Not like R. Zeira as written regarding idol-worship knife—and derive from it; and same regarding knife of neveilah (Rashba brings knife of neveilah and treifah see there)—peeling or rinsing; and some permit. As written 17b “did not R. Zeira say,” etc.—thus halachah decided and they did not answer—for no need to reject as Ra'osh in Ra'avad's name and so Tosafot haRosh (and he also rejects proof from knife of neveilah and treifah); nevertheless wrote to be stringent (end quote).
**And some permit.** Like Ra'avad's view R. Zeira not difficult except b'dieved; and what we concluded there that checking required—three times is l'chatchila.
**If he knew, etc.** As R. Yonah wrote [and so Ra'avad]—for there different he did not know to be careful and even b'dieved forbidden.
