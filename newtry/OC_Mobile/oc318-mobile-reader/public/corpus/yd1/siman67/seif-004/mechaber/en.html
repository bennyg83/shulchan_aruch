Blood that coagulated due to a blow — it is forbidden to cook it until he cuts the place and salts it well; but it is permitted to roast it whether on a spit or on coals, even without cutting and salting. {Rama: And some say that for this reason one should be stringent to scrape the slaughter-house or to cut that meat before salting, since the blood coagulated there at the time of slaughter (Arukh general rule 1 and Kol Bo).}
