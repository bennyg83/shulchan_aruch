Vinegar into which meat was placed once in order to constrict it — one may not constrict meat in it a second time because its strength has already weakened; but any [vinegar] in which nothing was ever pickled — permitted to pickle in it even if it is not strong. {Rama: Some say that in our time people are not expert in pickling and it is forbidden to constrict in vinegar (Rabbenu Yerucham, and so Beit Yosef in his name), and such is the custom in these lands that they do not constrict meat in vinegar before salting, and one should not change; nevertheless b'dieved it is permitted. And see below siman 73.}
