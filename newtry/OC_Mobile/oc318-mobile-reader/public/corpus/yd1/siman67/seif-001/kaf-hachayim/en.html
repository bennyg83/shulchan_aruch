(1) [Seif 1] Regarding blood of domestic animal and wild animal too there is no kares prohibition except on lifeblood, etc.; and slaughter-blood that congealed and when squeezed from it clear water exudes that has no redness — they are certainly forbidden, and if they fell into a pot it is obvious they forbid.
