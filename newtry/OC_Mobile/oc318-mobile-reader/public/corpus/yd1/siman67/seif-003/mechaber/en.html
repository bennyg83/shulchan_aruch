One who breaks the neck-bone of a domestic animal — it is forbidden to eat from its flesh raw unless he salts it well, and through salting it is permitted even for a pot; for roasting it is permitted even without salting. {Rama: And the custom is to be stringent to cut it and salt it even for roasting; and the same applies if one cuts meat from the slaughter-house before the soul departs (Ran). Some say one should be careful l'chatchila to break the animal's neck-bone or to thrust a knife into its heart in order to hasten its death because it causes blood to be absorbed in the limbs (Binyamin Zeev and Beit Yosef in the name of Shibolei HaLeket).}
