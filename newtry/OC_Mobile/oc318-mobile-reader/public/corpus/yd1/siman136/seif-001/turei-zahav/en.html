The koshering, if it belonged to a non-Jew. Meaning: if it originally belonged to a non-Jew and a Jew purchased it from him; but this does not include earthenware vessels — for if its original use was in the hands of a non-Jew, pouring is required even if it is not stored permanently; whereas in this case where it originally belonged to a Jew, nothing more is required, as set out in siman 135 seif 4.
One should not prohibit after the fact. Above in siman 122 seif 9 we elaborated that we do not hold this way — rather even after the fact it is forbidden.
