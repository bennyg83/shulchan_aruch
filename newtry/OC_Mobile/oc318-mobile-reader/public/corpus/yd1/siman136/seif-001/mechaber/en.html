One who sends vessels of wine in the hand of a non-Jew must seal them; it contains 1 seif. One who sends, in the hand of a non-Jew, vessels that are designated for wine must seal them with 1 seal, so that it will be recognizable if the non-Jew put wine into them; and there is one who requires a seal within a seal. {Rama: And if he transgressed and sent them without a seal, one may make it kosher according to the manner of kashering, if it was of a non-Jew (Tur; and see se’if katan 3). But if he already transgressed and used it, one should not forbid after the fact (and see above siman 122).} And if they are large barrels, it is permitted to leave them in the house of the non-Jew craftsman for a day or two to repair them, for with such a large vessel it is not common to use it temporarily.
