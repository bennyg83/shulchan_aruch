And it is not comparable to above siman 118 and 130 where in boiled wine or vinegar one seal suffices, since there is no concern for libation — for there the gentile cannot replace it except with boiled wine or vinegar which are slightly inferior; if the superiority of one were greater than the other, the substitution would be evident and he would be caught as a thief; and for a small superiority he does not bother to falsify; but here, because of frequent use, he does bother to falsify — Ran.
