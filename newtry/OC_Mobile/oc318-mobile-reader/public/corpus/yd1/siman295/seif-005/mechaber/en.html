And specifically in this manner, which is called by the name "tree" before it was layered; but it is permitted to sow next to the tree even though the roots of the vegetables enter within the roots of the tree, since the roots do not have the name "tree." And even regarding layering—specifically in a vine shoot because it is soft and the roots of seeds enter it; but one who layers a branch from another tree—it is permitted to sow on top of it even if there are not three fingerbreadths of earth on it.
