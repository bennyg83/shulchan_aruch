And specifically, etc. — Rosh; and he brought proof from a single vine whose work is 6 tefachim even though the roots spread outside six; and we say: if there was a fence between them, this one leans on the fence from here and that one leans on the fence from here. But Rosh in the first chapter of Kiddushin, brought in Beit Yosef and Beit HaGadol, wrote that vine roots spread downward from 6 tefachim; therefore it is permitted from the side; and so Rosh there — and from the side even for a vine it is permitted, for they do not walk from the side. And Tosafot Kiddushin 39a dh "Lo." And it appears, etc.; and Tosafot's words require study.
And even in layering, etc. — in Yerushalmi, and per Rosh's explanation there — "what are we dealing with," etc., and because of seeds on the vine — meaning specifically on the vine; and as Rosh wrote there in conclusion; and likewise Tosafot Bava Batra there s.v. hamariv, etc.
