And in Yerushalmi chapter 1 of Pe'ah it teaches: they sow seeds and tree seeds together, and one who sows grape pits with wheat receives forty lashes. R. Zeira: it is written "You shall not sow your vineyard kilayim" — the principal of your vineyard you shall not sow kilayim. R. Yudan of Kapudkia asked before R. Yosi: there we say tree seeds are called seeds, and you say tree seeds are not called seeds? He said to him: there Scripture limited that gentiles' way is not to call them seeds; but here Scripture expanded "every seed that is sown"; meaning for purpose of fitness. And so in Gemara Bekhorot 54a: just as "tirosh and yitzhar," etc. — "tirosh and dagan," etc.; as above, wheat and grape pits are liable on account of kilayei hakerem, and like R. Yonatan, not like R. Zeira said: "You shall not sow your vineyard kilayim — the principal of your vineyard you shall not sow kilayim"; R. Yudan of Kapudkia asked before R. Yosi: there you say there are no tree seeds, etc., as above; and meaning "the principal of your vineyard" is not like R. Yoshiah who said with kilayei zera'im "You shall not sow your vineyard" — two wheats, etc.; but "the principal," etc., because all trees are permitted with seeds, as above; and that which was asked there "you say," etc.; and Beit HaGadol and so Beit Yosef; but he retracted in dh "vehahu" and it is an error, for there it was stated to the contrary that it is kilayim; see there. And Rosh brought it in chapter 1 mishnah 2 Yerushalmi: R. Yosi, etc.; R. Yosi said, etc.; and so there end of mishnah 7 Yerushalmi: the version of R. Yosi switches, etc.; but there is no difficulty — any two kinds are called kilayim even though they are permitted; and this is what is meant "its principal is kilayim" — meaning of two kinds; and what is written mishnah 7 "switches," etc. — meaning R. Yosi who permits vegetable with tree because they do not crossbreed with each other; and there he said "one removes," etc., and "they join" there, etc. And in Tur it is written: but it is permitted to mix tree seeds with field seeds and sow them together, for the Torah forbade outside the Land only grafting, etc.; and Beit Yosef wrote: it appears from his words that in the Land it is forbidden. Heaven forbid to attribute such an error to him, for the Gemara in Bekhorot above is in the Land, since it says "tirosh and yitzhar" are kilayim, and that is in the Land specifically, as in Kiddushin there and as written in siman 296; rather it is a scribal error and must read "and the Torah did not forbid," etc.; and this is a matter by itself; and so Rosh.
