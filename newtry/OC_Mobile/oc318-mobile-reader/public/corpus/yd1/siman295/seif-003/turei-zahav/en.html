And seed of a tree as one. In Tur it is written in this that it is permitted outside the Land; and Beit Yosef wrote it implies in the Land it is forbidden in this — and he was not precise, for behold mishnah and Rambam deal in the Land and nevertheless they permitted — end quote; and likewise above from him citing Rambam chapter 5 of Kilayim; and in Derishah he wanted to settle that that permission is because of prohibition of grafting, but nevertheless there is prohibition because of kilayim of seeds; and afterward he rejected that answer. And Magen Avraham z"l wrote plainly thus — for so Rambam wrote explicitly in chapter 5 of Laws of Kilayim, unlike Beit Yosef's words; and I am astonished an hour — one: at all these sages, for truly in chapter 5 nothing of this was mentioned, only in chapter 1 so explicitly; and this is what he wrote: permitted to sow seeds and seed of a tree as one; and Raavad wrote on this it is not clear and proved from Yerushalmi seeds with tree are forbidden; and if so all is proper — certainly Tur holds like Raavad who is stringent, namely in the Land. And it is astonishing how Raavad's challenge escaped them; and more astonishing Kessef Mishneh did not challenge Raavad at all here, and here wrote it is plain and learned from mishnah — and therefore clear Beit Yosef was not precise in what he wrote on Tur.
