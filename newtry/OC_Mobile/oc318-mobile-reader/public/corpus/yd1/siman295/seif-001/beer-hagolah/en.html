The wording of Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilchot Kilayim, from Shmuel's statement, Kiddushin 39a, deriving 'your field' by analogy to 'your animal': just as the prohibition concerning your animal is crossbreeding, so too concerning your field.
There in Shmuel's statement, from the analogy to 'your animal.'
Mishnah 7, chapter 1 of Kilayim, following the first tanna.
