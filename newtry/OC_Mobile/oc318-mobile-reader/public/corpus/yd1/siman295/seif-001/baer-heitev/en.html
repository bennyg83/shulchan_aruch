Included. The Derishah wrote that it appears that if one sowed two types of trees in Eretz Yisrael, the prohibition is no less than kilayim of field seeds, etc. But he did not look at the Rambam who wrote explicitly that it is likewise permitted to mix tree seeds and sow them together, for there is no kilayim with trees except grafting alone — and this is even in Eretz Yisrael, for he wrote "you have no kilayim," etc. Thus far the Taz.
