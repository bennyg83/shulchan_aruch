Included in what is said "your field," etc. Meaning kilayim of seeds plainly deals with this; but also kilayim of trees. Tur wrote: this "your field you shall not sow" does not deal with kilayim of seeds — for those are not forbidden outside the Land but, etc. Certainly the plain sense of the verse refers to prohibition of kilayim of seeds, and with this we exclude outside the Land as it is written "your field"; but for kilayim of trees we say in Gemara it is forbidden even outside the Land, for we compare to prohibition of crossbreeding animal forbidden even outside the Land; and on this Tur said this comparison comes only for kilayim of trees, but not kilayim of seeds. And Derishah wrote: it appears to me from this language written here that kilayim of trees are included in "your field you shall not sow kilayim" — for if one sows two kinds of trees in the Land, its prohibition is no less than kilayim of the field, etc.; and besides his honor he did not examine Rambam chapter 1 of Kilayim who wrote explicitly: and likewise permitted to mix tree seeds and sow them as one, for you have no kilayim in trees except grafting alone — end quote. And this even in the Land of Israel, for he wrote you have no kilayim, etc.; and so is evident in Bekhorot 54 explicitly.
