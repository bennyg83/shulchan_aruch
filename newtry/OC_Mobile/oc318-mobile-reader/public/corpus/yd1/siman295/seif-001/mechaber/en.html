The laws of grafting trees. In it are 7 seifim. Kilayim of trees are included in what was said, "Your field you shall not sow with kilayim." How so? One who grafts a tree onto a tree—for example, grafted a shoot of apple onto citron or citron onto apple—receives lashes d'oraisa everywhere, both in Eretz Yisrael and in the Diaspora. And likewise one who grafts a vegetable onto a tree or a tree onto a vegetable receives lashes everywhere.
