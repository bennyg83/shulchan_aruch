My husband or fruit. A.D. B.C.:
(Collection) But, etc. In second chapter of Pesachim (39) and R. Zugi, etc.; and in Yerushalmi beginning of Kilayim — R. Yosi in name of R. Yochanan: all are pairs of pairs; from what on every chapter was it stated, or on this halachah? From what Rav said — five vegetables with which a person fulfills his obligation on Pesach — all are permitted to sow in a garden bed; and he said this statement of Rav disputes R. Yochanan — this said on every chapter it was stated; Rava in name of Rav — all pairs exchange their kinds; Rav's view is there he says all are one kind, and here he says thus — no, Rav said all are one kind — he only said they are kinds of vegetables and all permitted to sow in a garden bed, as written in Pesachim — end quote.
And likewise permitted, etc. — as written in Yerushalmi chapter 1, and Rosh brought there mishnah 1 Yerushalmi — "your field you shall not sow kilayim" — how so? Even two kinds, etc.; and there in mishnah 7 — "henceforth forbidden to graft black fig on white fig"; R. Avin said — and not from kilayim of cloth you learned: just as kilayim of cloth I forbade you — two kinds, not this from that kind and not that from this kind — so kilayim I forbade you in all places — not this from that kind and not that from this kind.
