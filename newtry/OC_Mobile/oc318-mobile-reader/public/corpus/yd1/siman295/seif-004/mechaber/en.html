There is a prohibition of grafting even under the ground—for example, a vine shoot that was layered under the ground: it is forbidden to sow vegetables upon it unless there is three tefachim of earth on top of it; and even if the layering was inside a gourd that dried out, or inside a pipe of earthenware, because it takes root in it and it becomes grafting of vegetable onto tree. And if there was less than this on top, it is forbidden to sow on top of it; but it is permitted to sow at its side. But layering in rock is difficult: even if there is no earth on it but three fingerbreadths, it is permitted to bring seed upon it. When does this apply? When the main vine is not visible; but if it is visible, one must distance six tefachim in every direction and then sow, as one distances from every individual vine that was not layered.
