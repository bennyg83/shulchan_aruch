Baraita, Bava Batra 19b; Tosefta, chapter 3 of Kilayim; and Yerushalmi, chapter 7. The Babylonian Talmud explains that ordinary seeds do not spread roots sideways. The Ran writes that although vine roots spread sideways, once they descend three handbreadths beneath the seeds, the roots of the seeds do not enter there.
