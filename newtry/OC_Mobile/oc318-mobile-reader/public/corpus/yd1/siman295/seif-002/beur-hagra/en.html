It is forbidden, etc. This is because of the prohibition of placing a stumbling block before the blind, since a gentile is commanded regarding this matter, as Rambam writes in chapter 10 of Hilchos Melachim, halachah 6. The discussion in Kiddushin somewhat supports the view of Rabbi Elazar, as do the Yerushalmi and the discussion in Chullin cited above according to Tosafos' final answer. See also the Behag. The Kesef Mishneh writes likewise. His words are puzzling, for if so, why did Rambam write this regarding grafting trees but not earlier regarding mixed seeds? Moreover, he should have written, “It is forbidden to instruct a gentile,” etc., as he writes in chapter 16 of Hilchos Issurei Biah regarding castration. The Kesef Mishneh evidently overlooked the above words of Rambam. The Mishneh LaMelech writes likewise.
