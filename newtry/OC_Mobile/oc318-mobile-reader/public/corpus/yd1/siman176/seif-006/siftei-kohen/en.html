This is permitted. Because rental is not due until the end (as written in CM siman 317); therefore when he takes from him a sela per month — which amounts to 12 sela — this is not payment for the waiting of money, for he was not obligated to pay the rental until the end of the month; and what he said to him "if you give me from now here are ten sela for you" — had he paid in advance he would have reduced the rental for him and rented to him at less than its value — Talmud and Rashi.
But if already etc. That is to say, he took possession and became obligated to give him now the rental of ten sela per year — it is forbidden to say "give a sela per month"; and all the more if he already became obligated for the rental — meaning the payment time of year-end arrived and he waits for him — and he gives him something in exchange for the waiting — it is forbidden; and it is clarified here that there is no distinction between rental money and loan money: always any payment for waiting — is forbidden; and likewise Darkei Moshe; and so Beit Yosef above end of siman 160; and not like Bach siman 161 seif 2.
