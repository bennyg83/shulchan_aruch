It is permitted to increase the rent of the land. (Language of Rambam, perek 7 of Hilchos Malveh, din 8.) How so? He rented him the courtyard, and said to him — before he took possession of it — “If from now you give me [the rent], it is yours for 10 sela’im each year; and if you give the rent month by month, it is for a sela each month,” this is permitted. And similarly, regarding the wages of a person, it is also permitted. {Rama: But if he already took possession of it to rent it to him for 10 sela’im per year, it is forbidden to say to him, “Give each month 1 sela, and I will wait for you for the money” (Beit Yosef in the name of the students of Rashba). And all the more so, if he was already obligated to him for the rent, and he gives him something for delaying the money, for it is forbidden (Hagahos Ashiri; and Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek Eizehu Neshech; and in Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 8). It is permitted to increase the dowry of bridegrooms; for example, he vowed a dowry to his daughter and stipulated with his son-in-law that each year that he leaves the dowry with him he will give him such-and-such wage — it is permitted, for this is only like adding to him dowry; and specifically when they stipulated so before the marriage, but not afterward, for it is “ager natar” (Rashba, siman 429; and Rivash, siman 402). And see below siman 177.}
