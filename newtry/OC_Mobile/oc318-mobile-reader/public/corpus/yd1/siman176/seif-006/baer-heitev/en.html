Permitted. Because rental is paid only at the end, as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 317 — therefore what he reduces for him now is because prices are falling against him. Beit Yosef wrote in name of Rashba author of Tosafot that the permit applies only if he enters the land immediately, otherwise it is watchman's fee. Darkei Moshe wrote: however, Maggid Mishneh wrote that even though he does not dwell immediately in the courtyard — it is permitted, since in land rental he acquires immediately, unlike worker hire since the worker is not obligated from now. {Rama: (And Nachalat Binyamin wrote that it appears the conclusion of Darkei Moshe is like Rashba, that there is a distinction between worker and land rental — see there.)}
