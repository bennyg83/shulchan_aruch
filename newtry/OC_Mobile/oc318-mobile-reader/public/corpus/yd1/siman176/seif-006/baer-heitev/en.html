Darkei Moshe cites Maggid Mishneh that land rental is acquired immediately even if occupancy begins later, unlike hiring a worker. Taz writes that the same applies whenever one presently obligates himself to another and stipulates that if payment is delayed beyond a certain date, an additional monthly amount will be added. Where the condition is included in the first marriage agreements before any dowry debt exists, the additional amounts may be part of the gift. If the dowry obligation was already created and profit is added only at the wedding, a proper heter is required. Levush likewise writes that an agreement made after the wedding is full interest, and recommends structuring the entire obligation as a stepped principal amount from the outset.
