(Siman 176, Taz s.k. 7) And Darkei Moshe wrote, however, etc. And it implies that the conclusion of Darkei Moshe is like Rashba (R' Shmuel ben Aderet) and not like Hagahot Maimoniyot in name of Rashba author of Tosafot; and it appears that even though in Tosafot chapter HaSapinah end of daf 86 they also wrote in name of R' Shmuel ben Avraham as Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote — nevertheless the essence is as the Maggid wrote in name of Rashba (R' Shmuel ben Aderet): the reasonable reason is specifically regarding a worker whose body is not acquired to him, for he can retract — unlike a courtyard where the substance of the courtyard is acquired to him immediately. And what difference whether he dwells in it or not — he can do with it from now as he wishes; and likewise in Nimukei Yosef chapter HaSapinah it is written like Rashba (R' Shmuel ben Aderet) — meaning we do not say in Gemara chapter Eizehu Neshekh thus regarding land rental, only in chapter HaSapinah regarding a worker; and likewise it implies from words of Rambam, Tur, and Mechaber that regarding land they wrote without qualification that it is permitted, and immediately afterward nearby regarding a worker they wrote that he must begin from now; and if it were that they are equivalent, they should have written likewise regarding land rental, and should not have written the law of a worker by itself but should have combined and taught them only as we said above. Hagah: see Rashbam in chapter HaSapinah beginning daf 87 regarding "could it be disparaging in rental — who forbids," etc., who explained that a worker's way is to hire himself for whatever when he has nothing to eat, etc.; and in my humble opinion it appears he forced unnecessarily that thus he challenges — could it be disparaging in rental who forbids, behold we learned in chapter Eizehu Neshekh one may increase on rental and one may not increase on sale, and the reason as explained there: rental is paid only at the end — see there, investigate.
