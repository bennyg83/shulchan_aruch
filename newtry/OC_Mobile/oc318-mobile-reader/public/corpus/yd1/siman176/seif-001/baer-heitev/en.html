In existence. If the recipient spent the money, then even if the identical coins later returned to his possession, payment for their use is forbidden as interest. Beit Yosef explains that this refers to coins rented for display or study. If rented for expenditure, they are forbidden even before being spent, because a money loan is deemed expended from the moment received.
Permit. Shach writes that Beit Yosef did not forbid practical reliance upon the leniency except where the borrower stipulated that he would not be believed regarding loss of principal unless the cantor and sexton confirmed it. Without such a condition, it is plainly permitted. Rama's wording should read, 'It is forbidden to rent them for expenditure.'
