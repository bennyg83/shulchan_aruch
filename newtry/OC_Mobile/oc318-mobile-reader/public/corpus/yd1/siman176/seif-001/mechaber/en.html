Which renting is permitted and which is forbidden; it contains 8 se’ifim. It is forbidden for a person to rent out his money; that he should say to him, “I will rent to you 10 dinarim for a dinar per month.” And this applies when he rents them to spend them. But if he rented them in order to learn with them or to display [them], and he returns them to him intact, it is permitted. {Rama: And this is specifically when he accepted upon himself only responsibility for theft and loss; but if he accepted upon himself all responsibility, it is forbidden (Beit Yosef in the name of Ba’al HaTerumos). And if the renter accepted upon himself all responsibility — both theft and loss and responsibility for accidents — some permit even when he rents to spend them (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 302), as below siman 177. However, if he accepted upon himself only theft and loss and not accidents, or accidents and not theft and loss, it is forbidden to spend them as their rent (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos; and Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 5; and Semag; and Rivash, simanim 305 and 308; and Terumas HaDeshen there; and according to Rosh). Nevertheless, it is only like avak ribbis; but if the renter accepted no responsibility at all, it is fixed interest (Rivash there).}
