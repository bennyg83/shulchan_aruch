When one rents them with permission to spend them. Although he will later return the very same items, nevertheless, since he had permission to spend them and the recipient bore responsibility for them, it is considered a loan, and it is forbidden to pay rental compensation, for that would constitute ribbis. However, in seif 2, concerning utensils, we say that the compensation is not for the loan, but for the depreciation of the utensil through its use, and the owner accepts that depreciation upon himself. Therefore, regarding a utensil that does not depreciate, such as utensils of silver and gold, concerning which Tosafos wrote in the chapter HaRibbis, 69a, it is forbidden to receive compensation when it is given as a loan and the recipient bears responsibility, just as with money.
