But if he hired them etc. However if he spent the money — even if those same coins came into his hand — they are forbidden because of ribbit, the Gamirei; and Beit Yosef wrote: and that is specifically when he hired them to study with or to look at; but if to spend — even if he did not spend them it is also forbidden, for since a loan is given to spend, from the moment they came into his hand it is as if he spent them.
Some permit etc. Beit Yosef wrote that it implies from the words of Terumas HaDeshen that nevertheless one should not act on the lenient ruling; but that is not so — for that is not what it says there, but rather where the borrower guarantees and stipulates that they should not be believed regarding the principal that was lost by coercion, theft, or loss except through the cantor and the sexton; but where he stipulates nothing — it is obviously permitted, as written below siman 177 s.k. 17 — see there.
It is forbidden to hire them in order to spend them — this is the correct reading.
