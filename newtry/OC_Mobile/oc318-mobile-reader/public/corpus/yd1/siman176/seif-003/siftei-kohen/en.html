Specifically utensils etc. And likewise he can stipulate that if it breaks through an accident he will pay its value — even though by law a renter is exempt from accidents, as written in CM siman 306; and if so, since he assessed them at a monetary value and takes on accident-liability, the money is like a loan with him and the rent he paid appears as ribbit — nevertheless it is permitted, since he is only obligated for its value if it breaks, and as long as it is intact he returns it as is; and until the moment of breakage it is in the owner's domain at the lower value; and also it depreciates etc. — Tur.
