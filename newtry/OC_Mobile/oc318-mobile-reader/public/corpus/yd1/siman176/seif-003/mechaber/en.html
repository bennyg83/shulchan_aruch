In a place where they are accustomed to rent the ship and take its rent, and if it broke they appraise for him what it diminished and he pays beyond its rent — this is permitted. And likewise, it is permitted to rent a copper pot and similar, and he takes the rent and the value of what diminished from its weight, and all similar. {Rama: And some say: specifically utensils like this, for even what remains diminishes somewhat, and for this he takes rent; therefore, if it broke, they evaluate how much it was worth at the time it broke (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos; and so wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham and Hagahos Ashiri; and so is implied by the language of the Tur).}
