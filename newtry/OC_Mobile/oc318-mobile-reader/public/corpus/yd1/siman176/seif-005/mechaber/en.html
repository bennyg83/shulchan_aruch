If one rents out a field to his fellow for 10 kor per year, and said to him, “Give me 200 zuz, that I will support the field with them, and I will give you 12 kor each year,” this is permitted, because if he supports the field with these dinarim, its rent will be more. And likewise, if he rented him a shop or a ship for 10 dinarim per year, and said to him, “Give me 200 zuz that I will build with them and paint it and plaster it, or that I will repair with them this ship and its implements, and I will add for you 12 dinarim each year,” this is permitted. (Since he spends the money on the body of the ship or the shop) (Tur). But if he said to him, “Give me 200 zuz in order to do business with them in the shop, or to spend on merchandise of the ship, or to hire sailors with them, and I will add for you in the rent,” this is forbidden.
