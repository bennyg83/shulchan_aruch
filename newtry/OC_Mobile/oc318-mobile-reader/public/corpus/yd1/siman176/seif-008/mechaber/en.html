One who hires a laborer in the winter days to work with him in the summer days for a dinar per day, and he advances his wages from now — while his wage is worth a sela per day in the summer days — it is forbidden. But if he said to him, “Do labor with me from today until such-and-such time for a dinar per day,” it is permitted, for since he begins to work with him from now, it does not appear like he is taking wages for his money that he advanced him.
