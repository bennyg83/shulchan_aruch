It is permitted to rent a cow and appraise it in money-value — for example, that he should say, “Behold, your cow is evaluated upon me for 30 dinar; if it dies. And as long as it is with me, I will pay you a sela per month as rent.” (And some say that if it dies they appraise how much it was worth at the time of death) (Ramban and Rabbeinu Yerucham and the Tur).
