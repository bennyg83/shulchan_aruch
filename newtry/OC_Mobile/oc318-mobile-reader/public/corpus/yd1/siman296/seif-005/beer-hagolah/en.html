Menachot 15b.
(°) Kesef Mishneh explains that these are species that endure for three years, as taught in Mishnah 5, chapter 2 of Kilayim, just as vineyard produce is not permitted until three years. The Ran writes that because they grow extensively, create intermingling, and form clusters like vines, they are forbidden as diverse species in a vineyard. This excludes other inedible seeds, but grain, legumes, and vegetables are forbidden by Torah law.
