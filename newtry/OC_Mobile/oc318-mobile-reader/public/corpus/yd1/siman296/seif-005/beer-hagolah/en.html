Menachot 15b. Kesef Mishneh explained there: meaning they do only three years as we taught chapter 2 of Kilayim mishnah 5, like vineyard produce is not permitted until three years; and Ran wrote: because they grow greatly there is mixture and they make clusters like vines — forbidden due to kilay hakerem; and he came to exclude other seeds that are not eaten; but grain, legumes, and vegetables are forbidden from Torah.
