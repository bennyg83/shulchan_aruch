And how much etc. Yerushalmi chapter 4 halachah 1 — R' Zeira, R' Yisa in R' Matna's name: a large vineyard has no tail and a small vineyard has no mechol; a large vineyard has no tail because you do not need it; a small vineyard has no mechol lest he forbid outside more than inside — inside eight, outside twelve; which is a small vineyard — three against three etc. [Tosefta chapter 3: how much its planting — three against three]; and so from mishnah 2 chapter 7 hamarbich three vines etc. per Rosh and Raavad.
And likewise if there were three etc. Ruled like the Sages, but specifically with three — for even if one sees the middle row as if it were not there, two rows remain and it is a vineyard; not so with two rows. Likut: and likewise if there were three etc. — in this case the Sages agree with R' Shimon from what is said in Bava Batra chapter 3 — "planted in a row" as we learned etc.; so Rava ruled there; cannot say the whole thing is like an individual; further they said there from R' Shimon half goes to the Sages etc.; the Sages here hold even less than four; must say they argue on the Sages there, but with three trees the Sages agree that if he takes one only two remain; what is written 37b2 — R' Zeira: tannaim dispute there with many trees; but Rif ruled there like Rava; Rosh wrote Rava holds even per the Sages there is no difficulty for the Sages except regarding kilayim because they are accustomed to plant thus etc.; Rashbam chapter 3 s.v. ro'in wrote it is a stringency for kilayim — his words seem astounding, for we say chapter 3 like tannaim as above; per Rosh it fits that R' Zeira holds thus and they forced all that to say Rava and all amoraim there are all per R' Shimon; for Rambam everything fits — end.
(Supplement) And likewise if it was, etc. For in such a case the Rabbis agree with R' Shimon — from what is written in Bava Batra 83a: 'consecutive — for the mishnah teaches,' etc.; and likewise Rava ruled there; and it is impossible to say that the whole case is like a [case of a] solitary [tree]; and furthermore, he said there: 'from the fact that according to R' Shimon it is half, but according to the Rabbis,' etc. — and the Rabbis here hold [the space is] even less than four [amot]; and it must be said that they disagree with the Rabbis there; except that regarding three trees the Rabbis agree: that if one of them is removed, only two will remain; and what is written there 37b: 'R' Zeira [said]: it is like a dispute between Tannaim,' etc. — there [the discussion is] regarding many trees. But Rif ruled there like Rava, and Rosh wrote there that he holds that Rava said 'even according to the Rabbis' — for the Rabbis only raise a difficulty regarding [the law of] kilayim, where it is common to plant in this way, etc.; and Rashbam 83a in s.v. 'we view,' etc. wrote that [this is] a stringency regarding kilayim; and seemingly his words are perplexing — for we say in chapter 3: 'like a dispute between Tannaim' — as above; but according to the Rosh's words it works out well: that R' Zeira holds this, and they all pressed to say that Rava and all the Amoraim there follow R' Shimon; but for Rambam, everything works out well (end).
