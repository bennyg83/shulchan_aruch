And how much etc. Yerushalmi chapter 4 halachah 1 ; R' Zeira, R' Yisa in R' Matna's name: a large vineyard has no tail and a small vineyard has no mechol; a large vineyard has no tail because you do not need it; a small vineyard has no mechol lest he forbid outside more than inside ; inside eight, outside twelve; which is a small vineyard ; three against three etc. [Tosefta chapter 3: how much its planting ; three against three]; and so from mishnah 2 chapter 7 hamarbich three vines etc. per Rosh and Raavad.
And likewise if there were three etc. Ruled like the Sages, but specifically with three ; for even if one sees the middle row as if it were not there, two rows remain and it is a vineyard; not so with two rows.
(Likkut) Likut: and likewise if there were three etc. ; in this case the Sages agree with R' Shimon from what is said in Bava Batra chapter 3 ; "planted in a row" as we learned etc.; so Rava ruled there; cannot say the whole thing is like an individual; further they said there from R' Shimon half goes to the Sages etc.; the Sages here hold even less than four; must say they argue on the Sages there, but with three trees the Sages agree that if he takes one only two remain; what is written 37b2 ; R' Zeira: tannaim dispute there with many trees; but Rif ruled there like Rava; Rosh wrote Rava holds even per the Sages there is no difficulty for the Sages except regarding kilayim because they are accustomed to plant thus etc.; Rashbam chapter 3 s.v. ro'in wrote it is a stringency for kilayim ; his words seem astounding, for we say chapter 3 like tannaim as above; per Rosh it fits that R' Zeira holds thus and they forced all that to say Rava and all amoraim there are all per R' Shimon; for Rambam everything fits ; end.
