If there was one row of vines one beside the other, even if there are a hundred, this is not a vineyard but like a single vine, and he distances six handbreadths from the row and sows. If there were two rows, behold this is a vineyard and he must distance four amot on every side and afterward sow. And how many must be in each row? Three vines or more. In what case is this said? When between each vine and vine there were from four amot up to eight; but if between the two rows there were eight amot apart from the place of the vines, behold these are like separated one from the other and are not one vineyard, and he distances only six handbreadths from each row. And likewise if between them there were less than four, behold these are like one vine and he distances six handbreadths in every direction.
