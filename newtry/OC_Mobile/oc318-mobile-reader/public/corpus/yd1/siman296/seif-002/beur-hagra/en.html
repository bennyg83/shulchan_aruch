There is none etc. — meaning: seeds not scattered by hand are permitted for four amot; even for R' Yonatan who argues on R' Yoshiah; what is written in seif 1 and other seeds forbidden rabbinically — that is in mafuleit yad as written; here it is permitted to sow them in a vineyard; "but other" as in mishnah chapter 5 ha-arus etc. and Tosefta chapter 3 [correct text in Tosefta as above; Tosefta teaches: in all of them they are kilayim in a vineyard except a tree — certainly scribal error; so Raavad chapter 5, but his view is not all seeds and grasses, only those from which nothing is sustained; Rambam reads grasses too as kilayim, and so is the halachah] — leshishut, levitzin, barbuvayar, bulfsin, saffron, kinara, chamita, teloit, siah, ezov, kodnit, cucumbers, gourds, avtin, lelafponot, Egyptian beans are seed types and not kilayim in a vineyard; iris, kisum, royal rose are seed types and not kilayim; chashifa, itan, reeds and all that grow from dust are grass types and not kilayim; R' Dostai b. Yehuda says grass types are likewise kilayim in a vineyard; reeds, hawthorn, rose, hawthorns are tree types and not kilayim; this is the rule: whatever produces leaves from its root is a vegetable, and whatever does not is a tree; leek — some say kilayim, Sages say not; mustard R' Tarfon etc.; caper Beit Shammai etc.; so Yerushalmi chapter 5 — "and all the more" etc.; so Gemara Berakhot 36a and Eruvin 34b; but grain and vegetables are explicit in several mishnayot — "one who plants a vegetable" etc., "grain that leans" etc.
