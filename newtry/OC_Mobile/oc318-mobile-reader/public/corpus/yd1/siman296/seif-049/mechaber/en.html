A small courtyard breached in its entirety into a large one, and the vines were in the large one—it is forbidden to sow in the small one; and if he sowed, the seeds are forbidden and the vines permitted. If the vines were in the small one, it is permitted to sow in the large one, since in the large one there are partitions on this side and that side—behold it is like separated from the small one, and the small one is not separated from the large one.
