Vines planted on a high step and its aris emerges and provides covering over the field—if he stands on the ground and harvests all of it, we view the whole place under the aris as though it were the place of the roots of the vines and forbid four amot in the field in every direction from the edge of the aris. And if he cannot harvest until he ascends the step or a ladder, it is forbidden to sow only under the aris alone.
