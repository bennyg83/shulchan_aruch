(Likut) Trellis, etc. — requires study why it should be less than other single trellises; and Rosh strained himself — I saw like Rivavan or, etc., and how strained his words; but it seems Rosh's explanation: such as he planted eleven vines by the fence on one side and the middle vine was destroyed to sow there — not per Rivavan's reason, etc.; rather prohibition of trellis is only opposite the width of the vines one sees all the vines; but in such a case need not distance — only sixteen like a single vine; and here in trellis rulings, etc.; and he said there in mishnah 7: trellis that protrudes, etc. — meaning like this form two, three... one planted partly on the wall at a corner to sow on the other end of the wall such as at end of two; and R' Yosi said if there is not free space four cubits such as a piece of two by three — it shall not come, etc. — not like Rambam's explanation in seif 63 (end).
