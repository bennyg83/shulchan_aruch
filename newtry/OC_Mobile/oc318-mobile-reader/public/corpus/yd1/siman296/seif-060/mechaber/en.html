An aris ruined in its middle and five vines remained on the side of the fence on this side and five vines on the side of the other fence opposite them—this is called broken sections of an aris: if between them there are eight amot, and one of sixty per amah, behold he distances from each row six handbreadths and sows, provided he does not sow under the trellis. If between them there were eight amot precisely, behold he does not sow there. And if he sowed, since he distanced from each row six handbreadths, behold he did not cause hikdish. And if there is no fence there, he distances from each row sixteen handbreadths and sows, for there is neither aris nor broken sections of aris. If he returned and built the fence, the aris returned to its place and the broken sections of aris returned to their place.
