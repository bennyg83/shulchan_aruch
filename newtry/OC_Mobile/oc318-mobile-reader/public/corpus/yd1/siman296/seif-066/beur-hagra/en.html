Vegetable seed etc. Yerushalmi chapter 5 ; R' Yaakov bar Iri in R' Levi's name: sometimes the straw is permitted and the grain forbidden; sometimes the straw forbidden and the grain permitted ; how is it done? Seed in permission and roofing over them ; straw permitted, grain forbidden; seed in prohibition and he removed the roofing ; straw forbidden, grain permitted. R' Zeira asked: it is fine if straw permitted and grain forbidden; straw forbidden and grain permitted ; it grew from prohibition and you say grain is permitted? R' Zeira as his view ; R' Zeira in R' Yonatan's name: a cabbage of kilay hakerem that he uprooted and replanted ; even if it adds much it is forbidden, for growths of prohibition do not rise; R' Levi ; growth of prohibition rises. Ruled like R' Zeira ; therefore he omitted it, for so we hold in chapter 7 of Nedarim and see seif 382.
