Vineyard, etc. — Tosefta end chapter 4 of Tevuah: grain that took root — this is forbidden and sanctifies and becomes sanctified; vines that took root — behold these are forbidden and sanctify but do not become sanctified until they make white double; some made white double and some did not — those that made white double are forbidden, those that did not are permitted. And meaning: forbidden to eat, sanctifies the grain for benefit, and itself becomes sanctified for benefit. Tosefta: this did not sanctify; and nevertheless, etc. — permitted; and if, etc. — one matter — meaning how permitted if he uprooted seed before, etc.; and what is written "unripe grape" 34 — behold unripe grape and white bean are one measure as Berachot 36b; rather meaning still small and did not reach a perutah; and he said in Menachot 15: incident, etc.; and the reason: because it is a samadar and had not yet reached a perutah, as Berachot there: unripe grape is not samadar, etc.; only the Gemara challenged there by a fortiori and similarly challenged this tosefta — only the Gemara challenged from incident and answered "now," etc.; but it is a rejection that we do not say a fortiori such — not like Tosafot there; and R' Yosi and R' Elazar dispute in the aforementioned Yerushalmi as I wrote briefly; and we rule like R' Yochanan as written — and not like Tosafot there s.v. "and they permitted" who wrote it follows R' Yosi and R' Shimon.
