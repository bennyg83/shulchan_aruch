A vineyard whose grapes have not reached forming the white double leaf but are still unripe (boser), and seed was sown within vegetable or grain and took root—this has not caused hikdish; nevertheless we penalize him and forbid the seed, but the unripe grapes are permitted. And if he uprooted the seed before the grapes formed the white double leaf, behold it is permitted in benefit. If part formed the white double leaf and part did not—those that formed were hallowed, and those that did not are permitted.
