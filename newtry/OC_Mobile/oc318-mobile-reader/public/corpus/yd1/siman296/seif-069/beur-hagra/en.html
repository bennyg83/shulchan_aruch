And if he said, etc. — not specifically that even initially permitted, as written; but he should not say, etc.; and as written in the Gemara; and so Taz [but requires study, for Gemara explains there in all kilay zera'im outside the land; and our sugya implies specifically in linen; and so Rashi there s.v. "he gave," etc.; and Tosafot s.v. "Rav," etc.; and it seems in others no distinction between land of Israel and outside the land].
And even though, etc. — R' Yoshiah's explanation: forbidden and sanctifies; so all mishnayot and braitot taught plainly they sanctify; and mishnah vineyard planted with vegetables, etc. — even outside the land; but Tosafot wrote all mishnayot and braitot are not like R' Yoshiah; but per R' Yoshiah permitted even to eat them; and so Rosh in Hilchot Kiddushin; and he said in Kiddushin 39a: you were not careful, etc.; and he did not tell him forbidden for benefit, for ostensibly he sowed to benefit from it; and so Rav Mesharshiya in Shabbat there; and Tosafot in Kiddushin there s.v. "no" and Menachot 15b s.v. "and they forbade," etc.; and he said in gloss "and some say," etc. — and these are Rosh's words in Hilchot Kiddushin; and Raavad's view: he does not explain R' Yoshiah except for lashes, but there is prohibition; and Rosh explains rabbinic prohibition; and must read that Kiddushin "outside permitted" — for we excommunicate for prohibition even rabbinic, as Semag wrote and Magen Avraham in siman 334; and see siman 296.
