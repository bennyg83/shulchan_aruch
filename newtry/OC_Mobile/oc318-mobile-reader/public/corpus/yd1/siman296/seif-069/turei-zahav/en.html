If he told a minor, etc. It is permitted even initially to instruct a minor non-Jew to do so, as Rav Mesharshiya did regarding vineyard mixtures. Outside Eretz Yisrael even sowing wheat, barley, and a grape seed together is rabbinic. This differs from castration through a non-Jew, which is prohibited because the underlying prohibition is from the Torah. See siman 297, seif 2.
