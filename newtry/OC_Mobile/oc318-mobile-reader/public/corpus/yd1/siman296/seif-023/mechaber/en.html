Two gardens one above the other—the lower is made a vineyard: he sows the upper until it reaches the airspace of ten handbreadths near the vineyard, for an airspace of ten handbreadths is adjacent to a vineyard or a single vine, and it is forbidden to sow seeds in it. And if the upper was made a vineyard, behold he sows the lower until it reaches below three handbreadths from the root of the vines.
