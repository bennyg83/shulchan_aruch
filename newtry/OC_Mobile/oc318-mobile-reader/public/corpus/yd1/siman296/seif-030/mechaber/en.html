A branch emerging from the trellis or from the single vine trained on stakes—we view it as though a weight were hanging from it to the ground, and it is forbidden to sow beneath it. And likewise one who stretches a vine branch from tree to tree—it is forbidden to sow beneath it. If he tied a rope or reed to the vine branch and tied the other end to a tree, behold it is permitted to sow beneath the rope; and if he stretched this rope so that tendrils and leaves would walk upon it, behold it is like a trellis and forbidden to sow beneath it.
