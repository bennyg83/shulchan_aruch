Mishnah 6, chapter 5. Rambam writes, based upon the Yerushalmi, that we estimate whether it would dry out in one hundred hours until no moisture remains. If it remained in the ground for half an hour after reaching this stage, it added one two-hundredth and prohibits. Raavad writes that one may determine this by uprooting stalks, weighing them immediately, and weighing them again after half an hour; if two ounces became one, the plants added one ounce during that half hour.
