Whether one plants or one maintains—once he saw kilayim grew in his vineyard and left them, behold he has caused hikdish. And a person does not cause hikdish something that is not his. Therefore one who roofs his vine over his friend's grain has caused hikdish to his vine and the grain was not hallowed; one who roofs his friend's vine over his grain has caused hikdish to his grain and did not cause hikdish to his friend's vine; one who roofs his friend's vine over his friend's grain—neither caused hikdish. And for this reason one who sows his vineyard in the shemittah year did not cause hikdish.
