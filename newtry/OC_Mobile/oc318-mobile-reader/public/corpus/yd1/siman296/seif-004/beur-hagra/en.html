One who roofs his fellow's vine over his fellow's grain ; we learn it from this: R' Yosi ; incident of one who sowed his vineyard in shemitah and the matter came before R' Akiva and he said: a person does not forbid what is not his ; behold neither the vine nor the grain is his ; and he was refuted; and Rosh brought there and explained like R' Yochanan.
