And even the straw etc. Baer Hagolah and Tosafot Pesachim 25a s.v. ikran etc. Likut: and even straw etc. — so Tosafot Pesachim 25a; but to me it appears clear that wood is permitted and so the straw; Pesachim says "this teaches" etc. — specifically ikran; if so, ikran is not worse than straw and wood; Ketubot chapter 1 and Bava Batra 36a — "ate orlah" etc., unlike Tosafot's forced reading; proof from Terumot chapter 10 and Orlah chapter 3 — bundles of flax in kilay hakerem must be burned; if so, why mention flax bundles specifically — only flax whose wood and fruit taste the same; Pesachim 26b bundles of kilay hakerem — also flax, similar to orlah peels only on walnut and pomegranate shells; Yerushalmi chapter 5 and Rosh there — mishnah 7, R' Zeira in R' Elazar's name: if grasses etc., R' Yochanan etc.; R' Yaakov bar Idi in R' Levi's name: sometimes straw permitted and grain forbidden, sometimes straw forbidden and grain permitted etc., as below seif 66 — must say this too is in flax, forced, requires study; but wood is certainly permitted — this resolves the bundles of vines case — end.
(Supplement) And even the straw, etc., and the wood, etc. And so Tosafot wrote in Pesachim 25a s.v. "their essence," etc.; but it appears to me clearly that the wood is permitted and likewise the straw; and this is what is written in Pesachim there: "this says that," etc. — "its essence," etc. — specifically its essence; and if so, the essence is no worse than the straw and the wood; and likewise in Ketubot 80a and in Bava Batra 36a: "she ate orlah," etc. — and unlike what Tosafot strained to explain there and there; and proof from what is written in chapter 10 of Terumot and chapter 3 of Orlah: "one who had bundles of fenugreek from kilayim in the vineyard — let them be burned"; and if so, why did it specify "bundles of fenugreek"? — rather, specifically fenugreek where the taste of its wood and fruit are the same; and what is written in Pesachim 26b "the straw of kilayim in the vineyard" — that too refers to fenugreek, similar to "shells of orlah" which applies only to the shells of nuts and pomegranates; but in the Yerushalmi end of chapter 5 of Kil'ayim, which R' Shimshon cited there in mishnah 7: R' Zeira in the name of R' Lazar: "if weeds," etc.; R' Yochanan, etc.; and furthermore there: R' Yaakov bar Idi in the name of R' Lazar: "sometimes the straw is permitted and the grain forbidden; sometimes the straw is forbidden and the grain permitted," etc.; and as I will write below in seif 66; and one must say this too refers to fenugreek — and it is forced; and it requires study; but the wood is certainly permitted, and with this the case of bundles of vine shoots aforementioned is resolved (end).
