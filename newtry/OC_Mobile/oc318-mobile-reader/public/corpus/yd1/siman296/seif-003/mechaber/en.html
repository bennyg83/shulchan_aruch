It is forbidden to sow vegetables or grain beside the vines, or to plant a vine beside the vegetable or the grain; and if he did so, even though he does not receive lashes, behold he has caused hikdish and both are forbidden in benefit—the vegetable or grain and the vines—and one burns both of them, as it says, "lest the fullness of the seed be hallowed." And even the straw of the grain and the wood of these vines are forbidden in benefit and one burns them, and one does not kindle an oven or stove with them nor cook with them at the time of their burning.
