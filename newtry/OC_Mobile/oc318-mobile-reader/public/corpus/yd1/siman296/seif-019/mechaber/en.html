One who sows grain or vegetable in a vineyard, or maintains it, until it increased by one in two hundred—behold he causes hikdish from the vines around it sixteen amot in every direction, circular—not square—and we view the whole circle whose width is thirty-two amot as though it were entirely full of vegetable, and every vine that is within this circle is hallowed with the vegetable; and whatever is outside the circle is not hallowed.
