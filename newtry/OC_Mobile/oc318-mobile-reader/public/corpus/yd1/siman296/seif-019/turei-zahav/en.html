And not square. If the plantings were square, vines would stand at the corners beyond the circle and extend more than sixteen amos from the vegetables sown in the center. We rule that the prohibition extends only sixteen amos from the place where the vegetables were planted. The Rambam and Rabbeinu Shimshon discuss this Mishnah at length. See the Kesef Mishneh, who cites R' Yisrael's responsum to the Rosh.
