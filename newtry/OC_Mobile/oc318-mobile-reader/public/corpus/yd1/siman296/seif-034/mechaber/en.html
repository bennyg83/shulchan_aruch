If there were three rows and between each row and row eight amot or more, behold he sows between the rows; therefore one who plants his vineyard at the outset and distances between each row and row eight amot may bring seed into it and distances from each row and row only six handbreadths. But if he sowed outside it, he must distance from the outer row four amot like other vineyards, and the law between the rows of this vineyard is not like a vineyard ruined in its middle, for from the outset they were planted distant.
