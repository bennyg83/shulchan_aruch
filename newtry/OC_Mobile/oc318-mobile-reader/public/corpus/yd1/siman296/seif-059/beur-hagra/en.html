One, etc. The Yerushalmi there, halachah 5, states that there is no distinction whether he built a fence and afterward planted eleven vines, or planted eleven vines and afterward built a fence. If the middle vine was then severed, there is both an aris and separated sections of an aris. If the fence was destroyed, there is neither an aris nor separated sections of an aris. Rabbi Avdimi, brother of Rabbi Yose, asked: if he rebuilt it, what is the law? This indicates that the status depends upon the existing fence.
