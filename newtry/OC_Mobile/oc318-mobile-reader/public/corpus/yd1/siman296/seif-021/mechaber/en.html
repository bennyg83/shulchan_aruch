In what case is this said? When one sows or maintains inside the vineyard; but when one sows outside the vineyard and adjoins it, behold he causes hikdish to two rows of vines adjacent to the seed—meaning, along the entire length of the seed—and another four amot outside the seed; and it is hallowed from the seed a width of four amot along the entire row of the vineyard. And if he sowed beside a single vine, he does not cause hikdish from the seed except six handbreadths in every direction.
