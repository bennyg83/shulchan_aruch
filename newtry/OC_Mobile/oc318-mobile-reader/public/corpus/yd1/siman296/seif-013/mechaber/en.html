If he was working in a vineyard and seeds fell from him, or came out with manure or with water, or he was sowing or winnowing in a white field and the wind blew them behind him and the seeds fell in the vineyard and grew—behold this was not hallowed, as it says, "that you sow"—and this he did not sow. And he is liable to uproot when he sees; and if he maintained them, behold he has caused hikdish. And if the wind blew them before him and he saw the seeds that fell to the vineyard, behold this is like one who sows. And how should he act? If grasses grew, he turns them with a plowshare and that suffices; and if he found them become ears, he beats that ear in order to ruin it, for everything is forbidden in benefit; and if he found them become grain, behold he burns it. And if he saw them and maintained them, behold these are burned with the vines adjacent to them.
