In the middle of the clearing. Four amos on each side belong to the vineyard's cultivation area, allowing oxen to plow and tend the vines. Those four amos are therefore treated as part of the vineyard. Another four amos of significant space are then required for sowing. Consequently, one may sow only in the exact center of a sixteen-amah clearing. If the clearing is smaller, after assigning four amos to each side there is insufficient significant space remaining for seed. The earlier rule prohibiting up to sixteen amos concerns a vineyard fully planted with vines.
