A vineyard ruined in its middle and intact on all its sides around—if in the bald patch in the middle there are sixteen amot, behold he distances four amot in every direction from the roots of the vines and sows in the middle of the bald patch; and if there are not sixteen amot in it, behold he does not bring seed there. And if he brought, since he distanced four amot in every direction from the vines of the vineyard, behold he did not cause hikdish.
