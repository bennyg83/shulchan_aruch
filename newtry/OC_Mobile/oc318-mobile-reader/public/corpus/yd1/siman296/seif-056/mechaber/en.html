Vines that grew in their natural state and behold their tendrils and clusters are cast on the ground—these are called a vineyard. But one who makes like a bed or like a high bench (meaning: raised) above the ground so that clusters and tendrils spread upon it, and he raised the vine's foliage above the ground upon that bed and suspended them—behold this is called an aris (trellis). And those reeds and the like from which he made a bed or bench and suspended upon them the vine's foliage—these are called a trellis (afifirah), and there are other laws for an aris.
