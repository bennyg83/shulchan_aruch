The Kesef Mishneh explains that these species remain productive for three years, like vineyard produce whose fruit is not permitted until three years. The Ran explains that hemp and luf are prohibited because they grow profusely, create intermingling, and form clusters like vines. The teaching does not exclude wheat and barley, for all five grain species are certainly forbidden by Torah law. It excludes only other garden seeds that are not ordinarily eaten and do not resemble hemp and luf.
