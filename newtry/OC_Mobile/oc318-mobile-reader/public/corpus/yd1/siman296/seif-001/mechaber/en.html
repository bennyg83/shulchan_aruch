The laws of kilay hakerem and kilay zera'im. In it are 69 seifim.
One who sows two species of grain or two species of vegetables together with vineyard seed—behold he receives lashes twice: once for "your field you shall not sow kilayim," and once for "you shall not sow your vineyard kilayim." And he does not receive lashes for "you shall not sow your vineyard kilayim" until he sows in Eretz Yisrael wheat, barley, and vine seed casually (mefolut yad). And likewise if he covered them with earth he receives lashes. And likewise if he sowed two species of vegetables and vine seed, or one species of vegetable and one species of grain and vine seed casually—behold he receives lashes. And he is liable d'oraisa only for hemp, luf, and similar seeds that complete their growth together with vineyard grain; but other seeds are forbidden by rabbinic decree. And likewise it is forbidden by rabbinic decree to sow kilay hakerem in the Diaspora. And one does not plow with a non-Jew in kilayim, but one uproots with him in order to lessen the loss.
