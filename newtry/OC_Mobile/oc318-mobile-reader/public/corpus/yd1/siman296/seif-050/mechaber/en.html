A ditch passing through a vineyard, ten deep and four wide—if it was open from the head of the vineyard to its end, behold it is viewed as between two vineyards and permitted to sow within it, provided the vines do not provide covering over it. And if it was not open, behold it is like a winepress in the middle of the vineyard—for even though it is ten deep and four wide or more, it is forbidden to sow within it unless in the bald patch there were sixteen amot.
