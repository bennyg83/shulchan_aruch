And if he brought a qua. The succinct:
In four cubits, etc. — sixteen and requires study per his view who ruled like R' Meir and R' Shimon that even in a large vineyard need only sixteen — if so no distinction and the law of vacant vineyard is void.
And likewise a large vineyard etc. Yerushalmi chapter 7 — destruction of the vineyard permitted; R' Eliezer — mechol of vineyard four amot permitted; vines four amot permitted; stakes four amot; apipiruta four amot; between them — what is the law? R' Yosi — forbidden and consecrates; Rashbal — forbidden and does not consecrate; in any case it is a large vineyard — why need its destruction? Even its planting — a small vineyard has no mechol except as they stand; a large vineyard planted on the planting of a small vineyard — R' Zeira: this teaches a small vineyard eight by eight has no mechol etc.
