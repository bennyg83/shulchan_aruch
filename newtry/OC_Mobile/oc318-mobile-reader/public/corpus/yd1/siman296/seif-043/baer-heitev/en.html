Vacant. (Meaning: the owner's way is to leave it vacant without vines.) Taz wrote: the reason is we need twelve cubits — for four cubits belong to vineyard work, and four cubits by the vineyard belong to the wall where they walk to support the wall; therefore four cubits must remain important space for seed. That we do not require eight cubits in the middle as above — because here there are vines only on one side — end quote.
