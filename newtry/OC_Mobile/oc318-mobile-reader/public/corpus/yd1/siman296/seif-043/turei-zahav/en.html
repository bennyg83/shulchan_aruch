The vineyard's surrounding open area. Twelve amos are required: four for working the vineyard, four beside the wall so people can walk there and maintain it, and four additional amos as a significant sowing area. Unlike a clearing between two banks of vines, eight central amos are unnecessary because vines stand on only one side.
Likewise, a large vineyard, etc. People do not ordinarily leave such an empty area there, since a large vineyard already contains ample open space elsewhere.
