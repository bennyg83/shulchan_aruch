And likewise a place left empty without vines between the end of the vineyard and its fence—and this is called the vineyard's bald area—if in it there are twelve amot, he distances four amot from the vines and sows the remainder. If in it there were less than twelve amot, he does not bring seed there. And if he brought, since he distanced four amot, behold he did not cause hikdish. In what case is this said? In a large vineyard; but in a small one it has no bald area except that he distances four amot from the end of the vines and sows until the fence. And likewise a large vineyard that had between each row and row eight amot or more has no bald area.
