A watchman's hut (meaning: a dwelling house made for the field watchman) in a vineyard—if it is ten high and four wide, it is permitted to sow vegetable on its roof, provided the tendrils do not touch it so that vegetable does not appear inside the vineyard from above. In what case is this said? When it is square; but if the watchman's hut was round, it needs a hollow of four handbreadths within so that it is prominent from the ground, and it needs earth on its roof from above three handbreadths.
