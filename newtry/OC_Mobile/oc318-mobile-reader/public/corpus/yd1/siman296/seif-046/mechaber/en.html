A fence separating between vineyard and vegetable that was breached up to ten amot—behold it is like an opening and permitted. If it was breached more than ten, opposite the breach it is forbidden until he distances from the vines according to the measure of the breach. If many breaches were made in it—if the standing portion is like the breached, behold it is permitted as though there is no breach there. If the breach exceeded the standing, he does not sow opposite all the breached places until he distances according to the measure.
