Likewise, all kinds of, etc. He wrote this according to the reading of the Tosefta there, that they constitute kilayim in a vineyard. That text, however, contains a scribal error, and I already recorded the correct reading of the Tosefta in seif 2. The same appears in the Yerushalmi at the end of the fifth chapter: the chasifah, the itan, reeds, and all other plants that grow in ashes are species of grasses and do not constitute kilayim in a vineyard. It is possible that he had a different reading of the Yerushalmi.
