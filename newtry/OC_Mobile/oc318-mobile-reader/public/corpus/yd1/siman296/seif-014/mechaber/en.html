One who sees in a vineyard a grass that people do not customarily sow, even though he wants to maintain it for an animal or for medicine—behold he has not caused hikdish until he maintains something that the majority of people in that place maintain similarly. How so? One who maintains thorns in a vineyard in the evening when they want thorns for their camels—behold he has caused hikdish. Iris, cassia, and royal rose—and other seed species are not kilayim in a vineyard. Kundos and cotton vine—behold they are like other vegetable species and cause hikdish in a vineyard. And likewise all types of grasses that grow on their own in a field—behold they cause hikdish in a vineyard. And Egyptian beans are a seed species and do not cause hikdish.
