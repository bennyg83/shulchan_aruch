A coerced person who sowed the vineyard—when the coercion ends he must harvest the seed immediately, even on the weekday of a festival. And if he did not find workers he adds up to a third to their wages; if they asked him for more or he did not find workers, behold he asks calmly and harvests. And if the seed was delayed until the season when it causes hikdish, behold he has caused hikdish and both are forbidden.
