How so? If he distanced the row of vines from the wall one amah and the aris from the vines is found, and to the wall—he measures from the wall four amot and sows, and between the seed and the roots of the vines five amot are found. And if he comes to sow from the side of the vines, he distances four amot from the roots of the vines and finds five amot distant from the wall. And on this manner we judge every aris.
