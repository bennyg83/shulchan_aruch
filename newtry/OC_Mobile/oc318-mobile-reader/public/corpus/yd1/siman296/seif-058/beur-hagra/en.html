And if he comes, etc. ; since Yerushalmi does not derive the opposite conclusion ; learn that here Beit Hillel agrees. But Raavad's version in Yerushalmi is reversed: one who says from the root of the fence they do not extend ; forbidden except sixteen; and one who says from the root of the vines ; forbidden another sixteen; and he wrote: but behind the fence one sows immediately since the fence separates ; and this version is correct; therefore not taught in Eduyot; and so it seems in Yerushalmi there we say between whether from root of vines they extend or from root of fence they extend ; what between them? R' Yonah answered: interpret that the vine roots were partly within four cubits and partly outside four cubits; one who says from root of vines they extend ; well, meaning need four cubits from root of vines; one who says from root of fence they extend ; where the rope ends forbidden and the rest permitted ; meaning permitted to sow by the vines; and "four" that need to distance sixteen and afterward said there were two cubits, etc. ; as above.
(Likut) How, etc. ; see what is written: Rosh and Raavad's explanation is correct that from the side of the fence need not distance; and further proof from what is written chapter 2 of Eduyot; and below seif 61 small garden, etc., if there is, etc. (end).
