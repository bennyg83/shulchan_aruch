And he shall spread — in last chapter of Eruvin we say where spreading a garment is impossible, one turns the writing, for if not turned there is greater disgrace of holy writing; and Bach wrote from here one learns a person should not go out and leave a book open without a cloth on it — not specifically sefer Torah but likewise other books; Shach wrote: and known to masters of truth there is an angel called Shad who stands and silences one who leaves a book open and goes out, causing him to forget his learning — end.
