And he shall spread a garment over it. In the last chapter of Eruvin (98a) we say that where it is not possible to spread a garment, he should turn the writing face-down, for if he does not turn it over, there is greater disgrace to the sacred writings. My father-in-law z"l wrote: from here one can learn that a person should not go outside and leave a sefer open unless it has a covering over it — and not specifically a Torah scroll, but the same applies to other sefarim. Thus far his words.
