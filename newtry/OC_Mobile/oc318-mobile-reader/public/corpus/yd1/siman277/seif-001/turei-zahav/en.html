And he should spread a cloth over it. At the end of Eruvin, 98a, the Gemara says that when it is impossible to spread a cloth, one should turn the writing face down, because otherwise the sacred writing is subjected to greater disgrace. My father-in-law writes that from here one may learn that a person should not leave a room while a sefer remains open if there is no cloth covering it. This applies not only to a Sefer Torah but to other sacred books as well.
