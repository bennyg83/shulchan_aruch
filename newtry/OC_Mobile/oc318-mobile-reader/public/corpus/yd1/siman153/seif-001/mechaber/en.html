A law of yichud of a Jew and a Jewess with a non-Jew; it contains 4 se’ifim.
One may not place an animal in the inns of non-Jews, and one may not hand over an animal to their shepherd, because they are suspected of bestiality. But in places where they are not suspected of it — and on the contrary, they strike and punish for it — it is permitted. (And one may not hand over to them a child to teach him a book or to teach him a trade, for they draw him toward heresy) (Tur).
