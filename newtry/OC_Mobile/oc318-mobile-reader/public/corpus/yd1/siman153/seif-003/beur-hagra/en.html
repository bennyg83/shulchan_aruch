In a place, etc. Pirkei D'Rabbi Eliezer, chapter 29: "Why did he circumcise them? For the sake of purity, because anyone who eats with a non-Jew," etc.; "and anyone who touches him is like one who touches a corpse, and anyone who bathes with him is like one who bathes with a metzora." Mordechai in Avodah Zarah 44b writes that when the Gemara states that Rabban Gamliel bathed, etc., the case was where the non-Jew entered first. Darkei Moshe writes that the reason people are not careful about this nowadays is that they wear trousers, as Agudah writes in chapter 4 of Pesachim, and as stated in Even HaEzer, siman 23, seif 6, in the Rama's gloss. But that case is not comparable. The later authorities have already written that Pirkei D'Rabbi Eliezer gives the reason as purity. It also appears that there the case is of bathing in the same bath. Therefore the incident involving Rabban Gamliel is likewise not difficult.
