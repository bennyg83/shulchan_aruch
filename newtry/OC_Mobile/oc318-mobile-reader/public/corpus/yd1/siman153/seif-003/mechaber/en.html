If a non-Jew with a sword at his belt came upon him on the road, he should place him at his right. (If the non-Jew has a stick in his hand, he should place him at his left) (Tur). If they were ascending or descending, a Jew should not be below and the non-Jew above (nevertheless, he should place him somewhat to his right) (Ran, perek Ein Ma’amidin). And he should never bow before him. If he asked him, “Where are you going?” and he needs to go 1 parsah, he should say, “I am going 2 parsah.” {Rama: In a place where the practice is to go to the bathhouse without trousers, it is forbidden to go to a bathhouse in which non-Jews bathe naked; but if a Jew is already in the bathhouse and non-Jews came, he does not need to leave (Mordechai, perek 25).}
