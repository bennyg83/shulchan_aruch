Already. The Taz disagrees with this gloss and rules that even if the Jew preceded the non-Jew, it is still prohibited, since the reason is on account of improper thoughts — what difference does it make whether he preceded [the non-Jew] or not. But the Siftei Kohen wrote that in our times everything is permitted even if the non-Jew preceded [the Jew], except as a matter of piety; and one who conducts himself in holiness and purity should distance himself from this — but not for ordinary people.
