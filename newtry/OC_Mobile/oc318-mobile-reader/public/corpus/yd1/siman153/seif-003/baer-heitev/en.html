Taz disputes this gloss and rules that even if the Jew preceded the gentile on the road, it is nevertheless forbidden, because the reason is concern for improper thoughts, and in that respect it makes no difference who went first. Shach, however, writes that nowadays all of this is permitted even if the gentile went first. Only as a matter of pious conduct, one who conducts himself with special holiness and purity should distance himself from this, but ordinary people need not. See there.
