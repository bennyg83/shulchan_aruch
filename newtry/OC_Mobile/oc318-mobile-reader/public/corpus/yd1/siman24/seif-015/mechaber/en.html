Ikkur — how so? Such as the trachea or esophagus was uprooted from the jaw and from the flesh and one of them slipped out or both of them before the shechitah was completed; but if he slaughtered one in a bird or the majority of it and afterward the second slipped out — his shechitah is kosher; if one of them slipped out and afterward he slaughtered the second — his shechitah is invalid. {Rama: And we practice to declare treifah any ikkur, whether in the first minority, the last minority, in the trachea, or in the esophagus (according to Mahariu) and specifically after it was slaughtered; but while alive it is kosher, only shechitah does not permit it, and there is a practical difference regarding its milk or its eggs that they are kosher (Beit Yosef from Rashba's responsum).}
