And we practice to declare treifah. See Shach, s.k. 23: we practice to declare treifah any ikkur, even any amount, etc., since a disqualification occurred in shechitah we practice to declare everything treifah from concern lest the esophagus was perforated — end; and see responsum of Givat Shaul, siman 28, who wrote: accordingly, where he grasped the trachea in his hand and knows certainly he did not touch the esophagus — his shechitah is kosher; but Shach's words are wondrous — see there — he ruled even if he grasped the trachea it is invalid; see Peri Megadim.
And there is a practical difference regarding its milk. See Atzei Erez, s.k. 10; and see responsum of Chasam Sofer, part 2, siman 29, what he wrote on this.
