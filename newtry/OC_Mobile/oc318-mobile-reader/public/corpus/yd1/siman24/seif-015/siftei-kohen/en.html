[And we practice to declare treifah any ikkur, etc. Explanation: from what appears the Mechaber holds that even ikkur at the time of shechitah is not invalid except when uprooted before completing the majority of its shechitah — in this we practice to declare treifah any ikkur, even any amount (meaning by force of custom, but by law it is kosher, as Ran and poskim, and so appears in Tur — not like Bach, who wrote Tur deals when it slipped before shechitah — see there and understand) whether he slaughtered the first minority and it was uprooted even any amount at the time of shechitah, or in the last minority, trachea, or esophagus — since a disqualification occurred in shechitah, we practice to declare treifah everything from concern lest the esophagus was perforated, as above siman 23 and siman 34 regarding crop severance, and in several places; but if it slipped before shechitah, we establish it according to law (so in shechitot of R' Tzvi and other latter shechitot, and so Bach) — for the Mechaber, only when all were uprooted from the flesh in the jaw, or the majority and the minority remained dangling, is it ikkur; and what Ba'er Heitev wrote "and specifically, etc." refers where even by law the animal is forbidden — nevertheless its milk is kosher; and it appears from Ba'er Heitev that even if the majority dangled while alive, its milk or eggs are kosher — so Rashba in Tur HaDeshen and Beit Yosef brings it; however, although so appears from Tur and Ramban whom Ran brings, chapter 1 of Chullin — nevertheless Tosafot, chapter Eizehu Mekoman (daf 44), s.v. vehai ika ikkur, hold that if the majority dangled it is actual treifah even while alive; and so appears Rif and Rambam, and Rashi daf 43a, as in siman 29, s.k. 9; and so Ra'ah in Bedek HaBayit daf 23; and so is proven in the sugya daf 42b regarding what they challenged there — see there.]
[Only shechitah does not permit it. Because due to ikkur the simanim sway and it is impossible to slaughter them properly — Ran in the name of Ramban.]
