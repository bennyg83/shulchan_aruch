When a person slaughters a bird and holds the simanim with his two fingers, he must hold them well — for if he does not hold them properly, sometimes they slip here and there and he cannot slaughter them through holacha and will come to darsah. {Rama: And likewise he should not rest his finger on the knife but hold it in the handle so he does not come to darsah (his own view from slaughterers' practice and Maharshal, chapter HaShochet, siman 12):} And even if he did not do darsah but only something of the esophagus — invalid. {Rama: And the custom is to declare treifah any darsah, whether in the first minority, the last minority, in the trachea, or in the esophagus (shechitot of Mahariu).}
