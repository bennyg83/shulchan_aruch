And one should not deviate. See Ba'er Heitev; and see responsum of Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 55: he practiced if found on the ground to validate in great loss; and likewise if the ring was cut in the middle and hangs on the trachea, or rests or hangs in the middle on the knife or ground — do not be stringent; and he further wrote he received from an expert slaughterer: if you wish to avoid this doubt of a crop ring common in geese, slaughter pen-slanted obliquely — then tested and proven no ring will be found in their slaughter — end.
