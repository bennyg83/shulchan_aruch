[And there is one who invalidates in darsah. But in hagramah he validates, for since hagramah is not the place of shechitah, it is as if he drew with his hand or foot after shechitah — Tosafot, Ra'osh, and other poskim.]
[Majority of the cavity of the trachea. But in the majority of the rings it does not suffice, for they do not surround the entire trachea; and even if he slaughtered the majority, he still did not slaughter the majority of the trachea except for the upper ring that surrounds the entire trachea — Tur; and see above siman 20.]
