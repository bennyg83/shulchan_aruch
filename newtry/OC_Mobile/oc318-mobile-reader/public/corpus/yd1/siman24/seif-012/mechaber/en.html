Hagramah — how so? This one who slaughters on the trachea above in a place not fit for shechitah; or who began to slaughter in the place of shechitah and slaughtered a little and tilted the knife outside the place of shechitah upward and finished there — but if he slaughtered the majority of the cavity of the trachea in the place of shechitah and tilted the knife outside the place of shechitah upward and finished there the cutting of the entire trachea — it is kosher; and the same applies if he slaughtered the majority of two in an animal in the place of shechitah and completed the shechitah in hagramah or darsah — kosher; and there is one who invalidates in darsah, and one should heed his words l'chatchila. {Rama: And the custom is to declare treifah whether in darsah, hagramah, the first minority, the last minority, in the trachea, or in the esophagus (also in Mahariu).}
