And finished there cutting the entire trachea — kosher. In Tur it is written: even for one who forbids shehiyah and haladah in the last minority — the reason here is he does not make invalid in the place of shechitah, for what he cuts afterward in the minority of the trachea is as if cutting with the foot after slaughtering the majority of the siman in a bird or after majority of two in an animal.
