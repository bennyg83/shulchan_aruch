If he slaughtered with holacha or hava'ah alone — if the knife has the full width of a neck and beyond the neck the full width of a neck {Rama: (with the skin and spinal column) (Mahariu)} — it is kosher; and if not, invalid, for whenever it does not have this measure it is impossible to slaughter without darsah through holacha or hava'ah alone; and if he did both holacha and hava'ah, even if he slaughtered with any awl whatsoever, it is kosher. {Rama: And some are stringent in an animal (Mordechai and Ran in the name of Rabbenu Tam, and some say in Rif and it is mentioned in Agudah and Kol Bo and Hagahot Alfasi) and the custom in these provinces is to invalidate an animal even if he did holacha and hava'ah if the knife does not have somewhat beyond a full neck and beyond the neck (this is Mahariu's view).}
