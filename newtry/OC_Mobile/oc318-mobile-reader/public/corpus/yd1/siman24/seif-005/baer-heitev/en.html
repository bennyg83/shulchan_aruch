And Shach wrote: it appears to him the Rav's view is specifically where there is no great loss one should be stringent; but in great loss one may be lenient {Rama: (and Peri Chadash wrote: even in small loss the lenient did not lose; but Beit Yisrael wrote slaughterers now declare treifah even in great loss; and so by experience. Further: one should not slaughter a bird, even a large bird, with a knife used for animals, for easily he will come to darsah. Kenesset HaGedolah wrote to warn slaughterers not to slaughter seated, for sitting leads to darsah. Peri Chadash wrote: b'dieved, whatever he knows he did not darsah is permitted, and so Beit Yisrael. Once someone slaughtered many birds for a wedding resting a finger — Vilna sages permitted b'dieved due to great loss and mitzvah need. One who does not know the laws of shechitah must stand over him from start to finish, even in the last minority, lest he darsah — Kenesset HaGedolah)}.
