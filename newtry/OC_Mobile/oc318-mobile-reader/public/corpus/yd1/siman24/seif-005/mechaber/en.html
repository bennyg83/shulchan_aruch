If he was slaughtering and cut the entire spinal column — it is kosher {Rama: (Mordechai in the name of Maharam; see s.k. 5): And the custom is to declare treifah even if he only cut the majority of the spinal column (Mahariu, Agur, and Tur HaDeshen) and one should not deviate, for many authorities hold thus (Rabeinu Ephraim deduced from Rashi's explanation).}
