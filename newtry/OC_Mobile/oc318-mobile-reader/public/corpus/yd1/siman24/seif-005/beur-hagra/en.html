(Likut) If he was, etc. — for what is written "not like," etc. — explained in Tur HaDeshen that blood should not be absorbed in limbs; and for blood it is needed in kodashim; and even in non-sacred it is forbidden, as in chapter Kol HaBasar (113a) — see there (end).
And the custom, etc. — as Rashi explained there, 27a, s.v. delo leshavya, etc.; and the first reasoning holds like Mordechai on what Rashi wrote "no more" — meaning no more is needed; and Rashba explained because of blood absorption in limbs it is forbidden.
(Likut) And the custom, etc. — see Mordechai and Hagahot Alfasi, chapter 2, s.v. vekara, etc.; however in the tosefta, etc. — but the tosefta is not understood as their explanation; but the essence is like the first reasoning, and the tosefta's explanation is regarding darsah, as in the mishnah "he was slaughtering and removed the head," etc. — kosher; and Or Zarua, siman 67, and provided he does not grasp, etc.; and in such a case the tosefta deals when the knife has a full neck and he did not draw the entire knife (end).
