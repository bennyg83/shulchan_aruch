And the custom is to declare treifah, etc. And study is needed on this, for many great poskim permit — behold they are Mordechai and Agudah, chapter HaShochet, in the name of Maharam, and Ramban and Ran, chapter HaShochet, on the mishnah "he was slaughtering and removed the head," etc.; and so Rashba wrote in the name of great commentators; and so Ra'ah in Bedek HaBayit (daf 23); and so appears Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilchot Shechitah, law 9, as explained in Kesef Mishneh there; and so in Ba'al HaItur; and so Rashba in his chiddushim and Tur HaDeshen and Maharshal, beginning of siman 2; and so appears in Tosafot, chapter HaShochet, s.v. vetu; and so appears in responsum of Binyamin Ze'ev, siman 327; and so is proven from the mishnah and sugya, chapter 1 of Chullin (daf 19b), and the tosefta Mordechai brought, chapter HaShochet — see there and understand; and so is proven from all the poskim; and Taz in siman 21 also in Tur HaDeshen, siman 187, wrote that by law it is permitted, only they practiced to be stringent; and so appears in Agur, who concluded "but most poskim permit" — accordingly; and perhaps Rabeinu Bechor's view is specifically to be stringent where there is no great loss; and in such a case.
