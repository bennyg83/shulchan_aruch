Majority of spinal column — Taz brought Rashba on "we do not make it a block" — meaning do not break spinal column before shechitah, then blood absorbs in limbs, etc. — end; and Taz challenged in chapter 2: if so by Torah one may not break spinal column — why does salting help? — and Rashba himself ruled salting helps — see there. Shach answered in Nekudot HaKesef: the verse teaches us that even in kodashim where blood is needed, therefore do not break spinal column, etc.; and wrote: so it is correct — do not say anything; for Rashba daf 23 brought one derivation for kodashim and one for non-sacred from "and you shall slaughter from your herd" — if so the challenge is on the verse for non-sacred, not Shach's words; therefore the essence is the mitzvah of shechitah is to break spinal column and remove head, for thus the soul departs, as the mishnah in Ohalot teaches — through this it leaves the category of alive; and if so, permitted to eat raw, for it is not blood — the verse teaches not commanded thus; and automatically we know raw is forbidden as blood (that was not) explained — only through salting permitted, blood absorbed separates and is expelled — simple.
