And one should not deviate — many authors are lenient; and already Maharal of Prague ruled to be lenient; and this appears specifically if he slaughtered unintentionally; but intentionally, l'chatchila, to slaughter with the spinal column one should be stringent, for thus the tosefta teaches, chapter 2 of Chullin: he was slaughtering and cut — in Beit HaMikdash, if he intended — invalid; if not — kosher; therefore all proofs to validate are without intent; but with intent there is no proof against the tosefta — one should be stringent.
