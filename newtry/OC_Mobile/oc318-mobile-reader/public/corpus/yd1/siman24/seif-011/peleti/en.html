Under the first minority and slaughtered from below upward — the matters imply haladah was inserting under the first minority and slaughtering it in haladah — not simply inserting under skin and slaughtering minority of siman and tearing skin afterward — haladah in first minority; and Shach in s.k. 11 wanted to explain thus in Shulchan Aruch — but the wording "inserted the knife under the first minority" does not imply thus, as Bach understood; and I do not know: since under skin we do not know, and haladah in minority of simanim was unresolved — it would be safek safeka: perhaps no haladah under skin; and if you say haladah — perhaps minority of simanim is not treifah; and likewise reverse; therefore Shulchan Aruch omitted skin — understand.
First minority in haladah — Shach and Perishah mean the esophagus — any amount forbids; but in the trachea — Perishah and Shach would permit by law; Maharshal and Bach forbid; Shach challenged; and see Peleti — do not reject Maharshal; he has great pillars; otherwise for us who declare treifah everywhere, no difference. Maharshal's words appear correct in shehiyah — though Ashkenazi shechitah Beit Yosef brought implies even in shehiyah one may say they were stringent; see there — he admits clearly this is only for validation; but by law he did not decide to validate; and so Ra'osh's words in Rashba's responsum 267 explicitly: for Rashi, even in trachea first minority not forbidden — examine and find like my words. Truth: the challenge from the baraita "half trachea slaughtered and paused — kosher" — at first glance a refutation; but to resolve: those authorities hold it depends on Ribbi and the rabbis — Ribbi suffices with majority in fitness, rabbis require all in fitness at start and end; see there daf 30, Rashi and Tosafot — you will see; and one may say that baraita on shehiyah in first minority is Ribbi; but as we rule stringently like the rabbis, as Rashi wrote, even shehiyah in trachea first minority invalidates. With this, resolve Rabbenu Tam's challenge from tosefta and baraitot contradicting each other — say one is rabbis, one Ribbi — see Tosafot daf 30b, s.v. hachalid; at least leave shehiyah in first minority in place; but darsah, haladah in trachea first minority — many authorities invalidated, as Beit Yosef in Ashkenazi shechitah, with reason: in shehiyah he stands in slaughter and does not slaughter in disqualification; unlike darsah and inserting, where he slaughters in disqualification and acts to disqualify. What more than BeHaG (and Rashba in responsum aimed at him), who wrote haladah in first minority of simanim is forbidden d'oraisa; and if he inserted the knife between simanim and regretted before slaughter — remove gently and slaughter elsewhere and check if he hit trachea or esophagus; and the challenge on hitting trachea — what difference, haladah in trachea minority? — learn he forbids as Ashkenazi shechitah wrote; and more authorities hold thus; and if so, heaven forbid to reject Maharshal's four words where he has a trusted pillar to be stringent.
