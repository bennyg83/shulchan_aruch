And Shach wrote: the esophagus is meant — for in the trachea haladah in the first minority is not forbidden, like half a defective trachea, above end of siman 21; and at the beginning he also forbids if he slaughtered majority of one siman and inserted under the remainder — specifically in an animal; but in a bird, once one siman was slaughtered it is fit and haladah no longer invalidates. Maharim Tiktin wrote: this latter part certainly deals when he inserted under skin and slaughtered the first minority from above downward and afterward slaughtered the rest not in haladah — this is what he adds to the first division, where he slaughtered the first minority from below upward — worse.
