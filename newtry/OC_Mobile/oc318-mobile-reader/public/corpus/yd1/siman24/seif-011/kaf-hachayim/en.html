(k) And the measure of two necks — in bird and animal; do not think: I have fourteen finger-widths in the knife, though the neck is wide, it reaches two necks since he draws and brings — enough even more beyond the neck; as I heard from an expert boasting he can slaughter calves with a bird knife — they do wrong: when the knife is short it slips into the slaughter-house mid-shechitah — comes to darsah, shehiyah, and ikkur — therefore measure for each species two necks with skin and spinal column. Minchat Yitzchak, in Leket Yosher, part 7.
