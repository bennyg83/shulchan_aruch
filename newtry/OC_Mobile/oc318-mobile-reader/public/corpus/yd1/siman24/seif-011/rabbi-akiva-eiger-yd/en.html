(Siman 24, seif 11) If he inserted the knife under the first minority and slaughtered it from below upward — according to the first explanation of Rabbenu Tam, brought in Shach, s.k. 11: required under the knife under the first minority and he slaughtered the majority below it properly. Plainly it may deal with inserting in the first minority of the trachea — no concern from perforation — and he slaughtered the simanim in this haladah; and the wording "inserted in minority of simanim" may refer only to the trachea, as Rabbenu Tam explains the unresolved shehiyah in minority of simanim refers only to the esophagus. However, for the other explanation — this is Shulchan Aruch's law: inserted under the first minority and slaughtered it in haladah from below upward — this must be inserting in the first minority of the esophagus, for in the trachea first minority haladah does not harm; if so, inserting the knife may be like perforating the esophagus. I saw in Sefer HaYashar of Rabbenu Tam (siman 257) who wrote the first explanation above and wrote on it: for perforating the esophagus there is no concern — it is the manner of shechitah, as we are not concerned for kodashim under skin [should read: under skin because of blemish] — and this is very difficult: skin is different whether cutting from above or perforating; unlike simanim, where the manner of shechitah is required, and inserting the knife is mere perforation. More difficult: what compelled Rabbenu Tam to this — in the first explanation plainly inserting in first minority of trachea. And for the other explanation: since the essence of the unresolved matter is whether slaughtering the first minority in haladah invalidates — one may say the unresolved matter plainly: inserted under skin and cut a minority of simanim; and also on the side that under skin there is no haladah, we find inserting under the simanim and slaughtering first minority of esophagus from below upward. Later I found in Sefer HaMakri'a of Riaz (siman 5), who brought Rabbenu Tam's words in Sefer HaYashar and wondered greatly and raised that there is concern of perforating the esophagus; and incidentally I saw a wondrous matter there: what he refuted Rabbenu Tam's proof from under skin in kodashim — blemish of skin is not a blemish — from what we establish in Chatas HaOf and Chullin: flawed — if so, proven flawed is not a blemish in kodashim; if flawed were a blemish, because of outside slaughter he would not be obligated to bring inside. In my humble opinion this is wondrous: we rule there is no tumah and zav status in birds; with this there was room to resolve Tosafot's challenge, Chullin (daf 29b), s.v. kegon sheshachat, etc. — he is lashed for animal and flawed knife — see there: in an animal blemish disqualifies, it is not accepted inside.
(Siman 24, Shach, s.k. 13) But certainly by law. In responsum of R' Yosef Migash (siman 41) he holds: by law shehiyah in the first minority of the trachea is forbidden — understand.
