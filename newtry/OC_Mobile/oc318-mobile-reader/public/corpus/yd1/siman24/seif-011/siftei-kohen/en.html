[If he inserted the knife, etc. All the laws in this seif are explanations of the unresolved matter "he inserted in a minority of simanim — what is the law?" — and it was not clarified, and we rule stringently and it is doubt of neveilah.]
[And slaughtered it from below upward, etc. Bach wrote: one must explain accordingly that before he inserted he cut the skin and inserted afterward under the first minority of the siman — otherwise it is under the skin and in any case invalid; and so Tosafot and Ra'osh according to Rabbenu Tam, who also explains insertion under the first minority — end; and I did not descend to the end of his view, for certainly according to Rabbenu Tam, who explains insertion under the first minority and slaughters again all the simanim in haladah, it must be so; but in this law, where he only slaughters the minority in haladah and completes shechitah not in haladah — likewise it would be under the skin; and from the unresolved matter whether haladah applies in a minority of simanim, or we say since he completed shechitah not in haladah it is kosher.]
[And likewise if he slaughtered the majority of one siman in an animal, etc. But in a bird, once one siman was slaughtered it is fit and haladah no longer invalidates — so all poskim; and see above siman 23, s.k. 9.]
[And likewise if he slaughtered the first minority in haladah, etc. The esophagus is meant — for in the trachea it is not forbidden because of haladah in the first minority, like half a defective trachea, as in the Gemara and poskim; and Taz, siman 21, and siman 23, seif 5; and so in Derishah. Bach wrote in seif 8: and I found Maharshal who wrote — and since Tur wrote there are those who forbid hagramah on a third and slaughter two thirds, all the more one should forbid shehiyah or haladah in the first minority, even in the trachea; otherwise one would say the same regarding hagramah on a third and slaughter two thirds; on the contrary I would say: even one who disagrees and holds kosher when he did hagramah on a third and slaughtered two thirds, since it is not the place of shechitah, nevertheless agrees regarding shehiyah or haladah, for what difference is there between the last minority, where everything was already slaughtered properly and nevertheless we forbid because it became like a disqualification in shechitah — all the more in the first minority — end; and these are clear words, not like Maharuch, who wrote that in the first minority of the trachea it is not forbidden because of haladah and permits from Maran — end Bach; and in truth I found thus in Hagahot Maharshal on Tur; but I wonder: if they wrote "is there one who says shehiyah in the first minority of the trachea is invalid?" — behold the sugya is arranged, chapter HaShochet (Chullin daf 28a): half the crop was slaughtered and he paused the time of shechitah and completed — kosher; and Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilchot Shechitah, law 6: if he slaughtered the trachea alone, half or a minority, and paused a long time — he returns and completes shechitah and there is nothing in it — end; and it is simple and agreed among all poskim, as explained above siman 23; and even those who forbid hagramah on a third and slaughter two thirds — Rashba explicitly permits shehiyah in the first minority of the trachea, and the reason hagramah alone is invalid is because at the time life departs (meaning when slaughtering the middle third the majority was slaughtered) there is no majority in the place of shechitah, unlike shehiyah and haladah, which are kosher according to all; and so appears in Tosafot daf 30b, s.v. hachalid, and Ra'osh there and other poskim; and Ribash, siman 187, explicitly according to Rambam — these the Mechaber cited deal with the esophagus, that in the trachea haladah in the first minority is kosher — see there; and in chapter HaShochet (Chullin daf 32b) it is proven explicitly that haladah is no worse than the mishnah of the crop, and we rule below siman 34 that it is treifah only in the majority; and therefore what is found there from Maharshal is for us, who are stringent in hagramah in the first minority — and the same in all laws of shechitah — from concern we are not expert in examining the esophagus and we fear he touched the esophagus, as above siman 23; but certainly by law and according to the poskim and Rambam, shehiyah and haladah in the first minority of the trachea are kosher, as Derishah wrote.]
