Under the first minority — this is haladah: he inserted under the minority and slaughtered in haladah from below upward that minority; but if he inserted under skin and slaughtered a minority of simanim and afterward tore skin and slaughtered the rest properly — this is not dealt with here; and it deals when skin was torn from the start, as Bach wrote; and see Peleti — insertion under skin and slaughtering only a minority in haladah — there is room to validate — see there.
In an animal — but in a bird, once one siman was slaughtered it is fit — what can haladah afterward invalidate?
And likewise if he slaughtered the first minority in the esophagus — certainly invalid, for it is only a disqualifying perforation; but in the trachea — Perishah and Shach would permit by law; and Maharshal and Bach forbid; and Shach challenged him; and see Peleti — do not reject Maharshal's words, he has great pillars; otherwise for us, who declare treifah everywhere, no difference.
