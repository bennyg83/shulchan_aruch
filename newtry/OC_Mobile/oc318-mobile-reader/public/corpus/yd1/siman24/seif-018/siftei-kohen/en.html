[He brings another animal and slaughters, etc. And the same applies: he brings another animal and uproots it and slaughters — if the first reddens more, kosher; if not, invalid; and that inversion on that siman itself does not help, as below siman 34 regarding crop severance — for here, since it is dangling it is not treifah, therefore the siman is considered alive; and even if it slipped before shechitah, the prior cut reddens more than the cut after shechitah — Tosafot, chapter Eizehu Mekoman, daf 54a.]
