Found, etc. — according to Ra'osh: R' Yochanan is stringent against Rav; and Rav Nachman explains their words; and halakhah follows R' Yochanan and Rav Nachman.
(Likut) Found, etc. — as the text "Rav Nachman said"; and to explain R' Yochanan's words. Ra'osh; and in Tur HaDeshen he wrote: even for the text "Rav Nachman said" one may say thus; and further, since it is a Torah prohibition and halakhah was not interrupted — if like Rav Nachman or like R' Yochanan, one must act stringently like both; but Rambam and Razah ruled only like R' Yochanan alone, and everything follows grasping or not (end).
And now, etc. — Hagahot Maimoniyot.
