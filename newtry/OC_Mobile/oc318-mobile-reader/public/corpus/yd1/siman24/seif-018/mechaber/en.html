If the slaughtered siman is found dangling — if when he slaughtered he grasped the simanim in his hand or the skin of the slaughter-house from behind it and the siman was pressed under the skin — behold, this is invalid; and if not, it is permitted through examination: he brings another animal and slaughters the siman and afterward uproots it — if the two slaughterings resemble each other, it is kosher; and if the second reddens more, the first is invalid; and now we are not expert in this examination — therefore in every case it is forbidden.
