The laws of darsah, haladah, hagramah, and ikkur. In it are 20 seifim.
Darsah — how so? Such as one who placed the knife on the neck and pressed and cut downward like cutting a radish or gourd — behold, this is invalid; and needless to say if he struck with the knife on the neck the way one strikes with a sword and cut the simanim in one motion.
