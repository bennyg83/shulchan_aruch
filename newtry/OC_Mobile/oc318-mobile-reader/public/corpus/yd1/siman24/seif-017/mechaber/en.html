If he slaughtered one of the simanim and the second is found dangling and it is unknown whether it slipped before shechitah or after shechitah — behold, this is invalid. {Rama: And even if he threw the bird from his hand, we do not say that because of the throw or convulsion it slipped (Maharik, shoresh 35); however, the slaughterer is believed to say it was not dangling at the time of shechitah (Piskei Maharai, siman 200).}
