Invalid. Explanation: from doubt; and we do not say if it had been dangling beforehand he would have seen or felt it — who can distinguish this? — therefore invalid from doubt; and it deals with a bird.
Shechitah. And Taz wrote: although I wrote above one cannot distinguish at shechitah — here it must deal where after shechitah in the place of slaughter a change is seen from what it was at shechitah — the uprooted place turned and came there; with this he is believed to say it was not so at shechitah; unlike above where nothing of uprooting is seen at slaughter place, only above at jaw connection still covered in skin — certainly he cannot distinguish.
