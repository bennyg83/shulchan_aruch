Behold, this is invalid. And Maharik concluded there: and they told him — if it had been dangling beforehand, he would have seen or felt it, for who can distinguish this? — it is like bones in a full belly — end.
However, the slaughterer is believed, etc. At first glance this contradicts what Maharik wrote above, that one cannot know at shechitah; here it deals where after shechitah in the place of slaughter a change is seen from what it was at shechitah — the uprooted place turned over and came there; with this he is believed to say it was not so at shechitah, and we must say it happened afterward through convulsion; unlike above, where nothing of uprooting is seen at the place of slaughter, only above at their connection in the jaw, still covered in skin — certainly he cannot distinguish this.
