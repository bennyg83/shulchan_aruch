If after he slaughtered the majority of the simanim he inserted the knife under the minority remaining of both or of one of them and paused — it is permitted; and there is one who forbids in this too, and it is fitting to heed his words l'chatchila. {Rama: And the custom is to declare treifah any haladah, whether in the first minority, the last minority, in the trachea, or in the esophagus (shechitah of Mahariu).}
