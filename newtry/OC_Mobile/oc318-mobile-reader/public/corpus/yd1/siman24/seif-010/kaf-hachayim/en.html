(j) That which they said: if the knife has two necks in what is slaughtered — kosher with holacha or hava'ah alone — if he begins from the knife head intending to draw or bring its entire length — then even if from sharpness it slaughtered one full neck — kosher; but if he began in part of the knife, from that part onward there must be two necks' measure from there; and if he intended not to draw or bring the entire knife, only until some point — from the start until that stopping point there must be two necks' measure. She'eilot uTeshuvot, part 2; Kometz, part 4; Ahel Yitzchak, siman 6, in Leket Yosher, part 2; Zivchei Riv, siman 5, on temidim, part 7; Minchat Yitzchak, part 3; Zivchei Tzedek, part 10.
