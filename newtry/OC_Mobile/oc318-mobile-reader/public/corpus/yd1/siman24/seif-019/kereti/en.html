Kosher — although it is doubt of prohibition, we hold it is not common for a crop ring to slip while alive without the blow being evident and blood clotted; and we always rely on the common case; and see below siman 50.
And some declare treifah — for Torah doubt is stringent; and it is a whole ring or majority; but for a partial amount we have no concern — crop severance in majority; and if doubt whether majority — Taz validates also from safek safeka; and see latter responsa who were lenient in several matters; and since I do not intend to be lenient, I did not bring their words — whoever wishes to rely may see in books.
