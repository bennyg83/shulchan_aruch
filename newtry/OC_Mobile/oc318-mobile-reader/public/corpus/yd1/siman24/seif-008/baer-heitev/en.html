And Taz challenged this view from Talmud Yerushalmi that it is not so: in chapter Behemah HaMekasheh (Chullin daf 74) Rav Oshaya asked: he put his hand in the animal's womb and slaughtered a nine-month fetus — why not forbid from haladah, for the knife is not exposed? Rather haladah is only under the cloth tied on its neck. See responsum of Shevut Yaakov, question 45, answer on this challenge; and in Sefer Nekudot HaKesef.
Haladah. And Shach wrote: but in birds, if he can slaughter without plucking feathers, better; and if impossible, he may pluck feathers so he does not come to haladah; but plucking feathers from a live bird from its wings for writing is forbidden due to tza'ar ba'alei chayim — Maharit in the name of Beit Yosef.
