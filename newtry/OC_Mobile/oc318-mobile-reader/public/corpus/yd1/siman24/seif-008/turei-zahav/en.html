This is Rambam's view in Tur (♯) and it is difficult from Talmud Yerushalmi that it is not so, for in chapter Behemah HaMekasheh (Chullin daf 74) Rav Oshaya asked: he put his hand into the animal's womb and slaughtered a nine-month fetus alive — what is the law? And why do we not forbid from haladah, for the knife is not exposed? Rather haladah applies only to what is tied on its neck; and Levush erred in this in siman 14, as written there.
