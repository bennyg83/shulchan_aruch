If he inserted the knife under the skin, or under tangled wool on the neck of the animal, or under a cloth tied on its neck, or the cloth is stuck to it with wax and he slaughtered — his slaughter is invalid; but if the cloth is spread on its neck and he slaughtered, his slaughter is kosher; and there is one who invalidates in this too, and l'chatchila one should heed his words. {Rama: And likewise one should be careful with sheep that have tangled wool on their necks to detach the tangled wool so he does not come to haladah (Mordechai, chapter HaShochet, and Rokeach).}
