[And if he darsahed or inserted, etc. Know that the entire seif is the language of Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilchot Shechitah; and Ribash in responsum 187, R' Yosef ibn Shooshan, and Kesef Mishneh were entangled in his explanation, for it was difficult for them: he wrote that haladah in the first third is certainly invalid, yet earlier wrote that if he slaughtered a minority of simanim in haladah and completed not in haladah — doubt of neveilah (as in s.k. 11); and what connection does darsah or haladah in the first or middle third have to what he wrote here regarding hagramah? Ribash was lengthy in resolving it and concluded Rambam's words are incorrect and hard to fit. Kesef Mishneh brought responsum of R' Yosef ibn Shooshan, who was lengthy and rejected all Ribash's words and intended one matter with Kesef Mishneh: Rambam's words "if he darsahed or inserted in the first third — invalid" mean: if he darsahed or inserted the first third and slaughtered properly the second third and darsahed or inserted the third third; and likewise if he slaughtered the first third and darsahed or inserted the middle third and slaughtered properly the last third — certainly neveilah, for we would not think that just as with hagramah, slaughter a third, hagramah a third, slaughter a third is kosher, so too with darsah and haladah — therefore he said there is no case of insert a third, slaughter a third, insert a third; in such a case even with hagramah it is invalid; and likewise all is invalid in darsah and haladah, for hagramah is not in the place of shechitah; therefore when he slaughtered the first and last thirds in the place of shechitah, he slaughtered two thirds in their place — what do we care about the middle that he did hagramah? But haladah and darsah, which are in the place of shechitah in the middle, also invalidate; but if he slaughtered a minority of simanim in haladah and completed properly — we are in doubt, therefore he ruled doubt of neveilah — end; therefore the Mechaber cited Rambam's language whether in the first third or the middle third without the letter hei; and all their words are very forced and not seen at all, as the knowledgeable know; and what can I do — in my humble opinion Rambam's words are simple; therefore he was precise in his concise language: "and if he darsahed or inserted, whether in the first third or the middle third" with the letter hei — and it refers to what he wrote: hagramah on a third and slaughter two thirds, or slaughter a third, hagramah a third, slaughter a third — kosher; and he said: do not think that since with hagramah on one third it is kosher, likewise if he darsahed or inserted the first or middle third it would be kosher; for since hagramah is not in the place of shechitah we do not count it at all — and if so it is as if he darsahed or inserted in the first minority; he teaches it is not so; and therefore he said: "and if he darsahed or inserted in the first third" — meaning not slaughter a third, hagramah a third, slaughter a third, but darsahed or inserted a third, hagramah a third, slaughter a third; or in the middle third — meaning not hagramah a third and slaughter two thirds, but hagramah a third, darsahed or inserted a third, slaughter a third — his shechitah is invalid; for we require majority in shechitah specifically; and now Rambam deals properly with the trachea, therefore it is certainly invalid — and this is clear to me.]
