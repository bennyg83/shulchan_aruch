If he did hagramah on the trachea at the beginning of a third and slaughtered two thirds — kosher. If he slaughtered a third and did hagramah on a third and returned and slaughtered the last third — kosher. If he did hagramah on a third and slaughtered a third and returned and did hagramah on the last third — behold, this is invalid; and if he darsahed or inserted haladah, whether in the first third or the middle third — behold, this is invalid.
