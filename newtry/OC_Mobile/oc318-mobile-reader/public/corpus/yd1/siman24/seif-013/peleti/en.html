And if he darsahed or inserted, etc. — Ribash and more rabbis cried out: behold Rambam wrote: and if he slaughtered a minority of simanim in haladah and completed not in haladah — doubt of neveilah, unresolved; and here he ruled certainly invalid. And see Shach who wants to say Rambam deals with the same third where he inserted, etc.; the forcedness is clear; as Peri Chadash wrote; and the shorter path in Rambam's words: since Rambam explains the unresolved matter of shehiyah in minority of simanim, as Mechaber above siman 23, seif 4 — meaning pause time of slaughtering minority of simanim near majority; see what I wrote there; in any case he does not explain simply pause from start when he slaughtered minority of simanim — he holds plainly treifah, for any perforation in esophagus forbids, and shehiyah is perforation, as Tosafot and tosefta on shehiyah in first minority of esophagus forbid. Accordingly, also in the unresolved haladah in minority of simanim — Rambam must explain like Rabbenu Tam: he slaughtered four measures of trachea, majority properly, minority remaining he inserted under and cut from below upward; if so, the doubt is whether this is called haladah, since otherwise majority was slaughtered. This is Rambam's intent; and "if he slaughtered a minority of simanim in haladah" — haladah was inserting under the minority and the haladah itself was inserting under the minority of siman not yet slaughtered; though wording is somewhat forced, he resolves himself and it is doubt; but afterward Rambam in the body of the law makes haladah that is haladah without doubt — only he did not slaughter in haladah only a minority of simanim — certainly treifah, as above from tosefta; on this Shulchan Aruch has no doubt, only doubt whether called haladah; and so the authorities teach. Peri Chadash's words, except he was lengthy unnecessarily and wrote Tur illuminated path, etc. — he did not speak correctly here: for according to the unresolved shehiyah in minority of simanim, Rambam cannot explain on first minority, for in his view certainly treifah without doubt; and therefore the explanation is as above — when fitness measure near majority, etc.; and Tur in siman 23 wrote explicitly Rambam explains that unresolved matter on first minority — if so, he does not hold thus in Rambam; and see Beit Yosef; but truth guides that this is better than all explanations.
