And Shach wrote: what is written here — if he darsahed or inserted in the first third, etc. — refers to what he wrote before: hagramah on a third and slaughter two thirds, or slaughter a third, hagramah a third, slaughter a third — kosher; on this he says: do not think that since with hagramah on one third it is kosher, likewise if he darsahed or inserted the first or middle third it would be kosher; for since hagramah is not in the place of shechitah we do not count it — and if so as if he darsahed or inserted in the first minority; he teaches it is not so; therefore he said: if he darsahed or inserted in the first third — meaning not slaughter a third, hagramah a third, slaughter a third, but darsahed or inserted a third, hagramah a third, slaughter a third; or in the middle third — meaning not hagramah a third and slaughter two thirds, but hagramah a third, darsahed or inserted a third, slaughter a third — his shechitah is invalid; for we require majority in shechitah specifically; and now the Mechaber deals properly with the trachea, therefore certainly invalid — and this is clear to me — end.
