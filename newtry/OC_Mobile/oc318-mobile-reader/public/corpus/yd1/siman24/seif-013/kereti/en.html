And if he darsahed, etc. — all wondered why certainly treifah and neveilah — behold Rambam wrote earlier: if he slaughtered a minority of simanim in haladah and completed not in haladah — doubt of neveilah; and Kesef Mishneh explained this too means doubt of neveilah — forced; we do not find the Gemara doubts darsah; and Shach wrote he speaks collectively — when he inserted a third, hagramah a third, slaughtered a third, in any case there are two majorities in disqualification; what difference haladah and hagramah; and see Peleti — truly complete haladah or darsah in the first minority is certainly invalid; and above regarding haladah called doubt because it was not clear haladah — the doubt was whether it is haladah at all when inserted under minority of siman — on this we judge; and this is clear; and so Peri Chadash's view.
