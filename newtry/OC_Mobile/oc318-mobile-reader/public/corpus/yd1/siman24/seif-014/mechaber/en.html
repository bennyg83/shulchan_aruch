All these distinctions apply when it is certain he did not touch the esophagus but only the trachea on the upward side; but in the esophagus, even if he slaughtered in it any amount outside the place of shechitah, whether on the upward side or the downward side; and likewise on the trachea on the downward side before the fitness of shechitah was completed — even though he completed all the rest in the place of shechitah — behold, this is invalid because perforation of these places invalidates in any amount {Rama: (his own opinion and R' Yerucham) and the custom is to declare treifah in any case and one should not deviate (Mahariu).}
