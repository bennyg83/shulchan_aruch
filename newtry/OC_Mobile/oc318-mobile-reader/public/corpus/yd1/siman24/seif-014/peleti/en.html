Invalid — meaning certainly; and Shach wrote s.k. 20: although haladah in first minority of esophagus is doubt of neveilah, Ribbi distinguishes: since in the manner of shechitah one may say it is not a perforation; but hagramah, not in the place of shechitah, is a perforation. And Ribbi's words were not needed — Ra'osh and Rashba truly do not explain on haladah in first minority as Ribbi — Ra'osh on haladah in last minority, Rashba like Rabbenu Tam; if so, one may say in complete haladah in first minority they agree it is invalid — and Ribbi's distinction is not needed.
