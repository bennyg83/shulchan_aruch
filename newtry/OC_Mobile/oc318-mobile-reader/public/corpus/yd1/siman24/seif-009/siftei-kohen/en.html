And one should heed his words l'chatchila — meaning in this, that where he slaughters it is exposed; but in the first law, when he slaughters at the head of the knife and the knife in the place of slaughter is covered by skin — it appears from the Mechaber that even b'dieved it is invalid; and so appears in Beit Yosef; and Atzei Erez wrote one must be careful not to insert the knife at the time of shechitah under the skin in any place, whether opposite the simanim or opposite the knife not opposite the simanim — end; and he did not explain well: at the beginning b'dieved invalid, and Bach challenged the Mechaber from Tosafot and Hagahot Ashiri who forbade even b'dieved — and I do not understand his challenge, for one may say Tosafot deal when the knife in the place he slaughters is covered, and in Hagahot Alfasi explicitly on this — see there; and see Maharshal, chapter 1 of Chullin, siman 42.
