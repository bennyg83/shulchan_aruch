Maharshal disagrees with this ruling because Tosafot, chapter 1 of Chullin (daf 20), in the name of Rashi, chapter Chatas HaOf, wrote: when the knife head is covered — it is haladah; therefore forbid even b'dieved — end; and from this there is no grasping: there it deals when he also slaughters the simanim at the knife head, and there deals with melikah with a knife and he must be careful not to cut the spinal column with majority flesh upon it, as I cited siman 20, seif 3 — therefore he must plant the knife head under the flesh on the spinal column so it remains, and below he cuts at the knife head the spinal column with the simanim — behold at the place of slaughter the knife is covered; and so explicitly in Hagahot Ashiri, beginning of chapter 1 of Chullin, as written; and so in Rokeach, laws of shechitah; but this law does not deal there at all.
