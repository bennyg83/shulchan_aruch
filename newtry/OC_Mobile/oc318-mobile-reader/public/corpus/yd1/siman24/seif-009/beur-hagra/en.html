One must, etc. — as written above, chapter 2, because he inserts; and by Torah law he followed the stringent view; and in Hagahot Alfasi there and in Tosafot there, s.v. mipnei, etc.; and Rashal wrote: although Tosafot rejected Rashi's words in Zevachim, nevertheless poskim upheld his words, for even though according to their view it cannot be explained, nevertheless according to truth Rava's words can be explained thus; and he wrote: although Rava holds it is not inserting, we do not rule like Rava, who holds drawing and bringing in melikah is invalid, and we rule like those who hold kosher, as Rambam wrote; and Rashal wrote his reason because we say there, 20a: R' Avin wanted to say, etc.; R' Yirmiyah told him, etc.; and we say there: sons of R' Chiya, etc. — they alone hold thus; and he wrote: although at first glance halakhah follows Rava as the later authority, truth is there is no Rava, only Rabbah — and so Rashi there, 20a, s.v. hanicha, etc.
