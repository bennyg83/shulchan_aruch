One must be careful when a person slaughters and spares the skin not to make a large tear in it and slaughter at the head of the knife while it is covered by the skin; however, if he slaughters in the middle of the knife there is no concern if its head is covered by skin, since the knife in the place where he slaughters opposite the simanim is not covered; and there is one who is stringent in this too, and one should heed his words l'chatchila.
