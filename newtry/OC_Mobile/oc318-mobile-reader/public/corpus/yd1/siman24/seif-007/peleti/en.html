Haladah — Taz wrote s.k. 3: language of hiding, therefore called haladah because it hides in earth — so in the Gemara. But the Gemara implies the opposite — on melikah with a knife they said slaughter invalid: one says darsah, one says inserting; the Gemara asked: one who says darsah — why not say inserting? And answered: how is haladah? Like a weasel returning to house foundations — it hides; and this reveals; and because he inserts there, Tosafot explained he slaughters simanim from below upward — see there; behold this is not like a weasel; and we rule haladah with a narrow knife slaughtering from below upward — if so, we do not require similarity to a weasel, only mere hiding; and in Maharshal this extra wording in Taz is superfluous.
