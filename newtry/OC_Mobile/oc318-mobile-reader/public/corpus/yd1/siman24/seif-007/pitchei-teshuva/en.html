See responsum of Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 56: asked regarding a slaughterer who slaughtered and pressed his finger on the trachea until it was crushed and he heard the sound of breaking crop rings — is there prohibition concern? He wrote: slaughterers do wrong pressing thus on simanim between fingers — concern lest a ring jump and through the ring's point perforate the esophagus — one should protest; nevertheless b'dieved we do not establish prohibition, for severing the trachea in width in the majority is not a concern; and in length, even if only something remained above — kosher; all the more when skin is whole — no concern when no other presumption — see there.
