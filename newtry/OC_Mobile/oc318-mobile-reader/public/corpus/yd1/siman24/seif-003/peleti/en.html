Both, etc. — in my humble opinion I could not descend to the end of Beit Yosef and the latter authorities' view; therefore I will copy the early authorities briefly. In Chullin daf 31 they said: we require beyond the neck a full neck — Tosafot wrote: at first glance it appears one must draw that entire full neck beyond the neck, therefore this measure is needed; however it appears one need not; this measure was only so he slaughters with ease; sometimes the knife is sharp and he slaughters without drawing the full neck — end; and so Ra'osh. Now see what Ra'osh wrote: there are two animals slaughtered as one, and the knife has only two necks' measure. And distinguish first from inner. We explain with a parable: one animal, neck four fingers abcd placed; beside it animal also four fingers hezach; knife eight fingers abcdefgh. If so, when he begins slaughter through drawing, knife letter aleph comes to animal one's daleth; when he draws, slaughter on both — behold aleph of knife on animal two's daleth. Ra'osh's view: validate first, invalidate second; and reverse — then aleph of knife comes to animal two's daleth; then second kosher, first invalid. Beit Yosef learned: always the full eight-finger knife must pass over each animal's entire neck; therefore in the first manner of drawing, he draws the knife over animal one's entire neck from cheth until aleph passed; but on animal two at completion only aleph of knife on its daleth — only four fingers passed on the neck — from this Beit Yosef derived: if he wanted to pass the entire knife beyond until cheth of knife on animal two's hei — all eight passed — kosher; and reverse in bringing — only four fingers passed on first animal, rest remain on second — treifah; but if he drew and brought until cheth of knife on animal one's aleph and entire knife beyond animal also — kosher. And if so, everything depends on Tosafot's reasoning that we require the entire two necks, i.e. eight fingers, on each animal; and Tosafot rejected this, especially Ra'osh — for with a sharp knife he cuts easily immediately without passing the entire knife; only Tosafot and Ra'osh: with a long knife he does not darsah; but a short knife only according to the animal darsahes — it appears this knife must be free, not busy with another slaughter — then it is a long, spacious knife; but when four fingers remain on the knife for another slaughter, there is no spaciousness — it is as slaughtering with a four-finger knife holding also four fingers — therefore here, slaughtering two animals as one with drawing or bringing alone, certainly the essence of slaughter cannot be as one, for one animal's majority was slaughtered before the second; only called as one because completion is as one — when bringing knife from one animal to the other, the first's majority was finished before reaching the second; but when coming to slaughter the second, the first's majority completes — called as one; and so Taz's view — see there; therefore one cannot say when beginning with drawing that both finish truly equally — only first head of knife cheth begins at first neck's start aleph and draws — at first neck's end daleth, hei of knife arrives and four letters remain outside animal — then he slaughters with spacious eight-finger knife — kosher; but at second animal's end, cheth of knife on its cheth — four fingers on first animal to complete majority onward, and four on second — no free knife, for on second cheth to hei, and aleph–daleth of knife on first to complete — no free knife, no spaciousness — therefore darsahed and second treifah; and reverse in bringing from second to first — half knife on first aleph–daleth and on first aleph–daleth, but hei–cheth remain on second to complete — no spaciousness — invalid; and if so, always one treifah one kosher — no difference if he passes knife afterward on all; for at slaughter time knife must be spacious and free — if not, treifah; and what he does afterward — what difference; therefore I did not descend to Beit Yosef's end. Ramban challenged: when one is darsahed, the second too; and in this too I did not succeed — for the first slaughtered with drawing has spacious eight-finger knife, neck only four — not darsah; afterward on second there is no spaciousness and he darsahed; if so, then on first too he darsahed after majority slaughtered — what is Ramban's view that darsah in last minority is treifah? — understand; and perhaps when he begins he sees on second animal there will be no spaciousness and prepares to slaughter forcefully — darsah — then even first was darsahed — understand; in any case for us who forbid darsah in last minority, Ramban's words are correct without doubt — examine, for the weakness of intellect in my humble opinion.
