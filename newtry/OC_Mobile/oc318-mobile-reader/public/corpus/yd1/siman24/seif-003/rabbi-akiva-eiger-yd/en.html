(Seif 15 in the gloss) But while alive it is kosher, only shechitah does not help. Even in a bird: if one siman slipped and he slaughtered the second — it does not help, as the baraita teaches explicitly: found the crop dangling and unknown if before or after shechitah, etc. — the reason: nevertheless we require both simanim fit for shechitah — so Tosafot. However, according to Ramban, dangling is treifah, only when uprooted at the time of shechitah it is neveilah, for simanim sway [Shach, s.k. 24, brought thus — wondrous; I saw he stood on this in Peri Megadim]. One may say: truly in a bird, if he slaughtered one siman and the second was uprooted at shechitah — shechitah helps purify from neveilah, for the slaughtered siman was slaughtered well; and though the second uprooted siman is not fit for shechitah, from swaying one may say we do not require both simanim fit — for Ramban there is no proof from that baraita — for in any case forbidden from treifah law; nevertheless one may say not neveilah, like treifah that was slaughtered. And likewise perforated esophagus — we rule (siman 33) it is neveilah; nevertheless in a bird, perforated esophagus and he slaughtered the crop — one may say its disqualification in shechitah is not called, since the slaughtered siman has no disqualification — only like ordinary treifah that was slaughtered. With this, Rav Yonah's proof can be resolved: broken leg, unknown if while alive or after shechitah — forbidden from that baraita on dangling — meaning there in a bird it is not dangling doubt in shechitah, for shechitah was valid — only doubt of treifah.
