See Ba'er Heitev; and he wrote in Shevut Yaakov, part 1, siman 46, on what Taz, siman 24, s.k. 17, wrote on a ring found on the knife — to validate from safek safeka, though Taz himself holds in established prohibition safek safeka does not help — nevertheless here he relies to be lenient because Agur and Beit Yosef, etc.; and though Shach implies like the stringent — since in Nekudot HaKesef he did not challenge, etc.; and see in Be'er HaGolah, siman 122, he tends to be stringent but not compelled; the lenient like Taz here did not lose — see there; and see Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 55: he practiced treifah for a whole ring on the knife; on the ground, validate in great loss, mitzvah need, Shabbat honor, or guest honor; if the ring was cut in the middle and hangs on the trachea, or rests or hangs in the middle on knife or ground — do not be stringent; he received from an expert slaughterer: to avoid crop-ring doubt common in geese, slaughter pen-slanted obliquely — then proven no ring in their slaughter — end. And in She'eilat Ya'avetz, part 2, siman 237, on a slaughterer who slaughtered a goose and found a crop-ring piece on the knife — pressed on his face to permit: Rama and followers to be stringent only on a whole ring, for one can suspect and attribute to pushing before shechitah; but here the crop and ring were cut through the width into two — clearly evident it could not be before shechitah — impossible to uproot without shechitah; especially a straight cut like this — no suspicion of pushing before shechitah as latter authorities fear when not whole width but a thin strip; not all aspects equal — all agree to permit; whoever is stringent after examination that it is not a whole-width ring but only a thin strip is among the astonished, etc.; and so my obligation: stuck in the knife or glued on his face and fell to ground — all one matter with clear proof the ring was entirely slaughtered; a fortiori from Taz's law, etc.; therefore he only doubted partial cut — not whole — certainly slaughtered — see there.
