The whole. And Taz wrote: even at the time of shechitah ikkur forbids only in the whole, not any amount; specifically when torn in one place that minority remained in strength, unlike when torn in many places — Maharim Tiktin in the name of Rashba, and the author of Hagahot shechitah did not write well; and Shach in Sefer Nekudot HaKesef disagrees with Taz; and so Peri Chadash wrote.
