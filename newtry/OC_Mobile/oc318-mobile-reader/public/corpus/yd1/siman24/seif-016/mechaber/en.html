That which is invalid in ikkur — this is when the whole was uprooted; but if something remained in it, even any amount — kosher, provided that which remained is in one place; but if the remaining minority is dangling — a little here and a little there — invalid, for it is evident the matter was uprooted by force and what remains connected is a dangling connection and it is as if the whole was uprooted and invalid. And this applies when the majority was uprooted; but if only a minority was uprooted and the majority remains, even though most of this remainder dangles a little here and a little there — kosher.
