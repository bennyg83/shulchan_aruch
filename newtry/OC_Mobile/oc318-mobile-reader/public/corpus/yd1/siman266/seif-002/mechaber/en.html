Circumcision overrides Yom Tov and Shabbat at its time; but not at its time it does not override; and even at its time it overrides only the circumcision itself, uncovering, metzitzah, and even if he finished he returns for strands that impede — that is, if skin of the foreskin covering the majority of the height of the corona remained, even in one place; and on those that do not impede — if he did not finish he returns, and if he finished he does not return, and they place a bandage on it: {Rama: And it is permitted to move the knife after circumcision to store it in a courtyard joined in an eruv, even though he no longer needs it that Shabbat, for it was not set aside at twilight since he needed it that Shabbat — so it appears to me (permitted moving is written in Teshuvat Or Zaru'a netiv 1; the reason is the Mechaber's words, not like Maharil).} But its preparatory acts do not override, since they could have been done by day; therefore they do not make a knife to circumcise with, nor bring it from place to place — even to take it out of the house and bring it via roofs, courtyards, and alleys that were not joined in an eruv; but if he forgot the knife on a roof or in a courtyard, it is permitted to bring it from this to that, even if the courtyards were joined with the houses.
