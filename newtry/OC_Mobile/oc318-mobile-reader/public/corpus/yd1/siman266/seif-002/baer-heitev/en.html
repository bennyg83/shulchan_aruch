And it is permitted. Taz disputes Rama and forbids moving the circumcision knife on Shabbat except as needed, even after the circumcision, comparing it to a shofar after blowing. Nekudot HaKesef rejects his proofs and agrees with Rama, but still permits only putting it away in an enclosed shared courtyard, not gratuitous handling.
