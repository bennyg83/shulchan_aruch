If they forgot and did not bring a knife before Shabbat — they tell a gentile to bring a knife on Shabbat, provided he does not bring it via the public domain. The general rule: any matter whose performance on Shabbat is forbidden to him as shevut — it is permitted for us to tell a gentile to do it in order to perform a mitzvah at its time; and a matter whose performance on Shabbat is forbidden to us as melacha — it is forbidden for us to tell a gentile to do it on Shabbat.
