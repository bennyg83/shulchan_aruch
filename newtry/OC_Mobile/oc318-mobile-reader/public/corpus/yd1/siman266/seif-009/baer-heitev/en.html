According to Rabbi Yose. Meaning a moment immediately before night. Once three stars are visible, it is night. Maharam Alashkar rules that a child born Friday after sunset but before three medium stars is not circumcised until Sunday.
According to Rabbah. He holds that the final three-quarters of a mil before night are twilight. The circumcision is therefore on the ninth day and does not override Shabbat. Shach.
