Like R' Yossi. That is, like the blink of an eye adjacent to night itself; and it was already explained in siman 262 seif 6 that when three stars are seen it is night. And in responsum Maharam Alashkar siman 96 — a question: an infant born on Shabbat after sunset before three medium stars were seen — should he be circumcised the first day according to Rif, Rambam, and Rosh z"l who hold bein hashemashot is immediately after sunset from above the earth; or should he be circumcised on Shabbat eve according to Rabbeinu Tam z"l who wrote there are two sunsets and bein hashemashot does not begin until the end of sunset after the sun entered the thickness of the firmament, but before that it is a full day; and so Ramban in Sefer Torat HaAdam, Semag, Ran, etc. — see there at length bringing the method of Geonim and poskim and explaining their words; and he concludes there a general principle regarding this infant — we do not circumcise him until day one and Shabbat eve.
Like Rabbah. For he said in chapter Bameh Madlikin (Shabbos 34a) that whenever the eastern horizon reddens it is also bein hashemashot, and it walks three-quarters of a mil before night (and unlike Rav Yosef who said there it is day); therefore circumcise on the ninth and not on Shabbat.
