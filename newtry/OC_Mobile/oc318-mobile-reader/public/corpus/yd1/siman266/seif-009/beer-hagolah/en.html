Shabbat 34b.
Following Rabbi Yochanan there, 35a.
Following Rabbi Yehudah there in the baraita.
He holds that twilight lasts only the blink of an eye. Meaning, he is uncertain during that blink, as Tosafot writes there, while before it is certainly day.
There in the name of Rav Yehudah in the name of Shmuel: three quarters of a mil.
