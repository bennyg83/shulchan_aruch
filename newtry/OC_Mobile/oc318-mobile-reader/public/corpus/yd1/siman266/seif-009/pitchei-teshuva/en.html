Two foreskins. See Magen Avraham siman 331; and see in responsum Or Zarua Pnei Me'orot part 2 siman 31 who wrote it is proper to warn mohalim when they see two skins one upon the other not to perform periah further on Shabbat and wait until after Shabbat, for there is no danger if they do not perform periah until after Shabbat — see further.
