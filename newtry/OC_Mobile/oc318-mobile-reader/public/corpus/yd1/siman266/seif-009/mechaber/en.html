In the measure of bein hashemashot, tannaim and amoraim disagree at the end of chapter Bameh Madlikin; and Baal HaItur wrote: we are uncertain of the halacha according to whom; therefore if a child was born from sunset, it is doubtful until bein hashemashot of R' Yosei ends, and they circumcise on the tenth; and if born motza'ei Shabbat from sunset, we are stringent like Rabbah: {Rama: (And see above siman 262 seif 5).}
