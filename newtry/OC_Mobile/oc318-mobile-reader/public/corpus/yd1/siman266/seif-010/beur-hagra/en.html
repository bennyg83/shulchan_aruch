A minor, etc. This follows Rabbah, who says that we are concerned, etc.
And one circumcises, etc.; one born by Caesarean section, and one born, etc. This is because it remains uncertain whether the halachah follows the first Tanna or Rabbi Chama, since Rav Asi agrees with Rabbi Chama. See there, 61a: “Perhaps Rabbi Yehudah,” etc.; “and if you wish,” etc.
And an androgynos. This follows the first Tanna, as stated at the end of chapter 8 of Yevamos.
A tumtum, etc. This is the Rosh's ruling, because Rav Shezbi's position was refuted there.
And some forbid. The Rif ruled according to Rav Shezbi, since his view was not conclusively refuted. See the Rashbam there, and chapter 3, 52b, in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel. The Rif and Rosh likewise rule this way at the beginning of chapter 4 of Berachos.
