A minor born already circumcised, and one who has two foreskins, and an androgynous, and one born by caesarean section, and a house-born slave whose mother did not immerse until she gave birth — even though they are circumcised on the eighth, they do not override Shabbat: {Rama: (A tumtum that was torn and found male — they circumcise him on Shabbat (so Rambam and Rosh); and some forbid) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rif, and so Rabbeinu Yerucham).}
