One born in the seventh month — they circumcise him on Shabbat even if his hair and nails did not finish; but one born in the eighth month — they do not circumcise him on Shabbat unless his hair and nails finished; and the same law for doubtful seven doubtful eight — they do not circumcise him on Shabbat unless his hair and nails finished: {Rama: And some say we circumcise him since he is doubtful seven-month — only that they do not desecrate Shabbat for him in other matters (Semag, and so implied from Rif and Rosh and Tur); and so appears to me as the essential ruling.}
