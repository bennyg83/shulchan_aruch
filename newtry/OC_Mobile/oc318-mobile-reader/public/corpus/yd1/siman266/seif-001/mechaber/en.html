Which circumcision overrides Shabbat and Yom Tov. And it contains 14 seifim.
Circumcision — whether at its time or not at its time — overrides tzara'at; for if there is a bright spot on the skin of the foreskin, even though there is bright spot in the cutting of the bright spot, they do not cut it with the foreskin; but if after he was circumcised flesh grew in his circumcision until he does not appear circumcised and it needs to be cut — if there is a bright spot on that flesh, it is forbidden to cut it, since he need not circumcise him again except rabbinically.
