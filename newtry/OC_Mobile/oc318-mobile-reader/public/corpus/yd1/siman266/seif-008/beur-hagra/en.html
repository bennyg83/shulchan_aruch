And even the second day of Yom Tov, etc. The Mishnah mentions the second day of Rosh Hashanah because the Mishnah was taught in Eretz Yisrael. This is the Rosh's explanation. Rambam, however, writes that circumcision overrides the second day of Yom Tov observed in the Diaspora.
