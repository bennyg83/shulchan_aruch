Even on the second festival day observed in the diaspora. In my opinion, the principal ruling follows the Rambam, that circumcision overrides the second festival day observed in the diaspora. This is also the view of the Semag, positive commandment 28, page 115, and the Kol Bo, siman 73. This also appears to be the view of the Bartenura, who explains that the two days of Rosh Hashanah have a single sanctity. The Rambam and those who follow him write there that likewise all other matters that do not override Shabbos do override the second festival day observed in the diaspora.
Even if only his head emerged, etc. The Semak, siman 157, writes that one must investigate carefully when a woman was in difficult labor on Friday. If the child’s head emerged beyond the birth canal on Friday, he is not circumcised on Shabbos, but rather on Friday. One must ask the women who attended the birth, and if they do not know, one follows the established practice. The Kol Bo, siman 73, writes likewise.
