(Shach s.k. 18) And likewise you have no mitzvah greater than this. See Eliyah Rabbah (Orach Chayyim siman 248); and in responsum HaRanach (siman 39); and see Tiferet Lemoshe who distinguishes that specifically when he was ill and recovered; but when born on Shabbat and it was impossible to circumcise him earlier due to doubt about mohalim on Thursday.
