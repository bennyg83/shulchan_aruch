One born at twilight, which is doubtful day doubtful night — they count from the night and circumcise on the ninth, which is doubtful eighth; and if born Friday eve at twilight, it does not override Shabbat, for Shabbat is not overridden from doubt; and likewise it does not override Yom Tov from doubt — even the second day of the Diaspora (Tur siman 7 in the name of Teshuvat Rosh, and it is in klal 26); and even if only his head came out at twilight — even though all of him came out on Shabbat, he is not circumcised on Shabbat.
