Of the Diaspora. Shach rules that the second day of Yom Tov in the Diaspora is overridden. Bartenura implies the same, distinguishing the two days of Rosh HaShanah as one sanctity. Rambam and others hold that acts not overriding Shabbat may nevertheless override the second festival day.
His head. Semak writes that the women should be asked whether the child's head emerged before Shabbat. If so, circumcision does not override the following Shabbat. If they do not know, local custom is followed.
