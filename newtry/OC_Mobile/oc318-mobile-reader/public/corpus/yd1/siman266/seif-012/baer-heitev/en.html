Who apostatized. Bedek HaBayit suggests that even where both parents are apostates, their son may be circumcised on Shabbat because they remain obligated in mitzvot. Shach and Taz infer otherwise and forbid it when the mother too is an apostate. Rambam permits circumcising Karaite children on Shabbat where the community respects rabbinic tradition and may return, but later authorities forbid doing so where they reject periah, because circumcision without periah is no mitzvah.
