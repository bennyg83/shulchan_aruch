Who apostatized. And Bedek HaBayit wrote it appears even one married to an apostate woman — they circumcise on Shabbat the son born from them since they are obligated in all mitzvot — end; but to me this does not imply from their conclusion "since his mother is Israelite" — implies but when his mother is apostate forbidden — see end Shach; and so Taz when his mother is also apostate they do not circumcise on Shabbat; and he wrote further in Beur HaDarkei HaZahav in Rambam's name: Karaites as long as they do not speak falsely against rabbis and do not mock the words of our holy sages of Mishnah and Gemara — we circumcise their sons and even on Shabbat lest proper seed emerge and they repent; and in responsum Maharatz Ashkenazi wrote Karaites dwelling among us nowadays do not have one of all qualities the Gaon mentioned, therefore forbidden to circumcise on Shabbat since they do not accept periah, and if circumcising without periah he violates Shabbat and does not perform mitzvah — end; and see above siman 262 s.k. 2 what is written regarding circumcision not in its time if permitted Thursday or Friday — see there.
