An Israelite, etc.; an Israelite, etc. See Even HaEzer, siman 44, se'if 9, and what is stated there. The Yerushalmi in chapter 2 of Yevamos and Bereishis Rabbah, chapter 7, relate: Yaakov of Kfar Gevuroya ruled in Tyre that it is permitted to circumcise the son of a gentile woman on Shabbos. Rabbi Chaggai heard and sent him a message: “Come and receive lashes,” etc. He said to him: “Do we desecrate Shabbos for anyone other than the son of an Israelite woman?” Yaakov asked him: “From where do you derive this?” Rabbi Chaggai answered: “From the verse, ‘And now let us make a covenant with our God to remove,’ etc.” Yaakov replied: “Would you administer lashes on the basis of a verse from the Writings?” Rabbi Chaggai said: “But it is written there, ‘and let it be done according to the Torah.’” Yaakov asked: “According to which Torah?” Rabbi Chaggai answered: “According to that which Rabbi Yochanan said in the name of Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai,” etc.: “Your son born from an Israelite woman is called your son, but your son born from a gentile woman is not called your son.”
