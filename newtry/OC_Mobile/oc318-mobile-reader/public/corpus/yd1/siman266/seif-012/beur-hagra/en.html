Israel, etc., Israel, etc. Even HaEzer siman 44 s.k. 9 and from what is written; and in Yerushalmi chapter 2 of Yevamos and Bereishit Rabbah chapter 7 — Yaakov of Kfar Gibboray taught in Tyre: permitted to circumcise the son of a gentile woman on Shabbat; R' Chagai heard and sent to him "come be lashed," etc. — "but do we not violate Shabbat except for the son of an Israelite alone?" He said to him: "from where do you know? From this that is written 'and now let us cut a covenant to our God to expel,' etc." He said to him: "and from Kabbalah you deserve lashes?" He said to him: "but it is written there 'and as Torah he shall do.'" He said to him: "from which Torah?" He said to him: "from that which R' Yossi said in the name of Rashbi, etc. — your son who comes from an Israelite woman is called your son, and your son who comes from a gentile woman is not called your son.'
