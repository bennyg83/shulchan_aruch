(Seif 12) We circumcise him on Shabbat. At first glance, even a non-Jew born from a Jewish mother should also be circumcised on Shabbat. However, according to what Maharsha wrote in Kiddushin (75b) in Tosafot there — that he holds a non-Jew who had relations with a Jewish woman, the child is a non-Jew and requires conversion — if so, we do not circumcise him on Shabbat. See the book Atzei Arazim in Even HaEzer (siman 4, subparagraph 4).
