His father. We do not say that with regard to his father this is a pesik reisha that is agreeable to him, since he is preparing his son's brother. Shach. The Taz wrote: this is not comparable to becoming established in slaughter, where we require three times, for there one must be concerned for even slight faintness, through which he may pause or press; but here the only concern is lest he be unable to train his hands, and therefore one time suffices.
