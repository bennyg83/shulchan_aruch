And even, etc. See there, 133a. The Gemara raises its difficulty specifically where one states that he intends to remove his son's baheres, and where another person is available. It does not challenge the act because it is a psik reisha, even though for the other person it is a psik reisha concerning a result in which he has no interest. This is comparable to the case stated in chapter 12 (103a), “in another person's field.” Nevertheless, the act is permitted when performed by the father, and all the more so in the present case.
