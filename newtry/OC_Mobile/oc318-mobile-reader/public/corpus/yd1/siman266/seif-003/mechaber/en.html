They do not grind spices, nor heat hot water for him, nor make a bandage for him, nor mix wine and oil for him — unless he ground cumin before Shabbat and puts it in his mouth; if he did not mix wine and oil before Shabbat, he gives this to himself and this to himself; and they do not make a shirt for him, but wrap a scarf on him; and if he did not prepare before Shabbat, he wraps on his finger and brings it as clothing, even from another courtyard that was not joined.
