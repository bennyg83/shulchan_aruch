And permitted. See Taz [and see in Levushei Serad who wrote in the name of Mahari Segal that when circumcising on Shabbat it is forbidden to wipe the knife from blood — see there]; and see in responsum Noda B'Yehuda tinyana Yoreh De'ah siman 162 who wrote regarding a mohel who circumcised on Shabbat and afterward threw the foreskin from his hand — it is permitted to take the foreskin and place it in prepared dust; and even though Rashal wrote in responsum siman 40 that after he threw it to dust it is muktzeh — nevertheless before he threw it his mitzvah was not yet done and it is not muktzeh; and he concluded in any case lekhatchilah the mohel should be careful that immediately when he cut the foreskin while still in his hand he should throw it to dust; but if it happened he threw it from his hand before then — whoever permits to lift the foreskin in order to place it in dust does not err — see there.
