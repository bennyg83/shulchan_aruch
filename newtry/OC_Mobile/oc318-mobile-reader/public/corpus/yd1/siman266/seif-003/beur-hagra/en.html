One may not grind, etc. The Mishnah there, 133a, and the baraisa, 134a.
Nor may one heat, etc. Chapter 6 of Eruvin (67b to 68a).
And one may not, etc. This is stated there in the Mishnah and Gemara, and as the Mechaber continues: “If he did not grind,” etc.
(Likkut) That did not make an eruv. It may not, however, be brought through a public domain, for the Sages permitted only a rabbinic prohibition performed with an unusual method, as stated above: “One may not grind,” etc.; rather, he chews it with his teeth, etc. This is unlike Ramban, who permits bringing it even through a public domain. This is the Rashba's view. See what I wrote, with the help of Heaven. (End of Likkut.)
