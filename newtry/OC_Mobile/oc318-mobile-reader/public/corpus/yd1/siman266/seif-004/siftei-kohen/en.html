And afterward the medicinal ingredients became scattered, etc. The Ran writes that if the ingredients prepared for after the circumcision became scattered before the circumcision, the Ramban maintains that a mitzvah is judged only at its present time. Therefore, they circumcise him and do not postpone the circumcision because they will afterward need to desecrate Shabbos, since at that later point it is preservation of life that overrides Shabbos, not preparations for circumcision. The Baal HaMaor and the Rashba maintain that the circumcision is postponed. The Beit Yosef writes that this also appears to be the opinion of the Rif and the Rambam, though this is not conclusive. Nevertheless, the Ateret Zahav writes that the circumcision is postponed, and the Bach rules likewise.
