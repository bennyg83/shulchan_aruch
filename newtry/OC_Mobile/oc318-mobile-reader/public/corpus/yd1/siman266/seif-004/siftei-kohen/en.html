And afterward the medicines were scattered, etc. The Ran wrote that if the medicines they prepared for after the circumcision were scattered before the circumcision, the Ramban’s view is that a mitzvah has only its time, and they circumcise him; they do not defer the circumcision because afterward they will need to override Shabbat, for later it is pikuach nefesh that overrides it, not preparatory acts for circumcision. The view of the Baal HaMaor and the Rashba is that the circumcision is deferred; end quote. The Beit Yosef wrote that this also appears to be the view of the Rif and the Rambam, but this is not necessary. However, the Atzei Zahav wrote that the circumcision is deferred, and so the Bach ruled.
