Dispersed. And Ran wrote that if those medications prepared for after circumcision dispersed before circumcision — Ramban's opinion that the mitzvah is only now and we circumcise him and do not postpone circumcision because we will later need to violate Shabbat, for afterward it is pikuach nefesh that overrides and not milah preparatives; and Raavad and Rashba hold circumcision is postponed; and Beit Yosef wrote thus appears Rif and Rambam's opinion though not compelled; however in Levush wrote circumcision is postponed and so Bach — end Shach.
