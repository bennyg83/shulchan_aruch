One should be careful that two mohalim should not, etc. The Beit Yosef derives a concern from Rashi's explanation of the Gemara, suggesting that although the first mohel would not incur kareis after beginning with permission, some prohibition may remain if he leaves the completion to another. This inference is astonishing. If there were any prohibition, why did Rav Pappa challenge the liability rather than distinguish between liability and a lesser prohibition? Rashi merely used the language of the Baraisa, which discusses liability, and did not imply an additional prohibition. Many major communities have the practice that one mohel performs the cutting and another performs periah on Shabbos. There is no prohibition in this practice.
