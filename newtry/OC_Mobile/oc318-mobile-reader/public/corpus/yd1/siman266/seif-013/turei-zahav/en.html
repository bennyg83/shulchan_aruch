He may not be circumcised, etc. The child of a non-Jewish woman follows her status. The Beit Yosef writes that the son of an apostate Jewish woman and an apostate Jewish man may be circumcised on Shabbos because, despite their sin, they remain Jews. This does not appear consistent with the Tur. The Tur's permission was based on the mother being observant enough that we do not presume the child will be raised outside Judaism. Where both parents remain apostates, there is substantial reason to presume that they will raise the child in their way, even if they presently permit circumcision. They may also be apostates regarding the entire Torah except for circumcision. Such a child should therefore not be circumcised on Shabbos.
