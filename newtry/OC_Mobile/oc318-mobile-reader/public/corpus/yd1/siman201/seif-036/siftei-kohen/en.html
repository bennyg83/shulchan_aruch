"And if he hollowed a pit in it," etc. — one should not challenge: although they are drawn, nevertheless they come to the mikveh through drawing and drawing that was drawn is valid, as below, seif 44; and it is strained to say it deals when he hollowed the pit near the mikveh in a case where there is no drawing. And further, that a pipe narrow on both sides, etc., implies even if the pipe is very long; and one can say here it deals when he comes to bring water to a mikveh that does not have a majority of valid water, for drawing that was drawn is not valid except when the mikveh has a majority of valid water, as below — and it is simple.

"And if the water comes," etc., "in a wheel," etc. — so Darkei Moshe wrote: Mordechai on the wheel that rolls water, and on the wheel small pierced vessels are fixed like a water channel, and when the wheel rolls the small vessels fill and pour, etc.; Rokeach wrote that it is permitted to immerse in that case even if there is no measure of a mikveh, since the water always rises there is connection, even though the vessels are not pierced like the opening of a wineskin, since the water floats on the surface of the bucket on both sides there is connection and they are not invalid because they were drawn in vessels, for the vessels are pierced like a water channel — end of his words; and my heart wavered, end of Mordechai; and Beit Yosef wrote that all commentators disagree with this permission, that there is no connection in such a case for a mikveh, even though the water floats above on the bucket, etc., end of Darkei Moshe; and these matters are explained in Beit Yosef, this siman, and explained there that if the vessels are pierced like the opening of a wineskin it is obvious there is connection; rather there they were not pierced only like a water channel; and it is straightforward as below, seif 52. And now one should wonder at the Rav who copied these words that are only according to the one who holds a vessel pierced like a water channel is not considered a vessel; but the Rav already wrote above, seif 7, that we require a hole like the opening of a wineskin, and so below, seif 40, plainly like Tur; if so, since they are pierced here in a way that is not called a vessel, there is connection too — requires further study.
