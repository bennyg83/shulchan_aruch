Two mikva’os that do not have 40 se’ah, and 3 logim fell into one of them and it is known into which of them they fell, and afterward 2 [logim] fell and it is not known into which of them they fell — I can rely and say: into the place where the first fell, the second fell. But if regarding the first it was not known into which of them they fell, and regarding the second it is known into which of them they fell, he cannot rely and say: into the place where the second fell, the first fell. One [mikveh] has 40 se’ah and one does not have: I say that [the 3 logim] fell into the one of 40. One is drawn and one is not drawn: I say they fell into the drawn one.
