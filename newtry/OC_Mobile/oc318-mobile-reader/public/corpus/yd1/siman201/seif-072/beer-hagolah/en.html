The conclusion of the Gemara, Zevachim, daf 22 side a, and all these measures over which tannaim argue in the mishnah — here they argue: one holds in this one drinks, and one holds in this one drinks. Rosh there in explanation of the mishnah; and so Tosafot in the first chapter of Sukkah, daf 19 side b; and Rosh at the end of Niddah; and so from the words of Rambam, chapter 8 of Hilchot Mikvaot.
