"One who comes to draw water." — even valid water must not be, etc.; and it deals when the water is not fit to come to the mikveh except through this drawing in a vessel that receives tumah; therefore, even though it is a vessel not made to receive, since it receives tumah it invalidates; but if the water is fit to come aside from it, it does not invalidate unless it was made to receive, as above, seif 35.
"He should not hold in his hand a page," etc. — since a person receives tumah, but the page is a simple wooden vessel and does not receive tumah from the Torah. Ma'adanei Melech wondered, daf 311 side b: above, seif 14, the Mechaber copied Rambam's words that even in something that receives tumah from rabbinic words one does not stream through it — requires further study, end of his words. And it appears to me that streaming is different, since it is only drop by drop; and further one can say that there he only said one does not stream through it, meaning the water should not be called streaming to purify even in flowing waters, but nevertheless they are called trickling and purify in ashboran — it appears to me.
