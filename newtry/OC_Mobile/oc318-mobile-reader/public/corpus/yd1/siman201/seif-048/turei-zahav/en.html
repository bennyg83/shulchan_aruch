If it is possible to stop it up, etc. Responsa Rosh, klal 30, siman 4, states that one may stop the opening even with an object that is susceptible to ritual impurity. Only bringing water into a mikveh must be accomplished by means of something that is not susceptible to ritual impurity, as explained above in seif 35. Preventing the water from leaving the mikveh, however, is not a concern. In seif 50, two opinions are cited concerning this matter.
