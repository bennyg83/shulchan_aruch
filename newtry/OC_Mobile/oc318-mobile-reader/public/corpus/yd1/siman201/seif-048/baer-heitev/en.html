Maadanei Melech asks that above, in seif 14, the Mechaber rules that even an object susceptible only to rabbinic tumah may not be used to make the water flow. The Shach answers that there the ruling means only that it may not make the water valid as flowing water. It nevertheless remains classified as dripping water and can purify when collected in a basin. Reference 4.
