Ma'adanei Melech asks from above, seif 14, where the Mechaber says that one may not create flowing water through even something that is susceptible to impurity only by rabbinic law. Shach answers that there the ruling means only that one may not use it to purify in the status of flowing spring water. Nevertheless, the water is still considered dripping water and purifies when collected and standing.
