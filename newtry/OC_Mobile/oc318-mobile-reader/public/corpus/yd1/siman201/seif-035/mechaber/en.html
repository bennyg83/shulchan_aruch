If one placed a board under the pipe near the mikveh so that the waters fall from it into the mikveh: if it has 4 rims, so that it is fit to receive water, it disqualifies; and if not, it does not disqualify. If he stood it upright on its side for washing, even if it has rims it does not disqualify, since it is not positioned in a manner fit to receive. And this is when the waters would have been fit to come to the mikveh without it; but if the waters would not have been fit to come to the mikveh without it, even if he stood it on its side or turned it over on its mouth, it disqualifies.
