Since it receives tumah because its formation requires taharah, so wrote Perishah and Beit Yosef, that even though it is a simple wooden vessel, nevertheless since it receives tumah d'rabbanan it invalidates; and Hagahat Drishah disagreed with this, for below, seif 48, it implies that only what receives tumah d'oraisa invalidates; and so concludes Bach, that here it deals with a board on which a woman bakes bread, or joined for work, that now even if it has no rim it receives tumah ; end of his words; and so Taz, that with a tray that has rims it deals, that is not a simple wooden vessel and receives tumah. And accordingly one must say that which we said above, seif 14, that even what receives tumah from rabbinic words one does not stream through it ; is different; examine.
