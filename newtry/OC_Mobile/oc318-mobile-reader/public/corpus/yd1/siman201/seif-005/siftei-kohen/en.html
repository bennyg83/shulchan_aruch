And in it forty se'ah.. And although a spring suffices for tamei vessels in any amount ; that is where there is connection of the spring; but here, where the wave was detached, forty se'ah are required, for anything that is not an actual spring does not purify in any amount. And in Tosafot Yom Tov, chapter 5 of Mikvaot, he left it requiring further study; and in Bach he explained in another manner; and below, seif 56, s.k. 122, I rejected his words; also the words of Perishah do not fit well; and the wording "therefore" they strained ; but nothing is difficult, for "therefore" refers to streaming, that it has the law of a sea to purify through streaming.
