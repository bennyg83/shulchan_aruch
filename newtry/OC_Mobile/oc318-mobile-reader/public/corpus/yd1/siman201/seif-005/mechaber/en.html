All seas have the law of a spring to purify when flowing; therefore, a wave that was torn from the sea and contains 40 se’ah, and fell upon a person or upon vessels — their immersion has taken effect. But if one immersed in the wave while it is in the air before it falls upon the ground — even though it contains 40 se’ah — or if one threw vessels into the middle of the wave when it is formed like a dome, their immersion has not taken effect.
