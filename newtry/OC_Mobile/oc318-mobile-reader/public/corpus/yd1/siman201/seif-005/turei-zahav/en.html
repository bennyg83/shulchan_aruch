In a mikveh of 40 se'ah. The Rav wrote thus per his method in Beit Yosef and wondered at Tur who wrote plainly one does not believe a gentile — implying even if there are 40 kosher se'ah; and Rosh wrote in a responsum that if there are 40 se'ah the matter depends; and truly the Rav in Beit Yosef did not analyze this, for certainly we do not believe a gentile even in a rabbinic safek as in many places in Talmud, all the more here where the gentile benefits if it is full he takes wages — therefore we do not believe him at all even when full where there is concern it diminished; but in Rosh's responsum it deals where the gentile cannot pour there except through roofs — thus it was drawing they drew and it is valid if majority kosher water, namely 40 se'ah, as below seif 44; this is explained in Rosh's responsum Beit Yosef brought — thus no concern except when there are not 40 kosher se'ah; but Tur deals where the gentile can pour without drawing and then there is invalidity even when full if one se'ah is missing — therefore do not believe him at all that there is concern it diminished even one se'ah; and Tur learned his law from his father's responsum and analyzed well; and so appears practical law; and these words Shulchan Aruch wrote here that concern is only when there are not 40 se'ah are imprecise; and Maharach also wrote as I said.
