Provided there was no name of a detached vessel upon it. meaning, even though they are vessels that do not invalidate the mikveh, they do not validate the drawn water to be considered as drawn, except when there was no name of a detached vessel upon it; and here it deals when the water was fit to come to the mikveh aside from this vessel; but if they were not fit to come aside from it, even if there was no name of a detached vessel upon it, if it is a vessel that receives tumah it invalidates, because formation through taharah is required, as below, seif 48.
