Hamshachah is effective only upon the ground, or upon a pipe that did not have the name of a vessel while detached; but if one drew upon vessels — even a vessel of dung and the like — it is not hamshachah (Ra’avad). {Rama: And some say that hamshachah is effective only specifically upon ground that is fit to absorb; but if one drew upon a stone floor that is not fit to absorb, and all the more so upon a board or vessel — even though it does not disqualify the mikveh — it does not help (Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai, perek 2 of Shevuos, who wrote so in the name of Rokeach). And it is good to be stringent l’chatchila.}
