There is no distinction whether he wrings out his garment. At first glance this might mean only intentional wringing, while water squeezed out incidentally would not invalidate because a garment is perforated. In truth, the language 'fell into it' shows that even an unintended occurrence is included. The ruling concerns drawn water entering a mikveh from a garment or vessel, and the status depends upon whether the water came through human agency, whether the item is deemed a vessel, and whether the prescribed measure of drawn water entered before the mikveh was complete. The detailed cases in the Mishnah, Rosh, Rambam, and Beit Yosef establish that incidental squeezing can also invalidate when it produces the legally significant amount and manner of drawn water.
