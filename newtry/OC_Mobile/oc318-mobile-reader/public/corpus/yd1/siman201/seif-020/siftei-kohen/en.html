And the channel enters,. etc. ; Rosh explained: this is a channel of rainwater; and Beit Yosef explained: if it were a spring it would purify the drawn water in the pit in any quantity, as above, seif 11; and so he wrote in the name of Ra'avad. And it deals when there are not forty se'ah in the channel ; for if so, behold the pit is purified through hashakah, as below, seif 52; and so in Tosafot Yom Tov, chapter 3, mishnah 3, in the name of Rosh and Rosh; and see above, seif 60.
Until it is taken into account,. etc. ; from the fact that it teaches "until it is taken into account" and not "until their fill-amount exits" (as below, seifim 21 and 22), it implies that the water floating and exiting from there ; we do not count all of them as from the first waters (i.e., the fill-amount) as calculated below; rather according to the calculation of the water that was in the pit and the water descending into it they exit, or half and half, because the water descending into the pit last is closer to exiting than the first ones; and so we taught, etc. ; end of Ra'avad that Beit Yosef brings above, daf 237 side b. And the meaning is: "until it is taken into account" is a stringency ; it is not sufficient that the fill-amount exit, rather we always see that the channel water also exited according to calculation; and if so, according to calculation it is always stringent, as Ra'avad wrote; or half and half, etc. ; sometimes it is more lenient than by calculation and sometimes more stringent, if the channel water is more than the pit water ; such as there are forty log in the pit and channel water is 120 log and 80 log exited ; according to calculation, three-quarters of the channel water exited, i.e., 60 log, and 20 log of pit water exited, leaving 20 log; but half and half, all pit water exited ; so half and half is more lenient; and when pit water is more abundant, according to calculation 60 log of pit water exited leaving 60 log, but half and half only 40 log exited leaving 80 log ; and it appears we go both ways to be stringent ; examine. And Ra'avad further wrote there: if you ask, what is different here that we calculate floating exiting water by calculation, and below we say that all invalid water floats and exits first ; there, all was valid but was invalidated by three log; and by right it would suffice to estimate by calculation until three log; but for stringency it requires the fill-amount, i.e., forty se'ah; and therefore we see only the invalidated water exited and do not estimate by calculation that the valid rainwater that descended last also exited; but here the water in the pit from the beginning was all drawn; and therefore it is not sufficient to say that when so much exited as was in the pit, all that was in the pit exited; rather we always say that according to calculation the channel water also exited ; examine. And such are the Rav's words, end of s.k. 22.
