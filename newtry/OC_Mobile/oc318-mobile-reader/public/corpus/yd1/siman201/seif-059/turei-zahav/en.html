As though it were connected to the spring. It is purified through hashakah. Beit Yosef writes that this differs from drawing water from a spring into a vessel in seif 8, where connection to the spring does not help, because there the water is in a receptacle and was invalidated as drawn water, while here the object has no receptacle, even though it is susceptible to tumah, and therefore hashakah works. I do not understand this distinction. Once the connection constitutes hashakah, it should make no difference that the water is drawn, because all drawn water is purified through hashakah. Above in seif 8 I explained that there is no difficulty, because that case is prohibited only by decree. Beit Yosef himself writes at the end of this siman, beginning with the words "and if one immerses in it a pot," that according to Rambam connection to a spring helps even when the mikveh is inside a vessel, while according to Rosh it does not. In my opinion, even according to Rosh it works by strict law, as I wrote in seif 8.
