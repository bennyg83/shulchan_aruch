Until none of the drawn water remains, etc. Since the Mechaber does not say here, as in seif 22, that water must flow out equal to the entire original contents, the overflowing water is not all attributed to the first water. Rather, outgoing water is calculated proportionally to the water originally in the pit and the water entering it. Alternatively, the mixture is treated half and half because the later water enters last. The practical calculation therefore differs from the earlier case, and the amount of valid water required must be determined according to the proportions explained by the authorities.
