One who comes to validate a drawn mikveh from a complete mikveh that is not drawn: even though he connects it only like a hair, it is kosher, even if the waters of the “kissing” do not see the surface of the air. {Rama: And this is specifically with the invalidity of drawing, which is d’rabbanan; but with an invalidity that is d’oraisa we require [a connection] like the tube of a leather flask (Terumas HaDeshen and Rashi in Mikvaos), as was explained. And even with the invalidity of drawing, there are those who disagree (Tur and Rosh), and so it is proper to rule.}
