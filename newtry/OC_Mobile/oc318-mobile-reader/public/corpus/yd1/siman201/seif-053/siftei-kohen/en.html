A drawn mikveh,. etc. ; for that which we require like the opening of a wineskin is specifically to validate a lacking mikveh from the complete; but to validate drawn water from a complete non-drawn mikveh, even if one only pours hashakah like a hair, it is valid, because drawn water only invalidates rabbinically and they were lenient in this. ; there.
Like a hair.. meaning, like a hair-thread.

Bach wondered: above, seif 7, requires like removing an olive, and so above, seif 40, a hole like a hair does not suffice to bring water by hand l'chatchila, and here he validates with a hole like a hair, like Rashba's view ; requires further study, end of his words; and I do not know what the difficulty is, for Rashba himself in a responsum holds that by law above we require like the opening of a wineskin, and Beit Yosef brings it above, daf 237 side b at length; rather certainly one must distinguish, for here is different since there is a complete mikveh here, only it is drawn, and there is a complete mikveh beside it; therefore a hair suffices for mixture of mikvaot, since the drawn is rabbinic ; examine.
There are disputants,. etc. ; according to their reasons: for above, seif 3, entirely drawn is invalid d'oraisa; and accordingly, where the mikveh was mostly valid and partly drawn, such as when three log of drawn water fell in, all agree a hair suffices; and so it appears to me to answer what Bach wondered on Rosh and his supporters who require a hole like the opening of a wineskin, for we learned: they purify mikvaot, upper from lower, etc., and pour hashakah even like a hair; and he strained to reconcile that Rosh also concedes a hair suffices, only he was stringent to decree drawn on account of non-drawn, end of his words. According to what I wrote, it is simple: Rosh establishes that mishnah when the upper mikveh is invalid only rabbinically; and what Rosh wrote in a responsum as proof for the law of a spring drawn through a pipe, above, seif 49, that it is valid from the mishnah above (and I brought it above, s.k. 104), is not to say that for entirely drawn a hair suffices, rather he brings proof that even though it was drawn through a pipe that receives tumah, hashakah helps him, as explained there in the responsum ; that is his intent; and so for each according to how he invalidates rabbinically with a hair and d'oraisa with like the opening of a wineskin, as stated.
