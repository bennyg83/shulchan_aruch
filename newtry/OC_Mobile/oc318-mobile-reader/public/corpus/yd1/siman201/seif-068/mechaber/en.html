Two mikva’os: one has 40 se’ah and one does not have. 3 logim of drawn water fell into one of them, and he does not know into which of them they fell — its safek is pure, because he has upon what to rely. If both were less than 40 se’ah and they fell into one of them and it is not known into which of them — each of them is invalid, for he has nothing upon which to rely.
