If the mikveh was lacking 3 logim and 3 logim of drawn water fell into it, it remains disqualified forever until rainwater increases upon it, or kosher waters are rinsed over it, until the point that we can estimate that there fell upon it like its first filling and more — for the waters coming upon it push out the waters that are within it and take them out. If the mikveh was lacking less, even a kortov, and drawn waters less than 3 logim fell upon it and completed it — they did not disqualify it and they did not validate it. How so? It remains in its disqualification until rainwater descends upon it, or it is rinsed by the amount of water it was lacking. If rainwater fell upon it in that amount, it is kosher. If it was lacking even a kortov and 3 logim of water fell into it, it disqualified it, and it remains in its disqualification until its filling and more goes out from it. {Rama: And specifically with 3 logim of drawn [water] we validate in such a manner; but if it was entirely drawn [water], even if one poured upon it until they went out like its filling and more, it does not help — rather we calculate the waters that go out in proportion to the kosher and invalid waters (Beit Yosef in the name of Ra’avad).}
