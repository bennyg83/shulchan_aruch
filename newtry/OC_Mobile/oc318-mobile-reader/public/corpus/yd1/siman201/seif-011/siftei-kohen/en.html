"And nevertheless," etc. — in Sefer Magen Avraham he wondered: why in seif 10, when the spring continues, it purifies in flowing waters? And it appears to him that there it deals when the trickling waters did not increase — and it is simple.
"Only in ashboran," etc. — and accordingly one must say that above, seif 8, that if it sprays on the lip of the vessel it is permitted outside it even if the water inside is abundant — that is specifically in ashboran; but the plain sense of the words there appears that the law of a spring is upon them for every matter; and so implies from Bach and other acharonim. And one can distinguish, for there is different since the water itself inside the vessel is also from the spring and now the spring still sprays into them and they are connected to the spring — unlike here — examine.
