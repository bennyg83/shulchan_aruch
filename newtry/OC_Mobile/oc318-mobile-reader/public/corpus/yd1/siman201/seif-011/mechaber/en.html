A mikveh of drawn water, upon which spring-water was drawn — even if the spring-water is few, the few spring-waters purify the many drawn waters, whether the spring-water preceded the drawn waters or the drawn waters preceded the spring-water. {Rama: As will be explained below; nevertheless, one should not immerse in it except in a gathered pool, for it is no better than rivers in which the drippings exceeded the flowing (Mahari”k).}
