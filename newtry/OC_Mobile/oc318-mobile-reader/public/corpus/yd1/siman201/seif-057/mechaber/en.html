Anything mixed into a mikveh is like a mikveh, and one may immerse in it. Hollows adjacent to the mikveh, and the places of the hooves of an animal that had waters mixed with the waters of the mikveh like the tube of a leather flask — one may immerse in them. {Rama: Therefore, [with] a vessel placed at the side of the mikveh, one may move the mikveh with his hand so that a wave is made in the water and passes over the vessel, and immersion takes effect for it — provided that he does not uproot the wave from its place, but it remains connected to the mikveh (so it appears in perek 6 of Mikvaos according to the commentators).}
