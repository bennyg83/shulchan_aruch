Three mikva’os: in two of them, each has 20 se’ah of kosher water, and in one of them there are 20 se’ah of drawn water, and they stand side by side. If the drawn [mikveh] is on the side, and 3 [people] descended and immersed in them, and through this they filled and went out over their edge and mixed together — all 3 were validated, since once they were connected together with 40 se’ah of kosher water; and those who immersed in them are pure. But if the drawn [mikveh] is in the middle, such that the 2 kosher [mikva’os] can mix only through it, they were not validated; rather, they remain as they were at first, and those who immersed in them were not purified.
