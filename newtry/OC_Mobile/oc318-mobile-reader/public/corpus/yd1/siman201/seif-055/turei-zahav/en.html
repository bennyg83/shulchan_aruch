Until they are half and half. Tur writes that if the mikveh contains only twenty se'ah and he drew twenty se'ah of water into it, even by hamshachah, it is invalid. It appears clear that this invalidity has the status of ordinary drawn-water invalidity: if three log of drawn water fell in, there is no remedy until the original water has gone out, as explained in seif 22. This differs from seif 55, where if the drawn water is in the middle, the two outer portions remain as they were and are not invalidated by the drawn water in the center. There the hamshachah itself was performed permissibly; the only issue is that the outer portions do not contain forty se'ah because they do not combine with the drawn middle portion. Here, by contrast, the hamshachah itself was invalid because there was not a majority of valid water. This appears correct to me. I do not know why Derishah thought it could be rendered valid by allowing rainwater to collect upon it. In my opinion that is incorrect.
