Until the valid and drawn waters are equal. The Tur writes that if the mikveh contains only twenty se'ah and one draws twenty se'ah into it, even by means of hamshachah, it is invalid. It is clear that this invalidity is equivalent to ordinary invalidity caused by drawn water. Thus, if three log of drawn water fell in, the mikveh cannot be repaired until the original water has departed, as explained in seif 22. This is unlike the case in seif 55, where the drawn water is in the middle and the outer pools remain as they were and are not invalidated by the middle pool. There the hamshachah itself was permitted, and the outer pools are invalid only because they do not contain forty se'ah and cannot combine with the drawn middle pool. Here, however, the hamshachah was performed improperly because the majority was not valid water. This appears correct to me. I do not understand why the Perishah thought that the mikveh could be validated by subsequently collecting rainwater upon it, for in my opinion that is incorrect.
