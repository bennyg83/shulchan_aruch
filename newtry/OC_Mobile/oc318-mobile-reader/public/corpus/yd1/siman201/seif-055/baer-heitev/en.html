Pure. Shach writes that the water that flowed out must have returned to its place. Otherwise it is considered flowing water and the immersion is ineffective. The case must also be one where no water was actually missing when it rose over the edge, such as where the mikveh walls are high. Otherwise the immersion would certainly be ineffective and the mikvaot would remain deficient. However, this requirement that the water return applies only where the mikveh had only the minimum measure. If it contains more than forty se'ah, there is no need for the overflow to return, because even while water flows outward forty se'ah remain. It is not considered invalid flowing water when it exits solely because a person immersed. Even the authority who invalidates below in seif 50 when water flows out through a crack despite forty se'ah remaining agrees here, because there the water flows out on its own, whereas here it flows out only during immersion.
