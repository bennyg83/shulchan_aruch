A wall between 2 mikva’os that cracked from side to side — even the slightest amount — joins for the mixing of the 2 mikva’os to validate them; but for mixing, they do not join until there is in one place [a connection] like the tube of a leather flask. If the wall was breached at the top, one to the other, to the height of a garlic peel and to the width like the tube of a leather flask, it is kosher. {Rama: And the same is true if there was a mound of dirt between 2 mikva’os: if he removed a little from the height of the mound until they pour to one another, at the width of the tube of a leather flask, at the height of a garlic peel, it suffices — for we do not require the full amount of the tube of a leather flask except with a hole (Mordechai, Hilchos Niddah).}
