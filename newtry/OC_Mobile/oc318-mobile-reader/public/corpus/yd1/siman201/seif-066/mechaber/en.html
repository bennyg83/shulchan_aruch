If a mikveh’s waters spread out and one cannot cover himself in it, he places on one side stones or bundles of wood, so that its waters gather into one place and rise in a manner that he can cover himself in it — provided that he does not divide the entire mikveh; but with vessels it is invalid. {Rama: And even if she can cover herself in the waters, if the waters are not deep up to her navel and above it a zeret, she should not immerse there l’chatchila, lest she not immerse properly (Beit Yosef in siman 198 in the name of a responsum of Rashba, and so wrote Rashbatz). Nevertheless, if there is no other mikveh and it is impossible to fix it — even if she needs to lie flat on her face because the waters are not deep — if her body is covered in this manner at one time, she immerses there (responsum of Rashba siman 4418). See above siman 198.}
