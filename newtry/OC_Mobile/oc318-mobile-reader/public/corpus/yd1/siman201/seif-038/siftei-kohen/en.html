There is no concern, etc. Although none of them has a single opening the size of the mouth of a wineskin, since they have many openings and do not hold water, they are not considered receptacles such that the water should be deemed drawn. This is the wording of the Ateret Zahav. This explanation was necessary even to permit bringing water through them into the mikveh initially, because in such a case the many openings are considered equivalent to an opening the size of the mouth of a wineskin, as stated above in seif 9. However, merely so that they should not invalidate the mikveh, an opening of any size suffices, as stated below in seif 40.
