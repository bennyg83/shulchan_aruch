A pipe that pours into the mikveh, and a mortar is placed at its side: there is doubt [whether the water is] from the pipe to the mikveh or from the mortar to the mikveh — this is invalid, since the invalidity is evident. But if in the mikveh most of it is kosher water, this is kosher, for this is a safek of drawn water, since there is there a fixed kosher mikveh.
