An impure person who descended to immerse — there is doubt whether he immersed or did not immerse; and even if he immersed, there is doubt whether there are 40 se’ah in it or there are not. Two mikva’os: one has 40 se’ah and one does not; he immersed in one of them and it is not known in which of them — he is impure, for the impure person remains with his status until it is known that he immersed properly. And likewise, a mikveh that was measured and found lacking: whether the mikveh was in the public domain or in the private domain, all purities that were done upon it retroactively are impure, until the time when it was measured and was complete is known (and see above se’if 65).
