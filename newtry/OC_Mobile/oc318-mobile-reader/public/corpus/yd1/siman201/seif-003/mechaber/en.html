The 40 se’ah that they said must be such that they are not drawn; for if they are drawn, they are invalid. {Rama: And if the entire mikveh is drawn [water], it is invalid by Torah law, and a safek is treated stringently. But if most of the mikveh is kosher and the minority is drawn [water], it is only d’rabbanan, and a safek is treated leniently (Tur in the name of Rosh and Rashi, and many poskim).}
