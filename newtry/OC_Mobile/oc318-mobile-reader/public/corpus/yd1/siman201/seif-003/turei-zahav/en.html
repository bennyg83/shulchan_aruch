And if the drippings exceeded the flowing waters, etc. — Terumat HaDeshen siman 294 wrote: I heard challengers — what is different about drippings that invalidate flowing waters in their creation and remove them from the category of flowing, and drawn waters that invalidate a mikveh entirely, yet if there was already a mikveh of 40 se'ah and drawn water fell in, even a thousand se'ah at once, they do not invalidate it, as in this siman seif 15 — in a mikveh and spring even in any amount; and he answered there in Terumat HaDeshen: Rosh wrote the reason drawn water does not invalidate a mikveh is as if they were planted inside and are like connected and valid — thus the difficulty is resolved, for the essence of invalidity of drawn water is that they are detached by a person, therefore planting helps and they are considered connected; but invalidity of drippings that do not purify in flowing is not because they are detached, for a complete mikveh of drippings is valid and nevertheless does not purify through flowing — therefore planting does not help when planted in flowing waters, which only makes detached into connected; and Maharach answered the Terumat HaDeshen difficulty that drawn water is kosher by Torah law as explained below in this siman, unlike invalid drippings which is Torah law. I wonder at these answers, for we explicitly taught in chapter 5 of Mikvaot: a spring that was drawn like a bucket and they drew it — behold it is as it was — and this refers to drippings the spring increased in it, as Beit Yosef in Ran here; also Ra'avad we bring below — thus we see even for drippings the validity of the spring helps. I also wonder what difference between this and that whether detached becomes connected or connected becomes like it; it appears the original difficulty does not exist, for Beit Yosef in Ra'avad s.v. and in what Maharik wanted from mishnah of bucket implies even if drippings exceeded flowing it is valid — against what we say on the Euphrates lest drippings exceed flowing, which implies increase of drippings invalidates, yet the Euphrates is a drawn spring; and he answered the Euphrates when it reaches Babylonia dries in summer and in rainy season rains exceed and continue it — thus it is as if the spring stood and does not flow, as in the end of mishnah of bucket that when drippings increased it does not purify in flowing; from this Ra'avad judged all rivers that diminish in summer and in rainy season they draw and make channels onward — there they do not purify in flowing; thus there is no Terumat HaDeshen difficulty, for truly if originally there was flowing and now drippings increased there, likewise it helps since it is a drawn spring; but he still challenged from seif 8 permitting outside it because little water purifies much invalid, as I wrote there in Rosh's name, and from seif 10 spring drawn to a pool with drippings considered like spring — why not say here too flowing purifies drippings? After investigation it appears to answer here invalid because we are concerned snowmelt and rains descend from a high place at the riverbank and there is no connection, for we hold a narrow strip is not connection — we are concerned she may immerse on that slope where there is no connection for flowing drawn below from the spring and she immerses in invalid flowing; proof from Mordechai Laws of Niddah: Ravyah challenged how immerse in a river — narrow strip on its descent is not connection; say specifically above one may not immerse, etc. — thus here too concern above is all rainwater and connection below does not help one who immerses above.
