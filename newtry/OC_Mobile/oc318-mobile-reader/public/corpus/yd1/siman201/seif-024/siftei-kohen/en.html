"They do not invalidate," etc. — and Rambam, chapter 7, wrote: beer is among those that invalidate and do not combine; and Beit Yosef brings it.
"Thirty-nine se'ah." — and the same law: lacking a kortov they do not complete; only he informs us that even lacking a se'ah they do not invalidate.
"But in drawn water," etc. — and Rambam's view is that in drawn water, only up to the majority is valid; and Beit Yosef brought Rashbatz who wrote so in the name of Ra'avad; and he wrote: even though the acharonim agreed to validate — nevertheless one should not put his head into the dispute — end of his words.
