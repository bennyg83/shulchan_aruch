Brine-water and boiled-vegetable water, and temed that has not fermented, and likewise dye-water disqualify the mikveh with 3 logim. But all other beverages, and fruit-juice, and fish brine, and temed once it fermented, do not disqualify a mikveh that is lacking with 3 logim, and they also do not complete it to validate it: for if it had 39 se’ah and 1 se’ah of these fell into it, they do not complete it. But if it has 40 se’ah, and 1 se’ah of these fell into it and he removed from them another se’ah, it is kosher — even if he did so 19 times. But with drawn waters, where a se’ah fell into 40 se’ah of kosher [water], and he removed from them another se’ah, and a se’ah of kosher water fell into it — even if he did so forever, it is kosher.
