There is one who forbids casting a pot full of hot water into the mikveh to warm it, and likewise to fill a mikveh with hot water and connect it to a river with the tube of a leather flask. {Rama: And there are those who are lenient and permit casting hot water into the mikveh in order to warm it (Hagahos Mordechai to Hilchos Niddah in the name of Ravyah and Riva). Nevertheless, one should be stringent, unless in a place where they practiced leniency, then one should not protest them (Binyamin Ze’ev). And with the hot springs of Tiberias, it is permitted according to all (Mordechai at the end of Shevuos). And after immersion in kosher mikveh waters, she is permitted to enter a bathhouse so that she warm herself (Mordechai), but to return and wash afterward, some forbid (Mordechai in the name of Rashbam), and so is the practice.}
