And if he transferred three log, etc. — this is Tur and Rosh per Tosefta Beit Yosef brought: Rabbi Yosi says: if he poured from his hands and feet three log into a mikveh it is invalid; if he carried it with the ground it is valid; legion passing place to place poured three log — valid; moreover even if they made a mikveh initially thus it is valid — meaning l'chatchila one may make the entire mikveh this way. Difficulty on that Tosefta for Rabbi Yosi says pouring invalidates and implies no dispute; Rosh explained the end regarding legion and remainder deals with animals' feet only; per this Rosh and Tur distinguished man from animal and it is reasonable: Rabbi Yosi's pouring that invalidates is from water in the ground that are not drawn, and although his hands and feet are not utensils to invalidate as drawn, and likewise his garment he drew from them — nevertheless invalid since it exits from under a person's hand we do not care about utensil, as I wrote before; therefore Tur wrote he transferred three log on the ground — valid; but splashed from their feet without carrying ground — not valid except for animal; Ra'avad in Baalei Nefesh Beit Yosef brought this view that whatever is by a person invalidates even without utensil; he rejected it from mishnah of pipe and wrote always what was not received in a utensil even from his hands to mikveh one immerses up to half; Rabbi Yosi invalidating pouring in hands and feet deals when he intended to pour to mikveh; end of legion even if they initially made mikveh valid — Rosh and Tur hold pouring invalidates only when three log at once, not each log separately; Ra'avad holds each log invalidates and one may not make mikveh initially this way; Shulchan Aruch ruled like Rosh and Tur; see there at length.
