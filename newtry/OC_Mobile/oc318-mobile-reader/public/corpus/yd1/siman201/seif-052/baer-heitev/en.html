In its valid state. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that the commentators were uncertain whether drawn water remains valid after its contact with a valid mikveh, or whether once the opening is sealed it returns to its original invalidity. It is proper to be stringent initially. The Bach writes that this validation does not include joining a deficient mikveh to a complete one. Once the opening is sealed, the deficient mikveh stands before us lacking its measure, so its former connection to a complete mikveh cannot help it since it is now deficient. Maharshal agrees, and this is also implied by Responsa Rosh. The Shach concludes that these matters are obvious.
To a river. The same applies beside a complete mikveh where seepage is visible. If, however, two or three adjacent pits are dug and all are deficient, with none containing forty se'ah, visible seepage from one to another does not join them. This is the Shach.
