That which comes. This is Mishnah 7 of chapter 6, according to Rosh's explanation there and in his rulings, siman 15. He lists drawn water, invalid water, and deficient water. Rash, however, writes only the latter two, as stated in seif 53. Therefore Mechaber writes here "invalid" in the place of "drawn," because regarding invalid water Rash agrees, as Rama notes there. Hashakah is effective for drawn water, as shown by Mishnah 2 of chapter 3, Mishnah 3 and Mishnah 8 of chapter 6. A deficient mikveh adjoining a complete one is treated in Mishnah 1 and 5 there. Two deficient mikvaot are treated in Mishnah 3 there. The opening must be the size of the mouth of a waterskin, as in those Mishnayot, and all the more so where both are deficient.
