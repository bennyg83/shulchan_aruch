One who brings, and so forth. Mishnah 7 of chapter 6, according to the Rosh. He lists drawn water, invalid water, or deficient water. The Rash gives only the final two, so the Mechaber says 'invalid' where even the Rash agrees. Drawn water can be repaired by contact with a valid mikvah, as shown by several Mishnayot in chapters 3 and 6. A deficient mikvah beside a complete one is also validated, as are two deficient mikvaot, provided there is an opening the size of a wineskin's mouth.
A stream, and so forth. The Rash at the end of chapter 5 of Taharot, and Tosafot in Chagigah 21b, Yevamot 15a, and Gittin 16a, unlike Rabbeinu Tam.
Afterward, and so forth. Mishnayot 8 and 3 of chapter 6.
It is permitted to dig, and so forth. Chagigah 22a. Separate pits, each lacking forty se'ah, do not combine merely through a basket or lattice. Where a complete mikvah surrounds the vessels, however, immersion is effective. This differs from a mikvah beside a river, since the river itself is complete.
The same applies if he divided, and so forth. From the same Gemara.
