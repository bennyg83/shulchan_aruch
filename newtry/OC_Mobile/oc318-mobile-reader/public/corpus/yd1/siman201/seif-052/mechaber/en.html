One who comes to mix an invalid mikveh or one that is lacking with a kosher mikveh to validate it, or when both are lacking and he comes to mix them to validate them, needs the hole between them to be as wide as the tube of a leather flask (and the flow of the water must be as wide as the hole) (Rivash siman 232 in the name of one of the commentators; and so wrote the Beit Yosef). And after the invalid mixed with the kosher even for a moment, it remains valid forever, even if the hole was afterward sealed. Anything that stands in the measure of the tube of a leather flask diminishes it, even something whose creation is from water. If there is a safek whether the hole is as wide as the tube of a leather flask or not, it is invalid. If there are many thin holes, they combine to [the measure of] the tube of a leather flask if the first mikveh is complete and the second is lacking. {Rama: And it is permitted to dig a mikveh alongside a river and immerse in it even though it does not have 40 se’ah, for the ground is porous and seeps through; and since it is close to the river and we see the seepages between them, that the waters come from the river through the seepage, meaning through thin holes in the ground, it is a connection (Mordechai, Hilchos Niddah, in the name of Ravyah).} But if both are lacking, the thin holes do not combine to [the measure of] the tube of a leather flask. (And the same is true if one divided a mikveh with a basket or a sack: if there is not the measure of a mikveh on one side, it is invalid.)
