A mikveh into which drawn water fell and it became invalid, and afterward one increased upon it kosher waters until the kosher [waters] were found to be 40 se’ah — it remains in its disqualification until all the waters that were in it go out and the drawn [waters] diminish to less than 3 logim. And likewise, if one made a mikveh that contains 40 se’ah of kosher water, and they mixed this invalid mikveh with it, these purify those and those purify these. (And the same is true of a spring of any amount that one drew: these drawn [waters] became purified, as was explained above.)
