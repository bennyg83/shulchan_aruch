To bring water through such a perforated vessel. It implies specifically such that is perforated any amount; but if like spout of skin-bottle one may bring even l'chatchila. Difficulty: above seif wrote whatever is by person invalidates even if not utensil, and as we wrote per Tur above seif 15; therefore say here it deals where not poured from this vessel by person holding it but places it near mikveh and water comes from vessel to mikveh and he draws water over it to mikveh — permitted by law even with any hole in bottom; nevertheless l'chatchila do not do so unless pierced in bottom like spout of skin-bottle. Difficulty: nearby he wrote one who wants to draw from mikveh to clean it pierces vessel in bottom — what helps the hole since nevertheless by person to mikveh and invalidates even without utensil; for this Ramban responsum 231 I brought above seif 15 wrote fixing bucket hole when cleaning mikveh does not help; I saw no commentator noted this yet all said make this bucket fix; in Ramban responsum end not copied fully after this difficulty nothing written — transcriber did not find more; appears answer: there he pierces bottom to draw out and water does not enter mikveh through hole but through top when he tilts — not "by person" invalidating; or after drawing he returns water through top not through hole; and hole only so water in bucket not drawn when tilted for cleaning; see there at length.
And according to this it is permitted even if a person draws with a perforated vessel like a skin-bottle and pours the water into the mikveh; and the Tur appropriately wrote that one should not bring water in such a vessel — implying that perforated like a skin-bottle is permitted even if a person does so through drawing.
But there is still a difficulty: even if it were like the spout of a skin-bottle and not considered drawn water even though done by a person's action — and as we explained — nevertheless: how can the entire mikveh l'chatchila be made through this vessel, since it is in any case not being made through rainwater? And the Raavad raised this difficulty above in seif 15 regarding the law of transferring three lugim, etc. — and on account of this the Raavad concluded there that one may only permit up to half [the mikveh this way], and from half onward it must be drawn along the ground. And here we are saying one may make the entire mikveh this way! Therefore one must say here too that it deals with bringing them via flowing along the ground [hamshakhah] to the mikveh; and the law of hamshakhah is explained in seif 46 — upon what it depends. And even though in hamshakhah drawn water invalidates by a majority — and here the entire mikveh is made this way — there it is different, since one is drawing that which is already drawn water; but here there is no drawing, since it has not been disconnected from the [natural] source, only requiring a correction so as not to be invalid since it is not coming through rainwater — in this case hamshakhah helps even if the entire [mikveh] is done this way. As I stated from the Tosefta I cited in the name of Raavad in seif 15 — that the entire mikveh may be made valid by flowing along the ground, as long as there is no invalidation of drawn water. And so too here: it is permitted since it is not a vessel — the vessel being perforated like a skin-bottle — and it has not been disconnected from the spring; and it is valid even with hamshakhah for the entire mikveh. And b'diavad, even if it was not perforated that much but at least some amount such that the water drips through there — and at minimum it must be the size of a lens [adashah], as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Maharik.
