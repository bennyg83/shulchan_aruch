That they exit. Immediately when they passed through a utensil they become drawn; Bach wrote: not when vessel is whole without hole and spring sprays into vessel and when full exits from mouth — not called drawn; nor hole in side spraying outside — called drawn because utensil name not canceled by side hole since it receives from hole downward; but hole in bottom shefoferet-size then certainly utensil name canceled and permitted to immerse even inside vessel — mikveh and spring equal in this. (Taz: all this when vessel made intentionally; unintentionally does not invalidate exiting water as drawn, like vessels under pipe seif 41 — see there).
