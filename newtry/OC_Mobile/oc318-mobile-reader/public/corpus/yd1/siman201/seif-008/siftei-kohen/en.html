A spring,. etc. ; meaning, if a spring sprays into the vessel (even if detached) and goes out from it, they are invalid to immerse in them, for what is inside the vessel is obviously invalid, and what is outside is also invalid, for once they passed through a vessel they became drawn water. And Bach wrote, seif 13: this is not speaking when the vessel is whole without any hole and the spring sprays into the vessel and when the vessel is filled they afterward go out from the mouth of the vessel outside ; this is not speaking; rather, there is a hole in the side of the vessel and from it they spray outside ; they are called drawn water, for the name of vessel was not nullified because of the hole in its side, since it receives water from the hole and below; but a hole in the base of the vessel like the opening of a wineskin ; then certainly the name of vessel was nullified and it is permitted to immerse even inside the vessel, as explained regarding a mikveh, that in this matter spring and mikveh are equal, end of his words; and it is straightforward.
And if the spring,. etc. ; meaning, most of the spring passes into the vessel and a bit passes and sprays at the lip of the vessel; since a few days of the spring did not pass through the vessel at all, they raise the abundant water and purify the drawn water, and even though they return and mix, all have the law of a spring (and see Beit Yosef what he wrote for the view of Rashba, and R' Yosef Tayitzak already challenged him, and Bach ; however, what Bach wrote on Beit Yosef and upheld his words from the language of Rashba's responsum, etc., and one immerses on its back and outside, as we learned, etc., and it is a great wonder, etc., that an error escaped him that the word "and outside" attaches to "one immerses," and it is like Beit Yosef's view, not like Bach who understood that "outside" attaches above, and therefore he strained needlessly to emend Rashba's words ; this is clear and there is no need to extend here in these words, since for the law the Mechaber himself ruled that spraying on the lip of the vessel is required, and it is simple) and Bach wrote: however, what Rashba permits to immerse inside the vessel when there are forty se'ah between them is a novel law and does not imply so from the poskim; rather inside the vessel is always invalid unless pierced in its base, even if there are forty se'ah between the spring and the vessel; and also from the language of Rosh himself it appears he did not decide this law, if so. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Mordechai in the name of R' Yoel and in the name of Rokeach that drawn water that was drawn through a channel in the ground to a large barrel holding more than forty se'ah, and the barrel was sunk in the ground ; it is forbidden to immerse inside the barrel, etc., even though the barrel-water, which is drawn, is connected to the channel ; if so; and it is straightforward that if there is in the base of the barrel a hole like the opening of a wineskin, it is valid.
