It is not invalidated. — because it does not have the substance of the matter that changes its appearance; and likewise dye water contains nothing except steeping of herbs; and the change of appearance has no minimum measure — rather even if its appearance changed from one log, it is invalid — end of Ra'avad, and Beit Yosef brings it; and so Taz.
"But," etc., "wine," etc. — even not as drawn water, such as wine drawn into it from the press and lees from the olive press not through vessels — Ra'avad — see there.
"In the quantity it was." — even though it did not change to the appearance of wine, it is invalid since it changed from the appearance of water that was at first — Ra'avad.
