Dye-waters have the law of water to disqualify a mikveh that is lacking with 3 logim, even though their appearance is different from the appearance of water. But a complete mikveh, even though dye-water fell into it and changed its appearance, is not disqualified. And likewise, if he rinsed vessels in it and its appearance changed, or if he soaked spices or foods in it and its appearance changed, it is not disqualified (Ra’avad and Rashba). But if wine or mohal fell into it (meaning: the waters that come out of olives at the start of pressing the olives, and they contain traces of oil) and changed its appearance from what it was, it is disqualified. What should he do? If it is lacking, he should wait until rains descend and it becomes full, and its appearance returns to the appearance of water. And if it has 40 se’ah, so that it is no longer disqualified by drawing, he should fill by carrying [water] on the shoulder and place into it until its appearance returns to the appearance of water.
