Spring. To purify whether in flowing or ashboren and for vessels in shefoferet; Taz wrote must say spring was drawn initially but now added drawing to pool; but if spring stood initially and drew to pool or rainwater increased on it — although drawing helps purify drawn to immerse therein, for flowing it does not help and purifies only in ashboren since it was not flowing initially — this is clear to me — end of his words.
