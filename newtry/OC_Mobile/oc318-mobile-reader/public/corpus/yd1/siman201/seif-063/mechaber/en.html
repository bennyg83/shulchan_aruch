If he immersed in it a thick garment, such that the water is absorbed into it: as long as the garment touches the mikveh it is kosher, even though 3 logim flowed from it into the mikveh. If he lifted it from it, it is invalid, for it becomes drawn from the waters that descended from the garment into it. If he immerses in it a pot or other vessels, he lowers them into it by way of their mouths, so that the waters do not splash when he inserts them into it and it is found that it is lacking; and he lifts it up by way of its bottom, so that no water remains in it and [thus] the mikveh will be diminished. {Rama: All the waters that are in the vessel, even though the waters in the vessel will be drawn, if they will afterward fall into the mikveh they will disqualify it, since there did not remain in it the measure of a mikveh (from the language of Rambam).}
