Two mikva’os, neither of which has 40 se’ah, and into this one a log and a half fell, and into that one a log and a half fell, and the two mikva’os mixed together — these are kosher, because the name of disqualification was not called upon either one of them. But a mikveh that does not have 40 se’ah, into which 3 logim of drawn water fell, and afterward it was divided into two, and one increased kosher waters upon each one of them — these are invalid.
