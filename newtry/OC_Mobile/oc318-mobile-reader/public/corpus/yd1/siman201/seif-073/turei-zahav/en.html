He shakes with his hand. So in mishnah end chapter 7 of Mikvaot: he brings and takes with water, etc. Difficulty: we say seif 48 one should not hold it in his hand; for Rosh who distinguishes drawing from rainwater versus spring or mikveh as I wrote seif 49 it is fine here; but for Rashba who forbids there as I wrote in name of those who do not distinguish — difficult, and would he argue on mishnah here? It appears even Rashba who forbids there only if he draws with something that receives tumah and where there was no water initially except through drawing he now draws and works there; unlike here he places hands inside spring or mikveh itself and by shaking water go outside their place — not drawing through something that receives tumah; therefore what is written afterward "only that he not uproot the wave" is fine; Rosh wrote if he uprooted he needs 40 se'ah — difficult, for even 40 se'ah does not help since they come through something that receives tumah; per what I wrote it is fine; Perishah wrote if uprooted one could say even if 40 se'ah they are not purified since they come through something that receives tumah; although above permitted drawing mikveh because connected to mikveh — end of his words; his words are wondrous for Rosh explicitly wrote needs 40 implying 40 helps; per my words all is resolved in my words.
