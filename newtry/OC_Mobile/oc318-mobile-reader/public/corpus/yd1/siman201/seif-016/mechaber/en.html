The sponge that had absorbed 3 logim, and when it fell into a mikveh the absorbed waters mixed with the waters of the mikveh; and likewise, a bucket whose mouth is narrow, and it contains 3 logim of drawn water, and it fell into a mikveh, and not all the water inside it went out, rather it mixed with the waters of the mikveh — it does not disqualify it, for they said only [in the case of] 3 logim that fell and all of them mixed with the waters of the mikveh.
