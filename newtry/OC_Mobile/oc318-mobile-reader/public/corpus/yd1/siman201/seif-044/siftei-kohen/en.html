"Until they are half-and-half." — and all the more so a majority of drawn water that invalidates through drawing. And Bach wrote, seif 26, in the name of Ra'avad: this is specifically when he drew intentionally; but unintentionally, even entirely through drawing, is valid — see there; and see end of seif 15.
[The words that do not rule out.] And it is not the most common of them, but rather on the ground that should be swallowed as a result of the Bible and as such
"If everything is found invalid." — since he filled on his shoulders directly into the mikveh and did not draw first — even so, if he afterward opened the pipe, etc., it is valid.
"But," etc. — meaning, he first drew drawn water to the mikveh and they are not twenty se'ah, and afterward he brought upon them a majority of valid water — meaning rainwater fell upon them more than twenty se'ah — it does not help even if he afterward opened the pipe and all were drawn to one place, for we do not say drawn water that was drawn entirely is valid except when there was initially a majority of valid water.
