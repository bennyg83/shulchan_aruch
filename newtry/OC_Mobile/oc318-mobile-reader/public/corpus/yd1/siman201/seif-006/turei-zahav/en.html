Therefore a wave that detached, etc. — meaning it purifies in flowing like a spring; and if so, since it was like a spring and nevertheless we need 40 se'ah — it is difficult against Rambam and Ra'avad Tur brought from them that a spring purifies in any amount even for a person; one could say nevertheless per their view the entire body of a person must be covered in that spring as Beit Yosef wrote there, and this measure of 40 se'ah is the measure one can be covered even the largest body, as in gemara "he washed all his flesh in water" — water in which his entire body rises — and how much? 40 se'ah; therefore the tanna stated here 40 se'ah which is a fixed matter.
