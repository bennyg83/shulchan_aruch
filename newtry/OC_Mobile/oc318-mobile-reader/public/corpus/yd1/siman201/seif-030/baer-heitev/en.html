The snow. This applies only if it was placed in the mikveh while still snow. If it melted into water before being placed there, it is like ordinary water and invalidates the mikveh. This is obvious, as the Shach writes.
Even. The Shach disagrees and brings several proofs that immersion in snow is ineffective even when it contains the volume of forty se'ah. Only immersing one's hands in snow before eating is permitted when no water is available, because netilas yadayim is rabbinic. See there.
He shall compress it. This means that it should be packed tightly and become firm, so that its volume will not be deficient when it melts, for as water it will not retain its present bulk.
