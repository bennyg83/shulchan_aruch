The plasterer who forgot a pot in the mikveh and it filled with water — even though there did not remain in the mikveh except a little, and the pot has the majority of the mikveh — he should break the pot in its place, and it is found that the entire mikveh is kosher. And likewise, one who arranges the jugs inside the mikveh in order to strengthen them, and they filled with water: even though the mikveh swallowed its waters and no water remained there at all except the waters inside the jugs, he should break the jugs, and the waters gathered from them are a kosher mikveh.
