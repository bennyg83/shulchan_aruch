"And if he tilted it on its side." The wording of R' Shimshon: with its mouth upward — since the water floats on its back, there is connection in any amount; but when tilted on its side it does not connect above, only from the side; therefore it requires the opening of a wineskin — end of his words; meaning: it requires that the vessel be immersed inside the mikveh and the mikveh water be on top of the vessel; if so, when its mouth is upward, then the water inside the vessel touching the mikveh connects to the mikveh and has the law of mikveh and covers the vessel — unlike when connecting from the side, when water on top of vessels is not considered mikveh water, only from the side what equals mikveh water is considered mikveh water. So explained the Gaon, may his light shine; and Taz explained in another manner and is ambiguous — see there; and see above, siman 202, seif 3.
