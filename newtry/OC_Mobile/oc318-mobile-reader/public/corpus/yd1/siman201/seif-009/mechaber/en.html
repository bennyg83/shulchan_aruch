An impure vessel into which one placed other vessels, and immersed everything — their immersion has taken effect, even though the mouth of the vessel is very narrow, since the waters enter into it; and since immersion took effect for the large vessel, immersion took effect for the vessels within it. But if he tilted it on its side and immersed it, their immersion has not taken effect until its mouth is as wide as the tube of a leather flask. And likewise, if the vessel was pure and one placed impure vessels into it and immersed them, their immersion has not taken effect until its mouth is as wide as the tube of a leather flask. {Rama: And it is permitted to immerse vessels in a basket or in a sack, for since it does not hold water, it is preferable even more than [a vessel] with a hole as wide as the tube of a leather flask (Beit Yosef in the name of the Mishnah, and Rashi’s explanation, perek 6 of Mikvaos, and Rambam perek 6).}
