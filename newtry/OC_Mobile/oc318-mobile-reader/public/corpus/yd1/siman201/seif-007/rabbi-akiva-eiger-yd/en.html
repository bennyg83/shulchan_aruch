(Shulchan Aruch, seif 7) "the opening that purifies it." — this is for an earthenware vessel, whose measure is like removing an olive, which is less than the opening of a wineskin; but for a wooden barrel, whose measure of purification is like removing a pomegranate, it does not require so much, and it helps with like the opening of a wineskin, as Beit Yosef wrote explicitly; and therefore Rema wrote: and some say that it requires a hole like the opening of a wineskin, meaning that also for earthenware we require so, and this suffices for wood too. But it is difficult to me: although it helps that they not be drawn, nevertheless, since it is still a vessel that receives tumah, it is no better than immersing on the back of a wooden vessel, for immersion did not take effect according to Ra'avad, as he ruled, siman 198 (131) — and requires further study.
