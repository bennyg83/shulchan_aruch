And they established in ground. See Merkavat HaMishnah chapter 6 law 4 — asked regarding vessel attached to ground whether piercing afterward helps; answered plainly helps in any case whether detached then attached considered detached; since permitted to pierce while in ground, permitted afterward to seal — at piercing canceled utensil and sealing is like pipe established and hollowed — simple; so Be'er Yaakov likewise permitted establish in ground whole then pierce while fixed shefoferet at most and seal afterward valid like establish and hollow; but since Perishah implies otherwise, l'chatchila or b'dieved without loss remove and pierce detached; where effort or loss permit; see R' Akiva Eiger 39 — piercing while fixed in ground helps plainly; but permitting seal afterward not his view — already acquired utensil name detached, connection to ground did not lose name, piercing makes invalid utensil, sealing returns utensil; only if attached while whole at time of attachment connection to ground did not help cancel name even if pierce and seal returns connected utensil; if pierced before attachment — at attachment no utensil name, canceled by attachment, sealing afterward not connected utensil like establish and hollow; nevertheless forbidden to immerse therein — only drawn through such vessel valid (as I wrote above seif 6 s.v. inside vessel); Be'er Yaakov: large vessel hole in rim fixed in house sealed with wood — moving requires new hole — returns detached utensil name — one erred; see there.
