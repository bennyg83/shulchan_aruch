He should not jump into it, etc. See above siman 198 seif katan 29. He should not immerse in it twice, etc. — lest the first time he did not immerse properly, relying on the second time, and the second time perhaps the water was lacking from the first time.
Even though his feet are in it.. and it is not similar to a garment in the next seif, for as long as it touches him it is also considered mikveh-water, for a garment is thick and absorbs much water, unlike a person whose water is not absorbed in the essence of his limbs, only on the surface of his body; and touching in order to touch again is not connection ; Rivash.
