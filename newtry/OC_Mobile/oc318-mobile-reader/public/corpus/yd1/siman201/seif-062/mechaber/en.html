A mikveh that has exactly 40 se’ah: a person who immerses in it should not jump into it, lest the waters be diminished by his jumping into it; and one should not immerse in it 2 times one after another. If 2 immersed in it one after another, even though the feet of the first touch the waters [around] the second, the second remains in his tumah, for the waters were diminished from 40 se’ah.
