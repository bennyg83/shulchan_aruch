A mikveh that is established to have its waters diminishing and to stand at less than 40 se’ah (and she immersed in it) — she must return and immerse, as long as we do not certainly know that at the time she immersed there were 40 se’ah in it. But if it is not established that its waters diminish so much that they would stand at less than 40 se’ah — even though sometimes its waters rise and sometimes diminish — she does not need to return and immerse. Nevertheless, it is proper to check before immersion whether there are 40 se’ah in it (and see below se’if 71).
