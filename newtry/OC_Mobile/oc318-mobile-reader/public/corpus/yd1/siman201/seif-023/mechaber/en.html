The 3 logim disqualify only if they are of water and their appearance is the appearance of water. Therefore, 3 logim of water into which wine fell, and their appearance is the appearance of wine — if they fell into the mikveh, they do not disqualify it. And likewise, 3 logim that were lacking any amount, into which a little milk fell and they completed it to 3 logim, and they fell into a mikveh that is lacking — they do not disqualify it.
