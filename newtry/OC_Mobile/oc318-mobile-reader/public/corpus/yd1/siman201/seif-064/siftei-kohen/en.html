Through their bottoms, etc. The water that enters them is not invalidated as drawn water when it falls into the mikveh. While the vessel is still in the mikveh, the water has not been drawn; and when one raises it and it overturns through its bottom, the water inside is no longer called drawn water, because it is not retained within the vessel. This is written in Tosafot Yom Tov. Ateret Zahav confused the matters here and corrupted the text because he had an erroneous version before him. See there.
