A safek of drawn waters is pure. How so? A mikveh about which there is doubt whether drawn waters fell into it or did not fall; and even if he certainly knows that they fell, [but] there is doubt whether they contain 3 logim or do not contain; and even if he certainly knows that they contain 3 logim, [but] there is doubt whether there were in the mikveh into which they fell 40 se’ah or there were not — this is kosher.
