Not that they are kosher to immerse therein as they are, for each lacks 40 se'ah since they were separated afterward; rather drawn water was purified and became through this hashakah like rainwater since from 40 se'ah they received hashakah; if afterward rainwater fills on drawn until 40 se'ah they are kosher for immersion; also teaches the valid portion was not invalidated by drawn descending into them; therefore he said all three were validated that they have an aspect of validity; unlike end where drawn remains invalid and rainwater hashakah on it does not help; and so Rivash Beit Yosef brought.
