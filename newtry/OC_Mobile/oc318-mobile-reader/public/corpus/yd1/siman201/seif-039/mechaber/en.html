This that they said that anything not made to receive water does not disqualify the mikveh — they said only when the waters fell from within it into the mikveh on their own; but if a person put them into the mikveh, it disqualifies, for anything done through a person — even if he sprinkled with his hands or with his feet, and even if he passed through water and they were splashed by his feet on their own into the mikveh — they disqualified it. In what case is this said? When they were splashed by his feet; but if he was riding upon an animal and waters were splashed by the feet of the animal, they did not disqualify it, even though through the animal upon which he is riding they were splashed — this is not like sprinkling with his feet (and it was already explained that there are those who disagree; see above se’if 15).
