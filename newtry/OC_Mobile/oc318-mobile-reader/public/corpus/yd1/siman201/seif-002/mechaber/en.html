Spring-water purifies even when flowing (meaning: “flowing” — they are drawn and go and are not gathered). Rainwater does not purify except in a gathered pool (meaning: a deep place in which the waters gather, called an “ashboren”) (but through flowing, they are invalid by Torah law (Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari”k, shoresh 115, and Terumas HaDeshen siman 224), if they are alone without a spring). If the flowing waters are from a spring and mix with dripping waters that are rainwater, everything is like a spring for every matter. But if the drippings exceeded the flowing, and likewise if rainwater exceeded the river-water, they do not purify when flowing, only in a gathered pool. Therefore, one needs to place around a “mappatz” (meaning: like a mat) and the like in that mixed river, until the waters gather, and immerse in them. {Rama: And so it is proper to rule and be stringent (Mahari”k and Terumas HaDeshen there; and other later authorities wrote that one should be stringent like the method of Rambam and Rosh and those who support them). But there are those who permit immersing in rivers all year, even during the rains and the melting of snows, and the drippings exceeded the flowing, because the main growth of the river is from its source-place (Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Tam; and Beit Yosef in the name of Rashi and Sefer HaTerumah and Sefer Mitzvos Gadol). And so they practiced in most places where there is no mikveh, and one should not protest those who practice leniency, for they have upon whom to rely (Mahari”k and Terumas HaDeshen there; and Mahari”v siman 70; and Be’er Sheva siman 154). Nevertheless, one should be careful not to immerse in a river that is formed entirely by rainwater, and when there is no rain it stops entirely — even though other rivers pour into it during the rains and it is formed through them — nevertheless, since it stops entirely when there is no rain, it is forbidden to immerse in it while it is flowing until the waters in it gather (there in Mahari”k). But a river that does not stop, even though during the rains it widens and spreads over all its banks, it is permitted to immerse in it in every place (Beit Yosef according to Mordechai and Ashiri and Tosafos), according to the reasoning of the lenient ones and according to the practice.}
