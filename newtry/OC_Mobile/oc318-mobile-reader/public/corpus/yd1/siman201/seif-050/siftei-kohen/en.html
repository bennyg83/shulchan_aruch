"Of rainwater." — to exclude a spring, as below.
"And all this is specifically," etc. — apparently he means that if it comes from a spring, even though the spring was stopped, the law of a spring is upon it to purify in flowing waters, as in Maharik; for if he comes to exclude whether it is still a spring, it is obvious; and so the language implies "it comes from a spring." But in s.k. 30 I challenged Maharik's words, for when the spring was stopped there is no longer a spring upon it; and therefore one can say here it deals that it still comes from a spring; and lest you say, since it is in a mikveh in a gathered place we should fear its streaming — he informs us.
