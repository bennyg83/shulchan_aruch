A mikveh of rainwater whose one wall was breached and the waters go out through the crack: if 40 se’ah will remain in it after some of them go out — until the crack — it is kosher; and if not, it is invalid, since it becomes flowing, and a mikveh does not purify when flowing. {Rama: And there are those who are stringent even if 40 se’ah will remain until the crack, and one should be concerned for their words l’chatchila to seal the crack (Tur in the name of “some say,” and Rashi; and Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai). And all this is specifically with a mikveh that does not come from a spring; but if it comes from a spring, there is no concern for its flowing (Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari”k, shoresh 156). And the water’s going out is called “flowing” only when they do not return to the mikveh; but when a little goes out and returns there, it is not called flowing (Rivash siman 292).} And if he comes to seal the crack so that 40 se’ah will remain in it, he should not seal it with his hand, nor with anything that receives tumah, and there is one who permits sealing with anything that receives tumah.
