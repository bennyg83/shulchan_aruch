One who placed vessels under the pipe to receive its waters, so that they will fall from them into the mikveh: if he placed them at a time when thick clouds were gathered, and before the clouds dispersed rains descended and they filled up, they are considered intentional and are drawn [waters]. But if the clouds were not gathered at the time he placed them, and afterward they gathered and they filled up; or even if he placed them at the time of cloud-gathering, and the clouds dispersed and they remained there until the clouds gathered again and rains descended and they filled up — they are not considered intentional and they do not disqualify. Provided that he breaks the vessel or turns it over in a way that he will not lift it from the ground; for if he lifts it from the ground with the water, they are considered drawn and disqualify the mikveh.
