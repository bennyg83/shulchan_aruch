The laws of a mikveh and its waters; it contains 75 se’ifim.
A woman does not ascend from her tumah by washing in a bathhouse; and even if all the waters of the world rose upon her, she is still in her tumah, and [one is] liable for her to kareis, until she immerses her entire body at one time in the waters of a mikveh or spring that has 40 se’ah. And its measure is: one amah by one amah, by a height of 3 amos, squared, with an amah that is 6 tefachim and a half-finger. And if it is wider and not as high, it is kosher if she can cover her entire body in it at one time. And it must add up in calculation to 44,118 fingers in the thumb-measure and another half-finger (these are my words in Sefer Ba’al HaBayis). And the trench in which the waters are must be larger than this measure, so that when the immersing person enters and the waters rise, 40 se’ah will remain there (Tosafos, perek Arvei Pesachim).
