(Likkut) Or a spring, etc. See Toras HaBayis in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, who brings proof from Mishnah 6 of chapter 5 concerning a wave, etc. This has already been answered: the requirement of 40 se'ah is so that a person can be fully covered by the water. See Taz, seif katan 6. The novelty is that the person's entire body must be covered at one time within the wave. On the contrary, that Mishnah proves the opposite, that a spring purifies while flowing even for a person, and the same reasoning applies that it purifies with any quantity. As the Rivash writes, the reason a spring purifies while flowing whereas a mikveh does not is that a descending slope does not join the waters together, whereas a spring purifies with any quantity. The words of the Shach there, seif katan 20, are not clear. He further brings proof from Yoma 78a, where it states, 'when it reaches the entrance of Beit David,' etc. This is difficult to understand: could one not immerse in it beforehand, since the entire spring is connected as one? Rather, the point is that there must be enough water to cover the person. End.
