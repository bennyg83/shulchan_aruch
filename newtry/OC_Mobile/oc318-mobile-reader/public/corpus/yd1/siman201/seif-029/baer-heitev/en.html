Drawn water. It is considered as though the entire mixture were wine and therefore it is no longer invalidated by three log of water. When one later completes the required amount, Ra'avad holds that the wine that fell in initially also joins to complete the measure. Beit Yosef writes that the words of Rash and Rambam imply that it does not join, and this also appears to be Rama's view. Shach.
