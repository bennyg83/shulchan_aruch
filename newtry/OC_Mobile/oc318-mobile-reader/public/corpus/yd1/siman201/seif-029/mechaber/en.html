If 3 logim of wine fell into it, it is as if they did not fall (and it is permitted to immerse) — whether in the place of the wine or in the place of the water. If it was drawn [water] and one “kissed” it: if he “kissed” in the place of the wine, neither this nor that was purified; if he “kissed” in the place of the water, the place of the water is purified, [but] the place of the wine is not purified. {Rama: A mikveh that is lacking, into which wine fell and its appearance changed, and 3 logim of drawn water fell there, do not disqualify it; and when he returned and completed the mikveh with kosher water and it returned to the appearance of water, it is kosher (Beit Yosef in the name of the Tosefta in the name of Ra’avad).}
