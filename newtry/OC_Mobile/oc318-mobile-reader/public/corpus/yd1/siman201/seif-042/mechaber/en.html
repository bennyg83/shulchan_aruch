One who placed jugs on the top of the roof to dry them, and rains descended upon them and they filled up: even though it is the rainy season, he should break the jugs or turn them over, and the waters that were in the jugs are kosher to immerse in — even though all these waters were in vessels — since he did not fill them with his hand. But if he lifted the jugs and poured them out, then all the waters in them are drawn [waters].
