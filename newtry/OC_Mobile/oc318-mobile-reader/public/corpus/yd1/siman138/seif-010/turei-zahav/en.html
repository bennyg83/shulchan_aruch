And if they are dry, he first applies water and afterwards ash, etc. This is how the text should read. This is likewise the opinion of the Ramah cited by the Tur, who wrote that with dry utensils one wipes them with water and afterwards rinses them well, etc. I saw in the Tur belonging to our teacher, the gaon Rabbi Yitzchak bar Betzalel, that he emended the text as follows: one wipes them with water, afterwards with ash, afterwards with water, afterwards with ash, and afterwards rinses them well. The Beit Yosef likewise explained it this way without emending the text.
