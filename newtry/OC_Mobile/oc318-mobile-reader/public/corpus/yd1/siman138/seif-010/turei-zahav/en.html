And if they are dry, he first applies water and afterwards ash, etc. Thus it should read; and so is Ra'ah's view in the Tur who wrote: for dry ones he wipes with water and afterwards rinses well, etc.; and I saw in the Tur of our teacher the Gaon Rabbi Yitzchak bar Betzalel that he emended thus: he wipes with water and afterwards ash and afterwards water and afterwards ash and afterwards rinses well; also Beit Yosef explained thus without hagahah.
