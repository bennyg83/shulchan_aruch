Wiping. Shach notes that with aging, the Gemara's requirement to untie the knots may not apply, since that requirement was stated only for kashering by wiping. Nevertheless, Shach's own view is that the knots must be untied even when relying on aging.
By rinsing. This implies cold rinsing. Hagahot Ashri, however, implies permission only after the customary laundering process, involving several washings with ash, hot water in a secondary vessel, and cold water. This concerns attached residue. Shach.
