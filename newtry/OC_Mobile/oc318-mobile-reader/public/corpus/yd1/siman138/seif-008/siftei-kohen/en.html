Rinsing suffices for them. This implies rinsing in cold water, and so it is in Hagahot Asheri and Mordechai there. However, they wrote that this is specifically with laundering, where they are accustomed to beat them several times; so too it is in Tosafot. However, in Asheri it implies that it is permitted only with laundering as they are accustomed to launder them in several washings, with ash and washing in hot water in a kli sheni and in cold water. It appears they do not disagree, because Tosafot, Mordechai, and Hagahot Asheri conclude: and it requires study whether one should distinguish regarding prohibitions between something that adheres and something that does not adhere, etc. Rosh there explains the practice of permitting all tablecloths upon which hot prohibition fell, even an adhering prohibition: in such a case specifically hot washing and ash are required. So it appears to me.
