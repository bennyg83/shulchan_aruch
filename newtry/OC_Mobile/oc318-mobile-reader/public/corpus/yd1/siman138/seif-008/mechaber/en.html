A chalf vessel (meaning, a type of grass from which they make mats and ropes), and “hotzin,” and the like — from basketry with which they strain wine: if they were sewn with ropes, one rinses them; if they were held to one another with a tight connection, they require wiping-dry; and if they were sewn with linen, one ages them [12 months]. (Our cloths upon which their wine fell — rinsing suffices for them, even if boiling wine fell upon them) (Tur and Mordechai, perek HaSocher, and in Hagahot Ashiri).
