And we do not obligate him to gather the pits. In Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Shimshon he concludes on this that this is not nullifying issur lekhatchilah, for he did not intend to nullify — end; and there is no difficulty from this on what I wrote in siman 137 seif 2 that where it is possible through another matter it is like nullifying issur lekhatchilah; for here also it is not possible through another matter, for after (washing) [cleaning] it properly they did not further trouble him to search for pits that are not visible before us; for whatever is visible goes away through (washing) [cleaning].
