And we do not require him to gather the grape pits. The Beit Yosef, in the name of Rabbeinu Shimshon, concludes concerning this that it is not considered intentionally nullifying a prohibition, since he did not intend to nullify it. End quote. This does not present a difficulty to what I wrote in siman 137, seif 2, that where another method is possible, it is considered intentionally nullifying a prohibition. Here too it is as though no other method is possible, for after he swept it properly, they did not trouble him further to search for grape pits that are not presently visible before us. Whatever is visible is removed through sweeping.
