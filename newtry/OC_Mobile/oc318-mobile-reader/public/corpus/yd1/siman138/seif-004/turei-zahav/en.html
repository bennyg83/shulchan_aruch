A large tub. This means that it has a spigot through which clear wine is drawn. If it has no spigot, no clear wine is present there at all, and even rinsing is unnecessary. This appears straightforward to me.
And measuring vessels. It appears to me that this also includes the siphon called a heber belonging to a non-Jew, which requires substantial kashering. This follows all the more so from the law of measuring vessels, since wine gathers in its bend, and rinsing will not be effective for it. Those who are lenient should be protested.
