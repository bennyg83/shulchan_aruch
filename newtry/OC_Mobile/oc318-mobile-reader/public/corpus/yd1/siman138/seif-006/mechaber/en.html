The “akalim” (meaning, basket-like woven [frames], like a net, “portims” in the vernacular) that they wrap around the vat: those made from palm fronds or hemp — one wipes them dry. Those of “shifah” and of “gomi” absorb more and require aging 12 months (and one should be stringent regarding those of reeds as with those of shifah and gomi) (Semag). And if he wishes to purify them immediately, he boils them in boiling water, or scalds them in water of olives (the view of Rambam), or places them under a spout whose water flows, or in a spring whose water runs, for 12 hours, and afterwards they are permitted.
