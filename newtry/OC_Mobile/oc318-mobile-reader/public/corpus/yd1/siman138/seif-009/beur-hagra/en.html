(Extract) But for kashering, etc. — for that which wiping-dry does not help in pitched [vessels] is because of cracks, as stated in the incident of Rav; and nevertheless iruy helps, as above seif 1. Rashba and Ran.
And likewise in aging, etc. The Rosh there 2 on "he permits them," etc.; and so said Rambam; but the plain meaning of the Gemara does not imply thus, for it implies it refers also to aging. Ran; and he wrote that Raavad's view is that in all of them one must loosen the knots except for rinsing; and Raavad according to his method who explained as stated Avodah Zarah 2b — only in peeling and iruy does he speak; but all poskim disagreed with him and explained through wiping-dry as stated (end).
