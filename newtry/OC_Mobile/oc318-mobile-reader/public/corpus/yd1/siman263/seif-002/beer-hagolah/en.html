A baraita in Yevamot 64b, following Rabbi, that two times establish a chazakah, because it is a matter of possible danger, and in a possible danger we are lenient; Rif and Rosh.
Rambam there, end of chapter 5. From his wording “circumcised a first one, etc.,” and not “circumcised a first son” in masculine form, our teacher learned that this applies whether from her first husband, etc.; Kesef Mishneh there.
Rabbi Manoach.
