One must draw, etc. One does not bless over the circumcision unless a covered foreskin appears to him. Tur. The Bach wrote that if it were certainly a covered foreskin it would override Shabbat. Therefore now, when it is not certain but appears to him, and is close to certain, that it is a covered foreskin, although it does not override Shabbat, nevertheless he must bless over drawing covenant blood. See above siman 262 s.k. 7.
And not with iron, etc. This refers to the examination: when one is born circumcised, they examine him to see whether no covered foreskin or other excess is found. Regarding this he says that this examination should be done gently by hand and by sight, not with iron. Regarding this too he concludes afterward, “and they see how to circumcise, etc.” Perishah.
