He must draw covenantal blood, etc. No blessing is recited over the circumcision unless what appears to him is a compressed foreskin. Tur. The Bach wrote that if it were certainly a compressed foreskin, the circumcision would override Shabbos. Therefore, in the present case, although it is not certain but merely appears so and is very likely a compressed foreskin, and thus does not override Shabbos, a blessing must nevertheless be recited over drawing covenantal blood. See above, siman 262, seif katan 7.
And not with iron, etc. This refers to the examination. When an infant is born circumcised, he is examined to determine whether there is a compressed foreskin or any other excess tissue. The ruling teaches that this examination should be performed gently by hand and by visual inspection, not with an iron instrument. The subsequent phrase, ‘and they observe how to circumcise,’ also refers to this. Perishah.
