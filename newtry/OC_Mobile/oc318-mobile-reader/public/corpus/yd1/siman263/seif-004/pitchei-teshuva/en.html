See in Pelati siman 38, who challenged why Tur and Shulchan Aruch omitted what is stated in chapter Rabbi Eliezer of circumcision — Rabbi Nachman said, etc.: the third time she brought him before him he saw he was red — he said to her wait until his blood is absorbed; again, etc.; the third time she brought him before him he saw he was green — he said to her wait until his blood falls on him, etc. — and why did they not also write Taz that if her pains seized her to die and they see in the newborn now that he is red or green — they circumcise him after his blood is absorbed or falls on him as Rabbi Nachman taught; and his answer is strained — see there. And see in responsum Beit Efrayim part 4 siman 57 who challenged him and wrote plainly it is satisfied that in truth we do not find this now for us, for we hold this infant who is red or green — we do not delay him until blood is absorbed or falls on him, and even the first time the law is thus; and in Rabbi Nachman's time this halachah had not yet been innovated that one must wait until blood is absorbed on him — see there where he elaborated.
