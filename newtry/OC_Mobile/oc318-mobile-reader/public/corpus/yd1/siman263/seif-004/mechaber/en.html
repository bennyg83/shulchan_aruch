One born already circumcised — one must draw from him blood of the covenant, but gently; and circumcision requires careful inspection by hand and by sight, not with iron lest it pain him; and they see and are careful how they circumcise him and wait for him a long time, and we are not concerned for the eighth day lest he come to danger: {Rama: And we do not circumcise him on Shabbat. (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam and Tur.)}
