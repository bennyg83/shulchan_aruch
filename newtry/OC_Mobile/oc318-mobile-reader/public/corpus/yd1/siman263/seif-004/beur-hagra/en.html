One born circumcised, etc. Although Rav and Shmuel disagree there, and in this matter the halachah ordinarily follows Rav, here it follows Shmuel, because Rabbah and Rav Yosef, who are later authorities, hold like him. The halachah follows Rabbah, in accordance with the established rule concerning Rabbah and Rav Yosef. Therefore Shabbat is not desecrated for such a child and no blessing is recited. This is the view of the Rif and the Geonim, unlike Tosafot and Behag there, who rule like Rav.
(Likkut) One born circumcised, etc. See siman 268, seif 1, where the same rule is stated regarding a convert who converted while already circumcised. The Geonim and Rif rule likewise. Their basis is the Yerushalmi and Bereishit Rabbah, section 46: the doubled expression “he shall surely circumcise” includes one born already circumcised. Rabbi Shimon bar Abba taught: Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel do not disagree that one born circumcised requires the drawing of covenantal blood. They disagree only regarding a convert who converted while already circumcised: Beit Shammai require the drawing of covenantal blood, and Beit Hillel do not. Rabbi Elazar, son of Rabbi Elazar HaKappar, says: Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel do not disagree concerning either case, and both require the drawing of covenantal blood. They disagree only where a child was born circumcised and his eighth day fell on Shabbat: Beit Shammai permit desecrating Shabbat for him, while Beit Hillel do not. The halachah follows the student. See below, siman 265, seif katan 15. End of Likkut.
