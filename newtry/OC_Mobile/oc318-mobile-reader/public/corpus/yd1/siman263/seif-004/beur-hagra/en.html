Born, etc. Although Rav and Shmuel disagreed there and halachah follows Rav here — halachah follows Shmuel, for Rabbah and Rav Yosef are the later ones and hold like him; and halachah follows Rabbah as we establish "Rabbah and Rav Yosef are the later ones," etc. — therefore we do not desecrate Shabbat and do not bless on him — Rif and Geonim, and not like Tosafot and Behag there who ruled like Rav. (Extract) Born, etc. And see siman 268 seif 1 that the same applies to a convert who converted already circumcised; and so Geonim and Rif wrote, and their reason from what is said in Yerushalmi and Bereishit Rabbah parashah 46: "he shall surely circumcise" — to include one born already circumcised. Rabbi Shimon bar Abba taught: Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel did not disagree regarding one born already circumcised that one must draw covenant blood from him — on this they disagreed regarding a convert who converted already circumcised: Beit Shammai say one must draw covenant blood from him, and Beit Hillel say one need not. Rabbi Elazar son of Rabbi Eliezer ben Hyrcanus says: Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel did not disagree on this and on that that one must draw covenant blood from him — on this they disagreed regarding one born circumcised and his eighth day falls on Shabbat: Beit Shammai desecrate Shabbat for him, and Beit Hillel do not desecrate; and halachah follows the student (see above siman 265 s.k. 15) (end extract).
