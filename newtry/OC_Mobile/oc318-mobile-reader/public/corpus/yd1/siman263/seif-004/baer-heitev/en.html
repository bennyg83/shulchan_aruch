To draw blood. No blessing is recited over the circumcision unless it appears to him to be a compressed foreskin. This is the Tur. The Bach writes that if it were certainly a compressed foreskin, the procedure would override Shabbos. Here it is not certain, but appears and is highly likely to be a compressed foreskin. Although it does not override Shabbos, a blessing must nevertheless be recited over drawing the covenantal blood. See below, siman 265.
