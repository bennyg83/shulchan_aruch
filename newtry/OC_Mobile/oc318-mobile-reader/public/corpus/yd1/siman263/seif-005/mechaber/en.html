An infant who died before reaching eight days — they circumcise him at his grave with a flint or reed, and do not recite the blessing on circumcision; but they give him a name so that they may have mercy on him from Heaven and he may live at the resurrection of the dead: {Rama: And we do not do so even on the second day of Yom Tov in the Diaspora, for it is forbidden to bury on Yom Tov. (Agudah and Or Zarua.)}
