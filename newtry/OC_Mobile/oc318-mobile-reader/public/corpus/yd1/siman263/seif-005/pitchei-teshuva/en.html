Until he grows and his strength increases. Noda BiYehudah discusses a healthy three-year-old whose brothers died from circumcision. Since Rambam, Tur, Nimukei Yosef, and Shulchan Aruch rule to wait until the child grows strong rather than omit circumcision permanently, he should be circumcised if demonstrably healthy, robust, and not greenish, preferably in clear weather. Chatam Sofer defends this ruling.
