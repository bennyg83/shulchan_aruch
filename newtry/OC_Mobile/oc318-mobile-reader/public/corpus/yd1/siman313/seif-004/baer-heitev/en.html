Intent. The Bach explains that because the non-Jewish woman serves in the household, she is responsible to serve the owner in all matters that benefit him. The Taz writes that no distinction should be made between a servant and another person, for the responsum of the Rosh does not mention that she was a servant. The distinction is instead this: Here she cut off the ear. The loss of a limb is obviously a blemish even according to non-Jews, so she certainly intended to make the animal permitted. In the case in seif 2, the official did not remove a limb but merely pierced the ear with a spear, and he did not know that this would permit the animal. Therefore it was prohibited only after he saw that such an act had permitted it.
