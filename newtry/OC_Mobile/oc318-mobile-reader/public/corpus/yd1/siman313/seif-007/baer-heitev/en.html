Before it emerged. It does not become consecrated until it emerges from the womb. In the time of the Temple, causing a blemish at that stage was prohibited because it constituted destruction of sacred property. Taz. The Shach writes that even if part of its limbs has emerged, so long as the majority or the head has not emerged in a manner that constitutes birth, as stated above in siman 14 and below in siman 319, one may cause a blemish. Thus, if a kid has long ears visible outside the womb before its entire head has emerged, one may nick its ear before most of its head emerges. The same applies in analogous cases. See siman 14.
