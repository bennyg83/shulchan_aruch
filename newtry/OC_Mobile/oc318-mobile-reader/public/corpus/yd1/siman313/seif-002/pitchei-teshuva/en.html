He commanded him: forbidden. [See Baer Heitev in the name of Shach; and see responsum Chatam Sofer siman 112, who wrote that what Shach wrote here, that where there is an umdana he is believed when speaking incidentally (misei le-ta'amo), this is the meaning: since initially the Sages enacted to forbid a bechor based on mere umdana, as written before this, if he inflicts a blemish on others we follow umdana to issur, so too we follow incidental speech with umdana, and the Sages' enactment regarding inflicting a blemish on a bechor was to follow umdana; which is not so with other issurim, even if there is an umdana we do not believe him even when speaking incidentally, as Rama wrote in siman 130 seif 8, second gloss. And from the language of Minchat Yaakov, klal 59 s.k. 7, it appears he did not quite understand it thus; see there. And see what I wrote above siman 57 seif 13 s.v. in the neck.]
