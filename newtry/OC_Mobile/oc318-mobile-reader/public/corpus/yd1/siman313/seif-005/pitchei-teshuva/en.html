The guardian placed a blemish in it. See Chavot Yair responsa simanim 34-35. It is clear from his words that this applies specifically where the blemish he placed is not one that they ordinarily place in their animals. But if he castrated the bekhor, and it is known to all that their common practice is to castrate males soon after birth, one acts wrongly by leaving the pregnant cow with him, for presumably he will make this blemish, which is to the animal's benefit. The guardian's law is to act with another's property as with his own, as stated in Choshen Mishpat siman 291; if so, this is actual causation and is considered intentional; see there.
