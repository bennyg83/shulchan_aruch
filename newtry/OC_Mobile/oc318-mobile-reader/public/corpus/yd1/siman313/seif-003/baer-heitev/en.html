Forbidden. The Shach wrote: although above in siman 16 seif 11 we say that a gentile speaking casually is not believed for testimony, even regarding prohibition, one may say that here is different. Since we follow assessment, where we see that the gentile intends to permit what is forbidden, the assessment of mind is that a gentile speaking casually is telling the truth; for if not, why did he place a blemish in it? The Taz wrote: therefore one must say that it speaks where the Jew is not before us so that he could contradict him; but if the Jew contradicts him, the gentile is not believed.
