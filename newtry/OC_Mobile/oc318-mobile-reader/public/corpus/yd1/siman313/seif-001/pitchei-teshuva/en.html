To place a blemish in a bekhor. See Chatam Sofer responsa siman 306, who discusses at length whether in this time it is a Torah prohibition or merely rabbinic. The Rambam in chapter 1 of Issurei Mizbeach rules that even nowadays it is Torah law, while Rashi, Tosafot, and the Rosh hold that it is only rabbinic. He wrote there that what the questioner suggested, that the practical difference would be a doubtful bekhor, in which it should be permitted to place a blemish ab initio, is incorrect. Certainly even according to Rashi, Tosafot, and the Rosh, one cannot say here that it is a rabbinic doubt and therefore lenient, since even the rabbinic prohibition exists only because it appears as though one is placing a blemish. If so, regarding a doubtful bekhor, since for shearing and work we treat it as a bekhor because of a Torah doubt, if we then place a blemish in it by hand, nothing appears more like placing a blemish in consecrated property than this. Therefore even through a non-Jew it should be forbidden. See there further at the end of siman 318 on this matter. See also there, and in siman 236, what he wrote regarding whether in reality one could offer sacrifices nowadays, at least communal offerings.
