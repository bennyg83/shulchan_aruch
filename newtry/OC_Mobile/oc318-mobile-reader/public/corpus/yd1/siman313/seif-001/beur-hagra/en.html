It is forbidden... and even. This is stated in Bechorot 34a and Yom Tov 27b.
Or if he says. This is stated on 35a, and the Gemara there discusses the case as one who says this to him.
Or through causing it. See Bechorot 35a concerning what this includes, and Yom Tov 27b. The Mishnah in Bechorot there likewise states, "all blemishes," and so forth.
