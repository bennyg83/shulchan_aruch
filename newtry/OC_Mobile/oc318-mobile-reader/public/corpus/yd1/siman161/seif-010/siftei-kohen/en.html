We extract from him etc. Beit Yosef wrote that the Rashba in a responsum was uncertain: if one says “I will give you 12 dinars interest on condition that my courtyard which rents for only 10 you rent for 12” — when we extract from him we extract only 10, for in the main condition he could not receive more than the rental value of the house which rents for only 10 — end; and Darkei Moshe brings it.
