The dinar. Taz writes that one who rented property at a high price because he expected profit from the interest cannot later argue that, once he must return the interest, he should pay only ordinary rent. The owner may answer, 'You understood and accepted.' The same applies where he bought an object or land for the amount of the interest; returning the item now does not discharge the money debt. Rashba was uncertain where the lender stipulated twelve dinars of interest on condition that the borrower rent a courtyard worth ten for twelve. Perhaps only ten is recoverable, because the excess condition could not legally create more than the property's actual rental value.
