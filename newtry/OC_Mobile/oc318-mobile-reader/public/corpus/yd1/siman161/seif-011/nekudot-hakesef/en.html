(In Taz s.k. 7, 8) not precise — on the contrary Tur, Shulchan Aruch, and Rama hold unlike Ramban, rather per Tosafot, Rosh, Mordechai, Hagahot Ashri, and other poskim witnesses are not disqualified thereby, for "do not place" does not mean to people except borrower and lender; and when ribbit is not explicit in document the rabbis agree he does not collect principal lest he collect ribbit as principal — and so plainly in Mechaber and Rama CM siman 52; and also from his words CM siman 34 seif 10 it seems witnesses are not disqualified on this.
(In Taz s.k. 9) and in Beit Yosef there was an erroneous old text, etc. Bach already preceded on this; and so plainly in Sefer HaTerumot shaar 46 beginning part 2: it is taught in Tosefta ch. Eizehu Neshekh: one who finds a document with ribbit should tear it, etc. — even per the rabbis the finder must tear it if it was in his hand and he transgressed by keeping it — end quote; and so in Gedulei Terumah there in Hagahot Ashri it should read thus — plain; and so in Hagahot Ashri Krakow.
