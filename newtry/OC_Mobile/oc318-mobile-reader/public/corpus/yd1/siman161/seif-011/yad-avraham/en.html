(Seif 11) Document containing ribbit whether Torah, etc. He collects principal alone and it must be recognizable as ribbit; but if not explicit, only principal with ribbit combined — he does not collect even principal specifically in it — Ran: in document he does not collect at all; but if borrower admits he collects principal as oral lender law. In CM siman 52 Beit Yosef brought Hagah two views on this; Taz here and Shach there in CM already wrote halachah is not per this view that holds he does not collect at all — no one holds thus — see there. In my humble opinion at first glance one can bring proof from Talmud Eizehu Neshekh (73a): Israelite who borrowed from gentile at ribbit and converted — if after conversion he fixed it as loan he collects principal and does not collect ribbit — poskim ruled and Taz CM siman 171. Explanation of "fixed as loan": Rashi there — he calculated with him ribbit and principal and wrote document he owes thus — end quote; Rav HaMaggid brings in Laws of Lending; and this is like "I fixed profit as principal" stated in Talmud there (68b) — meaning principal with ribbit combined; and nevertheless we do not penalize principal as profit and he collects principal if borrower admits as oral lender, for he did not say collect principal in document as here, rather collect without specification as oral lender. However per Rambam there it is ribbit derabbanan for what accrued before he converted.
