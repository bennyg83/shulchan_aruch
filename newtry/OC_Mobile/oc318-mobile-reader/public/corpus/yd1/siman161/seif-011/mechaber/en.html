A promissory note that contains interest — whether by Torah law or by rabbinic law — he collects only the principal, provided that it is recognizable that it is interest. {Rama: For example, where it is explicit in the promissory note — the principal by itself (Rosh, and Mordechai, and Hagahos Maimoniyos, and Nimukei Yosef, and Tosafos, and Hagahos Ashiri, and Tur-Choshen Mishpat siman 52, not like Ramban); or where only the principal was written in the promissory note and witnesses testify about the interest (Tur). And anyone into whose hand it comes should tear the promissory note, for we are concerned lest he collect the interest with it (Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 4; and Tosafos; and Mordechai; and Hagahos Ashiri in the name of the Tosefta).} But if it is not explicit, rather it includes the principal together with the interest, he does not collect with it — even the principal. (And see Choshen Mishpat siman 52.)
