To return to him. Avodat HaGolah; and see Maggid Mishneh ch. 8 Laws of Lending din 15: what we say specific item must be returned is due to disgrace — Baal Gedulei Terumah wrote if he used it and now it is not worth four zuz he returns the cloak and pays depreciation in money; if borrower forgave the cloak — uncertain whether forgiveness helps or not, for still there is disgrace that not everyone knows this forgiveness; however we already wrote above that certainly we do not say foul language if he does not return the cloak except when he wishes to benefit by borrower's force from the ruling that this acquisition came through prohibition; but if afterward borrower wished to forgive or agreed to accept money — then no foul language, for this acquisition is in permitted status — end quote; and see there further.
