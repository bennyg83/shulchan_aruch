Only the principal. Meaning, the entire amount is included under the designation of principal, and the witnesses did not know that it contained ribbis. Rather, other witnesses know that ribbis is included here. In this matter he rules in accordance with the Ramban.
Whoever comes into possession of it shall tear up the document. This is the correct reading. The reason given in the Hagahos Ashiri is that he transgresses by maintaining it. The Beit Yosef had before him an erroneous old version that read, “it is lost through maintaining it,” and he elaborated in explaining it unnecessarily, for in the newer editions it is written explicitly as I have stated.
He may not collect with it even the principal. The Beit Yosef explains in the name of Maharik that this means he may not collect by force of the document. If the borrower admits the principal, however, he is required to pay the principal. In Choshen Mishpat, siman 52, the Rama cites an opinion that he is exempt even from the principal, even if he admitted it. I wrote there that no authority holds this view and that it is not the halacha.
