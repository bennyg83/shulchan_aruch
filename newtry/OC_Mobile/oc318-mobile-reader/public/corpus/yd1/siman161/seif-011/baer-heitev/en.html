The principal. We decree lest he collect the ribit as though it were principal. However, where the borrower admits it, some say he may collect, as the Rav wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 52. In responsa Masat Binyamin he writes that nevertheless the borrower need not give anything from the profit he made with the lender's money, even if he profited greatly, though it is proper to fine him to give from the profit to the poor of the city. Thus the Shach. The Taz writes that in Choshen Mishpat siman 52 the Rama cites a view that he is exempt even from the principal, even if he admits it, and I wrote there that no opinion holds so and it is not halachah.
