He wrote in responsum Mahari Halevi siman 2: Reuven had a document on a gentile for three thousand and sold it to Shimon for two thousand on condition that if by a certain time he gives him 1,400 gold pieces he returns that document. He raised: even though purchase stands until fixed time, nevertheless when seller gives at that time two thousand he must return document — for it is advance loan and condition to give additional gold calves is forbidden to fulfill; if he gave mistakenly must return, for even avak ribbit given mistakenly must return as explained siman 161 — see there.
It is written in She'elat Yaavetz vol. 1 siman 247: one who took fixed ribbit from his fellow and the other does not claim — he is obligated to return by laws of Heaven; if he explicitly forgave him he cannot retract and claim; and see Taz siman 161 and Knesset HaGedolah disagrees and holds borrower collects fixed ribbit his borrower gave — and he wrote law is with Taz: when this one forgave after giving ribbit then his acts prove — see there several distinctions in matter.
He wrote in responsum Tiferet Tzvi siman 63: one who lent his fellow and gave him an etrog as ribbit — permitted to go out with it, for we hold even though must return, nevertheless we do not go down to his property; if so since he acquired it is his; and because he is obligated to return and does not return — this is not mitzvah coming through transgression, for transgression is not because he took it to go out — for even if he did not take he would transgress mitzvah "return to him so he lives" — see there.
