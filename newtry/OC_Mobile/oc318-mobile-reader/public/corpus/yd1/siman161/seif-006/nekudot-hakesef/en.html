(Siman 161 in Shach s.k. 11) specific item. Perishah wrote there is a printing error and this s.k. should be below seif 7 "if it is a specific item," etc.; but regarding sons it seems from Perishah's own words he specifically mentioned specific item — as Shach wrote on this. However in my humble opinion Perishah's words are strained to say specific item is not precise; all the more per what Perishah brought in name of Shach that plainly specific item regarding sons is precise — how say the language of specific item he mentioned regarding himself is not precise; and even though in CM siman 266 they wrote Taz "robbery exists" and did not mention specific item — it seems they hold one can distinguish between robbery and ribbit; and what Gemara distinguishes in ch. HaGozel etzim between existing robbery and non-existing robbery deals only regarding robbery as challenged there — but regarding ribbit the distinction is only between specific item or not as regarding sons — and so plainly in Rambam ch. 4 Laws of Lending: robbers and lenders at ribbit who returned — we do not accept from them, etc.; and if robbery existed and ribbit was a specific item and it is in his possession we accept from them — end quote; and even though R. Yeruham in Misharim beginning 58 wrote lenders at ribbit and robbers who come to repent we do not accept from them — specifically where robbery does not exist or ribbit does not exist, etc., i.e. Perishah's words — nevertheless Taz who wrote here regarding ribbit specific item and in CM regarding existing robbery certainly holds as Rambam that there is distinction between ribbit and robbery; reason: ribbit since it came to his hand with borrower's consent we accept from him only as specific item — unlike robbery.
