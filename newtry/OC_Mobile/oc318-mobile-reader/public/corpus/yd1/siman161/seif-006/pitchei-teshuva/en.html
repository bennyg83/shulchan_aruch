Sons need not return. See responsum Tiferet Tzvi YD siman 48 letter 1 — Maharal Akiva Eiger was uncertain: if one gave lender fixed ribbit and lender has not yet repaid principal and lender died — can he deduct from heirs from principal he owes them what he gave as fixed ribbit, or similar to taking without money per those who hold avak ribbit is extracting (below siman 172 seif 1 in Hagah; see Shach below siman 177 s.k. 2) — likewise one can say it is extracting and we do not extract from heirs; or no, avak ribbit there is his money for when he gave he acquired — but here he needed to return and it is like payment on debt; and he wrote at first glance even fixed ribbit he acquires only obligated to return from mitzvah side and an unborn child is not warned; he elaborated and concluded for another reason he can deduct: since if his father were alive he could not collect, he has no right to bequeath this money to heirs — see there; and see further responsum Maharal mentioned siman 80 on this matter; and see Degel Merubah who was uncertain if borrower died whether lender must return to borrower's heirs since essence of ribbit return is "your brother shall live" — return to him so he lives — and since borrower died it will not come to his life; he brought everyone's words that he must return ribbit to him and heirs; and wrote possibly this is when wishing to repent, but court seems not to compel return to borrower's heirs — see there.
