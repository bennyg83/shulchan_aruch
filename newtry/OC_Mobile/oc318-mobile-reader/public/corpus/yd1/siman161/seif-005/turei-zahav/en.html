Comes out in court. It seems specifically if they claimed; proof from Rashi ch. HaRibit (61a): "they would compel him in court to return if they claimed in his lifetime" — end quote; from this I wrote CM siman 9: regarding one who claimed he need not pay and no money found except what one already took from him as fixed ribbit and creditor comes to take this ribbit for borrower — law is not with him, for since no claim from borrower there is no compulsion from court on ribbit recipient to return. Further proof: we say here court does not go down to his property — only they would compel him to fulfill positive commandment "your brother shall live with you" as Beit Yosef wrote in name of Nimukei Yosef — and here this does not apply, for when we remove ribbit it will not come to borrower's life.
