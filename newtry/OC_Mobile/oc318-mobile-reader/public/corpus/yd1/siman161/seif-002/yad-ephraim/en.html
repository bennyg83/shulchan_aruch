He wrote in responsum Mahari Halevi siman 2: Reuven had a document on a gentile for three thousand and sold it to Shimon for two thousand on condition that if by a certain time he gives him 1,400 gold pieces he returns that document. He raised: even though purchase stands until fixed time, nevertheless when seller gives at that time two thousand he must return document — for it is advance loan and condition to give additional gold calves is forbidden to fulfill; if he gave mistakenly must return, for even avak ribbit given mistakenly must return as explained siman 161 — see there. Further there siman 5: what community leaders practice giving documents on themselves from principal and profit to lender under heter customary between individual and fellow that they will engage with them some time until they profit, etc. — this is not heter even though community need; and also lender forbidden to lend — but they can borrow from gentile and tell him they are community agents to pay principal and ribbit for a time — i.e. they collect from public and if not collect they pay from their pocket; and even though they become guarantors contractors — permitted; and not like Israelite guarantor for Israelite for gentile; and they can also give their collateral to gentile as loan that they lend to ease this loan — see there further path of repair in this loan — I shortened.
