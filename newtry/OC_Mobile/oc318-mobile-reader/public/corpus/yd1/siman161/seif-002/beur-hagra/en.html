And if he comes, etc. Avak ribbit for R. Eliezer is like fixed ribbit for R. Yochanan, and there certainly obligated to fulfill Heaven — meaning to fulfill Heaven; and so Ribban explained; but Tosafot 62a s.v. tnai, etc. disagree with Ribban — and so Bava Kamma 94a. And further they brought proof from Tosefta: one who lends at ribbit and repents is obligated to return — and since it says "and repented" implies like R. Yochanan. (Extract) And if he comes, etc. But Tosafot Bava Kamma 94b s.v. i, etc. and s.v. letzet, etc. disagree on this; but they (Ramban and Shach) explain Ribban's explanation in Tosafot there (that he explained) "for fear it was given," etc.; and further difficult for them Tosafot's question why does Gemara ask what does he want with him since he need not return even to fulfill Heaven — unless first answer of Tosafot or second text, or third; and one can answer Gemara's question was on "if he repented" — and if their answer does not depend on "with your people" but Tosafot holds third answer as Tosafot there (end). (Extract) And if he comes, etc. And so Ramban in Milchamot and Rosh in his name siman 17; and Ramban wrote, etc., and all the more one who lent him, etc., even to fulfill, etc.; and below siman 166 s.a. (end). (Extract) Rashba disagrees and wrote in Bava Kamma 75a from what we say there "your words are nothing" — we infer in Yerushalmi generally a penalty: wherever there are no witnesses even to fulfill Heaven he is not obligated, for otherwise R. Gamliel would free for sake of Heaven; and wrote the same regarding avak ribbit — and so Bava Metzia 64, 65 regarding Rav Yosef's case who said return from now on and we do not find he returned wages they did (per Ramban and Rosh siman 17 no proof from this) (end). (Extract) And if he comes, etc. For N.Y. from Tosefta is not necessary — for thus it is taught there end ch. 5: one who lends at ribbit and repents must return; if he died and left before his sons they need not return — on this it says "the wicked prepares and the righteous wears"; but if father left them a cow, field, and garment with liability they must return; and it says "repented" due to the end as stated Bava Kamma ch. 9 and Bava Metzia there (end).
(Extract) Besides ribbit, etc. For in a gift he gave it as stated in mishnah (75b) doron, etc. (end).
(Supplement) And if he comes, etc. And so the view of Ramban in Milchamot and the Rosh in his name, seif 17; and Ramban wrote, etc.; and all the more so if he lent him, etc.; and even to fulfill one's obligation, etc. And see above, siman 166 seif 1 (end).
(Supplement) Rashba disagrees with this, and wrote in Bava Kamma 75a — from what we say there: "there is nothing to your words" — like R' [X]: we deduce from the Yerushalmi in general that for a penalty, whenever there are no witnesses, he is not even obligated to fulfill the duty of Heaven; for otherwise R' Gamliel would free [a slave] in order to fulfill the duty of Heaven; and he wrote: the same applies to dust of interest; and likewise implied in chapter 5 of Bava Metzia (64–65) in the case of Rav Yosef bar Hama — who said "I retract" — meaning from here onward; and we do not find that he returned to them the wages of the servants they worked [for free] (according to the words of Ramban and Rosh seif 17 there is no proof from this) (end).
(Supplement) And if he comes, etc. For there is [a passage] from a Tosefta — it is not compelling, for so it is taught there at the end of chapter 5: "one who lent his fellow with interest and repented — he is obligated to return it; if he died and left [money] before his sons — his sons need not return it; regarding this it is said: 'the wicked man prepares and the righteous man wears it'"; but if his father left them a cow, field, garment, or anything with liability — they are obligated to return; and "[if] he repented" is [mentioned] because of the latter clause — as written in Bava Kamma chapter 9 and Bava Metzia there (end). (Supplement) Besides interest, etc. For it was given as a gift — as written there in the mishnah (75b): "a gift," etc. (end).
