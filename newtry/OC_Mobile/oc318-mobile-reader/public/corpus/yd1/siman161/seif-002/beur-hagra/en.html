Yochanan, and there certainly obligated to fulfill Heaven — meaning to fulfill Heaven; and so Ribban explained; but Tosafot 62a s.v. tnai, etc. disagree with Ribban — and so Bava Kamma 94a. And further they brought proof from Tosefta: one who lends at ribbit and repents is obligated to return — and since it says "and repented" implies like R. Yochanan. (Extract) And if he comes, etc. For N.Y. from Tosefta is not necessary — for thus it is taught there end ch. 5: one who lends at ribbit and repents must return; if he died and left before his sons they need not return — on this it says "the wicked prepares and the righteous wears"; but if father left them a cow, field, and garment with liability they must return; and it says "repented" due to the end as stated Bava Kamma ch. 9 and Bava Metzia there (end).
