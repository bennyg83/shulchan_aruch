(There in Hagah) Or principal alone was written in document and witnesses testify on ribbit. Tur — meaning all sum written in document is only principal, but witnesses testify orally condition was to give ribbit — therefore document is not disqualified. Even per Taz's ruling that if principal and ribbit combined we disqualify document — and so plainly in Tur and Beit Yosef — see there. I did not reach end of Taz's view who explains meaning witnesses testify sum written in document includes ribbit — i.e. principal and ribbit combined; and ruled like Ramban on this, disagreeing with Mechaber — and Beit Lechem Yehudah followed him; but this is not so — source of this Hagah is from Tur and there it is stated plainly as I wrote, and Hagah does not disagree with Mechaber at all. And again I saw Shach in CM siman 52 challenged and wrote Tosafot ch. HaSholeach (44b) document with ribbit we penalize and he never collects — even principal, for since penalized borrower acquired — end quote.
