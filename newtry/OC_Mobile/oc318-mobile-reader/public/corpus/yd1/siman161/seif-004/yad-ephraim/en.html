He wrote in responsum Tiferet Tzvi siman 63: one who lent his fellow and gave him an etrog as ribbit — permitted to go out with it, for we hold even though must return, nevertheless we do not go down to his property; if so since he acquired it is his; and because he is obligated to return and does not return — this is not mitzvah coming through transgression, for transgression is not because he took it to go out — for even if he did not take he would transgress mitzvah "return to him so he lives" — see there. Further there siman 48: one who gave lender fixed ribbit and lender died — deduct from heirs what he gave their father as fixed ribbit, for he has no right to bequeath this money to heirs — if alive borrower would deduct fixed ribbit from his debt — see there.
