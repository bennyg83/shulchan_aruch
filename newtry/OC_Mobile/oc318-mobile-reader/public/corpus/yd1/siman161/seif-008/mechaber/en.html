One who was owed by his fellow a dinar of interest, and he gave him on its account 5 measures of wheat, and they were selling 4 [measures] for a dinar: he must return to him 5 measures; and if he wants to return to him their value — a dinar and a quarter — he is permitted, for the sale is valid even though it was done in prohibition.
