That the purchase stands. See Maggid Mishneh ch. 8 Laws of Lending din 15 who wrote: it seems one who lent his fellow and gave him an object (meaning unspecified) as ribbit must return the object and lender cannot give its value — specifically when he fixed price with him and afterward gave object for the money, then we say purchase stands and he returns the money; but when not fixed he must return the object itself, for buyer did not acquire and seller did not sell; and regarding appreciation and depreciation (meaning where purchase stands), e.g. when he gave the object it was worth more than now — learn this from robbery where we hold he pays at time of robbery — see there; and see Sha'ar HaMelech ch. 5 Laws of Marriage din 15 who challenged him; his view: even when he stipulated to give object as ribbit he can return money — see there.
