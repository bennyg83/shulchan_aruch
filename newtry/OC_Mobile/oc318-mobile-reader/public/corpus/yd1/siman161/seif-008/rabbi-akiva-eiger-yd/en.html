(Taz, seif katan 7) For otherwise. These words, and those that follow in seif katan 8, are astonishing. The simple meaning is the reverse. The intent of the Mechaber and Rama is that the witnesses are not disqualified. The case is either one where the principal and the interest are written separately in the document, or where only the principal is written and the interest was stipulated orally. If, however, the interest was incorporated into the principal, we decree concerning the principal lest he collect the interest, and he may not collect even the principal.
