(Siman 161 Shulchan Aruch seif 1) Everything forbidden to borrow with addition, and even less than perutah value there is ribbit prohibition. In my humble opinion the essence is as Rosh and Tur in name of Rashi: regarding land and less than perutah there is no Torah prohibition — thus proven from sugya beginning Eizehu Neshekh (61a) regarding Rava's statement: why do I need verses "not in robbery, not in ribbit, not in onaah" — if so let it say "not in ribbit" for land ribbit and less than perutah which we could not derive thus from "not in robbery and onaah," for land is not robbed and there is no onaah for land; and there is no robbery for less than perutah as mishnah teaches ch. HaZahav (55a) five perutot, etc.; and onaah for everyone does not exist for less than perutah as proven there — learn there is no ribbit for land and less than perutah. However Rashi wrote Sanhedrin (57a) there is robbery prohibition for less than perutah — and so Shulchan Aruch CM siman 360; and so Rambam ch. 6 Laws of Lending regarding ribbit; see siman 165 and responsum Peri Megadim and Maggid Mishneh ch. 4 Laws of Lending and responsum Beit Yaakov siman 213 and Ba'er Yaakov and my novellae: from above sugya proven we do not hold like Rav Kahana who said there ch. HaZahav there is no onaah for perutot — as Mechaber wrote CM siman 227 seif 5; for otherwise how does Gemara conclude there "not in robbery" came for withholding worker's wages — for robbery itself we would not need, for we could derive from ribbit and onaah. Now we need for full robbery and perutah — how do we know perutah measure from onaah since no onaah for perutot; and likewise cannot derive from robbery and ribbit alone, for it is a novelty as stated there — rather implies we hold like Levi who said ch. HaZahav there is onaah for perutot — as Rama wrote in Hagah CM in mentioned siman. However one can say prohibition exists — see Rosh ch. HaZahav siman 20 and similarly Maggid Mishneh ch. 4 Laws of Lending and Ba'er Yaakov. Nevertheless question: in CM mentioned siman 66 it seems regarding coin there is no onaah where there is no onaah — if so necessarily he holds where there is onaah for perutot also Rosh's view that Rava is per the one who says no onaah — uncertain; one can answer and see Ba'er Yaakov.
(There in Hagah) Or principal alone was written in document and witnesses testify on ribbit. Tur — meaning all sum written in document is only principal, but witnesses testify orally condition was to give ribbit — therefore document is not disqualified. Even per Taz's ruling that if principal and ribbit combined we disqualify document — and so plainly in Tur and Beit Yosef — see there. I did not reach end of Taz's view who explains meaning witnesses testify sum written in document includes ribbit — i.e. principal and ribbit combined; and ruled like Ramban on this, disagreeing with Mechaber — and Beit Lechem Yehudah followed him; but this is not so — source of this Hagah is from Tur and there it is stated plainly as I wrote, and Hagah does not disagree with Mechaber at all. And again I saw Shach in CM siman 52 challenged and wrote Tosafot ch. HaSholeach (44b) document with ribbit we penalize and he never collects — even principal, for since penalized borrower acquired — end quote.
