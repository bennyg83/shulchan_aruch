He wrote in responsum Radbaz vol. 3 siman 435: one who lends his fellow — forbidden to pay lender's debt due to ribbit evasion; if he transgressed and paid we do not deduct, for avak ribbit is not extracted; he can say I appeased him — and this one sins and loses — see there. He wrote in responsum Noda B'Yehuda vol. 2 75: one who sells merchandise to Shimon for a maneh and Shimon obligates when Reuven has money to return to sell to him for two hundred if he stipulates at time of sale for a time and as long as time not finished he cannot sell to others — possibly ribbit; but if he did not stipulate and can sell whenever he wishes and then this one has no claim — no ribbit; therefore in appraisal of return even if appreciated as explained CM siman 103 — no ribbit since buyer, etc., does not return — see there. There siman 76: one who obligated to give apartment to his son several years for debt, and died (lender) [borrower], and this one comes to collect with another creditor — we do not deduct from him what he dwells due to fixed ribbit, because lender has no obligation to return fixed ribbit he took from their father to borrower's heirs; and all the more against borrower's creditor; and son is called holding property — if so if we deduct rent it is like extracting from his hand; law is we do not extract from his hand to borrower's heirs, and all the more to borrower's creditor — see there.
