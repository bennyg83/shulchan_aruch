The laws of avak ribbis, and what is called interest by Torah law; it contains 11 se’ifim. It is forbidden to borrow anything with an addition (and even) (Tur in the name of Ramah); for less than the value of a perutah there is a prohibition of interest, but they do not extract it in beis din. {Rama: The general rule of interest is that anything that is “ager natar” is forbidden, whether it is by way of sale or by way of a loan; except that by way of a sale it is only avak ribbis, which is by rabbinic law (language of the Tur).}
