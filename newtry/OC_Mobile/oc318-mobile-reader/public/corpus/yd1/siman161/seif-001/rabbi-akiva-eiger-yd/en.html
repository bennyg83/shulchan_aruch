(Siman 161 seif 1) Everything forbidden to borrow with addition. In Tur he wrote, and Rashi explained: except land, e.g. he lent him five laden vines, etc.; and see Shitah Mekubetzet who wrote further one finds cases where he may give him this land with a quarter-kav of land, and may give him entirely one land of this measure — therefore not like exchange since he may give his land; and also not like rent since he may give him other land — see there; and one should examine this against Tosafot (69b) s.v. mar' dhadra who conclude since anyway there is depreciation if he returns in kind even though permitted to give other — nevertheless since he can return in kind with depreciation it is rent even if now he gave other appearance — see there; and so too here — see there well on this.
