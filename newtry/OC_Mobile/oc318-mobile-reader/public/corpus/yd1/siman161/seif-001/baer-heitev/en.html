With an addition. The authorities dispute whether interest applies to a loan of land. Bach rules stringently, as appears to be the view of the Mechaber and Rama. Taz writes that rabbinic interest likewise applies to slaves. With promissory documents, full interest plainly applies, because lending the document is effectively lending the money it represents.
