It is written in responsum Mas'at Moshe YD vol. 74: regarding what Taz siman 161 s.k. 6 wrote on one who claimed he need only pay what is due from one who took ribbit from him — we do not collect this instruction — not clear to me, for property was subjugated; and even though he gave willingly Torah did not forgive; and what he wrote: since no "your brother shall live with you" — meaning if he spared his body all the more his soul that he not be wicked borrower, etc. — see there.
