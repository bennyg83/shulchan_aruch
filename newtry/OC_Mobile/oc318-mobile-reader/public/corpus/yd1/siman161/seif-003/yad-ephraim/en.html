It is written in She'elat Yaavetz vol. 1 siman 247: one who took fixed ribbit from his fellow and the other does not claim — he is obligated to return by laws of Heaven; if he explicitly forgave him he cannot retract and claim; and see Taz siman 161 and Knesset HaGedolah disagrees and holds borrower collects fixed ribbit his borrower gave — and he wrote law is with Taz: when this one forgave after giving ribbit then his acts prove — see there several distinctions in matter. It is written in responsum Mas'at Moshe YD vol. 74: regarding what Taz siman 161 s.k. 6 wrote on one who claimed he need only pay what is due from one who took ribbit from him — we do not collect this instruction — not clear to me, for property was subjugated; and even though he gave willingly Torah did not forgive; and what he wrote: since no "your brother shall live with you" — meaning if he spared his body all the more his soul that he not be wicked borrower, etc. — see there.
