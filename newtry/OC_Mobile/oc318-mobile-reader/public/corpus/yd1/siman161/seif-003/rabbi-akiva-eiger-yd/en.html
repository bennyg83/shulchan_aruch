(Seif 2) Avak ribbit does not come out in court. If borrower gave avak ribbit to lender's agent — one can say there is no agency for sin and lender did not acquire and borrower can say it should return to him — Maharit (vol. 1 116); and if he swore to pay him ribbit derabbanan see Beit Yosef (s.k. 40) in name of Rashba's responsum he is obligated to pay; but in Ran's responsum (72b) not so; and see responsum Maharit (vol. 2 YD 60).
