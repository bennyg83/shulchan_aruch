(Seif 2) Rabbinic interest is not recoverable through beit din. If the borrower gave rabbinic interest to the lender's agent, one may say that there is no agency for the commission of a transgression. The lender therefore did not acquire it, and the borrower may demand its return. See Maharit, part 1, siman 116. If the borrower swore to pay rabbinic interest, see Beit Yosef, seif katan 40, citing a responsum of the Rashba, who rules that he must pay it. Responsa HaRan, siman 72, does not rule this way. See also Responsa Maharit, part 2, Yoreh De'ah, siman 40.
