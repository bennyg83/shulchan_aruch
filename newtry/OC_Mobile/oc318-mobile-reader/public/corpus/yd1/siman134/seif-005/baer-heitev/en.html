Parts. Issur VeHeter writes that this applies specifically to yayin nesech mixed with water. If it mixes into wine or a cooked dish and improves it, all is forbidden until there is sixty.
Excluding. Shach writes that this applies even to ordinary non-Jewish wine nowadays. Although it is permitted for benefit, that is only in a major loss. If the ordinary non-Jewish wine belonged to a Jew, however, he may sell the entire mixture, including the value of the forbidden wine, because even the wine itself could have been sold.
