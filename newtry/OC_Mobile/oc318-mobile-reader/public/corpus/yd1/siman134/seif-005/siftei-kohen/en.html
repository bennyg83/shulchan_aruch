Six parts. Or HaTerumah wrote in general rule 23, end of law 15: specifically yayin nesech in water; but wine in wine or in a dish that improves it — certainly all is forbidden until 60 — end; and it is simple.
He sells all to a gentile, etc. Meaning: even though the wine renders the water fit in this; and likewise in every place it renders the food fit — it is permitted to sell it except for the money of the prohibition there.
Except the money of yayin nesech. It implies it deals also with stam yeinam; and even though stam yeinam in our time is permitted in benefit — as above beginning of siman 123 — that is specifically in a place of loss; and accordingly, if the stam yeinam belonged to the Jew, it is permitted to sell all of it including the money of the yayin nesech in it — for even if it were b’ein it would be permitted to sell; and it is clear.
That was forbidden through any amount. Meaning: that was forbidden due to the small amount of prohibition that fell into it and is not discernible; and likewise forbidden because there is not 60 against it; and it is clear; and see above siman 84 seif 18 and end of siman 86.
