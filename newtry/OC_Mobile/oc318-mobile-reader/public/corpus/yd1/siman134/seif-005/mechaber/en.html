How much must there be in the water so that it will have enough to be mevatel the taste of the wine? Six parts corresponding to it; and any time the water has this measure, it is permitted even for drinking. {Rama: And if the water does not have this measure, he sells everything to a non-Jew except for the value of the yayin nesech in it (Rashba agreed to this according to his teachers, unlike Ran in perek HaSocher). However, one should not sell a large quantity together to a non-Jew, lest he return and sell the water to a Jew (the view of Tosafos). And the same applies to anything forbidden with any amount, when one buys it from a non-Jew — the law is so (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran, who so wrote in the name of Ra’avad); and see below siman 137 regarding how we measure to be mevatel it if one placed wine into vessels that are forbidden due to yayin nesech.}
