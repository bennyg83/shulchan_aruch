And if there is none, etc. Like wine [mixed] with wine — and as above, seif 2. Rashba; and he wrote that Raavad wrote that if it was mixed such that the water would be fit for drinking through [contact with] the wine — even if barely — it is forbidden to sell, as stated there, 38b: "and pickled [foods], etc. — Chizkiyah said: this only applies, etc." — and the same applies to wine mixed with wine regarding stam yeinam: if it was mixed in the proportion such that its water-equivalent would be fit for drinking, etc. And there is support for this in the Jerusalem Talmud regarding the dispute between Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel and the Sages: "R. Yosi said: one of the Sages came out of the meeting house and said: R. Yochanan and Reish Lakish disagreed — one said the ruling follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, and one said the ruling does not follow Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel." And [what Rashba writes is] that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel rules that wine used for [cooking] food is forbidden — implying that in all food-preparation materials he rules [it is forbidden]. But Rashba's own statement contradicts this where he writes that "even wine in wine" — since it says Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel agrees — this implies that in wine mixed with wine even in such a case Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel disagrees; and further since the Gemara says "the ruling follows, etc., wine in wine but not, etc." — implying analogous to [the case of] a barrel, etc. — and from his words it is implied that regarding wine mixed with water Rashba agrees, even though he refuted his proof from pickled [foods], since Chizkiyah is explaining the mishnah and not following Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. Nonetheless from the Jerusalem Talmud mentioned above his position is proven; and so wrote the Ran, who also wrote: "and so holds Rashba." But in Beit Yosef, siman 114, he wrote that in the short Trumat HaDeshen he ruled not like the Raavad's view; and so he ruled there in the gloss, seif 10. However, not, etc. As stated there, 85b; and as written in seif 8; and see Tosafot there, 49b, s.v. "he wove, etc."; and Yevamot 81b; and Zevachim 72b, s.v. "they were mixed" — and he holds the same applies to wine in water; but [adding it] a little at a time is permitted — as written in Orach Chaim, siman 467, seif 1; and see Magen Avraham there, seif katan 2, who challenged this.
