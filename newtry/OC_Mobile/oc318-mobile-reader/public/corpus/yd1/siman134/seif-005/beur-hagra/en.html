Rashba; and he wrote that Ra'avad wrote that if it became mixed so that the water is fit for drinking as wine, even under duress it is forbidden to sell, as written 38b "and they press," etc. "Chizkiyah said," etc.; and the same in wine in wine in stam yeinam — if it became mixed to the measure corresponding to water fit for drinking, etc.; and there is support for this in Yerushalmi regarding the dispute of Rashbag and the Sages — Rabbi Yosi: one of the rabbis went out from the house of assembly and said they disputed — Rabbi Yehudah and Rabbi Elazar: one said halachah is like Rashbag and one said halachah is not like Rashbag; and Rashbag teaches regarding wine for a dish that it is forbidden — evidently in all food preparers he teaches, and this contradicts Rashba in what he wrote that even wine in wine — since he says "Rashbag admits," etc. — it implies that in wine in wine even in this case Rashbag disagrees; and further since it says in the Gemara "halachah," etc. "wine in wine but," etc. — it implies similar to a barrel, etc.; and it implies from his words that in wine in water Rashba admits, even though his proof from "and they press" he rejected there, for Chizkiyah explains the mishnah and not like Rashbag; nevertheless from the aforementioned Yerushalmi it is proven as his words; and likewise Ran; and he wrote that Rashba also [wrote so]; but in Beit Yosef siman 114 he wrote that in Tur Zahav HaKatzar he ruled not like Ra'avad, and likewise there in Hagah seif 10.
However not like, etc. As written perek 5, and as written in Shulchan Aruch; and Tosafot there 49b s.v. "arg," etc., and Yevamot perek 1, and Zevachim 72b s.v. "nis'arevu," and they hold the same in wine in water; but little by little it is permitted, as written in Orach Chaim siman 467 seif 1; and Magen Avraham there s"k 2 challenged this.
