At the end. The Rosh writes that chatichah naasit neveilah does not apply because the mixture was forbidden by even the slightest amount. Accordingly, nowadays, when ordinary non-Jewish wine forbids only when there is less than sixty, if it mixed with permitted wine without sixty, the entire mixture becomes prohibited and sixty against all of it is later required. Thus our leniency produces a stringency. On the other hand, nowadays permitted wine itself can join to nullify the prohibition. Taz. See Shach cited above.
