A pit of wine into which a pitcher of water fell first, and afterwards yayin nesech fell into it: we view the permitted wine as if it is not there, and we measure the water that fell — if there is in it enough to be mevatel the taste of that yayin nesech, behold the water is more than it and is mevatel it, and everything is permitted. And if stam yeinam became mixed with permitted wine: even if no pitcher of water fell into it, but rather [water fell] at the end, if there is in the water enough to be mevatel the taste of the forbidden wine, it is permitted.
