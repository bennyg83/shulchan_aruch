And if stam yeinam became mixed, etc. This he divided from his own reasoning, for Rambam is stringent and requires water first, and the Rosh is lenient and permits even at the end; and Shulchan Aruch decided to divide so; and know that the Rosh wrote: and that which we do not say the piece itself became neveilah and would need to nullify the wine of permitted and of prohibition — this is because permitted wine did not fall except a little, and whatever is forbidden in any amount we do not say the piece itself became neveilah — end quote; and according to this, for us who are lenient with stam yeinam that it forbids only in 60 — if it became mixed with forbidden and there is no 60 against it, certainly we say the piece itself became neveilah and 60 is needed against everything; it turns out that from our leniency follows stringency; but nevertheless there is another leniency for us, that the wine of permitted also helps to nullify the prohibition.
