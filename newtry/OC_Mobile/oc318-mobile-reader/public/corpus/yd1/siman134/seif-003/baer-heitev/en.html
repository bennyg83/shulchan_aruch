Taste. “And if the water fell into the wine of the water, it was a bit short that the water had been destroyed until there was no wine, it is permissible for the mother to say that he was hard, and that he would not say to him, and that he would not say anything to him, and that he was not in the water.”
Controversies. Behold the Taz wants to reconcile the Mechaber's words — he extends greatly in explaining them, and Nekudot HaKesef challenged him; his view inclines as Shach wrote, that it appears an error crept into the books and it should read "and there is an opinion — if the beverage increased..." and this reasoning is primary: taste is never batel, even min b'mino, and even in other prohibitions — whatever has enough to impart taste in its counterpart when not its kind is not batel and returns and revives — Shach [and it seems to us that what the Mechaber wrote "and if the beverage increased" means that he did not pour from a small tzinzror but from barrels where the column descending from the mouth of the barrels is large as in seif 1, and likewise Bach — see Taz].
