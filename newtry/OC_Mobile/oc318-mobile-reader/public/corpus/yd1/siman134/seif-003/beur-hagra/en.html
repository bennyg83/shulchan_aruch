(Likut) If it increased, etc. The same should apply in seif 1 to wine mixed with wine, as Tosafot and the Rosh write there. The Mechaber's formulation, however, is primary: this applies specifically when one type is mixed with a different type. With its own type, it is permitted only until the prohibited substance becomes the majority, as stated in Yevamot 82b. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'Rav said,' and what I wrote in siman 99, seif 6 in the gloss. (End of Likut.)
