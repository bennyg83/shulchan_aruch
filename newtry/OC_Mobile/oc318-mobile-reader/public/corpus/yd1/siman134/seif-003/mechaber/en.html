Water that became mixed with wine, or wine with water, is [measured] by noten ta’am, since they are min b’she’eino mino. In what case is this said? When the permitted beverage fell into the forbidden beverage; but if the forbidden beverage fell into the permitted beverage, the first [drop] is batel and then the next is batel, provided that he pours from a small tzinzror, such that it pours and descends little by little. And if the forbidden beverage increased until there is in it enough to impart taste, it forbids. And how can the water be forbidden? For example, if they were worshipped, or they were an offering to idolatry. {Rama: His words here contradict one another, for he wrote that if the forbidden beverage fell into the permitted, the first is batel and then the next is batel, and afterwards he wrote that if the forbidden increases it returns and forbids; this is a dispute among the poskim, and for halachic practice the latter reasoning is primary.}
