(Siman 134 in Shulchan Aruch seif 1) Wine that was poured as a libation to idolatry and became mixed with permitted wine is forbidden in benefit, etc. See Shach s"k 1, who brought in the name of Avodat HaKodesh that it is evident not like Ramban and Rosh, who challenged in Zevachim and Niddah on that which we say in Shabbat 65 and Nedarim 40 that Shmuel made ritual baths for his daughters in Nissan and plastered in Tishrei, lest the dripping increase on the flowing — and they challenged: according to what we hold, forbidden into heter — kama kama batel; if so, here too let us say kama kama batel — see there; and accordingly necessarily it must be that Tosafot hold not like Ramban, for according to Ramban who holds where it is forbidden in any amount from the Torah or rabbinically we do not say kama kama batel in a majority, even when forbidden fell into permitted — and regarding yayin nesech we say that if forbidden fell into permitted, kama kama batel and we do not say it returns and revives — if so, what is their difficulty from Shmuel? For Shmuel holds min b'mino in any amount; if so, there is no one who holds in this case that kama kama batel — rather necessarily Tosafot do not hold Ramban's approach, but even if 60 fell at once, since yayin nesech is forbidden from the Torah in any amount — if so, what difference whether 60 at once or not? And that forbidden into permitted is permitted is because the permitted stands in its place and the forbidden comes to its boundary — there is importance to the permitted, and we say the forbidden kama kama batel; therefore they properly challenged from this of Shmuel — and study it.
