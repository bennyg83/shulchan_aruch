(Siman 134, Taz s"k 1) He extended in vain and concluded "the secret of Hashem is for those who fear Him" — and this is not the secret of the upright; and I go as a talebearer revealing this secret — it is revealed and explained in Derishah there, that he explained there the Mechaber's words as he wrote; but in truth it is not correct at all, for in truth seif 3 until "little by little" is Rambam's language letter by letter, and it is all according to the simple meaning — namely, a small tzinzror: even if it has enough for natan ta'am it does not forbid, for the first is batel, according to the poskim who hold so, as is in Beit Yosef; and if so, how because the Mechaber afterwards wrote "and if the beverage increased" shall we remove this language from its simple meaning? Moreover this language cannot bear this explanation at all, for it should have said "but if the forbidden beverage fell into the permitted beverage it is never batel if he poured from a barrel — only when he poured from a small tzinzror into the pit then it is batel when there is no natan ta'am, and if the beverage increased in it"; and now the essential law of pouring from a barrel is missing from the book, unless the matter appears as he wrote that it should read "and there is an opinion — if it increased." I again saw in Bach in a later pamphlet that he challenged Derishah on this, but he did not explain the reason for his challenge — see there.
