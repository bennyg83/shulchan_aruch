Shach wrote: even permitted wine that fell once greatly onto a drop of yayin nesech until there are sixty—nevertheless forbidden; the reason is that thing standing in its place is considered more than what comes upon it; but when the permitted stands in its place and the forbidden comes upon it, the permitted nullifies the forbidden unless it fell at once enough to nullify. However with great loss and such cases one may rely on poskim who hold that permitted into forbidden also, if it fell at once and there are sixty, it is permitted—only when a little fell we say "first first is nullified" and even if more permitted was added afterward until sixty, it does not return and revive; though in other prohibitions and likewise wine in water it would return and revive—in libation wine min b'mino they were strict. However all this is certainly yayin nesech; but in ordinary gentile wine nowadays we hold that in every case it is nullified in sixty.
