(Likut) Wine, etc. All of this is Rambam's language. (End of Likut.)
When does this apply, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. 'Rabbi Dimi said to him,' etc. Tosafot there, s.v. 'We view,' explains likewise, as do the Raavad, Baal HaMaor, Ramban, and Rashba. This is also the Rif's view, since he omitted this entire discussion. The Rosh, however, writes that Ravin does not disagree, for he discusses pouring from a vat or wine that fell without being poured. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'When,' which answers that it fell, etc., and s.v. 'Specifically,' etc. Rambam and the Tur rule accordingly.
He poured from the barrel, etc. In accordance with Rabbi Yitzchak there, as ruled by Rambam. The Rosh and Tur, however, rule like Rabbi Dimi, because in matters of rabbinic law, etc.
