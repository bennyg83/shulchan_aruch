A law of wine that was poured as a libation and became mixed; it contains 13 se’ifim. Wine that was poured as a libation to idolatry and became mixed with permitted wine forbids [the mixture] in benefit with any amount. In what case is this said? When the permitted wine was poured onto a drop of yayin nesech. But if he poured yayin nesech from a small tzinzror (meaning, a small jug) into a pit of wine — even if he poured all day long — the first [drop] is batel and then the next is batel. If he poured from a barrel — whether he poured from the permitted into the forbidden or from the forbidden into the permitted — everything is forbidden, because the column descending from the mouth of the barrel is large.
