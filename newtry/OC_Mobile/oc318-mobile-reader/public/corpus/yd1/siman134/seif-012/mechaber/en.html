Vinegar of wine of a non-Jew that fell into hot grisin spoils [the dish] and is permitted; but into cold [grisin] it improves [the taste] and is forbidden, even if he afterwards boiled them and they returned to be a spoiled taste. {Rama: Spices that absorbed yayin nesech and were placed into a cooked dish: if there are 60 against the absorbed wine in them, everything is permitted, even though the spices are made for taste, since they are not forbidden on their own they are batel. And if the wine in them spoils the cooked dish, even 60 is not needed (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba).}
