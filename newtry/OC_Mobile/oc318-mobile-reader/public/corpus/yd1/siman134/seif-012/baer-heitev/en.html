In a cooked dish. Shach writes that if the wine improves the cooked dish, it is forbidden even if it impaired the spices, because it now imparts favorable taste to the dish, as stated in siman 103:2. If the wine impairs both the spices and the dish, the spices are permitted for eating and may even initially be placed in the dish. Rashba. See the ends of simanim 99 and 105.
