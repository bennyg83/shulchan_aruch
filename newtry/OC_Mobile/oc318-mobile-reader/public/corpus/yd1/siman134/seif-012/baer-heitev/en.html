In a dish. Shach wrote — but if it is for enhancement in the dish, even though the wine was nat-laf in spices, it is forbidden, for now it imparts taste in the dish as above siman 103 seif 2 in Hagah; but if the wine was nat-laf in spices and also in the dish, the spices are permitted to eat and permitted even lechatchilah to put them in a dish — so is in Rashba's responsum and it is simple, and see there siman 99 and siman 105 — end quote.
