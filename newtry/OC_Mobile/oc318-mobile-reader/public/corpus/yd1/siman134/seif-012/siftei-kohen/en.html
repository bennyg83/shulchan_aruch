In a dish. But if it imparted a beneficial taste in the dish — even though it would have imparted a defective taste in the spice — it is forbidden, for now they impart a beneficial taste in the dish — as above siman 103 seif 2 in Hagah; but if the wine imparts a defective taste in the spice and also in the dish, the spice is permitted for eating and it is even permitted l'chatchilah to place it in the dish — so in Rashba's responsum as cited in Beit Yosef; and it is simple.
