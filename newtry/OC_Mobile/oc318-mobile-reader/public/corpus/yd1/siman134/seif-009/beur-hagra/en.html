(Likut) Wine of, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'This is the correct reading,' etc. This is also the version of the Rif, Rosh, and the other authorities, unlike texts that read 'fell,' etc. Rashi's version is likewise so. See there at the beginning, 'an incident contradicting the rule,' etc. According to the other version, the Gemara's challenge is difficult, for from the concluding clause, 'This is the rule,' etc., one could answer that it refers only to figs or dates. (End of Likut.)
