As Hashem says, “No matter how long and long, Hashem’s name is not for Hashem’s sake, nor is it because of Hashem’s wrath, and if it has been done.” Hashem’s promise is that Hashem’s people will not be given to Hashem’s people, and that they will not be given to them. And then, he said, “The Lord is the Lord.”
