(There seif 11) And if it is in a place where they are accustomed to eat bread of gentiles, etc. See Taz s"k 11 what he wrote, and in Perishah he answered perhaps the Jew will return and sell it to the tavern keeper, etc., and it is not correct, for then it would be forbidden to buy any bread from a tavern from concern that he bought it from a gentile — end quote Taz — see there; and it is not so, for so it implies in the arranged Gemara in Chullin daf 95, that in a place where they do not announce, meat found in a gentile's hand is permitted, for we say "kol d'parish m'ruba parish"; nevertheless it is forbidden to sell him treifah meat in a place where they do not announce, for we are concerned lest he return and sell it to a Jew; and if so, the same here — lechatchilah it is permitted to buy from him, for we say "kol d'parish," etc.; but lechatchilah it is forbidden to sell, lest he sell it to the tavern keeper — and study it, and it is simple.
