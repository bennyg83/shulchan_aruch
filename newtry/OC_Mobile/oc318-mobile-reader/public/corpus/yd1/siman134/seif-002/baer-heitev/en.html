And casts it away. Alternatively, he may burn and bury it. The same applies to any place where it is lost, such as a deep river, or he may sell it to a non-Jew excluding the value of the prohibited wine. He may sell it without explicitly stating this condition. Until the forbidden benefit is destroyed or excluded, the entire mixture is forbidden for benefit. Where no substance of the prohibition is present, such as wine placed in non-Jewish jars, all benefit is permitted because there is no value of forbidden substance. Shach. See siman 137.
The value. If he sells it to a non-Jew excluding the value of one barrel, it is permitted and he need not cast any benefit into the Dead Sea. Shach.
In sixty. Bach writes that he need not cast the benefit into the Dead Sea or sell it excluding the value of the prohibition. Shach is inclined to be stringent: the reason ordinary non-Jewish wine is nullified nowadays and even drinkable is only because of financial loss. One should not intentionally benefit from the forbidden wine itself. Rather, he should discard the value of the forbidden portion, after which the mixture may be drunk.
