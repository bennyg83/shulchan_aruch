Ba'er Heitev in the name of Shach; and see in responsum Noda BiYehudah tinyana part 4 siman 68, who wrote to establish Beit Yosef's view that wherever it is forbidden in the time of the Gemara and all the poskim, we do not rely in our times on the reasoning that gentiles nowadays are not idol worshippers except in a case of loss; but regarding stam yeinam, according to Rabbeinu Tam even in our times it is batel in 60 — we combine his view with the reasoning that gentiles nowadays, etc., and we permit even not in a case of loss; and he wrote that nevertheless who will dispute Shach in a place where there is no loss at all. But if it happened that some teacher permitted it without loss, I would not contradict his ruling. And he further wrote that if they gave the forbidden wine to barrels in small jugs whose mouths are not so wide as a large vat, there is room to permit even without any loss, for one can combine the Rosh's view who ruled like Rav Dimi that if forbidden fell into permitted, even from barrel to pit it is permitted — see there.
