Tikkun. And one should not sell it to a non-Jew, for one does not buy from him as written siman 112, for there in Beit Yosef it is explained that sometimes there is permission even from a Jew's [wine], and in Perishah he answered perhaps the Jew will return and sell to the tavern keeper — and this is not correct, for then it would be forbidden to buy any bread from the tavern from concern that he bought it from a Jew — end quote Taz.
