If it fell upon wheat, they have no permissibility through rinsing, because since they have a crack, the wine is absorbed in them. Therefore, if there is in the wine enough to impart taste, they are forbidden for eating. And he should not sell them to a non-Jew, lest he return and sell them to a Jew; rather, what should he do? He grinds them and makes bread from them, and sells it to a non-Jew not in the presence of a Jew. And if it is in a place where they practice to eat the bread of a non-Jew, they have no remedy (to sell it to a non-Jew except in slices, for it is forbidden to buy [bread] from a non-Jew in every place) (Kol Bo) (as above siman 112).
