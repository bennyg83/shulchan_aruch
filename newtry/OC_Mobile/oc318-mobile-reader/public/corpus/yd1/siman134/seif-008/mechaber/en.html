Wine of a non-Jew, whether old or new, that fell upon grapes: if they are whole, he rinses them and they are permitted for eating. But if they are split, or their stems were removed: if it has enough to impart taste, they are forbidden for eating; and he should press them and sell them to a non-Jew except for the value of the yayin nesech in them. But he should not sell them as they are, lest he return and sell them to a Jew. And if it does not have enough to impart taste, they are permitted for eating.
