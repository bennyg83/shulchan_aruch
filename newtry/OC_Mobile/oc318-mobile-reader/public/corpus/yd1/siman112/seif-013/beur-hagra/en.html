One who is not, etc., he cuts, etc. As Tosafot wrote, Berachot, 39, side 2, s.v. but — from the Yerushalmi: impure bread is clean and cemetery bread is pure — he blesses on whichever he wishes.
And it is permitted, etc. As written (Yevamos, 7, side 2; Zevachim, 32, side 2): since it was permitted for his leprosy, etc.; and see Avodat HaTaharah.
