6) And Samaritan bread — one should not be more stringent than other gentiles' bread. Peri Chadash there. And in a place where they permitted gentile bread, the same applies to Samaritans. Section 84 there. However, in Sefer Kaftor VaFerach, chapter 5, daf 17, it appears Samaritans persist in their prohibitions — see there, Birkei Yosef on Shiyurei Berachah, section 4; and therefore one should be stringent where possible.
