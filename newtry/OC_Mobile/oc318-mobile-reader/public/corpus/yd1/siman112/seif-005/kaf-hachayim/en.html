5) And regarding bread of Karaites one should be stringent and forbid their bread like gentiles, for they too do not eat our bread. Peri Chadash, section 2. Lechem HaPanim, section 2. Binyamin Zeev, section 1. Section 84 there. However, what they wrote that they do not eat from our bread is no proof, only because they regard them as gentiles and there is marriage concern among them. Shulchan Gavoah, section 2.
