That if a palter, etc. So too Orach Chayim in the name of the Ra'ah; and in the Ra'ah's words in Bedek HaBayit, daf 88, side 2, it is explained that this is according to his view that we do not follow the beginning but the present; but according to the poskim who hold palter bread is permitted forever, even if baal habayit bought it from a gentile, because we follow the beginning — it is permitted, for since at the time of baking it was permitted it is not forbidden again; and so the Rashba in Torat HaBayit wrote explicitly that even if the palter was ordered by him it is permitted since it was permitted at the time of baking; and accordingly it is difficult, for the Mechaber holds below, seif 7, that we follow the beginning — how did he write this law here? Perhaps for that reason they wrote in the name of "some say," or it is possible he holds according to his reasoning to distinguish that when ordered by him it is worse.
