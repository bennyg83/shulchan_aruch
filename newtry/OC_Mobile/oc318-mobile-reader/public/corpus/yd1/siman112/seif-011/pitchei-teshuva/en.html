And forbidden to sell, etc. The Mordechai learned this law in chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah from that in chapter Kol Sha'ah regarding the boat that sank in a swamp; and it is astonishing how he learned from there to here. One may say it is different there, for it is a Torah prohibition, whereas here it is a rabbinic prohibition of sheluhot — perhaps for d'rabbanan we are not concerned for this doubt that perhaps he will sell them to a Jew; and it would have been better for the Mordechai to bring proof for this from Avodah Zarah, daf 65, regarding wheat upon which yayin nesech fell, according to what the Mordechai himself wrote in chapter Kol Sha'ah, section 525, that that incident deals with a rabbinic doubt — see there; and see in the responsum of Noda B'Yehuda, second series, Orach Chayim part, siman 70, and Yoreh De'ah part, section 286; and see in Shach above, siman 57, s.k. 51.
