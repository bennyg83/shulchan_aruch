Pat Yisrael that a non-Jew baked without Jewish chitui and without kisam is forbidden {Rama: (and forbidden)} to sell to a gentile lest he come to sell to a Jew; and if he split the loaf in two it is permitted to sell to a gentile: {Rama: And the same applies to all forbidden pat akum; and therefore the practice is not to buy pieces of bread from the gentile, for we are concerned lest it is forbidden bread that a Jew sold to him thus (Mordechai, chapter 1 of Avodah Zarah, and Arukh):}
