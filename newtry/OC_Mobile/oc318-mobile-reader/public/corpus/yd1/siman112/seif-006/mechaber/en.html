In a place where they practiced leniency regarding pat palter, even if it is kneaded with eggs or eggs are smeared on its surface it is permitted; but infanda that a non-Jew baked is forbidden to eat from its bread (see above, siman 113, seif 3): {Rama: And some forbid bread with eggs smeared on its surface because they are visible and are not nullified relative to the bread and there is concern of bishulei akum (Tosafot, Semag, Hagahot Ashiri, Arukh, Mordechai, chapter 1 of Avodah Zarah, and Semak, siman 207) and so is the practice) And those kneaded doughs called kichlach or kinds of sweets called lekuch are in the category of bread; and in a place where they practiced leniency regarding pat akum they too are permitted, and we do not say there is concern of bishulei akum. And there are kinds of kneaded dough called kichlach that are baked on irons and the iron is greased at the time of baking with milk or pork — regarding those one should be careful and forbid them, and so is the custom (Maharai in Hagahot ShaDa and Agur in the name of R' Yosef of Prizish):}
