In a place, etc. As written in second chapter of Yoreh De'ah, 16, side 2; and Avodah Zarah, 38, side 1 — it teaches us flour is primary; and see Tosafot there and there, and in Chullin, chapter 4, section 1.
But infanda, etc. For the oiliness is visible on it; and also if absorbed, from the beginning it was visible and was forbidden because of bishulei akum and forbids the dough — unlike dough kneaded with eggs where from the beginning it was nullified in it. See there and there.
And some forbid, etc. As above; and the first view holds that since it is only for appearance, it is not a matter of concern, as Rashi wrote in chapter 5 of Zevachim (see above, siman 102, s.k. 6).
And those kneaded doughs, etc. For the way of its baking he makes them; and furthermore, if one establishes a meal upon them he recites ha-Motzi.
