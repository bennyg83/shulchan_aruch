With eggs. Why should we be concerned? If because of blood — the majority of eggs have no blood, as above, siman 66, seif 8. If because of an egg of an impure bird — they are not common among us, as above, siman 86, seif 2. If because of bishulei akum — eggs are forbidden; we establish that flour is primary — end of his words, Shach; and see above, siman 113.
Sweet kinds. Since if one establishes a meal upon them he recites ha-Motzi, their law is like bread proper. Taz; and specifically those whose batter is [thick] and have the status of bread; and so too in Turei Chayim.
