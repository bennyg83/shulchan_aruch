2) There. The Sages forbade eating pat akum, etc. — it appears the view of the Mechaber and the Rav throughout this siman that there is no prohibition in pat akum except when a gentile baked it; and as Beit Yosef wrote according to the Rambam and Rashba that even though a gentile kneaded and shaped, whatever a Jew did in the baking is permitted; and so the Rav in Turei Chayim, general rule 71, law 2, and he brought there many later authorities who hold thus. Shach, s.k. 1.
