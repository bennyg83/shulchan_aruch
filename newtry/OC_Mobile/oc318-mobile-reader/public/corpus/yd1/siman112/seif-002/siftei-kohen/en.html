The gentile. Specifically pat akum; but pat Yisrael that a gentile baked — the Tur wrote that nowhere practiced permitting it, for it is like bishulei akum; and so Issur VeHeter, general rule 44, law 9, in the name of the Geonim' responsum, and it is from the Mordechai, chapter Ein Ma'amidin, who brought thus in the name of Maharam from the Geonim (and it is in Maharam's responsum, Prague edition, siman 33); and so Turei Chayim there, beginning of law 2; and it is explained in their words there that even though pat akum that a gentile baked — they practiced leniency regarding it; and so the Magen Avraham; and the reason they did not forbid such pat because of bishulei akum is that it is a decree the majority cannot abide by; therefore pat Yisrael that a gentile baked — that reason does not apply — is forbidden:
And some say, etc. In the Mordechai he wrote the reason: because those places practicing leniency did so since the prohibition of pat akum did not spread at the beginning of the decree in those places, for pat akum's prohibition did not spread throughout Israel. Accordingly it appears even pat baalei batim is permitted, for the prohibition of pat akum did not spread at all in those places; and so is further proven in the Mordechai there — see there; and even though in the Yerushalmi, chapter 1 of Avodah Zarah, pat baalei batim is forbidden, and so hold some poskim — behold they too forbid gentile palter bread when a Jewish palter exists, because they hold the prohibition spread in all places, only they returned and permitted gentile palter bread where there is no Jewish palter because of saving life; but one who holds the prohibition did not spread in those places at all in any regard — is permitted. In this I settled there the correctness of what the Bach challenged against the Tur's words; and according to the second view, of blessed memory, there is no distinction, etc.; and again I found support for myself in Maggid Meisharim — see there; but from Issur VeHeter, beginning of general rule 44, the Rama, and Magen Avraham, it is clear that even those who practiced leniency regarding pat palter forbid pat baalei batim even when a Jewish palter exists:
