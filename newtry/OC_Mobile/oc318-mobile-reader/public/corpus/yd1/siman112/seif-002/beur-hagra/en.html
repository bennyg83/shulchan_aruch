Ran. And he wrote: and that which R' Yosi said bread was not permitted by the court — meaning they did not seat a court on this, only incidentally he revealed his view; and the Rosh explained: it was not permitted by a court to permit in all places, only the decree did not spread in all places, as above. And to us it appears that above it says from which, etc.; and furthermore R' Yochanan said even according to the view that, etc. — from which for him it does not apply, only we do not rule like him, since the Gemara brought R' Chelbo's and R' Yochanan's words even according to the view that, etc., as above — hence he holds like those who permit, and as written in the Yerushalmi cited above; and in Avodat HaTaharah, chapter 8, section 1; and even though the Gemara says there Rava said, etc., do not speak, etc. — that is regarding that which is forbidden, such as baal habayit's, for Rava himself permits in Pesachim, 30, side 1; and see Rashi there, s.v. sons, etc.; and there, 41: the baraita teaches: a shop, etc., even for eating; and the anonymous teaching is bread — therefore the Mishnah did not teach "permitted for eating," as above; and see Tosafot, Avodah Zarah, 13, side 2, s.v.
