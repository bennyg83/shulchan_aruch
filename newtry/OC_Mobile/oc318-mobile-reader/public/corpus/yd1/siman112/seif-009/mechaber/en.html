If a non-Jew lit the oven and a Jew baked in it, or a Jew lit and a non-Jew baked, or the non-Jew lit and the non-Jew baked and a Jew came and stirred the fire slightly — behold, it is permitted; and even if he only threw one piece of wood into the oven he permits all the bread in it, for the matter is only to be a sign that their bread is forbidden. {Rama: (And if he blew on the fire it is like chitui) (also siman 113, and in Agur in the name of R' Yosef Molcho, and it is in the responsum of Maharil, 221):}
