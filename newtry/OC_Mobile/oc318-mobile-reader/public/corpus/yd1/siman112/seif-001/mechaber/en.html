The laws of pat akum. It contains 16 seifim:
The Sages forbade eating pat of gentiles, idolaters, because of marriage concern {Rama: (and even in a place where there is no marriage concern it is forbidden) (Rashba, siman 248)} and they only forbade pat of the five species of grain; but pat of legumes, rice, and millet is not in the category of ordinary pat that they forbade: {Rama: Also it is not forbidden because of bishulei akum if it does not go upon a king's table (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of the Rosh's responsum):}
