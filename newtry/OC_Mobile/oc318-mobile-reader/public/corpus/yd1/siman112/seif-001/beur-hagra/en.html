Because of marriage concern. Gemara, 35, side 2; and see there, 36, side 2; and in Shabbos, 17, side 2.
And even in a place, etc. Such as from heretics — for we also say regarding their wine because of their daughters, as above; and see Tosafot there, 29, side 2, s.v. wine, etc.; and one may say, etc.; and there, 57, side 1, a child, son, etc.; and this is what it means "because of their daughters," and there is no difficulty "because of gentiles' daughters."
And they did not forbid, etc. As written in chapter 6 of Berachot (37, side 1) and first chapter of Challah.
And also, etc. That is to say, bread has no concern of bishulei akum; and where bread was permitted it is entirely permitted, for it was not in the category of sheluhot; but a thing that is not bread remains in the category of sheluhot. And even when bread was permitted, only a thing that is bread was permitted — and see in the gloss.
