1) [Seif 1] The Sages forbade eating pat akum because of marriage concern. So in the Gemara, Avodah Zarah, daf 35, side 2; and we say there in the Gemara, daf 36, side 2: they decreed on their bread and oil because of their wine, and on their wine because of their daughters, and on their daughters because of something else — meaning, if you permit him to eat with you at one gathering, afterward he will come to drink his wine and marry his daughter and worship idolatry. Shulchan Gavoah, section 1.
