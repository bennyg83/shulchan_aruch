One who is careful about pat akum may eat in a bowl with one who is not careful about it, and even though the taste of pat akum mixes into pat Yisrael he is not concerned: {Rama: Some say that one who is careful about pat akum and eats with others who are not careful may eat with them because of enmity and quarrel, since if he does not eat with them bread, which is the essence of the meal, they permitted him because of enmity; and one may not learn from here to other prohibitions (Beit Yosef in the name of an Ashkenazic responsum, and it is the responsum of Maharil, siman 35):}
