At the beginning of this siman the Beit Yosef proved that one may buy gentile leaven and ferment dough with it, even for one who is careful about pat akum, even though taste, etc., for the Sages did not decree on pat akum regarding taste like the substance itself — so too Rashi; but regarding crumbs that are visible, they are certainly forbidden as long as they were not nullified in the majority.
