7) And it is permitted to buy gentile leaven and ferment dough with it, even for one who is careful about pat akum. So Beit Yosef to prove in this siman; and so Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef, section 3; and Yeshuot Yaakov agreed, end of section 1, Chacham Adam, general rule 65, section 20. But regarding crumbs that are visible, they are certainly forbidden as long as they were not nullified in the majority. Taz at the end of this siman. Lechem HaPanim, section 1. Binyamin Zeev, section 1. However, in places where worms are found in water or flour, it is forbidden to buy from gentiles even pat palter or leaven because of worms if they are not careful about cleanliness and do not strain the water and do not sift the flour. And see above, siman 84, sections 115 and 116. And regarding challah, if one bought leaven from a gentile, see below, siman 324, seif 11, regarding whether the leaven is exempt — see there.
