Treifah. And Shach wrote: that is before completing shechitah of the esophagus; but after he slaughtered the majority — kosher, even for us who disqualify all laws of shechitah in the last minority — there it is in the minority that remained by itself; but what was already slaughtered is kosher. And further this case is different, for disqualification through shechitah did not occur at all. And even if he had not yet slaughtered the trachea at all — so ruled Maharshal and all latter authorities, not like Bach who is stringent on this.
