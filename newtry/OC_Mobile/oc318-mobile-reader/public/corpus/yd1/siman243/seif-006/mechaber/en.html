It is a grave sin to disgrace Torah scholars or to hate them. Whoever disgraces the Sages has no share in the World to Come and is included in the verse, “For he has despised the word of Hashem.” Rama: It is forbidden to make personal use of one who teaches halachot, and certainly of one who teaches Talmud, meaning the Gemara. One should give precedence to serving one who teaches halachot before serving one who teaches Gemara. This is from Rabbeinu Yerucham, netiv 2.
