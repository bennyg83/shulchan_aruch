That there is no sage in our generation now. Shach in Choshen Mishpat end siman 1 wrote that in the responsum of Mahari"t s.s. 47 he disputes this and rejects with both hands, and holds there is law of Torah scholar regarding litra deDahava — see there. And see in the responsum of Knesset Yechezkel part Choshen Mishpat siman 95 who challenged Semag siman 15 s.k. 4 who wrote there is no Torah scholar in our time regarding overturning in his merit — it is not so, for the Rambam spoke only about judging alone or releasing vows (the Rambam's words in chapter 6 of Laws of Oaths law 5 were omitted, who wrote explicitly that every distinguished sage in his generation is a yachid mumcheh for vows — only we do not hold so, as above siman 228 seif 1) or litra deDahava — in this there is no Torah scholar nowadays; and likewise regarding judging for himself, etc.; however if there is no Torah scholar, at least a Torah scholar-burning exists, etc.; and at the end of his words he wrote he received from his teachers that nowadays each one takes authority for himself, etc.; and all the more all books in print and whoever has understanding — all the more learns from a book and says "I found, all my desire" — there is no law of Torah scholar-burning, and kal vachomer Torah scholar; and the matter is not divided between lamdan and lamdan; and from this reason we found our hand that not all learners are exempt from tax, only according to the appearance of the eyes of the city's good people; however one who is appointed to the public and many accepted him as their rabbi and establishes a study hall and yeshiva — certainly he has full law of Torah scholar, and even regarding that he judge alone when the public appointed him — permitted to judge — see there.
