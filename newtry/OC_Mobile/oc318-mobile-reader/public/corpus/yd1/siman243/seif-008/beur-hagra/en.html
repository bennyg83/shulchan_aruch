Some say that nowadays, etc. This is like the rule concerning a litra of gold, and additionally the generations have deteriorated. Tur, siman 334, cites the Yerushalmi: an elder who placed someone under nidui for his own benefit, even if technically justified, the nidui is not valid. Meaning, where he seeks financial gain, etc. In chapter 2 of Bava Metzia 23-24, the Gemara lists three matters, etc., and asks the practical difference, etc.; and in chapter 15 of Shabbos 114a, "Who is a Torah scholar? One who is particular," etc., and "in our generation," etc. See Mahariv, siman 163, who discusses this at length. Everything depends upon the circumstances. This is why the Rama writes, "and some disagree, provided," etc.
