The sage himself would excommunicate for his honor an am ha'aretz who treated him with contempt, and neither testimony nor forewarning was required, and they do not release him until the sage agrees; and if the sage died, three come and release him; and if the sage wished to forgive him and did not excommunicate him, the matter is in his hands: {Rama: Some say in our time a Torah scholar cannot excommunicate for his own honor nor judge his own case. (Maharik and Mahariiv there and in Piskei Maharai siman 295.) And some disagree, provided he is established and fit for this. (Rivash and Maharad Cohen.) (And see below end of siman 334.)}
