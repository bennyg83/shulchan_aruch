The mitzvah of escorting the dead. In it are 2 seifim.
When a dead person is in a city, all townspeople are forbidden in work, for everyone who sees a dead person and does not escort him until he has all his needs is under the ban; and if there are groups in the city where each engages with the dead on its day, permitted on a day that is not its day—this is when not at the time of taking him out; but at the time of taking him out, all are idle, as below.
