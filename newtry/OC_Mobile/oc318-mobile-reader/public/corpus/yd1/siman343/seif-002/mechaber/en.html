In a small village they do not greet one another peace when the dead is in the town. {Rama: And how much more they do not greet peace when there is a dead person on the cemetery (writings of Maharar Asherlin siman 25 in the name of Sefer Chasidim); but when there is no dead there they greet from four cubits from the grave (in Tosafot of Alfasi chapter 3 of Moed Katan, and all brought in the name of Ram).}
