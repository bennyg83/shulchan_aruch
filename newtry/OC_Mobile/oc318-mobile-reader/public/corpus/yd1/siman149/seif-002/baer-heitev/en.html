A city. One who is not a resident of the city may not enter it even if three or four Jews live there, and we do not presume that he entered to speak with one of them. It appears, however, that where a minyan lives there, even if there are not ten householders but the number is completed by youths and servants, they are called a community and entry is permitted. This is the Shach's ruling.
A matter. This applies even when it is not the day of their festival. The Rashba writes concerning a city that it may possibly be forbidden to enter even for communal business and even for preservation of life. The Ran, however, maintains that it is permitted in a case of danger to life. See siman 150, seif 3, and the end of siman 157.
