As we said; and so in the Rosh 11 ; from what the Gemara says they were not concerned about idolatry money; and what is different from other festival days that are forbidden only because of "he goes and acknowledges" or lifnei iver, etc. ; rather a fair for idolatry, etc.; and so in the Jerusalem Talmud, for we read there: a city that has idolatry within it ; within it is forbidden because it has an image; within it is forbidden ; meaning: it was taught regarding a fair ; see there (end).
(Collected) Permitted, etc. And so to borrow and lend; and as we said; and the Rif and poskim omitted all the give-and-take there in the question of Atalutza, etc., because we hold like the first version; and further because Rava and Rabba bar Ulla each add on the previous to be lenient ; Rava says because of idolatry money it is unnecessary, for idolatry money is permitted; and so that which we say there 33a "rather gentiles, etc." ; to establish even Abaye; but we hold like Rava and the Ran; and Rabba bar Ulla says that Rava's statement is obvious, for if the gentile were dragging with it he would not stand on his festival day outside it ; unlike Rashi who explains everything stringently; and see there in the Ran at length (end). (Collected) Permitted to go outside, etc. The language of the Rambam ; and it is wondrous, for the mishnah deals with borrowing and lending, and afterwards it says "what about going there" ; except the Rambam explains the latter regarding passing through there, as he explains onwards "one who goes, etc."; and so Rashi there ; end of passage; and so in the Gemara there the Sages were not concerned, etc.; and so the Raavad challenged from the Tosefta ; see there in hasagot (end).
