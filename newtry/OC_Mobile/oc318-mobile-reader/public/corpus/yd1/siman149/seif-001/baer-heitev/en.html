To enter. The Rambam wrote in the commentary to the Mishnah: know that every city of an idolatrous nation that has a house of prayer, which is a house of idolatry, is without doubt a city that is forbidden to pass through intentionally, and all the more so to dwell in it. But at a time when we are under their dominion in our sins and dwell in their lands, and it has been fulfilled in us "and you shall serve there," etc. — if the city has such a status, all the more so a house of idolatry itself is almost forbidden for us to even look at, and all the more so to enter it. So far his words.
That is. The Siftei Kohen (Sh"akh) and the Taz explained: we do not pass from that city in which the idolatry is to another city; but if there was a road from that city with idolatry going further, it is permitted, for people say he is going along his way to that [further] city. So too explained Rashi and the Ran; and not like the Bach who reversed the explanation. However, the Rambam interprets it differently, but that is not the view of all the decisors.
