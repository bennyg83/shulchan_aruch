As the Rambam in the Submarine, it is known that every city of the Starwork Nation that will be brought to them a place of worship that is the house of the stars in which the city is forbidden to pass in it, and that it is “for a generation in it, but when we are under their hands in our seasons and in their countries, we are disconnected from what Hashem has and worked there, and if the city is no work in it.”
Namely. Shach and Taz explain that one may not pass from the city in which the idolatry is located to another city by way of that place. But if his route already continues onward from the city containing the idolatry, it is permitted, because people will say that he is merely traveling along his normal route to the other city. Rashi and Ran explain likewise, unlike Bach, who reversed the matter. Rambam explains the case differently, but that is not the view of the other poskim.
