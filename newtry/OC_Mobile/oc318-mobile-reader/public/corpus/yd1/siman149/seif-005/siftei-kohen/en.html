That we say he sold idols. For if not, why would he go there.
With a gentile etc. For we do not assume he sold idols there — Tur; but if he was selling idols there it would be forbidden, as above siman 144, since proceeds of idolatry in a gentile’s hand are presumptively forbidden unless it is known he sold it not to buy another idol with the proceeds; and so Derushah — unlike the Taz who wrote: even if you say he sold an idol, we said above that proceeds of idolatry in a gentile’s hand are permitted — end, since he follows the Rif that Beit Yosef brought; but we do not rule this way as stated in siman 144.
