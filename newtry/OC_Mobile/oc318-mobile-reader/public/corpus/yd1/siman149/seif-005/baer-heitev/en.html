They say. For otherwise why would he go there? But with a non-Jew we do not assume he sold idolatry there. But if it was known that he sold idolatry there, even with a non-Jew it would have been forbidden — as stated above in siman 144, that the money from idolatry in the hands of a non-Jew is presumptively forbidden unless it is known he sold it without intending to purchase other idolatry with the proceeds. And not like the Levush who followed the Rif's view that the money from idolatry in the hands of a non-Jew is permitted — we do not rule this way. So the Siftei Kohen (Sh"akh).
