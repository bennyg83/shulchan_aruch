Or where it is known etc. The Maharik root 155 wrote: specifically where money leaves the Israelite's pocket for idolatry; but where there was previously a customs tax and they had not bought anything from it for idolatry, and then a minister arose and said the Jews' money shall be given to idolatry — but they did not add to the Jews' burden for this reason to pay more than they paid before — it is permitted, for it does not leave the Israelite's pocket but the minister's pocket, to whom the customs belongs — see there at length.
