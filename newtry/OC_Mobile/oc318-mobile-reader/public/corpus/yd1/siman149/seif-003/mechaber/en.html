One may go to the fair of non-Jews and buy from them animals, slaves and maidservants, houses, fields, and vineyards, and he may write and file [the documents] in their courts. In what case is this said? When he buys from a homeowner who does not pay customs; but one who buys there from a merchant is forbidden, because he pays customs (and even if they waive the customs for him, it is forbidden) (Tur). And the customs goes to idolatry. If he transgressed and bought an animal, he cuts off its hooves from the knee and below. If he bought garments and vessels, they should rot. If he bought money or metal vessels, he should take them to the Dead Sea. If he bought a slave, we do not raise him up and we do not lower him.
