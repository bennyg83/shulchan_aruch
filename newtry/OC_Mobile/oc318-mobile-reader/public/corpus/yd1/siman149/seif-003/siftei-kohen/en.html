Forbidden. For he benefits from the customs exemption they give him — Tur and Ran; and the Bach ruled this is specifically regarding individuals, but when they grant freedom equally to all people there is no prohibition; but if they give the customs exemption only when one places a crown on his head in honor of idolatry — even if they give to all people it is forbidden — see there where he elaborated; and see above siman 143.
He brings them to the Dead Sea.
And in the case where the customs exemption is given to an individual — which is forbidden because he benefits — it appears from the Bach's words that it suffices to send the benefit of the customs to the Dead Sea; see there.
