To lead the prayers. Orach Chaim, siman 131, states that Tachanun is not recited in a mourner's house throughout all seven days. The Rokeach writes that after the conclusion of the prayers Tachanun is recited. Hallel is not recited there on Rosh Chodesh because the state of acute mourning is present, and therefore the verse, "The dead do not praise God," is not said, due to mocking the helpless. The Bach writes that during the Days of Awe, if the mourner is fitting and no one comparable is available, he has precedence to lead the prayers even within thirty days for his father or mother. This is likewise found in a gloss, unlike those who object. This is the Taz. See siman 131.
