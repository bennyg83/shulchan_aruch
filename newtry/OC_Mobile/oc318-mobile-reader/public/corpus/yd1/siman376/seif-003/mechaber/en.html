A deceased who has no mourners to be comforted — ten fit men come and sit in his place all seven days of mourning, and the rest of the people gather upon them; and if there were not ten fixed there each day, each day ten from the rest of the people gather and sit in his place: {Rama: And I have not seen people practice thus; and it is written in Maharil that the custom is to pray with ten all seven days in the place where the person died; and this is regarding a person who left no known relatives to mourn him; but if he has somewhere that they mourn him, it is not needed (and so it is fit to practice).}
