Kaddish. See Chavot Yair responsa siman 222, who wrote regarding one who died without a son and instructed that his daughter recite kaddish. By strict law she may say it, for with a daughter too there is benefit and satisfaction for the soul; nevertheless, one must be concerned that this will weaken the force of Jewish customs, and since it is public, one should object; see there. See Noda BiYehudah Tinyana, Orach Chayim siman 8, regarding what he wrote there about the investigation of heavenly judgment, etc. In my humble opinion, apart from that, it is obvious that one should not distinguish whether he died before age twenty. He himself wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 164 that after death a person is punished for all his deeds, even from the age of pe'utot, once he knows that the act is a transgression. What they said in Shabbat 89, that Yitzchak said, "subtract twenty, for You do not punish them," refers to while they are still alive; see there. See Chatam Sofer responsa siman 155, some of which was brought above in siman 185 seif 4, and what I wrote there. See also Chavot Yair responsa in the omissions, p. 156, and Tiferet Tzvi responsa, Orach Chayim siman 5 note 6.
