They are accustomed to pray with ten all seven days. In Orach Chaim siman 131 he wrote that tachanun is not said in a mourner’s house, meaning all seven days. The Rokeach wrote that after the conclusion of the prayer they say tachanun, and on Rosh Chodesh they do not recite Hallel there in the mourner’s house, because there is aninut there; therefore they do not say “the dead do not praise God,” because of mocking the poor; end quote. My teacher and father-in-law, of blessed memory, wrote that during the Yamim Noraim, if the mourner is worthy and no one like him is found, he has precedence to lead the prayer even within thirty days for his father or mother. So it is found in a gloss, unlike those who are particular otherwise.
