One who comes to honor his fellow and to rise before him tells him, Sit — except if he is a mourner or sick person, for it implies: sit in your mourning, sit in your illness: {Rama: One should not say, I have not been repaid according to my deeds, or similar things, lest he open his mouth to the Satan (so it appears in the chapter Mi SheMeto and in Hagahot Alfasi there); and one should not say to a mourner, What do you have to do, for it is impossible to change — for this is like blasphemy, as it implies that if it were possible to change he would do so; rather he should accept upon himself the decree of Heaven with love (Nimmukei Yosef, chapter Shor SheNagach, as it appears from the Gemara).}
