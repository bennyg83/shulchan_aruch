If he said to his fellow, “Konam your house that I enter,” “your field that I take” — if he died, or sold them, or gave them to another, he is permitted. But anything that is in his possession is forbidden, even if he sold it and afterward reacquired it, or if it fell down and he rebuilt it, even if he did not rebuild it in its original place. {Rama: If he said to him, “Konam your father’s house that I not enter it” — even if the father died, it is forbidden, for all the descendants of his loins are called “father’s house” (Mordechai, beginning of perek of Gittin; and Orach Chayim, siman 146, and se’if katan 12).}
