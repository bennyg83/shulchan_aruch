Who sold them. This permission applies only where he sold them to another person. If he sold them to the person who made the vow, it is forbidden because the latter benefits from property that came to him through the prohibited house. Rivash.
And rebuilt it. If he purchased a completely different house, it is permitted. His intent concerned the house he owned when he vowed, whether unchanged or rebuilt, not an entirely different house. Shach.
His descendants. The phrase 'your father's house' is interpreted as the father's descendants. Accordingly, sale of the physical house permits it, though she remains forbidden to visit his descendants. Shach questions whether this interpretation applies to the wording, 'Konam your father's house that I enter,' which more naturally refers to the actual house.
