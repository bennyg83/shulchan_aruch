Or he gave them, etc. Ran 47a, s.v. “He died,” etc. This is likewise explicit there on 35a, “that was given,” etc., as stated in seif 8.
Even if he sold it, etc. This is derived from the case below where the house fell. It is not comparable to the statement in the fourth chapter of Bava Basra (63a) concerning a Levi who sold a field and then repurchased it, etc., as stated in Choshen Mishpat, siman 209, seif 7, because there the ruling is based on an implied reservation. See Rivash there.
Or, etc. This appears in Ran there. A phrase is missing there from the wording of the Yerushalmi. See Rosh there.
And even, etc. Ran there.
He said to him, “Konam,” etc. See Tosafos, Gittin 21b, s.v. “That it not,” etc.; Kiddushin 5a; and Eruvin 15b. This is clear.
