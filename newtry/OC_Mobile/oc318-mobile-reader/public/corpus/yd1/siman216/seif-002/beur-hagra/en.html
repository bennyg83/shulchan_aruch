Such as wheat, etc. See the second chapter of Kilayim, “until it becomes worm-eaten,” and Rambam there; also the ninth chapter of Terumos, Mishnah 6, s.v. “Rabbi Yehudah,” etc.
Such as garlic, etc. The Mishnah in Terumos cited there, according to the first tanna.
And likewise liquids, etc. There, 52b, this remains an unresolved question, and we rule stringently. In this respect there is no distinction whether its seed is consumed. The same applies to their substitutes, which are permitted, unlike Shach.
And likewise if, etc. See Baer HaGolah. This is the wording of the Tur, and Rambam rules accordingly. See what is written in Orach Chaim at the end of siman 443.
