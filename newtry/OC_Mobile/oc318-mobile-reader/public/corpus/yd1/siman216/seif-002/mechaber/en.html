If he said, “Konam what I eat or taste from them” — if it is a matter whose seed is consumed when sown, such as wheat and the like, he is permitted in their substitutes and growths. And if it is a matter whose seed is not consumed when sown, such as garlic and onions — even the growths of growths are forbidden, and likewise the liquids that come from them are forbidden. And likewise if he said, “Konam, these fruits are upon so-and-so” — he is forbidden in their substitutes and growths.
