Such as wheat. — and the same applies to tree fruits — Hagahot Derishah in the name of Maharshal.
Permitted in their substitutes, etc. — Derishah and Perishah wrote that their substitutes are permitted even when the seed is not consumed; and so it is explicit in Shulchan Aruch; and so it appears from Rosh and Ran; but nothing is implied from their words; and even in Tur the opposite appears; therefore it seems that since the growths are forbidden, they equated and also forbade the substitutes.
Forbidden to Ploni. — in all manners as was explained, just as he forbade upon himself.
