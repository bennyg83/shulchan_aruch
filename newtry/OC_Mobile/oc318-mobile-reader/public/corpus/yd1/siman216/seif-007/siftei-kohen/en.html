Konam this house, etc. — it implies even though he did not say "in my life and my death" it is forbidden; and it appears that if it fell and was rebuilt it is permitted, as above seif 5.
The one who forbids something that is his, etc. — specifically when the prohibition begins while it is still in his possession; but if he said konam when it leaves my possession — he cannot forbid it — Bach; and it is obvious.
Permitted. — and it appears that if it fell and he rebuilt it, it is forbidden, since he said "my house" and it is still his house, as above seif 4.
A community, etc. — see above siman 228 seif 49.
And he should not take in the betrothed woman, etc. — and Beit Yosef in siman 228 writes about what is written “he should beware not to marry the betrothed” — requires investigation; Darchei Moshe brings it. Possibly the reason is lest her intention was that no wife be married to him at all now. But in Bedek HaBayit he emended “and not marry the betrothed” to “and not marry another upon her” — implying he thus found it in a responsum of the Rashba; and so is implied from the language of the Rashba there; and according to this it is permitted to take in the betrothed, only he should not marry another woman upon her; and so is implied in responsum of Rashba siman 812 — see there; and if so the Rav’s words require investigation.
