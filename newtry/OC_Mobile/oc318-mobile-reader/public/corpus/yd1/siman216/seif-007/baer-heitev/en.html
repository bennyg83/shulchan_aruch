Forbidden. This implies even if he did not say, 'during my life and after my death.' If the house fell and was rebuilt, it becomes permitted, as above. If he said the prohibition should begin only after the property left his possession, it cannot take effect because the prohibition must begin while the object is still under his authority. Shach.
Permitted. If it fell and he rebuilt it, it remains forbidden because he said 'my house' and it is still his house. Shach.
It implies. Shach writes that this is not limited to the death of one party. Even while both live, permission from one may suffice, depending upon ordinary language. Other authorities require permission from both. See siman 228:40.
The betrothed woman. Shach cites Bedek HaBayit's emended text, according to which he may marry the betrothed woman but may not marry another in addition to her. Taz writes that the matter depends upon the exact wording used.
