One who forbids upon himself the wine of so-and-so, or of such-and-such a place — his law is like the law of one who forbids upon himself this wine: he is forbidden in a dish into which it fell if it has the taste of wine. And the same law applies if that wine became vinegar after the vow by which it is forbidden to him. But what was already vinegar at the time of the vow is permitted (for he forbade wine upon himself, not vinegar) (Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of Rashba).
