In a cooked dish. The Shach wrote: it appears that this is presumed generally, but if the taste of wine or meat is bad for him and the like, we follow his intent, and he is forbidden in their taste, as below siman 218.
Taste. If there is another Jew who is permitted to taste it, he may rely on him; and if no other person is here, we estimate with sixty. However, all this is when it is not its own type, but with its own type, even in a thousand it is not nullified. Shach.
