The Ran wrote likewise, that what the Mishnah teaches there, 57, “but something whose seed does not disappear, even the growths of its growths are forbidden,” also refers back to the first clause, “Konam, these fruits, etc.” Although we hold that where this and that jointly cause something it is permitted, vows are different, for they resemble hekdesh, which is very stringent, as the Ran wrote. Alternatively, vows are different because they are a davar she-yesh lo matirin, and therefore zeh v’zeh gorem does not permit them.
