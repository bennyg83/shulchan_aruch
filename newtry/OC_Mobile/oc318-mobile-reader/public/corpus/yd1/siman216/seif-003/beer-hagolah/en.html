The Tur, and the Ran likewise writes this. The Mishnah's rule that where the seed does not decompose even later growths remain forbidden applies also to the opening clause, concerning one who says, "These fruits are konam," and so forth. Although we generally rule that where a permitted and forbidden cause jointly produce the result it is permitted, vows differ because they resemble hekdesh and are especially stringent, as the Ran writes. Alternatively, vows are matters that can become permitted through release, and therefore the rule of joint causation does not permit them.
