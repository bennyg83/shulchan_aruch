The work of your hands, etc. — let it not trouble you that "the work of your hands" has no substance — for it can be established as saying "konam your hands for their work" (see above siman 234 s.k. 85); alternatively, the work of his wife's hands after she performs it is a matter with substance. Nevertheless Ran wrote in the name of Rabbenu Yonah that he is forbidden only in what corresponds to his wife's work — for if his wife ground and baked and sold bread, what corresponds to her grinding and baking is his, and what corresponds to his wheat is his — end of Ran.
