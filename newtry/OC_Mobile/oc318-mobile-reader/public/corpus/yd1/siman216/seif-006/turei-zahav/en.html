We are uncertain whether to follow the expression, “your house,” in which case it becomes permitted if sold but remains forbidden if it falls, or to follow the word, “this,” in which case the reverse is true. We therefore rule stringently and forbid it in both cases. There are those who permit it if the owner dies, for they hold that “your house” is primary and we follow the first expression. There are those who forbid it in that case, for they hold that the latter expression, “this,” is primary.
