And so Rosh in the second chapter of Kiddushin 56b in the Mishnah there, and in Rosh here in his rulings it is written "and in their growths and in substitutes of substitutes" — and it is evident that it is a scribal error, and likewise in the marginal notes he did not write them:
And yes, the judgment is so. "Arise," he says, "the Lord's name is the name of the Lord, and the Lord's name is the name of the Lord." He wrote that he was speaking from the words of the Lord in his mouth, and that he was called in the midst of his words, and that it was the source of his name:
