(Siman 216, Taz seif katan 2) This is difficult, and so forth. He erred here. How could one say that substitutes of substitutes are prohibited? Nedarim 46b explicitly proves they are permitted, and the same is explicit in Rosh at the end of Perek HaIsh Mekadesh. The wording in the printed rulings of Rosh, "forbidden in their substitutes, their growth, and the substitutes of their substitutes," is a scribal error, because Rosh is copying the Mishnah and the Mishnah does not say this. Proof is that otherwise he should have written "their substitutes and substitutes of substitutes and their growth," rather than interrupting the sequence with growth. This is clear.
