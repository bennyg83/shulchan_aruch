(Siman 216, Taz, seif katan 2) This is astonishing, etc. He erred in this. How can one say that exchanges of exchanges are forbidden? The Gemara in Nedarim 46b explicitly proves that they are permitted, and this is likewise explicit in the Rosh at the end of the chapter HaIsh Mekadesh. The text in the rulings of the Rosh stating, “forbidden in their exchanges, their growths, and exchanges of their exchanges,” contains a scribal error, because the Rosh copied the Mishnah, and the Mishnah does not say this. The proof is that otherwise he should not have interrupted with “their growths,” but should have written, “forbidden in their exchanges, exchanges of their exchanges, and their growths.” This is obvious. The number 100 is preserved for the source index.
