“This loaf of mine is forbidden,” etc. The Beit Yosef also wrote that he found this reading in the Tur. In older editions of the Tur, however, the word “this” does not appear, and it reads only, “my loaf.” This is also explicit in the Rosh’s commentary to Nedarim, and this is the correct reading. The entire reason that it becomes permitted if the owner dies and the loaf falls to him as an inheritance is that he did not say, “this.” Had he said, “this loaf of mine,” its law would be like, “this house,” in seif 7.
