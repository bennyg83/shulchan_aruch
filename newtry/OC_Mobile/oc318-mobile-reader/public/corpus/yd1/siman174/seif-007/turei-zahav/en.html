Buyer cannot eat fruits. Meaning from time doubt arose; and what he wrote even with condition — seems meaning: even if he says I will deduct afterward from account — this helps above seif 5; here does not help since he concludes until it is known sale will stand — implies all while sale not clear — no condition helps, for otherwise let him say until he says I will deduct from account; and what is difference from that case seif 5 where condition of deducting from account helps — no difficulty, for master of this seif is Nemukei Yosef in name of Rashba who also holds above condition of deducting does not help — for he wrote there in mishnah: he sold him the field, etc. — from Gemara we say they divide fruits and did not say both eat; and on condition to return afterward — implies on condition to return forbidden, for otherwise need not say they divide — wherever we say divide — specifically and has no remedy except thus; therefore where he bought and did not need money — buyer cannot eat fruits, etc. — end quote; and if he held that case seif 5 where remedy of deducting from account helps — still difficulty why they said divide specifically; rather certainly he holds this too is in category ribbit on condition to return; and he does not hold like that case seif 5 which is Tur's view; wonder on Beit Yosef who did not notice this dispute, for he wrote Tur's words agree with Nemukei Yosef in name of Rashba — we already proved not so; regarding law they are equal this law in this seif with seif 5 — if condition of deducting helps there, here too; and likewise reverse; for Nemukei Yosef there equated them — only for Rashba in both no condition helps; for Tur condition of deducting helps in both. Levush wrote: however it seems to me if he eats also intending to deduct from value — sales return — permitted, for this is not similar to what we said seif 4 where here is ribbit doubt — end quote; and there is error in his words why he wrote this is not similar to seif 4 — meaning what we say here seif 5 — for truly there too condition helps; possibly copyist error and should read: even if he ate intending to pay — forbidden seif 4, etc.; and this certainly not all hold; regarding law since ribbit on condition to return is rabbinic matter — may be lenient here too; and condition of deducting helps in both, not condition I will pay him — as above.
