One who rents from his fellow a house or field for a small matter per year, and he advanced to him rent of 20 years, and they stipulated that after 1 or 2 years, if the lessor wants to return to him, he will deduct the rent of the time that the land was under his hand as they fixed, and he will return to him his land — it is permitted. And in this way, responsibility for the land regarding collapse or fire is on the owner of the land.
