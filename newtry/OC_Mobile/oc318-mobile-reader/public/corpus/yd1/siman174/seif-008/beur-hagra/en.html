One who rents, etc. Beit Yosef.
And he advanced, etc. Meaning, although he rents it more cheaply because of the advance payment, this is permitted, as stated in the Mishnah, 65a. The stipulation is also permitted, since even with a mortgage such an arrangement is permitted, as explained above, siman 172, seif 1. This is unlike the case in seif 1, where the lender returns all the money; here he deducts an amount corresponding to the years during which the tenant occupied the property. The words, 'and in this manner,' etc., mean that this produces a practical distinction. With a mortgage, such a case is forbidden, as stated in siman 172, seif 3. With a rental, however, the law is that responsibility remains with the landlord, as stated in the chapter HaSho'el, and the concern of being near to profit, etc., does not apply here.
