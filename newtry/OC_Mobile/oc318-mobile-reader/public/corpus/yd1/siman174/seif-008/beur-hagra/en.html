The drunk as a. B:
And he preceded, etc. Meaning even though he rents for sake of advance for less — permitted as stated in mishnah folio 65 side 1; and condition too permitted, for even in mortgage permitted as above siman 172 seif 1; and some say to that which seif 1 there returns all money — unlike here where he deducts per years he dwelt; and he wrote: and in this manner, etc. — meaning there is difference: in mortgage forbidden in such case as stated siman 172 seif 3; unlike rental where law of rental is such that responsibility is on landlord as stated chapter HaSho'el; and close to profit does not apply here, etc.
