The renter, etc. Beit Yosef.
And he advanced, etc. Meaning: even though he rents for the purpose of early [payment] at a reduced [rate], it is permitted — as written in the mishnah, 65a; and the condition is also permitted, for even in a pledge-loan [such an arrangement] is permitted, as stated above, siman 172, seif 1. And it is not analogous to the case in seif 1, where he returns all the money — unlike here, where he deducts [the amount] corresponding to the years [the borrower] dwelt [in the property]. And it wrote "and in this manner, etc." — meaning: the practical difference is that in a pledge-loan such a case is forbidden, as written in siman 172, seif 3; unlike rental, where the law of rental is that responsibility rests with the landlord, as written in the chapter HaSho'el — and "close to profit, etc." does not apply here.
