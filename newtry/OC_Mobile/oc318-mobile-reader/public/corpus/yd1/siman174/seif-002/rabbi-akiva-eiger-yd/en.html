(There) Fixed ribbit. See Rivash (siman 305 and 364) brought in Beit Yosef here briefly: since possible house will be decided his retroactively as sale, e.g. he will not return money until limited time — and it seems one can say likewise here if he dies and heirs cannot return — as Tosafot wrote — therefore certainly cannot judge now as fixed ribbit as long as he did not return money and it was revealed sale void retroactively — then fixed ribbit; but beforehand only avak ribbit; and all he ate buyer cannot deduct — must first return all money and afterward we extract fruits he ate — see there well.
