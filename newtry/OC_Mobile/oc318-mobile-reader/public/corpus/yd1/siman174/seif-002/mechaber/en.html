He sold him a field for a maneh, and that same day he made for him a document that if he returns his money, he will return to him the sale document: if this condition was made before the sale or at the time of the sale, it does not have the law of a sale, but rather the law of a loan, and the buyer is obligated to return all that he ate. But if it was made after the transaction, even 1 hour [later], this is a complete sale, and even if he made him a document about this with acquisition and with all types of strengthening, it is an act of kindness that he wants to act kindly with him and return to him his purchase (a responsum of Rashba; Hagahos Ashiri in the name of R' Chananel; and Beit Yosef in Choshen Mishpat siman 207 seif 11 according to Rambam).
