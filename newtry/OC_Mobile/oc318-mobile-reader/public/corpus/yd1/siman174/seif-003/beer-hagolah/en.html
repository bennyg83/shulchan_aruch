The incident there on 67a involving the woman who said to a certain man, etc.
There, in accordance with Ravina, who issued a practical ruling, calculated the amount, and ordered the produce returned, in accordance with Rif's opinion in his Halachot. Rambam likewise ruled this way in chapter 6 of the Laws of Creditor and Debtor. This is also the opinion of Rashba's students, who wrote that Razah held likewise.
