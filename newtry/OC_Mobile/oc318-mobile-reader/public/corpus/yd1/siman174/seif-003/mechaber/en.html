One who said to his fellow, “Acquire for me a field from so-and-so,” and the seller said to the agent, “I am selling to you on condition that you return it to me when I have money,” and the agent responded, “You and the buyer are friends; compromise in good [faith]” — he sells only on this condition, and one must return it, and we extract from him fruits that he ate. (And see Choshen Mishpat siman 207 regarding which condition nullifies a sale.)
