And we extract from him fruits he ate. Tur wrote: fruits he ate — avak ribbit and does not come out in court — end quote; Beit Yosef challenged: explicitly Gemara concludes in this law Ravina acted, considered, extracted fruits — unlike Ravah bar Rav Huna — evidently fixed ribbit; and so Rif and Rambam wrote; and Rosh wrote Ravina extracted fruits because forgiveness in error — he ate fruits in error thinking complete sale; not because of ribbit for avak ribbit; and he resolved: Tur too intended here that from ribbit side we do not extract, but from forgiveness in error we extract; and many challenged: if so Tur should have written this; this is no difficulty, for Tur already wrote siman 164 all he ate because of forgiveness in error we extract; and here he comes only to inform there is no prohibition from ribbit; practical difference where forgiveness in error does not apply — e.g. he knew there is no sale and nevertheless left him to eat — one would say he must pay from fixed ribbit — teaches not fixed ribbit but avak ribbit; and as above correct; and Maharach wrote Tur disagrees with Rosh and ruled not like Ravina but like other amoraim in Gemara who hold avak ribbit — very puzzling how Tur would disagree with Rif, Rambam, Rosh and not bring at least their view; and further in brief rulings of Rosh chapter One Who Borrows he ruled explicitly avak ribbit — implies Rosh too law is thus; rather as Beit Yosef's words are essence; and as stated here for law: truly we extract because of forgiveness in error — majority poskim hold thus as Beit Yosef wrote.
