The laws of one who sells to his fellow on condition to return; it contains 8 se’ifim.
If he sold a field to his fellow and said to him, “When I have money, return the land to me,” he does not acquire, and all the fruits that he ate are fixed interest, and we extract them in beis din. {Rama: And even if he did not stipulate so, but the custom in the city is that anyone who buys must return when the seller has money — for anyone who sells sells with intent of the custom — and it is forbidden for the buyer to eat the fruits (Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of Rashba).} But if the buyer said of his own accord, “When you have money I will return the land to you,” it is not a condition, and the sale takes effect immediately; therefore the buyer eats fruits. But if, when the buyer said so, the seller pressed it from him and strengthened it, it is a condition. But whenever the buyer did it — even within the sale — and the seller was silent and did not strengthen it, this is not a condition, but mere words, and the sale is a sale, since the seller did not do it, which he should have done. {Rama: And likewise, if he sold him land “from now,” and the buyer waited for him for the money, the buyer is permitted to eat the fruits and the seller is forbidden, for he is eating as payment for delaying his money (Tur).}
