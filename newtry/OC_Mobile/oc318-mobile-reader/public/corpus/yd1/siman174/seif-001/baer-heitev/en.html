Fixed. Shach writes that according to those who classify a mortgage without deduction as avak ribbit, the same applies here. Beit Yosef and Rama already stated this above, and Rama relied upon that ruling.
A condition. Since the seller did not make the condition. Perishah limits this to a statement made before completion of the sale. If made when the sale begins, even a purchaser's condition may be effective. The rules in Choshen Mishpat 207 determine whether the sale stands; if the sale remains valid, the arrangement is permitted, and if not, it is forbidden.
From now. The authorities write that the same applies without the explicit words 'from now' if the buyer took possession or otherwise acquired the property properly, while the money remains an oral debt owed to him. Shach.
