It is not a condition.
Since the seller did not stipulate; and the Derishab wrote that this is specifically where he says so before the conclusion of the transaction; but if he said so at the time when the transaction begins — even if the buyer stipulated it is effective, as in CM siman 207 end; and likewise Bach; and see there in siman 207 which condition voids the transaction and which does not; and from there apply here — wherever the transaction stands it is permitted, and if not, it is forbidden.
And likewise if he sold him land. And said to him “acquire from now, and the money shall be a loan with you the buyer” etc. — Tur; and the poskim wrote that the same applies if he did not explicitly say “from now” but rather the buyer took possession or acquired properly, and the money is an oral loan on the buyer; and Beit Yosef brings them; and such are the words of the Rabbi.
