When I have money. See responsum of Noda BiYehudah second edition vol. 14 siman 75 who wrote: were I not afraid I would say specifically in this language implying when he returns money to him sale is void retroactively and money was loan in his hand; but if he said when I have money return and sell to me — even at time money returns there is no voiding of sale retroactively; until now it was complete sale — even one-sided ribbit there is none and permitted; all the more movables in such case there is more permit, for there is more concern of responsibility than houses and fields; he brings proof from what we hold appraisal returns to borrower and lender eats fruits meantime; and he again rejects this — for appraisal is in lender's hand to sell field against borrower's will and does not return unlike here — see there; see responsum of Shemesh Tzedakah YD vol. 2 siman 62.
