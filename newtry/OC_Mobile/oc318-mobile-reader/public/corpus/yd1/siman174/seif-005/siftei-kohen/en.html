Both are forbidden. The seller should not eat them, lest the buyer complete the payment and it will turn out that he acquired the field retroactively, and the seller will have eaten the produce in exchange for the money that remained in the buyer's hand; and the buyer should not eat them, lest he not complete the payment and the field will not leave the seller's possession, and it will turn out that he ate it in exchange for the money that was in the seller's hand — Tur.
And the same if the seller etc. That is to say that the seller says "I will eat them, and if the transaction is concluded I will deduct for him the value of the produce from the remainder" — it is permitted.
