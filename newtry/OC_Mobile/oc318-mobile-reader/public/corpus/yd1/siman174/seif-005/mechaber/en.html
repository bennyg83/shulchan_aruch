If the seller said to the buyer, “When you bring the rest of the money, acquire from now,” both are forbidden to eat the fruits; rather, they place them in the hand of a third party: if he brings the rest of the money, he gives them to the buyer, and if he does not bring, he returns them to the seller and he returns the money that he received. But if the buyer says, “I will eat them, and if the transaction is not concluded, when the seller comes to return me my money I will deduct the value of the fruits,” it is permitted; and the same applies if the seller says so. {Rama: And this is specifically when he said, “I will deduct for you from the money,” but if he said, “I will pay you for the fruits that I ate,” it is forbidden, for in the end he is taking interest, only that he goes back and pays him (Tur).}
