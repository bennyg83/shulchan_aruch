(Siman 303 seif 1 in the gloss) And there are those who say that if the wearer was shogeg, he need not tell him in the marketplace, for because of kevod habriyot he should be silent and not separate him from shogeg. In Responsa Noda BiYehudah, Orach Chaim part, siman 35, he proved that where if he does not separate him he will do an issur all his days, according to all it is forbidden to be silent because of kevod habriyot even regarding shogeg. However, in Sha'agat Aryeh siman 58 he ruled like the Rambam, that it is always forbidden to be silent because of kevod habriyot, for what his fellow will do as an act through his being silent and not separating him is considered an act. And so I have proven in my novellae to Shabbat (daf 40) and in my novellae to Sanhedrin (daf 73). But the Rosh according to his view does not hold this reasoning, as is proven from what he wrote in Shabbat (beginning of daf 3).
