That one may remove kilayim from his fellow even in the marketplace. In it are 2 seifim. One who sees Torah kilayim on his fellow—even if he was walking in the marketplace—jumps to him and tears it off him immediately, even if he was his teacher. (And some say: if the wearer was inadvertent, one need not tell him in the marketplace, for because of human dignity one should be silent and not separate him from his inadvertence (Tur in the name of the Rosh).) And if it was of their enactment, he does not tear it off him and does not remove it in the marketplace until he reaches his home (and likewise in the study hall one need not hurry to leave (Tur)); and if it was of Torah, he removes it immediately.
