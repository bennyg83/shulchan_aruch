The language of the Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilchot Kilayim, based on the statement of Rav Yehudah in the name of Rav in Berachot 19b, according to his reading of the Gemara there: "one who finds kilayim removes it," without reading "in his garment." It refers to one who finds kilayim on another person, as in the incident of Rav Adda bar Ahavah there on 20a. He concludes there that human dignity does not override a prohibition explicitly stated in the Torah.
