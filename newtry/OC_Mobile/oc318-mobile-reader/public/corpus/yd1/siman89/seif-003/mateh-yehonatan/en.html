(there seif 3 in the gloss) Waiter, etc. at the meal need not netilat yadayim. See Shach s.k. 20 and Peri Chadash who wondered at Beit Yosef and Rama who copied Rashi here that Rashi deals only with netilat yadayim at the meal; but wherever all netilat yadayim is because of what sticks to hands — certainly the waiter too needs netilat yadayim, see there; and in my humble opinion it appears to resolve: Beit Yosef did not intend to bring proof that the waiter even though he handles food need not netilat yadayim — that is plain, for from where would we say if one handles basar b'chalav not at eating time he needs netilat yadayim; or for this Beit Yosef did not need proof at all, for certainly he need not any netilat yadayim, for it teaches only about one who eats meat or milk — only that for the waiter one might say he properly needs netilat yadayim lest he come to eat from it; on this he properly brings proof from Rashi that we are not concerned lest he eat — and Beit Yosef's words are correct — investigate.
