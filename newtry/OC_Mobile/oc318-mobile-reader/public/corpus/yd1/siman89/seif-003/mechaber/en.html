One who ate a meat dish is permitted to eat afterward a cheese dish, and rinsing between them is only optional (and some require rinsing) (Sha'arim and hagahah ShaDa); but if he comes to eat the cheese itself after a meat dish, or the meat itself after a cheese dish — he must wash his hands: {Rama: And fat of meat has the law of meat itself (Rashba siman 310 and Mordechai and hagahah ShaDa); and now the custom is to be stringent not to eat cheese after a meat dish as after meat itself, and one should not change and breach the fence (Arukh and Beit Yosef); however if there is no meat in the dish only that it was cooked in a meat pot — permitted to eat cheese afterward (there) and there is no custom to be stringent; and so the custom is to eat meat after a dish that has cheese or milk; however one should wash hands between them, even if he will not eat actual meat but only a meat dish after a cheese dish if he touched them with his hands (in Sha'arim and hagahah Shaarei Dura); a waiter who serves at a meal and touches food does not need rinsing, for they required rinsing only for eaters (Beit Yosef in name of Rashi).}
