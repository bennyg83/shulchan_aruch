However waiter, etc. — Shach and Acharonim wondered this was said only for netilat yadayim at the meal and not for intermediate waters; and it appears Beit Yosef heaven forbid was not mistaken, only he held we do not find anywhere they enacted for one who touches meat in general not to touch cheese until he washes; and if you think so — why did they say for one who ate meat netilat yadayim is required for cheese — even touching would require netilat yadayim; only certainly they enacted only for the eater whose hands are very soiled, and also at eating they were more concerned with extra distance; and therefore the waiter who only touches is permitted — only lest you say they decreed lest he eat; on this he brings proof we see in netilat yadayim they did not decree so — learn they did not decree; and therefore here too no decree — and we return to the rule that we do not forbid touching what we never found — this is Beit Yosef's intent, it appears to me.
