(seif 3 in the gloss) And some require netilat yadayim. It appears without wiping and rinsing; and what Rama wrote not to eat cheese after a meat dish means cheese in substance, but a cheese dish after a meat dish is permitted with netilat yadayim alone; however in Tur Chaadash as Pithei Megadim explained Menachem Tzioni letter 10 his view there for meat dish then cheese dish requires also wiping and rinsing, and when reversed cheese first then meat wiping alone suffices; and in Menachem Tzioni there letter 11 he wrote in name of Maharshal: now they practice prohibition even for cheese dish after meat dish — hard to be lenient and appear before them while cleared and recited, end of his words.
