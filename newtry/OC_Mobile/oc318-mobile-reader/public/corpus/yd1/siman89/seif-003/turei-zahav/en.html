And fat of meat, etc. And it is written in Tur Chaadash general rule 77 in name of Hagahot ShaDa that even with goose fat one should be stringent so; and in Beit Yosef in Orach Chaim siman 173 he wrote in name of Hagahot Maimoniyot that meat broth has the law of meat; and Rabbenu Yonah in chapter Elu Devarim wrote it has the law of a meat dish since it is clear, but if thick like vegetables it has the law of meat.
