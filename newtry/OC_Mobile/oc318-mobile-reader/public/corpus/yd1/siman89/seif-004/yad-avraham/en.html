(there seif 4) Forbidden to eat cheese on a cloth where they ate meat. It appears on a long cloth it is permitted to eat on one side meat and on the other side cheese. Proof from what Mechaber wrote above siman 88 regarding two people eating one meat and one cheese on one cloth — if there is a heker between them it is permitted. And another proof from the Gemara chapter 25b (daf 107) and so Taz wrote siman 91: one wraps meat and cheese in one cloth provided they do not touch each other — investigate.
