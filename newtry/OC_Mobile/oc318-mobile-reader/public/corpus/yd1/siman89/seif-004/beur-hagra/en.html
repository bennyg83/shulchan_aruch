One who ate, etc. — Yerushalmi (chapter 6 of Pesachim): the law of one who ate chavitz and wants to eat kutach — he clears crumbs.
And forbidden, etc. — from the Yerushalmi cited and as written in Mishnah seif 7:2 and Gemara there, provided not, etc. — although the peel, etc.
And all the more, etc. — as written above siman 112:1 radish dragged, etc. and as written end of seif 96.
However, etc. — as written end of Avodah Zarah.
