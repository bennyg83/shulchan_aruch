However through sticking, etc. This refers to what was written that one wants to cut cheese with a meat knife; but what was written that it is forbidden to cut bread, etc. — one need only wipe the knife as appears in Beit Yosef in what he wrote in name of Orach Chaim in name of Rabbenu Shimshon — and there the reason is noten taam bar noten taam; and for us need not this, for it is explained in siman 96 that specifically with a sharp thing they say it absorbs because of pressure of the knife.
But already they practice, etc. It appears his intent: according to the custom it is forbidden to cut cheese with a meat knife even through sticking in the ground, for so they were stringent on themselves to take a separate knife for cheese; but nevertheless to cut bread with it to eat cheese — in this we may well rely on wiping it first — as it appears to me, plain.
