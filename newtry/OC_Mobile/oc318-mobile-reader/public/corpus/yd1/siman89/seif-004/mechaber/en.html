One who ate cheese and wants to eat meat must clear from the table the crumbs of bread they ate with the cheese; and it is forbidden to eat cheese on a cloth on which they ate meat (and likewise the reverse is forbidden) (as appears in Arukh); and all the more it is forbidden to cut cheese, even cold, with a knife they regularly use for meat; and not only that but even the bread they eat with cheese is forbidden to cut with the knife used for cutting meat: {Rama: And likewise the reverse is forbidden; however through sticking in hard ground it is permitted (Beit Yosef in name of Orach Chaim and Kol Bo); but already all Israel practice having two knives and marking one of them so it has a recognizable sign, and they practice marking the dairy one — and one should not change the custom of Israel.}
