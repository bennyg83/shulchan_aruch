One who ate cheese is permitted to eat meat immediately afterward, provided he examines his hands that nothing of cheese sticks to them; and if it is night and he cannot examine them well — he must wash them; and he must wipe his mouth and rinse it; and wiping is that he chews bread and wipes his mouth well with it; and so with anything he wishes except groats and dates and vegetables, because they stick in the crevices (meaning a place above the esophagus near the teeth) and do not wipe well; and afterward he rinses his mouth with water or wine. In what case is this said — with meat of domestic animal and wild animal; but if he comes to eat fowl meat after cheese he needs neither wiping nor rinsing: {Rama: And some are stringent even with other meat after cheese (Mordechai in name of Maharam and Beit Yosef in Orach Chaim siman 173); and so is the custom — that whenever the cheese is hard one does not eat afterward even fowl meat, as with cheese after meat (and so in Zohar); and some are lenient and one should not protest, only that they do wiping, rinsing, and netilat yadayim; however it is good to be stringent.}
