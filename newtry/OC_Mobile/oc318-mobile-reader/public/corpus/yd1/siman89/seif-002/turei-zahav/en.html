And some are stringent even in meat after cheese. Explanation: to wait six hours; and measure of hard cheese that Rama mentioned means six months passed on it or it is wormy — so Orach Chaim; and he wrote there there is no prohibition in meat after cheese, for only taste of meat in the mouth, since its essence is called meat, is subject to decree lest basar b'chalav — nevertheless from piety one should be scrupulous, end of his words; and it appears to me (even) for Rambam's reason I mentioned at the beginning of the siman that eating cheese after meat is because of meat between the teeth, but for taste drawn from meat in the mouth we are not concerned — one may say here for any cheese there is no prohibition to eat meat after it, for also meat between the teeth is not called meat unless the verse revealed "the meat is still between his teeth" as in the Gemara — but cheese between the teeth is not cheese at all; and for the reason I mentioned that it is because of fat, it is plain to forbid also wormy cheese whose taste lingers long in the mouth — if so one should be stringent; but it appears to me specifically wormy cheese one should be stringent so, and it is by law from the reason we mentioned; but aged cheese that is not wormy and was not made from milk set in the stomach to curdle milk but from mere milk and dried, or one eats butter — one should not be stringent more than wiping, cleaning teeth, rinsing mouth and hands, except one who practices added abstinence and scrupulousness — so it appears to me.
