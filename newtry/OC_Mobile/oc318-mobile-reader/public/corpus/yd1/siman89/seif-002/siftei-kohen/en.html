Must wash them. And Beit Yosef wrote in Orach Chaim siman 173, and so: it is written in Orach Chaim in name of Raavad that all matters practiced for mayim acharonim are practiced for intermediate waters between cheese and a dish, whether to be lenient or stringent — except rubbing on the body or wall or distraction, which disqualify intermediate waters but not mayim acharonim, because he still wishes to eat and his hands need guarding; and except drying the hands, for intermediate waters also require drying like the first waters; and except other liquids that are not valid for intermediate waters because they are oily and clear and do not clean the fat and food, end of his words — and so Tur Chaadash there; but Maharshal there disagreed and wrote: since all liquids are valid for mayim acharonim to clean filth, all the more they clean and remove moisture of food, end of his words. And it is not worthwhile in this reasoning to reject Raavad's words and those who follow him, especially to be lenient in a matter without any effort.
