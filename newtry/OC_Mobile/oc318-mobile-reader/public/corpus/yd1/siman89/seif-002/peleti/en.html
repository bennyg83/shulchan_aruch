And must, etc. — see Shach s.k. 16 who brought Raavad disqualifies liquids and Maharshal permits; and Tosafot s.v. fowl wrote per Rabbenu Tam's view need not wait from meal to meal — meaning without wiping and netilat yadayim, etc.; and it is difficult: the first is not difficult, for in the second meal he had to wash anyway for eating — he truly did not wash and therefore did not eat until another meal — and then he washed; but difficult: in the first meal at Birkat HaMazon he had to take mayim acharonim — so he already had netilat yadayim; strained to say the meal dealt with is bread in a pouch, etc. that need no netilat yadayim — that is not called a meal; and if he fixed a meal he must bless and also mayim acharonim — therefore must be they were accustomed to wash in other liquids like passing Sodomite salt — and this does not help intermediate waters between meat and cheese to remove oiliness — and Raavad's words are compelled; and perhaps Raavad inferred from this — investigate. And it appears what we distinguish between day and night is for cheese first; but for meat first for those who do not wait six hours, even by day washing is required lest something stuck — and with this Rabbenu Tam's words are understood: he wrote one who ate meat first is forbidden to eat cheese after without rinsing and wiping; but one who ate cheese first need not netilat yadayim nor wiping — Maharsha wondered: but netilat yadayim we say for Rabbenu Tam is required even for cheese first — and see what I wrote in his answer.
