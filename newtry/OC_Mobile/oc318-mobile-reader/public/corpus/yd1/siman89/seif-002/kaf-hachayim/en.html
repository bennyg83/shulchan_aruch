And within the time, even if there is no meat between the teeth it is forbidden because meat expels fat and draws taste a long time; and therefore the reason if they only chewed for a child need not wait since they did not eat it does not impart taste. Rambam gave reason for waiting because of meat between the teeth; per his words after waiting the measure it is permitted even if meat remains between the teeth; and one who chews for a child must wait; and good to follow stringencies of both reasons. Tur. Taz s.k. 1. Shach s.k. 2. And this too Shulchan Aruch ruled like both stringencies.
