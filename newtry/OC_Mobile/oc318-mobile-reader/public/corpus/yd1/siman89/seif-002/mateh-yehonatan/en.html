See Shach s.k. 10 who brought in name of Maharshal that intermediate waters are invalid in other liquids, etc.; and from the Talmud it is proven like Raavad who wrote in Orach Chaim that I brought above in section 1, and so: Rabbenu Tam explains one who ate meat is forbidden to eat cheese — meaning without netilat yadayim and mouth-wiping, etc.; and Mar Ukva who does not eat until another meal without netilat yadayim and mouth-wiping — according to his reasoning how is it sensible to say he does not eat until another meal without netilat yadayim and mouth-wiping, for in any case he must wash for Birkat HaMazon first and mayim acharonim are obligatory — so he has netilat yadayim; if so it must be Tosafot hold intermediate waters are invalid in other liquids and mayim acharonim are valid — and therefore even if he washed in other liquids for mayim acharonim, for intermediate waters it does not help at all.
