(there seif 3 in the gloss) Not to eat cheese after a meat dish like after meat itself. Logical proof shows not specifically cheese itself but even a cheese dish after a meat dish they practiced to be stringent — so Maharshal; but from the gloss below it does not appear to be stringent in this; and so Menachem Tzioni general rule 77 and Sefer Or Zarua in Orach Chaim siman 173 — this is the view of the gloss that wiping and rinsing help.
