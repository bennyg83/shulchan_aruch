(siman 89 seif 1 in the gloss) And some say need not wait six hours only, etc. And permitted through wiping and rinsing; and that wiping and rinsing are required even after Birkat HaMazon can be proven from the Talmud chapter 25 daf 107b s.v. fowl and cheese that he wrote there: say — how does it challenge from cheese first to meat later to cheese? And one may say it challenges thus: words of aggra imply fowl and cheese are eaten at an apikores meal without netilat yadayim and without wiping; but for other meat netilat yadayim is required — and how if meat first: even with netilat yadayim and mouth-wiping it does not suffice until another meal — unless from cheese first, end of his words; and if so it must be that Tosafot hold even for another meal wiping is required — otherwise it should have said we do not find wiping and rinsing; or that for cheese first too wiping and rinsing are required as Rama wrote there; but without Birkat HaMazon certainly waiting one hour does not help. And Magen Avraham in Orach Chaim wrote even for meat after cheese Birkat HaMazon is required; and it appears not like my view, for he says what are intermediate waters between meat and cheese — optional, meaning within the meal; and if so it must be Birkat HaMazon is not required — otherwise there is no case for intermediate waters to be optional at all, for before Birkat HaMazon one needs mayim acharonim at least — if so intermediate waters are without need; or Birkat HaMazon is not required at all between cheese and meat.
