Not to eat cheese after meat. In it are 4 seifim: One who ate meat — even of wild animal and fowl — may not eat cheese afterward until he waits six hours; and even if he waited the measure — if there is meat between the teeth he must remove it; and one who nurses a baby must wait: {Rama: And if he afterward finds meat between the teeth and removes it — he must rinse his mouth before eating cheese (Ran chapter 25). And some say one need not wait six hours but immediately if he cleared the table and recited Birkat HaMazon he is permitted through wiping and rinsing (Tosafot and Mordechai chapter 25 and Hagahot Ashiri and Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 9 Laws of Forbidden Foods and Ra'avyah); and the simple custom in these lands is to wait one hour after eating meat and then eat cheese; however they must also recite Birkat HaMazon after meat (per Arukh and hagahah ShaDa) — for then it is like another meal and permitted to eat per the lenient; but without Birkat HaMazon waiting an hour does not help, and there is no difference whether one waited the hour before or after Birkat HaMazon (logical opinion from Maharai and unlike Orach Chaim); and if he finds meat between his teeth after the hour he must pick and remove it (logical opinion from implication of Ran above); and some say one should not recite Birkat HaMazon intending to eat cheese (Arukh in name of Maharach); but we are not careful about this and some are meticulous to wait six hours after eating meat for cheese — and so is correct to do.}
