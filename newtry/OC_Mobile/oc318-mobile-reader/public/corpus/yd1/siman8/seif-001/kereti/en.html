Izmel was printed in Lechem haPanim book and he wrote in gloss, and these are his words: question—Reuven bought a knife from the official who executes those liable to death called talyon and gave craftsman to repair into shechitah knife—may one slaughter with it, for we hold sword is like corpse and imparts impurity to flesh slaughtered with it; one may say knife purified by immersion—end quote; and all his words are borit words; and I fear slaughterers will follow him in such nonsense—therefore I write: first, what of it if it imparts impurity—do we eat non-sacred in purity; and whatever knife you find is not impure with corpse impurity, and unfortunately we are all corpse-impure and all vessels and unfinished metal utensils enter corpse tent and corpse contact until R. Chiya Kohen sent to Rabbenu Tam who forbids priest to touch corpse-sword space “which house you will build for Me,” etc.; and if knife does not impart impurity to flesh—nevertheless by hand contact it becomes impure; and he added in borit to purify corpse-sword and vessel touching corpse by immersion—what requires third and seventh—and immersion does not help as explained in mishnah Ohalot; therefore mitzvah to erase from book and not mention and suffice.
