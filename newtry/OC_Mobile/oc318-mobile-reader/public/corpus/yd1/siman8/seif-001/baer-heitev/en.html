**That it is.** Explanation: only that he perform holacha u'va with it—and see below siman 24 seif 2.
**The izmel.** Question: Reuven bought a sword (talyon) with which capital cases were judged and gave it to a craftsman to repair into a shechitah knife—may one slaughter with it, for we hold a sword is like a corpse and imparts impurity to flesh slaughtered with it; one may say the knife is purified by immersion—so gloss Lechem haPanim; and I wonder what impurity applies nowadays, and if impurity applies what does immersion help—for it requires third and seventh; end quote.
**Similar to it.** Reason is in Taz: because small izmel slips and punctures at knife head—it implies with large knife we do not care when there is point on head; nevertheless custom no point even on large knife head.
**Necks.** Whether animal whether bird.
**14.** Maharshal and Bach ruled l'chatchila whether animal or bird one slaughters only with knife measure of two necks of that species—and see below siman 24 seif 2. Beit Hillel wrote: incident in Brisk of Lithuania—the slaughterer did not know hint of b'Zeh and they removed him for thirty days; Peri Chadash in kaf alef wrote it is nothing since he is expert in laws even if not expert in hints—one does not remove him for this.
