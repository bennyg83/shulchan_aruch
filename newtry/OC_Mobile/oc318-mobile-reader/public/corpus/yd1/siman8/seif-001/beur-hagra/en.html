**And it is that, etc.** There regarding izmel that is not, etc.—and Rambam explains in such case.
**And with a needle.** There.
**And one who cannot, etc.** For this is its measure even if he performed only holacha or only hava as written there.
**And some estimate, etc.** Midrash parashat Kedoshim and parashat Naso chapter 10 377d: rabbis said—knife showed them how much its length measure needed to be—how many “biZeh”—two, etc.—he said to them: in this order you shall live, slaughter, and eat.
