The law of one who cooks eggs and a repulsive thing found in a dish. In it are 2 seifim: One who cooks many eggs in their shells — he should not remove them from the water in which they were cooked until they have cooled, or he should put cold water on them to cool them, and afterward remove them — lest we fear that he will find an embryo in one of them; and if he were removing from them before they cooled — perhaps the one that has an embryo in it would remain with the last ones and would forbid them, because there would no longer be sixty there to nullify it: {Rama: And if he did not do so but poured them into a bowl and one of them was found to be tereifah — some forbid everything, lest the tereifah remained at the end and there was not sixty in the pot to nullify and what is in the pot became forbidden and in turn forbids everything in the bowl (Beit Yosef in name of Semag and Arukh); and likewise small fish in which an impure fish was found in the bowl if he did not pour them all at once into the bowl — then there is concern lest the issur remained at the end (Taz); and some permit in every case (Tur in name of Yesh Omrim) — for we do not establish an issur to say the issur remained without sixty (Beit Yosef in name of R"SH); and so is primary; and even according to those who forbid — one need not forbid the vessels in which it was cooked (Beit Yosef in name of Semag and Hagahot Maimoniyot) — for we maintain the vessel on its presumption.}
