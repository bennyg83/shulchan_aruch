(Siman 107 seif 1) One who cooks many eggs in their shells should not remove them from the water until they have cooled. Because we are concerned lest he find an embryo in one of them; and if he were removing from them — perhaps the one that remained, etc., with the last ones and would forbid them because there would no longer be sixty there to nullify it — one must say it is not specifically sixty, for sometimes sixty-one is required, as written siman 86 and siman 98 seif 7; and even though for Mechaber Shulchan Aruch who does not hold nat bar lichtamei in other issur as written siman 92 and end of siman 106 — if so the eggs that remained in the pot do not become nevelah, and when he pours everything to the bowl with sixty against the embryo suffices — nevertheless the eggs themselves become forbidden, as written end of siman 106 that the piece that became forbidden does not return to permission for all; and if so need that there be majority of permitted eggs against them to nullify them as law of dry in dry end of siman 109; and this is clear opinion of Shulchan Aruch; however opinion of Tur requires study, for according to end of siman 106 even the piece that became forbidden returns to permission — if so why did he write this law that follows Semag who holds nat bar lichtamei in other issurim — if so need sixty against all eggs that became forbidden; but Tur who holds we do not say nat bar lichtamei in other issurim and need not estimate except against the first issur absorbed in the piece — and when there is sixty also the piece returns to permission; and if so even if the embryo remained at the end and forbade the eggs that remained there — nevertheless when he afterward pours everything to the bowl, if there is only sixty against embryo all the eggs should be permitted; and I did not reach the depth of Tur's opinion on this — requires study.
