therefore I thought to clarify the matter briefly and include all their opinions herein, and what to add or subtract as seems to me; and this is in Semag and Tur and Turei Chayim general rule 51, who wrote in name of Semag: if many fish were cooked and an impure fish was found in the bowl — even though there is sixty in the bowl there is dispute between Ri Ba and Rabeinu Baruch whether there is concern lest the impure fish remained with the minority of fish in the pan and forbids others afterward, and likewise in eggs, etc.; and opinion of Rabeinu Baruch is not to forbid; nevertheless he wrote one should be stringent in eggs for it is not customary to cook many together, etc.; and regarding practical halachah it seems initially one should be stringent like Ri Ba; however b'dieved one may be lenient like words of Rabbeinu Shimshon who wrote reason to permit because we rely the issur is in the majority, etc., and in this the Acharonim pilpuled; and it seems certainly if a fish or egg is in the pan and he does not pour it into a bowl but only takes with a spoon the fish and eggs from the pot one after another and places them in a bowl — in this all the concern is perhaps the impure one lies at the bottom of the pot, and if so when he took the fish from the pot and gravy was at the end and there remains only that impure fish with 119 pure fish and no more — if so there is not sixty, for gravy conveys taste from fish to fish and there are no more fish to assist in nullifying it, and they became forbidden in this manner; certainly one should be stringent, for even R"SH who is lenient here agrees his whole reason to permit is because issur is in the majority, and here on the contrary if we say the issur was at the lip of the pot — that is the majority, for most fish were there and he took them with one spoon one by one and removed them outside, and the main point for stringency we say — certainly whatever separates from majority is presumed separated, and that he took with the spoon the fish and removed them outside — mostly he took those that are kosher until the issur remained at the end, as below siman 110, and we trapped them that they move — the Acharonim are forbidden because the issur is at the end (and words of Peri Chadash here are ambiguous); and if these 119 fish afterward mixed with fish in the pot — if among everything there are 119 pure fish, all permitted, for these 119 are nullified in majority which is sixty pure fish; but less than everything forbidden even according to Mechaber who does not hold in other issurim chadichah na'aseh nevelah — nevertheless the 119 fish that became forbidden through emission of impure fish are forbidden forever, for it is impossible to squeeze out the issur and they need nullification in majority; but when there are here 119 fish all permitted and we do not say these 119 fish forbade others through their emission, since there is no gravy there is no absorbed issur going from piece to piece without gravy; but if he poured from pot to bowl the fish with gravy as the order of pouring — if we know the impure fish was at the bottom of the pot there is no issur concern here, for he pours from below, bottom of pot upward according to the pot — if so if there is gravy at bottom of pot to convey absorbed from impure to 119 pure, how much more if there is gravy above it toward mouth of pot — if so all fish above join in nullification through gravy that conveys absorption in all of it, and if so issur is only concern to the contrary perhaps at mouth of pot lies the impure fish, and when he pours from below upward — if so all fish afterward in the row near mouth of pot already the gravy spilled and was poured to bowl and at the end there is gravy only on fish placed at mouth of pot and there lies impure fish and conveys the absorbed gravy to 119 pure fish, and fish below it cannot join in nullification because there is no gravy, and behold these 119 fish became forbidden; and if these 119 fish mixed with fish in the bowl, need also 119 fish so that 119 are nullified in majority as above; only there is another concern here, for he pours fish with gravy — if so gravy conveys taste of absorbed issur within the 119 forbidden fish as above to all fish in the bowl; gravy comes from pot, first vessel, and was poured on what is in bowl, second vessel, and it is pouring, and pouring we rule cooks and absorbs kedei kelipah — if so all fish peel became forbidden; and for this opinion of Shach and Peri Chadash that in truth all fish in bowl are forbidden only the emission on body of fish but kelipah is required (and that is for one who says chadichah na'aseh nevelah in other issurim, and if so all 119 forbidden fish forbid others; but according to Mechaber who does not say chadichah na'aseh nevelah, if so need only against emission of body of impure fish, and in this clearly kelipah of many fish in the bowl with joining will be sixty against the fish; and see Orach Chayim siman 447 regarding olives, etc.) — if so also opinion of Rabeinu Baruch agrees the peels are forbidden — what is this that he troubled to permit the forbidden fish that do not forbid others by touch since absorbed does not go from piece to piece without gravy — for fish from here in any case gravy forbids the peels and more than kelipah without this they do not forbid for it is only pouring; but perhaps for Maharshal who holds solid lump is judged like first vessel — if so even they are solid lump and forbid all fish as first vessel; but cannot be according to Rama who does not hold solid lump; also difficult if opinion of Rabeinu Baruch that peel of all fish forbidden how did he remain silent so much and did not explain, and how did he not infer stringently; therefore it seems the gravy poured from pot to bowl does not pass through all fish in the bowl but separately only first comes on the upper row placed at head of bowl and afterward goes down on fish below it — and if so they did not become forbidden, for the stream was interrupted, only the upper row became forbidden and mixed, and since they became forbidden only through pouring they do not forbid their mixture as we establish something forbidden only kedei kelipah through pouring and mixed does not forbid its mixture whether moist or dry; therefore Rabeinu Baruch was not concerned only in forbidden fish properly there is concern, for in solid lump interrupted stream does not apply as written at length and fish roll in the bowl back and forth, and especially according to Shach that kelipah standing separately and not mixing like a bowl in which they salted forbids mixtures — these fish see Shach siman 69; and my heart tells me in pouring from pot to bowl fish and gravy separately regarding gravy there is interrupted stream, for fish interrupt between first stream and it is not called pouring, unlike the fish themselves where this does not apply — therefore Rabeinu Baruch did not trouble to permit because absorbed issur does not go out without gravy — end of matter; and in this law Rabeinu Baruch holds for the fish themselves need 119 fish as above — on this Maharash disagreed and holds we do not establish presumption of kashrut and we say issur is in the majority; and Rav Peri Chadash challenged Gemara words against each other — that which we say in chapter of mixtures a ring of idol worship mixed in a hundred and they separated forty to one place and sixty to one place — forty does not forbid and sixty forbids, for they say issur is in the majority, and afterward we say there jars of terumah mixed in a hundred jars and the hundred that were nullified in their regard those fifty remaining are forbidden and we do not say issur is in the majority; and both are halachah and they challenge each other; and Peri Chadash resolved to distinguish if they remained in their fixed place that is when we say so, but if they separated we say whatever separates from majority is presumed separated, and through this he challenged Maharash, for here he separated from pot and we do not say issur is in the majority; and in truth there is no place for his words, for certainly all these sayings both deal when they are in fixed place and did not separate, for even though it teaches separated it deals when they separated before us and it is not called separated only fixed as if they did not separate at all, and so Tosafot and Rashba in Torat HaBayit, and see below in Tur siman 110 what he wrote on this; and in truth there is no contradiction at all, for that which we say sixty forbids because issur is in the majority — that is to be stringent, lest you say it is double doubt whether issur was within sixty or not, and if you say it was, perhaps this that mixed afterward in the second mixture there is no issur ring — therefore we say issur is in the majority and if so the first doubt is not in category of doubt; and likewise terumah mixed in a hundred jars we do not be lenient to say issur is in the majority, for to be lenient we do not say, but to be stringent we say, and requires study; and still challenge at first glance proof of Maharash from that which we say issur is in the majority that it helps initially for stringency we say but not to be lenient, and he said to be lenient — this is not so, for it seems he followed words of Rashi who wrote forty does not forbid if they mixed in others but those forty are forbidden and we do not say issur is in the majority because idol worship is not nullified in majority; behold Rashi holds the rule of law is to be lenient and permit the forty and say issur is in the majority only that idol worship is not nullified in majority; and that which we do not rely on majority regarding jars and to permit the fifty that were not opened — one must say it is not specifically idol worship that is not nullified in majority, likewise all issurim that are not nullified we do not say issur is in the majority, and there jars are important matter that is not nullified like idol worship; and if so Maharash holds in our case, and when we judge on cooked impure fish that even in a thousand it is not nullified — that is recognizable, only we judge on its emission whether it was in small amount and not nullified or in majority and nullified — if so this is matter that has nullification and not comparable to idol worship, and well we say issur is in the majority; but all this is superfluous and borrowed language R"SH spoke issur is in the majority — he should have said great principle in Torah whatever separates from majority is presumed separated, and this that he pours fish from pot to bowl all mixed fish and afterward this applies to say on everything whatever separates from majority is presumed separated and they are pure until impure remained at end as below siman 110 and as above, and plainly permitted; and some say here he separated before us and in hands — if so no longer applies whatever separates from majority, and it is fixed — this is not so, for we establish below siman 110 like Rashba that if separated before the doubt arose even separated by hand and before us nevertheless it is called separation from majority is presumed separated, and if so here it was not known from impure fish until afterward, and if so we say whatever separates, etc.; and clear this was not hidden from Rabeinu Baruch only he holds like opinions of his teacher R"Y who holds even before doubt arose nevertheless separated before us it is fixed; and see below siman 110 — if so according to what we establish like Rashba to distinguish between before and after doubt became known, certainly one may be lenient; and what seems to uphold Rama's words that these are pieces of issur that fell into pot — if so certainly it emitted and afterward fell into another pot there is doubt whether we say certainly it already emitted once and no longer emission here, for it will not emit again, or from doubt it does not emerge lest perhaps it emits now, and this doubt they uprooted in Tosafot in chapter Gid HaNasheh regarding udder that the Gemara challenges "rather it fell into another pot it should not forbid" — Tosafot wrote two answers, either it is doubt and rabbinic doubt for leniency, or certainly we establish for the Sages that certainly it will not emit again, and if so also here from the beginning the impure fish was among many fish in pot full of gravy — if so certainly emitted all possible and afterward when fish and gravy diminished and we come to judge second time whether it will return and emit to forbid — according to one answer no concern for second emission as found in the wagon that we say certainly emitted and will not add to emit more (and to distinguish between vessels and foods is forced), or at least from doubt it does not emerge, and I already wrote above end of seif 99 we do not say on one piece that it emits and returns and emits — if so likewise here; and if so in our case one may permit through double doubt whether impure fish was alone until it returns and forbids or not, only it was in middle of dish, and if you say it was not in middle lest it did not return and emit and there is place of heter — only this is if we do not say nat bar lichtamei, but for us who say nat bar lichtamei — if so this doubt is strengthened, for even though truly it emitted all emission it returned and absorbs from what is in pot and nat bar lichtamei, and afterward when it is with 119 fish it will emit and become forbidden;
