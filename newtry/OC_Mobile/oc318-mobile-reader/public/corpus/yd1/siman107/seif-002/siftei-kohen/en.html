Fly and the like, etc. See end of seif 104, and see what is written in this seif:
Forbidden to return the spoon to the pot. Even though there is sixty in the spoon against the issur — we do not estimate a vessel with sixty, so Turei Chayim there seif 6, and it was already explained thus above siman 92 seif 5:
And not against the whole spoon, for we do not say regarding a vessel nat bar lichtamei. And this is specifically in a new wooden or metal vessel; but old or earthenware, even new — opinion of the Rav above siman 98 seif 5 (and see there) that one should be stringent nat bar lichtamei and need sixty against the whole spoon, and likewise Turei Chayim general rule 51 seif 2:
And it was returned to the pot, etc. It implies that even if it was returned to the pot with the spoon he needs only sixty against the little dish if it is more than the issur nat bar lichtamei, but does not need sixty also against the whole spoon; and so it seems explicitly in Turei Chayim general rule 51 end of seif 2; and it is difficult, since we know there was a little dish with the issur on the spoon — if so who will say to us the whole spoon did not absorb from issur, for even though not chadichah na'aseh nevelah, nevertheless we do not know how much it absorbed, and so Issur VeHeter, and requires study at end of Shach in laws of fly and in Issur VeHeter general rule 27 seif 6 more from this:
For behold nat bar lichtamei in the spoon. For we maintain that spoon at that moment like the pot itself, since they removed it now from the pot boiling, and it is considered a first vessel for stringency, so Issur VeHeter general rule 27 seif 2; and regarding whether a fly forbids in its taste the Rav agreed to the Mechaber and his supporters — a pity, for behold the majority of poskim, early and late, [forbid], and so Issur VeHeter general rule 27 thus is practiced, and so Maharam Padua thus is practiced, and so Maharshal in Or Zarua and in his book chapter 3 siman 49 from tradition of Maharash thus is practiced; however in a place of great loss or time of pressure and the like it seems opinion of Maharam Padua in responsum to permit, and likewise Bach; however in vinegar and beer for all forbidden, as explained in this seif 104:
