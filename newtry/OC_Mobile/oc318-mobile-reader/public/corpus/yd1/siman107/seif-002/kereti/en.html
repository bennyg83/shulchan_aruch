To return the spoon — even though there is sixty in the spoon against the issur, nevertheless the vessel does not join to nullify the issur, as we say in the Gemara regarding a pot:
Against the whole spoon, for we do not say regarding a vessel nat bar lichtamei, and see siman 98, which distinguishes between earthenware and metal:
Against the dish, and the opinion of Shach is against the whole spoon, for we do not know how much was in the spoon and how much it absorbed, and one should be stringent:
For behold it became nevelah in the spoon, and even though the spoon is a second vessel, since it was inside the pot which is a first vessel and they removed it from there, the heat of the first vessel is still upon it; and regarding a fly, if it imparts taste for spoilage — opinion of Shach in a place where there is no great loss to forbid, and in vinegar and beer even in a place of great loss one should forbid:
