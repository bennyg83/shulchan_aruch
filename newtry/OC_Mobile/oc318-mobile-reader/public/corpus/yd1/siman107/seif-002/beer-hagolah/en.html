The Tur, in siman 104, in the name of the Rashba. This is inferred from the Gemara's discussion of the incident involving a mouse, which states that in other liquids, apart from vinegar and beer, it imparts a detrimental flavor; and from Rava's statement there that the halachah follows the rule of detrimental flavor, and so forth, Avodah Zarah 68a. The Ran likewise explains it there.
