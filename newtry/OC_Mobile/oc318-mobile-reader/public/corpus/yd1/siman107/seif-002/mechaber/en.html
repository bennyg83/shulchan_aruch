A fly and the like among repulsive things that a person's soul recoils from — that was found in a dish — one throws them out and the dish is permitted, for emission from these spoiled things does not forbid (and see s.k. 6): {Rama: And so is the widespread custom even though some are stringent — the words of the lenient are primary (Beit Yosef ruled like Rashba and Rokeach and Tur siman 104); and one should not change the custom; and if he took with the spoon an impure fish from the pot or another thing that does not spoil — forbidden to return the spoon to the pot (Gra); and if he returned it — need sixty from the pot against the issur, not against the whole spoon (logical opinion — for we do not say regarding a vessel "a piece becomes nevelah"); but if there was a little of the dish with the issur on the spoon and it was returned to the pot — need sixty also against the little dish, for the piece became nevelah on the spoon (Arukh general rule 27).}
