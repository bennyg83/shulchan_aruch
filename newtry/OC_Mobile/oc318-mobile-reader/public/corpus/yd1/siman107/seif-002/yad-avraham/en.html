(There in hagahah) And some permit in every case that we do not establish issur to say the issur remained without sixty, and so is primary. And according to this reason it is permitted in every case even if water remained in the pot and he poured them on the eggs in the bowl; and in Tur it states another reason, that those twenty or thirty fish that became forbidden are nullified in majority mixture as law of dry in dry; and likewise Gemara chapter 3 daf 41 regarding worm found in a spit; and one should not challenge, for we establish end of siman 109 in hagahah that dry in dry nullified in majority is in its kind, but not in its kind sixty is required since if he cooks them it imparts taste; one may say impure fish and pure have one taste; however Shach wrote they are not equal in taste; and see in Be'er Yaakov who challenged him from sugya of chapter 3 regarding that which is challenged regarding brine and Ari — min b'mino not nullified, for according to Abaye we follow taste, impure and pure are two kinds; and see Tosafot Menachot (22b) and so is proven explicitly in Gemara and Rif and Rosh and end of chapter 3 (daf 100) that impure fish and pure are nullified in majority dry in dry where it is not a beriah and piece worthy of honor, and here the fish forbidden because they received taste of issur do not have law of beriah as Tur says; and see end of siman 101; and regarding eggs one may say embryo its issur is rabbinic as written siman 86 seif 8; and in rabbinic even not in its kind nullified in majority dry in dry, for even if he cooks them and imparts taste it cannot come to Torah issur as written siman 109 Shach and Be'er Heitev there s.k. 8 and siman 69 s.k. 47; and behold according to this reason one should not permit except in dry but not when he poured hot from pot to bowl, for then through gravy the issur absorbed in fish goes to other fish — it is not dry in dry as all this is explained in Darkei Moshe see there; and in my humble opinion requires study, for in Tur and Beit Yosef it is proven explicitly that even if he poured hot water nevertheless it is dry in dry, for absorbed issur does not return and absorb in dry only through cooking; and Beit Yosef wrote his intent is through actual cooking and not through water that is poured; and it is wondrous, for we establish pouring emits and absorbs at least kedei kelipah as Taz wrote siman 98 seif 10; and answer after my writing I found this answer in Taz and Shach in name of Bach and their answers are forced as understood to one who examines their words; and it seems to me clearly opinion of Tur and Beit Yosef is like Issur VeHeter and hagahah above siman 95 seif 3 that if he poured from first vessel on vessels of meat and milk they do not become forbidden, for pouring is not like first vessel to make the vessels absorb from one another — end of his words; and that is even pouring where stream was not interrupted, for behold similar to what is written there that if he poured from meat vessel on milk vessel it is forbidden, dealing certainly where stream was not interrupted as is explicitly in Shach there and end of seif 94 and end of seif 98; if so if regarding vessels we say so that we establish absorbed issur in vessel goes out even without gravy as written siman 105 seif 7 in hagahah — if so a fortiori two pieces of issur that we establish absorbed issur in piece does not go from it to absorb in another without gravy, for pouring does not make this emit and that absorb; and I again saw explicitly Taz wrote end of seif 98 that Shulchan Aruch holds pouring does not emit and absorb as one; and so Taz wrote end of seif 95; and on this reason one may rely to permit here even if he poured water from pot to bowl; and Rama wrote in Darkei Moshe that custom is to permit in every case because of reason written in hagahah we do not establish issur, etc., and in my humble opinion as written; wrote in Hagahot Mei'iri part 15 from Magen Avraham in name of R"Y that nevertheless need not kasher the pan, for on account of this doubt need not be concerned they remained at end, only establish the pan on presumption of kashrut that it was from the beginning — end of his words, and brought in Turei Chayim:
