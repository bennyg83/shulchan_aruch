In chadichah na'aseh nevelah in the spoon, and even though the spoon is a second vessel, nevertheless it was in the pot which is a first vessel and heat of first vessel is still upon it; and I did not understand at first glance if he removed a large piece and it has sides from first vessel — nevertheless we establish not like Maharshal that solid lump is only pouring; and what he wrote large piece and what he wrote spoon that heat of first vessel is upon it — requires study at first glance; and what seems to resolve it is that certainly hot into cold, lower prevails because cold cools hot — therefore if he poured from first vessel to second vessel the cold sides of second vessel cool heat of first vessel, and therefore even in solid lump that has heat from first vessel when it reaches second vessel the second vessel cools it and it is also only second vessel; but that spoon that they removed from first vessel and is still in hand and was not put at all in second vessel — heat of first vessel is still upon it because it was not cooled by anything — therefore it has heat of first vessel — end of matter; and if so likewise solid lump that they removed from dish and before it was placed in second vessel issur fell upon it absorbs in heat of first vessel, for it was not yet cooled in second vessel; and in this will be understood more what we wrote above siman 105 in resolving Gemara words in chapter of mixtures that teaches one who cooked in it and one who poured into it boiling requires purging and rinsing, and we asked suspend it in air of oven, etc., and it resolved from the mishnah that teaches one who cooked in it and one who poured into it boiling — and we already challenged why it calls it so, since pouring does not cook how can there be cooking without absorption in mishnah, and we wrote on it resolution, and in this it will be sweetened more, for it seems in my humble opinion pouring is entirely like second vessel and does not absorb at all, only the piece that is poured into the vessel before the piece reaches sides of vessel heat of first vessel is still upon it and cooks within the piece the moisture and fat and gravy in the piece itself, and behold it is in air of vessel — and the question was resolved and the Gemara dismissed that it is absorption without cooking, for pouring absorbs — end of matter, investigate:
