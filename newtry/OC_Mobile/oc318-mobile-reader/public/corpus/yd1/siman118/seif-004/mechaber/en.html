A lock and seal count as two seals; but for things where one seal suffices, if it had only a lock, the lock is not counted as a seal (this too is duplicated there, seif 66): {Rama: If one sends something in a sack and seals the sack, it is not even like one seal (Kol Bo in the name of the Rif and Or Zarua and Tosefta, siman 130) unless he turned the stitches inward (Arukh); and if he sealed the thing he sent and also the sack, it counts as two seals (Kol Bo there).}
