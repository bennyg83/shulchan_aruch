But for things where one seal suffices, etc.—this means chamfig and the like; and it seems per the 'some say' view, for a depositor one does not need even for chavivta except one seal; and so the 'some say' view that with a non-Jew everything is permitted with a seal, as above seif 1—a lock is not counted as one seal. The Rav wrote in siman 130, seif 2, and siman 131, seif 1: libated wine in a non-Jew's hand b'dieved suffices with one seal—and he did not write more leniency that b'dieved even one lock suffices, because he stated here like the Mechaber, meaning as I explained; and even though for Rabbenu Tam certainly one lock suffices, nevertheless we do not rely on him except with one seal—and in Bach he wrote here and in siman 130 that b'dieved even one lock suffices, and so the Rav's view—see there—it is not clear. Also what he brought as proof from the Rashba's responsum in the Mordekhai, chapter R' Yishmael, and from the Rashba's responsum () klal 19, siman 3, for this law—I rejected all in my books; truly the construction, see there. Also his distinguishing against the Mechaber in chamfig, that it is permitted even l'chatchila with one lock—I do not see in his words grounds to dispute, since the Rashba and Taz agreed that specifically one seal is needed even b'dieved; especially since they said to be stringent.
