(Siman 118, Shach, s.k. 8) That d'oraisa min b'mino is nullified—in Minchat Kohen on Menachot (folio 22) he brought a challenge in the name of Baal Imrei Emet, the Gaon R' Meir Fischles, may his memory be blessed: if so, what does the Gemara challenge on Rav from what he said about cheese from milk—for according to his view min b'mino is not nullified, it would be milk, a d'oraisa prohibition—and understand; see there. Also this is the Shach's conclusion above (siman 98): the matter of nullification in the majority depends on taste—if so, here it would be not its species, for impure and pure liver do not have the same taste; and see responsum Noda Biyehudah (Mahadura Tinyana, siman 66).
