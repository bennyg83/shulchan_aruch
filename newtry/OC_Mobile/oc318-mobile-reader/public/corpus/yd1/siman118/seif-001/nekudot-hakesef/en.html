(Siman 118, Taz, s.k. 3) Therefore one should wonder at Or Zarua the Arukh, etc.—it is difficult for me: even though for Rabbenu Tam certainly a lock alone suffices, nevertheless we do not rely on him except with one seal—and this is the Rama's view, as the Shach wrote, s.k. 21; and we find similarly in many places.
