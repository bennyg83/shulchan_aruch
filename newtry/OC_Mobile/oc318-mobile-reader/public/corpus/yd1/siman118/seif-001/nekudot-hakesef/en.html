(There, s.k. 4) And in my humble opinion all agree, etc.—and what will he do with meat and a piece of fish? (And in truth the Taz meant only wine, as the Derech Mitzvot emended.)
(There, s.k. 5) And the Levush wrote, etc.—and it is not so, etc.—it is difficult for me, for the Levush wrote thus for the law, and the same for us where there is concern he touched by way of being occupied, as below siman 128.
(There, s.k. 11) I wonder greatly, etc.—it is difficult for me: specifically there, where it is only cut as an Israelite cuts it, is there concern in meat found in a non-Jew's hand; but here there is a seal or "kosher" written on it, which clearly indicates it is kosher—if so, if he learned afterward of a treifah doubt he should have removed the seal or writing; moreover, it is not customary to give a seal or write "kosher" until it is known with certainty that it is kosher—and this is simple.
