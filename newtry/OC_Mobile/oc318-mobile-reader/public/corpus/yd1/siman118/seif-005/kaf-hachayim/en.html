E) Via a non-Jew—and it seems this means specifically an idol-worshipping gentile; but Ishmaelites who are not idol worshippers—permitted to send via them or deposit with them wine with one seal, for the Mechaber rules like the Rif, Rambam, and Rashba that Torah prohibitions need seal within seal and rabbinic ones one seal; the Rashba wrote: even though stam yeinam is only d'rabbanan, they were stringent like Torah because of affection of libation—they trouble and forge one seal, end of his words; therefore with Ishmaelites, where affection of libation does not apply, one seal suffices. Even if one says perhaps he bought from another idol-worshipping gentile who libated—in Ishmaelite wine too one could say so, yet we are not concerned to forbid benefit, as below (siman 124, seif 6); see Kenesset HaGadol (there, Hagahat Beit Yosef letter 19); see Avodah Zarah (64) and below (siman 128); therefore the same here; and so from most Acharonim brought below s.k. 26.
E) And greater: we find the Taz, s.k. 4, wrote that in our time there is no affection of libation and wine suffices with one seal—see there; and Minchat Yaakov (klal 32, letter 32) agreed; and in his responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 1 (siman 63); and the conditions of those who support them: Or Zarua (chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah, siman 160) and Rokeach (siman 496) wrote that in our time, when gentiles do not libate to idolatry, one need not seal within seal, as we say regarding vinegar, etc.—see there; and so the Meiri (Avodah Zarah 31), regarding one who deposited his wine, in the name of some later rabbis who permitted wine even for drinking via one seal, because in these days libation is not common to them as in those times, end of his words (the publishing rabbi, letter 3, did not mention the above); see Arukh HaShulchan s.k. 13 that one should rule like the Taz; and it is true all this does not suffice for the Mechaber's view that we accepted his rulings that wine needs seal within seal (and it seems per his reason he forbids stam yeinam in our time even for benefit in siman 123—unlike dissenters); and so explicitly in responsum Chakrei Lev, part 1, Yoreh Deah (siman 199): from the poskim who ruled even in our time two seals for wine; and again brought the Rashba in Torat HaBayit (141a): even though gentiles today are not idol worshippers, nevertheless since some among them libate, we require seal within seal; Beit Yosef brought it (siman 128) and concluded even b'dieved forbidden; and so the Rama's hagahah there; he concluded: nevertheless to permit in great loss one may rely on this great authority—see there; and see responsum Yismach Lev, Gagin (Yoreh Deah, siman 8); in Yeshuot Yaakov s.k. 4 he wrote explicitly unlike the Taz; and so in Sefer Erekh Shai—see there; nevertheless with Ishmaelites it seems there is less concern; and I do not deny I saw in Or Sameach (1 Kings 5:32) he explained the Rambam there to forbid drinking when he transgressed and deposited with an Ishmaelite with one seal—see there; but in my opinion one may rely and be lenient on all the above; however l'chatchila one should heed Or Sameach to require two seals even with Ishmaelites; see Lechem Mishneh (1 Kings 5:32); see responsum Tashbetz, part 3 (siman 89); responsum Bnei Binyamin (siman 6); Sefer Ginzei Chaim (maarechet 8, end of letter 91)—and understand.
