If sent via a non-Jew, etc.—kosher—the language implies somewhat b'dieved only for nikkur and the like; but not l'chatchila. The Tur also wrote in this language; nevertheless the Mechaber wrote explicitly in siman 65 that it is permitted even l'chatchila via a non-Jew with a nikkur sign; but the Mechaber wrote nothing of this there; nevertheless Or Zarua wrote l'chatchila one should not rely on it; and so the Rav in Simanei Torat Chacham there.
But if he sent a d'rabbanan prohibition, etc.—explained in Torat HaBayit there: for d'rabbanan prohibition, even if a Jew saw the seal was completely broken and clearly the seal was opened—it is permitted in this matter; and so in Torat Chatas there, law 14—see there.
