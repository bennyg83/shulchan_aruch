If he sent via a non-Jew a thigh, etc.—it implies specifically b'dieved, but l'chatchila not; and so Or Zarua, klal 22; and it is explained in our words in siman 63 in the name of the Mordekhai that whenever one has a ta'am in what he sent via the non-Jew, it is permitted.
Any piece in which Israelite nikkur is recognizable, etc.—it seems obvious that this includes a chicken in which nikkur of the neck tendons is recognizable as Israelites do, on which one may rely for permission.
