Meat found in the hand of a non-Jew with a seal or "kosher" written on it—even though one does not know who wrote it—is kosher, for it is known that it belongs to an Israelite; and this is when non-Jews who know how to write are not found there: {Rama: And likewise it is permitted to buy cheeses stamped the way Israelites stamp them (Mordekhai, chapter 8 of Avodah Zarah); and this is specifically in a place where there is no concern that perhaps the stamps remained in the hands of non-Jews and they affixed them on their cheeses (Or Zarua and Arukh). And some forbid in all of this since we do not know who wrote or sealed them (Mahariq, end of root 33); and b'dieved one should not be stringent.}
