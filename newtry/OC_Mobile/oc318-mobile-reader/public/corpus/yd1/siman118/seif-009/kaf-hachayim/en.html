I) Or wine into which other things were mixed, such as honey—and it deals with mixing so much honey that the wine's taste changed because of it—for then there is no stam yeinam, as we hold (siman 123, seif 4); Lechem HaPanim—since there is stam yeinam concern, seal within seal is needed—Shach s.k. 6, Kenesset HaGadol hagahah letter 8, Peri Chadash s.k. 6, Minchat Yaakov klal 32 letter 34, Lechem HaPanim s.k. 4, Mesgeret HaShulchan, Zivchei Tzedek s.k. 4; see responsum Amek HaMelech (siman 48). In my opinion, since the Rambam's and Western Geonim's view permits even a little honey, and R' Elazar bar Natan in responsum of the Rivash (siman 180), even though other poskim dispute them—in b'dieved and great loss one may permit with one seal, since the custom in Egypt is lenient like the Rambam and Western Geonim even l'chatchila, as Or Zarua, part 2 (chapter 7 of Avodah Zarah, siman 262) and Hagahot Asheri (there 30); and so the Mechaber in Beit Yosef in responsum Avkat Rocheil (simanim 67); he rejected with both hands the sage who wanted to be stringent for them—see there; and responsum Radbaz, part 3 (siman 527) permitted in pressed circumstances to rely on Western Geonim—except he wrote this regarding Ishmaelite contact—see there; and with the Taz and Minchat Yaakov's views brought above (s.k. 5) that in our time gentiles are not idol worshippers, one seal suffices—in b'dieved and great loss one may well rely on them with a little honey; this is the case—to be lenient in responsum Mabit (siman 208)—see there.
