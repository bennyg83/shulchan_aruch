One who buys, etc.—Ba'al HaGadol; Tosefta there: sending via a non-Jew or a Kuti—no concern for tithes or shevi'it.
And even, etc.—for even for slaughter they are believed, as in the eighth chapter of Zevachim; Tosafot Pesachim 4b, they believed him, etc.
And some, etc.—Avodah Zarah 39b; as Rabbenu Tam explained: with a suspect Israelite it deals, as above; specifically an am ha'aretz suspect regarding demai, a matter the public are lenient about, is believed; the Rambam per his view explaining there with a non-Jew, as above.
