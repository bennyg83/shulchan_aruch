H) And beer—and even though beer of gentiles is permitted, one may say it deals with wanting to drink the beer deposited with the gentile in his house, as above siman 114 to forbid this—Beit Meir; or one may say it deals with beer made from raisin wine—Ma'aseh Rokeach, chapter 13 of Mishneh Torah, law 10, brought in Zivchei Tzedek s.k. 3; and it seems even if he deposited ordinary beer he needs one seal lest he switch it with beer made from raisin wine, unless raisin beer is more expensive—in such a case we are not concerned for switching, as we hold in Avodah Zarah (34) and Shulchan Aruch (siman 114, seif 11); and so in responsum Tiferet Adam in his memoirs (maarechet 10, letter 5)—see there; and one may concern he mixes white wine in it—see Beit Meir there; responsum Radbaz, part 4 (siman 239)—see there.
