Once they sealed properly with two seals on things that need two and with one on things where one suffices, one need not go back afterward to inspect it; but if he went back to his seal and did not recognize it, it is forbidden—even if he made two seals (this seif is duplicated in siman 130, seif 8): {Rama: However, this is specifically if it is something where there is concern that it was switched and he benefits from the switch (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot and other poskim), or it is wine and there is concern that he forbade it through contact; but if one sees the thing that was sent or deposited and it is good as it was sent, and the non-Jew would not benefit if he switched it, we are not concerned—even if the seal is damaged. And see below at the end of siman 130 these laws.}
