Two—and likewise more; and below siman 130, seif 8, the Rav set conditions on this—see there, Shach.
Through contact—Taz wrote: the explanation is that with wine there is concern even if clear he did not drink from it—e.g., guards outside prevented bringing water to mix, and we see nothing missing from its measure—still concern because of contact even without benefit, lest while occupied (because of affection of libation) he touched the wine, as the Rama wrote in siman 128, seif 4; and this is forbidden even for us, unlike the Levush—see there.
