Or it is wine and there is concern, etc.—this matter is puzzling, for certainly he benefits from switching—he will drink from it and add water; and in this one certainly cannot say that he sees the wine is good as at first, for it is forbidden to taste it; only for meat can one say so, that its appearance is fatty and good, but not for something that depends on taste. It seems his meaning regarding wine is that even if it is clear to us that he did not drink from it—e.g., guards outside prevented bringing water to mix—and we see nothing missing from its measure—still there is concern because of contact, even though he does not benefit; especially in a barrel or wide cup there is concern that while occupied he touched the wine, as the Rama wrote in siman 128, seif 4; and the Levush wrote the reason is affection of libation, which is not so, for then it should be permitted for us, which it is not; and so Or Zarua, klal 22, in the name of Semag.
