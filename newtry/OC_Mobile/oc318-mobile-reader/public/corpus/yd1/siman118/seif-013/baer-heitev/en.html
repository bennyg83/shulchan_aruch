Perhaps—Shach wrote: since there are many doubts, it seems permitted to buy other cheeses from him—see there; and likewise we are not concerned he smeared, etc., for we are not concerned he intended to cause stumbling as long as the non-Jew has no benefit in the matter, as in this seif.
Resting—meaning he left it in a special place on the cheeses; but if he left it elsewhere, even intentionally in a special place, since he did not leave it resting on the cheeses the non-Jew thinks the Israelite does not know where he left it—and forbidden (but if left resting on the cheeses, then the non-Jew fears to take the stamp lest the Israelite made a sign in how he placed it)—Shach.
