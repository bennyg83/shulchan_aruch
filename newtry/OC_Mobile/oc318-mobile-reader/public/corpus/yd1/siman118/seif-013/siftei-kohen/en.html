We are not concerned, etc.—the non-Jew will fear the Jew is accustomed to make his stamp in a special place; also lest the Israelite notice because the later ones made afterward are softer; also there are many doubts—for perhaps the lost stamp never came to the non-Jew's hand; and if it came, perhaps he did not forge; and if he forged, perhaps he only stamped with something permitted, end of Mordekhai. The first two reasons do not suffice except for those cheeses the Israelite left in the non-Jew's house; the last reason suffices also to buy other cheeses from him—see seif 9 in the hagahah and what is written there.
And likewise we are not concerned he smeared, etc.—for we are not concerned he intended to cause stumbling as long as the non-Jew has no benefit in the matter, as in seif 10—and see seif 2 in the hagahah.
But if he did not leave it so—meaning even if he left it elsewhere in a special place intentionally, nevertheless since he did not leave it resting on the cheeses, the non-Jew thinks the Israelite does not know where he left it—and forbidden.
