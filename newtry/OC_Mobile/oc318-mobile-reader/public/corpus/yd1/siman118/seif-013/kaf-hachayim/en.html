M) And morayes—meaning where their custom is to put wine in it, therefore concern he switches with their morayes; if wine was dearer than morayes, or where they do not put wine in it—permitted to deposit with a non-Jew and send via him without any seal, as Shulchan Aruch above (siman 114, seif 11); and so Shach s.k. 9 and other Acharonim.
