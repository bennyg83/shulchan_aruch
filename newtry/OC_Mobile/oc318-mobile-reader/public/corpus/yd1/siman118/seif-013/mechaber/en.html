An Israelite who made cheeses in the house of a non-Jew and stamped them with a wooden stamp seal and forgot the stamp in the house of the non-Jew—we are not concerned that perhaps the non-Jew forged and sealed others with that same stamp; and likewise we are not concerned that perhaps the non-Jew smeared the faces of the cheeses with pig fat: {Rama: And some say that one should not permit if he forgot the stamp in the house of the non-Jew unless he left it resting on the cheeses that the Israelite made; but if he did not leave it so, and there is concern that perhaps he made small cheeses and switched them for large ones or for better ones, it is forbidden (Arukh, rule 47, law 2); and in a place of loss one may permit in every case.}
