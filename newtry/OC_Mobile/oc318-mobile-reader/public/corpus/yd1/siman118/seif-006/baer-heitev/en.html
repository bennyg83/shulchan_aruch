Slaughtered—Shach wrote: and it is difficult regarding what is said in seif 1 that if his kids were lost, permitted if slaughtered—for there it was never in a non-Jew's hand, only we did not know who slaughtered, therefore we follow the majority that most found at slaughter are experts; but here, sending in a non-Jew's hand, there is concern he switched good for bad, therefore even where most Israelites are found we concern he slaughtered himself for the one he switched; Mordekhai wrote: an incident came—a slaughtered chicken sent via a non-Jew without a guard, they doubted perhaps another was switched, they asked the slaughterer if he slaughtered this chicken; he answered: I have no visual recognition in it; however now I slaughtered one chicken; but the owner recognized it and said this is it, and I permitted it since the owner recognized it, end of his words; Maharshal added: but if they did not ask the slaughterer it is not a sign—and it is simple (and see siman 63 what the Taz wrote there).
