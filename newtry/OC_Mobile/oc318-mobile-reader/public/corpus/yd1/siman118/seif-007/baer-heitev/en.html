To the public—Shach wrote: implies even with other Kutim who are not his slave and maidservant, permitted b'dieved in a public place (unlike Or Zarua who permitted only his slave and maidservant); and Taz wrote: even where Jews are not commonly found but only non-Jews—this non-Jew fears lest other non-Jews see him and tell the Israelite, as siman 129, seif 4; and in Torat Chatas he wrote the custom is to send via a non-Jew in a public thoroughfare that is an Israelite neighborhood where non-Jews are not found, end of his words.
