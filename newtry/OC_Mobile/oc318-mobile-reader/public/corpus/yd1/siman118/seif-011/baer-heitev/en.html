To concern—and Peri Chadash wrote the view of most poskim is to permit even with going out and entering, and so is primary—see there; and from the Rosh's responsum it is proven even with non-Jew and Israelite guests we are not concerned, for what we permit with going out and entering is even with guests who do not know each other, for they also fear each other—see there, Shach.
To be careful—Shach wrote: meaning because of sparks; and since he wrote "even," when mouths are not open one need not be careful with two pots; and the Taz wrote the reason is lest they switch one for the other—accordingly, if two Israelite pots, one meat and one milk, no prohibition even l'chatchila to cook in one oven, since switching does not apply.
