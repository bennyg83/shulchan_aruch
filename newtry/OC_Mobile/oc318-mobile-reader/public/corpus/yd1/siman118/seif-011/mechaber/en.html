An Israelite and a non-Jew who set two pots one next to the other—this one with slaughtered meat and that one with nevelah—it is permitted, and we are not concerned that perhaps when the Israelite turned away the non-Jew switched slaughtered for nevelah, even if the Israelite's was superior; and even if their mouths are open it is permitted, and we are not concerned that perhaps sparks will fly from one to the other (and the same applies to two things roasted one next to the other (Mordekhai, chapter 8 of Avodah Zarah); but l'chatchila one should be careful even with two pots) (Tur).
