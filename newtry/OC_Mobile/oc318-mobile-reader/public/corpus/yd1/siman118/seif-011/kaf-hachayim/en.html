K) And likewise vinegar—meaning vinegar of wine, bubbling when poured on the ground, then full vinegar unfit for libation, as we hold (siman 123, seif 6); therefore no concern for affection of libation since unfit for libation; if for switching—stam yeinam is only d'rabbanan, one seal suffices—Shach s.k. 7, Kenesset HaGadol hagahah letter 8, Peri Chadash s.k. 7, Lechem HaPanim s.k. 5, Minchat Yaakov klal 32 letter 34, Mesgeret HaShulchan, Zivchei Tzedek s.k. 5. In my opinion b'dieved there is room to be lenient with one seal even when it has not reached bubbling measure—since the Mechaber's main view stringency requiring this measure is not agreed by all poskim; as Beit Yosef in the name of the Rashba in Torat HaBayit: this is not from the essence of the Gemara; whatever was wine and soured, between beginning and end of souring is no more than three days, etc.—see there; see Ritva's commentary (Avodah Zarah 29:); responsum Shem Aryeh (Yoreh Deah, siman 29): this measure is from stringency and not law, since it is unfit for libation—see there; see Orach Chaim (siman 204, seif 4) and Acharonim there; see responsum Chesed LeAvraham Elkelai (Orach Chaim, siman 5)—if so, at least here there is no affection of libation; responsum Chatam Sofer (siman 124); and with poskim who hold to be lenient in our time with wine with one seal—it seems for vinegar one may be lenient b'dieved even without bubbling measure; see Kenesset HaGadol siman 123 hagahah letter 11.
