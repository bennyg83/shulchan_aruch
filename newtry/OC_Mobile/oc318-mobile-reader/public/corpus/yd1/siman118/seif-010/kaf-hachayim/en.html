J) And know: for cooked wine and wine with honey—some say we permit only when the gentile knows it is cooked or has honey, as Be'er HaGolah (siman 123, s.k. 4) in the name of two sons of David: only then there is no stam yeinam—see there; and so Zikhronenu LeChaim (folio 31b); therefore the same here—if the gentile does not know, one may not permit except seal within seal. However, in Egypt they practiced leniency in stam yeinam even without this; and poskim stated their words generally on this; in any case b'dieved one seal suffices even if the gentile does not know; and with the Taz and Minchat Yaakov as above; see responsum Shevut Yaakov (siman 63).
