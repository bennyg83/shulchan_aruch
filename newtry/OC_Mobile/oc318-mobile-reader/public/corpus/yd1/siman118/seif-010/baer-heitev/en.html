And enters—Shach wrote: it seems even a child going out and entering helps, as above siman 115, seif 1, in the hagahah (and even for a d'oraisa prohibition); and the Gemara concludes even l'chatchila permitted with going out and entering—therefore what the Tur wrote "one who leaves" is because of the end: he stayed long and did not inform he intends to stay, which is permitted only b'dieved—see siman 129, seif 1.
Locked—and from presumption we say he did not lock unless it became known it was locked—Shach.
