One should be stringent, etc.—as in this seif: without going out and entering forbidden if he benefits; and here too sometimes she puts milk in the pot to improve her portion; and when going to synagogue, as the Rosh wrote. (Likkut) One should be stringent, etc.—there in the Rosh: and for God-fearing, etc.—meaning when going to synagogue, for it is not actual going out and entering, for it is like informing he intends to stay—which is forbidden as in this seif; and even though we say seventy elders heard the sound of roasting and it is permitted—for he informs his donkey-driver there the non-Jew knows the Israelite is careful about him—and this is 'he informs his driver,' unlike here; and it is permitted only from the second reason that he does not benefit—and on this he said 'and also to improve her portion,' etc.; and this is the hagahah 'and he does not go out and enter'—for with going out and entering even l'chatchila permitted as at the beginning of Chullin (end of citation). (Likkut) One should be stringent, etc.—for the Rosh wrote there: on this we rely, etc., when going to synagogue—and apparently proof for the Rabbis from what is written: 38a, one, 'one who leaves,' etc., sets, etc.; but in the Yerushalmi they said on it: when the child sits and guards (end of citation).
