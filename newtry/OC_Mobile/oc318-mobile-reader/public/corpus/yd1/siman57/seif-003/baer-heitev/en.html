Large. Meaning even if it did not perforate to the cavity; if it reached the cavity it is already explained it has derisah even in kids and lambs; the entire siman deals even without reaching the cavity. Shach.
Like themselves. Even those below the hawk have derisah in a bird like themselves; those above the hawk are like the hawk itself and derise even in a bird larger than them — specifically in thin bird species like hawk, as Maharshal ruled — not like the Taz.
The large one. The Shach wrote: all the more those above it; eagle has law of large bird.
