Like doves, etc., but, etc. Rashi there. Selection: but not in a chicken. From what is written 52b "and that chicken," etc., and the Geonim challenged — what is the difficulty, for in a bird is there anyone who disagrees that a cat has derisah? And answer: since he adjoined it with a hawk, specifically in a thin bird like a hawk; and Terumat HaDeshen answered in another version; see there (side b).
Except the large one, etc. Mishnah; and as written in the Gemara that it teaches hawk even in a bird larger than itself — likewise for large; and Rambam and Shulchan Aruch explain the Gemara teaches specifically hawk even in a bird larger than itself.
Called falcon. Rashi and all commentators explain astur; and Terumat Chaim wrote but falcon is below the large; and he wrote that some say falcon, and likewise the Tur two explanations, and the Rav for stringency. Selection: called falcon. So explained in Terumat HaDeshen in the name of the Arukh — he explained pe'a that it is owl, and his version gaz; Rashi explained astur, and Terumat HaDeshen agreed there; therefore it appears from here that large means astur, etc., and there many times; Arukh explained hawk astur; but Rashi explained osprey, and likewise Ramban in the Torah; also wrote large is astur; in Michlol Yofi there in Rashi's name that kos is falcon and he disagreed; owl in Targum there kippof, and Rashi in last chapter of Berakhot (57b) that it is tsuitt, and Targum Psalms 102 on "cup kippof," and Rashi in Shemini there "cup and owl," etc.; and it should be said both species are in kippof; and Tosafot Niddah 23a s.v. kria and Chullin 63a s.v. ba (end of selection).
