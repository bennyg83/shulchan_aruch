The hawk has derisah. Even if it did not reach the cavity; but if it reached the cavity, it was already explained that it has derisah even in kids and lambs, and all the more so in all birds; and likewise the entire siman deals even if it did not reach the cavity, and it is simple.
And specifically in large ones, but in small ones they have derisah. So it should read.
And other birds, etc. The Mechaber's view is that even those below the hawk have derisah in a bird like themselves — not as the Taz wrote that those below the hawk have no derisah at all, and likewise those above the hawk have derisah only in a bird like themselves; and this is the Rashba's view that the Beit Yosef brought, and the Beit Yosef wrote that those above the hawk have derisah specifically in a thin bird like a hawk, and the Tur stated the matter plainly like the Rashba, end of his words (and the Bach attacked the Beit Yosef on this improperly; see there). And if so, the Mechaber who stated the matter plainly also holds this is specifically in a thin bird, not better than a hawk; however those above the hawk have the law of a hawk itself, that they have derisah even in a bird larger than them, and likewise the Rashba wrote in his novellae to be stringent like this view, and likewise is the Ran's view; however this is specifically in a species of thin bird similar to a hawk, as the Ran wrote, and likewise Maharshal ruled; and what the Bach ruled here is not clear to us.
Except the large one. And all the more so those above it, and this is simple among poskim and clear in the Rashba's novellae and in the four sections of the Rosh in Beit HaBedolah daf 43 side b that an eagle has the law of a large bird.
