There are those who say that wherever examination was mentioned — such as in fallen derusah and broken animals — in these generations we cannot rely on proper examination and it has no remedy, but rather to wait twelve months, or in a female that becomes pregnant and gives birth; and likewise in a bird, if it finished laying all the eggs of its first clutch and waited twenty-one days and then returned to lay kosher eggs; and likewise if it never laid eggs — if it waited after a safek treifah was born in it for twenty-one days and began to lay kosher eggs. And some say that even nowadays they examine, but one must be careful to bring all who are expert in the matter: {Rama: And we in recent generations are not expert in examination; therefore the custom is to declare treifah anything that has a basis for concern, and we do not rely on our examination at all; therefore one should practice according to the first view (R' Yosef Karo and Hagahot Maimoniot and Mordechai end of Perek HaBehemah HaMekasheh and all acharonim). And some are more stringent still — that anything requiring waiting twelve months — we are concerned for mishap, even if it is only one standing in safek; and one may not sell it alive to a gentile, but rather kill it first lest it return and be sold to a Jew (Mordechai and Semak and Hagahot Maimoniot and above siman 23 s.s.). And such is the custom in these countries. And wherever it is a definite treifah — even though it waited twelve months and is alive — it is forbidden (Rashba siman 98 and R' Yerucham and other acharonim).}
