Months. Shach wrote in Torat Chaim and Epi Ravrevi: pregnant year needs thirteen months; Peri Chadash disagrees — only twelve months everywhere in Gemara, not more; see there.
And gives birth. Meaning twelve months not needed, suffices when pregnant; obvious female also helps twelve months (Shach). Maharshal wrote: depends specifically on birth, not pregnancy alone (perhaps pregnancy not coming to birth but dying in it).
To mishap. Shach wrote: where need only wait twenty-one days, not concerned for mishap in short time; see siman 86 seif 9 (Beit Yosef siman 86: even for bird do not wait twenty-one days but sell to gentile and gentile kills immediately).
Definite. Shach wrote: but definite derusah — twelve months helps, not called definite treifah since permitted through examination; concludes also safek extra limb — twelve months helps.
