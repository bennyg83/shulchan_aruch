Rather to wait, etc. And it implies that even for definite derusah, waiting twelve months or giving birth helps, since it has examination. And what the Rav wrote in the gloss that for definite treifah twelve months does not help — this is specifically absolute definite treifah, not here where it becomes kosher through examination.
Twelve months. And he wrote in Torat Chaim general rule 72 law 2 that in a pregnant year thirteen months are needed, and likewise in Sefer Epi Ravrevi daf 73 side b.
Or in a female, etc. Meaning, twelve months are not needed, but it suffices when it becomes pregnant, etc.; but it is obvious that for a female, waiting twelve months also helps.
That it becomes pregnant and gives birth. Maharshal wrote Perek Elu Treifot siman 80 — it depends specifically on birth, not on pregnancy alone, for it is possible it becomes pregnant with a pregnancy that will not come to birth but die in it; and he brought proofs; see there.
And it waited twenty-one days. This is the gestation time of a bird; and whoever did not wait twenty-one days, there is concern perhaps it conceived before it became treifah, and these eggs are from its first clutch; and it is simple; and see further these laws in siman 86 seif 9.
For all that need, etc. But where it need only wait twenty-one days, we are not concerned for mishap in such a short time — so wrote the Rosh in Hagahot Sha'arei Dura siman 73, and Darkei Moshe and Torat Chaim general rule 73 law 4 bring it, and likewise it appears below siman 86.
And wherever it is definite treifah, etc. In the Rashba's responsum he wrote so regarding extra limb — that even though it certainly waited twelve months, it is forbidden, since it is definite treifah, and waiting twelve months does not help except for safek treifah and the like; and even though the reason it is treifah is because it cannot live — nevertheless if it lives twelve months it is forbidden (not as the Taz who wrote the reason that he holds treifah can live, and the Lev Chaim already attacked this daf 172 side b) — for we may not contradict in any matter the words of the Sages who said it is treifah. And it appears from the Rashba's words there that it must be that its living twelve months is a miracle, but nevertheless it is treifah. But Maharshal Perek Elu Treifot siman 80 wrote that the reason it is treifah is that it cannot live in most cases, but a minority of a minority lives even though it is treifah; see there. And it appears from Maharshal's words there that where there is a dispute among poskim, if we see it lived twelve months it is kosher — and these are correct words. From all this it is clear that it is not specifically extra limb, for although the questioner mentioned extra limb, nevertheless the Rashba forbade in every definite treifah; and this is simple, not as the Taz whose implication is that he divides thus; see there. However in the Rashba's responsum there he wrote that extra limb is treifah for another reason — they did not say the extra limb cannot live, but rather they said by kal va-chomer that the extra limb is considered as removed, and whatever if removed cannot live twelve months, even now without removal it is treifah as if removed; and even though it adds days with its surplus, end of his words. Therefore even for safek extra limb waiting does not help. Nevertheless Maharshal there wrote that the first answer he rejected is primary, and likewise it is proven below siman 81 s.k. 5 that even for safek extra limb waiting twelve months helps; and it appears the Rashba spoke only of definite extra limb so as not to contradict the Sages' words, but not for safek extra limb; and examine, and see further these laws in siman 81 seif 2.
