To a narrow place, etc. And Maharshal wrote that in a wide place, even if one of them is found derusah, they are not concerned for the others, and we say it did not reach them; and the Hagahot Derishah brings it seif 41, and likewise other acharonim.
Such as if it entered their pen. And some acharonim wrote that it is not called a wide place except in a valley not enclosed by fence and partition; but in a courtyard enclosed by fence and partition they are concerned. But for birds that slip away from the predator here and there, they are not concerned in a wide courtyard, only in a coop that is a narrow place for them; and likewise the Bach. However it is permitted to sell it to a gentile even for an animal in an enclosed wide courtyard, since some acharonim are lenient in this — it is a safek in the law.
And if it cut off, etc. And likewise if it killed and did not cut off — and such is the implication of the poskim.
But if it wounded, etc. And it implies that in such a case, even if it and they are silent, all are forbidden, as below seif 14.
Wounded. And likewise if it trampled even without wounding — and such is explicit in Terumat HaDeshen daf 44 side b.
But if it trampled others, etc. And it implies that whoever is found partly trampled — even if we do not know that it trampled them after it killed one of them — all are forbidden; and likewise at the end of responsum 42 and Orach Chaim general rule 57 he wrote that likewise if it killed more than one, all are forbidden; and Darkei Moshe and Bach bring it to rule halachah, and likewise in Sefer Torat Chaim and in responsum Masat Binyamin he attacked Orach Chaim on this, that we attribute it to say: the first first that came to its hand it killed until its wrath subsided; and his words are not compelling.
