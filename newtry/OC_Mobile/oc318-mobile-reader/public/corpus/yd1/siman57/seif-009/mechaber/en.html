They are concerned for a safek of derusah — such as if they saw a lion enter among cattle to a narrow place from which they cannot flee from it, such as if it entered their pen (Tur in name of Rashba), and likewise a predatory bird that entered a cage full of birds (or a chicken coop) (Orach Chaim general rule 57) — they are concerned that perhaps it trampled. In what case is this said? When it is silent and they are trembling (meaning trembling and crying out); but if it is silent and they are silent, or it and they are trembling — they are not concerned. And if it cut off the head of one of them — they are not concerned for the others, even if it is silent and they are trembling, for we say: its wrath has already subsided. And specifically when it killed it; but if it wounded one of them — they are concerned for all of them, for all the more so its anger was enflamed in it when it saved itself and did not kill it: {Rama: And even if it killed one of them — specifically when we did not see that it trampled others; but if it trampled others — behold we see that its wrath did not subside and everything in the pen is forbidden (Orach Chaim HaAruch general rule 56).}
