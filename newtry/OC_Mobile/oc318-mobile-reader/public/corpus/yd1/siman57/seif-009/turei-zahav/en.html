To a narrow place. The Beit Yosef wrote it appears from Semag and Semak that any place not very narrow where they can flee somewhat is considered wide; but from the Rashba that the Tur brings, a wide place is a valley and the like, but not a house or large courtyard. However one may reject and say he mentioned common usage, not precisely, end of his words. Maharshal wrote specifically where animals graze; what Semag and Semak wrote to permit in a wide place is only for birds that slip away from the predator — in a wide courtyard they are not concerned, only in a coop that is narrow for them. The reason: wherever they can flee from the predator, it is safek whether it entered — permitted unless they cannot escape at all — not like the Beit Yosef, end of his words. And so appears primary, for Terumat HaDeshen HaAruch also mentioned where they graze in open space, not as in Sefer Masat Binyamin to be lenient in this.
If it is silent and they are silent. We say perhaps they are accustomed to each other; and when both tremble we say each fears the other.
But if it trampled others. It appears likewise if it wounded others — one reason: we see its wrath did not subside from killing the first; therefore we say also after killing one its anger was enflamed since it did not kill the others it wounded; but if it did not wound others, only killed two or more — we also say its wrath subsided after all the killings, for the Ran wrote regarding cutting off one's head, etc., and these are his words: we are not concerned perhaps at first it trampled others and then killed this one, for since they stand where it can trample whichever it wishes, I say it trampled first and killed, end of his words. Behold we do not establish concern for the others when we can say it killed this one first; likewise when it killed many. And so I found in Sefer Masat Binyamin in novellae on laws at the end of the book, though he wrote there "and not as the implication of Orach Chaim" — he hints at what Orach Chaim wrote general rule 57 regarding an incident where a cat put its hand in a coop called wachtelin, took some, ate them, some remained dead in the coop and some alive, and they forbade all, end of his words. And if because of that — there it deals with a coop, and it was explained below that in a coop we are stringent in all cases.
