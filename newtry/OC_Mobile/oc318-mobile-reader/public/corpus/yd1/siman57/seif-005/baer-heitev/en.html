Struck it. The Shach wrote: implies if it struck they are concerned — deals when it is silent and they tremble; therefore even if it did not strike, chickens forbidden if not domesticated. From Rashba's language the Tur brings — specifically when it pursues and struck, since the blow is from anger; when both silent, for blow alone not concerned; nevertheless do not be lenient for blow alone. Doubt for practical law if permitted to sell to gentile from double safek; one stringent to kill first then sell — well done. Taz wrote: whatever is not manner of blow but standing and placing hand is mere play; Bach too lenient for all who did not strike through pursuit; likewise entering chicken coop silent and they cry without knowing if struck — permission to sell; stringent to kill first then sell — well done (Beit Yosef: all acharonim stringent — whoever we saw struck forbidden to sell except kill first; see there).
