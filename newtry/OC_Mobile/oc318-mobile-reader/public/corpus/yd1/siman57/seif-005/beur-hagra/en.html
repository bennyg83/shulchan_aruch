Selection: there are those who say that nowadays, etc. And it appears to me that in large ones one may practice leniency, even though there is derusah — nevertheless it is not their way, as written in chapter 1 of Bava Kamma and chapter 3 of Ketubot; and the Rosh wrote that even though a dog's way is to eat even large carcasses, nevertheless they are not accustomed to kill them — evidently it is not their way and there is no concern (end of selection).
