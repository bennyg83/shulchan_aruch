(15) [Seif 2] All birds have no derisah except hawk in kids and lambs, etc.; specifically hawk in kids and lambs; large has no derisah in kids and lambs. Perishah ch. 7; Kenesset HaGedolah Hagahot Tur ch. 12 in name of many poskim; Sha'arei De'ah ch. 8; Zivchei Arav ch. 2; but Shach s.k. 8 wrote all the more large falcon has derisah more than hawk; Peri Chadash ch. 8; Lechem Panim in seif 2; Zera Avraham ch. 299; Zivchei Torah ch. 9; Beit Avraham in Sha'arei De'ah ch. 8; Sha'arei De'ah ch. 3; lenient may rely on lenient authorities; Peri Chadash ch. 11; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 11 — lenient only in great loss.
(18) [Seif 3] Hawk has derisah, etc. Even if not reached cavity; if reached — explained derisah even in kids and lambs and all birds; entire siman even without reaching cavity. Shach s.k. 12; Peri Chadash ch. 13; Sha'arei De'ah ch. 14; Zera Avraham ch. 300; Chokhmat Adam general rule 25 ch. 9; Zivchei Torah ch. 13; Zivchei Arav ch. 3; Beit Avraham in Sha'arei De'ah ch. 11; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 13.
