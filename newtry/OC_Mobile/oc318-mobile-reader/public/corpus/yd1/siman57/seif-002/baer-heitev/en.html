The cavity. The Shach wrote: some poskim render treifah lest an internal organ was perforated, no less than thorn to cavity; I shortened here since we are not expert in derusah examination — render treifah in every case (and see seif 16: even where minute hole does not disqualify, derusah disqualifies in any amount since venom burns throughout).
