(Seif 2) All birds have no derisah, etc., and it perforates with its claw into the cavity, etc. Rambam's view: baraita hawk in animal not treifah until perforates cavity — not from thorn-hole but venom when perforates; final version Perek Elu Treifot 52b — large animals, rescuers; if perforates cavity — venom. Rambam does not distinguish rescuers. Question on Rambam from Tosafot: R' Chisda hawk in kids and lambs from baraita cat until cavity — perhaps baraita in large and like hawk in kids; Rambam: baraita even in large kids, Gemara knows hawk no derisah in large kids even if perforates — baraita in kids and lambs; Tosafot difficulty resolved; proves Rambam.
