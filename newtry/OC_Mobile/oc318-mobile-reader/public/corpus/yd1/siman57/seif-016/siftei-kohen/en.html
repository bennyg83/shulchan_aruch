They slaughter the trampled, etc. Indeed thus appears the view of Tosafot and poskim that examination is specifically after slaughter; but in my book I wrote that Rashi's view Perek Elu Treifot (daf 61 side b) is that examination is from outside while it is alive — its back, belly, and sides, and everything opposite the intestines; and if the flesh reddened there from outside, it is treifah, for it will certainly end up being burned before it until the intestines are perforated; and if it did not redden from outside, there is no need to examine again and it is kosher, for since the venom is not recognizable from outside, it certainly did not trample. On this basis I settled Rashi's words — they are not difficult at all for all the difficulties Tosafot, the Rosh, and their supporters raised; and I brought proof from the Gemara in Zevachim (daf 74 side b) regarding sacrifices mixed with treifot that they graze until they become unfit — it asks: this treifah — how? If he knows, let him come and take it; and if he does not know, how does he know? R' Yanai said: it can occur, such as if perforation of a thorn was mixed with trampling of a wolf, and it says there too: all hold like R' Yanai — we do not say between perforation of a thorn and trampling of a wolf it is known which is long and which is round, end of his words. And if so, why force it to be established simply, such as if safek derusah or definite derusah without recognizable place of trampling was mixed — since examination is needed specifically after slaughter, let them graze until unfit; and when he established it thus, all should have said so, and not "all hold like R' Yanai we do not say," etc.; and all the more so it is difficult from here for poskim that definite derusah — meaning we only saw it was trampled, even if the place of trampling is not recognizable from outside — examination does not help, even for Rav who is not concerned for safek derusah, and this is the view of the Rosh and Ran; see there; or examination is from outside; and examine, for I shortened since there is no practical difference for us, as we are not expert.
