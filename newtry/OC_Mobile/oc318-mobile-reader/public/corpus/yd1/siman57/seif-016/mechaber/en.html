How is the examination conducted? They slaughter the trampled animal and examine its entire body cavity from the palm of the thigh until the skull; if the whole is found intact from all kinds of treifot and no mark of derisah is found in it — behold it is permitted. And if a mark of derisah is found in it — it is forbidden. What is a mark of derusah? That the flesh reddens opposite the intestines; and if the flesh opposite the intestines was dissolved until it became like flesh that a doctor scrapes from a wound — they see the flesh as if deficient and it is treifah.
