(Siman 57 Shulchan Aruch seif 1) Such as in calves, etc. Shach s.k. 2 and Maharshal and Bach forbid calves; Maharshal brought support from Rama seif 3 "specifically in large but in small they have derisah." Taz s.k. 3 — to resolve: mishnah teaches hawk derisah in thin bird and large hawk in large bird — excludes other impure birds in large like hawk; "derisah of large in large bird" excludes hawk in large bird — large species; hawk in large unlike wolf in small where mishnah teaches wolf in small to exclude cat — even in its kind cat has no derisah; if wolf in its kind, lion in large would also be in its kind — would exclude wolf from large — but even in its kind wolf does not trample — Maharshal's proof hidden.
