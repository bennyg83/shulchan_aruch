The wolf. The Shach wrote: since many poskim forbid a wolf even in a large animal, therefore where possible to sell to a gentile without loss (and certainly permitted to sell, as the Bach wrote), one should sell and it is forbidden to slaughter.
In calves. The Shach wrote: some poskim wrote in soft calves; see siman 35 and 39; be stringent where there is no great loss to sell to a gentile even if one must reduce the price — obligated to sell, since otherwise some forbid a wolf even in a large animal as above (Beit Yosef wrote: now custom is to forbid wolf derusah even in large animal like Tosafot and Bach; Shach and Peri Chadash wrote where possible to sell without great loss — sell and forbid slaughter; Taz is lenient like Shulchan Aruch).
And upward. The Shach wrote: but the wolf itself does not; also be stringent with bear and leopard that derise in large animal — to sell to gentile certainly permitted (but in small animal forbidden, no less than wolf); and concern for stringency only, not leniency, such as covering blood and gifts if kohen seized — see Shach end.
