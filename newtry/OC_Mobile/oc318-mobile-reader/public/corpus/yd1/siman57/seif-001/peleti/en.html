Wolf has no derisah in large animal. Shach s.k. 1: many poskim forbid wolf even in large — be stringent. Rosh ruled leniently, challenged Rashi; Peri Chadash challenged Rosh; Rosh's Mordechai proof is Ra'avyah like Rambam. Orach Chaim like Rosh. Semak 86: concern in arkum. Rosh for first version Rav "from wolf upward"; Rashi difficult; conclusion: wolf no derisah in large. Beit Yosef thought Rashi stringent — not found. Rashi "from wolf" — wolf large, cat small; Gemara cat in small; wolf in large. Maharashal: undecided; safek lenient; definite stringent.
