The laws of an animal that was trampled (derusah) by a predatory beast. In it are 21 seifim: Derusah (the explanation of derisah is a blow with which the beast or bird struck with its claws) is treifah. Not every beast and bird are equal in their derisah; rather there are gradations. How so: a lion has derisah even in a large ox and in the largest of beasts. A wolf does not have derisah in a large animal, and not even in the smallest of them, such as calves; but it has derisah in a small animal, and even in the largest of small animals such as large sheep. A cat, mongoose, and fox do not have derisah in large sheep, but they have derisah in kids and lambs, and all the more so in all birds. A weasel does not have derisah in an animal at all, and not even in kids and lambs, but it has derisah in all birds, even in the largest of them. A dog does not have derisah at all, even in birds. And if it perforated into the cavity — its law is like a thorn that perforated into the cavity. All other impure beasts: any that is not as large as a wolf does not have derisah in the largest of small animals in sheep and goats, even if it is as large as a wolf; but it has derisah in kids and lambs, and all the more so in birds. And if it was larger than a wolf — its law is like a wolf and it has derisah in a small animal, but not in a large animal, even if it is as large as a lion. If it was larger than a lion — behold it is like a lion and has derisah in everything:
