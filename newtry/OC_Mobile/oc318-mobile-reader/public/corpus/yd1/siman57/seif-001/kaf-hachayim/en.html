(1) [Seif 1] Derusah is treifah. The reason for derusah is that venom descends and burns the flesh and perforates within the flesh, and there is concern it descended where it became treifah through minute perforation. Levush. Zivchei Torah ch. 1; and Tosafot wrote (Chullin 42a) the reason is not because it will ultimately perforate — for then it would be included in perforation — rather derusah's reason is venom burns and its end is to die. Zivchei Torah there.
