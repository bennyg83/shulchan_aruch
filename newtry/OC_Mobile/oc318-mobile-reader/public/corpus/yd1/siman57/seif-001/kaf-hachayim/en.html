(1) [Seif 1] Derusah is treifah. The reason for derusah is that venom descends and burns the flesh and perforates within the flesh, and there is concern it descended where it became treifah through minute perforation. Levush. Zivchei Torah ch. 1; and Tosafot wrote (Chullin 42a) the reason is not because it will ultimately perforate — for then it would be included in perforation — rather derusah's reason is venom burns and its end is to die. Zivchei Torah there.
(2) There. Derusah treifah. Meaning one who strikes with claws and injects venom, and it is red as they say in Perek Elu Treifot: two drops of blood were found and it was rendered treifah. Peri Chadash ch. 1; Zivchei Torah there; Mikdash Me'at ch. 1.
(3) There. Derusah treifah. And there is no difference whether the trampled or the predator is male or female. Orach Chaim general rule 57 ch. 1; Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur ch. 4; Anaf Yosef siman 40 ch. 5; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 1.
(4) There. How a lion has derisah even in a large ox, etc. Even if larger than a lion. Bach; Taz s.k. 2; Peri Chadash ch. 2; Sha'arei De'ah ch. 3; Anaf Yosef ch. 1; Zivchei Torah ch. 2; even whether there are rescuers or not — and all the more in smaller than it. Peri Toar there; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 2.
(5) There. A wolf has no derisah in a large animal, etc.; and some say a wolf has derisah in a large animal. Bach; Beit Yehudah ch. 1; and it appears where possible to sell to a gentile without loss — sell and forbid slaughter. Shach s.k. 1; Peri Chadash end ch. 3; Lev Chaim ch. 1; Sha'arei De'ah ch. 6; Chokhmat Adam general rule 25 ch. 2; Zivchei Torah ch. 3; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 3.
(6) There. And even in the smallest of them, such as calves. Maharshal Perek Elu Treifot siman 70 and Bach forbid calves; be stringent without great loss; but certainly permitted to sell — not like Beit Yosef who forbids selling to gentile; where no great loss in selling to gentile even if reducing price — obligated to sell. Shach s.k. 2; Peri Chadash ch. 4; Lev Chaim ch. 1; Chokhmat Adam there; in safek derusah be lenient — double safek perhaps halachah like those who hold no wolf derusah in large, and if you say derusah, perhaps it did not trample. Kenesset Olam ch. 1; Sha'arei De'ah ch. 1; Chokhmat Adam there; Beit Avraham in Orach Chaim ch. 1; Givat Sha'ul siman 7; Peri Toar ch. 2; Anaf Yosef siman 40 end ch. 2; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 3.
(7) There. Cat, mongoose, fox, etc. — but they have derisah in kids and lambs; whenever called so in human language. Chokhmat Adam there ch. 3; Beit Avraham in Orach Chaim ch. 1; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 10; see above siman 34 s.k. 162.
(8) There. And all the more in all birds. Even birds larger than kids and lambs. Peri Toar ch. 5; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 4.
(9) There. A dog has no derisah, etc.; if perforated to cavity — law like thorn; we are not expert in examination and treifah. Beit Avraham in Sha'arei De'ah ch. 5; Anaf Yosef ch. 10; Beit Yitzchak in Amudei Zahav ch. 8; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 5; if perforated in tooth — if wide, has examination like wide windpipe siman 91; Sha'arei De'ah ch. 4; Chokhmat Adam there; Beit Yitzchak there; Anaf Yosef there; Zivchei Tzedek there — and we did so in practice.
(10) Other impure beasts; Rama gloss: and some stringent still, etc. Bear and leopard above wolf — law like lion, derise in large animal; to sell trampled to gentile certainly permitted; in small animal forbidden to sell — no less than wolf. Shach s.k. 6; Peri Chadash end ch. 7; Lev Chaim ch. 4; Sha'arei De'ah ch. 6; Zera Avraham ch. 298; Chokhmat Adam there ch. 6; Zivchei Torah ch. 5; Zivchei Arav ch. 1 end ch. 1; Anaf Yosef ch. 4; Beit Avraham in Sha'arei De'ah ch. 6; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 6.
(11) There in gloss. And one should be concerned for stringency. In great loss permitted. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef ch. 13; Sha'arei De'ah end ch. 7; Yad Eliyahu end ch. 9; in large animal — permitted per Shulchan Aruch; in small — do not permit even in great loss since Shulchan Aruch also has derisah.
(13) All pure beasts and birds have no venomous derisah. Tosafot Chullin 53a s.v. other; Bach; Kenesset HaGedolah Hagahot Tur ch. 3; Mishnat Zekenim ch. 1; Beit Avraham in Sha'arei De'ah ch. 1; Anaf Yosef siman 40 ch. 5; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 8.
(14) Impure animal. No derisah. Kenesset HaGedolah there; Mishnat Zekenim there; Beit Avraham there; Anaf Yosef there; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 9.
(16) There. And it perforates with claw into cavity, etc.; see Peri Chadash ch. 9 who extended; concludes for law per siman 91 we are not expert in minute examination — now whatever perforated to cavity, even large sheep, even without reddening — treifah; not like Mechaber following Rambam. Zera Avraham there; Zivchei Arav there; Anaf Yosef siman 40 ch. 7; Zivchei Tzedek ch. 12.
(17) There. And it perforates into cavity, etc. Not like other derisah where reddened flesh is concerned — hawk venom not so strong; even reddened without hole — no derisah concern until perforates cavity. Zivchei Torah ch. 9.
(19) There in gloss. And specifically in large ones. Meaning in large chickens. Tur and Beit Yosef in Rashba's name; Levush likewise.
(20) There in gloss. But in small ones they have derisah. Even among large species such as geese and turkey while small. Sha'arei De'ah ch. 13-14; Beit Avraham in Sha'arei De'ah ch. 12.
(21) There in gloss. But in small ones they have derisah. Even though bodily larger than hawk. Rashba in Taharat HaBayit daf 44; Peri Chadash ch. 11; Sha'arei De'ah ch. 16; Zivchei Torah ch. 13.
