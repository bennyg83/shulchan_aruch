(Siman 57 seif 2) Except hawk has derisah in kids and lambs and perforates claw to cavity then injects venom. Difference: thorn to cavity no examination siman 91; hawk examined — Taz proves hawk perforated to cavity has examination if venom reddened, if not not concerned. But Rashi Perek Elu Treifot 53, Rashba Taharat HaBayit 45, Taz seif 19 — hawk venom not red, no examination. Why Taz wrote this law? It appears: if perforated before us lodged in organ e.g. heart not cavity, claw slipped remains lodged, slaughter immediately — for this we do not concern internal organ holes siman 91 seif 2; but from derusah treifah no examination even Mechaber; though claw remains lodged, when foot removed venom injects per Taz and Ba'er Heitev.
