There is no derisah except while the predator and the trampled animal are alive. How so: if its claw became lodged in the trampled animal and until it removed its claw the predator died, or its hand was cut off, or the trampled animal was slaughtered — behold this is kosher. In what case is this said? When we saw from beginning to end; but if it came before us with a claw lodged in it — we are concerned that perhaps it trampled and returned and trampled again; and therefore those sides in predatory birds — even if they slaughtered the trampled animal before removing its claw — it is forbidden, for thus is the way it strikes it many times until it captures it.
