And that which they are concerned — specifically when we knew that the lion entered; but if it is a safek whether it entered or not, or a safek whether it is a lion or a dog, or perhaps it was struck by a reed — they are not concerned. And specifically when we have before us a dog and a lion, or a lion and a reed; but when it comes before us bubbling blood and we do not know from what — we attribute to what is prevalent, whether to be lenient or to be stringent: (and it was already explained above siman 33 — if blood bubbles in the neck they are accustomed to forbid it) (my humble opinion and Orach Chaim general rule 57 law 8):
