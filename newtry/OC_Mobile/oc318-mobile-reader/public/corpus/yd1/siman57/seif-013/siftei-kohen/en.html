But if it is a safek whether it entered, etc. For there is a double safek — perhaps it did not enter at all, and if you say it entered, perhaps it did not trample. And in a safek of lion and dog, or windpipe — even though both are before us, we attribute to be lenient, for most animals are presumed not derusah and most beasts are presumed not to derise. And there is a difference in what we attribute to dog or windpipe, that examination is not needed except where blood bubbles — Rashba; and there are several ramifications in this, and I shortened since we forbid in every case, even an uncommon matter.
And it was already explained, etc. If the windpipe before us is a wide windpipe, it should be permitted even for us, for we are expert only in minute perforation, not in such a case, as the Tur and Rav in the gloss above siman 91 seif 2 wrote; and this is from the Rosh's words who wrote so regarding the law here; and examine.
