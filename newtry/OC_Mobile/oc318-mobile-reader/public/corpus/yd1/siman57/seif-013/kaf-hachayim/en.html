(12) And mouse. No derisah. Tuv Ta'am siman 40 ch. 4; likewise Beit Yitzchak in Taharat HaBayit ch. 3: some large mice called rats enter coops and harm birds — no derisah, only bite with teeth — like mere thorn, examine where minute hole disqualifies; if hole in head at fontanelle or neck opposite nape, examine skull; if near esophagus no examination and treifah; opposite intestines examine; if kosher — law of other dangerously ill for convulsions. Beit Avraham in Orach Chaim ch. 16; when known mice entered — not concerned by default as below seif 13.
