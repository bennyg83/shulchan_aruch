The reason for this reasoning is that we do not say we are concerned for a safek of derusah except where there is a basis for concern; if not so, most beasts are presumed not to derise, and likewise in Terumat HaDeshen; and the Taz wrote the reason is that it is a double safek — perhaps they were all silent or all cried out, and if you say it was silent and they cried out, perhaps they cried from fear and did not trample, end of his words. And I never heard such a double safek, for in the first safek, when you say perhaps they cried out or were all silent, you must also say perhaps it did not trample — and that is the second safek, and everything goes to one place. And do not say what is written in the Rashba's novellae — without our seeing it enter and a claw found on its back it is kosher — from venom a double safek: perhaps it did not enter, and if you say it entered, perhaps it rubbed against the wall; for Darkei Moshe and Maharshal Perek Elu Treifot siman 74 already explained that the reason is that it is more likely it rubbed against the wall, since we did not see it enter; and if so, I must say from the outset that it entered, and it is a double safek; not so here; and examine.
