Those who forbid — it appears their reason: since an animal while alive stands in presumption of prohibition, we do not lenient from safek, and there is no double safek here; therefore we go stringently, and the Gemara was lenient only when safek whether a lion was there or not, as in the next seif that has double safek; see siman 29 seif 1.
