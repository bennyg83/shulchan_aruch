But where we saw it leaped, etc. The reason in Terumat HaDeshen siman 178: here we say from fear it was silent; and there it deals with a bear that leaped on an ox — that bear was domesticated with people who were accustomed to play with it when chained, but it was not mentioned as domesticated with animals; therefore it is surprising why he concluded here "even though domesticated." Nevertheless according to this reason of the law he spoke well — even if domesticated, for we see it performed an act of trampling and abandoned its domestication; see what is written in seif 5 from this.
