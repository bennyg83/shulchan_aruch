With the hand. The Shach wrote: to exclude hind legs of beast; in bird only two legs — no division, derises with legs. If trampled — is fetus inside harmed by drop of trampling? See Taz s.s. 79 left unresolved; Beit Yosef siman 1 permits fetus but derisah of lion forbids fetus. Peri Chadash: now not expert in examination — if fetus not permitted by its slaughter, fetus forbidden; if fetus born and lives twelve months, permitted since even definite derusah twelve months permits; if slaughtered within twelve months forbidden — when trampled before us opposite cavity where concern fetus also trampled; if trampled in neck or sides, obvious drop does not harm fetus; see Peri Chadash siman 79.
