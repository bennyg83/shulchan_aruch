(6) There. And even in the smallest of them, such as calves. Maharshal Perek Elu Treifot siman 70 and Bach forbid calves; be stringent without great loss; but certainly permitted to sell — not like Beit Yosef who forbids selling to gentile; where no great loss in selling to gentile even if reducing price — obligated to sell.
