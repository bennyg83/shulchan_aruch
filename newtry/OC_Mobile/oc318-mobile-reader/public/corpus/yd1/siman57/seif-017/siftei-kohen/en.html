An animal that was trampled, etc. But a bird is kosher through the windpipe alone, and one may slaughter the windpipe l'chatchila, and after slaughter turn the esophagus and examine it from inside, as above siman 33 seif 6. And even though l'chatchila one must slaughter two simanim even for a bird, as above siman 21 — here, since it cannot be done otherwise, it is considered b'dieved. But for an animal, which needs two simanim even b'dieved, we are concerned lest it slaughtered in the place of venom, and therefore examination of the esophagus from inside does not help. However, if it is known that it trampled elsewhere and not the esophagus, examination helps even for an animal; and see in the Beit Yosef.
