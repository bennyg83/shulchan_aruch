A Sefer Torah must be written with ink made from the soot of oils soaked in gallnut water. (And l'chatchila it is good to be careful not to make the ink except from things that come from a tree—as appears in the Zohar, parashas Terumah, daf 72a.) And if one wrote it with gallnut water and gum, it is valid; but not with other kinds of dyes, such as red and green and the like—for if one wrote even a single letter with other kinds of dyes, or in gold, it is invalid. {Rama: And likewise it is forbidden to write any name from the twenty-four books except with ink (R' Yerucham in the name of Even Yerachi). And some say we require ink only for a Sefer Torah (Mordechai, beginning of Hilchos Ketanim).}
