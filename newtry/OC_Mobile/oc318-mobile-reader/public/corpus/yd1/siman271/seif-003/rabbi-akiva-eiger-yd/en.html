(Seif 5) A Torah scroll needs ruling. In Beit Yosef he brought Mordechai in Hilchot Ketanim who wrote in the name of Or Zarua that regarding the ruling itself there is doubt — whether it must be visible and permanent forever like with iron, or whether ruling suffices so the scribe sees he will write evenly like with lead even though afterward the ruling is erased; and the language of ruling suggests a groove and trench — end quote; and see in Sotah (17a) that Tosafot were uncertain in a Sotah scroll — a question whether ruling helps when one rules after writing between line and line.
