One writes a Sefer Torah on gevil, which is hide that was not split, and one writes on it on the side of the hair. And if one wrote a Sefer Torah on klaf, it is valid provided it is written on the inner side that faces the flesh. {Rama: And our klaf is more preferable than gevil, and one writes on them l'chatchila on the side of the flesh; and nowadays one does not write on gevil (R' Yitzchak Minz siman 66). And if one changed and wrote on our klaf on the side of hair, it is invalid (Or Zarua, Hilchos Tefillin). However, some are lenient in this (Tur and Rabbenu Yerucham).}
