A reed. Taz writes: I have heard people say that printed sacred books do not have the same sanctity as books written by hand, but it appears to me that there is no distinction at all. The act of printing is sacred just like writing. He brings proofs from the Gemara showing that printing is properly considered writing. Nevertheless, for a get one certainly should not use printing because it somewhat resembles engraving. Regarding the sanctity of sacred books, however, anyone who is lenient will ultimately be held accountable. Evening.
