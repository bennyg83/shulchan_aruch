A pen. The Taz writes: I have heard people say that printed sefarim do not possess the same sanctity as handwritten ones. It appears to me that there is no distinction at all for printing is sacred like writing. He brings proofs from the Gemara that printing is properly considered writing. Nevertheless, a get should certainly not be produced by printing, since it somewhat resembles engraving. Regarding the sanctity of sefarim, however, anyone who rules leniently will ultimately be held accountable. See siman 32 and siman 40. Mixed.
