And fowl — see in Sefer Derech Chaim on Laws of Shechitah and Tereifot siman 23 in the gloss who wrote that from Noda BiYehudah second series part 14 siman 12 it seems obvious to him that bird hide has the law of gevil, klaf, and duchsustos mentioned regarding books, tefillin, and mezuzot — doubtful, for we never heard such in Gemara and poskim on bird hide which is thin; and Rambam chapter 1 of Laws of Tefillin explicitly infers the law of klaf and duchsustos is specifically cattle and wild animal hide; bird hide without this distinction — one may say it has the law of our klafim; nevertheless it is doubtful on which side one writes — whether on the hair side as our klaf law or on every side — end.
