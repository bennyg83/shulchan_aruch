A mourner is forbidden all thirty days to marry a woman. And it contains 3 seifim. Forbidden to marry a woman all thirty days, even without a feast; and after thirty permitted, even for father and mother, even to make a feast; and permitted to betroth a woman even on the day of death, provided he not make a feast unless thirty days passed: {Rama: (and some say also betrothal forbidden all thirty days) (Tur and Rosh and Ramban, and so is primary).}
