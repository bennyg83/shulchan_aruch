It appears that the Rambam explains the Gemara's question, "he objects," etc., and likewise the Mishnah's phrase "except for a widow," as referring also to a meal. He explains "mourning is different," etc., to mean that the mourning of Tisha B'Av is stricter; therefore business activity is forbidden. According to Rashi's explanation, it is difficult why business should be forbidden, and see Tosafot cited above, who were pressed by this and wrote: Rabbeinu Tam explains, etc., and one must say that public mourning, etc. The Rambam further inferred this from the statement here, "all thirty days for marriage," as Tosafot asked there; see there.
(Likkut) Evel Rabbati chapter 7 states: thirty days for betrothal and thirty days for marriage. But there it says regarding both that this is specifically with a meal. See the Ramban's novellae to Yevamot there and Torat HaAdam: how so for betrothal, etc.; how so for marriage, etc.
