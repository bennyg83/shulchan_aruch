Three festivals. Dagul MeRevavah writes that Shemini Atzeret counts as an independent festival for this law as well. Chatam Sofer, siman 350, reports that his father-in-law R. Akiva Eiger disagreed, based on Rosh, Perek Elu Megalchin, siman 95. Chatam Sofer further discusses a man whose wife became ill on Erev Pesach and died on the third day of Chol HaMoed. More than twelve months had passed, but not three festivals, and he wished to remarry. R. Akiva Eiger permitted it and Chatam Sofer agreed, citing numerous grounds for leniency. In the preceding responsum Chatam Sofer similarly permits a great rav whose wife died to remarry after two festivals, because although his daughter and daughter-in-law were in the house, they were not accustomed to manage the household in the manner appropriate to the rav's stature and distinguished guests, whereas the proposed widow had managed the home of a distinguished talmid chacham in a large community. He adds that she herself was the daughter and widow of great Torah scholars and had endured severe suffering, so enabling her to leave loneliness and confinement was a significant mitzvah.
