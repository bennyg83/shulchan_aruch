It is permitted to marry her, etc. Since there is loss in the matter if he does not marry, and he has also not fulfilled peru u’rvu. See above siman 342.
It is permitted to marry her. Immediately, and he may have relations with her after seven days; so wrote the Atzei Zahav. The Bach wrote that he may not have relations with her until after thirty. It appears that they do not disagree: the Atzei Zahav speaks where he has not fulfilled peru u’rvu, and the Bach speaks where he has fulfilled it.
For she has compassion, etc. Therefore it is permitted even without loss.
