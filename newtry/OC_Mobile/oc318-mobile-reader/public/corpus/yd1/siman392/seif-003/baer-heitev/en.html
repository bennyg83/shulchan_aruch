To take her in. The Shach wrote: the reason is that there would be a financial loss if he does not take her in, and also he has not fulfilled the mitzvah of procreation. See above siman 342. The Taz wrote that this "taking her in" refers to after seven days, for within seven — even where there would be a loss — it is forbidden; see there.
He is permitted to take her in. Immediately, and to be with her after seven — so wrote the Levush. The Bach wrote that he should not be with her until after thirty days. It appears that they do not disagree: the Levush speaks of one who has not fulfilled the mitzvah of procreation, and the Bach speaks of one who has. Thus far the Shach.
