His wife died, etc. But a woman whose husband died mourns only thirty days, except that she is forbidden to become betrothed or marry until three months because of distinction, as stated in Yevamot and explained in Even HaEzer siman 13; end quote from the Tur. So wrote the poskim. The Mordechai wrote the reason that a woman need not wait three festivals: because a woman is content with any husband and forgets the love of the first after thirty days. If it happened that he married within thirty days of mourning for other relatives, he may not have relations. This is the Rambam’s view according to the Bach’s explanation of his words; see there.
His law is like other relatives. Above, siman 391. Likewise, all other mourning applies only thirty days, as with other relatives.
The mitzvah of peru u’rvu. In the manner explained in Even HaEzer siman 1.
It is permitted to betroth immediately. The Bach wrote that this follows the Mechaber’s view, who permits in seif 1 to betroth on the day of death, but we do not rule so; rather, he may betroth her and marry her immediately after seven days.
