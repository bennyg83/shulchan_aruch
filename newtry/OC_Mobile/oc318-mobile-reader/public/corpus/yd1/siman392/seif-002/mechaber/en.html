If his wife died — forbidden to marry another until three festivals pass: {Rama: (but to enter banquet house his law is like other relatives) (Tur);} and Rosh HaShanah and Yom Kippur are not considered festivals for this; and if he did not fulfill peru urevu, or has small children, or has no one to serve him — permitted to betroth immediately and enter after seven, and not have relations until after thirty days, unless he did not fulfill peru urevu — then permitted relations after seven: {Rama: Same for other mourning, even mourning father and mother — permitted to marry and have relations after seven if did not fulfill peru urevu; some say even if wealthy and can hire servants, if his daughter or daughter-in-law is not in his house to serve him in head-washing or other matters he is ashamed before others — called having no one to serve him, and similar (Piskei Maharai siman 13); therefore custom spread that many are lenient in marrying within three festivals; conscientious person should be concerned; one seized by official who will not release until he marries within three festivals after wife's death — some permit because of his distress (Beit Yosef), and so appears primary.}
