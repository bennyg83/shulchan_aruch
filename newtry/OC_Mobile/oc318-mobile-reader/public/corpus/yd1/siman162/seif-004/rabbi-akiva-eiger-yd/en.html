(Seif 4) One lends his sharecropper. Tur wrote: one who has sharecropper may lend him seah for seah, etc.; and in place where custom is landowner, etc., except unless he lent immediately when he descended into his field; and see Beit Yosef: when descended — not precisely only before descent, rather immediately after descent forbidden; in my opinion one can say Tur's intent: initially when he wrote "and sharecropper agreed" — specifically after descent as Beit Yosef wrote per Rashi; on this Tur wrote forbidden to lend after he already agreed and not permitted unless lent immediately when descended; and this is immediately when descended at time of agreement when he wishes to give as gift and at time of action tells him this seah landowner's obligation to give shall be upon me as loan, for I give him as gift that seed be mine — this is permitted as saying I give you maneh and each year dinar profit from it permitted as Beit Yosef wrote (s.k. 61) in name of Rivash; forbidden only after descent when sharecropper agreed to give as gift and after time wishes to borrow from him — debt already on sharecropper and like becoming obligated in his gift and after time fixed as loan — forbidden as written in Beit Yosef (seif 161).
