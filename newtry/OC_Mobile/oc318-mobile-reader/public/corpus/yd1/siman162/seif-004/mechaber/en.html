A person may lend his sharecropper (meaning: a sharecropper who plants or sows his fellow’s field and has half or a third in the produce) a se’ah for a se’ah for seed, whether before the sharecropper descended to the field or after he descended. To what does this apply? In a place where the practice is that the sharecropper provides the seed; but in a place where it is the practice for the landowner to provide the seed, if the sharecropper already descended to the field, it is forbidden.
