(Siman 162 Shulchan Aruch seif 1) Forbidden to borrow seah for seah even if he did not fix repayment time. That which mishnah teaches end ch. Eizehu Neshekh: one should not say to fellow lend me kor of wheat and I will give you at threshing floor — not to say forbidden only when fixing repayment time; rather teaches even if he said I will give at threshing floor where no way to appreciate at threshing floor — nevertheless forbidden; as Tosafot and Beit Yosef strained unnecessarily — see there.
