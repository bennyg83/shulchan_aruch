Seah for seah etc. Lending seah for seah is rabbinic and therefore cannot be extracted in court.
He did not fix etc. See above seif 3 in the hagahah.
Then etc. To exclude — if it does not go out then, even if it went out previously, e.g., a coin that was invalidated — it is forbidden, as stated in Tur and the poskim.
In this time etc. Meaning: even one who forbids in the Gemara's era lending a gold dinar for a gold dinar — that was because in their era the silver coins were good and valued and had little copper in them, and the gold dinar was considered produce relative to them; which is not so nowadays when most silver coins are copper — there; however he wrote there in the name of Tosafot that a gold dinar always has the law of a silver coin — see there; and so Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashba's responsum: this dinar which is a current coin is certainly permitted, for it is a coin and it is not what rises and falls but rather the produce — and so is explicit etc. — see there; and see Choshen Mishpat siman 203 seif 3-4.
