He lent him 2 se’ah-measures of wheat, and made a document for him for them: if the lender does not clarify that the market rate was set at the time of the loan, or that the borrower had a small amount of that type, the borrower swears that the market rate was not set and that he had nothing of that type, and he pays only money that they were worth at the time of the loan. (And the lender cannot say, “I acquired for him a small amount of my own through another,” for it is not effective without the knowledge of the borrower) (there). And if he became obligated to him for wheat, and the loan was in one place and the claim is in another place, and the wheat is more expensive in the place of the claim, he pays him only their value in the place of the loan. {Rama: They permitted borrowing when “he has” only a se’ah of wheat for a se’ah of wheat; but a se’ah of wheat for a se’ah of millet is forbidden, even though they are at one rate and he has millet (Tur in the name of Ra’avad and Sefer HaTerumos).}
