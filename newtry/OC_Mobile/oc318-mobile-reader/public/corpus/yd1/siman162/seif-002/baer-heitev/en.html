How much. For each seah and seah he lent we view his seah as immediately decided for lender; Bach wrote specifically that his intent be so — otherwise one of them intended ribbit and it is forbidden; and it seems to me even per his words this is lechatchilah; but if he lent and had a little of that kind even though he did not intend so — permitted and must pay; however specifically he has of that kind, but money even enough to buy all that kind — forbidden — end quote Shach. Taz wrote certainly lechatchilah both must know there is heter as Bach wrote; but bedieved if he lent seah for seah and has even if lender himself did not know he has — permitted since acquired per sages — unlike heter from market price where cannot say acquired for him since they belong to others; therefore no heter except they know market price exists; but if they did not know — forbidden to give him wheat if appreciated — as above main for halachah — end quote.
They may lend him. Bach disagrees and ruled: specifically if he gives as gift or transfers ownership — permitted afterward to borrow from him; but in loan since loan's beginning was given for spending — forbidden; and so Levush; Taz wrote it seems to me certainly if he already lent seah and fixed via heter — certainly can lend on it even many seahs since he already has seah in his possession; they forbade only if he now comes to make heter wishing to lend via this — no heter except he give one seah as gift — end quote.
