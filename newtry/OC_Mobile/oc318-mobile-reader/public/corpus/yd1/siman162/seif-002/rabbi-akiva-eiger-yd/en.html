(Seif 2 in Hagah) not called "he has." Even per Tosafot Chidushei Dinim: specifically to cheapen not called "he has," but for fixing price called "he has"; nevertheless one can say specifically regarding fixing price, but not regarding seah-for-seah loan; for we say in sugya: per one opinion since he has credit like "until my son comes" it is considered; and this is cheapening via purchase path; permitted to permit like seah-for-seah loan; and see Tosafot there who are more lenient than seah-for-seah loan; if so truthfully credit is not called "he has" for cheapening; likewise not called "he has" for seah-for-seah loan.
