(Siman 162, Shulchan Aruch, seif 1) It is forbidden to borrow one se'ah in exchange for one se'ah, even if he did not set a time for repayment. That which we learned at the end of Perek Eizehu Neshekh, "A person may not say to another: Lend me a kor of wheat, and I will give it to you at the threshing season," does not mean that it is forbidden only when he fixes a time for repayment. Rather, it teaches that even if he says, "I will give it to you at the threshing season," when produce does not ordinarily rise in price, it is nevertheless forbidden. This is as Tosafot wrote. The Beit Yosef strained unnecessarily to explain this; see there.
