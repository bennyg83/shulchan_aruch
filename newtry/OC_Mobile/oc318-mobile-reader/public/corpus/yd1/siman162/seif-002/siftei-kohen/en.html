Specifically from that same species. But money, even if it is enough that he could purchase all of that species — is forbidden; so is the consensus of the poskim; and so Beit Yosef.
He borrows on it several seahs. Even all together; and needless to say one who acquires in succession — for regarding each and every seah he borrows, we view his seah as if it is immediately acquired by the lender; and if it appreciates, it appreciates in his domain — Tur and the poskim.
To repay etc. Per Beit Yosef — it is not required that he intend to transfer against that small amount, for if so an ignorant person who does not know would be forbidden; rather the main reason is that since it is rabbinic, with any small reason they were lenient about it; and the Bach wrote specifically that he must intend thus, for otherwise one of them intended for the sake of interest and it is forbidden (and it seems to me Beit Yosef's view is also so); and it seems to me even per his words this is only lechatchilah — but if he borrowed and had a small amount of that species even if he did not intend thus, it is permitted and he is obligated to pay; and so it implies from the Rosh and the Tur in seif 5.
