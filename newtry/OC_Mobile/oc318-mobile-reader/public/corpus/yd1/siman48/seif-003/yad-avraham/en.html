(Seif 11) Needle found in kurkavan all swallowed in flesh thickness not recognizable any side — suspend lenient, etc. Hagahah: some stringent; concern their words if not great loss. Appears simple: if kofa inward — toward kurkavan side; point toward cavity — kosher for in kofa cannot puncture if kofa like date pit as siman 41 seif 6; so Ra'ash Orach Chaim regarding needle found in thigh thickness; Tur Yoreh De'ah siman 91 seif 3 implies explicitly when not recognizable any side; nevertheless if kofa outward — kosher; I would have said forbid kofa toward cavity; on this he gave reason its way to scrape wall; even kofa toward cavity enters — see there; but kofa outward without that — kosher.
