And it, etc., "rather since," etc. All this is Rambam's language; he explains what is written in the Gemara 52b "flesh covering most of the kereis in its majority" — meaning in the majority of its thickness; and all poskim disagree with him and wrote specifically mafpolash as the plain meaning of the sugya. (Collection:) Even though not, etc. — he learned from what is written "torn" and they did not say "pierced" as in gullet and spinal cord; and as written in the first chapter of Gittin — they tear paper for them, etc.; but in Tur haArukh he disagreed and said: why "torn" — because it is severed, specifically in all of it or its majority, unlike where even a tefach in a large animal, etc. — see there at length. And according to Tosafot in Gittin — there too they tear from side to side — see there 9b s.v. mikra'in, etc. (end of collection).
Since, etc., "or," etc. There Rav Ashi asked regarding majority, etc.; and Rashi there s.v. b'rov, etc.; and therefore it must be so — since it says "extend," etc., and he ruled stringently; and further we rule like R' Yosei bar Chanina and the question was resolved.
And how much, etc. Like R' Yehudah — all amoraim there hold like him.
