Sefer HaTe'udah wrote in name of Yam Shel Shlomo: if there is like a recognizable path until outside — treifah, for it appears from life it passed; and he wrote further in name of Tur Choshen Mishpat: specifically when punctures not found immediately when removed from animal but after a day or half day — we may suspect done by man; but if immediately when removed found thus — one may not permit, for certainly done before slaughter. And he wrote on this: for us there is no difference since Rama testified we practice to declare treifah in every manner without korat dam and without rust; nevertheless I wrote for other places that do not practice like Rama, etc. — see there; and see in She'elot uTeshuvot Tolodot Yitzchak siman 5 who wrote on this: even for us there is a difference per Shach s.k. 30 in name of Bach that even punctured from both sides if no korat dam found one may permit in our time in great loss; and so one should rule; nevertheless if there is like a path outside or if immediately when removed found thus — one may not permit even in great loss — see there; and it is simple; and it appears Turei Beit Shmuel for his view who wrote in Orach Chaim 17 one forbids even in great loss punctured from both sides — unlike Shach and Peri Megadim s.k. 30 — properly there is a difference:
