Treifah. Orach Chaim haArukh wrote: if a needle is found in the wall of beit hakosot along its length such that one cannot understand which side the pouch faces — treifah, since its cavity is wide; if through the esophagus it should have been found on the secretion — rather it certainly punctured and came. Maharshal explained: along its length means the needle lies in the wall of beit hakosot along the length of the needle — and one may not be lenient even from one side and from inside. Therefore there is no permit from one side except when the needle lies in the cavity of beit hakosot as food goes — not when stuck along the length of the wall.
