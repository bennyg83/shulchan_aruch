The laws of treifot in the kivah and kereis. In it are 12 seifim. Kivah (kivah is the pouch in the animal's innards into which food enters the kereis; at the end of the kereis called paunch it is shaped like a hat and is called beit hakosot, and food enters from beit hakosot to the meses and from the meses to the kivah and from the kivah to the dekin) — if punctured, treifah.
