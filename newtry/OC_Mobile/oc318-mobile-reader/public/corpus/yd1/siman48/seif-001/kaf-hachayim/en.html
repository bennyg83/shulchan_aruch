(1) [Seif 1] Kivah punctured — treifah; and if chelev on the yeter seals the puncture — kosher; but on the arch does not seal. Explanation: kivah shaped like arch; chelev on it outside called dakashta; inside circle called yeter — chelev in extra place on arch. Even though we rule siman 64 seif 14 chelev on yeter forbidden and tame chelev does not seal — reason here it seals because Gemara says people of Eretz Yisrael eat chelev on yeter; people of Bavel practiced forbid; nevertheless helps also Babylonians who permit through sealing puncture; but on arch forbidden to eat for everyone — therefore does not seal. Taz s.k. 1; from Gemara and Beit Yosef; see below ot 7.
