(Siman 48 seif 4) If this flesh was bored in circle or length if more than sela — treifah if this tear stretched will stand tefach. Appears to learn: in large animal tear measure tefach; in small animal measure is majority which is less than tefach; same in boring — if stretch tear will stand at its majority less than sela — examine well. However I saw Turei Beit Shmuel not so; see Peri Megadim and Lechem. And know: per Rambam and Shulchan Aruch previous seif if majority thickness torn — like mafpolash; same regarding bored; so clear from Rashba Tur haArukh (43) — see there; examine well. Peri Megadim simply; wondered on Shach s.k. 7. However Shach followed his long book that wrote per Rambam bored means mafpolash; if not mafpolash not bored; we estimate length width circle like sela — see there. Peri Megadim challenged: Gemara says if stretched stands tefach — implies because of tefach; if so even not mafpolash; no proof — nevertheless lenient from tefach measure in small animal as Turei Beit Shmuel wrote. Thus bored in circle no majority in length or width alone lenient; for this reason lenient also not mafpolash — only helps in mafpolash since no majority on one side only in circle. And in my humble opinion bring proof for Rambam — resolves Tosafot Orach Chaim 43 s.v. henakh, etc. — majority outer torn and flesh covering also counted as one; so Turei Beit Shmuel. What wondered on Beit Yosef — Rambam holds puncture to cavity requires examination — Shach already preceded in long book.
