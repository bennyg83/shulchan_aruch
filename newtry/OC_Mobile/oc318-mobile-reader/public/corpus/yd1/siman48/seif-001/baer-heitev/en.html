On the yeter. Taz wrote: even though we rule in siman 64 that chelev on the yeter is forbidden and tame chelev does not seal — nevertheless since the people of Eretz Yisrael eat chelev on the yeter, it helps the people of Bavel to permit sealing the puncture (but chelev on the arch is forbidden to everyone — therefore it does not seal. It turns out: a peg or needle stuck at the end of the animal's kivah and the kivah was not punctured from side to side, only a hard growth called wart stands on the kivah opposite the place the needle is stuck from inside — he answered in Shulchan Aruch siman 62 that the wart is proof it was punctured and it is crust that rose from a wound, except one may permit because the wart was covered with chelev on the yeter; and although black stain was found in the chelev where it adhered to the wart — nevertheless there was so much thick chelev outside and they saw no black stain outside — therefore it is not korat dam from outside and did not puncture from side to side, end of his words. And so Peri Chadash — even if peg or needle is found stuck in the wall of the kivah, if a bit of skin remains that did not puncture from side to side — one may permit for everyone. And see Tzitz Eliezer siman 49 who leans to be stringent.
