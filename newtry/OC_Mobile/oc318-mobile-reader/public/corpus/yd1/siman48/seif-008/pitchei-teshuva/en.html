Korat dam. Be'er Heitev s.k. 3 in name of Tzitz Eliezer the same for adhesion; and so in responsum Sha'ar Efrayim siman 62 and responsum Chut haShani siman 69 and other acharonim; and see in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 64 who wrote adhesion literally in the eye we require; but if slightly swollen outside and somewhat thick and no change in flesh to defect — no treifah, for this comes from pain of needle stuck in live flesh; do not add to treifot — see there. [And see responsum Chatam Sofer siman 42 who wrote what was produced: if recognizable punctured through illness and korat dam outside — do we also say if no wound whence korat dam; this is simpler than "expression in the vessel" — thus: needle thus puncture in general; whatever korat dam opposite it teaches blow ruled there too; slight proof from above siman 40 seif 6; and so from Taz siman 37 end s.k. 3, etc.; but it appears specifically for needle we must turn initially and examine if korat dam outside — for regarding needle it is common that it healed unlike through illness recognizable did not control more. Nay: if korat dam found opposite — treifah; but examination not required for this; and this law is true — end of his words — see there:]
