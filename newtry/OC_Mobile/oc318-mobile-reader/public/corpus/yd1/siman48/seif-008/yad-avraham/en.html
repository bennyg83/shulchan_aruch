(Seif 8) And if the needle passed from side to side while still stuck, etc., they examine; if no korat dam found after slaughter forced through kosher if not salted; but if salted — treifah even without korat dam — lest salt removed it. In my humble opinion learned from Avodah Zarah 32b; Rashi, Rif, Ra'ash, Ran, Semak regarding Lebuvin hides — round tear opposite heart forbidden in benefit; we say in Gemara and those poskim: if korat dam on it — then certainly from life; if no korat dam — we say after slaughter torn; and there: and when not salted; but salted — say salt removed prohibition even without korat dam. Clear proof for Shulchan Aruch. Not like Hagahot Asheri chapter 1 doubtful in this law if rinsed or salted whether we say shechutah hutrah — see there. Not so — we do not say thus unless there is reason to suspend it happened after slaughter as hagahah siman 50. Do not challenge Lebuvin hides: he said if korat dam — if no korat dam we say after slaughter; Gemara Orach Chaim 51 all general punctures where nothing to draw blood like needle thorn in puncture — no korat dam required; possible from life yet no korat dam — wiped by moisture of flesh with nothing to draw; one may say blood wiped by flesh moisture as Rashi there — see there; unlike hides blood can cling in hair; since no korat dam and not salted — certainly after slaughter; and who omitted this in Hilchot Avodah Zarah chapter 7 — appears because of difficulty above and holds that sugya pushed aside Chullin sugya we rule where nothing to draw no korat dam required; settles Lechem Mishneh's wonder there.
