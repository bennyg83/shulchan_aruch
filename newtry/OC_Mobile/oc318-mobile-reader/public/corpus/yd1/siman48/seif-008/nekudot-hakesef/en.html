(Taz s.k. 12) His words are astonishing — for Beit Yosef explains Rabbi turned it over by chance; if so Abaye spoke well that he troubled for nothing since this young scholar did not come to teach we must turn it over; also what he wrote regarding Rambam's view is not clear — for Rambam never mentioned law of one side; also what he wrote regarding Ra'ash is certainly not so as Shach s.k. 32 wrote; also what he brought from Rashba's responsum siman 360 — one may say it deals with puncture from two sides; but from one side one may say he holds no examination is required even ideally; and so Maharshal Orach Chaim siman 91 and responsum Ben Lev kelal 9 responsum 3 and Beit Chadash and other acharonim like Rabbi Rama; and so we rule.
(There s.k. 13) And in my humble opinion, etc. — Maharoch and Bach already preceded him in this as Shach s.k. 26 wrote.
