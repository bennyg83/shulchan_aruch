And they examine. Taz wrote: this examination is obligatory even when punctured from one side — not like Rama who is lenient in great loss.
On it. Shach wrote: but if korat dam is found around the puncture — kosher until korat dam is found opposite the mouth of the needle.
Kosher. Shach wrote: it implies even in the meses; and it is not comparable to "a thorn stuck in esophagus" above siman 33 where we are concerned lest it healed — one may say because the kereis is at rest forever; if it had passed from side to side as then it should be found now; and crust could not rise there. But regarding esophagus that always moves, as the Gemara says — there is concern lest initially both skins were punctured from side to side and through movement the needle returned inside and crust rose from outside; and so according to the Mechaber; but we already explained above in the hagahah that we practice to declare treifah in the meses if not in great loss.
Punctured. Shach wrote: even for those who forbid in the meses — they concede in beit hakosot it is kosher since its wall is thick — we are not concerned lest it healed.
Outside. Even though regarding liver siman 41 it is treifah — here since there are foods and liquids we say foods and liquids pressed it and the dekin were not punctured; and there is no distinction whether the needle is small or large.
Lost. Shach wrote: it appears to interpret when beit hakosot was lost and it was known it did not puncture outward; but where lost and not known whether it punctured outward — treifah even in great loss; and so Taz. However other poskim permit even in this (and all the more if certainly known it did not puncture outward, only whether there is korat dam outside was unknown and it was lost — see in Shach. And Peri Chadash ruled to be lenient specifically if known it did not puncture outward; but where lost and not known whether punctured outward — for everyone treifah — see there; requires further study):
Around. (And this is the distinction between one side and two sides: from one side specifically on it; and from two sides even around. And another distinction: from one side if korat dam is found inside — kosher until korat dam is found outside; and from two sides even if found inside — treifah; and so acharonim — from this it is proven it was from life.) Shach wrote: even if somewhat far from the puncture like the width of a straw — likewise treifah; and if punctured from two sides and korat dam is found whether inside or outside — treifah; and so Taz.
They rinsed. Shach wrote: in this all agree — since it punctured from two sides it is in presumption of prohibition; examination is to permit it; and as long as we do not clearly know there is no korat dam — it remains in presumption of prohibition.
Lost. Shach wrote: Rama comes to inform us when lost and they took the needle — its law is equal in all respects to salting and rinsing; and the practical difference is where punctured from one side and before examination it was lost or they took the needle from there — kosher in great loss like in salt and rinse:
Our examination. Shach wrote in name of Bach and Maharshal: in great loss one may permit even in our time. And if it punctured only from one side — one may permit in the meses even according to Rama if there is no korat dam — on this we rely on our examination even in our time.
Examination. Taz and Shach wrote: but if korat dam is found outside — even if not stuck at all — treifah; and see siman 33 seif 9 (for if there is no wound there — whence korat dam; and the same for a wound).
Great loss. Shach wrote: but where he examined and there is no korat dam outside — kosher even not in great loss; and specifically in beit hakosot. But in the meses, even if no korat dam outside but only inside — treifah not in great loss. And do not challenge: regarding lung, according to everyone we permit only with examination — one must say lung is different because its membranes are very thin. Taz disagrees with Rama and rules one may not rely on the lenient views even in great loss — see there (Taz in name of Shaarei Dura wrote: there is no permit from one side except when the needle lies in the cavity of beit hakosot as food goes — not stuck along the length of the wall; if lying in the wall of beit hakosot along its length — even from one side and from inside forbidden, since its cavity is wide; if through the esophagus it should have been found on the secretion — rather it certainly punctured and came. And see Shach, Taz, and Peri Chadash. Needle found in thickness of kereis and did not emerge outside — some students wanted to declare treifah; Radbaz siman 113 agreed with the butchers who permitted it.
