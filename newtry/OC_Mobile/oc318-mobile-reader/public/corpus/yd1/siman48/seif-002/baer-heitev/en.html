The chelev. Taz challenged: we find proper sealing — if punctured where the spleen is attached. And one must say: since the membrane on the spleen is forbidden as full chelev as written siman 64 seif 10 — therefore there is no sealing for the kereis, for tame chelev does not seal (a gentile who found a peg in the kereis — if he is believed to him, forbidden; even though the gentile says it was not stuck but only partly in the wall of the kereis inside — the gentile may not know to aim; but if he says he found inside the folds of the meses called heib — one does not forbid. Taz end of siman 50. A chicken whose stomach was swollen — after slaughter when opened, clear liquid squirted from it — kosher; responsum of Bach siman 157; but in an animal such a case Peri Chadash siman 50 s.k. 1 wrote one may not permit except in great loss. And in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 61 he heard from doctors there is danger from eating this, lest one also come to an illness called Wassersucht. A puncture found in the wall opposite the kereis — we are not concerned lest the kereis was punctured — kosher; see there siman 130. And what the Mechaber wrote "for the chelev on it is forbidden" — this is for those who forbid chelev adhering to the kereis under the diaphragm; but the people of Rhineland who practice leniency to eat it as written siman 64 in the hagahah — all the more if the kereis was punctured this chelev seals it (Peri Chadash).
