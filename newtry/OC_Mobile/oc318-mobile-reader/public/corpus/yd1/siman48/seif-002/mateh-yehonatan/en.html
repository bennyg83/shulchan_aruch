(Siman 48 seif 2) Kereis punctured, etc. See Taz s.k. 2; they challenge why spleen should not seal like chelev on yeter — we rule like Babylonians who forbid eating chelev on yeter; nevertheless for sealing it seals as Rabbenu Nissim said "they eat" and we seal; not astonishing; if so same in kereis since Rhineland eat chelev with it — for us it should seal. They answer: granted chelev on yeter is permitted by law — halacha like R' Akiva requires attachment of membrane and peeling; chelev on yeter is not attachment but resident; Babylonians forbid from stringency — one may say "they eat," etc.; unlike chelev on kereis which we forbid — we rule like Rabbenu Efrayim; for us it is full chelev and karet liable — no language to say "they eat." Only on what Rama wrote below siman 67 seif 9: but we do not forbid vessels of Rhineland — therefore reason as Maharil in collection: that generation agreed not to forbid vessels because for essential law it appeared to rule like Rabbenu Yoel; only on chelev itself they did not wish to permit from chelev stringency; if so still difficult why not seal like chelev on yeter; and one must say: here regarding kereis since from stringency they accepted Rabbenu Efrayim's view — for us it is d'oraisa chelev with karet — therefore no "they eat"; unlike chelev on yeter which is only general stringency — we properly say "they eat," etc.
