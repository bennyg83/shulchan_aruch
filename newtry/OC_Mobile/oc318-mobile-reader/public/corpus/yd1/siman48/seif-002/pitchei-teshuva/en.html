Kereis punctured treifah. [See in Sefer Binat Adam gate of prohibition and permission siman 26 who wrote a sharp question: they stabbed between the ribs near the belly and secretion emerged and it appeared the kereis was punctured; and afterward it lived twelve months and also became pregnant and gave birth — what is its law. He answered: ostensibly since secretion emerged, certainly the kereis was punctured — certainly treifah; and it would not help even if it lives many days as siman 57. But after examination it appears this is only safek treifah — for Tur brought view of some that inner kereis is specifically the lower part facing the ground, but above in the rib area if the kereis was punctured it is not treifah except in its majority or a tefach — and this is Ittur's view; but majority of poskim hold kereis punctured anywhere is treifah in any amount; and the annotator in Tur wrote sense and experience testify to the first view — therefore safek dispute; and since it lived twelve months or gave birth — it is kosher, end of his words — see there. In my humble opinion this permit requires further study — since majority of poskim forbid anywhere in any amount as he himself wrote — for Peri Megadim wrote siman 81 s.k. 165 safek dispute where many hold thus — even if alive twelve months one may not permit — see there. And what he brought from Tur annotator, etc. — intent seems even though majority forbid, annotator's words from sense and experience give at least balanced safek. Wonder he did not mention Bach who cried out on that annotation and wrote printers may Heaven save them insert annotation not halacha — see there. However in my humble opinion when the rabbi wished to prove kereis punctured between ribs is only safek treifah — he need not mention Ittur or annotator — seems majority who forbid anywhere in any amount do not concede obviously they hold halacha like Rabbah in chapter Elu Treifot 56b — rather from safek because halacha is doubtful like whom — see Rashi there who wrote since halacha not decided we act stringently and declare treifah entire kereis punctured in any amount; also Ra'ash there siman 33 wrote some hold halacha like Rabbah bar Rav Huna and some like Rabbach; Rashi ruled stringently like R' Yosei bar Chanina and so custom — end of his words. Presumably Tur and other poskim likewise rule stringently since safek d'oraisa — if so only safek treifah and living twelve months or birth helps per assumption safek dispute helps twelve months to permit. However on that main assumption Shach also siman 57 s.k. 48 implies so from Maharshal chapter Elu Treifot siman 80 and his words are correct; and Peri Chadash agreed s.k. 50; and so Peri Megadim and Levushei Serad and responsum Zichron Yosef Yoreh De'ah part 2 siman 2 and several acharonim — nevertheless matters require further study as Sefer Emek Halachah siman 8 wondered: if so what helps living twelve months for safek din — judged from rule whatever safek treifah such as this is kosher; and if we say no proof from living since minority live — how permit this animal; they will say two animals with defect one forbidden one permitted, etc. He concludes there for practical law not like Maharshal who holds twelve months helps safek in the act itself; not so safek in the din, etc. — end of his words; and so I found in Sefer Amudei Or newly printed siman 49 wondered on the sage in the goat incident likewise per that reasoning — if so that goat permitted us kereis puncture between ribs — why distinguish that goat or other animals since from that event the din was decided, etc. — see there. (In truth this wonder is not on the sage but on Maharshal Shach and acharonim who followed him.) Also from Bach's challenge on Tur annotator it is clear he does not hold Maharshal's view — requires further study; and see Turei Beit Shmuel siman 30 s.k. 8 and Sefer Beit Efrayim siman 35 s.k. 100:] (b) For the chelev.
