Kereis, etc., and you have nothing to seal it — Taz wrote s.k. 2: that the spleen does not seal because of the spleen membrane that interrupts; and I did not understand — the chelev covering the kereis interrupts between the kereis and spleen and it is tame chelev — how does the spleen seal.
That it seals it — certainly for those who hold like Rabbenu Yoel; if so it is obvious fat adhering to the kereis that they eat certainly seals; and Rambam holds like Rabbenu Efrayim that it is tame chelev; and that Rama did not emend for us that fat seals — since the people of Rhineland eat it; if so they eat forbidden food, etc. — see what I wrote on this siman 64; I did not understand him; nor that Rashi wrote tame chelev does not seal because it is not attached but a resident; and Rashba in Tur haArukh 37 wrote tame chelev does not seal because there is a membrane below; and he explains "they eat" chelev of yeter — they eat forbidden chelev of yeter because it is not attached, meaning the membrane does not interrupt between it and the flesh, etc. — and from this reason the law is that it should seal even for Babylonians; and if so this fat adhering that R' Eliezer and R' Yoel fought about is the fat adhering to the kereis itself and the membrane is above it — if so what difference whether forbidden or permitted — in the end it is not attached and seals. And one may say Rambam deduced from "they eat," etc. — and for us sealing does not seal as explained. And with this Maharshal's wonder in Orach Chaim siman 54 on R' Yehudah Zahav who doubted in that siman whether it seals — and he is included in "they eat," etc. — is settled; and he wondered why according to R' Eliezer it is karet prohibition — it is only a stringency of the law like R' Akiva who requires attachment; but all agree it is not attached and certainly seals. And why wonder on "they eat" — since all Israel do not eat the kivah it would not seal even though not attached; whereas since the people of Eretz Yisrael eat it, the reason "they do not divide" does not apply; and since it is not attached — it seals. Therefore the kereis — since Rambam holds like R' Eliezer and it is forbidden as chelev and karet punishment — therefore "they eat" does not apply; unlike chelev on the yeter which is only a general stringency — we properly say "they eat," etc. And with this Peleti settles Sha'ar Efrayim and Peri Chadash who debated wisely on a needle at the head of the dekin that chelev seals the puncture whether to permit because many poskim hold "head of intestine" refers only to the end of the intestines near the stomach and it is in the category of "they eat" — see there; and why did they trouble with this — it is explained in Mordechai that "head of intestine" is not attached at all — if so obviously it seals; and what difference tame or pure — and according to my words it is fine that even if we say even though not attached it does not seal — examine well.
