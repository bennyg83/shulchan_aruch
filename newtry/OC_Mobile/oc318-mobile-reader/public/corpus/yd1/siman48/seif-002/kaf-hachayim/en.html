(8) [Seif 2] Kereis punctured, etc. — meaning all the kereis; also saniya dibi; also where there is no word — all mentioned are in category kereis; treifah punctured in any amount. Turei Beit Shmuel end ot 4; Machatzit HaShekel ot 3; Zivchei Tzedek ot 4.
(9) Indeed there is a distinction: if puncture in kereis below toward ground — certainly treifah; but if puncture hidden under ribs on this side and that — safek treifah. Chochmat Adam kelal 20 ot 2; Machatzit HaShekel there; Zivchei Tzedek ot 5.
(10) A goat stabbed between ribs near belly; secretion emerged; appeared kereis punctured; afterward lived twelve months; also impregnated and gave birth — Binat Adam ot 26; on Chochmat Adam kelal 30 to permit; wrote reason since safek dispute — twelve months and birth help as Shach siman 57 s.k. 48 — see there. However Machatzit HaShekel there rejected his words — erred; Shach Rashba and Bach did not write except in dispute of Ittur; also already disputed; we do not rely; but here dispute of amoraim in Gemara — how can one permit; see there. And so Chok L'Yisrael Yoreh De'ah part 1 siman 25 daf 34 — between dispute in Gemara and unresolved question in Gemara — twelve months pregnancy and birth do not help; but in dispute of poskim after sealing Talmud — living twelve months and birth help — see there.
