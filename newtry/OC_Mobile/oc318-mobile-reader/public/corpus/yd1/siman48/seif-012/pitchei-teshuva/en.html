Great loss. Be'er Heitev; and what he wrote in name of Taz there is no permit from one side, etc. — see Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 64 who disagrees with Taz in what he declares treifah if stuck along length even if recognizable from inside — this is not so; Hagahot Shaarei Dura deals only when stuck in it and not recognizable inside or outside whence it came; and in this too one should not be stringent except not in great loss as Shulchan Aruch ruled in kurkavan — see there; and see Peri Chadash who also wrote per Taz it is not essential for practical law; however if stuck from outside along wall of beit hakosot — one should declare treifah — see there. Meaning: in that half-thickness of the wall from the outside side it was stuck inside the flesh along the wall and all swallowed in flesh thickness and no puncture recognizable inside or outside — treifah. Turei Beit Shmuel's view is to be more stringent: even if not stuck but lying along — treifah. Peri Megadim ruled lying — lenient; stuck — even from inside side — treifah. And see in responsum Tolodot Yitzchak siman 4 who elaborated bringing proof for Peri Chadash's view and leaned to permit in great loss; however if also found beit hakosot adhered to liver — then certainly in such a case one may not be lenient even in great loss — see there (see what I wrote above siman 41 s.k. 12 in his name); and see Noda BiYehudah Tinyana Yoreh De'ah end siman 2 who wrote on matter of needle stuck in beit hakosot and length of needle along beit hakosot — if truly stuck along length one may not be lenient except in great loss one may settle — see there:
