(7) Chelev of yeter — we rule tame does not seal; there is dispute between Rashi and Rambam; per Maran Beit Yosef siman 64: for Rashi chelev outside kivah is dakashta; inside circle yeter — place of yeter meaning chelev of arch; for Rambam it is a drawn string like extra; Shach and Peri Chadash followed below siman 64 — implied from their words there is dispute; Turei Beit Shmuel concerned; wrote when such comes before rabbi must be careful sealing chelev be yeter for everyone; Maharsham ot 1 wrote therefore fallen this warning in Bira — who knows today which permitted string; and it appears in great loss all chelev from inside one may be lenient. Machatzit HaShekel ot 10; Etz Chaim there ot 3; but Taz siman 63 ot 6 wrote per our minority and knowledge we may not rely at all; worthy to forbid puncture in kivah whether from inside or outside; sealing chelev does not help at all.
