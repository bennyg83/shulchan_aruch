And some, etc. From what is stated 50b: the rabbis taught regarding a needle, etc.; and since it left the meses' mate — we learn that there too from one side it is treifah; and as stated in the second chapter of Shabbat for the conclusion regarding a needle, etc.; and so they said in chapter 3 of Sukkah; and that which the mishnah says punctured outward — Rashi explained: excluding punctured one into the other, as in the mishnah below, as above; and Tosafot there and there and there were precise about "outward"; and further that there is no treifah when not mafpolash — see there.
(Collection) And some forbid, etc. — his words are not correct; Rashi does not disagree except regarding a needle, as stated second chapter of Shabbat and chapter 3 of Sukkah — we are concerned as in esophagus siman 33 seif 9; but without a needle it is not worse than esophagus, as written siman 64. (And even though in Tosafot and Ra'ash it appears the same without a needle — nevertheless one may say their words as above.) And it appears Rashi's words are primary as the plain meaning of the Gemara; and how forced is Rabbenu Tam's explanation in that Gemara; and likewise in the beraisa of Chullin 50b only beit hakosot is mentioned; and so there 49a; and so Ra'ash there 64 to rule like Rav Alfas that we rule like him; and it is possible, etc.; and even though one may say as Rabbenu Tam because it moves, etc.; and so in Hagahot Maimoniyot there s.v. aval, etc. — two reasons to distinguish — see there. Nevertheless the primary reason is the first, as above. And afterward I found in Tosafot Shabbat 36a s.v. l'machat in name of R' Yitzchak likewise — see there what he wrote "and that which it asks," etc. One may also say as Ra'ash in the first chapter of Chullin — such as punctured through illness — and that is the truth (end of collection).
