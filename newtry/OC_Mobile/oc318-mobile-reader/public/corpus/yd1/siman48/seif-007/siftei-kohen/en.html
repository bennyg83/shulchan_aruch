Tosafot wrote, and Bach brings them: even if both skins were punctured in the place where it lies on the meses and is stuck there in fat — it does not seal, for it is tame chelev, as below siman 64 seif 8; and so is implied in Tur Choshen Mishpat beginning kelal 95:
And some forbid, etc. In Darkei Moshe and Tur Choshen Mishpat there he concludes; however in Tur haArukh siman 165 he ruled like Rashi; and so Orach Chaim haArukh — we practice like Rashi. Rashi's reason: since it has no thick skin but thin skin — we are concerned lest it punctured from wall to wall and healed; unlike in the thickness of beit hakosot. And it is implied he forbids only when the puncture came through a needle — from concern lest it punctured and healed; and so Tur Choshen Mishpat and Orach Chaim haArukh kelal 91 — some forbid from this reason; and so he wrote in his signs in Tur Choshen Mishpat there — and so is the practice unless in great loss or time of pressure, since its wall is thin — we are concerned lest it punctured and healed, end of his words. But when it is evident it came through illness — there is no such concern — kosher; and so in poskim in name of Rashi; and so in Sefer Lechem siman 162b — it appears also Rashi forbids only through a needle, for in all places Rashi wrote this it refers only to a needle, end of his words. And so from Rashi's words in the mishnah "the meses and beit hakosot punctured outward" — Derishah Orach Chaim explains: punctured outward means the puncture is seen from outside, such as when this or that was punctured not in the place of their attachment, etc., end of his words. And so Hagahot Shaarei Dura siman 94 in name of R' Yehudah Zahav — Rashi deals here with a puncture not through a needle, meaning why is it not treifah until the puncture is seen from outside; but through a needle it is treifah in the meses from one side or in the thin place of beit hakosot for the reason mentioned. Maharshal in his Orach Chaim there did not understand R' Yehudah Zahav's words and strained greatly — the matter is simple as I wrote, and not as Bach wrote that Rashi forbids also through illness and ruled so even not in great loss — which is not clear at all; and even if Rashi's view were so, all poskim wrote not like him, and all acharonim forbade only through a needle; and so Taz and Lechem there:
In the meses, etc. Derishah wrote: even though Rashi mentions beit hakosot — around its lip there is thickness, etc. — one should not interpret that in the rest of beit hakosot the law is like the meses; rather it appears to me all of beit hakosot is doubled and thick, etc., end of his words. Bach in his last pamphlet challenged his words as not appearing at all — and there is no doubt they omitted Rabbenu Yerucham's words netiv 10 ot 10, who wrote explicitly that for Rashi in beit hakosot in the thin place it is treifah; and so Orach Chaim haArukh kelal 91 din 11; and so from Ran Orach Chaim — and this is his language: beit hakosot — part of it is thick and has two skins, and part thin with only one skin; and he says a needle found in the thick place — if from one side, meaning only one skin was punctured — kosher, end of his words. (And what Derishah wrote "it appears all of beit hakosot is doubled and thick," etc. — the known fact and poskim refute this.) And so from Semak siman 141, brought in Hagahot Shaarei Dura — and this is his language: it implies that if the needle is found in the kereis not in the thick place of beit hakosot — even from one side treifah, etc. And what Derishah wrote "let it distinguish and teach in the matter itself," etc. — it is no difficulty at all — see there. And see s.k. 22:
Even without mafpolash puncture, etc. The reason is already explained — we are concerned lest it punctured outward and the needle returned backward and healed; and it is implied according to this view that even examination to see if it punctured outward does not help — lest it healed so much it is not recognizable; and so Ran, and so Rashba in Tur haArukh long daf 38b, and so Tosafot Orach Chaim and chapter "With what may we light" and other places that reconcile: that which we learned punctured outward — we are not concerned lest it healed with slight changes; and if so it should have taught that we examined the other side — examine well. And it is implied in the meses treifah even if there is no korat dam outside; and so Taz — and it is simple that if there is korat dam outside, even beit hakosot is treifah as nearby seif 8.
