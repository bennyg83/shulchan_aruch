(There seif 7 in hagahah) And some forbid, etc. Difference between meses and beit hakosot per Tosafot for Rashi's view: beit hakosot thick — no concern lest healed; unlike meses thin — more concern. Ra'ash in sugya thorn stuck esophagus: Ra'avan ruled like Ulla; brought proof from needle in thickness beit hakosot; possible Rav Alfas distinguishes — thickness beit hakosot thick not concerned lest healed; plausible like Ra'avan — no distinction for any thickness; Tur in hints wrote Ra'ash holds like Rashi in meses puncture one side treifah; if so must distinguish thick and thin; Ra'ash certainly does not distinguish thick thin — difference meses beit hakosot: beit hakosot hard wall; meses soft wall — therefore one side also treifah. Still difficult: what proof from beit hakosot to esophagus; Taz view and Rashi implies even puncture one side through illness treifah in meses; unlike esophagus illness kosher; otherwise punctured one without other how find. From beginning Orach Chaim slight proof for Shach's view meses not forbidden one side only through thorn not illness — Gemara answers meses and beit hakosot counted as one; if so difficult for Rashi meses not equal beit hakosot in puncture — meses even one side treifah; beit hakosot must be two sides; reason there treifah one side concern lest healed; we say certainly punctured two sides and healed; if so equal beit hakosot.
(In Shach s.k. 17) And implied in meses treifah even without korat dam outside, etc. — but from inside must be korat dam as Shach s.k. 30; ostensibly not compelled; one may say: granted beit hakosot no concern lest healed — specifically korat dam required; but in meses concern lest healed — we say there was korat dam and korat dam also returned and healed; further distinguish: granted beit hakosot korat dam required outside — for although certainly stuck from life, nevertheless without korat dam outside we say stuck from inside but not punctured outward until after death; but this we certainly do not say it stuck after death inside. If so in meses since certainly stuck from life inside — treifah even without korat dam at all — for even punctured one side treifah; but Ran and Rashba prove like Shach who challenged esophagus why thorn stuck treifah even without korat dam — why not say after slaughter punctured like beit hakosot; answered since esophagus narrow place foods liquids pass frequently — we say foods liquids passed and impossible here concern lest healed — we also say korat dam afterward healed and recovered — no difference; for in esophagus too concern lest healed; and certainly descended from life; if so korat dam neither raises nor lowers; therefore no distinction as above; also in meses korat dam required from inside.
