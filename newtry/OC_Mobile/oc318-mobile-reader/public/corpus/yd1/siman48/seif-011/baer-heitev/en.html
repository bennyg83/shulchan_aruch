To be lenient. The reason is in Shach in name of Rashba: the way of the esophagus is to swallow, and through there food enters with the eaters — only the puncture was sealed; therefore one of the punctures was sealed — we suspend on what is its way; and further we established it on presumption of kashrut regarding these punctures.
Like punctured. Maharshal wrote: regarding kurkavan all agree that if not examined we establish on presumption of kashrut. Shach disagrees — for although we are not concerned lest it healed, we are concerned lest it punctured.
Kosher. Shach wrote: but if there is korat dam there — even if you say it came through the face — it punctured outward; for if there is no wound — whence korat dam. He wrote in Darkei Moshe: examination from outside is required according to those requiring examination from one side; and Shach wrote it is implied where rinsed or salted its law is like punctured from one side. Taz wrote: if examination is impossible, such as lost after the needle was found — one forbids from safek since examination is required and we do not say shechutah hutrah; and so Maharil in an incident where a child cried while eating that there is a needle in the kurkavan and forbade everything even mixtures. Shach wrote: the reason those forbid even without korat dam outside is because the needle is not recognizable from inside as from outside — we fear the needle came from outside and punctured other organs. It is implied from Orach Chaim haArukh: whatever recognition is from inside and not outside — such as korat dam inside and not outside — kosher.
