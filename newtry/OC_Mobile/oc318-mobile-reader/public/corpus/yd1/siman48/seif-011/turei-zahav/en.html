We suspend to be lenient. Since in any case there was a puncture on the outside side or inside and the puncture was sealed — we suspend to say it came from inside, since its way is to come from the esophagus; and further we established it on presumption of kashrut that it had — so Beit Yosef in name of Rashba.
And some are stringent and forbid even in such a case, etc. The reason: we are concerned lest the needle came from outside and punctured other organs; and if it appears to come from inside to outside and punctured entirely and there is no korat dam at all — Darkei Moshe wrote treifah; for we do not require korat dam except in the meses and beit hakosot whose wall is thin — one may say that after slaughter a small thing such as this was punctured; but kurkavan flesh is thick — we do not say lest it punctured in such a short time, and we do not suspend to say perhaps it punctured from life all the flesh except a minority and that minority was punctured after slaughter — we do not say that. And since in any case it must be that most was punctured from life, we suspend the minority after the majority likewise. So is implied from Tur's language: specifically regarding punctured inside and not outside is kurkavan law like meses and beit hakosot; but here where it punctured entirely regarding korat dam they are not equal. Therefore the practice every day is to declare treifah when punctured from both sides even if there is no korat dam outside. I wondered on Mahari Chaim z"l who wrote at end of siman 49 that also regarding korat dam when punctured from both sides kurkavan is like meses and beit hakosot — and he wrote it is simple; he did not see these words of Darkei Moshe.
If not in great loss. That which he ruled leniently is because it is more plausible that it came from inside through the esophagus, for such is the way of swallowing there, as Rashba brought at the beginning of this seif; and even though he wrote there another reason — that we established it on presumption of kashrut — it appears that reason alone is not sufficient to permit by itself; for otherwise there is difficulty from what Rashba himself wrote that Rama copied in seif 8: wherever examination is required and it was lost or they took the needle — forbidden; and we do not say we establish on its presumption — but certainly he did not write so here except combined with the first reason. And there is a difference in every case where there is doubt whether the kurkavan was also punctured from the outside and examination is impossible — such as lost after a needle was found in it, or cut in a way examination is impossible — one forbids from safek for the reason mentioned, since examination is required and impossible; and even though in responsum of R' Moshe Mintz siman 66 it appears his view leans to leniency in such a case from "shechutah hutrah" — it does not appear to me to rely on this; and so my brother Mahari z"l in a responsum regarding a child who felt in his mouth while eating that there is a needle in the kurkavan and cried — and they saw at the place of the tooth's bite in the kurkavan the needle emerged partly from there — and he forbade everything, even mixtures, because the chicken piece was worthy of honor even if it is weak; and the law is with him that even though the treifah is safek here, nevertheless what that the honorable piece is derabbanan does not help when there is safek derabbanan, as I wrote in name of Orach Chaim siman 100; and so is clear in Hagahot Shaarei Dura and Orach Chaim haArukh in name of Maharam regarding a needle in kurkavan that Rama mentioned here — some are stringent and forbid all mixtures; and even though Rama ruled not like those stringent — nevertheless in this safek where there is no reason for permit more than certain prohibition, we rule thus: also mixtures are forbidden whenever the prohibition is an honorable piece.
