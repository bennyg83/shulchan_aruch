Needle found, etc.; "for the needle," etc. Like a needle found in lung — and the large bronchus of the lung; and we say "bronchus" was mentioned; and all the more in such a case we say the way of the esophagus with the eaters entered — it is closer than those; and since the way of the esophagus is to swallow — one of the punctures was sealed — we suspend on what is its way.
And if there is no, etc. Even if punctured from the inside, if there is korat dam outside — treifah, as in the Gemara and as I wrote seif 8; and see siman 49 seif 3; and it means ideally examination is required according to the above Rashba. Darkei Moshe.
