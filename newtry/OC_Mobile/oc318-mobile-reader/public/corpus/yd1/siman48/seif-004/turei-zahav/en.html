When they have no food. Tur wrote anonymously if they entered with difficulty; and Beit Yosef wondered why he did not mention the distinction between whether food is on it or not. Rashba wrote the reason: since we do not know how much the coating will be — therefore they were stringent that in every manner it is treifah even if they enter with difficulty without food.
That if this tear is stretched, etc. If bored in a circle it works well — for any circle whose width is a tefach has a circumference of three tefachim; but if bored in length it does not mean the surrounding thread will be a tefach and no more — rather this is what it says: if it is in length and when stretched from lip to lip until it is like a circle it will be a tefach — all that has a sela in length as above.
