Like a sela. Shach explained: meaning deficient in mafpolash in length; and when you make a circle from the length it will be like a sela. And in seif 3 regarding removal — treifah in its length deals even where the strip is not wide, in a matter that if you make a circle there will not be a third of a tefach — nevertheless since its length is a tefach it is treifah. And Taz wrote: the Mechaber taught and then explained — first he wrote generally that there is treifah in tear and removal; afterward he explains first the law of the measure of the tear until the end of the seif; and in seif 4 he explains the law of removal — meaning bored in a circle or in length.
Loosely. Tur wrote anonymously if they entered with difficulty and did not distinguish between whether food is on it or not; and Rashba wrote the reason: since we do not know how much the coating will be — therefore they were stringent that in every manner it is treifah even if they enter with difficulty without food. And Taz wrote: one should wonder — if bored in a circle it works well that whatever has width of a tefach has circumference of three tefachim; but if bored in length it does not mean the surrounding thread will be a tefach — rather: if it is in length and stretches from lip to lip until it is like a circle, it will be a tefach — all that has a sela in length.
