(12) Chelev adhering to kereis — even though people of Rhineland eat it like Rabbenu Yoel — for us who do not eat it does not seal. Beit Levi ot 4; Machatzit HaShekel ot 6; Chochmat Adam kelal 30 ot 2; Zivchei Tzedek ot 8; and implied but for Rhineland who eat it seals; and so Beit Levi there; see below siman 64 seif 9 and our words there in s.d.
