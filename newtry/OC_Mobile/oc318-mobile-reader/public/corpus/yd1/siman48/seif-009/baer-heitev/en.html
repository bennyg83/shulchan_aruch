Rust. Maharshal disagrees and holds regarding puncture from one side rust neither raises nor lowers; and he also wrote in name of Maharam Mintz: rust in the middle of the needle — kosher; and Bach declares treifah in every case (and so Peri Chadash and Kenesset HaGedolah; and if the needle was lost before examination whether rust was on it — kosher when punctured from one side; see Kenesset HaGedolah daf 141).
