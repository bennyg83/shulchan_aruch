**(gloss in Shach end seif 5) And sell it to you since you caused me damage, etc.** And the damager cannot say “I hold” like one who says I need not pay since he has no damage in it—and he is obligated to sell all the wine, even partner's share; and cannot say I will sell my half and friend will sell his half himself—for per friend's words it is yayin nesech and forbidden to sell. And it appears if he has damage in what he takes partner's share he can say “I hold”; and second reason one may say because of this he benefited, etc., and we compel him on measure of Sodom; think.
