**(seif 3) Even if thought, etc.** There end of chapter—halachah like R. Yosi.
**And if Israel, etc.** So in Terumat HaDeshen—the dispute of R. Yosi and R. Elazar there 39a: in such a case it deals—for otherwise he is mumar to idol worship anyway. And Tosafot's view is not so—see there 14a s.v. hashochet, etc.
**(Likkut) And forbidden in benefit.** Their dispute is also regarding benefit, as in Avodah Zarah 34b and Chullin there 2—baraita like R. Yosi, etc., sacrifices of the dead, as in seif 1 (end).
