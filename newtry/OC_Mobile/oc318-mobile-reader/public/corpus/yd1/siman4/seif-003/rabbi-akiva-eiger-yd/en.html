From these words of Rashba on this proof why R. Elazar said permitted—proven even if he became mumar in that slaughter nevertheless called slaughter of mumar; and what he rejected—that it deals with saying at completion he worshiped—since completion and act of becoming mumar came together—not called slaughter of mumar; all the more where mumar from first time before slaughter; wonder what Rashba answers on Tosafot's difficulty in what we answer “owner's portion” in Israelite mumar—for per Rashba who holds Yishmael and gentile holding knife invalid.
