**(siman 4 seif 4) An Israelite who slaughtered his friend's animal for idol worship—he did not forbid it.** For no Israelite forbids what is not his—he does not intend except to pain him—Rambam's language. Proven explicitly in Tosafot chapter Merubah (71) s.v. bishlema regarding idol worship, etc.: when he gave agent permission to slaughter as he wishes, even for idol worship—then agent forbids, for cannot say “to pain him” since owners want this. And proven in Shas chapter Kol HaTzlamim (46) and chapter R. Yishmael (53b) regarding Israel who set up a brick—and Rav brought in gloss below siman 139—see there. Reason: since they revealed their da'at they are not stringent—not applicable to say “person does not forbid what is not his.” Not because of agency—for we do not hold agency for sin per one opinion; and since agency not required, only revelation of da'at and granting permission helps even for transgression. And such case Noda BiYehudah wrote part Even HaEzer siman 77. And so Birkei Yosef Orach Chayyim siman 5; and he settled that which Mahaneh Efrayim challenged from sugya Avodah Zarah—there is no agency for gentile. And he rejected that which Mahaneh Efrayim wrote proof from that sugya gentile has agency for gentile even for transgression—for there it is not because of agency but revelation that it pleases them—for there agency not required, only revelation of da'at that pleases him suffices—cannot say person forbids what is not his. And in Chiddushei R. Shmuel Bava Kamma (91) opposite from Mahaneh Efrayim; and in any case one may say per Maharshal in responsum Tur Even HaEtz siman 35 we do not say agency for transgression except where impossible without transgression, but where possible without transgression they did not send him to do specifically transgression—he is agent even though he did in transgression; and in Shushanat HaAmakim kelal 21 likewise Tosafot Bava Kamma's intent. However Tosafot not so much—only per view person forbids what is not his. And Perishah challenged from Avodah Zarah there who said his agency performed—for all forbids; and in Shushanat HaAmakim explain nevertheless first owners did not make him agent.
**And see there** in Tosafot one can prove from their words: if he gave agent to slaughter stam and agent slaughtered for idol worship b'shogeg—he did not forbid it—for otherwise should have taught thus—see there. And so responsa Radbaz vol. 3 siman 483—see there—proved from chapter Nedarim (36) must say “for your benefit I sent you not for detriment” regarding pigul. However per Rashba's comment there it is not proof—he challenged for we hold person forbids what is not his in great act at least. And answer: that applies to other issurim; but regarding pigul of offering—when he changes his agency to detriment he is found not to be agent, therefore not like slaughtering offering but like piercing—not pigul and not kosher either. Rather difficulty: why is it pigul and not kosher.
