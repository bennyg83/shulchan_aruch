He did not forbid it—see Peleti I extended that conclusion of poskim: even in small act can forbid friend's property; and that one can forbid in small act is d'rabbanan, and from Torah Israel cannot forbid at all, even in great act.
Also here Choshen Mishpat siman 385 regarding libating wine—Mechaber rules anonymously if partnership does not say “to pain him”; here Mechaber brings two views—see Peleti main point as Semag wrote wine is different—for he could separate friend's wine alone and libate it if intending to pain him, unlike animal impossible to divide without shechitah; plausible; see Peleti Shach challenged from Gemara and I defended with good reason.
To pain him—but nevertheless his shechitah is nothing—as if he did not slaughter—so Ran and Taz agreed and Maharshal; Shach challenged on this; see Peleti I settled all difficulties and brought proof for Ran's words—one should be stringent in his words. If slaughtered b'shogeg for idol worship—see Peleti I wrote nevertheless permitted—for he can claim I do not believe you—even b'shogeg only when intentional was and truly to pain him, and now he returns; even if he has miggo—not believed against general rule he acts to pain him; possible even if he comes on his own—and truly he slaughtered so and intent was intentional—we do not attend to him regarding himself certainly forbidden for he made it a doubt.
