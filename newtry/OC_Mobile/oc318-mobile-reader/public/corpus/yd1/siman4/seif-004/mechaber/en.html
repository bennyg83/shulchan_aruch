An Israelite who slaughtered his friend's animal for idol worship—he did not forbid it; certainly he did not intend except to pain him. If he has partnership in it—some say he forbids his friend's share too. Some say even here he does not intend except to pain his partner—not to forbid. {Rama: If they warned him and he transgressed the warning—he forbids like other mumarim (Tur). (And see below siman 145.)}
