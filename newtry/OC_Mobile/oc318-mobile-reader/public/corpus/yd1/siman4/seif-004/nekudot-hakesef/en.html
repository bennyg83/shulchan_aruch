**(siman 4 Taz seif 4) And you might wonder on Beit Yosef and Rama, etc.** It escaped him that Beit Yosef brings him below end siman 145, and Rama brings in Darkei Moshe and challenged from slaughtering on Shabbat, as Shach wrote seif 5. Also what he wrote on Bach that he did not examine this—to the contrary, Bach did not examine what Bach wrote earlier on Ran's words: meaning it is like plain slaughter of gentile for idol worship although their intent was not idol worship but like died by itself—end quote; meaning certainly cannot say like died by itself per this Mechaber—for behold slaughtered before you; rather like gentile slaughtered—for his shechitah is not shechitah and like died by itself; if so what difficulty from two holding knife—for in any case like gentile and Israel holding knife—it is invalid; all clear.
**(there seif 5) And difficulty, etc.** And one may say, etc.—there is no difficulty and answer here, only the straightforward is as Shach wrote seif 6.
**(gloss in Shach end seif 5) And sell it to you since you caused me damage, etc.** And the damager cannot say “I hold” like one who says I need not pay since he has no damage in it—and he is obligated to sell all the wine, even partner's share; and cannot say I will sell my half and friend will sell his half himself—for per friend's words it is yayin nesech and forbidden to sell. And it appears if he has damage in what he takes partner's share he can say “I hold”; and second reason one may say because of this he benefited, etc., and we compel him on measure of Sodom; think.
