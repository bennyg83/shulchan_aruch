**To pain him.** Taz wrote in name of Ran: regarding eating certainly forbidden; and although he does not forbid what is not his—here he did not permit it for her and it is as if it did not slaughter but died by itself; and Taz wrote this is main, not like Bach who disagrees.
**To forbid.** Shach challenged: here Tur wrote two views, and in Choshen Mishpat siman 385 regarding libating friend's wine if partnership must pay—and one must say although for issur there is dispute, regarding money injured party can say perhaps we hold like one who forbids in benefit—what do you say we hold like one who renders kosher; take wine I do not want to enter doubt.
**Mumarim.** And forbidden even in benefit; Shach wrote in this entire siman when we say slaughtered for idol worship—it deals even with any act whatsoever, as below siman 145 seif 8.
