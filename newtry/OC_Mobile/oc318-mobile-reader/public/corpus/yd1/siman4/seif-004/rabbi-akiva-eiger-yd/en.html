**(Taz seif 4) And you might wonder on Beit Yosef.** Apparently wonder on his wonder on Ran—not so, only regarding even if he does not forbid what is not his—nevertheless forbidden in eating, for he did not forbid but did not permit; but per Shulchan Aruch ruling person forbids what is not his except we say “to pain him”—Ran's reasoning does not apply—for he said he did not slaughter at all for idol worship and thereby also permitted in eating; see nearby Shach seif 5.
