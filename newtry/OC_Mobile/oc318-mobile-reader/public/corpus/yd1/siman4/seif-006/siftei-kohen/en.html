**Or for one minister, etc.** Sun and moon also have ministers, as we say in midrash twelve angels lead the sun—Tosafot.
**This is sacrifices of the dead, etc.** Implies it refers even if he did not intend to worship them—for it ascends from him that nevertheless it is sacrifices of the dead like Rambam, not like R. Yitzchak {two views in Tur see there}; and so Maharshal there.
