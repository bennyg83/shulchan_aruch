Also what he wrote on Bach that he did not examine this—to the contrary, Bach did not examine what Bach wrote earlier on Ran's words: meaning it is like plain slaughter of gentile for idol worship although their intent was not idol worship but like died by itself—end quote; meaning certainly cannot say like died by itself per this Mechaber—for behold slaughtered before you; rather like gentile slaughtered—for his shechitah is not shechitah and like died by itself; if so what difficulty from two holding knife—for in any case like gentile and Israel holding knife—it is invalid; all clear.
