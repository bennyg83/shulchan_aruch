**a) [seif 1] One who slaughters for idol worship, etc.** Meaning shechitah itself for idol worship; but slaughtering to offer meat for idol worship is end to throw blood, etc.—Taz seif 1. Shulchan Malachim letter 1. Yad Eliyahu letter 1. And see below letter 7.
**b) There. One who slaughters for idol worship, etc.** Likewise if he said he slaughters for partnership—for Heaven and another matter—also slaughter for idol worship is so called. Shulchan Malachim letter 1. And needless to say if Israel slaughtered for partnership—it is sacrifices of the dead since commanded regarding partnership; but even gentile who slaughtered for partnership—even though not warned on it as Rama wrote Orach Chayyim siman 256—for us it is offering and included in sacrifices of the dead. Turei Zahav letter 4.
**c) There. One who slaughters for idol worship, etc.** Even if he thought for idol worship and did not express with lips—forbidden. Shulchan Malachim there. Kereti letter 2. Shulchan Aruch letter 1. Zivchei Tzedek letter 1. And all the more if there is proof for his thought, e.g. crouching before idol worship to offer it and gentile or mumar came and slaughtered stam—as if he explained he slaughtered for idol worship. Shulchan Malachim there.
**d) And even** if he slaughtered in it only siman 1 for idol worship—forbidden. Levush. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz letter 2. Zivchei Shlomo letter 1. Zivchei Tzedek letter 3.
**e) And not** specifically siman 1—even slaughtering any amount of siman for idol worship forbidden thereby; and needless to say any amount in windpipe that makes nevelah; but even slaughtering any amount in kaneh that does not make nevelah—also forbidden, as Shach wrote in Nekudot HaKesef end siman 2—see there. Plucking feathers for idol worship—no concern; nevertheless even plucking feathers this law—tzarich iyun. Arukh HaShulchan letter 1. Gedolei HaKodesh letter 1. Zivchei Tzedek letter 4. And see below letter 9.
**f) And know** with gentile only three avodot are written—shechitah, throwing, and burning; therefore not disqualified in shechitah unless he thought at shechitah to throw blood or burn fat for idol worship; but if he thought for other avodot—did not disqualify. Peri Chadash letter 1. And so Shulchan Malachim letter 2—not disqualified except in three avodot mentioned; but if he thought at shechitah to receive blood or carry blood toward idol worship and not sacrifice before it—animal not forbidden. And mistake in our understanding in that man who knew this is not avodah liable for idol worship—but in any case we are concerned perhaps this thought proved also his shechitah was for idol worship—end quote.
**g) There. To throw blood, etc.** Likewise burning from its meat for idol worship—Taz seif 1. Shulchan Malachim letter 2. Peri Toar letter 1. Shulchan Malachim letter 1. And this is burning olive-size from its meat for idol worship—Peri Toar there. Zivchei Tzedek letter 8.
