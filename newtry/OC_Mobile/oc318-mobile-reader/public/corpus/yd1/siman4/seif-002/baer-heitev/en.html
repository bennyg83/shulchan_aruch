**Dead.** Reason for doubt in Taz: doubt whether we say “his end proved his beginning” or not; and he challenged Tur—for in Choshen Mishpat siman 245 seif 11 Tur ruled we do not say “his end proved his beginning”—see there. And in Maharshal he forbids here in eating and permits in benefit; and where he slaughtered initially explicitly for mitzvah of shechitah—even if afterward threw for idol worship he does not disqualify—Shach; but Taz holds even in this invalid.
