**If he slaughtered stam, etc.** See Bach who ruled like Rabbenu Yerucham; and Taz's view appears there is no distinction between thought to throw blood—for even if he thought afterward and did not throw, this is doubtful sacrifices of the dead, as Beit Yosef wrote; and likewise appears Rambam's view chapter 2 law 16 and all in it; and Rashba in Terumat HaDeshen; and so Maharshal chapter HaShochet siman 20.
**This is doubtful, etc.** And Maharshal there ruled like Rabbenu Yerucham that forbidden in eating and permitted in benefit; and so Derishah and Bach; and appears from Taz's words that where he slaughtered initially explicitly for mitzvah of shechitah—even if he threw or burned after shechitah for idol worship he does not disqualify; and so poskim.
