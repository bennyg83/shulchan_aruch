**b) There. One who slaughters for idol worship, etc.** Likewise if he said he slaughters for partnership—for Heaven and another matter—also slaughter for idol worship is so called. Shulchan Malachim letter 1. And needless to say if Israel slaughtered for partnership—it is sacrifices of the dead since commanded regarding partnership; but even gentile who slaughtered for partnership—even though not warned on it as Rama wrote Orach Chayyim siman 256—for us it is offering and included in sacrifices of the dead. Turei Zahav letter 4.
