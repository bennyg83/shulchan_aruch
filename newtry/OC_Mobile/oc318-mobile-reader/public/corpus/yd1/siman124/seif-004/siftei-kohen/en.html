They forbid the wine for drinking. I do not know whence this, for since they have not yet immersed they are still idolaters and their touch forbids even in benefit — we establish he is not a convert until he circumcises and immerses; and there is an opinion for a convert who circumcised and did not immerse as Tosafot and the Rosh wrote that slaves are different since their heart is not for Heaven but from the time of their master they become Jewish; and likewise Ran explicitly that their touch forbids in benefit; and likewise it appears explicitly from Rashba's words in Terumat HaDeshen and the short version — see there; and likewise in Tosafot perek R' Yishmael (Avodah Zarah daf 57 side 1) in "Gedolim, there is not," etc.; and so is proven also in Tosafot there beginning side 2 — see there; and so the plain meaning of the baraita's language appears: they make stam yeinam — meaning even in benefit; and likewise it appears in the Tosefta Rashba brought: behold these are idolaters — stam yeinam: regarding adults forbidden; regarding minors permitted; and Rambam and all other poskim wrote simply "they forbid wine" — it implies even in benefit; and so I found proof from the Gemara (perek R' Yishmael there) that establishes the baraita that teaches: one who buys slaves from idolaters who circumcised and did not immerse, and likewise children of maidservants who circumcised and did not immerse — adults make stam yeinam, minors do not make stam yeinam — like Rav (whom we establish like him) who said an idolatrous minor does not make stam yeinam except for drinking; and minors who do not make even for drinking refers to children of maidservants; but adult slaves — perforce "adults make stam yeinam" in benefit, for it also refers to slaves, and slaves — perforce in benefit; for otherwise why mention adults — even minors too. However for us that idolaters in our time are not idolaters — all touch of idolaters is permitted in benefit in a place of loss, as above beginning siman 123.
Since they circumcised, etc. The author follows his reason, for he wrote Beit Yosef that Rambam's view is like Ra'ah and the Tur; and for practical halachah since Rambam and Ramah agree with one view, so we rule — end of his words; but in my books I wrote that from Rambam's words nothing is decided; and I brought there Kesef Mishnah who rejected Rashba's words and I proved all his words are not aligned, for Rashba's words are primary that even if they did not circumcise their touch is permitted even for drinking; and further that R' Chananel, Beit HaGefen, R' Yitzchak, Sefer HaTerumah, and other poskim ruled completely like Shmuel that an idolatrous minor's touch is permitted even for drinking; if so the baraita that teaches circumcised and did not immerse was not because of minors; and if so, although regarding an idolatrous minor Rav disagrees and we establish like him, nevertheless regarding children of maidservants we do not find he disagrees; and on the contrary we say in the Gemara he concedes regarding children of maidservants they are better than an idolatrous minor — and this is clear. However an idolatrous child who is not a maidservant's child — even if circumcised he makes stam yeinam for drinking as long as he did not immerse, as Rashba wrote in Terumat HaDeshen — we establish he is not a convert until he circumcises and immerses; and the author also concedes in this as appears from his words in several places there; and likewise in Tosafot perek R' Yishmael daf 57 side 1 end of "Gedolim, there is not," etc.
