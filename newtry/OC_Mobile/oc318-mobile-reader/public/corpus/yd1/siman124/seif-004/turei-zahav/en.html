Since they circumcised, etc. He follows his reason, for he wrote in Beit Yosef that Rambam also holds like Ra'ah that we require specifically that they circumcised, unlike Rashba who holds even if they did not circumcise; and I am very astonished whence Beit Yosef infers this from Rambam's words, for behold in the Gemara it also states circumcised and Rashba explained the circumcised stated is to include adults — even if circumcised they forbid; and why should we not say likewise in Rambam; and further it is difficult why he did not rule like Rashba, since we explicitly say so in Tosefta as brought in Terumat HaDeshen; and on the Tur it is difficult that he did not see Sefer Terumat HaDeshen and did not know this Tosefta — certainly one has no power to disagree with Tosefta.
