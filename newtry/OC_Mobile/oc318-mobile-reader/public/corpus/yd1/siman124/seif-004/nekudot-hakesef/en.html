(Hagahah there seif kaf 15) And many disagree, etc. But according to what appears to me in my humble opinion to be lenient, etc., he brings proof from Rashba in our text in Beit Yosef — nothing is mentioned of lifting; rather he wrote "but if he carries or shakes it" and did not mention lifting; and one may say regarding lifting Rama's law is correct, and even for Taz's text in Terumat HaDeshen one may say the phrase "and shakes it" refers to carrying and not to lifting; but regarding lifting and shaking one may say Rashba agrees with Rama's law. And carrying on his shoulder is not called lifting, and behold it is like placed on the ground; and know that Rambam's law is from what we say in the Gemara regarding Zika — whether full or lacking it is permitted, etc.; see Beit Yosef, and from this Rambam learned that even without lifting; and if what he carries on his shoulder is called lifting, then what non-Jews carry would be called lifting, and whence does Rambam learn that stirring without lifting is forbidden — perhaps there it is different because non-Jews carry and it is lifting; rather certainly as we said — examine carefully.
