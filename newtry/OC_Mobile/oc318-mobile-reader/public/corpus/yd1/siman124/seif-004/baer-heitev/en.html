Who were circumcised. Taz and Shach disagree with the Mechaber. They rule that minor children of maidservants do not forbid wine even for drinking, though they were not circumcised. An ordinary non-Jewish infant, however, even if circumcised, renders wine forbidden for drinking until immersion, since conversion is incomplete without both circumcision and immersion.
