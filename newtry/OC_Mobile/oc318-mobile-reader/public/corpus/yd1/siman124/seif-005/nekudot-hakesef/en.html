(There) And all this regarding benefit; but for drinking forbidden through touch alone, as I mentioned in siman 123 seif 6 in the name of Raavad. His words are mistaken; and if he meant what he wrote above in this siman letter heh — that for Raavad touch is forbidden for drinking even without stirring — there it deals with touch in the wine itself; and it must be that such is the continuation of his language. And that which is permitted here through touch alone without stirring — it appears likewise regarding touch in the wine itself, that all who did not stir are permitted, namely specifically in benefit, etc.
