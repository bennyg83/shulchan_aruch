(Siman 124, Taz, seif katan 2) The Maharshal wrote that the Karaites have the status of a ger toshav, and it is permitted to drink Jewish wine with one of them. This is the correct reading. In my works I discussed the law of the Karaites at length, but here I will briefly state the matter. The Maharshal and the Bach permit it, and their words imply that Karaites are superior even to a ger toshav. The same is written in Orach Chaim in the name of a responsum of the Rambam, which is cited in Bedek HaBayit. However, Responsa Mabit, part II, siman 38, records a responsum of Rabbi Shimshon stating that Karaites who desecrate the festivals are regarded as desecrating Shabbos and denying the entire Torah, and that their wine is yayin nesech like the wine of idolaters. This seemingly implies that wine is also forbidden through their touch, as with idolaters. The same appears from the responsum of the Rashba cited by Beit Yosef in siman 119, and this is also Beit Yosef's view in Bedek HaBayit, siman 128: their touch forbids the wine, and they are not believed even if they swear that they did not touch it; see there. Responsa Rabbi Betzalel, siman 3, likewise discusses the laws of the Karaites at great length and writes that they are treated entirely like full idolaters. His words imply that wine is also forbidden through their touch. He explains that the Rambam's responsum addressed only the Karaites of his own time, unlike the Karaites of the present era, whose conduct had deteriorated; see there. Nevertheless, it appears that their wine should be forbidden only for drinking, while benefit from it is permitted according to all opinions. Since they do not worship idolatry, they are no worse than a ger toshav.
