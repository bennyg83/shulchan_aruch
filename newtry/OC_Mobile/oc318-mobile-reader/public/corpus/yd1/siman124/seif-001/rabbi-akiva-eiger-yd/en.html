(Siman 124 Shach seif yud) But in my humble opinion this law is clear. In my humble opinion the author stated "there are those who say" — for it appears the view of Ramban brought in Nekudat HaKesef (perek HaChalitzah daf 18 side 2) holds that what we say regarding a pregnant woman who converted, her son needs no immersion — means nevertheless circumcision is required; and if so before he is circumcised he forbids through his touch. But according to Ra'ah there and this is Tosafot's approach in Yevamot — he needs no circumcision in actual law, being like an Israelite — see there. And in siman 268 the author stated simply that the son needs no immersion and did not mention at all whether circumcision is required; and the later authorities there also did not mention this at all.
