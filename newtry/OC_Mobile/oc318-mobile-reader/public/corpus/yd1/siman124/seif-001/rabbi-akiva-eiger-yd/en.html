But in my humble opinion, this ruling is clear. In my humble opinion, the Mechaber used the expression "there are those who say" because the view of the Ramban, cited by the Nimukei Yosef in the chapter HaCholetz, page 18b, appears to be that when the Gemara states that the child of a pregnant woman who converted does not require immersion, it nevertheless means that he requires circumcision. Accordingly, before he is circumcised, his contact renders wine forbidden. According to the Raah there, however, which is also the view of Tosafot in Yevamot, he does not require circumcision, and his status is truly that of a Jew. See there. Yet in siman 268 the Mechaber rules without qualification that her child does not require immersion, and he makes no mention at all of whether circumcision is required. The later authorities there likewise make no mention of this.
