There was no Jew here. Be'er Heitev and responsum Pri Megadim part 3 siman 69: a Jew hired non-Jews to take out large barrels from the cellar and set them on wagons; they are skilled craftsmen at rolling the barrels to set them on the wagon; it happened during rolling that the barrel overturned and the stopper fell from the mouth of the barrel and wine began to spurt; the non-Jew put his hand to stop it, and other non-Jews came and helped — see there: permitted, for it is no worse than the touch of a non-Jew through something else, which is not forbidden for us; and all the more so here, where there was no Jew to save it.
