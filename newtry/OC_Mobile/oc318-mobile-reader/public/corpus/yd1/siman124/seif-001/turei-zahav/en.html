Who does not mention idolatry, etc. The Tur wrote that one is considered an adult when he understands the nature of idolatry and its attendants, meaning anyone who remembers and mentions verbally idolatry and its attendants. This is based on the Tosefta cited by the Terumas HaDeshen, except that there the word “verbally” is not mentioned. The Prishah explained that “remembers” refers to thought. It appears to me, however, that “verbally” refers to both expressions. This accords with the wording found in the first chapter of Kesubos: “Whoever sullies his mouth and utters a shameful matter from his mouth, even if a decree was issued against him,” etc. Its meaning appears to be that it excludes one who at times speaks obscenely without intending evil. Therefore, it uses a doubled expression, that he utters a shameful matter from his mouth intentionally and willingly. Here too, since it is possible that he remembers the name of idolatry without an evil intention, it therefore says that he “mentions” it, to indicate that he mentions it intentionally and willingly. The expression “mentions” means that he directs his attention to mention it. Therefore, the Shulchan Aruch states only “and mentions.”
