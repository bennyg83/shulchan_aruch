One who has withdrawn from Judaism, and an apostate. Chatam Sofer discusses a Jew accused by conflicting witnesses of treading wine after nightfall on Friday. His conduct was certainly wicked and he should not be trusted regarding prohibitions, but this alone does not necessarily render the wine stam yeinam. All the more so where he did not touch the wine directly but only caused it to flow by his force: wine expressed before he became an apostate was permitted, and later wine became mixed into it incrementally.
