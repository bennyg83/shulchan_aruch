(There seif 27 in the hagahah) A non-Jew who touched our wine of Israel to provoke, etc. There was an incident when I was in the regions of Mehrin and a non-Jew touched my wine and I did not want to drink; and he said to me in these words: drink, for the wine is permitted; and ask your father and he will tell you it is permitted, since I did it to provoke you so you would not drink; and one who acts to provoke is permitted — so were his words. And I asked my teacher the Gaon may his light shine where is his law; and he said the wine is forbidden, for the reason it is permitted is because he acts to provoke; but since he knows the law, therefore he did not act to provoke and returns to the original prohibition as though he did not act to provoke. And even though to provoke is permitted to drink even not in the non-Jew's presence, because he did not intend to pour a libation but only acted to provoke — if so here too he did not intend to pour a libation but only to show he knows the law; however one may explain and say: since he knows that to provoke is permitted, he never acted to provoke but did this to pour a libation. And what the non-Jew said to ask the law of to-provoke-permitted — to cause Israel to stumble to drink yayin nesech — and it is not similar to one who does not know the non-Jew acts to ruin his wine so he will not drink, therefore permitted; but here since he knows that to-provoke is permitted, therefore he acts to pour a libation and it is forbidden — examine carefully.
