A wooden barrel that drips or trickles: a Jew should take oakum in his hand and place it opposite the hole so that it will prevent the wine somewhat from going out, and a non-Jewish craftsman should come and tighten it with a knife in the hole until it is sealed well. And if the wine is trickling a lot, one must be especially careful that the non-Jew not touch the wine (with his hand; but with a knife, b'dieved it is permitted) (Tur and the poskim). And one who wants to do the best way should tighten the barrel with a rope and stop the trickling of the wine, and afterwards repair it. {Rama: A non-Jew who touched the Jew’s wine to anger him, in order to forbid it for him (Tosafot, perek R. Yishmael, daf 58b, and Mordechai and Agudah there) — even if they do not know this, but it is by evident circumstances (Terumat HaDeshen siman 26, and Binyamin Ze’ev siman 348; see there at length) — it is permitted even for drinking. And one should drink it in front of the non-Jew so that they will not accustom themselves to this (Tosafot and Mordechai, perek R. Yishmael, in the name of Or Zarua).}
