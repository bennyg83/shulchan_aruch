An Israelite should take the oakum. As Beit Yosef — from Tosafot's and Hagahot Or Zarua's words it appears for the beauty of the matter and not for blocking; and it is astonishing why, for if the non-Jew gives the oakum it is like resting his hand (above seif 23) since he prevents wine from coming out; and perhaps one may say since this is not a complete hole but a crack we are not so stringent — end; and see further in the Tur what he wrote "and some distinguish," etc., and in Beit Yosef and Bach.
