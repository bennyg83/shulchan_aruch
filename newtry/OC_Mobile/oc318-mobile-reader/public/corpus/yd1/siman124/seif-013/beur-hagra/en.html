To take, etc. According to the first explanation in Tosafot there "they took it," etc.; and Rambam omitted all this since he follows his approach that touch by hand even when engaged in his craft is forbidden ; except because he did not stir; and according to his approach he holds we require actual stirring; but Tosafot and other decisors hold whoever inserted his hand it is stirring; and as written in Terumat HaDeshen that all touch through something else is without stirring unless he took it in his hand.
