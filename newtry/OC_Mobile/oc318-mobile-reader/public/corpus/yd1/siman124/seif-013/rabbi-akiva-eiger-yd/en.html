(Letter dalet) For Rambam. It is astonishing, for according to Rambam who forbids through force in benefit — truly also regarding our non-Jews it is forbidden; but nevertheless for us who establish force is permitted in benefit; and we establish like Rambam we descend one level — if so regarding our non-Jews permitted for drinking; and similar to what Bach wrote regarding measuring, brought in Taz (seif kaf 31); and likewise explicitly in Shach (siman 125 seif kaf 2); and more in my humble opinion that even for Rambam one may be lenient, for in his touch in another thing with intention that is forbidden in benefit by law — nevertheless regarding our non-Jew it is permitted, being his touch through something else without intention of touch; if so here too in force it is force unintentionally that is permitted.
