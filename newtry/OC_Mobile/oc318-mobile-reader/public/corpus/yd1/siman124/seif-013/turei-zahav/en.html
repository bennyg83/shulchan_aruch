And he will not shake it. Meaning: if he had stirred it would be forbidden after he took what fell there and wants to remove it; but without stirring it is not forbidden in benefit from mere touch alone; and that which he did not forbid the wine through inserting his hand — Tosafot and the Rosh explained: then he was occupied saving what fell to him and it is like measuring in seif 19; but in removal he is no longer occupied; and Levush compared to measuring that we mentioned, that there occupied even in removal since he must remove in a manner that he intends the measurement — unlike here where at the time of this removal he was without any occupation — end; therefore then guarding is needed lest he stir; and Tosafot wrote in "and what he wrote regarding stirring," etc., that from Tosafot's and the Rosh's words it appears if he intended to touch wine it would be forbidden even though he did not stir; and in my humble opinion it does not appear so, for it is obvious they also hold without stirring there is no prohibition of benefit except they needed to permit insertion because he was occupied; here where he wants to take from there what fell — impossible without stirring, for at the time of insertion he searches here and there — it fell; perforce it would be forbidden if not for occupation; and likewise proven from Terumat HaDeshen daf 134 who also gave occupation as reason; however he holds there that all touch through something else is without stirring unless he took it in his hand; and likewise proven from the Tur's words who ruled we require stirring and did not bring his father's view to disagree on this except as I explained; nevertheless for practical halachah we must be concerned for Rashba's words and forbid through touch alone, for impossible without stirring; and all the more for what Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Ran that touch alone also forbids.
