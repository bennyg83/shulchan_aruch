To take from there. — See above seif kaf 20.
Permitted in benefit. Since at the time he inserted his hand his intent was only to take the item that fell there, he did not intend to pour libation. Afterward, when he touches it, since he does not move the wine, he is not shaking it; therefore it is permitted in benefit. For us, since we hold below in s.k. 71 that wherever by strict law it is permitted in benefit, for us it is permitted even for drinking in a case of loss, the same applies here; but the matter should not be publicized, as below.
