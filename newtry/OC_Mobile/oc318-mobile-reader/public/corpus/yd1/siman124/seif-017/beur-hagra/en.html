There are those who, etc. From what is written 60a side 1: and if he inclined, etc.; and the law of Zika there that R' Eliezer says there is no way of libation in that ; some explain tied knot; and in Yerushalmi on the mishnah: he rolls Zika from above to below ; there is no matter of pouring from vessel to vessel; it also implies in such a case; and likewise in some texts it is found and it is closing his mouth; and Rambam wrote he holds the mouth of the leather bottle in his hand; and in what is stated in siman 125 seif 9: the leather bottle lacks ; permitted because he does not want noise ; as Rashi wrote there; but in Terumat HaDeshen and other decisors they disagree with him; and Tosafot there in "Zika," etc., there is no way, etc.; and likewise in Terumat HaDeshen and other decisors; and they brought proof from what is written 72b side 2 regarding a non-Jew pouring, etc., inside, etc., impossible without noise; and according to their approach they hold noise is connection; and as stated; and this is what he wrote "and many disagree," etc.
