And many disagree saying, etc. It appears from his words one should be lenient only without lifting; but when lifting it is called stirring even though he only touches the vessel ; namely according to what Darkhei Moshe wrote wanting to establish that Rivevan whom the Tur brings siman 125 does not hold like Rambam here and not as Beit Yosef wrote here that he holds like Rambam ; rather Rivevan deals specifically with lifting; but according to what appears to me in my humble opinion one should be lenient even when lifting, for Rashba wrote in Terumat HaDeshen daf 137 that Rambam's words are not clear except we do not find stirring forbids except touching wine itself whether in his body or through something else; but in lifting or carrying and shaking ; not, for such is not the way of those who pour libations at all ; end; and likewise Raavad, and this is primary ; end; and he brings there clear proof for this from a baraita; and such are the Tur's words end of siman 125: a non-Jew carries wine of Israel even if lacking and shakes through his walking ; permitted even if open ; end; and likewise Mahari Chai like Rashba; and so is primary in my opinion; and all this regarding benefit; but for drinking forbidden through touch alone as I mentioned in siman 123 seif 6 in the name of Raavad; and see what I wrote in siman 125 seif 8 that there are three disputes in the matter; and the language Rama wrote here "and many disagree" are Raavad and Rashba.
