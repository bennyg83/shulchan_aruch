There is one who says that if a non-Jew held an open utensil of wine and agitated it, even though he did not lift it and did not touch the wine, it becomes forbidden. {Rama: And many disagree and say that if he agitated the body of the utensil without lifting, it is not called agitation (Rashba, Raavad, Rosh, and Rabbeinu Yerucham in the name of most poskim); therefore, one should not be stringent in a place of loss.}
