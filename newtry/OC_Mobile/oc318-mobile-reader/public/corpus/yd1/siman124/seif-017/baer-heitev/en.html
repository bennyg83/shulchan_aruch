Permitted. Shach writes that nowadays it is permitted even for drinking in a case of loss, though one should not publicize this. The same applies below where a non-Jew extended his hand to take something from among the barrels. As a general rule in these laws, whatever is permitted by strict law for benefit is permitted nowadays even for drinking in a case of loss; whatever is forbidden by strict law for benefit is permitted nowadays for benefit in a case of loss. If our yayin nesech became mixed, it is measured against sixty and may then be drunk. Likewise, a doubt whether the non-Jew touched intentionally is treated leniently because the prohibition is rabbinic.
