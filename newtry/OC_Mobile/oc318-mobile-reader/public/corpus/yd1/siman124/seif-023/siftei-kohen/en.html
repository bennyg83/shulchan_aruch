Permissible to drink—so it is in most books; and it is simple that this is a scribal error and it should read "for benefit" (behanah); but for drinking it is forbidden, as with "he measured by hand." So too in Turei Zahav and in some books, and so is written in the responsum of R. Eliyahu of Candia printed in the responsa of R. Eliyahu Mizrachi, siman 54. And it is written in the responsum of R. Eliyahu Mizrachi siman 56: for us, who hold that gentiles nowadays are not idol-worshippers and their contact without intent is permitted for drinking—here too it is permitted for drinking. And it is implied explicitly from his words there that likewise where an Israelite is present we descend one level, and for us the whole thing is permitted; however this is only where there is financial loss, as stated below in se'if kuf alef.
But if he inserted, etc. — There is a slight difficulty: he should also have distinguished here between whether there is a Jew present to save or not; and you cannot say it is different here since he touches the wine itself, for when measuring by hand too he touches the wine; and it appears that here even if there is no Jew to save, the matter is not proven that he intended to save, for he could have saved by placing his hand over the hole without inserting his hand inside; and accordingly where it is impossible to save unless he inserts his finger inside and there is no Jew to save — permitted in benefit; and likewise Bach: even if he inserted his finger or a long spigot, where there was no Jew there — all permitted in benefit, for he is occupied in his work — end; and this deals with the case I explained.
All of it is forbidden in benefit. — For us: when he inserted his finger, permitted in benefit in a place of loss; when he inserted a long spigot, permitted even for drinking in a place of loss, as below seif 24; but if the situation is such that he cannot save unless he inserts his finger and there is no Jew who can save — permitted even for drinking in a place of loss for us; and likewise Bach — to rule leniently in practice.
And through it all the wine in the barrel comes out. — But if the pump-reed does not reach the bottom of the barrel, so that not all the wine in the barrel would come out — only up to where the pump-reed reaches — then only up to where the pump-reed reaches is forbidden in benefit, and from there onward permitted in benefit, like the spigot nearby; so it appears from the poskim; and for us all permitted in benefit in a place of loss even if it reaches the bottom of the barrel.
