And it implies from his words there explicitly that likewise when there is a Jew we descend one level and for us all is permitted; however this is specifically in a place of loss — Shach. And Taz wrote that he was asked about a non-Jew who repaired a wine barrel to strengthen it, and through striking the barrel a crack opened and wine was spurting through it and he sealed it with his hand; and a woman stood there with a lamp in her hand to illuminate for him to repair, and meanwhile her husband came and rebuked him and he sealed the crack — what is the law of this wine? And he answered at length and his conclusion is to be lenient, for since the non-Jew was in a panic lest he need pay the damage we call him occupied, and permitted for us since we establish nowadays unintentional touch of a non-Jew is permitted as Rama wrote in siman 124 — in my humble opinion. However this matter is novel and was not explicitly mentioned in the poskim, but it is clear and has no doubt in my humble opinion — end of his words.
