(There seif kaf 16) This is very astonishing, etc. All the later authorities preceded him in this, and I too recalled this in siman 125 seif kaf 8:
(there) this is astonishing. And in my humble opinion Rambam follows the Rif's approach, who wrote this law that is difficult for Rambam — why did the Rif not rule like Rav Ashi? Rather certainly the Rif holds he explains the mishnah "and they permitted it" means in benefit, even in his anger; and the Rif holds the reason they permitted it is occupation and not because of throwing; for since he touches wine, what difference whether through throwing or through touch not in anger where there is no occupation — forbidden even in benefit. And that which he challenges Rav Ashi from not in anger — it is to challenge from in anger; there is no difficulty, for Rav Ashi can reject that he explains the mishnah and they permitted it for drinking, therefore he had to challenge from not in anger — so I found in Kesef Mishnah explaining Rambam according to the Rif's approach, that the halachah is not like Rav Ashi, and he did not explain all his words according to the plain meaning of the baraita; and it is possible he explains as I wrote — examine carefully.
