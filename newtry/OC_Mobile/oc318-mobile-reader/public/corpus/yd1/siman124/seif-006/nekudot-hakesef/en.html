Rather certainly the Rif holds he explains the mishnah "and they permitted it" means in benefit, even in his anger; and the Rif holds the reason they permitted it is occupation and not because of throwing; for since he touches wine, what difference whether through throwing or through touch not in anger where there is no occupation — forbidden even in benefit. And that which he challenges Rav Ashi from not in anger — it is to challenge from in anger; there is no difficulty, for Rav Ashi can reject that he explains the mishnah and they permitted it for drinking, therefore he had to challenge from not in anger — so I found in Kesef Mishnah explaining Rambam according to the Rif's approach, that the halachah is not like Rav Ashi, and he did not explain all his words according to the plain meaning of the baraita; and it is possible he explains as I wrote — examine carefully.
(There) And all this regarding benefit; but for drinking forbidden through touch alone, as I mentioned in siman 123 seif 6 in the name of Raavad. His words are mistaken; and if he meant what he wrote above in this siman letter heh — that for Raavad touch is forbidden for drinking even without stirring — there it deals with touch in the wine itself; and it must be that such is the continuation of his language. And that which is permitted here through touch alone without stirring — it appears likewise regarding touch in the wine itself, that all who did not stir are permitted, namely specifically in benefit, etc.
