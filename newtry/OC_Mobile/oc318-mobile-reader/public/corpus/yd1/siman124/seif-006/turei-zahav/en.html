Who is not an idolater, etc. Such as Ishmaelites; and he learns it from ger toshav ; for what he ruled in seif 2 that he forbids only for drinking; and even though there he wrote he accepted upon himself seven commandments ; here he informs us as a stringency that even so forbidden for drinking; but regarding prohibition of benefit we do not care, only regarding idolaters as already explained ; prohibition of drinking is because of their daughters and prohibition of benefit is because of avodah zarah; therefore whoever is not an idolater there is no prohibition of benefit here; and according to "there are those who are lenient" that Rama brought there ; nevertheless here we descend a level and at minimum permitted in benefit; and in the Tur he wrote in the name of Rashba that one should be lenient regarding Ishmaelites since they do not worship avodah zarah; and Derishah challenged from what Beit Yosef wrote above in this siman in the name of Rashba in this language ; behold whoever accepted not to worship avodah zarah and seven Noahide commandments ; their wine is not yayin nesech to forbid in benefit; and here it deals because he will not worship avodah zarah alone; and he expanded to resolve ; and there is no difficulty at all, for there it means whether according to R' Meir if he accepted not to worship avodah zarah or according to the Sages if he accepted seven commandments ; and we establish like the Sages who require accepting not to worship avodah zarah also seven commandments, and accordingly in this at least permitted; except some Geonim say one should be lenient even with not worshiping avodah zarah alone even for the Sages, from Rav Yehuda who sent to Avodarni on their festival day as Beit Yosef wrote; and there is astonishment in Beit Yosef's words who wrote to resolve Rambam's words to himself ; R' Yehuda deals where the entire nation does not worship avodah zarah and the baraita deals where his nation worships avodah zarah; and it is difficult what the Gemara challenges "but it is taught: what is a ger toshav," etc. ; namely R' Meir's words who requires acceptance before three; and it answers this is for sustaining him ; and what difficulty at all, for Rav Yehuda deals where the entire nation does not worship avodah zarah, and if so the baraita also holds that when one accepts not to worship avodah zarah and the entire nation is so ; R' Meir agrees that suffices; but if the nation worships avodah zarah ; R' Meir and the rabbis disagree; and it is difficult to resolve.
