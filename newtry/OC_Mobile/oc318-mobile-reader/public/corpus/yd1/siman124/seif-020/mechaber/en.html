A non-Jew who fell into a pit of wine, and they brought him up from there dead — it is permitted in benefit, and forbidden for drinking. But if he came up alive, it is forbidden in benefit. {Rama: And the same law applies if he stumbled and rolled within the wine, and also rolled out outside — for it was by coercion even in the end (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba).}
