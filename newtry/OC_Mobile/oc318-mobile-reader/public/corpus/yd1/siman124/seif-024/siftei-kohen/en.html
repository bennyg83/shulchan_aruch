It is stream within a stream. — As Beit Yosef: it deals when there is a reed in the hole of the barrel — then there is room to distinguish between the stream that stands in the channel and the rest of the wine; but if there is no reed in the hole, all is forbidden because of stream — end; and Darkhei Moshe brings it; and see above siman 126 seif 2.
And likewise if he touched, etc., or he did not know, etc. Since even if he intended to touch he forbids only for drinking — since he is not an idolater; if so unintentionally even for drinking permitted immediately — like an Ishmaelite who touched unintentionally, as above (seif kaf 12) — end Darkhei Moshe; and it implies likewise in every place where by Talmudic law it is forbidden only for drinking — for us permitted even for drinking; and likewise Bach — end of his words; and likewise in responsum R' Eliyahu Mizrachi siman 56; and likewise it appears in responsum Rav Chaviv siman 41 that he suspended the reason of descending a level — see there; however it was already explained above beginning siman 123 that not in a place of loss we establish that even in our time they are idolaters; and also in responsum Maharil siman 38 I found he wrote that intentional touch of a non-Jew through something else in our time should not be permitted except in great loss; and even though the rabbi and those who follow his view do not hold thus — perhaps they did not see Maharil's words, for behold they did not bring his words; and it is possible if they had heard they would not be so lenient; and further this leniency was not mentioned by the early or famous later poskim; and also in Sefer Api Ravrevi daf 79 side 2 he brought the rabbi's words and wrote on it: one should not be lenient in this except in great loss; and also the rabbi and Bach concede to this, for behold it appears explicitly from their words beginning siman 123 that not in a place of loss we establish that even in our time they are idolaters; and so I received from my teacher the Gaon may his light shine that one should not be lenient on grounds that non-Jews nowadays are not idolaters except in a place of loss, and he said he likewise saw this from many great decisors; however wherever permitted by law in benefit because he is not an idolater, such as a minor and ger toshav and the like at the beginning of this siman — even for us forbidden for drinking even in a place of loss; for what of it that idolaters in our time are not idolaters — without this, even if he is not an idolater forbidden for drinking; and this is what Rambam and the author wrote above seif 7: wherever they said, etc., because of a side of touch, etc. — it implies they came to exclude other things not permitted in benefit because of a side of touch, namely those I explained; and see below siman 131 seif kaf 15 — examine carefully.
What went outside. That which comes from his force is forbidden — end Turei Zahav; and he was not precise, for now we stand that wine forbidden by law for drinking — for us permitted; and force forbids only for drinking, as above seif 14 and below beginning siman 125; rather the reason is in Mordechai that what went outside presumably he touched it; and Beit Yosef and Darkhei Moshe bring it below siman 129; and see there seif 11.
