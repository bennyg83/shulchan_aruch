(Taz seif kaf 32) And if because of a returning drop of wine. In my humble opinion it is not a drop; rather the question is sixty against all that is in the portion of the spigot — for all that is in the spigot is like touching with his mouth; and likewise explained is Rambam's wording chapter 12 law 15 of Forbidden Foods, who wrote that when he stops, wine that rose in the spigot returns; and likewise explained in Ravyah — see there.
