Forbidden for benefit. Nowadays it is permitted for benefit in a case of loss. Shach. Bach writes that although he inserted his finger to save the wine, we fear that after saving it he may agitate it for libation. Taz rejects this concern and explains that the case is where a Jew was present and able to save the wine, but the non-Jew preceded him. He is then not considered occupied with rescue, and concern is justified.
Knew. If he did not know it was wine, it is permitted even for drinking. Whenever it is uncertain whether he knew, we rule leniently regarding yayin nesech. The same applies where it is uncertain whether he touched intentionally, since the decree is rabbinic. See seif 24 and siman 125:6. Shach.
