"Until the whole wine is not touched. In the pleasure of a column and a friend who sings in pleasure instead of a loss.”
“But if you don’t know. The bzard goes through all the groves, or he did not know that he was allowed even in drinking as follows
If he did not know, etc. And from Hagahot Maimoni chapter 12 in the name of Semak who wrote: even though that wood touched the wine, since there is room to suspend that the non-Jew did not intend — permitted — end; and Beit Yosef and Darkhei Moshe bring it seif 22 below regarding unintentional touch of a non-Jew through something else — it appears explicitly that wherever we do not know whether he knew or not we suspend to be lenient regarding yayin nesech, because it is a rabbinic decree; and in matters of scribes we suspend to be lenient; and likewise it appears from the Rosh's words regarding the barrel whose spigot was removed, who wrote on Raavad's words: since there is room to suspend for permission why do we forbid from doubt — see there; and similarly we suspend to be lenient below siman 125 seif 6; and with this the rabbi's words below seif 24 regarding piercing with a knife are resolved — we say he did not intend except to pierce the barrel and not to touch — it is possible he will pierce and not touch — examine carefully.
And what remains in the barrel is permitted even for drinking. Likewise the Tur; and it is difficult, for the Tur follows his reason who holds below beginning siman 125 that if he poured from a wine vessel what remains inside is permitted, for they did not stringently forbid what is connected to him through stream by the non-Jew's force; and likewise Mordechai wrote in the name of Ravyah and Beit Yosef brings it — the reason here in drawing out the spigot is: even though what went outside is forbidden, nevertheless since there is no complete libation that he did not stir, they did not stringently forbid stream to this extent — end of his words; but according to what the author ruled beginning siman 125 that it is forbidden through stream by the non-Jew's force — if so likewise here; and it appears this is what Rashba wrote in the name of Ramban regarding the spigot that all the wine is forbidden because he holds as below siman 125, unlike Beit Yosef who wrote he forbade only the wine up to the hole because of force; and nevertheless for us all, even what went outside, permitted for drinking in a place of loss, as below seif 24; and regarding if the spigot was removed and the non-Jew sealed it — will be explained below seif 23.
Forbidden for drinking. As Beit Yosef from Ramban's words that Rashba brought — it appears even if afterward he sealed the hole the wine in the barrel up to the hole is forbidden since it was uprooted by the non-Jew's force to spill; but Ran wrote in perek R' Yishmael Ramban's words and from his wording it appears if he sealed the hole simply it is permitted — end.
