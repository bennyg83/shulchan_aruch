(Siman 124 in Shulchan Aruch seif 14) A barrel that was taken, etc.; but if it does not pass the full thickness of the rims, etc. And see Shach seif kaf 34 what he challenges on the author from below beginning siman 135, that the author ruled there that it is forbidden through stream by the non-Jew's force — if so, the same here — see there in Shach. And my teacher may his light shine answered to resolve: the author holds to distinguish between this case and the case below; for there, what is forbidden that comes out is because it is his actual force — for if not his force nothing would come out at all, namely that he lifted the vessel — if so, all drawing of wine was from the non-Jew's force, therefore also what is inside is forbidden through stream; but here the drawing is caused only by his removing the spigot and wine comes out afterward — this is lighter than force below siman 125; therefore all the wine is not forbidden through stream. And this is what the author precisely stated in his wording "it is like his force" — examine carefully.
