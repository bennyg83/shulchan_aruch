That he guarded he did not touch, etc. Our rabbi wrote in the name of Rivash siman 310 that this non-Jew is not called occupied in what the Israelite raises, like we find in the Gemara regarding the etrog that fell, as above seif 13 — lest the non-Jew be more panicked to raise his etrog than to raise for Israel, etc.; and if so it was fit to forbid, being touch through something else — namely he touches wine through the Israelite; except he gave a reason (how) [that there is no] applicable touch of a non-Jew in wine — the Israelite touches and not the non-Jew; and likewise we say regarding touch on a vessel that has wine inside — this is not called touch of a non-Jew, rather he touches the vessel and the vessel touches wine, since the vessel already touched wine, etc. — end; and this is like what I wrote in seif 22 that touch through something else requires the toucher to bring the something else and not that it was already there; and this is what Rama wrote "and it is not called touch in what touches through a person" — not specifically person but meaning: since the person was already there it is not called touch — examine carefully; and why did Rivash not permit on grounds that nowadays touch of a non-Jew through something else is permitted for drinking as Rama wrote this siman seif 24.
