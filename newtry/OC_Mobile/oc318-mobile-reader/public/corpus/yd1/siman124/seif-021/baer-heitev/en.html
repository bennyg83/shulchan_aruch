So too we say: if he touches the vessel containing the wine, this is not called contact by a non-Jew with the wine via an intermediary — rather he is touching the vessel and the vessel is touching the wine. Similarly with a reed one can say: as long as the reed touched the wine before a person touched the reed, this is not contact by a non-Jew via an intermediary (which implies he brings something else into contact with the wine), since in fact he is only touching the reed which is touching the wine. Nevertheless, one should deliberate on this matter — so far the words of the Rivash. The Siftei Kohen wrote: it appears that according to our practice, where we hold in siman 24 that contact by a non-Jew via an intermediary is permitted even for drinking in a case of loss, here too where the reed touched the wine first it is permitted for drinking even not in a case of loss.
