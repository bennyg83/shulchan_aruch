And even if the non-Jew intends to pour a libation in this manner and says explicitly that he is pouring it thereby — his words are nothing; and so too we say if he touches a vessel that the wine is inside: this is not called contact of a non-Jew with wine by way of another object; rather he touches the vessel and the vessel touches the wine. And also regarding a reed one can say so: whenever the reed's contact with the wine preceded a person's contact with the reed, this is not contact of a non-Jew by way of another object — which implies that he brings another object to the wine; but this one touches only the reed that touches the wine; but one must settle carefully regarding this matter, end of the Rivash's words. And the Shach wrote: And it appears that for us, who hold in seif katan 24 that contact of a non-Jew by way of another object is permitted even for drinking in a place of loss — if so, here where the reed's contact with the wine preceded, it is permitted for drinking even not in a place of loss.
