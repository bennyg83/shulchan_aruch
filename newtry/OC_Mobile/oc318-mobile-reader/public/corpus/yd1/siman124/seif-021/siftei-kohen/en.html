And it is not called touch what touches through a person. And even if the non-Jew intends to pour a libation in this way and says explicitly he is pouring a libation through this — his words are nothing; and likewise we say one who touches a vessel that has wine inside — this is not called touch of a non-Jew in wine through something else, rather he touches the vessel and the vessel touches the wine; and also with a reed one may say thus: whenever the reed's touch in wine preceded the person's touch on the reed, this is not touch of a non-Jew through something else — it implies he brings something else to wine; but this one only touches the reed that touches wine; but one must settle this matter — end Rivash there; and Darkhei Moshe brings it; and it appears for us who establish below seif 24 that touch of idolatry through something else is permitted even for drinking in a place of loss — if so here when the reed's touch in wine preceded, permitted for drinking even not in a place of loss.
