In drinking. According to our practice, it is permitted for drinking in a case of loss. The Siftei Kohen (Sh"akh) and the Ateret Zekenim bring proof from the Talmud that even by strict law it is permitted for drinking if he threw it in anger — see later in siman 125 seif 5.
Intentionally. The Siftei Kohen wrote: meaning, not with intent to pour a libation; but if he did not intend to touch at all, it is permitted even for drinking via a reed, since this is contact by a non-Jew via an intermediary, which is permitted for drinking as stated above in siman 14 and below in siman 24.
Loss. The Siftei Kohen wrote: that is, even a minor loss; and according to our practice it is permitted even for drinking in a case of loss. And so throughout this seif, whenever the author wrote "permitted for benefit," according to our practice it is permitted even for drinking. And similarly if there were flies on the mouth of the barrel and he flicked them away, whether with a reed or by hand — it is permitted for benefit and forbidden for drinking, but according to our practice it is permitted even for drinking in a case of loss.
In drinking. Specifically when [the flow] continued, but if it did not continue it is permitted even for drinking. Siftei Kohen.
