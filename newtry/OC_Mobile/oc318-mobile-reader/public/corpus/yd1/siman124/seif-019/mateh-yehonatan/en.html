(There seif 22) A barrel that cracked lengthwise, etc. See Shach seif kaf 58 what he wrote in the name of Turei Zahav that here even if there is a Jew it is permitted in benefit — see there; and it is astonishing at first glance, for if so Raavad is still not resolved at all, for all that compelled Raavad to his answer was to resolve what is the difference between this and the barrel whose spigot was removed that is forbidden in benefit while here permitted — therefore he had to say regarding the barrel whose spigot was removed it deals when there was a Jew; if so according to Turei Zahav's words that here even if there was a Jew it is also permitted, Raavad's difficulty from "what is said here and what is said there" remains. And it is possible Shach intended in what he wrote on him that it is not clear, as appears from Raavad's words, etc.; and he intended what my teacher may his light shine wrote. However one may reject: certainly one may distinguish between here and the barrel whose spigot was removed regarding rescue — for regarding a barrel that burst, in panic to save immediately we properly say the non-Jew is occupied in rescue even if there is a Jew, because if he delays a moment all the wine will spill to the ground; but regarding the spigot being removed, if a Jew stands there there is no occupation at all in rescue since at this time he will lose only a little and there is no occupation here at all; but if there is no Jew at all, certainly there is no distinction at all in occupation of rescue between this case and the spigot removal, since if he does not save he will lose everything like the burst barrel; and accordingly Raavad proves that there must be no Jew and it is forbidden; if so there is a difficulty what is said here and what is said regarding the burst barrel where we say there is occupation of rescue; but when there is a Jew one may properly distinguish between here and spigot removal as Turei Zahav wrote — examine carefully.
