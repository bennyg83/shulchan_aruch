Turei Zahav wrote: great loss; and in my humble opinion likewise minor loss; and likewise it appears from his words in Darkhei Moshe: and in my humble opinion since it is a rabbinic prohibition the halachah follows the lenient; and further they are later and know better the words of the early ones and ruled to permit; and even our rabbi the Tur did not mention at all the words of the early ones — from this it is obvious to him the halachah is to be lenient — end; and likewise it appears from Bach's words — even in minor loss we establish thus; and further that the stringent ones are the Rif according to Ran and Rambam according to Beit Yosef who wrote they omitted that baraita of measuring whether by hand or foot — from this they hold forbidden in benefit by hand; and in my humble opinion on the contrary it is more reasonable: they compared it to the oil case (above seif 12) and other similar cases where unintentional touch is permitted in benefit; and it appears to him like R' Nasan who forbids — because he holds unintentional touch forbids in benefit, and we do not establish like him; and since they wrote regarding oil and unintentional touch of a non-Jew that it is permitted in benefit, they did not need to write measuring by hand permitted in benefit — examine carefully.
