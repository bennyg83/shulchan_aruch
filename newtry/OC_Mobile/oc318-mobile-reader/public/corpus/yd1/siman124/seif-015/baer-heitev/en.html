The faucet. The Siftei Kohen (Sh"akh) wrote: it seems to me that even if we saw the faucet removed, as long as he did not touch the wine itself with his hand, it is permitted — since he does not know the nature of libation, this is considered contact by a non-Jew unintentionally via an intermediary, which is permitted for drinking; and here it is entirely permitted even not in a case of loss.
