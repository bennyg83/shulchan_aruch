For we suspend that he threw vessels in his foolishness with a reed and through that it swayed and wine came out — for all who do not know the nature of libation we suspend in whatever is possible — Ran there; and in my humble opinion even if we saw he drew out the spigot, as long as he did not touch with his hand the wine itself it is permitted, for since he does not know the nature of libation it is unintentional touch of a non-Jew through something else permitted for drinking, as below seif 24; and all the more according to what we establish in seif 24 that even a non-Jew who drew out the spigot is permitted for drinking in a place of loss — here entirely permitted.
