A skin-flask, etc. — Beit Yosef explains: this is a sealed skin-flask, for an open one — Rambam and the author hold above seif 17 that there is a libation concern; and likewise Turei Zahav wrote sealed skin-flask; and according to what the rabbi wrote there that in a place of loss even open is permitted — likewise here; and see below siman 125 seif 8, 9, 10.
