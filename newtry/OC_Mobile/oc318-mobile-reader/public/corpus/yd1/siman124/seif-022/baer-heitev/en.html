In benefit. The Taz wrote: But for drinking it is forbidden, for it is contact of a non-Jew by way of another object. And if you say: if so, let us say regarding one who touches a vessel from the outside that he should forbid for this reason — there is nothing difficult: when the vessel is whole, "touching" is not applicable, for every touching by way of another object means that the toucher brings another object at the time of contact to the wine; which is not so with a whole vessel — the vessel already had wine inside it, and this has no contact with the wine; but here the barrel cracked and it is as if it moved away from the wine, therefore it is forbidden for drinking and only permitted in benefit, for he intends to save the wine; and here it deals with where there is no Jew there to save — and unlike the Levush, who wrote that here even if there is a Jew there it is permitted in benefit, and he was not precise at all.
And embraced it. The language of the Ran: It is permitted to tighten by way of a non-Jew; and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe bring it.
