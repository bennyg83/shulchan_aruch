But for drinking forbidden, for this is touch of a non-Jew through something else — namely he holds the wine through the two halves of the barrel that were separating were it not for the non-Jew; and one could say: if so let us say regarding touch on a vessel from outside it should be forbidden — touch of wine through the vessel walls; for when the vessel is whole touch does not apply, for all touch through something else means the toucher brings something else at the time of touching to wine; but when the vessel is whole the vessel is already wine inside and there is no touch in wine; but here since the barrel cracked it is as though it separated from wine and separated from it — since it was separating were it not for his holding; therefore properly he brings the something else; and one could say from what the Gemara challenges daf 58 — behold he touched the stopper — learn that this is called touch through something else; for Rashi explained there he thought to draw with a vessel called stopper from the barrel, etc. — found also here the toucher brings the something else to wine; but in benefit permitted because he intends to save wine; and here it deals when there is no Jew to save, as will be explained nearby; and likewise in Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba.
