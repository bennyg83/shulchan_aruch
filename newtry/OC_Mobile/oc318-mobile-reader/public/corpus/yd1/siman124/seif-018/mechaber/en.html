If a non-Jew took a utensil of wine and lifted it and poured the wine, even though he did not agitate it, it becomes forbidden, since the wine came by his ko’ach. If he lifted and did not agitate and did not touch, it is permitted. {Rama: And all the more so he does not forbid merely through touching the utensil alone (the poskim mentioned above and Rivash siman 310).}
