He took, and so forth. This follows the cases of a non-Jew with a wine jar and a non-Jew pouring wine. The opinion cited holds the entire wine becomes prohibited, as stated at the beginning of siman 125, but only where he lifted the vessel. Beit Yosef says this appears from the Rif and Rosh. Mordechai, Hagahot Ashiri, Rashba, Tur, and the Shulchan Aruch above, seif 14, imply otherwise, and this requires study. See siman 125, seif katan 5.
He lifted, and so forth. This follows the cases of a wineskin and a jug, and the case of pouring, where the wine remaining inside is permitted and only what emerged is prohibited because it came through his force.
(Supplement) He lifted but did not, and so forth. This follows Avodah Zarah 71b: when the non-Jew drew it he acquired it, but it did not thereby become non-Jewish wine. See siman 132, seif 2. [4] [125] [132]
