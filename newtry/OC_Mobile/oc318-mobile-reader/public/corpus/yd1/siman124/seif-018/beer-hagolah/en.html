From that Mishnah concerning one who measured the wine.
The Rif, Rosh, and Agudah rule stringently. The Rashba, Ran, Rabbeinu Yerucham, and Mordechai conclude, however, that libation performed with the foot is not considered libation sufficient to prohibit benefit. This likewise appears from the Ramban and Rambam in chapter 11 of Hilchot Maachalot Assurot.
