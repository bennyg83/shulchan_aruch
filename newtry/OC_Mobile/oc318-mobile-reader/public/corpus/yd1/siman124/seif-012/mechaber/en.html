A non-Jew who entered the house or shop of a Jew to request wine, and extended his hand while searching and touched the wine and agitated it — it becomes forbidden in benefit. And if it became mixed with other wine, the value of the wine that was agitated is forbidden in benefit, and the rest is permitted in benefit. But if he extended his hand toward the barrel thinking it was of oil and it turned out to be of wine, it is permitted in benefit.
