If he touched the boiling, etc. Bach end of seif 14 wrote: it is written in Hagahot Or Zarua — a barrel that was fermenting and raising boiling is connection to wine; and if a non-Jew touched the boiling of wine it is forbidden; and that which he stated patting and did not state touching when descending is to inform there is no way of libation in that and permitted in benefit; and likewise regarding touch forbidden for drinking — end; and the rabbi brings it in hagahah to Shulchan Aruch seif 1 — end of his words. It appears the rabbi understood the rabbi's view is forbidden only for drinking; and it is astonishing, for it appears the rabbi's view forbids in benefit like touch in the wine itself; and in truth the rabbi did not know whence the rabbi took his words, for the rabbi did not bring at all in Darkhei Moshe the Hagahot Or Zarua's words, but wrote in Darkhei Moshe seif 13: further as Beit Yosef — that which he patted the boiling barrel so the boiling would settle is permitted in benefit, namely specifically patting since he does not touch except through patting, even though it is in his hand permitted in benefit, for there is no way of libation in that; and it further appears they said only regarding patting the boiling; but if he patted the wine itself forbidden in benefit — end; and likewise in Mordechai perek HaSocher and Semag: if he touched the boiling it is as though he touched the wine itself — end; Darkhei Moshe — behold explicitly forbidden in benefit too; however for us even if he touched the wine itself permitted in benefit in a place of loss.
Forbidden in benefit. — For us permitted in benefit in a place of loss.
And the rest is permitted, etc. — He shall sell to a non-Jew except for the value of yayin nesech in it, as below seif kaf 62; and if there are sixty there, permitted even for drinking, as below siman 134 seif kaf 21.
