And the rest is permitted, etc. — He shall sell to a non-Jew except for the value of yayin nesech in it, as below seif kaf 62; and if there are sixty there, permitted even for drinking, as below siman 134 seif kaf 21.
And it is found, etc. — See above seif 19 in the hagahah.
That he sucked with a pump-reed in the barrel and drew it up, etc. — Tosafot wrote, end of tractate Avodah Zarah daf 58: it deals when he did not touch the wine with the pump-reed, such as when they first placed the pump-reed in wine and its head was outside the barrel and sealed the mouth of the barrel and the mouth of the pump-reed; and when one wants to open it, one drills the pump-reed, etc.; alternatively when he drilled he did not touch the reed to wine, but when he wants to draw up the wine he tilts the barrel until the wine reaches the pump-reed — for otherwise even if he did not draw up it would be forbidden in benefit because of touching wine through the pump-reed (unlike measuring with a reed below seif 19 — there he had no intention of stirring at all); but here he intended to stir and it is forbidden in benefit — end of their words; however for us the touch of non-Jews through something else is permitted even for drinking in a place of loss, as below seif 24.
