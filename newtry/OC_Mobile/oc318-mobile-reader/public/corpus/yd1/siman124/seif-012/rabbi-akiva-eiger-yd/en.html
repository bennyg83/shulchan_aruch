And if the concern is the drop of wine that returns. In my humble opinion, this does not mean literally one drop. Rather, sixty times the quantity of all the wine contained in the suction tube is required, because all the wine in the tube is regarded as having been touched by his mouth. This is also evident from the Rambam's wording in chapter 12, halachah 15, of Hilchot Maachalot Assurot, where he writes that when the person stops sucking, the wine that rose into the tube returns. This is likewise explicit in Rabbeinu Yerucham. See there.
