(Taz seif kaf 12) And Shulchan Aruch did not mention here anything of this. Otherwise it would not be possible for the author, for he ruled in seif 9 like Rif and Rambam that measuring by hand is forbidden in benefit. And also on Rama one may say: regarding actual touch by hand, since there are two reasons to be stringent — first the Rosh's approach that saving wine is not called occupation; second that measuring by hand is forbidden in benefit — therefore they did not want to be lenient in such a case except in measuring by hand in seif 9 where there is certainly occupation, he relied on poskim who permit in benefit; and likewise resting his hand on a spigot in seif 23 he relied on the reasoning that rescue is occupation, that in such a case even for poskim who forbid measuring by hand one may say it is permitted since he did not actually touch, as Shach wrote there seif kaf 65. But regarding touching with his hand to rely on occupation of rescue — there are two weaknesses to be stringent, in my humble opinion.
(Taz seif kaf 18) The reason there is because he did not, etc. I am astonished — nevertheless it should be forbidden because of preventing wine from coming out, like resting his hand on the hole; and Rashi for his reason holds resting his hand is sharp — forbidden for drinking; but for the Rosh who forbids in benefit it is difficult — examine carefully.
