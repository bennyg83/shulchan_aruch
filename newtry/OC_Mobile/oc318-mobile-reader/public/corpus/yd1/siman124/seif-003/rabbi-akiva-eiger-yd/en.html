And some say that if he touched it with his foot. This accords with the Rashba's responsum explaining the Rambam's view, cited by the Shach above, siman 123, seif katan 43, that the leniency applies specifically to the foot, whereas the other limbs are comparable to the hand. Rabbeinu Yerucham, however, as cited by the Beit Yosef, states: "With the other limbs, apart from his hand and mouth, it is not customary to pour a libation."
