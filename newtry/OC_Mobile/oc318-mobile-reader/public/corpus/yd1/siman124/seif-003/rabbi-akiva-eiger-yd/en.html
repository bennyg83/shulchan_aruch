(Seif 11 in the hagahah) And there are those who say if he touched with his foot. As Rashba wrote in a responsum according to Rambam's view, brought in Shach (above siman 123 seif kaf 43) — specifically with the foot, but other limbs are like his hand; but in Ravyah brought in Beit Yosef it is found: other limbs besides his hand and mouth have no way to pour libation — end of his words.
