Certainly forbidden. — For drinking — Beit Yosef; and likewise proven in the Gemara. Bach wrote: however, that the wine of a ger toshav is permitted for benefit, and likewise the wine of a non-Jew who does not worship idols in sixty — that is specifically when it is known the non-Jew did not touch it; and see below siman 128 seif kaf 3; however for us in our time in every case permitted for benefit in a place of loss, as above beginning siman 123.
And they circumcised and immersed. And Ran wrote that Shmuel in the Gemara who forbids regarding slaves who circumcised and immersed until eighteen months that avodah zarah comes out of their mouths — means specifically when they circumcised and immersed against their will, for it is possible to circumcise and immerse them for the sake of slavery against their will as found in perek HaChalitzah; but according to their will and desire permitted immediately for everyone — end of his words; and Beit Yosef brings it; and likewise Ra'ah in Sefer Ba'alei HaNefesh daf 129 side 2; and likewise Raavan siman 306 in the name of a Gaon's responsum; and accordingly for us who establish like Rav who disagrees with Shmuel, even if immersed against their will permitted immediately; but it appears Ran and his camp hold like Rashi in perek HaChalitzah (Yevamot daf 48) that one may immerse his slave against his will; but according to what we establish below siman 267 seif 5 that if he circumcised and immersed his slave against his will for the sake of slavery it is as though he did not circumcise and did not immerse — if so certainly here it deals when he circumcised and immersed willingly, for otherwise their touch forbids like other idolaters, for behold he is as though he did not circumcise and did not immerse; and likewise it appears from Tosafot perek R' Yishmael (Avodah Zarah daf 57 side 2) who wrote: Or Zarua says specifically regarding slaves Shmuel said requires sinking, for even though they need immersion and circumcision willingly as found in HaChalitzah, nevertheless the fear of his master is upon him and he does not convert wholeheartedly; but idolaters who circumcise and immerse willingly — according to their view their touch is permitted immediately — end of his words; and even though in Tosafot's rulings there it is not so — it is a copying error — see there; and likewise it appears in Mordechai there; however also Nekudat Yosef perek HaChalitzah and Semak siman 259 hold like Rashi and his camp.
