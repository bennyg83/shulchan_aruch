A ger toshav — that is, one who accepted upon himself 7 mitzvot — and likewise a convert who was circumcised but did not immerse: their touch forbids only for drinking. {Rama: And so long as he did not immerse properly, it is called that he did not immerse (Ran, perek HaSocher, and Hagahot Mordechai to Yevamot, perek HaCholeitz). And some are lenient even regarding the touch of a ger toshav (Tur in the name of the Rosh, and Tosafot, and Mordechai). But his wine is certainly forbidden (Beit Yosef).}
