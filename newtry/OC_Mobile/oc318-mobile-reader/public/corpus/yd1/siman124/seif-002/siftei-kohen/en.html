But only for drinking. — And it is found in the Gemara (Avodah Zarah daf 57) that even a newborn makes yayin nesech for drinking — and so in the poskim.
Seven mitzvot of Noah's sons. Bach wrote: it appears that even if he accepted upon himself only not to worship idols, his contact is permitted for benefit; and he did not list the seven mitzvot except to clarify that nevertheless he forbids his contact in drinking. So too Perishah siman 11; and so it appears from what Taz wrote: any gentile who does not worship idols—his wine is forbidden in drinking and permitted for benefit.
And as long as he did not immerse properly. — In the manner explained below siman 268 he is considered to not have immersed.
And there are those who are lenient even in touch of a ger toshav. At first glance it implies specifically touch of a ger toshav but not touch of a convert who circumcised and did not immerse; and it is difficult, for there is no one who holds thus, for Tosafot and the Rosh wrote that whether touch of a ger toshav or touch of a convert who circumcised and did not immerse — permitted even for drinking; and it is possible the rabbi holds in his reasoning one may not rely on those who are lenient in any matter except touch of a ger toshav but not touch of a convert who circumcised and did not immerse, as Ran wrote that a convert who circumcised and did not immerse is worse than a ger toshav, for a ger toshav accepts from now and a convert who circumcised and did not immerse does not accept until after immersion. And more likely this is what the rabbi meant: and there are those who are lenient even in touch of a ger toshav — all the more a convert who circumcised and accepted all the commandments; and likewise Tosafot and the Rosh: a ger toshav does not forbid wine through his touch — all the more a convert who circumcised and accepted all the commandments, etc.; meaning the rabbi wrote "even," for otherwise he should have said only "and there are those who permit touch of a ger toshav," etc.; and likewise Turei Zahav: and there are those who are lenient in their touch; but their wine is certainly forbidden — end of his words; behold it refers to both; and as he explained; and likewise in Sefer Api Ravrevi daf 78 side 1: and there are those who are lenient in their touch; and in our time it appears to be lenient — end.
