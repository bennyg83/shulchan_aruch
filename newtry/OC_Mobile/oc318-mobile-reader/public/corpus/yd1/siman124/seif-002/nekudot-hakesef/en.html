(Ibid., seif katan 12) He was not precise, etc. It is he who was not precise. The Bach explains the matter according to the Tur, who follows the Rosh and maintains that, with respect to a spigot, the concern of preoccupation does not apply, as I wrote in their names in Shach, seif katan 23. The Bach's difficulty is only that we see plainly that the non-Jew intends solely to preserve the wine from being lost. He answers that, since this is not classified as preoccupation, we are concerned that the non-Jew may subsequently pour a libation. What he wrote according to Rama is likewise incorrect, because this case is different: the non-Jew inserted his finger inside and touched the wine, as I wrote in Shach, seif katan 67; see there.
