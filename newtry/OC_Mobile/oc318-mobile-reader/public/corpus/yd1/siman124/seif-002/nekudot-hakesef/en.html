(There, seif katan 12) He was not precise, etc. It is he who was not precise. The Bach explains this according to the Tur, who follows the Rosh that the distraction rationale does not apply to a spigot, as I wrote in their names in the Shach, seif katan 23. His difficulty was that our eyes show that the gentile intends only to prevent the wine from being lost. He answers that since this is not deemed a distracting preoccupation, we suspect that the gentile may afterward pour it as an idolatrous libation. What he wrote according to the Rama is also incorrect, because here the gentile inserted his finger inside and touched the wine, as stated in the Shach, seif katan 67; see there.
