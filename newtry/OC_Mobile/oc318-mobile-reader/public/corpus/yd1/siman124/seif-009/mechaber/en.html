The anusim — even the best among them — cannot be careful about the touch of a non-Jew; and since they are suspected regarding it, they are not trusted regarding what is theirs even with an oath, but they are trusted regarding what belongs to others. {Rama: And they do not forbid wine through their touch, and this is specifically those who still live among non-Jews and commit transgressions publicly due to coercion, but in private are careful and it is possible for them to flee for their lives, but they delay because of money or the like. But if they also transgress in private — even though at first they did so under coercion — they are like non-Jews (Rivash siman 4).}
