A non-Jew who does not worship idols, such as Ishmaelites who touched, etc. — Rambam there — and the author omitted it; it appears they hold this is not exclusive, and as Beit Yosef wrote in his conclusion: any non-Jew whom we know not to worship idols — his law is like a nation that does not worship avodah zarah — see there.
But if his prohibition, etc. — Meaning: wherever we say forbidden for drinking but permitted for benefit because of a touch aspect — as will be explained, such as unintentional touch, and patting over the barrel as below seif 19, and all the more force — all such that are forbidden for drinking because of a touch aspect: if the non-Jew is not an idolater, permitted even for drinking; and see below seif kaf 49 and seif kaf 71, and siman 125 seif kaf 2.
