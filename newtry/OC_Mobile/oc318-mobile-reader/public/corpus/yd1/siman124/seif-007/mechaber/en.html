There is one who says that any place they said regarding our wine that it is permitted in benefit and forbidden for drinking, because of a type of touching that a non-Jew touched it when the non-Jew was an idol-worshipper; but if its prohibition was because of a non-Jew who is not an idol-worshipper who touched our wine unintentionally, or who tapped on the mouth of the barrel, it is permitted for drinking; and likewise in all similar cases.
