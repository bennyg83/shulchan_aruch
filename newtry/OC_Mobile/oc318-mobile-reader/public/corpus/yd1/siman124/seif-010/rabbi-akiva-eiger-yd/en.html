(Taz seif kaf 21) And this is what Rama wrote. At first glance in precision Rama stated "person" — for according to Rambam's and the author's ruling seif 17 that stirring in the body of the vessel is forbidden, at first glance Rivash's law does not exist; for what of it that the reed was already there — nevertheless there will be stirring like in the vessel itself; if so Rama who ruled there to be stringent not in a place of loss — perforce in precision he stated "person." And indeed Rivash's novelty is astonishing, for he stated it as reasoning and did not write that for Rambam it is concealed; and perhaps for this reason he concluded that one must settle the matter — it is strained.
