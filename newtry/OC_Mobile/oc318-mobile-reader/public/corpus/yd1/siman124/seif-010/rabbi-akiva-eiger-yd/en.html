And this is what the Rama wrote. At first glance, the Rama precisely used the word "person." According to the Rambam and the Mechaber's ruling in seif 17 that shaking the wine by moving the vessel itself renders it forbidden, the Rivash's ruling would seemingly not apply. What difference does it make that the reed was already there? Moving it should still constitute shaking, just as moving the vessel itself does. Since the Rama rules there stringently except in a case of loss, he must have deliberately specified a person. Indeed, I find the Rivash's ruling surprising, for he states it as a matter of reasoning without noting that it is contradicted by the Rambam's position. Perhaps this is why he concluded that the matter requires deliberation, although this explanation is strained.
