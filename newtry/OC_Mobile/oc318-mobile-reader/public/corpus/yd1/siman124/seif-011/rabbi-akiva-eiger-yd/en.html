(Seif 11 in the hagahah) And there are those who say if he touched with his foot. As Rashba wrote in a responsum according to Rambam's view, brought in Shach (above siman 123 seif kaf 43) — specifically with the foot, but other limbs are like his hand; but in Ravyah brought in Beit Yosef it is found: other limbs besides his hand and mouth have no way to pour libation — end of his words.
(Taz seif kaf 7) And it would be like touching all of it. At first glance from Rashba's and Ran's words brought in Taz (seif kaf 8) it appears only through the law of stream; and if so there is a difficulty on the author who ruled all forbidden in benefit; for according to the author's ruling (siman 126 seif 7) stream even through touch of a non-Jew is not like touching all of it but only as though the spurting mixed — and according to Rashba's approach; and accordingly stream for stam yeinam is permitted in benefit, for even when mixed it is permitted to sell except the value of forbidden benefit; and as Rashba explicitly wrote brought in Beit Yosef there; and if so here the law would be permitted in benefit. And perforce the author holds it is worse than stream, being like touching all of it actually. I again saw in Nekudat HaKesef (seif kaf 133) who wrote thus explicitly: what Ran wrote that it is like touching all of it means through the law of stream; and the reason Pri Megadim ruled that we establish in Pri Megadim letter aleph stream is not connection — see there; and if so there is a difficulty on Shulchan Aruch in what he wrote all forbidden in benefit — and likewise as stated.
(Taz, seif katan 8) Ran wrote in the name of Raavad. See Tiferes LeMoshe and Lechem Mishneh, who explain that according to this, the Jew must likewise begin sucking before the non-Jew does.
