What is the “touch” by which a non-Jew forbids the wine in benefit? If he touched with his hand, or with his foot (and there are those who say that if he touched with his foot he does not forbid in benefit, for that is not the way of libation — in Beit Yosef), or with another item (that is in his hand), and he agitated it — even with his mouth, such as if he drank from it, or if he sucked with a spout in the barrel and brought it up to his mouth — all the wine is forbidden in benefit. Nevertheless, a utensil that has two spouts, like a utensil used for washing hands, and there is wine in it: a Jew can suck from this spout and a non-Jew from that spout at the same time, provided that the Jew stops before the non-Jew stops. {Rama: If he touched the bubbling that is on the wine, it is as though he touched the wine itself (Mordechai, last perek of Avodah Zarah, and Beit Yosef according to the opinion of Rif and Rambam).}
