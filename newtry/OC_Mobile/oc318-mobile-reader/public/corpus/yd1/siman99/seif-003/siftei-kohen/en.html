The absorbed piece, etc. Explanation: the piece that absorbed from prohibition that fell into permitted food, and there is not sixty in the permitted food to nullify the prohibition except through combining the piece — they also combine the piece to nullify it; however the piece itself is forbidden, because the prohibition in it is not entirely emitted, as Rashba and Mechaber wrote at the beginning of siman 106; see siman 92 s.k. 10.
We do not practice that way. Rather, one needs sixty against the entire piece, and even so the piece itself remains forbidden, as will be explained below at the beginning of siman 106 in the gloss; and see there what I wrote.
