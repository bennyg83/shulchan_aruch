One does not nullify a forbidden item l'chatchila; and even if a forbidden item fell into permitted food that does not have enough measure to nullify it, one does not add permitted food in order to nullify it. If one transgressed and nullified it, or increased permitted food upon it — if b'shogeg it is permitted; if b'mezid it is forbidden for the one who nullifies it himself if it is his, and likewise for one for whom it was nullified (and a forbidden item that was nullified through a non-Jew's action is permitted, as below seif 7):
