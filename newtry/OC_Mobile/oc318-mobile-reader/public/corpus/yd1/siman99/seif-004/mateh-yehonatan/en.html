(There in hagahah) Investigate whether dry mixed with dry or even wet with wet. And that Rama wrote stam without distinguishing between basar b'chalav and other prohibitions — in basar b'chalav certainly we do not say chein nafsho in wet in wet since certainly sixty against the meat — the milk is forbidden in itself; if so the mixture should be forbidden because it can be squeezed; and some say this deals with when there is sixty against the entire mixture, and for this he disagrees between wet in wet and dry in dry — only regarding that piece itself chein nafsho is not needed; as Shach wrote s.k. 10; and that Shach wrote in basar b'chalav one need not inquire — that is because in wet in wet we do not say chein nafsho, see there; still difficult — if before us a piece upon which a drop of milk fell on the piece, why did Tur not write that in such a case one must inquire; one must say this need not be written — certainly without knowledge one must inquire, lest it was placed entirely outside the sauce — if so sixty is needed against the entire piece; if so certainly one comes to inquire the entire action, and Tur need not write a general rule; and what Shach challenges from Darkei Moshe from the drop of milk nevelah, etc. — see siman 93 for proper resolution.
