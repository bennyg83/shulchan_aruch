And in the kippah. It is a thin fine layer of meat and spice gathered at the bottom of the pot.
Even though there was more permitted food, etc. Rashba in his novellae and Ran wrote that if we saw the prohibition at the time it fell and it did not diminish by one measure together with the permitted food, we estimate the permitted food that was absorbed in the pot and combine it with what is inside the pot, and we estimate the prohibition as at the time it fell against the permitted food. And Rashal Pischei HaGolah siman 34 wrote to be stringent even when we saw, for one cannot stand on the root of the matter and does not know how much the pot absorbed — rather one always estimates as it comes before us — end of his words; and accordingly one may not rule permissively in what Shulchan Aruch wrote in this seif "and we estimate with a good estimate," etc.
And this is only not its kind in its kind. For in this one needs sixty from the Torah, since it gives taste — therefore one cannot estimate what was absorbed; and some say: why do we permit now when there is sixty before us? Perhaps at the time the prohibition fell the prohibition was very large and much contracted from what the permitted food contracted — we do not establish prohibition presumptively since now there is no presumption and there is sixty; and through this is resolved what he extended in Derishah to challenge why we combine the bones with the permitted food though they do not diminish in cooking, and the prohibition may have been greater and diminished — for we presume as it is found so it was, in order not to establish presumption to say there was change in this pot initially from what there is now; and what Tur wrote that the prohibition too was greater, etc. — that is, since you wish to be lenient in this pot and consider also what was absorbed, since it is known there was more initially — if so we tell you that in the prohibition too there was more in this pot, since it is not like an ordinary pot — and in vain did the Rav toil in this.
But in its kind. It is voided from the Torah in the majority, only the Sages required sixty — we suspend to be lenient and we estimate with a good estimate, etc.; and this was already explained that we do not establish thus.
