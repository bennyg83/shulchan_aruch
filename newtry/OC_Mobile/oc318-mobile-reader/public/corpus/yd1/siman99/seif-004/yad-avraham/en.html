(Seif 4) We estimate the permitted food and the prohibition as they come before us, even though there was more permitted food initially and it diminished in cooking and was absorbed in the pot, since from the prohibition too was absorbed and diminished from the quantity it was. Rashi and Tur. It appears if the prohibition is meat of an old ox whose way is to diminish through cooking, and the permitted food is tender calf meat whose way is to expand through cooking, as stated in Uktzin chapter 2 mishnah 8 and brought in Menachot (daf 54) — we do not estimate the prohibition as it came before us, for certainly it diminished from the quantity it was and the taste remained in the pot; and this is not comparable to what we taught in Taharot chapter 3 mishnah 4 and Rambam ruled chapter 4 of Laws of Fathers of Impurity: an olive-volume of nevelah that diminished — we follow the present regarding impurity — because the taste remained in the pot; and if the permitted food too is ox meat we say it diminished proportionally. And it appears to me: if the prohibition is calf meat that expanded through cooking and one must estimate against it as it is now; and if the gravy spilled and it is known there was sixty against it as it was when it fell, only it is not known whether there was sixty against it when it expanded — we go to leniency, for what expanded is rabbinic prohibition, as stated in Menachot (daf 54); and see in responsa Radbaz vol. 3.
