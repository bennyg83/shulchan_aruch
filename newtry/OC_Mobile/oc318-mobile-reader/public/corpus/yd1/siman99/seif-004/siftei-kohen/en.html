Even though there was more permitted food at first and it diminished in cooking and was absorbed in the pot. Even so one does not estimate except as it comes before us — that in the prohibition too there was more and it diminished and was absorbed in the pot — end of Tur's words, and this is from Rashi and Rosh; and Maharshal wrote chullin 98 siman 34: it appears in my view the explanation is where we saw the prohibition fell, etc. — one should not estimate what the pot absorbed, for one cannot stand on the root of the matter and does not know how much the pot absorbed and how many times spoilage will come from this; rather one always estimates as it comes before us. Rashi did not rule thus as law, but nevertheless the essence is not to estimate except as it comes before us — such is the correct way l'chatchila — end of his words.
