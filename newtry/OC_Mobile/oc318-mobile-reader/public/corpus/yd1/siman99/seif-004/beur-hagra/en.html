And they estimate the heter, etc. And this is, etc. — per Rashi there s.v. bamai, etc., and the essence of the matter, etc., meaning if we did not see the prohibition at the time it fell; and R' Chanina deals with where we saw the prohibition at the time it fell and it is now as it was and did not contract and the heter contracted. And Rashba wrote and it appears from his words that in such a case even what was consumed and lost through the fire also combines; and to me — what do we care what was at first, now it diminished and the prohibition returns and forbids; and as written (there) and the piece itself is forbidden, for if it fell, etc.; and so from Rambam's words who wrote in what the pot absorbed and stands in its walls, etc., and they estimate, etc., as written in Shulchan Aruch. And proof from what is written in chapter 5 of Terumot: just as the hullin diminished, so, etc. — it appears if the terumah did not diminish it is forbidden; and he wrote further that one may say the first version is in the pot itself — meaning in what it is now in the pot and not in what the pot absorbed; and the latter version is even in what it absorbed; and they ruled like the latter version of Mar bar Rav Ashi 98a — he holds like him and they did not challenge him except "is that so," etc., and without knowing the measure of prohibition as above; and he wrote and it is reasonable specifically min b'mino, but min she'eino mino the halachah is like the first version stringently, for it is a Torah doubt; and one must say he holds milk and meat are min b'mino — therefore Mar bar Rav Ashi was lenient; and see above siman 98 seif 1 from Shach [and behold Rambam and Rashi are like the latter version that the pot absorbed, only for Rashi if we did not see it at the fall we estimate as it is without the pot, and so Rashba holds in min b'mino; and as written "is that so prohibition," etc.; and as written that the halachah is like the latter version of Mar bar Rav Ashi, etc., and they did not challenge, etc., except in min she'eino mino they divide like the first version and one may say meaning even if we saw it at the time of the fall and it did not contract — and this is the distinction between the first version and the latter version; and so in Tur "and they estimate the heter," etc., that also in prohibition, etc., and Rashba, etc. And Shulchan Aruch's words are not precise — he began in Tur's language and concluded "and this is min she'eino mino," etc.]:
