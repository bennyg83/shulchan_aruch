One must say as if the pot had absorbed beforehand from water — why should that absorption not enter the calculation, for it absorbs and emits also; and one must say we are not certain what is absorbed in the pot whether it emitted or received part of prohibition — if so even here perhaps immediately before he put prohibition taste in permitted matter the pot already absorbed from permitted matter, and this does not enter the calculation as if it had absorbed beforehand — as written and afterward he puts prohibition in permitted matter taste and there is no sixty here; therefore permitted matter itself needs sixty — and this appears Rashba's reason to be stringent. And behold Tosafot explained in that which we say in the pot itself — the explanation is not against the earthenware of the pot, only if it absorbed beforehand two olives and afterward an olive of prohibition — we say in any case if it emits also the two olives absorbed beforehand it will emit and combine to nullify; and if it does not emit also prohibition it will not emit; and it appears Tosafot hold we do not say in absorbed piece it became nevelah — for if not these two olives already became nevelah, and it is forced to say it deals where it was absorbing meat that is still permitted — this is not mentioned in Tosafot. And accordingly one may say the latter version that speaks in what the pot absorbed does not disagree on this law — only he holds the explanation of the pot is in another matter; but for the law he concedes to this law; and from this Issur VeHeter end of klal 24 derives: if one stuck in two absorbed handfuls — the second absorbed combines to nullify the first absorbed — and this is as above in any case if it emits this too will emit, and if not this too will not emit — and these are Tosafot's words as they are and as their form as written in any case in a pot or in two handfuls when they are one kind; therefore he wrote if the pot is earthenware and the handful metal — this any case does not apply that one rests to emit more than its fellow — behold if the pot and handful are metal it also applies in any case — only afterward he strained to be stringent in a pot — perhaps the handful touches immediately the pot and the pot absorbs from it and the absorbed became nevelah and afterward emits — if so how does absorbed in a pot that already became chein nafsho combine — and it is a mere stringency; and in any case its law is founded on Tosafot's words as solid proof, and all the more so in species in its species — for one might say the latter version disagrees in a rabbinic matter we act leniently as Rosh wrote and so Beit Yosef explicitly; and if so clear law from Tosafot's view.
