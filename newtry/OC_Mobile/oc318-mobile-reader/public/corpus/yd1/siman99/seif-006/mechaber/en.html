A rabbinic prohibition — one does not mix it by hand in order to nullify it; and if one did so b'mezid it is forbidden. But if it fell by itself and there is not enough permitted food to nullify it, one increases permitted food and nullifies it: {Rama: And some say one may not nullify a d'rabbanan prohibition or add to it as with a d'oraisa prohibition, and so is the essential practice (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba and Tur and Hagahot Maimoniyot and Semag and Hagahat Or Zarua and Mordechai and Ran and Hagahot ShaDa).}
