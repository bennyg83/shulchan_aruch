(Seif 6) But if it fell by itself. Hagahot Issur VeHeter wrote specifically that it fell by itself, but if one cast b'shogeg it is forbidden to add and nullify it (see there in siman 477).
(Shach s.k. 21) And in such a case one may be lenient. And in Yerushalmi (chapter 2 of Orlah) they ask whether a companion's knowledge counts as knowledge at the time of mixing, or only the owner's knowledge — I did not resolve this; see Sefer HaArukh from Shach (siman 94).
