(Siman 99 seif 4) And this is in not its kind in its kind. From Beit Yosef's words it appears Rashba deals with a case where we did not see the quality of the forbidden item at first, and therefore in not its kind in its kind it is forbidden — we say the forbidden item too was large and diminished, yet in not its kind in its kind we are lenient regarding chein nafsho as Taz and Shach hold. However it appears to me in my weak view to learn from Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba that without seeing the forbidden item at first, in not its kind in its kind it is also forbidden; only if we saw the forbidden item at first and there was sixty and the permitted food diminished greatly while the forbidden did not diminish so much — in not its kind in its kind we estimate the absorption with a good estimate and it helps nullify; in not its kind in its kind it is forbidden since now there is not sixty. Accordingly Tur wrote well, and Rosh disagrees — for Rosh also permits in not its kind in its kind where we saw the forbidden at first and there was sixty; and Beit Yosef's difficulty is removed as it appears to me for practical halachah.
