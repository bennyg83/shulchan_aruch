(Siman 99 seif 1) A piece of nevelah with meat and bones that fell into a pot. The bones in it combine to nullify the prohibition — so too Rabbeinu Shimshon chapter 5 of Terumot, Rosh, Ran Pischei HaGolah, Rashba in Taharat HaBayit (daf 96 side 2), and Ra'avad in Hagahot chapter 13 of Laws of Terumot. From that which is stated in Yerushalmi: peels of prohibition raise the permitted food — and likewise for nevelah bones that have no taste — see there. And in my humble opinion it is clear they said only peels of prohibition that themselves are not prohibition; and since they have no taste they combine to nullify. But prohibition itself cannot combine to nullify even though it has no taste at all. Know that gid hanasheh — we establish there is no noten taam in sinews, and behold it is like wood; and nevertheless R' Nachman said in the Gemara Pischei HaGolah (daf 97): gid in sixty and gid is not counted. And Rosh wrote: he refers to the mishnah who holds there is noten taam in sinews, or because of its fat — end of his words; and so the poskim, and Tur siman 100 seif 2, and so Rambam explicitly chapter 15 of Laws of Forbidden Foods: fat of the gid in sixty and the gid is not counted — only we require sixty of permitted food. And so in siman 90 regarding kchal: in chapter 1 it is counted because it itself is permitted, as Rashi and Ran explained at the beginning of the chapter; and in chapter 2, once forbidden, it no longer combines even though its entire substance is meat and it should be nullified in the milk — see there and investigate; and if so the law emerges for us regarding a limb from a live animal that has meat and bones that fell into a pot — the bones in it do not combine to nullify, since they themselves are prohibition and combine for lashes with an olive-volume, as stated in the Gemara end of chapter 3 (daf 102) and Tosafot there (daf 96 side 2) and Rambam chapter 5 of Laws of Forbidden Foods. And specifically in a piece of nevelah and the like whose bones are not forbidden, as Ran wrote, and one is not lashed for them unless with an olive-volume of meat, as Rambam wrote chapter 4 of Laws of Forbidden Foods halachah 18 — they combine to nullify; but not in a limb from a live animal, whose bones combine for an olive-volume of meat for lashes — they do not combine; and likewise in raising outside according to R' Yosi in Zevachim (107 side 2) and Menachot (23). And see Tosafot Zevachim (daf 78). And see in Pri Megadim end of seif 2 whether sixty is needed to nullify sinews and bones in a limb from a live animal; and it appears for permission certainly they do not combine.
