A) [Seif 1] A piece of nevelah with meat and bones, etc. — forbidden bones combine with permitted food to nullify the forbidden item, etc., because they are not edible they are not in the category of prohibition, nevertheless they absorb through cooking and therefore combine to nullify the prohibition because the prohibition spreads into them and they also have moisture to blend the prohibition absorbed from the forbidden meat. Taz s.k. 1. Chochmat Adam letter 1.
