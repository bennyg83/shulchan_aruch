Bones of prohibition, etc. Ri Migash in the name of the Gaon brought that this is only species in its species, for Torah law is nullified in the majority, but in not its species not; and the master Peri Chadash upheld him — since they have no pelitah from their body they are like a pot, for we say "did permitted matter absorb and prohibition not?" — why should it combine to nullify, and why should the body of earthenware not combine? Therefore this is only species in its species, which is only a rabbinic measure; and the master in all five seifim wrote it does not appear thus according to Rashba and Rosh; and the correct view with them is: besides that they closed and did not distinguish — also they challenged from what we say in the Gemara according to one who says in sixty "meat with bones we estimate" — it appears bones do not combine to nullify prohibition. And according to Ri Migash from the difficulty — this is only an asmachta that the Sages learned for not its species, for in its species it is nullified in the majority and they did not say in sixty except because of taste of not its species; and in not its species in truth bones do not combine to nullify the prohibition; and on the contrary according to Peri Chadash's words even bones of permitted matter in not its species should not be combined, for it is like a pot. Therefore it appears that otherwise they wondered how we say in the Gemara according to one who says in sixty meat with bones we estimate — behold bones combine to nullify prohibition; and see Rosh who strained that in the Gemara it is only a mere asmachta, and Ra'ah already wondered at this in Beit HaBechirah; therefore what appears in Peri Chadash's difficulties — that Rosh and all answered and said one may say there is moisture in bones, only nevertheless it is no better than moisture from gid, for we establish there is no noten taam in gidin. Therefore in truth there is weak taste in them, for the senses testify — if one cooks bones in water the water changes in taste — and how many times must we say in this that the Sages disagreed whether there is noten taam in gidin or not — certainly they did not disagree in the reality that the palate of one who eats tastes, as Rashba wrote — only there is weak taste and they disagreed whether it is complete taste; and if so bones too have pelitah, only it is weak taste like gid and not taste like the essence the Torah forbade; but in any case since there is taste, even if extremely weak, it at least helps nullify prohibition taste like water and is not like a pot that has no pelitah at all. And if so Peri Chadash's difficulty is settled correctly; but accordingly according to one who says there is noten taam in gidin who holds even weak taste the Torah forbade — also in bones one may say thus to be stringent in the difficulty. One should understand why the Gemara seeks support from foreleg in stew for the measure of prohibitions — even though Rashi wrote explicitly the halachah leMoshe miSinai that we estimate meat with peel, and this is in sixty as known, and as we say in the Gemara; and one must say those amoraim hold the asmachta was in gid as Rashi wrote, and it appears from the Gemara and gid is different for its taste is weak for everyone — if so well they needed doubt in the measure of pelitah; but according to one who says there is no noten taam in gidin they would not disagree on halachah leMoshe miSinai — here the dispute is difficult; and as Rambam wrote — and if so it was regarding other prohibitions — and if so another doubt. And accordingly this sugya of foreleg in stew is all according to one who says there is noten taam in gidin — if so well one who says in sixty holds bones have weak pelitah; and Tosafot's difficulty that it is no better than gid — for us in truth it is forbidden, and therefore bones of prohibition forbid and bones of permitted matter nullify. And the other view holds like the poskim that they have no pelitah at all and are not comparable to gid — therefore they are not in the category of prohibition and also not in the category of combination, for it is like a pot as Peri Chadash wrote they have no pelitah at all. But still it is difficult — you may say bones one may say they have pelitah from their body — an egg shell in not its species certainly needs no pelitah and is like a pot — Peri Chadash's question; and one must say only in its species where they were lenient as Peri Chadash wrote. And it is difficult in Yerushalmi — he asked if bones of permitted matter combine to nullify, and it resolves peels of permitted matter raise the forbidden — how do we learn one from its fellow regarding bones even in not its species, and peels must be in dry and species in its species — how do we learn one from its fellow? But in truth in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of its kinds he asked whether in a hundred or in sixty, and he said according to one who says in a hundred you remove bones of prohibition — and for that view one must say they have no pelitah at all as above — if so well he asked afterward even bones of permitted matter do not help, for it is like a pot; and it answers you cannot — for d'rabbanan they made it like its species that peels of permitted matter help nullify; and even here species in its species is only a mere stringency to help nullify and it is only an asmachta that is only in its species. And Yerushalmi's words are correct; but according to one who says in sixty holds bones have weak pelitah and therefore they forbid, and of permitted matter help — and so. But this is according to one who says noten taam in gidin; but according to one who says there is no noten taam in gidin as we establish — if so this pelitah to forbid is no better than gid and permitted and helps nullify — for us; and the Gemara's words are correct and the law is true law. And that which we say in Yerushalmi Terumah chapter onion in an egg — the vegetable and water and its peel combine — even though peel has no pelitah and is no better than a bowl and it is its species in not its species — for us it also deals where there are eggs; moreover Torah law is nullified in its majority, and even though vegetable and water are not its species it ascends, etc. — and this is proof for Rashba in the previous siman that even in not its species we say ascends, or an egg otherwise does not give taste so much that they considered it to estimate in forty and fifty as explained in the Gemara; and Yerushalmi only required sixty because they did not want to distinguish between other prohibitions — if so in any case peels combine lest all peels amount to more than this measure as Shach wrote explicitly in siman 86 s.k. 15 see there — and so; and the law is true law — investigate between its species and not its species — for us; and all Peri Chadash's and Rosh's difficulties are settled:
