If a small amount of forbidden food was absorbed into a permitted vessel — if it is the way of that vessel to be used abundantly with permitted food, it is permitted to use it l'chatchila, since the forbidden amount is small and it cannot come to impart taste; therefore a slight forbidden item that was absorbed in a pot or inside jugs and the like is permitted to use l'chatchila with the same kind, and one need not kasher the vessel; and this is when it is not ben yomo (Tur). And if it is ben yomo, it is forbidden l'chatchila to cook in it even the same kind, until one kasher it (there in the name of Semak). And if one cooked in it b'dieved, it is permitted (Mordechai and Ran and Rashba and Tur and Beit Yosef). And some forbid even b'dieved (Hagahot Or Zarua and Semag); and the first view is primary.
