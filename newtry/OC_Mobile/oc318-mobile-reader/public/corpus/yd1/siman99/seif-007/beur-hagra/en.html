And this is what is written in a pot — for that in Pesachim or jugs as above, and not as Tosafot wrote in Pesachim there; and R' Yosef explained, etc., for in chapter 5 of Avodah Zarah (68b) Rav is in doubt whether Rav holds nat bar lichta forbidden or permitted — see there in Tosafot who strained; and this is what "and even ben yomo," and so not as the second wrote there in the name of R' Yitzchak because he considers b'dieved since hag'alah is impossible — for jugs are possible through hag'alah and pouring; and this is what is written in a pot or inside jugs — only Tosafot hold that jugs is because it spoils; and R' Yosef wrote — only they needed R' Yitzchak's explanation because of the difficulty above that for Rav it is doubly in doubt as above.
