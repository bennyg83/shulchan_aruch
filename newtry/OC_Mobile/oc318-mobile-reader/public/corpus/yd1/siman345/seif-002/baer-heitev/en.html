Who said. Maharshal wrote that if they did not see him act on his words, we do not rely on his speech, and he is not presumed to have intentionally destroyed himself. The Bach wrote that his words are correct.
Who stole. The Shach wrote: it implies that because of the sin of theft or robbery he is not considered one who separated from the ways of the community, and one mourns for him. According to this, thieves and robbers who die in their beds should be mourned. The Bach wrote that this is the custom, namely where he confessed before death. What the Rav wrote in siman 340 seif 5, that one who is accustomed to commit transgression is not mourned, appears to refer to where he did not confess before death. End quote.
