Who is one who kills himself knowingly—such as one who said behold he goes up to the roof and they saw him go up immediately in anger or he was distressed and fell and died—behold he is presumed to have killed himself knowingly; but if they saw him strangled and hanging on a tree, or killed and cast on his sword, behold he is presumed like all the dead and they engage with him and do not withhold anything from him. {Rama: One who stole and robbed and through this was killed by royal law—they mourn him if there is no danger because of fear of the kingdom and he is not called one who kills himself knowingly (Mahariv siman 114).}
