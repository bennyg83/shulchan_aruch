All who separate from community ways—men who cast off the yoke of mitzvot from their necks and are not included among Israel in their deeds and honor of festivals and sitting in synagogues and study houses but are like free men to themselves like other nations; and likewise apostates and informers—all these have no onenut and are not mourned for them, but their brothers and other relatives wear white and wrap in white and eat and drink and rejoice. {Rama: One who separates from the community and did not wish to bear taxes and levies with them—they mourn him (responsum Rashba siman 963); but the rest of the townspeople need not cease their work for him to engage with him (as Nimukei Yosef implies at the end of Moed Katan).}
