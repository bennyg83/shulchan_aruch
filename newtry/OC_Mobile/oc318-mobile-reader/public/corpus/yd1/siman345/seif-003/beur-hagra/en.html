(Likkut) A minor, etc. There: an incident involving his son, etc.; an incident involving an infant, etc. Seif 5 and seif 7: all who separate, etc.; one who fell, etc. There.
And so too a grown person, etc. From what is written in the chapter of HaArel (78b), that they were punished for not eulogizing Saul — as written in Bereishit Rabbah, chapter of Noach: one might think even one who was persecuted like Saul — therefore the verse states "only."
