A minor, etc. From what is stated in the Tosefta at the end of Sanhedrin: regarding minors of an idolatrous city, Rabbi Eliezer says they are killed and the Sages say they are not killed. This implies that regarding mourning all agree. See above siman 340, where he objects to him, and so the Mordechai there writes in the name of the Ri; see there, siman 1538.
