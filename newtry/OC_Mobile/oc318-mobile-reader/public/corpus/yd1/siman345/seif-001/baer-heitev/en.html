They rend. The wording implies that no one rends for him at all, even his mourners. Ramban and Tur, however, hold that the actual mourners do rend; "they do not rend" refers to more distant people. Shach. Responsa Rashba writes that wherever the Sages say, "we do not occupy ourselves with him," this does not mean that burial and shrouds are withheld.
