The law of one who kills himself knowingly, a banished person, those executed by the court, and those who separate from the community. In it are 8 seifim.
One who kills himself knowingly—one does not engage with him for anything; one does not mourn him; one does not eulogize him; one does not tear or remove shoes; but they stand in a row over him and say the mourners' blessing and anything that is honor for the living.
