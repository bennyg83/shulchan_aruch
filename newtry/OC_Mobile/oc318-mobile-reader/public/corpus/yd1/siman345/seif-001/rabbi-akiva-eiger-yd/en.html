(Siman 345, seif 1) One who deliberately takes his own life. See Responsa Besamim Rosh, siman 345. Regarding whether a person may take his own life where his evil inclination will certainly overpower him and he will not withstand the test, see Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit, Yoreh Deah siman 157. Birkei Yosef, in Shem HaGedolim, letter yud, siman 27, writes that Ritva says in his chiddushim that this is permitted. If so, at minimum such a person is not legally classified as one who deliberately takes his own life. If he acts from a motive of repentance, see Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 111.
