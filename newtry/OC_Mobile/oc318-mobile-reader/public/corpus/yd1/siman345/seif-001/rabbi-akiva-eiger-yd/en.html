(Siman 345, seif 1) One who intentionally takes his own life. See Besamim Rosh, siman 345. Whether a person may take his life when he fears that his evil inclination will overpower him and he will not withstand a religious trial is discussed by Beit Yosef, Bedek HaBayit, Yoreh Deah 157. Birkei Yosef, in Shem HaGedolim, letter yod, siman 27, writes that Ritva permits it; such a person would therefore at least not be classified as one who intentionally took his own life. If he acts from repentance, see Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 111. [Citation 56.]
