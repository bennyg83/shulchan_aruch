And from when is it prohibited. See the responsum of the Radbaz, Part 1, Siman 21, where he wrote that before [the non-Jew] began to prepare the needs of the feast, even if we know with certainty that it is on account of a festival celebration — since the non-Jew stated this explicitly — it is permitted to go there, as his intention is disregarded by everyone. However, if circumstances indicate that there is concern of idolatry at that feast, it is certainly prohibited for a Jew to go there. See there.
