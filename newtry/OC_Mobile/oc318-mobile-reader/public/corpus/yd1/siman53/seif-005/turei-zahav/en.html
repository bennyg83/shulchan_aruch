A bird that had a blow, etc. Ostensibly it appears since we are not expert in inspection one should not rely on this seif; but the opposite is more reasonable, for there is no clear treifah here — why establish a lung defect; we see no sign of illness at all; and we say explicitly in several places that wherever illness did not rule we are not concerned; and furthermore in an animal we are not concerned for lung from a blow on the wall opposite it, as long as it is not scorched, as implied in siman 39; rather this lung concern is only a stringency; accordingly we rely on this inspection that is for stringency:
