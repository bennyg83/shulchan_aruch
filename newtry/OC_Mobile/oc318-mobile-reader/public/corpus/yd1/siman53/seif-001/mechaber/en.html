Laws of a broken bone or blow in an animal and bird. In it are 5 seifim. If an animal's limbs slipped from their root, or broke, or were cut off entirely, or it has three legs — kosher. But the bone (that broke) itself (with a little above the break) is forbidden when broken if skin and flesh do not cover most of it. {Rama: And there are those who declare treifah also the animal if the limb slipped (Shulchan De'ah and Hagahot Maimoniot and Or Zarua) or broke near the body (Hagahot Ashri chapter HaMekasheh); and one looks whether the blood coagulated across the ribs, that one sees the blow passed through the body (so is implied in Issur VeHeter HaAruch general rule 50 din 6); and such is the custom; but one need not be concerned for this as long as one does not see the blood coagulated, and inspection is not needed (we learn from bird in seif 2).}
