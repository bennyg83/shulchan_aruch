And one looks, etc. — And in Shulchan De'ah siman 86 he declares treifah in an animal that is dislocated or has a broken limb in the body, from concern perhaps its nivin were eaten; and so Bach found written thus in Semak manuscript; and accordingly even if the blood did not coagulate across the ribs — treifah; and so I found in Teshuvot Tashbetz siman 257: dislocated wing in a bird at its connection to the shoulder — treifah, because its nivin were eaten, end of his words; and therefore it is a scribal error and it must read: dislocated limb in an animal at its connection, etc.; for in a bird it is obvious it is treifah even if its nivin were not eaten, as stated explicitly in the Gemara and all poskim that dislocated wing in a bird is treifah from concern the lung was perforated; and all the more so per Bach who holds a shoulder does not apply to a bird; and Maharshal in Issur VeHeter and in his book Perek Avodah Zarah siman 99 wrote: in a break one should be stringent like Shulchan De'ah's words, which is not against the Gemara, for it is possible broken and cut is worse than slipped; but in slipped one should not be stringent; and Bach is stringent even in slipped (and so Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe and Tur HaChadash general rule 87 din 14 in the name of Hagahot Maimoniot):
