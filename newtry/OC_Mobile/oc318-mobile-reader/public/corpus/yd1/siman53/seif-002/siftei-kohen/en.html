And there are those who forbid, etc. Bach wrote: Mahariik root 38 wrote that if it healed well, fragment to fragment adhering — kosher even near connection to body; but this is not in Mahariik; rather Mahariik deals there where the prohibition is from a limb that went outside, such as where it makes treifah or in a bird's wing; and regarding that limb itself forbidden from concern it went outside as a dangling limb; and when rejoined — kosher, as below siman 55 seif 5 and 12, because if it had gone outside it would not rejoin fragment to fragment; and so is implied in responsa Masat Binyamin siman 40 that Mahariik deals in such a case; but in a broken wing near the body that we declare treifah from concern for lung perforation — what does healing fragment to fragment help; and it is like below siman 56 s.k. 12 regarding break at tendon junction even if rejoined — treifah, see there; and so I proposed this before the Gaon may his memory be blessed and before several halachic authorities and they debated and agreed with my words; however Maharshal at end of Perek HaMekasheh siman 16 wrote: in breaking a wing near the body, which we practice to declare treifah from concern for lung perforation — wherever it healed and fragments adhered and no defect is seen in ribs — kosher, for it is impossible that in that time of healing no change occurred in ribs, for easily recognizable, end of his words — these are only prophetic words to be lenient; perhaps he too understood Mahariik's words as Bach brought Mahariik's opening words; nevertheless one may side and be lenient since in any case most poskim permit in a break; and regarding a break far from the body where the limb itself is forbidden if cooked thus — see above siman 55 s.k. 13:

Tur HaChadash wrote there end of din 13: and furthermore it appears there is no distinction whether there is a spur on the broken bone or not; and even though in responsa Ashir"i it is written: if there is a spur on the break one must inspect the lung lest it entered inside and perforated — this is specifically if near the body; but far from the body there is no concern; and since the custom is to declare treifah at finger-width in every case, and beyond that they practice to permit — called far — one need not afterward be concerned for lung perforation, so it appears to me, end of his words; and in Perishah it is written: and it is simple that if the spur entered within a thumb near the body, even though the break is more than a thumb from the body — treifah, and requires further study, end of his words; and from Tur HaChadash's words above it does not imply thus; and also difficult: what of it that the spur entered within a thumb — after all the place of breaking is far a thumb from the body; and it appears Perishah means: even though the main break is more than a thumb, i.e., the middle of the break such as broken diagonally when measuring from where the main break began, i.e., from the middle of the break there is far a thumb; nevertheless since the spur entered within a thumb — treifah; and so is implied in Taz that in such a case there must be a thumb from where the break began toward the body; and in any case this law of broken wing is mere stringency; give it to them not to add; and if on the broken bone something remains connected to the body as thumb-measure, even if not from the outer bone that connects a thumb — since the break was made far a thumb — kosher; and it appears we measure the thumb apart from what is stuck in the body like a peg in a hole that does not join; and so is implied in the Rav's language and other acharonim:
