And likewise in a bird: if its wings were cut or broke — kosher, even if they broke near its connection to the body; provided the outer bone fragment does not have a spur, for if it had a spur one inspects the lung. {Rama: And there are those who forbid if it broke near the body, and say its law is like dislocated (Hagahot Mei'iri); and the simple custom is to measure the width of a thumb even in narrow measure — kosher (Agur and Agudah and so Shach writes is the custom), if one does not see blood coagulated across the ribs; but one need not be concerned for this and permits anonymously (Mahariik root 38 and Terumat HaDeshen siman 190); and there is no distinction whether there is a spur on the break or not, since it is far from the body; but if there is not the width of a thumb between the place of the break and the body — then it is treifah in every case, and lung inspection does not help, for we are not expert in inspection (Shulchan De'ah and Issur VeHeter general rule 50 din 7 and Maharil and Agur) wherever there is a defect, and such is the custom.}
