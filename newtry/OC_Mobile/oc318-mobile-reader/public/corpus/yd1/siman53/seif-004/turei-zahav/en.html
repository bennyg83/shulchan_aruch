This is not a complete double doubt, for this doubt whether not perforated depends on our lack of expertise in inspection — so Mordechai and Agudah in the name of Hagahot Gedolot, and Maharshal bring them; and also Mahariik siman 35 did not count this as double doubt; if so, Rama was stringent here in slipped, since it is explicit in the Talmud as forbidden without inspection — we do not rely on this double doubt; and in siman 110 Rama wrote one law in break and slipped that in both it is permitted from double doubt; and it must be said below he wrote from what appears to him according to the law, and here he wrote from stringency, as his language here implies: one should be stringent and forbid; and so Maharshal explained Rama's words; and Maharshal ruled one should be stringent also in a break — doubt when it occurred:
One should be stringent and forbid. Maharshal wrote: if he has something to hang on that it was done after slaughter — we hang on it; and he brings proof from Ran at beginning of Chullin who concluded his words: the halacha is wherever there is room to hang for permission, such as they trampled the wing or it convulsed and was pressed to the wall and there is reason to say perhaps its wing slipped from that — we hang leniently, end of Ran; and Maharshal concluded: trampled or pressed to wall, etc., end of his words.
