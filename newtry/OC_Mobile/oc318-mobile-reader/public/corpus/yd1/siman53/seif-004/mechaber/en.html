If a wing is found broken and it is not known whether it broke before slaughter or afterward — treifah. {Rama: And there are those who permit from the force of double doubt — perhaps it broke after slaughter; and if you will say before slaughter — perhaps the lung was not perforated (Issur VeHeter HaAruch); and one may rely on this to permit in a break, for there are those who permit in any case (private opinion); but when it is found dislocated and we do not know whether before or after slaughter — there is room to be stringent and forbid; and see below end of siman 110.}
