Positive commandment, etc. Mishnah Menachot 28a; and see there 31b.
And every, etc. — as written in chapter 1 of Kiddushin (34a).
And the days of his sons, etc. — as written in chapter 2 of Shabbat (32b).
Nevertheless, etc. Yerushalmi end of Megillah — "tefillin and mezuzah — which precedes? Shmuel said mezuzah precedes; Rav Huna said tefillin precedes. What is Shmuel's reason? For so it is practiced on festivals and Sabbaths. What is R. Huna's reason? For so it is practiced by seafarers and desert travelers." Mishnah supports Shmuel — "worn-out tefillin one makes them into a mezuzah; worn-out mezuzah one does not make them into tefillin — why? We elevate in sanctity and do not lower." Rosh ruled like R. Huna, that bodily mitzvah takes precedence; and his words are astonishing, for this reason was not mentioned at all there (and see in Chiddushei Ra'ak). And he decided from his own view — one must say because that baraita was stated in our Gemara (Shabbat 79b and Menachot) to the contrary, and it supports R. Huna; and Orach Chaim siman 38 seif 12.
