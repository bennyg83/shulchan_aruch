The reward of the mitzvah of mezuzah. In it are 2 seifim. It is a positive commandment to write the parashah of Shema and "Ve'hayah im shamo'a" and affix them on the doorpost of the entrance; and one must be very careful with it. And whoever is careful with it—his days and his children's days are lengthened; and if he is not careful with it, they are shortened.
{Rama: Nevertheless, if his hand cannot afford to buy tefillin and a mezuzah, he should buy tefillin and not a mezuzah (Yerushalmi end of Megillah and Orach Chaim siman 38, seif 12), for a mitzvah that is an obligation of the body takes precedence.}
