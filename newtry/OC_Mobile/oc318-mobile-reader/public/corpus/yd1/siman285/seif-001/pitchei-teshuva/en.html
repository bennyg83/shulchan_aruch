Positive commandment to write. It appears one does not compel for this positive commandment, for it is a positive commandment whose reward is alongside it, as it is written "and you shall write them… that your days may be multiplied"; and see Netziv above siman 245 seif 4 where I elaborated on this. And see Pri Megadim Orach Chaim siman 31 — if on Shabbat or festival it became known to him there is no mezuzah on his doorway and he has another house — he is obligated to leave; but if he has no other house, it is permitted to dwell within, similar to tzitzit in siman 13 — see there. And it appears the same even on weekdays — if no mezuzah is found in the city, his law is like on Shabbat, and he is permitted to dwell within if he has no other house, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 13 s.k. 8 regarding tzitzit; and see Magen Avraham siman 126 s.k. 1 — understand well.
