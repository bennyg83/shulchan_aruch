Whoever is careful with it. Tur adds that the house is protected through the mezuzah. Beit Yosef calls this especially great because it is an open miracle and reverses the ordinary order, with God guarding from outside. It appears further that one who constantly remembers God's unity through the mezuzah merits long life for himself and his children. Even while a person sleeps and is not actively engaged in the mitzvah, the mezuzah protects the house. This is greater than the ordinary rule that a mitzvah protects only while one is engaged in it.
