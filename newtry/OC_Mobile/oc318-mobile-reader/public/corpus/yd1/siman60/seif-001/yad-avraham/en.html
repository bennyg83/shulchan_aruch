(Siman 60 seif 1 in the hagahah) Animal that was fattened on forbidden things is permitted, etc. Peri Chadash wrote: regarding prohibitions of eating it is permitted l'chatchila to fatten; and regarding prohibitions of benefit too one need not forbid unless it was fattened all its days on them. And even this is permitted according to the view that this-and-that together permits, even when there is not one matter in the issue. And the same here: there is a body of permitted and eating of prohibition. And therefore one may permit eating milk from animals that eat chametz on Pesach. Only on Pesach, since we say "leavening of leavening" they forbade this-and-that together only what was milked before Pesach is permitted.
