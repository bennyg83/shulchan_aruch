Blood. Meaning that blood seized it and it became ill; and Rambam in his explanation of the mishnah explained that blood overcame it until it choked. Smoked — Rambam explained that black bile overcame it. Chilled — that white phlegm overcame it until its senses were nullified; and he wrote that they did not mention overcoming of red bile, because it is very rare in animals, as explained in books of animal medicine, end of his words.
Bad. Meaning: exposed water.
Its days. Shach wrote in the name of Tur HaShalem: l'chatchila it is forbidden to buy an animal from a gentile that nursed from impure milk; and it implies there that even if it did not nurse all its days, it is forbidden to buy l'chatchila; and therefore all the more forbidden l'chatchila to feed it forbidden things; and see below siman 142 seif 11. But Mordechai's view is that it is permitted to feed it forbidden things, and even if it was fattened all its days — also permitted, for it is not comparable to idolatrous beans, which are prohibitions of benefit — and requires study (and in Beit Yosef siman 1 he wrote: an animal that ate a forbidden thing — l'chatchila forbidden to slaughter until it waits me'eit le'eit — see there, and in Shach and Peri Chadash. And Levushei Prad wrote that it is permitted to fatten birds on flour that became wormy, called mehlbein — see siman 84).
