Seized by blood — that boiling blood seized it and red bile overcame it. Smoked — that smoke rose in its body and thereby black bile overcame it — so appears from Rashi and Rambam.
Bad water — exposed water; and this does not apply in our time.
Fattened all its days — and Shach's view, and so Peri Chadash: specifically fattened on prohibitions of benefit and not prohibitions of eating; and see Peleti, whose view is to permit even on prohibitions of benefit, except idolatrous beans and the like — see there where I brought proof to permit.
