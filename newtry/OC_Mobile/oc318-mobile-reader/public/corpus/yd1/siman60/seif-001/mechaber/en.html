Laws regarding an animal that ate deadly poison. In it are 3 seifim. One seized by blood, and one smoked (smoke entered its body), and one chilled (from cold illness), or one that ate deadly poison that kills the animal, or that drank bad water — behold it is permitted. {Rama: An animal that was fattened on forbidden things is permitted (Mordechai Yevamot chapter HaAlmanah leKohen Gadol); but if it was not fattened all its days only on forbidden things — forbidden (Tosafot Temurah chapter Kol HaAsurim).}
