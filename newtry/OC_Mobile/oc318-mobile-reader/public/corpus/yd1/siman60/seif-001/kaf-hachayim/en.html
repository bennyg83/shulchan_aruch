(1) [Seif 1] Seized by blood — that blood seized it and it became ill. Rashi and Ran. And Rambam in his explanation of the mishnah explained that blood overcame it until it choked. Shach s.k. 1. And an incident came before me in one bird where blood overcame it until all its intestines inside were very red with blood, and especially near its throat below the esophagus; and I found all its intestines whole and clean from any defect, and I declared it kosher. Radak in his hagahot manuscript Berakhot in Zivchei Tzedek ot 1 ot 2; and therefore if such is found, one must examine the intestines whether they are whole and clean.
(2) There. And smoked — that smoke entered its body. Rashi and Ran. And Rambam explained that black bile overcame it. Shach s.k. 2. And so the acharonim.
(3) There. And chilled — illness from cold. Rashi and Ran. And Rambam explained that white phlegm overcame it until its senses were nullified; and he wrote that they did not mention overcoming of red bile, because it is very rare in animals, as written in books of animal medicine. Shach s.k. 3. And so the acharonim. And there is no practical difference in this — whatever has no visible effect in its body is kosher. Shulchan Aruch siman 84 ot 1; Binyan Olam in Shulchan Aruch siman 87 ot 1.
