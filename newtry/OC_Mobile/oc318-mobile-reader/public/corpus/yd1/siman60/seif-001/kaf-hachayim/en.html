(1) [Seif 1] Seized by blood — that blood seized it and it became ill. Rashi and Ran. And Rambam in his explanation of the mishnah explained that blood overcame it until it choked. Shach s.k. 1. And an incident came before me in one bird where blood overcame it until all its intestines inside were very red with blood, and especially near its throat below the esophagus; and I found all its intestines whole and clean from any defect, and I declared it kosher. Radak in his hagahot manuscript Berakhot in Zivchei Tzedek ot 1 ot 2; and therefore if such is found, one must examine the intestines whether they are whole and clean.
