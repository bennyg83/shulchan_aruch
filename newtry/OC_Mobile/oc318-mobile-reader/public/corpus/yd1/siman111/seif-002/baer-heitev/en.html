"But." The Shach wrote that this deals only with two pots; but with two pieces and one pot, even if the issur pieces are greater than the pot it is permitted. And one may say: with one piece and two pots, where is this found that the issur is greater than the heter? If we know the issur is the majority we examine the pot of heter — one may say it is found well when the issur was removed beforehand (and in Nachalat Binyamin he resolved another matter, see there). However, the Raah disagrees and holds that even with two pots it is permitted even if the issur is greater than the heter, because of a d'rabbanan doubt to be lenient, see there, end of his words (and the Taz reconciles another matter).
