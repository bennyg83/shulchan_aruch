"If the heter was, etc." Yevamot there, and Rabbi Yochanan's view, etc., and Rabbi Yehoshua even though, etc.
"How so, etc." Even though it is d'oraisa, since the measure is d'rabbanan, as written in chapter 3 of Pesachim and chapter 6 of Nazir — it challenged him with two spice-measures, etc.; and if you say, etc., it challenged him, etc. — thus it is clear that it deals with terumah that is d'oraisa, and nevertheless if an olive's bulk is in the measure of eating a peras it is acceptable.
"That it is d'oraisa, etc." Chapter ha'Te'arovot (88b, 89a).
"Rather, etc." Pesachim (97b).
"If there were two, etc." Tosafot chapter 3 of Pesachim there, s.v. it challenged him, etc., and Or Zarua, etc. — thus in species not in its species a majority does not help when it is forbidden d'oraisa; and we establish that species not in its species is by taste d'oraisa, as written in chapter ha'Te'arovot (there); and the general matter is that we suspend only where it is permitted d'oraisa.
