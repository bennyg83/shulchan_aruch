If there were two pots of heter and issur fell into one of them and it is known into which it fell, and again issur fell into one of them and it is not known into which — I say that to the place of the first issur the second also fell; but if issur fell into one of them and it is not known into which, and afterward issur fell into one of them and it is known into which — both are forbidden: {Rama: And specifically when there is not sixty to nullify the issur that fell there; but if there was sixty to nullify the issur that fell there it is as if no issur fell there at all (Beit Yosef in name of Rashba).}
