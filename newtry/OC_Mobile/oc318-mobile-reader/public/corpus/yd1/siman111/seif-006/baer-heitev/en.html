"The second." The Bach wrote that here it teaches that even for d'oraisa issur it is permitted; and the Taz and Shach challenged him, for this is a great principle that we do not say "I say" for d'oraisa issur.
"Forbidden." And we do not say that to the place where the last issur fell the first also fell; rather its law is as if issur fell and it is not known where it fell. Shach.
