I say to the place of the first issur, etc. The Bach challenged: what does it teach? Is it not all the more so, for above, seif 1, with two pots, one heter and one issur, and one issur piece — we suspend that issur fell into issur, and all the more so here that we can say to the place where the first issur fell the second also fell? He resolved that here it teaches that even for d'oraisa issur we suspend to be lenient to say that to the place where the first issur fell the second also fell; and so in Issur VeHeter, general rule 26, seif 13, that all that is written in seif 5 and seif 6 — there is no distinction between d'rabbanan and d'oraisa issur, end of his words. I wonder: how can one say \"I say\" for d'oraisa? Behold an arranged Talmud, chapter 1 of Pesachim, daf 10, and all poskim agree we do not say \"I say\" for d'oraisa — and what of that issur fell first there? Every d'oraisa doubt is stringent. And further: why say in seif 4 for d'oraisa issur that fell where there is enough to nullify? I examined Issur VeHeter there and saw he did not write as the Bach, only after bringing seif 5 and seif 6 he wrote: it appears that in all this there is no distinction whether the issur is d'oraisa or d'rabbanan, end of his words. It appears he comes to say: it should not be, since the Rashba and those who follow him did not write that d'oraisa issur and d'rabbanan measure are like d'rabbanan issur, only with two pots of which one is issur — if so, specifically when one pot was already established as issur we say there is no distinction between d'rabbanan and d'oraisa issur and d'rabbanan measure such as species in its species by majority; but here the pots are heter — thus one should say specifically for issur that is purely d'rabbanan we suspend to be lenient, but not for d'oraisa issur and d'rabbanan measure; it teaches that this is not so — in all this there is no distinction between d'oraisa issur and d'rabbanan measure or d'rabbanan issur; and with this the Bach's challenge is resolved — why it should teach here that even though the pot was already established heter, only now at falling it deteriorated, nevertheless we suspend to be lenient; but for actual d'oraisa issur certainly we do not suspend to be lenient:
