"If there were here, etc." There and Yerushalmi chapter 4: Two baskets - in this forty se'ah and in that forty se'ah; a se'ah of terumah fell into one of them and it is known, etc., and afterward a second fell and it is not known into which - I can suspend and say to the place where the first fell, there the second fell. A se'ah of terumah fell into one of them and it is not known into which, and afterward a second fell and it is known into which - he cannot suspend and say to the place where the second fell, there the first fell. And likewise two mikvaot that do not have forty se'ah; three log fell into one of them and it is known into which they fell, and afterward two fell and it is not known into which - I can suspend and say to the place where the first fell, there the second fell. Three log fell into one of them and it is not known into which, and afterward two fell and it is known into which - he cannot suspend and say to the place where the second fell, there the first fell. And it deals with Rabbi Eliezer, seif 68, side 2, side 3.
"And specifically, etc." As above — we suspend only where it does not forbid, as written in that Tosefta and there chapter 6: And if it was tevel, etc. — they did not permit doubt, etc., as above; or where it is already forbidden, as written in chapter 6 there: One is mixed and one is not mixed — I say it fell into the mixed one; it specified that it is mixed. And likewise in Mikvaot there: One is drawn and one is not drawn — I say they fell into the drawn one; and many baraitot in chapter 6 of Terumot there.
