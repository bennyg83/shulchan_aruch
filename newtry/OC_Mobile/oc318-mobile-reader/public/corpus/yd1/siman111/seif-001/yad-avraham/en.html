(Siman 111, Taz, s.k. 6) The Taz wrote that even for d'oraisa issur we suspend to be lenient one after the other. He brought proof from chapter 7 of Terumot: two baskets, one hullin and one terumah, and it is unknown which; if one ate from one he is exempt; another came and ate the second — exempt; and the Yerushalmi compares this to two paths one after the other; and he left in doubt the Rashba who forbids even with one falling one after the other. Likewise he challenged in Orach Chaim siman 439 on the Tur regarding a mouse that entered one of two houses and it is unknown which — he requires bedikah one after the other. In siman 69 of Hilchot Niddah, siman 129, he wrote on this that it is not necessary. It appears to me as the Magen Avraham wrote in chapter 19 of Hilchot She'ar Avot HaTumah: where doubt comes first whether both come from one cause — we are lenient one after the other; and so with chametz and falling of issur, unlike terumah and two paths. But what the Peri Megadim in Orach Chaim wrote to reject the Taz's proof from terumah — that it deals with terumah in the present era or terumat perot that is d'rabbanan — does not appear so, for Rabbi Yochanan establishes it in Yerushalmi according to Rabbi Yosi, who holds in Yevamot that terumah in the present era is d'oraisa; and also it does not deal with perot, as there it teaches the dispute of Rabbi Meir and Rabbi Yehudah on challah that applies only to the five species. And the Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilchot Terumot, wrote the reason they are exempt: he removes from his fellow — it appears also for d'oraisa terumah, only that one should not learn from this to chametz, as the Magen Avraham wrote. However, in the Raavad's hasagot, chapter 2 of Hilchot Chametz, it is explained as the Taz wrote.
