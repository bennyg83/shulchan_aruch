"If the piece, etc." As written regarding two spice-measures in chapter 3 of Pesachim (44a), and chapter 6 of Nazir regarding terumat tavlin that is d'rabbanan, and regarding two baskets there and there, and chapter 1 of Pesachim regarding terumah in the present era that is d'rabbanan.
"For example, its fat, etc." Chapter gid hanasheh, 92b.
"And even if, etc." Chapter ha'Areil (2a), according to Rabbi Yochanan.
"And the same law if, etc." Tosefta end of chapter 8 of Terumot, and there all three cases: Before him were two pots, one of terumah and one of hullin, and before him one spice-measure of terumah; he put it into one of them and it is not known into which he put it — I say he put it into the terumah. Before him were two spice-measures, one of hullin and one of terumah, and before him one pot of hullin; he put from one of them and it is not known from which he put — I say from the hullin. Before him were two pots, one of hullin and one of terumah, and before him two spice-measures, one of hullin and one of terumah; he put from both and does not know whether from this into that or from that into this — I say terumah into terumah and hullin into hullin. And if it was tevel and first tithe and second tithe — it is forbidden, for they permitted doubt of dimu'a only in something that has a permitted aspect; meaning, specifically we suspend terumah into terumah; but if one was tevel and one hullin we do not suspend that terumah fell into tevel, for we suspend only where it does not forbid at all.
"Or if, etc." Mishnah chapter 4 of Terumot and that Tosefta and in chapter 6 there.
