(Seif 5) But an old spoon and a ben yomo — we estimate against all of it, for all that is absorbed in it becomes nevelah. This is the view of the Ramban, that there is one who says, whom the Mechaber brought, that we do not say chein nafsho in what is absorbed; and the Tur wrote in these words. But an old spoon that was stirred in several times — one does not know how much was absorbed, and we estimate against all of it. The Beit Yosef wrote that the Tur wrote stam in accordance with the Rashba, who said that we apply chein nafsho in what is absorbed. The Derishah wrote that this is incorrect, for in siman 107 the Tur explicitly wrote like the Ramban. He means that since the Tur wrote there regarding a fly that was removed from the pot with a spoon, that one need estimate only against the fly, since it is known how much issur was absorbed in it — evidently he holds that what is absorbed in the spoon does not become nevelah. His words are astounding: what proof does he bring from this? For the Tur holds in siman 92, at the end of seif 106, and in other places, that we do not say chein nafsho at all in other issurim. What proof is there from a fly to basar bechalav? Tzarich iyun. I again saw that the Acharonim noticed this, as brought in the Minchat Yitzchak in general rule 91.
