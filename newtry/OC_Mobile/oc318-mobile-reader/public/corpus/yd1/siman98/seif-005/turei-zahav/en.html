If known, etc. Before we explain these matters we explain there is a distinction in explanation of Tur's words between Beit Yosef and Rama — Tur wrote: if the amount of the prohibition is known, such as a new spoon shaken in it with olive-volume of milk and afterward that day shaken in meat pot — one needs only sixty to nullify the olive-volume absorbed; but old spoon shaken many times — we do not know how much absorbed and estimate against all of it — end of Tur's language; Turei Chayim siman 183 explained Tur's words that he holds a vessel does not become nevelah at all; what Tur wrote "but old spoon," etc. — explained that today shaken many times in meat and inserted into milk within twenty-four hours requires sixty against all of it; Tur means if new for all suffices against prohibition, if old for all needs against all of it — unless already prohibition in spoon because permitted was in it and absorbed prohibition olive-volume and inserted into permitted — on this he relied on what will be written siman 106 there is dispute — so Turei Chayim appears explained Tur's words; Beit Yosef explained another way and this is the essence of his explanation even though not his exact language — Tur holds like first view of Rashba Beit Yosef brings unlike Ramban — that even in absorbed in vessel we say piece becomes nevelah; found Tur means specifically if spoon new and absorbed olive-volume milk and not specifically new but also old spoon already used before within twenty-four hours much meat and today absorbed olive-volume milk and still called new since not ben yomo considered as if new since its taste does not forbid — therefore chein nafsho does not apply, for at time of milk absorption it was permitted — if inserted afterward into meat needs only sixty against milk absorption only. But if old spoon — explained on day one used much meat and afterward absorbed olive-volume milk — since at absorption time there was meat-in-milk prohibition in it, all absorbed in it i.e. meat absorption becomes prohibition because piece becomes nevelah; if returned and inserted into permitted meat — since we do not know how much meat absorption was at first — therefore estimate against entire spoon since all its absorption became prohibition through piece itself nevelah; and some say from Tur's words siman 107 regarding removing fly need only sixty against fly not against spoon — specifically here in meat in milk we say piece itself nevelah unlike other prohibitions Tur holds like R' Efraim siman 92; on this Shulchan Aruch wrote here if spoon new or not ben yomo — meaning old meat spoon only today absorbed olive-volume milk — chein nafsho does not apply as above since no prohibition here; Rama wrote according to his reasoning of Shulchan Aruch we do not say piece becomes nevelah regarding vessel even absorbed prohibition — absorbed prohibition is not here; therefore cannot say on vessel body it becomes nevelah like other forbidden piece; meaning not only what Shulchan Aruch wrote new or old spoon, etc. — since still permitted chein nafsho does not apply; but even if absorbed prohibition such as nevelah meat olive-volume — nevertheless we do not say the vessel itself which is new becomes all nevelah — this according to all; but in latter part dealing with old spoon ben yomo — spoon is of meat and used today much meat and also absorbed olive-volume milk — automatically prohibition absorbed and same day inserted into meat — all absorbed in spoon becomes nevelah; Rama concludes all pot absorbed — even meat taste before milk absorption nevelah — automatically must estimate against entire pot since we do not know how much prohibition is in it. And some say Shulchan Aruch brings view of Ran in name of Ramban that even meat spoon used today and afterward absorbed olive-volume milk same day and inserted into meat — does not need sixty in meat only against milk for we do not say absorbed in vessel piece becomes nevelah — a fortiori vessel body; and what was noted in source reference on hagahah "we do not say regarding vessel," etc. — that copier erred and did not understand hagahah at all — hagahah does not deal with absorbed nevelah only vessel body we do not say so as I wrote; Rama properly concludes first reasoning is primary that in absorbed we say nevelah if prohibition — for regarding drop milk on pot explained all not opposite gravy forbidden from pot — plainly we say absorbed piece becomes nevelah; and in truth this is not proof for there too there is permitted as written siman 92 seif 4 and so Issur VeHeter general rule 24 and afterward Rama brings "some do not distinguish," etc. — Mordechai's view who wrote Pesachim chapter 7 whatever can separate absorbed prohibition from piece we do not say piece itself nevelah — therefore in wood and metal vessels possible through hagalah and libun if prohibition fell in one place we do not say entire vessel becomes nevelah to increase prohibition and estimate entire vessel if cooked in that permitted vessel — estimate according to prohibition that fell where clear to us how much fell; if unknown must estimate against all from doubt — end of his words; from his words Mordechai deals only earthenware impossible to separate absorbed prohibition through hagalah and libun unless return to kiln called new face — even if we know how much absorbed such as new spoon — nevertheless since absorbed prohibition earthenware body becomes nevelah like ordinary piece; Mordechai for his reason holds in all prohibitions we say piece itself nevelah — what difference piece body became prohibition from absorbed prohibition and earthenware body since became prohibition is nevelah. It appears clear to me Mordechai deals only whether need sixty even against earthenware itself or metal itself — absorption of prohibition absorbed within twenty-four hours is known — in metal we say no need to nullify body of earthenware since can separate prohibition from there; in earthenware need sixty even against earthenware; but old vessel with absorbed permitted within twenty-four hours and absorbed olive-volume prohibition and no sixty in absorbed permitted — certainly absorbed becomes nevelah even metal vessel for even through hagalah no difference between permitted and prohibition absorption; so Turei Chayim general rule 85 — and these are his words: however perhaps Mordechai deals with new vessel, etc. — end; even Rama wrote here doubtful language nevertheless appears clear it is so; found according to this no leniency in Mordechai's words here on first view but additional stringency in new earthenware vessel we say vessel becomes nevelah even though know how much prohibition fell there; or old vessel not ben yomo at prohibition absorption; but from Rama's language "some do not distinguish," etc. only between earthenware and other vessels — implies somewhat in other vessels leniency that even old vessel metal not nevelah even absorbed — from stam language no distinction old and new; for halachah no practical difference Rama concludes good to be concerned stringency — we say concern Mordechai's stringency; Turei Chayim general rule 51 wrote — and these are his words: where loss one may be lenient in all prohibitions except meat in milk for majority poskim agreed we do not say piece itself nevelah except meat in milk; and though we are stringent — nevertheless in vessel no stringency — so it appears to me — end; Rashal chapter Gid HaNasheh siman 37 does not distinguish meat-in-milk prohibition from other prohibitions but concludes both require even earthenware vessel no stringency to estimate against all except if used that day with permitted matter; but if not used that day with permitted even earthenware need not estimate only against prohibition absorption; also wrote there to resolve where we say in vessel even permitted absorbed already became prohibition — do not challenge from what we hold generally absorbed prohibition does not forbid absorbed permitted — there drop of prohibition fell and there is permitted gravy in pot with enough to nullify prohibition alone not enough to nullify prohibition and absorbed permitted together — there we say absorbed prohibition does not forbid absorbed permitted; but without falling drop but vessel absorbed prohibition — even permitted absorbed already became prohibition and need sixty against all in earthenware vessel — end; in my humble opinion according to Mordechai one should be stringent earthenware even new to estimate against all;
