To nullify the swallowed olive — for everything in the pot joins and combines with what the pot swallowed, and chein nafsho does not apply.
Regarding vessel chein nafsho — that is the body of the vessel and not the swallowed matter.
What was swallowed etc. — for he holds chein nafsho applies to swallowed matter.
One needs only sixty — for we do not say chein nafsho on swallowed matter.
Earthenware vessel — Taz's view that the earthenware body becomes nevelah; but not so the view of other Acharonim, which depends on whether we say chein nafsho in half a piece or not, as Maharach wrote; Shach — all deals with swallowed matter — like the Acharonim.
