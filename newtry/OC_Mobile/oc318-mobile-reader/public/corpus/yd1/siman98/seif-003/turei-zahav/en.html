And impossible to determine its measure, etc. In Mordechai chapter HaSocher he gave taste that the opinion of fools counts for nothing; and so in Sh"D on this; and similar Beit Yosef wrote below in siman 129 in the law of a laundered shirt on which a stain was found in the name of Rashba — and these are his words: this is not a rabbinic double doubt, for doubt coming from lack of recognition is not doubt — for if this one is not expert another can recognize — that is, possible to establish the matter — end of his words. One may challenge from what Tur wrote siman 81 to permit cheeses from treifot through sircha because if we were expert to clarify possibly they would have permission through examination — if so it would be double doubt, etc.; plainly lack of expertise is called doubt regarding Torah prohibition requiring double doubt; and here we do not consider it doubt at all where one doubt suffices since it is rabbinic. One must say specifically here where there is lack of expertise for one who is not expert, but for others certainly this can be clarified — specifically this is not called doubt at all; unlike sircha where there the whole generation are not expert in this; but it is difficult why Tur brought here "therefore udder," etc., we go to stringency because impossible for us to determine the measure and this includes the whole generation. It appears to resolve: there are two sides in doubt of lack of expertise. One side: where there is doubt whether permitted or forbidden and no remedy to say how far the prohibition goes — if you do not taste it is fully forbidden — that is this case here if not expert to estimate; in this we say if the lack was for the whole generation we go to leniency since rabbinic doubt; but if the lack is for individuals this is not doubt. The second side: if the doubt is in what matter to estimate, for we have two measures — that of udder where doubt whether estimated in the udder itself or in what emerges from it — we cannot say even certainly there is not sixty against all of it there is doubt whether sixty against what emerges from it, for if so you could permit in every case — where will you find prohibition — we go to stringency even if lack for the whole world since in any case another measure emerges from doubt and certainly we are concerned for it and do not say rabbinic doubt to leniency since doubt is from lack of knowledge; found Tur says here we go to stringency if not expert to estimate since others know — therefore fully forbidden; therefore regarding udder where doubt between all of it or what emerges from it we also go to stringency and estimate against all of it since before us and impossible to estimate; but wherever doubt for whole world and doubt whether fully forbidden or permitted we go to leniency — that is cheeses case considered doubt and double doubt; thus also Beit Yosef's difficulty resolved why Tur wrote "therefore udder" and Rashba learned this law from udder — according to what I wrote it works, for already dispute in siman 81 between Rashba and Rosh — Rashba says even doubt for whole world from lack of expertise is like individual and not considered doubt and therefore cheeses forbidden and not considered double doubt — therefore properly derives this matter from udder even though udder was doubt for whole world and here doubt for individual — nevertheless equal for him; but Tur here follows this reasoning after his father Rosh that there is distinction between lack of expertise — he cannot learn law here from udder for they truly differ, only they wrote in language "therefore" meaning as with lack of expertise for individual we say here stringency, so too udder where lack of expertise of many we go to stringency regarding distinction of measure between all of it and what emerges — all works with heaven's help. Also siman 81 seif 2 I wrote similar that where dependent on lack of knowledge one should be stringent where possible to make remedy to exit doubt even though rabbinic — see there.
