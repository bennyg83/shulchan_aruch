To rely. Avodat HaTaharah in name of Shach; and see in Sefer Bechor Shor in his chiddushim to Chullin 96 who questions this, and his view that one should not be lenient because Rama z"l wrote above siman 42 seif 3 in the hagahah that even now one may rely on tasting liver, and he explained the reason in Darkei Moshe because many poskim permit with insufficient marah — plainly in another place one may not rely now on tasting even of a Jew — see there; and see Peri Megadim siman 42 there that his view is to distinguish: in tasting by licking with the tongue alone only an expert is expert; and on this Rama wrote one may rely since many poskim permit; but regarding tasting food, all are expert and an artisan is not needed, and therefore a Jew is believed — see there.
