(Seif 4, hagahah) But through the boiling of salted food, absorption in a vessel is only kedei kelipah, and one need estimate only against the peel. It appears that this is specifically the first time it was cooked therein; but once the issur was cooked there once without sixty, what was absorbed in that kedei kelipah throughout has gone, and all that was absorbed in it from what was used within twenty-four hours becomes nevelah, for according to us we say chein nafsho in what is absorbed, as in seif 5 and siman 92 seif 6. If so, in siman 69 seif 96 regarding salt in an unperforated vessel — it is likewise explained there that only kedei kelipah is forbidden, and one need estimate only against the peel — this deals with a case where it had not been heated before. But if it had been heated before, the nevelah of all the heter absorbed within twenty-four hours requires estimation against all of it. From the words of the Shach there, it does not appear that he distinguishes in this way. I again saw in the Peri Megadim that he left it as tzarich iyun. However, according to what I wrote in siman 105, that we do not say chein nafsho except in cooking; if so, one may say that heter absorbed as nevelah is not in the category of "absorbed," for in basar bechalav what is absorbed was not cooking when both the meat and the milk were absorbed, as in siman 87 and 94. See the Minchat Yitzchak at the beginning of general rule 56; and likewise as an explanation of Tosafot in Chullin (97b).
