If it became known etc. — even though in a place where we establish prohibitory presumption, even a rabbinic doubt for stringency — here it is not called prohibitory presumption, for until the issur fell in everything was permitted and the food itself is in presumption of permissibility, and you come to forbid it from doubt through falling — this is not within prohibitory presumption.
And regarding etc. — Shach challenged: specifically regarding species in its species that is not nullified even in the slightest; in Zevachim one should follow lest; in this Abaye and Rava disagree; but here which depends on noten taam — species in its species has no noten taam and in not its species there is noticeable taste — what nafka mina in the rule whether they sense issur taste — forbidden, and if not — permitted, for issur/heter does not depend on species and not its species only on sensing taste, for all agree from maseres d'oraisa; and see what Peleti wrote — Rama seems to have proof from the Gemara and Rambam's words seem so too; but I raised — nevertheless for practical halachah like Shach; and I also clarified what is called its species and not its species — see there; since for shila and other things forbidden in the slightest it depends on species and not species — follow lest.
In not its species — and if difficulty, let an expert taste — according to Rama that name change even when equal in taste is called not its species, for kamma mamar one cannot taste; but according to Shach it is difficult and one must say it fell into vegetables, an olive of nevelah meat and an olive of slaughtered meat, and it spilled and is unknown whether the vegetables were sixty combined with the olive of slaughtered meat and the olive of nevelah was nullified or not — and this is impossible to clarify, for even if one senses meat taste, nevertheless perhaps it was noten taam of vegetables and olive of slaughtered meat and there is sixty, and nevertheless one senses taste from slaughtered olive. And likewise according to him who holds if it can be squeezed it is forbidden even in other issurim — if so one may say before it spilled much heter was added until it is impossible to taste and sense the issur and there is no issur taste here; nevertheless if l'chatchila there was less than sixty, what of it if it increased — if it can be squeezed, forbidden.
