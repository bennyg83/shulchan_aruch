"We estimate in sixty." See in responsa Shaarei Efrayim siman 91 and responsa Avodat HaGereshuni siman 34 that they estimate by measure and not by weight — see there; and so wrote in responsa Rashbash siman 299. And see in responsa Chinuch Beit Yehudah siman 33 that his view: if issur and heter are equal in weight — that is, of one species where one is not naturally heavier than its fellow, and there is no hollow or cavity greater than its fellow — plainly we may estimate in both by measurement or weight; however if one portion has a cavity greater than its fellow, then if estimated by measurement one must compress the cavity — we say weighing is preferable, lest perhaps the cavity was not compressed well; and in two species it is impossible to estimate by weight, and even if there is a cavity one must compress the cavity — see there. And see in responsa Beit Efrayim ch. 14 siman 36 who wrote: a dried olive that fell into a dish, such as an olive of fat that fell into a dairy dish or an olive of butter that fell into a meat dish — even though immediately when it fell into the dish it melted and became a revi'it, nevertheless one does not need sixty revi'iot because it melted, only sixty olives suffice, for even though its measure increased, nevertheless its taste that was initially remained; and possible that if it melted first and fell after melting, one needs sixty revi'iot against it rabbinically, since the issur was recognizable by itself at that measure — see there where he expanded. And see in responsa Pe'er HaDor to Rambam siman 65: he was asked regarding issurim that the Sages said are nullified in sixty, if permitted to teach so to am ha'aretz or not, and he answered certainly permitted to teach so even to am ha'aretz, and we do not decree lest they breach, because a matter where issur mixed in heter from Torah law is nullified one in two, etc. — say now: since one in sixty is a decree, shall we stand and decree a decree upon a decree — end of his words — see there. And one must say according to his view he holds ta'am ke'ikar is not d'oraisa, as Shach wrote s.k. 2; and see in Beit Yosef; and accordingly for us who hold ta'am ke'ikar d'oraisa, if issur mixed in heter not its species — forbidden to teach so to am ha'aretz that it nullifies in sixty — requires study, for we never heard this; and see in Shach below siman 165 s.k. 22.
