To stand on its measure. See in responsa Emunat Shmuel siman 21 who wrote: a minor or gentile who put blood in a meat pot and it is known that it was nullified in the majority, only doubt whether there is sixty — since blood that was cooked is rabbinic, a doubt such as this that is not incumbent upon him to know is literally like nishpach that we rely on leniency, and one cannot say this is the view of fools, for behold he is not a prophet to know how much fell; and likewise Ran's taste (brought in Be'er Heitev s.k. 10) since it is an uncommon matter, certainly its law is like nishpach; nevertheless he did not decide the matter except for halachah and not for practice; and Peri Chadash brought it and brought proof for his words — see there (and thereby resolved Shach's words above siman 14 s.k. 12; and see in Shach siman 81 s.k. 8 — investigate); and see Peri Megadim who wrote that even though there is a gentile, one need not clarify to have an expert taste, since we are not accustomed to rely at all on a gentile — it is as impossible to clarify — see there.
