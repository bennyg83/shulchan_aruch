Even in a thousand etc. — and Issur VeHeter wrote that it is rabbinic; and see what Peleti wrote and I raised: if they are not famous spices but only spices that season the pot and the like — then from Torah in sixty and rabbinically they were stringent; but full spices such as pepper and cinnamon and the like — however much one senses taste, from Torah it is not nullified.
They are not nullified — see what Peleti wrote and I brought the view of Issur VeHeter to distinguish: if it gives taste in that pot — then not nullified; but if it is not noten taam even though in another pot it gives taste — nullified; and see what Peleti wrote — if it is customary to put — that is species in its species and they decreed; and if it is not customary — they did not decree — see there.
