(Seif 8, hagahah) Therefore salt and spices, etc. In Sefer Yad HaMelekh: that spices are nullified deals with she'eino mino, when the taste is not sensed, as the Shach wrote; but min b'mino does not season and does not leaven, and nullification does not apply at all. See the Peri Chadash, who expanded on this and concluded that everything depends on whether there is enough to season: in she'eino mino it is not nullified, but in mino it is. See Sefer R' Yosef in chapter 3, where he expanded on this. See also in Sefer Beit Yisrael in the laws of challah, where he wrote that sourdough in dough is nullified in 101, and he did not say that it is not nullified in a thousand. He also ruled there leniently regarding a mixture of challat chutz la'aretz, which is d'rabbanan, in rov, according to the Mechaber.
