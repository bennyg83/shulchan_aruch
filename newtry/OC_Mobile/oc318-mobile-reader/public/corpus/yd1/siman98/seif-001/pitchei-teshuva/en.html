"They shall taste it for us" by a gentile. Avodat HaTaharah in name of Taz; and see above siman 42 in Be'er Heitev s.k. 3 who wrote in name of Shach that therefore regarding marah it is permitted to taste, for it is likely he will taste bitter taste — see there; it appears that if not for this, even tasting with the tongue would be forbidden; and so too Peri Megadim siman 95 in Mishbetzot Zahav s.k. 15 that so we hold, and forbidden to lick with the tongue if salted — see there. And see in responsa Tzemach Tzedek siman 47: he was asked if permitted to taste lye if it has full salt, and he answered that even though it is a spoiled matter, rabbinically nevertheless forbidden l'chatchila; and tasting means specifically for eating, but for tasting it is permitted l'chatchila; and tasting is forbidden only rabbinically, and in rabbinic matters they did not decree — see there. And the understanding of his words: he holds the same in all rabbinic issurim tasting is permitted, even to taste in his mouth itself; and so understood the son of the author of Tzemach Tzedek at end of the book, and Peri Chadash siman 108 s.k. 22 — see there (and accordingly resolved Rashi's words in Chullin 97b in what he explained that the issur such as meat in milk that a Jew cannot taste — see there; and also possible to resolve Tosafot's challenge there side 1 s.v. but in basar bechalav from that one who vows in water, etc.; however Tosafot according to their view on daf 98 side 2 s.v. Rava disagree with Rashi z"l and hold even Rava holds in all issurim ta'am ke'ikar d'oraisa — see there — investigate). However in responsa Noda B'Yehudah Tinyana Yoreh De'ah siman 52 he wrote that Tzemach Tzedek permitted only tasting with the tongue, and concludes that even tasting with the tongue is not permitted except in spoiled issur, and not in all rabbinic issurim — see there.
