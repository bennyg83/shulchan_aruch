Prohibited matter, etc. Gid haNasheh that was cooked, etc., and likewise a piece, etc., and Rashi there; and there 97b cheilev in sixty, etc., and cheilev itself is forbidden; and Hagahot Maimoniot.
Against all, etc. There 97b.
Whether, etc. Meaning to exclude the view of Raavad who wrote specifically of earthenware, similar to cheilev that cannot be separated, for cheilev itself is forbidden; unlike in all other vessels where it is possible through hag'alah — we estimate well.
(Lekut) Whether, etc. — to exclude Raavad; and he wrote "and how is it possible to estimate the whole as if all of it is forbidden, behold you permit it through hag'alah"; but in Taharat HaBayit he challenged him — for if so cheilev too, for even though now it has no kashering, nevertheless before it fell you scrape it with tearing and plastering — and how is it possible, etc.; and furthermore since they said in the Gemara because we do not know how much emerged, why should it return to kashering or not; and he wrote and so too our French rabbis and so too Ramban — end of his words (until here).
Or metal. To exclude the first view; see siman 94.
Provided, etc. As written there 97b from a kal va'chomer, etc.
And every prohibition, etc. As written in the Mishnah there "if one recognizes it," etc., and in Yerushalmi chapter 2 of Orlah: sourdough of terumah that fell into the dough and he removed it and afterward the dough became chametz — it is permitted; and similarly a fig of terumah that fell into one hundred, one was removed and afterward the terumah was heated — it shall be permitted; there the prohibition was not recognized, but here the prohibition was recognized.
Prohibition that was nullified, etc. There "but from now if it fell into another pot," etc., and Rashi there s.v. since, etc.
But if, etc. So too there, for he said "into another pot" and there is no challenge to that pot twice, for it refers to what was nullified in sixty; and even though the absorbed matter in it became nevelah and is like another prohibition that fell, as written in siman 99 seif 6 in the hagahah; and the reason is that that absorbed matter is in the pot min b'mino, and in such a case they established it d'oraisa in the majority, as Rosh wrote in chapter 3 that min b'mino in sixty is a decree on account of not in its species; unlike here where there is nothing to decree, for in that pot there is only in its species; and Shach siman 94 s.k. 5.
