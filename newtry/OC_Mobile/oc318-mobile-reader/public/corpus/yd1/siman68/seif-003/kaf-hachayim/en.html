And if he placed it on its sides, the brain and membrane are forbidden and the rest of the head permitted, as written below in hagahah seif 4; and even peeling is not needed — Shach s.k. 1. Pri Chadash observation 1; Kereti observation 1; and that which we say the brain and membrane are forbidden — it deals even when the head was salted, for since fire has no power to expel the head's blood without cutting, all the more so salt will not expel it.
