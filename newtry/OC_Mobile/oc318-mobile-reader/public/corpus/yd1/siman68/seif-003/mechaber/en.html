Some wrote that to remove the hair alone, even [if] he placed it on its sides, is permitted; and some forbid (Rashba in the name of one authority). Hagahah: And the first reason is primary, and so they are accustomed to singe the head to remove the hair; and likewise birds from the remaining feathers on them, for since it is not placed in one place but he moves it back and forth, we are not concerned that blood is absorbed in it, and it is forbidden only when buried and always placed in one place (Ran and Rabbenu Yerucham and HaAroch general rule 17). And they are accustomed to singe on straw and stubble that do not have so much heat to cook the blood (Ba'al HaAroch). Nevertheless he should pass them over the fire always so it not remain in one place long.
