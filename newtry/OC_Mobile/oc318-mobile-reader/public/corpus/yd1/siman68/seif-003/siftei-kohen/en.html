And the first reason is primary, etc. The wording is unclear, for it implies that according to the first reason even burial placed in one place is permitted, since he does not wish to roast it but only to remove its hair, and afterward he finishes — and it is forbidden only burial; and it appears the Rav follows his reasoning, who wrote in Darkei Moshe: and it appears to me that Ran's words are like Rabbenu Yerucham, who wrote in Netiv Chayim: specifically burial; but placing on hot coals and always changing its place — permitted in every matter, end of his words; and with this we do not find Tur disagrees, and likewise in Orchot Chaim general rule 16, that it is permitted to singe the head and remove hair on fire even if he places it on its sides, since he does not bury it in hot ashes always in one place — and likewise regarding legs, end of his words. And hence such is the explanation of his words here in hagahah: the first reason is primary, for the second reason too does not disagree except regarding burial; but in singeing permitted — and so they are accustomed to singe, etc., and it is forbidden only burial, etc.
