Even if he placed it on its sides, etc. — and likewise Ran, who gave the reason that to remove hair he does not leave it on hot ashes long, and it is permitted; and Pri Chadash challenged: Rama in hagahah gave the reason that it is not placed in one place — behold, placed in one place is forbidden. And one may say: in an animal, even though placed in one place, since he does not leave it long it does not absorb and expel — permitted; but in a bird, in a short moment it expels and absorbs; and Rama spoke of a bird, and therefore must give the reason that it is not placed in one place. And what he further challenged — that he is against Rashi and Tosafot, who explain Rashi in Kevesa as to remove hair, while Rama wrote the main view is like Ran to remove hair, contradicting Rashi and Tosafot — it appears the Rav holds Rashi and Tosafot do not disagree with Ran, for clearly he does not leave it long to remove hair lest the skin soften and be ruined — and this is when there is no water on the hair; but when there is water on the hair it protects so the meat not burn, and he waits longer — therefore Tosafot and Rashi wrote when there is water on the hair it is forbidden because he waits — not so Ran and the Rav, who deal when he did not put water; if so, he does not wait — permitted.
