The head to roast it. In Tur it is written to skin it, and there is no difference between them for practical halacha, for skinning and roasting are equal in this; for in the Gemara it states one who buries a head in hot ashes — only Beit Yosef inferred from that the Rav wrote to skin it, that he did not speak of roasting; nevertheless he ruled for halacha like Rambam, who wrote explicitly to roast it.
The brain and membrane are forbidden. Even though in siman 65 seif 1 he ruled that for roasting one need not cut or salt, for fire draws out — here is different, for the whole head is on the fire and the blood gathers inside, and it is as if the brain is in a pot since there is no place to exit — not like above, which deals with a piece of meat on the fire; and so in Perishah, and it is simple. And in Beit Yosef he wrote in the name of Agur in the name of Mahari Molin that the brain becomes nevelah and forbids the whole head; and he wrote on this there is no concern, since the poskim explicitly permitted, end of his words. And the reason appears: if you say the bone of the pot absorbs, we would say as it absorbs so it expels to fire.
And l'chatchila they are accustomed, etc. Rashal wrote: what women are accustomed, to throw the brain in a goose head and to remove even the membrane, is because they place the head in hot ashes on two sides, end of his words.
