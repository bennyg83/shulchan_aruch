To roast it. Taz wrote: in Tur it states to skin it, and there is no difference between them for practical halacha, for skinning and roasting are equal in this law.
Forbidden. Taz wrote: even though in siman 65 he ruled that for roasting one need not cut or salt, for fire draws out — here is different, for the whole head is on the fire and it is as if the brain is in a pot since there is no place for it to exit; and Shach wrote that all the rest of the meat is permitted, and even peeling is not needed, for the membrane separates between the blood inside and the meat, and the blood is not expelled outside; and even if expelled — as it absorbs so it expels.
Bone. Shach wrote: even if he did not pierce the membrane — nevertheless permitted.
They are accustomed. Rather, they regularly remove the brain and salt it first before roasting, and its membrane they throw outside (and people are accustomed to throw the brain with the membrane in a goose; and in a rooster and hen they throw away the head entirely; and in an animal they salt the brain well and throw the membrane outside) — Shach in the name of responsum of the Rosh.
