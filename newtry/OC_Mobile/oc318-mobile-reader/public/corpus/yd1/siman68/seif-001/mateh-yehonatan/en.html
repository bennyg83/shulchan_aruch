(Siman 68 seif 3 in hagahah) And the first reason is primary, etc. Shach s.k. 5 — the wording is unclear, etc. And one may say: cutting that is by fire has greater power to extract from burial in ashes; if so, the dispute of Ran and Rashba deals with burial in ashes; but in burial in fire all agree it is forbidden. And if so, one may say: that which Rama wrote is forbidden only burial placed immediately in one place — meaning, similar to singeing that is on fire; and in burial in fire in one place on the fire, certainly forbidden even according to the first reason.
