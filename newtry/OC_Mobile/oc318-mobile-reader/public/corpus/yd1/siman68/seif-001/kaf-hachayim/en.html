(1) [Seif 1] One who buries the head to roast it, etc. In Tur it is written: one who buries the head in hot ashes to skin it, and there is no difference between them for practical halacha, for skinning and roasting are equal in this. Taz s.k. 1. And although below in Shulchan Aruch seif 3 he brought a dispute in skinning, it appears Taz's view is to be stringent like those who forbid, and therefore he wrote for practical halacha they are equal in this. And likewise Chayei Adam general rule 33, observation 3; and see below observation 9.
