(Likkut) One who buries, etc. Like the first version in cooking of Torah — follow the stringent. Terumat HaDeshen 75:2, and so he wrote in many places; and likewise Rif and Shulchan Pitchei; and Ra'ah challenged him there, for we hold the halacha follows the last version, whether lenient or stringent; and Magen Avraham answered that the Geonim did not say so except in monetary laws, where there is no leniency and stringency, but not in Torah prohibitions — and it is not so, for Rif and Rambam ruled thus many times even in Torah prohibitions. And Ra'ah further challenged there: behold blood in its cooking is d'rabbanan (end of his words).
The brain and membrane. Tosafot there, s.v. reisha: but the head is permitted — and as stated in the Gemara there in Mezivrei, that fire draws out; only here, since the skull bone is like a pot for the brain, the blood cooks — not so in a head, which certainly fire draws out.
Unless, etc. For then it is immediately forbidden like beit shechitah; and even if he did not pierce the membrane, it is immediately forbidden like in Mezivrei; and see above siman 65 seif 1.
