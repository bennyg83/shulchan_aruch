Laws of skinning the head and birds. In it are 15 seifim. One who buries the head to roast it must intend that the beit shechitah be below so the blood flows out through there; and if he placed it on its sides, the brain and membrane are forbidden unless he pierced the brain bone and the hole was below, for the blood flows out through there.
{Rama: And l'chatchila they are accustomed to be stringent not to roast any whole head, even if he wishes to place it on the beit shechitah, for we are concerned lest it turn on its sides (responsum of the Rosh, general rule 20, and he brings it in Beit Yosef siman 71).}
