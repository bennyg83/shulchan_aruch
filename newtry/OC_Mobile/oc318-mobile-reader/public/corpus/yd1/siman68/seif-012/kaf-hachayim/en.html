(12) There. Unless there are sixty against the brain and membrane. There are two membranes for the brain: one attached to the skull bone, and one attached to the brain itself; and there is not sixty in the brain against both membranes; and if the brain was cooked with both membranes, everything is forbidden; but against the membrane attached to it there is sixty in the brain against it, and if cooked with this membrane, everything is permitted. Orchot Chaim general rule 19 observations 4 and 6, from what is stated in Shabbat daf 129a; Kenesset HaGedolah in Beit Gavriel observation 17; Beit Yehudah observation 3; Shulchan Gavoah observation 12; Zivchei Tzedek observation 14.
