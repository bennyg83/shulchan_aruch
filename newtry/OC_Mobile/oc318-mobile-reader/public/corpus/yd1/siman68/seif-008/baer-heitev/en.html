The hooves. Shach wrote: and there is no difference whether they lie on their length or stand on their hooves — so in Darkei Moshe (for in any case most poskim permit even what is inside the hooves — Shach); but Maharshal's view is to forbid the rest too when they stand on their hooves.
In singeing. Shach wrote: and Maharshal forbids even in singeing; it appears it deals when he leaves them a while on the fire; but when he passes them over the fire and always changes their place — he agrees it is permitted; nevertheless l'chatchila it is good to be stringent even in passing-through to remove the tops of the hooves, etc.
