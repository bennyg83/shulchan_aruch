Hooves of the leg that were salted in their shell and he did not cut their tops were not forbidden. {Rama: And some forbid what is inside the shell, namely the hooves; but the rest is permitted (hagahot of Mahari Weil in the name of Semak and hagahot of Shach Devarim and HaAroch) — and so is the custom; and the same law applies if he buried them in hot ashes; but in mere singeing of hair where he passes it over the fire and always changes its place — everything is permitted (HaAroch and Maharil).}
