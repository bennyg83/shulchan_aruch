And likewise, etc. See Tosafot there s.v. aval, etc., and Tosafot Zevachim 95b s.v. irah, etc. — see there, and Tosafot Pesachim 40b s.v. ha'alpas, etc.: (Likkut) And likewise he should not pour, etc. See Maharshal, and there in Jerusalem Talmud: what about pouring with the stream? R' Chiya, son of R' Hillel: dispute of R' Yonah and R' Yosi; R' Yitzchak bar Nafcha asked before R' Mana: he did so on Shabbat — liable for cooking; he did so in meat and milk — liable for cooking. He said to him: as R' Alexandri [said]: what is clear porridge? Whatever fire passes beneath it and comes; what is clear stew? Whatever fire passes beneath it (explained above siman 105 s.k. 13) }and Rashba wrote it appears the conclusion of irui is not like kli rishon, like R' Yosi; but nevertheless one must be careful like Rabbenu Tam's words. And it appears to me the conclusion is certainly like R' Yosi, that it does not cook — and so in our Gemara explicitly in Zevachim 95b: absorption without cooking, etc.; and Tosafot there (who forced this); and thereby the proof of R' Yonah and R' Yose bar Bun in the Jerusalem Talmud is resolved; and nevertheless here all agree it is forbidden, for it absorbs, as stated in the Gemara there; and likewise the Jerusalem Talmud is explained generally that it absorbs; and R' Yitzchak bar Nafcha did not need to ask except regarding Shabbat and meat and milk whether liable for cooking — but he did not ask regarding other prohibitions whether permitted in eating (end of his words).
