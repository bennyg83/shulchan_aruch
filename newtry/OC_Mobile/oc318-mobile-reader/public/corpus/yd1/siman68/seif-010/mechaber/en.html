One does not skin kids, lambs, and birds in a kli rishon unless he pierced the forbidden fat and salted them; and likewise he should not pour from a kli rishon onto them; and if he poured, peeling the skin suffices; and if the bird's head is upward when he pours on it, he need not peel. {Rama: And it is not called irui unless the stream from the kli rishon did not stop when it touched below; but if it already stopped, it is not called irui and does not forbid (Ran and other poskim, and see above siman 105 s.k. 4).}
