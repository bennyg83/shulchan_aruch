In kli rishon — even b'dieved it forbids, for kli rishon cooks.
And likewise he should not pour — see what I wrote in siman 105 regarding laws of irui.
With peeling of the skin — for irui cooks to the depth of a peel; and even if legs are upward from the bird.
Need not peel — for the feathers on the head are in place of peeling; and if he poured twice, the feathers already fell and it is forbidden — Shach.
