Shach wrote: one should wonder for what purpose the Rav wrote in hagahah above seif 10 that it is not called irui when the stream stopped — behold the Mechaber wrote here two views on this; and it is forced to say that here it deals when the chicken is placed in a pan and he poured on the chicken and the pan together, for the plain law does not imply this at all; and further, in this case the Rav wrote in hagahah siman 95 seif 3 plainly that it is permitted because irui is not like a true kli rishon that would cause mutual absorption; therefore it appears the matters are as their plain sense, and 'there are those who permit' is Rashbam, whom Tur and Beit Yosef brought, who holds that if he poured on the chicken he need not peel even if its head is downward; and even though in seif 10 the Mechaber ruled anonymously like Rabbenu Tam whom Tur brought, that peeling is needed — he relied on what he would bring at the end of the siman also the permitting view; and see above siman 105.
