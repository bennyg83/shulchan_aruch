But if they poured water, etc. Eitan HaEzrachi explained: but if they poured on the chicken and on the vessel, and the chicken's head was not upward — some forbid the vessel, that the water absorbed from the chicken and was absorbed in the vessel; and some permit, that the water already cooled when poured on the chicken before reaching the vessel; but the chicken in any case needs peeling, end of his words; and in Sefer Lamed Chet daf 199b he brought his words and wrote: and he explained well — only that nothing of the permitting view is mentioned in Beit Yosef, end of his words; and I wonder, for also nothing of the forbidding view is mentioned in Beit Yosef; and further, if so, for what purpose did the Rav write in hagahah above seif 10 that it is not called irui when the stream stopped — behold the Mechaber wrote here two views on this; and it is forced to say that here it deals when the chicken is in a pan and he poured on the chicken and pan together in a manner where irui did not stop but the stream flowed once on the chicken and pan — for the plain wording does not imply this at all; and further, in this case the Rav wrote in hagahah siman 95 seif 3 plainly that it is permitted because irui is not like a true kli rishon that would cause mutual absorption — see there; and further, the Mechaber's plain wording implies it refers to the chicken; therefore it appears the matters are as their plain sense, and 'there are those who permit' is Rashbam, whom Tur and Beit Yosef brought, who holds that if he poured on the chicken he need not peel even if its head is downward; and even though in seif 10 the Mechaber ruled anonymously like Rabbenu Tam whom Tur brought, that peeling is needed — he relied on what he would bring at the end of the siman also the permitting view; and see above siman 105 s.k. 4.
