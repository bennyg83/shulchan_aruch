(15) There, in hagahah. And b'dieved if he cut in two directions it suffices, etc. — meaning: if he salted b'dieved, even if he had not yet cooked and did not remove the membrane before melicha — it suffices if he cut in two directions, and he need not remove the brain; but if he cooked — permitted without cutting in two directions, if he pierced the bone opposite the membrane before melicha and left the hole below, as below siman 71 seif 3 — see there. Shach s.k. 8; Lehem HaPanim observation 6.
