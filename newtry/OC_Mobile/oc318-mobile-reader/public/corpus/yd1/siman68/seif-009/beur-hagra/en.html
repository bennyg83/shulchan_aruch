And if it is, etc. Not so for roasting, for we say as it absorbs so it expels, as written in the beginning of chapter Keitzad Tzolin; not so in melicha, which does not expel what was absorbed through fire, for fire is stronger than melicha and 'as it absorbs so it expels' does not apply.
And they are not accustomed, etc. For we are concerned lest he not open it because of soiling from ash, and we say one authority: liver on meat is forbidden l'chatchila even for roasting — all the more so in such a case.
The whole head, etc. For one cannot distinguish the membrane in a bird — Orchot Chaim.
And in an animal, etc. As above seif 2.
And if he did not, etc. For in roasting it forbids only to the depth of peeling or removal; even though we are stringent about sixty, as written above in siman 105 in such a case — one should not be stringent.
