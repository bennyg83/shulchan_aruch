Chicken — meaning the body; but the head — in a bird it is eaten; in fowl forbidden as in an animal, for there bones block the blood.
That he rinse — and likewise other places where there is visible blood, there it is absorbed by fire — Peri Toar.
And they are not accustomed — they refrain from opening to remove the intestines lest ash enter and soil it; and if he buries it whole — forbidden because of the liver, for it is like liver on meat, forbidden l'chatchila even for roasting — Terumat HaDeshen.
To remove — meaning according to Ashkenazim, who are stringent about sixty, in any case removal is needed; but for Sephardim, who do not require sixty, peeling suffices — Peri Chadash.
