It is permitted to skin a chicken in hot ashes (meaning: hot coals) }or to pass it through fire, provided he rinses the beit shechitah first very well (and likewise other blood if it adheres visibly) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbenu Yerucham) }and if it is for a pot he should beware not to leave it so long on the fire that the heart and liver can heat up and expel (Rashba in Torat HaBayit daf 75 and Ran). Hagahah: And they are not now accustomed to skin at all in hot ashes, even a chicken (Terumat HaDeshen siman 148). However, if he transgressed and did so regarding a bird — the whole head is forbidden and the rest is permitted (Mordechai, beginning of chapter 3, and HaAroch general rule 16, and Rashba there, and Shach Devarim). And regarding an animal, the whole head is not forbidden — only the membrane and brain if he did not place it on the beit shechitah or on its nostrils and did not pierce anything in it; and if he did not rinse the beit shechitah or other visible blood on it — he must remove or peel the place of the blood, and the rest is permitted.}
