That he rinse. Shach wrote in the name of Ran: if he did not rinse, even b'dieved forbidden even in roasting, for visible blood does not exit even through roasting, as below siman 76; but Rashba wrote that because of expulsion of heart and liver one need not be concerned for roasting — requires further study, for in siman 71 I wrote in the name of Rashba that even blood of the heart is considered visible blood; and it is possible that in singeing, which is lighter than roasting, they are not so stringent to consider it visible blood.
The head. And the reason Shach wrote: one cannot distinguish then the brain membrane; but Maharshal wrote there is no prohibition in the rest of the chicken's head, only they throw it away because it is not important; and Orchot Chaim wrote: but in melicha alone the membrane is still recognizable; and likewise the Rav Rama in siman 71 seif 3.
To remove. Shach wrote: requires further study from where the Rav derived this law, for since we hold regarding visible blood we do not say as it absorbs so it expels, and even in roasting sixty are needed as below beginning of siman 76 — all the more so in burial in hot ashes; and it appears the Rav holds that its blood is not so considered visible blood, for for this reason we hold in siman 76 that meat roasted without first rinsing is permitted; and although there it is entirely permitted — nevertheless here, since it is blood of the beit shechitah and also in burial, which is weaker than roasting, removal or peeling is needed; and one who does not wish to be so stringent — peeling suffices.
