Against the brain, etc. It deals when it was salted first — and it is simple; however we hold that always sixty are needed against the whole head, as will be explained:
And if he roasted it thus, etc. This teaches us here that even if he roasted without burial, the membrane and brain are forbidden — and this was not explained in the Mechaber's words, seif 1:
And b'dieved if he cut, etc. Meaning: if he salted b'dieved, even if he had not yet cooked and did not remove the membrane before melicha — it suffices if he cut in two directions, and he need not remove the brain; but if he cooked — permitted without cutting in two directions, if he pierced the bone opposite the membrane before melicha and left the hole below, as below siman 71 — see there:
If we say chanan, etc. Meaning: just as we hold below siman 72 and 92 regarding heart attached to the bird, that if there is not sixty in the bird itself against the heart, sixty are needed in the pot against the whole bird, and nevertheless the bird is forbidden — so too here, if there is not sixty in the piece itself against the brain and membrane, sixty are needed in the pot against the whole piece, and nevertheless the piece is forbidden; and therefore where the head alone was cooked — certainly there is not sixty in the head against the brain and membrane; sixty are needed in the pot against the whole head, and nevertheless the head is forbidden — and likewise Orchot Chaim general rule 17 din 7; and if the head is attached to the bird, the whole bird combines for sixty against the brain and membrane — and likewise in Torat Chatat general rule 27 end of din 6 and general rule 91 din 50 — see there:
