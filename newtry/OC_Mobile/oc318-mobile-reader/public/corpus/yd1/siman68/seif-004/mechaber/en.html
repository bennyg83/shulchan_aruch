If he cooked the whole head, everything is forbidden unless there are sixty against the brain and its membrane (likewise in the name of the Rambam and Ramban and Rabbenu Yerucham and Or Zarua). Hagahah: And if he roasted it thus whole and did not place it on the beit shechitah or on its nostrils and pierced something in it — the membrane and brain are forbidden and the rest is permitted (Magen Avraham in the name of Ramban). And l'chatchila they are accustomed to remove the brain before melicha from inside the skull and to cut the skull in two directions, for then the membrane is cut well; and some remove the membrane. And b'dieved, if he cut in two directions or one direction it suffices, even if he did not remove the brain before melicha, and even if he cooked it thus (and see below siman 71 and siman 72 and 92). If the head became forbidden — whether we say the piece becomes nevelah, for in this it is like other prohibitions (hagahot of Shach Devarim).
