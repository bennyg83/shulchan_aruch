Nullified — if the head is by itself, the head is nullified and sixty are needed against it, and nevertheless the head remains in its prohibition as issur davuk; and if there is sixty in the whole bird against the brain, everything is permitted, even the head itself; but the brain is forbidden like a piece cooked in its blood.
Like other prohibitions — see Peleti, where I raised regarding a bird cooked with its whole head and it is not known whether the bird has sixty against blood in the brain and membrane; if there are further branches to be lenient, one may rely on Rabbenu Kalonymus, who permits in Mordechai.
