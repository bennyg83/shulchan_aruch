The brain. Shach wrote: and it deals when it was salted first; however we hold that always sixty are needed against the whole head.
Cutting. Shach wrote: and where the head alone was cooked — certainly there is not sixty in the head against the brain and membrane; sixty are needed in the pot against the whole head, and nevertheless the head is forbidden, for this is called issur davuk; and if the head is attached to the bird, the whole bird combines for sixty against the brain and membrane; and if he pierced the bone opposite the membrane before melicha and left the hole below and cooked it — permitted even without cutting in two directions.
