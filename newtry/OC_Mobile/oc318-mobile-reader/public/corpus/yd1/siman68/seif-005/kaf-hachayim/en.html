(11) [Seif 4] If he cooked the whole head, everything is forbidden unless there are sixty, etc. — and it deals when it was salted first. Shach s.k. 10. Kenesset HaGedolah in Beit Gavriel observation 18; Shulchan Gavoah observation 14; Beit Yosef Avraham in Shulchan Pitchei 7 observation 6; Zivchei Tzedek observation 8. However we hold that always sixty are needed against the whole head — Shach there; Beit Yosef Avraham there. And meaning: that which Shulchan Aruch wrote 'unless there are sixty against the brain and membrane' — this is according to Shulchan Aruch's ruling in siman 92 seif 4, that we do not say chanan except in meat and milk; but according to our Rabbi's ruling there in hagahah, that in all prohibitions we say chanan — sixty are needed against the whole head and the head itself is forbidden — see there; and likewise Shiyurei Berachah observation 6; and the same if he roasted thus and did not place it on its nostrils and afterward cooked it — the law is thus. Shiyurei Berachah there; Beit Yosef Avraham there. And see below observation 16.
