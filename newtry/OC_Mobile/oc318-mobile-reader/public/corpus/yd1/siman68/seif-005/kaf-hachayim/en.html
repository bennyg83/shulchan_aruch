(5) Once they brought before me a roasted lamb head that was not cut at the brain bone, but a nail was stuck in the brain bone; and I told them: even though Maran wrote that if the brain bone is pierced the blood flows through there — this is where the hole remained open; but here, where the nail and its head seal the hole — in what way will its blood flow? And even though Maran wrote nearby that where he placed it on its nostrils, if he stuck a reed or other object — it is different regarding nostrils, for there is no bone on that flat line, only hard flesh separates between it and the brain; hence whatever is pierced there, even if he left the reed there, its blood flows through there. But the brain bone is hard, and the nail-head seals it, and no way remains for its blood to flow — Shulchan Gavoah observation 4; Zivchei Tzedek observation 4.
