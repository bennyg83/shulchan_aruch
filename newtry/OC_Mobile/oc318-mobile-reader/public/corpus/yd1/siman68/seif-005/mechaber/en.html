One who comes to skin the head must cut the sinews in the jaw before skinning; and likewise he must rinse the beit shechitah so no blood remains there; and likewise when skinning the bird (Ran). {Rama: And even if he only singes to remove the hair, the law is thus (in HaAroch general rule 17). Nevertheless b'dieved he is not forbidden by mere singeing if he did not remove the sinews and did not rinse them.}
