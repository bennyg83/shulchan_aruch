To cut. Shach wrote: and even though in siman 65 he brought the words of those who permit roasting without cutting — one may distinguish between roasting and skinning; and Maharshal did not write thus.
Singeing. Shach wrote: in singeing on wood it deals — then he must rinse the beit shechitah; and b'dieved if he did not rinse — permitted; but on straw and stubble, even l'chatchila they are not careful, and one should not salt before the passing-through.
