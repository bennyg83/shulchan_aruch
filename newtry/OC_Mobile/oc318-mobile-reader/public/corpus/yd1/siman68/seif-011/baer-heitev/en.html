L'chatchila. Meaning: if yad soledes bo (but if yad soledes bo does not apply, even in kli rishon permitted).
Permitted. Even if yad soledes bo.
To be careful. If it is possible to be careful.
Copper. Shach wrote in the name of Rabbenu Yerucham and other poskim: if he poured meat into a copper vessel of milk, hag'alah is not needed, for a copper vessel does not absorb unless the fire passes beneath it; and he challenged this and proved from the Talmud and poskim that even a copper vessel needs hag'alah; and the Gaon Rabbi Yehoshua agreed with him.
