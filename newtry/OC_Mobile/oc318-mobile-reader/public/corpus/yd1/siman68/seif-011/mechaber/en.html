Even in a kli sheni, some say one should not skin; and if he skinned — permitted. {Rama: For it is settled that a kli sheni does not cook, even if yad soledes bo (Mordechai, chapter HaShocher), and b'dieved permitted; but l'chatchila forbidden (hagahot of Shach Devarim, chapter 14). Therefore they are accustomed in these countries not to skin anything, only after melicha. However, for honoring guests or Shabbat need, it is permitted to place in a kli sheni even l'chatchila a piece full of ice and snow to hasten its melicha (there). And it is good to be careful l'chatchila to place it only in water where yad soledes bo does not apply; and there is no difference between earthenware, copper, or other vessels regarding kli sheni (so it appears to him from the words of the poskim).}
