(Likkut) Even in kli sheni, etc. And the aforementioned Jerusalem Talmud, chapter 3 of Shabbat and chapter 1 of Ma'aserot — Ramban and Rashba explained in Torat HaBayit 92b that they did not judge there cooking from absorption, but because Scripture made irui like kli rishon and requires hag'alah, purging, and rinsing — and these in kli sheni for hag'alah kli sheni suffices and purging and rinsing are not needed; but absorption — even kli sheni, even copper vessel — see there (end of his words).
