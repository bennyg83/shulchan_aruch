(2) There. He must intend that the beit shechitah be below so the blood flows out through there, etc.; and if he hung it and the beit shechitah is below — permitted, for the blood exits and drips. Rambam, chapter 6 of Laws of Forbidden Foods, halacha 14. And this is to exclude that the beit shechitah not be below and touch the ground — for if it touches the ground, the ground blocks the blood from exiting, and it is like salting in an unperforated vessel; rather he must hang it in air and not touch the ground so the blood flows. However, this is if he roasts it on hard ground; but in hot ashes, which are like fine dust, one need not be particular about this, and even if the beit shechitah touches below in hot ashes — permitted, for it is not called salting in an unperforated vessel. As Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahat HaTur, observation 5; Zivchei Tzedek observation 1.
