The laws of the physician. In it are 3 seifim. The Torah gave permission to a physician to heal, and it is a mitzvah and included in saving life; and if he withholds himself, behold he sheds blood; and even if he has another who would heal him, not everyone merits healing; however he should not engage in healing unless he is expert and there is no greater name than his, for if not behold he sheds blood; and if he healed without court permission he is liable for payment even if expert; and if he healed with court permission and erred and caused harm, he is exempt by human law and liable by heavenly law; and if he killed and it became known he was inadvertent, he goes to exile because of him.
