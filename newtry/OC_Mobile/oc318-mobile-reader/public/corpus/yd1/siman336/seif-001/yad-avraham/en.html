(Siman 336, seif 1) The Torah granted a physician permission to heal, and it is a mitzvah. This is provided that his heart is directed toward Heaven and he recognizes that true healing comes from Hashem. He should seek Hashem and should not intend that everything depends upon a particular drug or upon the physician, as it is written, 'Even in his illness he did not seek Hashem, but only the physicians.'
