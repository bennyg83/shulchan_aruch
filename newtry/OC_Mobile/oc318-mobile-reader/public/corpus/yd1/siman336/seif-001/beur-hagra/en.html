And if he healed... and if. Tosefta Bava Kamma chapter 6 states: an expert physician who treated with authorization of beit din is exempt from liability in human court, but his judgment is entrusted to Heaven. This implies specifically where he acted with authorization of beit din.
And if. The Tosefta, cited by Ramban in Torat HaAdam, states that an expert physician who treated with authorization of beit din and caused injury is subject to exile if the case is one of accidental killing.
