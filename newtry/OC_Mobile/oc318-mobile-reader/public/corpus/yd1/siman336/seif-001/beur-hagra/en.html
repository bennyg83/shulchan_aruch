(Likkut) And it is a commandment. As stated in chapter 4 of Nedarim, 41b; see the Rosh in his commentary there, s.v. "refu'at, etc." and in his decisions there (end of note).
And if he withholds, etc. As stated in the Yerushalmi regarding chapter 50, cited by the Rosh at the end of tractate Yoma: one who asks — this is bloodshed.
(Likkut) And even, etc. Yerushalmi there, cited by the Rosh in his decisions; see there and the Ran there, s.v. "ella refu'at, etc."
And yet, etc. An a fortiori inference from the other laws and rulings of the Torah, which are forbidden except in such a case. And we say at the beginning of chapter 10 of Sanhedrin: Rav did not leave, etc.; if so, another, etc. And at the end of Kiddushin: the best of physicians, etc. (end of note).
And if he healed, etc., and if, etc. Tosefta (ch. 6) of Bava Kamma: a skilled physician who healed with the permission of the court is exempt from human judgment, and his judgment is given over to Heaven — implying specifically with the permission of the court.
And if, etc. Tosefta (cited by the Ramban in Torat HaAdam): a skilled physician who healed with the permission of the court and caused harm — he is exiled.
