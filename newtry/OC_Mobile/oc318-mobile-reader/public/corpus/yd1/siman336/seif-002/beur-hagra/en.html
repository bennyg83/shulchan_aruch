(Likkut) The physician, etc. For we say in the Sifrei, Bava Kamma 81b, and Sanhedrin 73a: "and you shall return it to him" includes the loss of his body. And for anything one is commanded to do, it is forbidden to take payment for the act itself, as stated in Bekhorot chapter 4, 29a, and likewise regarding returning lost property.
But, etc. As stated there, and at the end of chapter 2 of Kiddushin, in the Gemara: this is not difficult; here it refers to payment, etc.
And compensation for idleness. This is in the Mishnah and Gemara there, and at the end of chapter 2 of Bava Metzia.
