Who is called and moreover. From the passage in Yevamot 106a.
(Likkut) But if he stipulated, etc., and even though, etc. Not like those who explain that the reason a fugitive flees is because he is obligated to save him due to the restoration of lost property — and according to their view, even if the physician stipulated a fee this would apply; but this is not so, for if so, what proof did Rav Pappa bring there from chalitzah? — for chalitzah is not incumbent upon the yavam to perform, and even though an unworthy yavam — nevertheless if he enters into levirate marriage he has not transgressed a prohibition. Rather, the reason is that when he stipulates a fee above the fair rate due to compulsion and distress, and the same applies to a woman. And even though in the case of a woman the extra payment does not apply since she can say "you have caused me to lose both a husband and property," as stated in Bava Kamma 116 — since he is unworthy of her and the Torah has betrothed her (as stated in Yevamot 44a, 101b, 106b) to a proper match for her, as stated — and the Tosafot there, s.v. "ein, etc." — and so wrote the Rosh there; but Rashi there, s.v. "amar leih, since, etc." wrote that even in chalitzah the reason is because it is incumbent upon her, etc., because he is unworthy and the Torah said "and they shall speak to him, etc." — as above; see Even HaEzer siman 264 s.7 (end of note).
(Likkut) And even though, etc. This comes to resolve the difficulty of the Ritva, cited by the Nimukei Yosef at the end of the chapter on chalitzah — for he is biblically obligated to save him due to the loss of his person, as stated in Sanhedrin 73a, and as stated in s.2 and in Choshen Mishpat siman 426 (end of note).
(Likkut) And even though, etc. From what is stated in Kiddushin 8b: "a dog runs, etc." or perhaps, etc. — and it remained unresolved. And even though regarding a poor person there he said "and even a poor person who is supported, etc." — what is the reason, etc. — and the Ran resolved it there differently (end of note).
