Theirs. The Bach wrote that it should read "his obligation." This is the explanation: not only if the physician already received the fee they fixed for him do we not extract it from his hand, but even if he received nothing, if they fixed a fee and obligated themselves to him for such-and-such amount, they must pay everything to which they obligated themselves. The Taz wrote: although regarding one fleeing from prison who had a ferry before him and said, "take me across and I will give you a dinar," he need give him only his wage, as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 264, there is different, because that action has a set price; but medical wisdom has no measure, and at first he had no obligation to teach this. Therefore the condition is effective specifically in this case; end quote.
