One who has medicines and his fellow is sick and needs them—it is forbidden to raise their price more than appropriate; and moreover, even if they assessed a high price for him because of the need of the hour when medicines were found only in his hand, he has only their value; but if he stipulated a large physician's fee, he must pay him, for he sold him his wisdom and it has no price. {Rama: And even though there is a mitzvah on him to heal him—for every positive mitzvah incumbent on everyone, if it happens to one and he does not wish to fulfill it except for money, we do not extract the money from his hand nor remove from his hand their obligation (Tur).}
