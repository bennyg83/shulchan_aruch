They delayed him for his honor. Radbaz, part 1, responsum 107, was asked about delaying burial from Thursday until shortly before Shabbat in order, supposedly, to spare the deceased from chibut hakever. He rules that even were this spiritually effective, it is forbidden and is not called delay for the deceased's honor. Honor refers to obtaining a coffin and shrouds, not attempting to alter punishment. In any event, the delay would not achieve the claimed result.
