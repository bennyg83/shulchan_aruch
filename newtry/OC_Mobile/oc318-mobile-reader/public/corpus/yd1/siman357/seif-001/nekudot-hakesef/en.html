(Siman 357 in Taz s.k. 1) And the same applies to waiting for his relatives, so wrote Maharshal. They wrote this in the name of Maharshal for nothing, for it is explicit in the Shulchan Aruch, and so too in the Tur; it is a baraita in Evel Rabbati, and the Ramban, the Rosh, and the other poskim wrote likewise.
