(Siman 357, seif 1) It is forbidden to leave the deceased overnight. Binat Adam writes that the prohibition applies even if the body is not left the entire night until dawn, because the verse does not explicitly say "until morning." He overlooked that later authorities already disputed this. Birkei Yosef cites Ginat Veradim as taking Binat Adam's view, while Chavot Yair says the prohibition is violated only if the deceased is left the entire night. In my opinion Chavot Yair is primary. Tosafot Bava Metzia 110b write that even where Scripture merely says "lodging" without mentioning morning, it means until dawn. Tosafot at the end of Avodah Zarah 76 similarly infer from Zevachim that dawn is what establishes the status of linah. I later saw that Radbaz, siman 311, brings these same proofs. Sefer HaChayyim, Hilchot Yom Tov, agrees with Binat Adam but also seems to have overlooked these sources.
