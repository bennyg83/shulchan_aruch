(Siman 357, Shulchan Aruch seif 1) It is forbidden to leave the dead overnight. He wrote in the book Binat Adam that there is a prohibition even if he does not leave him the entire night until dawn — for it is not written "do not leave until morning." But he did not see that the later authorities already disputed this, as written in Birkhei Yosef — for in Gan Hamedot it is so written, but in Responsa Chavot Yair he wrote that one does not transgress unless he left him the entire night — and so it seems to me to be the primary ruling, for Tosafot in Bava Metzia (110b) wrote that even where "morning" is not stated but only "leaving" — it means dawn. And in the end of Avodah Zarah (76a) Tosafot wrote that it implies in ch. 2 of Zevachim that regarding sanctified things, dawn is considered leaving overnight. Afterwards I also saw that Radbaz in siman 311 and the book Ha-Chayyim, Laws of Yom Tov, likewise wrote as Binat Adam — and they too overlooked what was written there.
