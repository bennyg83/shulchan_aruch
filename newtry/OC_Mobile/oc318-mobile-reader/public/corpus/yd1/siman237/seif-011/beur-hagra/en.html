Collected note: He said to him, 'An oath,' etc. This is the Mishnah in Nedarim, according to Rav Ashi's explanation there, and at the beginning of the third chapter of Shevuos. On this point there is no dispute. See Ran there.
Or, 'for an oath,' etc. See Ran there on the Mishnah, s.v. Shevuah. At the beginning of the third chapter of Shevuos, however, he explains that the meaning is: "It shall be as an oath; therefore I will not eat with you." See there.
