A substitute for an oath is like an oath. How? "Shevuata" or "shevuqa" that I will not eat this loaf; or "momi" or "omi." But if he said "be-omta" or "be-omi" with a bet—it is nothing. And these substitutes are according to their time for people who were not expert in the nature of the holy tongue; and everything is according to time and place to add to these substitutes or diminish from them. (Opinion in Beit Yosef.) {Rama: Those who are accustomed to say in the holy tongue "emunah, I will do" or "I will not do"—even though they are like vows of urging, nevertheless require annulment. (Hagahot Maimoni chapter 4 of Nedarim in the name of Maharam.)}
