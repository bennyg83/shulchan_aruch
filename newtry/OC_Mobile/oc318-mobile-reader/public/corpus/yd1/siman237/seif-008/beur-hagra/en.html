Provided that he intends, etc. As stated in the second chapter of Nedarim, 18b, concerning the phrase "as cherem, etc." All the more so here, where the expression could mean a person's right hand. See Shevuos 35b, "The cases are not comparable, etc." Here, however, the matter may differ because the expression was used in connection with an oath. Nevertheless, this requires study. The Raavad's position cited above in se'if 7 is sound: even there the act is prohibited, and the same applies here. The Gemara's discussion concerns only liability, not the prohibition itself, as stated there regarding saying "no, no," and as explained above in se'if 1.
