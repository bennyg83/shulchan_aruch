One who swears, etc. Mordechai in the name of Rav Chananel Gaon; Ran there, 22b, s.v. Amar Rava, also cites this in his name; see there. This applies specifically when he is holding the object in his hand, as above and as the Rosh writes there. All these are stringencies, since above we stated that one who swears by the Torah, etc., is not bound. Maharam Mintz writes likewise. However, according to the stringency stated at the end of siman 212 concerning the Torah, the same applies here. See Ran and Rosh there.
