One who swears by the Ten Commandments, etc. This implies that if he merely says, “I swear by the Ten Commandments,” it is a complete oath even though he is not holding them in his hand. The responsa of Maharam Mintz, siman 46, writes that he must hold them in his hand. It appears, however, that this is the strict law, just as by strict law one who swears by the Torah must hold it in his hands. But according to what the Beis Yosef and the Rama wrote above in siman 212, that one who swears by the Torah requires release in every case because some Geonim rule stringently that even release does not help, those Geonim are likewise stringent regarding the Ten Commandments. Therefore release is required in every case. This also appears from the responsa of Maharam Mintz there; see there.
