One who swears by the Ten Utterances, etc. — implies that if he only said "I swear by the Ten Utterances," even though he does not hold them in his hand, it is a complete oath. And in responsum of Maharam Mintz siman 46 he wrote we require holding in hand; however it seems this is by law, for also one who swears on the Torah we require holding in hand by law; but according to what Bach and the Rav wrote above siman 212, that one who swears on the Torah in any case needs release since there are geonim who are stringent that even release does not help — if so, also regarding the Ten Utterances those geonim are stringent there; if so, in any case release is needed. And so it seems in responsum of Maharam Mintz there — see there.
