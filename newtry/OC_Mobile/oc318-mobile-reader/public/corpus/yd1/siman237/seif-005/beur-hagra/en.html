And this is, etc. This follows the Rosh's reading there: the two negatives are "I will not again" and "I will not again," which Hashem stated as an oath. The phrases "shall not be cut off," "shall no longer be," and "shall no longer be again" were not said as an oath, but as narrative and assurance. Otherwise, how would we know that two negatives suffice? Perhaps three would be required. Rosh there.
When does this apply, etc. This is also implied there, 20a, in the baraisa, where it states, etc., and then says, "No," etc. Were it otherwise, he would already have sworn even without that addition.
