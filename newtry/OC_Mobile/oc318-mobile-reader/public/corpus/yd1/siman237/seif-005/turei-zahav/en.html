It constitutes an oath. This is learned from the verse, "As I swore concerning the waters of Noach." We do not find that Hashem made an explicit oath there; He merely said twice, "I will not again." Since repeating "no" twice constitutes an oath, repeating "yes" twice likewise constitutes an oath.
For the sake of an oath. Meaning, just as Hashem repeated His statement for the sake of an oath. This explains Ra'avad's phrase, "the yes, yes that the Creator said," which puzzled the Tur.
