Whether one who swears must swear by the Name or by a substitute. It contains 12 se'ifim: One who says, "I swear that I will do such-and-such," or "that I will not do it"—this is an oath, even though he mentioned neither the Name nor a substitute. {Rama: And there is no distinction whether he uttered the oath in the holy tongue or in any language in which he says it. (Responsum of Rashba siman 442, Mordechai chapter Shevuot Shetayim, and Rambam chapter 12 of Hilchot Shevuot.)}
