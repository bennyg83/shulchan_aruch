Or in any language, etc. The responsa of Maharam, Prague edition, siman 6, cites the Riva as ruling that if one mentioned the Divine Name in a foreign language, such as “Dio” or “God,” then even if he did not introduce his words by saying that they were an oath, the expression is effective as an incomplete formulation of an oath.
