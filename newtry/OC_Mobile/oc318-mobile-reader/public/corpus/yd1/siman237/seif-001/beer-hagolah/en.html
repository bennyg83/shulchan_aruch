The Tur in the name of the Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilchot Shevuot, and in the name of his father, the Rosh. An incomplete oath-formulation is no less effective than an incomplete vow-formulation, which has the force of a vow. Rambam writes there that one is not liable for a sacrifice until he mentions one of the unique Divine Names or one of their substitutes.
