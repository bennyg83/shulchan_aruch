One who swore by heaven and earth, sun, and the like—even though his intention is only to Him who created them, it is not an oath. The same applies to one who swore by a prophet of the prophets. (Or by another writing from the holy writings.) (Rambam.) Or by the table of the Temple or by one of the holy vessels. Nevertheless, they intimidate them and show them that this is an oath so that they not treat oaths lightly, and they annul them for them. {Rama: And all this is when he did not take it in his hand. But if he took in his hand a scroll in Assyrian script—even if it is only external wisdom, or an aleph-bet that contains only letters—if he swore by them and they are in his hand, it is an oath. (Opinion of Beit Yosef.) And see above siman 212. The same applies if he did not grasp them in his hand but they were placed before him and he swore by what is written in them.}
