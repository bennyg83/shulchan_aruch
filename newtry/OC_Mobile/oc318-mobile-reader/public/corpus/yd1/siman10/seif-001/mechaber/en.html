Additional knife laws. In it are 3 seifim. A knife of attendants of avodah zarah—whether new or old in which there is no concern of defilement from avodah zarah—is permitted to slaughter with it a healthy animal, because it spoils (the animal) and does not provide benefit. But it is forbidden to slaughter with it a dangerously ill animal, because it repairs (heals). A knife of a gentile that was sharpened on his sharpening wheel (see above siman 121)—or if it was fine so that it had no nicks and he stuck it in hard ground ten times and afterward slaughtered with it—it is permitted. And if he slaughtered with it without any kashering, he rinses the slaughterhouse; and if he peeled (the meat), this is preferable (most poskim). {Rama: And in a situation of pressure where he has no other knife, it is permitted to slaughter with it in such a manner l'chatchila; but he should not slaughter intending to peel afterward (Tur in the name of the Rosh and R' Yerucham).}
