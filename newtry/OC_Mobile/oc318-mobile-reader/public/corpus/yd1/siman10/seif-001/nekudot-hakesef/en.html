(Siman 10 s.k. 6) And he was not precise that in siman 122, etc. He intended well in this; and so I also wrote in the margin showing my place in siman 122 seif 6—not like the Derishah—see there.
(There) And in Derishah and Perishah, etc. And he did not have before him, etc. And in my humble opinion the Derishah and Perishah intended well in this also according to his view—it is strained to say the Rosh speaks of a knife that is not clean, for he does not divide except whether it is ben yomo or not; it does not imply if ben yomo, even if known clean, forbidden—rather the point of sharpening and sticking is that thereby that thin peel in the knife—which sticking is everywhere in place of peeling, as Orach Chaim wrote and I brought in the Shach siman 93 s.k. 6.
