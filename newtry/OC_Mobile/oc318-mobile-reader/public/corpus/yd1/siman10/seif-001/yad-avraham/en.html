(Siman 10, Shach s.k. 4) Because in its lifetime it stands for three things—for eating, to grow young, and for plowing, end of his words. And in Kerem Shlomo he explained: for a bird, which does not have these three things—for it does not stand for labor—if so it does not spoil; see there. And it escaped me and other acharonim: that which is explicit in the Gemara Sanhedrin chapter four deaths (daf 59b), that a bird is fit for labor—namely for plowing. And it is certainly that plowing that Rashi and the Shach mention is not specifically—for in every animal it speaks, namely whose legs were cut from the knee down, that it is fit only for plowing and is kosher. And as Rashi wrote in the first chapter of Chullin (daf 7b)—see there, and think. And see in Hagahot Aharon chapter HaZahav, that a lamb is not fit for labor—and think.
