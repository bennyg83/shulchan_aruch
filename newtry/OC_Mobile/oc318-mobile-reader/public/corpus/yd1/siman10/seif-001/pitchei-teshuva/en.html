To slaughter with it a dangerously ill animal. See responsum Penei Aryeh siman 65 in the middle of the answer, who wrote: it is not specifically slaughter, but even stabbing a dangerously ill animal with such a knife to sell the meat to a gentile is also forbidden—see there; and see what he wrote below siman 117 s.k. 5 in his name. And see there in siman 66, who wrote it is permitted to pierce meat of veins of fat and blood with a gentile's knife; and see what he wrote from this below siman 121 s.k. 4.
On his (sharpening wheel). Avodat HaGershuni: on a knife, not being ben yomo does not help; and see responsum Perach Mateh Aharon part 2 end of siman 47, who wrote: this is only where they are not accustomed to clean knives well; but we, whose way is to clean well, are certainly permitted—see there. And see responsum Chut HaShani siman 55, who wrote regarding cutting on Pesach with a chametz knife: even if he says it was wiped, he is not believed—for we say an unfounded matter, etc.; and see what he wrote below siman 94 s.k. 1 in the name of Noda BiYehudah.
This is praiseworthy (peeling). See Taz and Shach, who challenged: from law, peeling is required; and see responsum Beit Shmuel Acharon part 4 siman 8.
