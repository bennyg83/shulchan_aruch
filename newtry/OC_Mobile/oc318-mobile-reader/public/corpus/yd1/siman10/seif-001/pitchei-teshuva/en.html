To slaughter with it a dangerously ill animal. See responsum Penei Aryeh siman 65 in the middle of the answer, who wrote: it is not specifically slaughter, but even stabbing a dangerously ill animal with such a knife to sell the meat to a gentile is also forbidden—see there; and see what he wrote below siman 117 s.k. 5 in his name. And see there in siman 66, who wrote it is permitted to pierce meat of veins of fat and blood with a gentile's knife; and see what he wrote from this below siman 121 s.k. 4.
