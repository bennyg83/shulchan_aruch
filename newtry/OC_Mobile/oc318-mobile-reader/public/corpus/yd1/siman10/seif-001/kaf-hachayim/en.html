(a) [Seif 1] A knife of attendants of avodah zarah—new. Statement of Rav Nachman and Rava, first chapter of Chullin daf 8; and he explains there: "new" means they did not use it with forbidden grease, but nevertheless they used it for avodah zarah—such as cutting wood—for if completely new, never cut with it, it is not forbidden because of avodah zarah until used for avodah zarah, as in Avodah Zarah chapter R' Yishmael (Avodah Zarah daf 52), and below siman 139. Bach. Shach s.k. 1. Peri Chadash note 1; Kereti note 1—and this is such as they cut wood with it for an avodah zarah offering. Beit Yehudah note 1; Shomer Shabbos note 2. However what he wrote there in Beit Yehudah: the same if he cut wood to make avodah zarah—Peri Chadash in Kuntres Acharon challenged him; and so Shomer Shabbos there—unless in this cutting the wood itself became avodah zarah; see there.
