Permitted to slaughter with it a healthy animal, etc. It implies even l'chatchila; and below siman 142 seif 2 he wrote: a knife of avodah zarah with which he slaughtered—is permitted, etc.—which implies forbidden l'chatchila when not kashered from defilement of avodah zarah; and so is explained from Hagahot HaRav there. Shach s.k. 3. Lechem Panim note 3; Zivchei Tzedek note 4. However Peri To'ar note 2 disagreed with the above Shach's words and wrote: l'chatchila forbidden as in siman 142; and what he wrote in the gloss is his own view, but according to the Shulchan Aruch forbidden l'chatchila even though kashered from defilement of avodah zarah—see there. And so Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahat HaTur note 4.
