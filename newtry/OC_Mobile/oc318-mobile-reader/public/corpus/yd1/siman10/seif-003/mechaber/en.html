A knife with which one slaughtered a treifah—it is forbidden to slaughter with it until one rinses it when cold or wipes it with something hard. And they are accustomed now to wipe it well with animal hair between each slaughter and slaughter, and this is fine; and if he slaughtered with it without rinsing, he rinses the slaughterhouse. And if he is accustomed to slaughter treifot with it frequently, he needs sticking (the knife) ten times in hard ground.
