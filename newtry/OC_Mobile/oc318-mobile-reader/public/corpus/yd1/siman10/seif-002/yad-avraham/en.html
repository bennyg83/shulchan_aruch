(Shach s.k. 5) Sharpening helps even l'chatchila. Meaning: even not in time of pressure—for in time of pressure even sticking is permitted l'chatchila, as the gloss says. But the Taz appears not to hold thus—for he wrote on what the gloss says: and in time of pressure permitted to slaughter with it in such a manner l'chatchila—explanation: if he sharpened on a whetstone or stuck in hard ground; therefore without pressure even sharpening does not help l'chatchila. And one may wonder on the Baal HaBayit Lechem Yehudah, who copied first the Shach's words and afterward the Taz's words as if in one blow—and it is not so, and requires investigation.
