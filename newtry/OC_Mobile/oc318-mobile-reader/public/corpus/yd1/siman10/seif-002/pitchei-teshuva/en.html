On his (sharpening wheel). Avodat HaGershuni: on a knife, not being ben yomo does not help; and see responsum Perach Mateh Aharon part 2 end of siman 47, who wrote: this is only where they are not accustomed to clean knives well; but we, whose way is to clean well, are certainly permitted—see there. And see responsum Chut HaShani siman 55, who wrote regarding cutting on Pesach with a chametz knife: even if he says it was wiped, he is not believed—for we say an unfounded matter, etc.; and see what he wrote below siman 94 s.k. 1 in the name of Noda BiYehudah.
