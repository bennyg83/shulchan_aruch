Another time. And the Shach wrote: permitted to slaughter with it when soiled even without rinsing, because the simanim are busy expelling blood and do not absorb.
Boiling. And he wrote in the Shach: even if he slaughtered only once, it is forbidden afterward to cut boiling l'chatchila if not with hag'alah; and pouring suffices for hag'alah; and b'dieved if he cut something boiling without hag'alah—only after rinsing or something sharp, peeling suffices; the reason: the knife absorbed a slight thing from beit hashchitah; therefore pouring suffices for hag'alah even l'chatchila, and b'dieved by peeling.
First. And he wrote in the Shach: even if he slaughtered with it many times, permitted to cut cold by rinsing—and specifically by rinsing, but wiping does not help, as he wrote afterward in seif 3, because in seif 3 it speaks of beit hashchitah and there is another reason that simanim are busy expelling blood, which is not so here, end of his words. And if he cut cold without rinsing, he rinses that cold afterward.
