A house that has a doorpost on this side and on that side and an arch like a bow over the two doorposts in place of the lintel—if the doorposts are ten tefachim in height or more, it is obligated; and if it does not have ten tefachim, it is exempt because it has no lintel (°). {Rama: And all the more so a doorway that does not have the height of ten tefachim is exempt from a mezuzah (so is explicit in Tur and poskim, Rabbenu Yerucham). The custom has spread in these lands that most of the world rely on one doorpost that they make on the doorway of their houses, and this is not correct; and they have nothing on which to rely. Therefore every God-fearing person should fix his house according to the law in all doorways that are obligated, as was explained (Maharil in a responsum and in Hilchos Mezuzah).}
