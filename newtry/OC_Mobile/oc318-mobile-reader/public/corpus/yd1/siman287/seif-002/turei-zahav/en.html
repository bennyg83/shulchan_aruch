Mezuzah from here, etc. Meaning that we require it to be ten high from the ground evenly without curvature — from the beginning of curvature is not counted within the ten handbreadths; and this is Rambam's view. But Rashi holds that even the curvature joins to height of ten handbreadths, provided there are four handbreadths in its hollow; and even if it begins to curve from the ground — but afterward, when there is no hollow of four, it is called curvature and does not join to the measure of ten handbreadths from the ground. And in Tur on this one must be precise: since he first cited "and if it has two mezuzot," etc. — it implies we require mezuzot like Rambam's view; and at the end he wrote "if the height of the mezuzah is ten before it was shortened so there are not four — obligated; and if not — exempt" — it implies like Rashi, that when shortened to less than four the matter depends. And it appears the whole is like Rashi; and that he first cited two mezuzot is because of the novelty at the end that nevertheless he is exempt when there is not height of ten handbreadths. And it requires study practically how we act if there are ten handbreadths of height evenly without curvature and afterward there is curvature but there are four in its hollow — a large measure up high — if the mezuzah is placed where the even place ends, which is like Rambam: one must be concerned perhaps we hold like Rashi and Tur that even the curvature above in the measure of height counts, since there are four in its hollow; automatically the mezuzah is placed below the upper third, and this is invalid even after the fact as we will explain in siman 289. And if it is placed above in the wide place in the upper third which is curved — perhaps we hold like Rambam that there it does not join to the height of mezuzot at all — and it is as one who places a mezuzah above on the lintel. And it appears one should place it in the upper third, i.e., above the lower two thirds — for in this it is valid also for Rambam, for although the curvature does not join for height of ten from below in mezuzot, nevertheless since it is valid below at height of ten before the curvature and there is obligation upon it, then the upper hollow too belongs to the obligation below, and all is within "your doorposts until the lintel" — for the essence of invalidity without height of ten is because it has no lintel, as he concludes here; automatically since there is height of ten there is a lintel and all below the lintel is valid — so it appears to me. And in any case one must not place it above his shoulders as Beit Yosef wrote in siman 289 regarding great height in name of Mordechai — that one must not raise above the measure between his shoulders so that it be seen opposite his eyes.
