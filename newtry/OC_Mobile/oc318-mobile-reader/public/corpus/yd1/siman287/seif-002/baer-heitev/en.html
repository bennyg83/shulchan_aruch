In height. Rambam requires ten straight handbreadths before any arch begins. Rashi allows the curved portion to count where the interior remains four handbreadths wide. Taz discusses the resulting placement dilemma and concludes that the mezuzah should be placed in the upper third calculated from the lower valid ten handbreadths, yet not above shoulder height.
A God-fearing person. Taz writes that anyone who wishes to dwell securely should place mezuzot on every doorway of a true dwelling. Ordinary filth does not remove the obligation where the mezuzah is above ten handbreadths and properly covered. In a bedroom used for marital relations, it should be covered opaquely.
