Because part of the day is considered the whole day. Tur cites a dispute whether this requires part of the daytime specifically or whether part of the preceding night also counts. Mechaber follows Maharam Rothenburg that part of the day is required. Radbaz, part 3, siman 559, distinguishes: regarding Torah study and marital relations, which are themselves mitzvot, one may follow the lenient opinion and treat part of the night as the whole day. Regarding washing, anointing, and other non-mitzvah activities, one should follow the stringent opinion and wait until part of the daytime has passed. Even regarding marital relations, he advises waiting a little longer than one's usual time so that some mourning practice is observed at night as well. See also Radbaz, new responsa siman 268.
