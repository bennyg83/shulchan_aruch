Because part of the day is considered the whole day. The Tur records a dispute whether part of the daytime is required or even part of the preceding night suffices. The Mechaber follows Maharam of Rothenburg that part of the day is required. Radbaz, part 3, responsum 559, rules that for Torah study and marital relations, which involve mitzvah, one may follow the lenient view that part of the night counts as the whole day. For washing, anointing, and other non-mitzvah matters, one must wait until part of the day has passed. Even for marital relations he should delay slightly beyond his usual time so that some mourning is observed at night. See also Radbaz, new responsa 268. [Citation 25.]
