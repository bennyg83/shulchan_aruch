Part of the seventh day and thirtieth day is like the whole. And it contains 3 seifim. Once comforters stood up from beside the mourner on the seventh day, he is permitted in all matters forbidden within seven, for part of the day is like the whole — whether part of the seventh day or part of the thirtieth day; once the sun rose on the thirtieth day, the decree of thirty is nullified for him: {Rama: (Rosh and Rabbeinu Yerucham); and in these lands where comforters are not accustomed to come on day seven, he must wait until the hour comforters are accustomed to come on other days — that is after leaving synagogue when comforters are accustomed to come, so it appears to me, not as they are accustomed to wait an hour on the day, for the matter depends only on comforters standing (so implied in Ashiri).}
