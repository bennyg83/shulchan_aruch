And the custom is to add. Even if that day falls on Shabbat, he observes the law of twelve months on it; so it is in Terumat HaDeshen, siman 292. However, when the year is intercalated and he is permitted to enter a feast after twelve months, as above in siman 391 seif katan 2, he does not observe the law of twelve months on the day his father or mother died, even though he says kaddish and Barekhu and fasts that day, because once he has already ceased his mourning he does not return to his mourning; so wrote the Bach. It appears that this applies specifically to other types of mourning, but eating at a meal of joy and feasting is forbidden every year on the day his father or mother died, as the Rav wrote below at the end of siman 402; all the more so here.
