We do not say this on the twenty-ninth of Adar, and so forth. Terumat HaDeshen, responsum 292, explains that whenever the law depends upon a count of days, part of the final day is considered like the whole day. Mourning for a parent, however, is consistently defined as twelve months. If part of the final month were treated as the whole, one would need to observe only a small portion of that month, which is clearly incorrect.
