There are other seeds and trees that even though they are two species by nature, since the leaves of this resemble the leaves of that, or the fruit of this resembles the fruit of that—a great resemblance until they appear like two shades of one species—they were not concerned for kilayim one with another. How so? Turnip with cucumber—they are not kilayim because their fruits are equal; turnip with nefus—they are not kilayim because the leaves are equal; but cucumber with nefus—even though the leaves resemble one another and fruit resembles fruit, behold these are kilayim because the taste of this fruit is very far from the taste of that fruit; and likewise all similar cases.
