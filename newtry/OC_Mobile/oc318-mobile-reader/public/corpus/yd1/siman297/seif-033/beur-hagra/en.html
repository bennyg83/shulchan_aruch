And what is the difference, etc. The Yerushalmi there states: Rebbe says that one begins with an area of a beit rova and continues narrowing until it reaches three furrows of fallow ground. Does Rebbe not require a beit rova between one grain crop and another? There it is square, whereas here it is straight. Do the Sages not recognize a straight division? R' Yudan said: what is the end of a straight division according to Rebbe is its beginning according to the Sages.
