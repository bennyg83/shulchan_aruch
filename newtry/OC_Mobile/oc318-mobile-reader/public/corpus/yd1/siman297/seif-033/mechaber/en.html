If he wished to make his field bald patches (karachat) of each species and species, he does not make within each se'ah more than nine bald patches—each bald patch a beit rova—and the distance between bald patch and bald patch is close to ten amot less a quarter, for each se'ah is fifty by fifty. And what is the difference between a bed and a bald patch? The bed is long and the bald patch is square.
