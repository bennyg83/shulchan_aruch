Although their fathers were of one species. This requires study. It should seemingly have said: although their fathers were of two species, it is forbidden, and we do not say that each horse element joins the corresponding horse element and each donkey element joins the corresponding donkey element, since perhaps paternal lineage is disregarded while maternal lineage is certainly considered, making this an actual mixture. As written, where their fathers were of one species, it is forbidden even if paternal lineage is certainly considered, because one of the animals does not contain two corresponding elements. One must explain that the phrase means: although their fathers were of one species and outwardly the mixture appears less pronounced, it is nevertheless forbidden. This is strained.
