Siman 297 (a) — laws of crossbreeding and conduct of kilay behemah. In an animal. Shach wrote: implies even [pure] with pure one is lashed; and this is Rosh and Tur's view — not Rambam who holds one is not lashed for this except rabbinically — end quote.
With an animal. The Shach writes that it implies even a kosher species with a kosher species receives lashes. This is the view of the Rosh and Tur, unlike the Rambam, who holds that in this case he receives lashes only rabbinically. Thus his language.
