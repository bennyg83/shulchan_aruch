Siman 297.
That is not of its own species. The Yerushalmi at the beginning of Kilayim, cited by the Rash there, derives from "You shall not sow your field," etc., and "You shall not mate your animal with kilayim," that one might otherwise have thought the prohibition applies even within a single species. Compare Bava Metzia 91a: had the verse stated, etc.; and there Rava said that mating within one species is permitted. This is also evident from Mishnah 6 there and from Bava Kamma 55a.
