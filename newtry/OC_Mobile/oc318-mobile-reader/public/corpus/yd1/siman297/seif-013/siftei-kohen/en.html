They follow Rambam and the Mechaber, although the Bedek HaBayit and Kesef Mishneh write that Rambam's position is not conclusively established, and hold that even when the animals are not tied together it is forbidden to pull or lead them as one. The Bedek HaBayit cites Orchot Chaim: Jews who hunt with the bird called an esparver must not tie the cord attached to the bird to the horse's saddle, because this constitutes mixed species.
