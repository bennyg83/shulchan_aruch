His field was sown with wheat and he reconsidered to sow it barley—before the wheat sprouts he waits for it until the wheat spoils and rots in the ground, about three days if his field was moist; and afterward he turns them with a plowshare and sows the other species. And he need not turn all of it until no wheat remains unuprooted, but he plows the field as they plow it before rain so it will be moist. If wheat grew and afterward he reconsidered to sow it barley—he turns over and afterward sows them. And if he brought his animal into it and it cropped the plant, behold it is permitted to sow another species.
