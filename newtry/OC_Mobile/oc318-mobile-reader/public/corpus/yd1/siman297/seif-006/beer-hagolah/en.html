The Ra'avad, the Ran, and the Tur challenged him on this; and Rav Yosef Korkus resolved that it refers to a field of a non-Jew — since the one sowing is a non-Jew and the land is of a non-Jew it is not forbidden, and even if it is at Israel's behest; but he himself is forbidden to sow for him, since we are in the Land of Israel and a non-Jew has no acquisition in it.
