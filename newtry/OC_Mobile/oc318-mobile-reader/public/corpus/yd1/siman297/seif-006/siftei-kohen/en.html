Permitted. And in Tosafot Yom Tov chapter 8 of Hilchot Kilayim from mishnayot: wild ox is a species of animal and R' Yosi says species of chayah; and Rambam and Bertinoro explained species of animal — and its milk forbidden and does not require covering, etc.; and he wrote: regarding kilayim there is no difference, for any two species even both animal species or chayah species are forbidden one with another as we taught above, etc. — end quote; and I did not stand on the end of his view, for behold Yerushalmi, Rambam, Taz, and other poskim explain there is difference regarding kilayim; and one could say for two species here it is one species; and as Yerushalmi, Bertinoro, and poskim: its essence was from settlement but fled to wilderness and became wild ox; and as Rambam and Taz wrote: a species that has wilderness and settlement aspects, etc. — and this is plain; and that Rambam and Bertinoro explained regarding its milk and covering — because mishnah language "wild ox species of animal" implies difference also for other matters; otherwise it should have said wild ox permitted with ox — as we taught above in this language — plain.
