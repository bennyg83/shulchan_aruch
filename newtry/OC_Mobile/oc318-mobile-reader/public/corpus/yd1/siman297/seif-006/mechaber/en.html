In what case is this said? When grain species became mixed one with another, or legume species one with another, or grain became mixed with legumes or legumes with grain. But garden seed mixed with grain or legumes—their measure is one in twenty-four of what is sown per se'ah of that species: if mixed in a se'ah of grain or legumes, he does not sow until he diminishes or adds to the grain. How so? Mustard mixed with grain—and behold mustard is sown a kav per se'ah—if one twenty-fourth of the kav mixed in a se'ah of grain or legumes, he must diminish. And likewise if this garden species is sown two se'ah per se'ah and half a kav mixed in every se'ah of grain or legumes, he diminishes. Therefore grain in which flax seed mixed—if there were three quarters in every se'ah, behold he diminishes; and if not, he need not diminish, for per se'ah they sow three se'ah of flax seed. And on this manner we estimate all other seeds.
