And one who sows, etc.; even though he ruled not like R' Akiva as written in seif 2 "and if they remain," etc.; but here the first tanna agrees; and as Makkot 21b: they counted and concluded, etc.; if not that you flatter, etc.; evidently per R' Yanai and R' Yosi one who covers in kilayim is lashed even per the rabbis; not as Mo'ed Katan 2b "all R' Akiva," etc., that specifically for Rabbah needed to explain thus; therefore Rambam ruled chapter 8 of Hilchot Shabbat like Rav Yosef; and so Yerushalmi chapter 8 of Kilayim; and Rosh brought Yerushalmi there: it is written "you shall not sow," etc.; R' Yosi said, etc., like this, etc.; and there R' Yosi said in the name of R' Yanai: one who covers in kilayim is lashed; R' Yochanan said: but is it not mishnah "kilayim in a vineyard"; how possible kilayim via plowing, not via covering? And R' Yanai praised him "these praises are gold," etc.; R' Elazar said: after all these praises I can resolve it like R' Akiva; R' Akiva said one who sustains kilayim transgresses a negative commandment; nothing; R' Akiva said: rather to transgress perhaps to be lashed; and here we stand for lashes; meaning per the rabbis he does not transgress, but nevertheless forbidden rabbinically as above; but for lashes via action; no explanation at all.
Between, etc. As stated in Makkot there, "as he proceeds," meaning through an act of plowing with his foot or with a tool. Likewise, the Yerushalmi cited above speaks of an act, etc.
