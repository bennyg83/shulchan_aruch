The laws of kilay zera'im and its details. In it are 40 seifim.
One who sows two species of seeds together in Eretz Yisrael receives lashes, as it says, "your field you shall not sow kilayim"—whether one sows or weeds or covers, such as when there was one wheat and one barley or one bean and one lentil placed on the ground and he covered them with earth, whether with his hand or with his foot—he receives lashes. And whether one sows in the ground or in a perforated pot; but one who sows in an unperforated pot receives lashes of rebellion.
