For, etc. The Yerushalmi there records: Chizkiyah said that this applies only to turnips and radishes, but not to other things. R' Yochanan said there is no distinction between turnips, radishes, and other things. What is the case? If the issue is sowing beneath a tree, why specify a vine and radishes, since every other species beneath a tree should be the same? If the issue is that he does not desire their taking root, why specify turnips and radishes, since the same applies to all other things? From R' Chiya's teaching, "such as a bundle of turnips or a bundle of radishes," it follows that the reason is that he does not desire their taking root.
