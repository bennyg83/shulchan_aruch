His field was sown with one vegetable species and he seeks to sow within it rows of gourds—he uproots from the vegetable the place where he sows a row of gourds and separates between it and the vegetable with a furrow, and leaves from the vegetable a width of twelve amot; and makes a second row of gourds and separates between it and the vegetable with a furrow; and so until where he wishes—so that between every two rows of gourds there are twelve amot. But less than this is forbidden because of leaves intertwining from here and here in the vegetable between them, and everything appears as though sown mixed.
