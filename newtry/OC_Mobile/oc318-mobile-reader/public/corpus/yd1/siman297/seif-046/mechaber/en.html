(297—6) Every two species of livestock or wild animal—even though they resemble one another and grow one with another and conceive one from another—since they are two species, they are forbidden because of kilayim. How so? Wolf with dog, village dog (meaning: a dog raised in villages—it is small and resembles a fox) with fox, goats with gazelles, ibexes with sheep, horse with mule, mule with donkey, donkey with wild ass (meaning: a wild donkey growing in the desert)—kilayim one with another.
