His field was sown with wheat and his friend's field beside it was sown with wheat—it is permitted for him to sow one furrow of flax beside his wheat adjoining his friend's field, for the viewer knows there is no way to sow one furrow of flax; and he did not intend this except to test his field whether it is fit to sow flax—if not, it is as though he sows to ruin it. But another species is forbidden to sow between these two fields that are one species until he distances within his own.
