If the bed was six by six and had a border a handbreadth high and a handbreadth wide around, it is permitted to sow in it even eighteen species—three on each border and border and six in the middle, and he distances between each species and species a handbreadth and a half. And he does not sow the head of the turnip inside the border lest it fill it more than this. More than this he does not sow. And it is forbidden to sow in a bed seed species in this manner because they appear as kilayim; but vegetable species, since people do not customarily sow them except little by little, behold it is permitted as explained.
