In what case is this said? In a bed that is in a ruin and there is no seed outside it. But a bed among beds—it is forbidden to sow in it five species, for if he sows on every direction of this bed and on every direction of beds surrounding it, everything will appear mixed. And if he bent the leaves in this bed to here and leaves in the bed beside it from here until they appear separated, it is permitted. And likewise if he made a furrow between each bed and bed, it is permitted.
