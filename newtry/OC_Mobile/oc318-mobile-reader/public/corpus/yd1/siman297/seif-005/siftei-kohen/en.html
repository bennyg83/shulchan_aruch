Such as a wild ox, etc., are permitted. This is difficult. Above, siman 28, seif 4, the Rav and the Levush cite the view that the wild ox is a species of undomesticated animal. If so, it is plainly forbidden with an ordinary ox, as is evident from the Mishnah, the Yerushalmi, and the poskim. How, then, could the Rav rule here without qualification like the Mechaber? The Levush likewise copied the Mechaber's ruling here. This requires study.
