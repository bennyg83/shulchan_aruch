Seed in which another seed became mixed—if one was one in twenty-four, such as a se'ah of wheat mixed in twenty-four se'ah of barley, behold it is forbidden to sow the mixture until he diminishes the wheat or adds to the barley. And if he sowed, he receives lashes. And whatever is kilayim with the seed combines to one in twenty-four. How so? Twenty-four se'ah of wheat in which two kav of barley, two kav of lentils, and two kav of beans became mixed—behold he does not sow all until he diminishes a se'ah of the mixture and sorts part of it, or adds to the wheat—for barley, lentils, and beans are all kilayim with wheat.
