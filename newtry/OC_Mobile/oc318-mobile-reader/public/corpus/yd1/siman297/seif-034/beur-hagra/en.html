And he must distance, etc.; but more than, etc. See the commentary there. That explanation is exceedingly difficult. According to it, the statement in Shabbos that "the Sages determined" has no connection with nourishment from the soil. Nor would there be any reason to require specifically a bed of six handbreadths, since even a quarter-handbreadth would suffice. Likewise, there would be no need for the border to be high, and the discussion of a border that had been diminished would be irrelevant. There are additional major difficulties.
