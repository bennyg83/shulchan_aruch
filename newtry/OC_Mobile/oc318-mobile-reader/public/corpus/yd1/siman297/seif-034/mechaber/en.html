Vegetable species that people do not customarily sow except a little—it is permitted to sow from them even five species within one bed that is six by six handbreadths, provided he sows four species on the four directions of the bed and one in the middle, and distances between species and species like a handbreadth and a half so they do not draw nourishment one from another. But more than five species he does not sow even though he distances between them, for many species in such a bed behold are like planted mixed.
