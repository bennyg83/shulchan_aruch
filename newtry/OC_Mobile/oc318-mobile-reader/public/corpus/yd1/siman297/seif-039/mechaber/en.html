One who wishes to fill his whole garden with many vegetables and not distance between them—he makes the whole garden square beds even six by six, and makes in each bed five circles—four on its four directions and one in the middle—and sows a species in each circle and sows four other species in the four corners of the bed; nine species are found in each bed and they appear separated one from another. And he loses only what is between the circles alone, which he leaves fallow so the circles appear separated from the corners and separated one from another. And if he wished not to lose anything at all—if the circles were sown two, what is between them he sows mixed; and if they were sown mixed, what is between them he sows two, so they appear separated.
