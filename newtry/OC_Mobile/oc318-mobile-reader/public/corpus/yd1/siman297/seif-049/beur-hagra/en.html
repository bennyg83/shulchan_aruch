This is the correct reading, as stated in the Gemara: "But the Sages say that every kind of mule," etc., and "this offspring," etc. If their parents were of one species, one of the offspring would not be kilayim, and it would obviously be forbidden even according to the view that paternal lineage is taken into account. The text must therefore be read as above, as is stated afterward: "But a mule," etc.
