(Siman 297 seif 1) Whether in animals. Shach wrote: implies even with pure one is lashed — not Rambam who holds forbidden only rabbinically. From his words it seems in kilay harevah too Rambam holds exempt with pure and pure — and it does not seem so, for Shulchan Aruch's language is Rambam's language: in kilay harevah per Rambam one is lashed even pure with pure; and he disputes only in kilay driving and plowing, as explained in his words in explaining the mishnah brought in Kesef Mishneh in Hilchot Kilayim chapter 9.
