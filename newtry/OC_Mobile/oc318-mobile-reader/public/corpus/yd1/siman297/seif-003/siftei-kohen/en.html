Forbidden to an Israelite, etc. — Beit Yosef, Taz, and Bach wrote the reason due to speech to a gentile — rabbinic; and this is the essence, as will be explained; but Derishah wrote: I say, etc., that the reason is because children of Noah are commanded regarding grafting trees and crossbreeding animals; and so Rambam stated plainly in chapter 6 of Hilchot Melachim, etc. — and I do not believe such words came from his mouth, for we rule seven commandments were commanded to children of Noah and no more; and truth is the view of R' Eliezer in chapter four deaths (96b) that they were also commanded regarding crossbreeding animals and grafting trees; but we rule like the rabbis; and what is written "and so Rambam stated plainly in chapter 6," etc. — there is no hint or allusion; and on the contrary from Rambam chapter 9 of Hilchot Melachim it is proven plainly they were commanded only in seven: idolatry, cursing God, murder, theft, justice, blessing God, and limb from a live animal — and this is plain.
