It is forbidden only because of kilay zera'im for seeds fit for human food; but bitter herbs and the like from roots not fit except for medicine and the like have no kilay zera'im. Seeds are divided into three parts: one called grain—the five species of grain; the second called legumes, such as beans, peas, lentils, rice, millet, sesame, poppy, and sparrow (meaning: like a pomegranate full of seeds—pashor in vernacular; and sparrow is a legume species—tsitsirik in vernacular), and the like; the third called garden seeds—the rest of seeds not fit for human food but whose fruit of that seed is human food, such as onion seed, garlic seed, grass seed, turnip seed, and the like; and flax seed is in the category of garden seeds.
