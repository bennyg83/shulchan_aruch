One who sows one species and when it grew he saw kilayim in it—if the other species was one in twenty-four in the field, behold he harvests until he diminishes it, because of marit ayin lest they say he sowed kilayim intentionally—whether the other species that grew was grain and legumes in grain, or garden seed in grain and legumes and in garden seed. And if what grew was less than this, he need not diminish it.
