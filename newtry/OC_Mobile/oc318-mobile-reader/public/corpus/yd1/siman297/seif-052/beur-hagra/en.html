Rif, however, rules according to the anonymous Mishnah and the first tanna, as does Rambam, because the Gemara raises many objections against Shmuel and his answers are forced. The Rosh in Hilchot Kilayim appears to agree with Rif, and Tur writes likewise. In the first chapter of Bava Metzia, however, the Rosh rules like Shmuel, and Tur cites him accordingly in Choshen Mishpat 197. The ruling is left unspecified here because it has no practical difference nowadays. In Choshen Mishpat 197:3 and 197:5, and siman 271, the Shulchan Aruch rules like Rif and Rambam, unlike Shmuel.
