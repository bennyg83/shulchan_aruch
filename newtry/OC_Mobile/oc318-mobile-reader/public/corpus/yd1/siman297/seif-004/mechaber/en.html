When all these seed types are sown and grow, the plant is called grass all the time the seed is not recognizable; and it is called vegetable. And among garden seeds there are those customarily sown as fields, such as flax and mustard—these are called seed species; and among garden seeds there are those people do not customarily sow as fields but only small beds, such as turnip, cucumber, beets, onions, coriander (meaning: like large seed—Targum kisbara; coriander white—koliandro in vernacular), cress, bitter herbs, and the like—these are called vegetable species.
