But if, etc. This is implied by the Yerushalmi at the beginning of chapter 8: one might think that one may not place males beside females or females beside males. Scripture therefore states, "You shall not cause your animal to mate with kilayim"; the prohibition is only to cause the mating itself, but one may place males beside females and females beside males. The Yerushalmi asks, what act has he performed? It answers that when he places them together with this intention, the animal acts according to his design. This implies that an intentional act of arranging the mating is nevertheless prohibited.
