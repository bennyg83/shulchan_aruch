It is permitted to sow two rows one beside the other of cucumbers, and two rows beside them of gourds, and two rows of Egyptian beans, and a furrow between each species and species. But he does not sow one row of cucumbers and one row of gourds and one row of Egyptian beans, even though a furrow separates between each species and species, because these species have long leaves that spread and intertwine; and if he sowed them row beside row, everything will mix and appear sown mixed.
