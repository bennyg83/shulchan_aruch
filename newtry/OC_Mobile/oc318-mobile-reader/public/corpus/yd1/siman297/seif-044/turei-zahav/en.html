Israel is forbidden to give his animal to a non-Jew, etc. — They did not say here that if he transgressed and did so he must sell the animal, as they said regarding castrating an animal in Even HaEzer siman 5 — for there the transgression is frequent daily among non-Jews; but here since the matter is uncommon they did not impose a penalty, as I explained; see what I wrote in the preceding siman seif 2.
