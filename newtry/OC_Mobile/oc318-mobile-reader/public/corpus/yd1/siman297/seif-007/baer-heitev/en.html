Intended. Taz wrote: and he said to me from what is written in siman 295: forbidden to sow the mixture until he reduces, etc.; and when he reduces — nevertheless it is fine, for Kesef Mishneh already explained above: there it speaks where it fell unintentionally and became mixed, and afterward at sowing time he does not want that species that mixed to grow; but if he wanted at time of falling or afterward — certainly forbidden even one wheat kernel.
