But if he intended to mix seed with another seed or to sow two species, etc. — so should read. And difficult from seif 5: forbidden to sow the mixture until he reduces, etc.; and when he reduces — nevertheless fine; and Yerushalmi establishes that which is permitted when mixed — via gathering produce, meaning at harvest time it mixed unintentionally; and Raavad explained: if his intent was for eating and he changed to sow it — reduce and suffice; and what they said "intentionally even one wheat" — means intentionally at harvest time to sow it — perforce; and Kesef Mishneh explained above: there it fell unintentionally and became mixed, and afterward at sowing he does not want that species to grow; but if he wanted at falling or afterward — certainly forbidden even one wheat — and this is plain.
