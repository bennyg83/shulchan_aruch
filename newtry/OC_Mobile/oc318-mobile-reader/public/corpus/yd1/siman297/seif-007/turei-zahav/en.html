But if he intended to mix one seed with another or to sow the two species, etc. This is the correct text. The Yerushalmi distinguishes between seed that became mixed unintentionally during gathering and seed deliberately mixed for sowing. If he originally intended the mixture for food and later decided to sow it, reducing the minority species suffices. If he intended at the time of gathering or afterward that both species grow, even one grain creates a prohibition. The Kesef Mishneh similarly explains that the lenient case is where the seed fell unintentionally and he does not desire the mixed species to grow.
