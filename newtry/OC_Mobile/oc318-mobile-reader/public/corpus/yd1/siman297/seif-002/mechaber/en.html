It is forbidden to sow kilayim for a non-Jew; and it is permitted to tell a non-Jew to sow kilay zera'im for him. And it is forbidden to maintain them in his field except that he uproots them; and if he maintained them he does not receive lashes. And it is permitted for an Israelite to sow kilay zera'im with his hand in the Diaspora, and even to mix seeds initially and sow them in the Diaspora—it is permitted. And these matters are words of tradition.
