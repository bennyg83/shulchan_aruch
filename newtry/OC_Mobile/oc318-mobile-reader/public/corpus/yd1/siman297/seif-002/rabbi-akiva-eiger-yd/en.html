(There, se'if katan 3) And I will not believe, etc. Mishneh LaMelech already challenged Beit Yosef concerning this, for the primary view follows Derishah. Rambam rules explicitly in chapter 10 of Hilkhot Melachim that Noahides are commanded concerning mating different animal species and grafting trees. The reference in Derishah to chapter 6 is a printing error and should read chapter 10. Peri Chadash writes likewise in his collected notes on Yoreh De'ah.
