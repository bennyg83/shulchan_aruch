(There s.k. 3) And I will not believe, etc. The Mishneh LaMelekh already challenged the Beit Yosef regarding this, that the essential law is like the Derishah, as the Rambam explicitly ruled in chapter 10 of Hilkhot Melakhim, that Bnei Noach are commanded concerning mating animals and grafting trees. There is a printing error in the Derishah, which wrote chapter 6; it should read chapter 10. So too wrote the Pri Chadash in his likkutim on Yoreh Deah.
