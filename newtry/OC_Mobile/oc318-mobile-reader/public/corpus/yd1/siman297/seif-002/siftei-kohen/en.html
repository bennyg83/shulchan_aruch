And in a place where there are, etc. — so in all books; and so in Taz: workers prohibition — and it is an error; rather must read: workers forbidden, etc.; meaning in a place where they are not Torah scholars, or where concern for licentiousness of workers whose presumption is licentious — concern they will crossbreed by hand; and so Nissim Gaon chapter HaPo'alim; and in Beit Yosef. However from Rif, Rosh, Taz, Rambam, Agudah, and other poskim who omitted this licentiousness of workers — in the Talmud there 91b plainly implies no concern for licentiousness of workers; and it seems they explain that which the Talmud says "and he ruled plainly forbidden not like halacha due to licentiousness of workers" — this too due to licentiousness of workers is also not like halacha.
