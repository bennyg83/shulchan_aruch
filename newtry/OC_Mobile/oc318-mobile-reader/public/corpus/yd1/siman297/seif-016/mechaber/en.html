How much does one distance between two seed species that are kilayim one with another so they are separated one from another? But if they appear sown mixed, behold it is forbidden. And there are many measures in this distancing—all according to the size of the field sown and according to the abundance of leaves and the spread of suckers. How so? His field was sown with one grain species and he seeks to sow beside it in another field another grain species—he distances between them a beit rova, which is about ten amot and a fifth of an amah on ten amot and a fifth of an amah, square, whether from the middle or from the side. And if between them there was not this measure, it is forbidden and he does not receive lashes until they are close within six handbreadths.
