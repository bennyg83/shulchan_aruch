They must keep a distance, etc. Or Zarua wrote that the reason is so that one not always encounter it and be saddened. He was not precise — for the actual reason is on account of foul odor, as implied in "one may not dig" at the beginning of page 25, which teaches: one must keep distance from carcasses, from graves, and from tanneries, etc. — and so Rashi and other commentators explain there; and so Rambam and the Mechaber wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 155 seif 23.
