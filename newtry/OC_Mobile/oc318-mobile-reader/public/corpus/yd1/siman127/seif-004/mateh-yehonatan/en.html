(There seif 3 in the gloss) However some say that in a matter that can be clarified, etc. See there Shach s.k. 24, that Rama should not have written in the language "some say," for it is an explicit Gemara in Kiddushin regarding a blemished person; and this is what it says there: and Abaye said forever that he contradicts him; and what you say — why is he believed? He told him "go and show," etc.; and from this Tosafot learned regarding gentile wine; and these are their words: but in a matter that can be clarified, etc. — come and I will show you — it is forbidden in this; certainly there is concern for his words and to investigate, as we say later in Bava Metzia "he can say go and show," and it can be clarified; therefore he is believed; and at first glance the comparison between libated wine and a blemished person is not understood at all; for there he told him "you are a blemished person," and since he can show and clarify immediately at the time he told him "you are a blemished person," and if he does not clarify it appears somewhat that he believes his words — therefore he must clarify; unlike regarding gentile wine, where he told him "come and I will show you" — he cannot clarify at the time of the statement; and if he does not clarify there is no concern of belief at all, for he can say he does not want to trouble himself to clarify the matter; nevertheless Tosafot compare them to each other; and accordingly Rama wrote in the language "some say," for it is not an explicit Gemara — one can well distinguish between gentile wine and a blemished person as above — investigate:
