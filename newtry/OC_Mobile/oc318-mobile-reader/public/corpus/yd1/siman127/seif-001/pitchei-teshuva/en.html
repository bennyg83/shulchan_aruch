And see in Darkei Moshe who wrote: if his intent is that even before cooking it was in his hand but now it is not called "in his hand" since it was already cooked — then it deals that he did not tell him so the first time; but this law is not necessary, for since he did not take it out from tithe status yet and it is impossible to come to a stumbling block, he need not tell him the first time; therefore it appears his intent is that before cooking it was not in his hand at all, only now they gave it into his hand after cooking — end of his words; and with this the difficulty that Avodat HaTaharah brought from Mahariyat in the name of R.
