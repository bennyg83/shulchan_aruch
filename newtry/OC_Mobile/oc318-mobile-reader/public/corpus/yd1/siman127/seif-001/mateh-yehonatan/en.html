(Siman 127 in Shulchan Aruch seif 1) If it is in his hand. See Shach, who brought the dispute of Rosh and Rashi in explaining "if it is in his hand" — Rashi explained because of migo, and explained "in his hand" as in his hand to libate it; and Rosh explained that he gave it into his hand to guard it, and because it became in the owner's possession even if it is not in his hand to libate it; and it appears to me there is no dispute here, for Rashba in his novellae challenges the view of those who hold Abaye holds he is believed without migo only if it is in his hand — how does Rav Assi bring proof from the Yom Kippur service, and what does the Gemara mean: for if not that he is believed even though we heard him, perhaps he says afterward? For according to all, in such a case he is believed, for whoever is under his hand — even though he finished the service — is believed — end of Rashba's words there; and therefore one may say that certainly in explaining Abaye's words the Rosh explains like Rashi, that he means it is in his hand to libate it, and not like the Raavad, because of Rashba's challenge; and for Rashi the Gemara properly proves from such a case on Yom Kippur; however for us, who establish like Rava, it may well be said: if it is in his hand, even the second or third time he is believed, for the entire reason he is not believed the second time is because if it were true as he said, he should have said so immediately the first time; and all this applies only if they already returned it to him — if so, at the time they returned it, the wine mentioned was libated; but when it is still in his hand, he can say "I will inform him when I return it"; therefore there is no dispute at all — Rashi, who explains the reason because of migo, explains thus according to Abaye; and Rosh wrote this for us, who establish like Rava; and likewise one may say in the Mechaber's words — investigate. [Gloss: and according to this view Darkei Moshe wrote here in the margin of Shach, and according to the explanation there in Shach's words, Shach also agrees to this.]
