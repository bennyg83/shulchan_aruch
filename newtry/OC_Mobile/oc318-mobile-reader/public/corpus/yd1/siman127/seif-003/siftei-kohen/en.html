A minor does not have the status of a witness, etc. In the responsum of the Rivash siman 245 it appears he deals with when the owner of the wine is not silent and admitting, but only that he does not know; but when he is silent due to admission it appears he is believed; and the Rivash follows the view of Rashba and his supporters that I brought above in s.k. 15, that one witness is believed even if the owner of the wine says "I do not know"; and according to this, the rabbi, who holds one witness is not believed when the owner of the wine says "I do not know" — comes to inform us that a minor has no status of witness at all; and there is a practical difference where he says "you knew circumstantial evidence," or where another contradicts him and the owners are not present; or in a matter that can be clarified; or where he says "come and I will show you a gentile libating your wine"; or in a matter where neither issur nor heter was established; or with two pieces, one of issur and one of heter, and all similar cases, where the witness is believed without the silence of the owner of the wine that is like admission — a minor is not believed; however where it is in his hand, a minor is believed even to remove a matter from its presumption to permit or to forbid — such appears in the responsum of the Rivash there; and such is its language: "That which we say in tractate Sukkah — a minor who knows to guard his hands, they feed him by means of pure foods — that is with pure foods in his hand; but regarding what is not in his hand, a minor is not believed to remove a matter from its presumption to heter or to issur" — examine; and see in Orach Chaim at the end of siman 457.
