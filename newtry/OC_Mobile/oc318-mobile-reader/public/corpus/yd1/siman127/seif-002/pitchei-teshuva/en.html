See in responsum Beit Yaakov end of siman 150, who wrote that if one witness told a minor "your wine was libated" and the minor is silent or contradicts him — his law is like an adult, even though he is not believed to testify in prohibitions; nevertheless in such a case he is believed; and the same applies to a woman in such a case, and even regarding a matter that has presumption of prohibition the minor or woman is believed to contradict the witness — see there; [and what Avodat HaTaharah wrote s.k. 3 "and then it is permitted to the whole world," etc. — see in responsum Chatam Sofer siman 121 what he wrote on this].
