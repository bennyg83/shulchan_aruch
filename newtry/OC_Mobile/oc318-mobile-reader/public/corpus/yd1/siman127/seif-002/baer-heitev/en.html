First. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that he is believed even if the owners contradict him. Shach explains that this excludes only silence: 'contradict him' there means saying, 'I do not believe you,' and the like. If they clearly deny the facts, he is not believed. Where the prohibition was already established, such as untithed produce, the owners are believed only if they have the ability to correct it.
Was mistaken. This does not mean only that he mistakenly thought he would lose all his wages. The same law applies if he thought he would lose a small portion of his wages. Taz.
