In what case is this stated? When he is doing with him for free, or even for pay, but in a manner that he will not lose his wages — such as if he says that it was poured as a libation due to coercion, or they hired him for a small matter. But if they hired him for much, and it is in a manner that he loses all his wages — such as if he says that it was poured as a libation through his negligence — he is believed even if it is not in his hand now, and even if he did not tell him the first time he found it. {Rama: And this is specifically when he says it was forbidden in a manner that is revealed and known to all, that he loses his wages, such as by negligence that he mentions; but if he said it in a manner such that it is possible to say that the worker erred and he would not lose his wages — even though he causes him to lose by law — he is not believed (Rabbeinu Yerucham).}
