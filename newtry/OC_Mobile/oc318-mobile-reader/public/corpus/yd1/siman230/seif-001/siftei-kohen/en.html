Some say, etc. In Choshen Mishpat siman 208 it is explained that if one sold something in violation of a communal enactment or cherem, there is doubt whether the sale is valid. The Rosh's words imply that where the enactment was made only as a penalty or prohibition upon the person, the transaction may nevertheless take effect; but where the enactment was directed at nullifying the transaction itself, the sale is void. Therefore each enactment must be judged according to its language and purpose.
