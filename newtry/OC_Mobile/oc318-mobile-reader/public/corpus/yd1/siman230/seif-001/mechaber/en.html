It contains one seif. They release oaths and vows (meaning: that a person makes between himself and himself), even if he swore by mentioning the Name (so they practiced according to the Talmud and poskim; see Beit Yosef).
{Rama: Nevertheless, one should not ask about it l'chatchila except under duress; and see above, siman 243 (Geonim and Tur). Every vow whose release involves a hint of transgression — it may not be released (Rav David Cohen and Rivash, siman 432). There is one who says: anyone who swore not to do something — for example, he swore not to sell a certain object, and he transgressed and sold it — his sale has no validity, since he transgressed his oath (Mordechai, hagahot on Shevuot).}
