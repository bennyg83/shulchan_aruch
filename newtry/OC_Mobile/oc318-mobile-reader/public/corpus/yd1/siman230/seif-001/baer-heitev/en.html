It is written in the responsum of Maharam Mintz: nevertheless, when it comes to my hands in such a case to permit the multitude of transgressors in vows, I impose upon them to fast on a Monday-Thursday-Monday, and the like according to the person and according to the vow; and likewise to give money for a mitzvah matter, gold or the like according to his wealth, so they will not treat vows lightly, saying "what do I care — if I swear whatever I want I will go to a sage and he will release me"; and likewise I heard from my teachers — end quote; and so it is proper to do. Shach.
