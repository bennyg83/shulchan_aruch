Not to sell. See in the responsum of Noda BiYehudah tinyana part Even HaEzer siman 129 ot 4, who wrote that perforce Mordechai speaks only of one who swore absolutely not to sell this thing, for then it is impossible in any manner that he sell except that he transgress the oath — behold the substance of the purchase becomes forbidden; but if he swore not to sell except with the knowledge of so-and-so, and he transgressed and sold before he asked so-and-so — then the purchase could have been permitted, only what he did without so-and-so's knowledge he did as forbidden; and behold this is similar to one who slaughters on Shabbat and Yom Kippur, etc. See there.
