And from the majority onward. The Shach explains that even if he cut it limb by limb and cast the pieces to dogs, whatever emerges after the majority has emerged requires burial. What emerged beforehand does not require burial even after the majority later emerges, according to the view that sanctity takes effect only prospectively. This is also implied by Tosafot.
Retroactively. What he cut before the majority emerged may be cast to dogs, but from the point that the majority emerges onward it requires burial. Shach.
