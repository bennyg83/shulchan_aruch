He may cut, etc. For whatever has not mostly emerged has not been sanctified. See above at the end of siman 313.
And from its majority onward, etc. Meaning: even when he cut it limb by limb and threw it to the dogs, nevertheless what emerges afterward, from the majority onward, requires burial. But what already emerged, even if afterward the majority emerged, need not be buried according to the view that it is sanctified only going forward; and so it is explained in Tosafot.
And the one after it is a bekhor. Since it is not considered born, it is not considered the petter rechem, and the one after it is the bekhor.
And some say, etc. For although it is not considered born, it is nevertheless considered the petter rechem.
And she is exempt from bekhorah. Meaning the one that comes after it.
And some say that it is not, etc. Therefore, even where he cuts it limb by limb and leaves it until the majority is completed, he throws to the dogs what emerged up to the majority, and from the majority onward it requires burial.
