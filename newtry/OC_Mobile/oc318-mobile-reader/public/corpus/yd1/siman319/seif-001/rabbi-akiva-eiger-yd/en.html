(Siman 319 Shulchan Aruch seif 1) All the limbs require burial. And he also committed a prohibition retroactively in cutting a limb, for it is called inflicting a blemish in kodashim if it is not a stillbirth. And it is implied in Tosafos that if he cuts and throws limb by limb, that even regarding a viable child it is permitted, for when its majority comes out it will not be holy retroactively, only that which came out and is extant, but not that which is not in the world at the time of the emergence of its majority. And it is difficult for me: nevertheless how is he permitted to cut a limb that came out, lest before he throws it to the dogs the rest will be born, and this limb too will be holy, and he inflicts a blemish in a bechor; for we are concerned lest it die even in a short time, and all the more so regarding birth, for she is standing for that, and especially in a woman having difficulty giving birth, which implies that it is the time of birth — and why should we not be concerned that immediately before he throws the limb to the dogs the child will be born, and he will have transgressed retroactively the inflicting of a blemish in a bechor?
