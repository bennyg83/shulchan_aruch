(Siman 319, Shulchan Aruch, seif 1) All the limbs require burial. Cutting off a limb may also retroactively constitute inflicting a blemish upon sacred property if the fetus is viable. Tosafot imply that if one cuts and immediately discards each limb, even a viable fetus is permitted, because when most of it emerges only the parts then existing become sanctified. I find this difficult. How may one cut the emerged limb, since before it is discarded the remainder may be born, causing this limb to become sanctified retroactively and making the cutting an inflicted blemish? We are concerned that death may occur even within a short time, and birth is all the more foreseeable, especially during obstructed labor.
