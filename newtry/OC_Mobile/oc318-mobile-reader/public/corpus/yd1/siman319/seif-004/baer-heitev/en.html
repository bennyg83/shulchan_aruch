After it. The Shach wrote that according to the master's view one must say that here it speaks where afterward it was not born entirely, but the remaining half emerged through the wall, etc. But if after half emerged as the majority of a limb it then emerged entirely, and after some time she gave birth to a fetus, it is obvious that it is exempt from bekhorah. What difference does it make that at first half emerged as the majority of a limb? In the end afterward it was born entirely, and this is simple; end quote.
