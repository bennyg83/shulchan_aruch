To affix a mezuzah. See Magen Avraham siman 19 s.k. 1, who wrote that although we hold mezuzah is an obligation of dwelling — that as long as he does not dwell within he is exempt from mezuzah — nevertheless he blesses when affixing, for the default is one affixes a mezuzah in order within it; and it is possible if he affixed before dwelling within — when he enters to dwell he blesses "Who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to dwell in a house that has a mezuzah" — end quote — see there. And it seems to me I saw in book of R. Yosef in Orach Chaim that he disputes him; and see above siman 291 s.k. 3.
