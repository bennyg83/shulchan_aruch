There is a doorway from this to that. And it is not known which is considered the entrance or the exit — therefore we follow the hinge identification. And Ateret Zekenim wrote: a doorway through which one enters from the main house to our winter room — it seems to me one makes the mezuzah on the right of the entrance from the main house to the winter room, for our main use is in our winter rooms, and we do not at all consider the hinge identification: whether the hinge is outside or inside — one makes the mezuzah on the right of the entrance. End quote, and it is simple. And see further in Beit Yosef regarding these laws.
