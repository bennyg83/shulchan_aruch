The hinge. The Taz writes that in such a case there is doubt whether the right side is determined by entry or by exit, so we follow the hinge indication. From this one may learn the law of a mezuzah on a doorway opening from a house into a courtyard: do we follow the right side of one entering the courtyard, or the right side of one entering the house from the courtyard? If the courtyard is enclosed on all sides and has no entrance other than through the house, it is regarded as a room. The hinge indication is disregarded, and the mezuzah is placed on the right of one entering from the house into the courtyard. If the courtyard has another opening to an alley or public domain in addition to the house opening, we follow the hinge indication. The side on which the hinge indicates that the door opens inward is treated as the room, and the mezuzah is placed on the right of one entering it. Responsa Maharil further discusses a winter house and a large room, each opening to a courtyard, with a small room between them that has one doorway to the winter house and another to the large room. For the small room's doorways we follow each hinge indication. If the hinge of the doorway to the winter house is in the winter house and that of the doorway to the large room is in the large room, both mezuzos are placed according to entry into those respective rooms. If both hinge indications are inside the small room, both mezuzos are placed on the right of entry into the small room, whether from the winter house or from the large room. If the large room had no opening to the courtyard, we would not follow the small room's hinge indications. The small room would be subordinate to the large room, and the mezuzos would be placed on the right entering the small room from the winter house and on the right entering the large room from the small room. The Levush writes that on the doorway from the house into our winter room, the mezuzah is placed on the right when entering the winter room, because that is our principal place of use. The hinge indication is entirely disregarded. Whether it is outside or inside, the mezuzah is always placed on the right of entry. The Shach writes that this is obvious. See siman 40 and siman 50.
