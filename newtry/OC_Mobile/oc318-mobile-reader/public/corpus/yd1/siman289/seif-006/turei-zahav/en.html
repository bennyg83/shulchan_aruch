It must be upright. In the Gemara it says: if he made it like a bolt it is invalid — and Rashi explained: that he inserted it like a bar that enters a hole; rather it must be upright. And Rabbenu Tam — who is the “some say” mentioned shortly — holds the opposite: that in an upright position it is invalid, and that is like a bolt. And Mordechai wrote in his name that he said: when I build an ark, if I merit I will enlarge it according to how a sefer Torah is placed sitting — like the sefer Torah that was placed in the ark; and this is the order of its reading — end quote.
