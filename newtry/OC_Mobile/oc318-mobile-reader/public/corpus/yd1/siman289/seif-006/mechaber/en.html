It must stand upright—its length along the length of the doorpost; and he should intend that Shema—that is, the end of the rolling—be toward the outside. {Rama: And so they practice (Beit Yosef). But some say it is invalid standing upright; rather it must lie with its length along the width of the doorpost (Tur and poskim in the name of Rabbenu Tam). And the meticulous satisfy both views and place it on a slant diagonally (Tur, Hagahos Maimoniyos, Maharil, and Terumat HaDeshen siman 52); and so it is proper to practice, and so they practice in these lands—and one should intend that the head of the mezuzah, namely Shema, be toward the inside and the last line toward the outside.}
