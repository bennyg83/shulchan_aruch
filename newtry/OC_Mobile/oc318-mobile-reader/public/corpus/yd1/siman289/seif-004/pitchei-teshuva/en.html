In the place where one makes the hole. See in book Chomot Yerushalayim siman 146, who wrote an incident occurred in a study hall of men and women with two doors on the women's side — hinges were affixed from both sides and ovens in both rooms; it appears we consider the entrance from the men's to the women's side — for a woman in the Temple courtyard, from where? Unlike from men's side the attendant goes frequently with cloths and candles and the like; also sometimes community heads gather together in the women's section at midday. However one may say the main door is because of a word — and if so the entrance is from women's to men's. But truly one may say it is far that women go out at the hour the baby is brought — they only stand in the women's section and pass hand to hand alone, and there is no entry here to obligate because of this; and even if the baby is passed, even a child of education he is not, and he does not go but is passed — see there, for he did not raise a clear matter here. And see in She'elat Ya'avetz part 1 siman 70; and see in responsum Teshuvah MeAhavah part 1 siman 61 what he wrote on this.
