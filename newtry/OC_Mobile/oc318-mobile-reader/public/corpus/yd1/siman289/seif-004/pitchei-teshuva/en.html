At the place where he makes the hole. Chomot Yerushalayim discusses a doorway between the men's and women's sections of a beit midrash where the direction of entry was unclear. Frequent traffic by the attendant favored entry toward the women's section, while circumcision processions might suggest the reverse. Since the infant is merely passed hand to hand and the women ordinarily remain in their section, he reaches no definite conclusion. See Yaavetz and Teshuvah MeAhavah.
