And in every part of an opening, etc. — for in this there is doubt which of them is right for entry or exit; therefore we follow hinge recognition. And from this one may learn regarding mezuzah placed in an opening open from house to courtyard — if one follows the right of one entering the house, or the right of one entering from courtyard to house. And the law is thus: if the courtyard is closed on all sides without an opening except from the house into it — then the courtyard is like a room and we do not care about door-hinge recognition; even if hinge recognition were inside the house, we follow the right of one entering from house to courtyard, and there one places the mezuzah. But if there is an opening from courtyard to another alley or public domain besides the house's opening into it — then we follow hinge recognition; on whichever side there is hinge recognition of the opening, it is considered like a house, and we follow the right of one entering into it. And so it is in responsum of Maharil siman 54; and I have seen many puzzled over this many times — therefore I wrote it. And it is further explained in responsum of Maharil there — if there is a winter house and a large room, each has an opening to the courtyard; and in that room there is a small room with two openings — one to the winter house and one to the large room — we follow the openings of the small room after their hinge recognition; if hinge recognition of the opening to the winter house is in the winter house, and hinge recognition of the opening to the large room is in the large room — he is obligated to place both mezuzot of the small room's openings — one to the right of one entering the winter house, and the second to the right of one entering from small room to large. And if their hinge recognition is inside the small room — one places both to the right of one entering the small room, whether from the winter house or from the large room. Whereas if there were no opening from large room to courtyard — we would not follow hinge recognition in the small room where it is; rather the small room would be considered part of the large room, and one places mezuzah on the right of one entering from winter house to small room, and on the right of one entering from small room to large. And Beit Yosef brought this matter in name of an Ashkenazic responsum, and it is not understood — therefore I explained it; and also that this is practical halachah widespread by mouth.
