(Shulchan Aruch, seif 5) It is invalid. Responsa Maharam Alshashon at the end writes that even if the mezuzah was affixed properly, and afterward the entire doorway together with the mezuzah was removed and later rebuilt into the wall with the mezuzah still attached, this is considered ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy. According to Magen Avraham, Orach Chayyim siman 15, however, this should apply specifically when it was set into another house, where that constitutes a new act. If returned to its original place, it may not be considered ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy.
