(Shulchan Aruch, seif 5) It is invalid. See Responsa Mahara Sasson at its end, who rules that even if the mezuzah was affixed properly, and afterward the entire doorway was removed together with the mezuzah, the wall rebuilt, and the doorway with its mezuzah reinstalled as it had been, this too is disqualified as 'made and not from that which is already made.' According to Magen Avraham, Orach Chaim, siman 15, it appears that this applies specifically when affixed to another house, for there it constitutes a new act of installation; but if returned to its original location, it is not disqualified on that ground.
