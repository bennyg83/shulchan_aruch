The place of its affixing, how it is affixed, and its blessing. In it are 6 seifim.
When he comes to affix it, he places it in a tube of reed or of any matter and affixes it in its place; and he recites the blessing "...who commanded us to affix a mezuzah." And he does not recite a blessing at the time of writing. (And one who affixes two or three mezuzot recites one blessing for all of them) (Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of the Rashba).
