When he comes to affix it. Birkei Yosef questions whether one who removed a mezuzah for examination and intends to replace it must recite a new blessing. Pitchei Teshuva compares this to removing a tallit or tefillin with intent to put them back, but suggests that inspection differs because the mezuzah may prove invalid. If it fell by itself and is reaffixed, a new blessing is certainly required.
