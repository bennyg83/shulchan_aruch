One does not bless at the time of its writing. For any mitzvah whose performance is not the completion of the mitzvah — one blesses only at the completion of the mitzvah. And Perishah wrote: the reason one does not bless "She’hecheyanu" is because it does not come from time to time; and tefillin and a garment that one wears are different, for one blesses over them "She’hecheyanu" (as explained in Orach Chayim siman 223) because it is due to joy; and regarding mezuzah, one does not bless that God has privileged him with a house, but rather on the mitzvah of fixing the mezuzah — until here. And see above siman 28 note 5.
