And it is not comparable to tefillin, where a left-handed person places them on his right hand — as explained in Orach Chayim siman 27 seif 6 — for mezuzah is different: it is to guard all members of the household; unlike tefillin which are for himself alone. And it appears likewise if only one person dwells in the house and he is left-handed, or if all the members of the household are left-handed — for mezuzah is not comparable to tefillin which are a bodily commandment, and therefore we follow his own right; unlike mezuzah which is only an obligation of the house, and therefore we follow the right of all people; and this is why the Talmud and authorities made no distinction between whether the household members are left-handed or not.
