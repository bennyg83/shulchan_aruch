Within a handbreadth, etc. — there, and there 33b.
At the beginning, etc.; and if, etc.; and it, etc. — Rambam; and he holds R' Yosei does not dispute R' Yehudah except in what he said regarding raising from the ground a handbreadth — he said this refers to height; and furthermore he holds that nevertheless ab initio one should place it at the beginning of the third; and R' Yosei's words are after the fact specifically. But Rosh and Shulchan Piskei dispute him in all this, and hold that ab initio from one-third to the lintel.
There is no distinction, etc. — see Shach.
And if he fixed it, etc. — from what is stated there "like whom — like R' Meir — you may say," etc. It implies it is entirely exempt; and if it were valid after the fact, he would not have been exempt.
