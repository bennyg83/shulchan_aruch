Changed. The Shach wrote that the master's view implies that even if he placed it behind the door it is valid after the fact, but the Rambam's view appears to be that it is invalid, and so wrote the Levush. Therefore one should return and fix it properly. In Ma'adanei Melech he wrote that those houses open to the gentile street, where for this reason they place the mezuzot behind the door, have what to rely on in the words of the Tur and the Rav; end quote.
A tefach. The Beit Yosef wrote: but after the fact it is valid without distancing it from above. If the doorway is very high, one should not place the mezuzah higher than his shoulders, for then it is not visible to his eyes when entering and leaving; end quote.
Left-handed. The Shach wrote: it appears that the same applies if only one person lives in the house and he is left-handed, or if all members of the house are left-handed; even so, he should place it on the right side of ordinary people. This is not comparable to tefillin, which are mitzvot upon his body, and therefore we follow his own right side; but mezuzah is an obligation of the house, and therefore we follow the right side of ordinary people. So too appears to be the master's view, that one always places it on the right, and so is the practice; end quote.
