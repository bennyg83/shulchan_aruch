Left-handed. Shach writes that even if a person lives alone in the house and is left-handed, or all the members of the household are left-handed, the mezuzah is nevertheless placed on the right side as defined for ordinary people. Tefillin is a mitzvah upon the person's body, so we follow his own dominant hand. Mezuzah is an obligation of the house, so we follow the right side for people generally. This also appears to be Rama's view, that the mezuzah is always placed on the right. This is the custom.
