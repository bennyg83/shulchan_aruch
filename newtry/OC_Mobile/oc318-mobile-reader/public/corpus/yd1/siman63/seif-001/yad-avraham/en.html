(Siman 63 seif 1) Meat found in the hand of an idolater, etc., if the meat sellers were Israelites — permitted, etc. See Mishnah Yomit at the end of general rule 32, who brought in the name of several poskim that where sellers and slaughterers are Israelites they declare kosher regarding found in the hand of an idolater; and the same regarding sending or depositing in the hand of an idolater without a seal — permitted, not like Shach. And there s.k. 40 regarding sending a bird via an idolater to slaughter — he wrote: wherever there is no benefit in the matter we are not concerned for exchange. And there s.k. 4 he wrote: wherever it was seized like a thief and to forge — the law is thus.
