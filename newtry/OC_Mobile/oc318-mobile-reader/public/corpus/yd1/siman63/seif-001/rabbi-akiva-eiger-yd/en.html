(Siman 63 Taz s.k. 1) For it is a rabbinic prohibition in general. In Nekudat HaKesef and Peri Chadash and Turei Zahav they wrote that even regarding a d'oraisa prohibition — such as found in the market among a majority of idolater slaughterers — an am ha'aretz is believed.
