Teviyat ayin. This is like Rav, who holds that meat that disappeared from sight is forbidden without siman and teviyat ayin; and it is explained in Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban that even any person is believed in this. And one may ask: what is different from a lost object, that we do not return it by teviyat ayin except for a Torah scholar?
