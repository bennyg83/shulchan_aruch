The law of meat that is found and whose identity is unknown. In it are 2 seifim.
Meat that is found tossed in the market — one follows the majority, for everything that separates from the majority is presumed to have separated from the majority. If most of the sellers were idolaters — forbidden; if most of the sellers were Israelites — permitted. And likewise meat that is found in the hand of an idolater and it is not known from whom he took it: if the meat sellers were Israelites — permitted. This is Torah law. And the Sages already forbade all meat that is found, whether in the market or in the hand of an idolater, even though all the sellers and all the slaughterers are Israelites, except for one who bought meat and left it in his house and it disappeared from sight — forbidden, unless he had a siman upon it, or he had teviyat ayin upon it and he recognizes it with certainty that it is this one, or it was tied and sealed.
