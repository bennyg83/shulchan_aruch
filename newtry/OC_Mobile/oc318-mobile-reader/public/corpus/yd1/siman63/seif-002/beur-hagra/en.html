He hung, etc., and if he hung it, etc. He explains that which is at the end of chapter 19, when the pieces fell to the ground:
And there are those who permit, etc. He explains not like Rav, for all those mishnayot he brings there — nine shops, and found in the borders not like Rav — and even though we answered that these are forced answers, he says there: and Rav, how does he eat, etc. And there is no difficulty where do we eat among Israelites; and the incident of Rav Kahana there is not like Rav; and likewise in chapter 2 of Bava Metzia the incident of R' Huna and the incident of R' Ami; and that which is at the end of chapter 10, disciples of Rav — and that which is in chapter 2 of Bava Metzia where ravens took it — in that we agree that presumably we do not rule like Rav, but only when he found it in the place where he left it [and this is what is written 'if he found it,' etc.; and see Sefer HaTerumah siman 47]. And those incidents there where they said to him: do you have a siman inside it, etc. — deal with a city that is mostly idolaters — and this is what is written in the hagahah:
And even if, etc., and as written regarding found in the hand of an idolater — even though according to Rav it is all the more so for us; and all the more so regarding found in the market: (Likkut) And some say, etc., in the place where he left it.
