And the Sages already forbade all meat, etc. And it is from the Gemara Chullin 95a: Rav said — meat, once it disappeared from sight, is forbidden; and Rashi explained: forbidden lest it was exchanged for nevelah. And although there are disputants, Beit Yosef wrote regarding practical halacha: since Rif and Rambam agree to rule like Rav, one must forbid meat that disappeared from sight that has neither siman nor teviyat ayin, unless he hung it on a nail and the like such that it is impossible for a creeping thing to take and replace, end of his words; and therefore he ruled thus in Shulchan Aruch.
