The law of one who forbids himself benefit from his son for the sake of Torah study; it contains four se'ifim. One who forbids himself from benefit of his son so that the son not neglect his study on his account — the son is permitted to fill for him a barrel of water, to light the lamp for him, and to roast for him a small fish; and likewise he is permitted to buy for him from the market what he needs, if he is a person who is not accustomed to buy for himself, or if she is a woman who forbade benefit from her son:
