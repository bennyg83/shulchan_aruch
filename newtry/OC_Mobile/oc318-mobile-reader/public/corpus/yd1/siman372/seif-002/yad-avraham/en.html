I saw that Rabbi Ozer, of blessed memory, questioned the Rama from Tosafot and Rashba in Berachot 20, who derive from an unattended corpse that human dignity overrides a kohen's impurity prohibition. Penei Yehoshua argues that this case also contains the positive mitzvah of burial, and cannot serve as a general source. His alternative explanation of the Gemara is strained. The Lechem Mishneh in chapter 12 of Mourning provides a basis through which the Rambam's position in the laws of kilayim can be reconciled, as I explain elsewhere.
