(Likkut) But one who passes... a kohen who lies. See Tosafot, Berachot 20a. Ramban in Torat HaAdam writes that some texts say this explicitly, and Rav Hai Gaon rules accordingly. On that approach the earlier explanation limiting the case to rabbinic impurity is rejected, because every prohibition of impurity is itself a prohibition not equally applicable to all Jews. Rashi's explanation there appears more correct, however, consistent with Yevamot concerning when a positive commandment overrides a negative commandment.
