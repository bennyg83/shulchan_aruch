To learn Torah. See Mareh HaPanim on the Yerushalmi, Berakhot chapter 3, s.v. Rabbi Yose havi yativ matni, who wrote that if a kohen is sitting and learning in a house where there is only rabbinic tumah, such as where the end of the tumah is destined to leave, according to the Shach brought by Be'er Heitev s.k. 2 and as I wrote there s.k. 3, perhaps he is not obligated to stop and leave; see there. See Chavot Yair responsa, end of siman 112, who wrote that he saw in several communities on Yom Tov, when news came that a child had died near the synagogue, all the kohanim immediately ran outside and canceled the mitzvah of dukhaning. There is room to question this, since there is only rabbinic tumah here, as the Shach wrote; also, the kohen who remains is not actively violating a negative commandment, and one might say there is no transgression at all, even of a positive commandment, etc.; see there. However, if the news came after the shaliach tzibbur had called "Kohanim," the matter requires study.
