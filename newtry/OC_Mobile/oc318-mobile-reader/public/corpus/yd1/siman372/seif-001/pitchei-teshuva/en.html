To study Torah. Mareh HaPanim on Yerushalmi Berachot chapter 3, s.v. R. Yose was sitting and teaching, writes that where a kohen is sitting and learning in a house containing only rabbinic tumah, such as where the concern is merely that the tumah will eventually be taken out according to Shach's approach, it is possible that he need not interrupt his learning and leave. Chavot Yair, end of siman 112, reports seeing in several communities on Yom Tov that when word arrived that a child had died near the synagogue, all the kohanim immediately ran outside and thereby lost the mitzvah of Birkat Kohanim. He writes that there is room to question this practice, since according to Shach only rabbinic tumah is involved, and remaining there may not constitute an active violation at all. If the report arrives after the chazzan has already called "Kohanim," however, the matter requires further study.
