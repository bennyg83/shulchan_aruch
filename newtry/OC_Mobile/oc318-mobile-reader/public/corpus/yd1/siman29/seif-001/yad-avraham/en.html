(Siman 29, seif 1) Eight types of treifot, and their mnemonic DaN ChaNeK NeFeSH. There is great confusion here in Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe; and I said I too will answer my portion:
We learn in the Talmud, beginning of chapter Elu Treifot: Ulla said: eight types of treifot were said to Moses at Sinai; and they are these eight of Shulchan Aruch here. And the Talmud says: excluding laktah of Rachi bar Papa, who said laktah in the milt ; treifah; and Rashi explained Ulla disputes him, for it is not included in these. And he further wrote: and if you ask, it is included in nekuvah, etc. ; behold perforation of the milt is permitted. And Beit Yosef wondered on Rambam and Tur, who counted only these eight ; for they ruled like Rachi; if so, it should say nine types of treifot. And his answer is very strained, as Darkei Moshe wrote ; see there.
And in my humble opinion to settle correctly: Rambam and Tur hold laktah of the milt is included in netulah; for laktah ; we hold the majority of simanim (rubo simanim), meaning nismases (dissolved); and we hold nismases of flesh ; we see it as though it is not, as found in the Talmud, beginning of Elu Treifot (daf 53); and the poskim ruled, and Taz in siman 44, s.k. 5, and s.k. 50, and siman 55, s.k. 10: wherever treifah is through removal (nitul), nismases ; behold it is as though it is not, and removed through illness. If so, well when the milt nismases ; it is removal through illness; and so we hold in siman 44, s.k. 5 and 6: when the milt shrank, or was removed entirely through illness ; treifah; if so, well laktah ; meaning nismases ; is included in removal through illness, for it is as though removed and is not. And this appears to me very correct.
However the Talmud analyzed well that Ulla disputes Rachi ; for laktah is not included in any one of them; one cannot say it is in netulah that he said ; for we taught (daf 54): the kidneys were removed ; kosher; and how did Ulla simply [say] kidneys removed are treifah? And if it refers to laktah ; meaning nismases ; which is netulah, Ulla did not inform us of this anywhere; and he should have explained that we hold an amora must explain his words (and likewise in Tosafot, Menachot (daf 39b), who said he disputes because an amora must explain his words). And the Talmud holds Ulla, who counted netulah, refers to other organs that become treifah through netulah, such as liver ; Rambam, siman 41; but it does not refer at all to the milt ; how did he simply [contradict] the mishnah? And if he holds treifah through laktah, he should have said explicitly, and not included its treifot in netulah generally. Rather learn: it does not refer to the milt ; he disputes Rachi. And it is also proven in Beit Yosef and Rashba that the Talmud must hold Ulla does not hold any treifah in the milt ; even shrinkage; for if he held it is treifah ; when he counted netulah, it would refer also to the milt; for if it shrank, behold it was removed; if so, laktah too would be included; and whence does he exclude laktah? Rather it is proven the Talmud holds it is impossible to include any treifah of the milt in netulah that he said; and that is because he should explain and not conceal contrary to the mishnah. But Rambam, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch ruled explicitly in siman 44 that shrinkage is treifah, and laktah is treifah; if so, well it is in netulah. And they already explained that which we taught "the kidneys were removed ; kosher" ; in a newly created [animal] thus, as written siman 44, s.k. 6.
End of the matter: since it is well included, it is included; only the Talmud analyzed that Ulla should not have concealed it. But Rambam and Taz explicitly explained treifah of the milt, and well included it here, as written.
