Derusah. Explanation: an animal mauled by a lion, and so in siman 57.
Nekuvah. Explanation: perforation of the membrane of the brain in the siman after this; and torbats haveshet in siman 33; and perforation of the trachea in siman 34; and perforation of the lung in siman 36; and perforation of the gallbladder in siman 42; and perforation of the heart in siman 40; and perforation of the spleen in siman 43; and perforation of the womb in siman 45; and perforation of the small intestines in siman 46; and perforation of stomach, kereis, mesas, and beit hakosot in siman 48; and perforation of the craw in siman 49.
Chaserah. At the beginning of its formation; and lacking the testicles, in siman 35.
Netulah. Removal of the lower or upper jaw in siman 33; and removal of the liver in siman 41; and removal of the junction of the gidim in siman 56; and included in netulah is also geludah in siman 59.
Keru'ah. Explanation: the majority of flesh covering the kereis was torn, in siman 48.
Nefulah. Actual falling, in siman 58.
Pesukah. Explanation: severing of the spinal cord in siman 32; and severing of the windpipe in siman 34.
Shevurah. Explanation: majority of its ribs were broken in siman 54; and crushing of the skull in the siman after this; and uprooting a rib in siman 54; and all treifot are included in these eight — Shach. And Rambam wrote: you have none explicit except derusah; therefore they were stringent in it, and any doubt one will doubt in derusah — forbidden; and the other types of treifot — their doubts are permitted. And Beit Yosef wondered that he found no doubt of treifah that would be permitted; and I wonder: if so, why does the Gemara in Chullin (daf 43b) not challenge immediately? And see Tosafot and Ra'osh. And see in Pri Chadash and Taz; and see in Bach, who also challenged on this (Maharam Lublin).
