Eight types of treifot and their mnemonic. In it is one seif.
There are eight types of treifot, and their mnemonic is DaN ChaNeK NeFeSH ; derusah (mauled), nekuvah (perforated), chaserah (lacking), netulah (removed), keru'ah (torn), nefulah (fallen), pesukah (severed), shevurah (broken). (This siman is in Semag page 141b, and Beit Yosef did not bring it.)
