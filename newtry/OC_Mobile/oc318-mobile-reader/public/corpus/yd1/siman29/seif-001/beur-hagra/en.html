Eight, etc. Even though in the mishnah eighteen treifot are taught and all are included in these eight, as explained — all perforations we count as one, and they are ten; and even though in the Gemara they said eight perforations — that is if we count mesas and beit hakosot as one, and exclude gallbladder and windpipe severance we also count as one, as explained, and these eight remain. And in the Gemara they answered there in the order they said in the mishnah — only derusah; and here he counts according to the mnemonic, and it is from Semag's words; and likewise in Seger — and seven heard matters (shivah shema'ata) are included in them alone, laktah (diseased spleen), as explained; and Rashi there — but we hold like Rachi — and so he says there siman 55b: halacha like Rachi, excluding Ulla; and Beit Yosef already noted this; see Darkei Moshe there; and there:
(Likut) Eight, etc. And even though they said there excluding laktah, and we hold like Rachi as above 55b, nevertheless laktah is included in chaserah according to Rif's explanation — like nismases (dissolved); and even though removal of the milt is permitted — laktah is worse, like milt that shrank; and even though they said excluding laktah — that is for the West, who say whatever disqualifies a healthy [animal] is permitted for milt; therefore it is not in chaserah — for if so, healthy would also be disqualified like perforated; but according to the conclusion that healthy is also disqualified, it is in chaserah like diseased lung (ri'ah); and likewise anything dissolved is among the treifot (end).
Chaserah. The lung that is lacking; even though there siman 47b they said only according to R' Shimon, and we do not hold like R' Shimon as written siman 49a — nevertheless not specifically according to R' Shimon, for even according to Rabbanan we find lacking, as written siman 47a: lacking, etc.; and see above siman 35, s.k. 6, and siman 36, s.k. 14. Rather, in any case he answers well, and with this the sugya there siman 42 is settled and we need not distance to R' Shimon as Tosafot wrote there s.v. afik, etc.; and see Shach s.k. 4. And likewise we find lacking in spinal column and skull; and Tosafot (there s.v. ve'amar) strained themselves on skull — and see Ramban and Rashba.
