Eight — in truth they are more than eight, and it is fitting to be nine; and the mnemonic ד"ן חנ"ק נפ"ש — and laktah in the milt (kulya) is treifah, as explained below siman 44; and Shulchan Aruch used the Gemara's language; see Peleti siman 49 on this.
Types — for each one includes many treifot; and every law of nekuvah in all organs is a subcategory of nekuvah; and likewise in all matters; and in detail Rambam enumerated them in chapter 9 of Hilchot Shechitah — and they are seventy; and the mnemonic is ול"א יחי"ה.
Treifot — treifah is from what is written in the Torah: and flesh in the field, treifah you shall not eat — which means an animal or bird mauled by a predator. Only according to tradition the verse did not speak only of the present case and common occurrence; but we learn from it every illness and pain that causes weakness and death to living creatures, such as derusah — treifah; and they received that they are the seventy treifot mentioned.
They are — and in these treifot there is certainly doubt; and certainly there is no living animal (ein chayah); and the Gemara says at the beginning of chapter Elu Treifot: the reason is as written: this is the animal that you may eat — a living animal eat, and what is not a living animal do not eat; from here for treifah that is not alive. However Rambam in Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot wrote: since it is written "to a dog you shall cast it" — meaning it stands to be cast to a dog because it is dead; and from it we derive for other treifot; and Tur copied this. And the Rav Taz wondered, and more than the author — why did Rambam change from the Gemara's derivation? And likewise the reverse; see Peleti what he wrote at length; there too you will find an answer to the difficulty on Rashi in the Torah on this matter.
And their mnemonic — in the Gemara it is said halacha leMoshe miSinai they were transmitted; see Peleti that it should read: they were transmitted thus in general for treifot — nekuvah, etc.; and Chazal, according to their wisdom and depth of understanding, in pilpul of Torah and similar, clarified the general principle — how its particulars branch from it; and each will be explained in its place on its own basis; therefore there is no place here to pilpul in it.
