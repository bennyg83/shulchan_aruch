(Hagahah) It is found in the Gemara, beginning of chapter Elu Treifot (daf 43a): Ulla said: eight treifot were said to him at Sinai, etc. — and they are these eight that Tur and Shulchan Aruch enumerated; and the Gemara says excluding laktah of Rachi bar Papa; and Rashi explained: excluding laktah — Ulla disputes him. And Beit Yosef wondered on Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilchot Shechitah, who counted only these eight — for according to the one who rules laktah is treifah, there would be nine; and likewise it is difficult on Semag, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch. And in my humble opinion there is no difficulty when one analyzes: why does the Talmud say excluding laktah of Rachi bar Papa, and not say simply excluding laktah? Rather certainly the Talmud holds this laktah is halacha, and there is no one who disputes it; and that is why the Talmud challenges there the tanna of the house of R' Yishmael, who counts eighteen treifot, to also count seven heard matters, and one of them is laktah; rather Ulla says that all those treifot mentioned and in the mishnah are halacha leMoshe miSinai — excluding laktah of Rachi bar Papa, who said it and it was not mentioned in the mishnah, for it is not halacha leMoshe miSinai; and not like Rashi, who explained Ulla disputes him. This is Rambam's view and his supporters, who above analyzed and wrote: eight types of treifot are halacha leMoshe miSinai, etc.; and Shulchan Aruch, who did not write halacha leMoshe miSinai, follows his reason that he answered in Beit Yosef: laktah is included in chaserah — so it appears to me.
