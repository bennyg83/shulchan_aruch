[Eight types of treifot they are, etc. Meaning: all treifot are included within these eight.]
[Derusah. That is, an animal mauled by a lion and the like, as will be explained in siman 57.]
[Nekuvah. Perforation of the membrane of the brain, in the siman after this; and perforation of the lung cavity (torbats haveshet) and lung tissue, in siman 33; and perforation of the trachea where it spreads to the lung and liver, at the end of siman 34; and perforation of the lung and bronchi (semponya), in siman 36; and perforation of the gallbladder, in siman 42; and perforation of the spleen, in siman 43; and perforation of the womb, in siman 45; and perforation of the small intestines (dekin), in siman 46; and perforation of the stomach, kereis, dissolved parts (mesas), and beit hakosot, in siman 48; and perforation of the craw (korekevan), in siman 49; and similar cases.]
[Chaserah. At the beginning of its formation; and lacking the testicles (onim), in siman 35, and similar; and likewise anything perforated — if it is lacking, it is treifah, as below siman 50.]
[Netulah. Removal of the lower or upper jaw, in siman 33; and removal of the liver, in siman 41; and removal of the junction of the gidim (tendons), in siman 56; and likewise any organ that was perforated — the same law applies if it was removed, as below siman 50. And Rashi explained that netulah includes geludah (flayed), in siman 59, where the skin was removed — but not according to Kesef Mishneh, chapter 8 of Hilchot Shechitah, who wrote that geludah is included in keru'ah.]
[Keru'ah. The majority of flesh covering the kereis was torn, in siman 48, and similar.]
[Nefulah. Actual falling, below siman 58.]
[Pesukah. Severing of the spinal cord, in siman 32; and severing of the windpipe (gargeret), in siman 34, and similar.]
[Shevurah. Majority of its ribs were broken, in siman 54. And according to Rashi, where the majority of simanim were loosened (nidaldelu), in siman 24, s.k. 16; and a crushed skull, in the siman after this; and uprooting a rib, in siman 54 — all are included in shevurah. But not according to Kesef Mishneh there, who wrote that loosened simanim are included in nefulah. And likewise from Rambam's language, chapter 9 of Hilchot Shechitah: simanim that loosened in the majority — treifah even if not from a fall — end. And since Rashi had to explain that loosened simanim are included in shevurah, evidently he holds that simanim loosened in the majority is actual treifah; and likewise Rambam's language implies. See what I wrote in siman 24, s.k. 23.]
