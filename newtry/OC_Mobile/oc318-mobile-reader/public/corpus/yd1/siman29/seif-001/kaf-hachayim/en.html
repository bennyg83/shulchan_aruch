(1) [Seif 1] Eight types of treifot they are. Meaning: all treifot are included within these eight. Tur. Levush. Shach, s.k. 1. And all treifot are explained below in the coming simanim. And Rambam wrote in chapter 9 of Hilchot Shechitah: when they are detailed, all treifot are found; and it is possible that in an animal and chayah seventy are found. And see there: he counted all seventy treifot in detail — examine there.
