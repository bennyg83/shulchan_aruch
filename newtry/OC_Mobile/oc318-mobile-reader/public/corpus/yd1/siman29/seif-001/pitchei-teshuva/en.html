Types of treifot. See in responsum of Avodat HaGershuni, Panim Me'irot, part 2, siman 41, and part 3, siman 19, who wrote regarding one who ate a bird and afterward found in it one of the eighteen treifot — he need not bring a korban for what he ate, for it is ones (compelled); and he brought proof from the mishnah in Pesachim (daf 41a) and from Tosafot in Beitzah (daf 25), s.v. orach ar'a — see there. [And see in Chemdat Shlomo, siman 1, what he wrote on this.]
