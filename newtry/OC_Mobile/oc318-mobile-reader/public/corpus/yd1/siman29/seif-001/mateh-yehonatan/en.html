(Siman 29) Seif 1 Eight types of treifot ; DaN ChaNeK NeFeSH. Thus Rambam wrote ; see there. And Beit Yosef challenged Rambam: for they say in the Gemara on Ulla's statement that it comes to exclude laktah of Rachi bar Papa; therefore it counts eight types of treifot and no more. But Rambam, who brings Rachi's words for halakhah, should have brought nine types of treifot. See Tur at length on Beit Yosef's language. And one may say: behold for this view, who wish to say regarding junction of gidim that if cut at the junction it is treifah, and if he went and cut above it returns to fitness ; one may say likewise regarding the milt: certainly even if laktah in the milt is treifah, but if afterward he went and removed the entire milt by hand it returns to fitness; therefore perforce one may say Ulla minimizes laktah in the milt because he counts only those treifot that remain treifah forever; but treifot that can return to fitness he does not count. But in truth he concedes laktah in the milt is treifah if the milt was not removed afterward; and likewise Rambam counts such among the eight types of treifot that we forbid forever; but laktah in the milt he does not count among them at all. And in truth laktah of the milt is treifah if the milt was not removed ; examine well. [The editor said: what is settled here in Rambam's words is astounding ; first, it appears Rambam does not hold at all that treifot of junction of gidim can return to fitness. And further, behold Rambam wrote in chapter 8, law 16: netulah ; how? etc., and these are junction of gidim ; evidently he counts also things that return to fitness. Therefore the hagahah of the third Rav Maharash, may his light shine, rabbi in the holy community of Vilna:]
In another manner one may say according to what we say in chapter Elu Treifot (daf 54a): it was stated ; R' Yehudah said: these are treifot specifically; and R' Levi said: these are kosher specifically; and Tosafot wrote, s.v. l'drav matna: and if you ask, why establish their dispute in Rav Matna ; establish it in Rachi, etc.; and one may say: it was clear to the arranger of the Gemara that they dispute in Rav Matna; perforce one may say Rambam and Tur were not pleased to strain and answer as written; rather they hold to answer that the Talmud holds there is no one who permits Rachi; and that which we say excluding Rachi ; that is, it is not treifah from itself, but because the end of the intestines will be perforated, and it is in the category of nekuvah; and in this Ulla and Rachi dispute ; Rachi holds it is treifah from itself, and it should be counted among the eighteen treifot; and Ulla holds it is not treifah from itself and cannot be counted among the eighteen treifot; therefore the view of Rambam and Tur, who wrote according to Ulla ; and even though they ruled laktah in the milt is treifah [the editor said: this too is astounding ; whence for the Talmud does Rachi hold it is treifah from itself? It would be better to say they do not dispute at all, only that which Ulla did not count it is because it is not treifah from itself ; and it requires study].
