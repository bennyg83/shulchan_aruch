They mourn, etc. From the incident of Rabbi Chiya and Rav at the beginning of Pesachim and in chapter 3 of Moed Katan. In Yevamot 116b, mishnah, they said: "You permitted a severe prohibition of ervah," and so forth. The same reasoning applies to mourning. See Rosh, siman 79, and what I wrote in Even HaEzer, siman 17, seif 37.
