And a gentile speaking incidentally. Maharshal cites Or Zarua: if a gentile woman incidentally tells Reuven that his sister died, Raavyah rules that he must mourn because her statement may be accepted to impose stringency. Another authority rules that the halachah follows the lenient view in mourning and a gentile is not believed to create mourning obligations. R. Yeshayah says that a gentile speaking incidentally is believed only in the special contexts of testimony permitting a woman to remarry, or where the gentile speaks about his own produce and says it is orlah. In ordinary testimony a gentile speaking incidentally is not believed, whether to permit or to prohibit.
