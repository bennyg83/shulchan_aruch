And a gentile speaking innocently. Maharshal wrote as follows: A gentile woman who innocently told Reuven that his sister had died; Raavyah ruled that he mourns on her word, for she is believed to impose stringency. Avi HaEzri ruled that the halachah follows the lenient view in mourning, and a gentile is not believed to impose stringency in matters of mourning. Rabbi Yeshayah says that a gentile is not believed when speaking innocently except for testimony permitting a woman to remarry, or where he testifies about his own fruits that they are orlah; but in all ordinary testimony a gentile is not believed when speaking innocently, neither to be lenient nor to be stringent. Thus Responsa Or Zarua; end quote.
