And a star-worshipper. Maharshal wrote: a female star-worshipper who related in her innocence to Reuven that his sister died, Raavyah ruled that he mourns on her word, for she is believed to be stringent. And my master ruled that the halacha is in accordance with the one who is lenient in mourning, and a star-worshipper is not believed to be stringent in matters of mourning. And HaRav Yeshayah says that a star-worshipper who speaks in innocence is believed only for the testimony of a woman or regarding his produce that they are of orlah, but in all other testimonies in the world a star-worshipper who speaks in innocence is not believed, whether to be lenient or to be stringent; until here, teshuva of Or Zarua. End of his words.
