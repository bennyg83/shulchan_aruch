and a non-Jew. Maharsha”l wrote: a non-Jewish woman who reported in passing to Reuven that his sister died — Ravya ruled that he mourns based on her word, since she is believed to be stringent. And the Or Zaru’a ruled that the halacha follows the lenient opinion in mourning, and a non-Jew is not believed to be stringent in matters of mourning. And R. Yeshayah says that a non-Jew reporting without deliberate intent (mesiach lefi tumo) is only believed for the testimony of a woman [to permit her to remarry] or regarding his produce that it is orla — but for all other testimony a non-Jew reporting without deliberate intent is not believed, whether to be lenient or to be stringent — end of the responsum of Or Zaru’a, end of his words:
