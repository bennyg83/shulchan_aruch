The dead of non-Jews, etc. The word 'with' in the cited passage is not precise. Likewise, the Yerushalmi at the end of chapter 5 of Gittin does not use 'with' in these laws, and the Tosefta at the end of chapter 3 states: one eulogizes and buries the dead of non-Jews and comforts their mourners for the sake of peaceful relations.
