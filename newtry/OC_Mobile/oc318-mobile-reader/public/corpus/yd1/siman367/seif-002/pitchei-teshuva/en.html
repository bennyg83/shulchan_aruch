And read from it. Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Orach Chaim 109, was asked whether those who pray at righteous persons' graves during distress may bring a Torah scroll into the cemetery. The Talmud and poskim do not expressly address mere carrying, but Rambam's wording implies prohibition. In any event it must not be done, for the Zohar warns that if the scroll is missing even one letter it can cause great harm, and we possess no scroll certainly checked for every defective and plene spelling.
