Beit HaTalmud of Mahari"t in the name of Shevut Yaakov; and see responsum of Beit Efrayim Yoreh De'ah siman 56 who was asked about a similar matter: one who was ill and commanded his household to give his garments to a certain poor relative; he recovered and wants to keep the garments and give their value to that poor person without the poor person's knowledge, and his intent is to be stringent that he must give the body of the object to the poor and giving money does not help — see there what he wrote on the words of Shevut Yaakov cited.
