Charities volunteered for the need of the synagogue or for the need of the cemetery — the people of the city may change them for the need of the study hall or Torah school, even if the owners object; but not from Torah study to the need of the synagogue: {Rama: And specifically when there is concern they will not have enough for Torah study; but if there is enough, they may change even from Torah study to the synagogue.
