If the donor's name has been forgotten, and so forth. This resolves Tosafot's difficulty and accords with Tosefta Megillah: before the donor's name has been forgotten, a menorah or lamp donated to a synagogue may be redirected, but afterward it may not be redirected to ordinary use. The Yerushalmi similarly teaches that an engraved donor's name is treated as though the name has not been forgotten.
