"They cannot change it, etc." Mordekhai chapter 1 of Bava Batra in the name of Maharam: it should say that they already used them and lit in synagogue — for if they did not light in synagogue, even for a mundane matter it is permitted for us, since we hold designation is not a matter; and although the noder cannot retract and block for himself once he consecrated to light in synagogue it becomes a vow, nevertheless the public may change even for a mundane matter — end quote; brought by Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe; examine, for other poskim did not distinguish thus, and on the contrary from their distinction between "was submerged" implies without submersion in every case it is forbidden to change; and perhaps Mordekhai deals when owners agree, teaching that from "forbidden to change" implies prohibition exists in the matter even if owners agree — specifically when they used them, for otherwise designation is not a matter; examine.
"But for a mitzvah matter they may change." Mordekhai in the name of Maharam: even for a lesser mitzvah — only regarding sacred use do we say "we ascend in holiness and do not descend," not regarding mitzvah use; and in Darkei Moshe he wrote on this, and it appears Rosh (and Tur seif 2) disagree with this, for Rosh wrote it is forbidden to change money of Torah study for synagogue need, and likewise Mahari"k in root 128 in the name of Rashba; brought in Hagahot Darishah siman 26; but in truth in Mahari"k there he wrote: per my humble opinion no posek may disagree with Mordekhai, for if not say thus, distinguish and teach the entire case for mitzvah matter and say "we only taught regarding a mitzvah matter that is equivalent, but for a lesser mitzvah not" etc.; and although Rashba was asked if charity money may be changed to synagogue such as building synagogue or buying Sefer Torah, he answered only for elevation like building synagogue or buying Sefer Torah — implying only elevation; and likewise Tur Yoreh Deah wrote Rosh wrote etc. until "but not from Torah study for synagogue need" — it appears to my humble opinion there the community is not confident in both, and if they give money for synagogue need Torah study will cease; and likewise in what Rashba answered — but where the other mitzvah is not nullified etc. it is permitted to all — end quote; and likewise in Perishah he brought Mordekchai's words against Tur's words — implying they do not disagree; and from Bach's words it appears he distinguishes that Rosh's case is sacred use, as he wrote on Rosh's words "only regarding sacred use we say ascend in holiness and do not descend, but not regarding mitzvah use" — so Mordekhai chapter 9 of Bava Batra in the name of Maharam, and he called lamp and menorah mitzvah use because they were not given in synagogue except to illuminate for worshippers during prayer and mahzorim — this is only mitzvah use for the person himself; similarly Mahar"i wrote in his writings that covers on a Sefer Torah are not sacred use since they were made only as markers so people not err to take out a Sefer Torah not called up for that day's obligation; likewise so people not err in prayer when there is no light in synagogue — end quote; and it does not appear so to Mahari"k; and it appears he holds money donated for synagogue need is preferable to mitzvah use since there is no holiness on the money.
