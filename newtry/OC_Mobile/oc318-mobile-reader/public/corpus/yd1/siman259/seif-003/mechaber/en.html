An Israelite who volunteered a lamp or candelabrum for the synagogue — if its owner abandoned it there, it is not called by his name; the public may change it even for a mundane matter (even if the donor objects) (Maharik shoresh 123–124, in the name of Rashba and Teshuvat Rosh); but if its owner did not abandon it there, they may not change it without his consent.
