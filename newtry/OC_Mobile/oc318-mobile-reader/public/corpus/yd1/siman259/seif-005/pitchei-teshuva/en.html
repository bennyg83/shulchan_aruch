Torah study for the need of the synagogue. See responsum of Har HaKarmel Yoreh De'ah siman 24 regarding whether the gabbai may take from Torah-study funds to make clothing for one decent boy who has nothing with which to cover himself in order to go to his rabbi's house to learn; he wrote he is permitted, for this clothing itself is called Torah study since it causes it, and if he were not clothed this boy would not fulfill Torah study; and there is no change at all unless there is another boy before us to learn and he does not need clothing — and if we clothe this one, the other will be pushed from Torah study; in such a case it is fine to prioritize this one who immediately needs Torah study — see there. And see responsum of Shemesh Tzedakah Yoreh De'ah siman 16 who was asked about former Torah-study administrators: when they clothed students from the fund's money they used to clothe from that money also the teachers — whether they acted properly or not; he answered it is forbidden to do so, for this is lowering holiness and they did not act properly — see there.
