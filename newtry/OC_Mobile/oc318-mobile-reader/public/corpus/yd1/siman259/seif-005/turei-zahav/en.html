In doubtful language, etc. In Choshen Mishpat siman 200 I wrote study is needed to reconcile Rama's view, for in siman 258 seif 1 he ruled also the second side is charity and it depends on doubtful language — he ruled strictly, and here leniently (and there CM seif 3 he also ruled strictly — examine); and Mahar"i in his writings siman 73 ruled that in hekdesh and nedarim wherever we can say "this or that" we do not say to be strict but establish money in owner's presumption; and likewise regarding baal hashetar yado al hatachtonah — end quote; and again I considered reconciling Rama and saw his words are correct: in every matter that is doubt that can never be clarified, like siman 258 — if we say there is "hand" for charity it is as if he said explicitly and this too would be charity, and even if he says his intent was for my expense he is not believed; and likewise here where Shulchan Aruch mentioned money is doubtful if charity — certainly we are strict, unlike our case where the dying person said some language we doubt his intent — if he were alive and revealed intent we would believe him as siman 208 — unspecified vows to be strict and their explanation to be lenient; therefore one may say if father were alive he would reveal his intent that it be his — therefore we do not remove from heirs' presumption. And in Levush I saw he distinguished between first and second clause — in the first he had no presumption the money was his, and there is no beginning to this distinction, for also in the first it was in his presumption since we derive leket minor from Beit Yosef and it is placed in his field; and as I wrote it appears correct, with Heaven's help.
