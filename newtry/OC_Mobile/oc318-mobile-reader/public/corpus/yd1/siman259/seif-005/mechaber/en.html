One who has money in his hand and is uncertain whether it is charity money — he is obligated to give it to charity: {Rama: But one who dedicated something in ambiguous language and died, so that his intent is unknown — the heirs are presumed holders and the hekdesh must prove to extract from them; and as long as he does not clearly protest, we do not remove them from their presumption. (Maharik and Mahariiv.)}
