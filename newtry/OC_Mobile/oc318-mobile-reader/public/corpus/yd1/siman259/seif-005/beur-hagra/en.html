One who has, etc. From mishnah end of chapter 4 of Peah, and as stated in Yerushalmi and Torat Kohanim, and the Rosh brought them there and Beit HaGadol; but in Chullin 134a it is proven the opposite that we go leniently like every safek of money unless there was a prior presumption of obligation. And you will say in Yerushalmi Peah there regarding safek leket, etc. — R. Yirmiyah said it is R. Yosi ben Chalafta who taught: a convert who converted — if standing grain before he converted he is exempt, after he converted he is obligated; and if safek he is exempt — R. Yosi ben Chalafta obligates, Rashal said: an Israelite whose essence is obligated — his safek is obligated; a gentile whose essence is exempt — his safek is exempt; and this is as stated in the Gemara cited; and even though R. Yirmiyah disagrees with him, nevertheless our sugya is like Rashal, and all the more so like R. Yochanan who exempts in all cases; and similarly Rabbenu Nissim in Nedarim 10a from several sugyot in the Gemara; and similarly Rashba in a responsum, and as stated in the gloss; and the rabbi distinguishes between heirs, etc. — it is not so.
