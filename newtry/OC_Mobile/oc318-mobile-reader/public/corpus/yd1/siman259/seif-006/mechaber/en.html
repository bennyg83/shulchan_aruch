The community may not claim taxes and tariffs from charity money: {Rama: One who found a purse full of money in his box and it is written on it "charity" — we rely on the writing and behold it is charity. (Hagahot Mordechai, first chapter of Bava Batra.) And likewise if one said to his children, "This money is charity," if it appears to them he did this to remove it from his estate so they not quarrel over it, it is not charity unless he gave it to charity collectors. (Maharik.)}
