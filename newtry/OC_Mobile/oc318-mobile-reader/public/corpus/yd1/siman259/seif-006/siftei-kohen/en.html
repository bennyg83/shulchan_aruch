These are the coins that your father hid, etc. This means that he does not know where his father hid the money. The figure appearing in the dream shows him the money and says, ‘These are the coins your father hid.’ The account in Sanhedrin 30a likewise states that the dream figure came and said to him: ‘There are such-and-such an amount, they are in a particular place, and they are second-tithe money.’ This incident occurred, and the sages ruled that words of dreams neither raise nor lower, meaning they have no legal effect. The novelty of the ruling is now clear.
