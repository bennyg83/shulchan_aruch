In order to act. This means in order to compel others, by stating in their presence that there is no money in the charity fund.
Lest poor people arrive and there will be nothing to distribute to them. Above, in siman 256, seif 4, I wrote that this does not present a difficulty regarding R' Yannai, who borrowed charity money because doing so ultimately benefited the poor. The Shulchan Aruch likewise writes here at the beginning that if delaying the money benefits the poor, it is permitted. That case concerns one who is entirely confident that he can immediately collect from the community whenever money is required for distribution to the poor, without any obstruction.
