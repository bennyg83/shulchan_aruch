Since he does not specify explicitly for what purpose he gave them to her, it is a gift for every matter, and the condition "on condition that your husband has no right," etc., no longer helps. In Even HaEzer siman 85 seif 11 the Mechaber ruled simply like the first view and did not bring the dissenting view. The Bach writes that it appears that where the great authorities dispute regarding nedarim, we follow stringency, but regarding money, leniency favors the defendant and stringency the claimant, and whoever is in possession has the advantage, for the burden of proof is on the claimant. Thus the Shach.
