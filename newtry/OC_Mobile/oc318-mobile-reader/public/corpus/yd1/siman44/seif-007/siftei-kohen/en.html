If there were three kidneys. Bach wrote: the wording implies the third does not stand in one row with the two but is found elsewhere; but if they stand one next to the other and there is affliction in one — then it is treifah, for who says the permitted one was afflicted — perhaps the kulya that is not the excess was afflicted, and it is laketah in one kulya and treifah — end; and there is apparently proof for his words from what Raavan wrote there: when there are three kidneys and one of them shrank — treifah; but Maharshal there siman 89 wrote the opposite: if there are three kidneys and all stand in one row and there is affliction or other deficiency in one of them — kosher; but if the third does not stand in the row and the affliction or deficiency is in one of those two standing in the row — treifah — end; see there; and per his words Raavan must deal with the case where the third does not stand in the row and one of those standing in the row was afflicted; and requires study for practical law.
