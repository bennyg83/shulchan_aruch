And the excess ones shrank, etc. Moharach z"l wrote the wording implies the third does not stand in one row with the two; but if they stand one next to the other and one was afflicted — treifah, for who says the permitted one was afflicted — perhaps the one that is not excess was afflicted, and it is laketah in one kulya and treifah — end of his language. And I do not know who introduced this to add treifot through this distinction — for certainly on each there is an excess, and whenever two remain as required we say they are its kidneys — and this is simple in my humble opinion.
