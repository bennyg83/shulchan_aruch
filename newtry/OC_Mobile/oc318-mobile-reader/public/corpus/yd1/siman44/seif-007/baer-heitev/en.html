Shach wrote in name of Bach: the wording implies the third does not stand in one row with the two but is found elsewhere; but if they stand one next to the other and there is affliction in one — treifah, for who says the permitted one was afflicted — perhaps the one that is not excess was afflicted, and it is laketah in one kulya and treifah {Rama: (and Tzitz Eliezer followed him siman 98)} {Rama: (And Taz wrote: I do not know who introduced this to add treifot through this distinction — for certainly on each there is an excess, and whenever two remain as required we say they are the kidneys — and this is simple — end; and Maharshal wrote: if there are three kidneys and all stand in one row and there is affliction or other deficiency in one of them — kosher; but if the third does not stand in the row and the affliction or deficiency is in one of those two standing in the row — treifah; and requires study for practical law; and Shach left this matter in requires study. And Pri Chadash wrote: even if an extra kulya is found, whenever there is affliction in one of them — treifah — see there)}.
