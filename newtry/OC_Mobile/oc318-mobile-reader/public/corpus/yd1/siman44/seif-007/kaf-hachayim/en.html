D) There. Or with three kidneys — kosher. And likewise four, five, and more. Machatzit HaShekel ot 4 in name of several poskim; Anaf Yosef there; Zivchei Tzedek ot 3; and so below seif 7.
E) And even if the three kidneys adhere to the charitz — kosher for all, even per Ramban. Bach. Pri Chadash ot 1; Lev HaPanim ot 1; Beit Lechem Yehudah ot 1; Machatzit HaShekel ot 5; Anaf Yosef there; Beit Yitzchak in Sha'ar 7 ot 2; Zivchei Tzedek there ot 4.
F) And although if they adhere on account of illness at the place of the charitz and it is possible to know they adhere from illness — one may declare kosher even without double doubt. Machatzit HaShekel ot 6; Anaf Yosef there ot 3; Zivchei Tzedek ot 5.
