And any change of appearance, etc. Tur wrote if it is green — kosher; and Beit Yosef thought he learned this from Beit Yosef's words; therefore he wondered from where he got this; and it appears simple this is his own view, that this is not a general rule of change of appearance and it is not laketah at all, as we say regarding lung because blood did not fall on it, and they did not include every change of appearance among laketot; and so is the essence for halachah. And Turei Chadashim wrote appearance of "jaune" is kosher for it leans to redness, and "gris" and "bleu" are treifah — very astonishing why it should differ from lung where we say the opposite; especially in green, the reason is because blood did not fall; and so is the essence, for Tur wrote explicitly green is kosher, and there is reason to permit — namely green "gris," and this did not enter the general rule of appearance at all. And what he wrote that "jaune" leans to redness is difficult, for in siman 188 regarding menstrual blood it is not so; therefore one may not permit appearance of "jaune." And Maharshal wrote: since change of appearance in the kulya has no proof from the Talmud, therefore one forbids in change of appearance only when it reaches its loven; and it does not imply thus in Beit Yosef's words that he brought, who did not distinguish between kulya and lung except in appearances that are not equal; but in this that in lung treifah even if it did not enter inside, as we proved above siman 38 — likewise in kulya, and the reason is it will ruin everything in the end, as stated.
