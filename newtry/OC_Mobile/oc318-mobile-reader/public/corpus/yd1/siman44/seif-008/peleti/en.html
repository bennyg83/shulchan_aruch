And how can it return to kosher status — and Maharshal cried out "what of it"; and see what acharonim wrote, especially Taz; and I brought proof per what Maharshal wrote that treifah can become pregnant but cannot give birth; if so the Gemara's forcing regarding fetus of treifah — R' Eliezer says do not offer and R' Yochanan says offer — according to the one who says treifah does not bear, what do they argue; and there is no need for both to hold treifah does not bear; and the Gemara is established in pregnancy and finally treifah, and in the fetus it leans on its mother. We challenged the first case: in this scenario where the kulya shrank and it is treifah and became pregnant — treifah becomes pregnant and before its days to give birth it healed when the kulya was removed and behold it is kosher and able to give birth; at least when it became pregnant the animal was treifah, and R' Eliezer and R' Yochanan argue whether each causes or is permitted or forbidden; no need to establish in another dispute. However this may be rejected: what Maharshal wrote that it can become pregnant but not give birth means it cannot bear living offspring from permanent seed, only bear a non-viable birth; therefore if it gives birth and the offspring lives, that is a sign it is not treifah; but to give birth alone Maharshal concedes, for in "became pregnant and afterward became treifah" the Gemara says it bears; if so my proof falls, for when it became pregnant it was treifah — it had no ability to become pregnant with living offspring that it bore afterward — what of it that it returned to kosher status; nevertheless Tosafot do not hold like Maharshal's view in Chullin 11b that we follow majority from false witnesses, etc. — Tosafot challenged: bind him twelve months, if he lives it is not treifah; what is the difficulty, since we do not follow majority and we are concerned for all doubts — perhaps at the time of testimony the murderer's kulya shrank and he was treifah and witnesses are exempt, and what shrank afterward until the kulya was removed and returned to kosher status and he lived even a hundred years, and either way witnesses at time of testimony are exempt; and similarly one may infer in what Tosafot challenged in Zevachim 101a: perhaps it was treifah at the time of the Flood; it lived many years — one may say it returned to kosher status as above; only this is not such a proof, for Tosafot wrote in 101a that treifot need not be visible, and therefore Maharshal's law almost does not exist in reality; but from my proof it is difficult — examine. And for practical law per Shulchan Aruch: even in spleen removed by illness it is kosher — illness where it shrank, for spleen is not treifah through shrinking and there is no measure for its size; but if removed by illness where it melted — treifah, for initially it melted in the supports, i.e. flesh rotted that the physician scrapes, and it is treifah; how can it return to kosher status when removed; therefore in truth treifah; and Shulchan Aruch relied on the kulya — examine.
