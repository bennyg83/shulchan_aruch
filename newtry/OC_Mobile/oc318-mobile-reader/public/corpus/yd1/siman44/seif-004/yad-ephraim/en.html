And any change of appearance. Baer Heitev in name of Pri Chadash; and Machatzit HaShekel wrote in name of Ohel Yosef siman 14: a kulya whose appearance changed — they practiced in Saloniki to boil it; if it returned to its appearance — kosher, for sometimes blackening in the kulya is from fatigue and this is not laketah — see there that his view is change of appearance does not disqualify unless it reached the charitz, as Maharshal wrote, unlike Taz. And if a Jew said he saw a black spot on the kulya and a non-Jew took it — permit from double doubt per what Chatam Sofer wrote regarding shrunken kulya — see there.
