Stones found in the kulya — kosher. For from there they descend to the ligament of the penis — end Rokeach; and it implies even if found in the place of the charitz; and Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 87 and Bach wrote the opposite — see there Rokeach; and I do not know from where they got this; perhaps they wanted to equate it to Orach Chaim's words; however in Turei Chadashim there law 6 he wrote: perhaps Orach Chaim also concedes in this, only he stated the incident that occurred; and so it appears to me, since the way of a stone is to grow in kidneys and it is among physicians' practices — one should not declare treifah even in the place of the charitz — end; and also the proof Maharshal brought there from Hagahot Ashiri's incident in Shafir'an is no proof, for an incident that occurred thus is not proof; nevertheless from Orach Chaim's words it implies he stated it specifically because we read "in the place of the charitz," etc. — see there; and Beit Yosef brings it.
