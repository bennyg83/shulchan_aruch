And a grape is included. Beit Yosef in name of Mahari ibn Chaviv explained what he explained here, for it was challenged: behold a grape is not as large as a bean; and Maharshal wrote this is not a wonder, for fruits of the seven species praised in Eretz Yisrael were exceptional in size according to nature, for we have received tradition from Tosafot authors that olive is half an egg of a hen though not found thus now, only they changed — end.
