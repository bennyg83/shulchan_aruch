(There seif 5) A kulya that shrank, etc. Shach agreed in his long book that in great financial loss, in such a case one expert in estimating may declare kosher between whether a bean is included or not, per Bahag and Tosafot who wrote there is no need to be stringent except in Mishnah or Baraisa. However it is difficult, for even in an amora's statement we inquire in Piskei Avodah Zarah and it was not resolved. And similarly Mahadeni challenged in Berakhot chapter 2; and I wrote there to resolve — but here it cannot be resolved. However what Sefer Etz Chaim brought as proof to forbid from Yerushalmi Sukkah regarding "etrog until like a nut — like the nut itself" — there it is a Baraisa and we hold measures to be stringent; not so here, for it is not a Baraisa.
