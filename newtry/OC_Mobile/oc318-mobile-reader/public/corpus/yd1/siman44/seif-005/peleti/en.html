Sword-blow, etc. They wondered: behold Rama permits in cut; Bach and Taz explain it deals with split in two; and Shach already rejected and wrote well: if split in two, whoever declares treifah declares treifah even without a sword, even by illness; and the reason they learned from Job "He splits my kidneys" — the Satan did not stab him with a sword. And Shach explained with force — this too Pri Chadash rejected; the law is with him, for who can measure and weigh whether it was done with force or not, and not all forces are equal when the matter is entrusted to each person. But the matter is simple: certainly if something was cut, even by sword, it does not pain so much; circumcision proves — they circumcise the foreskin with a knife and it heals easily; but if one makes wounds in flesh by striking with a sword — that generates heat and poison inside and it is danger — and this is sword-blow; and see Avodah Zarah 28b Tosafot entry "a blow on the hand" who wrote: illness not from sword-blow, but from sword-blow we say everywhere one desecrates Shabbat as they said per his view, etc.; and they explain sword-blow as wounds from sword-blow — see there; and in poskim and Mordechai you will find; and this is what Rama intended. And one may learn for Maharshach and his supporters who hold split in two is treifah and derive from Job.
