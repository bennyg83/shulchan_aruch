Like a grape. Taz wrote: do not challenge — behold a grape is not as large as a bean; Maharshal already answered that it deals with fruits of the seven species praised in Eretz Yisrael that were exceptional in size according to nature, as we find regarding olive measure that it is half an egg of a hen though not found thus now. And Shach wrote in name of Kol Bo: if it is entirely full and there is no cavity around it and no hollowness at all — then it is recognizable that such was the beginning of its creation and kosher; and if a cavity is found there, it truly shrank and is forbidden — end {Rama: (A kulya that was small and they did not manage to measure whether it was more than a bean or grape until the animal was lost — permitted from double doubt: doubt that it was more than a bean, and doubt that perhaps from the beginning of creation it was small and not on account of illness. However, if the other kulya was rotting or any flaw — although it is not treifah from this, there is only one doubt that perhaps it was larger than a bean; but doubt that perhaps from the beginning of creation it was thus small — one cannot say, since we saw the other kulya rotting from illness we are witnesses that this kulya too shrank from illness — Kenesset HaGedolah daf 169)}.
