And a bean is included — treifah. And in Bahag it is written that bean and grape are kosher; and so Raavan in siman 257: until a bean — if the kulya is [the size of] a bean it is kosher; and in a large animal like a medium grape, which is more than a bean; and in their places in Eretz Yisrael grapes were large; and I saw an incident in Mainz in a large animal that shrank like a grape and they declared it kosher — end; and so Or Zaru'a; and Maharshal brings it in his Orach Chaim siman 88.
Like a grape. And Turei Chadashim there law 7 wrote; and Beit Yosef wrote in name of Mahari ibn Chaviv: it does not mean an actual grape, but the kulya of a large animal is made of somewhat separate pieces, one of which is called a grape — end; and so in Simanim. And it is astonishing, for Mahari ibn Chaviv concludes the essence is an actual grape; and so agreed to this explanation Maharshal end of siman 88, Bach, Perishah, and so Raavan whom I brought in the previous s.k.
And how do we know, etc. And in Kol Bo: how do we know? We examine their places: if it is entirely full and there is no cavity around it and no hollowness at all — then it is recognizable that such was the beginning of its creation and kosher; and if a cavity is found there, it truly shrank and is forbidden — thus far.
