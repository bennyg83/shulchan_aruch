Cut — see Peleti who explained that it was cut in thickness and not across, only part of the thickness was cut.
Even per Tur's view that in deficiency even if the deficiency reached the charitz — kosher; and Maharshal challenged; and Taz strengthened Tur's words; and see Peleti where I brought support and proof for both views — therefore one should be stringent; and likewise puncture by illness — Ra'ah's view is to be stringent; and for us who are not expert in dissolving — it is obvious treifah.
Until the place, etc. And internal deficiency — Maharshal's view is to permit only almost, which is not applicable, for affliction must reach the place of the charitz where it is exposed; and I did not understand that kulya flesh and fat outside can be fine while inside is bad full of putrefaction and laketah — see Peleti where I wrote proof they call deficiency in the kulya.
Place of the charitz; and if the loven itself was punctured — later authorities [permit].
Charitz is the loven, for there is fat; and "until" is inclusive — so is the view of later authorities. And see Peleti: one may say if affliction reached the loven somewhat, even if not majority of the loven — treifah; unlike if there is only laketah in the loven — must be majority of the loven. Also if we saw laketah and did not manage to examine the loven until it was lost — treifah; but if there is only laketah in the loven — must know for certain, and if not — not kosher.
