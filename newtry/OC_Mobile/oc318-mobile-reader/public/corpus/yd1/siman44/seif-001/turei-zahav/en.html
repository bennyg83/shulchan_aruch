Punctured or cut. The reason in Beit Yosef in name of Rosh: only regarding laketah in seif 2 is it treifah, for it pains more than the pain of laketah in dissolving; puncture or cut — and in Tur it is written punctured or deficient even if the deficiency reached, etc.; and Maharshal wrote on this and it is not clear, for we do not declare treifah in dissolving except that it is considered as if it is not; if so, all the more so deficiency — treifah if the deficiency reached the charitz; and this proof is not worth even to be stringent against Tur's view, for certainly dissolving is worse than deficiency, for in dissolving the pain grows and ruins even the place of the charitz, unlike in deficiency.
Until the place of the charitz. Meaning: white fat under the loins that enters inside the kulya where sinews are entwined, and it is the middle of the kulya and is called charitz. Rashi and Ran. And even if the loven itself was punctured or cut — kosher as above; and proof from what we say in the Gemara Avodah Zarah: disqualifies in lung, kosher in kulya — meaning puncture; from which we learn in the entire kulya it is kosher; and further from what Rosh taught that punctured or cut is kosher from what we say in the Gemara they struck the kulya and it was not treifah and they did not examine whether it reached the loven — we learn even if it reached the loven itself it is kosher in puncture or cut; and so Moharach z"l.
