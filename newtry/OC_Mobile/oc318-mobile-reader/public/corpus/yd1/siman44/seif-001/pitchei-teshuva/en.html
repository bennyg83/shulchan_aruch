Baer Heitev: likewise deficient; and see Taz who wrote Maharshal disagrees with Tur and is stringent in deficiency, and Taz rejected Maharshal's reasoning and rules like Tur to be lenient; and so Pri Chadash. And see responsum Shemen Rokeach siman 35 who brought proof for Maharshal's view and concluded that without great financial loss one should be stringent — see there; and see responsum Toldot Yitzchak siman 14 what he wrote. And what Baer Heitev further wrote: specifically by needle, etc. — see responsum Toldot Yitzchak there who brought in name of Sefer Be'er Avraham to be lenient even in puncture by illness; and he challenged him and elaborated to strengthen Orach Chaim's words — Heaven forbid to be lenient; and he wrote: specifically if it reached the place of the charitz; but otherwise one may be lenient — see there; and it appears he means even if the puncture is open outside one may be lenient; but if the puncture is only from inside, in putrefaction too it is kosher if it did not reach the loven per Maharshal whom Baer Heitev brought below s.k. 4; and I wrote there that this is the essence, because internal deficiency is not called deficiency — we say likewise regarding the kulya.
