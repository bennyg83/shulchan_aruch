If not, etc. So Beit Yosef learned from Rashba's words in siman 52 seif 1: "or if it yellowed," etc.; and so in siman 41 seif 63 in the hagahah "and if they dried," etc.; and proof for their words from what is stated 46a: he said to him regarding this excess, if, etc. — it implies specifically that these two olive-volumes remained.
