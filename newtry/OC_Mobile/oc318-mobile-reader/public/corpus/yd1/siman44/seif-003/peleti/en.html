Taz brought in s.k. 4 in name of Maharshal: internal deficiency in the kulya is kosher — only requires study, since it reaches the charitz internal deficiency is not applicable; and I did not understand, for we say kosher in lung, disqualifies in kulya — meaning putrefaction per Rashi: tin-roof, Heaven forbid there should be putrefaction passing through a membrane — there is no greater laketah than this; and it must be putrefaction only under the membrane and from it the substance of tin-roof; and behold internal deficiency; and it is difficult; and in such a case even in the kulya kosher. And there is no difference between lung and kulya; unless where it teaches punctured like lung, and as stated regarding spleen — there internal deficiency is not called deficiency; but where it teaches stam laketah — ultimately there is laketah and treifah, and it is clear.
