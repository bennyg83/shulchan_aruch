If the kulya was smitten (laketah) — and this means its flesh becomes like flesh of the dead that reeks after days, that if one holds part of it, it dissolves and falls — and this illness reached the loven within the kulya — behold it is treifah. {Rama: (A sword-blow — its law is like laketah of the kulya) (Hagahot Ashiri and Mordechai; and see s.k. 6)} And likewise if putrefied material was found in the kulya even though it is not rotting, or murky or rotting water was found in it — behold it is treifah — and this is when they reach the loven; but if clear water was found in it — kosher, even if they reached the loven in it {Rama: (And even if clear water was found in a small pouch in the kulya — kosher) (Mordechai Piskei Avodah Zarah and Hagahot Ashiri and Kol Bo and Orach Chaim general rule 93)} And if blood was found in it — its law is like water; and if it was found full of water whose appearance is bad but they were not thick and murky, rather their appearance is like saffron like the appearance of honey — kosher; and there is one who forbids {Rama: (and such is the custom):}
