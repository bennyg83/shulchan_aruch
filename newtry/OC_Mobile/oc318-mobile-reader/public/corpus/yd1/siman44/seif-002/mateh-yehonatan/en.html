(Siman 44 seif 2) If blood was found in it — its law is like water. And per Shach's view it must be that even if the loven itself changed to blood it returns to kosher status, and if it changed to milk — not so; but per Atzei Tzvi one may say it deals when laketah reached the loven and there it is red and blood was found. And Baal Shevuyim challenges from what is stated above regarding liver in siman 41 that melted liver is treifah, and what is stated here regarding kulya that it is kosher; and the resolution: there it deals when the entire liver melted, i.e. the whole liver like this — certainly it does not return to kosher; not so here, it deals when the kulya is whole on all sides and only inside the flesh is full of blood — in this it certainly returns to health.
