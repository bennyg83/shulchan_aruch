Until the loven. Proven in Talmud and several poskim that we require reaching inside the loven; and so Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe in name of Orach Chaim explicitly; and so Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 87; therefore one must say what Tur and Mechaber wrote "until the loven" is not precise; and so Bach that it is not precise; and so in some versions of Tur and in Perishah and Beit Yosef it was copied "on the loven," unlike the implication in Atzei Tzvi that until the loven alone is treifah.
The loven. That enters inside the kidneys under the loins where sinews are entwined in the middle of the kulya, and it is in the place of the charitz. Rashi.
Sword-blow. Bach explained this means specifically that it split in two, and it is not clear as I wrote in my book; and so is explicit in Maharshal's words Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 86 that sword-blow means even if it did not split in two; and so is implied from Orach Chaim's words that I brought in s.k. 8; however in Maharshal's words there it is explained that sword-blow means cut; and if so Taz and other poskim who wrote in seif 1 that cut is kosher do not seem to hold thus; indeed Orach Chaim after writing that in sword-blow or split in two is treifah wrote: but if only cut in them and they did not divide in two, even if the cut reached the place of the charitz — kosher — until here his language; therefore cut is not sword-blow; and this is R' Baruch's view in the hagahah; and it appears sword-blow means with force as one strikes with a sword, namely laketah of the kulya; but cut with a calm cut — kosher; and so it appears to me to reconcile the poskim; and in Lev Chaim 168b he left R' Baruch's words in requires study.
Until the loven. I already wrote in s.k. 5 that this means on the loven; and in clear water — even on the loven — kosher; and as stated "even if they reached the loven," unlike Atzei Tzvi who wrote that in murky or rotting water it is treifah when they reach the loven even though the loven is whole, and in clear water kosher only until the loven specifically provided the loven is whole — end — this does not exist; rather the essence of treifah depends on the loven; therefore the Mechaber wrote in seif 3 (and so Atzei Tzvi himself) that if the affliction was only in the loven alone — treifah; and all this is simple. However this is specifically per Tur, Mechaber, Rashba and their supporters; but Orach Chaim general rule 53 law 4 wrote: R' Shmuel ben Chafni the priest wrote in his Sha'arim: the animal was afflicted even only in one kulya in murky or rotting water, or its flesh became like flesh of the dead that reeks after days, that if one holds part of it, it dissolves and falls — treifah, even if it did not reach the place of the charitz, i.e. until the place of the loven — end; and Raavyah also wrote this: that requiring reaching the place of the charitz in the kulya is in sword-blow, but through putrefaction — even if it did not reach the place of the charitz — treifah; and he concludes: and likewise if split in two — treifah, from "He splits my kidneys" (Job 16:13) — end Orach Chaim (and it implies that even in a small amount of putrefaction far from the place of the charitz — treifah); and Beit Yosef brought this view of these poskim — see there Hagahot Ashiri; and so in Mordechai Piskei Avodah Zarah. And he wrote: it implies they explain what we say in the Talmud that we require the affliction reach the place of the charitz — only in sword-blow laketah; and so Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 86: in putrefaction even if it did not reach the loven — treifah; and so in Turei Chadashim general rule 9 law 1 he brings the forbidders at the end; but certainly in sword-blow all agree we require the affliction reach the place of the charitz (and what the rabbi wrote in the hagahah that sword-blow's law is like laketah of the kulya means it is treifah for all when it reached the place of the charitz — examine); and so Mordechai and Hagahot Ashiri in name of Rashi; and so Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 46, unlike Bach who forbids even in sword-blow if it did not reach the place of the charitz; and Bach is further stringent in several laws in this seif that are not essential in Talmud and poskim.
Kosher. So Beit Yosef per Hagahot Ashiri who permits blood; and Sefer Lev Chaim end of daf 168 wrote: one should wonder on Rama that he did not emend to forbid blood as he emended to forbid appearance of honey — end; and incidentally he did not examine Darkei Moshe or Turei Chadashim (and he answered that in his book Torat HaEsh he corrected this) — in Darkei Moshe he wrote on Beit Yosef: and it is not clear, for the reason for blood is as Orach Chaim wrote this is not laketah since it is entirely blood; but otherwise no (meaning: and like appearance of honey it is called murky water; and so Darkei Moshe and Turei Chadashim there explicitly, and not because of change of appearance we declare treifah, for he permits in Turei Chadashim there law 6 appearance of "jaune" that is like saffron as written in beginning of siman 38 — examine); therefore one should rule as Rashba wrote — end; and so Turei Chadashim general rule 9 law 4; and for this he wrote here in the hagahah "and such is the custom."
