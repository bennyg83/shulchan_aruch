Until the loven is the charitz passing through the middle of the kulya; above it is fat and below it to its right are sinews entwined in them.
Sword-blow — Maharshal explained it split in two; Shach challenged that this is not the intent in Shulchan Aruch; and so Peleti; and I wrote that if it split in two — even without a sword — treifah; and see what I wrote on this; and Shach wrote the explanation is that it was not cut calmly but with force — this is strained; and see Peleti where I wrote: not that it was cut, but wounds were made in the kulya by sword — this is danger, and it is written "exceedingly" as we hold regarding desecration of Shabbat — see there.
Until the loven — Shach's view in name of many authors: in dissolving we do not require affliction until the charitz; and see Peleti where I brought proof for Shulchan Aruch's words.
Blood — Maharshal is stringent; and see Peleti where I wrote: when the measure of the kulya is whole and only the remainder melted and was blood; or only its appearance is like blood and it is not actually blood.
