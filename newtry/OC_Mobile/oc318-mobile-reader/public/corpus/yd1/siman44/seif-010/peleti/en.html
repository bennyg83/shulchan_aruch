There are no treifot in the kulya of fowl. The more correct and simple reason: there is no charitz in it, and all treifot depend on the charitz — this contradicts those who hold in putrefaction and such we do not require charitz; if so, it should disqualify in fowl too; and similarly from shrunken kulya that charitz must also shrink proportionally — it should disqualify in fowl; and see similarly; this is not so, for the measure of kulya in a thin animal is a bean — likewise the measure of charitz in a thin animal is the length of a bean along the length of the kulya; if it shrank, the charitz too shrank below its proper measure — therefore treifah; and this does not apply in fowl, for there is no charitz. And Agur gave the reason that they are enclosed and there is no concern intestines will damage — as those who hold in putrefaction we require charitz. And what was challenged on him: from where do we know kidney treifah is from damage to intestines — it appears he holds like Rashi at the beginning of chapter Behemah HaMaksheh explains: therefore spleen and kidneys were stated in the Mishnah — not that they are treifah in them, meaning life does not depend on them to permit or forbid; and spleen is treifah in the supports because it damages the rumen; and Agur holds likewise kidneys — from where; and examine. And if challenged: how could the beginning of chapter Elu Treifot count laketah in the kulya among treifot — behold treifot from intestines as Tosafot wrote, therefore it counted deficiency of the skull; see above siman 29 what I wrote there.
