Kulya of fowl — for all treifot require the charitz, and this does not apply in fowl; and see Peleti: the difficulty from shrunken kulya that the charitz must also shrink proportionally is not a difficulty; but if in length it remained and only in width it shrank and there is no laketah to the place of the charitz — one may say kosher; requires study; see Peleti resolving Agur.
