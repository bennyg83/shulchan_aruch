Fowl. And he wrote {Rama: (Beit Yosef in name of Orach Chaim)} that Rosh permitted even if it is swollen and black as ink {Rama: (because fowl kidneys are enclosed in bone and intestinal contents do not reach there — specifically in fowl; but in animal and wild beast there is no distinction between thin and large, and their laws are equal — as stated daf 146)}.
