And how can it return to kosher status. This is Kol Bo's view; and Maharshal disagrees and wrote: behold we find likewise a lung that was punctured and sealing in its flesh helps even though before sealing it was treifah; and only by human action do we say regarding meeting of sinews it does not return to kosher — end; he holds even if removed by illness it is kosher. And in my humble opinion, like Kol Bo: certainly where we find explicitly this matter that treifah can return to kosher status we find it; but here who will tell us they permitted this? One may say they did not intend this. [Only] if removed from birth; and from where do we say even by illness it is so, since once it was treifah? And there is no general rule for lung punctured and sealed in flesh, for there the essence of treifah is from puncture and the puncture was already sealed, unlike here. And further we hold in simanim 39 and 36 regarding lung sealing does not help, for it is initial sealing as Rashi and poskim wrote, unlike here; and so acharonim wrote. But say: since it will entirely rot, why declare treifah when it shrinks from illness — it will return healthy; do not say perhaps healing will come that it will not rot further — if so all the more so why declare treifah, for when its wound heals it will certainly return as before; rather it is simple they forbade when it shrinks, for it will waste away and entirely rot — therefore treifah; and this reasoning is certainly sound, and so one should rule like Kol Bo — so it appears to me.
