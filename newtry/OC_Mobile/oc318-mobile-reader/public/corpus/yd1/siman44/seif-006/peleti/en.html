Until the loven — Shach in s.k. 8 and more acharonim brought the view of many authors that in sword-blow we require until the place of the charitz, but in putrefaction in laketah any amount is treifah. And I did not understand, for it appears from the Gemara people of the west that they were concerned until the charitz, but adhesion holds even if it did not reach the charitz; and if you say it deals in sword-blow — how is the Mishnah "one cuts from spleen and kidneys" resolved, and why the first answer in the Gemara that in truth it is treifah; and if so, why did the Gemara challenge R' Elazar and not adhesion; or adhesion who said laketah — say only in putrefaction; and it is proven that here too we require until the charitz; and so is correct.
