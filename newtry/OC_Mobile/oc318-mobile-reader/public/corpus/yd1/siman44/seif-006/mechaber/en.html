There is one who wrote that when they said "if the kidneys were removed — kosher," they meant when removed by hand or when created thus; but if removed on account of illness — since it shrank to less than the measure it was already forbidden — and how can it return to kosher status when they finished consuming and melting? And according to this one must check: if their pouch is full of recognizable milk, this is the beginning of its creation and kosher; and if a cavity and recognizable empty place is found there, it had kidneys but they melted — and treifah.
