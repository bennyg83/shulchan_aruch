And there is no general rule for lung punctured and sealed in flesh, for there the essence of treifah is from puncture and the puncture was already sealed, unlike here; and further we hold in simanim 39 and 36 regarding lung sealing does not help, for it is initial sealing as Rashi and poskim wrote, unlike here; and so acharonim wrote.
