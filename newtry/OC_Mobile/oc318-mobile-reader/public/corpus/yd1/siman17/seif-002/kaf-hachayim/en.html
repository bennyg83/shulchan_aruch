(2) And if one stood it up by hand—even though afterward it walked nicely four cubits—it did not leave presumption of dangerously ill, for it is easier to go from place to place by itself. Turei Beit Shalom seif 13; Zivchei Ratzon there; Minchat Yosef seif 1. However the rabbi Zivchei Tzedek seif 2 wrote to prove not like Turei Beit Shalom, and wrote that if it did not stand in shout or stick and they stood it by hand and afterward it walked four cubits nicely—it is not dangerously ill; and he wrote they acted thus to render fit—see there.
