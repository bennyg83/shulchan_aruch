**Shoes.** Shach wrote: I do not know what relevance shoes on his feet has here — for one who permits because of shoes on his feet permits even Ishmaelite wrapping, as is clear in the Gemara and Ramban. In truth, Mahariv wrote that here it is permitted because since they practice this wrapping even after the seven days of mourning, we do not forbid a matter done in public except what applies only within the seven; and afterward he wrote that it is also permitted because of shoes on his feet — see there. And the Levush omitted this conclusion — "since he has shoes on his feet" — end.
**He returns it.** Shach wrote: and nowadays they do not practice to be strict about this. And it is possible that the shoes prove for them, as Beit Yosef wrote — where he has shoes on his feet he need not return the tear — see there, end.
**The portion.** Darishah wrote in the name of Maharshal that likewise to return twice mikra and once targum is permitted on erev Shabbat.
