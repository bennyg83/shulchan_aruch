The rule is like all other prohibitions, where even less than the prescribed measure is prohibited according to the Torah under Rabbi Yochanan's view in Yoma 74a. Although here the prohibition was created by his own oath, and one might argue that he did not intend to prohibit less than the stated measure, as indicated by the dispute concerning an unspecified oath but not an explicit one, nevertheless, since the Gemara in Yoma explains the prohibition by the possibility that the amounts may combine, the same reasoning applies here, unlike the Ran's statement in his novellae.
