An oath that one will not eat this loaf—he is liable for every kezayit and kezayit that he eats from it, and it is forbidden even in any amount whatsoever. "An oath that I will not eat it"—he is not liable until he eats all of it, but it is forbidden even in any amount whatsoever. Nevertheless, if he left from it less than a kezayit, he is liable as if he ate all of it. Therefore, if he said, "An oath that I will not eat it," and afterward said, "An oath that I will not eat this loaf"—the second also takes effect, for initially he swore only on all of it, but he may eat part of it; and afterward he swore on part of it; and when he eats a kezayit from it he transgresses the second, and when he finishes eating it he becomes liable for the first.
