(There seif 20) He was asked about it. In responsum Shaar Efraim siman 72 he wrote: only where there is punishment of sacrifice or lashes; but intentionally and they did not warn him — he is not asked about it. And against this the Gaon in his book Mishnat Derech Eretz Binyamin (section 35) in Tosafot "but she was led astray," etc., challenged this — for behold there are no lashes nowadays, and nevertheless we rule here in Shulchan Aruch for practical law that he is asked about it — see there. And truly so is explained explicitly in Rosh (chapter 3 of Shevuot): even intentionally and they did not warn him — he is asked about it. However in Shaar HaMelech he brought in the name of Kenesset HaGedolah in this siman in Hagahot Tur that he brought in the name of Raavad in Sefer Temim De'im: intentionally and they did not warn — he is not asked about it.
