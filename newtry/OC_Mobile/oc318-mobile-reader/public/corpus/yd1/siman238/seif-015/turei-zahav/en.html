He need not eat the permitted portion. He swore concerning eating the entire loaf and does not fulfill the oath by eating only part. So wrote the Rosh, Tur, and Ran. From this it appears to me that if one swore to pay his fellow one hundred zuz on a specified day and was prevented from paying the entire amount because he had neither all the money nor equivalent property on that day, he is exempt from the oath, since he could not fulfill it as sworn. We may further learn that if one swore to pay one hundred zuz on a specified day and undertook a penalty of ten gold coins if he violated the oath, and he paid half, he owes the entire penalty, because the oath concerned the entire sum. If, however, he said, “I swear that I will pay you one hundred zuz on such-and-such day, and if I do not pay the one hundred zuz I will give a penalty of ten gold coins,” and he paid part, he owes a proportionate penalty according to what remained unpaid.
