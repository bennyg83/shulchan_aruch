(Ibid., seif 11) That I will not eat it. Likewise, if one first swore not to eat today, and then swore regarding a loaf that he would not eat it, and ate the entire loaf that day, he is liable only once. So rules Rambam. Raavad maintains that the second oath takes effect, because initially he swore only for that day, whereas afterward he swore regarding that loaf forever. Kesef Mishneh writes that nevertheless, with respect to that day, the second oath adds nothing, and he is liable only once. See there.
