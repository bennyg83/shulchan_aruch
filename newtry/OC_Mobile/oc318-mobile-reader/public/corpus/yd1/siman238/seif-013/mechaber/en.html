An oath that one will not eat ten, an oath that one will not eat nine—here there are two oaths; and if he eats nine he transgresses the second, and when he eats the tenth he transgresses also the first. And this applies when he did not vow from known loaves; but if he vowed from known loaves, he is liable only once, for behold in the first oath he was forbidden in every eating and eating in them.
