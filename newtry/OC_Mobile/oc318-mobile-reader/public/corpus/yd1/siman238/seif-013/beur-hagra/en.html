This also appears from the Yerushalmi concerning the dispute between Rabbi Akiva and the Sages at the beginning of the chapter regarding one who ate any amount. It states there: "What practical difference is there between them? One who swore, 'I will not eat this loaf,' and ate all but the smallest amount. According to Rabbi Akiva he is exempt; according to the Sages he is liable." This means that he said, "I will not eat it." Ran.
