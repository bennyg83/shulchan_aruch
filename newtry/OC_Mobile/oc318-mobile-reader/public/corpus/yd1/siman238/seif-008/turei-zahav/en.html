For he regarded it as eating. He certainly did so in order to fulfill his oath. It appears that if he had already eaten earth that day and then said, “A certain item shall be a konam upon me if I ate today,” the item does not become forbidden, because he had not previously treated eating earth as eating. This is also implied by seif 7, which mentions specifically neveilos and treifos in the case of, “My wife shall be a konam,” because those items are intrinsically edible though prohibited. Earth is not. This is obvious.
