Nidui or a prohibition takes effect on an oath. One who says, "Konam, my wife is forbidden to me if I do not eat nevelot and tereifot today"—if he did not eat, his wife is forbidden. And similarly if he said, "Behold, he is under nidui if he does not eat nevelot and tereifot today"—if the day passed and he did not eat, behold he is under nidui. And this is not a stipulation on what is written in the Torah that they say to him, "Do not eat and you will be under nidui."
