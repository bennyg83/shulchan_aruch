The second does not take effect — and behold nine and ten unspecified are a vow; if so he cannot eat ten without there being nine among them that he first vowed on; and in every ten he eats one may say he swore on nine of these in the first oath — automatically there are here two oaths on nine, and an oath does not take effect on an oath; not so when he says "these nine" and an oath that he shall not eat ten unspecified — for those ten unspecified cannot contain these nine he first swore on, since he swore on these nine; it follows if he would eat these nine and ten unspecified, plainly he would be liable for two; therefore if he eats these nine and one unspecified, this one can combine with the nine unspecified — if he would eat another nine unspecified he would be liable on them because of the oath; and since the one unspecified takes effect, these nine too take effect, that this one combines with these nine so that he transgresses two oaths — one on ten and also he already has an oath on these nine — he transgresses two oaths, and it is similar to one who swore not to eat figs and again swore not to eat figs and grapes — he is liable twice on figs; and it is possible if he ate these nine and ten unspecified he is liable for three oaths, similar to figs and grapes — if he also ate the grapes, plainly he is liable three — two on figs and one on grapes. Perishah.
