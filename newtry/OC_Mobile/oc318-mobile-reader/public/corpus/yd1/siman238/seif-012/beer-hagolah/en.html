Statement of Rava there 24b.
(°) Meaning: if she did not consent before Passover and consented after Passover, he is not obligated to betroth her since the time passed; and as written above siman 228 seif 41 — if he were not obligated in the matter without this oath and the day passed, again there is no oath obligation upon him. And since it takes effect after Passover, it also takes effect before Passover in inclusive prohibition; and when the oath is for the woman's benefit, or through matchmaking that without doubt was for her benefit and so she not be shamed — then release is needed even if the time passed, per the first opinion there; and as written there in the name of Rivash, per Rosh's view — so it seems to me.
