The statement of Rava there, Shevuot 24b.
(°) If she did not consent before Pesach but consented afterward, he is not obligated to betroth her, because the designated time has passed. As explained above in siman 228, seif 41, when he had no independent obligation apart from the oath and the day passed, the oath no longer obligates him. Since the oath could take effect regarding the period after Pesach, it also took effect regarding the period before Pesach through an inclusive prohibition. If, however, the oath was undertaken for the woman's benefit, or through a match arranged for her benefit so that she would not be embarrassed, release is required even after the time has passed, according to the first opinion there and the Rivash's explanation of the Rosh.
