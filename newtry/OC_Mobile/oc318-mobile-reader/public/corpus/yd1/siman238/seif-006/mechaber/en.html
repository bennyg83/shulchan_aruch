An oath that one will not eat nevelah and slaughtered meat—or even if he said, "An oath that I will not eat," unspecified—the oath takes effect also on nevelah. And similarly if he said, "An oath that I will not eat figs," and returned and said, "An oath that I will not eat figs and grapes"—both oaths take effect on the figs. {Rama: One who swore to betroth a certain woman if she consents until Pesach, and afterward swore not to betroth her—a second oath takes effect by inclusion, for behold he is obligated to betroth her only when she consents until Pesach, and in any case he is not obligated; and if the first oath was for the woman's benefit or through matchmaking, he has no power to nullify her right; therefore he must annul the second and fulfill the first. (Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of Ran siman 82.)}
