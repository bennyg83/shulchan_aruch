The two weeks. The second P. A.D. is a Christian because of this:
For behold he is not obligated, etc. — so in Beit Yosef in the name of Ran; and he wrote in these words: from the force of the first oath he is not obligated to betroth her except when that so-and-so requests from him; and even after so-and-so's request he is not obligated to betroth her until after Passover; and it turns out the second oath takes effect upon him after Passover; and also before Passover, all the time that so-and-so does not request from him, it also takes effect when he is sworn to betroth her, etc. — only I do not delve into this because it appears the first oath was for his fellow's benefit, after he said "all the time so-and-so requests from me"; and also it appears that oath was on condition of matchmaking between him and the woman, and not entirely in his power to annul others' rights; therefore he will be asked about the second, etc. — end quote. And Rama who wrote "for the woman's benefit or through matchmaking conditions" is not precise — both are one matter; not so Ran who wrote for his fellow's benefit who urged him to betroth this woman — and also the woman's benefit, since done through matchmaking that reveals her mind that she is pleased with this. And I wonder at Ran's view — why tie it to being done through matchmaking? For even without matchmaking she has a right nevertheless, that he is obligated to betroth her if she wants. And one can say: without matchmaking she did not rely that perhaps he will release his oath through a sage; but through matchmaking he binds himself with a penalty if he transgresses — for he compared the woman's right to that so-and-so's right who requested from him to betroth her; and upon that so-and-so there was no penalty, only the oath alone is considered a right that his will was done; therefore it seems to my humble view that for clarity Ran mentioned done through matchmaking conditions, explicitly clear she is pleased; but even without this she is also pleased that she has the choice. And even though in the eyes of the world this match is not fitting for her, nevertheless it is possible it is good in her eyes — for a woman in any case is pleased, for it is good to sit with two legs, as stated in several places — so it seems to me.
