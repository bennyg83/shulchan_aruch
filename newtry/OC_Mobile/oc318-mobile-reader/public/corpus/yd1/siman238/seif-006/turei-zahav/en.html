For he is not obligated, etc. The Beit Yosef cites the Ran in the following language: By virtue of the first oath, he is not obligated to betroth her unless that particular person asks him to do so. Even after that request, he is not obligated to betroth her until after Pesach. The second oath therefore takes effect after Pesach. It likewise takes effect before Pesach so long as the person has not yet asked him, even though once he is asked he will be sworn to betroth her. Nevertheless, I do not establish a definitive ruling, because the first oath appears to have been made for another person’s benefit, since he said, “whenever so-and-so asks me.” It also appears that the oath was connected to engagement conditions between him and the woman, and he has no authority to remove the rights of others. Therefore, he should seek release from the second oath, end quote. The Rama’s phrase, “for the woman’s benefit or through engagement conditions,” is imprecise, for these are one matter. The Ran, however, speaks of two benefits: the benefit of the fellow who urged him to betroth this woman, and the woman’s benefit arising from the engagement conditions, which reveal her satisfaction. I am puzzled why the Ran made her right depend upon formal engagement conditions. Even without them she possesses a right, for he is obligated to betroth her if she wishes. One cannot answer that without formal conditions she does not rely upon the oath because he might obtain release, whereas through conditions he binds himself with a penalty. The Ran compares her right to the right of the person who requested the match, and that person had no penalty, yet the oath itself is considered a benefit to him because his wish is fulfilled. It therefore appears that the Ran mentioned formal engagement conditions only for additional clarity, because then her satisfaction is explicit. Even without them, however, we assume she is satisfied because she has the choice. Even if the match appears unsuitable in others’ eyes, it may be desirable to her, for a woman prefers living as a pair, as stated in many places. So it appears to me.
