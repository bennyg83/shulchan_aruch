(Shulchan Aruch 96) If they did not release the cherem. And likewise oath on cherem and cherem on oath — Rashdam in responsa (simanim 87 and 94) and responsum Mishpetei Shmuel (siman 69). But Maharam of Trani's view (part 1 siman 92) is they take effect one on the other; and see in responsum Be'er Chayim to Kenesset HaGedolah (Yoreh De'ah siman 214).
