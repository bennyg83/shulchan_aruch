The oath takes effect, etc. Since he included forbidden items together with permitted items, the oath takes effect even upon the forbidden items, by virtue of taking effect upon the permitted items. The same applies to figs. Although an oath does not take effect upon an existing oath, as stated below in seif 21, it does take effect through an inclusive formulation.
For he is not obligated, etc. Since the oath takes effect during the period when he is not already under oath to sanctify her, it also takes effect during the period when he is already under oath to sanctify her.
