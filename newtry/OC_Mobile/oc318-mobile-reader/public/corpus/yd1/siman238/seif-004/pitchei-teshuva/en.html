He is not sworn from the Torah on half a measure. See in Sefer Rav Peninim chapter Noach, who wrote there is room to doubt whether an oath takes effect on half a measure in basar b'chalav and kilayim of the vineyard, for eating is not written regarding them; in basar b'chalav it is written "you shall not cook," and in kilayim "lest you be sanctified" — one can say all the more so there is a negative commandment from the Torah; only he is not lashed since these measures are halachah leMoshe miSinai that less than an olive's worth is not lashed — if so no oath takes effect upon him; or perhaps the halachah leMoshe miSinai uproots the verse from its implication and there is only prohibition alone — end quote; see there. And see in his book Teivat Gema, parashat Tolodot, who wrote plainly that one who swears on half a measure from basar b'chalav — no oath takes effect, for all the more so there is a negative commandment — see there.
