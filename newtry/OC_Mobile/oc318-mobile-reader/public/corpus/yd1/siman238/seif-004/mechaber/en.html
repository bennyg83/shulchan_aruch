If he said, "An oath that I will not eat nevelot and tereifot," and he ate—he is not liable for an oath, for behold he is already sworn and standing from Mount Sinai. And if he said, "An oath that I will not eat any amount of nevelot and tereifot," and he ate less than a kezayit—he is liable for the oath, for behold he is not already sworn from Mount Sinai for half the measure. And similarly if he said, "An oath that I will eat less than a kezayit of nevelah and tereifah"—he is liable for the oath. (Rambam chapter 5 of Hilchot Shevuot law 8.)
