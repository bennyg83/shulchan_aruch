It seems that DNA is not allowed to feed a patient who is allowed to eat it, and that it is permissible for him to eat when he was sworn in when he was healthy, but he swore that he was not allowed to eat, and that he should not be allowed to eat, and he should not be cursed, and he shall not swear to him
And likewise if he said "oath that I will eat less than an olive's worth," etc. And Rashba's view in responsum siman 276: the oath does not take effect on half a Torah measure; and so Tosafos wrote in Shevuot end of 23b: since half a measure is forbidden from the Torah, he is sworn and standing from Mount Sinai and not obligated for an oath. However initially — one who swears not to eat any amount from nevelah and terefah — it seems also from Tosafos there he is liable for the oath (Orach Chayim). And it seems he should release the oath and not transgress. And see above siman 239 s.k. 19.
