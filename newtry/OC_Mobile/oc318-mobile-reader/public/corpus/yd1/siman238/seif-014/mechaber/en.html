An oath that one will not eat this loaf—part of it is also included, for he swore on every kezayit and kezayit of it that he will eat it; therefore, if part of it was burned, he is obligated to eat the permitted part, for behold he fulfills his oath thereby, that he swore on every kezayit and kezayit of it that he will eat it. Nevertheless, when it is whole before us, he must eat all of it.
