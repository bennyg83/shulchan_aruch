An oath that one will not eat, and he ate dirt—he is exempt, for it is not included in eating when unspecified. And if he specified that he will not eat dirt and he ate it, he is liable for a kezayit; and for less than a kezayit he is exempt but it is forbidden. And similarly, an oath that one will not eat a pit—he is exempt but it is forbidden for less than a kezayit.
