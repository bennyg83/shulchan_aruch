One who swore to pay so-and-so a maneh in the month of Adar, and afterward swore to pay another at the same time, and it is written in the first document, "whenever he claims it from him"—and similarly in the second; and now the second comes to claim him—all the time the first did not claim him, he is obligated to pay the second, for behold the oath found a place to take effect; but afterward he may fulfill the first if he can.
