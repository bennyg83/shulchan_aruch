There was an incident before the sages of Vilna: the spinal cord was black from beginning to end and the brain inside remained intact and there was no other defect, and they permitted it — for we do not find that a change in appearance disqualifies the spinal cord. And some say this blackness is redness, only it was singed — see Ba'er Heitev. And Peri Chadash wrote in siman 101 and ruled well: since there is no defect on account of the change, and change in appearance alone does not disqualify except for lung and kidney. Indeed if the skin is defective on account of change in appearance — the majority of the circumference of skin covering the brain — treifah, on this account (Maharam Tit). And Shach wrote: even if nothing at all remains above or below — kosher; and with this he was lenient regarding dragging; and Peri Chadash disagrees on this too.
