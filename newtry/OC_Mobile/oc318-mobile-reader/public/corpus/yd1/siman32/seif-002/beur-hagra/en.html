There — nismamaz (quivered) — and as Arukh and Ri"f and Rashba explain: (Likut) Or it was crushed, etc., liquefied, etc. — as Arukh's text and he explains the gemara's version there: humerakh — invalid; nismamas — invalid; nismamem — kosher. And he wrote: humerakh — meaning spilled like a pitcher; nismamas — melted like wax from fire and cannot stand upright but bends; nismamem — wobbles and weakens — "the shades tremble" — Targum — perhaps from gevraya (men) nismamemim — end (enough):
