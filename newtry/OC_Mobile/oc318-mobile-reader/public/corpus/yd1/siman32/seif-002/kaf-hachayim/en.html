Spinal cord severed, etc. Bach wrote we do not declare treifah for adjacent punctures around circumference combining to majority like gargeret except majority severed in one place; Beit Din, Shach s.k. 1. But Peri Chadash ot 1 on Bach's words — not clear to be lenient at all logically in spinal cord more than gargeret; and so Pri Tevu'ah ot 1 — likewise if punctured punctures like wing combines to majority circumference and also whole between them combines all if gap between puncture and puncture not full puncture — unlike Bach.
