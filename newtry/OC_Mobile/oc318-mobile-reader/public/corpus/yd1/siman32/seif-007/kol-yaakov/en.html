G) There in this order etc. if after writing in order one or two of first parshiyot disqualified and known clearly has other parshiyot like those disqualified written before these last ones — permitted to attach; even though parshiyot not written from start to attach to these parshiyot but to other parshiyot; but if doubt whether written before or not — disqualified. Avodat HaGeressik siman 60, Orach Mishpat ot 1 Yud-Aleph Hagahat Pri Megadim Mishnat Hasidim ot 1, Yeshuot Yaakov ot 8, Sha'arei Teshuvah ot 2, Maamar Mordechai ot 1, Maleh Hashoar kelal 8 ot 2 in Chokmat, Knesset HaGedolah siman 9 ot 2 in Leshon Hakodesh Emek Melech kelal 20 ot 1.
