We are concerned for the place of the knots. Ran wrote it requires waiting and examination like the law of a fallen animal in siman 58; and so Maharshal. And it appears to me that even though Rama ruled in siman 58 seif 6 that we are not expert in examination — that is regarding crushing; but not here where there is concern because of breaking the spine, and in this we are expert:
