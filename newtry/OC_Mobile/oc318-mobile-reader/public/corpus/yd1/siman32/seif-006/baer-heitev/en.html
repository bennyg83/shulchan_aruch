The knots. And likewise if the stick broke at the time of the blow — it makes a blow like at the head of the stick (Kol Bo). And nevertheless one need not examine entirely but only the place that was struck — see Peri Chadash. And Taz wrote it requires waiting and examination like the law of fallen animal in siman 58 seif 6; and even though Rama wrote there we are not expert in examination — nevertheless for this matter where the only concern is breaking the spine, we are expert.
