(Siman 32 seif 6) Animal struck on spine with stick, etc. If knots — concerned for knots perhaps cord severed. And Rashi on gemara (daf 51). Difficult — gemara proves concern is for crushing limbs — since beginning says no concern for crushing limbs, implies where says concerned is for cord. And so Tur explicitly in hints and Rosh. And Hagahot siman 58 wrote if struck in one place no concern except opposite it. And therefore concern is crushing that place — entire siman deals thus. And from gemara ordering Rav Yehuda struck head in niflin section not in cord section (daf 45) — simple. Must say Rashi and Mechaber deal with walking normally — presumably from what struck on spine with stick it can walk; since walking — no further concern for cord as siman 58. And informs us here nevertheless requires examination of cord at knots; as Ran in name "there is an opinion" brought Beit Yosef — examine.
