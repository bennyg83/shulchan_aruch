Bayit Chadash; see Dagul MeRevavah who challenged this explanation and explained another — deals when fell from place not ten handbreadths high; if could not walk at all would be permitted and attribute to some mere pain; but since drags legs worse — thing indicates from cord severance — end; see in his book Teshuvat Noda Biyehudah YD siman 22 and Tiferet Tzvi chelek YD siman 67 ot 3. See Noda Biyehudah Tinyana Chullin siman 17 who wrote if limps only on one leg — no concern for spinal cord — for if from spine it would be in middle and spread equally to both legs and should limp on both (and so somewhat from Shivat Tzion vol 2 end siman 59 — we'll bring below siman 55 s.k. 4). Also wrote if Jew did not see fall — no concern for fall; and what gentile says after leaving butcher shop not believed; even if said from start perhaps intended to enhance his merchandise thinking if says from fall preferred — on his name.
