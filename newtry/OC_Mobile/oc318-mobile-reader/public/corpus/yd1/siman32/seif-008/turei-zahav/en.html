And Mordechai wrote on Chullin in the name of R' Tuvia of Vienna regarding one who bought a goose from the market and it could not stand — permitted, since we did not see it fall one does not forbid out of doubt; and one says shigrona seized it; and so Orach Chaim general rule 55.
