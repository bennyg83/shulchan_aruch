But if it was known that it fell — we are concerned. Perhaps with its fall the spinal cord was severed; and therefore it drags its legs, for severing the spinal cord is common with its fall — so ends Tur. And even though for a fallen animal in general we are concerned, as explained below siman 58 — one could say that for a fallen animal in general it is permitted when it walked four amot as stated there; but here, since it drags its legs, it is not called walking, and examination is always required; and for us who are not expert in examination — forbidden forever, as will be explained in siman 58:
