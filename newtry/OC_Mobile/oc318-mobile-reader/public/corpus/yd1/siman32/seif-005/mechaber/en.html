These treifos of the spinal cord apply until the end of the first third of a parshah and including; and if one of the two cords of the first or second splits was severed — treifah; but if the cords of the third split were severed — kosher. And in a bird, the treifah of the cord extends until between the wings — the beginning of the place where they are joined to the body. {Rama: And some render treifah in a bird until the place where the bone's lying ends where it joins the body (Tosafot, Rashba, Mordechai, and the Long Orach Chaim general rule 55, section 2, and Ta'alumot 158); and so is the custom unless there is great financial loss (Sephardic custom).}
