Until the place where the bone's lying ends. This is written in Orach Chaim in the name of Rashba; but Tur wrote in the name of Rashba until the place their joining to the body ends; and accordingly if the spinal cord was severed opposite the place where the bone lies between the wings — kosher. Darkei Moshe brought these views; and in the Long Orach Chaim general rule 55 siman 2 ruled to be stringent like Orach Chaim's words. And it appears that what Rama wrote here that in great financial loss one should not be stringent — means one should not be so stringent as to declare treifah until opposite the place where the bone's lying ends; but nevertheless to be so lenient as to permit even if severed opposite the place of their joining to the body and rely on the first view that wrote until the beginning of the place they are joined, etc. — does not appear correct; for in Darkei Moshe he wrote one should be stringent not to rule so; and Maharshal ruled one should be stringent even in a place where the bone lies; and so ruled Maharach z"l:
