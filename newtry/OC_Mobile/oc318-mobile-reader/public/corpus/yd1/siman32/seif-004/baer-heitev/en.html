And Shach wrote: therefore the Mechaber writes "a bit of the brain missing" — because if much were missing then certainly when holding it upright the empty portion outside his hand would sag; and when melted like wax one does not distinguish between little and much because its way is to melt entirely. And if water is found in the spinal cord — in any case treifah — for it is not relevant to say brain surrounding it; as gemara daf 92, brought in Knesset HaGedolah.
