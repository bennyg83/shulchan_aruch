(seif 2) But something customary to rinse. Ra'ah's view in s.v. "specifically to place forbidden in a permitted vessel" — permitted where its custom is to rinse the vessel; but with two types of food that touched each other — forbidden in every case; and Rashba in Ma'adanei HaMelekh wrote that this too is permitted.
(Taz s.k. 3) In every case one should forbid. It is not understood — for in truth Tur forbids placing in a permitted vessel lest one not rinse; and perhaps Taz's intent per his words in s.k. 1 is regarding a vessel that was rinsed, where one could distinguish between meat and cheese and between vessels; but these deal with without rinsing and the forbidden substance is on the surface, like meat and cheese; and what did Ba'al HaItur innovate, and what Taz wrote that certainly in every case it is forbidden — meaning it is obvious in every case, namely meat and cheese, and likewise a vessel, for there is no leniency here more than meat and cheese.
