Everything, etc. — as written in the Mishnah "provided, etc." and the Gemara says "let it suffice," etc., and this is what "and specifically," etc. means:
(Lekut) Everything, etc., and specifically, etc. — on Rashi Chullin 8a s.v. "let it suffice," etc. — he explains forbidden and not permitted, for the language of "let it suffice" implies forbidden and not permitted; and that he did not challenge on permission is because Abaye could not challenge that he did not mention permission l'chatchila and slaughtering is also unnecessary, lest one say he holds rinsing is required even for roasted meat and rinsing is not needed for roasted meat because of blood on the surface unlike meat — see above siman 120 s.k. 12 (end).
(Lekut) And specifically, etc. — in Me'iri he brought proof from "they placed the fats on the breasts" and Tosafot 8b s.v. "not," etc. — it is not proof, for from the Torah it is certainly permitted; furthermore priests are zealous; and Ra'ah disagreed there 112a (end).
(Lekut) And specifically, etc. — from the challenge in Chullin 8b "and it was fixed one," etc., and Rashi: fats in the market to sell; and Hagahot Ashiri there s.v. "from here," etc. — his reason is that because of scoring the meat, rinsing suffices, for milk is lean; and as written siman 64 — see there unlike actual milk — (end).
And specifically a thing, etc. — on Tosafot Avodah Zarah 32a s.v. "but," and from here Rabbenu Tam, etc.
