Except that rinsing is required, etc. — this means plain rinsing without rubbing; but where there is knife pressure and greasiness we require rubbing until one knows by estimate that greasiness left him, such as cutting meat with a knife that cut cheese; but pressure without greasiness or greasiness without pressure is permitted with plain rinsing. Chochmat Adam section 2 and in Biur section 1; Or HaYashar section 1; Pit'hei Teshuva there; Beit Yitzchak there; Zivchei Tzedek section 3; and specifically milk that was not dissolved in water, but milk dissolved in water adheres more and requires great rubbing. Or HaYashar there. Pit'hei Teshuva there. Yad Ephraim on seif 2, and he wrote that great rubbing is required until one knows by estimate that the prohibition left him. Zivchei Tzedek there.
(4) [Seif 2] Everything that requires rinsing, such as placing permitted cold meat in a forbidden cold bowl — forbidden l'chatchila, etc.; and the same to place forbidden cold in a permitted cold vessel that is not customary to rinse — forbidden l'chatchila; and if customary to rinse — permitted. Beit Yosef below siman 94 in s.v. "sheep"; Netiv 15 section 29. Shach s.k. 2 in the name of Rashba in Tur HaShalem long and short. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur section 2; Peri Chadash section 2; Levush section 3; Beit Lechem Yehudah section 3; Shulchan Petach David section 2; Beit Yitzchak section 2; Zivchei Tzedek section 4.
(5) And that which we say it is forbidden l'chatchila to place cold meat in a forbidden cold bowl lest one eat without rinsing, etc. — this is in a vessel not rinsed initially; but in a metal vessel, if the forbidden vessel was rinsed initially, it is permitted l'chatchila to place cold meat there and the meat does not require rinsing. Shach s.k. 3.
