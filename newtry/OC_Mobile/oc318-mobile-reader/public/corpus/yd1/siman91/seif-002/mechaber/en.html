Everything that requires rinsing — such as placing permitted cold meat in a cold forbidden bowl — is forbidden l'chatchila, lest one eat without rinsing; and this applies specifically to cooked meat, which is not customary to rinse, but something customary to rinse — such as raw meat and the like — is permitted l'chatchila: {Rama: And specifically something that has a little moisture; but something completely dry — if the vessel did not absorb except through cold use — it is permitted to place dry food in it without rinsing at all (Da'at Yachid), and see below siman 122.}
