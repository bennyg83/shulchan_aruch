(6) And if the vessel was wiped and not rinsed — some say wiping helps like rinsing, for certainly if the vessel was wiped in a way that has no greasiness at all, there is no reason to require rinsing. Shach there in the name of Ra'ah. And some say wiping does not help, for even if wiped well and one placed meat, the meat also requires rinsing, and l'chatchila it is forbidden to place in the said vessel that was wiped without rinsing; and specifically if the vessel was rinsed may one place meat. Batei Kehunah there daf 93 side 2 in the name of Rashba; and so Peri Chadash there — here it deals with a forbidden bowl that was wiped but not rinsed, and since rinsing is required, if used without rinsing one rinses what was used. Mishnat Zekenim there. Zivchei Tzedek section 6, and he wrote and so is primary.
