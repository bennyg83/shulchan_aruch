Where we say, etc. — as written 97a, and the same for salted meat as written there 2 that it is like roasted, and this is what "and likewise its companion," etc. means; and as written that milk is different because it spreads and forbids the kid, and so here; and even the fatty piece is forbidden as Pesachim 76b "slaughtered meat that is fatty," etc., and even Levi did not explain except because he holds smell is not a matter (and see above siman 105 s.k. 55); and this is what "all of it is forbidden, and this, and all the more this" means.
