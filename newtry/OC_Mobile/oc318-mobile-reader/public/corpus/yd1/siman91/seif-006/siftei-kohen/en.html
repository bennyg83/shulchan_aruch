If it were fatty, etc. Meaning if one of the pieces were fatty — whether meat or cheese — all is forbidden, for then one goes and flavors the other and it is as if all is fatty, as above siman 70 s.k. 4 and siman 105.
And see below siman 105 s.k. 9. It is explained that for us there is no difference between lean and fatty, and all salting we estimate at sixty — see there.
