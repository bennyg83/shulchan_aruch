(seif 5 in hagahah) And it is good to be stringent where there is no great loss. And in lean meat one may say there are not two uncertainties — we are not expert between lean and fatty, and we are not expert between salting of roast and pot — peeling suffices; afterward I saw in Peri Megadim siman 105 in Siftei Da'at section 26 that he wrote plainly that even in lean meat, salting of roast is forbidden; and in my view it is not necessary.
