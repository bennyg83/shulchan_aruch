And it waited — but before it waited it does not return to hot; and that which above siman 69 implies it is immediately considered hot — the rabbis distinguish between meat and gravy, for gravy is immediately hot; and Shach distinguishes regarding blood — the nature of salt for blood is immediately hot, but regarding the heat of the piece itself one needs the measure of salting; and see Peleti who proved not like Shach, only the Mechaber was stringent regarding a Torah prohibition even before the measure of salting, but in basar b'chalav, which is rabbinic salting, he was lenient until it waits the measure of salting — see there.
For great need, etc.; and within the measure of salting Shach's view is to forbid even where there is loss; and see Peleti — when it is within the measure of salting and was salted to extract blood, there is not so much heat to cook; but if its action is to extract blood it is immediately hot and one may not permit even with great loss; and likewise if it already expelled its blood and was salted again, even with loss; and see there what he wrote about gravy from meat — even within the measure of salting it forbids according to all, and great loss does not help.
That we are not expert — it appears specifically regarding nevelah and the like, which is a Torah doubt; but in basar b'chalav, which is a rabbinic doubt, it is permitted like nispach.
And there is no difference — certainly the Mechaber holds like Rashba that even in salted meat there is a difference between lower and upper prevails — but this is only in something fatty; but in lean meat, which forbids only the measure of peeling, there is no difference, for it does not forbid more than peeling; and in peeling, even though the lower is cold it absorbed a little — nevertheless peeling is required; therefore there is no difference.
Therefore — and we do not say as in other places, or "since it is busy expelling it does not absorb," etc. — we do not say this only regarding blood and not regarding other absorption, not like Orach Chaim general rule 12 at all — Shach; and see Peleti who ties it to the dispute of Rashi and Rashbam — see there.
On the entire vessel — peeling does not apply; but on part of the vessel one can peel and hag'alah is not required.
Nevertheless the gravy — see Peleti, the main reason is that we are not expert between eaten and not eaten because of salt — see there.
