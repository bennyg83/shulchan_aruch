and this is clear to me — if so in meat and cheese there is no salt action to extract blood, it immediately heats and forbids; and it is clear one should be stringent even though rabbinic prohibition.
And for great need, etc. — the Acharonim raised that if meat already expelled blood, even with loss one may not permit, for Rosh wrote where the strength of salt did not cease, etc.; and since blood already expelled, how does salt's strength cease; and the Acharonim do not hold that exudation is mainly blood and gravy is minor; and I add even if salted to extract blood and there is gravy exuding within the measure of salting, this forbids even after long waiting, for regarding exuded gravy we do not say the strength of salt ceased; and Rosh's words require saying the same regarding gravy; and Rashi also appears to hold pot salting is hot, proven from kodshim blood; and one more proof — only within the measure of salting and not afterward, as in Rashi's incident above siman 69 s.k. 4; and the challenge from "hot" implies those not eaten because of salt are still hot — Rashi does not hold the strength of salt ceased here; and Rashi's challenge from "hot" stands; and Rashi resolved the Gemara's "hot" means not eaten because of salt — forbidden forever as salted for roasting; and without waiting Rashi agrees even pot salting forbids; and all Tosafot's challenges on him are resolved — investigate, and there is no distinction here.
