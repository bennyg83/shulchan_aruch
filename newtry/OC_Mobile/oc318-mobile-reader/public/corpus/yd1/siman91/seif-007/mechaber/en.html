That which we distinguish between "eaten because of its salt" and "not eaten because of its salt" — these matters apply to raw meat; but hot roasted meat that fell into salted meat — even if it is eaten because of its salt — requires peeling (see s.k. 20), and if it has cracks or is seasoned with spices and it is hot roasted — all of it is forbidden: {Rama: And likewise baked and cooked (Maharai in Hagahot Shach, Hagahot Mordechai, Semak, and Maharshal); and some say even if they are cold the law is thus (Beit Yosef according to Rosh, Terumat HaDeshen, and Tur), and so one should practice if there is no great loss.}
