With cheese. Avodat HaTaharah, and this is Shach's words; and Peri Megadim who brought that Knesset HaGedolah wondered at Shach, for in Tur and Shulchan Aruch it implies there is no remedy through rinsing in bread that adheres strongly, since they wrote that if touched it is forbidden to eat with cheese; and Peri Megadim wrote that thereby Taz's "one must be careful," etc. is resolved — as it appears there are two concerns: lest one eat with cheese, and lest one forget to rinse; and whence do we learn that if we say rinsing does not help in bread it is resolved — investigate; and in my humble opinion it rather appears from Tur and Mechaber's language that Shach's view is somewhat implied, for they first wrote Ba'al HaItur's words that everything requiring rinsing is forbidden l'chatchila, then wrote this law "one must be careful," etc., and did not write immediately after seif 1 — it implies this law depends on seif 2; and more clear in Tur's language — see there; and what is written that from Taz's words it implies there is no remedy in bread that adheres strongly can be rejected — for certainly even if it adheres strongly, scraping certainly helps like cucumbers siman 96 seif 5; if so what Taz wrote "forbidden to eat with cheese" means it is permitted to eat through scraping; therefore their intent is forbidden to eat with cheese without any remedy; if so also no proof that scraping is not required and rinsing suffices per Shach; nevertheless it appears there is a difference in bread whether touched on whole bread or sliced outside on the hard side rinsing suffices, but if touched inside on the soft part scraping is required as s.k. 1; and what Peri Megadim wrote "thereby it is resolved," etc. — is not so, for even if rinsing does not suffice, scraping helps, and therefore there are always two concerns; however in truth there is difficulty — for this doubt "lest he not eat with cheese" is not considered a doubt at all, as Shach wrote siman 97 s.k. 2, because it is customary to eat bread with all kinds of foods.
