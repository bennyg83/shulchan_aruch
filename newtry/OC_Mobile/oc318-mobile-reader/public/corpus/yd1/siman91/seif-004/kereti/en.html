Hot meat — specifically in a pot; but on a spit it is forbidden only the measure of peeling.
One peels — because the upper prevails and it absorbed a little; nevertheless peeling is required.
Permitted — the conclusion of the poskim; not only meat into milk but the same law applies to permitted into forbidden — nevertheless the law is so.
And in the place where Peleti wrote — for Magen Avraham's view is specifically where peeling is required because the lower prevails, for then it absorbed a little — they are stringent to peel; in that case 60 is not required; but where by law peeling is required, the peel forbids like any other prohibition; and Shach, Peri Chadash, and Maharsha hold to distinguish between when the peel is recognizable and stands in place — then its taste forbids — unlike when the peel is not recognizable and does not stand in place — then it is not nullified; and I wrote to distinguish between when the permitted absorbed, as in basar b'chalav, and when the prohibition absorbed, as in all prohibitions; and see Peleti — investigate.
