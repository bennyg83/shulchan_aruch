All is forbidden. If there is no 60, and specifically when falling into boiling meat in a pot; but not on roast hot outside the pot — per Tur and for us forbidden in every case up to 60, as siman 92 end s.k. 3 and siman 105 — see there.
Because the lower prevails. Meaning the lower that is hot heats the upper that is cold.
One peels the meat, etc. Because here the lower is cold it cools the upper, except it absorbed a little; therefore peeling is required; and see siman 105 s.k. 3 further regarding lower prevails — even hot milk on cold meat through pouring without interrupting stream, peeling suffices; and likewise if stream interrupted, peeling required — see there.
And in a place where the meat, etc. — requires study, for Tosafot Pesachim (96b) s.v. 'if there is,' etc. (brought by Beit Yosef s.k. 100, R' Yitzchak Netter section 13, Beit Yosef s.k. 105) and Hagahot Ashiri in Mahariach's name, R' Yitzchak, Mordechai, and Agudah there wrote that a gid of the sciatic nerve that was salted and cooked afterward requires 60 against the peel first forbidden through salting; and so Agur siman 1078 in Maharil's name on fowl cooked without removing veins — requires 60 against peel first forbidden through salting; and Beit Yosef brings it plainly siman 22 s.k. 4 and Bach there plainly siman 10 s.k. 5 regarding slaughtering with gentile knife — peel the place of slaughter, for even b'dieved forbidden the dish as any meat cooked without peeling — end of his words; and it appears the Mechaber is not challenged, for where peeling is required and cooked without peeling, permitted b'dieved — similar to here where milk is permitted because Tosafot, Rosh, and poskim wrote peeling does not apply, namely something blended; and similarly if meat cooked without peeling and blended unrecognizably, no peeling applies, permitted b'dieved; and now the general 'permitted b'dieved' is resolved — if recognizable, peeling would be required as siman 72; but if cooked and still recognizable where peeling applies, 60 required; and somewhat implied in Orach Chaim general rule 29 for the discerning — see there; and now the Mechaber's hagahah siman 98 s.k. 4 that a vessel forbidden through salting the measure of peeling requires afterward 60 is resolved; and therefore do not be lenient where recognizable and peeling applies; and anyway Maharai, Maharshal chapter 25 s.k. 75, and Terumat HaChacham ruled like Riva that even in milk 60 is required against the peel; and Terumat HaChacham general rule 23 s.k. 5 is stringent even in milk where there is not so much loss — see there.

And if they fell into each other while cold, etc. And Orach Chaim general rule 40 din 10 wrote in Mordechai and Semag's name — namely specifically in basar b'chalav; but if permitted cold fell into forbidden clear cold, peeling is required; and he brought the Mechaber at the beginning of general rule 22; and it requires study, for this is not found in Mordechai and Semag, and on the contrary their words imply the opposite — see there; and so appears in the Gemara chapter Keitzad Tzolin (Pesachim 76a) there is no distinction; and so Shach siman 20; and Maharil in Orach Chaim explicitly; and so implied by poskim.
