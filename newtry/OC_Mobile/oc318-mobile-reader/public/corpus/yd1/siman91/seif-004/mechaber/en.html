Hot meat and milk that mixed together — and even cold meat into hot milk or cold milk into hot meat — all are forbidden, because the lower one prevails; but hot milk that fell on cold meat, or hot meat that fell into cold milk — one peels (kli'ah) the meat and the rest of the meat is permitted (and in a place where the meat requires peeling, if one did not peel it and cooked it thus, it is permitted b'dieved) (Arukh general rule 29), and all the milk is permitted (Rambam, R' Chananel, R' Yitzchak, and Rif), and if they fell into each other while cold, one rinses the meat and it is permitted.
