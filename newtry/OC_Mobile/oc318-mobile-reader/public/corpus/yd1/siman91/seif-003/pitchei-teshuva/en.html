Customary to rinse. See Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav end s.k. 1 that he concludes specifically placing in a forbidden wiped vessel is permitted if customary to rinse; but on actual forbidden substance, even if not greasy, it is forbidden and requires great rubbing; therefore Tur brought Ba'al HaItur's words regarding vessels — see there; and see Shach here s.k. 4 and below siman 105 s.k. 5 — it is explained there is no distinction, and even on actual forbidden substance it is permitted if customary to rinse, for rubbing is not required, as he himself wrote in Shulchan Petach David that I brought above section 1 — specifically if forbidden grease was touched requires rubbing; and Chavat Da'at and responsum Beit Ephraim disagree as written there; and specifically where there is knife pressure and greasiness requires rubbing; and from Tur's words who wrote Ba'al HaItur's words regarding vessels — there is no proof he means that if not customary to rinse, even on a forbidden vessel, but if customary to rinse one may say even on actual forbidden substance it is permitted; and Tur mentioned the vessel only initially and not at the end — see there; and see responsum Radbaz Chadashot siman 295.
