That he wrote and it appears to them that if chametz was absorbed hot, etc. — one may say it appears to them thus, for otherwise whence would we learn that it is permitted to use matzah hot — perhaps it speaks of cold use; and it is strained to say they imply that even for beit se'or and charoset it is permitted cold, and since it says except beit se'or and charoset it proves it deals with hot use; but one may say that in truth beit se'or and charoset are forbidden even cold, but if we say that even if chametz was absorbed hot it is permitted cold — what difference whether absorbed by hot or cold — therefore when it says permitted to use matzah it means even hot use; and therefore it specifically mentions chametz was used cold; and nevertheless per Rosh's words one must explain what Tur wrote below siman 94, etc., that it implies even cold moist use, and so Beit Yosef there, and it implies even something not customary to rinse like fruits, etc.; and therefore what is written "and it implies even something not customary," etc. — is only for permission, for certainly if one will not forget he will rinse it, unlike cold moist where rinsing does not apply.
