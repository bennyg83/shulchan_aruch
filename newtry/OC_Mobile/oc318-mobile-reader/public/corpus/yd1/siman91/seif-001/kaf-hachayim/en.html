(1) [Seif 1] Meat and cheese that touched each other are permitted except that rinsing is required, etc., and specifically when one of them is moist, but if both are dry even rinsing is not required. Bach. Shach s.k. 1. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur section 1; Peri Chadash section 1; Levush section 1; Beit Lechem Yehudah section 1; Kereti section 1; Mateh Yehonatan on Terumat HaChacham general rule 21 section 1; Berit Kehunah in Sha'ar 6 section 1; Chayei Adam general rule 42 section 1; Chochmat Adam section 1; Beit Yitzchak in Amud HaZahav section 1; and when both are salted, even if dry, rinsing is required as Shulchan Aruch writes below seif 5 — Chochmat Adam in Biurim section 2; and likewise sharp foods even if dry require rinsing — Chochmat Adam there and in Chidush section 1; Pit'hei Teshuva section 1; Beit Yitzchak there; Zivchei Tzedek section 1.
