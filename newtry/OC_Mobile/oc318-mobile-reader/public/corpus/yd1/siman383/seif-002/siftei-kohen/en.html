But if he died... When the Sages permitted a groom to observe the seven days of celebration first, there is concern that he may treat mourning lightly. If the marriage canopy had already taken place, there is no such concern because no leniency was granted; the days of celebration simply preceded the mourning. Similarly, one whose mourning occurs during a festival may be alone with his wife, as the Rama writes below in siman 399:2, because the festival itself postpones mourning and its joy is less intense than that of a groom. So write the authorities.
