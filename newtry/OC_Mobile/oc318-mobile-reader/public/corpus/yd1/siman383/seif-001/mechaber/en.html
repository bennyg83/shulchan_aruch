Prohibition of use of the bed for a mourner. And it contains 2 seifim. A mourner is forbidden in use of the bed; but in other closeness permitted, even pouring the cup and spreading the bed and washing his face, hands, and feet — whether in his mourning or her mourning:
{Rama: (but hugging and kissing one should be strict) (Rabbeinu Yerucham);} and permitted to eat with her from one bowl; and permitted to sleep with her — he in his garment and she in her garment; nevertheless because of "Go, go" we say regarding a nazir, a mourner should be strict not to sleep with her on a bed at all.
