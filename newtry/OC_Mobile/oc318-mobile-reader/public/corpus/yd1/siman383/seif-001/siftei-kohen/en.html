Whether in his mourning, etc. The Perishah wrote: meaning even in her mourning, where there is more concern, since he is not a mourner and everything depends on him and not on her; end quote. He was not precise. On the contrary, in his mourning there is greater concern, for if his inclination overpowers him she will listen to him, since she is not a mourner, as stated in the first chapter of Ketubot 4; see there.
He in his garment, etc. But without garments it is forbidden, even if an apron separates, even on Shabbat and the festival. Tur and Ramban.
