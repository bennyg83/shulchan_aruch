Not to arouse mourning for one's dead thirty days before a festival. In it are 3 seifim.
One to whom a relative died before the thirty days adjacent to a festival—he should not eulogize him once the thirty days adjacent to the festival enter; and even if he has a eulogy unrelated to this, such as one to whom a relative died within thirty whom he may eulogize; and even if he died on the eve of the festival, forbidden to eulogize with him for the dead who died before him thirty days before the festival.
