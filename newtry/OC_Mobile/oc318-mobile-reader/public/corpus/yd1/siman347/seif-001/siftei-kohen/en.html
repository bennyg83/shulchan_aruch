One whose deceased, etc. See Orach Chaim, siman 547, seif 3, regarding this law. The rule that on Shabbat one may wait near the techum in order to bring the needs of the deceased is stated in Orach Chaim 306:3. The law of a non-Jew who made a grave or brought flutes on Shabbat is explained there in siman 325, seifim 14 and 15; see there.
