Kuzu, etc. These are the letters that immediately follow, in the alphabet, the letters of the phrase “Hashem, our God, Hashem is One.”
But nothing may be added inside. The Rambam wrote this because people were inserting names of angels inside the mezuzah for protection, and he strongly objected. The mezuzah must be written exactly as it appears in the Torah. Protection comes automatically through fulfilling the mitzvah commanded by Hashem, not through added formulas.
