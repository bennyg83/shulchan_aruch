On, etc., in one scroll, etc. — Yerushalmi chapter 1 of Megillah: Ribl asked — why do we not say there is in sefarim as for tefillin and mezuzot? Because sefarim are written on two folios while tefillin and mezuzot are written only on one folio. R' Yochanan son of R' Chiyya objected: the written ruling of Rabbah is that sefarim are written on two hides while tefillin and mezuzot are written only on one hide. And see seif 2.
And at its end, etc. For even in sefarim one needs only enough to wind a column — unlike here.
