With what and how it is written. In it are 15 seifim. How they write the mezuzah: they write the two parashiyot of Shema and "Ve'hayah im shamo'a" on one page on one sheet; and make a space below and a space above like half a fingernail; and at its beginning leave enough to roll it after it is rolled. (And at its end one need not leave any at all) (Tur).
