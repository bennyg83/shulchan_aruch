He should leave enough to roll it. Shevut Yaakov defends the scribal custom not to leave enough blank parchment to roll the mezuzah. Although the Shulchan Aruch states this requirement without qualification, most authorities appear not to require it. Moreover, leaving extra parchment may cover the divine Name Shaddai or become damaged and disgraceful, so established custom may be followed.
