As with a Torah scroll. The other laws concerning defective and plene spellings are the same as those governing the writing of the passages of Shema and Vehayah im shamoa in tefillin, as explained in Orach Chaim, siman 32. The Tur writes, based on the Gemara, that a mezuzah may be written without copying from a written text. The Mechaber and Rama do not state this here because they relied upon what was explained in Orach Chaim, siman 32, regarding tefillin, that the passages of Shema and Vehayah may be written without a written text when they are fluent in the scribe's mouth. It is therefore obvious that the same applies to a mezuzah. Maadanei Melech, folio 87a, questioned them without cause.
