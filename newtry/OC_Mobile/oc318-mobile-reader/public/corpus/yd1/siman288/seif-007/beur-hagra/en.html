Its law, etc. — as written in first chapter of Megillah 8b: there is no difference, etc.; and in Yerushalmi there Rabbi Zeira in the name of R' Ami: as scribes write, so one writes tefillin and mezuzot.
And the tagin. Rambam: seven more tagin, but all on Shaatnez Getz; and per his view; and as written in Orach Chayim siman 36. And nevertheless also in tefillin some of them are on Shaatnez Getz; and commentators wrote they are large tagin.
