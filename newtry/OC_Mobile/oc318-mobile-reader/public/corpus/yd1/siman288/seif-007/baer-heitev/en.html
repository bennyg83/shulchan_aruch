Regarding. Shach wrote: and the other laws of its writing with missing and extra letters is as they write those sections in tefillin, and it is explained in Orach Chayim siman 32. And Tur wrote that it was written not from a written text; and the Rav did not write that he relied on what is written in Orach Chayim regarding tefillin — and the same for mezuzah — end quote. And Taz wrote: nevertheless one should write from a written text — meaning from a corrected book, lest he forget the missing and extra letters; and therefore they did not mention it in Shulchan Aruch.
