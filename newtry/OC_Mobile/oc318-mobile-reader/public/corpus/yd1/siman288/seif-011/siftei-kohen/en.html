Twenty-two lines. Their opening words are as follows: Shema; Havayah, the word following "and you shall love"; hadevarim; levavecha; uveshochbecha; bein; vehayah; metzaveh; bechol; yoreh; eisev; pen; vehishtachavisem; hashamayim; vaavadetem; vesamtem; osam; osam, which must appear twice, unlike the text in Ateret Zekenim; baderech; uvisharecha; asher; al haaretz. This is the text of the Tur and the halachic authorities.
