Closed. Shach wrote: and nevertheless he cannot make a closed form that is fit according to all — meaning there should be a space in the middle, as written in siman 275 seif 2, for he must begin the seventh line "and it shall be if hearing," etc.; rather he should make the closed form fit according to Rambam, as the Rav wrote there. And see in responsum of Maharshal siman 37 — until here his language.
