Closed, etc. — see above siman 275 seif 2, which closed form; and nevertheless here in mezuzah he cannot make a closed form fit according to all, as Mechaber wrote there — meaning he finishes the preceding section at the beginning of a line and leaves space for nine letters and begins to write at the end of that same line, etc., for he must begin the seventh line "and it shall be if hearing," etc.; and as written nearby in the name of poskim — rather make the closed form fit according to Rambam, as the Rav wrote there, that if he cannot aim thus he should not deviate from Rambam's words. And see in responsum of Maharshal siman 37.
