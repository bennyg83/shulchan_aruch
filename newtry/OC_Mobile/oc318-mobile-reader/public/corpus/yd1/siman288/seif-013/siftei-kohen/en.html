Closed, etc. See above, siman 275, seif 2, regarding the forms of a closed paragraph. Here, however, a mezuzah cannot be written in the form that is valid according to all opinions, as the Mechaber describes there, namely, ending the preceding paragraph near the beginning of a line, leaving a blank space equal to nine letters, and beginning the next paragraph at the end of that same line. This is because Vehayah im shamoa must begin on the seventh line, as stated nearby in the name of the halachic authorities. One should therefore use the closed form that is valid according to the Rambam, as the Rama writes there. If one cannot arrange it otherwise, he should not depart from the Rambam's ruling. See responsa Maharshal, siman 37.
