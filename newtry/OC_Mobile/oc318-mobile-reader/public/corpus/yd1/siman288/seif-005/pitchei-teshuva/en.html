Needs ruling lines. See in responsum Rabbeinu Akiva Eiger siman 56 on a mezuzah found where the letters are written between ruling line and ruling line — if there is repair to rule now at the heads of the lines as required, and he answered it does not help; and proof from the sugya in Menachot — the reason we do not lower, etc.; and behold we rule that tefillin do not need ruling lines and mezuzah needs ruling lines; and if it were that ruling afterward helps, what is the difficulty — he could rule now? And so from Rambam's and Shulchan Aruch's language "and if he did not rule, it is invalid" — implies there is no repair for this (and it is explained there not because mezuzah requires in order, therefore ruling afterward does not help — this would be since there is no deficiency in the body of the letters and it is only repair afterward like scratching a touch that helps in mezuzah; rather it must be said since the halachah leMoshe miSinai requires ruling lines, if he wrote without ruling lines it is not called writing — and it is difficult regarding a Torah scroll). And he wrote further there is room to doubt: if he ruled in lead before writing, and after writing while the lead ruling still existed he ruled with a reed as required — one can say since there was never a time without ruling and the writing was as required, it helps; and even after the lead rulings spread one can say it helps to rule with a reed since the writing was in fitness, and also now he rules with a reed; and what in the interim was not ruled — one can say it is not worse, for it is not rejection since it is in his power to rule, and it is like one who received fit and made unfit — end of chapter 3 of Zevachim 34 — and it is difficult for practical law — end quote; see there.
