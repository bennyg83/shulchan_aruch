Initially. The view of most halachic authorities appears to be that even after the fact a mezuzah written on parchment that was not processed for its own sake is invalid. The Bach rules accordingly. Nevertheless, if waiting for parchment processed for its own sake would cause the mitzvah to be entirely neglected, it appears that one should affix this mezuzah without a blessing. The Rambam and those who agree with him are sufficiently authoritative to rely upon in a pressing circumstance. This is unlike the Bach, who writes that it should not be affixed at all. Rather, one should affix it and endeavor to obtain another valid mezuzah.
