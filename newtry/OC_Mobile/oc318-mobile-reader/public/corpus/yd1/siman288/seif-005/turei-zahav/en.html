But in a place, etc. — so, because Rambam does not require processing for its sake at all regarding mezuzah, since it is not an obligation of the body like tefillin and Torah scroll; and other poskim do not hold thus, but it is like them, and automatically even post factum invalid without processing for its sake. And Shulchan Aruch relied on Rambam in time of duress; and it is simple he does not bless on that mezuzah. And in several places he challenged Rambam from that which we learned: there is no difference between Torah scroll and tefillin and mezuzah, etc.; and in tractate Menachot 32 they challenge the writing of tefillin and mezuzah that both are on hide [invalid]; and what they answered there applies to answer here also — see there. And Maharach wrote: even though he has no fit mezuzah, he should affix thus without that invalid mezuzah made from parchment not processed for its sake, lest afterward he be lazy to seek another fit one; and he brings proof from Yerushalmi that Beit Yosef brings in Orach Chayim siman 143: we do not read from chumashim in public since souls are depressed they buy Torah — meaning as long as our soul is depressed that we are forbidden to read from a chumash, they buy Torah; and the same here.
