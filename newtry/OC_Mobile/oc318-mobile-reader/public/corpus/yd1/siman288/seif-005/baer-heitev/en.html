Initially. And the view of most authorities appears to be that even after the fact it is invalid on hide that is not tanned for its sake. So write Taz and Shach.
And affix. The Shach writes that he should affix it without a berachah, for the Rambam and those who assist him are worthy to be relied upon in a pressing situation. This is unlike what the Bach wrote, that he should not affix it at all; rather, he should affix it there and make an effort to obtain another valid one. Thus his language.
