Initially. The view of most poskim appears to be that even after the fact a mezuzah written on parchment not processed lishmah is invalid. Taz and Shach write this.
And he should fulfill the mitzvah with it. Shach writes that he should affix it without a beracha, because Rambam and those who agree with him are sufficient authorities to rely upon in a pressing situation. This is unlike Bach, who writes that one should not affix it at all. Rather, he should affix it there and make efforts to obtain another valid mezuzah.
