Even though he sewed them. See in responsum Rabbeinu Akiva Eiger siman 213: if he sewed them before writing, it is implied from Rashi's words Megillah 8 end 2b, s.v. Litforan in sinews that it is fit; and so one can infer from Rambam's language "he wrote them on two hides and sewed" — see there. And behold what is written to settle Rashi's words here must be said his intention is the case where he sewed one below the other in a manner that they are one page; for otherwise it is difficult — behold Rashi himself holds in Menachot 33b regarding "fit for two columns" he says even on one hide it is invalid on two pages; and so Tur in his name — only we do not rule thus. And if so, what helps that he sewed before and it is a connection and is like one hide, since it is on two pages? And all the more so the case is where he sewed in a manner that they are on one page, and it is not "fit for two columns" — speaks where he did not leave a blank space in the middle, only the lines adjacent to each other — and it is difficult. And see further there siman 11.
