(Siman 288, Taz seif katan 1) This is difficult to me, and so forth. There is no difficulty. With respect to writing, the passages must be written in the order in which they appear in the Torah. Taz's statement that this is also proven from Rambam proves nothing. Rambam did not write in chapter 1 what Taz quotes. Rather, he wrote: "In a mezuzah and tefillin one may not suspend even a single omitted letter; if even one letter was forgotten, the item is placed in genizah," and so forth. Therefore Rambam still needed to write in chapter 5 the law of one who wrote a later passage before an earlier one, because chapter 1 dealt with the prohibition against suspending an omitted letter. In any event, even if the later rule were a kal vachomer, Rambam may still have written it explicitly, as we find frequently among the poskim. Taz's statement that he proved the matter in siman 290 will be addressed there. It proves nothing.
