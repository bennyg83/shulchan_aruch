It cannot be repaired. Missing text may not be suspended above the line in a mezuzah as it may be in a Torah scroll, because the mezuzah must be written in sequence. This is the reason given by the halachic authorities and later authorities. According to this, one may scrape away the writing from below upward until reaching the omission, write the missing text there, and then rewrite on the scraped area everything that was removed. In this manner the text is written in sequence. See Orach Chaim, siman 32, seifim 23 and 24.
