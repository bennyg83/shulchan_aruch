And all the more so if he preceded a section, etc. It is a wonder to me — the opposite seems reasonable: there is more invalidity in writing out of order within that section itself than in preceding one section to another; for we find in Orach Chayim siman 64: he read retroactively — he did not fulfill; in another matter in order of verses; but he preceded one section to its fellow — even though he is not permitted, he fulfilled, since it is not adjacent in the Torah. And here also not adjacent. And so proven from Rambam — for in chapter 1 he wrote if he preceded one letter out of order he did not fulfill; and in chapter 5 he needed to write preceding a section; and in siman 288 I proved that according to Tosafos it is fit in truth when preceding a section — see there.
