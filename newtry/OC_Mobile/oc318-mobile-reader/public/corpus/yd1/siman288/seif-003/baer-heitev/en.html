Remedy. Because one does not suspend in it as one suspends in a sefer Torah — for we require that it be written in order; and per this, one may scrape from bottom to top up to the place of the omission and write the missing part there, and afterward write what he scraped in the place of the scraping — for in such a case it is in order; and see Orach Chayim siman 32 seifim 23 and 24 — end quote, Shach. And Taz wrote: one should wonder at the Mechaber who wrote "and needless to say regarding advancing a parashah" — for the opposite is logical: there is more reason to disqualify one who writes out of order within the parashah itself than one who advances one parashah before another; and likewise we find in Orach Chayim siman 64: if read in reverse order, this applies to the order of the verses, but if one advanced a parashah, etc. — see there.
