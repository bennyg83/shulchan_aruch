Impure that gave birth, etc., and pure that gave birth, etc. — the reason is explained in the mishnah: everything from the impure is impure and from the pure is pure; and this rule applies only to what is forbidden by negative commandment, not to what is forbidden only by positive commandment — therefore human milk is permitted though his flesh is forbidden — as below siman 81 seif 7; so Rav HaMagid and Korban Aharon there:
And pure that gave birth to impure form is permitted. It appears specifically like impure domestic animal; but if it gave birth like pure bird — forbidden — as Taz wrote above siman 13 seif 5 that one who slaughters an animal and finds likeness of pure bird — forbidden to eat; one should not distinguish found inside versus born — for Gemara chapter HaMakish (daf 61b) implies born is worse — there even for R' Shimon who forbids only when it entered the world, in its mother's belly permitted — see there; and so Tosafot there (daf 69a) s.v. Alma — and in Asheri there explicitly likeness of bird forbidden also when born — see there; and the reason: because it is not fulfilled:
And afterward found impure species. Even if no impure one in its herd we are concerned perhaps it came from another herd — Torah doubt to be stringent — so Rav HaMagid laws of forbidden foods and Maharshal and Acharonim agreed:
Even though wrapped after it, etc. Taz wrote: and it appears to me the same for impure left pregnant and we did not see it give birth and we see pure form wrapped after it — we are also stringent lest this one was born from it and forbidden — end of his words; and it is simple and proven explicitly in Gemara chapter 3 of Bechorot (daf 24a) — see there; and see siman 16 s.k. 5 and siman 316 seif 2:
