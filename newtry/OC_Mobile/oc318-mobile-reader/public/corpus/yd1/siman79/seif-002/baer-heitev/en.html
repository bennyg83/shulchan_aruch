Forbidden. Reason in mishnah: from impure impure, from pure pure; rule only for negative commandment prohibition — therefore human milk permitted though flesh forbidden — siman 81 seif 7; specifically gave birth like impure domestic animal; like pure bird forbidden — siman 13.
Wrapped. Taz: and I say same impure left pregnant unseen — see pure wrapped after — stringent; Levush same; if saw pure wrapped after impure — forbidden lest born from impure.
