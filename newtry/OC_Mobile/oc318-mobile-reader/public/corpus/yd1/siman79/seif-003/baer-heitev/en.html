Treifah. Shach: deals pregnant before treifah — otherwise treifah does not bear; or definite treifah — siman 57 seif 8; though generally fetus is thigh of mother — CM 409 cow damages collects offspring for treifot — different — depends on vitality — treifah not alive, fetus alive — Gemara Acharai Mot; Taz Maharar Piovish: if mother trampled while pregnant whether harms offspring — requires further study.
