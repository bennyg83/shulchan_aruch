One born of treifah — does not exist that treifah gives birth; Shach wrote deals definite treifah — we do not say definite treifah if it gave birth nevertheless treifah — but in reality does not exist — only pregnant then treifah — see siman 56 Kereti and Peleti at length.
Permitted — for treifot we do not say fetus is thigh of mother — treifot depend on weakness of body — animal body was healthy — offspring healthy; however Taz wrote Maharar Piovish: if mother trampled while pregnant whether trampling harms offspring; if alive b'nei chorin permitted like other doubt of trampling; Peri Chadash: if trampled opposite offspring concerned poison reached offspring; if trampled above toward neck poison does not reach offspring — no doubt permitted; see Peleti question from chapter HaTaruvot — see there.
