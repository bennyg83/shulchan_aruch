(1) [Seif 1] Signs of domestic animal and wild animal were explained in Torah, etc. — meaning signs between pure and impure in Torah as explained; signs between wild animal and domestic animal not in Torah — Sages gave signs as below siman 80 — Shach s.k. 1. Shaarei De'ah 1.
(2) There. And every domestic animal without teeth in upper jaw, etc. — specifically no teeth in upper jaw; but bumps it has above and below — as known.
(3) There. And its hooves split — separated above and below with two nails; some hooves split above but not fully separated below connected — Rashi in Chumash Shemini. Kenesset HaGedolah Hagahat Tur 4, Leket HaPardes 1, Beit Yosef 1, Shulchan Gavoah 1, Shaarei De'ah 1, Beit Yitzchak 1, Arukh HaShulchan 3, Zivchei Tzedek 1.
