And if it has horns — removed from pig doubt — proves horns alone are not a sign — it is pure since split hooves and doubt only on pig which has horns as proof — Rivam Chullin 59 s.v. elu explains Gemara: these are signs of wild animal to permit its milk — must return to check hooves; if has hooves must return to horns; R' Dosa holds horns alone sign for wild animal — impure have no horns; Niddah "everything with horns has hooves" — R' Dosa; Ran agrees; stam mishnah R' Dosa — halachah like R' Dosa since stam mishnah — Peri Chadash: halachah like stam mishnah; from this arose question Maharam of Rekesh on Ran Rosh Hashanah shofar of impure animal invalid for mitzvah — how exist — all with horns have hooves; they did not err plainly; Shulchan Aruch here requires both horns and hooves — only there Ran holds halachah R' Dosa — question Ran contradicts himself. But if halachah R' Dosa nevertheless Gemara chapter 1 teaches all with horns is sign for wild animal — grooved split horns; R' Dosa on "everything with horns has hooves" — grooved horns; two meanings horns — do not distance dispute — plainly even R' Dosa impure wild animals have no horns but impure domestic may have grooved horns — wild animal certainly; if has grooved horns and hooves certainly wild animal; Niddah mishnah R' Dosa — grooved horns; Rambam explains mishnah: horns to distinguish wild from domestic for milk — Maharsha; if so no question — halachah stam mishnah grooved only; but other domestic impure may have horns; Ran forbids shofar for mitzvah — clear; Tosafot Yom Tov Niddah. For practical halachah most poskim including Rambam like first Tanna and Rif did not cite R' Dosa or Niddah mishnah — halachah first Tanna; though halachah stam mishnah — Chullin stam domestic and wild animal said from Torah — did not also say horns sign — like Beitzah fish asks spine visible — therefore first Tanna; horns not sign at all only for milk not whether pure — mishnah deals with that. But from Shabbat question — tachash in Moses' days had one horn on forehead like primordial ox — Tosafot Chullin 59b s.v. vekares — Shabbat follows R' Dosa — horns sign for pure domestic animal — especially one horn — all the more impure may have two horns; hard say entire Gemara not halachah — Peri Chadash also — do not say horns grooved — still question from tachash; say in sharpness: certainly halachah only two horns per R' Dosa — not one horn — otherwise what proof from tachash; must say rule only for tachash known pure with one horn for milk; not in count of pure species — Gemara wanted prove all agree pure — for first Tanna simple; for R' Dosa say only temporary and does not break Moses' rule all with horns have hooves — tachash impure had horns — rule false — say temporary — does not break rule transmitted; if only one horn does not break rule as above; tachash pure not counted — Gemara proves all agree pure — why not decide beast or wild animal — if impure species could have one horn; if pure no known domestic with one horn — certainly wild animal; also primordial ox and unicorn have one horn — did not decide; all answered in sharpness. Plainly most poskim agree majority with horns are pure; minority also impure have horns — do not rely on sign alone — like lung checks — do not rely majority kosher — if doubt before us do not rely majority since happened — all the more cut hooves — forbid; but majority with horns are kosher — when live animal testified horns — presume majority separate — has signs; if check certainly has signs; if debate permit or forbid distant testimony horns — presumption majority separate has signs.
