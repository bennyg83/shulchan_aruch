(Siman 79 seif 1 in hagahah) Human flesh forbidden from Torah. See Bayit Chadash what he wrote incident: infant fell in honey vat and died — Tov Ayin forbade honey because human flesh forbidden from Torah; in my opinion no prohibition for poskim who permit after not separated and does not enter honey only taste; but forbid other reason — died there — flesh of dead forbidden benefit from Torah — we derive from ever min hachai; Mishnah chapter 2 Avodah Zarah ever min hachai forbids in any amount — so dead forbids any amount — his words not compelled — ever min hachai forbids in amount because counted item; here only if actually mixed not mere taste.
