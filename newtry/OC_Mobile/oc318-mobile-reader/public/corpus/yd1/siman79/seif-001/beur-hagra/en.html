And some say, etc. Avodat HaTaharah; and they brought proof from what is written chapter 6 of Shabbat they remove its nivin and chapter 2 of Bava Kamma he removed its nivin, etc.
If it has horns, etc. Rashi in Niddah 61b s.v. veyesh — there is none like it without cloven hooves except pig as written in Torah; and what mishnah says "and there are" refers to impure — nevertheless not distinguished by horns alone since mishnah there is R' Dosa as Tosafot in name of Rivam Chullin s.v. elu hen and Avodat Yom Tov siman 80; and so Ran and all commentators like Rivam, not like Rabbenu Tam there.
Human flesh, etc. — for it is written "this you shall eat" and no other; and it counted only animal species — "and it was." Rambam relied on Torat Kohanim Shemini chapter 4 — and human flesh, etc., implies negative commandment only; and though he also said milk because of flesh — and proof: first he said hoofed walkers impure are impure implying pure completely — here changed and said not negative commandment; thus resolved Gemara Acharai Mot 60a counts blood and milk not flesh; Torat Kohanim answered flesh and milk from two teachings; Gemara only one teaching; and from Torat Kohanim and end of Keritot we derive blood from bird and animal but not that blood and milk derive even from issur and from bird and animal we only hear not warned on blood negative commandment — but from issur of what comes from impure we do not derive; therefore Acharai Mot learns both from one verse; Torat Kohanim learns on flesh that is not negative commandment — Ran.
(Lekut) Human flesh, etc. — from what is written beginning chapter Dam Shechitah to exclude blood of walkers, etc.; also no question since entirely permitted; but Rashba wrote siman 79 permitted from Torah — only mitzvah of separation; proof from Dam Shechitah — rejected because gid forbidden in him prohibition of gid depends on palm of hand — and question Chullin 92b on animal but not bird, etc.; but everything in him and his species certainly forbidden — main case was human — see there at length (end).
