An eight-month child etc. The Tur — and his honor remains in its place — and it is a great error, for regarding an eight-month child there is no distinction anywhere between alive and dead [for this reason it is not listed there in the seifa in the Mishnah 47b, and there it lists things simpler than this: a shefir that is not formed, filled with water etc., even though it is proven from the reisha that it said specifically a formed shefir, and similarly the other matters there]. And that is what the Mishnah said: even though it emerged etc. — and certainly it is not a difficulty as a novelty regarding inheritance, just as it did not say in the seifa "and a nine-month child even though etc." as a novelty regarding a kohen (because inheritance is specifically in such a case; similarly in the reisha, if it were true that regarding a kohen it is specifically [so], it would not have used "even though" as a novelty regarding inheritance; it must be that regarding a kohen there is no practical difference). And there on [daf] 2 Shmuel said: the head does not etc., and they raised a difficulty against him and they did not establish Shmuel as referring to a dead nefel, for nefalim more imply the dead; and certainly specifically his head does not exempt, for regarding the whole of him there is no need, for we say there 47b specifically one who miscarries on day forty, and in Chullin 68a: and the seifa, what does it teach us etc., and if you say it teaches us etc. that it is considered a face etc. — we already learned: the placenta etc.; hence even with a placenta the head exempts. And see Tosafot there s.v. "placenta" etc., and in Bechorot there [daf] 2 s.v. "it is a refutation" etc. And if you say: since he was refuted etc., behold even the dead etc. — and one can say etc. And there are several more proofs, and there is no need to elaborate because of the great obviousness.
