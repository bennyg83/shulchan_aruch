(Seif 23, Baer Heitev seif katan 12) In the name of Responsa Chacham Tzvi: if forty days passed and she miscarried a sac, the next child is exempt from redemption. Baer Heitev omits Chacham Tzvi's qualification: this is where the sac was not examined. If it was examined and found to contain only water or similar material, the law follows Maharik.
