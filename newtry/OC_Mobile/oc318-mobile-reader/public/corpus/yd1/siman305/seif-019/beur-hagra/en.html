A kohen, etc. Like Rabbi Chiya, for Reish Lakish holds like him.
Therefore, etc. The Rosh; see Baer HaGolah. As stated there 11a, an Israelite who had petter rechem firstborns, etc., and an Israelite who had untithed produce, etc.; the same applies here, for they are not exempt on their own account. Kohanim and Levi'im are exempt only because their sanctity removes the obligation, as stated in the first chapter there, 4a; here, however, it is only by virtue of the father's acquisition in them.
