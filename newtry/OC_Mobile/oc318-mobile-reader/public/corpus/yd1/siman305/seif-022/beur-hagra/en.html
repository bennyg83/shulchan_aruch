And from when his forehead emerges, etc. As stated there: Reish Lakish said forehead, etc., and Rabbi Yochanan, etc.
That half the face, etc. As stated in Niddah 23b. They have one law, as stated in the Mishnah here and there, and so the Rambam wrote here: anything for which the mother is tamei with birth exempts from bekhorah.
Dissolved limbs, etc. There on 29a we say this is specifically where neither its head nor the majority of it emerged. See Rashi on the Mishnah in Bekhorot there and Tosafot s.v. “and that which emerges,” etc. This requires study regarding the Rambam, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch, who wrote generally.
