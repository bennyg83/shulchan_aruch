And from when his forehead emerges etc. As written there: said Reish Lakish, the forehead etc., and Rabbi Yochanan etc.
That half a face etc. As written in Niddah 23b, and they have one law, as written in the Mishnah here and there; and so wrote the Rambam here: whatever makes his mother tamei with tumat leidah exempts from bechorah.
Melted limbs etc. We say there 29a: specifically when neither his head nor most of him emerged; and see Rashi in Bechorot on the Mishnah there, and Tosafot s.v. "and the one who emerges" etc.; and this requires further study regarding the Rambam and Tur Shulchan Aruch who stated it without specification.
