The burden of proof rests on the claimant. Tiferet LeMoshe writes that if the money was already given to the kohen, it is not removed from the kohen, similar to seif 27 regarding payment to two kohanim. Although distinctions can be made between the cases, he nevertheless rules this way. See there.
