When he grows up he is obligated. See Responsa Zikhron Yosef, Yoreh De'ah at the end of siman 26, who wrote that this means specifically when he becomes thirteen years old. Although he is obligated in all mitzvot rabbinically from the age of education, that applies only to mitzvot that he can also fulfill after he grows up. This mitzvah of pidyon is different, for if he fulfills it now he cannot fulfill it again when he grows. A similar line of reasoning was written by Mizrachi at the end of Parashat Lech Lecha regarding why Avraham Avinu did not circumcise himself before he was commanded; see there.
