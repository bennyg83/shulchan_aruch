They bore. The Taz wrote that this refers back to seif 29, to two women of two men, neither of whom had previously borne. It is difficult: why write here of two males and two females, where there is no definite bekhor because each can say a female emerged before him? Let the greater novelty be stated even with two males and one female, where one is certainly a bekhor, and nevertheless both are exempt because each can push the obligation to the other, saying he is the bekhor. In truth the Tur wrote that in this case too they are exempt. It appears that the Mechaber follows his own reasoning in seif 26, where he wrote that the sons are obligated when their father died within thirty days and each cannot push the obligation to the other. So too here. But this is difficult, for there is an explicit baraita here that even in such a case he is exempt, etc.; see there.
