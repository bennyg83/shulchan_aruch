They gave birth. Taz writes that this refers back to seif 29, concerning two women belonging to two different men, neither of whom had previously given birth. It is difficult why the Mechaber here discusses two males and two females, where there is no definite firstborn because each father can say that the female was born first. He could have stated a greater novelty: even if there were two males and one female, where one of the males is certainly a firstborn, both fathers are still exempt because each can push the claim onto the other and say that the other child is the firstborn. Tur in fact rules that they are exempt in that case too. It appears that the Mechaber follows his own view in seif 26, where he says the sons are obligated when their father dies within thirty days and each cannot deflect the obligation onto the other. The same would apply here. But this remains difficult because there is an explicit baraita stating that even in such a case they are exempt. See there.
