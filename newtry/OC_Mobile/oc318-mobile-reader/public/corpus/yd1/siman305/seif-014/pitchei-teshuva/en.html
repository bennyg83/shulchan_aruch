A gift on condition of return is called a gift. See Responsa Teshuvah MeAhavah I, simanim 55, 56, and 57. Specifically where the condition is to return that very object it is called a gift, for at least during the time it is given to him it is his and he is exempt from unavoidable accidents. But on condition to return other money it is not called a gift, only a complete loan. Therefore, if he gives him five selaim on condition that he return other five selaim, his son is not redeemed; see there at length.
