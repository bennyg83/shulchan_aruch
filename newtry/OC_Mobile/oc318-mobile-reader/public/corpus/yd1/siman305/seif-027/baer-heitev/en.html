And a female. The Shach wrote that this means one woman gave birth to a male and one to a female, and they became mixed so that it is not known which woman bore the male. Nevertheless, five selaim are owed to the kohen, for this is the first birth for both women. Likewise, where they gave birth to two males and one female and they became mixed, only five selaim are owed, for we can say this woman bore the two males and this one bore the female, so there is only one bekhor. Although it is also possible that this one bore a male and the other bore first a male and then a female, making two bekhorim, nevertheless one who seeks to extract bears the burden of proof. Therefore, if one of the males died within thirty days, he gives the kohen nothing, for the father can say: the bekhor died. End quote.
