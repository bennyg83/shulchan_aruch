They bore a male and a female. This means they gave birth in hiding and the children became mixed; likewise with two males and one female. Therefore he gives five selaim to the kohen, for in any event one is a bekhor: if the first woman bore two males, the first male is the bekhor; and if the first woman bore a male and a female, then the other woman bore a male alone and he is the bekhor, while the one born with the female is exempt, since perhaps the female emerged first.
