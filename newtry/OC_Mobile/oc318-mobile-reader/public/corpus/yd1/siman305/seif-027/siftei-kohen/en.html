They bore a male and a female. This means one woman bore a male and one bore a female, and they became mixed so that it is not known which bore the male. Nevertheless, five selaim are owed to the kohen, for this is the first birth for both. Likewise, where they bore two males and one female and they became mixed, only five selaim are owed, for we can say this one bore two males and that one bore the female, and there is only one bekhor. Although it is also possible that this one bore a male and that one also bore first a male and afterward a female, making two bekhorim, nevertheless one who seeks to extract bears the burden of proof. Therefore, if one of the males died within thirty days, he gives the kohen nothing, for the father can say: the bekhor died.
