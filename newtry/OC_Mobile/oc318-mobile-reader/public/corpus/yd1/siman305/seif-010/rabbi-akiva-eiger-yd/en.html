(Seif 10 in the gloss) And the father cannot redeem through an agent. In Sha'ar HaMelech (chapter 6 of Hilchot Ishut) he wondered from the law of seif 8 regarding a gift on condition to return, his son is redeemed, and it is a fully laid-out sugya in the first chapter of Kiddushin; and if it were that he cannot redeem through an agent, we would say that even if he did not return the money it is effective, since the act cannot be fulfilled through an agent, his condition is void and the act stands; see there that he left it as requiring further study. And in my humble opinion there is no difficulty, for certainly what this approach holds that he cannot redeem through an agent means that the agent should perform the act of pidyon, that he say to the kohen "with this money the son shall be redeemed"; but it is obvious that he can transfer the money to the kohen through an agent, and he himself says "with this money that I transfer to you through the agent, my son shall be redeemed." And if so, with a gift on condition to return the condition is not upon the pidyon, that if he does not return he will not be redeemed, but rather the condition is upon the transfer of the money, that he acquire it as a gift on condition to return, and if he does not return he will not acquire the money; and in this the condition stands, for regarding transfer it is possible through an agent; and the father will say to the kohen that with these coins that I transferred to you as a gift on condition to return, the son shall be redeemed:

(There) And to make the seudah at night. The reason for the custom that they do not do the pidyon and the seudah together on the night of the 31st, one can say, is because sometimes 29 days 12 hours and 793 chalakim will not yet have been completed if he was born at the end of the day; therefore they delay until the 32nd day, and if the 32nd is a fast day one should redeem and the seudah on the night preceding the fast.
