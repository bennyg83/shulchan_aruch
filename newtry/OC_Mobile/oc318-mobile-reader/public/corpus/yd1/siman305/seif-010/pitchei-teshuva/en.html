His son is redeemed. See Chokhmat Adam, klal 150 din 2, who wrote that although the language of the Gemara and poskim, “he has fulfilled,” implies that lechatchilah one should not do so, it appears that this is not precise, for it should be no worse than other gifts of kehunah, above siman 61 seif 9. He further wrote that according to the Perishah in the name of Rashal, one must give it to one kohen at one time; see there. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, end of siman 297, who wrote: it appears to me that lechatchilah one may not divide the five selaim between two kohanim, for anything for which the Torah gives a measure, if one divides it, it is a half-measure. Although after the fact one fulfills through combination, nevertheless the preferred mitzvah is to consume the measure at once or to give the required measure at once. End quote. I do not know how he will answer the law in siman 61 mentioned above, where it is clear that the concern is only an amount worthy of giving, not the full required measure.
