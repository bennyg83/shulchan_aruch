His son is redeemed. See in Chochmat Adam klal 150 din 2, who wrote that even though from the language of the Gemara and the poskim who wrote "he has fulfilled" it implies that l'chatchila he should not do so, it appears that this is not precise, for it should be no less than other matanot kehunah [above siman 61 seif 9]. And he wrote further that the view of the Perishah in the name of Maharshal is that to one kohen he must give all at once; see there. [And see in the teshuvah of Chatam Sofer at the end of siman 297, who wrote, and this is his language: and it appears to me that he cannot l'chatchila divide the five sela'im between two kohanim, for wherever the Torah set a shiur in it, if he divides it it is like a half-shiur; even though b'dieved he fulfills by combining, nevertheless the choicest mitzvah is to eat the shiur at once, or to give the shiur of his giving at once, end of his words. And I do not know what he would answer regarding that of the aforementioned siman 61, where it is explained that there is no insistence except regarding the manner of giving, and not regarding the shiur.]
