(There, seif 14) Within thirty days. If he died on day thirty, one must consider whether to compare this to a debt, where we rule in Choshen Mishpat 74 that a person commonly pays on the day his time expires, or whether here it is worse, since the obligation is not certain, for perhaps the infant will die that very day and be exempt. See Tosafot, first chapter of Bava Batra 5. Afterward I saw in Shitah Mekubetzet, Bava Metzia, chapter HaShoel, in the name of the Ramakh, that on day thirty he is presumed to have been redeemed; the practical law requires study.
