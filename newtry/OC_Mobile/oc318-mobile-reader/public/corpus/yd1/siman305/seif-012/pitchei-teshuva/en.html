"And not," who wrote that when the father wants to go on his way within 30 he should make an agent to redeem, but that the father should redeem him in the place where he will be on the thirtieth (and first) day, in my opinion he cannot bless, for perhaps the son died in his house; and to give the son a chazakah of life also there is reason to question, for as long as thirty days have not passed he has no chazakah of life, for perhaps he is a nefel; and there is room to discuss this and say that it would be effective by reason of a chazakah that comes by force of a majority, that the majority of a majority are not nefalim; and the matter requires further study; and therefore the choicest of ways is that he should make an agent here, end of his words] See in the responsa Beit Shmuel Acharon, Yoreh De'ah siman 29, regarding one who left his wife in one city and went to another country, and they wrote to him there that his wife gave birth to a bechor, whether he is obligated to redeem his son after 30 days from the day of his birth even though he does not know if he is alive or not; and he concluded that he can certainly redeem and bless, and there is no concern that perhaps the child died within 30 days, for we establish him on his chazakah, for a chazakah that comes by force of a majority is a chazakah; see there where he elaborated on this [also in the responsa Chatam Sofer siman 294 and the end of siman 295 he challenged the aforementioned Dagul Mervavah from the end of the first chapter of Arachin, and concluded that it is better that he redeem him himself in the place where he is than that he redeem him through an agent, and so he saw an incident from his teacher the Gaon Moreinu HaRav R' Natan Adler, may the memory of the righteous be for a blessing; see there]:
