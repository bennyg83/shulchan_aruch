Treifah. The Shach wrote that this appears to mean a definite treifah. In such a case, even if he lived a long time afterward he is exempt from pidyon haben, for we do not say “a treifah cannot live” against a definite treifah. In a doubtful treifah he is exempt from pidyon because the burden of proof rests on the claimant; however, if he then lived twelve months he is obligated, for he has left the doubt of treifah by living twelve months. See above, siman 55.
