A tereifah. Shach writes that this means a definite tereifah. In such a case, even if the child later lives for a long time, he is exempt from pidyon haben, because the rule that a tereifah does not live applies to a definite tereifah. In a case of doubtful tereifah, he is exempt because one who seeks to extract money bears the burden of proof. However, if the child subsequently lives twelve months, he becomes obligated, because living twelve months removes the doubt of tereifah. See above, siman 55.
