Obligated. When he grows up, for the kohenet is not obligated to redeem her son, just like any other woman, as written in seif 2. The Israelite husband of the kohenet is obviously not obligated, for he is not the father, since she conceived from the non-Jew. According to this, if she did not conceive from the non-Jew, but was only disqualified by intercourse with him and afterward conceived from a Jew, the son is obligated in pidyon. End quote from Shach.
Exempt. Even when he grows up, for perhaps he is lying and she is telling the truth. According to this, with a full wife, since he is believed to say the child is not his, even if she contradicts him, the child is automatically a mamzer and she was disqualified by intercourse with the adulterer, so the son is obligated in pidyon when he grows up. The practical law still requires study. End quote from Shach.
