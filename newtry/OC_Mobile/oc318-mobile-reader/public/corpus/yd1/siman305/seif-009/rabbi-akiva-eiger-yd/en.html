(There) And to make the meal at night. The reason for the custom not to make the pidyon and the meal together on the night of the thirty-first may be that sometimes twenty-nine days, twelve hours, and 793 parts have not yet elapsed, where the child was born at the end of the day. Therefore, if it was delayed until day thirty-two, and day thirty-two is a fast day, one should perform the pidyon and make the meal on the night before the fast. Responsa Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Yoreh De'ah siman 186.
