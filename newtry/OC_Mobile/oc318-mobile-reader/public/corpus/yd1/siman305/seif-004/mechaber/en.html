If he wrote to the kohen that he owes him five selaim on account of the pidyon of his son, he is obligated to give them to him, and his son is not redeemed (Tur in the name of Halakhot Gedolot). If he told the kohen that he will give him to redeem his son, it is forbidden to retract; however, if he retracted it is considered retraction (Mordechai end of chapter Kol HaGet) (and see above siman 264):
