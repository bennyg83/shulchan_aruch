He said to the kohen. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, end of siman 297, who writes there that with gifts of kehunah, once one says, even not in the kohen's presence, that he wishes to give to a particular kohen, he cannot retract, for it is a small gift; and there is also the defect of breach of trust, as explained in Choshen Mishpat 204 and 249. Since he has in it only the benefit of choosing the recipient, he can no longer retract; see there.
