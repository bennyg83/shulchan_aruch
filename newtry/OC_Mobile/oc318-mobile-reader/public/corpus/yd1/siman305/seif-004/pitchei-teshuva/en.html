Giving to the kohen. See above siman 61 s.k. 1 regarding compelled giving. Chatam Sofer, siman 252, discusses a redemption day on which no adult kohen is available, only a minor kohen or an agent of an adult kohen. From Peri Chadash, siman 61, it is clear that one may fulfill gifts of kehunah through the kohen's agent when this is with the kohen's knowledge and will; without his knowledge, one may not, because the kohen has a mitzvah in receiving. With the kohen's will the agent acquires for him and the owners fulfill their obligation. A minor kohen can also receive gifts, as follows from Lulav HaGazul, for even if a minor lacks Torah-level acquisition, gifts of kehunah do not require giving specifically into his hand. But since the geonim instituted the dialogue in pidyon haben, “Which do you prefer,” it implies that the kohen himself has a role in the pidyon. Therefore he cannot appoint a Yisrael as agent for that role, and a minor lacks the power to release the bekhor; it is better to bring a kohen from elsewhere.
