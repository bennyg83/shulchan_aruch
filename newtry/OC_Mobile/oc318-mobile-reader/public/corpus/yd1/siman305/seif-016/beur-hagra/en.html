(Likut) If there is no etc. So wrote the Tur, and Beit Yosef wrote: it is known that the halacha is like the Rabanan. And apparently his words are not correct: even though the Rabanan dispute R' Yehudah, nevertheless Rav and Shmuel dispute this with Rabbi Yochanan and Reish Lakish in Kiddushin 13a, and Rav Papa ruled there that shiabuda is d'oraisa. Rather, the Tur here followed the view of Tosafot there s.v. "said Rav Papa" etc. and "and Rabbeinu Tam said" etc. But the Rif and the Rosh at the end of Bava Batra explained it regarding a loan, and answered the difficulty there: that what was said in Bava Batra there "so that one not lock the door" — for that reason they established it as d'oraisa; and according to this, regarding a loan written in the Torah one can say that it remains according to the law of the Torah, for it is an uncommon matter, and we are not concerned about the loss of purchasers. But after examination one can say that even according to the Rif's view, Rav Papa said even regarding a loan written in the Torah, for he stated it without specification. And so it appears in Bechorot 49, where it says "and all agree they hold like Rav Papa" etc. And there is also support for the Rif's explanation: therefore one must say there "and all agree they do not hold like Rav Asi" etc. and "and all agree" etc. — for according to Tosafot's explanation it would have been necessary to say only "like Rav Papa of Kiddushin"; rather, Rav Papa stated it regarding a loan without specification, and if a loan written in the Torah is not like one written in a shtar, it is also included in Rav Papa's [statement]. And so wrote Maharshal, and unlike the Shach (in Choshen Mishpat siman 39 se'if katan 2). And from that of Choshen Mishpat siman 119 seif 4 there is no proof, for damages are different, for they have publicity, as written in the third chapter of Bava Kamma (33b). And so wrote the Rashba (in Bava Kamma 8a and there 14b, and so wrote Tosafot there at the end of s.v. "we learn," and specifically according to the one who holds shiabuda d'oraisa. And one can say that what Tosafot wrote there 8a is according to the one who holds shiabuda is not d'oraisa). Nevertheless, chapter 19 of Hilchot Malveh is unlike Tosafot there in Kiddushin and in Bechorot there, who included even damages; and therefore they were forced to stretch [the explanation] in Bava Kamma 8a s.v. "all of them" etc. And in this the aforementioned words of Beit Yosef are correct: specifically because the halacha is like the Tanna Kamma, he does not collect, as written in Bechorot 49 (until here).
