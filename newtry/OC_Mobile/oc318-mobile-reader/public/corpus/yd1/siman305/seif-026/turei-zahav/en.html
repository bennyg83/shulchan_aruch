Even if they already divided the assets. We do not say that this one can push him off and that one can push him off. This follows Rabbi Yehudah according to the first formulation in the Gemara. The Tur disagrees with this and holds like the latter formulation, that even according to Rabbi Yehudah we say that each one can push the kohen off onto the other.
