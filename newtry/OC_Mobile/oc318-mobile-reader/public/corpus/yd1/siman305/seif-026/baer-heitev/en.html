They divided. Tur holds that if the father died within thirty days, the sons are exempt even if they had not yet divided the estate. If he died after thirty days, then even if they later divided, they must together give five sela'im. Rambam himself gives the same view in his Commentary on the Mishnah. It is surprising that Rama omitted this opinion, especially because it appears to be the primary reading of the Gemara. Bach likewise rules that if the father died within thirty days they are exempt even if they had not divided, because one who seeks to extract money bears the burden of proof. Shach.
