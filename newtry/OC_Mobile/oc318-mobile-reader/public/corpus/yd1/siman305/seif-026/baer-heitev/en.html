They divided. The Tur's view is that if he died within thirty days, even if they had not divided the estate they are exempt; and if he died after thirty days, even after they divided the estate they are obligated to give five selaim between them. This is also the Rambam's own view in his commentary to the Mishnah. It is astonishing that the Rav omitted this view, especially since it appears primary in the Gemara. So ruled the Bach, that within thirty days they are exempt even if they did not divide, because one who seeks to extract bears the burden of proof. End quote from Shach.
