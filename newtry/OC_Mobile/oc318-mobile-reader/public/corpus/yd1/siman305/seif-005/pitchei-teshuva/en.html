He gave him a vessel. Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of Rabbi Bnei Yaakov and Rabbi Yaakov Alfandari that if he gave the kohen collateral for the five selaim, meaning only as security, and after some time he gives him five selaim to take back the collateral, his son is not redeemed, for collateral is not payment but mere security, and for pidyon a strong act of giving is required. However, where he gives him a silver vessel so that he should take the vessel itself, or he appraised the vessel and told him to collect from it, in such a case his son is redeemed. He brought the words of Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 2 siman 107, also cited in Baer Heitev of Rabbi Maharit, s.k. 5, who wrote that if it happened that one redeemed on Shabbat through collateral, his son is redeemed, etc., from which it appears that even ordinary collateral redeems. He wrote that one must say it refers to this latter kind of case; see there.
