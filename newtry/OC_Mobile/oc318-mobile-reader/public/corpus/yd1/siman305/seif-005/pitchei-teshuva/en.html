He gave him a vessel. He wrote in Rabbi Akiva Eiger in the name of the Rav Bnei Yaakov and Mahar"i Alfandari that if he gave the kohen a mashkon for five sela'im, that is, that it is for assurance, and after some time he gives him five sela'im to take the mashkon, his son is not redeemed, for a mashkon is not payment but mere assurance, and regarding pidyon we require a strong giving. However, where he gives him a silver vessel that he should take the vessel itself, or he made an appraisal of the vessel and told him to collect from it, in such a case his son is redeemed. And he brought the words of the teshuvah of Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 107 (also brought in Be'er Heitev of the Rav Maharit, of blessed memory, se'if katan 5), who wrote that if it happened that he redeemed on Shabbat by means of a mashkon, his son is redeemed etc., from which it is proven that with a mere mashkon his son is redeemed; and he wrote that one must say that it deals with such a case; see there.
