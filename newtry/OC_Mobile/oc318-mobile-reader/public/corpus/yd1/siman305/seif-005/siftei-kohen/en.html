This is the Rambam's language. The Mechaber's addition, “provided it is worth,” is based on the Beit Yosef, but the Beit Yosef's explanation is forced, as is the Bach's explanation and Rabbenu Yerucham's explanation. The correct understanding is that the Mechaber follows the version of Halakhot Gedolot: if the kohen accepted the item, the son is redeemed even if it is not worth five selaim in the market. Rav Ashi's statement means only that a great man like Rav Kahana knows to waive in such a case. The plain baraita supports that whenever the kohen accepted it, the son is redeemed.
