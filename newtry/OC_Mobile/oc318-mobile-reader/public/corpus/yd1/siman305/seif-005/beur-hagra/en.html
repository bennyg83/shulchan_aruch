If he gave him, etc. until "redeemed" — the language of the Rambam. And the Ran wondered at him why he omitted what is written there, "Rav Ashi said, etc." And Beit Yosef answered that it is not specifically such as Rav Kahana, but that it should be worth it to some person. And his words are astonishing; and see the Taz. And furthermore, why did the Rambam leave it unexplained? And the main point is that his version is like the version of the Bahag, and as the Shach wrote; see there. (Likut) If he gave, etc. The Rambam's version: Rava said, we said this only regarding Rav Kahana, who is a great man and knows to forgive, but everyone else, no; like that of Mar son of Rav Ashi, etc. And he explains it regarding ona'ah, that there is no ona'ah in it. But the approach of Rashi and Tosafot and the Rosh and all the poskim is specifically that it is worth it to him. And see Rashba and Ran (etc.). (Likut) If he gave, etc. until "redeemed" — the language of the Rambam, and so the Tur wrote, and it is according to the version of the Bahag. But according to our version, specifically that it is worth it to the kohen who typically overvalues this object up to these amounts, and specifically for that kohen who typically overvalues this object at those amounts. And see the Ran in Kiddushin there. And the version of the Bahag requires further study (etc.). (Likut) The Rashba wrote there that the Rambam holds that what is written there, "it was said only, etc.," is specifically regarding a tallit which it is not typical to overvalue except a talmid chacham, but other things all people are equal. And he wrote: and this requires further study (etc.). (Likut) If he gave, etc. And it is, etc. Kesef Mishneh there 12a, for we say there: he betrothed her with a date, etc., we are concerned, etc. And the Rambam explains that it is a d'oraisa doubt, as is written there. And the reason is: since she accepted it, it is evident that it is worth a perutah to her, and it is also worth it to some person who has a way there. And see the Ran there at length (etc.).
