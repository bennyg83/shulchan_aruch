(Likkut) According to the Rambam's version, Rav Ashi says that only a great man like Rav Kahana knows to waive, while others do not; the matter is interpreted in relation to ona'ah.
According to Rashi, Tosafot, and the Rosh, it must actually be worth that amount to this kohen.
(Another likkut) Our version requires that it be worth that amount to a kohen who is accustomed to value this sort of item. The version of Halakhot Gedolot requires study. The Rashba explains that the Rambam held the limitation applies only to a tallit, which only a talmid chacham values, but other items are valued by everyone; this too requires study. The final law resembles Kiddushin 12 regarding dates: the Rambam treats it as a Torah doubt because her acceptance shows that it is worth a perutah to her and to someone elsewhere where dates circulate; see Ran.
