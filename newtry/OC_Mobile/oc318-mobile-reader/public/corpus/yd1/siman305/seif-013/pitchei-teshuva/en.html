His son is not redeemed. See in the responsa Yad Eliyahu, end of siman 78, who wrote that it is possible that the kohen need not return the money, for he gave it to him of his own will, and it is not similar to if the child died within 30, etc.; see there; and the same applies in the first clause when he said "from now." And see in the responsa Keter Kehunah siman 52 who wrote that if he gave the redemption money after 29 days 12 hours 793 chalakim and said that the pidyon should take effect after 30 days, his son is redeemed, for according to the opinion of the Shach the pidyon is effective after 29 days 12 hours 793 chalakim, and even though the Magen Avraham disagrees with him in siman 339 se'if katan 8 and the main ruling is like the Magen Avraham, nevertheless here where he said that it should take effect after 30 there is room to join the opinion of the Mechaber that his son is redeemed even if the money is not extant; see there; and see in the responsa Dat Esh siman 11:
