One who redeemed his son within thirty days: if he said to him "from now" his son is not redeemed; and if he said "after thirty days" his son is redeemed even though the money does not exist after 30 days: {Rama: And some say that if the money does not exist or he returned them to the father within 30, even b'dieved his son is not redeemed (Maharai in Terumat HaDeshen siman 264 and in his writings siman 234); and it is good to be stringent and redeem again}. One who is in doubt whether he is obligated in pidyon is exempt, for the burden of proof rests on the one who extracts from his fellow:
