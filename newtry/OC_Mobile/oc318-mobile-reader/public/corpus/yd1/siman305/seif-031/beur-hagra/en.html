Or two males etc. Gemara there 49a: a Tanna [taught in the name of] Rav Huna etc. And behold, Rashi's view is that it refers to what was written in seif 29: two women of two men who have not given birth, and that is what it said "and our Tanna" etc. But the Rosh wrote, and cites: a male and a female — there is nothing here for the kohen; Rav Huna taught: two males and a female — there is nothing here for the kohen. And so wrote the Rambam and the Raavad; except that the Raavad's opinion is that it refers to the seifa alone, to what was written here in seif 31, and as written there: "and our Tanna, since with two" etc. And the Rambam wrote that it refers to the reisha alone: two women of one man, one who has given birth and one who has not given birth, of seif 28, and his girsa was: "and our Tanna, since with one man" etc. But the Tur explains that it refers to both of them, for there is no reason to distinguish between them, and therefore he wrote here and in seif 28. But what is written there "and our Tanna" etc. is difficult according to his words, and he followed the Rosh's words, who did not mention what is written there "and our Tanna" etc.
