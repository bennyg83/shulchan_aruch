Accustomed to return. See in She'elat Yaavetz part 1 siman 155, who wrote that nowadays the law has changed: that which they said that the kohen should not be accustomed to do so, that is regarding yichus-verified kohanim of theirs; but regarding mere chezkat kohanim like nowadays, even though we go with them to stringency for all matters and we redeem bechorim through them because it is not possible with others, nevertheless not to be lenient, that is, to extract the father's money from a doubt; and one can almost say that by law they must return, and at least every kohen should be concerned for himself to refrain from a doubt of gezel, lest he is not a kohen; and for this reason it appears that it is proper to act nowadays to redeem on condition to return, l'chatchila. Also it appears that to fulfill every possible doubt, the father also has to redeem his bechor from all kohanim he can find, perhaps a definitely yichus-verified kohen will happen to him; and for this reason as well, the son of a kohenet and a leviah should be redeemed now from a doubt, for what is the difference from every d'oraisa doubt, that what can be fixed we fix; see there. [And in the teshuvah of Chatam Sofer at the end of siman 291 he wrote briefly that the words of the teshuvah of She'elat Yaavetz in this do not appear to him.] And see what I wrote above siman 322 se'if katan 3 about this.
On condition that it be returned, the son is redeemed. See Even HaEzer siman 29, Beit Shmuel seif katan 1, and Bnei Ahubah, Hilchot Ishut chapter 5, halachah 24, regarding whether a doubled condition is required here.
A gift given on condition that it be returned is legally a gift. See Responsa Teshuvah MeAhavah, part 1, simanim 55 to 57. He writes that this applies specifically where the very same object is to be returned, because during the interim it genuinely belongs to the recipient, who is even exempt from unavoidable loss. If the condition is to return different money, however, it is not a gift but a full loan. Therefore, if one gives a kohen five sela on condition that the kohen return five different sela, the son is not redeemed. See his lengthy discussion.
