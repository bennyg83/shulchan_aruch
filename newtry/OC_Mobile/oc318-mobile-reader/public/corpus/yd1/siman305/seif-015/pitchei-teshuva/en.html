When he grows up he is obligated. See in the responsa Zichron Yosef, Yoreh De'ah, end of siman 26, who wrote that this means specifically that he be 13 years old, for even though he is obligated in all the mitzvot d'rabbanan once he has reached chinuch, that is specifically regarding mitzvot that he can also fulfill after he grows up, which is not so with this mitzvah of pidyon, that if he fulfills it now he cannot fulfill it again when he grows up; see there.
