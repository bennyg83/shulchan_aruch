(Taz s.k. 20) He is not a firstborn for inheritance. According to this, if the first child was delivered by caesarean and was a nefel, the second is a bekhor for the kohen, for he is also the firstborn for inheritance since the first was a nefel. See Sidrei Taharah, novellae to Niddah 26, s.v. “Maharsha asks,” etc.
