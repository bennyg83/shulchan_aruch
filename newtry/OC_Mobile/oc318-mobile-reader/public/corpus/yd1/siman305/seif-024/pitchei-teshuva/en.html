His son is not redeemed. See Responsa Yad Eliyahu, end of siman 78, who wrote that perhaps the kohen need not return the money, since the father gave it willingly; it is not like where the infant died within thirty days, etc. See there. The same applies in the first clause, where he said “from now.” See Responsa Keter Kehunah, siman 52, who wrote that if he gave the pidyon money after twenty-nine days, twelve hours, and 793 parts, and said that the pidyon should take effect after thirty days, his son is redeemed. According to the Shach, pidyon is effective after twenty-nine days, twelve hours, and 793 parts. Although the Magen Avraham disagrees in siman 339 s.k. 8, and the main law follows the Magen Avraham, here, where he said it should take effect after thirty days, one may combine the Mechaber's view that his son is redeemed even if the money is no longer intact. See there, and see Responsa Dat Esh, siman 11.
