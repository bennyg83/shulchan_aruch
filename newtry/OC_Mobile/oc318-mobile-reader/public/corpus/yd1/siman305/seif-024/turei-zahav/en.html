Because another preceded him. Even though that other one did not open the womb, nevertheless since this one born after him is not a bechor for inheritance, for he is not the first of his strength, he is also not a bechor for the kohen; for a bechor for one matter — that is, for the womb and not for offspring — is not a bechor. So we say in chapter Yesh Bechor (Bechoros daf 47b).
