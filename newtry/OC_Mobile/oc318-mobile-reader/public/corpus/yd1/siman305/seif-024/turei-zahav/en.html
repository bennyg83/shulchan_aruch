Because another preceded him. Although that other one was not petter rechem, nevertheless since the child born after him is not a firstborn for inheritance, for he is not the first of his father's strength, he is also not a bekhor for the kohen. One who is a bekhor in only one respect, namely for the womb but not for birth order, is not a bekhor; so we say in chapter Yesh Bekhor, Bekhorot 47b.
