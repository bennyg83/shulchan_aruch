His head dead. See Dagul MeRevavah, who wrote that here it means he retracted it and afterward his brothers emerged, such as in a twin birth. But if he was born entirely, it is obvious that he exempts the one born afterward, for a dead eight-month child is no worse than a formed sac, which exempts the one who follows. See there, and see Responsa of my grandfather Panim Meirot I, siman 7.
