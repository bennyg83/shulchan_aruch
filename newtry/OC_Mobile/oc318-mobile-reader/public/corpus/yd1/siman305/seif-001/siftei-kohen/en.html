Two gold coins are required. Hagahot Derishah writes that it should not be less than one and two-thirds reichsthaler, which is now five Polish gold coins. The Sema in Choshen Mishpat 88 wondered how an eighth of a peni can count for kiddushin; perhaps in their days produce was cheap and a perutah purchased much. By that reasoning, today a perutah would not suffice for kiddushin and two gold coins would not suffice for pidyon haben. This is surprising: even if one says so regarding kiddushin, pidyon haben with two gold coins is certainly significant money. Maadanei Melekh requires three reichsthaler, and even according to the Rama it should not be less than two; lechatchilah one should satisfy all views.
