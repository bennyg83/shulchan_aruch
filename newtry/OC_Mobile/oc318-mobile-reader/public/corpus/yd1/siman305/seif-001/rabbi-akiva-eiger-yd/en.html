(Siman 305 seif 3) He gives them to a kohen. Even to a minor kohen, if he is in the category of “throw him a pebble and he throws it back; give him a nut and he takes it.” So writes Responsa Mayim Chayyim of the Peri Chadash. In Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah he wrote that R. Avraham Lapinha zt"l testified that the rabbis' view was that even a minor kohen can effect pidyon haben, and R. Avraham Mutal did so in practice. End quote.
