In value. Shach writes that Ma'adanei Melech discusses this at length and requires three Reichsthaler. He writes that even according to Rama, the amount today is not less than two Reichsthaler. Initially it is proper to satisfy all opinions. Taz writes that one who wishes to perform the mitzvah in the best manner should give the value of five lot and one quint according to the imperial measure, not the Polish measure, because an imperial zekuk of sixteen lot is considerably larger than a Polish zekuk. In fact, eleven imperial lot and one quint equal a Polish zekuk.
