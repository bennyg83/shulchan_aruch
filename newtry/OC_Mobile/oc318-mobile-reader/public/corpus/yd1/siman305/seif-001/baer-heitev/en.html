In value. The Shach wrote that in Ma'adanei Melech he discussed this at length, that we require three Reichsthaler, and he wrote that even according to the master's words it is now not less than two Reichsthaler; see there. At the outset it is good to satisfy all views; end quote. The Taz wrote that anyone who wishes to perform it in the best manner should give the value of the weight of five lot and a quint according to the weight of the imperial lands, and not according to Polish weight, for a zekuk of the imperial lands, which is sixteen lot, is much larger than that of Poland, such that eleven lot and a quint of the imperial weight is a zekuk in Poland.
