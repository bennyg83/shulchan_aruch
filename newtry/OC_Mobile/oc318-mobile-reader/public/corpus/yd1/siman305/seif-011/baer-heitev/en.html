On Shabbos. The redemption is not performed on Shabbos because it resembles buying and selling. For the same reason it is not performed on Yom Tov. It appears that it may be performed at night, although the custom is to redeem during the day. If day 31 falls on a public fast, one must determine whether the child has completed twenty-nine days, twelve hours, and 793 parts before the fast day, which is his thirtieth day. If so, he may be redeemed on day 30 after that precise time. Perhaps we do not advance the redemption to day 30 merely for the sake of the meal. If he has not yet reached that point, he should be redeemed on the night of day 31, the night before the fast. He should not be redeemed during the fast day with the meal postponed until after the fast, because there is insufficient publicity in that arrangement. Nevertheless, I have heard that the custom is to redeem during the fast day and hold the meal that night.
