One third of a shekel. This is one sixth of an ounce of silver, as stated by the Tur. This follows what is written below at the beginning of siman 305, that five shekalim equal two and one half ounces according to Rashi. According to the Rama there, it is approximately two Rhenish gold coins, and a gloss to the Derishah states that this means five gold coins in the present era. Alternatively, a shekel is one third of a Rhenish thaler, namely one Polish gold coin, in which case the minimum annual charitable obligation is ten groschen. Therefore, the custom in these lands is proper, whereby the charity collector circulates in the synagogue on Mondays and Thursdays to collect charity from each person. Even one who gives the smallest coin nevertheless gives more than one third of a shekel over the course of the year. Although Orchot Chaim of the Rosh, printed at the end of the siddurim, states that one should give one and one half gold coins at the end of every year, that is a measure of piety. By strict law, one is obligated to give only one third of a shekel for the entire year, as implied by the Gemara and poskim.
