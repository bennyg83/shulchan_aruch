Lower, etc.; and close to, etc. Meaning, he should place the money in the Chamber of Secrets mentioned in the fifth chapter of Shekalim. The Tosefta there states: Just as there was a Chamber of Secrets in the Mikdash, so there was one in every city, etc. The Sifrei expounds, "To him, privately": From here the Sages instituted the chamber, etc. The statement, "And close to, etc.," is based on Bava Batra 10a: "Which form is, etc.? He gives it, etc. But, etc."
Trustworthy, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. Ela, etc.
