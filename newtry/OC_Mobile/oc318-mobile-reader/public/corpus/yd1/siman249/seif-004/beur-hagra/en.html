If he asked, etc. There: "for because of this matter, etc." — if he said he would give and gave, he receives reward for the saying and reward for the action, etc. He did not say he would give, nor did he tell others to give, but he spoke to him with kind words — from where [do we know] that he receives reward for this? Scripture states: "for because of this matter He will bless you, etc." And so it is in the Tosefta, end of Pe'ah; and in Bava Batra there: "one who gives, etc." and "one who appeases him, etc."; and see Tosafot there, s.v. "and one who appeases him, etc."
(Supplement) If he asked, etc. There (in the above-mentioned Avot D'Rabbi Natan): "but the recipient, etc."; and in Sifri and Tosefta of Pe'ah: "he said, etc." (End.)
