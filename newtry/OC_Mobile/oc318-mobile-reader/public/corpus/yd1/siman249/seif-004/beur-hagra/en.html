If he asked, etc. The Sifrei expounds the verse, "Because of this matter, etc.": If one said he would give and then gave, he receives reward for the statement and reward for the deed, etc. If he did not say that he would give and did not tell others to give, but nevertheless comforted the poor person with kind words, how do we know that he receives reward for this? Scripture states, "Because of this matter Hashem your God will bless you, etc." The same appears in the Tosefta at the end of Peah and in Bava Batra there: "One who gives, etc., and one who consoles him, etc." See Tosafot there, s.v. Vehamefayeso, etc.
(Supplement) If he asked, etc. There, in the aforementioned Avot DeRabbi Natan: "But one who receives, etc." See also the Sifrei and Tosefta of Peah: "He said, etc." (End of supplement.)
