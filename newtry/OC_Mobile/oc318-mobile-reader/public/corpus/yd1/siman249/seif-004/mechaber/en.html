If the poor person asked him and he has nothing to give him — he should not shout at him or raise his voice against him, but appease him with words and show him a good heart that he wished to give him but his hand does not reach: {Rama: And it is forbidden to send the poor person who asks away empty-handed — even if he gives him only one dried fig, as it says, "Lest the downtrodden return ashamed" (language of Tur below siman 257 in the name of Rambam).}
