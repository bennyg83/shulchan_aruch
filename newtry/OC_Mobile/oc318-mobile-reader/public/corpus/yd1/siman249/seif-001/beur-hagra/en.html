The prescribed measure, etc. As stated at the beginning of siman 250; see there.
And one tenth, etc. This is stated in the Sifrei, cited by Tosafot, Taanit 9a, s.v. Aser te'aser: "Tithe, etc."
(Supplement) And one tenth, etc. The Yerushalmi states that the poor tithe applies even to money, deriving this from the verse, "Honor Hashem from your wealth and from the first of all your produce." See Mordechai, first chapter of Bava Batra. (End of supplement.)
And one fifth, etc. This is stated in the Yerushalmi, cited there by Tosafot and the Rosh, and by the Rash in the first chapter of Peah.
And one should not use, etc. As stated in the second chapter of Beitzah, 19b.
