And all the more so to pay taxes with them it is forbidden; even though it is in the first chapter of Bava Basra that even what amei ha'aretz take by force is counted as tzedakah, nevertheless this is called paying his debt from tzedakah; know this, for even regarding what a person feeds his young children we say in the chapter Na'arah sheNitpatah that it is included in "those who do tzedakah at all times" — and would it enter your mind that a person should spend his ma'aser to feed his young children? And regarding buying mitzvos in the synagogue with ma'aser money, it appears to me that if at the time of buying the mitzvos his intent was for this, it is permitted, for the money then goes to tzedakah, and even though he benefits from honoring others to be called to the sefer Torah, this is not a prohibition, for in every ma'aser there is tovas hana'ah for the owners; which is not so if at the time of buying the mitzvos he did not intend to give from ma'aser and afterward he wants to pay from ma'aser — this is like paying his debt from ma'aser, as it appears to me on this. And he wrote in the Derishah in the name of a responsum of our teacher Rabbi Menachem that it is permitted to give from ma'aser to be a ba'al bris or to bring a chasan to the chuppah or to buy sefarim to learn in them and to lend them to others to learn in them, if he did not have the ability and would not have done the mitzvah, end of his words. And it appears to me that when he buys a sefer for himself he must write on it that this is from ma'aser, so that his children know this and not hold it as theirs.
