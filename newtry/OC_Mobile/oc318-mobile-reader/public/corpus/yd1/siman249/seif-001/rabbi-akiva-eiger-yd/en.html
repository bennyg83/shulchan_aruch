(siman 249 seif 1 in Hagahah) Whatever he wishes. And in She'eilot (parashat Terumah) and Tosafot that three and not more — see there; and they brought it in Mishnat Hasidim Orach Chaim (siman 456).
(Taz seif katan 1) He intended this, it is permitted. Shelah, page 262, writes that if he adds more than his fellow, he may use ma'aser funds only for the amount he adds, but not for the amount he would have given anyway, because that portion did not create any additional benefit for the charity fund.
