(siman 249 seif 1 in Hagahah) Whatever he wishes. And in She'eilot (parashat Terumah) and Tosafot that three and not more — see there; and they brought it in Mishnat Hasidim Orach Chaim (siman 456).
(Taz, seif katan 1) If he had this in mind, it is permitted. Shelah writes that if he adds more than his fellow, he may use maaser funds only for the amount he added, but not for the amount that his fellow had also intended to give, because with respect to that amount he provided no benefit to the charity fund.
