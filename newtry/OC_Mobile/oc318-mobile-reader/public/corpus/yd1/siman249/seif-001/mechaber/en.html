How much one must give and how he should give it. And it contains 16 seifim. The measure of giving — if his hand reaches abundance, he gives according to the need of the poor; and if his hand does not reach so much, he gives up to a fifth of his assets — a mitzvah from the best; and one tenth is the intermediate measure; less than this — an evil eye. And this fifth that they said — the first year from the principal; from here onward, a fifth of what he profited each year: {Rama: And a person should not squander more than a fifth, lest he need medical treatment (Beit Yosef in the name of the Gemara, chapter Na'arah Shenitatpatah); and specifically all his life, but at the time of death a person may give charity as much as he wishes (same there, chapter Metzi'at HaIshah, and Rif, Rosh, Ran, and Mordechai bring it); and one should not make from his tithe something of a mitzvah — such as candles for the synagogue or other mitzvah matter — but give it to the poor (Maharil, Laws of Rosh Hashanah).}
