And he gives him a gift. Since the person has not yet become completely impoverished, he is not embarrassed, for friends commonly give gifts to one another. Beit Yosef.
Or he enters into a partnership with him. This is preferable to a loan, as stated in the Gemara. The Beit Yosef explains that one who lends money to another without profit causes the borrower embarrassment, since the borrower benefits from his fellow while the lender receives no benefit at all. But in a partnership he is not embarrassed, since both parties benefit.
