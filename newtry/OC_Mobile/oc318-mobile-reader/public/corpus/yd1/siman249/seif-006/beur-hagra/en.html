Eight, etc.; one who supports, etc. As stated in Torat Kohanim on, "If your brother becomes impoverished, etc.": Do not allow him to fall. To what may this be compared? To a load upon a donkey. While it is still in place, one person can grasp it and steady it; once it has fallen to the ground, even five people, etc. Likewise, Shabbos 63a states: "Greater is, etc., and provides him with capital, etc." This is the meaning of, "Whether, etc., or, etc."
