That he write his name. See in responsum Emunat Shmuel siman 35: Reuven was asked who was a charity collector and hired workers to plaster and whitewash the synagogue with charity money, and the craftsman wrote "and this was done by order of Reuven ben Yaakov charity collector"; and after several years the congregation stood and protested on this, and Reuven answered: although I collected the money from the congregation — since I toiled for the synagogue, by law my name may be written on it; and furthermore since they did not protest until now — there is chazakah; and he answered the law is with the congregation: specifically where he did it himself in his body or with his money he has right to write his name on it, not in such a case where he did not do himself, only hired workers with charity money; and also what he claims he has chazakah — is nothing, and permission is in their hands to erase — see there. And see in responsum Tzemach Tzedek siman 56 what he wrote on this.
