(Taz s.k. 16) D'oraisa. Accordingly it appears to me that for glass vessels we believe a minor, for in any case according to everyone immersion is only d'rabbanan, as Peri Chadash wrote (this siman s.k. 3 and s.k. 26); likewise Sha'agat Aryeh and likewise responsum Pri Megadim (part 2 siman 96). However, according to what Tosafot Eruvin (daf 41) wrote — that specifically regarding their own matter that rests upon them minors are believed in d'rabbanan matters, but not for agency — see there. If so, here when one sends a minor to immerse glass vessels, one may say no; and see Minchat Chinuch (siman 337 seif 8 katan) and what he wrote in the gloss there.
(Seif 7 in the gloss) And likewise a pepper mill. And a coffee mill — according to Taz (s.k. 4) one immerses without a blessing, since coffee requires cooking; according to Shach (s.k. 11) one may say it does not require immersion, for it is not fit for anything else.
