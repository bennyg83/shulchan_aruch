Requires tevilah. Tur likewise wrote in the name of Sefer HaMitzvos and concluded: since one uses the silver inside, but not outside, even though it stabilizes; and some are stringent to immerse without a blessing. Beit Yosef explained his second way, which is primary in his view: a silver cup standing on a wooden vessel that surrounds and attaches it requires tevilah, as we say regarding a wooden vessel with iron hoops outside — but not outside, meaning if silver is outside and wood inside, even though silver stabilizes the wood like the wooden vessel with iron hoops that does not require tevilah; and "some are stringent" applies to both, whether silver outside stabilizing wood or the reverse, for we are uncertain whether we follow the stabilizer — such is his explanation. And to my humble opinion it is astonishing if Tur explained Semak thus, for then he should have ruled the same for iron hoops above, for Beit Yosef has one law for them — and he contradicts himself within the same breath, for he ruled immediately below iron hoops do not require tevilah even for one who follows the stabilizer; therefore it appears Tur explained Semak regarding vessel within vessel, as Semak explicitly states: silver vessel inside wooden vessel, where the outer is seen somewhat inside too; therefore there is a stabilizer like R. Meir in Shabbat chapter 1 above; only Semak holds we do not follow the outer stabilizer but the essence of use, which is a complete vessel only partly seen as outer inside; on this he said some are stringent when the outer is silver and fully stabilizes to immerse it, but one need not be stringent and bless; but in the first case where the inner is silver and use is essentially silver, certainly tevilah with blessing is required, for whatever is used inside — the stabilizer cannot nullify and exempt from tevilah; and in our Semak it explicitly writes "some are stringent to immerse where it stabilizes without blessing" — from this it applies only to this case; and so it appears from Shulchan Aruch here, who first wrote iron hoops do not require tevilah and then wrote silver vessel here requires tevilah — implying even with blessing; he retracted from Beit Yosef and holds "some are stringent" applies only to silver outside, as I explained; and Maharshal likewise; and not as Levush wrote that this tevilah Shulchan Aruch mentioned in seif 7 without blessing — rather it requires tevilah with blessing; this is clear. And not only that, but even in Tur's latter case where the outer is silver, since it stabilizes, tevilah with blessing is required, as Rama wrote in seif 10 "and if part of the silver," as I wrote there following those who go after the stabilizer — so here too; but for practical halachah I wrote in seif 10 one should not bless, neither here with outer silver nor there with part of the silver:
