A silver cup attached to a wooden vessel requires immersion: {Rama: And likewise a pepper mill within wood that has fixed iron with which one grinds requires immersion (Mordechai); and likewise iron funnels or iron spigots and all other metals — all require immersion (Arukh); and specifically when the main vessel is of metal; but if the main vessel is of wood and only a little fixed iron is in it — since it is possible to use it without the iron — it does not require immersion; but a vessel fixed with iron pegs without which it would not be possible to use it, and they are on the inside — requires immersion (so it appears from Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 17).}
