Immersion. It implies that a blessing is required; and even though Beit Yosef ruled regarding a silver cup whose stabilizer is wood that one immerses without a blessing, one may say that there, if not for the wood the cup would not be fit at all and the wood is what stabilizes it; but here the case is where the cup is fit as is and is only attached to a wooden vessel — end of Shach's words; and Taz explained another matter — see there.
Iron. Ohr U'Me'ir wrote that the lower part that receives the grindings does not require immersion, for it is a wooden vessel; and a wooden funnel that has an iron tube does not require immersion even though one uses it via the iron, since it does not stabilize relative to the funnel; and likewise all similar cases — end of his words.
