Cards. The same applies if he bought it for commerce; Shach ruled that one immerses it without a blessing, and likewise Taz; he further wrote that likewise one who borrows a vessel from a Jewish shopkeeper for a meal immerses it without a blessing, only the shopkeeper should inform the one who buys it from him afterward not to immerse it again with a blessing. What Rama wrote — that the first is forbidden to use for meal purposes, but for non-meal purposes it is permitted even though he took it for cards — does not require immersion; and conversely, if the second bought it for a meal, even though the first took it for cards and the like, nevertheless he requires immersion.
