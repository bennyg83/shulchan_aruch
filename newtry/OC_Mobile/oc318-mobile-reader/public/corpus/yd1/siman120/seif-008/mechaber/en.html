One who borrows or rents a vessel from a non-Jew does not require immersion; but if a Jew bought it from the non-Jew and lent it to his fellow — it requires immersion, for it was already obligated in the hands of the first; and some say that if the first did not take it for the purpose of a meal but to cut cards and the like — he need not immerse it: {Rama: But the first is forbidden to use it for meal purposes even casually without immersion, even though he took it to cut cards (logical opinion and Orach Chaim); and likewise if the second Jew bought it from the first for meal purposes — the second requires immersion (Beit Yosef).}
