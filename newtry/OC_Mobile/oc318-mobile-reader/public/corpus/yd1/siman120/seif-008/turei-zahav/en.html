To cut cards. Beit Yosef wrote: likewise if he bought for commerce it does not require tevilah; Hagahot Ashiri concludes: since it is borrowed in his hand, Beit Yosef learned if he bought it requires tevilah, as Rama wrote shortly; but Issur VeHeter general rule 28 law 87 wrote: since if the first Jew wanted to use it for a meal, even temporarily, he would have had to immerse it — he is thereby obligated — therefore the second Jew borrowing from him must immerse it.
