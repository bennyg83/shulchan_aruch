Loose in his hand, etc. Requires study, for Beit Yosef and the later authorities did not bring any dispute on this; and Rambam chapter 2 of Hilchot Mikvaot law 11 wrote: one who holds a person and vessels and immerses them — they remain in their impurity, and even if he loosened his hands it is a decree lest he not loosen — end of his words; and Rav Yerocham brings it in his conclusion; and likewise Rashba at the end of Terumat HaDeshen in the name of Rambam; and likewise Rosh in Bava Kama there; and Bach ruled below siman 198 seif 20 regarding immersion of a person that even if loose his tevilah did not take effect; and at the end of chapter 8 of Mikvaot we plainly teach a person and vessels together — see there; requires study:
