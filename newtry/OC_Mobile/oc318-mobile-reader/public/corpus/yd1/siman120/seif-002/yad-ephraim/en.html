Be'er Heitev and Beit Yosef siman 161 — the coating of the inside of a vessel of a non-Jew requires immersion, for the main use is on the inside; and the custom is to immerse without a blessing. And see Taz who wrote like Tur that coating on the inside is only for ornament, and one immerses without a blessing; and the custom is as he wrote there. And see Shach who disagrees and requires immersion with a blessing if coated inside and outside, as it is like metal vessels.
