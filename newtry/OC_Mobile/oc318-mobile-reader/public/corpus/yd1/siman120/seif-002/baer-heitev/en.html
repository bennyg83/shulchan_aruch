Detached water. The Taz disagrees and holds that even with detached water it is effective, provided only that he not grip the vessel forcefully but only with moderate contact — see there at length. Shach wrote that it requires study, for here the Mechaber plainly rules that if the vessel is loose in his hand at the time of immersion it is effective; yet Rambam in chapter 2 of Hilchot Mikvaot wrote that one who holds a person and vessels and immerses them — they remain in their impurity, and even if he loosened his hands it is a decree lest he not loosen; and Bach ruled in siman 198 seif 20 regarding immersion of a person that even if loose his immersion did not take effect; and in Mikvaot end of chapter 8 we plainly teach "a person and vessels together" — see there; requires study.
