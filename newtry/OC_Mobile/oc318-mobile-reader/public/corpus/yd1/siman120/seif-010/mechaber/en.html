A Jew who gave money to a non-Jewish craftsman to make a vessel for him from it does not require immersion (so it appears from Mordechai): {Rama: And some disagree (Tur in the name of Ritzba) and one should immerse it without a blessing; and if some of the money from which the vessel was made belongs to the non-Jew — it requires immersion (it appears from Mordechai); and likewise a Jewish craftsman who made a vessel for a non-Jew and bought it from him — he immerses it likewise without a blessing if the non-Jew gave all the metals (Arukh); but if he made it for himself and only bought the metals from the non-Jew, or gave some metals of his own — he does not require immersion (Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Ashiri).}
