If a non-Jew pawned a vessel to a Jew — if it appears from the mind of the non-Jew that he wishes to sink it in his possession, it requires immersion; and if not — he immerses it without a blessing, or buys another vessel and immerses it with it: (and if in the end it sank in his possession, he returns and immerses it without a blessing) (Beit Yosef in the name of an Ashkenazi responsum and Agur, and it is in the responsum of Maharil siman 60).
