We do not believe a minor, etc. Because tevilat kelim is d'oraisa; and if he immersed before an adult, tevilah is valid — Tur wrote the reason in Terumat HaDeshen: even for Rosh who holds a niddah immersing requires intent, nevertheless here an adult can teach the minor to intend to purify; and further, in this tevilah that is not from impurity to purity, who says intent is required? — end; and since Rama did not write here that the adult must teach him, it implies he adopts the latter answer that here intent is not required at all:
