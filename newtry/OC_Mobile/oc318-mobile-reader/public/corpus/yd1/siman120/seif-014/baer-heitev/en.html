It implies from Rama's words that here we do not require intent at all (and likewise if a Jew immersed them afterward without the owner's knowledge, the immersion is valid) — Taz; and for this reason immersion by a non-Jew is effective b'dieved but not l'chatchila because of the blessing, unlike Bach who ruled that his immersion did not take effect, like a niddah — see siman 198; here it is different; accordingly, if vessels fell into water before their immersion they do not require immersion again — Shach; nevertheless Taz wrote that l'chatchila one should not immerse solely through a non-Jew, for how can he bless on what the non-Jew immerses; but if a Jew also immerses some vessel at that time while the non-Jew assists him to immerse the other vessels, the blessing refers to what the Jew does and it is fine — end of his words.
