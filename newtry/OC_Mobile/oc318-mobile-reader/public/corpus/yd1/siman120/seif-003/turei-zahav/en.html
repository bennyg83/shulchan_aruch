He blesses on tevilat kelim. Darkei Moshe wrote in the name of Mordechai, chapter HaSho'el: some require blessing on tevilah of metal vessels so it not imply other vessels not liable for tevilah — end of his words; and likewise in Hagahot Ashiri — end of his words. I wondered: if so, he should also bless on metal eating vessels so it not imply vessels not for eating; rather one need not mention in the blessing all details of the mitzvos' explanations. And it is not comparable to Chanukah candle blessing of "ner" without specifying Chanukah, for there the essence of the mitzvah is Chanukah, as here with vessels; and covering blood with earth — we wrote the reason in its place that he blesses on two coverings.
