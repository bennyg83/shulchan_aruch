Chochmas Adam wrote there law 15: one must insert the entire vessel in water at one time so that none of it remains outside the water, even any amount; and if it is large and one cannot immerse it all at once, it appears to me it is forbidden to immerse in halves, similar to immersion of a niddah, and it is not comparable to hag'alah, which is to expel the prohibition — and once he hag'alah part, he has expelled — but here it is because of impurity, and since part of it remains outside the water, impurity returns and spreads through the entire vessel — end of his words:
