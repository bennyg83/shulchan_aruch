(There seif 3) He blesses on immersion of a vessel. Shach wrote s.k. 8 that some rule to bless on immersion of metal vessels; one may say that what he wrote to bless on immersion of metal vessels is for those poskim who hold tevilat kelim is d'oraisa; if so, the essential mitzvah written in the Torah is metal vessels, as it is written "everything that comes into fire you shall pass through fire," and that is metal vessels; unlike those who hold it is d'rabbanan, for from the rabbinic decree glass vessels also require immersion; if so, one should not bless on metal vessels, for glass vessels also require immersion.
