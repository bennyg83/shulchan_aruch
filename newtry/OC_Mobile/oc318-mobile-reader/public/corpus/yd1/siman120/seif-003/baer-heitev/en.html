Vessels. Shach wrote that some poskim bless on immersion of metal vessels; Bach wrote that this is the essential practice and that he saw them act thus according to the elders; Taz wrote that one need not mention metal, and greater still he found at the end of Ohr U'Me'ir in a responsum: Mahari'z Ronkel answered that one should not mention "vessel" but only immerse plainly (b'dieved, if he blessed on many vessels "on immersion of a vessel," or on one vessel "on immersion of vessels," he has fulfilled — Peri Chadash).
