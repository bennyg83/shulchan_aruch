A Jew who sold a vessel to a non-Jew and returned and bought it from him requires immersion; but if he pawned it in his possession and returned and redeemed it from him — he does not require immersion: {Rama: A Jew and a non-Jew who bought a vessel in partnership do not require immersion; a Jew from whom they were stolen or robbed and his vessels were returned to him — he does not require immersion; but a minister or governor who forced a Jew and took his vessels and they were returned to him — they require immersion, for they already sank in the possession of the non-Jew (Arukh).}
