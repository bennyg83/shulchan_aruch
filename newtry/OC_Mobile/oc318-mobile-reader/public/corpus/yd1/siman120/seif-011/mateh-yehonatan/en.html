(There seif 11) And he returned and redeemed it from him — it does not require immersion. See Tosafot Avodah Zarah chapter 5 daf 75b s.v. "if because he holds, etc."; and what Maharsha explained there implies that specifically after he returned and redeemed it immersion is not required, unlike while it is still in the non-Jew's hand, if a Jew uses it he requires immersion, for he explains the reason of what Tosafot wrote at the end: "but one who pledges a vessel to a non-Jew and returned and redeemed it does not require immersion," because now that he redeemed it we see that initially he did not truly intend to sink it; but beforehand it is fine to be uncertain lest he intended to sink it, and if so he requires immersion in any case without a blessing; unlike if the reason is because we establish that a non-Jew does not acquire a pledge from a Jew — if so, it is the Jew's from beginning to end; requires study.
