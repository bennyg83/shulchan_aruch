And he redeemed it — no tevilah. Maharshal wrote: even if he despaired of redeeming; but if initially he pledged with intent not to redeem and changed his mind and redeemed, tevilah is required — end of his words; and although above there is doubt regarding a Jew with a pledge from a non-Jew, here it is different, with proof from chapter Kol Sha'ah (Pesachim daf 31): there is a dispute whether a Jew acquires a pledge from a non-Jew; but a non-Jew from a Jew — all agree he does not acquire:
