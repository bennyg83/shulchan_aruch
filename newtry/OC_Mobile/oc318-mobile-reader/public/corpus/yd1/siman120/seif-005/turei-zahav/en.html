Slaughter knife. Mordechai wrote the reason it is not considered an eating vessel: it is forbidden to cut with it even cold; and although the animal is fit for eating, nevertheless it still requires cooking — end of his words. And likewise the skinning knife is not used to eat with as it is now — likewise Issur VeHeter. And it appears since Rama wrote on this "and good to immerse without a blessing," the same applies to iron implements for matzah that he wrote afterward do not require tevilah — likewise immerse without blessing, for one reason applies to both; only Rama wrote it here because Issur VeHeter wrote it in a gloss on this, and Tur listed scissors among vessels without eating needs — from the Talmud at the end of Avodah Zarah, where R. Ashi asked: even wool shears too? Rashi explained: scissors with which they shear wool garments; and the Gemara answers: eating vessels the Torah spoke of. And Perishah wrote Tur's scissors means even what they trim vegetables with. Maharshal disagreed and wrote he was not precise, for vegetable scissors are eating vessels, for in Choshen Mishpat siman 97 it lists tools for making food — end of his words. I say Perishah is correct, for at the end of chapter HaMekabel they say a butcher's resting-knife is for food needs, and Rashi explains it is butchers' knife for cutting meat — and see the skinning knife is also butchers' knife and does not require tevilah for the reason we mentioned; rather certainly "tool for making food" does not apply to eating vessels at all. The questioner who asked from wool shears could have asked from vegetables, but he preferred to ask; and furthermore Choshen Mishpat there lists slaughter knife, and here Mordechai and Shulchan Aruch ruled no tevilah is needed — Perishah's words are true. And Issur VeHeter wrote: likewise iron with which they fix kreplekh on Purim or scrape dough in the kneading trough — only if they remove burnt matter from bread when taking it from the oven requires tevilah — end of his words; and in all of them the reason is whatever the user needs cooking for does not require tevilah:
