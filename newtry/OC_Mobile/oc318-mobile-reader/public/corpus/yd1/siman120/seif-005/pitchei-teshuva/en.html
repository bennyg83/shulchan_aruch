That it does not require tevilah. See responsum Shev Yaakov siman 31 regarding vessels fixed in an oven from which they take hot water for kneading dough — he inclined to say they do not require tevilah at all, even if they are of metal, and even though sometimes they pour hot tea-water from them; nevertheless we follow the majority of its use — see there:
The matzah irons. Baer Heitev in the name of Taz and Shach. And see Peri Megadim Orach Chaim siman 491 in Mishbetzot s.k. 26, who wrote in Yiddish that they place matzos on them — even according to Shach tevilah is required (without a blessing), for it is fit for actual food use.
