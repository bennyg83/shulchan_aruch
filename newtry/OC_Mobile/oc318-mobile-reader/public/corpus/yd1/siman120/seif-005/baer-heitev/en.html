Matzah irons. In this too one immerses without a blessing — Taz; but Shach wrote that those irons are even more inferior, since it is impossible to use them for another food, therefore they do not require immersion at all; accordingly it appears that vegetable scissors with which one trims them also do not require immersion, unlike Bach; and certainly the awl with which one pierces barrel hoops does not require immersion, and so is the practice; Ohr U'Me'ir wrote that likewise the iron with which one fixes "kreplekh" on Purim, or with which one scrapes dough in the kneading trough, does not require immersion — only if one removes burnt matter from bread when taking it from the oven does it require immersion.
