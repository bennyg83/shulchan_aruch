The laws of tevilat kelim (immersion of vessels). In it are 16 seifim: One who buys from a non-Jew eating vessels of metal or of glass, or vessels coated with lead on the inside — even though they are new — must immerse them in a mikveh or a spring of forty se'ah (Tur in the name of Sefer HaMitzvot and Avi Ezri):
{Rama: Some say that vessels coated with lead, even on the inside, one immerses without a blessing (Beit Yosef in the name of Semak and Arukh) — and so is the practice.}
