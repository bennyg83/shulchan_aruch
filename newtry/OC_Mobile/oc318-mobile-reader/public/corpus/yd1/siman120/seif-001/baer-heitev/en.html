Glass. The reason is stated in the Taz that glass is also called metal, for it can be repaired by melting; and the reason for immersion is so that they emerge from the impurity of the non-Jew to the sanctity of Israel. Ohr U'Me'ir wrote that vessels coated with glass have the same law as vessels coated with lead.
Blessing. Or he immerses them together with some other vessel with a blessing, as below seif 9 (Ohr U'Me'ir: for the coating on the inside is only for ornament; nevertheless, if coated inside and outside, everyone immerses it with a blessing — Peri Chadash). Mordechai wrote that even if it is coated only on the outside with lead, one immerses it without a blessing; Bach wrote that one should wonder at the custom of the world who use pots coated with lead on the inside without any immersion at all.
