Scripture teaches: however — a spring, a spring purifies in a kli sheni, and a mikveh in forty se'ah; and in the first chapter of Mikvaot mishnah 7 and for a spring, etc.; and there chapter 5 mishnah 1 that the spring, etc.; and mishnah 3 and for a spring, etc.; and likewise Rambam and Raavad; but R. Yitzchak explained: only for vessels that are d'oraisa, even in a mikveh a revi'it suffices, as written in the first chapter of Pesachim (17b), even though they nullified revi'it of a mikveh as written in chapter 6 of Nazir (38a) — the spring remains on Torah law and d'rabbanan is discussed there; and even though they expound a verse in Torat Kohanim, it is a rabbinic support; but a person, even in a spring from the Torah requires forty se'ah, from what is written in chapter 3 of Chagigah (22a), for the ground trembles, etc.; and this applies only to a spring where earth roots connect in water under the ground (and see above siman 201 s.k. 6). And Semak wrote: this is for impurity; but here even in a spring we require forty se'ah, as written "waters in which a niddah immerses," etc.; this is his language there: if it wrote "and it shall be pure," etc. — that is in a spring or revi'it of a mikveh d'oraisa, for "and it shall be pure" is in what was purified from impurity; and this is Tosafot there s.v. waters even in two; and the same applies to a spring, for they are equal d'oraisa:
