(Siman 120 in Shulchan Aruch seif 2 in the gloss) And specifically if he moistened his hands in mikveh water but not in drawn water, etc. See Taz s.k. 4 and Shach s.k. 2, who expanded for our benefit, and it appears that they explain Rama's words "he moistened his hand in mikveh water" to mean even if he detached water from mikveh water it is effective; therefore they raised the difficulty that was raised — see there at length. But the truth guides his way: the explanation of Rama's words is not so; rather, certainly if one detaches water from mikveh water it is not effective without loosening the hands. And what he wrote "and specifically if he moistened his hands" means that he first placed his hand under mikveh water, and afterward, while it remains under the water without any detaching, he takes the vessel within his hands — then there is no interposition, since nothing was detached from the mikveh; the name of the mikveh is still upon it and is not nullified, since in any case there is contact of a handbreadth; unlike if he first removed and detached the water from its place of connection, for then there is no name of mikveh upon it, and connection to the mikveh also does not exist if he does not loosen his hands, for we do not say connection for a handbreadth in such a case to constitute the name of mikveh upon it. This is the proof Mordechai wrote from chapter 7 of Mikvaot: "one who immerses, etc., because the water precedes, etc." — meaning, since it is impossible that the water not precede the place of the legs, therefore since they are within the mikveh the name of mikveh is not nullified from him, and therefore it is effective even without loosening; requires study; and see Taz.
