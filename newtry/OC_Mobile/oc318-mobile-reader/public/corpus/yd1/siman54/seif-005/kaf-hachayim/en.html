(5) An incident occurred: a rib broke and rejoined and it had a spur, and the lung was recognizable at the place where the spur rested like a depression; and I declared it kosher through checking the lung; and if because of the defect of the depression in the lung there is remedy through checking — but for us who are not expert, everywhere inspection is required; here it is a known place, and the sages of the city agreed with me to permit. Tiferet Lemoshe daf 12b. Machazik Berachah ot 7, Anaf Yosef there ot 15. Zivchei Tzedek ot 6.
