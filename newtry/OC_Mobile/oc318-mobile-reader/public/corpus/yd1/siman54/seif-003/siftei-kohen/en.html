A rib with half a vertebra, or an entire vertebra missing from the beginning of its creation — kosher, as Rambam wrote: an organ regarding which it is said "removed" — if created missing, kosher (and it was explained above siman 50 seif 2); so too in Terumat HaDeshen, and Darkei Moshe brings it; and he wondered there on Beit Yosef who brought Terumat HaDeshen's words here anonymously, whereas above siman 50 he ruled like Rashba from this — if so, that which he wrote in the hagahah here "deficient — kosher" means for practical law, as Darkei Moshe wrote in siman 50 in the name of Issur VeHeter that we rule like Rambam; and so Maharshal in Perek HaMekasheh siman 17 — but where there is no great loss one should be stringent, as above siman 50 s.k. 2 in the hagahah; and so in Tur HaChadash general rule 87 din 5 one should be stringent:
And the same if there is extra, etc. Darkei Moshe wrote: it is written in Terumat HaDeshen siman 177 — if twelve large ribs are found on each side — kosher; and expert butchers told me that sometimes such is found in animals, and no one objects or complains, only some refrain from eating an extra rib because it is close to the kidney fat, etc.; and in Rashba's responsum siman 196 — an animal in which fifteen ribs were found on one side and on one side as proper — kosher; for even if we say all excess is considered as removed, an uprooted rib is kosher until the majority are uprooted, end of his words — if so, it is implied that if there were an extra vertebra it would be treifah according to his view who declares deficient like removed; and specifically for us who rule all excess is considered as removed, even another with it, as above siman 41; but Rashba permits also in this, who wrote in his responsum there: and there is another reason to permit — I explain "all excess is considered as removed" applies only to that excess alone, end of his words; if so, according to that view it is kosher in every case; and even though we do not rule like Rashba's explanation of "all excess is considered as removed" (as explained above siman 41 s.k. 15) — nevertheless it is kosher if more than eleven ribs and eighteen vertebrae are found, for I wrote above that we rule like Rambam that deficient is not like removed (and excess like removed means deficient — and requires further study); and even for Beit Yosef who is stringent like Rashba that deficient is like removed — nevertheless for Rashba it is permitted for another reason; if so, for all it is permitted in every case, end of Darkei Moshe; and R' Yehuda Leib wrote: although above siman 50 s.k. 2 we rule that where there is no great loss one should be stringent that deficient is like removed — nevertheless here, since there is another view to permit, one should not be stringent (and accordingly, even if extra ribs are found beyond their majority — kosher;
