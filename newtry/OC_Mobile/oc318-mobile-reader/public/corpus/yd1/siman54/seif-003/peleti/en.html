Uprooted from the large ribs — Rashba holds that even if a rib was uprooted we require a large rib; and his proof may be rejected, for his proof is that we do not establish R' Yosi's "six from here and six from here" to include those small ribs — for it is simple we require large ribs without vertebrae, as explained in the Gemara; but here we establish Shmuel's "a rib and half a vertebra" — one may say even in small ribs; and therefore Tosafot at the beginning of Avodah Zarah challenged R' Yosi: Shmuel's matter is not in the count — and they do not answer that it is in the count of small ribs; rather Tosafot hold like Beit HaGadol that we do not require a vertebra. And Yeshuot Shmuel brought proof to refute — if so, what does the Gemara challenge on R' Chanina and R' Eliezer; one may say Rav deals with large ribs and they hung it on small ribs — this may be rejected, for according to Rashba anonymous "rib" means large, for in the mishnah too it does not teach "large" and nevertheless we explain large — this is anonymous rib; if so, also in R' Chanina and R' Eliezer's matter one can only explain large ribs; and as Yeshuot Shmuel himself wrote, one may say therefore the Rosh wrote anonymously rib means those that have marrow. But if this is difficult — the difficulty is why do we forcefully establish that deficiency in the spine at the end of Keifli; and in Shulchan De'ah it is not in the category of spine at all — and we do not establish vertebrae in small ribs that are after the large ribs, as explained in Tur that because a rib was uprooted it is kosher and requires a vertebra for treifah; why need the end of Keifli; and accordingly even in small ones, uprooting a rib and half vertebra is treifah; and still the Gemara's difficulty remains and it had to answer at the end of Keifli. However, one may say that in any case one should take to heart: why does it challenge — it would be a leniency of Beit Shammai and stringency of Beit Hillel — what relevance does this question have here in Chullin; in Bechorot Shmuel spoke to the matter, and likewise in Eruvin and above beginning of Avodah Zarah where the Gemara deals with it — it should have challenged. And one may say: certainly that deficiency in the spine does not defile is the halacha leMoshe miSinai; certainly if a person has other treifot, because of that he would not defile in a tent — rather they do not pay attention except to deficiency in the spine — halacha leMoshe miSinai that in deficiency not fit to sustain life he does not defile; accordingly one may say certainly Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel did not disagree at all regarding treifot — Beit Shammai concedes treifah, only Beit Shammai holds they do not pay attention whether treifah or kosher — halacha leMoshe miSinai that two are needed, and Beit Hillel hold the halacha leMoshe miSinai of deficiency that applies to treifah applies also to tumah; therefore Beit Hillel hold regarding deficiency of vertebra, etc., the same for treifot; if so, the difficulty is resolved — count it among Beit Shammai's leniencies for treifot, Beit Shammai concede and did not disagree at all in this; and see Tosafot Eruvin where with this the above difficulty is resolved. And behold, one may establish it at the end of Keifli that perhaps there is a vertebra without a rib — for anonymously it teaches deficiency in the spine, Beit Shammai say two vertebrae, etc. — how can we say it refers to the spine at the end of Keifli, and does the mishnah usually leave so much unstated. But one may say: the difficulty is how can Beit Shammai say two vertebrae — when one vertebra is detached and taken, it is not enough, for the spinal cord is severed, which passes inside the vertebrae; how is it possible the vertebra was taken and the cord remains; if so, it should be taken from the living and die from severing the cord; but if it deals below the end of Keifli, the difficulty is resolved, for there severing the cord does not harm, for it already passed between the sections; and so Tosafot Eruvin daf 7 (and Rama wrote in siman 57 that a vertebra removed even where severing the cord does not disqualify — nevertheless disqualifies; and Lechem Mishneh wrote: I do not know his source; and from what is written the matter is compelled; and at that time Tosafot Eruvin were hidden from him). But according to the above that Beit Shammai concede treifot, there is no proof here that Beit Shammai hold treifah does not depend on tumah; and in truth, even though treifah is from severing the cord — nevertheless it defiles only in deficiency of the spine itself — halacha leMoshe miSinai of two vertebrae; if so, it is still not compelled to say at the end of Keifli; if so, Levush should not have challenged and counted among Beit Shammai's leniencies — one may say: Beit Shammai concede treifah with two vertebrae in one vertebra, but nevertheless it does not defile until two vertebrae are lacking; but now that it answers at the end of Keifli — it is difficult how it teaches anonymously; it must be said that otherwise it would be treifah from deficiency and cord severance — nevertheless it does not help Beit Shammai until the spine is lacking; and accordingly it is not so, for one depends on the other; if treifah it does not defile — it challenges well and one may count, etc. And if so, also the above difficulty is resolved — there is no need for small ribs, how does it conclude it deals with them, for there too between sections severing the cord disqualifies. But with all this, one may resolve before weighing, for we lean stringent in every case, since the essence of treifot depends on uprooting half a vertebra; and what we challenged — why does the Gemara cut off that count specifically and count among Beit Shammai's leniencies — one may say another way: they said "so that it be taken from the living and die" — there are two explanations: (1) it dies and is not already dead, which is nevelah with death; accordingly both hold one vertebra — treifah, two — nevelah, and on this Beit Shammai hold specifically deficiency must be nevelah, and Beit Hillel hold treifah suffices; if so, no difficulty, and count leniencies, etc., for they did not disagree regarding treifah at all. But now that it establishes at the end of Keifli and challenges how anonymously — it must be said that when a vertebra is removed it is gistera and nevelah; and the mishnah implies only treifah; accordingly the above is unnecessary and it challenges well — count and requires further study:
But if it occurred thus deficient, etc. This law is from the author of Terumat HaDeshen; on this our later rabbis disagreed — one forbids and one permits. We must investigate somewhat according to the plain meaning: this ostensibly depends on the dispute of Rambam and Rashba whether created missing is in the category of removed or not — for Rambam one may say it is not in the category of uprooted; and the same for deficiency in the spine where we say "so that it be taken from the living and die" — "taken" implies, but not created missing; and even though "taken from the living" is said only regarding deficiency of the skull — nevertheless if deficiency in the skull means specifically removed, the same for deficiency in the spine means removed and not created missing; but according to Rashba — treifah. And even though Rashba holds "all excess is considered as removed" — meaning it makes a hole at its place of connection; if so, what of it that they were perforated — does perforation declare treifah? In that law we rule like Rambam, and in this like Rashba, and the matters depend on each other. Rather, the Rav in Terumat HaDeshen wrote it is not reasonable even for Rashba that it would be pure in a tent if at the beginning of its creation there were not eighteen vertebrae and it was created missing — for Beit Shammai two and Beit Hillel one; and the same for uprooted ribs when created missing; and in truth this reasoning is not recognized, as the Rav Peri Chadash truly investigated and made for himself supports from Rashba's responsum. And here is some proof for Peri Chadash from that which we challenge on "R' Yosi says uprooted rib — treifah" from deficiency in the spine that R' Yosi says; and likewise for treifah — without deficiency in the spine it is treifah from lacking a rib; and it answers at the end of Keifli; and the challenger did not know from the end of Keifli what this uprooted rib deals with — in brief, if detachment and lacking a vertebra — the cord is severed, for even if the cord hangs in air, with slight movement it severs — and obviously treifah. And below between the sections he had not yet concluded it is obvious there are not twelve ribs; if so, lacking a vertebra means from birth and there is no cord severance at all, for the cord descends in order without any defect, and as it descended before between eighteen vertebrae it descends now between seventeen — the matter is compelled; and ostensibly one may reject as above that he knew it is below between sections; nevertheless he holds ribs without marrow exist there, and the question is from ribs without marrow per the Rosh and his supporters who declare treifah also in them. But from Tosafot it is proven explicitly the opposite — they explain therefore it does not challenge and count deficiency in the skull, for the essence of treifot is that ultimately the membrane will be perforated — and it taught me; but regarding deficiency in the spine it is not so — the cord will ultimately be severed; it deals even below between sections; and it is difficult in brief: behold deficiency in spine and skull where Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel disagree deal even with deficiency from birth; if so, in the skull what of it that created missing — nevertheless the membrane will eventually sever, for the bone does not protect when missing from creation, unlike the spine created with seventeen vertebrae — how does this harm the cord until it causes severance; therefore Tosafot hold created missing one vertebra — kosher; and it is strained to say Tosafot hold like Rambam that wherever "removed" is said, created missing too — for it did not teach "removed" only deficiency in the spine; as Peri Chadash wrote; rather Tosafot hold like Terumat HaDeshen's view — the matter is somewhat compelled. And here regarding uprooted rib it also appears created missing is kosher; and specifically "uprooted" we learned — for if we say in the category of uprooted also created missing, then that which R' Chanina and R' Eliezer asked Rav "uprooted rib from here and rib from here" — what is the question; perhaps they asked about created missing rib from here and rib from here, with ribs without marrow stuck in vertebrae without marrow; if so, no vertebra was lacking — what gistera; and if not created in place of eleven ribs with marrow death from every side — how is gistera relevant; and if in the category of uprooted also created missing — nevertheless the language uprooted means actually uprooted and is gistera; and Rav was angry at the language uprooted that they said — it itself is difficult why they asked about uprooted which is gistera and did not ask about created missing whether there is treifah from the deficiencies and not from gistera which does not exist; rather created missing is kosher; if so, it is proven also created missing vertebra is kosher; otherwise why does the Gemara challenge Shmuel who said uprooted rib — treifah from that deficiency in the spine; deficiency deals with created missing; and with this uprooted rib is kosher; and accordingly also vertebra created missing is kosher — and it challenges well. And Tosafot Eruvin daf 7 challenged: what does Beit Hillel say on deficiency in the skull "so that it be taken from the living and die" — that is the measure of a sela, and Beit Shammai also hold the measure of a tefach is like a full drill, for Beit Shammai declare treifah with ten; if so, what does Beit Hillel say "so that it be taken from the living and die" — behold Beit Shammai also hold this, only they hold it does not die until a full drill. And what does R' Yosi say "and likewise for treifah" — behold it teaches "so that it be taken and die"; and Tosafot explain: it is accustomed to die through removal in a smaller deficiency than through decay or illness, end of his words — Maharsha and Maharam of Lublin: deficiency in the skull is through decay and illness; therefore Beit Shammai hold a sela, which is the measure for removal, does not suffice, since it is through illness — a full drill is needed; and Beit Hillel disagree and hold both measures are equal; and it says "so that it be taken" — specifically Beit Shammai concede a sela suffices; so in every deficiency, even through illness, a sela suffices as if removed, see there. Accordingly, that which teaches "how much deficiency in the skull" means it was lacking through illness as usual when removed through illness; and if deficiency "sovel" means created missing — if so, certainly there is no illness and pain at all, it is like removed by hand and a sela suffices; how can Beit Shammai say a full drill anonymously; or it does not deal at all with created missing, only deficiency afterward — and that is anonymously through illness. And in truth it is a great strain in Tosafot per what sparked there, that the reason for treifah of skull deficiency is that decay causes the membrane to perforate; if so, every natural being, as poskim wrote several times, decay and illness control the membrane more than when removed by hand — there is no illness or rot to control the lower membrane; how can Tosafot write the opposite of nature that here a greater measure is needed; Tosafot should have resolved simply: certainly if created missing in the skull, hands and illness did not control it — certainly not easily will membrane perforation be rejected unless much is lacking that air dominates the membrane; unlike removal where there is decay in the membrane and no great deficiency is needed, only a sela; if so, the usual deficiency deals with deficiency from birth, and Beit Shammai hold it does not die except like a full drill; but they concede in removal it is like a sela; and Beit Hillel disagree and say also in this its measure is like a sela "so that it be taken from the living" — specifically a sela suffices; and the same in deficiency; and this is "R' Yosi says and likewise for treifah" — also created missing, its measure is a sela. All Tosafot's wonder is resolved expansively; and accordingly Tosafot do not hold this law that created missing disqualifies in spine and skull, as Terumat HaDeshen wrote — requires further study. And after we clarified this as arises in our hands, we return to Terumat HaDeshen's words on excess in ribs and vertebrae that are twelve and fifteen. And for Terumat HaDeshen's view that in this created missing is permitted — even excess is kosher per his view. This is not according to what I wrote in siman 41 that all excess is considered as removed, and so Chazal estimated excess — as if removed by hand or illness, for it has no end of enduring and will be lost and taken — behold this is like removed by hand. Second, it is implied from Rashba's responsum, as Shach wrote: if there is an extra rib — what of it, like removed; meaning one rib does not declare treifah in one vertebra per his view — created missing is kosher, this too is kosher. And in truth I did not understand per their words: if here there are three rows it would be kosher, one remains alone. And such a mistake happened to the Rav Peri Chadash from excessive pilpul; they cried out on him in the beit midrash and he took his reward since he did not place a barrier on his mouth to speak very high against great men — one may call him "proud heart" who thought in three rows to permit; and this is not so, for all excess of that kind is like removed, even a thousand rows — all are like removed, for nature's taste cannot sustain them and all will be lost and their end perishes; if so, in excess rib or vertebra it is like all ribs and all vertebrae removed; if so, what helps "created missing is kosher" — absurd to permit when all ribs and all vertebrae are lacking; there we learned: split that has three backs and two spines — treifah and does not bear offspring; the reason: all excess is considered as removed; if so, how can we say it was not sixteen; and even two spines would he permit; what difference one rib and vertebra or many.
