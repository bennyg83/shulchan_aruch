(3) There. If most of the twenty-two large ribs broke from their midpoint and upward toward the spine, etc. — and if they broke not straight — meaning on one side in the width of the rib it is from midpoint and upward and on the second side from midpoint and downward — in this we follow the majority; and if there was no part of the upper side that is most of the rib, rather the majority is the part of the lower side — behold this is kosher, for all laws of rib breakage are judged by majority law; but if it was half and half — half the rib broke toward the upper side and half toward the lower side — one must be stringent. Peri Toar ot 3, Zivchei Torah ot 3, Anaf Yosef siman 36 ot 3, Zivchei Tzedek ot 3.
