Uprooted, etc. The halacha follows Rav in prohibitions:
(Likkut) From the large ribs. For that which is written "whether uprooted" means uprooted from the large ribs, since we include them with "broke"; and furthermore, otherwise what is the challenge, and the lion in most, etc.; if so, the same applies in this case, since uprooting a rib does not disqualify in the small ones — it is as if half a vertebra alone was uprooted — kosher.
