Laws of treifot in its ribs. In it are 5 seifim. The animal has 22 large ribs that have marrow — 11 from here and 11 from here — and each is stuck in its vertebra; and below them there are more small ribs that have no marrow. And after all the ribs end there are vertebrae in which no ribs are stuck. If most of the 22 large ribs break from their midpoint and upward toward the spine — whether six from this side and six from that, or eleven from this side and one from the other side — treifah. And if they were uprooted from the vertebra and the vertebra remains — it is as if they broke.
