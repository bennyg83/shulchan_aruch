His fellow heard, etc. — Rif wrote also in such a case it is not like taking an oath from his mouth, and even no issur per one opinion like regarding bread; but Ran wrote it seems to me in such a case he attaches, for he challenged there: since we derive attaching in vows, derive also for oaths, as with yados — even though written only regarding vows we say also in oaths; and for Abaye too we derive attaching of vows from oaths. And further he challenged: why did we not teach stringency of vows over oaths also in such a case? Only specifically regarding bread attaching does not help — vows are different, for in vows one attaches an object and can also attach another object; not so in oath, for the person is forbidden; therefore no derivation from there; not so in this case of this seif, even in oath it helps; therefore it did not teach stringency, etc.; and therefore also said it is not like taking an oath from his mouth; and no difficulty of "no" is nothing, because there is a type that helps — he said it is not like taking, etc., which helps for all — see there.
