If he swore to dwell in a place where he married a woman—even though thereby he nullifies honoring his father—if not for this he would not be swearing to nullify the mitzvah, since they would not give him a wife until he swore thus. {Rama: And all the more so if he married an elderly woman and swore to her that he will not marry another woman—even though he did not fulfill peru u-revu—the oath takes effect, for behold the general rule in this: matters upon which an oath takes effect regarding women, that he will not be able to fulfill the mitzvah of peru u-revu; and it is like one who swore according to his fellow's intent. (Beit Yosef simanim 228, 232, Rivash, and Rashbatz.) (And see above siman 228 for its law.)}
