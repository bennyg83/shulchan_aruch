All the more so, etc. The connection is difficult. It appears that Rama holds that an oath to reside in the place of one’s wife takes effect for two reasons. First, since they would not give him a wife unless he swore, he was compelled to swear in order to marry, and the mitzvah of honoring parents yields to that mitzvah, as explained below in siman 240. Second, since they would not give him the woman otherwise, his intent in swearing was for the sake of marriage and not to uproot the mitzvah of honoring parents, so the oath was inclusive. All the more so where he married an elderly woman, etc., for that is clearly inclusive. This is also implied by the Levush.
For he included within it, etc. This requires study in light of the responsum of the Or Zarua Katan cited by the Bach at the end of siman 228. It states that the oath does not take effect even if he has already fulfilled procreation. It also implies there that this is not considered an inclusive oath, since by swearing not to marry he swore to nullify the mitzvah to marry. Nevertheless, the responsum concludes that, for greater assurance, they should release him. See there. The responsum of Rabbi Aharon Sasson, siman 64, likewise cites only the responsum of the Rivash without qualification. See there.
With whom he cannot fulfill, etc. Such as an elderly woman, a minor, or an aylonis.
