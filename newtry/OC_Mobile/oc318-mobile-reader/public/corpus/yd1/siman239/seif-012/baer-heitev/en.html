Nezirut. Shach wrote: know that nezirut applies before the Temple and not before the Temple, both in the Land and abroad; so is proven in tractate Nazir; only Rambam wrote one who vows nowadays abroad; they compel him to ascend to Eretz Yisrael and observe nezirut there; and all the time he is abroad he is forbidden to drink wine, become impure to the dead, shave, and all details of nezirut upon him, even though these days do not count; end quote. And Raavad challenged him that they do not compel ascent to Eretz Yisrael, for tumah and taharah are not among us. And from what Mechaber wrote laws of nezirut here, it seems plain nezirut applies everywhere and always; nevertheless it seems even Rambam did not say they compel ascent except for standard nezirut due to impurity of lands of nations; but for a vow of Samson nezirut, since impurity is permitted and only wine and shaving forbidden; nezirut applies abroad. However there is a distinction: standard nezirut; asking helps like vows; but Samson nezirut; asking does not apply. And father and husband annul standard nezirut of daughter and wife; but in Samson nezirut Beit Yosef wrote in the name of R' Yehuda Bei Rav that the husband cannot annul; end quote.
