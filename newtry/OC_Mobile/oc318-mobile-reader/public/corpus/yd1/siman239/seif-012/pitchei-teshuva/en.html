Two nazirite terms. The laws of ordinary and Samsonite naziriteship are discussed extensively in Maharit, Mahari of the House of Levi, Mahari ben Chaviv, Radbaz, Rosh Yosef, and Chavot Yair. Pitchei Teshuva notes that the common pre-Rosh HaShanah annulment text mistakenly includes Samsonite naziriteship, which cannot be annulled. He also discusses the vow to become a nazir on the day Mashiach arrives.
