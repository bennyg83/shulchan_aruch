There is an authority who says, etc. Since Beis Yosef cited only Ran for this ruling, the Mechaber introduced it as “there is an authority who says.” In truth, Tosafos in the second chapter of Nedarim also proves it, and it is likewise implied by Rashi and Rosh, who explain “a vow within a vow” as requiring two periods of nezirus rather than additional lashes. Rashba also proves this in responsum 615.
