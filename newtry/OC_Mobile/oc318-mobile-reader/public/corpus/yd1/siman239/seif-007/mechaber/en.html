One who swore to perform chalitzah with his yevamah—this is not swearing to nullify the mitzvah, for the Merciful One cast it upon him: if he wishes he may perform yibbum, if he wishes he may perform chalitzah. {Rama: Some say that one who swore not to reveal to his fellow a matter that would save him from loss is swearing to nullify a mitzvah and it does not take effect. (Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh kelal 11.) Nevertheless, it appears to me that one should annul it first and afterward reveal to him, for it is no better than a matter that is rabbinic exposition. Public Torah reading is only rabbinic, and an oath or vow takes effect upon it. (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashbatz.)}
