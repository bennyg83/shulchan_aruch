Braita Nedarim 25, and explains there such as Rav Kahana and Rav Assi, etc.; and Ran explains there, and the same for urging oaths is permitted; and so in Yerushalmi, etc., similar to the mishnah. And the version on 24b in the braita: "false oaths are permitted"; and explains specifically regarding something exaggeration applies, such as "I saw like the Exodus," etc.; and so Rosh, for so it is in Tosefta; and so Raavad wrote in his hasagah there.
