Baraita, Nedarim 25a. The Gemara explains it as in the disagreement between Rav Kahana and Rav Asi, and the Ran explains that oaths of encouragement are likewise permitted. The Yerushalmi states similarly, like the Mishnah. The version in the baraita on 24b reads, 'Oaths of exaggeration are permitted,' specifically where exaggeration is applicable, such as, 'I saw a multitude like those who left Egypt.' The Rosh writes that this is the Tosefta's version, and the Raavad likewise writes this in his gloss. See also the end of this siman.
