Whether he expressed it in language of a vow, etc. Although some say above at the end of siman 206 that a vow expressed in language of an oath is ineffective, they agree that an oath expressed in language of a vow is effective, if it would take effect upon a mitzvah matter. So wrote the later authorities; see Bach.
Where he said, “sitting, etc.” The Bach wrote that in practice one should be stringent where one says, “Sitting in the sukkah is upon me,” according to the first explanation in the Asheri and the Semag; see there.
