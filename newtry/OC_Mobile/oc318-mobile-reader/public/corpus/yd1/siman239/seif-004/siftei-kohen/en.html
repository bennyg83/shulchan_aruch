Whether he expressed it in the language of a vow, etc. Although some authorities above, at the end of siman 206, hold that a vow expressed in the language of an oath is ineffective, they agree that an oath expressed in the language of a vow would be effective were it otherwise capable of taking effect against a mitzvah. So write the later authorities. See the Bach.
Who said, “Sitting, etc.” The Bach writes that in practice one should be stringent where a person says, “Sitting in the sukkah shall be forbidden to me,” in accordance with the first explanation in the Rosh and Semag. See there.
