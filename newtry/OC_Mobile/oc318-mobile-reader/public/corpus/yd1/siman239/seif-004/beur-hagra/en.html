Whether, etc. See Tosafot to Shevuot 25a, s.v. “What,” regarding Rabbi Elchanan, etc., and Rabbeinu Tam, etc. This depends on the dispute above at the end of siman 206. Tosafot to Rosh Hashanah 28a, s.v. “One forbidden by a vow,” also follows Rabbi Elchanan. This is likewise the Rosh’s conclusion in Shevuot there and the Ran’s view. The Ran writes that even according to the opinion that an oath expressed in the language of a vow takes effect, it does so only as an incomplete formulation, as stated at the end of siman 206, and is no stronger than that. See also the Ran at the beginning of Nedarim 2b, s.v. “Since.”
