An oath does not take effect on a matter of mitzvah—whether he uttered it in the language of oath, having said, "An oath that I will not sit in a sukkah;" whether he uttered it in the language of vow, having said, "Sitting in a sukkah is upon me by oath." {Rama: And this applies specifically when he swore about the mitzvah alone; but if he swore about it in a general formulation, it takes effect also on a matter of mitzvah, as explained above siman 236 and siman 238.}
