Negative commandment. Shach wrote: I wonder; above siman 238 seif 4 he ruled like Rambam that one who swears to eat less than an olive's worth of nevelah is liable for an oath even though half a measure is forbidden from the Torah, since one is not lashed for it from the Torah; and here he ruled that even for a rabbinic prohibition the oath does not take effect. And it is possible he means the oath does not take effect that he should transgress it, but he should release it; and it implies that even if he swore inclusively on permitted matters with forbidden matters, he should not transgress but release his oath.
