An oath takes effect on a matter that has no substance, such as if he swore that he will not sleep or that he will not speak. {Rama: Nidui has the law of an oath. (Beit Yosef end of siman 206 in the name of Yilmadenu.) And some say we rule stringently that it takes effect on everything on which an oath takes effect and on everything on which a vow takes effect. (There in the name of Rashbatz.)}
