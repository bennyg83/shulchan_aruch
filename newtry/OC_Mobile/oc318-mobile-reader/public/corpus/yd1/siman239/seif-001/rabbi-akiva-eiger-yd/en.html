(Siman 239 seif 2 in the gloss) And requires release. In Darkei Moshe wrote from Mordechai's language it implies cherem is considered an actual oath if given in oath language — not like Mahariv who wrote in siman 337: if not that I fear, I would say they were not stringent on cherem except when given to a person to do a matter for that person himself; but if Reuven gave cherem to Shimon to do a matter for Levi — not to be so stringent; and it is a great reasoning, since the reason is due to cutting the covenant — end quote. And see in Ketzot HaChoshen siman 287 s.k. 29 that cherem for past is not like an oath — see there; and if he said first "I give cherem" — one can say it is oath matter. And see responsum Shevut Yaakov part 3 siman 122; and see in responsum Shei LeMorah at end of the book in chiddushim to Tur Choshen Mishpat siman 129 seif 5, s.v. Katav Maharika; and see in Shach Choshen Mishpat siman 207 s.k. 27; and see in responsum Tashbatz part 1 (94) and part 2 siman 141).
