Forty-two. The Taz wrote that as a matter of halachah one should be stringent like the first view, which is the Tur's view, that it is forbidden for forty-two days; and generally unspecified vows are treated stringently. However, it appears obvious that if he explicitly states what his intent was, everyone agrees that we follow his explanation.
Two times. The Shach wrote that the master's words are not compelling, for he learned this from the law of a nazir, but it is not comparable: specifically by nezirut the Talmud derives it from the verse "nazir le-hazir," whereas by vows this does not apply. See there at length.
