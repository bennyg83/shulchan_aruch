That he is prohibited from meat for forty-two days. This is the view of the Tur. The Beis Yosef challenged him: what is different from one who says "I shall not eat these nine," etc. in siman 238 seif 12 — where if he ate one more he violated the oath? Therefore he concluded here as well: "This does not appear correct to me," etc. In my humble opinion, the Tur's words are well-founded. For in truth there are two possible interpretations of the second oath: it is possible that regarding those twenty days he already swore about, he swore again about them, now including them together with two additional days. And it is possible that he is speaking of entirely different days. Therefore we say that he intended both in all cases — that is to say, he will be prohibited for that many days total. Similarly above in every case — if he says "ten I shall not eat" — since we hold in siman 208 that unspecified vows are ruled stringently, therefore he is obligated above for ten, and here for forty-two days. However, it appears obvious that if he explicitly states what his intention was, there is no dispute — all agree we follow his explanation. Regarding the halachah, one should be stringent like the view of the Tur and he is prohibited for forty-two days.
