If he said, "An oath that I will not eat meat for twenty days," and returned and said, "An oath that I will not eat meat for twenty-two days"—there are those who say he is forbidden meat for forty-two days; but it does not appear to me so, but rather he is forbidden meat only for twenty-two days. {Rama: And this applies specifically when he adds a second vow upon a first vow. But if he said twice "twenty days," he certainly did not intend the first days—for if so, why did he vow twice?—and he is forbidden meat for forty days. (So is inferred from the sugya, chapter 2 of Nedarim.) And even when he adds to his vow, some say the first days do not count for him unless he vowed both vows on one day. (Opinion of Tosafot chapter Hareini Nazir and Mordechai end of Hilchot Aveilut.) But if he already began counting the first twenty days and afterward vowed twenty-two days, the first count does not count for him at all and he is forbidden meat for forty-two days; and one should be concerned for this. And see end of siman 217 more on this.}
