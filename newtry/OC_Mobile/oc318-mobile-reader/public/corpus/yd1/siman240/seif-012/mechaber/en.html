His father said to him, "Give me water to drink," and before him was a mitzvah to perform that passes — such as burial of the dead or a funeral — if the mitzvah can be done by others, he occupies himself with his father's honor (and if he already began the mitzvah first, he finishes it, for one occupied in a mitzvah is exempt from a mitzvah) (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran); and if there are no others to do it, he occupies himself with the mitzvah and leaves his father's honor. {Rama: However, if the time of the mitzvah will pass, he occupies himself with his father's honor and afterward performs the mitzvah. (Rabbeinu Yerucham netiv 1 in the name of Rosh)}
