And engages in honor of his father. See in responsum of Rabbeinu Akiva Eiger siman 68 — he was asked regarding a mother's will that is against the father's will, and the father's will is regarding a matter that involves karet — how should the son act; and the opinion of the sage who asked (he is the honor of the Gaavad of Pinsk) — and even if the son wishes to listen to his father and not be concerned for karet — he is not permitted; since in such a case there is no mitzvah to listen to his father (as above seif 5) — automatically he must listen to his mother; and he answered him: at first glance it appears that even if there were no karet in the father's will — one may say it is like a mother divorced from his father that both are equal and to whichever he wishes he may give precedence; likewise when his mother dies — for death separates them. However this matter is hidden from the words of the Beit Yosef below siman 376 regarding saying Kaddish for his mother during his father's lifetime — see there; and in any case in our case since there is karet — to whichever he wishes he should listen; but what the asking rabbi wrote that automatically he must listen to his mother and not his father — this is: since in essence upon him is the obligation of honoring father — only the Torah did not obligate him in karet; and nevertheless if he does so he is not called "not commanded and does" — for the mitzvah in essence he fulfills as required — only he is not concerned for karet — see there where he brought proof for his reasoning. And see there further regarding one who commands his sons by way of will to give from their inheritance money to so-and-so such and such — whether they need to fulfill the will from law of honor — see there what he wrote on this.
