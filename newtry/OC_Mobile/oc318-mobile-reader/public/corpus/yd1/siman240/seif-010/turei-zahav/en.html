And he commanded others to guard them. In the Tur he brought Ra’avad's challenge in this language: this is not instruction; for if he leaves them and goes; to whom will he command to guard them; end quote; and the Ran and Beit Yosef wondered; this is Talmud in the incident of Rav Assi with his mother whose mind was disturbed, etc.; and it appears the essence of his wonder is on commanding to guard them; for this is not in the Gemara; and on this Ra’avad wondered; what did the Rambam think to add commanding others to guard them; since in any case guarding does not help her; for if it helps why would he go and leave her; is it not possible for her with guarding; and if impossible to guard; what does commanding others help with guarding; and so I saw in the Derishah; and so is the essence.
