And some say, etc. In Beit Yosef he wrote that thus he inferred the language of the Rambam and Tur which is like the language of the Mechaber; and according to this he should not have said in language "and some say"; but the Rav goes according to his reasoning since he wrote in Darkei Moshe: and the language "the son is not obligated to beg door to door" does not imply so — for it implies that if he need not beg door to door he is obligated to sustain him even though certainly in such a case he is not so obligated to forgo and give as charity — difficult — end quote. And Bach: what he wrote in name of Semag and Semak that they are also stringent; however in explicit words of Rambam and Tur also the opinion of the Bach is as the Beit Yosef wrote; and likewise in the Perishah on this there is no contradiction to what he wrote "they compel him and sustain" — for court does not compel for honor of father; and if it is a positive commandment whose reward is beside it — rather they compel him from law of charity which also has a negative commandment as below section 248.
