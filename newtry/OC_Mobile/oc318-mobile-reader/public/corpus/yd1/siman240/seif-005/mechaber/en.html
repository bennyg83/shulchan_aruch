One who feeds him and gives him drink does so from the father's property if the father has means; and if the father has nothing and the son has — they compel him and he sustains his father according to what he is able; and if the son has nothing — he is not obligated to go door to door to feed his father: {Rama: And some say he is not obligated to give him except what he is obligated to give for charity. (So wrote Beit Yosef, for so it appears from the words of Rif and Rosh, and likewise Ran, beginning of Kiddushin.) Nevertheless, if his hand reaches abundance, a curse comes upon one who supports his father from charity funds. (Hagahot Mordechai Bava Batra and Hagahot beginning of Bava Metzia and Chiddushei Aggudah.) And if he has many sons, they calculate according to their wealth; and if some are wealthy and some poor, they obligate the wealthy alone. (Teshuvat Maimoniyot, Laws of Rebels, in the name of Maharam, and Beit Yosef brought it.)} But he is obligated to honor him with his person, even though through this he neglects his work and must go door to door; and specifically when the son has sustenance for that day — but if he does not have, he is not obligated to neglect his work and go door to door.
