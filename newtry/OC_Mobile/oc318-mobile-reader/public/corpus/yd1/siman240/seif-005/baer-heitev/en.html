The Taz wrote: although it is a positive commandment whose reward is beside it, as it is written regarding charity "so that He may bless you," etc. — Tosafot answered that charity is different, for it is also written therein a negative commandment "do not harden your heart"; and the Shach wrote: likewise he is not obligated to honor him from his own and he must go door to door (he wrote in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 70: if the son showed exemption from the father from now and forever — nevertheless the son is obligated to sustain his father even as Torah obligation — see there).
For charity. The Taz wrote that the words of the Rema are astonishing, for also the first opinion holds so — according to what he is able, meaning as charity according to his wealth — end quote (and in Nekudot HaKesef he wrote that the language of Darkei Moshe was omitted from him and he brought it in the Shach who wrote that the language "the son is not obligated to beg door to door" does not imply so; for it implies that if he need not beg door to door he is obligated to sustain him even though certainly in such a case he is not so obligated to forgo and give as charity — end quote).
