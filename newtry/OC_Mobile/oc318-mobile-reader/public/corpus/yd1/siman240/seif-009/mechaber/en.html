He is obligated to honor him even after death — how? If he would say a teaching in his mouth, he says, "Thus said my father, my master avi mari — may I be atonement for his resting-place" if within twelve months; and if after twelve months he says, "May his memory be for a blessing." {Rama: And there is no distinction whether for a father or mother in any case. (Maharil siman 27.) Some say that if he writes within twelve months something and mentions his father, he need not write "may I be atonement for his resting-place," but rather "may his memory be for a blessing," for his writing endures after twelve months. (Third [opinion] in Alfasi, beginning of Kiddushin.) And some are stringent even in writing. (Binyamin Ze'ev and other authors, and so appears in Beit Yosef end of siman 242 in the name of Rashbatz and Teshuvat Rabbi Chabib resud.) And so is the custom.}
