There is no helicopter. See what I have written in accordance with paragraph:
May his resting place be for a blessing. Rashi explained: upon him shall come every evil thing fit to come upon his soul; but after twelve months he already accepted what he accepted; that there is no judgment of the wicked in Gehinnom more than twelve months; end quote. And it is difficult for me from this on what the Rama wrote in siman 376; the custom is not to say Kaddish more than eleven months so as not to hold him as wicked; and why should he conclude here "may his resting place be for a blessing" all twelve months; and we must say that regarding the Kesef Mishneh it is different; since after twelve months one must say zikhrono livrakhah and so they say even on another person who died; it turns out in the twelfth month one cannot say zikhrono livrakhah lest he has not yet merited the World to Come and there is falsehood here; and if he does not say anything but "my father my teacher"; this is disrespect since they know he died; automatically impossible to be in sit and do not act; therefore better to mention him with the Kesef Mishneh formula which is not disrespect and also not falsehood for he says "if there is judgment upon him I accept it"; for even on a completely righteous person they say so and it is possible he did not see the face of Gehinnom; and this is the more correct way; unlike regarding Kaddish the more correct way is not to say anything and there is no contradiction at all since the son is in sit and do not act; for even if he does not yet merit nevertheless no falsehood comes from his mouth; so it seems correct to me.
Rather "zichrono livrachah." In the Gemara the text reads "zichrono livrachah l'chayei olam haba." So it is printed in some editions of the Tur. Those who sign z"l are omitting the lamed, and they should sign zatzal.
