(Collection) And some are stringent, etc. Greater than this we find the opposite regarding the wicked — in Midrash Rabbah of Esther on parashah "who was exiled from Jerusalem" and Semak; and Yerushalmi (chapter 3 of Megillah): when Rabbi Yonatan and the rabbis reached this verse "who was exiled" — they would say "may his bones be ground" — and why did they not say so in Yirmeyahu; but because every "may his bones be ground" in Yirmeyahu was while alive; here he was dead; and likewise here even mentioning after twelve months — what is written within twelve months — he said zikhrono livrakhah — only the Kesef Mishneh; and all the more at time of writing (end).
