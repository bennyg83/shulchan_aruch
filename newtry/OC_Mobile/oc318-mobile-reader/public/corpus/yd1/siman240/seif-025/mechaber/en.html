If the father wishes to be served by the son, it is permitted to receive from him; however a disciple who wishes to go to another place where he trusts he will see a sign of blessing in his studies before the rabbi who is there, and his father objects because he fears that in that city the gentiles make false accusations — he need not listen to his father in this: {Rama: And likewise if the father objects regarding whom the son should marry, whom the son desires — the son need not listen to the father. (Maharik shoresh 167.)}
