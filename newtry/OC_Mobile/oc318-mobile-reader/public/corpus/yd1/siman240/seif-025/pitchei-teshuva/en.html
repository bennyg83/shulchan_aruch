Because he trusts that he will see blessing. Chamudei Daniel writes that even uncertainty whether he will succeed may suffice. He also rules that one who wishes to pray in a synagogue where the prayer is more concentrated need not obey his mother if she objects.
He need not listen to his father. Chavot Yair discusses a mother's dying instruction never to rent out a house. Even though a righteous Torah scholar wished to rent it and study there for her merit, that benefit does not override the testament because it is not a personal mitzvah obligation upon the son. Since the instruction applied generally to all tenants, it also includes the scholar.
