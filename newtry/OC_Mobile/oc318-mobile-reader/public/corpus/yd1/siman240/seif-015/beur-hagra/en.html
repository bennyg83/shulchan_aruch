(Collection) His father said to him, etc. Even though they said "slaughter for me," "cook for me," etc.; and it implies at the beginning of Yevamot — specifically a negative commandment that has karet. He relied on Midrash Rabbah who said parashat Naso chapter 14: I might think even if he says to transgress, etc.; and similarly "a man's mother," etc. — it implies like the example of the king; and all the more he — for a king is more stringent than his father and mother, as written in chapter 1 of Kiddushin (32); and one who transgresses the king's command is liable death; and with his father he did alone; and we say in chapter 6 of Sanhedrin "every man," etc. — I might think even for words of Torah, etc. (and Tosafot Yevamot 6 side 1 s.v. concluded and s.v. but and s.v. since) (end). (Collection) He said to him, etc. In Tanna deVei Eliyahu chapter 27: I might think even if his father said to him transgress one of all the commandments stated in the Torah, etc. — say regarding all he shall listen to him except for offense and robbery and ugly matter; therefore it is said "the priests despised," etc. (end).
