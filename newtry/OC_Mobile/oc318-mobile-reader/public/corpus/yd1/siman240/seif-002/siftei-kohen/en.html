He should not stand, etc. It is permitted, however, to stand in the place where his father ordinarily sits in his own home, since this involves no disrespect at all. Beis Yosef.
Nor contradict his words. Whether he directly disputes his father, or tells one who disputes him, “Your words appear correct,” he has contradicted his father. It appears that contradicting his father is forbidden even outside his presence. This is also implied by Derishah, seif 4, and is stated explicitly in Perishah below, siman 242, seif katan 20.
Outside his presence, etc. Derishah writes that regarding other people, one may disagree even in their presence if the person is not his primary teacher, and regarding his primary teacher one may disagree outside his presence. See there at length. Bach writes that in the presence of one’s teacher there is no formal prohibition, although one must be careful out of respect. See below, siman 242, seif 15.
