And he should not contradict his words. Atzmot Yosef writes that a son may challenge, answer, and analyze a halakhic matter together with his father until they reach the correct conclusion; this is not considered contradicting him.
And he should not call him by name. If others ask whose son he is, he may answer, 'I am the son of Rabbi so-and-so,' because identification requires mentioning the name. Maharshal similarly explains why Rosh named Maharam when distinguishing him from other teachers. Tur mentioned his father honorifically as 'Rosh,' meaning foremost of Israel.
They alter their names. See Rashi to Gittin 34b regarding Abbaye.
