Special. He wants to stand or sit alone without any person on the path of advice as his father is not allowed to make an App, nor does he want to eat but standing and sitting at the side are forbidden:
Contradicting. Whether he disagrees with him or says to the one who disagrees with him "your words appear correct" — this is contradicting his words; and it appears that contradicting his words is forbidden even not in his presence — end quote, Shach.
Not in his presence. And the Derishah wrote that regarding others — even in his presence if he is not extraordinary it is permitted; and likewise regarding an extraordinary one not in his presence. And the Bach wrote that not in the presence of one's rabbi there is no prohibition; however one should be careful for the sake of honor; and see above siman 242 seif 15. And the Taz wrote: this that we find in Tur Choshen Mishpat that the Tur disagreed with his father the Rosh several times — this was not in his presence; and nevertheless it seems to me that if he disagrees with his father and there is another who says like his father and he wants to write his opinion like the opinion of the one who disagrees with his father — he should not mention his father's name but the name of the other, since he can do so respectfully he should do so — end quote.
