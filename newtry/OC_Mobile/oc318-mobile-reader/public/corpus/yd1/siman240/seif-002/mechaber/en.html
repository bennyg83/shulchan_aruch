What is reverence? He may not stand in his designated place to stand — such as the place designated for him to stand there in the council of elders with his colleagues — or his designated place for prayer; nor may he sit in his designated place for reclining in his home; nor contradict his words or decide in his favor in his presence — even to say, "Father's words appear correct"; nor call him by his name — neither in his lifetime nor after his death — but rather says, "My father, my master [avi mari]." If his father's name was the same as other people's names, he changes the name if it is a name that is remarkable such that people are not accustomed to call by it. {Rama: But a name that people are accustomed to use — it is permitted to call others by it when not in his presence. (Tur)}
