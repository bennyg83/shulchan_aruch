What is honor? Feeding him and giving him drink, clothing him and covering him, bringing him in and taking him out — and he must attend to him with a cheerful countenance, for even if he feeds him fattened birds every day, yet shows him a scowling face, he is punished for it: {Rama: And likewise the reverse — if he sets his father to grind at the mill and his intention is for the good, so that his father be spared something harder than this, and he speaks words of appeasement to his father's heart and shows him that his intention is for the good until his father agrees to grind at the mill — he inherits the World to Come. (Gemara and Rashi beginning of Kiddushin and Yerushalmi.) And he may serve him in other matters in which a servant serves his master.}
