(Ibid., Taz, seif katan 20) Thus Rashi explained, etc. It is also a Midrash. His further statement, 'This is surprising according to the first opinion,' is the question of the Rama in Darkei Moshe, which is noted in the source reference in Shulchan Aruch. I do not know what he added here. Perhaps he understood that Rashi's commentary on the Torah is more commonly studied than Midrash, because some authorities wrote that Rashi serves in place of Targum for the obligation to read the weekly portion twice in Scripture and once in translation.
