How far does honoring father and mother extend? Even if they took his purse of gold coins and threw it before him into the sea — he may not humiliate them, nor cause them distress in their presence, nor grow angry against them, but accepts the decree of Scripture and is silent: {Rama: And some say that if one wishes to throw the son's money into the sea, he may prevent him, for he is not obligated to honor him except from the father's property but not from the son's. (Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Yehudah.) And there is no distinction between honoring and causing distress. (Terumat HaDeshen siman 40.) And specifically before they threw them, when it is possible he will refrain and not do it; but if they already threw them, it is forbidden to humiliate him, but he may claim him in court. (Tur in the name of Rosh.) And specifically if he wishes to throw his purse into the sea, for there is monetary loss; but if he wishes to transfer from him mere empty space, it is forbidden in every case. (Ran beginning of Kiddushin.) A son who has litigation with his father and the father is the plaintiff — the son must follow the father even though the son is the defendant and lives in another city, for this is his father's honor; but the father must pay the son's expenses, for he is not obligated to honor him from the son's property. (Maharik shoresh 58) as explained below.}
