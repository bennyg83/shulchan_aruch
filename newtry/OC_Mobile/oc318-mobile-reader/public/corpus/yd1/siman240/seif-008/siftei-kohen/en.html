For he is not obligated, etc. The Bach wrote that the first reasoning also holds that one should not distinguish between honoring him and distressing him. Rather, its reason is that since the son must afterward pay all his loss, he should not shame him because of this. The “some say” holds that in order not to be troubled to stand in litigation and judgment, he may prevent him; see there.
