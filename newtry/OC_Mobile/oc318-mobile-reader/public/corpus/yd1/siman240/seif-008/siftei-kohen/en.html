For he is not obligated, etc. Bach writes that the first opinion also sees no essential distinction between honoring a father and preventing him from suffering. Its reasoning is that since the son will afterward have to reimburse the entire loss, he should not shame his father in order to prevent it. The second opinion holds that the son may prevent the damage so that he will not need to undergo the burden of litigation. See there.
