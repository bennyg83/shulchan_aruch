Divorced from his father — both equal. See in responsum Noda BiYehudah tinyana part Even HaEzer end siman 45 who wrote that the matter of choice in the son's hand to listen to whomever he wishes — specifically regarding divorcee; but regarding a widow — if his father commanded him something to do after his death and his mother the widow commands him the opposite — he should set aside honor of father and fulfill honor of mother; for always honor of the living takes precedence over honor of the dead; and even though we say honor him in his life and honor him in his death — nevertheless the essence of honor is in life; and if so he sets aside honor of his dead father and engages in honor of his mother who is alive — see there; and see in responsum of Rabbeinu Akiva Eiger siman 68 who wrote that this matter is hidden from the words of the Beit Yosef below siman 376 regarding saying Kaddish for his mother during his father's lifetime — that if his father is particular, honor of father takes precedence over honor of mother — see there; and he did not say because honor of the living takes precedence over honor after death — and there would be a ramification in law even in the reverse: if his father died and commanded him to say Kaddish after him and his mother is particular — he should listen to his mother — see there.
