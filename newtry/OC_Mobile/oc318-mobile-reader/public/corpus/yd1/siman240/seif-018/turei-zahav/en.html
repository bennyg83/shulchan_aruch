Even if his father was wicked. We learn from what is stated in chapter HaNechnakim; for all, the son does not become an agent to flog his father except for a mesit; and some say; this is the Tur's difficulty on this from the matter in chapter Eizehu Neshech: he left them a stolen cow; explanation of interest (and also in Rashi there he calls interest theft) and all marked things they are obligated to return because of honor of their father; and we challenge there "and who is obligated because of honor of his father"; but it is written "and a leader among your people you shall not curse" and this one is not doing action with your people; and they answer when he repented and did not manage to return before he died; learn that without repentance he is not obligated in his honor. And Beit Yosef answered: when he repented he considered in his mind to return the cow; it turns out it was as if already given and the son inherits nothing and the son cannot say "it is mine and I am not obligated to honor him from my own"; unlike without repentance the money was the son's by inheritance; and he elaborated on this. And it is difficult; for if so why did the challengers need to challenge from "not doing action with your people"; let it emerge that the money is the son's since he did not repent; and furthermore if the matter depends on what he considered to return; why did they distinguish specifically marked things; for even in unmarked things he is obligated since he considered to return; end quote; the Tur properly challenged and the matter of HaNechnakim the Tur establishes when he repented as written in Tosafos that the Beit Yosef brought. And one may say the Tur distinguishes between striking and cursing versus honor; namely even though to strike him and curse him is forbidden; nevertheless he is not obligated to honor him; and one may say this that we say "not doing action with your people" is not in the category of "you shall not curse"; learn that it is permitted to curse him; namely to exempt if he did; but nevertheless there is prohibition in the matter; and one may say why does the challenger challenge from "doing action with your people"; for in any case there is prohibition in the matter; one may say since for cursing him there is only prohibition; automatically for honoring him there is no obligation at all. Also the Ran chapter 1 of Kiddushin for one answer distinguishes between causing him pain and honoring him; and not as the Rama wrote seif 8 that there is no distinction between causing him pain or honoring him; also from four Tosafos chapter HaKotev in chapter 6 s.v. periyot they distinguish between honor and father's disgrace.
