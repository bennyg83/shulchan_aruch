In honor of his older brother. See in responsum Knesset Yechezkel siman 25 section 36 side 3 who brought that there is a dispute between the Rambam and Ramban in Sefer HaShorashim whether one is obligated in honor of his older brother even after death of his father and mother; another reason for the honor is only because of honor of his father and mother like father's wife — and he is not obligated to honor him except as long as his father and mother exist — see there; and see in Darkei Moshe s.k. 7.
