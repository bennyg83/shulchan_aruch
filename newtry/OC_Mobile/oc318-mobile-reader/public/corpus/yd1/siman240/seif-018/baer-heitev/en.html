To honor. The Shach wrote: however even though he is not obligated to honor him, it is forbidden to cause him pain, as below siman 241 seif 4 and 5 (and he wrote in Shulchan Atzei Shittim the incident where he was sleeping under his father's head and did not wake him — specifically when the father is not pained by not being awakened; but if the father is very happy if awakened — mitzvah to awaken him and they awaken him to go to synagogue or for a mitzvah matter — see there siman 337).
