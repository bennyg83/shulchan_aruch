What is honor and what is reverence, and their laws. And it contains 25 seifim. One must be very careful with the honor of one's father and mother and with reverence for them: {Rama: Nevertheless, beit din does not coerce regarding the mitzvah of honoring father and mother, for it is a positive mitzvah whose reward is given alongside it, concerning which beit din does not coerce. (Beit Yosef in the name of the Gemara, and in Teshuvot Avraham netiv 1)}
