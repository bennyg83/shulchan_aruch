To stand before his father. One should be uncertain whether a blind person is obligated to stand before his father and mother and rabbi when he knows they are before him or not. And in Netzach Yisrael I elaborated on this and proved from the Gemara that there is a distinction between a sage and his father and mother and established rabbi: regarding a sage and rabbi who is not established — a blind person is exempt — unlike Ba'er Heitev of Maharit below siman 244 s.k. 1 in name of Beit Yosef; but regarding his father and mother and established rabbi — even a blind person is obligated.
