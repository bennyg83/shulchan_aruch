And he knows that they will fulfill, etc. If he is uncertain, he may act as he chooses. Perishah.
In order to attribute the honor to his father. If he knows that they will not fulfill his request for his father’s sake, but would fulfill it for his own sake, he should not make the request in his father’s name, for that would only disgrace his father when they refuse to act for him. This is the wording of the Levush, based on the Tur and Rashi. Beis Yosef cites Rabbeinu Yerucham in the name of Ramah, however, that even where no honor at all is accorded to his father in that place, he should attribute the request to his father, since they will act for the son even though he attached the request to his father. Darkei Moshe cites this view.
