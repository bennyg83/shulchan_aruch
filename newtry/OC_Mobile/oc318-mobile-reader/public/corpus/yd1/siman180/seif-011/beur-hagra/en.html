One who makes a bald spot, etc. The Tosefta there states: One who rounds the corner of another person's head, both are liable. When does this apply? When both acted intentionally. If both acted unwittingly, they are exempt. If one acted unwittingly and the other intentionally, the unwitting party is exempt and the intentional party is liable. One who destroys the corner of another person's beard, both are liable. When does this apply? When both acted intentionally, etc.; the unwitting party is exempt and the intentional party is liable. One who makes a bald spot on another person's head, both are liable. When does this apply, etc.; the intentional party is liable. One who makes a cut in another person's flesh, both are liable. When does this apply, etc.? All of these cases refer to one who assists, as stated in the Gemara regarding one who rounds the head, and as stated there concerning one who eats, etc.
