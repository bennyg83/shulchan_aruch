Women are also, etc. This does not follow Isi ben Yehudah in Kiddushin 35b, but rather the Mishnah. So too, the Yerushalmi states that Rabbi Hamnuna instructed his colleagues: Warn your wives that when they stand over the dead they should not tear out their hair, lest they come to violate the prohibition against making a bald spot, as it is stated, 'For you are a holy people.' This applies equally to men and women. The Rif cites this there, as do the Rosh there and at the end of Moed Katan. This is the meaning of the Rama's words, 'And there are, etc.'
And certainly, etc. For even Isi ben Yehudah concedes this, as stated there: Or perhaps it means only, etc. This is certainly so according to the Mishnah.
