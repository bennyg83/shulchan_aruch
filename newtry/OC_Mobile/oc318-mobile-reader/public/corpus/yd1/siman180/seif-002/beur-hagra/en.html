If he performs it, etc. This follows the Tosefta in chapter 3: One who writes a tattoo inscription in another person's flesh, both are liable. When does this apply? When both acted intentionally. If both acted unwittingly, they are exempt. If one acted unwittingly and the other intentionally, the unwitting party is exempt and the intentional party is liable. This necessarily refers to one who assists, as stated there in the Gemara, 20b, regarding one who eats dates, etc. See what I wrote in chapter 11.
