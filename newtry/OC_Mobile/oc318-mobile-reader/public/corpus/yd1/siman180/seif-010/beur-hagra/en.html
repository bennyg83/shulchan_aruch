From this the Rosh inferred that in the first clause he is liable only once, since the baraisa teaches multiple liability only in such a case. For this reason, the Mishnah did not group together one who makes a cut and one who makes a bald spot for five deceased persons, etc. However, the Tosefta there teaches that making a bald spot and making a cut are governed alike. The Tosefta states: One who makes a bald spot for five deceased persons is liable for each deceased person. Rabbi Yose says he is liable only once. One who makes five bald spots for one deceased person is liable for each bald spot, and he is not liable unless he makes the bald spot because of a deceased person. How much must he remove, etc.? One who makes a cut for five deceased persons is liable for each deceased person. Rabbi Yose says he is liable only once. One who makes five cuts for one deceased person is liable for each cut, and he is not liable unless he makes the cut because of a deceased person. How much must he cut to be liable, etc.? Yet in the Gemara, Rabbi Yose's rulings are stated in the reverse manner.
