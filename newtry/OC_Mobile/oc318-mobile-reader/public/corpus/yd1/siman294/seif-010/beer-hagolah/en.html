Following Rabbi Eliezer in the final Mishnah of chapter 4 of Maasrot. The correct text is as emended, for so it appears in the Mishnah and is proven from the Tur and Beit Yosef. The berries are the fruit, the young shoots grow among the leaves and protrude like palm shoots, and the caper husks are the large peel surrounding the fruit, like the husk surrounding walnuts.
