(Likut) Vineyard, etc. And even picking by hand is permitted, and so in all places; the reason is specifically when there are orlah plantings in it — certainly presumption of prohibition applies, unlike such a case, as written (Keritot 18 side a) in asham talui one is liable only for a piece of two pieces; and this is taken from Tosefta at the end of Orlah: if a small planting was found in his field — its doubt in Eretz Yisrael is forbidden, in Syria and abroad permitted.
