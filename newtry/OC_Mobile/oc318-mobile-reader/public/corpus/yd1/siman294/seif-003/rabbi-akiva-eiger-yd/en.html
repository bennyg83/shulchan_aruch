(Seif 8, gloss) And it is forbidden to gather it for a non-Jew. Rabbeinu Yonah writes similarly in Shaarei Teshuvah, entry 83. It appears to me that Tosafot, Pesachim 22b, s.v. VeEver Min HaChai, holds the same. See Rashba's novellae at the end of the second chapter of Kiddushin, s.v. BeEfer Parah. This requires study.
