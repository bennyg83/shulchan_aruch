And caprifigs. The Pri Megadim wrote caprifigs in Eretz Yisrael are also exempt — not clear; and so is proven explicitly in the Gemara chapter Keitzad Mevarchin (daf 36) — one who obligates on dates all the more on caprifigs, which are more fruit than dates; rather the opposite is reasonable — he did not need to state at the outset that they are obligated, for all the more from dates; and the explanation of aviyonot, dates, and caprifigs — see Rashi and Rabbeinu Yehudah, whom Beit Yosef brought; and I again found in Sefer Ma'aseh Merkavah chapter Keitzad Mevarchin — he challenged the Pri Megadim on this.
