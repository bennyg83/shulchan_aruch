And caper husks. The Levush wrote that caper husks are exempt even in Eretz Yisrael, but this is not convincing. The Gemara in Berakhot 36 explicitly indicates that one who obligates palm shoots certainly obligates caper husks, since they are more fruit-like than palm shoots. Rather, the reverse is reasonable: the earlier clause did not need to say that they are obligated, because this follows all the more so from palm shoots. For the meanings of caper berries, palm shoots, and caper husks, see Rashi and Rabbeinu Yonah as cited by the Beit Yosef. I later found that the Ma'adanei Yom Tov likewise challenged the Levush on this point.
