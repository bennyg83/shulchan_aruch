The caper berries, etc. ; Tur, and as written in Berakhot 36a; R' Yosef: caper, etc.; but Rambam ruled that even in Eretz Yisrael it applies only to abyonot, and his words appear correct, for we hold like R' Akiva even in Torah law against R' Eliezer; only from R' Yosi: caper abroad, etc., implies in Eretz Yisrael we hold here like R' Eliezer, as written in the Gemara; and this is Tur's opinion; but Rambam holds like Mar bar Rav Ashi there, and we say R' Akiva in the place of R' Eliezer; we act like him not because it is lenient but because halacha follows him; and with this Tosafot's difficulty there s.v. vehalachta is resolved, and see Orach Chaim siman 202 seif 6.
