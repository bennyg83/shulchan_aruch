The berries, caper husks, and shoots of the caper bush, etc. This is the correct text. The berries are the fruit itself. The caper husks are the large shells surrounding the fruit, like the husk that grows around small nuts. The shoots grow among the leaves in a date-like form and protrude from the leaf, as on willow leaves. Rashi and Maharshal.
But outside Eretz Yisrael, etc. Because of the stringency of Eretz Yisrael, all of these are regarded as fruit there. Outside Eretz Yisrael, where orlah is treated more leniently, only the berries, which are the principal fruit, are regarded as fruit. Gemara.
