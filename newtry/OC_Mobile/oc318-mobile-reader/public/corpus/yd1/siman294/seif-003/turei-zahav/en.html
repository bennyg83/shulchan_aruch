The avyonot and the kaperisin and the tamarot of the caper-bush. As it should read: the avyonot are the fruit itself; the kaperisin is the large husk surrounding the fruit like the peel that grows around small nuts; and the tamarot grow within the leaves like a kind of date and protrude in the leaf like willow leaves. Rashi and Maharashal.
But outside the land, etc. Due to the stringency of the land of Israel we consider everything as the fruit itself; but outside the land, since orlah is rabbinic, we do not consider everything as fruit — only the avyonot which are the primary fruit. Gemara.
