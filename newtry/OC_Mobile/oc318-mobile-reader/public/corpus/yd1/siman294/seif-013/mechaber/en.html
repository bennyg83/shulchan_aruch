A non-Jew and an Israelite who were partners in a planting—if they did not stipulate at the beginning, it is forbidden. And if they stipulated at the beginning of the partnership that the non-Jew would eat the two years of orlah and the Israelite would eat the three permitted years corresponding to the two years of orlah—this is permitted, provided they do not come to an accounting, such as calculating how much fruit the non-Jew ate in the two years of orlah until the Israelite eats corresponding to those fruits—for this is forbidden because it is like exchanging orlah fruits. {Rama: And there are those who permit even if they did not stipulate at the beginning, only that the Israelite says to the non-Jew: take you the two years of orlah and I will take two permitted years corresponding to them (Tur and Rosh).}
