They stipulated. Bach permits later accounting unless they initially stipulated that the years would offset one another. Taz disagrees: once permitted fruit is exchanged against orlah fruit, it resembles substitution and is forbidden. He derives the same rule from Rambam concerning Shabbat and reads the Mechaber's phrase 'provided they do not make an accounting' as applying in every case.
