Can live. See responsum Perach Mateh Aharon part 2 siman 75 who raised that we require it can live three years like years of orlah — see there. [And see Levushei Sharad siman 105 — one asked someone who planted for him an orchard whether permitted to bring from elsewhere an old tree with the clod of earth around it so it can live from the clod and plant in his orchard to exempt from orlah — he ruled forbidden, for mishnah and Shulchan Aruch only said b'dieved etc.; also for us forbidden l'chatchila as we do not know to what extent; especially per Perach Mateh Aharon we require three years and certainly impossible now to ascertain. Nevertheless by the law explained in Shulchan Aruch — a tree uprooted by wind or river with dirt around roots — one should not protest at the hand of one who permits, provided he does so by expert in nature of trees and does the examination in Tosafeta brought in Beit Yosef to dig a pit and place the tree inside — see there.] Indeed in responsum Shivat Tzion siman 49 he elaborated and raised that only in Eretz Yisrael we require three years, but abroad if it can live a few days suffices. And he wrote more: Tur and Shulchan Aruch estimate if it can live etc. — implies need expert estimate; in Eretz Yisrael where its doubt is forbidden they wrote thus; but abroad where its doubt is permitted, even if doubt leans to prohibition and we establish prohibition — permitted abroad as I explained above, all the more in this doubt which is balanced; and even in doubt of lacking knowledge, where generally we do not treat as doubt — it appears permitted abroad — see there he elaborated greatly.
