It can survive. Perach Mateh Aharon requires that the transplanted tree be able to survive from its surrounding soil for three full orlah years. Levushei Serad therefore forbids intentionally transplanting an old tree with a soil clod to evade orlah, especially because reliable assessment is difficult. Shivat Tzion distinguishes outside Eretz Yisrael, where survival for a short period may suffice because even substantial doubt is permitted.
