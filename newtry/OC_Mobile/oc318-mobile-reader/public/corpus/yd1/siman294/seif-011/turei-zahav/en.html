And he hides it inside. He fears that the grape pressers will find it. Rashi. Tosafos asks why the wine is not prohibited as non-Jewish wine, since most people are non-Jews, and answers that most thieves at that time were Jews. See siman 129, where this depends upon most surrounding vineyards being Jewish-owned.
But grapes, etc. Grapes may be hidden there outside the harvest season, when wine pressers are not present. Although the Gemara initially suggests that the ruling depends on following the majority rather than proximity, it ultimately gives the stronger factual reason that wine is not ordinarily hidden there, whereas grapes are. This strong local presumption can outweigh the ordinary rule favoring the majority, just as we say, “Here they were found, and here they were.” Therefore the Tur and Shulchan Aruch correctly rule that the matter is strongly presumed to have come from the nearby vineyard.
