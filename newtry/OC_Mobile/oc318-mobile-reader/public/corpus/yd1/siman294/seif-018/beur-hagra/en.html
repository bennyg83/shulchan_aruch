A tree, etc. Yerushalmi: R' Yudan bar Tarfon said, etc.; and Rambam ruled like the Sages, for halacha follows the Sages and moreover we establish them in one line of reasoning; but Rosh ruled like Rabbi Yehuda, because R' Shimon holds like him, and in our opinion as above. However it appears that that which is in Sotah 43b is decisive, as written in siman 221, and they have one reason.
