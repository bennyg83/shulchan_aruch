A layering that was disconnected while full of fruit; and likewise an old tree that was uprooted with fruit on it and replanted elsewhere; or one took a branch full of fruit from an old tree and grafted it onto a young tree—if the fruit increased so that the original permitted fruit is not one part in one hundred of the addition, it is forbidden; if not, it is permitted. But if he grafted a branch full of orlah fruit into an old tree, the fruits of the branch are forbidden forever, even if they grew greatly.
