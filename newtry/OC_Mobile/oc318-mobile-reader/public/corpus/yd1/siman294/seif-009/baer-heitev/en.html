Permitted. Although a doubt concerning a halakhah transmitted to Moshe at Sinai is normally treated like a Torah doubt, the tradition concerning orlah outside Eretz Yisrael was expressly given with the rule that its doubt is permitted. Shach.
Orlah. And permitted plantings as well.
All the more so. Shach explains that Rama rejects the implication that one may actively take from a doubtful vineyard. He follows Tur that one may not take by hand, though one may benefit even while seeing the non-Jew harvest from it.
