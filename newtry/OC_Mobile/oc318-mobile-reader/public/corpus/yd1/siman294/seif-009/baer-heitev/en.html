All the more so. The Shach writes that it requires study why the Rama wrote this law, since it is explained in the Mechaber's words in seif 10. It appears that because the Mechaber copied the language of the Rambam, and his wording implies that in a vineyard that is doubtful orlah one may take from it directly, for it is like a permitted vineyard, the Rav does not hold so. Rather, he follows the view of the Tur and those who assist him, that one may not take from it directly; only this is permitted even if he sees the non-Jew harvesting from it. Therefore he needed to write, "all the more so," etc.
