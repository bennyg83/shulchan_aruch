A doubt of orlah in Eretz Yisrael is forbidden; in the Diaspora it is permitted. How so? A vineyard that has plantings of orlah and grapes are sold outside it—in Eretz Yisrael it is forbidden; in Syria it is permitted when he did not know they were brought from that vineyard. And in the Diaspora it is permitted even if he knows they were brought from that vineyard, provided it does not appear they were harvested from orlah plantings. {Rama: And all the more so a vineyard that is a doubt of orlah is permitted (so it appears from the Tur, Rosh, and Mordechai on the first chapter of Kiddushin).}
