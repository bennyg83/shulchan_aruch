Doubtful orlah in Eretz Yisrael. See Radbaz, siman 580.
Outside Eretz Yisrael it is permitted. Chatam Sofer discusses an orchard leased for twelve years in which new trees were planted annually and more than one hundred trees were reportedly within their first three years. Fully developed ordinary fruit need not be suspected, especially because doubtful orlah outside Eretz Yisrael is permitted. Fruit that is unusually thin and underdeveloped may present a strong recurring doubt and should not be expressly permitted, though the owners need not search for it. Proceeds received after the gardeners sold the fruit need not be treated as prohibited exchanges. He does not issue this as a practical ruling.
All the more so a vineyard. See Shach, Pe'er Halakhah, and Shivat Tzion.
