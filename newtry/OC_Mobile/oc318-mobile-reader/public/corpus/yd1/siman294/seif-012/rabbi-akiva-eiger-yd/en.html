(Seif 24) Or an etrog, it is obligated. This refers to the fruit of the palm tree. Maadanei Yom Tov challenges this from the Yerushalmi, which discusses only one who plants for the mitzvah of etrog, meaning that he plants for the fruit and not for the wood. If he plants for the mitzvah of lulav, meaning for the wood, even the fruit is not subject to orlah. He rules accordingly.
