And even if he said, etc. ; In the Tosefta chapter 5 of Demai: a non-Jew who was calling out and saying “Come, take fruits of Azekah; they are revai”; he is not believed because he is merely enhancing his sale; but if he said “from so-and-so, a non-Jew, I took them; from so-and-so, a Cuthite, I took them; from my own I brought them”; he is believed to be strict. These are the words of Rabbi [Yehudah]; Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says he is not believed, for the words of a non-Jew neither add nor detract. The Rosh ruled in chapter 3 of Mo’ed Katan like Rabbi because the halakha follows Rabbi over his colleague. And Rav Yerucham’s reason: because in chapter 5 of Gittin page 65 it is written that the halakha follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel regarding Rabbi. See what I wrote in Even HaEzer siman 17 seif 37; that all the poskim ruled like the first tanna that he is believed to be strict; they only dispute whether it is specifically what is under his hand or everywhere he is believed; but in this they do not dispute; see what I wrote there.
