He is not believed, etc. See above, siman 127, seif katan 20. Above, siman 98, seif katan 26, it was explained that orlah mixed with other produce is nullified only in a ratio of two hundred to one when mixed with its own kind. Even in a dry mixture, ordinary one-in-two nullification does not apply until that ratio is reached, as explained in siman 109, seif katan 6. When mixed with a different kind, however, it is always nullified in sixty like other prohibitions.
