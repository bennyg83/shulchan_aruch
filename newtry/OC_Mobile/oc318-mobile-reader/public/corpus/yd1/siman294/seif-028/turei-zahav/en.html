A non-Jew who was selling fruits, etc. — In the Gemara at the end of Yevamot we say this, and the reason is that to enhance his sale he says so. See what I wrote in siman 122 seif 6 regarding where enhancing the sale does not apply. In the Tur on this siman there is a scribal error; it should read: until the first fruit — which is permitted — does not constitute two hundred times the addition; the addition is forbidden, etc. — end quote.
