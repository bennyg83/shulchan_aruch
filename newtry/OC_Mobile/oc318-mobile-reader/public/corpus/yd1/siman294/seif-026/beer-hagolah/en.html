From Rambam's words in chapter 10 of Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot from mishnah end of tractate Orlah; and as Kesef Mishneh wrote and in Beit Yosef. Because it is proven they are garden fruits and there is only one doubt — Ran. Meaning: even to gather by hand; and as Kesef Mishneh and Beit Yosef wrote per Rambam in the next seif.
