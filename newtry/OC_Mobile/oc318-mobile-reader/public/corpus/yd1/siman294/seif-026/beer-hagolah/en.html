From Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilchot Maachalot Assurot, based upon the Mishnah at the end of tractate Orlah, as Kesef Mishneh and Beit Yosef explain. Since it is evident that the fruits came from the garden, there is only one uncertainty. The Ran. Meaning, even to gather them by hand, as Kesef Mishneh and Beit Yosef explain according to Rambam in the following seif.
