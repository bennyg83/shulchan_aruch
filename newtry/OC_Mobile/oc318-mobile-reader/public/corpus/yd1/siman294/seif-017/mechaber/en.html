Even though it is the custom of farmers to layer vines every year, it is permitted to drink wine from non-Jewish vineyards because a doubt of orlah is permitted. And even in vineyards that Israelites work and layer every year it is permitted, because one who layers and grafts is liable for orlah only in Eretz Yisrael but not in the Diaspora. And one can find permission also for children of Eretz Yisrael in wine of non-Jewish vineyards, because the majority of vines are not orlah. And even in vineyards known with certainty to contain orlah, one may permit them because before two years of orlah pass they do not produce fruit; and if they produce, they are thin and weak and unfit to make wine from.
