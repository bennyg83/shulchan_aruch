One who plants, etc. ; Tosefta beginning of Orlah: one who plants for a fence, for beams, and for wood; exempt from orlah; Rashbag: when he planted for fence, beams, and wood something fitting for them; but planted for fence, beams, and wood something not fitting; liable in orlah; planted for wood and considered it for eating; from when do we count. From planting; one who plants for the public; liable, and R' Yehuda exempts; Rashbag says in his name: one who plants for the public is liable in orlah; what grows for the public is exempt; one who plants in a ship, pot, and on a roof; liable in orlah (but in Avodah Zarah Hilchot Orlah siman 313 reads exempt; see there); and Yerushalmi beginning of Orlah: “and you shall plant every food tree”; what is for food is liable; for fence, beams, and wood; exempt, etc.; teaches Rashbag: when planted for fence, beams, and wood something fitting; planted something not fitting; liable. Correct is Rashbag. What is the reason of the Sages. Meaning: the Sages argue on Rashbag and exempt even what is not fitting; R' Zeira in mishnah: order their planting; for wood in a row, for beams in a beam-structure, for fence; place of fence proves on him, etc.; R' Yochanan in name of R' Shimon ben Yehotzadak: vine that grew in a forest place is exempt from orlah; R' Yosi: even if planted; but we learned what grows by itself is liable in orlah; there when planted in a settled place, but here planted in a forest place; R' Elazar: this you say when it does not produce enough to graft; but if it produces enough to graft; liable; R' Hoshaya asked: citron planted for its mitzvah; what, is it liable in orlah. R' Hoshaya returned and said: citron planted for its mitzvah is liable in orlah, etc.; R' Shimon ben Yakim asked before R' Yochanan: planted the lower side for fence and upper for food, lower for food and upper for fence; he said: it is this, it is this, etc.; R' Zeira asked: is it settled lower for fence upper for food, lower for food upper for fence; grows from prohibition and you say thus. R' Zeira like his view of R' Zeira in name of R' Yonatan: cabbage of kilayei hakerem that one uprooted and replanted; even if it increases much, forbidden, for growths do not remove prohibition; planted for fence and considered for food; comes to mind for food and considered for fence; not all the time; planted first year for fence; from here on considered for food; once he considered it with intent of liability, liable, etc.; planted three years for fence; from here on considered for food, etc.; and concludes there: whatever has no orlah has no revai, etc.; teaches Rashbag says in his name: one who plants for the public is liable in orlah; what grows by itself; exempt; one who plants for the public in public domain; exempt; one who plants for the public; liable; planting within his own, etc.; R' Yosi bar Chakula in name of Chizkiyah: one who plants in an unperforated pot; liable in orlah; R' Yosi said: because roots spread through it; R' Yona expounded the language “earthenware vessel”; standing before the roots; meaning in astonishment; R' Yirmiyah asked: planted in it a gourd; since perforated toward the tree is like perforated toward seeds; R' Yochanan in name of R' Yanai: tree planted inside the house is liable in orlah and exempt from maasrot, etc.; and in sheviit requires, etc.
Whether the inner portion, etc. This is derived from the foregoing. He rules in accordance with Rabbi Yose in the Mishnah because the Yerushalmi discusses the matter according to his view, as explained above.
Whether the lower portion, etc. ; even though R' Zeira challenged; he did not pay attention, as R' Chananel in name of Geonim (Rashbam Bava Batra 52b): whatever is not established we act like him; and he also challenged on mishnah that we teach there: tree part planted in ground and part abroad; since part planted in ground, as if all in ground, etc.; R' Elazar in name of R' Yochanan: it becomes like tevel and maaser mixed one in another.
