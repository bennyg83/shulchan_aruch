Whether the lower or the upper one, etc. Although the upper one grew from the prohibited lower growth, permitted growth raises the prohibition. This does not conflict with seif 22 concerning a branch full of orlah fruit, where permitted growth does not raise the prohibition. There, the orlah fruit was identifiable before the permitted growth developed. Here, the fruit develops simultaneously from both the permitted and prohibited causes, and the case is therefore one of two contributing causes. This is the explanation of the Beit Yosef and the later authorities.
