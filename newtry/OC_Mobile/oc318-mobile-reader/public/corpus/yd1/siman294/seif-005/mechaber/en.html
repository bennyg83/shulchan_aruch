That which we said that after Rosh Chodesh Tishrei of the fourth year, all fruit that chanata before the fifteenth of Shevat have the status of orlah even though they complete later—this is specifically in a planting where we were lenient at its beginning, since because forty-four days passed for it a year was counted; therefore we were stringent at its end. If not so, no. And some say there is no difference.
