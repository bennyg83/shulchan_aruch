(Taz, seif katan 24) It is exempt from orlah, and therefore the other is obligated. The Yerushalmi records a dispute among Amoraim whether the years are counted from the planting of the non-fruit-bearing tree, or whether it is treated like a tree newly planted in the ground and the count begins at grafting. See Menachot 68a, where there is a dispute whether a young tree planted and rooted before being joined to an older tree is nevertheless nullified into the older tree, since its rooting preceded the joining.
