(Taz s.k. 24) That it is exempt from orlah therefore the grafted one is subject — see Yerushalmi: dispute among Amoraim whether we count from the time of planting the sterile tree or it is like one planting in the ground and we count from the time of grafting; see Menachot (68) that if one planted a tree and it took root and afterward intertwined the vines with its fellow old tree — there is a dispute whether we say here too that the young is nullified in the old, since the rooting was before it was connected to the old tree.
