Orlah applies in every place and at every time, both in that of a non-Jew and in that of an Israelite; except that in Eretz Yisrael it is d'oraisa and in the Diaspora it is halacha l'Moshe mi'Sinai. {Rama: And it is forbidden to gather orlah fruits for a non-Jew, because one benefits from the good will the non-Jew feels toward him on account of this (Kol Bo and Beit Yosef in the name of Orach Chaim).}
