That which grows by itself in a private domain. Like Rashbag in Tosefta and Yerushalmi cited. Kiryat Sefer wrote: because in Tosefta it teaches "and you shall plant" — excludes what grows by itself; and so that there should not be a contradiction in the mishnah — therefore the mishnah too must be according to Rashbag in a private domain.
Even one who plants, etc. — like R' Yosi in Yerushalmi as above.
