That which grows by itself in a private domain. Like Rashbag in Tosefta and Yerushalmi cited. Kesef Mishneh wrote: because in Tosefta it teaches “and you shall plant”; excludes what grows by itself; and so that there should not be a contradiction in the mishnah; therefore the mishnah too must be according to Rashbag in a private domain.
Even one who plants, etc. This follows Rabbi Yose in the Yerushalmi, as explained above.
