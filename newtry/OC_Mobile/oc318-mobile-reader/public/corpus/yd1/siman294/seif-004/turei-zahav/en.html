All fruits that ripen before the fifteenth of Shevat. For even though Tishrei is the New Year for plantings, the fifteenth of Shevat is the New Year for trees.
They also have the law of orlah. The reason: everything that ripens between Tishrei and the fifteenth of Shevat is due to irrigation water before Rosh Hashanah — and from that irrigation these fruits ripen; and they are as if they ripened before Tishrei — that they ripened from sap before Tishrei; and since so, there is no distinction whether planted 44 days before Rosh Hashanah or less. And after the fifteenth [of Shevat]. The explanation is trees that finished ripening afterward; and Rosh wrote in Hilchot Orlah: it appears that nowadays there is no ripening on any tree before the fifteenth of Shevat, and therefore we are not careful about orlah except three years.
