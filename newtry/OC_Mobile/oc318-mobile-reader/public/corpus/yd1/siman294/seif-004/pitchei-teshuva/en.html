Before the sixteenth of Av. Shivat Tzion discusses a tree planted during summer when it is unknown whether planting occurred before or after the sixteenth of Av. Since doubtful orlah outside Eretz Yisrael is permitted, the years are calculated leniently even though the tree has an established status of prohibition.
There still remain forty-four days. Chatam Sofer suggests that for orlah outside Eretz Yisrael, where doubt is permitted, three days for rooting before the thirty days preceding Rosh HaShanah may suffice instead of fourteen. He states this as theoretical halakhah, not a practical ruling.
