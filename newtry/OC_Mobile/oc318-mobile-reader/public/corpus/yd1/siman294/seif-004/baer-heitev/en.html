The reason: anything hardened between Tishrei and 15 Shevat is due to irrigation water before Rosh Hashanah — and they are as if hardened before Tishrei; and since so, there is no distinction whether planted 44 days before Rosh Hashanah or less. Ran wrote; Shach in the name of Rosh: nowadays there is no hardening in any tree before 15 Shevat, and therefore we are careful about orlah only three years. That the author did not write this — because he holds the essence is like Rambam in siman 295 who holds even if hardened before 15 Shevat, never find more than three years, for when planted less than 44 days before Rosh Hashanah one need not wait until 15 Shevat; therefore the author omitted Rosh's words — see above siman 295.
