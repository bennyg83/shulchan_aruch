Forty-four. Fifteen days of Av, which is always full, plus twenty-nine days of Elul, which is always deficient. Thirty days count as a year for planting, but the tree does not take root in fewer than fourteen days.
That they bud. Fruit budding between Tishrei and the fifteenth of Shevat grew from moisture absorbed before Rosh HaShanah and is treated as though it budded before Tishrei. Therefore no distinction exists between planting forty-four days before Rosh HaShanah or fewer. Shach cites Rosh that nowadays no tree buds before the fifteenth of Shevat, so orlah is observed only three years. The Mechaber omits this because he follows Rambam, whose calculation never produces more than three years.
