M.D. The Day of Daw, who is a whole world and a “day of Dahl, which is never lacking, and the taste of the day of the year is a year of abandonment, but which does not absorb less than one day:
Until they bud. The reason is that whatever buds between Tishrei and the fifteenth of Shevat is due to the water absorbed before Rosh Hashanah, and they are therefore as though they budded before Tishrei. Since so, there is no distinction between planting forty-four days before Rosh Hashanah or less than that. The Ran. The Shach writes in the name of the Rosh that nowadays no tree buds before the fifteenth of Shevat, and therefore we are careful with orlah only for three years. The Mechaber did not write this because he holds the main view follows the Rambam in seif 5, who holds that even if they budded before the fifteenth of Shevat, one never finds more than three years; for when he planted less than thirty days before Rosh Hashanah, he need not wait until the fifteenth of Shevat. Therefore the Mechaber omitted the Rosh's words. See below seif 5.
