Since they have not yet come into existence. I do not know the source of this ruling. It is difficult, because if produce not yet grown may be sold later without concern, why must the partnership arrangement in seif 12 be established specifically at the beginning? Nor can the permission rest merely on receiving payment before the prohibited produce comes into existence, since the ruling is stated without that limitation. If the supposed reason is that a sale of something not yet in existence is ineffective, then, according to Rabbeinu Tam that money does not acquire from a non-Jew without meshichah, one could similarly sell actual orlah fruit for money, which is certainly false. This ruling therefore requires study.
