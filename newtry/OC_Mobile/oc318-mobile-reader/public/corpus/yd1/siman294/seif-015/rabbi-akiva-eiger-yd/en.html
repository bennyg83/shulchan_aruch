(Taz) To sell to a non-Jew — it is explained there in the Rashba that the reason is he sells only a place of trees, and the trees of orlah are permitted for benefit; brought in Nekudot HaKesef; and one must say that even though he did not explicitly say "the palm tree for its fruits," nevertheless since his whole intent in this sale is to separate from the prohibition, it is per the sages' intention that he sells in an effective manner; and similarly wrote the Perishah, which I brought below (siman 320); and one could distinguish that there it is a mitzvah to sell.
