It is permitted to tell idolaters etc. — so taught Rav Yerucham from the preceding seif "some permit," because thus the trial teaches: this one works and this one eats; and so he concluded here that the worker's law is to take fruits. Therefore it is difficult on the Mechaber, since he ruled in seif 13 unlike those who permit — how can he write here Rav Yerucham's words? One must say he holds as the Pri Megadim wrote — even the Rambam agrees here, specifically there where they accepted in partnership manner: this one works and this one eats, and he acts as a partner who toils for his fellow's sake, unlike here — see there.
