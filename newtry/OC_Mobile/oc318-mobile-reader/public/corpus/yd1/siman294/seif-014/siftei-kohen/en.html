It is permitted to say to non-Jews, etc. Rabbeinu Yerucham derived this from the preceding seif, according to the view that permits because the arrangement is that the one who works receives the produce. He concludes here likewise that the worker is entitled to take the fruit. This is difficult according to the Mechaber, who ruled in seif 13 against that permissive view. One must say, as the Levush explains, that even Rambam agrees here. There, they accepted the field in the ordinary manner of partners, one working while the other eats, so one partner labors for the other. That concern does not apply here. See there.
