It is permitted to say, etc. The Beit Yosef explains in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham that a worker is entitled to fruit as his compensation. Even according to the Rambam, this case may be permitted for another reason: the Israelite effectively transfers the use of his land in exchange for the other person's labor upon that person's land, which resembles a sale for money rather than an exchange of orlah fruit.
