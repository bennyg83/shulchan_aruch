Permitted to say, etc. — Beit Yosef gave a reason in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham: the worker's law is he takes fruits; and per this must say he holds like those who permit that Rama mentions nearby: per Rambam above we do not say so; and if so Shulchan Aruch should not have brought this law since he ruled seif 12 like Rambam; and one could say another reason: it is like a sale where he sells his land for wages giving his land to work and it is like selling for money — and this all permit.
