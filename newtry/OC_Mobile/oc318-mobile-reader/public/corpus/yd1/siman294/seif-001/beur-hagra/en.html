And grapes, etc. — Rosh there s.v. anav kuklot, etc., and their dispute regarding revai, but regarding orlah there is no difference; and from "and exempt" it implies like R' Yosi; and it implies R' Chiya bar Aba supports him, and so in Rambam's explanation — see there.
And there are those who — and as Rambam wrote in his explanation, and navelot, etc., only he explained navelot as dried dates (Asidei Zahav), and his words require study [and also Rosh's words require study, who brought an amoraic dispute there whether dried dates, etc., and it appears something is missing in the transcription and should read as Rosh there]; but Tur explained navelot — all of all fruits, but it does not refer to an unripe cluster; and his words are astounding, for from this he should have said "all of them," since pits too are of all fruits; and in the Gemara he did not explain navelot but dates; and it should read "and navelot" after "and pits" before "forbidden"; and what Beit Yosef brought as proof from Yerushalmi — they said fruit you redeem and not unripe and not pagim — pagim are not like navelot, for navelot ripen by burying in earth, unlike pagim; and so he listed above pagim and navelot as two.
