(Siman 294 seif 3) The poor ones are obligated. We say in the Talmud: one throws the poor ones; and see in chapter 10 that one need not be concerned they will eat them; and Rif and Rambam's view: even in the land of Israel one throws the poor ones and eats caper berries; and Peri Chadash agreed (Orach Chayim siman 202).
