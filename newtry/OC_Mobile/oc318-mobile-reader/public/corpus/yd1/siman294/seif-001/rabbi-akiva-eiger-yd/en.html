(Siman 294, seif 3) The caper berries are obligated. The Gemara states that one throws away the caper berries. Penei Yehoshua writes that there is no need to fear that another person will eat them. Rif and Rambam hold that even in Eretz Yisrael one throws away the caper berries and may eat the husks. Pri Chadash, Orach Chaim, siman 202, concurs.
