(Siman 294 seif 1) Obligated in orlah. In Beit Yosef he challenged: why did Tur and Shulchan Aruch not write orlah requires burning as we taught end of Temurah; and one could say Shulchan Aruch follows his reason that he wrote below siman 331: nowadays terumah even in the land is rabbinic; and if so the same for orlah nowadays, as Magen Avraham wrote.
