The laws of orlah—where it applies and for whom. In it are 28 seifim.
One who plants a food tree counts three years from the time of its planting, and all fruit that will be on it within three years are forbidden in benefit forever—whether the essence of the fruit, the pits, or the shells, such as walnut shells and pomegranate rinds, and their sap and dyes, and grape pits and the beer made from them, and unripe fruit (pagim), and dates that do not cook—and grapes that were scalded and do not finish in their cooking—all are liable for orlah and exempt from revai. And there are those who forbid dates that do not cook even for revai.
