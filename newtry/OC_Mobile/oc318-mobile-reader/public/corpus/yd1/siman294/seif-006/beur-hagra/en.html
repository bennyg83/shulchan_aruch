And detach. Tosefta at the end of Maaser Sheni: fourth-year vineyard — Beit Shammai: one does not redeem them as grapes except as wine; Beit Hillel: grapes and wine; all agree one does not redeem attached to the ground; Rambam wrote the reason because we derive from maaser as above, and there is no maaser on what is attached, as written in chapter 2 of Bikkurim; so Tosafot Bava Kama 69 side a s.v. aima, and Rosh chapter 5 of Maaser Sheni at the end of mishnah 5; and they contradicted this reason from Bava Kama "whoever gathers," and wrote because on what is attached they are not expert in appraisal; but the modest ones made an enactment, etc.; therefore in the responsum of Ramban and Rashba on Bava Kama in our times they redeem with appraisal while attached — to me requires study, for according to their reasoning likewise regarding hekdesh; and mishnah in chapter 1 and 4 of Peah and chapter 3 of Challah — one redeems attached; Rambam's reason is primary, and so Yerushalmi cited explicitly that they challenged Beit Shammai, who does not derive netsar from maaser, and redeems attached as above; and this resolves what Tosafot brought from Tosefta on Bava Kama, but since it is not established in the conclusion he was not concerned, as Rashi and Tosafot wrote in many places; and so Rambam there halacha 7 and the modest ones, etc., "whoever gathers," etc., for it is impossible to redeem attached, as we explained.
And one may redeem, etc. — Tosafot in Berakhot 35 side a s.v. uleman, etc., and Rosh and Shulchan Pesak; and this is Rambam's wording in chapter 2 of Maaser Sheni: the Geonim ruled that if one wished to redeem the value of a maneh with a prutah ab initio in our times, he redeems — this is not more stringent than hekdesh, and he throws the prutah into the Great Sea. At first glance requires study, for there is no proof from hekdesh, since the reason for hekdesh is there is no onaah for hekdesh, as written in Bava Metzia 57 side a and Temurah 27 side a; and even though one could say maaser sheni nowadays is worth nothing, nevertheless it is not like hekdesh; and further, the amoraim after the Temple would redeem at value, as written in Yerushalmi chapter 4 of Maaser Sheni: R' Shimon bar [Rabbi] would deposit with those Romans who would belittle it — Nechemyah bar Kefar and Kartamin before him — he said to him: it is good, is it not anything, etc.; R' Imi: in what did he succeed before R' Yochanan and Rashbi? They said: go and pay as this braita teaches — one redeems maaser sheni at the cheap rate, as the shopkeeper buys, etc.; R' Nachman bar Yaakov showed a pouch to the carpenter and unloaded on his mouth; R' Simon showed fruits to R' Chelbo — he said to him: at their value; he said: yes — R' Chelbo in the name of R' Simon, etc.; and how many actions they brought there that they would redeem at value — only Rambam was careful about this and wrote there a measure of piety that one redeems maaser sheni at its value, like the way one redeems it before a barrel; and this does not remove the difficulty, for they ruled there in practice; and R' Shimon bar [Rabbi] was strict with the Romans as above; and it is strained to say all these argue on Shmuel — rather it appears the Geonim's reason is we hold in Kedushin 64 side b like R' Meir that maaser sheni has the law of hekdesh for him, as written "whose words? Ilfa says R' Meir," etc.; and likewise regarding netsar, as written "teach," etc.; and Yerushalmi holds halacha is like R' Yosi regarding R' Meir, and Sages argue on R' Meir as in chapter 2 of Pesachim (37–38) and chapter 3 of Sukkah; but we hold like our gemara, and therefore he wrote in Teshuvot HaRash that even a measure of piety one does not do. And even though in Meilah 14 we say only after the fact, and even though in Bava Metzia there and Temurah there amoraim argue on this — Tosafot already wrote there and there that according to all it is only after the fact; nevertheless we say in Arakhin 29 side a that nowadays one redeems even ab initio.
