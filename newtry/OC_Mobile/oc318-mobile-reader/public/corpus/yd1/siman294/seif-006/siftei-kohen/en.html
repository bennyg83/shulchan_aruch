The half-viner, etc. This does not mean that specifically a half-viner is required. For according to what the Rav wrote below at the beginning of siman 305, that five selaim, which are 120 maah, are approximately two Rhenish gold coins, and this too is from the words of Mahari, then one maah is four viners, and a perutah is one-eighth of a viner. So wrote Sema in Choshen Mishpat siman 88 s.k. 2. Rather, since there is no coin smaller than a half-viner, he wrote that if one redeemed with a half-viner it suffices, for in any case it is worth more than a perutah. According to this, when one redeems with something worth a perutah and not with an actual coin, it is enough that it be worth one-eighth of a viner. Alternatively, according to what Sema wrote there, that in our times one cannot buy with a perutah except an exceedingly tiny amount, one may say that a perutah does not suffice; therefore here it must be worth a half-viner.
