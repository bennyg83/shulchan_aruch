Applies also outside the land. See Sha'ar HaMelech chapter 9 of Hilchot Ma'aser Sheni who wondered on the author: here he ruled plainly like the view of the students of R' Yonah and the elder R' Yitzchak brought in Beit Yosef that neta revai applies outside the land — because we do not say "whoever is lenient in the land," etc., except where stated in the Gemara; but afterward he wrote Rambam's view in the name of "some say"; while in seif 17 he wrote: even though the custom, etc., because grafting and layering — obligated in orlah only in the land of Israel but not outside — and this is Rosh's view, because "whoever is lenient in the land"; if so the author's words contradict each other — see there; and see Biur HaGra.
