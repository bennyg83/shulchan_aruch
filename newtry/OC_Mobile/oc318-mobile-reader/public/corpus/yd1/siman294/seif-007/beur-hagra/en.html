The law of netsar, etc., and Yeish Omrim, etc., and Yeish Omrim, etc. — the third reasoning is primary, and it is the Geonim's reasoning, and so unspecified below in siman 17; and so Tosafot in Berakhot 35 side a s.v. uleman, etc.; and R' Yona wondered on this — if so, why need we say in Kedushin 39 side a "you did not include," we do not hold, etc. — for in any case whoever is lenient, etc., only we do not say except regarding caper specifically — and this is the first reasoning, and his words are astounding, for we say in Shabbat 139 side a and we hold, etc. [and likewise in Yerushalmi end ch 4 of Kilayim: words of Sages — R' Yaakov bar Iddi in the name of R' Yehoshua ben Levi: halacha follows one who is lenient abroad; R' Yaakov bar Acha — and it teaches there: halacha follows one who is lenient abroad, and so there chapter 6 end of mishnah 1, and it implies there in all places]. And his difficulty that he asked is harder there — why need we say like R' Tarfon, it should say like R' Yoshiah because of the reason — rather it must be said as Tosafot there s.v. velishlach, etc., see there. The second reasoning is Rambam's reasoning, and this is his wording in chapter 10 of Maaser Sheni: it appears to me netsar does not apply abroad, and a fortiori from Syria that does not apply, as in Maaser Sheni — a fortiori to abroad, etc.; and this is his wording in chapter 9 of Maaser Sheni: and just as there is no maaser sheni in Syria, so there is no nets revai in Syria, and his words are taken from Yerushalmi chapter 5 of Maaser Sheni — but it is very astounding, for from there is proof to the contrary [and I found in Rashba in Bava Kama 69 side a that he wrote Rambam learned from this Yerushalmi, and he too wondered on him — see there where he elaborated on the Yerushalmi]. And this is its Yerushalmi language there: we learned — R' Yishmael: a gentile has no fourth-year vineyard, and the Sages say he has, etc.; Rav Beivai said before R' Zeira in the name of R' Eliezer: R' Yishmael's reasoning follows Beit Shammai — just as Beit Shammai said they did not learn netsar except from maaser sheni, as one who says there is no maaser sheni in sheviit and likewise no netsar in sheviit, so R' Yishmael did not learn netsar except from maaser sheni, as one who says there is no maaser sheni in Syria and likewise no netsar in Syria, etc.; R' Yishmael: a gentile has no netsar in Syria — meaning: just as he is not obligated in a gentile's [produce] in Syria in maaser sheni, for in Syria a gentile has acquisition to remove from tithes, as in mishnah chapter 6 of Demai "one who sells produce," etc., and end of Maasrot "one who buys a field," etc., and chapter 4 of Challah "Israel who were," etc., and as written in siman 331 see there from siman 173 until siman 181. And this is R' Yishmael's wording: a gentile has no vineyard — it requires specifically Syria and specifically a gentile; and for the Sages, even a gentile in Syria; and so explained explicitly in Tosefta and Rosh in chapter 3 of Terumot there s.v. ein le'oved kochavim, etc., as the braita teaches, etc. (Likut) The law of planting, etc. — from what he wrote; but one can establish this reasoning from another side, that Yeish Omrim they argue only from Torah regarding maaser sheni, etrog is equivalent to a tree, etc. — Tosafot Kedushin 2 side b s.v. etrog. And Yeish Omrim, etc., and there is, etc. (end of comment). (Likut) The law, etc. — in Tosafot Kedushin 2 side b s.v. etrog they wrote: Yeish Omrim that d'rabbanan he admits — even one who teaches "vineyard applies to all trees," and proof — we learned etrog is equivalent to a tree, etc., and revai; and accordingly it applies abroad d'rabbanan according to R' Yehudah — there. But in Tosafot Rosh Hashanah 14 side b s.v. lerevai and Sukkah 39 side b s.v. lerevai they wrote like the first reasoning there in Tosafot on that mishnah — it follows one who says nets revai (end of comment).
