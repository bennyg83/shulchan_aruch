(Seif 15) To sell it to a non-Jew. Rashba explains there that he sells only the place occupied by the wood, while orlah wood itself is permitted for benefit. Nekudot HaKesef cites this. One must say that even though he did not expressly sell "the palm tree for its fruit," since his entire purpose is to separate himself from a prohibition, he sells it in the effective manner intended by the Sages. Perishah writes something similar, as I cite below in siman 320. One may distinguish, however, because there selling is itself a mitzvah.
