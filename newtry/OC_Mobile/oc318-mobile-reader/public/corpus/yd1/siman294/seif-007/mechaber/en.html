The law of netah revai applies even in the Diaspora. And there are those who say it applies only in Eretz Yisrael, but in the Diaspora fruits that come after the two years of orlah are permitted without redemption. {Rama: And some say it does not apply in the Diaspora except in a vineyard and not in other trees (Tur in the name of the Geonim, Ramban, and Rosh, and so it appears).}
