For the honor of the deceased. Anything not for the deceased's honor is certainly forbidden. Some speakers seek to display their own distinction through a eulogy unrelated to the deceased's honor, and they act improperly. I heard that many great scholars were particular about this. The Tur cites Rav Hai Gaon that Torah study is forbidden even beyond four cubits.
In Torat HaAdam it is written that one may speak Torah words concerning the deceased and his virtues, for these are for his honor. One may proceed from aggadah and verses to descriptions of his Torah, piety, and good qualities, but not turn the occasion into an unrelated discourse.
