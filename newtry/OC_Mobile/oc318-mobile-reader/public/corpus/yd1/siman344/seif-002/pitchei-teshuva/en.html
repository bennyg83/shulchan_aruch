But if he instructed. Iyun Yaakov on Sanhedrin 47 disputes Mahari Weil and the Rama, holding that the deceased's instruction should be obeyed even regarding seven days of mourning and rending, because these too honor the deceased. He ruled accordingly in practice, and repeated the position in Shevut Yaakov, part 2, responsum 102. Teshuvah MeAhavah, part 1, end of responsum 207, rejects this leniency and rules that it should not be relied upon.
