Who is one who kills himself intentionally — such as one who said behold he goes up to the roof and they saw him go up immediately in anger or he was distressed and fell and died — behold he is presumed to have killed himself intentionally; but if they saw him hanged and hanging on a tree, or slain and cast upon his sword — behold he is presumed like all the dead, and they attend to him and do not withhold anything from him: {Rama: One who stole and robbed and through this was killed by royal law — they mourn him if there is no danger from fear of the kingdom, and he is not called one who killed himself intentionally (Mahariv siman 114).}
