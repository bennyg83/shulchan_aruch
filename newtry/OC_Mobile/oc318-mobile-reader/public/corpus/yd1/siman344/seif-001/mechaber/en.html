The law of one who kills himself intentionally, a menudeh, those executed by beit din, and those who separate from the community. It contains 8 seifim.
One who kills himself intentionally — they do not attend to him for anything, do not mourn him, do not eulogize him, do not tear, nor remove a shoe; but they stand in a row over him and say upon him the mourners' blessing and anything that is honor for the living.
