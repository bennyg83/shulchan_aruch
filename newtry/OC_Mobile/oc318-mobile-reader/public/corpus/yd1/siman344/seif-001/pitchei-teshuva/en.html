We heed him. Baer Heitev of Maharit cites Responsa Beit Yaakov, siman 83, that if the leading sage of the generation dies after instructing that he not be eulogized, one who is lenient and does not heed that instruction has not lost. Teshuvah MeAhavah, part 1, siman 174, discusses this at length and reports that Noda BiYehudah, when eulogizing Pnei Yehoshua, said that although he had ordered that no eulogy be given, one does not heed such an instruction from a master of such stature. Yet after analysis he concludes that he has no clear permission to override an explicit request, "Do not eulogize me." See also siman 207. Beit Yaakov further writes that if two talmidei chachamim in one city hated one another and one dies, the eulogy for the deceased may not be diminished out of respect for the surviving scholar. Responsa Even Shoham, siman 72, writes that if a person instructed that his son not recite Kaddish for him, the instruction is followed.
