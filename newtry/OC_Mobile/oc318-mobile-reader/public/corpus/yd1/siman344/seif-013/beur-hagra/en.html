(Supplement) After, etc. Jerusalem Talmud, cited by the Rosh in chapter 3 of Mo'ed Katan and in chapter 3 of Berakhot, siman 11. And Ramban wrote that the ruling follows our Sages, and his reading of the Jerusalem Talmud there is: "they interrupt [their activities] for Shema but not, etc. — an incident, etc." And he explained: "on the first day" refers to the [funeral] row. And he wrote that we learn from this: before burial, in the presence of the dead, they slip away and recite [Shema] but do not pray; [if] the dead is not before them, they recite and pray; after burial, the row does not interrupt either for Shema or for prayer; but for the eulogy they do interrupt both for Shema and for prayer. This is unlike the words of the Shulchan Aruch in seif 12, which states "and they pray." See Beit Yosef.
