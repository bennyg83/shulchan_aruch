Torah. The Bach rules that Torah discussion is forbidden even beyond four cubits of the corpse, while ordinary conversation is forbidden only within four cubits. Matters concerning the deceased are permitted even when they involve Torah and even within four cubits. The custom is therefore to expound aggadic teachings and verses within four cubits, moving from topic to topic until reaching praise of the deceased's Torah, piety, and good qualities. The Taz criticizes those who use a funeral discourse to display their own scholarship rather than honor the deceased, and reports that many great authorities objected to this. Mordechai writes that the entire room containing the corpse is considered within four cubits, and the Bach rules accordingly. See simanim 366 and 416.
