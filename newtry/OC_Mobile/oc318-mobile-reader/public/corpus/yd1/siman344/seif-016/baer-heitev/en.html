Torah. The Bach rules that words of Torah are forbidden even outside four amot of the deceased, while ordinary conversation is forbidden only within four amot. Matters relating to the deceased are permitted even when they involve Torah, and even within four amot. Accordingly, the practice is to expound aggadic teachings and verses even within four amot of the deceased, moving from one subject to another until reaching the praise of the deceased, his Torah study, piety, and other good qualities. The Taz writes that this is unlike those who deliberately display their own stature through the eulogy of the deceased, in a manner that does not honor him; they act improperly. I have heard that many great authorities were particular about this. The Mordechai writes that the entire room in which the deceased lies is considered as four amot, and the Bach rules accordingly.
