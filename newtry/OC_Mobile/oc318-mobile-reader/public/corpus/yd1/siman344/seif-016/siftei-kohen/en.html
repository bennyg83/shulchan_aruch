One may not say, etc. The Mechaber appears to hold that words of Torah are forbidden even beyond four cubits from the deceased, while ordinary conversation is permitted even within four cubits. The Bach rules that Torah discussion is forbidden even beyond four cubits, ordinary conversation is forbidden only within four cubits, and matters concerning the deceased are permitted even when they include Torah and are said within four cubits. The custom is therefore to expound aggadic teachings and verses before the deceased, moving from topic to topic until discussing his Torah, piety, and other virtues. The Mordechai writes that the entire room in which the deceased lies is considered his four cubits, and the Bach rules accordingly. See below, siman 366.
