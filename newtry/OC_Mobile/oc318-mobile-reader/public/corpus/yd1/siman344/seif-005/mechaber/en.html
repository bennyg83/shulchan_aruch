All who separate from the ways of the community — they are people who cast off the yoke of mitzvot from their necks and are not included among Israel in their deeds and in honoring the festivals and sitting in synagogues and study houses; rather behold they are like free people for themselves like other nations; and likewise apostates and informers — all these do not become onenim and they do not mourn them except their brothers and other relatives, who wear white and wrap in white and eat and drink and rejoice: {Rama: One who separates from the community and did not wish to bear with them taxes and levies — they mourn him (responsum of Rashba siman 563); but the rest of the townspeople need not cease from their work on his account to attend to him (so it appears in Nimmukei Yosef end of Moed Katan).}
