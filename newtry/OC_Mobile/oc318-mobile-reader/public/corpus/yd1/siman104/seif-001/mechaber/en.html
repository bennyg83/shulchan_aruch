The law of a mouse found in wine or beer. In it are 3 seifim: A field mouse imparts beneficial taste, for it is served on kings' tables; but a city mouse — we are uncertain whether it imparts beneficial taste in beer and vinegar or whether it spoils; and therefore if it fell into beer or vinegar while cold and they removed it whole, if it did not remain inside twenty-four hours it is permitted; but if it was boiling, or even cold and remained inside twenty-four hours — whether they removed it whole, whether it was cut into fine pieces and one can strain it in a sieve so nothing remains, whether it dissolved completely and became entirely liquid and no substance remained — it is permitted by having sixty in the heter against the mouse; and if it was cut into fine pieces and cannot be strained, such as when the beer or vinegar was mixed into thick food — all is forbidden and there is no nullification, for we fear one may encounter the substance of the issur and not notice: {Rama: and specifically for sheretz we are concerned if something remains that cannot be removed; but for other issurim there is no concern (so it appears in Arukh general rule 32).}
