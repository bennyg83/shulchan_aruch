(Shulchan Aruch siman 104 seif 1) Mouse fell into beer, etc.; and if cut into fine pieces, etc. — all forbidden for we fear encountering issur substance. Hagahah wrote: and specifically in sheretz we are concerned, etc. — reason explained in Beit David: sheretz from the beginning of creation is like a lentil as in Chagigah daf 11, and that is its measure for tumah and lashes, and so for being beriah and not nullified — see there. I have objections to one of the acharonim in this siman who wrote per Shach that sheretz's measure is like a lentil and is beriah and not nullified — then the question on siman 100 regarding embryo egg where Taz wrote it is beriah is resolved: whence find it — if recognizable from the shell, taste is nullified; if not recognizable, crushed in dish it should nullify, for we hold crushed beriah is nullified as siman 101 seif 6; one can say it deals when crushed in thick food and embryo is sheretz with lentil measure and is beriah — end; his words are surprising, for sheretz like a lentil for beriah is specifically the eight sheratzim with tumah, and when their tumah measure is like a lentil, so eating measure for lashes is like a lentil as in Me'ilah daf 16; since so is their measure for tumah and lashes, so too for not being nullified, for beriah is not nullified because that is its lashes measure, as if the Torah said do not eat them in any amount, as explicit in Ashiri Pesachim and Tosafot and Shita Mekubetzet and Beit Yosef siman 100; but all other sheratzim without tumah — eating measure is kezayit as explicit in Me'ilah, Rambam chapters 2 and 4 of Forbidden Foods and Tum'at Foods, and Tosafot Zevachim 106b and Yevamot 114; if so one cannot say like a lentil of them is beriah and not nullified — there is neither tumah nor lashes; furthermore their beginning of creation is not like a lentil to be considered beriah; furthermore embryo of pure fowl is not sheretz d'oraisa — issur is only rabbinic, as Rambam and Taz siman 88 seif 8 wrote — only impure fowl embryo is d'oraisa as sheretz of fowl; but clear there is no tumah on fowl sheretz and measure is kezayit; see Pesachim 64; therefore his words here are incorrect — requires study.
