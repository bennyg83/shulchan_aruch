If it fell into wine, oil, or other liquids — it certainly spoils and one does not need sixty to nullify its emission: {Rama: and accordingly if it fell into fat the law is the same; and some are stringent in fat, even if hard before us: if they pour fat daily into the pot, there is room to suspect the mouse was there when they heated boiling fat on it — all is forbidden, even if there is sixty in the fat against the whole mouse; and if they did not heat on it, or heated and it is known the mouse was not there when they heated and the fat came before us hard (responsum of Rosh, Hagahot Maimonides chapter 15, Hagahot Mordechai on Chullin, Hagahat Sh"D, and Arukh) — and likewise if the mouse is found on it, removal of place suffices and we do not establish issur lest the fat was soft when it fell there, for pickling is like cooking and permitted from double safek: safek it fell when hard; and if you say when soft, perhaps it hardened before pickling. And where the fat is repulsive to eat, it is forbidden to light it in synagogue because of "bring it to your fire" (Ran in the name of a Gaon, Rashba Pesachim, R' Yerucham, and Or Zarua in the name of Mordechai Pesachim, and the author in Orach Chaim siman 124 seif 12). And where there is great loss one may rely on the lenient words.}
