All forbidden — each time they heated it becomes forbidden like absorption, and Peri Chadash wrote: this is for those who hold piece becomes nevelah; but for those who do not hold piece becomes nevelah, if there is now sixty against all it is permitted; and truly difficult per his words, for this law is also in Or Zarua and he holds without noda b'hutrah piece becomes nevelah; and here it was not noda b'hutrah — one must say here it is not wet in wet, it does not blend well, it immediately congeals; if so Or Zarua wrote and Tosafot Bekhorot as I wrote above: even if permitted, it can be squeezed and is forbidden; so too for those who do not hold piece becomes nevelah, nevertheless it can be squeezed and is forbidden — if so. And so is proven that this law is in responsum of Rosh, and Rosh holds we do not say piece becomes nevelah in all issurim; if so contradictory — unless what is forbidden through absorption does not need sixty to nullify, so what if it became forbidden like absorption — nevertheless it is nullified and sixty is not needed; strained to say the absorption layer grew so much daily until majority — Rabbenu Tam and R' Yehudah who disagree with R' Abba hold this is strained; therefore it seems as I wrote: forbidden piece agrees and they forbade only in ben yomo as I wrote.
Permitted from double safek, etc. Beit David general rule 32 law 3 wrote: if known the fat was soft when the mouse fell, we forbid from safek perhaps it was pickled in it a full day even if now hard before us, unless known clearly it was not soft a full day after the mouse entered; and if known it was soft a full day and later hardened and we do not know if the mouse was in it while soft, all is forbidden — d'oraisa safek like Rosh and Mordechai if safek it was there when boiling fat was heated, unlike Or Zarua who permitted in both — see there, brought by Shach. I wonder: why not establish the fat on presumption of heter and now a moment before it hardened — from the Torah we establish merit; what Tosafot wrote at the beginning of Niddah: this is for kodshim, but for hullin all is permitted — we say "now it happened"; they wrote explicitly regarding a box they used and later a sheretz was found — we do not establish impurity presumption for taharot used earlier, for hullin all permit and Chizkiyah and R' Yochanan disagreed only for kodshim — see there beginning of Niddah; if so why establish impurity presumption retroactively? Truly for Rashba regarding an animal found tereifah, if the wound scabbed and it was certainly three days before, we establish issur retroactively: whatever you cannot say happened now and must say happened earlier, presumption already failed — if so here too: now it hardened and one cannot say it fell into the fat now and must say retroactively — perhaps it was pickled a full day before; but per Rama who ruled above siman 71 that even with scabbed wound, cheeses made before are permitted — we establish on presumption and say "now it happened" and it became tereifah; why not say so here? I searched and found no distinction in any way. What Rama brought as proof from tumah following time of finding — one can answer when we do not know it was soft twenty-four hours before, we say as now hard before us so it was before; but when we know it was soft twenty-four hours before and only do not know if the mouse was initially inside — on the contrary we establish from time to time and say: here it is found, here it was; but I already wrote this is all for kodshim; see Niddah 4a s.v. tevel, etc.; and wonder at Or Zarua who himself rules cheeses like above and wrote not like mikveh "lacking and came" — so too here; requires study.
