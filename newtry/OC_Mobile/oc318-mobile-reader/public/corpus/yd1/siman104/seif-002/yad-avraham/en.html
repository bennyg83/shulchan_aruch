(Seif 2) If it fell into wine and oil, etc. — one does not need sixty to nullify its emission. Not specifically emission — even if its substance mixed, only that it melted completely, removal suffices as next seif and siman 103; and so must be in what Rosh Pesachim wrote, brought by Beit Yosef s.k. regarding beriah not nullified in a thousand; and what Yerushalmi taught R' Yochanan one mouse in a thousand — not dealing with mouse mixed in heter, etc., until if melted and taste not recognizable, nullified in sixty — end; dealing with mouse mixed in heter that imparts beneficial taste, such as beer and vinegar or fat per Rosh — for in spoiling matter one does not need sixty even when its substance melted, only complete melting or possible straining as Taz seif 1 — clear.
