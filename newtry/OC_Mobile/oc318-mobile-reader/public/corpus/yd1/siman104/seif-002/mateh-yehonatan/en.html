(There in hagahah) And specifically in sheretz there is concern, etc.; but in other issurim there is no concern. See Shach s.k. 3 and what he wrote in the name of Ran on Rashi: why was this needed — in other issurim too there is concern at least for less than a kezayit, for we hold half a measure is forbidden d'oraisa — see there. It seems Tosafot's question: kezayit within the time of eating a half-loaf — we learn that half a measure is forbidden d'oraisa; and "half a measure" is forbidden only b'ein, but through mixture it is permitted; but kezayit within the time of eating a half-loaf is forbidden even through mixture; if so when mixed in more than the time of eating a half-loaf one cannot say half a measure; and this is only when the issur measure is a kezayit; but if the issur measure is very small like sheretz the size of a lentil, through mixture one may forbid since mixture does not negate to nullify; therefore one can distinguish sheretz from other issurim; and Ran disagrees with Rashi — clear.
