If it fell, etc. From what they were uncertain — from this it is clear that other liquids are obviously permitted.
And some, etc. So from responsum of Rosh; and possibly he holds the same for other liquids; and so from Rambam who wrote: if for wine and oil it is permitted because these must be spiced — implying other liquids are forbidden; and the question in vinegar is to exclude wine that is permitted.
Even, etc. For whatever is poured and touches the mouse becomes forbidden and is like issur that fell into two pots, each forbids the other; and the poured fat mixes again with the rest and all becomes forbidden. Beit David.
And where the fat, etc. Meaning: even if permitted to eat as stated in chapter 6 of Bava Batra (97b) and other places.
