For it certainly spoils; and those who are stringent hold the law of beer and vinegar applies; and so is explained in Beit Yosef and Beit David end of general rule 32; if so, wonder at the Rav's words why he did not permit with one safek in pickling alone, for there is also safek whether it imparts beneficial taste; and if so it is double safek; and it seems this safek whether beneficial or spoiling is not a safek since the Gemara itself was uncertain; or perhaps it is like safek due to lack of knowledge as above siman 98 s.k. 9 — requires study.
And some are stringent in fat, etc. So is Maharshal Pesachim siman 49 — to forbid in goose fat and other liquids, and he permits only wine, oil, and honey because Rambam wrote these must be spiced — see there.
Even if there is sixty in the fat. For whatever is poured and touches the mouse becomes forbidden and mixes with the rest of the fat and all becomes forbidden; and even though sometimes the mouse lies at the bottom of the bowl — if so we should permit all fat above the layer on the mouse — one can say it is known when they pour boiling fat everything melts, so the pour always touches the mouse and all becomes forbidden — Beit David general rule 32 law 9 — see there.
And if they did not heat on it. Meaning boiling, etc., and the fat came before us hard; but if it came soft before us, there is only one safek: perhaps it was there a full day; and safek pickling is forbidden as below siman 105; and if known it was soft when the mouse fell, we forbid from safek perhaps it was pickled in it a full day even if now hard, unless known clearly it was not soft a full day after the mouse entered; and if known soft a full day and later hardened and we do not know if the mouse was in it while soft or not — forbidden; however in all this permitted if there is sixty, unlike heating boiling fat on it — all this Beit David raised there — see there.
And where the fat is repulsive, etc. Or Zarua wrote general rule 32 law 26: likewise for him if repulsive to him, even if not repulsive to others, forbidden to him even with sixty and a hundred if still repulsive to him — end; and so in Sefer Api Ravrevi daf 57a.
