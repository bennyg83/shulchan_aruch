If the heter is greater, etc. Even if there is a kezayit within the time of eating a half-loaf it is permitted — so Rashba wrote; Beit Yosef wrote: even though there are disputants on other noten ta'am lifgam (as explained siman 103 seif 2), it seems for things spoiled in themselves they agree; and there is great reason to distinguish between them — clear; end; and see siman 101.
