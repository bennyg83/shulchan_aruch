Repulsive things at which a person's soul is disgusted, such as ants, flies, and mosquitoes that every person separates from because of their repulsiveness — even if they were mixed into a dish and their bodies were crushed into it, if the heter is greater they are permitted; and nevertheless, wherever one can check and pass through a sieve, one checks and strains (however in vinegar and beer there is concern like with a mouse) (Beit Yosef according to Rashba).
