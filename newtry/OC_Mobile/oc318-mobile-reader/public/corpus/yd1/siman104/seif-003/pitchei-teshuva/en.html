[On "into the pot and there is safek." See in responsum Chatam Sofer siman 94 who wrote to a rabbi: why establish on presumption of life — I do not know what is wrong that it was alive; even if it remained inside the time for emission while alive or dead, sheretz always forbids; and what Peri Megadim Orach Chaim siman 497 wrote: in live fish swallowing does not apply — he means they swallowed chametz or milk and the reason is that through heat nature turned to meat and blood; I know an incident: a chicken flew into boiling butter on the fire, died inside, and a gaon ruled the butter forbidden even for a non-Jew and they poured it in the streets; and they mentioned again that fowl in milk is permitted b'hanaah and he answered: so too b'hanaah is permitted and to sell to a non-Jew is forbidden because of absorption of a Jew that is forbidden to a non-Jew; and wrote there that Hashem prepared an answer to save from embarrassment since initially he did not intend this; and from Peri Megadim siman 98 s.k. 1 it seems he too did not lean on this; from this we learn emission applies even while alive; and clear proof from Rosh's words in the sugya of mouse in beer — see there. What he further challenged: let us establish now that it fell — end there see. However in Sefer Ikkei Dinim siman 7 section 22 he brought in the name of Teshurat Chen siman 80: if a live mouse or impure thing fell, etc., we do not count twenty-four hours, only from death, for while alive even if it remained many days it does not forbid — see there; and so is the leaning opinion; and the challenge and proof above must be resolved; and see Taz siman 497 and responsa mentioned in Peri Chadash there s.k. 1 — requires study]:
