Repulsive things, etc. We learn from the mouse; and as the Gemara says there, not like Rav Sheshet who said it is a novelty, etc.; therefore whatever the heter is greater it is permitted, for the substance is nullified in the majority; and see siman 103 seif 2; and if its substance is not in the mixture it is always permitted as there; and so at the end of Gittin: "you have a person for whom a fly," etc. — he sucks it and is not called wicked except because of "do not abominate yourselves," and he is not an istenis; with this even Ran who disagrees in siman 103 seif 2 agrees that here, where they are completely spoiled and repulsive, it does not help at all that one's soul is disgusted by them.
However, etc. So too Ran, for we learn from there.
