(Hagahah) And some are stringent in fat even if hard before us, etc. Taz wrote Or Zarua permits if it came hard — we follow time of finding; he challenged Beit David and Beit David: with one safek we are stringent; I wonder why not establish fat on presumption of heter; what Rama brought as proof — from tumah following finding — suffices when we do not know it was soft twenty-four hours before; but when we know it was soft twenty-four hours and only do not know if mouse was inside initially — on the contrary we establish from time to time: here found, here it was; but I wrote this is all for kodshim; see Niddah; wonder at Or Zarua who rules cheeses — not like mikveh; so too here; requires study. Even for us who are stringent in both, if within twenty-four hours the vessel with fat was carried place to place and mouse found there, one may permit even if it came soft and we do not know when it fell — we do not establish issur place to place; we say: here it is found, here it was; so in Maimonides Laws of Pesach and responsum Shemen Rokeach — he challenged this; see responsum Panim Me'irrot question 15.
