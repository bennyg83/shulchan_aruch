I have a place to study. “And thou shalt see thee, and thou shalt see thee.”
(There s.k. 6) And these words are astonishing, etc. This is not difficult at all. Although while alive it also stands to produce offspring, nevertheless, since it stands for eating, the ordinary seller assumes that the buyer is purchasing it for eating. And what he wrote further, “and moreover, if so, etc.,” is not difficult at all, for certainly Rav and Rabbi Yochanan do not hold this way, but we rule like Shmuel, that the doubt is because the seller is resident and presumably buys it for eating. Examine carefully.
