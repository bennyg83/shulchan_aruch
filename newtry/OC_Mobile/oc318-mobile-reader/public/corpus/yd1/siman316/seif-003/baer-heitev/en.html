Uncertainty (safeka). The Shakh writes that this does not mean a complete double doubt (sefeik sefeika) does not help — for certainly a sefeik sefeika is permitted everywhere — rather it means a sefeik sefeika like this, which is not a complete double doubt, does not allow us to rely on the fact that she is nursing. The Taz writes that it requires examination in practice if she gave birth to two kids, one male and one female — for then there is a complete double doubt: perhaps she had already given birth previously before these were born, and even if she had not, perhaps the male was born last and there is no firstborn here at all, etc. See there. He further writes: a case came before him of one who had a debt with a non-Jew who said he had no money to pay but one cow, and led him to the yard and showed him one cow with a calf standing beside her but not nursing it. The non-Jew said: "I will give you this cow without the calf, for I will raise the calf for myself." The Israelite said: "I think this cow is old and this is her sixth or seventh calf; I want a cow that has calved more often — the more she has calved, the more I will pay." The non-Jew replied: "What can I tell you — that she has calved more than this? That is not so. But she is not as old as you say. Believe me that this is her fourth calf, no more." He accepted the cow from him. Afterward she gave birth at the Israelite’s — what is the law? He concludes: since the non-Jew saw that the Israelite wanted a cow that had calved more often, and even if one could argue the non-Jew thought perhaps the Israelite did not want a cow that had not yet calved due to the danger of birth — and that he specifically intended to give him a cow that had already calved — nonetheless the non-Jew should have said this was her first calf and then boasted more; why did he harm himself by saying it was her fourth? Therefore he certainly was telling the truth, and we rely on him that she had already calved for him, and the calf born for the Israelite is not a firstborn at all. End of quote.
