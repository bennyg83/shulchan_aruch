That she bore it. The Shach explains that this does not mean she certainly bore it. Rather, the offspring she later bore in the Jew's domain is subject to the law of the firstborn because she is presumed possibly to have borne the earlier young. It is therefore a doubtful firstborn and, after it develops a blemish, the owner may eat it. The offspring that follows is exempt in either event. Even if the first young was not hers, an animal does not show maternal affection unless it has previously given birth, as stated in seif 2.
