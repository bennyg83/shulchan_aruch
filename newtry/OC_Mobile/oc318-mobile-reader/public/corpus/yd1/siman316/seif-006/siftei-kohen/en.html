And some say that it is doubtful. Perhaps he forgot to warn him, since it was not on his mind; or perhaps he thought he needed it for slaughter. With this, what is stated above in siman 16 seif 7 works well, where we say that presumably it is permitted, for otherwise he would have informed him that the seller must inform him. There too, even if we say he needs it for slaughter, it is forbidden because of oto ve'et beno; and this is clear.
