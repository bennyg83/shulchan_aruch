We do not rely, etc. For a gentile is not believed except in woman's testimony alone; and all the more here where he speaks to enhance his merchandise (so Rashba in responsa siman 243); and therefore even a gentile speaking mesisach l'fi tumo is not believed—for regarding woman's testimony we rule he is not believed except mesisach l'fi tumo, as explained in Orach Chaim siman 17 and see siman 16 seif 11 and siman 98 s.k. 2 from this.
