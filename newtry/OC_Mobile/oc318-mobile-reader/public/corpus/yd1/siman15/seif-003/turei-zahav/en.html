We do not rely on a gentile, etc. It is difficult—for behold it is a double doubt: doubt whether its months completed, and if you say not completed, doubt whether it has eight days. One may say he holds like the one who says in siman 110 seif 8 that in a davar sheyesh lo matirin a double doubt does not help. However it is difficult on Rama who wrote there that for need one may be lenient; and so Taz end of siman 70 in name of Ran to be lenient in a double doubt in a thing that has permission—and he should have permitted here too even without gentile reliability. And one may say a double doubt is not relevant here—for in any case there is doubt of stillborn or not, and you cannot say "if you find to say stillborn"—and this is not similar to doubt of adultery in first chapter of Kesubos in Tosafot.
