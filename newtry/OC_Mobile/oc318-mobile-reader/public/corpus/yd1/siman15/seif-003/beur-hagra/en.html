(Seif 3) We do not rely, etc. For a gentile is not believed except in woman's testimony mesisach l'fi tumo; and so in rabbinic matters; but in Torah law—not; and nevertheless siman 513 seif 6 in hagahah; and see siman 67 seif 11; and all the more in a matter where it is to enhance his merchandise—even to be stringent; and what is written "under his hand" is not believed, as written there.
