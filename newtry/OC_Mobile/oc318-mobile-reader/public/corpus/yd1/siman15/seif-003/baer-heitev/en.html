And Taz wrote: and we do not say it is a double doubt as Rama wrote in siman 110 seif 8—because a double doubt is not relevant here, for in any case there is doubt of stillborn or not, and you cannot say "if you find to say stillborn," and this is not similar to doubt of adultery in first chapter of Kesubos. And Shach wrote: even if the gentile speaks mesisach l'fi tumo he is not believed—for only in woman's testimony alone, all the more here where it is to enhance his merchandise; and see below siman 16 seif 11 and siman 98.
