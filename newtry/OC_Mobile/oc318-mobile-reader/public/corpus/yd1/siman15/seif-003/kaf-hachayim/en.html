(3) There. And after it goes out into the air of the world—it is permitted immediately. It implies even if feathers did not grow and eyes did not open—it is permitted. However in Tur he brought R' Eliezer ben Yaakov's view that we require eyes opened; and wrote so ruled his father the Rosh z"l; but in Beit Yosef he brought several poskim who hold like first Tanna that we do not require eyes opened; and wrote so we rule, and therefore ruled so in Shulchan Aruch. However he wrote in Hagahot Maor first chapter of Beitzah: "and such as born with wings—that is, large feather on its body that has quills; for all birds whose wings have not grown, Rabbi Yoel forbade because of sheketz"—end of his words, Or Zarua. And Beit Din, Bach wrote so we rule. And so Shach s.k. 2; Peri Chadash ot 2; Perishah ot 1; Lev Pri ot 1; Binyan Yehoshua ot 1; She'eirit Menachem ot 1; place of Shmuel in Chiddushei Dinim for Yoreh De'ah this siman. Mishnat Hashem ot 1; Machberet Avraham ot 1; Erech HaShulchan ot 2; Kometz HaMinchah ot 1; Zivchei Shabbat ot 1; and if slaughtered b'dieved before feathers grew—Perishah there permits; but She'eirit Menachem there wrote they are forbidden; and so Kometz HaMinchah.
