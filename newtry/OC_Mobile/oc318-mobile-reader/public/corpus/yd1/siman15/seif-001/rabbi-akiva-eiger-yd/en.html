(Siman 15, Taz s.k. 1) And we return to the principle of individual and many, etc. That which is decided for him in the Rif's view that this principle of mishnat R' Elazar ben Azarya kav v'naki applies even where many disagree with him—it appears to me that the Rif in chapter Ha'Ediyot HaDayanim who said sometimes a person swears on his own claim and the rabbis disagree with him—and the Rif wrote halachah is like R' Elazar ben Azarya from his mishnah kav v'naki—behold this principle was stated even where many disagree with him—see there; and in Ran who wrote the Geonim ruled there like the rabbis—he holds this principle does not exist where many disagree with him—see there.
