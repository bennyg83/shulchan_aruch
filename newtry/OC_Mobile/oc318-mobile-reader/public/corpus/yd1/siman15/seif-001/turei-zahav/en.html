Into the air of the world. In first chapter of Beitzah (daf 6) R' Eliezer ben Yaakov and the rabbis dispute: R' Eliezer ben Yaakov holds "all the creeping thing that creeps upon the earth" includes chicks whose eyes have not opened; and the rabbis hold all the time it has not gone out into the air of the world; and the Rif ruled like the rabbis. And the Ran wrote: even though we rule mishnat R' Elazar ben Azarya kav v'naki, nevertheless since R' Yochanan is established like the rabbis and Rav like R' Eliezer ben Yaakov, and we rule Rav and R' Yochanan—the halachah is like R' Yochanan. And it is difficult: what is the superiority of this principle over that principle of mishnat R' Elazar ben Azarya kav v'naki. And it appears we revoke this because of that and return to the principle of individual and many—the halachah is like the many; and so the Rosh in first chapter and so here Tur and Shulchan Aruch. And one may wonder on the Rosh who rules in first chapter of Beitzah like R' Eliezer ben Yaakov and contradicts his words in first chapter—and I wrote this because I saw in Hagahot Ashiri first chapter of Beitzah: "and such as one born with wings—that is, large feather on its body that has quills; but birds whose wings have not grown, Rabbi Yoel forbade because of sheketz, Or Zarua"—end of his words; and Maran z"l brought this hagahah to halachah. And I wonder on Rabbi Yoel who forbade because of sheketz—like whom does he hold. And it appears to me he holds like R' Eliezer ben Yaakov; and the measure of eyes opened and measure of wings grown are one measure; and if so, according to what Shulchan Aruch ruled like the rabbis, we do not rule also like this hagahah of Ashiri.
