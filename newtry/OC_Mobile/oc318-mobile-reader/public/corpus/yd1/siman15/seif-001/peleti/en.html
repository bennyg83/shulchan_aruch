Lest its limbs were crushed from birth pains—and this is as the Ran wrote: even if it struggled at birth, nevertheless we are not concerned, for we follow majority—and majority of offspring even in great difficulty nevertheless offspring are healthy and strong—so from Ran's words. And it must be said this is not when the animal struggled entirely with deficiency—for otherwise why would the Gemara bring support for Rav Nachman from a calf born on Yom Tov, lest there the mother did not struggle. And it must be said the Gemara holds as simple: without difficulty we are not concerned for crushing, and even with difficulty we are not concerned. And so Tosafot and Rosh raised—even if it cannot stand (and in Tosafot it says cannot walk, but the essence is like Rosh: cannot stand). And this is when we see clearly it cannot stand—for otherwise why need Tosafot and Rosh for proof, behold the Gemara is arranged regarding spreading on ground that all agree it is fit and Rav Nachman's words are unnecessary. Only there we see it stood and it is simple there is no concern only if we do not see whether it stood or not—then without Rav Nachman in any case checking is required, and Tosafot and Rosh teach that even this Rav Nachman does not teach—only he teaches regarding deficiency before us that we see it cannot stand or walk, nevertheless we are not concerned; and see Tosafot and Maharsha—and it is simple.
