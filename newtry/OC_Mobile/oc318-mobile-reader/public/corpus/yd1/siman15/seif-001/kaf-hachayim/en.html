(1) [Seif 1] A chick—all the time it has not gone out, etc.—forbidden. Because of "the creeping thing that creeps upon the earth." Chullin 64a. Tur. And it implies there that this prohibition is only rabbinic and the verse is mere asmakhta. And so Bach—except he brought from there Seder Torah siman 33 that it is forbidden from Torah because of neveilah, only prohibition because of sheretz is asmakhta—see there; and see in Perishah ot 1 what he expanded on this matter—see there. Therefore for practical law certainly the doubt does not emerge and it is doubt of Torah prohibition; and practical difference if there are two pots, one permitted and one forbidden, and such a chick fell and we do not know into which—it is double doubt: perhaps it did not fall here, and perhaps from Torah it is permitted and its prohibition is rabbinic; and everything rabbinic I say it fell into prohibition and it is permitted. Shulchan Aruch HaRav ot 1; Zivchei Tzedek ot 1; and it appears there must also be majority in the dish—for from Torah it is nullified in majority.
(3) There. And after it goes out into the air of the world—it is permitted immediately. It implies even if feathers did not grow and eyes did not open—it is permitted. However in Tur he brought R' Eliezer ben Yaakov's view that we require eyes opened; and wrote so ruled his father the Rosh z"l; but in Beit Yosef he brought several poskim who hold like first Tanna that we do not require eyes opened; and wrote so we rule, and therefore ruled so in Shulchan Aruch. However he wrote in Hagahot Maor first chapter of Beitzah: "and such as born with wings—that is, large feather on its body that has quills; for all birds whose wings have not grown, Rabbi Yoel forbade because of sheketz"—end of his words, Or Zarua. And Beit Din, Bach wrote so we rule. And so Shach s.k. 2; Peri Chadash ot 2; Perishah ot 1; Lev Pri ot 1; Binyan Yehoshua ot 1; She'eirit Menachem ot 1; place of Shmuel in Chiddushei Dinim for Yoreh De'ah this siman. Mishnat Hashem ot 1; Machberet Avraham ot 1; Erech HaShulchan ot 2; Kometz HaMinchah ot 1; Zivchei Shabbat ot 1; and if slaughtered b'dieved before feathers grew—Perishah there permits; but She'eirit Menachem there wrote they are forbidden; and so Kometz HaMinchah. Zivchei Tzedek ot 2.
