(1) [Seif 1] A chick—all the time it has not gone out, etc.—forbidden. Because of "the creeping thing that creeps upon the earth." Chullin 64a. Tur. And it implies there that this prohibition is only rabbinic and the verse is mere asmakhta. And so Bach—except he brought from there Seder Torah siman 33 that it is forbidden from Torah because of neveilah, only prohibition because of sheretz is asmakhta—see there; and see in Perishah ot 1 what he expanded on this matter—see there. Therefore for practical law certainly the doubt does not emerge and it is doubt of Torah prohibition; and practical difference if there are two pots, one permitted and one forbidden, and such a chick fell and we do not know into which—it is double doubt: perhaps it did not fall here, and perhaps from Torah it is permitted and its prohibition is rabbinic; and everything rabbinic I say it fell into prohibition and it is permitted. Shulchan Aruch HaRav ot 1; Zivchei Tzedek ot 1; and it appears there must also be majority in the dish—for from Torah it is nullified in majority.
