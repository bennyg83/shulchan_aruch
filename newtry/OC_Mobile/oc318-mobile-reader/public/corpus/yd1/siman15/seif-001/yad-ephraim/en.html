Bach; and Noda B'Yehuda wrote part Yoreh De'ah siman 7 regarding calf born day 1 an hour before night—it is proved in several places that we taught seven days do not require from time to time, as Taz's view on this; and if so permitted to slaughter at Motzei Shabbat; however l'chatchila one should not act, for Pri Megadim's view requires from time to time specifically; and one may be stringent l'chatchila; and nevertheless one who rules leniently does not lose; and also since those seven days are rabbinic—for from Torah majority of offspring are not stillborn—see there; and so in last Beit Shmuel siman 11 wrote and agreed from time to time is not required; and explained from his words that such is the custom; and see in book Beit Avraham on treifot in responsa compendium that I expanded on this matter and brought Rambam's words that appear from his words it is Torah law except it is doubt stillborn and we do not flog on doubt; but Torah doubt nevertheless exists; and I expanded on Talmud and poskim words there regarding whether to rely on mesisach l'fi tumo that they are eight days—see there; and see in responsa Bigdei Kehunah what he wrote to reconcile Shach's words that not completed months, eight days do not help—this is against Tosafot Shabbat—see there.
He wrote in She'eilat Ya'avetz part 2 siman 56 regarding calves that are not eight days old that they are accustomed to permit slaughtering within eight days to take their stomach to set cheeses, and the seller does not wish to wait until the calf is eight days old but sells immediately when born; and if Jews do not buy, gentiles will buy—and he wrote if possible to appease him to wait, one should do so; and if he does not wish, then slaughter with certain kashrut; and if not, do not bless on the shechitah lest they come to permit the meat for eating—only puncture them; and see siman 119—see there. And he wrote in Tiferet Tzvi siman 67 regarding calves where there is doubt whether they are eight days and whether to rely on permission of horns and teeth, etc.; and there siman 68 he also wrote on this—see there; and see in book responsa Beit Avraham siman 3 that I wrote when it became clear that in those places signs are not clear and it is common to find horns and teeth even before eight days—one who is lenient on this will give judgment in the future—see there. And regarding relying on estimation—for need one may be lenient from mouth of expert slaughterer expert in nature of calves' growth in the place where he resides, and it is clear to him it cannot be less than seven days old—provided he is presumed in fear of Heaven not to treat estimation lightly—see there.
