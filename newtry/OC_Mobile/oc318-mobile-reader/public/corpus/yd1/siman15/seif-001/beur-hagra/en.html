(Extract) A chick, etc. In Chullin 64, as taught "all the creeping thing," etc.—words of R' Eliezer ben Yaakov (in Behemah HaMekishah); and therefore the rabbis disagree with him only where it went out into the air of the world, but if it did not go out into the air of the world—not so; and so in all books of poskim (and even for our girsa nevertheless this is clear in first chapter of Yoma daf 1—that not like Beit Yosef end of siman 86 who needed to infer from words of Rif and Rashba). And after, etc.—like the rabbis; and even though they said mishnat R' Elazar ben Azarya kav v'naki—specifically in the mishnah, but not in the beraisa. Terumat HaDeshen 54 ot 1; but the Rosh in first chapter of Yoma ruled like R' Eliezer ben Yaakov and wrote that even in the beraisa we rule like R' Eliezer ben Yaakov, as written in Yevamos (37) seif 1; and further wrote in Terumat HaDeshen and the Ran that Rav and Shmuel hold like them—the rabbis—from their dispute in first chapter of Yoma there on Yom Tov we learn that on a weekday R' Eliezer permits; and the stam regarding eyes not opened—from that R' Eliezer ben Yaakov said even on a weekday, etc.—according to his words; but Tosafot there s.v. "a chick" wrote it happened to coincide, etc. (end).
