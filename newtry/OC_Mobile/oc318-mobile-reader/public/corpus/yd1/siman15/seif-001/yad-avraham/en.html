(Siman 15 seif 2) And if it is not known that its months are completed—it is forbidden because of doubt of stillborn until beginning of the eighth night. In Birkhei Yosef he wrote: a kid slaughtered and two witnesses disputed whether it was eight days old—permitted through double doubt. And one may say for our case that Rashba regarding gentile mesisach l'fi tumo—and it appears this is because Taz wrote there it is davar sheyesh lo matirin, and this is when not yet slaughtered; unlike when slaughtered it is not davar sheyesh lo matirin and double doubt helps. And if so, similarly in doubt whether it was nine months one may permit from Torah. And in book Tzitz Eliezer he permits for another reason. But according to Pri Megadim in Sha'ar HaTefillah that there is no double doubt where sages concerned for minority—if so there is no double doubt even after slaughter.
