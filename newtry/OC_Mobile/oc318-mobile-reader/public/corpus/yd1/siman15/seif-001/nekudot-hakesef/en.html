(Siman 66 s.k. 1) And one may wonder on the Rosh, etc. Requires study—for in chapter Elu Treifot he did not copy except words of Rav Alfas, as known to one who knows our rabbi's way who copies Rif's language anonymously in several places even though it does not seem right to him; and in first chapter of Beitzah it is his essential ruling. And therefore Tur and Beit Yosef and other later authorities wrote in name of Rosh that he ruled like R' Eliezer ben Yaakov—not as he wrote in his name that he rules like first Tanna. And so it appears explicitly from words of Maadanei Melech 4:179b as I wrote; also what he wrote hagahah Ashiri ruled like R' Eliezer ben Yaakov—does not exist, for if so what does he say "and such as born with wings," etc.—it should have said explanation "born with wings," etc.; and further what does he say "Rabbi Yoel forbade because of sheketz"—what praise of Rabbi Yoel who forbade thus, for this is like R' Eliezer ben Yaakov. Rather certainly he says thus: that which R' Eliezer ben Yaakov and rabbis dispute is such as born with wings, etc.; all birds whose wings have not grown, Rabbi Yoel forbade because of sheketz—meaning that although from "creeping thing upon the earth" there is nothing, nevertheless from sheketz and disgust it is forbidden to eat; and so appears from all later authorities who although ruled like first Tanna, copied Hagahot Maor words for halachic ruling.
