Forbidden. Because of "the creeping thing that creeps upon the earth" (Tur)—for even its creeping within its shell is called creeping; and see below siman 66 seif 2 and siman 86 seif 8.
Permitted immediately. Even if its eyes have not opened (Beit Yosef in name of most poskim). However, Hagahot Maor wrote from Or Zarua that because of "abomination you shall not eat it" one may not eat until wings grow—that is, large feather on its body that has quills; for all birds whose wings have not grown, Rabbi Yoel forbade because of sheketz—end of his words; and so Or HaChaim kelal 55 din 11; and so later authorities.
