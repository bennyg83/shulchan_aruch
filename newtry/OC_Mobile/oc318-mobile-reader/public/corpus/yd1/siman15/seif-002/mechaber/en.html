An animal that gave birth—if it is known that its months are completed—that is, nine months for a large animal and five for a small one—it is permitted immediately on the day it was born; and we are not concerned lest its limbs were crushed (meaning: were smashed and broken) from the pains of birth. And if it is not known that its months are completed—it is forbidden because of doubt of stillborn until the beginning of the eighth night.
