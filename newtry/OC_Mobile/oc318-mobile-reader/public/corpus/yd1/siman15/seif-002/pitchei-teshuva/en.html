Beginning of the eighth night. See Pri Megadim who wrote we require seven days from time to time pass; and if born end of day 1, if beginning of Motzei Shabbat night arrived it still did not leave category of stillborn until end of next day—see there; and so implied from Taz's language s.k. 2 who uses language from time to time. However in responsa Noda B'Yehuda tinyana part Yoreh De'ah siman 7 in responsa of the author's son he expanded and brought many proofs that from time to time is not required; and wrote so is Taz's view in siman 14; and concluded nevertheless if a case comes to my hand it is hard for me to be lenient since it is explicit in famous decisors for prohibition; and nevertheless one who is lenient does not lose, since in any case it is only rabbinic prohibition (see on this in Dagul MeRevavah and responsa Noda B'Yehuda tinyana part Even HaEzer siman 19 and in his book responsa Shivat Tzion siman 24 if it is clear those seven days are only rabbinically)—see there. Again I saw in new Pri Megadim books printed in name of one great one who wrote on Pri Megadim's aforementioned words they are mistaken words [now I saw the rabbi z"l himself retracted in his book Pri Megadim Orach Chaim siman 498 in Mishbetzot s.k. 2 see there; also in his book Teivat Gome chapter Re'eh]. Also in last responsa Beit Shmuel part Yoreh De'ah siman 11 he raised from time to time is not required—see there; and so raised in responsa Teshuvah MeAhavah part 1 siman 196 and 197—see there; and so agreed in responsa Rabbeinu Akiva Eiger siman 257—see there [and in responsa of his son-in-law Gaon Chatam Sofer siman 17]. And b'dieved if they slaughtered within eight—see in Dagul MeRevavah what its law. And see in s.k. after it.
