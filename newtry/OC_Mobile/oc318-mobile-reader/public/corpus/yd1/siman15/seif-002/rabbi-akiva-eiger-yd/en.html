(There, Taz s.k. 2) And if so why forbidden, etc., seven times from time to time. From this language it implies seven times from time to time are required; and one may say part of the day of birth counts as all of it; and so Pri Megadim wrote; but Taz wrote simply to be lenient; and in my humble opinion the essence is like Taz—for Rashbag relied on verse "seven days it shall be with its mother" and there from time to time is not required, as proved in Tosafot Arachin (daf 18b) s.v. "from its mishnah." They proved we count years in korban from time of birth and not from time of designation. From that we say hours disqualify in kodshim—that if between shechitah and sprinkling it became a year old it is invalid. And if it were that we count from designation, you would not find this law—see there; behold it was simple to them that for designation from time to time is not required; and if so you again find the year completes from designation between shechitah and sprinkling—as explained in my humble opinion with God's help.
