And he was not precise that Talmud is arranged in chapter 4 of Beitzah daf 34, etc. As if Rama did not see this Talmud—truly Rama saw it, and behold it is explained in Tur and Shulchan Aruch Orach Chaim there seif 8; and Rama in Darkei Moshe brings it and answers it: "so Rambam ruled chapter 2; and Maggid Mishneh wrote so is Rashba's view, and Beit Yosef; and so Rosh; and so we rule, not like Rabbeinu Nissim who ruled forbidden; and so all in it. And it appears to me specifically regarding crushed bird the Rosh permits because we establish it on its original presumption of fitness, and therefore we are not concerned lest it be treifah; but where there is no presumption of fitness—it is forbidden—as I wrote above regarding calf born on Yom Tov forbidden according to Rosh; so it appears to me"—end of Darkei Moshe. And with this what he challenged—what did R' Zeira resolve from checking roof tiles, etc.—certainly the explainers did not seem to him to distinguish thus; however we who doubt the halachah whether leniently or stringently, since it was not resolved in investigation—therefore where there is good presumption we rule leniently, and therefore Rosh ruled there leniently; but regarding calf born where there is no good presumption, he ruled stringently. However in any case requires study—for that which R' Zeira resolved from roof tiles: stam roof tiles too, majority are good and do not crack, and it is like presumption of crushed bird we establish on its presumption, for majority animals are in presumption of fitness—and so majority roof tiles do not crack; unlike calf born that immediately when born it has deficiency and is not in category of majority—and requires study. Also what he challenged Maharshal and expanded in words of lips—the Bach already preceded in all his words in Orach Chaim there, and so Gaon Av Beit Din z"l wrote in responsa.
