(s.k. 4) And one may say he holds like one who says, etc. This is not so—for here the permitter is not certainly coming, and in such case it is not davar sheyesh lo matirin at all, as below siman 102 seif 2. But in truth there is no beginning to this question: how can you say "if you find to say not completed, perhaps it has eight—what of it that it has eight, nevertheless not completed and it is stillborn"; and further when you say not completed, it cannot have eight—for when it is eight days old it is removed from doubt of stillborn, as stated in Talmud and poskim in chapter R' Eliezer of Milah and in good places; and perhaps this is included in what he wrote afterward "and one may say a double doubt is not relevant here," etc.; but in truth he did not need to raise it in writing, for it is simpler to express in the bosom of the cloak.
