Seif 4 in Shulchan Aruch siman 1. The seven clean days must be consecutive, etc.; and the question of Taz: behold after R. Zeira's stringency there is no practical difference whether she negates or not; and it appears there is practical difference if counting of seven clean days of zivah continues within blood of purity — for counting seven clean days of zavah, blood of purity does not help, for we require completely clean, as Taz wrote and as stated siman 194; unlike if we say she does not negate but only has a new sight — behold it is blood of purity, and simple; and even though the custom is to refrain from relations on blood of purity, nevertheless many laws have practical difference whether forbidden by law or because of custom; and likewise regarding attributing to it, as explained above siman 190.
