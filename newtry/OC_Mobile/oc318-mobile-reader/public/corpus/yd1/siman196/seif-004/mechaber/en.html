Each day of the seven counting days she must check lechatchila twice each day — once in the morning and once near bein hashemashos (Tur in the name of Sefer HaTaharah and responsa); and if she did not check all seven, only once — no distinction whether she checked on the first day of the seven or on the seventh day, or on one of the middle days — after she checked on the day before the seventh and found pure, it counts for her; but if she did not check all seven and on the eighth day checked and found pure, she has only day eight alone and completes for it; and some say she must check on the first day of the seven and on the seventh day, and one may not be lenient; and the checking should be by daylight and not by candlelight (Tashbetz 126 and Rashba in Teshuvot), and bediavad it helps even by candlelight (so appears in Beit Yosef).}
