And not by candlelight. And that which is stated at the beginning of Pesachim: candlelight is good for checking; and even a portico where light is great — one should not check lechatchila by daylight; and as explained in Orach Chayyim siman 433 — one may say it is different regarding checking for chametz, which requires checking holes and cracks in a place daylight cannot reach, unlike by candle one can search holes and cracks; unlike checking the garment and cloth and the like, which can go in an exposed place — therefore better to check by daylight; and further one may say the color of the stain is recognized more by day than at night by candlelight (unlike chametz, which is a physical object); and this is the reason also in negaim, that they are seen only by day; and see Ramban chapter 9 of Laws of Impurity of Tzara'at.
