Counting days. The holy Gaon, author of Shelah page 101, required that on the days of counting clean days she count each day and say "today is day," etc., as written "and she shall count to her"; but responsum Noda Biyhudah second part Yoreh Deah siman 123 disagrees; and see responsum Maharam bar Baruch, Prague edition siman 252, who also wrote she need not count, for it is not like the omer end quote; and so responsum Radvaz part 1 siman 27 also wrote she need not pronounce the number on her lips end quote — the reason.
