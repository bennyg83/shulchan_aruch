One who emits semen during the days of her counting: if it is within 6 onos from her having intercourse, it nullifies that day. Therefore, one who had intercourse and afterward saw and stopped does not begin to count 7 nekiyim until 6 complete onos pass for her, lest she emit. Therefore, she does not begin to count until the 5th day from her having intercourse; for example, if she had intercourse on Motzaei Shabbos, she does not begin to count until the 5th day, for we hold that semen does not spoil until 6 complete onos pass, from 24 hours to 24 hours. And if she had intercourse on Motzaei Shabbos and emitted on Wednesday night before the time of her having intercourse on Motzaei Shabbos, she is still within the sixth onah from her having intercourse, and it nullifies; therefore, the 5th day will be the first of her counting. {Rama: And she should stop on Wednesday toward evening, and the 5th day counts toward the count. And some wrote that one should wait one additional day — meaning, that she should not begin counting until Friday, and it will be the first day of her counting — for we are concerned lest she have intercourse on Sunday at bein hashemashos and think it is day, and perhaps it is night; and if she begins counting from Thursday, it will be within 6 onos of her having intercourse. Therefore, one should add one more day, for from now it is impossible to come to error (Terumas HaDeshen siman 245, and Agur in the name of Mahari"l, and Sha"d; and so wrote Mahara"i and Mahari"k shoresh 35). And so is the practice in all these lands, and one should not change. And there are women who practiced to be stringent further, to wait until 7 days (there in Terumas HaDeshen), and there is no reason for the matter; and the stringent one may be stringent, and the lenient one is rewarded to hasten himself for the mitzvah. And some wrote that now there is no distinction between whether she had intercourse with her husband or did not have intercourse, and any woman who sees, even a ketem, needs to wait 5 days together with the day that she saw, and stop toward evening, and count 7 nekiyim (there in Terumas HaDeshen in the name of Or Zarua and Mahari"k); and so is the practice in these lands, and one should not change (Sefer HaTerumah and Sefer Mitzvos Gadol).}
