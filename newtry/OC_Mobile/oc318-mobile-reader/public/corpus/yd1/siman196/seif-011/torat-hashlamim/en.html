Without having relations. Even if her husband is in the city — so Shach and Taz, unlike Bach; and Shach wrote: and greater than this they practice that even a bride does not count seven clean days until day five of her sight, even though the groom has not yet come to the city; even though per my view there is no reason to be stringent at all, nor mentioned in any early or late posek — nevertheless let it be only among permitted matters where others practiced prohibition, etc.; and nevertheless in time of pressure one may permit a bride that immediately when she stops in purity she counts seven clean days — for thus is better than the custom that they marry while niddah end quote; and it appears this "time of pressure" that Shach used literally means such as they already slaughtered the feast and the like, in a matter impossible to delay the wedding until she becomes pure — then better to count the seven clean days immediately so she is not married while niddah — a fortiori; and see Taz s.k. 7, that it is clear in his words one should not be stringent at all regarding a bride, even a widow after her chuppah, as long as she has not yet entered complete relations with him; and law of bride after relations of mitzvah — see above siman 193 and in Magen Avraham this siman s.k. 5.

And one should not change. And Shelah wrote page 101a, thus: an incident came before me with a woman on day four of her niddah, but she did not have relations with her husband on the night before her sight — and I permitted her to wear whites on day four and begin counting on day five; and my reason was: even though Terumat HaDeshen was stringent not to begin counting until day six of her sight, and we are stringent stringency upon stringency even though she did not have relations before her sight — nevertheless in this incident I did not wish to be stringent, for it is a stringency that leads to leniency: if she had begun counting on day six, then her night of immersion would fall on the night of Shabbat after Yom Tov; better to leave the above stringency in order to bring the washing close to immersion and she immerses on the night of Yom Tov end quote; and likewise even though one should not change the custom — nevertheless where there is room to be lenient without relations one should not be stringent; and in detail, as I already wrote above ch"q 15, that some great poskim hold even with relations she does not negate what she discharges after three onot.
