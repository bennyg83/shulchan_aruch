If she found a stain. See responsum Tiferet Tzvi Yoreh Deah siman 24, who wrote specifically if she found the stain within the first three days she does not attribute; but if she found the stain after three days, only there is concern that this stain she saw within three days and did not know until now — she attributes to her wound and her niddah and counts also the first three days for counting — not like one rav who erred in this end quote; and so it appears in responsum A.A.Z. Pnei Me'orot part 2 siman 170 end quote; however in responsum Beit Efrayim, which I will cite below in name of his brother, it is clear his view is not so [and examine this in Sha"ch above siman 190 seif 8 and responsum Chemdat Shlomo siman 20, what he wrote at length].
