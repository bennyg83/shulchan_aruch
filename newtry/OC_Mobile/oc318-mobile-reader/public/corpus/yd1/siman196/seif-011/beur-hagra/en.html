One who discharges, etc. Tosafot page 33a s.v. ro'eh, etc.; and Raavad disagrees with them and said this is specifically for taharot; and as stated 37a: one who reads "it negates" — and for her husband one who discharges is permitted; and proof from what is stated 67b: after the act, etc.; but they said, etc., otherwise even though she discharges she is permitted; and in chapter 3 29b in all of them she had relations — and R. Yonah answered: on day seven discharge does not negate, but in Tashbetz they negated it; and Rosh there and in Taharat HaKodesh; and he wrote in Tashbetz that therefore the Geonim did not mention this at all; and nevertheless he wrote one should be stringent.
Six onot. Chapter 9 of Shabbat (86b): but the Sages say, etc.; but Rambam's version is three onot; and so Razah there, for so is the reading in precise books; and so in Yerushalmi there.
And there are women who practiced, etc. Rambam already distanced this; and it appears their error came from Tosafot Shabbat 13b s.v. bimei, etc., and R. Tam, etc.
