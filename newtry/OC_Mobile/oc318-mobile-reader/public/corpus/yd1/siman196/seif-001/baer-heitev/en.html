In purity. Bein hashemashos and bediavad even if she only checked in the morning — meaning from day two of her sight onward, as below seif 2. Shach.
The congregation. Even if she has not yet prayed Maariv.
Shabbat. Shach wrote that from Maharil's words it appears that even though she also prayed and they accepted Shabbat it is permitted; but the Gaon Ammo"z"l wrote for the Rav that if the prayer is Maariv one may not be lenient even bediavad; and so is Maharshal's view in a responsum (and from the Agur I cited it is proven that even for those who are lenient it is only until 24 hours; and if so when the day is so long we consider day and night equal. But after that, for all it is forbidden) end quote (and Taz wrote in name of Rashal that since our women begin counting from day 6 it is obvious she can count even after Maariv prayer on erev Shabbat).
Toward evening. Shach wrote: it appears to me per the Rav's view, even if she saw blood near evening she stops before Barchu at the beginning of her count; and if she sees blood until near evening, it is obvious there is no greater bediavad than this, and she begins to stop after Barchu; and what he wrote "and they practice lechatchila to be careful" — means where possible, such as when she does not see blood before Barchu end quote.
