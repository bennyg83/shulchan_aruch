Siman 61 in Shulchan Aruch siman 1. And they consider this bediavad; per my humble opinion I say, to resolve Tosafot's question on Shach siman 375: why regarding mourning that day did not count — because here if we are stringent to consider night, sometimes there is also a leniency, as explained s.k. 89: a woman established a veset three times and is not concerned further until uprooted three times, and all stains she attributes to be lenient; and likewise if uprooted three times she is no longer concerned; and it is clear it must be specifically in one onah — equal day or night; and if so, if a woman sees twice at night on twentieth of the month and on third saw on Yom Tov while still a long day after the congregation prayed Maariv — if we consider it night, this woman establishes on the tenth a veset and need not be concerned for any other veset; and similarly if she establishes veset by day and uprooted it twice at night and on third saw by day after Maariv prayer — if we consider it night it is uprooting and she is permitted even in that onah she was accustomed to before, for uprooting as required by law; and in this matter there would be a great leniency; and therefore they consider it day, for in truth it is still a long day, unlike mourning where we are properly stringent because no leniency emerges — a fortiori; and one may reject that also regarding mourning a leniency is relevant for distant hearing — if it is night it is after thirty days.
