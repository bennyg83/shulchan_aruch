(Siman 196 in Taz s.k. 11) And I have astonishment, etc. It is not difficult at all — for there, since it became known that not in holiness he is not a complete Jew, for in several laws he differs from Israel, as below siman 269; but even one born from a niddah we hold kosher, as explained in Even HaEzer siman 4 seif 13.
