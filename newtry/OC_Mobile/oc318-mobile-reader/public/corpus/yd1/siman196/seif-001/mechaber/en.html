The laws of wearing white garments and examining them; it contains 13 se'ifim. The seven days that the zavah counts begin from the day after the day she stopped on. And such is her law: if she sees two days or three and stopped seeing, she examines on the day she stopped in order that she stop in purity; and this examination should be near bein hashemashos (and so they practice lechatchila, and bediavad even if she did not examine herself, only in the morning, and found herself pure, that suffices) (Tur in the name of Rashba, and Beit Yosef even according to the words of [Rosh]). And a person should always teach [to be stringent lechatchila] within his house that she should examine on the day of her cessation of purity with a tightly inserted cloth, and that it should remain there all bein hashemashos, for this examination removes from all doubt (Rashba in Taharat HaKodesh):
{Rama: Some say if the congregation prayed ma'ariv and the day is still great, she cannot examine (or) then wear whites and begin counting from the next day, since the congregation has already made it night (Terumas HaDeshen). And some say it is permitted even if the congregation made Shabbat (Agur in the name of Mahari"l). And they practice to be careful lechatchila, and bediavad one need not be concerned. And some women practice that if she stopped before Barchu and returned to see a ketem or blood within her counting days, then they stop even after Barchu if she was corrupted near evening; and they consider this matter bediavad, and one should not protest against them, for they received thus from a certain sage who instructed them, and it is an old custom.}
