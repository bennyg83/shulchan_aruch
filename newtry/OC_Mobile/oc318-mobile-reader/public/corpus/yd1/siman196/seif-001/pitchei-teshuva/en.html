Shabbat. Avodat HaTashbitz wrote in name of Shach, but after that for all it is forbidden; and see in Dagul MeRevavah, who challenged this and wrote one should not be stringent at all, even lechatchila; and it appears even Shach was not stringent except when she also prayed and the congregation prayed Maariv and accepted Shabbat; and also in this it appears to be lenient bediavad end quote. And what Baer Heitev and Taz wrote in name of Rashal, etc. — see Dagul MeRevavah, who also challenged this: Maharshal deals (on Shach s.k. 19) with a sight that she saw after Maariv prayer while it is still day; and regarding when to wear whites — if on Thursday of her sight while it is still day, or Thursday from the morrow — on this he wrote that since our women begin from day six one may be lenient; but here it deals with checking of hefsek taharah — there is no difference for our women that if after Maariv prayer it is night, the morrow does not enter the count of seven clean end quote — these matters were said and repeated in his book, responsum Noda Biyhudah second part siman 131 end quote.
