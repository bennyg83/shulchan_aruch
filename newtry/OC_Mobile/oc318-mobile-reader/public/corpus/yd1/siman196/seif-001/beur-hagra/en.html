Seven days, etc. Tosafot page 33a s.v. veihu, etc.
And such is her law if, etc. Meaning to exclude one day, as written seif 2.
She checks, etc. There in the Gemara 69a and Tosafot there s.v. vehakha, etc.
And this checking, etc. To be stringent lechatchila like R. Yehudah in the mishnah there 68a; and this is what the Hagahah states "and so," etc., and bediavad, etc. — for we hold like the Sages.
And forever, etc., and it should be, etc. Tashbetz. And as stated in the Gemara: they taught, R. Yehudah said to them, if only, etc., and as stated above Negaim (1); and I say to them, if only, etc., and he said to them when we say, etc.; and this is impossible except in such a case with a tightly inserted cloth, etc.
And some say permitted, etc. Tosafot Ketubot 47a s.v. demasar, etc., and Pesachim 99b s.v. ad, etc., and Berakhot 27b — they raised a dilemma: one says Kiddush, etc., one says Havdalah, etc. — implies that for other matters not related to Shabbat it is certainly not night.
