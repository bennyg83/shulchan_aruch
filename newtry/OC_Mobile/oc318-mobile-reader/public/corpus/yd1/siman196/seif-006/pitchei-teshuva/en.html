First of the seven. See in Dagul MeRevavah, who wrote that even if she did not check on the first day and checked on one of the middle days and also on the seventh day — even Semag agrees they count for her, provided at least on the day she stopped she checked in order to stop in purity end quote; and see in his book responsum Noda Biyhudah second part Yoreh Deah siman 128, who wrote there specifically first and seventh are required; but if she checked by way of example on first and third it does not help and she cannot immerse on seventh; and if afterward she again did not check until tenth she has only tenth; but if she checked afterward on ninth she immerses toward evening — for lacking one day from here, behold checking of third and ninth counts as first and seventh; and even if she checked on eighth — meaning she checked on first, third, and eighth — she immerses on eighth toward evening, and eighth joins with first even though more than five days between eighth and first the third combines them end quote; and from this it is proven that if she did not check on first only on middle days and seventh it does not help — unlike Dagul MeRevavah end quote.
