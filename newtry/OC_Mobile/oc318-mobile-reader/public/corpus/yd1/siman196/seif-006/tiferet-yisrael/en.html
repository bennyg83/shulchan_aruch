Seif 6 in Shulchan Aruch siman 1. One who discharges, etc. — six onot, the first in Orach Chayyim challenged: if so, on four he makes separation; if so on seven in Sivan the Torah was given — how do we say time of giving Torah on sixth of Sivan? (And one need not say time fifty for counting, for some say for counting the Torah was given — thus one must interpret his words.) And the Yerushalmi chapter Re'eh was hidden from him, for it is clear there: six onot he does not learn from Sinai at all, only from this gezeirah shavah: "this is the law of zav" and "that which issues from him" — Sha"z: just as zav three, so Sha"z three; and he holds one does not learn generations from Sinai, as explained there in the matter of R. Yochanan; and one may say that even on five he makes separation — there is different, for revealed before the Omnipresent those who had relations on day four and night will not discharge at all; and for generations we learn from that gezeirah shavah of zav — examine.
