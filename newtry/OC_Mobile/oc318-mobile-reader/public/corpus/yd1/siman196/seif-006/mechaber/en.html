All these examinations — both the examination of cessation of purity and the examination of all seven — must be with a white old linen garment, or cotton wool, or clean soft white wool; and she should insert it in that place, deep into the holes and cracks, until the place where the sun reaches, and see if there is any reddish appearance in it; and she should not insert it only a little to wipe herself. And if it is very difficult for her eyes to insert it so deep — at least the examination of the day of cessation of purity and the examination of the first day of the seven should be until the place where the sun reaches. {Rama: And if she did not do so on the first day's examination, she should do so once from the examinations of the other days (Beit Yosef). However, bediavad if she did not do so at all, only that she examined herself well in holes and cracks, deeply as her strength allows, even though it did not reach the place where the sun reaches — that suffices for her (Beit Yosef, for so say most poskim, and so Mahari in his rulings siman 77 and Beit Chadash).}
