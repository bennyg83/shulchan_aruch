Only day one. And her opening is open; and it is written in Sefer Ma'adanei Melekh page 300a, and this is the language: and you will ask — for us what practical difference? Behold even a woman who sees a stain — the custom is she must wait five days with the day she saw, and afterward stops and counts seven clean days (as below siman 11); and it appears there is practical difference for us, such as a bride who is permitted immediately when she stops to count seven clean days; and see Shach, who wrote in name of Hagahot Maimoniyot another practical difference: if she returned and saw within the counting days — since she saw only one day within the counting days, morning checking does not help even bediavad; but if she saw only a stain, one may be lenient both within counting days and at the beginning end quote.
