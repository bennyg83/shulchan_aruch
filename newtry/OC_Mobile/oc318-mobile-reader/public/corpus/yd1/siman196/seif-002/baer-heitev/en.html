And her opening is open; and Shach wrote: and it appears to me there is practical difference for us, such as if she returned and saw within the days of counting — since she saw only one day within the days of counting, morning checking does not help even bediavad; but if she saw only a stain, one may be lenient both within counting days and at the beginning. So too in Sefer Ma'adanei Melekh.
