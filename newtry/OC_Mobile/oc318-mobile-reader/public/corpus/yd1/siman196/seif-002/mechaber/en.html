She saw only one day and stopped on it — on that day she needs to examine herself with a tightly inserted cloth, and that it should remain there all bein hashemashos. {Rama: And bediavad if she examined herself near bein hashemashos and found herself pure, even though the cloth was not with her all bein hashemashos, that suffices (Tur in the name of Rashba, and the same in the name of Ramban; Beit Yosef [explains] for Rambam, and so appears in the Gemara); but a morning examination does not help, since she saw only one day (Rashba and Ramban in the name of Rivash, and so too Rambam chapter 6 of Avot HaTumah and in commentary on Mishnah end of chapter 4 and in Bertinoro there).}
