Seif 1 in Shulchan Aruch siman 1 in the Hagahah. Since the congregation already made it night; and per my humble opinion I rely on what R. Yehudah said in the mishnah, that she must check from minchah and upward; and Beit Yosef already wondered: the day is still long two and a half hours — what is this great measure from night that he mentioned? Indeed R. Yehudah according to his reasoning holds minchah until plag minchah; and see Rashi to Berakhot, who holds from there his law is like night for all matters; and if so hefsek taharah does not help; and therefore he gave time from minchah and upward; and per this they already said in Berakhot the halachah was not stated whether like R. Yehudah or like the Sages — one who acts like this acts, and one who acts like that acts; if so the congregation who prayed Maariv while still day took the essence like R. Yehudah, that it is night — if so how can she stop in purity? What for R. Yehudah does not help — examine.
