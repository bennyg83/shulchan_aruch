A deaf woman who hears but does not speak, or who speaks but does not hear — they are like sound-minded women; but if she does not hear and does not speak, and likewise the shoteh or one whose mind was disturbed from illness — they need a sound-minded woman to examine them and establish vestot for them so they may be permitted to their husbands; if vestot were established for them, behold they are like all other women; if not established, they are concerned from thirty days to thirty days and examine through a sound-minded woman.
