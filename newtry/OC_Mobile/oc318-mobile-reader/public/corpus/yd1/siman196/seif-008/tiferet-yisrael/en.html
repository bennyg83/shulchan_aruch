Seif 8 in Shulchan Aruch 13. To discharge all seed and it is not forbidden because of wasting seed — Tosafot were puzzled regarding relations with a cloth; and these matters are an arranged mishnah; and it must be that even though in three days for absorption most seed fit for pregnancy is absorbed immediately, and the minority absorbed after two or three days — what is absorbed will not help at all from bathing, since the womb already absorbed; and if so permitted to bathe, for what was not absorbed, on the majority it will not be absorbed further, and there is no wasting of seed here — permitted; and this was in hidden manner from the Rav, first in laws of Yom Kippur, who wondered that they permitted a woman to bathe, for behold it is wasting seed — see there.
