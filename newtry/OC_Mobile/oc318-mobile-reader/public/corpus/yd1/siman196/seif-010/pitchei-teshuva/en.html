In three days, etc. And if she counts clean days on a stain she found — if she afterward finds a stain in the first clean garments, see Even HaEzer in Beit Shmuel siman 63 s.k. 3; and see in Dagul MeRevavah, who wrote one may be lenient in this; and a fortiori one may be lenient when they urged her to marry and she reconciled and found a stain in the first three days end quote. [And see responsum Chatam Sofer siman 187: a woman who checked herself to go to synagogue, and on the day of hefsek taharah she stopped and counted, and within three days found a stain and has something to attribute — if we say that within three days we do not attribute a stain, or we consider from the day she checked to go to synagogue — already they went in this with rulings: Avnei Nezer siman 79 is lenient and Tzitz Eliezer siman 65 is stringent in his view; and in Sha"ch s.k. 33 leans to leniency, but wrote that Rashi's explanation in the mishnah opening Niddah — from deposit to deposit, morning and evening checking — Maharam of Lublin learned specifically regarding checking instituted by Chazal, but not another checking, for she does not examine well; and he brings Maharam Lublin part 3 from Moharach din 4 — somewhat like Tzitz Eliezer; but one may say there for taharot it is different end quote. And he z"l wrote that Rashi never intended this at all, only wrote so for another intent; and in the case of the question he raised that if she now says it is clear to me I checked well, one may be lenient end quote.]
