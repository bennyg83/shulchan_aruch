One. Shach wrote, and it appears here we do not practice being stringent because of the decree of bein hashemashot, and she immerses immediately after four days and one clean day, for it is uncommon that she errs in counting; and in an uncommon matter the Sages did not decree; and Taz wrote, and to him perhaps she saw blood in those six onot but covered it — Sha"z, as written siman 193 regarding relations of mitzvah, for there is different, since blood is common from relations.
Time. For behold she already counted seven clean days, only lacking immersion; and it is surprising from what is written in Even HaEzer siman 13 seif 5: a convert and his wife, when they converted, separate them ninety days to distinguish between seed planted in holiness and seed planted not in holiness; and likewise we should separate her after he had relations in prohibition, to distinguish if she conceived in prohibition when she immersed improperly; and it appears here it deals with a woman whose husband was available to her before this — therefore the distinction is not relevant; but if her husband was not with her before this act within ninety days, automatically she needs separation after this forbidden relations for ninety days; and difficult practically; but transgressing the law requiring seven clean days because of anticipation, as written end siman 193, is not at all like this law, for there is only a rabbinic prohibition alone end quote (and in Nechakel Tzeddek he answered that there, since planted not in holiness he is not a complete Jew — in several laws he differs from Israel, as written siman 269; but even one born from a niddah we hold kosher end quote (and see Beit Shmuel there s.k. 15, who also sensed this)); and in Beit Yosef wrote in name of Rosh that if she immersed on night seven and had relations on day seven, also it is not a contradiction, and she immerses immediately after seven; and Shach wrote difficult practically — he wished to equate words of Semak to Rosh, and the simple meaning of Semak implies only after seven specifically end quote).
