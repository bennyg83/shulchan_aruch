If she erred in the count on day one. It is simple that the same applies for two days — she counts after the six onot two more days; only that one is not accustomed to err so much, therefore he mentioned day one.
She must wait six onot. For those days when she discharges semen do not count for her toward cleanliness; nevertheless do not challenge: behold it states in seif 10 we require seven consecutive, and here she stops during days of semen discharge — discharge of semen does not negate retroactively like blood, only those days themselves are not counted as clean — so it states in the Gemara. And one may say perhaps she saw blood in those six onot, only semen covered it — Sha"z, as stated siman 193 regarding relations of mitzvah, for there is different since blood is common from relations.
Behold she immerses at any time. For behold she already counted seven clean days, only lacking immersion; and I have great astonishment in this seif from what is written in Even HaEzer siman 13 seif 5: a convert and his wife, when converted, separate them ninety days to distinguish between seed planted in holiness and seed planted not in holiness; and likewise we should separate her after he had relations in prohibition, to distinguish if she conceived in prohibition when she immersed improperly; and it appears here it deals with a woman whose husband was available to her before this — therefore the distinction is not relevant, similar to a woman raped under her husband who need not wait ninety days if her husband had relations with her first, as Rama wrote there Even HaEzer seif 6; and per this, if her husband was not with her before this act within ninety days, automatically she needs separation after this forbidden relations for ninety days — so it appeared to me; and difficult practically; but what I wrote above end siman (193) [192] transgressing the law requiring seven clean days because of anticipation — she need not separate — this is not at all like that case of convert we mentioned, for there is only a rabbinic prohibition alone, as written end siman 192.
