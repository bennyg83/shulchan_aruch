[See responsum Chemdat Shlomo siman 20, who raised that if the stain is found on a garment that does not accept tumah, even within three days we are not concerned, since something that does not accept tumah is not included in the decree of stains; and so from words of Hagahot Maimoniyot; and in Sefer HaTaharah they wrote only that within three days we do not attribute — meaning in something that requires attributing properly we say it is more reasonable to attribute to her body; but regarding something that does not accept tumah, we do not come to attribute because of it — even if she checked the ground of the world and sat on it she is pure; and this is since without sensation she sees, the Sages did not decree on this; if so, what does it help that within three days her opening is open — ultimately she sees without sensation. And again I saw in responsum Me'il Tzedakah siman 62 that from his words it appears simple to permit stains found on dyed clothing within three days end quote.]
