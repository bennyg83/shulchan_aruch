A woman who had intercourse and afterward saw and stopped and wants to count from the day after her seeing — she should wipe that place well with a cloth or garment to expel all the semen, or bathe in hot water and they will expel all the semen. {Rama: And some say that we are not expert in this in our time, and one should not rely on this (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 6 and Semak). And the practice is thus, for it has already been explained that we practice to wait even if she did not have intercourse at all, in order not to distinguish between one count and another; all the more so in this case. And whoever breaches the fence in these matters in a place where they practiced to be stringent — a snake will bite him.}
