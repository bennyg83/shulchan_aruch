On the day she stopped seeing, and when she examines herself as mentioned, she should wear a garment that was examined for her that it has no ketem; and at night she should put on sheets examined from stains; and from the next day she should begin to count seven nekiyim. {Rama: And this is a kosher custom, that when the woman stops in purity she should bathe and wear white garments; however, if she did not bathe, only her lower part suffices (Mordechai in the name of Rokeach). And so they practice, and one should not change; but in a time of pressure, such as a woman traveling on the road who does not have garments, she may count 7 nekiyim as long as the garment is clean and examined from blood (Agur and Hagahot Sha"d end of seif 19).}
