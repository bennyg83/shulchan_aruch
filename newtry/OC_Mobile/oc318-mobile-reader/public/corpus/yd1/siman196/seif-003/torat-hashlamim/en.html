She should bathe and wear whites. The reason in all this: lest her body or garment become soiled with some stain and she finds it within the seven clean days — then we do not attribute, as explained shortly seif 10; and per this, if bediavad she counted seven clean days without bathing and wearing at all, and afterward checks garment and body and no stain is found — she is permitted to immerse; but lechatchila one should not change the custom to count seven clean days without bathing and wearing whites unless she is on the road, as the Rav concludes.
