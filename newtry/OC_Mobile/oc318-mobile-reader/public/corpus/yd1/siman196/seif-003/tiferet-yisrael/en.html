And they consider this bediavad; and what is below siman 375 — the Mechaber and Rama did not emend that one may not count within seven end quote Shach there; and one may say here if we are stringent to consider night it is a stringency that leads to leniency — needless to say in Talmudic times before R. Zeira's stringency: if on day three of her zivah she saw only after Maariv — if you consider it night there is no zavah gedolah here and she observes one day against one day; unlike if it is day, behold she saw three consecutive days and she is zavah gedolah; and if so the law did not move from its place; and one may say also for us: if she saw blood and there is something to attribute to a wound — if one considers night, behold she was presumed for three days she did not see and attributes; unlike if they consider day, behold she was not presumed to have stopped seeing three days and she is forbidden; and therefore they are not stringent, for the stringency is a leniency — examine.
