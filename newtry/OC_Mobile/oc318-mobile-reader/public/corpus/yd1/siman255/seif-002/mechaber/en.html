Whoever is not in need of taking charity yet deceives the people and takes — he will not die until he needs creatures; and whoever needs to take and cannot live unless he takes — such as an elder, or a sick person, or one with suffering, yet is haughty and does not take — behold, he sheds blood and is guilty regarding his soul, and his suffering is only sins; and whoever needs to take yet afflicts himself and presses the hour and lives a life of hardship so as not to burden the community — he will not die until he supports others; and of him Scripture says, "Blessed is the man who trusts in Him."
