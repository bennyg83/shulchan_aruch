To place it into a confined enclosure. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, Yoreh Deah siman 81. He initially writes that the reason is concern for the kohen's financial loss. Accordingly, with a doubtful firstborn, which even after developing a blemish need not be given to a kohen because the burden of proof rests on the claimant, the owner could confine it. The gaon Maharam Fischels disagreed, and Noda BiYehudah later retracted and agreed that even a doubtful firstborn may not be placed into such an enclosure. See simanim 82 and 83. See also Responsa Chatam Sofer at the ends of simanim 314 and 318.
