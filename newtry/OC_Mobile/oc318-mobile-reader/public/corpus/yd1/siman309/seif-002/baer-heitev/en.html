The tragus. In the chapter Metzora, Rashi explains this as the middle ridge of the ear, meaning the raised portion in the middle of the ear that forms a kind of partition before the ear canal. Taz. The "edge of the ear" means the soft earlobe, called skin, where healthy flesh returns. Rashi writes so.
