The tenukh. In Parashat Metzora Rashi explains it as the middle partition in the ear, meaning the raised part in the middle of the ear that is made like a partition before the ear opening. Taz. “The lip of the ear” means the soft lobe of the ear, which is called skin, for there it returns and heals. So wrote Rashi.
Blemishes. See Levush, who brought all the blemishes that are blemishes according to all views and explained them.
