The laws of heart treifot. In it are 6 seifim. The heart has three cavities. If one of them was punctured — treifah. And even if terefes ha-lev (the heart sac) sealed it — it seals; but if heart fat itself sealed it — kosher.
{Rama: And some say sealing of heart fat does not help, as stated below at end of this siman (this is implied from Mordechai Piskei Avodah Zarah and Hagahot Ashiri); and there is concern for their words.}
