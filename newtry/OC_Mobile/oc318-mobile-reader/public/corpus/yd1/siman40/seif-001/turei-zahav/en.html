And even if milk terefes. Meaning: a pouch in which the heart is hidden; since it is made like a hat it does not seal tightly.
But if fat, etc. His proof from seif 6 that sealing of fat does not help there; and so Orach Chaim sha'ar 52 and Moharach z"l wrote that there he deals with terefes of the heart, which is also called heart fat — for Rashi explains there daf 49 terefes of the heart is heart fat, etc.; and therefore one must say thus — for if he dealt in seif 6 with the fat itself, how is it relevant to say fat stuck and adherent, for such is its growth; and his words are correct — for if it does not refer to terefes, what novelty did they see in that it was stuck; and why did Raavyah need to cut there; nevertheless it is difficult to be lenient since Orach Chaim and Rama forbid.
