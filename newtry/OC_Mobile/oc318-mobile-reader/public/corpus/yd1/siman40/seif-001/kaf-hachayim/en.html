A) [Seif 1] The heart has three cavities — one large in middle and one small on its right and one small on its left; punctured to one of cavities — treifah. Tur. Levush. From Rashba in short Teshuvot HaRashba second house third gate entry gargeret. I heard from expert examiner: heart has large cavity which is heart canal; on animal's left small cavity from it; on right one small cavity smaller than left's — but this cavity does not descend to bottom, rather splits and enters large middle cavity — and I saw with my eyes. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur ot 2 wrote thus is settled even for those who say two cavities in heart — three on name of beginning and two because small enters large — see his words. However Rashi Chullin 45b entry small cavity: many small chambers around — end; and so Ralbag. And Perishah end ot 1: reasonable every cavity treifah. And so Beit Efrayim responsum 6; and Mekor Chaim ot 1: also small cavities open to these three treifah when punctured — end. However Mishnat Zekenim ot 1 wrote we have only Shulchan Aruch words — treifot only in three cavities; and so acharonim who cite Shulchan Aruch language. Chayei Adam general rule 17 ot 1; Ananah siman 19 ot 1; Toldot Zevi siman 68 ot 1; Beit Yitzchak ot 1 — therefore appears in pressing need one may be lenient per Shulchan Aruch and his view.
B) There. And even if milk terefes heart seals it. Meaning: pouch heart hidden in, since made like hat does not seal. Beit Yosef. Taz s.k. 1. Shach s.k. 1. Lechem Panim ot 1. Beit Lechem Yehudah ot 1.
D) There hagahah: and some say heart fat sealing does not help, etc. — concern for their words. And so Levush: be stringent. And so Taz s.k. 2 and Shach s.k. 2. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 3; Pri Chadash ot 1; Lechem Panim ot 2; Beit Lechem Yehudah ot 2; Peleti ot 2; Turei Zahav ot 3; Shach Piskei Din ot 2; She'erit Menachem ot 5; Machatzit HaShekel ot 1; Erech Lechem ot 2; Ananah there. Toldot Zevi there ot 3; Beit Yitzchak there. Zivchei Tzedek ot 1; Perishah ot 2 wrote in financial loss be lenient. Chayei Adam there. Zivchei Tzedek ot 4 — appears in pressing need and great need rely on lenient in financial loss. Zivchei Tzedek ot 2.
E) And all this in animal; but heart fat of bird since its way is to seal — Peleti there. And Mishnat Zekenim ot 2 agreed; and Shach Piskei Din ot 2; in bird lenient in heart fat he did not lose. Mekor Chaim ot 2. Zivchei Tzedek ot 3.
F) Whiteness on heart — doubt fat doubt blow doubt to its cavity; though safek safeka to lenient — nevertheless safek lacking knowledge and treifah. Da'at Kedoshim end ot 1. Zivchei Tzedek ot 5.
