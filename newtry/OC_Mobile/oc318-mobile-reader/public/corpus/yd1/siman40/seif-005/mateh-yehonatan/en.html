(Shulchan Aruch seif 5) Heart removed whether by hand or illness treifah. Rambam did not cite "by illness" because he holds no reality can live moment lacking heart; from these Rambam words Gaon Maharatz Ashkenazi overstepped his measure and permitted if opened bird or animal and found no heart though nothing to hang on — kosher since animal or bird was healthy before slaughter without any weakness sign — certainly lost after slaughter. In his humble opinion does not appear to permit except where something to hang, though distant and strange — nevertheless we hang on it.
